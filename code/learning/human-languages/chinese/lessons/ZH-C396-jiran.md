## You'll want to know: jìrán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C396-NOMATTER396-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jìrán* — "given that; now that".

> *jìrán nǐ bìng le jiù zài jiā xiūxi* — Since you're ill, stay at home and rest.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìrán* is built from *jì* 'already' and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kj+jH* and *nyen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'finished' and the second 'like this'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
