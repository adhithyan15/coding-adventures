# মেট্রো (meṭro) — the metro; an underground railway

## You'll want to know: মেট্রো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C388-TRAM388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**মেট্রো** — *meṭro* — "the metro; an underground railway".

> মেট্রো ট্রামের চেয়ে দ্রুত।
>
> *meṭro ṭrāmer cheye drutô.*
>
> The metro is faster than the tram.

মেট্রোতে, by metro, on the metro.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেট্রো** (*meṭro*) is borrowed from English *metro*, short for *metropolitan railway*.
