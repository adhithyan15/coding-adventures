## You'll want to know: ちこくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C451-COMMUTE451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちこくする** — *chikoku suru* — "to arrive late".

> **あさねぼうして、ちこくしました。** — *asanebō shite, chikoku shimashita* — "I overslept and arrived late."

Polite **ちこくします**, *chikokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが ちこくしました。**, *gakusei ga chikoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちこくする** (*chikoku suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *chikoku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chi*, "late" (Middle Chinese *drij*) and *koku*, "time" (*khok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
