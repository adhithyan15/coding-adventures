## You'll want to know: சந்தா
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C451-VALAKAM451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்தா** — *cantā* — "a subscription; a membership fee".

> நாளிதழுக்கு மாதச் சந்தா கட்டினேன்.
>
> *nāḷitaḻukku mātac cantā kaṭṭiṉēṉ.*
>
> I paid the monthly subscription for the newspaper.

சந்தா கட்டு is to pay a subscription.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்தா** (*cantā*) is usually traced to Hindustani *candā*, "a subscription, a contribution"; the path into Tamil is not settled.
