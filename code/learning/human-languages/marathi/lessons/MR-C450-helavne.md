## You'll want to know: हेलावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C450-MAN450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हेलावणे** — *helāvṇe* — "to be deeply moved".

> आईचं पत्र वाचून तो हेलावला.
>
> *āīcaṁ patra vācūn to helāvlā.*
>
> Reading his mother's letter, he was deeply moved.

Past (he …): तो हेलावला, *to helāvlā*.

Often with मन: तिचं मन हेलावलं, her heart was stirred.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हेलावणे** (*helāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
