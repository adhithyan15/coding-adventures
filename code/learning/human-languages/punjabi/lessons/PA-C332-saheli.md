## You'll want to know: ਸਹੇਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C332-KIN332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹੇਲੀ** — *sahelī* — "a woman's female friend".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਆਪਣੀ ਸਹੇਲੀ ਨਾਲ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਗਈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ āpṇī sahelī nāl bāzār gaī.*
>
> My sister went to the market with her friend.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹੇਲੀ** (*sahelī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sakhī*, "a woman's confidante"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sahelī*, "woman's female friend", under it (CDIAL 13074).
