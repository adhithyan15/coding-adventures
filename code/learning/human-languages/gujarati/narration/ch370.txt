6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ખતરો (khatro) — a peril.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a misgiving, then the Gujarati for to frighten.

You'll want to know: ખતરો.
ખતરો — khatro — "a peril".
જો આ જૂના પુલ પર ભારે ગાડીઓ ચાલશે તો ખતરો છે. jo ā jūnā pul par bhāre gāḍīo cālśe to khatro che. If heavy vehicles use this old bridge, it is dangerous.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખતરો (khatro) is usually traced to Arabic khaṭar, "danger, risk", through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 6.
બગાડવું (bagāḍvũ) — to spoil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for harm, then the Gujarati for a misfortune.

You'll want to know: બગાડવું.
બગાડવું — bagāḍvũ — "to spoil".
જો તમે વધારે મીઠું નાખશો તો દાળ બગાડશો. jo tame vadhāre mīṭhũ nākhśo to dāḷ bagāḍśo. If you put in too much salt, you will spoil the dal.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આખું કામ બગાડ્યું, teṇe ākhũ kām bagāḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
બગાડવું (bagāḍvũ) is the transitive partner of બગડવું (bagaḍvũ), "to go bad", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit vighaṭatē, "flies apart" (CDIAL 11673).

Lesson 5 of 6.
વેઠવું (veṭhvũ) — to put up with (hardship).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a misfortune, then the Gujarati for to spoil.

You'll want to know: વેઠવું.
વેઠવું — veṭhvũ — "to put up with (hardship)".
જો તમે અત્યારે મહેનત કરશો તો પછી દુઃખ વેઠવું નહીં પડે. jo tame atyāre mahenat karśo to pachī duḥkh veṭhvũ nahī̃ paḍe. If you work hard now, you will not have to suffer later.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘણું દુઃખ વેઠ્યું, teṇe ghaṇũ duḥkh veṭhyũ.

The word, taken apart.
વેઠવું (veṭhvũ) is built on veṭh, "forced labour", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit viṣṭi (CDIAL 11989); "to endure hardship" is the verb's sense, which CDIAL does not give.
