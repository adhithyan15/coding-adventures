Spanish, chapter 758: Más deportes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el voleibol — volleyball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for defeat, then the Spanish for the medal.

You'll want to know: el voleibol.
el voleibol — "volleyball".
Jugamos al voleibol en la playa. We play volleyball on the beach.
Also el vóley. In some countries el vóleibol.

The word, taken apart.
voleibol was borrowed from English volleyball (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
esquiar — to ski.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for volleyball, then the Spanish for boxing.

You'll want to know: esquiar.
esquiar — "to ski".
En invierno vamos a esquiar. In winter we go skiing.
Forms: esquío, esquías. El esquí — skiing, a ski.

The word, taken apart.
esquiar is built on esquí, which is usually traced to Norwegian ski "a strip of wood", partly through French; Spanish added e- before s.

Lesson 4 of 5.
patinar — to skate; to skid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for boxing, then the Spanish for to ski.

You'll want to know: patinar.
patinar — "to skate; to skid".
Los niños patinan en el parque. The children skate in the park.
A car can also patinar on ice: to skid.

The word, taken apart.
patinar is built on patín "skate" (EtymDB), which was borrowed from French patin, built on patte "paw, foot".

Lesson 5 of 5.
el aficionado — the fan; the amateur.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to ski, then the Spanish for to skate.

You'll want to know: el aficionado.
el aficionado — "the fan; the amateur".
Los aficionados cantan en el estadio. The fans sing in the stadium.
Also an amateur: un fotógrafo aficionado.

The word, taken apart.
aficionado is the participle of aficionar (EtymDB), built on afición "liking", which comes from Latin affectiō.
