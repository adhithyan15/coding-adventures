## You'll want to know: il pernottamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C432-ALLOGGIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il pernottamento** — "the overnight stay".

> Il prezzo comprende pernottamento e colazione.
>
> *The price includes the overnight stay and breakfast.*

On a hotel bill, one line per night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pernottamento* is built on *pernottare*, a learned borrowing from Latin *pernoctāre* 'to stay all night', from *nox* 'night' (Lewis & Short).
