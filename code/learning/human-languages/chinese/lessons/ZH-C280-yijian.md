## You'll want to know: 意见
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C280-YIJIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**意见** — *yìjiàn* — "a suggestion; an objection".

> **你有什么意见** *nǐ yǒu shénme yìjiàn* — What do you suggest? Or: what's your objection?

> **我没有意见** *wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn* — I've no objection; fine by me.

The next lesson puts the first character in your hand. It is the yì of *yǒuyìsi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**意见** (*yìjiàn*) is built from 意 'intention' and 见 'to see', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'iH* and *kenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'will, intention' and the second 'to look'; 'a suggestion' or 'an objection' for the pair is the modern word's own sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C256-THINK256-02] -->

*kànfǎ* is your view of a matter; 意见 is what you put forward about it, and often it is a point against.

It is a noun: you have one, 有意见, or you have none, 没有意见.
