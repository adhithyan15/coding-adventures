## You'll want to know: die Nadel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C499-HANDARBEIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Nadel** — "needle".

> Die Nadel ist so klein, dass meine Oma eine Brille braucht.
>
> *The needle is so small that my grandma needs glasses.*

Plural: *die Nadeln*. Also a pin, or the needle of a pine tree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Nadel* is inherited from Middle High German *nādele* and Old High German *nādala*, from Proto-Germanic *nēþlō* "needle" (EtymDB); English *needle* is a cousin.
