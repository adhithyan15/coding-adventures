## You'll want to know: તાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C474-CAVVU474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**તાળવું** — *tāḷvũ* — "the palate".

> ગરમ ચાથી મારું તાળવું બળી ગયું.
>
> *garam cāthī mārũ tāḷvũ baḷī gayũ.*
>
> The hot tea burned the roof of my mouth.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તાળવું** (*tāḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tālu*, "palate"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "palate, crown of head" (CDIAL 5803).
