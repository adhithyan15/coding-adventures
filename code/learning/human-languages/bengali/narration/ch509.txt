Bengali, chapter 509: Rugs and Quilts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গালিচা (gālichā) — a rug; a carpet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a door frame, then the Bengali for to hang up.

You'll want to know: গালিচা.
গালিচা — gālichā — "a rug; a carpet".
বসার ঘরে একটা লাল গালিচা আছে। Bôsār ghôre ekṭā lāl gālichā āchhe. There's a red rug in the sitting room.

The word, taken apart.
গালিচা (gālichā) is usually traced to Persian ghālīcha, "a small carpet", itself thought to come from Turkish.

Lesson 5 of 5.
বিছানো (bichhāno) — to spread out (a mat or a sheet).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a doormat, then the Bengali for a cushion.

You'll want to know: বিছানো.
বিছানো — bichhāno — "to spread out (a mat or a sheet)".
আমরা মেঝেতে মাদুর বিছিয়ে খেলাম। Āmrā mejhete mādur bichhiye khelām. We spread a mat on the floor and ate.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বিছানায় চাদর বিছালেন, tini bichhānāy chādôr bichhālen.

The word, taken apart.
বিছানো (bichhāno) is a tadbhava from a reconstructed vicchādayati; CDIAL lists Bengali bichāna, "to spread, to strew" (CDIAL 11692). বিছানা, a bed, comes from the same family.
