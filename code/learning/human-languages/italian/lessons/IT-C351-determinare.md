## You'll want to know: determinare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C351-ORIGINE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**determinare** — "to determine".

> Il tempo determina il nostro programma per domenica.
>
> *The weather determines our plan for Sunday.*

*Determinato* also means determined, resolute: *una ragazza determinata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*determinare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēterminō* "to limit, to set a bound" (EtymDB), built on *terminus* "a boundary stone".
