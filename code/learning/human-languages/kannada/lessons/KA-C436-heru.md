## You'll want to know: ಹೇರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C436-HAMALI436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೇರು** — *hēru* — "to load; to impose".

> ಅವರು ವಾಹನದ ಮೇಲೆ ಸಾಮಾನು ಹೇರಿದರು.
>
> *avaru vāhanada mēle sāmānu hēridaru.*
>
> They loaded the luggage on top of the vehicle.

Past (I …): ಹೇರಿದೆ, *hēride*.

What is loaded takes -ಅನ್ನು or no ending; figuratively, ತೆರಿಗೆ ಹೇರು, to impose a tax.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೇರು** (*hēru*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4446 lists Kannada *pēṟu*, "to lift up and put on, to load; a load", the older form with the *p-* that modern Kannada turns into *h-*. The same article holds Telugu *pērcu*, to pile up.
