## You'll want to know: el carril
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C648-APARCAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el carril** — "the lane; the rail".

> El carril de la derecha es para autobuses.
>
> *The right-hand lane is for buses.*

*El carril bici* — the cycle lane.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carril* is usually traced to *carro* "cart", from Latin *carrus*; it first named the rut that a cart wheel leaves on a road.
