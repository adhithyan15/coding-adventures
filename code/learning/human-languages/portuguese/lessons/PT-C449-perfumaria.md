## You'll want to know: a perfumaria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C449-LOJAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a perfumaria** — "the perfume shop".

> Ela trabalha numa perfumaria do centro comercial.
>
> *She works in a perfume shop in the shopping centre.*

Built on *o perfume*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perfumaria* is built on *perfume*, which probably came through Italian or French from Latin *per* 'through' and *fūmus* 'smoke' (Lewis & Short): first a scent rising in smoke.
