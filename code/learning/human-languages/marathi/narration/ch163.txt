Marathi, chapter 163: To cool, To empty, To weigh, To check, To sign.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
थंड करणे (thaṇḍ karṇe) — to cool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to call over, then the Marathi for to thank.

You'll want to know: थंड करणे.
थंड करणे — thaṇḍ karṇe — "to cool".

The word, taken apart.
थंड CDIAL lists under Sanskrit stabdha, "stiff; solidified", through a form it says was influenced by another word (CDIAL 13676). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 3 of 5.
वजन करणे (vajan karṇe) — to weigh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to cool, then the Marathi for to empty.

You'll want to know: वजन करणे.
वजन करणे — vajan karṇe — "to weigh".

The word, taken apart.
वजन is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic wazn, "weight", whose root is w-z-n (Lane). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सही करणे (sahī karṇe) — to sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to weigh, then the Marathi for to check.

You'll want to know: सही करणे.
सही करणे — sahī karṇe — "to sign".

The word, taken apart.
सही is a loan from Arabic ṣaḥīḥ, "sound, correct", which reached Marathi through Persian; its root is ṣ-ḥ-ḥ (Lane). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
