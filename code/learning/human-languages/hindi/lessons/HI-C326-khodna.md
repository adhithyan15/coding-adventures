## You'll want to know: खोदना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C326-KHANDAHAR326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खोदना** — *khodnā* — "to dig".

> मज़दूरों ने खंडहर के पास मिट्टी खोदी।
>
> *mazdūroṁ ne khaṇḍhar ke pās miṭṭī khodī.*
>
> The labourers dug up the earth near the ruins.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कुआँ खोदा, *usne kuā̃ khodā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खोदना** (*khodnā*) is inherited from *\*khōdd*, a form CDIAL reconstructs for digging (CDIAL 3934). What is dug agrees: **मिट्टी खोदी**, **कुआँ खोदा**.
