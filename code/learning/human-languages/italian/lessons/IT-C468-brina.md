## You'll want to know: la brina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C468-GELO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la brina** — "the white frost, the hoarfrost".

> La mattina i campi sono bianchi di brina.
>
> *In the morning the fields are white with frost.*

Much thinner than snow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brina* is inherited from Latin *pruīna* 'hoarfrost' (EtymDB).
