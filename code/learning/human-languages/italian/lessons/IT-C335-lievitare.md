## You'll want to know: lievitare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C335-IMPASTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lievitare** — "to rise (of dough)".

> Lascia lievitare l'impasto per due ore.
>
> *Let the dough rise for two hours.*

With *essere*: *è lievitato*. Prices *lievitano* too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lievitare* is built on *lievito* "yeast" (EtymDB), from Latin *levāre* "to raise".
