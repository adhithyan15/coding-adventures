## You'll want to know: ఆదేశించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C365-DROHAM365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆదేశించు** — *ādēśiñcu* — "to order; to command".

> రాజు ద్రోహిని పట్టుకోమని ఆదేశించాడు.
>
> *rāju drōhini paṭṭukōmani ādēśiñcāḍu.*
>
> The king ordered the traitor to be caught.

Past (I …): ఆదేశించాను, *ādēśiñcānu*.

Its noun is ఆదేశం, an order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆదేశించు** (*ādēśiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ādeśa*, "advice, instruction" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
