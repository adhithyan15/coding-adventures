## You'll want to know: ಆರ್ಥಿಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C401-ABHIVRIDDHI401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರ್ಥಿಕ** — *ārthika* — "economic; financial".

> ಆ ಕುಟುಂಬಕ್ಕೆ ಆರ್ಥಿಕ ತೊಂದರೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ā kuṭumbakke ārthika tondare ide.*
>
> That family has money troubles.

ಆರ್ಥಿಕತೆ, the economy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರ್ಥಿಕ** (*ārthika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ārthika* (Monier-Williams), an adjective from *artha*, "aim, wealth".
