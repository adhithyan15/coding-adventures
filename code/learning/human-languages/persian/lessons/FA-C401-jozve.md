## You'll want to know: جزوه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C401-MORUR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جزوه** (*jozve*) — "lecture notes, a handout".

> جزوه استاد را از دوستم گرفتم.
>
> *jozve-ye ostâd râ az dustam gereftam.*
>
> I got the professor's lecture notes from my friend.

Plural *jozvehâ*. Students lend and copy each other's *jozve* before exams.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جزوه** is usually traced to Arabic *juz'* "a part, a section": a booklet that is one part of a course.
