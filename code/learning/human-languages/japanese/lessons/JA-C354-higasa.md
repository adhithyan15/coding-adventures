## You'll want to know: ひがさ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C354-TENKI354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ひがさ** — *higasa* — "a parasol".

> **はれたら、ひがさを もって ください。** — *haretara, higasa o motte kudasai* — "If it is sunny, please take a parasol."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひがさ** (*higasa*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *hi*, "sun" and *gasa*, "umbrella (kasa)", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
