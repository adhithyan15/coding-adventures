## You'll want to know: जिद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C269-BAHANO269-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जिद** — *jid* — "stubbornness".

> जिद मत करो।
>
> *jid mat karo.*
>
> Don't be stubborn.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिद** (*jid*) is usually traced, through Persian *zidd*, to Arabic *ḍidd*, "the opposite, opposition".
