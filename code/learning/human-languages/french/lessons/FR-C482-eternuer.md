## You'll want to know: éternuer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C482-ALLERGIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**éternuer** — "to sneeze".

> Au printemps, j'éternue tout le temps.
>
> *In spring I sneeze all the time.*

*À vos souhaits !* — bless you! (said after a sneeze).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*éternuer* is inherited, through Old French *esternuer*, from Latin *sternūtāre* "to sneeze" (EtymDB), built on *sternuere* (Lewis & Short).
