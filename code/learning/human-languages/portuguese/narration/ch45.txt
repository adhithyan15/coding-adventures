Lesson 1 of 5.
a fruta — fruit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for salt, then the Portuguese for chicken.

You'll want to know: a fruta.
a fruta — "fruit".

The word, taken apart.
fruta from Late Latin fructa, "fruits", from Latin fructus, "enjoyment, produce" — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 has no usable row for fruta; it derives Spanish fruta from Late Latin fructa.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for chicken, then the Portuguese for fruit.

You'll want to know: a maçã.
a maçã — "an apple".

The word, taken apart.
maçã from Latin malum Matianum, "Matian apple" — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 traces it only to Old Portuguese maçãa; it derives Spanish manzana from malum Matianum. The apple was named after a Roman called Matius.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a banana — a banana.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for fruit, then the Portuguese for an apple.

You'll want to know: a banana.
a banana — "a banana".

The word, taken apart.
banana from a West African language, probably Wolof banaana (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. Portuguese traders passed the word on to English and the rest of Europe.
