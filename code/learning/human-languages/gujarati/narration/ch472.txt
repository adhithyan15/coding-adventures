Gujarati, chapter 472: Inside the Body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નસ (nas) — a vein; a nerve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wrinkles, then the Gujarati for grey hairs.

You'll want to know: નસ.
નસ — nas — "a vein; a nerve".
દાક્તરે મારા હાથની નસ તપાસી. dāktare mārā hāthnī nas tapāsī. The doctor examined the vein in my arm.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નસ (nas) is inherited from Sanskrit snasā, "tendon, muscle"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "vein, nerve, tendon" (CDIAL 13784).

કસરતથી સ્નાયુ મજબૂત બને છે. kasratthī snāyu majbūt bane che. Exercise makes the muscles strong.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સ્નાયુ (snāyu) is a tatsama, Sanskrit snāyu, which Monier-Williams gives as "any sinew or ligament", "tendon, muscle, nerve"; modern Gujarati uses it for a muscle.

ખોપરી મગજને બચાવે છે. khoprī magajne bacāve che. The skull protects the brain.

ખોપરી (khoprī) goes back to a reconstructed khōppa-, "coconut shell"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "skull" (CDIAL 3936): the shell of the head.
