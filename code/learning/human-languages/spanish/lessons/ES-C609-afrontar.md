## You'll want to know: afrontar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C609-PROGRESO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**afrontar** — "to face, to confront".

> Tenemos que afrontar el problema.
>
> *We have to face the problem.*

*Afrontar la realidad* — to face reality.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afrontar* is inherited from spoken Latin *affrontare* 'to meet face to face', built on *frons, frontis* 'forehead, front' (EtymDB).
