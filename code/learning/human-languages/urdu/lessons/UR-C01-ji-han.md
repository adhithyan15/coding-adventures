# جی ہاں — yes, respectfully

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-SHIN-RECOGNITION-01, UR-SCRIPT-KAF-RECOGNITION-01, UR-SCRIPT-RE-RECOGNITION-01, UR-SCRIPT-YE-RECOGNITION-01, UR-SCRIPT-HE-RECOGNITION-01, UR-LEX-SHUKRIYA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **شکریہ** once. Point to its first three shapes, then its final
two. Your pen can rest; this lesson is for a spoken respectful answer.

## The two words
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-JI-HAN, UR-SCRIPT-NUN-GHUNNA-01]; assesses=[] -->

The phrase is written right to left, so **جی** is the word on the right:

> **جی** *jī* + **ہاں** *hā̃* → **jī hā̃** → yes

In **جی**, **ج** gives *j* and **ی** stretches the vowel to *ī*. In **ہاں**,
the final sign **ں** marks a nasal vowel: let some air pass through your nose
as you say *ā*. It is not a full English *n* at the end.

## Grammar Lens: respect inside “yes”
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-REGISTER-JI-RESPECT]; assesses=[] -->

**ہاں** alone means “yes.” **جی** adds respect and attentive warmth; it can also
be used by itself as a polite response. Learning **jī hā̃** first gives you a
safe answer for many situations.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-JI-HAN, UR-REGISTER-JI-RESPECT, UR-SCRIPT-NUN-GHUNNA-01, UR-LEX-SHUKRIYA] -->

- “Do you understand?” — **جی ہاں۔** — *jī hā̃.*
- “Would you like to continue?” — **جی ہاں، شکریہ۔** — Yes, thank you.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-JI-HAN, UR-REGISTER-JI-RESPECT, UR-SCRIPT-NUN-GHUNNA-01] -->

Which mark makes the vowel nasal? Final **ں**. Answer politely: **jī hā̃**.
