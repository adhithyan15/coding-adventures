# ખીચડી (khīcḍī) — khichdi, rice and lentils

## You'll want to know: ખીચડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C169-DISH169-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ખીચડી** — *khīcḍī* — "khichdi, rice and lentils".

> **રાત્રે અમે ખીચડી જમીએ છીએ.** — *rātre ame khīcḍī jamīe chīe* — "At night we eat khichdi."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખીચડી** (*khīcḍī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *khiccā*, "a dish of rice and peas", extended with *-ḍ-*, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "rice and lentils" (CDIAL 3880).
