## You'll want to know: ಏರ್ಪೋರ್ಟ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C437-VIMANA437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏರ್ಪೋರ್ಟ್** — *ērpōrṭ* — "an airport".

> ಏರ್ಪೋರ್ಟ್ ನಗರದಿಂದ ತುಂಬಾ ದೂರ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ērpōrṭ nagaradinda tumbā dūra ide.*
>
> The airport is a long way from the city.

The Kannada name is ವಿಮಾನ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ, an aeroplane station; in speech the English loan is commoner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏರ್ಪೋರ್ಟ್** (*ērpōrṭ*) is a loan from English *airport*.
