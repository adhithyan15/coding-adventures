## You'll want to know: متعدی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C474-SPREAD474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**متعدی** — *mutāddī* — "contagious".

> زکام ایک متعدی بیماری ہے۔
>
> *zukām ek mutāddī bīmārī hai.*
>
> A cold is a contagious illness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متعدی** (*mutāddī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mutaʿaddī*, "passing on to others".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
