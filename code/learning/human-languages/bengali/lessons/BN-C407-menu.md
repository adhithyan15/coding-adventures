## You'll want to know: মেনু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C407-BOKSHISH407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মেনু** — *menu* — "a menu".

> ওয়েটার আমাদের মেনু দিলেন।
>
> *oyeṭār āmāder menu dilen.*
>
> The waiter gave us the menu.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেনু** (*menu*) is English *menu*, itself from French.
