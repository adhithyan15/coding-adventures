## You'll want to know: gùshi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C250-HOBBY250-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gùshi* — "story".

> *zhège gùshi hěn yǒu yìsi* — This story is really interesting.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gùshi* is built from *gù* 'old' and 事 'an affair', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuH* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'old'. In the word the second loses its tone.
