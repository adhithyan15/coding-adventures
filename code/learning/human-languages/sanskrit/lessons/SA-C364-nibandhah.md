## You'll want to know: निबन्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C364-VISHESHAJNA364-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निबन्धः** — *nibandhaḥ* — "an essay; a composition".

> छात्रः धर्मस्य विषये निबन्धं लिखति।
>
> *chātraḥ dharmasya viṣaye nibandhaṃ likhati.*
>
> The student writes an essay about dharma.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निबन्धः** (*nibandhaḥ*) is built on *ni*, "down", and the root √bandh, "to bind"; MW gives *ni-bandha* "binding" and "any literary composition or work", and "an essay" is the sense modern Sanskrit gives it (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
