# ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ — put the quality before the noun

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-01, PA-GRAMMAR-EXISTENTIAL-LOCATION-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-03, PA-LEX-CHANGA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] First retrieve the last chapter's location frame once: **ਕਿਤਾਬ ਘਰ
ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ**. Then retrieve **ਚੰਗਾ** (*changā*, good) and **ਮੁੰਡਾ** (*muṇḍā*,
boy). Name **ਮੁੰਡਾ** as masculine. Keep the last two words separate for one
breath.

## Grammar Lens: one attributive position
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-PRENOMINAL-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-03, PA-LEX-CHANGA] -->

Join them in this order:

> **ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ** — *changā muṇḍā* — **good boy**

In this noun phrase, **ਚੰਗਾ** describes **ਮੁੰਡਾ** and sits immediately before
it: adjective first, noun second. This lesson owns that attributive pattern. It
does not claim that an adjective can never appear after a noun in another kind
of Punjabi sentence.
