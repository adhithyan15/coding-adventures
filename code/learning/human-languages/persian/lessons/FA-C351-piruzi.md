## You'll want to know: پیروزی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C351-SEPAH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پیروزی** (*piruzi*) — "a victory".

> مردم پیروزی را جشن گرفتند.
>
> *Mardom piruzi râ jashn gereftand.*
>
> The people celebrated the victory.

From *piruz* "victorious". Also a win in sport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piruzi* is inherited from Middle Persian *pērōzīh*, "victory" (EtymDB).
