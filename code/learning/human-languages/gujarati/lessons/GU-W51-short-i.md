# ઇ — the standing vowel i

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C281-ICCHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->

- [YOU RECALL: say *a wish* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say **ઈ**, as in **ઈજા**, and the sign **િ** that gives a consonant a short *i*]

**ઇચ્છા** opens on a short *i* with no consonant to carry it.

## You'll want to know: ઈ without its curl
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C227-INJURY227-01, GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->

| | after a consonant | standing alone |
|---|---|---|
| short *i* | **િ** | ? |
| long *ī* | **ી** | **ઈ** |

The missing letter is **ઇ**. It has the same small upper-left loop, narrow middle crossing and broad lower loop as **ઈ**. Its hook rises on the upper right and stops there; **ઈ** carries it on, curling over the top.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-I-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ઇ** — *i*

Keep **ઇ** visible. Follow the numbered strip:

1. begin in the upper-left and circle the small loop down to the middle crossing
2. continue through the crossing and sweep clockwise around the broad lower loop
3. rise along the right side and finish in the upper-right hook without lifting

One stroke, no lift: a verified one-stroke teaching-app variant fitted to the bundled printed outline (t30apps.com, ‘Gujarati Alphabet Writing Practice’, version 1.0, ઇ animation, first SVG path).

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-I-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઇ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice where the hook stops.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-I-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઇ** once. Compare only two things: are both loops there, and does the hook stop on the upper right? Repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-I-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id": "GU-W51-short-i-recall", "kind": "text", "assesses": ["GU-SCRIPT-C281-I-01"], "prompt": "Hide the model and write the standing vowel short i from memory.", "answer": "ઇ", "accepted": [], "feedback": {"correct": "ઇ — two loops, then the hook that stops on the right.", "incorrect": "Look once at ઇ, hide it, and repair only the landmark that differs."}, "response_seconds": 20} -->

Hide the model and write the standing vowel *i* once. Uncover **ઇ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C281-I-01, GU-LEX-C281-ICCHA-01] -->
