## You'll want to know: ટાવર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C415-LANDMARK415-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાવર** — *ṭāvar* — "a clock tower; a tower".

> શહેરના ટાવરની ઘડિયાળ બંધ છે.
>
> *śahernā ṭāvarnī ghaḍiyāḷ bandh che.*
>
> The clock on the town's clock tower has stopped.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાવર** (*ṭāvar*) is borrowed from English *tower*; in many Gujarati towns the ટાવર is the clock tower in the main square.
