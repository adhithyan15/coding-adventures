## You'll want to know: ಮುರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C360-BETE360-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುರಿ** — *muri* — "to snap; to break".

> ಆನೆ ಮರದ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಕೊಂಬೆಯನ್ನು ಮುರಿಯಿತು.
>
> *āne marada doḍḍa kombeyannu muriyitu.*
>
> The elephant broke a big branch of the tree.

Past (I …): ಮುರಿದೆ, *muride*.

Sticks, bones and promises break: ಮಾತು ಮುರಿ, to break one's word. ಮುರಿತ is a fracture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುರಿ** (*muri*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5008 lists Kannada *muṟi*, "to break, to break off, to break down".
