## You'll want to know: अग्रलेख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C311-LEKH311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्रलेख** — *agralekh* — "an editorial".

> आजचा अग्रलेख पाण्याच्या प्रश्नावर आहे.
>
> *ājcā agralekh pāṇyācyā praśnāvar āhe.*
>
> Today's editorial is about the water problem.

Masculine.

अग्र-, front, + लेख: the leading article.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्रलेख** (*agralekh*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *agra*, 'front, foremost', and *lekha*, 'a writing' (Monier-Williams).
