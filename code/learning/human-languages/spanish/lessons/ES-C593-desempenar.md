## You'll want to know: desempeñar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C593-TAREA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**desempeñar** — "to carry out, to perform; to hold (a post)".

> Desempeña un papel importante en la empresa.
>
> *She plays an important role in the company.*

*Desempeñar un cargo* — to hold a post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desempeñar* is built on *empeñar* 'to pawn' with *des-*. *Empeñar* goes with *empeño* 'a pledge', which EtymDB traces to Latin *in pignus* 'in pledge'. To redeem a pledge became to carry out a duty.
