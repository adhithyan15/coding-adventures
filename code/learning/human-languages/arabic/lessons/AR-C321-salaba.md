# سلب (salaba) — to rob, to snatch away

## You'll want to know: سلب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C321-SARIQ321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سلب** — *salaba* — "to rob, to snatch away".

> سلب رجلان المسافر في الطريق.
>
> *salaba rajulān al-musāfir fī aṭ-ṭarīq.*
>
> Two men robbed the traveller on the road.

Present: يسلب, *yaslub*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلب** (*salaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-l-b* (Lane), "to strip a man of his clothes or goods"; *salb* is taking away, and so *salbī*, negative.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
