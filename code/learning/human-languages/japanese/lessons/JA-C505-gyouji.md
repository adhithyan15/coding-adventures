## You'll want to know: ぎょうじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C505-SUMO505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょうじ** — *gyōji* — "a sumo referee".

> **ぎょうじは どひょうの なかに います。** — *gyōji wa dohyō no naka ni imasu* — "The referee stands in the sumo ring."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょうじ** (*gyōji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gyō*, "going" (Middle Chinese *haeng*) and *ji*, "director" (*si*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
