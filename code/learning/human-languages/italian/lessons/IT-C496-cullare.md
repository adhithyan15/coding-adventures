## You'll want to know: cullare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C496-NEONATO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**cullare** — "to rock (a baby)".

> La mamma culla il bambino finché si addormenta.
>
> *The mother rocks the baby until he falls asleep.*

Built on *la culla* "cradle".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cullare* is built on *culla* "cradle", inherited from Vulgar Latin *cunla* (EtymDB), a shortened little form of *cūnae* "cradle" (Lewis & Short has *cūnulae* "a little cradle").
