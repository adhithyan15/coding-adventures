## You'll want to know: jiù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C62-FAST62-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jiù* — "old (of things); no longer new".

> *wǒ de chē hěn jiù* — My car is old.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiù* is inherited from Middle Chinese *gjuwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'former, of old'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
