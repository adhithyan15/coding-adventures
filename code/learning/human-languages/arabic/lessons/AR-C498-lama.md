## You'll want to know: لام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C498-ATHAR498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لام** — *lāma* — "to blame".

> لامني المدير على التأخير.
>
> *lāmanī al-mudīr ʿalā at-taʾkhīr.*
>
> The manager blamed me for the delay.

Present: يلوم, *yalūm*.

It takes على for the fault.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لام** (*lāma*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-w-m*, "to blame, to censure" (Lane). Its middle root letter w shows as alif in the past and as ū in the present.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
