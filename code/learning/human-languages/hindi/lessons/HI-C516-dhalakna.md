## You'll want to know: ढलकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C516-RISNA516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ढलकना** — *ḍhalaknā* — "to trickle down; to roll off".

> छत से पानी ढलककर आँगन में गिरा।
>
> *Chat se pānī ḍhalakkar āṁgan meṁ girā.*
>
> Water ran off the roof and fell into the courtyard.

Past (it …): वह पानी ढलक गया, *vah pānī ḍhalak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढलकना** (*ḍhalaknā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*ḍhalati*, "bends over, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5581). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to lean over, be spilt".
