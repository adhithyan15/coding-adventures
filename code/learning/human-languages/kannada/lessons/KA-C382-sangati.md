## You'll want to know: ಸಂಗತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C382-VASTAVA382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಗತಿ** — *saṅgati* — "a fact; a matter".

> ಈ ಸಂಗತಿ ಯಾರಿಗೂ ಗೊತ್ತಿರಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ī saṅgati yārigū gottiralilla.*
>
> Nobody knew this fact.

ವಿಷಯ is a topic; ಸಂಗತಿ is something that is so.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಗತಿ** (*saṅgati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃgati*, "a coming together, a meeting" (Monier-Williams); Kannada uses it for a matter or a fact.
