## You'll want to know: げんざい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C527-TIME527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**げんざい** — *genzai* — "the present; now".

> **げんざいの じかんは くにに よります。** — *genzai no jikan wa kuni ni yorimasu* — "The time right now depends on the country."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**げんざい** (*genzai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gen*, "present" (Middle Chinese *henH*) and *zai*, "exist" (*dzojX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
