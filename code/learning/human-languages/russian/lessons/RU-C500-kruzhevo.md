## You'll want to know: кружево
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C500-KNITTING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**кружево** (*krúzhevo*) — "lace".

> На ней было белое платье с кружевами.
>
> *Na ney býlo béloye plátye s kruzhevámi.*
>
> She was wearing a white dress with lace.

Neuter; usually plural *кружева*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *кружево* without a source; it is usually traced to the root of *круг* 'circle', after the round patterns of lace.
