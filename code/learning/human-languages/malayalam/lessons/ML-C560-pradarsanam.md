# പ്രദർശനം (pradarśanaṁ) — an exhibition; a show

## You'll want to know: പ്രദർശനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C560-SILPAM560-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രദർശനം** — *pradarśanaṁ* — "an exhibition; a show".

> നഗരത്തിൽ ഒരു പുസ്തക പ്രദർശനം നടക്കുന്നു.
>
> *nagarattil oru pustaka pradarśanaṁ naṭakkunnu.*
>
> A book exhibition is on in the city.

Also a screening of a film: സിനിമയുടെ പ്രദർശനം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രദർശനം** (*pradarśanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pradarśana*, "appearance, showing, pointing out" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
