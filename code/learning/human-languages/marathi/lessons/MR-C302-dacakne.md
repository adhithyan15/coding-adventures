## You'll want to know: दचकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C302-KSHAN302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दचकणे** — *dacakṇe* — "to be startled".

> दार अचानक वाजलं आणि मी दचकलो.
>
> *dār acānak vājlaṁ āṇi mī dacaklo.*
>
> The door suddenly banged and I started.

Past (he …): तो दचकला, *to dacaklā*.

Intransitive: आवाजाने दचकणे, to start at a sound; a woman says मी दचकले. The letter ऋ opens ऋतू, a season.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दचकणे** (*dacakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *dacakṇe*, beside *dackā*, 'fear', under *\*dacc*, 'fear' (CDIAL 6126).
