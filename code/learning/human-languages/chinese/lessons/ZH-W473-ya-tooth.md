## Script — four strokes, each one different
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YA-TOOTH-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 牙

**One piece of four strokes.** Count them as you go.

**The first two.** A short bar along the top, from left to right. Then under it on the left a short stroke down that turns and runs right, longer than the bar.

**The last two.** A vertical from the top bar down through the second stroke, hooking to the left at the foot. Then a long stroke falling to the left, from the middle down to the bottom left.

2 + 2 = four.
