Gujarati, chapter 112: Shoes, Belt, Handkerchief, Sweater, Coat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.

બૂટ (būṭ) — shoes.

Lesson 2 of 5.
પટ્ટો (paṭṭo) — a belt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for socks, then the Gujarati for shoes.
[pause 1 second]
Offer a choice: "tea or milk." (Chā athavā dūdh.)

You'll want to know: પટ્ટો.
પટ્ટો (paṭṭo) — "a belt".

The word, taken apart.
પટ્ટો (paṭṭo) goes back to Sanskrit paṭṭa, "cloth; bandage, fillet", which CDIAL thinks probably non-Aryan (CDIAL 7700). CDIAL lists the inherited Gujarati pāṭo, "bandage".
