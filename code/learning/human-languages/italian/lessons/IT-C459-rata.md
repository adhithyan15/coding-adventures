## You'll want to know: la rata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C459-BANCA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la rata** — "the instalment".

> Pago la macchina a rate.
>
> *I am paying for the car in instalments.*

*A rate* — in instalments.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rata* is inherited from Latin *rata* (EtymDB), short for *pars rata* 'a fixed share', from *rērī* 'to reckon'.
