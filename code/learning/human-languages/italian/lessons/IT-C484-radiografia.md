## You'll want to know: la radiografia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C484-GESSO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la radiografia** — "X-ray".

> Il medico mi ha fatto una radiografia al braccio.
>
> *The doctor X-rayed my arm.*

*Fare una radiografia* — to have an X-ray. In speech often *le lastre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*radiografia* is a modern learned word built from Latin *radius* "a ray" (Lewis & Short) and Greek *-graphía* "writing" (EtymDB).
