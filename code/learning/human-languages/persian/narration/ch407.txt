Persian, chapter 407: Crime and arrest.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
جرم (jorm) — a crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a verdict, then the Persian for a trial.

You'll want to know: جرم.
جرم (jorm) — "a crime".
دزدی یک جرم است. dozdi yek jorm ast. Theft is a crime.
Plural jarâyem, an Arabic broken plural. More serious than takhallof.

The word, taken apart.
جرم (jorm) is usually traced to Arabic jurm "offence, crime", from the same Arabic family as jarime "a fine".

Lesson 3 of 6.
دستگیر کردن (dastgir kardan) — to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a crime, then the Persian for a criminal.

You'll want to know: دستگیر کردن.
دستگیر کردن (dastgir kardan) — "to arrest".
پلیس دزد را در خیابان دستگیر کرد. polis dozd râ dar khiyâbân dastgir kard. The police arrested the thief in the street.
Dast "hand" + gir "taking" + kardan. Dastgir shodan — to be arrested.

The word, taken apart.
دستگیر کردن (dastgir kardan) is native Persian, built from dast "hand" + gir-, the present stem of gereftan "to take", + kardan "to do". To arrest is to take someone by the hand.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a criminal, then the Persian for to arrest.

You'll want to know: بی گناه.
بی گناه (bigonâh) — "innocent".
قاضی گفت که او بی گناه است. qâzi goft ke u bigonâh ast. The judge said that he was innocent.
Bi- "without" + gonâh "guilt". The opposite is gonâhkâr, guilty.

The word, taken apart.
بی گناه (bigonâh) is native Persian, built from bi- "without" + gonâh "sin, guilt", usually traced to Middle Persian wināh.

Lesson 5 of 6.
محکوم کردن (mahkum kardan) — to sentence; to condemn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to arrest, then the Persian for innocent.

You'll want to know: محکوم کردن.
محکوم کردن (mahkum kardan) — "to sentence; to condemn".
قاضی او را به یک سال زندان محکوم کرد. qâzi u râ be yek sâl zendân mahkum kard. The judge sentenced him to a year in prison.
Be chizi mahkum kardan — to sentence to something. Also to condemn in public.

The word, taken apart.
محکوم is usually traced to Arabic maḥkūm "judged, sentenced", from the same Arabic family as ḥukm "judgement"; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.
