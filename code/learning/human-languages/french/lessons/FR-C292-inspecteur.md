## You'll want to know: l'inspecteur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C292-INDICES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'inspecteur** — "the inspector, the police inspector".

> L'inspecteur a posé beaucoup de questions.
>
> *The inspector asked a lot of questions.*

Also at school or at work: *un inspecteur des impôts* — a tax inspector.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inspecteur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnspector* "examiner", from *īnspicere* "to look into" (Lewis & Short).
