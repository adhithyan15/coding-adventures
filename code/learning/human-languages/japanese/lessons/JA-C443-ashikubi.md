## You'll want to know: あしくび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C443-WRIST443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あしくび** — *ashikubi* — "an ankle".

> **はしって、あしくびが いたく なりました。** — *hashitte, ashikubi ga itaku narimashita* — "I ran, and my ankle started to hurt."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あしくび** (*ashikubi*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ashi*, "foot" and *kubi*, "neck" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
