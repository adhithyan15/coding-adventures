Russian, chapter 388: The law.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
закон (zakón) — a law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to expel, then the Russian for educated.

You'll want to know: закон.
закон (zakón) — "a law".
Новый закон начнёт действовать в январе. Nóvyy zakón nachnyót déystvovat v yanvaré. The new law will come into force in January.
Masculine. По закону — by law.

The word, taken apart.
закон (zakón) is inherited from Proto-Slavic zakonŭ (EtymDB), built from за- and кон, an old word for a limit or a starting line that is still heard in испокон веков "from time immemorial".

Lesson 4 of 5.
нарушать (narushát) — to break (a rule), to violate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for legal, then the Russian for illegal.

You'll want to know: нарушать.
нарушать (narushát) — "to break (a rule), to violate".
Он часто нарушает правила. On chásto narusháyet právila. He often breaks the rules.
Imperfective; perfective нарушить. Нарушать закон (zakón), правила, тишину.

The word, taken apart.
нарушать (narushát) is built from на- and рушить "to pull down, to wreck", an inherited Slavic verb: to break a rule is to knock part of it down.
