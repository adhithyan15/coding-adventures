# ઘટાડવું (ghaṭāḍvũ) — to bring down

## You'll want to know: ઘટાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C363-ANKDO363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘટાડવું** — *ghaṭāḍvũ* — "to bring down".

> સરકાર પેટ્રોલનો ભાવ ઘટાડશે.
>
> *sarkār peṭrolno bhāv ghaṭāḍśe.*
>
> The government will bring down the price of petrol.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ભાવ ઘટાડ્યો, *teṇe bhāv ghaṭāḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘટાડવું** (*ghaṭāḍvũ*) is the causative of **ઘટવું** (*ghaṭvũ*), "to go down", from a reconstructed *ghaṭṭati*, "decreases" (CDIAL 4415).

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
