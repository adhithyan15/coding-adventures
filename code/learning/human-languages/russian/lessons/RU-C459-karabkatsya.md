## You'll want to know: карабкаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C459-POKHOD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**карабкаться** (*karábkatsya*) — "to scramble (up), to clamber".

> Дети карабкаются по камням.
>
> *Déti karábkayutsya po kamnyám.*
>
> The children are scrambling over the rocks.

Imperfective; perfective *вскарабкаться*. Present *карабкаюсь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *карабкаться* is not settled; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
