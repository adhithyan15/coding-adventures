## You'll want to know: die Regelung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C385-AUSNAHME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Regelung** — "the rule (that applies), the arrangement".

> Für Kinder unter sechs gibt es eine besondere Regelung.
>
> *There is a special rule for children under six.*

Plural: *die Regelungen*. From *regeln* — to regulate, to sort out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Regelung* is the noun of *regeln*, built on *Regel* "rule", from Old High German *regula* and Latin *rēgula* "a straight piece of wood, a ruler, a rule" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
