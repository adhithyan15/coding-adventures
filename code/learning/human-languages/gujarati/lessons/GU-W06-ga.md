# ગ — the consonant ga

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BA-01] -->

Write **બ** once from memory. Put it away after one comparison. Today adds one
new sound and one new shape: *ga*.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-GA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ગ** visible. Circle the rounded left body to the lower left. Lift once,
descend the separate right spine, and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-GA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ગ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice rounded body, separate
right spine, lower foot; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-GA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ગ** once. Compare only the body and spine; repair one landmark
if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-GA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W06-ga-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-GA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write ga from memory.","answer":"ગ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ગ — the rounded body and separate spine are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ગ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *ga* once. Uncover **ગ**, compare, and stop after one
repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-GA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the sound, the two main landmarks, and the pen-lift count.
