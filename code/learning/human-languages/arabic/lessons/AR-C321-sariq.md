## You'll want to know: سارق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C321-SARIQ321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سارق** — *sāriq* — "a robber".

> ركض السارق بين السيارات.
>
> *rakaḍa as-sāriq bayna as-sayyārāt.*
>
> The robber ran between the cars.

Plural سارقون. لص is the everyday word; سارق is the one who stole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سارق** (*sāriq*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-q* (Lane), "to take a thing secretly". *Sāriq* is its active participle, "one who steals".
