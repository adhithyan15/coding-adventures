## You'll want to know: निवांत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C466-SAMADHAN466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निवांत** — *nivānt* — "relaxed; at leisure".

> सुट्टीच्या दिवशी आम्ही निवांत बसलो.
>
> *suṭṭīcyā divśī āmhī nivānt baslo.*
>
> On the holiday we sat around at leisure.

Unchanging; also an adverb: निवांत झोपणे, to sleep undisturbed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवांत** (*nivānt*) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled.
