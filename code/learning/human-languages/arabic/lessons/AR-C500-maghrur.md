## You'll want to know: مغرور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C500-GHURUR500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مغرور** — *maghrūr* — "conceited, vain".

> لا يحب الفريق اللاعب المغرور.
>
> *lā yuḥibbu al-farīq al-lāʿib al-maghrūr.*
>
> The team does not like the conceited player.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مغرور** (*maghrūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-r-r*, "to deceive, to beguile" (Lane). Maghrūr is the passive participle: one beguiled, here by his own worth.
