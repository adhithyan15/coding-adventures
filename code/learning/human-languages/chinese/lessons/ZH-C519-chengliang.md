## You'll want to know: chéngliáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C519-OUTDOORS519-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngliáng* — "to sit in the cool (out of the sun)".

> *xiàtiān de wǎnshang dàjiā dōu zài wàibian chéngliáng* — On summer evenings everyone sits outside in the cool.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngliáng* is built from *chéng* 'to ride on' and *liáng* 'cool', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zying* and *ljang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to drive' and 'to mount' and the second 'thin' and 'cold': to take the cool air.
