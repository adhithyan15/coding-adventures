## You'll want to know: સમયપત્રક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C204-TIMES204-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સમયપત્રક** — *samaypatrak* — "timetable".

> **સ્ટેશન પર નવું સમયપત્રક હતું.** — *sṭeśan par navũ samaypatrak hatũ* — "There was a new timetable at the station."

Built on **સમય**, *samay*, 'time'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમયપત્રક** (*samaypatrak*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *samaya*, "time", and *patraka*, "leaf, sheet", a form of *páttra*, "leaf" (CDIAL 7733).
