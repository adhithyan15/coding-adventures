## You'll want to know: il rospo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C465-PICCOLI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il rospo** — "the toad".

> Di sera i rospi cantano vicino allo stagno.
>
> *In the evening the toads croak near the pond.*

*Ingoiare il rospo* — to swallow something unpleasant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *rospo* is not settled.
