## You'll want to know: کپتان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C521-MAICH521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کپتان** — *kaptān* — "a captain (of a team)".

> کپتان نے کھلاڑیوں کو بلایا۔
>
> *kaptān ne khilāṛiyoṅ ko bulāyā.*
>
> The captain called the players over.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کپتان** (*kaptān*) is usually traced to Portuguese *capitão*, "a captain".
