## You'll want to know: かさねる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C159-TIDY159-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かさねる** — *kasaneru* — "to stack; to pile one on another".

> **はこを かさねます。** — *hako o kasanemasu* — "I stack the boxes."

Polite **かさねます**, *kasanemasu*; an ichidan verb. Close to **つむ**, *tsumu*, "to pile up".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かさねる** (*kasaneru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
