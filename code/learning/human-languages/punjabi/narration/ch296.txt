Punjabi, chapter 296: Slippery Paths.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਖਿਸਕਣਾ (khisakṇā) — to slide; to slip away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a ravine, then the Punjabi for to rot.

You'll want to know: ਖਿਸਕਣਾ.
ਖਿਸਕਣਾ — khisakṇā — "to slide; to slip away".
ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਹਾੜ ਤੋਂ ਪੱਥਰ ਖਿਸਕਦੇ ਹਨ। mīṁh vich pahāṛ ton patthar khisakde han. In the rain, stones slide down from the mountain.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਖਿਸਕਿਆ, oh patthar khiskiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਖਿਸਕਣਾ (khisakṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed khis, "slip"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khiskaṇā, "to slip away", under it (CDIAL 3888).

ਚਿੱਕੜ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਨਾਲ ਚਿਪਕਦਾ ਹੈ। chikkaṛ juttī nāl chipakdā hai. Mud sticks to the shoe.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to stick, then the Punjabi for thick.

You'll want to know: ਚਿਕਨਾ.
ਚਿਕਨਾ — chiknā — "slick; greasy".
ਮੀਂਹ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਚਿਕਨਾ ਹੈ। mīṁh ton bāad farsh chiknā hai. After the rain the floor is slick.
