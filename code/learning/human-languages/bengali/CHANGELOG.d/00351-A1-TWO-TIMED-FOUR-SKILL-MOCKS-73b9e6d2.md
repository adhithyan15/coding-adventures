## A1: two timed four-skill mocks

Added two distinct Bengali-script A1 papers, answer keys, and a shared rubric
for the checked-in A1 task shape. Each form has 20 reading and 17 listening
items, a seven-field practical form, a 30–40 word named-reader message, and
interview, prepared-description, and transactional speaking tasks. Examiner
scripts meet the three spoken-source duration bands at a target 100 words per
minute. Earlier skill sheets are collected before independent writing.

A level-owned test checks part counts, source lengths, key coverage, and the
administration guard. This is assessment infrastructure, not evidence that
the gentle writing runway or book-only human validation is complete.
