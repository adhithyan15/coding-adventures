## You'll want to know: zuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C44-PLACES44-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zuò* — "to sit; to travel by".

> *wǒmen zuò chē qù* — We're going by car.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuò* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as bending the joints. Its use for travelling by car or bus is not given there.
