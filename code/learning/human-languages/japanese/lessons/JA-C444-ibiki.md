## You'll want to know: いびき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C444-PULSE444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いびき** — *ibiki* — "snoring; a snore".

> **ちちの いびきは おおきいです。** — *chichi no ibiki wa ōkii desu* — "My father snores loudly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いびき** (*ibiki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
