Kannada, chapter 424: Checks and Reviews.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು (avalōkisu) — to observe; to review.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a trick, then the Kannada for to divide.

You'll want to know: ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು.
ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು — avalōkisu — "to observe; to review".
ವರ್ಷದ ಕೊನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ನನ್ನ ಖರ್ಚನ್ನು ಅವಲೋಕಿಸಿದೆ. varṣada koneyalli nānu nanna kharcannu avalōkiside. At the end of the year I reviewed my spending.
Past (I …): ಅವಲೋಕಿಸಿದೆ, avalōkiside.
ಅವಲೋಕನ, a review, an overview.

The word, taken apart.
ಅವಲೋಕಿಸು (avalōkisu) is built on Sanskrit avalokana, "seeing, viewing, observing" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

What you've built.

You'll want to know: ಸುತ್ತೋಲೆ.
ಸುತ್ತೋಲೆ — suttōle — "a circular".
ರಜೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಶಾಲೆ ಒಂದು ಸುತ್ತೋಲೆ ಕಳುಹಿಸಿತು. rajeya bagge śāle ondu suttōle kaḷuhisitu. The school sent a circular about the holiday.
ಸುತ್ತು (to go round) + ಓಲೆ (a letter).

The word, taken apart.
ಸುತ್ತೋಲೆ (suttōle) is native Dravidian, built from suttu, "to go round" (DEDR 2715), and ōle, "a palm leaf written on, a letter" (DEDR 1070).
