6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
логика (lógika) — logic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to condemn, then the Russian for to reproach.

You'll want to know: логика.
логика (lógika) — "logic".
В его словах нет логики. V yegó slovákh net lógiki. There is no logic in what he says.
Feminine. По логике — logically speaking.

The word, taken apart.
логика (lógika) is usually traced, through western languages, to Greek logikḗ "the art of reasoning", built on lógos "word, reason".

Lesson 3 of 6.
вывод (vývod) — a conclusion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for logic, then the Russian for logical.

You'll want to know: вывод.
вывод (vývod) — "a conclusion".
Какой вывод можно сделать? Kakóy vývod mózhno sdélat? What conclusion can we draw?
Masculine. Сделать вывод (vývod) — to draw a conclusion. It also means withdrawal: вывод (vývod) войск.

The word, taken apart.
вывод (vývod) is built from вы- "out" and вод-, the root of водить "to lead", from Proto-Slavic voditi "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). A conclusion is what you lead out of the facts.

Lesson 4 of 6.
рассуждать (rassuzhdát) — to reason, to think aloud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for logical, then the Russian for a conclusion.

You'll want to know: рассуждать.
рассуждать (rassuzhdát) — "to reason, to think aloud".
Он любит рассуждать о жизни. On lyúbit rassuzhdát o zhízni. He likes to reason about life.
Imperfective only. Рассуждать о + prepositional.

The word, taken apart.
рассуждать (rassuzhdát) is built from рас- "apart" and судить "to judge", on суд, inherited from Proto-Slavic sǫdŭ (EtymDB). To reason is to take a question apart and judge it.

Lesson 5 of 6.
очевидный (ochevídnyy) — obvious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a conclusion, then the Russian for to reason.

You'll want to know: очевидный.
очевидный (ochevídnyy) — "obvious".
Ответ был очевиден для всех. Otvét byl ochevíden dlya vsekh. The answer was obvious to everyone.
Short forms очевиден, очевидна. Очевидно — evidently, clearly.

The word, taken apart.
очевидный (ochevídnyy) is built from очи, the old plural of око "eye", and вид-, the root of видеть "to see", from Proto-Slavic viděti (EtymDB). What is obvious is seen with your own eyes.
