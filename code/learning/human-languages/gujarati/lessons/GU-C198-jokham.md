## You'll want to know: જોખમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C198-SIGNS198-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જોખમ** — *jokham* — "danger, risk".

> **આ રસ્તા પર જોખમ છે.** — *ā rastā par jokham che* — "This road is dangerous."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જોખમ** (*jokham*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "risk, venture, responsibility, loss", under Sanskrit *yōgakṣēmá*, "preservation of one's property; insurance charge" (CDIAL 10527).
