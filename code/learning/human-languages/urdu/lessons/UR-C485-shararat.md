## You'll want to know: شرارت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C485-CHILDHOOD485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شرارت** — *sharārat* — "mischief".

> بچے کلاس میں شرارت کر رہے تھے۔
>
> *bachche klās meṅ sharārat kar rahe the.*
>
> The children were up to mischief in class.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرارت** (*sharārat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *sharāra*, "wickedness"; in Urdu it is mostly a child's mischief.
