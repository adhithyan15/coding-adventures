## You'll want to know: दौर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C535-DAUR535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दौर** — *daur* — "a round, a phase".

> पहले दौर में हमारी टीम जीती।
>
> *Pahle daur meṁ hamārī ṭīm jītī.*
>
> Our team won in the first round.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दौर** (*daur*) is usually traced to Arabic *dawr*, "a turn, a round", through Persian.
