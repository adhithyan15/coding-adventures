## 0.2.0 - coverage-guided fuzz engine (VER06e)

### Added
- `fuzz` package: the VER06 mutator algebra (flips by parity, O(len) for any
  count; arithmetic and boundary widths 1/2/4/8, both endians; block delete,
  insert, duplicate; splice; `MutateSequence` with a growth cap), each with a
  `...Big` form for arbitrary-size integers.
- 65,536-edge `Bitmap`, `CorpusAdmit`, `CalculateEnergy` (half away from zero).
- Deterministic `Run` / `RunOutcomes` campaign meeting every VER06 section 9.1
  contract: exact iteration budget, input-length cap, empty corpus becomes `[0]`,
  crashing seeds never mutated, every execution accounted for, honest `IsClean`.
- Panic-recovering executor: crashes (recovered panics, kind = Go type, frames
  normalised to colon-free `package.Function`, deep recursion collapsed),
  deterministic timeouts (`Handle.Tick` step budget) and OOM (`Handle.Reserve`).
- Coverage by caller-reported edges (`Handle.Edge`, `EdgeID`); the tier label is
  `caller-reported` or `black-box-uninstrumented`, never more than was collected.
  `testing.Coverage` / `runtime/coverage` were investigated and rejected (VER06e 4.2).
- Signature dedup, exact-signature minimiser under a per-crash and a
  campaign-wide budget (`Minimized` flag), closed failure-id vocabulary,
  sanitised `FormatTAPFailure`, `TAPFailures`; `Report` / `CrashRecord` print
  no input bytes or seeds under any `fmt` verb or `encoding/json`.
- `fuzz.EvaluateCorpus`: production runner for the 25-case `fuzz-v1` corpus with
  negative controls, engine sabotage hooks, CWE-400 caps and clipped problems.
- 820 reference-oracle vectors (`fuzz/testdata/gen_reference_vectors.py`) for the
  corpus weaknesses of backlog PT-F1.
- `internal/corpusjson`: the strict corpus JSON reader, decoder, `Same`, `Canon`
  and clipping, extracted from `pbt` and shared by both runners.

### Changed
- `pbt` keeps its API; its corpus plumbing now delegates to `internal/corpusjson`.
- BUILD and BUILD_windows gate `./fuzz` and `./internal/...` at 95% per package.
