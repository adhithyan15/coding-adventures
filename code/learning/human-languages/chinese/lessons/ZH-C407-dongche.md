## You'll want to know: dòngchē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C407-PULLIN407-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dòngchē* — "D-class bullet train".

> *zuò dòngchē qù Běijīng yào duōjiǔ* — How long does the bullet train take to Beijing?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dòngchē* is built from *dòng* 'to move' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duwngX* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stir, to move'. It is a short form of a longer name, a train whose carriages move under their own power.
