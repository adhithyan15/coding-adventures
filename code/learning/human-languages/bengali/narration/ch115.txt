Bengali, chapter 115: Bamboo, Mango tree, Coconut, Pineapple, Papaya.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বাঁশ (bā̃sh) — bamboo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a seed, then the Bengali for a thorn.

You'll want to know: বাঁশ.
বাঁশ — bā̃sh — "bamboo".

The word, taken apart.
বাঁশ (bā̃sh) (bāṁsh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vaṁśa, "bamboo" (CDIAL 11175).

Lesson 3 of 5.
নারকেল (nārkel) — a coconut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for bamboo, then the Bengali for a mango tree.

You'll want to know: নারকেল.
নারকেল — nārkel — "a coconut".

The word, taken apart.
নারকেল (nārkel) goes back to Sanskrit nārikela, "coconut palm and fruit". CDIAL gives no Bengali form under that entry, but it lists Oriya naṛhera and naṛiā, "coconut", the forms nearest to Bengali (CDIAL 7075). CDIAL reports Bloch's view that nārikela is a Dravidian compound.
