## You'll want to know: карнавал
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C316-KARNAVAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**карнавал** (*karnavál*) — "a carnival".

> Летом в городе был карнавал.
>
> *Létom v górode byl karnavál.*
>
> In the summer there was a carnival in the town.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*карнавал* is usually traced through French *carnaval* to Italian *carnevale*; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
