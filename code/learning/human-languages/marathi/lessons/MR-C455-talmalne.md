## You'll want to know: तळमळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C455-DHASTI455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तळमळणे** — *taḷmaḷṇe* — "to toss and turn; to fret".

> काळजीमुळे तो रात्रभर तळमळला.
>
> *kāḷjīmuḷe to rātrabhar taḷmaḷlā.*
>
> He tossed and turned all night with worry.

Past (he …): तो तळमळला, *to taḷmaḷlā*.

Of a sleepless night or a restless longing; तळमळ, the restlessness itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तळमळणे** (*taḷmaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable, तळ-मळ; its origin is not settled.
