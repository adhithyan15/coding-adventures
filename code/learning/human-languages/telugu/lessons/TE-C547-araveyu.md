# ఆరవేయు (āravēyu) — to hang to dry; to put out to dry

## You'll want to know: ఆరవేయు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C547-INTIPANI547-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరవేయు** — *āravēyu* — "to hang to dry; to put out to dry".

> ఉతికిన బట్టలను ఎండలో ఆరవేశాను.
>
> *utikina baṭṭalanu eṇḍalō āravēśānu.*
>
> I hung the washed clothes out in the sun to dry.

Past (I …): ఆరవేశాను, *āravēśānu*.

ఆరు, to dry, + వేయు, to put.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరవేయు** (*āravēyu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 404 lists Telugu *āṟu*, "to cool; to dry, to become dry"; *vēyu*, "to put", makes it something you do to the clothes.
