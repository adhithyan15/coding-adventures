## You'll want to know: সংসার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C489-SONGSAR489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সংসার** — *sôngsār* — "a household; family life".

> বিয়ের পরে মীরা নতুন সংসার শুরু করল।
>
> *biyer pôre mīrā nôtun sôngsār shuru kôrlô.*
>
> After the wedding Mira set up a new household.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংসার** (*sôngsār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṃsāra*, "wandering through", the round of worldly life (Monier-Williams); Bengali brought it down to the household and everyday family life.
