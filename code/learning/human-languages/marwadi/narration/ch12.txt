Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 12: Maternal Grandparents and Husband: One New Sign.
8 lessons. You can do the first 5 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
Hear nānā — maternal grandfather.

Warm-up.
[pause 15 seconds]
Say the paternal-grandfather word, write परिवार, then recall the seven-word family map. Today one matching two-beat word changes only the family side.

You'll want to know.
nā-nā — maternal grandfather.
Listen twice. Say it once. Keep dādā and nānā tied to their meanings before writing enters.

Wrap-up Recall.
Say nānā once without seeing its spelling.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Which taught relation does nānā name?


Lesson 2 of 8.
नाना (nānā) — one known unit twice.

Warm-up.
[pause 12 seconds]
Say nānā, then write familiar न and ा.

You'll want to know.
ना + ना becomes नाना — nānā — maternal grandfather.

Guided Practice.
Look for five seconds, cover the word, and write it. Then label दादा and नाना (nānā) orally without ranking either side of the family.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From nānā, write the taught relation word.

You'll want to know.
nānī — maternal grandmother.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 4 of 8.

You'll want to know.
ना + नी becomes नानी — nānī — maternal grandmother.

Guided Practice.
Cover नानी (nānī), wait five seconds, write it, and circle only the final ी.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 5 of 8.

Say the two maternal-grandparent labels, write नानी (nānī), then write familiar र and भाई from sound cues.

You'll want to know.
pati — husband.
This is a relationship label, not a required household pattern. Say the two short beats. The spelling waits until its new consonant has its own lesson.

Lesson 6 of 8.
त — a quiet dental stop.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one sign until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 12 seconds]
Say pati. Write known ठ and थ once; today's unaspirated dental sound gets its own sign.

Script — one sign.
त — ta.

Guided Practice.
Trace once with the model visible, copy once, cover it, and write it once.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Unicode Devanagari chart.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Write the new Devanagari sign for dental ta.


Lesson 7 of 8.
पति (pati) — husband on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say husband and maternal grandfather. Write प, त (ta), ि, राम, and बहन; point to म inside राम.

You'll want to know.
प + ति becomes पति — pati — husband.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write पति (pati). Check that ि appears before त (ta) on the page but is read after it.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From pati, write the taught relationship word.


Lesson 8 of 8.
Checkpoint — three more relationship labels.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Say and write mother and father, write स, and recall the earlier four-label payoff before extending the map.

Guided Practice.
Hear and identify all three labels.
Produce each from a meaning cue.
Read नाना — नानी — पति (nānā — nānī — pati).
Cover the line and write all three.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
After all four skill checks, write maternal grandfather, maternal grandmother, and husband in order.
