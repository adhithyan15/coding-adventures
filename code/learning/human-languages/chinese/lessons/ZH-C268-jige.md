## You'll want to know: jígé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C268-EXAM268-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jígé* — "to pass an exam".

> *tā kǎoshì méi jígé* — He didn't pass the exam.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jígé* is built from *jí* 'to reach' and *gé* 'a standard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gip* and *kaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to reach' and the second 'a model' and 'a measure'.
