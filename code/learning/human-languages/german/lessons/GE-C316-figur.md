## You'll want to know: die Figur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C316-ERZAEHLER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Figur** — "the character, the figure".

> Die wichtigste Figur im Buch ist ein Junge.
>
> *The most important character in the book is a boy.*

Plural: *die Figuren*. Also a person's figure, or a chess piece.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Figur* was borrowed from Latin *figūra* "a shape, a form" (EtymDB), built on *fingere* "to form, to shape" (Lewis & Short).
