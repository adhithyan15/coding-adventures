## You'll want to know: સંશોધન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C362-SANSHODHAN362-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સંશોધન** — *sanśodhan* — "research".

> મીરા નવી દવા પર સંશોધન કરે છે.
>
> *Mīrā navī davā par sanśodhan kare che.*
>
> Mira is doing research on a new medicine.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંશોધન** (*sanśodhan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃśodhana*, "thoroughly cleansing" (Monier-Williams); "research", sifting a question clean, is its modern sense.
