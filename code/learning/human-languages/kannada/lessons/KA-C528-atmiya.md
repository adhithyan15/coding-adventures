# ಆತ್ಮೀಯ (ātmīya) — dear; close

## You'll want to know: ಆತ್ಮೀಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C528-SNEHA528-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆತ್ಮೀಯ** — *ātmīya* — "dear; close".

> ಅವನು ನನ್ನ ಆತ್ಮೀಯ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ.
>
> *avanu nanna ātmīya snēhita.*
>
> He is my close friend.

Letters to friends begin ಆತ್ಮೀಯ …, dear ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆತ್ಮೀಯ** (*ātmīya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ātmīya*, "one's own" (Monier-Williams), built on *ātman*, "self".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
