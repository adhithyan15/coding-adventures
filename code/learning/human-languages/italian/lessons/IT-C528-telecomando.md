## You'll want to know: il telecomando
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C528-SERIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il telecomando** — "remote control".

> Dov'è il telecomando? Non lo trovo.
>
> *Where is the remote? I cannot find it.*

*Tele-* "far" as in *televisione*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*telecomando* is built from Greek *tēle* "far" and *comando* "command", from Latin *mandāre* "to entrust" (Lewis & Short).
