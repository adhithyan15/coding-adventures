## You'll want to know: সফর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C387-POTH387-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সফর** — *sôphôr* — "a trip; a tour".

> আমাদের সফর সাত দিনের ছিল।
>
> *āmāder sôphôr sāt diner chhilô.*
>
> Our trip lasted seven days.

Often a trip with a purpose: an official tour, a team's tour abroad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সফর** (*sôphôr*) is usually traced to Arabic *safar*, "a journey", which reached Bengali through Persian.
