## You'll want to know: मन्त्रयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C355-SANSAD355-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्त्रयते** — *mantrayate* — "consults; deliberates".

> मीरा मित्रेण सह मन्त्रयते।
>
> *mīrā mitreṇa saha mantrayate.*
>
> Mira talks it over with her friend.

Past (he …): सः मित्रेण सह मन्त्रितवान्, *saḥ mitreṇa saha mantritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्त्रयते** (*mantrayate*) is built on *mantra*, "a sacred text, counsel"; MW gives *mantrita* "discussed, deliberated, determined" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
