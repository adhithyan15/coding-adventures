## You'll want to know: ज़रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C387-BEHAD387-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़रा** — *zarā* — "a little, a bit".

> ज़रा धीरे बोलो, वरना बच्चा जाग जाएगा।
>
> *zarā dhīre bolo, varnā baccā jāg jāegā.*
>
> Speak a little more quietly, or the baby will wake up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़रा** (*zarā*) is usually traced to Arabic *dharra*, "a speck, an atom", which reached Hindi through Persian. Persian says *zarra*; the Hindi sense is a little.
