## You'll want to know: استقلال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C329-THAWRA329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استقلال** — *istiqlāl* — "independence".

> يحتفل البلد بعيد الاستقلال كل سنة.
>
> *yaḥtafilu al-balad bi-ʿīd al-istiqlāl kull sana.*
>
> The country celebrates Independence Day every year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استقلال** (*istiqlāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-l-l* (Lane), "to lift". *Istiqlāl* is the verbal noun of Form X *istaqalla*, "to stand on one's own".
