## You'll want to know: ঘুরপথ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C402-MAJHKHAN402-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুরপথ** — *ghurpôth* — "a roundabout way; a detour".

> সেতু বন্ধ ছিল, তাই আমরা ঘুরপথে গেলাম।
>
> *setu bôndhô chhilô, tāi āmrā ghurpôthe gelām.*
>
> The bridge was closed, so we went the long way round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুরপথ** (*ghurpôth*) joins the stem ঘুর- of ঘোরা, "to go round" (CDIAL 4488), to **পথ**, "a way": the way that goes round.
