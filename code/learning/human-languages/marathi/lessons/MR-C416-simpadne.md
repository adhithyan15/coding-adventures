## You'll want to know: शिंपडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C416-HAMAL416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शिंपडणे** — *śimpaḍṇe* — "to sprinkle".

> इस्त्रीआधी तो कपड्यांवर पाणी शिंपडतो.
>
> *istrīādhī to kapaḍyāṁvar pāṇī śimpaḍto.*
>
> Before ironing he sprinkles water on the clothes.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने शिंपडलं, *tyāne śimpaḍlaṁ*.

The place sprinkled takes -वर: अंगणात पाणी शिंपडणे, to sprinkle water in the yard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिंपडणे** (*śimpaḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *śĩpaḍṇẽ*, "to sprinkle, scatter", under Sanskrit *sikta*, "sprinkled, wetted" (CDIAL 13388).
