## You'll want to know: seufzen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C469-ENTTAEUSCHUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**seufzen** — "to sigh".

> Sie sah auf die Uhr und seufzte.
>
> *She looked at the clock and sighed.*

Regular: *hat geseufzt*. The noun: *der Seufzer* — a sigh.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seufzen* comes from Middle High German *siufzen* (EtymDB); its older history is not settled.
