## You'll want to know: しんぱい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C137-SHINPAI]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-N-01, JA-SCRIPT-I-01] -->

**しんぱい** — *shinpai* — "worry": the feeling, or being worried about
someone. It is the word in *Don't worry*.

The new sign is spent at once. **し** and **ん**, then **ぱ** and **い**: four
beats, *shi–n–pa–i*. After **ん** the *p* comes out clearly, the lips closed
first. You write every sign in it now.
