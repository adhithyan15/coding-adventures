## You'll want to know: غش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C351-KHIDA351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غش** — *ghashsha* — "to cheat".

> غش الطالب في الامتحان.
>
> *ghashsha aṭ-ṭālib fī al-imtiḥān.*
>
> The student cheated in the exam.

Present: يغش, *yaghushsh*.

Cheating is غش.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غش** (*ghashsha*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-sh-sh* (Lane), "to be insincere towards someone", to show what one does not mean.
