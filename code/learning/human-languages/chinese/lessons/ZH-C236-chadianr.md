# chàdiǎnr — nearly; almost

## You'll want to know: chàdiǎnr
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C236-ODD236-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chàdiǎnr* — "nearly; almost".

> *wǒ chàdiǎnr chídào le* — I was nearly late.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chàdiǎnr* is built from *chà* 'short of' and 点儿 'a little', inherited from Middle Chinese *tsrhae*, *temX* and *nye* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to choose' and 'to go wrong'; the falling tone of chà is not the regular outcome of that form.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
