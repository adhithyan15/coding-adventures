Kannada, chapter 433: At the Railway Station.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕ (prayāṇika) — a passenger; a traveller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for chalk, then the Kannada for a ruler.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕ.
ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕ — prayāṇika — "a passenger; a traveller".
ರೈಲು ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕರು ಇದ್ದರು. railu nildāṇadalli tumbā prayāṇikaru iddaru. There were a lot of passengers at the railway station.
Plural: ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕರು, prayāṇikaru.
Built on ಪ್ರಯಾಣ, a journey, with -ಇಕ, one who belongs to it: the one who makes the journey.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕ (prayāṇika) is built from Sanskrit prayāṇa, "setting out, a journey" (CDIAL 8732), with the ending -ika, "one who belongs to". The same prayāṇa gives ಪ್ರಯಾಣ, a journey.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್ (plāṭphārm) — a railway platform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ruler, then the Kannada for a passenger.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್.
ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್ — plāṭphārm — "a railway platform".
ನಮ್ಮ ರೈಲು ಮೂರನೇ ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್ಗೆ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. namma railu mūranē plāṭphārmge baruttade. Our train comes in at the third platform.
ವೇದಿಕೆ is a stage or any raised platform; at a station people say ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್ (plāṭphārm).

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್ (plāṭphārm) is a loan from English platform, which came in with the railways. Its last consonant keeps the virama, as recent English loans do.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬೋಗಿ (bōgi) — a railway coach; a carriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a passenger, then the Kannada for a railway platform.

You'll want to know: ಬೋಗಿ.
ಬೋಗಿ — bōgi — "a railway coach; a carriage".
ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣ ಮುಂದಿನ ಬೋಗಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕುಳಿತಿದ್ದಾನೆ. nanna aṇṇa mundina bōgiyalli kuḷitiddāne. My elder brother is sitting in the next coach.
Plural: ಬೋಗಿಗಳು, bōgigaḷu.
A train is a line of ಬೋಗಿಗಳು pulled by an engine.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೋಗಿ (bōgi) is a loan from English bogie, the wheeled frame under a railway carriage. In Indian use the word came to name the whole coach.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಹಳಿ (haḷi) — a rail track; a rail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a railway platform, then the Kannada for a railway coach.

You'll want to know: ಹಳಿ.
ಹಳಿ — haḷi — "a rail track; a rail".
ರೈಲು ಹಳಿಯ ಮೇಲೆ ನಡೆಯಬಾರದು. railu haḷiya mēle naḍeyabāradu. Nobody should walk on the rail track.
Plural: ಹಳಿಗಳು, haḷigaḷu.
ಹಳಿ (haḷi) ತಪ್ಪು, to leave the rails, is said of a train and, loosely, of a plan that goes wrong.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಳಿ (haḷi) looks like a native Kannada word, but its origin is not settled: DEDR shows no clear source for haḷi in the sense of a rail or a track.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತಲುಪು (talupu) — to reach; to arrive at.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a railway coach, then the Kannada for a rail track.

You'll want to know: ತಲುಪು.
ತಲುಪು — talupu — "to reach; to arrive at".
ರೈಲು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹತ್ತು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಊರು ತಲುಪಿತು. railu rātri hattu gaṇṭege ūru talupitu. The train reached the town at ten at night.
Past (I …): ತಲುಪಿದೆ, talupide.
The place may take -ಅನ್ನು or no ending: ಊರನ್ನು ತಲುಪು (talupu), ಊರು ತಲುಪು (talupu). ತಲುಪಿಸು, to deliver, makes something else reach.

The word, taken apart.
ತಲುಪು (talupu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3102 lists Kannada talapu, talupu, "to reach, to arrive", beside talapisu, "to cause to reach", the source of ತಲುಪಿಸು.
