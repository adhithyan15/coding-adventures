## You'll want to know: નમવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C359-IJJAT359-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નમવું** — *namvũ* — "to bow".

> બાળકો ગુરુ આગળ નમે છે.
>
> *bāḷko guru āgaḷ name che.*
>
> The children bow before the teacher.

Past (he …): તે મંદિરમાં નમ્યો, *te mandirmā̃ namyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નમવું** (*namvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *námati*, "bends, bows"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 6956).
