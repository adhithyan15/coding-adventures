## You'll want to know: निधी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C361-ARTHIK361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निधी** — *nidhī* — "a fund".

> आम्ही ग्रंथालयासाठी निधी गोळा केला.
>
> *āmhī granthālyāsāṭhī nidhī goḷā kelā.*
>
> We raised a fund for the library.

Masculine.

Masculine: मोठा निधी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निधी** (*nidhī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nidhi*, "setting down, a hoard" (CDIAL 7207).
