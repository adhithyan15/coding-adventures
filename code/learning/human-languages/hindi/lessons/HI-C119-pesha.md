## You'll want to know: पेशा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C119-ABOUT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पेशा** (*peśā*) — "a profession", a trade.

**नौकरी** is the job you hold; **पेशा** is what you are by trade. **आपका पेशा क्या है?**

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A job, and the profession behind it.
