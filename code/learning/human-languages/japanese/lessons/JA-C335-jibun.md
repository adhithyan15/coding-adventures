## You'll want to know: じぶん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C335-DANTAI335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**じぶん** — *jibun* — "oneself".

> **じぶんで きめた ほうが いいです。** — *jibun de kimeta hō ga ii desu* — "You had better decide for yourself."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じぶん** (*jibun*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "oneself" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*) and *bun*, "part" (*pjun*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
