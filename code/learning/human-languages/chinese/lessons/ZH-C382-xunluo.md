## You'll want to know: xúnluó
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C382-GUARD382-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xúnluó* — "to patrol".

> *wǎnshang bǎo'ān yīnggāi zài xiǎoqū lǐ xúnluó* — At night the guards should patrol the estate.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xúnluó* is built from *xún* 'to go round' and *luó* 'a patrol', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zwin* and *laH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go about looking' and the second 'roving soldiers'. Mandarin luó has a rising tone where the dictionary gives a departing one.
