# Hear *dàjiā* — everyone

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-DA-01, ZH-LEX-JIA-01] -->

[PAUSE 10s] Say *dà*, then *jiā*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-DAJIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *dàjiā* — **everyone; everybody**

Hear two beats: falling *dà*, then level *jiā*. The whole word means everyone.
Its written form will contain only characters you already know.
