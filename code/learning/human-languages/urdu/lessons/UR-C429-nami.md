## You'll want to know: نمی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C429-THAND429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نمی** — *namī* — "moisture; damp".

> بارش کے بعد دیوار میں نمی ہے۔
>
> *bārish ke bād dīvār meṅ namī hai.*
>
> After the rain the wall is damp.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نمی** (*namī*) is built on *nam*, "damp, moist", usually traced to Persian *nam*, with the ending *-ī*.
