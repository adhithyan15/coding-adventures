## You'll want to know: পিছপা হওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C537-MONOSTHIR537-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পিছপা হওয়া** — *pichhpā hôoyā* — "to shrink back; to hold back".

> বিপদে ছেলেটা পিছপা হল না।
>
> *Bipôde chheleṭā pichhpā hôlô nā.*
>
> The boy didn't shrink back in the face of danger.

Past (he/she …): তিনি পিছপা হলেন না, *tini pichhpā hôlen nā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পিছপা হওয়া** (*pichhpā hôoyā*) joins পিছপা, "foot backwards", to হওয়া, "to become". The first part belongs with *paśca*, "the hind part", under which CDIAL lists Bengali *pichā*, "the rear" (CDIAL 7990); the second is পা, "a foot".
