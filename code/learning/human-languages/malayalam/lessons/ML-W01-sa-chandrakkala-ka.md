# സ · ് · ക — build ska without a leap

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-NA-01, ML-SCRIPT-MA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **നമ** from the heard cue *na-ma*. Now set it aside. This
lesson adds exactly three shapes.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-SA-01, ML-SCRIPT-CHANDRAKKALA-01, ML-SCRIPT-KA-01]; assesses=[] -->

- **സ** is *sa*, with its inherent *a*.
- **്** is the *chandrakkala*. Attach it to remove that inherent vowel: **സ്**.
- **ക** is *ka*.

Join the vowel-less first consonant to the next one: **സ്ക**, *ska*. The
cluster is not a fourth shape; it is the three pieces you just met working
together.

## Writing — observe and trace three pieces
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-SA-01, ML-SCRIPT-CHANDRAKKALA-01, ML-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Keep the models visible. Trace **സ**, then **്**, then **ക** once each. Point
through **സ്ക** and say *ska*. Stop after one slow pass.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-SA-01, ML-SCRIPT-CHANDRAKKALA-01, ML-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What does **്** remove? (**The inherent vowel.**) Read **സ** and
then **സ്**. (***sa; s.***) What does **സ്ക** say? (***ska.***)
