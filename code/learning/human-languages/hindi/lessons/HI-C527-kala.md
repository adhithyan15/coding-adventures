## You'll want to know: कला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C527-KALA527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कला** — *kalā* — "art".

> हमारे स्कूल में हर बुधवार कला की कक्षा होती है।
>
> *Hamāre skūl meṁ har budhvār kalā kī kakṣā hotī hai.*
>
> Our school has an art class every Wednesday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कला** (*kalā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *kalā*, "a small part; any practical art" (Monier-Williams).
