# ئ (hamza) — the hamza on its seat in ہوئی

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-ZUAD-01, UR-SCRIPT-C37-ZOE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ض and ظ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ہوئی** (*huī*), "happened (in pleased to meet you)".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ئ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-HAMZA-SEAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ئ** — *hamza* on its seat.

It is inside **ہوئی** (*huī*), the last word of **آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی**. Say
*hu-ī*: two vowels side by side. The small **hamza** sits on a short tooth, its
**seat**, and marks the place where one vowel ends and the next begins.

## Writing: ئ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-HAMZA-SEAT-01] -->

Put your pen on ئ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

- [YOU LOOK: at **ہوئی** and point at ئ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ئ three times, saying *hu-ī* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ظ, then ئ, from memory]
