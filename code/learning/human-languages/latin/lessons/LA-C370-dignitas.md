## You'll want to know: dignitās, dignitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C370-AUCTORITAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dignitās, dignitātis** — "worth, rank, dignity".

> Dignitātem suam semper servāvit.
>
> *He always kept his dignity.*

*dignitās, dignitātis* f., built on *dignus*, worthy. A Roman's *dignitās* is his rank and good name. English *dignity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dignitās* is built on *dignus* "worthy" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tās* that makes a quality. English *dignity* comes from it.
