## You'll want to know: amuar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C474-RABUGENTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**amuar** — "to sulk".

> Quando perde um jogo, o miúdo amua e não fala com ninguém.
>
> *When he loses a game, the kid sulks and does not talk to anyone.*

A sulk is *um amuo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amuar* is built with the prefix *a-* (EtymDB), but the origin of the rest of the word is not settled.
