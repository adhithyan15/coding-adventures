Gujarati, chapter 364: Discoveries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શોધ (śodh) — a discovery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a count, then the Gujarati for to bring down.

You'll want to know: શોધ.
શોધ — śodh — "a discovery".
આ દવા એક મોટી શોધ છે. ā davā ek moṭī śodh che. This medicine is a major discovery.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શોધ (śodh) is the noun of શોધવું (śodhvũ), "to look for", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit śoddhum, "to purify" (CDIAL 12626): what a search turns up.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઉઘાડવું (ughāḍvũ) — to uncover; to open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for proven, then the Gujarati for a mystery.

You'll want to know: ઉઘાડવું.
ઉઘાડવું — ughāḍvũ — "to uncover; to open".
મીરાએ સવારે બારી ઉઘાડી. Mīrāe savāre bārī ughāḍī. Mira opened the window in the morning.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બારણું ઉઘાડ્યું, teṇe bārṇũ ughāḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઉઘાડવું (ughāḍvũ) is the transitive partner of ઊઘડવું (ūghaḍvũ), "to open up"; CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit udghāṭayati, "opens", beside a reconstructed udghaṭati, "is open" (CDIAL 1968).
