Urdu, chapter 533: A Game of Chance.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
جوا (juā) — gambling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a draw (of lots), then the Urdu for a toss (of a coin).

You'll want to know: جوا.
جوا — juā — "gambling".
اگر تم نے جوا کھیلا تو سب ہار جاؤ گے۔ agar tum ne juā khelā to sab hār jāo ge. If you gamble, you will lose everything.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
جوا (juā) is inherited from Sanskrit dyūta, "gambling" (CDIAL 6608).

چال — chāl — "a move (in a game)".
اگر وہ یہ چال سمجھ گیا تو جیت جائے گا۔ agar voh yih chāl samajh gayā to jīt jāe gā. If he sees through this move, he will win.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
چال (chāl) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin cāl, "behaviour", under calyā, "gait, conduct" (CDIAL 4722); the sense "a move in a game" is a later one that CDIAL does not record.

پلٹانا — palṭānā — "to turn over (something)".
اگر آپ نے پتا پلٹایا تو کھیل بدل جائے گا۔ agar āp ne pattā palṭāyā to khel badal jāe gā. If you turn the card over, the game will change.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پتا پلٹایا, us ne pattā palṭāyā.
