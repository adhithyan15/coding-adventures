## You'll want to know: fouiller
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C417-DOUANE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fouiller** — "to search (a bag, a person)".

> Un agent a fouillé mon sac à dos.
>
> *An officer searched my rucksack.*

*La fouille* — the search.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fouiller* goes back to Vulgar Latin *fodiculāre*, from Latin *fodere* 'to dig' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB links it with *fouir* 'to dig'.
