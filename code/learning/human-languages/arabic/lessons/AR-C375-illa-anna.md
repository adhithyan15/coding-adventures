# إلا أن (illā anna) — yet; but

## You'll want to know: إلا أن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C375-TADADD375-02]; assesses=[] -->

**إلا أن** — *illā anna* — "yet; but".

> الفندق جميل، إلا أن الغرف صغيرة.
>
> *al-funduq jamīl, illā anna al-ghuraf ṣaghīra.*
>
> The hotel is beautiful, but the rooms are small.

More formal than لكن.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إلا أن** (*illā anna*) joins the native Arabic particles *illā*, "except", and *anna*, "that": except that, and so yet, but. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.
