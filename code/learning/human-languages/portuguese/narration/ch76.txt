Lesson 1 of 5.
a aldeia — a village.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a hotel, then the Portuguese for a pharmacy.

You'll want to know: a aldeia.
a aldeia — "a village".

The word, taken apart.
aldeia from Arabic aḍ-ḍayʿa, "the farm, the village" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. The al- is the Arabic word for "the".
