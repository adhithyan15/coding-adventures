## You'll want to know: چکلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C507-ROTI507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چکلا** — *chaklā* — "a board for rolling dough".

> چکلا باورچی خانے میں میز پر ہے۔
>
> *chaklā bāvarchī khāne meṅ mez par hai.*
>
> The rolling board is on the table in the kitchen.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چکلا** (*chaklā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *caklā*, "round and flat", under *\*cakrala*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4548).
