## You'll want to know: el convenio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C591-DESPIDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el convenio** — "the agreement; the collective agreement".

> El convenio sube los sueldos un dos por ciento.
>
> *The collective agreement raises wages by two per cent.*

*El convenio* sets pay and hours for everyone in one kind of work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convenio* comes from *convenir*, inherited from Latin *convenire* 'to come together, to agree' (EtymDB), built on *venire* 'to come'.
