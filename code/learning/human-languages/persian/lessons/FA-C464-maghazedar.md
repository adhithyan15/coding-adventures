## You'll want to know: مغازه دار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C464-MAGHAZE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مغازه دار** (*maghâzedâr*) — "a shopkeeper".

> مغازه دار به ما تخفیف داد.
>
> *Maghâzedâr be mâ takhfif dâd.*
>
> The shopkeeper gave us a discount.

*maghâze* 'shop' + *dâr* 'keeper'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maghâzedâr* is built from *maghâze* 'shop', which EtymDB takes from Russian *magazín*, and native *dâr* 'holder, keeper', from the stem of *dâshtan* 'to have'.
