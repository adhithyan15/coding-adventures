Persian, chapter 301: Things happen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اتفاق افتادن (ettefâq oftâdan) — to happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the moment that, then the Persian for by chance.

You'll want to know: اتفاق افتادن.
اتفاق افتادن (ettefâq oftâdan) — "to happen".
دیشب در خیابان ما یک اتفاق عجیب افتاد. Dishab dar khiyâbân-e mâ yek ettefâq-e 'ajib oftâd. Last night something strange happened in our street.
Literally "an event falls". Present stem oft-: in ziyâd ettefâq mioftad "this happens a lot". Che ettefâqi oftâd? "What happened?"

The word, taken apart.
The noun ettefâq is borrowed from Arabic ittifāq (EtymDB). The verb oftâdan "to fall" is inherited Persian, so the phrase is a Persian compound verb built on an Arabic noun.

Lesson 2 of 5.
روی دادن (ruy dâdan) — to occur, to happen (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for by chance, then the Persian for to happen.

You'll want to know: روی دادن.
روی دادن (ruy dâdan) — "to occur, to happen (formal)".
این اتفاق ساعت ده شب روی داد. In ettefâq sâ'at-e dah-e shab ruy dâd. This happened at ten at night.
Formal and common in the news. Literally "to give a face", to show itself. Present stem dah-: ruy midahad.

The word, taken apart.
ruy dâdan joins ruy "face" and dâdan "to give", both inherited Persian; EtymDB takes dâdan back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".

Lesson 3 of 5.
به وقوع پیوستن (be voqu' peyvastan) — to come about, to take place (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to happen, then the Persian for to occur.

You'll want to know: به وقوع پیوستن.
به وقوع پیوستن (be voqu' peyvastan) — "to come about, to take place (formal)".
این اتفاق دو سال پیش به وقوع پیوست. In ettefâq do sâl pish be voqu' peyvast. This came about two years ago.
Very formal: news and history writing. voqu' "occurrence" + peyvastan "to join"; present stem peyvand-.

The word, taken apart.
voqu' is usually traced to Arabic wuqū', "a falling, an occurrence"; EtymDB lists no entry. peyvastan "to join" is inherited from Middle Persian paywastan (EtymDB). Literally "to join with occurrence".

Lesson 4 of 5.
صورت گرفتن (surat gereftan) — to take place, to be carried out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to occur, then the Persian for to come about.

You'll want to know: صورت گرفتن.
صورت گرفتن (surat gereftan) — "to take place, to be carried out".
جلسه دیروز صبح صورت گرفت. Jalase diruz sobh surat gereft. The meeting took place yesterday morning.
Formal, for planned actions. Literally "to take form". Present stem gir-: surat migirad.

The word, taken apart.
surat is borrowed from Arabic ṣūra, "form, image" (EtymDB). gereftan "to take" is inherited Persian, from the Old Persian root grab- "to seize".

Lesson 5 of 5.
دوام آوردن (davâm âvardan) — to last, to hold out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to come about, then the Persian for to take place.

You'll want to know: دوام آوردن.
دوام آوردن (davâm âvardan) — "to last, to hold out".
سقف خانه زیر برف سنگین دوام نیاورد. Saqf-e khâne zir-e barf-e sangin davâm nayâvard. The roof of the house did not hold out under the heavy snow.
Literally "to bring durability". Present stem âvar-: davâm miâvarad; the negative past is davâm nayâvard.

The word, taken apart.
davâm "duration, lasting" is borrowed from Arabic dawām; EtymDB lists the Arabic word, though not the Persian one. âvardan "to bring" is inherited Persian.
