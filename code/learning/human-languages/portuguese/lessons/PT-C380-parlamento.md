## You'll want to know: o parlamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C380-POLITICA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o parlamento** — "the parliament".

> O parlamento aprovou a lei ontem.
>
> *Parliament passed the law yesterday.*

Its members are *os deputados*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parlamento* is usually traced to Old French *parlement* "discussion, meeting, assembly" (EtymDB), built on *parler* "to speak", which goes back to Late Latin *parabolāre*, on *parabola* "a comparison" (Lewis & Short).
