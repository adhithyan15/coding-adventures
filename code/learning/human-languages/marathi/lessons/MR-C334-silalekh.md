## You'll want to know: शिलालेख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C334-KRANTI334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शिलालेख** — *śilālekh* — "an inscription".

> मंदिराच्या भिंतीवर एक जुना शिलालेख आहे.
>
> *mandirācyā bhintīvar ek junā śilālekh āhe.*
>
> There is an old inscription on the temple wall.

Masculine.

शिला, a stone, + लेख, a writing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिलालेख** (*śilālekh*) is built from Sanskrit *śilā*, 'a stone', and *lekha*, 'a writing' (Monier-Williams): writing in stone.
