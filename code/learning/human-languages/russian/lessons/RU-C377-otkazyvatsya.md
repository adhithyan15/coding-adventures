## You'll want to know: отказываться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C377-PLANY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**отказываться** (*otkázyvatsya*) — "to refuse, to turn down; to give up".

> Она отказывается от сладкого.
>
> *Oná otkázyvayetsya ot sládkogo.*
>
> She is giving up sweet things.

Imperfective; perfective *отказаться*. *Отказываться от* + genitive (to turn down or give up), or + infinitive: *отказался помочь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**отказываться** is built from *от-* "away" and *казать* "to show, to say", Proto-Slavic *\*kazati* (EtymDB): to say a thing away from yourself.
