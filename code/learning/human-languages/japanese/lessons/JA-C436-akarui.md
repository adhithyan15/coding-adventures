## You'll want to know: あかるい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C436-BRIGHT436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あかるい** — *akarui* — "bright; light".

> **かれは あかるい せいかくです。** — *kare wa akarui seikaku desu* — "He has a bright personality."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あかるい** (*akarui*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
