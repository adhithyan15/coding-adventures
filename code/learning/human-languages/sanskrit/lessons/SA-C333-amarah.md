## You'll want to know: अमरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C333-SHAPA333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अमरः** — *amaraḥ* — "immortal".

> वरेण ऋषिः अमरः भवति।
>
> *vareṇa ṛṣiḥ amaraḥ bhavati.*
>
> Through the boon the seer becomes immortal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अमरः** (*amaraḥ*) is built from *a*, "not", and *mara*, "dying", from the root √mṛ, as in *mriyate*; MW gives *amara* as undying, immortal, and as a noun a god (MW).
