## You'll want to know: el vagón
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C651-TREN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el vagón** — "the carriage, the railway car".

> Nuestro asiento está en el vagón tres.
>
> *Our seat is in carriage three.*

*El vagón restaurante* — the dining car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vagón* was borrowed from English *wagon* (EtymDB), which itself came from Dutch *wagen*.
