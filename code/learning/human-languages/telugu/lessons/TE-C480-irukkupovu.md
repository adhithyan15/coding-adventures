# ఇరుక్కుపోవు (irukkupōvu) — to get stuck

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C480-MUNCETTU480-03, TE-LEX-C480-MUNCETTU480-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to snap, then the Telugu for to get bogged down.

## You'll want to know: ఇరుక్కుపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C480-MUNCETTU480-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇరుక్కుపోవు** — *irukkupōvu* — "to get stuck".

> వరదలో చాలా కార్లు ఇరుక్కుపోయాయి.
>
> *varadalō cālā kārlu irukkupōyāyi.*
>
> Many cars got stuck in the flood.

Past (I …): ఇరుక్కుపోయాను, *irukkupōyānu*.

ఇరుకు, narrow, tight, + -కొని + పోవు: caught in a tight place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇరుక్కుపోవు** (*irukkupōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 524 lists Telugu *iṟuku*, "to be squeezed, to get stuck in", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
