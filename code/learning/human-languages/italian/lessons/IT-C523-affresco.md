## You'll want to know: l'affresco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C523-MOSTRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'affresco** — "fresco".

> Nella chiesa ci sono affreschi molto antichi.
>
> *In the church there are very old frescoes.*

*A fresco*: painted on wet plaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*affresco* is built from *a fresco* "on the fresh (plaster)"; *fresco* comes from Medieval Latin *friscus*, from a Germanic word *friskaz* "fresh" (EtymDB).
