## You'll want to know: 庆祝
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C339-NATION339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**庆祝** — *qìngzhù* — "to celebrate".

> **我们一起庆祝国庆** *wǒmen yìqǐ qìngzhù Guóqìng* — We celebrate the national holiday together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**庆祝** (*qìngzhù*) is built from 庆 'to congratulate' and 祝 'to bless', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khjaengH* and *tsyuwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to congratulate'.
