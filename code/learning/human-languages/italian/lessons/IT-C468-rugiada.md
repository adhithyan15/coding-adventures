## You'll want to know: la rugiada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C468-GELO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la rugiada** — "the dew".

> L'erba è bagnata di rugiada.
>
> *The grass is wet with dew.*

In the morning, after a clear night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rugiada* goes back to Latin *rōs, rōris* 'dew' (EtymDB); its shape points to a Gallo-Romance go-between like Old French *rosée*, though that path is not settled.
