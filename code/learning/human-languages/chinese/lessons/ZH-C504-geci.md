## You'll want to know: gēcí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C504-SONGS504-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gēcí* — "the words of a song; lyrics".

> *wǒ tīng le liǎng tiān cái jìzhù gēcí* — It took two days of listening before I knew the words.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gēcí* is built from *gē* 'a song' and *cí* 'words', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ka* and *zi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the voice of a person singing' and the second 'to speak'.
