## You'll want to know: ぜいかん

**ぜいかん** — *zeikan* — "customs".

> **くうこうで ぜいかんを とおりました。** — *kūkō de zeikan o tōrimashita* — "I went through customs at the airport."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜいかん** (*zeikan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zei*, "tax" (Middle Chinese *sywejH*) and *kan*, "barrier" (*kwaen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
