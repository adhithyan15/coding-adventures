## You'll want to know: تماس گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C456-PEYK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تماس گرفتن** (*tamâs gereftan*) — "to get in touch, to call (someone)".

> با هتل تماس گرفتیم و اتاق را رزرو کردیم.
>
> *Bâ hotel tamâs gereftim va otâq râ rezerv kardim.*
>
> We called the hotel and booked the room.

The person called takes *bâ*. More formal than *zang zadan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tamâs gereftan* is built from *tamâs* 'contact', a loan usually traced to Arabic *tamāss* 'touching one another', and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take'. EtymDB does not list *tamâs*.
