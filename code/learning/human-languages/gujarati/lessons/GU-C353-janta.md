## You'll want to know: જનતા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C353-MATDAN353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જનતા** — *jantā* — "the public".

> જનતા આ નિયમથી નારાજ છે.
>
> *jantā ā niyamthī nārāj che.*
>
> The public is unhappy with this rule.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જનતા** (*jantā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *janatā*, "a body of people" (Monier-Williams).
