## You'll want to know: समीहते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C549-LALASA549-05]; assesses=[] -->

**समीहते** — *samīhate* — "strives for".

> यदि सः सुखं समीहते, तर्हि सः अन्यस्य साहाय्यं करोतु।
>
> *yadi saḥ sukhaṃ samīhate, tarhi saḥ anyasya sāhāyyaṃ karotu.*
>
> If he strives for happiness, let him help others.

Past (he …): सः सुखं समीहितवान्, *saḥ sukhaṃ samīhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समीहते** (*samīhate*) is built on *sam* and the root √īh, "to strive"; MW gives *samīh* as to strive after, to wish for, to endeavour to gain (MW).
