## You'll want to know: انجن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C386-DABBA386-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انجن** — *injan* — "an engine".

> ریل گاڑی کا انجن کالا اور بڑا ہے۔
>
> *rel gāṛī kā injan kālā aur baṛā hai.*
>
> The train's engine is big and black.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انجن** (*injan*) is borrowed from English *engine*; at a station it means the locomotive at the front of the train.
