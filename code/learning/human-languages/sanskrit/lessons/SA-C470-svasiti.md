## You'll want to know: श्वसिति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C470-PHUPPHUSA470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**श्वसिति** — *śvasiti* — "breathes".

> शिशुः शान्तं श्वसिति।
>
> *śiśuḥ śāntaṃ śvasiti.*
>
> The baby breathes calmly.

Past (he …): सः शान्तं श्वसितवान्, *saḥ śāntaṃ śvasitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्वसिति** (*śvasiti*) comes from the root √śvas, "to breathe", the root of **श्वासः** (MW).
