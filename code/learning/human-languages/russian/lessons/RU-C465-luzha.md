## You'll want to know: лужа
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C465-LIVEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**лужа** (*lúzha*) — "a puddle".

> Ребёнок прыгал по лужам.
>
> *Rebyónok prýgal po lúzham.*
>
> The child was jumping in the puddles.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*лужа* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *luža* 'a pool' (EtymDB).
