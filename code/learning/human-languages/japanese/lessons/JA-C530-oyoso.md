## You'll want to know: およそ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C530-ATLEAST530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**およそ** — *oyoso* — "about; roughly".

> **でんしゃで いったら、およそ にじかん かかるでしょう。** — *densha de ittara, oyoso nijikan kakaru deshō* — "If you go by train, it will take about two hours."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**およそ** (*oyoso*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
