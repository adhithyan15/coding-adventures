# Review — should, must, and so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think it will rain". (*mujhe lagtā hai ki bārish ho gī*.) Then "because of the rain". (*bārish kī vajah se*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-02, UR-LEX-C269-CHUNANCHE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-02, UR-LEX-C41-HEALTH41-04, UR-LEX-C182-ACT182-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: You should rest. You shouldn't go.]
- [YOU SAY: I have to work every day.]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday I had to go to the office.]
- [YOU SAY: I was ill, so I didn't come.]
- [YOU SAY: It's raining? Then I'll stay at home.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "You should drink water"? (**آپ کو پانی پینا چاہیے**.) Now the other way round: which word says there is no choice, **چاہیے** or **پڑنا**? (**پڑنا**.) The formal word for **اس لیے**? (**چنانچہ**.)
