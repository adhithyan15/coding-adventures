Lesson 1 of 5.
собака (sobáka) — a dog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the weather, then the Russian for the world; peace.

You'll want to know: собака.
собака (sobáka) — "a dog".

The word, taken apart.
собака (sobáka) — origin disputed. Vasmer takes it from a Middle Iranian sabāka-, compare Median spaka, "dog", which Herodotus records, a cousin of Greek kýōn, "dog". Trubachev, in a note, finds an Iranian source unlikely and prefers Turkic köbäk, "dog".

You'll want to know: кошка.

The word, taken apart.
кошка (kóshka) — probably a fond form built on кот, "a tomcat", as the pet name Маша is built on Мария, "Mary" (Vasmer). Old Russian had kotŭka.

Lesson 3 of 5.
лошадь (lóshad) — a horse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dog, then the Russian for a cat.

You'll want to know: лошадь.
лошадь (lóshad) — "a horse".

The word, taken apart.
лошадь (lóshad) — an old loan from Turkic, says Vasmer: compare Chuvash laša, "horse", and Turkish and Tatar alaša, "a horse, a gelding". It grew from лоша, with a suffix; the native word is конь, "a steed".

Lesson 4 of 5.
корова (koróva) — a cow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cat, then the Russian for a horse.

You'll want to know: корова.
корова (koróva) — "a cow". It gives молоко.

The word, taken apart.
корова (koróva) from Proto-Slavic korva — inherited, a cousin of Lithuanian kárvė, "cow", and, Vasmer says, of Greek keraós, "horned", and Latin cervus, "deer": the horned one. Church Slavonic has крава, "cow".

Lesson 5 of 5.
свинья (svinyá) — a pig.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a horse, then the Russian for a cow.

You'll want to know: свинья.
свинья (svinyá) — "a pig".

The word, taken apart.
свинья (svinyá) from Proto-Slavic svinĭja — inherited, built on the PIE word suH-, "pig", that gave Latin sūs and English sow; English swine is a close cousin of the Slavic form. Vasmer thinks the root most likely imitates a pig's grunt su-.
