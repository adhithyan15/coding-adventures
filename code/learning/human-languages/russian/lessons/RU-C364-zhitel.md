## You'll want to know: житель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C364-OBSHCHESTVO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**житель** (*zhítel*) — "an inhabitant, a resident".

> Жители деревни против новой дороги.
>
> *Zhíteli derévni prótiv nóvoy dorógi.*
>
> The villagers are against the new road.

Masculine; a woman is *жительница*. *Жители города* — the people of a town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**житель** is built on *жить* "to live", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*žiti*, with the agent suffix *-тель*: one who lives there.
