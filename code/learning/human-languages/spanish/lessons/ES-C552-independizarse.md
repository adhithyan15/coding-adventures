## You'll want to know: independizarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C552-EXILIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**independizarse** — "to become independent; to leave home".

> Me independicé a los veintidós años.
>
> *I left home at twenty-two.*

For a young person it means moving out of the parents' house. It takes *c* before *e*: *me independicé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*independizarse* is built on *independiente* with the ending *-izar*. The root is *depender*, from Latin *dēpendēre* 'to hang down from', built on *pendēre* 'to hang' (Lewis & Short).
