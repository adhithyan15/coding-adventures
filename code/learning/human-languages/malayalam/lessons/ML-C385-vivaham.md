# വിവാഹം (vivāhaṁ) — a marriage; a wedding

## You'll want to know: വിവാഹം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C385-VIVAHAM385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**വിവാഹം** — *vivāhaṁ* — "a marriage; a wedding".

> അവരുടെ വിവാഹം അടുത്ത മാസമാണ്.
>
> *avaruṭe vivāhaṁ aṭutta māsamāṇŭ.*
>
> Their wedding is next month.

The formal word beside കല്യാണം; invitations and the news use വിവാഹം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിവാഹം** (*vivāhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vivāha*, "marriage" (CDIAL 11920), from *vi-vah*, "to lead away": the bride was led from her father's house.
