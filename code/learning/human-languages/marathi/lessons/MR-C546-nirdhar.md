## You'll want to know: निर्धार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C546-NISHCHAY546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्धार** — *nirdhār* — "determination".

> परीक्षा पास होण्याचा तिचा निर्धार पक्का होता.
>
> *parīkṣā pās hoṇyācā ticā nirdhār pakkā hotā.*
>
> Her determination to pass the exam was firm.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्धार** (*nirdhār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nirdhāra*, "settling, certainty" (Monier-Williams).
