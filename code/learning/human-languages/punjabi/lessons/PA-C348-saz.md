## You'll want to know: ਸਾਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C348-MUSIC348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਜ਼** — *sāz* — "a musical instrument".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਹਰ ਸਾਜ਼ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ har sāz pasand hai.*
>
> I like every musical instrument.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਜ਼** (*sāz*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *sāz*, "instrument".
