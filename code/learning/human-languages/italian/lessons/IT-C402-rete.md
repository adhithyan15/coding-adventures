## You'll want to know: la rete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C402-RETE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la rete** — "the network; the internet; the net".

> Ho trovato la ricetta in rete.
>
> *I found the recipe online.*

*In rete* — online. In football, *la rete* is the goal net, and a goal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rete* is inherited from Latin *rēte* "a net" (Lewis & Short): a fishing net, then any network of threads, roads or computers.
