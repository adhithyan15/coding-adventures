Portuguese, chapter 296: Algemas e celas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
as algemas — the handcuffs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the arrest, then the Portuguese for the person arrested.

You'll want to know: as algemas.
as algemas — "the handcuffs".
O agente pôs as algemas ao suspeito. The officer put the handcuffs on the suspect.
Usually plural; one cuff is a algema.

The word, taken apart.
algema comes from Arabic al-jāmiʿa "the fetter", literally "that which joins" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
o preso — the prisoner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to handcuff, then the Portuguese for to catch.

You'll want to know: o preso.
o preso — "the prisoner".
O preso escreve cartas à mãe. The prisoner writes letters to his mother.
As an adjective: estar preso — to be in jail, or to be stuck.

The word, taken apart.
preso comes from Latin prehēnsus "seized" (EtymDB), the past participle of prehendere.
