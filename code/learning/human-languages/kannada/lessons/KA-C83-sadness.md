# ದುಃಖ (duḥkha) — sadness

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-04, KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-05] -->

## You'll want to know: ದುಃಖ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C83-FEELINGS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ದುಃಖ** (*duḥkha*) — "sadness, sorrow". The two small circles **ಃ** after **ದು**
are the *visarga*: a breath at the end of the syllable, *duḥ-kha*.

It is the opposite of **ಸಂತೋಷ** (*santōṣa*), "happiness", which you met at the start.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The other side of santōṣa.
