## You'll want to know: remorquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C405-DEPANNEUSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**remorquer** — "to tow".

> Ils ont remorqué la voiture jusqu'au garage.
>
> *They towed the car to the garage.*

*Une remorque* — a trailer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remorquer* was borrowed from Italian *rimorchiare* 'to tow' (EtymDB), which is usually traced through Late Latin to a Greek word for towing with a rope.
