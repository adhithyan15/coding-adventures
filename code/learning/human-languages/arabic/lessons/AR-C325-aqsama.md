## You'll want to know: أقسم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C325-SHAHID325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أقسم** — *aqsama* — "to swear".

> أقسم الشاهد أن كلامه صحيح.
>
> *aqsama ash-shāhid anna kalāmahu ṣaḥīḥ.*
>
> The witness swore that what he said was true.

Present: يقسم, *yuqsim*.

The oath itself is قسم, *qasam*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أقسم** (*aqsama*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-s-m* (Lane), "to divide", the root of *qism*, a part. Lane gives *qasam* as an oath, and Form IV *aqsama*, to swear.
