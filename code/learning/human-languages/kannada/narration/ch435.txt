Kannada, chapter 435: Berths and Missed Trains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬರ್ತ್ (bart) — a berth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a halt, then the Kannada for to enquire.

You'll want to know: ಬರ್ತ್.
ಬರ್ತ್ — bart — "a berth".
ನನಗೆ ಮೇಲಿನ ಬರ್ತ್ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. nanage mēlina bart sikkitu. I got the upper berth.
ಮೇಲಿನ, ನಡುವಿನ, ಕೆಳಗಿನ ಬರ್ತ್ (bart): the upper, middle and lower berth.

The word, taken apart.
ಬರ್ತ್ (bart) is a loan from English berth, a sleeping place on a train or a ship.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಆಸನ (āsana) — a formal seat; a posture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to enquire, then the Kannada for a berth.

You'll want to know: ಆಸನ.
ಆಸನ — āsana — "a formal seat; a posture".
ಬಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಹಿರಿಯರಿಗೆ ಬೇರೆ ಆಸನಗಳು ಇವೆ. bassinalli hiriyarige bēre āsanagaḷu ive. On the bus there are separate seats for elders.
Plural: ಆಸನಗಳು, āsanagaḷu.
The formal word of signs and tickets, where everyday speech says ಸೀಟು; in yoga an ಆಸನ (āsana) is a posture.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಸನ (āsana) is borrowed from Sanskrit āsana, "sitting, a seat" (CDIAL 1484), from the root ās, "to sit".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕೈತಪ್ಪು (kaitappu) — to slip away; to be missed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a berth, then the Kannada for a formal seat.

You'll want to know: ಕೈತಪ್ಪು.
ಕೈತಪ್ಪು — kaitappu — "to slip away; to be missed".
ಎರಡು ನಿಮಿಷ ತಡವಾದ್ದರಿಂದ ರೈಲು ಕೈತಪ್ಪಿತು. eraḍu nimiṣa taḍavāddarinda railu kaitappitu. Being two minutes late, I missed the train.
Past (it …): ಕೈತಪ್ಪಿತು, kaitappitu.
The thing missed is the subject: ಅವಕಾಶ ಕೈತಪ್ಪಿತು, the chance slipped away.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೈತಪ್ಪು (kaitappu) is a native Kannada compound: kai, "hand", and ತಪ್ಪು, "to slip, to miss" (DEDR 3071): to slip out of the hand.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಇಣುಕು (iṇuku) — to peep; to peer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a formal seat, then the Kannada for to slip away.

You'll want to know: ಇಣುಕು.
ಇಣುಕು — iṇuku — "to peep; to peer".
ಮಗು ಕಿಟಕಿಯಿಂದ ಹೊರಗೆ ಇಣುಕಿತು. magu kiṭakiyinda horage iṇukitu. The child peeped out of the window.
Past (I …): ಇಣುಕಿದೆ, iṇukide.
ಇಣುಕಿ ನೋಡು, to peep and look, is the common pair.

The word, taken apart.
ಇಣುಕು (iṇuku) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled; DEDR shows no clear source for it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ದರ (dara) — a fare; a rate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to slip away, then the Kannada for to peep.

You'll want to know: ದರ.
ದರ — dara — "a fare; a rate".
ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಬಸ್ ದರ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಯಿತು. ī varṣa bas dara heccāyitu. This year the bus fare went up.
ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ದರ (dara) is a fare; ಬಡ್ಡಿ ದರ (dara) is a rate of interest.

The word, taken apart.
ದರ (dara) is usually traced to Hindustani dar, "rate, price"; its earlier source is not settled.
