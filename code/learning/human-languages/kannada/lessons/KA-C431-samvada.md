## You'll want to know: ಸಂವಾದ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C431-TIRMANISU431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂವಾದ** — *saṃvāda* — "a dialogue; a discussion".

> ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳು ಲೇಖಕರ ಜೊತೆ ಸಂವಾದ ನಡೆಸಿದರು.
>
> *vidyārthigaḷu lēkhakara jote saṃvāda naḍesidaru.*
>
> The students held a discussion with the author.

ಸಂವಾದ ನಡೆಸು, to hold a dialogue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂವಾದ** (*saṃvāda*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃvāda*, "speaking together, conversation" (Monier-Williams).
