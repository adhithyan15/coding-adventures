## You'll want to know: मंज़ूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C551-MAJBUR551-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मंज़ूर** — *manzūr* — "approved; accepted".

> मालिक को मेरी छुट्टी मंज़ूर है।
>
> *Mālik ko merī chuṭṭī manzūr hai.*
>
> The owner has approved my leave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंज़ूर** (*manzūr*) is usually traced to Arabic *manẓūr*, "seen, looked upon", through Persian. Persian and Hindi use it for something approved.
