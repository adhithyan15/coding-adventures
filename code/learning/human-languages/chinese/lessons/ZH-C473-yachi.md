## You'll want to know: yáchǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C473-TEETH473-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yáchǐ* — "tooth; teeth".

> *tā de yáchǐ bǐ wǒ de bái* — His teeth are whiter than mine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yáchǐ* is built from 牙 and *chǐ*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngae* and *tsyhiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses each with this very word: 'teeth'.
