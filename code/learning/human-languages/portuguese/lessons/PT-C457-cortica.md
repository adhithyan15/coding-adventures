## You'll want to know: a cortiça
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C457-ARVORES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a cortiça** — "cork".

> As rolhas das garrafas são de cortiça.
>
> *Bottle corks are made of cork.*

Bark cut from the cork oak every nine years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cortiça* is inherited from Latin *corticea* 'made of bark' (EtymDB), from *cortex* 'bark'.
