## You'll want to know: غثيان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C507-MARAD507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**غثيان** — *ghathayān* — "nausea".

> شعرت بالغثيان في السفينة.
>
> *shaʿartu bi-l-ghathayān fī as-safīna.*
>
> I felt sick on the ship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غثيان** (*ghathayān*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-th-y*, "to be troubled, to heave (of the stomach)" (Lane). The pattern faʿalān often names a restless movement.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
