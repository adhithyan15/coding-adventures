## You'll want to know: pulverulentus, pulverulenta, pulverulentum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C426-LUTULENTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pulverulentus, pulverulenta, pulverulentum** — "dusty, covered with dust".

> Aestāte via pulverulenta est, et viātōrēs aquam quaerunt.
>
> *In summer the road is dusty, and travellers look for water.*

An adjective of three endings, from *pulvis, pulveris* 'dust' with the ending *-ulentus* 'full of'. English *pulverize* is a cousin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pulverulentus* is built on *pulvis, pulveris* 'dust' with the suffix *-ulentus* 'full of' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *pulverize* comes from the same noun.
