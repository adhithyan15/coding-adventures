# గాయపరచు (gāyaparacu) — to injure; to wound

## You'll want to know: గాయపరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C378-KOTLATA378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గాయపరచు** — *gāyaparacu* — "to injure; to wound".

> దొంగ కాపలాదారును కత్తితో గాయపరిచాడు.
>
> *doṅga kāpalādārunu kattitō gāyaparicāḍu.*
>
> The thief injured the watchman with a knife.

Past (I …): గాయపరిచాను, *gāyaparicānu*.

You know గాయం, a wound; the ending -పరచు turns it into something done to another.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గాయపరచు** (*gāyaparacu*) is built on గాయం, "a wound", which goes back to Sanskrit *ghāta*, "a blow, a bruise" (CDIAL 4460), with *paracu*, which makes a verb of a noun.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
