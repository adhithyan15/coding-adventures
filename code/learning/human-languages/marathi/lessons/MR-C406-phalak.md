## You'll want to know: फलक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C406-PADPATH406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फलक** — *phalak* — "a signboard; a notice board".

> दुकानावर नवीन फलक लावला आहे.
>
> *dukānāvar navīn phalak lāvlā āhe.*
>
> A new signboard has been put up on the shop.

Masculine.

फलक लावणे, to put up a sign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फलक** (*phalak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *phalaka*, "a board, a plank" (Monier-Williams).
