# লেনদেন (lenden) — transactions; dealings (in money)

## You'll want to know: লেনদেন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C415-SUD415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**লেনদেন** — *lenden* — "transactions; dealings (in money)".

> আজ ব্যাংকে অনেক লেনদেন হল।
>
> *āj bængke ônek lenden hôlô.*
>
> There were a lot of transactions at the bank today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লেনদেন** (*lenden*) pairs লেন, "taking", built on **নেওয়া**, to take, with দেন, "giving", built on দেওয়া, to give: taking and giving, the whole business of money.
