## You'll want to know: સમ્રાટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C324-SAMRAT324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સમ્રાટ** — *samrāṭ* — "an emperor".

> સમ્રાટ પાસે મોટી સેના હતી.
>
> *samrāṭ pāse moṭī senā hatī.*
>
> The emperor had a large army.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમ્રાટ** (*samrāṭ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *samrāj*, "a supreme ruler" (Monier-Williams), whose nominative is *samrāṭ*: a king above kings. His realm is the **સામ્રાજ્ય**.
