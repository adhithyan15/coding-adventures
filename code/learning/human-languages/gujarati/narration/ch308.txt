Lesson 1 of 6.
તરાપો (tarāpo) — a raft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for safe and sound, then the Gujarati for phew.

You'll want to know: તરાપો.
તરાપો — tarāpo — "a raft".
પૂરમાં ગામના લોકોએ એક તરાપો બનાવ્યો. pūrmā̃ gāmnā lokoe ek tarāpo banāvyo. During the flood the villagers built a raft.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તરાપો (tarāpo) comes from tarpa, "a raft", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, a raft of crossed timbers (CDIAL 5726). It is masculine: તરાપો બનાવ્યો, built a raft; તરાપા પર, on the raft.

Lesson 2 of 6.
સાહસ (sāhas) — daring; an adventure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for phew, then the Gujarati for a raft.

You'll want to know: સાહસ.
સાહસ — sāhas — "daring; an adventure".
નદીમાં કૂદીને અરુણે મોટું સાહસ કર્યું. nadīmā̃ kūdīne Aruṇe moṭũ sāhas karyũ. By jumping into the river Arun showed great daring.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સાહસ (sāhas) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sāhasa, "boldness, rashness" (CDIAL 13378). Gujarati makes it good: daring, and the bold deed itself: સાહસ કર્યું.

ગામના લોકો તેને વીર કહે છે. gāmnā loko tene vīr kahe che. The villagers call him a hero.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વીર (vīr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vīra, "a man, a hero" (CDIAL 12056). It is the brave one in war stories and in everyday courage alike.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ધીરજ (dhīraj) — patience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for daring, then the Gujarati for a hero.

You'll want to know: ધીરજ.
ધીરજ — dhīraj — "patience".
મીરાએ ધીરજથી રાહ જોઈ. Mīrāe dhīrajthī rāh joī. Mira waited patiently.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધીરજ (dhīraj) probably goes back to Sanskrit dhairya, "firmness, calmness, patience" (Monier-Williams), the noun of dhīra, "steady"; CDIAL lists Gujarati dhīr, "patience", under dhīra but not this longer form (CDIAL 6817). It is kept: ધીરજ રાખો, be patient.

કરગરવું — kargarvũ — "to plead; to beg".
છોકરાની માતા લોકોને મદદ માટે કરગરતી હતી. chokrānī mātā lokone madad māṭe kargartī hatī. The boy's mother was pleading with people for help.
Past (she …): તે માતા કરગરી, te mātā karagrī.

The word, taken apart.
કરગરવું (kargarvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. The person you plead with takes ને: લોકોને કરગરતી હતી.
