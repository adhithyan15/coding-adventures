## You'll want to know: rifornire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C408-BENZINAIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rifornire** — "to refuel; to supply".

> Dobbiamo rifornire la macchina prima del viaggio.
>
> *We need to refuel the car before the trip.*

*Il rifornimento* is refuelling. Conjugated like *finire*: *rifornisco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rifornire* is built from *ri-* 'again' and *fornire* 'to supply', which is usually traced to a Germanic verb; it was borrowed into Italian through Old French *fornir*.
