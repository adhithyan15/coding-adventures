Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 19: Cheap and Goods: No New Signs.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Hear sastā — cheap.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the three-word shopping payoff, say price, write ु, दुकान, वस्तु, भाव, and भाई, then say heat.

You'll want to know.
sastā — cheap; inexpensive.
Say the two beats once from a low-price cue. This is the source's dictionary form; full bargaining phrases remain later work.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What does sastā say about a price?


Lesson 2 of 7.
सस्ता (sastā) — cheap on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 25 seconds]
Say cheap and price. Write भाव, वस्तु, बरसात, and पति, say sister, then recall ् and त.

You'll want to know.
सस् + ता becomes सस्ता — sastā — cheap.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write सस्ता (sastā). Check the middle consonant join and final long ा.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From sastā, write cheap.


Lesson 3 of 7.
Hear sāmān — goods.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Say and write cheap and shop, recall item and price, then write home, sister, and गर्मी.

You'll want to know.
sāmān — goods; merchandise.
Hear the two long-vowel beats and say the word once from a group-of-items cue.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What does sāmān name in a shop?


Lesson 4 of 7.
सामान (sāmān) — goods on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 25 seconds]
Say goods and cheap. Write सस्ता (sastā), दुकान, वस्तु, मौसम, and नाम, retrieve the four-word family payoff, then form ा once.

You'll want to know.
सा + मान becomes सामान — sāmān — goods.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write सामान (sāmān). Check both long ा marks and the final न.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From sāmān, write goods.


Lesson 5 of 7.
Retrieve cheap and goods — cheap and goods.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the three-word shopping payoff, then write ु, ौ, ग, and ड, then say paternal grandfather and cold.

Guided Practice.
Hear the two words in both orders, give each meaning, read two cards, then write both from sound.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9; Unicode Devanagari chart.
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Write cheap and goods in order.


Lesson 6 of 7.
Payoff — five shopping words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the earlier shopping and weather payoffs, say money, then write द and ड.

Guided Practice.
Identify five heard words.
Produce five words from meaning cues.
Match five printed cards to meanings.
Write all five heard words without a model.
Pass each skill separately; a strong word cannot compensate for a missed one.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 42 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write shop, item, price, cheap, and goods.


Lesson 7 of 7.
Close the five-word shopping loop — close the five-word shopping loop.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Write short ु, then ask and answer the known name and wellbeing exchanges, then write दादा and ठंडी.

Guided Practice.
Without a model, hear two words, say two from meaning cues, read the fifth, then write all five in a fresh mixed order.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9; Unicode Devanagari chart.
[question — say your answer, then pause 42 seconds]
After the four-skill pass, write all five shopping words without a model.
