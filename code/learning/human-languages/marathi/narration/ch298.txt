Marathi, chapter 298: Uproar in the Street.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
किंचाळणे (kiñcāḷṇe) — to scream; to shriek.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to loot, then the Marathi for to fleece.

You'll want to know: किंचाळणे.
किंचाळणे — kiñcāḷṇe — "to scream; to shriek".
साप बघून ती जोरात किंचाळली. sāp baghūn tī jorāt kiñcāḷlī. She screamed loudly when she saw the snake.
Past (he …): तो किंचाळला, to kiñcāḷlā.
Intransitive: जोरात किंचाळणे (kiñcāḷṇe), to scream aloud.

The word, taken apart.
किंचाळणे (kiñcāḷṇe) is a Marathi verb of crying out; CDIAL gathers the Marathi word for a shriek under the cry-word kīkkati, 'screams' (CDIAL 3192), but the verb itself is not listed and its origin is not settled.

गोंधळ — gondhaḷ — "commotion; confusion".
वीज गेल्यावर सभागृहात एकच गोंधळ झाला. vīj gelyāvar sabhāgṛhāt ekac gondhaḷ jhālā. When the power went out, the hall was in total commotion.
Masculine.
गोंधळ (gondhaḷ) होणे, a commotion breaks out; गोंधळात पडणे, to be confused.

The word, taken apart.
गोंधळ (gondhaḷ) is the Marathi word for an uproar or a muddle; its origin is not settled.

You'll want to know: हाहाकार.
हाहाकार — hāhākār — "havoc; an outcry".
पुरामुळे गावात हाहाकार माजला. purāmuḷe gāvāt hāhākār mājlā. The flood caused havoc in the village.
Masculine.
हाहाकार (hāhākār) माजणे, havoc breaks out.

The word, taken apart.
हाहाकार (hāhākār) is built from the cry of distress hā hā and Sanskrit kāra, 'a making, a doing' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
जमाव (jamāv) — a mob; a gathered crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for commotion, then the Marathi for havoc.

You'll want to know: जमाव.
जमाव — jamāv — "a mob; a gathered crowd".
अपघाताच्या ठिकाणी मोठा जमाव जमला. apghātācyā ṭhikāṇī moṭhā jamāv jamlā. A large crowd gathered at the scene of the accident.
Masculine.
Built on जमणे, to gather. गर्दी is any crowd; जमाव (jamāv) is one that has gathered round something.

The word, taken apart.
जमाव (jamāv) is built on jamṇe, 'to gather', which CDIAL lists, as Marathi 'to collect', under Sanskrit yamyate (CDIAL 10428).

वर्गात दोन मुली हळू आवाजात कुजबुजत होत्या. vargāt don mulī haḷū āvājāt kujbujat hotyā. Two girls were whispering softly in class.

The word, taken apart.
कुजबुजणे (kujbujṇe) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled sound, kuj-buj; its origin is not settled.
