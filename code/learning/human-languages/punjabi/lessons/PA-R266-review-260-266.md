[PAUSE 3s] Plus a quarter? (**ਸਵਾ**, *savā*.) Plus a half? (**ਸਾਢੇ**, *sāḍhe*.) Less a quarter? (**ਪੌਣੇ**, *pauṇe*.) One and a half? (**ਡੇਢ**, *ḍeḍh*.) Two and a half? (**ਢਾਈ**, *ḍhāī*.) Half? (**ਅੱਧਾ**, *addhā*.) A railway platform? (**ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ**, *paleṭfāram*.) To halt? (**ਠਹਿਰਨਾ**, *ṭhahirnā*.) To set off? (**ਚੱਲਣਾ**, *challṇā*.) Departing? (**ਰਵਾਨਾ**, *ravānā*.) A fee? (**ਫ਼ੀਸ**, *fīs*.) A fare? (**ਕਿਰਾਇਆ**, *kirāiā*.) A fine? (**ਜੁਰਮਾਨਾ**, *jurmānā*.) A receipt? (**ਰਸੀਦ**, *rasīd*.) To pay off? (**ਚੁਕਾਉਣਾ**, *chukāuṇā*.) A bend in the road? (**ਮੋੜ**, *moṛ*.) To turn? (**ਮੁੜਨਾ**, *muṛnā*.) A side? (**ਪਾਸਾ**, *pāsā*.) Right-hand? (**ਸੱਜਾ**, *sajjā*.) Left-hand? (**ਖੱਬਾ**, *khabbā*.) Across? (**ਪਾਰ**, *pār*.) To pass through? (**ਲੰਘਣਾ**, *laṅghṇā*.) A big gate? (**ਫਾਟਕ**, *phāṭak*.) A pole? (**ਖੰਭਾ**, *khambhā*.) A corner? (**ਕੋਨਾ**, *konā*.) On this side? (**ਉਰੇ**, *ure*.) Farther off? (**ਪਰੇ**, *pare*.) In the middle? (**ਵਿਚਕਾਰ**, *vichkār*.) The distance to travel? (**ਵਾਟ**, *vāṭ*.) A kos? (**ਕੋਹ**, *koh*.) A neighbourhood? (**ਮਹੱਲਾ**, *mahallā*.) A small town? (**ਕਸਬਾ**, *kasbā*.) A small shop? (**ਹੱਟੀ**, *haṭṭī*.) A roadside eatery? (**ਢਾਬਾ**, *ḍhābā*.) A building? (**ਇਮਾਰਤ**, *imārat*.)
