## You'll want to know: zerstören
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C295-PANNE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**zerstören** — "to destroy".

> Das Feuer zerstörte das ganze Haus.
>
> *The fire destroyed the whole house.*

*Die Zerstörung* — the destruction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zerstören* is built on *stören* "to disturb", Middle High German *stören* (EtymDB), with *zer-* "apart, to pieces".
