# बंद (band) — closed

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C74-SIGNS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *khulā*, then write **खुला**: ख with ु beneath it, then ल, then ा.

## You'll want to know: बंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C74-SIGNS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बंद** (*band*) — "closed".

Persian بند (*band*), "tied, shut", and its family is enormous. The ancient root
meant "to bind", and English *bind*, *band* and *bond* are its western cousins. A
closed door is a **tied** door.

Two things to keep straight. First, बंद is not the beginning of **बंदर**, the
monkey you met among the animals — the eye will want to join them and they are
unrelated. Second, बंद stands opposite खुला on exactly the same signboards, so
learn them as one pair and you have read half a street.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The pair every shutter in the country is painted with.
