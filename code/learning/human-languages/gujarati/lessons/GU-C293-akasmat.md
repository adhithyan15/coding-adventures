## You'll want to know: અકસ્માત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C293-AKASMAT293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અકસ્માત** — *akasmāt* — "an accident".

> ગઈકાલે સાંજે પુલ પાસે મોટો અકસ્માત થયો.
>
> *gaīkāle sā̃je pul pāse moṭo akasmāt thayo.*
>
> Yesterday evening there was a big accident near the bridge.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અકસ્માત** (*akasmāt*) is Sanskrit *akasmāt*, "without a why or a wherefore, suddenly" (Monier-Williams), used in Gujarati as a noun: what happens without warning. It is masculine: **અકસ્માત થયો**, there was an accident.
