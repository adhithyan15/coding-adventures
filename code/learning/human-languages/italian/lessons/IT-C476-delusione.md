## You'll want to know: la delusione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C476-DELUSIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la delusione** — "disappointment".

> Non passare l'esame è stata una grande delusione.
>
> *Failing the exam was a big disappointment.*

*Una delusione d'amore* — a heartbreak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delusione* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *dēlūsiō* "a deceiving" (Lewis & Short), from *dēlūdere*; in Italian it came to mean the feeling when hopes are let down.
