## You'll want to know: 最后
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C278-ZUIHOU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**最后** — *zuìhòu* — "last; finally; in the end".

> **最后我们回家了** *zuìhòu wǒmen huí jiā le* — In the end we went home.

> **今天是最后一天** *jīntiān shì zuìhòu yì tiān* — Today is the last day.

The next lesson puts the first character in your hand. It is the *zuì* you already say for most.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**最后** (*zuìhòu*) is built from 最 'most' and 后 'after', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tswajH* and *huwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'utmost' and lists the second, under its traditional form, with 'before and after'; 'last' for the pair is the word's own sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-04, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-COMPLETION-01] -->

最后 opens the last step of a story: **最后我们回家了** *zuìhòu wǒmen huí jiā le*. Before a noun it picks out the last one: **最后一天** *zuìhòu yì tiān*.

The 了 at the end is the 了 you know: it happened.
