## You'll want to know: هدد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C324-KHATAR324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**هدد** — *haddada* — "to threaten".

> هدد الرجل جاره بالشرطة.
>
> *haddada ar-rajul jārahu bi-sh-shurṭa.*
>
> The man threatened his neighbour with the police.

Present: يهدد, *yuhaddid*.

The danger itself is تهديد.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هدد** (*haddada*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-d-d* (Lane), "to pull down a building". Form II *haddada*, to threaten, is usually explained as threatening with that ruin.
