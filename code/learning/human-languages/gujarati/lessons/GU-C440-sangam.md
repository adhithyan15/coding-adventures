## You'll want to know: સંગમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C440-STREAM440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સંગમ** — *sangam* — "a confluence (of rivers)".

> બે નદીઓના સંગમ પર એક મંદિર છે.
>
> *be nadīonā sangam par ek mandir che.*
>
> There is a temple where the two rivers meet.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંગમ** (*sangam*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṁgama*, "a coming together, a meeting" (Monier-Williams); a place where rivers meet is often a holy place.
