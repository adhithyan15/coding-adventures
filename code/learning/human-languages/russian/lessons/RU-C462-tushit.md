## You'll want to know: тушить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C462-KOSTYOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**тушить** (*tushít*) — "to put out (a fire); to stew".

> Перед сном мы тушим костёр водой.
>
> *Péred snom my túshim kostyór vodóy.*
>
> Before going to sleep we put out the campfire with water.

Imperfective; perfective *потушить*. Present *тушу, тушишь*. In cooking: *тушить мясо* — to stew meat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тушить* goes with *тухнуть* 'to go out (of a fire)'. EtymDB lists *тушить* without a source, so the deeper origin is left open.
