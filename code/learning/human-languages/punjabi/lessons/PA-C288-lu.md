## You'll want to know: ਲੂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C288-HEAT288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੂ** — *lū* — "a hot wind; the summer heatwave".

> ਲੂ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਹਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *lū vich asīṁ bāhar nahīṁ jānde hāṁ.*
>
> We do not go out in the hot wind.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੂ** (*lū*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*lūṣā*, "burning"; CDIAL lists Punjabi only *lūh*, "burnt, scalded", under it; the sense "hot wind" is the one it gives for Lahnda *lō* (CDIAL 11099).
