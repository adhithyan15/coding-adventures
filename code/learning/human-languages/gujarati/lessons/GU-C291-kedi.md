## You'll want to know: કેદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C291-GHERVU291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કેદી** — *kedī* — "a prisoner; a captive".

> જો પોલીસ સમયસર પહોંચી ગઈ તો કેદી બચી જશે.
>
> *jo polīs samaysar pahõcī gaī to kedī bacī jaśe.*
>
> If the police get there in time, the captive will be saved.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કેદી** (*kedī*) is usually traced to Persian *qaidī*, "a prisoner", from *qaid*, captivity, a word Persian took from Arabic. It is used of a man or a woman.
