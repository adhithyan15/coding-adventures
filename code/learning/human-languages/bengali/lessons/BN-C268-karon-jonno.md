# … কারণ … / -এর জন্য (kārôn / -er jônyô) — giving the reason

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C268-KARON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-I-THINK-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'll stay at home, because there's a storm outside". (*āmi bāṛite thākbô, kārôn bāire jhôṛ hôchchhe*.)

## You'll want to know: because, with a sentence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C268-KARON-BECAUSE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C268-KARON-01, BN-LEX-C18-KENONA-01, BN-LEX-C137-QUAL137-04, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-NOT-01] -->

> **আজ আমি যাব না, কারণ আমি ক্লান্ত।**
>
> *āj āmi jābô nā, kārôn āmi klāntô.* — "I won't go today, because I'm tired."

A whole reason, with its own verb or a describing word such as **ক্লান্ত**, follows **কারণ**. **কেননা**, which you know, does the same job in the same place: **আজ আমি যাব না, কেননা আমি ক্লান্ত**. To ask for one, **কেন?** (*kenô?*), "why?", the word inside **কেননা**.

## You'll want to know: because of, for
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C268-JONNO-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-04, BN-LEX-C156-SOMOY156-05, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

For a reason that is only a noun, Bengali puts the noun in its **-র** form and adds **জন্য** (*jônyô*): **-এর জন্য**, "because of".

> **ঝড়ের জন্য ট্রেন দেরিতে এল।**
>
> *jhôṛer jônyô ṭren derite elô.* — "The train came late because of the storm."

The same **জন্য** is "for": **এটা তোমার জন্য** (*eṭā tomār jônyô*), "this is for you". After a vowel the ending is **-র**: **মীরার জন্য**.

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "Why? Because I'm tired." — *kenô? kārôn āmi klāntô.*]
- [YOU SAY: "The train came late because of the storm" — *jhôṛer jônyô ṭren derite elô*]
- [YOU SAY: "This is for you" — *eṭā tomār jônyô*]
