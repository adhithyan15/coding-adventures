Punjabi, chapter 330: Brothers' and Sisters' Spouses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਭਰਜਾਈ (bharjāī) — a brother's wife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a son-in-law, then the Punjabi for a child's father-in-law.

You'll want to know: ਭਰਜਾਈ.
ਭਰਜਾਈ — bharjāī — "a brother's wife".
ਮੇਰੀ ਭਰਜਾਈ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਸਿਖਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। merī bharjāī sakūl vich bachchiāṁ nū̃ panjābī sikhāundī hai. My brother's wife teaches Punjabi to children at school.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਭਰਜਾਈ (bharjāī) is inherited from Sanskrit bhrāturjāyā, "a brother's wife"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bharjāī, "brother's wife", under it (CDIAL 9660).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a child's father-in-law, then the Punjabi for a brother's wife.

ਮੇਰਾ ਜੀਜਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਹੱਸਦਾ ਹੈ। merā jījā bahut hassdā hai. My sister's husband laughs a lot.

ਜੀਜਾ (jījā) is inherited from a reconstructed jījja, "a nursery word for breast, mother, sister, relative"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jījā, "sister's husband", under it (CDIAL 5232).
