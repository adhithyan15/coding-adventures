# ਖਾਂਦਾ, ਪੀਂਦਾ — eats, drinks

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-LEX-KHANA, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I go to school every day*. Then **ਖਾਣਾ** and **ਪੀਣਾ**.

## Grammar Lens: ਖਾ and ਪੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KHANA, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-02, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211] -->

**ਖਾ** and **ਪੀ** end in vowels too, so they work like **ਜਾ**: **ਖਾਂਦਾ**,
**ਖਾਂਦੀ**; **ਪੀਂਦਾ**, **ਪੀਂਦੀ**.

> **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** — *maiṁ roz roṭī khāndā hāṁ.* — I eat roti every day (a man speaking).

> **ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹੈ।** — *uh roz duddh pīndī hai.* — She drinks milk every day.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-LEX-ROTI, PA-LEX-DUDH, PA-LEX-PAANI, PA-LEX-CHA] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I drink water every day, as a woman*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *he eats roti every day*] Say **ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਪੀਂਦਾ**] Point to **ਂ**, then say *pīndā*.
- [YOU WRITE: copy **ਖਾਂਦਾ** once]

## Wrap-up Recall
