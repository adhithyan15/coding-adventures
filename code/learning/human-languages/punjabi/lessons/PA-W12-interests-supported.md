# Two interests, one familiar frame

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-PASAND, PA-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-LIKING] -->

The old liking frame begins **ਮੈਨੂੰ** and ends **ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ**. Name the two
already taught things Aman likes: Punjabi and reading.

## Reading — change only the middle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-PASAND, PA-LEX-PARHNA] -->

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੜ੍ਹਨਾ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।

The frame does not change. The first middle is a language; the second is
reading. **ਪੜ੍ਹਨਾ** was taught earlier, including its joined spelling.

## Writing — supported interest block
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-LIKING, PA-LEX-PARHNA] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy both short sentences. Point to **ਮੈਨੂੰ** and **ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ** in each, then
check the one word that changed. Do not invent a new interest for this pass.
