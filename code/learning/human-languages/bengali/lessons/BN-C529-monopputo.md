# মনঃপূত (mônôppūtô) — pleasing; to one's liking

## You'll want to know: মনঃপূত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C529-DOTANA529-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মনঃপূত** — *mônôppūtô* — "pleasing; to one's liking".

> জামাটা মনঃপূত হলে আমি সেটা কিনব।
>
> *Jāmāṭā mônôppūtô hôle āmi seṭā kinbô.*
>
> If the shirt is to my liking, I'll buy it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মনঃপূত** (*mônôppūtô*) is Sanskrit *manaḥpūta*, "pure in mind" (Monier-Williams): *manas*, "mind", and *pūta*, "purified"; the sense "to one's liking" is Bengali's own.
