## You'll want to know: ઉપાય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C373-UPAY373-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપાય** — *upāy* — "a way out".

> જો આ ઉપાય કામ કરશે તો તાવ જલદી ઊતરશે.
>
> *jo ā upāy kām karśe to tāv jaldī ūtarśe.*
>
> If this remedy works, the fever will soon come down.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપાય** (*upāy*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *upāya*, "coming near, a means, a way" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
