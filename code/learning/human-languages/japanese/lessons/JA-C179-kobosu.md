## You'll want to know: こぼす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C179-DRINK179-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こぼす** — *kobosu* — "to spill; to drop".

> **こどもが ぎゅうにゅうを こぼします。** — *kodomo ga gyūnyū o koboshimasu* — "The child spills milk."

Polite **こぼします**, *koboshimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こぼす** (*kobosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
