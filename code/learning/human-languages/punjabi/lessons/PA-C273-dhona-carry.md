## You'll want to know: ਢੋਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C273-WEIGH273-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੋਣਾ** — *ḍhoṇā* — "to carry a load".

> ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਤੋਂ ਸਬਜ਼ੀ ਘਰ ਢੋਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ bāzār ton sabzī ghar ḍhondā hāṁ.*
>
> I carry the vegetables home from the market.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਲੂ ਢੋਏ, *maiṁ ālū ḍhoe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੋਣਾ** (*ḍhoṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ḍhaukayati*, "brings near"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhoṇā*, "to carry, convey", under it (CDIAL 5610).
