## You'll want to know: బలహీనత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-01]; assesses=[] -->

**బలహీనత** — *balahīnata* — "frailty; weakness".

> జ్వరం తరువాత కొన్ని రోజులు బలహీనత ఉంటుంది.
>
> *jvaraṁ taruvāta konni rōjulu balahīnata uṇṭundi.*
>
> After a fever there is weakness for a few days.

బలహీనంగా, weak; బలం, strength, + హీన, lacking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బలహీనత** (*balahīnata*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *bala*, "strength" (CDIAL 9161), and *hīna*, "lacking" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త.
