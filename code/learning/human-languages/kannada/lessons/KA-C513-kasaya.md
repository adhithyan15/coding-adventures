## You'll want to know: ಕಷಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C513-CHETARIKE513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಷಾಯ** — *kaṣāya* — "a herbal decoction".

> ಶೀತಕ್ಕೆ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಶುಂಠಿಯ ಕಷಾಯ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *śītakke ajji śuṇṭhiya kaṣāya māḍidaru.*
>
> Grandmother made a ginger decoction for the cold.

Boiled from ginger, pepper or tulasi leaves and drunk hot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಷಾಯ** (*kaṣāya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kaṣāya*, "astringent; an astringent juice" (CDIAL 2974).
