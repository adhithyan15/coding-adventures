# करिष्यामि (kariṣyāmi) — I will do

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-KAROMI, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “I do,” **करोमि**, then “I will go,” **गमिष्यामि**.

## You'll want to know: करिष्यामि

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**करिष्यामि** — *kariṣyāmi* — means “I will do” or “I will make.” Keep the
whole future form together: **करिष्यामि**.

## Taken Apart

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01] -->

**करिष्यामि** comes from the root **कृ** (*kṛ*), “do, make,” already met inside
**करोमि**. The future stem appears as **करिष्य-** (*kariṣya-*), followed here
by first-person **-आमि**. Whitney, *Sanskrit Grammar* §§932–935, describes the
Sanskrit s-future and its **-iṣya- / -sya-** formation.
