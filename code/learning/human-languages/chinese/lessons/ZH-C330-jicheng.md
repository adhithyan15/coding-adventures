## You'll want to know: jìchéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C330-KING330-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jìchéng* — "to inherit; to take over (from a parent)".

> *tā èrshí suì de shíhou jìchéng le tā bàba de shūdiàn* — When he was twenty he took over his father's bookshop.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìchéng* is built from *jì* and *chéng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kejH* and *dzying* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to carry on' and the second 'to receive'.
