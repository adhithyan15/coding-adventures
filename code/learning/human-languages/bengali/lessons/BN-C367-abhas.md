## You'll want to know: আভাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C367-ANUMAN367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আভাস** — *ābhās* — "a hint; an indication".

> আকাশ কালো হলে আমরা ঝড়ের আভাস পাই।
>
> *ākāsh kālo hôle āmrā jhôṛer ābhās pāi.*
>
> When the sky turns dark, we get a hint of a storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আভাস** (*ābhās*) is Sanskrit *ābhāsa*, appearance (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the faint appearance of something still to come, a hint.
