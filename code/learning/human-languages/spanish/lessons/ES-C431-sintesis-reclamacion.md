# (una reclamación) — the whole transaction, gone wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-01, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You are about to read a real notice of the kind a shop sends. Before
you do, say *el pedido* and *el retraso* once each.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-01, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-03, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-04, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-05] -->

Read it once without stopping, then answer.

> Estimado cliente: su **pedido** número 3182 sufrirá un **retraso** de tres
> días por un problema con nuestro **proveedor**. No se le **cobrará** el envío.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: how many days late it is]
- [YOU SAY: whose problem the shop says it is]
- [YOU SAY: whether you will be charged for delivery]

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-02, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-05, ES-LEX-PAGAR] -->

Now the other direction. Say aloud, as if on the telephone:

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: my order is arriving with a delay of three days]
- [YOU SAY: I paid on Monday and nobody has charged me for the delivery]
- [YOU SAY: if it does not arrive this week I want to return it]

**Four sentences ago you could not have said any of that.** The transaction you
could describe stopped at the moment of paying; it now runs all the way to the
complaint.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-01, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-02, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Your order? (**mi pedido**.) With a delay? (**con retraso**.) And if
you want to send it back? (**devolver**.)
