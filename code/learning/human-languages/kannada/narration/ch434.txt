Kannada, chapter 434: A Late Train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಿಳಂಬ (viḷamba) — a delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a rail track, then the Kannada for to reach.

You'll want to know: ವಿಳಂಬ.
ವಿಳಂಬ — viḷamba — "a delay".
ಮಳೆಯ ಕಾರಣ ರೈಲು ಎರಡು ಗಂಟೆ ವಿಳಂಬವಾಯಿತು. maḷeya kāraṇa railu eraḍu gaṇṭe viḷambavāyitu. Because of the rain the train was two hours late.
With ಆಗು it says that something ran late: ವಿಳಂಬವಾಯಿತು, it was delayed. In speech people say ತಡವಾಯಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಳಂಬ (viḷamba) is borrowed from Sanskrit vilamba, "delay" (CDIAL 11890), from the root lamb, "to hang down, to lag behind". Kannada writes its l as ಳ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಆಗಮಿಸು (āgamisu) — to arrive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to reach, then the Kannada for a delay.

You'll want to know: ಆಗಮಿಸು.
ಆಗಮಿಸು — āgamisu — "to arrive".
ವಿಮಾನ ಸಮಯಕ್ಕೆ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ಆಗಮಿಸಿತು. vimāna samayakke sariyāgi āgamisitu. The plane arrived right on time.
Past (I …): ಆಗಮಿಸಿದೆ, āgamiside.
A formal verb of notices and announcements; in speech people say ಬಂದಿತು, it came.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಗಮಿಸು (āgamisu) is built from Sanskrit āgama, "coming, arrival" (Monier-Williams), from ā- "towards" and gam "to go", with the Kannada ending -isu that makes a verb of a Sanskrit noun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು (nirgamisu) — to depart; to leave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a delay, then the Kannada for to arrive.

You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು.
ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು — nirgamisu — "to depart; to leave".
ರೈಲು ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸುತ್ತದೆ. railu beḷagge āru gaṇṭege nirgamisuttade. The train departs at six in the morning.
Past (I …): ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸಿದೆ, nirgamiside.
Its partner is ಆಗಮಿಸು (āgamisu), to arrive; station boards pair the two.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು (nirgamisu) is built from Sanskrit nirgama, "departure, a way out" (CDIAL 7294), from nis- "out" and gam "to go", with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಿಲುಗಡೆ (nilugaḍe) — a halt; a stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to arrive, then the Kannada for to depart.

You'll want to know: ನಿಲುಗಡೆ.
ನಿಲುಗಡೆ — nilugaḍe — "a halt; a stop".
ಈ ರೈಲಿಗೆ ಆ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲುಗಡೆ ಇಲ್ಲ. ī railige ā ūrinalli nilugaḍe illa. This train has no stop in that town.
Built on ನಿಲ್ಲು, to stop: a bus stop or a parking place can also be a ನಿಲುಗಡೆ (nilugaḍe).

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಲುಗಡೆ (nilugaḍe) is a native Kannada noun built on nil, nilu, "to stand, to stop" (DEDR 3675). Where the ending -gaḍe comes from is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ವಿಚಾರಿಸು (vicārisu) — to enquire; to ask after.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to depart, then the Kannada for a halt.

You'll want to know: ವಿಚಾರಿಸು.
ವಿಚಾರಿಸು — vicārisu — "to enquire; to ask after".
ನಾನು ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದಲ್ಲಿ ರೈಲಿನ ಸಮಯ ವಿಚಾರಿಸಿದೆ. nānu nildāṇadalli railina samaya vicāriside. I enquired about the train times at the station.
Past (I …): ವಿಚಾರಿಸಿದೆ, vicāriside.
What is asked about takes -ಅನ್ನು or no ending; ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ವಿಚಾರಿಸು (vicārisu), to ask after someone's health.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಚಾರಿಸು (vicārisu) is built from Sanskrit vicāra, "deliberation, examination, investigation" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu. The noun ವಿಚಾರ, an idea or a matter, comes from the same Sanskrit word.
