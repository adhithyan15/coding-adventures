## You'll want to know: il fabbro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C455-ARTIGIANI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il fabbro** — "the locksmith; the blacksmith".

> Ho perso le chiavi e ho chiamato il fabbro.
>
> *I lost my keys and called the locksmith.*

Today mostly the locksmith; once the blacksmith.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fabbro* is inherited from Latin *faber, fabrum* 'craftsman, smith' (EtymDB).
