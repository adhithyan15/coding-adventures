## You'll want to know: যকৃৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C468-HRITPINDO468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**যকৃৎ** — *jôkrit* — "the liver".

> দাদুর যকৃৎ দুর্বল।
>
> *dādur jôkrit durbôl.*
>
> Grandfather's liver is weak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যকৃৎ** (*jôkrit*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *yakṛt*, "the liver" (Monier-Williams), which the dictionary compares with Latin *jecur*.
