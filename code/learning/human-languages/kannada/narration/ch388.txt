Kannada, chapter 388: Right and Wrong.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನೈತಿಕ (naitika) — ethical; moral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a priority, then the Kannada for a value.

You'll want to know: ನೈತಿಕ.
ನೈತಿಕ — naitika — "ethical; moral".
ಇದು ಹಣದ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ ಅಲ್ಲ, ನೈತಿಕ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ. idu haṇada praśne alla, naitika praśne. This is not a question of money but a moral one.
ನೈತಿಕತೆ, morality.

The word, taken apart.
ನೈತಿಕ (naitika) is built on Sanskrit nīti, "conduct, right behaviour" (CDIAL 7546), with the adjective ending -ika.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
