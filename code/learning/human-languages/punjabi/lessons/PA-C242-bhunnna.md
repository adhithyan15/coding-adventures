# ਭੁੰਨਣਾ (bhunnṇā) — to roast; to parch

## You'll want to know: ਭੁੰਨਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C242-KAM242-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭੁੰਨਣਾ** — *bhunnṇā* — "to roast; to parch".

> ਸਰਦੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਮੂੰਗਫਲੀ ਭੁੰਨਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *sardī vich asīṁ mū̃gphalī bhunnde hāṁ.*
>
> In winter we roast peanuts.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੱਕੀ ਭੁੰਨੀ, *maiṁ makkī bhunnī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭੁੰਨਣਾ** (*bhunnṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*bhṛgṇa*, "fried, roasted"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhunnaṇā*, "to parch, fry" (CDIAL 9577).
