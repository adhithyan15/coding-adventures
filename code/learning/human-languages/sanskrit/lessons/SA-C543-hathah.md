## You'll want to know: हठः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C543-HATHA543-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हठः** — *haṭhaḥ* — "obstinacy".

> यदि बालः हठं करोति, तर्हि माता कुपिता भवति।
>
> *yadi bālaḥ haṭhaṃ karoti, tarhi mātā kupitā bhavati.*
>
> If the child is stubborn, my mother gets angry.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हठः** (*haṭhaḥ*) is the word MW gives for violence, force, and obstinacy, pertinacity; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
