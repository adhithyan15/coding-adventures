# પરોપકાર (paropkār) — doing good to others

## You'll want to know: પરોપકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C358-SEVA358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પરોપકાર** — *paropkār* — "doing good to others".

> પરોપકાર એ સૌથી મોટો ગુણ છે.
>
> *paropkār e sauthī moṭo guṇ che.*
>
> Doing good to others is the greatest virtue.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરોપકાર** (*paropkār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *paropakāra*, "assisting others, benevolence" (Monier-Williams), built from *para*, "another", and *upakāra*, "help".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
