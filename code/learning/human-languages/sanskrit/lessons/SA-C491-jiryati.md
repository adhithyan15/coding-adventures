## You'll want to know: जीर्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C491-JARA491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जीर्यति** — *jīryati* — "wears out; grows old".

> वस्त्रं कालेन जीर्यति।
>
> *vastraṃ kālena jīryati.*
>
> Cloth wears out with time.

Past (it …): वस्त्रं जीर्णवत्, *vastraṃ jīrṇavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जीर्यति** (*jīryati*) comes from the root √jṝ, "to grow old, to wear out", whose ppp *jīrṇa* is the A2 word **जीर्णः** (MW).
