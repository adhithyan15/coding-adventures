## You'll want to know: ढीठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C461-SANKOCH461-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ढीठ** — *ḍhīṭh* — "brazen; cheeky".

> वह बच्चा बहुत ढीठ है, डाँटने पर भी हँसता है।
>
> *vah baccā bahut ḍhīṭh hai, ḍā̃ṭne par bhī hãstā hai.*
>
> That child is very cheeky; he laughs even when he's scolded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढीठ** (*ḍhīṭh*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *ḍhīṭh*, "bold, impudent", under Sanskrit *dhṛṣṭá*, "bold" (CDIAL 6875).
