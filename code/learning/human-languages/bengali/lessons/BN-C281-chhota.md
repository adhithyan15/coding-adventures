## You'll want to know: ছোটা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C281-BHAGYO281-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ছোটা** — *chhoṭā* — "to run; to rush".

> আগুন দেখে লোকজন জল নিয়ে ছুটল।
>
> *āgun dekhe lokjôn jôl niye chhuṭlô.*
>
> When they saw the fire, people ran with water.

Past (he/she …): সেই কুকুর ছুটল, *sei kukur chhuṭlô*.

The stem vowel rises in the past: ছুটল, ran.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছোটা** (*chhoṭā*) is filed in CDIAL 3707 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *kṣuṭyatē*, 'is released', which lists Bengali *chuṭā*, 'to get loose, run away, run'. **ছুটি**, a holiday, a time let loose, is listed beside it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
