## You'll want to know: भजन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C277-BHAJAN277-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भजन** — *bhajan* — "a devotional song".

> दादी रोज भजन गावै है।
>
> *dādī roj bhajan gāvai hai.*
>
> Grandmother sings a hymn every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भजन** (*bhajan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhajana*, "reverence, worship" (Monier-Williams); in Marwadi it is the song of worship.
