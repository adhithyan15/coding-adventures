Punjabi, chapter 347: The Big Drum.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਢੋਲ (ḍhol) — a drum; a big two-sided drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for helpless, then the Punjabi for a pity.

You'll want to know: ਢੋਲ.
ਢੋਲ — ḍhol — "a drum; a big two-sided drum".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਢੋਲ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ। mainū̃ ḍhol dī āvāz bahut pasand hai. I really like the sound of the drum.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਢੋਲ (ḍhol) is inherited from Sanskrit ḍhola, "drum"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍhol, "drum", under it (CDIAL 5608).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਵਜਾਉਣਾ (vajāuṇā) — to play an instrument; to beat a drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a pity, then the Punjabi for a drum.

You'll want to know: ਵਜਾਉਣਾ.
ਵਜਾਉਣਾ — vajāuṇā — "to play an instrument; to beat a drum".
ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਢੋਲ ਵਜਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। merā bharā shām nū̃ ḍhol vajāundā hai. My brother plays the drum in the evening.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਢੋਲ ਵਜਾਇਆ, maiṁ ḍhol vajāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਜਾਉਣਾ (vajāuṇā) is the causative partner of vajjṇā, "to sound", which goes back to Sanskrit vādyate, "is made to sound"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vajjṇā under it (CDIAL 11513).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਥਾਪ (thāp) — a drumbeat; a beat of the hand on a drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to play an instrument, then the Punjabi for rhythm.

You'll want to know: ਥਾਪ.
ਥਾਪ — thāp — "a drumbeat; a beat of the hand on a drum".
ਢੋਲ ਦੀ ਥਾਪ ਦੂਰ ਤੱਕ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ। ḍhol dī thāp dūr tak jāndī hai. The beat of the drum carries a long way.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਥਾਪ (thāp) is inherited from a reconstructed thapp, "slap, pat"; CDIAL lists Punjabi thāp, "pat, slap", under it and marks it masculine (CDIAL 6091). CDIAL does not give the sense "drumbeat", and the feminine use taught here (the beat of the drum) is not recorded there.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਤਾੜੀ (tāṛī) — a clap; clapping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for rhythm, then the Punjabi for a drumbeat.

You'll want to know: ਤਾੜੀ.
ਤਾੜੀ — tāṛī — "a clap; clapping".
ਗੀਤ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਸਭ ਲੋਕ ਤਾੜੀਆਂ ਵਜਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ। gīt ton bāad sabh lok tāṛīāṁ vajāunde han. After the song everyone claps.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਾੜੀ (tāṛī) is inherited from Sanskrit tāla, "clap, rhythm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi tālī, "clapping", and Lahnda tāṛī, "clapping the hands", under it (CDIAL 5748).
