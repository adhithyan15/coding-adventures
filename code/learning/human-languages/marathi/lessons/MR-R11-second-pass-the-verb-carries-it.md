# आहे — one verb, reshaped for whoever is speaking

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-PHRASE-MAJHE-NAAV-AAHE, MR-GRAMMAR-POSSESSIVE-RESPECT] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Give your name, then ask for someone else's, respectfully. Both
sentences end on the same word.

## Grammar Lens: the verb carries the person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-COPULA-PERSON, MR-GRAMMAR-PLURAL-RESPECT-AGREEMENT, MR-GRAMMAR-ADJECTIVE-TAKES-PERSON-GENDER] -->

Three chapters have been quietly teaching one thing:

| | |
|---|---|
| **मी आहे** *mī āhe* | I am |
| **तू आहेस** *tū āhes* | you are, familiar |
| **तुम्ही आहात** *tumhī āhāt* | you are, respectful |

One verb, three shapes. And the agreement does not stop at the verb — **कसे**
in *तुम्ही कसे आहात?* is plural to match the respectful *तुम्ही*, and **बरा** in
*मी बरा आहे* takes the speaker's own gender. **The person and the gender spread
outward from the verb across the whole sentence.**

## What you've built — the first meeting, end to end
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-PHRASE-MAJHE-NAAV-AAHE, MR-GRAMMAR-POSSESSIVE-RESPECT, MR-LEX-ANAND, MR-PHRASE-MI-BARA-AAHE, MR-PHRASE-KAAHI-HARKAT-NAHI, MR-PHRASE-UDYA-BHETU] -->

Every sentence puts the verb **last**:

> — तुमचं नाव काय आहे? — *your name what is?*
> — माझं नाव … आहे. भेटून आनंद झाला.
> — तुम्ही कसे आहात? — मी बरा आहे.
> — धन्यवाद. — काही हरकत नाही.
> — उद्या भेटू.

**भेटू** is the one to notice at the end. It carries *we* inside it — no
separate word is needed — and unlike the present tense it does **not** split by
gender: everyone says *bheṭū*.

## You'll want to know: नाव, and a sound that leaned
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-M-TO-V-SOFTENING] -->

**नाव** came from a Sanskrit form with an *m* where Marathi now has a *v*.
Marathi softened many old consonants that way, and spotting the lean lets you
read a Sanskrit word straight into its Marathi shape.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-COPULA-PERSON, MR-GRAMMAR-ADJECTIVE-TAKES-PERSON-GENDER, MR-GRAMMAR-WE-LIVES-IN-THE-VERB, MR-PHRASE-KAAHI-HARKAT-NAHI, MR-LEX-ANAND, MR-GRAMMAR-M-TO-V-SOFTENING] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Where does a Marathi sentence put its verb? (**Last.**) What changes between *आहे*, *आहेस* and *आहात*? (**Who is being spoken to** — the verb carries it.) Which word inside *बरा* changes with the speaker? (**Its ending** — it takes your gender.) What hides inside **भेटू**? (***We***, with no separate word.) And what did the *m* of the older form become? (***V***.)
