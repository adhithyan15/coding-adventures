## You'll want to know: مرضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C530-MARZI530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مرضی** — *marzī* — "one's own choice; consent".

> اگر آپ کی مرضی ہے تو ہم چلیں گے۔
>
> *agar āp kī marzī hai to ham chaleṅ ge.*
>
> If you wish, we will go.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرضی** (*marzī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *marḍī*, "pleasing, approved".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
