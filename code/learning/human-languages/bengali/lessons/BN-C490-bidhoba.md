# বিধবা (bidhôbā) — a widow

## You'll want to know: বিধবা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C490-BICHCHHED490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিধবা** — *bidhôbā* — "a widow".

> যুদ্ধে স্বামীর মৃত্যুর পরে তিনি বিধবা হলেন।
>
> *juddhe svāmīr mrityur pôre tini bidhôbā hôlen.*
>
> After her husband died in the war, she was widowed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিধবা** (*bidhôbā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vidhavā*, "a husbandless woman, a widow" (Monier-Williams).
