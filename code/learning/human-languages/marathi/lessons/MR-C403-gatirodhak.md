## You'll want to know: गतिरोधक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C403-KHADDA403-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गतिरोधक** — *gatirodhak* — "a speed breaker".

> शाळेजवळ दोन गतिरोधक आहेत.
>
> *śāḷejavaḷ don gatirodhak āhet.*
>
> There are two speed breakers near the school.

Masculine.

The plural is the same: दोन गतिरोधक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गतिरोधक** (*gatirodhak*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *gati*, "motion", and *rodhaka*, "blocking, checking" (Monier-Williams): a thing that holds speed back.
