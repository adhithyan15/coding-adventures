Russian, chapter 342: Talking it over.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обсуждать (obsuzhdát) — to discuss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to insist, then the Russian for to admit.

You'll want to know: обсуждать.
обсуждать (obsuzhdát) — "to discuss".
Вчера мы обсуждали план на лето. Vcherá my obsuzhdáli plan na léto. Yesterday we discussed the plan for the summer.
Imperfective; perfective обсудить. It takes the accusative with no preposition: обсуждать (obsuzhdát) фильм.

The word, taken apart.
обсуждать (obsuzhdát) is built from об- "around" and судить "to judge", on суд "a court, a judgement", inherited from Proto-Slavic sǫdŭ (EtymDB). To discuss is to judge a thing from all sides.

Lesson 2 of 5.
обсуждение (obsuzhdéniye) — a discussion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: обсуждение.
обсуждение (obsuzhdéniye) — "a discussion".
Обсуждение продолжалось два часа. Obsuzhdéniye prodolzhálos dva chasá. The discussion went on for two hours.
Neuter. Вынести на обсуждение (obsuzhdéniye) — to put up for discussion.

The word, taken apart.
обсуждение (obsuzhdéniye) is built from обсуждать (obsuzhdát) "to discuss" and the suffix -ение (EtymDB); the root is суд, Proto-Slavic sǫdŭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
дискуссия (diskússiya) — a debate, an organised discussion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to discuss, then the Russian for a discussion.

You'll want to know: дискуссия.
дискуссия (diskússiya) — "a debate, an organised discussion".
После фильма началась интересная дискуссия. Pósle fílma nachalás interésnaya diskússiya. After the film an interesting debate began.
Feminine. More formal than обсуждение (obsuzhdéniye): a public or organised debate.

The word, taken apart.
дискуссия (diskússiya) was borrowed from Latin discussiō (EtymDB) "a shaking apart, an examination", from discutere "to strike apart, to examine" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
высказываться (vyskázyvatsya) — to speak one’s mind, to give one’s opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a discussion, then the Russian for a debate.

You'll want to know: высказываться.
высказываться (vyskázyvatsya) — "to speak one’s mind, to give one’s opinion".
На собрании каждый мог высказаться. Na sobránii kázhdyy mog výskazatsya. At the meeting everyone could give their opinion.
Imperfective; perfective высказаться (stressed on the first syllable). Высказываться за / против — to speak for or against.

The word, taken apart.
высказываться (vyskázyvatsya) is built from вы- "out" and сказать "to say"; the root is казать "to show", inherited from Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB). To speak your mind is to say it out.

Lesson 5 of 5.
делиться (delítsya) — to share.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a debate, then the Russian for to speak one’s mind.

You'll want to know: делиться.
делиться (delítsya) — "to share".
Она делится с нами своими идеями. Oná délitsya s námi svoími idéyami. She shares her ideas with us.
Imperfective; present делюсь, делишься; perfective поделиться. Делиться чем с кем: the thing shared goes in the instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
делиться (delítsya) is the reflexive of делить "to divide, to share", inherited from Proto-Slavic děliti "to divide", built on dělŭ "a part" (EtymDB); EtymDB sets Proto-Germanic dailą "part, deal" beside it.
