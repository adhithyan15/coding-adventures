German, chapter 424: Wo schlafen wir.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Gasthof — the inn, the country hotel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the sailor, then the German for to capsize.

You'll want to know: der Gasthof.
der Gasthof — "the inn, the country hotel".
Im Dorf gibt es einen alten Gasthof mit gutem Essen. In the village there is an old inn with good food.
Plural: die Gasthöfe. Common in the south and in Austria.

The word, taken apart.
Gasthof is built from Gast 'guest', inherited from Proto-Germanic gastiz and a cousin of English guest (EtymDB), and Hof 'yard, farm, court', also an inherited word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Ferienwohnung — the holiday flat, the holiday apartment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to capsize, then the German for the inn.

You'll want to know: die Ferienwohnung.
die Ferienwohnung — "the holiday flat, the holiday apartment".
Wir mieten eine Ferienwohnung am See. We are renting a holiday flat by the lake.
Plural: die Ferienwohnungen.

The word, taken apart.
Ferienwohnung is built from Ferien 'holidays', a learned borrowing from Latin fēriae 'days of rest, festivals' (Lewis & Short), and Wohnung 'flat', from wohnen 'to live', inherited from Old High German wonēn (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
übernachten — to stay the night, to spend the night.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the inn, then the German for the holiday flat.

You'll want to know: übernachten.
übernachten — "to stay the night, to spend the night".
Auf dem Weg in den Süden übernachten wir in einem kleinen Hotel. On the way south we spend the night in a small hotel.
Inseparable: wir übernachten, wir haben übernachtet.

The word, taken apart.
übernachten is built from über 'over' and Nacht 'night', both inherited Germanic words: Nacht goes back to Old High German naht (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Halbpension — the half board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to stay the night, then the German for the overnight stay.

You'll want to know: die Halbpension.
die Halbpension — "the half board".
Mit Halbpension bekommen wir Frühstück und Abendessen. With half board we get breakfast and dinner.
No plural. Die Vollpension — full board.

The word, taken apart.
Halbpension is built from halb 'half', inherited from Proto-Germanic halbaz (EtymDB), and Pension 'board and lodging', borrowed from French pension (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin pēnsiō 'payment' (Lewis & Short).
