Persian, chapter 459: Goods.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
جنس (jens) — material; goods, stock; a kind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a wholesaler, then the Persian for a retailer.

You'll want to know: جنس.
جنس (jens) — "material; goods, stock; a kind".
جنس (jens) این پیراهن پنبه است. Jens-e in pirâhan panbe ast. This shirt is made of cotton.
Arabic plural ajnâs 'goods'. Jens-e khub — good-quality goods.

The word, taken apart.
jens was borrowed from Arabic jins 'kind, sort' (EtymDB). In Persian it came to mean the stuff a thing is made of, and goods for sale.

Lesson 2 of 6.
کالا (kâlâ) — goods, a product, a commodity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a retailer, then the Persian for material.

You'll want to know: کالا.
کالا (kâlâ) — "goods, a product, a commodity".
کالاهای این فروشگاه همه خارجی است. Kâlâ-hâ-ye in forushgâh hame khâreji ast. The goods in this shop are all foreign.
Formal; kâlâ-ye irâni — goods made at home.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of kâlâ is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it. It is the usual word for goods in trade and in the news.

Lesson 3 of 6.
اصل (asl) — genuine, original (of goods); the origin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for material, then the Persian for goods.

You'll want to know: اصل.
اصل (asl) — "genuine, original (of goods); the origin".
این فرش اصل است. In farsh asl ast. This carpet is genuine.
Arabic plural osul 'principles'. Dar asl — originally.

The word, taken apart.
asl was borrowed from Arabic aṣl 'origin, original' (EtymDB). Said of goods, it means the real thing.

Lesson 4 of 6.
تقلبی (taqallobi) — fake, counterfeit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for goods, then the Persian for genuine.

You'll want to know: تقلبی.
تقلبی (taqallobi) — "fake, counterfeit".
این ساعت تقلبی است. In sâ'at taqallobi ast. This watch is a fake.
Built on taqallob 'cheating' + -i.

The word, taken apart.
taqallobi is built from taqallob 'cheating, fraud', a loan usually traced to Arabic taqallub 'turning over', and the Persian ending -i. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 5 of 6.
گارانتی (gârânti) — a guarantee, a warranty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for genuine, then the Persian for fake.

You'll want to know: گارانتی.
گارانتی (gârânti) — "a guarantee, a warranty".
این یخچال دو سال گارانتی دارد. In yakhchâl do sâl gârânti dârad. This fridge has a two-year guarantee.
The formal word is zemânat.

The word, taken apart.
gârânti was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French garantie 'guarantee'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
