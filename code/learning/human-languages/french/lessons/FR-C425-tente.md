## You'll want to know: la tente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C425-CAMPING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la tente** — "the tent".

> Les enfants ont monté la tente tout seuls.
>
> *The children put up the tent on their own.*

*Monter la tente* — to pitch the tent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tente* goes back to Vulgar Latin *tenta* 'something stretched', from Latin *tendere* 'to stretch' (Lewis & Short).
