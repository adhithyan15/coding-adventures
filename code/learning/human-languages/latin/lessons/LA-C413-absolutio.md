## You'll want to know: absolūtiō, absolūtiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C413-CONDEMNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**absolūtiō, absolūtiōnis** — "an acquittal".

> Post absolūtiōnem domum rediit.
>
> *After his acquittal he went home.*

*absolūtiō, absolūtiōnis* f., the noun of *absolvō*, to set loose. English *absolution*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*absolūtiō* is the noun built on *absolvō* "to set loose, to acquit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *ab* and *solvō* "to loosen". English *absolution* comes from it.
