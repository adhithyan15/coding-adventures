## You'll want to know: смешивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C330-SMESHIVAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**смешивать** (*sméshivat*) — "to mix".

> Она смешала муку с сахаром.
>
> *Oná smeshála múku s sákharom.*
>
> She mixed the flour with the sugar.

Imperfective; perfective *смешать* (*smeshát*). It takes *с* + instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*смешивать* is built on *с-* and *мешать* "to stir, to mix", a cousin of *месить* "to knead", which EtymDB traces to PIE *meyḱ-* "to mix".
