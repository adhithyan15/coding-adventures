## You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਮੀਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C293-LAND293-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਮੀਨ** — *zamīn* — "land; the ground".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਪਿਤਾ ਜੀ ਕੋਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਜ਼ਮੀਨ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere pitā jī kol bahut zamīn hai.*
>
> My father has a lot of land.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਮੀਨ** (*zamīn*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *zamīn*, "earth, land".
