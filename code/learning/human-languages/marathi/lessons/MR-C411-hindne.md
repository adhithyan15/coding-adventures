## You'll want to know: हिंडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C411-SAFAR411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंडणे** — *hiṇḍṇe* — "to roam".

> सुट्टीत आम्ही गावभर हिंडलो.
>
> *suṭṭīt āmhī gāvbhar hiṇḍlo.*
>
> In the holidays we roamed all over the village.

Past (he …): तो हिंडला, *to hiṇḍlā*.

It needs no goal: इकडेतिकडे हिंडणे, to roam here and there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंडणे** (*hiṇḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *hĩḍṇẽ*, "to move, walk", under Sanskrit *hiṇḍate*, "roams" (CDIAL 14089).
