## You'll want to know: zhǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C63-PHONE63-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎo* — "to look for".

> *nǐ zhǎo shénme* — What are you looking for?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎo* is the everyday verb for looking for something. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
