## You'll want to know: امتد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C331-IMBARATUR331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**امتد** — *imtadda* — "to extend, to stretch".

> امتدت الحديقة حتى النهر.
>
> *imtaddat al-ḥadīqa ḥattā an-nahr.*
>
> The garden stretched as far as the river.

Present: يمتد, *yamtadd*.

Of time too: امتد الاجتماع ساعتين.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امتد** (*imtadda*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-d-d* (Lane), "to stretch out". It is Form VIII, "to be stretched, to reach".
