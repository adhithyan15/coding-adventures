## You'll want to know: నటన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C541-NATAKAM541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నటన** — *naṭana* — "acting; a performance".

> ఆ సినిమాలో ఆమె నటన అద్భుతం.
>
> *ā sinimālō āme naṭana adbhutaṁ.*
>
> Her acting in that film is wonderful.

నటుడు is an actor; నటించు is to act.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నటన** (*naṭana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *naṭana*, "dancing, dance, pantomime" (Monier-Williams).
