## You'll want to know: केवल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C378-ZARURAT378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**केवल** — *keval* — "only, solely".

> अगर तुम केवल सच बोलोगे तो डरने की कोई बात नहीं होगी।
>
> *agar tum keval sac bologe to ḍarne kī koī bāt nahīṃ hogī.*
>
> If you tell nothing but the truth, there will be nothing to fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**केवल** (*keval*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *kevala*, "alone, only, mere" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
