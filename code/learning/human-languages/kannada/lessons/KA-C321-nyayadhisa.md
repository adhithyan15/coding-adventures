# ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ (nyāyādhīśa) — a judge

## You'll want to know: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C321-NYAYA321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ** — *nyāyādhīśa* — "a judge".

> ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಎರಡೂ ಕಡೆಯ ಮಾತು ಕೇಳಿದರು.
>
> *nyāyādhīśaru eraḍū kaḍeya mātu kēḷidaru.*
>
> The judge heard both sides.

Plural: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು, *nyāyādhīśaru*.

People speak of a judge with the respectful plural: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಬಂದರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ** (*nyāyādhīśa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *nyāya*, "justice" (CDIAL 7614), and *adhīśa*, "a lord, a master" (CDIAL 267).
