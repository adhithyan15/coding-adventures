## You'll want to know: punir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C388-PENA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**punir** — "to punish".

> A lei pune quem conduz depois de beber.
>
> *The law punishes people who drive after drinking.*

Formal; *ser punido* — to be punished.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*punir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pūnīre* "to punish" (EtymDB), built on *poena* "penalty, punishment" (Lewis & Short).
