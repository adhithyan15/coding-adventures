## You'll want to know: химчистка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C448-KHIMCHISTKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**химчистка** (*khimchístka*) — "a dry cleaner's; dry cleaning".

> Мы отдали пальто в химчистку.
>
> *My otdáli paltó v khimchístku.*
>
> We took the coat to the dry cleaner's.

Feminine; short for *химическая чистка* — chemical cleaning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*химчистка* is a short form of *химическая чистка* 'chemical cleaning', from *химия* 'chemistry' and *чистка* 'cleaning', on *чистый* 'clean', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *čistŭ* (EtymDB).
