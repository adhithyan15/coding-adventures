## You'll want to know: گرفتار کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C283-ADALAT283-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گرفتار کرنا** — *giraftār karnā* — "to arrest".

> پولیس نے چور کو گرفتار کیا۔
>
> *polīs ne chor ko giraftār kiyā.*
>
> The police arrested the thief.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجرم کو گرفتار کیا, *us ne mujrim ko giraftār kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گرفتار کرنا** (*giraftār karnā*) is built from Persian *giriftār*, "seized, taken captive", usually traced to the Persian verb for taking hold, and **کرنا**. The person arrested takes **کو**: **چور کو گرفتار کیا**.
