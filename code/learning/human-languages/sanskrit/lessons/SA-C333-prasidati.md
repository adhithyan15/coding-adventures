## You'll want to know: प्रसीदति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C333-SHAPA333-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसीदति** — *prasīdati* — "shows favour; is gracious".

> तपः दृष्ट्वा देवः प्रसीदति।
>
> *tapaḥ dṛṣṭvā devaḥ prasīdati.*
>
> Seeing the penance, the god shows favour.

Past (he …): सः अन्ते प्रसन्नवान्, *saḥ ante prasannavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसीदति** (*prasīdati*) is built from *pra* and the root √sad, "to sit, settle"; MW gives *pra-sad* as to settle down, grow clear and calm, and to be gracious (MW).
