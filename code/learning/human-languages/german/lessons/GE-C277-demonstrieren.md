## You'll want to know: demonstrieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C277-STREIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**demonstrieren** — "to demonstrate".

> Am Samstag demonstrierten tausend Menschen in der Stadt.
>
> *On Saturday a thousand people demonstrated in the town.*

Short noun: *die Demo*. Also to show how something works.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*demonstrieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēmonstrāre* "to show" (EtymDB), built on *monstrāre* "to show" (Lewis & Short).
