Bengali, chapter 269: Ought To, Have To, and So.
5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উচিত (uchit) — ought to; proper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "because I'm tired" and "because of the storm". (kārôn āmi klāntô, jhôṛer jônyô.)

You'll want to know: উচিত.
উচিত — uchit — "ought to; proper".
তোমার ডাক্তারের কাছে যাওয়া উচিত। tomār ḍāktārer kāchhe jāoyā uchit. — "You ought to go to the doctor."
The one who ought to is in the -র form, তোমার, as in তোমার মনে হয়; the action is the dictionary form, যাওয়া; উচিত (uchit) comes last. Word for word: "your going to the doctor is proper".

The word, taken apart.
উচিত (uchit) is a tatsama: Sanskrit ucita, which Monier-Williams gives as "customary" and "proper, suitable". CDIAL has no entry for it.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: uchit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You ought to go to the doctor" — tomār ḍāktārer kāchhe jāoyā uchit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You ought to rest" — tomār bishrām neoyā uchit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does উচিত (uchit) mean? ("Ought to; proper".) Who goes in the -র form? (The one who ought to: তোমার.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
উ (u) — the letter at the start of উচিত (uchit).

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script you'll notice: উ, Guided Practice and Wrap-up Recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say উচিত (uchit). Then read তু (tu): the sign ু under ত is the vowel of this lesson.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script you'll notice: উ.
উ — u. After a consonant you write it as the sign ু: তুমি. At the start of a word, with nothing to ride on, it is the letter উ: উচিত (uchit). আ and া are the same pair.
In the print of this book, উ (u) is ড with the raised hook of ট on top. Below the headline it has ড's body: a slanting left arm that is the left side of a round bowl, a short stem hanging from the headline in the middle, and a curve joining that stem to the bowl's right side, which ends in a point. Above the headline it has ট's hook: a curve standing on the headline, its free end a tip at the upper left.

Writing: উ — copy the printed shape.
Put your pen beside উ (u) and copy the shape you can see, slowly and large. Check the copy against the print: the hook above the headline, the left arm and bowl, the short middle stem and its curve, the point on the right.
This book does not yet tell you where to start উ (u) or which way the pen goes. That is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying the printed shape needs no such source, and it is how the shape becomes yours in the meantime.

Lesson 3 of 5.
… উচিত / -তে হবে (… uchit / -te hôbe) (uchit / -te hôbe) — ought to, have to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "You ought to go to the doctor". (tomār ḍāktārer kāchhe jāoyā uchit.)

You'll want to know: ought to, ought not.
উচিত (uchit) gives advice. না after it gives a warning:
রাতে তোমার একা যাওয়া উচিত না। rāte tomār ekā jāoyā uchit nā. — "You shouldn't go alone at night."
For yourself, আমার: আমার বিশ্রাম নেওয়া উচিত (āmār bishrām neoyā uchit), "I ought to rest".

You'll want to know: have to.
What you must do, not only ought to, is -তে হবে: the one who must in the -কে form, the verb in its -তে form, then হবে.
কাল আমাকে স্টেশনে যেতে হবে। kāl āmāke sṭeshône jete hôbe. — "Tomorrow I have to go to the station."
The -তে form: যাওয়া becomes যেতে (jete), খাওয়া becomes খেতে (khete), করা becomes করতে (kôrte), আসা becomes আসতে (āste). In the past, হবে becomes হল: আমাকে হেঁটে যেতে হল (āmāke hẽṭe jete hôlô), "I had to walk".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "You shouldn't go alone at night" — rāte tomār ekā jāoyā uchit nā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Tomorrow I have to go to the station" — kāl āmāke sṭeshône jete hôbe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I had to walk" — āmāke hẽṭe jete hôlô]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Say that you ought to rest, then that tomorrow you have to go to the station.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Ought not"? (উচিত (uchit) না.) Which form of "I" goes with -তে হবে? (আমাকে.) যাওয়া in the -তে form? (যেতে.)


Lesson 4 of 5.
তাই / সেজন্য (tāi / sejônyô) — so, that is why.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I had to walk". (āmāke hẽṭe jete hôlô.) Then তাই, "so", from chapter one hundred and fifty-nine.

You'll want to know: so.
কারণ puts the result first and the reason after it. তাই turns that round: the reason first, then "so", then the result.
বাইরে ঝড় হচ্ছিল, তাই আমরা বাড়িতে থাকলাম। bāire jhôṛ hôchchhilô, tāi āmrā bāṛite thāklām. — "There was a storm outside, so we stayed at home."

You'll want to know: that is why.
সেজন্য (sejônyô), "for that, that is why", is সে, "that", and the জন্য of the last chapter. It usually opens a new sentence:
আমার জ্বর ছিল। সেজন্য আমাকে বাড়িতে থাকতে হল। āmār jbôr chhilô. sejônyô āmāke bāṛite thākte hôlô. — "I had a fever. That's why I had to stay at home."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "There was a storm, so we stayed at home" — jhôṛ hôchchhilô, tāi āmrā bāṛite thāklām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I had a fever. That's why I had to stay at home." — āmār jbôr chhilô. sejônyô āmāke bāṛite thākte hôlô.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "The train was late, so I took a bus" — ṭren derite elô, tāi āmi bāse gelām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Say that you were tired, so you went home; then say it again with sejônyô.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which comes first with তাই, the reason or the result? (The reason.) And with কারণ? (The result.) What is সেজন্য made of? (সে and জন্য: "for that".)
