## You'll want to know: مضيق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C477-QIRSH477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مضيق** — *maḍīq* — "a strait".

> عبرت السفينة المضيق في الليل.
>
> *ʿabarat as-safīna al-maḍīq fī al-layl.*
>
> The ship crossed the strait at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مضيق** (*maḍīq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-y-q*, "to be narrow" (Lane). It is a narrow place, beside *ḍayyiq*, "narrow".
