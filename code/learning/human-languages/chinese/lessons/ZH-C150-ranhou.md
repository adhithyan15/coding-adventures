## You'll want to know: ránhòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C150-RANHOU-01]; assesses=[] -->

*ránhòu* — "then; after that".

> *wǒ xiān chī fàn, ránhòu kàn shū* — First I eat, then I read.

Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ránhòu* is built from rán 'so; thus' and 后 'after'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyen* and *huwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the Guangyun glosses *nyen* as a particle, 'thus', and 'then' for the pair is assumed here, not quoted.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C150-HOULAI-01] -->

*ránhòu* joins two steps in order, the second after the first, now or in the past: *wǒ xiān chī fàn, ránhòu kàn shū*.

后来 only looks back at what happened next; *ránhòu* can plan tomorrow too: *míngtiān wǒ qù dàxué, ránhòu qù kàn shū*.
