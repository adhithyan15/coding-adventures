## You'll want to know: حفاظت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C399-HIFAZAT399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حفاظت** — *hifāzat* — "protection; safety".

> مسافروں کی حفاظت ضروری ہے۔
>
> *musāfiroṅ kī hifāzat zarūrī hai.*
>
> Keeping passengers safe matters.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حفاظت** (*hifāzat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥifāẓa*, "guarding, keeping safe"; **محفوظ**, "safe", belongs to the same family.
