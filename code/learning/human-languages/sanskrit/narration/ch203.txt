Sanskrit, chapter 203: Rejoices, Salutes, Begs, Speaks, Shines.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मोदते (modate) — rejoices.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the west, then the Sanskrit for the north.

You'll want to know: मोदते.
मोदते — modate — "rejoices".

The word, taken apart.
modate is the present of the root √mud, "to be merry, glad, rejoice" (MW). The same root gives mud, "joy"; MW gives it no older source, so its origin is uncertain.

Lesson 2 of 5.
वन्दते (vandate) — salutes, greets with respect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the north, then the Sanskrit for rejoices.

You'll want to know: वन्दते.
वन्दते — vandate — "salutes, greets with respect".

The word, taken apart.
vandate is the present of the root √vand, "to praise, salute respectfully" (MW). In the Rigveda it means "praises", later "greets respectfully" (CDIAL 11270), and its noun vandana, "reverence", is borrowed in Malayalam.

Lesson 3 of 5.
याचते (yācate) — begs, asks for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rejoices, then the Sanskrit for salutes.

You'll want to know: याचते.
याचते — yācate — "begs, asks for".

The word, taken apart.
yācate is the middle present of the root √yāc, "to ask, beg" (MW): the middle voice means asking for yourself. It is already in the Rigveda (CDIAL 10447), and yācaka, "beggar", is built on it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
भाषते (bhāṣate) — speaks, talks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for salutes, then the Sanskrit for begs.

You'll want to know: भाषते.
भाषते — bhāṣate — "speaks, talks".

The word, taken apart.
bhāṣate is the present of the root √bhāṣ, "to speak, say" (MW), first found in the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa (CDIAL 9478). The nouns bhāṣā, "language", and bhāṣaṇa, "speech", are built on the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
शोभते (śobhate) — shines, looks fine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for begs, then the Sanskrit for speaks.

You'll want to know: शोभते.
शोभते — śobhate — "shines, looks fine".

The word, taken apart.
śobhate is the present of the root √śubh, "to beautify; to look beautiful, shine" (MW). The adjective śubha, "bright, auspicious", comes from the same root, as in Hindi śubh rātri, "good night".
