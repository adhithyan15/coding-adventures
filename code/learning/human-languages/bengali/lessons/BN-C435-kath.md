## You'll want to know: কাঠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C435-GHAS435-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঠ** — *kāṭh* — "wood; timber".

> শীতে আমরা কাঠ জ্বালালাম।
>
> *shīte āmrā kāṭh jvālālām.*
>
> In winter we burned wood.

ঠ, the breathy partner of ট, closes the word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঠ** (*kāṭh*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *kāṭh*, "wood", under Sanskrit *kāṣṭhá*, "a piece of wood" (CDIAL 3120).
