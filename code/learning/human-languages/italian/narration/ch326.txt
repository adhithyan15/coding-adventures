Italian, chapter 326: Draghi e streghe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il drago — the dragon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the kingdom, then the Italian for to reign.

You'll want to know: il drago.
il drago — "the dragon".
Il drago dorme nella grotta. The dragon sleeps in the cave.
Plural i draghi.

The word, taken apart.
drago is inherited from Latin dracō "a serpent, a dragon" (EtymDB), from Greek drákōn.
