## You'll want to know: धणियो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C185-MASALO185-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धणियो** — *dhaṇiyo* — "coriander".

> सबजी मांय धणियो घालो।
>
> *sabjī mā̃y dhaṇiyo ghālo.*
>
> Put coriander in the vegetables.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धणियो** (*dhaṇiyo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhānaka*, "coriander"; CDIAL lists Hindi *dhaniyā* (CDIAL 6776). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
