# Review — what you should do, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C284-DEBERE-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C284-CAUSA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you should rest". (*Quiēscere dēbēs.*) Then "because you
are tired". (*Quia fessus es.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C284-DEBEBAS-02, LA-LEX-C284-QUOD-CAUSA-02, LA-LEX-C283-MEA-SENTENTIA-01, LA-LEX-C283-MIHI-VIDETUR-02] -->

Two chapters, and you can give reasons. An opinion: *putō* with the
accusative and infinitive, *meā sententiā*, *mihi vidētur*. Advice:
*dēbēs* and an infinitive, and *dēbēbās* for what should have happened. A
reason: *quia*, *nam*, and the *quod* that follows a feeling. A
conclusion: *ergō*.

> *Meā sententiā, quiēscere dēbēs, quia aeger es. Herī labōrāre nōn
> dēbēbās.*
> — In my opinion you should rest, because you are ill. You shouldn't have
> worked yesterday.

[PAUSE 2s] Four pieces in two sentences: the opinion, the advice, the
reason, and the regret.
