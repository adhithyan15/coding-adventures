## You'll want to know: zhìyuànzhě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C375-HELP375-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhìyuànzhě* — "volunteer".

> *wǒ xiǎng dāng zhìyuànzhě yīnwèi wǒ xiǎng bāng biérén* — I want to be a volunteer because I want to help other people.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhìyuànzhě* is built from *zhì* 'what the heart longs for', *yuàn* 'to wish' and *zhě* 'one who', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyiH*, *ngjwonH* and *tsyaeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the last as a grammar word. A volunteer is one who serves of their own will.
