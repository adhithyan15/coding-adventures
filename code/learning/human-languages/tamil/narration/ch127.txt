Tamil, chapter 127: Star, Dew, Thunder, Lightning, Storm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நட்சத்திரம் (naṭcattiram) — a star.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a leaf, then the Tamil for soil.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the day words, நாள் and the formal தினம், then the night words, இரவு, ராத்திரி, and இருள், darkness.

You'll want to know: நட்சத்திரம்.
நட்சத்திரம் — naṭcattiram — "a star".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: naṭcattiram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: naṭcattiram, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say naṭcattiram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for a leaf, then the Tamil for soil, then say naṭcattiram again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does நட்சத்திரம் (naṭcattiram) mean? ("A star".) Say it once more.
