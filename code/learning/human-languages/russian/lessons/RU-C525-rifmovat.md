## You'll want to know: рифмовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C525-POETRY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**рифмовать** (*rifmovát*) — "to rhyme".

> Мой сын любит рифмовать слова.
>
> *Moy syn lyúbit rifmovát slová.*
>
> My son likes making words rhyme.

Imperfective; present рифмую. *Рифмоваться* — to rhyme (of words).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рифмовать* is built on *рифма* 'a rhyme' with *-овать*; EtymDB has no entry, and *рифма* is usually traced to French *rime*.
