## You'll want to know: qiānshōu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C215-DELIVER215-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qiānshōu* — "to sign for (a delivery)".

> *kuàidì dào le qǐng qiānshōu* — Your delivery is here; please sign for it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiānshōu* is built from *qiān* 'to sign' and *shōu* 'to receive'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *syuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
