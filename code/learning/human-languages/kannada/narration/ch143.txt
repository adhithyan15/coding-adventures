Kannada, chapter 143: Dry, Poor, Rich, Famous, Safe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಒಣ (oṇa) — dry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for narrow, then the Kannada for wet.

You'll want to know: ಒಣ.
ಒಣ — oṇa — "dry".

The word, taken apart.
ಒಣ (oṇa) is native Dravidian, from oṇagu, "to dry", and DEDR 601 lists oṇa, "dryness", itself.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಡ (baḍa) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vaṭi, Telugu baḍugu and Malayalam vaṭu (DEDR 5222). DEDR lists baḍa itself, "poor, weak": the old v- has become b-.
