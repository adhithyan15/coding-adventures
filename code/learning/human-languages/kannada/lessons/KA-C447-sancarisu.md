# ಸಂಚರಿಸು (sañcarisu) — to ply; to travel about

## You'll want to know: ಸಂಚರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C447-TADE447-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಚರಿಸು** — *sañcarisu* — "to ply; to travel about".

> ಈ ಮಾರ್ಗದಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ರತಿ ಹತ್ತು ನಿಮಿಷಕ್ಕೆ ಬಸ್ ಸಂಚರಿಸುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ī mārgadalli prati hattu nimiṣakke bas sañcarisuttade.*
>
> A bus runs on this route every ten minutes.

Past (I …): ಸಂಚರಿಸಿದೆ, *sañcariside*.

Said of buses and trains that run a route, and of people who travel about.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಚರಿಸು** (*saṃcarisu*) is built from Sanskrit *sam-car*, "to move about" (the noun *saṃcāra*, CDIAL 12869), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
