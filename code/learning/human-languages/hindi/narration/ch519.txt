Hindi, chapter 519: Clearing the Clutter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संदूक़ (sandūq) — a trunk; a chest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a peg, then the Hindi for to hang up.

You'll want to know: संदूक़.
संदूक़ — sandūq — "a trunk; a chest".
दादी के पुराने कपड़े एक संदूक़ में हैं। Dādī ke purāne kapṛe ek sandūq meṁ haiṁ. Grandmother's old clothes are in a trunk.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संदूक़ (sandūq) is usually traced to Arabic ṣandūq, "a chest, a box", through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a spider, then the Hindi for waste paper.

You'll want to know: बुहारना.
बुहारना — buhārnā — "to sweep (with a broom)".
दादी हर सुबह आँगन बुहारती हैं। Dādī har subah āṁgan buhārtī haiṁ. Grandmother sweeps the courtyard every morning.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कमरा बुहारा, usne kamrā buhārā.

The word, taken apart.
बुहारना (buhārnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL files it under Sanskrit bahukāra, "effecting much", whose feminine bahukarī is "a broom", and gives the Hindi verb as "to sweep" (CDIAL 9188).

Lesson 5 of 5.
माँजना (mā̃jnā) — to scour (pots); to scrub clean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for waste paper, then the Hindi for to sweep (with a broom).

You'll want to know: माँजना.
माँजना — mā̃jnā — "to scour (pots); to scrub clean".
खाने के बाद मीरा ने सब बर्तन माँजे। Khāne ke bād Mīrā ne sab bartan mā̃je. After the meal Mira scoured all the pots.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कड़ाही माँजी, usne kaṛāhī mā̃jī.

The word, taken apart.
माँजना (mā̃jnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit mārjati, "wipes, cleans" (CDIAL 10080). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to rub, clean, polish".
