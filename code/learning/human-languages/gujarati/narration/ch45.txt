Gujarati, chapter 45: Letters From Home and Money.
4 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
ઘ (gha) — the letter that opens ઘર.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ઘ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write તે and ઠ once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say ઘર (ghar), "home". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ઘ.
ઘ — gha, a g with a puff of breath after it.
It opens ઘર (ghar), "home". Say gar, then ghar: the second has the breath.

Writing: ઘ.
1. start here: circle the upper lobe, turn through the middle, and round the lower body.
2. lift, then descend the right spine and turn through its lower foot — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 1.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: t30apps.com, ‘Gujarati Alphabet Writing Practice’, version 1.0, ઘ (gha) animation, first and second SVG paths.

Lesson 2 of 4.
ૈ (ai sign) — the ai sign in પૈસા.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ૈ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write ઠ and ઘ (gha) once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say પૈસા (paisā), "money". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ૈ.
ૈ — the ai sign.
It sits above પ in પૈસા (paisā), "money". You already write ે, one stroke above a consonant, for e. Two strokes, ૈ (ai sign), make it ai.

Writing: ૈ.
Put your pen on ૈ (ai sign) and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is printed.
This book does not yet tell you where to start this letter or which way to travel. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
