## You'll want to know: закладка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C526-BOOKS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**закладка** (*zakládka*) — "a bookmark".

> Я потерял закладку и не помню страницу.
>
> *Ya poteryál zakládku i ne pómnyu stranítsu.*
>
> I've lost my bookmark and can't remember the page.

Feminine. Also a browser bookmark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*закладка* is built on *закладывать* 'to put in, to insert', from *класть* 'to put'; EtymDB has no entry.
