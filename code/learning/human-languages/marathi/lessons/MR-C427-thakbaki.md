## You'll want to know: थकबाकी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C427-VYAJ427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**थकबाकी** — *thakbākī* — "arrears".

> त्याच्यावर विजेच्या बिलाची थकबाकी आहे.
>
> *tyācyāvar vijecyā bilācī thakbākī āhe.*
>
> He has arrears on his electricity bill.

Feminine.

Money owed and overdue; the person owing takes -वर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थकबाकी** (*thakbākī*) is built from the stem of **थकणे**, "to tire, to fall behind", and **बाकी**, "what remains", a loan usually traced to Arabic *bāqī*.
