# ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ (hottiko) — to catch fire

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to suspect, then the Kannada for a firefighter.

## You'll want to know: ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C324-BENKI324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ** — *hottiko* — "to catch fire".

> ಅಡುಗೆಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಎಣ್ಣೆ ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡಿತು.
>
> *aḍugemaneyalli eṇṇe hottikoṇḍitu.*
>
> The oil caught fire in the kitchen.

Past (it …): ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡಿತು, *hottikoṇḍitu*.

The thing that burns is the subject: ಮನೆ ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡಿತು, the house caught fire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ** (*hottiko*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4517 lists Kannada *pottu*, "to be kindled, catch fire, flame", and modern Kannada adds -*ko*, from *koḷ* "to take" (DEDR 2151).

## What you've built
