## You'll want to know: نوبت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C452-NOBAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نوبت** (*nowbat*) — "a turn (in a queue); an appointment".

> در بانک باید منتظر نوبت بمانیم.
>
> *Dar bânk bâyad montazer-e nowbat bemânim.*
>
> At the bank we have to wait our turn.

*Nowbat-e shomâst* — it's your turn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nowbat* was borrowed from Arabic *nawba* 'a turn, a change of the guard' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
