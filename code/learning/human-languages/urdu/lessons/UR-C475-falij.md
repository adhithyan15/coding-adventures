## You'll want to know: فالج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C475-LONG475-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فالج** — *fālij* — "paralysis; a stroke".

> دادا کو فالج ہوا تھا۔
>
> *dādā ko fālij huā thā.*
>
> My grandfather had had a stroke.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فالج** (*fālij*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fālij*, "paralysis".
