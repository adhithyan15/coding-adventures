## You'll want to know: speculātor, speculātōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C302-SPECULATOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**speculātor, speculātōris** — "a spy, a lookout".

> Speculātor noctū in castra hostium vēnit.
>
> *A spy came into the enemy camp by night.*

Masculine, 3rd declension. A soldier sent ahead to watch. English *speculator* now means someone who gambles on prices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**speculātor** is built on *speculor*, "to keep watch", from *specula*, "a lookout", which belongs with *speciō*, "to look" (Lewis & Short). English *speculate* is from the same verb.
