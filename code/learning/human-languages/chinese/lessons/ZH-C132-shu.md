## You'll want to know: shǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C132-BIG132-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shǔ* — "to count".

> *wǒ shǔ le yígòng yǒu bā ge* — I counted: there are eight in all.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shǔ* is inherited from Middle Chinese *srjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes an older book: 'to reckon'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for counting.
