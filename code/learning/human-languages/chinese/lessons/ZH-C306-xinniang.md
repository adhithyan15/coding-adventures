## You'll want to know: xīnniáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C306-WEDDING306-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnniáng* — "bride".

> *xīnniáng jīntiān tèbié piàoliang* — The bride is especially beautiful today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnniáng* is built from 新 'new' and *niáng* 'a young woman', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sin* and *nrjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a title for a young woman.
