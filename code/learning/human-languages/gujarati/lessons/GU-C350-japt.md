## You'll want to know: જપ્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C350-KARAR350-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જપ્ત** — *japt* — "seized (by the authorities)".

> પોલીસે નકલી માલ જપ્ત કર્યો.
>
> *polīse naklī māl japt karyo.*
>
> The police seized the fake goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જપ્ત** (*japt*) is usually traced to Arabic *ẓabt*, "seizure, control", through Persian.
