## You'll want to know: la gru
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C449-LAVORI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la gru** — "the crane (the machine and the bird)".

> Dalla finestra vedo una gru gialla.
>
> *From the window I see a yellow crane.*

Plural unchanged: *le gru*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gru* is inherited from Latin *grus* 'crane' (EtymDB); the machine is named after the bird's long neck.
