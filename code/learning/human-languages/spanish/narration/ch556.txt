Spanish, chapter 556: El entierro.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el entierro — the burial, the funeral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for dead, then the Spanish for the deceased.

You'll want to know: el entierro.
el entierro — "the burial, the funeral".
Fuimos al entierro de su abuelo. We went to his grandfather's funeral.

The word, taken apart.
entierro is built on enterrar 'to bury', which comes from spoken Latin interrāre 'to put in the earth' (EtymDB), from terra 'earth'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
el velatorio — the wake; the funeral parlour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the burial, then the Spanish for to bury.

You'll want to know: el velatorio.
el velatorio — "the wake; the funeral parlour".
Fuimos al velatorio por la noche. We went to the wake in the evening.
In Latin America el velorio is common.

The word, taken apart.
velatorio is built on velar 'to stay awake, to keep watch over', which is inherited from Latin vigilāre 'to stay awake' (EtymDB). Family and friends stay up with the dead.
