## You'll want to know: perturbō, perturbāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C374-NOCEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**perturbō, perturbāre** — "to throw into confusion, to upset".

> Nūntius malus mē perturbāvit.
>
> *The bad news upset me.*

*perturbō, perturbāre, perturbāvī, perturbātum*. *Per-*, thoroughly, with a verb for stirring up. English *perturb*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perturbō* is built from *per* "thoroughly" and *turbō* "to stir up, to disturb" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which belongs with *turba* "an uproar". English *perturb* comes from it.
