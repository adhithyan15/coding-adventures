## You'll want to know: 吵
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C129-PLACE129-02]; assesses=[] -->

**吵** — *chǎo* — "noisy".

> **他的音乐太吵了** *tā de yīnyuè tài chǎo le* — His music is far too loud.

The new character, in pieces: 吵 — 口 on the left and 少 on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**吵** (*chǎo*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsrhaewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'sound', a different word from the *chǎo* of stir-frying, which has the same old form. The dictionary notes that the character first had another reading, *mjiewX*, glossed 'the call of a pheasant'.
