# चिढ़ाना (ciṛhānā) — to tease

## You'll want to know: चिढ़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C471-VINAMRA471-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चिढ़ाना** — *ciṛhānā* — "to tease".

> बड़ा भाई मीरा को उसके नाम पर चिढ़ाता है।
>
> *baṛā bhāī Mīrā ko uske nām par ciṛhātā hai.*
>
> Mira's big brother teases her about her name.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बहन को चिढ़ाया, *usne bahan ko ciṛhāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिढ़ाना** (*ciṛhānā*) is the causative partner of *ciṛhnā*, "to be provoked", which CDIAL lists under *\*ciḍ* (CDIAL 4794): to make someone cross on purpose, usually in fun.
