Urdu, chapter 431: Clouds Gather and Clear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ابر (abr) — a rain cloud; cloud (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for airless heat, then the Urdu for to fan (someone).

You'll want to know: ابر.
ابر — abr — "a rain cloud; cloud (formal)".
آسمان پر ابر ہے۔ āsmān par abr hai. The sky is overcast.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ابر (abr) is usually traced to Persian abr, "a cloud"; it is a more formal word than بادل.

Lesson 2 of 5.
گھرنا (ghirnā) — to be surrounded; to close in (of clouds).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to fan (someone), then the Urdu for a rain cloud.

You'll want to know: گھرنا.
گھرنا — ghirnā — "to be surrounded; to close in (of clouds)".
پہاڑوں پر بادل گھر آئے۔ pahāṛoṅ par bādal ghir āe. Clouds closed in over the mountains.
Past (it …): وہ آسمان بادلوں سے گھر گیا, voh āsmān bādaloṅ se ghir gayā.

The word, taken apart.
گھرنا (ghirnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ghirnā, "to be surrounded", under ghir, "go round", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4474).

Lesson 3 of 5.
چھانا (chhānā) — to spread over; to cover (of clouds or mist).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a rain cloud, then the Urdu for to be surrounded.

You'll want to know: چھانا.
چھانا — chhānā — "to spread over; to cover (of clouds or mist)".
شام کو آسمان پر ابر چھا گیا۔ shām ko āsmān par abr chhā gayā. In the evening cloud spread across the sky.
Past (it …): وہ کہرا شہر پر چھا گیا, voh kohrā shahr par chhā gayā.

The word, taken apart.
چھانا (chhānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin chānā, "to thatch, to spread around", under Sanskrit chādáyati, "covers" (CDIAL 5018).

تھمنا — thamnā — "to let up; to stop (of rain or wind)".

Past (it …): وہ طوفان تھم گیا, voh tūfān tham gayā.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin thāmnā, "to prop, to stop", under Sanskrit stambhate (CDIAL 13683); تھمنا (thamnā) is the form for something that stops by itself, تھمانا the one for handing something over.
