## You'll want to know: tōngcháng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C162-OFTEN162-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tōngcháng* — "normally; usually".

> *zhōumò wǒ tōngcháng zài jiā* — At weekends I'm normally at home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tōngcháng* is built from *tōng* 'to go through' and *cháng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thuwng* and *dzyang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): what holds right through. The sense 'normal' is not given there.
