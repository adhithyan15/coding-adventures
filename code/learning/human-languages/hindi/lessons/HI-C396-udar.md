## You'll want to know: उदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C396-SANSKAR396-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उदार** — *udār* — "generous, broad-minded".

> अगर वह उदार होता तो ग़रीबों की मदद करता।
>
> *agar vah udār hotā to ġarīboṁ kī madad kartā.*
>
> If he were generous, he would help the poor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उदार** (*udār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *udāra*, "great, noble" (Monier-Williams).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
