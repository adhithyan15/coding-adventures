# పులకించు (pulakiñcu) — to thrill; to be thrilled

## You'll want to know: పులకించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C500-ANANDAM500-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పులకించు** — *pulakiñcu* — "to thrill; to be thrilled".

> ఆ పాట విని నా మనసు పులకించింది.
>
> *ā pāṭa vini nā manasu pulakiñcindi.*
>
> Hearing that song, my heart thrilled.

Past (I …): పులకించాను, *pulakiñcānu*.

Said of the body or the heart: the hair stands on end with joy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పులకించు** (*pulakiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *pulaka*, the hair of the body standing on end, a sign of rapture rather than fear (Monier-Williams); the Telugu ending -ఇంచు makes it a verb.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
