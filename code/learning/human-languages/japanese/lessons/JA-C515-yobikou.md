## You'll want to know: よびこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C515-EXAM515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**よびこう** — *yobikō* — "a cram school (for entrance exams)".

> **もし しけんに おちたら、よびこうに かよいます。** — *moshi shiken ni ochitara, yobikō ni kayoimasu* — "If I fail the exam, I will go to a cram school."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よびこう** (*yobikō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yo*, "beforehand" (Middle Chinese *yoH*), *bi*, "equip" (*bijH*) and *kō*, "school" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
