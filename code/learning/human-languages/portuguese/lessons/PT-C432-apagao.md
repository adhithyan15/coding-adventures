## You'll want to know: o apagão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C432-LUZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o apagão** — "the power cut, the blackout".

> Houve um apagão em todo o bairro.
>
> *There was a power cut in the whole neighbourhood.*

Built on *apagar*, to switch off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apagão* is built on *apagar* 'to put out, to switch off' with *-ão*; *apagar* goes back to Latin *pācāre* 'to calm, to pacify' (Lewis & Short).
