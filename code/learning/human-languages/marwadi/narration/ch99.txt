Lesson 6 of 7.
Times of day, seasons and months — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a stepwell and a pond.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: midday, the evening, in the early morning, dawn, the day after tomorrow; the day before yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: afterwards, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Saturday, Sunday, Chait, Baisakh, Jeth]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Savan, Bhadvo, Asoj, Kati, Migsar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Pos, Magh, the hot season, the cold season, the rainy season]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Midday? (दोपार, dopār.) The evening? (आथण, āthaṇ.) In the early morning? (दिनूगै, dinūgai.) Dawn? (परभात, parbhāt.) The day after tomorrow; the day before yesterday? (परसूं, parsū̃.) Afterwards? (पछै, pachhai.) Monday? (सोमवार, somvār.) Tuesday? (मंगळवार, maṅgaḷvār.) Thursday? (गुरुवार, guruvār.) Friday? (सुकरवार, sukarvār.) Saturday? (सनिवार, sanivār.) Sunday? (रविवार, ravivār.) Chait? (चैत, chait.) Baisakh? (बैसाख, baisākh.) Jeth? (जेठ, jeṭh.) Savan? (सावण, sāvaṇ.) Bhadvo? (भादवो, bhādvo.) Asoj? (आसोज, āsoj.) Kati? (काती, kātī.) Migsar? (मिगसर, migsar.) Pos? (पोस, pos.) Magh? (माघ, māgh.) The hot season? (उनाळो, unāḷo.) The cold season? (सियाळो, siyāḷo.) The rainy season? (चौमासो, chaumāso.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Type the Marwadi for 'a stepwell'.
