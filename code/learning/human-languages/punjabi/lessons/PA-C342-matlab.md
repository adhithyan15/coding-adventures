## You'll want to know: ਮਤਲਬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C342-MEAN342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਤਲਬ** — *matlab* — "meaning".

> ਕੀ ਉਸ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਮਤਲਬ ਹੈ?
>
> *kī us shabad dā koī matlab hai?*
>
> Does that word have any meaning?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਤਲਬ** (*matlab*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *maṭlab*, "something sought".
