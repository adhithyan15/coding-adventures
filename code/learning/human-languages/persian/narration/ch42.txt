Persian, chapter 42: Sugar, Salt, Butter, Grapes, Orange.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شکر (shekar) — sugar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tomato, then the Persian for a carrot.

You'll want to know: شکر.
شکر — shekar — "sugar".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.
