## You'll want to know: おせん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C336-KANKYOU336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おせん** — *osen* — "pollution".

> **かわの おせんが ひどいから、さかなが いません。** — *kawa no osen ga hidoi kara, sakana ga imasen* — "The river is badly polluted, so there are no fish."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おせん** (*osen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *o*, "dirty" (Middle Chinese *'u*) and *sen*, "dye" (*nyemX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
