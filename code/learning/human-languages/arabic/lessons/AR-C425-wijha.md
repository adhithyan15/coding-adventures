## You'll want to know: وجهة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C425-RAKIB425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**وجهة** — *wijha* — "a destination; a direction".

> وجهتنا مدينة على البحر.
>
> *wijhatunā madīna ʿalā al-baḥr.*
>
> Our destination is a city by the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وجهة** (*wijha*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-j-h*, "the face; to face" (Lane). A *wijha* is the place one turns one's face towards.
