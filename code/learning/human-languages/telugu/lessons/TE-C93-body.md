# ఒళ్ళు (oḷḷu) — the body

## You'll want to know: ఒళ్ళు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C93-FINE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఒళ్ళు** (*oḷḷu*) — "the body".

**ఒళ్ళు నొప్పులు** — "body aches", with the plural of **నొప్పి**. What a fever leaves behind.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Well, comfortable, at ease, glad, and the body that aches when you are not.
