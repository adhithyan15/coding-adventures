## You'll want to know: изолента
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C533-REPAIRS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**изолента** (*izolénta*) — "insulating tape".

> Он обмотал провод изолентой.
>
> *On obmotál próvod izoléntoy.*
>
> He wrapped the wire in insulating tape.

Feminine. Short for *изоляционная лента*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*изолента* is a clipped compound of *изоляционная* 'insulating' and *лента* 'a ribbon, a tape'; EtymDB has no entry.
