## You'll want to know: பயில்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C364-PERACIRIYAR364-04]; assesses=[] -->

**பயில்** — *payil* — "to study; to train".

> அவள் கல்லூரியில் மருத்துவம் பயில்கிறாள்.
>
> *avaḷ kallūriyil maruttuvam payilkiṟāḷ.*
>
> She studies medicine at college.

Past (I …): பயின்றேன், *payiṉṟēṉ*.

A formal word for படி, to study. Its noun பயிற்சி, training, is taught already.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பயில்** (*payil*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3942 lists Tamil *payil*, "to become trained, practise, learn by practice", and *payiṟci*, "practice, training".
