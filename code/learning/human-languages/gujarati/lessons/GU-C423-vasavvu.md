## You'll want to know: વસાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C423-TOWN423-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વસાવવું** — *vasāvvũ* — "to set up a home; to found (a town)".

> દાદાએ ગામમાં નવું ઘર વસાવ્યું.
>
> *dādāe gāmmā̃ navũ ghar vasāvyũ.*
>
> Grandfather set up a new home in the village.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નવું ગામ વસાવ્યું, *teṇe navũ gām vasāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વસાવવું** (*vasāvvũ*) is the causative partner of વસવું, to settle, a tadbhava of Sanskrit *vasati*, "dwells" (CDIAL 11435): to make people or a home settle in a place.
