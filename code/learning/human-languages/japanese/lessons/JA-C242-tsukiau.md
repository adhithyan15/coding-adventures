## You'll want to know: つきあう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C242-FRIENDS242-05]; assesses=[] -->

**つきあう** — *tsukiau* — "to go out with; to keep company with".

> **あねは かれと つきあいますか。** — *ane wa kare to tsukiaimasu ka* — "Will my elder sister go out with him?"

Polite **つきあいます**, *tsukiaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つきあう** (*tsukiau*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *tsuki*, from *tsuku*, "to stick to", and *au*, "to do together" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
