# زمزمه کردن (zemzeme kardan) — to hum, to murmur

## You'll want to know: زمزمه کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C519-SINGING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**زمزمه کردن** (*zemzeme kardan*) — "to hum, to murmur".

> او در حمام آهنگ زمزمه میکند.
>
> *u dar hammâm âhang zemzeme mikonad.*
>
> He hums tunes in the bath.

*zemzeme* 'a murmur' + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zemzeme* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *zamzama*, 'a low murmuring'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *kardan* is inherited Persian.
