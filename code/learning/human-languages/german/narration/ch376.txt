German, chapter 376: Für die Prüfung lernen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pauken — to cram, to swot (informal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for written, then the German for the school report.

You'll want to know: pauken.
pauken — "to cram, to swot (informal)".
Vor der Prüfung hat sie jeden Abend Vokabeln gepaukt. Before the exam she crammed vocabulary every evening.
Informal; büffeln means the same. In Austria people also say stucken.

The word, taken apart.
pauken is built on Pauke "kettledrum", from Middle High German pūke (EtymDB). The school sense is usually explained as drumming facts into your head.

Lesson 2 of 5.
auswendig — by heart.

You'll want to know: auswendig.
auswendig — "by heart".
Die Kinder lernen das Lied auswendig. The children are learning the song by heart.
Auswendig lernen — to learn by heart; auswendig können — to know by heart.

The word, taken apart.
auswendig is built from aus "out" and wenden "to turn", Old High German wenten (EtymDB), and first meant "on the outside": you can say the text without looking into the book.

Lesson 3 of 5.
konzentrieren — to concentrate (mostly reflexive, sich konzentrieren).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to cram, then the German for by heart.

You'll want to know: konzentrieren.
konzentrieren — "to concentrate (mostly reflexive, sich konzentrieren)".
Bei dem Lärm kann ich mich nicht konzentrieren. I cannot concentrate with this noise.
Mostly reflexive: sich auf etwas konzentrieren. Perfekt: hat sich konzentriert.

The word, taken apart.
konzentrieren was borrowed from French concentrer (EtymDB), built from con- "together" and centre, from Latin centrum "the middle point" (Lewis & Short): you bring everything to one centre.

Nach zwei Stunden lässt meine Konzentration nach. After two hours my concentration fades.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Wiederholung — the revision (for an exam); the repetition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to concentrate, then the German for concentration.

You'll want to know: die Wiederholung.
die Wiederholung — "the revision (for an exam); the repetition".
Vor der Klausur machen wir eine Wiederholung des ganzen Stoffes. Before the exam we revise all the material.
Plural: die Wiederholungen. From wiederholen — to repeat, to revise. On television, die Wiederholung is a repeat.

The word, taken apart.
Wiederholung is formed from wiederholen with -ung (EtymDB); the verb joins wieder "again" to holen "to fetch", from Middle High German holen (EtymDB): you fetch the material again.
