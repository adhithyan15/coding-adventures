## You'll want to know: しらべる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C225-STUDY225-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しらべる** — *shiraberu* — "to look up; to check".

> **としょかんで こたえを しらべます。** — *toshokan de kotae o shirabemasu* — "I look up the answer at the library."

Polite **しらべます**, *shirabemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しらべる** (*shiraberu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
