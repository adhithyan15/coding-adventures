Lesson 1 of 5.
ದೇಹ (dēha) — the body.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to stack, then the Kannada for to collect.

You'll want to know: ದೇಹ.
ದೇಹ — dēha — "the body".

The word, taken apart.
ದೇಹ (dēha) is Sanskrit deha, "body", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 6557).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಚುಚ್ಚುಮದ್ದು (cuccumaddu) — an injection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to collect, then the Kannada for the body.

You'll want to know: ಚುಚ್ಚುಮದ್ದು.
ಚುಚ್ಚುಮದ್ದು — cuccumaddu — "an injection".

The word, taken apart.
ಚುಚ್ಚುಮದ್ದು (cuccumaddu) is native Dravidian: cuccu, "to insert, put in as on a spit" (DEDR 2778), and maddu, "medicine" (DEDR 4719, with Tamil maruntu): "medicine pushed in".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ರೋಗಿ (rōgi) — a patient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an ointment, then the Kannada for an illness.

You'll want to know: ರೋಗಿ.
ರೋಗಿ — rōgi — "a patient".

The word, taken apart.
ರೋಗಿ (rōgi) is Sanskrit rogin, "one who is ill", from roga, "illness". Kannada drops the final -n.
