## You'll want to know: ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C255-POST255-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼** — *kāghaz* — "paper".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਅਤੇ ਕਲਮ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere kol kāghaz ate kalam hai.*
>
> I have paper and a pen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼** (*kāghaz*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *kāghaz*, "paper".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
