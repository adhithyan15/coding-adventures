Telugu, chapter 445: On the Highway.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
రహదారి (rahadāri) — a highway; a main road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a puncture, then the Telugu for to mend.

You'll want to know: రహదారి.
రహదారి — rahadāri — "a highway; a main road".
జాతీయ రహదారి మీద వాహనాలు వేగంగా వెళ్తాయి. jātīya rahadāri mīda vāhanālu vēgaṅgā veḷtāyi. Vehicles go fast on the national highway.
జాతీయ రహదారి (rahadāri) is a national highway. దారి alone is any way or path.

The word, taken apart.
రహదారి (rahadāri) is a compound whose second part is దారి, "a way, a road"; the first part is usually traced to Persian rāh, "a road", and the whole is read as "the main road".

Lesson 2 of 6.
లారీ (lārī) — a lorry; a truck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to mend, then the Telugu for a highway.

You'll want to know: లారీ.
లారీ — lārī — "a lorry; a truck".
లారీ నిండా ఇసుక ఉంది. lārī niṇḍā isuka undi. The lorry is full of sand.
Plural: లారీలు, lārīlu.
నిండా, full of, follows the load: ఇసుక, sand.

The word, taken apart.
లారీ (lārī) is a loan from English lorry, a goods truck.

Lesson 3 of 6.
దూసుకుపోవు (dūsukupōvu) — to shoot ahead; to dash past.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a highway, then the Telugu for a lorry.

You'll want to know: దూసుకుపోవు.
దూసుకుపోవు — dūsukupōvu — "to shoot ahead; to dash past".
ఒక కారు మా బస్సును దాటి దూసుకుపోయింది. oka kāru mā bassunu dāṭi dūsukupōyindi. A car overtook our bus and shot ahead.
Past (I …): దూసుకుపోయాను, dūsukupōyānu.
Said of something moving fast and pushing through: a car, a ball, a crowd.

The word, taken apart.
దూసుకుపోవు (dūsukupōvu) is a Telugu compound: its first part has an origin that is not settled, and its second part is the native పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 4 of 6.
నెమ్మదించు (nemmadiñcu) — to slow down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a lorry, then the Telugu for to shoot ahead.

You'll want to know: నెమ్మదించు.
నెమ్మదించు — nemmadiñcu — "to slow down".
మలుపు దగ్గర బస్సు నెమ్మదించింది. malupu daggara bassu nemmadiñcindi. At the bend the bus slowed down.
Past (I …): నెమ్మదించాను, nemmadiñcānu.
నెమ్మదిగా, slowly, is the same word. It is also said of rain that eases and of prices that stop rising.

The word, taken apart.
నెమ్మదించు (nemmadiñcu) is native Dravidian: నెమ్మది, "tranquillity, ease" (DEDR 3602), made into a verb with -ఇంచు.

Lesson 5 of 6.
అదుపు తప్పు (adupu tappu) — to lose control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to shoot ahead, then the Telugu for to slow down.

You'll want to know: అదుపు తప్పు.
అదుపు తప్పు — adupu tappu — "to lose control".
వర్షంలో కారు అదుపు తప్పింది. varṣaṁlō kāru adupu tappindi. In the rain the car went out of control.
Past (I …): అదుపు తప్పాను, adupu tappānu.
అదుపు, control, + తప్పు, to slip. It is said of a vehicle, a fire, a crowd or a temper.

The word, taken apart.
అదుపు తప్పు (adupu tappu) is a Telugu phrase: అదుపు, "control, restraint", whose origin is not settled, and the native తప్పు, "to slip, to miss" (DEDR 3071).
