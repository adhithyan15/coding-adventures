## You'll want to know: ساکٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C504-SVICH504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ساکٹ** — *sākeṭ* — "a socket (for a plug)".

> اس کمرے میں دو ساکٹ ہیں۔
>
> *is kamre meṅ do sākeṭ haiṅ.*
>
> There are two sockets in this room.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ساکٹ** (*sākeṭ*) is borrowed from English *socket*.
