## You'll want to know: कार्ड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C429-PARCHI429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कार्ड** — *kārḍ* — "a card".

> क्या आप कार्ड से पैसे लेते हैं?
>
> *kyā āp kārḍ se paise lete haiṁ?*
>
> Do you take cards?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कार्ड** (*kārḍ*) is borrowed from English *card*: a bank card, a wedding card, a visiting card.
