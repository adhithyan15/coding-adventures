## You'll want to know: abrandar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C409-FILAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**abrandar** — "to slow down".

> Os carros abrandam perto da escola.
>
> *The cars slow down near the school.*

Also of rain or wind: *a chuva abrandou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abrandar* is built from *a-*, *brando* 'soft, gentle' and *-ar* (EtymDB); *brando* is inherited from Latin *blandus* 'gentle, pleasant'.
