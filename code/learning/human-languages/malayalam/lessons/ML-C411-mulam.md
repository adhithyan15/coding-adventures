# മൂലം (mūlaṁ) — because of; due to

## You'll want to know: മൂലം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C411-KARANAM411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**മൂലം** — *mūlaṁ* — "because of; due to".

> മഴ മൂലം ബസ് വൈകി.
>
> *maḻa mūlaṁ basŭ vaiki.*
>
> The bus was late because of the rain.

It follows the noun directly: മഴ മൂലം, because of the rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മൂലം** (*mūlaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mūla*, "a root" (CDIAL 10250). What a thing grows from is its cause.
