# خير (khayr) — "good, goodness"

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] The "good" that completes "good morning" and "good evening." Note
Arabic doesn't say "good morning" as adjective + noun — it says "morning **of
goodness**," which you'll assemble next lesson.

## Sounds you'll need

- `kha` — the **خ** (kh) is a guttural scrape at the back of the throat — the
  *ch* in Scottish *loch* or German *Bach* (and the same sound as Spanish *j*
  in *jueves*). *KHAYR*. → [reference](../pronunciation-reference.md)

## The word, taken apart

**خير** (*khayr*) — "good, goodness, best, welfare." Root: **خ–ي–ر**
(**kh–y–r**), the idea of **good / choice / the better option**:

- **khayr** — good, goodness
- **khiyār** — choice (the good option); also "cucumber," oddly
- **ikhtiyār** — a selection, choosing (the *ikhti---* pattern = "the act of
  ___ing for oneself")
- **al-akhyār** — the good/best people

So *khayr* is not just "good" as a bland adjective — it's *goodness*, welfare,
the better path. When you wish someone *ṣabāḥ al-khayr*, you're wishing them a
"morning of goodness."
