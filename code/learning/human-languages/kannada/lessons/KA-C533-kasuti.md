# ಕಸೂತಿ (kasūti) — embroidery

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-04, KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-05] -->

## You'll want to know: ಕಸೂತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C533-KASUTI533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಸೂತಿ** — *kasūti* — "embroidery".

> ಅಮ್ಮ ಕರವಸ್ತ್ರದ ಮೇಲೆ ಕಸೂತಿ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *amma karavastrada mēle kasūti māḍidaru.*
>
> Mother embroidered a handkerchief.

ಕಸೂತಿ ಮಾಡು, to embroider.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಸೂತಿ** (*kasūti*) is the Kannada word for embroidery; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
