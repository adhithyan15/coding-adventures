## You'll want to know: ਗਾਹਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C277-CHOOSE277-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਹਕ** — *gāhak* — "a customer".

> ਸਵੇਰੇ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਗਾਹਕ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *savere dukān vich bahut gāhak hunde han.*
>
> In the morning there are many customers in the shop.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਹਕ** (*gāhak*) goes back to Sanskrit *grāhaka*, "one who takes; a purchaser" (Monier-Williams); CDIAL has no entry for it.
