## You'll want to know: しりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C453-PAPERS453-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しりょう** — *shiryō* — "handouts; materials".

> **かいぎの しりょうを みんなに わたしました。** — *kaigi no shiryō o minna ni watashimashita* — "I gave everyone the handouts for the meeting."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しりょう** (*shiryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "resources" (Middle Chinese *tsij*) and *ryō*, "materials" (*lew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
