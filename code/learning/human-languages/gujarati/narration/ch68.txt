Gujarati, chapter 68: Tiger, Lion and Elephant.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
વાઘ (vāgh) — a tiger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a dog, then the Gujarati for a mouse.

You'll want to know: વાઘ.
વાઘ (vāgh) — "a tiger".

What you've built.
One more word for tiger, lion and elephant.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vāgh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vāgh, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say vāgh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a dog, then the Gujarati for a mouse, then say vāgh again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does વાઘ (vāgh) mean? ("A tiger".) Say it once more.
