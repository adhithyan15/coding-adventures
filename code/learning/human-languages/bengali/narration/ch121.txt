Bengali, chapter 121: Owl, Parrot, Pigeon, Sparrow, Heron.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
প্যাঁচা (pyā̃chā) — an owl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a jackal, then the Bengali for a bat.

You'll want to know: প্যাঁচা.
প্যাঁচা — pyā̃chā — "an owl".

The word, taken apart.
প্যাঁচা (pyā̃chā) (pyāṁchā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pecaka, "owl" (CDIAL 8375).

চড়ুই (chôṛui) — a sparrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a parrot, then the Bengali for a pigeon.

You'll want to know: চড়ুই.
চড়ুই — chôṛui — "a sparrow".

The word, taken apart.
চড়ুই (chôṛui) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from caṭaka, "sparrow" (CDIAL 4571). CDIAL calls the word imitative of the bird's call.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pigeon, then the Bengali for a sparrow.

You'll want to know: বক.
বক — bôk — "a heron".

The word, taken apart.
বক (bôk) is inherited from Sanskrit baka, "the egret Ardea nivea", and has come down with almost no change (CDIAL 9115). CDIAL thinks the word probably imitative in origin.
