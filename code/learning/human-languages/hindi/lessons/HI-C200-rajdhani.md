## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राजधानी** (*rājdhānī*) is Sanskrit *rājadhānī*, "a king's seat", from *rāja-*, "king", and *dhānī*, "a holder, a seat", taken over whole as a tatsama.
