## You'll want to know: उद्देश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C342-HETU342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्देश** — *uddeś* — "a purpose; an aim".

> या प्रवासाचा उद्देश काय आहे?
>
> *yā pravāsācā uddeś kāy āhe?*
>
> What is the purpose of this trip?

Masculine.

The thing whose purpose it is takes -चा: प्रवासाचा उद्देश.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्देश** (*uddeś*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *uddeśa*, "pointing at; aim, object" (Monier-Williams).
