## You'll want to know: નુકસાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C304-PUR304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નુકસાન** — *nuksān* — "damage".

> પૂરથી ગામને ખૂબ નુકસાન થયું.
>
> *pūrthī gāmne khūb nuksān thayũ.*
>
> The flood did the village a great deal of damage.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નુકસાન** (*nuksān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nuqṣān*, "a decrease, a loss". Harm is done with **થવું**: **નુકસાન થયું**, there was damage.
