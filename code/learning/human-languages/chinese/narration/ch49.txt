Mandarin Chinese, chapter 49: A Day from Morning to Night.
7 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
上午 (shàngwǔ) — morning (before noon).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for how many, then the Mandarin for to wait.

You'll want to know: 上午.
上午 — shàngwǔ — "morning (before noon)".
上午十点我去大学 shàngwǔ shí diǎn wǒ qù dàxué — At ten in the morning I go to the university.
The new character, in pieces: 午 (wǔ) — the top of 年: a short fall, two bars and a vertical. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
上午 (shàngwǔ) is built from 上 'upper, earlier' and 午 (wǔ) 'noon', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyangH and nguX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary gives nguX as the name of one of the branches of the old calendar.

What you've built.

Script — the top of 年.
午 (wǔ).
One piece, four strokes.
A short left-falling stroke at the top left.
A shorter horizontal, then a long horizontal under it.
Last, the vertical down through the middle of both bars.
It is 年 with its short vertical and bottom bar left out.

Lesson 4 of 7.
晚上 (wǎnshang) — evening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for before noon, then the Mandarin for noon.

You'll want to know: 晚上.
晚上 — wǎnshang — "evening".
晚上我看书 wǎnshang wǒ kàn shū — I read in the evenings.
The new character, in pieces: 晚 (wǎn) — 日 on the left and a seven-stroke piece on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
晚上 (wǎnshang) is built from 晚 (wǎn) 'dusk' and the light ending shang, as in 早上. 晚 (wǎn) is inherited from Middle Chinese mjonX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'dusk'.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
日 多.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — rì, duō]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — 日, then seven strokes.
晚 (wǎn).
Two pieces, left before right.
日, four strokes, narrow on the left.
The right piece, seven strokes. A short left-falling stroke and a short stroke that runs across and falls, like the top of 多. A vertical, a horizontal that turns down, and a horizontal under it to close a small box. Then a left-falling stroke, and a vertical that bends right along the bottom and hooks up.
4 + 7 = eleven.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
