# स्टेशन कैसे जाते हैं? (sṭeśan kaise jāte haiṁ?) — asking the way, and following it

## You'll want to know: asking the way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C03-KAISE-01, HI-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-06, HI-CONCEPT-C09-MAAF-KIJIYE-01, HI-LEX-C191-TIME191-05, HI-LEX-C181-THG181-02, HI-LEX-C282-BATANA-01, HI-LEX-C282-DUR-01] -->

> **माफ़ कीजिए, स्टेशन कैसे जाते हैं?**
>
> *māf kījiye, sṭeśan kaise jāte haiṁ?* — "Excuse me, how do I get to the station?"

**जाते हैं** with no one named is "people go": it asks for the way, not for anyone's plans. Or ask where it is: **स्टेशन कहाँ है?** (*sṭeśan kahā̃ hai?*)

The answer often comes in minutes: **पाँच मिनट का रास्ता है** (*pā̃c minaṭ kā rāstā hai*), "it's five minutes away".

## You'll want to know: following the way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C234-LOC234-02, HI-LEX-C234-LOC234-01, HI-LEX-C233-LOC233-05, HI-LEX-C181-THG181-04, HI-LEX-C153-THINGS153-03, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-06, HI-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-02, HI-LEX-C272-THG272-01] -->

> **सीधे जाइए, फिर बाएँ मुड़िए।**
>
> *sīdhe jāie, phir bāẽ muṛiye.* — "Go straight on, then turn left."

**मुड़ना** (*muṛnā*) is "to turn", and **मुड़िए** asks it politely, as **बोलिए** does. **सीधे**, **बाएँ**, **दाएँ** (*sīdhe*, *bāẽ*, *dāẽ*) are the forms of **सीधा**, **बायाँ** and **दायाँ** that say which way.

- **चौराहे से दाएँ मुड़िए।** (*caurāhe se dāẽ muṛiye.*) — Turn right at the crossroads.
- **पुल के बाद दूसरी गली में बाएँ मुड़िए।** (*pul ke bād dūsrī galī meṁ bāẽ muṛiye.*) — After the bridge, take the second side street on the left. **गली** is feminine, so **दूसरी**.
