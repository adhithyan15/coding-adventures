## You'll want to know: ஒழுகு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C444-KARAI444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒழுகு** — *oḻuku* — "to drip; to leak".

> மழையில் கூரை ஒழுகியது.
>
> *maḻaiyil kūrai oḻukiyatu.*
>
> The roof leaked in the rain.

Past (it …): ஒழுகியது, *oḻukiyatu*.

Of a tap: குழாய் ஒழுகுகிறது, the tap is dripping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒழுகு** (*oḻuku*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1010), "to flow, to leak, to trickle down".
