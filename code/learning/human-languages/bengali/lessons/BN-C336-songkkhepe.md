## You'll want to know: সংক্ষেপে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C336-GURUTTO336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সংক্ষেপে** — *sôngkkhepe* — "briefly; in short".

> সংক্ষেপে বলো, কী হল?
>
> *sôngkkhepe bôlo, kī hôlô?*
>
> Tell me briefly, what happened?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংক্ষেপে** (*sôngkkhepe*) is Sanskrit *saṃkṣepa*, a condensing or abridgment (Monier-Williams), with the Bengali ending -এ: 'in brief'.
