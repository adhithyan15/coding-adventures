## You'll want to know: గోళీలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C537-ATALU537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**గోళీలు** — *gōḷīlu* — "marbles".

> చిన్నప్పుడు మేము గోళీలు ఆడేవాళ్ళం.
>
> *cinnappuḍu mēmu gōḷīlu āḍēvāḷḷaṁ.*
>
> As children we used to play marbles.

One marble is గోళీ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గోళీలు** (*gōḷīlu*) is usually traced to Hindustani *golī*, "a small ball", from Sanskrit *gōla*, "a ball", with *golikā*, "a little ball" (CDIAL 4321).
