## You'll want to know: ನಗರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C103-HOME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಗರ** (*nagara*) — "city". **ಊರು** is any town, and home; **ನಗರ** is the big city.

## What you've built
