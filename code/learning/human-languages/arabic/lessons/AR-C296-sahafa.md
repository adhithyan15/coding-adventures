## You'll want to know: صحافة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C296-SAHAFA296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صحافة** — *ṣaḥāfa* — "journalism; the press".

> درس أخي الصحافة في الجامعة.
>
> *darasa akhī aṣ-ṣaḥāfa fī al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> My brother studied journalism at university.

A journalist is صحفي; the newspaper is صحيفة: one root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صحافة** (*ṣaḥāfa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-ḥ-f* (Lane), a written leaf or page, the root of *ṣaḥīfa*. The modern sense, the press, comes from *ṣaḥīfa*, a newspaper.
