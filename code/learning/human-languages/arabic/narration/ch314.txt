Arabic, chapter 314: A funeral.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جنازة (janāza) — a funeral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for anew, then the Arabic for to regain.

You'll want to know: جنازة.
جنازة — janāza — "a funeral".
حضر الجيران جنازة (janāza) الرجل العجوز. ḥaḍara al-jīrān janāzat ar-rajul al-ʿajūz. The neighbours attended the old man’s funeral.

The word, taken apart.
جنازة (janāza) is built on the native Arabic root j-n-z (Lane), where janāza is the bier with the dead person upon it, and then the procession that carries it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
توفي (tuwuffiya) — to pass away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to regain, then the Arabic for a funeral.

You'll want to know: توفي.
توفي — tuwuffiya — "to pass away".
توفي جدي قبل سنتين. tuwuffiya jaddī qabla sanatayn. My grandfather passed away two years ago.
Present: يتوفى, yutawaffā.
A polite word for to die, passive in form.

The word, taken apart.
توفي (tuwuffiya) is built on the native Arabic root w-f-y (Lane), "to be complete, to fulfil". Tawaffā is "to take a thing in full"; its passive, tuwuffiya, is said of one whose life is complete.

Lesson 4 of 5.
شيع (shayyaʿa) — to escort a coffin; to see off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pass away, then the Arabic for to bury.

You'll want to know: شيع.
شيع — shayyaʿa — "to escort a coffin; to see off".
شيع أهل القرية الرجل إلى المقبرة. shayyaʿa ahl al-qarya ar-rajul ilā al-maqbara. The villagers walked with the man’s coffin to the cemetery.
Present: يشيع, yushayyiʿ.
The noun is تشييع; شيع (shayyaʿa) ضيفه: he saw his guest off.

The word, taken apart.
شيع (shayyaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root sh-y-ʿ (Lane), "to follow". It is Form II, "to go along with one who is leaving", and so with the dead.
