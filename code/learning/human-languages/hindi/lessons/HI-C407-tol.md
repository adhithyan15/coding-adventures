## You'll want to know: टोल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C407-NAKA407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टोल** — *ṭol* — "a toll (on a road)".

> राजमार्ग पर हमें दो जगह टोल देना पड़ा।
>
> *rājmārg par hamẽ do jagah ṭol denā paṛā.*
>
> We had to pay a toll at two places on the highway.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टोल** (*ṭol*) is borrowed from English *toll*, the charge paid at a barrier on a highway or a bridge.
