# Tomorrow: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I will go*, *she will go*, *we will go*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-LEX-KALL-216, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I will go to the market tomorrow*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *she went yesterday; he will go tomorrow*] Say **ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਗਈ। ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।**] Give its meaning: we will go to school tomorrow.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ।**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say where you will go tomorrow, and who will go with you.
