## HL-C360 — Hindi second declares its taught feminine form

Chapter 82 teaches masculine **दूसरा** beside feminine **दूसरी**, models both
agreement patterns, and asks the learner to produce “the second book” at
wrap-up. The declaration exposes only **दूसरा**, leaving its explicitly taught
feminine form looking untaught to the corpus report.

Declare **दूसरी** beside **दूसरा**. This makes the lesson's taught and assessed
agreement contrast visible to reports and downstream study artifacts without
claiming any form the lesson does not present.
