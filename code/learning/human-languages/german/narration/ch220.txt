Lesson 1 of 5.
pünktlich — punctual.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for tidy, proper, then the German for personal.

You'll want to know: pünktlich.
pünktlich — "punctual".

The word, taken apart.
pünktlich is the word for "point", from Latin pūnctum, "a prick, a point" (EtymDB 2.1), + -lich: on the dot.
