## You'll want to know: સંભળાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C335-SADHU335-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભળાવવું** — *sambhaḷāvvũ* — "to recite; to tell (a story) aloud".

> સાધુએ બાળકોને એક વાર્તા સંભળાવી.
>
> *sādhue bāḷkone ek vārtā sambhḷāvī.*
>
> The holy man told the children a story.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વાર્તા સંભળાવી, *teṇe vārtā sambhḷāvī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભળાવવું** (*sambhaḷāvvũ*) is the causative of **સાંભળવું**, to hear, from *\*saṁbhalati*, "attends to", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 12962): to make others hear, to tell a story aloud. The long *ā* of **સાંભળવું** shortens in the longer verb.
