## You'll want to know: gärtnern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C503-GARTEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**gärtnern** — "to garden".

> In ihrer Freizeit gärtnert meine Mutter gern.
>
> *In her free time my mother likes gardening.*

Built on *der Gärtner* — gardener.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gärtnern* is built on *Gärtner* "gardener", from *Garten*, which is inherited from Old High German *garto* "garden, enclosure", from Proto-Germanic *gardô* (EtymDB).
