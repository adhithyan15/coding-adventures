Russian, chapter 311: Celebrating.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
праздновать (prázdnovat) — to celebrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a christening, then the Russian for to christen.

You'll want to know: праздновать.
праздновать (prázdnovat) — "to celebrate".
Мы праздновали день рождения до поздней ночи. My prázdnovali den rozhdéniya do pózdney nóchi. We celebrated the birthday until late at night.
Imperfective; the perfective is отпраздновать (otprázdnovat). Present праздную.

The word, taken apart.
праздновать (prázdnovat) is built on праздник "a holiday" (EtymDB), from праздный "idle", the Church Slavonic shape of native порожний "empty": a day free of work.

Lesson 2 of 5.
отмечать (otmechát) — to mark (an occasion), to celebrate; to note.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to christen, then the Russian for to celebrate.

You'll want to know: отмечать.
отмечать (otmechát) — "to mark (an occasion), to celebrate; to note".
Каждый год мы отмечаем этот день вместе. Kázhdyy god my otmecháyem étot den vméste. Every year we mark this day together.
Imperfective; perfective отметить (otmétit), отмечу. The everyday word for celebrating: отметить день рождения.

The word, taken apart.
отмечать (otmechát) is built on от- and метить "to mark", inherited from Proto-Slavic mětiti (EtymDB): to set a mark on a day.

Lesson 3 of 5.
торжество (torzhestvó) — a celebration, a festive occasion; triumph.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to celebrate, then the Russian for to mark.

You'll want to know: торжество.
торжество (torzhestvó) — "a celebration, a festive occasion; triumph".
Торжество началось в шесть часов. Torzhestvó nachalós v shest chasóv. The celebration began at six.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
торжество (torzhestvó) is inherited from Old East Slavic tŭržĭstvo (EtymDB), usually linked with торг "a market, a gathering of people".
