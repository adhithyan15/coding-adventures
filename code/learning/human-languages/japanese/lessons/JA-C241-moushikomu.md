## You'll want to know: もうしこむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C241-OUTING241-04]; assesses=[] -->

**もうしこむ** — *mōshikomu* — "to sign up; to apply".

> **せんげつ あには じゅうどうの しあいに もうしこみました。** — *sengetsu ani wa jūdō no shiai ni mōshikomimashita* — "Last month my elder brother signed up for a judo match."

Polite **もうしこみます**, *mōshikomimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もうしこむ** (*mōshikomu*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *mōshi*, from *mōsu*, "to say", and *komu*, "to go in, to crowd in" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
