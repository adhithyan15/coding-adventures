## You'll want to know: la lanterna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C322-FUOCHI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la lanterna** — "the lantern".

> Le lanterne illuminano la piazza.
>
> *The lanterns light up the square.*

In some ports, the lighthouse is called *la lanterna*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lanterna* is inherited from Latin *lanterna* (EtymDB), from Greek *lamptḗr* "a torch".
