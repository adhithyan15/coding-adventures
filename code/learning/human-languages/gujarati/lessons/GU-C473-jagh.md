## You'll want to know: જાંઘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C473-JANGH473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**જાંઘ** — *jā̃gh* — "a thigh".

> પડ્યા પછી મારી જાંઘ પર સોજો આવ્યો.
>
> *paḍyā pachī mārī jā̃gh par sojo āvyo.*
>
> After I fell, my thigh swelled up.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાંઘ** (*jā̃gh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jaṅghā*, "shank, ankle to knee"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "thigh" (CDIAL 5082).
