# ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ (siṃhāsana) — a throne

## You'll want to know: ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C322-RAJA322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ** — *siṃhāsana* — "a throne".

> ರಾಜ ಸಿಂಹಾಸನದ ಮೇಲೆ ಕುಳಿತಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *rāja siṃhāsanada mēle kuḷitiddanu.*
>
> The king was sitting on the throne.

The lion seat, as ಸಿಂಹ is a lion. ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ ಏರು, to climb the throne, is to become king.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ** (*siṃhāsana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *siṃhāsana*, "the lion's seat, the king's seat, a throne" (Monier-Williams), from *siṃha* "a lion" and *āsana* "a seat".
