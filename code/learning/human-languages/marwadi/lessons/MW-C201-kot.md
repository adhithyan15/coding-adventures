## You'll want to know: कोट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C201-TOWN201-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कोट** — *koṭ* — "a fort".

> कोट कठै है?
>
> *koṭ kaṭhai hai?*
>
> Where is the fort?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोट** (*koṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kōṭṭa*, "fort"; CDIAL lists Marwari *koṭ*, "fort" (CDIAL 3500).
