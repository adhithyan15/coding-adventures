## You'll want to know: अधीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C472-HATTI472-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अधीर** — *adhīr* — "impatient".

> निकाल ऐकायला सगळे अधीर झाले होते.
>
> *nikāl aikāylā sagḷe adhīr jhāle hote.*
>
> Everyone was impatient to hear the result.

अ-, not, + धीर, patience: अधीर होणे, to grow impatient.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधीर** (*adhīr*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *adhīra* (Monier-Williams), the negative *a-* with *dhīra*, "steady".
