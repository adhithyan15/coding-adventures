## You'll want to know: canōrus, canōra, canōrum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C543-CANT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**canōrus, canōra, canōrum** — "melodious, tuneful".

> Vōx puellae canōra est.
>
> *The girl's voice is melodious.*

From *canor* — melody, and so from *canō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**canōrus** is built on *canor* "melody", from *canō* "to sing" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
