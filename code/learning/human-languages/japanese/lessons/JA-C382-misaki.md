## You'll want to know: みさき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C382-KAIGAN382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**みさき** — *misaki* — "a cape; a headland".

> **みさきから うみが よく みえます。** — *misaki kara umi ga yoku miemasu* — "You can see the sea well from the cape."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みさき** (*misaki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
