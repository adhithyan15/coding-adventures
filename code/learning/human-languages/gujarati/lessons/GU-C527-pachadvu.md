# પછાડવું (pachāḍvũ) — to throw down

## You'll want to know: પછાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C527-AKHADO527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પછાડવું** — *pachāḍvũ* — "to throw down".

> પહેલવાને તેના હરીફને ધરતી પર પછાડ્યો.
>
> *pahelvāne tenā harīphne dhartī par pachāḍyo.*
>
> The wrestler threw his rival to the ground.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હરીફને પછાડ્યો, *teṇe harīphne pachāḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પછાડવું** (*pachāḍvũ*) comes from *\*pracchaṭ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to knock down" (CDIAL 8493).
