# ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ — Saturday

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-WEEKDAY-VEERVAR-01, PA-WEEKDAY-SHUKKARVAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਵੀਰਵਾਰ**, then **ਸ਼ੁੱਕਰਵਾਰ**.

## Grammar Lens: one weekday by ear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-WEEKDAY-SHANIVAR-01]; assesses=[PA-WEEKDAY-SHUKKARVAR-01] -->

[YOU HEAR: *shanivaar*; YOU SAY: **ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ**]

> **ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ** — *śanīvār* — Saturday

This is the shorter weekday form used in the draft; other Punjabi materials may use a longer Saturday form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Śanīvār* joins Sanskrit *śani*, the name of Saturn, with *vāra*, "day of the week" (CDIAL 11547).
