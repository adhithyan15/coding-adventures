Telugu, chapter 522: A Profession.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వృత్తి (vṛtti) — a profession; a calling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to put on weight, then the Telugu for nutrition.

You'll want to know: వృత్తి.
వృత్తి — vṛtti — "a profession; a calling".
మా నాన్నగారి వృత్తి వైద్యం. mā nānnagāri vṛtti vaidyaṁ. My father's profession is medicine.
Your calling: వృత్తిరీత్యా, by profession.

The word, taken apart.
వృత్తి (vṛtti) is borrowed from Sanskrit vṛtti, "mode of life, course of action" (Monier-Williams): the way you make your living.

హోదా — hōdā — "status; a rank".
కొత్త ఉద్యోగంలో అతని హోదా పెరిగింది. kotta udyōgaṁlō atani hōdā perigindi. In his new job his status rose.
Rank in an office or in society.

The word, taken apart.
హోదా (hōdā) came into Telugu through Hindustani ohdā, "an office, a rank", which is usually traced to Arabic.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for status, then the Telugu for a position.

You'll want to know: పదోన్నతి.
పదోన్నతి — padōnnati — "a promotion".
కష్టపడి పనిచేసినందుకు ఆమెకు పదోన్నతి వచ్చింది. kaṣṭapaḍi panicēsinanduku āmeku padōnnati vaccindi. She got a promotion for working hard.
Formal; in speech many say ప్రమోషను.

The word, taken apart.
పదోన్నతి (padōnnati) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit pada, "step, position", and unnati, "rising, advancement" (Monier-Williams).

A life goal, nobler than a passing wish.

The word, taken apart.
ఆశయం (āśayaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit āśaya (CDIAL 1453), which first meant a resting place; in Telugu it names the aim a person lives for.
