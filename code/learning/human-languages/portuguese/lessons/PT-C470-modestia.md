## You'll want to know: a modéstia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C470-MODESTIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a modéstia** — "modesty".

> Com modéstia, disse que a vitória era da equipa.
>
> *Modestly, she said the victory belonged to the team.*

*Modéstia à parte* — modesty aside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modéstia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *modestia* "moderation, modesty" (Lewis & Short), from *modestus*.
