## You'll want to know: ڈھال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C310-DHAL310-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھال** — *ḍhāl* — "a shield".

> اس نے ڈھال اٹھائی۔
>
> *us ne ḍhāl uṭhāī.*
>
> He raised his shield.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھال** (*ḍhāl*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ḍhāla*, "a shield" (CDIAL 5583). It is feminine: **ڈھال اٹھائی**.
