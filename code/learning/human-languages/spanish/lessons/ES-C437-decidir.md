# decidir — to cut the question off

## Warm-up

what you now have to do.

## You'll want to know: decidir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-01]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-03] -->

**decidir** — to decide. **decidirse** — to make up your mind.

*Usted decide* — you decide. It is what a notice says when it has finished
offering and the choice is yours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-01] -->

**de-** off, plus **-cidir** from Latin *caedere*, to cut. To **cut the question
off**.

The same *caedere* is under a family that all involve cutting something away:

| word | what is cut |
|---|---|
| decidir | the question |
| preciso | cut exactly to size |
| conciso | cut short |

**decidir** and **decidirse** are not the same. *Decido el menú* is choosing a
thing; *me decido* is the inner moment when you stop hesitating. Spanish marks
that difference with the reflexive, where English leans on *decide* versus *make
up my mind*.
