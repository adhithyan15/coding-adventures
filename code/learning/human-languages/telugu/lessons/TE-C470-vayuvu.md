## You'll want to know: వాయువు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C470-KALUSHYAM470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**వాయువు** — *vāyuvu* — "a gas".

> ఆ కర్మాగారం నుంచి విష వాయువు బయటకు వచ్చింది.
>
> *ā karmāgāraṁ nuñci viṣa vāyuvu bayaṭaku vaccindi.*
>
> A poisonous gas came out of that factory.

A formal word for a gas; in Sanskrit it is the wind. In a kitchen people say గ్యాస్.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వాయువు** (*vāyuvu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vāyu*, "wind, air" (CDIAL 11544); modern Telugu uses it for a gas.
