## You'll want to know: વસ્તુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C188-GOODS188-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વસ્તુ** — *vastu* — "thing, item".

> **આ દુકાનમાં દરેક વસ્તુ સસ્તી છે.** — *ā dukānmā̃ darek vastu sastī che* — "Everything in this shop is cheap."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વસ્તુ** (*vastu*) is Sanskrit *vástu*, "existing thing; affair", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 11449).
