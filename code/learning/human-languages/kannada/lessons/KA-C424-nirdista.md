## You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C424-AVALOKISU424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟ** — *nirdiṣṭa* — "specific; definite".

> ಅವನಿಗೆ ಯಾವುದೇ ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟ ಗುರಿ ಇಲ್ಲ.
>
> *avanige yāvudē nirdiṣṭa guri illa.*
>
> He has no definite goal.

ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟವಾಗಿ, specifically.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟ** (*nirdiṣṭa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nirdiṣṭa*, "pointed out, indicated" (Monier-Williams).
