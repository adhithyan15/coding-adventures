## You'll want to know: ಸಾಗರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C453-HADAGU453-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಗರ** — *sāgara* — "an ocean".

> ಹಡಗು ಸಾಗರವನ್ನು ಹತ್ತು ದಿನದಲ್ಲಿ ದಾಟಿತು.
>
> *haḍagu sāgaravannu hattu dinadalli dāṭitu.*
>
> The ship crossed the ocean in ten days.

ಸಮುದ್ರ is the sea; ಸಾಗರ is the wider, grander word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಗರ** (*sāgara*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sāgara*, "the ocean" (CDIAL 13325).
