## You'll want to know: മീട്ടുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C564-RACANA564-04]; assesses=[] -->

**മീട്ടുക** — *mīṭṭuka* — "to strum; to pluck (strings)".

> അവൻ ഗിറ്റാറിന്റെ കമ്പികൾ പതുക്കെ മീട്ടി.
>
> *avan giṟṟāṟinṟe kampikaḷ patukke mīṭṭi.*
>
> He gently strummed the strings of the guitar.

Past: മീട്ടി, *mīṭṭi*.

Said of strings; a drum is കൊട്ടുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മീട്ടുക** (*mīṭṭuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4848 gives it as to tap or strike, as the strings of an instrument (DEDR 4848).
