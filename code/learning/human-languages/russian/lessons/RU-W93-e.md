# э (e) — the e that opens этот

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-C28-YA-SMALL-01, RU-SCRIPT-C81-YO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write я and ё once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **этот** (*étot*), "this".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: э
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SCRIPT-C93-E-01]; assesses=[] -->

**э** — *e*.

It opens **этот** (*étot*), "this", and **эта**. It looks like a **с** turned to
face the other way, with a short bar across the middle. It is the plain *e*
sound, with no *y* in front of it, which is why it is mostly found at the start
of a word.

## Writing: э
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-C93-E-01] -->

Put your pen on э and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
