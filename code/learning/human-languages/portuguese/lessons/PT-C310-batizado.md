## You'll want to know: o batizado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C310-BATIZADO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o batizado** — "the christening, the baptism".

> O batizado foi na igreja da aldeia.
>
> *The christening was in the village church.*

Before the 1990 spelling reform, *baptizado*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*batizado* is the past participle of *batizar* used as a noun; the verb goes back to Latin *baptizāre*, from Greek *baptízein* "to dip, to plunge" (Lewis & Short).
