## You'll want to know: कर्पूरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C397-DIPA397-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्पूरः** — *karpūraḥ* — "camphor".

> दीपे कर्पूरः ज्वलति।
>
> *dīpe karpūraḥ jvalati.*
>
> Camphor burns in the lamp.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्पूरः** (*karpūraḥ*) is the word MW gives for camphor; MW records only a commentator's guess at a root √kṛp, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
