Bengali, chapter 390: Highways and Bends.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সড়ক (sôṛôk) — a highway; a main road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to signal, then the Bengali for to call out.

You'll want to know: সড়ক.
সড়ক — sôṛôk — "a highway; a main road".
নতুন সড়কে বাস দ্রুত চলে। nôtun sôṛôke bās drutô chôle. Buses go fast on the new highway.
রাস্তা is any road; সড়ক (sôṛôk) is a main road between towns, the word on maps: জাতীয় সড়ক (sôṛôk), a national highway.

The word, taken apart.
সড়ক (sôṛôk) probably belongs with Sanskrit sṛtí, "a road, a path", the entry where CDIAL sets Bengali saṛak, "a road" (CDIAL 13577); the Hindi word is filed under a second entry as well (CDIAL 12269), so the link is hedged.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বাঁক (bā̃k) — a bend; a turn (in a road or river).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to call out, then the Bengali for a highway.

You'll want to know: বাঁক.
বাঁক — bā̃k — "a bend; a turn (in a road or river)".
সামনের বাঁকে বাসটা ধীরে চলল। sāmner bā̃ke bāsṭā dhīre chôllô. At the bend ahead the bus slowed down.
বাঁকা, crooked, is the same root as an adjective.

The word, taken apart.
বাঁক (bā̃k) is inherited: CDIAL gives vaṅka, "bent, crooked", with the sense "a bend of a river", and lists Bengali bā̃kā, "crooked", under it (CDIAL 11191); the nasal of vaṅ survives as the ঁ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
পিচ (pich) — tar (on a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bend, then the Bengali for a pothole.

You'll want to know: পিচ.
পিচ — pich — "tar (on a road)".
গরমে রাস্তার পিচ নরম হয়ে গেল। gôrôme rāstār pich nôrôm hôye gelô. In the heat the tar on the road went soft.
পিচের রাস্তা, a tarred road; a কাঁচা রাস্তা is a dirt road.

The word, taken apart.
পিচ (pich) is borrowed from English pitch, the black tar that roads are sealed with.

Lesson 5 of 5.
গড়ানো (gôṛāno) — to roll; to roll down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pothole, then the Bengali for tar.

You'll want to know: গড়ানো.
গড়ানো — gôṛāno — "to roll; to roll down".
পাথরটা পাহাড় থেকে গড়িয়ে পড়ল। pāthôrṭā pāhāṛ theke gôṛiye pôṛlô. The stone rolled down the hill.
Past (he/she …): সেই পাথর গড়াল, sei pāthôr gôṛālô.
Not গড়া, to build: a different verb that sounds close.

The word, taken apart.
গড়ানো (gôṛāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali gaṛāna, "to make slide or roll", under Sanskrit gáḍati, "drops, runs as a liquid" (CDIAL 3973).
