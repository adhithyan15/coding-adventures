# సంఘం (saṅghaṁ) — an association; a union

## You'll want to know: సంఘం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C408-SAMAJAM408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సంఘం** — *saṅghaṁ* — "an association; a union".

> మా వీధిలో ఒక యువజన సంఘం ఉంది.
>
> *mā vīdhilō oka yuvajana saṅghaṁ undi.*
>
> There is a youth association in our street.

Plural: సంఘాలు, *saṅghālu*.

Also a trade union. Plural సంఘాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంఘం** (*saṅghaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁgha*, "an association, a community" (CDIAL 12854).
