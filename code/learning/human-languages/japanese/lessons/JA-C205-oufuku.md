## You'll want to know: おうふく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C205-TICKET205-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おうふく** — *ōfuku* — "a round trip; a return ticket".

> **おうふくの きっぷは やすいです。** — *ōfuku no kippu wa yasui desu* — "A return ticket is cheap."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おうふく** (*ōfuku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ō*, "to go" (Middle Chinese *hjwangX*) and *fuku*, "to return" (*bjuwk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
