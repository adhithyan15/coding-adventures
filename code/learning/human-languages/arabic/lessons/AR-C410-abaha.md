## You'll want to know: أباح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C410-IBAHA410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أباح** — *abāḥa* — "to permit".

> أباح القانون العمل يوم السبت.
>
> *abāḥa al-qānūn al-ʿamal yawm as-sabt.*
>
> The law permitted work on Saturdays.

Present: يبيح, *yubīḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أباح** (*abāḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-w-ḥ*, "to become known, to disclose" (Lane). It is Form IV, to lay a thing open to people: to permit it.
