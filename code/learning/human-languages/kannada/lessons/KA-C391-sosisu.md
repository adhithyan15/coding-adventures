## You'll want to know: ಶೋಷಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C391-SAMANATE391-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶೋಷಿಸು** — *śōṣisu* — "to exploit".

> ಆ ಕಂಪನಿ ಕೆಲಸಗಾರರನ್ನು ಶೋಷಿಸಿತು.
>
> *ā kampani kelasagārarannu śōṣisitu.*
>
> That company exploited its workers.

Past (I …): ಶೋಷಿಸಿದೆ, *śōṣiside*.

Who is exploited takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಶೋಷಣೆ, exploitation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶೋಷಿಸು** (*śōṣisu*) is built on Sanskrit *śoṣaṇa*, "drying up, draining" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*: to drain someone.
