# Review — how it felt, and what you hope for

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-FEELING-BUDAM-01, FA-GRAMMAR-FEELING-SHODAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was tired", then "I got tired". (*Khaste budam*, *khaste
shodam*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-FEELING-BUDAM-01, FA-GRAMMAR-FEELING-SHODAM-01, FA-LEX-C296-GHAZA-01, FA-GRAMMAR-OMIDVARAM-KE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-DUST-DARAM-SUBJ-01, FA-LEX-C161-SEFAT161-01] -->

Three lessons, and an experience now has its colour. *Budam* gives the
feeling of a whole stretch; *shodam* gives the moment it arrived. *Ghazâ*
brings in the meal, and *omidvâram ke*, *dust dâram* and *ârezu dâram* carry
the hope forward, each with the subjunctive after it.

[PAUSE 2s] Back to the bad morning at the station, told as an experience:

> *qatâr râ az dast dâdam va nârâhat shodam. ba'd mas'ul-e istgâh bilit-e
> fardâ râ dâd, barâ-ye hamin khoshhâl shodam. ghazâ-ye istgâh khoshmaze
> bud. omidvâram ke fardâ dir naresam.*

The last verb, *naresam*, is the subjunctive with *na-*: "that I don't
arrive".
