## You'll want to know: streamen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C525-STREAMEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**streamen** — "to stream".

> Wir streamen die meisten Filme.
>
> *We stream most films.*

Regular: *hat gestreamt*. Said as in English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*streamen* was borrowed from English *to stream* (EtymDB).
