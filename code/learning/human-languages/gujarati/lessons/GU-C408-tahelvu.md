## You'll want to know: ટહેલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C408-ROUNDS408-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ટહેલવું** — *ṭahelvũ* — "to walk up and down; to stroll".

> જમ્યા પછી દાદા આંગણામાં ટહેલે છે.
>
> *jamyā pachī dādā ā̃gṇāmā̃ ṭahele che.*
>
> After eating, grandfather walks up and down the courtyard.

Past (he …): તે આંગણામાં ટહેલ્યો, *te ā̃gṇāmā̃ ṭahelyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટહેલવું** (*ṭahelvũ*) goes back to *\*ṭahall*, "to walk up and down", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, in the sense of going round from house to house, and Hindi *ṭahalnā*, "to take a walk" (CDIAL 5453).
