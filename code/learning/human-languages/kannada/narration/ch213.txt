5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕೋಟೆ (kōṭe) — a fort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ground, then the Kannada for a direction.

You'll want to know: ಕೋಟೆ.
ಕೋಟೆ — kōṭe — "a fort".

The word, taken apart.
ಕೋಟೆ (kōṭe) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kōṭṭai (DEDR 2207). DEDR lists kōṭe itself, "fort, rampart". DEDR 2207 compares Sanskrit koṭṭa.

The word, taken apart.
ವಸ್ತುಸಂಗ್ರಹಾಲಯ (vastusaṅgrahālaya) is three Sanskrit words: vastu, "thing" (CDIAL 11449), saṅgraha, "collection" (CDIAL 12852), and ālaya, "house" (CDIAL 1366): "a house for a collection of things".
