## You'll want to know: लहरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C472-HATTI472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लहरी** — *lahrī* — "moody; whimsical".

> आमचे मामा लहरी आहेत, कधी हसतात तर कधी रागावतात.
>
> *āmce māmā lahrī āhet, kadhī hastāt tar kadhī rāgāvtāt.*
>
> Our uncle is moody: sometimes he laughs, sometimes he gets angry.

From लहर, a wave or a whim, + -ई. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लहरी** (*lahrī*) is a Marathi adjective built on लहर, "wave, caprice", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *laharī*, "large wave" (CDIAL 10999).
