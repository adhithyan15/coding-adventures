Portuguese, chapter 159: Battery, Charger, Singer, Show, Actor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a bateria — a battery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a law, then the Portuguese for a court.

You'll want to know: a bateria.
a bateria — "a battery".

The word, taken apart.
bateria from French batterie, "a battering, a set of guns" — borrowed. The French word comes from battre, "to beat".

The word, taken apart.
carregador is built from the verb "to load" and -dor, "one that does" (EtymDB 2.1). The verb goes back to Latin carrus, "wagon".
