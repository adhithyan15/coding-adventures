## You'll want to know: memorizzare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C382-RIPASSO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**memorizzare** — "to memorise, to learn by heart".

> Devo memorizzare venti parole al giorno.
>
> *I have to memorise twenty words a day.*

On a phone, also to save: *memorizzare un numero*. *Imparare a memoria* is more colloquial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*memorizzare* is built on *memoria* "memory", a learned borrowing from Latin *memoria* (Lewis & Short), with the verb ending *-izzare*; the modern word was probably helped along by English *memorize*.
