## You'll want to know: rapāx, rapācis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C450-QUERELA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rapāx, rapācis** — "grasping, greedy".

> Caupō rapāx magnum pretium prō cēnā parvā rogat.
>
> *The grasping innkeeper asks a big price for a small dinner.*

An adjective of one ending: *rapāx*, genitive *rapācis*. Also of wild beasts: ravenous. English *rapacious* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rapāx* is built on *rapiō* 'to seize, to snatch' with the suffix *-āx* 'given to' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *rapacious* comes from it.
