## You'll want to know: おや
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C477-PARENT477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おや** — *oya* — "a parent".

> **おやは こどもを しんぱいします。** — *oya wa kodomo o shinpai shimasu* — "Parents worry about their children."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おや** (*oya*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
