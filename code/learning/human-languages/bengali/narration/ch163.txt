Lesson 1 of 5.
মালপত্র (mālpôtrô) — luggage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for whole, then the Bengali for slight.

You'll want to know: মালপত্র.
মালপত্র — mālpôtrô — "luggage".

The word, taken apart.
মালপত্র (mālpôtrô) is māl, "goods", from Arabic māl, "property", which reached Bengali through Persian (Lane), + pôtrô, Sanskrit pattra, "leaf, letter", here meaning "and such things".

Lesson 2 of 5.
আসবাব (āsbāb) — furniture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for slight, then the Bengali for luggage.

You'll want to know: আসবাব.
আসবাব — āsbāb — "furniture".

The word, taken apart.
আসবাব (āsbāb) is a loan from Arabic asbāb, "means, gear", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is s-b-b (Lane). It is the plural of sabab, "a cord; a means".
