## You'll want to know: पुतला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C332-PARAMPARA332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुतला** — *putlā* — "an effigy; a large doll".

> त्योहार की रात लोगों ने एक बड़ा पुतला जलाया।
>
> *tyohār kī rāt logoṁ ne ek baṛā putlā jalāyā.*
>
> On the festival night people burned a huge effigy.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from *\*putrala*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8269), with the sense of a doll. A huge **पुतला** of a demon is burned at some festivals.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
