## You'll want to know: 心疼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C458-MOOD458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**心疼** — *xīnténg* — "to feel for someone; to love dearly".

> **孩子病了妈妈很心疼** *háizi bìng le māma hěn xīnténg* — The child is ill, and her mother's heart aches for her.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**心疼** (*xīnténg*) is built from 心 'heart' and 疼 'pain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim* and *downg* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'pain'. The regular sound change would give *tóng* for the second; why the vowel differs is not settled.
