## You'll want to know: 花店
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C224-BLOOM224-02]; assesses=[] -->

**花店** — *huādiàn* — "florist's; flower shop".

> **我去花店买花** *wǒ qù huādiàn mǎi huā* — I'm going to the florist's to buy flowers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**花店** (*huādiàn*) is built from 花 'flower' and 店 'shop', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwae* and *temH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
