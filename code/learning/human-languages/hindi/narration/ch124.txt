Hindi, chapter 124: Hungry, Thirsty, Sleepy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भूखा (bhūkhā) — hungry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for what's going on, then the Hindi for have you eaten.

You'll want to know: भूखा.
भूखा (bhūkhā) — "hungry". A woman says भूखी.
It is भूख, "hunger", made into a description. मैं भूखा (bhūkhā) हूँ — "I am hungry".

What you've built.
Hunger, made into how you are.

Lesson 3 of 5.
नींद आ रही है (nīnd ā rahī hai) — I'm sleepy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for hungry, then the Hindi for thirsty.

You'll want to know: नींद आ रही है.
नींद आ रही है (nīnd ā rahī hai) — "I'm sleepy", literally "sleep is coming".
नींद is the sleep you know. Sleep comes to you, the way anger and laughter do.

What you've built.
Sleep, which comes to you.
