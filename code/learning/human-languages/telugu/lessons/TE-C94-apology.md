## You'll want to know: క్షమాపణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**క్షమాపణ** (*kṣamāpaṇa*) — "an apology", the noun.

**క్షమాపణ చెప్పండి** — "say sorry". It comes from the same root as **క్షమించండి**: to forgive,
and to ask forgiveness.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The apology as a thing you can give.
