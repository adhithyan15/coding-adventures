Portuguese, chapter 47: Cake, Honey, Lunch, Dinner, Lemon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o bolo — a cake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a carrot, then the Portuguese for beans.

You'll want to know: o bolo.
o bolo — "a cake".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
