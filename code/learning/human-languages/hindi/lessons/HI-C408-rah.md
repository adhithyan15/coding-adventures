## You'll want to know: राह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C408-MIL408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**राह** — *rāh* — "a path; a way (literary)".

> यह राह जंगल से होकर जाती है।
>
> *yah rāh jaṅgal se hokar jātī hai.*
>
> This path goes through the forest.

Feminine.

Songs and sayings prefer राह; in plain talk people say रास्ता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राह** (*rāh*) is usually traced to Persian *rāh*, "a road, a way", the word inside चौराहा, where four ways meet.
