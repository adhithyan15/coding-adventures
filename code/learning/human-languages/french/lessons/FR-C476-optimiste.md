## You'll want to know: optimiste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C476-INQUIETUDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**optimiste** — "optimistic; optimist".

> Malgré la pluie, elle reste optimiste.
>
> *In spite of the rain, she stays optimistic.*

The same form for a man and a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*optimiste* is built on *optimisme*, a modern learned word made from Latin *optimus* "best" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
