## You'll want to know: धूड़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C172-JHADU172-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धूड़** — *dhūṛ* — "dust".

> मेज माथै धूड़ है।
>
> *mej māthai dhūṛ hai.*
>
> There is dust on the table.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूड़** (*dhūṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhūḍi*, "dust, powder"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *dhūli* and Gujarati *dhūṛ* (CDIAL 6835).
