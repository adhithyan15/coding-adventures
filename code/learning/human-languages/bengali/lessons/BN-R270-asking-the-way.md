# Review — which way, and how far

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C270-ASK-WAY-01, BN-LEX-C270-DUR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask "How far is the station?" (*sṭeshôn kôtô dūr?*)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C270-DUR-01, BN-SCRIPT-C270-UU-KAR-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C270-ASK-WAY-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C270-GIVE-WAY-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-THINK-NOT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-UCHIT-SHOULD-01, BN-LEX-C83-TOWN83-05, BN-LEX-C44-BODY44-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Excuse me, how do I get to the hospital?]
- [YOU SAY: Go straight, then turn right at the corner.]
- [YOU SAY: The hotel is next to the station. It isn't far.]
- [YOU SAY: How far is the river? It's a ten-minute walk.]
- [YOU SAY: I don't think the market is far, but you ought to take a bus.]
- [YOU WRITE: **দূর**, then **উচিত**]
