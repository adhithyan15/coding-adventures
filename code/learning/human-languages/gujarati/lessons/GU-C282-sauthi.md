# સૌથી (sauthī) — most; of all

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C281-VU-CHE-01, GU-LEX-C72-SIZE-02, GU-LEX-C187-PRICE187-03, GU-LEX-GAMVU] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I want to become a doctor". (*māre dāktar banvũ che*.) Then "small", "more" and "to like". (**નાનું**, **વધારે**, **ગમવું**.)

## You'll want to know: સૌથી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C282-SAUTHI-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C93-TIME93-04] -->

**સૌથી** — *sauthī* — "most; of all". Put it before a describing word and it becomes the most of all:

> **મીરા સૌથી નાની છે.**
>
> *Mīrā sauthī nānī che.* — "Mira is the youngest."

**નાની** follows Mira; **સૌથી** stays as it is. With **વધારે** it is "most of all":

> **મને ચોમાસું સૌથી વધારે ગમે છે.**
>
> *mane comāsũ sauthī vadhāre game che.* — "I like the monsoon most of all."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૌથી** (*sauthī*) is built from *sau*, "all, everyone", and **-થી**, "from": "from all", so "more than all". CDIAL lists Gujarati *sau*, "wholly", under Sanskrit *sarva*, "all, whole" (CDIAL 13276).
