## You'll want to know: ਉੱਡਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C318-MISHAP318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਡਣਾ** — *uḍḍṇā* — "to fly".

> ਤੇਜ਼ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੀ ਟੋਪੀ ਉੱਡੀ।
>
> *tez havā vich merī ṭopī uḍḍī.*
>
> My cap blew away in the strong wind.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੰਛੀ ਰੁੱਖ ਤੋਂ ਉੱਡਿਆ, *oh panchhī rukkh ton uḍḍiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਡਣਾ** (*uḍḍṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uḍḍīyate*, "flies up"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *uḍḍṇā*, "flies", under it (CDIAL 1697).
