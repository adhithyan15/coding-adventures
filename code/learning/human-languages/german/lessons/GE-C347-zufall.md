## You'll want to know: der Zufall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C347-ZUFALL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zufall** — "the chance, the coincidence".

> Was für ein Zufall! Wir sind im selben Hotel.
>
> *What a coincidence! We are in the same hotel.*

Plural: *die Zufälle*. *Durch Zufall* — by chance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zufall* is built from *zu* "to" and *Fall* "fall, case", from Old High German *val* (EtymDB): what falls to you. It is usually explained as a rendering of Latin *accidēns*, from *accidere* "to fall upon, to happen" (Lewis & Short).
