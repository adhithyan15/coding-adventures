## You'll want to know: ਮੋਢਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C324-BREATH324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਢਾ** — *moḍhā* — "a shoulder".

> ਪਿਤਾ ਜੀ ਬੱਚੇ ਨੂੰ ਮੋਢੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਚੁੱਕਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *pitā jī bachche nū̃ moḍhe utte chukkde han.*
>
> Father carries the child on his shoulder.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਢਾ** (*moḍhā*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*maurdhaka*, "pertaining to the head"; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Lahnda *moḍhā*, "shoulder" (CDIAL 10372).
