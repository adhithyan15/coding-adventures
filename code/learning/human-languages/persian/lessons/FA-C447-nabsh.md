## You'll want to know: نبش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C447-NABSH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نبش** (*nabsh*) — "a (street) corner".

> نانوایی در نبش خیابان است.
>
> *Nânvâyi dar nabsh-e khiyâbân ast.*
>
> The bakery is on the corner of the street.

Used in addresses: *nabsh-e kuche-ye dovvom* — on the corner of the second lane.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *nabsh* is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it. It is the corner where a lane meets a street, and it appears in many Iranian addresses.
