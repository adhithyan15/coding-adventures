Lesson 1 of 7.
विजयते (vijayate) — triumphs; wins through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for co-operation, then the Sanskrit for a kindness.

You'll want to know: विजयते.
विजयते — vijayate — "triumphs; wins through".
यदि धैर्यम् अस्ति, तर्हि सः अवश्यं विजयते। yadi dhairyam asti, tarhi saḥ avaśyaṃ vijayate. If he has courage, he will surely win through.
Past (he …): सः शत्रुं विजितवान्, saḥ śatruṃ vijitavān.

The word, taken apart.
विजयते (vijayate) is built on vi and the root √ji, "to win"; MW gives vi-√ji "to conquer, to be victorious" (MW).

You'll want to know: अविचलः.
अविचलः — avicalaḥ — "unshakable; steady".
यदि मनः अविचलम्, तर्हि भयं न भवति। yadi manaḥ avicalam, tarhi bhayaṃ na bhavati. If the mind is unshakable, there is no fear.

The word, taken apart.
अविचलः (avicalaḥ) is built from a-, "not", and vi-cala, "moving about", from the root √cal, "to move"; MW gives a-vicala "immovable, steady, firm" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 7.
निरन्तरम् (nirantaram) — without a break; constantly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for triumphs, then the Sanskrit for unshakable.

You'll want to know: निरन्तरम्.
निरन्तरम् — nirantaram — "without a break; constantly".
यदि भवान् निरन्तरं पठति, तर्हि परीक्षायाम् उत्तमः भविष्यति। yadi bhavān nirantaraṃ paṭhati, tarhi parīkṣāyām uttamaḥ bhaviṣyati. If you study without a break, you will do well in the exam.

The word, taken apart.
निरन्तरम् (nirantaram) is built from nir, "without", and antara, "an interval"; MW gives nir-antaram "closely" and "constantly, continually" (MW).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: तत्परः.
तत्परः — tatparaḥ — "intent on; fully engaged".
यदि छात्रः अध्ययने तत्परः, तर्हि सः सफलतां प्राप्नोति। yadi chātraḥ adhyayane tatparaḥ, tarhi saḥ saphalatāṃ prāpnoti. If a student is intent on study, he succeeds.

The word, taken apart.
तत्परः (tatparaḥ) is built from tat, "that", and para, "highest"; MW gives tat-para "having that as one's highest object, totally devoted to, eagerly engaged in" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for without a break, then the Sanskrit for intent on.

You'll want to know: कृतकृत्यः.
कृतकृत्यः — kṛtakṛtyaḥ — "fulfilled; content with what one has done".
यदि सः लक्ष्यं प्राप्नोति, तर्हि कृतकृत्यः भविष्यति। yadi saḥ lakṣyaṃ prāpnoti, tarhi kṛtakṛtyaḥ bhaviṣyati. If he reaches his goal, he will feel fulfilled.

The word, taken apart.
कृतकृत्यः (kṛtakṛtyaḥ) is built from kṛta, "done", and kṛtya, "what is to be done", both from the root √kṛ; MW gives kṛta-kṛtya "one who has done his duty" and "contented, satisfied" (MW).
