## You'll want to know: झेलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C531-KHELADU531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झेलणे** — *jhelṇe* — "to catch a ball".

> मीराने एका हाताने चेंडू झेलला.
>
> *mīrāne ekā hātāne ceṇḍū jhellā.*
>
> Meera caught the ball with one hand.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चेंडू झेलला, *tyāne ceṇḍū jhellā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झेलणे** (*jhelṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *jhelṇẽ*, "to catch", under *\*jhēlati*, "undergoes" (CDIAL 5413).
