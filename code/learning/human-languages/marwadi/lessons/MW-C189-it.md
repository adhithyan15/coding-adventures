## You'll want to know: ईंट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C189-THANBHO189-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ईंट** — *ī̃ṭ* — "a brick".

> ईंट लाल है।
>
> *ī̃ṭ lāl hai.*
>
> The brick is red.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ईंट** (*ī̃ṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *iṣṭakā*, "a brick"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ī̃ṭ* (CDIAL 1600). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
