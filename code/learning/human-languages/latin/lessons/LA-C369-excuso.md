## You'll want to know: excūsō, excūsāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C369-HORTOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**excūsō, excūsāre** — "to excuse; to plead as an excuse".

> Morbum excūsāvit.
>
> *He pleaded illness as his excuse.*

*excūsō, excūsāre, excūsāvī, excūsātum*. *Sē excūsāre*, to excuse oneself. Built on *causa*: out of the case. English *excuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*excūsō* is built from *ex* "out of" and *causa* "a case, a charge" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to take someone out of the charge. English *excuse* comes from it.
