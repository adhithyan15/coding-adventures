## You'll want to know: o balneário
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C515-LESAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o balneário** — "changing room".

> Depois do jogo, os jogadores tomam banho no balneário.
>
> *After the match, the players shower in the changing room.*

In Brazil: *o vestiário*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*balneário* is a learned word built on Latin *balneārius* "of a bath" (Lewis & Short), from *balneum* "a bath".
