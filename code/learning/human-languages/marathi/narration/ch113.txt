Marathi, chapter 113: Shop, Road, Lane, Station, Car.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दुकान (dukān) — a shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for behind, then the Marathi for ahead.

You'll want to know: दुकान.
दुकान — dukān — "a shop".

The word, taken apart.
दुकान (dukān) is a loan from Arabic dukkān, "shop", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is d-k-n (Lane).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a shop, then the Marathi for a road.

You'll want to know: गल्ली.
गल्ली — gallī — "a lane".

The word, taken apart.
गल्ली (gallī) matches Hindi galī, "lane", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed galī, "defile, lane" (CDIAL 4085). CDIAL lists no Marathi form.

Lesson 5 of 5.
गाडी (gāḍī) — a car; a train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a lane, then the Marathi for a station.

You'll want to know: गाडी.
गाडी — gāḍī — "a car; a train".

The word, taken apart.
गाडी (gāḍī) comes from a reconstructed gāḍḍa, "cart" (CDIAL 4116). CDIAL suggests, despite the long vowel, that it may be the same word as its garda, "seat", comparing Sanskrit garta, "chariot seat".
