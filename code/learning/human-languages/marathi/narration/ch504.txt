Marathi, chapter 504: Trades of the Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सुतार (sutār) — a carpenter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for dance, then the Marathi for a stage.

You'll want to know: सुतार.
सुतार — sutār — "a carpenter".
सुताराने नवीन दार बनवलं. sutārāne navīn dār banavlaṁ. The carpenter made a new door.
Masculine.
In the past the oblique सुतारा- takes -ने: सुताराने दार बनवलं.

The word, taken apart.
सुतार (sutār) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit sūtradhāra, "carpenter" (CDIAL 13563), from sūtra, "a measuring line", and dhāra, "holding".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

गवंडी — gavaṇḍī — "a mason".
गवंड्याने विटांची भिंत बांधली. gavaṇḍyāne viṭāñcī bhint bāndhlī. The mason built a brick wall.
Masculine.
A builder in brick and stone; the oblique is गवंड्या-, as in गवंड्याने.

The word, taken apart.
गवंडी (gavaṇḍī) is the everyday Marathi word for a builder in brick and stone; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ठोकणे (ṭhokṇe) — to hammer in; to knock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a mason, then the Marathi for a barber.

You'll want to know: ठोकणे.
ठोकणे — ṭhokṇe — "to hammer in; to knock".
सुताराने भिंतीत एक खिळा ठोकला. sutārāne bhintīt ek khiḷā ṭhoklā. The carpenter hammered a nail into the wall.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने खिळा ठोकला, tyāne khiḷā ṭhoklā.
The place takes -त: भिंतीत खिळा ठोकणे (ṭhokṇe), to drive a nail into a wall.

The word, taken apart.
ठोकणे (ṭhokṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ṭhokṇẽ, "to strike", under ṭhōkk, "knock" (CDIAL 5513), a sound word shared with Hindi ṭhoknā.
