## You'll want to know: काटकसर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C557-KUVAT557-03]; assesses=[] -->

**काटकसर** — *kāṭkasar* — "thrift".

> आजीने काटकसर करून थोडे पैसे वाचवले.
>
> *ājīne kāṭkasar karūn thoḍe paise vācavle.*
>
> By being thrifty Grandmother saved a little money.

Feminine.

काटकसर करणे, to economize.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काटकसर** (*kāṭkasar*) is a Marathi word for careful spending; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
