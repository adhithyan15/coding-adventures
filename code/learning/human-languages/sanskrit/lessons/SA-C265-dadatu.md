## Grammar Lens: one useful request form

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C72-HOUSEWORK-05] -->

You know **ददाति** (*dadāti*), “he or she gives.” For a gentle request, use
the complete form **ददातु** (*dadātu*): “let him or her give,” or in this
exchange, “please give.”

> **पानीयम् ददातु।**
>
> *Pānīyaṃ dadātu.*
>
> Please give water.

Here **ददातु** is the **third-person singular imperative**. Whitney,
*Sanskrit Grammar* §§617 and 668–669, records the present imperative and this
reduplicating root. Keep the whole form together. This is **not a complete
imperative paradigm**: other persons, numbers, and roots wait for later.
