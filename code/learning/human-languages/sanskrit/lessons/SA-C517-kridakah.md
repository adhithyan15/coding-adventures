## You'll want to know: क्रीडकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C517-CATURANGA517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रीडकः** — *krīḍakaḥ* — "a player".

> सः उत्तमः क्रीडकः अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ uttamaḥ krīḍakaḥ asti.*
>
> He is a good player.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रीडकः** (*krīḍakaḥ*) comes from the root √krīḍ, "to play", which also gives *krīḍati*, with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *krīḍaka* as one who sports, a player (MW).
