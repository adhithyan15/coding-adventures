## You'll want to know: slogarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C485-STORTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**slogarsi** — "to sprain, to dislocate".

> Giocando a tennis mi sono slogato la caviglia.
>
> *Playing tennis I sprained my ankle.*

*Slogarsi la caviglia* — to sprain one's ankle; in speech also *prendere una storta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*slogarsi* is built from *s-* "out of" and *luogo* "place", inherited from Latin *locus* (Lewis & Short): a joint put out of its place.
