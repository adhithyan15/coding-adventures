# श — the *sh* at the roof of the mouth

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

You write the plain *s*. Slide the tongue back along the roof of your mouth and it becomes something else.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-SHA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-LA-01, MR-SCRIPT-PA-01] -->

> श

**श** is *śa*, English *sh*. Marathi keeps it distinct from the **स** you learned
in your first chapter, and the pair behaves like every other pair in this
runway: same air, different place.

It opens *śahar*, "city" — one of the six labels the A1 form chapters will ask
you to fill in. Draw the left hook, the upright, and the headline; the small
loop at the top left is what separates it from **म**.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-SHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **श** twice with the model visible. Then write **स** once beside it and say
both, sliding the tongue back only for the first.
