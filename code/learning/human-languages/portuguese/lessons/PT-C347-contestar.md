## You'll want to know: contestar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C347-POLEMICA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**contestar** — "to dispute, to challenge".

> Os vizinhos contestaram a decisão da câmara.
>
> *The neighbours challenged the council's decision.*

*Contestar uma multa* — to dispute a fine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contestar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *contestārī* "to call to witness" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *con-* and *testārī* "to bear witness", on *testis* "witness".
