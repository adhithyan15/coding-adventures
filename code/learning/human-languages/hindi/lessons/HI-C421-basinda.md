## You'll want to know: बाशिंदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C421-BASTI421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बाशिंदा** — *bāśindā* — "a resident, an inhabitant".

> मैं इस शहर का पुराना बाशिंदा हूँ।
>
> *maiṁ is śahar kā purānā bāśindā hūṁ.*
>
> I am a long-time resident of this city.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाशिंदा** (*bāśindā*) is usually traced to Persian *bāšinda*, "one who stays, an inhabitant".
