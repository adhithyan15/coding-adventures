# పరిపాలన (paripālana) — administration; governance

## You'll want to know: పరిపాలన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C403-PARIPALANA403-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిపాలన** — *paripālana* — "administration; governance".

> మంచి పరిపాలన వల్ల జనం సంతోషంగా ఉన్నారు.
>
> *mañci paripālana valla janaṁ santōṣaṅgā unnāru.*
>
> Thanks to good administration the people are happy.

From the same word as పాలించు, with పరి-, all round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిపాలన** (*paripālana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paripālana*, "the act of guarding; fostering" (Monier-Williams), *pari-*, "all round", added to *pālana*.
