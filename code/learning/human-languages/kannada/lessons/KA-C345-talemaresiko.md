# ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ (talemaresiko) — to go into hiding

## You'll want to know: ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C345-ADAGU345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ** — *talemaresiko* — "to go into hiding".

> ಆರೋಪಿ ಆರು ತಿಂಗಳು ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊಂಡಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *ārōpi āru tiṅgaḷu talemaresikoṇḍiddanu.*
>
> The accused had been in hiding for six months.

Past (I …): ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊಂಡೆ, *talemaresikoṇḍe*.

Literally "to hide one's head": a news phrase for someone wanted by the police.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ** (*talemaresiko*) is a native Dravidian compound: *tale*, "head" (DEDR 3103), and *maresu*, "to hide", from *mare* (DEDR 4760), with -*ko*, from *koḷ*, "to take" (DEDR 2151).
