## Guided Practice — the distant band
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C01-DHONNOBAD-01, BN-CONCEPT-C03-KONOBEPARNA-01, BN-SCRIPT-YA-01] -->

- [YOU SAY: *thank you* — *dhônyobad*]
- [YOU SAY: "it's no matter / you're welcome" — *kono bæpār nā*]
- [YOU SAY: what **য** sounds like standing alone (***j***)]
- [YOU SAY: what it sounds like hung under another letter (**nothing** — it bends
  the vowel to **æ**)]

Both of the words above carry that hung **য**, and it is the same job in each:
the **ny** in *dhônyobad* and the **byæ** in *bæpār*. Two words from two different
chapters, one piece of machinery.
