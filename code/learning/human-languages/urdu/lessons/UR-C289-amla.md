## You'll want to know: عملہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C289-BUJHI289-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عملہ** — *amlā* — "the staff; a crew".

> عملے نے آگ بجھائی۔
>
> *amle ne āg bujhāī.*
>
> The crew put the fire out.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عملہ** (*amlā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿamala*, "workers": the people who do the work of an office, a hospital or a fire station.
