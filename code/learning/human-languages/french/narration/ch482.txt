French, chapter 482: Éternuements et allergies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
éternuer — to sneeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for splint, then the French for physiotherapist.

You'll want to know: éternuer.
éternuer — "to sneeze".
Au printemps, j'éternue tout le temps. In spring I sneeze all the time.
À vos souhaits! — bless you! (said after a sneeze).

The word, taken apart.
éternuer is inherited, through Old French esternuer, from Latin sternūtāre "to sneeze" (EtymDB), built on sternuere (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'allergie — allergy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for physiotherapist, then the French for to sneeze.

You'll want to know: l'allergie.
l'allergie — "allergy".
Mon fils a une allergie aux chats. My son has an allergy to cats.
Feminine. Être allergique à — to be allergic to.

The word, taken apart.
l'allergie was borrowed from German Allergie, a modern learned word built from Greek állos "other" and érgon "work, action".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la démangeaison — itch, itching.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for allergy, then the French for pollen.

You'll want to know: la démangeaison.
la démangeaison — "itch, itching".
Cette crème calme les démangeaisons. This cream soothes the itching.
The verb is démanger: ça me démange — it itches.

The word, taken apart.
la démangeaison is built on démanger "to itch" with -aison (EtymDB); démanger is manger "to eat", from Latin mandūcāre "to chew", with dé-: the skin feels eaten.

Lesson 5 of 5.
gratter — to scratch; to itch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for pollen, then the French for itch.

You'll want to know: gratter.
gratter — "to scratch; to itch".
Le chien se gratte derrière l'oreille. The dog is scratching behind its ear.
Se gratter — to scratch oneself. Ce pull gratte — this jumper is itchy.

The word, taken apart.
gratter comes from Frankish krattōn "to scratch", a Germanic word related to German kratzen (EtymDB).
