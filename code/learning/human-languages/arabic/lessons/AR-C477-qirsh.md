## You'll want to know: قرش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C477-QIRSH477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قرش** — *qirsh* — "a shark".

> رأى الصيادون قرشا بجانب القارب.
>
> *raʾā aṣ-ṣayyādūn qirshan bi-jānib al-qārib.*
>
> The fishermen saw a shark beside the boat.

*qirsh* is also an old coin, a piastre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرش** (*qirsh*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *q-r-sh*, "to gather, to scrape together".
