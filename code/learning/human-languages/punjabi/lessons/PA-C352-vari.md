## You'll want to know: ਵਾਰੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C352-BOARD352-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਰੀ** — *vārī* — "one's turn (in a game or a queue)".

> ਹੁਣ ਮੇਰੀ ਵਾਰੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *huṇ merī vārī hai.*
>
> Now it's my turn.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਰੀ** (*vārī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vāra*, "appointed time, one's turn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vār*, "occasion", and Lahnda *vārī*, "turn, time", under it (CDIAL 11547).
