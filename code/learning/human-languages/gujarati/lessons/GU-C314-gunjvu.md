## You'll want to know: ગુંજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C314-HADTAL314-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુંજવું** — *gunjvũ* — "to resound".

> આખો ચોક સૂત્રોથી ગુંજતો હતો.
>
> *ākho cok sūtrothī gunjto hato.*
>
> The whole square was ringing with slogans.

Past (it …): તે ચોક ગુંજ્યો, *te cok gunjyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુંજવું** (*gunjvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *guñja*, "humming", a sound word; CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gū̃j*, an echo, but not the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 4173). A place **ગુંજે છે**, rings, with voices or music.
