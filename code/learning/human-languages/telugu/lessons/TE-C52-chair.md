## You'll want to know: కుర్చీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C52-WELCOME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కుర్చీ** (*kurcī*) — "a chair".

Arabic *kursī* is a seat, a throne — the word standing in the Qur'ān's Throne Verse. Persian took it, Urdu carried it across the Deccan, and Telugu has కుర్చీ.

The word came because the thing came. A house welcoming a guest before the chair arrived offered a పీట, a low wooden plank set on the floor, and many still do. Borrowed furniture brings borrowed nouns with it.

One trap: కుర్చీ and కూర్చో, "sit", look like a pair and are not. The verb is inherited, the noun is Arabic, and the resemblance is an accident. Sound alone never proves a relation.
