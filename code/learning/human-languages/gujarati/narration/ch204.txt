Gujarati, chapter 204: Reading the timetable.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સમયપત્રક (samaypatrak) — timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to drop off, then the Gujarati for to see off.

You'll want to know: સમયપત્રક.
સમયપત્રક — samaypatrak — "timetable".
સ્ટેશન પર નવું સમયપત્રક હતું. — sṭeśan par navũ samaypatrak hatũ — "There was a new timetable at the station."
Built on સમય, samay, 'time'.

The word, taken apart.
સમયપત્રક (samaypatrak) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit samaya, "time", and patraka, "leaf, sheet", a form of páttra, "leaf" (CDIAL 7733).

Lesson 2 of 5.
પૂછપરછ (pūchparach) — enquiry, enquiries.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to see off, then the Gujarati for timetable.

You'll want to know: પૂછપરછ.
પૂછપરછ — pūchparach — "enquiry, enquiries".
મેં પૂછપરછમાં સમય પૂછ્યો. — mẽ pūchparachmā̃ samay pūchyo — "I asked the time at the enquiry desk."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પૂછપરછ (pūchparach) is built on pūch, "asking", doubled with a rhyming partner. CDIAL lists Gujarati puch-parach, "questioning", under Sanskrit pṛcchā, "asking" (CDIAL 8354), the noun beside pṛccháti, "asks", which gave pūchvũ, "to ask" (CDIAL 8352).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઊપડવું (ūpaḍvũ) — to start off, to depart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for enquiry, then the Gujarati for platform.

You'll want to know: ઊપડવું.
ઊપડવું — ūpaḍvũ — "to start off, to depart".
ટ્રેન સવારે ઊપડી. — ṭren savāre ūpaḍī — "The train left in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
ઊપડવું (ūpaḍvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it as upaṛvũ, "to move away, depart, be lifted, rise", under Sanskrit útpatati, "flies up, ascends" (CDIAL 1810).
