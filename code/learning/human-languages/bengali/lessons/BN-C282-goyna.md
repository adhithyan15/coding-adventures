## You'll want to know: গয়না
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C282-CHURI282-02]; assesses=[] -->

**গয়না** — *gôynā* — "jewellery; an ornament".

> চোর আলমারি খুলে দিদিমার গয়না নিয়ে পালাল।
>
> *chor ālmāri khule didimār gôynā niye pālālô.*
>
> The thief opened the cupboard and ran off with my grandmother's jewellery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গয়না** (*gôynā*) is filed in CDIAL 4364 under Sanskrit *grahaṇa*, 'seizing, holding; putting on clothes', where Bengali *gaynā* appears; how it came to mean an ornament is not set out there.
