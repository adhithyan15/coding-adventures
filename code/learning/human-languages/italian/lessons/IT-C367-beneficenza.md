## You'll want to know: la beneficenza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C367-VOLONTARIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la beneficenza** — "the charity (giving)".

> I soldi del concerto vanno in beneficenza.
>
> *The money from the concert goes to charity.*

*Dare in beneficenza* — to give to charity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beneficenza* is a learned borrowing from Latin *beneficentia* "kindness, doing good" (Lewis & Short), built from *bene* "well" and *facere* "to do".
