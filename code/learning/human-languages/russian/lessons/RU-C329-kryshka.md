## You'll want to know: крышка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C329-KIPYATIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**крышка** (*krýshka*) — "a lid".

> Крышка от кастрюли упала на пол.
>
> *Krýshka ot kastryúli upála na pol.*
>
> The saucepan lid fell on the floor.

Feminine; genitive plural *крышек*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*крышка* is built on *крыть* "to cover", an inherited Slavic verb, with the ending *-шка*: a little cover. EtymDB lists no parent.
