## You'll want to know: পিয়ন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C411-DAKGHOR411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**পিয়ন** — *piyôn* — "a postman; an office messenger".

> পিয়ন দুপুরে আমাদের চিঠি দিয়ে গেল।
>
> *piyôn dupure āmāder chiṭhi diye gelô.*
>
> The postman left our letters at midday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পিয়ন** (*piyôn*) is usually traced to Portuguese *peão*, "a foot-soldier, a man on foot", the same word as English *peon*.
