# ನಲುಗು (nalugu) — to droop; to be worn down

## You'll want to know: ನಲುಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C515-KURUDA515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಲುಗು** — *nalugu* — "to droop; to be worn down".

> ಬಿಸಿಲಿಗೆ ತೋಟದ ಹೂವುಗಳು ನಲುಗಿದವು.
>
> *bisilige tōṭada hūvugaḷu nalugidavu.*
>
> The garden flowers drooped in the sun.

Past (I …): ನಲುಗಿದೆ, *nalugide*.

Of people too: ಕಷ್ಟದಿಂದ ನಲುಗು, to be worn down by hardship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಲುಗು** (*nalugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3611 lists Kannada *nalagu*, *nalugu*, "to become rumpled, to droop, to grow weak or faint from sickness, trouble or grief".
