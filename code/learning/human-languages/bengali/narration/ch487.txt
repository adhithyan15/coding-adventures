Lesson 1 of 5.
সম্পর্ক (sômpôrkô) — a relationship; a connection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to put to sleep, then the Bengali for to crawl.

You'll want to know: সম্পর্ক.
সম্পর্ক — sômpôrkô — "a relationship; a connection".
দুই ভাইয়ের সম্পর্ক ভালো। dui bhāiyer sômpôrkô bhālo. The two brothers get on well.

The word, taken apart.
সম্পর্ক (sômpôrkô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit samparka, "mixing together, union, contact" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: ছোঁয়া.
ছোঁয়া — chhõyā — "to touch".
বাচ্চাটা গরম কেটলিটা ছুঁল। bāchchāṭā gôrôm keṭliṭā chhũlô. The child touched the hot kettle.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শিশুর মাথা ছুঁলেন, tini shishur māthā chhũlen.
As a noun, a touch: মায়ের ছোঁয়া (chhõyā), a mother's touch.

ছোঁয়া (chhõyā) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under Sanskrit chupati, "touches" (CDIAL 5055); ছোঁয়াচে, contagious, is built on it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
আগলানো (āglāno) — to shelter; to guard protectively.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a lap, then the Bengali for to touch.

You'll want to know: আগলানো.
আগলানো — āglāno — "to shelter; to guard protectively".
মুরগিটা বাচ্চাদের আগলে রাখল। murgiṭā bāchchāder āgle rākhlô. The hen kept her chicks sheltered.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নাতিকে আগলে রাখলেন, tini nātike āgle rākhlen.
Usually with রাখা: আগলে রাখা, to keep sheltered.

The word, taken apart.
আগলানো (āglāno) is a Bengali verb for keeping someone safe inside your guard; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

দিদিমা নাতির মাথায় হাত বোলালেন। didimā nātir māthāy hāt bolālen. Grandmother stroked her grandson's head.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কুকুরটার পিঠে হাত বোলালেন, tini kukurṭār piṭhe hāt bolālen.
Its object is the hand: মাথায় হাত বোলানো (bolāno), to stroke someone's head.

The word, taken apart.
বোলানো (bolāno) is a Bengali verb for passing the hand gently over something; its origin is not settled.
