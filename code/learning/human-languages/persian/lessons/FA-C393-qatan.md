## You'll want to know: قطعا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C393-ETMINAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قطعا** (*qat'an*) — "definitely, certainly".

> او قطعا فردا میآید.
>
> *u qat'an fardâ miâyad.*
>
> He is definitely coming tomorrow.

Close to *hatman* in meaning, and a little more formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قطعا** is usually traced to Arabic *qaṭ'an* "decisively", an adverb built on *qaṭ'* "cutting", like *qat'i* "definite".
