## You'll want to know: رینگنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C316-SURANG316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رینگنا** — *rengnā* — "to crawl".

> وہ سرنگ میں رینگ رہے تھے۔
>
> *voh surang meṅ reng rahe the.*
>
> They were crawling through the tunnel.

Past (he …): وہ رینگا, *voh rengā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رینگنا** (*rengnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *rẽgnā*, "to crawl", under Sanskrit *ríṅgati* (CDIAL 10739). Babies, snakes and soldiers under wire all **رینگتے ہیں**.
