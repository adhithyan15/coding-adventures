## You'll want to know: きつね
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C300-KITSUNE300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きつね** — *kitsune* — "a fox".

> **やまに きつねが すんでいました。** — *yama ni kitsune ga sunde imashita* — "A fox lived in the mountains."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きつね** (*kitsune*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
