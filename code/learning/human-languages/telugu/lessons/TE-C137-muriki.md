## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మురికి** (*murikī*) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5007 lists *muṟiki*, "dirt, filth; dirty", with Tamil *muṟi*, "to be spoiled", and Kannada *muṟikil*, "impurity".
