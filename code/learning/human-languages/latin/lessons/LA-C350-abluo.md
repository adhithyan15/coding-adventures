## You'll want to know: abluō, abluere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C350-OBSONO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**abluō, abluere** — "to wash off, to wash clean".

> Coquus holera aquā frīgidā abluit.
>
> *The cook washed the vegetables in cold water.*

*abluō, abluere, abluī, ablūtum*. English *ablution*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**abluō** joins *ab*, "away", and *luō*, "to wash", a cousin of *lavō* (EtymDB). English *ablution* comes from it.
