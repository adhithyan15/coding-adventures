Lesson 1 of 5.
der Arbeitsplatz — a workplace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to repeat, then the German for to pull.

You'll want to know: der Arbeitsplatz.
der Arbeitsplatz — "a workplace".

The word, taken apart.
Arbeitsplatz is Arbeit + the linking -s- + Platz, "place" (EtymDB 2.1): a place of work.

The word, taken apart.

You'll want to know: die Stelle.
die Stelle — "a job, a position".

The word, taken apart.
Stelle, "a place", is the noun beside stellen, "to put" from Proto-Germanic stallijaną (EtymDB 2.1). A job is a place you are put in.
