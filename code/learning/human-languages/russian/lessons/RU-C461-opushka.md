## You'll want to know: опушка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C461-POLYANA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**опушка** (*opúshka*) — "the edge of a forest".

> На опушке леса стоит маленький дом.
>
> *Na opúshke lésa stoít málenkiy dom.*
>
> There is a small house at the edge of the forest.

Feminine. *На опушке* — at the forest edge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*опушка* is usually linked with *опушить* 'to edge with fur', on *пух* 'down': the forest's fringe. EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the line is hedged.
