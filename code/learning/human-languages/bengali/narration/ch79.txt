Bengali, chapter 79: Year, Minute, Today, Now, Always.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বছর (bôchhôr) — a year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a week, then the Bengali for a month.

You'll want to know: বছর.
বছর — bôchhôr — "a year".

The word, taken apart.
বছর (bôchhôr) goes back to Sanskrit vatsara, "year" (CDIAL 11242). CDIAL asks whether the Bengali form was borrowed from Sanskrit rather than inherited.

You'll want to know: আজ.
আজ — āj — "today".

The word, taken apart.
আজ (āj) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from adya, "today" (CDIAL 242).

Lesson 4 of 5.
এখন (ekhôn) — now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a minute, then the Bengali for today.

You'll want to know: এখন.
এখন — ekhôn — "now".

The word, taken apart.
এখন (ekhôn) is the near head e- on -khôn, from Sanskrit kṣaṇa, "moment": CDIAL lists takhan, "then", under Sanskrit tatkṣaṇa, "that very moment", with the same ending on the other pronoun stems (CDIAL 5640).

সবসময় (sôbsômôy) is sab, "all", from Sanskrit sarva, "all" (CDIAL 13276), + sômôy, "time", Sanskrit samaya taken over whole.
