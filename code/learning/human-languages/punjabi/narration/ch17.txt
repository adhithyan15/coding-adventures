Punjabi, chapter 17: The Second Form Line: Language.
4 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
Select one known language value — select between two separately practised language values.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — attach one cue to each value until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Read the closed bank once:
A — ਪੰਜਾਬੀ B — ਹਿੰਦੀ.

Script — attach one cue to each value.
Point to A, then ਪੰਜਾਬੀ. Point to B, then ਹਿੰਦੀ. Cover the bank and say which value B requests. Selection is separate from spelling.

Guided Practice.
Reveal, point, cover, and select once more. Do not write the form line yet.

Wrap-up recall.
This cue belongs only to this two-item practice bank.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
In this closed practice bank, which Gurmukhi value is paired with B?


Lesson 2 of 4.
Fill one supported language field — fill one language line with a visible two-value bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Keep this bank visible:
A — ਪੰਜਾਬੀ B — ਹਿੰਦੀ.

Writing — model visible.
Complete ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: __________ (language field after a short delay) for A. Read the label, select the A value, and copy it. This is supported entry, not independent writing evidence.

Guided Practice.
Compare selection, spelling, spacing, and placement in separate passes.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one supported line.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
With the two-value bank visible, complete the language field for A.


Lesson 3 of 4.
Hide, wait, then fill the language field — fill one language line after hiding the known bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read A — ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, B — ਹਿੰਦੀ once. Hide the bank and count slowly to ten.

Writing — short delayed entry.
With the bank hidden, complete ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: __________ (language field after a short delay) for B. Finish the attempt before revealing the bank.

Guided Practice.
Reveal and compare selection, spelling, spacing, then placement. Repair one dimension only.

Wrap-up recall.
One delayed line is enough today.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
After hiding the bank for ten seconds, complete the language field for B.


Lesson 4 of 4.
One no-model language field — choose and write one known language from a nonverbal cue with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Close the previous lesson. There is no value bank, support-language label, or romanized answer below.

Writing — independent controlled choice.
Complete the requested field:
A — ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: __________ (language field after a short delay).
Choose the known value attached to A and write it from memory. Do not reopen an earlier lesson until the attempt is complete.

Guided Practice — separate repair passes.
After writing, compare selection, spelling, spacing, and placement. Repair only the first differing dimension.

Wrap-up recall.
This closes only the language-field ladder. The remaining form fields are still separate backlog work.
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
With no bank, romanization, or copyable answer, complete the language field for A.
