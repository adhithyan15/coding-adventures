Telugu, chapter 360: Weapons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఖడ్గం (khaḍgaṁ) — a sword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a princess, then the Telugu for to found.

You'll want to know: ఖడ్గం.
ఖడ్గం — khaḍgaṁ — "a sword".
సైనికుడు ఖడ్గం పట్టుకుని నిలబడ్డాడు. sainikuḍu khaḍgaṁ paṭṭukuni nilabaḍḍāḍu. The soldier stood holding a sword.
A word of stories; at home a knife is కత్తి.

The word, taken apart.
ఖడ్గం (khaḍgaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit khaḍga, "a sword" (CDIAL 3787).

[pause 2 seconds]

కవచం — kavacaṁ — "armour; a shield".
సైనికులు ఇనుప కవచాలు ధరించారు. sainikulu inupa kavacālu dhariñcāru. The soldiers wore iron armour.

The word, taken apart.
కవచం (kavacaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit kavaca, "a coat of mail" (CDIAL 2957); CDIAL notes a view that the Sanskrit word itself came from Dravidian.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sword, then the Telugu for armour.

You'll want to know: ఆయుధం.
ఆయుధం — āyudhaṁ — "a weapon".
దొంగల దగ్గర ఆయుధాలు దొరికాయి. doṅgala daggara āyudhālu dorikāyi. Weapons were found on the robbers.
Plural: ఆయుధాలు, āyudhālu.

The word, taken apart.
ఆయుధం (āyudhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit āyudha, "a weapon" (CDIAL 1291), from yudh, "to fight", the root of యుద్ధం.

వేటగాడి దగ్గర ఒక విల్లు ఉంది. vēṭagāḍi daggara oka villu undi. The hunter has a bow.
A hunter's weapon in the old stories.

The word, taken apart.
విల్లు (villu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5422 lists Telugu villu, "a bow", with Tamil vil.

తుపాకి — tupāki — "a gun".
పోలీసు దగ్గర తుపాకి ఉంది. pōlīsu daggara tupāki undi. The policeman has a gun.
Plural: తుపాకులు, tupākulu.
తుపాకి (tupāki) పేల్చు, to fire a gun.

The word, taken apart.
తుపాకి (tupāki) is the Telugu word for a gun; its origin is not settled.
