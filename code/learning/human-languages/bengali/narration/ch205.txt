Bengali, chapter 205: Horn, Signal, Map, Kilo, Litre.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
হর্ন (hôrn) — a horn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a date, then the Bengali for a nut.

You'll want to know: হর্ন.
হর্ন — hôrn — "a horn".
