Marathi, chapter 480: Feeling Unwell.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तब्येत (tabyet) — one's health.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cell, then the Marathi for the innermost heart.

You'll want to know: तब्येत.
तब्येत — tabyet — "one's health".
आजीची तब्येत आता बरी आहे. ājīcī tabyet ātā barī āhe. Grandmother's health is better now.
Feminine.
तब्येत (tabyet) बरी नाही, I am unwell; तब्येतीची काळजी, care of one's health.

The word, taken apart.
तब्येत (tabyet) is a loan from Persian tabiyat, "nature, health", usually traced to Arabic.

Lesson 3 of 5.
थकवा (thakvā) — fatigue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for one's health, then the Marathi for a symptom.

You'll want to know: थकवा.
थकवा — thakvā — "fatigue".
प्रवासानंतर त्याला खूप थकवा आला. pravāsānantar tyālā khūp thakvā ālā. He felt very tired after the journey.
Masculine.
From थकणे, to get tired: थकवा (thakvā) येणे, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
थकवा (thakvā) is the Marathi noun of थकणे, "to get tired", which goes back to the reconstructed sthakk, "stop, halt" (CDIAL 13737).

Lesson 5 of 5.
मळमळणे (maḷmaḷṇe) — to feel sick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for fatigue, then the Marathi for weak.

You'll want to know: मळमळणे.
मळमळणे — maḷmaḷṇe — "to feel sick".
बसच्या प्रवासात मला मळमळतं. bascyā pravāsāt malā maḷmaḷtaṁ. I feel sick on bus journeys.
Past (to him …): त्याला मळमळलं, tyālā maḷmaḷlaṁ.
Dative and impersonal: मला मळमळतं, I feel queasy. मळमळ, nausea.

The word, taken apart.
मळमळणे (maḷmaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.
