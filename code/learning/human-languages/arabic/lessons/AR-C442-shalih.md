## You'll want to know: شاليه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C442-MUNTAJA442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شاليه** — *shālīh* — "a chalet".

> استأجرنا شاليه صغيرا على البحر.
>
> *istaʾjarnā shālīh ṣaghīran ʿalā al-baḥr.*
>
> We rented a small chalet by the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شاليه** (*shālīh*) is usually traced to French *chalet*.
