## You'll want to know: fraccionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C632-PAGO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**fraccionar** — "to split into instalments, to divide".

> Puedes fraccionar el pago en tres meses.
>
> *You can split the payment over three months.*

Common in shops and banks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fraccionar* is built on *fracción*, a learned borrowing from Latin *fractio* 'a breaking' (EtymDB), from *frangere* 'to break'.
