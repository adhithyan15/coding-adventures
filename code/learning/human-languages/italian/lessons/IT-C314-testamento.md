## You'll want to know: il testamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C314-EREDITA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il testamento** — "the will, the testament".

> Il nonno ha lasciato un testamento.
>
> *Grandfather left a will.*

*Fare testamento* — to make a will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testamento* is inherited from Latin *testāmentum* "a will" (EtymDB), built on *testārī* "to bear witness".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
