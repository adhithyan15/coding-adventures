## You'll want to know: मोती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C283-MOTI283-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मोती** — *motī* — "a pearl".

> यो मोती घणो महंगो है।
>
> *yo motī ghaṇo mahaṅgo hai.*
>
> This pearl is very expensive.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोती** (*motī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mauktika*, "a pearl"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *motī*, "pearl" (CDIAL 10365).
