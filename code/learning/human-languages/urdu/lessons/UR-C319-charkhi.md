## You'll want to know: چرخی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C319-BASANT319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چرخی** — *charkhī* — "a spool (for kite string)".

> بھائی نے چرخی پکڑی۔
>
> *bhāī ne charkhī pakṛī.*
>
> My brother held the spool.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چرخی** (*charkhī*) is usually traced to Persian *charkh*, "a wheel", with the small ending *-ī*: the little wheel the string winds on.
