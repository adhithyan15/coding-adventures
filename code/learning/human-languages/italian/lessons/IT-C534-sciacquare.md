## You'll want to know: sciacquare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C534-PIATTI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sciacquare** — "to rinse".

> Sciacquo i bicchieri sotto l'acqua.
>
> *I rinse the glasses under the water.*

*Sciacquarsi la bocca* — to rinse one's mouth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sciacquare* is inherited from Late Latin *exaquāre* (EtymDB), built from *ex-* and *aqua* "water".
