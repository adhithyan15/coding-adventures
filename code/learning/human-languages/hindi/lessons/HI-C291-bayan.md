## You'll want to know: बयान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C291-GAVAHI291-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बयान** — *bayān* — "a statement".

> गवाह ने थाने में अपना बयान दिया।
>
> *gavāh ne thāne meṁ apnā bayān diyā.*
>
> The witness gave a statement at the police station.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बयान** (*bayān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bayān*, "an explanation, a making clear". **बयान देना** is to give a statement.
