## You'll want to know: humilde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C470-MODESTIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**humilde** — "humble".

> Apesar do sucesso, a cantora continua humilde.
>
> *Despite her success, the singer is still humble.*

Of a family or a house it means poor: *uma casa humilde*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*humilde* is inherited from Latin *humilis* "low, lowly" (EtymDB), built on *humus* "earth, ground".
