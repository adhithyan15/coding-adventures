## You'll want to know: guì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C42-SHOP42-05]; assesses=[] -->

*guì* — "expensive".

> *nàge tài guì le* — That one's too expensive.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guì* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kjw+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'honoured, high'. The price sense is not given there; from valued to costly is a likely step, but it is not settled.
