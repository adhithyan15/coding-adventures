## You'll want to know: circumeō, circumīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C454-DEXTRORSUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**circumeō, circumīre** — "to go round, to walk round; to visit in turn".

> Via mūrōs oppidī circumit.
>
> *The road goes round the walls of the town.*

Irregular like *eō*: *circumeō, circumīre, circumiī, circumitum*. Often written *circueō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*circumeō* is built from *circum* 'around' and *eō* 'to go' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
