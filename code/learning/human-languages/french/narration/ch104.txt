French, chapter 104: Trousers, Shoes, Hat, Coat, Skirt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le pantalon — trousers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a shirt, then the French for a dress.

You'll want to know: le pantalon.
le pantalon — "trousers".

The word, taken apart.
pantalon — named after Pantalone (EtymDB 2.1), the old Venetian of Italian comedy, who wore long trousers.

Lesson 2 of 5.
les chaussures — shoes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a dress, then the French for trousers.

You'll want to know: les chaussures.
les chaussures — "shoes".

The word, taken apart.
chaussures is the plural of the word for "footwear", built from the verb for "to put on shoes", which from Latin calceare, from calceus, "a shoe" (Lewis & Short).
