# ਤੋੜਨਾ (toṛnā) — to break something

## You'll want to know: ਤੋੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C310-SLIP310-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੋੜਨਾ** — *toṛnā* — "to break something".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੈਂ ਰਸੋਈ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਥਾਲੀ ਤੋੜੀ।
>
> *kall maiṁ rasoī vich ikk thālī toṛī.*
>
> Yesterday I broke a plate in the kitchen.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ਾ ਤੋੜਿਆ, *maiṁ shīshā toṛiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੋੜਨਾ** (*toṛnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *troṭayati*, "breaks, tears"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *toṛṇā*, "to break, pluck", under it (CDIAL 6079).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
