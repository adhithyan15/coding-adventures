Persian, chapter 492: Feeling Unwell.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حالت تهوع (hâlat-e tahavvo') — nausea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an itch, then the Persian for to hiccup.

You'll want to know: حالت تهوع.
حالت تهوع (hâlat-e tahavvo') — "nausea".
در ماشین حالت تهوع میگیرم. dar mâshin hâlat-e tahavvo' migiram. I feel sick in the car.
hâlat 'state' + tahavvo' 'retching'. hâlat-e tahavvo' dâshtan — to feel sick.

The word, taken apart.
hâlat-e tahavvo' is built from two Arabic loans: hâlat, from Arabic ḥāla, 'state' (EtymDB), and tahavvo', usually traced to Arabic tahawwuʿ, 'retching'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
استفراغ کردن (estefrâgh kardan) — to vomit, to be sick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to hiccup, then the Persian for nausea.

You'll want to know: استفراغ کردن.
استفراغ کردن (estefrâgh kardan) — "to vomit, to be sick".
بچه در اتوبوس استفراغ کرد. bachche dar otobus estefrâgh kard. The child was sick on the bus.
estefrâgh 'vomiting' + kardan. More medical than bâlâ âvardan.

The word, taken apart.
estefrâgh is a loan, usually traced to Arabic istifrāġ, 'emptying out'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
غش کردن (ghash kardan) — to faint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for nausea, then the Persian for to vomit.

You'll want to know: غش کردن.
غش کردن (ghash kardan) — "to faint".
او در گرما غش کرد. u dar garmâ ghash kard. She fainted in the heat.
ghash 'a faint' + kardan. Colloquially also 'to laugh till you drop': az khande ghash kard.

The word, taken apart.
ghash is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ġašy, 'a fainting fit'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

صبحانه نخوردم و ضعف کردم. sobhâne nakhordam va za'f kardam. I skipped breakfast and felt faint.
za'f 'weakness' + kardan. The adjective is za'if 'weak'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عرق کردن ('araq kardan) — to sweat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to faint, then the Persian for to feel faint.

You'll want to know: عرق کردن.
عرق کردن ('araq kardan) — "to sweat".
در باشگاه خیلی عرق کردم. dar bâshgâh kheyli 'araq kardam. I sweated a lot at the gym.
'araq 'sweat' + kardan. 'araq also names a distilled herbal water, such as 'araq-e na'nâ 'mint water'.

The word, taken apart.
'araq comes from Arabic ʿaraq, 'sweat' (EtymDB). kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
