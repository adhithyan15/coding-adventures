## You'll want to know: もむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C448-STIFF448-02]; assesses=[] -->

**もむ** — *momu* — "to massage; to rub".

> **かたを もんで ください。** — *kata o monde kudasai* — "Please massage my shoulders."

Polite **もみます**, *momimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **むすめが そぼの かたを もみました。**, *musume ga sobo no kata o momimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もむ** (*momu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
