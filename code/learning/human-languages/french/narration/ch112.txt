Lesson 1 of 5.
la douche — a shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a fridge, then the French for a radio.

You'll want to know: la douche.
la douche — "a shower".

The word, taken apart.
douche from Italian doccia, "a water pipe", from Latin ductus, "a leading, a conduit", from ducere, "to lead" — borrowed. (EtymDB 2.1).
