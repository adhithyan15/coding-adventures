## You'll want to know: تعريف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C396-MUSTALAH396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تعريف** — *taʿrīf* — "a definition".

> كتب المعلم تعريف الكلمة.
>
> *kataba al-muʿallim taʿrīf al-kalima.*
>
> The teacher wrote down the definition of the word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعريف** (*taʿrīf*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-f*, "to know" (Lane). *Taʿrīf* is the Form II verbal noun, making known: a definition.
