Arabic, chapter 379: Parties and campaigns.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حزب (ḥizb) — a political party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a voter, then the Arabic for a candidate.

You'll want to know: حزب.
حزب — ḥizb — "a political party".
لكل حزب برنامج مختلف. li-kulli ḥizb barnāmaj mukhtalif. Each party has a different programme.
Plural: أحزاب, aḥzāb.

The word, taken apart.
حزب (ḥizb) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-z-b (Lane), where it names a band of people who hold together. Modern Arabic gave it the political party.

Lesson 2 of 5.
حملة (ḥamla) — a campaign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a candidate, then the Arabic for a political party.

You'll want to know: حملة.
حملة — ḥamla — "a campaign".
بدأت الحملة قبل شهرين. badaʾat al-ḥamla qabla shahrayn. The campaign began two months ago.

The word, taken apart.
حملة (ḥamla) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-l, "to carry, to bear" (Lane). A ḥamla is one charge, one carrying forward: an attack, and so a campaign.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ترشح (tarashshaḥa) — to run for office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a political party, then the Arabic for a campaign.

You'll want to know: ترشح.
ترشح — tarashshaḥa — "to run for office".
ترشحت جارتنا في الانتخابات. tarashshaḥat jāratunā fī al-intikhābāt. Our neighbour ran in the elections.
Present: يترشح, yatarashshaḥ.
It takes لـ for the post one runs for.

The word, taken apart.
ترشح (tarashshaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root r-sh-ḥ, "to sweat, to ooze" (Lane). It is Form V, to make oneself ready for a post: to stand as a candidate.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ناشط (nāshiṭ) — an activist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a campaign, then the Arabic for to run for office.

You'll want to know: ناشط.
ناشط — nāshiṭ — "an activist".
تعمل الناشطة مع الأطفال الفقراء. taʿmalu an-nāshiṭa maʿa al-aṭfāl al-fuqarāʾ. The activist works with poor children.

The word, taken apart.
ناشط (nāshiṭ) is built on the native Arabic root n-sh-ṭ, "to be brisk, lively" (Lane). Nāshiṭ is the active participle: one who is active, an activist. نشيط, active, is from the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ديمقراطية (dīmuqrāṭiyya) — democracy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to run for office, then the Arabic for an activist.

You'll want to know: ديمقراطية.
ديمقراطية — dīmuqrāṭiyya — "democracy".
يتكلم الكتاب عن تاريخ الديمقراطية. yatakallamu al-kitāb ʿan tārīkh ad-dīmuqrāṭiyya. The book talks about the history of democracy.

The word, taken apart.
ديمقراطية (dīmuqrāṭiyya) is a modern loan, usually traced to Greek dēmokratia, "rule of the people", through European languages. Arabic added its own abstract ending -iyya.
