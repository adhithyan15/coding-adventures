## You'll want to know: nachgeben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C336-EINIGUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**nachgeben** — "to give in, to give way".

> Am Ende hat meine Schwester nachgegeben, und wir haben den Film gesehen.
>
> *In the end my sister gave in, and we watched the film.*

Separable and strong: *sie gibt nach*, *sie hat nachgegeben*. Also of things: *Der Boden gibt nach* — the ground gives way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nachgeben* is built from *nach* "after" and *geben* "to give", inherited from Old High German *geban* and Proto-Germanic *gebaną*; EtymDB glosses the compound "to give way, to stretch".
