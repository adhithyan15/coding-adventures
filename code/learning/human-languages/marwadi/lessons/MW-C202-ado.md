## You'll want to know: आडो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C202-WAY202-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आडो** — *āḍo* — "across; crosswise".

> रूंख सड़क माथै आडो है।
>
> *rū̃kh saṛak māthai āḍo hai.*
>
> A tree lies across the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आडो** (*āḍo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*aḍḍa*, "transverse"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *āḍo*, "transverse" (CDIAL 189).
