## You'll want to know: die Niederlage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C318-KRIEG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Niederlage** — "the defeat".

> Nach der Niederlage gingen die Soldaten nach Hause.
>
> *After the defeat the soldiers went home.*

Plural: *die Niederlagen*. Also in sport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Niederlage* comes from Middle High German *niderlāge* (EtymDB), from *nieder* "down", Old High German *nidar*, and *Lage* "a lying, a laying", usually linked to *legen* "to lay": being laid low.
