# よる (yoru) — to drop in; to stop by

## You'll want to know: よる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C204-TRIP204-04]; assesses=[] -->

**よる** — *yoru* — "to drop in; to stop by".

> **えきの ちかくの ほんやに よります。** — *eki no chikaku no hon'ya ni yorimasu* — "I drop in at the bookshop near the station."

Polite **よります**, *yorimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よる** (*yoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
