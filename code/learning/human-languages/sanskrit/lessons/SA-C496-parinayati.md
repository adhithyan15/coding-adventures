## You'll want to know: परिणयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C496-VIVAHA496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परिणयति** — *pariṇayati* — "marries".

> युवकः युवतिं परिणयति।
>
> *yuvakaḥ yuvatiṃ pariṇayati.*
>
> The young man marries the young woman.

Past (he …): सः युवतिं परिणीतवान्, *saḥ yuvatiṃ pariṇītavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिणयति** (*pariṇayati*) is built from *pari*, "around", and the root √nī, "to lead": to lead the bride round the fire; MW's ppp *pariṇīta* is "married" (MW).
