## You'll want to know: तमिस्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C443-ASTA443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तमिस्रा** — *tamisrā* — "a dark night".

> तमिस्रायां दीपं विना गन्तुं कठिनम्।
>
> *tamisrāyāṃ dīpaṃ vinā gantuṃ kaṭhinam.*
>
> On a dark night it is hard to go without a lamp.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तमिस्रा** (*tamisrā*) is built on *tamas*, "darkness", with an ending Pāṇini (5.2.114) gives; MW gives *tamisrā* as a dark night (MW).
