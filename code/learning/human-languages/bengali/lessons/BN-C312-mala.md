## You'll want to know: মালা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C312-ONUSHTHAN312-02]; assesses=[] -->

**মালা** — *mālā* — "a garland".

> বিয়েতে বর আর কনে ফুলের মালা পরল।
>
> *biyete bôr ār kône phuler mālā pôrlô.*
>
> At the wedding the bride and groom wore flower garlands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মালা** (*mālā*) is Sanskrit *mālā*, 'a wreath, a garland; a string of beads' (Monier-Williams), unchanged.
