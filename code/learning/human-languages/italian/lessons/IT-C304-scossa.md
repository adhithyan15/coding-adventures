## You'll want to know: la scossa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C304-TERREMOTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la scossa** — "the tremor; the shock".

> Stanotte abbiamo sentito una scossa.
>
> *Last night we felt a tremor.*

*Una scossa elettrica* — an electric shock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scossa* is built on *scosso*, the past participle of *scuotere* "to shake", from Latin *excussus* (EtymDB).
