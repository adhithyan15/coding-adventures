6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
o mergulho — dive; (quick) swim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for easel, then the Portuguese for (picture) frame.

You'll want to know: o mergulho.
o mergulho — "dive; (quick) swim".
Demos um mergulho no mar antes do almoço. We had a quick swim in the sea before lunch.
Dar um mergulho — to have a dip.

The word, taken apart.
mergulho comes from mergulhar "to dive", which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin form built on Latin mergere "to plunge, to dip" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 6.
velejar — to sail.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for dive, then the Portuguese for kayak.

You'll want to know: velejar.
velejar — "to sail".
Aos fins de semana, velejamos na baía. At weekends, we sail in the bay.
Built on a vela — a sail.

The word, taken apart.
velejar is built on vela "sail", which is inherited from Latin vēla, plural of vēlum "a sail, a cloth" (Lewis & Short).
