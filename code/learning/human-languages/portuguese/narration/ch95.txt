Portuguese, chapter 95: Towel, Bar of soap, Toothbrush, Toothpaste, Comb.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a toalha — a towel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a curtain, then the Portuguese for a blanket.

You'll want to know: a toalha.
a toalha — "a towel".

The word, taken apart.
toalha from Old Provençal toalha, "cloth" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. The Provençal word is Frankish.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a escova de dentes — a toothbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a towel, then the Portuguese for a bar of soap.

You'll want to know: a escova de dentes.
a escova de dentes — "a toothbrush".

The word, taken apart.
escova de dentes is "brush of teeth": escova from Latin scopa, "twigs, a broom" (Lewis & Short), and dentes is the plural of dente from Latin dens.
