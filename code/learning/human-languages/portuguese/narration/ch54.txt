Portuguese, chapter 54: Glasses, Box, Suitcase, Ticket, Map.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
os óculos — glasses, spectacles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a present, then the Portuguese for a watch.

You'll want to know: os óculos.
os óculos — "glasses, spectacles". Plural in form.
