## You'll want to know: prōgredior, prōgredī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C428-PROFECTIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prōgredior, prōgredī** — "to go forward, to advance".

> Nox venit, sed viātōrēs ad oppidum prōgrediuntur.
>
> *Night comes, but the travellers go on towards the town.*

Deponent: *prōgredior, prōgredī, prōgressus sum*. English *progress* comes from its participle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prōgredior* is built from *prō-* 'forward' and *gradior* 'to step' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *progress* comes from its participle *prōgressus*.
