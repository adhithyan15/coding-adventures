## You'll want to know: удаваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C374-OZHIDANIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**удаваться** (*udavátsya*) — "to succeed, to manage (impersonal)".

> Мне не удаётся выспаться.
>
> *Mne ne udayótsya výspatsya.*
>
> I cannot manage to get enough sleep.

Imperfective; present *удаётся*; perfective *удаться* (*удастся*). Impersonal, with the dative: *мне удалось* — I managed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**удаваться** is built from *у-* and *давать* "to give", the ongoing partner of *дать*: when a thing succeeds, it gives itself to you.
