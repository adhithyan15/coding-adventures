Telugu, chapter 398: Laws and Rules.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చట్టం (caṭṭaṁ) — a law; an act.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to mention, then the Telugu for to express.

You'll want to know: చట్టం.
చట్టం — caṭṭaṁ — "a law; an act".
ఆ పని చట్టం ప్రకారం తప్పు. ā pani caṭṭaṁ prakāraṁ tappu. That act is wrong under the law.
Plural: చట్టాలు, caṭṭālu.
చట్టం (caṭṭaṁ) ప్రకారం, according to the law.

The word, taken apart.
చట్టం (caṭṭaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2304 lists Telugu caṭṭamu, "a frame, a plan, a system, a law", with Tamil caṭṭam, "a frame; a rule, a regulation".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఉల్లంఘించు (ullaṅghiñcu) — to violate; to break (a rule).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to express, then the Telugu for a law.

You'll want to know: ఉల్లంఘించు.
ఉల్లంఘించు — ullaṅghiñcu — "to violate; to break (a rule)".
అతను కంపెనీ నియమాలను ఉల్లంఘించాడు. atanu kampenī niyamālanu ullaṅghiñcāḍu. He broke the company rules.
Past (I …): ఉల్లంఘించాను, ullaṅghiñcānu.
Formal: for rules, laws and agreements.

The word, taken apart.
ఉల్లంఘించు (ullaṅghiñcu) is built on Sanskrit ullaṅghana, "the act of leaping over" (CDIAL 2365), and so "transgression" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 3 of 5.
అమలు చేయు (amalu cēyu) — to implement; to enforce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a law, then the Telugu for to violate.

You'll want to know: అమలు చేయు.
అమలు చేయు — amalu cēyu — "to implement; to enforce".
ప్రభుత్వం ఆ చట్టాన్ని వెంటనే అమలు చేసింది. prabhutvaṁ ā caṭṭānni veṇṭanē amalu cēsindi. The government put that law into effect at once.
Past (I …): అమలు చేశాను, amalu cēśānu.
అమలు alone is enforcement: అమలులోకి వచ్చు, to come into force.

The word, taken apart.
అమలు చేయు (amalu cēyu) is usually traced to Hindustani amal, "action, execution", a word of Arabic origin, with the native verb చేయు, "to do" (DEDR 1957).

Lesson 4 of 5.
శిక్ష (śikṣa) — a punishment; a sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to violate, then the Telugu for to implement.

You'll want to know: శిక్ష.
శిక్ష — śikṣa — "a punishment; a sentence".
దొంగకు రెండు సంవత్సరాల శిక్ష పడింది. doṅgaku reṇḍu saṁvatsarāla śikṣa paḍindi. The thief got a two-year sentence.
Plural: శిక్షలు, śikṣalu.
శిక్ష (śikṣa) పడు, to be punished (a punishment falls on someone); శిక్షించు, to punish.

The word, taken apart.
శిక్ష (śikṣa) is borrowed from Sanskrit śikṣā, "learning, study", and later also "punishment" (CDIAL 12432). Telugu shortens the final long a.

The word, taken apart.
నిషేధించు (niṣēdhiñcu) is built on Sanskrit niṣedha, "warding off, hindering, prohibition" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
