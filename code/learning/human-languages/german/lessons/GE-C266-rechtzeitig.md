## You'll want to know: rechtzeitig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C266-PLOETZLICH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rechtzeitig** — "in time, in good time".

> Wir sind rechtzeitig am Bahnhof angekommen.
>
> *We arrived at the station in time.*

In time for something; *pünktlich* means exactly at the time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rechtzeitig* joins *recht* "right" and *Zeit* "time", both inherited Germanic words (EtymDB), with the ending *-ig*: at the right time.
