## You'll want to know: engomar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C529-ROUPA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**engomar** — "to iron; to starch".

> O meu pai engoma as camisas ao domingo.
>
> *My father irons the shirts on Sundays.*

Also *passar a ferro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*engomar* is built on *goma* "starch, gum", which goes back to Latin *gummi*, from Greek *kómmi*; the Greek word is usually traced to Egyptian.
