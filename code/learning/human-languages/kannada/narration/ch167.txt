Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉದುರು (uduru) — to drop off, to shed (leaves, hair).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to leak, then the Kannada for to float.

You'll want to know: ಉದುರು.
ಉದುರು — uduru — "to drop off, to shed (leaves, hair)".

The word, taken apart.
ಉದುರು (uduru) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil utir (DEDR 615). DEDR lists udir, udur, "to fall off, drop".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸುಧಾರಿಸು (sudhārisu) — to improve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಸುಧಾರಿಸು.
ಸುಧಾರಿಸು — sudhārisu — "to improve".

The word, taken apart.
ಸುಧಾರಿಸು (sudhārisu) is sudhāra, "improvement", made into a verb with -isu. The noun is usually traced to Sanskrit, through Hindustani sudhār, but no source settles its history.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬೆಂಬಲಿಸು (bembalisu) — to support, to back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to drop off, then the Kannada for to improve.

You'll want to know: ಬೆಂಬಲಿಸು.
ಬೆಂಬಲಿಸು — bembalisu — "to support, to back".

The word, taken apart.
ಬೆಂಬಲಿಸು (bembalisu) is native Dravidian, usually explained as built on ben, "the back" (DEDR 5488), which lists bembaḻi, "following": to support is to stand at someone's back. DEDR does not list bembala itself.

ಗೊರಕೆ ಹೊಡೆ (gorake hoḍe) is native Dravidian: gorake, "snoring", which DEDR 1852 lists as goṟaku, gorke, and hoḍe, "to strike, beat", from older poḍe (DEDR 4252).
