## You'll want to know: دكتوراه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C397-SHAHADA397-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دكتوراه** — *duktūrāh* — "a doctorate".

> كتب أبي أطروحة الدكتوراه في عامين.
>
> *kataba abī uṭrūḥat ad-duktūrāh fī ʿāmayn.*
>
> My father wrote his doctoral thesis in two years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دكتوراه** (*duktūrāh*) is a modern loan, usually traced to Latin *doctor*, "a teacher", through European languages. Its final h is written with ه.
