## You'll want to know: ねぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C472-INGR472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ねぎ** — *negi* — "a green onion".

> **みそしるに ねぎを いれました。** — *misoshiru ni negi o iremashita* — "I put green onion in the miso soup."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねぎ** (*negi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
