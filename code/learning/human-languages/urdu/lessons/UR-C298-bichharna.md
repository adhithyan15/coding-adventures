## You'll want to know: بچھڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C298-BICHHAR298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بچھڑنا** — *bichhaṛnā* — "to be separated".

> میلے میں بچہ ماں سے بچھڑ گیا۔
>
> *mele meṅ bachchā māṅ se bichhaṛ gayā.*
>
> At the fair the child got separated from his mother.

Past (she …): وہ بچھڑ گئی, *voh bichhaṛ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بچھڑنا** (*bichhaṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bichuṛnā*, "is separated", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11651). The person you lose takes **سے**: **ماں سے بچھڑ گیا**.
