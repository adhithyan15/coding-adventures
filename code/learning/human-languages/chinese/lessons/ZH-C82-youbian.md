## You'll want to know: yòubian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C82-SIDES82-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yòubian* — "the right; on the right".

> *yòubian shì shāngdiàn* — The shop is on the right.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yòubian* is built from yòu 'right' and bian 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuwX* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built

One more word for meeting people.
