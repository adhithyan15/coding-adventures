Lesson 1 of 7.
bèizi — quilt; duvet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to come over, then the Mandarin for to make a date with.

You'll want to know: bèizi.
bèizi — "quilt; duvet".
tiānqì lěng le wǒmen yào huàn bèizi — It's turned cold: we need to change the quilt.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bèizi is built from bèi 'bedclothes' and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, bjeX and tsiX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). In the word the second loses its tone.

Lesson 3 of 7.
间 (jiān) — measure word for rooms.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a quilt, then the Mandarin for a pillow.

You'll want to know: 间.
间 — jiān — "measure word for rooms".
后边有两间书房 hòubian yǒu liǎng jiān shūfáng — There are two studies at the back.
The new character, in pieces: 间 (jiān) — seven strokes: the door piece 门, three strokes, and 日 inside it, four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
间 (jiān) is inherited from Middle Chinese kean (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which writes it another way and glosses it 'a gap' and 'in between': the space between walls, and so a room.

What you've built.
One more word for everyday things.

Lesson 4 of 7.
间 (jiān) — 门 with 日 inside.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — 门 with 日 inside and Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — mén, shí]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — 门 with 日 inside.
间 (jiān).
Two pieces, the frame first. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
门, three strokes, as you write it: a dot, a vertical down the left, and a horizontal that turns down the right side and hooks.
Then 日 inside it, four strokes, as you write it, but smaller: a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, a bar across the middle, and the bottom bar.
3 + 4 = seven.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 间 (jiān), all seven, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 间 (jiān)? (Seven.) Which piece comes first? (门, the frame.) What sits inside? (日.)
