Urdu, chapter 339: Rights and Duties.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اختیار (ikhtiyār) — authority; a power (to decide).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to enumerate, then the Urdu for a reference.

You'll want to know: اختیار.
اختیار — ikhtiyār — "authority; a power (to decide)".
یہ میرے اختیار میں نہیں ہے۔ yih mere ikhtiyār meṅ nahīṅ hai. This is not in my power.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اختیار (ikhtiyār) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ikhtiyār, "choice": the power to choose.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for justice, then the Urdu for suitable.

You'll want to know: عمل کرنا.
عمل کرنا — amal karnā — "to act on; to follow (a rule, advice)".
ہمیں قانون پر عمل کرنا چاہیے۔ hameṅ qānūn par amal karnā chāhiye. We should keep to the law.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مشورے پر عمل کیا, us ne mashvare par amal kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
عمل کرنا (amal karnā) is built from amal, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿamal, "an action", and کرنا, "to do": to put a rule into action.
