Arabic, chapter 264: Season, Childhood, Evening out, Ramadan, Afternoon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فصل (faṣl) — a season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a thumb, then the Arabic for a muscle.

You'll want to know: فصل.
فصل — faṣl — "a season".

The word, taken apart.
فصل (faṣl) is built on the native Arabic root f-ṣ-l, "to separate" (Lane). Faṣl is a division: a season, a chapter.
