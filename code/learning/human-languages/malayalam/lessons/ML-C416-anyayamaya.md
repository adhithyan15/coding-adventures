## You'll want to know: അന്യായമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C416-NITI416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**അന്യായമായ** — *anyāyamāya* — "unfair; unjust".

> അത് തികച്ചും അന്യായമായ ഒരു തീരുമാനമായിരുന്നു.
>
> *atŭ tikaccuṁ anyāyamāya oru tīrumānamāyirunnu.*
>
> That was a completely unfair decision.

The noun is അന്യായം, a wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അന്യായമായ** (*anyāyamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *a-*, "not", and *nyāya*, "rule, right method" (CDIAL 7614), with the adjective ending -ആയ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
