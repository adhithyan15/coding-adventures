Gujarati, chapter 525: Carving and Shaping.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શિલ્પ (śilp) — a sculpture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for hide-and-seek, then the Gujarati for a puzzle.

You'll want to know: શિલ્પ.
શિલ્પ — śilp — "a sculpture".
મંદિરમાં પથ્થરનાં ઘણાં સુંદર શિલ્પ છે. mandirmā̃ paththarnā̃ ghaṇā̃ sundar śilp che. There are many beautiful stone sculptures in the temple.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
શિલ્પ (śilp) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit śilpa, "an art, a craft" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is a work of sculpture.

You'll want to know: કોતરવું.
કોતરવું — kotarvũ — "to carve".
કારીગરે લાકડામાં એક મોર કોતર્યો. kārīgre lākḍāmā̃ ek mor kotaryo. The craftsman carved a peacock in the wood.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મોર કોતર્યો, teṇe mor kotaryo.

The word, taken apart.
કોતરવું (kotarvũ) comes from kōtr, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to dig, carve" (CDIAL 3512).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to carve, then the Gujarati for a shape.

You'll want to know: મઢવું.
મઢવું — maḍhvũ — "to frame (a picture)".
અરુણે મીરાનું ચિત્ર મઢ્યું. Aruṇe Mīrānũ citra maḍhyũ. Arun framed Mira's picture.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ચિત્ર મઢ્યું, teṇe citra maḍhyũ.

The word, taken apart.
મઢવું (maḍhvũ) comes from maḍh, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to cover", said of a book or a drum (CDIAL 9729); a picture is covered with glass and a frame.
