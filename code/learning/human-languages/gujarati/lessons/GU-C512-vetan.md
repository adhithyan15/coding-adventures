## You'll want to know: વેતન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C512-NOKRI512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વેતન** — *vetan* — "a wage".

> આ કારખાનામાં મજૂરોનું વેતન ઓછું છે.
>
> *ā kārkhānāmā̃ majūronũ vetan ochũ che.*
>
> The workers' wages in this factory are low.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વેતન** (*vetan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vetana*, "wages, hire, salary" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal word beside the everyday **પગાર**.
