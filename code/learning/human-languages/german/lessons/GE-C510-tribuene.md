## You'll want to know: die Tribüne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C510-TRIBUENE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Tribüne** — "stand, grandstand".

> Wir saßen auf der Tribüne direkt hinter dem Tor.
>
> *We sat in the stand right behind the goal.*

Plural: *die Tribünen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tribüne* came from French *tribune*, from Italian *tribuna* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *tribūnal*, the raised platform of the magistrates (Lewis & Short).
