# प्रार्थना (prārthnā) — a prayer; a request

## You'll want to know: प्रार्थना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C539-DUA539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रार्थना** — *prārthnā* — "a prayer; a request".

> स्कूल में हर सुबह प्रार्थना होती है।
>
> *Skūl meṁ har subah prārthnā hotī hai.*
>
> There is a prayer at school every morning.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रार्थना** (*prārthnā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *prārthanā*, "a wish, a request, a prayer" (Monier-Williams).
