## You'll want to know: la formation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C360-CARRIERE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la formation** — "the training, the education".

> L'entreprise paie ma formation.
>
> *The company is paying for my training.*

*La formation continue* — training for adults in work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*formation* comes through Old French *formacion* from Latin *fōrmātiō* "a shaping" (EtymDB), from *fōrmāre* "to shape", built on *fōrma* "shape" (Lewis & Short).
