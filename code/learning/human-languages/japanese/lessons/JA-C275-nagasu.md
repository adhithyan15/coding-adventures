# ながす (nagasu) — to wash away; to let flow

## You'll want to know: ながす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C275-TSUNAMI275-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ながす** — *nagasu* — "to wash away; to let flow".

> **つなみが うちを ながしました。** — *tsunami ga uchi o nagashimashita* — "The tsunami swept our house away."

Polite **ながします**, *nagashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **つなみが くるまを ながしました。**, *tsunami ga kuruma o nagashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ながす** (*nagasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the transitive partner of *nagareru*, "to flow".
