## You'll want to know: こんきょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C318-SETSUMEI318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こんきょ** — *konkyo* — "grounds; a basis (for a claim)".

> **あの いけんには こんきょが ないと おもいます。** — *ano iken ni wa konkyo ga nai to omoimasu* — "I think that opinion has no grounds."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こんきょ** (*konkyo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kon*, "root" (Middle Chinese *kon*) and *kyo*, "foothold" (*kjoH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
