Kannada, chapter 463: Hotel Services.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸೇವೆ (sēve) — a service.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a heater, then the Kannada for to creak.

You'll want to know: ಸೇವೆ.
ಸೇವೆ — sēve — "a service".
ಈ ಹೋಟೆಲ್ನ ಸೇವೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ. ī hōṭelna sēve tumbā cennāgide. The service in this hotel is very good.
ಕೊಠಡಿ ಸೇವೆ (sēve), room service; ಬಸ್ ಸೇವೆ (sēve), a bus service.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೇವೆ (sēve) is borrowed from Sanskrit sēvā, "attendance on, service" (CDIAL 13595); Kannada ends it in -e.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ (saulabhya) — a facility; an amenity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to creak, then the Kannada for a service.

You'll want to know: ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ.
ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ — saulabhya — "a facility; an amenity".
ಲಾಡ್ಜ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಇಂಟರ್ನೆಟ್ ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ ಇಲ್ಲ. lāḍjnalli iṇṭarneṭ saulabhya illa. The lodge has no internet facility.
Plural: ಸೌಲಭ್ಯಗಳು, saulabhyagaḷu.
ಸೌಲಭ್ಯಗಳು, facilities: water, power, parking.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ (saulabhya) is borrowed from Sanskrit saulabhya, "easiness of attainment" (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it names what makes a stay easy.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಒದಗಿಸು (odagisu) — to provide; to supply.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a service, then the Kannada for a facility.

You'll want to know: ಒದಗಿಸು.
ಒದಗಿಸು — odagisu — "to provide; to supply".
ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಪ್ರವಾಸಿಗರಿಗೆ ಉಚಿತ ತಿಂಡಿ ಒದಗಿಸುತ್ತದೆ. hōṭel pravāsigarige ucita tiṇḍi odagisuttade. The hotel provides tourists with a free breakfast snack.
Past (I …): ಒದಗಿಸಿದೆ, odagiside.
What is given takes -ಅನ್ನು or no ending; the receiver takes -ಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಒದಗಿಸು (odagisu) is native Dravidian: the causative of odagu, "to come to hand, to be of use" (DEDR 609): to make a thing be at hand.

ಗೀಸರ್ — gīsar — "a water heater".
ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಗೀಸರ್ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಲಿಲ್ಲ. beḷagge gīsar kelasa māḍalilla. In the morning the water heater did not work.
In Indian English a geyser heats bath water.

The word, taken apart.
ಗೀಸರ್ (gīsar) is a loan from English geyser, which in Indian English names a bathroom water heater.
