Urdu, chapter 525: Putting Things Off.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
مہلت (mohlat) — extra time; a respite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a way out, then the Urdu for to have (something) made or built.

You'll want to know: مہلت.
مہلت — mohlat — "extra time; a respite".
اگر ایک ہفتے کی مہلت ملی تو کام ہو گا۔ agar ek hafte kī mohlat milī to kām ho gā. If I get a week's grace, the work will be finished.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
مہلت (mohlat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muhla, "a delay, a respite".

Lesson 2 of 6.
التوا (iltivā) — a postponement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (something) made or built, then the Urdu for extra time.

You'll want to know: التوا.
التوا — iltivā — "a postponement".
اگر التوا نہیں ہوا تو امتحان کل ہو گا۔ agar iltivā nahīṅ huā to imtihān kal ho gā. If it is not postponed, the exam will be tomorrow.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
التوا (iltivā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iltiwāʾ, "a bending, a turning aside"; the sense "putting off" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a postponement, then the Urdu for suspended.

You'll want to know: برقرار.
برقرار — barqarār — "still standing; unchanged".
اگر موسم ٹھیک رہا تو پروگرام برقرار رہے گا۔ agar mausam ṭhīk rahā to progrām barqarār rahe gā. If the weather stays fine, the plan will stand.

The word, taken apart.
برقرار (barqarār) is usually traced to Persian barqarār, "firm, established", built from bar, "on", and Arabic qarār, "rest, firmness".

Lesson 5 of 6.
اٹکانا (aṭkānā) — to keep (something) pending; to hold up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for suspended, then the Urdu for still standing.

You'll want to know: اٹکانا.
اٹکانا — aṭkānā — "to keep (something) pending; to hold up".
اگر افسر نے فائل اٹکائی تو کام رک جائے گا۔ agar afsar ne fāil aṭkāī to kām ruk jāe gā. If the officer holds up the file, the work will stop.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے میری فائل اٹکائی, us ne merī fāil aṭkāī.

The word, taken apart.
اٹکانا (aṭkānā) is the causative of aṭaknā, "to get stuck", which CDIAL lists under aṭṭakk, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 182).
