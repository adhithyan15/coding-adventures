## You'll want to know: aestīvus, aestīva, aestīvum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C488-BRUMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**aestīvus, aestīva, aestīvum** — "of summer, summer".

> Dīves vīllam aestīvam in montibus habet.
>
> *The rich man has a summer house in the mountains.*

An adjective of three endings, from *aestās* 'summer'. *Aestīva* (n. pl.), a summer camp. English *aestivate* comes from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aestīvus* is built on *aestās* 'summer' with the suffix *-īvus* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), and *aestās* goes back to PIE *h₂eydʰ-* 'to burn'.
