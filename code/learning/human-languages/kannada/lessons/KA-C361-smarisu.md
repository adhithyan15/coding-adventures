# ಸ್ಮರಿಸು (smarisu) — to commemorate; to remember

## You'll want to know: ಸ್ಮರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C361-SMARAKA361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಮರಿಸು** — *smarisu* — "to commemorate; to remember".

> ಪ್ರತಿ ವರ್ಷ ಊರಿನವರು ಆ ಯೋಧರನ್ನು ಸ್ಮರಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *prati varṣa ūrinavaru ā yōdharannu smarisuttāre.*
>
> Every year the townspeople commemorate those warriors.

Past (I …): ಸ್ಮರಿಸಿದೆ, *smariside*.

Who is remembered takes -ಅನ್ನು. A formal word; in talk, ನೆನಪಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಮರಿಸು** (*smarisu*) is built on Sanskrit *smaraṇa*, "remembering, memory" (CDIAL 13862), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
