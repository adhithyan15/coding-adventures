## You'll want to know: potare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C461-ALBERI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**potare** — "to prune".

> In inverno il nonno pota gli alberi.
>
> *In winter Grandad prunes the trees.*

*La potatura* — pruning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*potare* is inherited from Latin *putāre* 'to prune, to cut back' (EtymDB), the same verb that also meant to reckon.
