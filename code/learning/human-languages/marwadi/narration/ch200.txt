Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 200: Scales and Weights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तराजू (tarājū) — a pair of scales.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a reckoning, then the Marwadi for to write down.

You'll want to know: तराजू.
तराजू — tarājū — "a pair of scales".
तराजू दुकान मांय है। tarājū dukān mā̃y hai. The scales are in the shop.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तराजू (tarājū) is usually traced to Persian tarāzū, "balance, scales".

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Lesson 2 of 5.
सेर (ser) — a seer (a measure of weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to write down, then the Marwadi for scales.

You'll want to know: सेर.
सेर — ser — "a seer (a measure of weight)".
एक सेर आटो दो। ek ser āṭo do. Give me a seer of flour.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सेर (ser) is inherited from a reconstructed satēra, "a measure of weight"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari sera (CDIAL 13106).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पाव (pāv) — a quarter (of a seer).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a seer, then the Marwadi for a kilo.

You'll want to know: पाव.
पाव — pāv — "a quarter (of a seer)".
एक पाव घी दो। ek pāv ghī do. Give me a quarter of ghee.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पाव (pāv) is inherited from Sanskrit pāda, "foot; a fourth part"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pāva, "foot", and Hindi pāu, "quarter" (CDIAL 8056).
