## You'll want to know: சதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C337-SATHI337-01]; assesses=[] -->

**சதி** — *cati* — "a conspiracy; a plot".

> மன்னரைக் கொல்ல ஒரு சதி நடந்தது.
>
> *maṉṉaraik kolla oru cati naṭantatu.*
>
> There was a plot to kill the king.

சதி செய், to conspire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சதி** (*cati*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2323): to deceive, and as a noun treachery, wiles.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
