## You'll want to know: समेटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C170-DHOVO170-05]; assesses=[] -->

**समेटणो** — *sameṭṇo* — "to gather up; to fold away".

> आथण मां कपड़ा समेटै है।
>
> *āthaṇ mā̃ kapṛā sameṭai hai.*
>
> In the evening my mother gathers up the clothes.

Past (I …): म्हैं कपड़ा समेट्या, *mhaĩ kapṛā sameṭyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समेटणो** (*sameṭṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *samveṣṭayati*, "wraps up"; CDIAL lists Hindi *sameṭnā*, "to roll up", and Gujarati *sameṭvũ* (CDIAL 13026). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
