# मी जाणार आहे (mī jāṇār āhe) — a plan, and what will not happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C160-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-LEX-JANE, MR-LEX-AAHE-COPULA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I will go", then **जाणे**, then **आहे**.

## You'll want to know: -णार आहे for a plan
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C160-NAR-PLAN-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-JANE, MR-LEX-KARNE, MR-LEX-AAHE-COPULA, MR-LEX-C160-BHAV160-03] -->

> **पुढच्या आठवड्यात मी गावाला जाणार आहे.**
>
> *puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt mī gāvālā jāṇār āhe.* — "Next week I am going to go to the village."

Take **-णे** off the verb and add **-णार**: **जाणे → जाणार**, **करणे →
करणार**, **येणे → येणार**. Add **आहे** for a present plan. **-णार** stays the
same with every person in this bounded A2 pattern.

## You'll want to know: -णार नाही for won't
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C160-NAR-NAHI-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-NEGATE-NAHI, MR-LEX-YENE, MR-LEX-UDYA] -->

Replace **आहे** with **नाही** for a future refusal or firm negative:
**मी उद्या येणार नाही**, "I won't come tomorrow."
