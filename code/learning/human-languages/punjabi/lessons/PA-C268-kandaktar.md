## You'll want to know: ਕੰਡਕਟਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C268-STAND268-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੰਡਕਟਰ** — *kaṇḍakṭar* — "a bus conductor".

> ਕੰਡਕਟਰ ਬੱਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਟਿਕਟਾਂ ਵੇਚਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *kaṇḍakṭar bass vich ṭikṭāṁ vechdā hai.*
>
> The conductor sells tickets on the bus.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੰਡਕਟਰ** (*kaṇḍakṭar*) is borrowed from English *conductor*.
