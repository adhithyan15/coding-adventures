## You'll want to know: آیین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C340-EYDI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آیین** (*âyin*) — "a rite, a ritual".

> پریدن از روی آتش یک آیین قدیمی است.
>
> *Paridan az ru-ye âtash yek âyin-e qadimi ast.*
>
> Jumping over a fire is an old ritual.

It also means "a religion, a creed". *Âyin-e Nowruz* "the Nowruz rites".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âyin* is a native Persian word that EtymDB glosses "custom" with no parent; it is usually traced to Middle Persian *ēwēn*, "manner, custom".
