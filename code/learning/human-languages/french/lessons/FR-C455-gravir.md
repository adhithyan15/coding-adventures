## You'll want to know: gravir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C455-RANDONNEE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gravir** — "to climb (up)".

> Ils ont gravi la colline en une heure.
>
> *They climbed the hill in an hour.*

Conjugated like *finir*. A bit formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
