## You'll want to know: وصیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C492-OLDAGE492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وصیت** — *vasiyat* — "a last will".

> نانا نے اپنی وصیت لکھی۔
>
> *nānā ne apnī vasiyat likhī.*
>
> My grandfather wrote his will.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وصیت** (*vasiyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *waṣiyya*, "a bequest, a last instruction".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
