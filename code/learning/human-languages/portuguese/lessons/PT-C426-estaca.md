## You'll want to know: a estaca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C426-TENDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a estaca** — "the tent peg; the stake".

> O vento arrancou as estacas da tenda.
>
> *The wind pulled out the tent pegs.*

A thicker *estaca* holds up a young tree or a fence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estaca* is usually traced to a Germanic word for a pointed stick; EtymDB lists English *stake* and Old French *estaque* as its cousins.
