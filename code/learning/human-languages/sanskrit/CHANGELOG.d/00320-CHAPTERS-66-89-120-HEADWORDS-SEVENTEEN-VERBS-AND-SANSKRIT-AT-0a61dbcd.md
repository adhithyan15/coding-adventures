## Chapters 66-89: 120 headwords, seventeen verbs, and Sanskrit attains pre-A1

Sanskrit was 116 headwords and 5 verbs short of pre-A1. This entry adds
twenty-four vocabulary chapters of five words each, closed by four review
lessons (two after chapter 77, two after chapter 89). Every word is chained, so
each atom is revisited twice.

- **66-77**:
  - illness, needs, feelings, and two chapters on the body
  - three chapters of verbs (moving; cooking, washing, bringing, giving;
    singing, telling, crying, calling, bowing)
  - the market (with क्रीणाति), travel (with प्रविशति), the weather and the rooms
    of the house
- **78-89**: food, animals, birds, plants, water, school, places, the seven days
  of the week with time and a moment, eleven to a thousand, people, and five
  describing words.

Each headword was checked three ways:

- it is new to the track;
- it uses only glyphs the script lessons teach, and that
  `data/scripts/devanagari.json` covers;
- it does not appear as a whole word in earlier lesson text.

Three first-draft headwords used ङ, which the track teaches but the script file
does not yet cover. Two were replaced: the finger became ललाटम्, "forehead", and
the courtyard became छदिः, "roof". Tuesday is written मंगलवासरः, with the
anusvara spelling.
