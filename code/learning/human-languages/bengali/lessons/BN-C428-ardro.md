## You'll want to know: আর্দ্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C428-ARDRO428-01]; assesses=[] -->

**আর্দ্র** — *ārdrô* — "humid; moist".

> সমুদ্রের কাছে বাতাস আর্দ্র।
>
> *sômudrer kāchhe bātās ārdrô.*
>
> Near the sea the air is humid.

A careful word, heard in weather reports.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আর্দ্র** (*ārdrô*) is Sanskrit *ārdra*, "wet, moist, damp" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.
