## You'll want to know: ਪੋਚਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C235-KAM235-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਚਾ** — *pochā* — "a floor cloth (for mopping)".

> ਮੈਂ ਗਿੱਲੇ ਪੋਚੇ ਨਾਲ ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਸਾਫ਼ ਕਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ gille poche nāl farsh sāf kardī hāṁ.*
>
> I clean the floor with a wet floor cloth.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਚਾ** (*pochā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pōta*, "cloth"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *poccā*, "cloth or brush for smearing" (CDIAL 8400).
