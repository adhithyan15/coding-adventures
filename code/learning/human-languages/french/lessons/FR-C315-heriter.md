## You'll want to know: hériter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C315-DEUIL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**hériter** — "to inherit".

> Elle a hérité de la maison de sa tante.
>
> *She inherited her aunt's house.*

*Hériter de quelque chose* — with *de*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hériter* comes from Old French *eriter* (EtymDB), from Late Latin *hērēditāre* "to inherit", built on *hērēs* "heir" (Lewis & Short).
