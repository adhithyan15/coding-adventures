## You'll want to know: yǔnxǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C209-RULE209-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yǔnxǔ* — "to allow; to permit".

> *zhèr bù yǔnxǔ xīyān* — Smoking isn't allowed here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: yǔn is said with a rising tone before xǔ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǔnxǔ* is built from *yǔn* and *xǔ*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ywinX* and *xjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'true' and the second 'to permit'.

One more word for reading notices and messages.
