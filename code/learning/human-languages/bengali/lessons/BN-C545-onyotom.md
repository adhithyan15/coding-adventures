## You'll want to know: অন্যতম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C545-SADHYO545-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অন্যতম** — *ônyôtôm* — "one of several".

> ইলিশ আমার প্রিয় মাছগুলোর অন্যতম।
>
> *Ilish āmār priyô māchhgulor ônyôtôm.*
>
> Hilsa is one of my favourite fish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অন্যতম** (*ônyôtôm*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *anyatama*, "any one of many" (Monier-Williams).
