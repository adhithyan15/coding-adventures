## You'll want to know: ingrato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C499-GRATIDAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ingrato** — "ungrateful".

> O filho ingrato nunca telefona à mãe.
>
> *The ungrateful son never phones his mother.*

Of a job: thankless, *um trabalho ingrato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingrato* comes from Latin *ingrātus* "unpleasant, ungrateful" (EtymDB), the opposite of *grātus*.
