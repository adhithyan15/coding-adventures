# Review — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C343-RODU-01, ML-SCRIPT-C343-DA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a road". (*ṟōḍŭ*.) Write **ഡ** once from memory.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C343-RODU-01, ML-SCRIPT-C343-DA-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C343-ASK-WAY-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C343-GIVE-WAY-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ANAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ATUKONDU-CLAUSE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-PLAN-REASON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: "Excuse me, where is the station?"]
- [YOU SAY: "How do I get to the station? How far is it from here?"]
- [YOU SAY: "Go straight along this road. At the junction turn right."]
- [YOU SAY: "Because the road is busy, take the second road." — *ṟōḍil tirakkuḷḷatukoṇṭŭ raṇṭāmatte ṟōḍil pōkū.*]
- [YOU SAY: the way from your door to the nearest shop, in three steps]
- [YOU SAY: "Tomorrow I will go to the station, because I want a ticket." — *nāḷe ñān sṟṟēṣanil pōkuṁ, kāraṇaṁ enikkŭ oru ṭikkaṟṟŭ vēṇaṁ.*]
- [YOU WRITE: **റോഡ്**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C343-RODU-01, ML-SCRIPT-C343-DA-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C343-ASK-WAY-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C343-GIVE-WAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "Where is …?" (**… എവിടെയാണ്?**) "How do I get to …?" (**… -ലേക്ക് എങ്ങനെ പോകണം?**)
"To the right"? (**വലത്തോട്ട്**.) "A road"? (**റോഡ്**, which ends in **ഡ്**.)
