## You'll want to know: ख़ज़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C337-KHAZANA337-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ज़ाना** — *khazānā* — "a treasure; a treasury".

> कहानी में राजा का ख़ज़ाना एक पुराने क़िले में था।
>
> *kahānī meṁ rājā kā khazānā ek purāne qile meṁ thā.*
>
> In the story the king's treasure was in an old fort.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ज़ाना** (*khazānā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khizāna*, "a storehouse". It is a king's treasury and, in stories, gold hidden underground.
