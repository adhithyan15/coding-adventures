## You'll want to know: पेशकश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C542-SAMJHAUTA542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पेशकश** — *peśkaś* — "an offer".

> उसने मुझे नौकरी की पेशकश की।
>
> *Usne mujhe naukrī kī peśkaś kī.*
>
> He offered me a job.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेशकश** (*peśkaś*) is usually traced to Persian *pēshkash*, "a present, an offering".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
