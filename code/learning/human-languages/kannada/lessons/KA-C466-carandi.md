# ಚರಂಡಿ (caraṇḍi) — a drain

## You'll want to know: ಚರಂಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C466-UPANAGARA466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚರಂಡಿ** — *caraṇḍi* — "a drain".

> ಮಳೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಚರಂಡಿ ತುಂಬಿ ನೀರು ರಸ್ತೆಗೆ ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *maḷeyalli caraṇḍi tumbi nīru rastege banditu.*
>
> In the rain the drain filled up and water came onto the road.

The open drain beside a street; ಒಳಚರಂಡಿ is an underground sewer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚರಂಡಿ** (*caraṇḍi*) is a Kannada word for a drain whose origin is not settled.
