## You'll want to know: కోలాటం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C543-KACERI543-05]; assesses=[] -->

**కోలాటం** — *kōlāṭaṁ* — "a stick dance".

> పండుగలో ఆడపిల్లలు కోలాటం ఆడారు.
>
> *paṇḍugalō āḍapillalu kōlāṭaṁ āḍāru.*
>
> At the festival the girls did the stick dance.

Dancers strike short sticks in rhythm: కోలు, stick, + ఆటం, play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కోలాటం** (*kōlāṭaṁ*) is built from కోలు, "a stick", and native *āṭaṁ*, "play, dance" (DEDR 347).
