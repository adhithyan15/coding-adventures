## You'll want to know: かいぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C229-OFFICE229-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かいぎ** — *kaigi* — "a meeting; a conference".

> **かいしゃは まいしゅう かいぎが あります。** — *kaisha wa maishū kaigi ga arimasu* — "The company has a meeting every week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいぎ** (*kaigi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "meet" (Middle Chinese *hwajH*) and *gi*, "discuss" (*ngjeH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
