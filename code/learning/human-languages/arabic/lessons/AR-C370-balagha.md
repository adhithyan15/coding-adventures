## You'll want to know: بالغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C370-NAQD370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بالغ** — *bālagha* — "to exaggerate".

> بالغ صديقي في وصف الفندق.
>
> *bālagha ṣadīqī fī waṣf al-funduq.*
>
> My friend exaggerated in describing the hotel.

Present: يبالغ, *yubāligh*.

It takes في: بالغ في ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بالغ** (*bālagha*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-l-gh*, "to reach" (Lane). It is Form III, to strive to reach the utmost: to overdo, to exaggerate.
