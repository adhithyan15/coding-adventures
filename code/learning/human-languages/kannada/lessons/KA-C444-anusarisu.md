# ಅನುಸರಿಸು (anusarisu) — to comply with; to go by

## You'll want to know: ಅನುಸರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C444-NIYAMA444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅನುಸರಿಸು** — *anusarisu* — "to comply with; to go by".

> ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಚಾಲಕರು ಸಿಗ್ನಲ್ ಅನುಸರಿಸಬೇಕು.
>
> *ellā cālakaru signal anusarisabēku.*
>
> All drivers must go by the signals.

Past (I …): ಅನುಸರಿಸಿದೆ, *anusariside*.

ನಿಯಮ ಅನುಸರಿಸು, to follow a rule; ಹಿಂಬಾಲಿಸು is to follow behind someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅನುಸರಿಸು** (*anusarisu*) is built from Sanskrit *anusaraṇa*, "going after", the noun of *anusarati*, "runs after, follows" (CDIAL 338), with the verb ending -*isu*.
