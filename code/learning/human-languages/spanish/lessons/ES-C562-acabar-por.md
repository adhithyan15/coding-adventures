## You'll want to know: acabar por
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C562-VOLVER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**acabar por** — "to end up (doing)".

> Acabó por aceptar el trabajo.
>
> *He ended up accepting the job.*

*Acabar por* + infinitive, or *acabar* + gerund: *Acabó aceptando.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acabar por* is built on *acabar* 'to finish', made from *a-* and *cabo* 'an end', which comes from Latin *caput* 'head' (Lewis & Short). To end up doing something is to reach its end.
