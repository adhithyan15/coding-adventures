Sanskrit, chapter 366: Reaching a Conclusion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निष्कर्षः (niṣkarṣaḥ) — a conclusion; the gist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for curiosity, then the Sanskrit for notices.

You'll want to know: निष्कर्षः.
निष्कर्षः — niṣkarṣaḥ — "a conclusion; the gist".
अनुसन्धानस्य निष्कर्षः स्पष्टः अस्ति। anusandhānasya niṣkarṣaḥ spaṣṭaḥ asti. The conclusion of the research is clear.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
निष्कर्षः (niṣkarṣaḥ) is built on nis, "out", and the root √kṛṣ, "to draw"; MW gives niṣ-karṣa "drawing out" and "the essence, the chief point", and "a conclusion" is its modern sense (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विचारयति (vicārayati) — considers; deliberates.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a review, then the Sanskrit for a calculation.

You'll want to know: विचारयति.
विचारयति — vicārayati — "considers; deliberates".
न्यायाधीशः सर्वं विचारयति। nyāyādhīśaḥ sarvaṃ vicārayati. The judge considers everything.
Past (he …): सः प्रश्नं विचारितवान्, saḥ praśnaṃ vicāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
विचारयति (vicārayati) is built on vi and the root √car, "to move", in its causative form; MW gives vi-cārita "deliberated, considered, discussed, judged" (MW).

The word, taken apart.
निश्चिनोति (niścinoti) is built on niś and the root √ci, "to gather"; MW gives niś-cita "one who has come to a conclusion, determined" (MW).
