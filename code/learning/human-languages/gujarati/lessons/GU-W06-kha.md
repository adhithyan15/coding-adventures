# ખ — the consonant kha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->

Write unaspirated **ક** once. Say *ka*. Add a clear puff of air aloud for
*kha*, then put the old shape away.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-KHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ખ** visible. Descend through the joined left lobe and curl right through
the middle. Lift once, descend the separate right spine, and turn through its
lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ખ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice left lobe, inner curl,
separate spine; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ખ** once. Compare only the inner curl and right spine; repair
one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W06-kha-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-KHA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write aspirated kha from memory.","answer":"ખ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ખ — the lobe, curl, and separate spine are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ખ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *kha* once. Uncover **ખ**, compare, and stop after one
repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **ક ખ** aloud and keep unaspirated *ka* apart from aspirated
*kha*.
