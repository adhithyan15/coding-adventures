## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہار** (*hār*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *hāra*, "necklace" (CDIAL 14059).
