## You'll want to know: двоюродный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C492-STEPFAMILY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**двоюродный** (*dvoyúrodnyy*) — "related as cousins".

> Двоюродная сестра пригласила меня на свадьбу.
>
> *Dvoyúrodnaya sestrá priglasíla menyá na svádbu.*
>
> My cousin invited me to her wedding.

*Двоюродный брат / двоюродная сестра* — a (first) cousin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*двоюродный* is a compound that EtymDB traces to Old East Slavic *dъvoju rodu* 'of two generations', with *род* 'kin'.
