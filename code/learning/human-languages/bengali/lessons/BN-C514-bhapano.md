## You'll want to know: ভাপানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C514-UNUNE514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাপানো** — *bhāpāno* — "to steam (food)".

> দিদিমা ইলিশ মাছ ভাপালেন।
>
> *Didimā ilish māchh bhāpālen.*
>
> My grandmother steamed some hilsa.

Past (he/she …): তিনি মাছ ভাপালেন, *tini māchh bhāpālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাপানো** (*bhāpāno*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *bhāpā*, "to emit steam", under Sanskrit *bāṣpāyate* (CDIAL 9225).
