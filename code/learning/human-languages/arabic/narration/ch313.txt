Arabic, chapter 313: Right away.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
على الفور (ʿalā al-fawr) — on the spot, at once.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an explorer, then the Arabic for a treasure.

You'll want to know: على الفور.
على الفور — ʿalā al-fawr — "on the spot, at once".
اتصلت بالطبيب على الفور. ittaṣaltu bi-ṭ-ṭabīb ʿalā al-fawr. I called the doctor at once.
The taught فورا is the same word as an adverb.

The word, taken apart.
على الفور (ʿalā al-fawr) is "on the boiling": fawr is built on the native Arabic root f-w-r, "to boil, to gush" (Lane), the root of the taught fawran. What is done at the boil is done at once.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سرعان ما (surʿāna mā) — before long, soon after.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a treasure, then the Arabic for on the spot.

You'll want to know: سرعان ما.
سرعان ما — surʿāna mā — "before long, soon after".
بدأ المطر وسرعان ما توقف. badaʾa al-maṭar wa-surʿāna mā tawaqqafa. The rain began and soon stopped.
A past verb follows it: سرعان ما (surʿāna mā) عاد.

The word, taken apart.
سرعان ما (surʿāna mā) is built on the native Arabic root s-r-ʿ, "to be quick" (Lane), the root of surʿa, speed: surʿāna means "how quick!".

Lesson 3 of 5.
تدريجيا (tadrījiyyan) — gradually, step by step.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for on the spot, then the Arabic for before long.

You'll want to know: تدريجيا.
تدريجيا — tadrījiyyan — "gradually, step by step".
تحسن الطقس تدريجيا. taḥassana aṭ-ṭaqs tadrījiyyan. The weather gradually got better.

The word, taken apart.
تدريجيا (tadrījiyyan) is built on the native Arabic root d-r-j (Lane), "to walk step by step", the root of daraja, a step. Tadrīj is the verbal noun of Form II; -iyyan makes it an adverb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
استعاد (istaʿāda) — to regain, to get back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for gradually, then the Arabic for anew.

You'll want to know: استعاد.
استعاد — istaʿāda — "to regain, to get back".
استعاد الرجل حقيبته من الشرطة. istaʿāda ar-rajul ḥaqībatahu min ash-shurṭa. The man got his bag back from the police.
Present: يستعيد, yastaʿīd.
استعاد (istaʿāda) ذكرياته: he went back over his memories.

The word, taken apart.
استعاد (istaʿāda) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-w-d (Lane), "to return", the root of ʿāda, he came back. It is Form X, "to seek the return of".
