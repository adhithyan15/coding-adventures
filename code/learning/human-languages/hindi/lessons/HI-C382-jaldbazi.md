## You'll want to know: जल्दबाज़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C382-JALDBAZI382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जल्दबाज़ी** — *jaldbāzī* — "haste, rushing".

> जल्दबाज़ी मत करो, वरना ग़लती होगी।
>
> *jaldbāzī mat karo, varnā ġaltī hogī.*
>
> Don't rush, or you'll make a mistake.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जल्दबाज़ी** (*jaldbāzī*) is usually traced to Persian *jald*, "quick", and *bāzī*, "play, a game": playing at speed.
