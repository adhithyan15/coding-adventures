# దండించు (daṇḍiñcu) — to chastise; to discipline

## You'll want to know: దండించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C370-GATHA370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**దండించు** — *daṇḍiñcu* — "to chastise; to discipline".

> తండ్రి అల్లరి చేసిన కొడుకును దండించాడు.
>
> *taṇḍri allari cēsina koḍukunu daṇḍiñcāḍu.*
>
> The father punished his naughty son.

Past (I …): దండించాను, *daṇḍiñcānu*.

Built on the Sanskrit word for a rod.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దండించు** (*daṇḍiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *daṇḍayati*, "chastises, fines" (CDIAL 6136), from *daṇḍa*, "a stick, a rod" (CDIAL 6128).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
