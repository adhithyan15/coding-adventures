## You'll want to know: зашивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C448-KHIMCHISTKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**зашивать** (*zashivát*) — "to sew up, to mend (by sewing)".

> Мама зашивает дыру на брюках.
>
> *Máma zashiváyet dyrú na bryúkakh.*
>
> Mum is sewing up the hole in the trousers.

Imperfective; perfective *зашить* (*зашью, зашьёшь*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*зашивать* is built from *за-* and *-шивать*, from *шить* 'to sew', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *šiti* 'to sew' (EtymDB).
