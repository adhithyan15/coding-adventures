Urdu, chapter 330: Turning the Pages.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
صفحہ (safhā) — a page.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a chapter of a book, then the Urdu for an ending.

You'll want to know: صفحہ.
صفحہ — safhā — "a page".
اس کتاب میں سو صفحے ہیں۔ is kitāb meṅ sau safhe haiṅ. This book has a hundred pages.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
صفحہ (safhā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṣafḥa, "a flat surface, a page". The plural is صفحے.

Lesson 3 of 7.
قاری (qārī) — a reader.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a page, then the Urdu for a title of a book.

You'll want to know: قاری.
قاری — qārī — "a reader".
یہ کتاب ہر قاری کو پسند آئی۔ yih kitāb har qārī ko pasand āī. Every reader liked this book.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
قاری (qārī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qāriʾ, "one who reads aloud"; in Urdu it is any reader of a book or a paper.

Past (it …): وہ کتاب بکی, voh kitāb bikī.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin biknā, "to be sold", under Sanskrit vikrīyatē (CDIAL 11642). It is the partner of بیچنا: you بیچنا a thing, and it بکنا.
