## You'll want to know: കഴയ്ക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C540-KLINIKKU540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**കഴയ്ക്കുക** — *kaḻaykkuka* — "to feel sore (of limbs)".

> ഒരുപാട് നടന്നതുകൊണ്ട് കാലുകൾ കഴച്ചു.
>
> *orupāṭŭ naṭannatukoṇṭŭ kālukaḷ kaḻaccu.*
>
> My legs ached from walking so much.

Past: കഴച്ചു, *kaḻaccu*.

The tired ache in arms and legs after hard use.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കഴയ്ക്കുക** (*kaḻaykkuka*) looks native, but DEDR has no entry that clearly holds it, so its origin is not settled.
