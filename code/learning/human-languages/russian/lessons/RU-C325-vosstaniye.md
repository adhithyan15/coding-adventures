## You'll want to know: восстание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C325-IMPERIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**восстание** (*vosstániye*) — "an uprising, a revolt".

> Восстание началось на юге страны.
>
> *Vosstániye nachalós na yúge straný.*
>
> The uprising began in the south of the country.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*восстание* is built on *восстать* "to rise up", from *вос-* "up" and *стать* "to stand", inherited from Proto-Slavic *stati*, PIE *steh₂-* (EtymDB).
