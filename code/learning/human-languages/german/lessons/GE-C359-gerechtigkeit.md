## You'll want to know: die Gerechtigkeit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C359-GERECHTIGKEIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Gerechtigkeit** — "justice, fairness".

> Viele junge Leute kämpfen für mehr Gerechtigkeit.
>
> *Many young people fight for more justice.*

No plural in everyday use. From *gerecht* — fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gerechtigkeit* is formed from *gerecht* "fair, righteous", Old High German *gireht* (EtymDB), with *-igkeit*; the adjective is built on the word for "right", Proto-Germanic *rehtaz*.
