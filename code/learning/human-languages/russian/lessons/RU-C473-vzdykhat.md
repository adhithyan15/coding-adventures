## You'll want to know: вздыхать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C473-LETDOWN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**вздыхать** (*vzdykhát*) — "to sigh".

> Бабушка вздыхает и смотрит в окно.
>
> *Bábushka vzdykháyet i smótrit v oknó.*
>
> Grandma sighs and gazes out of the window.

Imperfective; perfective вздохнуть. The noun is *вздох* — a sigh.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вздыхать* is built on *вз-* 'up' and the root of *дышать* 'to breathe' and *дух* 'breath', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic *duxъ* 'breath, spirit'.
