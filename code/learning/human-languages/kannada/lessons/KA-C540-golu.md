## You'll want to know: ಗೋಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C540-KUSTI540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೋಲು** — *gōlu* — "a goal in sport".

> ಕೊನೆಯ ನಿಮಿಷದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನು ಗೋಲು ಹೊಡೆದನು.
>
> *koneya nimiṣadalli avanu gōlu hoḍedanu.*
>
> He scored a goal in the last minute.

ಗೋಲು ಹೊಡೆ, to score a goal. A goal in life is ಗುರಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೋಲು** (*gōlu*) is a loan from English *goal*, with the -ು many loans take in speech.
