## You'll want to know: عفا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C534-NASIHA534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عفا** — *ʿafā* — "to pardon".

> عفا المعلم عن الطالب.
>
> *ʿafā al-muʿallim ʿan aṭ-ṭālib.*
>
> The teacher pardoned the student.

Present: يعفو, *yaʿfū*.

It takes عن.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عفا** (*ʿafā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-f-w*, "to efface; to pardon; to give health" (Lane). ʿAfwan, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
