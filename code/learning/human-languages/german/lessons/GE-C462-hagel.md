## You'll want to know: der Hagel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C462-UNWETTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Hagel** — "the hail".

> Der Hagel hat viele Autos beschädigt.
>
> *The hail damaged lots of cars.*

No plural. *Die Hagelkörner* — hailstones.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hagel* is inherited from Old High German *hagal*, and English *hail* is a cousin. EtymDB lists the verb *hageln* from Middle High German *hagelen*.
