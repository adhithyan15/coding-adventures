## You'll want to know: die Ungleichheit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C359-GERECHTIGKEIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ungleichheit** — "inequality".

> Die Ungleichheit zwischen Arm und Reich wird größer.
>
> *The inequality between rich and poor is growing.*

Plural: *die Ungleichheiten*. From *ungleich* — unequal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ungleichheit* is built from *un-* "not", *gleich* "equal", inherited from Old High German *gilīh* and Proto-Germanic *galīkaz* "alike" (EtymDB), and the ending *-heit*.
