Urdu, chapter 462: Sense and Innocence.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عقل (aql) — sense; wits.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for moral conduct, then the Urdu for to show respect.

You'll want to know: عقل.
عقل — aql — "sense; wits".
اس لڑکے میں بڑی عقل ہے۔ is laṛke meṅ baṛī aql hai. This boy has a lot of sense.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
عقل (aql) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿaql, "reason, intelligence".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بہکنا (bahaknā) — to be led astray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for naive, then the Urdu for innocent.

You'll want to know: بہکنا.
بہکنا — bahaknā — "to be led astray".
وہ غلط دوستوں کے ساتھ بہک گیا۔ voh ghalat dostoṅ ke sāth bahak gayā. He was led astray by the wrong friends.
Past (he …): وہ بہک گیا, voh bahak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
بہکنا (bahaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bahaknā, "to be misled", under Sanskrit vyáthatē, "trembles, goes astray" (CDIAL 12164).
