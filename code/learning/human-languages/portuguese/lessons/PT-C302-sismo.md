## You'll want to know: o sismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C302-SISMO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o sismo** — "the quake (formal)".

> O sismo foi sentido em todo o país.
>
> *The quake was felt all over the country.*

A news word in Portugal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sismo* was borrowed from Greek *seismós* "a shaking, an earthquake" (EtymDB), from *seíein* "to shake".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
