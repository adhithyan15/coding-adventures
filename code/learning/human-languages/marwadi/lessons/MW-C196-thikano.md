## You'll want to know: ठिकाणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C196-POST196-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ठिकाणो** — *ṭhikāṇo* — "an address; whereabouts".

> म्हारो ठिकाणो चिट्ठी माथै है।
>
> *mhāro ṭhikāṇo chiṭṭhī māthai hai.*
>
> My address is on the letter.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठिकाणो** (*ṭhikāṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭhīkka*, "firm, right"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ṭhīka*, "trace, whereabouts", and Hindi *ṭhikānā* under it (CDIAL 5503).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
