## You'll want to know: il ruolo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C520-RECITARE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il ruolo** — "role, part".

> Nello spettacolo ho il ruolo del padre.
>
> *In the play I have the part of the father.*

Also at work: *il mio ruolo in azienda*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruolo* was borrowed from French *rôle* "a roll, a list", from Medieval Latin *rotulus* "a roll of parchment", from *rota* "wheel" (Lewis & Short).
