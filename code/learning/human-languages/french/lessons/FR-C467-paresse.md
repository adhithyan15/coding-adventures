## You'll want to know: la paresse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C467-DEFAUTS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la paresse** — "laziness".

> Le dimanche, je me laisse aller à la paresse.
>
> *On Sundays I give in to laziness.*

The noun of *paresseux* "lazy".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la paresse* is inherited, through Old French *peresce*, from Latin *pigritia* "sluggishness" (EtymDB), built on *piger* "reluctant, slow" (Lewis & Short).
