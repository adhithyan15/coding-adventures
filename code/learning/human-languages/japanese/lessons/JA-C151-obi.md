## You'll want to know: おび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C151-KIMONO151-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おび** — *obi* — "a kimono sash".

> **あねは あかい おびを むすびます。** — *ane wa akai obi o musubimasu* — "My elder sister ties a red sash."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おび** (*obi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
