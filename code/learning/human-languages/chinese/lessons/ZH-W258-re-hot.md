## Script — the hand piece and a hooked bend, over four dots
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-RE-HOT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 热

**Three pieces: two side by side on top, and a row of dots under them.**

**The top left piece, three strokes,** the hand piece of 打: a short bar, a vertical with a hook, then a stroke rising to the right across it.

**The top right piece, three strokes.** A stroke falling to the left. A bar that runs right, turns down, bends out to the right and hooks up. Then a dot inside.

**The foot, four dots,** left to right: the first leans to the left, the other three to the right.

3 + 3 + 4 = ten.
