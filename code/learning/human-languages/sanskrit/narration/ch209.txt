Lesson 1 of 5.
कविः (kaviḥ) — a poet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an enemy, then the Sanskrit for a neighbour.

You'll want to know: कविः.
कविः — kaviḥ — "a poet".

The word, taken apart.
kavi is a Rigvedic word for a wise man or seer, and later a poet (CDIAL 2964). MW links it to a root √kū and to kāvya, "poetry". Hindi, Telugu and Malayalam took it over as their word for "poet".

Lesson 2 of 5.
गायकः (gāyakaḥ) — a singer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a neighbour, then the Sanskrit for a poet.

You'll want to know: गायकः.
गायकः — gāyakaḥ — "a singer".

The word, taken apart.
gāyaka is built on the root √gai, "to sing", with the agent suffix -aka (MW): "one who sings". The verb gāyati lives on as Gujarati gāvũ, "sings" (CDIAL 4135).

Lesson 3 of 5.
नर्तकः (nartakaḥ) — a dancer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: नर्तकः.
नर्तकः — nartakaḥ — "a dancer".

The word, taken apart.
nartaka is built on the causative of the root √nṛt, "to dance" (MW), the root of nṛtya, "dance", which you have already met. The verb nṛtyati lives on as Hindi nācnā, "to dance" (CDIAL 7583).

Lesson 4 of 5.
चित्रकारः (citrakāraḥ) — a painter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a singer, then the Sanskrit for a dancer.

You'll want to know: चित्रकारः.
चित्रकारः — citrakāraḥ — "a painter".

The word, taken apart.
citrakāra is a compound of citra, "bright, variegated; a picture", and kāra, "maker", from the root √kṛ (MW; CDIAL 4807). It lives on as Hindi citārī, "painter".

Lesson 5 of 5.
शिल्पी (śilpī) — a craftsman, a sculptor.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a dancer, then the Sanskrit for a painter.

You'll want to know: शिल्पी.
शिल्पी — śilpī — "a craftsman, a sculptor".

The word, taken apart.
śilpī is the nominative of śilpin, "one who has a craft", built on śilpa, "art, craft" (MW). MW calls śilpa "of doubtful derivation", so its origin is uncertain.
