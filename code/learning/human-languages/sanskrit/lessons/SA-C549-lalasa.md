## You'll want to know: लालसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C549-LALASA549-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लालसा** — *lālasā* — "a craving".

> यदि मधुरे लालसा अस्ति, तर्हि एकं मोदकं खादतु।
>
> *yadi madhure lālasā asti, tarhi ekaṃ modakaṃ khādatu.*
>
> If you crave something sweet, eat a sweet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लालसा** (*lālasā*) comes from the intensive of the root √las, "to play, to shine", as MW derives it; MW gives *lālasa* and *lālasā* as longing, ardent desire (MW).
