# ન and નહીં — the two smaller *nots*

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NEGATE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C03-VANDHONAHI-01, GU-LEX-C50-FEEL-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I don't drink tea*. Then the reply you have given since the first
chapters to thanks or to an apology: **વાંધો નહીં**, *no problem*. And the word
for *worry*: **ચિંતા**.

## Grammar Lens: ન in front of a request
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NA-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C50-FEEL-04, GU-LEX-MAAF-01] -->

**નથી** denies a statement. A request is not a statement, so it takes the bare
**ન** (*na*) in front of the verb:

> **ચિંતા ન કરો.** — *cintā na karo.* — Don't worry.

**કરો** is the polite *do!* that **માફ કરો** has carried since the courtesy
chapter. Put **ન** in front of it and the request becomes a request *not* to.

## You'll want to know: નહીં, for one word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NAHI-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-VANDHONAHI-01, GU-LEX-PAANI, GU-LEX-C94-TIME94-05] -->

**નહીં** (*nahī̃*) cancels a word or a phrase rather than a whole sentence:

> **ચા નહીં, પાણી.** — *cā nahī̃, pāṇī.* — Not tea: water.
> **હમણાં નહીં.** — *hamṇā̃ nahī̃.* — Not now.

**વાંધો નહીં** is built this way: *objection — none*.

Three negatives, three jobs:

- **નથી** — a statement: *it is not so*;
- **ન** — a request: *don't*;
- **નહીં** — one word: *not this*.
