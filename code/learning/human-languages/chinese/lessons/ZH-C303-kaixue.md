## You'll want to know: 开学
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C303-NAME303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**开学** — *kāixué* — "to start the school term".

> *kāixué de shíhou, wǒ rènshi le tā* — I got to know him when term started.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开学** (*kāixué*) is built from 开 'to open' and 学 'to study', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *haewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to open up': the opening of the school year.
