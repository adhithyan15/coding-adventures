# もしくは (moshikuwa) — or else; or

## You'll want to know: もしくは
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C528-OR528-04]; assesses=[] -->

**もしくは** — *moshikuwa* — "or else; or".

> **もし あめが ふったら、しあいは げつようび もしくは かようびです。** — *moshi ame ga futtara, shiai wa getsuyōbi moshikuwa kayōbi desu* — "If it rains, the match will be on Monday or Tuesday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もしくは** (*moshikuwa*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
