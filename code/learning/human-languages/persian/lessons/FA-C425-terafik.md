## You'll want to know: ترافیک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C425-OTOBAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ترافیک** (*terâfik*) — "traffic; a traffic jam".

> صبحها در اتوبان ترافیک سنگین است.
>
> *Sobh-hâ dar otobân terâfik sangin ast.*
>
> In the mornings the traffic on the motorway is heavy.

*Dar terâfik mândan* — to be stuck in traffic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terâfik* was borrowed from a European language: it is usually traced to English *traffic* or French *trafic*. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
