## You'll want to know: 自从
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C308-SINCE308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**自从** — *zìcóng* — "since; ever since".

> **自从那天以后我天天写日记** *zìcóng nà tiān yǐhòu, wǒ tiāntiān xiě rìjì* — Since that day, I've kept a diary every day.

The new character, in pieces: 自 — six strokes: a short sweep down to the left on top, then a tall frame: a vertical, a bar that turns down the right side, two bars inside, and the bar that closes the foot, like 日 grown taller. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**自从** (*zìcóng*) is built from 自 'from' and 从 'from', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH* and *dzjowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from' and 'by way of' and the second 'to go along with'.
