Kannada, chapter 293: Mustard seeds, Coriander, Curry leaves, Tomato, Carrot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಾಸಿವೆ (sāsive) — mustard seeds.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for messy, then the Kannada for convenient.

You'll want to know: ಸಾಸಿವೆ.
ಸಾಸಿವೆ — sāsive — "mustard seeds".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಸಿವೆ (sāsive) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit sarṣapa, "mustard seed" (CDIAL 13281), close to Pali sāsapa: the rṣ became s and the p became v.

ಕೊತ್ತಂಬರಿ (kottambari) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit kustumbarī, "coriander" (CDIAL 3380), close to the reconstructed kōstambarī, "coriander", that CDIAL also gives. CDIAL calls the Sanskrit word itself non-Aryan.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕರಿಬೇವು (karibēvu) — curry leaves.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for mustard seeds, then the Kannada for coriander.

You'll want to know: ಕರಿಬೇವು.
ಕರಿಬೇವು — karibēvu — "curry leaves".

The word, taken apart.
ಕರಿಬೇವು (karibēvu) is native Dravidian: kari, "black", which DEDR 1278 lists, and bēvu, "neem tree" (DEDR 5531): "black neem".
