## You'll want to know: बुरकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C523-CHAKU523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बुरकना** — *buraknā* — "to sprinkle (a powder)".

> माँ ने सब्ज़ी पर थोड़ा नमक बुरका।
>
> *Māṁ ne sabzī par thoṛā namak burkā.*
>
> Mother sprinkled a little salt over the vegetables.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने हलवे पर चीनी बुरकी, *usne halve par cīnī burkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुरकना** (*buraknā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*būra*, "powder", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9298). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to sprinkle".
