## You'll want to know: শ্মশান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C491-SHRADDHO491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**শ্মশান** — *shmôshān* — "a cremation ground".

> আমরা দাদুকে নদীর ধারের শ্মশানে নিয়ে গেলাম।
>
> *āmrā dāduke nôdīr dhārer shmôshāne niye gelām.*
>
> We took my grandfather to the cremation ground by the river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শ্মশান** (*shmôshān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śmaśāna*, "a place for burning the dead" (CDIAL 12658); CDIAL lists a worn-down Bengali *masān* beside it.
