## You'll want to know: гантели
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C513-TRAINING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**гантели** (*gantéli*) — "dumbbells".

> Дома у меня есть гантели и коврик.
>
> *Dóma u menyá yest gantéli i kóvrik.*
>
> At home I have dumbbells and a mat.

Plural of *гантель* (feminine).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гантели* is the plural of *гантель*, borrowed from German *Hantel* 'dumbbell' (EtymDB).
