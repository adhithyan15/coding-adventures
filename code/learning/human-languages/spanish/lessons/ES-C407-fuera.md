# fuera — outside

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-CASA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **la casa**. Inside it is one location; beyond it is
**fuera**, outside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-FUERA]; assesses=[] -->

**Fuera** comes from Latin **foras**, “outward.” The core picture is crossing
the boundary and standing beyond it. The opening **fue-** is one syllable: say
FWE-ra, not fu-E-ra.

## Grammar Lens: fuera and fuera de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-FUERA, ES-LEX-CASA] -->

**Está fuera** means it is outside. Add **de** for the boundary: **fuera de la
casa**, outside the house.
