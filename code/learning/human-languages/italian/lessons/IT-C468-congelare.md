## You'll want to know: congelare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C468-GELO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**congelare** — "to deep-freeze; to freeze (food)".

> Congeliamo il pane per la settimana.
>
> *We freeze the bread for the week.*

*Il congelatore* — the freezer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*congelare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *congelāre* 'to freeze solid' (EtymDB), from *gelū* 'frost'.
