Japanese, chapter 258: How It Felt, and What I Hope For.
7 lessons. You can do the first 4 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
たのしかった / うれしかった (tanoshikatta / ureshikatta) — how it felt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "fun" and "glad". (tanoshii, ureshii.) Then "a trip". (ryokō.) Today they move into the past.

You'll want to know: -かった.
りょこうは たのしかったです。 ryokō wa tanoshikatta desu. — "The trip was fun."
An i-adjective carries its own past: the last い becomes かった, and です stays as it is to keep the sentence polite. たのしい gives たのしかった (tanoshikatta), うれしい gives うれしかった (ureshikatta).
うれしかったです。 ureshikatta desu. — "I was glad."
きのうは さむかったです。 kinō wa samukatta desu. — "It was cold yesterday."
いい works from its older form よい: よかった (yokatta), "it was good".
えいがは よかったです。 eiga wa yokatta desu. — "The film was good."

You'll want to know: -くなかった, and でした.
"Was not" comes from the -くない you know: おもしろくない becomes おもしろくなかった (omoshirokunakatta).
えいがは おもしろくなかったです。 eiga wa omoshirokunakatta desu. — "The film was not interesting."
A na-adjective keeps the noun's way: でした (deshita) after it.
うみは しずかでした。 umi wa shizuka deshita. — "The sea was calm."
ちちは げんきでした。 chichi wa genki deshita. — "My father was well."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "It was cold yesterday" — kinō wa samukatta desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It was hot yesterday" — kinō wa atsukatta desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It was cool yesterday" — kinō wa suzushikatta desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that the trip was fun.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"It was sad"? (かなしかったです.) "It was good"? (よかったです.) "It was not fun"? (たのしくなかったです.) "It was quiet"? (しずかでした.)


Lesson 2 of 7.
しょうらい (shōrai) — the future; one's prospects.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say "next year" and "worry". (rainen, shinpai.)

You'll want to know: しょうらい.
しょうらい — shōrai — "the future", the years ahead of you: further off than らいねん.
しょうらいが しんぱいです。 shōrai ga shinpai desu. — "I worry about the future."
しょうらい りょこうに いく つもりです。 shōrai ryokō ni iku tsumori desu. — "In the future I plan to travel."

The word, taken apart.
しょうらい (shōrai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shō, which KANJIDIC2 glosses "from now on" among other senses, and rai, "come" (Middle Chinese loj), the rai of らいねん (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict glosses the word "future; (future) prospects" (JMdict).

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 3 of 7.
〜に なりたいです (ni naritai desu) — what I want to become.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "my elder brother became a teacher". (ani wa sensei ni narimashita.) Then "a doctor". (isha.)

You'll want to know: 〜に なりたいです.
いしゃに なりたいです。 isha ni naritai desu. — "I want to become a doctor."
なる takes -たい, as いく does in いきたい: なりたい (naritai), "want to become". です makes it polite.
しょうらい せんせいに なりたいです。 shōrai sensei ni naritai desu. — "In the future I want to become a teacher."
A na-adjective takes に too:
日本語が じょうずに なりたいです。 nihongo ga jōzu ni naritai desu. — "I want to get good at Japanese."

You'll want to know: ゆめは 〜ことです.
こと (koto) is "a thing, a matter". After the dictionary form of a verb it turns the action into a thing, and ゆめは (yume wa), "my dream is", can name it:
ゆめは 日本に すむ ことです。 yume wa nihon ni sumu koto desu. — "My dream is to live in Japan."
ゆめは うみの ちかくに すむ ことです。 yume wa umi no chikaku ni sumu koto desu. — "My dream is to live near the sea."

[your turn — say: "I want to become a nurse" — kangoshi ni naritai desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I want to become a singer" — kashu ni naritai desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 4 of 7.
いった ことが あります (itta koto ga arimasu) — I have done it before.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "my dream is to live in Japan". (yume wa nihon ni sumu koto desu.) Then say "once". (ichido.)

You'll want to know: -た ことが あります.
日本に いった ことが あります。 nihon ni itta koto ga arimasu. — "I have been to Japan."
The plain past, こと, then が あります (ga arimasu): "there is the matter of having gone". It says you have done something at least once, at some time in your life.
すしを たべた ことが あります。 sushi o tabeta koto ga arimasu. — "I have eaten sushi."

You'll want to know: ありますか, and いちども.
To ask, か goes at the end:
うみで およいだ ことが ありますか。 umi de oyoida koto ga arimasu ka. — "Have you ever swum in the sea?"
For "never", いちども (ichido mo), "not even once", and ありません:
いちども およいだ ことが ありません。 ichido mo oyoida koto ga arimasen. — "I have never swum."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I have eaten sushi" — sushi o tabeta koto ga arimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I have eaten tempura" — tenpura o tabeta koto ga arimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I have eaten natto" — nattō o tabeta koto ga arimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that you have been to Japan.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I have been there"? (いった ことが あります (itta koto ga arimasu).) "Have you ever swum?" (およいだ ことが ありますか.) "I have never swum"? (いちども およいだ ことが ありません.)


Lesson 5 of 7.
今まで (imamade) — until now.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: You'll want to know: 今まで.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say "now". (ima.) Then "I have never swum". (ichido mo oyoida koto ga arimasen.)

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
You'll want to know: 今まで.
今まで — imamade — "until now", "so far": いま, "now", with まで, "as far as".
今まで いちども いった ことが ありません。 imamade ichido mo itta koto ga arimasen. — "I have never been there until now."
今まで ちかくに すんでいました。 imamade chikaku ni sunde imashita. — "Until now I lived nearby."
The first sign is new: the kanji for いま, read いま (ima) here. The next lesson writes it.

The word, taken apart.
今まで (imamade) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: いま (ima), "now", written with its kanji and read the native way, and まで (made), "as far as". JMdict glosses it "until now; so far", and KANJIDIC2 gives いま as the kanji's native reading (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 6 of 7.
今 (ima) — now, four strokes.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Script — the shape and Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say imamade, until now. Then call up 人: two strokes, the second starting on the first.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — the shape.
今 — ima.
Its parts:
人 (a roof) on top.
a short bar.
a bar turning down to the left.
Four strokes, three lifts:
draw the falling stroke on the left of the roof, from the top down to the left.
lift, draw the falling stroke on the right of the roof, down to the right.
lift, draw the short bar under the roof from left to right.
lift, draw the lower bar from left to right, then without lifting turn down to the left.
The roof is drawn like 人, wider. Here it is read いま (ima), as in 今まで (imamade); this lesson asks only for that reading.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
Find 今 (ima) in 今まで and say imamade.
Copy 今 three times, saying ima at each one.
Find it once more, in 今まで ちかくに すんでいました (imamade chikaku ni sunde imashita), and say the line.

Wrap-up Recall.
Which sign is its roof drawn like? (人.)
