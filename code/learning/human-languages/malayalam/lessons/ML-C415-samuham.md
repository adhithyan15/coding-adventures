## You'll want to know: സമൂഹം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C415-SAMUHAM415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**സമൂഹം** — *samūhaṁ* — "society".

> സമൂഹത്തിൽ എല്ലാവർക്കും ഒരേ അവകാശമാണ്.
>
> *samūhattil ellāvarkkuṁ orē avakāśamāṇŭ.*
>
> Everyone has the same rights in society.

The adjective is സാമൂഹിക, social: സാമൂഹിക പ്രശ്നം, a social problem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സമൂഹം** (*samūhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samūha*, "a heap, a collection, an assembly" (CDIAL 13241). A society is people gathered together.
