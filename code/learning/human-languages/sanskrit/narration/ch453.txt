Sanskrit, chapter 453: Missing Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उत्कण्ठा (utkaṇṭhā) — pining for someone absent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for agitated, then the Sanskrit for wretched.

You'll want to know: उत्कण्ठा.
उत्कण्ठा — utkaṇṭhā — "pining for someone absent".
प्रवासे मम गृहस्य उत्कण्ठा भवति। pravāse mama gṛhasya utkaṇṭhā bhavati. When I am away, I long for home.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उत्कण्ठा (utkaṇṭhā) is built from ud, "up", and kaṇṭha, "the neck" (कण्ठः): MW's ut-kaṇṭha is "having the neck uplifted", and utkaṇṭhā "longing for a beloved person, missing someone" (MW).

विधुरः — vidhuraḥ — "bereft; alone".
मित्रं दूरे वसति, अतः अहं विधुरः अस्मि। mitraṃ dūre vasati, ataḥ ahaṃ vidhuraḥ asmi. My friend lives far away, so I feel alone.

The word, taken apart.
विधुरः (vidhuraḥ) MW gives "bereft, bereaved (esp. of any loved person), alone, solitary"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रवसति (pravasati) — lives away from home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for pining for someone absent, then the Sanskrit for bereft.

You'll want to know: प्रवसति.
प्रवसति — pravasati — "lives away from home".
मम भ्राता दूरे नगरे प्रवसति। mama bhrātā dūre nagare pravasati. My brother lives away from home in a distant city.
Past (he …): सः संवत्सरं प्रोषितवान्, saḥ saṃvatsaraṃ proṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रवसति (pravasati) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √vas, "to dwell", the root of वसति; MW gives "to go or sojourn abroad" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सन्दिशति (sandiśati) — sends word; sends a message.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for lives away from home, then the Sanskrit for a dear friend.

You'll want to know: सन्दिशति.
सन्दिशति — sandiśati — "sends word; sends a message".
भ्राता नगरात् प्रतिदिनं सन्दिशति। bhrātā nagarāt pratidinaṃ sandiśati. My brother sends word from the city every day.
Past (he …): सः नगरात् सन्दिष्टवान्, saḥ nagarāt sandiṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
सन्दिशति (sandiśati) is built from sam, "together", and the root √diś, "to point out, to show"; MW gives "to state, tell, direct" and "to give an order or message to" someone (MW).
