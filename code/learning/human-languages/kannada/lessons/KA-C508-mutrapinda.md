# ಮೂತ್ರಪಿಂಡ (mūtrapiṇḍa) — a kidney

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C508-DEHA508-01, KA-LEX-C508-DEHA508-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for the brain, then the Kannada for a lung.

## You'll want to know: ಮೂತ್ರಪಿಂಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C508-DEHA508-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂತ್ರಪಿಂಡ** — *mūtrapiṇḍa* — "a kidney".

> ಸಾಕಷ್ಟು ನೀರು ಕುಡಿದರೆ ಮೂತ್ರಪಿಂಡ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *sākaṣṭu nīru kuḍidare mūtrapiṇḍa cennāgiruttade.*
>
> If you drink enough water, the kidneys stay healthy.

Doctors and patients also say ಕಿಡ್ನಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂತ್ರಪಿಂಡ** (*mūtrapiṇḍa*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *mūtra*, "urine" (CDIAL 10234), and *piṇḍa*, "a lump, a ball" (CDIAL 8168).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
