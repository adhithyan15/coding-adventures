## You'll want to know: चढ़णो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C217-CROSS217-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चढ़णो** — *chaṛhṇo* — "to climb; to get on (an animal or a vehicle)".

> हूं काल ऊंट माथै चढ़सूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl ū̃ṭ māthai chaṛhsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll climb up onto the camel.

Past (he …): वो ऊंट माथै चढ़्यो, *vo ū̃ṭ māthai chaṛhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चढ़णो** (*chaṛhṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*caḍhati*, "rises"; CDIAL lists Marwari *caṛhṇo*, "to climb" (CDIAL 4578).
