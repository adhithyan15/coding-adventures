## You'll want to know: zuòwèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C284-ZUOWEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòwèi* — "a seat".

> *zhèr yǒu zuòwèi ma* — Is there a seat here?

Its characters come later. The first is not the 坐 you write but one that shares its sound and holds it under a roof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòwèi* is built from zuò 'a seat' and wèi 'a place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzwaH* and *hwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the stand of a bed' and 'a position; a rank'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZUO-01, ZH-LEX-C44-PLACES44-04] -->

坐 is the verb, to sit; *zuòwèi* is the noun, the seat itself: *wǒ de zuòwèi zài nǎr?*, where's my seat?
