## You'll want to know: افسانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C347-AFSANE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**افسانه** (*afsâne*) — "a legend, a fable".

> این افسانه درباره یک کوه بلند است.
>
> *In afsâne darbâre-ye yek kuh-e boland ast.*
>
> This legend is about a high mountain.

Also "a made-up story": *afsâne ast* "it is a fairy tale". *Afsâne-i* "legendary".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afsâne* is inherited from Middle Persian *afsānag*, "fable" (EtymDB).
