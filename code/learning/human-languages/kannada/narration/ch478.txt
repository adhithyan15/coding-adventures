Kannada, chapter 478: From Eagles to Whales.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹದ್ದು (haddu) — an eagle; a kite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to graze, then the Kannada for a termite.

You'll want to know: ಹದ್ದು.
ಹದ್ದು — haddu — "an eagle; a kite".
ಆಕಾಶದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಹದ್ದು ಸುತ್ತುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. ākāśadalli ondu haddu suttuttittu. An eagle was circling in the sky.
A large bird of prey that circles high up.

The word, taken apart.
ಹದ್ದು (haddu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3977 lists Kannada pardu, paddu, "a kite, a hawk, a vulture", the old p- turned into h-.

ಕೆರೆಯ ದಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಿಳಿ ಕೊಕ್ಕರೆಗಳು ನಿಂತಿದ್ದವು. kereya daḍadalli biḷi kokkaregaḷu nintiddavu. White storks were standing on the bank of the lake.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮಿಂಚುಹುಳು (miñcuhuḷu) — a firefly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an eagle, then the Kannada for a stork.

You'll want to know: ಮಿಂಚುಹುಳು.
ಮಿಂಚುಹುಳು — miñcuhuḷu — "a firefly".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹೊಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಿಂಚುಹುಳುಗಳು ಹೊಳೆಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು. rātri holadalli miñcuhuḷugaḷu hoḷeyuttiddavu. At night fireflies were glowing in the field.
ಮಿಂಚು, lightning, plus ಹುಳು, an insect or worm.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಿಂಚುಹುಳು (miñcuhuḷu) is a native Kannada compound of ಮಿಂಚು, "lightning, a flash" (DEDR 4876), and ಹುಳು, "a worm, an insect".

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

ಏಡಿ — ēḍi — "a crab".
ಕಡಲತೀರದ ಮರಳಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಏಡಿಗಳು ಓಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು. kaḍalatīrada maraḷinalli ēḍigaḷu ōḍuttiddavu. Crabs were running about on the sand of the beach.
Plural: ಏಡಿಗಳು, ēḍigaḷu.

ಏಡಿ (ēḍi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2901 lists Kannada ēḍi, beside esaḍi and naḷḷi, with Tamil ñaṇṭu, "a crab".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತಿಮಿಂಗಿಲ (timiṅgila) — a whale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a firefly, then the Kannada for a crab.

You'll want to know: ತಿಮಿಂಗಿಲ.
ತಿಮಿಂಗಿಲ — timiṅgila — "a whale".
ಸಮುದ್ರದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವು ಒಂದು ತಿಮಿಂಗಿಲವನ್ನು ನೋಡಿದೆವು. samudradalli nāvu ondu timiṅgilavannu nōḍidevu. We saw a whale in the sea.
In Sanskrit stories a huge fish that swallows other fish.

The word, taken apart.
ತಿಮಿಂಗಿಲ (timiṅgila) is borrowed from Sanskrit timiṃgila, "a large fabulous fish" (Monier-Williams), the swallower of the timi fish.
