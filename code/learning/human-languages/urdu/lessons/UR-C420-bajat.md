## You'll want to know: بجٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C420-AAMDANI420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بجٹ** — *bajaṭ* — "a budget".

> ہمارا بجٹ ہماری آمدنی پر منحصر ہے۔
>
> *hamārā bajaṭ hamārī āmdanī par munhasir hai.*
>
> Our budget depends on our income.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بجٹ** (*bajaṭ*) is borrowed from English *budget*.
