# شرط (shart) — a condition

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C274-REAL-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "if she comes tomorrow, we'll go". (*agar voh kal āe gī, to ham jāeṅ ge*.) Her coming is the condition.

## You'll want to know: شرط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C274-SHART-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-EK, UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-04, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

**شرط** — *shart* — "a condition". It is feminine.

> **ایک شرط ہے۔**
>
> *ek shart hai.* — "There's one condition."

> **میں ایک شرط پر آؤں گا۔**
>
> *maiṅ ek shart par āūṅ gā.* — "I'll come on one condition."

> **شرط یہ ہے کہ آپ بھی آئیں گے۔**
>
> *shart yih hai ki āp bhī āeṅ ge.* — "The condition is that you'll come too."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرط** (*shart*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *sharṭ*, "a condition".
