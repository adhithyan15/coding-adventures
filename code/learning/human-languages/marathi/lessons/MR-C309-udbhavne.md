## You'll want to know: उद्भवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C309-MRUTYU309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्भवणे** — *udbhavṇe* — "to arise; to crop up".

> पावसामुळे पाण्याचा नवा प्रश्न उद्भवला.
>
> *pāvsāmuḷe pāṇyācā navā praśna udbhavlā.*
>
> A new water problem arose because of the rain.

Past (he …): तो उद्भवला, *to udbhavlā*.

Intransitive and formal: प्रश्न उद्भवणे, a problem arises.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्भवणे** (*udbhavṇe*) is built on Sanskrit *udbhava*, 'arising, origin' (Monier-Williams), with the Marathi verb ending *-ṇe*.
