## You'll want to know: o oculista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C452-CUIDADOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o oculista** — "the optician".

> O oculista disse que preciso de óculos novos.
>
> *The optician said I need new glasses.*

Also the shop: *ir ao oculista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*oculista* is built on *óculo* 'eyeglass', from Latin *oculus* 'eye' (Lewis & Short).
