## You'll want to know: ひえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C212-TEMP212-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひえる** — *hieru* — "to get cold; to grow chilly".

> **こんやは ひえます。** — *kon'ya wa hiemasu* — "It will get cold tonight."

Polite **ひえます**, *hiemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひえる** (*hieru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
