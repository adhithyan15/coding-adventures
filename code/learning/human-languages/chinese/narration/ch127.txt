Mandarin Chinese, chapter 127: These and Those.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
这些 (zhèxiē) — these.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a moment ago, then the Mandarin for already.

You'll want to know: 这些.
这些 — zhèxiē — "these".
这些是我以前的书 zhèxiē shì wǒ yǐqián de shū — These are my old books.
The new character, in pieces: 些 (xiē) — on top a four-stroke piece and a two-stroke piece side by side, and under them the two bars of 二. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
这些 (zhèxiē) is built from 这 'this' and 些 (xiē) 'a few'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese sjae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a little'.

Script — two small pieces over 二.
些 (xiē).
Three pieces: two side by side on top, then 二 underneath.
The top left piece, four strokes. A short vertical, a short horizontal from its middle to the right, a shorter vertical to the left of the first, then a stroke along the foot that rises to the right.
The top right piece, two strokes. A short left-falling stroke, then a vertical that bends to the right along the bottom and hooks up.
二, two strokes: the short bar, then the long one beneath it.
4 + 2 + 2 = eight.

[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 3 of 6.
那些 (nàxiē) — those.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for already, then the Mandarin for these.

You'll want to know: 那些.
那些 — nàxiē — "those".
那些人是我以前的同学 nàxiē rén shì wǒ yǐqián de tóngxué — Those people are my old classmates.

The word, taken apart.
那些 (nàxiē) is built from 那 'that' and 些 (xiē) 'a few', both inherited from Middle Chinese, na and sjae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'how, what', not 'that', and the second 'a little'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
一些 (yìxiē) — some; a few.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for those, then the Mandarin for which ones.

You'll want to know: 一些.
一些 — yìxiē — "some; a few".
我想喝一些水 wǒ xiǎng hē yìxiē shuǐ — I'd like to drink some water.
The tone of 一 changes here: yī is said yì before the first tone of xiē.

The word, taken apart.
一些 (yìxiē) is built from 一 'one' and 些 (xiē) 'a few', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit and sjae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 6 of 6.
zhèyàng — like this; this way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for which ones, then the Mandarin for a few.

You'll want to know: zhèyàng.
zhèyàng — "like this; this way".
nǐ kěyǐ zhèyàng zuò — You can do it this way.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhèyàng is built from 这 'this' and yàng 'a pattern', the yàng of yíyàng. No dictionary we use records an older form of either part in these senses, so the history of the pair is not settled.
