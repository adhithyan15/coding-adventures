## You'll want to know: der Wanderweg
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C454-WANDERUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Wanderweg** — "the trail, the footpath".

> Der Wanderweg ist mit roten Zeichen markiert.
>
> *The trail is marked with red signs.*

Plural: *die Wanderwege*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wanderweg* is built from *wandern* 'to hike' and *Weg* 'way', inherited from Proto-Germanic *wegaz* (EtymDB).
