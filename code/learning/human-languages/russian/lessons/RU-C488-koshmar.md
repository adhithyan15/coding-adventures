## You'll want to know: кошмар
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C488-NIGHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**кошмар** (*koshmár*) — "a nightmare".

> Ночью мне приснился кошмар.
>
> *Nóchyu mne prisnílsya koshmár.*
>
> I had a nightmare in the night.

Masculine. Colloquially also an exclamation: *Какой кошмар!* — How awful!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *кошмар* without a source; it is usually traced to French *cauchemar* 'nightmare'.
