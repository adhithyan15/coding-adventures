## You'll want to know: подключать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C402-SVYAZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**подключать** (*podklyuchát*) — "to connect, to plug in".

> Мы подключаем интернет в новой квартире.
>
> *My podklyucháyem internét v nóvoy kvartíre.*
>
> We are getting the internet connected in the new flat.

Imperfective; perfective *подключить*. *Подключаться к* + dative — to connect to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**подключать** is built from *под-* and *ключ* "a key", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*ključĭ*: to lock one thing into another.
