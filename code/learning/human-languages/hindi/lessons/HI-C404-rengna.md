## You'll want to know: रेंगना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C404-TRAIFIK404-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रेंगना** — *reṅgnā* — "to crawl".

> जाम में गाड़ियाँ रेंग रही थीं।
>
> *jām meṁ gāṛiyā̃ reṅg rahī thīṁ.*
>
> The cars were crawling along in the jam.

Past (it …): वह साँप घास में रेंगा, *vah sā̃p ghās meṁ reṅgā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेंगना** (*reṅgnā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *riṅgati*, "crawls" (CDIAL 10739), which lists Hindi *rẽgnā*. A baby, a snake and a jammed lane of traffic all crawl.
