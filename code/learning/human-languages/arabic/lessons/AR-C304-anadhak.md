# آنذاك (ānadhāk) — at that time, back then

## You'll want to know: آنذاك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C304-ZAMAN304-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آنذاك** — *ānadhāk* — "at that time, back then".

> كان أبي آنذاك طالبا في الجامعة.
>
> *kāna abī ānadhāk ṭāliban fī al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> My father was a university student at that time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آنذاك** (*ānadhāk*) is usually traced to *ān*, "a time", the same word as in the taught الآن, now, joined to *dhāka*, "that": "at that time".
