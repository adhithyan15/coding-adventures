## You'll want to know: क्रांती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C334-KRANTI334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रांती** — *krāntī* — "a revolution".

> नव्या बियाण्यांमुळे शेतीत मोठी क्रांती झाली.
>
> *navyā biyāṇyāmmuḷe śetīt moṭhī krāntī jhālī.*
>
> New seeds brought about a revolution in farming.

Feminine.

क्रांती होणे, a revolution happens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रांती** (*krāntī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *krānti*, 'a step, a going; an overcoming' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a revolution' is modern.

One more word for telling what happened.
