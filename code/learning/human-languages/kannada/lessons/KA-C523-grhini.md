# ಗೃಹಿಣಿ (gṛhiṇi) — a homemaker; a housewife

## You'll want to know: ಗೃಹಿಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೃಹಿಣಿ** — *gṛhiṇi* — "a homemaker; a housewife".

> ನನ್ನ ಅಮ್ಮ ಗೃಹಿಣಿ, ಮನೆಯ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಕೆಲಸ ನೋಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *nanna amma gṛhiṇi, maneya ellā kelasa nōḍikoḷḷuttāre.*
>
> My mother is a homemaker; she looks after all the work of the house.

ಗೃಹ, a house + -ಇಣಿ, the woman of it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೃಹಿಣಿ** (*gṛhiṇi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gṛhiṇī*, "the mistress of a house, a wife" (Monier-Williams).
