## You'll want to know: బహుశా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C87-ANSWER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**బహుశా** (*bahuśā*) — "probably, perhaps".

**బహుశా రేపు** — "probably tomorrow". Compare **ఏమో**: *ēmō* shrugs, "who knows", while
*bahuśā* makes a guess.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A guess, next to the shrug you already had.
