## You'll want to know: ಟರ್ಮಿನಲ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C437-VIMANA437-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟರ್ಮಿನಲ್** — *ṭarminal* — "a terminal".

> ನಮ್ಮ ವಿಮಾನ ಎರಡನೇ ಟರ್ಮಿನಲ್ನಿಂದ ಹೊರಡುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *namma vimāna eraḍanē ṭarminalninda horaḍuttade.*
>
> Our flight leaves from the second terminal.

An airport building, or the last stop of a bus line.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟರ್ಮಿನಲ್** (*ṭarminal*) is a loan from English *terminal*, the end point of a route.
