Spanish, chapter 479: Papeles y plazos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
inscribirse — to sign up, to enrol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the space, then the Spanish for available.

You'll want to know: inscribirse.
inscribirse — "to sign up, to enrol".
Me inscribo en el curso de cocina. I am signing up for the cooking class.
La inscripción is the registration.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
