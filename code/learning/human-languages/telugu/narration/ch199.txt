Telugu, chapter 199: To pound, To fill, To put out to dry, To fold, To arrange.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
దంచు (dañcu) — to pound (in a mortar).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a uniform, then the Telugu for staff.

You'll want to know: దంచు.
దంచు — dañcu — "to pound (in a mortar)".

The word, taken apart.
దంచు (dañcu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 77 lists dañcu, "to pound, clean grain in a mortar", with Tamil aṭu, "to pound (as rice)".

You'll want to know: నింపు.
నింపు — nimpu — "to fill".

The word, taken apart.
నింపు (nimpu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3682 lists nimpu, "to fill", with Tamil niṟai, "to fill", and notes that in Telugu this root has run together with another, so that the two cannot be told apart.

The word, taken apart.
ఆరబెట్టు (ārabeṭṭu) is native Dravidian: āru, "to dry, cool", one word with Tamil āṟu, "to cool" (DEDR 404), and peṭṭu, "to put" (DEDR 4390).
