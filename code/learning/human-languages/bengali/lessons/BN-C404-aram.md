## You'll want to know: আরাম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C404-ARAM404-01]; assesses=[] -->

**আরাম** — *ārām* — "comfort; ease; rest".

> লম্বা সফরের পরে বাড়িতে আরাম লাগল।
>
> *lômbā sôphôrer pôre bāṛite ārām lāglô.*
>
> After the long trip it felt good to be home.

আরাম লাগা, to feel comfortable; আরামদায়ক, comfortable, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আরাম** (*ārām*) is usually traced to Persian *ārām*, "rest, quiet, ease".
