## You'll want to know: a prancha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C506-AGUA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a prancha** — "board (for surfing); plank".

> Levou a prancha para a praia.
>
> *He took his board to the beach.*

*Prancha de surf* — surfboard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prancha* comes from French *planche* "a board" (EtymDB), from Late Latin *planca* "a plank".
