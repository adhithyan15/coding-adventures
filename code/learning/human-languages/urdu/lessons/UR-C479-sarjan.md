## You'll want to know: سرجن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C479-WARD479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سرجن** — *sarjan* — "a surgeon".

> سرجن نے دو گھنٹے آپریشن کیا۔
>
> *sarjan ne do ghanṭe āpreshan kiyā.*
>
> The surgeon operated for two hours.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرجن** (*sarjan*) is borrowed from English *surgeon*.
