## You'll want to know: o depoimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C297-TRIBUNAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o depoimento** — "the statement (to police or court), the deposition".

> O depoimento da vítima foi muito importante.
>
> *The victim’s statement was very important.*

*Prestar depoimento* — to give a statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*depoimento* is built on *depor* "to testify, to put down", from Latin *dēpōnere* "to put down", built on *pōnere* "to place" (Lewis & Short).
