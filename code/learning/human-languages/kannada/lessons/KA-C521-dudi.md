# ದುಡಿ (duḍi) — to toil; to labour

## You'll want to know: ದುಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C521-DUDIME521-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ದುಡಿ** — *duḍi* — "to toil; to labour".

> ಕುಟುಂಬಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಅಪ್ಪ ಹಗಲು ರಾತ್ರಿ ದುಡಿದರು.
>
> *kuṭumbakkāgi appa hagalu rātri duḍidaru.*
>
> Father toiled day and night for the family.

Past (I …): ದುಡಿದೆ, *duḍide*.

ದುಡಿಮೆ is the work and what it earns.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದುಡಿ** (*duḍi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3295 lists Kannada *duḍi*, "to labour, acquire by one's labour", and *duḍime*, "gain".
