## You'll want to know: خوش اخلاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C488-CHARACTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خوش اخلاق** (*khosh akhlâq*) — "good-natured, pleasant".

> همسایه جدید ما خیلی خوش اخلاق است.
>
> *hamsâye-ye jadid-e mâ kheyli khosh akhlâq ast.*
>
> Our new neighbour is very pleasant.

*khosh* 'good' + *akhlâq* 'manners, character'. The opposite is *badakhlâq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khosh akhlâq* is built in Persian on native *khosh*, 'good', and *akhlâq*, 'character, manners', which was borrowed from Arabic *aḫlāq*.
