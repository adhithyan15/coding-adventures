## You'll want to know: രാജ്ഞി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C397-RAJAVU397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**രാജ്ഞി** — *rājñi* — "a queen".

> രാജാവും രാജ്ഞിയും ഗ്രാമം സന്ദർശിച്ചു.
>
> *rājāvuṁ rājñiyuṁ grāmaṁ sandarśiccu.*
>
> The king and the queen visited the village.

The queen who rules in her own right, or the king's wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**രാജ്ഞി** (*rājñi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rājñī*, "a queen" (CDIAL 10692), the feminine of *rājan*.
