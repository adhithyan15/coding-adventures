## You'll want to know: jǐngqū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C430-SIGHTS430-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jǐngqū* — "scenic area".

> *zhège jǐngqū zǎoshang bā diǎn kāimén* — This scenic area opens at eight in the morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jǐngqū* is built from *jǐng* 'a view' and *qū* 'an area', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjaengX* and *khju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'bright' and 'great' and gives the second as the name of a marsh; the senses 'a view' and 'an area' are not given there.
