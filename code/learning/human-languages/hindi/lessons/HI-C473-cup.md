## You'll want to know: चुप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C473-BATUNI473-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चुप** — *cup* — "silent; quiet".

> माँ ने डाँटा तो बच्चा चुप हो गया।
>
> *māṁ ne ḍā̃ṭā to baccā cup ho gayā.*
>
> When his mother scolded him, the child went quiet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुप** (*cup*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *cup*, "silent", under *\*cuppa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4864). *cup raho!* is "be quiet!".
