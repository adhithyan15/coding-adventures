## You'll want to know: hǎopíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C452-AFTERMEAL452-02]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎopíng* — "good review; favourable comment".

> *zhè jiā fànguǎn yǒu hěn duō hǎopíng* — This restaurant has lots of good reviews.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎopíng* is built from 好 'good' and *píng* 'to judge', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xawX* and *bjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to weigh up'.
