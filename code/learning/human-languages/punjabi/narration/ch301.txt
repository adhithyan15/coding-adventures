Punjabi, chapter 301: Ploughing the Field.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਹਲ (hal) — a plough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for raw cotton, then the Punjabi for sugar cane.

You'll want to know: ਹਲ.
ਹਲ — hal — "a plough".
ਸਾਡਾ ਹਲ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਹੈ। sāḍā hal purāṇā hai. Our plough is old.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਹਲ (hal) is inherited from Sanskrit hala, "plough"; CDIAL lists Punjabi hal under it (CDIAL 14000).
