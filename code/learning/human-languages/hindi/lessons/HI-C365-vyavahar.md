## You'll want to know: व्यवहार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C365-SIDDHANT365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवहार** — *vyavahār* — "conduct, behaviour; practice".

> सिद्धांत अच्छा है, लेकिन व्यवहार में यह काम नहीं करता।
>
> *siddhānt acchā hai, lekin vyavahār meṁ yah kām nahīṃ kartā.*
>
> The theory is good, but in practice it doesn't work.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवहार** (*vyavahār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vyavahāra*, "doing, practice, conduct" (Monier-Williams).
