## You'll want to know: il fruttivendolo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C454-ALIMENTARI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il fruttivendolo** — "the greengrocer".

> Il fruttivendolo ha le fragole buone.
>
> *The greengrocer has good strawberries.*

The person and the shop: *vado dal fruttivendolo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fruttivendolo* is built from *frutta* 'fruit', inherited from Late Latin *fructa*, and *-vendolo*, from *vendere* 'to sell', inherited from Latin *vēndere* (EtymDB).
