Italian, chapter 335: Mani in pasta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
impastare — to knead, to mix (a dough).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to deglaze, then the Italian for the soffritto.

You'll want to know: impastare.
impastare — "to knead, to mix (a dough)".
Ho impastato la farina con l'acqua. I kneaded the flour with the water.
Built on pasta, ‘dough’.

The word, taken apart.
impastare is built on pasta "dough, paste", from Late Latin pasta "paste" (Lewis & Short), from Greek pástē.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: il lievito.
il lievito — "the yeast, the raising agent".
Aggiungi il lievito e un po' d'acqua. Add the yeast and a little water.
Il lievito di birra — fresh yeast.

The word, taken apart.
lievito goes back to Latin levātum, from levāre "to raise" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
lievitare — to rise (of dough).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the dough, then the Italian for the yeast.

You'll want to know: lievitare.
lievitare — "to rise (of dough)".
Lascia lievitare l'impasto per due ore. Let the dough rise for two hours.
With essere: è lievitato. Prices lievitano too.

The word, taken apart.
lievitare is built on lievito "yeast" (EtymDB), from Latin levāre "to raise".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

il mattarello — "the rolling pin".
Con il mattarello la pasta diventa sottile. With the rolling pin the pastry gets thin.
Also written matterello.

The word, taken apart.
mattarello is usually traced to Latin mateola "a mallet" (Lewis & Short), but the origin is not settled.
