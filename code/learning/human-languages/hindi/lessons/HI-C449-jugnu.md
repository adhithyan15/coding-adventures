## You'll want to know: जुगनू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C449-SIYAR449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जुगनू** — *jugnū* — "a firefly".

> रात को खेतों में जुगनू दिखते हैं।
>
> *rāt ko khetoṁ meṁ jugnū dikhte haiṁ.*
>
> You can see fireflies in the fields at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जुगनू** (*jugnū*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *jyotiriṅgaṇa*, "a firefly", literally a moving light (CDIAL 5298), which lists Hindi *jugnū*.
