## You'll want to know: اتفاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C361-IMKAN361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اتفاق** — *ittifāq* — "chance; a coincidence".

> اگر اتفاق سے وہ مل گیا تو میں بتاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar ittifāq se voh mil gayā to maiṅ batāūṅ gā.*
>
> If I happen to meet him, I will tell him.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اتفاق** (*ittifāq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ittifāq*, "a coming together, agreement": things that happen to come together.
