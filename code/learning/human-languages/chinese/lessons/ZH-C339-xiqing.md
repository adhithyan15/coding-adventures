## You'll want to know: 喜庆
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C339-NATION339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**喜庆** — *xǐqìng* — "festive; joyous".

> **过年的时候家里很喜庆** *guònián de shíhou jiā lǐ hěn xǐqìng* — At the new year the house is full of festive joy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**喜庆** (*xǐqìng*) is built from 喜 'joy' and 庆 'good fortune', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xiX* and *khjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'joy' and the second 'good fortune'.
