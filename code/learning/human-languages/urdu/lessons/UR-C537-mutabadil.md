## You'll want to know: متبادل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C537-MUTABADIL537-01]; assesses=[] -->

**متبادل** — *mutabādil* — "an alternative".

> اگر بس نہیں آئی تو رکشہ ایک متبادل ہے۔
>
> *agar bas nahīṅ āī to rikshā ek mutabādil hai.*
>
> If the bus doesn't come, a rickshaw is another option.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متبادل** (*mutabādil*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mutabādil*, "exchanged, interchangeable".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
