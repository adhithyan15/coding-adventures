## You'll want to know: वार्धक्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C498-VAY498-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्धक्य** — *vārdhakya* — "old age".

> वार्धक्यात आजोबा गावी शांतपणे राहतात.
>
> *vārdhakyāt ājobā gāvī śāntpaṇe rāhtāt.*
>
> In his old age Grandfather lives quietly in the village.

Neuter.

Formal, beside म्हातारपण.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्धक्य** (*vārdhakya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vārddhakya*, "old age" (Monier-Williams).
