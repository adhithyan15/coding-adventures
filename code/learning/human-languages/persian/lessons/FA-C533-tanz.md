## You'll want to know: طنز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C533-MEDIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**طنز** (*tanz*) — "satire, humour".

> این نویسنده داستانهای طنز مینویسد.
>
> *in nevisande dâstânhâ-ye tanz minevisad.*
>
> This writer writes humorous stories.

*tanz* — satire, comic writing. *tanznevis* — a humorist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tanz* comes from Arabic *ṭanz* (EtymDB), a word for mockery; Persian made it the name of satire.
