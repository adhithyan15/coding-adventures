## You'll want to know: a manifestação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C364-PROTESTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a manifestação** — "the demonstration, the march; the expression (of a feeling)".

> Milhares de pessoas foram à manifestação.
>
> *Thousands of people went to the demonstration.*

In speech *a manif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manifestação* is a learned borrowing from Latin *manifēstātiō* "a manifesting, a manifestation" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *manifēstāre* "to show", on *manifēstus* "clear, evident".
