Lesson 1 of 6.
tígāo — to push up (a level).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for natural resources, then the Mandarin for to increase (in number).

You'll want to know: tígāo.
tígāo — "to push up (a level)".
zhè jiā fàndiàn tígāo le jiàqián — This restaurant has put its prices up.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tígāo is built from tí 'to carry by the handle' and 高 'high', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dej and kaw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to carry in the hand'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
八成 (bāchéng) — most likely; eighty per cent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to increase (in number), then the Mandarin for to push up (a level).

You'll want to know: 八成.
八成 — bāchéng — "most likely; eighty per cent".
他八成病了所以没来 tā bāchéng bìng le suǒyǐ méi lái — He's most likely ill, which is why he didn't come.

The word, taken apart.
八成 (bāchéng) is built from 八 'eight' and 成 'a tenth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, peat and dzyeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'to complete'; 成 for one part in ten is later and is not given there.

Lesson 3 of 6.
节能 (jiénéng) — to save energy; energy-saving.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to push up (a level), then the Mandarin for most likely (eight in ten).

You'll want to know: 节能.
节能 — jiénéng — "to save energy; energy-saving".
我觉得坐火车比开车节能 wǒ juéde zuò huǒchē bǐ kāichē jiénéng — I think taking the train saves more energy than driving.

The word, taken apart.
节能 (jiénéng) is built from 节 'to restrain' and 能 'energy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tset and nong (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the joint of a bamboo' and 'to hold back'. The word is modern.

Lesson 4 of 6.
zhòngshì — to attach importance to.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for most likely (eight in ten), then the Mandarin for to save energy.

You'll want to know: zhòngshì.
zhòngshì — "to attach importance to".
zhèngfǔ yīnggāi zhòngshì huánjìng — The government should take the environment seriously.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhòngshì is built from 重 'heavy' and shì 'to look at', both inherited from Middle Chinese, drjowngX and dzyijH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'thick' and 'careful' and the second 'to look'.
