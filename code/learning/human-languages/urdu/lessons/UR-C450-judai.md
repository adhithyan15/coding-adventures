# جدائی (judāī) — separation (from someone dear)

## You'll want to know: جدائی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C450-MISS450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جدائی** — *judāī* — "separation (from someone dear)".

> بہن کی جدائی میں وہ روتی ہے۔
>
> *bahan kī judāī meṅ voh rotī hai.*
>
> She cries because her sister is far away.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جدائی** (*judāī*) is usually traced to Persian *judāī*, "separation", built on *judā*, "apart".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
