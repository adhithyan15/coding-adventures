Hindi, chapter 538: Getting Things Ready.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सँजोना (sãjonā) — to treasure; to keep with care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an auspicious moment (to begin something), then the Hindi for to shift.

You'll want to know: सँजोना.
सँजोना — sãjonā — "to treasure; to keep with care".
दादी ने पुरानी तस्वीरें एक डिब्बे में सँजोकर रखी हैं। Dādī ne purānī tasvīreṁ ek ḍibbe meṁ sãjokar rakhī haiṁ. Grandmother has kept the old photographs carefully in a box.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पुरानी चिट्ठियाँ सँजोईं, usne purānī ciṭṭhiyā̃ sãjoīṁ.

The word, taken apart.
सँजोना (sãjonā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit saṁyōjayati, "brings together" (CDIAL 12990). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to decorate, make ready"; keeping something with care grew from that.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तैयार (taiyār) — ready; prepared.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to shift, then the Hindi for to treasure.

You'll want to know: तैयार.
तैयार — taiyār — "ready; prepared".
खाना तैयार है, सब लोग आइए। Khānā taiyār hai, sab log āie. The food is ready; come along, everyone.

The word, taken apart.
तैयार (taiyār) is usually traced to Arabic ṭayyār, "swift, flying", through Persian, where it came to mean "ready".

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रबंध (prabandh) — management; arrangements.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to treasure, then the Hindi for ready.

You'll want to know: प्रबंध.
प्रबंध — prabandh — "management; arrangements".
दावत का प्रबंध मेरी चाची ने किया। Dāvat kā prabandh merī cācī ne kiyā. My aunt saw to the arrangements for the feast.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रबंध (prabandh) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit prabandha, "a connection, a tie" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for managing and arranging.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सजना (sajnā) — to be decorated; to be dressed up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for ready, then the Hindi for management.

You'll want to know: सजना.
सजना — sajnā — "to be decorated; to be dressed up".
शादी के लिए पूरा घर सज रहा है। Śādī ke liye pūrā ghar saj rahā hai. The whole house is being decorated for the wedding.
Past (it …): वह कमरा सजा, vah kamrā sajā.

The word, taken apart.
सजना (sajnā) is probably related to Hindi sājnā, "to be prepared", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit sajjyatē, "is equipped, is prepared" (CDIAL 13093); CDIAL does not list sajnā itself; it is the intransitive partner of sajānā, "to decorate".

Lesson 5 of 5.
बीनना (bīnnā) — to pick out, to pick over (grain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for management, then the Hindi for to be decorated.

You'll want to know: बीनना.
बीनना — bīnnā — "to pick out, to pick over (grain)".
माँ ने दाल बीनी। Māṁ ne dāl bīnī. Mother picked over the lentils.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चावल बीने, usne cāval bīne.

The word, taken apart.
बीनना (bīnnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit vicinōti, "picks out, chooses" (CDIAL 11686). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to pick up, choose".
