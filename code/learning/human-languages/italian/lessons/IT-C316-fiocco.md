## You'll want to know: il fiocco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C316-CANDELINE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il fiocco** — "the bow (ribbon); the flake".

> Sul pacco c'è un fiocco rosso.
>
> *There is a red bow on the parcel.*

*Fiocchi di neve* — snowflakes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fiocco* is inherited from Latin *floccus* "a tuft of wool" (EtymDB).
