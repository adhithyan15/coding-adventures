## You'll want to know: mēnrè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C218-WIND218-03]; assesses=[] -->

*mēnrè* — "hot and stuffy; muggy".

> *jīntiān tài mēnrè le* — It's so muggy today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mēnrè* is built from *mēn* 'stifled' and *rè* 'hot', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mwonH* and *nyet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'vexed' and explains the second as burning, as if by fire. The first is listed with a departing tone, so the level tone of mēn is not the regular outcome.
