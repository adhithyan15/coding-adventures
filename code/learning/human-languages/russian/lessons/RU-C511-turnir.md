## You'll want to know: турнир
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C511-CHAMPIONS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**турнир** (*turnír*) — "a tournament".

> В турнире участвуют восемь команд.
>
> *V turníre uchástvuyut vósem kománd.*
>
> Eight teams are taking part in the tournament.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *турнир* without a source; it is usually traced through German *Turnier* to Old French *tornei*.
