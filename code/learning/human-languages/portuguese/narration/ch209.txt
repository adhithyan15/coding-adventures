Portuguese, chapter 209: To disappear, To record, To keep, To exist, To express.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
desaparecer — to disappear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to ski, then the Portuguese for to slip.

You'll want to know: desaparecer.
desaparecer — "to disappear".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: desaparecer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: desaparecer, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say desaparecer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for to ski, then the Portuguese for to slip, then say desaparecer again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does desaparecer mean? ("To disappear".) Say it once more.
