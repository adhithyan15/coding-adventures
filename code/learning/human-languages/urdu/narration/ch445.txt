Urdu, chapter 445: Fear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خوف (khauf) — dread; fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for displeased, then the Urdu for a fit of rage.

You'll want to know: خوف.
خوف — khauf — "dread; fear".
جنگل میں رات کو خوف لگتا ہے۔ jangal meṅ rāt ko khauf lagtā hai. In the forest at night you feel a sense of dread.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
خوف (khauf) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khawf, "fear".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ڈرانا (ḍarānā) — to frighten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fit of rage, then the Urdu for dread.

You'll want to know: ڈرانا.
ڈرانا — ḍarānā — "to frighten".
بلی نے چوہے کو ڈرایا۔ billī ne chūhe ko ḍarāyā. The cat frightened the mouse.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچوں کو ڈرایا, us ne bachchoṅ ko ḍarāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ڈرانا (ḍarānā) is the causative of ḍarnā, "to be afraid", inherited from Sanskrit darati, "is frightened" (CDIAL 6190); the noun ڈر, "fear", belongs to the same word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سہمنا (sahamnā) — to cower (in fright).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for dread, then the Urdu for to frighten.

You'll want to know: سہمنا.
سہمنا — sahamnā — "to cower (in fright)".
کتے کو دیکھ کر لڑکی سہم گئی۔ kutte ko dekh kar laṛkī saham gaī. The girl cowered when she saw the dog.
Past (she …): وہ سہم گئی, voh saham gaī.

The word, taken apart.
سہمنا (sahamnā) is usually traced to Persian sahm, "fear, dread", made into an Urdu verb with -nā.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دبکنا (dubaknā) — to crouch out of sight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to frighten, then the Urdu for to cower.

You'll want to know: دبکنا.
دبکنا — dubaknā — "to crouch out of sight".
بلی ڈر کر کرسی کے نیچے دبک گئی۔ billī ḍar kar kursī ke nīche dubak gaī. The cat took fright and crouched under the chair.
Past (he …): وہ دبک گیا, voh dubak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
دبکنا (dubaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin dabaknā, "to be hidden", under dabb, "press", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6173).

Lesson 5 of 5.
سمٹنا (simaṭnā) — to huddle; to shrink into oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to cower, then the Urdu for to crouch out of sight.

You'll want to know: سمٹنا.
سمٹنا — simaṭnā — "to huddle; to shrink into oneself".
سردی میں بچے کمبل میں سمٹ گئے۔ sardī meṅ bachche kambal meṅ simaṭ gae. In the cold the children huddled under the blanket.
Past (she …): وہ کونے میں سمٹ گئی, voh kone meṅ simaṭ gaī.

The word, taken apart.
سمٹنا (simaṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin simaṭnā, "to shrink, to be gathered in", under Sanskrit saṃvēṣṭayati, "wraps up" (CDIAL 13026).
