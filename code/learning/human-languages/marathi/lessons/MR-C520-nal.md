## You'll want to know: नळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C520-NAL520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नळ** — *naḷ* — "a faucet; a water pipe".

> आज सकाळपासून नळाला पाणी नाही.
>
> *āj sakāḷpāsūn naḷālā pāṇī nāhī.*
>
> There has been no water in the tap since this morning.

Masculine.

The tap takes -ला: नळाला पाणी येतं, water comes in the tap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नळ** (*naḷ*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *naḷ*, "lotus stalk; pipe, conduit", under Sanskrit *naḍa*, "a reed" (CDIAL 6936): a hollow stalk and a pipe share one shape.
