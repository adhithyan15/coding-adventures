## You'll want to know: vesperī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C295-NOCTU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vesperī** — "in the evening".

> Vesperī omnēs ad cēnam vēnērunt.
>
> *In the evening everyone came to dinner.*

An old locative of *vesper* (taught): at evening. *Herī vesperī*: yesterday evening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vesperī** is an old locative of *vesper*, "evening" (Lewis & Short), used as an adverb.
