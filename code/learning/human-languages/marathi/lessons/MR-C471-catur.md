## You'll want to know: चतुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C471-SAHANA471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चतुर** — *catur* — "shrewd; clever".

> चतुर कोल्ह्याने कावळ्याला फसवलं.
>
> *catur kolhyāne kāvḷyālā phasavlaṁ.*
>
> The clever fox tricked the crow.

Unchanging; चतुराई, shrewdness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चतुर** (*catur*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *catura*, "quick, clever" (CDIAL 4594).
