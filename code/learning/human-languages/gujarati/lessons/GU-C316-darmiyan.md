## You'll want to know: દરમિયાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C316-MUJAB316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**દરમિયાન** — *darmiyān* — "during".

> વરસાદ દરમિયાન વીજળી ગઈ.
>
> *varsād darmiyān vījḷī gaī.*
>
> The power went off during the rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દરમિયાન** (*darmiyān*) is usually traced to Persian *darmiyān*, "in the middle, between". It follows its noun: **વરસાદ દરમિયાન**, during the rain.
