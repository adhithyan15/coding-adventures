## You'll want to know: desmaiar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C308-SUSTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**desmaiar** — "to faint".

> Com o calor, uma senhora desmaiou no autocarro.
>
> *In the heat, a lady fainted on the bus.*

*O desmaio* — the faint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desmaiar* came from Old French *desmaier* (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *exmagāre* "to rob of strength".
