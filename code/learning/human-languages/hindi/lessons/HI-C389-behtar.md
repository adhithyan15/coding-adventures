## You'll want to know: बेहतर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C389-BEHTAR389-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहतर** — *behtar* — "better".

> अगर तुम आराम करोगे तो कल तबीयत बेहतर होगी।
>
> *agar tum ārām karoge to kal tabīyat behtar hogī.*
>
> If you rest, you'll feel better tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहतर** (*behtar*) is usually traced to Persian *behtar*, "better".
