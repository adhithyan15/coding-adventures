## You'll want to know: ഗവർണർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C421-BHARANAM421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗവർണർ** — *gavarṇar* — "a governor".

> ഗവർണർ ഇന്നലെ നഗരത്തിൽ എത്തി.
>
> *gavarṇar innale nagarattil etti.*
>
> The governor reached the city yesterday.

Plural: ഗവർണർമാർ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗവർണർ** (*gavarṇar*) was borrowed from English *governor*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
