## You'll want to know: گروه کر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C520-MUSICIANS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گروه کر** (*goruh-e kor*) — "a choir".

> دخترم در گروه کر مدرسه آواز میخواند.
>
> *dokhtaram dar goruh-e kor-e madrese âvâz mikhânad.*
>
> My daughter sings in the school choir.

*goruh* 'group' + *kor* 'choir'. Not *kar* 'deaf', spelled the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*goruh-e kor* joins inherited *goruh*, 'group', which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for 'group, crowd' (EtymDB), and *kor*, a modern loan usually traced to French *chœur*, 'choir'. EtymDB's entry for this spelling is the other word, *kar*, 'deaf'.
