## You'll want to know: दम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C483-TAQAT483-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दम** — *dam* — "stamina; breath".

> दादा में अभी भी बहुत दम है।
>
> *dādā meṁ abhī bhī bahut dam hai.*
>
> Grandfather still has plenty of stamina.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दम** (*dam*) is usually traced to Persian *dam*, "breath", and so the strength to keep going. *dam lenā* is to catch one's breath.
