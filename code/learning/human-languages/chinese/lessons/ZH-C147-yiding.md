## You'll want to know: yídìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C147-YIDING-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yídìng* — "certainly; definitely".

> *tā yídìng huì lái* — He'll definitely come.

一 rises before the fall of dìng: *yídìng*, as in *yí ge*.

Its second character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yídìng* is built from 一 'one' and dìng 'settled'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *dengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), *dengH* glossed 'calm; settled'. 'Certainly' is the pair's own sense, not the gloss's.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C147-KENENG-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-HUI-FUTURE-01] -->

*kěnéng* and *yídìng* take the same place, before 会 or the verb: a chance, then a certainty.

- *tā kěnéng huì lái* — he may come.
- *tā yídìng huì lái* — he will, for sure.
