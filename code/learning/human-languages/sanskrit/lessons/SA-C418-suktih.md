## You'll want to know: शुक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C418-JALA418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शुक्तिः** — *śuktiḥ* — "an oyster shell".

> समुद्रस्य तीरे एका शुक्तिः अस्ति।
>
> *samudrasya tīre ekā śuktiḥ asti.*
>
> There is a shell on the seashore.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शुक्तिः** (*śuktiḥ*) probably comes from the root √śuc, "to shine", as MW suggests: MW gives *śukti* as a pearl-oyster or an oyster shell (MW).
