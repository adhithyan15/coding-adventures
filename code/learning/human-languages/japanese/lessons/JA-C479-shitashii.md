## You'll want to know: したしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C479-BOND479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**したしい** — *shitashii* — "close to someone; friendly".

> **あには どうりょうと したしいです。** — *ani wa dōryō to shitashii desu* — "My elder brother is close to his colleagues."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**したしい** (*shitashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
