# 看 — a hand above an eye

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KAN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZHONG-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **look or watch** as *kàn*. Then write the familiar **中** once
and put it away before meeting the new shape.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

Write **看** in nine strokes. Make the short top stroke first, then the two
crossing strokes and the long left fall above. Finish with **目** below: left,
top-and-right, two inner bars, bottom. Nine strokes; eight lifts.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **看** once. Say **hand above, eye below** while keeping the lower box
wide enough for its two inner bars.
