## You'll want to know: 晚饭
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-04]; assesses=[] -->

**晚饭** — *wǎnfàn* — "dinner; supper".

> **今天晚饭吃什么** *jīntiān wǎnfàn chī shénme* — What's for dinner tonight?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**晚饭** (*wǎnfàn*) is built from wǎn 'evening' and 饭 'meal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjonX* and *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
