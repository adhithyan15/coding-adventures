## You'll want to know: diàntái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C290-NEWS290-03]; assesses=[] -->

*diàntái* — "radio station".

> *wǒ bàba zài diàntái gōngzuò guo* — My dad once worked at a radio station.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diàntái* is built from 电 'electricity' and *tái* 'a platform', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *doj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as lightning and the second 'a high, square mound of earth'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
