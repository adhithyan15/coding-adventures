## You'll want to know: અવધિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C530-TAKID530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અવધિ** — *avdhi* — "a time limit".

> જો તમે આ અવધિમાં અરજી નહીં કરો તો તમે આ તક ગુમાવશો.
>
> *jo tame ā avdhimā̃ arjī nahī̃ karo to tame ā tak gumāvśo.*
>
> If you don't apply within this time limit, you will lose this chance.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવધિ** (*avdhi*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *avadhi*, "a term, a limit" (Monier-Williams).
