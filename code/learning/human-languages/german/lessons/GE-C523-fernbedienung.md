## You'll want to know: die Fernbedienung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C523-FERNSEHEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Fernbedienung** — "remote control".

> Die Fernbedienung liegt unter dem Sofa.
>
> *The remote control is under the sofa.*

Plural: *die Fernbedienungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fernbedienung* is built on *fern* "far", from Old High German *ferrana* (EtymDB), and *Bedienung* "operation, service", from *bedienen*.
