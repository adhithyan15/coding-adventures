## You'll want to know: આશંકા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C369-BHAY369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આશંકા** — *āśankā* — "a misgiving".

> મને આશંકા છે કે જો આપણે મોડા પડીશું તો ટ્રેન છૂટી જશે.
>
> *mane āśankā che ke jo āpaṇe moḍā paḍīśũ to ṭren chūṭī jaśe.*
>
> I am afraid that if we are late we will miss the train.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આશંકા** (*āśankā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āśaṅkā*, "fear, apprehension" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
