## You'll want to know: ゆれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C214-STORM214-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆれる** — *yureru* — "to shake; to sway".

> **かぜで きが ゆれます。** — *kaze de ki ga yuremasu* — "The trees sway in the wind."

Polite **ゆれます**, *yuremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆれる** (*yureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
