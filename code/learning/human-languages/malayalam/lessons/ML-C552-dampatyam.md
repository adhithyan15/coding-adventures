# ദാമ്പത്യം (dāmpatyaṁ) — married life

## You'll want to know: ദാമ്പത്യം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C552-BANDHAM552-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ദാമ്പത്യം** — *dāmpatyaṁ* — "married life".

> മുത്തച്ഛന്റെയും മുത്തശ്ശിയുടെയും ദാമ്പത്യം അമ്പത് വർഷം നീണ്ടു.
>
> *muttacchanṟeyuṁ muttaśśiyuṭeyuṁ dāmpatyaṁ ampatŭ varṣaṁ nīṇṭu.*
>
> My grandparents' married life lasted fifty years.

ദമ്പതികൾ, a married couple, is already familiar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ദാമ്പത്യം** (*dāmpatyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dāmpatya*, "the state of husband and wife" (Monier-Williams), the noun of *dampati*, "a married couple".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
