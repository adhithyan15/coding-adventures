## You'll want to know: il parabrezza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C407-VETRI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il parabrezza** — "the windscreen".

> Un sasso ha rotto il parabrezza.
>
> *A stone cracked the windscreen.*

Built like *parasole*: *para-* means it shields you from something. Plural unchanged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parabrezza* is built from *parare* 'to ward off', which is inherited from Latin *parāre* 'to prepare' (Lewis & Short), and *brezza* 'breeze': the glass that holds back the wind.
