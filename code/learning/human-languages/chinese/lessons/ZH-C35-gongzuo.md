## You'll want to know: gōngzuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C35-WORK35-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngzuò* — "to work; job".

> *wǒ jiějie gōngzuò, wǒ shàngxué* — My sister works; I go to school.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngzuò* is built from gōng 'craft' and zuò 'to do', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *tsak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'office' and 'skill', the second 'to do'.
