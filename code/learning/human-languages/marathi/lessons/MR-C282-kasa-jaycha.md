# स्टेशनला कसं जायचं? (sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ?) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-QUESTION-KUTHE, MR-LEX-C113-TOWN113-04, MR-GRAMMAR-C278-HABITUAL-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "where is the station?" (*sṭeśan kuṭhe āhe?*) Then "I used to go". (*mī jāyco*.)

## You'll want to know: … ला कसं जायचं?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01]; assesses=[MR-PHRASE-KRUPAYA, MR-LEX-C271-VASTU271-02, MR-LEX-C128-LOC128-04, MR-GRAMMAR-QUESTION-KAA] -->

> **कृपया, स्टेशनला कसं जायचं?**
>
> *kṛpayā, sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ?* — "Please, how do I get to the station?"

**जायचं** is the **-ायच-** of **जायचो** with **-ं**: "how is it to be gone". Put **-ला** on the place: **बसस्थानकाला** (*basasthānakālā*), **विमानतळाला** (*vimāntaḷālā*). To ask for a place by name, the question you know stays the best: **बसस्थानक कुठे आहे?**

## You'll want to know: how far
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C282-HOW-FAR-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-LAAMB, MR-POSTP-JAVAL, MR-QUESTION-KITI] -->

> **इथून किती लांब आहे?**
>
> *ithūn kitī lāmb āhe?* — "How far is it from here?"

**इथून** (*ithūn*, "from here") is **इथे** with **-ऊन**, "from". The answers: **जवळ आहे** (*javaḷ āhe*), "it is near"; **खूप लांब आहे** (*khūp lāmb āhe*), "it is a long way"; **दहा मिनिटं** (*dahā miniṭaṁ*), "ten minutes".
