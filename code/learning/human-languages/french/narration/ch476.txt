French, chapter 476: Optimistes et inquiets.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'inquiétude — worry, anxiety.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for resentful, then the French for to reconcile.

You'll want to know: l'inquiétude.
l'inquiétude — "worry, anxiety".
Elle attend le résultat avec inquiétude. She is waiting for the result with worry.
Built on inquiet "worried". Feminine: une inquiétude.

The word, taken apart.
l'inquiétude is a learned borrowing from Latin inquiētūdō "restlessness" (EtymDB), built on quiēs "rest" with the negative in- (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
méfier — to be wary, to distrust (se méfier).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for worry, then the French for anxious.

You'll want to know: méfier.
méfier — "to be wary, to distrust (se méfier)".
Je me méfie des gens trop gentils. I am wary of people who are too nice.
Always reflexive in use: se méfier de. The adjective is méfiant.

The word, taken apart.
se méfier is built from mé- "badly" and se fier "to trust", which goes back to Vulgar Latin fīdāre, from Latin fīdere "to trust" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
optimiste — optimistic; optimist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for anxious, then the French for to be wary.

You'll want to know: optimiste.
optimiste — "optimistic; optimist".
Malgré la pluie, elle reste optimiste. In spite of the rain, she stays optimistic.
The same form for a man and a woman.

The word, taken apart.
optimiste is built on optimisme, a modern learned word made from Latin optimus "best" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
