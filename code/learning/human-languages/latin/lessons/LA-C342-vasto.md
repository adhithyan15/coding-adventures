## You'll want to know: vāstō, vāstāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C342-CLADES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vāstō, vāstāre** — "to lay waste, to ravage".

> Hostēs agrōs vīllāsque vāstāvērunt.
>
> *The enemy laid waste the fields and the farmhouses.*

*vāstō, vāstāre, vāstāvī, vāstātum*: to make a land empty. English *devastate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vāstō** is built on *vāstus*, "empty, desolate" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short): to make a place empty. English *waste* and *devastate* come from its family.
