## You'll want to know: はる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C209-SEASON209-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はる** — *haru* — "spring (the season)".

> **らいげつは はるです。** — *raigetsu wa haru desu* — "Next month is spring."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はる** (*haru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
