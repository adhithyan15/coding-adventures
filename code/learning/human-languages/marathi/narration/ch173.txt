You'll want to know: तरीही.
तरीही — tarīhī — "even so".

The word, taken apart.
तरीही (tarīhī) is tarī, which CDIAL lists, as "then", under Sanskrit tarhi, "then" (CDIAL 5730), with hī, "even".

Lesson 2 of 5.
जरी (jarī) — although.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for in the middle, then the Marathi for even so.

You'll want to know: जरी.
जरी — jarī — "although".

The word, taken apart.
जरी (jarī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from yarhi, "when" (CDIAL 10429). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "if".

Lesson 3 of 5.
असूनही (asūnhī) — despite, even though.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for even so, then the Marathi for although.

You'll want to know: असूनही.
असूनही — asūnhī — "despite, even though".

The word, taken apart.
असूनही (asūnhī) is asūn, "being", from the verb asṇẽ, "to be", with hī, "even". CDIAL lists asṇẽ under Sanskrit āste, "sits, exists, remains" (CDIAL 1480), or perhaps ākṣeti, "abides" (CDIAL 1031).

Lesson 4 of 5.
दुर्दैवाने (durdaivāne) — unfortunately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for although, then the Marathi for despite.

You'll want to know: दुर्दैवाने.
दुर्दैवाने — durdaivāne — "unfortunately".

The word, taken apart.
दुर्दैवाने (durdaivāne) is Sanskrit durdaiva, "ill luck": dur-, "bad", and daiva, "fate", with the ending -ने, "by".
