## You'll want to know: تعذر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C407-TAJANNUB407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعذر** — *taʿadhdhara* — "to prove impossible".

> تعذر علينا السفر بسبب الطقس.
>
> *taʿadhdhara ʿalaynā as-safar bi-sabab aṭ-ṭaqs.*
>
> Travelling proved impossible for us because of the weather.

Present: يتعذر, *yataʿadhdhar*.

It takes على for the person: تعذر عليه ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعذر** (*taʿadhdhara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-dh-r*, "to excuse" (Lane). It is Form V; Lane gives it for an affair that became hard to bring about, and so impracticable: to prove impossible.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
