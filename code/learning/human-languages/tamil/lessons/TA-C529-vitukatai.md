## You'll want to know: விடுகதை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C529-VILAIYATTU529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**விடுகதை** — *viṭukatai* — "a riddle".

> பாட்டி எங்களுக்கு ஒரு விடுகதை சொன்னாள்.
>
> *pāṭṭi eṅkaḷukku oru viṭukatai coṉṉāḷ.*
>
> Grandmother told us a riddle.

A spoken riddle, given as a little story; the listener must find the answer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**விடுகதை** (*viṭukatai*) ends in கதை (*katai*), "a story", from Sanskrit *kathā* (CDIAL 2704); how the first part, விடு, joins it is not settled.
