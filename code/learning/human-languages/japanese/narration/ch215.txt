Japanese, chapter 215: Arms, Fingers and Necks.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
からだ (karada) — the body.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to burn, then the Japanese for to vanish.

You'll want to know: からだ.
からだ — karada — "the body".
これは からだに いいですか。 — kore wa karada ni ii desu ka — "Is this good for your body?"

The word, taken apart.
からだ (karada) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

What you've built.
One more word for everyday life at A2.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: karada, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
