## You'll want to know: die Geige
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C513-INSTRUMENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Geige** — "violin, fiddle".

> Er spielt Geige in einer kleinen Band.
>
> *He plays the violin in a small band.*

Plural: *die Geigen*. The formal word is *die Violine*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Geige* is inherited from Middle High German *gīge* and Old High German *gīga* "fiddle" (EtymDB).
