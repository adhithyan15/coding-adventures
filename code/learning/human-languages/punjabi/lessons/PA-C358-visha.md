## You'll want to know: ਵਿਸ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C358-SUBJ358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਸ਼ਾ** — *vishā* — "a school subject; a topic".

> ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਿਸ਼ੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *sakūl vich mere chhe vishe han.*
>
> I have six subjects at school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਸ਼ਾ** (*vishā*) is taken over from Sanskrit *viṣaya*, "sphere, scope", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
