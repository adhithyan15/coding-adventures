## You'll want to know: hóngchá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C166-TEA166-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hóngchá* — "black tea".

> *nǐ yào hóngchá háishi lǜchá* — Black tea or green tea?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

English calls this tea black; Mandarin calls it red.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hóngchá* is built from *hóng* 'red' and 茶 'tea', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwng* and *drae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
