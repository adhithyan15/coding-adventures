## You'll want to know: سكة حديد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C428-SIKKA428-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سكة حديد** — *sikkat ḥadīd* — "a railway".

> وصلت سكة الحديد إلى قريتنا قبل قرن.
>
> *waṣalat sikkat al-ḥadīd ilā qaryatinā qabla qarn.*
>
> The railway reached our village a century ago.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سكة حديد** (*sikkat ḥadīd*) is built from two native Arabic words: *sikka*, a straight road or row, on the root *s-k-k* (Lane), and *ḥadīd*, "iron", on the root *ḥ-d-d* (Lane): a road of iron.
