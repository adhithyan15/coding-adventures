## You'll want to know: رشوة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C388-FASAD388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رشوة** — *rashwa* — "a bribe".

> رفض الموظف الرشوة.
>
> *rafaḍa al-muwaẓẓaf ar-rashwa.*
>
> The employee refused the bribe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رشوة** (*rashwa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-sh-w*, "a well rope" (Lane). It is usually linked with *rishāʾ*, a well rope: a bribe is the rope let down to draw up what one wants.
