## You'll want to know: liceor, licērī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C420-AUCTIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**liceor, licērī** — "to bid (at an auction)".

> Hunc equum centum dēnāriīs licitus est.
>
> *He bid a hundred denarii for this horse.*

Deponent: *liceor, licērī, licitus sum*. The thing bid for goes in the accusative and the price in the ablative. It belongs with *licet*: to name what you are allowed to pay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liceor* belongs with *liceō* "to be for sale, to be valued" and *licet* "it is allowed" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to put a value on a thing.
