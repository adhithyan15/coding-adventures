# विचारपूस करणे (vicārpūs karṇe) — to ask after

## You'll want to know: विचारपूस करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C414-ATITHI414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विचारपूस करणे** — *vicārpūs karṇe* — "to ask after".

> आजीने फोनवर सगळ्यांची विचारपूस केली.
>
> *ājīne phonvar sagaḷyāñcī vicārpūs kelī.*
>
> Grandmother asked after everyone on the phone.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने विचारपूस केली, *tyāne vicārpūs kelī*.

The person asked about takes -ची: माझी विचारपूस केली, asked after me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विचारपूस करणे** (*vicārpūs karṇe*) is built from **विचार**, "a thought, a question", and *pūs*, "asking", from the family of Sanskrit *pṛcchati*, "asks", where CDIAL lists Marathi *pusṇẽ*, "to ask" (CDIAL 8352); **करणे** makes it a verb.
