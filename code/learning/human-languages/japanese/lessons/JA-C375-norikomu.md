## You'll want to know: のりこむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C375-SHUUDEN375-05]; assesses=[] -->

**のりこむ** — *norikomu* — "to get on board".

> **みんなが しはつに のりこみました。** — *minna ga shihatsu ni norikomimashita* — "Everyone got on board the first train."

Polite **のりこみます**, *norikomimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **じょうきゃくが でんしゃに のりこみました。**, *jōkyaku ga densha ni norikomimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のりこむ** (*norikomu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *nori*, "riding" (from *noru*) and *komu*, "to go in"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
