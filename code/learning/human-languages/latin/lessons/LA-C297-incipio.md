## You'll want to know: incipiō, incipere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C297-INCIPIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**incipiō, incipere** — "to begin".

> Mīlitēs mūrum aedificāre incēpērunt.
>
> *The soldiers began to build a wall.*

Perfect *incēpī*. With an infinitive: *incipit loquī*, he begins to speak. English *incipient*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**incipiō** is built on *capiō*, "to take", with *in-* (Lewis & Short): to lay hold of a task, to take it in hand. English *incipient* comes from it.
