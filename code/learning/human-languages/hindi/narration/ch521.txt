Hindi, chapter 521: Heat in the Kitchen.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
आँच (ā̃c) — the heat of a flame; a flame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to roll out (dough), then the Hindi for to puff up.

You'll want to know: आँच.
आँच — ā̃c — "the heat of a flame; a flame".
दाल को धीमी आँच पर पकाइए। Dāl ko dhīmī ā̃c par pakāie. Cook the dal on a low flame.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
आँच (ā̃c) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit arcis, "a flame, a ray" (CDIAL 635).

गरम तेल में थोड़ा नमक डालिए। Garam tel meṁ thoṛā namak ḍāliye. Put a little salt into the hot oil.

Lesson 4 of 6.
बघारना (baghārnā) — to temper (with hot spiced oil).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to put in, then the Hindi for cumin.

You'll want to know: बघारना.
बघारना — baghārnā — "to temper (with hot spiced oil)".
माँ ने दाल को घी और जीरे से बघारा। Māṁ ne dāl ko ghī aur jīre se baghārā. Mother tempered the dal with ghee and cumin.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सब्ज़ी बघारी, usne sabzī baghārī.

The word, taken apart.
बघारना (baghārnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit vyāghārayati, "sprinkles (with hot fat)" (CDIAL 12191). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to brown onions and spices in heated oil".

दादी कड़ाही में मूँगफली भूँज रही हैं। Dādī kaṛāhī meṁ mū̃gphalī bhū̃j rahī haiṁ. Grandmother is roasting peanuts in the wok.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मूँगफली भूँजी, usne mū̃gphalī bhū̃jī.

The word, taken apart.
भूँजना (bhū̃jnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from bhṛñjati, "parches", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9586). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to parch, fry".
