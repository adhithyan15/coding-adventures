Bengali, chapter 385: Hours and Waiting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘণ্টা (ghônṭā) — an hour; a bell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a local train, then the Bengali for to blow a whistle.

You'll want to know: ঘণ্টা.
ঘণ্টা — ghônṭā — "an hour; a bell".
ট্রেনটা এক ঘণ্টা দেরিতে এল। ṭrenṭā ek ghônṭā derite elô. The train came an hour late.
After a number there is no plural: দুই ঘণ্টা (ghônṭā), two hours.
The first letter is ঘ, the breathy partner of গ.

The word, taken apart.
ঘণ্টা (ghônṭā) is Sanskrit ghaṇṭā, "a bell" (Monier-Williams); the sense "an hour" is probably the bell or gong that was struck to mark the time.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ঘড়ি (ghôṛi) — a clock; a watch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to blow a whistle, then the Bengali for an hour.

You'll want to know: ঘড়ি.
ঘড়ি — ghôṛi — "a clock; a watch".
আমার ঘড়িটা হারিয়ে গেছে। āmār ghôṛiṭā hāriye gechhe. My watch has gone missing.
One word for the clock on the wall and the watch on your wrist.

The word, taken apart.
ঘড়ি (ghôṛi) goes back to Sanskrit ghaṭikā, "a small water-jar" (Monier-Williams): a pierced bowl that sank in a set time was an old Indian clock, and the word probably passed from the bowl to the time it kept and then to any clock.

Lesson 3 of 5.
টিকটিক করা (ṭikṭik kôrā) — to tick (of a clock).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an hour, then the Bengali for a clock.

You'll want to know: টিকটিক করা.
টিকটিক করা — ṭikṭik kôrā — "to tick (of a clock)".
স্টেশনের বড় ঘড়িটা টিকটিক করছিল। sṭeshôner bôṛo ghôṛiṭā ṭikṭik kôrchhilô. The big station clock was ticking.
Past (he/she …): সেই ঘড়ি টিকটিক করল, sei ghôṛi ṭikṭik kôrlô.
টিকটিকি, a gecko, is named the same way, for its clicking call.

The word, taken apart.
টিকটিক করা (ṭikṭik kôrā) joins টিকটিক, an imitative word for the tick of a clock, to করা, to do. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
অপেক্ষা করা (ôpekkhā kôrā) — to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a clock, then the Bengali for to tick (of a clock).

You'll want to know: অপেক্ষা করা.
অপেক্ষা করা — ôpekkhā kôrā — "to wait".
আমরা প্ল্যাটফর্মে এক ঘণ্টা অপেক্ষা করলাম। āmrā plæṭphôrme ek ghônṭā ôpekkhā kôrlām. We waited on the platform for an hour.
Past (he/she …): তিনি অপেক্ষা করলেন, tini ôpekkhā kôrlen.
The one you wait for takes -এর জন্য: ট্রেনের জন্য অপেক্ষা করা (ôpekkhā kôrā), to wait for the train.

The word, taken apart.
অপেক্ষা করা (ôpekkhā kôrā) joins Sanskrit apekṣā, "looking round, regard, expectation" (Monier-Williams), to করা, to do; Bengali narrowed the looking-out to waiting.

Lesson 5 of 5.
বেঞ্চ (bench) — a bench.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to tick (of a clock), then the Bengali for to wait.

You'll want to know: বেঞ্চ.
বেঞ্চ — bench — "a bench".
প্ল্যাটফর্মের বেঞ্চে বসে আমরা দুই ঘণ্টা অপেক্ষা করলাম। plæṭphôrmer benche bôse āmrā dui ghônṭā ôpekkhā kôrlām. We sat on a bench on the platform and waited for two hours.

The word, taken apart.
বেঞ্চ (bench) is borrowed from English bench; ঞ্চ is the n-ch joined letter.
