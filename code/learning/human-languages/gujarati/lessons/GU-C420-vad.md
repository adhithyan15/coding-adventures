## You'll want to know: વાડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C420-HOUSE420-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વાડ** — *vāḍ* — "a fence; a hedge".

> બગીચાની બહાર લીલી વાડ છે.
>
> *bagīcānī bahār līlī vāḍ che.*
>
> There is a green hedge outside the garden.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાડ** (*vāḍ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *vāṭa*, "an enclosure"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a fence" (CDIAL 11480).
