## You'll want to know: huíyì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C247-TRIP247-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huíyì* — "to recall; to think back on".

> *wǒ chángcháng huíyì nà cì lǚyóu* — I often think back to that trip.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huíyì* is built from 回 'to return' and *yì* 'to remember'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second, *'ik* there, 'to think of'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
