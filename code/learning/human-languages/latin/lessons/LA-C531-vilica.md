## You'll want to know: vīlica, vīlicae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C531-IANI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vīlica, vīlicae** — "a farm housekeeper".

> Vīlica cēnam familiae rūsticae parat.
>
> *The farm housekeeper prepares dinner for the farm slaves.*

Feminine, first declension: the wife of the *vīlicus*, the farm manager. She ran the farm kitchen and stores.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vīlica** is the feminine of *vīlicus* "farm manager", built on *vīlla* "country house, farm" (Lewis & Short).
