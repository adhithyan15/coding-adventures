Hindi, chapter 395: Getting the Go-Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मंज़ूरी (maṁzūrī) — approval, sanction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for capable, then the Hindi for to put in order.

You'll want to know: मंज़ूरी.
मंज़ूरी — maṁzūrī — "approval, sanction".
अगर सरकार मंज़ूरी देगी तो पुल बनेगा। agar sarkār maṁzūrī degī to pul banegā. If the government gives approval, the bridge will be built.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
मंज़ूरी (maṁzūrī) is usually traced to Arabic manẓūr, "seen, approved", which reached Hindi through Persian. The Hindi noun adds the ending -ī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बटोरना (baṭornā) — to collect up, to sweep together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for approval, then the Hindi for an arrangement.

You'll want to know: बटोरना.
बटोरना — baṭornā — "to collect up, to sweep together".
अगर बारिश होगी तो कपड़े जल्दी बटोरो। agar bāriś hogī to kapṛe jaldī baṭoro. If it rains, bring the washing in quickly.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपनी चीज़ें बटोरीं, usne apnī cīzeṁ baṭorīṁ.

The word, taken apart.
बटोरना (baṭornā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit vartula, "round" (CDIAL 11365). CDIAL lists the Hindi verb, "to collect", under this entry; how "round" led to gathering up is not spelled out there.

Lesson 4 of 5.
फ़ेल होना (fel honā) — to fail (an exam).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an arrangement, then the Hindi for to collect up.

You'll want to know: फ़ेल होना.
फ़ेल होना — fel honā — "to fail (an exam)".
अगर तुम नहीं पढ़ोगे तो फ़ेल हो जाओगे। agar tum nahīṃ paṛhoge to fel ho jāoge. If you don't study, you'll fail.
Past (he …): वह परीक्षा में फ़ेल हो गया, vah parīkṣā meṁ fel ho gayā.

The word, taken apart.
फ़ेल होना (fel honā) joins फ़ेल to होना, "to be, to become". फ़ेल is a loan from English fail.
