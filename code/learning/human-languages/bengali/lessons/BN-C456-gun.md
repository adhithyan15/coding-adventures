## You'll want to know: গুণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C456-CHORITRO456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**গুণ** — *gun* — "a virtue; a good quality".

> ধৈর্য মীরার সবচেয়ে বড় গুণ।
>
> *dhoirjô mīrār sôbcheye bôṛo gun.*
>
> Patience is Mira's greatest virtue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গুণ** (*gun*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *guṇa*, first "a single strand of a cord" and then "a quality, a virtue" (Monier-Williams).
