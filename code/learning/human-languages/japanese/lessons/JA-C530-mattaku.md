# まったく (mattaku) — (not) at all; entirely

## You'll want to know: まったく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C530-ATLEAST530-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まったく** — *mattaku* — "(not) at all; entirely".

> **もし ちずを わすれたら、みちが まったく わかりません。** — *moshi chizu o wasuretara, michi ga mattaku wakarimasen* — "If you forget the map, you will not know the way at all."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まったく** (*mattaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
