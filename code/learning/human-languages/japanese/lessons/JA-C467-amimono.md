## You'll want to know: あみもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C467-CRAFT467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あみもの** — *amimono* — "knitting".

> **ふゆは あみものを します。** — *fuyu wa amimono o shimasu* — "I do knitting in winter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あみもの** (*amimono*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ami*, "knitting (amu)" and *mono*, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
