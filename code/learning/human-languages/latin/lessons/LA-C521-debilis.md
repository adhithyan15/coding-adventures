# dēbilis, dēbile — weak, disabled

## You'll want to know: dēbilis, dēbile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C521-AEGR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dēbilis, dēbile** — "weak, disabled".

> Senex dēbilis lentē ambulat.
>
> *The weak old man walks slowly.*

Two endings. Of a limb that will not work, or of general weakness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēbilis** is built on *dē-* "away from" and *habilis* "handy, able" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
