Lesson 1 of 5.
داستان کوتاه (dâstân-e kutâh) — a short story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to study, then the Persian for a bookworm.

You'll want to know: داستان کوتاه.
داستان کوتاه (dâstân-e kutâh) — "a short story".
این نویسنده بیشتر داستان کوتاه مینویسد. in nevisande bishtar dâstân-e kutâh minevisad. This writer mostly writes short stories.
dâstân 'story' + kutâh 'short'. Plural dâstânhâ-ye kutâh.

The word, taken apart.
dâstân-e kutâh is built on two native Persian words, dâstân, 'story', and kutâh, 'short', which EtymDB sets beside kuchek, 'small' (EtymDB).

[pause 2 seconds]

ترسناک (tarsnâk) — "scary, frightening".
دیشب یک فیلم ترسناک دیدیم. dishab yek film-e tarsnâk didim. Last night we watched a scary film.
tars 'fear' + -nâk 'full of'.

The word, taken apart.
tarsnâk is built on native Persian tars, 'fear', from inherited tarsidan, 'to fear', with the ending -nâk, 'full of'.

پایان داستان غم انگیز بود. pâyân-e dâstân ghamangiz bud. The end of the story was sad.
gham 'sorrow' + angiz 'stirring' (stem of angikhtan): sorrow-stirring.
