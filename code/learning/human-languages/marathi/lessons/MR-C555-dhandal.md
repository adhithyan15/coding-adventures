## You'll want to know: धांदल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C555-GHAI555-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धांदल** — *dhāndal* — "a rush; a flurry".

> सकाळी शाळेच्या वेळी घरात खूप धांदल असते.
>
> *sakāḷī śāḷecyā veḷī gharāt khūp dhāndal aste.*
>
> In the morning at school time the house is in a great rush.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धांदल** (*dhāndal*) is a Marathi word for a busy rush; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
