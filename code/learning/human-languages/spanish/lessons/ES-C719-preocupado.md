## You'll want to know: preocupado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C719-CONFIANZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**preocupado** — "worried".

> Estoy preocupado por mi padre.
>
> *I am worried about my father.*

Used with *estar*: *estar preocupado por*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preocupado* is the participle of *preocupar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *praeoccupāre* "to seize beforehand" (EtymDB), built on *occupāre* "to seize".
