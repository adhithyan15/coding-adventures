# reservar — set it aside

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-FIN-DE-SEMANA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have chosen **el fin de semana**. To book a room for it, use
**reservar**: **reservar una habitación**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-RESERVAR]; assesses=[] -->

Latin **reservāre** meant “keep back” or “preserve.” That image still works:
a hotel sets a room aside for you. English **reserve** is a close relative.
Keep the history at that safe level; resemblance to **servir** is not a reason
to invent a separate family story.

## Grammar Lens: book a thing for people
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-RESERVAR] -->

The thing follows directly: **reservar una habitación**. Add the people with
**para**: **para dos personas**. Together: **quiero reservar una habitación
para dos personas**.
