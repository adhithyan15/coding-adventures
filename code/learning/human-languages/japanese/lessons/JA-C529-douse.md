## You'll want to know: どうせ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C529-ANYWAY529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**どうせ** — *dōse* — "anyway; in any case".

> **もし あめが ふったら、どうせ しあいは ちゅうしでしょう。** — *moshi ame ga futtara, dōse shiai wa chūshi deshō* — "If it rains, the match will be called off anyway."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どうせ** (*dōse*) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict gives it no kanji spelling, and no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
