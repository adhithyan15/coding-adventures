# సభ్యుడు (sabhyuḍu) — a member

## You'll want to know: సభ్యుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C408-SAMAJAM408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సభ్యుడు** — *sabhyuḍu* — "a member".

> నేను ఆ సంఘంలో సభ్యుడిని.
>
> *nēnu ā saṅghaṁlō sabhyuḍini.*
>
> I am a member of that association.

Plural: సభ్యులు, *sabhyulu*.

A woman member is సభ్యురాలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సభ్యుడు** (*sabhyuḍu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sabhya*, "being in an assembly" (CDIAL 13171), from *sabhā*, "an assembly", with the Telugu masculine ending -డు.
