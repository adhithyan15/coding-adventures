## You'll want to know: அதே
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C95-RIGHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அதே** (*atē*) — "the same", "that very one".

It is **அது**, "that", with **-ஏ**, "exactly". When someone names what you meant: **அதே!**

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Exactly, really, nothing, not yet, the same.
