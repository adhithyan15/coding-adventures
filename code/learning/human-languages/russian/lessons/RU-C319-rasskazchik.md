## You'll want to know: рассказчик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C319-LEGENDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**рассказчик** (*rasskázchik*) — "a storyteller, a narrator".

> Дедушка был отличным рассказчиком.
>
> *Dédushka byl otlíchnym rasskázchikom.*
>
> Grandpa was an excellent storyteller.

A woman is *рассказчица*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рассказчик* is built on *рассказать* "to tell", from *рас-* and *казать* "to show, to tell", inherited from Proto-Slavic *kazati* (EtymDB), with the ending *-чик* for a person.
