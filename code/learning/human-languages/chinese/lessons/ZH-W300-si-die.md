## Script — a long bar over two small pieces
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SI-DIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 死

**Three pieces: a bar on top, then two side by side under it.**

**The bar, one stroke**, long, across the top.

**Under it on the left, three strokes.** The evening piece on the left of 外: a short sweep, a bar that turns into a long sweep to the lower left, and a dot.

**On the right, two strokes.** The right half of 比: a short sweep down to the left, then a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up.

1 + 3 + 2 = six.
