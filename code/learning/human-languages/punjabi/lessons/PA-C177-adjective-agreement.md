# ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ · ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ — let the adjective agree

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-01, PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-PRENOMINAL-01, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-03, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-04, PA-LEX-CHANGA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ**. Then retrieve the familiar feminine noun
**ਕੁੜੀ**. Keep the adjective before the noun in both phrases.

## Grammar Lens: change the adjective ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-GENDER-AGREEMENT-01]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-01, PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-PRENOMINAL-01, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-03, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-04, PA-LEX-CHANGA] -->

**ਚੰਗਾ** is a variable adjective. In this singular direct contrast, its ending
agrees with the noun's gender:

| noun gender | adjective + noun | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ** — *changā muṇḍā* | good boy |
| feminine | **ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ** — *changī kuṛī* | good girl |

The noun changes from **ਮੁੰਡਾ** to **ਕੁੜੀ**; the adjective changes from
**ਚੰਗਾ** to **ਚੰਗੀ**. This is one bounded singular direct contrast, not the
whole Punjabi adjective system. Plural and oblique forms wait.
