# തോരുക (tōruka) — to stop raining; to dry

## You'll want to know: തോരുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C507-MAZHA507-04]; assesses=[] -->

**തോരുക** — *tōruka* — "to stop raining; to dry".

> ഉച്ചയോടെ മഴ തോർന്നു.
>
> *uccayōṭe maḻa tōrnnu.*
>
> By noon the rain had stopped.

Past: തോർന്നു, *tōrnnu*.

For rain, tears and wet things: കണ്ണീർ തോർന്നു, the tears dried.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തോരുക** (*tōruka*) is native Dravidian, from a root that means to dry or to wipe off moisture (DEDR 3351). DEDR also lists under it a word for fair weather when the monsoon rain stops.
