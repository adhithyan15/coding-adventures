## You'll want to know: पितृष्वसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C499-SVASR499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पितृष्वसा** — *pitṛṣvasā* — "a father's sister".

> मम पितृष्वसा ग्रामे वसति।
>
> *mama pitṛṣvasā grāme vasati.*
>
> My father's sister lives in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पितृष्वसा** (*pitṛṣvasā*) is built from *pitṛ*, "father" (**पिता**), and *svasṛ*, "sister" (MW); CDIAL 8177 lists it beside *pituḥṣvasṛ*, the form behind Bengali *pisi* (CDIAL 8177).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
