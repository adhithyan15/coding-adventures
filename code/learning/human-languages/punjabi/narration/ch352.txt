Punjabi, chapter 352: Games Indoors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ (shatranj) — chess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a match, then the Punjabi for to defeat.

You'll want to know: ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ.
ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ — shatranj — "chess".
ਮੇਰਾ ਦਾਦਾ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ ਖੇਡਦਾ ਹੈ। merā dādā shām nū̃ shatranj kheḍdā hai. My grandfather plays chess in the evening.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ (shatranj) is a loan, usually traced to Persian shatranj, "chess", which goes back to Sanskrit caturaṅga, "four-limbed".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਵਾਰੀ (vārī) — one's turn (in a game or a queue).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for chess, then the Punjabi for a round of a game.

You'll want to know: ਵਾਰੀ.
ਵਾਰੀ — vārī — "one's turn (in a game or a queue)".
ਹੁਣ ਮੇਰੀ ਵਾਰੀ ਹੈ। huṇ merī vārī hai. Now it's my turn.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਾਰੀ (vārī) is inherited from Sanskrit vāra, "appointed time, one's turn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vār, "occasion", and Lahnda vārī, "turn, time", under it (CDIAL 11547).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਗੋਟੀ (goṭī) — a game piece; a counter on a board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a round of a game, then the Punjabi for one's turn.

You'll want to know: ਗੋਟੀ.
ਗੋਟੀ — goṭī — "a game piece; a counter on a board".
ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਚਾਰ ਲਾਲ ਗੋਟੀਆਂ ਹਨ। mere kol chār lāl goṭīāṁ han. I have four red pieces.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੋਟੀ (goṭī) is inherited from a reconstructed goṭṭa, "something round"; CDIAL lists Punjabi goṭ, "piece on a chequer board", under it (CDIAL 4271), but not the form goṭī itself.

Warm-up.

A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਇਨਾਮ (inām) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic inʿām, "favour".
