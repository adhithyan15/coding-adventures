Marathi, chapter 108: Black, White, Brown, Pink, Young.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
काळा (kāḷā) — black.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for red, then the Marathi for blue.

You'll want to know: काळा.
काळा — kāḷā — "black".

The word, taken apart.
काळा (kāḷā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit kāla, "black, dark-blue" (CDIAL 3083). CDIAL derives the Sanskrit word from Dravidian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पांढरा (pāṇḍhrā) — white.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for blue, then the Marathi for black.

You'll want to know: पांढरा.
पांढरा — pāṇḍhrā — "white".

The word, taken apart.
पांढरा (pāṇḍhrā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pāṇḍura, "whitish, pale, white" (CDIAL 8047). CDIAL lists Marathi pāṇḍhrā, "white".

तपकिरी — tapkirī — "brown".

The word, taken apart.
तपकिरी (tapkirī) is tapkīr, "snuff", with the ending -ī: "snuff-coloured". The origin of tapkīr is not settled; CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
गुलाबी (gulābī) — pink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for white, then the Marathi for brown.

You'll want to know: गुलाबी.
गुलाबी — gulābī — "pink".

The word, taken apart.
गुलाबी (gulābī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian gulābī, "rose-coloured". It is built on gulāb, "rose-water, rose": gul, "rose", and āb, "water".
