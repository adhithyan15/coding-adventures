Arabic, chapter 395: Reading and quoting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرجع (marjiʿ) — a reference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to summarize, then the Arabic for a summary.

You'll want to know: مرجع.
مرجع — marjiʿ — "a reference".
هذا القاموس مرجع مهم. hādhā al-qāmūs marjiʿ muhimm. This dictionary is an important reference.
Plural: مراجع, marājiʿ.

The word, taken apart.
مرجع (marjiʿ) is built on the native Arabic root r-j-ʿ, "to return, to go back" (Lane). Marjiʿ is a noun of place, a place one goes back to: a reference.

Lesson 2 of 5.
استشهد (istashhada) — to cite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a summary, then the Arabic for a reference.

You'll want to know: استشهد.
استشهد — istashhada — "to cite".
استشهد الكاتب بكلام جده. istashhada al-kātib bi-kalām jaddih. The writer cited his grandfather's words.
Present: يستشهد, yastashhid.
It takes بـ: استشهد (istashhada) بـ …. In another sense it means to die as a martyr.

The word, taken apart.
استشهد (istashhada) is built on the native Arabic root sh-h-d, "to witness, to be present" (Lane). It is Form X, to call for a witness: to cite as evidence.

Lesson 3 of 5.
اقتبس (iqtabasa) — to quote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a reference, then the Arabic for to cite.

You'll want to know: اقتبس.
اقتبس — iqtabasa — "to quote".
اقتبس الصحفي جملة من الخطاب. iqtabasa aṣ-ṣaḥafī jumla min al-khiṭāb. The journalist quoted a sentence from the speech.
Present: يقتبس, yaqtabis.

The word, taken apart.
اقتبس (iqtabasa) is built on the native Arabic root q-b-s, "to take fire from" (Lane). Lane gives the root for taking a flame from a fire, and also for taking knowledge: Form VIII came to mean to quote.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تأمل (taʾammala) — to reflect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to quote, then the Arabic for to read up on.

You'll want to know: تأمل.
تأمل — taʾammala — "to reflect".
تأمل الرجل البحر طويلا. taʾammala ar-rajul al-baḥr ṭawīlan. The man contemplated the sea for a long time.
Present: يتأمل, yataʾammal.
It takes a direct object or في: تأمل (taʾammala) في ….

The word, taken apart.
تأمل (taʾammala) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-m-l, "to hope for" (Lane). It is Form V: to gaze on a thing long and carefully, to reflect on it.
