## You'll want to know: le mythe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C322-RECIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le mythe** — "the myth".

> Les mythes grecs parlent de dieux et de monstres.
>
> *Greek myths talk about gods and monsters.*

Also a false belief: *c'est un mythe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mythe* comes from Late Latin *mythos*, a borrowing from Greek *mûthos* "word, story" (EtymDB).
