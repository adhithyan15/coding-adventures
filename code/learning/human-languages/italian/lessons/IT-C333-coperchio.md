## You'll want to know: il coperchio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C333-PENTOLA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il coperchio** — "the lid".

> Metti il coperchio sulla pentola.
>
> *Put the lid on the pot.*

Built on *coprire*, ‘to cover’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coperchio* is inherited from Latin *cooperculum* "a lid" (EtymDB), from *cooperīre* "to cover".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
