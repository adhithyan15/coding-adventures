## You'll want to know: बेहाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C307-PHANSA307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहाल** — *behāl* — "in a sorry state".

> दो दिन बाद लोग भूख से बेहाल थे।
>
> *do din bād log bhūkh se behāl the.*
>
> Two days later people were in a sorry state from hunger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहाल** (*behāl*) is usually traced to Persian *bē*, "without", with *ḥāl*, "a state, a condition", an Arabic word Persian took over: without a good state, at the end of one's strength.
