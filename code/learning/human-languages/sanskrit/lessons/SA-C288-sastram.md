## You'll want to know: शस्त्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C288-APARADHA288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शस्त्रम्** — *śastram* — "a weapon".

> चौरस्य हस्ते शस्त्रम् अस्ति।
>
> *caurasya haste śastram asti.*
>
> The thief has a weapon in his hand.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शस्त्रम्** (*śastram*) comes from the root √śas, "to cut, wound"; MW gives *śastra* as an instrument for cutting, a knife, a sword or any weapon (MW).
