# ঝ (jha) — the letter inside বোঝা

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-ANUSVAR-01, BN-SCRIPT-C41-BISARGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ং and ঃ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **বোঝা** (*bojhā*), "to understand".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ঝ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-JHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝ** — *jha*, a *j* with a puff of breath.

It is inside **বোঝা** (*bojhā*), "to understand". Say *boja*, then *bojhā*: the
second has the breath.

## Writing: ঝ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-JHA-01] -->

Put your pen on ঝ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
