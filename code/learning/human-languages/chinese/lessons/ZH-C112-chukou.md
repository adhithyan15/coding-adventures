## You'll want to know: 出口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C112-EXIT112-01]; assesses=[] -->

**出口** — *chūkǒu* — "exit; way out".

> **出口在前边** *chūkǒu zài qiánbian* — The way out is up ahead.

The new character, in pieces: 出 — two halves like 山, one on top of the other, with one long vertical down through both. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出口** (*chūkǒu*) is built from 出 'to go out' and 口 'mouth, opening', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the opening you go out by.
