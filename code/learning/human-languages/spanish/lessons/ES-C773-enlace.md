## You'll want to know: el enlace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C773-COBERTURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el enlace** — "the link; the connection".

> Te mando el enlace del billete.
>
> *I'll send you the link to the ticket.*

At a station or airport it is a connection: *perder el enlace*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enlace* is built on *enlazar* 'to tie together' (EtymDB), which comes from Latin *inlaqueāre*, built on *laqueus* 'a noose' (Lewis & Short).
