## You'll want to know: ফানুস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C305-PRODIP305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ফানুস** — *phānus* — "a sky lantern".

> উৎসবের রাতে আকাশে অনেক ফানুস উড়ছিল।
>
> *utsôber rāte ākāshe ônek phānus uṛchhilô.*
>
> On the night of the festival many sky lanterns were floating in the sky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফানুস** (*phānus*) is usually traced to Persian *fānūs*, 'a lantern'; in Bengal it is the paper lantern sent up into the sky.
