## You'll want to know: la sorcière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C321-CONTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la sorcière** — "the witch".

> La sorcière vivait seule dans une maison noire.
>
> *The witch lived alone in a black house.*

The masculine *le sorcier* is a wizard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sorcière* is the feminine of *sorcier*, inherited from Vulgar Latin *sortiārius* "one who casts lots", built on *sors* "a lot, fate" (Lewis & Short).
