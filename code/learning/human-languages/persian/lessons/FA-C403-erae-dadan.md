## You'll want to know: ارائه دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C403-TAHSILAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ارائه دادن** (*erâ'e dâdan*) — "to present, to submit".

> هر دانشجو باید یک مقاله ارائه دهد.
>
> *har dâneshju bâyad yek maqâle erâ'e dahad.*
>
> Every student must present a paper.

*Erâ'e* "presentation" + *dâdan* (stem *deh-*). Formal; spelled with ئ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارائه** is usually traced to Arabic *irā'a* "showing"; with *dâdan* "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE *deh₃-*, it means to put something before people.
