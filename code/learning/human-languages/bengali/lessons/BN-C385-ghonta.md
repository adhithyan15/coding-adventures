## You'll want to know: ঘণ্টা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C385-GHONTA385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘণ্টা** — *ghônṭā* — "an hour; a bell".

> ট্রেনটা এক ঘণ্টা দেরিতে এল।
>
> *ṭrenṭā ek ghônṭā derite elô.*
>
> The train came an hour late.

After a number there is no plural: দুই ঘণ্টা, two hours.

The first letter is ঘ, the breathy partner of গ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘণ্টা** (*ghônṭā*) is Sanskrit *ghaṇṭā*, "a bell" (Monier-Williams); the sense "an hour" is probably the bell or gong that was struck to mark the time.
