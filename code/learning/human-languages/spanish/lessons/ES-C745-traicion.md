## You'll want to know: la traición
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C745-RUPTURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la traición** — "betrayal, treason".

> Fue una traición imperdonable.
>
> *It was an unforgivable betrayal.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*traición* is usually traced to Latin *trāditiō*, *trāditiōnem* "a handing over", built on *trādere* "to hand over"; English *treason* is the same word through French.
