## You'll want to know: उकाडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C436-UKADA436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उकाडा** — *ukāḍā* — "sultry heat".

> पावसाआधी खूप उकाडा होतो.
>
> *pāvsāādhī khūp ukāḍā hoto.*
>
> Before the rain it gets very sultry.

Masculine.

Heavy, sticky heat; उकाडा होणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उकाडा** (*ukāḍā*) is inherited, from the family of Sanskrit *utkvathati*, "boils out", where CDIAL lists Marathi *ukaḍṇẽ*, "to be hot (of weather)" (CDIAL 1741).
