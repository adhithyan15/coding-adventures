## You'll want to know: ಪರ್ವತಾರೋಹಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C541-VYAYAMA541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರ್ವತಾರೋಹಣ** — *parvatārōhaṇa* — "mountaineering".

> ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣನಿಗೆ ಪರ್ವತಾರೋಹಣ ತುಂಬಾ ಇಷ್ಟ.
>
> *nanna aṇṇanige parvatārōhaṇa tumbā iṣṭa.*
>
> My elder brother loves mountaineering.

ಪರ್ವತ, mountain + ಆರೋಹಣ, climbing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರ್ವತಾರೋಹಣ** (*parvatārōhaṇa*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *parvata*, "rock, mountain" (CDIAL 7945), and *ārohaṇa*, "ascending" (Monier-Williams).
