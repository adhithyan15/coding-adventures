## You'll want to know: l'abete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C461-ALBERI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'abete** — "the fir (tree)".

> In montagna ci sono boschi di abeti.
>
> *In the mountains there are fir forests.*

The usual Christmas tree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abete* is inherited from Latin *abiēs, abiētem* 'fir' (EtymDB).
