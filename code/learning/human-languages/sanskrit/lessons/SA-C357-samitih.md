## You'll want to know: समितिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C357-SAMITI357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**समितिः** — *samitiḥ* — "a committee".

> समितिः श्वः निर्णयं करिष्यति।
>
> *samitiḥ śvaḥ nirṇayaṃ kariṣyati.*
>
> The committee will decide tomorrow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समितिः** (*samitiḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *sam-iti* "a meeting, an assembly, a council", and "a committee" is its modern sense (MW).
