German, chapter 263: Nachdem und bevor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
nachdem — after (joining two clauses).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for last of all, then the German for the order.

You'll want to know: nachdem.
nachdem — "after (joining two clauses)".
Nachdem wir gegessen hatten, gingen wir spazieren. After we had eaten, we went for a walk.
The verb goes to the end, and the earlier event takes the Plusquamperfekt: nachdem er angekommen war.

The word, taken apart.
nachdem joins the preposition nach "after" and dem "that", so "after that". Both pieces are inherited Germanic words; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

Lesson 2 of 5.
bevor — before (joining two clauses).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the order, then the German for after.

You'll want to know: bevor.
bevor — "before (joining two clauses)".
Bevor er schlafen ging, las er ein Buch. Before he went to bed, he read a book.
The verb goes to the end: bevor der Film beginnt. The preposition is vor: vor dem Film.

The word, taken apart.
bevor is built on vor "before", an inherited Germanic word and a cousin of English fore, with the old prefix be- in front; EtymDB lists no parent for the conjunction.

Lesson 3 of 5.
sobald — as soon as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for after, then the German for before.

You'll want to know: sobald.
sobald — "as soon as".
Sobald der Regen aufhörte, spielten die Kinder im Garten. As soon as the rain stopped, the children played in the garden.
The verb goes to the end. For the future the present is enough: Sobald ich Zeit habe, rufe ich an.

The word, taken apart.
sobald joins so and bald "soon". Bald is inherited and is a cousin of English bold (EtymDB), from an older sense "bold, quick".

You'll want to know: solange.
solange — "as long as".
Solange es regnete, blieben wir im Haus. As long as it was raining, we stayed in the house.
The verb goes to the end. It can also set a condition: solange du pünktlich bist.

The word, taken apart.
solange joins so and lange "for a long time", built on lang "long", an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
seitdem — since then; since (joining two clauses).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for as soon as, then the German for as long as.

You'll want to know: seitdem.
seitdem — "since then; since (joining two clauses)".
Seitdem sie ein Auto hat, fährt sie nicht mehr mit dem Bus. Since she has had a car, she no longer goes by bus.
Two uses: an adverb, with the verb second (Seitdem ist er glücklich), and a conjunction, with the verb at the end. The present covers something still true.

The word, taken apart.
seitdem joins seit "since" and dem "that". Seit comes from Middle High German sīt (EtymDB), usually traced to Old High German sīd "later, since", a cousin of English since.
