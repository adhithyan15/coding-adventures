## You'll want to know: le manuel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C377-REVISION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le manuel** — "the textbook, the manual".

> Le manuel de maths coûte cher.
>
> *The maths textbook is expensive.*

As an adjective: done by hand, *un travail manuel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manuel* comes from Late Latin *manuāle* "a handbook" (EtymDB), from *manuālis* "of the hand", built on *manus* "hand" (Lewis & Short).
