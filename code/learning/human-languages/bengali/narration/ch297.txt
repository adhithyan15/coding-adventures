Bengali, chapter 297: Who Said What.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
কথা (kôthā) — words; talk; what someone said.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to publish, then the Bengali for an editor.

You'll want to know: কথা.
কথা — kôthā — "words; talk; what someone said".
সাক্ষীর কথা শুনে দারোগা কিছু বললেন না। sākkhīr kôthā shune dārogā kichhu bôllen nā. The inspector listened to the witness and said nothing.
কথা (kôthā) বলা, to talk; কথা (kôthā) দেওয়া, to give your word.

The word, taken apart.
কথা (kôthā) is Sanskrit kathā, 'talk, conversation; a story' (Monier-Williams), and it keeps both senses in Bengali.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: সাক্ষাৎকার.
সাক্ষাৎকার — sākkhātkār — "an interview".
টিভিতে সাক্ষীর একটা সাক্ষাৎকার দেখাল। ṭibhite sākkhīr ekṭā sākkhātkār dekhālô. The television showed an interview with the witness.

The word, taken apart.
সাক্ষাৎকার (sākkhātkār) is Sanskrit sākṣātkāra, 'a direct perceiving' (Monier-Williams), from sākṣāt, 'before one's eyes'; the sense 'an interview', a meeting face to face, is the modern one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ঘোষণা করা (ghoshônā kôrā) — to announce; to declare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for talk, then the Bengali for an interview.

You'll want to know: ঘোষণা করা.
ঘোষণা করা — ghoshônā kôrā — "to announce; to declare".
সরকার দুই দিনের ছুটি ঘোষণা করল। sôrkār dui diner chhuṭi ghoshônā kôrlô. The government announced a two-day holiday.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ফলাফল ঘোষণা করলেন, tini phôlāphôl ghoshônā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
ঘোষণা করা (ghoshônā kôrā) puts Sanskrit ghoṣaṇā, 'a proclaiming aloud' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to make a thing known to everyone at once.

Lesson 4 of 5.
বিবৃতি (bibriti) — a public statement; a statement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an interview, then the Bengali for to announce.

You'll want to know: বিবৃতি.
বিবৃতি — bibriti — "a public statement; a statement".
মন্ত্রী দুর্ঘটনার পরে একটা বিবৃতি দিলেন। môntrī durghôṭônār pôre ekṭā bibriti dilen. The minister made a statement after the accident.

The word, taken apart.
বিবৃতি (bibriti) is Sanskrit vivṛti, 'a making manifest, an exposition' (Monier-Williams): a statement opens a matter up. বিবৃতি দেওয়া, to issue a statement.

Lesson 5 of 5.
উল্লেখ করা (ullekh kôrā) — to mention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to announce, then the Bengali for a public statement.

You'll want to know: উল্লেখ করা.
উল্লেখ করা — ullekh kôrā — "to mention".
প্রতিবেদনে সাংবাদিক সাক্ষীর নাম উল্লেখ করলেন না। prôtibedône sāngbādik sākkhīr nām ullekh kôrlen nā. In the report the journalist did not mention the witness's name.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ঘটনাটা উল্লেখ করলেন, tini ghôṭônāṭā ullekh kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
উল্লেখ করা (ullekh kôrā) puts Sanskrit ullekha, 'causing to come forth clearly' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to bring a name or a fact out into what you say.
