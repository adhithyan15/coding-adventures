## You'll want to know: 体力
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C474-STRENGTH474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**体力** — *tǐlì* — "stamina; physical fitness".

> **他病了一个月脸色和体力都不好** *tā bìng le yí ge yuè liǎnsè hé tǐlì dōu bù hǎo* — He was ill for a month, and his colour and stamina are both poor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**体力** (*tǐlì*) is built from 体 'body' and 力 'strength', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thejX* and *lik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the body' and the second 'sinew'.
