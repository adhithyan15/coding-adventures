## You'll want to know: ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C327-THATKSHANA327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ** — *haṭhāttāgi* — "all of a sudden".

> ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ ದೀಪ ಆರಿಹೋಯಿತು.
>
> *haṭhāttāgi dīpa ārihōyitu.*
>
> All of a sudden the light went out.

A more bookish partner of ಇದ್ದಕ್ಕಿದ್ದಂತೆ. ಹಠಾತ್ ಸಾವು is a sudden death.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಠಾತ್ತಾಗಿ** (*haṭhāttāgi*) is built on Sanskrit *haṭhāt*, "by force", the ablative of *haṭha*, "violence, force" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada adverb ending -*āgi*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
