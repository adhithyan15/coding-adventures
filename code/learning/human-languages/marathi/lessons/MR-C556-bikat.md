## You'll want to know: बिकट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C556-ANUKUL556-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बिकट** — *bikaṭ* — "dire; critical".

> पुरामुळे गावातली परिस्थिती बिकट झाली.
>
> *purāmuḷe gāvātlī paristhitī bikaṭ jhālī.*
>
> Because of the flood, things in the village became dire.

Mostly of a situation or a road: बिकट परिस्थिती, बिकट वाट.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिकट** (*bikaṭ*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *vikaṭa*, "dreadful, monstrous, of unusual size" (Monier-Williams), with the v- said as b-; CDIAL lists no Marathi बिकट, so the path is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
