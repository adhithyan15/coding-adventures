## You'll want to know: こううん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C508-LUCK508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こううん** — *kōun* — "good fortune".

> **もし いい ともだちに あったら、それは こううんでしょう。** — *moshi ii tomodachi ni attara, sore wa kōun deshō* — "If you met a good friend, that would be good fortune."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こううん** (*kōun*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "happiness" (Middle Chinese *heangX*) and *un*, "luck" (*hjunH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
