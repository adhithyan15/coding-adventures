## You'll want to know: o juízo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C344-JUIZO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o juízo** — "the judgement; good sense".

> Ele tem pouco juízo: conduz depressa demais.
>
> *He has little sense: he drives too fast.*

*Ter juízo* — to be sensible; *perder o juízo* — to lose your mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juízo* comes from Latin *iūdicium* "a judicial investigation, a judgement" (Lewis & Short), built on *iūdex* "judge"; Spanish *juicio* and Italian *giudizio* are its cousins (EtymDB).
