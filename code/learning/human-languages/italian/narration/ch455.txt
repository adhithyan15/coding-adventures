Italian, chapter 455: Riparare e sistemare.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il calzolaio — the cobbler, the shoemaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the cake shop, then the Italian for the rotisserie.

You'll want to know: il calzolaio.
il calzolaio — "the cobbler, the shoemaker".
Il calzolaio ripara le mie scarpe vecchie. The cobbler repairs my old shoes.
Le calze — socks: both go back to Latin calceus, shoe.

The word, taken apart.
calzolaio is built on calzare 'to put on shoes', which is inherited from Latin calceāre (EtymDB), from calceus 'shoe' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

A woman is la sarta.

sarto is inherited from Late Latin sartor 'tailor' (EtymDB), from sarcīre 'to patch, to mend'.

Mi può accorciare questi pantaloni? Can you take up these trousers for me?

accorciare is built from a- and corto 'short', which is inherited from Latin curtus 'cut short' (Lewis & Short).
