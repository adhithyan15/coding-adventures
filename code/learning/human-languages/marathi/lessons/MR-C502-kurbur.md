## You'll want to know: कुरबुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C502-DURAVA502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरबुर** — *kurbur* — "bickering".

> घरात लहान गोष्टींवरून कुरबुर चालते.
>
> *gharāt lahān goṣṭīṁvarūn kurbur cālte.*
>
> At home there is bickering over little things.

Feminine.

Petty grumbles and squabbles: कुरबुर करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुरबुर** (*kurbur*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
