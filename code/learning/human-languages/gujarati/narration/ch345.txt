Lesson 1 of 5.
ઇનકાર (inkār) — a refusal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for uncertainty, then the Gujarati for to indicate.

You'll want to know: ઇનકાર.
ઇનકાર — inkār — "a refusal".
દુકાનદારે પૈસા પાછા આપવાનો ઇનકાર કર્યો. dukāndāre paisā pāchā āpvāno inkār karyo. The shopkeeper refused to give the money back.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઇનકાર (inkār) is usually traced to Arabic inkār, "denial", through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ચોખવટ (cokhvaṭ) — a clear understanding (on a point).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a refusal, then the Gujarati for an explanation.

You'll want to know: ચોખવટ.
ચોખવટ — cokhvaṭ — "a clear understanding (on a point)".
પૈસાની ચોખવટ પહેલેથી કરવી જોઈએ. paisānī cokhvaṭ pahelethī karvī joīe. Money matters should be made clear beforehand.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચોખવટ (cokhvaṭ) is built on cokh, "clean, clear", the base of ચોખ્ખું (cokhkhũ), which goes back to Sanskrit cokṣa, "pure, clean" (CDIAL 4918): a matter made clean and clear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an explanation, then the Gujarati for a clear understanding.

You'll want to know: ગણાવવું.
ગણાવવું — gaṇāvvũ — "to list (reasons, one by one)".
અરુણે નોકરી છોડવાનાં ત્રણ કારણો ગણાવ્યાં. Aruṇe nokrī choḍvānā̃ traṇ kāraṇo gaṇāvyā̃. Arun gave three reasons for leaving his job.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધાં કારણો ગણાવ્યાં, teṇe badhā̃ kāraṇo gaṇāvyā̃.

The word, taken apart.
ગણાવવું (gaṇāvvũ) is the causative of ગણવું (gaṇvũ), "to count", from Sanskrit gaṇayati, "counts" (CDIAL 3993): to count things out for someone.
