## You'll want to know: फोन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C197-PHONE197-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फोन** — *phon* — "a phone".

> फोन मेज माथै है।
>
> *phon mej māthai hai.*
>
> The phone is on the table.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फोन** (*phon*) is borrowed from English *phone*, short for telephone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
