## You'll want to know: अधित्यका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C446-SHIKHARA446-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अधित्यका** — *adhityakā* — "a plateau; high table-land".

> पर्वतस्य उपरि विशाला अधित्यका अस्ति।
>
> *parvatasya upari viśālā adhityakā asti.*
>
> On top of the mountain there is a wide plateau.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधित्यका** (*adhityakā*) is built on *adhi*, "over, above", through *adhi-tya*, as MW gives it (Pāṇini 5.2.34); MW gives *adhityakā* as land on the upper part of a mountain, table-land (MW).
