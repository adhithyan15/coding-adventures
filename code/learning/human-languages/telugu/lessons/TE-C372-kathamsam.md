# కథాంశం (kathāṁśaṁ) — a plot (of a story)

## You'll want to know: కథాంశం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C372-KATHAKUDU372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కథాంశం** — *kathāṁśaṁ* — "a plot (of a story)".

> ఆ సినిమా కథాంశం చాలా సులభం.
>
> *ā sinimā kathāṁśaṁ cālā sulabhaṁ.*
>
> That film's plot is very simple.

కథ, a story, with a Sanskrit word for a part or a point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కథాంశం** (*kathāṁśaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *kathā*, "a story", and *aṁśa*, "a share, a part" (CDIAL 2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
