## You'll want to know: consoler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C471-DECEPTION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**consoler** — "to comfort, to console".

> Mon ami m'a consolé après mon échec.
>
> *My friend comforted me after my failure.*

*Se consoler* — to get over something sad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consoler* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnsōlārī* "to comfort" (EtymDB), built on *sōlārī* "to soothe" (Lewis & Short).
