## You'll want to know: arroser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C503-JARDIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**arroser** — "to water (plants)".

> Pendant les vacances, le voisin arrose nos plantes.
>
> *During the holidays the neighbour waters our plants.*

Informally, to celebrate with a drink: *on va arroser ça*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arroser* comes from Late Latin *arrōsāre* "to bedew", built on Latin *rōs* "dew" (Lewis & Short).
