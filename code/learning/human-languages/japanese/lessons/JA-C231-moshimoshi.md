# もしもし (moshimoshi) — hello on the phone; excuse me (calling out)

## You'll want to know: もしもし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C231-PHONE231-01]; assesses=[] -->

**もしもし** — *moshimoshi* — "hello on the phone; excuse me (calling out)".

> **でんわで もしもしと いいます。** — *denwa de moshimoshi to iimasu* — "On the phone, people say moshimoshi."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もしもし** (*moshimoshi*) is usually called a native Japanese word (wago), not borrowed from Chinese, and JMdict gives it in kana alone; it is a doubled form, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
