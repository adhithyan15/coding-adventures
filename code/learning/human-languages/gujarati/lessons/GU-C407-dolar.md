## You'll want to know: ડોલર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C407-ABROAD407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ડોલર** — *ḍolar* — "a dollar".

> એક ડોલરના કેટલા રૂપિયા મળે છે?
>
> *ek ḍolarnā keṭlā rūpiyā maḷe che?*
>
> How many rupees do you get for a dollar?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડોલર** (*ḍolar*) is borrowed from English *dollar*.
