# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a ticket, please" and "my bag got lost". (*ek ṭikaṭ dījiye*, *merā thailā kho gayā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C272-DAR-01, UR-SCRIPT-C272-DDAL-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-02, UR-LEX-C272-BANNA-01, UR-LEX-C272-UMMID-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-02, UR-LEX-C42-HEALTH42-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I'm happy today. Yesterday I was sad.]
- [YOU SAY: I'm afraid of dogs.]
- [YOU SAY: I liked this city. How did you like the film?]
- [YOU SAY: She became a teacher. I want to become a nurse.]
- [YOU SAY: My dream is to become a player. I hope I will become one.]
- [YOU WRITE: **ڈر** once, and point at ڈ inside it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I got scared"? (**مجھے ڈر لگا**.) Now the other way round: in **مجھے یہ شہر اچھا لگا**, which word sets **اچھا لگا**? (**شہر**.) "I hope"? (**مجھے امید ہے**.)
