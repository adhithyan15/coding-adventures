## You'll want to know: кража
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C288-KRAZHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**кража** (*krázha*) — "a theft".

> В магазине была кража.
>
> *V magazíne bylá krázha.*
>
> There was a theft at the shop.

Feminine. *Кража со взломом* — burglary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*кража* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *kradja* "a theft" (EtymDB), built on *krasti* "to steal".
