## You'll want to know: fēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fēng* — "wind".

> *tiānqì bù hǎo fēng hěn dà* — The weather's bad: the wind is strong.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *pjuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an old book calling the wind the messenger of sky and earth.
