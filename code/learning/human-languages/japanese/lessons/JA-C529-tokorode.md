## You'll want to know: ところで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C529-ANYWAY529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ところで** — *tokorode* — "by the way".

> **ところで、もし じかんが あったら、えいがに いきませんか。** — *tokorode, moshi jikan ga attara, eiga ni ikimasen ka* — "By the way, if you have time, would you like to see a film?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ところで** (*tokorode*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from *tokoro*, "place", and the particle *de* (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
