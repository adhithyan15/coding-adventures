## You'll want to know: محاضر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C392-MUHADIR392-01]; assesses=[] -->

**محاضر** — *muḥāḍir* — "a lecturer".

> يشرح المحاضر الدرس بوضوح.
>
> *yashraḥu al-muḥāḍir ad-dars bi-wuḍūḥ.*
>
> The lecturer explains the lesson clearly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محاضر** (*muḥāḍir*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ḍ-r*, "to be present" (Lane). *Muḥāḍir* is the Form III active participle, one who speaks to those present: a lecturer.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
