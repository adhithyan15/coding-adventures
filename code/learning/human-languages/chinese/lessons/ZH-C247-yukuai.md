## You'll want to know: yúkuài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C247-TRIP247-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yúkuài* — "pleasant; cheerful".

> *zhōumò wǒmen wán de hěn yúkuài* — We had a lovely time at the weekend.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yúkuài* is built from *yú* 'glad' and 快 'pleased', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yu* and *khwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'pleased' and 'happy' and the second 'to one's liking' and 'glad'.
