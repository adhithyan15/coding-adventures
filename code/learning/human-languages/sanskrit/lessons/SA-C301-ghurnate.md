# घूर्णते (ghūrṇate) — whirls; spins round

## You'll want to know: घूर्णते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C301-CAKRAVATA301-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घूर्णते** — *ghūrṇate* — "whirls; spins round".

> चक्रवातः वेगेन घूर्णते।
>
> *cakravātaḥ vegena ghūrṇate.*
>
> The whirlwind spins fast.

Past (it …): वायुः वेगेन घूर्णितवान्, *vāyuḥ vegena ghūrṇitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घूर्णते** (*ghūrṇate*) comes from the root √ghūrṇ, "to move to and fro, roll about" (MW).
