## You'll want to know: عیب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C455-FLAW455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عیب** — *aib* — "a defect; a fault".

> اس گاڑی میں ایک عیب ہے۔
>
> *is gāṛī meṅ ek aib hai.*
>
> There is a defect in this car.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عیب** (*aib*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿayb*, "a fault, a blemish".
