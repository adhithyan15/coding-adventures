## You'll want to know: શબ્દકોશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C251-MEANING251-03]; assesses=[] -->

**શબ્દકોશ** — *śabdkoś* — "dictionary".

> **હું શબ્દકોશમાં અર્થ શોધું છું.** — *hũ śabdkośmā̃ arth śodhũ chũ* — "I look up the meaning in the dictionary."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શબ્દકોશ** (*śabdkoś*), a store of words, is a learned compound of two tatsamas: Sanskrit *śabda*, "sound, word" (CDIAL 12298), and *kōśa*, "case, storeroom" (CDIAL 3539).
