## You'll want to know: o bolor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C433-BARULHO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o bolor** — "mould".

> Há bolor na parede da casa de banho.
>
> *There is mould on the bathroom wall.*

*Ganhar bolor* — to go mouldy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
