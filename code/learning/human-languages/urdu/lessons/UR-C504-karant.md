## You'll want to know: کرنٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C504-SVICH504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کرنٹ** — *karanṭ* — "an electric current".

> اس تار کو نہ چھوئیے، اس میں کرنٹ ہے۔
>
> *is tār ko na chhūiye, is meṅ karanṭ hai.*
>
> Please don't touch this wire: there is current in it.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کرنٹ** (*karanṭ*) is borrowed from English *current*.
