## You'll want to know: स्वागतकक्ष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C414-ATITHI414-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वागतकक्ष** — *svāgatkakṣa* — "a reception desk".

> स्वागतकक्षात आम्हाला खोलीची किल्ली मिळाली.
>
> *svāgatkakṣāt āmhālā kholīcī killī miḷālī.*
>
> We got our room key at the reception desk.

Masculine.

The front desk of a hotel or an office; कक्ष is a room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वागतकक्ष** (*svāgatkakṣa*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *svāgata*, "welcome", and *kakṣa*, "an inner room" (Monier-Williams).
