## You'll want to know: वाचक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C314-VACHAK314-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वाचक** — *vācak* — "a reader".

> या मासिकाचे हजारो वाचक आहेत.
>
> *yā māsikāce hajāro vācak āhet.*
>
> This magazine has thousands of readers.

Masculine.

Plural वाचक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाचक** (*vācak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vācaka*, 'speaking, expressing' (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it for the one who reads, from *vācṇe*, 'to read'.
