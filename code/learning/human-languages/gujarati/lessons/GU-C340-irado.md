## You'll want to know: ઇરાદો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C340-HETU340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇરાદો** — *irādo* — "an intention".

> અરુણનો ઇરાદો સારો હતો, પણ પરિણામ ખરાબ આવ્યું.
>
> *Aruṇno irādo sāro hato, paṇ pariṇām kharāb āvyũ.*
>
> Arun meant well, but the result was bad.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇરાદો** (*irādo*) is usually traced to Arabic *irāda*, "will, intention", which came to Gujarati through Persian.
