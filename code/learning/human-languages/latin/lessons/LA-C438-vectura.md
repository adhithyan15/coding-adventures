## You'll want to know: vectūra, vectūrae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C438-CONSCENDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**vectūra, vectūrae** — "transport, carriage (of goods or people); the fare".

> Mercātor prō vectūrā mercium nummōs dat.
>
> *The merchant pays coins for the transport of his goods.*

First declension, feminine: *vectūra, vectūrae f.*, from *vehō* 'to carry'. Also the price paid for it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vectūra* is built on *vehō* 'to carry' (Lewis & Short), from its participle *vectus*, with the ending *-ūra*.
