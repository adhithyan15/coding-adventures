Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼ (koshish) — an effort; a try.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to carve, then the Punjabi for to try out.

You'll want to know: ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼.
ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼ — koshish — "an effort; a try".
ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ har roz koshish kardā hāṁ. I make an effort every day.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼ (koshish) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kōshish, "effort".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਉਸਾਰਨਾ (usārnā) — to put up a building; to build.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to try out, then the Punjabi for an effort.

You'll want to know: ਉਸਾਰਨਾ.
ਉਸਾਰਨਾ — usārnā — "to put up a building; to build".
ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿਤਾ ਪਿੰਡ ਵਿੱਚ ਘਰ ਉਸਾਰਦਾ ਹੈ। merā pitā piṇḍ vich ghar usārdā hai. My father builds houses in the village.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਕਮਰਾ ਉਸਾਰਿਆ, maiṁ ikk kamrā usāriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਸਾਰਨਾ (usārnā) is the transitive partner of ussarṇā, "to rise (of a building)", which goes back to Sanskrit útsarati, "hastens away", with a sense "rises" that CDIAL infers; CDIAL lists Punjabi ussarṇā under it (CDIAL 1874), but not the transitive form itself.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਨਿਭਾਉਣਾ (nibhāuṇā) — to carry out a duty; to keep a promise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an effort, then the Punjabi for to put up a building.

You'll want to know: ਨਿਭਾਉਣਾ.
ਨਿਭਾਉਣਾ — nibhāuṇā — "to carry out a duty; to keep a promise".
ਉਹ ਹਮੇਸ਼ਾ ਆਪਣਾ ਵਾਅਦਾ ਨਿਭਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। oh hameshā āpṇā vāadā nibhāundā hai. He always keeps his promise.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਵਾਅਦਾ ਨਿਭਾਇਆ, maiṁ āpṇā vāadā nibhāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਿਭਾਉਣਾ (nibhāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit nirvahati, "leads out of, accomplishes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi nibhāuṇā, "to accomplish", under it (CDIAL 7397).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਸੰਵਾਰਨਾ (saṁvārnā) — to tidy up; to set in order.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to put up a building, then the Punjabi for to carry out a duty.

You'll want to know: ਸੰਵਾਰਨਾ.
ਸੰਵਾਰਨਾ — saṁvārnā — "to tidy up; to set in order".
ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਸੰਵਾਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ savere dukān saṁvārdā hāṁ. I set the shop in order in the morning.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਮਰਾ ਸੰਵਾਰਿਆ, maiṁ kamrā saṁvāriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੰਵਾਰਨਾ (saṁvārnā) is inherited from a reconstructed śodhakāra, "making clean"; CDIAL lists Punjabi savārṇā, "to adorn, improve, prepare, adjust", under it (CDIAL 12628); the spelling with the nasal is not listed there.
