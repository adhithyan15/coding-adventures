Urdu, chapter 303: Claims and Denials.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انکار کرنا (inkār karnā) — to deny; to refuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to stir up, then the Urdu for the public.

You'll want to know: انکار کرنا.
انکار کرنا — inkār karnā — "to deny; to refuse".
اس نے چوری سے انکار کیا۔ us ne chorī se inkār kiyā. He denied the theft.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے انکار کیا, us ne inkār kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
انکار کرنا (inkār karnā) is built from inkār, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic inkār, "a denial", and کرنا. What is denied takes سے: چوری سے انکار کیا.

Lesson 4 of 5.
معلومات (mālūmāt) — information.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an admission, then the Urdu for to put off.

You'll want to know: معلومات.
معلومات — mālūmāt — "information".
صحافی کے پاس نئی معلومات تھیں۔ sahāfī ke pās naī mālūmāt thīṅ. The journalist had new information.
Feminine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
معلومات (mālūmāt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maʿlūmāt, "things known", a plural. Urdu treats it as feminine plural: نئی معلومات تھیں.

The word, taken apart.
حقیقت (haqīqat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥaqīqa, "the truth of a thing", a cousin of حق. حقیقت میں is in reality.
