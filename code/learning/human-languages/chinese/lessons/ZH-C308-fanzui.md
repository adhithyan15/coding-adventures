## You'll want to know: fànzuì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C308-SINCE308-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fànzuì* — "to commit a crime".

> *tā shuō tā méiyǒu fànzuì* — He says he hasn't committed any crime.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fànzuì* is built from *fàn* 'to violate' and *zuì* 'a crime', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjomX* and *dzwojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to trespass' and 'to violate' and records that the second was once written with another character.
