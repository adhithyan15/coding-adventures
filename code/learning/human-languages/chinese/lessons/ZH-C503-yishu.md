## You'll want to know: yìshù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C503-ART503-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yìshù* — "art; the arts".

> *tā zuì xǐhuan yìshù* — Art is what she loves most.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìshù* is built from *yì* 'a skill' and *shù* 'a technique', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjiejH* and *zywit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'ability' and the second 'technique'.
