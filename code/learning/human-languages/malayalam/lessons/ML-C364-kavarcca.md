# കവർച്ച (kavarcca) — a robbery

## You'll want to know: കവർച്ച
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C364-KALLAN364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**കവർച്ച** — *kavarcca* — "a robbery".

> കവലയിലെ കടയിൽ ഒരു കവർച്ച നടന്നു.
>
> *kavalayile kaṭayil oru kavarcca naṭannu.*
>
> A robbery took place at the shop at the junction.

A robbery takes place: കവർച്ച നടന്നു. Stronger than a theft: someone takes by force.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കവർച്ച** (*kavarcca*) is native Dravidian, the noun of കവരുക (*kavaruka*), "to plunder, to rob" (DEDR 1326), which DEDR sets beside Tamil *kavar*, "to seize, to grasp".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
