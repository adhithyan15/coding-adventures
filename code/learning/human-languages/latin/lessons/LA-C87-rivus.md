## You'll want to know: rīvus, rīvī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C87-AQUA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rīvus, rīvī** — "a stream". English *rival*: people who share a stream.

## What you've built

One more word for river, lake, stream, spring, coast.
