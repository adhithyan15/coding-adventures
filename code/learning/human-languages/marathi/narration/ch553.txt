Marathi, chapter 553: Reaching the Goal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साध्य करणे (sādhya karṇe) — to achieve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to break down, then the Marathi for to come loose.

You'll want to know: साध्य करणे.
साध्य करणे — sādhya karṇe — "to achieve".
खूप कष्ट करून तिने तिचं ध्येय साध्य केलं. khūp kaṣṭa karūn tine ticaṁ dhyey sādhya kelaṁ. With much hard work she achieved her goal.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ध्येय साध्य केलं, tyāne dhyey sādhya kelaṁ.

The word, taken apart.
साध्य करणे (sādhya karṇe) is the tatsama साध्य, from Sanskrit sādhya, "to be accomplished" (Monier-Williams), with करणे.

Lesson 2 of 5.
हुलकावणी देणे (hulkāvṇī deṇe) — to elude; to give the slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to come loose, then the Marathi for to achieve.

You'll want to know: हुलकावणी देणे.
हुलकावणी देणे — hulkāvṇī deṇe — "to elude; to give the slip".
यशाने त्याला दोनदा हुलकावणी दिली. yaśāne tyālā dondā hulkāvṇī dilī. Success eluded him twice.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हुलकावणी दिली, tyāne hulkāvṇī dilī.
The one dodged takes -ला: त्याला हुलकावणी दिली.

The word, taken apart.
हुलकावणी देणे (hulkāvṇī deṇe) is हुलकावणी, "a dodge, a feint", with देणे; the origin of हुलकावणी is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अचूक (acūk) — accurate; unerring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to achieve, then the Marathi for to elude.

You'll want to know: अचूक.
अचूक — acūk — "accurate; unerring".
त्याचं उत्तर अगदी अचूक होतं. tyācaṁ uttar agdī acūk hotaṁ. His answer was quite accurate.
Built on चूक, a mistake: without a mistake.

The word, taken apart.
अचूक (acūk) is built on चूक, "a mistake", with a-, "without"; CDIAL lists Marathi cukṇẽ, "to blunder", under cukk (CDIAL 4848).

Lesson 4 of 5.
हमखास (hamkhās) — without fail; surely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to elude, then the Marathi for accurate.

You'll want to know: हमखास.
हमखास — hamkhās — "without fail; surely".
तो दर रविवारी हमखास आईला फोन करतो. to dar ravivārī hamkhās āīlā phon karto. He calls his mother every Sunday without fail.

The word, taken apart.
हमखास (hamkhās) is a loan from Persian, built from ham, "also", and Arabic khāṣṣ, "special".
