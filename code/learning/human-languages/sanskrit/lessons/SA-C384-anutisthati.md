## You'll want to know: अनुतिष्ठति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C384-UDYAMA384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुतिष्ठति** — *anutiṣṭhati* — "carries out".

> यदि सः योजनाम् अनुतिष्ठति, तर्हि लाभः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi saḥ yojanām anutiṣṭhati, tarhi lābhaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If he carries out the plan, there will be a gain.

Past (he …): सः योजनाम् अनुष्ठितवान्, *saḥ yojanām anuṣṭhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुतिष्ठति** (*anutiṣṭhati*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives *anu-ṣṭhā* "to carry out, to perform, to practise" and *anu-ṣṭhita* "done, practised" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
