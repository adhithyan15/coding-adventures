## You'll want to know: سحر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C344-HIKAYA344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سحر** — *saḥara* — "to enchant, to bewitch".

> سحرت الموسيقى الجمهور.
>
> *saḥarat al-mūsīqā al-jumhūr.*
>
> The music enchanted the audience.

Present: يسحر, *yasḥar*.

The noun سحر, *siḥr*, magic, is spelled the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سحر** (*saḥara*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ḥ-r* (Lane), "to enchant"; *siḥr* is magic, and *saḥar*, the time before dawn, is spelled the same.
