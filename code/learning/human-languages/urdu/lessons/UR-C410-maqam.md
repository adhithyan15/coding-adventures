## You'll want to know: مقام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C410-IMARAT410-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مقام** — *maqām* — "a spot; a place".

> یہ مقام پہاڑوں کے بیچ ہے۔
>
> *yih maqām pahāṛoṅ ke bīch hai.*
>
> This spot lies among the mountains.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقام** (*maqām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maqām*, "a place where one stands"; **مقامی**, "local", is built on it.
