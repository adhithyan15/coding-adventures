## You'll want to know: remédier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C353-REMEDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**remédier** — "to remedy, to put right".

> Il faut remédier à ce problème rapidement.
>
> *This problem has to be put right quickly.*

*Remédier à quelque chose*, with *à*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remédier* is a learned borrowing from Latin *remediāre* "to cure" (EtymDB), from *remedium* "a cure", built on *medērī* "to heal" (Lewis & Short).
