## You'll want to know: поражение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C510-RESULTS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поражение** (*porazhéniye*) — "a defeat".

> После поражения игроки долго молчали.
>
> *Pósle porazhéniya igrokí dólgo molcháli.*
>
> After the defeat the players were silent for a long time.

Neuter. *Потерпеть поражение* — to suffer a defeat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поражение* is built on *поразить* 'to strike, to defeat', from *разить* 'to strike'; EtymDB lists it, glossed 'defeat', without a source.
