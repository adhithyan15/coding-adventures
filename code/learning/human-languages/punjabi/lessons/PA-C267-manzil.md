# ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ (manzil) — a storey; a floor of a building

## You'll want to know: ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C267-OFFICE267-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ** — *manzil* — "a storey; a floor of a building".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਦੂਜੀ ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā daftar dūjī manzil utte hai.*
>
> My office is on the second floor.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ** (*manzil*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *manzil*, "a halting place".
