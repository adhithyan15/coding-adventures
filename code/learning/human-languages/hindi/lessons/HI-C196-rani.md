## You'll want to know: रानी

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रानी** (*rānī*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *rājñī*, "queen" (CDIAL 10692).
