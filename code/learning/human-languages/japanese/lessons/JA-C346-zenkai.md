## You'll want to know: ぜんかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C346-KONDO346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんかい** — *zenkai* — "last time; the previous time".

> **ぜんかいより はやく ついたら、えきで まって ください。** — *zenkai yori hayaku tsuitara, eki de matte kudasai* — "If you arrive earlier than last time, please wait at the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんかい** (*zenkai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zen*, "before" (Middle Chinese *dzen*) and *kai*, "-times" (*hwoj*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
