# fēriātus, fēriāta, fēriātum — on holiday, keeping holiday

## You'll want to know: fēriātus, fēriāta, fēriātum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C538-OTIU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**fēriātus, fēriāta, fēriātum** — "on holiday, keeping holiday".

> Puerī fēriātī tōtum diem lūdunt.
>
> *The boys on holiday play all day.*

The participle of *fērior* — to keep holiday, from *fēriae*, already known.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fēriātus** is the participle of *fērior* "to keep holiday", built on *fēriae* "holidays" (Lewis & Short).
