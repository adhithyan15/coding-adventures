## You'll want to know: чудовище
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C320-VOLSHEBNIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**чудовище** (*chudóvishche*) — "a monster".

> Чудовище жило в глубокой пещере.
>
> *Chudóvishche zhílo v glubókoy peshchére.*
>
> The monster lived in a deep cave.

Neuter, though it ends in *-е*: *страшное чудовище*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*чудовище* is built on *чудо* "a wonder, a marvel", an inherited Slavic word, with the ending *-овище*; EtymDB lists no parent.
