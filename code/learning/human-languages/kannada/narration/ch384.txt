Kannada, chapter 384: Grasping an Idea.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗ್ರಹಿಸು (grahisu) — to grasp; to comprehend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for impossible, then the Kannada for to deny.

You'll want to know: ಗ್ರಹಿಸು.
ಗ್ರಹಿಸು — grahisu — "to grasp; to comprehend".
ಮಗು ಪಾಠವನ್ನು ಬೇಗ ಗ್ರಹಿಸಿತು. magu pāṭhavannu bēga grahisitu. The child grasped the lesson quickly.
Past (I …): ಗ್ರಹಿಸಿದೆ, grahiside.
ಗ್ರಹಿಕೆ, understanding.

The word, taken apart.
ಗ್ರಹಿಸು (grahisu) is built on Sanskrit grahaṇa, "seizing, grasping" (CDIAL 4364), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to grasp, then the Kannada for an interpretation.

You'll want to know: ಕಲ್ಪನೆ.
ಕಲ್ಪನೆ — kalpane — "a notion; an idea".
ಇದು ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಕಲ್ಪನೆ. idu oḷḷeya kalpane. This is a good idea.
Also imagination: ಕಲ್ಪನೆಯ ಕಥೆ, a made-up story.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಲ್ಪನೆ (kalpane) is borrowed from Sanskrit kalpanā, "making, forming, arranging" (Monier-Williams), from kḷp, "to be fit, to arrange".
