Hindi, chapter 368: Making Things Better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सुधरना (sudharnā) — to get better, to mend one's ways.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for criticism, then the Hindi for to endure.

You'll want to know: सुधरना.
सुधरना — sudharnā — "to get better, to mend one's ways".
दवा के बाद उसकी तबीयत सुधर रही है। davā ke bād uskī tabīyat sudhar rahī hai. After the medicine his health is getting better.
Past (he …): वह सुधर गया, vah sudhar gayā.

The word, taken apart.
सुधरना (sudharnā) is inherited from śuddhakāra, "making clean", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12521).

Lesson 5 of 5.
निखारना (nikhārnā) — to polish (a skill), to bring out the best in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for advancement, then the Hindi for discipline.

You'll want to know: निखारना.
निखारना — nikhārnā — "to polish (a skill), to bring out the best in".
अभ्यास से वह अपनी हिंदी निखार रहा है। abhyās se vah apnī hindī nikhār rahā hai. With practice he is polishing his Hindi.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना गाना निखारा, usne apnā gānā nikhārā.

The word, taken apart.
निखारना (nikhārnā) is inherited from niḥkṣārayati, "causes to flow out", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7098). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to clarify, to strain"; polishing a skill is not among its glosses, and may be the same idea of clearing.
