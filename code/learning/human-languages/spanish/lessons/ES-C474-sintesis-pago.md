# (el curso que paga la empresa) — match it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-02, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el importe** and **de golpe**. The exchange below turns on
whether the second applies to the first.

## Reading: the exchange, and the statement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-03, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-04] -->

> — El setenta por ciento. El resto lo pones tú, pero te lo **descuentan** en
> doce meses, no de **golpe**.
> — Ah, eso no lo sabía.

And the statement you have to match to it:

> Pensaba que había que pagar el **importe** completo de una vez.

## Grammar Lens: the match is between two phrases, not two words
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-02, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-04] -->

This is a matching task, so nothing is quoted back at you. The speaker and the
statement describe the same fact in different words, and you have to see it.

| the speaker says | the statement says |
|---|---|
| no **de golpe** | **de una vez** |
| te lo descuentan en doce meses | el importe completo |

Neither phrase appears in the other. *De golpe* and *de una vez* are the pair
that carries the match, and if you only hold one of them the item is closed to
you even though you understood every sentence.

That is worth knowing in general: a matching task rewards **paraphrase**, so
the words to learn are the ones that come in pairs.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-01, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-02, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-03, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say what the company pays and what happens to the rest, using
*descontar*.

[PAUSE 3s] Now say the misunderstanding twice — once with *de golpe* and once
with *de una vez* — and hear that they are the same claim.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-01, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-02, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-03, ES-LEX-C474-PAGO-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What happens to the rest? (**Te lo descuentan en doce meses**.) Not
how? (**De golpe**.) What did the listener think? (**Que había que pagar el
importe completo de una vez**.)
