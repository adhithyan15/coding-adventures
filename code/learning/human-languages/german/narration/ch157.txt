Lesson 1 of 5.
die Tablette — a tablet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a cough, then the German for the flu.

You'll want to know: die Tablette.
die Tablette — "a tablet".

The word, taken apart.
Tablette from French tablette, "a little tablet" (EtymDB 2.1), a diminutive of the word that goes back to Latin tabula, "board" — borrowed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the flu, then the German for a tablet.

You'll want to know: die Versicherung.
die Versicherung — "insurance".

The word, taken apart.
Versicherung is the noun of the verb "to insure" (EtymDB 2.1), built from ver- + sicher, "safe", which goes back to Latin sēcūrus: insurance makes you safe.
