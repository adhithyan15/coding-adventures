Spanish, chapter 610: Convivir.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la tolerancia — the tolerance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to face, then the Spanish for to persevere.

You'll want to know: la tolerancia.
la tolerancia — "the tolerance".
La tolerancia es importante en una sociedad. Tolerance is important in a society.
Tolerante — tolerant.

The word, taken apart.
tolerancia is a learned borrowing from Latin tolerantia 'endurance', built on tolerare 'to bear' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
