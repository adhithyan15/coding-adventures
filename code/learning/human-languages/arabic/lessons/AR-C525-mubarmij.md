## You'll want to know: مبرمج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C525-TASMIM525-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مبرمج** — *mubarmij* — "a programmer".

> تعمل أختي مبرمجة في بنك.
>
> *taʿmalu ukhtī mubarmija fī bank.*
>
> My sister works as a programmer in a bank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مبرمج** (*mubarmij*) is the participle of *barmaja*, "to program", a modern verb built from *barnāmaj*, "a programme", which is usually traced to Persian *barnāma*.
