## You'll want to know: योगायोग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C550-NASHIB550-01]; assesses=[] -->

**योगायोग** — *yogāyog* — "a coincidence".

> बाजारात जुना मित्र भेटला, हा मोठा योगायोग होता.
>
> *bājārāt junā mitra bheṭlā, hā moṭhā yogāyog hotā.*
>
> Meeting an old friend in the market was a great coincidence.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**योगायोग** (*yogāyog*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *yogāyoga*, *yoga*, "joining", with *ayoga*, its opposite, glossed as fitness and unfitness (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for things that happen to meet.
