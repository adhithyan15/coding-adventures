Sanskrit, chapter 314: A Brave Deed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शौर्यम् (śauryam) — valour; heroism.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for surrounds, then the Sanskrit for quells.

You'll want to know: शौर्यम्.
शौर्यम् — śauryam — "valour; heroism".
शौर्येण सः आहताय औषधम् आनयति। śauryeṇa saḥ āhatāya auṣadham ānayati. With great valour he brings medicine to the injured man.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शौर्यम् (śauryam) is usually traced to śūra, "a hero", with the lengthened first vowel; MW gives śaurya as heroism, valour, prowess (MW).

धीरः सैनिकः न बिभेति। dhīraḥ sainikaḥ na bibheti. The steadfast soldier is not afraid.

The word, taken apart.
धीरः (dhīraḥ) MW gives dhīra as steady, firm, resolute, brave, and suggests, with a question mark, the root √dhṛ, "to hold", or √dhā, "to put", so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: कीर्तिः.
कीर्तिः — kīrtiḥ — "fame; renown".
तस्य कीर्तिः सर्वत्र प्रसरति। tasya kīrtiḥ sarvatra prasarati. His fame spreads everywhere.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कीर्तिः (kīrtiḥ) comes from the root that MW numbers √2. kṛ, "to mention, praise"; MW gives kīrti as mention, report and good report, fame (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: गौरवम्.
गौरवम् — gauravam — "honour; respect".
ग्रामः तस्मै गौरवं ददाति। grāmaḥ tasmai gauravaṃ dadāti. The village honours him.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
गौरवम् (gauravam) is built on guru, "heavy, venerable", with the lengthened first vowel; MW gives gaurava as weight, venerableness, and respect shown to a person (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अभिनन्दति (abhinandati) — congratulates; hails.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for fame, then the Sanskrit for honour.

You'll want to know: अभिनन्दति.
अभिनन्दति — abhinandati — "congratulates; hails".
राजा वीरम् अभिनन्दति। rājā vīram abhinandati. The king congratulates the hero.
Past (he …): सः वीरम् अभिनन्दितवान्, saḥ vīram abhinanditavān.

The word, taken apart.
अभिनन्दति (abhinandati) is built from abhi and the root √nand, "to rejoice", as in nandati; MW gives abhi-nand as to rejoice at, welcome, greet, hail, praise (MW).
