## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-KE-01, GU-JOIN-VICHAARUN-KE-01, GU-JOIN-JAANUN-KE-01, GU-SCRIPT-KE-01] -->

- **કે** — the word that puts a whole sentence **inside** another.
- **વિચારું છું કે …** — an opinion rather than a fact.
- **જાણું છું કે …** — and, negated, *jāṇto nathī*, "I don't know".
- **કે** on the page, on two signs that were already yours.

Two chapters ago you could not say *and*. You can now hand an entire sentence to
a verb.
