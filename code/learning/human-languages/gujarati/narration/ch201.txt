Gujarati, chapter 201: Getting things mended.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સુધારવું (sudhārvũ) — to mend, to repair, to correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to put in, then the Gujarati for to register.

You'll want to know: સુધારવું.
સુધારવું — sudhārvũ — "to mend, to repair, to correct".
તે જૂની સાઈકલ સુધારે છે. — te jūnī saikal sudhāre che — "He repairs the old bicycle."

The word, taken apart.
સુધારવું (sudhārvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL traces it to śuddhakāra, "making clean", built from Sanskrit śuddhá, "clean", and kāra, "making" (CDIAL 12521).

Lesson 2 of 5.
મોચી (mocī) — cobbler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to register, then the Gujarati for to mend.

You'll want to know: મોચી.
મોચી — mocī — "cobbler".
મોચીએ મારા બૂટ સુધાર્યા. — mocīe mārā būṭ sudhāryā — "The cobbler mended my shoes."

The word, taken apart.
મોચી (mocī) comes from Sanskrit mōcika, "tanner and cobbler", built on mōca, "shoe", which CDIAL takes from Iranian, as in Pahlavi mōč, "shoe"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "cobbler" (CDIAL 10349).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ધોબી (dhobī) — washerman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to mend, then the Gujarati for cobbler.

You'll want to know: ધોબી.
ધોબી — dhobī — "washerman".
ધોબી ગલીના નાકે છે. — dhobī galīnā nāke che — "The washerman is at the end of the lane."

The word, taken apart.
ધોબી (dhobī) comes from dhauvati, "washes", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit dhautá, "washed". CDIAL says the forms with -b- began in the east, and that Hindi dhobī spread from there into the other languages, Gujarati among them, so the Gujarati word is a loan from Hindi (CDIAL 6886).

Lesson 4 of 5.
હજામ (hajām) — barber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for cobbler, then the Gujarati for washerman.

You'll want to know: હજામ.
હજામ — hajām — "barber".
હજામની દુકાન ચોકમાં છે. — hajāmnī dukān cokmā̃ che — "The barber's shop is in the square."

The word, taken apart.
હજામ (hajām) is a loan from Arabic ḥajjām, "cupper", one who let blood as a cure, which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḥ-j-m (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
સુથાર (suthār) — carpenter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for washerman, then the Gujarati for barber.

You'll want to know: સુથાર.
સુથાર — suthār — "carpenter".
સુથારે નવું બારણું બનાવ્યું. — suthāre navũ bārṇũ banāvyũ — "The carpenter made a new door."

The word, taken apart.
સુથાર (suthār) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit sūtradhāra, "carpenter", built from sū́tra, "thread, line", and dhāra, "holding", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "carpenter" (CDIAL 13563).
