# राज्य करणे (rājya karṇe) — to reign

## You'll want to know: राज्य करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C325-RAJA325-05]; assesses=[] -->

**राज्य करणे** — *rājya karṇe* — "to reign".

> त्या राजाने चाळीस वर्षं राज्य केलं.
>
> *tyā rājāne cāḷīs varṣaṁ rājya kelaṁ.*
>
> That king reigned for forty years.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने राज्य केलं, *tyāne rājya kelaṁ*.

The land ruled takes -वर: देशावर राज्य करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राज्य करणे** (*rājya karṇe*) joins *rājya*, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *rājya*, 'kingship, a kingdom' (Monier-Williams), to *karṇe*, 'to do'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
