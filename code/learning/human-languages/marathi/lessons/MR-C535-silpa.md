## You'll want to know: शिल्प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C535-CHITRA535-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्प** — *śilpa* — "a sculpture".

> बागेत दगडाचं एक मोठं शिल्प आहे.
>
> *bāget dagḍācaṁ ek moṭhaṁ śilpa āhe.*
>
> There is a big stone sculpture in the garden.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्प** (*śilpa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śilpa*, "artistic work, a craft" (Monier-Williams).
