# પેઢી (peḍhī) — a generation

## You'll want to know: પેઢી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C493-PEDHI493-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પેઢી** — *peḍhī* — "a generation".

> અમારા કુટુંબની ચાર પેઢી આ ગામમાં રહી છે.
>
> *amārā kuṭumbnī cār peḍhī ā gāmmā̃ rahī che.*
>
> Four generations of our family have lived in this village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પેઢી** (*peḍhī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pīṭha*, "stool, bench"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "step of a ladder" (CDIAL 8222). The sense "a generation", one step in a family line, is probably a later use of the same word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
