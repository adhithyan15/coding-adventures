## You'll want to know: तुळसी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C168-SURAJ168-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तुळसी** — *tuḷsī* — "holy basil".

> म्हारी तुळसी हरी अर सुंदर है।
>
> *mhārī tuḷsī harī ar sundar hai.*
>
> My holy basil is green and beautiful.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तुळसी** (*tuḷsī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tulasī*, "the sacred basil plant"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *tuḷsī* and Hindi *tulsī* (CDIAL 5885). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
