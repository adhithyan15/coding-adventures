Bengali, chapter 368: On One Condition.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
শর্ত (shôrtô) — a condition; a term (of an agreement).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an expectation, then the Bengali for the future.

You'll want to know: শর্ত.
শর্ত — shôrtô — "a condition; a term (of an agreement)".
আমার একটা শর্ত আছে: তুমি যদি আসো, তাহলে আমিও যাব। āmār ekṭā shôrtô āchhe: tumi jôdi āso, tāhôle āmio jābô. I have one condition: if you come, I'll go too.

The word, taken apart.
শর্ত (shôrtô) is usually traced to Arabic sharṭ, a condition, through Persian: the 'if' a deal depends on.

You'll want to know: অবস্থা.
অবস্থা — ôbôsthā — "a state; a condition (of something)".
রাস্তার অবস্থা খারাপ হলে আমরা বাসে যাব না। rāstār ôbôsthā khārāp hôle āmrā bāse jābô nā. If the roads are in a bad state, we won't go by bus.

The word, taken apart.
অবস্থা (ôbôsthā) is Sanskrit avasthā, a state or condition (Monier-Williams): how a thing or a person is at present.

Lesson 3 of 5.
পরিস্থিতি (pôristhiti) — a situation; circumstances.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a condition, then the Bengali for a state of things.

You'll want to know: পরিস্থিতি.
পরিস্থিতি — pôristhiti — "a situation; circumstances".
পরিস্থিতি বদলালে আমাকে ফোন করো। pôristhiti bôdlāle āmāke phon kôro. Call me if the situation changes.

The word, taken apart.
পরিস্থিতি (pôristhiti) is a modern learned compound of Sanskrit pari-, around, and sthiti, a standing or state (Monier-Williams): how things stand all around. The compound itself is not in the dictionary.

Lesson 4 of 5.
যতক্ষণ (jôtôkkhôn) — as long as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a state of things, then the Bengali for a situation.

You'll want to know: যতক্ষণ.
যতক্ষণ — jôtôkkhôn — "as long as".
ঝড় না থামলে আমরা যতক্ষণ দরকার এখানে থাকব। jhôṛ nā thāmle āmrā jôtôkkhôn dôrkār ekhāne thākbô. If the storm doesn't stop, we'll stay here as long as we need to.

The word, taken apart.
যতক্ষণ (jôtôkkhôn) is যত, 'as much as', with Sanskrit kṣaṇa, a moment (Monier-Williams): for as many moments as. It is often answered by ততক্ষণ, 'for that long'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
অথবা (ôthôbā) — or else; or otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a situation, then the Bengali for as long as.

You'll want to know: অথবা.
অথবা — ôthôbā — "or else; or otherwise".
কাল ছুটি হলে আমরা সিনেমায় যাব, অথবা বাড়িতে থাকব। kāl chhuṭi hôle āmrā sinemāy jābô, ôthôbā bāṛite thākbô. If tomorrow is a holiday, we'll go to the cinema or else stay at home.

The word, taken apart.
অথবা (ôthôbā) is Sanskrit athavā, 'or' (Monier-Williams). It is more weighty than বা and often offers a second plan.
