## You'll want to know: معتدل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C477-SHARJI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**معتدل** (*mo'tadel*) — "mild, temperate".

> هوای بهار در این شهر معتدل است.
>
> *Havâ-ye bahâr dar in shahr mo'tadel ast.*
>
> The spring weather in this town is mild.

Also of people: moderate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mo'tadel* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *muʿtadil* 'balanced, moderate'. EtymDB does not list it.
