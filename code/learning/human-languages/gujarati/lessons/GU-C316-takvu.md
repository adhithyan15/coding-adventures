## You'll want to know: ટકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C316-MUJAB316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ટકવું** — *ṭakvũ* — "to last; to hold out".

> હડતાળ ત્રણ દિવસ ટકી.
>
> *haḍtāḷ traṇ divas ṭakī.*
>
> The strike lasted three days.

Past (it …): તે મકાન ટક્યું, *te makān ṭakyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટકવું** (*ṭakvũ*) comes from *\*ṭakk*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati *ṭakvũ*, "to stop" (CDIAL 5420); today it is to stay standing, to last: **ત્રણ દિવસ ટકી**.
