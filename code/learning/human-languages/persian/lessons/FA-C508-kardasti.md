## You'll want to know: کاردستی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C508-CRAFTS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کاردستی** (*kârdasti*) — "a handicraft, craft work".

> بچه ها در مدرسه کاردستی درست کردند.
>
> *bachchehâ dar madrese kârdasti dorost kardand.*
>
> The children made crafts at school.

*kâr* 'work' + *dast* 'hand' + *-i*: handwork.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kârdasti* is built on native Persian *kâr*, 'work', and *dast*, 'hand', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), with the ending *-i*.
