## You'll want to know: húmedo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C696-CLIMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**húmedo** — "humid, damp".

> El aire es muy húmedo en la costa.
>
> *The air is very humid on the coast.*

*Ropa húmeda* — damp clothes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*húmedo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *hūmidus* "moist, damp" (EtymDB).
