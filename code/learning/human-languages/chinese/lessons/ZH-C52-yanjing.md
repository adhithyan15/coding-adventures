## You'll want to know: yǎnjing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C52-BODY52-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnjing* — "eye".

> *tā de yǎnjing hěn hǎokàn* — She has lovely eyes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnjing* is built from yǎn 'eye' and jing 'eyeball', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngeanX* and *tsjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
