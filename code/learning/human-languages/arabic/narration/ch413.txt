Arabic, chapter 413: Change over time.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
تطور (taṭawwara) — to evolve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to reduce, then the Arabic for a proportion.

You'll want to know: تطور.
تطور — taṭawwara — "to evolve".
تطورت المدينة كثيرا. taṭawwarat al-madīna kathīran. The city has evolved a lot.
Present: يتطور, yataṭawwar.

The word, taken apart.
تطور (taṭawwara) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-w-r, "a time, a stage" (Lane). It is Form V, to pass from stage to stage: to evolve.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a proportion, then the Arabic for to evolve.

You'll want to know: نما.
نما — namā — "to grow".
نما الاقتصاد هذه السنة. namā al-iqtiṣād hādhihi as-sana. The economy grew this year.
Present: ينمو, yanmū.

The word, taken apart.
نما (namā) is built on the native Arabic root n-m-w, usually traced to the sense "to grow, to increase". Namā is a Form I verb with a weak last letter: to grow. Its present is yanmū.
