## You'll want to know: предсказывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C380-BUDUSHCHEYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**предсказывать** (*predskázyvat*) — "to predict, to forecast".

> Трудно предсказывать будущее.
>
> *Trúdno predskázyvat búdushcheye.*
>
> It is hard to predict the future.

Imperfective; perfective *предсказать* (*предскажу, предскажешь*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**предсказывать** is built from *пред-* "before" and *сказать* "to say", on *казать*, Proto-Slavic *\*kazati* (EtymDB): to say a thing before it happens.
