## You'll want to know: patefaciō, patefacere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C302-SPECULATOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**patefaciō, patefacere** — "to open up; to disclose".

> Ūna epistula tōtum cōnsilium patefēcit.
>
> *A single letter disclosed the whole plan.*

*patefaciō, patefacere, patefēcī, patefactum*. Cicero uses it of plots brought to light.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**patefaciō** joins *pateō*, "to be open", and *faciō*, "to make" (EtymDB): to make open.
