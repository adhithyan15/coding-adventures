Lesson 1 of 5.
la boîte — a box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a present, then the French for glasses.

You'll want to know: la boîte.
la boîte — "a box".

The word, taken apart.
boîte from Old French boiste from Vulgar Latin buxita, from Late Latin buxis, "a box" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) The circumflex marks the lost s.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a suitcase, then the French for a ticket; a banknote.

You'll want to know: la carte.
la carte — "a map; a card".

The word, taken apart.
carte from Latin charta, "a sheet of paper", from Greek chartes (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing: an inherited form would begin with ch-. The worn-down form gave French its word for a charter.
