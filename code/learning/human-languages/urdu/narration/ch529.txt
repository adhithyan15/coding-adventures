Urdu, chapter 529: A Tangle of Troubles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جھمیلا (jhamelā) — a tiresome muddle; a hassle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an offering, then the Urdu for to bestow favour on.

You'll want to know: جھمیلا.
جھمیلا — jhamelā — "a tiresome muddle; a hassle".
اگر ٹکٹ کھو گیا تو بڑا جھمیلا ہو گا۔ agar ṭikaṭ kho gayā to baṛā jhamelā ho gā. If the ticket gets lost, it will be a real hassle.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
جھمیلا (jhamelā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhamelā, "wrangling", under jhamm, "entanglement", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5340).

Lesson 2 of 5.
مخمصہ (makhmasā) — a quandary; a fix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to bestow favour on, then the Urdu for a tiresome muddle.

You'll want to know: مخمصہ.
مخمصہ — makhmasā — "a quandary; a fix".
اگر دونوں دوست کل آئے تو مخمصہ ہو گا۔ agar donoṅ dost kal āe to makhmasā ho gā. If both friends come tomorrow, I'll be in a fix.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مخمصہ (makhmasā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic makhmaṣa, "hunger, a hard plight"; the sense "a quandary" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.

Lesson 5 of 5.
الجھانا (uljhānā) — to tangle; to confuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an inner struggle, then the Urdu for complicated.

You'll want to know: الجھانا.
الجھانا — uljhānā — "to tangle; to confuse".
اگر بلی نے اون الجھائی تو مشکل ہو گی۔ agar billī ne ūn uljhāī to mushkil ho gī. If the cat tangles the wool, there will be trouble.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دھاگا الجھایا, us ne dhāgā uljhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
الجھانا (uljhānā) is the causative of ulajhnā, "to get tangled", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit uparundhati, "obstructs" (CDIAL 2221).
