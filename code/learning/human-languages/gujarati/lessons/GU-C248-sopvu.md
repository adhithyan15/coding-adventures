## You'll want to know: સોંપવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C248-OFFICE248-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સોંપવું** — *sõpvũ* — "to hand over, to entrust".

> **સાહેબ મને નવું કામ સોંપે છે.** — *sāheb mane navũ kām sõpe che* — "The boss hands me a new task."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સોંપવું** (*sõpvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *sámarpayati*, "delivers over"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "hands over" (CDIAL 13192).
