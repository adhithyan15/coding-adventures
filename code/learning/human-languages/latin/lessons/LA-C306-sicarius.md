## You'll want to know: sīcārius, sīcāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C306-CAEDES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sīcārius, sīcāriī** — "an assassin".

> Sīcāriī cōnsulem in viā exspectābant.
>
> *The assassins were waiting for the consul in the street.*

Masculine. Murder trials at Rome were held *inter sīcāriōs*: among the assassins.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sīcārius** is built on *sīca*, "a curved dagger" (Lewis & Short): the dagger man.
