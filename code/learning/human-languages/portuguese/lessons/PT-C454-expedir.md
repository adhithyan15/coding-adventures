## You'll want to know: expedir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C454-CORREIOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**expedir** — "to dispatch, to send off".

> A loja expediu a encomenda ontem.
>
> *The shop dispatched the parcel yesterday.*

*A expedição* — the dispatch, the shipping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*expedir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *expedīre* 'to free the feet, to get ready' (Lewis & Short), on *pēs* 'foot'.
