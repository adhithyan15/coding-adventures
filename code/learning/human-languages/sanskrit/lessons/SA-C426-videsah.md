# विदेशः (videśaḥ) — a foreign country; abroad

## You'll want to know: विदेशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C426-VIDESHA426-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विदेशः** — *videśaḥ* — "a foreign country; abroad".

> मम भ्राता विदेशे वसति।
>
> *mama bhrātā videśe vasati.*
>
> My brother lives abroad.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विदेशः** (*videśaḥ*) is built from *vi*, "apart, away", and *deśa*, "a country"; MW gives *videśa* as another country, a foreign land, abroad (MW).
