## You'll want to know: としより
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C292-JINSEI292-02]; assesses=[] -->

**としより** — *toshiyori* — "an old person; the elderly".

> **その まちには としよりが おおいです。** — *sono machi ni wa toshiyori ga ōi desu* — "There are many old people in that town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**としより** (*toshiyori*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *toshi*, "years", and *yori*, "gathering" (from *yoru*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
