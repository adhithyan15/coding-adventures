## You'll want to know: निमंत्रण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C114-SORRY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निमंत्रण** (*nimantraṇ*) — "an invitation", the formal word.

**निमंत्रण के लिए धन्यवाद** — "thank you for the invitation", with **के लिए** and **धन्यवाद**.

A written invitation has the three parts of the short message you learned to write: the line
that names the reader, **प्रिय मीरा,**; the sentences under it; and the writer's name at the foot.
