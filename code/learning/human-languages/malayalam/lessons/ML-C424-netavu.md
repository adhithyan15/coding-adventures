## You'll want to know: നേതാവ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C424-ADHIKARAM424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**നേതാവ്** — *nētāvŭ* — "a leader".

> ഗ്രാമത്തിലെ ആളുകൾ അവനെ നേതാവായി തിരഞ്ഞെടുത്തു.
>
> *grāmattile āḷukaḷ avane nētāvāyi tiraññeṭuttu.*
>
> The people of the village chose him as their leader.

Plural: നേതാക്കൾ, *nētākkaḷ*.

Plural: നേതാക്കൾ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നേതാവ്** (*nētāvŭ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *netṛ*, "leading, a leader" (Monier-Williams), whose nominative is *netā*, with the Malayalam ending -വ്.
