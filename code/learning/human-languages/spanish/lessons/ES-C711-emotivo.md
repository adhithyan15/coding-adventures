## You'll want to know: emotivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C711-EMOCION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**emotivo** — "moving, emotional".

> Fue una boda muy emotiva.
>
> *It was a very moving wedding.*

*Emotivo* describes the moment; *emocionado* describes the person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emotivo* is usually explained as a learned word built on Latin *ēmōtus* "stirred up", the participle of *ēmovēre*, from *movēre* "to move".
