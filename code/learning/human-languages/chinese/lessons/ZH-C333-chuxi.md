## You'll want to know: Chúxī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C333-EVE333-02]; assesses=[] -->

*Chúxī* — "lunar New Year's Eve".

> *Chúxī de shíhou wǒmen dōu huí jiā* — On the eve of the lunar new year we all go home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Chúxī* is built from *chú* 'to clear away' and *xī* 'dusk', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjo* and *zjek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'steps' and 'to remove', and the second 'dusk'.
