## You'll want to know: نادى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C310-NAJDA310-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نادى** — *nādā* — "to call out to".

> نادت الأم ابنها من الشرفة.
>
> *nādat al-umm ibnahā min ash-shurfa.*
>
> The mother called to her son from the balcony.

Present: ينادي, *yunādī*.

Not the taught نادي, a club: the final letter tells them apart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نادى** (*nādā*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-d-w* (Lane), "to call; to meet together", the root of the taught *nādī*, a club. It is Form III, "to call to someone".
