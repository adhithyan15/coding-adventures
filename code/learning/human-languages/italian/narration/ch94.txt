Italian, chapter 94: Rug, Television, Computer, Fridge, Oven.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il tappeto — a rug, a carpet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a ship, then the Italian for a boat.

You'll want to know: il tappeto.
il tappeto — "a rug, a carpet".

The word, taken apart.
tappeto from Latin tapete, "a carpet, a hanging" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a television, then the Italian for a computer.

You'll want to know: il frigorifero.
il frigorifero — "a fridge".

The word, taken apart.
frigorifero is built from Latin frigus, "cold", and ferre, "to carry" (Lewis & Short): a bringer of cold. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for it.
