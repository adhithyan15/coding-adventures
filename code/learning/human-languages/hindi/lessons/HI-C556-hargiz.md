## You'll want to know: हरगिज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C556-SANDEH556-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हरगिज़** — *hargiz* — "ever; (with नहीं) never ever".

> मैं हरगिज़ झूठ नहीं बोलूँगा।
>
> *Maiṁ hargiz jhūṭh nahīṃ bolūṅgā.*
>
> I will never, ever lie.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हरगिज़** (*hargiz*) is usually traced to Persian *hargiz*, "ever".
