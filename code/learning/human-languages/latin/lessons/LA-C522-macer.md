## You'll want to know: macer, macra, macrum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C522-PALL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**macer, macra, macrum** — "thin, lean".

> Canis macer cibum quaerit.
>
> *The thin dog looks for food.*

Like *ruber*: *macer*, *macra*, *macrum*. English *meagre* comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**macer** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European word meaning "thin" (EtymDB), and German *mager* "lean" is a cousin (Lewis & Short). English *meagre* comes from it through French.
