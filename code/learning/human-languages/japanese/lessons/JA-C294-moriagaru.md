## You'll want to know: もりあがる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C294-MIKOSHI294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**もりあがる** — *moriagaru* — "to liven up; to get going".

> **ゆうがたに なって、まつりは もりあがりました。** — *yūgata ni natte, matsuri wa moriagarimashita* — "As evening came on, the festival really got going."

Polite **もりあがります**, *moriagarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **まつりが もりあがりました。**, *matsuri ga moriagarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もりあがる** (*moriagaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *mori*, "heaping" (from *moru*), and *agaru*, "to rise" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

One more word for telling what happened.
