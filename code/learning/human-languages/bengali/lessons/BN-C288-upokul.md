# উপকূল (upôkūl) — a coast

## You'll want to know: উপকূল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C288-GHURNIJHOR288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**উপকূল** — *upôkūl* — "a coast".

> ঘূর্ণিঝড়ের আগে উপকূলের মানুষ গ্রাম ছেড়ে চলে গেল।
>
> *ghūrnijhôṛer āge upôkūler mānush grām chheṛe chôle gelô.*
>
> Before the cyclone the people of the coast left their villages.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপকূল** (*upôkūl*) is Sanskrit *upakūla*, 'being on the shore' (Monier-Williams): *upa-*, 'near', and *kūla*, 'a bank'.
