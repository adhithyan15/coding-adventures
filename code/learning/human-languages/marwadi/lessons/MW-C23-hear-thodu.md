> *thoṛu* — **a little**

Two beats. The first is the breathed *th* of *ṭhelā*; the middle carries the
flapped *ṛ* you already say inside *kapṛā* and *ghoṛo*.

Hold it against *ghaṇo*, which you learned four lessons ago. *ghaṇo* makes a
word bigger; *thoṛu* makes an amount smaller. A seller hears the difference at
once, and it is the difference between complaining about a price and offering
to meet it.
