## You'll want to know: अनुमती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C557-KUVAT557-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमती** — *anumatī* — "formal permission".

> शिक्षकांची अनुमती घेऊन मी वर्गाबाहेर गेलो.
>
> *śikṣakāñcī anumatī gheūn mī vargābāher gelo.*
>
> With the teacher's permission I went out of the class.

Feminine.

अनुमती is the formal word beside परवानगी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमती** (*anumatī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *anumati*, "assent, permission" (Monier-Williams).
