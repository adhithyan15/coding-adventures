Bengali, chapter 87: Twenty, Thirty, Forty, Fifty, Hundred.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
কুড়ি (kuṛi) — twenty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for eight, then the Bengali for ten.

You'll want to know: কুড়ি.
কুড়ি — kuṛi — "twenty".

The word, taken apart.
কুড়ি (kuṛi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from koḍi, "a score, twenty", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3503). CDIAL reports Przyluski's view that the word is Austroasiatic, perhaps first a word for "man", twenty being the count of a person's fingers and toes.

You'll want to know: একশো.
একশো — ekshô — "a hundred".

The word, taken apart.
একশো (ekshô) is ek, "one", from Sanskrit ēka (CDIAL 2462), + shô, "hundred", which CDIAL lists as Bengali sa under Sanskrit śata, "hundred" (CDIAL 12278).
