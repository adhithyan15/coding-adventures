Gujarati, chapter 416: Areas and Places.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
કેન્દ્ર (kendra) — a centre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a clock tower, then the Gujarati for to hunt for.

You'll want to know: કેન્દ્ર.
કેન્દ્ર — kendra — "a centre".
શહેરના કેન્દ્રમાં મોટું બજાર છે. śahernā kendramā̃ moṭũ bajār che. There is a big market in the centre of the city.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
કેન્દ્ર (kendra) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kendra, "the centre of a circle", a word Sanskrit itself took from Greek (Monier-Williams).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: વિસ્તાર.
વિસ્તાર — vistār — "an area; a part of a town".
આ વિસ્તારમાં ઘણી દુકાનો છે. ā vistārmā̃ ghaṇī dukāno che. There are many shops in this area.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વિસ્તાર (vistār) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vistāra, "spreading, extent" (Monier-Williams): the stretch of ground a place spreads over.

Warm-up.

અમારું ઘર શહેરના પરામાં છે. amārũ ghar śahernā parāmā̃ che. Our house is in a suburb of the city.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પરું (parũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit pura, "a town, a fortress"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a suburb" (CDIAL 8278).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ભાગોળ (bhāgoḷ) — the outskirts of a village (by its gate).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an area, then the Gujarati for a suburb.

You'll want to know: ભાગોળ.
ભાગોળ — bhāgoḷ — "the outskirts of a village (by its gate)".
સાંજે ગામની ભાગોળે બાળકો રમે છે. sā̃je gāmnī bhāgoḷe bāḷko rame che. In the evening children play at the edge of the village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભાગોળ (bhāgoḷ) goes back to bhagnatōlī, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for the gate in a village wall and the ground around it (CDIAL 9352).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ભમવું.
ભમવું — bhamvũ — "to roam; to travel about".
તે ઘણાં શહેરોમાં ભમ્યો. te ghaṇā̃ śaheromā̃ bhamyo. He roamed about many cities.
Past (he …): તે ઘણું ભમ્યો, te ghaṇũ bhamyo.

The word, taken apart.
ભમવું (bhamvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit bhramati, "wanders, roams"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 9648).
