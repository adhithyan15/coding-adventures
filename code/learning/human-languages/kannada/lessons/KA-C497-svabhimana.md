# ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ (svābhimāna) — self-respect

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-03, KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for arrogance, then the Kannada for modesty.

## You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ** — *svābhimāna* — "self-respect".

> ಅವನು ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನದಿಂದ ಆ ಹಣವನ್ನು ನಿರಾಕರಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *avanu svābhimānadinda ā haṇavannu nirākarisidanu.*
>
> Out of self-respect he refused that money.

ಸ್ವ, self + ಅಭಿಮಾನ, pride: the word from earlier in this chapter, turned on yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ** (*svābhimāna*) is a compound built from Sanskrit: *sva*, "own, self", and *abhimāna*, "pride" (CDIAL 524).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
