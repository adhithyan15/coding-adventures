## You'll want to know: सचोटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C366-MULYA366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सचोटी** — *sacoṭī* — "integrity; honesty".

> तो दुकानदार सचोटीने व्यवहार करतो.
>
> *to dukāndār sacoṭīne vyavhār karto.*
>
> That shopkeeper deals with integrity.

Feminine.

सचोटीने, honestly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सचोटी** (*sacoṭī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *saċoṭī*, "honesty", under Sanskrit *satyavṛtti*, "devotion to truth" (CDIAL 13115).
