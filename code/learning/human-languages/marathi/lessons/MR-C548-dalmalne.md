# डळमळणे (ḍaḷmaḷṇe) — to waver; to wobble

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C548-ADTHALA548-03, MR-LEX-C548-ADTHALA548-04] -->

## You'll want to know: डळमळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C548-ADTHALA548-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डळमळणे** — *ḍaḷmaḷṇe* — "to waver; to wobble".

> अडचणी आल्या तरी त्याचा निर्धार डळमळला नाही.
>
> *aḍcaṇī ālyā tarī tyācā nirdhār ḍaḷmaḷlā nāhī.*
>
> Even when difficulties came, his determination did not waver.

Past (he …): तो डळमळला, *to ḍaḷmaḷlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डळमळणे** (*ḍaḷmaḷṇe*) is an expressive Marathi verb for wobbling; its origin is not settled.
