Sanskrit, chapter 367: Is It Possible?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सम्भवः (sambhavaḥ) — a possibility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for considers, then the Sanskrit for ascertains.

You'll want to know: सम्भवः.
सम्भवः — sambhavaḥ — "a possibility".
यदि धनम् अस्ति, तर्हि यात्रायाः सम्भवः अस्ति। yadi dhanam asti, tarhi yātrāyāḥ sambhavaḥ asti. If there is money, a journey is possible.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सम्भवः (sambhavaḥ) is built on sam, "together", and the root √bhū, "to be"; MW gives sam-bhava "capacity, ability, possibility" (MW).

You'll want to know: सम्भावयति.
सम्भावयति — sambhāvayati — "thinks likely; supposes".
यदि आकाशः कृष्णः, तर्हि अहं वर्षां सम्भावयामि। yadi ākāśaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ, tarhi ahaṃ varṣāṃ sambhāvayāmi. If the sky is dark, I think rain likely.
Past (he …): सः वर्षां सम्भावितवान्, saḥ varṣāṃ sambhāvitavān.

The word, taken apart.
सम्भावयति (sambhāvayati) is built on sam and the root √bhū, "to be", in its causative form; MW gives the senses "to suggest a possibility" and "to suppose anything possible" (MW).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: क्षिप्रम्.
क्षिप्रम् — kṣipram — "swiftly; without delay".
यदि वायुः वहति, तर्हि क्षिप्रं वर्षा भविष्यति। yadi vāyuḥ vahati, tarhi kṣipraṃ varṣā bhaviṣyati. If the wind blows, rain will come swiftly.

The word, taken apart.
क्षिप्रम् (kṣipram) comes from the root √kṣip, "to throw" (CDIAL 3688 sets kṣiprá under it); MW gives kṣipra "springing" and "quick, speedy, swift", and kṣipram "quickly, immediately" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अपेक्षते (apekṣate) — expects.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for for certain, then the Sanskrit for swiftly.

You'll want to know: अपेक्षते.
अपेक्षते — apekṣate — "expects".
यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि कृषकः उत्तमं धान्यम् अपेक्षते। yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi kṛṣakaḥ uttamaṃ dhānyam apekṣate. If it rains, the farmer expects a good harvest.
Past (he …): सः उत्तरम् अपेक्षितवान्, saḥ uttaram apekṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अपेक्षते (apekṣate) is built on apa, "away", and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives apekṣ "to look for, to wait for, to expect" (MW).
