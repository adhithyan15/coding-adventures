## You'll want to know: por fim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C269-PRIMEIRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**por fim** — "finally, in the end".

> Esperámos duas horas e, por fim, o comboio chegou.
>
> *We waited two hours and in the end the train arrived.*

Close to *finalmente*; it closes a list of steps or a story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fim* is inherited from Latin *fīnis* "boundary, end" (EtymDB); *por* comes from Latin *pro* "for, in front of".
