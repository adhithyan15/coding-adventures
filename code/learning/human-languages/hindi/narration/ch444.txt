Hindi, chapter 444: Harbours and Bays.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
बंदरगाह (bandargāh) — a harbour, a port.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to irrigate, then the Hindi for to weed.

You'll want to know: बंदरगाह.
बंदरगाह — bandargāh — "a harbour, a port".
बंदरगाह पर बड़े जहाज़ खड़े थे। bandargāh par baṛe jahāz khaṛe the. Big ships were standing in the harbour.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
बंदरगाह (bandargāh) is usually traced to Persian bandargāh, from bandar, "a port", and -gāh, "a place".

लंगर — laṅgar — "an anchor; a free kitchen".
जहाज़ का लंगर बहुत भारी था। jahāz kā laṅgar bahut bhārī thā. The ship's anchor was very heavy.
Masculine.
At a gurdwara, the लंगर (laṅgar) is the free kitchen where everyone eats together.

The word, taken apart.
लंगर (laṅgar) is usually traced to Persian langar, "an anchor", and also a place where food is given out; both senses live in Hindi.

Lesson 3 of 6.
खाड़ी (khāṛī) — a bay, a gulf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a harbour, then the Hindi for an anchor.

You'll want to know: खाड़ी.
खाड़ी — khāṛī — "a bay, a gulf".
हमारा गाँव खाड़ी के किनारे है। hamārā gā̃v khāṛī ke kināre hai. Our village is on the shore of the bay.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
खाड़ी (khāṛī) goes back to khaḍḍa, "a hole, a pit", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3790), which lists Hindi khāṛī, "a creek, an inlet": the hollow the sea fills.

तट — taṭ — "a coast; a bank".
हम समुद्र के तट पर टहले। ham samudra ke taṭ par ṭahle. We strolled along the seashore.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
तट (taṭ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit taṭa, "a slope, a bank, a shore" (Monier-Williams): the formal partner of किनारा.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a bay, then the Hindi for a coast.

You'll want to know: डुबकी.
डुबकी — ḍubkī — "a dip (in water)".
ठंडे पानी में एक डुबकी के बाद मैं काँप रहा था। ṭhaṇḍe pānī meṁ ek ḍubkī ke bād maiṁ kā̃p rahā thā. After one dip in the cold water I was shivering.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
डुबकी (ḍubkī) is built on the stem of डूबना, "to sink", from ḍubb, "sink", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5561); CDIAL does not list the noun itself.
