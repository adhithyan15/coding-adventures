Spanish, chapter 486: En la consulta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
afeitarse — to shave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to dry, then the Spanish for the rubbish.

You'll want to know: afeitarse.
afeitarse — "to shave".
Mi padre se afeita cada mañana. My father shaves every morning.
Reflexive, because you shave yourself.

The word, taken apart.
afeitar from Latin affectare, "to strive after" (Lewis & Short). In Spanish it first meant "to adorn, to make up", and only later "to shave".

toser from Latin tussire, "to cough" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited: the u became o. La tos, "the cough", is Latin tussis.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: la aspirina.
la aspirina — "the aspirin".
Tómate una aspirina con agua. Take an aspirin with water.

The word, taken apart.
aspirina from German Aspirin, a brand name coined by the Bayer company in 1899: a- for acetyl, -spir- for Spiraea, the meadowsweet plant, and the chemists' ending -in.

The word, taken apart.
vacuna is built on vacuno, "of cattle" (EtymDB 2.1), from Latin vacca, "cow": the first vaccine, in 1796, was made from cowpox.
