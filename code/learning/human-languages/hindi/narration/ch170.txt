Hindi, chapter 170: To die, To live, To pass (of time), To lift, To tell (a story).
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मरना (marnā) — to die.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to save, then the Hindi for to escape.

You'll want to know: मरना.
मरना — marnā — "to die".

The word, taken apart.
मरना (marnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from marate, "will die" (CDIAL 9871). CDIAL calls direct descent from this old form possible, even probable.

Lesson 4 of 5.
उठाना (uṭhānā) — to lift, to pick up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to live, then the Hindi for to pass.

You'll want to know: उठाना.
उठाना — uṭhānā — "to lift, to pick up".

The word, taken apart.
उठाना (uṭhānā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ut-sthāpayati, "raises", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 1903). It is built on sthā-, "to stand": to lift is to make something stand up.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुनाना (sunānā) — to tell (a story), to recite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to pass, then the Hindi for to lift.

You'll want to know: सुनाना.
सुनाना — sunānā — "to tell (a story), to recite".

The word, taken apart.
सुनाना (sunānā) is the causing form of sunnā, "to hear": to tell a story is to make someone hear it. sunnā comes from Sanskrit śṛṇoti, "hears" (CDIAL 12598).
