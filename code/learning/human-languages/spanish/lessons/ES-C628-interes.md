## You'll want to know: el interés
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C628-BANCO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el interés** — "the interest".

> El banco cobra un interés alto.
>
> *The bank charges high interest.*

Also interest in a subject: *tengo interés en la historia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*interés* is a learned borrowing from Latin *interesse* 'to be between, to make a difference', built from *inter* and *esse* 'to be' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
