Russian, chapter 124: Purple, Brown, Grey, Orange, Busy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
фиолетовый (fiolétovyy) — purple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for stairs, then the Russian for pink.
