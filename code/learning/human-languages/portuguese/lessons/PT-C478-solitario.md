## You'll want to know: solitário
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C478-SOLIDAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**solitário** — "lonely, solitary".

> É um homem solitário que vive no campo.
>
> *He is a solitary man who lives in the country.*

Also a noun: *um solitário* — a loner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solitário* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sōlitārius* "lonely, solitary" (EtymDB), from *sōlus* "alone".
