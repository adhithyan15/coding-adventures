## You'll want to know: वसवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C327-PRAJA327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वसवणे** — *vasavṇe* — "to found a settlement".

> नदीकाठी राजाने एक नवं शहर वसवलं.
>
> *nadīkāṭhī rājāne ek navaṁ śahar vasavlaṁ.*
>
> The king founded a new city on the riverbank.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वसवलं, *tyāne vasavlaṁ*.

Transitive: शहर वसवणे, to found a town; the place itself वसतो, settles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वसवणे** (*vasavṇe*) is a Marathi causative, 'to make settle', built from the inherited stem *vas-* with *-av-*: CDIAL lists Marathi *vasṇẽ*, 'settles', under Sanskrit *vasati*, 'stays, dwells' (CDIAL 11435).
