## You'll want to know: ನೆರೆಯವರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C90-KIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೆರೆಯವರು** (*nereyavaru*) — "the neighbours, the people next door", from **ನೆರೆ**
(*nere*), "nearby, next door".

## What you've built

The people who are close without being family.
