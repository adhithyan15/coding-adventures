Punjabi, chapter 214: Are You a Traveller?.
5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
Return to a familiar question — retrieve the familiar what question and traveller by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: tuhāḍā nāṁ kī hai?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ?, "what is your name?"

Grammar Lens.
In that question ਕੀ means "what", and it sits where the answer will go. You also know ਯਾਤਰੀ, "a traveller", and ਤੁਸੀਂ … ਹੋ, "you are".

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: what is your name?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ?
[your turn — hear: a traveller]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਯਾਤਰੀ.
[your turn — hear: how are you?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਹੋ?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say what, then a traveller.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? (kī tusīṁ yātrī ho?) — are you a traveller?

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say what is your name? Then you are a traveller: ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ.

Grammar Lens: ਕੀ at the front.
Put ਕੀ in front of a statement and it becomes a yes-or-no question. The voice rises at the end:
ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ। — tusīṁ yātrī ho. — You are a traveller.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? — kī tusīṁ yātrī ho? — Are you a traveller?
ਕੀ ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ? — kī bāgh khullhā hai? — Is the garden open?
Same word, two jobs. In ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ? it stands where the answer goes, and means "what". At the very front it asks only yes or no.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: is he a traveller?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਉਹ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੈ?
[your turn — hear: is the garden closed?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਬਾਗ਼ ਬੰਦ ਹੈ?
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? (kī tusīṁ yātrī ho?)]
Point to ਯ in ਯਾਤਰੀ and say ya.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? (kī tusīṁ yātrī ho?) once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
ਕੀ at the front: what does it ask? (Yes or no.) In the middle? (What.)

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ — yes, no.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say are you a traveller? Then yes, no, and the polite ਜੀ.

Grammar Lens: answering.
Answer with ਹਾਂ ਜੀ or ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, then say the whole sentence back. A no answer puts ਨਹੀਂ before the verb, as you already do:
ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹਾਂ। — hāṁ jī, maiṁ yātrī hāṁ. — Yes, I am a traveller.
ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹਾਂ। — nahīṁ jī, maiṁ yātrī nahīṁ hāṁ. — No, I am not a traveller.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: is the garden open? Yes.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ।.
[your turn — hear: is the garden closed? No.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਬਾਗ਼ ਬੰਦ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹਾਂ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਹਾਂ ਜੀ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Are you a traveller? Answer it truthfully, in a whole sentence.
