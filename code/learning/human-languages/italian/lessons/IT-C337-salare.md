## You'll want to know: salare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C337-CONDIRE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**salare** — "to salt".

> Hai salato l'acqua della pasta?
>
> *Have you salted the pasta water?*

*Salato* — salty; also expensive: *il conto è salato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*salare* is built on *sale* "salt", from Latin *sāl* (EtymDB).
