## You'll want to know: தாடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C480-MUKAM480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தாடை** — *tāṭai* — "the jaw".

> பல் வலியால் என் தாடை வீங்கியது.
>
> *pal valiyāl eṉ tāṭai vīṅkiyatu.*
>
> My jaw swelled up because of a toothache.

தாடை and தாடி "beard" are different words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **தாடை** (*tāṭai*) is not settled; we could not find it in DEDR.

## What you've built
