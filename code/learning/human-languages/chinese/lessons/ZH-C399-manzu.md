## You'll want to know: mǎnzú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C399-ONLYIF399-04]; assesses=[] -->

*mǎnzú* — "to meet (a requirement); to satisfy".

> *zhǐyào mǎnzú zhèxiē yāoqiú nǐ jiù néng qù* — As long as you meet these requirements, you can go.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mǎnzú* is built from *mǎn* 'full' and *zú* 'enough', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *manX* and *tsjowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'brimming' and the second 'the foot' and 'full'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
