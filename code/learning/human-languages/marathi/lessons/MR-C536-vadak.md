## You'll want to know: वादक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C536-GAYAN536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वादक** — *vādak* — "an instrumentalist".

> तबला वादकाने गायकाला सुंदर साथ दिली.
>
> *tablā vādakāne gāyakālā sundar sāth dilī.*
>
> The tabla player accompanied the singer beautifully.

Masculine.

साथ देणे, to accompany.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वादक** (*vādak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vādaka*, "one who makes sound, a player" (Monier-Williams).
