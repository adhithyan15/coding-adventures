## You'll want to know: ख़बरदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C379-YADI379-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़बरदार** — *khabardār* — "beware!, watch out".

> ख़बरदार, अगर तुम फिर देर से आए तो दरवाज़ा बंद मिलेगा।
>
> *khabardār, agar tum phir der se āe to darvāzā band milegā.*
>
> Watch out: if you come late again, you'll find the door shut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़बरदार** (*khabardār*) is usually traced to Persian *khabardār*, "aware, informed". It joins Arabic *khabar*, "news", to Persian *-dār*, "holding": one who has been told.
