Kannada, chapter 465: Town and Capital.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪಟ್ಟಣ (paṭṭaṇa) — a small town; a town.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a gratuity, then the Kannada for to order.

You'll want to know: ಪಟ್ಟಣ.
ಪಟ್ಟಣ — paṭṭaṇa — "a small town; a town".
ನನ್ನ ಊರು ಒಂದು ಚಿಕ್ಕ ಪಟ್ಟಣ. nanna ūru ondu cikka paṭṭaṇa. My home town is a small town.
Plural: ಪಟ್ಟಣಗಳು, paṭṭaṇagaḷu.
Bigger than a ಹಳ್ಳಿ, smaller than a ನಗರ.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಟ್ಟಣ (paṭṭaṇa) comes from Sanskrit paṭṭana, "a town" (CDIAL 7705); Turner notes that the Sanskrit word itself was probably taken from a Dravidian source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ರಾಜಧಾನಿ (rājadhāni) — a capital city.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to order, then the Kannada for a small town.

You'll want to know: ರಾಜಧಾನಿ.
ರಾಜಧಾನಿ — rājadhāni — "a capital city".
ಪ್ರತಿ ರಾಜ್ಯಕ್ಕೆ ಒಂದು ರಾಜಧಾನಿ ಇದೆ. prati rājyakke ondu rājadhāni ide. Every state has a capital.
ರಾಜ, a king, plus ಧಾನಿ, a seat: where the ruler sits.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಜಧಾನಿ (rājadhāni) is borrowed from Sanskrit rājadhānī, "a king's seat, a capital" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪ್ರದೇಶ (pradēśa) — a region; an area.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a small town, then the Kannada for a capital city.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರದೇಶ.
ಪ್ರದೇಶ — pradēśa — "a region; an area".
ಈ ಪ್ರದೇಶದಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಕಾಡು ಇದೆ. ī pradēśadalli tumbā kāḍu ide. There is a lot of forest in this region.
ಗ್ರಾಮೀಣ ಪ್ರದೇಶ (pradēśa), a rural area.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರದೇಶ (pradēśa) is borrowed from Sanskrit pradēśa, "a region, a country" (CDIAL 8668), built on dēśa, the source of ದೇಶ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸ್ಥಳ (sthaḷa) — a place; a site.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a capital city, then the Kannada for a region.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಥಳ.
ಸ್ಥಳ — sthaḷa — "a place; a site".
ಊಟಕ್ಕೆ ನಾವು ಒಂದು ಶಾಂತ ಸ್ಥಳ ಹುಡುಕಿದೆವು. ūṭakke nāvu ondu śānta sthaḷa huḍukidevu. We looked for a quiet place to eat.
ಸ್ಥಳೀಯ, local, is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಥಳ (sthaḷa) is borrowed from Sanskrit sthala, "dry land, ground, a place" (CDIAL 13744); Kannada writes its l as ಳ.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕೇಂದ್ರ (kēndra) — a centre; a hub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a region, then the Kannada for a place.

You'll want to know: ಕೇಂದ್ರ.
ಕೇಂದ್ರ — kēndra — "a centre; a hub".
ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ನಗರದ ಕೇಂದ್ರದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. bas nildāṇa nagarada kēndradalli ide. The bus station is in the centre of the city.
ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಕೇಂದ್ರ (kēndra), an information centre.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೇಂದ್ರ (kēndra) is borrowed from Sanskrit kendra, "the centre of a circle", which Sanskrit itself took from Greek kentron (Monier-Williams).
