## You'll want to know: సూత్రం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C390-SIDDHANTAM390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సూత్రం** — *sūtraṁ* — "a principle; a formula".

> గణితంలో ప్రతి సూత్రానికి ఒక కారణం ఉంటుంది.
>
> *gaṇitaṁlō prati sūtrāniki oka kāraṇaṁ uṇṭundi.*
>
> Every formula in mathematics has a reason behind it.

Plural: సూత్రాలు, *sūtrālu*.

First a thread, then a short rule that holds a lot together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సూత్రం** (*sūtraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sūtra*, "a thread, a cord" (CDIAL 13561), which Sanskrit also used for a short rule or formula (Monier-Williams).
