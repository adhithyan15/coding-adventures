## You'll want to know: चक्कर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C419-TIRAHA419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्कर** — *cakkar* — "a round; a detour; dizziness".

> ग़लत रास्ते की वजह से हमें लंबा चक्कर लगाना पड़ा।
>
> *ġalat rāste kī vajah se hamẽ lambā cakkar lagānā paṛā.*
>
> Because of the wrong road we had to go a long way round.

Masculine.

चक्कर आना, to feel dizzy: मुझे चक्कर आ रहा है.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्कर** (*cakkar*) is a semi-learned form of Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel, a circle" (CDIAL 4538); the fully inherited Hindi form in that entry is *cāk*, "a wheel, a millstone". A चक्कर is any going round: a detour, a round of the market, a spinning head.
