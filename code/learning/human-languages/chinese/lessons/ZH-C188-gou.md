## You'll want to know: gòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C188-MONEY188-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gòu* — "enough".

> *wǒ de qián bú gòu* — I haven't got enough money.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gòu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'many'. The dictionary lists it with a level tone only, so the falling tone of gòu is not the regular outcome, and how it came is not settled.
