## You'll want to know: torris, torris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C317-INCENDIUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**torris, torris** — "a firebrand, a burning log".

> Servus torrem ē focō rapuit.
>
> *A slave snatched a burning log from the hearth.*

Masculine, 3rd declension: a piece of wood still burning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**torris** belongs with *torreō*, "to scorch, to roast" (Lewis & Short): the scorching log.
