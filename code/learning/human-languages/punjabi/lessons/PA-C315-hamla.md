## You'll want to know: ਹਮਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C315-BANDIT315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਮਲਾ** — *hamlā* — "an attack".

> ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬੱਸ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਮਲਾ ਹੋਇਆ।
>
> *rāt nū̃ bass utte hamlā hoiā.*
>
> At night there was an attack on the bus.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਮਲਾ** (*hamlā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ḥamla*, "an attack, a charge".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
