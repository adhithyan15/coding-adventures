German, chapter 513: Klavier und Geige.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
das Klavier — piano.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for ski run, then the German for ski lift.

You'll want to know: das Klavier.
das Klavier — "piano".
Meine Tochter spielt seit drei Jahren Klavier. My daughter has been playing the piano for three years.
Plural: die Klaviere. No article in Klavier spielen.

The word, taken apart.
Klavier was borrowed from French clavier "keyboard", which is built on Latin clāvis "key" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 6.
die Flöte — flute; recorder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for piano, then the German for violin.

You'll want to know: die Flöte.
die Flöte — "flute; recorder".
In der Grundschule lernen viele Kinder Flöte. Many children learn the recorder at primary school.
Plural: die Flöten. A recorder is die Blockflöte.

The word, taken apart.
Flöte goes back to Middle High German floite, borrowed from Old French flaüte, whose own origin is not settled; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.

Lesson 4 of 6.
das Schlagzeug — drums, drum kit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for violin, then the German for flute.

You'll want to know: das Schlagzeug.
das Schlagzeug — "drums, drum kit".
Der Nachbar übt jeden Abend Schlagzeug. The neighbour practises the drums every evening.
Plural: die Schlagzeuge. One drum is die Trommel.

The word, taken apart.
Schlagzeug is built on Schlag "blow", from Proto-Germanic slagiz, and Zeug "gear, stuff", from Middle High German ziuc (EtymDB): the kit you strike.

Lesson 5 of 6.
trommeln — to drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for flute, then the German for drums.

You'll want to know: trommeln.
trommeln — "to drum".
Der Regen trommelt auf das Dach. The rain drums on the roof.
Regular: hat getrommelt. Built on die Trommel — drum.

The word, taken apart.
trommeln is built on Trommel "drum" (EtymDB lists the noun), usually traced to Middle High German trumme, an imitative word for the sound of a drum.
