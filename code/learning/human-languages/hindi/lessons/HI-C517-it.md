## You'll want to know: ईंट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C517-EENT517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ईंट** — *ī̃ṭ* — "a brick".

> मज़दूर ईंटें सिर पर उठाकर ले जा रहे हैं।
>
> *Mazdūr ī̃ṭeṁ sir par uṭhākar le jā rahe haiṁ.*
>
> The labourers are carrying bricks on their heads.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ईंट** (*ī̃ṭ*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *iṣṭakā*, "a brick" (CDIAL 1600).
