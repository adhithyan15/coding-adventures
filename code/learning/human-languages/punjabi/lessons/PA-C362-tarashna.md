## You'll want to know: ਤਰਾਸ਼ਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C362-SKILL362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਰਾਸ਼ਣਾ** — *tarāshṇā* — "to carve; to cut to shape".

> ਤਰਖਾਣ ਲੱਕੜ ਤਰਾਸ਼ਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *tarkhāṇ lakkaṛ tarāshdā hai.*
>
> The carpenter carves the wood.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਲੱਕੜ ਤਰਾਸ਼ੀ, *maiṁ lakkaṛ tarāshī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਰਾਸ਼ਣਾ** (*tarāshṇā*) is a verb made in Punjabi from a loan, usually traced to Persian *tarāsh*, "cutting".
