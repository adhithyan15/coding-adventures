## You'll want to know: संकल्प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C546-NISHCHAY546-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संकल्प** — *saṅkalpa* — "a resolution; a vow".

> नवीन वर्षी मी रोज चालण्याचा संकल्प केला.
>
> *navīn varṣī mī roj cālṇyācā saṅkalpa kelā.*
>
> In the new year I resolved to walk every day.

Masculine.

संकल्प करणे, to make a resolution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकल्प** (*saṅkalpa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṁkalpa*, "resolve, intention" (Monier-Williams).
