## You'll want to know: aos poucos
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C274-OUTRORA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**aos poucos** — "little by little, bit by bit".

> Aos poucos, aprendi a nadar.
>
> *Little by little I learned to swim.*

Also *pouco a pouco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pouco* is inherited from Latin *paucus* "few, little" (Lewis & Short); *aos* is *a* with the article *os*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
