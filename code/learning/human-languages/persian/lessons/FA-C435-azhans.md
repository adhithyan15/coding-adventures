## You'll want to know: آژانس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C435-DARBAST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**آژانس** (*âzhâns*) — "a car service, a minicab office; an agency".

> شب دیر بود و آژانس گرفتیم.
>
> *Shab dir bud va âzhâns gereftim.*
>
> It was late at night and we called a car service.

*Âzhâns-e mosâferati* — a travel agency.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âzhâns* was borrowed from French *agence* 'agency' (EtymDB).
