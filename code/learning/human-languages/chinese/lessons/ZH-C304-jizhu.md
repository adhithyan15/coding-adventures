## You'll want to know: jìzhù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C304-SCHOOL304-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jìzhù* — "to commit to memory; to keep in mind".

> *wǒ jìzhù le tā de diànhuà hàomǎ* — I memorized his phone number.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìzhù* is built from 记 'to note down' and *zhù* 'to stay', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kiH* and *drjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to stop': noted so that it stays.
