## You'll want to know: der Samen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C504-SAEEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Samen** — "seed".

> Die Samen brauchen viel Wasser und Sonne.
>
> *The seeds need lots of water and sun.*

Plural: *die Samen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Samen* is inherited from Middle High German *sām* (EtymDB), built on the same Germanic root as *säen* "to sow".
