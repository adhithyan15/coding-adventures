## You'll want to know: зато
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C347-ODNAKO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**зато** (*zató*) — "but then, on the other hand".

> Квартира маленькая, зато в центре.
>
> *Kvartíra málenkaya, zató v tséntre.*
>
> The flat is small, but then it is in the centre.

It brings in a plus that makes up for a minus: *дорого, зато удобно*. Often *но зато*. Stress on the second syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**зато** is fused from *за* "for, in return for" and *то* "that": the good side you get in return for the bad.
