## You'll want to know: कुशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C446-SHIKHARA446-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुशः** — *kuśaḥ* — "kusha grass".

> नदीतीरे कुशाः वर्धन्ते।
>
> *nadītīre kuśāḥ vardhante.*
>
> Kusha grass grows on the riverbank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुशः** (*kuśaḥ*) is the word MW gives for the sacred grass of the rites, *Poa cynosuroides*; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
