## You'll want to know: せんりつ

**せんりつ** — *senritsu* — "a melody; a tune".

> **この うたの せんりつは うつくしいです。** — *kono uta no senritsu wa utsukushii desu* — "The melody of this song is beautiful."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんりつ** (*senritsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "rotation" (Middle Chinese *zjwen*) and *ritsu*, "rhythm" (*lwit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
