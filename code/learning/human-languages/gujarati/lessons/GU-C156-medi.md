# મેંદી (mẽdī) — henna

## You'll want to know: મેંદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C156-ADORN156-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મેંદી** — *mẽdī* — "henna".

> **તહેવારમાં છોકરીઓ મેંદી લગાડે છે.** — *tahevārmā̃ chokrīo mẽdī lagāḍe che* — "Girls put on henna at festivals."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મેંદી** (*mẽdī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *mēndhī*, *mēndhikā*, the plant *Lawsonia alba*, which gives henna, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with that sense (CDIAL 10328).
