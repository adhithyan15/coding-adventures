## You'll want to know: das Polizeirevier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C439-VIERTEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Polizeirevier** — "the police station".

> Nach dem Diebstahl geht er zum Polizeirevier.
>
> *After the theft he goes to the police station.*

Plural: *die Polizeireviere*. Also *die Polizeiwache* or *die Wache*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Polizeirevier* is built from *Polizei* 'police', from Latin *polītīa* 'the state, government' (EtymDB), and *Revier* 'district', which goes back to Middle High German *riviere* 'hunting ground' (EtymDB), usually traced to French.
