# నిరుద్యోగం (nirudyōgaṁ) — unemployment

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C404-PEDARIKAM404-03, TE-LEX-C404-PEDARIKAM404-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for wealth, then the Telugu for independent.

## You'll want to know: నిరుద్యోగం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C404-PEDARIKAM404-05]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరుద్యోగం** — *nirudyōgaṁ* — "unemployment".

> మా ప్రాంతంలో నిరుద్యోగం పెరిగింది.
>
> *mā prāntaṁlō nirudyōgaṁ perigindi.*
>
> Unemployment has risen in our area.

నిర్-, without, + ఉద్యోగం, a job, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరుద్యోగం** (*nirudyōgaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *nir-*, "without", and *udyoga*, "exertion, undertaking" (Monier-Williams), the word Telugu has as ఉద్యోగం, a job.
