## You'll want to know: shuā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C521-CHORES521-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shuā* — "to scrub; to brush clean".

> *wǒ shuā le yí ge xiǎoshí de guō* — I spent an hour scrubbing the pot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuā* is inherited from Middle Chinese *srwaet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to wipe clean'; the dictionary also lists *srjwet*, glossed 'to sweep'. The level tone of shuā cannot be read off *srwaet*: words that once ended in t, as this one did, are spread over all four Mandarin tones.
