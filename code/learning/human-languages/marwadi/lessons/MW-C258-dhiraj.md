## You'll want to know: धीरज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C258-CHINTA258-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धीरज** — *dhīraj* — "patience".

> धीरज राखो।
>
> *dhīraj rākho.*
>
> Be patient.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धीरज** (*dhīraj*) goes back to Sanskrit *dhairya*, "firmness, patience" (Monier-Williams), in a spoken shape.
