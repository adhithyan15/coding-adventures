Marathi, chapter 461: Hurt Feelings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अपमान (apmān) — an insult; humiliation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for closeness, then the Marathi for beloved.

You'll want to know: अपमान.
अपमान — apmān — "an insult; humiliation".
सगळ्यांसमोर झालेला अपमान तो विसरला नाही. sagḷyāṁsamor jhālelā apmān to visarlā nāhī. He did not forget being humiliated in front of everyone.
Masculine.
अपमान (apmān) करणे, to insult; अपमान (apmān) होणे, to be humiliated.

The word, taken apart.
अपमान (apmān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit apamāna, "disrespect, disgrace" (CDIAL 433).

Before the new one: say the Marathi for beloved, then the Marathi for an insult.

You'll want to know: टोमणा.
टोमणा — ṭomṇā — "a taunt; a dig".
शेजारच्या बाईंनी आईला टोमणा मारला. śejārcyā bāīnnī āīlā ṭomṇā mārlā. The lady next door made a dig at Mother.
Masculine.
टोमणा (ṭomṇā) मारणे, to make a dig: the target takes -ला.

The word, taken apart.
टोमणा (ṭomṇā) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled; CDIAL lists a Marathi ṭumṇī, "goading", under trumpati (CDIAL 6070), but does not list this word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
तिरस्कार (tiraskār) — contempt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an insult, then the Marathi for a taunt.

You'll want to know: तिरस्कार.
तिरस्कार — tiraskār — "contempt".
त्याला खोटेपणाचा तिरस्कार वाटतो. tyālā khoṭepaṇācā tiraskār vāṭto. He feels contempt for dishonesty.
Masculine.
तिरस्कार (tiraskār) वाटणे, to feel contempt, with the dative; तिरस्कार (tiraskār) करणे, to scorn.

The word, taken apart.
तिरस्कार (tiraskār) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit tiraskāra, "placing aside, concealment" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it is contempt.

Lesson 4 of 5.
दुखावणे (dukhāvṇe) — to hurt someone's feelings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a taunt, then the Marathi for contempt.

You'll want to know: दुखावणे.
दुखावणे — dukhāvṇe — "to hurt someone's feelings".
माझ्या बोलण्याने मी तिला दुखावलं. mājhyā bolṇyāne mī tilā dukhāvlaṁ. I hurt her feelings with what I said.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने तिला दुखावलं, tyāne tilā dukhāvlaṁ.
The one hurt takes -ला; मन दुखावलं, feelings were hurt.

The word, taken apart.
दुखावणे (dukhāvṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi dukhāviṇẽ under Sanskrit duḥkhati, "pains" (CDIAL 6376).

Lesson 5 of 5.
हिणवणे (hiṇavṇe) — to belittle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for contempt, then the Marathi for to hurt someone's feelings.

You'll want to know: हिणवणे.
हिणवणे — hiṇavṇe — "to belittle".
दारिद्र्यावरून कोणालाही हिणवू नये. dāridryāvarūn koṇālāhī hiṇvū naye. No one should be belittled for being poor.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने तिला हिणवलं, tyāne tilā hiṇavlaṁ.
The one belittled takes -ला; the reason takes -वरून.

The word, taken apart.
हिणवणे (hiṇavṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi hiṇaviṇẽ, "to lower in estimation", under hīnayati, "depreciates", built on Sanskrit hīna, "low" (CDIAL 14126a).
