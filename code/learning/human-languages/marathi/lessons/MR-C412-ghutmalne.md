# घुटमळणे (ghuṭmaḷṇe) — to hang about; to hesitate

## You'll want to know: घुटमळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C412-DISHA412-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घुटमळणे** — *ghuṭmaḷṇe* — "to hang about; to hesitate".

> तो दुकानाबाहेर बराच वेळ घुटमळला.
>
> *to dukānābāher barāc veḷ ghuṭmaḷlā.*
>
> He hung about outside the shop for quite a while.

Past (he …): तो घुटमळला, *to ghuṭmaḷlā*.

Moving a little but not leaving, as if unsure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घुटमळणे** (*ghuṭmaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb for hovering in one place, unsure whether to go; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
