Malayalam, chapter 541: Getting Worse, Getting Better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ശമിക്കുക (śamikkuka) — to subside; to ease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to go numb, then the Malayalam for to feel sore.

You'll want to know: ശമിക്കുക.
ശമിക്കുക — śamikkuka — "to subside; to ease".
മരുന്ന് കഴിച്ചപ്പോൾ വേദന ശമിച്ചു. marunnŭ kaḻiccappōḷ vēdana śamiccu. The pain eased when I took the medicine.
Past: ശമിച്ചു, śamiccu.
Formal; also said of rain, anger and fire.

The word, taken apart.
ശമിക്കുക (śamikkuka) is built from Sanskrit śama, "tranquillity, calm" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

Lesson 2 of 5.
വഷളാകുക (vaṣaḷākuka) — to get worse; to worsen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: വഷളാകുക.
വഷളാകുക — vaṣaḷākuka — "to get worse; to worsen".
ചികിത്സ വൈകിയതുകൊണ്ട് രോഗം വഷളായി. cikitsa vaikiyatukoṇṭŭ rōgaṁ vaṣaḷāyi. The illness got worse because treatment was late.
Past: വഷളായി, vaṣaḷāyi.
വഷളാക്കുക is to make worse, or to spoil a child.

The word, taken apart.
വഷളാകുക (vaṣaḷākuka) is an everyday word; no source we can open explains it, so its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
പകരുക (pakaruka) — to spread by contact; to be passed on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to subside, then the Malayalam for to get worse.

You'll want to know: പകരുക.
പകരുക — pakaruka — "to spread by contact; to be passed on".
ജലദോഷം ഒരാളിൽ നിന്ന് മറ്റൊരാൾക്ക് പകരും. jaladōṣaṁ orāḷil ninnŭ maṟṟorāḷkkŭ pakaruṁ. A cold spreads from one person to another.
Past: പകർന്നു, pakarnnu.
It also means to pour: അമ്മ ചായ ഗ്ലാസിലേക്ക് പകർന്നു.

The word, taken apart.
പകരുക (pakaruka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3803 gives it as to change place and to be exchanged (DEDR 3803). An illness that is caught changes place from one person to the next.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ദഹിക്കുക (dahikkuka) — to be digested; to digest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to spread by contact, then the Malayalam for to go without food.

You'll want to know: ദഹിക്കുക.
ദഹിക്കുക — dahikkuka — "to be digested; to digest".
ഇറച്ചി എളുപ്പം ദഹിക്കില്ല. iṟacci eḷuppaṁ dahikkilla. Meat does not digest easily.
Past: ദഹിച്ചു, dahiccu.
The noun is ദഹനം, digestion; ദഹനക്കേട് is indigestion.

The word, taken apart.
ദഹിക്കുക (dahikkuka) is built from Sanskrit dahati, "burns" (CDIAL 6245), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക: in Malayalam the burning up of food became digesting.
