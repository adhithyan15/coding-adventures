## You'll want to know: ಜನನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C334-JANANA334-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜನನ** — *janana* — "a birth".

> ಮಗುವಿನ ಜನನ ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *maguvina janana āspatreyalli āyitu.*
>
> The baby was born in the hospital.

A formal word: ಜನನ ಪ್ರಮಾಣಪತ್ರ, a birth certificate. At home people say ಹುಟ್ಟು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜನನ** (*janana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *janana*, "birth" (CDIAL 5099), from the root *jan*, "to be born".
