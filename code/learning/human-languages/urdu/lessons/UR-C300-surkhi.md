## You'll want to know: سرخی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C300-KHABAR300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سرخی** — *surkhī* — "a headline".

> اخبار میں بڑی سرخی تھی۔
>
> *akhbār meṅ baṛī surkhī thī.*
>
> There was a big headline in the paper.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرخی** (*surkhī*) is usually traced to Persian *surkhī*, "redness", from *surkh*, "red"; headlines are thought to take the name from the red ink they were once printed in.
