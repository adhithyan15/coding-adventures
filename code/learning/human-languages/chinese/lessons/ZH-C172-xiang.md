## You'll want to know: xiāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C172-TASTE172-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāng* — "smelling good; fragrant; tasty".

> *nǐ zuò de fàn zhēn xiāng* — The food you cook smells wonderful.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *xjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'fragrant'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
