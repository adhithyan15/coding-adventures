## You'll want to know: царапина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C483-SCRATCHES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**царапина** (*tsarápina*) — "a scratch".

> После игры с кошкой у меня на руке царапина.
>
> *Pósle igrý s kóshkoy u menyá na ruké tsarápina.*
>
> After playing with the cat I've got a scratch on my hand.

Feminine. The verb is *царапать / поцарапать* — to scratch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*царапина* is built on *царапать* 'to scratch', which EtymDB lists without a source; it is usually taken as imitative.
