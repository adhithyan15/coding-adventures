## You'll want to know: ઠગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C292-KAUBHAND292-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠગ** — *ṭhag* — "a swindler".

> એક ઠગે ગામના લોકોને નકલી નોટો આપી.
>
> *ek ṭhage gāmnā lokone naklī noṭo āpī.*
>
> A swindler gave the villagers fake banknotes.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠગ** (*ṭhag*) comes from *\*ṭhagg*, "to cheat", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun (CDIAL 5489). English *thug* was taken from this family of words.
