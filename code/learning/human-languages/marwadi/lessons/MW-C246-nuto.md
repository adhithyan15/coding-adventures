## You'll want to know: नूतो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C246-PIHAR246-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नूतो** — *nūto* — "an invitation (to a wedding or a feast)".

> काल गांव सूं नूतो आयो।
>
> *kāl gā̃v sū̃ nūto āyo.*
>
> An invitation came from the village yesterday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नूतो** (*nūto*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*nimantra*, "invitation". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *nautā*, "an invitation to a wedding" (CDIAL 7233).
