## You'll want to know: あんない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C192-POLICE192-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あんない** — *annai* — "guidance; showing the way, information".

> **おまわりさんが みちを あんないします。** — *omawarisan ga michi o annai shimasu* — "The police officer shows the way."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あんない** (*annai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *an*, "plan, idea" (Middle Chinese *'anH*) and *nai*, "inside" (*nwojH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
