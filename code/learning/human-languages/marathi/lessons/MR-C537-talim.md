## You'll want to know: तालीम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C537-NATYA537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तालीम** — *tālīm* — "a rehearsal".

> नाटकाची तालीम रोज संध्याकाळी चालते.
>
> *nāṭkācī tālīm roj sandhyākāḷī cālte.*
>
> The play's rehearsal goes on every evening.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तालीम** (*tālīm*) is a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic *taʿlīm*, "teaching".
