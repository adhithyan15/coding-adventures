Persian, chapter 526: Art and Design.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مجسمه ساز (mojassame sâz) — a sculptor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for watercolour, then the Persian for a gallery.

You'll want to know: مجسمه ساز.
مجسمه ساز (mojassame sâz) — "a sculptor".
مجسمه ساز با سنگ کار میکند. mojassame sâz bâ sang kâr mikonad. The sculptor works in stone.
mojassame 'statue' + sâz 'maker' (stem of sâkhtan).

The word, taken apart.
mojassame sâz is built in Persian on mojassame, 'statue', which was borrowed from Arabic mujassama (EtymDB), and sâz, the present stem of native sâkhtan, 'to make'.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: مینیاتور.
مینیاتور (miniyâtor) — "a miniature painting".
در موزه مینیاتورهای قدیمی دیدیم. dar muze miniyâtorhâ-ye qadimi didim. We saw old miniatures in the museum.
Persian miniature painting is an old art of book illustration.

The word, taken apart.
miniyâtor is a modern loan, usually traced to French miniature. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.

طراحی کردن (tarrâhi kardan) — to design.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for architecture, then the Persian for a miniature painting.

You'll want to know: طراحی کردن.
طراحی کردن (tarrâhi kardan) — "to design".
او لباسهای زنانه طراحی میکند. u lebâshâ-ye zanâne tarrâhi mikonad. She designs women's clothes.
tarrâhi 'design' + kardan; built on tarh 'plan, design'.

The word, taken apart.
tarrâhi is built in Persian on tarrâh, 'designer', from tarh, 'plan, design', a loan usually traced to Arabic ṭarḥ. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خلق کردن (khalq kardan) — to create.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a miniature painting, then the Persian for to design.

You'll want to know: خلق کردن.
خلق کردن (khalq kardan) — "to create".
او با چند رنگ ساده یک نقاشی زیبا خلق کرد. u bâ chand rang-e sâde yek naqqâshi-ye zibâ khalq kard. With a few simple colours she created a beautiful painting.
khalq 'creation' + kardan. Formal; in speech dorost kardan or sâkhtan.

The word, taken apart.
khalq comes from Arabic ḫalq (EtymDB), which EtymDB glosses 'people, creature': what has been created. kardan is inherited Persian.
