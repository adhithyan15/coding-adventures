## You'll want to know: अस्थिभंगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C480-DAMSA480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्थिभंगः** — *asthibhaṅgaḥ* — "a broken bone".

> वृद्धः सोपानात् अपतत्, तस्य अस्थिभंगः अभवत्।
>
> *vṛddhaḥ sopānāt apatat, tasya asthibhaṅgaḥ abhavat.*
>
> The old man fell down the stairs and broke a bone.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्थिभंगः** (*asthibhaṅgaḥ*) is built from **अस्थि**, "a bone", and *bhaṅga*, "breaking", from the root √bhañj; MW gives "fracture of the bones" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
