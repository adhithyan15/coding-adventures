## You'll want to know: circēnsis, circēnse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C335-AURIGA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**circēnsis, circēnse** — "of the circus".

> Lūdī circēnsēs per quīnque diēs fīēbant.
>
> *The races in the circus went on for five days.*

Two endings. *Lūdī circēnsēs*: the races. A satirist said the people wanted only bread and races.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**circēnsis** is built on *circus*, "ring, racecourse" (EtymDB), with the ending *-ēnsis*, "belonging to".
