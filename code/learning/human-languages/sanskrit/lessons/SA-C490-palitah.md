## You'll want to know: पलितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C490-RUPA490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पलितः** — *palitaḥ* — "grey-haired".

> पितामहस्य केशाः पलिताः सन्ति।
>
> *pitāmahasya keśāḥ palitāḥ santi.*
>
> Grandfather's hair is grey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पलितः** (*palitaḥ*) MW gives "grey, hoary, old" and the noun "grey hair"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
