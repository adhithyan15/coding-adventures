## You'll want to know: ਟੋਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C338-PERM338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੋਕਣਾ** — *ṭokṇā* — "to interrupt; to stop someone with a remark".

> ਮਿਹਰਬਾਨੀ ਕਰਕੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਾ ਟੋਕੋ।
>
> *miharbānī karke mainū̃ nā ṭoko.*
>
> Please don't interrupt me.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਟੋਕਿਆ, *maiṁ Aman nū̃ ṭokiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੋਕਣਾ** (*ṭokṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭōkk*, "strike, bite"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭokṇā*, "to hinder", under it (CDIAL 5476).
