## You'll want to know: gōngsī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngsī* — "company; firm".

> *wǒ de gōngsī hěn dà* — My company is big.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngsī* is built from gōng 'public, shared' and sī 'to manage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *si* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
