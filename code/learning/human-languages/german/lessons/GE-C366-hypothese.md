## You'll want to know: die Hypothese
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C366-HYPOTHESE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Hypothese** — "the hypothesis".

> Die Forscherin will ihre Hypothese mit einem Experiment prüfen.
>
> *The researcher wants to test her hypothesis with an experiment.*

Plural: *die Hypothesen*. The stress is on the third syllable: *hy-po-THE-se*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hypothese* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *hypothesis*, from Greek *hypóthesis* "a base, the basis of an argument" (EtymDB), built from *hypó* "under" and *thésis* "a setting down": what you put underneath.
