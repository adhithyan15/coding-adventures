## You'll want to know: ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C362-SKILL362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਣਾ** — *azmāuṇā* — "to try out; to put to the test".

> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਨਵਾਂ ਸੰਦ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ har navāṁ sand pahilāṁ azmāundā hāṁ.*
>
> I try out every new tool first.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਨਵਾਂ ਸੰਦ ਅਜ਼ਮਾਇਆ, *maiṁ navāṁ sand azmāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਜ਼ਮਾਉਣਾ** (*azmāuṇā*) is a verb made in Punjabi from a loan, usually traced to Persian *āzmā-*, "to try, test", the stem of *āzmūdan*.
