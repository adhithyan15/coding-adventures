French, chapter 410: Bouchons sur l'autoroute.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le bouchon — the traffic jam; the cork.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the road surface, then the French for to turn aside.

You'll want to know: le bouchon.
le bouchon — "the traffic jam; the cork".
Il y a dix kilomètres de bouchon avant le tunnel. There is a ten-kilometre jam before the tunnel.
Un bouchon is also a cork for a bottle.

The word, taken apart.
bouchon is built on Middle French bousche 'handful of straw' (EtymDB): first a twist of straw that stopped up a bottle, now a cork and a road blocked by cars.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'embouteillage — the gridlock, the traffic jam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to turn aside, then the French for the traffic jam.

You'll want to know: l'embouteillage.
l'embouteillage — "the gridlock, the traffic jam".
Le vendredi soir, les embouteillages sont terribles. On Friday evenings the traffic jams are terrible.
A masculine noun; close to bouchon.

The word, taken apart.
embouteillage is built on embouteiller 'to put in bottles', from bouteille 'bottle', which goes back to Late Latin butticula, a small buttis 'cask' (EtymDB): cars stuck as in the neck of a bottle.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la déviation — the diversion, the detour (for traffic).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the traffic jam, then the French for the gridlock.

You'll want to know: la déviation.
la déviation — "the diversion, the detour (for traffic)".
À cause des travaux, il y a une déviation par le village. Because of the roadworks there is a diversion through the village.
Signs say Déviation with an arrow.

The word, taken apart.
déviation is a learned borrowing from Late Latin dēviātiō, from dēviāre 'to leave the road', built on via 'way' (Lewis & Short).

The word, taken apart.
doubler is inherited through Old French dobler from Late Latin dūplāre 'to double', from duplus 'double' (EtymDB).
