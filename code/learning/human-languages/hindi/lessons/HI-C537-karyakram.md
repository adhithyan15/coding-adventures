## You'll want to know: कार्यक्रम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C537-KARYAKRAM537-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कार्यक्रम** — *kāryakram* — "a programme; a schedule".

> स्कूल का कार्यक्रम शाम छह बजे है।
>
> *Skūl kā kāryakram śām chah baje hai.*
>
> The school programme is at six in the evening.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कार्यक्रम** (*kāryakram*) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, *kārya*, "to be done", and *krama*, "a step, a course" (Monier-Williams): the things to be done, one after another.
