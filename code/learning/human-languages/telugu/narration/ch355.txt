Telugu, chapter 355: Anniversaries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వార్షికోత్సవం (vārṣikōtsavaṁ) — an anniversary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to cover up, then the Telugu for a spy.

You'll want to know: వార్షికోత్సవం.
వార్షికోత్సవం — vārṣikōtsavaṁ — "an anniversary".
మా బడి వార్షికోత్సవం ఘనంగా జరిగింది. mā baḍi vārṣikōtsavaṁ ghanaṅgā jarigindi. Our school's anniversary was celebrated in style.
వార్షిక, yearly: a yearly celebration.

The word, taken apart.
వార్షికోత్సవం (vārṣikōtsavaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit: vārṣika, "annual" (CDIAL 11571), and utsava, "a festival" (CDIAL 1876).

Lesson 2 of 5.
పెళ్ళిరోజు (peḷḷirōju) — a wedding anniversary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a spy, then the Telugu for an anniversary.

You'll want to know: పెళ్ళిరోజు.
పెళ్ళిరోజు — peḷḷirōju — "a wedding anniversary".
నిన్న అమ్మానాన్నల పెళ్ళిరోజు. ninna ammānānnala peḷḷirōju. Yesterday was my parents' wedding anniversary.
పెళ్ళి, a wedding, and రోజు, a day: the day of the wedding, each year.

The word, taken apart.
పెళ్ళిరోజు (peḷḷirōju) is a Telugu compound: native peḷḷi, "a marriage" (DEDR 4395), and rōju, "a day".

ఆ కవి జయంతి సందర్భంగా సభ జరిగింది. ā kavi jayanti sandarbhaṅgā sabha jarigindi. A meeting was held for the poet's birth anniversary.
For the famous: a poet's or a leader's జయంతి (jayanti).

The word, taken apart.
జయంతి (jayanti) is borrowed from Sanskrit jayantī (Monier-Williams), a word built on jaya, "victory" (CDIAL 5141).
