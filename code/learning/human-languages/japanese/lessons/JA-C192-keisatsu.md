## You'll want to know: けいさつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C192-POLICE192-03]; assesses=[] -->

**けいさつ** — *keisatsu* — "police".

> **けいさつに でんわを します。** — *keisatsu ni denwa o shimasu* — "I phone the police."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けいさつ** (*keisatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kei*, "admonish, commandment" (Middle Chinese *kjaengX*) and *satsu*, "judge, understand" (*tsrheat*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
