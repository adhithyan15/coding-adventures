# ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? — are you a traveller?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02, PA-LEX-YATRI-202] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *what is your name?* Then *you are a traveller*:
**ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ**.

## Grammar Lens: ਕੀ at the front
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KI-02, PA-LEX-YATRI-202, PA-GRAMMAR-TUSI-HO-03, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-YA-01] -->

Put **ਕੀ** in front of a statement and it becomes a yes-or-no question. The
voice rises at the end:

> **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ।** — *tusīṁ yātrī ho.* — You are a traveller.

> **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ?** — *kī tusīṁ yātrī ho?* — Are you a traveller?

> **ਕੀ ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ?** — *kī bāgh khullhā hai?* — Is the garden open?

Same word, two jobs. In **ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ?** it stands where the answer goes,
and means "what". At the very front it asks only yes or no.
