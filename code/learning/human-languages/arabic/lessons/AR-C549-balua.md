## You'll want to know: بالوعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C549-BAB549-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بالوعة** — *bālūʿa* — "a drain".

> سدت الأوراق البالوعة في الشارع.
>
> *saddat al-awrāq al-bālūʿa fī ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> Leaves blocked the drain in the street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بالوعة** (*bālūʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-l-ʿ*, "to swallow" (Lane). A bālūʿa swallows the water. Ibtalaʿa, to swallow, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
