## You'll want to know: ഇടിയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C513-CAMP513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടിയുക** — *iṭiyuka* — "to cave in; to crumble".

> കനത്ത മഴയിൽ വഴിയുടെ ഒരു ഭാഗം ഇടിഞ്ഞു.
>
> *kanatta maḻayil vaḻiyuṭe oru bhāgaṁ iṭiññu.*
>
> In the heavy rain part of the road caved in.

Past: ഇടിഞ്ഞു, *iṭiññu*.

For earth, walls and banks: മണ്ണ് ഇടിഞ്ഞു, the earth gave way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടിയുക** (*iṭiyuka*) is native Dravidian, "to crumble, to fall to pieces" (DEDR 432).
