Portuguese, chapter 33: Skin, Nail, Chin, Throat, Stomach.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a pele — skin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the face, then the Portuguese for blood.

You'll want to know: a pele.
a pele — "skin".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 3 seconds]
What does a pele mean? ("Skin".) Say it once more.
