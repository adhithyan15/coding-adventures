## You'll want to know: поляна
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C461-POLYANA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поляна** (*polyána*) — "a clearing, a glade".

> На поляне мы увидели оленя.
>
> *Na polyáne my uvídeli olénya.*
>
> We saw a deer in the clearing.

Feminine. Built on *поле* — a field.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поляна* is built on *поле* 'a field, open land' with *-яна*. EtymDB lists *поляна* without a deeper source.
