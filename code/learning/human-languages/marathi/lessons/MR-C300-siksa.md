## You'll want to know: शिक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C300-LACH300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिक्षा** — *śikṣā* — "a punishment; a sentence".

> न्यायालयाने त्याला तीन वर्षांची शिक्षा दिली.
>
> *nyāyālayāne tyālā tīn varṣāñcī śikṣā dilī.*
>
> The court sentenced him to three years.

Feminine.

शिक्षा देणे, to sentence; शिक्षा होणे, to be punished. In Marathi it means punishment, not teaching.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिक्षा** (*śikṣā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śikṣā*, 'learning, teaching; also chastisement, punishment' (Monier-Williams); Marathi keeps the last sense.
