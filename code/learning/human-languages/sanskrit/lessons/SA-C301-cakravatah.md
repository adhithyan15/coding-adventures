## You'll want to know: चक्रवातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C301-CAKRAVATA301-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्रवातः** — *cakravātaḥ* — "a whirlwind".

> समुद्रात् चक्रवातः आगच्छति।
>
> *samudrāt cakravātaḥ āgacchati.*
>
> A whirlwind is coming from the sea.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्रवातः** (*cakravātaḥ*) is built from *cakra*, "a wheel", and *vāta*, "wind"; MW gives *cakravāta* as a whirlwind, and a cyclone is its modern use (MW).
