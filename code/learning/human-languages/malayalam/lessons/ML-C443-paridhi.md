# പരിധി (paridhi) — a limit

## You'll want to know: പരിധി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C443-PARIDHI443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിധി** — *paridhi* — "a limit".

> എല്ലാത്തിനും ഒരു പരിധി ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *ellāttinuṁ oru paridhi uṇṭŭ.*
>
> Everything has a limit.

Within the limit: പരിധിക്കുള്ളിൽ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പരിധി** (*paridhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paridhi*, "an enclosure, a fence, a wall" (Monier-Williams): a limit is the fence round a thing.
