## You'll want to know: سيف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C326-SILAH326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سيف** — *sayf* — "a sword".

> علق الفارس سيفه على الجدار.
>
> *ʿallaqa al-fāris sayfahu ʿalā al-jidār.*
>
> The knight hung his sword on the wall.

Plural: سيوف, *suyūf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سيف** (*sayf*) is filed by Lane under the root *s-y-f*. A link with Greek *xiphos*, a sword, is often proposed, but the direction of the borrowing, if any, is not settled.
