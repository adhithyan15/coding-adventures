Marathi, chapter 293: When the Earth Shook.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भूकंप (bhūkamp) — an earthquake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for ash, then the Marathi for a fire brigade.

You'll want to know: भूकंप.
भूकंप — bhūkamp — "an earthquake".
जर भूकंप झाला तर आम्ही मोकळ्या मैदानात जाऊ. jar bhūkamp jhālā tar āmhī mokaḷyā maidānāt jāū. If there is an earthquake, we will go out to open ground.
Masculine.
भूकंप (bhūkamp) होणे, an earthquake happens; जर … झाला तर, if one happens, with the past after जर and the future after तर.

The word, taken apart.
भूकंप (bhūkamp) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit bhūkampa, 'an earthquake' (Monier-Williams): bhū, 'the earth', and kampa, 'a trembling'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
हादरणे (hādarṇe) — to shake; to be shaken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fire brigade, then the Marathi for an earthquake.

You'll want to know: हादरणे.
हादरणे — hādarṇe — "to shake; to be shaken".
भूकंपात आमचं संपूर्ण घर हादरलं. bhūkampāt āmcaṁ sampūrṇa ghar hādarlaṁ. Our whole house shook in the earthquake.
Past (he …): तो हादरला, to hādarlā.
Also of people: ती बातमी ऐकून तो हादरला, the news shook him.

The word, taken apart.
हादरणे (hādarṇe) is a Marathi verb of violent shaking; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कोसळणे (kosaḷṇe) — to collapse; to come crashing down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an earthquake, then the Marathi for to shake.

You'll want to know: कोसळणे.
कोसळणे — kosaḷṇe — "to collapse; to come crashing down".
जुनी भिंत पावसात कोसळली. junī bhint pāvsāt kosaḷlī. The old wall collapsed in the rain.
Past (he …): तो कोसळला, to kosaḷlā.
Also of rain: पाऊस कोसळतो, the rain pours down.

The word, taken apart.
कोसळणे (kosaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb of falling in a heap; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
खचणे (khacṇe) — to cave in; to sink down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to shake, then the Marathi for to collapse.

You'll want to know: खचणे.
खचणे — khacṇe — "to cave in; to sink down".
पावसामुळे गावातला रस्ता खचला. pāvsāmuḷe gāvātlā rastā khaclā. Because of the rain, the village road caved in.
Past (he …): तो खचला, to khaclā.
Also of courage: तो मनाने खचला, he lost heart.

The word, taken apart.
खचणे (khacṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi khacṇe, 'to sink down', under khacc, 'sink down or back' (CDIAL 3764).

Lesson 5 of 5.
दरड (daraḍ) — a landslide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to collapse, then the Marathi for to cave in.

You'll want to know: दरड.
दरड — daraḍ — "a landslide".
घाटात दरड कोसळली आणि वाहतूक थांबली. ghāṭāt daraḍ kosaḷlī āṇi vāhtūk thāmblī. A landslide came down in the pass and the traffic stopped.
Feminine.
दरड (daraḍ) कोसळणे (kosaḷṇe), a landslide comes down.

The word, taken apart.
दरड (daraḍ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi daraḍ, 'a steep bank', under Sanskrit darad, 'a bank, a precipice' (CDIAL 6191).
