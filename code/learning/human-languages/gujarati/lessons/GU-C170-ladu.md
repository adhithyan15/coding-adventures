# લાડુ (lāḍu) — laddoo, sweet ball

## You'll want to know: લાડુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C170-SWEET170-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લાડુ** — *lāḍu* — "laddoo, sweet ball".

> **દાદી લાડુ બનાવે છે.** — *dādī lāḍu banāve che* — "Grandmother makes laddoos."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાડુ** (*lāḍu*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *laḍḍu*, "a kind of sweetmeat", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "a sweetmeat of sweetened meal in a ball" (CDIAL 10926).
