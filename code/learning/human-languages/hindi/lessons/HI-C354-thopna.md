## You'll want to know: थोपना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C354-NAGRIK354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**थोपना** — *thopnā* — "to impose, to force (on someone)".

> कोई तुम पर अपनी राय नहीं थोप सकता।
>
> *koī tum par apnī rāy nahīṃ thop saktā.*
>
> Nobody can force their opinion on you.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपनी राय थोपी, *usne apnī rāy thopī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थोपना** (*thopnā*) is inherited from *\*stōpyate*, "is obstructed", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13723). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to cover, to heap"; the sense of forcing a thing on someone is not among its glosses, and may have grown from heaping a thing on.
