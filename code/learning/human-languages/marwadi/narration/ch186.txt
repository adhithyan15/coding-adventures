Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 186: Seats, Beds and Lids.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ढक्कण (ḍhakkaṇ) — a lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for asafoetida, then the Marwadi for fennel seed.

You'll want to know: ढक्कण.
ढक्कण — ḍhakkaṇ — "a lid".
हांडी माथै ढक्कण है। hā̃ḍī māthai ḍhakkaṇ hai. There's a lid on the pot.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ढक्कण (ḍhakkaṇ) is built on a reconstructed ḍhakk, "cover", the word behind ढांकणो; CDIAL lists Sindhi and Gujarati words for a lid under it, beside Marwari ḍhā̃kṇo (CDIAL 5574).

Lesson 2 of 5.
कूंडो (kū̃ḍo) — an earthen basin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for fennel seed, then the Marwadi for a lid.

You'll want to know: कूंडो.
कूंडो — kū̃ḍo — "an earthen basin".
कूंडो भारी है। kū̃ḍo bhārī hai. The earthen basin is heavy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कूंडो (kū̃ḍo) is inherited from Sanskrit kuṇḍa, "a bowl, a waterpot"; CDIAL lists Hindi kū̃ḍā, a clay pot that dough is kneaded in (CDIAL 3264). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a lid, then the Marwadi for an earthen basin.

पीढो — pīḍho — "a low stool".
रसोई मांय एक पीढो है। rasoī mā̃y ek pīḍho hai. There's a low stool in the kitchen.
A masculine noun.

पीढो (pīḍho) is inherited from Sanskrit pīṭha, "a stool, a bench"; CDIAL lists Hindi pīṛhā, "a large square stool" (CDIAL 8222). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an earthen basin, then the Marwadi for a low stool.

You'll want to know: पाटो.
पाटो — pāṭo — "a plank seat".
पाटो नीचो है। pāṭo nīcho hai. The plank seat is low.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पाटो (pāṭo) is inherited from Sanskrit paṭṭa, "a slab, a tablet"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pāṭī, "a plank", and a Hindi board to sit on while eating (CDIAL 7699).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पलंग (palaṅg) — a bedstead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a low stool, then the Marwadi for a plank seat.

You'll want to know: पलंग.
पलंग — palaṅg — "a bedstead".
म्हारो पलंग नुवो है। mhāro palaṅg nuvo hai. My bed is new.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पलंग (palaṅg) is inherited from Sanskrit palyaṅka, "a bed"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari word for a bed under it (CDIAL 7964). It is a framed bed; खाट is the string bed.
