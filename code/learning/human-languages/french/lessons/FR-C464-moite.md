## You'll want to know: moite
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C464-CANICULE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**moite** — "clammy, sticky (with damp heat)".

> Dans le métro, l'air est chaud et moite.
>
> *In the metro the air is hot and clammy.*

*Les mains moites* — sweaty hands. Same form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*moite* comes from Old French *moiste* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *mūcidus* 'mouldy, slimy' (Lewis & Short); the details are not settled.
