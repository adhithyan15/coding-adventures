## Added Japanese pre-A1 secure four-skill mock 1

Added the first project-defined Japanese pre-A1 practice mock, with a pinned
manifest, separate learner packets, confidential recording and answer masters,
and an independent 60% floor for reading, listening, writing, and speaking.
The 20-minute written plus 5-minute spoken form draws its scored Japanese
from directly taught early doorway and handwriting lessons. It does not claim
JLPT/JF Standard equivalence or learner readiness.

The assessment contract now declares the real paper and manifest, and the
artifact and legacy paper-omission ceilings fall accordingly. Focused tests
pin part counts, timings, keyed meanings, and hidden writing targets.
Qualified Japanese review, recordings, and book-only human validation remain
required before the form is used with learners.
