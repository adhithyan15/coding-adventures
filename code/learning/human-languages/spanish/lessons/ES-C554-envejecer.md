## You'll want to know: envejecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C554-GRADUA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**envejecer** — "to grow old, to age".

> Mi perro ha envejecido mucho.
>
> *My dog has aged a lot.*

It takes a *z*: *envejezco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*envejecer* is built on *viejo* 'old' with *en-* and *-ecer*. *Viejo* is inherited from Latin *vetulus* 'little old', from *vetus* 'old' (Lewis & Short).
