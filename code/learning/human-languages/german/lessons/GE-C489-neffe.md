## You'll want to know: der Neffe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C489-VERWANDTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Neffe** — "nephew".

> Mein Neffe ist zehn und spielt gern Fußball.
>
> *My nephew is ten and likes playing football.*

Weak noun: *den Neffen*, *dem Neffen*. Plural: *die Neffen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Neffe* is inherited from Old High German *nefo* (EtymDB), an old Germanic family word.
