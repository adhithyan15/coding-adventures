## You'll want to know: a coleção
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C504-OLARIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a coleção** — "collection".

> Tem uma coleção enorme de discos.
>
> *He has a huge record collection.*

Before AO90 spelled *colecção* in Portugal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coleção* is a learned borrowing from Latin *collēctiō* "a gathering together" (EtymDB), from *colligere* "to gather".
