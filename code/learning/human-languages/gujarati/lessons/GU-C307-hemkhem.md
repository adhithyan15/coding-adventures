## You'll want to know: હેમખેમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C307-RUNI307-04]; assesses=[] -->

**હેમખેમ** — *hemkhem* — "safe and sound".

> બાળક હેમખેમ ઘરે પહોંચ્યું.
>
> *bāḷak hemkhem ghare pahõcyũ.*
>
> The child got home safe and sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હેમખેમ** (*hemkhem*) is an echo pair built on *khem*, "wellbeing", which CDIAL lists for Gujarati under Sanskrit *kṣḗma*, "safety, ease" (CDIAL 3745); the rhyming *hem* in front adds weight. It is said of people who come through danger: **હેમખેમ પહોંચ્યું**.
