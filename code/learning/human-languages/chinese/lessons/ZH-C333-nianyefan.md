## You'll want to know: niányèfàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C333-EVE333-03]; assesses=[] -->

*niányèfàn* — "New Year's Eve dinner".

> *Chúxī de wǎnshang wǒmen zài nǎinai jiā chī le niányèfàn* — On the last night of the year we had the reunion dinner at my grandma's.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niányèfàn* is built from 年 'a year', *yè* 'night' and 饭 'a meal', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, *nen*, *yaeH* and *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'evening' and says of the third that grain was first cooked into it.
