# Repair without rewriting the clock

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-A1-TIMED-REPAIR-01]; assesses=[PA-A1-FORM-TIMED-01, PA-A1-MESSAGE-TIMED-01] -->

Keep the original timed form and message unchanged. Copy neither into a new
scored answer. The clock showed what you could produce then; this short
lesson shows what one careful repair can teach next.

## Guided Practice — separate checks
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-TIMED-REPAIR-01, PA-FORM-SIX-REPAIR-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

First inspect task fulfilment: six requested form values, one named reader,
the requested message details, and a 30–40-word message. Then inspect order:
each value beside its own label, and greeting, content, invitation, and close
in a readable sequence. Third, inspect Punjabi vocabulary and grammar,
including agreement and tone. Last, inspect Gurmukhi spelling, word spaces,
punctuation, digit order, and phone grouping. Do one pass at a time.

Choose one error dimension and write one corrected line on a separate page.
Say what changed. Do not present this later line as the timed response.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-TIMED-REPAIR-01] -->

Record one next practice target for the form and one for the message. A full
paper still asks for both tasks in twenty minutes; only the complete timed
mocks can test that combined condition.
