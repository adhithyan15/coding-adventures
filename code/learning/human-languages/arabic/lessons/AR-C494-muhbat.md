## You'll want to know: محبط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C494-KAABA494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**محبط** — *muḥbaṭ* — "frustrated".

> الطالب محبط لأنه رسب في الامتحان.
>
> *aṭ-ṭālib muḥbaṭ li-annahu rasaba fī al-imtiḥān.*
>
> The student is frustrated because he failed the exam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محبط** (*muḥbaṭ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-b-ṭ*, "to come to nothing, to be made void" (Lane). Muḥbaṭ is the passive participle of Form IV.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
