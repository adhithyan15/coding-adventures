## You'll want to know: idcircō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C366-PROINDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**idcircō** — "for that reason, therefore".

> Aeger erat; idcircō nōn vēnit.
>
> *He was ill; for that reason he did not come.*

*Idcircō* points ahead to a reason or an aim: *idcircō … quod*, for this reason, that; *idcircō … ut*, for this purpose, that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*idcircō* is built from *id* "that" and an old form of *circā*, *circum* "around, about" (Lewis & Short give *id-circa*, EtymDB *id* + *circum*): about that, for that reason.
