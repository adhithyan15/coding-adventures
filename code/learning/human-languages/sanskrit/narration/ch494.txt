Lesson 1 of 6.
लालयति (lālayati) — pampers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the first haircut, then the Sanskrit for the sacred-thread ceremony.

You'll want to know: लालयति.
लालयति — lālayati — "pampers".
पितामही पौत्रं लालयति। pitāmahī pautraṃ lālayati. Grandmother pampers her grandson.
Past (she …): सा पौत्रं लालितवती, sā pautraṃ lālitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
लालयति (lālayati) is built on the causative of the root √lal, "to play, to frolic"; MW's ppp lālita is "caressed, fondled, indulged" (MW).

You'll want to know: संवर्धयति.
संवर्धयति — saṃvardhayati — "brings up".
माता पिता च पुत्रं स्नेहेन संवर्धयतः। mātā pitā ca putraṃ snehena saṃvardhayataḥ. The mother and father bring up their son with love.
Past (she …): सा पुत्रीं स्नेहेन संवर्धितवती, sā putrīṃ snehena saṃvardhitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
संवर्धयति (saṃvardhayati) is built on sam and the causative of the root √vṛdh, "to grow", the root of वर्धते; MW's ppp saṃvardhita is "brought up, reared" (MW).

You'll want to know: अनुजानाति.
अनुजानाति — anujānāti — "permits".
पिता पुत्रस्य यात्राम् अनुजानाति। pitā putrasya yātrām anujānāti. Father allows his son's journey.
Past (he …): सः यात्राम् अनुज्ञातवान्, saḥ yātrām anujñātavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुजानाति (anujānāti) is built from anu, "after", and the root √jñā, "to know", the root of जानाति; MW gives "to permit, grant, allow" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
बाल्यम् (bālyam) — childhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for scolds, then the Sanskrit for permits.

You'll want to know: बाल्यम्.
बाल्यम् — bālyam — "childhood".
बाल्ये अहं ग्रामे अवसम्। bālye ahaṃ grāme avasam. In childhood I lived in the village.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
बाल्यम् (bālyam) is built on बालः, "a child", by lengthening its vowel and adding -ya; MW gives "boyhood, childhood, infancy" (MW).
