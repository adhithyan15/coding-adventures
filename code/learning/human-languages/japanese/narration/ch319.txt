Japanese, chapter 319: What People Really Think.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ほんね (honne) — one's real feelings; what one really thinks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a meaning, then the Japanese for for what reason.

You'll want to know: ほんね.
ほんね — honne — "one's real feelings; what one really thinks".
しんゆうには ほんねを はなします。 — shin'yū ni wa honne o hanashimasu — "I tell my best friend what I really think."

The word, taken apart.
ほんね (honne) is built from a Sino-Japanese part and a native part: hon, "true", an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese pwonX), and ne, "sound", a native kun'yomi (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

じょうしが いたから、たてまえを 言いました。 — jōshi ga ita kara, tatemae o iimashita — "My boss was there, so I said what I was expected to say."

The word, taken apart.
たてまえ (tatemae) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from tate, "building (tateru)" and mae, "front", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
みとめる (mitomeru) — to admit; to recognize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for one's real feelings, then the Japanese for the official line.

You'll want to know: みとめる.
みとめる — mitomeru — "to admit; to recognize".
あには まちがえたので、それを みとめました。 — ani wa machigaeta node, sore o mitomemashita — "My brother had made a mistake, so he admitted it."
Polite みとめます, mitomemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): あには みとめました。, ani wa mitomemashita.

The word, taken apart.
みとめる (mitomeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ごかいする (gokai suru) — to misunderstand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the official line, then the Japanese for to admit.

You'll want to know: ごかいする.
ごかいする — gokai suru — "to misunderstand".
せつめいを まちがえたので、みんな ごかいしました。 — setsumei o machigaeta node, minna gokai shimashita — "I got the explanation wrong, so everyone misunderstood."
Polite ごかいします, gokaishimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): ともだちが ごかいしました。, tomodachi ga gokai shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
ごかいする (gokai suru) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb suru, "to do". The noun, gokai, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: go, "mistake" (Middle Chinese nguH) and kai, "understanding" (keaX) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

ほんき — honki — "serious; in earnest".
ちちは ほんきで はなしたと おもいます。 — chichi wa honki de hanashita to omoimasu — "I think my father meant what he said."
A na-adjective: な before a noun.

The word, taken apart.
ほんき (honki) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are hon, "true" (Middle Chinese pwonX) and ki, "spirit" (khj+jH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
