## You'll want to know: کاخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C350-PADESHAH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کاخ** (*kâkh*) — "a palace".

> کاخ پادشاه روی تپه بود.
>
> *Kâkh-e pâdeshâh ru-ye tappe bud.*
>
> The king's palace stood on a hill.

Plural *kâkh-hâ*; many old palaces are museums now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kâkh* is a native Persian word for a palace. EtymDB lists no entry, and its deeper origin is not settled.
