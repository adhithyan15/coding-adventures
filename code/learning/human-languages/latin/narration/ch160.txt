Latin, chapter 160: To count, To destroy, To go away, To arrive, To live.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
numerō, numerāre — to count.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to keep, then the Latin for to change.

You'll want to know: numerō, numerāre.
numerō, numerāre — "to count".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: numerō]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: numerō, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say numerō]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Latin for to keep, then the Latin for to change, then say numerō again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does numerō, numerāre mean? ("To count".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Proud? (superbus, superba, superbum.) Kind? (benignus, benigna, benignum.) Cruel? (crūdēlis, crūdēle.) Ugly? (turpis, turpe.) Beautiful? (fōrmōsus, fōrmōsa, fōrmōsum.) Strong? (validus, valida, validum.) Weak? (īnfirmus, īnfirma, īnfirmum.) Alone? (sōlus, sōla, sōlum.) Icy? (gelidus, gelida, gelidum.) Clear? (serēnus, serēna, serēnum.) Cloudy? (nūbilus, nūbila, nūbilum.) Windy? (ventōsus, ventōsa, ventōsum.) To build? (aedificō, aedificāre.) To climb? (ascendō, ascendere.) To go down? (dēscendō, dēscendere.) To join? (iungō, iungere.) To visit? (vīsitō, vīsitāre.) To sing? (cantō, cantāre.) To paint? (pingō, pingere.) To lead? (dūcō, dūcere.) To make? (faciō, facere.) To carry? (gerō, gerere.) To put on? (induō, induere.) To take off? (exuō, exuere.) To clean? (pūrgō, pūrgāre.)
