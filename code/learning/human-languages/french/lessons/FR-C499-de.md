## You'll want to know: le dé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C499-JEUX-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le dé** — "dice, die".

> C'est à toi de lancer le dé.
>
> *It is your turn to throw the dice.*

Plural *les dés*. *Un dé à coudre* — a thimble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le dé* is usually traced to Latin *datum* "what is given", from *dare* "to give" (Lewis & Short), but the origin is not settled.
