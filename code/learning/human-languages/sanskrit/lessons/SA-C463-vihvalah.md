## You'll want to know: विह्वलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C463-PARAKRAMA463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विह्वलः** — *vihvalaḥ* — "distraught; perturbed".

> भयेन विह्वलः बालः मातरम् आह्वयति।
>
> *bhayena vihvalaḥ bālaḥ mātaram āhvayati.*
>
> Distraught with fear, the child calls his mother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विह्वलः** (*vihvalaḥ*) is built from *vi* and the root √hval, "to go crookedly, to stagger"; MW gives "agitated, perturbed, distressed, afflicted" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
