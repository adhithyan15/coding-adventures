Italian, chapter 276: Una lunga vicenda.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'avvenimento — the event, the happening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to take place, then the Italian for what happened.

You'll want to know: l'avvenimento.
l'avvenimento — "the event, the happening".
Il concerto è stato l'avvenimento dell'estate. The concert was the event of the summer.
An important happening. Built on avvenire, ‘to take place’.

The word, taken apart.
avvenimento is built on avvenire "to take place", from Latin advenīre "to come to" (EtymDB), with the noun ending -mento.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la vicenda — the affair, the chain of events.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for what happened, then the Italian for the event.

You'll want to know: la vicenda.
la vicenda — "the affair, the chain of events".
È una vicenda strana: nessuno sa la verità. It is a strange affair: nobody knows the truth.
A vicenda means ‘in turn, each other’: si aiutano a vicenda.

The word, taken apart.
vicenda is traced in EtymDB to Latin vicis "change, turn, alternation": a story is a chain of turns.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la cronologia — the chronology, the order of events; browsing history.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the affair, then the Italian for the matter.

You'll want to know: la cronologia.
la cronologia — "the chronology, the order of events; browsing history".
Facciamo la cronologia dei fatti. Let us put together the chronology of the facts.
On a phone or a computer, la cronologia is the browsing history.

The word, taken apart.
cronologia is a learned word built from Greek khrónos "time" (EtymDB) and -logía "a reckoning, a study".
