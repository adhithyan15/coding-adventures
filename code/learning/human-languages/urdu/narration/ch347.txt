Urdu, chapter 347: The Issue at Hand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
معاملہ (muāmalā) — a matter to be settled; an affair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for welfare, then the Urdu for to make known.

You'll want to know: معاملہ.
معاملہ — muāmalā — "a matter to be settled; an affair".
یہ معاملہ بڑا مشکل ہے۔ yih muāmalā baṛā mushkil hai. This is a very difficult affair.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
معاملہ (muāmalā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muʿāmala, "dealing, a transaction".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: سمجھوتا.
سمجھوتا — samjhautā — "a compromise; a settlement".
دونوں فریقوں میں سمجھوتا ہو گیا۔ donoṅ farīqoṅ meṅ samjhautā ho gayā. The two sides reached a compromise.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
سمجھوتا (samjhautā) is a noun made on سمجھنا, "to understand", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin samajhnā under Sanskrit saṃbudhyate (CDIAL 12959). An understanding both sides reach.

You'll want to know: چھیڑنا.
چھیڑنا — chheṛnā — "to bring up a topic; to tease".

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نئی بات چھیڑی, us ne naī bāt chheṛī.

The word, taken apart.
چھیڑنا (chheṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin cheṛnā, "to touch, to irritate", under ciḍ, "be angry", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4794).
