## You'll want to know: دسترخوان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C511-DASTARKHVAN511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دسترخوان** — *dastarkhvān* — "a cloth spread for a meal".

> امی نے فرش پر دسترخوان بچھایا۔
>
> *ammī ne farsh par dastarkhvān bichhāyā.*
>
> Mum spread the dining cloth on the floor.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دسترخوان** (*dastarkhvān*) is usually traced to Persian *dastarkhwān*, "a cloth spread for a meal".
