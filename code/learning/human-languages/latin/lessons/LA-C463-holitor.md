## You'll want to know: holitor, holitōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C463-HOLITOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**holitor, holitōris** — "a vegetable grower, a market gardener".

> Holitor holera in forum portat et vendit.
>
> *The market gardener carries vegetables to the forum and sells them.*

Third declension, masculine: *holitor, holitōris m.*, also spelled *olitor*. From *holus* 'vegetables'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*holitor* is built on *holus, holeris* 'vegetables, greens' with the suffix *-tor* 'one who' (Lewis & Short, under *olitor*).
