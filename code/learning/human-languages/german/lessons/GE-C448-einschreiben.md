## You'll want to know: das Einschreiben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C448-POST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Einschreiben** — "the registered letter, registered post".

> Den Vertrag schickt er per Einschreiben.
>
> *He sends the contract by registered post.*

Same form in the plural. The verb *sich einschreiben* means to enrol.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Einschreiben* is the verb *einschreiben* 'to write in, to register' used as a noun; *schreiben* is an early borrowing from Latin *scrībere* 'to write' (Lewis & Short).
