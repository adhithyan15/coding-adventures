## You'll want to know: legal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C385-BUROCRACIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**legal** — "legal, lawful; cool (informal, in Brazil)".

> Fazer isso é legal, mas não é bonito.
>
> *Doing that is legal, but it is not nice.*

In Brazil, informal *legal!* means cool, great; in Portugal it is mostly lawful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*legal* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lēgālis* "legal" (EtymDB), built on *lēx, lēgis* "law" (Lewis & Short); the same Latin word gave the inherited *leal* "loyal".
