## You'll want to know: வரன்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C498-ILAMAI498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**வரன்** — *varaṉ* — "a prospective groom; a match".

> மகளுக்கு அப்பா நல்ல வரன் தேடுகிறார்.
>
> *makaḷukku appā nalla varaṉ tēṭukiṟār.*
>
> Father is looking for a good match for his daughter.

Said of an arranged marriage: வரன் பார், to look for a match.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வரன்** (*varaṉ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vara*, "suitor, bridegroom" (CDIAL 11309).
