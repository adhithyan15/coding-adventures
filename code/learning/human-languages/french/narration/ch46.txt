1 lesson. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 1.
Your own line — the first writing with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Everything you have written so far had something to reach for — a shape, a word, a sound.
[pause 1 second]
This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed: only what this book has taught you.

Writing — controlled composition.
[pause 2 seconds]
Write one line about yourself. Subject, verb, then the rest — the order you already know.
[pause 2 seconds]
One thing to decide deliberately rather than by accident before you start: the accents are letters, not decoration.
[pause 2 seconds]
An acute on an e is not a flourish on the same letter; it makes a different sound and, often enough, a different word. A hand that writes French without accents has not written French untidily — it has written something else and hoped the reader would guess.
[pause 3 seconds]
Write the line. Then read it back and check the accents before you check anything else.

Wrap-up recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Are French accents decoration? (No — they are part of the letter.)
[pause 2 seconds]
And what did you choose here that the earlier lessons chose for you? (The words.)
