## You'll want to know: o assaltante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C288-ASSALTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o assaltante** — "the robber, the mugger".

> O assaltante levou o dinheiro todo.
>
> *The robber took all the money.*

The same form for a woman: *a assaltante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assaltante* is built on *assaltar* "to rob" with the ending *-ante*; the verb is usually traced to Latin *saltāre* "to jump" (Lewis & Short).
