8 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
ਰੇਲ (rel) — rail or train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ, then the older train word ਗੱਡੀ.

Grammar Lens: one transport word.
ਰੇਲ — rel — rail; train.
Keep this as one practical transport word. The older ਗੱਡੀ remains useful.

The word, taken apart.
