## You'll want to know: le cimetière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C314-FUNERAILLES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le cimetière** — "the cemetery, the graveyard".

> Le cimetière est derrière l'église.
>
> *The cemetery is behind the church.*

Masculine: *le cimetière*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cimetière* comes from Church Latin *coemētērium*, a borrowing from Greek *koimētḗrion* "a sleeping place" (Lewis & Short).
