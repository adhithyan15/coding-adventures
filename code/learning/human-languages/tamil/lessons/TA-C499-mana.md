## You'll want to know: மண
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C499-MANAM499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**மண** — *maṇa* — "to marry".

> அவர் ஒரு மருத்துவரை மணந்தார்.
>
> *avar oru maruttuvarai maṇantār.*
>
> He married a doctor.

Past (I …): மணந்தேன், *maṇantēṉ*.

Formal and literary; the everyday phrase is திருமணம் செய். The one married takes -ஐ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மண** (*maṇa*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4667). DEDR keeps மண "to emit fragrance" as a separate entry (4668).
