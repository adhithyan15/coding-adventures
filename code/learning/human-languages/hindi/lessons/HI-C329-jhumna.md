## You'll want to know: झूमना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C329-RANG329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**झूमना** — *jhūmnā* — "to sway (with joy or music)".

> ढोल बज रहा था और सब लोग झूम रहे थे।
>
> *ḍhol baj rahā thā aur sab log jhūm rahe the.*
>
> The drum was playing and everyone was swaying.

Past (he …): वह झूमा, *vah jhūmā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झूमना** (*jhūmnā*) is set by CDIAL under *\*kṣumbhati*, "shakes" (CDIAL 3726), with a query of its own: the link is not certain. It is a happy, rocking movement: trees in the wind and people at a festival **झूमते हैं**.
