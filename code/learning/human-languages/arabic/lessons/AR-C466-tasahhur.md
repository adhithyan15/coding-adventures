## You'll want to know: تصحر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C466-GHUBAR466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تصحر** — *taṣaḥḥur* — "desertification".

> التصحر مشكلة في جنوب البلد.
>
> *at-taṣaḥḥur mushkila fī janūb al-balad.*
>
> Desertification is a problem in the south of the country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تصحر** (*taṣaḥḥur*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-ḥ-r*, "a desert" (Lane). It is a modern Form V verbal noun, "becoming a desert".
