# ആക്രമണം (ākramaṇaṁ) — an attack

## You'll want to know: ആക്രമണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C373-AKRAMANAM373-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ആക്രമണം** — *ākramaṇaṁ* — "an attack".

> ആനയുടെ ആക്രമണത്തിൽ ഒരു വീട് തകർന്നു.
>
> *ānayuṭe ākramaṇattil oru vīṭŭ takarnnu.*
>
> A house was wrecked in an elephant's attack.

Also of animals: an elephant or a dog attack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആക്രമണം** (*ākramaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ākramaṇa*, "stepping on, approaching" (CDIAL 1016), from *ā-kram*, "to step upon": an attack steps onto someone's ground.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
