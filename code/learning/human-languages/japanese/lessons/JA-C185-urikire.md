## You'll want to know: うりきれ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C185-SALE185-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うりきれ** — *urikire* — "sold out".

> **やすい たまごは もう うりきれです。** — *yasui tamago wa mō urikire desu* — "The cheap eggs are already sold out."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うりきれ** (*urikire*) is native Japanese, built from *uri*, "selling", and *kire*, "running out", from *kireru*, a verb on the same stem as *kiru*, "to cut" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
