## You'll want to know: عضو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C385-MUNAZZAMA385-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عضو** — *ʿuḍw* — "a member".

> أخي عضو في نادي الشطرنج.
>
> *akhī ʿuḍw fī nādī ash-shaṭranj.*
>
> My brother is a member of the chess club.

Plural: أعضاء, *aʿḍāʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عضو** (*ʿuḍw*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ḍ-w*, "a limb" (Lane). A *ʿuḍw* is a limb of the body, and so a member of any body of people.
