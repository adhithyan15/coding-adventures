## You'll want to know: la cartoleria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C452-EDICOLA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cartoleria** — "the stationer's".

> In cartoleria compro quaderni e penne.
>
> *At the stationer's I buy exercise books and pens.*

Busy in September, before school starts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cartoleria* is built on *carta* 'paper', which is inherited from Latin *charta*, from Greek *khártēs* 'papyrus' (EtymDB).
