# (cambiar un plan) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-02, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *coincidir* and *decidir*. The message you are about to read
turns on both.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-02, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-03, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-04, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-05] -->

Read it once straight through, then answer.

> Hola: al final no puedo el jueves, me **coincide** con el curso. Perdona, me
> **equivoqué** de semana al apuntarlo. ¿**Cenamos** el viernes? Yo **tardo**
> media hora en llegar, así que sobre las nueve.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: why Thursday is impossible]
- [YOU SAY: what mistake the writer admits]
- [YOU SAY: what is proposed instead, and at what time]

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-01, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-02, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-03, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-04, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-05] -->

Now write the reply. Say aloud:

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: Friday clashes with my class]
- [YOU SAY: I got the week wrong too]
- [YOU SAY: I take twenty minutes to get there]
- [YOU SAY: let us decide now and have dinner on Saturday]

**Every sentence there is an ordinary message between two friends**, and none of
it was sayable seven chapters ago — not because the grammar was missing, but
because the verbs were.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-02, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-03, ES-LEX-C437-PLAN-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] It clashes? (**me coincide**.) I got it wrong? (**me equivoqué**.)
Shall we have dinner? (**¿cenamos?**)
