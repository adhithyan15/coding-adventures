## You'll want to know: भ्रान्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C311-KIMVADANTI311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रान्तिः** — *bhrāntiḥ* — "a misunderstanding; a false belief".

> सा किंवदन्ती केवलं भ्रान्तिः अस्ति।
>
> *sā kiṃvadantī kevalaṃ bhrāntiḥ asti.*
>
> That rumour is only a misunderstanding.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रान्तिः** (*bhrāntiḥ*) comes from the root √bhram, "to wander, roam", as in *bhramati*; MW gives *bhrānti* as wandering, and also as perplexity, error, false opinion (MW).
