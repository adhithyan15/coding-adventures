## You'll want to know: げいじゅつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C466-ARTS466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**げいじゅつ** — *geijutsu* — "the arts; art".

> **まちの げいじゅつの まつりに いきました。** — *machi no geijutsu no matsuri ni ikimashita* — "I went to the town's arts festival."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**げいじゅつ** (*geijutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gei*, "art" (Middle Chinese *ngjiejH*) and *jutsu*, "art" (*zywit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
