## You'll want to know: ਜੂਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C301-PLOUGH301-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੂਲਾ** — *jūlā* — "a yoke".

> ਜੂਲਾ ਬਲਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਧੌਣ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *jūlā baldāṁ dī dhauṇ utte hundā hai.*
>
> The yoke sits on the oxen's necks.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੂਲਾ** (*jūlā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*yugahala*, "plough with yoke"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jūlā*, "crossbar of yoke", under it (CDIAL 10491).
