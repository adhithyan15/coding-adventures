## You'll want to know: guānzhù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C293-PAPER293-05]; assesses=[] -->

*guānzhù* — "to follow a story closely; to keep an eye on".

> *rúguǒ nǐ guānzhù xīnwén, nǐ jiù huì zhīdào* — If you followed the news, you'd know.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guānzhù* is built from 关 'to shut' and *zhù* 'to pour', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaen* and *tsyuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the bar that holds a gate shut' and the second 'to pour into'; how the pair came to mean 'to follow closely' is not settled.
