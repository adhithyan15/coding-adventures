# أتمنى أن (atamannā an) — I hope to

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C289-TAJRIBA289-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-SHAARTU-BI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the trip was a new experience". (*Kānat ar-riḥla tajribatan
jadīda*.) Then "I felt afraid". (*Shaʿartu bi-l-khawf*.)

[PAUSE 1s] That was the past. Now turn ahead: what you hope for, what you
dream of, what you want to become.

## Grammar Lens: atamannā an, aḥlumu bi-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C289-WISH-AN-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C114-WANT114-05, AR-LEX-C62-VERBS62-03, AR-LEX-C143-THINGS143-04, AR-LEX-C113-WANT113-04, AR-LEX-C98-WORLD98-03] -->

*Tamannā*, to wish, from chapter one hundred and fourteen, has the present
*atamannā*, I hope, I wish. *An* and the subjunctive follow it, as after
*urīdu*:

> **أتمنى أن أجد وظيفة جديدة.** — *atamannā an ajida waẓīfa jadīda* — I hope
> to find a new job.

*Ajida* is the subjunctive of *ajidu*, I find, from *wajada*; the *w* has
dropped again.

For a dream, use the verb of *ḥulm*: *aḥlumu bi-*, I dream of, with a noun
after *bi-*: *aḥlumu bi-riḥla ṭawīla*, I dream of a long journey.

## Grammar Lens: what you want to become
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C289-BECOME-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C280-AMAL280-02, AR-LEX-C61-VERBS61-03, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-KANA-ACCUSATIVE-01] -->

*Aṣbaḥa*, to become, behaves like *kāna*: what it becomes takes *-an*.

> **أريد أن أصبح مهندسا.** — *urīdu an uṣbiḥa muhandisan* — I want to become
> an engineer.

*Uṣbiḥa* is its subjunctive, after *an*. In the past: *aṣbaḥtu muhandisan*,
I became an engineer.
