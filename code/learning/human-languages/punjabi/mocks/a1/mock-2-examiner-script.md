# Punjabi A1 mock 2 — confidential examiner and recording script

Do not give this file to the learner. A qualified Punjabi speaker records each
named listening file before administration. Use one documented pronunciation
variety consistently and speak at 90–110 words per minute. Play every named
file twice. Silence used to separate items is part of the mastered recording;
a technical restart replaces a play rather than adding a third play. Reject
and rerecord a mastered part outside its published duration band.

The announcements part must last 60–90 seconds per play. The short-exchanges
part must last 90–120 seconds per play. The personal-account part must last
120–180 seconds per play. Natural silence may complete a band; do not stretch,
repeat, translate, or slow Punjabi unnaturally.

## Speaking — live interlocutor

Give the learner five minutes to prepare parts 2 and 3. Do not show an answer
model or permit a full written script. Repeat each prompt at most once, without
supplying content. Record the complete speaking paper for independent scoring.

### Part 1 — personal interview

Deliver the five prompts in 10–45 seconds total, excluding response time.
Allow 60–120 seconds of candidate response time across the five answers.

1. **ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ?**
2. **ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: ਪਿੰਡ ਜਾਂ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ?**
3. **ਭਾਸ਼ਾ?**
4. **ਕੀ ਕਰਨਾ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ?**
5. **ਕਿਤਾਬ ਜਾਂ ਕਲਮ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ?**

### Part 2 — prepared description

Show this 10–30-word cue during preparation and the exactly 60-second response:
**ਦੋਸਤ ਕੌਣ ਹੈ? ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ? ਭਾਸ਼ਾ ਕੀ ਹੈ? ਕੀ ਕਰਨਾ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ?** Do not
interrupt the first minute. If the learner stops early, ask **ਹੋਰ ਕੁਝ?**

### Part 3 — transactional role-play

Deliver the opening and necessary follow-up in 10–30 seconds total, excluding
response time. Keep the candidate exchange open for 60–120 seconds.

Situation: a small shop. Open with **ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ। ਕਿਤਾਬ ਜਾਂ ਕਲਮ?**
Respond naturally to a book or pen request. Give the price as **ਦਸ**. Ask
**ਹੋਰ ਕੁਝ?** The learner must greet, request one item, ask or acknowledge the
price, and close politely. Do not show or speak an answer model.
