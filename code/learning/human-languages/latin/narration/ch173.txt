(signō, signāre.) To discuss? (disputō, disputāre.) To promise? (prōmittō, prōmittere.) To allow? (permittō, permittere.) To burn? (ārdeō, ārdēre.) To set on fire? (incendō, incendere.) To throw away? (abiciō, abicere.) To calculate? (computō, computāre.) To knock? (pulsō, pulsāre.) To fold? (plicō, plicāre.) To own? (possideō, possidēre.) To make ready in advance? (praeparō, praeparāre.) To lend? (commodō, commodāre.) To propose? (prōpōnō, prōpōnere.)

To slip? (lābor, lābī.) To lighten? (levō, levāre.) To admire? (admīror, admīrārī.) To suspect? (suspicor, suspicārī.) To press? (premō, premere.) To deceive? (fallō, fallere.) To endure? (tolerō, tolerāre.) To found; to hide away? (condō, condere.) To create? (creō, creāre.) To believe? (crēdō, crēdere.) To sow? (serō, serere.) To reap? (metō, metere.) To hunt? (vēnor, vēnārī.) To go fishing? (piscor, piscārī.) To deny? (negō, negāre.) Health? (valētūdō, valētūdinis.) The brain? (cerebrum, cerebrī.) A lung? (pulmō, pulmōnis.) The liver? (iecur, iecoris.) A kidney?
