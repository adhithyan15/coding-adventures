## You'll want to know: مسلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C370-JALDBAZI370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مسلنا** — *masalnā* — "to crush; to squash".

> اگر آپ نے کیڑا مسلا تو وہ مر جائے گا۔
>
> *agar āp ne kīṛā maslā to voh mar jāe gā.*
>
> If you squash the insect, it will die.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پھول مسلا, *us ne phūl maslā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسلنا** (*masalnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *masalnā*, "to rub to pieces", under Sanskrit *maṣati* (CDIAL 9919).
