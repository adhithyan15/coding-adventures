## You'll want to know: माया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C460-MAYA460-01]; assesses=[] -->

**माया** — *māyā* — "affection; tender love".

> आजीची माया सगळ्या मुलांवर असते.
>
> *ājīcī māyā sagḷyā mulāṁvar aste.*
>
> Grandmother's affection is on all the children.

Feminine.

Feminine; मायाळू, loving. In other settings माया also means illusion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माया** (*māyā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *māyā* (CDIAL 10060), which CDIAL glosses "supernatural power, skill; illusion" and, in the lexicons, "compassion".
