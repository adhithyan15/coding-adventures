## You'll want to know: छाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C437-MANSUN437-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छाना** — *chānā* — "to spread over (of clouds); to cover".

> आसमान में काले बादल छा गए।
>
> *āsmān meṁ kāle bādal chā gae.*
>
> Dark clouds spread across the sky.

Past (it …): वह कोहरा पूरे शहर पर छा गया, *vah kohrā pūre śahar par chā gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छाना** (*chānā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *chādayati*, "covers, veils" (CDIAL 5018), which lists Hindi *chānā*, "to thatch, spread around": clouds that छा जाते cover the sky like a roof.
