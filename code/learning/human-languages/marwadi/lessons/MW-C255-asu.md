## You'll want to know: आंसू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C255-TABIYAT255-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आंसू** — *ā̃sū* — "a tear (from the eye)".

> छोरी रोवै है, आंख मांय आंसू है।
>
> *chhorī rovai hai, ā̃kh mā̃y ā̃sū hai.*
>
> The girl is crying; there are tears in her eyes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आंसू** (*ā̃sū*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aśru*, "a tear". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *ā̃sū* (CDIAL 919).
