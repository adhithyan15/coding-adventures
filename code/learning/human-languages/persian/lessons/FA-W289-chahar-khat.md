# Four lines, and the gap that sits inside a word

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C92-WHY92-03, FA-LEX-C158-CHERA158-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson asked for writing **finished on time**. This one asks
for writing that **holds together**.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one person who will read it: a friend, by name.

## You'll want to know — two joiners, one letter that will not join
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C92-WHY92-03, FA-LEX-C158-CHERA158-02, FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-JOINING-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Connected writing is not **longer** writing. Four short lines can be
connected; one long line can fail to be. What makes it connected is that a
sentence stops standing on its own.

[PAUSE 2s] You hold two joiners. **ولی** sets one thing against another.
**چون** gives the reason.

[PAUSE 2s] And both of them carry **و**, the letter that takes the hand of the
letter on its right and offers nothing to the left. So each word breaks open
after it: **و** then **لی**, and **چو** then **ن**. A Persian word can have a
gap inside it.

[PAUSE 2s] That makes your spacing part of the spelling. The gap **between**
words has to be clearly wider than the break **inside** one. Make them equal and
a reader cannot tell whether **ولی** is one word or two.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C92-WHY92-03, FA-LEX-C158-CHERA158-02, FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-JOINING-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **four lines** to the person you picked, right to left.

At least one line must use **ولی**. At least one must use **چون** to give a
reason.

[PAUSE 3s] Use only words you already write. This lesson adds no vocabulary;
the whole exercise is the joining, and a simpler familiar line is better than an
invented one. This attempt is untimed.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C92-WHY92-03, FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-JOINING-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Cover your first line and read the second alone. Does it still make
complete sense without the first? If it does, you wrote a **list**, like the
form one lesson ago.

[PAUSE 2s] Then find every **و** you wrote. Is the break after it smaller than
the space between your words? It should be.

Four untimed lines close this writing rung. They are not the 30 to 40 word
message of the full A1 paper, and they do not prove A1 attainment, readiness,
or human validation.
