## You'll want to know: えんがわ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C400-TOKONOMA400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**えんがわ** — *engawa* — "a veranda (along a Japanese house)".

> **えんがわで おちゃを のみました。** — *engawa de ocha o nomimashita* — "We drank tea on the veranda."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんがわ** (*engawa*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese *en*, "edge" (Middle Chinese *ywenH*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) *gawa*, "side", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
