## You'll want to know: el cansancio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C741-CANSANCIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el cansancio** — "tiredness".

> Siento mucho cansancio.
>
> *I feel very tired.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cansancio* is built on *cansar* "to tire", which comes from Latin *campsāre* "to bend, to sail round a headland" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). How the sense moved to tiring is not settled.
