## You'll want to know: qiūtiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C131-SEASON131-03]; assesses=[] -->

*qiūtiān* — "autumn".

> *qiūtiān bù lěng yě bú rè* — Autumn is neither cold nor hot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiūtiān* is built from *qiū* 'autumn' and 天 'sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjuw* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an older book for the first: the time when grain is ripe.
