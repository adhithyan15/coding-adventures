## You'll want to know: सहमत होना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C347-PAKSH347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सहमत होना** — *sahmat honā* — "to agree".

> मैं तुमसे सहमत हूँ, लेकिन मीरा सहमत नहीं है।
>
> *maiṁ tumse sahmat hūṁ, lekin Mīrā sahmat nahīṃ hai.*
>
> I agree with you, but Mira doesn't.

Past (he …): वह सहमत हुआ, *vah sahmat huā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहमत होना** (*sahmat honā*) joins सहमत to होना, "to be, to become". सहमत is built from two Sanskrit words, *saha*, "together with", and *mata*, "an opinion" (Monier-Williams): of one opinion with someone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
