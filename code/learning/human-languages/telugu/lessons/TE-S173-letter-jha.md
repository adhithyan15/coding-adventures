# ఝ — the breathy first letter in ఝరి

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C152-STREAM-01, TE-LEX-C150-LITERATURE-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-171] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One gentle look back first: **వాఙ్మయం** means "literature," and its
uncommon letter is **ఙ**. Point to **ఙ** without copying it. Today you will keep
that shape distinct from a different uncommon letter.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ఝరి** once. What does it mean? ("A stream" or "mountain
stream.")

[PAUSE 1s] Now slow the first piece: *jha*. That breathy **jh** base letter is
today's whole task.

## Script you'll notice: ఝ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-173]; assesses=[] -->

**ఝ** — *jha*.

In **ఝరి**, the first base letter is **ఝ**. The rest, **రి**, is already
familiar. Read the whole word in two pieces: **ఝ-రి**.

This aspirated letter is uncommon in everyday Telugu vocabulary. That is why
the book gave you the authentic word **ఝరి** before asking you to practise the
shape.

## Writing: ఝ — five movements, five runs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-173] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Follow the filmstrip slowly. Movement 1 circles the broad left bowl. Lift.
Movement 2 circles the middle bowl. Lift again. Movement 3 circles the right
bowl. Movement 4 sweeps through the separate upper flourish. Movement 5 draws
the separate stem downward below the middle.

The numbered route is one attested school-style order; Telugu handwriting can
vary. Keep all five pen-down runs distinct and let the three bowls stay round.
