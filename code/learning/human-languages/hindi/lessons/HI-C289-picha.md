## You'll want to know: पीछा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C289-BHAGA289-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पीछा** — *pīchā* — "a chase; the pursuit".

> अगर मेरे पास साइकिल होती तो मैं चोर का पीछा करता।
>
> *agar mere pās sāikil hotī to maiṁ cor kā pīchā kartā.*
>
> If I had had a bicycle, I would have gone after the thief.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पीछा** (*pīchā*) is the noun behind **पीछे**, behind, from *\*paśca*, the back part, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 7990). **किसी का पीछा करना** is to go after someone: to make yourself the one behind them.
