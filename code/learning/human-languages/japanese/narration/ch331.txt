Japanese, chapter 331: Society.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しゃかい (shakai) — society.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to line up neatly, then the Japanese for to revise.

You'll want to know: しゃかい.
しゃかい — shakai — "society".
しゃかいは かわったと おもいます。 — shakai wa kawatta to omoimasu — "I think society has changed."

The word, taken apart.
しゃかい (shakai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are sha, "association" (Middle Chinese dzyaeX) and kai, "meeting" (hwajH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
