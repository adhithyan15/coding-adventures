## You'll want to know: аккумулятор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C404-KAPOT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**аккумулятор** (*akkumulyátor*) — "a car battery; a rechargeable battery".

> Ночью был сильный холод, и аккумулятор сел.
>
> *Nóchyu byl sílnyy khólod, i akkumulyátor sel.*
>
> It was very cold in the night, and the battery went flat.

*Аккумулятор сел* — the battery is flat. Small batteries for a remote are *батарейки*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*аккумулятор* is usually traced to Latin *accumulātor* 'one who heaps up', from *accumulāre* 'to pile up' (EtymDB lists the Latin words): a battery stores up charge. EtymDB does not list the Russian word, and the route into Russian, probably through German, is not settled.
