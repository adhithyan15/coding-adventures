Russian, chapter 321: Kings and queens.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
король (koról) — a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dragon, then the Russian for to turn into.

You'll want to know: король.
король (koról) — "a king".
Король жил в большом замке. Koról zhil v bolshóm zámke. The king lived in a big castle.
Genitive короля. A Russian ruler was a царь.

The word, taken apart.
король (koról) is inherited through Old East Slavic korolĭ from Proto-Slavic korľĭ "king" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to the name of a great Frankish emperor.

Младший принц любил охоту. Mládshiy prints lyubíl okhótu. The younger prince loved hunting.
A king's son.

The word, taken apart.
принц (prints) was borrowed from German Prinz (EtymDB), which comes from French prince, from Latin prīnceps "first, chief", built on prīmus "first" and capere "to take".
