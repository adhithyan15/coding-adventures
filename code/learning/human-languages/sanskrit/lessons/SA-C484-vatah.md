## You'll want to know: वातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C484-AYURVEDA484-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वातः** — *vātaḥ* — "the wind humour".

> वातः शीते काले वर्धते।
>
> *vātaḥ śīte kāle vardhate.*
>
> The wind humour rises in the cold season.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वातः** (*vātaḥ*) comes from the root √vā, "to blow"; MW gives "wind" and "wind or air as one of the humours of the body" (MW).
