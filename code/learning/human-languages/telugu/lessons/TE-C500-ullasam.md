# ఉల్లాసం (ullāsaṁ) — cheerfulness; gaiety

## You'll want to know: ఉల్లాసం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C500-ANANDAM500-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉల్లాసం** — *ullāsaṁ* — "cheerfulness; gaiety".

> పండుగ రోజు ఇల్లంతా ఉల్లాసంగా ఉంది.
>
> *paṇḍuga rōju illantā ullāsaṅgā undi.*
>
> On the festival day the whole house is cheerful.

ఉల్లాసంగా, cheerfully, in high spirits.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉల్లాసం** (*ullāsaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ullāsa*, both "light" and "joy, merriness" (Monier-Williams).
