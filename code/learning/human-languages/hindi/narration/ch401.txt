Hindi, chapter 401: Gates and Junctions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फाटक (phāṭak) — a gate; a level crossing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to check, then the Hindi for setting off.

You'll want to know: फाटक.
फाटक — phāṭak — "a gate; a level crossing".
फाटक बंद था, इसलिए हमें रुकना पड़ा। phāṭak band thā, isliye hamẽ ruknā paṛā. The crossing gate was shut, so we had to stop.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
फाटक (phāṭak) goes back to phāṭṭakka, "gate, door", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9069), which lists Hindi phāṭak. Beside a railway line it is the barrier that drops across the road.

Lesson 4 of 5.
लाँघना (lā̃ghnā) — to step over; to cross (a line).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a signal, then the Hindi for a junction.

You'll want to know: लाँघना.
लाँघना — lā̃ghnā — "to step over; to cross (a line)".
रेल की लाइन लाँघना ख़तरनाक है। rel kī lāin lā̃ghnā khatarnāk hai. Crossing the railway line on foot is dangerous.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दीवार लाँघी, usne dīvār lā̃ghī.

The word, taken apart.
लाँघना (lā̃ghnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit laṅghayati, "leaps over" (CDIAL 10905); the nasal before gh has become the nasal vowel ā̃.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ताकना (tāknā) — to look out (at); to stare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a junction, then the Hindi for to step over.

You'll want to know: ताकना.
ताकना — tāknā — "to look out (at); to stare".
मीरा खिड़की से खेतों को ताक रही थी। Mīrā khiṛkī se khetoṁ ko tāk rahī thī. Mira was gazing at the fields from the window.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आसमान को ताका, usne āsmān ko tākā.

The word, taken apart.
ताकना (tāknā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit tarkayati, "guesses, thinks" (CDIAL 5716), which lists Hindi tāknā, "to look for". How the sense moved to a long, fixed look is not spelled out there, so take the path as likely, not proven.
