## You'll want to know: процеживать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C334-FARSH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**процеживать** (*protsézhivat*) — "to strain".

> Он процедил бульон.
>
> *On protsedíl bulón.*
>
> He strained the broth.

Imperfective; perfective *процедить* (*protsedít*), *процежу*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*процеживать* is built on *про-* "through" and *цедить* "to strain", inherited from Proto-Slavic *cěditi* "to strain, to filter" (EtymDB).
