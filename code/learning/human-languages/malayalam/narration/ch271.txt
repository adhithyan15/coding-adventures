Malayalam, chapter 271: To borrow, To lend, To build, To break down, To continue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കടം വാങ്ങുക (kaṭaṁ vāṅṅuka) — to borrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to argue, then the Malayalam for to pay attention.

You'll want to know: കടം വാങ്ങുക.
കടം വാങ്ങുക — kaṭaṁ vāṅṅuka — "to borrow".

The word, taken apart.
കടം വാങ്ങുക (kaṭaṁ vāṅṅuka) is native Dravidian, "take a debt": kaṭam, "debt, obligation" (DEDR 1113, with Tamil kaṭam), and vāṅṅuka, "to take, receive" (DEDR 5336).
