## You'll want to know: zwar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C341-OBWOHL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**zwar** — "admittedly (zwar … aber); to be precise (und zwar)".

> Das Hotel ist zwar teuer, aber es liegt direkt am Strand.
>
> *The hotel is expensive, admittedly, but it is right on the beach.*

*Zwar … aber* grants one point and then turns; the verb stays second: *das Hotel ist zwar teuer*. *Und zwar* adds detail: *heute, und zwar um acht*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zwar* is inherited from Middle High German *ze wāre* "in truth, indeed" (EtymDB), from *ze* "to, in" and *wār* "true": "true, but …".
