## You'll want to know: সুর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C525-SUR525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সুর** — *sur* — "a tune; a melody".

> এই গানের সুর সুন্দর।
>
> *Ei gāner sur sundôr.*
>
> This song has a lovely tune.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সুর** (*sur*) goes back to Sanskrit *svara*, "sound, a note" (Monier-Williams), worn down in everyday speech.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
