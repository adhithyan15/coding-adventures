## You'll want to know: ओढा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C443-ODHA443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढा** — *oḍhā* — "a stream; a brook".

> गावाबाहेरून एक लहान ओढा वाहतो.
>
> *gāvābāherūn ek lahān oḍhā vāhto.*
>
> A small stream flows past the village.

Masculine. Plural: ओढे, *oḍhe*.

Smaller than a नदी; in the dry season many ओढे dry up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढा** (*oḍhā*) is a Marathi word for a small stream; its origin is not settled.
