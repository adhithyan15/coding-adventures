## You'll want to know: अहंकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C360-YASHAS360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अहंकारः** — *ahaṅkāraḥ* — "arrogance; ego".

> अहंकारः मित्राणि दूरं नयति।
>
> *ahaṅkāraḥ mitrāṇi dūraṃ nayati.*
>
> Arrogance drives friends away.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अहंकारः** (*ahaṅkāraḥ*) is built from *aham*, "I", and *kāra*, "a making"; MW gives *ahaṃ-kāra* "the making of self, egotism" and "pride, haughtiness" (MW).
