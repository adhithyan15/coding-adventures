## You'll want to know: استرد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C453-QIST453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استرد** — *istaradda* — "to get back (money); to recover".

> استرددت ثمن التذكرة.
>
> *istaradadtu thaman at-tadhkira.*
>
> I got the price of the ticket back.

Present: يسترد, *yastaridd*.

A doubled verb: *istaradda*, "he got back", but *istaradadtu*, "I got back".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استرد** (*istaradda*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-d-d*, "to send back, to return" (Lane). It is Form X, "to ask for back".
