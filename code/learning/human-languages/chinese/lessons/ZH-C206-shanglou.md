## You'll want to know: shànglóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C206-FLOOR206-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shànglóu* — "to go upstairs".

> *wǒmen zuò diàntī shànglóu* — We're taking the lift upstairs.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shànglóu* is built from 上 'to go up' and *lóu* 'a building of several floors', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *luw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to climb'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
