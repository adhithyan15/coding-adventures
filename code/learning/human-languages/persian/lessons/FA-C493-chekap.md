## You'll want to know: چکاپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C493-DOCTOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**چکاپ** (*chekâp*) — "a check-up".

> او هر سال یک چکاپ کامل میکند.
>
> *u har sâl yek chekâp-e kâmel mikonad.*
>
> He has a full check-up every year.

*chekâp kardan* — to have a check-up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chekâp* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *check-up*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
