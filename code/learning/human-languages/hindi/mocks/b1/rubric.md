# Hindi B1 mock rubric

Score either B1 mock against [`task-shapes/b1.json`](../../task-shapes/b1.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are four independent 100-point
papers. The learner must earn at least 60/100 in every paper; compensation is
not permitted.

## Reading — 100 points

Correspondence, public information, narrative, and informational article parts
are worth 25 points each. Divide a part's points equally among its keyed items
and retain one decimal place. Items may test explicit detail, sequence,
paragraph purpose, inference, motive, and writer stance. Credit only an answer
supported by the source, not outside knowledge.

## Listening — 100 points

Transactions, interview, narrative, and short-talk parts are worth 25 points
each. Short transaction and interview items play twice; the connected narrative
and talk play once after an unscored orienting preview. Delivery is 130–150
words per minute. Score keyed responses only. No transcript, Romanization,
dictionary, translator, or learner-controlled audio is allowed.

## Writing — 100 points

The 50–70 word functional text is worth 35 points: task fulfilment 10, required
content 7, reader/purpose/register 6, organisation 4, vocabulary and grammar 5,
and orthographic control 3. The 130–170 word connected text is worth 65 points:
task fulfilment and development 18, organisation and cohesion 13, audience and
register 9, vocabulary and grammar 17, and orthographic control 8. A response
outside the range loses only unevidenced content. A Romanized, translated, or
copied answer earns no independent-writing credit.

## Speaking — 100 points

The interview is worth 20 points, prepared three-minute account 30,
collaborative planning 30, and follow-up discussion 20. In each part award 30%
for task fulfilment and development, 25% for interaction and repair, 25% for
Hindi vocabulary and grammar, 10% for organisation, and 10% for pronunciation
and fluency. The learner may use paper keywords made during ten minutes of
preparation, not a written answer script. A paired candidate is scored only on
their own contribution; a partner's strength cannot compensate.

## Readiness boundary

The mocks become readiness evidence only after clean timed administrations and
qualified human review of stimuli, recordings, productive responses, and
scoring. Until then, the project records an executable target, not a proven
pass.
