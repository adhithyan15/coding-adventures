## You'll want to know: بلاط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C340-HUKM340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بلاط** — *balāṭ* — "a royal court; paving stones".

> كان الشاعر يعمل في بلاط الملك.
>
> *kāna ash-shāʿir yaʿmalu fī balāṭ al-malik.*
>
> The poet worked at the king’s court.

At home, بلاط is floor tiles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بلاط** (*balāṭ*) is given by Lane under *b-l-ṭ* as stones laid for paving. It was borrowed from Aramaic *pallāṭīn* (EtymDB); the sense of a royal court is usually traced further to Latin *palatium*, a palace.
