## You'll want to know: اقرار کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C321-QASAM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اقرار کردن** (*eqrâr kardan*) — "to admit, to own up".

> پسر اقرار کرد که پنجره را شکسته است.
>
> *Pesar eqrâr kard ke panjere râ shekaste ast.*
>
> The boy admitted that he had broken the window.

Present *eqrâr mikonad*. Close in meaning to "to confess".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eqrâr* is borrowed from Arabic *iqrār* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "acknowledgement". *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
