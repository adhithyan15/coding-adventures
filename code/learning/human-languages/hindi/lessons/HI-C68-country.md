## You'll want to know: देश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**देश** (*desh*) — "a country".

Sanskrit देश (*deśa*) meant a place, a region, a stretch of land — and it comes
from the root दिश् (*diś-*), "to point out, to show". A देश began as whatever a
pointing hand took in.

That root travelled. Latin *dīcere*, "to say", and *index*, the pointing finger,
grew from the same ancient root as दिश्. The finger you use to show someone
where you are from is the finger the word is named after.

Every Hindi form you will ever fill in has a देश line on it, and every
conversation that starts with a greeting reaches it within a minute.
