## You'll want to know: બંદૂક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C290-DAKU290-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બંદૂક** — *bandūk* — "a gun".

> ડાકુએ કહ્યું કે જો કોઈ ભાગશે તો તે બંદૂક ચલાવશે.
>
> *ḍākue kahyũ ke jo koī bhāgśe to te bandūk calāvśe.*
>
> The bandit said that if anyone ran, he would fire his gun.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બંદૂક** (*bandūk*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bunduq*, a hazelnut and then a small round pellet; the step to a gun is said to have come through the pellet bow. It is feminine, and a gun is fired with **ચલાવવું**: **બંદૂક ચલાવી**.
