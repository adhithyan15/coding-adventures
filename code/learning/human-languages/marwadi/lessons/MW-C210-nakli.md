## You'll want to know: नकली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C210-CHOOSE210-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नकली** — *naklī* — "fake".

> ये घड़ी नकली है।
>
> *ye ghaṛī naklī hai.*
>
> This watch is a fake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नकली** (*naklī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naql*, "a copy".
