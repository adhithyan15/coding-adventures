## You'll want to know: कोरणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C281-CHAK281-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कोरणो** — *korṇo* — "to carve".

> काको लकड़ी कोरै है।
>
> *kāko lakṛī korai hai.*
>
> My uncle carves wood.

Past (I …): म्हैं लकड़ी कोरी, *mhaĩ lakṛī korī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोरणो** (*korṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kōrati*, "scrapes"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *kornā*, "to scrape or scoop out", and Gujarati *korvũ*, "to scoop, carve" (CDIAL 3530).
