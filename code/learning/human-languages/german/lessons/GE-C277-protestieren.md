## You'll want to know: protestieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C277-STREIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**protestieren** — "to protest".

> Viele Bürger protestierten gegen die neue Straße.
>
> *Many citizens protested against the new road.*

With *gegen*: *gegen etwas protestieren*. Perfekt without *ge-*: *hat protestiert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protestieren* was borrowed from French *protester*, from Latin *prōtestārī* (EtymDB) "to declare in public", built on *testis* "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
