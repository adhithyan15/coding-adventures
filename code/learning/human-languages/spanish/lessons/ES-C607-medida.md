## You'll want to know: la medida
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C607-AMBIENTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la medida** — "the measure, the step; the size".

> El gobierno tomó nuevas medidas.
>
> *The government took new measures.*

*Tomar medidas* — to take steps. *A medida que* — as.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*medida* is built on *medir*, which is inherited from spoken Latin *metire*, Latin *metiri* 'to measure' (EtymDB).
