## You'll want to know: die Mülltonne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C528-MUELL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Mülltonne** — "dustbin, wheelie bin".

> Am Dienstag stellen wir die Mülltonne an die Straße.
>
> *On Tuesday we put the bin out on the street.*

Plural: *die Mülltonnen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Mülltonne* is built on *Müll* "rubbish" and *Tonne* "barrel, drum", inherited from Old High German *tunna* (EtymDB).
