### Venture positioned formatting and clipping

- Added a reusable `layout-positioned` contract for relative, absolute, fixed,
  and sticky geometry, insets, stable z-order, overflow, scroll extents, and
  producer diagnostics.
- Integrated computed CSS, shared block/flex/grid dispatch, backend-neutral
  clips, fixed/sticky viewport scrolling, clip-aware link hit testing, and a
  deterministic browser fixture consumed by the shared host pipeline.
