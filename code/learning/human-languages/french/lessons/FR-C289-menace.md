## You'll want to know: la menace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C289-AGRESSION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la menace** — "the threat".

> Elle a reçu des menaces au téléphone.
>
> *She got threats over the phone.*

*Une menace de mort* — a death threat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*menace* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *minācia* "a threat" (EtymDB), built on Latin *mināx* "threatening".
