## You'll want to know: इकलौता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C502-POTA502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**इकलौता** — *iklautā* — "only (child)".

> अरुण अपने माँ बाप का इकलौता बेटा है।
>
> *Aruṇ apne māṁ bāp kā iklautā beṭā hai.*
>
> Arun is his parents' only son.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इकलौता** (*iklautā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *iklautā*, "only begotten, only son", under *\*ēkkalaputra*, a form it reconstructs from *ēkkala*, "alone", and *putra*, "son" (CDIAL 2507). *akelā*, "alone", comes from the same *ēkkala*.
