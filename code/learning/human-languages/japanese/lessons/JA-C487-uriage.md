## You'll want to know: うりあげ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C487-SHOP487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うりあげ** — *uriage* — "sales; takings".

> **せんしゅうの うりあげは よかったです。** — *senshū no uriage wa yokatta desu* — "Last week's sales were good."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うりあげ** (*uriage*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *uri*, "selling" and *age*, "raising" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
