## You'll want to know: でし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C480-MAKE480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**でし** — *deshi* — "an apprentice; a pupil".

> **だいくの でしは まいにち はたらきます。** — *daiku no deshi wa mainichi hatarakimasu* — "The carpenter's apprentice works every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**でし** (*deshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *de*, "younger brother" (Middle Chinese *dejX*) and *shi*, "child" (*tsiX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
