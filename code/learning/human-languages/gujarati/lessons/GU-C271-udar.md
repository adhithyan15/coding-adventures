## You'll want to know: ઉદાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C271-MANNERS271-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદાર** — *udār* — "generous".

> **અમારા પડોશી ઉદાર નથી.** — *amārā paḍośī udār nathī* — "Our neighbours are not generous."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદાર** (*udār*) is Sanskrit *udārá*, "exciting" and later "lofty, noble", taken over as a tatsama (CDIAL 1935).
