## You'll want to know: निरन्तरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C393-VIJAYA393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निरन्तरम्** — *nirantaram* — "without a break; constantly".

> यदि भवान् निरन्तरं पठति, तर्हि परीक्षायाम् उत्तमः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān nirantaraṃ paṭhati, tarhi parīkṣāyām uttamaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you study without a break, you will do well in the exam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निरन्तरम्** (*nirantaram*) is built from *nir*, "without", and *antara*, "an interval"; MW gives *nir-antaram* "closely" and "constantly, continually" (MW).
