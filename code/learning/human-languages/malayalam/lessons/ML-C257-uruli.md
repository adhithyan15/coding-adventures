## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉരുളി** (*uruḷi*) is native Dravidian, "the round one", from *uruḷ*, "circular, round" (DEDR 664, with Tamil *uruḷ*). DEDR 664 compares Sanskrit *uṇḍeraka*.
