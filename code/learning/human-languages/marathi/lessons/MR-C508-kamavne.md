## You'll want to know: कमावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C508-PAGAR508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कमावणे** — *kamāvṇe* — "to earn".

> अरुणने पहिल्या नोकरीत थोडे पैसे कमावले.
>
> *aruṇne pahilyā nokrīt thoḍe paise kamāvle.*
>
> Arun earned a little money in his first job.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पैसे कमावले, *tyāne paise kamāvle*.

With पैसे the past is plural: पैसे कमावले.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कमावणे** (*kamāvṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *kamāviṇẽ*, "works, earns", under *\*karmāpayati* (CDIAL 2897), built on Sanskrit *karman*, "work".
