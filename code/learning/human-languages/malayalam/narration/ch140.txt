Malayalam, chapter 140: In Class.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പേന (pēna) — a pen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a month, then the Malayalam for a year.

You'll want to know: പേന.
പേന (pēna) — "a pen". ഒരു പേന തരൂ — "please give me a pen".

What you've built.
The first thing you ask to borrow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: pēna]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pēna, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask oru pēna tarū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a month, then the Malayalam for a year, then say pēna again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does പേന (pēna) mean? ("A pen".) Say it once more.
