## You'll want to know: xìnghǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C236-ODD236-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnghǎo* — "luckily; fortunately".

> *xìnghǎo nǐ lái le* — Thank goodness you came.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnghǎo* is built from *xìng* 'fortunate' and 好 'good', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *heangX* and *xawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as getting the good and escaping the bad.

## What you've built
