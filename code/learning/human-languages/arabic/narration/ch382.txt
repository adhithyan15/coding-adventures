Arabic, chapter 382: Protest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
احتجاج (iḥtijāj) — a protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for violence, then the Arabic for to suffer.

You'll want to know: احتجاج.
احتجاج — iḥtijāj — "a protest".
خرج العمال في احتجاج على الرواتب. kharaja al-ʿummāl fī iḥtijāj ʿalā ar-rawātib. The workers came out in protest over wages.

The word, taken apart.
احتجاج (iḥtijāj) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-j-j, "to make for; to overcome in argument" (Lane). Iḥtijāj is the Form VIII verbal noun, bringing an argument against: a protest.

You'll want to know: كرامة.
كرامة — karāma — "dignity".
لكل إنسان كرامة. li-kulli insān karāma. Every human being has dignity.

The word, taken apart.
كرامة (karāma) is built on the native Arabic root k-r-m, "to be noble, generous" (Lane). Karāma is nobility: dignity. كرم, generosity, is from the same root.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تظاهر (taẓāhara) — to demonstrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a protest, then the Arabic for dignity.

You'll want to know: تظاهر.
تظاهر — taẓāhara — "to demonstrate".
تظاهر الطلاب أمام الجامعة. taẓāhara aṭ-ṭullāb amāma al-jāmiʿa. The students demonstrated in front of the university.
Present: يتظاهر, yataẓāhar.
Also to pretend: تظاهر (taẓāhara) بالنوم, pretended to sleep.

The word, taken apart.
تظاهر (taẓāhara) is built on the native Arabic root ẓ-h-r, "to appear, to come out" (Lane). Lane gives Form VI for appearing and for people backing one another; modern Arabic uses it for a public show, to demonstrate, and with بـ for a false show, to pretend.

Lesson 5 of 5.
إضراب (iḍrāb) — a strike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to demonstrate, then the Arabic for to protest.

You'll want to know: إضراب.
إضراب — iḍrāb — "a strike".
أعلن العمال إضرابا. aʿlana al-ʿummāl iḍrāban. The workers announced a strike.

The word, taken apart.
إضراب (iḍrāb) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-r-b, "to strike, to beat" (Lane). Iḍrāb is the Form IV verbal noun; aḍraba ʿan is to turn away from a thing, and so to refuse to work: a strike.
