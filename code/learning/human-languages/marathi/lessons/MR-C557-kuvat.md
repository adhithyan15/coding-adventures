## You'll want to know: कुवत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C557-KUVAT557-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कुवत** — *kuvat* — "capacity; means".

> घर घेण्याची आमची अजून कुवत नाही.
>
> *ghar gheṇyācī āmcī ajūn kuvat nāhī.*
>
> We do not yet have the means to buy a house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुवत** (*kuvat*) is a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic *quwwa*, "strength".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
