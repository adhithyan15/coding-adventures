# ਜਾਂਦਾ, ਜਾਂਦੀ — goes, every day

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-JANA, PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183, PA-LEX-C100-TIME100-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਜਾਣਾ**, "to go", then **ਸੌਂਦਾ**, "sleeps". Then *every day*:
**ਰੋਜ਼**.

## Grammar Lens: a stem that ends in a vowel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211]; assesses=[PA-LEX-JANA, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05, PA-LEX-MAIN-03] -->

Take **-ਣਾ** off **ਜਾਣਾ** and the stem **ਜਾ** ends in a vowel. Before **-ਦਾ** such
a stem takes a nasal, the dot **ਂ**, as **ਸੌਂਦਾ** does:

> **ਜਾ** + **ਂ** + **ਦਾ** → **ਜਾਂਦਾ**

> **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** — *maiṁ roz sakūl jāndā hāṁ.* — I go to school every day (a man speaking).

> **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।** — *maiṁ roz sakūl jāndī hāṁ.* — I go to school every day (a woman speaking).

The ending agrees with the one who goes, as in **ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ**. The
helper **ਹਾਂ** stays at the end.
