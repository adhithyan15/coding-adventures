## You'll want to know: ವಿಜಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C373-UDAYA373-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಜಯ** — *vijaya* — "a triumph; a victory".

> ಯುದ್ಧದಲ್ಲಿ ವಿಜಯದ ನಂತರ ರಾಜ ಊರಿಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗಿದನು.
>
> *yuddhadalli vijayada nantara rāja ūrige hintirugidanu.*
>
> After the victory in the war the king returned to the town.

ವಿಜಯ is the grand word; ಗೆಲುವು is the everyday one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಜಯ** (*vijaya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vijaya*, "contest for victory, victory, conquest, triumph" (Monier-Williams), from *ji*, "to win".
