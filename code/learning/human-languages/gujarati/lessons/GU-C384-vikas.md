## You'll want to know: વિકાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C384-SAFAL384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વિકાસ** — *vikās* — "development".

> જો સારા રસ્તા બનશે તો શહેરનો વિકાસ ઝડપી થશે.
>
> *jo sārā rastā banśe to śaherno vikās jhaḍpī thaśe.*
>
> If good roads are built, the city will develop faster.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિકાસ** (*vikās*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vikāsa*, "expanding, blossoming" (Monier-Williams).
