Sanskrit, chapter 293: The Case in Court.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
दोषः (doṣaḥ) — guilt; a fault.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a secret, then the Sanskrit for reveals.

You'll want to know: दोषः.
दोषः — doṣaḥ — "guilt; a fault".
चौरस्य दोषः स्पष्टः अस्ति। caurasya doṣaḥ spaṣṭaḥ asti. The thief's guilt is clear.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दोषः (doṣaḥ) comes from the root √duṣ, "to become bad"; MW gives doṣa as a fault or defect and also as an offence or guilt (MW).

Lesson 2 of 6.
दण्डयति (daṇḍayati) — punishes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for reveals, then the Sanskrit for guilt.

You'll want to know: दण्डयति.
दण्डयति — daṇḍayati — "punishes".
यदि दोषः अस्ति, तर्हि न्यायाधीशः दण्डयति। yadi doṣaḥ asti, tarhi nyāyādhīśaḥ daṇḍayati. If there is guilt, then the judge punishes.
Past (he …): सः चौरं दण्डितवान्, saḥ cauraṃ daṇḍitavān.

The word, taken apart.
दण्डयति (daṇḍayati) is built on daṇḍa, "a stick", and so "punishment"; MW gives daṇḍayati as a verb made from it, to chastise or punish (MW).

चौरः कारागारे वसति। cauraḥ kārāgāre vasati. The thief lives in prison.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
कारागारम् (kārāgāram) is built from kārā, "confinement", and āgāra, "a house"; MW gives kārāgāra as a gaol (MW).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sets free, then the Sanskrit for a prison.

You'll want to know: शृंखला.
शृंखला — śṛṅkhalā — "a chain".
चौरस्य पादे शृंखला अस्ति। caurasya pāde śṛṅkhalā asti. There is a chain on the thief's foot.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
शृंखला (śṛṅkhalā) MW gives śṛṅkhalā as a chain or fetter, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
