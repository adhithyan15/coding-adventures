## You'll want to know: सिद्धिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C386-SIDDHI386-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिद्धिः** — *siddhiḥ* — "an accomplishment".

> यदि प्रयत्नः दृढः, तर्हि सिद्धिः अवश्यं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi prayatnaḥ dṛḍhaḥ, tarhi siddhiḥ avaśyaṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the effort is firm, success will surely come.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिद्धिः** (*siddhiḥ*) comes from the root √sidh, "to succeed"; MW gives *siddhi* "accomplishment, performance, fulfilment, success" (MW).
