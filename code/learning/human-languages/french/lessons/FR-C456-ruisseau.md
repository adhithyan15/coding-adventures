## You'll want to know: le ruisseau
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C456-RUISSEAU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le ruisseau** — "the stream, the brook".

> Un petit ruisseau traverse le jardin.
>
> *A little stream runs across the garden.*

Plural *les ruisseaux*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruisseau* goes back to Vulgar Latin *rīvuscellus* (EtymDB), a small *rīvus* 'stream'.
