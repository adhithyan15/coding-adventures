# વાજબી (vājbī) — reasonable; fair

## You'll want to know: વાજબી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C349-SAMAN349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વાજબી** — *vājbī* — "reasonable; fair".

> દુકાનદારે વાજબી ભાવ માગ્યો.
>
> *dukāndāre vājbī bhāv māgyo.*
>
> The shopkeeper asked a fair price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાજબી** (*vājbī*) is usually traced to Arabic *wājib*, "due, proper", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
