## You'll want to know: जाळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C172-JHADU172-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जाळो** — *jāḷo* — "a cobweb".

> भींत माथै जाळो है।
>
> *bhī̃t māthai jāḷo hai.*
>
> There's a cobweb on the wall.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जाळो** (*jāḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jāla*, "a net, a snare"; CDIAL lists Marwari *jāḷ*, "a net" (CDIAL 5213). A cobweb is the spider's net.
