## You'll want to know: सूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C536-GAYAN536-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर** — *sūr* — "a musical note; a tune".

> गायिकेचा पहिलाच सूर खूप गोड होता.
>
> *gāyikecā pahilāc sūr khūp goḍ hotā.*
>
> The singer's very first note was very sweet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर** (*sūr*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *sūr*, "musical note", under Sanskrit *sura*, "sound", a variant of *svara* (CDIAL 13498).
