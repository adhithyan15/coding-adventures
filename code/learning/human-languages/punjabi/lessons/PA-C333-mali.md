## You'll want to know: ਮਾਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C333-BARBER333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਲੀ** — *mālī* — "a gardener".

> ਮਾਲੀ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਬਾਗ਼ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *mālī savere bāgh vich pāṇī pāundā hai.*
>
> The gardener waters the garden in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਲੀ** (*mālī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mālin*, "garlanded; a gardener"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *mālī*, "garlanded; gardener", under it (CDIAL 10094).
