# যদি … তাহলে … (jôdi … tāhôle …) — if, then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C273-DHORE-01, BN-LEX-C159-KARON159-03, BN-LEX-C218-KARON218-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "for two years". (*dui bôchhôr dhôre*.) Then **যদি**, "if", and **তাহলে**, "then, in that case": you met them in chapters one hundred and fifty-nine and two hundred and eighteen.

## You'll want to know: if, then
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-THEN-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C159-KARON159-03, BN-LEX-C218-KARON218-04, BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-NOT-01, BN-LEX-C148-ACT148-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

> **যদি কাল ঝড় হয়, তাহলে আমরা যাব না।**
>
> *jôdi kāl jhôṛ hôy, tāhôle āmrā jābô nā.* — "If there's a storm tomorrow, we won't go."

The **যদি** half takes the present, even about tomorrow: **হয়**, not **হবে**. The **তাহলে** half takes the future. **তাহলে** may be left out: **যদি তুমি আসো, আমি রান্না করব** (*jôdi tumi āso, āmi rānnā kôrbô*), "if you come, I'll cook".

## You'll want to know: if not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-NOT-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C187-SOMOY187-01, BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-04, BN-LEX-C83-TOWN83-02] -->

Inside a **যদি** half, **না** comes before the verb, not after it:

> **যদি ট্রেন সময়মতো না আসে, তাহলে আমি বাসে যাব।**
>
> *jôdi ṭren sômôymôto nā āse, tāhôle āmi bāse jābô.* — "If the train doesn't come on time, I'll take the bus."
