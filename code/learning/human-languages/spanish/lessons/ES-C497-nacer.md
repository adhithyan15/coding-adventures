## You'll want to know: nacer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C497-FAMILIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**nacer** — "to be born".

> Nací en Sevilla.
>
> *I was born in Seville.*

It takes a *z* in the first person present: *nazco*.
