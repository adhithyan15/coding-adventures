## You'll want to know: മാറാല
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C572-VITTUJOLI572-05]; assesses=[] -->

**മാറാല** — *māṟāla* — "a cobweb".

> പഴയ മുറിയുടെ മൂലകളിൽ മാറാല ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു.
>
> *paḻaya muṟiyuṭe mūlakaḷil māṟāla uṇṭāyirunnu.*
>
> There were cobwebs in the corners of the old room.

Old cobwebs full of dust; a fresh web is ചിലന്തിവല.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മാറാല** (*māṟāla*) is a household word; no source we can open explains it, so its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
