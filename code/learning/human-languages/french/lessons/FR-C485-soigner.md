## You'll want to know: soigner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C485-GUERIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**soigner** — "to look after, to treat, to nurse".

> Le vétérinaire a bien soigné notre chat.
>
> *The vet treated our cat well.*

*Se soigner* — to look after oneself. *Soigné* — neat, well-groomed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soigner* is built on *soin* "care", usually traced to a Frankish word *sunnija* "care, concern" (EtymDB).
