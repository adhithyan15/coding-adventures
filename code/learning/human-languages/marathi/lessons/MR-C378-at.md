## You'll want to know: अट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C378-ATI378-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अट** — *aṭ* — "a condition (stipulation)".

> करारात एक महत्त्वाची अट होती.
>
> *karārāt ek mahattvācī aṭ hotī.*
>
> There was an important condition in the contract.

Feminine. Plural: अटी, *aṭī*.

अटीवर, on condition: एका अटीवर हो म्हणणे, to say yes on one condition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अट** (*aṭ*) is usually traced to the root *\*aṭṭ-*, "obstruct, stop", under which CDIAL lists Marathi *aṭ*, "obstruction" (CDIAL 179): a condition holds a thing back.
