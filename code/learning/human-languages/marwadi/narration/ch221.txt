Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 221: Rain to Come.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मेह (meh) — the rains; rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to change, then the Marwadi for to find out.

You'll want to know: मेह.
मेह — meh — "the rains; rain".
सावण मांय मेह आवै है। sāvaṇ mā̃y meh āvai hai. The rains come in the monsoon month.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मेह (meh) is inherited from Sanskrit megha, "a cloud, rain"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari meha, "rain" (CDIAL 10302).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बरसणो (barasṇo) — to rain; to pour down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to find out, then the Marwadi for the rains.

You'll want to know: बरसणो.
बरसणो — barasṇo — "to rain; to pour down".
सावण मांय मेह घणो बरसै है। sāvaṇ mā̃y meh ghaṇo barsai hai. In the monsoon month it rains hard.
Past (it …): वो मेह बरस्यो, vo meh barsyo.

The word, taken apart.
बरसणो (barasṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit varṣati, "rains"; CDIAL lists Hindi barasnā, "to rain". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11394). बरसात, rain, belongs to the same family.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बीजळी (bījḷī) — lightning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the rains, then the Marwadi for to rain.

You'll want to know: बीजळी.
बीजळी — bījḷī — "lightning".
बादल मांय बीजळी है। bādal mā̃y bījḷī hai. There's lightning in the clouds.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बीजळी (bījḷī) is inherited from Sanskrit vidyullatā, "forked lightning", built from vidyut, "lightning", and latā, "a creeper"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bījalī (CDIAL 11745).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to rain, then the Marwadi for lightning.

You'll want to know: गाजणो.
गाजणो — gājṇo — "to thunder; to roar".
आज रात बादल गाजै है। āj rāt bādal gājai hai. Tonight the clouds are thundering.
Past (it …): वो बादल गाज्यो, vo bādal gājyo.

The word, taken apart.
गाजणो (gājṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit garjati, "thunders, roars"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb gājaï, "thunders" (CDIAL 4046).

Lesson 5 of 5.
झड़ी (jhaṛī) — a spell of steady rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for lightning, then the Marwadi for to thunder.

You'll want to know: झड़ी.
झड़ी — jhaṛī — "a spell of steady rain".
सावण मांय झड़ी लागै है। sāvaṇ mā̃y jhaṛī lāgai hai. In the monsoon month the rain sets in for days.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
झड़ी (jhaṛī) is inherited from a reconstructed jhaḍī, "a rainstorm"; CDIAL lists Marwari jhaṛī, "continuous heavy rain", and points to a Dravidian word for fine steady rain (CDIAL 5329).
