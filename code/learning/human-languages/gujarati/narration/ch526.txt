Gujarati, chapter 526: Notes and Rhythm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સૂર (sūr) — a musical note.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to frame (a picture), then the Gujarati for an exhibition.

You'll want to know: સૂર.
સૂર — sūr — "a musical note".
મીરાનો સૂર ઘણો મીઠો છે. Mīrāno sūr ghaṇo mīṭho che. Mira sings very sweetly.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સૂર (sūr) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit sura, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "a modulation of the voice, a tune" (CDIAL 13498).

Lesson 3 of 5.

ગાયક — gāyak — "a singer".
આ ગાયકનાં ગીતો મને ઘણાં ગમે છે. ā gāyaknā̃ gīto mane ghaṇā̃ game che. I like this singer's songs very much.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ગાયક (gāyak) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit gāyaka, "a singer" (Monier-Williams), from the root of ગાવું, to sing.

Lesson 5 of 5.
છેડવું (cheḍvũ) — to strike up (a tune).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a singer, then the Gujarati for a raga.

You'll want to know: છેડવું.
છેડવું — cheḍvũ — "to strike up (a tune)".
મીરાએ વાંસળી પર એક મીઠો સૂર છેડ્યો. Mīrāe vā̃sḷī par ek mīṭho sūr cheḍyo. Mira struck up a sweet tune on the flute.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સૂર છેડ્યો, teṇe sūr cheḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
છેડવું (cheḍvũ) is filed by CDIAL under ciḍ, "to be angry", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to irritate" (CDIAL 4794); Gujarati also uses it for touching off a tune on an instrument.
