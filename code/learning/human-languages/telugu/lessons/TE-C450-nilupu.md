## You'll want to know: నిలుపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C450-PARKINGU450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నిలుపు** — *nilupu* — "to pull up; to park".

> అతను కారును చెట్టు కింద నిలిపాడు.
>
> *atanu kārunu ceṭṭu kinda nilipāḍu.*
>
> He parked the car under a tree.

Past (I …): నిలిపాను, *nilipānu*.

The causative of నిలుచు, to stand: to make something stand. It also means to stop a process.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిలుపు** (*nilupu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3675 lists Telugu *nilupu*, "to cause to stand, to stop", beside *nilucu*, "to stand".
