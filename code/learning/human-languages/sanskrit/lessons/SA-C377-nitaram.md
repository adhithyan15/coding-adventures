## You'll want to know: नितराम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C377-ATIVA377-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नितराम्** — *nitarām* — "completely; wholly".

> यदि भवान् नितरां श्रान्तः, तर्हि विश्रामं करोतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān nitarāṃ śrāntaḥ, tarhi viśrāmaṃ karotu.*
>
> If you are completely worn out, take a rest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नितराम्** (*nitarām*) is built on *ni*, "down", with the comparative ending *-tarām*; MW gives *ni-tarām* "completely, wholly, entirely" (MW).
