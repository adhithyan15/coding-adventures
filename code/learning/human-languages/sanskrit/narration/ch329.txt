Sanskrit, chapter 329: At the Temple.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पूजा (pūjā) — worship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for offers, then the Sanskrit for a blessed offering.

You'll want to know: पूजा.
पूजा — pūjā — "worship".
प्रातः मन्दिरे पूजा भवति। prātaḥ mandire pūjā bhavati. There is worship in the temple in the morning.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पूजा (pūjā) comes from the root √pūj, "to honour, worship", as in pūjayati; MW gives pūjā as honour, worship, respect, reverence (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आरात्रिकम् (ārātrikam) — the waving of lamps in worship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bell, then the Sanskrit for incense.

You'll want to know: आरात्रिकम्.
आरात्रिकम् — ārātrikam — "the waving of lamps in worship".
सायं मन्दिरे आरात्रिकं भवति। sāyaṃ mandire ārātrikaṃ bhavati. In the evening there is the waving of lamps in the temple.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
आरात्रिकम् (ārātrikam) is built on ā and rātri, "night"; MW gives ārātrika as the light waved at night before an image, and the ceremony itself (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रार्थयते (prārthayate) — prays for; asks for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for incense, then the Sanskrit for the waving of lamps.

You'll want to know: प्रार्थयते.
प्रार्थयते — prārthayate — "prays for; asks for".
वृद्धा माता शान्तिं प्रार्थयते। vṛddhā mātā śāntiṃ prārthayate. The old mother prays for peace.
Past (she …): सा शान्तिं प्रार्थितवती, sā śāntiṃ prārthitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
प्रार्थयते (prārthayate) is built from pra and the root √arth, "to ask, wish"; MW gives prārthayate as to wish for, desire, and to ask a person for something (MW).
