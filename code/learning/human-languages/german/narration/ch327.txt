German, chapter 327: Auf dem Herd.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
köcheln — to simmer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the baking dish, then the German for to brown.

You'll want to know: köcheln.
köcheln — "to simmer".
Die Suppe köchelte eine Stunde. The soup simmered for an hour.
A small form of kochen: gentle cooking.

The word, taken apart.
köcheln is built on kochen "to cook", Old High German kochōn, which EtymDB traces to Latin coquus "a cook", from coquere "to cook"; the ending -eln makes it small and gentle.
