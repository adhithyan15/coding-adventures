# പൊതുജനം (potujanaṁ) — the general public

## You'll want to know: പൊതുജനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C415-SAMUHAM415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**പൊതുജനം** — *potujanaṁ* — "the general public".

> പാർക്ക് പൊതുജനത്തിന് തുറന്നുകൊടുത്തു.
>
> *pārkkŭ potujanattinŭ tuṟannukoṭuttu.*
>
> The park was opened to the public.

Often in the plural: പൊതുജനങ്ങൾ, members of the public.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പൊതുജനം** (*potujanaṁ*) is a Malayalam compound: പൊതു, "common, general", native Dravidian (DEDR 4507), and ജനം, "people", from Sanskrit *jana*, "a person, a people" (CDIAL 5098).
