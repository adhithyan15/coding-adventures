## You'll want to know: 想法
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C349-VIEW349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**想法** — *xiǎngfǎ* — "idea; way of thinking".

> **我觉得你的想法很好** *wǒ juéde nǐ de xiǎngfǎ hěn hǎo* — I think your idea is a good one.

The new character, in pieces: 法 — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left the water piece, three strokes, as on the left of 汉; on the right 去 *qù*, five strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

Two third tones meet in this word: xiǎng is said with a rising tone before fǎ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**想法** (*xiǎngfǎ*) is built from 想 'to think' and 法 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjangX* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to think' and the second 'a rule' and 'a norm'.
