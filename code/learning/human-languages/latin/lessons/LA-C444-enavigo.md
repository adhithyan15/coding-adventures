## You'll want to know: ēnāvigō, ēnāvigāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C444-MALACIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ēnāvigō, ēnāvigāre** — "to sail out, to sail away; to sail across".

> Nāvis ē portū ēnāvigat et in altum it.
>
> *The ship sails out of the harbour and goes out to sea.*

*ēnāvigō, ēnāvigāre, ēnāvigāvī, ēnāvigātum*. With an accusative, to sail right across: *mare ēnāvigāre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ēnāvigō* is built from *ē(x)-* 'out' and *nāvigō* 'to sail' (Lewis & Short).
