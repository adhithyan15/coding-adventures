## You'll want to know: el batido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C499-MERIENDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el batido** — "the milkshake, the smoothie".

> Quiero un batido de fresa.
>
> *I would like a strawberry milkshake.*

In parts of Latin America it is *el licuado*.
