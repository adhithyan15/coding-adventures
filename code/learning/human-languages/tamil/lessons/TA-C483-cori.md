## You'll want to know: சொறி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C483-TOL483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**சொறி** — *coṟi* — "to scratch an itch".

> கொசு கடித்த இடத்தை அவன் சொறிந்தான்.
>
> *kocu kaṭitta iṭattai avaṉ coṟintāṉ.*
>
> He scratched where the mosquito had bitten.

Past (I …): சொறிந்தேன், *coṟintēṉ*.

As a noun, an itchy rash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சொறி** (*coṟi*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2865), both the itch and the scratching.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
