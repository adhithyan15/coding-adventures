Persian, chapter 469: Water in the land.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرداب (mordâb) — a marsh, a swamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the foothills, then the Persian for to climb.

You'll want to know: مرداب.
مرداب (mordâb) — "a marsh, a swamp".
پرنده های زیادی در مرداب زندگی میکنند. Parande-hâ-ye ziyâdi dar mordâb zendegi mikonand. Many birds live in the marsh.
Literally 'dead water': mord 'dead' + âb 'water'.

The word, taken apart.
mordâb is usually analysed as native mord 'dead', from mordan 'to die', and âb 'water', from Middle Persian āb (EtymDB): still, standing water.

Iran's wetlands are winter homes for many birds from the north.

Lesson 3 of 5.
نهر (nahr) — a stream, a brook; a channel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a marsh, then the Persian for a wetland.

You'll want to know: نهر.
نهر (nahr) — "a stream, a brook; a channel".
کنار نهر نشستیم و چای خوردیم. Kenâr-e nahr neshastim va chây khordim. We sat by the stream and drank tea.
In Arabic nahr is a river; in Persian a smaller stream.

The word, taken apart.
nahr was borrowed from Arabic nahr 'river' (EtymDB); in Persian it names a stream or a channel.

Lesson 5 of 5.
جاری شدن (jâri shodan) — to flow, to run (of water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a stream, then the Persian for a dam.

You'll want to know: جاری شدن.
جاری شدن (jâri shodan) — "to flow, to run (of water)".
بعد از باران آب در جوب جاری شد. Ba'd az bârân âb dar jub jâri shod. After the rain water ran in the gutter.
jâri 'flowing' + shodan. Âb-e jâri — running water.

The word, taken apart.
jâri shodan is built from jâri 'flowing', borrowed from Arabic jārī (EtymDB), and the native verb shodan 'to become'.
