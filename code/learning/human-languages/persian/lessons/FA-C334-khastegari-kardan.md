## You'll want to know: خواستگاری کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C334-ASHEQ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خواستگاری کردن** (*khâstegâri kardan*) — "to propose marriage".

> او یک سال بعد از دختر خواستگاری کرد.
>
> *U yek sâl ba'd az dokhtar khâstegâri kard.*
>
> A year later he proposed to the young woman.

The person asked takes *az*. Present *khâstegâri mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khâstegâri kardan* joins the native noun *khâstegâri* "a proposal", from *khâstan* "to want", with *kardan* "to do", inherited Persian.
