Malayalam, chapter 199: Rubbish, Between, Beyond, On this side, At the foot of.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മാലിന്യം (mālinyaṁ) — rubbish, waste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for against, then the Malayalam for around.

You'll want to know: മാലിന്യം.
മാലിന്യം — mālinyaṁ — "rubbish, waste".

The word, taken apart.
മാലിന്യം (mālinyaṁ) from Sanskrit mālinya, "dirtiness", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ. It is built on malina, "dirty".

Lesson 3 of 5.
അപ്പുറം (appuṟaṁ) — beyond, on the far side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for rubbish, then the Malayalam for between.

You'll want to know: അപ്പുറം.
അപ്പുറം — appuṟaṁ — "beyond, on the far side".

The word, taken apart.
അപ്പുറം (appuṟaṁ) is native Dravidian, "that side": the far-pointing base (DEDR 1, which lists it) with puṟam, "side, outside" (DEDR 4333, with Tamil puṟam).
