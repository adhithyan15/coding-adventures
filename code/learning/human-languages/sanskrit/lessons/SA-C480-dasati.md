## You'll want to know: दशति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C480-DAMSA480-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दशति** — *daśati* — "bites".

> सर्पः पथिकं दशति।
>
> *sarpaḥ pathikaṃ daśati.*
>
> The snake bites the traveller.

Past (it …): शुनकः बालं दष्टवान्, *śunakaḥ bālaṃ daṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दशति** (*daśati*) comes from the root √daṃś, "to bite", which drops its nasal in the present *daśati* (MW).
