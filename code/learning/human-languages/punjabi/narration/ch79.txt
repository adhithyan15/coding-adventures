Punjabi, chapter 79: Snake, Big, Small, New, Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸੱਪ (sapp) — a snake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an elephant, then the Punjabi for a monkey.

You'll want to know: ਸੱਪ.
ਸੱਪ — sapp — "a snake".

The word, taken apart.
ਸੱਪ (sapp) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sarpa, "snake" (CDIAL 13271).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a monkey, then the Punjabi for a snake.

You'll want to know: ਵੱਡਾ.
ਵੱਡਾ — vaḍḍā — "big".

The word, taken apart.
ਵੱਡਾ (vaḍḍā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vaḍra, "big" (CDIAL 11225). CDIAL notes that the Sanskrit word is known only from the old word-lists, and doubts a link with vṛddha, "grown".
