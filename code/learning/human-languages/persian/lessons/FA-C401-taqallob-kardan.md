## You'll want to know: تقلب کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C401-MORUR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تقلب کردن** (*taqallob kardan*) — "to cheat".

> او در امتحان تقلب کرد و مردود شد.
>
> *u dar emtehân taqallob kard va mardud shod.*
>
> He cheated in the exam and failed.

*Taqallob* "cheating, fraud" + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تقلب** is usually traced to Arabic *taqallub*, a verbal noun usually explained as turning over or changing; in Persian it is cheating.
