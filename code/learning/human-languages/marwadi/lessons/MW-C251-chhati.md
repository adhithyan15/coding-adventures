## You'll want to know: छाती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C251-KANDHO251-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छाती** — *chhātī* — "the chest".

> म्हारी छाती मांय दरद है।
>
> *mhārī chhātī mā̃y darad hai.*
>
> I have a pain in my chest.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छाती** (*chhātī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*chātti*, "chest, breast"; CDIAL lists Marwari *chātī* under it (CDIAL 5014).
