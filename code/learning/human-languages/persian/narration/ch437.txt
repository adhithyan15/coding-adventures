Persian, chapter 437: Places to stay.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مسافرخانه (mosâferkhâne) — a guesthouse, a cheap inn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a dhow, then the Persian for to drop anchor.

You'll want to know: مسافرخانه.
مسافرخانه (mosâferkhâne) — "a guesthouse, a cheap inn".
شب در یک مسافرخانه ارزان ماندیم. Shab dar yek mosâferkhâne-ye arzân mândim. We spent the night in a cheap guesthouse.
mosâfer 'traveller' + khâne 'house'. Simpler and cheaper than a hotel.

The word, taken apart.
mosâferkhâne is built from mosâfer 'traveller', borrowed from Arabic musāfir, and native khâne 'house', usually traced to Middle Persian xānag.

Lesson 2 of 5.
اقامتگاه (eqâmatgâh) — lodgings, a place to stay; a residence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to drop anchor, then the Persian for a guesthouse.

You'll want to know: اقامتگاه.
اقامتگاه (eqâmatgâh) — "lodgings, a place to stay; a residence".
اقامتگاه (eqâmatgâh) ما نزدیک دریا بود. Eqâmatgâh-e mâ nazdik-e daryâ bud. Our lodgings were near the sea.
Eqâmatgâh-e bumgardi — a guesthouse in a village home, now common in the countryside.

The word, taken apart.
eqâmatgâh is built from eqâmat 'a stay', a loan usually traced to Arabic iqāma 'staying, residing', and native gâh 'place'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پانسیون (pânsiyon) — a boarding house; full board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a guesthouse, then the Persian for lodgings.

You'll want to know: پانسیون.
پانسیون (pânsiyon) — "a boarding house; full board".
دانشجوها در یک پانسیون زندگی میکنند. Dâneshju-hâ dar yek pânsiyon zendegi mikonand. The students live in a boarding house.
Also meals included with a room: bâ pânsiyon — with full board.

The word, taken apart.
pânsiyon was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French pension 'board and lodging'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for lodgings, then the Persian for a boarding house.

You'll want to know: سوئیت.
سوئیت (su'it) — "a suite; a small self-contained flat".
در شمال یک سوئیت کوچک اجاره کردیم. Dar shomâl yek su'it-e kuchek ejâre kardim. In the north we rented a small flat.
At the seaside in Iran a su'it is often a one-room holiday flat with a kitchen.

The word, taken apart.
su'it was borrowed from a European language and is usually traced to French suite 'a set of rooms'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اقامت کردن (eqâmat kardan) — to stay (somewhere), to reside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a boarding house, then the Persian for a suite.

You'll want to know: اقامت کردن.
اقامت کردن (eqâmat kardan) — "to stay (somewhere), to reside".
سه شب در این هتل اقامت کردیم. Se shab dar in hotel eqâmat kardim. We stayed three nights in this hotel.
eqâmat 'stay' + kardan: more formal than mândan.

The word, taken apart.
eqâmat kardan is built from eqâmat 'a stay', a loan usually traced to Arabic iqāma 'staying, residing', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
