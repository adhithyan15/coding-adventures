## You'll want to know: piàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C309-THIEF309-03]; assesses=[] -->

*piàn* — "to cheat; to con".

> *tā piàn le wǒ de qián* — He conned me out of my money.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piàn* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
