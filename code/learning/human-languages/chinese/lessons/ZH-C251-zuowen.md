## You'll want to know: 作文
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C251-ESSAY251-02]; assesses=[] -->

**作文** — *zuòwén* — "essay; composition (at school)".

> *wǒ zài xiě zuòwén* — I'm writing an essay.

The new character, in pieces: 作 — seven strokes: the person piece 亻; then a short stroke falling to the left, a bar, a long vertical, and two short bars to the right of it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**作文** (*zuòwén*) is built from 作 'to make' and 文 'writing', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsak* and *mjun* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to do' and 'to rise', and the second 'a piece of writing'.
