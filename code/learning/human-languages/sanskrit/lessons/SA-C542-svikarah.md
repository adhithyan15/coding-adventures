## You'll want to know: स्वीकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C542-UTKRSTA542-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वीकारः** — *svīkāraḥ* — "acceptance".

> यदि भवतः स्वीकारः अस्ति, तर्हि वयं कार्यम् आरभामहे।
>
> *yadi bhavataḥ svīkāraḥ asti, tarhi vayaṃ kāryam ārabhāmahe.*
>
> If you accept, we will begin the work.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वीकारः** (*svīkāraḥ*) is built from *sva*, "one's own", in the form *svī*, and *kāra*, "making", from the root √kṛ; MW gives *svīkāra* as making one's own, and reception (MW).
