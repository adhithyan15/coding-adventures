## You'll want to know: સ્ક્રૂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C514-DHILU514-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્ક્રૂ** — *skrū* — "a screw".

> બારીનો એક સ્ક્રૂ ખોવાઈ ગયો.
>
> *bārīno ek skrū khovāī gayo.*
>
> One of the window's screws has gone missing.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્ક્રૂ** (*skrū*) is borrowed from English *screw*.
