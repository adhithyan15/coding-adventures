# rīdiculus, rīdicula, rīdiculum — funny, laughable; absurd

## You'll want to know: rīdiculus, rīdicula, rīdiculum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C502-IOCU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rīdiculus, rīdicula, rīdiculum** — "funny, laughable; absurd".

> Histriō in theātrō rīdiculus est.
>
> *The actor is funny in the theatre.*

From *rīdeō* — to laugh. Funny in a good sense, absurd in a bad one. English *ridiculous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**rīdiculus** is built on *rīdeō* "to laugh" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *ridiculous* and *ridicule* come from it.
