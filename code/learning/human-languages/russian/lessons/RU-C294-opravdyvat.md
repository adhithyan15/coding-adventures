## You'll want to know: оправдывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C294-NAKAZANIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**оправдывать** (*oprávdyvat*) — "to acquit; to justify".

> Суд оправдал молодого человека.
>
> *Sud opravdál molodóvo chelovéka.*
>
> The court acquitted the young man.

Imperfective; the perfective is *оправдать* (*opravdát*). Also *оправдать надежды* — to live up to hopes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*оправдывать* is built on *о-* and *правда* "truth", from *правый* "right, fair", an inherited Slavic word: to show someone is in the right. EtymDB lists no parent.
