## You'll want to know: ambūrō, ambūrere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C319-SIPHO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ambūrō, ambūrere** — "to scorch, to singe".

> Ignis manūs eius ambussit, sed vīvus fūgit.
>
> *The fire scorched his hands, but he got away alive.*

*ambūrō, ambūrere, ambussī, ambustum*: to burn all round, not right through.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ambūrō** is built on *ūrō*, "to burn", with *amb-*, "around" (EtymDB): to burn round the edges.
