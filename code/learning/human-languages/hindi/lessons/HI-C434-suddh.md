## You'll want to know: शुद्ध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C434-SHUDDH434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शुद्ध** — *śuddh* — "pure; unmixed".

> सुनार ने कहा कि यह शुद्ध सोना है।
>
> *sunār ne kahā ki yah śuddh sonā hai.*
>
> The goldsmith said that this is pure gold.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शुद्ध** (*śuddh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śuddha*, "cleansed, clear, pure" (Monier-Williams): gold with nothing mixed in, or water fit to drink.
