## You'll want to know: dāngrán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C217-FINE217-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dāngrán* — "of course".

> *dāngrán kěyǐ* — Of course you can.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dāngrán* is built from *dāng* 'to match' and *rán* 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tang* and *nyen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'like this' and 'it is so'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
