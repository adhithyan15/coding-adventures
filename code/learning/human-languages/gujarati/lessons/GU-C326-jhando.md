## You'll want to know: ઝંડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C326-ZANDO326-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝંડો** — *jhanḍo* — "a flag".

> ચોકમાં એક મોટો ઝંડો હતો.
>
> *cokmā̃ ek moṭo jhanḍo hato.*
>
> There was a big flag in the square.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝંડો** (*jhanḍo*) comes from *\*dhvajadaṇḍa*, "a flagstaff", a form CDIAL reconstructs, which lists Gujarati *jhuṇḍo*, "a flagstaff, a flag" (CDIAL 6898). It is masculine: **મોટો ઝંડો**.
