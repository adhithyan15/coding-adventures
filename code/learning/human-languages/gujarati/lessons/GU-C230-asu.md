## You'll want to know: આંસુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-03]; assesses=[] -->

**આંસુ** — *ā̃su* — "tear, teardrop".

> **તેની આંખમાં આંસુ નથી.** — *tenī ā̃khmā̃ ā̃su nathī* — "There are no tears in her eyes."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આંસુ** (*ā̃su*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *aśru*, "tear", which picked up its nasal early, as the word for eye did (CDIAL 919).
