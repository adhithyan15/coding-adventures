## You'll want to know: माळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C263-TELI263-02]; assesses=[] -->

**माळी** — *māḷī* — "a gardener".

> माळी रूंख लगावै है।
>
> *māḷī rū̃kh lagāvai hai.*
>
> The gardener plants trees.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माळी** (*māḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mālin*, "garlanded; a gardener"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *mālī* under it (CDIAL 10094).
