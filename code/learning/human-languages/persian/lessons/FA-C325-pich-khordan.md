## You'll want to know: پیچ خوردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C325-ZAMINKHORDAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پیچ خوردن** (*pich khordan*) — "to get twisted, to sprain".

> پایم در کوه پیچ خورد.
>
> *Pâyam dar kuh pich khord.*
>
> I twisted my ankle on the mountain.

The body part is the subject: *dastam pich khord* "I sprained my wrist". Present *pich mikhorad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pich* "a twist, a turn" is a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; *khordan* "to eat, to strike" is inherited Persian.
