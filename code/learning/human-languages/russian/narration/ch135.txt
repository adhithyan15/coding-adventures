Russian, chapter 135: To lose, To win, To look, To draw, To travel.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
терять (teryát) — to lose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to find, then the Russian for to look for.

You'll want to know: терять.
терять (teryát) — "to lose".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: teryát]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: teryát, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say teryát]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for to find, then the Russian for to look for, then say teryát again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does терять (teryát) mean? ("To lose".) Say it once more.

(правильный.) Wrong? (неправильный.) Ready? (готовый.) Dangerous? (опасный.) Wet? (мокрый.) Dry? (сухой.) Soft? (мягкий.) Hard? (твёрдый.) Round? (круглый.) Wide? (широкий.) Narrow? (узкий.) Bitter? (горький.) Salty? (солёный.) Heavy? (тяжёлый.) Thin? (худой.) Fat? (толстый.)
