Punjabi, chapter 182: A Week on the Noticeboard.
10 lessons. You can do the first 7 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 10.
ਸੋਮਵਾਰ (somvaar) — Monday.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: somvaar; YOU SAY: ਸੋਮਵਾਰ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Start with the sound. It means Monday.

Grammar Lens: one weekday.
ਸੋਮਵਾਰ (somvaar) — somvār — Monday.
Keep it as one calendar label. A full week comes slowly, one day at a time.

The word, taken apart.
Somvār joins Sanskrit soma, "moon", with vāra, "day of the week" (CDIAL 11547): the moon day.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: somvaar; YOU SAY: ਸੋਮਵਾਰ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਸੋਮਵਾਰ (somvaar)]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say Monday. (ਸੋਮਵਾਰ (somvaar).)
Sources: Punjabi University, Punjabi-English Dictionary, for the weekday; Turner, CDIAL 11547, for vāra.

ਐ (ai).
aiṛā with dulāvā̃ — ai. Keep the independent vowel in view. It opens the Sunday word aitvaar that you already know by ear.
