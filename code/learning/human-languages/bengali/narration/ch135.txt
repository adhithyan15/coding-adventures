Bengali, chapter 135: Wet, Dry, Full, Fast, Slow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ভিজে (bhije) — wet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for hard, then the Bengali for soft.

You'll want to know: ভিজে.
ভিজে — bhije — "wet".

The word, taken apart.
ভিজে (bhije) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhiyajyate, "is smeared, gets wet", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9502). CDIAL lists Bengali bhijā, "to get wet"; bhije is its participle.
