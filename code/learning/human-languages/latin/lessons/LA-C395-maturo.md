## You'll want to know: mātūrō, mātūrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C395-DIFFERO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**mātūrō, mātūrāre** — "to hasten; to ripen".

> Iter mātūrāvit.
>
> *He hurried on his journey.*

*mātūrō, mātūrāre, mātūrāvī, mātūrātum*, built on *mātūrus*, ripe, early: to bring on early. English *mature*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mātūrō* is built on *mātūrus* "ripe, early, timely" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *mature* comes from the adjective.
