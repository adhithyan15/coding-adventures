# જિલ્લો (jillo) — a district (of a state)

## You'll want to know: જિલ્લો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C423-TOWN423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જિલ્લો** — *jillo* — "a district (of a state)".

> અમારા જિલ્લામાં ઘણાં ગામ છે.
>
> *amārā jillāmā̃ ghaṇā̃ gām che.*
>
> There are many villages in our district.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જિલ્લો** (*jillo*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḍilʿ*, "a side, a rib", which came through Persian as a word for a division of land.
