# ৈ (-oi) — the sign in front of the দ of দৈনিক

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C273-DOINIK-01, BN-SCRIPT-E-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say **দৈনিক**. Then write **দে** (*de*) and **নৌ** (*nou*).

## Script you'll notice: ৈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-C273-OI-KAR-01]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-E-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01, BN-LEX-C273-DOINIK-01] -->

**ৈ** — *-oi*, the vowel of **দৈনিক**. Like **ে**, it is written in front of its consonant, though it is said after it.

Set the print of **দৈ** beside **দে**. **দে** has **ে** in front: an upright curve with a small knob at its foot. **দৈ** has the same, and above it a hook stands on the headline, bent over to the left, its tip at the upper left: the same hook as the one on the stroke of **ৌ**.

## Writing: ৈ — copy the printed shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C273-OI-KAR-01] -->

Put your pen beside **দৈ** and copy the shape you can see, slowly and large: the **ে** with its hook above the headline, then the **দ**. Check the copy against **দে**: only the hook differs.

> This book does not yet tell you where to start ৈ or which way the pen goes. That is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying the printed shape needs no such source, and it is how the shape becomes yours in the meantime.
