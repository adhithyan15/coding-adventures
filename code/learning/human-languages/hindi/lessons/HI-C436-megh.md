## You'll want to know: मेघ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C436-BUND436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मेघ** — *megh* — "a cloud (literary)".

> पुराने गानों में मेघ बहुत आते हैं।
>
> *purāne gānoṁ meṁ megh bahut āte haiṁ.*
>
> Clouds come up a lot in old songs.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेघ** (*megh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *megha*, "a cloud", literally "the sprinkler" (Monier-Williams); poems and songs of the rains are full of it, while talk says बादल.
