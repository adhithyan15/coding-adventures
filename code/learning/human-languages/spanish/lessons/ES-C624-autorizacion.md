## You'll want to know: la autorización
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C624-PERMISO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la autorización** — "the authorisation, the permission".

> Necesitas una autorización de tus padres.
>
> *You need your parents' permission.*

More formal than *el permiso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*autorización* is built on *autorizar*, a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *auctorizare*, built on *auctor* 'guarantor' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
