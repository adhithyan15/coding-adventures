## You'll want to know: समूहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C308-SAMUHA308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**समूहः** — *samūhaḥ* — "a group; a crowd".

> एकः समूहः शिबिरे साहाय्यं करोति।
>
> *ekaḥ samūhaḥ śibire sāhāyyaṃ karoti.*
>
> A group helps out in the camp.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समूहः** (*samūhaḥ*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √ūh, "to move, push"; MW gives *samūha* as a collection, assemblage, multitude (MW).
