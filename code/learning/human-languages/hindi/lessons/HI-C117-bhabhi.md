## You'll want to know: भाभी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C117-AUNT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भाभी** (*bhābhī*) — "sister-in-law", specifically your brother's wife.

It shares its start with **भाई**, "brother": she is named through him.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A relative by marriage, named through your brother.
