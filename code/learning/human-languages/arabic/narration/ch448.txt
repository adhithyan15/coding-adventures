Arabic, chapter 448: A noisy night.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ضجيج (ḍajīj) — noise; a din.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to break down, then the Arabic for to check on.

You'll want to know: ضجيج.
ضجيج — ḍajīj — "noise; a din".
سمعنا ضجيجا من الشارع في الليل. samiʿnā ḍajījan min ash-shāriʿ fī al-layl. We heard noise from the street at night.

The word, taken apart.
ضجيج (ḍajīj) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-j-j, "to cry out, to clamour" (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
أزعج (azʿaja) — to disturb; to bother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to check on, then the Arabic for noise.

You'll want to know: أزعج.
أزعج — azʿaja — "to disturb; to bother".
أزعجنا ضجيج الشارع. azʿajanā ḍajīj ash-shāriʿ. The noise of the street disturbed us.
Present: يزعج, yuzʿij.

The word, taken apart.
أزعج (azʿaja) is built on the native Arabic root z-ʿ-j, "to unsettle, to disquiet" (Lane). It is Form IV; muzʿij, "annoying", is its participle.

Lesson 3 of 5.
أيقظ (ayqaẓa) — to wake (someone) up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for noise, then the Arabic for to disturb.

You'll want to know: أيقظ.
أيقظ — ayqaẓa — "to wake (someone) up".
أيقظتني أمي مبكرا. ayqaẓatnī ummī mubakkiran. My mother woke me up early.
Present: يوقظ, yūqiẓ.
istayqaẓa is to wake up oneself; ayqaẓa wakes someone else.

The word, taken apart.
أيقظ (ayqaẓa) is built on the native Arabic root y-q-ẓ, "to be awake" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to make awake".

هدأ (hadaʾa) is built on the native Arabic root h-d-ʾ, "to be still, quiet" (Lane). It is the plain verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

Present: يشتكي, yashtakī.

The word, taken apart.
اشتكى (ishtakā) is built on the native Arabic root sh-k-w, "to complain" (Lane). It is Form VIII.
