Marathi, chapter 238: Pilot, Researcher, Foreigner, Tourist, Adult.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वैमानिक (vaimānik) — a pilot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a website, then the Marathi for a keyboard.

You'll want to know: वैमानिक.
वैमानिक — vaimānik — "a pilot".

The word, taken apart.
वैमानिक (vaimānik) is Sanskrit vaimānika, "one who rides a celestial car", a tatsama built on vimāna, "car of the gods" (CDIAL 11823), now "aeroplane".

प्रौढ (prauḍh) is Sanskrit prauḍha, "full of; strong, full-grown", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 9021). CDIAL thinks the Sanskrit form was shaped in part by a non-Aryan word.
