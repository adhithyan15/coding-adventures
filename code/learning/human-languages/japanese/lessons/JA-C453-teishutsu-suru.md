## You'll want to know: ていしゅつする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C453-PAPERS453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ていしゅつする** — *teishutsu suru* — "to hand in; to submit".

> **しめきりの まえに ていしゅつして ください。** — *shimekiri no mae ni teishutsu shite kudasai* — "Please hand it in before the cut-off date."

Polite **ていしゅつします**, *teishutsushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが しゅくだいを ていしゅつしました。**, *gakusei ga shukudai o teishutsu shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ていしゅつする** (*teishutsu suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *teishutsu* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tei*, "carry in hand" (Middle Chinese *dej*) and *shutsu*, "go out" (*tsyhwit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
