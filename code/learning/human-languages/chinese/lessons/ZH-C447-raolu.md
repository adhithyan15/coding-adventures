## You'll want to know: ràolù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C447-WALK447-04]; assesses=[] -->

*ràolù* — "to take a detour; to take the long way round".

> *qiánbian zài xiū lù wǒmen děi ràolù* — There are roadworks ahead, so we'll have to go the long way round.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ràolù* is built from *rào* 'to wind round' and 路 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyewX* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to wind round'; today's falling tone is irregular beside it.
