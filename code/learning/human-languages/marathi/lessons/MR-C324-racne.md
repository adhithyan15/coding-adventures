## You'll want to know: रचणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C324-SHRADDHA324-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रचणे** — *racṇe* — "to compose; to arrange".

> त्यांच्या मित्राने त्यांच्यावर एक कविता रचली.
>
> *tyāñcyā mitrāne tyāñcyāvar ek kavitā raclī.*
>
> His friend composed a poem about him.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने रचलं, *tyāne raclaṁ*.

Transitive: कविता रचणे, to compose a poem; दगड रचणे, to stack stones.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रचणे** (*racṇe*) is built on Sanskrit *racayati*, 'produces, arranges, composes' (Monier-Williams).
