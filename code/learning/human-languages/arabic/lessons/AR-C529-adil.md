## You'll want to know: عديل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C529-AHL529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عديل** — *ʿadīl* — "a brother-in-law (by sisters)".

> عديلي متزوج من أخت زوجتي.
>
> *ʿadīlī mutazawwij min ukht zawjatī.*
>
> My brother-in-law is married to my wife's sister.

Two men married to two sisters are عديلان.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عديل** (*ʿadīl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-l*, "to be equal, to be fair" (Lane). Lane gives ʿadīl as an equal, a counterpart; the sense of two men with sisters for wives is usually explained from it.
