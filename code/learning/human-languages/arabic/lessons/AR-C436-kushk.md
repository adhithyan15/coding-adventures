## You'll want to know: كشك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C436-KUSHK436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**كشك** — *kushk* — "a kiosk".

> اشتريت جريدة من الكشك.
>
> *ishtaraytu jarīda min al-kushk.*
>
> I bought a newspaper at the kiosk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كشك** (*kushk*) is usually traced to Persian *kushk*, "a pavilion"; English *kiosk* comes from the same word.
