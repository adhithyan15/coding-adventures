Gujarati, chapter 176: Hours and quarters.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ઘડિયાળ (ghaḍiyāḷ) — clock, watch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for half, then the Gujarati for constantly.

You'll want to know: ઘડિયાળ.
ઘડિયાળ — ghaḍiyāḷ — "clock, watch".
હું નવી ઘડિયાળ ખરીદીશ. — hũ navī ghaḍiyāḷ kharīdīś — "I will buy a new watch."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઘડિયાળ (ghaḍiyāḷ) is a tadbhava. It comes from ghaṭītāḍa, "striking the time", a reconstructed compound of ghaṭī, "water-clock", and tāḍa, "striking", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "clock" (CDIAL 4413).

સાડા — sāḍā — "and a half".
હું સાડા ત્રણ કલાક ભણીશ. — hũ sāḍā traṇ kalāk bhaṇīś — "I will study for three and a half hours."
Before a number: સાડા (sāḍā) ત્રણ, three and a half.

The word, taken apart.
સાડા (sāḍā) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit sārdha, "plus a half", built from sa-, "with", and ardha, "half"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 13369).

Lesson 3 of 6.
સવા (savā) — and a quarter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for clock, then the Gujarati for and a half.

You'll want to know: સવા.
સવા — savā — "and a quarter".
કામ સવા કલાક ચાલશે. — kām savā kalāk cālśe — "The work will take an hour and a quarter."

The word, taken apart.
સવા (savā) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit sapāda, "increased by a fourth", built from sa-, "with", and pāda, "foot, quarter"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "plus a quarter" (CDIAL 13134).

Lesson 4 of 6.
પોણું (poṇũ) — three quarters.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for and a half, then the Gujarati for and a quarter.

You'll want to know: પોણું.
પોણું — poṇũ — "three quarters".
હું પોણો કલાક ચાલીશ. — hũ poṇo kalāk cālīś — "I will walk for three quarters of an hour."
Before a number it means a quarter less: પોણા બે, one and three quarters.

The word, taken apart.
પોણું (poṇũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from padūna, "less by a quarter", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit pādona; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "three quarters" (CDIAL 7765).
