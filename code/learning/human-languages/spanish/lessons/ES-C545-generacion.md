## You'll want to know: la generación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C545-NINEZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la generación** — "the generation".

> Tres generaciones viven en esa casa.
>
> *Three generations live in that house.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*generación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *generātiō* 'a begetting' (EtymDB), from *generāre*, built on *genus* 'birth, kind'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
