# જકડવું (jakaḍvũ) — to hold fast

## You'll want to know: જકડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C540-BALJABRI540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જકડવું** — *jakaḍvũ* — "to hold fast".

> જો તમે બાળકનો હાથ જકડી રાખશો તો તે નહીં ખોવાય.
>
> *jo tame bāḷakno hāth jakḍī rākhśo to te nahī̃ khovāy.*
>
> If you hold the child's hand tight, he won't get lost.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હાથ જકડ્યો, *teṇe hāth jakaḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જકડવું** (*jakaḍvũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *yatá*, "held, restrained", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tighten, bind" (CDIAL 10400).
