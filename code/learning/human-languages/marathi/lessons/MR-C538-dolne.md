## You'll want to know: डोलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C538-NRUTYA538-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डोलणे** — *ḍolṇe* — "to sway".

> संगीत ऐकून आजोबा खुर्चीत डोलू लागले.
>
> *saṅgīt aikūn ājobā khurcīt ḍolū lāgle.*
>
> Listening to the music, Grandfather began to sway in his chair.

Past (he …): तो डोलला, *to ḍollā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डोलणे** (*ḍolṇe*) is perhaps kin to Sanskrit *dōla*, "swinging", under which CDIAL lists Hindi *ḍol*, "swinging" (CDIAL 6582); CDIAL gives no Marathi verb there, so the link is not settled.
