## You'll want to know: perlegō, perlegere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C550-LIBR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**perlegō, perlegere** — "to read through".

> Epistulam tuam ter perlēgī.
>
> *I have read your letter through three times.*

*perlegō, perlegere, perlēgī, perlēctum*: *per* 'through' + *legō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**perlegō** is built on *per-* "through" and *legō* "to read" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
