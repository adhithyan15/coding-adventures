## You'll want to know: തുല്യമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C455-BAHUMANAM455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**തുല്യമായ** — *tulyamāya* — "equal".

> എല്ലാവർക്കും തുല്യമായ അവസരം വേണം.
>
> *ellāvarkkuṁ tulyamāya avasaraṁ vēṇaṁ.*
>
> Everyone needs an equal chance.

The adverb is തുല്യമായി, equally.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തുല്യമായ** (*tulyamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *tulya*, "equal to, like" (CDIAL 5888), from the root *tul*, "to weigh", with the adjective ending -ആയ: of equal weight.
