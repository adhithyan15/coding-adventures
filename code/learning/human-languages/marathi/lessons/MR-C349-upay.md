## You'll want to know: उपाय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C349-UPAY349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उपाय** — *upāy* — "a remedy; a solution".

> या प्रश्नावर एकच उपाय आहे.
>
> *yā praśnāvar ekac upāy āhe.*
>
> There is only one remedy for this problem.

Masculine.

X वर उपाय, a remedy for X; उपाय करणे, to take measures.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपाय** (*upāy*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *upāya*, "approach, means, stratagem" (CDIAL 2308).
