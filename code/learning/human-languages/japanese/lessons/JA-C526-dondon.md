## You'll want to know: どんどん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C526-PROGRESS526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**どんどん** — *dondon* — "rapidly; steadily (and fast)".

> **もし あめが ふったら、かわの みずが どんどん ふえるでしょう。** — *moshi ame ga futtara, kawa no mizu ga dondon fueru deshō* — "If it rains, the river will rise fast."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どんどん** (*dondon*) is a native mimetic word, imitating a sound rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict's first sense is the noise of drumming, and its later senses are "rapidly" and "steadily"; no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).
