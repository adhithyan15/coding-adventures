## You'll want to know: રોપવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C240-GARDEN240-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રોપવું** — *ropvũ* — "to plant, to set out".

> **તેણે બગીચામાં છોડ રોપ્યો.** — *teṇe bagīcāmā̃ choḍ ropyo* — "She planted a plant in the garden."

**વાવવું** is to sow seed; **રોપવું** is to put a young plant in the ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રોપવું** (*ropvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to plant", under *ropyate*, "is planted", the passive of Sanskrit *ropayati*, "plants", in its entry for *\*rupati*, "pierces, makes a hole, plants" (CDIAL 10783).
