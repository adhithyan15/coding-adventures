Marathi, chapter 451: Cheerful and Gloomy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रसन्न (prasanna) — cheerful; bright and clear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for surprise, then the Marathi for to be deeply moved.

You'll want to know: प्रसन्न.
प्रसन्न — prasanna — "cheerful; bright and clear".
सकाळी बागेत फिरल्यावर मन प्रसन्न होतं. sakāḷī bāget phirlyāvar man prasanna hotaṁ. After a morning walk in the garden, the mind feels bright.
Unchanging for gender: प्रसन्न (prasanna) चेहरा, a cheerful face; प्रसन्न (prasanna) सकाळ, a bright morning.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसन्न (prasanna) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prasanna, "pleased; clear, bright" (CDIAL 8823).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उदास (udās) — gloomy; low, downcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be deeply moved, then the Marathi for cheerful.

You'll want to know: उदास.
उदास — udās — "gloomy; low, downcast".
पावसाळ्यात अरुण कधीकधी उदास असतो. pāvsāḷyāt aruṇ kadhīkadhī udās asto. In the monsoon Arun is sometimes low.
Unchanging: उदास (udās) मुलगा, उदास (udās) मुलगी. उदास (udās) वाटणे, to feel low.

The word, taken apart.
उदास (udās) is usually traced to Sanskrit udāsa, a noun, "indifference, apathy" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it is an adjective of low spirits.

Lesson 3 of 5.
खुश (khuś) — pleased; glad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for cheerful, then the Marathi for gloomy.

You'll want to know: खुश.
खुश — khuś — "pleased; glad".
बक्षीस मिळाल्यावर मुली खुश झाल्या. bakṣīs miḷālyāvar mulī khuś jhālyā. The girls were pleased when they got the prize.
Unchanging; खुश (khuś) होणे, to become pleased. A Persian word beside the Sanskrit आनंदी.

The word, taken apart.
खुश (khuś) is a loan from Persian khush, "happy, pleasant"; Marathi writes the Persian kh with ख.

Lesson 4 of 5.
नाराज (nārāj) — displeased; upset.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for gloomy, then the Marathi for pleased.

You'll want to know: नाराज.
नाराज — nārāj — "displeased; upset".
मी उशिरा आलो म्हणून वडील नाराज झाले. mī uśirā ālo mhaṇūn vaḍīl nārāj jhāle. Father was displeased because I came late.
Unchanging. The person one is upset with takes -वर: ती माझ्यावर नाराज (nārāj) आहे.

The word, taken apart.
नाराज (nārāj) is a loan from Persian nārāz, "displeased", built with the Persian negative nā-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
वैतागणे (vaitāgṇe) — to get fed up; to be exasperated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for pleased, then the Marathi for displeased.

You'll want to know: वैतागणे.
वैतागणे — vaitāgṇe — "to get fed up; to be exasperated".
दिवसभर पाऊस पडत होता, म्हणून मुलं वैतागली. divasbhar pāūs paḍat hotā, mhaṇūn mulaṁ vaitāglī. It rained all day, so the children got fed up.
Past (he …): तो वैतागला, to vaitāglā.
What one is fed up with takes -ला: कामाला वैतागणे (vaitāgṇe). The noun वैताग, exasperation.

The word, taken apart.
वैतागणे (vaitāgṇe) is a Marathi verb built on वैताग, "exasperation"; the origin of वैताग is not settled.
