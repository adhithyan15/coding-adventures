## You'll want to know: ઝંઝટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C531-JHANJHAT531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝંઝટ** — *jhanjhaṭ* — "a hassle".

> જો ટ્રેન મોડી હશે તો ઘણી ઝંઝટ થશે.
>
> *jo ṭren moḍī haśe to ghaṇī jhanjhaṭ thaśe.*
>
> If the train is late, there will be a lot of hassle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝંઝટ** (*jhanjhaṭ*) sounds like what it means, a tangle of small troubles. CDIAL lists Old Gujarati *jhaṁjha*, "a quarrel", and Gujarati *jhā̃jh*, "rage", under Sanskrit *jhañjhā*, "the noise of rain" (CDIAL 5326); it does not list this word, so its history is not settled.
