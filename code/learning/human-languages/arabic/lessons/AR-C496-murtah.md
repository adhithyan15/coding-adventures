# مرتاح (murtāḥ) — at ease, relaxed

## You'll want to know: مرتاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C496-HAMAS496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مرتاح** — *murtāḥ* — "at ease, relaxed".

> أنا مرتاح في عملي الجديد.
>
> *anā murtāḥ fī ʿamalī al-jadīd.*
>
> I am at ease in my new job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرتاح** (*murtāḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-w-ḥ*, "wind, breath, rest" (Lane). Murtāḥ is the participle of Form VIII.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
