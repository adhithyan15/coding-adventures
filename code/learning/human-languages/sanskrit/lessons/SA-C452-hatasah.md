## You'll want to know: हताशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C452-UDVEGA452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हताशः** — *hatāśaḥ* — "despairing; without hope".

> वर्षा न भवति, अतः कृषकः हताशः अस्ति।
>
> *varṣā na bhavati, ataḥ kṛṣakaḥ hatāśaḥ asti.*
>
> There is no rain, so the farmer has lost hope.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हताशः** (*hatāśaḥ*) is built from *hata*, "struck, destroyed", from the root √han, and **आशा**, "hope": one whose hopes are struck down; MW gives "whose hopes are destroyed, desperate" (MW).
