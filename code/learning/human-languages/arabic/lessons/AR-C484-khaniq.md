## You'll want to know: خانق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C484-HARR484-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خانق** — *khāniq* — "stifling; suffocating".

> كان الحر خانقا في الحافلة.
>
> *kāna al-ḥarr khāniqan fī al-ḥāfila.*
>
> The heat was stifling on the bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خانق** (*khāniq*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-n-q*, "to strangle" (Lane). It is the active participle, "choking".
