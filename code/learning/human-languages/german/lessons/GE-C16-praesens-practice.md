# Practice — the whole present of sein

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-SEIN-01, GE-SOUND-SEIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. Every form below has had its own lesson, so this is
the first time it is safe to put them all on one page — as a recap, not as
something to memorise from cold.

## Grammar Lens: the six forms, assembled
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ICH-BIN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-DU-BIST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ER-IST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-WIR-SIND-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-IHR-SEID-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-SIE-SIND-01] -->

| singular | plural |
|---|---|
| ich **bin** | wir **sind** |
| du **bist** | ihr **seid** |
| er/sie/es **ist** | sie/Sie **sind** |

Six subjects, five different forms — *sind* does double duty. Look at that with
fresh eyes now that you can say all of it: *bin* and *ist* do not look like
forms of the same word, and the next chapter explains why they aren't.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-MUEDE-01, GE-LEX-GUT-02, GE-LEX-DANKE-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-DU-BIST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ICH-BIN-01] -->

| German | English |
|---|---|
| *Hallo! Wie geht es dir?* | Hello! How are you? |
| *Danke, ich bin müde.* | Thanks — I am tired. |
| *Du bist müde? Ich auch.* | You are tired? Me too. |
| *Dann sind wir müde. Aber das ist gut.* | Then we are tired. But that is good. |
| *Bis morgen!* | See you tomorrow! |

Three small joining words slip in there without needing lessons of their own,
because each can be read straight off the translation: **auch** ("too"),
**dann** ("then") and **aber** ("but").

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ICH-BIN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-DU-BIST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ER-IST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-WIR-SIND-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-IHR-SEID-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-SIE-SIND-01, GE-LEX-MUEDE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the six in order — "ich bin, du bist, er ist, wir sind, ihr seid, sie sind"]
- [YOU SAY: the same six backwards]
- [YOU SAY: "Ich bin müde." "Du bist müde." "Wir sind müde."]

[REPEAT x2] "ich bin, du bist, er ist, wir sind, ihr seid, sie sind."

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ICH-BIN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ER-IST-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-IHR-SEID-01, GE-LEX-MUEDE-01, GE-LEX-GUT-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Run the six forms without looking. Which one form covers *wir*,
*sie* and *Sie*? (***Sind***.) Which is the only form built out of another one?
(***Bist***, from *bin*.) Say "that is good." (**Das ist gut**.) Say "you are
tired" to a group. (**Ihr seid müde**.) Next: where these forms came from —
three different verbs, none of them each other.
