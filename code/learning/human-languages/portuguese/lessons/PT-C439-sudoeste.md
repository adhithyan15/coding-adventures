## You'll want to know: o sudoeste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C439-CARDEAIS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o sudoeste** — "the south-west".

> A praia fica a sudoeste da cidade.
>
> *The beach is south-west of the town.*

*A costa sudoeste* — the south-west coast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sudoeste* came from French *sud-ouest* 'south-west' (EtymDB), built on Germanic words for 'south' and 'west'.
