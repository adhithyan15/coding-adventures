## You'll want to know: excūsātiō, excūsātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C369-HORTOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**excūsātiō, excūsātiōnis** — "an excuse, a plea".

> Haec excūsātiō nōn accipitur.
>
> *This excuse is not accepted.*

*excūsātiō, excūsātiōnis* f., the noun of *excūsō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*excūsātiō* is the noun built on *excūsō* "to excuse" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *ex* and *causa*.
