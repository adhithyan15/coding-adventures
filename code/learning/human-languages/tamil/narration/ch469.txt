Tamil, chapter 469: Enemies and Grudges.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பகை (pakai) — enmity; a feud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to weaken, then the Tamil for to nod off.

You'll want to know: பகை.
பகை — pakai — "enmity; a feud".
இரண்டு குடும்பங்களுக்கும் இடையில் பல வருடமாகப் பகை. iraṇṭu kuṭumpaṅkaḷukkum iṭaiyil pala varuṭamākap pakai. There has been a feud between the two families for many years.
பகைவன், an enemy, in written Tamil.

The word, taken apart.
பகை (pakai) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3808), in the family of பகு paku, "to be split, to be at variance".

Lesson 3 of 5.
வஞ்சம் (vañcam) — a grudge; spite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for enmity, then the Tamil for hostility.

You'll want to know: வஞ்சம்.
வஞ்சம் — vañcam — "a grudge; spite".
அவன் மனதில் பழைய வஞ்சம் இருந்தது. avaṉ maṉatil paḻaiya vañcam iruntatu. He bore an old grudge.
வஞ்சம் (vañcam) தீர், to settle a score.

The word, taken apart.
வஞ்சம் (vañcam) is usually traced to Sanskrit vañcana, "cheating, deception" (Monier-Williams); in Tamil it is a deceit-filled grudge.

Lesson 4 of 5.
எதிரி (etiri) — an enemy; an opponent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for hostility, then the Tamil for a grudge.

You'll want to know: எதிரி.
எதிரி — etiri — "an enemy; an opponent".
அவன் என் நண்பன், எதிரி இல்லை. avaṉ eṉ naṇpaṉ, etiri illai. He is my friend, not my enemy.
Plural: எதிரிகள், etirikaḷ.
From எதிர் "opposite": எதிரே, opposite, shares it.

The word, taken apart.
எதிரி (etiri) is native Dravidian, built on எதிர் etir, "that which is opposite, in front" (DEDR 795).

Lesson 5 of 5.
பழிவாங்கு (paḻivāṅku) — to take revenge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a grudge, then the Tamil for an enemy.

You'll want to know: பழிவாங்கு.
பழிவாங்கு — paḻivāṅku — "to take revenge".
அந்த அவமானத்துக்காக அவன் பழிவாங்க நினைத்தான். anta avamāṉattukkāka avaṉ paḻivāṅka niṉaittāṉ. He thought of taking revenge for that humiliation.
Past (I …): பழிவாங்கினேன், paḻivāṅkiṉēṉ.
பழி "blame, vengeance" + வாங்கு "to take".

The word, taken apart.
பழிவாங்கு (paḻivāṅku) is a native compound of பழி paḻi, "blame, reproach" (DEDR 4002), and வாங்கு, "to take".
