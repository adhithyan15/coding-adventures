Bengali, chapter 343: Whose Fault Was It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দোষ (dosh) — a fault; blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a formal complaint, then the Bengali for to settle a dispute.

You'll want to know: দোষ.
দোষ — dosh — "a fault; blame".
এটা তোমার দোষ নয়। eṭā tomār dosh nôy. It's not your fault.

The word, taken apart.
দোষ (dosh) is Sanskrit doṣa, a fault or defect (Monier-Williams). It belongs to the one at fault: তোমার দোষ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
দায়ী (dāyī) — responsible for a fault; to blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to settle a dispute, then the Bengali for blame.

You'll want to know: দায়ী.
দায়ী — dāyī — "responsible for a fault; to blame".
দুর্ঘটনার জন্য কে দায়ী? durghôṭônār jônyô ke dāyī? Who is to blame for the accident?

The word, taken apart.
দায়ী (dāyī) is Sanskrit dāyin, which Monier-Williams gives as giving and also as owing or having to pay. Bengali uses it for the one who owes an answer, the one to blame; that narrower sense is not one the dictionary gives. দায়িত্ব, a responsibility, is its abstract.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ক্ষতিপূরণ.
ক্ষতিপূরণ — khôtipūrôn — "compensation".
কোম্পানি ক্ষতিপূরণ দিতে রাজি হল। kompāni khôtipūrôn dite rāji hôlô. The company agreed to pay compensation.

The word, taken apart.
ক্ষতিপূরণ (khôtipūrôn) is Sanskrit kṣati, loss, with pūraṇa, filling up (Monier-Williams): filling the hole a loss has made.

Lesson 4 of 5.
জবাবদিহি করা (jôbābdihi kôrā) — to answer for; to account for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for responsible for a fault, then the Bengali for compensation.

You'll want to know: জবাবদিহি করা.
জবাবদিহি করা — jôbābdihi kôrā — "to answer for; to account for".
মন্ত্রীকে এই খরচের জন্য জবাবদিহি করতে হবে। môntrīke ei khôrôcher jônyô jôbābdihi kôrte hôbe. The minister will have to answer for this spending.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সভায় জবাবদিহি করলেন, tini sôbhāy jôbābdihi kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
জবাবদিহি করা (jôbābdihi kôrā) is usually traced to Persian javāb-dihī, the giving of an answer, built on Arabic jawāb, an answer; with করা it means to explain yourself to those who can hold you to account.

Lesson 5 of 5.
রেহাই দেওয়া (rehāi deoyā) — to let off; to spare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for compensation, then the Bengali for to answer for.

You'll want to know: রেহাই দেওয়া.
রেহাই দেওয়া — rehāi deoyā — "to let off; to spare".
বিচারক ছেলেটাকে রেহাই দিলেন। bichārôk chheleṭāke rehāi dilen. The judge let the boy off.
Past (he/she …): তিনি চোরকে রেহাই দিলেন, tini chorke rehāi dilen.

The word, taken apart.
রেহাই দেওয়া (rehāi deoyā) is usually traced to Persian rihāʾī, release, with দেওয়া: to give someone their release from a punishment or a duty.
