## You'll want to know: ਗੱਜਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C286-STORM286-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੱਜਣਾ** — *gajjṇā* — "to thunder; to roar".

> ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬੱਦਲ ਗੱਜਣਗੇ।
>
> *rāt nū̃ baddal gajjaṇge.*
>
> The clouds will thunder at night.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬੱਦਲ ਗੱਜਿਆ, *oh baddal gajjiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੱਜਣਾ** (*gajjṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *garjati*, "thunders, roars"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gajjṇā* under it (CDIAL 4046).
