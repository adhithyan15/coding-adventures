Bengali, chapter 446: Comfort and Relief.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সান্ত্বনা (sāntvônā) — consolation; comfort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sob, then the Bengali for to lament.

You'll want to know: সান্ত্বনা.
সান্ত্বনা — sāntvônā — "consolation; comfort".
বন্ধুরা এসে মীরাকে সান্ত্বনা দিল। bôndhurā ese mīrāke sāntvônā dilô. Her friends came and consoled Mira.
সান্ত্বনা (sāntvônā) দেওয়া, to console.

The word, taken apart.
সান্ত্বনা (sāntvônā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sāntvanā, "appeasing, soothing with kind words" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
হাঁফ ছাড়া (hā̃ph chhāṛā) — to breathe a sigh of relief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for ease of mind, then the Bengali for reliance.

You'll want to know: হাঁফ ছাড়া.
হাঁফ ছাড়া — hā̃ph chhāṛā — "to breathe a sigh of relief".
কাজটা শেষ করে আমি হাঁফ ছাড়লাম। kājṭā shesh kôre āmi hā̃ph chhāṛlām. I finished the job and breathed a sigh of relief.
Past (he/she …): তিনি হাঁফ ছাড়লেন, tini hā̃ph chhāṛlen.

The word, taken apart.
হাঁফ ছাড়া (hā̃ph chhāṛā) is hā̃ph, "a gasp", from the reconstructed root for panting behind hā̃pāno, to pant (CDIAL 13973), with ছাড়া (taught), to let go (CDIAL 4998): to let out the held breath.
