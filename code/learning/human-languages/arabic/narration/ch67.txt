Arabic, chapter 67: Tomatoes, Carrots, Butter, Honey, Grapes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طماطم (ṭamāṭim) — tomatoes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for bananas, then the Arabic for potatoes.

You'll want to know: طماطم.
طماطم — ṭamāṭim — "tomatoes".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does طماطم (ṭamāṭim) mean? ("Tomatoes".) Say it once more.
