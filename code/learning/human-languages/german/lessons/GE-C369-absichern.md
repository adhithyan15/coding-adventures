## You'll want to know: absichern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C369-RISIKO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**absichern** — "to protect, to safeguard (mostly reflexive, sich absichern)".

> Bevor ich unterschreibe, will ich mich gut absichern.
>
> *Before I sign, I want to protect myself properly.*

Separable: *er sichert sich ab*, *er hat sich abgesichert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*absichern* is built on *sichern* "to make safe", from *sicher* "safe", inherited from Old High German *sihhur*, which goes back to Latin *sēcūrus* "free from care" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
