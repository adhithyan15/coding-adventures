## You'll want to know: まもなく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C310-HENJI310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まもなく** — *mamonaku* — "shortly; in a moment".

> **まもなく でんしゃが きます。** — *mamonaku densha ga kimasu* — "The train will be here shortly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まもなく** (*mamonaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ma*, "an interval", *mo*, "even", and *naku*, "without" (from *nai*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
