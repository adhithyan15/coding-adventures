## You'll want to know: शिल्पम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C519-MURTI519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्पम्** — *śilpam* — "a craft".

> शिल्पं कठिनं परन्तु सुन्दरम् अस्ति।
>
> *śilpaṃ kaṭhinaṃ parantu sundaram asti.*
>
> A craft is hard but beautiful.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्पम्** (*śilpam*) is the word MW gives for decoration, ornament, artistic work, and MW itself calls it of doubtful derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
