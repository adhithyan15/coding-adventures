## You'll want to know: ಮಲತಾಯಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C527-POSHANE527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಲತಾಯಿ** — *malatāyi* — "a stepmother".

> ಆ ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮಲತಾಯಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯವಳು.
>
> *ā katheyalli malatāyi tumbā oḷḷeyavaḷu.*
>
> In that story the stepmother is very kind.

ಮಲ- marks a step-relation: ಮಲತಮ್ಮ, a younger half-brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಲತಾಯಿ** (*malatāyi*) is a Kannada compound: *mala-*, "step-", whose origin is not settled, and *tāyi*, "mother" (DEDR 364).
