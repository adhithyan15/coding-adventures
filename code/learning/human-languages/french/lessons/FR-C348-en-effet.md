## You'll want to know: en effet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C348-GRACE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**en effet** — "indeed; and that is because".

> Il est fatigué ; en effet, il a travaillé toute la nuit.
>
> *He is tired; indeed, he worked all night.*

It confirms or explains what came before; it does not mean *in effect*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*effet* comes through Old French *effet* from Latin *effectus* "an effect, a result" (EtymDB), from *efficere* "to bring about", built on *facere* "to do, to make" (Lewis & Short).
