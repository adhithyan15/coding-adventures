Lesson 1 of 5.
die Brücke — a bridge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an aeroplane, then the German for a car.

You'll want to know: die Brücke.
die Brücke — "a bridge".

The word, taken apart.
Brücke from Old High German brugga from Proto-Germanic brugjōn — inherited, the same word as English bridge. (EtymDB 2.1).
