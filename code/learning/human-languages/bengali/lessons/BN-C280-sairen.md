## You'll want to know: সাইরেন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C280-UDDHAR280-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সাইরেন** — *sāiren* — "a siren".

> সাইরেন শুনে সবাই রাস্তার পাশে দাঁড়াল।
>
> *sāiren shune sôbāi rāstār pāshe dā̃ṛālô.*
>
> When they heard the siren, everyone stood at the side of the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাইরেন** (*sāiren*) is borrowed from English *siren*, the warning wail of a fire engine or an ambulance; English took the name from the Sirens of Greek legend.
