Arabic, chapter 58: To stand up, To walk, To run, To play, To buy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قام (qāma) — to stand up (he stood up).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to sit, then the Arabic for to sleep.

You'll want to know: قام.
قام — qāma — "to stand up (he stood up)".

What you've built.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does قام (qāma) mean? ("To stand up (he stood up)".) Say it once more.
