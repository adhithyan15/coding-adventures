## You'll want to know: o portátil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C397-INSTALAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o portátil** — "the laptop; portable".

> Levo o portátil para o trabalho todos os dias.
>
> *I take my laptop to work every day.*

European Portuguese; in Brazil *o notebook*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*portátil* comes from Medieval Latin *portātilis* (EtymDB), built on *portāre* "to carry" (Lewis & Short).
