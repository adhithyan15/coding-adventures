# ل — alef, with a bowl at its foot

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-ALEF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write ا. One stroke, top to bottom.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-LAM-01]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-ALEF-01] -->

> ل

Start exactly as you started alef — a tall stroke coming down — and then, instead
of lifting, **swing left into a shallow bowl** at the foot and come back up a
little.

One movement, no pen lift. If you can write ا you are most of the way to ل
already; the difference is what happens at the bottom.

Its name is **lâm** and it says **l**, as in English *long*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-LAM-01] -->

- [YOU WRITE: ا, then ل — the same descent, and then the bowl]
- [YOU SAY: **l**]
