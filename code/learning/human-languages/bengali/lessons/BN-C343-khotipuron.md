## You'll want to know: ক্ষতিপূরণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C343-DOSH343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষতিপূরণ** — *khôtipūrôn* — "compensation".

> কোম্পানি ক্ষতিপূরণ দিতে রাজি হল।
>
> *kompāni khôtipūrôn dite rāji hôlô.*
>
> The company agreed to pay compensation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষতিপূরণ** (*khôtipūrôn*) is Sanskrit *kṣati*, loss, with *pūraṇa*, filling up (Monier-Williams): filling the hole a loss has made.
