# ಅಂತೂ (antū) — at last; anyhow

## You'll want to know: ಅಂತೂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C369-ANTHU369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂತೂ** — *antū* — "at last; anyhow".

> ಅಂತೂ ಮೂರು ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಅಣ್ಣ ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದನು.
>
> *antū mūru varṣada nantara aṇṇa manege bandanu.*
>
> At last, after three years, my elder brother came home.

Often with ಇಂತೂ: ಅಂತೂ ಇಂತೂ, somehow or other, at long last.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂತೂ** (*antū*) is native Dravidian: it is built on the pointing base *a-*, "that, yonder" (DEDR 1), with the ending -*ū*, "even".

One more word for telling what happened.
