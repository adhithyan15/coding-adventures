## You'll want to know: zuìjìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C178-DAY178-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zuìjìn* — "lately; recently".

> *nǐ zuìjìn máng ma* — Have you been busy lately?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuìjìn* is built from *zuì* 'most' and *jìn* 'near', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tswajH* and *gj+nX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the nearest time.
