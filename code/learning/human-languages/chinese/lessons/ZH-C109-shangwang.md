## You'll want to know: 上网
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C109-SCREEN109-01]; assesses=[] -->

**上网** — *shàngwǎng* — "to go online".

> *nǐ měitiān shàngwǎng ma* — Do you go online every day?

The new character, in pieces: 网 — a frame open at the foot, like the outside of 两, with two small crosses inside. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上网** (*shàngwǎng*) is built from 上 'to go up, to get on' and wǎng 'net', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *mjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary describes the net as knotted cord for hunting and fishing. Getting on to the net is a modern sense.
