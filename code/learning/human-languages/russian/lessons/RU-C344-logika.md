## You'll want to know: логика
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C344-LOGIKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**логика** (*lógika*) — "logic".

> В его словах нет логики.
>
> *V yegó slovákh net lógiki.*
>
> There is no logic in what he says.

Feminine. *По логике* — logically speaking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**логика** is usually traced, through western languages, to Greek *logikḗ* "the art of reasoning", built on *lógos* "word, reason".
