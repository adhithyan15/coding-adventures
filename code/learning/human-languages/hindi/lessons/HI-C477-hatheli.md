## You'll want to know: हथेली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C477-HATHELI477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हथेली** — *hathelī* — "the palm of the hand".

> बच्चे की हथेली बहुत छोटी और नरम है।
>
> *bacce kī hathelī bahut choṭī aur naram hai.*
>
> The baby's palm is very small and soft.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हथेली** (*hathelī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *hathelī*, "palm", under Sanskrit *hastatala*, "the flat of the hand" (CDIAL 14029), built on *hasta*, the word that gave *hāth*.
