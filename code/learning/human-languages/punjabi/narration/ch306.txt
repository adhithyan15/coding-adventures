Punjabi, chapter 306: Grain and Straw.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਛੱਜ (chhajj) — a winnowing basket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to thresh, then the Punjabi for a heap.

You'll want to know: ਛੱਜ.
ਛੱਜ — chhajj — "a winnowing basket".
ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਛੱਜ ਨਾਲ ਦਾਣੇ ਸਾਫ਼ ਕਰਦੇ ਹਨ। mātā jī chhajj nāl dāṇe sāf karde han. Mother cleans the grain with a winnowing basket.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਛੱਜ (chhajj) is inherited from a reconstructed chajja, "basket"; CDIAL lists Punjabi chajj, "winnowing basket", under it (CDIAL 4964).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ਛੱਟਣਾ.

ਅਸੀਂ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਣੇ ਛੱਟਾਂਗੇ। asīṁ havā vich dāṇe chhaṭṭāṅge. We will winnow the grain in the wind.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦਾਣੇ ਛੱਟੇ, maiṁ dāṇe chhaṭṭe.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਤੂੜੀ (tūṛī) — chopped straw; chaff.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a winnowing basket, then the Punjabi for to winnow.

You'll want to know: ਤੂੜੀ.
ਤੂੜੀ — tūṛī — "chopped straw; chaff".
ਗਾਂ ਤੂੜੀ ਖਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ। gāṁ tūṛī khāndī hai. The cow eats chopped straw.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਤੂੜੀ (tūṛī) goes back to Sanskrit tuṣa, "chaff of grain"; CDIAL lists Punjabi only tuh, "chaff", under it; the form tūṛī, "straw, chaff", is the one it gives for Lahnda (CDIAL 5892).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਪਰਾਲੀ (parālī) — rice straw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to winnow, then the Punjabi for chopped straw.

You'll want to know: ਪਰਾਲੀ.
ਪਰਾਲੀ — parālī — "rice straw".
ਸਿਆਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਪਰਾਲੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਸੌਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ। siāl vich asīṁ parālī utte saunde hāṁ. In winter we sleep on rice straw.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਰਾਲੀ (parālī) is inherited from Sanskrit palāla, "stalk, straw"; CDIAL lists Punjabi parālī, "straw of rice or millet", under it (CDIAL 7958).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for chopped straw, then the Punjabi for rice straw.

You'll want to know: ਬੋਰੀ.
