## Eight more filmstrips lift the pen where native writers do

- The filmstrips for ద (TE-S116), డ (TE-S123), ణ (TE-S126), బ (TE-S157),
  ఫ (TE-S160), ఐ (TE-S164), ఋ (TE-S129) and ళ (TE-S121) had a pen lift
  between every numbered movement of their tracing source. They now lift
  only where native writers do: ద, ణ, బ, ఐ and ళ in one unbroken stroke, డ
  in two, and ఫ and ఋ in three. In HP Labs India's native-writer samples,
  70% to 97% of writers use those counts; for ఋ, three strokes is the most
  common count (48%). Every movement and its direction is still shown,
  frame by frame.
- Every Telugu filmstrip caption now fits on two lines. 51 captions in 23
  other filmstrips wrapped to three or four lines; they and three more that
  sat one word from a third line were shortened, keeping their meaning. For
  example, "loop counterclockwise around the left bowl" became "loop the
  left bowl counterclockwise", and "draw down and up through the separate
  upper chevron" became "draw the separate chevron down and up".
