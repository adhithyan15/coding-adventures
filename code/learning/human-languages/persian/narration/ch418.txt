Lesson 1 of 5.
نرم افزار (narm afzâr) — software.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for access, then the Persian for virtual.

You'll want to know: نرم افزار.
نرم افزار (narm afzâr) — "software".
این نرم افزار رایگان است. in narm afzâr râygân ast. This software is free.
Narm "soft" + afzâr "tool". Also an app.

The word, taken apart.
نرم افزار (narm afzâr) is a modern native calque of English software, built from narm "soft" + afzâr "tool, equipment".

Lesson 4 of 5.
آپلود کردن (âplod kardan) — to upload.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for hardware, then the Persian for a computer file.

You'll want to know: آپلود کردن.
آپلود کردن (âplod kardan) — "to upload".
او ویدیو را در اینترنت آپلود کرد. u vide'o râ dar internet âplod kard. He uploaded the video to the internet.
An English loan + kardan. Persian also says bârgozâri kardan.

The word, taken apart.
آپلود کردن (âplod kardan) joins the English loan upload to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do", the usual way Persian takes in new verbs.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پشتیبانی (poshtibâni) — technical support; backing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a computer file, then the Persian for to upload.

You'll want to know: پشتیبانی.
پشتیبانی (poshtibâni) — "technical support; backing".
پشتیبانی شرکت مشکل را حل کرد. poshtibâni-ye sherkat moshkel râ hal kard. The company's support team solved the problem.
Posht "back" + -bân "keeper" + -i. Poshtibâni kardan — to support.

The word, taken apart.
پشتیبانی (poshtibâni) is native Persian, built from posht "back", usually traced to Middle Persian pušt, + -bân "keeper" + -i. Support is keeping someone's back.
