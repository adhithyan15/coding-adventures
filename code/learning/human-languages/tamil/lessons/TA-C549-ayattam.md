## You'll want to know: ஆயத்தம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C549-VIZHA549-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆயத்தம்** — *āyattam* — "readiness; preparation".

> விருந்தினர் வந்தால் எல்லாம் ஆயத்தமாக இருக்க வேண்டும்.
>
> *viruntiṉar vantāl ellām āyattamāka irukka vēṇṭum.*
>
> If the guests come, everything must be ready.

With -ஆக: ஆயத்தமாக இரு, to be ready.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ஆயத்தம்** (*āyattam*) is not settled; one suggestion is Sanskrit *āyatta*, "being at someone's disposal" (Monier-Williams), with the Tamil ending -அம்.
