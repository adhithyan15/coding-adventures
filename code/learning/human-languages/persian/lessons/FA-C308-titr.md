## You'll want to know: تیتر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C308-GOZARESH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تیتر** (*titr*) — "a headline".

> تیتر روزنامه امروز خیلی بزرگ بود.
>
> *Titr-e ruznâme-ye emruz kheyli bozorg bud.*
>
> Today's newspaper headline was very big.

Plural *titr-hâ*. Also the title line of a television news item.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*titr* is usually traced to French *titre*, "title"; EtymDB lists no entry.
