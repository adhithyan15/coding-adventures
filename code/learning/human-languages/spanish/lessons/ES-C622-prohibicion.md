## You'll want to know: la prohibición
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C622-REGLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la prohibición** — "the ban, the prohibition".

> Hay una prohibición de fumar.
>
> *There is a smoking ban.*

Built on *prohibir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prohibición* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prohibitio* 'a forbidding', built on *prohibere* 'to hold back', from *habere* 'to hold' (Lewis & Short).
