# விரட்டு (viraṭṭu) — to drive away; to chase off

## You'll want to know: விரட்டு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C303-KOOCHAL303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**விரட்டு** — *viraṭṭu* — "to drive away; to chase off".

> காவலர்கள் கூட்டத்தை விரட்டினார்கள்.
>
> *kāvalarkaḷ kūṭṭattai viraṭṭiṉārkaḷ.*
>
> The police drove the crowd away.

Past (I …): விரட்டினேன், *viraṭṭiṉēṉ*.

What is chased off takes -ஐ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**விரட்டு** (*viraṭṭu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 5489), in a family of words for fear: to frighten off.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
