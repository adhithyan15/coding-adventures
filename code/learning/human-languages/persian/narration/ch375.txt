6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
مزیت (maziyat) — an advantage, a merit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to make progress, then the Persian for to fall behind.

You'll want to know: مزیت.
مزیت (maziyat) — "an advantage, a merit".
مزیت این کار این است که نزدیک خانه است. maziyat-e in kâr in ast ke nazdik-e khâne ast. The advantage of this job is that it is near home.
Its Arabic broken plural mazâyâ also means benefits at work.

The word, taken apart.
مزیت (maziyat) is usually traced to Arabic maziyya "merit, distinction", the quality that sets one thing above another.

Lesson 2 of 6.
عیب ('eyb) — a flaw, a defect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to fall behind, then the Persian for an advantage.

You'll want to know: عیب.
عیب ('eyb) — "a flaw, a defect".
تنها عیب این ماشین رنگ آن است. tanhâ 'eyb-e in mâshin rang-e ân ast. The only flaw of this car is its colour.
'Eyb nadârad — never mind, it doesn't matter. Plural 'oyub, an Arabic broken plural.

The word, taken apart.
عیب ('eyb) is usually traced to Arabic 'ayb "fault, blemish"; in Persian it is a flaw in a thing or in a person.

زیان (ziyân) — "harm, a loss".
سیگار برای بدن زیان دارد. sigâr barâye badan ziyân dârad. Smoking is harmful to the body.
Ziyân dâshtan — to be harmful; ziyân didan — to suffer a loss.

The word, taken apart.
زیان (ziyân) "damage, injury" is listed in EtymDB without a parent; it is an inherited Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ارزش داشتن (arzesh dâshtan) — to be worth it, to be worth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a flaw, then the Persian for harm.

You'll want to know: ارزش داشتن.
ارزش داشتن (arzesh dâshtan) — "to be worth it, to be worth".
این فیلم ارزش دیدن دارد. in film arzesh-e didan dârad. This film is worth seeing.
Arzesh "value" + dâshtan (stem dâr-), with an ezafe: arzesh-e … dârad.

The word, taken apart.
ارزش داشتن (arzesh dâshtan) is native Persian, built from arzesh "value", itself built on arz-, the present stem of arzidan "to be worth", and dâshtan "to have", the inherited verb.

Lesson 5 of 6.
نتیجه دادن (natije dâdan) — to pay off, to work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for harm, then the Persian for to be worth it.

You'll want to know: نتیجه دادن.
نتیجه دادن (natije dâdan) — "to pay off, to work".
تلاش او بالاخره نتیجه داد. talâsh-e u bâlâkhare natije dâd. Her effort finally paid off.
Natije "result" + dâdan "to give" (stem deh-): literally to give a result.

The word, taken apart.
نتیجه دادن (natije dâdan) is literally "to give a result": natije comes from Arabic natīja "result" (EtymDB), and dâdan "to give" is traced by EtymDB to PIE deh₃-.
