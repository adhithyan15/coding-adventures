## You'll want to know: la investigación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C520-DELITO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la investigación** — "the investigation; research".

> La investigación duró dos años.
>
> *The investigation lasted two years.*

It also means scientific research: *la investigación médica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investigación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *investīgātiō* 'a searching out', from *investīgāre* 'to track, as a dog does' (Lewis & Short), built on *vestīgium* 'a footprint'.
