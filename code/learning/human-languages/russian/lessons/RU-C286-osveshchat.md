## You'll want to know: освещать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C286-OSVESHCHAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**освещать** (*osveshchát*) — "to report on, to cover (in the news); to light up".

> Газеты широко освещали эту историю.
>
> *Gazéty shirokó osveshcháli étu istóriyu.*
>
> The newspapers covered this story widely.

Imperfective; perfective *осветить* (*osvetít*), *я освещу*. First sense: *лампа освещает комнату* — the lamp lights the room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*освещать* is built on *о-* and *свет* "light", an inherited Slavic word (Proto-Slavic *světŭ*), with *-щ-*, the Church Slavonic shape *-свещ-* (native Russian has *ч*, as in *свеча*): to throw light on.
