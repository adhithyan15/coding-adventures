## You'll want to know: خوش گذشتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C510-LEISURE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خوش گذشتن** (*khosh gozashtan*) — "to have a good time".

> در مهمانی به ما خیلی خوش گذشت.
>
> *dar mehmâni be mâ kheyli khosh gozasht.*
>
> We had a great time at the party.

Impersonal: *be man khosh gozasht* — 'it passed pleasantly for me'. Present stem *gozar-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khosh gozashtan* is built on two native Persian words: *khosh*, 'pleasant', and *gozashtan*, 'to pass': time passed pleasantly. EtymDB has no entry for the phrase.
