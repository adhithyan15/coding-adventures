## You'll want to know: रमणीयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C424-PARYATANA424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रमणीयः** — *ramaṇīyaḥ* — "delightful; lovely".

> सरोवरस्य तीरं रमणीयम् अस्ति।
>
> *sarovarasya tīraṃ ramaṇīyam asti.*
>
> The lake shore is delightful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रमणीयः** (*ramaṇīyaḥ*) is built on the root √ram, "to delight", which also gives *ramate*, "enjoys", with the ending *-anīya*; MW gives *ramaṇīya* as to be enjoyed, pleasant, delightful (MW).
