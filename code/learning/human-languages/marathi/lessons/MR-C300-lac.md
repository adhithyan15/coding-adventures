## You'll want to know: लाच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C300-LACH300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लाच** — *lāc* — "a bribe".

> लाच घेताना एका कारकुनाला पकडलं.
>
> *lāc ghetānā ekā kārkunālā pakaḍlaṁ.*
>
> A clerk was caught taking a bribe.

Feminine.

लाच घेणे, to take a bribe; लाच देणे, to give one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाच** (*lāc*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *lañcā*, 'a present, a bribe' (CDIAL 10914).
