## You'll want to know: ತಾಳ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C534-SANGITA534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಳ** — *tāḷa* — "a rhythm; a beat".

> ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಹಾಡಿಗೆ ತಾಳ ಹಾಕಿದರು.
>
> *makkaḷu hāḍige tāḷa hākidaru.*
>
> The children kept time to the song.

ತಾಳ ಹಾಕು, to keep time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಳ** (*tāḷa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tāla*, "a cymbal" (CDIAL 5801): the beat the cymbals keep.
