## You'll want to know: ಮೊದಲನೇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-08]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C74-ORDINAL-04, KA-GRAMMAR-C74-ORDINAL-02] -->

**ಮೊದಲನೇ** (*modalanē*) — "**first**", again.

In speech the ending **-ಅನೆಯ** is usually shortened to **-ಅನೇ**. The **ಯ** goes,
the **ೆ** lengthens to **ೇ**, and that is all:

| careful | everyday |
|---|---|
| **ಮೊದಲನೆಯ** *modalaneya* | **ಮೊದಲನೇ** *modalanē* |
| **ಎರಡನೆಯ** *eraḍaneya* | **ಎರಡನೇ** *eraḍanē* |
| **ಮೂರನೆಯ** *mūraneya* | **ಮೂರನೇ** *mūranē* |

Both are worth having, and for different reasons: you need to recognise the long
one on a printed page, and you need to produce the short one at a door.
