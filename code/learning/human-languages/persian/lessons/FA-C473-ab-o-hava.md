## You'll want to know: آب و هوا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C473-HAVASHENASI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آب و هوا** (*âb-o havâ*) — "the climate; the weather".

> آب و هوای شمال مرطوب است.
>
> *Âb-o havâ-ye shomâl martub ast.*
>
> The climate in the north is humid.

Literally 'water and air'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âb-o havâ* is listed in EtymDB as 'water and air': native *âb* 'water', from Middle Persian *āb*, joined with *havâ* 'air', borrowed from Arabic *hawāʾ*.
