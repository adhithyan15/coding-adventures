Malayalam, chapter 276: Always, Regularly, Often, Usually, Rarely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
എപ്പോഴും (eppōḻuṁ) — always.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for jaundice, then the Malayalam for blood pressure.

You'll want to know: എപ്പോഴും.
എപ്പോഴും — eppōḻuṁ — "always".

The word, taken apart.
എപ്പോഴും (eppōḻuṁ) is native Dravidian: eppōḷ, "when", which DEDR 5151 lists, built on the word for "time" (DEDR 4559), with -uṁ: "at any time".

The word, taken apart.
പതിവായി (pativāyi) is native Dravidian: pativu, "settlement, custom" (DEDR 3911, with Tamil pati), and āyi, "as, having become" (DEDR 333): "as a custom". DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

You'll want to know: അപൂർവമായി.

The word, taken apart.
അപൂർവമായി (apūrvamāyi) joins Sanskrit apūrva, "unprecedented, rare" (CDIAL 494), to native āyi, "as" (DEDR 333). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.
