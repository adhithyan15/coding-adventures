## You'll want to know: はなむこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C289-KEKKON289-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はなむこ** — *hanamuko* — "a bridegroom".

> **はなむこは わらっていました。** — *hanamuko wa waratte imashita* — "The groom was smiling."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はなむこ** (*hanamuko*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hana*, "flower", and *muko*, "son-in-law" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
