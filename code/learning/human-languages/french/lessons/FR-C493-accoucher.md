## You'll want to know: accoucher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C493-BEBE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**accoucher** — "to give birth".

> Ma sœur a accouché d'un garçon ce matin.
>
> *My sister gave birth to a boy this morning.*

*Accoucher de* — to give birth to. *L'accouchement* — the birth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accoucher* comes from Old French *acouchier* "to lay down, to take to one's bed" (EtymDB), built on *coucher*, from Latin *collocāre* "to place", itself built on *locus* "place" (Lewis & Short).
