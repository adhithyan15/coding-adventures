## You'll want to know: l'avenue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C437-RUELLES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'avenue** — "the avenue".

> Les grands magasins sont sur l'avenue principale.
>
> *The big shops are on the main avenue.*

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avenue* is the old feminine past participle of *avenir* 'to come to' (EtymDB), from Latin *advenīre*, built on *venīre* 'to come': the way that leads up to a place.
