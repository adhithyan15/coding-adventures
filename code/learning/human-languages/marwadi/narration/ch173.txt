Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 173: At the Hearth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फूंकणो (phū̃kṇo) — to blow on (a fire).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to plaster, then the Marwadi for mud plaster.

You'll want to know: फूंकणो.
फूंकणो — phū̃kṇo — "to blow on (a fire)".
दादी आग फूंकै है। dādī āg phū̃kai hai. Grandmother blows on the fire.
Past (I …): म्हैं आग फूंकी, mhaĩ āg phū̃kī.

The word, taken apart.
फूंकणो (phū̃kṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed phūtka, "blowing", a word that imitates the sound of a puff; CDIAL lists Old Marwari phūkaï, "to blow" (CDIAL 9102).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for mud plaster, then the Marwadi for to blow on.

You'll want to know: धूंवो.
धूंवो — dhū̃vo — "smoke".
रसोई मांय घणो धूंवो है। rasoī mā̃y ghaṇo dhū̃vo hai. There's a lot of smoke in the kitchen.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धूंवो (dhū̃vo) is inherited from Sanskrit dhūma, "smoke, vapour"; CDIAL lists Hindi dhūā̃ (CDIAL 6849). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

ईंधण सूको है। ī̃dhaṇ sūko hai. The firewood is dry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for smoke, then the Marwadi for firewood.

You'll want to know: बुझावणो.
बुझावणो — bujhāvṇo — "to put out (a fire or a lamp)".
रात मांय हूं दीयो बुझाऊं हूं। rāt mā̃y hū̃ dīyo bujhāū̃ hū̃. At night I put out the lamp.
Past (I …): म्हैं दीयो बुझायो, mhaĩ dīyo bujhāyo.

The word, taken apart.
बुझावणो (bujhāvṇo) is built on a reconstructed vijjhāyati, "is burnt out, goes out"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bujhāvaï, "to put out" (CDIAL 11703).
