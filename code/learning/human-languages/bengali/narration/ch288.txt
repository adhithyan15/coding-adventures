Bengali, chapter 288: The Cyclone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘূর্ণিঝড় (ghūrnijhôṛ) — a cyclone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for at once, then the Bengali for to crash down.

You'll want to know: ঘূর্ণিঝড়.
ঘূর্ণিঝড় — ghūrnijhôṛ — "a cyclone".
সমুদ্র থেকে একটা বড় ঘূর্ণিঝড় আসছিল। sômudrô theke ekṭā bôṛo ghūrnijhôṛ āschhilô. A big cyclone was coming in from the sea.

The word, taken apart.
ঘূর্ণিঝড় (ghūrnijhôṛ) joins Sanskrit ghūrṇi, 'a whirling' (Monier-Williams), to ঝড়, a storm: a whirling storm.

Lesson 2 of 5.
উপকূল (upôkūl) — a coast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to crash down, then the Bengali for a cyclone.

You'll want to know: উপকূল.
উপকূল — upôkūl — "a coast".
ঘূর্ণিঝড়ের আগে উপকূলের মানুষ গ্রাম ছেড়ে চলে গেল। ghūrnijhôṛer āge upôkūler mānush grām chheṛe chôle gelô. Before the cyclone the people of the coast left their villages.

The word, taken apart.
উপকূল (upôkūl) is Sanskrit upakūla, 'being on the shore' (Monier-Williams): upa-, 'near', and kūla, 'a bank'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
এগোনো (egono) — to move forward; to advance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cyclone, then the Bengali for a coast.

You'll want to know: এগোনো.
এগোনো — egono — "to move forward; to advance".
ঝড়টা ধীরে ধীরে উপকূলের দিকে এগোচ্ছিল। jhôṛṭā dhīre dhīre upôkūler dike egochchhilô. The storm was slowly moving towards the coast.
Past (he/she …): সেই ঝড় এগোল, sei jhôṛ egolô.

The word, taken apart.
এগোনো (egono) is usually explained as built on আগে, in front — Sanskrit agra, 'top, front' (CDIAL 68) — with the verb ending: to go to the front, to move forward.

Lesson 4 of 5.
উপড়ানো (upṛāno) — to uproot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a coast, then the Bengali for to move forward.

You'll want to know: উপড়ানো.
উপড়ানো — upṛāno — "to uproot".
ঝড়ে রাস্তার অনেক গাছ উপড়ে গেল। jhôṛe rāstār ônek gāchh upṛe gelô. The storm uprooted many trees along the road.
Past (he/she …): সেই ঝড় গাছটা উপড়াল, sei jhôṛ gāchhṭā upṛālô.

The word, taken apart.
উপড়ানো (upṛāno) comes down from Sanskrit utpāṭayati, 'roots up', and its partner utpaṭati, 'bursts open' (CDIAL 1809, which lists Bengali upaṛā, 'to come up by the roots').

বিদ্যুৎ (bidyut) is Sanskrit vidyut, lightning, from vi-√dyut, 'to flash forth' (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for lightning and for the power in the wires.
