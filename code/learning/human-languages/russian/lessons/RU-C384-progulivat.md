## You'll want to know: прогуливать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C384-UCHYOBA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**прогуливать** (*progúlivat*) — "to skip (classes), to play truant".

> Он часто прогуливает лекции.
>
> *On chásto progúlivayet léktsii.*
>
> He often skips lectures.

Imperfective; perfective *прогулять*. *Прогул* — a missed day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**прогуливать** is built from *про-* "past, through" and *гулять* "to walk, to stroll": to stroll past the lesson instead of going in.
