## You'll want to know: व्याख्यान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C370-VYAKHYAN370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याख्यान** — *vyākhyān* — "a lecture".

> प्राध्यापकांचं व्याख्यान दोन तास चाललं.
>
> *prādhyāpkāñcaṁ vyākhyān don tās cāllaṁ.*
>
> The professor's lecture went on for two hours.

Neuter.

व्याख्यान देणे, to give a lecture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याख्यान** (*vyākhyān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vyākhyāna*, "comment, narration" (CDIAL 12188); the same entry lists an inherited Marathi *vākhāṇ*, "praise".
