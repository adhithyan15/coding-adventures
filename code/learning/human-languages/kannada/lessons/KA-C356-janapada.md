## You'll want to know: ಜಾನಪದ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C356-JANAPADA356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾನಪದ** — *jānapada* — "folklore; folk".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಜಾನಪದ ಹಾಡುಗಳು ಗೊತ್ತು.
>
> *ajjige tumbā jānapada hāḍugaḷu gottu.*
>
> Grandmother knows many folk songs.

Before a noun it means "folk": ಜಾನಪದ ಕಥೆ, a folk tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾನಪದ** (*jānapada*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jānapada*, "living in the country", and as a noun "a country person" (Monier-Williams), from *janapada*, "a country, a people".
