## You'll want to know: accorciare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C455-ARTIGIANI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**accorciare** — "to shorten, to take up".

> Mi può accorciare questi pantaloni?
>
> *Can you take up these trousers for me?*

*Accorciare la strada* — to take a short cut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accorciare* is built from *a-* and *corto* 'short', which is inherited from Latin *curtus* 'cut short' (Lewis & Short).
