## You'll want to know: यात्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C320-UTSAV320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**यात्रा** — *yātrā* — "a pilgrimage; a temple fair".

> दरवर्षी हजारो लोक या यात्रेला येतात.
>
> *darvarṣī hajāro lok yā yātrelā yetāt.*
>
> Every year thousands of people come to this pilgrimage.

Feminine.

यात्रेला जाणे, to go on a pilgrimage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यात्रा** (*yātrā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *yātrā*, 'a going, a journey, a festive procession' (Monier-Williams).
