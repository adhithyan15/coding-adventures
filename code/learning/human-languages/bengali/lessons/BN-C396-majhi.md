## You'll want to know: মাঝি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C396-MAJHI396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মাঝি** — *mājhi* — "a boatman".

> বুড়ো মাঝি নৌকায় বসে গান গাইছিল।
>
> *buṛo mājhi noukāy bôse gān gāichhilô.*
>
> The old boatman sat in the boat, singing.

The মাঝি's river songs are a whole branch of Bengali folk music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মাঝি** (*mājhi*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *māji*, "a steersman", under the reconstructed *majjhika*, "a boatman" (CDIAL 9714). It is not filed under *mádhya*, middle, the source of মাঝ.
