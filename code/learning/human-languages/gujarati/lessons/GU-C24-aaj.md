# Āj reaches the page

## Warm-up

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-AAJ-01, GU-LEX-AAJ-01, GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01, GU-SCRIPT-JA-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-C24-aaj-reading","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-AAJ-01","GU-LEX-AAJ-01"],"prompt":"Read this word aloud and give its meaning: આજ.","answer":"āj — today","accepted":["aaj today","āj today"],"feedback":{"correct":"You read āj from signs you already knew.","incorrect":"Name the two signs one at a time, then read the word again."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Uncover **આજ** once. Two signs, both old friends: the standalone **આ** that
opens *āvjo*, and the **જ** of the doorway nine. Name them in order, then read
the word once.
