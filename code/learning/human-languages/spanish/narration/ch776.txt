Spanish, chapter 776: Si olvidas la contraseña.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la contraseña — the password.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the piece, then the Spanish for the smell.

You'll want to know: la contraseña.
la contraseña — "the password".
Si olvidas la contraseña, pulsa aquí. If you forget your password, click here.
La clave is also used, especially for a PIN.

The word, taken apart.
contraseña is built on contra 'against, in return' and seña 'a sign', which comes from Latin signa, the plural of signum 'a mark' (Lewis & Short). It was first a soldier's countersign.

archivo is a learned borrowing from Late Latin archīvum 'the archives' (Lewis & Short), which comes from Greek archeîon 'a town hall' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: la carpeta.
la carpeta — "the folder".
Si no encuentras la foto, mira en esta carpeta. If you can't find the photo, look in this folder.
Also a cardboard folder for papers.

The word, taken apart.
carpeta was borrowed from French carpette (EtymDB), a cloth cover for a table, which goes back to Medieval Latin carpīta. The cover for papers became a folder.

Lesson 4 of 5.
actualizar — to update.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the file, then the Spanish for the folder.

You'll want to know: actualizar.
actualizar — "to update".
Si no funciona, actualiza la aplicación. If it doesn't work, update the app.
It takes c before e: actualicé. La actualización is the update.

The word, taken apart.
actualizar is built on actual 'current' with the ending -izar. Actual comes from Latin āctuālis 'active, practical' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from agere 'to do'. In Spanish actual means current, not real.
