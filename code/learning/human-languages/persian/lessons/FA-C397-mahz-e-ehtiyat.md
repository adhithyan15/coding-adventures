## You'll want to know: محض احتیاط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C397-EHTIYAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**محض احتیاط** (*mahz-e ehtiyât*) — "as a precaution, in case".

> محض احتیاط یک چتر برداشتم.
>
> *mahz-e ehtiyât yek chatr bardâshtam.*
>
> I took an umbrella in case.

*Mahz* "pure" + *ehtiyât* "caution": purely for caution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محض احتیاط** joins two words usually traced to Arabic, *maḥḍ* "pure, mere" and *iḥtiyāṭ* "caution", with a Persian ezafe: purely for caution's sake.
