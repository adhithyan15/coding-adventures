## You'll want to know: ప్రారంభం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C376-SPANDANA376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రారంభం** — *prārambhaṁ* — "a start; a beginning".

> కథ ప్రారంభం చాలా బాగుంది.
>
> *katha prārambhaṁ cālā bāgundi.*
>
> The start of the story is very good.

A formal word; in speech మొదలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రారంభం** (*prārambhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prārambha*, "commencement, beginning" (Monier-Williams).
