Arabic, chapter 133: Desert, Lake, Lightning, Thunder, Storm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صحراء (ṣaḥrāʾ) — a desert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a valley, then the Arabic for a hill.

You'll want to know: صحراء.
صحراء — ṣaḥrāʾ — "a desert".

The word, taken apart.
صحراء (ṣaḥrāʾ) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ḥ-r, "a dun, whitish-red colour" (Lane). Ṣaḥrāʾ is the dun land, the desert; English Sahara comes from it.

You'll want to know: رعد.
رعد — raʿd — "thunder".

The word, taken apart.
رعد (raʿd) is built on the native Arabic root r-ʿ-d, "to thunder; to tremble" (Lane). Raʿd is the thunder itself.

عاصفة (ʿāṣifa) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ṣ-f, "to blow violently" (Lane). ʿĀṣifa is the feminine active participle, "a violent wind".
