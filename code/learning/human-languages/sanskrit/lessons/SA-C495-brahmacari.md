## You'll want to know: ब्रह्मचारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C495-YAUVANA495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्रह्मचारी** — *brahmacārī* — "a celibate student".

> ब्रह्मचारी गुरोः गृहे वसति।
>
> *brahmacārī guroḥ gṛhe vasati.*
>
> The student lives in his teacher's house.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्रह्मचारी** (*brahmacārī*) is built from *brahman*, "sacred knowledge", and *cārin*, "practising", from the root √car; MW gives "a young Brāhman who is a student of the Veda, before marriage" (MW).
