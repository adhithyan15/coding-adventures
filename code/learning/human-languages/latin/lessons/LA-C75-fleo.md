## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fleō** is inherited through Proto-Italic *\*flēō*, "to weep", and is probably traced to Proto-Indo-European *\*bhleh1-*, "to cry out, to howl" (EtymDB 2.1).
