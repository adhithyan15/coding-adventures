## You'll want to know: el ejército
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C567-GUERRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el ejército** — "the army".

> Mi tío estuvo en el ejército.
>
> *My uncle was in the army.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ejército* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exercitus* 'a trained army' (EtymDB), from *exercēre* 'to train, to exercise'.
