# двадцать — twenty, and both halves still audible

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Count one to ten, then say ten on its own.

## You'll want to know first — one word, and a piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-DVADTSAT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-TSAT-TENS-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-DVA-01, RU-LEX-DESYAT-01, RU-LEX-TRI-01] -->

> **двадцать** — *dvádtsat* — **twenty**

Take it apart and both pieces are words you have: **два** at the front, and
**-дцать**, which is **десять** worn down, at the back. Two tens.

That worn ending does the same job further up: **тридцать** is three tens,
thirty. Russian's tens are not a new vocabulary; they are numbers you already
know with **десять** ground down onto the end of them.
