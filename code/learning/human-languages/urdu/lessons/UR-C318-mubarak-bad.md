## You'll want to know: مبارک باد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C318-EIDMORNING318-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مبارک باد** — *mubārak bād* — "congratulations".

> پڑوسیوں نے ہمیں عید کی مبارک باد دی۔
>
> *paṛosiyoṅ ne hameṅ īd kī mubārak bād dī.*
>
> The neighbours wished us a happy Eid.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مبارک باد** (*mubārak bād*) is usually traced to Arabic *mubārak*, "blessed", and Persian *bād*, "may it be", a form of the Persian verb "to be": "may it be blessed". It is feminine, and the one wished takes **کو**: **ہمیں مبارک باد دی**.
