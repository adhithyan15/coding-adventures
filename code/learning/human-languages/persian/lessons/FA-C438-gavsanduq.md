## You'll want to know: گاوصندوق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C438-TAHVIL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گاوصندوق** (*gâvsanduq*) — "a safe, a strongbox".

> پولمان را در گاوصندوق هتل گذاشتیم.
>
> *Pulemân râ dar gâvsanduq-e hotel gozâshtim.*
>
> We put our money in the hotel safe.

*gâv* 'ox', here 'big and strong', + *sanduq* 'chest'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gâvsanduq* is built from native *gâv* 'ox', used for things that are big and heavy, and *sanduq* 'chest, box', borrowed from Arabic *ṣundūq* (EtymDB).
