## You'll want to know: bǎojiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C483-BETTER483-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎojiàn* — "health care; looking after one's health".

> *wǒ yéye bǐ wǒ nǎinai zhùyì bǎojiàn* — My grandfather takes more care of his health than my grandmother does.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎojiàn* is built from 保 'to keep' and *jiàn* 'strong', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *gjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to keep safe' and the second 'strong'.
