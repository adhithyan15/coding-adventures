## You'll want to know: পদক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C520-PODOK520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**পদক** — *pôdôk* — "a medal".

> সাঁতারে মেয়েটা একটা পদক জিতল।
>
> *Sā̃tāre meyeṭā ekṭā pôdôk jitlô.*
>
> The girl won a medal in swimming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পদক** (*pôdôk*) is a Sanskrit word built on *pada*; Monier-Williams gives *padaka* as a kind of ornament, and the sense "a medal" is the modern language's own.
