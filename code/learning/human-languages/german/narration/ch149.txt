German, chapter 149: To name, To smell, To collect, To give, To shout.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
nennen — to name, to call.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bring along, then the German for to take along.

You'll want to know: nennen.
nennen — "to name, to call".

The word, taken apart.
nennen from Old High German nemnen from Proto-Germanic namnijaną, built on namô, "name" (EtymDB 2.1) — the verb of the word English keeps as name.
