## You'll want to know: ટળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C399-DELAY399-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ટળવું** — *ṭaḷvũ* — "to be averted; to be put off".

> પવનની દિશા ફરી ગઈ, એટલે મોટું તોફાન ટળી ગયું.
>
> *pavannī diśā pharī gaī, eṭle moṭũ tophān ṭaḷī gayũ.*
>
> The wind changed direction, so the big storm was averted.

Past (it …): તે તોફાન ટળી ગયું, *te tophān ṭaḷī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટળવું** (*ṭaḷvũ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *ṭálati*, "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450); ટાળવું, to avoid, is its causative partner: what you put off, ટાળવું; what passes off by itself, ટળવું.
