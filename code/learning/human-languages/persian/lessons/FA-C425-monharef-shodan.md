## You'll want to know: منحرف شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C425-OTOBAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**منحرف شدن** (*monharef shodan*) — "to swerve, to veer off; to go astray".

> ماشین روی برف منحرف شد.
>
> *Mâshin ru-ye barf monharef shod.*
>
> The car swerved on the snow.

*monharef* 'turned aside' + *shodan*. Also figurative: to go off the right path.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*monharef shodan* is built from *monharef* 'turned aside', a loan usually traced to Arabic *munḥarif*, and the native verb *shodan* 'to become'. EtymDB does not list it.
