## You'll want to know: ઉપકરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C392-SAJJ392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપકરણ** — *upakaraṇ* — "a device".

> જો વીજળી જશે તો આ ઉપકરણ કામ નહીં કરે.
>
> *jo vījḷī jaśe to ā upakaraṇ kām nahī̃ kare.*
>
> If the power goes, this device will not work.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપકરણ** (*upakaraṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *upakaraṇa*, "doing a service; an implement, a means" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
