## You'll want to know: 岛
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C444-ISLAND444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**岛** — *dǎo* — "island".

> **我们坐船去那个岛** *wǒmen zuò chuán qù nàge dǎo* — We're taking a boat to that island.

The new character, in pieces: 岛 — seven strokes in two pieces: on top a short stroke falling to the left, a small bar that turns down and hooks, a dot inside it, and a long stroke that runs down, turns right, turns down again and hooks; tucked under it 山 shān, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**岛** (*dǎo*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *tawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it, after an older dictionary, as the hills here and there in the sea where one can stop and rest.
