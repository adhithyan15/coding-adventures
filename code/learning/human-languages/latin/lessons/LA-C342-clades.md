## You'll want to know: clādēs, clādis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C342-CLADES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**clādēs, clādis** — "a defeat, a disaster".

> Post clādem paucī mīlitēs in castra rediērunt.
>
> *After the defeat few soldiers returned to the camp.*

Feminine, 3rd declension. A heavy defeat with great loss of life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**clādēs** is usually traced to a root meaning "to beat, to break": EtymDB gives a Proto-Indo-European root of that sense, and Lewis & Short compare Greek *klaō*, "to break". The details are not settled.
