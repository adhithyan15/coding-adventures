## You'll want to know: گاڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C327-KHAZANA327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گاڑنا** — *gāṛnā* — "to bury".

> چور نے خزانہ زمین میں گاڑا۔
>
> *chor ne khazānā zamīn meṅ gāṛā.*
>
> The thief buried the treasure in the ground.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بیج گاڑا, *us ne bīj gāṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گاڑنا** (*gāṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gāṛnā*, "to bury, to drive in", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3979).
