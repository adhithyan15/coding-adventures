## You'll want to know: مستحق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C382-INAM382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مستحق** — *mustahiq* — "deserving".

> اگر وہ مستحق ہے تو اسے انعام ملنا چاہیے۔
>
> *agar voh mustahiq hai to use inām milnā chāhiye.*
>
> If he deserves it, he should get the prize.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستحق** (*mustahiq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mustaḥiqq*, "deserving, having a right", of the same family as **حق**, "a right".
