## You'll want to know: おおどおり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C389-OODOORI389-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おおどおり** — *ōdōri* — "a main street; an avenue".

> **おおどおりに ひゃっかてんが あります。** — *ōdōri ni hyakkaten ga arimasu* — "There is a department store on the main street."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おおどおり** (*ōdōri*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ō*, "big" and *dōri*, "street" (from *tōru*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
