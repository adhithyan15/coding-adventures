# సముదాయించు (samudāyiñcu) — to console; to pacify

## You'll want to know: సముదాయించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C505-KARUNA505-05]; assesses=[] -->

**సముదాయించు** — *samudāyiñcu* — "to console; to pacify".

> పరీక్ష తప్పిన తమ్ముడిని అక్క సముదాయించింది.
>
> *parīkṣa tappina tammuḍini akka samudāyiñcindi.*
>
> Big sister consoled our little brother, who had failed the exam.

Past (I …): సముదాయించాను, *samudāyiñcānu*.

Calming someone who is upset, often a child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సముదాయించు** (*samudāyiñcu*) is a Telugu verb for calming and consoling someone; its origin is not settled.
