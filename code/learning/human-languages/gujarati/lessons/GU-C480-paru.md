## You'll want to know: પરુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C480-KHAS480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પરુ** — *paru* — "pus".

> ઘામાં પરુ થયું છે, તેથી દાક્તર પાસે જાઓ.
>
> *ghāmā̃ paru thayũ che, tethī dāktar pāse jāo.*
>
> The wound has pus in it, so go to the doctor.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરુ** (*paru*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*paripūya-*, "purulent"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 7846).
