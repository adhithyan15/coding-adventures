Lesson 1 of 5.
das Gegenteil — the opposite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a feeling, then the German for an area.

You'll want to know: das Gegenteil.
das Gegenteil — "the opposite".

The word, taken apart.
Gegenteil is the word for "against" + the word for "part", Old High German teil from Proto-Germanic dailą, English deal (EtymDB 2.1): the opposite part.
