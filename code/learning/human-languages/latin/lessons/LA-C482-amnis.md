## You'll want to know: amnis, amnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C482-AMNIS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**amnis, amnis** — "a river, a broad stream".

> Amnis magnus et altus per campōs it.
>
> *A big, deep river runs through the plains.*

Third declension, masculine: *amnis, amnis m.* A big, deep river, a word more of poetry and history than *flūmen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amnis* is inherited from Old Latin *abnis*, from PIE *h₂ep-* 'water' (EtymDB). Lewis & Short also derive it from the Sanskrit word for water.
