Portuguese, chapter 439: Norte, sul, leste e oeste.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o nordeste — the north-east.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to run alongside, then the Portuguese for to come out.

You'll want to know: o nordeste.
o nordeste — "the north-east".
A aldeia fica no nordeste do país. The village is in the north-east of the country.
Written with a capital as a region's name.

The word, taken apart.
nordeste came from French nord-est (EtymDB), from nord 'north' and est 'east', words of Germanic origin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
o marco — the landmark; the milestone; the boundary stone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the south-east, then the Portuguese for the south-west.

You'll want to know: o marco.
o marco — "the landmark; the milestone; the boundary stone".
A torre é um marco da cidade. The tower is a landmark of the town.
Um marco do correio — a pillar box. Um marco histórico — a milestone.

The word, taken apart.
marco comes from Medieval Latin marcus, from Germanic markō 'boundary, border' (EtymDB): first a stone that marked the edge of a field.
