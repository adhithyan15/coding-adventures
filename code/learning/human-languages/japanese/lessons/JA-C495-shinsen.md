## You'll want to know: しんせん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C495-SMELL495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しんせん** — *shinsen* — "fresh".

> **いちばの さかなは しんせんです。** — *ichiba no sakana wa shinsen desu* — "The fish at the market is fresh."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんせん** (*shinsen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "new" (Middle Chinese *sin*) and *sen*, "fresh" (*sjen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
