## You'll want to know: किंमत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C423-KHAREDI423-03]; assesses=[] -->

**किंमत** — *kimmat* — "a price; a value".

> या साडीची किंमत किती आहे?
>
> *yā sāḍīcī kimmat kitī āhe?*
>
> What is the price of this sari?

Feminine.

Also worth: त्याला किंमत नाही, it has no value.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किंमत** (*kimmat*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *qīmat*, "value, price".
