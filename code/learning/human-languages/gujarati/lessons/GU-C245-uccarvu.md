## You'll want to know: ઉચ્ચારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C245-SPELL245-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉચ્ચારવું** — *uccārvũ* — "to pronounce, to utter".

> **તે દરેક શબ્દ ધીમે ઉચ્ચારે છે.** — *te darek śabda dhīme uccāre che* — "She pronounces every word slowly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉચ્ચારવું** (*uccārvũ*) comes from Sanskrit *uccārayati*, "utters", the causative of *úccarati*. CDIAL lists the inherited Gujarati *ucārvũ*, "to pronounce, speak"; the written *cc* follows the Sanskrit, a semi-tatsama (CDIAL 1641).
