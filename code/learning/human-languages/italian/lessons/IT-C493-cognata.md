## You'll want to know: la cognata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C493-PARENTI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la cognata** — "sister-in-law".

> Mia cognata viene a pranzo con i bambini.
>
> *My sister-in-law is coming to lunch with the children.*

The wife of your brother, or the sister of your husband or wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cognata* is the feminine of *cognato*, inherited from Latin *cognātus* "related by birth" (EtymDB), from *co-* "with" and *nātus* "born".
