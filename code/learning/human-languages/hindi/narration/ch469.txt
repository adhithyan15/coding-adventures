Hindi, chapter 469: Generous and Mean.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कृपण (kṛpaṇ) — miserly; tight-fisted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to snap at, then the Hindi for to snap irritably.

You'll want to know: कृपण.
कृपण — kṛpaṇ — "miserly; tight-fisted".
मेरा पड़ोसी बहुत कृपण है, अपने बच्चों को भी पैसे नहीं देता। merā paṛosī bahut kṛpaṇ hai, apne baccoṁ ko bhī paise nahīṃ detā. My neighbour is terribly tight-fisted; he won't give money even to his own children.

The word, taken apart.
कृपण (kṛpaṇ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit kṛpaṇa, first "pitiable" and then "miserly, stingy" (Monier-Williams). It is a formal word, the one books use for a miser.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मितव्ययी (mitvyayī) — thrifty; careful with money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to snap irritably, then the Hindi for miserly.

You'll want to know: मितव्ययी.
मितव्ययी — mitvyayī — "thrifty; careful with money".
दादी बहुत मितव्ययी हैं, एक पैसा भी बेकार नहीं करतीं। dādī bahut mitvyayī haiṁ, ek paisā bhī bekār nahīṃ kartīṃ. Grandmother is very thrifty; she doesn't waste a single paisa.

The word, taken apart.
मितव्ययी (mitvyayī) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit mitavyayin, "spending little, frugal", built on mita, "measured", and vyaya, "spending" (Monier-Williams). kṛpaṇ is mean; mitvyayī is sensible. It is a formal word; speech often says someone is careful with money.

Lesson 3 of 5.
दान (dān) — a donation; charity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for miserly, then the Hindi for thrifty.

You'll want to know: दान.
दान — dān — "a donation; charity".
दादी हर साल मंदिर में दान देती हैं। dādī har sāl mandir meṁ dān detī haiṁ. Grandmother gives to the temple every year.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
दान (dān) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit dāna, "the act of giving", from dā, "to give" (Monier-Williams), the same root as denā.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
भिखारी (bhikhārī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi bhikhārī, "mendicant", under Sanskrit bhikṣācara, "one who goes about begging" (CDIAL 9486). The feminine is bhikhārin.
