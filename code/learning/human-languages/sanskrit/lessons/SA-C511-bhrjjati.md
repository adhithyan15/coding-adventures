## You'll want to know: भृज्जति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C511-KASTHA511-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भृज्जति** — *bhṛjjati* — "roasts".

> सः चणकान् भृज्जति।
>
> *saḥ caṇakān bhṛjjati.*
>
> He roasts chickpeas.

Past (he …): सः चणकान् भृष्टवान्, *saḥ caṇakān bhṛṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भृज्जति** (*bhṛjjati*) comes from the root √bhrajj, "to roast, to parch, to fry", whose present is *bhṛjjati*; its past participle is *bhṛṣṭa*, and MW compares Greek *phrygō* and Latin *frīgere* (MW).
