## You'll want to know: おやこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C477-PARENT477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おやこ** — *oyako* — "parent and child".

> **こうえんで おやこが あそんでいます。** — *kōen de oyako ga asonde imasu* — "A parent and child are playing in the park."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おやこ** (*oyako*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *oya*, "parent" and *ko*, "child" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
