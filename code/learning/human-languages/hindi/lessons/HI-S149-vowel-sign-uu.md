# ◌ू — the same hook, held longer

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-148] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Yesterday's dot sat above the line. Where does today's mark go?

[PAUSE 2s] Below — and you can already draw its shorter twin.

## Script you'll notice: ◌ू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-149]; assesses=[] -->

**◌ू** — *ū*.

It is a **vowel sign**: it attaches under a consonant and replaces the *a* built
into it.

Where it sits: a **longer hook below** the consonant.

Worked through: **त** + **◌ू** = **तू**.

## Script: length is the whole difference
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-149] -->

| mark | vowel | hook |
|---|---|---|
| ◌ु | u | short |
| ◌ू | ū | long |

**The shape is the same and the tail is longer**, and so is the vowel. That
matching of the writing to the sound is a small gift, and it is worth noticing
because it is not always true elsewhere in the script.

Words already yours that carry the long one:

- **हूँ** — I am
- **पूर्ण विराम** — the full stop

## Writing: ◌ू — copy what you see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-149] -->

Put your pen on **◌ू** and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start the character or which way to
> travel**. That is a real thing, taught with real variation from school to
> school, and it is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
