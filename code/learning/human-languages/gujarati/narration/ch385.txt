Gujarati, chapter 385: Rewards.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
પુરસ્કાર (puraskār) — an award.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get one's way, then the Gujarati for to deliver.

You'll want to know: પુરસ્કાર.
પુરસ્કાર — puraskār — "an award".
જો મીરા હરીફાઈ જીતશે તો શાળા તેને પુરસ્કાર આપશે. jo Mīrā harīphāī jītśe to śāḷā tene puraskār āpśe. If Mira wins the contest, the school will give her an award.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પુરસ્કાર (puraskār) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit puraskāra, "placing in front, honouring" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 6.
મહેનતાણું (mahentāṇũ) — a fee (payment for work).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an award, then the Gujarati for appreciation.

You'll want to know: મહેનતાણું.
મહેનતાણું — mahentāṇũ — "a fee (payment for work)".
જો કામ સારું થશે તો હું વધારે મહેનતાણું ચૂકવીશ. jo kām sārũ thaśe to hũ vadhāre mahentāṇũ cūkvīś. If the work is good, I will pay a higher fee.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મહેનતાણું (mahentāṇũ) is built on mahenat, "hard work", usually traced to Arabic miḥnat, "trial, toil", through Persian, with the ending -āṇũ: what labour earns.

You'll want to know: પામવું.
પામવું — pāmvũ — "to attain".
જો તે સતત મહેનત કરશે તો બધાનો આદર પામશે. jo te satat mahenat karśe to badhāno ādar pāmśe. If he keeps working hard, he will win everyone's respect.
Past (he …): તે બધાનો આદર પામ્યો, te badhāno ādar pāmyo.

The word, taken apart.
પામવું (pāmvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit prāpnōti, "attains to, obtains"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 8947).

Lesson 5 of 6.
નવાજવું (navājvũ) — to honour with an award.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for payment for work, then the Gujarati for to attain.

You'll want to know: નવાજવું.
નવાજવું — navājvũ — "to honour with an award".
જો તે જીતશે તો સરકાર તેને પુરસ્કારથી નવાજશે. jo te jītśe to sarkār tene puraskārthī navājśe. If he wins, the government will honour him with an award.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ખેલાડીને પુરસ્કારથી નવાજ્યો, teṇe khelāḍīne puraskārthī navājyo.

The word, taken apart.
નવાજવું (navājvũ) is usually traced to Persian navāz, "caressing, favouring", with the Gujarati verb ending.
