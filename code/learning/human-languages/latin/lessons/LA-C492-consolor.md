## You'll want to know: cōnsōlor, cōnsōlārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C492-SOLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnsōlor, cōnsōlārī** — "to console, comfort".

> Amīca puellam trīstem cōnsōlātur.
>
> *Her friend comforts the sad girl.*

Deponent: *cōnsōlor, cōnsōlārī, cōnsōlātus sum*, with the accusative. English *console*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnsōlor** is built on *con-* and *sōlor* "to comfort" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *console* and *consolation* come from it.
