Portuguese, chapter 346: Pensar com lógica.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o raciocínio — the reasoning, the train of thought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the criticism, then the Portuguese for to weigh up.

You'll want to know: o raciocínio.
o raciocínio — "the reasoning, the train of thought".
O teu raciocínio está certo, mas a conta está errada. Your reasoning is right, but the sum is wrong.
Raciocinar — to reason.

The word, taken apart.
raciocínio is a learned borrowing from Latin ratiōcinium "a reckoning, a computation" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on ratiō "reckoning, reason".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
deduzir — to deduce; to deduct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to weigh up, then the Portuguese for the reasoning.

You'll want to know: deduzir.
deduzir — "to deduce; to deduct".
Pelas malas, deduzi que eles iam viajar. From the suitcases, I deduced that they were going to travel.
Conjugated like conduzir: deduzo, deduzes, deduz. Also to deduct a sum.

The word, taken apart.
deduzir is a learned borrowing from Latin dēdūcere "to lead down, to lead away, to derive" (EtymDB), built from dē and dūcere "to lead".

Lesson 3 of 5.
concluir — to conclude; to finish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the reasoning, then the Portuguese for to deduce.

You'll want to know: concluir.
concluir — "to conclude; to finish".
Depois de ler tudo, concluí que ele tinha razão. After reading everything, I concluded that he was right.
Also to finish: concluir o curso — to finish the degree.

The word, taken apart.
concluir is a learned borrowing from Latin conclūdere "to shut up, to close, to end" (EtymDB), built from con- and claudere "to shut".

Lesson 5 of 5.
o critério — the criterion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to conclude, then the Portuguese for the conclusion.

You'll want to know: o critério.
o critério — "the criterion".
Qual é o critério para escolher os jogadores? What is the criterion for choosing the players?
Plural os critérios. Com critério — with good judgement.

The word, taken apart.
critério is a learned word from Greek kritḗrion "a means of judging, a standard", built on krínein "to judge"; compare Latin criticus "capable of judging" (Lewis & Short).
