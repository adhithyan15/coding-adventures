## You'll want to know: 生日
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C75-PARTY75-02]; assesses=[] -->

**生日** — *shēngrì* — "birthday".

> **明天是我的生日** *míngtiān shì wǒ de shēngrì* — Tomorrow is my birthday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**生日** (*shēngrì*) is built from 生 'to be born' and 日 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjaeng* and *nyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
