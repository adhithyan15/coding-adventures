## You'll want to know: भारावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C465-NAVAL465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भारावणे** — *bhārāvṇe* — "to be overwhelmed".

> सगळ्यांचं प्रेम पाहून ती भारावली.
>
> *sagḷyāñcaṁ prem pāhūn tī bhārāvlī.*
>
> She was overwhelmed by everyone's love.

Past (he …): तो भारावला, *to bhārāvlā*.

Built on भार, a load: weighed down with feeling, usually glad. भारावून जाणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भारावणे** (*bhārāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on भार, "a load", from Sanskrit *bhāra* (CDIAL 9459).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
