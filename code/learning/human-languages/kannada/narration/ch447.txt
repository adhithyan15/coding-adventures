Kannada, chapter 447: A Blocked Road.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ (rastetaḍe) — a roadblock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to lose one's way, then the Kannada for a footstep.

You'll want to know: ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ.
ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ — rastetaḍe — "a roadblock".
ಮುಷ್ಕರದ ಕಾರಣ ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ ಇತ್ತು. muṣkarada kāraṇa heddāriyalli rastetaḍe ittu. Because of the strike there was a roadblock on the highway.
ರಸ್ತೆ, road, plus ತಡೆ, a stop: a road held shut.

The word, taken apart.
ರಸ್ತೆತಡೆ (rastetaḍe) is a Kannada compound of ರಸ್ತೆ, "a road", usually traced to Persian rāsta, and native ತಡೆ, "to stop, to block" (DEDR 3031).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ತೆರವುಗೊಳಿಸು (teravugoḷisu) — to clear away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ತೆರವುಗೊಳಿಸು.
ತೆರವುಗೊಳಿಸು — teravugoḷisu — "to clear away".
ಕೆಲಸಗಾರರು ರಸ್ತೆಯಿಂದ ಮರವನ್ನು ತೆರವುಗೊಳಿಸಿದರು. kelasagāraru rasteyinda maravannu teravugoḷisidaru. The workers cleared the tree from the road.
Past (I …): ತೆರವುಗೊಳಿಸಿದೆ, teravugoḷiside.
ತೆರವು is an opening; -ಗೊಳಿಸು makes it a verb: to make open.

The word, taken apart.
ತೆರವುಗೊಳಿಸು (teravugoḷisu) is native: teṟa, teṟavu, "an opening, a clearing" (DEDR 3259), with the Kannada verb-maker -goḷisu.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಅಡ್ಡಿಪಡಿಸು.
ಅಡ್ಡಿಪಡಿಸು — aḍḍipaḍisu — "to obstruct; to disrupt".
ಬಿದ್ದ ಮರ ಸಂಚಾರವನ್ನು ಅಡ್ಡಿಪಡಿಸಿತು. bidda mara sañcāravannu aḍḍipaḍisitu. The fallen tree held up the traffic.
Past (I …): ಅಡ್ಡಿಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, aḍḍipaḍiside.
ಅಡ್ಡಿ is a hindrance: ಅಡ್ಡಿ ಇಲ್ಲ, there is no objection.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಡ್ಡಿಪಡಿಸು (aḍḍipaḍisu) is native: DEDR 83 lists Kannada aḍḍi, "an obstacle, opposition, delay", here with the verb-maker -paḍisu.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಸ್ಥಗಿತಗೊಳ್ಳು (sthagitagoḷḷu) — to stall; to come to a standstill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to clear away, then the Kannada for to obstruct.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಥಗಿತಗೊಳ್ಳು.
ಸ್ಥಗಿತಗೊಳ್ಳು — sthagitagoḷḷu — "to stall; to come to a standstill".
ಮುಷ್ಕರದಿಂದ ನಗರದ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಸ್ಸುಗಳು ಸ್ಥಗಿತಗೊಂಡವು. muṣkaradinda nagarada ellā bassugaḷu sthagitagoṇḍavu. Because of the strike all the city buses came to a standstill.
Past (it …): ಸ್ಥಗಿತಗೊಂಡಿತು, sthagitagoṇḍitu.
Said of services, work and traffic that stop.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಥಗಿತಗೊಳ್ಳು (sthagitagoḷḷu) is built from Sanskrit sthagita, "covered, closed, shut" (Monier-Williams), and the Kannada verb-maker -goḷḷu: to become shut.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಸಂಚರಿಸು (sañcarisu) — to ply; to travel about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to obstruct, then the Kannada for to stall.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಚರಿಸು.
ಸಂಚರಿಸು — sañcarisu — "to ply; to travel about".
ಈ ಮಾರ್ಗದಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ರತಿ ಹತ್ತು ನಿಮಿಷಕ್ಕೆ ಬಸ್ ಸಂಚರಿಸುತ್ತದೆ. ī mārgadalli prati hattu nimiṣakke bas sañcarisuttade. A bus runs on this route every ten minutes.
Past (I …): ಸಂಚರಿಸಿದೆ, sañcariside.
Said of buses and trains that run a route, and of people who travel about.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಚರಿಸು (sañcarisu) (saṃcarisu) is built from Sanskrit sam-car, "to move about" (the noun saṃcāra, CDIAL 12869), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
