## You'll want to know: دعا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C322-ROZA322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دعا** — *duā* — "a prayer of asking; a blessing".

> دادی نے سب کے لیے دعا کی۔
>
> *dādī ne sab ke liye duā kī.*
>
> Grandmother prayed for everyone.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *duʿā*, "a calling upon": asking God for something. **نماز** is the set prayer; **دعا** is your own.
