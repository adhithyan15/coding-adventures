# ఉపసంహరించు (upasaṁhariñcu) — to withdraw; to take back

## You'll want to know: ఉపసంహరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C424-OTTIDI424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపసంహరించు** — *upasaṁhariñcu* — "to withdraw; to take back".

> అతను తన దరఖాస్తును ఉపసంహరించాడు.
>
> *atanu tana darakhāstunu upasaṁhariñcāḍu.*
>
> He withdrew his application.

Past (I …): ఉపసంహరించాను, *upasaṁhariñcānu*.

The noun is ఉపసంహరణ, a withdrawal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపసంహరించు** (*upasaṁhariñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *upasaṁhāra*, "the act of withdrawing, withholding" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
