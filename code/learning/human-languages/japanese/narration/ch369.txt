Japanese, chapter 369: What Matters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
じゅうよう (jūyō) — essential; important.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for one of a pair, then the Japanese for to be one-sided.

You'll want to know: じゅうよう.
じゅうよう — jūyō — "essential; important".
しけんが ちかく なったら、べんきょうが じゅうようでしょう。 — shiken ga chikaku nattara, benkyō ga jūyō deshō — "When the exam gets close, studying will be what matters."
A na-adjective: な before a noun.

The word, taken apart.
じゅうよう (jūyō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are jū, "important" (Middle Chinese drjowngX) and yō, "need" ('jiewH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
