## You'll want to know: पासा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C545-JUA545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पासा** — *pāsā* — "a die; dice".

> उसने पासा फेंका और छह आया।
>
> *Usne pāsā pheṅkā aur chah āyā.*
>
> He threw the die and got a six.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पासा** (*pāsā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *pāśa*, "a die, dice" (CDIAL 8132).
