Portuguese, chapter 65: Pretty, Ugly, Long, Short, Hot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bonito — pretty, handsome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for old, then the Portuguese for bad.

You'll want to know: bonito.
bonito — "pretty, handsome".

The word, taken apart.
bonito from Spanish bonito, "pretty" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. The Spanish word is a small form of the word for "good", from Latin bonus.
