# উনুন (unun) — a stove (of clay); an oven

## You'll want to know: উনুন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C512-UNUN512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উনুন** — *unun* — "a stove (of clay); an oven".

> দিদিমা উনুনে ভাত রান্না করেন।
>
> *Didimā unune bhāt rānnā kôren.*
>
> My grandmother cooks rice on the clay stove.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উনুন** (*unun*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *unun*, "oven, fireplace", under Sanskrit *uṣṇayati*, "heats" (CDIAL 2394).
