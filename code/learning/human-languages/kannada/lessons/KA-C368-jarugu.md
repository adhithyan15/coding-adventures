## You'll want to know: ಜರುಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C368-KRAMA368-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜರುಗು** — *jarugu* — "to take place; to shift".

> ಸಭೆ ಶಾಂತವಾಗಿ ಜರುಗಿತು.
>
> *sabhe śāntavāgi jarugitu.*
>
> The meeting took place peacefully.

Past (it …): ಜರುಗಿತು, *jarugitu*.

Events take place: ಸಮಾರಂಭ ಜರುಗಿತು. It also means to move aside, as ಸರಿ does.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜರುಗು** (*jarugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2360 lists Kannada *jarugu* beside Tamil *cari*, "to slip away, to slide, to give way".
