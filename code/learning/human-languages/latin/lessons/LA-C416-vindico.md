## You'll want to know: vindicō, vindicāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C416-IUSTITIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vindicō, vindicāre** — "to claim; to avenge, to punish".

> Iniūriam vindicāvit.
>
> *He avenged the wrong.*

*vindicō, vindicāre, vindicāvī, vindicātum*. In law, to claim a thing as one's own; *in lībertātem vindicāre*, to set free. English *vindicate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vindicō* is built on *vindex, vindicis* "a claimant, a protector, an avenger" (EtymDB); Lewis & Short explain it as *vim dīcō*, to assert force. English *vindicate* comes from it.
