## You'll want to know: تعداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C376-MIQDAR376-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعداد** — *tādād* — "a number (of people or things); a count".

> اگر تعداد زیادہ ہوئی تو کمرہ چھوٹا پڑے گا۔
>
> *agar tādād zyādā huī to kamrā chhoṭā paṛe gā.*
>
> If there are too many people, the room will be too small.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعداد** (*tādād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taʿdād*, "counting".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
