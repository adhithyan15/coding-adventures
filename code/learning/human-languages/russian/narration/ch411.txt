Russian, chapter 411: Giving someone a lift.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
попутчик (popútchik) — a fellow traveller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to knock down, then the Russian for a dent.

You'll want to know: попутчик.
попутчик (popútchik) — "a fellow traveller".
В поезде у меня был весёлый попутчик. V póyezde u menyá byl vesyólyy popútchik. I had a cheerful fellow traveller on the train.
Feminine попутчица. Built on по пути — on the way.

The word, taken apart.
попутчик (popútchik) is built on попутный 'going the same way', from по пути 'on the way'. Путь is inherited from Proto-Slavic pǫtĭ 'a path, a road' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
попутка (popútka) — a lift (in a passing car), a hitched ride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dent, then the Russian for a fellow traveller.

You'll want to know: попутка.
попутка (popútka) — "a lift (in a passing car), a hitched ride".
До деревни мы ехали на попутке. Do derévni my yékhali na popútke. We got to the village in a passing car.
Colloquial. Ловить попутку — to hitch a ride.

The word, taken apart.
попутка (popútka) is a colloquial short form of попутная машина 'a car going the same way', from по пути 'on the way'. Путь is inherited from Proto-Slavic pǫtĭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
подвозить (podvozít) — to give (someone) a lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fellow traveller, then the Russian for a lift.

You'll want to know: подвозить.
подвозить (podvozít) — "to give (someone) a lift".
Каждое утро сосед подвозит меня до работы. Kázhdoye útro soséd podvózit menyá do rabóty. Every morning the neighbour gives me a lift to work.
Imperfective; perfective подвезти (подвезу, подвезёшь). Present подвожу, подвозишь.

The word, taken apart.
подвозить (podvozít) is built from под- 'up to' and возить 'to carry by vehicle'. Возить is inherited from Proto-Slavic voziti 'to cart, to convey' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
высаживать (vysázhivat) — to drop off (a passenger); to plant out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a lift, then the Russian for to give.

You'll want to know: высаживать.
высаживать (vysázhivat) — "to drop off (a passenger); to plant out".
Такси высаживает нас у входа. Taksí vysázhivayet nas u vkhóda. The taxi drops us off at the entrance.
Imperfective; the perfective is высадить (высажу, высадишь). Also высаживать (vysázhivat) цветы — to plant out flowers.

The word, taken apart.
высаживать (vysázhivat) is built from вы- 'out' and -саживать, from садить 'to seat, to plant', the 'make sit' partner of сидеть 'to sit'. EtymDB lists садить without a deeper source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
пристёгиваться (pristyógivatsya) — to fasten one's seat belt, to buckle up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to give, then the Russian for to drop off.

You'll want to know: пристёгиваться.
пристёгиваться (pristyógivatsya) — "to fasten one's seat belt, to buckle up".
В машине мы всегда пристёгиваемся. V mashíne my vsegdá pristyógivayemsya. We always fasten our seat belts in the car.
Imperfective; perfective пристегнуться. Пристегнуть ремень — to fasten a belt.

The word, taken apart.
пристёгиваться (pristyógivatsya) is built from при- 'onto', -стёгивать, which goes with стегать 'to stitch, to lash', and -ся. EtymDB does not list the verb, so the deeper origin is left open.
