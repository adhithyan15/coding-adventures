## You'll want to know: こうかいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C432-WORRY432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こうかいする** — *kōkai suru* — "to regret".

> **あの ときの ことを こうかいしています。** — *ano toki no koto o kōkai shite imasu* — "I regret what happened that time."

Polite **こうかいします**, *kōkaishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かれは こうかいしました。**, *kare wa kōkai shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうかいする** (*kōkai suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *kōkai* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "later" (Middle Chinese *huwX*) and *kai*, "regret" (*xwojH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
