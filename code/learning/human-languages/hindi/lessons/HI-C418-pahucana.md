## You'll want to know: पहुँचाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C418-NAZDIK418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पहुँचाना** — *pahũcānā* — "to take (someone) to; to deliver".

> ड्राइवर ने हमें होटल तक पहुँचाया।
>
> *ḍrāivar ne hamẽ hoṭal tak pahũcāyā.*
>
> The driver took us all the way to the hotel.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चिट्ठी घर तक पहुँचाई, *usne ciṭṭhī ghar tak pahũcāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पहुँचाना** (*pahũcānā*) is the causative partner of पहुँचना, "to arrive", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit *prabhūta*, "much, great" (CDIAL 8716): to make someone or something arrive.
