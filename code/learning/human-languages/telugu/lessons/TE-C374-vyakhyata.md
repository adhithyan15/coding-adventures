## You'll want to know: వ్యాఖ్యాత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C374-PRASARAM374-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యాఖ్యాత** — *vyākhyāta* — "a commentator; a presenter".

> ఆ వ్యాఖ్యాత మాటలు అందరికీ నచ్చాయి.
>
> *ā vyākhyāta māṭalu andarikī naccāyi.*
>
> Everyone liked that commentator's words.

Plural: వ్యాఖ్యాతలు, *vyākhyātalu*.

The person behind a వ్యాఖ్యానం, a commentary, on radio or TV.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యాఖ్యాత** (*vyākhyāta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vyākhyātṛ*, "an explainer, a commentator" (Monier-Williams), from the same root as వ్యాఖ్యానం.
