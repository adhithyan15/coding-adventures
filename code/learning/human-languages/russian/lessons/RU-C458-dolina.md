## You'll want to know: долина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C458-DOLINA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**долина** (*dolína*) — "a valley".

> В долине течёт маленькая река.
>
> *V dolíne techyót málenkaya reká.*
>
> A small river flows through the valley.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*долина* is built on the old word *дол* 'a valley', still heard in *по горам, по долам* 'over hill and dale'. *Дол* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dolŭ* 'a dale, a valley' (EtymDB); EtymDB lists *долина* itself without a source.
