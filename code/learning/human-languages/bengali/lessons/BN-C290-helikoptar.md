## You'll want to know: হেলিকপ্টার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C290-DHEU290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**হেলিকপ্টার** — *helikôpṭār* — "a helicopter".

> সেনাবাহিনীর হেলিকপ্টার ছাদ থেকে মানুষ তুলল।
>
> *senābāhinīr helikôpṭār chhād theke mānush tullô.*
>
> Army helicopters lifted people off the rooftops.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হেলিকপ্টার** (*helikôpṭār*) is English *helicopter*, built in the nineteenth century from Greek words for 'spiral' and 'wing'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
