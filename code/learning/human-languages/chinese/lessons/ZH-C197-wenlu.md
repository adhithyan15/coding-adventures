## You'll want to know: 问路
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-01]; assesses=[] -->

**问路** — *wènlù* — "to ask the way".

> **他在问路** *tā zài wènlù* — He's asking the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**问路** (*wènlù*) is built from 问 'to ask' and 路 'road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjunH* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
