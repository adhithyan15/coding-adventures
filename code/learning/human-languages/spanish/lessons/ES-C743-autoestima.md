## You'll want to know: la autoestima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C743-MENTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la autoestima** — "self-esteem".

> Tiene la autoestima muy baja.
>
> *He has very low self-esteem.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*autoestima* is built on Greek *autós* "self" and *estima* "esteem", from *estimar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *aestimāre* "to value" (EtymDB).
