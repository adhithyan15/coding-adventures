Kannada, chapter 403: Society and Self-Reliance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಮಾಜ (samāja) — society.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to conceal, then the Kannada for to recommend.

You'll want to know: ಸಮಾಜ.
ಸಮಾಜ — samāja — "society".
ಸಮಾಜದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಗೌರವ ಸಿಗಬೇಕು. samājadalli ellarigū gaurava sigabēku. Everyone in society should be respected.
ಸಾಮಾಜಿಕ, social.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಾಜ (samāja) is borrowed from Sanskrit samāja, "a meeting, an assembly" (CDIAL 13207).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸ್ವಾವಲಂಬನೆ (svāvalambane) — self-reliance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to recommend, then the Kannada for society.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಾವಲಂಬನೆ.
ಸ್ವಾವಲಂಬನೆ — svāvalambane — "self-reliance".
ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಹೆಂಗಸರು ಹೊಲಿಗೆ ಕಲಿತು ಸ್ವಾವಲಂಬನೆ ಸಾಧಿಸಿದರು. haḷḷiya heṅgasaru holige kalitu svāvalambane sādhisidaru. The village women learned tailoring and became self-reliant.
ಸ್ವ (self) + ಅವಲಂಬನೆ (leaning on).

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ವಾವಲಂಬನೆ (svāvalambane) is built from Sanskrit sva, "self", and avalambana, "leaning on, depending upon" (Monier-Williams): depending on oneself.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for self-reliance, then the Kannada for awareness.

You'll want to know: ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ.
ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ — vaividhya — "diversity; variety".
ಈ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಭಾಷೆಗಳ ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ ಇದೆ. ī ūrinalli bhāṣegaḷa vaividhya ide. This town has a variety of languages.
ವೈವಿಧ್ಯಮಯ, varied.

The word, taken apart.
ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ (vaividhya) is built on Sanskrit vividha, "of various sorts, manifold" (Monier-Williams), with the vowel strengthening that makes an abstract noun.
