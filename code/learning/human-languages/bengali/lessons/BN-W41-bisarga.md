# ঃ (bisarga) — the two dots in দুঃখিত

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-NYA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C41-ANUSVAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ঞ and ং once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **দুঃখিত** (*dukkhito*), "sorry".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ঃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-BISARGA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঃ** — *bisarga*, two small circles one above the other.

It is inside **দুঃখিত** (*dukkhito*), "sorry". Bengali does not say the *bisarga* as
a sound of its own here: it doubles the consonant after it, so **দুঃখিত** is said
*dukkhito*.

## Writing: ঃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-BISARGA-01] -->

Follow the numbered strip: start where movement 1 begins, and let each panel
show you the next movement before your pen makes it. Then write ঃ yourself —
slowly, and larger than it is printed.

> The strip shows **where to start this letter and which way to travel**, in one
> attested order, and names its source beneath it. Teachers vary, so treat it as
> a sound way in, not the only one.
