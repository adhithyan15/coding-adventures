## You'll want to know: yǎngchéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C484-WELL484-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎngchéng* — "to form (a habit); to get into a habit".

> *wǒ yǎngchéng le zǎoshang pǎobù de xíguàn* — I've got into the habit of running in the morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎngchéng* is built from *yǎng* 'to raise' and 成 'to complete', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yangX* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rear' and the second 'to finish'.
