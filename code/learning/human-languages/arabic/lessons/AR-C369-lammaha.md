## You'll want to know: لمح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C369-NASIHA369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لمح** — *lammaḥa* — "to hint".

> لمح المدير إلى تغيير قريب.
>
> *lammaḥa al-mudīr ilā taghyīr qarīb.*
>
> The manager hinted at a change coming soon.

Present: يلمح, *yulammiḥ*.

It takes إلى: لمح إلى ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لمح** (*lammaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-m-ḥ*, "to glance" (Lane). It is Form II, to make someone catch a glance: to hint at a thing.
