## You'll want to know: spontan
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C373-UNVORHERGESEHEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**spontan** — "spontaneous, on the spur of the moment".

> Wir sind ganz spontan ans Meer gefahren.
>
> *We drove to the seaside on the spur of the moment.*

Very common for plans made at the last minute: *Ich bin da ganz spontan.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spontan* goes back to Latin *spontāneus* "of one's own accord" (EtymDB), from *sponte* "of one's free will" (Lewis & Short).
