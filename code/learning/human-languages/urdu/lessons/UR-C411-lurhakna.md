## You'll want to know: لڑھکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C411-DHALAN411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لڑھکنا** — *luṛhaknā* — "to roll (down)".

> ایک پتھر ڈھلان سے نیچے لڑھکا۔
>
> *ek patthar ḍhalān se nīche luṛhkā.*
>
> A stone rolled down the slope.

Past (it …): وہ گیند نیچے لڑھکی, *voh gend nīche luṛhkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لڑھکنا** (*luṛhaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *luṛhakṇā*, "to roll, to slip", under Sanskrit *luṭhati*, "rolls" (CDIAL 11079).
