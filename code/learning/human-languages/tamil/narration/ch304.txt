Tamil, chapter 304: Shaken and Shattered.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பதறு (pataṟu) — to get flustered.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for refuge, then the Tamil for to drive away.

You'll want to know: பதறு.
பதறு — pataṟu — "to get flustered".
சத்தம் கேட்டதும் எல்லோரும் பதறினார்கள். cattam kēṭṭatum ellōrum pataṟiṉārkaḷ. Everyone got flustered when they heard the noise.
Past (I …): பதறினேன், pataṟiṉēṉ.
பதறாதே, do not panic: a common way to calm someone.

The word, taken apart.
பதறு (pataṟu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3910): to be flurried, confused, overhasty.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to snatch, then the Tamil for to smash.

You'll want to know: உலுக்கு.
உலுக்கு — ulukku — "to shake up".
அந்தச் செய்தி ஊர் முழுவதையும் உலுக்கியது. antac ceyti ūr muḻuvataiyum ulukkiyatu. That news shook up the whole town.
Past (I …): உலுக்கினேன், ulukkiṉēṉ.
Also literally: மரத்தை உலுக்கு (ulukku), to shake a tree.

The word, taken apart.
உலுக்கு (ulukku) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1003): to shake, as a tree, or to tremble, as in an earthquake.
