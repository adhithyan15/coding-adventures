# અથવા (athavā) — or

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-JOIN-ATHAVA-01]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-ANE-01] -->

**અને** offers you both. **અથવા** offers you one of two:

> **ચા અથવા દૂધ** — *chā athavā dūdh* — **tea or milk**

It stands in the same place *ane* does, between the two things, and it asks the
listener to pick.

Inside it are two older words: Sanskrit **અથ** (*atha*, "then, moreover") and
**વા** (*vā*, "or"). Gujarati fused a linking word and a choosing word and kept
both.
