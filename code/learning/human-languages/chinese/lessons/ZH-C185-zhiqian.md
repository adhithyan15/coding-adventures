## You'll want to know: zhīqián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C185-WHEN185-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīqián* — "before (a time); before (an event)".

> *chī fàn zhīqián yào xǐ shǒu* — Wash your hands before you eat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīqián* is built from *zhī*, the old word that links a thing to what it belongs to, and 前 'before', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyi* and *dzen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to go to'; the linking sense is not given there.
