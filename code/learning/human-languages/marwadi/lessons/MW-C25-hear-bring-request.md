## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-BRING-REQUEST-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-LAVO-01, MW-LEX-CHAAY-01] -->

> *chāy lāvo* — **bring tea**

Two words. The food comes first and the asking word last, the same order you
already use in *ye kapṛā dikhāvo*, with the pointing word left out because you
are not choosing between cups in front of you.

Any of the seven food words fits the first slot. Say the line three times, with
*chāy*, then *roṭī*, then *dāl*, and notice that nothing else in it moves.
