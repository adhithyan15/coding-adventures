## You'll want to know: शिविर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C309-RAHAT309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शिविर** — *śivir* — "a camp".

> माफ़ कीजिए, राहत शिविर कैसे जाते हैं?
>
> *māf kījiye, rāhat śivir kaise jāte haiṁ?*
>
> Excuse me, how do I get to the relief camp?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिविर** (*śivir*) is Sanskrit *śibira* (also written *śivira*), "a royal camp, a tent" (Monier-Williams). Today it is a camp of any kind: **राहत शिविर**, a relief camp.
