Urdu, chapter 415: Street Food.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ڈھابا (ḍhābā) — a roadside eatery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to growl, then the Urdu for to chase off.

You'll want to know: ڈھابا.
ڈھابا — ḍhābā — "a roadside eatery".
شاہراہ کے کنارے ایک ڈھابا ہے۔ shāhrāh ke kināre ek ḍhābā hai. There's a roadside eatery beside the highway.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ڈھابا (ḍhābā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi ḍhābā, "thatched roofing", under ḍhapp, "cover", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5579); the sense "a roadside eatery", a place under a thatch, is probably a later one.
