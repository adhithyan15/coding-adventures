## You'll want to know: la surréservation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C421-ANNULATION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la surréservation** — "the overbooking".

> À cause de la surréservation, deux passagers n'ont pas pu partir.
>
> *Because of the overbooking, two passengers could not leave.*

Many people say *le surbooking*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*surréservation* is built from *sur-* 'over' and *réservation*, from *réserver* 'to keep aside', a learned borrowing from Latin *reservāre* (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
