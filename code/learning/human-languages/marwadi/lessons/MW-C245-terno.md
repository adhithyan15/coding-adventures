## You'll want to know: तेड़णो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C245-TEDNO245-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तेड़णो** — *teṛṇo* — "to call someone over; to invite".

> मां पावणा तेड़ै है।
>
> *mā̃ pāvṇā teṛai hai.*
>
> Mother invites guests over.

Past (I …): म्हैं पावणा तेड़्या, *mhaĩ pāvṇā teṛyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तेड़णो** (*teṛṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*tēḍati*, "summons"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari noun *teṛū̃*, "a summons", under it (CDIAL 5947a).
