Italian, chapter 333: Sul fuoco.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bollire — to boil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to peel, then the Italian for to grate.

You'll want to know: bollire.
bollire — "to boil".
Quando l'acqua bolle, butta la pasta. When the water boils, put the pasta in.
Present bollo, bolli, bolle.

The word, taken apart.
bollire is inherited from Latin bullīre "to bubble, to boil" (EtymDB), from bulla "a bubble".

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
lessare — to cook in boiling water, to boil (food).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to grate, then the Italian for to boil.

You'll want to know: lessare.
lessare — "to cook in boiling water, to boil (food)".
Ho lessato le patate per venti minuti. I boiled the potatoes for twenty minutes.
La carne lessa or il lesso — boiled meat.

The word, taken apart.
lessare is inherited from Late Latin ēlixāre "to boil" (EtymDB), built on lixa "hot water".

scolare — "to drain".
Scola la pasta al dente. Drain the pasta when it is al dente.
For pasta and vegetables: scolare la pasta.

The word, taken apart.
scolare is inherited from Vulgar Latin excōlāre (EtymDB), built on Latin cōlāre "to filter".

il tegame — "the shallow pan, the casserole".
Cuoci le uova nel tegame. Cook the eggs in the shallow pan.
A wide, low pan with two handles.
