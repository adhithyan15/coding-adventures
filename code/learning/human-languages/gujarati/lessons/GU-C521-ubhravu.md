## You'll want to know: ઊભરાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C521-VALONU521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊભરાવું** — *ūbhrāvũ* — "to boil over".

> દૂધ ઊભરાઈ ગયું અને ચૂલો ગંદો થઈ ગયો.
>
> *dūdh ūbhrāī gayũ ane cūlo gando thaī gayo.*
>
> The milk boiled over and the stove got dirty.

Past (it …): તે દૂધ ઊભરાઈ ગયું, *te dūdh ūbhrāī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊભરાવું** (*ūbhrāvũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *údbharati*, "raises", with the Gujarati verb listed as *ubhrāvũ*, "to effervesce" (CDIAL 2038).
