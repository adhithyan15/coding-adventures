## You'll want to know: стрелка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C438-UKAZATEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**стрелка** (*strélka*) — "an arrow (on a sign); a hand (of a clock)".

> Стрелка на знаке показывает дорогу налево.
>
> *Strélka na znáke pokázyvayet dorógu nalévo.*
>
> The arrow on the sign shows the way to the left.

Feminine. *Часовая стрелка* — the hour hand; *по часовой стрелке* — clockwise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стрелка* is a diminutive of *стрела* 'an arrow', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *strěla* 'an arrow' (EtymDB). EtymDB lists *стрелка* itself without a source.
