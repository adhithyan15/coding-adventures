5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گلوکار (gulūkār) — a singer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a farmer, then the Urdu for a tailor.

You'll want to know: گلوکار.
گلوکار — gulūkār — "a singer".

The word, taken apart.
گلوکار (gulūkār) joins Persian gulū, "throat", and -kār, "one who works": a singer works with the throat.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ملاح (mallāh) — a sailor, a boatman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a singer, then the Urdu for a pilot.

You'll want to know: ملاح.
ملاح — mallāh — "a sailor, a boatman".

The word, taken apart.
ملاح (mallāh) is a loan from Arabic mallāḥ, "a sailor", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is m-l-ḥ (Lane). It is built on the same root as milḥ, "salt".

The word, taken apart.
مچھیرا (machherā) is "one who works with fish". It is built on machh, "fish", which CDIAL lists, as Hindi māch, under Sanskrit matsya, "fish" (CDIAL 9758).

Lesson 5 of 5.
مالی (mālī) — a gardener.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sailor, then the Urdu for a fisherman.

You'll want to know: مالی.
مالی — mālī — "a gardener".

The word, taken apart.
مالی (mālī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mālin, "garlanded; gardener" (CDIAL 10094). It is built on mālā, "garland".
