## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-LEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*lèi* — **fourth tone**, a short clean fall. Its character comes later; this
chapter keeps it to the ear.

## Grammar Lens: a statement, then 吗
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-LEI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-MA-QUESTION-01, ZH-LEX-HEN-01] -->

*lèi* is an adjective too. Ask with 吗 on the end, and answer with 很:

> *nǐ lèi ma* — Are you tired?
> *wǒ hěn lèi* — I'm tired.

The question is the statement with 吗 added; nothing moves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The character for *lèi* is in the Guangyun, read *ljweX* and *ljweH* (Baxter's
transcription), but glossed "accumulate" and "implicate". "Tired" is not among its
senses there, so how the word came to mean it is not settled.
