Gujarati, chapter 213: Tomorrow's weather.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હવામાન (havāmān) — weather, climate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to believe, then the Gujarati for to change.

You'll want to know: હવામાન.
હવામાન — havāmān — "weather, climate".
કાલે હવામાન સારું હશે. — kāle havāmān sārũ haśe — "The weather will be good tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
હવામાન (havāmān) is built from two older words: havā, "air", a loan from Arabic hawā', which reached Gujarati through Persian (Lane, under the root h-w-y), and mān, "measure", from Sanskrit māna. CDIAL has no entry for the compound.

Lesson 2 of 5.
તડકો (taḍko) — sunshine, the sun's heat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to change, then the Gujarati for weather.

You'll want to know: તડકો.
તડકો — taḍko — "sunshine, the sun's heat".
આવતીકાલે તડકો હશે. — āvtīkāle taḍko haśe — "It will be sunny tomorrow."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of તડકો (taḍko) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it; its nearest entry, Sanskrit tāpa, "heat", gives Old Gujarati tāvaḍu, "heat of the sun", but no link to taḍko is made there (CDIAL 5767).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઠંડું (ṭhanḍũ) — cold, cool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for weather, then the Gujarati for sunshine.

You'll want to know: ઠંડું.
ઠંડું — ṭhanḍũ — "cold, cool".
રાત્રે પાણી ઠંડું હશે. — rātre pāṇī ṭhanḍũ haśe — "The water will be cold at night."

The word, taken apart.
ઠંડું (ṭhanḍũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. CDIAL lists it, glossed "cold", under stabdha, "firmly fixed; stiff; solidified (as water)", by way of a reshaped ṭhaṇḍha-, so cold is what has set hard (CDIAL 13676).

Lesson 4 of 5.
વાદળછાયું (vādaḷchāyũ) — cloudy, overcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for sunshine, then the Gujarati for cold.

You'll want to know: વાદળછાયું.
વાદળછાયું — vādaḷchāyũ — "cloudy, overcast".
કાલે આકાશ વાદળછાયું હશે. — kāle ākāś vādaḷchāyũ haśe — "The sky will be cloudy tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
વાદળછાયું (vādaḷchāyũ) is built from vādaḷ, "cloud", inherited from Sanskrit vārdala, "rainy day" (CDIAL 11567), and chāyũ, "covered", from the verb chāvũ, "to cover", which CDIAL lists, glossed "to thatch", under Sanskrit chādayati, "covers, veils" (CDIAL 5018).
