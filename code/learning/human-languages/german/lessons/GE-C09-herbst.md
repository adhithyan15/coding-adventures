# der Herbst — "autumn"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-SOMMER-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The third season, and the only one whose name you will not recognise
at first hearing.

## You'll want to know: der Herbst
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-HERBST-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-JAHRESZEIT-01] -->

> **der Herbst** — "autumn," or "fall."

It is the **reaping** season: the stretch of the year when the crop comes in.
That meaning is the whole of the word, and the next lesson is about where else
you already have it.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-HERBST-01] -->

- `herz-short-e` — a short, open *e*, the vowel of English *bed*, with the
  German *r* colouring it from behind.
- `st-initial-scht` — the *-st* here is at the **end**, so it stays a plain
  *st*, exactly as it did inside *August*.

*hairpst*, one syllable, no vowel between the *b* and the *st*.
