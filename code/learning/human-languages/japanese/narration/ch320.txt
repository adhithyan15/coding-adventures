Japanese, chapter 320: Seeing Both Sides.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
たちば (tachiba) — a position; a point of view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to misunderstand, then the Japanese for in earnest.

You'll want to know: たちば.
たちば — tachiba — "a position; a point of view".
せんせいの たちばも かんがえた ほうが いいです。 — sensei no tachiba mo kangaeta hō ga ii desu — "You should think about the teacher's position too."

The word, taken apart.
たちば (tachiba) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from tachi, "standing (tatsu)" and ba, "place", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.

ぎもん — gimon — "a doubt; a question in one's mind".
ぎもんが あるので、しつもんします。 — gimon ga aru node, shitsumon shimasu — "I have a doubt, so I will ask a question."

The word, taken apart.
ぎもん (gimon) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are gi, "doubt" (Middle Chinese ngi) and mon, "question" (mjunH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 5.
しゅちょうする (shuchō suru) — to insist; to claim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a point of view, then the Japanese for a doubt.

You'll want to know: しゅちょうする.
しゅちょうする — shuchō suru — "to insist; to claim".
ただしいと おもったから、つよく しゅちょうしました。 — tadashii to omotta kara, tsuyoku shuchō shimashita — "He thought he was right, so he insisted strongly."
Polite しゅちょうします, shuchōshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): おとうとが しゅちょうしました。, otōto ga shuchō shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しゅちょうする (shuchō suru) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb suru, "to do". The noun, shuchō, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: shu, "main thing" (Middle Chinese tsyuX) and chō, "stretch" (trjang) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
