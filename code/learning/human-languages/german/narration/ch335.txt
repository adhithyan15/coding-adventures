German, chapter 335: Die Diskussion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Diskussion — the discussion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to admit, then the German for to concede.

You'll want to know: die Diskussion.
die Diskussion — "the discussion".
Nach dem Film gab es eine lange Diskussion. After the film there was a long discussion.
Plural: die Diskussionen. It goes with the verb diskutieren.

The word, taken apart.
Diskussion was borrowed from Latin discussiō (EtymDB), from discutere "to strike apart, to scatter", which later came to mean "to examine" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Debatte — the debate.

You'll want to know: die Debatte.
die Debatte — "the debate".
Im Parlament gibt es heute eine Debatte über die Schulen. Today there is a debate in parliament about schools.
Plural: die Debatten. More formal and more public than die Diskussion.

The word, taken apart.
Debatte was borrowed from French; EtymDB links it to débattre, Old French debatre "to fight, to contend". That verb is built on battre "to beat", from Latin battuere "to beat" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Vorschlag — the suggestion, the proposal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the discussion, then the German for the debate.

You'll want to know: der Vorschlag.
der Vorschlag — "the suggestion, the proposal".
Ich habe einen Vorschlag: Wir essen heute draußen. I have a suggestion: we eat outside today.
Plural: die Vorschläge. Einen Vorschlag machen — to make a suggestion.

The word, taken apart.
Vorschlag is the noun of vorschlagen, built from vor "before, forward" and schlagen "to strike": you put an idea forward. EtymDB has no entry for the compound, so this is the plain reading of its parts.

Lesson 4 of 5.
vorschlagen — to suggest, to propose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the debate, then the German for the suggestion.

You'll want to know: vorschlagen.
vorschlagen — "to suggest, to propose".
Mein Freund schlägt vor, dass wir zusammen kochen. My friend suggests that we cook together.
Separable and strong: er schlägt vor, er hat vorgeschlagen.

The word, taken apart.
vorschlagen is built from vor "forward" and schlagen "to strike", which is usually traced to Proto-Germanic slahaną. The sense of putting an idea forward grew out of the parts; EtymDB has no entry for the compound.

Lesson 5 of 5.
betonen — to stress, to emphasize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the suggestion, then the German for to suggest.

You'll want to know: betonen.
betonen — "to stress, to emphasize".
Die Lehrerin betont, dass die Prüfung sehr wichtig ist. The teacher stresses that the exam is very important.
Inseparable: sie betont, sie hat betont. First of sounds: die erste Silbe betonen — to stress the first syllable.

The word, taken apart.
betonen is built on Ton "tone, accent", which came through Latin tonus "a sound, a tone" (Lewis & Short) from Greek tónos "a stretching, a tone": you give a word more tone.
