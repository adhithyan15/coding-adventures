# ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿಸು (apēkṣisu) — to look to; to expect

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for overall, then the Kannada for to hope for.

## You'll want to know: ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C429-ASHISU429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿಸು** — *apēkṣisu* — "to look to; to expect".

> ಅವನು ಯಾರಿಂದಲೂ ಸಹಾಯ ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *avanu yārindalū sahāya apēkṣisalilla.*
>
> He expected help from no one.

Past (I …): ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿಸಿದೆ, *apēkṣiside*.

ಅಪೇಕ್ಷೆ, a wish or an expectation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿಸು** (*apēkṣisu*) is built on Sanskrit *apekṣā*, "looking for, expectation, need" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
