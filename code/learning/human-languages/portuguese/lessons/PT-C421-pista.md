## You'll want to know: a pista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C421-AEROPORTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a pista** — "the runway; the track; the clue".

> O avião está parado na pista.
>
> *The plane is standing on the runway.*

*A pista de dança* — the dance floor. *Uma pista* in a mystery is a clue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pista* was borrowed from Italian *pista* 'beaten track', which is usually linked with Latin *pinsere* 'to pound' (Lewis & Short), but that is not settled.
