Russian, chapter 359: Duties at work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обязанность (obyázannost) — a duty, a responsibility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to hire, then the Russian for length of service.

You'll want to know: обязанность.
обязанность (obyázannost) — "a duty, a responsibility".
В мои обязанности входит работа с клиентами. V moí obyázannosti vkhódit rabóta s kliyéntami. My duties include working with clients.
Feminine. Входить в обязанности — to be part of one’s duties.

The word, taken apart.
обязанность (obyázannost) is built on обязать "to oblige", made of о- and вязать "to tie, to bind", from Proto-Slavic vęzati: a duty is what binds you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
поручать (poruchát) — to entrust, to put in charge of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for length of service, then the Russian for a duty.

You'll want to know: поручать.
поручать (poruchát) — "to entrust, to put in charge of".
Ему часто поручают самые трудные задачи. Yemú chásto porucháyut sámyye trúdnyye zadáchi. He is often given the hardest tasks.
Imperfective; perfective поручить (поручу, поручишь). Поручать кому что, or + infinitive.

The word, taken apart.
поручать (poruchát) "to commission, to entrust" is built on ручать "to vouch, to guarantee", from рука "hand" (EtymDB). To entrust a task is to put it into someone’s hands.

Lesson 4 of 5.
выполнять (vypolnyát) — to carry out, to fulfil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to entrust, then the Russian for an assignment.

You'll want to know: выполнять.
выполнять (vypolnyát) — "to carry out, to fulfil".
Он всегда выполняет свои обещания. On vsegdá vypolnyáyet svoí obeshchániya. He always keeps his promises.
Imperfective; perfective выполнить. Выполнять работу, план, просьбу.

The word, taken apart.
выполнять (vypolnyát) is built from вы- "out" and the root of полный "full", inherited from Proto-Slavic pĭlnŭ (EtymDB): to fill a task out to the end.

Lesson 5 of 5.
справляться (spravlyátsya) — to cope, to manage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an assignment, then the Russian for to carry out.

You'll want to know: справляться.
справляться (spravlyátsya) — "to cope, to manage".
Я не справляюсь с работой один. Ya ne spravlyáyus s rabótoy odín. I cannot cope with the work on my own.
Imperfective; perfective справиться (справлюсь, справишься). It takes с + instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
справляться (spravlyátsya) is built on править "to set straight, to steer", from правый "right", inherited from Proto-Slavic pravŭ "right, correct" (EtymDB). To cope is to set a thing straight.
