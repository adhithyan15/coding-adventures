## You'll want to know: தேர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C334-THER334-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தேர்** — *tēr* — "a chariot; a temple car".

> திருவிழாவில் பெரிய தேர் தெருவில் வந்தது.
>
> *tiruviḻāvil periya tēr teruvil vantatu.*
>
> At the festival a big chariot came down the street.

Today mostly a temple car pulled through the streets at festivals.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தேர்** (*tēr*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3459): car, chariot, vehicle.
