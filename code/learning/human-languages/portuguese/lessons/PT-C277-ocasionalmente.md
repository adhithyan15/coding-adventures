## You'll want to know: ocasionalmente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C277-FREQUENCIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ocasionalmente** — "occasionally, now and then".

> Ocasionalmente, jantamos fora.
>
> *Now and then we eat out.*

Close to *de vez em quando*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ocasionalmente* is built on *ocasião* "occasion", from Latin *occāsiō* "a fitting moment, a chance" (Lewis & Short), from *occidere* "to fall", built on *cadere*.
