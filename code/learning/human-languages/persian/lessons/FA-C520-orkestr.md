## You'll want to know: ارکستر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C520-MUSICIANS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ارکستر** (*orkestr*) — "an orchestra".

> ارکستر شهر هر ماه یک کنسرت دارد.
>
> *orkestr-e shahr har mâh yek konsert dârad.*
>
> The city orchestra gives a concert every month.

*rahbar-e orkestr* — a conductor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orkestr* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *orchestre*. EtymDB lists it without a source.
