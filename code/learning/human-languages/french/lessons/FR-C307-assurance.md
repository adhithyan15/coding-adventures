## You'll want to know: l'assurance
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C307-DEGATS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'assurance** — "the insurance; self-confidence".

> L'assurance va tout payer.
>
> *The insurance is going to pay for everything.*

*Avoir de l'assurance* — to be self-assured.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assurance* is built on *assurer* "to make safe", which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *assēcūrāre*, built on Latin *sēcūrus* "safe, free from care" (Lewis & Short).
