Urdu, chapter 266: What Was Going On.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
پڑھ رہا تھا (paṛh rahā thā) — what was going on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I used to read". (maiṅ paṛhtā thā.) That is a habit. This lesson is one moment in the past, with something still going on.

Grammar Lens: was doing.
سو رہی تھی۔ so rahī thī. — "She was sleeping."
Ask what Sara was doing last night, and the answer is three words: the stem سو (so), then رہی (rahī), then تھی (thī). Both follow Sara:
سارا سو رہی تھی۔ Sārā so rahī thī. — "Sara was sleeping."
میں خط لکھ رہا تھا۔ maiṅ khat likh rahā thā. — "I was writing a letter."
A verb with an object takes no نے (ne) here: the writing was still going on.
ہم کھانا کھا رہے تھے۔ ham khānā khā rahe the. — "We were eating."
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
who: a man. was doing: رہا تھا (rahā thā).
who: a woman. was doing: رہی تھی (rahī thī).
who: men; ہم; آپ. was doing: رہے تھے (rahe the).
who: women. was doing: رہی تھیں (rahī thīṅ).

You'll want to know: the scene and the event.
باہر بارش ہو رہی تھی۔ bāhar bārish ho rahī thī. — "It was raining outside."
That is a scene: going on, with no edge. Drop an event into it with جب (jab), "when", and تو (to), "then":
جب میں گھر آیا، jab maiṅ ghar āyā, تو سارا سو رہی تھی۔ to Sārā so rahī thī. — "When I came home, Sara was sleeping."
This تو is to, "then", not تو tū, "you": the romanization tells them apart. آیا (āyā) is the event, over in a moment. سو رہی تھی is the scene it cut into.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I was reading" — maiṅ paṛh rahā thā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "She was reading" — voh paṛh rahī thī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "We were reading" — ham paṛh rahe the]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Say that it was raining outside, then that Sara was sleeping when you came home.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"She was writing a letter"? (وہ خط لکھ رہی تھی.) Does it take نے? (No: it was still going on.) In the sentence about coming home, which verb is the event? (آیا.) And which is the scene? (سو رہی تھی.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
دوران (daurān) — during.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "it was raining". (bārish ho rahī thī.) Then "a journey". (safar.)

You'll want to know: دوران.
دوران — daurān — "during".
سفر کے دوران بارش ہوئی۔ safar ke daurān bārish huī. — "It rained during the journey."
It stands after its noun, with کے (ke) in front of it, as in کے لیے (ke liye).
کھانے کے دوران وہ آیا۔ khāne ke daurān voh āyā. — "He came during the meal."
کھانا (khānā) becomes کھانے (khāne) before کے, as most masculine nouns in -ا do.

The word, taken apart.
دوران (daurān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic dawarān, "a turning round".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: daurān, then safar ke daurān]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "During the meal she came" — khāne ke daurān voh āī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "During the journey I was reading" — safar ke daurān maiṅ paṛh rahā thā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does دوران (daurān) mean? ("During".) "During the journey"? (سفر کے دوران (daurān).) What comes in front of it? (کے.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — what was going on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "Sara wrote a letter" and "I will go". (Sārā ne khat likhā, maiṅ jāūṅ gā.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I was writing a letter. Sara was reading.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It was raining outside.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: When I came home, Sara was sleeping.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: During the journey it was raining.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: He came during the meal.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"We were eating"? (ہم کھا رہے تھے.) Now the other way round: what does رہی تھیں tell you? (Women, and something going on.) In جب … تو …, which half is the event? (The جب half, in the plain past.) "During the journey"? (سفر کے دوران (daurān).)
