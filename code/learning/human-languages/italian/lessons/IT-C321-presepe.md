## You'll want to know: il presepe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C321-PRESEPE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il presepe** — "the nativity scene, the crib".

> Ogni anno facciamo il presepe in salotto.
>
> *Every year we set up the nativity scene in the living room.*

Also *il presepio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*presepe* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praesaepe* "a stall, a manger" (EtymDB).
