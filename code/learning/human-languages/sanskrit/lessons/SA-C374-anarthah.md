## You'll want to know: अनर्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C374-PRAMADA374-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनर्थः** — *anarthaḥ* — "a mishap; a bad turn".

> यदि चालकः श्रान्तः, तर्हि अनर्थः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi cālakaḥ śrāntaḥ, tarhi anarthaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the driver is tired, there will be a mishap.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनर्थः** (*anarthaḥ*) is built from *an-*, "not", and *artha*, "aim, use"; MW gives *an-artha* "a worthless object" and "a disappointing occurrence, a reverse, an evil" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
