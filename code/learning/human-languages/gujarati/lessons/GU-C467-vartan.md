## You'll want to know: વર્તન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C467-VARTAN467-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વર્તન** — *vartan* — "behaviour".

> મહેમાનો સાથે તમારું વર્તન સારું હતું.
>
> *mahemāno sāthe tamārũ vartan sārũ hatũ.*
>
> You behaved well with the guests.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વર્તન** (*vartan*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vartana*, which Monier-Williams gives as "abiding" and "setting in motion"; Gujarati uses it for the way a person conducts himself.
