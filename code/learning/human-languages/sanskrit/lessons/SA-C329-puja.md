## You'll want to know: पूजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C329-PUJA329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पूजा** — *pūjā* — "worship".

> प्रातः मन्दिरे पूजा भवति।
>
> *prātaḥ mandire pūjā bhavati.*
>
> There is worship in the temple in the morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूजा** (*pūjā*) comes from the root √pūj, "to honour, worship", as in *pūjayati*; MW gives *pūjā* as honour, worship, respect, reverence (MW).
