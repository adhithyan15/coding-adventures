## You'll want to know: tuìxiū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C276-NEWJOB276-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìxiū* — "to retire".

> *wǒ yéye tuìxiū le* — My grandpa has retired.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìxiū* is built from *tuì* 'to step back' and *xiū* 'to rest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thwojH* and *xjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to step back' and the second 'to rest'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
