## You'll want to know: pernoctō, pernoctāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C445-DEVERTICULUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pernoctō, pernoctāre** — "to spend the night, to stay all night".

> Viātōrēs in stabulō pernoctant, quod caupōna plēna est.
>
> *The travellers spend the night in the stable, because the inn is full.*

*pernoctō, pernoctāre, pernoctāvī, pernoctātum*. From *per* 'through' and *nox* 'night'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pernoctō* is built from *per* 'through' and *nox, noctis* 'night' (Lewis & Short): to stay through the night.
