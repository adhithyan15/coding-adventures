## You'll want to know: شکنجہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C496-VARKSHAP496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شکنجہ** — *shikanjā* — "a clamp; a vice".

> شکنجہ میز کے کونے پر ہے۔
>
> *shikanjā mez ke kone par hai.*
>
> The clamp is on the corner of the table.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شکنجہ** (*shikanjā*) is usually traced to Persian *shikanja*, "a press, a clamp".
