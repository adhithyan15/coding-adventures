## You'll want to know: اعتصاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C384-ETESAB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتصاب** (*e'tesâb*) — "a strike".

> کارگرها برای حقوق بیشتر اعتصاب کردند.
>
> *kârgarhâ barâye hoquq-e bishtar e'tesâb kardand.*
>
> The workers went on strike for higher pay.

*E'tesâb kardan* — to go on strike. Plural *e'tesâbât*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتصاب** was borrowed from Arabic *i'tiṣāb* (EtymDB), a verbal noun usually explained as banding together; in modern Persian it is a strike.
