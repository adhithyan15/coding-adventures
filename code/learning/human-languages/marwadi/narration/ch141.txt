Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 141: Good, Bad, Narrow, Wide, Deep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चोखो (chokho) — good, fine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an ant, then the Marwadi for a scorpion.

You'll want to know: चोखो.
चोखो — chokho — "good, fine".

The word, taken apart.
चोखो (chokho) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cokṣa, "pure, clean". CDIAL lists Old Marwari cokha under it (CDIAL 4918). The Old Marwari row means "well".

Lesson 2 of 5.
खराब (kharāb) — bad, spoiled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a scorpion, then the Marwadi for good.

You'll want to know: खराब.
खराब — kharāb — "bad, spoiled".

The word, taken apart.
खराब (kharāb) is a loan from Arabic ḫarāb, "ruin, desolation", which reached Marwadi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḫ-r-b (Lane).

सांकड़ो (sā̃kṛo) — narrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for good, then the Marwadi for bad.

You'll want to know: सांकड़ो.
सांकड़ो — sā̃kṛo — "narrow".

The word, taken apart.
सांकड़ो (sā̃kṛo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from saṃkaṭa, "contracted, narrow". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 12817); the match is through Gujarati sā̃kṛũ and Hindi sā̃kar. CDIAL thinks the Sanskrit word probably comes from saṃkṛta, "contracted".

Lesson 4 of 5.
चौड़ो (chauṛo) — wide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for bad, then the Marwadi for narrow.

You'll want to know: चौड़ो.
चौड़ो — chauṛo — "wide".

The word, taken apart.
चौड़ो (chauṛo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ca-uḍa, "wide, open", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari cauḍaï under it (CDIAL 4534). The Old Marwari row means "in the open"; CDIAL thinks the word may be built on caü-, "four", from compounds of catur.

गैरो (gairo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from gabhīra, "deep". CDIAL lists Old Marwari gaharau under it (CDIAL 4024). Marwadi has lost the h that the Old Marwari form kept.
