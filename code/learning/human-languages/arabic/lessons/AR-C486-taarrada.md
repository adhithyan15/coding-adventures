## You'll want to know: تعرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C486-TAQALLUB486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تعرض** — *taʿarraḍa* — "to be exposed (to); to suffer".

> تعرض المسافرون للمطر.
>
> *taʿarraḍa al-musāfirūn li-l-maṭar.*
>
> The travellers were caught in the rain.

Present: يتعرض, *yataʿarraḍ*.

It takes *li-*: *taʿarraḍa li-*, "to be exposed to".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعرض** (*taʿarraḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-ḍ*, "to show, to present" (Lane). It is Form V, "to put oneself in the way of".
