## You'll want to know: ختم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C426-JAMARIK426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ختم** — *khatama* — "to stamp; to seal".

> ختم الموظف جوازي عند الوصول.
>
> *khatama al-muwaẓẓaf jawāzī ʿinda al-wuṣūl.*
>
> The officer stamped my passport on arrival.

Present: يختم, *yakhtim*.

The noun *khatm* is a stamp; *khatama* also means to finish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ختم** (*khatama*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-t-m*, "to seal" (Lane). It is the root of *khātam*, "a ring", once a seal ring.
