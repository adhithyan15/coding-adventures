## You'll want to know: लपाछपी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C532-BAITHE532-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लपाछपी** — *lapāchpī* — "hide and seek".

> सुट्टीत मुलं अंगणात लपाछपी खेळली.
>
> *suṭṭīt mulaṁ aṅgaṇāt lapāchpī kheḷlī.*
>
> In the holidays the children played hide and seek in the yard.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लपाछपी** (*lapāchpī*) is a rhyming Marathi compound built on लपणे, "to hide"; the origin of the second half is not settled.
