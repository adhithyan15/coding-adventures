## You'll want to know: der Albtraum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C486-MUEDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Albtraum** — "nightmare".

> Nach dem Horrorfilm hatte ich einen Albtraum.
>
> *After the horror film I had a nightmare.*

Plural: *die Albträume*. Also spelt *der Alptraum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Albtraum* is built on *Alb* "elf, night spirit", inherited from Middle High German *alb* and Old High German *alp*, from Proto-Germanic *albiz* (EtymDB), and *Traum* "dream".
