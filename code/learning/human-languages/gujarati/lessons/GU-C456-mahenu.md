## You'll want to know: મહેણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C456-DUBHA456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેણું** — *maheṇũ* — "a taunt".

> પડોશીએ તેને મહેણું માર્યું.
>
> *paḍośīe tene maheṇũ māryũ.*
>
> The neighbour taunted her.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેણું** (*maheṇũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*mahākathana-*, "big words"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "taunt, bitter remark" (CDIAL 9938).
