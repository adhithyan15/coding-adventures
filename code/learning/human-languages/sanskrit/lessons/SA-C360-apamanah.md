## You'll want to know: अपमानः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C360-YASHAS360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अपमानः** — *apamānaḥ* — "an insult; dishonour".

> सः अपमानं सहते, किन्तु न विस्मरति।
>
> *saḥ apamānaṃ sahate, kintu na vismarati.*
>
> He bears an insult but does not forget it.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपमानः** (*apamānaḥ*) is built on *apa*, "away", and *māna*, "honour", from the root √man; MW gives *apa-māna* "disrespect, contempt, disgrace" (MW).
