## You'll want to know: 年假
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C263-LEAVE263-05]; assesses=[] -->

**年假** — *niánjià* — "annual leave".

> **我今年的年假有十天** *wǒ jīnnián de niánjià yǒu shí tiān* — I get ten days' leave this year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**年假** (*niánjià*) is built from 年 'a year' and 假 'leave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nen* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
