## You'll want to know: कपर्दकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C435-DINARA435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कपर्दकः** — *kapardakaḥ* — "a cowrie shell (as money)".

> पूर्वं जनाः कपर्दकैः धान्यं क्रीणन्ति स्म।
>
> *pūrvaṃ janāḥ kapardakaiḥ dhānyaṃ krīṇanti sma.*
>
> Long ago people used to buy grain with cowrie shells.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कपर्दकः** (*kapardakaḥ*) is built on *kaparda*, "a cowrie shell", with the ending *-ka*; MW gives *kapardaka* as the cowrie shell, used as a small coin, but no derivation for *kaparda*, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
