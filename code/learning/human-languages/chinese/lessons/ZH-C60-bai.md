## You'll want to know: 白
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C60-COLOR60-01]; assesses=[] -->

**白** — *bái* — "white; pale".

> **他的手很白** *tā de shǒu hěn bái* — His hands are very pale.

The new character, in pieces: 白 — a short fall on top of 日. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**白** (*bái*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *baek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the colour of the west', the direction that the old scheme of five colours paired with white.
