## You'll want to know: दिनचर्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C552-PRAYASA552-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दिनचर्या** — *dinacaryā* — "a daily routine".

> यदि दिनचर्या उत्तमा अस्ति, तर्हि स्वास्थ्यम् अपि उत्तमं भवति।
>
> *yadi dinacaryā uttamā asti, tarhi svāsthyam api uttamaṃ bhavati.*
>
> If your daily routine is good, your health is good too.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिनचर्या** (*dinacaryā*) is built from *dina*, "a day", and *caryā*, "going about, conduct", from the root √car, "to move"; MW gives *dinacaryā* as daily work (MW).
