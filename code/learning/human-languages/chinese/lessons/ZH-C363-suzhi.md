## You'll want to know: sùzhì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C363-PUBLIC363-05]; assesses=[] -->

*sùzhì* — "a person's quality; upbringing".

> *wǒ juéde zhège rén sùzhì hěn gāo* — I think he's a very well brought-up person.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sùzhì* is built from *sù* and *zhì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *suH* and *tsyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'empty' and 'silk', and the second 'plain' and 'sincere'.
