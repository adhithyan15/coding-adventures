## You'll want to know: महँगा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C72-COST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**महँगा** (*mahãgā*) — "expensive".

Sanskrit महार्घ (*mahārgha*): **महा** is "great" and **अर्घ** is "value, price".
So the word is "great-priced", which is what it still means.

The moon-and-dot ँ over the ह sends the vowel through the nose. Say it aloud with
the nasal and without it, and you will hear that the nasal is not decoration —
it is part of the word.

महँगा behaves like **बड़ा** and **पुराना**, which you already own: it comes before
the thing it describes and changes its ending to agree with it. You do not have
to work the agreement yet. Hear it, and notice the **-आ** ending it shares with
those two.

**बहुत महँगा** puts last chapter's word to work on this one.
