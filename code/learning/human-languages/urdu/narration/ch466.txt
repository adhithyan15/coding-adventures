Urdu, chapter 466: Arms and Legs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بازو (bāzū) — an arm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the backbone, then the Urdu for a jaw.

You'll want to know: بازو.
بازو — bāzū — "an arm".
بھائی کے بازو بڑے مضبوط ہیں۔ bhāī ke bāzū baṛe mazbūt haiṅ. My brother's arms are very strong.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
بازو (bāzū) is usually traced to Persian bāzū, "arm".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پنڈلی (pinḍlī) — the calf of the leg.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an arm, then the Urdu for a thigh.

You'll want to know: پنڈلی.
پنڈلی — pinḍlī — "the calf of the leg".
دوڑنے کے بعد میری پنڈلی میں درد ہے۔ dauṛne ke bād merī pinḍlī meṅ dard hai. My calf hurts after running.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
پنڈلی (pinḍlī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin piṇḍlī, "calf of the leg", under Sanskrit píṇḍa, "a lump", which already meant the calf (CDIAL 8168).
