## You'll want to know: خواب ماندن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C499-SLEEP-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خواب ماندن** (*khâb mândan*) — "to oversleep".

> امروز خواب ماندم و دیر به کلاس رسیدم.
>
> *emruz khâb mândam va dir be kelâs residam.*
>
> Today I overslept and got to class late.

*khâb* 'sleep' + *mândan* 'to stay'; present stem *mân-*: to stay asleep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khâb mândan* is built on two inherited words: *khâb*, 'sleep', and *mândan*, 'to stay', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
