Telugu, chapter 532: Bringing Up Children.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పెంపకం (pempakaṁ) — upbringing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an heir, then the Telugu for a lullaby.

You'll want to know: పెంపకం.
పెంపకం — pempakaṁ — "upbringing".
పిల్లల మంచి నడవడికి తల్లిదండ్రుల పెంపకమే కారణం. pillala mañci naḍavaḍiki tallidaṇḍrula pempakamē kāraṇaṁ. Good parenting is the reason for the children's good behaviour.
The noun of పెంచు, to raise.

The word, taken apart.
పెంపకం (pempakaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4436 lists Telugu pempakamu, "rearing", beside pen(u)cu, "to nourish, to foster, to rear".

గారాబంగా పెంచు, to bring up a child spoilt.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for upbringing, then the Telugu for pampering.

You'll want to know: పోషించు.
పోషించు — pōṣiñcu — "to support; to provide for".
అన్నయ్యే కుటుంబాన్ని పోషించాడు. annayyē kuṭumbānni pōṣiñcāḍu. It was my elder brother who provided for the family.
Past (I …): పోషించాను, pōṣiñcānu.
Also to play a part: పాత్ర పోషించు (pōṣiñcu), to play a role.

The word, taken apart.
పోషించు (pōṣiñcu) is built on Sanskrit poṣaṇa, "nourishing, fostering, supporting" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
