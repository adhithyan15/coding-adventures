[PAUSE 3s] For the sake of? (**कृते**, *kṛte*.) By which? (**येन**, *yena*.) By that? (**तेन**, *tena*.) That? (**यत्**, *yat*.) However? (**परन्तु**, *parantu*.) Easy to get? (**सुलभः**, *sulabhaḥ*.) Hard to get? (**दुर्लभः**, *durlabhaḥ*.) Complicated? (**जटिलः**, *jaṭilaḥ*.) Easy to do? (**सुकरः**, *sukaraḥ*.) Hard to do? (**दुष्करः**, *duṣkaraḥ*.) Mighty? (**बलवान्**, *balavān*.) Sick? (**रुग्णः**, *rugṇaḥ*.) Healthy? (**स्वस्थः**, *svasthaḥ*.) Happy? (**सुखी**, *sukhī*.) Unhappy? (**दुःखी**, *duḥkhī*.) Proper? (**उचितः**, *ucitaḥ*.) Improper? (**अनुचितः**, *anucitaḥ*.) Necessary? (**आवश्यकः**, *āvaśyakaḥ*.) Other? (**अन्यः**, *anyaḥ*.) Alone? (**एकाकी**, *ekākī*.)
