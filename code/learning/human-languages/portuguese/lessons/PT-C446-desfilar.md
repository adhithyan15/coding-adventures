## You'll want to know: desfilar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C446-ARRAIAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**desfilar** — "to parade, to march past".

> As bandas desfilam pela avenida.
>
> *The bands parade along the avenue.*

Models *desfilam* on a catwalk too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desfilar* was borrowed from French *défiler* 'to march in file', built on *fil* 'thread', from Latin *fīlum* (Lewis & Short).
