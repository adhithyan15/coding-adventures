## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-JOIN-AUR-01, HI-JOIN-YA-01, HI-JOIN-LEKIN-01, HI-JOIN-KI-01, HI-JOIN-KYONKI-01, HI-GRAMMAR-KYA-POLAR-01, HI-LEX-CHAHNA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-INFINITIVE-OBJECT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which joining words treat the two halves as EQUALS? (***aur*, **या**,
**लेकिन**.) Which ones put one half UNDERNEATH the other? (**कि**, **क्योंकि**,
**जब … तब**, **-कर**.) And which single word means *what* in the middle of a
sentence and nothing at all at the front? (**क्या**.) Then stop.
