## You'll want to know: indemniser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C433-DEDOMMAGEMENT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**indemniser** — "to pay damages to, to indemnify".

> L'assurance va nous indemniser pour la valise perdue.
>
> *The insurance is going to pay us for the lost suitcase.*

Formal, used by insurers: *l'indemnisation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indemniser* is built on *indemne* 'unharmed', a learned borrowing from Latin *indemnis*, from *in-* 'not' and *damnum* 'loss' (Lewis & Short).
