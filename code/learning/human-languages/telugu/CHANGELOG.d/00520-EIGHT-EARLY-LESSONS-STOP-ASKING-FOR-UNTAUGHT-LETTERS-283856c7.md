## Eight early lessons stop asking for untaught letters

- Script closure (a lesson may ask the reader to decode only letters an
  earlier lesson taught) found eight Telugu violations in chapters 1-9; there
  are now none.
- Words whose letters are not yet taught are now given in romanization only:
  *vaccu* in `TE-C04-vellu`, *uṇḍu* in `TE-C03-miiru-elaa-unnaaru`,
  *nēnu Hyderābādlō uṇṭānu* in `TE-C05-undu`, *prārambhin̄cu* in
  `TE-C09-kshaminchandi`, and three farewells in `TE-C04-practice` and one
  line in `TE-C05-practice`. The practice notes now say those lines stay in
  romanization until their letters are taught, instead of offering them for
  recognition.
- `TE-C09-kshaminchandi` writes the suffix as the attached sign **‑ించు**,
  as it appears in the headword, not with the independent vowel.
- `TE-C01-sare` gives the Kannada and Tamil cousins in their own scripts
  only, without an untaught Telugu vowel sign.
- `TE-C02-practice`: the second speaker is now Anu (అను), whose name uses
  only taught letters, instead of Arun.
