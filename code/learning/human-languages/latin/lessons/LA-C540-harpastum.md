## You'll want to know: harpastum, harpastī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C540-PUPA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**harpastum, harpastī** — "a hand-ball game".

> Iuvenēs in campō harpastō lūdunt.
>
> *The young men play hand-ball on the field.*

Neuter, second declension. A rough team game with a small ball, from Greek.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**harpastum** was borrowed from Greek *harpaston* (Lewis & Short), a ball one snatches from another, from *harpazo* "to seize".
