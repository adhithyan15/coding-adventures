## You'll want to know: la dama
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C503-GIOCHI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la dama** — "draughts, checkers; lady".

> Giochiamo a dama dopo cena?
>
> *Shall we play draughts after dinner?*

*La dama* is also a lady, or a partner at a dance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dama* was borrowed from French *dame* "lady" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *domina* "mistress of the house".
