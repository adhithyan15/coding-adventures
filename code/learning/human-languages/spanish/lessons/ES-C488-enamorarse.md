## You'll want to know: enamorarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C488-GENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**enamorarse** — "to fall in love".

> Se enamoró de su vecino.
>
> *She fell in love with her neighbour.*

It goes with *de*: *enamorarse de alguien*.
