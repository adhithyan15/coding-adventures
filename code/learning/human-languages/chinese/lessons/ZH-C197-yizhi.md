## You'll want to know: yìzhí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yìzhí* — "straight on; all the way".

> *cóng zhèr yìzhí zǒu* — Go straight on from here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

一 changes its tone here: yì, before the rising tone of zhí.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìzhí* is built from 一 'one' and *zhí* 'straight', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *drik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'upright'.

## What you've built

One more word for asking where things are.
