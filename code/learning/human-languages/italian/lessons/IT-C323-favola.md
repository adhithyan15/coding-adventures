## You'll want to know: la favola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C323-FAVOLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la favola** — "the fable, the tale".

> Nella favola la volpe parla con il lupo.
>
> *In the fable the fox talks to the wolf.*

*Una vita da favola* — a fairy-tale life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*favola* is inherited from Latin *fābula* "a story, a tale" (EtymDB), from *fārī* "to speak".
