## You'll want to know: lùkuàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C423-SOUTH423-01]; assesses=[] -->

*lùkuàng* — "road conditions".

> *lùkuàng bù hǎo dehuà wǒmen zuò dìtiě qù* — If the roads are bad, we'll take the metro.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lùkuàng* is built from 路 'a road' and *kuàng* 'a state of things', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luH* and *xjwangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to compare'; the sense 'state of things' is not given there.
