## You'll want to know: calculus, calculī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C405-CALCULUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**calculus, calculī** — "a pebble, a counter; a reckoning".

> Calculīs numerābat.
>
> *He was counting with pebbles.*

*calculus, calculī* m., a little stone. *Calculum pōnere*, to reckon. English *calculate* and *calculus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*calculus* is a small form of *calx, calcis* "a stone, lime" (Lewis & Short): a little pebble used for counting. English *calculate* comes from it.
