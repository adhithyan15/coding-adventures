## You'll want to know: வாய்மொழி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C312-VIRIVAKA312-05]; assesses=[] -->

**வாய்மொழி** — *vāymoḻi* — "oral tradition; word of mouth".

> இந்தக் கதைகள் வாய்மொழியாக வந்தவை.
>
> *intak kataikaḷ vāymoḻiyāka vantavai.*
>
> These stories have come down by word of mouth.

வாய்மொழியாக, orally; வாய், mouth + மொழி, word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வாய்மொழி** (*vāymoḻi*) is a Tamil compound built from வாய், "mouth" (DEDR 5352), and மொழி, "word, saying" (DEDR 4989).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
