## You'll want to know: ಪರಿಶ್ರಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C521-DUDIME521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಶ್ರಮ** — *pariśrama* — "hard work; toil".

> ಅವಳ ಯಶಸ್ಸಿಗೆ ಅವಳ ಪರಿಶ್ರಮವೇ ಕಾರಣ.
>
> *avaḷa yaśassige avaḷa pariśramavē kāraṇa.*
>
> Her hard work is the reason for her success.

ಪರಿ-, all round + ಶ್ರಮ, labour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಶ್ರಮ** (*pariśrama*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pariśrama*, "fatigue, pain, exertion" (CDIAL 7892).
