## You'll want to know: toser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C486-MEDICO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**toser** — "to cough".

> El niño tose mucho por la noche.
>
> *The child coughs a lot at night.*

*La tos* is the cough.
