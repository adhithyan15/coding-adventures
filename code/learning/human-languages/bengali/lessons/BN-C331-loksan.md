## You'll want to know: লোকসান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C331-UDDESHYO331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**লোকসান** — *loksān* — "a financial loss".

> গত বছর দোকানটার লোকসান হল।
>
> *gôtô bôchhôr dokānṭār loksān hôlô.*
>
> The shop made a loss last year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লোকসান** (*loksān*) is usually traced to Arabic *nuqṣān*, a shortfall, which would have come into Bengali through Persian, with the first n heard as l. It is the opposite of লাভ in trade.
