## Script — 十 over a frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-NAN-SOUTH-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 南

**Two pieces, top to bottom.**

**The top piece, 十, two strokes.** A short bar, then a vertical down through it.

**The frame, two strokes.** A vertical down the left. Then a bar across that turns down the right side and hooks to the left at the foot: the frame is open at the bottom.

**Inside, five strokes.** A dot and a short stroke falling to the left, side by side. Two bars, one under the other. Then a vertical from the upper bar down through the lower one.

2 + 2 + 5 = nine.
