Hindi, chapter 376: Clearly So.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ज़ाहिर (zāhir) — evident, plain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a hint, then the Hindi for to charm.

You'll want to know: ज़ाहिर.
ज़ाहिर — zāhir — "evident, plain".
ज़ाहिर है कि अगर तुम सोओगे नहीं तो थक जाओगे। zāhir hai ki agar tum sooge nahīṃ to thak jāoge. It's plain that if you don't sleep, you'll get tired.

The word, taken apart.
ज़ाहिर (zāhir) is usually traced to Arabic ẓāhir, "outward, apparent", which reached Hindi through Persian.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

You'll want to know: लाज़िमी.
लाज़िमी — lāzimī — "bound to happen, inevitable".

The word, taken apart.
लाज़िमी (lāzimī) is usually traced to Persian lāzimī, "necessary", which is built on Arabic lāzim, "binding, necessary".
