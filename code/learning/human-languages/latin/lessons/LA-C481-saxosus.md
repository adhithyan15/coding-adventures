## You'll want to know: saxōsus, saxōsa, saxōsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C481-CONVALLIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**saxōsus, saxōsa, saxōsum** — "rocky, stony".

> Ager saxōsus est; agricola eum arāre nōn potest.
>
> *The field is stony; the farmer cannot plough it.*

An adjective of three endings, from *saxum* 'rock' with *-ōsus* 'full of'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saxōsus* is built on *saxum* 'rock, stone' with the suffix *-ōsus* 'full of' (Lewis & Short).
