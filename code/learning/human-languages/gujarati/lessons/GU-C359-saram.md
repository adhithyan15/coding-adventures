## You'll want to know: શરમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C359-IJJAT359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**શરમ** — *śaram* — "shame".

> ખોટું બોલ્યા પછી તેને શરમ આવી.
>
> *khoṭũ bolyā pachī tene śaram āvī.*
>
> After telling a lie he felt ashamed.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શરમ** (*śaram*) is usually traced to Persian *sharm*, "shame, modesty". **શરમાવું** (*śarmāvũ*), "to feel shy", is built on it.
