## You'll want to know: капитан
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C427-MAYAK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**капитан** (*kapitán*) — "a captain".

> Капитан сказал, что море спокойное.
>
> *Kapitán skazál, chto móre spokóynoye.*
>
> The captain said the sea was calm.

Masculine. Also the captain of a team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*капитан* is usually traced through Italian *capitano* or French *capitaine* to Late Latin *capitāneus* 'a chief', from *caput* 'head' (EtymDB lists those words; the Russian entry has no source).
