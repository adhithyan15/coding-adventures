## You'll want to know: उस्तरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C484-SANVARNA484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उस्तरा** — *ustarā* — "a razor".

> नाई ने उस्तरे से दाढ़ी बनाई।
>
> *nāī ne ustare se dāṛhī banāī.*
>
> The barber shaved the beard with a razor.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उस्तरा** (*ustarā*) is usually traced to Persian *ustura*, "razor". *dāṛhī banānā*, "to make the beard", is how Hindi says to shave.
