Marathi, chapter 131: Curtain, Lock, Bucket, Rope, Nail.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पडदा (paḍdā) — a curtain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a bedsheet, then the Marathi for a mattress.

You'll want to know: पडदा.
