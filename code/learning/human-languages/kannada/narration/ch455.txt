5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪ್ರವಾಸೋದ್ಯಮ (pravāsōdyama) — tourism; the tourist trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for high tide, then the Kannada for a whirlpool.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರವಾಸೋದ್ಯಮ.
ಪ್ರವಾಸೋದ್ಯಮ — pravāsōdyama — "tourism; the tourist trade".
ಈ ಜಿಲ್ಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ರವಾಸೋದ್ಯಮ ತುಂಬಾ ಬೆಳೆದಿದೆ. ī jilleyalli pravāsōdyama tumbā beḷedide. Tourism has grown a lot in this district.
ಪ್ರವಾಸ, a trip, plus ಉದ್ಯಮ, an industry; ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ is a tourist.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರವಾಸೋದ್ಯಮ (pravāsōdyama) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: pravāsa, "dwelling abroad, a journey" (CDIAL 8802), and udyama, "an undertaking, effort" (CDIAL 2048).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ (gamyasthāna) — a destination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a whirlpool, then the Kannada for tourism.

You'll want to know: ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ.
ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ — gamyasthāna — "a destination".
ನಮ್ಮ ಬಸ್ ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನವನ್ನು ಮಧ್ಯರಾತ್ರಿ ತಲುಪಿತು. namma bas gamyasthānavannu madhyarātri talupitu. Our bus reached its destination at midnight.
ಗಮ್ಯ, to be gone to, plus ಸ್ಥಾನ, a place.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಮ್ಯಸ್ಥಾನ (gamyasthāna) is built from two Sanskrit words: gamya, "to be gone to, accessible" (CDIAL 4032), and sthāna, "a place" (CDIAL 13753).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು (buk māḍu) — to make a booking; to book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for tourism, then the Kannada for a destination.

You'll want to know: ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು.
ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು — buk māḍu — "to make a booking; to book".
ನಾನು ಆಪ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಎರಡು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ. nānu āpnalli eraḍu ṭikeṭ buk māḍide. I booked two tickets on an app.
Past (I …): ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, buk māḍide.
Everyday speech for ಕಾಯ್ದಿರಿಸು, to reserve.

The word, taken apart.
ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು (buk māḍu) joins a loan from English book, "to reserve", and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ (mārgadarśi) — a guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a destination, then the Kannada for to make a booking.

You'll want to know: ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ.
ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ — mārgadarśi — "a guide".
ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ ನಮಗೆ ಕೋಟೆಯ ಇತಿಹಾಸ ಹೇಳಿದನು. mārgadarśi namage kōṭeya itihāsa hēḷidanu. The guide told us the history of the fort.
Plural: ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿಗಳು, mārgadarśigaḷu.
ಮಾರ್ಗ, a way, plus -ದರ್ಶಿ, one who shows; a guidebook can be one too.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ (mārgadarśi) is built from Sanskrit mārga, "a way" (CDIAL 10071), and -darśin, "one who shows", from dṛś, "to see"; Sanskrit also has mārgadarśaka, "a guide" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಣೀಯ (prēkṣaṇīya) — worth seeing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to make a booking, then the Kannada for a guide.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಣೀಯ.
ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಣೀಯ — prēkṣaṇīya — "worth seeing".
ಈ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಣೀಯ ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನಗಳಿವೆ. ī ūrinalli tumbā prēkṣaṇīya dēvasthānagaḷive. There are many temples worth seeing in this town.
ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಕ, a viewer, comes from the same root.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಣೀಯ (prēkṣaṇīya) is borrowed from Sanskrit prēkṣaṇīya, "to be seen, visible" (Monier-Williams), on prēkṣaṇa, "viewing, a show" (CDIAL 8991).
