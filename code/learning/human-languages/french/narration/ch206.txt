Lesson 1 of 5.
casser — to break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to order, then the French for to dry.

You'll want to know: casser.
casser — "to break".

The word, taken apart.
casser from Old French quasser from Latin quassare, "to shake hard, to shatter" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
