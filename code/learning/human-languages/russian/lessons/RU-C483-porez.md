## You'll want to know: порез
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C483-SCRATCHES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**порез** (*poréz*) — "a cut".

> Мама промыла порез и заклеила его пластырем.
>
> *Máma promýla poréz i zakléila yegó plástyrem.*
>
> Mum washed the cut and put a plaster on it.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*порез* is built on *по-* and the root of *резать* 'to cut', which is inherited from Old East Slavic and Proto-Slavic *rězati* (EtymDB).
