## You'll want to know: postuler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C354-CANDIDATURE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**postuler** — "to apply (for a job)".

> Elle a postulé à un poste de vendeuse.
>
> *She applied for a job as a shop assistant.*

*Postuler à* or *pour un poste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*postuler* is a learned borrowing from Latin *postulāre* "to ask, to demand" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
