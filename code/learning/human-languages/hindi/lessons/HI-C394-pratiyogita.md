## You'll want to know: प्रतियोगिता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C394-INAAM394-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतियोगिता** — *pratiyogitā* — "a competition".

> अगर कल प्रतियोगिता होगी तो पूरा स्कूल आएगा।
>
> *agar kal pratiyogitā hogī to pūrā skūl āegā.*
>
> If the competition is tomorrow, the whole school will come.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतियोगिता** (*pratiyogitā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pratiyogitā*, "correlation" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams does not give the sense of a contest, which is usually taken as a modern Hindi one.
