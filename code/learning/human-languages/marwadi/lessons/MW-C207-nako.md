## You'll want to know: नाको
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C207-GARDEN207-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नाको** — *nāko* — "a checkpoint; the mouth of a road".

> नाको अठै सूं नजीक है।
>
> *nāko aṭhai sū̃ najīk hai.*
>
> The checkpoint is close to here.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाको** (*nāko*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*nākka*, "like a nose, projecting". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *nākā*, "end of road, passage", and Sindhi *nāko*, "entrance, pass" (CDIAL 7037).
