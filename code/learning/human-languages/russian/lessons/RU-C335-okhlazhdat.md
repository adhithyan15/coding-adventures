## You'll want to know: охлаждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C335-OSTYVAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**охлаждать** (*okhlazhdát*) — "to chill, to cool".

> Сок нужно охладить.
>
> *Sok núzhno okhladít.*
>
> The juice needs chilling.

Imperfective; perfective *охладить* (*okhladít*), *охлажу*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*охлаждать* is built on *о-* and *хлад* "cold", the Church Slavonic shape of native *холод*, from Proto-Slavic *xoldŭ* "cold" (EtymDB).
