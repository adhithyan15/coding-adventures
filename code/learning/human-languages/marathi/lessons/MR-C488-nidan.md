## You'll want to know: निदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C488-RUGNA488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निदान** — *nidān* — "a diagnosis".

> रक्त तपासल्यानंतर निदान झालं.
>
> *rakta tapāslyānantar nidān jhālaṁ.*
>
> The diagnosis was made after a blood test.

Neuter.

निदान करणे, to diagnose. As an adverb निदान means at least.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निदान** (*nidān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nidāna*, "band; primary cause" (CDIAL 7196).
