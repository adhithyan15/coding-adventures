## You'll want to know: انتقاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C365-QEZAVAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقاد** (*enteqâd*) — "criticism".

> او به انتقاد گوش میدهد.
>
> *u be enteqâd gush midahad.*
>
> He listens to criticism.

Plural *enteqâdât* or *enteqâdhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقاد** comes from Arabic *intiqād* "criticism" (EtymDB).
