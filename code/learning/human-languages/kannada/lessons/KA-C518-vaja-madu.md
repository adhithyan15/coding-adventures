# ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು (vajā māḍu) — to dismiss; to sack

## You'll want to know: ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C518-NEMAKA518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು** — *vajā māḍu* — "to dismiss; to sack".

> ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬರುತ್ತಿದ್ದ ನೌಕರನನ್ನು ಕಂಪನಿ ವಜಾ ಮಾಡಿತು.
>
> *taḍavāgi baruttidda naukaranannu kampani vajā māḍitu.*
>
> The company sacked the employee who kept coming late.

Past (I …): ವಜಾ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *vajā māḍide*.

Also of a court case: ಅರ್ಜಿ ವಜಾ ಆಯಿತು, the petition was dismissed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು** (*vajā māḍu*) joins *vajā*, a word that came into Kannada through the language of offices and courts, with ಮಾಡು, "to do"; the earlier source of *vajā* is not settled.
