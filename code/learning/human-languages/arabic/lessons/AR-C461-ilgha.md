## You'll want to know: إلغاء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C461-ILGHA461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**إلغاء** — *ilghāʾ* — "a cancellation".

> سمعنا عن إلغاء الرحلة في المطار.
>
> *samiʿnā ʿan ilghāʾ ar-riḥla fī al-maṭār.*
>
> We heard about the cancellation of the flight at the airport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إلغاء** (*ilghāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-gh-w*, "to be of no account" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form IV, "to make null".
