## You'll want to know: ようしょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C422-WASHOKU422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ようしょく** — *yōshoku* — "food in the Western style".

> **あには ようしょくより わしょくが すきです。** — *ani wa yōshoku yori washoku ga suki desu* — "My brother likes Japanese food better than food in the western style."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ようしょく** (*yōshoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yō*, "ocean" (Middle Chinese *yang*) and *shoku*, "food" (*zyik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
