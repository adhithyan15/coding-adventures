## You'll want to know: aussitôt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C269-QUAND-04]; assesses=[] -->

**aussitôt** — "at once, immediately".

> Il a vu la lettre et il l'a aussitôt ouverte.
>
> *He saw the letter and opened it at once.*

*Aussitôt que* means the same as *dès que*: as soon as.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aussitôt* joins *aussi* "so" and *tôt* "early"; *tôt* is usually traced to Latin *tostum* "scorched, burnt", from *torrēre* "to roast" (Lewis & Short).
