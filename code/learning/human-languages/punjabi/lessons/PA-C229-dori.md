## You'll want to know: ਡੋਰੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C229-DIN229-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੋਰੀ** — *ḍorī* — "a cord; a drawstring".

> ਸਲਵਾਰ ਦੀ ਡੋਰੀ ਲੰਮੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *salvār dī ḍorī lammī hai.*
>
> The drawstring of the salwar is long.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੋਰੀ** (*ḍorī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *davara*, "string"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍorī*, "string" (CDIAL 6225).
