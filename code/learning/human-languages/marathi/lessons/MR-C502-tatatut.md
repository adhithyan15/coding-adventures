## You'll want to know: ताटातूट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C502-DURAVA502-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ताटातूट** — *tāṭātūṭ* — "a separation (of people)".

> नोकरीमुळे दोन भावांची ताटातूट झाली.
>
> *nokrīmuḷe don bhāvāñcī tāṭātūṭ jhālī.*
>
> Work separated the two brothers.

Feminine.

ताटातूट होणे, to be parted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताटातूट** (*tāṭātūṭ*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
