## You'll want to know: تعب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C521-MUKAFAA521-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تعب** — *taʿiba* — "to get tired".

> تعب العمال بعد يوم طويل.
>
> *taʿiba al-ʿummāl baʿda yawm ṭawīl.*
>
> The workers got tired after a long day.

Present: يتعب, *yatʿab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعب** (*taʿiba*) is built on the native Arabic root *t-ʿ-b*, "to be tired, to toil" (Lane). Mutʿab, tired, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
