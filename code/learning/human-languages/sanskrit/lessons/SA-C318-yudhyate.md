## You'll want to know: युध्यते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C318-SENA318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**युध्यते** — *yudhyate* — "fights".

> योधाः शौर्येण युध्यन्ते।
>
> *yodhāḥ śauryeṇa yudhyante.*
>
> The warriors fight with valour.

Past (he …): सः शौर्येण युद्धवान्, *saḥ śauryeṇa yuddhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**युध्यते** (*yudhyate*) comes from the root √yudh, "to fight", which also gives *yuddha*, "a war" (MW).
