## You'll want to know: la flecha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C672-SENAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la flecha** — "the arrow".

> Sigue las flechas amarillas.
>
> *Follow the yellow arrows.*

On a map or a sign, the arrow shows the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flecha* is usually traced to French *flèche* "arrow", a word of Germanic origin.
