Urdu, chapter 287: Fire!.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انگارا (angārā) — an ember; a live coal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to crush, then the Urdu for to steady oneself.

You'll want to know: انگارا.
انگارا — angārā — "an ember; a live coal".
چولہے میں ایک انگارا تھا۔ chūlhe meṅ ek angārā thā. There was an ember in the stove.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
انگارا (angārā) is inherited from Sanskrit áṅgāra, "glowing charcoal" (CDIAL 125), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ãgārā.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سلگنا (sulagnā) — to smoulder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to steady oneself, then the Urdu for an ember.

You'll want to know: سلگنا.
سلگنا — sulagnā — "to smoulder".
چولہے میں آگ سلگ رہی تھی۔ chūlhe meṅ āg sulag rahī thī. A fire was smouldering in the stove.
Past (it …): وہ آگ سلگی, voh āg sulgī.

The word, taken apart.
سلگنا (sulagnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sulagnā, "to be set on fire", under Sanskrit saṃlagna, "attached" (CDIAL 12999). It is the slow burning before the flames come.

Masculine.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بھڑکنا (bhaṛaknā) — to flare up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to smoulder, then the Urdu for a spark.

You'll want to know: بھڑکنا.
بھڑکنا — bhaṛaknā — "to flare up".
ہوا سے آگ بھڑک اٹھی۔ hawā se āg bhaṛak uṭhī. The wind made the fire flare up.
Past (it …): وہ آگ بھڑکی, voh āg bhaṛkī.

The word, taken apart.
بھڑکنا (bhaṛaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bhaṛaknā, "to be excited, to take fright", under bhaṭ (CDIAL 9365), with bhaṛak, "a flash"; a fire that flares is thought to share that sudden start. بھڑک اٹھی is the leap of the flames.

Lesson 5 of 5.
جلنا (jalnā) — to burn; to be on fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a spark, then the Urdu for to flare up.

You'll want to know: جلنا.
جلنا — jalnā — "to burn; to be on fire".
دکان جل گئی۔ dukān jal gaī. The shop burned down.
Past (it …): وہ گھر جل گیا, voh ghar jal gayā.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from Sanskrit jválati, "burns, blazes" (CDIAL 5306). It is the partner of جلانا: you جلانا a fire, and a thing جلنا (jalnā) by itself.
