## You'll want to know: पलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C498-PARVARISH498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पलना** — *palnā* — "to be brought up".

> अरुण अपने नाना के घर पला।
>
> *Aruṇ apne nānā ke ghar palā.*
>
> Arun was brought up at his grandfather's house.

Past (he …): वह गाँव में पला, *vah gā̃v meṁ palā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पलना** (*palnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *palnā*, "to be reared", beside *pālnā*, "to rear", under Sanskrit *pāláyati*, "protects" (CDIAL 8129). It is the intransitive partner of *pālnā*.
