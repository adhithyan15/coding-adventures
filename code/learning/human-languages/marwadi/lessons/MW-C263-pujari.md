## You'll want to know: पुजारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C263-TELI263-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पुजारी** — *pujārī* — "a temple priest".

> पुजारी मंदिर मांय पूजा करै है।
>
> *pujārī mandir mā̃y pūjā karai hai.*
>
> The priest performs worship in the temple.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुजारी** (*pujārī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pūjākara*, "performing worship". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *pujerī*, "temple priest" (CDIAL 8317a).
