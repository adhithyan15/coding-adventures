## You'll want to know: ほぼ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C530-ATLEAST530-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ほぼ** — *hobo* — "nearly; almost".

> **もし ごじに でたら、ちちは ほぼ まにあうでしょう。** — *moshi goji ni detara, chichi wa hobo maniau deshō* — "If he leaves at five, my father will make it, more or less."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほぼ** (*hobo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
