## You'll want to know: vagare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C446-BORGO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vagare** — "to wander, to roam".

> Abbiamo vagato per il borgo tutto il pomeriggio.
>
> *We wandered round the village all afternoon.*

With no fixed goal; *vago* — vague.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vagare* is inherited from Latin *vagārī* 'to wander, to stroll about' (EtymDB), from *vagus* 'roaming'.
