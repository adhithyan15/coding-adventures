## You'll want to know: फुरसत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C528-CHHAND528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फुरसत** — *phursat* — "free time; leisure".

> आज मला अजिबात फुरसत नाही.
>
> *āj malā ajibāt phursat nāhī.*
>
> I have no free time at all today.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फुरसत** (*phursat*) is a loan from Persian *fursat*, "leisure", usually traced to Arabic *furṣa*, "occasion".
