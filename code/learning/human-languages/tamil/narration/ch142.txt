Tamil, chapter 142: Camel, Duck, Cheek, Wrist, Elbow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ஒட்டகம் (ottakam) — a camel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a pig, then the Tamil for a donkey.

You'll want to know: ஒட்டகம்.
ஒட்டகம் — ottakam — "a camel".
