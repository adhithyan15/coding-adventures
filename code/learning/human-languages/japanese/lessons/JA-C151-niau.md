## You'll want to know: にあう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C151-KIMONO151-05]; assesses=[] -->

**にあう** — *niau* — "to suit; to look good on".

> **この きものは ははに にあいます。** — *kono kimono wa haha ni niaimasu* — "This kimono suits my mother."

Polite **にあいます**, *niaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にあう** (*niau*) is native Japanese, built from *ni*, the stem of *niru*, "to resemble", and *au*, "to match", both inherited rather than borrowed; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
