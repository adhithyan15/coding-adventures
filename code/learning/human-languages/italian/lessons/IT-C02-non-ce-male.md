# non c'è male — "not bad"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-COSI-COSI-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You can answer **Come stai?** with **bene** or **così così**. Add one
more answer, on its own step.

## You'll want to know — The formula
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-NON-CE-MALE-02]; assesses=[] -->

> **Non c'è male.** — "Not bad."

Learn the four sounds as one useful answer for now. The pieces behind it —
negation and the **c'è** construction — belong to later lessons. You do not need
their rules to answer honestly today.
