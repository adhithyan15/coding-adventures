# Tomorrow's work: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask *what will you do tomorrow?*

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-NEG-QUESTION-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I will work tomorrow*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *will they work tomorrow?*] Say **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਨਗੇ?**
- [YOU READ: **ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਵੇਗਾ।**] Give its meaning: no, Aman won't come tomorrow.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੀ ਕਰੋਗੇ?**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-NEG-QUESTION-222] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say one thing you will do tomorrow and one thing you will not.
