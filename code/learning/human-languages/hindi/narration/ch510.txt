Hindi, chapter 510: In the Carpenter's Shop.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रंदा (randā) — a carpenter's plane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for high-handedness, then the Hindi for a slogan.

You'll want to know: रंदा.
रंदा — randā — "a carpenter's plane".
बढ़ई ने रंदे से दरवाज़ा चिकना किया। Baṛhaī ne rande se darvāzā cikanā kiyā. The carpenter made the door smooth with a plane.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
रंदा (randā) is usually traced to Persian randa, "a plane (the tool)".

घिसना — ghisnā — "to rub, to wear down".
बढ़ई ने लकड़ी को पत्थर पर घिसा। Baṛhaī ne lakṛī ko patthar par ghisā. The carpenter rubbed the wood on a stone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ठोकना (ṭhoknā) — to knock in; to drive (a nail).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a chisel, then the Hindi for to rub, to wear down.

You'll want to know: ठोकना.
ठोकना — ṭhoknā — "to knock in; to drive (a nail)".
उसने दीवार में एक कील ठोकी। Usne dīvār meṁ ek kīl ṭhokī. He drove a nail into the wall.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मेज़ में कील ठोकी, usne mez meṁ kīl ṭhokī.

The word, taken apart.
ठोकना (ṭhoknā) is a tadbhava, inherited from ṭhōkk, "knock", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5513).

Lesson 5 of 5.
छेदना (chednā) — to bore; to pierce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to rub, to wear down, then the Hindi for to knock in.

You'll want to know: छेदना.
छेदना — chednā — "to bore; to pierce".
बढ़ई ने कील से लकड़ी छेदी। Baṛhaī ne kīl se lakṛī chedī. The carpenter bored through the wood with a nail.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने काग़ज़ छेदा, usne kāġaz chedā.

The word, taken apart.
छेदना (chednā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit chidra, "a hole" (CDIAL 5043). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to bore".
