## You'll want to know: ぐっすり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C146-SLEEP146-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぐっすり** — *gussuri* — "soundly (asleep)".

> **まいにち ぐっすり ねむります。** — *mainichi gussuri nemurimasu* — "I sleep soundly every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぐっすり** (*gussuri*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
