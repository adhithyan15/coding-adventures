## You'll want to know: احتجز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C317-TAWQIF317-02]; assesses=[] -->

**احتجز** — *iḥtajaza* — "to detain, to hold".

> احتجز الموظف جواز سفري ساعة.
>
> *iḥtajaza al-muwaẓẓaf jawāz safarī sāʿa.*
>
> The official held my passport for an hour.

Present: يحتجز, *yaḥtajiz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتجز** (*iḥtajaza*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-j-z* (Lane), "to keep apart, to hold back". It is Form VIII, "to hold back for oneself"; a hotel booking, *ḥajz*, is the same root.
