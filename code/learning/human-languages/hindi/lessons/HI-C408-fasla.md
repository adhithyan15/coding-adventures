## You'll want to know: फ़ासला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C408-MIL408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ासला** — *fāslā* — "a distance; a gap".

> दोनों गाँवों के बीच दस मील का फ़ासला है।
>
> *donõ gā̃voṁ ke bīc das mīl kā fāslā hai.*
>
> The two villages are ten miles apart.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ासला** (*fāslā*) is usually traced to Arabic *fāṣila*, "a separating space, an interval", which came to Hindi through Persian.
