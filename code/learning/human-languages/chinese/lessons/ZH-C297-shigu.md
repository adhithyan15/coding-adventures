## You'll want to know: 事故
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C297-FAULT297-02]; assesses=[] -->

**事故** — *shìgù* — "accident".

> **这个路口常常发生事故** *zhège lùkǒu chángcháng fāshēng shìgù* — Accidents often happen at this crossing.

The new character, in pieces: 故 — nine strokes in two pieces, the right two pieces of 做 without its person piece: on the left 十 over 口, five strokes; on the right the striking piece, four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**事故** (*shìgù*) is built from 事 'a matter' and 故, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzriH* and *kuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'old' and 'a matter'; how the pair came to mean an accident is not settled.
