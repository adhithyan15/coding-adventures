## You'll want to know: принц
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C321-KOROL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**принц** (*prints*) — "a prince".

> Младший принц любил охоту.
>
> *Mládshiy prints lyubíl okhótu.*
>
> The younger prince loved hunting.

A king's son.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*принц* was borrowed from German *Prinz* (EtymDB), which comes from French *prince*, from Latin *prīnceps* "first, chief", built on *prīmus* "first" and *capere* "to take".
