## You'll want to know: नर्स
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C488-JANCH488-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नर्स** — *nars* — "a nurse".

> नर्स ने मरीज़ को दवा दी।
>
> *nars ne marīz ko davā dī.*
>
> The nurse gave the patient his medicine.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नर्स** (*nars*) is borrowed from English *nurse*.
