Bengali, chapter 431: Bays, Creeks and Deltas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উপসাগর (upôsāgôr) — a bay; a gulf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to irrigate, then the Bengali for to farm.

You'll want to know: উপসাগর.
উপসাগর — upôsāgôr — "a bay; a gulf".
নদীটা দক্ষিণে গিয়ে উপসাগরে পড়ে। nôdīṭā dôkkhine giye upôsāgôre pôṛe. The river flows south and falls into the bay.
The Bay of Bengal has its own name, built on this word.

The word, taken apart.
উপসাগর (upôsāgôr) joins Sanskrit upa, "near, lesser", to sāgara, "the sea" (Monier-Williams): a lesser sea, a modern coinage for a bay.

Lesson 3 of 5.
বদ্বীপ (bôdvīp) — a delta.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bay, then the Bengali for a creek.

You'll want to know: বদ্বীপ.
বদ্বীপ — bôdvīp — "a delta".
বদ্বীপে অনেক নদী আর খাল আছে। bôdvīpe ônek nôdī ār khāl āchhe. There are many rivers and canals in the delta.
The ব in front is the letter: an island shaped like ব, as the Greek delta is shaped like its letter.

The word, taken apart.
বদ্বীপ (bôdvīp) is usually explained as ব, the letter, and দ্বীপ, "an island", Sanskrit dvīpa (Monier-Williams): a coinage that renders the Greek delta, named for a triangular letter, with a Bengali one.

Lesson 4 of 5.
পলি (pôli) — silt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a creek, then the Bengali for a delta.

You'll want to know: পলি.
পলি — pôli — "silt".
বন্যার পরে খেতে নতুন পলি পড়ল। bônyār pôre khete nôtun pôli pôṛlô. After the flood fresh silt settled on the fields.
The river mud that makes Bengal's fields rich: পলিমাটি, silt soil.

The word, taken apart.
পলি (pôli) is the fine river mud left by floods; its origin is not settled.

The word, taken apart.
ভরাট করা (bhôrāṭ kôrā) joins ভরাট, "filled up, solid", built on ভরা, "full" (CDIAL 9397), to করা, to do.
