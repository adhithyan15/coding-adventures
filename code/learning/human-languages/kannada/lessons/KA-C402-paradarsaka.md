# ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕ (pāradarśaka) — transparent

## You'll want to know: ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C402-LANCHA402-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕ** — *pāradarśaka* — "transparent".

> ಆಡಳಿತ ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕವಾಗಿ ಇರಬೇಕು.
>
> *āḍaḷita pāradarśakavāgi irabēku.*
>
> Administration should be transparent.

Also of glass or water: ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕ ಗಾಜು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾರದರ್ಶಕ** (*pāradarśaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pāradarśaka*, "showing the opposite shore" (Monier-Williams): you see through to the far side.
