## You'll want to know: झोंपड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C422-KOTHI422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**झोंपड़ी** — *jhõpṛī* — "a hut".

> खेत के पास एक झोंपड़ी है।
>
> *khet ke pās ek jhõpṛī hai.*
>
> There is a hut near the field.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झोंपड़ी** (*jhõpṛī*) goes back to *\*jhuppa*, "a hut", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5403), which lists Hindi *jhõpṛī*.
