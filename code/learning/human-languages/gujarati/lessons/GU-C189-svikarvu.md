## You'll want to know: સ્વીકારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C189-ORDER189-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વીકારવું** — *svīkārvũ* — "to accept".

> **આ દુકાન સિક્કા સ્વીકારે છે.** — *ā dukān sikkā svīkāre che* — "This shop accepts coins."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વીકારવું** (*svīkārvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *svīkāra*, "acceptance, making one's own", from *sva*, "own", and *kāra*, "making": a learned word with the Gujarati verb ending *-vũ*. CDIAL has no entry for it.
