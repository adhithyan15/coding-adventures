## You'll want to know: 山村
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C440-HILLS440-02]; assesses=[] -->

**山村** — *shāncūn* — "mountain village".

> *tā zhù zài yí ge xiǎo shāncūn* — He lives in a little mountain village.

The new character, in pieces: 村 — seven strokes in two pieces: on the left the tree piece of 机, four strokes; on the right the right piece of 对, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**山村** (*shāncūn*) is built from 山 'a mountain' and 村 'a village', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srean* and *tshwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a farmstead.
