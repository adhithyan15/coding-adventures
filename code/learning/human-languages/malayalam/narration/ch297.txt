Malayalam, chapter 297: Refined white flour, Flattened rice, Mushroom, Guava, Indian gooseberry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മൈദ (maida) — refined white flour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a carpet, then the Malayalam for a bedsheet.

You'll want to know: മൈദ.
മൈദ — maida — "refined white flour".

The word, taken apart.
മൈദ (maida) from Hindustani maidā, "fine flour", a word from Persian.
