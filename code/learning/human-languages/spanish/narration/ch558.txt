5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la celebración — the celebration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the coffin, then the Spanish for the inheritance.

You'll want to know: la celebración.
la celebración — "the celebration".
La celebración duró hasta las tres. The celebration went on until three.

The word, taken apart.
celebración is a learned borrowing from Latin celebrātiō 'a crowded gathering, a festival' (Lewis & Short), from celebrāre 'to fill, to celebrate'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
festejar — to celebrate, to party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the inheritance, then the Spanish for the celebration.

You'll want to know: festejar.
festejar — "to celebrate, to party".
Festejamos el cumpleaños de la abuela en el jardín. We celebrated grandma's birthday in the garden.
Very common in Latin America; celebrar is used everywhere.

The word, taken apart.
festejar is built on fiesta, from Latin fēsta, the plural of fēstum 'a feast day' (Lewis & Short), with the ending -ejar.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el homenaje — the tribute, the homage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to celebrate, then the Spanish for to commemorate.

You'll want to know: el homenaje.
el homenaje — "the tribute, the homage".
Le hicieron un homenaje al viejo profesor. They held a tribute to the old teacher.
En homenaje a — in honour of.

The word, taken apart.
homenaje is usually traced to Occitan or Catalan homenatge, built on the word for 'man', from Latin homō (Lewis & Short). Homage was the vow of a lord's man.
