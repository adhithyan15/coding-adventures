## You'll want to know: misericordia, misericordiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C513-MISE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**misericordia, misericordiae** — "pity, compassion".

> Misericordiā mōtus, pauperī pecūniam dat.
>
> *Moved by pity, he gives the poor man money.*

Feminine, first declension, from *misericors*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**misericordia** is built on *misericors* "compassionate", from *miser* and *cor* "heart" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
