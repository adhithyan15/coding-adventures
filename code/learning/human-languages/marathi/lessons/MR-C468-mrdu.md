## You'll want to know: मृदू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C468-SAUMYA468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मृदू** — *mṛdū* — "soft-hearted; gentle".

> आमच्या शिक्षिका स्वभावाने मृदू आहेत.
>
> *āmcyā śikṣikā svabhāvāne mṛdū āhet.*
>
> Our teacher is gentle by nature.

Unchanging: मृदू आवाज, a gentle voice. Sanskrit writes the word with a short final vowel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मृदू** (*mṛdū*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mṛdu*, "soft, weak" (CDIAL 10292), with the final vowel lengthened as Marathi writes it.
