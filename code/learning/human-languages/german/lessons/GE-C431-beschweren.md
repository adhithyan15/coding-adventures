## You'll want to know: beschweren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C431-BESCHWERDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**beschweren** — "to complain (mostly reflexive: sich beschweren)".

> Sie beschwert sich über den Lärm im Flur.
>
> *She complains about the noise in the corridor.*

Reflexive, with *über*: *ich beschwere mich über das Essen*. Without *sich* it means to weigh down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beschweren* is built from *be-* and *schwer* 'heavy', inherited from Old High German *swār* (EtymDB); the reflexive sense 'to complain' grew from 'to feel weighed down'.
