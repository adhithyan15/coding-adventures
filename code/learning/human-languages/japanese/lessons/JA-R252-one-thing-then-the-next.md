# Review — one thing, then the next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-01, JA-LEX-C252-SOREKARA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I got up and washed my face", then "after that, I went out". (*okite, kao o araimashita*; *sorekara, dekakemashita*.)

- [YOU SAY: In the morning I got up, and washed my face.]
- [YOU SAY: After eating breakfast, I went out.]
- [YOU SAY: I went to the sea. And I swam.]
- [YOU SAY: I wrote a letter. After that, I went to bed.]
- [YOU SAY: Time flies.]
