Kannada, chapter 454: Crossing the River.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ತೆಪ್ಪ (teppa) — a coracle; a raft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a compass, then the Kannada for an ocean.

You'll want to know: ತೆಪ್ಪ.
ತೆಪ್ಪ — teppa — "a coracle; a raft".
ನಾವು ತೆಪ್ಪದಲ್ಲಿ ನದಿಯನ್ನು ದಾಟಿದೆವು. nāvu teppadalli nadiyannu dāṭidevu. We crossed the river in a coracle.
A round basket boat, still used for river crossings.

The word, taken apart.
ತೆಪ್ಪ (teppa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3414 lists Kannada teppa, "a raft, a float".

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಅಂಬಿಗ (ambiga) — a ferryman; a boatman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an ocean, then the Kannada for a coracle.

You'll want to know: ಅಂಬಿಗ.
ಅಂಬಿಗ — ambiga — "a ferryman; a boatman".
ಅಂಬಿಗ ತೆಪ್ಪವನ್ನು ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ನಡೆಸಿದನು. ambiga teppavannu nidhānavāgi naḍesidanu. The ferryman steered the coracle slowly.
Plural: ಅಂಬಿಗರು, ambigaru.
ಅಂಬಿ is an old word for a boat; -ಗ marks the man who works it.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂಬಿಗ (ambiga) is native Dravidian: DEDR 177 lists Kannada ambi, "a boat", and ambiga, "a man who rows a boat".

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಉಬ್ಬರ (ubbara) — high tide; a surge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ferryman, then the Kannada for a shore.

You'll want to know: ಉಬ್ಬರ.
ಉಬ್ಬರ — ubbara — "high tide; a surge".
ಸಂಜೆ ಉಬ್ಬರದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಲೆಗಳು ದಡದವರೆಗೆ ಬಂದವು. sañje ubbaradalli alegaḷu daḍadavarege bandavu. At high tide in the evening the waves came up to the bank.
Its pair is ಇಳಿತ, low tide.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಬ್ಬರ (ubbara) is native Dravidian, built on ubbu, "to swell, to rise" (DEDR 666), the family that also names the rising flood tide.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಸುಳಿ (suḷi) — a whirlpool; an eddy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a shore, then the Kannada for high tide.

You'll want to know: ಸುಳಿ.
ಸುಳಿ — suḷi — "a whirlpool; an eddy".
ನದಿಯ ಮಧ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಪಾಯಕಾರಿ ಸುಳಿ ಇದೆ. nadiya madhyadalli apāyakāri suḷi ide. There is a dangerous whirlpool in the middle of the river.
ಸುಳಿಗಾಳಿ, a whirlwind; ಸುಳಿ (suḷi) also names the whorl of hair on the head.

The word, taken apart.
ಸುಳಿ (suḷi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2698 lists older Kannada suḻi, "to turn round; a whirl, a whirlpool, an eddy", beside Tamil cuḻi.
