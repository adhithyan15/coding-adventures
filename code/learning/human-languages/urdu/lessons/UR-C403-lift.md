## You'll want to know: لفٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C403-SARAE403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لفٹ** — *lifṭ* — "a lift".

> ہوٹل کی لفٹ بند تھی۔
>
> *hoṭal kī lifṭ band thī.*
>
> The hotel lift was out of service.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لفٹ** (*lifṭ*) is borrowed from English *lift*.
