## You'll want to know: ગબડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C294-LAPAS294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગબડવું** — *gabaḍvũ* — "to tumble".

> છોકરો સાઈકલ પરથી ગબડી પડ્યો.
>
> *chokro sāīkal parthī gabḍī paḍyo.*
>
> The boy tumbled off his bicycle.

Past (he …): તે છોકરો ગબડી પડ્યો, *te chokro gabḍī paḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગબડવું** (*gabaḍvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. It is falling and rolling over: **ગબડી પડ્યો**, he went tumbling down.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
