## You'll want to know: प्रख्यात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C337-DANTKATHA337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रख्यात** — *prakhyāt* — "renowned".

> ते एक प्रख्यात इतिहासकार आहेत.
>
> *te ek prakhyāt itihāskār āhet.*
>
> He is a renowned historian.

प्रख्यात keeps one form; प्रसिद्ध is the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रख्यात** (*prakhyāt*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prakhyāta*, 'known, celebrated' (Monier-Williams).
