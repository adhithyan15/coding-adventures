Spanish, chapter 711: Emociones fuertes.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la emoción — emotion; excitement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for tension, then the Spanish for to calm.

You'll want to know: la emoción.
la emoción — "emotion; excitement".
¡Qué emoción! Mañana nos vamos. How exciting! We leave tomorrow.
¡Qué emoción! — How exciting!

The word, taken apart.
emoción was borrowed from French émotion (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin ēmovēre "to stir up", from movēre "to move".

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.
emocionado — excited; moved.

You'll want to know: emocionado.
emocionado — "excited; moved".
Estoy muy emocionado por el viaje. I am very excited about the trip.
Used with estar. It covers both excited and touched.

The word, taken apart.
emocionado is the participle of emocionar, built on emoción, which was borrowed from French émotion (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 6.
emotivo — moving, emotional.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for excited, then the Spanish for exciting.

You'll want to know: emotivo.
emotivo — "moving, emotional".
Fue una boda muy emotiva. It was a very moving wedding.
Emotivo describes the moment; emocionado describes the person.

The word, taken apart.
emotivo is usually explained as a learned word built on Latin ēmōtus "stirred up", the participle of ēmovēre, from movēre "to move".

Lesson 5 of 6.
conmover — to move, to touch (emotionally).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for exciting, then the Spanish for moving.

You'll want to know: conmover.
conmover — "to move, to touch (emotionally)".
Su historia me conmovió. Her story moved me.
Stem change: conmueve. Conmovido — moved, touched.

The word, taken apart.
conmover is inherited from Latin commovēre "to stir deeply" (EtymDB), built on movēre "to move".
