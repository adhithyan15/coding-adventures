# औषधम् (auṣadham) — a medicine

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-01, SA-LEX-C279-UPADESAH-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I am ill". (*ahaṃ rugṇaḥ asmi*.) Then "you should go to the doctor", and "the doctor will give advice". (*bhavān vaidyaṃ gacchet*; *vaidyaḥ upadeśaṃ dāsyati*.)

## You'll want to know: औषधम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C281-AUSADHAM-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C26-ROLE-05, SA-LEX-C141-ACT141-01, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-06] -->

**औषधम्** — *auṣadham* — "a medicine". It is a neuter noun.

> **औषधं कुत्र अस्ति?**
>
> *auṣadhaṃ kutra asti?* — "Where is the medicine?"

> **वैद्यः औषधं दास्यति।**
>
> *vaidyaḥ auṣadhaṃ dāsyati.* — "The doctor will give medicine."

> **भवान् औषधं पिबेत्।**
>
> *bhavān auṣadhaṃ pibet.* — "You should take the medicine."

**पिबेत्** (*pibet*) is **पिबति** (*pibati*), "drinks", with the *-et* of advice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**औषधम्** (*auṣadham*) is built on **ओषधि** (*oṣadhi*), "a herb": Monier-Williams gives *auṣadha* as "consisting of herbs", and then herbs used as medicine. It lives on as Oriya *usuā*, "medicine" (CDIAL 2571).
