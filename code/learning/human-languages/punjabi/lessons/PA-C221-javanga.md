# ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ, ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ — I will go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-LEX-KALL-216, PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *Aman will come*. Then *we will meet tomorrow*.

## Grammar Lens: ਮੈਂ in the future
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-LEX-MAIN-03, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

After **ਜਾ** comes **ਵ**, as in **ਆਵੇਗਾ**. With **ਮੈਂ** the ending is **-ਾਂਗਾ**
for a man and **-ਾਂਗੀ** for a woman:

> **ਜਾ** + **ਵ** + **ਾਂਗਾ** → **ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ**

> **ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ।** — *maiṁ kallh sakūl jāvāṅgā.* — I will go to school tomorrow (a man speaking).

> **ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ।** — *maiṁ kallh sakūl jāvāṅgī.* — I will go to school tomorrow (a woman speaking).

With a future form, **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ** means tomorrow.
