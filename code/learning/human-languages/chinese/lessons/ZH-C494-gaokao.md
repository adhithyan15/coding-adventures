## You'll want to know: 高考
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C494-GAOKAO494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**高考** — *gāokǎo* — "college entrance exam".

> *gāokǎo de shíhou wǒ gǎnjué hěn jǐnzhāng* — I felt very nervous during the entrance exam.

The new character, in pieces: 考 — six strokes: the top of 老 *lǎo*, four strokes, then a short bar and a stroke that drops, runs right, turns down and hooks. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**高考** (*gāokǎo*) is built from 高 'high' and 考 'to examine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaw* and *khawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to check' and 'to complete'. It is short for a longer official name, the exam for the higher schools.
