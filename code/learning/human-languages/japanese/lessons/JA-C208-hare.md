## You'll want to know: はれ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C208-FORECAST208-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はれ** — *hare* — "fine weather; clear skies".

> **あしたは はれでしょう。** — *ashita wa hare deshō* — "Tomorrow will probably be fine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はれ** (*hare*) is native Japanese (wago), derived from the verb *hareru*, "to clear up", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
