## You'll want to know: にこむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C473-SIMMER473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**にこむ** — *nikomu* — "to stew; to simmer".

> **やさいを ながく にこんで ください。** — *yasai o nagaku nikonde kudasai* — "Please simmer the vegetables for a long time."

Polite **にこみます**, *nikomimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが にくを にこみました。**, *haha ga niku o nikomimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にこむ** (*nikomu*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ni*, "boiling (niru)" and *komu*, "going in" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
