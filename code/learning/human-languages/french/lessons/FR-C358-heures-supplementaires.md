## You'll want to know: les heures supplémentaires
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C358-GREVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**les heures supplémentaires** — "the overtime".

> Ce mois-ci, j'ai fait vingt heures supplémentaires.
>
> *This month, I did twenty hours of overtime.*

Often shortened to *les heures sup*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*supplémentaire* is built on *supplément*, from Latin *supplēmentum* "a filling up, a supply" (Lewis & Short); *heure* is inherited from Latin *hōra* "hour".
