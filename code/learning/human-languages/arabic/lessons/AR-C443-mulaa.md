## You'll want to know: ملاءة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C443-MULAA443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ملاءة** — *mulāʾa* — "a bed sheet".

> كانت الملاءة نظيفة وناعمة.
>
> *kānat al-mulāʾa naẓīfa wa-nāʿima.*
>
> The sheet was clean and soft.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملاءة** (*mulāʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-l-ʾ*, "to fill" (Lane). Lane gives *mulāʾa* as a cloth wrapped about the body, often two pieces sewn together; modern Arabic uses it for a bed sheet.
