# తేల్చు (tēlcu) — to sort out; to settle

## You'll want to know: తేల్చు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C425-NIRNAYAM425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తేల్చు** — *tēlcu* — "to sort out; to settle".

> ఆ గొడవను పెద్దలు తేల్చారు.
>
> *ā goḍavanu peddalu tēlcāru.*
>
> The elders settled that quarrel.

Past (I …): తేల్చాను, *tēlcānu*.

తేల్చి చెప్పు, to say plainly, once and for all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తేల్చు** (*tēlcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3464 lists Telugu *tēlucu*, "to cause to float", beside *tēlu*, "to float"; in everyday Telugu it means to settle a matter.
