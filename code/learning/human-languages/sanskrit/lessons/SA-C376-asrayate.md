## You'll want to know: आश्रयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C376-YOGYA376-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्रयते** — *āśrayate* — "takes refuge in; leans on".

> यदि संकटम् अस्ति, तर्हि बालः मातरम् आश्रयते।
>
> *yadi saṅkaṭam asti, tarhi bālaḥ mātaram āśrayate.*
>
> If there is danger, the child takes refuge in its mother.

Past (he …): सः मित्रम् आश्रितवान्, *saḥ mitram āśritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्रयते** (*āśrayate*) is built on *ā* and the root √śri, "to lean on"; MW gives *ā-√śri* "to seek refuge in" and "to depend on" (MW).
