## You'll want to know: لحاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C534-FURNISH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لحاف** (*lahâf*) — "a quilt, a duvet".

> در زمستان زیر لحاف میخوابیم.
>
> *dar zemestân zir-e lahâf mikhâbim.*
>
> In winter we sleep under a quilt.

Plural *lahâfhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lahâf* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *liḥāf*, 'a covering, a wrap'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
