## You'll want to know: शपथ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C543-VACHAN543-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शपथ** — *śapath* — "an oath (formal)".

> नए मंत्री ने शपथ ली।
>
> *Nae mantrī ne śapath lī.*
>
> The new minister took the oath.

Feminine.

A formal word, used for an official oath; everyday speech says क़सम.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शपथ** (*śapath*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *śapatha*, "a curse; an oath" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
