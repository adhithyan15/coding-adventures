## You'll want to know: o voleibol
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C512-PAVILHAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o voleibol** — "volleyball".

> Na praia jogamos voleibol.
>
> *On the beach we play volleyball.*

Also *o vólei*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
