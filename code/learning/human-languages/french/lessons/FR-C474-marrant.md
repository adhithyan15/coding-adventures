## You'll want to know: marrant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C474-GAIETE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**marrant** — "funny (informal)".

> Ton frère est vraiment marrant.
>
> *Your brother is really funny.*

Informal, like *drôle*. *Se marrer* — to laugh.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marrant* is the present participle of *se marrer* "to laugh", a word of familiar French. The origin is not settled.
