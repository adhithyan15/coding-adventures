Italian, chapter 516: Strumenti musicali.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il violoncello — cello.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for skier, then the Italian for chairlift.

You'll want to know: il violoncello.
il violoncello — "cello".
Suono il violoncello da dieci anni. I have played the cello for ten years.
Often shortened to il cello.

The word, taken apart.
violoncello is built on violone "a large viol" with -cello, a little ending (EtymDB): a smaller version of the big viol.

You'll want to know: il flauto.
il flauto — "flute".
A scuola tutti imparano il flauto dolce. At school everyone learns the recorder.
Il flauto dolce — the recorder.

The word, taken apart.
flauto comes from Old Occitan flaüt (EtymDB); the deeper origin is not settled, though it may imitate a breath.

Il vicino suona la tromba la domenica mattina. The neighbour plays the trumpet on Sunday mornings.

The word, taken apart.
tromba is usually traced to a Germanic word trumba "trumpet", which may imitate the sound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la fisarmonica — accordion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for flute, then the Italian for trumpet.

You'll want to know: la fisarmonica.
la fisarmonica — "accordion".
Alla festa un signore suonava la fisarmonica. At the party a man was playing the accordion.
The plural is le fisarmoniche.

The word, taken apart.
fisarmonica is a modern learned word built from Greek phŷsa "bellows, breath" and armonica "harmonica", from Greek harmonía "harmony" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
strimpellare — to strum, to play badly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for trumpet, then the Italian for accordion.

You'll want to know: strimpellare.
strimpellare — "to strum, to play badly".
Strimpello un po' la chitarra, ma non so suonare bene. I strum the guitar a bit, but I cannot play well.
Playing without much skill, for fun.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of strimpellare is not settled; it is usually taken to imitate the sound of plucked strings.
