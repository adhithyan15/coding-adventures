## You'll want to know: বক্তৃতা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C360-BOKTRITA360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বক্তৃতা** — *bôktritā* — "a speech; a lecture".

> কাল কলেজে একটা বক্তৃতা আছে।
>
> *kāl kôleje ekṭā bôktritā āchhe.*
>
> There is a lecture at the college tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বক্তৃতা** (*bôktritā*) is Sanskrit *vaktṛtā*, the gift of speech, eloquence (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the speech itself: a lecture or an address.
