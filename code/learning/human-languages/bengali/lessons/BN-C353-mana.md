## You'll want to know: মানা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C353-RITI353-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মানা** — *mānā* — "to obey; to keep (a custom, a rule)".

> আমরা আমাদের পুরনো রীতি মানি।
>
> *āmrā āmāder purôno rīti māni.*
>
> We keep our old customs.

Past (he/she …): তিনি নিয়ম মানলেন, *tini niyôm mānlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মানা** (*mānā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *mányatē*, thinks or considers; CDIAL 9857 lists Bengali *mānā* 'to consider, obey'. To hold a rule worth heeding is to obey it.
