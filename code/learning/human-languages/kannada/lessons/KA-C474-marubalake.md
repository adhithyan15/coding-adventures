# ಮರುಬಳಕೆ (marubaḷake) — recycling; reuse

## You'll want to know: ಮರುಬಳಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C474-TYAJYA474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರುಬಳಕೆ** — *marubaḷake* — "recycling; reuse".

> ಪ್ಲಾಸ್ಟಿಕ್ ಬಾಟಲಿಗಳ ಮರುಬಳಕೆ ಸಾಧ್ಯ.
>
> *plāsṭik bāṭaligaḷa marubaḷake sādhya.*
>
> Plastic bottles can be recycled.

ಮರು-, again, plus ಬಳಕೆ, use, the noun of ಬಳಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರುಬಳಕೆ** (*marubaḷake*) joins the native prefix *maṟu*, "again" (DEDR 4766), and *baḷake*, "use", the noun of ಬಳಸು.
