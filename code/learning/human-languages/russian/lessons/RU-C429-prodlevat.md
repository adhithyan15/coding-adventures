## You'll want to know: продлевать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C429-ZASELENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**продлевать** (*prodlevát*) — "to extend (a stay, a visa)".

> Каждый месяц он продлевает визу.
>
> *Kázhdyy mésyats on prodleváyet vízu.*
>
> Every month he extends his visa.

Imperfective; perfective *продлить* (*продлю, продлишь*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*продлевать* is built from *про-* and *длить* 'to make long', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dĭliti* (EtymDB).
