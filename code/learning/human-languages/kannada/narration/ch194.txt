Kannada, chapter 194: Salary, Customer, Tailor, Housework, Boss.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಬಳ (sambaḷa) — a salary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a square, then the Kannada for a triangle.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಬಳ.
ಸಂಬಳ — sambaḷa — "a salary".

The word, taken apart.
Where ಸಂಬಳ (sambaḷa) comes from is not settled: DEDR does not record it. It may be Sanskrit śambala, "provisions for a journey" (CDIAL 12315), but CDIAL does not list the Kannada word.

ಗ್ರಾಹಕ — grāhaka — "a customer".

The word, taken apart.
ಗ್ರಾಹಕ (grāhaka) is Sanskrit grāhaka, "one who takes, a taker", taken over whole: the customer is the one who takes the goods.

ದರ್ಜಿ — darji — "a tailor".

ದರ್ಜಿ (darji) is a loan from Persian darzī, "tailor", from darz, "seam", which came through Hindustani.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮನೆಗೆಲಸ (manegelasa) — housework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a customer, then the Kannada for a tailor.

You'll want to know: ಮನೆಗೆಲಸ.
ಮನೆಗೆಲಸ — manegelasa — "housework".

The word, taken apart.
ಮನೆಗೆಲಸ (manegelasa) is native Dravidian: mane, "house" (DEDR 4776), and kelasa, "work" (DEDR 1970), with the k- softened to g- inside the compound.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮೇಲಧಿಕಾರಿ (mēladhikāri) — a boss, a superior.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tailor, then the Kannada for housework.

You'll want to know: ಮೇಲಧಿಕಾರಿ.
ಮೇಲಧಿಕಾರಿ — mēladhikāri — "a boss, a superior".

The word, taken apart.
ಮೇಲಧಿಕಾರಿ (mēladhikāri) is native mēl, "above" (DEDR 5086), and adhikāri, "an official", from Sanskrit adhikārin, "one in charge": "the official above".
