Kannada, chapter 217: Cheap, Empty, Tight, Loose, Thick.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಗ್ಗ (agga) — cheap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for responsibility, then the Kannada for a duty.

You'll want to know: ಅಗ್ಗ.
ಅಗ್ಗ — agga — "cheap".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ಅಗ್ಗ (agga) is uncertain. DEDR has no entry for it in the sense "cheap", CDIAL does not record it, and no source for it is settled.

The word, taken apart.
ಖಾಲಿ (khāli) is a loan from Arabic xālī, "empty, vacant", which came through Persian and Hindustani.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬಿಗಿ (bigi) — tight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for cheap, then the Kannada for empty.

You'll want to know: ಬಿಗಿ.
ಬಿಗಿ — bigi — "tight".

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿಗಿ (bigi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil viku and Telugu bigi (DEDR 5382). DEDR lists bigi itself, "to tighten, bind; tightness".
