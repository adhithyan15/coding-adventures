## You'll want to know: しびれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C448-STIFF448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しびれる** — *shibireru* — "to go numb".

> **たたみの うえで、あしが しびれました。** — *tatami no ue de, ashi ga shibiremashita* — "My legs went numb on the tatami."

Polite **しびれます**, *shibiremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **おとうとの あしが しびれました。**, *otōto no ashi ga shibiremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しびれる** (*shibireru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
