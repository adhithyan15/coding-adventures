## You'll want to know: खात्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C346-KHATRI346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खात्री** — *khātrī* — "certainty; assurance".

> तो येईल याची मला खात्री आहे.
>
> *to yeīl yācī malā khātrī āhe.*
>
> I am sure that he will come.

Feminine.

X ची खात्री असणे, to be sure of X; खात्री करणे, to make sure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खात्री** (*khātrī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *khāṭir*, "mind", itself from Arabic: a mind set at rest.
