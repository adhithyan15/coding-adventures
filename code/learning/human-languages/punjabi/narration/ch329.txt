Punjabi, chapter 329: Parents-in-Law.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਸਹੁਰਾ (sahurā) — a father-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to press gently, then the Punjabi for to put up with.

You'll want to know: ਸਹੁਰਾ.
ਸਹੁਰਾ — sahurā — "a father-in-law".
ਮੇਰਾ ਸਹੁਰਾ ਇੱਕ ਅਧਿਆਪਕ ਹੈ। merā sahurā ikk adhiāpak hai. My father-in-law is a teacher.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਹੁਰਾ (sahurā) is inherited from Sanskrit śvaśura, "a husband's or wife's father"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sahurā, "father-in-law", under it (CDIAL 12753).

What you've built.

ਕੁੜਮ — kuṛam — "a child's father-in-law".
ਸਾਡੇ ਕੁੜਮ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਆਏ। sāḍe kuṛam kall sāḍe ghar āe. Our daughter's father-in-law came to our house yesterday.

ਕੁੜਮ (kuṛam) is inherited from Sanskrit kuṭumbin, "a householder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kuṛam, "a relation by marriage, child's father-in-law", under it (CDIAL 3235).
