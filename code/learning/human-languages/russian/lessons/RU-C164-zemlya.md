## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**земля** ← Proto-Slavic *\*zemja* — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian *žẽmė*, "earth", Avestan *zam-*, Greek *chamaí*, "on the ground", and Latin *humus*, "soil".
