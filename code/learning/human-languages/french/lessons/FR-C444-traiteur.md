## You'll want to know: le traiteur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C444-COMMERCES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le traiteur** — "the caterer; the deli (with ready meals)".

> Pour la fête, nous avons commandé des plats chez le traiteur.
>
> *For the party we ordered dishes from the caterer.*

A shop that sells ready-made dishes and caters for parties.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*traiteur* is built on *traiter* 'to deal with, to serve food to', which is inherited from Latin *tractāre* 'to handle', from *trahere* 'to draw' (Lewis & Short).
