# ఉండి ఉంటే (uṇḍi uṇṭē) — what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-HABITUAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-TE-CONDITIONAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-CONJUNCTIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I used to go", as a man and as a woman (*veḷḷēvāḍini*, *veḷḷēdānni*). Then "if
there is" (*uṇṭē*) and "having come" (*vacci*).

## You'll want to know: -ఇ ఉంటే, 'if … had'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C325-UNREAL-IF-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C72-MARKET-02, TE-LEX-C100-LOC100-04, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-TE-CONDITIONAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-CONJUNCTIVE-01] -->

> **నా దగ్గర డబ్బు ఉండి ఉంటే, నేను వెళ్ళేవాడిని.**
>
> *nā daggara ḍabbu uṇḍi uṇṭē, nēnu veḷḷēvāḍini.* — "If I had had money, I would have gone."

Two pieces from this core, joined: the **-ఇ** form and **ఉంటే**. **ఉండి ఉంటే**, "if there had been";
**వచ్చి ఉంటే**, "if (you) had come"; **చెప్పి ఉంటే**, "if (you) had told". It looks at something that did not
happen. **నా దగ్గర**, "near me", is how Telugu says "I have (it on me)".

## You'll want to know: -ఏవాడిని, 'would have'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C325-WOULD-01]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-HABITUAL-01, TE-LEX-C280-KRIYA280-03, TE-LEX-C75-WHEN-04, TE-LEX-C105-LOC105-02, TE-LEX-C133-THINGS133-05, TE-LEX-C280-KRIYA280-02] -->

The main verb is the form of the habit, from the first chapter of this core: **-ఏవాడిని**, **-ఏదాన్ని**. The
same form says "used to" and "would have"; the **-ఇ ఉంటే** clause in front tells you which.

> **నువ్వు ముందే చెప్పి ఉంటే, నేను వచ్చేదాన్ని.**
>
> *nuvvu mundē ceppi uṇṭē, nēnu vaccēdānni.* — "If you had told me earlier, I would have come."

> **బస్సు సమయానికి వచ్చి ఉంటే, మేము రైలు అందుకునేవాళ్ళం.**
>
> *bassu samayāniki vacci uṇṭē, mēmu railu andukunēvāḷḷaṁ.* — "If the bus had come on time, we would have caught the train."

**ముందే** (*mundē*, "earlier, beforehand") is **ముందు** with **-ఏ**; **సమయానికి** (*samayāniki*, "on time") is **సమయం** with
**-కి**.
