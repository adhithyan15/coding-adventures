## You'll want to know: der Tatort
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C280-VERBRECHEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Tatort** — "the crime scene".

> Die Polizei kam schnell zum Tatort.
>
> *The police came quickly to the crime scene.*

Plural: *die Tatorte*. *Tat* 'deed' plus *Ort* 'place'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tatort* joins *Tat* "deed", Proto-Germanic *dēdiz*, and *Ort* "place", Old High German *ort* "point, place" (EtymDB): the place of the deed.
