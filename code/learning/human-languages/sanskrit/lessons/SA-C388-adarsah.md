## You'll want to know: आदर्शः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C388-VYAVAHARA388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आदर्शः** — *ādarśaḥ* — "an ideal; a model".

> यदि पिता सत्यवादी, तर्हि सः पुत्रस्य आदर्शः भवति।
>
> *yadi pitā satyavādī, tarhi saḥ putrasya ādarśaḥ bhavati.*
>
> If a father is truthful, he is a model for his son.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आदर्शः** (*ādarśaḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √dṛś, "to see"; MW gives *ā-darśa* "a mirror", "a copy" and "ideal perfection" (MW).
