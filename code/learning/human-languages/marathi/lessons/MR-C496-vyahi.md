## You'll want to know: व्याही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C496-SASAR496-05]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याही** — *vyāhī* — "a child's father-in-law".

> लग्नात दोन्ही व्याही आनंदाने भेटले.
>
> *lagnāt donhī vyāhī ānandāne bheṭle.*
>
> At the wedding the two fathers-in-law met happily.

Masculine.

विहीण, a child's mother-in-law: the parents of a married couple are each other's व्याही.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याही** (*vyāhī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *vyāhī*, "son's or daughter's father-in-law", under Sanskrit *vivāhin* (CDIAL 11925), a word of marriage.
