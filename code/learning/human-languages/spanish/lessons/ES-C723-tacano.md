## You'll want to know: tacaño
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C723-GENEROSO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**tacaño** — "stingy, mean".

> No seas tacaño y paga la cena.
>
> *Do not be stingy, pay for dinner.*

The opposite of *generoso*. Feminine *tacaña*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tacaño* was borrowed from Italian *taccagno* "stingy" (EtymDB); further back the origin is not certain.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
