## You'll want to know: ਉੱਤਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C271-COMPASS271-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਤਰ** — *uttar* — "the north".

> ਪਹਾੜ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਉੱਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਨ।
>
> *pahāṛ piṇḍ de uttar vich han.*
>
> The mountains are to the north of the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਤਰ** (*uttar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uttara*, "upper, northern"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *uttar*, "the north", under it (CDIAL 1767).
