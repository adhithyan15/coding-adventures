## You'll want to know: annegare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C305-ALLUVIONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**annegare** — "to drown".

> Un uomo è annegato nel fiume.
>
> *A man drowned in the river.*

With *essere*: *è annegato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*annegare* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *adnecāre* (EtymDB), from Latin *necāre* "to kill": first, to kill by water.
