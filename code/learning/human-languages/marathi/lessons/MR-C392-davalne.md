## You'll want to know: डावलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C392-KALAM392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**डावलणे** — *ḍāvalṇe* — "to pass over; to sideline".

> निवड करताना त्यांनी अनुभवी लोकांना डावललं.
>
> *nivaḍ kartānā tyānnī anubhvī lokānnā ḍāvallaṁ.*
>
> While choosing, they passed over experienced people.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने डावललं, *tyāne ḍāvallaṁ*.

The one passed over takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डावलणे** (*ḍāvalṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
