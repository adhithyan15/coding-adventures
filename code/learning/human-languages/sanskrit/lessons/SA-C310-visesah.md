## You'll want to know: विशेषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C310-GHATANA310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विशेषः** — *viśeṣaḥ* — "a detail; a particular".

> वार्तायां कः विशेषः अस्ति?
>
> *vārtāyāṃ kaḥ viśeṣaḥ asti?*
>
> What is the special point in the news?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विशेषः** (*viśeṣaḥ*) is built from *vi* and the root √śiṣ, "to leave, distinguish"; MW gives *viśeṣa* as a distinction, a peculiar mark, a peculiarity (MW).
