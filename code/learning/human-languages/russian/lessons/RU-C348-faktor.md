## You'll want to know: фактор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C348-POVOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**фактор** (*fáktor*) — "a factor".

> Погода — важный фактор для туризма.
>
> *Pogóda — vázhnyy fáktor dlya turízma.*
>
> The weather is an important factor for tourism.

Masculine. *Человеческий фактор* — the human factor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**фактор** is usually traced, through German, to Latin *factor* "a maker, a doer" (Lewis & Short), built on *facere* "to do, to make". A factor is something that does part of the work.
