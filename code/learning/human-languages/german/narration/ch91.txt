German, chapter 91: Strong, Weak, Rich, Ugly, Sweet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
stark — strong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for low, then the German for empty.

You'll want to know: stark.
stark — "strong".

The word, taken apart.
stark from Old High German stark from Proto-Germanic starkuz, "stiff, strong" — inherited, the same word as English stark. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for strong, then the German for weak.

You'll want to know: reich.
reich — "rich".

The word, taken apart.
reich from Old High German rīhhi, "mighty" from Proto-Germanic rīkijaz, from a Celtic word for "king" — inherited, the same word as English rich. (EtymDB 2.1) Power came first, wealth after.

The word, taken apart.
hässlich from Middle High German hezzelich, "hateful" (EtymDB 2.1): the word for "hate" + -lich. What was hateful came to mean ugly.
