## You'll want to know: गोंगाट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C422-MANDAI422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंगाट** — *goṅgāṭ* — "noise; a din".

> वर्गात खूप गोंगाट होता.
>
> *vargāt khūp goṅgāṭ hotā.*
>
> There was a lot of noise in the classroom.

Masculine.

गोंगाट करणे, to make a din.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंगाट** (*goṅgāṭ*) is a Marathi word that imitates loud chatter; its origin is not settled.
