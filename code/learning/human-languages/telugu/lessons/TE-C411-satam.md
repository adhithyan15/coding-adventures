## You'll want to know: శాతం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C411-SATAM411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**శాతం** — *śātaṁ* — "a percentage; per cent".

> మా తరగతిలో తొంభై శాతం మంది పాసయ్యారు.
>
> *mā taragatilō tombhai śātaṁ mandi pāsayyāru.*
>
> Ninety per cent of our class passed.

With numbers spelt out: యాభై శాతం, fifty per cent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శాతం** (*śātaṁ*) goes back to Sanskrit *śata*, "a hundred" (CDIAL 12278): so many out of a hundred.
