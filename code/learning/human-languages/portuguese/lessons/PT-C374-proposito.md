## You'll want to know: o propósito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C374-INTENCAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o propósito** — "the purpose, the aim".

> Qual é o propósito desta reunião?
>
> *What is the purpose of this meeting?*

*De propósito* — on purpose; *a propósito* — by the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*propósito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōpositum* "a plan, a purpose" (Lewis & Short), the participle of *prōpōnere* "to set before", on *pōnere* "to put".
