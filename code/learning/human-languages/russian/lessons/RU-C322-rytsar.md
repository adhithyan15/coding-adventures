## You'll want to know: рыцарь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C322-RYTSAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**рыцарь** (*rýtsar*) — "a knight".

> Рыцарь сел на коня и уехал.
>
> *Rýtsar sel na konyá i uyékhal.*
>
> The knight got on his horse and rode off.

Genitive *рыцаря*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рыцарь* was borrowed through Polish *rycerz* from German *Ritter* "a rider, a knight" (EtymDB).
