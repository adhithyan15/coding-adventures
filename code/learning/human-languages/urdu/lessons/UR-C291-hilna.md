## You'll want to know: ہلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C291-ZALZALA291-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ہلنا** — *hilnā* — "to shake (by itself); to move".

> رات کو زمین ہلی۔
>
> *rāt ko zamīn hilī.*
>
> The ground shook in the night.

Past (it …): وہ زمین ہلی, *voh zamīn hilī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہلنا** (*hilnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *hilnā*, "to shake", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 14120). It is a thing moving by itself: the ground, a tooth, a leaf.
