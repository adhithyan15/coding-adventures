Portuguese, chapter 507: Rodas e montanhas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o skate — skateboard; skateboarding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for board, then the Portuguese for to surf.

You'll want to know: o skate.
o skate — "skateboard; skateboarding".
O rapaz vai para a escola de skate. The boy goes to school on his skateboard.
Andar de skate — to skateboard.

The word, taken apart.
skate was borrowed from English skate (EtymDB), which came from Dutch schaats.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with o skate]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.

What does o skate mean? ("Skateboard; skateboarding".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
patinar — to skate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to surf, then the Portuguese for skateboard.

You'll want to know: patinar.
patinar — "to skate".
No inverno patinamos no gelo. In winter we skate on the ice.
Of a car: to skid. O carro patinou na estrada.

The word, taken apart.
patinar is usually traced to French patiner "to skate", built on patte "a paw", a word whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
a caminhada — (long) walk, hike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to skate, then the Portuguese for skates.

You'll want to know: a caminhada.
a caminhada — "(long) walk, hike".
Fizemos uma caminhada de dez quilómetros. We went on a ten-kilometre walk.
Fazer uma caminhada — to go for a walk or a hike.

The word, taken apart.
caminhada is built on caminho "path", inherited from Late Latin camminus "a road", which is usually traced to a Celtic word.
