## You'll want to know: o santo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C319-SANTOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o santo** — "the saint".

> Cada aldeia tem o seu santo.
>
> *Every village has its own saint.*

Also an adjective, ‘holy’: *a água santa*. Before a name it shortens to *São*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*santo* is inherited from Latin *sānctus* "holy, sacred" (EtymDB), from *sancīre* "to make sacred".
