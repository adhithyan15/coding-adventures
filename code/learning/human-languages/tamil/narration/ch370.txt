6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
வறுமை (vaṟumai) — poverty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a citizen, then the Tamil for to classify.

You'll want to know: வறுமை.
வறுமை — vaṟumai — "poverty".
பல குடும்பங்கள் இன்னும் வறுமையில் வாழ்கின்றன. pala kuṭumpaṅkaḷ iṉṉum vaṟumaiyil vāḻkiṉṟaṉa. Many families still live in poverty.
வறுமையில், in poverty. ஏழை, poor, is the word for the person.

The word, taken apart.
வறுமை (vaṟumai) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5513 lists it in the family of veṟu, "empty", with veṟumai, "emptiness, poverty": an empty hand.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு (ēṟṟattāḻvu) — inequality; ups and downs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for poverty, then the Tamil for wealth.

You'll want to know: ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு.
ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு — ēṟṟattāḻvu — "inequality; ups and downs".
சமூகத்தில் ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு குறைய வேண்டும். camūkattil ēṟṟattāḻvu kuṟaiya vēṇṭum. Inequality in society must come down.
Literally a rising and a falling (தாழ்வு): highs and lows.

The word, taken apart.
ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு (ēṟṟattāḻvu) is native: ēṟṟam, "mounting, rising", from ēṟu, "to rise" (DEDR 916), and tāḻvu, "lowness", from tāḻ, "to be low" (DEDR 3178).

Lesson 4 of 6.
வேலையின்மை (vēlaiyiṉmai) — unemployment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for wealth, then the Tamil for inequality.

You'll want to know: வேலையின்மை.
வேலையின்மை — vēlaiyiṉmai — "unemployment".
இந்த ஊரில் வேலையின்மை ஒரு பெரிய பிரச்சினை. inta ūril vēlaiyiṉmai oru periya piracciṉai. Unemployment is a big problem in this town.
வேலை, work + இன்மை, absence: -இன்மை makes nouns of not having.

The word, taken apart.
வேலையின்மை (vēlaiyiṉmai) is built from vēlai, "work", and native iṉmai, "non-existence, absence", from il, "not to be" (DEDR 2559).

Lesson 5 of 6.
சுரண்டு (curaṇṭu) — to exploit; to scrape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for inequality, then the Tamil for unemployment.

You'll want to know: சுரண்டு.
சுரண்டு — curaṇṭu — "to exploit; to scrape".
அந்த முதலாளி தொழிலாளர்களைச் சுரண்டினார். anta mutalāḷi toḻilāḷarkaḷaic curaṇṭiṉār. That employer exploited the workers.
Past (I …): சுரண்டினேன், curaṇṭiṉēṉ.
Who is exploited takes -ஐ. Its first sense is to scrape, as a burnt pot is scraped clean.

The word, taken apart.
சுரண்டு (curaṇṭu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1564 lists Tamil curaṇṭu, "to scrape", in the family of cirai, "to shave, cut"; to exploit is to scrape someone bare.
