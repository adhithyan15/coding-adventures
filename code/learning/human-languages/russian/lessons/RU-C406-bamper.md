## You'll want to know: бампер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C406-BAGAZHNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**бампер** (*bámper*) — "a bumper".

> После аварии бампер пришлось поменять.
>
> *Pósle avárii bámper prishlós pomenyát.*
>
> After the accident the bumper had to be replaced.

Masculine; the stress is on the first syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бампер* was borrowed from English *bumper* (EtymDB), the bar that takes the bump.
