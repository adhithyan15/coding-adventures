# jiàngjià — to get cheaper; to come down in price

## You'll want to know: jiàngjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C193-PRICE193-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàngjià* — "to get cheaper; to come down in price".

> *diànnǎo jiàngjià le* — Computers have got cheaper.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàngjià* is built from *jiàng* 'to come down' and *jià* 'price', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewngH* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go down' and 'to fall'.

## What you've built
