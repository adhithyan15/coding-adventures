6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
o painel — the board, the panel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the train driver, then the Portuguese for the engine.

You'll want to know: o painel.
o painel — "the board, the panel".
O painel da estação mostra as partidas. The station board shows the departures.
O painel solar — the solar panel. O painel of a car is the dashboard.

The word, taken apart.
painel came from Old French panel, from Vulgar Latin pannellus, a small form of Latin pannus 'piece of cloth' (EtymDB).

O comboio tem um atraso de vinte minutos. The train is twenty minutes late.
Estar atrasado — to be late. Com atraso — late.

The word, taken apart.
atraso is built on atrasar, which is built on atrás 'behind' (from a and trás) with -ar (EtymDB); trás goes back to Latin trāns 'across, beyond'.

Lesson 4 of 6.
suprimir — to cancel (a service); to suppress, to remove.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: suprimir.
suprimir — "to cancel (a service); to suppress, to remove".
Por causa da greve, suprimiram três comboios. Because of the strike, they cancelled three trains.
On the board: comboio suprimido — train cancelled.

The word, taken apart.
suprimir is a learned borrowing from Latin supprimere 'to press down, to hold back' (EtymDB), built on premere 'to press'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
o transbordo — the change (of trains or buses), the transfer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to delay, then the Portuguese for to cancel.

You'll want to know: o transbordo.
o transbordo — "the change (of trains or buses), the transfer".
Esta viagem tem um transbordo a meio do caminho. This trip has a change of trains halfway.
Fazer transbordo — to change trains.

The word, taken apart.
transbordo is built from trans- 'across' and bordo 'ship's side': first moving cargo from one ship to another. Bordo goes back to Old Portuguese bordo (EtymDB) and is usually traced to a Germanic word for 'plank, side'.
