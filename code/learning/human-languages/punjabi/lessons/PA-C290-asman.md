## You'll want to know: ਅਸਮਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C290-WEEK290-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਸਮਾਨ** — *asmān* — "the sky".

> ਅੱਜ ਅਸਮਾਨ ਨੀਲਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ajj asmān nīlā hai.*
>
> Today the sky is blue.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਸਮਾਨ** (*asmān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *āsmān*, "sky".
