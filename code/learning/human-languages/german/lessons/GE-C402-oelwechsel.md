## You'll want to know: der Ölwechsel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C402-TANKEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ölwechsel** — "the oil change".

> Nach dem Ölwechsel fährt das Auto wieder ruhig.
>
> *After the oil change the car runs smoothly again.*

Same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ölwechsel* is built from *Öl* 'oil', an early borrowing through Old High German *oli* from Latin *oleum* (EtymDB), and *Wechsel* 'change', inherited from Old High German *wehsal* (EtymDB).
