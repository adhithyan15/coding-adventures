## You'll want to know: сиденье
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C406-BAGAZHNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**сиденье** (*sidénye*) — "a seat (in a car or a bus)".

> Дети сидят на заднем сиденье.
>
> *Déti sidyát na zádnem sidénye.*
>
> The children are sitting on the back seat.

Neuter. Also spelled *сидение*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сиденье* is built on *сидеть* 'to sit', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sěděti* 'to sit' (EtymDB).
