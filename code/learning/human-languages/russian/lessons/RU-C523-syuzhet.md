## You'll want to know: сюжет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C523-PLOT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сюжет** (*syuzhét*) — "a plot".

> У этого романа интересный сюжет.
>
> *U étogo romána interésnyy syuzhét.*
>
> This novel has an interesting plot.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сюжет* was borrowed from French *sujet* 'subject', which comes from Latin *subiectum* (EtymDB), from *subicere* 'to place under'.
