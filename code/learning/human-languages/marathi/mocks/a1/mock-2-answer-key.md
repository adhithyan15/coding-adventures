## Writing

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
Score six independently completed fields. Accept the learner's own consistent
details or internally consistent invented details. The requested field type
must be respected, and no visible model may be copied. Accept recognizable
Devanagari and documented regional Marathi forms. Apply the 40-point form
rubric.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->
The response must contain 30–40 Marathi words, address **नील**, introduce the
writer, name a city and language, state one routine or preference, invite the
reader to a familiar place at a stated time, and close appropriately. Accept
natural first-person gender choices when internally consistent. Apply the
60-point message rubric; copied or romanized text is not independent Marathi
writing evidence.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-reader-purpose-message -->

## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
Score five relevant personal responses across the combined 60–120 second
window. Accept natural regional vocabulary and internally consistent gender
agreement. Each response must address the question asked.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-interview -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
The learner must sustain a one-minute description of a familiar person or
place, organize at least three relevant details, and speak from keywords rather
than a written answer script. Apply the 30-point speaking criteria.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-prepared-description -->
<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
The learner must greet, request a book, ask the price, answer the clarification,
and close politely within 60–120 seconds. Accept a comprehensible taught request
and documented regional alternative without requiring one memorized sentence.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-transactional-role-play -->
