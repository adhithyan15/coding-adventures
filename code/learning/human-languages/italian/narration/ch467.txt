Italian, chapter 467: Il temporale.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il lampo — the flash of lightning, the flash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the drought, then the Italian for the breeze.

You'll want to know: il lampo.
il lampo — "the flash of lightning, the flash".
Ho visto un lampo sopra le colline. I saw a flash of lightning over the hills.
In un lampo — in a flash.

The word, taken apart.
lampo goes with lampa 'lamp', which EtymDB traces through Old French lampe to Late Latin lampada, from Greek lampás 'torch'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
lampeggiare — to flash (with lightning); to blink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the flash of lightning, then the Italian for to thunder.

You'll want to know: lampeggiare.
lampeggiare — "to flash (with lightning); to blink".
Lampeggia lontano, sul mare. Lightning is flashing far off, over the sea.
In a car: la freccia lampeggia — the indicator is flashing.

The word, taken apart.
lampeggiare is built on lampo 'flash', which goes with lampa 'lamp'; EtymDB traces lampa through Old French lampe to Late Latin lampada, from Greek lampás 'torch'.
