## You'll want to know: आपदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C302-AILAN302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आपदा** — *āpdā* — "a disaster; a calamity".

> पिछले साल गाँव में बाढ़ की बड़ी आपदा आई।
>
> *pichle sāl gā̃v meṁ bāṛh kī baṛī āpdā āī.*
>
> Last year a great flood disaster struck the village.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is Sanskrit *āpad*, "misfortune, distress" (Monier-Williams), with the feminine ending Hindi gives it. It is the word of the news and of government: a flood, a storm or an earthquake is an **आपदा**.
