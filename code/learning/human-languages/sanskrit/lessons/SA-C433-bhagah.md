## You'll want to know: भागः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C433-AYA433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भागः** — *bhāgaḥ* — "a share".

> मम भागः अल्पः अस्ति।
>
> *mama bhāgaḥ alpaḥ asti.*
>
> My share is small.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भागः** (*bhāgaḥ*) comes from the root √bhaj, "to share, to divide", which also gives *bhajati*, "shares"; MW gives *bhāga* as a part, a portion, a share (MW).
