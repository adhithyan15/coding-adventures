## You'll want to know: a inscrição
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C376-FACULDADE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a inscrição** — "the enrolment, the registration; the inscription".

> A inscrição no curso custa cinquenta euros.
>
> *Enrolment on this course costs fifty euros.*

*Fazer a inscrição* — to enrol, to sign up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inscrição* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnscrīptiō* "a writing upon, an inscription, a title" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *īnscrībere*, on *scrībere* "to write": your name written on the list.
