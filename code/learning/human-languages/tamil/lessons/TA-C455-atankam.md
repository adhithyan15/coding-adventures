# ஆதங்கம் (ātaṅkam) — frustration; anguished regret

## You'll want to know: ஆதங்கம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C455-KAVALAI455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆதங்கம்** — *ātaṅkam* — "frustration; anguished regret".

> மகன் படிக்கவில்லை என்று அப்பாவுக்கு ஆதங்கம்.
>
> *makaṉ paṭikkavillai eṉṟu appāvukku ātaṅkam.*
>
> The father is anguished that his son did not study.

A frustrated wish for someone else: … என்று ஆதங்கம், anguish that ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆதங்கம்** (*ātaṅkam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ātaṅka*, "disease; affliction, anxiety" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
