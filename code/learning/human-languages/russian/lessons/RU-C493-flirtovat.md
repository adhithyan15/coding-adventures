## You'll want to know: флиртовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C493-SWEETHEARTS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**флиртовать** (*flirtovát*) — "to flirt".

> На вечеринке он флиртовал со всеми.
>
> *Na vecherínke on flirtovál so vsémi.*
>
> At the party he flirted with everyone.

Imperfective; present флиртую. *Флиртовать с кем*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*флиртовать* is built on *флирт*, which was borrowed from English *flirt* (EtymDB), with the verb ending *-овать*; EtymDB has no entry for the verb.
