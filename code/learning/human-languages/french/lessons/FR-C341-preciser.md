## You'll want to know: préciser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C341-MALENTENDU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**préciser** — "to specify, to make precise".

> Il a précisé l'heure de la réunion dans son message.
>
> *He specified the time of the meeting in his message.*

*Préciser que* — to point out exactly. From *précis*, precise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*préciser* is built on *précis* "precise" (EtymDB), from Latin *praecīsus* "cut off, abridged", the participle of *praecīdere* "to cut off in front" (Lewis & Short).
