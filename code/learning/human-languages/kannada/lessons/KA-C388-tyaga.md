## You'll want to know: ತ್ಯಾಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C388-NAITIKA388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತ್ಯಾಗ** — *tyāga* — "a sacrifice".

> ತಾಯಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳಿಗಾಗಿ ತುಂಬಾ ತ್ಯಾಗ ಮಾಡಿದಳು.
>
> *tāyi makkaḷigāgi tumbā tyāga māḍidaḷu.*
>
> The mother made many sacrifices for her children.

ತ್ಯಾಗ ಮಾಡು, to make a sacrifice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತ್ಯಾಗ** (*tyāga*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tyāga*, "leaving, giving up" (CDIAL 5985).
