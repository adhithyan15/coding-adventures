Hindi, chapter 418: Near and Across.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नज़दीक (nazdīk) — close by; near.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a route, then the Hindi for the way something faces.

You'll want to know: नज़दीक.
नज़दीक — nazdīk — "close by; near".
मेरा घर स्टेशन के नज़दीक है। merā ghar sṭeśan ke nazdīk hai. My house is close to the station.

The word, taken apart.
नज़दीक (nazdīk) is usually traced to Persian nazdīk, "near"; like पास it follows के: घर के नज़दीक.

Lesson 2 of 5.
निकट (nikaṭ) — near (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the way something faces, then the Hindi for close by.

You'll want to know: निकट.
निकट — nikaṭ — "near (formal)".
होटल स्टेशन के निकट है। hoṭal sṭeśan ke nikaṭ hai. The hotel is near the station.

The word, taken apart.
निकट (nikaṭ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit nikaṭa, "being at the side, near" (Monier-Williams); it is the formal partner of नज़दीक (nazdīk) and पास.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पार (pār) — across; the far side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for close by, then the Hindi for near (formal).

You'll want to know: पार.
पार — pār — "across; the far side".
हमने नाव से नदी पार की। hamne nāv se nadī pār kī. We crossed the river by boat.
पार (pār) करना, to cross; नदी के पार (pār), across the river; उस पार (pār), on the far side.

The word, taken apart.
पार (pār) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit pāra, "the further bank, the far end" (CDIAL 8100), which lists Hindi pār, "on the other side of, across".

Lesson 4 of 5.
बग़ल (baġal) — the side; beside (with में).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for near (formal), then the Hindi for across.

You'll want to know: बग़ल.
बग़ल — baġal — "the side; beside (with में)".
बैंक के बग़ल में एक दवाख़ाना है। baiṅk ke baġal meṁ ek davākhānā hai. There is a pharmacy next to the bank.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
बग़ल (baġal) is usually traced to Persian baġal, "the armpit, the side of the body": what is in your armpit is right beside you.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पहुँचाना (pahũcānā) — to take (someone) to; to deliver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for across, then the Hindi for beside.

You'll want to know: पहुँचाना.
पहुँचाना — pahũcānā — "to take (someone) to; to deliver".
ड्राइवर ने हमें होटल तक पहुँचाया। ḍrāivar ne hamẽ hoṭal tak pahũcāyā. The driver took us all the way to the hotel.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चिट्ठी घर तक पहुँचाई, usne ciṭṭhī ghar tak pahũcāī.

The word, taken apart.
पहुँचाना (pahũcānā) is the causative partner of पहुँचना, "to arrive", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit prabhūta, "much, great" (CDIAL 8716): to make someone or something arrive.
