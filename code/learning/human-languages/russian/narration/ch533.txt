Russian, chapter 533: Small Repairs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
шуруп (shurúp) — a screw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a power drill, then the Russian for a screwdriver.

You'll want to know: шуруп.
шуруп (shurúp) — "a screw".
Для полки нужны четыре шурупа. Dlya pólki nuzhný chetýre shurúpa. The shelf needs four screws.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
шуруп (shurúp) was borrowed from German Schraube 'a screw' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
плоскогубцы (ploskogúbtsy) — pliers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a screwdriver, then the Russian for a screw.

You'll want to know: плоскогубцы.
плоскогубцы (ploskogúbtsy) — "pliers".
Плоскогубцы лежат в ящике с инструментами. Ploskogúbtsy lezhát v yáshchike s instruméntami. The pliers are in the toolbox.
Plural only.

The word, taken apart.
плоскогубцы (ploskogúbtsy) is a compound of плоский 'flat' and губа 'lip': pliers with flat jaws; EtymDB has no entry.

откручивать (otkrúchivat) — "to unscrew".
Папа откручивает старую лампочку. Pápa otkrúchivayet stáruyu lámpochku. Dad is unscrewing the old light bulb.
Imperfective; perfective открутить (откручу).

The word, taken apart.
откручивать (otkrúchivat) is built on от- 'off' and крутить 'to twist, to turn'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
изолента (izolénta) — insulating tape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to unscrew, then the Russian for to burn out.

You'll want to know: изолента.
изолента (izolénta) — "insulating tape".
Он обмотал провод изолентой. On obmotál próvod izoléntoy. He wrapped the wire in insulating tape.
Feminine. Short for изоляционная лента.

The word, taken apart.
изолента (izolénta) is a clipped compound of изоляционная 'insulating' and лента 'a ribbon, a tape'; EtymDB has no entry.
