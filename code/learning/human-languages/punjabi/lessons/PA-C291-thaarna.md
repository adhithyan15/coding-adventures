# ਠਾਰਨਾ (ṭhārnā) — to cool something down

## You'll want to know: ਠਾਰਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C291-WARM291-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠਾਰਨਾ** — *ṭhārnā* — "to cool something down".

> ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚੇ ਲਈ ਦੁੱਧ ਠਾਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ bachche laī dudh ṭhārdā hāṁ.*
>
> I cool the milk down for the child.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਠਾਰਿਆ, *maiṁ dudh ṭhāriā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠਾਰਨਾ** (*ṭhārnā*) is the causative partner of *ṭharnā*, inherited from a reconstructed *\*sthārayati*, "makes firm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭhārnā*, "to cool", under it (CDIAL 13742).
