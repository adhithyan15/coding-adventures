Sanskrit, chapter 486: Fasting and Meditation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उपवसति (upavasati) — fasts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for relief, then the Sanskrit for a rejuvenating tonic.

You'll want to know: उपवसति.
उपवसति — upavasati — "fasts".
रुग्णः बालः अद्य उपवसति। rugṇaḥ bālaḥ adya upavasati. The sick child fasts today.
Past (he …): सः उपोषितवान्, saḥ upoṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपवसति (upavasati) is built from upa and the root √vas, "to dwell", the root of वसति: to stay in abstinence; MW gives "to abstain from food, fast" (MW).

You'll want to know: प्राणायामः.
प्राणायामः — prāṇāyāmaḥ — "breath control".
पिता प्रतिदिनं प्राणायामं करोति। pitā pratidinaṃ prāṇāyāmaṃ karoti. Father does breath control every day.

The word, taken apart.
प्राणायामः (prāṇāyāmaḥ) is built from prāṇa, "breath", and āyāma, "restraint", from ā and the root √yam; MW names it as the "breath-exercises" of the Saṃdhyā (MW).
