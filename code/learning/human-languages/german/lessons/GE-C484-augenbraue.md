## You'll want to know: die Augenbraue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C484-GESICHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Augenbraue** — "eyebrow".

> Überrascht hob sie die Augenbrauen.
>
> *In surprise she raised her eyebrows.*

Plural: *die Augenbrauen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Augenbraue* is inherited from Middle High German *ougebrā*, a compound of *ouge* "eye" and *brā* "brow" (EtymDB).
