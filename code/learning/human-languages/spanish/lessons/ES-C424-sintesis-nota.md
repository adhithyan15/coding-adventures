# una nota con todos los signos — the marks doing their jobs at once

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTH-C424-03, ES-ORTH-C424-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say how a Spanish note opens after the name, and say whether a list
of three takes a comma before its *y*.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTH-C424-03, ES-ORTH-C424-02, ES-ORTH-C424-06, ES-ORTH-C424-08, ES-ORTH-C424-01] -->

Read this note and count the marks. Every word in it has been taught:

> Querida Ana:
>
> Mañana voy al teatro con mi hermana (la mayor). Vamos a ver una película, una
> exposición y un concierto. Mi dirección es c/ Mayor, 5.
>
> Un abrazo,
> Marta

Six of the eight marks are at work: the colon after the name, commas inside the
list with none before the *y*, a parenthesis around the aside, a slash in the
address, and points closing the sentences.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTH-C424-04, ES-ORTH-C424-05] -->

And the two the note had no use for, doing their own jobs:

> — ¿Vamos al cine?
> — Sí. Traigo un «sandwich».

The first line opens with **la raya** because it is a turn of speech. The
borrowed word sits inside **las comillas** because it is not naturalised.
