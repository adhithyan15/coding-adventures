## You'll want to know: soffiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C469-TEMPESTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**soffiare** — "to blow".

> Il vento soffia forte dal nord.
>
> *The wind is blowing hard from the north.*

*Soffiarsi il naso* — to blow your nose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soffiare* is inherited from Latin *sufflāre* 'to blow into' (EtymDB), from *flāre* 'to blow'.
