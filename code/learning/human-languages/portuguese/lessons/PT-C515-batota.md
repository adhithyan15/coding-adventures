## You'll want to know: a batota
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C515-LESAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a batota** — "cheating (at games)".

> O meu irmão faz batota nas cartas.
>
> *My brother cheats at cards.*

*Fazer batota* — to cheat. European Portuguese; in Brazil, *trapaça*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
