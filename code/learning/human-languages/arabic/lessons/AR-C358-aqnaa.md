## You'll want to know: أقنع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C358-HUJJA358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أقنع** — *aqnaʿa* — "to convince".

> أقنعت أختي أبي بشراء سيارة جديدة.
>
> *aqnaʿat ukhtī abī bi-shirāʾ sayyāra jadīda.*
>
> My sister convinced my father to buy a new car.

Present: يقنع, *yuqniʿ*.

What you convince someone of comes after بـ: أقنعه بـ ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أقنع** (*aqnaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-n-ʿ*, "to be content, satisfied" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make content: to bring someone round to an idea.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
