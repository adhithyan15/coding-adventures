# কমা (kômā) — to fall; to decrease

## You'll want to know: কমা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C372-BARA372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কমা** — *kômā* — "to fall; to decrease".

> জ্বর না কমলে ডাক্তার ডাকো।
>
> *jbôr nā kômle ḍāktār ḍāko.*
>
> If the fever doesn't come down, call a doctor.

Past (he/she …): সেই জ্বর কমল, *sei jbôr kômlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কমা** (*kômā*) is a Bengali verb made on কম, less, usually traced to Persian *kam*. It is the partner of the taught কমানো: a fever কমে by itself; medicine কমায় it.
