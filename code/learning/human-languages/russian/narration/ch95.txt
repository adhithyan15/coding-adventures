Russian, chapter 95: Bathtub, Shelf, Curtain, Pillow, Blanket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ванна (vánna) — a bathtub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cooker, then the Russian for a shower.

You'll want to know: ванна.
ванна (vánna) — "a bathtub".

The word, taken apart.
ванна (vánna) comes from German Wanne, "a tub", or French vanne (Vasmer); Trubachev notes it by 1697.

The word, taken apart.
полка (pólka) comes from полица, "a shelf", built on пол, "floor, board" (Vasmer), which Vasmer relates to Sanskrit phálakam, "a board, a plank".

The word, taken apart.
занавеска (zanavéska) is занавес, "a curtain", with -ка, "little". занавес is за-, "across", with навес, "what is hung", from вешать, "to hang".

Lesson 4 of 5.
подушка (podúshka) — a pillow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a shelf, then the Russian for a curtain.

You'll want to know: подушка.
подушка (podúshka) — "a pillow".

The word, taken apart.
подушка (podúshka) from Proto-Slavic podušĭka. Vasmer reports the usual link with дух, "breath": a pillow as "a thing blown up". Machek's reading, под ухо, "under the ear", has been rejected by others.

Lesson 5 of 5.
одеяло (odeyálo) — a blanket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a curtain, then the Russian for a pillow.

You'll want to know: одеяло.
одеяло (odeyálo) — "a blanket".

The word, taken apart.
одеяло (odeyálo) comes from Old Russian одеяти, "to cover, to clothe", the repeated-action form of одеть, "to dress" (Vasmer): a blanket is what covers you.
