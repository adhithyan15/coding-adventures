# ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਏ? — where did they go yesterday?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KALL-216, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-Q-KITTHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *yesterday*, then *where?* Then *the girl went to school*.

## Grammar Lens: asking where
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-Q-KITTHE-01, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-LEX-KALL-216, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-E-206] -->

**ਕਿੱਥੇ** goes right before the went form, where the place will go in the answer:

> **ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਏ?** — *uh kallh kitthe gae?* — Where did they go yesterday?

> **ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਗਏ।** — *uh kallh bāzār gae.* — They went to the market yesterday.

> **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਿਆ?** — *Aman kallh kitthe giā?* — Where did Aman go yesterday?
