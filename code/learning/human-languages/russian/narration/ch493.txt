Russian, chapter 493: Sweethearts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
влюблённый (vlyublyónnyy) — in love; a lover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for twins, then the Russian for related as cousins.

You'll want to know: влюблённый.
влюблённый (vlyublyónnyy) — "in love; a lover".
Влюблённые гуляли по парку до утра. Vlyublyónnyye gulyáli po párku do utrá. The lovers walked in the park until morning.
From влюбиться — to fall in love. Влюблён в кого — in love with someone.

The word, taken apart.
влюблённый (vlyublyónnyy) is the past participle of влюбиться 'to fall in love', built on в 'into' and любить 'to love'; EtymDB lists the participle without a source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
поцелуй (potselúy) — a kiss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for related as cousins, then the Russian for in love.

You'll want to know: поцелуй.
поцелуй (potselúy) — "a kiss".
Она на всю жизнь запомнила первый поцелуй. Oná na vsyu zhizn zapómnila pérvyy potselúy. She remembered her first kiss for the rest of her life.
Masculine; genitive поцелуя. The verb is целовать — to kiss.

The word, taken apart.
поцелуй (potselúy) is built on целовать 'to kiss', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic cělovati 'to greet; to kiss', derived from cělъ 'whole', as if a kiss once wished someone good health; EtymDB has no entry for поцелуй (potselúy).

Lesson 3 of 5.
флиртовать (flirtovát) — to flirt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for in love, then the Russian for a kiss.

You'll want to know: флиртовать.
флиртовать (flirtovát) — "to flirt".
На вечеринке он флиртовал со всеми. Na vecherínke on flirtovál so vsémi. At the party he flirted with everyone.
Imperfective; present флиртую. Флиртовать с кем.

The word, taken apart.
флиртовать (flirtovát) is built on флирт, which was borrowed from English flirt (EtymDB), with the verb ending -овать; EtymDB has no entry for the verb.

Lesson 4 of 5.
нежность (nézhnost) — tenderness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a kiss, then the Russian for to flirt.

You'll want to know: нежность.
нежность (nézhnost) — "tenderness".
Бабушка смотрела на внука с нежностью. Bábushka smotréla na vnúka s nézhnostyu. Grandma gazed at her grandson with tenderness.
Feminine. Built on нежный — tender, gentle.

The word, taken apart.
нежность (nézhnost) is built on нежный 'tender' with -ость; EtymDB has no entry, and нежный is usually traced to Proto-Slavic něžьnъ.
