## You'll want to know: gǎndòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C271-MOOD271-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎndòng* — "to move; to touch someone's heart".

> *zhège gùshi gǎndòngle dàjiā* — This story moved everyone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎndòng* is built from *gǎn* and *dòng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *komX* and *duwngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move' and the second 'to stir'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
