## You'll want to know: महक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C480-SPARSH480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**महक** — *mahak* — "an aroma; a scent".

> बारिश के बाद मिट्टी की महक आती है।
>
> *bāriś ke bād miṭṭī kī mahak ātī hai.*
>
> After the rain there is a scent of earth.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महक** (*mahak*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *mahak*, "fragrance", under Sanskrit *magha*, which it glosses as a fragrance and the name of a flower (CDIAL 9703). It is a strong, spreading smell, usually a pleasant one.
