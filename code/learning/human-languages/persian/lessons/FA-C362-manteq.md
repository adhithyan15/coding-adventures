## You'll want to know: منطق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C362-ESTEDLAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**منطق** (*manteq*) — "logic".

> حرف تو منطق ندارد.
>
> *harf-e to manteq nadârad.*
>
> What you say has no logic.

Also the subject of logic at university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منطق** was borrowed from Arabic *manṭiq* (EtymDB glosses it "speech"); in Arabic and Persian it came to mean logic, the art of correct speech and reasoning.
