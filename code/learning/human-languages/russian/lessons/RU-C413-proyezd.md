## You'll want to know: проезд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C413-PROYEZD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**проезд** (*proyézd*) — "a fare; a passage, a way through".

> Сколько стоит проезд на метро?
>
> *Skólko stóit proyézd na metró?*
>
> How much is the metro fare?

Masculine. *Проезд закрыт* — no through road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*проезд* is built from *про-* 'through' and *-езд* 'riding', the root of *ездить* 'to ride, to travel'. EtymDB lists *проезд* without a deeper source.
