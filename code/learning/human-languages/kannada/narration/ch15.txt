Kannada, chapter 15: Water and Rice.
1 lesson. All 1 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 1.
ನೀರು, ಅಕ್ಕಿ, ಅನ್ನ — water matching Tamil, and rice, raw and cooked.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Kannada's water-word sides with Tamil, not with Malayalam's surprising break from the pack.

The word, taken apart: ನೀರು — matching Tamil.
ನೀರು (nīru) = "water" — matching Tamil's நீர் (nīr, the root inside taṇṇīr) closely. Kannada didn't make Malayalam's switch to the veḷ- "bright/white" root.

The word, taken apart: ಅಕ್ಕಿ, ಅನ್ನ — rice, raw and cooked.
ಅಕ್ಕಿ (akki) = "rice" (raw grain).
ಅನ್ನ (anna) = "cooked rice" (the meal) — likely Sanskrit- influenced, the same general idea as Tamil's sātam.
