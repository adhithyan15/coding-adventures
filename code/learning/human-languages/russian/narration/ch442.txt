Russian, chapter 442: Sightseeing.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
экскурсия (ekskúrsiya) — a guided tour, an excursion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to search for, then the Russian for to ask again.

You'll want to know: экскурсия.
экскурсия (ekskúrsiya) — "a guided tour, an excursion".
В субботу у нас экскурсия по городу. V subbótu u nas ekskúrsiya po górodu. On Saturday we have a guided tour of the town.
Feminine. Экскурсия по музею — a tour of the museum.

The word, taken apart.
экскурсия (ekskúrsiya) goes back to Latin excursiō 'a running out, a setting out' (EtymDB), from excurrere 'to run out'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
экскурсовод (ekskursovód) — a tour guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to ask again, then the Russian for a guided tour.

You'll want to know: экскурсовод.
экскурсовод (ekskursovód) — "a tour guide".
Экскурсовод рассказывал о старых домах. Ekskursovód rasskázyval o stárykh domákh. The guide talked about the old houses.
Masculine, for a woman too. Also гид.

The word, taken apart.
экскурсовод (ekskursovód) is built from экскурсия (ekskúrsiya) 'a tour' and -вод, from водить 'to lead'. Экскурсия goes back to Latin excursiō and водить to Proto-Slavic voditi (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: путеводитель.
путеводитель (putevodítel) — "a guidebook".
В путеводителе есть хорошая карта центра. V putevodítele yest khoróshaya kárta tséntra. There is a good map of the centre in the guidebook.
Masculine. Literally a way-leader: путь + водитель.

The word, taken apart.
путеводитель (putevodítel) is built from путь 'a way' and водитель 'one who leads', from водить 'to lead' (EtymDB): a book that leads you on your way.

Lesson 4 of 6.
достопримечательность (dostoprimechátelnost) — a sight, a place of interest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a tour guide, then the Russian for a guidebook.

You'll want to know: достопримечательность.
достопримечательность (dostoprimechátelnost) — "a sight, a place of interest".
Главная достопримечательность города — старый мост. Glávnaya dostoprimechátelnost góroda — stáryy most. The main sight of the town is the old bridge.
Feminine; often plural: достопримечательности города — the sights of the town.

The word, taken apart.
достопримечательность (dostoprimechátelnost) is built from досто- 'worthy', as in достойный, and примечательный 'notable', from примечать 'to notice': a thing worth noticing. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 5 of 6.
осматривать (osmátrivat) — to look round (a place), to go sightseeing; to examine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a guidebook, then the Russian for a sight.

You'll want to know: осматривать.
осматривать (osmátrivat) — "to look round (a place), to go sightseeing; to examine".
Весь день туристы осматривали старый город. Ves den turísty osmátrivali stáryy górod. All day the tourists looked round the old town.
Imperfective; perfective осмотреть (осмотрю, осмотришь). Also of a doctor: to examine.

The word, taken apart.
осматривать (osmátrivat) is built from о- 'round' and -сматривать, from смотреть 'to look', which EtymDB relates to Proto-Slavic motriti 'to watch'.
