## You'll want to know: पूर्वज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C327-VANSH327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वज** — *pūrvaj* — "an ancestor".

> हमारे पूर्वज सौ साल पहले इस गाँव में आए।
>
> *hamāre pūrvaj sau sāl pahle is gā̃v meṁ āe.*
>
> Our ancestors came to this village a hundred years ago.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वज** (*pūrvaj*) is Sanskrit *pūrvaja*, "born before" (Monier-Williams), *pūrva*, earlier, and *-ja*, born: those who came before us.
