# ವೃತ್ತಾಂತ (vṛttānta) — a narrative; an account

## You'll want to know: ವೃತ್ತಾಂತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C373-UDAYA373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೃತ್ತಾಂತ** — *vṛttānta* — "a narrative; an account".

> ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ ತನ್ನ ಪ್ರಯಾಣದ ವೃತ್ತಾಂತ ಬರೆದನು.
>
> *pravāsi tanna prayāṇada vṛttānta baredanu.*
>
> The traveller wrote an account of his journey.

A full telling of what happened, in order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೃತ್ತಾಂತ** (*vṛttānta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vṛttānta*, "the end of a course of action, an occurrence, a tale" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
