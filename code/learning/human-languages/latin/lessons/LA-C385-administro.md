## You'll want to know: administrō, administrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C385-REGO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**administrō, administrāre** — "to manage, to run; to administer".

> Prōvinciam bene administrāvit.
>
> *He governed the province well.*

*administrō, administrāre, administrāvī, administrātum*, built on *minister*, a servant, a helper. English *administer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*administrō* is built from *ad* and *ministrō* "to serve" (EtymDB), which comes from *minister* "a servant, a helper" (Lewis & Short). English *administer* comes from it.
