## You'll want to know: вакансия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C358-VAKANSIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**вакансия** (*vakánsiya*) — "a vacancy, a job opening".

> В этой компании есть вакансия бухгалтера.
>
> *V étoy kompánii yest vakánsiya bukhgáltera.*
>
> This company has a vacancy for an accountant.

Feminine. *Открытая вакансия* — an open position.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вакансия** was borrowed from French *vacance*, from Latin *vacantia* "emptiness, vacancy" (EtymDB), built on *vacāre* "to be empty" (Lewis & Short). A vacancy is an empty place.
