## You'll want to know: धोणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C170-DHOVO170-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धोणो** — *dhoṇo* — "to wash".

> हूं रोज कपड़ा धोऊं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ roj kapṛā dhoū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I wash clothes every day.

Past (I …): म्हैं कपड़ा धोया, *mhaĩ kapṛā dhoyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धोणो** (*dhoṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhauvati*, "washes"; CDIAL lists Marwari *dhoṇo* under it (CDIAL 6886).
