## You'll want to know: पकवान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C331-VRAT331-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पकवान** — *pakvān* — "a festive dish; a delicacy".

> माँ ने त्योहार के लिए कई पकवान बनाए।
>
> *māṁ ne tyohār ke liye kaī pakvān banāe.*
>
> Mother made several festive dishes for the festival.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पकवान** (*pakvān*) is Sanskrit *pakvānna*, *pakva*, cooked, and *anna*, food (Monier-Williams); in Hindi it is the rich, fried food of a feast day.
