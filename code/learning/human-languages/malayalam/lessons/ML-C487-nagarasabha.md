## You'll want to know: നഗരസഭ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C487-NAGARAM487-01]; assesses=[] -->

**നഗരസഭ** — *nagarasabha* — "a municipality".

> നഗരസഭ തെരുവുകളിൽ പുതിയ വിളക്കുകൾ വച്ചു.
>
> *nagarasabha teruvukaḷil putiya viḷakkukaḷ vaccu.*
>
> The municipality put up new lamps in the streets.

The council of a town; a village has a പഞ്ചായത്ത്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നഗരസഭ** (*nagarasabha*) is a Malayalam compound built from നഗരം, a town, from Sanskrit *nagara* (CDIAL 6924), and സഭ, an assembly, from Sanskrit *sabhā* (Monier-Williams).
