## You'll want to know: ডেস্ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C493-OPHIS493-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ডেস্ক** — *ḍesk* — "a desk".

> আমার ডেস্কে অনেক ফাইল আছে।
>
> *Āmār ḍeske ônek phāil āchhe.*
>
> There are lots of files on my desk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডেস্ক** (*ḍesk*) is borrowed from English *desk*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
