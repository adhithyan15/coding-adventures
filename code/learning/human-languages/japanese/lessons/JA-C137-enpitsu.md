## You'll want to know: えんぴつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-ENPITSU]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-E-01, JA-SCRIPT-N-01, JA-SCRIPT-TSU-01] -->

**えんぴつ** — *enpitsu* — "a pencil", the one you may be writing with now.

Four beats: *e–n–pi–tsu*. **え**, **ん** and **つ** you already write. The
third sign is **ひ** with the small circle, so *hi* becomes *pi*. The next
lesson writes it.
