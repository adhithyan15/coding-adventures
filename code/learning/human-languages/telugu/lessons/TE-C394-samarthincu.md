# సమర్థించు (samarthiñcu) — to justify; to defend (a view)

## You'll want to know: సమర్థించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C394-VIVARANA394-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సమర్థించు** — *samarthiñcu* — "to justify; to defend (a view)".

> అతను తన పనిని గట్టిగా సమర్థించాడు.
>
> *atanu tana panini gaṭṭigā samarthiñcāḍu.*
>
> He firmly defended his work.

Past (I …): సమర్థించాను, *samarthiñcānu*.

Also to back someone else: ఆ ప్రతిపాదనను సమర్థించు, to support that proposal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమర్థించు** (*samarthiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *samarthana* (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; the noun is సమర్థన.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
