## You'll want to know: きちんと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C440-CAREFUL440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きちんと** — *kichinto* — "neatly; properly".

> **ふくを きちんと たたんで ください。** — *fuku o kichinto tatande kudasai* — "Please fold the clothes neatly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きちんと** (*kichinto*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
