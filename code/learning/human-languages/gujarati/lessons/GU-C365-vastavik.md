## You'll want to know: વાસ્તવિક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C365-SIDDHANT365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વાસ્તવિક** — *vāstavik* — "actual".

> યોજનાની વાસ્તવિક કિંમત ઘણી વધારે હતી.
>
> *yojnānī vāstavik kimmat ghaṇī vadhāre hatī.*
>
> The actual cost of the plan was much higher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાસ્તવિક** (*vāstavik*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vāstavika*, "real, substantial" (Monier-Williams).
