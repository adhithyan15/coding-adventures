Kannada, chapter 378: Taking a Stand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ (dṛṣṭikōna) — a point of view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to object, then the Kannada for reasonable.

You'll want to know: ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ.
ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ — dṛṣṭikōna — "a point of view".
ಪ್ರತಿಯೊಬ್ಬರಿಗೂ ಬೇರೆ ಬೇರೆ ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ. pratiyobbarigū bēre bēre dṛṣṭikōna iruttade. Everyone has a different point of view.
Word by word it is sight + angle.

The word, taken apart.
ದೃಷ್ಟಿಕೋನ (dṛṣṭikōna) is built from Sanskrit dṛṣṭi, "sight, view", and koṇa, "a corner, an angle" (Monier-Williams): the angle you look from.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for reasonable, then the Kannada for a point of view.

You'll want to know: ನಿಲುವು.
ನಿಲುವು — niluvu — "a stance; a position".
ಈ ವಿಷಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಮ್ಮ ನಿಲುವು ಏನು? ī viṣayadalli nimma niluvu ēnu? What is your position on this matter?
From ನಿಲ್ಲು, to stand: where one stands.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಲುವು (niluvu) is native Dravidian, a noun from nil, "to stand" (DEDR 3675), the root of ನಿಲ್ಲು.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a point of view, then the Kannada for a stance.

You'll want to know: ತಟಸ್ಥ.
ತಟಸ್ಥ — taṭastha — "neutral".
ಜಗಳದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ತಟಸ್ಥವಾಗಿ ಇದ್ದೆ. jagaḷadalli nānu taṭasthavāgi idde. In the quarrel I stayed neutral.
Word by word it is standing on the bank, out of the stream.

The word, taken apart.
ತಟಸ್ಥ (taṭastha) is borrowed from Sanskrit taṭastha, "standing on a bank" (Monier-Williams), from taṭa, "a bank", and stha, "standing".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸು (pratipādisu) — to advocate; to argue for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for neutral, then the Kannada for bias.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸು.
ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸು — pratipādisu — "to advocate; to argue for".
ಅವರು ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಉಚಿತ ಶಿಕ್ಷಣವನ್ನು ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸಿದರು. avaru ellarigū ucita śikṣaṇavannu pratipādisidaru. They argued for free education for all.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸಿದೆ, pratipādiside.
What you argue for takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರತಿಪಾದಿಸು (pratipādisu) is built on Sanskrit pratipādana, "presenting, setting forth" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
