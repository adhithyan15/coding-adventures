## You'll want to know: جلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C287-AAG287-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جلنا** — *jalnā* — "to burn; to be on fire".

> دکان جل گئی۔
>
> *dukān jal gaī.*
>
> The shop burned down.

Past (it …): وہ گھر جل گیا, *voh ghar jal gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from Sanskrit *jválati*, "burns, blazes" (CDIAL 5306). It is the partner of **جلانا**: you **جلانا** a fire, and a thing **جلنا** by itself.
