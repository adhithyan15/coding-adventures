## You'll want to know: پلٹانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C533-JUA533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پلٹانا** — *palṭānā* — "to turn over (something)".

> اگر آپ نے پتا پلٹایا تو کھیل بدل جائے گا۔
>
> *agar āp ne pattā palṭāyā to khel badal jāe gā.*
>
> If you turn the card over, the game will change.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پتا پلٹایا, *us ne pattā palṭāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پلٹانا** (*palṭānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *palṭānā*, "to turn", under *\*pallaṭṭ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 7968).
