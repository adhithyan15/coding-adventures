## You'll want to know: كشف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C312-KASHF312-01]; assesses=[] -->

**كشف** — *kashafa* — "to uncover, to reveal".

> كشف التحقيق سبب الحريق.
>
> *kashafa at-taḥqīq sabab al-ḥarīq.*
>
> The investigation revealed the cause of the fire.

Present: يكشف, *yakshif*.

كشف عن: to disclose. A medical check-up is كشف طبي.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كشف** (*kashafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-sh-f* (Lane), "to take away a covering from a thing".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
