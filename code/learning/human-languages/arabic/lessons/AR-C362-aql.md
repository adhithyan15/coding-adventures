## You'll want to know: عقل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C362-MANTIQ362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عقل** — *ʿaql* — "the mind".

> فكر الرجل بعقله لا بقلبه.
>
> *fakkara ar-rajul bi-ʿaqlihi lā bi-qalbih.*
>
> The man thought with his head, not with his heart.

Plural: عقول, *ʿuqūl*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقل** (*ʿaql*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-l*, "to tie, to restrain" (Lane). Lane gives the root as tying a camel's leg; *ʿaql* is the mind, which holds a person back from folly.
