## You'll want to know: はずむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C463-TRAIN463-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はずむ** — *hazumu* — "to bounce".

> **この まりは よく はずみます。** — *kono mari wa yoku hazumimasu* — "This ball bounces well."

Polite **はずみます**, *hazumimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **まりが たかく はずみました。**, *mari ga takaku hazumimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はずむ** (*hazumu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
