## You'll want to know: l'aggressione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C298-BANDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'aggressione** — "the attack, the assault".

> L'aggressione è avvenuta davanti a un bar.
>
> *The attack took place outside a bar.*

A news word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aggressione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aggressiō* "a going towards, an onset", from *aggredī* "to go at, to attack" (Lewis & Short).
