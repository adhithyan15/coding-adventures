## You'll want to know: झाँकी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C330-MELA330-02]; assesses=[] -->

**झाँकी** — *jhā̃kī* — "a tableau; a float in a procession".

> जुलूस में बच्चों की झाँकी सबसे सुंदर थी।
>
> *julūs meṁ baccoṁ kī jhā̃kī sabse sundar thī.*
>
> The children's float was the most beautiful in the procession.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झाँकी** (*jhā̃kī*) is built on *jhā̃knā*, to peep, which CDIAL 5325 records as *jhā̃khnā* under *\*jhaṅkh*: a glimpse, and then a scene set up for people to look at.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
