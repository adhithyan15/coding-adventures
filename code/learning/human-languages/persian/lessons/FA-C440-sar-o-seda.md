## You'll want to know: سر و صدا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C440-SARSEDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سر و صدا** (*sar-o sedâ*) — "noise, a din".

> اتاق ما شب پر از سر و صدا بود.
>
> *Otâq-e mâ shab por az sar-o sedâ bud.*
>
> Our room was full of noise at night.

*Sar-o sedâ kardan* — to make a racket. Also a fuss: *sar-o sedâ-ye ziyâdi kard* — it caused a stir.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sar-o sedâ* is a pair of native *sar* 'head', from Middle Persian *sar* (EtymDB), and *sedâ* 'sound', which EtymDB takes from Arabic *ṣadā* 'echo'.
