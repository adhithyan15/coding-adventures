# Review — what happened

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to the market". (*kāl āmi bājāre gelām*.) Then "she came" and "we ate". (*she elô*, *āmrā khelām*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-NOT-01, BN-LEX-C264-GHOTA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C264-GHA-01, BN-LEX-C84-TOWN84-04, BN-LEX-C82-PLACE82-05, BN-LEX-C65-SCHOOL65-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: In the evening I came back home. I was tired.]
- [YOU SAY: What happened in the village yesterday?]
- [YOU SAY: Mira was at the station. Arun was not there.]
- [YOU SAY: There was a big tree in the neighbourhood.]
- [YOU SAY: We wanted tea, and we got tea.]
- [YOU WRITE: ঘ, then **ঘটল**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-NOT-01, BN-LEX-C264-GHOTA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C264-GHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which ending is I and we? (**-লাম**: **গেলাম**.) Which verbs gave **এল** and **পেল**? (**আসা**, **পাওয়া**.) "There was not"? (**ছিল না**.) Which letter opens **ঘটা**? (**ঘ**.)
