## You'll want to know: anbraten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C327-KOECHELN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**anbraten** — "to brown quickly, to sear".

> Zuerst briet sie das Fleisch kurz an.
>
> *First she seared the meat briefly.*

Separable, strong: *brät an*, *briet an*, *hat angebraten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anbraten* is built on *braten* "to roast, to fry", Old High German *brātan* (EtymDB), with *an*.
