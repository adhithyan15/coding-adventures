Persian, chapter 29: Blood, Knee, Back, Belly, Lip.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خون (khun) — blood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the neck, then the Persian for the face.

You'll want to know: خون.
خون — khun — "blood".
