## You'll want to know: tíngdiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C167-PAST167-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tíngdiàn* — "to have a power cut".

> *qiántiān wǎnshang tíngdiàn le* — There was a power cut the night before last.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tíngdiàn* is built from *tíng* 'to stop' and 电 'lightning, electricity', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *deng* and *denH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
