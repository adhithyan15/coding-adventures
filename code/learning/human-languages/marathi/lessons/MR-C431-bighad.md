## You'll want to know: बिघाड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C431-BIGHAD431-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बिघाड** — *bighāḍ* — "a breakdown; a fault".

> इंजिनात बिघाड झाल्यामुळे गाडी थांबली.
>
> *iñjināt bighāḍ jhālyāmuḷe gāḍī thāmblī.*
>
> The train stopped because of a fault in the engine.

Masculine.

बिघाड होणे, a fault occurs; the place of the fault takes -त. The verb is बिघडणे, to go wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिघाड** (*bighāḍ*) is inherited, from the family of Sanskrit *vighaṭate*, "flies apart, is broken", where CDIAL lists Marathi *bighaḍṇẽ*, "to be spoilt" (CDIAL 11673).
