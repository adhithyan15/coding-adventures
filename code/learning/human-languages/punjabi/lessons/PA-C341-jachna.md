## You'll want to know: ਜਚਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C341-DOUBT341-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਚਣਾ** — *jachṇā* — "to seem right; to convince".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਉਸ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਚਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ us dī gall nahīṁ jachdī hai.*
>
> What he says doesn't convince me.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਜਵਾਬ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਜਚਿਆ, *oh javāb mainū̃ jachiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਚਣਾ** (*jachṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *yācyate*, "is asked"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jacāuṇā*, "to cause to be examined", under it and notes *jacṇā*, "to be tested", formed from it (CDIAL 10449). CDIAL does not give the everyday sense "to seem right".
