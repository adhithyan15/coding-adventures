## You'll want to know: tèjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C193-PRICE193-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tèjià* — "special offer; reduced price".

> *jīntiān shuǐguǒ tèjià* — Fruit is on special offer today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tèjià* is built from *tè* 'special' and *jià* 'price', the *jià* of *jiàqián*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dok* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'a bull' and 'alone'.
