## You'll want to know: ਉੱਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C343-MIXUP343-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਕਣਾ** — *ukkṇā* — "to slip up; to make a mistake".

> ਅਮਨ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਦੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਉੱਕਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *Aman hisāb vich kade nahīṁ ukkdā hai.*
>
> Aman never slips up in his sums.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਜਵਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਉੱਕਿਆ, *maiṁ javāb vich ukkiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਕਣਾ** (*ukkṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *utkramati*, "ascends", later "neglects, transgresses"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ukkṇā*, "to make a mistake", under it (CDIAL 1737).
