## You'll want to know: خبرگزاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C311-MANBA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خبرگزاری** (*khabargozâri*) — "a news agency".

> این خبر را اول یک خبرگزاری خارجی داد.
>
> *In khabar râ avval yek khabargozâri-ye khâreji dâd.*
>
> A foreign news agency gave this news first.

*khabar* "news" + *gozâri* "rendering", from the stem *gozâr-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khabargozâri* joins *khabar* "news", borrowed from Arabic *khabar* (EtymDB), and the native Persian *gozâri* "rendering", from the stem *gozâr-*.
