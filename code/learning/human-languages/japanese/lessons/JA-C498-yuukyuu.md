## You'll want to know: ゆうきゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C498-REST498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうきゅう** — *yūkyū* — "paid leave; a paid holiday".

> **なつの ゆうきゅうに りょこうします。** — *natsu no yūkyū ni ryokō shimasu* — "I go travelling on my summer paid leave."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうきゅう** (*yūkyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yū*, "have" (Middle Chinese *hjuwX*) and *kyū*, "salary" (*kip*); JMdict gives "paid" and "paid leave" among its senses and marks it as a shortened word (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
