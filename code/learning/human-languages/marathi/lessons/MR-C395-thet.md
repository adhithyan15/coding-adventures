## You'll want to know: थेट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C395-UPAYUKTA395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**थेट** — *theṭ* — "direct; straight".

> ही बस थेट पुण्याला जाते.
>
> *hī bas theṭ puṇyālā jāte.*
>
> This bus goes straight to Pune.

Also an adverb: थेट सांगणे, to say straight out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थेट** (*theṭ*) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled. CDIAL lists a Marathi *theṭ*, "genuine", under Sanskrit *sthēṣṭha*, "most fixed, durable" (CDIAL 13778); whether the sense direct comes from it is not clear.
