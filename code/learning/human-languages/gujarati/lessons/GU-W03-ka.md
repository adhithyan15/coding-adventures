# ક — the consonant ka

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01] -->

Write chha once from memory. Check the two upper lobes before looking back.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-KA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ક** visible. It is the consonant ka, pronounced *ka*. Begin at the upper right, circle the upper loop, and continue diagonally through the rounded lower body. Lift once; sweep the separate diagonal cross-stroke from lower left to upper right.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ક** once, then air-trace it once. Notice looped body plus a separate rising cross-stroke; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ક** once. Compare only the landmark named above and repair it once if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W03-ka-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-KA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write the consonant ka from memory.","answer":"ક","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ક — the taught form is intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ક, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *ka* once. Uncover **ક**, compare, and stop after one repair.
