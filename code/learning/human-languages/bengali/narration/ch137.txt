Bengali, chapter 137: Pink, Sad, Angry, Tired, Healthy.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
গোলাপি (golāpi) — pink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for round, then the Bengali for purple.
[pause 1 second]
Read without stopping: আমি ভাত আর দুধ খাই। চা বা জল।.

You'll want to know: গোলাপি.
গোলাপি — golāpi — "pink".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: golāpi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: golāpi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say golāpi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for round, then the Bengali for purple, then say golāpi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does গোলাপি (golāpi) mean? ("Pink".) Say it once more.

(তবলা, tôblā.) A guitar? (গিটার, giṭār.) A camera? (ক্যামেরা, kyāmerā.) A phone? (ফোন, phon.) A radio? (রেডিও, rediyo.) Light? (আলো, ālo.) A bed? (খাট, khāṭ.) A mattress? (তোশক, toshôk.) A mosquito net? (মশারি, môshāri.) A blanket? (কম্বল, kômbôl.) A curtain? (পর্দা, pôrdā.) A vase? (ফুলদানি, phuldāni.) A cup? (কাপ, kāp.)
