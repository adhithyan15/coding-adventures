## You'll want to know: जप्त करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C299-TAPAS299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जप्त करणे** — *japta karṇe* — "to confiscate; to seize".

> पोलिसांनी चोरीची गाडी जप्त केली.
>
> *polisānnī corīcī gāḍī japta kelī.*
>
> The police seized the stolen car.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने जप्त केलं, *tyāne japta kelaṁ*.

What is seized agrees with the past: गाडी जप्त केली, पैसे जप्त केले.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जप्त करणे** (*japta karṇe*) joins *japta*, 'seized', a loan from Persian *zabt*, usually traced to Arabic *ḍabṭ*, 'a seizing', to *karṇe*, 'to do'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
