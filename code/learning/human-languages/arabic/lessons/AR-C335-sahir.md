## You'll want to know: ساحر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C335-SIHR335-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ساحر** — *sāḥir* — "a magician, a sorcerer".

> حول الساحر الأمير إلى ضفدع.
>
> *ḥawwala as-sāḥir al-amīr ilā ḍifdaʿ.*
>
> The magician turned the prince into a frog.

Plural: سحرة, *saḥara*.

Of a place or a smile, ساحر is enchanting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ساحر** (*sāḥir*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ḥ-r* (Lane), "to bewitch, to enchant"; *siḥr* is magic and *sāḥir* the one who works it.
