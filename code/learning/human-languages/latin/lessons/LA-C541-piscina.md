## You'll want to know: piscīna, piscīnae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C541-BALN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**piscīna, piscīnae** — "a pool; a fish-pond".

> In piscīnā thermārum puerī natant.
>
> *Boys swim in the pool of the baths.*

Feminine, first declension, from *piscis* — fish: first a fish-pond, then any pool for swimming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**piscīna** is built on *piscis* "fish" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB): a fish-pond, then a swimming pool. French *piscine* "swimming pool" comes from it.
