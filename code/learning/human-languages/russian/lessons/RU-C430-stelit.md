## You'll want to know: стелить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C430-POSTEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**стелить** (*stelít*) — "to make up (a bed); to spread".

> Бабушка стелит нам постель на диване.
>
> *Bábushka stélit nam postél na diváne.*
>
> Grandma makes up a bed for us on the sofa.

Imperfective; perfective *постелить*. Present *стелю, стелешь*. Also *стлать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стелить* is a form of *стлать* 'to spread', the root of *постель* 'bedding'. EtymDB lists *стелить* without a deeper source.
