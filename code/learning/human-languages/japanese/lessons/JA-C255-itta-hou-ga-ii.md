# いった ほうが いいです (itta hō ga ii desu) — you had better

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C250-TA-SOUND-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-01, JA-LEX-C23-HEALTH23-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say, plainly, "I rested" and "I won't drink". (*yasunda*, *nomanai*.) Then say "medicine". (*kusuri*.)

## You'll want to know: -た ほうが いいです
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C255-SHOULD-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C29-VERBS29-04, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-BYOUIN, JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-05] -->

> **やすんだ ほうが いいです。**
>
> *yasunda hō ga ii desu.* — "You had better rest."

**ほう** (*hō*) is "side, way": "the side of having rested is good". The plain past goes before it even when the advice is about now or later.

> **くすりを のんだ ほうが いいです。**
>
> *kusuri o nonda hō ga ii desu.* — "You had better take some medicine."

> **びょういんに いった ほうが いいです。**
>
> *byōin ni itta hō ga ii desu.* — "You had better go to the hospital."

## You'll want to know: -ない ほうが いいです
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C255-SHOULD-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C179-DRINK179-02, JA-LEX-C223-SAFETY223-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C254-REASON-01] -->

Advice against takes the plain "not":

> **おさけを のまない ほうが いいです。**
>
> *osake o nomanai hō ga ii desu.* — "You had better not drink alcohol."

> **あぶないから、いかない ほうが いいです。**
>
> *abunai kara, ikanai hō ga ii desu.* — "It is dangerous, so you had better not go."

This is advice. Saying that something must be done needs another form, which comes later.
