Mandarin Chinese, chapter 324: Remembering.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
想起 (xiǎngqǐ) — to think of; to call to mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for ancient times, then the Mandarin for modern times.

You'll want to know: 想起.
想起 — xiǎngqǐ — "to think of; to call to mind".
我突然想起了他的信 wǒ tūrán xiǎngqǐ le tā de xìn — I suddenly remembered his letter.
Two third tones meet in this word: xiǎng is said with a rising tone before qǐ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
想起 (xiǎngqǐ) is built from 想 'to think' and 起 'to rise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sjangX and khiX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to think' and the second 'to rise'.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
jìniàn — to commemorate; to honour the memory of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for modern times, then the Mandarin for to call to mind.

You'll want to know: jìniàn.
jìniàn — "to commemorate; to honour the memory of".
měinián zhè yì tiān wǒmen dōu jìniàn tā — Every year on this day we remember him.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jìniàn is built from jì and niàn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, kiX and nemH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'to note' and 'a span of twelve years', and the second 'to think of'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
时代 (shídài) — era; age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to honour the memory of, then the Mandarin for to leave behind for others.

You'll want to know: 时代.
时代 — shídài — "era; age".
现在是手机的时代 xiànzài shì shǒujī de shídài — This is the age of the mobile phone.

The word, taken apart.
时代 (shídài) is built from 时 'a time' and 代 'an age', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyi and dojH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with another word for a time and the second 'to take turns'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
yìnxiàng — impression.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to leave behind for others, then the Mandarin for an age in history.

You'll want to know: yìnxiàng.
yìnxiàng — "impression".
wǒ duì nàge dìfang de yìnxiàng hěn hǎo — That place made a very good impression on me.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yìnxiàng is built from yìn 'a seal' and xiàng 'an image', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jinH and zjangX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a seal' and describes the second as the long-nosed beast of the south. The sense 'an image' is not given there.
