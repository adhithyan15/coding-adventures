Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 259: My Husband's Family.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
सासू (sāsū) — a mother-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to bear (pain), then the Marwadi for the heart (as the seat of feeling).

You'll want to know: सासू.
सासू — sāsū — "a mother-in-law".
म्हारी सासू घणी भली है। mhārī sāsū ghaṇī bhalī hai. My mother-in-law is very kind.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सासू (sāsū) is inherited from Sanskrit śvaśrū, "a husband's or wife's mother". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi sāsū (CDIAL 12759).

म्हारो धणी खेत गयो। mhāro dhaṇī khet gayo. My husband went to the field.

The word, taken apart.
धणी (dhaṇī) is inherited from Sanskrit dhanin, "rich"; CDIAL lists Marwari dhaṇī̃, "owner", Old Marwari dhaṇī, "master", and Gujarati dhaṇī, "husband", under it (CDIAL 6722).
