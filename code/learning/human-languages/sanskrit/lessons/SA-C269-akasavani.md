## You'll want to know: आकाशवाणी

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आकाशवाणी** — *ākāśavāṇī* — “radio.” The [Kosha Sanskrit Today radio
entry](https://kosha.sanskrit.today/word/en/radio) explicitly attests this
modern use. This tranche teaches **only the modern radio meaning**. It does
not ask you to generalise from the word's older “voice from the sky” sense.

## The word, taken apart

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-01] -->

**आकाश** means “sky,” and **वाणी** means “voice.” That shape can help you
remember the sourced modern word, but the one meaning to retrieve here is
“radio.”
