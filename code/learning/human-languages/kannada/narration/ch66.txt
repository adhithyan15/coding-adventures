Lesson 3 of 5.
ಭತ್ತ (bhatta) — paddy, rice in the husk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: what did hullu mean?

You'll want to know: ಭತ್ತ.
ಭತ್ತ (bhatta) — "paddy, rice in the husk".
ಭತ್ತ (bhatta) completes a set you have been assembling since the water-and-rice chapter. One plant, three names, and Kannada keeps them strictly apart: ಭತ್ತ (bhatta) standing in the ಹೊಲ (hola), ಅಕ್ಕಿ once it is husked and in the sack, ಅನ್ನ once it is cooked and on the plate.
English makes do with rice for all three and adds a word when it must. Kannada gives each stage its own name, and a speaker who used the wrong one would be understood but marked as not from here.

What you've built.
Three.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe) — a crop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: what did bhatta mean?

You'll want to know: ಬೆಳೆ.
ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe) — "a crop".
ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe) is the general one: ಭತ್ತ (bhatta) is a ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe), and so is everything else a ಹೊಲ (hola) is put to. Native, and made from the everyday word for growing — so the same shape names the growing and the grown thing, the way ನೂಲು named the thread and the spinning.
The chain runs the length of this book by now. A ಬೀಜ goes into the ground, the ಮಳೆಗಾಲ arrives, and a ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe) stands in the ಹೊಲ (hola).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸುಗ್ಗಿ (suggi) — the harvest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: what did beḷe mean?

You'll want to know: ಸುಗ್ಗಿ.
ಸುಗ್ಗಿ (suggi) — "the harvest".
ಸುಗ್ಗಿ (suggi) is not only the cutting. It is the season, and it is the mood of the season — the stretch of weeks when the ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe) is in and there is enough, and Karnataka's harvest festival carries the word in its name.
So these five words end where a farming year does. It opened with ಮಳೆಗಾಲ in the seasons chapter, ran through the ಬೀಜ and the ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe), and arrives at the ಸುಗ್ಗಿ (suggi) with the ಹೊಲ (hola) empty and the house full.
Which makes this the right place to take your leave of the ಹಳ್ಳಿ. You can name what it grows now, and thank the people who grew it.

What you've built.
Five: ಹೊಲ (hola), ಹುಲ್ಲು (hullu), ಭತ್ತ (bhatta), ಬೆಳೆ (beḷe), ಸುಗ್ಗಿ (suggi). A field, what grows on it, and the season that takes it in.
