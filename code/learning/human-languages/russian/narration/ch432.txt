Russian, chapter 432: A leaking tap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
протекать (protekát) — to leak (of a roof or a tap); to flow through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to air, then the Russian for to dry.

You'll want to know: протекать.
протекать (protekát) — "to leak (of a roof or a tap); to flow through".
В ванной протекает кран. V vánnoy protekáyet kran. The tap in the bathroom is leaking.
Imperfective; perfective протечь. Крыша протекает — the roof leaks.

The word, taken apart.
протекать (protekát) is built from про- 'through' and -текать, from течь (tech) 'to flow' (EtymDB lists течь (tech) with that sense).

Всю ночь из крана капала вода. Vsyu noch iz krána kápala vodá. All night water dripped from the tap.
Imperfective; perfective капнуть. Present капает.

В старых домах часто засоряются трубы. V stárykh domákh chásto zasoryáyutsya trúby. In old houses the pipes often get blocked.
Imperfective; perfective засориться. Раковина засорилась — the sink is blocked.

The word, taken apart.
засоряться (zasoryátsya) is built from за-, сор 'litter, rubbish' and -ся: to fill up with rubbish. EtymDB lists сор without a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
течь (tech) — to flow; to leak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to drip, then the Russian for to get blocked.

You'll want to know: течь.
течь (tech) — "to flow; to leak".
Под раковиной течёт вода. Pod rákovinoy techyót vodá. Water is leaking under the sink.
Imperfective. Present теку, течёшь, текут; past тёк, текла. As a feminine noun, течь (tech) is a leak.

The word, taken apart.
течь (tech) 'to flow' is usually counted an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists it with that sense but without a source. Восток 'east' is often linked with the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
потоп (potóp) — a flood (in a flat); a deluge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get blocked, then the Russian for to flow.

You'll want to know: потоп.
потоп (potóp) — "a flood (in a flat); a deluge".
Соседи сверху устроили нам потоп. Sosédi svérkhu ustróili nam potóp. The upstairs neighbours flooded our flat.
Masculine; colloquial for a flooded flat. Всемирный потоп (potóp) — the Flood.

The word, taken apart.
потоп (potóp) is built on потопить 'to flood, to sink', from топить 'to drown', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic topiti.
