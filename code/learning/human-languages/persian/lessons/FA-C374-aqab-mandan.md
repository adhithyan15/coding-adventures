## You'll want to know: عقب ماندن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C374-BEHBUD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عقب ماندن** (*'aqab mândan*) — "to fall behind, to lag".

> او به خاطر بیماری از درسها عقب ماند.
>
> *u be khâter-e bimâri az darshâ 'aqab mând.*
>
> Because of his illness he fell behind with his lessons.

*'aqab* "back" + *mândan* "to stay" (present stem *mân-*). Takes *az*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقب ماندن** is literally "to stay behind": *'aqab* "back" is usually traced to Arabic *'aqab* "heel", and *mândan* "to stay" is inherited Persian (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian).
