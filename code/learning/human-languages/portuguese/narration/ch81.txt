Portuguese, chapter 81: Remember, Forget, Look For, Lose, Arrive.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
lembrar — to remember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to clean, then the Portuguese for to cut.

You'll want to know: lembrar.
lembrar — "to remember".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: lembrar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lembrar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say lembrar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for to clean, then the Portuguese for to cut, then say lembrar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does lembrar mean? ("To remember".) Say it once more.
