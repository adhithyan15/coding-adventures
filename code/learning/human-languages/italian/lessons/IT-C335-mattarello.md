## You'll want to know: il mattarello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C335-IMPASTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il mattarello** — "the rolling pin".

> Con il mattarello la pasta diventa sottile.
>
> *With the rolling pin the pastry gets thin.*

Also written *matterello*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mattarello* is usually traced to Latin *mateola* "a mallet" (Lewis & Short), but the origin is not settled.
