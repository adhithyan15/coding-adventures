## You'll want to know: जनाकीर्णः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C426-VIDESHA426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जनाकीर्णः** — *janākīrṇaḥ* — "crowded".

> विदेशस्य नगराणि जनाकीर्णानि सन्ति।
>
> *videśasya nagarāṇi janākīrṇāni santi.*
>
> The cities abroad are crowded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जनाकीर्णः** (*janākīrṇaḥ*) is built from *jana*, "people", and *ākīrṇa*, "scattered over, filled", from *ā* and the root √kṝ, "to scatter"; MW gives the compound as crowded with people (MW).
