Sanskrit, chapter 484: Ayurveda.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आयुर्वेदः (āyurvedaḥ) — the science of health; Ayurveda.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a decoction, then the Sanskrit for smears.

You'll want to know: आयुर्वेदः.
आयुर्वेदः — āyurvedaḥ — "the science of health; Ayurveda".
आयुर्वेदः आरोग्यस्य ज्ञानम् अस्ति। āyurvedaḥ ārogyasya jñānam asti. Ayurveda is the knowledge of health.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आयुर्वेदः (āyurvedaḥ) is built from āyus, "life", and veda, "knowledge", from the root √vid; MW gives "the science of health or medicine" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for smears, then the Sanskrit for the science of health.

You'll want to know: वातः.
वातः — vātaḥ — "the wind humour".
वातः शीते काले वर्धते। vātaḥ śīte kāle vardhate. The wind humour rises in the cold season.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वातः (vātaḥ) comes from the root √vā, "to blow"; MW gives "wind" and "wind or air as one of the humours of the body" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

पित्तम् — pittam — "bile".
ग्रीष्मे पित्तं वर्धते। grīṣme pittaṃ vardhate. Bile increases in summer.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पित्तम् (pittam) MW calls its etymology unknown, so its origin is unknown; it gives "bile, the bilious humour, one of the three humours" (MW).
