## You'll want to know: प्रपितामहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C500-DEVARA500-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रपितामहः** — *prapitāmahaḥ* — "a great-grandfather".

> मम प्रपितामहः कृषकः अभवत्।
>
> *mama prapitāmahaḥ kṛṣakaḥ abhavat.*
>
> My great-grandfather was a farmer.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रपितामहः** (*prapitāmahaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "before", and **पितामहः**, "a grandfather"; MW gives "a paternal great-grandfather" and, in the plural, "ancestors" (MW).

## What you've built
