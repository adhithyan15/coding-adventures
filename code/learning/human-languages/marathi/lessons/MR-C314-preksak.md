## You'll want to know: प्रेक्षक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C314-VACHAK314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेक्षक** — *prekṣak* — "a viewer; a spectator".

> नाटक पाहायला हजारो प्रेक्षक आले होते.
>
> *nāṭak pāhāylā hajāro prekṣak āle hote.*
>
> Thousands of spectators had come to watch the play.

Masculine.

Plural प्रेक्षक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेक्षक** (*prekṣak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prekṣaka*, 'a looker-on, a spectator' (Monier-Williams).
