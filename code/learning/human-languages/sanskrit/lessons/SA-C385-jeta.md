## You'll want to know: जेता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C385-SPARDHA385-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जेता** — *jetā* — "a winner; a victor".

> यदि भवान् जेता भवति, तर्हि पारितोषिकं लभते।
>
> *yadi bhavān jetā bhavati, tarhi pāritoṣikaṃ labhate.*
>
> If you are the winner, you get the prize.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जेता** (*jetā*) is built on the root √ji, "to win", with the agent ending *-tṛ*; MW gives *jetṛ* "victorious, triumphant, a conqueror" (MW).
