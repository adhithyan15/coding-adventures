## You'll want to know: विलंब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C411-VAZAN411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विलंब** — *vilamb* — "a delay (formal)".

> ट्रेन दो घंटे के विलंब से आई।
>
> *ṭren do ghaṇṭe ke vilamb se āī.*
>
> The train came two hours late.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विलंब** (*vilamb*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vilamba*, "hanging down" and so "slowness, delay" (Monier-Williams); announcements use it where talk says देर.
