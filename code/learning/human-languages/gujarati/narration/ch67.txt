Gujarati, chapter 67: Cow, Buffalo and Goat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગાય (gāy) — a cow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ice, then the Gujarati for fog.

You'll want to know: ગાય.
ગાય (gāy) — "a cow". It gives દૂધ.

What you've built.
One more word for cow, buffalo and goat.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gāy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gāy, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gāy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for ice, then the Gujarati for fog, then say gāy again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ગાય (gāy) mean? ("A cow".) Say it once more.
