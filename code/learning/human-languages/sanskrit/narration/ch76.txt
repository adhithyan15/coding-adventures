Sanskrit, chapter 76: Rain, Cold and Heat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वर्षा (varṣā) — rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an aeroplane, then the Sanskrit for he goes in.

You'll want to know: वर्षा.
वर्षा (varṣā) — "rain", and in the plural the rainy season.

What you've built.
Water from the sky.
