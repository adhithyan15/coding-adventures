## You'll want to know: ਖੰਭਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C264-CROSS264-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੰਭਾ** — *khambhā* — "a pole; a post".

> ਬੱਸ ਬਿਜਲੀ ਦੇ ਖੰਭੇ ਕੋਲ ਰੁਕਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *bass bijlī de khambhe kol rukdī hai.*
>
> The bus stops by the electricity pole.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੰਭਾ** (*khambhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *skambha*, "prop, pillar"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khambhā*, "wooden prop, post", under it (CDIAL 13639).
