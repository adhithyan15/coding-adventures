## You'll want to know: संमती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C543-HOKAR543-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संमती** — *sammatī* — "consent".

> पालकांच्या संमतीशिवाय मुलं सहलीला जाऊ शकत नाहीत.
>
> *pālkāñcyā sammatīśivāy mulaṁ sahlīlā jāū śakat nāhīt.*
>
> Children cannot go on the trip without their parents' consent.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संमती** (*sammatī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sammati*, "agreement, approval" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
