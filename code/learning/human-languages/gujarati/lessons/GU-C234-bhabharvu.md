# ભાંભરવું (bhā̃bharvũ) — to moo, to low

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C233-WORK233-05, GU-LEX-C234-MILK234-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to plough, then the Gujarati for calf.

## You'll want to know: ભાંભરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C234-MILK234-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાંભરવું** — *bhā̃bharvũ* — "to moo, to low".

> **સવારે વાછરડું ભાંભર્યું.** — *savāre vācharḍũ bhā̃bharyũ* — "The calf mooed in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ભાંભરવું** (*bhā̃bharvũ*) is not settled. It is probably imitative of the sound a cow makes, and CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it.
