Punjabi, chapter 186: What Will Happen? Who Will Come?.
8 lessons. You can do the first 7 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
Start with two verbs you know — return to two familiar infinitives before future forms.

Warm-up.

Grammar Lens: one future contrast at a time.
Keep the two familiar meanings steady. The next steps will make a masculine future form and then a feminine future form from the first verb. Only after that contrast is secure will the same two endings move to the second verb.

Lesson 2 of 8.
ਹੋਵੇਗਾ (hovegaa) — it will be or happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਹੋਣਾ, then say ਕੰਮ.

Grammar Lens: the masculine singular future.
ਹੋਵੇਗਾ — hovegaa — it will be or it will happen.
This is the masculine singular future form. First keep it whole; the feminine contrast comes in the next small step.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: hovegaa; YOU SAY: ਹੋਵੇਗਾ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੰਮ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ (hovegaa)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਹੋਵੇਗਾ (hovegaa) once and check the final ਾ]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read the exact form for “it will happen.” (ਹੋਵੇਗਾ (hovegaa).)
Source: standard Punjabi uses this form as the masculine singular future of the already-taught infinitive ਹੋਣਾ.


Lesson 3 of 8.
ਹੋਵੇਗੀ (hovegii) — it will be, with a feminine subject.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਹੋਵੇਗਾ (hovegaa), then say ਯਾਤਰਾ.

Grammar Lens: change only the agreement ending.
ਹੋਵੇਗੀ — hovegii — it will be or happen, feminine singular.
The ending changes from ਾ to ੀ. The time stays future; the form now agrees with a feminine singular subject.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: hovegaa, hovegii; YOU SAY: ਹੋਵੇਗਾ, ਹੋਵੇਗੀ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਯਾਤਰਾ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ (hovegii)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਹੋਵੇਗੀ (hovegii) once and check the final ੀ]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the masculine form, then the feminine form.
Source: standard Punjabi future agreement changes the final masculine ਾ to feminine ੀ here.

Lesson 5 of 8.

Grammar Lens: transfer the masculine future.
ਆਵੇਗਾ — aavegaa — he or it will come.
The verb changes, but the masculine singular future ending remains familiar.

Lesson 6 of 8.
ਆਵੇਗੀ (aavegii) — she or it will come.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਆਵੇਗਾ (aavegaa), then say ਰੇਲ.

Grammar Lens: transfer the feminine future.
ਆਵੇਗੀ — aavegii — she or it will come.
Change the final ਾ to ੀ, matching the first future pair. The verb still means “come”; agreement is now feminine singular.

Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — read: ਰੇਲ ਆਵੇਗੀ (aavegii)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਆਵੇਗੀ (aavegii) once and check the final ੀ]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the masculine “will come,” then the feminine “will come.”
Source: standard Punjabi future agreement changes the final masculine ਾ to feminine ੀ here too.
