## You'll want to know: обложка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C526-BOOKS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**обложка** (*oblózhka*) — "a cover (of a book)".

> Мне понравилась обложка этой книги.
>
> *Mne ponrávilas oblózhka étoy knígi.*
>
> I liked the cover of this book.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обложка* is built on *обложить* 'to cover, to lay round', from the root of *ложить* 'to lay'; EtymDB has no entry.
