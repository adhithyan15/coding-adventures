## You'll want to know: अंजाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C340-UPANYAS340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अंजाम** — *añjām* — "an outcome; the end".

> उपन्यास का अंजाम मुझे पसंद नहीं आया।
>
> *upanyās kā añjām mujhe pasand nahīṃ āyā.*
>
> I didn't like the way the novel ended.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंजाम** (*añjām*) is usually traced to Persian *anjām*, "an end, a completion". It is how things turn out: **इसका अंजाम बुरा होगा**, this will end badly.
