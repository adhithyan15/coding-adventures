Tamil, chapter 120: Mirror, Soap, Bucket, Pen, Paper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கண்ணாடி (kaṇṇāṭi) — a mirror; glasses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a pillow, then the Tamil for a blanket.

You'll want to know: கண்ணாடி.
கண்ணாடி — kaṇṇāṭi — "a mirror; glasses".

The word, taken apart.
கண்ணாடி (kaṇṇāṭi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1182 lists Malayalam kaṇṇāṭi and Kannada kannaḍi. The entry points back to kaṇ, "eye" (DEDR 1159).

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kaṇṇāṭi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kaṇṇāṭi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kaṇṇāṭi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for a pillow, then the Tamil for a blanket, then say kaṇṇāṭi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does கண்ணாடி (kaṇṇāṭi) mean? ("A mirror; glasses".) Say it once more.
