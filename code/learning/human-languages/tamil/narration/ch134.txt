Lesson 1 of 5.
திரைச்சீலை (tiraiccīlai) — a curtain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for hair, then the Tamil for the skin.

You'll want to know: திரைச்சீலை.
திரைச்சீலை — tiraiccīlai — "a curtain".

The word, taken apart.
திரைச்சீலை (tiraiccīlai) is native: tirai, "curtain, screen" (DEDR 3244, which also has Malayalam tira), and cīlai, "cloth" (DEDR 2629).

Lesson 3 of 5.
தொலைக்காட்சி (tolaikkāṭci) — a television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a curtain, then the Tamil for a mattress.

You'll want to know: தொலைக்காட்சி.
தொலைக்காட்சி — tolaikkāṭci — "a television".

The word, taken apart.
தொலைக்காட்சி (tolaikkāṭci) is a modern coinage from native parts: tolai, "far" (DEDR 3519), and kāṭci, "sight", from kāṇ, "to see" (DEDR 1443). It is a word-for-word "tele-vision".
