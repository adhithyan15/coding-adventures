## You'll want to know: せいかつひ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C360-SHUUNYUU360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**せいかつひ** — *seikatsuhi* — "living costs".

> **とかいに すんだら、せいかつひが たかいでしょう。** — *tokai ni sundara, seikatsuhi ga takai deshō* — "If you live in the city, your living costs will probably be high."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいかつひ** (*seikatsuhi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "life" (Middle Chinese *srjaeng*), *katsu*, "lively" (*hwat*) and *hi*, "expense" (*phj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
