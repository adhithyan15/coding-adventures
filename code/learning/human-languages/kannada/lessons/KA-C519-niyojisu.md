## You'll want to know: ನಿಯೋಜಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C519-HANAKASU519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಯೋಜಿಸು** — *niyōjisu* — "to assign; to post".

> ಕಂಪನಿ ಅವಳನ್ನು ಹೊಸ ಶಾಖೆಗೆ ನಿಯೋಜಿಸಿತು.
>
> *kampani avaḷannu hosa śākhege niyōjisitu.*
>
> The company posted her to the new branch.

Past (I …): ನಿಯೋಜಿಸಿದೆ, *niyōjiside*.

The person takes -ಅನ್ನು, the post or task -ಗೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಯೋಜಿಸು** (*niyōjisu*) is built on Sanskrit *niyojana*, "enjoining, directing, appointing to" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
