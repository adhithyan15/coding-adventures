## Writing

Task 1 must identify the Saturday/Sunday booking error, explain why Saturday is
needed, request correction to Saturday, and ask when a revised confirmation
will arrive. Task 2 must address a repair-and-sharing day, develop a benefit and
a practical difficulty, propose a response to that difficulty, and state a
reasoned recommendation for newsletter readers. Apply the rubric rather than
matching fixed wording. Scorer-only reference openings:

- **नमस्ते। मैंने शनिवार को अपने अध्ययन-समूह के लिए कमरा बुक किया था, लेकिन…**
- **मासिक मरम्मत और वस्तु-साझा दिवस से पड़ोसी कम सामान फेंकेंगे और…**

Do not show these stems to the learner.

## Speaking

The interview requires relevant extended answers with reasons or examples. The
prepared account must sustain approximately three minutes and connect the
initial problem, two steps, a difficulty, the result, and a future adjustment.
The collaborative task must propose, compare, negotiate, and explicitly agree
on a heat-support plan. The follow-up discussion must express and respond to
reasoned views. Score each learner's task development, interaction and repair,
Hindi control, organisation, and intelligibility with the rubric; no single
wording or position is required.
