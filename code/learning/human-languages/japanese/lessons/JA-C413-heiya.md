## You'll want to know: へいや
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C413-HEIYA413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**へいや** — *heiya* — "a plain (flat land)".

> **ひろい へいやに たんぼが あります。** — *hiroi heiya ni tanbo ga arimasu* — "There are rice fields on the wide plain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へいや** (*heiya*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hei*, "flat" (Middle Chinese *bjaeng*) and *ya*, "plains" (*yaeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
