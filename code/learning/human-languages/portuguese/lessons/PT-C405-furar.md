## You'll want to know: furar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C405-PNEU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**furar** — "to puncture, to make a hole in".

> Um prego furou o pneu.
>
> *A nail punctured the tyre.*

*Furar a fila* — to jump the queue (informal).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*furar* is inherited from Latin *forāre* 'to bore, to pierce' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
