## You'll want to know: प्रचार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C355-MATDAN355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रचार** — *pracār* — "canvassing; publicity; propaganda".

> उमेदवारांनी घरोघरी जाऊन प्रचार केला.
>
> *umedvārānnī gharoghrī jāūn pracār kelā.*
>
> The candidates canvassed from door to door.

Masculine.

प्रचार करणे, to campaign; also to spread an idea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रचार** (*pracār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pracāra*, "going about; coming forth, currency" (Monier-Williams).
