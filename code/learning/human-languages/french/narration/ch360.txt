French, chapter 360: Faire carrière.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'augmentation — the pay rise, the increase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the extension, then the French for the delivery.

You'll want to know: l'augmentation.
l'augmentation — "the pay rise, the increase".
J'ai demandé une augmentation à mon patron. I asked my boss for a pay rise.
From augmenter. Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
augmentation comes from Late Latin augmentātiō "an increase" (EtymDB), from augēre "to increase" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la promotion — the promotion; the special offer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the delivery, then the French for the pay rise.

You'll want to know: la promotion.
la promotion — "the promotion; the special offer".
Elle a eu une promotion: elle dirige maintenant l'équipe. She got a promotion: she now runs the team.
In shops, en promotion — on special offer.

The word, taken apart.
promotion is a learned borrowing from Latin prōmōtiō "advancement" (EtymDB), from prōmovēre "to move forward", built on movēre "to move" (Lewis & Short).
