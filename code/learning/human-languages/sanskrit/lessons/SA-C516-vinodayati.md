## You'll want to know: विनोदयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C516-AVAKASA516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विनोदयति** — *vinodayati* — "amuses".

> पितामहः गीतेन बालान् विनोदयति।
>
> *pitāmahaḥ gītena bālān vinodayati.*
>
> Grandfather amuses the children with a song.

Past (he …): सः बालान् विनोदितवान्, *saḥ bālān vinoditavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विनोदयति** (*vinodayati*) is built on *vi*, "away", and the causative of the root √nud, "to push", which also gives *nudati*: to drive dullness away; MW gives *vinud* as to drive away, and to divert (MW).
