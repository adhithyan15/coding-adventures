Gujarati, chapter 67: Cow, Buffalo and Goat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગાય (gāy) — a cow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ice, then the Gujarati for fog.

You'll want to know: ગાય.
ગાય (gāy) — "a cow". It gives દૂધ.

The word, taken apart.
ગાય (gāy) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit gāvī, "cow", a form of go (CDIAL 4147).

What you've built.
One more word for cow, buffalo and goat.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
