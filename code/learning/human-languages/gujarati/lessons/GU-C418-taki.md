## You'll want to know: ટાંકી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C418-SOCIETY418-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાંકી** — *ṭā̃kī* — "a water tank".

> છત પરની ટાંકીમાં પાણી નથી.
>
> *chat parnī ṭā̃kīmā̃ pāṇī nathī.*
>
> There is no water in the tank on the roof.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાંકી** (*ṭā̃kī*) goes back to *\*ṭaṅka*, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a cistern" (CDIAL 5430).
