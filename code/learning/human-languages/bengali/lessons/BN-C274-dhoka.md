## You'll want to know: ঢোকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C274-DHOKA-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C59-FOOD59-04, BN-LEX-C86-CLOTHES86-01, BN-LEX-C49-VERBS49-05, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-LEX-C82-PLACE82-02] -->

**ঢোকা** — *ḍhokā* — "to enter, to go in".

> **বাড়িতে ঢুকে আমি জুতো খুললাম।**
>
> *bāṛite ḍhuke āmi juto khullām.* — "I came into the house and took my shoes off."

The place you enter takes **-এ** or **-তে**: **বাড়িতে**, **দোকানে**. The **ও** of **ঢোকা** rises to **উ** in the past, the *-e* form and the **-লে** form, as **খোলা** does: **সে দোকানে ঢুকল** (*she dokāne ḍhuklô*), "she went into the shop"; **ঢুকে**; **ঢুকলে**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢোকা** (*ḍhokā*) is listed in CDIAL, as *ḍhukā*, "to enter", under a reconstructed *\*ḍhukyati*, "approaches" (CDIAL 5592): a form no text records, hence the star.
