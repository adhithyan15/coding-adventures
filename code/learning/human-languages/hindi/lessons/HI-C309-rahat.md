## You'll want to know: राहत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C309-RAHAT309-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राहत** — *rāhat* — "relief".

> सरकार ने गाँव में राहत भेजी।
>
> *sarkār ne gā̃v meṁ rāhat bhejī.*
>
> The government sent relief to the village.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राहत** (*rāhat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rāḥa*, "rest, ease". It is both the feeling of relief and the food and blankets sent after a disaster.
