## You'll want to know: うすめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C474-PREP474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うすめる** — *usumeru* — "to dilute; to water down".

> **しょうゆを みずで うすめて ください。** — *shōyu o mizu de usumete kudasai* — "Please dilute the soy sauce with water."

Polite **うすめます**, *usumemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが おさけを うすめました。**, *chichi ga osake o usumemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うすめる** (*usumeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
