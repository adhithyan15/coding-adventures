## You'll want to know: البم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C513-FURSAT513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**البم** — *album* — "an album (for photos or stamps)".

> سارا نے نیا البم خریدا۔
>
> *Sārā ne nayā album kharīdā.*
>
> Sara bought a new album.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**البم** (*album*) is borrowed from English *album*.
