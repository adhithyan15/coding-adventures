## You'll want to know: ફૂંકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C509-DHAMAN509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂંકવું** — *phū̃kvũ* — "to blow out (a flame)".

> જન્મદિવસે મીરાએ બધી મીણબત્તીઓ ફૂંકી.
>
> *janmadivse Mīrāe badhī mīṇbattīo phū̃kī.*
>
> On her birthday Mira blew out all the candles.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મીણબત્તી ફૂંકી, *teṇe mīṇbattī phū̃kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂંકવું** (*phū̃kvũ*) comes from *\*phūtka*, "blowing", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 9102). The wind's own verb is **ફૂંકાવું**.
