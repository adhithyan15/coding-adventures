## You'll want to know: दिशादर्शक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C407-VEG407-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दिशादर्शक** — *diśādarśak* — "a signpost".

> चौकात एक मोठा दिशादर्शक आहे.
>
> *caukāt ek moṭhā diśādarśak āhe.*
>
> There is a big signpost at the junction.

Masculine.

A formal word: a board that shows the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिशादर्शक** (*diśādarśak*) is built from Sanskrit *diśā*, "direction", and *darśaka*, "showing" (Monier-Williams).
