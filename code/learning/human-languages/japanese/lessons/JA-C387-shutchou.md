## You'll want to know: しゅっちょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C387-DEMUKAERU387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっちょう** — *shutchō* — "a business trip".

> **ちちは しゅっちょうで がいこくに いきました。** — *chichi wa shutchō de gaikoku ni ikimashita* — "My father went abroad on a business trip."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっちょう** (*shutchō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shutsu*, "exit" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwit*) and *chō*, "stretch" (*trjang*), joined as *shutchō* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
