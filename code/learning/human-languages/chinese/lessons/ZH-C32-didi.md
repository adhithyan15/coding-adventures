## You'll want to know: dìdi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C32-FAMILY32-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dìdi* — "younger brother".

> *wǒ dìdi bā suì* — My little brother is eight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìdi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as the brother born later.
