## You'll want to know: pistillum, pistillī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C351-CONCIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pistillum, pistillī** — "a pestle".

> Pistillum ligneum erat et grave.
>
> *The pestle was wooden and heavy.*

Neuter, from a verb meaning 'to pound'. English *pestle* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pistillum** is built on *pīnsō*, "to pound" (EtymDB), with a small-tool ending. English *pestle* comes from it through French, and *pistil* directly.
