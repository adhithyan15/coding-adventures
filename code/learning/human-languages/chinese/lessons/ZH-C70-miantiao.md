## You'll want to know: miàntiáo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C70-FOOD70-03]; assesses=[] -->

*miàntiáo* — "noodles".

> *wǒ xǐhuan chī miàntiáo* — I love noodles.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miàntiáo* is built from miàn 'flour' and tiáo 'a twig, a strip', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *menH* and *dew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): strips of flour.
