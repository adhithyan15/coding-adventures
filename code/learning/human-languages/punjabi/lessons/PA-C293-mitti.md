## You'll want to know: ਮਿੱਟੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C293-LAND293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਿੱਟੀ** — *miṭṭī* — "soil; earth".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਖੇਤ ਦੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਨਰਮ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍe khet dī miṭṭī naram hai.*
>
> The soil of our field is soft.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਿੱਟੀ** (*miṭṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mṛttikā*, "earth, clay"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *miṭṭī*, "earth, soil, clay", under it (CDIAL 10286).
