## You'll want to know: بسطة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C455-WAJIHA455-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بسطة** — *basṭa* — "a market stall".

> يبيع جاري الفاكهة على بسطة في السوق.
>
> *yabīʿu jārī al-fākiha ʿalā basṭa fī as-sūq.*
>
> My neighbour sells fruit on a stall in the market.

The word is common in the Levant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بسطة** (*basṭa*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-s-ṭ*, "to spread out" (Lane). A *basṭa* is goods spread out for sale.
