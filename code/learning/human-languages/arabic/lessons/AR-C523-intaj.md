## You'll want to know: إنتاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C523-INJAZ523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**إنتاج** — *intāj* — "production".

> زاد إنتاج المصنع هذه السنة.
>
> *zāda intāj al-maṣnaʿ hādhihi as-sana.*
>
> The factory's production increased this year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إنتاج** (*intāj*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-t-j*, "to bring forth young" (Lane). Intāj is the verbal noun of antaja, to produce.
