## You'll want to know: వీలునామా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C345-SMRTI345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**వీలునామా** — *vīlunāmā* — "a will; a testament".

> తాతగారు ఇల్లు నా పేరు మీద వీలునామా రాశారు.
>
> *tātagāru illu nā pēru mīda vīlunāmā rāśāru.*
>
> Grandfather wrote a will leaving the house to me.

వీలునామా రాయు, to make a will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వీలునామా** (*vīlunāmā*) ends in Persian *nāma*, "a writing, a deed", as several loans for documents do; the origin of the first part is not settled.
