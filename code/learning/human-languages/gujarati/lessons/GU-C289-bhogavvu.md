# ભોગવવું (bhogavvũ) — to undergo; to serve (a sentence)

## You'll want to know: ભોગવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C289-ADALAT289-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભોગવવું** — *bhogavvũ* — "to undergo; to serve (a sentence)".

> ગુનેગારે બે વરસની જેલ ભોગવી.
>
> *gunegāre be varasnī jel bhogvī.*
>
> The criminal served two years in jail.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સજા ભોગવી, *teṇe sajā bhogvī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભોગવવું** (*bhogavvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *bhoga*, "enjoyment, use" (Monier-Williams): to have something to the full. In Gujarati it is also used of what one has to go through, good or bad: a prisoner **સજા ભોગવે છે**, serves his sentence.

One more word for telling what happened.
