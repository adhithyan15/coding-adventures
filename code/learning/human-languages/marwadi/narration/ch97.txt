Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 97: Underneath, In the middle, Near, Opposite, Left.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हेठै (heṭhai) — underneath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for behind, then the Marwadi for on top of.

You'll want to know: हेठै.
हेठै — heṭhai — "underneath".

The word, taken apart.
हेठै (heṭhai) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It goes back to adhastāt, "underneath", through a variant CDIAL reconstructs, adhiṣṭāt, whose ṣṭ gave the ṭh. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 248); the match is through Hindi heṭh and Gujarati heṭhe, which it lists under adhiṣṭāt.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कनै (kanai) — near, beside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for underneath, then the Marwadi for in the middle.

You'll want to know: कनै.
कनै — kanai — "near, beside".

The word, taken apart.
कनै (kanai) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from karṇa, "ear". CDIAL lists Old Marwari kanai under it (CDIAL 2830). CDIAL explains it as the word for "ear" used as an adverb, "at the side, near".

सामी (sāmī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from saṃmukha, "facing". CDIAL lists Old Marwari sāmo under it (CDIAL 12982). It is built on sam, "with", and mukha, "face".

Lesson 5 of 5.
डावो (ḍāvo) — left.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for near, then the Marwadi for opposite.

You'll want to know: डावो.
डावो — ḍāvo — "left".

The word, taken apart.
डावो (ḍāvo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ḍāva, "lefthand", one of a group of forms CDIAL reconstructs from their descendants and heads ḍavva, "lefthand, crosswise". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5539); the match is through Hindi ḍāwā, which it lists under ḍāva. CDIAL calls the word non-Aryan, and cites a view that it is Dravidian.
