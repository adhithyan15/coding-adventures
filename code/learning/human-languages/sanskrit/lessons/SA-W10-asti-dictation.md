## Writing — conjunct dictation

[YOU HEAR: *asti*, “is”]

Write the word from sound and meaning alone. Stop after one attempt. Now
uncover and compare: **अस्ति**. Check **अ**, joined **स्त**, and the short-*i*
sign. Repair only the first difference. Credit requires the whole Devanagari
word; a Latin-letter transliteration or separated consonants do not count.
