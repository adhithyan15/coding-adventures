## Chapters 99-152: 270 headwords, two number lessons split, and Punjabi attains A1

Punjabi had four kinds of A1 gap:

- four A1 spine nodes had no lesson: time of day, definite reference, everyday
  things, and saying what I have and can do;
- it taught 344 headwords against 600;
- two chapter-6 number lessons introduced more than three atoms each;
- thirteen atoms were revisited fewer than twice.

**The words.** Fifty-four chapters of five, closed by twelve review lessons:

- **time:** **ਅੱਧੀ ਰਾਤ**, **ਤੜਕਾ**, **ਪਰਸੋਂ**, the seasons (**ਗਰਮੀ**,
  **ਸਰਦੀ**, **ਬਰਸਾਤ**, **ਬਸੰਤ**, **ਪਤਝੜ**), and the months in both
  calendars, from **ਜਨਵਰੀ** to **ਚੇਤ** and **ਫੱਗਣ** (TIME-OF-DAY)
- **this and that:** **ਕਿਤੇ**, **ਕਦੇ**, **ਹਰੇਕ**, **ਅਜਿਹਾ**, **ਕੁਝ**,
  **ਦੋਵੇਂ** and more (DEFINITE-REFERENCE)
- **have and can:** **ਸਕਣਾ**, **ਕੋਲ**, **ਤਾਕਤ**, **ਹੱਕ**, **ਮੌਕਾ**,
  **ਮਾਹਰ** and more (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **things:** 135 of them, covering the house, clothes, food, nature,
  animals, the body, the town, school and work (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** forty describing words (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** twenty-five, from **ਟੱਪਣਾ** to **ਬਦਲਣਾ**, plus three among the
  have-and-can words

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text. Every word uses only the 47 signs the book
already teaches, so no word uses ਯ, ਢ or an independent ਏ or ਓ.

**The splits.**

- **one to five** keeps the five numbers and the top-line clue. The two marks
  that look alike (the addak **ੱ** and the tippi **ੰ**) and *panj* as a
  number move to *numbers 1-5, more*.
- **panj convergence** was two splits deep. It now keeps the comparison: every
  neighbour says *-ch*, and Persian says *panj*. The next continuation shows
  Punjabi's *panj* is inherited from Sanskrit *pañca*. The one after shows
  Persian reached the same shape separately.

The continuations pushed the top-line clue, the addak and the tippi past their
R1 window. Each is retrieved in the next lesson's warm-up.

**The revisits.** Thirteen thin atoms get warm-up retrievals in the new
chapters, including:

- *rahiṇā* and the dropped "in";
- *kamm karnā*;
- *panjvāṁ* and the *-vāṁ* ordinal;
- *jo … oh*;
- *kitthe*'s addak;
- the reading skills.
