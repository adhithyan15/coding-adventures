## Fixed — chapters 6, 18, 20 and 25 payoffs cover their chapters

Nine chapter payoffs sat under the 0.5 representativeness floor. Four of them
now clear it. The other five stay below it because of lesson order, and each
one's ledger note now gives the reason.

- **Chapter 6** (2/12 → 9/12). TA-C06-dative-subject-family gains a "Guided
  Practice — the ending underneath" recall: *peyarukku*, *vēlaikku* and
  irregular *enakku*, how *-ukku* stacks where Latin *-īs* fuses, and why
  knowing takes the dative. It also gains a "Script — வ, ம and ண" section:
  point to the three letters in வணக்கம், say the retroflex *ṇ*, and recall
  their shapes. TA-S01-va and TA-W02-ma-retroflex-na are now prerequisites.
  The three atoms left out (ண TA-S02-nna, TA-W02-three-ns, ப TA-S101-letter-pa)
  are taught after the payoff. Computed duration 242 s; max_seconds 240 → 250.
- **Chapter 18** (3/8 → 7/8). TA-C18-mani-homophone-time gains a
  "Script — the puḷḷi and இல்லை" section: write இல்லை piece by piece, point
  to the puḷḷi and the ai hook, and say what each one does. TA-S08-pulli and
  TA-W06-write-illai are now prerequisites, and the lesson now lists the
  writing skill. த (TA-S122-letter-ta) is taught after the payoff.
  Computed duration 207 s (max 240).
- **Chapter 20** (2/6 → 5/6). TA-C20-vaanilai gains a "Guided Practice —
  eleven to twenty" recall: *patiṉoṉḏṟu* and *paṉṉiraṇḍu* as ten plus a
  digit, *irupathu* as "two-tens", and the contrast with Sanskrit, Latin and
  Arabic. TA-C20-pathinondru-irupathu is now a prerequisite. This matches the
  Kannada and Malayalam chapter-20 fixes. The "native" hedge is shorter, so
  the computed duration is 274 s; max_seconds 240 → 280. ய (TA-S124-letter-ya)
  is taught after the payoff.
- **Chapter 25** (2/5 → 5/5). TA-C25-goodnight-alternatives gains a recall of
  the shared *śubha rātri* that Tamil does without, plus a "Script — ா and
  நான்" section: read மா, நா, நான் and point to the ா sign and the puḷḷi.
  TA-W10-read-naan is now a prerequisite. Computed duration 217 s (max 240).
