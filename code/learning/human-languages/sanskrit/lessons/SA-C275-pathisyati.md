# पठिष्यति · द्रक्ष्यति (paṭhiṣyati · drakṣyati) — each verb's own future

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-02, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "she will do it" and "we will do it". (*sā kariṣyati*, *vayaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ*.) Then say "I will read". (*paṭhiṣyāmi*.) The endings stay the same; the stem belongs to each verb.

## You'll want to know: -इष्य-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-STEM-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-PATHATI-READ, SA-LEX-KHADATI-EAT, SA-LEX-LIKHATI-WRITE, SA-LEX-ASTI-BHAVATI-BE, SA-LEX-C35-HOUSE-04, SA-LEX-C24-ASK-03] -->

> **सः पठिष्यति।**
>
> *saḥ paṭhiṣyati.* — "He will read."

Most verbs you know make the future with **-इष्य-** (*-iṣya-*) after the root: **पठति** gives **पठिष्यति**, and **खादति** (*khādati*), "eats", gives **खादिष्यति** (*khādiṣyati*).

> **मीरा पुस्तकं पठिष्यति।**
>
> *mīrā pustakaṃ paṭhiṣyati.* — "Mira will read the book."

> **बालः फलं खादिष्यति।**
>
> *bālaḥ phalaṃ khādiṣyati.* — "The boy will eat the fruit."

**वदति** (*vadati*), "speaks", gives **वदिष्यति** (*vadiṣyati*). **भवति** gives **भविष्यति** (*bhaviṣyati*), the "will be" you know. **लिखति** (*likhati*) changes its vowel: **लेखिष्यति** (*lekhiṣyati*), "will write".

> **सा श्वः लेखिष्यति।**
>
> *sā śvaḥ lekhiṣyati.* — "She will write tomorrow."

## You'll want to know: -स्य-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-STEM-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-PASHYATI-SEE, SA-LEX-C72-HOUSEWORK-05, SA-LEX-STAND, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-03, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-05, SA-LEX-PANIYAM-WATER] -->

A few verbs take **-स्य-** (*-sya-*) instead, on the root itself. **पश्यति** (*paśyati*), "sees", has the root √dṛś, and its future is **द्रक्ष्यति** (*drakṣyati*):

> **सः नगरं द्रक्ष्यति।**
>
> *saḥ nagaraṃ drakṣyati.* — "He will see the city."

**ददाति** (*dadāti*), "gives", has **दास्यति** (*dāsyati*); **तिष्ठति** (*tiṣṭhati*), "stands, stays", has **स्थास्यति** (*sthāsyati*):

> **सा पानीयं दास्यति।**
>
> *sā pānīyaṃ dāsyati.* — "She will give water."

> **अहम् अत्र स्थास्यामि।**
>
> *aham atra sthāsyāmi.* — "I will stay here."

| the present | the future |
|---|---|
| **पठति**, **खादति**, **वदति** | **पठिष्यति**, **खादिष्यति**, **वदिष्यति** |
| **लिखति** | **लेखिष्यति** |
| **गच्छति** | **गमिष्यति** |
| **पश्यति** | **द्रक्ष्यति** |
| **ददाति**, **तिष्ठति** | **दास्यति**, **स्थास्यति** |

So a future is learned with its verb, as a dictionary gives it: Monier-Williams lists **द्रक्ष्यति** under √dṛś and **लेखिष्यति** under √likh.
