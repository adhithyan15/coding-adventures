# meiner Meinung nach — in my opinion

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-DASS-VERB-ENDE-01, GE-LEX-MEINEN-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think that is right", with *dass*. (*Ich finde, dass das
richtig ist.*)

[PAUSE 1s] There is a shorter way in, and it does not move the verb to the
end.

## You'll want to know: meiner Meinung nach
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-MEINER-MEINUNG-NACH-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-C165-THING165-05, GE-LEX-MEIN-01] -->

**meiner Meinung nach** — "in my opinion": *MY-ner MY-noong nahkh*.

> *Meiner Meinung nach ist das richtig.* — In my opinion, that is right.

Two things are unusual in it. **Nach** comes after its noun, and here it
means "according to". *Nach meiner Meinung* exists too, but *nach* last is
the usual order. And **meiner** is *meine* with a case ending,
the form *nach* asks for. With "your": *deiner Meinung nach*.

## Grammar Lens: one place, then the verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-GRAMMAR-MEINUNG-ERSTE-STELLE-02]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-VERB-SECOND-02, GE-GRAMMAR-DANN-VERB-ZWEI-02, GE-LEX-C93-ADJ5-03] -->

The whole phrase, three words long, takes the first place of the sentence,
exactly as *dann* did. So the verb comes next, and the subject waits behind
it:

> *Meiner Meinung nach* **ist** *das wichtig.*
> *Meiner Meinung nach* **hat** *der Zug Verspätung.*

Compare the two ways in:

> *Ich finde, dass das wichtig* **ist**. — the verb at the end.
> *Meiner Meinung nach* **ist** *das wichtig.* — the verb second.

Same opinion, two word orders. *Meiner Meinung nach* is at home in a
discussion and in writing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-MEINEN-02] -->

**Meinung** is the verb *meinen* + *-ung*, from Old High German *meinen*
(EtymDB 2.1).

**nach** is set by EtymDB 2.1 beside *nah*, "near", from Old High German
*nāh*, and beside English *nigh*. From "near" it came to mean "after" and,
behind a noun, "according to"; EtymDB does not record those steps, so they
are given here as the usual account.
