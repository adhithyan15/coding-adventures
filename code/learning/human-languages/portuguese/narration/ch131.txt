Portuguese, chapter 131: To visit, To put, To believe, To drive, To get dressed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
visitar — to visit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for ninety, then the Portuguese for to begin.

You'll want to know: visitar.
visitar — "to visit".
