Japanese, chapter 374: Along the Railway Line.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
せんろ (senro) — a railway track.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for fare adjustment, then the Japanese for to pass by.

You'll want to know: せんろ.
せんろ — senro — "a railway track".
せんろの むこうに こうえんが あります。 — senro no mukō ni kōen ga arimasu — "There is a park on the other side of the railway line."

The word, taken apart.
せんろ (senro) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are sen, "line" (Middle Chinese sjenH) and ro, "road" (luH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ふみきり (fumikiri) — a level crossing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to pass by, then the Japanese for a railway track.

You'll want to know: ふみきり.
ふみきり — fumikiri — "a level crossing".
ふみきりの まえで くるまが とまりました。 — fumikiri no mae de kuruma ga tomarimashita — "The car stopped in front of the level crossing."

The word, taken apart.
ふみきり (fumikiri) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from fumi, "stepping" (from fumu) and kiri, "cutting" (from kiru); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ちかよる (chikayoru) — to go near; to approach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for stopping (of a train or bus), then the Japanese for departure (of a train or bus).

You'll want to know: ちかよる.
ちかよる — chikayoru — "to go near; to approach".
せんろに ちかよらないで ください。 — senro ni chikayoranaide kudasai — "Please keep away from the railway line."
Polite ちかよります, chikayorimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): こどもが せんろに ちかよりました。, kodomo ga senro ni chikayorimashita.

The word, taken apart.
ちかよる (chikayoru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from chika, "near" (from chikai) and yoru, "to draw close"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
