## You'll want to know: der Kredit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C388-KREDIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kredit** — "the bank loan, the credit".

> Für das Haus haben sie einen Kredit bei der Bank aufgenommen.
>
> *They took out a bank loan for the house.*

Plural: *die Kredite*. *Einen Kredit aufnehmen* — to take out a loan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kredit* was borrowed from French *crédit* (EtymDB), which goes back through Italian *credito* to Latin *crēditum* "a loan", from *crēdere* "to trust, to believe" (Lewis & Short).
