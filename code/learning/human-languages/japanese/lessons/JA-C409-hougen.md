## You'll want to know: ほうげん

**ほうげん** — *hōgen* — "a dialect".

> **そふは いなかの ほうげんで はなします。** — *sofu wa inaka no hōgen de hanashimasu* — "My grandfather speaks a country dialect."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうげん** (*hōgen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hō*, "direction" (Middle Chinese *pjang*) and *gen*, "say" (*ngjon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
