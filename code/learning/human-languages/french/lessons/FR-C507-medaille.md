## You'll want to know: la médaille
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C507-TOURNOI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la médaille** — "medal".

> Elle a gagné une médaille d'argent.
>
> *She won a silver medal.*

*Le revers de la médaille* — the other side of the coin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la médaille* was borrowed from Italian *medaglia* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *metallum* "metal, a mine", from Greek *métallon*.
