Lesson 1 of 5.
আটকানো (āṭkāno) — to block, to hold up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to give back, then the Bengali for to remember.

You'll want to know: আটকানো.
আটকানো — āṭkāno — "to block, to hold up".

The word, taken apart.
আটকানো (āṭkāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aṭṭakk, "obstruct, stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 182). CDIAL lists Bengali āṭkāna, "to obstruct".

জমা দেওয়া (jômā deoyā) — to hand in, to deposit.

You'll want to know: জমা দেওয়া.
জমা দেওয়া — jômā deoyā — "to hand in, to deposit".

The phrase, taken apart.
জমা দেওয়া (jômā deoyā) is jômā, "a deposit, a sum", from Arabic jamʿ, "a gathering together", which reached Bengali through Persian (Lane), + deoyā, "to give", from Sanskrit dadāti, "gives" (CDIAL 6141).

ডোবা — ḍobā — "to sink, to drown".

The word, taken apart.
ডোবা (ḍobā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ḍubb, "sink", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5561). CDIAL lists Bengali ḍubā, "to sink", and takes the form as a reshuffling of Middle Indo-Aryan buḍḍaï.

ভাসা (bhāsā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhraśyati, "falls" (CDIAL 9654). CDIAL lists Bengali bhāsā, "to float, drift along".
