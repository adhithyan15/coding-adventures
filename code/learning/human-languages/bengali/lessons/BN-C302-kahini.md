## You'll want to know: কাহিনি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C302-KAHINI302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**কাহিনি** — *kāhini* — "a tale; a narrative".

> দাদু রাতে আমাদের একটা পুরনো কাহিনি বললেন।
>
> *dādu rāte āmāder ekṭā purôno kāhini bôllen.*
>
> At night my grandfather told us an old tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাহিনি** (*kāhini*) comes down from Sanskrit *kathana*, 'narration' (CDIAL 2702, which lists Bengali *kahinī*, 'a story'); it is a cousin of **কথা**, talk.
