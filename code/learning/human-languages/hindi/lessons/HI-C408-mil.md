## You'll want to know: मील
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C408-MIL408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मील** — *mīl* — "a mile".

> स्टेशन यहाँ से दो मील है।
>
> *sṭeśan yahā̃ se do mīl hai.*
>
> The station is two miles from here.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मील** (*mīl*) is borrowed from English *mile*; road signs now give kilometres, but people still count in miles.
