## You'll want to know: l'inchiesta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C284-INCHIESTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'inchiesta** — "the inquiry, the investigation by journalists or officials".

> Il giornale ha fatto un'inchiesta sugli ospedali.
>
> *The newspaper ran an inquiry into the hospitals.*

Police, judges and journalists all run *inchieste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inchiesta* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *inquaesita* "a thing sought into", from Latin *inquīrere* "to search into", built on *quaerere* "to seek" (Lewis & Short).
