## You'll want to know: la profumeria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C453-NEGOZI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la profumeria** — "the perfume shop, the perfumery".

> In profumeria ho comprato una crema.
>
> *At the perfume shop I bought a cream.*

Make-up and soaps too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*profumeria* is built on *profumo* 'scent', from *per* 'through' and *fumo* 'smoke', which is inherited from Latin *fūmus* (Lewis & Short): scent carried through smoke.
