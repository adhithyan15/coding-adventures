Bengali, chapter 283: Crime and the Police.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
অপরাধ (ôpôrādh) — a crime; an offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to push, then the Bengali for a pickpocket.

You'll want to know: অপরাধ.
অপরাধ — ôpôrādh — "a crime; an offence".
যদি পুলিশ তাড়াতাড়ি না আসে, তাহলে চোরেরা আবার অপরাধ করবে। jôdi pulish tāṛātāṛi nā āse, tāhôle chorerā ābār ôpôrādh kôrbe. If the police don't come quickly, the thieves will commit crimes again.

The word, taken apart.
অপরাধ (ôpôrādh) is Sanskrit aparādha, 'an offence, a transgression' (Monier-Williams), with apa-, 'away': a going astray. অপরাধ করা, to commit a crime.

অভিযোগ — ôbhijog — "a complaint; an accusation".
দোকানদার থানায় গিয়ে অভিযোগ করল। dokāndār thānāy giye ôbhijog kôrlô. The shopkeeper went to the police station and made a complaint.

The word, taken apart.
অভিযোগ (ôbhijog) is Sanskrit abhiyoga, 'an application, an earnest effort' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a complaint, a charge' is the one Bengali uses. অভিযোগ করা, to complain.

Lesson 3 of 6.
গ্রেফতার করা (grephtār kôrā) — to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a crime, then the Bengali for a complaint.

You'll want to know: গ্রেফতার করা.
গ্রেফতার করা — grephtār kôrā — "to arrest".
পুলিশ দুই দিন পরে চোরকে গ্রেফতার করল। pulish dui din pôre chorke grephtār kôrlô. The police arrested the thief two days later.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ডাকাতকে গ্রেফতার করলেন, tini ḍākātke grephtār kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
গ্রেফতার করা (grephtār kôrā) puts গ্রেফতার — usually traced to Persian giriftār, 'taken, captured' — before করা: to make someone a captive.

Lesson 4 of 6.
তদন্ত (tôdôntô) — an investigation; an inquiry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a complaint, then the Bengali for to arrest.

You'll want to know: তদন্ত.
তদন্ত — tôdôntô — "an investigation; an inquiry".
পুলিশ ঘটনার তদন্ত শুরু করল। pulish ghôṭônār tôdôntô shuru kôrlô. The police began an investigation into the incident.

The word, taken apart.
তদন্ত (tôdôntô) is usually explained as Sanskrit tad, 'that', and anta, 'end': going to the end of a matter. Monier-Williams lists tadanta, 'coming to an end by that'; the sense 'an inquiry' is the modern one.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: দারোগা.
দারোগা — dārogā — "a police inspector".
থানার দারোগা সাক্ষীকে অনেক প্রশ্ন জিজ্ঞাসা করলেন। thānār dārogā sākkhīke ônek prôshno jigyāsā kôrlen. The inspector at the station asked the witness a lot of questions.

The word, taken apart.
দারোগা (dārogā) is usually traced to Persian dārogha, an overseer or chief officer; in Bengali it is the officer in charge of a police station.
