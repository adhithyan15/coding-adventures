## You'll want to know: divorciado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C550-DIVORCIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**divorciado** — "divorced".

> Está divorciado y tiene dos hijos.
>
> *He is divorced and has two children.*

Used with *estar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*divorciado* is built on the participle of *divorciar*, from *divorcio*, a learned word from Latin *dīvortium* 'a separation' (EtymDB).
