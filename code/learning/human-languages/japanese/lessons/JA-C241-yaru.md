## You'll want to know: やる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C241-OUTING241-05]; assesses=[] -->

**やる** — *yaru* — "to do; to play (a game)".

> **きのう ともだちと しょうぎを やりました。** — *kinō tomodachi to shōgi o yarimashita* — "Yesterday I played shogi with a friend."

Polite **やります**, *yarimasu*; a godan verb, more casual than **します**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やる** (*yaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and the word is usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
