Russian, chapter 345: Fair and unfair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
справедливый (spravedlívyy) — fair, even-handed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to reason, then the Russian for obvious.

You'll want to know: справедливый.
справедливый (spravedlívyy) — "fair, even-handed".
Наш учитель строгий, но справедливый. Nash uchítel strógiy, no spravedlívyy. Our teacher is strict but fair.
Справедливо — fairly, or it is fair.

The word, taken apart.
справедливый (spravedlívyy) is usually traced to the root of правда "truth" and право "right", Proto-Slavic pravŭ "right, correct" (EtymDB), through a Church Slavonic formation.

Lesson 2 of 5.
несправедливый (nespravedlívyy) — unfair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for obvious, then the Russian for fair.

You'll want to know: несправедливый.
несправедливый (nespravedlívyy) — "unfair".
Это несправедливо: он работал больше всех. Éto nespravedlívo: on rabótal bólshe vsekh. That is unfair: he worked more than anyone.
Short form несправедлив. Несправедливо — it is unfair.

The word, taken apart.
несправедливый (nespravedlívyy) is не- "not" in front of справедливый (spravedlívyy) "fair", which is usually traced to the root of право, Proto-Slavic pravŭ "right, correct" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
справедливость (spravedlívost) — fairness, justice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for fair, then the Russian for unfair.

You'll want to know: справедливость.
справедливость (spravedlívost) — "fairness, justice".
Люди хотят справедливости. Lyúdi khotyát spravedlívosti. People want justice.
Feminine. After хотеть in this sense it takes the genitive.

The word, taken apart.
справедливость (spravedlívost) is built on справедливый (spravedlívyy) "fair" with the abstract suffix -ость; the root is that of право, Proto-Slavic pravŭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
разумный (razúmnyy) — reasonable, sensible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for unfair, then the Russian for fairness.

You'll want to know: разумный.
разумный (razúmnyy) — "reasonable, sensible".
Это разумное решение. Éto razúmnoye reshéniye. That is a sensible decision.
Short form разумен. В разумных пределах — within reason.

The word, taken apart.
разумный (razúmnyy) is built on разум "reason", which Russian borrowed from Church Slavonic; it comes from Proto-Slavic orzumŭ, built on umŭ "mind", the word that gives ум (EtymDB). The ра- at the start is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian also has роз-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
истина (ístina) — the truth (in a deep sense).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for fairness, then the Russian for reasonable.

You'll want to know: истина.
истина (ístina) — "the truth (in a deep sense)".
Он думает, что знает истину. On dúmayet, chto znáyet ístinu. He thinks he knows the truth.
Feminine. More solemn than правда: a truth in philosophy or science.

The word, taken apart.
истина (ístina) is built on the old adjective истый "true, genuine"; EtymDB sets Latvian īstens "real, true" beside it as a cousin (EtymDB).
