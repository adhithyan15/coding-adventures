## You'll want to know: le gilet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C422-TRAVERSEE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le gilet** — "the waistcoat; the cardigan; the life jacket (gilet de sauvetage)".

> Sous chaque siège, il y a un gilet de sauvetage.
>
> *Under every seat there is a life jacket.*

*Le gilet de sauvetage* — the life jacket. *Un gilet* can also be a cardigan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gilet* goes back to Turkish *yelek* 'waistcoat', which reached French through Arabic (EtymDB), probably by way of Spanish.
