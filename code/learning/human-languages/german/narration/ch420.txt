German, chapter 420: Flug gestrichen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
stornieren — to cancel (a booking).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to be stuck, then the German for to make an emergency landing.

You'll want to know: stornieren.
stornieren — "to cancel (a booking)".
Er storniert das Hotel, weil er krank ist. He cancels the hotel because he is ill.
Past participle without ge-: ich habe den Flug storniert.

The word, taken apart.
stornieren was borrowed from Italian stornare 'to turn back, to reverse an entry in the books' (EtymDB), a word from bookkeeping.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: umbuchen.
umbuchen — "to rebook, to change a booking".
Die Fluggesellschaft hat uns auf einen späteren Flug umgebucht. The airline has rebooked us onto a later flight.
Separable: ich buche um, ich habe umgebucht.

The word, taken apart.
umbuchen is built from um 'over, round' and buchen 'to book', first 'to enter in a book', from Buch 'book' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
erstatten — to refund, to pay back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the cancellation, then the German for to rebook.

You'll want to know: erstatten.
erstatten — "to refund, to pay back".
Die Firma erstattet uns die Kosten für das Hotel. The company refunds us the cost of the hotel.
Formal. Anzeige erstatten — to report a crime to the police.

The word, taken apart.
erstatten is built from er- and the old verb statten 'to put in place', from Statt 'place', an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB lists erstatten): to put money back where it was.
