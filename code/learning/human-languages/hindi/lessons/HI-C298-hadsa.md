## You'll want to know: हादसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C298-GHAYAL298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हादसा** — *hādsā* — "a mishap; an accident".

> हादसे की वजह यह थी कि ड्राइवर बहुत तेज़ गाड़ी चला रहा था।
>
> *hādse kī vajah yah thī ki ḍrāivar bahut tez gāṛī calā rahā thā.*
>
> The cause of the accident was that the driver was going too fast.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हादसा** (*hādsā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥāditha*, "something that happens". It is the everyday word beside the formal **दुर्घटना**.
