## You'll want to know: scolare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C333-PENTOLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**scolare** — "to drain".

> Scola la pasta al dente.
>
> *Drain the pasta when it is al dente.*

For pasta and vegetables: *scolare la pasta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scolare* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *excōlāre* (EtymDB), built on Latin *cōlāre* "to filter".
