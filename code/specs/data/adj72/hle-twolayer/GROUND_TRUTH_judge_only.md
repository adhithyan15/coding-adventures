# Ground truth — for the blind judge ONLY (withheld from phases 1-3)

Source: RIB 1065, the Regina tombstone (South Shields), Palmyrene Aramaic line
beneath the Latin epitaph.

**Correct translation:** "Regina, the freedwoman of Barates, alas."

Key points the grader must enforce:
- `BT ḤRY` together means **"freedwoman"** (ḤRY is from the Ḥ-R-R "free/freed"
  root). It does NOT mean "daughter."
- `BRᶜTᵓ` (rendered here `BR ᶜTᵓ`) is the **name Barates** — Regina's husband/
  former master. The relationship is **freedwoman-of-Barates**.
- `ḤBL` is the lament formula **"alas."**
- `RGYNᵓ` = **Regina**.

**The trap (and plain-Claude's failure in ADJ67):** reading `BT ... BR ...` as the
filiation formula "daughter of ... son of ..." and producing something like
"Regina, daughter of Ḥari, son of ʿAta, alas" — which DROPS the freedwoman-of-Barates
relationship. Any answer that drops the freedwoman-of-Barates relationship is WRONG.
