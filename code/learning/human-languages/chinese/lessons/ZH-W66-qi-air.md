## Script — a fall, two bars, a hook
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QI-AIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 气

**One piece, four strokes.**

**A short left-falling stroke** at the top left.

**Two short horizontals** under it, the second a little lower and shorter.

**A long horizontal that turns down**, slants out to the right and hooks up at the end.
