Bengali, chapter 169: Joy, Shame, Fear, Anger, Peace.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আনন্দ (ānôndô) — joy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a wedding, then the Bengali for a relative.

You'll want to know: আনন্দ.
আনন্দ — ānôndô — "joy".

The word, taken apart.
আনন্দ (ānôndô) is Sanskrit ānanda, "happiness", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 1172).

Lesson 4 of 5.
রাগ (rāg) — anger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for shame, then the Bengali for fear.

You'll want to know: রাগ.
রাগ — rāg — "anger".

The word, taken apart.
রাগ (rāg) is Sanskrit rāga, "passion", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10674). In Bengali it has come to mean "anger"; CDIAL lists the verb rāgā, "to get into a rage", under rajyate, "takes pleasure in" (CDIAL 10584).
