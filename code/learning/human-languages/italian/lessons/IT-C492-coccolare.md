## You'll want to know: coccolare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C492-ABBRACCI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**coccolare** — "to cuddle, to pamper".

> Il nonno coccola il nipotino tutto il giorno.
>
> *Grandpa cuddles his little grandson all day.*

Also to spoil with attention: *coccolarsi* — to treat oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *coccolare* is not settled; it may come from a word children use.
