## You'll want to know: 当真
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C373-PROSCONS373-05]; assesses=[] -->

**当真** — *dàngzhēn* — "to take it seriously; to take at face value".

> *nà shì qìhuà nǐ bié dàngzhēn* — Those were angry words; don't take them seriously.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**当真** (*dàngzhēn*) is built from 当 and 真 'true', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tangH* and *tsyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be in charge' and the second 'true, as against false'.
