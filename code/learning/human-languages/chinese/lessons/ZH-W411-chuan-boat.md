## Script — the boat piece, then 口 under a small frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CHUAN-BOAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 船

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the boat piece, six strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left. A long stroke down the left side, falling to the left at the foot. A bar that turns down the right side and hooks to the left. A dot inside, a bar rising to the right across the middle, and a last dot under it.

**The right piece, five strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left, and a bar that turns down and bends to the right at the foot: a small frame. Then 口 under it, three strokes.

6 + 5 = eleven.
