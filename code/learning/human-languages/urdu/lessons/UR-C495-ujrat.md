## You'll want to know: اجرت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C495-AJRAT495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اجرت** — *ujrat* — "wages; payment for work".

> مزدوروں کو ان کی اجرت شام کو ملتی ہے۔
>
> *mazdūroṅ ko un kī ujrat shām ko miltī hai.*
>
> The labourers get their wages in the evening.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجرت** (*ujrat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ujra*, "a wage, a fee".
