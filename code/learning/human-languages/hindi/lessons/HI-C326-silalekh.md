## You'll want to know: शिलालेख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C326-KHANDAHAR326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शिलालेख** — *śilālekh* — "an inscription (on stone)".

> उस शिलालेख पर एक राजा का नाम है।
>
> *us śilālekh par ek rājā kā nām hai.*
>
> That inscription bears a king's name.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिलालेख** (*śilālekh*) joins Sanskrit *śilā*, "a stone", and *lekha*, "a writing" (Monier-Williams): words cut into rock, one of the oldest sources of history.
