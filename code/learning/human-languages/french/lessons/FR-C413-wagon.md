## You'll want to know: le wagon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C413-QUAI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le wagon** — "the carriage (of a train)".

> Notre place est dans le wagon numéro douze.
>
> *Our seat is in carriage number twelve.*

Rail staff often say *la voiture*; most people say *le wagon*. *Le wagon-restaurant* — the dining car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wagon* was borrowed from English *wagon*, which came from Dutch *wagen* (EtymDB).
