## You'll want to know: கீறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C443-VIRISAL443-05]; assesses=[] -->

**கீறு** — *kīṟu* — "to scratch".

> பூனை என் கையைக் கீறியது.
>
> *pūṉai eṉ kaiyaik kīṟiyatu.*
>
> The cat scratched my hand.

Past (I …): கீறினேன், *kīṟiṉēṉ*.

Also to cut a line: கோடு கீறு, to scratch a line.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கீறு** (*kīṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1623), "to scratch, to draw lines".
