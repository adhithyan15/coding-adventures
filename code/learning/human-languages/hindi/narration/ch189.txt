Hindi, chapter 189: A letter, A story, A question, An answer, A mistake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चिट्ठी (ciṭṭhī) — a letter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an aunt, then the Hindi for a nephew.

You'll want to know: चिट्ठी.
चिट्ठी — ciṭṭhī — "a letter".

The word, taken apart.
चिट्ठी (ciṭṭhī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ciṣṭa, "message", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4832). CDIAL compares Avestan činasti, "makes known".

The word, taken apart.
कहानी (kahānī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kathānaka, "a little tale" (CDIAL 2705). It is built on kathā, "story".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ग़लती (ġaltī) — a mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a question, then the Hindi for an answer.

You'll want to know: ग़लती.
ग़लती — ġaltī — "a mistake".

The word, taken apart.
ग़लती (ġaltī) is ġalat, "wrong", with the ending -ī that makes a noun. ġalat comes from Arabic ghalaṭ, "an error", through Persian; its root is gh-l-ṭ (Lane).
