## You'll want to know: lùxiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-05]; assesses=[] -->

*lùxiàn* — "route".

> *qù jīchǎng de lùxiàn hěn jiǎndān* — The route to the airport is simple.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lùxiàn* is built from 路 'road' and *xiàn* 'a line', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luH* and *sjenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'thread'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
