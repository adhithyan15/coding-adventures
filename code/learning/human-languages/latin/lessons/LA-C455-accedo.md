## You'll want to know: accēdō, accēdere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C455-CONSPICOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**accēdō, accēdere** — "to come near, to approach; to be added".

> Nāvis ad portum accēdit.
>
> *The ship comes near the harbour.*

*accēdō, accēdere, accessī, accessum*, with *ad* and the accusative. English *access* and *accede* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accēdō* is built from *ad-* 'towards' and *cēdō* 'to go' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *access* and *accede* come from it.
