## You'll want to know: रागः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C520-SVARA520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रागः** — *rāgaḥ* — "a raga".

> प्रातः गायकः मधुरं रागं गायति।
>
> *prātaḥ gāyakaḥ madhuraṃ rāgaṃ gāyati.*
>
> In the morning the singer sings a sweet raga.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रागः** (*rāgaḥ*) comes from the root √rañj, "to be coloured, to be moved"; MW gives *rāga* as colour and passion, and as a melody, a musical mode (MW).
