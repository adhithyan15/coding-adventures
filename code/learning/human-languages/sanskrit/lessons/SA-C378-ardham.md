## You'll want to know: अर्धम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C378-LESHA378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्धम्** — *ardham* — "a half".

> यदि भवान् अर्धं खादति, तर्हि अर्धं मह्यं ददातु।
>
> *yadi bhavān ardhaṃ khādati, tarhi ardhaṃ mahyaṃ dadātu.*
>
> If you eat half, give half to me.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्धम्** (*ardham*) MW gives *ardha* "half, halved" and, as a noun, "the half"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
