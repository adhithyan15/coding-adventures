Urdu, chapter 473: Aches and Stiffness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کسکنا (kasaknā) — to ache dully; to twinge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for retching, then the Urdu for nourishment.

You'll want to know: کسکنا.
کسکنا — kasaknā — "to ache dully; to twinge".
سردی میں پرانا زخم کسکتا ہے۔ sardī meṅ purānā zakhm kasaktā hai. The old wound aches in the cold.
Past (it …): وہ زخم رات بھر کسکا, voh zakhm rāt bhar kaskā.

The word, taken apart.
کسکنا (kasaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kasaknā, "to ache", under Sanskrit kaṣati, "scratches", which an old list of roots also gives as "hurts" (CDIAL 2972).
