# ಹುಮ್ಮಸ್ಸು (hummassu) — zest; eagerness

## You'll want to know: ಹುಮ್ಮಸ್ಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C492-HARSHA492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುಮ್ಮಸ್ಸು** — *hummassu* — "zest; eagerness".

> ಹೊಸ ಕೆಲಸ ಶುರು ಮಾಡಲು ಅವನಿಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಹುಮ್ಮಸ್ಸು ಇದೆ.
>
> *hosa kelasa śuru māḍalu avanige tumbā hummassu ide.*
>
> He is full of zest to start the new job.

Close to ಉತ್ಸಾಹ, but warmer and more everyday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುಮ್ಮಸ್ಸು** (*hummassu*) is an everyday Kannada word for eager energy; its origin is not settled.
