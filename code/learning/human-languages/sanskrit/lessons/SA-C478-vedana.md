## You'll want to know: वेदना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C478-VEDANA478-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वेदना** — *vedanā* — "agony; acute pain".

> रात्रौ मम दन्ते वेदना अभवत्।
>
> *rātrau mama dante vedanā abhavat.*
>
> In the night I had a terrible toothache.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेदना** (*vedanā*) comes from the root √vid, "to know, to feel": what is felt; MW gives "pain, torture, agony" (MW).
