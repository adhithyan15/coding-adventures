## You'll want to know: бал
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C318-TRADITSIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**бал** (*bal*) — "a ball (a dance)".

> Студенты готовились к новогоднему балу.
>
> *Studénty gotóvilis k novogódnemu bálu.*
>
> The students were getting ready for the New Year ball.

*На балу* — at the ball.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бал* is usually traced through German *Ball* or French *bal* to Late Latin *ballāre* "to dance"; EtymDB lists no parent.
