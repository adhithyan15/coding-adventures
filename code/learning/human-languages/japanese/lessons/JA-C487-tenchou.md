## You'll want to know: てんちょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C487-SHOP487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**てんちょう** — *tenchō* — "a shop manager".

> **てんちょうは まいあさ みせを あけます。** — *tenchō wa maiasa mise o akemasu* — "The manager opens the shop every morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てんちょう** (*tenchō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ten*, "store" (Middle Chinese *temH*) and *chō*, "leader" (*trjangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
