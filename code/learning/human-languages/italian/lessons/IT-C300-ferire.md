## You'll want to know: ferire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C300-FERITI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ferire** — "to injure, to wound".

> Nessuno si è ferito, per fortuna.
>
> *Nobody was hurt, luckily.*

*Ferirsi* — to hurt oneself. Present like *capire*: *ferisce*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferire* is inherited from Latin *ferīre* "to strike" (EtymDB).
