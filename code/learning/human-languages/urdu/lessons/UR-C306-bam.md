## You'll want to know: بم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C306-DHAMAKA306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بم** — *bam* — "a bomb".

> پولیس کو ایک بم ملا۔
>
> *polīs ko ek bam milā.*
>
> The police found a bomb.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بم** (*bam*) is borrowed from English *bomb*. Urdu says it with a short *a*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
