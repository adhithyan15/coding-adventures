## You'll want to know: il violoncello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C516-STRUMENTI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il violoncello** — "cello".

> Suono il violoncello da dieci anni.
>
> *I have played the cello for ten years.*

Often shortened to *il cello*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*violoncello* is built on *violone* "a large viol" with *-cello*, a little ending (EtymDB): a smaller version of the big viol.
