## You'll want to know: a velhice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C311-IDADES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a velhice** — "old age".

> A minha avó passou a velhice com a família.
>
> *My grandmother spent her old age with the family.*

Built on *velho*. A gentler word is *a terceira idade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*velhice* is built on *velho* "old", which is inherited from Latin *vetulus* "a little old", from *vetus* "old" (Lewis & Short).
