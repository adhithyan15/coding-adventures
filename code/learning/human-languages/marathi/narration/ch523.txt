Marathi, chapter 523: Carpets and Quilts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गालिचा (gālicā) — a carpet; a rug.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a floor tile, then the Marathi for to hang up.

You'll want to know: गालिचा.
गालिचा — gālicā — "a carpet; a rug".
दिवाणखान्यात लाल गालिचा आहे. divāṇkhānyāt lāl gālicā āhe. There is a red carpet in the living room.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
गालिचा (gālicā) is a loan from Persian qālīča, "a small carpet".

Lesson 3 of 5.
मच्छरदाणी (macchardāṇī) — a mosquito net.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a carpet, then the Marathi for a quilt.

You'll want to know: मच्छरदाणी.
मच्छरदाणी — macchardāṇī — "a mosquito net".
डास खूप होते, म्हणून आम्ही मच्छरदाणी लावली. ḍās khūp hote, mhaṇūn āmhī macchardāṇī lāvlī. There were lots of mosquitoes, so we put up a mosquito net.
Feminine.
मच्छरदाणी (macchardāṇī) लावणे, to put up a net.

The word, taken apart.
मच्छरदाणी (macchardāṇī) is a compound of मच्छर, "mosquito", and -दाणी, "a holder"; the origin of मच्छर is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
गुंडाळणे (guṇḍāḷṇe) — to roll up; to wrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a mosquito net, then the Marathi for a mat.

You'll want to know: गुंडाळणे.
गुंडाळणे — guṇḍāḷṇe — "to roll up; to wrap".
सकाळी त्याने गालिचा गुंडाळला आणि बाहेर नेला. sakāḷī tyāne gālicā guṇḍāḷlā āṇi bāher nelā. In the morning he rolled up the carpet and took it outside.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चटई गुंडाळली, tyāne caṭaī guṇḍāḷlī.

The word, taken apart.
गुंडाळणे (guṇḍāḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for rolling or wrapping; its origin is not settled.
