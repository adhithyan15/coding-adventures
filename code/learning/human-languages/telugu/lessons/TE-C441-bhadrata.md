## You'll want to know: భద్రత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C441-PASPORTU441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**భద్రత** — *bhadrata* — "security; safety".

> విమానాశ్రయంలో భద్రత కోసం సామాను తనిఖీ చేశారు.
>
> *vimānāśrayaṁlō bhadrata kōsaṁ sāmānu tanikhī cēśāru.*
>
> At the airport they checked the luggage for security.

The ending -త makes an abstract noun of a Sanskrit adjective: భద్ర, safe, gives భద్రత, safety.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భద్రత** (*bhadrata*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bhadratā* (Monier-Williams), the abstract noun of *bhadra*, "fortunate, good" (CDIAL 9377). In Telugu it came to mean safety and security.
