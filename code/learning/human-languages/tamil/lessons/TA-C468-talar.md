## You'll want to know: தளர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C468-CORVU468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தளர்** — *taḷar* — "to weaken; to slacken".

> வயதான பிறகு தாத்தாவின் உடல் தளர்ந்தது.
>
> *vayatāṉa piṟaku tāttāviṉ uṭal taḷarntatu.*
>
> As he grew old, grandfather’s body weakened.

Past (I …): தளர்ந்தேன், *taḷarntēṉ*.

தளர்வான "loose" comes from the same verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தளர்** (*taḷar*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3127), of tired limbs, slack ropes and old age.

## What you've built
