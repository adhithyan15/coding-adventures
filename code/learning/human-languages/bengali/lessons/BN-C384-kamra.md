## You'll want to know: কামরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C384-REL384-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কামরা** — *kāmrā* — "a compartment (of a train); a chamber".

> ট্রেনের এই কামরাটা খালি।
>
> *ṭrener ei kāmrāṭā khāli.*
>
> This compartment of the train is empty.

With the classifier: কামরাটা, the compartment; মহিলাদের কামরা, the ladies' compartment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কামরা** (*kāmrā*) is usually traced to Portuguese *câmara*, "a room, a chamber", one of the words the Portuguese left along the Bengal coast; in Bengali it settled on the rooms of a train.
