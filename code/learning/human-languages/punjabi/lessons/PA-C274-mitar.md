## You'll want to know: ਮੀਟਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C274-SIZE274-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੀਟਰ** — *mīṭar* — "a metre".

> ਸੜਕ ਦਸ ਮੀਟਰ ਚੌੜੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *saṛak das mīṭar chauṛī hai.*
>
> The road is ten metres wide.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੀਟਰ** (*mīṭar*) is borrowed from English *metre*.
