## You'll want to know: تشريع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C409-QANUN409-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تشريع** — *tashrīʿ* — "legislation".

> تحتاج البلاد إلى تشريع جديد للعمل.
>
> *taḥtāju al-bilād ilā tashrīʿ jadīd li-l-ʿamal.*
>
> The country needs new labour legislation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تشريع** (*tashrīʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-ʿ*, "to lay down a way" (Lane). *Tashrīʿ* is the Form II verbal noun, laying down a way for others to follow: legislation.
