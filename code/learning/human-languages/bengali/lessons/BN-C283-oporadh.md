## You'll want to know: অপরাধ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C283-OPORADH283-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অপরাধ** — *ôpôrādh* — "a crime; an offence".

> যদি পুলিশ তাড়াতাড়ি না আসে, তাহলে চোরেরা আবার অপরাধ করবে।
>
> *jôdi pulish tāṛātāṛi nā āse, tāhôle chorerā ābār ôpôrādh kôrbe.*
>
> If the police don't come quickly, the thieves will commit crimes again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অপরাধ** (*ôpôrādh*) is Sanskrit *aparādha*, 'an offence, a transgression' (Monier-Williams), with *apa-*, 'away': a going astray. অপরাধ করা, to commit a crime.
