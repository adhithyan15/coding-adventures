# ટાળવું (ṭāḷvũ) — to avoid, to put off

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hurry, then the Gujarati for to postpone.

## You'll want to know: ટાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C181-DELAY181-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાળવું** — *ṭāḷvũ* — "to avoid, to put off".

> **તે ફરી કામ ટાળશે.** — *te pharī kām ṭāḷśe* — "He will dodge the work again."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાળવું** (*ṭāḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *ṭālayati*, the causative of *ṭalati*, "moves aside", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to avoid" (CDIAL 5450).
