## You'll want to know: شبہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C279-GAVAHI279-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شبہ** — *shubah* — "a suspicion".

> مجھے پڑوسی پر شبہ ہے۔
>
> *mujhe paṛosī par shubah hai.*
>
> I suspect the neighbour.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شبہ** (*shubah*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shubha*, "a doubt, an uncertainty". The person suspected takes **پر**: **پڑوسی پر شبہ**.
