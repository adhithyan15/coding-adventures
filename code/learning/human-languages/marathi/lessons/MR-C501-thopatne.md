## You'll want to know: थोपटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C501-MITHI501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**थोपटणे** — *thopaṭṇe* — "to pat".

> आई बाळाला थोपटून झोपवते.
>
> *āī bāḷālā thopṭūn jhopavte.*
>
> Mother pats the baby to sleep.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाळाला थोपटलं, *tyāne bāḷālā thopaṭlaṁ*.

पाठ थोपटणे, to pat someone on the back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थोपटणे** (*thopaṭṇe*) is usually traced to the expressive *\*thapp*, "slap, pat", under which CDIAL lists Marathi *thābṇẽ*, "to pat" (CDIAL 6091).
