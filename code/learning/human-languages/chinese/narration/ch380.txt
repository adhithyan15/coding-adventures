Mandarin Chinese, chapter 380: Clean Air.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
huánjìng — environment; surroundings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a card-entry door, then the Mandarin for law and order.

You'll want to know: huánjìng.
huánjìng — "environment; surroundings".
wǒ juéde zhèr de huánjìng bǐ yǐqián hǎo — I think the environment here is better than before.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huánjìng is built from huán 'a ring' and jìng 'a border', both inherited from Middle Chinese, hwaen and kjaengX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a jade ring' and the second 'a boundary': what lies around you up to the edge. The modern sense is not given there.

Lesson 2 of 5.
kōngqì — air.

kōngqì — "air".
yīnwèi xiàyǔ le suǒyǐ kōngqì hěn hǎo — It has rained, so the air is fresh.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kōngqì is built from kōng 'empty' and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khuwng and khj+jH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'hollow' and the second 'breath'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
wūrǎn — to pollute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the environment, then the Mandarin for the air we breathe.

You'll want to know: wūrǎn.
wūrǎn — "to pollute".
kāichē huì wūrǎn kōngqì — Driving pollutes the air.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
wūrǎn is built from wū 'dirty water' and rǎn 'to dye', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'u and nyemX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, in another spelling, 'water that stands and does not flow' and the second 'to colour'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
dìqiú — the earth; our planet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the air we breathe, then the Mandarin for to pollute.

You'll want to know: dìqiú.
dìqiú — "the earth; our planet".
dìqiú shì wǒmen dàjiā de jiā — The earth is home to all of us.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dìqiú is built from dì 'the ground' and qiú 'a ball', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dijH and gjuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the land' and, under another spelling, the second 'a ball of fur that is struck'. The earth as a ball is a later idea.

Lesson 5 of 5.
huánbǎo — eco-friendly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to pollute, then the Mandarin for our planet.

You'll want to know: huánbǎo.
huánbǎo — "eco-friendly".
zuò dìtiě bǐ kāichē huánbǎo — Taking the underground is greener than driving.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huánbǎo is built from huán, the first syllable of huánjìng 'the environment', and bǎo 'to keep', both inherited from Middle Chinese, hwaen and pawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to guard': a short form of 'protecting the environment'.
