Portuguese, chapter 481: Olhos e boca.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a sobrancelha — eyebrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to embarrass, then the Portuguese for ashamed.

You'll want to know: a sobrancelha.
a sobrancelha — "eyebrow".
Quando fica surpreendido, levanta as sobrancelhas. When he is surprised, he raises his eyebrows.
Franzir as sobrancelhas — to frown.

The word, taken apart.
sobrancelha is inherited, through Old Portuguese sobrencella, from Latin supercilia "eyebrows" (EtymDB), built on super "above" and cilium "eyelid".

You'll want to know: a pálpebra.
a pálpebra — "eyelid".
Com sono, as pálpebras ficam pesadas. When you are sleepy, your eyelids get heavy.
A learned word; in everyday speech people talk about os olhos.

The word, taken apart.
pálpebra is a learned borrowing from Latin palpebra "eyelid" (EtymDB), related to palpāre "to stroke gently".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for eyelash, then the Portuguese for eyelid.

You'll want to know: a narina.
a narina — "nostril".
O ar entra pelas narinas. Air goes in through the nostrils.
Usually plural: as narinas. The whole nose is o nariz.

The word, taken apart.
narina is inherited from Vulgar Latin narīna (EtymDB), built on Latin nāris "a nostril" (Lewis & Short).
