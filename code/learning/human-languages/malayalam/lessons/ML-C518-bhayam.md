## You'll want to know: ഭയം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C518-BHAYAM518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭയം** — *bhayaṁ* — "dread; fear".

> കുട്ടിക്ക് ഇരുട്ടിനെ ഭയമായിരുന്നു.
>
> *kuṭṭikkŭ iruṭṭine bhayamāyirunnu.*
>
> The child was afraid of the dark.

Written and formal; in speech പേടി is more usual. The one afraid takes -ക്ക്, the thing feared -എ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭയം** (*bhayaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bhaya*, "fear, peril", from the root *bhī*, "to fear" (CDIAL 9390).
