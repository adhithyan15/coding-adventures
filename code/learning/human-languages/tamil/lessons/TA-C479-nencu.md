## You'll want to know: நெஞ்சு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C479-TACAI479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**நெஞ்சு** — *neñcu* — "the bosom; the chest; the heart".

> படிக்கட்டு ஏறியதும் எனக்கு நெஞ்சு வலித்தது.
>
> *paṭikkaṭṭu ēṟiyatum eṉakku neñcu valittatu.*
>
> After climbing the stairs I had a pain in my chest.

Also the heart as the seat of feeling: நெஞ்சு நிறைந்த நன்றி, heartfelt thanks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நெஞ்சு** (*neñcu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3736), for the chest and the heart as the seat of courage and feeling.
