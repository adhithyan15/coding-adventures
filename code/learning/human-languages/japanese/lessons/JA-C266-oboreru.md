## You'll want to know: おぼれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C266-OBORE266-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おぼれる** — *oboreru* — "to drown; to be drowning".

> **こどもが うみで おぼれました。** — *kodomo ga umi de oboremashita* — "A child got into trouble in the sea."

Polite **おぼれます**, *oboremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが おぼれました。**, *otoko ga oboremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おぼれる** (*oboreru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
