Sanskrit, chapter 216: Able, Expert, Ignorant, Wise, Intelligent.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कुशलः (kuśalaḥ) — able, well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for blunt, then the Sanskrit for valuable.

You'll want to know: कुशलः.
कुशलः — kuśalaḥ — "able, well".

The word, taken apart.
kuśala is the word behind kuśalam, "well-being", from your first greetings, and its origin is not settled (CDIAL 3365 judges the one modern derivation it cites "very doubtful"). A popular explanation takes it from kuśa, the sacred grass, with a root of cutting, "skilled at cutting the grass", but no dictionary we checked (MW, Apte, CDIAL) supports it. MW gives both "well, healthy" and "competent, skilful".

Lesson 3 of 5.
अज्ञः (ajñaḥ) — ignorant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for able, then the Sanskrit for expert.

You'll want to know: अज्ञः.
अज्ञः — ajñaḥ — "ignorant".

The word, taken apart.
ajña is a-, "not", with jña, "knowing", from the root √jñā, "to know" (MW; CDIAL 160). √jñā is the root of jānāti, "knows", which you have already met.

Lesson 4 of 5.
विज्ञः (vijñaḥ) — wise, knowing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for expert, then the Sanskrit for ignorant.

You'll want to know: विज्ञः.
विज्ञः — vijñaḥ — "wise, knowing".

The word, taken apart.
vijña is built on vi-, "apart", and jña, "knowing", from the root √jñā, "to know" (MW): one who knows things apart, wise. It is the opposite of ajña, and vijñāna, "science", is built the same way.

Lesson 5 of 5.
बुद्धिमान् (buddhimān) — intelligent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for ignorant, then the Sanskrit for wise.

You'll want to know: बुद्धिमान्.
बुद्धिमान् — buddhimān — "intelligent".

The word, taken apart.
buddhimān is the nominative of buddhimat, buddhi, "intelligence", with the suffix -mat, "having" (MW). Buddhi is built on the root √budh, "to wake, notice". Hindi and Bengali took the word over as buddhimān.
