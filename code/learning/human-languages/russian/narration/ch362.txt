Russian, chapter 362: Getting a promotion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
повышение (povyshéniye) — a promotion; a rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for income, then the Russian for a deal.

You'll want to know: повышение.
повышение (povyshéniye) — "a promotion; a rise".
Он получил повышение на работе. On poluchíl povyshéniye na rabóte. He got a promotion at work.
Neuter. Повышение зарплаты — a pay rise. From повышать.

The word, taken apart.
повышение (povyshéniye) is built on повысить "to raise", from выше "higher", the comparative of высокий, inherited from Proto-Slavic vysokŭ "high, tall" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
премия (prémiya) — a bonus; a prize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a deal, then the Russian for a promotion.

You'll want to know: премия.
премия (prémiya) — "a bonus; a prize".
В конце года все получили премию. V kontsé góda vse poluchíli prémiyu. At the end of the year everyone got a bonus.
Feminine. A bonus at work, or a prize: литературная премия (prémiya).

The word, taken apart.
премия (prémiya) is usually traced, through German Prämie, to Latin praemium "a reward, a prize" (Lewis & Short), built from prae "before" and emere "to take, to buy".

Lesson 3 of 5.
навык (návyk) — a skill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a promotion, then the Russian for a bonus.

You'll want to know: навык.
навык (návyk) — "a skill".
Для этой работы нужны компьютерные навыки. Dlya étoy rabóty nuzhný kompyúternyye návyki. This job needs computer skills.
Masculine; usually plural, навыки.

The word, taken apart.
навык (návyk) is built from на- and the root of привыкать "to get used to" and отвыкать "to lose a habit": a skill is something you have grown used to doing.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bonus, then the Russian for a skill.

You'll want to know: специалист.
специалист (spetsialíst) — "a specialist, an expert".
Он хороший специалист по компьютерам. On khoróshiy spetsialíst po kompyúteram. He is a good computer specialist.
Masculine, also of a woman. Специалист по + dative — an expert in.

The word, taken apart.
специалист (spetsialíst) is usually traced to German Spezialist, built on Latin speciālis "particular", from speciēs "a look, a kind" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
увольнять (uvolnyát) — to dismiss, to fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a skill, then the Russian for a specialist.

You'll want to know: увольнять.
увольнять (uvolnyát) — "to dismiss, to fire".
Компания увольняет сто человек. Kompániya uvolnyáyet sto chelovék. The company is laying off a hundred people.
Imperfective; perfective уволить. Увольняться — to resign, to quit.

The word, taken apart.
увольнять (uvolnyát) is formed from уволить with the suffix -ять (EtymDB). Уволить is built on воля "will, freedom", inherited from Proto-Slavic voľa (EtymDB): to dismiss someone was first to set them free from service.
