## You'll want to know: enterrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C314-FUNERAILLES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**enterrer** — "to bury".

> Ils ont enterré leur grand-père près de l'église.
>
> *They buried their grandfather near the church.*

Figurative: *enterrer un projet* — to drop a plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enterrer* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *interrāre* "to put in the earth" (EtymDB), built on Latin *terra* "earth".
