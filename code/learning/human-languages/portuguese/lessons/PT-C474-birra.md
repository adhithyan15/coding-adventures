## You'll want to know: a birra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C474-RABUGENTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a birra** — "tantrum".

> O menino fez uma birra no supermercado.
>
> *The little boy threw a tantrum in the supermarket.*

*Fazer uma birra* — to throw a tantrum. In Brazil *birra* can also mean stubbornness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*birra* is traced in EtymDB to Spanish *birria*, which goes back to Latin *verrēs* "a boar"; the link is not certain.
