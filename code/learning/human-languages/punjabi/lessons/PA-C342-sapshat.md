## You'll want to know: ਸਪਸ਼ਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C342-MEAN342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਪਸ਼ਟ** — *sapshaṭ* — "clear; plain to understand".

> ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਜਵਾਬ ਸਪਸ਼ਟ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *tuhāḍā javāb sapshaṭ nahīṁ hai.*
>
> Your answer is not clear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਪਸ਼ਟ** (*sapshaṭ*) is taken over from Sanskrit *spaṣṭa*, "clear", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
