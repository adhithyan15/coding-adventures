## You'll want to know: とうちゃく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C199-DEPART199-02]; assesses=[] -->

**とうちゃく** — *tōchaku* — "arrival".

> **とうちゃくは ゆうがたです。** — *tōchaku wa yūgata desu* — "Arrival is in the evening."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とうちゃく** (*tōchaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tō*, "to reach" (Middle Chinese *tawH*) and *chaku*, "to arrive" (*drjak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
