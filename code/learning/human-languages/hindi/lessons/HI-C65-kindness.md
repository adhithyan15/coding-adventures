## You'll want to know: मेहरबानी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C65-GRACE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मेहरबानी** (*mehrabānī*) — "kindness".

Persian *mihrbān*, 'kind', with the quality-ending on the back of it. Under *mihrbān* is *mihr*, an old Iranian word for love and for the sun, and the god Mithra before either.

Two words in your Hindi now begin *meh-* and are not related at all: मेहमान, the guest, is Persian *mihmān*, a different word entirely. Same opening syllable in our letters, separate families.
