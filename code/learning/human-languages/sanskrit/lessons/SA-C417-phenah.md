## You'll want to know: फेनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C417-PULINA417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**फेनः** — *phenaḥ* — "foam".

> तरंगेषु श्वेतः फेनः अस्ति।
>
> *taraṅgeṣu śvetaḥ phenaḥ asti.*
>
> There is white foam on the waves.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फेनः** (*phenaḥ*) is the word MW gives for foam, froth; MW connects it, probably, with a root √phaṇ, so its origin is uncertain; MW compares English *foam* and Slavonic *pěna* (MW).
