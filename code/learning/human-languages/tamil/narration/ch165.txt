Tamil, chapter 165: To get to know, To celebrate, To decorate, To dance, To worship.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பழகு (paḻaku) — to get to know, to mix with (people).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to pass, then the Tamil for to pay attention.

You'll want to know: பழகு.
பழகு — paḻaku — "to get to know, to mix with (people)".

The word, taken apart.
பழகு (paḻaku) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4000 lists Malayalam paḻakuka, "to be accustomed, acquainted".
