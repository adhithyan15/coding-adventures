# ದಾಂಪತ್ಯ (dāmpatya) — married life

## You'll want to know: ದಾಂಪತ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಾಂಪತ್ಯ** — *dāmpatya* — "married life".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಅಜ್ಜಿಯ ದಾಂಪತ್ಯಕ್ಕೆ ಐವತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *ajja ajjiya dāmpatyakke aivattu varṣa āyitu.*
>
> My grandparents have been married for fifty years.

From ದಂಪತಿ: the life of the pair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಾಂಪತ್ಯ** (*dāmpatya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dāmpatya*, "the state of husband and wife, married life", built from *dampati* (Monier-Williams).
