Arabic, chapter 530: Babies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حامل (ḥāmil) — pregnant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a brother-in-law, then the Arabic for a marriage break-up.

You'll want to know: حامل.
حامل — ḥāmil — "pregnant".
زوجة أخي حامل بطفلها الأول. zawjat akhī ḥāmil bi-ṭiflihā al-awwal. My brother's wife is pregnant with her first child.
It has no feminine ending, since only a woman is حامل (ḥāmil).

The word, taken apart.
حامل (ḥāmil) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-l, "to carry" (Lane). Ḥamala, to carry, which you know, is from the same root.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مهد (mahd) — a cradle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a marriage break-up, then the Arabic for pregnant.

You'll want to know: مهد.
مهد — mahd — "a cradle".
نام الطفل في المهد. nāma aṭ-ṭifl fī al-mahd. The baby slept in the cradle.
من المهد is from the cradle, from early childhood.

The word, taken apart.
مهد (mahd) is built on the native Arabic root m-h-d, "to smooth, to spread out" (Lane). A mahd is a bed made smooth for a child.

You'll want to know: أرضع.
أرضع — arḍaʿa — "to breastfeed".
أرضعت الأم طفلها في الصباح. arḍaʿat al-umm ṭiflahā fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ. The mother fed her baby in the morning.

The word, taken apart.
أرضع (arḍaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root r-ḍ-ʿ, "to suck the breast" (Lane). Form IV arḍaʿa is to give the breast.

Lesson 4 of 5.
فطم (faṭama) — to wean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a cradle, then the Arabic for to breastfeed.

You'll want to know: فطم.
فطم — faṭama — "to wean".
فطمت أختي ابنها بعد سنتين. faṭamat ukhtī ibnahā baʿda sanatayn. My sister weaned her son after two years.
Present: يفطم, yafṭim.

The word, taken apart.
فطم (faṭama) is built on the native Arabic root f-ṭ-m, "to cut off" (Lane). Lane gives faṭama, aor. yafṭimu, as to cut off: weaning cuts the child off the breast.
