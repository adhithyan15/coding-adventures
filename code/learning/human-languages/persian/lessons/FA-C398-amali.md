## You'll want to know: عملی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C398-AMALI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عملی** (*'amali*) — "practical, workable".

> پیشنهاد تو جالب است، اما عملی نیست.
>
> *pishnahâd-e to jâleb ast, ammâ 'amali nist.*
>
> Your suggestion is interesting, but it is not practical.

*'Amal* "action, deed" + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عملی** is built on *'amal* "deed, action", usually traced to Arabic *'amal*, with the adjective suffix *-i*: something that can be done.
