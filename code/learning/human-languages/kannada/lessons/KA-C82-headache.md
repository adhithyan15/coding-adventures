# ತಲೆನೋವು (talenōvu) — a headache

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-02, KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cold, then the Kannada for a cough.

## You'll want to know: ತಲೆನೋವು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲೆನೋವು** (*talenōvu*) — "a headache". It is two words you already know, joined:
**ತಲೆ** (*tale*), "head", and **ನೋವು** (*nōvu*), "pain".

**ನನಗೆ ತಲೆನೋವು** (*nanage talenōvu*) — "I have a headache".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two known words make a new one: head + pain.
