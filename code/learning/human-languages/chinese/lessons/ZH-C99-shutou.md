## You'll want to know: shūtóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C99-WASH99-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shūtóu* — "to comb one's hair".

> *wǒ zǎoshang shūtóu* — I comb my hair in the morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shūtóu* is built from shū 'to comb' and tóu 'head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjo* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a comb, for putting the hair in order.
