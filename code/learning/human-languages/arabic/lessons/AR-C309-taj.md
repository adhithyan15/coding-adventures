## You'll want to know: تاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C309-QASR309-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تاج** — *tāj* — "a crown".

> لبست الملكة تاجا من الذهب.
>
> *labisat al-malika tājan min adh-dhahab.*
>
> The queen wore a crown of gold.

Plural: تيجان, *tījān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تاج** (*tāj*) was borrowed from Middle Iranian (Parthian) *tāg*, "a crown" (EtymDB), an old borrowing; Aramaic *tāgā* is its cousin. Arabic made a verb from it: *tawwaja*, to crown.
