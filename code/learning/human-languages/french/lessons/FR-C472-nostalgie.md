## You'll want to know: la nostalgie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C472-NOSTALGIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la nostalgie** — "nostalgia, longing for the past".

> Je pense à mon enfance avec nostalgie.
>
> *I think of my childhood with nostalgia.*

*Avoir la nostalgie de* — to miss, to long for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la nostalgie* comes from New Latin *nostalgia* (EtymDB), coined from Greek *nóstos* "return home" and *álgos* "pain": it first named homesickness.
