## You'll want to know: nāvigium, nāvigiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C435-NAVIGIUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**nāvigium, nāvigiī** — "a vessel, a craft (any ship or boat)".

> In portū multa nāvigia sunt, magna et parva.
>
> *In the harbour there are many vessels, large and small.*

Second declension, neuter: *nāvigium, nāvigiī n.* A general word for anything that sails.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nāvigium* is built on *nāvigō* 'to sail' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which comes from *nāvis* 'ship', inherited from PIE *néh₂us* 'boat'.
