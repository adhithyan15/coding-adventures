## You'll want to know: दोष देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C350-SVIKAR350-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दोष देणे** — *doṣ deṇe* — "to blame".

> तो आपल्या अपयशासाठी नशिबाला दोष देतो.
>
> *to āpalyā apayśāsāṭhī naśibālā doṣ deto.*
>
> He blames fate for his failure.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दोष दिला, *tyāne doṣ dilā*.

The one blamed takes -ला; the fault takes -साठी or -बद्दल.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दोष देणे** (*doṣ deṇe*) joins the tatsama दोष, Sanskrit *doṣa*, "fault, defect" (Monier-Williams), to देणे, "to give".

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
