## You'll want to know: la pastelería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C680-TIENDAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la pastelería** — "the cake shop, the pastry shop".

> Compramos un pastel en la pastelería.
>
> *We bought a cake at the cake shop.*

The baker of cakes is *el pastelero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pastelería* is built on *pastel* "cake" with *-ería* (EtymDB); *pastel* is usually traced to Late Latin *pasta* "dough".
