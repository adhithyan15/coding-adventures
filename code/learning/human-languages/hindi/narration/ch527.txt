Hindi, chapter 527: Colours on the Wall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कला (kalā) — art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an ace (in cards), then the Hindi for chess.

You'll want to know: कला.
कला — kalā — "art".
हमारे स्कूल में हर बुधवार कला की कक्षा होती है। Hamāre skūl meṁ har budhvār kalā kī kakṣā hotī hai. Our school has an art class every Wednesday.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कला (kalā) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit kalā, "a small part; any practical art" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
रंगना (raṅgnā) — to colour; to paint; to dye.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a paintbrush, then the Hindi for a line (drawn).

You'll want to know: रंगना.
रंगना — raṅgnā — "to colour; to paint; to dye".
बच्चों ने दीवार हरे रंग से रंगी। Baccoṁ ne dīvār hare raṅg se raṅgī. The children painted the wall green.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कुर्सी रंगी, usne kursī raṅgī.

The word, taken apart.
रंगना (raṅgnā) is the verb of raṅg, "colour", probably from the same source as Hindi rãgānā, "to be coloured", which CDIAL files under raṅgayati, "dyes", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 10570).
