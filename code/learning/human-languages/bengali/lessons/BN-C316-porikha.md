## You'll want to know: পরিখা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C316-GHERA316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিখা** — *pôrikhā* — "a moat; a trench".

> প্রাচীরের বাইরে জলে ভরা একটা পরিখা ছিল।
>
> *prāchīrer bāire jôle bhôrā ekṭā pôrikhā chhilô.*
>
> Outside the wall there was a moat full of water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিখা** (*pôrikhā*) is Sanskrit *parikhā*, 'a moat, a ditch round a fort' (Monier-Williams): *pari-*, 'around', and *√khan*, 'to dig'.
