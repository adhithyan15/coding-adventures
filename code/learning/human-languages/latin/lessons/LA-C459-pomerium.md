## You'll want to know: pōmērium, pōmēriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C459-PORTICUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pōmērium, pōmēriī** — "the sacred boundary (of a city)".

> Intrā pōmērium mīlitēs arma nōn gerunt.
>
> *Inside the sacred boundary soldiers do not carry weapons.*

Second declension, neuter: *pōmērium, pōmēriī n.*, also *pōmoerium*. The holy line round a Roman town, marked with stones.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pōmērium* is usually explained, as by ancient writers, as *post* 'behind' and *moerus*, an old form of *mūrus* 'wall' (Lewis & Short): the strip of land along the wall. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
