Lesson 1 of 5.
ಭೂಗೋಳ (bhūgōḷa) — geography.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for information, then the Kannada for a mark.

You'll want to know: ಭೂಗೋಳ.
ಭೂಗೋಳ — bhūgōḷa — "geography".

The word, taken apart.
ಭೂಗೋಳ (bhūgōḷa) is Sanskrit bhū, "world, ground" (CDIAL 9546), and gōla, "ball" (CDIAL 4321), with the l written ḷ: "the earth's ball".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪದ (pada) — a word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mark, then the Kannada for geography.

You'll want to know: ಪದ.
ಪದ — pada — "a word".

The word, taken apart.
ಪದ (pada) is Sanskrit pada, "footstep, track, place", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 7747). In Sanskrit grammar the same word names a word, a single step of speech.
