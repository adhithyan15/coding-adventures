## You'll want to know: la rubrique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C282-TITRES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la rubrique** — "the section, the regular feature (in a paper)".

> Il écrit dans la rubrique sport.
>
> *He writes in the sports section.*

*La rubrique nécrologique* — the death notices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rubrique* is a learned borrowing from Latin *rubrīca* "red earth, a heading written in red", from *ruber* "red" (Lewis & Short).
