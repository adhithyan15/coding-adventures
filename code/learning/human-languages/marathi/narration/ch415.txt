Lesson 1 of 5.
सुविधा (suvidhā) — an amenity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to ask after, then the Marathi for to drop by.

You'll want to know: सुविधा.
सुविधा — suvidhā — "an amenity".
या विश्रामगृहात सगळ्या सुविधा आहेत. yā viśrāmgṛhāt sagaḷyā suvidhā āhet. This guest house has all the amenities.
Feminine.
A feminine noun; the plural is the same in the direct case.

The word, taken apart.
सुविधा (suvidhā) is a modern word built from Sanskrit su-, "good", and vidhā, "arrangement, manner" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बिछाना (bichānā) — bedding; a bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to drop by, then the Marathi for an amenity.

You'll want to know: बिछाना.
बिछाना — bichānā — "bedding; a bed".
आईने पाहुण्यांसाठी बिछाना तयार केला. āīne pāhuṇyāṁsāṭhī bichānā tayār kelā. Mother got the bedding ready for the guests.
Masculine.
The mattress, sheet and pillow together, laid out for sleep.

The word, taken apart.
बिछाना (bichānā) is a loan, usually traced to Hindustani: CDIAL lists Hindi bichānā, "to spread", under vicchādayati, "spread out" (CDIAL 11692).

घोंगडी (ghoṅgḍī) — a woollen blanket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an amenity, then the Marathi for bedding.

You'll want to know: घोंगडी.
घोंगडी — ghoṅgḍī — "a woollen blanket".
थंडीत आजोबा घोंगडी अंगावर घेतात. thaṇḍīt ājobā ghoṅgḍī aṅgāvar ghetāt. In the cold Grandfather wraps himself in a woollen blanket.
Feminine.
A rough blanket of sheep’s wool, woven by shepherds.

The word, taken apart.
घोंगडी (ghoṅgḍī) is a Marathi word for the coarse wool blanket shepherds weave; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
अंथरणे (antharṇe) — to spread out bedding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for bedding, then the Marathi for a woollen blanket.

You'll want to know: अंथरणे.
अंथरणे — antharṇe — "to spread out bedding".
आईने जमिनीवर चादर अंथरली. āīne jaminīvar cādar antharlī. Mother spread a sheet out on the floor.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अंथरलं, tyāne antharlaṁ.
The thing spread is the object: चादर अंथरली, गादी अंथरली.

The word, taken apart.
अंथरणे (antharṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ā̃tharṇẽ, "to spread out", under Sanskrit āstarati, "spreads" (CDIAL 1507).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पांघरणे (pāṅgharṇe) — to cover oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a woollen blanket, then the Marathi for to spread out bedding.

You'll want to know: पांघरणे.
पांघरणे — pāṅgharṇe — "to cover oneself".
रात्री मी जाड चादर पांघरली. rātrī mī jāḍ cādar pāṅgharlī. At night I covered myself with a thick sheet.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पांघरलं, tyāne pāṅgharlaṁ.
The cover is the object, and the verb agrees with it: चादर पांघरली.

The word, taken apart.
पांघरणे (pāṅgharṇe) is a Marathi verb for pulling a cover over yourself; its origin is not settled; the noun पांघरूण, a covering, belongs with it.
