## You'll want to know: kōng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C134-THING134-04]; assesses=[] -->

*kōng* — "empty".

> *bīngxiāng shì kōng de* — The fridge is empty.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kōng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *khuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'empty, hollow'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
