Hindi, chapter 477: Arms and Legs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हथेली (hathelī) — the palm of the hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a beard, then the Hindi for a jaw.

You'll want to know: हथेली.
हथेली — hathelī — "the palm of the hand".
बच्चे की हथेली बहुत छोटी और नरम है। bacce kī hathelī bahut choṭī aur naram hai. The baby's palm is very small and soft.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
हथेली (hathelī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi hathelī, "palm", under Sanskrit hastatala, "the flat of the hand" (CDIAL 14029), built on hasta, the word that gave hāth.
