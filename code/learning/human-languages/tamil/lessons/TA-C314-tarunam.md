# தருணம் (taruṇam) — a moment; an occasion

## You'll want to know: தருணம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C314-NINAIVU314-01]; assesses=[] -->

**தருணம்** — *taruṇam* — "a moment; an occasion".

> அது என் வாழ்க்கையின் மிக மகிழ்ச்சியான தருணம்.
>
> *atu eṉ vāḻkkaiyiṉ mika makiḻcciyāṉa taruṇam.*
>
> That was the happiest moment of my life.

Also the right moment: சரியான தருணம்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தருணம்** (*taruṇam*), "a moment, an occasion", is a learned Tamil word whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
