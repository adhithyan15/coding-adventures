## You'll want to know: dǒngshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C508-SIBLINGS508-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dǒngshì* — "sensible; grown-up for one's age (of a child)".

> *jiějie bǐ wǒ dǒngshì* — My big sister is more grown-up than me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǒngshì* is built from *dǒng* 'to understand' and 事 'a matter'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to employ'; the Guangyun has no entry for the first, so its history is not settled.
