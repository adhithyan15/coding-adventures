## You'll want to know: おろす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C187-BANK187-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おろす** — *orosu* — "to withdraw (money); to take down".

> **ぎんこうで おかねを おろします。** — *ginkō de okane o oroshimasu* — "I withdraw money at the bank."

Polite **おろします**, *oroshimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おろす** (*orosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
