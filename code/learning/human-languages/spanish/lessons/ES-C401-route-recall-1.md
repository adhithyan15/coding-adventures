# route recall one — hear the chain, then rebuild it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-AVENIDA, ES-LEX-SEGUIR, ES-LEX-RECTO, ES-LEX-CAMBIAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Give the four-word chain from a broad road to a changed route.
(*La avenida, seguir, recto, cambiar*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-AVENIDA, ES-LEX-SEGUIR, ES-LEX-RECTO, ES-LEX-CAMBIAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You are on **la avenida**. The instruction is **seguir recto**.
Then the route changes: **cambiar de dirección**. Say those three steps again
without the English prompts.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-AVENIDA, ES-LEX-SEGUIR, ES-LEX-RECTO, ES-LEX-CAMBIAR] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *la avenida*]
- [YOU SAY: *seguir recto*]
- [YOU SAY: *cambiar de dirección*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-AVENIDA, ES-LEX-SEGUIR, ES-LEX-RECTO, ES-LEX-CAMBIAR] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say the route in one line. (*Seguir recto por la avenida y después
cambiar de dirección*.)
