## You'll want to know: しょうこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C273-SAIBAN273-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうこ** — *shōko* — "evidence; proof".

> **けいさつは しょうこを さがしています。** — *keisatsu wa shōko o sagashite imasu* — "The police are looking for evidence."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうこ** (*shōko*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "evidence" (Middle Chinese *tsyingH*) and *ko*, "based on" (*kjoH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
