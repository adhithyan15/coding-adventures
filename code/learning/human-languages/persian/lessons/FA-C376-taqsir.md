## You'll want to know: تقصیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C376-TAQSIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تقصیر** (*taqsir*) — "a fault, the blame".

> این تقصیر من نبود.
>
> *in taqsir-e man nabud.*
>
> It wasn't my fault.

*Taqsir-e kasi budan* — to be someone's fault. Very common in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تقصیر** comes from Arabic *taqṣīr* (EtymDB), a verbal noun usually explained as falling short; in Persian it is the fault or the blame.
