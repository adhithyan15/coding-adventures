## You'll want to know: மேடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C540-MEDAI540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**மேடை** — *mēṭai* — "a stage; a platform".

> பாடகி மேடையில் ஏறினாள்.
>
> *pāṭaki mēṭaiyil ēṟiṉāḷ.*
>
> The singer went up on the stage.

Also a speaker's platform at a meeting. Where one stands takes -இல்: மேடையில்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **மேடை** (*mēṭai*) is not settled: no dictionary we can check gives its source.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
