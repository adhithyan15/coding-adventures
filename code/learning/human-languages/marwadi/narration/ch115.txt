You'll want to know: जमीन.
जमीन — jamīn — "land".

The word, taken apart.
जमीन (jamīn) is a loan, usually traced to Persian zamīn, "earth, ground". Marwadi says the z as j.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पूंजी (pū̃jī) — capital, savings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for land, then the Marwadi for property.

You'll want to know: पूंजी.
पूंजी — pū̃jī — "capital, savings".

The word, taken apart.
पूंजी (pū̃jī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from puñja, "heap, quantity". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8251); the match is through Hindi pū̃jī. The sense moved from "heap" to "store of money". CDIAL thinks the further history of the Sanskrit word doubtful.

Lesson 4 of 5.
कमाई (kamāī) — earnings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for property, then the Marwadi for capital.

You'll want to know: कमाई.
कमाई — kamāī — "earnings".

The word, taken apart.
कमाई (kamāī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from karmāpayati, "works, earns", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2897); the match is through Hindi kamānā and Gujarati kamāvũ. कमाई (kamāī) is the noun of the verb "to earn"; CDIAL builds that verb on Sanskrit karman, "work".

Lesson 5 of 5.
नौकरी (naukrī) — a job.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for capital, then the Marwadi for earnings.

You'll want to know: नौकरी.
नौकरी — naukrī — "a job".

The word, taken apart.
नौकरी (naukrī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian naukar, "servant". Persian took naukar from Mongolic nökör, "companion"; Marwadi added the ending -ī, so "the work of a servant".
