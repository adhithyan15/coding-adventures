# Return to going tomorrow

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221] -->

[YOU HEAR: *jāvāṅgā; jāvegā; jāvāṅge*] Say **ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ · ਜਾਵੇਗਾ · ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ**.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KARNA-05, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221] -->

Those three futures needed **ਵ** after the vowel of **ਜਾ**. The verb **ਕਰਨਾ**,
"to do", has a stem that ends in a consonant, **ਕਰ**. Keep it ready.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-LEX-KARNA-05] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I will go tomorrow*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *she will go tomorrow*] Say **ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *to do*] Say **ਕਰਨਾ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-LEX-KARNA-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *we will go*, then *to do*.
