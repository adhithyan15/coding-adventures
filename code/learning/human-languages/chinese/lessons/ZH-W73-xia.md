## Script — a bar, a vertical, a dot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 下

**One piece, three strokes.**

**A long horizontal** across the top.

**A vertical** hanging down from its middle.

**A dot** to the right of the vertical, slanting down to the right.
