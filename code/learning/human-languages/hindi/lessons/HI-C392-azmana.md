## You'll want to know: आज़माना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C392-TAIYARI392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आज़माना** — *āzmānā* — "to try out, to test".

> अगर यह दवा काम नहीं करेगी तो डाक्टर दूसरी दवा आज़माएगा।
>
> *agar yah davā kām nahīṃ karegī to ḍākṭar dūsrī davā āzmāegā.*
>
> If this medicine doesn't work, the doctor will try another one.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नई दवा आज़माई, *usne naī davā āzmāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आज़माना** (*āzmānā*) is usually traced to Persian *āzmā-*, "test". This is the stem of *āzmūdan*, "to test".
