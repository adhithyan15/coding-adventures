# 代 — the person piece, then a slanting hook

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] The word first, then the character inside it.

- [YOU RECALL: say *niándài* — **R1**, one lesson back, before it decays]

> 信 你

[YOU READ: each one aloud, with nothing said first — *xìn*, *nǐ*]

## Script — the person piece, then a slanting hook
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DAI-ERA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 代

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, 亻, two strokes,** as in 你 and 信.

**The right piece, three strokes.** A short bar that rises a little to the right. Then a long stroke from the top, crossing the bar and slanting down to the right, flicking up at the foot. Last, a dot at the top right.

2 + 3 = five.

- [YOU WRITE: 代, all five, with the model in front of you]
- [YOU COVER: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

[PAUSE 3s] How many strokes in 代? (**Five**.) Which stroke flicks up at the foot? (**The long slanting stroke, the fourth**.) What comes last? (**A dot at the top right**.)
