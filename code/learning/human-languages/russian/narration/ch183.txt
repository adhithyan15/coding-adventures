Lesson 1 of 5.
переулок (pereúlok) — a side street.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a day off, then the Russian for on time.

You'll want to know: переулок.
переулок (pereúlok) — "a side street".

The word, taken apart.
переулок (pereúlok) is пере-, "across", with -улок, from улица, "street" (Vasmer): a lane that crosses between streets.

Lesson 3 of 5.
дорога (doróga) — a road; the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a side street, then the Russian for a pavement.

You'll want to know: дорога.
дорога (doróga) — "a road; the way".

The word, taken apart.
дорога (doróga), "road", from Proto-Slavic dorga — inherited. Vasmer links it with дёргать, "to pull, to tear": first "a strip torn through the forest". Serbo-Croatian драга means "a valley".

шоссе (shossé) came through German Chaussée or straight from French chaussée, "a paved road", from Latin calciāta (via), "a road laid with lime" (Vasmer).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: порт.
порт (port) — "a port".

The word, taken apart.
порт, "harbour", is recorded from 1702; it came through English port or German Port from French port, Latin portus (Vasmer).
