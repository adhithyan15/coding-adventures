# മധുവിധു (madhuvidhu) — a honeymoon

## You'll want to know: മധുവിധു
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C389-GRIHAM389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**മധുവിധു** — *madhuvidhu* — "a honeymoon".

> വിവാഹം കഴിഞ്ഞ് അവർ മധുവിധുവിന് ഒരു ദ്വീപിൽ പോയി.
>
> *vivāhaṁ kaḻiññŭ avar madhuvidhuvinŭ oru dvīpil pōyi.*
>
> After the wedding they went to an island for their honeymoon.

Usually explained as a calque on the English word: മധു, honey, + വിധു, the moon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മധുവിധു** (*madhuvidhu*) is a modern compound built from two Sanskrit words, *madhu*, "honey" (CDIAL 9784), and *vidhu*, a name of the moon (Monier-Williams). It is usually explained as a calque on English *honeymoon*.
