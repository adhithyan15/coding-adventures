## You'll want to know: કડાકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C435-STORM435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કડાકો** — *kaḍāko* — "a crash of thunder; a loud crack".

> રાત્રે વીજળીનો મોટો કડાકો થયો.
>
> *rātre vījḷīno moṭo kaḍāko thayo.*
>
> At night there was a great crash of thunder.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કડાકો** (*kaḍāko*) goes back to Sanskrit *kaṭakaṭā*, an imitative word for a grating or crackling sound; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a loud crack" (CDIAL 2632).
