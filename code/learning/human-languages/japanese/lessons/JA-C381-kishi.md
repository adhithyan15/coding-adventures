## You'll want to know: きし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C381-FUNATABI381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きし** — *kishi* — "a shore; a bank".

> **ふねが きしに つきました。** — *fune ga kishi ni tsukimashita* — "The boat reached the shore."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きし** (*kishi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
