Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 195: At the Bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बैंक (baiṅk) — a bank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a profit, then the Marwadi for credit.

You'll want to know: बैंक.
बैंक — baiṅk — "a bank".
बैंक बाजार कनै है। baiṅk bājār kanai hai. The bank is near the market.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बैंक (baiṅk) is borrowed from English bank.

Lesson 2 of 5.
खातो (khāto) — a bank account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for credit, then the Marwadi for a bank.

You'll want to know: खातो.
खातो — khāto — "a bank account".
बैंक मांय म्हारो खातो है। baiṅk mā̃y mhāro khāto hai. I have an account at the bank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
खातो (khāto) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit kṣatra, "might, rule", with Hindi khātā and Sindhi khāto, "account book" (CDIAL 3648). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
बचणो (bachṇo) — to be left over; to escape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a debt, then the Marwadi for remaining.

You'll want to know: बचणो.
बचणो — bachṇo — "to be left over; to escape".
म्हारी तनखा सूं सो रुपिया बचै है। mhārī tankhā sū̃ so rupiyā bachai hai. A hundred rupees are left over from my salary.
Past (it …): वो सिक्को बच्यो, vo sikko bachyo.

The word, taken apart.
बचणो (bachṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vañcati, "moves; escapes from"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari vacaï, "is saved" (CDIAL 11208). बचावणो, "to save", is its partner.
