## You'll want to know: investir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C391-EPARGNE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**investir** — "to invest".

> Il a investi son argent dans un appartement.
>
> *He invested his money in a flat.*

A *-ir* verb like *finir*: *nous investissons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *investīre* "to clothe, to cover" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *vestis* "clothing"; the money sense came later, probably through Italian.
