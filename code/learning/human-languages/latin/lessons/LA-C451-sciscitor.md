## You'll want to know: scīscitor, scīscitārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C451-PERCONTOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**scīscitor, scīscitārī** — "to ask around, to try to find out".

> Mercātor in forō dē viā scīscitātur.
>
> *The merchant asks around in the forum about the road.*

Deponent: *scīscitor, scīscitārī, scīscitātus sum*. A frequentative: asking again and again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scīscitor* is built on *scīscō* 'to seek to know' with the frequentative ending *-itor* (EtymDB), and *scīscō* comes from *sciō* 'to know' (Lewis & Short).
