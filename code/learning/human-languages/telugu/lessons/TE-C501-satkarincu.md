# సత్కరించు (satkariñcu) — to honour formally; to felicitate

## You'll want to know: సత్కరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C501-NAVVU501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సత్కరించు** — *satkariñcu* — "to honour formally; to felicitate".

> బడిలో మంచి మార్కులు వచ్చిన విద్యార్థులను సత్కరించారు.
>
> *baḍilō mañci mārkulu vaccina vidyārthulanu satkariñcāru.*
>
> The school honoured the students who got good marks.

Past (I …): సత్కరించాను, *satkariñcānu*.

Honouring someone in public, often with a shawl and a garland. The noun is సత్కారం.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సత్కరించు** (*satkariñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *satkāra*, "kind treatment, honour, hospitality" (Monier-Williams), from *sat*, "good", and *kāra*, "doing", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
