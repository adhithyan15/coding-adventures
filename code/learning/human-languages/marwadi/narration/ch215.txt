Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 215: Wrapping Up.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
लपेटणो (lapeṭṇo) — to wrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to flip over, then the Marwadi for to turn up.

You'll want to know: लपेटणो.
लपेटणो — lapeṭṇo — "to wrap".
मां रोटी कपड़ा मांय लपेटै है। mā̃ roṭī kapṛā mā̃y lapeṭai hai. Mother wraps the flatbread in a cloth.
Past (I …): म्हैं रोटी लपेटी, mhaĩ roṭī lapeṭī.

The word, taken apart.
लपेटणो (lapeṭṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed lappēṭṭ, "wrap". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi lapeṭnā, "to wrap, fold, wind" (CDIAL 10942).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for paper, then the Marwadi for a small bundle.

You'll want to know: ओछो.
ओछो — ochho — "too small; short".
म्हारी टोपी ओछी है। mhārī ṭopī ochhī hai. My cap is too small.

The word, taken apart.
ओछो (ochho) is inherited from a reconstructed ōccha, "small, thin, mean". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi ochā, "empty, small", and Gujarati ochũ, "deficient" (CDIAL 2540).

Lesson 5 of 7.
खुटणो (khuṭṇo) — to run out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a small bundle, then the Marwadi for too small.

You'll want to know: खुटणो.
खुटणो — khuṭṇo — "to run out".
घर मांय आटो खुटै है। ghar mā̃y āṭo khuṭai hai. We're running out of flour at home.
Past (it …): वो आटो खुट्यो, vo āṭo khuṭyo.

The word, taken apart.
खुटणो (khuṭṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed khuṭṭa, "defective". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati khuṭvũ, "to be exhausted", and Sindhi khuṭaṇu, "to fall short" (CDIAL 3895).
