# ఖజానా (khajānā) — a treasury

## You'll want to know: ఖజానా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C366-NIDHI366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖజానా** — *khajānā* — "a treasury".

> రాజు ఖజానా బంగారంతో నిండి ఉండేది.
>
> *rāju khajānā baṅgārantō niṇḍi uṇḍēdi.*
>
> The king's treasury used to be full of gold.

Today also the public purse: ప్రభుత్వ ఖజానా.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖజానా** (*khajānā*) is a loan from Arabic *khizāna*, "a storehouse, a treasury".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
