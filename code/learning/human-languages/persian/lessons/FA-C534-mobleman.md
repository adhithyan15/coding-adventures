## You'll want to know: مبلمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C534-FURNISH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مبلمان** (*moblemân*) — "furniture".

> آنها برای خانه جدید مبلمان خریدند.
>
> *ânhâ barâ-ye khâne-ye jadid moblemân kharidand.*
>
> They bought furniture for their new house.

A collective noun; *mobl* is one sofa or armchair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*moblemân* was borrowed from French *meublement*, 'furnishing' (EtymDB).
