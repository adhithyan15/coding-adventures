## You'll want to know: શક્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C366-SHARAT366-02]; assesses=[] -->

**શક્ય** — *śakya* — "possible".

> જો બધા મદદ કરશે તો આ કામ શક્ય છે.
>
> *jo badhā madad karśe to ā kām śakya che.*
>
> If everyone helps, this job can be done.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શક્ય** (*śakya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *śakya*, "able, possible, practicable" (Monier-Williams).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
