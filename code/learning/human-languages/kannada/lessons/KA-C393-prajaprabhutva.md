## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C393-SAMSATTU393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ** — *prajāprabhutva* — "democracy".

> ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವದಲ್ಲಿ ಜನರೇ ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಆರಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *prajāprabhutvadalli janarē sarkāra ārisuttāre.*
>
> In a democracy the people choose the government.

ಪ್ರಜೆ (citizen) + ಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ (rule).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಜಾಪ್ರಭುತ್ವ** (*prajāprabhutva*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *prajā*, "people, subjects", and *prabhutva*, "lordship, rule" (Monier-Williams): rule by the people.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
