## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-JOIN-PAR-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-ATE-CLAUSE-01, PA-NEG-CLAUSE-01] -->

**ਪਰ** joins two clauses the way **ਅਤੇ** does, and warns that the second will
pull against the first:

> *(first sentence)* **ਪਰ** *(second sentence)* — "… **but** …"

It works best with the chapter before it, because the sharpest contrast a
beginner can draw is between something that is and something that **ਨਹੀਂ**.

**ਪਰ** is the **inherited** word — it came down with the language rather than
being borrowed into it. That matters more than it usually would, because the
next lesson is a second word for *but* that arrived from somewhere else
entirely, and this book already teaches one such pair without saying so.
