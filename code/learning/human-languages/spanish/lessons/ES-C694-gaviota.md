## You'll want to know: la gaviota
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C694-ANIMALES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la gaviota** — "the seagull".

> Las gaviotas vuelan sobre el puerto.
>
> *The seagulls fly over the harbour.*

Common in ports and on beaches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gaviota* is built on Latin *gāvia* "a sea bird, perhaps the seamew" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
