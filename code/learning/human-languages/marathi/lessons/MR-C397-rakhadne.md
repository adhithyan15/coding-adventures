# रखडणे (rakhaḍṇe) — to be stalled

## You'll want to know: रखडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C397-VILAMB397-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रखडणे** — *rakhaḍṇe* — "to be stalled".

> पुलाचं काम दोन वर्षं रखडलं.
>
> *pulācaṁ kām don varṣaṁ rakhaḍlaṁ.*
>
> Work on the bridge was stalled for two years.

Past (he …): तो रखडला, *to rakhaḍlā*.

काम रखडणे is the usual pair: work that stopped and drags on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रखडणे** (*rakhaḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb for work or plans that stop and drag on; its origin is not settled.
