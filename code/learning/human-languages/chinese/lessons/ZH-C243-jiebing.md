## You'll want to know: jiébīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C243-SEASON243-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiébīng* — "to freeze over".

> *dōngtiān hú lǐ de shuǐ jiébīng le* — In winter the water in the lake freezes over.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiébīng* is built from *jié* 'to knot' and *bīng* 'ice', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ket* and *ping* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tie' and the second 'water frozen'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
