## You'll want to know: আঙুল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C466-SHORIR466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আঙুল** — *āngul* — "a finger; a toe".

> ছুরিতে মীরার আঙুল কাটল।
>
> *chhurite mīrār āngul kāṭlô.*
>
> Mira cut her finger on the knife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আঙুল** (*āngul*) comes from Sanskrit *aṅguli*, "finger" (CDIAL 135), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form.
