## You'll want to know: کسکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C473-ACHE473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کسکنا** — *kasaknā* — "to ache dully; to twinge".

> سردی میں پرانا زخم کسکتا ہے۔
>
> *sardī meṅ purānā zakhm kasaktā hai.*
>
> The old wound aches in the cold.

Past (it …): وہ زخم رات بھر کسکا, *voh zakhm rāt bhar kaskā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کسکنا** (*kasaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kasaknā*, "to ache", under Sanskrit *kaṣati*, "scratches", which an old list of roots also gives as "hurts" (CDIAL 2972).
