## You'll want to know: zuòmèng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C158-WAKE158-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòmèng* — "to dream; to have a dream".

> *wǒ chángcháng zuòmèng* — I dream a lot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòmèng* is built from 做 'to do' and *mèng* 'a dream'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *mjuwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as the spirit wandering in sleep; 做 has no form in that dictionary, so the history of the first part is not settled.
