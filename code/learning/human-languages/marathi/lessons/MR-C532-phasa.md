## You'll want to know: फासा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C532-BAITHE532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फासा** — *phāsā* — "a die for games".

> मी फासा टाकला आणि सहा पडले.
>
> *mī phāsā ṭāklā āṇi sahā paḍle.*
>
> I threw the die and got a six.

Masculine. Plural: फासे, *phāse*.

फासा टाकणे, to throw the die.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फासा** (*phāsā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *phāsā*, "die", under Sanskrit *pāśa*, "die, dice" (CDIAL 8132).
