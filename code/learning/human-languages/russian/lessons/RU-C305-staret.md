## You'll want to know: стареть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C305-VOSPITYVAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**стареть** (*starét*) — "to grow old, to age".

> Мой пёс стареет и много спит.
>
> *Moy pyos staréyet i mnógo spit.*
>
> My dog is getting old and sleeps a lot.

Imperfective; perfective *постареть* (*postarét*). Of things: *устареть* — to go out of date.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стареть* is built on *старый* "old", inherited from Proto-Slavic *starŭ* "old" (EtymDB), with the verb ending *-еть* for becoming.
