## You'll want to know: previamente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C538-ANTES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**previamente** — "beforehand, previously".

> Hay que reservar previamente.
>
> *You have to book beforehand.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*previamente* is built on *previo* and the ending *-mente* (EtymDB). *Previo* is a learned word from Latin *praevius* 'going before', built on *prae* and *via* 'way' (Lewis & Short).
