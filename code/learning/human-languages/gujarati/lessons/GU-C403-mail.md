## You'll want to know: માઈલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C403-HIGHWAY403-02]; assesses=[] -->

**માઈલ** — *māīl* — "a mile".

> સ્ટેશન અહીંથી બે માઈલ દૂર છે.
>
> *sṭeśan ahī̃thī be māīl dūr che.*
>
> The station is two miles from here.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માઈલ** (*māīl*) is borrowed from English *mile*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
