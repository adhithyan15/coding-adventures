## You'll want to know: పాలన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C335-KIRITAM335-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పాలన** — *pālana* — "a reign; governance".

> ఆ రాజు పాలనలో ప్రజలు సుఖంగా ఉండేవారు.
>
> *ā rāju pālanalō prajalu sukhaṅgā uṇḍēvāru.*
>
> Under that king's rule the people lived in comfort.

పాలనలో, under the rule of: the locative -లో.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పాలన** (*pālana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pālana*, "protecting, nourishing" (CDIAL 8128): a good ruler protects.
