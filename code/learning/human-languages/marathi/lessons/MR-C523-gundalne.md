# गुंडाळणे (guṇḍāḷṇe) — to roll up; to wrap

## You'll want to know: गुंडाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C523-GALICHA523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गुंडाळणे** — *guṇḍāḷṇe* — "to roll up; to wrap".

> सकाळी त्याने गालिचा गुंडाळला आणि बाहेर नेला.
>
> *sakāḷī tyāne gālicā guṇḍāḷlā āṇi bāher nelā.*
>
> In the morning he rolled up the carpet and took it outside.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चटई गुंडाळली, *tyāne caṭaī guṇḍāḷlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुंडाळणे** (*guṇḍāḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb for rolling or wrapping; its origin is not settled.
