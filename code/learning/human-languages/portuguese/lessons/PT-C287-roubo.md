## You'll want to know: o roubo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C287-ROUBO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o roubo** — "the robbery".

> Houve um roubo na loja ao lado.
>
> *There was a robbery in the shop next door.*

Taking something by force or threat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*roubo* is built on *roubar* "to rob", which EtymDB traces through Medieval Latin to Frankish *raubōn* "to plunder".
