Telugu, chapter 119: Honey, Idli, Dosa, Vada, Coffee.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
తేనె (tēne) — honey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for ginger, then the Telugu for garlic.

You'll want to know: తేనె.
తేనె — tēne — "honey".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tēne]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tēne, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say tēne]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for ginger, then the Telugu for garlic, then say tēne again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does తేనె (tēne) mean? ("Honey".) Say it once more.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a comb, soap, a towel, glasses, a clock]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a bag, a pen, paper, a drinking glass, a spoon]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a stove, a candle, a matchbox, a doll; a picture, a ball]

(చెంచా, cemcā.) A stove? (పొయ్యి, poyyi.) A candle? (కొవ్వొత్తి, kovvotti.) A matchbox? (అగ్గిపెట్టె, aggipeṭṭe.) A doll; a picture? (బొమ్మ, bomma.) A ball? (బంతి, banti.) A kite?
