## You'll want to know: てつやする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C453-PAPERS453-05]; assesses=[] -->

**てつやする** — *tetsuya suru* — "to stay up all night".

> **しけんの まえに てつやしました。** — *shiken no mae ni tetsuya shimashita* — "I stayed up all night before the exam."

Polite **てつやします**, *tetsuyashimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **あにが てつやしました。**, *ani ga tetsuya shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てつやする** (*tetsuya suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *tetsuya* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tetsu*, "sit up (all night)" (Middle Chinese *drjet*) and *ya*, "night" (*yaeH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
