## You'll want to know: der Flugsteig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C416-FLUGSTEIG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Flugsteig** — "the gate (at an airport)".

> Der Flugsteig für unseren Flug ist ganz am Ende.
>
> *The gate for our flight is right at the end.*

Plural: *die Flugsteige*. People also say *das Gate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Flugsteig* is built from *Flug* 'flight', inherited from Proto-Germanic *flugiz* (EtymDB), and *Steig* 'path, way up', from *steigen* 'to climb'.
