## You'll want to know: आळस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C254-VAID254-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आळस** — *āḷas* — "sluggishness; laziness".

> ताव मांय घणो आळस लागै है।
>
> *tāv mā̃y ghaṇo āḷas lāgai hai.*
>
> With a fever you feel very sluggish.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आळस** (*āḷas*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ālasya*, "sloth". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati *āḷas*, with this book's ळ (CDIAL 1371).
