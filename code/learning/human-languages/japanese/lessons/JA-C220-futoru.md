## You'll want to know: ふとる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C220-WEIGHT220-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふとる** — *futoru* — "to gain weight".

> **いぬは ふとりましたか。** — *inu wa futorimashita ka* — "Has the dog put on weight?"

Polite **ふとります**, *futorimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふとる** (*futoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed and built on the same stem as *futoi*, "thick"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
