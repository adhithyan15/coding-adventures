# Review — a plan, some advice, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C285-OPINION-ANNA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C285-FA-SO-01, AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I have a question", then "I think that it is easy". (*ʿIndī
suʾāl*, *aʿtaqidu annahu sahl*.) Then "so", the one-letter way. (*Fa-*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-SUBJUNCTIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-YAJIBU-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-YANBAGHI-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-BI-SABAB-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-FUTURE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-LAN-01] -->

Four lessons, and one vowel at the end of a verb. After *an*, after *yajibu
an* and *yanbaghī an*, and after *lan*, the present ends in *-a*: the shape
for a thing wanted, needed or refused, and not yet a fact. *Sa-* and *sawfa*
keep the plain present. *Bi-sabab* gives a reason that is a thing, *li-anna*
one that is a sentence.

[PAUSE 2s] A need and its reason in one breath:

> **يجب أن أزور الطبيب غدا، لأن عندي سؤالا.** — *yajibu an azūra
> aṭ-ṭabība ghadan, li-anna ʿindī suʾālan* — I must visit the doctor tomorrow,
> because I have a question.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-AS-SABAB-HUWA-01, AR-LEX-C40-LIANNA-01, AR-LEX-C54-HEALTH54-04, AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-01, AR-LEX-C282-AMAL282-01, AR-LEX-C280-AMAL280-03] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "I want to travel", then "I won't travel" — *urīdu an usāfira*, *lan usāfira*]
- [YOU SAY: "You ought to rest" — *yanbaghī an tastarīḥa*]
- [YOU SAY: "The lawyer must come now" — *yajibu an yajīʾa al-muḥāmī al-ān*]
- [YOU SAY: "The reason is that the calculator is not here" — *as-sababu huwa anna al-ḥāsibata laysat hunā*]
- [YOU SAY: a plan of your own with *sa-*, and its reason with *li-anna* or *bi-sabab*]
