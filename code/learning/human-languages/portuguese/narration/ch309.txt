Portuguese, chapter 309: Nasceu o bebé.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o nascimento — the birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to faint, then the Portuguese for to twist.

You'll want to know: o nascimento.
o nascimento — "the birth".
O nascimento da minha filha mudou a minha vida. My daughter’s birth changed my life.
Data de nascimento — date of birth.

The word, taken apart.
nascimento is built on nascer "to be born", which goes back to Latin nāscī "to be born" (Lewis & Short), with the ending -mento.

Lesson 5 of 5.
o recém-nascido — the newborn baby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for pregnant, then the Portuguese for pregnancy.

You'll want to know: o recém-nascido.
o recém-nascido — "the newborn baby".
O recém-nascido dorme quase o dia todo. The newborn sleeps almost all day.
Recém- means ‘newly’: recém-casados.

The word, taken apart.
recém-nascido joins recém, a short form of recente, from Latin recēns "fresh, new", and nascido "born", from Latin nāscī (Lewis & Short).
