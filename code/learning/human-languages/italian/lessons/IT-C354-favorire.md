## You'll want to know: favorire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C354-OSTACOLO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**favorire** — "to favour, to encourage, to help along".

> Il sole e la pioggia favoriscono la crescita delle piante.
>
> *Sun and rain help the plants grow.*

*Favorisca!* — please, help yourself (formal, offering food or a seat).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*favorire* is built on *favore* "favour", a learned borrowing from Latin *favor* "goodwill", from *favēre* "to be kind to, to support" (Lewis & Short).
