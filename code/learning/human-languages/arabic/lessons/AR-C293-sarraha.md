## You'll want to know: صرح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C293-AKHBAR293-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صرح** — *ṣarraḥa* — "to declare, to state".

> صرح المدير بأن المكتب مغلق.
>
> *ṣarraḥa al-mudīr bi-anna al-maktab mughlaq.*
>
> The manager stated that the office was closed.

Present: يصرح, *yuṣarriḥ*.

It takes بـ before what is stated: صرح بأن …

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صرح** (*ṣarraḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-r-ḥ* (Lane), "pure, unmixed, clear". It is Form II, "to make clear": to say a thing plainly.
