Bengali, chapter 372: Going Up and Down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বাড়া (bāṛā) — to grow; to go up (in amount).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to supply, then the Bengali for a limit.

You'll want to know: বাড়া.
বাড়া — bāṛā — "to grow; to go up (in amount)".
দাম বাড়লে লোকে কম কেনে। dām bāṛle loke kôm kene. When prices go up, people buy less.
Past (he/she …): সেই নদীর জল বাড়ল, sei nôdīr jôl bāṛlô.

The word, taken apart.
বাড়া (bāṛā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit várdhatē, grows (CDIAL 11376 lists Bengali bāṛā). It is the partner of the taught বাড়ানো: prices বাড়ে by themselves; someone বাড়ায় them.

Lesson 2 of 5.
কমা (kômā) — to fall; to decrease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a limit, then the Bengali for to grow.

You'll want to know: কমা.
কমা — kômā — "to fall; to decrease".
জ্বর না কমলে ডাক্তার ডাকো। jbôr nā kômle ḍāktār ḍāko. If the fever doesn't come down, call a doctor.
Past (he/she …): সেই জ্বর কমল, sei jbôr kômlô.

The word, taken apart.
কমা (kômā) is a Bengali verb made on কম, less, usually traced to Persian kam. It is the partner of the taught কমানো: a fever কমে by itself; medicine কমায় it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
স্তর (stôr) — a level; a layer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to decrease, then the Bengali for twice as much.

You'll want to know: স্তর.
স্তর — stôr — "a level; a layer".
বৃষ্টির পরে নদীর জলের স্তর অনেক বাড়ল। brishṭir pôre nôdīr jôler stôr ônek bāṛlô. After the rain the water level in the river rose a lot.

The word, taken apart.
স্তর (stôr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit stara, 'a layer' (CDIAL 13685). In modern use a layer of something has also become its level: জলের স্তর, the water level.
