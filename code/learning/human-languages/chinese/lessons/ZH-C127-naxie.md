## You'll want to know: 那些
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C127-THESE127-02]; assesses=[] -->

**那些** — *nàxiē* — "those".

> **那些人是我以前的同学** *nàxiē rén shì wǒ yǐqián de tóngxué* — Those people are my old classmates.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**那些** (*nàxiē*) is built from 那 'that' and 些 'a few', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *na* and *sjae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'how, what', not 'that', and the second 'a little'.
