## You'll want to know: die Müdigkeit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C486-MUEDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Müdigkeit** — "tiredness".

> Nach dem Mittagessen kommt bei mir oft die Müdigkeit.
>
> *After lunch I often get tired.*

No plural. Built on *müde* — tired.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Müdigkeit* is built on *müde* "tired" with *-keit*; *müde* is inherited from Old High German *muodi*, from Proto-Germanic *mōþijaz* (EtymDB).
