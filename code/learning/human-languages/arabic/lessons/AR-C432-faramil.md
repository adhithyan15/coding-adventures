## You'll want to know: فرامل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C432-WAQUD432-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فرامل** — *farāmil* — "brakes".

> ضغط أبي على الفرامل بسرعة.
>
> *ḍaghaṭa abī ʿalā al-farāmil bi-surʿa.*
>
> My father pressed the brakes quickly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرامل** (*farāmil*) is a loanword whose origin is not settled; it is often traced to Italian *freno*, "a brake".
