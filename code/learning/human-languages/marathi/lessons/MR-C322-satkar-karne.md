# सत्कार करणे (satkār karṇe) — to felicitate; to honour publicly

## You'll want to know: सत्कार करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C322-PURASKAR322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्कार करणे** — *satkār karṇe* — "to felicitate; to honour publicly".

> गावाने विजेत्या खेळाडूचा सत्कार केला.
>
> *gāvāne vijetyā kheḷāḍūcā satkār kelā.*
>
> The village honoured the winning player.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सत्कार केला, *tyāne satkār kelā*.

The person honoured takes -चा: शिक्षकांचा सत्कार करणे; सत्कार is masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्कार करणे** (*satkār karṇe*) joins *satkār*, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *satkāra*, 'kind treatment, honour' (Monier-Williams), to *karṇe*, 'to do'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
