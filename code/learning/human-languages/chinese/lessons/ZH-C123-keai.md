## You'll want to know: kě'ài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C123-PEOPLE123-03]; assesses=[] -->

*kě'ài* — "cute; lovable".

> *zhè zhī māo hěn kě'ài* — This cat is so cute.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kě'ài* is built from *kě*, glossed 'to permit', and *ài* 'to love', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaX* and *'ojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): worth loving.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
