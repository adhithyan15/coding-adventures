## You'll want to know: ہارن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C393-JAM393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہارن** — *hārn* — "a horn (of a vehicle)".

> جام میں سب لوگ ہارن بجاتے ہیں۔
>
> *jām meṅ sab log hārn bajāte haiṅ.*
>
> In a jam everyone sounds the horn.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہارن** (*hārn*) is borrowed from English *horn*; you sound it with **بجانا**.
