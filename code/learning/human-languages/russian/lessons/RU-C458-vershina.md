## You'll want to know: вершина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C458-DOLINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**вершина** (*vershína*) — "a summit, a peak".

> До вершины горы мы шли пять часов.
>
> *Do vershíny gorý my shli pyat chasóv.*
>
> It took us five hours to walk to the top of the mountain.

Feminine. Built on *верх* — the top.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вершина* is built on *верх* 'the top' with *-ина*. EtymDB lists *вершина* without a deeper source.
