## You'll want to know: عمدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C377-BADTAR377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عمدہ** — *umdā* — "excellent; fine".

> اگر کھانا عمدہ ہوا تو ہم پھر آئیں گے۔
>
> *agar khānā umdā huā to ham phir āeṅ ge.*
>
> If the food is excellent, we will come again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عمدہ** (*umdā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿumda*, "a support; the best part".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
