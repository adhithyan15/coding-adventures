## You'll want to know: jiémù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C267-FILM267-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jiémù* — "programme; TV show".

> *zhège jiémù hěn hǎokàn* — This show is really good.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiémù* is built from 节 and *mù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tset* and *mjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes an older dictionary for the first, 'a joint of bamboo', and glosses the second 'the eye'. How the pair came to mean a programme is not settled.
