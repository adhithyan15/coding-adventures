Persian, chapter 484: Sadness and Regret.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غمگین (ghamgin) — sad, sorrowful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to lie, then the Persian for a liar.

You'll want to know: غمگین.
غمگین (ghamgin) — "sad, sorrowful".
او بعد از رفتن دوستش غمگین بود. u ba'd az raftan-e dustash ghamgin bud. She was sad after her friend left.
gham 'sorrow' + the adjective ending -gin. More literary than nârâhat.

The word, taken apart.
ghamgin is built in Persian on gham, 'sorrow', which was borrowed from Arabic ġamm (EtymDB), with the native adjective ending -gin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
غصه خوردن (ghosse khordan) — to fret; to grieve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for sad, then the Persian for sorrow.

You'll want to know: غصه خوردن.
غصه خوردن (ghosse khordan) — "to fret; to grieve".
مادربزرگ برای همه غصه میخورد. mâdarbozorg barâ-ye hame ghosse mikhorad. Grandmother frets about everyone.
ghosse 'grief' + khordan 'to eat': 'to eat grief'. Present stem khor-.

The word, taken apart.
ghosse was borrowed from Arabic ġuṣṣa, 'distress, pang' (EtymDB); Hindi gussa 'anger' is usually traced to the same word. khordan, 'to eat', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
افسرده (afsorde) — depressed, low.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for sorrow, then the Persian for to fret.

You'll want to know: افسرده.
افسرده (afsorde) — "depressed, low".
او در زمستان کمی افسرده میشود. u dar zemestân kami afsorde mishavad. He gets a little low in winter.
The past participle of afsordan 'to wither, to freeze'. The noun is afsordegi 'depression'.

The word, taken apart.
afsorde is built on the native Persian verb afsordan, 'to wither, to freeze': a withered mood. EtymDB has no entry for it, and its older form is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پشیمان (pashimân) — sorry, regretful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to fret, then the Persian for depressed.

You'll want to know: پشیمان.
پشیمان (pashimân) — "sorry, regretful".
او از حرفش پشیمان شد. u az harfash pashimân shod. He regretted what he had said.
az chizi pashimân shodan — to come to regret something. The noun is pashimâni.

The word, taken apart.
pashimân is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian pašēmān. EtymDB has no entry for it, so the link is hedged.
