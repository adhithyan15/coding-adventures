## You'll want to know: きぬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C485-CLOTH485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きぬ** — *kinu* — "silk".

> **きぬの きものは たかいです。** — *kinu no kimono wa takai desu* — "Silk kimono are expensive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きぬ** (*kinu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
