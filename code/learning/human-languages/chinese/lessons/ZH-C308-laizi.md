## You'll want to know: 来自
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C308-SINCE308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**来自** — *láizì* — "to come from".

> **来自中国的新生经常来我家** *láizì Zhōngguó de xīnshēng jīngcháng lái wǒ jiā* — The new students from China often come to our house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**来自** (*láizì*) is built from 来 'to come' and 自 'from', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *loj* and *dzijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to arrive'.
