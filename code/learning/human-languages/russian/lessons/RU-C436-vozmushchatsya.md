## You'll want to know: возмущаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C436-VOZVRAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**возмущаться** (*vozmushchátsya*) — "to be indignant, to protest".

> Пассажиры возмущались, когда рейс отменили.
>
> *Passazhíry vozmushchális, kogdá reys otmeníli.*
>
> The passengers were outraged when the flight was cancelled.

Imperfective; the perfective is *возмутиться*. Takes the instrumental: *возмущаться ценами*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*возмущаться* is built from *воз-* 'up', *мутить* 'to stir up, to trouble' and *-ся*. *Мутить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *mǫtiti* 'to stir, to trouble' (EtymDB).
