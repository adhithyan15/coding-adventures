French, chapter 362: Citoyens.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le citoyen — the citizen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the efficiency, then the French for the productivity.

You'll want to know: le citoyen.
le citoyen — "the citizen".
Chaque citoyen a le droit de voter. Every citizen has the right to vote.
Feminine citoyenne.

The word, taken apart.
citoyen is inherited from Old French citoien (EtymDB), built on cité "city", from Latin cīvitās "citizenship, a state", from cīvis "citizen" (Lewis & Short).

Elle a la double nationalité, française et canadienne. She has dual nationality, French and Canadian.
De nationalité française — of French nationality.

The word, taken apart.
nationalité is built from nation and -ité. nation comes from Latin nātiō "birth, a people" (Lewis & Short), from nāscī "to be born".
