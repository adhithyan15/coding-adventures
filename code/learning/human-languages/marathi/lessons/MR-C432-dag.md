## You'll want to know: डाग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C432-BHOK432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**डाग** — *ḍāg* — "a stain; a spot".

> शर्टवर चहाचा डाग पडला.
>
> *śarṭvar cahācā ḍāg paḍlā.*
>
> A tea stain got on the shirt.

Masculine.

डाग पडणे, a stain appears; डाग काढणे, to remove one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डाग** (*ḍāg*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *dāġ*, "a brand, a mark".
