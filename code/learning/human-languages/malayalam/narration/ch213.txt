Malayalam, chapter 213: Worm, Bat, Koel, Kite, Crane.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പുഴു (puḻu) — a worm, a caterpillar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for God, then the Malayalam for bamboo.

You'll want to know: പുഴു.
പുഴു — puḻu — "a worm, a caterpillar".

The word, taken apart.
പുഴു (puḻu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil puḻu, Kannada puḻu and Telugu pur(u)gu (DEDR 4312). DEDR 4312 compares Sanskrit pīlu.
