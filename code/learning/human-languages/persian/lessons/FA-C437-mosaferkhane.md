## You'll want to know: مسافرخانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C437-MOSAFERKHANE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مسافرخانه** (*mosâferkhâne*) — "a guesthouse, a cheap inn".

> شب در یک مسافرخانه ارزان ماندیم.
>
> *Shab dar yek mosâferkhâne-ye arzân mândim.*
>
> We spent the night in a cheap guesthouse.

*mosâfer* 'traveller' + *khâne* 'house'. Simpler and cheaper than a *hotel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mosâferkhâne* is built from *mosâfer* 'traveller', borrowed from Arabic *musāfir*, and native *khâne* 'house', usually traced to Middle Persian *xānag*.
