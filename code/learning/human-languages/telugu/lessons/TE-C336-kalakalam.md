# కలకలం (kalakalaṁ) — an uproar; a stir

## You'll want to know: కలకలం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C336-PUKARU336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కలకలం** — *kalakalaṁ* — "an uproar; a stir".

> ఆ వార్త పట్టణంలో కలకలం రేపింది.
>
> *ā vārta paṭṭaṇaṁlō kalakalaṁ rēpindi.*
>
> The news caused an uproar in the town.

కలకలం రేపు, to cause a stir, is the headline phrase.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కలకలం** (*kalakalaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kalakala*, "any confused noise, the murmur of a crowd" (Monier-Williams).
