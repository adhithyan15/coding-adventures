Lesson 1 of 6.
রাক্ষস (rākkhôs) — an ogre; a demon who eats people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for magic, then the Bengali for to sleep.

You'll want to know: রাক্ষস.
রাক্ষস — rākkhôs — "an ogre; a demon who eats people".
রাক্ষসটা রাতে গ্রামে এসে মানুষ খেত। rākkhôsṭā rāte grāme ese mānush khetô. The ogre used to come to the village at night and eat people.

The word, taken apart.
রাক্ষস (rākkhôs) is Sanskrit rākṣasa, a Rakṣas, a malignant demon of the old epics (Monier-Williams). Its ক্ষ is read kkh.

রূপকথার ডাইনিটা বনের ভিতরে একা থাকত। rūpkôthār ḍāiniṭā bôner bhitôre ekā thāktô. The witch in the fairy tale lived alone deep in the forest.

The word, taken apart.
ডাইনি (ḍāini) comes down from Sanskrit ḍākinī, a female spirit in the train of the goddess Kālī (CDIAL 5542, which lists Bengali ḍāin and ḍāini, 'a witch').

The word, taken apart.
ভূত (bhūt) is Sanskrit bhūta, 'become, been, past' (Monier-Williams), and so a being, a spirit: one who has been.
