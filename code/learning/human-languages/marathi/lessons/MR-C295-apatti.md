## You'll want to know: आपत्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C295-SANKAT295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्ती** — *āpattī* — "a calamity".

> अशा आपत्तीत सगळे एकमेकांना मदत करतात.
>
> *aśā āpattīt sagḷe ekmekānnā madat kartāt.*
>
> In such a calamity everyone helps one another.

Feminine.

नैसर्गिक आपत्ती, a natural calamity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्ती** (*āpattī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *āpatti*, 'a happening, an occurrence' (Monier-Williams); Marathi keeps it for the happenings that bring ruin.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
