## You'll want to know: ਘਿਉ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C239-KAM239-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘਿਉ** — *ghiu* — "ghee; clarified butter".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਰੋਟੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਘਿਉ ਮਲਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī roṭī utte ghiu maldī hai.*
>
> Grandmother rubs ghee on the roti.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘਿਉ** (*ghiu*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ghṛta*, "clarified butter, ghee"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gheu*, "ghee" (CDIAL 4501).
