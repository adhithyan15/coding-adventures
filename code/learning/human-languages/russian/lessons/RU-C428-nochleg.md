## You'll want to know: ночлег
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C428-NOCHLEG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ночлег** (*nochlég*) — "a bed for the night, a place to spend the night".

> Уже темно, а ночлега у нас ещё нет.
>
> *Uzhé temnó, a nochléga u nas yeshchyó net.*
>
> It is already dark, and we still have nowhere to sleep.

Masculine. *Искать ночлег* — to look for a place to stay the night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ночлег* is built from *ночь* 'night' and *-лег*, the root of *лечь* 'to lie down': a place to lie down for the night. EtymDB lists the word without a deeper source.
