# ਵਿੱਚ (vich) — in, at

[PAUSE 2s] Near and farther: **ਇਹ / ਉਹ**. Now bring back two familiar places:
**ਘਰ** (*ghar*, home) and **ਸਕੂਲ** (*sakūl*, school). Ask **ਕਿੱਥੇ?**
(*kitthe?*, where?).

## You'll want to know: ਵਿੱਚ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-05] -->

> **ਵਿੱਚ** — *vich* — **in, at**

English puts *in* before a place. Punjabi puts **ਵਿੱਚ** after it. That is why
it is called a postposition: the place comes first.

> **ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ** — *ghar vich* — at home, in the house
>
> **ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ** — *sakūl vich* — at school, in the school

Do not add a verb yet. This step owns only the place chunk: noun, then **ਵਿੱਚ**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01] -->

**ਵਿੱਚ** is inherited through Old Punjabi *vici* and Prakrit *vicca* from a
form reconstructed as Sanskrit *vīcya*, “middle” (CDIAL 12042). Hindi *bīc*,
“middle”, is a cousin. The old idea of being in the middle became Punjabi's
ordinary word for being **in** a place.
