## You'll want to know: خلل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C460-ATAL460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خلل** — *khalal* — "a malfunction; a defect".

> وجد المهندس خللا في الجهاز.
>
> *wajada al-muhandis khalalan fī al-jihāz.*
>
> The engineer found a defect in the device.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خلل** (*khalal*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-l-l*, "a gap, an opening" (Lane).
