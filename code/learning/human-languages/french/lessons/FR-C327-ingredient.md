## You'll want to know: l'ingrédient
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C327-RECETTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ingrédient** — "the ingredient".

> Elle a acheté tous les ingrédients au marché.
>
> *She bought all the ingredients at the market.*

Masculine: *un ingrédient*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingrédient* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ingrediēns* "going in" (EtymDB), from *ingredī* "to enter", built on *gradī* "to step".
