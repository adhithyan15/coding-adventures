## You'll want to know: प्रहिणोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C527-DHANURDHARA527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहिणोति** — *prahiṇoti* — "shoots; sends forth".

> धनुर्धरः लक्ष्यं प्रति बाणं प्रहिणोति।
>
> *dhanurdharaḥ lakṣyaṃ prati bāṇaṃ prahiṇoti.*
>
> The archer shoots an arrow at the target.

Past (he …): सः बाणं प्रहितवान्, *saḥ bāṇaṃ prahitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रहिणोति** (*prahiṇoti*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √hi, "to send, to impel"; MW gives *prahi* as to urge on, to send, to hurl; its past participle is *prahita* (MW).
