## You'll want to know: نهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C469-TALAB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نهر** (*nahr*) — "a stream, a brook; a channel".

> کنار نهر نشستیم و چای خوردیم.
>
> *Kenâr-e nahr neshastim va chây khordim.*
>
> We sat by the stream and drank tea.

In Arabic *nahr* is a river; in Persian a smaller stream.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nahr* was borrowed from Arabic *nahr* 'river' (EtymDB); in Persian it names a stream or a channel.
