Urdu, chapter 458: Patience and Temper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صبر کرنا (sabr karnā) — to be patient; to bear a loss calmly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for integrity, then the Urdu for a promise.

You'll want to know: صبر کرنا.
صبر کرنا — sabr karnā — "to be patient; to bear a loss calmly".
ہمیں صبر کرنا چاہیے۔ hameṅ sabr karnā chāhiye. We should be patient.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے صبر کیا, us ne sabr kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
صبر کرنا (sabr karnā) is built from صبر, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṣabr, "patience, endurance", and کرنا, "to do".

ابو نے بھائی کو جھڑکا۔ abbū ne bhāī ko jhiṛkā. Dad snapped at my brother.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو جھڑکا, us ne bachche ko jhiṛkā.

The word, taken apart.
جھڑکنا (jhiṛaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhiṛaknā, "to shake, to scold", under jhaṭ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5327).
