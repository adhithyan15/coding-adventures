## You'll want to know: たから
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C301-TAKARA301-01]; assesses=[] -->

**たから** — *takara* — "a treasure".

> **むかし ここに たからが ありました。** — *mukashi koko ni takara ga arimashita* — "Long ago there was treasure here."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たから** (*takara*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
