## You'll want to know: eleggere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C369-PARTITO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**eleggere** — "to elect".

> I cittadini eleggono il sindaco ogni cinque anni.
>
> *The citizens elect the mayor every five years.*

Conjugated like *leggere*; past participle *eletto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eleggere* comes from Latin *ēligere* "to pick out, to choose" (EtymDB), built from *ē* "out" and *legere* "to gather, to choose".
