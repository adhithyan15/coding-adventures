## You'll want to know: जाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C445-KISHTI445-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जाल** — *jāl* — "a net".

> मल्लाह ने पानी में जाल फेंका।
>
> *mallāh ne pānī meṁ jāl phẽkā.*
>
> The boatman cast his net into the water.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जाल** (*jāl*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *jāla*, "a net" (CDIAL 5213), which lists Hindi *jāl*, a net for birds or fish; it is a different word from *jala*, water.
