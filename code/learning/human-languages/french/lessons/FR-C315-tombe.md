## You'll want to know: la tombe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C315-DEUIL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la tombe** — "the grave, the tomb".

> Elle met des fleurs sur la tombe de sa mère.
>
> *She puts flowers on her mother's grave.*

Not to be confused with *il tombe*, he falls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tombe* comes from Late Latin *tumba* "a tomb" (EtymDB), a borrowing from Greek *týmbos* "a burial mound".
