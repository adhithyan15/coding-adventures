Russian, chapter 477: Longing and Loneliness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
тоска (toská) — longing; melancholy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a coward, then the Russian for courage.

You'll want to know: тоска.
тоска (toská) — "longing; melancholy".
Она чувствует тоску по родине. Oná chúvstvuyet toskú po ródine. She feels homesick.
Feminine. Тоска по + dative — longing for.

The word, taken apart.
тоска (toská) is inherited from Proto-Slavic tъska (EtymDB), which EtymDB glosses 'emptiness'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
тосковать (toskovát) — to pine, to miss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for courage, then the Russian for longing.

You'll want to know: тосковать.
тосковать (toskovát) — "to pine, to miss".
Бабушка тоскует по деревне. Bábushka toskúyet po derévne. Grandma misses the village.
Imperfective; present тоскую, тоскуешь. Тосковать по + dative.

The word, taken apart.
тосковать (toskovát) is built on тоска (toská) 'longing' with -овать; тоска (toská) is inherited from Proto-Slavic tъska (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
одиночество (odinóchestvo) — loneliness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for longing, then the Russian for to pine.

You'll want to know: одиночество.
одиночество (odinóchestvo) — "loneliness".
После переезда он долго страдал от одиночества. Pósle pereyézda on dólgo stradál ot odinóchestva. After the move he suffered from loneliness for a long time.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
одиночество (odinóchestvo) is built on одинокий (odinókiy) 'lonely', from один 'one', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic edinъ (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
одинокий (odinókiy) — lonely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to pine, then the Russian for loneliness.

You'll want to know: одинокий.
одинокий (odinókiy) — "lonely".
Наш сосед — одинокий старик. Nash soséd — odinókiy starík. Our neighbour is a lonely old man.
Also single, living alone. Short forms: одинок, одинока.

The word, taken apart.
одинокий (odinókiy) is built on один 'one', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic edinъ, from PIE óynos 'one' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
сожалеть (sozhalét) — to regret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for loneliness, then the Russian for lonely.

You'll want to know: сожалеть.
сожалеть (sozhalét) — "to regret".
Я сожалею, что не поехал с вами. Ya sozhaléyu, chto ne poyékhal s vámi. I regret not going with you.
Imperfective; present сожалею. Сожалеть о + prepositional. More formal than жалеть.

The word, taken apart.
сожалеть (sozhalét) is built on со- and жалеть 'to pity', from жаль 'pity'; EtymDB lists these words without a source, and they are usually traced to Proto-Slavic žalь 'grief'.
