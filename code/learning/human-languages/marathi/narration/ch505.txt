Marathi, chapter 505: Old Crafts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कुंभार (kumbhār) — a potter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a barber, then the Marathi for to hammer in.

You'll want to know: कुंभार.
कुंभार — kumbhār — "a potter".
कुंभार मातीची भांडी बनवतो. kumbhār mātīcī bhāṇḍī banavto. The potter makes clay pots.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
कुंभार (kumbhār) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit kumbhakāra, "potter" (CDIAL 3310), from kumbha, "a pot", and kāra, "maker".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a blacksmith, then the Marathi for a goldsmith.

You'll want to know: विणकर.
विणकर — viṇkar — "a weaver".
गावातला विणकर रेशमी साड्या विणतो. gāvātlā viṇkar reśmī sāḍyā viṇto. The village weaver weaves silk saris.
Masculine.
Built on विणणे, to weave, with -कर, one who does.

The word, taken apart.
विणकर (viṇkar) is a Marathi derivation built on विणणे, "to weave", with the agent ending -कर, "one who does", from Sanskrit kara (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
थाटणे (thāṭṇe) — to set up; to lay out with care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a goldsmith, then the Marathi for a weaver.

You'll want to know: थाटणे.
थाटणे — thāṭṇe — "to set up; to lay out with care".
विणकराने बाजारात नवीन दुकान थाटलं. viṇkarāne bājārāt navīn dukān thāṭlaṁ. The weaver set up a new shop in the market.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दुकान थाटलं, tyāne dukān thāṭlaṁ.
थाटणे (thāṭṇe) is said of a shop or a home set up with some show; थाट is the show itself.

The word, taken apart.
थाटणे (thāṭṇe) is built on थाट, "pomp, a fine arrangement"; CDIAL sets Hindi ṭhāṭh, "pomp", and Marathi thaṭṇẽ, "to act pompously", under thaṭṭha, "pomp, ceremony" (CDIAL 6090).
