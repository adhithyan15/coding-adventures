## You'll want to know: उजड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C327-VANSH327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उजड़ना** — *ujaṛnā* — "to be ruined; to become deserted".

> अकाल के बाद पूरा गाँव उजड़ गया।
>
> *akāl ke bād pūrā gā̃v ujaṛ gayā.*
>
> After the famine the whole village was deserted.

Past (it …): वह गाँव उजड़ गया, *vah gā̃v ujaṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उजड़ना** (*ujaṛnā*) is inherited from *\*ujjaṭati*, "is uprooted", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1661). A place **उजड़ता है** when its people leave and it falls empty.
