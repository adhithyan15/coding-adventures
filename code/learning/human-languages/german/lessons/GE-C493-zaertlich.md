## You'll want to know: zärtlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C493-ZUNEIGUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**zärtlich** — "tender, affectionate".

> Die Mutter streichelt zärtlich den Kopf des Babys.
>
> *The mother tenderly strokes the baby's head.*

The noun: *die Zärtlichkeit* — tenderness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zärtlich* is built on *zart* "delicate, tender", inherited from Old High German *zart*, from Proto-Germanic *tardaz* (EtymDB), with *-lich*.
