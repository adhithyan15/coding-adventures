Marathi, chapter 132: Hammer, Scissors, Needle, Thread, Comb.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हातोडा (hātoḍā) — a hammer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rope, then the Marathi for a nail.

You'll want to know: हातोडा.
हातोडा — hātoḍā — "a hammer".
