French, chapter 531: Le bricolage.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
le bricolage — DIY, odd jobs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to wipe, then the French for tea towel.

You'll want to know: le bricolage.
le bricolage — "DIY, odd jobs".
Le week-end, mon oncle fait du bricolage. At the weekend my uncle does DIY.
Un magasin de bricolage — a DIY shop.

The word, taken apart.
le bricolage is built on bricoler, from Old French bricole (EtymDB), once a catapult and later a small job. The earlier origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 7.
la vis — screw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for DIY, then the French for to do DIY.

You'll want to know: la vis.
la vis — "screw".
Il manque une vis à cette chaise. This chair is missing a screw.
The s is sounded: vis rhymes with fils.

The word, taken apart.
la vis is inherited from Latin vītis "a vine" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the screw is probably named for the spiral of the vine's tendrils.

Lesson 4 of 7.
le tournevis — screwdriver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to do DIY, then the French for screw.

You'll want to know: le tournevis.
le tournevis — "screwdriver".
Pour ouvrir la radio, il a pris un petit tournevis. To open the radio, he took a small screwdriver.
Literally turn-screw. The s is sounded.

The word, taken apart.
le tournevis is built from tourner "to turn", from Latin tornāre, and vis "screw", inherited from Latin vītis "a vine" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 7.
percer — to drill, to pierce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for screw, then the French for screwdriver.

You'll want to know: percer.
percer — "to drill, to pierce".
Mon voisin perce un trou dans le mur. My neighbour is drilling a hole in the wall.
Spelling: nous perçons. Une perceuse — a drill.

The word, taken apart.
percer is inherited from Old French percier (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin pertūsiāre, built on Latin pertundere "to bore through" (Lewis & Short).
