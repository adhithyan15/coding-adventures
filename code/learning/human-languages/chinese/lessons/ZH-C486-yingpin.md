## You'll want to know: yìngpìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C486-JOBHUNT486-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yìngpìn* — "to apply for a job".

> *qù yìngpìn de shíhou xīntài zuì zhòngyào* — When you go to apply for a job, your frame of mind matters most.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìngpìn* is built from 应 'to answer' and *pìn* 'to engage by invitation', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'ingH* and *phjiengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'things answering each other'.
