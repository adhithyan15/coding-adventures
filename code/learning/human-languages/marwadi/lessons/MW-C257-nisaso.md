## You'll want to know: निसासो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C257-RIS257-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निसासो** — *nisāso* — "a sigh".

> दादी निसासो नाखै है।
>
> *dādī nisāso nākhai hai.*
>
> Grandmother heaves a sigh.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निसासो** (*nisāso*) is inherited from Sanskrit *niḥśvāsa*, "a sigh"; CDIAL lists Marwari *nisāso* under it (CDIAL 7111).
