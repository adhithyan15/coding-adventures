## You'll want to know: grandinare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C467-TEMPORALE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**grandinare** — "to hail".

> Ieri ha grandinato per dieci minuti.
>
> *Yesterday it hailed for ten minutes.*

It takes *avere* or *essere*: *ha grandinato*, *è grandinato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grandinare* is inherited from Latin *grandināre* 'to hail' (EtymDB), from *grandō* 'hail'.
