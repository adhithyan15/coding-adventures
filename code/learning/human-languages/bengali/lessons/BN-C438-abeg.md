## You'll want to know: আবেগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C438-MON438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আবেগ** — *ābeg* — "emotion; strong feeling".

> বাবা আবেগে কেঁদে ফেললেন।
>
> *bābā ābege kẽde phellen.*
>
> Father was so moved that he burst into tears.

আবেগে, overcome with feeling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আবেগ** (*ābeg*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *āvega*, "haste born of excitement, agitation" (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for a strong feeling that carries you along.
