# ફરિયાદ (phariyād) — a complaint

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C280-NEXT-TRAIN-01, GU-LEX-C84-TOWN-03, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-NE-OBJECT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: ask when the next train is. (*āvtī ṭren kyāre che?*) Then say "the police". (**પોલીસ**.)

## You'll want to know: ફરિયાદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C280-PHARIYAD-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERGATIVE-01] -->

**ફરિયાદ** — *phariyād* — "a complaint". It is feminine, so a past of **કરવું** follows it with **કરી**:

> **મેં પોલીસમાં ફરિયાદ કરી.**
>
> *mẽ polīsmā̃ phariyād karī.* — "I made a complaint to the police."

**પોલીસમાં** (*polīsmā̃*) is "with the police", word for word "in the police". A complaint can be about anything:

> **તમારી ફરિયાદ શું છે?**
>
> *tamārī phariyād śũ che?* — "What is your complaint?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફરિયાદ** (*phariyād*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *faryād*, "an outcry; a call for help", and from there "a complaint".
