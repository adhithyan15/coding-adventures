## You'll want to know: le débat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C335-DEBAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le débat** — "the debate".

> Ce soir, il y a un débat à la télévision sur l'école.
>
> *Tonight there is a debate on television about schools.*

*Un débat animé* — a lively debate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*débat* is formed from the verb *débattre*, built on *battre* "to beat", which is inherited from Latin *battuere* "to beat, to strike" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). Ideas are knocked back and forth.
