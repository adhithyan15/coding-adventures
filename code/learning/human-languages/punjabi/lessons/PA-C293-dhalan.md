## You'll want to know: ਢਲਾਣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C293-LAND293-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਲਾਣ** — *ḍhalāṇ* — "a slope".

> ਪਹਾੜ ਦੀ ਢਲਾਣ ਉੱਤੇ ਰੁੱਖ ਹਨ।
>
> *pahāṛ dī ḍhalāṇ utte rukkh han.*
>
> There are trees on the slope of the mountain.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਲਾਣ** (*ḍhalāṇ*) is built on *ḍhalṇā*, "to fall, be poured out", from a reconstructed *\*ḍhalati*, "bends over, falls"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhalṇā*, "to be poured out, fall", under it (CDIAL 5581).
