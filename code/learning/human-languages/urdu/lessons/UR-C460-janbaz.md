## You'll want to know: جانباز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C460-BRAVE460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جانباز** — *jānbāz* — "daring; ready to risk one's life".

> جانباز سپاہی نے بچے کو بچایا۔
>
> *jānbāz sipāhī ne bachche ko bachāyā.*
>
> The daring soldier saved the child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جانباز** (*jānbāz*) is usually traced to Persian *jānbāz*, "one who stakes his life": *jān*, "life", and *bāz*, "playing".
