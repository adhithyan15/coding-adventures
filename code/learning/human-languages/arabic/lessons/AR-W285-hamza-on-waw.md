# ؤ (hamza on waw) — the catch inside سؤال

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-W06-HAMZA-01, AR-SCRIPT-WRITE-FULL-GREETING-13] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ء and و once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **سؤال** (*suʾāl*), "a question". You met it a moment ago.
This lesson takes its catch out and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ؤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C285-HAMZA-WAW-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-02] -->

**ؤ** — *hamza* on a *wāw* seat, the glottal catch written *ʾ* in this
book's romanization.

It is the second letter of **سؤال**, between **س** and **ا**. It is two
pieces: a **و** below, the seat, and the small hamza mark riding on top of
it. Like a plain **و**, it takes the hand of **س** on its right and does not
join to the letter after it, so a gap opens before **ال**.

The seat does not change the sound. **أ**, **ء** and **ؤ** all say the same
catch; the seat follows the vowels around it, and here the *u* before it
calls for **و**.

## Writing: ؤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C285-HAMZA-WAW-01] -->

Put your pen on ؤ and follow its line. Write the **و** first, then add the
hamza above it. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is
printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this character or which way
> to travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
