## You'll want to know: ਵਾਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C265-SIDE265-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਟ** — *vāṭ* — "the distance to travel; the way".

> ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੀ ਵਾਟ ਲੰਮੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *shahir dī vāṭ lammī hai.*
>
> The way to the town is long.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਟ** (*vāṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vartman*, "track of a wheel, path"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vāṭ*, "path, road", under it (CDIAL 11366).
