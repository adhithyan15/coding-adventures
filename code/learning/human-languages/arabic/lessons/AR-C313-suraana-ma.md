## You'll want to know: سرعان ما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C313-FAWR313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سرعان ما** — *surʿāna mā* — "before long, soon after".

> بدأ المطر وسرعان ما توقف.
>
> *badaʾa al-maṭar wa-surʿāna mā tawaqqafa.*
>
> The rain began and soon stopped.

A past verb follows it: سرعان ما عاد.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرعان ما** (*surʿāna mā*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-ʿ*, "to be quick" (Lane), the root of *surʿa*, speed: *surʿāna* means "how quick!".
