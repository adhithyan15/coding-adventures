## You'll want to know: ਦਰਾਣੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C330-SPOUSE330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਰਾਣੀ** — *darāṇī* — "a husband's younger brother's wife".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਦਰਾਣੀ ਮੇਰੇ ਨਾਲ ਰਸੋਈ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī darāṇī mere nāl rasoī vich kamm kardī hai.*
>
> My husband's younger brother's wife works with me in the kitchen.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਰਾਣੀ** (*darāṇī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*devarajāni*, "a husband's younger brother's wife"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *darāṇī*, "husband's younger brother's wife", under it (CDIAL 6534).
