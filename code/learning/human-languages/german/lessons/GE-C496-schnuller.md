## You'll want to know: der Schnuller
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C496-BABY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schnuller** — "dummy, pacifier".

> Ohne Schnuller schläft das Baby nicht ein.
>
> *The baby won't fall asleep without a dummy.*

Plural: *die Schnuller*. In Switzerland: *der Nuggi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schnuller* is usually traced to the dialect verb *schnullen* "to suck"; EtymDB has no entry for it.
