Punjabi, chapter 246: Beams and Roofs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਲੱਕੜ (lakkaṛ) — wood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a pillar, then the Punjabi for a brick.

You'll want to know: ਲੱਕੜ.
ਲੱਕੜ — lakkaṛ — "wood".
ਇਹ ਮੇਜ਼ ਲੱਕੜ ਦਾ ਹੈ। eh mez lakkaṛ dā hai. This table is made of wood.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੱਕੜ (lakkaṛ) is inherited from Sanskrit lakuṭa, "club"; CDIAL lists Punjabi lakkaṛ, "wood" (CDIAL 10875). CDIAL marks lakkaṛ masculine and lists a feminine Punjabi form, also "wood", beside it; here ਲੱਕੜ is taught as feminine (ਗਿੱਲੀ ਲੱਕੜ).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for wood, then the Punjabi for a rafter.

You'll want to know: ਛੱਪਰ.
ਛੱਪਰ — chhappar — "a thatched roof; a shed".
ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਛੋਟਾ ਛੱਪਰ ਹੈ। khet vich ikk chhoṭā chhappar hai. There is a small thatched shed in the field.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਛੱਪਰ (chhappar) is inherited from Sanskrit chattvara, "house, bower"; CDIAL lists Punjabi chappar, "thatched roof" (CDIAL 4976).

Lesson 5 of 5.

ਪੁਰਾਣੇ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਪਰਛੱਤੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਨ। purāṇe bhāṇḍe parchhattī utte han. The old pots are on the high shelf.

ਪਰਛੱਤੀ (parchhattī) is inherited from a reconstructed praticchattika, "near the roof"; CDIAL lists Punjabi paṛchattī, "shelf under roof" (CDIAL 8556).
