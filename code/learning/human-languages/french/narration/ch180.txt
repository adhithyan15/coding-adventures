Lesson 1 of 5.
préparer — to prepare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to throw, then the French for to turn.

You'll want to know: préparer.
préparer — "to prepare".

The word, taken apart.
préparer from Latin praeparare, "to make ready beforehand" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).
