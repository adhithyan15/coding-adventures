# One heard greeting — write what you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTHO-HOLA-01] -->

Cover the answer line lower on this page. You will hear one familiar greeting.
No new Spanish is hiding in this lesson.

## Writing — short dictation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTHO-HOLA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

[YOU HEAR: *OH-la*]

Write the Spanish greeting from that sound alone.

Three sounds reached your ear. Four letters belong on your page. Your pen has
to supply a letter that the sound never gave you — and it goes first.

Now uncover and compare: **hola**. Check the four letters and the **h** at the
front. Repair one place if you need to.
