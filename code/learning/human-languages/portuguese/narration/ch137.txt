Portuguese, chapter 137: Opening Hours, a Notice, the Rent, an Advert, a Bill.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
o horário — opening hours, a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to plan, then the Portuguese for to organise.

You'll want to know: o horário.
o horário — "opening hours, a timetable".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the door — Horário: segunda a sexta, 9h–18h.]
Open on weekdays from nine to six: not on sábado.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for opening hours, then the Portuguese for a notice.

You'll want to know: a renda.
a renda — "the rent". The money for a quarto, paid every mês: A renda é de 400 euros por mês. In Brazil the rent is o aluguel; the verb alugar is the same in both.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: nothing, none, nobody, which, nor]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: recently, the day before yesterday, formerly, the past, to happen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Nothing? (nada.) None? (nenhum / nenhuma.) Nobody? (ninguém.) Which? (qual.) Nor? (nem.) Recently? (recentemente.) The day before yesterday? (anteontem.) Formerly? (antigamente.) The past? (o passado.) To happen? (acontecer.)
