## You'll want to know: consolare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C476-DELUSIONE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**consolare** — "to comfort, to console".

> Ho consolato la mia amica dopo la brutta notizia.
>
> *I comforted my friend after the bad news.*

Reflexive *consolarsi* — to cheer oneself up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consolare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnsōlārī* "to comfort, to cheer" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
