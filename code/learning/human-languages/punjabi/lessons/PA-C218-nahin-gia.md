# ਮੈਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ — I didn't go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I don't drink tea*. Then *Aman went to school*.

## Grammar Lens: ਨਹੀਂ before the past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-IA-218, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

**ਨਹੀਂ** goes before the past form, as before any verb. A past like this has no
helper to keep:

> **ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ।** — *maiṁ kallh sakūl nahīṁ giā.* — I didn't go to school yesterday (a man speaking).

> **ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਈ।** — *maiṁ kallh sakūl nahīṁ gaī.* — I didn't go to school yesterday (a woman speaking).

> **ਉਹ ਜਲਦੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਉੱਠਿਆ।** — *uh jaldī nahīṁ uṭṭhiā.* — He didn't get up early.

- [YOU WRITE: copy **ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ** once]
