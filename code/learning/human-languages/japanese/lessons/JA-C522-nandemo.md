## You'll want to know: なんでも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C522-SOME522-05]; assesses=[] -->

**なんでも** — *nandemo* — "anything; whatever".

> **もし しつもんが あったら、なんでも きいて ください。** — *moshi shitsumon ga attara, nandemo kiite kudasai* — "If you have questions, ask me anything."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なんでも** (*nandemo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from *nan*, "what", and *demo*, "even" (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
