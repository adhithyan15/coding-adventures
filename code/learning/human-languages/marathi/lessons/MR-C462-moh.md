## You'll want to know: मोह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C462-HEVA462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मोह** — *moh* — "temptation".

> लाडू पाहून मला खाण्याचा मोह झाला.
>
> *lāḍū pāhūn malā khāṇyācā moh jhālā.*
>
> Seeing the ladoos, I was tempted to eat one.

Masculine.

मोह होणे, to be tempted, with the dative; मोह टाळणे, to resist temptation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोह** (*moh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *moha*, "bewilderment" (CDIAL 10360); in Marathi it is the pull of temptation.
