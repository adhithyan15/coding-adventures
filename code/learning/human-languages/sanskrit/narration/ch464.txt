Sanskrit, chapter 464: Talkers and Quiet Ones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वाचालः (vācālaḥ) — talkative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for trembles with fear, then the Sanskrit for bold.

You'll want to know: वाचालः.
वाचालः — vācālaḥ — "talkative".
वाचालः नापितः सदा जल्पति। vācālaḥ nāpitaḥ sadā jalpati. The talkative barber is always chattering.

The word, taken apart.
वाचालः (vācālaḥ) is built on vāc, "speech, voice"; MW gives "talkative, chattering" and "boasting" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for bold, then the Sanskrit for talkative.

You'll want to know: मितभाषी.
मितभाषी — mitabhāṣī — "of few words".
मम पिता मितभाषी अस्ति। mama pitā mitabhāṣī asti. My father is a man of few words.

The word, taken apart.
मितभाषी (mitabhāṣī) is built from mita, "measured", and bhāṣin, "speaking", from the root √bhāṣ of भाषते: one who speaks by measure (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सम्बोधयति (sambodhayati) — calls out to; addresses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for talkative, then the Sanskrit for of few words.

You'll want to know: सम्बोधयति.
सम्बोधयति — sambodhayati — "calls out to; addresses".
माता द्वारात् पुत्रं सम्बोधयति। mātā dvārāt putraṃ sambodhayati. Mother calls out to her son from the door.
Past (she …): सा पुत्रं सम्बोधितवती, sā putraṃ sambodhitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
सम्बोधयति (sambodhayati) is built on sam and the causative of the root √budh, "to wake, to know", the root of बुध्यते: to rouse someone's notice; MW gives the causative the senses "to cause to wake up", "to inform, advise" and "to call to" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आक्रोशति (ākrośati) — reviles; scolds loudly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for of few words, then the Sanskrit for calls out to.

You'll want to know: आक्रोशति.
आक्रोशति — ākrośati — "reviles; scolds loudly".
कुपितः स्वामी सेवकम् आक्रोशति। kupitaḥ svāmī sevakam ākrośati. The angry master reviles his servant.
Past (he …): सः सेवकम् आक्रुष्टवान्, saḥ sevakam ākruṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
आक्रोशति (ākrośati) is built from ā and the root √kruś, "to cry out"; MW gives "to call to anyone in an abusive manner, scold at, revile" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रलापः (pralāpaḥ) — prattle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for calls out to, then the Sanskrit for reviles.

You'll want to know: प्रलापः.
प्रलापः — pralāpaḥ — "prattle".
तस्य प्रलापं कः शृणोति? tasya pralāpaṃ kaḥ śṛṇoti? Who listens to his prattle?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रलापः (pralāpaḥ) is built from pra and the root √lap, "to talk"; MW gives "talk, prattling, chattering" and also "lamentation" (MW).
