## You'll want to know: wēibōlú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C170-POT170-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wēibōlú* — "microwave oven".

> *fàn zài wēibōlú lǐ* — The food is in the microwave.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wēibōlú* is built from *wēi* 'tiny', *bō* 'wave' and *lú* 'stove', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, *mj+j*, *pa* and *lu* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): old words put together to name a new machine.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
