## You'll want to know: فرع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C452-FAR452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فرع** — *farʿ* — "a branch office; a branch".

> فتح البنك فرعا جديدا في حينا.
>
> *fataḥa al-bank farʿan jadīdan fī ḥayyinā.*
>
> The bank opened a new branch in our neighbourhood.

Plural: فروع, *furūʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرع** (*farʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-ʿ*, "a branch of a tree" (Lane).
