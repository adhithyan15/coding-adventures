## You'll want to know: patrulhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C293-PATRULHA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**patrulhar** — "to patrol".

> Os polícias patrulham o bairro de bicicleta.
>
> *The police patrol the neighbourhood by bike.*

Takes a direct object: *patrulhar a costa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patrulhar* was borrowed from French *patrouiller*, first "to paddle about in mud", then "to patrol", built on *patte* "a paw" (EtymDB).
