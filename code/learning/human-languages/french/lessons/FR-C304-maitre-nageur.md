## You'll want to know: le maître-nageur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C304-NOYADE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le maître-nageur** — "the lifeguard, the swimming instructor".

> Le maître-nageur surveille la piscine.
>
> *The lifeguard watches over the pool.*

Feminine: *la maître-nageuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maître* is inherited from Latin *magister* "master", and *nageur* is built on *nager* "to swim", which comes from Latin *nāvigāre* "to sail" (Lewis & Short).
