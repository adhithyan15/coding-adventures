Persian, chapter 138: Next, From Now On, a Plan, To Decide, To Hope.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آینده (âyande) — next, coming; the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for once, then the Persian for to spend.

You'll want to know: آینده.
آینده — âyande — "next, coming; the future". The future uses خواهم with the verb's past stem (رفت from رفتن): هفته آینده به مشهد خواهم رفت — hafte-ye âyande be mashhad khâham raft — "Next week I will go to Mashhad."
