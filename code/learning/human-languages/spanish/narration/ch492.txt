Spanish, chapter 492: Intentarlo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
intentar — to try.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the wallet, then the Spanish for the tip.

You'll want to know: intentar.
intentar — "to try".
Intento llegar a tiempo. I try to arrive on time.
Followed by an infinitive, with no preposition.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: intentar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: intentar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with intentar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the wallet, then the Spanish for the tip, then say intentar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does intentar mean? ("To try".) Say it once more.
