## You'll want to know: l'otage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C296-BRAQUAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'otage** — "the hostage".

> Les braqueurs ont pris trois otages.
>
> *The robbers took three hostages.*

Masculine even when the hostage is a woman: *un otage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*otage* comes from Old French *ostage*, usually traced to *oste* "guest, host", from Latin *hospes* (Lewis & Short): a guest held as a pledge. EtymDB lists no parent.
