## You'll want to know: मूर्छति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C294-SANGHATTA294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मूर्छति** — *mūrchati* — "faints".

> आहतः बालः मूर्छति।
>
> *āhataḥ bālaḥ mūrchati.*
>
> The injured boy faints.

Past (he …): सः मार्गे मूर्छितवान्, *saḥ mārge mūrchitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूर्छति** (*mūrchati*) comes from the root √mūrch, which MW gives as to thicken or grow rigid and also as to faint or swoon; MW spells it with one *c*, *mūrchati* (MW).
