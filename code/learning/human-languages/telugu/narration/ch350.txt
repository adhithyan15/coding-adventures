Telugu, chapter 350: Crime and Punishment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నేరం (nēraṁ) — a crime; an offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a verdict, then the Telugu for bail.

You'll want to know: నేరం.
నేరం — nēraṁ — "a crime; an offence".
దొంగతనం పెద్ద నేరం. doṅgatanaṁ pedda nēraṁ. Theft is a serious crime.
Plural: నేరాలు, nērālu.
నేరం (nēraṁ) చేయు, to commit a crime.

The word, taken apart.
నేరం (nēraṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3775 lists Telugu nēramu, "fault, offence, crime", with Tamil nēram, "guilt, fault".

Lesson 2 of 5.
నేరస్థుడు (nērasthuḍu) — a criminal; a culprit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for bail, then the Telugu for a crime.

You'll want to know: నేరస్థుడు.
నేరస్థుడు — nērasthuḍu — "a criminal; a culprit".
పోలీసులు నేరస్థుడిని పట్టుకున్నారు. pōlīsulu nērasthuḍini paṭṭukunnāru. The police caught the criminal.
Plural: నేరస్థులు, nērasthulu.
నేరం (nēraṁ) with the Sanskrit -స్థ, standing in: one who stands in a crime.

The word, taken apart.
నేరస్థుడు (nērasthuḍu) is a mixed compound: native nēram, "crime" (DEDR 3775), with the Sanskrit ending -stha, "standing in, being in" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
దోషి (dōṣi) — a guilty person; a culprit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a crime, then the Telugu for a criminal.

You'll want to know: దోషి.
దోషి — dōṣi — "a guilty person; a culprit".
న్యాయస్థానం అతన్ని దోషిగా తేల్చింది. nyāyasthānaṁ atanni dōṣigā tēlcindi. The court found him guilty.
దోషం is a fault: దోషి (dōṣi) is the one at fault.

The word, taken apart.
దోషి (dōṣi) is borrowed from Sanskrit doṣin, "faulty; guilty of an offence" (Monier-Williams), from doṣa, "a fault".

Lesson 4 of 5.
నిర్దోషి (nirdōṣi) — an innocent person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a criminal, then the Telugu for a guilty person.

You'll want to know: నిర్దోషి.
నిర్దోషి — nirdōṣi — "an innocent person".
చివరికి అతను నిర్దోషి అని తేలింది. civariki atanu nirdōṣi ani tēlindi. In the end he turned out to be innocent.
నిర్-, without, and దోషి (dōṣi): one without fault.

The word, taken apart.
నిర్దోషి (nirdōṣi) is built on Sanskrit nirdoṣa, "faultless, guiltless, innocent" (Monier-Williams), in the shape of దోషి (dōṣi).

Lesson 5 of 5.
శిక్షించు (śikṣiñcu) — to punish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a guilty person, then the Telugu for an innocent person.

You'll want to know: శిక్షించు.
శిక్షించు — śikṣiñcu — "to punish".
న్యాయమూర్తి నేరస్థుడిని శిక్షించాడు. nyāyamūrti nērasthuḍini śikṣiñcāḍu. The judge punished the criminal.
Past (I …): శిక్షించాను, śikṣiñcānu.
Who is punished takes -ను; a teacher or a parent can శిక్షించు (śikṣiñcu) too.

The word, taken apart.
శిక్షించు (śikṣiñcu) is built on Sanskrit śikṣā, "learning, study", which also came to mean "punishment" (CDIAL 12432), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.
