Russian, chapter 338: Arguments and evidence.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
аргумент (argumént) — an argument (a reason).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to declare, then the Russian for to be considered.

You'll want to know: аргумент.
аргумент (argumént) — "an argument (a reason)".
Это сильный аргумент. Éto sílnyy argumént. That is a strong argument.
Masculine. A reason in a debate, not a quarrel: a quarrel is спор or ссора.

The word, taken apart.
аргумент (argumént) is usually traced, through German and Polish, to Latin argūmentum "proof, evidence" (Lewis & Short), built on arguere "to make clear, to prove".

Lesson 2 of 5.
довод (dóvod) — a point made, a reason given.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be considered, then the Russian for an argument.

You'll want to know: довод.
довод (dóvod) — "a point made, a reason given".
Его доводы меня не убедили. Yegó dóvody menyá ne ubedíli. His points did not convince me.
Masculine. More bookish than аргумент (argumént): привести довод (dóvod) — to put forward a point.

The word, taken apart.
довод (dóvod) is built from до- "up to" and вод-, the root of водить "to lead", the iterative partner of вести, from Proto-Slavic voditi "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). A point made leads the listener up to a conclusion.

Lesson 3 of 5.
доказывать (dokázyvat) — to prove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an argument, then the Russian for a point made.

You'll want to know: доказывать.
доказывать (dokázyvat) — "to prove".
Он долго доказывал, что прав. On dólgo dokázyval, chto prav. He spent a long time proving that he was right.
Imperfective, with the perfective доказать (докажу, докажешь). Доказывать, что… — to prove that…

The word, taken apart.
доказывать (dokázyvat) is built from до- and казать "to show", the verb inside показывать and сказать. Казать is inherited from Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB): to prove is to show a thing through to the end.

Lesson 4 of 5.
доказательство (dokazátelstvo) — proof, evidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a point made, then the Russian for to prove.

You'll want to know: доказательство.
доказательство (dokazátelstvo) — "proof, evidence".
У полиции нет доказательств. U polítsii net dokazátelstv. The police have no evidence.
Neuter. The plural доказательства means pieces of evidence.

The word, taken apart.
доказательство (dokazátelstvo) is built on доказать "to prove" with the abstract suffix -тельство; the root is казать "to show", from Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
убедительный (ubedítelnyy) — convincing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to prove, then the Russian for proof.

You'll want to know: убедительный.
убедительный (ubedítelnyy) — "convincing".
Его ответ был не очень убедительным. Yegó otvét byl ne óchen ubedítelnym. His answer was not very convincing.
From убедить, to convince. Убедительная просьба — an urgent request, a fixed formula on notices.

The word, taken apart.
убедительный (ubedítelnyy) is built on убедить "to convince". The root is that of беда "trouble", inherited from Proto-Slavic běda, which EtymDB sets beside Greek peíthō "I persuade" (EtymDB). To convince someone was first to press them hard.
