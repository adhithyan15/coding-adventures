Italian, chapter 439: Orientarsi.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
orientarsi — to find one's way, to get one's bearings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to put right, then the Italian for to compensate.

You'll want to know: orientarsi.
orientarsi — "to find one's way, to get one's bearings".
In questa città è facile orientarsi. In this city it is easy to find your way.
Built on oriente, the east: once you knew where the sun rose, you knew the rest.

The word, taken apart.
orientarsi is built on oriente 'east', which is inherited from Latin oriēns 'rising (sun)' (EtymDB), from orīrī 'to rise'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
smarrirsi — to get lost, to go astray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to compensate, then the Italian for to find one's way.

You'll want to know: smarrirsi.
smarrirsi — "to get lost, to go astray".
Senza la mappa ci siamo smarriti. Without the map we got lost.
A little more literary than perdersi; oggetti smarriti — lost property.

The word, taken apart.
smarrirsi was borrowed from Old French esmarrir 'to trouble, to dismay' (EtymDB), which came from a Germanic word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la cartina — the map (of a town or an area).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to find one's way, then the Italian for to get lost.

You'll want to know: la cartina.
la cartina — "the map (of a town or an area)".
All'ufficio turistico danno una cartina gratis. At the tourist office they give you a free map.
A little carta; la cartina stradale — road map.

The word, taken apart.
cartina is a little carta 'paper, map', which is inherited from Latin charta, from Greek khártēs 'papyrus' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la piantina — the street plan; the floor plan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: la piantina.
la piantina — "the street plan; the floor plan".
Sulla piantina il museo è vicino al fiume. On the plan the museum is near the river.
Also a little plant: una piantina di basilico.

The word, taken apart.
piantina is a little pianta 'plan, plant', which is inherited from Latin planta 'sole of the foot' (EtymDB): a plan is the footprint of a place.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la bussola — the compass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the map, then the Italian for the street plan.

You'll want to know: la bussola.
la bussola — "the compass".
In montagna porto la bussola. In the mountains I carry a compass.
Perdere la bussola — to lose your head.

The word, taken apart.
bussola is inherited from Late Latin buxula 'little box' (EtymDB), from buxus 'boxwood': the needle sat in a small wooden box.
