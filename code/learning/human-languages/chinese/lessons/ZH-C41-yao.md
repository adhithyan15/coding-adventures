## You'll want to know: yào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yào* — "to want; to ask for".

> *nǐ yào duōshao* — How many would you like?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yào* is inherited from Middle Chinese *'jiewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to bind by agreement'. The sense 'to want' is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
