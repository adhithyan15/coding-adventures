Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 249: Holi and Diwali.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
होळी (hoḷī) — the spring festival of colours, Holi.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to dress up, then the Marwadi for a bridegroom's garland.

You'll want to know: होळी.
होळी — hoḷī — "the spring festival of colours, Holi".
होळी बसंत मांय आवै है। hoḷī basant mā̃y āvai hai. Holi comes in spring.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
होळी (hoḷī) is inherited from Sanskrit holā, "the spring festival". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi holī and Gujarati hoḷī, with this book's ळ (CDIAL 14182).

Lesson 2 of 5.
गुलाल (gulāl) — coloured powder for Holi.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a bridegroom's garland, then the Marwadi for Holi.

You'll want to know: गुलाल.
गुलाल — gulāl — "coloured powder for Holi".
गुलाल लाल अर गुलाबी है। gulāl lāl ar gulābī hai. The Holi powder is red and pink.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गुलाल (gulāl) is usually traced to Persian gulāl, a red powder; no dictionary we can open gives an older Indian form.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सुगन (sugan) — a good omen; an auspicious sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for Holi, then the Marwadi for coloured powder for Holi.

You'll want to know: सुगन.
सुगन — sugan — "a good omen; an auspicious sign".
आज घणो आछो सुगन है। āj ghaṇo āchho sugan hai. Today there is a very good omen.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सुगन (sugan) is probably a later borrowing of Sanskrit śakuna, "a bird", and so "an omen" read from birds. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi saun, "good omen", a more worn-down shape of the same word (CDIAL 12245).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for coloured powder for Holi, then the Marwadi for a good omen.

You'll want to know: दिवाळी.
दिवाळी — divāḷī — "the festival of lamps, Diwali".
दिवाळी घणी चोखी लागै है। divāḷī ghaṇī chokhī lāgai hai. Diwali feels wonderful.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दिवाळी (divāḷī) is inherited from Sanskrit dīpāvali, "a row of lamps". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati divāḷī (CDIAL 6358).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पूजा (pūjā) — worship; a prayer ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a good omen, then the Marwadi for Diwali.

You'll want to know: पूजा.
पूजा — pūjā — "worship; a prayer ceremony".
मां रोज पूजा करै है। mā̃ roj pūjā karai hai. Mother does the worship every day.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पूजा (pūjā) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit word pūjā, "honour, worship", taken over unchanged (Monier-Williams).
