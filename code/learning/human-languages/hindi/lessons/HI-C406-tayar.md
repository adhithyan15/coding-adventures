## You'll want to know: टायर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C406-TAIKSI406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टायर** — *ṭāyar* — "a tyre".

> गाड़ी का एक टायर पुराना था।
>
> *gāṛī kā ek ṭāyar purānā thā.*
>
> One of the car's tyres was old.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टायर** (*ṭāyar*) is borrowed from English *tyre*, the rubber round a wheel.
