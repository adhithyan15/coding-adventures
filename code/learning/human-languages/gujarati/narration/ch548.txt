Gujarati, chapter 548: If We Win the Match.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ટીમ (ṭīm) — a team.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for rather, then the Gujarati for adverse.

You'll want to know: ટીમ.
ટીમ — ṭīm — "a team".
જો અમારી ટીમ આજે જીતશે તો અમે મીઠાઈ વહેંચીશું. jo amārī ṭīm āje jītśe to ame mīṭhāī vahẽcīśũ. If our team wins today, we'll hand out sweets.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટીમ (ṭīm) is borrowed from English team.

Lesson 2 of 5.
કપ્તાન (kaptān) — a captain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for adverse, then the Gujarati for a team.

You'll want to know: કપ્તાન.
કપ્તાન — kaptān — "a captain".
જો કપ્તાન સારું રમશે તો ટીમ જીતશે. jo kaptān sārũ ramśe to ṭīm jītśe. If the captain plays well, the team will win.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કપ્તાન (kaptān) is usually traced to Portuguese capitão, "a captain", the word of the ships that reached the Indian coast.

જો કાલે વરસાદ પડશે તો મુકાબલો રવિવારે થશે. jo kāle varsād paḍśe to mukāblo ravivāre thaśe. If it rains tomorrow, the contest will be held on Sunday.

Lesson 4 of 5.
વિજેતા (vijetā) — a winner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a captain, then the Gujarati for a match-up.

You'll want to know: વિજેતા.
વિજેતા — vijetā — "a winner".
જો તું છેલ્લી બાજી જીતીશ તો તું વિજેતા બનીશ. jo tũ chellī bājī jītīś to tũ vijetā banīś. If you win the last round, you'll be the winner.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
વિજેતા (vijetā) is a tatsama, from Sanskrit vijetṛ, "a conqueror, a victor" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
જિતાડવું (jitāḍvũ) — to win it for (a team).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a match-up, then the Gujarati for a winner.

You'll want to know: જિતાડવું.
જિતાડવું — jitāḍvũ — "to win it for (a team)".
જો આપણો કપ્તાન સારું રમત તો તે ટીમને જિતાડત. jo āpṇo kaptān sārũ ramat to te ṭīmne jitāḍat. If our captain had played well, he would have won it for the team.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ટીમને જિતાડી, teṇe ṭīmne jitāḍī.

The word, taken apart.
જિતાડવું (jitāḍvũ) is built from જીતવું, "to win", with the causative -āḍ- and a shortened vowel: to make someone win. CDIAL files જીતવું under Sanskrit jita, "won" (CDIAL 5224).
