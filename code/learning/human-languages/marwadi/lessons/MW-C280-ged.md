## You'll want to know: गेंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C280-MAIDAN280-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गेंद** — *gẽd* — "a ball".

> म्हारी गेंद लाल है।
>
> *mhārī gẽd lāl hai.*
>
> My ball is red.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गेंद** (*gẽd*) is inherited from Sanskrit *genduka*, "a ball for play"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *gẽd*, "ball" (CDIAL 4248).
