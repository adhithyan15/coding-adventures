# ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ (svāgatakāra) — a receptionist

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C460-VASATI460-02, KA-LEX-C460-VASATI460-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lodge, then the Kannada for a chamber.

## You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C460-VASATI460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ** — *svāgatakāra* — "a receptionist".

> ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ ನಮಗೆ ಕೊಠಡಿಯ ಕೀಲಿ ಕೊಟ್ಟನು.
>
> *svāgatakāra namage koṭhaḍiya kīli koṭṭanu.*
>
> The receptionist gave us the key to the room.

ಸ್ವಾಗತ, welcome, plus -ಕಾರ, one who does it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಾಗತಕಾರ** (*svāgatakāra*) is built from Sanskrit *svāgata*, "welcome" (CDIAL 13920), and *-kāra*, "one who does": the one who welcomes.
