## You'll want to know: la réception
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C426-RECEPTION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la réception** — "the reception desk; the reception (party)".

> Nous laissons la clé à la réception.
>
> *We leave the key at reception.*

*Une réception* is also a formal party.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réception* is a learned borrowing from Latin *receptiō* 'a receiving', from *recipere* 'to receive', built on *capere* 'to take' (EtymDB).
