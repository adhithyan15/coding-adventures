## You'll want to know: اثر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C292-TUFAN292-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اثر** — *asar* — "an effect".

> طوفان کا اثر دو دن رہا۔
>
> *tūfān kā asar do din rahā.*
>
> The storm's effects lasted two days.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اثر** (*asar*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *athar*, "a trace, a mark left behind". Urdu says its middle letter as *s*.
