5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দর (dôr) — a rate, a price.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a battery, then the Bengali for a switch.

You'll want to know: দর.
দর — dôr — "a rate, a price".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of দর (dôr) is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it in this sense, and a search by the glosses "rate" and "price" finds only words of other shapes, such as Bengali dām (CDIAL 6622).

Lesson 4 of 5.
আবেদন (ābedôn) — an application.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a discount, then the Bengali for a form.

You'll want to know: আবেদন.
আবেদন — ābedôn — "an application".

The word, taken apart.
আবেদন (ābedôn) is Sanskrit āvedana, "announcing, a petition", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পরিচয়পত্র (pôrichôypôtrô) — an identity card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: পরিচয়পত্র.
পরিচয়পত্র — pôrichôypôtrô — "an identity card".

The word, taken apart.
পরিচয়পত্র (pôrichôypôtrô) is built from Sanskrit paricaya, "acquaintance", and pattra, "leaf, letter", both taken over whole: "acquaintance letter".
