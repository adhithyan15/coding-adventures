Urdu, chapter 505: Up in the Loft.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ردی (raddī) — waste paper; scrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a peg (on a wall), then the Urdu for a socket (for a plug).

You'll want to know: ردی.
ردی — raddī — "waste paper; scrap".
ہم پرانے اخبار ردی میں بیچتے ہیں۔ ham purāne akhbār raddī meṅ bechte haiṅ. We sell our old newspapers as waste paper.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ردی (raddī) is usually traced to Persian raddī, "rejected, worthless".

پھاڑنا — phāṛnā — "to tear (something)".
بچے نے پرانا اخبار پھاڑا۔ bachche ne purānā akhbār phāṛā. The child tore up the old newspaper.

Lesson 5 of 5.
رکھوانا (rakhvānā) — to have (something) put away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a loft, then the Urdu for to tear (something).

You'll want to know: رکھوانا.
رکھوانا — rakhvānā — "to have (something) put away".
امی نے ردی کے اخبار باہر رکھوائے۔ ammī ne raddī ke akhbār bāhar rakhvāe. Mum had the old newspapers put outside.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سامان گاڑی میں رکھوایا, us ne sāmān gāṛī meṅ rakhvāyā.

The word, taken apart.
رکھوانا (rakhvānā) is the causative of rakhnā, "to put, to keep", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit rakṣati, "guards" (CDIAL 10547): to have someone put a thing away.
