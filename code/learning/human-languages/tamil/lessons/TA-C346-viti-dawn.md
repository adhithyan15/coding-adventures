## You'll want to know: விடி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C346-MUDIVU346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**விடி** — *viṭi* — "to grow light; to dawn".

> கதை முடிந்தபோது பொழுது விடிந்தது.
>
> *katai muṭintapōtu poḻutu viṭintatu.*
>
> By the time the story ended, day had dawned.

Past (it …): விடிந்தது, *viṭintatu*.

Of the day: பொழுது விடிந்தது, it grew light.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**விடி** (*viṭi*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 5475): to dawn, to break, as the day.
