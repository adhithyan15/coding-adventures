## You'll want to know: कूंपळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C239-CROP239-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कूंपळ** — *kū̃paḷ* — "a new shoot; a bud".

> रूंख माथै नुवी कूंपळ है।
>
> *rū̃kh māthai nuvī kū̃paḷ hai.*
>
> There's a new shoot on the tree.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूंपळ** (*kū̃paḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kuḍmala*, "a bud"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *kūpaḷa*, "a fresh bud or shoot" (CDIAL 3250).
