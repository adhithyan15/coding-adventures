## You'll want to know: كمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C544-ALAT544-02]; assesses=[] -->

**كمان** — *kamān* — "a violin".

> تتعلم أختي الكمان في المدرسة.
>
> *tataʿallamu ukhtī al-kamān fī al-madrasa.*
>
> My sister is learning the violin at school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كمان** (*kamān*) was borrowed into Arabic and is usually traced to Persian *kamān*, "a bow": the violin is the instrument played with a bow.
