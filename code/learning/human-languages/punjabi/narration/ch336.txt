Punjabi, chapter 336: Work in the Bazaar.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਕਾਰੀਗਰ (kārīgar) — a craftsman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to hammer into shape, then the Punjabi for a mechanic.

You'll want to know: ਕਾਰੀਗਰ.
ਕਾਰੀਗਰ — kārīgar — "a craftsman".
ਇਹ ਕਾਰੀਗਰ ਲੱਕੜ ਦਾ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ। eh kārīgar lakkaṛ dā kamm kardā hai. This craftsman does woodwork.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਾਰੀਗਰ (kārīgar) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kārīgar, "a craftsman".
