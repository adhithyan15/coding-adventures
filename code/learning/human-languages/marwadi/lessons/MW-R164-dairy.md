# A notice at the dairy — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *the shop is open*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-02, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-03, MW-SKILL-C164-READ-NOTICE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-ISNOT-01, MW-LEX-C155-FOOD155-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *the house is open*; *the shop is open*; *the shop is closed*]
- [YOU SAY: *from six until ten*; *in the evening, from five until eight*]
- [YOU SAY: *on Monday the shop is closed*; *there is no ghee today*]
- [YOU ANSWER: in the notice, when can you buy milk on a Sunday morning? (From six until twelve.)]
