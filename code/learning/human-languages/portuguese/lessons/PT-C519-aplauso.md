## You'll want to know: o aplauso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C519-ENSAIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o aplauso** — "applause".

> No fim, os músicos receberam um grande aplauso.
>
> *At the end, the musicians got a big round of applause.*

Often plural: *os aplausos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aplauso* comes from Latin *applausus* "applause" (EtymDB), from *applaudere*, built on *plaudere* "to clap".
