## You'll want to know: ساز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C519-DHOL519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ساز** — *sāz* — "a musical instrument".

> میرا بھائی تین ساز بجاتا ہے۔
>
> *merā bhāī tīn sāz bajātā hai.*
>
> My brother plays three instruments.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ساز** (*sāz*) is usually traced to Persian *sāz*, "equipment; a musical instrument".
