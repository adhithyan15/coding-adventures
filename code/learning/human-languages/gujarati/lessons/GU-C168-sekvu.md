## You'll want to know: શેકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C168-ROTLI168-05]; assesses=[] -->

**શેકવું** — *śekvũ* — "to roast, to toast".

> **તે ધીમે રોટલી શેકે છે.** — *te dhīme roṭlī śeke che* — "She roasts the rotli slowly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શેકવું** (*śekvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*sēkk*, "heat, foment, roast", a reconstructed form; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *sekvũ*, "to foment, bake" (CDIAL 13581).
