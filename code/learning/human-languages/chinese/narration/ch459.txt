Lesson 1 of 6.
gūdān — lonely; on one's own.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for relaxed and at ease, then the Mandarin for fed up.

You'll want to know: gūdān.
gūdān — "lonely; on one's own".
wǒ yí ge rén zhù le liǎng nián yǒudiǎnr gūdān — I've lived on my own for two years, and I'm a bit lonely.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gūdān is built from gū 'an orphan' and dān 'single', both inherited from Middle Chinese, ku and tan (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a fatherless child' and the second as single against double.

Lesson 2 of 6.
事情 (shìqing) — matter; affair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for fed up, then the Mandarin for lonely.

You'll want to know: 事情.
事情 — shìqing — "matter; affair".
今天我有很多事情要做 jīntiān wǒ yǒu hěn duō shìqing yào zuò — I've got a lot of things to do today.
The new character, in pieces: 情 (qíng) — eleven strokes in three pieces: on the left the heart piece, a dot, a second dot and a long vertical; at the top right a bar, a second bar, a vertical through them and a long bar; and under them 月 yuè, four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
事情 (shìqing) is built from 事 'an affair' and 情 (qíng) 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzriH and dzjeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to employ' and 'to set up', and explains the second, after an older book, as what a person desires; in this word the second is said light, and why it joins the first is not settled.

Script — the heart piece, then a top over 月.
情 (qíng).
Three pieces: the left first, then the top right, then the bottom right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, three strokes: the heart piece. A short dot on the left, a short dot on the right falling to the right, then a long vertical down between them from top to foot.
The top right, four strokes. A bar from left to right, a second bar under it, a vertical down through both, then a long bar under them, wider than the two above.
The bottom right, 月, four strokes. The stroke down the left that curves to the left at the foot, the stroke that runs right, turns down and hooks, then the two short bars inside.
3 + 4 + 4 = eleven.

[pause 3 seconds]

感情 — gǎnqíng — "affection between people; feelings for each other".
wǒmen rènshi le shí nián gǎnqíng hěn hǎo — We've known each other for ten years, and we're very close.

The word, taken apart.
感情 (gǎnqíng) is built from 感 'to move' and 情 (qíng) 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, komX and dzjeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move'.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a matter to deal with, then the Mandarin for affection between people.

You'll want to know: 心里话.
心里话 — xīnlihuà — "what is really on one's mind; words from the heart".
zhè shì wǒ de xīnlihuà bàituō nǐ bié gàosu biérén — This is what I really think, so please don't tell anyone.

The word, taken apart.
心里话 (xīnlihuà) is built from 心 'heart', 里 'inside' and 话 'words', all inherited from Middle Chinese, sim, liX and hwaejH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'inside' and the third 'speech'. The middle syllable is said light.
