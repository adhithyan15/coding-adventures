- Script closure found eleven Kannada violations in chapters 1-9; there are
  now none.
- `KA-C01-practice` keeps ಇಲ್ಲ and ಸರಿ in romanization until their letters
  arrive, and its examples keep to letters already taught.

  *nimmannu bhēṭiyāgi santōṣa* (`KA-C02-santosha`), *nānu cennāgiddēne*
  (`KA-C03-cennaagi`), *bā* (`KA-C04-hoogu`), *-ōṇa*
  (`KA-C04-naale-sigona`), *nānu Beṅgaḷūrinalli iddēne* and *-alli*
  (`KA-C05-iru`), *aḍuge māḍu* (`KA-C05-kelasa-maadu`), *ombattu*
  (`KA-C08-dayavittu`) and *prārambhisu* (`KA-C09-kshamisi`).
