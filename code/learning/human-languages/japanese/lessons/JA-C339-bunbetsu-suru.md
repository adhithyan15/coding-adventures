## You'll want to know: ぶんべつする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C339-BUNBETSU339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶんべつする** — *bunbetsu suru* — "to sort rubbish; to separate waste".

> **きそくが あるので、ごみを ぶんべつします。** — *kisoku ga aru node, gomi o bunbetsu shimasu* — "There is a rule, so we sort our rubbish."

Polite **ぶんべつします**, *bunbetsushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ははが ごみを ぶんべつしました。**, *haha ga gomi o bunbetsu shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶんべつする** (*bunbetsu suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *bunbetsu*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *bun*, "part" (Middle Chinese *pjun*) and *betsu*, "separate" (*bjet*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
