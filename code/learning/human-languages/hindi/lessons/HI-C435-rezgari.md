## You'll want to know: रेज़गारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C435-REZGARI435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रेज़गारी** — *rezgārī* — "loose coins, change".

> बस के लिए रेज़गारी रखिए।
>
> *bas ke liye rezgārī rakhiye.*
>
> Keep some loose change for the bus.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेज़गारी** (*rezgārī*) is usually traced to Persian *reza*, "a small piece, a fragment": money broken into small pieces.
