Lesson 1 of 5.
la escultura — the sculpture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the cover, then the Spanish for to leaf through.

You'll want to know: la escultura.
la escultura — "the sculpture".
En el museo hay una escultura enorme. There is a huge sculpture in the museum.
El escultor — the sculptor.

The word, taken apart.
escultura comes from Latin sculptūra "sculpture" (EtymDB), from sculpere "to carve in stone".

Lesson 5 of 5.
fotografiar — to photograph.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the canvas, then the Spanish for the painter.

You'll want to know: fotografiar.
fotografiar — "to photograph".
Está prohibido fotografiar los cuadros. Photographing the paintings is not allowed.
Everyday speech prefers hacer fotos (Spain) or tomar fotos (Latin America). Forms: fotografío.

The word, taken apart.
fotografiar is built on fotografía, made from Greek phōs, phōtós "light" and gráphein "to write": writing with light.
