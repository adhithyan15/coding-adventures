## You'll want to know: ਪੇਸ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C364-JOB364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੇਸ਼ਾ** — *peshā* — "a profession; a trade".

> ਪੜ੍ਹਾਉਣਾ ਮੇਰਾ ਪੇਸ਼ਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *paṛhāuṇā merā peshā hai.*
>
> Teaching is my profession.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੇਸ਼ਾ** (*peshā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *pēsha*, "craft".
