## You'll want to know: こうちゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C179-DRINK179-01]; assesses=[] -->

**こうちゃ** — *kōcha* — "black tea".

> **あさは こうちゃを のみます。** — *asa wa kōcha o nomimasu* — "In the morning I drink black tea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうちゃ** (*kōcha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "crimson" (Middle Chinese *huwng*) and *cha*, "tea" (*drae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
