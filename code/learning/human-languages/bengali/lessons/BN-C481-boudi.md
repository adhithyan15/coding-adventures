# বউদি (boudi) — an elder brother's wife; a sister-in-law

## You'll want to know: বউদি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C481-MA481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বউদি** — *boudi* — "an elder brother's wife; a sister-in-law".

> বউদি আমাদের জন্য পায়েস রান্না করলেন।
>
> *boudi āmāder jônyô pāyes rānnā kôrlen.*
>
> My sister-in-law made rice pudding for us.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বউদি** (*boudi*) puts *bou*, "wife, bride", from Sanskrit *vadhū* (CDIAL 11250), with দিদি, elder sister: the elder brother's wife, called by a sister's name.
