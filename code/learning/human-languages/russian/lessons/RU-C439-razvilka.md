## You'll want to know: развилка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C439-RAZVILKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**развилка** (*razvílka*) — "a fork (in a road)".

> На развилке мы повернули налево.
>
> *Na razvílke my povernúli nalévo.*
>
> At the fork we turned left.

Feminine. Built on *вилка* — a fork.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*развилка* is built from *раз-* 'apart' and *вилка* 'a fork', from *вилы* 'a pitchfork'. EtymDB lists *вилка* without a deeper source.
