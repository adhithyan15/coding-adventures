## You'll want to know: jiěmèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C508-SIBLINGS508-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiěmèi* — "sisters".

> *wǒmen jiěmèi sān ge wǒ zuì xiǎo* — There are three of us sisters, and I'm the youngest.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiěmèi* is built from *jiě* 'an elder sister' and *mèi* 'a younger sister', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsjaeX* and *mwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a word for mother among a people of the west and the second 'a younger sister'; the sense 'elder sister' is not given for the first there.
