## You'll want to know: el retrato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C763-ARTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el retrato** — "the portrait".

> Este retrato es de mi abuela.
>
> *This portrait is of my grandmother.*

*Un retrato robot* — an identikit picture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*retrato* was borrowed from Italian *ritratto* "portrait" (EtymDB), from Latin *retractus*, built on *trahere* "to draw, to pull".
