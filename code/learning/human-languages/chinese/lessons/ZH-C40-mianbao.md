## You'll want to know: miànbāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C40-FRUIT40-04]; assesses=[] -->

*miànbāo* — "bread".

> *wǒ chī le yí ge miànbāo* — I had a bread roll.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miànbāo* is built from miàn 'flour' and bāo 'to wrap', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *menH* and *paew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
