## You'll want to know: ٹرالی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C401-ISTIQBAL401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹرالی** — *ṭrālī* — "a luggage trolley".

> میں نے اپنا سامان ٹرالی پر رکھا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne apnā sāmān ṭrālī par rakhā.*
>
> I put my luggage on a trolley.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹرالی** (*ṭrālī*) is borrowed from English *trolley*.
