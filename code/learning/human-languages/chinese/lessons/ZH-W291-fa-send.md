## Script — a bent stroke, a sweep, a fork and a dot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-FA-SEND-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 发

**One shape, three small groups.** Count the groups, not the strokes.

**The frame, two strokes.** A short stroke that drops and turns to run across to the right, rising a little. Then a long sweep from the top middle, down to the lower left, crossing it.

**The fork, two strokes.** A bar that turns into a sweep down to the lower left. Then a long stroke falling to the right from under the bar, out to the foot.

**The dot, one stroke**, high on the right.

2 + 2 + 1 = five.
