## You'll want to know: die Auffahrt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C398-LANDSTRASSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Auffahrt** — "the slip road (onto a motorway); the driveway".

> An der Auffahrt zur Autobahn steht ein langer Stau.
>
> *There is a long traffic jam at the slip road onto the motorway.*

Plural: *die Auffahrten*. Also the drive up to a big house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Auffahrt* is built from *auf* 'up, onto' and *Fahrt* 'journey, drive', from the inherited verb *fahren* 'to travel' (EtymDB). EtymDB lists the word but gives no older form.
