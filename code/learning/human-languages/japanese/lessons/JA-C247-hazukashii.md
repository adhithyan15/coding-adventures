## You'll want to know: はずかしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C247-PRAISE247-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はずかしい** — *hazukashii* — "embarrassed; shy".

> **いま はずかしいですか。** — *ima hazukashii desu ka* — "Are you embarrassed now?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はずかしい** (*hazukashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
