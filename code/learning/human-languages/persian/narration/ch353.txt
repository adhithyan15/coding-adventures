Persian, chapter 353: Eras and ancestors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دوران (dowrân) — an era, a period.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an empire, then the Persian for a dynasty.

You'll want to know: دوران.
دوران (dowrân) — "an era, a period".
در دوران جنگ زندگی سخت بود. Dar dowrân-e jang zendegi sakht bud. During the war years life was hard.
Dowrân-e kudaki "childhood".

The word, taken apart.
dowrân is usually traced to Arabic dawarān, "a turning, a cycle"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سده (sade) — a century.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a dynasty, then the Persian for an era.

You'll want to know: سده.
سده (sade) — "a century".
این مسجد در سده دوازدهم ساخته شد. In masjed dar sade-ye davâzdahom sâkhte shod. This mosque was built in the twelfth century.
The native word for a century; qarn is the Arabic one. Also the name of a winter fire festival.

The word, taken apart.
sade is a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; it is usually linked with sad "hundred".

Lesson 3 of 5.
مورخ (movarrekh) — a historian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an era, then the Persian for a century.

You'll want to know: مورخ.
مورخ (movarrekh) — "a historian".
مورخ (movarrekh) ها درباره این جنگ زیاد نوشته اند. Movarrekh-hâ darbâre-ye in jang ziyâd neveshte-and. Historians have written a lot about this war.
Also plural movarrekhân. The same Arabic root gives târikh "history".

The word, taken apart.
movarrekh is usually traced to Arabic mu'arrikh, "historian", of the same root as ta'rīkh "history"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
نیاکان (niyâkân) — ancestors, forefathers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a century, then the Persian for a historian.

You'll want to know: نیاکان.
نیاکان (niyâkân) — "ancestors, forefathers".
نیاکان (niyâkân) ما در این روستا زندگی میکردند. Niyâkân-e mâ dar in rustâ zendegi mikardand. Our ancestors lived in this village.
The plural of the rare niyâ "grandfather". Literary.

The word, taken apart.
niyâkân is the plural of niyâ "grandfather, forebear", a native Persian word; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
میراث (mirâs) — heritage, a legacy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a historian, then the Persian for ancestors.

You'll want to know: میراث.
میراث (mirâs) — "heritage, a legacy".
این بنای قدیمی میراث همه مردم است. In banâ-ye qadimi mirâs-e hame-ye mardom ast. This old building is the heritage of all the people.
Written with ث. Mirâs-e farhangi "cultural heritage".

The word, taken apart.
mirâs is borrowed from Arabic mīrāth, "inheritance" (EtymDB).
