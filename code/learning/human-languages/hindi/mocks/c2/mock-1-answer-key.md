## Writing

The synthesis must accurately distinguish the epidemiology note's association,
controls, limits, and longitudinal recommendation from the coalition's access,
livelihood, maintenance, and decision-power conditions and the essay's warning
that both mapped greenery and inherited memory can conceal unequal use. It should
reframe evidence into usable meeting questions without presenting canopy as a
proven single cause, romanticising the old neighbourhood, or treating resident
knowledge as decoration. The editorial must develop and qualify the visible-error
claim across two settings, address accountability, continuing harm, decision
authority, institutional self-dramatisation, and review, and end with an
operational principle. Score fidelity, selection and reframing, genre, argument,
organisation, register, Hindi control, and orthography; no fixed wording or
position is required.

## Speaking

The presentation must distinguish archive evidence from its recommendation and
mediate consent scope, transcription disparities, compound identification risk,
discoverability, correction, research value, representative distortion, and the
limits of removal. A viable policy names first-year tiers, paid community power,
review and appeal, restricted-material representation, and promises the archive
can actually keep. Negotiation must address safe first-year scope, worker decision
power, accessibility, heritage machinery, the sponsor, and explicit stop/review
conditions while preserving unresolved conflicts where necessary. Defence must
respond to all selected challenges, identify evidence capable of changing a
recommendation, and reformulate faithfully for each second audience. Score only
the learner's own contribution; no fixed policy is required.
