5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आकाशवाणी (ākāśvāṇī) — radio; the radio service.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an editorial, then the Marathi for to publish.

You'll want to know: आकाशवाणी.
आकाशवाणी — ākāśvāṇī — "radio; the radio service".
आजी रोज सकाळी आकाशवाणीवर गाणी ऐकते. ājī roj sakāḷī ākāśvāṇīvar gāṇī aikte. Grandma listens to songs on the radio every morning.
Feminine.
आकाशवाणीवर, on the radio. रेडिओ is also said.

The word, taken apart.
आकाशवाणी (ākāśvāṇī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit ākāśavāṇī, 'a voice from the air or from heaven' (Monier-Williams), a fitting name for the radio.

Lesson 2 of 5.
दूरदर्शन (dūrdarśan) — television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to publish, then the Marathi for radio.

You'll want to know: दूरदर्शन.
दूरदर्शन — dūrdarśan — "television".
रात्री आम्ही दूरदर्शनवर बातम्या पाहतो. rātrī āmhī dūrdarśanvar bātamyā pāhto. At night we watch the news on television.
Neuter.
Also the name of the national channel; in speech टीव्ही.

The word, taken apart.
दूरदर्शन (dūrdarśan) is a modern coinage built from Sanskrit dūra, 'far', and darśana, 'seeing' (Monier-Williams): seeing far.

Lesson 3 of 5.
वाहिनी (vāhinī) — a channel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for radio, then the Marathi for television.

You'll want to know: वाहिनी.
वाहिनी — vāhinī — "a channel".
ही वाहिनी दिवसभर फक्त बातम्या दाखवते. hī vāhinī divasbhar phakta bātamyā dākhavte. This channel shows only news all day.
Feminine.
वृत्तवाहिनी, a news channel.

The word, taken apart.
वाहिनी (vāhinī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vāhinī, 'an army, a host' (Monier-Williams), a word built on vah, 'to carry'; the sense 'a channel' is modern.

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रसारित करणे (prasārit karṇe) — to broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a channel, then the Marathi for a newsreader.

You'll want to know: प्रसारित करणे.
प्रसारित करणे — prasārit karṇe — "to broadcast".
वाहिनीने ती मुलाखत संध्याकाळी प्रसारित केली. vāhinīne tī mulākhat sandhyākāḷī prasārit kelī. The channel broadcast that interview in the evening.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने प्रसारित केलं, tyāne prasārit kelaṁ.
What is broadcast agrees: मुलाखत प्रसारित केली, कार्यक्रम प्रसारित केला.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसारित करणे (prasārit karṇe) joins prasārit, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit prasārita, 'spread out, stretched forth' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.
