## You'll want to know: ਸ਼ੱਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C341-DOUBT341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ੱਕ** — *shakk* — "doubt; suspicion".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਗੱਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ੱਕ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ tuhāḍī gall utte koī shakk nahīṁ hai.*
>
> I have no doubt about what you say.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ੱਕ** (*shakk*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *shakk*, "doubt".
