## Script — 人 twice, the left one narrower
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CONG-FROM-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 从

**One piece you own, written twice.**

**The left 人, two strokes**, narrow: the left-falling stroke, then a short stroke falling to the right that stops early, almost a dot, so the second 人 has room.

**The right 人, two strokes**, full size: the left-falling stroke, then the long stroke falling to the right.

2 + 2 = four. One person following another.
