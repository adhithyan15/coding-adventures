## You'll want to know: el colectivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C600-SOCIEDAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el colectivo** — "the group; collective".

> Es un colectivo de artistas.
>
> *It is a collective of artists.*

Also a group of people with something in common: *el colectivo de jóvenes*. In Argentina *el colectivo* is also the city bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*colectivo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *collectivus* 'gathered together', built on *colligere*, from *legere* 'to gather' (Lewis & Short).
