# ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ (vātsalya) — tender affection

## You'll want to know: ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C495-MAMATE495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ** — *vātsalya* — "tender affection".

> ಅಜ್ಜನ ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ ನನಗೆ ಇನ್ನೂ ನೆನಪಿದೆ.
>
> *ajjana vātsalya nanage innū nenapide.*
>
> I still remember grandfather's tender love.

The love of an elder for a little one; Sanskrit *vatsa* is a calf.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ** (*vātsalya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vātsalya*, "affection" (CDIAL 11506), built on *vatsala*, "fond", from *vatsa*, "a calf".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
