# Marwadi roadmap — pre-A1 to C2

The destination is a book-only learner who can pass a project-defined Marwadi
four-skill assessment equivalent at every rung through C2. A chapter merely
touching a level does not count. Each level needs complete coverage, timed task
practice, writing progression, full mocks, answer keys, rubrics, and a pass rule.

## Dependency order

1. **pre-A1 foundation** — greetings, responses, names, wellbeing, courtesy,
   leave-taking, time, location, and 1–5; Devanagari moves from trace to copy to
   delayed copy to independent writing inside the same gentle ramp.
2. **A1 survival range** — home, people, food, daily actions, simple questions,
   present/past/future control, short messages, forms, and dictated words.
3. **A2 connected life** — narration, reasons, travel, experience, comparison,
   paragraph-length reading and writing, and regional-listening introductions.
4. **B1 independence** — conditions, reported speech, argument, extended prose,
   correspondence, summaries, and timed four-skill tasks.
5. **B2 precision** — implicit meaning, register, regional variation, source
   synthesis, sustained interaction, and multi-paragraph writing.
6. **C1 flexibility** — fine shades, cultural weight, literary and formal prose,
   high-speed listening, genre control, and evidence-based extended writing.
7. **C2 command** — difficult regional and literary input, exact reformulation,
   nuanced spontaneous interaction, and timed full-length production.

## Current tranche

Chapters 1–9 contain 54 lessons for a greeting, formal thanks, polite yes,
water, a name exchange, a wellbeing exchange, and taking leave. Chapters 10–11
add 22 lessons and seven family labels: **मां, बाप, भाई, बहन, दादा, दादी,
परिवार**. Each word begins with sound and meaning, writing adds only **ब**, **द**,
and **व** one at a time, and the tranche closes with separately scored
listening, speaking, reading, and writing. The strict gentle-ramp report is
all-zero at 76 lessons: no duration, load, script closure, ordering,
forward-reference, or reinforcement-window findings.

This brings the first vocabulary expansion from 11 to 18 distinct taught
headwords. The active 35-headword pre-A1 tranche still needs 17 more; the next
independent slice should favor high-frequency survival nouns that reuse the
current script inventory. The contrasting no response before a complete yes/no
exchange, okay, casual gratitude, a you're-welcome response, and a full request
for water also remain explicit debt.
