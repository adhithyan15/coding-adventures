## You'll want to know: قدرت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C436-QUDRAT436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قدرت** — *qudrat* — "nature; the natural world".

> یہ وادی قدرت کا تحفہ ہے۔
>
> *yih vādī qudrat kā tohfā hai.*
>
> This valley is a gift of nature.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قدرت** (*qudrat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qudra*, "power, might"; in Urdu it is also the natural world.
