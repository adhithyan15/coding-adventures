Telugu, chapter 247: To pinch, To scratch, To whisper, To persuade, To plead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
గిల్లు (gillu) — to pinch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to decrease, then the Telugu for to refuse.

You'll want to know: గిల్లు.
గిల్లు — gillu — "to pinch".

The word, taken apart.
గిల్లు (gillu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kiḷḷu and Malayalam kiḷḷuka (DEDR 1589). DEDR lists Telugu gillu, "to pinch".

You'll want to know: గోకు.

The word, taken apart.
గోకు (gōku) is native Dravidian. DEDR 2257 lists a nasalised gōku, "to scratch with the nails", with Kannada gōṟu, "to scratch", and Tamil kōṟai, "a scratch".

Lesson 3 of 5.
గుసగుసలాడు (gusagusalāḍu) — to whisper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to pinch, then the Telugu for to scratch.

You'll want to know: గుసగుసలాడు.
గుసగుసలాడు — gusagusalāḍu — "to whisper".

The word, taken apart.
గుసగుసలాడు (gusagusalāḍu) is native Dravidian: gusagusa, "whisper", which DEDR 1638 lists with Tamil kucukucu, "to whisper", and āḍu, "to play, act" (DEDR 347). DEDR notes related whisper-words in Prakrit and Hindi.

Lesson 5 of 5.
బతిమాలు (batimālu) — to plead, to beg.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Telugu for to whisper, then the Telugu for to persuade.

You'll want to know: బతిమాలు.
బతిమాలు — batimālu — "to plead, to beg".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of బతిమాలు (batimālu) is uncertain. DEDR and CDIAL list no word of this shape meaning "to plead, beg".
