## You'll want to know: il falò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C322-FUOCHI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il falò** — "the bonfire".

> Sulla spiaggia abbiamo acceso un falò.
>
> *We lit a bonfire on the beach.*

Stress on the last syllable; plural unchanged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falò* comes from Greek *phanós* "a lantern, a torch" (EtymDB), probably through a Byzantine Greek form.
