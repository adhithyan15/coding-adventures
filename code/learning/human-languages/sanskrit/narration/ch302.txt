Sanskrit, chapter 302: The Water Rose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जलौघः (jalaughaḥ) — a flood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for flashes, then the Sanskrit for uproots.

You'll want to know: जलौघः.
जलौघः — jalaughaḥ — "a flood".
वर्षायाः अनन्तरं ग्रामे जलौघः आगच्छति। varṣāyāḥ anantaraṃ grāme jalaughaḥ āgacchati. After the rain a flood comes to the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जलौघः (jalaughaḥ) is built from jala, "water", and ogha, "a flood, a rapid flow of water"; MW gives jalaugha as a mass of water (MW).

You'll want to know: प्लावयति.
प्लावयति — plāvayati — "floods".
नदी क्षेत्राणि प्लावयति। nadī kṣetrāṇi plāvayati. The river floods the fields.
Past (it …): नदी क्षेत्राणि प्लावितवती, nadī kṣetrāṇi plāvitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
प्लावयति (plāvayati) comes from the root √plu, "to float, swim", which also gives plavate, "floats"; MW gives its causative plāvayati as to make float, wash and inundate (MW).

नद्याः प्रवाहः वेगेन वहति। nadyāḥ pravāhaḥ vegena vahati. The river's current flows fast.

प्रवाहः (pravāhaḥ) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √vah, "to carry, flow", as in pravahati, "flows"; MW gives pravāha as a stream or current (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
निमज्जति (nimajjati) — sinks; goes under.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for floods, then the Sanskrit for a current.

You'll want to know: निमज्जति.
निमज्जति — nimajjati — "sinks; goes under".
जले नौका शनैः निमज्जति। jale naukā śanaiḥ nimajjati. The boat slowly goes under in the water.
Past (it …): नौका जले निमग्नवती, naukā jale nimagnavatī.

The word, taken apart.
निमज्जति (nimajjati) is built from ni, "down", and the root √majj, "to sink, dive", as in majjati; MW gives ni-majj as to sink down, plunge into or disappear (MW).
