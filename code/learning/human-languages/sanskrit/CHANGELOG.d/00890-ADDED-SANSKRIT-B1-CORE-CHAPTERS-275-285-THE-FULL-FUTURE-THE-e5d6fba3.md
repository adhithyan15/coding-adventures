## Added — Sanskrit B1 core, chapters 275–285: the full future, the -tavat past and all five B1 spine nodes

- Added eleven core chapters, 49 lessons (sequence 16200–16680), and with
  them the track's first running Sanskrit sentences: the A2 vocabulary
  chapters carry no example sentences, so every core line is short (at most
  five written words in 275–277, seven after), carries at most one new form,
  and is followed by its romanization and its English.
- Chapter 275 bridges the future the A2 spine still omitted: every person and
  number on करिष्य- (करिष्यामि … करिष्यन्ति, the dual करिष्यतः), questions with
  किम् and न before the verb, and each verb's own future stem, -इष्य-
  (पठिष्यति, खादिष्यति, वदिष्यति, लेखिष्यति) or -स्य- (द्रक्ष्यति, दास्यति,
  स्थास्यति), each the form Monier-Williams or Lanman gives. The spine mirror
  0230 no longer omits VERB-FUTURE.
- Chapters 276–285 realize the five B1 nodes with one grammar lesson per B1
  concept under its exact concept tag: narrating (the -tavat past that follows
  the doer, सः गतवान् / सा गतवती / ते गतवन्तः, Pāṇini 1.1.26, with the A2
  imperfect kept and called correct; the present with स्म in a यदा … तदा
  scene, Pāṇini 3.2.118; -त्वा and -य after a prefix, Pāṇini 7.1.37;
  प्रथमम्, ततः, अनन्तरम्, अन्ते), giving reasons (मम मतेन, मन्ये यत् / … इति
  मन्ये, यतः, -कारणात्, हेतुः; the optative of advice on a thematic present,
  Pāṇini 3.3.161, with मया गन्तव्यम् shown once for recognition; अतः, तस्मात्),
  handling travel (स्थानकं कुत्र अस्ति?, कियत् दूरम्?, वामतः / दक्षिणतः / ऋजु
  with the polite -तु; tickets with the dual द्वे यानपत्रे and the future of
  leaving; मम स्यूतः नष्टः, धूमयानं गतम्), describing experience (the feeling
  agreeing with the speaker, अहं प्रसन्नः / प्रसन्ना अस्मि; भवितुम् इच्छामि; मम
  स्वप्नः अस्ति, यत् …; -अपेक्षया and Y-आत् + -तर, Pāṇini 2.3.42; सर्वेषु …
  श्रेष्ठः; आरभ्य … पर्यन्तम् and the accusative of time, Pāṇini 2.3.5) and
  expressing conditions (यदि … तर्हि … with the present or the future in both
  halves, Lanman s.v. yadi; -अधीन at the end of a compound, as Monier-Williams
  and Macdonell give it; the conditional for what would have been, अभविष्यत्,
  आगमिष्यम्, Pāṇini 3.3.139 and 6.4.71, with the optative यदि-clause once for
  recognition). Spine mirrors 0250–0290 no longer omit any concept. No
  passive, perfect or aorist anywhere in the core.
