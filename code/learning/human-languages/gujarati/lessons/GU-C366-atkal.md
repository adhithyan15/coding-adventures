## You'll want to know: અટકળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C366-SHARAT366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અટકળ** — *aṭkaḷ* — "a conjecture".

> જો મારી અટકળ સાચી હશે તો તે કાલે આવશે.
>
> *jo mārī aṭkaḷ sācī haśe to te kāle āvśe.*
>
> If my guess is right, he will come tomorrow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અટકળ** (*aṭkaḷ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "conjecture", under a reconstructed *aṭṭakkalā*, "a guess" (CDIAL 183).
