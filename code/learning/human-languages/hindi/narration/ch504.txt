Hindi, chapter 504: Growing Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
याददाश्त (yāddāśt) — memory (the power to remember).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a generation, then the Hindi for to nurture.

You'll want to know: याददाश्त.
याददाश्त — yāddāśt — "memory (the power to remember)".
बुढ़ापे में दादी की याददाश्त कमज़ोर हो गई है। buṛhāpe meṁ dādī kī yāddāśt kamzor ho gaī hai. In her old age my grandmother's memory has grown weak.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
याददाश्त (yāddāśt) is usually traced to Persian yāddāsht, "a memorandum, memory", built on yād, "remembrance", and a word for "holding": holding things in mind.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बिसरना (bisarnā) — to slip from memory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a walking stick, then the Hindi for a pension.

You'll want to know: बिसरना.
बिसरना — bisarnā — "to slip from memory".
दादी के मन से कई पुराने नाम बिसर गए हैं। dādī ke man se kaī purāne nām bisar gae haiṁ. Grandmother has forgotten many old names.
Past (it …): वह बात बिसर गई, vah bāt bisar gaī.

The word, taken apart.
बिसरना (bisarnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi bisarnā, "to forget", under Sanskrit vismarati, "forgets" (CDIAL 12021). It belongs to songs and older speech; the thing forgotten is the subject.

Lesson 5 of 5.
विरासत (virāsat) — an inheritance; a legacy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a pension, then the Hindi for to slip from memory.

You'll want to know: विरासत.
विरासत — virāsat — "an inheritance; a legacy".
दादा की किताबें मुझे विरासत में मिलीं। dādā kī kitābeṁ mujhe virāsat meṁ milīṃ. I inherited my grandfather's books.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
विरासत (virāsat) is usually traced to Arabic wirātha, "inheritance", which reached Hindi through Persian. virāsat meṁ milnā is to inherit.
