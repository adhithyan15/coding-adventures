## You'll want to know: चुणणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C210-CHOOSE210-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चुणणो** — *chuṇṇo* — "to pick out; to choose".

> हूं चोखी जूती चुणूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ chokhī jūtī chuṇū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I pick out a good pair of shoes.

Past (I …): म्हैं लाल टोपी चुणी, *mhaĩ lāl ṭopī chuṇī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुणणो** (*chuṇṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cinoti*, "heaps up, collects". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *cunnā*, "to pick up, gather" (CDIAL 4814); picking out the best is probably a later step from gathering.
