Persian, chapter 105: Leopard, Rabbit, Deer, Fox, Pig.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پلنگ (palang) — a leopard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for fog, then the Persian for dew.

You'll want to know: پلنگ.
پلنگ — palang — "a leopard".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: palang]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: palang, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say palang]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for fog, then the Persian for dew, then say palang again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does پلنگ (palang) mean? ("A leopard".) Say it once more.
