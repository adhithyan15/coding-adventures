Lesson 1 of 6.
چھاچھ (chhāchh) — buttermilk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to cover, then the Urdu for a skimmer.

You'll want to know: چھاچھ.
چھاچھ — chhāchh — "buttermilk".
گرمیوں میں چھاچھ اچھی لگتی ہے۔ garmiyoṅ meṅ chhāchh achchhī lagtī hai. Buttermilk is lovely in the summer.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
چھاچھ (chhāchh) is inherited from chācchī, "buttermilk", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5012).

Lesson 3 of 6.

You'll want to know: جمانا.
جمانا — jamānā — "to set (curd)".
امی رات کو دہی جماتی ہیں۔ ammī rāt ko dahī jamātī haiṅ. Mum sets the yoghurt at night.

The word, taken apart.
جمانا (jamānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi jamānā and jāman, "a piece of curd for curdling milk", under Sanskrit yamyatē, "is held" (CDIAL 10428).

Lesson 5 of 6.
چھلکانا (chhalkānā) — to spill (a liquid).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to set (curd), then the Urdu for an earthen cooking pot.

You'll want to know: چھلکانا.
چھلکانا — chhalkānā — "to spill (a liquid)".
بچے نے میز پر دودھ چھلکایا۔ bachche ne mez par dūdh chhalkāyā. The child spilt milk on the table.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پانی چھلکایا, us ne pānī chhalkāyā.

The word, taken apart.
چھلکانا (chhalkānā) is the causative of chhalaknā, "to be spilt", which CDIAL lists under chala, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5002).
