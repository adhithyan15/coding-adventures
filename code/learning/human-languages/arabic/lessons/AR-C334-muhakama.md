## You'll want to know: محاكمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C334-MUHAKAMA334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**محاكمة** — *muḥākama* — "a trial".

> بدأت محاكمة الرجل أمس.
>
> *badaʾat muḥākamat ar-rajul ams.*
>
> The man’s trial began yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محاكمة** (*muḥākama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-m* (Lane), "to judge". *Muḥākama* is the verbal noun of Form III, "to bring someone to judgment".
