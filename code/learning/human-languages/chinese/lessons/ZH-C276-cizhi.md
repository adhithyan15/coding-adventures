## You'll want to know: cízhí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C276-NEWJOB276-02]; assesses=[] -->

*cízhí* — "to resign; to quit a job".

> *tā shàng ge yuè cízhí le* — She quit her job last month.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cízhí* is built from *cí* and *zhí* 'a post', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zi* and *tsyik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'words' and 'a plea'. The sense 'to take leave' is not given there, so how it came is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
