# ഉത്കണ്ഠ (utkaṇṭha) — anxiety; nervous worry

## You'll want to know: ഉത്കണ്ഠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C519-SAMADHANAM519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉത്കണ്ഠ** — *utkaṇṭha* — "anxiety; nervous worry".

> പരീക്ഷയ്ക്ക് മുൻപ് അവൾക്ക് വലിയ ഉത്കണ്ഠ ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു.
>
> *parīkṣaykkŭ munpŭ avaḷkkŭ valiya utkaṇṭha uṇṭāyirunnu.*
>
> She was very anxious before the exam.

Formal and close to ആശങ്ക; the one who feels it takes -ക്ക്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉത്കണ്ഠ** (*utkaṇṭha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *utkaṇṭhā*, "longing for someone, missing them" (Monier-Williams). In Malayalam the anxious waiting in that longing became the main sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
