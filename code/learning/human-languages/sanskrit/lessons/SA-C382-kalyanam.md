## You'll want to know: कल्याणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C382-HITA382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्याणम्** — *kalyāṇam* — "welfare; good fortune".

> यदि सः सत्यं वदति, तर्हि तस्य कल्याणं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi saḥ satyaṃ vadati, tarhi tasya kalyāṇaṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If he tells the truth, good will come to him.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्याणम्** (*kalyāṇam*) MW gives *kalyāṇa* "happy, prosperous, fortunate" and the neuter "good fortune, happiness, prosperity"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
