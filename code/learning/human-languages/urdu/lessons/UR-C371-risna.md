## You'll want to know: رسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C371-BUHRAN371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رسنا** — *risnā* — "to seep; to leak (of a liquid)".

> اگر چھت سے پانی رسا تو کمرہ گیلا ہو گا۔
>
> *agar chhat se pānī risā to kamrā gīlā ho gā.*
>
> If water seeps through the roof, the room will get wet.

Past (it …): وہ پانی دیوار سے رسا, *voh pānī dīvār se risā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رسنا** (*risnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *risnā*, "to leak out, to drip", under *\*riśyate*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10745).
