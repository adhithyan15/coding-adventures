## You'll want to know: le tramway
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C415-METRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le tramway** — "the tram".

> Le tramway passe toutes les cinq minutes.
>
> *The tram comes every five minutes.*

In speech, *le tram*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tramway* was borrowed from English *tramway* (EtymDB). English *tram* 'rail' comes from Middle Dutch *trame* 'beam'.
