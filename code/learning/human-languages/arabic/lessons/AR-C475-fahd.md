## You'll want to know: فهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C475-DABU475-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فهد** — *fahd* — "a cheetah; a leopard".

> رأينا فهدا في المحمية.
>
> *raʾaynā fahdan fī al-maḥmiyya.*
>
> We saw a cheetah in the reserve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فهد** (*fahd*) is an old Arabic name; the origin is not settled.
