# સંતાન (santān) — offspring; a child

## You'll want to know: સંતાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C493-PEDHI493-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સંતાન** — *santān* — "offspring; a child".

> તેમને બે સંતાન છે, એક દીકરો અને એક દીકરી.
>
> *temne be santān che, ek dīkro ane ek dīkrī.*
>
> They have two children, a son and a daughter.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંતાન** (*santān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṃtāna*, "continued succession", as Monier-Williams records it: the line that carries a family on.
