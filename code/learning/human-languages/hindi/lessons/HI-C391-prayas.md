## You'll want to know: प्रयास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C391-PRAYAS391-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयास** — *prayās* — "an effort (formal)".

> अगर सब मिलकर प्रयास करेंगे तो गाँव बदलेगा।
>
> *agar sab milkar prayās kareṅge to gā̃v badlegā.*
>
> If everyone makes an effort together, the village will change.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयास** (*prayās*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prayāsa*, "exertion, effort" (Monier-Williams).
