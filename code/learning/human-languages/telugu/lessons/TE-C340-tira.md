# తీరా (tīrā) — in the end; when it came to it

## You'll want to know: తీరా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C340-ANUKOKUNDA340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**తీరా** — *tīrā* — "in the end; when it came to it".

> చాలా రోజులు వెతికాము, తీరా అది ఇంట్లోనే ఉంది.
>
> *cālā rōjulu vetikāmu, tīrā adi iṇṭlōnē undi.*
>
> We searched for days, and in the end it was right at home.

A spoken word that brings a surprise ending, often an ironic one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తీరా** (*tīrā*) is native Dravidian, built on *tīru*, "to be finished, concluded" (DEDR 3278): when everything was done.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
