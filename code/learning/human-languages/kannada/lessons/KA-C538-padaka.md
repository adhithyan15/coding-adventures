## You'll want to know: ಪದಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C538-PADAKA538-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪದಕ** — *padaka* — "a medal".

> ಓಟದ ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅವಳಿಗೆ ಚಿನ್ನದ ಪದಕ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *ōṭada spardheyalli avaḷige cinnada padaka sikkitu.*
>
> She won a gold medal in the race.

ಚಿನ್ನ, ಬೆಳ್ಳಿ, ಕಂಚು: gold, silver, bronze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪದಕ** (*padaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *padaka*, which Monier-Williams lists as "a kind of ornament" (Monier-Williams); a medal is an ornament you earn.
