## You'll want to know: મોરચો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C314-HADTAL314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મોરચો** — *morco* — "a protest march".

> લોકોએ શહેરમાં મોટો મોરચો કાઢ્યો.
>
> *lokoe śahermā̃ moṭo morco kāḍhyo.*
>
> People held a big protest march in the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મોરચો** (*morco*) is usually traced to Persian *mōrča*, "an entrenchment", a soldiers' word: in Gujarati it is a march of protest, which people **કાઢે છે**, bring out into the street.
