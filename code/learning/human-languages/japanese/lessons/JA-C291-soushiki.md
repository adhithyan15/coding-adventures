## You'll want to know: そうしき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C291-SOUSHIKI291-02]; assesses=[] -->

**そうしき** — *sōshiki* — "a funeral".

> **あした そうしきが あります。** — *ashita sōshiki ga arimasu* — "The funeral is tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そうしき** (*sōshiki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sō*, "bury" (Middle Chinese *tsangH*) and *shiki*, "ceremony" (*syik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
