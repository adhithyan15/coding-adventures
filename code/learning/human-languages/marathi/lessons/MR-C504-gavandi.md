## You'll want to know: गवंडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C504-KAMGAR504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गवंडी** — *gavaṇḍī* — "a mason".

> गवंड्याने विटांची भिंत बांधली.
>
> *gavaṇḍyāne viṭāñcī bhint bāndhlī.*
>
> The mason built a brick wall.

Masculine.

A builder in brick and stone; the oblique is गवंड्या-, as in गवंड्याने.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गवंडी** (*gavaṇḍī*) is the everyday Marathi word for a builder in brick and stone; its origin is not settled.
