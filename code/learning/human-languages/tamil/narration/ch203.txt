Tamil, chapter 203: Century, Until now, Deadline, Until then, Childhood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நூற்றாண்டு (nūṟṟāṇṭu) — a century.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for Deepavali, the festival of lights, then the Tamil for the New Year.

You'll want to know: நூற்றாண்டு.
நூற்றாண்டு — nūṟṟāṇṭu — "a century".

The word, taken apart.
நூற்றாண்டு (nūṟṟāṇṭu) is native: nūṟu, "hundred" (DEDR 3729), and āṇṭu, "year" (DEDR 5153). DEDR lists Malayalam nūṟṟ-āṇṭu, "100 years", built the same way.

Lesson 3 of 5.
காலக்கெடு (kālakkeṭu) — a deadline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: காலக்கெடு.
காலக்கெடு — kālakkeṭu — "a deadline".

The word, taken apart.
காலக்கெடு (kālakkeṭu) is kālam, "time", from Sanskrit kāla (CDIAL 3084), and native keṭu, "fixed time, term", which DEDR 1109 lists among words for passing through and limits of time.
