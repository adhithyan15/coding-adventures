# Every day — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *I speak a little*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HO-01, MW-LEX-C95-TIME95-04] -->

Every cell of the present so far, now that each one is taught:

| who | to speak | to read |
|---|---|---|
| **हूं** | **बोलूं हूं** | **बांचूं हूं** |
| **वो** | **बोलै है** | **बांचै है** |
| **आप** | **बोलो हो** | **बांचो हो** |

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *I listen to songs every day*; *mother listens to songs*]
- [YOU SAY: *Ram reads the newspaper every day*; *you read the newspaper*]
- [YOU SAY: *what do you do?*, to someone you call **आप**]
- [YOU SAY: *I write letters*; *he writes letters*]
