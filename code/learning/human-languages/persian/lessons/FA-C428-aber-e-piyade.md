## You'll want to know: عابر پیاده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C428-KAMIYUN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عابر پیاده** (*'âber-e piyâde*) — "a pedestrian".

> راننده برای عابر پیاده ایستاد.
>
> *Rânande barâ-ye 'âber-e piyâde istâd.*
>
> The driver stopped for the pedestrian.

*'âber* 'passer-by' + *piyâde* 'on foot'. Plural *'âberân-e piyâde*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'âber-e piyâde* is built from *'âber* 'one who passes', a loan usually traced to Arabic *ʿābir* 'crossing, passing', and native *piyâde* 'on foot', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian *payādag*.
