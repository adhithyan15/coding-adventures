Arabic, chapter 318: Ancient civilizations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حضارة (ḥaḍāra) — a civilization.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a patrol, then the Arabic for a checkpoint.

You'll want to know: حضارة.
حضارة — ḥaḍāra — "a civilization".
درسنا حضارة قديمة في المدرسة. darasnā ḥaḍāra qadīma fī al-madrasa. We studied an ancient civilization at school.

The word, taken apart.
حضارة (ḥaḍāra) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-ḍ-r (Lane), "to be present", the root of ḥaḍara, to attend, and of the taught ḥaḍḍara, to prepare. Lane gives ḥaḍāra as dwelling in towns and villages, the opposite of desert life.

Lesson 2 of 5.
أثر (athar) — a trace, a mark; an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a checkpoint, then the Arabic for a civilization.

You'll want to know: أثر.
أثر — athar — "a trace, a mark; an effect".
رأينا أثر قدم في الرمل. raʾaynā athar qadam fī ar-raml. We saw a footprint in the sand.
Plural: آثار, āthār.
The plural الآثار is antiquities: عالم آثار, an archaeologist.

The word, taken apart.
أثر (athar) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-th-r (Lane), where athar is a mark or track left behind; athara is also "to hand down a report".

Lesson 3 of 5.
أطلال (aṭlāl) — remains of a dwelling, ruins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a civilization, then the Arabic for a trace.

You'll want to know: أطلال.
أطلال — aṭlāl — "remains of a dwelling, ruins".
وقف الشاعر على أطلال البيت. waqafa ash-shāʿir ʿalā aṭlāl al-bayt. The poet stood at the remains of the house.
The old Arabic ode opens with the poet weeping at the أطلال (aṭlāl) of a camp.

The word, taken apart.
أطلال (aṭlāl) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-l-l (Lane), where ṭalal is the part of a dwelling that remains standing after people leave; aṭlāl is its plural.

Lesson 5 of 5.
هرم (haram) — a pyramid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for remains of a dwelling, then the Arabic for a temple.

You'll want to know: هرم.
هرم — haram — "a pyramid".
الهرم الكبير قديم جدا. al-haram al-kabīr qadīm jiddan. The Great Pyramid is very old.
Plural: أهرام, ahrām.

The word, taken apart.
هرم (haram) shares its letters with the root h-r-m, "to grow very old" (Lane). Whether the pyramid's name comes from it or from another language is not settled.
