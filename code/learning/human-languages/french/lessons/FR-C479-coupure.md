## You'll want to know: la coupure
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C479-BLESSURES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la coupure** — "cut".

> J'ai une petite coupure au doigt.
>
> *I have a small cut on my finger.*

Also a break in a supply: *une coupure de courant* — a power cut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la coupure* is built on *couper* "to cut" with *-ure* (EtymDB); *couper* comes from *coup* "blow", from Latin *colaphus* "a punch".
