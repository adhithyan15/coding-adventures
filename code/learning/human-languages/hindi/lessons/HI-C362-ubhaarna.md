## You'll want to know: उभारना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C362-NISHKARSH362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उभारना** — *ubhārnā* — "to bring out, to raise (a question)".

> इस प्रयोग ने कई नए सवाल उभारे।
>
> *is prayog ne kaī nae savāl ubhāre.*
>
> This experiment raised several new questions.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने वही सवाल उभारा, *usne vahī savāl ubhārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उभारना** (*ubhārnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *udbharati*, "raises up" (CDIAL 2038).
