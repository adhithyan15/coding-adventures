## You'll want to know: مشروط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C541-SURAT541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مشروط** — *mashrūt* — "conditional; with strings attached".

> اگر ان کی ہاں مشروط ہے تو شرط بتائیے۔
>
> *agar un kī hā̃ mashrūt hai to shart batāiye.*
>
> If their yes comes with a condition, tell me the condition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشروط** (*mashrūt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mashrūṭ*, "made conditional".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
