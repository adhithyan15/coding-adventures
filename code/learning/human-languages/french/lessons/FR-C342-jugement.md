## You'll want to know: le jugement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C342-JUGEMENT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le jugement** — "the judgement".

> J'ai confiance en ton jugement.
>
> *I trust your judgement.*

In court, *un jugement* is a ruling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jugement* is inherited through Old French *jugement* from Late Latin *iūdicāmentum* (EtymDB), built on *iūdicāre* "to judge" and *iūdex* "judge" (Lewis & Short).
