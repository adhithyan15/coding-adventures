## You'll want to know: रांगणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C499-BAL499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रांगणे** — *rāṅgṇe* — "to crawl".

> आमचं बाळ आता रांगायला लागलं.
>
> *āmcaṁ bāḷ ātā rāṅgāylā lāglaṁ.*
>
> Our baby has started crawling now.

Past (he …): तो रांगला, *to rāṅglā*.

रांगायला लागणे, to start crawling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रांगणे** (*rāṅgṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
