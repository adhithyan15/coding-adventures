## You'll want to know: संस्करण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C342-PRAKASHAK342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्करण** — *saṁskaraṇ* — "an edition".

> मुझे उम्मीद है कि इस किताब का नया संस्करण अगले साल आएगा।
>
> *mujhe ummīd hai ki is kitāb kā nayā saṁskaraṇ agle sāl āegā.*
>
> I hope a new edition of this book will come out next year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्करण** (*saṁskaraṇ*) is Sanskrit *saṁskaraṇa*, "a preparing, a making ready" (Monier-Williams): a book made ready again.
