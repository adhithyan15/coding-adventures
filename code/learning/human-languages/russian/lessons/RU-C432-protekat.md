## You'll want to know: протекать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C432-PROTEKAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**протекать** (*protekát*) — "to leak (of a roof or a tap); to flow through".

> В ванной протекает кран.
>
> *V vánnoy protekáyet kran.*
>
> The tap in the bathroom is leaking.

Imperfective; perfective *протечь*. *Крыша протекает* — the roof leaks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*протекать* is built from *про-* 'through' and *-текать*, from *течь* 'to flow' (EtymDB lists *течь* with that sense).
