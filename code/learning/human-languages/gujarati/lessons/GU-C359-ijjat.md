## You'll want to know: ઇજ્જત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C359-IJJAT359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇજ્જત** — *ijjat* — "one's good name; honour".

> ગામમાં તેના કુટુંબની ઘણી ઇજ્જત છે.
>
> *gāmmā̃ tenā kuṭumbnī ghaṇī ijjat che.*
>
> His family is highly respected in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇજ્જત** (*ijjat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿizzat*, "might, honour", through Persian.
