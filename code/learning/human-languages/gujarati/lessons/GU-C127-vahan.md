## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વહાણ** (*vahāṇ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *vāhana*, "any vehicle or draught animal" (CDIAL 11610).
