## You'll want to know: l'adolescent
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C310-JEUNESSE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'adolescent** — "the teenager, the adolescent".

> Les adolescents passent des heures sur leur téléphone.
>
> *Teenagers spend hours on their phones.*

Often *un ado*, *une ado* in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adolescent* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adolēscēns* "growing up" (EtymDB), the present participle of *adolēscere*.
