## You'll want to know: இன்னும் இல்லை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C95-RIGHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**இன்னும் இல்லை** (*iṉṉum illai*) — "not yet".

**இன்னும்** is "still"; with **இல்லை**, "still no".
