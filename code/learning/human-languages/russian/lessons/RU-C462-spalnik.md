## You'll want to know: спальник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C462-KOSTYOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**спальник** (*spálnik*) — "a sleeping bag".

> Ночью в спальнике было тепло.
>
> *Nóchyu v spálnike býlo tepló.*
>
> At night it was warm in the sleeping bag.

Colloquial for *спальный мешок*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*спальник* is a colloquial short form of *спальный мешок* 'a sleeping bag', from *спать* 'to sleep', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sŭpati* (EtymDB).
