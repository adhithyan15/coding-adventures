## You'll want to know: نهض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C515-NAWM515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نهض** — *nahaḍa* — "to get up, to rise".

> نهض جدي من الكرسي ببطء.
>
> *nahaḍa jaddī min al-kursī bi-buṭʾ.*
>
> My grandfather got up slowly from the chair.

Present: ينهض, *yanhaḍ*.

The noun نهضة is a rising, a revival.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نهض** (*nahaḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-h-ḍ*, "to rise, to stand up" (Lane). Nahaḍa is to rise up from sitting.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
