# முன்னேறு (muṉṉēṟu) — to move forward; to advance

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C412-VAZHI412-02, TA-LEX-C412-VAZHI412-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to lose one's way, then the Tamil for to lag behind.

## You'll want to know: முன்னேறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C412-VAZHI412-04]; assesses=[] -->

**முன்னேறு** — *muṉṉēṟu* — "to move forward; to advance".

> நெரிசலில் வண்டிகள் மெதுவாக முன்னேறின.
>
> *nericalil vaṇṭikaḷ metuvāka muṉṉēṟiṉa.*
>
> In the traffic jam the vehicles moved forward slowly.

Past (I …): முன்னேறினேன், *muṉṉēṟiṉēṉ*.

Also to make progress in life: அவர் வேலையில் முன்னேறினார்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**முன்னேறு** (*muṉṉēṟu*) is built from முன், "the front" (DEDR 5020), and ஏறு, "to climb, to rise" (DEDR 916).
