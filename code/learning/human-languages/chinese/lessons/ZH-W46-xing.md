## Script — 日 above 生
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XING-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 星

**Two pieces you know, top to bottom: 日, then 生.**

**日, four strokes**, short and wide on top.

**生, five strokes**, as in 学生: a left-falling stroke, a horizontal, a shorter horizontal, the vertical through them, and the long bar at the foot.

4 + 5 = nine.
