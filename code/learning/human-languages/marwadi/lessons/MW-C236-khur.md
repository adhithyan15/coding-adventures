## You'll want to know: खुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C236-BEAST236-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खुर** — *khur* — "a hoof".

> खुर कड़क है।
>
> *khur kaṛak hai.*
>
> The hoof is hard.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खुर** (*khur*) is inherited from Sanskrit *khura*, "a hoof"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *khur*, "a hoof". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 3906).
