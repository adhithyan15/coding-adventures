## You'll want to know: ندى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C485-SAMA485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ندى** — *nadā* — "dew".

> كان الندى على العشب في الصباح.
>
> *kāna an-nadā ʿalā al-ʿushb fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> There was dew on the grass in the morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ندى** (*nadā*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *n-d-y*, "to be moist".
