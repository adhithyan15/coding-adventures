## You'll want to know: danneggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C299-SCONTRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**danneggiare** — "to damage".

> Il vento ha danneggiato il tetto.
>
> *The wind damaged the roof.*

Present *danneggio*, *danneggi*, *danneggia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*danneggiare* is built on *danno* "damage", which is inherited from Latin *damnum* "loss, harm" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
