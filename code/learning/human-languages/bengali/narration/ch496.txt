Bengali, chapter 496: Moving Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পদ (pôd) — a post; a position.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pension, then the Bengali for to be worth it.

You'll want to know: পদ.
পদ — pôd — "a post; a position".
তিনি অফিসে একটা বড় পদে আছেন। Tini ôphise ekṭā bôṛo pôde āchhen. She holds a senior post at the office.

The word, taken apart.
পদ (pôd) is a tatsama, Sanskrit pada, "a step", and from that "a place, a rank" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
বহাল থাকা (bôhāl thākā) — to stay in post; to remain in force.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an assistant, then the Bengali for senior in rank.

You'll want to know: বহাল থাকা.
বহাল থাকা — bôhāl thākā — "to stay in post; to remain in force".
নতুন মালিক এল, কিন্তু পুরনো ম্যানেজার বহাল থাকলেন। Nôtun mālik elô, kintu purôno mænejār bôhāl thāklen. A new owner came, but the old manager kept his post.
Past (he/she …): তিনি পদে বহাল থাকলেন, tini pôde bôhāl thāklen.

The word, taken apart.
বহাল থাকা (bôhāl thākā) joins বহাল, "in place, in force", usually traced to Persian bahāl, "restored", to থাকা, "to stay".
