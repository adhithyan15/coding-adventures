## You'll want to know: பேருந்து
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C69-GOING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பேருந்து** (*pērundu*) — "a bus".

A made word, and you can take it apart. **பேர்-** is the big-word பெரு, and **உந்து** is
"to push along". A big pushed thing.

This is the printed and announced word: it is what stands on the board at the stop and in the
newspaper. In the street you will also hear the English "bus" said plainly, and both are
understood — but only one of them is written on the board you have to read.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two, and one of them you can take apart.
