## You'll want to know: గర్జించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C473-DVIPAM473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**గర్జించు** — *garjiñcu* — "to roar".

> రాత్రంతా సముద్రం గర్జించింది.
>
> *rātrantā samudraṁ garjiñcindi.*
>
> The sea roared all night.

Past (I …): గర్జించాను, *garjiñcānu*.

Said of a lion, of thunder and of an angry sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గర్జించు** (*garjiñcu*) is built from Sanskrit *garj*, "to thunder, to roar" (CDIAL 4046, *garjati*), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
