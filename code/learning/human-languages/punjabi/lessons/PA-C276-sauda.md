## You'll want to know: ਸੌਦਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C276-PRICE276-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੌਦਾ** — *saudā* — "a purchase; a deal".

> ਇਹ ਚੰਗਾ ਸੌਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh changā saudā hai.*
>
> This is a good deal.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੌਦਾ** (*saudā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *saudā*, "trade, a bargain".
