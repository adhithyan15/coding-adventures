# One rental exchange, held as a whole

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-01, SA-VERB-C266-KRINAMI-01, SA-LEX-C72-HOUSEWORK-01, SA-LEX-C72-HOUSEWORK-03, SA-LEX-C136-ACT136-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Recall **गृहम्**, “house” or “home,” and **क्रीणामि**, “I buy.”
Then recall the familiar household actions **पचति**, **प्रक्षालयति**, and
**मार्जयति**: cooking, washing, and cleaning. This is your known house, buying, and household-work field.

## You'll want to know: one complete attested exchange

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C274-RENTAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **किं तत् भाटकितं गृहम्?**
>
> *kiṃ tat bhāṭakitaṃ gṛham?*
>
> “Is that a rented house?”
>
> **न, अहं भाटकं न ददामि।**
>
> *na, ahaṃ bhāṭakaṃ na dadāmi.*
>
> “No, I do not pay rent.”

The [direct conversation source](https://sanskritkalp.com/samskrit-vaartaalaap/kim)
supplies this complete attested exchange. A 1954
[*Saṃskṛtavyavahārapraśnottarī* conversation](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/vyavahAraprashnottarI.html)
and its Hindi gloss corroborate the rented-house and rent-payment function.

The older volunteer rendering prints **भाटं**, with an apparent dropped syllable.
The Government of India's
[Commission for Scientific and Technical Terminology](https://www.cstt.education.gov.in/sites/default/files/fundamintal-administrative-terminology-english-sanskrit.pdf)
gives **भाटकम्** for “hire, rent” and **गृहभाटकम्** for “house rent.” The
conversation above independently preserves **भाटकं**, so this lesson uses the
cross-checked complete exchange rather than copying that apparent omission.

## Grammar Lens: keep the exchange bounded

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C274-RENTAL-01] -->

Hear the question and answer as one practical pair. The lesson does not turn
**भाटकितम्**, **गृहम्**, or **भाटकम्** into a productive participle or case
paradigm, and it does not ask you to derive their sandhi.

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C274-RENTAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Read the question and answer once slowly. Then say them together as
one exchange about whether a house is rented.
