Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 231: Bent and Straight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बांको (bā̃ko) — crooked; bent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for swallow-wort, then the Marwadi for a thorn.

You'll want to know: बांको.
बांको — bā̃ko — "crooked; bent".
डूंगर माथै गेलो बांको है। ḍū̃gar māthai gelo bā̃ko hai. On the hill the path is crooked.

The word, taken apart.
बांको (bā̃ko) is inherited from Sanskrit vaṅka, "bent, crooked"; CDIAL lists Marwari bā̃ko (CDIAL 11191).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सीधो (sīdho) — straight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a thorn, then the Marwadi for crooked.

You'll want to know: सीधो.
सीधो — sīdho — "straight".
सड़क सीधी है। saṛak sīdhī hai. The road is straight.

The word, taken apart.
सीधो (sīdho) is inherited from Sanskrit siddha, "accomplished"; CDIAL lists Hindi sīdhā, "straight", and Gujarati sīdhũ, "straightforward". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 13401).
