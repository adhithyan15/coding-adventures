# ശല്യം (śalyaṁ) — a nuisance; a bother

## You'll want to know: ശല്യം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C487-NAGARAM487-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ശല്യം** — *śalyaṁ* — "a nuisance; a bother".

> രാത്രി നായകളുടെ ശല്യം കാരണം ഞങ്ങൾക്ക് ഉറങ്ങാൻ കഴിഞ്ഞില്ല.
>
> *rātri nāyakaḷuṭe śalyaṁ kāraṇaṁ ñaṅṅaḷkkŭ uṟaṅṅān kaḻiññilla.*
>
> Because of the dogs' nuisance at night, we could not sleep.

What bothers you comes first: കൊതുകുശല്യം, the trouble from mosquitoes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശല്യം** (*śalyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śalya*, "a dart, a thorn" (Monier-Williams): something that sticks in and will not leave you alone.
