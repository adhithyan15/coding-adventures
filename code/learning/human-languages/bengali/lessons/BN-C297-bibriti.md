# বিবৃতি (bibriti) — a public statement; a statement

## You'll want to know: বিবৃতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C297-KOTHA297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বিবৃতি** — *bibriti* — "a public statement; a statement".

> মন্ত্রী দুর্ঘটনার পরে একটা বিবৃতি দিলেন।
>
> *môntrī durghôṭônār pôre ekṭā bibriti dilen.*
>
> The minister made a statement after the accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিবৃতি** (*bibriti*) is Sanskrit *vivṛti*, 'a making manifest, an exposition' (Monier-Williams): a statement opens a matter up. বিবৃতি দেওয়া, to issue a statement.
