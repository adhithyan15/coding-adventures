# ਤਿਆਰ (tiār) — ready

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to lie down, then the Punjabi for in a hurry.

## You'll want to know: ਤਿਆਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C226-DIN226-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਿਆਰ** — *tiār* — "ready".

> ਮੈਂ ਸੱਤ ਵਜੇ ਤਿਆਰ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ satt vaje tiār hundī hāṁ.*
>
> I am ready at seven.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਿਆਰ** (*tiār*) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian *tayyār*, "ready".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what you do.
