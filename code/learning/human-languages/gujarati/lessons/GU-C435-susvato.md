# સુસવાટો (susvāṭo) — a howl of wind; a whistling gust

## You'll want to know: સુસવાટો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C435-STORM435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સુસવાટો** — *susvāṭo* — "a howl of wind; a whistling gust".

> રાત્રે પવનના સુસવાટા સંભળાતા હતા.
>
> *rātre pavannā susvāṭā sambhḷātā hatā.*
>
> At night you could hear the wind howling.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સુસવાટો** (*susvāṭo*) goes back to *\*śūṣa*, "blowing", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a blast of wind" (CDIAL 12577).
