## You'll want to know: मुनाफ़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C428-KAMANA428-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मुनाफ़ा** — *munāfā* — "a profit (in trade)".

> इस साल दुकान में अच्छा मुनाफ़ा हुआ।
>
> *is sāl dukān meṁ acchā munāfā huā.*
>
> The shop made a good profit this year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुनाफ़ा** (*munāfā*) is usually traced to Arabic *manāfiʿ*, "profits, uses", through Persian: what a trader gains over the cost.
