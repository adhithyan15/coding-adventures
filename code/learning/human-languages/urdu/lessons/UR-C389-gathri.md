## You'll want to know: گٹھری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C389-UNGHNA389-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گٹھری** — *gaṭhrī* — "a bundle (tied in cloth)".

> ایک مسافر کے پاس کپڑوں کی گٹھری تھی۔
>
> *ek musāfir ke pās kapṛoṅ kī gaṭhrī thī.*
>
> One passenger had a bundle of clothes.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گٹھری** (*gaṭhrī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gaṭhṛī*, "a bundle", under Sanskrit *grathna*, "a bunch" (CDIAL 4348).
