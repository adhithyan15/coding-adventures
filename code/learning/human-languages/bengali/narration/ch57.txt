Bengali, chapter 57: Carrot, Salt, Butter, Honey, Oil.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গাজর (gājôr) — a carrot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a banana, then the Bengali for a potato.

You'll want to know: গাজর.
গাজর — gājôr — "a carrot".

The word, taken apart.
গাজর (gājôr) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from gārjara, "carrot" (CDIAL 4140).

You'll want to know: নুন.
নুন — nun — "salt".

The word, taken apart.
নুন (nun) goes back to Sanskrit lavaṇa, "salt". CDIAL gives no Bengali form under that entry, but it lists Oriya nuṇa there (CDIAL 10978), and Bengali nuniyā, "salted", under the related lavaṇita, "salted" (CDIAL 10983).

You'll want to know: মধু.
মধু — môdhu — "honey".

The word, taken apart.
মধু (môdhu) is Sanskrit madhu, "honey, mead", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 9784). The word inherited from it is mau, which CDIAL lists.
