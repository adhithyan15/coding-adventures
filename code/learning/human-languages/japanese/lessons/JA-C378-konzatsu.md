## You'll want to know: こんざつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C378-MANIN378-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こんざつ** — *konzatsu* — "congestion; crowding".

> **しゅうまつの えきは こんざつしていました。** — *shūmatsu no eki wa konzatsu shite imashita* — "The station was crowded at the weekend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こんざつ** (*konzatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kon*, "mix" (Middle Chinese *hwonX*) and *zatsu*, "miscellaneous" (*dzop*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
