## You'll want to know: परहेज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C495-KASRAT495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परहेज़** — *parhez* — "avoiding certain foods; abstinence".

> मधुमेह में मीठे से परहेज़ करना पड़ता है।
>
> *madhumeh meṁ mīṭhe se parhez karnā paṛtā hai.*
>
> With diabetes, you have to keep off sweet things.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परहेज़** (*parhez*) is usually traced to Persian *parhēz*, "abstinence, keeping away". *X se parhez karnā* is to keep off X.
