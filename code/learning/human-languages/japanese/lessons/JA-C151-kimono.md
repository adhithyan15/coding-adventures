## You'll want to know: きもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C151-KIMONO151-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きもの** — *kimono* — "a kimono".

> **まつりで きものを きます。** — *matsuri de kimono o kimasu* — "I wear a kimono at the festival."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きもの** (*kimono*) is native Japanese, built from *ki*, "wearing", from *kiru*, and *mono*, "thing": a thing to wear. Both parts are inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
