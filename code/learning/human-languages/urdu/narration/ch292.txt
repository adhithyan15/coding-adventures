Urdu, chapter 292: The Storm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اندھیرا (andherā) — darkness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to cave in, then the Urdu for to be trapped under.

You'll want to know: اندھیرا.
اندھیرا — andherā — "darkness".
شہر میں اندھیرا تھا۔ shahr meṅ andherā thā. The city was dark.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اندھیرا (andherā) is inherited from Sanskrit andhakāra, "darkness" (CDIAL 386), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ãdherā.

You'll want to know: گرجنا.
گرجنا — garajnā — "to thunder; to roar".
رات کو بادل گرج رہے تھے۔ rāt ko bādal garaj rahe the. The clouds were thundering in the night.
Past (it …): وہ شیر گرج اٹھا, voh sher garaj uṭhā.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from Sanskrit gárjati, "thunders, roars", where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gājnā (CDIAL 4046); گرجنا (garajnā) keeps the Sanskrit shape closer. Clouds گرجتے ہیں, and so does a lion.

The word, taken apart.
چمکنا (chamaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin camaknā, "to glitter", under camakka, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4676).

Lesson 5 of 5.
اثر (asar) — an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to flash, then the Urdu for destruction.

You'll want to know: اثر.
اثر — asar — "an effect".
طوفان کا اثر دو دن رہا۔ tūfān kā asar do din rahā. The storm's effects lasted two days.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اثر (asar) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic athar, "a trace, a mark left behind". Urdu says its middle letter as s.
