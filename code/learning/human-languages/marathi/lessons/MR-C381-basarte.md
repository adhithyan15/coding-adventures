## You'll want to know: बशर्ते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C381-JOPARYANT381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बशर्ते** — *baśarte* — "provided that".

> मी मदत करीन, बशर्ते तुम्ही वेळेवर याल.
>
> *mī madat karīn, baśarte tumhī veḷevar yāl.*
>
> I will help, provided that you come on time.

Formal; it stands before the condition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बशर्ते** (*baśarte*) is a loan from Persian *ba-sharṭ*, "on condition", usually traced to Arabic *sharṭ*, "condition".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
