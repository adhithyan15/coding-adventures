# … પર આધાર રાખે છે (… par ādhār rākhe che) — it depends on …

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C284-ADHAR-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-FUTURE-01, GU-LEX-C105-CAN105-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "my support". (*māro ādhār*.) Then "if it rains". (*jo varsād paḍśe*.)

## Grammar Lens: it keeps its support on …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C284-PAR-ADHAR-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C213-WEATHER213-01, GU-LEX-C76-HOWMUCH-02, GU-LEX-C79-WEEK2-05] -->

> **તે હવામાન પર આધાર રાખે છે.**
>
> *te havāmān par ādhār rākhe che.* — "It depends on the weather."

Word for word: "it keeps its support on the weather". **પર** (*par*), "on", comes after its word, as **સુધી** does. The thing that depends is the subject of **રાખે છે**: **બધું સમય પર આધાર રાખે છે** (*badhũ samay par ādhār rākhe che*), "everything depends on the time". A person takes **મારા**, **તમારા**: **તે તમારા પર આધાર રાખે છે** (*te tamārā par ādhār rākhe che*), "it depends on you".

## You'll want to know: "it depends", and then
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C284-DEPENDS-ANSWER-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C274-NEGATIVE-FUTURE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-JO-TO-01, GU-JOIN-KE-01] -->

> **અમે જઈશું કે નહીં? તે વરસાદ પર આધાર રાખે છે.**
>
> *ame jaīśũ ke nahī̃? te varsād par ādhār rākhe che.* — "Will we go or not? It depends on the rain."

"It depends" is seldom the end of an answer. Say on what, then follow with **જો**: **જો વરસાદ નહીં પડે, તો અમે જઈશું** (*jo varsād nahī̃ paḍe, to ame jaīśũ*).
