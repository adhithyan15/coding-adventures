## You'll want to know: ત્યજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C500-VARSO500-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્યજવું** — *tyajvũ* — "to abandon; to give up".

> તેણે ગુસ્સો ત્યજી દીધો.
>
> *teṇe gusso tyajī dīdho.*
>
> He gave up his anger.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘર ત્યજ્યું, *teṇe ghar tyajyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્યજવું** (*tyajvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *tyajati*, "leaves, abandons", which Monier-Williams records under *tyaj*.
