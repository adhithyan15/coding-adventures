## You'll want to know: અદેખાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C457-NAFRAT457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અદેખાઈ** — *adekhāī* — "envy".

> મિત્રની નવી ગાડી જોઈને તેને અદેખાઈ થઈ.
>
> *mitranī navī gāḍī joīne tene adekhāī thaī.*
>
> Seeing his friend's new car, he felt envious.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અદેખાઈ** (*adekhāī*) is built from the negative *a-*, "not", the stem of *dekhvũ*, "to see", which CDIAL traces to *\*dṛkṣati*, "sees" (CDIAL 6507), and the noun ending *-āī*: probably the feeling of not bearing to see another's good.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
