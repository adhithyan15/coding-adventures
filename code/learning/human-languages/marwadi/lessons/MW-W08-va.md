# व — the middle of family

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-PARIVAAR-01, MW-SCRIPT-DA-01, MW-LEX-DADI-01, MW-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 16s] Say family and paternal grandmother. Write **द** and **ि** once.

## Script — one sign
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-VA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **व** — *va*

Keep the model visible. Trace the rounded lower body, rise to the upright, and
finish with the headline. Say *va* after the hand stops.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-VA-01, MW-LEX-PARIVAAR-01] -->

Copy **व** once. Cover it, wait five seconds, and write it once. Say the middle
sound group *vār* from *parivār*.
