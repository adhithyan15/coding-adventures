# (synthesis)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-DECIR-PRETERITE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Here is a very short conversation. Two people, four lines.

> — *¿Dónde trabajas?*
> — *Trabajo en casa.*
> — *¿Estás cansado?*
> — *Sí.*

[PAUSE 1s] You were there. Somebody else was not.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-WH-ACCENT, ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-QUE-OBLIGATORY, ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-YESNO-SI] -->

Carry it across, line by line. Every line needs a joint, and the joint is
different every time.

[PAUSE 2s] The first line was a question with an asking word.
(*Preguntó dónde trabajaba*.)

[PAUSE 2s] The second was a statement. (*Dijo que trabajaba en casa*.)

[PAUSE 2s] The third was a yes-or-no question. (*Preguntó si estaba cansado*.)

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-BACKSHIFT, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-IMPERFECT-SINGULAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read your three sentences back and name what is **not** in them. (No
question marks — and not one verb left in the present.)

[PAUSE 2s] Four lines of live speech went in; one steady account came out. What
is new is not that you said something in Spanish — it is that you **carried**
somebody else's words to a person who was not there, and marked every seam where
they stopped being your own.
