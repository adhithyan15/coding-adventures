## You'll want to know: īnsomnis, īnsomne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C447-CIMEX-04]; assesses=[] -->

**īnsomnis, īnsomne** — "sleepless".

> Propter cīmicēs viātor īnsomnis in lectō iacet.
>
> *Because of the bedbugs the traveller lies sleepless in bed.*

An adjective of two endings, from *in-* 'not' and *somnus* 'sleep'. English *insomnia* comes from a noun built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*īnsomnis* is built from *in-* 'not' and *somnus* 'sleep' (Lewis & Short). English *insomnia* comes from the related noun *īnsomnia*.
