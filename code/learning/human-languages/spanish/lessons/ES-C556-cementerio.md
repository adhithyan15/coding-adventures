## You'll want to know: el cementerio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C556-ENTIERRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el cementerio** — "the cemetery".

> El cementerio está detrás de la iglesia.
>
> *The cemetery is behind the church.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cementerio* is a learned borrowing from Church Latin *coemētērium* (EtymDB), taken from Greek *koimētḗrion* 'a sleeping place'.
