Marathi, chapter 481: Coughs and Colds.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
पडसे (paḍse) — a runny cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for weak, then the Marathi for to feel sick.

You'll want to know: पडसे.
पडसे — paḍse — "a runny cold".
हवा बदलली की बाळाला पडसं होतं. havā badallī kī bāḷālā paḍsaṁ hotaṁ. When the weather changes the baby gets a runny cold.
Neuter.
पडसं होणे, with the dative. Sentences write the neuter as पडसं. Close to सर्दी.

The word, taken apart.
पडसे (paḍse) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit pratiśyāya, "a cold in the head"; CDIAL lists Marathi paḍsẽ (CDIAL 8604).

धुळीमुळे आजोबा सारखे शिंकत होते. dhuḷīmuḷe ājobā sārkhe śiṅkat hote. Grandfather kept sneezing because of the dust.

शिंकरणे — śiṅkarṇe — "to blow one's nose".
त्याने रुमालात नाक शिंकरलं. tyāne rumālāt nāk śiṅkarlaṁ. He blew his nose into a handkerchief.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नाक शिंकरलं, tyāne nāk śiṅkarlaṁ.
The object is नाक: नाक शिंकरणे (śiṅkarṇe).

The word, taken apart.
शिंकरणे (śiṅkarṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
खाकरणे (khākarṇe) — to clear one's throat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sneeze, then the Marathi for to blow one's nose.

You'll want to know: खाकरणे.
खाकरणे — khākarṇe — "to clear one's throat".
बोलण्यापूर्वी आजोबा खाकरले. bolṇyāpūrvī ājobā khākarle. Grandfather cleared his throat before speaking.
Past (he …): तो खाकरला, to khākarlā.
A small cough to clear the throat, or to catch someone's attention.

The word, taken apart.
खाकरणे (khākarṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi khākarṇẽ, "clearing the throat", under the reconstructed khāṭkāra (CDIAL 3859).
