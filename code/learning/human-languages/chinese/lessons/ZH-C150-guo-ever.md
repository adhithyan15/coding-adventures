## Grammar Lens: 了 says it happened; 过 says it has happened at least once
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-GUO-EXPERIENCE-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-COMPLETION-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-BU-MEI-01, ZH-LEX-C150-GUOQU-01] -->

> **过 straight after a verb says this has happened at least once, at some time. Here it is said light: *guo*.**

> **你去过中国吗** *nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma* — Have you ever been to China?
> **我去过** *wǒ qù guo* — I have.

The negative keeps 过 and puts 没 in front; 不 has no place here, because the question is about what has happened:

> **我没去过** *wǒ méi qù guo* — I've not been.

Set it beside 了. 我去了中国 reports one trip; 我去过中国 says China is somewhere you have been, at some point in your life:

> **我去了中国** *wǒ qù le Zhōngguó* — I went to China.
> **我去过中国** *wǒ qù guo Zhōngguó* — I've been to China.
