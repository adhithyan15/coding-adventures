## You'll want to know: ài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-02]; assesses=[] -->

*ài* — "to love".

> *wǒ ài nǐ* — I love you.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ài* is inherited from Middle Chinese *'ojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to cherish'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
