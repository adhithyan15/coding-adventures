## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HUNDA-207, PA-DEM-EH-01, PA-LEX-C100-TIME100-04, PA-LEX-HAI-02, PA-GRAMMAR-VERB-LAST-02] -->

> **ਇਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ** — *eh roz hundaa hai* — this happens every day

For this one frame, put familiar **ਇਹ ਰੋਜ਼** before the new whole form
**ਹੁੰਦਾ**, then keep familiar **ਹੈ** last. Nothing else in the habitual or
agreement system is added.
