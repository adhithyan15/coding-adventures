## You'll want to know: こむぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C489-FARM489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**こむぎ** — *komugi* — "wheat".

> **こむぎの こなで うどんを つくります。** — *komugi no kona de udon o tsukurimasu* — "We make udon from wheat flour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こむぎ** (*komugi*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *ko*, "small" and *mugi*, "wheat, barley" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
