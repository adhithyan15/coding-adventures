## You'll want to know: கூன்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C488-EDAI488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கூன்** — *kūṉ* — "a hunch; a stoop".

> வயதானதால் பாட்டிக்குக் கூன் விழுந்தது.
>
> *vayatāṉatāl pāṭṭikkuk kūṉ viḻuntatu.*
>
> With age grandmother developed a stoop.

கூன் விழு, to become hunched.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கூன்** (*kūṉ*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1927), a bend or a hump.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
