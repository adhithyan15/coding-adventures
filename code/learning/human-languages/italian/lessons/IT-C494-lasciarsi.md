## You'll want to know: lasciarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C494-SEPARARSI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lasciarsi** — "to break up".

> Ci siamo lasciati dopo tre anni insieme.
>
> *We broke up after three years together.*

The reflexive of *lasciare* "to leave": *mi ha lasciato* — he left me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lasciarsi* is the reflexive of *lasciare* "to leave", inherited from Latin *laxāre* "to loosen, to release" (Lewis & Short).
