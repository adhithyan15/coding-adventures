## You'll want to know: ವಿವಾಹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C325-VIVAHA325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿವಾಹ** — *vivāha* — "a marriage".

> ಎಲ್ಲವೂ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ನಡೆದರೆ ಮುಂದಿನ ತಿಂಗಳು ಅವರ ವಿವಾಹ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ellavū sariyāgi naḍedare mundina tiṅgaḷu avara vivāha naḍeyuttade.*
>
> If all goes well, their marriage takes place next month.

ವಿವಾಹ is the formal word, on cards and in the news; at home people say ಮದುವೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿವಾಹ** (*vivāha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vivāha*, "marriage", literally "leading away" of the bride (CDIAL 11920).
