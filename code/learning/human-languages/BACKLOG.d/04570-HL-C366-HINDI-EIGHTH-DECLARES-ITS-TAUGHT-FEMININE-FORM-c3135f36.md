## HL-C366 — Hindi eighth declares its taught feminine form

Chapter 83 presents feminine **आठवीं** beside masculine **आठवाँ**, but its
declaration and guided practice expose only the masculine form. The corpus report
therefore leaves the agreement form visible in the lesson looking untaught.

Declare and practise **आठवीं** beside **आठवाँ** so the metadata and retrieval work
match the lesson's presentation without introducing an unsupported form.
