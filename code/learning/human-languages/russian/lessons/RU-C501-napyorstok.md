## You'll want to know: напёрсток
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C501-SEWING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**напёрсток** (*napyórstok*) — "a thimble".

> Без напёрстка я всегда колю палец.
>
> *Bez napyórstka ya vsegdá kolyú pálets.*
>
> Without a thimble I always prick my finger.

Masculine; genitive напёрстка.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*напёрсток* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *napьrstъkъ*, derived from *pьrstъ* 'finger' (EtymDB): a cap on the finger. The old word *перст* 'finger', which EtymDB marks as a Church Slavonic borrowing, is usually linked to *перчатки* 'gloves'.
