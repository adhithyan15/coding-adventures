# 汉 — watch five strokes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-HANZI-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Say **Chinese character**, then point to the new first half of 汉字.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

In **汉**, write 氵 first. On the right, stroke four crosses right and turns
down-left without lifting. Stroke five falls down-right. Five strokes; four
lifts. Watch the crossing open into two different falling directions.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-HAN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **汉** once, slowly. Say **dot, dot, rise; turn left; fall right**.
