Persian, chapter 461: Paying.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کارتخوان (kârtkhân) — a card machine, a card reader.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fitting room, then the Persian for to like.

You'll want to know: کارتخوان.
کارتخوان (kârtkhân) — "a card machine, a card reader".
کارتخوان (kârtkhân) نانوایی خراب بود. Kârtkhân-e nânvâyi kharâb bud. The bakery's card machine was out of order.
kârt 'card' + khân 'reader', from khândan.

The word, taken apart.
kârtkhân is built from kârt 'card', which EtymDB takes from French carte, and native khân, the present stem of khândan 'to read'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
کارت به کارت کردن (kârt-be-kârt kardan) — to transfer money from card to card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to like, then the Persian for a card machine.

You'll want to know: کارت به کارت کردن.
کارت به کارت کردن (kârt-be-kârt kardan) — "to transfer money from card to card".
پول بلیت را برای دوستم کارت به کارت کردم. Pul-e bilit râ barâ-ye dustam kârt-be-kârt kardam. I transferred the ticket money to my friend by card.
Very common in Iran: money goes from one bank card to another by phone or cash machine.

The word, taken apart.
kârt-be-kârt kardan is built from kârt 'card', which EtymDB takes from French carte, twice over with be 'to', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
نسیه (nesye) — on credit, on account (buying now and paying later).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a card machine, then the Persian for to transfer money from card to card.

You'll want to know: نسیه.
نسیه (nesye) — "on credit, on account (buying now and paying later)".
بقال محله به ما نسیه میدهد. Baqqâl-e mahalle be mâ nesye midahad. The local grocer lets us buy on credit.
Nesye kharidan — to buy on credit. The opposite is naqd 'cash'.

The word, taken apart.
nesye was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic nasīʾa 'deferment, sale on credit'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پول خرد (pul-e khord) — small change, coins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to transfer money from card to card, then the Persian for on credit.

You'll want to know: پول خرد.
پول خرد (pul-e khord) — "small change, coins".
راننده تاکسی پول خرد نداشت. Rânande-ye tâksi pul-e khord nadâsht. The taxi driver didn't have any change.
pul 'money' + khord 'small'.

The word, taken apart.
pul-e khord is built from pul 'money', which EtymDB glosses 'change, small money', and native khord 'small, broken', usually traced to Middle Persian xward.

صرافی (sarrâfi) — "a bureau de change, an exchange office".
در صرافی دلار را به ریال تبدیل کردیم. Dar sarrâfi dolâr râ be riyâl tabdil kardim. At the exchange office we changed dollars into rials.
The money changer is the sarrâf.

The word, taken apart.
sarrâfi is built from sarrâf 'money changer', a loan usually traced to Arabic ṣarrāf, and the Persian ending -i. EtymDB does not list it.
