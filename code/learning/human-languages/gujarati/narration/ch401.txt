Gujarati, chapter 401: A Flat Tyre.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ટાયર (ṭāyar) — a tyre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a handlebar, then the Gujarati for to toss.

You'll want to know: ટાયર.
ટાયર — ṭāyar — "a tyre".
ગાડીના ટાયરમાં હવા ઓછી છે. gāḍīnā ṭāyarmā̃ havā ochī che. There is too little air in the car's tyre.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટાયર (ṭāyar) is borrowed from English tyre.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઘસડવું (ghasaḍvũ) — to drag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a puncture, then the Gujarati for a truck.

You'll want to know: ઘસડવું.
ઘસડવું — ghasaḍvũ — "to drag".
તે ભારે પટારો રસ્તા પર ઘસડે છે. te bhāre paṭāro rastā par ghasḍe che. He drags the heavy trunk along the road.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પટારો ઘસડ્યો, teṇe paṭāro ghasaḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઘસડવું (ghasaḍvũ) seems to be built on the root of the Gujarati verb ghasvũ, "to rub", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit gharṣati, "rubs" (CDIAL 4450): what you drag rubs along the ground. CDIAL gives no form for ઘસડવું itself, so the link is hedged.

One more word for getting around.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઉથલવું (uthalvũ) — to overturn; to tip over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a truck, then the Gujarati for to drag.

You'll want to know: ઉથલવું.
ઉથલવું — uthalvũ — "to overturn; to tip over".
ખાડામાં પડીને ટ્રક ઉથલી ગઈ. khāḍāmā̃ paḍīne ṭrak uthlī gaī. The truck fell into a pit and overturned.
Past (it …): તે ટ્રક ઉથલી ગઈ, te ṭrak uthlī gaī.

The word, taken apart.
ઉથલવું (uthalvũ) goes back to utthalati, "is upset, is turned over", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 1805).
