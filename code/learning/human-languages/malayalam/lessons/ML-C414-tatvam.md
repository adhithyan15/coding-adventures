## You'll want to know: തത്വം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C414-TATVAM414-01]; assesses=[] -->

**തത്വം** — *tatvaṁ* — "a principle".

> സത്യം പറയുക എന്നതാണ് അവന്റെ തത്വം.
>
> *satyaṁ paṟayuka ennatāṇŭ avanṟe tatvaṁ.*
>
> Telling the truth is his principle.

Also spelled തത്ത്വം in older books.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തത്വം** (*tatvaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tattva*, "the real state of a thing, truth" (CDIAL 5642), from *tad*, "that": thatness, what a thing is.
