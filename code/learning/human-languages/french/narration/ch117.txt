Lesson 1 of 5.
les bottes — boots.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for gloves, then the French for a scarf.

You'll want to know: les bottes.
les bottes — "boots".

The word, taken apart.
bottes is the plural of the word for "boot", whose origin is uncertain. EtymDB 2.1 records Old French bote, "a high, thick shoe", and offers a Frankish source that is not secure.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the French for a scarf, then the French for boots.

You'll want to know: la casquette.
la casquette — "a cap".

The word, taken apart.
casquette — a little form of the French word for "helmet", with -ette. That word came from Spanish casco, "skull, helmet" (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: le maillot.
le maillot — "a swimsuit; a sports shirt".

The word, taken apart.
maillot from Old French mailloel, a swaddling band, from maille, "a stitch, a mesh", from Latin macula, "a spot, a mesh". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la taille — size; the waist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a cap, then the French for a swimsuit; a sports shirt.

You'll want to know: la taille.
la taille — "size; the waist".

The word, taken apart.
taille — formed from the verb for "to cut", from Late Latin taliare, from talea, "a cutting, a twig" (EtymDB 2.1): the cut of a garment, then its size.

portefeuille — "carry-leaves": porte, from porter, "to carry", and feuille, "a leaf, a sheet": a case for carrying papers.
