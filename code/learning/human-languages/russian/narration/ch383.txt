Russian, chapter 383: Getting into university.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поступление (postupléniye) — admission (to a university); entry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for preparation, then the Russian for to prepare oneself.

You'll want to know: поступление.
поступление (postupléniye) — "admission (to a university); entry".
Поступление в университет — важный шаг. Postupléniye v universitét — vázhnyy shag. Getting into university is an important step.
Neuter. From поступать, to enter a university. It also means money coming in: поступление (postupléniye) денег.

The word, taken apart.
поступление (postupléniye) is built on поступить "to enter", from по- and ступить "to step", an inherited Slavic verb: the step into a university.

Lesson 2 of 5.
абитуриент (abituriyént) — a university applicant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to prepare oneself, then the Russian for admission.

You'll want to know: абитуриент.
абитуриент (abituriyént) — "a university applicant".
Абитуриенты сдают экзамены летом. Abituriyénty sdayút ekzámeny létom. University applicants sit their exams in the summer.
Masculine. Someone applying to a university; in German the same word means a school leaver.

The word, taken apart.
абитуриент (abituriyént) was borrowed from German Abiturient (EtymDB), from Latin abiturīre "to wish to leave", built on abīre "to go away", from īre "to go": a pupil about to leave school.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for a university applicant, then the Russian for entrance.

You'll want to know: факультет.
факультет (fakultét) — "a faculty (of a university)".
Сестра учится на историческом факультете. Sestrá úchitsya na istorícheskom fakultéte. My sister studies at the history faculty.
Masculine. Students study на факультете, with на.

The word, taken apart.
факультет (fakultét) was borrowed from German Fakultät, from Latin facultās "capability, ability, skill" (EtymDB): a branch of learning as a power of the mind.

Lesson 5 of 5.
стипендия (stipéndiya) — a student grant, a scholarship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for entrance, then the Russian for a faculty.

You'll want to know: стипендия.
стипендия (stipéndiya) — "a student grant, a scholarship".
Он получает стипендию, потому что хорошо учится. On polucháyet stipéndiyu, potomú chto khoroshó úchitsya. He gets a grant because he does well at his studies.
Feminine. A monthly payment to students.

The word, taken apart.
стипендия (stipéndiya) is usually traced to Latin stīpendium "a tax, a soldier’s pay" (Lewis & Short).
