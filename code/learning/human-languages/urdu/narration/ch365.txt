Urdu, chapter 365: As Things Stand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حالت (hālat) — a state (of health, of repair).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for prominent, then the Urdu for to emerge.

You'll want to know: حالت.
حالت — hālat — "a state (of health, of repair)".
اگر مریض کی حالت ٹھیک نہیں تو ہسپتال لے جائیے۔ agar marīz kī hālat ṭhīk nahīṅ to haspatāl le jāiye. If the patient is not well, take him to hospital.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
حالت (hālat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥāla, "a state, a condition".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 5.
صورت حال (sūrat-e-hāl) — a situation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to emerge, then the Urdu for a state.

You'll want to know: صورت حال.
صورت حال — sūrat-e-hāl — "a situation".
اگر صورت حال بدلی تو ہم بھی بدلیں گے۔ agar sūrat-e-hāl badlī to ham bhī badleṅ ge. If the situation changes, we will change too.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
صورت حال (sūrat-e-hāl) is built from two loans, each usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic: Arabic ṣūra, "a form", and Arabic ḥāl, "a state". The shape things are in.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پھنسانا (phansānā) — to land in trouble; to trap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a hardship, then the Urdu for a change.

You'll want to know: پھنسانا.
پھنسانا — phansānā — "to land in trouble; to trap".
اگر تم نے جھوٹ کہا تو مجھے بھی پھنساؤ گے۔ agar tum ne jhūṭ kahā to mujhe bhī phansāo ge. If you tell a lie, you will drag me into trouble too.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجھے پھنسایا, us ne mujhe phansāyā.

The word, taken apart.
پھنسانا (phansānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phãsānā, "to ensnare", under Sanskrit spāśáyati, "causes to be bound" (CDIAL 13814).
