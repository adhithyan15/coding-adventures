# der Bruder — "brother"

read already.

## You'll want to know: der Bruder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-BRUDER-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-DER-DIE-DAS-02] -->

> **der Bruder** — "brother."

A *der*-word. Set it beside English **brother** and one vowel is the whole
distance: *oo* where English has *uh*.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-BRUDER-01] -->

- `vowel-u-long` — a long *oo*, as in English *food*.
- `r-uvular` — the *r* after *B* is made at the back of the throat, a light
  scrape rather than a roll.
- `r-final` — and the ending is the soft closing *r* again.

*BROO-der*, weight on the first, exactly the rhythm of *Vater* and *Mutter*.
