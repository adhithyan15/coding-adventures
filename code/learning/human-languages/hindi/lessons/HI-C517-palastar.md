## You'll want to know: पलस्तर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C517-EENT517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पलस्तर** — *palastar* — "plaster (on a wall)".

> दीवार पर नया पलस्तर अभी गीला है।
>
> *Dīvār par nayā palastar abhī gīlā hai.*
>
> The new plaster on the wall is still wet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पलस्तर** (*palastar*) is a loan from English *plaster*.
