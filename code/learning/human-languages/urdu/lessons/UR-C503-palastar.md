## You'll want to know: پلستر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C503-SILAN503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پلستر** — *palastar* — "plaster (on a wall)".

> برسات میں دیوار کا پلستر گر گیا۔
>
> *barsāt meṅ dīvār kā palastar gir gayā.*
>
> The plaster came off the wall in the rainy season.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پلستر** (*palastar*) is borrowed from English *plaster*.
