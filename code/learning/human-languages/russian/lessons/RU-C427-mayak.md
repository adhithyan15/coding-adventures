## You'll want to know: маяк
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C427-MAYAK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**маяк** (*mayák*) — "a lighthouse; a beacon".

> На скале стоит старый маяк.
>
> *Na skalé stoít stáryy mayák.*
>
> An old lighthouse stands on the rock.

Masculine; the stress moves: *маяка* (*mayaká*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*маяк* is usually traced to a Turkic word for a pole or a marker. EtymDB lists the Russian word with the sense 'lighthouse' but no source, so the line is hedged.
