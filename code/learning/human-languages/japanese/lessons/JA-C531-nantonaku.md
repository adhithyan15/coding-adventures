## You'll want to know: なんとなく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C531-PURPOSE531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**なんとなく** — *nantonaku* — "somehow; vaguely".

> **もし なんとなく ねつが あったら、やすんで ください。** — *moshi nantonaku netsu ga attara, yasunde kudasai* — "If you feel a little feverish, please rest."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なんとなく** (*nantonaku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
