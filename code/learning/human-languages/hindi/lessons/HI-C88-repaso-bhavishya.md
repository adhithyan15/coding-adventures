# bhaviṣya kī tālikā — the future, on one page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C88-FUT-01, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the ending for *I* and the ending for *he*, and say which piece
of each one is the tense, before you look at the table.

## Grammar Lens: the grid
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C88-FUT-01, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-02, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-03, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-04, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-05] -->

| subject | with आ- |
|---|---|
| मैं | आऊँगा / आऊँगी |
| तुम | आओगे |
| वह | आएगा / आएगी |
| हम, वे, आप | आएँगे / आएँगी |

Four rows, one verb, and **-गा** in every single cell. What moves is the vowel
in front of it, and that vowel is the person.

## Grammar Lens: two slots, three tenses
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C88-FUT-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-03] -->

| tense | how it is built |
|---|---|
| now | stem + -ता + हूँ / है |
| earlier | stem + -ता + था |
| later | stem + person vowel + -गा |

The first two share a shape: the habit is in **-ता** and the time is in the word
after it. The future is the odd one out — it has **no separate word at all**, and
packs the person and the tense into one ending.

## Grammar Lens: where the full letters appear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C88-FUT-02, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-05] -->

| stem ends in | how the vowel is written |
|---|---|
| a consonant (कर-) | a mark on the letter |
| a vowel (आ-, जा-) | a full letter — **ऊ** or **ओ** |

That is the whole reason two new letters were needed before this chapter could
be written.
