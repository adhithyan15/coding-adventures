## You'll want to know: जहाति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C551-PARITYAJATI551-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जहाति** — *jahāti* — "leaves behind; abandons".

> यदि सः आलस्यं जहाति, तर्हि सः सुखी भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi saḥ ālasyaṃ jahāti, tarhi saḥ sukhī bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If he leaves laziness behind, he will be happy.

Past (he …): सः गृहं हीनवान्, *saḥ gṛhaṃ hīnavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जहाति** (*jahāti*) comes from the root √hā, "to leave, to abandon", whose present is *jahāti*; its past participle is *hīna* (MW).
