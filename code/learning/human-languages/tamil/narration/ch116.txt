5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தோட்டம் (tōṭṭam) — a garden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a beach, then the Tamil for an office.

You'll want to know: தோட்டம்.
தோட்டம் — tōṭṭam — "a garden".

The word, taken apart.
தோட்டம் (tōṭṭam) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3549). The entry lists tōṭṭam, "garden, orchard", under the native verb "to dig": a garden is dug ground.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: திருப்பம்.
திருப்பம் — tiruppam — "a turn".

The word, taken apart.
திருப்பம் (tiruppam) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3246 lists Kannada tiri, "to turn round". It is the noun of tiruppu, "to turn", from tiri, "to turn, to revolve".
