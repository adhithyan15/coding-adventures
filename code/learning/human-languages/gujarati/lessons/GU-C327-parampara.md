## You'll want to know: પરંપરા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C327-UTSAV327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પરંપરા** — *paramparā* — "a tradition".

> આ અમારા ગામની જૂની પરંપરા છે.
>
> *ā amārā gāmnī jūnī paramparā che.*
>
> This is an old tradition of our village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરંપરા** (*paramparā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *paramparā*, "an unbroken series, a succession" (Monier-Williams): what is handed down from one generation to the next.
