Hindi, chapter 226: To dry, To go wrong, To make, To be made, To break.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सूखना (sūkhnā) — to dry (by itself).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to wring, then the Hindi for to dry.

You'll want to know: सूखना.
सूखना — sūkhnā — "to dry (by itself)".

The word, taken apart.
सूखना (sūkhnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śuṣkati, "becomes dry", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12552). It is built on Sanskrit śuṣka, "dried".
