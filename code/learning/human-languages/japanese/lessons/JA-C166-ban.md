## You'll want to know: ばん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C166-TIMEOFDAY166-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ばん** — *ban* — "night-time; the evening".

> **あしたの ばんは うちで ゆうはんを たべます。** — *ashita no ban wa uchi de yūhan o tabemasu* — "Tomorrow evening I will have dinner at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばん** (*ban*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *ban*, "nightfall, evening" (Middle Chinese *mjonX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
