## You'll want to know: ميراث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C337-IRTH337-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ميراث** — *mīrāth* — "an inheritance".

> ترك الأب لأولاده ميراثا صغيرا.
>
> *taraka al-ab li-awlādihi mīrāthan ṣaghīran.*
>
> The father left his children a small inheritance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ميراث** (*mīrāth*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-r-th* (Lane), "to inherit". The *w* of the root shows as *ī* after the *mi-* of the pattern.
