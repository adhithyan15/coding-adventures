## You'll want to know: రథం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C369-RAJABHAVANAM369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**రథం** — *rathaṁ* — "a chariot".

> రాజు రథం మీద యుద్ధానికి వెళ్ళాడు.
>
> *rāju rathaṁ mīda yuddhāniki veḷḷāḍu.*
>
> The king went to war in a chariot.

Plural: రథాలు, *rathālu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రథం** (*rathaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ratha*, "a chariot, a cart" (CDIAL 10602).
