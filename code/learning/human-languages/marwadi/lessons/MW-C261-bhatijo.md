## You'll want to know: भतीजो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C261-MASI261-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भतीजो** — *bhatījo* — "a brother's son; a nephew".

> म्हारो भतीजो काल आयो।
>
> *mhāro bhatījo kāl āyo.*
>
> My brother's son came yesterday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भतीजो** (*bhatījo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhrātrīya*, "a brother's son". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *bhatījā* (CDIAL 9672).
