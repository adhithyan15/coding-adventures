## Script — two signs, and a caution worth more than the signs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-PAR-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-LEKIN-01] -->

> **ਪ** — *pa* · **ਰ** — *ra*

Both old, and the word is two letters long. The caution is the real lesson.

**ਪਰ** are also the first two letters of **ਪਰਿਵਾਰ**, *family*, which this book
taught many chapters ago. So when you meet these two signs on a page, check that
they stand **alone** — inside a longer word they are not this word at all.

That is not a hypothetical. It is the reason a search of this corpus for *but*
came back with hits and no actual occurrences.

<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

either side, standing between two clauses, which is where it belongs.
