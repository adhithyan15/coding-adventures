Bengali, chapter 399: Trips and Camps.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ভ্রমণ করা (bhrômôn kôrā) — to travel; to tour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to take a dip, then the Bengali for to splash.

You'll want to know: ভ্রমণ করা.
ভ্রমণ করা — bhrômôn kôrā — "to travel; to tour".
গত বছর আমরা পাহাড়ে ভ্রমণ করলাম। gôtô bôchhôr āmrā pāhāṛe bhrômôn kôrlām. Last year we travelled in the hills.
Past (he/she …): তিনি অনেক দেশ ভ্রমণ করলেন, tini ônek desh bhrômôn kôrlen.
A careful word, for travel writing and the news; বেড়ানো is the everyday one.

The word, taken apart.
ভ্রমণ করা (bhrômôn kôrā) joins Sanskrit bhramaṇa, "wandering, roaming about" (Monier-Williams), to করা, to do.

Lesson 2 of 6.
বনভোজন (bônbhojôn) — a picnic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to splash, then the Bengali for to travel.

You'll want to know: বনভোজন.
বনভোজন — bônbhojôn — "a picnic".
শীতে আমরা নদীর পাড়ে বনভোজন করলাম। shīte āmrā nôdīr pāṛe bônbhojôn kôrlām. In winter we had a picnic on the riverbank.
Winter is the season for it in Bengal, with a cook-pot carried out to a field or a riverbank.

The word, taken apart.
বনভোজন (bônbhojôn) joins বন, "a forest", Sanskrit vana, to Sanskrit bhojana, "eating, a meal" (Monier-Williams for both): a meal out in the woods.

Lesson 4 of 6.
পোহানো (pohāno) — to warm oneself (at a fire or in the sun).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a picnic, then the Bengali for a tent.

You'll want to know: পোহানো.
পোহানো — pohāno — "to warm oneself (at a fire or in the sun)".
রাতে তাঁবুর বাইরে আমরা আগুন পোহালাম। rāte tā̃bur bāire āmrā āgun pohālām. At night we warmed ourselves at a fire outside the tent.
Past (he/she …): তিনি আগুন পোহালেন, tini āgun pohālen.
আগুন পোহানো (pohāno), to warm oneself at a fire; রোদ পোহানো (pohāno), to sit in the winter sun.

The word, taken apart.
পোহানো (pohāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali pohāna, "to warm oneself by the fire", under Sanskrit pratāpayati, "warms, heats" (CDIAL 8539).
