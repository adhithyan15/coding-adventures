# ડેલી (ḍelī) — an entrance gateway (of a house or a pol)

## You'll want to know: ડેલી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C417-POL417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ડેલી** — *ḍelī* — "an entrance gateway (of a house or a pol)".

> ડેલી પાસે ચોકીદાર બેઠો છે.
>
> *ḍelī pāse cokīdār beṭho che.*
>
> A watchman is sitting by the gateway.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડેલી** (*ḍelī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *dēhalī*, "a threshold"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for a porch or a covered entrance (CDIAL 6559).
