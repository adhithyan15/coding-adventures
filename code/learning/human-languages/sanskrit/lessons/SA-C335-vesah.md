## You'll want to know: वेषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C335-MAYA335-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वेषः** — *veṣaḥ* — "a disguise; dress".

> राक्षसः यात्रिकस्य वेषं धारयति।
>
> *rākṣasaḥ yātrikasya veṣaṃ dhārayati.*
>
> The demon takes on the disguise of a pilgrim.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेषः** (*veṣaḥ*) comes from the root √viṣ, "to be active, work", by MW's account; MW gives *veṣa* as work, and as dress, an artificial exterior, an assumed appearance (MW).
