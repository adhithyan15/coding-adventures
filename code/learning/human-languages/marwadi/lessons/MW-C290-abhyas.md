## You'll want to know: अभ्यास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C290-SARU290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्यास** — *abhyās* — "practice".

> रोज अभ्यास करो।
>
> *roj abhyās karo.*
>
> Practise every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्यास** (*abhyās*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *abhyāsa*, "repetition; repeated exercise" (Monier-Williams).
