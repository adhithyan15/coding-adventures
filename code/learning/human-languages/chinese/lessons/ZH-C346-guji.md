## You'll want to know: gūjì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C346-THINK346-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gūjì* — "to reckon; to make a rough guess".

> *yīnwèi xiàyǔ suǒyǐ wǒ gūjì tā bú huì lái* — It's raining, so I reckon he won't come.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gūjì* is built from *gū* and *jì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *kejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a market tax' and the second 'to reckon up'. How the first came to mean estimating is not settled.
