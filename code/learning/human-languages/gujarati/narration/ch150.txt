Gujarati, chapter 150: Plans and perhaps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હું જવાનો છું — a plan already made.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I'll go to the market tomorrow. Then name the ending every verb's name wears: -વું, as in જવું.

Grammar Lens: -વાનો and the copula.
હું જઈશ says I will go. હું જવાનો છું says I'm going to go: the plan is already made.
હું કાલે જવાનો છું. — hũ kāle javāno chũ. — I'm going tomorrow. (a man) હું કાલે જવાની છું. — hũ kāle javānī chũ. — the same, said by a woman.
The pieces: the verb's name જવું, with -વું turned into -વાનો, then the present copula, છું, છે, છીએ or છો.
-વાનો agrees like my: -વાનો, -વાની, -વાનું, -વાના. So a group says અમે જવાના છીએ, we're going to go.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hũ kāle javāno chũ, or javānī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: તે કામ કરવાનો છે — te kām karvāno che, he is going to work]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — contrast: hũ jaīś and hũ javāno chũ: which one is a plan already made]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that you are going to go to the market tomorrow, as a plan already made.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which part of જવાનો agrees with the speaker? (The last sign: -ો or -ી.) Which copula follows it? (The present one: છું.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
તમે શું કરવાના છો? (śũ karvānā cho?) — asking about plans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I'm going tomorrow, as a plan already made. Then ask do you speak Gujarati? with શું in front.

Grammar Lens: the question.
તમે આવતીકાલે શું કરવાના છો? — tame āvtīkāle śũ karvānā cho? — What are you going to do tomorrow?
તમે takes -વાના and છો: respect takes the -ા forms here as it does with મારા પિતા. Put શું at the front instead of inside, and the plan becomes a yes-or-no question:
શું તમે આવવાના છો? — śũ tame āvvānā cho? — Are you going to come?

You'll want to know: the plan is off.
નથી takes the copula's place, as in every present negative:
ના, હું નથી આવવાનો. — nā, hũ nathī āvvāno. — No, I'm not going to come.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tame śũ karvānā cho?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: with હું … જવાનો છું or જવાની છું]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hũ nathī āvvāno, or āvvānī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does તમે take before છો? (-વાના.) Where does નથી go when a plan is off? (In the copula's place: હું નથી આવવાનો.)


Lesson 3 of 5.
કદાચ (kadāc) — perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: ask what are you going to do tomorrow?, then say I'm not going to come.

You'll want to know: કદાચ.
કદાચ (kadāc) — "perhaps".
A plan is sure; a guess is not. કદાચ (kadāc) usually stands at the front, and it pairs easily with the future:
કદાચ તે આવશે. — kadāc te āvśe. — Perhaps she'll come. કદાચ આજે વરસાદ આવશે. — kadāc āje varsād āvśe. — Maybe it will rain today.

The word, taken apart.
કદાચ (kadāc) is usually traced to Sanskrit kadācit, "at some time, once", built on kadā, "when". Sanskrit already let kadācit lean towards "perhaps", and that is the sense Gujarati kept.

What you've built.
One more word: the future can now be sure, planned, or only possible.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kadāc]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kadāc te āvśe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: કદાચ હું મોડો આવીશ — kadāc hũ moḍo āvīś, perhaps I'll come late]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does કદાચ (kadāc) mean? (Perhaps.) Which tense does it pair with most easily? (The future.)


Lesson 4 of 5.
Sure, planned, possible — a practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say perhaps she'll come.

The exchange: sure, planned, possible.
— તમે આવતીકાલે શું કરવાના છો? (śũ karvānā cho?) — હું બજાર જવાની છું. તમે? — હું ઘરે રહેવાનો છું. હું બજાર નથી જવાનો. — કદાચ (kadāc) મારી બહેન આવશે. — તો સાંજે આપણે ચા પીશું.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: the exchange aloud, taking each part in turn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: which line is a plan, which is a guess, and which is a promise]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
જવાની છું and રહેવાનો છું are plans already made. કદાચ (kadāc) … આવશે is a guess. પીશું is a plain future, close to a promise.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds]
[your turn — answer: તમે આવતીકાલે શું કરવાના છો? (śũ karvānā cho?) with one plan and one guess]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the plain-future question beside it: તમે ક્યારે આવશો? — tame kyāre āvśo?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that you are not going to go to the market, and that perhaps your sister will come.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word turns a statement about tomorrow into a guess? (કદાચ (kadāc).) Which form says the plan is already made? (-વાનો છું, or -વાની છું.)
