## You'll want to know: cigolare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C437-RUMORE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cigolare** — "to creak, to squeak".

> La porta cigola ogni volta che la apri.
>
> *The door creaks every time you open it.*

*Il cigolio* — creaking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cigolare* seems to imitate the squeak itself; the origin is not settled.
