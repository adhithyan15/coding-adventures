# ط (tâ) — the letter inside چطور

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-SHIN-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-SAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ش and ص once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **چطور** (*chetor*), "how".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-TA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ط** — *tâ*, the sound *t*.

It is inside **چطور** (*chetor*), "how". Like **ص**, it sounds **exactly like a
letter you know**, here **ت**. A closed oval on the line and a **tall upright**
drawn from the top down.

## Writing: ط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-TA-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop counterclockwise around the closed oval
- **2.** finish left along the baseline without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then draw the tall upright top-to-bottom — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ط demonstration at 01:54–01:56 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-SHIN-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-SAD-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-TA-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **چطور** and point at ط inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ط three times, saying *t* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ص, then ط, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **چطور** aloud once more]

come from? (**چطور**, *chetor*, "how".)
