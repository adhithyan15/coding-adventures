## You'll want to know: ਵਿਹੜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C245-GHR245-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਹੜਾ** — *vihṛā* — "a courtyard".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਦਾ ਵਿਹੜਾ ਵੱਡਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍe ghar dā vihṛā vaḍḍā hai.*
>
> Our house has a big courtyard.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਹੜਾ** (*vihṛā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vēṣṭa*, "band, enclosure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *veṛhā*, "enclosure, courtyard" (CDIAL 12130).
