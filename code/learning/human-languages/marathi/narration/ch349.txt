Marathi, chapter 349: Problems and Solutions.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
समस्या (samasyā) — a problem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for linked, then the Marathi for on the contrary.

You'll want to know: समस्या.
समस्या — samasyā — "a problem".
शहरात पाण्याची समस्या गंभीर आहे. śahrāt pāṇyācī samasyā gambhīr āhe. The water problem in the city is serious.
Feminine.
Formal; in speech प्रश्न also means an issue.

The word, taken apart.
समस्या (samasyā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit samasyā, "joining together; a half-verse set to be completed" (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for any problem to be solved.

Lesson 2 of 6.
अडचण (aḍcaṇ) — a difficulty; a snag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for on the contrary, then the Marathi for a problem.

You'll want to know: अडचण.
अडचण — aḍcaṇ — "a difficulty; a snag".
नव्या शहरात सुरुवातीला खूप अडचणी आल्या. navyā śahrāt suruvātīlā khūp aḍcaṇī ālyā. There were many difficulties at first in the new city.
Feminine. Plural: अडचणी, aḍcaṇī.
अडचण (aḍcaṇ) येणे, a difficulty comes up; अडचणीत असणे, to be in trouble.

The word, taken apart.
अडचण (aḍcaṇ) is usually traced to the root aḍḍ-, "obstruct, stop", under which CDIAL lists Marathi aḍṇẽ, "to be stopped" (CDIAL 188).

या प्रश्नावर एकच उपाय आहे. yā praśnāvar ekac upāy āhe. There is only one remedy for this problem.

Lesson 4 of 6.
पर्याय (paryāy) — an alternative; an option.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a difficulty, then the Marathi for a remedy.

You'll want to know: पर्याय.
पर्याय — paryāy — "an alternative; an option".
आमच्याकडे दुसरा पर्याय नव्हता. āmacyākaḍe dusrā paryāy navhatā. We had no other option.
Masculine.
X ला पर्याय (paryāy) नाही, there is no alternative to X.

The word, taken apart.
पर्याय (paryāy) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit paryāya, "turning round; succession, turn" (Monier-Williams). A turn taken in place of another became an alternative.

Lesson 5 of 6.
उकलणे (ukalṇe) — to unravel; to solve (a puzzle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a remedy, then the Marathi for an alternative.

You'll want to know: उकलणे.
उकलणे — ukalṇe — "to unravel; to solve (a puzzle)".
मुलाने ते कोडं दहा मिनिटांत उकललं. mulāne te koḍaṁ dahā miniṭānt ukallaṁ. The boy solved the puzzle in ten minutes.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोडं उकललं, tyāne koḍaṁ ukallaṁ.
Also intransitive: गाठ उकलली, the knot came undone.

The word, taken apart.
उकलणे (ukalṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ukalṇẽ, "to be loosened, be disentangled", under utkalati, "goes out", beside Sanskrit utkalayati, "loosens" (CDIAL 1716).
