Lesson 1 of 6.
ಸ್ಮಾರಕ (smāraka) — a memorial; a monument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to barge in, then the Kannada for to snap.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಮಾರಕ.
ಸ್ಮಾರಕ — smāraka — "a memorial; a monument".
ಊರಿನ ಮಧ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಸೈನಿಕರ ಸ್ಮಾರಕ ಇದೆ. ūrina madhyadalli sainikara smāraka ide. There is a soldiers' memorial in the middle of the town.
Plural: ಸ್ಮಾರಕಗಳು, smārakagaḷu.
Old temples and forts are ಐತಿಹಾಸಿಕ ಸ್ಮಾರಕಗಳು, historic monuments.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಮಾರಕ (smāraka) is borrowed from Sanskrit smāraka, "recalling, reminding of" (Monier-Williams), from smṛ, "to remember".

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಸ್ಮರಿಸು (smarisu) — to commemorate; to remember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a memorial, then the Kannada for a tomb.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಮರಿಸು.
ಸ್ಮರಿಸು — smarisu — "to commemorate; to remember".
ಪ್ರತಿ ವರ್ಷ ಊರಿನವರು ಆ ಯೋಧರನ್ನು ಸ್ಮರಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ. prati varṣa ūrinavaru ā yōdharannu smarisuttāre. Every year the townspeople commemorate those warriors.
Past (I …): ಸ್ಮರಿಸಿದೆ, smariside.
Who is remembered takes -ಅನ್ನು. A formal word; in talk, ನೆನಪಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಮರಿಸು (smarisu) is built on Sanskrit smaraṇa, "remembering, memory" (CDIAL 13862), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

You'll want to know: ಬುರುಜು.
ಬುರುಜು — buruju — "a bastion; a tower".
ಕೋಟೆಯ ಬುರುಜಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಸೈನಿಕರ ಸ್ಮಾರಕ ಇದೆ. kōṭeya burujina mēle sainikara smāraka ide. There is a soldiers' memorial on the fort's bastion.
Plural: ಬುರುಜುಗಳು, burujugaḷu.
The round towers on a fort wall where guns stood.

The word, taken apart.
ಬುರುಜು (buruju) is a loan from Arabic burj, "a tower", which came through Persian and Hindustani.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಹೂಳು (hūḷu) — to bury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to commemorate, then the Kannada for a bastion.

You'll want to know: ಹೂಳು.
ಹೂಳು — hūḷu — "to bury".
ಅವರು ಸತ್ತ ನಾಯಿಯನ್ನು ತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೂಳಿದರು. avaru satta nāyiyannu tōṭadalli hūḷidaru. They buried the dead dog in the garden.
Past (I …): ಹೂಳಿದೆ, hūḷide.
What is buried takes -ಅನ್ನು; where takes -ಅಲ್ಲಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೂಳು (hūḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4376 lists the older Kannada forms of this verb, "to cover, to bury", beginning with p- and later h-.
