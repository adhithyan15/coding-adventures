## You'll want to know: бедность
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C367-RAVENSTVO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**бедность** (*bédnost*) — "poverty".

> Многие семьи живут в бедности.
>
> *Mnógiye sémi zhivút v bédnosti.*
>
> Many families live in poverty.

Feminine. From *бедный*, poor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**бедность** is built from *бедный* "poor" and the suffix *-ость* (EtymDB); *бедный* comes from *беда* "trouble", Proto-Slavic *\*běda* (EtymDB).
