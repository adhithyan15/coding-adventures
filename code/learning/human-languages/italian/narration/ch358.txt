Italian, chapter 358: Il primo impiego.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il tirocinio — the internship, the traineeship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for unemployed, then the Italian for the notice.

You'll want to know: il tirocinio.
il tirocinio — "the internship, the traineeship".
Faccio un tirocinio di tre mesi in uno studio di architetti. I am doing a three-month internship in an architects' office.
Many people also say lo stage, from French.

The word, taken apart.
tirocinio is a learned borrowing from Latin tīrōcinium "first military service, apprenticeship" (Lewis & Short), from tīrō "a young recruit".

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'apprendista — the apprentice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the internship, then the Italian for the intern.

You'll want to know: l'apprendista.
l'apprendista — "the apprentice".
Ho cominciato come apprendista in una panetteria. I started as an apprentice in a bakery.
One form for a man or a woman: un apprendista, un'apprendista.

The word, taken apart.
apprendista is built on apprendere "to learn", which comes from Latin apprehendere "to seize, to grasp" (Lewis & Short), built on prehendere "to take hold of": learning is grasping.

Lesson 4 of 5.
il datore di lavoro — the employer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the intern, then the Italian for the apprentice.

You'll want to know: il datore di lavoro.
il datore di lavoro — "the employer".
Il mio datore di lavoro mi paga il corso di inglese. My employer pays for my English lessons.
Literally the giver of work. In speech people often say il capo or l'azienda.

The word, taken apart.
datore is a learned borrowing from Latin dator "a giver" (Lewis & Short), from dare "to give"; lavoro is built on lavorare, inherited from Latin labōrāre "to toil".

Lesson 5 of 5.
il dipendente — the employee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the apprentice, then the Italian for the employer.

You'll want to know: il dipendente.
il dipendente — "the employee".
L'azienda ha cinquanta dipendenti. The company has fifty employees.
As an adjective, lavoro dipendente is salaried work. Built on dipendere.

The word, taken apart.
dipendente is the present participle of dipendere "to depend", from Latin dēpendēre "to hang from" (Lewis & Short), built on pendēre "to hang".
