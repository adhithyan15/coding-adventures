## You'll want to know: la lucertola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C465-PICCOLI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la lucertola** — "the lizard".

> Una lucertola prende il sole sul muro.
>
> *A lizard is sunbathing on the wall.*

Very common on old walls in summer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lucertola* is inherited from Latin *lacertula*, a little *lacerta* 'lizard' (EtymDB).
