Persian, chapter 76: Seventeen, Eighteen, Nineteen, Thirty, Forty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
هفده (hefdah) — seventeen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for fourteen, then the Persian for sixteen.

You'll want to know: هفده.
هفده — hefdah — "seventeen".

The word, taken apart.
hefdah is haft, "seven", with dah, "ten" (EtymDB), the a of haft worn to e. Both numbers are inherited from Middle Persian.
