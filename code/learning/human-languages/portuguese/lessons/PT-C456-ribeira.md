## You'll want to know: a ribeira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C456-BOSQUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a ribeira** — "the stream; the riverside".

> As crianças apanham pedras na ribeira.
>
> *The children pick up stones in the stream.*

In old town names *a Ribeira* is the riverside quarter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ribeira* is inherited from Latin *rīpāria* 'of the riverbank', from *rīpa* 'bank' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists Spanish *ribera* and French *rivière* as cousins.
