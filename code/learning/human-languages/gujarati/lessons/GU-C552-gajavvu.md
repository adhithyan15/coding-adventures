## You'll want to know: ગજાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C552-MANCH552-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગજાવવું** — *gajāvvũ* — "to make ring (with noise)".

> જો આપણી ટીમ જીતશે તો આપણે આખું મેદાન ગજાવીશું.
>
> *jo āpṇī ṭīm jītśe to āpaṇe ākhũ medān gajāvīśũ.*
>
> If our team wins, we'll make the whole ground ring.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઓરડો ગજાવ્યો, *teṇe orḍo gajāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગજાવવું** (*gajāvvũ*) is built from **ગાજવું**, "to thunder", with the causative *-āv-* and a shortened vowel: to make a place thunder. CDIAL files **ગાજવું** under Sanskrit *garjati*, "roars" (CDIAL 4046).
