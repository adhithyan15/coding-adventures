## You'll want to know: familiāris, familiāris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C533-FAMI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**familiāris, familiāris** — "a close friend".

> Hic vir familiāris patris meī est.
>
> *This man is a close friend of my father's.*

As an adjective (*familiāris, familiāre*), of the household; as a noun, an intimate friend. English *familiar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**familiāris** is built on *familia* "household" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *familiar* comes from it.
