## You'll want to know: recuperar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C488-CURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**recuperar** — "to recover".

> Recuperei depressa depois da operação.
>
> *I recovered quickly after the operation.*

*Recuperar de* — to recover from; *recuperar o tempo perdido* — to make up for lost time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recuperar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *recuperāre* "to get back, to regain" (EtymDB).
