## You'll want to know: ರಾಜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C322-RAJA322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಜ** — *rāja* — "a king".

> ನಾನು ರಾಜ ಆಗಿದ್ದರೆ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಮನೆ ಕಟ್ಟಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ.
>
> *nānu rāja āgiddare ellarigū mane kaṭṭisuttidde.*
>
> If I were king, I would have houses built for everyone.

Plural: ರಾಜರು, *rājaru*.

The land a king rules is his ರಾಜ್ಯ, and he lives in an ಅರಮನೆ, a palace.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಜ** (*rāja*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rājan*, "a chieftain, a king" (CDIAL 10679), in its compound form *rāja*.
