# લ — the consonant la

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->

Write retroflex **ળ** once from memory. Say *lla*, then put it away. The new
letter is the ordinary *l* sound.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-LA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **લ** visible. It is *la*. Circle counterclockwise around the broad rounded
left body. Lift once and sweep the separate middle shoulder from left to right.
Lift again; descend the tall right spine and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **લ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice rounded body, separate
shoulder, separate spine; two pen lifts.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **લ** once. Compare only the rounded body and separate shoulder;
repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W05-la-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-LA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write ordinary la from memory.","answer":"લ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"લ — the rounded body, shoulder, and spine are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at લ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *la* once. Uncover **લ**, compare, and stop after one
repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LA-01, GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to ordinary *la* in **લ ળ**. Then point to retroflex *lla*.
