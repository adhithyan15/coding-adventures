Lesson 1 of 6.
じょうけん (jōken) — a condition; terms.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a possibility, then the Japanese for to miss the mark.

You'll want to know: じょうけん.
じょうけん — jōken — "a condition; terms".
じょうけんが あったら、言って ください。 — jōken ga attara, itte kudasai — "If you have any conditions, please say so."

The word, taken apart.
じょうけん (jōken) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are jō, "article" and ken, "matter" (Middle Chinese gjenX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ただし (tadashi) — but; however; provided that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to miss the mark, then the Japanese for a condition.

You'll want to know: ただし.
ただし — tadashi — "but; however; provided that".
ただし、ねつが あったら、やすんで ください。 — tadashi, netsu ga attara, yasunde kudasai — "But if you have a fever, take a rest."

The word, taken apart.
ただし (tadashi) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana, and KANJIDIC2 gives tadashi as a native reading of its kanji (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
