## You'll want to know: aedēs, aedis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C459-PORTICUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aedēs, aedis** — "a temple; (plural) a house".

> In forō aedēs magna et antīqua est.
>
> *In the forum there is a great old temple.*

Third declension, feminine: *aedēs* or *aedis, aedis f.* In the singular a temple, a house of a god; in the plural a house of people, with all its rooms.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aedēs* is usually traced to PIE *h₂eydʰ-* 'to burn', since Lewis & Short give 'hearth' as an early sense; *aestās* 'summer' is from the same root. English *edifice* comes from *aedificium*, built on it.
