## You'll want to know: అర్హత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C417-NIBANDHANA417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అర్హత** — *arhata* — "eligibility; a qualification".

> ఆ ఉద్యోగానికి నాకు అర్హత ఉంది.
>
> *ā udyōgāniki nāku arhata undi.*
>
> I am qualified for that job.

With the -తే of a condition: అర్హత ఉంటే, if you qualify.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అర్హత** (*arhata*) is built on Sanskrit *arha*, "meriting, deserving, worthy of" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
