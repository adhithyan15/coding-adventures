# फ (pha) — the letter that opens फेर

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-PA-01, MW-LEX-C163-PLAN163-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **प** once, and name it.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **फेर** (*pher*), "again". It opens on a **प** with a breath after
it: *ph*. This lesson puts that letter in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: फ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-C163-PHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फ** — *pha*, the breathed *p*.

It opens **फेर**. It stands beside **प** as **ध** stands beside **द**. Its left part
is a stem and a lower bowl joined to a central stem, much like **प**; then comes a
separate arch on the right, and the headline.

## Writing: फ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-C163-PHA-01] -->

- **1.** descend the left stem, curve right around the lower bowl, rise along the central side to the headline, then retrace down the central stem without lifting
- **2.** lift at the central junction and sweep clockwise over and down through the open right arch
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> verified three-stroke teaching form; another learner source stages the components differently

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: JackPotte, ‘Devanagari pʰ फ.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 29 March 2009.
