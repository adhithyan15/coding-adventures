Lesson 1 of 5.
ફરસાણ (pharsāṇ) — savoury snacks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to soak, then the Gujarati for to mill grain.

You'll want to know: ફરસાણ.
ફરસાણ — pharsāṇ — "savoury snacks".
દિવાળીમાં અમે ઘરે ઘણું ફરસાણ બનાવીએ છીએ. divāḷīmā̃ ame ghare ghaṇũ pharsāṇ banāvīe chīe. At Diwali we make lots of savoury snacks at home.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફરસાણ (pharsāṇ) is the savoury side of a Gujarati meal or a festival tray. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઊંધિયું (ū̃dhiyũ) — undhiyu (a mixed winter vegetable dish).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for savoury snacks, then the Gujarati for khaman.

You'll want to know: ઊંધિયું.
ઊંધિયું — ū̃dhiyũ — "undhiyu (a mixed winter vegetable dish)".
શિયાળામાં અમારા ઘરે ઊંધિયું બને છે. śiyāḷāmā̃ amārā ghare ū̃dhiyũ bane che. In winter undhiyu is made in our house.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઊંધિયું (ū̃dhiyũ) is built on ū̃dhũ, "upside down", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit avamūrdha, "head downwards" (CDIAL 804); the dish is said to take its name from the pot once cooked upside down in the ground.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ભાવવું (bhāvvũ) — to be to one's taste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for khaman, then the Gujarati for undhiyu.

You'll want to know: ભાવવું.
ભાવવું — bhāvvũ — "to be to one's taste".
મને ઊંધિયું ઘણું ભાવે છે. mane ū̃dhiyũ ghaṇũ bhāve che. I really love undhiyu.
Past (it …): તે શાક તેને ભાવ્યું, te śāk tene bhāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ભાવવું (bhāvvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit bhāti, "shines", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to please, be agreeable to" (CDIAL 9445). Said of food, the eater takes ને and the dish is the subject: મને ઊંધિયું (ū̃dhiyũ) ભાવે છે.
