## You'll want to know: raptar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C291-RAPTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**raptar** — "to kidnap, to abduct".

> Os homens raptaram o filho do empresário.
>
> *The men kidnapped the businessman’s son.*

*Raptar alguém* — to kidnap someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raptar* is built on *rapto* "a kidnapping", from Latin *raptus*, from *rapere* "to seize" (Lewis & Short).
