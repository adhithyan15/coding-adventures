## You'll want to know: ઓઢવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C154-CLOTH154-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓઢવું** — *oḍhvũ* — "to wrap oneself in, to cover oneself".

> **શિયાળામાં હું ધાબળો ઓઢું છું.** — *śiyāḷāmā̃ hũ dhābaḷo oḍhũ chũ* — "In winter I wrap myself in a blanket."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓઢવું** (*oḍhvũ*) is inherited through Prakrit. It comes from *\*ōḍḍh*, "put on, wear, cover", a reconstructed form that CDIAL takes to be borrowed from Dravidian, beside Tamil *uṭu-*, "to dress", though it notes that Burrow derives the Tamil verb otherwise (CDIAL 2547).
