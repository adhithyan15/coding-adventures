## You'll want to know: regō, regere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C385-REGO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**regō, regere** — "to rule, to guide, to direct".

> Rēx populum iūstē regēbat.
>
> *The king ruled the people justly.*

*regō, regere, rēxī, rēctum*. *Rēctus*, straight, is its participle; *rēx* is the man who rules. English *regent* and *correct*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regō* is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to make straight, to direct" (EtymDB), the same root as in *rēx* and *rēctus* (Lewis & Short). English *regent* and *correct* come from it.
