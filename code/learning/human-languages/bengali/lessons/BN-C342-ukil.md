## You'll want to know: উকিল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C342-BICHAR342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উকিল** — *ukil* — "a lawyer".

> আমাদের একজন ভালো উকিল দরকার।
>
> *āmāder ekjôn bhālo ukil dôrkār.*
>
> We need a good lawyer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উকিল** (*ukil*) is usually traced to Arabic *wakīl*, one who acts for another, through Persian. The lawyer is the one who speaks for you.
