## You'll want to know: ಸಾಮಂತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C356-JANAPADA356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಮಂತ** — *sāmanta* — "a vassal king".

> ಆ ಸಾಮಂತ ಚಕ್ರವರ್ತಿಗೆ ಪ್ರತಿ ವರ್ಷ ಕಪ್ಪ ಕೊಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *ā sāmanta cakravartige prati varṣa kappa koḍuttiddanu.*
>
> That vassal king paid tribute to the emperor every year.

Plural: ಸಾಮಂತರು, *sāmantaru*.

A local ruler under a greater king.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಮಂತ** (*sāmanta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sāmanta*, "being on all sides", and as a noun "a neighbour", later "a vassal" (CDIAL 13348).
