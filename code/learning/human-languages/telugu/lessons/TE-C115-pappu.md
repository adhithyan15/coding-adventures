## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పప్పు** (*pappu*) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil *paruppu*, Kannada *pappu* and Malayalam *parippu* (DEDR 3978). DEDR lists Telugu *pappu*, "split pulse, dal".
