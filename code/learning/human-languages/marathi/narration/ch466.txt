Marathi, chapter 466: Peace of Mind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
समाधान (samādhān) — satisfaction; contentment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be baffled, then the Marathi for to be amazed.

You'll want to know: समाधान.
समाधान — samādhān — "satisfaction; contentment".
काम पूर्ण झाल्यावर त्याला समाधान वाटलं. kām pūrṇa jhālyāvar tyālā samādhān vāṭlaṁ. He felt satisfied when the work was done.
Neuter.
समाधान (samādhān) वाटणे, with the dative; समाधानी, content.

The word, taken apart.
समाधान (samādhān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit samādhāna, "putting together; reconciliation" (CDIAL 13210); in Marathi it is contentment.

Lesson 4 of 5.
विसावणे (visāvṇe) — to rest a while.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for well-being, then the Marathi for relaxed.

You'll want to know: विसावणे.
विसावणे — visāvṇe — "to rest a while".
चालून थकल्यावर आम्ही झाडाखाली विसावलो. cālūn thaklyāvar āmhī jhāḍākhālī visāvlo. Tired from walking, we rested a while under a tree.
Past (he …): तो विसावला, to visāvlā.
A short rest on the way: विसावा, a halt.

The word, taken apart.
विसावणे (visāvṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi visavṇẽ, "to come to a halt, rest", under Sanskrit viśramate, "rests" (CDIAL 11949).

Lesson 5 of 5.
खुलणे (khulṇe) — to brighten up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for relaxed, then the Marathi for to rest a while.

You'll want to know: खुलणे.
खुलणे — khulṇe — "to brighten up".
आजोबांना पाहून बाळाचा चेहरा खुलला. ājobānnā pāhūn bāḷācā cehrā khullā. The baby's face brightened at the sight of Grandfather.
Past (he …): तो खुलला, to khullā.
Of a face, a mood or a colour: रंग खुलणे (khulṇe), a colour sets something off.

The word, taken apart.
खुलणे (khulṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi khulṇẽ, "to be open", under the reconstructed khull- (CDIAL 3945); a face that opens up brightens.
