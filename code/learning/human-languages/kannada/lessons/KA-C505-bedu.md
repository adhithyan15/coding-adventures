## You'll want to know: ಬೇಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C505-SHRADDHE505-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೇಡು** — *bēḍu* — "to beg; to plead".

> ಮುದುಕ ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಊಟಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಬೇಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *muduka dāriyalli ūṭakkāgi bēḍuttiddanu.*
>
> The old man was begging for food by the road.

Past (I …): ಬೇಡಿದೆ, *bēḍide*.

What you beg for takes -ಗಾಗಿ or -ಅನ್ನು. ಭಿಕ್ಷೆ ಬೇಡು, to beg for alms.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೇಡು** (*bēḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5528 lists Kannada *bēḍu*, "to wish, desire, beg, entreat, request", in the same article as ಬೇಕು and ಬೇಡ.
