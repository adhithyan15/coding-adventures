## You'll want to know: கணக்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C446-VANKI446-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கணக்கு** — *kaṇakku* — "an account; a calculation".

> நான் வங்கியில் ஒரு கணக்கு திறந்தேன்.
>
> *nāṉ vaṅkiyil oru kaṇakku tiṟantēṉ.*
>
> I opened an account at the bank.

Also a sum in arithmetic: கணக்குப் போடு, to work out a sum.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கணக்கு** (*kaṇakku*) is usually traced to the Sanskrit family of *gaṇana*, "counting" (CDIAL 3992).
