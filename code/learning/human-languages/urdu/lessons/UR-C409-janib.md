## You'll want to know: جانب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C409-JANIB409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جانب** — *jānib* — "towards; a side (formal)".

> دریا کی جانب ایک راستہ جاتا ہے۔
>
> *daryā kī jānib ek rāstā jātā hai.*
>
> A path leads towards the river.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جانب** (*jānib*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jānib*, "a side"; **کی جانب** means "towards", a more formal **کی طرف**.
