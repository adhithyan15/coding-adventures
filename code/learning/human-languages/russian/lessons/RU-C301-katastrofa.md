## You'll want to know: катастрофа
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C301-KATASTROFA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**катастрофа** (*katastrófa*) — "a disaster, a catastrophe; a crash".

> Это была настоящая катастрофа.
>
> *Éto bylá nastoyáshchaya katastrófa.*
>
> It was a real disaster.

Also a crash: *авиакатастрофа* — a plane crash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*катастрофа* goes back to Greek *katastrophḗ* "an overturning", from *katastréphō* "I overturn", built on *katá* "down" and *stréphō* "I turn" (EtymDB).
