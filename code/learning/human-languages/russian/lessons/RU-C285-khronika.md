## You'll want to know: хроника
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C285-SOBYTIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**хроника** (*khrónika*) — "a news round-up; a chronicle".

> Каждый вечер он слушает хронику новостей.
>
> *Kázhdyy vécher on slúshayet khróniku novostéy.*
>
> Every evening he listens to the news round-up.

Feminine. *Хроника* — a record of events in order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хроника* came through German *Chronik* from Latin *chronica* "a chronicle", from Greek *khronikós* "of time", built on *khrónos* "time" (EtymDB).
