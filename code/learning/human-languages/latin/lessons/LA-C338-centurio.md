## You'll want to know: centuriō, centuriōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C338-LEGIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**centuriō, centuriōnis** — "a centurion".

> Centuriō mīlitēs suōs ad mūrum dūxit.
>
> *The centurion led his soldiers to the wall.*

Masculine, 3rd declension, from *centuria*, a company of a hundred.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**centuriō** is built on *centuria*, "a division of a hundred", from *centum*, "hundred" (EtymDB).
