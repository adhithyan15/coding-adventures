## You'll want to know: flackern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C429-SCHIMMEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**flackern** — "to flicker".

> Die Lampe über dem Bett flackert.
>
> *The lamp above the bed flickers.*

*Es flackert*, *es hat geflackert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flackern* is an old German verb for a light that flutters (EtymDB lists 'to flicker, to flutter'); its origin before that is not settled.
