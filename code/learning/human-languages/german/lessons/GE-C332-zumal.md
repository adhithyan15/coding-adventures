## You'll want to know: zumal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C332-BEGRUENDEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**zumal** — "especially since, all the more as".

> Wir bleiben zu Hause, zumal es draußen regnet.
>
> *We are staying at home, especially since it is raining outside.*

*Zumal* starts a clause with the verb at the end, like *weil*. It adds a second, extra reason.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zumal* was built from *zu* "at" and *Mal* "time, occasion", from Old High German *māl* (EtymDB), and is usually said to have first meant "at the same time, all together", from which the sense "especially since" grew; EtymDB has no entry for the compound.
