## You'll want to know: متهم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C406-QAZI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**متهم** (*mottaham*) — "accused; a defendant".

> متهم گفت که آن شب خانه بوده است.
>
> *mottaham goft ke ân shab khâne bude ast.*
>
> The accused said that he had been at home that night.

Plural *mottahamân*. *Mottaham kardan* — to accuse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متهم** is usually traced to Arabic *muttaham* "accused", from the same Arabic family as *ittihām* "accusation".
