Italian, chapter 454: Il cibo in negozio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la salumeria — the delicatessen (for cured meats).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the herbalist's, then the Italian for the jeweller's.

You'll want to know: la salumeria.
la salumeria — "the delicatessen (for cured meats)".
In salumeria compro prosciutto e formaggio. At the deli I buy ham and cheese.
I salumi — cured meats.

The word, taken apart.
salumeria is built on salume 'cured meat', from sale 'salt', which is inherited from Latin sāl (EtymDB).

Il fruttivendolo ha le fragole buone. The greengrocer has good strawberries.
The person and the shop: vado dal fruttivendolo.

The word, taken apart.
fruttivendolo is built from frutta 'fruit', inherited from Late Latin fructa, and -vendolo, from vendere 'to sell', inherited from Latin vēndere (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la pasticceria — the cake shop, the pastry shop.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la pasticceria.
la pasticceria — "the cake shop, the pastry shop".
La domenica compriamo le paste in pasticceria. On Sundays we buy pastries at the cake shop.
Le paste here are pastries, not pasta.

The word, taken apart.
pasticceria is built on pasticcio 'pie', from pasta 'dough', which is inherited from Late Latin pasta, from Greek pastá 'barley porridge' (EtymDB).
