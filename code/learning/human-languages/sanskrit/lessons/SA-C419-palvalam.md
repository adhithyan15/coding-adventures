## You'll want to know: पल्वलम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C419-VAPI419-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पल्वलम्** — *palvalam* — "a puddle; a small pond".

> वर्षायाः अनन्तरं मार्गे पल्वलानि सन्ति।
>
> *varṣāyāḥ anantaraṃ mārge palvalāni santi.*
>
> After the rain there are puddles on the road.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पल्वलम्** (*palvalam*) is the word MW gives for a pool or small pond; MW compares Greek *pēlos*, "mud", and Latin *palus*, "a marsh", with a query, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
