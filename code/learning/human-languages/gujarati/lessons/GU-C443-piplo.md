## You'll want to know: પીપળો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C443-TREE443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પીપળો** — *pīpḷo* — "a peepal tree".

> મંદિર પાસે એક પીપળો છે.
>
> *mandir pāse ek pīpḷo che.*
>
> There is a peepal tree by the temple.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પીપળો** (*pīpḷo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pippala*, "the sacred fig tree"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8205).
