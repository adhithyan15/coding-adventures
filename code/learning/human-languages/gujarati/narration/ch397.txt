Gujarati, chapter 397: The Rush at the Door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધમાલ (dhamāl) — a commotion; a bustle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to ring, then the Gujarati for a large bell.

You'll want to know: ધમાલ.
ધમાલ — dhamāl — "a commotion; a bustle".
ટ્રેન આવી ત્યારે પ્લેટફોર્મ પર ધમાલ થઈ. ṭren āvī tyāre pleṭphorm par dhamāl thaī. When the train came in there was a commotion on the platform.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધમાલ (dhamāl) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઉંબરો (umbro) — a threshold (of a door).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a large bell, then the Gujarati for a commotion.

You'll want to know: ઉંબરો.
ઉંબરો — umbro — "a threshold (of a door)".
ઘરનો ઉંબરો ઓળંગીને તે બહાર ગયો. gharno umbro oḷangīne te bahār gayo. He stepped over the threshold of the house and went out.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉંબરો (umbro) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit umbara, the timber across the top of a door frame; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a threshold, a sill" (CDIAL 2341): the beam has moved from the top of the door to the bottom.

Lesson 3 of 5.

દાદી પાટલા પર બેસીને જમે છે. dādī pāṭlā par besīne jame che. Grandmother sits on a low wooden seat to eat.

The word, taken apart.
પાટલો (pāṭlo) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit paṭṭa, "a slab, a board"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a bench" (CDIAL 7699). પાટિયું, a plank, comes from the same word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
હડસેલવું (haḍselvũ) — to shove; to elbow aside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a threshold (of a door), then the Gujarati for a low wooden seat.

You'll want to know: હડસેલવું.
હડસેલવું — haḍselvũ — "to shove; to elbow aside".
ભીડમાં બધા માણસો બીજાને હડસેલતા હતા. bhīḍmā̃ badhā māṇso bījāne haḍseltā hatā. In the crowd everyone was shoving everyone else.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે થેલી હડસેલી, teṇe thelī haḍselī.

The word, taken apart.
હડસેલવું (haḍselvũ) goes back to haṭ, "to move or cry out violently", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to push violently" (CDIAL 13942).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભરાવું (bharāvũ) — to fill up; to become full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a low wooden seat, then the Gujarati for to shove.

You'll want to know: ભરાવું.
ભરાવું — bharāvũ — "to fill up; to become full".
સાંજે આ ટ્રેન તરત ભરાઈ જાય છે. sā̃je ā ṭren tarat bharāī jāy che. In the evening this train fills up at once.
Past (it …): તે ડબ્બો ભરાઈ ગયો, te ḍabbo bharāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ભરાવું (bharāvũ) is the intransitive partner of ભરવું, "to fill", a tadbhava of Sanskrit bhárati, "bears, fills" (CDIAL 9397): what you fill, ભરવું; what fills up by itself, ભરાવું.
