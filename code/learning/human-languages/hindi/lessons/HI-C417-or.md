## You'll want to know: ओर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C417-DISHA417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ओर** — *or* — "a side; towards (with की)".

> हम पहाड़ की ओर चल पड़े।
>
> *ham pahāṛ kī or cal paṛe.*
>
> We set off towards the mountain.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओर** (*or*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *avarā*, "the near side" (CDIAL 812), which lists Hindi *or*, "side, nearness". It is the inherited partner of तरफ़: की ओर, towards.
