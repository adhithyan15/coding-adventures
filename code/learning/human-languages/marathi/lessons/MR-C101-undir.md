## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उंदीर** (*undīr*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *undura*, "rat, mouse" (CDIAL 2095). CDIAL notes the view that the Sanskrit word comes from Austroasiatic.
