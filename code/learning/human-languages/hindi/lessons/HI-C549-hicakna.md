## You'll want to know: हिचकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C549-VIKALP549-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हिचकना** — *hicaknā* — "to hesitate, to hold back".

> बोलने से पहले वह थोड़ा हिचका।
>
> *Bolne se pahle vah thoṛā hickā.*
>
> He hesitated a little before speaking.

Past (he …): वह हिचका, *vah hickā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिचकना** (*hicaknā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*hicc*, "draw back", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 14083).
