# د (dâl) — the letter inside خدا

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ر and ز once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **خدا** (*khodâ*), "God".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: د
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-DAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**د** — *dâl*, the sound *d*.

It is inside **خدا** (*khodâ*), the first half of the farewell **خدا حافظ**. It is
the third letter that never joins forward: after **د**, as after **ر** and **ا**,
the pen lifts and the next letter starts fresh.

## Writing: د
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-DAL-01] -->

- **1.** start here: begin at the upper tip and descend through the folded shoulder
- **2.** turn left along the baseline without lifting — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 0.** The pen never leaves the paper.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, د demonstration at 01:04–01:06 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-DAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **خدا** and point at د inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: د three times, saying *d* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ز, then د, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **خدا** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-DAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — د? (**dâl**, *d*.) Which word did it
come from? (**خدا**, *khodâ*, "God".)
