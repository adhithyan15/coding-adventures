## Chapters 181-222: 210 A2 headwords, fifty-five of them verbs

The first of four Malayalam A2 vocabulary tranches: forty-two chapters of five
words, in five runs, each closed by two review lessons. Fifty-five are verbs,
which closes the A2 verb gap. The word list was drafted by theme, checked
against every headword the course already teaches, and reviewed by a
native-level reader. Every headword avoids the letters the course has not
taught (and ഏ, whose uses the script inventory pins), and words an earlier
lesson already uses in its text were left out, so the track gains no forward
reference. Level gate: 335 headwords short of A2.
