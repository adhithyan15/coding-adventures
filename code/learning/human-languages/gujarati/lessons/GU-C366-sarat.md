## You'll want to know: શરત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C366-SHARAT366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શરત** — *śarat* — "a stipulation; a condition".

> જો તમે મારી શરત માનશો તો હું આ કામ કરીશ.
>
> *jo tame mārī śarat mānśo to hũ ā kām karīś.*
>
> If you accept my condition, I will do this job.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શરત** (*śarat*) is usually traced to Arabic *sharṭ*, "a condition", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
