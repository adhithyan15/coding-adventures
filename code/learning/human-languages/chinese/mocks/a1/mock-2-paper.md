# Chinese A1 project mock — form 2 (learner paper)

This is an original, project-defined assessment, **not an official HSK paper**.
Standard Mandarin and simplified characters are assessed. Reading takes 17
minutes, listening 15 minutes, writing 15 minutes, and individual speaking 8
minutes after two separate minutes of speaking preparation. Each skill is
scored out of 100; pass requires
**60/100 in each skill** in one sitting. The examiner keeps the recording script,
answer key, and speaking prompts separate. A mock is not a lesson; preparation
lessons remain at most five minutes.

Do not use a dictionary, translator, pinyin aid, answer bank, or copyable model.
No pinyin is printed over an assessed character. Circle one answer per reading
and listening item. Write your own responses by hand for writing.

## Speaking — 8 minutes, 100 points

The examiner speaks only the prompts in the separate key. Respond without a
written script; one slow repeat request is allowed. For 43–47, make a short
answer and, where natural, a reciprocal move in 2–15 seconds. For 48, sustain
a 20–45-second exchange. Allow two minutes of preparation before starting
the eight-minute speaking clock; do not show the examiner key.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->
43. Return a greeting.
44. Say your name and ask the examiner's name.
45. Say whether you will read at home today.
46. Say whether you want water and ask the examiner.
47. Say whether you eat breakfast.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->
48. You are meeting a reading partner at a station. Greet them, ask if they
    are going to university today, respond to their answer, and ask where the exit
    is. The examiner is the partner. Use spoken Mandarin, not a written script.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->

*End of form 2. The examiner scores from the separate key and rubric.*
