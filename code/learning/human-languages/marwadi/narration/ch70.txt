Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 70: Sky, Dog, Cat, Cow, Buffalo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आभो (ābho) — the sky.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the sea, then the Marwadi for fire.

You'll want to know: आभो.
आभो — ābho — "the sky".

The word, taken apart.
आभो (ābho) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from abhra, "cloud, rainy weather". CDIAL lists Old Marwari ābhai under it (CDIAL 549). The Old Marwari row means "in the sky"; Gujarati ābh means "sky, clouds".

The word, taken apart.
कुत्तो (kutto) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kutta, "dog", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Marwari kuto under it (CDIAL 3275). CDIAL thinks the word imitates a call to a dog.

The word, taken apart.
बिल्ली (billī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from biḍāla, "cat". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 9237); the match is through Hindi billī. CDIAL lists Hindi billī under a variant it reconstructs, billa.

गाय (gāy) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from gāvī, "cow". CDIAL lists Old Marwari gāi under it (CDIAL 4147). It is a form of Sanskrit go, "cow", the same ancient word as English cow.
