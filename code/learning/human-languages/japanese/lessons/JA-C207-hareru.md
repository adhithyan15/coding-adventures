# はれる (hareru) — to clear up; to be sunny

## You'll want to know: はれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はれる** — *hareru* — "to clear up; to be sunny".

> **あしたの あさは はれます。** — *ashita no asa wa haremasu* — "It will be sunny tomorrow morning."

Polite **はれます**, *haremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はれる** (*hareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
