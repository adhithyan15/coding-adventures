## You'll want to know: जवः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C405-JAVA405-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जवः** — *javaḥ* — "swiftness".

> अश्वस्य जवः महान् अस्ति।
>
> *aśvasya javaḥ mahān asti.*
>
> The horse's swiftness is great.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जवः** (*javaḥ*) comes from the root √ju, "to hasten"; MW gives *java* as speed, velocity, swiftness (MW).
