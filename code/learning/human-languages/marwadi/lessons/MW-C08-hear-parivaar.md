# Hear *parivār* — family

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DADA-01, MW-LEX-DADI-01, MW-SCRIPT-DADI-01, MW-LEX-BAHAN-01, MW-SCRIPT-E-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 18s] Say paternal grandfather and sister, write paternal grandmother,
then write **े** once.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-PARIVAAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *parivār* — **family**

Hear three small groups: *pa-ri-vār*. Say them smoothly once. The word names a
family group; it does not require every family to have the same members.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-PARIVAAR-01, MW-LEX-DADA-01, MW-LEX-DADI-01] -->

Hear one group word and two relation words. Identify *parivār* as family, then
say it once from the meaning alone.
