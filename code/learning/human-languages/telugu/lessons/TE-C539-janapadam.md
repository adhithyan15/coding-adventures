## You'll want to know: జానపదం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C539-PADYAM539-05]; assesses=[] -->

**జానపదం** — *jānapadaṁ* — "folk tradition; country life".

> జానపద పాటలు పల్లెల్లో పుట్టాయి.
>
> *jānapada pāṭalu pallellō puṭṭāyi.*
>
> Folk songs were born in the villages.

Mostly as జానపద, folk: జానపద కళలు, folk arts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జానపదం** (*jānapadaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jānapada*, "living in the country; a country dweller" (Monier-Williams).
