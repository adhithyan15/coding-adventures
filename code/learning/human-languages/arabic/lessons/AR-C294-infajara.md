## You'll want to know: انفجر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C294-FAJATAN294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**انفجر** — *infajara* — "to explode, to burst".

> انفجر البالون فوق رأس الطفل.
>
> *infajara al-bālūn fawqa raʾs aṭ-ṭifl.*
>
> The balloon burst above the child’s head.

Present: ينفجر, *yanfajir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انفجر** (*infajara*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-j-r* (Lane), "to cleave, to make water gush out", the root of *fajr*, "dawn". It is Form VII, "to burst open".
