# غلط (ghalat) — wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C268-KHAYAL-01, UR-LEX-THIK, UR-LEX-C63-SCHOOL63-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "a good idea". (*achchhā khayāl*.) Then "fine, correct". (*ṭhīk*.) This lesson gives its opposite.

## You'll want to know: غلط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C268-GHALAT-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C63-SCHOOL63-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01] -->

**غلط** — *ghalat* — "wrong".

> **یہ جواب غلط ہے۔**
>
> *yih javāb ghalat hai.* — "This answer is wrong."

> **میں غلط تھا۔**
>
> *maiṅ ghalat thā.* — "I was wrong."

It does not change for a man or a woman: **میں غلط تھی** (*maiṅ ghalat thī*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غلط** (*ghalat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghalaṭ*, "an error". Its first letter is new to your hand; the next lesson takes it out.
