Italian, chapter 420: Ritardi e rimborsi.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
il tabellone — the departures board, the big board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the couchette, then the Italian for the guard.

You'll want to know: il tabellone.
il tabellone — "the departures board, the big board".
Sul tabellone c'è scritto: ritardo venti minuti. The board says: twenty minutes late.
Il tabellone delle partenze — departures; degli arrivi — arrivals.

The word, taken apart.
tabellone is a big tabella 'board, chart', a learned borrowing from Latin tabella 'small board', from tabula 'board' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

posticipare — "to postpone, to put back".
Hanno posticipato la partenza a domani. They put the departure back to tomorrow.
A more formal word than rimandare.

The word, taken apart.
posticipare is a learned formation on Latin post 'after' (Lewis & Short), shaped on the model of anticipare 'to bring forward'.

Lesson 4 of 6.
il rimborso — the refund.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to be late, then the Italian for to postpone.

You'll want to know: il rimborso.
il rimborso — "the refund".
Ho chiesto il rimborso del biglietto. I asked for a refund on the ticket.
Un rimborso parziale — a partial refund.

The word, taken apart.
rimborso is built on borsa 'purse', which is inherited from Late Latin bursa, from Greek býrsa 'hide, leather': money back into the purse.

rimborsare is built from ri- 'back' and borsa 'purse', which is inherited from Late Latin bursa, from Greek býrsa 'hide, leather'.
