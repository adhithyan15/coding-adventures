## You'll want to know: obligō, obligāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C398-PRAESTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**obligō, obligāre** — "to oblige, to bind".

> Prōmissō mē obligāvī.
>
> *I bound myself by a promise.*

*obligō, obligāre, obligāvī, obligātum*, *ob* + *ligō*, to tie. English *oblige* and *obligation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obligō* is built from *ob* and *ligō* "to tie" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to tie someone to a duty. English *oblige* and *obligation* come from it.
