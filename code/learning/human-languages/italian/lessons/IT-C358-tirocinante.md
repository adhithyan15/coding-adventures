## You'll want to know: il tirocinante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C358-DIPENDENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il tirocinante** — "the intern, the trainee".

> I tirocinanti cominciano lunedì.
>
> *The interns start on Monday.*

A woman is *la tirocinante*; one form for both.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tirocinante* is built on *tirocinio* "internship", a learned borrowing from Latin *tīrōcinium* "apprenticeship", from *tīrō* "a recruit" (Lewis & Short).
