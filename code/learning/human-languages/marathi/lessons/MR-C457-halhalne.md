# हळहळणे (haḷhaḷṇe) — to feel sorry; to lament

## You'll want to know: हळहळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C457-KHED457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हळहळणे** — *haḷhaḷṇe* — "to feel sorry; to lament".

> जुनं झाड तोडलं म्हणून आजी हळहळली.
>
> *junaṁ jhāḍ toḍlaṁ mhaṇūn ājī haḷhaḷlī.*
>
> Grandmother felt sorry that the old tree was cut down.

Past (he …): तो हळहळला, *to haḷhaḷlā*.

Sorrow for a loss: हळहळ वाटणे, to feel a pang of regret.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हळहळणे** (*haḷhaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
