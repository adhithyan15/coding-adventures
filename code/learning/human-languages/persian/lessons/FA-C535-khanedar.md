## You'll want to know: خانه دار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C535-COSY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خانه دار** (*khânedâr*) — "a homemaker".

> مادرم خانه دار است و پدرم کارمند است.
>
> *mâdaram khânedâr ast va pedaram kârmand ast.*
>
> My mother is a homemaker and my father is an office worker.

*khâne* 'house' + *dâr* 'keeping'. *khânedâri* — housekeeping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khânedâr* is built on native Persian *khâne*, 'house', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *dâr*, the stem of *dâshtan*, 'to have, to keep'.
