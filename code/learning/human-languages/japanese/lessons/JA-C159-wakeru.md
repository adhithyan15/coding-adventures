## You'll want to know: わける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C159-TIDY159-03]; assesses=[] -->

**わける** — *wakeru* — "to sort; to divide".

> **ごみを みっつに わけます。** — *gomi o mittsu ni wakemasu* — "I sort the rubbish into three kinds."

Polite **わけます**, *wakemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わける** (*wakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
