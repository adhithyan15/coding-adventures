## You'll want to know: afinar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C517-SOPROS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**afinar** — "to tune".

> Antes do concerto, os músicos afinam os instrumentos.
>
> *Before the concert, the musicians tune their instruments.*

*Desafinar* — to sing or play out of tune.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afinar* is built from *a-* and *fino* "fine, thin": to make a sound fine and exact. *Fino* is usually traced to Latin *fīnis* "end" (EtymDB), but the path of meaning is not settled.
