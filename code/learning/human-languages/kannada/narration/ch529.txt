Kannada, chapter 529: Stages of Life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಯಸ್ಕ (vayaska) — an adult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for dear, then the Kannada for to make up.

You'll want to know: ವಯಸ್ಕ.
ವಯಸ್ಕ — vayaska — "an adult".
ಹದಿನೆಂಟು ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ವಯಸ್ಕರು ಆಗುತ್ತಾರೆ. hadineṇṭu varṣada nantara makkaḷu vayaskaru āguttāre. After eighteen, children become adults.
Plural: ವಯಸ್ಕರು, vayaskaru.
Built on ವಯಸ್ಸು, age.

The word, taken apart.
ವಯಸ್ಕ (vayaska) is built on Sanskrit vayas, "age, vigour of life", which Monier-Williams also lists in compounds with -vayaska (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹದಿಹರೆಯ (hadihareya) — the teenage years.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to make up, then the Kannada for an adult.

You'll want to know: ಹದಿಹರೆಯ.
ಹದಿಹರೆಯ — hadihareya — "the teenage years".
ಹದಿಹರೆಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳ ಸ್ವಭಾವ ಬದಲಾಗುತ್ತದೆ. hadihareyadalli makkaḷa svabhāva badalāguttade. Children's nature changes in the teenage years.
ಹದಿ- is the "-teen" of ಹದಿನಾರು, sixteen; ಹರೆಯ is youth.

The word, taken apart.
ಹದಿಹರೆಯ (hadihareya) is a Kannada compound of hadi-, the "ten" in ಹದಿನಾರು (DEDR 3918 lists Kannada padi-, ten, in compounds), and hareya, "youth"; the origin of hareya is not settled.

ಏಕಾಂತ — ēkānta — "solitude".
ವಯಸ್ಸಾದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಜ್ಜನಿಗೆ ಏಕಾಂತ ಇಷ್ಟ. vayassāda mēle ajjanige ēkānta iṣṭa. In old age grandfather likes solitude.
Chosen quiet; ಒಂಟಿತನ is loneliness you did not choose.

The word, taken apart.
ಏಕಾಂತ (ēkānta) is borrowed from Sanskrit ekānta, "a lonely or retired place" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮೋಹ (mōha) — infatuation; enchantment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the teenage years, then the Kannada for solitude.

You'll want to know: ಮೋಹ.
ಮೋಹ — mōha — "infatuation; enchantment".
ಹದಿಹರೆಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಸಿನಿಮಾದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮೋಹ ಇತ್ತು. hadihareyadalli avanige sinimāda mēle mōha ittu. In his teens he was infatuated with the cinema.
For what: … ಮೇಲೆ ಮೋಹ (mōha).

The word, taken apart.
ಮೋಹ (mōha) is borrowed from Sanskrit moha, from muh, "to be bewildered": "loss of consciousness, bewilderment, infatuation" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಬದುಕು (baduku) — to live; to survive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for solitude, then the Kannada for infatuation.

You'll want to know: ಬದುಕು.
ಬದುಕು — baduku — "to live; to survive".
ಅಜ್ಜ ತೊಂಬತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ಬದುಕಿದರು. ajja tombattu varṣa badukidaru. Grandfather lived to ninety.
Past (I …): ಬದುಕಿದೆ, badukide.
Also a noun, life: ಬದುಕು (baduku) ಕಷ್ಟ, life is hard.

The word, taken apart.
ಬದುಕು (baduku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5372 lists Kannada barduku and baduku, "to live, be alive, make a livelihood".
