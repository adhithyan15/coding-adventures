Telugu, chapter 92: At the Doctor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
జలుబు (jalubu) — a cold, a runny nose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for where to, then the Telugu for have you eaten.

You'll want to know: జలుబు.
జలుబు (jalubu) — "a cold", the kind with a runny nose.
నాకు జలుబు (jalubu) చేసింది — "I have caught a cold", literally "a cold has done to me". Telugu keeps this apart from చలి, the cold of the weather.

What you've built.
The illness, next to the weather word you already have.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jalubu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jalubu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nāku jalubu cēsindi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for where to, then the Telugu for have you eaten, then say jalubu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does జలుబు (jalubu) mean? ("A cold, a runny nose".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
గాయం (gāyaṁ) — a wound, an injury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for medicine, then the Telugu for a hospital.

You'll want to know: గాయం.
గాయం (gāyaṁ) — "a wound, an injury".
గాయం (gāyaṁ) అయింది — "I have been hurt", literally "a wound has happened". Like a wedding, an injury is something that happens.

What you've built.
A way to say you are hurt.

Lesson 5 of 5.
నీరసం (nīrasaṁ) — weakness, lethargy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a hospital, then the Telugu for a wound.

You'll want to know: నీరసం.
నీరసం (nīrasaṁ) — "weakness, lethargy", the drained feeling after a fever.
నాకు నీరసంగా ఉంది — "I feel weak", in the frame of అలసటగా and దాహంగా.

What you've built.
A cold, medicine, a hospital, a wound, weakness: enough for a visit to the doctor.
