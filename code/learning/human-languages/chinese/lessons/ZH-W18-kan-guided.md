# 看 — copy hand above, eye below

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01, ZH-ORTHO-ZHONGWEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Read **中文** and give its meaning. Then air-write **hand above,
eye below**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **看** twice beside the model. On the second copy, pause before **目** and
check that the long falling stroke points toward the eye rather than through it.

## Guided Practice — Spaced Return
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-ORTHO-ZHONGWEN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Hear **Chinese language with 中** and write 中文 once without pinyin.
