## You'll want to know: мастерить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C502-CRAFTS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**мастерить** (*masterít*) — "to make things by hand".

> Папа любит мастерить в гараже.
>
> *Pápa lyúbit masterít v garazhé.*
>
> Dad likes making things in the garage.

Imperfective; perfective смастерить.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мастерить* is built on *мастер* 'master, craftsman', which EtymDB traces through Old French *maistre* to Latin *magister* 'master'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
