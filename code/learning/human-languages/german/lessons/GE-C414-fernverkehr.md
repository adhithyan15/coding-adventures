## You'll want to know: der Fernverkehr
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C414-PENDELN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Fernverkehr** — "the long-distance transport".

> Im Fernverkehr sind die Züge schneller und teurer.
>
> *On long-distance routes the trains are faster and dearer.*

No plural. *Der Fernzug* — the long-distance train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fernverkehr* is built from *fern* 'far', inherited from Old High German *ferrana* 'from afar' (EtymDB), and *Verkehr* 'traffic', from *kehren* 'to turn'.
