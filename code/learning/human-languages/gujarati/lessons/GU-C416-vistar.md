## You'll want to know: વિસ્તાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C416-AREA416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વિસ્તાર** — *vistār* — "an area; a part of a town".

> આ વિસ્તારમાં ઘણી દુકાનો છે.
>
> *ā vistārmā̃ ghaṇī dukāno che.*
>
> There are many shops in this area.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિસ્તાર** (*vistār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vistāra*, "spreading, extent" (Monier-Williams): the stretch of ground a place spreads over.
