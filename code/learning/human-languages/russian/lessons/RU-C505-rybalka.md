## You'll want to know: рыбалка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C505-FISHING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**рыбалка** (*rybálka*) — "fishing".

> В субботу мы с папой едем на рыбалку.
>
> *V subbótu my s pápoy yédem na rybálku.*
>
> On Saturday Dad and I are going fishing.

Feminine. *Ехать на рыбалку* — to go fishing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рыбалка* is built on *рыба* 'fish', through *рыбак* 'fisherman'; EtymDB has no entry.
