German, chapter 120: Socks, Blouse, Beef, Vegetables, Mushroom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Socken — socks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a coat, then the German for shoes.
