## You'll want to know: дремать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C488-NIGHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**дремать** (*dremát*) — "to doze".

> Дедушка дремлет в кресле.
>
> *Dédushka drémlet v krésle.*
>
> Grandad is dozing in the armchair.

Imperfective; present дремлю, дремлешь (м becomes мл). Perfective задремать — to doze off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дремать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *drěmati* (EtymDB).
