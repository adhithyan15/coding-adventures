## You'll want to know: l'auberge
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C424-HEBERGEMENT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'auberge** — "the inn; the hostel".

> Nous avons dormi dans une petite auberge de campagne.
>
> *We slept in a little country inn.*

A feminine noun. *Une auberge de jeunesse* — a youth hostel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*auberge* was borrowed from Provençal *alberga*, which is usually traced to a Germanic word meaning 'shelter for an army'. EtymDB lists it without a source.
