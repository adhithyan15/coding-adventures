## You'll want to know: тотчас
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C271-VNEZAPNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**тотчас** (*totchás*) — "at once, immediately".

> Врач тотчас пришёл.
>
> *Vrach totchás prishyól.*
>
> The doctor came at once.

Stressed on the end, *totchás* (*tótchas* is also heard). Bookish and a little old-fashioned; in speech Russians say *сразу*. Often strengthened: *тотчас же*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тотчас* joins *тот* "that" and *час* "hour", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *časŭ* "time" (EtymDB): that very hour.
