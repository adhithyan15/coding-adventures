## You'll want to know: l'invasion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C324-CONQUETE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'invasion** — "the invasion".

> L'invasion a commencé au printemps.
>
> *The invasion began in the spring.*

Figurative: *une invasion de moustiques*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invasion* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *invāsiō* (EtymDB), from *invādere* "to go into".
