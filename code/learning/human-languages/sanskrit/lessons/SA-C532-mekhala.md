## You'll want to know: मेखला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C532-UPAYANA532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मेखला** — *mekhalā* — "a girdle".

> यदि एषा मेखला सुन्दरी अस्ति, तर्हि ताम् अहं क्रीणामि।
>
> *yadi eṣā mekhalā sundarī asti, tarhi tām ahaṃ krīṇāmi.*
>
> If this girdle is pretty, I'll buy it.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेखला** (*mekhalā*) is the word MW records for a girdle, a belt; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
