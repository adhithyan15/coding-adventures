## You'll want to know: o chafariz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C441-PRACA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o chafariz** — "the public fountain".

> Antigamente, as pessoas iam buscar água ao chafariz.
>
> *In the old days, people went to fetch water from the fountain.*

A stone fountain where people once filled their jugs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chafariz* comes from Arabic *ṣihrīj* 'cistern, water tank' (EtymDB), like Catalan *safareig*.
