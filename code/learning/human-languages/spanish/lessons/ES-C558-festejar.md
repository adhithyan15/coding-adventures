## You'll want to know: festejar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C558-HOMENAJE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**festejar** — "to celebrate, to party".

> Festejamos el cumpleaños de la abuela en el jardín.
>
> *We celebrated grandma's birthday in the garden.*

Very common in Latin America; *celebrar* is used everywhere.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*festejar* is built on *fiesta*, from Latin *fēsta*, the plural of *fēstum* 'a feast day' (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-ejar*.
