## You'll want to know: मनवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C454-RUSVA454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मनवणे** — *manavṇe* — "to coax round; to win over".

> रुसलेल्या बहिणीला त्याने गोड बोलून मनवलं.
>
> *ruslelyā bahiṇīlā tyāne goḍ bolūn manavlaṁ.*
>
> He coaxed his sulking sister round with sweet words.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने तिला मनवलं, *tyāne tilā manavlaṁ*.

The person coaxed takes -ला: त्याने बहिणीला मनवलं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मनवणे** (*manavṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *mānaviṇẽ*, "to persuade", under Sanskrit *manyate*, "thinks, honours" (CDIAL 9857); मनवणे is the everyday form.
