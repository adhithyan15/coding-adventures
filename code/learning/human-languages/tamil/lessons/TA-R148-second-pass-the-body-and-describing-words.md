## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C144-QUAL144-01, TA-LEX-C144-QUAL144-02, TA-LEX-C144-QUAL144-03, TA-LEX-C144-QUAL144-04, TA-LEX-C144-QUAL144-05, TA-LEX-C145-QUAL145-01, TA-LEX-C145-QUAL145-02, TA-LEX-C145-QUAL145-03, TA-LEX-C145-QUAL145-04, TA-LEX-C145-QUAL145-05, TA-LEX-C146-QUAL146-01, TA-LEX-C146-QUAL146-02, TA-LEX-C146-QUAL146-03, TA-LEX-C146-QUAL146-04, TA-LEX-C146-QUAL146-05, TA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-01, TA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-02, TA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-03, TA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-04, TA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-05, TA-LEX-C148-QUAL148-01, TA-LEX-C148-QUAL148-02, TA-LEX-C148-QUAL148-03, TA-LEX-C148-QUAL148-04, TA-LEX-C148-QUAL148-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Tall? (**உயரமான**, *uyaramāṉa*.) Hot? (**சூடான**, *cūṭāṉa*.) Cold? (**குளிர்ந்த**, *kuḷirnta*.) Heavy? (**கனமான**, *kaṉamāṉa*.) Light? (**லேசான**, *lēcāṉa*.) Soft? (**மென்மையான**, *meṉmaiyāṉa*.) Hard? (**கடினமான**, *kaṭiṉamāṉa*.) Clean? (**சுத்தமான**, *cuttamāṉa*.) Dirty? (**அழுக்கான**, *aḻukkāṉa*.) Fast? (**வேகமான**, *vēkamāṉa*.) Bitter? (**கசப்பு**, *kacappu*.) Sour? (**புளிப்பு**, *puḷippu*.) Spicy? (**காரம்**, *kāram*.) Rich? (**பணக்கார**, *paṇakkāra*.) Poor? (**ஏழை**, *ēḻai*.) Young? (**இளைய**, *iḷaiya*.) Old? (**வயதான**, *vayatāṉa*.) Clever; a clever person? (**புத்திசாலி**, *putticāli*.) Lazy; a lazy person? (**சோம்பேறி**, *cōmpēṟi*.) Brave? (**தைரியமான**, *tairiyamāṉa*.) Deep? (**ஆழமான**, *āḻamāṉa*.) Wide? (**அகலமான**, *akalamāṉa*.) Narrow? (**குறுகிய**, *kuṟukiya*.) Dry? (**காய்ந்த**, *kāynta*.) Wet? (**நனைந்த**, *naṉainta*.)
