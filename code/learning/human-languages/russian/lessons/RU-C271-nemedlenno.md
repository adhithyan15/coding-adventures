## You'll want to know: немедленно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C271-VNEZAPNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**немедленно** (*nemédlenno*) — "immediately, without delay".

> Он немедленно позвонил домой.
>
> *On nemédlenno pozvoníl domóy.*
>
> He phoned home immediately.

Literally "not slowly". Firmer than *сразу*; common in orders and official notices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*немедленно* is built on *не* "not" and *медленно* "slowly" (EtymDB lists *медленно* as its source), the adverb of *медленный* "slow": without lingering.
