## You'll want to know: la alcaldía
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C683-REGION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la alcaldía** — "the mayor’s office; the office of mayor".

> La alcaldía está en la plaza mayor.
>
> *The mayor’s office is on the main square.*

Built on *el alcalde*, the mayor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alcaldía* is built on *alcalde* "mayor", which comes from Arabic *al-qāḍī* "the judge" (EtymDB).
