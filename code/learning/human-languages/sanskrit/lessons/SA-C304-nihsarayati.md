# निःसारयति (niḥsārayati) — brings out; gets out

## You'll want to know: निःसारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C304-RASHI304-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निःसारयति** — *niḥsārayati* — "brings out; gets out".

> जनाः तं नरं राशेः निःसारयन्ति।
>
> *janāḥ taṃ naraṃ rāśeḥ niḥsārayanti.*
>
> People bring the man out of the heap.

Past (he …): सः नरं निःसारितवान्, *saḥ naraṃ niḥsāritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निःसारयति** (*niḥsārayati*) is built from *niḥ*, "out", and the causative of the root √sṛ, "to go, run"; MW gives *niḥ-sārayati* as to cause to go out, turn out (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
