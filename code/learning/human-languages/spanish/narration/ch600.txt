Spanish, chapter 600: La sociedad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la ciudadanía — the citizenship; the citizens.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the conclusion, then the Spanish for the method.

You'll want to know: la ciudadanía.
la ciudadanía — "the citizenship; the citizens".
Tiene la ciudadanía española. He has Spanish citizenship.
It also means all the citizens together: la ciudadanía decide.

The word, taken apart.
ciudadanía is built on ciudadano, from ciudad, inherited from Latin civitatem 'city, state' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
el colectivo — the group; collective.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the method, then the Spanish for the citizenship.

You'll want to know: el colectivo.
el colectivo — "the group; collective".
Es un colectivo de artistas. It is a collective of artists.
Also a group of people with something in common: el colectivo de jóvenes. In Argentina el colectivo is also the city bus.

The word, taken apart.
colectivo is a learned borrowing from Latin collectivus 'gathered together', built on colligere, from legere 'to gather' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
el civismo — civic-mindedness, good citizenship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the citizenship, then the Spanish for the group.

You'll want to know: el civismo.
el civismo — "civic-mindedness, good citizenship".
Tirar basura en la calle es falta de civismo. Dropping litter in the street shows a lack of civic-mindedness.
Respect for shared spaces and for the people around you.

The word, taken apart.
civismo is built on cívico, a learned borrowing from Latin civicus 'of citizens' (EtymDB), from civis 'citizen'; it probably came in through French civisme.

Lesson 5 of 5.
pertenecer — to belong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for civic-mindedness, then the Spanish for the citizen.

You'll want to know: pertenecer.
pertenecer — "to belong".
Este libro pertenece a la biblioteca. This book belongs to the library.
Like conocer: pertenezco. Always with a.

The word, taken apart.
pertenecer is inherited from Latin pertinere 'to stretch to, to belong', built from per and tenere 'to hold', with the ending -ecer (EtymDB).
