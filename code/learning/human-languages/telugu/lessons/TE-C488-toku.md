# టోకు (ṭōku) — wholesale

## You'll want to know: టోకు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C488-TOKU488-01]; assesses=[] -->

**టోకు** — *ṭōku* — "wholesale".

> అతను టోకు ధరకు బియ్యం కొంటాడు.
>
> *atanu ṭōku dharaku biyyaṁ koṇṭāḍu.*
>
> He buys rice at the wholesale price.

టోకు వ్యాపారి, a wholesale dealer; its opposite is చిల్లర, retail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**టోకు** (*ṭōku*) is a market word for wholesale whose origin is not settled.
