## You'll want to know: てんらんかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C468-WORKS468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**てんらんかい** — *tenrankai* — "an exhibition".

> **びじゅつかんで てんらんかいを みました。** — *bijutsukan de tenrankai o mimashita* — "I saw an exhibition at the art museum."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てんらんかい** (*tenrankai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ten*, "unfold" (Middle Chinese *trjenX*), *ran*, "see" (*lamX*), and *kai*, "meeting" (*hwajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
