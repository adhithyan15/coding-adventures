## You'll want to know: ఫలహారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C459-PHALAHARAM459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఫలహారం** — *phalahāraṁ* — "a snack; a light meal".

> ప్రయాణంలో అమ్మ ఫలహారం తెచ్చింది.
>
> *prayāṇaṁlō amma phalahāraṁ teccindi.*
>
> Mother brought a snack for the journey.

In Sanskrit it meant living on fruit; in Telugu it is any light meal, such as tiffin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఫలహారం** (*phalahāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *phalāhāra*, "feeding on fruit" (Monier-Williams), from *phala*, "fruit", and *āhāra*, "food".
