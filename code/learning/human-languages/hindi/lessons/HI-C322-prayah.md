## You'll want to know: प्रायः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C322-HATHIYAR322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रायः** — *prāyaḥ* — "for the most part; usually".

> उस समय युद्ध प्रायः तलवार और भाले से होते थे।
>
> *us samay yuddh prāyaḥ talvār aur bhāle se hote the.*
>
> In those days wars were mostly fought with swords and spears.

Formal and written; speech says अक्सर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रायः** (*prāyaḥ*) is Sanskrit *prāyas*, "for the most part, mostly" (Monier-Williams), and keeps the final sign **ः**. It belongs to writing and to formal talk.
