Hindi, chapter 439: Heat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ताप (tāp) — heat; a fever (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for snowfall, then the Hindi for to wrap oneself in.

You'll want to know: ताप.
ताप — tāp — "heat; a fever (formal)".
दोपहर का ताप सहना मुश्किल था। dopahar kā tāp sahnā muśkil thā. The midday heat was hard to bear.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ताप (tāp) is a tatsama, Sanskrit tāpa, "heat, glow" (Monier-Williams), from the root tap, "to be hot", which comes back in a verb later in this chapter.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पारा (pārā) — mercury; the temperature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to wrap oneself in, then the Hindi for heat (formal).

You'll want to know: पारा.
पारा — pārā — "mercury; the temperature".
आज पारा बहुत ऊपर चला गया। āj pārā bahut ūpar calā gayā. The temperature shot up today.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पारा (pārā) comes from Sanskrit pārada, "quicksilver" (Monier-Williams); the mercury in a thermometer gave the weather news its पारा चढ़ा, the temperature rose.

Lesson 3 of 5.
तापमान (tāpmān) — temperature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for heat (formal), then the Hindi for mercury.

You'll want to know: तापमान.
तापमान — tāpmān — "temperature".
रात को तापमान बहुत नीचे गिर गया। rāt ko tāpmān bahut nīce gir gayā. At night the temperature dropped a long way.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
तापमान (tāpmān) is a modern compound of Sanskrit tāpa, "heat", and māna, "measure" (Monier-Williams for both): the measure of heat.

आज दोपहर को चालीस डिग्री गर्मी थी। āj dopahar ko cālīs ḍigrī garmī thī. It was forty degrees this afternoon.

The word, taken apart.
डिग्री (ḍigrī) is borrowed from English degree, of heat and also of a university; it is feminine.

Lesson 5 of 5.
तपना (tapnā) — to scorch; to be burning hot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for temperature, then the Hindi for a degree.

You'll want to know: तपना.
तपना — tapnā — "to scorch; to be burning hot".
जून में सड़कें धूप में तपती हैं। jūn meṁ saṛkeṁ dhūp meṁ taptī haiṁ. In June the roads bake in the sun.
Past (it …): वह छत धूप में तपी, vah chat dhūp meṁ tapī.

The word, taken apart.
तपना (tapnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit tapyati, "is hot" (CDIAL 5684), the same root as ताप (tāp).
