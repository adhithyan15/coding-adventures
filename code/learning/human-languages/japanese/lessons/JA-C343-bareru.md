## You'll want to know: ばれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C343-SHINYOU343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ばれる** — *bareru* — "to come out (of a lie or secret); to be found out".

> **うそが ばれたので、ちちは おこりました。** — *uso ga bareta node, chichi wa okorimashita* — "The lie came out, so my father got angry."

Polite **ばれます**, *baremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **うそが ばれました。**, *uso ga baremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばれる** (*bareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled; JMdict gives it no kanji spelling (JMdict).
