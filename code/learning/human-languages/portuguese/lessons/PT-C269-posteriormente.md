## You'll want to know: posteriormente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C269-PRIMEIRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**posteriormente** — "later, subsequently".

> O homem foi ao hospital e, posteriormente, voltou para casa.
>
> *The man went to the hospital and later went back home.*

Formal; you hear it on the news. In speech, *mais tarde* or *depois*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*posteriormente* is built on a Latin comparative, *posterior* "coming after, later" (EtymDB), from *post* "after", with the adverb ending *-mente*.
