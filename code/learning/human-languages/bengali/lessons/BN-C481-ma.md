## You'll want to know: মা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C481-MA481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মা** — *mā* — "a mother".

> আমার মা রোজ সকালে চা বানান।
>
> *āmār mā roj sôkāle chā bānān.*
>
> My mother makes tea every morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মা** (*mā*) comes from Sanskrit *mātṛ*, "mother" (CDIAL 10016), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form; মেয়ে (taught), a girl, is filed under the same entry.
