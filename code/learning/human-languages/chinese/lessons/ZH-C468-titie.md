## You'll want to know: tǐtiē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C468-GOOD468-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐtiē* — "considerate; thoughtful of others' needs".

> *wǒ bìng de shíhou tā duì wǒ hěn tǐtiē* — When I was ill, he was very considerate to me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐtiē* is built from 体 'body' and *tiē* 'to stick to', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thejX* and *thep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'the body' and the second as pawning a thing for money; the sense 'considerate' is not given there.
