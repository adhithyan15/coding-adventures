Tamil, chapter 209: Spoiled, Full, Empty, Tight, Loose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கெட்டுப்போன (keṭṭuppōṉa) — spoiled, gone off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for information, then the Tamil for a secret.

You'll want to know: கெட்டுப்போன.
கெட்டுப்போன — keṭṭuppōṉa — "spoiled, gone off".

The word, taken apart.
கெட்டுப்போன (keṭṭuppōṉa) is native: keṭṭu, "having spoiled", from keṭu, "to perish, to go bad" (DEDR 1942), and pōṉa, "gone", from pō (DEDR 4572).
