## You'll want to know: ऊगणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C168-SURAJ168-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊगणो** — *ūgṇo* — "to rise (of the sun)".

> सूरज दिनूगै ऊगै है।
>
> *sūraj dinūgai ūgai hai.*
>
> The sun rises early in the morning.

Past (it …): वो सूरज ऊग्यो, *vo sūraj ūgyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊगणो** (*ūgṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*udgāti*, "rises, comes forth"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ūgaï*, "rises, appears" (CDIAL 1954).
