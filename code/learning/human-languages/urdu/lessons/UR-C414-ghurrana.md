## You'll want to know: غرانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C414-GARD414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**غرانا** — *ghurrānā* — "to growl".

> اجنبی کو دیکھ کر کتا غرایا۔
>
> *ajnabī ko dekh kar kuttā ghurrāyā.*
>
> The dog growled when it saw the stranger.

Past (it …): وہ کتا غرایا, *voh kuttā ghurrāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غرانا** (*ghurrānā*) is imitative: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gurrānā*, "to growl", under *guragura*, a form it reconstructs from the sound (CDIAL 4207); Urdu writes it with **غ**.
