Persian, chapter 501: In-laws and Twins.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
برادرزاده (barâdarzâde) — a brother's child, a nephew or niece.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a maternal cousin, then the Persian for a sister's child.

You'll want to know: برادرزاده.
برادرزاده (barâdarzâde) — "a brother's child, a nephew or niece".
برادرزاده من تازه راه رفتن یاد گرفته است. barâdarzâde-ye man tâze râh raftan yâd gerefte ast. My nephew has recently learned to walk.
barâdar 'brother' + zâde 'born of'.

The word, taken apart.
barâdarzâde is built on two inherited words: barâdar, 'brother', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and zâde, 'born of'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مادرشوهر (mâdarshohar) — a mother-in-law (husband's mother).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sister's child, then the Persian for a brother's child.

You'll want to know: مادرشوهر.
مادرشوهر (mâdarshohar) — "a mother-in-law (husband's mother)".
مادرشوهرش زن مهربانی است. mâdarshoharash zan-e mehrabâni ast. Her mother-in-law is a kind woman.
mâdar 'mother' + shohar 'husband'. A wife's mother is mâdarzan.

The word, taken apart.
mâdarshohar is built on two native words: mâdar, 'mother', inherited from Middle Persian, with Sanskrit mātṛ and Russian mat' among its cousins (EtymDB), and shohar, 'husband'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دوقلو (doqolu) — twins; a twin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a mother-in-law, then the Persian for a father-in-law.

You'll want to know: دوقلو.
دوقلو (doqolu) — "twins; a twin".
همسایه ما دوقلو دارد. hamsâye-ye mâ doqolu dârad. Our neighbour has twins.
do 'two' + -qolu. Triplets are seqolu.

The word, taken apart.
doqolu begins with inherited do, 'two', but no source used here explains the second part, and its origin is not settled.

فرزند (farzand) — "a child (son or daughter), offspring".
آنها سه فرزند دارند. ânhâ se farzand dârand. They have three children.
More formal than bachche. Plural farzandân.

The word, taken apart.
farzand is inherited Persian: EtymDB lists it beside Avestan frazanti-, 'offspring, progeny' (EtymDB).
