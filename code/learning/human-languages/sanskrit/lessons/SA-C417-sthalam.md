## You'll want to know: स्थलम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C417-PULINA417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थलम्** — *sthalam* — "dry land".

> नौका स्थलं प्राप्नोति।
>
> *naukā sthalaṃ prāpnoti.*
>
> The boat reaches dry land.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थलम्** (*sthalam*) is the word MW gives for dry land, firm earth as against water, and also a place or spot; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
