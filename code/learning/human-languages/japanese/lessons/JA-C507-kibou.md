## You'll want to know: きぼう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C507-HOPE507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きぼう** — *kibō* — "a hope; a wish".

> **もし きぼうが あったら、おしえて ください。** — *moshi kibō ga attara, oshiete kudasai* — "If you have any wishes, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きぼう** (*kibō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "hope" (Middle Chinese *xj+j*) and *bō*, "ambition" (*mjangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
