## You'll want to know: ઘૂસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C199-QUEUE199-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘૂસવું** — *ghūsvũ* — "to sneak in, to push in".

> **એક માણસ કતારમાં ઘૂસે છે.** — *ek māṇas katārmā̃ ghūse che* — "A man pushes into the queue."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘૂસવું** (*ghūsvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to enter forcibly", under *\*ghuss*, "thrust in, pierce", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 4492).
