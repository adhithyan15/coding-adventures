## You'll want to know: پدرزن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C501-INLAWS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پدرزن** (*pedarzan*) — "a father-in-law (wife's father)".

> او هر هفته با پدرزنش شطرنج بازی میکند.
>
> *u har hafte bâ pedarzanash shatranj bâzi mikonad.*
>
> He plays chess with his father-in-law every week.

*pedar* 'father' + *zan* 'wife'. A husband's father is *pedarshohar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pedarzan* is built on two inherited words: *pedar*, 'father', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *zan*, 'woman, wife'.
