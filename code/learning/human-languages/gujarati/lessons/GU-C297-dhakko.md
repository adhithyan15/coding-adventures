## You'll want to know: ધક્કો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C297-CHETAV297-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ધક્કો** — *dhakko* — "a push".

> ટોળામાં કોઈએ મને ધક્કો માર્યો.
>
> *ṭoḷāmā̃ koīe mane dhakko māryo.*
>
> Someone in the crowd pushed me.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધક્કો** (*dhakko*) comes from *\*dhakk*, "push, strike", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun beside **ધકેલવું**, to push (CDIAL 6701). A push is given with **મારવું**: **ધક્કો માર્યો**.
