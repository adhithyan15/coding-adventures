## You'll want to know: mǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C134-THING134-05]; assesses=[] -->

*mǎn* — "full".

> *wǒ de bēizi mǎn le* — My cup is full.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mǎn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *manX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'full' and 'to fill'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
