# … কীভাবে যাব? কত দূর? (kībhābe jābô? kôtô dūr?) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C270-DUR-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "The market is far from our house". (*bājār āmāder bāṛi theke dūre*.) Then "I will go". (*jābô*.)

## You'll want to know: asking
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C270-ASK-WAY-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C82-PLACE82-05, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-LEX-C270-DUR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-KI-01] -->

> **স্টেশনে কীভাবে যাব?**
>
> *sṭeshône kībhābe jābô?* — "How do I get to the station?"

**কীভাবে** (*kībhābe*), "how", is **কী**, "what", with **ভাবে**, "in the manner": "how shall I go?". **কত** (*kôtô*), "how much", with **দূর** asks the distance:

> **স্টেশন কত দূর?**
>
> *sṭeshôn kôtô dūr?* — "How far is the station?"

To ask a stranger, start with **মাফ করবেন**, "excuse me".

## You'll want to know: giving the way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C270-GIVE-WAY-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C139-QUAL139-04, BN-LEX-C157-JAYGA157-03, BN-LEX-C216-JAYGA216-02, BN-LEX-C84-TOWN84-03, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-LEX-C79-TIME79-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01] -->

The answer often uses the polite future, "you will go", with **সোজা**, "straight on", **বাঁদিকে** (*bā̃dike*), "to the left", and **ডানদিকে** (*ḍāndike*), "to the right": **বাঁ**, "left", **ডান**, "right", with **দিক**, "a side", and **-এ**.

> **সোজা গিয়ে মোড় থেকে ডানদিকে যাবেন। স্টেশন বাজারের পাশে।**
>
> *sojā giye moṛ theke ḍāndike jāben. sṭeshôn bājārer pāshe.* — "Go straight, then turn right at the corner. The station is next to the market."

And the distance: **দশ মিনিট হাঁটতে হবে** (*dôsh miniṭ hā̃ṭte hôbe*), "it's a ten-minute walk".

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "Excuse me, how do I get to the market?" — *māph kôrben, bājāre kībhābe jābô?*]
- [YOU SAY: "Go straight, then turn left" — *sojā giye bā̃dike jāben*]
- [YOU SAY: "It's a ten-minute walk" — *dôsh miniṭ hā̃ṭte hôbe*]
