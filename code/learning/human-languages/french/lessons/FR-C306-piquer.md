## You'll want to know: piquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C306-PIQURES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**piquer** — "to sting, to bite (of insects); to prick".

> Un insecte l'a piqué au bras.
>
> *An insect stung him on the arm.*

Informal: *piquer* also means to pinch, to steal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piquer* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *piccāre* "to prick, to sting" (EtymDB).
