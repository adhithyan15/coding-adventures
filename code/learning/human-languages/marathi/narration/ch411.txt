Marathi, chapter 411: Trips and Outings.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
सफर (saphar) — a trip; a tour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a peninsula, then the Marathi for a beachfront.

You'll want to know: सफर.
सफर — saphar — "a trip; a tour".
आम्ही होडीने बेटाची सफर केली. āmhī hoḍīne beṭācī saphar kelī. We took a trip to the island by boat.
Feminine.
सफर (saphar) करणे, to make a trip; it sounds light and happy.

The word, taken apart.
सफर (saphar) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic safar, "a journey".

Lesson 3 of 6.
फेरफटका (pherphaṭkā) — a stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a trip, then the Marathi for a ride.

You'll want to know: फेरफटका.
फेरफटका — pherphaṭkā — "a stroll".
जेवणानंतर आम्ही बागेत फेरफटका मारला. jevṇānantar āmhī bāget pherphaṭkā mārlā. After dinner we took a stroll in the garden.
Masculine.
फेरफटका (pherphaṭkā) मारणे, to take a stroll.

The word, taken apart.
फेरफटका (pherphaṭkā) is an echo compound built on फेर, "a turn, a round", from the family of phirati, "turns" (CDIAL 9078), with phaṭkā added for rhythm.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a ride, then the Marathi for a stroll.

You'll want to know: हिंडणे.
हिंडणे — hiṇḍṇe — "to roam".
सुट्टीत आम्ही गावभर हिंडलो. suṭṭīt āmhī gāvbhar hiṇḍlo. In the holidays we roamed all over the village.
Past (he …): तो हिंडला, to hiṇḍlā.
It needs no goal: इकडेतिकडे हिंडणे (hiṇḍṇe), to roam here and there.

The word, taken apart.
हिंडणे (hiṇḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi hĩḍṇẽ, "to move, walk", under Sanskrit hiṇḍate, "roams" (CDIAL 14089).

You'll want to know: रेंगाळणे.
रेंगाळणे — reṅgāḷṇe — "to linger".
मुलं बागेत उशिरापर्यंत रेंगाळली. mulaṁ bāget uśirāparyant reṅgāḷlī. The children lingered in the park until late.
Past (he …): तो रेंगाळला, to reṅgāḷlā.
It hints that you should already be gone.

The word, taken apart.
रेंगाळणे (reṅgāḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for staying on longer than you should; its origin is not settled.
