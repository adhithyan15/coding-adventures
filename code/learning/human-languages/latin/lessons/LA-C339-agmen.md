## You'll want to know: agmen, agminis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C339-PROELIUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**agmen, agminis** — "an army on the march; a crowd".

> Agmen longum per vallem prōcēdēbat.
>
> *A long line of soldiers was moving forward through the valley.*

Neuter, 3rd declension: a body in motion. *Novissimum agmen*: the rearguard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**agmen** is built on *agō*, "to drive, to lead" (EtymDB), with the ending *-men*: a train in motion.
