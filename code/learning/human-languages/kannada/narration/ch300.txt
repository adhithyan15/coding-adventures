ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮ (kāryakrama) is Sanskrit kārya, "work, business", and krama, "step, order" (CDIAL 3577): "an order of business".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಾದಂಬರಿ (kādambari) — a novel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a programme, then the Kannada for a TV serial.

You'll want to know: ಕಾದಂಬರಿ.
ಕಾದಂಬರಿ — kādambari — "a novel".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾದಂಬರಿ (kādambari) is the title of a famous Sanskrit prose romance, Bāṇa's Kādambarī. The word comes from that title, which came to mean any novel.
