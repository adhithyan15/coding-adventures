# Four lines to one person — where the second needs the first

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-PHONE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson asked for writing that was **finished on time**. This
one asks for writing that **holds together**, which is a different thing.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one person who will read this. A real one.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-ATE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Connected writing is not **longer** writing. Four short lines can be
connected, and one long line can fail to be.

[PAUSE 2s] What makes writing connected is that a sentence stops standing on its
own — line two is about line one, and a reader who skipped line one would not
follow it.

[PAUSE 2s] You already hold the two words that do this, and they do opposite
jobs. **ਅਤੇ** adds one thing to another. **ਪਰ** sets one thing *against* another.

[PAUSE 2s] That is the difference between a list and a paragraph. A list can be
read in any order. A paragraph cannot.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-ATE-01, PA-SCRIPT-PAR-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **four lines** to the person you picked. Greet them, tell them
two things, and close.

At least one line must use **ਅਤੇ**, and at least one must use **ਪਰ**.

[PAUSE 3s] Use only words you already write. This lesson adds no vocabulary —
the whole exercise is the joining, not the reaching.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-PAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Cover your first line and read the second on its own. Does it still
make sense without the first?

[PAUSE 2s] If it does, you wrote a **list**. That is not a failure — it is the
stage you were at one lesson ago — but go back and make one line depend on
another before you stop.

[PAUSE 2s] And read it as the person you named would. Writing for a reader you
can picture is what makes this a task rather than an exercise, and it is what
every level above this one keeps asking for.
