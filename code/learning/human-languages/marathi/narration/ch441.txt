Marathi, chapter 441: Sunrise and Moonlight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क्षितिज (kṣitij) — the horizon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to lash, then the Marathi for to recede.

You'll want to know: क्षितिज.
क्षितिज — kṣitij — "the horizon".
समुद्रावर क्षितिज लांबवर दिसतं. samudrāvar kṣitij lāmbvar distaṁ. At sea the horizon looks far away.
Neuter.
A neuter noun: क्षितिजावर, on the horizon.

The word, taken apart.
क्षितिज (kṣitij) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kṣitija, "born of the earth" (Monier-Williams), used in Marathi for the line where earth meets sky.

Lesson 2 of 5.
चांदणे (cāndṇe) — moonlight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to recede, then the Marathi for the horizon.

You'll want to know: चांदणे.
चांदणे — cāndṇe — "moonlight".
अंगणात चांदणं पडलं होतं. aṅgaṇāt cāndṇaṁ paḍlaṁ hotaṁ. Moonlight lay in the courtyard.
Neuter.
Written चांदणे (cāndṇe), said and often written चांदणं, like other neuters in -णे.

The word, taken apart.
चांदणे (cāndṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ċā̃dṇẽ, "moonlight", under cāndraṇa, "moonlight", a derivative of Sanskrit candra, "the moon" (CDIAL 4745).

Lesson 3 of 5.
उगवणे (ugavṇe) — to rise; to sprout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the horizon, then the Marathi for moonlight.

You'll want to know: उगवणे.
उगवणे — ugavṇe — "to rise; to sprout".
आज सूर्य सहा वाजता उगवला. āj sūrya sahā vājtā ugavlā. Today the sun rose at six.
Past (he …): तो उगवला, to ugavlā.
For the sun and moon, and for seeds coming up.

The word, taken apart.
उगवणे (ugavṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ugavṇẽ, "to rise (of sun), shoot up", under udgamati, "comes forth, rises" (CDIAL 1947).

Lesson 4 of 5.
मावळणे (māvaḷṇe) — to set.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for moonlight, then the Marathi for to rise.

You'll want to know: मावळणे.
मावळणे — māvaḷṇe — "to set".
सूर्य मावळला आणि थंडी पडली. sūrya māvaḷlā āṇi thaṇḍī paḍlī. The sun set and it turned cold.
Past (he …): तो मावळला, to māvaḷlā.
Mostly for the sun and moon; the evening is मावळतीची वेळ.

The word, taken apart.
मावळणे (māvaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for the sun or moon going down; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
चमकणे (camakṇe) — to shine; to flash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rise, then the Marathi for to set.

You'll want to know: चमकणे.
चमकणे — camakṇe — "to shine; to flash".
रात्री आकाशात तारे चमकतात. rātrī ākāśāt tāre camaktāt. At night the stars shine in the sky.
Past (he …): तो चमकला, to camaklā.
A short bright shine: वीज चमकली, lightning flashed.

The word, taken apart.
चमकणे (camakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ċamakṇẽ, "to glitter", under camakka, "sudden movement" (CDIAL 4676).
