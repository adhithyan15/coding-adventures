## You'll want to know: নালা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C433-JHIL433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**নালা** — *nālā* — "a ditch; a small channel".

> বৃষ্টির পরে নালায় জল ভরে গেল।
>
> *brishṭir pôre nālāy jôl bhôre gelô.*
>
> After the rain the ditch filled with water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নালা** (*nālā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *nālā*, "a ditch", under Sanskrit *nāḍī́*, "a tube, a pipe" (CDIAL 7047).
