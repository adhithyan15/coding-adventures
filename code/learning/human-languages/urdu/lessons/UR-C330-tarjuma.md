## You'll want to know: ترجمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C330-SAFHA330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ترجمہ** — *tarjumā* — "a translation".

> اس ناول کا انگریزی میں ترجمہ ہوا۔
>
> *is nāvil kā angrezī meṅ tarjumā huā.*
>
> This novel was translated into English.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترجمہ** (*tarjumā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tarjama*, "a rendering into another tongue". **ترجمہ کرنا** is to translate.
