Spanish, chapter 660: Dónde dormir.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el alojamiento — the accommodation, the lodging.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the sailor, then the Spanish for the captain.

You'll want to know: el alojamiento.
el alojamiento — "the accommodation, the lodging".
El precio incluye el alojamiento. The price includes accommodation.
Alojamiento y desayuno — bed and breakfast.

The word, taken apart.
alojamiento is built on alojar "to lodge", which came from Occitan alotjar (EtymDB), built on a Germanic word for a shelter or hut.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: alojarse.
alojarse — "to stay (at a hotel or a house), to lodge".
Nos alojamos en un hotel del centro. We are staying at a hotel in the centre.
Without se, alojar is to put someone up.

The word, taken apart.
alojarse comes from Occitan alotjar "to lodge" (EtymDB), which is built on a Germanic word for a shelter or hut.

Lesson 3 of 5.
hospedarse — to stay as a guest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the accommodation, then the Spanish for to stay.

You'll want to know: hospedarse.
hospedarse — "to stay as a guest".
Se hospedó en casa de unos amigos. He stayed at some friends’ house.
A close twin of alojarse, very common in Latin America.

The word, taken apart.
hospedarse is inherited from Late Latin hospitāre (EtymDB), Latin hospitārī "to be a guest, to put up" (Lewis & Short), built on hospes "guest, host".

Lesson 4 of 5.
el hostal — the guesthouse, the small hotel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to stay, then the Spanish for to stay as a guest.

You'll want to know: el hostal.
el hostal — "the guesthouse, the small hotel".
El hostal es barato y limpio. The guesthouse is cheap and clean.
In Spain un hostal is a modest, often family-run hotel.

The word, taken apart.
hostal is inherited through Old Spanish ostal (EtymDB) from Late Latin hospitāle "a guest house", built on hospes "guest".

Lesson 5 of 5.
el albergue — the hostel; the shelter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to stay as a guest, then the Spanish for the guesthouse.

You'll want to know: el albergue.
el albergue — "the hostel; the shelter".
Dormimos en un albergue juvenil. We slept in a youth hostel.
Albergue juvenil — youth hostel.

The word, taken apart.
albergue is built on albergar "to shelter" (EtymDB), which comes from a Germanic compound heriberga "a shelter for an army", from harjaz "army" and bergô "shelter".
