## You'll want to know: حوالة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C452-FAR452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حوالة** — *ḥawāla* — "a money transfer".

> أرسلت حوالة إلى أختي في الخارج.
>
> *arsaltu ḥawāla ilā ukhtī fī al-khārij.*
>
> I sent a money transfer to my sister abroad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حوالة** (*ḥawāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-l*, "to change, to shift" (Lane). A *ḥawāla* shifts a sum from one hand to another.
