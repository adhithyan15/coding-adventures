## Eleven letters lift the pen where native writers do

- The writing steps for क (HI-S111), य (HI-S115), र (HI-S110), प (HI-S113), ध (HI-S124),
  ल (HI-S121), द (HI-S123), ठ (HI-S143), घ (HI-S136) and औ (HI-S135) now lift the pen only where most native
  writers do (HP Labs India's native-writer samples): once, before the headline, for
  the first nine, and five times for औ. The cited
  animations stage each letter one part at a time; their parts are still
  taught in the same order and direction, joined by short climbs up (or
  back along) ink the pen draws anyway.
- Each lesson's "Pen lifts" line, step list and source note changed, and
  the filmstrips were redrawn to match. ठ is now drawn like ट, stem and body in one
  run, and its lesson says so. घ and ठ answer "one" to "how many pen lifts".
  औ ties with ओ for the most lifts (five), so its gloss now calls it the
  longest build. ढ (HI-S137) no longer says no other letter is drawn in two
  strokes, or that it takes fewer lifts than घ.
