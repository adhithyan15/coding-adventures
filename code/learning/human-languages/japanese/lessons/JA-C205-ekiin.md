## You'll want to know: えきいん

**えきいん** — *ekiin* — "a station attendant".

> **えきいんが まどぐちに います。** — *ekiin ga madoguchi ni imasu* — "A station attendant is at the ticket window."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えきいん** (*ekiin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *eki*, "relay station" (Middle Chinese *yek*) and *in*, "member" (*hjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
