## You'll want to know: il cassiere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C457-CASSA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il cassiere** — "the cashier".

> Il cassiere mi chiede la tessera.
>
> *The cashier asks me for my loyalty card.*

*La cassa* — the till; a woman is *la cassiera*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cassiere* is built on *cassa* 'till, box', which is inherited from Latin *capsa* 'box' (EtymDB).
