## You'll want to know: সতর্ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C294-SOTORKO294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সতর্ক** — *sôtôrkô* — "alert; on guard".

> রেডিওতে সবাইকে সতর্ক থাকতে বলল।
>
> *reḍiote sôbāike sôtôrkô thākte bôllô.*
>
> The radio told everyone to stay alert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সতর্ক** (*sôtôrkô*) is Sanskrit *satarka*, 'with reasoning', and so 'cautious' (Monier-Williams): *sa-*, 'with', and *tarka*, 'reasoning' — **তর্ক**, in Bengali, is an argument.
