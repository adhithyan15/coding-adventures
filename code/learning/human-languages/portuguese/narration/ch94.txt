Portuguese, chapter 94: Shower, Bathtub, Shelf, Curtain, Blanket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o duche — a shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a fridge, then the Portuguese for an oven.

You'll want to know: o duche.
o duche — "a shower". In Brazil, o chuveiro.
