## You'll want to know: exigir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C364-PROTESTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**exigir** — "to demand, to require".

> Os pais exigem mais segurança na escola.
>
> *The parents are demanding more safety at the school.*

*Exigir que* takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exigir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exigere* "to drive out, to demand" (EtymDB), built from *ex* and *agere* "to drive" (Lewis & Short).
