## You'll want to know: dorer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C331-FOUR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dorer** — "to turn golden, to brown; to gild".

> Le poulet dore lentement au four.
>
> *The chicken is slowly turning golden in the oven.*

*Faire dorer les oignons* — to brown the onions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dorer* is inherited through Old French *dorer* (EtymDB) from Latin *deaurāre* "to gild", built on *aurum* "gold" (Lewis & Short).
