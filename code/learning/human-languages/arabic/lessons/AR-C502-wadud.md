## You'll want to know: ودود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C502-THARTHAR502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ودود** — *wadūd* — "friendly".

> الناس في القرية ودودون.
>
> *an-nās fī al-qarya wadūdūn.*
>
> The people in the village are friendly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ودود** (*wadūd*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-d-d*, "to love, to wish" (Lane). Wadūd is one full of affection.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
