## You'll want to know: كبسولة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C511-JURA511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**كبسولة** — *kabsūla* — "a capsule".

> يأخذ جدي كبسولة كل صباح.
>
> *yaʾkhudhu jaddī kabsūla kull ṣabāḥ.*
>
> My grandfather takes a capsule every morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كبسولة** (*kabsūla*) is a modern loan, usually traced to French *capsule*, from Latin *capsula*, "a small box".
