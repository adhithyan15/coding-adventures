## You'll want to know: политика
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C365-VLAST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**политика** (*polítika*) — "politics; a policy".

> Я не интересуюсь политикой.
>
> *Ya ne interesúyus polítikoy.*
>
> I am not interested in politics.

Feminine. Both politics and a policy: *политика компании*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**политика** is usually traced, through western languages, to Greek *politikḗ* "the art of running a state", built on *pólis* "a city, a state".
