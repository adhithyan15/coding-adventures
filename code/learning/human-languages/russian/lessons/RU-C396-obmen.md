## You'll want to know: обмен
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C396-TSENY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**обмен** (*obmén*) — "an exchange".

> Пункт обмена работает до восьми.
>
> *Punkt obména rabótayet do vosmí.*
>
> The exchange office is open until eight.

Masculine. *Обмен опытом* — sharing experience.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**обмен** is formed from *обменять* "to exchange", from *менять* "to change", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*měniti*.
