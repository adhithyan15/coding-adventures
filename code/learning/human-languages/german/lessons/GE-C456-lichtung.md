## You'll want to know: die Lichtung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C456-LICHTUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Lichtung** — "the clearing (in a wood)".

> Auf der Lichtung machen wir ein Picknick.
>
> *We have a picnic in the clearing.*

Plural: *die Lichtungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Lichtung* is built on *lichten* 'to thin out, to make clear', from *licht* 'light, open', inherited from Proto-Germanic *leuhtą* 'light' (EtymDB).
