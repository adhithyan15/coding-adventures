## You'll want to know: ਇਮਾਰਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C266-TOWN266-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਮਾਰਤ** — *imārat* — "a building".

> ਬੈਂਕ ਇੱਕ ਵੱਡੀ ਇਮਾਰਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *baiṅk ikk vaḍḍī imārat vich hai.*
>
> The bank is in a big building.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਮਾਰਤ** (*imārat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *imāra*, "a building".
