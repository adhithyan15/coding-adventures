# শেয়ার (sheyār) — a company share; a share

## You'll want to know: শেয়ার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C498-UDYOG498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**শেয়ার** — *sheyār* — "a company share; a share".

> বাবা একটা কোম্পানির শেয়ার কিনলেন।
>
> *Bābā ekṭā kompānir sheyār kinlen.*
>
> My father bought shares in a company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শেয়ার** (*sheyār*) is borrowed from English *share*, used of a share in a company.
