## Reading — the pinyin and the English, after you have tried
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-READ-STORY-01, ZH-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

- *shàng ge xīngqīliù, wǒ hé liǎng ge tóngxué qù shān shàng* — Last Saturday two classmates and I went up the mountain.
- *wǒmen xiān kāichē, ránhòu xiàchē kàn huā* — First we drove, then we got out to look at the flowers.
- *zhōngwǔ wǒmen zài shān shàng chī fàn* — At noon we ate on the mountain.
- *chī le fàn yǐhòu, tūrán xiàyǔ le* — After we had eaten, it suddenly started to rain.
- *yǔ hěn dà, wǒmen dōu méiyǒu yǔyī* — The rain was heavy, and none of us had a raincoat.
- *wǒmen zhèngzài zhǎo chē de shíhou, yí ge lǎorén lái le* — While we were looking for the car, an old man came along.
- *tā shuō, qù wǒ jiā* — He said: come to my house.
- *tā jiā zài shān xià* — His house was at the foot of the mountain.
- *wǒmen zài tā jiā hē le chá* — We had tea at his house.
- *dì'èr tiān, tiānqì hěn hǎo* — The next day the weather was fine.
- *wǒmen xiān qù kàn le shān shàng de huā, ránhòu huí jiā le* — First we went to see the flowers on the mountain, then we went home.
- *zuìhòu, wǒmen gěi tā dǎ le diànhuà, shuō xièxie* — Last of all, we phoned him to say thank you.
- *nà tiān shì wǒ zuì kāixīn de yì tiān* — That was my happiest day.
