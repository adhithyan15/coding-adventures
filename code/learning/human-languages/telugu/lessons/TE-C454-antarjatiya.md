# అంతర్జాతీయ (antarjātīya) — international

## You'll want to know: అంతర్జాతీయ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C454-PAILATU454-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతర్జాతీయ** — *antarjātīya* — "international".

> మా నగరంలో కొత్త అంతర్జాతీయ విమానాశ్రయం ఉంది.
>
> *mā nagaraṁlō kotta antarjātīya vimānāśrayaṁ undi.*
>
> There is a new international airport in our city.

An adjective that stands before its noun and does not change: అంతర్జాతీయ విమానం, an international flight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతర్జాతీయ** (*antarjātīya*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit: *antar*, "between", *jāti*, "birth, a people, a nation", and the adjective ending *-īya* (Monier-Williams): between the nations.
