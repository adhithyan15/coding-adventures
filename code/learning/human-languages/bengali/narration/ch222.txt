Bengali, chapter 222: Light bulb, Television, Remote control, Laptop, Printer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বাল্ব (bālb) — a light bulb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for urban, then the Bengali for regular.

You'll want to know: বাল্ব.
বাল্ব — bālb — "a light bulb".

The word, taken apart.
বাল্ব (bālb) is a loan from English bulb.
