## You'll want to know: ఓటు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C319-OTU-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C180-GUNAM180-03, TE-LEX-C75-WHEN-02] -->

**ఓటు** — *ōṭu* — "a vote".

> **ప్రతి ఒక్కరూ ఓటు వేయాలి.**
>
> *prati okkarū ōṭu vēyāli.* — "Everyone must vote."

> **నేను నిన్న ఓటు వేశాను.**
>
> *nēnu ninna ōṭu vēśānu.* — "I voted yesterday."

A vote is "put": **ఓటు వేయు** (*ōṭu vēyu*, "to cast a vote"), with **వేయు**, "to put". **ప్రతి ఒక్కరూ** (*prati okkarū*, "every single person") is **ప్రతి**, "every", with a word for "one person".

Its first letter, a long *ō* at the start of a word, is one you have not written. The next lesson takes it out
of this word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఓటు** (*ōṭu*) is a loan from English *vote*, with the **-ఉ** that Telugu gives most borrowed nouns, as in
**బస్సు** and **టిక్కెట్టు**. The English *v* drops away, the vowel stays long, and the English *t* becomes
**ట**, as it does in **టిక్కెట్టు**.
