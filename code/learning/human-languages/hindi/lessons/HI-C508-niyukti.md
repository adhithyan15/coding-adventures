## You'll want to know: नियुक्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C508-NAUKRI508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नियुक्ति** — *niyukti* — "an appointment (to a post)".

> अगले साल मेरी नियुक्ति एक नए स्कूल में होगी।
>
> *Agle sāl merī niyukti ek nae skūl meṁ hogī.*
>
> Next year I will be appointed to a new school.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियुक्ति** (*niyukti*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *niyukti*, "a charge, an office" (Monier-Williams).
