## You'll want to know: هشدار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C330-TUFAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**هشدار** (*hoshdâr*) — "a warning, an alert".

> رادیو هشدار طوفان داد.
>
> *Râdiyo hoshdâr-e tufân dâd.*
>
> The radio gave a storm warning.

*hosh* "mind, attention" + *dâr* "holding": keep your mind on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hoshdâr* joins *hosh* "mind, attention", inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *dâr-*, the present stem of *dâshtan* "to hold".
