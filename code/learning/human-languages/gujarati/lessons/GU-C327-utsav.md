## You'll want to know: ઉત્સવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C327-UTSAV327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્સવ** — *utsav* — "a celebration".

> ગામમાં ત્રણ દિવસનો ઉત્સવ હતો.
>
> *gāmmā̃ traṇ divasno utsav hato.*
>
> There was a three-day celebration in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is a tatsama, Sanskrit *utsava*, "a festival, a joyful occasion" (Monier-Williams). **તહેવાર** is a festival of the calendar; a **ઉત્સવ** is any celebration, big or small.
