## You'll want to know: சகி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C461-PORUMAI461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சகி** — *caki* — "to tolerate; to put up with".

> அவனுடைய கெட்ட பழக்கத்தை என்னால் சகிக்க முடியவில்லை.
>
> *avaṉuṭaiya keṭṭa paḻakkattai eṉṉāl cakikka muṭiyavillai.*
>
> I could not tolerate his bad habit.

Past (I …): சகித்தேன், *cakittēṉ*.

With என்னால் … முடியவில்லை: I could not bear it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சகி** (*caki*) is borrowed from the Sanskrit verb *sahatē*, "bears, suffers" (CDIAL 13304); Tamil turns the root into a verb in -இ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
