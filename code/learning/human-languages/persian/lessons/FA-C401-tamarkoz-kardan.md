## You'll want to know: تمرکز کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C401-MORUR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تمرکز کردن** (*tamarkoz kardan*) — "to concentrate".

> وقتی تلویزیون روشن است نمیتوانم تمرکز کنم.
>
> *vaqti televiziyon roshan ast nemitavânam tamarkoz konam.*
>
> When the television is on I cannot concentrate.

*Tamarkoz* "concentration" + *kardan*. It takes *ruye*: *ruye chizi tamarkoz kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمرکز** is usually traced to Arabic *tamarkuz*, a verbal noun built on *markaz* "centre": to concentrate is to gather at the centre.
