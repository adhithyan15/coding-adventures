## You'll want to know: تقاطع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C445-TAGHATO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تقاطع** (*taqâto'*) — "a crossroads, an intersection".

> تاکسی در تقاطع دوم به راست پیچید.
>
> *Tâksi dar taqâto'-e dovvom be râst pichid.*
>
> The taxi turned right at the second crossroads.

More formal than *chahârrâh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taqâto'* was borrowed from Arabic *taqāṭuʿ*, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'cutting across each other'.
