## You'll want to know: ਛੱਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C306-WINNOW306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੱਟਣਾ** — *chhaṭṭṇā* — "to winnow".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਣੇ ਛੱਟਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ havā vich dāṇe chhaṭṭāṅge.*
>
> We will winnow the grain in the wind.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦਾਣੇ ਛੱਟੇ, *maiṁ dāṇe chhaṭṭe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੱਟਣਾ** (*chhaṭṭṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*chaṭṭ*, "scatter"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chaṭṭṇā*, "to winnow", under it (CDIAL 4968).
