## You'll want to know: じどうしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C202-DRIVE202-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じどうしゃ** — *jidōsha* — "an automobile; a motor car".

> **ちちの じどうしゃは くろいです。** — *chichi no jidōsha wa kuroi desu* — "My father's car is black."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じどうしゃ** (*jidōsha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "self" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*), *dō*, "to move" (*duwngX*) and *sha*, "cart" (*tsyhae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
