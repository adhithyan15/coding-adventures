## You'll want to know: cháyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C166-TEA166-04]; assesses=[] -->

*cháyè* — "tea leaves".

> *cháyè zài nǎr* — Where are the tea leaves?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cháyè* is built from 茶 'tea' and *yè* 'leaf', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drae* and *yep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the leaves of a branch'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
