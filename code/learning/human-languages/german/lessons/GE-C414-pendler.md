## You'll want to know: der Pendler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C414-PENDELN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Pendler** — "the commuter".

> Am Morgen ist der Zug voll mit Pendlern.
>
> *In the morning the train is full of commuters.*

Same form in the plural. A woman: *die Pendlerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pendler* is built on *pendeln* 'to commute' (EtymDB), from *Pendel* 'pendulum', which goes back to Latin *pendulus* 'hanging' (Lewis & Short).
