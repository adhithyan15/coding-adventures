## You'll want to know: नक़्क़ाशी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C528-SHILP528-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नक़्क़ाशी** — *naqqāśī* — "carving; engraved decoration".

> महल के दरवाज़ों पर सुंदर नक़्क़ाशी है।
>
> *Mahal ke darvāzoṁ par sundar naqqāśī hai.*
>
> There is beautiful carving on the palace doors.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नक़्क़ाशी** (*naqqāśī*) is usually traced to Arabic *naqqāsh*, "a painter, an engraver", through Persian. Hindi adds -ī for the work itself.
