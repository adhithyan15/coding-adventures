## You'll want to know: はば
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C394-SAKAMICHI394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はば** — *haba* — "width".

> **この みちは はばが せまいです。** — *kono michi wa haba ga semai desu* — "This road is narrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はば** (*haba*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
