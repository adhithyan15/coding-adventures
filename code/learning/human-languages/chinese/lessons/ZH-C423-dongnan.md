## You'll want to know: 东南
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C423-SOUTH423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**东南** — *dōngnán* — "southeast".

> *huǒchēzhàn zài jīchǎng de dōngnán fāngxiàng* — The railway station is southeast of the airport.

The new character, in pieces: 南 — nine strokes: 十 on top; under it a frame open at the foot, a vertical down the left and a bar that turns down the right side and hooks; inside it a dot and a short stroke falling to the left, two bars, and a vertical down through them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**东南** (*dōngnán*) is built from 东 'east' and 南 'south', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tuwng* and *nom* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the direction of fire.
