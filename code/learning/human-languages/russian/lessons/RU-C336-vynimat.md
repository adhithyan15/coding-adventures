## You'll want to know: вынимать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C336-GOTOVO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**вынимать** (*vynimát*) — "to take out".

> Мама вынула пирог из духовки.
>
> *Máma výnula piróg iz dukhóvki.*
>
> Mum took the pie out of the oven.

Imperfective; perfective *вынуть* (*výnut*), *выну*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вынимать* is built on *вы-* "out" and the old Slavic root *-ним-*, *-ять* "to take", seen in *взять* and *понимать*; EtymDB traces this root to PIE *h₁em-*.
