## You'll want to know: bewirten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C307-GASTGEBER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bewirten** — "to treat guests, to serve food and drink to".

> Sie bewirtete ihre Gäste mit Kaffee und Kuchen.
>
> *She treated her guests to coffee and cake.*

Built on *der Wirt*, the landlord of an inn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bewirten* is inherited from Middle High German *bewirten* (EtymDB), built on *Wirt* "host", Old High German *wirt*, Proto-Germanic *werduz* "host".
