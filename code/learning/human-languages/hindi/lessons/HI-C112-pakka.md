## You'll want to know: पक्का
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C112-RIGHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्का** (*pakkā*) — "sure, certain", and of a thing, "firm, ripe".

**पक्का?** — "for sure?". **पक्का!** — "definitely!".
