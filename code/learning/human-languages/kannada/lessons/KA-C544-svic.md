## You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಿಚ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C544-VIDYUT544-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಿಚ್** — *svic* — "a switch".

> ಬಾಗಿಲಿನ ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ ದೀಪದ ಸ್ವಿಚ್ ಇದೆ.
>
> *bāgilina pakkadalli dīpada svic ide.*
>
> The light switch is beside the door.

ಸ್ವಿಚ್ ಹಾಕು, to switch on; ಆನ್ ಮಾಡು is also said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಿಚ್** (*svic*) is a loan from English *switch*, written with a final virama as Kannada writes many English loans.
