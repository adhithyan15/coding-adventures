# मेरी दिनचर्या — the minute you are asked to fill

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-03, HI-LEX-C88-TIME-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say what time you get up, using the clock word from the future
chapter. That is the first line of what follows.

## Reading: a whole day
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-01, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-02, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-03, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-04, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-05, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-06, HI-CONCEPT-C05-BOLTAHUN-01] -->

> पहले मैं सुबह सात बजे उठती हूँ। मैं बस से काम पर जाती हूँ। काम के बाद मैं घर
> आती हूँ। घर पर मैं हिंदी बोलती हूँ।

Four sentences, and **every word in them is one you have been taught**. Read it
twice — once for meaning, once listening for how the joints do the work.

## Grammar Lens: what is holding it together
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-05, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-06, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-01] -->

| joint | what it does |
|---|---|
| पहले | opens the sequence |
| से | names what carries her |
| पर | names where she ends up |
| के बाद | hooks sentence three to sentence two |

Take those four out and you have four unrelated statements. Put them back and
you have a **description**, which is what the one-minute task is graded on.

## How to answer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-03, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-04, HI-LEX-C88-TIME-01] -->

Say your own, out loud, in four sentences:

- what time you get up
- how you travel, and where to
- what you do after that
- what you do once you are home

[PAUSE 6s]

Say it a second time and change nothing but the endings, as the other gender
would. The habit endings and the joints stay exactly where they are.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-05, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-06, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which four words turn four statements into a description? (**पहले**,
**से**, **पर**, **के बाद**.) Which tense is a routine told in? (The habit, with
**-ता**.) What changes when a woman says the same day? (Only the endings.)
