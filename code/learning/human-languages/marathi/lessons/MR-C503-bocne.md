## You'll want to know: बोचणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C503-MAPH503-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बोचणे** — *bocṇe* — "to rankle; to prick".

> त्याचे कडू शब्द मला अजून बोचतात.
>
> *tyāce kaḍū śabda malā ajūn boctāt.*
>
> His bitter words still rankle with me.

Past (to him …): त्याला ते बोचलं, *tyālā te boclaṁ*.

Of thorns and of words; the one hurt takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोचणे** (*bocṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
