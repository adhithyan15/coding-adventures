Telugu, chapter 358: Making a Life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
జీవించు (jīviñcu) — to live; to lead a life.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an orphan, then the Telugu for to survive.

You'll want to know: జీవించు.
జీవించు — jīviñcu — "to live; to lead a life".
అతను వంద సంవత్సరాలు జీవించాడు. atanu vanda saṁvatsarālu jīviñcāḍu. He lived for a hundred years.
Past (I …): జీవించాను, jīviñcānu.
A formal partner of బతుకు.

The word, taken apart.
జీవించు (jīviñcu) is built on Sanskrit jīvati, "is alive" (CDIAL 5241), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
తలదాచుకో (taladācukō) — to take shelter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to live, then the Telugu for to settle down.

You'll want to know: తలదాచుకో.
తలదాచుకో — taladācukō — "to take shelter".
వర్షం వచ్చి మేము ఒక గుడిలో తలదాచుకున్నాము. varṣaṁ vacci mēmu oka guḍilō taladācukunnāmu. It rained, and we took shelter in a temple.
Past (I …): తలదాచుకున్నాను, taladācukunnānu.
Literally to hide one's head: తల, head, and దాచుకో, to hide for oneself.

The word, taken apart.
తలదాచుకో (taladācukō) is a native compound: tala, "the head" (DEDR 3103), and dācu, "to hide" (DEDR 63), with the self-ending -kō.

Lesson 4 of 5.
కలగను (kalaganu) — to dream of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to settle down, then the Telugu for to take shelter.

You'll want to know: కలగను.
కలగను — kalaganu — "to dream of".
చిన్నప్పుడు నేను డాక్టరు కావాలని కలగన్నాను. cinnappuḍu nēnu ḍākṭaru kāvālani kalagannānu. As a child I dreamed of becoming a doctor.
Past (I …): కలగన్నాను, kalagannānu.
కల, a dream, with కను, to see: to see a dream.

The word, taken apart.
కలగను (kalaganu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1407 lists Telugu kala, "a dream", and kalaganu, "to dream".

Lesson 5 of 5.
అదృష్టవశాత్తు (adṛṣṭavaśāttu) — luckily; fortunately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to take shelter, then the Telugu for to dream of.

You'll want to know: అదృష్టవశాత్తు.
అదృష్టవశాత్తు — adṛṣṭavaśāttu — "luckily; fortunately".
అదృష్టవశాత్తు ఆ ప్రమాదంలో ఎవరికీ ఏమీ కాలేదు. adṛṣṭavaśāttu ā pramādaṁlō evarikī ēmī kālēdu. Luckily nobody was hurt in that accident.
అదృష్టం, luck, with వశాత్తు, by the power of.

The word, taken apart.
అదృష్టవశాత్తు (adṛṣṭavaśāttu) is built from Sanskrit adṛṣṭa, "unseen, unforeseen" (Monier-Williams), and vaśāt, "by force of", from vaśa, "will, power" (CDIAL 11430).
