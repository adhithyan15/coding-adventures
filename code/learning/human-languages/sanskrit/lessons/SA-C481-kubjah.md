## You'll want to know: कुब्जः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C481-ANDHA481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कुब्जः** — *kubjaḥ* — "hunchbacked".

> कुब्जः वृद्धः यष्ट्या चलति।
>
> *kubjaḥ vṛddhaḥ yaṣṭyā calati.*
>
> The hunchbacked old man walks with a staff.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुब्जः** (*kubjaḥ*) MW gives "hump-backed, crooked", from the Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā on; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
