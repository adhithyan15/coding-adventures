Kannada, chapter 510: Aches and Swellings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನೋಯು (nōyu) — to ache; to be hurt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an arm, then the Kannada for to limp.

You'll want to know: ನೋಯು.
ನೋಯು — nōyu — "to ache; to be hurt".
ತುಂಬಾ ನಡೆದಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನನ್ನ ಕಾಲು ನೋಯುತ್ತಿದೆ. tumbā naḍediddarinda nanna kālu nōyuttide. My leg aches because I walked a lot.
Past (I …): ನೊಂದೆ, nonde.
The noun is ನೋವು, pain. Feelings ache too: ಅವನ ಮಾತಿನಿಂದ ನಾನು ನೊಂದೆ, his words hurt me.

The word, taken apart.
ನೋಯು (nōyu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3793 lists Kannada nōyu, "to feel pain, be grieved", beside Tamil nō. The noun ನೋವು, pain, is built on it.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಜೋಮು (jōmu) — numbness; pins and needles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to limp, then the Kannada for to ache.

You'll want to know: ಜೋಮು.
ಜೋಮು — jōmu — "numbness; pins and needles".
ತುಂಬಾ ಹೊತ್ತು ಕುಳಿತಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನನ್ನ ಕಾಲಿಗೆ ಜೋಮು ಹಿಡಿಯಿತು. tumbā hottu kuḷitiddarinda nanna kālige jōmu hiḍiyitu. From sitting too long, my leg went numb.
ಜೋಮು (jōmu) ಹಿಡಿ, to go numb: the numbness "grips" the limb.

The word, taken apart.
ಜೋಮು (jōmu) is native Dravidian and imitative: DEDR 2578 lists Kannada jum, jummu and also jommu, jōmu for the tingling numbness of a limb kept long in one position.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಉಬ್ಬು (ubbu) — to swell; to bulge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to ache, then the Kannada for numbness.

You'll want to know: ಉಬ್ಬು.
ಉಬ್ಬು — ubbu — "to swell; to bulge".
ಜೇನುನೊಣ ಕಚ್ಚಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನ ಕೈ ಉಬ್ಬಿತು. jēnunoṇa kaccida mēle avana kai ubbitu. After the bee stung him, his hand swelled.
Past (it …): ಉಬ್ಬಿತು, ubbitu.
ಊತ is a swelling; ಉಬ್ಬು (ubbu) is the swelling up.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಬ್ಬು (ubbu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 666 lists Kannada ubbu, "to swell, rise up", with the noun ubbuvike, "swelling".

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for numbness, then the Kannada for to swell.

You'll want to know: ಒಸಡು.
ಒಸಡು — osaḍu — "the gums".
ಒಸಡಿನಿಂದ ರಕ್ತ ಬರುತ್ತಿದೆ ಅಂತ ಅವನು ವೈದ್ಯರಿಗೆ ಹೇಳಿದನು. osaḍininda rakta baruttide anta avanu vaidyarige hēḷidanu. He told the doctor that his gums were bleeding.
Plural: ಒಸಡುಗಳು, osaḍugaḷu.
Often in the plural, ಒಸಡುಗಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಸಡು (osaḍu) is a native Kannada word for the gums; its origin is not settled.
