## You'll want to know: चरखो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C282-CHARKHO282-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चरखो** — *charkho* — "a spinning wheel".

> दादी कनै एक चरखो है।
>
> *dādī kanai ek charkho hai.*
>
> Grandmother has a spinning wheel.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरखो** (*charkho*) is usually traced to Persian *charkh*, "a wheel"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
