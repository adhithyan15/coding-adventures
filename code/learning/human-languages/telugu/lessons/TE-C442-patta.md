# పట్టా (paṭṭā) — a rail; a track

## You'll want to know: పట్టా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C442-PATTA442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టా** — *paṭṭā* — "a rail; a track".

> పిల్లలు రైలు పట్టాల దగ్గర ఆడకూడదు.
>
> *pillalu railu paṭṭāla daggara āḍakūḍadu.*
>
> Children must not play near the railway tracks.

Plural: పట్టాలు, *paṭṭālu*.

The rails of a line are పట్టాలు. పట్టా is also a strip of land and a title deed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టా** (*paṭṭā*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "a slab, a tablet, a strip" (CDIAL 7699); a rail is a long strip of iron.
