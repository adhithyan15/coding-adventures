# పరిచయం (paricayaṁ) — an introduction, acquaintance

## You'll want to know: పరిచయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిచయం** (*paricayaṁ*) — "an introduction, an acquaintance".

**మీతో పరిచయం సంతోషం** — "glad to have met you", literally "the acquaintance with you is a
joy". A fuller form of the **సంతోషం** you said at your first meeting.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Pardon, an apology, an offer, a refusal, and a fuller pleased-to-meet-you.
