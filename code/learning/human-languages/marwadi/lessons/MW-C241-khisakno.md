## You'll want to know: खिसकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C241-FALL241-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खिसकणो** — *khisakṇo* — "to slip; to slide out of place".

> सड़क माथै म्हारो पग खिसक्यो।
>
> *saṛak māthai mhāro pag khisakyo.*
>
> My foot slipped on the road.

Past (I …): हूं खिसक्यो, *hū̃ khisakyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खिसकणो** (*khisakṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khis*, "slip". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *khisaknā*, "to slip away" (CDIAL 3888).
