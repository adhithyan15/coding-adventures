## You'll want to know: しぬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C292-JINSEI292-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しぬ** — *shinu* — "to die".

> **そふの いぬが きょねん しにました。** — *sofu no inu ga kyonen shinimashita* — "Grandfather's dog died last year."

Polite **しにます**, *shinimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **とりが しにました。**, *tori ga shinimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しぬ** (*shinu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
