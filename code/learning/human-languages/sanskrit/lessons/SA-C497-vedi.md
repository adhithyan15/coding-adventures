## You'll want to know: वेदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C497-KANYADANA497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वेदी** — *vedī* — "a fire altar".

> वेद्याम् अग्निः ज्वलति।
>
> *vedyām agniḥ jvalati.*
>
> Fire burns on the altar.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेदी** (*vedī*) MW records *vedi*, later also *vedī*, "an elevated piece of ground serving for a sacrificial altar"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
