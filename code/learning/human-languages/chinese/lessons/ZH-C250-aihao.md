## You'll want to know: àihào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C250-HOBBY250-01]; assesses=[] -->

*àihào* — "hobby".

> *nǐ yǒu shénme àihào* — What hobbies do you have?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*àihào* is built from *ài* 'to love' and *hào* 'to be fond of', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'ojH* and *xawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to cherish' and the second with this very word. The second is 好 said with a falling tone.
