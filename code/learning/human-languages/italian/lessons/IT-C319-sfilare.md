## You'll want to know: sfilare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C319-CARNEVALE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sfilare** — "to parade, to march past".

> I carri sfilano per le vie del centro.
>
> *The floats parade through the streets of the centre.*

Models *sfilano* at a fashion show, too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sfilare* is built on *fila* "a line", from Latin *fīlum* "thread" (Lewis & Short): to go by in a line.
