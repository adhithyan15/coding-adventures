## You'll want to know: पुचकारना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C459-SNEH459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुचकारना** — *puckārnā* — "to soothe with soft words".

> बच्चा रो रहा था, तो दादी ने उसे पुचकारा।
>
> *baccā ro rahā thā, to dādī ne use puckārā.*
>
> The child was crying, so my grandmother soothed him with soft words.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को पुचकारा, *usne bacce ko puckārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुचकारना** (*puckārnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *puckārnā*, "to caress", under *\*pucca*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8248). It is the soft clicking and cooing used to calm a child or an animal.
