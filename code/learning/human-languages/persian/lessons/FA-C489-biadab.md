## You'll want to know: بیادب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C489-MANNERS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بیادب** (*biadab*) — "rude, impolite".

> آن مرد با فروشنده بیادب بود.
>
> *ân mard bâ forushande biadab bud.*
>
> That man was rude to the shop assistant.

*bi-* 'without' + *adab* 'manners'. *bâ kasi biadab budan* — to be rude to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biadab* is built in Persian from the prefix *bi-*, 'without', and *adab*, 'manners', a loan usually traced to Arabic *adab*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
