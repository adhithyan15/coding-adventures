# थांभो (thā̃bho) — a pillar

## You'll want to know: थांभो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C189-THANBHO189-01]; assesses=[] -->

**थांभो** — *thā̃bho* — "a pillar".

> थांभो लांबो है।
>
> *thā̃bho lā̃bo hai.*
>
> The pillar is tall.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थांभो** (*thā̃bho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *stambha*, "a pillar, a post"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *thāma*, "a pillar" (CDIAL 13682).
