Bengali, chapter 328: Inside a Book.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পৃষ্ঠা (prishṭhā) — a page.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a chapter of a book, then the Bengali for a publisher.

You'll want to know: পৃষ্ঠা.
পৃষ্ঠা — prishṭhā — "a page".
বইটার প্রথম পৃষ্ঠায় লেখকের ছবি আছে। bôiṭār prothom prishṭhāy lekhôker chhôbi āchhe. There is a picture of the writer on the first page of the book.

The word, taken apart.
পৃষ্ঠা (prishṭhā) is Sanskrit pṛṣṭha, 'the back; the upper side, a surface' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a page', one face of a leaf, is the later one. Everyday speech also says পাতা.

অনুবাদ করা — ônubād kôrā — "to translate".
তিনি বাংলা উপন্যাসটা ইংরেজিতে অনুবাদ করলেন। tini bānglā upônyāsṭā ingrejite ônubād kôrlen. He translated the Bengali novel into English.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কবিতাটা অনুবাদ করলেন, tini kôbitāṭā ônubād kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
অনুবাদ করা (ônubād kôrā) puts Sanskrit anuvāda, 'saying after, repeating; translation' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to say again in another language.

Lesson 3 of 5.
পাণ্ডুলিপি (pānḍulipi) — a manuscript.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a page, then the Bengali for to translate.

You'll want to know: পাণ্ডুলিপি.
পাণ্ডুলিপি — pānḍulipi — "a manuscript".
নাতি দাদুর বাক্সে কবিতার পাণ্ডুলিপি পেল। nāti dādur bākse kôbitār pānḍulipi pelô. The grandson found the manuscript of the poems in his grandfather's box.

The word, taken apart.
পাণ্ডুলিপি (pānḍulipi) joins Sanskrit pāṇḍu, 'pale, yellowish white' (Monier-Williams), to lipi, 'writing'. Monier-Williams has the older pāṇḍulekha, a first sketch in chalk; পাণ্ডুলিপি is a work in the writer's own hand, before it is printed.

সারাংশ — sārāngshô — "a summary".
ক্লাসে আমরা গল্পটার সারাংশ লিখলাম। klāse āmrā gôlpoṭār sārāngshô likhlām. In class we wrote a summary of the story.

The word, taken apart.
সারাংশ (sārāngshô) joins Sanskrit sāra, 'the core, the essence', to aṃśa, 'a share, a part' (Monier-Williams): the part that holds the core.

The word, taken apart.
রচনা করা (rôchônā kôrā) puts Sanskrit racanā, 'arrangement, preparation; a literary work' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to arrange words into a poem or a song.
