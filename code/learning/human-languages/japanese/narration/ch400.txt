Japanese, chapter 400: A Japanese-Style Room.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
とこのま (tokonoma) — an alcove (for flowers or a hanging scroll).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to wake from sleep, then the Japanese for to lie down.

You'll want to know: とこのま.
とこのま — tokonoma — "an alcove (for flowers or a hanging scroll)".
とこのまに はなを かざりました。 — tokonoma ni hana o kazarimashita — "I put flowers in the alcove."

The word, taken apart.
とこのま (tokonoma) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from toko, "raised floor", the particle no and ma, "space, room"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ざしき (zashiki) — a tatami room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to lie down, then the Japanese for an alcove.

You'll want to know: ざしき.
ざしき — zashiki — "a tatami room".
ざしきで みんなと ごはんを たべました。 — zashiki de minna to gohan o tabemashita — "We all had a meal in the tatami room."

The word, taken apart.
ざしき (zashiki) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese za, "seat" (Middle Chinese dzwaH), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) shiki, "spreading" (from shiku), inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
しめる (shimeru) — to shut (a door or window).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a veranda, then the Japanese for a toilet.

You'll want to know: しめる.
しめる — shimeru — "to shut (a door or window)".
さむいので、まどを しめて ください。 — samui node, mado o shimete kudasai — "It is cold, so please shut the window."
Polite しめます, shimemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): ははが まどを しめました。, haha ga mado o shimemashita.

The word, taken apart.
しめる (shimeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the partner of shimaru, "to close" (JMdict).
