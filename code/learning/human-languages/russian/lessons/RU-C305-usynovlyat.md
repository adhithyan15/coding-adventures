## You'll want to know: усыновлять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C305-VOSPITYVAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**усыновлять** (*usynovlyát*) — "to adopt (a child)".

> Они усыновили мальчика из детского дома.
>
> *Oní usynovíli málchika iz détskovo dóma.*
>
> They adopted a boy from a children's home.

Imperfective; the perfective is *усыновить* (*usynovít*). For a girl also *удочерить*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*усыновлять* is built on *у-* and *сын* "son", inherited from Proto-Slavic *synŭ*, from PIE *suHnús* (EtymDB): to make someone a son.
