## You'll want to know: la ansiedad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C710-NERVIOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la ansiedad** — "anxiety".

> La ansiedad no me deja dormir.
>
> *Anxiety does not let me sleep.*

Also eagerness: *esperar con ansiedad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ansiedad* is a learned borrowing from Latin *anxietās* "anxiety" (EtymDB), built on *anxius* "troubled"; English *anxious* comes from the same Latin.
