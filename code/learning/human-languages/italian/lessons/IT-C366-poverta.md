## You'll want to know: la povertà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C366-SOCIETA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la povertà** — "the poverty".

> Molte famiglie vivono in povertà.
>
> *Many families live in poverty.*

Built on *povero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*povertà* is inherited from Latin *paupertās, paupertātis* "poverty" (Lewis & Short), from *pauper* "poor".
