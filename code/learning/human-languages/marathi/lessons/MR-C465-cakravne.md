## You'll want to know: चक्रावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C465-NAVAL465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्रावणे** — *cakrāvṇe* — "to be baffled".

> इतका कठीण प्रश्न पाहून सगळे चक्रावले.
>
> *itkā kaṭhīṇ praśna pāhūn sagḷe cakrāvle.*
>
> Everyone was baffled by such a hard question.

Past (he …): तो चक्रावला, *to cakrāvlā*.

Built on चक्र, a wheel: the head spins.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्रावणे** (*cakrāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on चक्र, "a wheel", a tatsama from Sanskrit *cakra* (CDIAL 4538).
