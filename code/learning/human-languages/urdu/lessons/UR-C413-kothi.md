## You'll want to know: کوٹھی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C413-KOTHI413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کوٹھی** — *koṭhī* — "a large house; a bungalow".

> امیر لوگ بڑی کوٹھیوں میں رہتے ہیں۔
>
> *amīr log baṛī koṭhiyoṅ meṅ rahte haiṅ.*
>
> Rich people live in big houses.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کوٹھی** (*koṭhī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *koṭhī*, "a granary, a large house", under Sanskrit *kóṣṭha*, "a storeroom" (CDIAL 3546).
