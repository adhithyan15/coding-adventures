## You'll want to know: die Absage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C371-ZUSAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Absage** — "the rejection; the cancellation".

> Leider habe ich auf meine Bewerbung eine Absage bekommen.
>
> *Unfortunately I got a rejection for my application.*

Plural: *die Absagen*. The opposite of *die Zusage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Absage* is the noun of *absagen*, from *ab* and *sagen* "to say", Proto-Germanic *sagjaną* (EtymDB).
