## You'll want to know: выздоровление
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C490-RECOVERY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**выздоровление** (*vyzdorovléniye*) — "recovery".

> Желаем вам скорого выздоровления!
>
> *Zheláyem vam skórogo vyzdorovléniya!*
>
> We wish you a speedy recovery!

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выздоровление* is built on *выздороветь* 'to recover', from *здоровый* 'healthy', with *-ение*; EtymDB has no entry for the noun.
