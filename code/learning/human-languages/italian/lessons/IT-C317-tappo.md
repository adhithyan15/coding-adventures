## You'll want to know: il tappo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C317-SPUMANTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il tappo** — "the cork, the cap".

> Il tappo è volato fino al soffitto.
>
> *The cork flew up to the ceiling.*

Informally, *un tappo* is also a short person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tappo* comes from a Frankish *tappo* "stopper, plug" (EtymDB).
