## You'll want to know: けいき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C365-KEIZAI365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**けいき** — *keiki* — "business conditions".

> **けいきが よく なったら、みせが ふえるでしょう。** — *keiki ga yoku nattara, mise ga fueru deshō* — "If business picks up, there will probably be more shops."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けいき** (*keiki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kei*, "scenery" and *ki*, "atmosphere" (Middle Chinese *khj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
