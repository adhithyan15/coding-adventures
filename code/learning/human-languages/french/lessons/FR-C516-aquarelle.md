## You'll want to know: l'aquarelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C516-PEINTURE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'aquarelle** — "watercolour".

> Elle peint des paysages à l'aquarelle.
>
> *She paints landscapes in watercolour.*

Feminine. Both the technique and a painting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'aquarelle* was borrowed from Italian *acquarella*, built on *acqua* "water", inherited from Latin *aqua* (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
