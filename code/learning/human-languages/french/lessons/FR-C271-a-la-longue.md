## You'll want to know: à la longue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C271-PEU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**à la longue** — "in the long run, eventually".

> À la longue, on s'habitue au bruit.
>
> *In the long run, you get used to the noise.*

Here *la longue* is a noun: a long stretch of time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*longue* is the feminine of *long*, inherited from Latin *longus* "long" (Lewis & Short).
