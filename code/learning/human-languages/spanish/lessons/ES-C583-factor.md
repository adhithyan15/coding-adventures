## You'll want to know: el factor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C583-INFLUYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el factor** — "the factor".

> El precio es un factor importante.
>
> *The price is an important factor.*

*Un factor clave* — a key factor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*factor* is a learned borrowing from Latin *factor* 'maker, doer', built on *facere* 'to make, to do' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
