# تولى (tawallā) — to take office; to take charge

## You'll want to know: تولى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C340-HUKM340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تولى** — *tawallā* — "to take office; to take charge".

> تولى الأمير الحكم بعد أبيه.
>
> *tawallā al-amīr al-ḥukm baʿda abīhi.*
>
> The prince took power after his father.

Present: يتولى, *yatawallā*.

تولى الأمر: he took charge of the matter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تولى** (*tawallā*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-l-y* (Lane), "to be near; to have charge of", the root of *walī*, a guardian. It is Form V, "to take charge".
