## You'll want to know: شاعر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C521-POETRY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شاعر** (*shâ'er*) — "a poet".

> پدرم شاعر است و کتاب شعر دارد.
>
> *pedaram shâ'er ast va ketâb-e she'r dârad.*
>
> My father is a poet and has a book of poems.

Plural *shâ'erân*, or the Arabic *sho'arâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shâ'er* was borrowed from Arabic *šāʿir*, 'poet' (EtymDB), a word from the same root as *she'r*.
