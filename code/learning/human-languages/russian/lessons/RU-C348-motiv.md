## You'll want to know: мотив
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C348-POVOD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**мотив** (*motív*) — "a motive; a tune".

> Какой у него был мотив?
>
> *Kakóy u negó byl motív?*
>
> What was his motive?

Masculine. It also means a melody: *знакомый мотив*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**мотив** was borrowed from German *Motiv*, from French *motif*, from Medieval Latin *motivum*, from Latin *mōtīvus* "serving to move" (EtymDB), built on *movēre* "to move".
