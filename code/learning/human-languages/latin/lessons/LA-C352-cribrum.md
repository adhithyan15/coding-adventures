## You'll want to know: crībrum, crībrī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C352-SUBIGO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**crībrum, crībrī** — "a sieve".

> Pistor farīnam crībrō pūrgābat.
>
> *The baker was cleaning the flour with a sieve.*

Neuter. Pouring water into a sieve was a Roman picture of a hopeless task.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**crībrum** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to sift" (EtymDB), also seen, says Lewis & Short, in *cernō*, "to sift, to separate".
