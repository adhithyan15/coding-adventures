## You'll want to know: cōnfestim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C292-REPENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnfestim** — "at once, without delay".

> Dux, ubi nūntium audīvit, cōnfestim mīlitēs mīsit.
>
> *When the leader heard the news, he sent soldiers at once.*

Common in Caesar and Livy for a swift reaction: one thing heard, the next done.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnfestim** is linked by Lewis & Short with *festīnō*, "to hurry" (taught), with the prefix *con-*: all in a rush. The exact formation is not settled.
