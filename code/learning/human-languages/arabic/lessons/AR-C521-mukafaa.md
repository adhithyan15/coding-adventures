## You'll want to know: مكافأة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C521-MUKAFAA521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مكافأة** — *mukāfaʾa* — "a bonus, a reward".

> أعطت الشركة الموظفين مكافأة.
>
> *aʿṭat ash-sharika al-muwaẓẓafīn mukāfaʾa.*
>
> The company gave the staff a bonus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مكافأة** (*mukāfaʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-f-ʾ*, "to be equal to, to match" (Lane). Kafāʾa, competence, which you know, is from the same root: a reward matches the work.
