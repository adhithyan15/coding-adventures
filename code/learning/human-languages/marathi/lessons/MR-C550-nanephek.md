## You'll want to know: नाणेफेक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C550-NASHIB550-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नाणेफेक** — *nāṇephek* — "a coin toss".

> सामन्याआधी कर्णधारांनी नाणेफेक केली.
>
> *sāmnyāādhī karṇadhārānnī nāṇephek kelī.*
>
> Before the match the captains tossed a coin.

Feminine.

नाणेफेक करणे, to toss a coin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाणेफेक** (*nāṇephek*) is a Marathi compound built from नाणे, "a coin", inherited from Sanskrit *nāṇaka*, "coin" (CDIAL 7050), and फेक, "a throw", from फेकणे.
