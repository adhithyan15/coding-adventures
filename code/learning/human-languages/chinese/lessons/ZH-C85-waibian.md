## You'll want to know: wàibian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C85-WAIBIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

*wàibian* — "outside".

> *tā zài wàibian* — She's outside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wàibian* is built from wài 'outside', the wài of *wàiguó*, and bian 'side'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwajH* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), *ngwajH* glossed 'the outside' and 'far'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C82-SIDES82-02, ZH-LEX-C82-SIDES82-03] -->

*lǐbian* and *wàibian*, inside and outside, beside the pair you already own, *zuǒbian* and *yòubian*: one ending, bian, makes all four, and all four follow 在.
