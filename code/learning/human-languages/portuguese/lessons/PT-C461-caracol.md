## You'll want to know: o caracol
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C461-PEQUENOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o caracol** — "the snail; the curl".

> Depois da chuva, há caracóis no jardim.
>
> *After the rain, there are snails in the garden.*

In Portugal, *caracóis* are a summer snack. *Escada de caracol* — a spiral staircase.
