## You'll want to know: वाहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C408-VAHA408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहः** — *vāhaḥ* — "a draught animal".

> ग्रामे वृषभः वाहः अस्ति।
>
> *grāme vṛṣabhaḥ vāhaḥ asti.*
>
> In the village the bull is the draught animal.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहः** (*vāhaḥ*) comes from the root √vah, "to carry, to pull", which also gives *vahati*, "carries"; MW gives *vāha* as a draught animal: a horse, a bull or an ass (MW).
