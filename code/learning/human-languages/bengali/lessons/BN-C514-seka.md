## You'll want to know: সেঁকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C514-UNUNE514-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সেঁকা** — *sẽkā* — "to toast; to roast lightly".

> আমি সকালে রুটি সেঁকলাম।
>
> *Āmi sôkāle ruṭi sẽklām.*
>
> I toasted bread in the morning.

Past (he/she …): তিনি রুটি সেঁকলেন, *tini ruṭi sẽklen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সেঁকা** (*sẽkā*) comes from a reconstructed root *sēkk*; CDIAL lists Bengali *sẽkā*, "to roast, to sear" (CDIAL 13581).
