# উপভোগ করা (upôbhog kôrā) — to enjoy

## You'll want to know: উপভোগ করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C439-SUKH439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**উপভোগ করা** — *upôbhog kôrā* — "to enjoy".

> আমরা ছুটিটা পুরো উপভোগ করলাম।
>
> *āmrā chhuṭiṭā puro upôbhog kôrlām.*
>
> We enjoyed the holiday to the full.

Past (he/she …): তিনি গানটা উপভোগ করলেন, *tini gānṭā upôbhog kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপভোগ করা** (*upôbhog kôrā*) is Sanskrit *upabhoga*, "enjoyment, using" (Monier-Williams), with *kôrā*, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
