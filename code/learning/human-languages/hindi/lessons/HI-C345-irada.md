## You'll want to know: इरादा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C345-UDDESHYA345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**इरादा** — *irādā* — "an intention".

> मेरा इरादा कल जल्दी निकलने का है।
>
> *merā irādā kal jaldī nikalne kā hai.*
>
> I intend to set off early tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इरादा** (*irādā*) is usually traced to Arabic *irāda*, "will, wish", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
