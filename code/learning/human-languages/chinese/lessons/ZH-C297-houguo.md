## You'll want to know: 后果
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C297-FAULT297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**后果** — *hòuguǒ* — "consequences; what comes of it".

> *kāichē tài kuài, hòuguǒ huì hěn yánzhòng* — If you drive too fast, the consequences will be serious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**后果** (*hòuguǒ*) is built from 后 'after' and 果 'fruit', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwX* and *kwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the fruit of a tree' and 'resolute': the fruit that comes after.
