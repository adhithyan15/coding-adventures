## You'll want to know: अंदेशा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C557-ANDESHA557-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अंदेशा** — *andeśā* — "a worry; a misgiving".

> मुझे अंदेशा था कि ट्रेन देर से आएगी।
>
> *Mujhe andeśā thā ki ṭren der se āegī.*
>
> I had a feeling that the train would be late.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंदेशा** (*andeśā*) is usually traced to Persian *andēsha*, "thought, anxiety".
