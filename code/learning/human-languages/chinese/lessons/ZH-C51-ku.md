## You'll want to know: kū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C51-FEEL51-05]; assesses=[] -->

*kū* — "to cry; to weep".

> *háizi kū le* — The child started crying.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kū* is inherited from Middle Chinese *khuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a sound of grief'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
