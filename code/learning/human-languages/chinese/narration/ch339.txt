6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
长假 (chángjià) — long holiday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a day off work, then the Mandarin for a local custom.

You'll want to know: 长假.
长假 — chángjià — "long holiday".
十月有一个长假 shíyuè yǒu yí ge chángjià — There's a long holiday in October.

The word, taken apart.
长假 (chángjià) is built from 长 'long' and 假 'leave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, drjang and kaeH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'long' and 'far' and the second 'a holiday from work'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
国庆 (Guóqìng) — National Day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a local custom, then the Mandarin for a long holiday.

You'll want to know: 国庆.
国庆 — Guóqìng — "National Day".
国庆的时候我们回了老家 Guóqìng de shíhou wǒmen huí le lǎojiā — Over the national holiday we went back to our home town.
The new character, in pieces: 庆 (qìng) — six strokes: the roof piece of 店, a dot, a bar and a long stroke falling to the left; under it 大. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
国庆 (Guóqìng) is built from 国 'a state' and qìng 'to celebrate', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwok and khjaengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to congratulate' and 'good fortune'.

Lesson 3 of 6.
庆 (qìng) — the roof piece over 大.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — the roof piece over 大 and Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
店 大.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — diàn, dà]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — the roof piece over 大.
庆 (qìng).
Two pieces: the roof first, then what sits under it.
The roof piece, three strokes, as on 店: a dot, a bar, then a long stroke falling to the left down the side.
The piece under it, 大, three strokes: a bar, a stroke falling to the left through it, then a stroke falling to the right.
3 + 3 = six.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 庆 (qìng), all six, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 庆 (qìng)? (Six.) What is the roof? (The roof piece of 店.) What sits under it? (大.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 庆 (qìng), pinned stroke paths and medians.

You'll want to know: yānhuā.
yānhuā — "fireworks".
Guóqìng de wǎnshang wǒmen kàn le yānhuā — On the evening of the national holiday we saw the fireworks.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yānhuā is built from yān 'smoke' and 花 'a flower', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'en and xwae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the breath of fire'.
