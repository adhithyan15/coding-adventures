## You'll want to know: سلطعون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C477-QIRSH477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سلطعون** — *salṭaʿūn* — "a crab".

> أمسك أخي سلطعونا على الصخرة.
>
> *amsaka akhī salṭaʿūnan ʿalā aṣ-ṣakhra.*
>
> My brother caught a crab on the rock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلطعون** (*salṭaʿūn*) names the crab; the origin is not settled.
