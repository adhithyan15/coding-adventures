## You'll want to know: reiniciar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C777-ENCHUFAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**reiniciar** — "to restart, to reboot".

> Si se bloquea, reinicia el móvil.
>
> *If it freezes, restart your phone.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reiniciar* is built on *iniciar* with *re-* 'again'. *Iniciar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *initiāre* 'to begin', from *initium* 'a beginning' (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
