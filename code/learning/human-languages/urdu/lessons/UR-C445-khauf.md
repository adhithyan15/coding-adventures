## You'll want to know: خوف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C445-FEAR445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خوف** — *khauf* — "dread; fear".

> جنگل میں رات کو خوف لگتا ہے۔
>
> *jangal meṅ rāt ko khauf lagtā hai.*
>
> In the forest at night you feel a sense of dread.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خوف** (*khauf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khawf*, "fear".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
