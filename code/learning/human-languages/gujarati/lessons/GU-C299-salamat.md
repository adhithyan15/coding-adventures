## You'll want to know: સલામત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C299-BUM299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સલામત** — *salāmat* — "safe".

> છેવટે બધા સલામત હતા.
>
> *chevṭe badhā salāmat hatā.*
>
> In the end everyone was safe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સલામત** (*salāmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *salāma*, "safety, soundness". It describes people and things out of danger: **બધા સલામત છે**, everyone is safe.
