## You'll want to know: ಹರ್ಷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C492-HARSHA492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹರ್ಷ** — *harṣa* — "delight; joy".

> ಮದುವೆಯ ಸುದ್ದಿಯಿಂದ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹರ್ಷ ತುಂಬಿತು.
>
> *maduveya suddiyinda maneyalli harṣa tumbitu.*
>
> The wedding news filled the house with joy.

Written and formal; in talk people say ಸಂತೋಷ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹರ್ಷ** (*harṣa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *harṣa*, "bristling, a thrill" (CDIAL 13999): the hair standing on end with excitement, and so delight.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
