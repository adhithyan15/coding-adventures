## You'll want to know: píláo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C484-WELL484-04]; assesses=[] -->

*píláo* — "worn out; fatigued".

> *wǒ gōngzuò le yì tiān gǎnjué hěn píláo* — I've worked all day, and I feel worn out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*píláo* is built from *pí* 'weary' and *láo* 'toil', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bje* and *law* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'tired' and 'spent' and the second 'tired' and 'hard work'.
