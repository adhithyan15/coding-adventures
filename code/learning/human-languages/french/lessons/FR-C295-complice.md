## You'll want to know: le complice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C295-AVEUX-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le complice** — "the accomplice".

> Il avait un complice qui l'attendait dans la voiture.
>
> *He had an accomplice who was waiting for him in the car.*

Also an adjective: *un sourire complice* — a knowing smile.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*complice* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *complex*, *complicem* "partner, one folded in with" (EtymDB), built on *plicāre* "to fold".
