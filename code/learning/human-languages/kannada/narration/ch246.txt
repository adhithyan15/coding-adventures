Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಳುಕು (uḷuku) — a sprain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pimple, then the Kannada for medical treatment.

You'll want to know: ಉಳುಕು.
ಉಳುಕು — uḷuku — "a sprain".

The word, taken apart.
ಉಳುಕು (uḷuku) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil uḷukku (DEDR 2702). DEDR lists uḷuku, "a sprain".

ಹುಣ್ಣು (huṇṇu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil puṇ (DEDR 4268). DEDR gives the older Kannada puṇ, "wound, ulcer": the p- has become h-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮಧುಮೇಹ (madhumēha) — diabetes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a sore, then the Kannada for a bandage.

You'll want to know: ಮಧುಮೇಹ.
ಮಧುಮೇಹ — madhumēha — "diabetes".

The word, taken apart.
ಮಧುಮೇಹ (madhumēha) is Sanskrit madhumeha: madhu, "honey", and meha, "urine". The old physicians named the illness for the sweetness of the urine.
