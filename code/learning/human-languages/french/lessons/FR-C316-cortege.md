## You'll want to know: le cortège
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C316-CELEBRER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le cortège** — "the procession".

> Le cortège a traversé le centre-ville.
>
> *The procession went through the town centre.*

A wedding, funeral or protest procession.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cortège* was borrowed from Italian *corteggio* "a retinue" (EtymDB), built on *corte* "court", from Latin *cohors* "an enclosure, a company" (Lewis & Short).
