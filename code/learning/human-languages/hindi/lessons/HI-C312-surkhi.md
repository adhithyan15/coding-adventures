## You'll want to know: सुर्ख़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C312-SAMACHAR312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सुर्ख़ी** — *surkhī* — "a headline".

> आज अख़बार की सबसे बड़ी सुर्ख़ी बाढ़ पर है।
>
> *āj akhbār kī sabse baṛī surkhī bāṛh par hai.*
>
> Today the newspaper's biggest headline is about the flood.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुर्ख़ी** (*surkhī*) is usually traced to Persian *surkhī*, "redness", from *surkh*, red; it is said that headings were once set in red. **सुर्ख़ियों में होना** is to be in the headlines.
