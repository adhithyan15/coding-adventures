# أزعج (azʿaja) — to disturb; to bother

## You'll want to know: أزعج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C448-DAJIJ448-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أزعج** — *azʿaja* — "to disturb; to bother".

> أزعجنا ضجيج الشارع.
>
> *azʿajanā ḍajīj ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> The noise of the street disturbed us.

Present: يزعج, *yuzʿij*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أزعج** (*azʿaja*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-ʿ-j*, "to unsettle, to disquiet" (Lane). It is Form IV; *muzʿij*, "annoying", is its participle.
