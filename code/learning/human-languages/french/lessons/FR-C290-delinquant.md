## You'll want to know: le délinquant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C290-CRIME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le délinquant** — "the offender, the delinquent".

> Le juge a parlé avec le jeune délinquant.
>
> *The judge spoke with the young offender.*

*Un délinquant mineur* — a young offender.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*délinquant* is a learned word from Latin *dēlinquēns*, the present participle of *dēlinquere* "to fail, to do wrong" (Lewis & Short).
