## You'll want to know: facinus, facinoris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C305-SCELUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**facinus, facinoris** — "a deed; a bad deed".

> Facinus audāx erat, sed nōn turpe.
>
> *It was a daring deed, but not a shameful one.*

Neuter, 3rd declension, from *faciō*. Alone it often means a crime; praised, a fine deed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**facinus** is built on *faciō*, "to do, to make" (EtymDB): a thing done, and very often a wicked thing done.
