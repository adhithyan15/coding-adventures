## You'll want to know: غم انگیز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C530-STORIES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**غم انگیز** (*ghamangiz*) — "tragic, sad".

> پایان داستان غم انگیز بود.
>
> *pâyân-e dâstân ghamangiz bud.*
>
> The end of the story was sad.

*gham* 'sorrow' + *angiz* 'stirring' (stem of *angikhtan*): sorrow-stirring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ghamangiz* is built in Persian on *gham*, 'sorrow', from Arabic *ġamm* (EtymDB), and *angiz*, the stem of native *angikhtan*, 'to stir up'.
