## You'll want to know: অলস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C462-OLOS462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অলস** — *ôlôs* — "lazy; idle".

> ছুটির দিনে আমি অলস হয়ে বিছানায় শুয়ে থাকি।
>
> *chhuṭir dine āmi ôlôs hôye bichhānāy shuye thāki.*
>
> On holidays I lie lazily in bed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অলস** (*ôlôs*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *alasa*, "inactive, lazy" (CDIAL 708).
