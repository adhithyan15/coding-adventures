## You'll want to know: la dune
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C458-LITTORAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la dune** — "the dune".

> Derrière la plage, il y a de hautes dunes.
>
> *Behind the beach there are high dunes.*

*Une dune de sable* — a sand dune.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dune* was borrowed from Middle Dutch *dūne* (EtymDB).
