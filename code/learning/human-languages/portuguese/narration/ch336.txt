Portuguese, chapter 336: O debate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o tema — the topic, the subject; the theme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to give an opinion, then the Portuguese for to maintain.

You'll want to know: o tema.
o tema — "the topic, the subject; the theme".
Qual é o tema da reunião de hoje? What is the topic of today's meeting?
Mudar de tema — to change the subject.

The word, taken apart.
tema is a learned borrowing from Latin thema (EtymDB), which took it from Greek théma "something set down, a proposition", built on tithénai "to put".

Lesson 2 of 5.
a questão — the issue, the matter; the question.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to maintain, then the Portuguese for the topic.

You'll want to know: a questão.
a questão — "the issue, the matter; the question".
A questão não é o dinheiro, é o tempo. The issue is not the money, it is the time.
Uma pergunta is something you ask; uma questão is an issue to discuss. Em questão — in question.

The word, taken apart.
questão is a learned borrowing from Latin quaestiō "a seeking, an inquiry" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on quaerere "to seek, to ask".

Lesson 3 of 5.
o debate — the debate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the topic, then the Portuguese for the issue.

You'll want to know: o debate.
o debate — "the debate".
Ontem houve um debate na televisão sobre esta questão. Yesterday there was a debate on television about this issue.
Um debate aceso — a heated debate.

The word, taken apart.
debate is built on the verb debater, which is usually traced to Old French debatre "to fight, to contend, to debate" (EtymDB lists the French verb), a formation on Latin battuere "to beat" (Lewis & Short): ideas are knocked back and forth.

Lesson 4 of 5.
debater — to debate, to discuss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the issue, then the Portuguese for the debate.

You'll want to know: debater.
debater — "to debate, to discuss".
Os alunos debateram o tema durante uma hora. The students debated the topic for an hour.
Debater um tema — to debate a topic. More formal than discutir.

The word, taken apart.
debater is usually traced to Old French debatre "to fight, to contend, to debate" (EtymDB), built from de- and batre "to beat", from Latin battuere "to beat, to strike" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
o argumento — the argument (a reason given); the plot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the debate, then the Portuguese for to debate.

You'll want to know: o argumento.
o argumento — "the argument (a reason given); the plot".
É um bom argumento, mas não me convence. It is a good argument, but it does not convince me.
An argumento is a reason you give; a quarrel is uma discussão. It is also the plot or script of a film.

The word, taken apart.
argumento is a learned borrowing from Latin argūmentum "proof, evidence" (EtymDB), built on arguere "to make clear, to prove" (Lewis & Short).
