Marathi, chapter 431: When Things Break Down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बिघाड (bighāḍ) — a breakdown; a fault.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a share, then the Marathi for to be left over.

You'll want to know: बिघाड.
बिघाड — bighāḍ — "a breakdown; a fault".
इंजिनात बिघाड झाल्यामुळे गाडी थांबली. iñjināt bighāḍ jhālyāmuḷe gāḍī thāmblī. The train stopped because of a fault in the engine.
Masculine.
बिघाड (bighāḍ) होणे, a fault occurs; the place of the fault takes -त. The verb is बिघडणे, to go wrong.

The word, taken apart.
बिघाड (bighāḍ) is inherited, from the family of Sanskrit vighaṭate, "flies apart, is broken", where CDIAL lists Marathi bighaḍṇẽ, "to be spoilt" (CDIAL 11673).

Lesson 2 of 5.
निकामी (nikāmī) — out of action; useless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be left over, then the Marathi for a breakdown.

You'll want to know: निकामी.
निकामी — nikāmī — "out of action; useless".
स्टेशनवरचा जुना पंखा आता निकामी झाला आहे. sṭeśanvarcā junā paṅkhā ātā nikāmī jhālā āhe. The old fan at the station no longer works at all.
It does not change: निकामी (nikāmī) पंखा, निकामी (nikāmī) गाडी; निकामी (nikāmī) होणे, to stop working for good.

The word, taken apart.
निकामी (nikāmī) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi nikāmī, "idle, useless", under Sanskrit niṣkarman, "inactive", that is, without work (CDIAL 7475).

Lesson 3 of 5.
तुटणे (tuṭṇe) — to snap; to break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a breakdown, then the Marathi for out of action.

You'll want to know: तुटणे.
तुटणे — tuṭṇe — "to snap; to break".
वादळात झाडाची फांदी तुटली. vādaḷāt jhāḍācī phāndī tuṭlī. The branch of the tree snapped in the storm.
Past (he …): तो तुटला, to tuṭlā.
It happens by itself: फांदी तुटली; to break something is तोडणे.

The word, taken apart.
तुटणे (tuṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi tuṭṇẽ, "is broken", under Sanskrit truṭyati, "is broken, falls asunder" (CDIAL 6065).

Lesson 4 of 5.
फुटणे (phuṭṇe) — to burst.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for out of action, then the Marathi for to snap.

You'll want to know: फुटणे.
फुटणे — phuṭṇe — "to burst".
वाटेत गाडीचा टायर फुटला. vāṭet gāḍīcā ṭāyar phuṭlā. On the way a tyre of the car burst.
Past (he …): तो फुटला, to phuṭlā.
Also a crack in glass or a pot: काच फुटली, the glass broke.

The word, taken apart.
फुटणे (phuṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi phuṭṇẽ, "to split, burst", under sphuṭyati, "is burst open" (CDIAL 13845).

Lesson 5 of 5.

गर्दीत माझी पिशवी फाटली. gardīt mājhī piśvī phāṭlī. My bag ripped in the crowd.

Cloth and paper फाटतात; to tear something is फाडणे.
