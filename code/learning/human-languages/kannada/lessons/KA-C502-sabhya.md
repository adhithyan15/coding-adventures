## You'll want to know: ಸಭ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C502-SABHYA502-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಭ್ಯ** — *sabhya* — "polite; decent".

> ಅವನು ಸಭ್ಯ ಹುಡುಗ, ಎಲ್ಲರ ಜೊತೆ ಗೌರವದಿಂದ ಮಾತಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *avanu sabhya huḍuga, ellara jote gauravadinda mātāḍuttāne.*
>
> He is a polite boy; he speaks respectfully with everyone.

ಸಭೆ is an assembly: ಸಭ್ಯ, fit to be in company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಭ್ಯ** (*sabhya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sabhya*, "being in an assembly", and so "polite" (CDIAL 13171).
