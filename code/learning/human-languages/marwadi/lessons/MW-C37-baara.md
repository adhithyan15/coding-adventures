# बारा — twelve on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-BAARA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DO-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s]
- [YOU RECALL: say *bārā* before you look at it — **R1**]
- [YOU RECALL: write **दो** — **R4**]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-BAARA-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-BA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

> **बा** + **रा** → **बारा** — *bārā* — twelve

Two pieces, no new sign, and both halves take the same **ा**. **ब** came in with
*bīs*; **र** has been in the hand since the first greeting.

Written beside **दो**, the point the ear made is visible: twelve and two share
one letter and nothing else.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-BAARA-01, MW-SCRIPT-IGYAARA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **बारा**, cover it, wait five seconds, write it. Then write **इग्यारा**
under it and ring the **रा** that ends both.
