## You'll want to know: पूर्वज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C308-PIDHI308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वज** — *pūrvaj* — "an ancestor".

> आमचे पूर्वज या गावात तीनशे वर्षांपूर्वी आले.
>
> *āmce pūrvaj yā gāvāt tīnśe varṣāmpūrvī āle.*
>
> Our ancestors came to this village three hundred years ago.

Masculine.

Mostly in the plural: पूर्वजांचं घर, the ancestral house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वज** (*pūrvaj*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pūrvaja*, 'born before; an ancestor' (Monier-Williams).
