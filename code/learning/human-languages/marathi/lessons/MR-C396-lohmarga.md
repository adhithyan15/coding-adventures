## You'll want to know: लोहमार्ग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C396-AAGGADI396-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लोहमार्ग** — *lohmārga* — "a railway line".

> हा लोहमार्ग डोंगरातून जातो.
>
> *hā lohmārga ḍoṅgarātūn jāto.*
>
> This railway line runs through the hills.

Masculine.

A formal word, common on signs and in the news; people say रेल्वे or गाडीचा रस्ता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोहमार्ग** (*lohmārga*) is built from two tatsama words, Sanskrit *loha*, "iron", and *mārga*, "road, way" (Monier-Williams): the iron road.
