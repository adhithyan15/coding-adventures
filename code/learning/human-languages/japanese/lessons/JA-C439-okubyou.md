## You'll want to know: おくびょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C439-BRAVE439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おくびょう** — *okubyō* — "timid; cowardly".

> **うちの いぬは おくびょうです。** — *uchi no inu wa okubyō desu* — "Our dog is timid."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おくびょう** (*okubyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *oku*, "timidity" (Middle Chinese *'ik*) and *byō*, "sick" (*bjaengH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
