## You'll want to know: biǎoshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C352-AGREE352-05]; assesses=[] -->

*biǎoshì* — "to make your view known; to express".

> *yīnwèi dàjiā dōu tóngyì suǒyǐ tā yě biǎoshì tóngyì* — Everyone agreed, so he said he agreed too.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biǎoshì* is built from *biǎo* and *shì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjewX* and *zyijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to make clear' and the second 'to hand down a sign'.
