## You'll want to know: গাইড
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C399-BHROMON399-05]; assesses=[] -->

**গাইড** — *gāiḍ* — "a guide (for visitors)".

> গাইড আমাদের পুরনো দুর্গটা দেখালেন।
>
> *gāiḍ āmāder purôno durgôṭā dekhālen.*
>
> The guide showed us the old fort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গাইড** (*gāiḍ*) is borrowed from English *guide*, with the final d said as the hard ড.
