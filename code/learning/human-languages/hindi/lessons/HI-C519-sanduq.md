## You'll want to know: संदूक़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C519-DHOOL519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संदूक़** — *sandūq* — "a trunk; a chest".

> दादी के पुराने कपड़े एक संदूक़ में हैं।
>
> *Dādī ke purāne kapṛe ek sandūq meṁ haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother's old clothes are in a trunk.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संदूक़** (*sandūq*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṣandūq*, "a chest, a box", through Persian.
