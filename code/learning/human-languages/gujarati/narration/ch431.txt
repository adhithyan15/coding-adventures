Gujarati, chapter 431: Stock and Shortages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગોદામ (godām) — a warehouse; a godown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a regular buyer, then the Gujarati for to weigh out.

You'll want to know: ગોદામ.
ગોદામ — godām — "a warehouse; a godown".
વેપારીના ગોદામમાં ઘઉં ભરેલા છે. vepārīnā godāmmā̃ ghaũ bharelā che. The merchant's warehouse is full of wheat.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગોદામ (godām) is usually traced to Malay gudang, "a storehouse", which came round the coasts through Portuguese; English godown is the same word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
પુરવઠો (puravṭho) — a supply (of goods, water or power).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to weigh out, then the Gujarati for a warehouse.

You'll want to know: પુરવઠો.
પુરવઠો — puravṭho — "a supply (of goods, water or power)".
ગામમાં પાણીનો પુરવઠો ઓછો છે. gāmmā̃ pāṇīno puravṭho ocho che. The water supply in the village is low.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પુરવઠો (puravṭho) seems to belong with the verb CDIAL lists for Gujarati, pūrvũ, "to fill", and Marathi puraviṇẽ, "to supply to the full", under Sanskrit pūrayati, "fills" (CDIAL 8335); CDIAL gives no form for પુરવઠો itself, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઊણપ (ūṇap) — a shortfall; a lack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a warehouse, then the Gujarati for a supply.

You'll want to know: ઊણપ.
ઊણપ — ūṇap — "a shortfall; a lack".
ઉનાળામાં ગામમાં પાણીની ઊણપ રહે છે. unāḷāmā̃ gāmmā̃ pāṇīnī ūṇap rahe che. In summer the village runs short of water.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઊણપ (ūṇap) is built on ઊણું, "wanting, short", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit ūna, "deficient" (CDIAL 2405): what is missing from the full amount.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a supply, then the Gujarati for a shortfall.

You'll want to know: જથ્થાબંધ.
જથ્થાબંધ — jaththābandh — "wholesale; in bulk".
વેપારી જથ્થાબંધ ભાવે માલ વેચે છે. vepārī jaththābandh bhāve māl vece che. The trader sells goods at wholesale prices.

The word, taken apart.
જથ્થાબંધ (jaththābandh) is built from જથ્થો, "a quantity, a bulk", and -બંધ, "bound, tied", usually traced to Persian band: goods sold tied up in bulk.

Lesson 5 of 5.
સંઘરવું (sangharvũ) — to store up; to hoard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a shortfall, then the Gujarati for wholesale.

You'll want to know: સંઘરવું.
સંઘરવું — sangharvũ — "to store up; to hoard".
વેપારીએ ગોદામમાં ખાંડ સંઘરી. vepārīe godāmmā̃ khā̃ḍ sanghrī. The trader stored up sugar in his warehouse.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘઉં સંઘર્યા, teṇe ghaũ sangharyā.

The word, taken apart.
સંઘરવું (sangharvũ) is built on Sanskrit saṁgraha, "holding together, gathering in" (Monier-Williams): to keep gathering things and not let them go.
