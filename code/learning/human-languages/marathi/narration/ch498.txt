Marathi, chapter 498: Growing Up and Growing Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बालपण (bālpaṇ) — childhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for of a paternal uncle, then the Marathi for step-.

You'll want to know: बालपण.
बालपण — bālpaṇ — "childhood".
माझं बालपण गावात गेलं. mājhaṁ bālpaṇ gāvāt gelaṁ. I spent my childhood in the village.
Neuter.
बाल, a child, + -पण, -hood.

The word, taken apart.
बालपण (bālpaṇ) is a Marathi derivation: बाल, "child", a tatsama of Sanskrit bāla (CDIAL 9216), with -paṇ, "-hood".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for youth, then the Marathi for old age.

You'll want to know: वय.
वय — vay — "age".
तुझं वय किती आहे? tujhaṁ vay kitī āhe? How old are you?
Neuter.
Neuter: माझं वय (vay) दहा वर्षं आहे. वयस्कर, elderly.

The word, taken apart.
वय (vay) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vayas, "energy, prime of life, age" (CDIAL 11305), without its final -s.
