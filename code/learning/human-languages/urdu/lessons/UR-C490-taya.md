## You'll want to know: تایا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C490-STEP490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تایا** — *tāyā* — "a father's elder brother".

> میرے تایا ابو سے بڑے ہیں۔
>
> *mere tāyā abbū se baṛe haiṅ.*
>
> My uncle is older than my father.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تایا** (*tāyā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *tāyā* under Sanskrit *tātyá*, "fatherly" (CDIAL 5759).
