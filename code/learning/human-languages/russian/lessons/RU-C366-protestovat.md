## You'll want to know: протестовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C366-VYBORY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**протестовать** (*protestovát*) — "to protest".

> Жители протестуют против стройки.
>
> *Zhíteli protestúyut prótiv stróyki.*
>
> The residents are protesting against the building work.

Imperfective; the present is *протестую, протестуешь*. *Протестовать против* + genitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**протестовать** is built on *протест* with the verb suffix *-овать*; the noun is usually traced to Latin *prōtestārī* "to declare in public", on *testis* "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
