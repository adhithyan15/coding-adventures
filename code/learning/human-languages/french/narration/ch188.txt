Lesson 1 of 5.
près de — near to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for until, then the French for since.

You'll want to know: près de.
près de — "near to".

The phrase, taken apart.
près de — près, "near", and de, "of". Près from Latin presse, "closely" (EtymDB 2.1), and de from Latin dē.
