## You'll want to know: विमानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C75-TRAVEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विमानम्** (*vimānam*) — "an aeroplane". In the old stories it was a vehicle of the
gods that crossed the **आकाशः**.

## What you've built
