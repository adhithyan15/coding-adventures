## You'll want to know: उपन्यास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C340-UPANYAS340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उपन्यास** — *upanyās* — "a novel".

> मीरा ने छुट्टी में एक लंबा उपन्यास पढ़ा।
>
> *Mīrā ne chuṭṭī meṁ ek lambā upanyās paṛhā.*
>
> Mira read a long novel during the holidays.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपन्यास** (*upanyās*) is Sanskrit *upanyāsa*, "a placing near; a statement" (Monier-Williams). Modern Hindi uses it for a novel; the dictionary gives only the older senses.
