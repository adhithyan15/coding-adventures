## You'll want to know: stressato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C362-STRESS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**stressato** — "stressed".

> In questo periodo sono molto stressata.
>
> *I am very stressed at the moment.*

A woman says *stressata*. The noun is *lo stress*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stressato* is built on *stress*, which was borrowed from English *stress* "strain, pressure" (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
