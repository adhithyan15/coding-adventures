## You'll want to know: ముద్రణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C375-PATRIKEYUDU375-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ముద్రణ** — *mudraṇa* — "printing; an edition".

> ఆ పుస్తకం మూడో ముద్రణ వచ్చింది.
>
> *ā pustakaṁ mūḍō mudraṇa vaccindi.*
>
> The book's third edition came out.

You know ముద్రించు, to print; ముద్రణ is the printing, or an edition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ముద్రణ** (*mudraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mudraṇa*, "the act of sealing up" (CDIAL 10201), from *mudrā*, "a seal".
