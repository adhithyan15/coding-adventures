# Hear *bhāī* — brother

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-MAA-01, MW-LEX-BAAP-01, MW-SCRIPT-BAAP-01, MW-LEX-AABHAAR-01, MW-DIALOGUE-NAME-EXCHANGE-01, MW-QUESTION-NAME-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Say mother and write father. Ask and answer the known name exchange,
then recall the formal gratitude word that begins with breathy *bh*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-BHAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *bhāī* — **brother**

Hear two long vowel parts: *bhā-ī*. Say them smoothly once. The first consonant
matches the familiar start of *ābhār*; spelling waits for the next step.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-BHAI-01, MW-LEX-BAAP-01] -->

Distinguish *bāp* from *bhāī* by meaning and by the breath after *bh*. Answer a
brother cue with *bhāī* once.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-BHAI-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"MW-C08-hear-bhai-meaning","kind":"text","assesses":["MW-LEX-BHAI-01"],"prompt":"Which family meaning does bhāī carry?","answer":"brother","accepted":["भाई"],"feedback":{"correct":"Right: bhāī means brother.","incorrect":"Keep the sound pair small: bhāī — brother."},"response_seconds":8} -->

Say *bhāī* once, then stop.
