Mandarin Chinese, chapter 422: Going North.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
dǎoháng — to navigate by satnav.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a toll gate, then the Mandarin for to break the speed limit.

You'll want to know: dǎoháng.
dǎoháng — "to navigate by satnav".
wǒ yòng shǒujī dǎoháng zhǎodào le nà jiā fàndiàn — I found the way to that restaurant with my phone.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dǎoháng is built from dǎo 'to lead' and háng 'to sail', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dawH and hang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to lead' and the second 'a boat'. dawH would regularly give dào: the falling-rising tone of dǎo is irregular.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.
东北 (dōngběi) — northeast; the Northeast (of China).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to break the speed limit, then the Mandarin for to navigate by satnav.

You'll want to know: 东北.
东北 — dōngběi — "northeast; the Northeast (of China)".
火车进入了东北 huǒchē jìnrù le dōngběi — The train has come into the northeast of China.
The new character, in pieces: 北 (běi) — five strokes in two halves: on the left a vertical, a short bar into it from the left and a bar rising to the right at its foot; on the right a stroke falling to the left, then a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
东北 (dōngběi) is built from 东 'east' and 北 (běi) 'north', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tuwng and pok (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'north' and 'to flee'.

Lesson 3 of 6.
北 (běi) — two halves back to back.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. Script — two halves back to back.
北 (běi).
Two halves, left before right.
The left half, three strokes. A vertical. A short bar from the left into it. Then a bar rising to the right into its foot, from the left.
The right half, two strokes, as on the right of 比: a stroke falling to the left, then a vertical that bends to the right along the foot and hooks up.
3 + 2 = five.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 北 (běi), all five, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 北 (běi)? (Five.) What is the right half? (The right half of 比.)
