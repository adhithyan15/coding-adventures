# (reading) — a whole meeting

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SKILL-READ-LINES] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Six words, then six lines. Now two people, from hello to goodbye.

[PAUSE 1s] Thirty-five words, and not one of them is new.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01, GU-LEX-BHAI] -->

> તમે કેમ છો?
> હું મજામાં છું।
> તમારું નામ શું છે?
> મારું નામ મીરા છે।
> તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો।
> આ મારો ભાઈ છે।
> આ મારું ઘર છે।
> માફ કરો, હું સમજતો નથી।
> વાંધો નહીં।
> કાલે મળીશું।

[PAUSE 3s] Read it through once without stopping. Do not translate as you go —
let the turns arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Now again, and count how many people are speaking.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-GHAR-01, GU-NEG-SAMAJTO-NATHI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Two. Nothing on the page says so — no names in front of the lines,
no he-said. The turns alternate and you followed them.

[PAUSE 2s] Notice the eighth line. **માફ કરો, હું સમજતો નથી।** One of them has
failed to understand, and the conversation carries on anyway. That is why the
book taught the repair before it taught much else.

[PAUSE 2s] This is the first whole exchange you have read rather than said,
and it works because every line was paid for, one chapter at a time.
