# ಕಲುಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸು (kaluṣitagoḷisu) — to pollute; to contaminate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C473-PARISARA473-01, KA-LEX-C473-PARISARA473-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for the environment, then the Kannada for pollution.

## You'll want to know: ಕಲುಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C473-PARISARA473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಲುಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸು** — *kaluṣitagoḷisu* — "to pollute; to contaminate".

> ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆ ನದಿಯ ನೀರನ್ನು ಕಲುಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸಿತು.
>
> *kārkhāne nadiya nīrannu kaluṣitagoḷisitu.*
>
> The factory polluted the river water.

Past (I …): ಕಲುಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸಿದೆ, *kaluṣitagoḷiside*.

ಕಲುಷಿತ ನೀರು, polluted water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಲುಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸು** (*kaluṣitagoḷisu*) is built from Sanskrit *kaluṣita*, "foul, impure" (Monier-Williams), and the Kannada verb-maker -*goḷisu*: to make foul.
