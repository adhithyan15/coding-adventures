## You'll want to know: martelar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**martelar** — "to hammer".

> O vizinho martela a parede todas as manhãs.
>
> *The neighbour hammers at the wall every morning.*

Built on *o martelo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*martelar* is built on *martelo* "hammer", which goes back to Late Latin *martellus* (EtymDB).
