## You'll want to know: पपड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C490-CHHALA490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पपड़ी** — *papṛī* — "a scab; a thin crust".

> घुटने के घाव पर पपड़ी जम गई है।
>
> *ghuṭne ke ghāv par papṛī jam gaī hai.*
>
> A scab has formed on the wound on my knee.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पपड़ी** (*papṛī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *papṛī*, "thin crust, scab", under Sanskrit *párpaṭa* (CDIAL 7934). The thin, crisp *papṛī* of the snack seller is the same word.
