## You'll want to know: रुख़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C417-DISHA417-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रुख़** — *rukh* — "a direction; the way something faces".

> शाम को हवा का रुख़ बदल गया।
>
> *śām ko havā kā rukh badal gayā.*
>
> In the evening the wind changed direction.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुख़** (*rukh*) is usually traced to Persian *rukh*, "the face, the cheek": the way a face is turned, and so the direction of the wind, a road or a person's mood.
