Spanish, chapter 693: Árboles y plantas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el olivo — the olive tree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the well, then the Spanish for to flower.

You'll want to know: el olivo.
el olivo — "the olive tree".
En el campo hay miles de olivos. There are thousands of olive trees in the countryside.
The fruit is la aceituna; the oil is el aceite de oliva.

The word, taken apart.
olivo is inherited from Vulgar Latin olīvus, from Latin olīva, which comes from Greek elaía "olive" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la vegetación — the vegetation, the plant life.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the pine tree, then the Spanish for the bush.

You'll want to know: la vegetación.
la vegetación — "the vegetation, the plant life".
La vegetación es muy verde en el norte. The vegetation is very green in the north.
A general word for all the plants of a place.

The word, taken apart.
vegetación is a learned borrowing from Late Latin vegetātiō "an enlivening, a quickening" (Lewis & Short), from vegetāre "to enliven".
