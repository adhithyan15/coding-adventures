## You'll want to know: qiánshuǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C444-ISLAND444-01]; assesses=[] -->

*qiánshuǐ* — "to go diving; to dive under water".

> *xiàtiān wǒmen qù hǎibiān qiánshuǐ* — In summer we go diving at the seaside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiánshuǐ* is built from *qián* 'to go under' and 水 'water', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjem* and *sywijX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as water flowing hidden and 'to hide'.
