Marathi, chapter 545: Guessing the Future.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भवितव्य (bhavitavya) — destiny; what lies ahead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to postpone, then the Marathi for to see through.

You'll want to know: भवितव्य.
भवितव्य — bhavitavya — "destiny; what lies ahead".
मुलांचं भवितव्य शिक्षणावर अवलंबून असतं. mulāñcaṁ bhavitavya śikṣaṇāvar avlambūn astaṁ. Children's future depends on their education.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
भवितव्य (bhavitavya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit bhavitavya, "what is to be" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
वर्तवणे (vartavṇe) — to forecast; to predict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a convenience, then the Marathi for to conjecture.

You'll want to know: वर्तवणे.
वर्तवणे — vartavṇe — "to forecast; to predict".
हवामान विभागाने उद्या पावसाचा अंदाज वर्तवला. havāmān vibhāgāne udyā pāvsācā andāj vartavlā. The weather department forecast rain for tomorrow.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अंदाज वर्तवला, tyāne andāj vartavlā.
Mostly with अंदाज or भाकीत: अंदाज वर्तवणे (vartavṇe).

The word, taken apart.
वर्तवणे (vartavṇe) is a learned Marathi verb, usually traced to the Sanskrit stem vart-, "to turn, to set going", as in vartana, "setting in motion" (Monier-Williams). How it came to mean "to forecast" is not settled.
