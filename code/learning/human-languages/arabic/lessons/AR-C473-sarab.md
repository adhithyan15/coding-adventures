## You'll want to know: سراب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C473-WAHA473-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سراب** — *sarāb* — "a mirage".

> رأى المسافر سرابا في الصحراء.
>
> *raʾā al-musāfir sarāban fī aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ.*
>
> The traveller saw a mirage in the desert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سراب** (*sarāb*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-b*, "to flow; to go away" (Lane). A mirage shimmers like flowing water and then is gone.
