# अत्यन्तम् (atyantam) — extremely

## You'll want to know: अत्यन्तम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C377-ATIVA377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अत्यन्तम्** — *atyantam* — "extremely".

> यदि ग्रीष्मः अत्यन्तम् उष्णः, तर्हि जनाः बहिः न गच्छन्ति।
>
> *yadi grīṣmaḥ atyantam uṣṇaḥ, tarhi janāḥ bahiḥ na gacchanti.*
>
> If the summer is extremely hot, people do not go out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अत्यन्तम्** (*atyantam*) is built from *ati*, "beyond", and *anta*, "an end"; MW gives *aty-anta* "excessive, very great, very strong" and "endless" (MW).
