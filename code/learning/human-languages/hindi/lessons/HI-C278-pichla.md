# पिछला (pichlā) — last, previous

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C191-TIME191-01, HI-LEX-C191-TIME191-02] -->

## You'll want to know: पिछला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C278-PICHLA-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C191-TIME191-01, HI-LEX-C191-TIME191-02, HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-02, HI-LEX-C214-QUAL214-02, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

**पिछला** — *pichlā* — "last, previous": the one before this one.

> **पिछले हफ़्ते मैं गाँव गया।**
>
> *pichle hafte maiṁ gā̃v gayā.* — "Last week I went to the village."

**पिछला** is an adjective, and it agrees: **पिछला हफ़्ता** (*pichlā haftā*), "last week", but **पिछली बार** (*pichlī bār*), "last time", because **बार** is feminine.

Said as a time, "last week" or "last year" takes the form you met in **बच्चे को**: **पिछले हफ़्ते**, **पिछले साल**.

> **पिछले साल हम बहुत व्यस्त थे।**
>
> *pichle sāl ham bahut vyast the.* — "Last year we were very busy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पिछला** (*pichlā*) has no row of its own in CDIAL. It is usually connected with **पीछे** (*pīche*), "behind", which CDIAL traces to *\*paśca*, "hinder part" (CDIAL 7990). The last week, on that picture, is the one behind you.
