## You'll want to know: обрушиться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C301-KATASTROFA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**обрушиться** (*obrúshitsya*) — "to collapse, to cave in".

> Старый мост обрушился.
>
> *Stáryy most obrúshilsya.*
>
> The old bridge collapsed.

Perfective; imperfective *обрушиваться* (*obrúshivatsya*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обрушиться* is built on *об-* and *рушить* "to pull down", an old East Slavic verb; EtymDB lists *рушить* with no parent.
