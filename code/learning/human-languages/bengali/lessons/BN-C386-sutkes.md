## You'll want to know: সুটকেস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C386-KULI386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সুটকেস** — *suṭkes* — "a suitcase".

> আমি সুটকেসটা খাটের নিচে রাখলাম।
>
> *āmi suṭkesṭā khāṭer niche rākhlām.*
>
> I put the suitcase under the bed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সুটকেস** (*suṭkes*) is borrowed from English *suitcase*, a case for a suit of clothes, with the English t said as the hard Bengali ট.
