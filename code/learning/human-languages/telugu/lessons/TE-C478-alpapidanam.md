## You'll want to know: అల్పపీడనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C478-PEDAGALI478-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అల్పపీడనం** — *alpapīḍanaṁ* — "a low-pressure area".

> సముద్రంలో అల్పపీడనం ఏర్పడింది.
>
> *samudraṁlō alpapīḍanaṁ ērpaḍindi.*
>
> A low-pressure area has formed over the sea.

The weather news says it every monsoon: అల్ప, low, + పీడనం, pressure. A storm often follows.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అల్పపీడనం** (*alpapīḍanaṁ*) is a modern weather word built from two Sanskrit words: *alpa*, "small, little", and *pīḍana*, "pressing, squeezing" (Monier-Williams).
