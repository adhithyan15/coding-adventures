## You'll want to know: व्याप्नोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C303-DURBHIKSHA303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याप्नोति** — *vyāpnoti* — "spreads through; fills".

> रोगः सर्वं नगरं व्याप्नोति।
>
> *rogaḥ sarvaṃ nagaraṃ vyāpnoti.*
>
> The disease spreads through the whole city.

Past (it …): रोगः नगरं व्याप्तवान्, *rogaḥ nagaraṃ vyāptavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याप्नोति** (*vyāpnoti*) is built from *vi* and the root √āp, "to reach", which also gives *prāpnoti*; MW gives *vy-āp* as to reach through, pervade, fill (MW).
