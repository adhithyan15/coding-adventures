# تحقق (taḥaqqaqa) — to come true; to make sure

## You'll want to know: تحقق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C344-HIKAYA344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقق** — *taḥaqqaqa* — "to come true; to make sure".

> تحققت أمنية البنت الصغيرة.
>
> *taḥaqqaqat umniyyat al-bint aṣ-ṣaghīra.*
>
> The little girl’s wish came true.

Present: يتحقق, *yataḥaqqaq*.

تحقق من: to check: تحقق من الموعد.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقق** (*taḥaqqaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-q-q* (Lane), the root of *ḥaqq*, truth. It is Form V, "to become true".
