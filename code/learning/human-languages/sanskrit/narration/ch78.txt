Sanskrit, chapter 78: Mango, Banana and Bread.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आम्रम् (āmram) — a mango.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the roof, then the Sanskrit for a key.

You'll want to know: आम्रम्.
आम्रम् (āmram) — "a mango", the फलम् of summer.

What you've built.
The summer fruit.
