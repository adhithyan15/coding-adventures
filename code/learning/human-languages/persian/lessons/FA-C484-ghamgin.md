## You'll want to know: غمگین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C484-SADNESS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غمگین** (*ghamgin*) — "sad, sorrowful".

> او بعد از رفتن دوستش غمگین بود.
>
> *u ba'd az raftan-e dustash ghamgin bud.*
>
> She was sad after her friend left.

*gham* 'sorrow' + the adjective ending *-gin*. More literary than *nârâhat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ghamgin* is built in Persian on *gham*, 'sorrow', which was borrowed from Arabic *ġamm* (EtymDB), with the native adjective ending *-gin*.
