# apagar — make the active thing quiet

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C312-ASK-12] -->

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *a-pa-GAR*: keep all three **a** sounds clear and stress the final syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-APAGAR]; assesses=[] -->

**apagar** — “to turn off” a device or light, and “to put out” a fire.

It continues Latin **pacāre**, “to calm or pacify.” The meaning moved from
making something quiet to stopping its light, fire or activity. In the cinema,
**apagar el teléfono** means “to turn off the phone.”
