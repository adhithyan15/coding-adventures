## You'll want to know: la catedral
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C677-MONUMENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la catedral** — "the cathedral".

> La catedral es del siglo XIII.
>
> *The cathedral is from the thirteenth century.*

The bishop’s church of a city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*catedral* is a learned borrowing from Latin *(ecclesia) cathedrālis* "the church with the chair", from *cathedra* "a chair" (Lewis & Short), Greek *kathédra*: the bishop’s seat.
