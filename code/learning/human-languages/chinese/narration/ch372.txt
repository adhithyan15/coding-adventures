Mandarin Chinese, chapter 372: Anyway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
fǎnzhèng — anyway; in any case.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to sum up, then the Mandarin for a principle you live by.

You'll want to know: fǎnzhèng.
fǎnzhèng — "anyway; in any case".
nǐ zuìhǎo bié zháojí fǎnzhèng hái yǒu shíjiān — Try not to worry; there's still time anyway.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fǎnzhèng is built from fǎn and 正 'proper', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjonX and tsyengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn over' and the second 'proper'. How the pair came to mean anyway is not settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for anyway, then the Mandarin for on earth (in a question).

You'll want to know: 同时.
同时 — tóngshí — "simultaneously; at the same moment".
他们两个人同时到了 tāmen liǎng ge rén tóngshí dào le — The two of them arrived at the same time.

The word, taken apart.
同时 (tóngshí) is built from 同 'the same' and 时 'a time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, duwng and dzyi (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'equal' and the second 'a time of day'.
