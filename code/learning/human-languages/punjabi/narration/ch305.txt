Punjabi, chapter 305: Harvest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਵਾਢੀ (vāḍhī) — the harvest; reaping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an ear of grain, then the Punjabi for a grain.

You'll want to know: ਵਾਢੀ.
ਵਾਢੀ — vāḍhī — "the harvest; reaping".
ਵਾਢੀ ਵਿਸਾਖ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ। vāḍhī visākh vich hovegī. The reaping will be in Visakh.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਾਢੀ (vāḍhī) is inherited from Sanskrit vṛddhi, "cutting off"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vāḍhī, "harvesting, harvest", under it (CDIAL 12075).

ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਕਣਕ ਲੁਣਾਂਗੇ। asīṁ agle hafte kaṇak luṇāṅge. We will reap the wheat next week.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਣਕ ਲੁਣੀ, maiṁ kaṇak luṇī.

ਲੁਣਨਾ (luṇnā) is inherited from Sanskrit lunāti, "cuts, reaps"; CDIAL lists Punjabi luṇnā, "cuts, reaps", under it (CDIAL 11082).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਢੇਰ (ḍher) — a heap; a pile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a sickle, then the Punjabi for to thresh.

You'll want to know: ਢੇਰ.
ਢੇਰ — ḍher — "a heap; a pile".
ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਣਿਆਂ ਦਾ ਵੱਡਾ ਢੇਰ ਹੈ। khet vich dāṇiāṁ dā vaḍḍā ḍher hai. There is a big heap of grain in the field.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਢੇਰ (ḍher) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍhēra, "lump, heap"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍher, "heap", under it (CDIAL 5599).
