# ఆరోపించు (ārōpiñcu) — to allege; to accuse

## You'll want to know: ఆరోపించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C406-AVINITI406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరోపించు** — *ārōpiñcu* — "to allege; to accuse".

> అతను డబ్బు తీసుకున్నాడని ఆమె ఆరోపించింది.
>
> *atanu ḍabbu tīsukunnāḍani āme ārōpiñcindi.*
>
> She alleged that he had taken the money.

Past (I …): ఆరోపించాను, *ārōpiñcānu*.

What is alleged is a clause ending in అని.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరోపించు** (*ārōpiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *āropa*, "charging with; assigning or attributing to" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
