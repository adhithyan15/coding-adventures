## You'll want to know: やきとり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C422-WASHOKU422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**やきとり** — *yakitori* — "grilled chicken on skewers".

> **えきまえの みせで やきとりを たべました。** — *ekimae no mise de yakitori o tabemashita* — "We had grilled chicken at a shop in front of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やきとり** (*yakitori*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yaki*, "grilling" (from *yaku*) and *tori*, "bird"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
