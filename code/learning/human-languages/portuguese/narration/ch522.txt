Portuguese, chapter 522: Na galeria.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
a exposição — exhibition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for ballerina, then the Portuguese for (theatre) dressing room.

You'll want to know: a exposição.
a exposição — "exhibition".
Há uma exposição de fotografia no museu. There is a photography exhibition at the museum.
Inaugurar uma exposição — to open an exhibition.

The word, taken apart.
exposição is a learned borrowing from Latin expositiō "a setting out" (EtymDB), from expōnere, built on pōnere "to put".

What you've built.
