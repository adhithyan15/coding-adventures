## You'll want to know: स्पर्धा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C524-SPARDHA524-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्धा** — *spardhā* — "a contest; rivalry".

> श्वः विद्यालये क्रीडायाः स्पर्धा अस्ति।
>
> *śvaḥ vidyālaye krīḍāyāḥ spardhā asti.*
>
> Tomorrow there is a sports contest at school.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्धा** (*spardhā*) comes from the root √spardh, "to contend, to vie with"; MW gives *spardhā* as emulation, rivalry, competition (MW).
