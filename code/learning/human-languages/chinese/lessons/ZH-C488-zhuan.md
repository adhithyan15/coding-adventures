## You'll want to know: zhuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C488-TRADES488-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuàn* — "to earn money; to make a profit".

> *tā yí ge yuè zhuàn yíwàn kuài* — He earns ten thousand yuan a month.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuàn* has no entry in the Guangyun, the dictionary this track cites for older forms. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
