## You'll want to know: o velório
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C314-FALECER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o velório** — "the wake".

> Muitos vizinhos foram ao velório.
>
> *Many neighbours went to the wake.*

The night when people keep watch with the dead before the burial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*velório* is built on *velar* "to keep watch" with the ending *-ório*; *velar* comes from Latin *vigilāre* "to stay awake, to keep watch" (Lewis & Short).
