## You'll want to know: 月饼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C341-MOONCAKE341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**月饼** — *yuèbǐng* — "mooncake".

> **我买了两个月饼** *wǒ mǎi le liǎng ge yuèbǐng* — I bought two mooncakes.

The new character, in pieces: 饼 — nine strokes: on the left the food piece of 饭, three strokes; on the right two short strokes, two bars, and two strokes down. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**月饼** (*yuèbǐng*) is built from 月 'the moon' and 饼 'a cake', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjwot* and *pjiengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which gives the second only its reading.
