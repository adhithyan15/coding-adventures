## You'll want to know: il graffio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C483-FERITE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il graffio** — "scratch".

> Il gatto mi ha fatto un graffio sulla mano.
>
> *The cat scratched my hand.*

The verb is *graffiare* — to scratch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *graffio* is not settled; it is often linked to a Germanic word for a hook or claw.
