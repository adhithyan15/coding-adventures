Lesson 1 of 6.
जुआ (juā) — gambling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an opportunity (formal), then the Hindi for a turn (in a game).

You'll want to know: जुआ.
जुआ — juā — "gambling".
जुआ (juā) खेलना एक बुरी आदत है। Juā khelnā ek burī ādat hai. Gambling is a bad habit.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
जुआ (juā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit dyūta, "gambling" (CDIAL 6608).
