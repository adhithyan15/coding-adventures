## You'll want to know: آواز خواندن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C519-SINGING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آواز خواندن** (*âvâz khândan*) — "to sing".

> مادرم در آشپزخانه آواز میخواند.
>
> *mâdaram dar âshpazkhâne âvâz mikhânad.*
>
> My mother sings in the kitchen.

*âvâz* 'song, voice' + *khândan* 'to read, to sing'; present stem *khân-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âvâz* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Middle Persian word for 'voice, sound, tune' and lists Latin *vōx*, 'voice', as a cousin (EtymDB). *khândan* is inherited too.
