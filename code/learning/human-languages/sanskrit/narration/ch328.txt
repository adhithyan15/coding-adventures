Sanskrit, chapter 328: Fasting and Offerings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
व्रतम् (vratam) — a vow; a religious observance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for scatters, then the Sanskrit for tosses up.

You'll want to know: व्रतम्.
व्रतम् — vratam — "a vow; a religious observance".
माता अद्य व्रतं पालयति। mātā adya vrataṃ pālayati. Mother keeps a vow today.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
व्रतम् (vratam) comes from the root √vṛ, "to choose", by MW's account; MW gives vrata as will, command, rule, and also a vow or religious observance (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उपवासः (upavāsaḥ) — a fast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tosses up, then the Sanskrit for a vow.

You'll want to know: उपवासः.
उपवासः — upavāsaḥ — "a fast".
सा सर्वं दिनम् उपवासं करोति। sā sarvaṃ dinam upavāsaṃ karoti. She fasts the whole day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उपवासः (upavāsaḥ) is built from upa and the root √vas, "to dwell, stay"; MW gives upavāsa as a fast, fasting as a religious act (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
नैवेद्यम् (naivedyam) — a food offering.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a vow, then the Sanskrit for a fast.

You'll want to know: नैवेद्यम्.
नैवेद्यम् — naivedyam — "a food offering".
माता मन्दिरे नैवेद्यं स्थापयति। mātā mandire naivedyaṃ sthāpayati. Mother places a food offering in the temple.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
नैवेद्यम् (naivedyam) is usually traced to nivedya, "to be offered", from the verb of nivedayati, with the lengthened first vowel; MW gives naivedya as an offering of food presented to a deity (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अर्पयति (arpayati) — offers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a fast, then the Sanskrit for a food offering.

You'll want to know: अर्पयति.
अर्पयति — arpayati — "offers".
बालिका पुष्पाणि अर्पयति। bālikā puṣpāṇi arpayati. The girl offers flowers.
Past (she …): सा पुष्पाणि अर्पितवती, sā puṣpāṇi arpitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
अर्पयति (arpayati) comes from the root √ṛ, "to go, move": MW lists arpaya as its causative, to make go, hand over, offer (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रसादः (prasādaḥ) — a blessed offering of food; grace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a food offering, then the Sanskrit for offers.

You'll want to know: प्रसादः.
प्रसादः — prasādaḥ — "a blessed offering of food; grace".
उत्सवस्य अनन्तरं सर्वे प्रसादं खादन्ति। utsavasya anantaraṃ sarve prasādaṃ khādanti. After the festival everyone eats the blessed food.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसादः (prasādaḥ) is built from pra and the root √sad, "to sit, settle"; MW gives prasāda as clearness, favour, grace, and also the food presented to a deity and then shared (MW).
