## You'll want to know: ಮಾಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C468-GATRA468-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾಸು** — *māsu* — "to fade; to grow dull".

> ಎರಡು ಸಲ ತೊಳೆದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಂಗಿಯ ಬಣ್ಣ ಮಾಸಿತು.
>
> *eraḍu sala toḷeda mēle aṅgiya baṇṇa māsitu.*
>
> After two washes the colour of the shirt faded.

Past (it …): ಮಾಸಿತು, *māsitu*.

Of colours, cloth and old paint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾಸು** (*māsu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4781 lists Kannada *māsu*, "to grow dim, to be obscured, to fade".
