Urdu, chapter 419: Profit and Loss.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منافع (munāfā) — a profit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a payment, then the Urdu for to bring down (a price).

You'll want to know: منافع.
منافع — munāfā — "a profit".
اس سال دکان کو اچھا منافع ہوا۔ is sāl dukān ko achchhā munāfā huā. The shop made a good profit this year.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
منافع (munāfā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic manāfiʿ, "benefits, gains".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

دکان کے لیے سرمایہ چاہیے۔ dukān ke liye sarmāyā chāhiye. You need capital to open a shop.

The word, taken apart.
سرمایہ (sarmāyā) is built from Persian sar, "a head", and māya, "substance": it is usually traced to Persian sarmāya, "capital".

Lesson 5 of 5.
سمیٹنا (sameṭnā) — to pack up; to gather up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for capital (money), then the Urdu for an asset.

You'll want to know: سمیٹنا.
سمیٹنا — sameṭnā — "to pack up; to gather up".
شام کو دکاندار نے اپنا سامان سمیٹا۔ shām ko dukāndār ne apnā sāmān sameṭā. In the evening the shopkeeper packed up his goods.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کپڑے سمیٹے, us ne kapṛe sameṭe.

The word, taken apart.
سمیٹنا (sameṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sameṭnā, "to roll up", under Sanskrit saṃveṣṭayati, "wraps up" (CDIAL 13026).
