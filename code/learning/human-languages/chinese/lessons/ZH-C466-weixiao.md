## You'll want to know: wēixiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C466-CALM466-04]; assesses=[] -->

*wēixiào* — "to give a gentle smile".

> *tā wēixiào de shíhou zuì hǎokàn* — She looks her best when she smiles.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wēixiào* is built from *wēi* 'slight' and 笑 'to laugh', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mj+j* and *sjewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'fine' and 'small' and the second 'glad'.
