Telugu, chapter 337: Under Arrest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అరెస్టు చేయు (aresṭu cēyu) — to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an uproar, then the Telugu for to disclose.

You'll want to know: అరెస్టు చేయు.
అరెస్టు చేయు — aresṭu cēyu — "to arrest".
పోలీసులు దొంగను అరెస్టు చేశారు. pōlīsulu doṅganu aresṭu cēśāru. The police arrested the thief.
Past (I …): అరెస్టు చేశాను, aresṭu cēśānu.
An English word with చేయు, as in టైప్ చేయు; who is arrested takes -ను.

The word, taken apart.
అరెస్టు చేయు (aresṭu cēyu) is a loan from English arrest with the native verb cēyu, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.
పట్టుబడు (paṭṭubaḍu) — to get caught.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to disclose, then the Telugu for to arrest.

You'll want to know: పట్టుబడు.
పట్టుబడు — paṭṭubaḍu — "to get caught".
దొంగ బస్టాండులో పట్టుబడ్డాడు. doṅga basṭāṇḍulō paṭṭubaḍḍāḍu. The thief got caught at the bus stand.
Past (I …): పట్టుబడ్డాను, paṭṭubaḍḍānu.
పట్టు, to catch, with పడు, which turns an action into something that happens to you.

The word, taken apart.
పట్టుబడు (paṭṭubaḍu) is a native compound: paṭṭu, "to hold, catch" (DEDR 4034), with paḍu, the helping verb that makes it something suffered.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఖైదీ (khaidī) — a prisoner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to arrest, then the Telugu for to get caught.

You'll want to know: ఖైదీ.
ఖైదీ — khaidī — "a prisoner".
ఖైదీ జైలు నుంచి పారిపోయాడు. khaidī jailu nuñci pāripōyāḍu. The prisoner escaped from jail.
Plural: ఖైదీలు, khaidīlu.
From ఖైదు, prison or custody: ఖైదీ (khaidī) is the one held.

The word, taken apart.
ఖైదీ (khaidī) is a loan from Persian qaidī, "a prisoner".

Lesson 4 of 5.
సంకెళ్ళు (saṅkeḷḷu) — shackles; chains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to get caught, then the Telugu for a prisoner.

You'll want to know: సంకెళ్ళు.
సంకెళ్ళు — saṅkeḷḷu — "shackles; chains".
పోలీసులు అతని చేతులకు సంకెళ్ళు వేశారు. pōlīsulu atani cētulaku saṅkeḷḷu vēśāru. The police put chains on his hands.
A plural word: సంకెళ్ళు (saṅkeḷḷu) వేయు, to put in chains.

The word, taken apart.
సంకెళ్ళు (saṅkeḷḷu) is usually traced to Sanskrit śṛṅkhalā, "a chain, a fetter" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
నిందితుడు (nindituḍu) — the accused; a suspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a prisoner, then the Telugu for shackles.

You'll want to know: నిందితుడు.
నిందితుడు — nindituḍu — "the accused; a suspect".
నిందితుడు తాను దొంగ కాదని చెప్పాడు. nindituḍu tānu doṅga kādani ceppāḍu. The accused said that he was not the thief.
Built on the Sanskrit word for blame, నింద; a woman is నిందితురాలు.

The word, taken apart.
నిందితుడు (nindituḍu) is borrowed from Sanskrit nindita, "blamed, censured" (CDIAL 7213), with the Telugu masculine ending -uḍu.
