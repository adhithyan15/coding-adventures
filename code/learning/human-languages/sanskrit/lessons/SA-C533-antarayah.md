## You'll want to know: अन्तरायः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C533-ANTARAYA533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्तरायः** — *antarāyaḥ* — "an impediment".

> यदि मार्गे अन्तरायः अस्ति, तर्हि वयम् अन्यं मार्गं गच्छामः।
>
> *yadi mārge antarāyaḥ asti, tarhi vayam anyaṃ mārgaṃ gacchāmaḥ.*
>
> If there is something in the way, we take another road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्तरायः** (*antarāyaḥ*) is built on *antar*, "between", and *aya*, "a going", from the root √i, "to go": what comes between; MW gives *antarāya* as intervention, an obstacle (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
