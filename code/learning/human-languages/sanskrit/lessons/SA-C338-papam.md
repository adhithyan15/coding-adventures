## You'll want to know: पापम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C338-SVARGA338-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पापम्** — *pāpam* — "a sin; evil".

> चौर्यं पापम् अस्ति।
>
> *cauryaṃ pāpam asti.*
>
> Theft is a sin.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पापम्** (*pāpam*) MW gives *pāpa* as bad, wicked, evil and, as a noun, evil, sin, without a root in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
