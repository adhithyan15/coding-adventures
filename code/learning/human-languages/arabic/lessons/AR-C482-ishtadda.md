## You'll want to know: اشتد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C482-RIH482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتد** — *ishtadda* — "to grow stronger; to intensify".

> اشتدت العاصفة في المساء.
>
> *ishtaddat al-ʿāṣifa fī al-masāʾ.*
>
> The storm grew stronger in the evening.

Present: يشتد, *yashtadd*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتد** (*ishtadda*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-d-d*, "to be firm; to bind tightly" (Lane). It is Form VIII.
