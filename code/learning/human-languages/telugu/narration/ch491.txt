Telugu, chapter 491: Wear and Tear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అరిగిపోవు (arigipōvu) — to wear down; to wear out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to split, then the Telugu for to break off.

You'll want to know: అరిగిపోవు.
అరిగిపోవు — arigipōvu — "to wear down; to wear out".
ఎక్కువ నడవడంతో నా చెప్పులు అరిగిపోయాయి. ekkuva naḍavaḍantō nā ceppulu arigipōyāyi. With so much walking, my sandals have worn down.
Past (it …): అరిగిపోయింది, arigipōyindi.
Said of soles, tyres and steps worn thin by use. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: అరిగిపోయింది, it wore down.

The word, taken apart.
అరిగిపోవు (arigipōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 316 lists Telugu aṟugu, "to wear away, to waste away; to be digested", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572), which marks a change that is complete.

Lesson 2 of 5.
బూజుపట్టు (būjupaṭṭu) — to go mouldy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to break off, then the Telugu for to wear down.

You'll want to know: బూజుపట్టు.
బూజుపట్టు — būjupaṭṭu — "to go mouldy".
తేమ వల్ల రొట్టె బూజుపట్టింది. tēma valla roṭṭe būjupaṭṭindi. Because of the damp the bread went mouldy.
Past (it …): బూజుపట్టింది, būjupaṭṭindi.
బూజు, mould, + పట్టు, to catch. A house cobweb is also బూజు. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: బూజుపట్టింది, it went mouldy.

The word, taken apart.
బూజుపట్టు (būjupaṭṭu) is native Dravidian: బూజు, "mould, mildew" (DEDR 4357), and పట్టు, "to catch" (DEDR 4034).

Lesson 3 of 5.
వెలిసిపోవు (velisipōvu) — to fade.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to wear down, then the Telugu for to go mouldy.

You'll want to know: వెలిసిపోవు.
వెలిసిపోవు — velisipōvu — "to fade".
ఎండకు చీర రంగు వెలిసిపోయింది. eṇḍaku cīra raṅgu velisipōyindi. In the sun the colour of the sari faded.
Past (it …): వెలిసిపోయింది, velisipōyindi.
Said of colours and of old photos and memories. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: వెలిసిపోయింది, it faded.

The word, taken apart.
వెలిసిపోవు (velisipōvu) is a Telugu compound whose first part has an origin that is not settled; the second is the native పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 5 of 5.
క్షీణించు (kṣīṇiñcu) — to deteriorate; to decline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to fade, then the Telugu for to crack.

You'll want to know: క్షీణించు.
క్షీణించు — kṣīṇiñcu — "to deteriorate; to decline".
వృద్ధుడి ఆరోగ్యం క్షీణించింది. vṛddhuḍi ārōgyaṁ kṣīṇiñcindi. The old man's health deteriorated.
Past (it …): క్షీణించింది, kṣīṇiñcindi.
Said of health, of an old building and of a river that runs low. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: క్షీణించింది, it declined.

The word, taken apart.
క్షీణించు (kṣīṇiñcu) is built from Sanskrit kṣīṇa, "diminished, wasted away" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
