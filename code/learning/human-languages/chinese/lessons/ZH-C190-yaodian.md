## You'll want to know: yàodiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C190-TOWN190-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yàodiàn* — "chemist's; pharmacy".

> *yàodiàn jǐ diǎn kāimén* — What time does the chemist's open?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yàodiàn* is built from *yào* 'medicine' and 店 'shop', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yak* and *temH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a herb that cures illness.

## What you've built
