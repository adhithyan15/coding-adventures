## You'll want to know: 进站
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C407-PULLIN407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**进站** — *jìnzhàn* — "to pull into the station (of a train or bus)".

> **火车进站了现在检票** *huǒchē jìnzhàn le xiànzài jiǎnpiào* — The train's pulling in: they're checking tickets now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**进站** (*jìnzhàn*) is built from 进 'to go forward, to go in' and 站 'a station', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsinH* and *treamH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
