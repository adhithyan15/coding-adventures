Gujarati, chapter 353: Citizens and Votes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નાગરિક (nāgrik) — a citizen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the people of a state, then the Gujarati for to shape.

You'll want to know: નાગરિક.
નાગરિક — nāgrik — "a citizen".
દરેક નાગરિકને મત આપવાનો હક છે. darek nāgrikne mat āpvāno hak che. Every citizen has the right to vote.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
નાગરિક (nāgrik) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit nāgarika, "living in a town, civic" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
મતદાન (matdān) — voting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a citizen, then the Gujarati for the public.

You'll want to know: મતદાન.
મતદાન — matdān — "voting".
રવિવારે ગામમાં મતદાન થશે. ravivāre gāmmā̃ matdān thaśe. The village votes on Sunday.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મતદાન (matdān) is built from મત (mat), "an opinion, a vote", and Sanskrit dāna, "giving" (Monier-Williams): the giving of votes.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઉમેદવાર (umedvār) — a candidate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the public, then the Gujarati for voting.

You'll want to know: ઉમેદવાર.
ઉમેદવાર — umedvār — "a candidate".
આ વખતે ત્રણ ઉમેદવાર છે. ā vakhte traṇ umedvār che. There are three candidates this time.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ઉમેદવાર (umedvār) is usually traced to Persian umīdvār, "hopeful", from umīd, "hope": the one who hopes to be chosen.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ચૂંટાવું (cū̃ṭāvũ) — to be elected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for voting, then the Gujarati for a candidate.

You'll want to know: ચૂંટાવું.
ચૂંટાવું — cū̃ṭāvũ — "to be elected".
આ વખતે એક જુવાન ઉમેદવાર ચૂંટાશે. ā vakhte ek juvān umedvār cū̃ṭāśe. This time a young candidate will be elected.
Past (he …): તે ઉમેદવાર ચૂંટાયો, te umedvār cū̃ṭāyo.

The word, taken apart.
ચૂંટાવું (cū̃ṭāvũ) is the -āvũ partner of ચૂંટવું (cū̃ṭvũ), "to pick", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit cuṭáti, "cuts off" (CDIAL 4857): to be picked by the voters.
