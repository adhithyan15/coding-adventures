## You'll want to know: ухаживать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C306-POMOLVKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ухаживать** (*ukházhivat*) — "to court; to look after".

> Он красиво ухаживал за ней.
>
> *On krasívo ukházhival za ney.*
>
> He courted her beautifully.

Imperfective; it takes *за* + instrumental. Also to look after someone: *ухаживать за бабушкой*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ухаживать* is built on *у-* and *хаживать*, an old repeated form of *ходить* "to go", inherited from Proto-Slavic *xoditi* (EtymDB): to keep going round someone.
