# పదండి (padaṇḍi) — let's go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-03, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for home, then the Telugu for leave.

## You'll want to know: పదండి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పదండి** (*padaṇḍi*) — "let's go", said to the group you are leaving with.

It carries the respectful **-అండి** like **raṇḍi** and **āgaṇḍi**, so it stays polite even when
it hurries people along.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Take care, come soon, I am going home, farewell, let's go: a whole exit.
