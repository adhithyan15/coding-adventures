# とける (tokeru) — to melt; to thaw

## You'll want to know: とける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C212-TEMP212-05]; assesses=[] -->

**とける** — *tokeru* — "to melt; to thaw".

> **らいしゅう ゆきが とけます。** — *raishū yuki ga tokemasu* — "The snow will melt next week."

Polite **とけます**, *tokemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とける** (*tokeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
