## You'll want to know: fēnshǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C307-SPLIT307-01]; assesses=[] -->

*fēnshǒu* — "to break up; to end a relationship".

> *tāmen fēnshǒu le, wǒ hěn nánguò* — They've split up, and I'm sad.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēnshǒu* is built from *fēn* 'to part' and 手 'a hand', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjun* and *syuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to give out' and, quoting an older dictionary, 'to separate': to part hands.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
