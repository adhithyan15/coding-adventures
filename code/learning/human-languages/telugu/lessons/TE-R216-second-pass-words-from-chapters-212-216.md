(**అకస్మాత్తుగా**, *akasmāttugā*.) Again and again? (**పదే పదే**, *padē padē*.) A little while? (**కాసేపు**, *kāsēpu*.) A second? (**సెకను**, *sekanu*.) The day before yesterday?
