## You'll want to know: 果然
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C301-ESCAPE301-05]; assesses=[] -->

**果然** — *guǒrán* — "sure enough; as expected".

> **他说会下雨果然下雨了** *tā shuō huì xiàyǔ, guǒrán xiàyǔ le* — He said it would rain, and sure enough it did.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**果然** (*guǒrán*) is built from 果 'resolute' and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaX* and *nyen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'resolute' and 'certain' and the second 'so, like this': so, for certain.
