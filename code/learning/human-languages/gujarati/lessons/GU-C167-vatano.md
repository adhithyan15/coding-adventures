## You'll want to know: વટાણો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C167-OKRA167-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વટાણો** — *vaṭāṇo* — "pea".

> **બાળક વટાણા ગણે છે.** — *bāḷak vaṭāṇā gaṇe che* — "The child counts the peas."

Mostly in the plural, **વટાણા**, *vaṭāṇā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **વટાણો** (*vaṭāṇo*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
