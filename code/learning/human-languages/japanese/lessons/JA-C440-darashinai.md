## You'll want to know: だらしない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C440-CAREFUL440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**だらしない** — *darashinai* — "sloppy; slovenly".

> **あには ふくが だらしないです。** — *ani wa fuku ga darashinai desu* — "My elder brother dresses sloppily."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だらしない** (*darashinai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; JMdict writes it in kana (JMdict).
