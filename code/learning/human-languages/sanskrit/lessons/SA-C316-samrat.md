## You'll want to know: सम्राट्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C316-SAMRAT316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्राट्** — *samrāṭ* — "an emperor".

> सम्राट् प्रासादे वसति।
>
> *samrāṭ prāsāde vasati.*
>
> The emperor lives in the palace.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्राट्** (*samrāṭ*) is built from *sam*, "fully", and the root √rāj, "to shine, rule"; MW gives *samrāj* as a universal ruler, a paramount sovereign (MW).
