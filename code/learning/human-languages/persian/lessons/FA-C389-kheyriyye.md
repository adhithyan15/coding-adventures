## You'll want to know: خیریه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C389-DAVTALAB-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خیریه** (*kheyriye*) — "a charity".

> او هر ماه به یک خیریه پول میدهد.
>
> *u har mâh be yek kheyriye pul midahad.*
>
> She gives money to a charity every month.

From *kheyr* "good". As an adjective: *mo'assese-ye kheyriye*, a charitable foundation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خیریه** is built on *kheyr* "good", borrowed from Arabic *khayr* "good, well" (EtymDB), with the ending *-iye*: a body that does good.
