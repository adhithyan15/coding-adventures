## HL-C341 — Hindi asking declares its stem and present forms

After the seeing-form repair, **पूछता** is the highest-ranked independent
modern Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 35 already
derives **पूछता**, **पूछती**, and **पूछते** from **पूछना**, practises the
gendered forms, and uses the respectful form in its explanation. Its headword
declares only the infinitive, however, so reports and downstream cards cannot
see the forms learners are explicitly taught.

Declare **पूछ**, **पूछता**, **पूछती**, and **पूछते** beside **पूछना**, make the
regular derivation explicit, practise the respectful form, and recall the
complete family. This closes the reporting gap without introducing a new
construction or increasing the gentle-ramp debt.
