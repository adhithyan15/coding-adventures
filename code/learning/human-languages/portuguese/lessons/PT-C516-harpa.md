## You'll want to know: a harpa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C516-ORQUESTRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a harpa** — "harp".

> A harpa é um dos instrumentos mais antigos.
>
> *The harp is one of the oldest instruments.*

The player is *o harpista* or *a harpista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*harpa* goes back, through Old Portuguese *arpa*, to Late Latin *harpa*, which was taken from Frankish *harpa* (EtymDB).
