Lesson 1 of 5.
شطرنج (shatranj) — chess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tulip, then the Persian for jasmine.

You'll want to know: شطرنج.
شطرنج — shatranj — "chess".

The word, taken apart.
shatranj is borrowed from Arabic šaṭranj, which goes back to Middle Persian čatrang, and that to Sanskrit caturaṅga, the "four-limbed" army (EtymDB). Persian took its own word back in Arabic dress.

Lesson 2 of 5.
کوهنوردی (kuhnavardi) — mountain climbing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for jasmine, then the Persian for chess.

You'll want to know: کوهنوردی.
کوهنوردی — kuhnavardi — "mountain climbing".

The word, taken apart.
kuhnavardi is kuh, "mountain" (Middle Persian kōf, EtymDB), with navard, "roaming" (from navardidan), and -i: mountain-roaming. All are native Persian.

You'll want to know: نقاشی.
نقاشی — naqqâshi — "painting".

The word, taken apart.
naqqâshi is naqqâsh, "painter", with the ending -i. naqqâsh is borrowed from Arabic naqqāš, "engraver, painter" (root n-q-š, Lane).
