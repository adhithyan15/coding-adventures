# ಬಂಧು (bandhu) — kin; a relative

## You'll want to know: ಬಂಧು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C526-BALAGA526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಧು** — *bandhu* — "kin; a relative".

> ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಂಧುಗಳು ನಮ್ಮ ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದರು.
>
> *habbakke ellā bandhugaḷu namma manege bandaru.*
>
> All our relatives came to our house for the festival.

Plural: ಬಂಧುಗಳು, *bandhugaḷu*.

ಬಂಧು-ಬಳಗ, relatives and friends.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಧು** (*bandhu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bandhu*, "kinsman" (CDIAL 9144), from *bandh*, "to bind".
