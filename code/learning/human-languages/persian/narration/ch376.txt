Persian, chapter 376: Whose fault is it.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تقصیر (taqsir) — a fault, the blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be worth it, then the Persian for to pay off.

You'll want to know: تقصیر.
تقصیر (taqsir) — "a fault, the blame".
این تقصیر من نبود. in taqsir-e man nabud. It wasn't my fault.
Taqsir-e kasi budan — to be someone's fault. Very common in speech.

The word, taken apart.
تقصیر (taqsir) comes from Arabic taqṣīr (EtymDB), a verbal noun usually explained as falling short; in Persian it is the fault or the blame.

Lesson 2 of 5.
گناه (gonâh) — a sin; guilt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pay off, then the Persian for a fault.

You'll want to know: گناه.
گناه (gonâh) — "a sin; guilt".
دزدی گناه است. dozdi gonâh ast. Stealing is a sin.
Gonâh-e u nist — it is not his fault. Gonâhkâr — guilty.

The word, taken apart.
گناه (gonâh) is an inherited Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian wināh "sin, offence"; EtymDB lists it without a parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
نقطه ضعف (noqte-ye za'f) — a weak point, a weakness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fault, then the Persian for a sin.

You'll want to know: نقطه ضعف.
نقطه ضعف (noqte-ye za'f) — "a weak point, a weakness".
نقطه ضعف او بیصبری است. noqte-ye za'f-e u bisabri ast. His weak point is impatience.
Noqte "point" + za'f "weakness". The opposite is noqte-ye qovvat, a strong point.

The word, taken apart.
نقطه ضعف (noqte-ye za'f) joins two words of Arabic origin with a Persian ezafe: noqte from Arabic nuqṭa "dot" (EtymDB), and za'f, usually traced to Arabic ḍa'f "weakness".

Lesson 4 of 5.
رخ دادن (rokh dâdan) — to happen, to occur.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sin, then the Persian for a weak point.

You'll want to know: رخ دادن.
رخ دادن (rokh dâdan) — "to happen, to occur".
این حادثه دیشب رخ داد. in hâdese dishab rokh dâd. This accident happened last night.
Formal; in speech ettefâq oftâdan. Stem rokh deh-.

The word, taken apart.
رخ دادن (rokh dâdan) "to occur" is in EtymDB; it is native Persian, built from rokh "face, appearance" and dâdan "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE deh₃-: an event shows its face.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اعتراف کردن (e'terâf kardan) — to admit, to confess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a weak point, then the Persian for to happen.

You'll want to know: اعتراف کردن.
اعتراف کردن (e'terâf kardan) — "to admit, to confess".
او به اشتباهش اعتراف کرد. u be eshtebâhash e'terâf kard. He admitted his mistake.
Takes be: be chizi e'terâf kardan. E'terâf "confession" + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
اعتراف was borrowed from Arabic i'tirāf "confession, admission" (EtymDB); with kardan "to do" it becomes a Persian compound verb.
