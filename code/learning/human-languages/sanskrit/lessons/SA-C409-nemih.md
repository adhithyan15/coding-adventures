## You'll want to know: नेमिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C409-CAKRA409-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नेमिः** — *nemiḥ* — "a wheel's rim".

> रथस्य नेमिः दृढा अस्ति।
>
> *rathasya nemiḥ dṛḍhā asti.*
>
> The chariot wheel's rim is strong.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नेमिः** (*nemiḥ*) comes from the root √nam, "to bend", which also gives *namati*, "bows"; MW gives *nemi* as the felly or rim of a wheel (MW).
