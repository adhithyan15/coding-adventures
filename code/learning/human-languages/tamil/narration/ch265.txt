Tamil, chapter 265: District, Hut, Tower, Prison, World.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மாவட்டம் (māvaṭṭam) — a district.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for land, then the Tamil for a valley.

You'll want to know: மாவட்டம்.
மாவட்டம் — māvaṭṭam — "a district".

The word, taken apart.
மாவட்டம் (māvaṭṭam) is mā, "great", and vaṭṭam, "circle", usually traced to Sanskrit vṛtta, "rounded" (CDIAL 12069): "the great circle".

Lesson 2 of 5.
குடிசை (kuṭicai) — a hut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a valley, then the Tamil for a district.

You'll want to know: குடிசை.
குடிசை — kuṭicai — "a hut".

The word, taken apart.
குடிசை (kuṭicai) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1655 lists Malayalam kuṭi, "house, hut" and Telugu guḍise. It is built on kuṭi, "dwelling". The entry compares Sanskrit kuṭi, "hut" (CDIAL 3232).
