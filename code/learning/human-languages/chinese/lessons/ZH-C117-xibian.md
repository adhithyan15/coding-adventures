## You'll want to know: xībian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C117-COMPASS117-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xībian* — "the west; on the west side".

> *xībian yǒu shān* — There are hills to the west.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xībian* is built from xī 'west' and 边 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sej* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary calls the west the direction of autumn, and explains the character as a bird settling on its nest when the sun is in the west.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
