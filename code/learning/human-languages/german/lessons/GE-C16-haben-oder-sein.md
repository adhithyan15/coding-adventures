# Practice — haben or sein?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-PERFEKT-SEIN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SEIN-ICH-BIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. The only skill left in this chapter is picking the
right helper without stopping to think, and that is drill.

## Grammar Lens: the whole family in one place
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-KOMMEN-01, GE-LEX-FAHREN-01, GE-LEX-WERDEN-01, GE-LEX-BLEIBEN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-PERFEKT-SEIN-AUSNAHMEN-01] -->

| verb | perfect | why |
|---|---|---|
| *gehen* | *ich bin gegangen* | motion |
| *kommen* | *ich bin gekommen* | motion |
| *fahren* | *ich bin gefahren* | motion |
| *werden* | *ich bin geworden* | change of state |
| *bleiben* | *ich bin geblieben* | learned |
| *sein* | *ich bin gewesen* | learned |
| *lernen* | *ich **habe** gelernt* | ordinary activity |
| *machen* | *ich **habe** gemacht* | ordinary activity |

Read the right-hand column and you can see how much of the work the motion
rule does on its own: only two rows have to be memorised outright.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-FAHREN-01, GE-LEX-BLEIBEN-01, GE-LEX-MUEDE-01, GE-GRAMMAR-PERFEKT-KEINE-KONGRUENZ-01] -->

| German | English |
|---|---|
| *Wo bist du gewesen?* | Where have you been? |
| *Ich bin nach Berlin gefahren.* | I travelled to Berlin. |
| *Und Anna? Ist sie gekommen?* | And Anna? Did she come? |
| *Anna ist zu Hause geblieben.* | Anna stayed at home. |
| *Ich habe viel gelernt. Jetzt bin ich müde.* | I learned a lot. Now I am tired. |

Note the third and fourth lines: *ist sie gekommen*, *Anna ist geblieben* — a
feminine subject both times, and not one letter added to either participle.
