## You'll want to know: ಸರಾಸರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C413-PANA413-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಾಸರಿ** — *sarāsari* — "an average".

> ತರಗತಿಯ ಸರಾಸರಿ ಅಂಕ ಅರವತ್ತು.
>
> *taragatiya sarāsari aṅka aravattu.*
>
> The class's average mark is sixty.

ಸರಾಸರಿ ತೆಗೆ, to work out the average.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಾಸರಿ** (*sarāsari*) is usually traced to Persian *sarāsarī*, "from end to end, general", which reached Kannada through Hindustani or Marathi.
