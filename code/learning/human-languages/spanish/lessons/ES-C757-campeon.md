## You'll want to know: el campeón
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C757-CAMPEON-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el campeón** — "the champion".

> Somos los campeones de la liga.
>
> *We are the league champions.*

*La campeona* for a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*campeón* was borrowed from Italian *campione* (EtymDB), from Late Latin *campiō* "a fighter in the field", built on *campus* "field".
