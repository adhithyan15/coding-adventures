# ڑ (ṛe) — the letter inside پڑھنا

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-CHE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C36-DAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write چ and د once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **پڑھنا** (*paṛhnā*), "to read".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ڑ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C36-RRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ڑ** — *ṛe*, an *r* made by curling the tongue back and flicking it forward.

It is inside **پڑھنا** (*paṛhnā*), "to read". It is **ر** with the same **small
curled-back mark** you put on **ٹ**. Urdu uses one mark for one idea: tongue
curled back.

## Writing: ڑ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C36-RRE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the independent re-series body downward
- **2.** without lifting, continue curving to the left
- **3.** after one lift, draw the small retroflex mark downward, back upward, and down again to close its loop — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Fe, qaf, te, dal, and re, independent ڑ calligraphic and handwriting animations and Re instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-CHE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C36-DAL-01, UR-SCRIPT-C36-RRE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **پڑھنا** and point at ڑ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ڑ three times, saying *ṛ* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: د, then ڑ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **پڑھنا** aloud once more]

come from? (**پڑھنا**, *paṛhnā*, "to read".)
