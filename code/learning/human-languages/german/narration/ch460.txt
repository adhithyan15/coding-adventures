German, chapter 460: Der Wetterbericht.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Wetterbericht — the weather report, the forecast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to graze, then the German for to milk.

You'll want to know: der Wetterbericht.
der Wetterbericht — "the weather report, the forecast".
Laut Wetterbericht regnet es morgen den ganzen Tag. According to the weather report it will rain all day tomorrow.
Plural: die Wetterberichte.

The word, taken apart.
Wetterbericht is built from Wetter 'weather', an inherited word and a cousin of English weather (EtymDB), and Bericht 'report', from Middle High German beriht (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
wolkenlos — cloudless.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: wolkenlos.
wolkenlos — "cloudless".
Am Nachmittag ist der Himmel wolkenlos. In the afternoon the sky is cloudless.
-los means without: arbeitslos, kostenlos.

The word, taken apart.
wolkenlos is built from Wolke 'cloud', inherited from Old High German wolkan (EtymDB), and -los 'without', from Proto-Germanic lausaz 'loose, free'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
bedeckt — overcast; covered.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the weather situation, then the German for cloudless.

You'll want to know: bedeckt.
bedeckt — "overcast; covered".
Morgens ist der Himmel bedeckt, später kommt die Sonne. In the morning the sky is overcast; later the sun comes out.
The past participle of bedecken 'to cover', used as an adjective.

The word, taken apart.
bedeckt is the participle of bedecken 'to cover', from decken, inherited from Old High German decchen and Proto-Germanic þakjaną (EtymDB), the root of Dach 'roof'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
umschlagen — to change suddenly (of the weather); to turn over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for cloudless, then the German for overcast.

You'll want to know: umschlagen.
umschlagen — "to change suddenly (of the weather); to turn over".
In den Bergen schlägt das Wetter schnell um. In the mountains the weather changes quickly.
Separable; Perfekt with sein for the weather: das Wetter ist umgeschlagen. Der Umschlag — the envelope.

The word, taken apart.
umschlagen is built from um 'over, round' and schlagen 'to strike, to turn', inherited from Proto-Germanic slahaną (EtymDB).
