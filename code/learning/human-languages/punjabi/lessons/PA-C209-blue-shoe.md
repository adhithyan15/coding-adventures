## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-NILI-209, PA-LEX-C94-CLOTHES94-02, PA-DEM-EH-01, PA-LEX-HAI-02, PA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-01, PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-PRENOMINAL-01, PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-GENDER-AGREEMENT-01, PA-GRAMMAR-VERB-LAST-02] -->

> **ਇਹ ਨੀਲੀ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਹੈ** — *eh nīlī juttī hai* — This is a blue shoe.

For this one frame, begin with familiar **ਇਹ**, place new **ਨੀਲੀ** before the
familiar feminine noun **ਜੁੱਤੀ**, and keep familiar **ਹੈ** at the end. The
adjective is feminine singular direct because it agrees with this one noun.
