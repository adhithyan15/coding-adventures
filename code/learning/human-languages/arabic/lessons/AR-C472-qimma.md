## You'll want to know: قمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C472-QIMMA472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قمة** — *qimma* — "a summit; a peak".

> وصلنا إلى قمة الجبل بعد الظهر.
>
> *waṣalnā ilā qimmat al-jabal baʿda aẓ-ẓuhr.*
>
> We reached the summit of the mountain in the afternoon.

Plural: قمم, *qimam*.

A meeting of heads of state is also a *qimma*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قمة** (*qimma*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-m-m*, "the top of a thing" (Lane).
