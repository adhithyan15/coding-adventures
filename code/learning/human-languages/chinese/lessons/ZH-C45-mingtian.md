## You'll want to know: míngtiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-05]; assesses=[] -->

*míngtiān* — "tomorrow".

> *wǒ jīntiān hěn máng, míngtiān bù máng* — I'm busy today, but not tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*míngtiān* is built from míng 'bright' and 天 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjaeng* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
