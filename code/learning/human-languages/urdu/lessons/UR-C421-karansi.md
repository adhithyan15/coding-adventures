## You'll want to know: کرنسی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C421-KAGHAZ421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کرنسی** — *karansī* — "a currency".

> سرحد پر ہم نے کرنسی بدلی۔
>
> *sarhad par ham ne karansī badlī.*
>
> We changed our money at the border.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کرنسی** (*karansī*) is borrowed from English *currency*.
