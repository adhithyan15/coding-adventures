Gujarati, chapter 214: Rain on the way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પડવું (paḍvũ) — to fall, to happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for cloudy, then the Gujarati for to pour down.

You'll want to know: પડવું.
પડવું — paḍvũ — "to fall, to happen".
સાંજે વરસાદ પડશે. — sā̃je varsād paḍśe — "It will rain in the evening."

The word, taken apart.
પડવું (paḍvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit patati, "flies; falls", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to fall, happen" (CDIAL 7722).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ધોધમાર (dhodhmār) — pouring, torrential.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pour down, then the Gujarati for to fall.

You'll want to know: ધોધમાર.
ધોધમાર — dhodhmār — "pouring, torrential".
કાલે ધોધમાર વરસાદ પડશે. — kāle dhodhmār varsād paḍśe — "There will be very heavy rain tomorrow."
It does not change for gender.

The word, taken apart.
ધોધમાર (dhodhmār) is built on dhodh, "waterfall", so the rain comes down like a waterfall. The second part, -mār, is often linked to mārvũ, "to strike", but CDIAL lists no form of the compound, so its make-up is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ટીપું (ṭīpũ) — a drop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to fall, then the Gujarati for pouring.

You'll want to know: ટીપું.
ટીપું — ṭīpũ — "a drop".
પાણીનું એક ટીપું પડશે. — pāṇīnũ ek ṭīpũ paḍśe — "A drop of water will fall."

The word, taken apart.
ટીપું (ṭīpũ) comes from ṭipp, "drip, drop", one of a set of imitative reconstructed forms that CDIAL gathers under ṭapp; it lists Gujarati ṭīpũ, "drop" (CDIAL 5444).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ટપકવું (ṭapakvũ) — to drip, to leak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for pouring, then the Gujarati for a drop.

You'll want to know: ટપકવું.
ટપકવું — ṭapakvũ — "to drip, to leak".
વરસાદમાં છત ટપકશે. — varsādmā̃ chat ṭapakśe — "The roof will leak in the rain."

The word, taken apart.
ટપકવું (ṭapakvũ) is built on ṭapp, "drip, drop", an imitative reconstructed form. CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun ṭapkũ, "drop", there, but not the verb itself (CDIAL 5444).

Lesson 5 of 5.
પલળવું (palaḷvũ) — to get soaked, to get wet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a drop, then the Gujarati for to drip.

You'll want to know: પલળવું.
પલળવું — palaḷvũ — "to get soaked, to get wet".
હું વરસાદમાં પલળીશ. — hũ varsādmā̃ palaḷīś — "I will get soaked in the rain."

The word, taken apart.
પલળવું (palaḷvũ) is probably a tadbhava. CDIAL lists its transitive partner palāḷvũ, "to make wet, soak", under pralālayate, "slobbers", a reconstructed form; palaḷvũ itself is not listed (CDIAL 8759).
