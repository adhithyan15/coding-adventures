## You'll want to know: ચોક્કસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C179-PLAN179-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોક્કસ** — *cokkas* — "certain, sure; exact".

> **હું ચોક્કસ આવીશ.** — *hũ cokkas āvīś* — "I will certainly come."
