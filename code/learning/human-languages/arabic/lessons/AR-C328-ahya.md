## You'll want to know: أحيا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C328-HAFL328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أحيا** — *aḥyā* — "to perform at; to revive".

> أحيا المطرب حفل العرس.
>
> *aḥyā al-muṭrib ḥafl al-ʿurs.*
>
> The singer performed at the wedding party.

Present: يحيي, *yuḥyī*.

أحيا حفلا: a singer performs at a party. أحيا ذكرى: to mark an anniversary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أحيا** (*aḥyā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-y-y* (Lane), "to live", the root of *ḥayāh*, life, and of the taught *ḥayawān*, an animal. It is Form IV, "to give life to": to bring a night or a memory to life.
