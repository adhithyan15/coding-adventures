## You'll want to know: तळहात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C477-HAT477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तळहात** — *taḷhāt* — "the palm".

> आजीने माझ्या तळहातावर साखर ठेवली.
>
> *ājīne mājhyā taḷhātāvar sākhar ṭhevlī.*
>
> Grandmother put some sugar on my palm.

Masculine.

तळ, the base or flat, + हात. The sole of the foot is तळपाय.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तळहात** (*taḷhāt*) is a Marathi compound of तळ, "base, flat", and हात, "hand", a tadbhava of Sanskrit *hasta* (CDIAL 14024).
