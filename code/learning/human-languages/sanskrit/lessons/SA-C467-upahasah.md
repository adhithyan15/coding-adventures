## You'll want to know: उपहासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C467-SISTA467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपहासः** — *upahāsaḥ* — "mockery; derision".

> अतिथेः उपहासः न उचितः।
>
> *atitheḥ upahāsaḥ na ucitaḥ.*
>
> Mocking a guest is not proper.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपहासः** (*upahāsaḥ*) is built from *upa*, "towards", and the root √has, "to laugh", the root of **हसति** and **उपहसति**; MW gives "laughter, derision, mockery, jeer" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
