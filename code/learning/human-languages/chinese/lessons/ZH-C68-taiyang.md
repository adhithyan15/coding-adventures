## You'll want to know: tàiyang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C68-NATURE68-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tàiyang* — "sun".

> *jīntiān de tàiyang hěn dà* — The sun is strong today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tàiyang* is built from tài 'great' and yang, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thajH* and *yang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second as the yang of yin and yang, 'high and bright': the great bright one.
