## You'll want to know: حالت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C365-HALAT365-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حالت** — *hālat* — "a state (of health, of repair)".

> اگر مریض کی حالت ٹھیک نہیں تو ہسپتال لے جائیے۔
>
> *agar marīz kī hālat ṭhīk nahīṅ to haspatāl le jāiye.*
>
> If the patient is not well, take him to hospital.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حالت** (*hālat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥāla*, "a state, a condition".
