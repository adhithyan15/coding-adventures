## You'll want to know: ਲੱਕੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C246-GHR246-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੱਕੜ** — *lakkaṛ* — "wood".

> ਇਹ ਮੇਜ਼ ਲੱਕੜ ਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh mez lakkaṛ dā hai.*
>
> This table is made of wood.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੱਕੜ** (*lakkaṛ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lakuṭa*, "club"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *lakkaṛ*, "wood" (CDIAL 10875). CDIAL marks *lakkaṛ* masculine and lists a feminine Punjabi form, also "wood", beside it; here ਲੱਕੜ is taught as feminine (ਗਿੱਲੀ ਲੱਕੜ).
