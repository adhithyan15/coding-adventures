Urdu, chapter 478: Cuts and Stitches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چبھنا (chubhnā) — to prick; to stick in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a paper packet, then the Urdu for to toss into the mouth.

You'll want to know: چبھنا.
چبھنا — chubhnā — "to prick; to stick in".
میری انگلی میں کانٹا چبھ گیا۔ merī unglī meṅ kā̃ṭā chubh gayā. A thorn stuck in my finger.
Past (it …): وہ کانٹا چبھا, voh kā̃ṭā chubhā.

The word, taken apart.
چبھنا (chubhnā) is inherited from cubhyatē, "is pierced", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4867).
