## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C392-CHUNAVANE392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡು** — *pracāra māḍu* — "to campaign; to publicise".

> ಅವಳು ಮನೆ ಮನೆಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡಿದಳು.
>
> *avaḷu mane manege hōgi pracāra māḍidaḷu.*
>
> She campaigned from door to door.

Past (I …): ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *pracāra māḍide*.

ಪ್ರಚಾರ alone is publicity or a campaign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡು** (*pracāra māḍu*) is built from Sanskrit *pracāra*, "coming forth, showing oneself" (Monier-Williams), with *māḍu*, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
