Punjabi, chapter 342: What Do You Mean?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਮਝਾਉਣਾ (samjhāuṇā) — to explain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a guess, then the Punjabi for to seem right.

You'll want to know: ਸਮਝਾਉਣਾ.
ਸਮਝਾਉਣਾ — samjhāuṇā — "to explain".
ਕੀ ਅਧਿਆਪਕ ਹਰ ਸਵਾਲ ਸਮਝਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ? kī adhiāpak har savāl samjhāundā hai? Does the teacher explain every question?
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਵਾਲ ਸਮਝਾਇਆ, maiṁ savāl samjhāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਮਝਾਉਣਾ (samjhāuṇā) is the causative partner of samajhṇā, "to understand", which goes back to Sanskrit saṁbudhyate, "wakes up, understands"; CDIAL lists Punjabi samajhṇā under it (CDIAL 12959).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਮਤਲਬ (matlab) — meaning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to seem right, then the Punjabi for to explain.

You'll want to know: ਮਤਲਬ.
ਮਤਲਬ — matlab — "meaning".
ਕੀ ਉਸ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਮਤਲਬ ਹੈ? kī us shabad dā koī matlab hai? Does that word have any meaning?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਤਲਬ (matlab) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic maṭlab, "something sought".

ਸਪਸ਼ਟ (sapshaṭ) is taken over from Sanskrit spaṣṭa, "clear", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸੁਝਾਉਣਾ (sujhāuṇā) — to suggest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a reason, then the Punjabi for plain to understand.

You'll want to know: ਸੁਝਾਉਣਾ.
ਸੁਝਾਉਣਾ — sujhāuṇā — "to suggest".
ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਕਦੇ ਕੋਈ ਰਸਤਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਸੁਝਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। merā dost kade koī rastā nahīṁ sujhāundā hai. My friend never suggests a way out.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਰਸਤਾ ਸੁਝਾਇਆ, maiṁ ikk rastā sujhāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁਝਾਉਣਾ (sujhāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit śudhyati, "is purified", also "becomes clear (of doubts)"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sujhāuṇā, "to explain", under it (CDIAL 12527). CDIAL does not give the sense "to suggest".
