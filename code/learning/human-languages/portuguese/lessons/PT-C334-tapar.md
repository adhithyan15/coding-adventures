## You'll want to know: tapar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C334-GRATINAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tapar** — "to cover (with a lid)".

> O cozinheiro tapou o tacho e esperou.
>
> *The cook put the lid on the pot and waited.*

Also to cover anything: *tapar os olhos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tapar* is usually traced to a Germanic word for a stopper or plug, like English *tap*; the origin is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry.
