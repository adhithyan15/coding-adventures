## You'll want to know: praescrībō, praescrībere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C410-ROGATIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**praescrībō, praescrībere** — "to lay down, to prescribe".

> Lēx praescrībit quid facere dēbeāmus.
>
> *The law lays down what we must do.*

*praescrībō, praescrībere, praescrīpsī, praescrīptum*: to write at the head, then to lay down a rule. English *prescribe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*praescrībō* is built from *prae* "before, at the head" and *scrībō* "to write" (Lewis & Short). English *prescribe* comes from it.
