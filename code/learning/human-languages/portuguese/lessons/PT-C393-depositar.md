## You'll want to know: depositar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C393-BANCO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**depositar** — "to deposit, to pay in".

> Depositei o dinheiro na minha conta.
>
> *I paid the money into my account.*

*Depositar um cheque* — to pay in a cheque.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*depositar* is built on *depósito* "deposit", from Latin *dēpositum* "a thing laid down for safekeeping" (Lewis & Short), on *pōnere* "to put".
