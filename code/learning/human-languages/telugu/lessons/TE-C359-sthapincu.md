# స్థాపించు (sthāpiñcu) — to found; to establish

## You'll want to know: స్థాపించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C359-SAMRAJYAM359-05]; assesses=[] -->

**స్థాపించు** — *sthāpiñcu* — "to found; to establish".

> ఆ రాజు ఒక కొత్త పట్టణం స్థాపించాడు.
>
> *ā rāju oka kotta paṭṭaṇaṁ sthāpiñcāḍu.*
>
> That king founded a new town.

Past (I …): స్థాపించాను, *sthāpiñcānu*.

Also of schools and companies: బడి స్థాపించు, to found a school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**స్థాపించు** (*sthāpiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *sthāpayati*, "causes to stand, establishes" (CDIAL 13756), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
