Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रत्यक्षदर्शी (pratyakṣadarśī) — an eyewitness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a title, then the Marathi for a front page.

You'll want to know: प्रत्यक्षदर्शी.
प्रत्यक्षदर्शी — pratyakṣadarśī — "an eyewitness".
एका प्रत्यक्षदर्शीने पोलिसांना सगळं सांगितलं. ekā pratyakṣadarśīne polisānnā sagḷaṁ sāṅgitlaṁ. An eyewitness told the police everything.
Masculine.
प्रत्यक्ष, before the eyes, + -दर्शी, one who sees.

The word, taken apart.
प्रत्यक्षदर्शी (pratyakṣadarśī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pratyakṣadarśin, 'one who has seen with his own eyes' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
माहितीपट (māhitīpaṭ) — a documentary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a front page, then the Marathi for an eyewitness.

You'll want to know: माहितीपट.
माहितीपट — māhitīpaṭ — "a documentary".
आम्ही वाघांवरचा एक माहितीपट पाहिला. āmhī vāghāṁvarcā ek māhitīpaṭ pāhilā. We watched a documentary about tigers.
Masculine.
पट, a screen, is masculine, and so is the compound; compare चित्रपट, a film.

The word, taken apart.
माहितीपट (māhitīpaṭ) is a modern compound built from māhitī, 'information', and Sanskrit paṭa, 'a cloth, a screen' (Monier-Williams), on the model of citrapaṭ, 'a film'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अंक (aṅk) — a magazine issue; an issue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an eyewitness, then the Marathi for a documentary.

You'll want to know: अंक.
अंक — aṅk — "a magazine issue; an issue".
मासिकाचा नवा अंक मला खूप आवडला. māsikācā navā aṅk malā khūp āvaḍlā. I really liked the magazine's new issue.
Masculine.
It also means a numeral: अंक (aṅk) म्हणजे आकडा.

The word, taken apart.
अंक (aṅk) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit aṅka, 'a hook, a mark, a numeral' (Monier-Williams); Marathi also names a numbered issue with it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
नोंदवणे (nondavṇe) — to register; to put on record.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a documentary, then the Marathi for a magazine issue.

You'll want to know: नोंदवणे.
नोंदवणे — nondavṇe — "to register; to put on record".
हवालदाराने माझी तक्रार नोंदवली. havāldārāne mājhī takrār nondavlī. The constable registered my complaint.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नोंदवलं, tyāne nondavlaṁ.
तक्रार नोंदवणे (nondavṇe), to lodge a complaint; मत नोंदवणे (nondavṇe), to put an opinion on record.

The word, taken apart.
नोंदवणे (nondavṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi nõdṇẽ, 'to copy and enter a document', under nibandhati, 'binds on; writes down' (CDIAL 7223).

Lesson 5 of 5.
भटकणे (bhaṭakṇe) — to wander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a magazine issue, then the Marathi for to register.

You'll want to know: भटकणे.
भटकणे — bhaṭakṇe — "to wander".
रस्ता चुकल्यावर आम्ही दोन तास जंगलात भटकलो. rastā cukalyāvar āmhī don tās jaṅglāt bhaṭaklo. After losing the way we wandered in the forest for two hours.
Past (he …): तो भटकला, to bhaṭaklā.
Intransitive; भटकी कुत्री, stray dogs.

The word, taken apart.
भटकणे (bhaṭakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi bhaṭakṇe, 'to wander', under bhaṭ, 'a sudden movement' (CDIAL 9365).
