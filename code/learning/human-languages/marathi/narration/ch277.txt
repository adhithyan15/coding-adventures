Marathi, chapter 277: Will, Going To, Will Not.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
मी जाईन (mī jāīn) — the future, person by person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "see you tomorrow": udyā bheṭū. Then "I went": mī gelo.
[pause 1 second]
भेटू, "we'll meet", has been a future all along. Here are the others.

You'll want to know: the future, person by person.
उद्या मी पुण्याला जाईन. udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn. — "Tomorrow I will go to Pune."
मी जाईन (mī jāīn) (jāīn) — I will go.
तू जाशील (jāśīl) — you will go, to a friend.
तो, ती, ते जाईल (jāīl) — he, she, it will go.
आम्ही जाऊ (jāū) — we will go: the -ऊ of भेटू.
तुम्ही जाल (jāl) — you will go, with respect.
ते जातील (jātīl) — they will go.
There is no gender here: a man and a woman both say मी जाईन (mī jāīn).

You'll want to know: more verbs in the future.
The endings stay; the stem in front changes:
येणे becomes मी येईन (mī yeīn), तो येईल (to yeīl), तुम्ही याल (tumhī yāl).
करणे becomes मी करीन (mī karīn), तो करेल (to karel), आम्ही करू (āmhī karū).
वाचणे becomes मी वाचीन (mī vācīn), ती वाचेल (tī vācel).
पडणे becomes पाऊस पडेल (pāūs paḍel), "it will rain".
असणे becomes मी घरी असेन (mī gharī asen), "I will be at home"; तो असेल (to asel).
होणे becomes काय होईल? (kāy hoīl?), "What will happen?"
Many people say करेन (karen) and वाचेन (vācen) for "I will"; both are heard.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Tomorrow I will go to Pune" — udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It will rain in the evening" — sandhyākāḷī pāūs paḍel]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "We will meet tomorrow" — āmhī udyā bheṭū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What will happen?" — kāy hoīl?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you will go to Pune tomorrow, then that it will rain in the evening.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I will go"? (जाईन.) "They will go"? (जातील.) "We will go"? (जाऊ.) "It will rain"? (पाऊस पडेल.) Does a woman say it differently? (No: the future has no gender.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
जाणार आहे (jāṇār āhe) — a plan, and won't.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I will go". (mī jāīn.) Then "next week". (puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt.)

You'll want to know: -णार आहे, 'going to'.
पुढच्या आठवड्यात मी गावाला जाणार आहे. puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt mī gāvālā jāṇār āhe. — "Next week I am going to go to the village."
Take -णे off the dictionary form and put -णार on: जाणे becomes जाणार (jāṇār), करणे becomes करणार (karṇār). Then the present of "to be" carries the person: मी जाणार आहे (mī jāṇār āhe), तुम्ही जाणार आहात (tumhī jāṇār āhāt), and, with आहोत (āhot, "we are"), आम्ही जाणार आहोत (āmhī jāṇār āhot). It says a plan already made, where जाईन can be a promise made on the spot.

You'll want to know: -णार नाही, 'won't'.
मी उद्या येणार नाही. mī udyā yeṇār nāhī. — "I won't come tomorrow."
Put नाही in place of आहे. This is the everyday "won't" for every person: तो जाणार नाही (to jāṇār nāhī), आम्ही करणार नाही (āmhī karṇār nāhī).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Next week I am going to the village" — puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt mī gāvālā jāṇār āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I won't come tomorrow" — mī udyā yeṇār nāhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What are you going to do?" — tumhī kāy karṇār āhāt?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you are going to go to the village next week, then that you won't come tomorrow.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I am going to go"? (मी जाणार आहे (jāṇār āhe).) "I won't go"? (मी जाणार नाही.) "What are you going to do?" (तुम्ही काय करणार आहात?)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — what will happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "Yesterday I went to Pune" and "Tomorrow I will go to Pune". (kāl mī puṇyālā gelo, udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn.) Then "he did the work". (tyāne kām kelaṁ.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Yesterday it rained. Tomorrow it will rain too.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Yesterday I was not at home. Tomorrow I will be at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Next week she is going to go to the village.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I won't come tomorrow, because I am going to read a book.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: What will happen? What happened?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I will"? (-ईन: जाईन, येईन.) "It will"? (-एल or -ईल: पडेल, जाईल.) A plan? (-णार आहे.) "Won't"? (-णार नाही.)
