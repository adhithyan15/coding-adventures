## You'll want to know: भीरुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C463-PARAKRAMA463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भीरुः** — *bhīruḥ* — "timid".

> भीरुः शशकः सहसा धावति।
>
> *bhīruḥ śaśakaḥ sahasā dhāvati.*
>
> The timid rabbit suddenly runs off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भीरुः** (*bhīruḥ*) comes from the root √bhī, "to fear", the root of **बिभेति** and **भयम्**; MW gives "fearful, timid, cowardly" (MW).
