# Retrieve the rental exchange: round one

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C274-RENTAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Prepare to retrieve both turns from their meaning.

## Guided Practice: independent retrieval

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C274-RENTAL-01] -->

No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.

You visit someone at home. Ask, “Is that a rented house?” Then answer, “No, I
do not pay rent.” Say the Sanskrit rental exchange.

[PAUSE 8s]

## What you've built: check after answering

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C274-RENTAL-01] -->

Now check: **किं तत् भाटकितं गृहम्? — न, अहं भाटकं न ददामि।**
(*kiṃ tat bhāṭakitaṃ gṛham? — na, ahaṃ bhāṭakaṃ na dadāmi.*)

## Wrap-up Recall: boundary

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C274-RENTAL-01] -->

One successful retrieval is not evidence of productive participle, case, or
sandhi mastery, a general renting repertoire, housing competence, human
validation, learner attainment, or exam readiness.
