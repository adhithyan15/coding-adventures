# س and ل — recall one shape, add one

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *salām*. You already know what it means and traced **س** once.
Now recall that shape and add only its next consonant. Arabic runs **right to
left**, so the first shape waits on the right.

## Script — recall sīn and add lām
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01] -->

- **س** is *sīn*, the *s* sound you are retrieving from lesson one.
- **ل** is *lām*, the *l* sound. Notice its tall upright and low curve.

Slide a finger from right to left under **س ل** and say *s — l*. Do not build
the whole word yet.

## Writing — trace, then recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01, AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

1. Trace **س** once with a finger: three teeth, then the bowl.
2. Trace **ل** once: tall upright, then the low curve.
3. Hide the models. Air-write the three teeth of *sīn*, then the tall *lām*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01, AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU POINT: **س**, and say *sīn*]
- [YOU POINT: **ل**, and say *lām*]
- [YOU WRITE: **س ل** once, leaving them separate]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01, AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which way do Arabic words run? (**Right to left.**) Which letter has
three teeth? (**س, sīn.**) Which has the tall upright? (**ل, lām.**)
