## You'll want to know: सल्लागार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C515-VISHESH515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सल्लागार** — *sallāgār* — "a consultant; an adviser".

> कंपनीने एका सल्लागाराची मदत घेतली.
>
> *kampanīne ekā sallāgārācī madat ghetlī.*
>
> The company took the help of a consultant.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सल्लागार** (*sallāgār*) is built on सल्ला, "advice", a loan usually traced to Arabic *ṣalāḥ*, with the Persian ending *-gār*, "one who does".
