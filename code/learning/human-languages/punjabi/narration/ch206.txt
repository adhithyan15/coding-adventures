Lesson 2 of 6.
One bounded past form of to go — went, masculine plural or respectful masculine singular.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar infinitive ਜਾਣਾ, “to go,” then listen for one irregular past form.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਗਏ — gae — went, masculine plural.
Hear and say the form before reading it. Punjabi also uses this masculine plural form for one respectfully mentioned masculine person. Keep the irregular form whole. This chapter practises only the small “they went” frame; it does not open the wider perfective or agreement system.

The word, taken apart — source boundary.
Punjabi University Patiala's lexical statistics identifies ਗਏ (gae) as the masculine plural perfect form of the verb “go.” Its verb reference places the same form in the perfective family of that verb, and its reference grammar independently labels it masculine plural. Sources: Punjabi University lexical statistics, Punjabi University verb reference, and Punjabi University reference grammar.
Those sources support the modern form. Reuse the history already attached to ਜਾਣਾ; do not invent a second etymology for an inflected form. For this lesson, remember the whole irregular form and one bounded meaning.

Script you'll notice: ਏ.
ਗ + ਏ (e) becomes ਗਏ (gae).
ਏ — e — an independent Gurmukhi vowel.
Keep ਏ (e) in view. It is the second letter in the known word ਗਏ (gae).
No stroke order is taught here: the track does not yet have a verified source for one.

Lesson 5 of 6.
