Urdu, chapter 504: Wires and Switches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سوئچ (svich) — a switch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (something) painted, then the Urdu for to smear (with mud).

You'll want to know: سوئچ.
سوئچ — svich — "a switch".
بتی کا سوئچ دروازے کے پاس ہے۔ battī kā svich darvāze ke pās hai. The light switch is by the door.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
سوئچ (svich) is borrowed from English switch.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: کھونٹی.
کھونٹی — khūṅṭī — "a peg (on a wall)".
اس کا تھیلا کھونٹی پر ہے۔ us kā thailā khūṅṭī par hai. Her bag is hanging on the peg.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
کھونٹی (khūṅṭī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin khū̃ṭī, "a small peg", under khuṭṭa, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3893).
