# Review — it depends, and what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C286-NAHIN-TO-01, HI-LEX-C286-VARNA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-SUPERLATIVE-GROUP-01, HI-SCRIPT-C280-AI-01, HI-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "leave early, or you'll be late". (*jaldī niklo, varnā der ho jāegī*.) Then "of all fruits I like mangoes the most". (*mujhe phaloṁ meṁ ām sabse zyādā pasand hai*.) Then read aloud one of your six lines that has **ऐ** or **ः**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C287-NIRBHARKARNA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-DEPENDS-ON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-DEPENDS-KI-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-COUNTERFACTUAL-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-UNREAL-PAST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-AGAR-TO-01, HI-LEX-C142-THINGS142-05, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-MORE-LESS-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-ALL-DAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Will we go tomorrow? It depends on the weather.]
- [YOU SAY: Is the bus cheaper than the train? It depends on the day.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had worked all day, I would have been tired.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had time, I would come.]
- [YOU SAY: If it hadn't rained, we would have gone to the river.]
- [YOU SAY: one trip that depends on something, and one that would have gone better if …]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C287-NIRBHARKARNA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-DEPENDS-ON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-DEPENDS-KI-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-COUNTERFACTUAL-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C287-UNREAL-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "It depends on"? (**… पर निर्भर करता है**.) "It depends on whether …"? (**यह इस पर निर्भर करता है कि …**) "I would come"? (**मैं आता**.) "If I had known"? (**अगर मुझे पता होता**.) Which letters did **ऐसा** and **अतः** bring? (**ऐ** and **ः**.)
