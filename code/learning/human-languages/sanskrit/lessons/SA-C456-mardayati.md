## You'll want to know: मर्दयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C456-SAMA456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मर्दयति** — *mardayati* — "rubs; massages".

> माता बालस्य पादौ मर्दयति।
>
> *mātā bālasya pādau mardayati.*
>
> Mother rubs the child's feet.

Past (she …): सा बालस्य पादौ मर्दितवती, *sā bālasya pādau marditavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मर्दयति** (*mardayati*) is built on the causative of the root √mṛd, "to press, to crush"; MW gives "to press or squeeze hard" and "to rub" (MW).
