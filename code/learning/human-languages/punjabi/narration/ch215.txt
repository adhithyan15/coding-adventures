Punjabi, chapter 215: Do You Drink Tea?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
Return to asking and saying no — retrieve a yes-or-no question and a no answer by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: are you a traveller?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? Then answer: ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹਾਂ।.

Grammar Lens.
You can also say ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ and ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ. The next lesson puts ਕੀ in front of the second one.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: is the garden open?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ?
[your turn — hear: I don't drink tea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: you drink tea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ।.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask one yes-or-no question, then say one thing you don't do.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ? (kī tusīṁ chāh pīnde ho?) — do you drink tea?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say you drink tea. Then are you a traveller?

Grammar Lens: ਕੀ and the every-day present.
Nothing new to build: put ਕੀ in front of a present sentence, and let the voice rise.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ? — kī tusīṁ chāh pīnde ho? — Do you drink tea?
ਕੀ ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ? — kī uh roz sakūl jāndī hai? — Does she go to school every day?
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਹੋ? — kī tusīṁ roz kamm karde ho? — Do you work every day?

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: do you eat roti?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੇ ਹੋ?
[your turn — hear: does he drink milk?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਉਹ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹੈ?
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ? (kī tusīṁ chāh pīnde ho?)]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ? (kī tusīṁ chāh pīnde ho?) once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask someone whether they drink tea, and whether they go to school every day.

Lesson 4 of 5.
A short exchange: tea or milk? — a four-line exchange: ask, say no, ask again, say yes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask do you drink tea? Answer it no, in a whole sentence.

Grammar Lens: four lines.
A host and a guest. Hear the turns; each answer repeats the verb.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ? — kī tusīṁ chāh pīnde ho? — Do you drink tea?
ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। — nahīṁ jī, maiṁ chāh nahīṁ pīndī hāṁ. — No, I don't drink tea.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ? — kī tusīṁ duddh pīnde ho? — Do you drink milk?
ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। — hāṁ jī, maiṁ duddh pīndī hāṁ. — Yes, I drink milk.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: do you drink tea?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer no as yourself.
[your turn — hear: do you drink milk?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer yes as yourself.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the four lines]
Say both parts aloud.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy the second line once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Play the host: ask both questions. Then play the guest.


Lesson 5 of 5.
Tea or milk: a four-skill payoff — ask and answer do-you questions in a short exchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask do you drink tea? and answer it.

Guided Practice: four skills.
[your turn — hear: do you go home every day?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਘਰ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹੋ?
[your turn — hear: no, I don't go home every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Answer as yourself.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੀ ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ?]
Give its meaning: does she eat roti?
[once you have stopped driving — write: cover the line and write ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ?]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Hold the whole four-line exchange once more, both parts, without the model.
