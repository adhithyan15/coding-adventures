## You'll want to know: अतिवाहयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C540-ASANNA540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिवाहयति** — *ativāhayati* — "spends time".

> यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि वयं दिनं गृहे अतिवाहयामः।
>
> *yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ dinaṃ gṛhe ativāhayāmaḥ.*
>
> If it rains, we spend the day at home.

Past (he …): सः दिनं गृहे अतिवाहितवान्, *saḥ dinaṃ gṛhe ativāhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिवाहयति** (*ativāhayati*) is built on *ati*, "beyond", and the causative of the root √vah, "to carry", which also gives *vahati*; MW gives *ativāhayati* as to let pass, to let time pass, to spend it (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
