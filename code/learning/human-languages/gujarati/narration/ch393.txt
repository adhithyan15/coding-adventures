Gujarati, chapter 393: Engines and Lines.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
એન્જિન (enjin) — an engine (of a train).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pester, then the Gujarati for to hope.

You'll want to know: એન્જિન.
એન્જિન — enjin — "an engine (of a train)".
આ ટ્રેનનું એન્જિન ઘણું જૂનું છે. ā ṭrennũ enjin ghaṇũ jūnũ che. The engine of this train is very old.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
એન્જિન (enjin) is borrowed from English engine; on the railway it is the engine at the front that pulls the train.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સીટી (sīṭī) — a whistle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a railway line, then the Gujarati for a junction (of railway lines).

You'll want to know: સીટી.
સીટી — sīṭī — "a whistle".
ટ્રેનની સીટી સાંભળીને બાળકો દોડ્યાં. ṭrennī sīṭī sā̃bhḷīne bāḷko doḍyā̃. Hearing the train's whistle, the children ran.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સીટી (sīṭī) goes back to sīṭṭa, "a whistle", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as imitative of the sound itself; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 13427).

Lesson 5 of 5.
જોડવું (joḍvũ) — to couple (a carriage or an engine); to attach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a junction (of railway lines), then the Gujarati for a whistle.

You'll want to know: જોડવું.
જોડવું — joḍvũ — "to couple (a carriage or an engine); to attach".
સ્ટેશન પર તેમણે ટ્રેનમાં નવું એન્જિન જોડ્યું. sṭeśan par temṇe ṭrenmā̃ navũ enjin joḍyũ. At the station they coupled a new engine to the train.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે એન્જિન જોડ્યું, teṇe enjin joḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
જોડવું (joḍvũ) goes back to yuṭati, "is joined", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to join, to fasten" (CDIAL 10496). જોડી, a pair, and જોડાવું, to join, are its relatives.
