## You'll want to know: तक्षति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C502-KARMASALA502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तक्षति** — *takṣati* — "pares; shapes wood".

> तक्षकः दारु तक्षति।
>
> *takṣakaḥ dāru takṣati.*
>
> The carpenter shapes the wood.

Past (he …): सः दारु तष्टवान्, *saḥ dāru taṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तक्षति** (*takṣati*) comes from the root √takṣ, "to form by cutting, to chisel"; its past participle is *taṣṭa*, and KRM lists the past *taṣṭavān* used here (MW).
