## You'll want to know: しゃちょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C230-BOSS230-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃちょう** — *shachō* — "a company president".

> **しゃちょうは いつも かいぎで はなします。** — *shachō wa itsumo kaigi de hanashimasu* — "The company president always speaks at meetings."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃちょう** (*shachō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sha*, "company, association" (Middle Chinese *dzyaeX*) and *chō*, "leader, senior" (*trjangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
