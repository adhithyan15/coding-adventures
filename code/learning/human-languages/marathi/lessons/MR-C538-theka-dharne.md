## You'll want to know: ठेका धरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C538-NRUTYA538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ठेका धरणे** — *ṭhekā dharṇe* — "to keep the beat".

> गाणं सुरू झालं आणि सगळ्यांनी टाळ्यांनी ठेका धरला.
>
> *gāṇaṁ surū jhālaṁ āṇi sagḷyānnī ṭāḷyānnī ṭhekā dharlā.*
>
> The song began and everyone kept the beat with their claps.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ठेका धरला, *tyāne ṭhekā dharlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठेका धरणे** (*ṭhekā dharṇe*) is ठेका, inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ṭhekā*, "mode of beating a drum", under *\*ṭhēkk*, "fix" (CDIAL 5509), with धरणे, "to hold".
