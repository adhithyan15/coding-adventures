# とき (toki) — when; the time when

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C253-ONGOING-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C250-PAST-01, JA-LEX-HAHA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "My mother was asleep". (*haha wa nete imashita*.) Then say "I got up", to a friend. (*okita*.)

## You'll want to know: とき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C253-TOKI-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-KODOMO, JA-LEX-C138-KAERIMASU, JA-GRAMMAR-C250-TA-SOUND-01] -->

**とき** — *toki* — "when", "the time when". The plain form goes before it, and **、** closes the time:

> **かえった とき、ははは ねていました。**
>
> *kaetta toki, haha wa nete imashita.* — "When I got home, my mother was asleep."

> **おきた とき、あめが ふっていました。**
>
> *okita toki, ame ga futte imashita.* — "When I got up, it was raining."

After a noun it takes **の**:

> **こどもの とき、うみに いきました。**
>
> *kodomo no toki, umi ni ikimashita.* — "When I was a child, I went to the sea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とき** (*toki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict writes it with one kanji and glosses it "time; hour; moment" and "occasion; case"; KANJIDIC2 glosses that kanji "time, hour" and gives とき as its native reading. It is the **とき** inside **ときどき**, "sometimes" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
