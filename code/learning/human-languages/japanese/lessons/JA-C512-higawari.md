## You'll want to know: ひがわり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C512-CHANGE512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ひがわり** — *higawari* — "changing every day; of the day".

> **ねだんは ひがわりの りょうりに よります。** — *nedan wa higawari no ryōri ni yorimasu* — "The price depends on the dish of the day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひがわり** (*higawari*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *hi*, "day" and *gawari*, "changing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
