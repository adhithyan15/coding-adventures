Lesson 1 of 5.
ದಡ (daḍa) — a riverbank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a platform, then the Kannada for abroad.

You'll want to know: ದಡ.
ದಡ — daḍa — "a riverbank".

The word, taken apart.
ದಡ (daḍa) is probably a tadbhava of Sanskrit taṭa, "slope, shore" (CDIAL 5629), through Prakrit taḍa; CDIAL does not list the Kannada word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಬರ (bara) — a drought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for abroad, then the Kannada for a riverbank.

You'll want to know: ಬರ.
ಬರ — bara — "a drought".

The word, taken apart.
ಬರ (bara) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vaṟa, Telugu vaṟapu and Malayalam vaṟu (DEDR 5320). DEDR lists older baṟa, "dryness, barrenness, drought".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಭೂಕಂಪ (bhūkampa) — an earthquake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a riverbank, then the Kannada for a drought.

You'll want to know: ಭೂಕಂಪ.
ಭೂಕಂಪ — bhūkampa — "an earthquake".

The word, taken apart.
ಭೂಕಂಪ (bhūkampa) is Sanskrit bhūkampa, "earthquake", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 9547). Bhū, "earth", and kampa, "trembling".

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಗೋಡೆ.
ಗೋಡೆ — gōḍe — "a wall".

The word, taken apart.
ಗೋಡೆ (gōḍe) is native Dravidian, one word with Telugu gōḍa and Tulu gōḍè, "wall" (DEDR 2207). DEDR keeps it in a part of its own, apart from kōṭe, "fort, rampart".
