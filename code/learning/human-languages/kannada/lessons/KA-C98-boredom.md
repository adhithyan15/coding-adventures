## You'll want to know: ಬೇಸರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C98-MOODS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೇಸರ** (*bēsara*) — "boredom", and the low mood that comes with it.

**ನನಗೆ ಬೇಸರ ಆಗಿದೆ** (*nanage bēsara āgide*) — "I'm bored", with the person in
**ನನಗೆ** once more.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A mood, said like hunger and tiredness.
