## You'll want to know: o fugitivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C292-FUGA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o fugitivo** — "the fugitive".

> O fugitivo escondeu-se numa quinta.
>
> *The fugitive hid on a farm.*

A woman is *a fugitiva*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fugitivo* was borrowed from Latin *fugitīvus* "runaway" (EtymDB), from *fugere* "to flee".
