## You'll want to know: sospendere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C279-RINVIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sospendere** — "to suspend, to halt for a while".

> Hanno sospeso la partita per la pioggia.
>
> *They suspended the match because of the rain.*

Past participle *sospeso*. It also means ‘to hang up’: *sospendere una lampada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sospendere* is inherited from Latin *suspendere* "to hang up, to suspend" (EtymDB), from *sub* "under" and *pendere* "to hang".
