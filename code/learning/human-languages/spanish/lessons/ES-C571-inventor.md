## You'll want to know: el inventor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C571-DESCUBRIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el inventor** — "the inventor".

> El inventor del teléfono fue muy famoso.
>
> *The inventor of the telephone was very famous.*

A woman is *la inventora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inventor* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inventor* 'one who finds out, a deviser' (EtymDB), from *invenīre* 'to come upon' (Lewis & Short).
