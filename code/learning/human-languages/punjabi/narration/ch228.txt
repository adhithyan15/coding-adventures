Punjabi, chapter 228: Drying the Clothes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਨਿਚੋੜਨਾ (nichoṛnā) — to wring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for grime, then the Punjabi for a tap.

You'll want to know: ਨਿਚੋੜਨਾ.
ਨਿਚੋੜਨਾ — nichoṛnā — "to wring".
ਮੈਂ ਗਿੱਲੇ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਨਿਚੋੜਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ gille kappṛe nichoṛdī hāṁ. I wring out the wet clothes.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਤੌਲੀਆ ਨਿਚੋੜਿਆ, maiṁ taulīā nichoṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਿਚੋੜਨਾ (nichoṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed niścōṭayati, "presses out"; CDIAL lists Punjabi nicoṛṇā, "to wring" (CDIAL 7449).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ (sukāuṇā) — to dry something.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a tap, then the Punjabi for to wring.

You'll want to know: ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ.
ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ — sukāuṇā — "to dry something".
ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਛੱਤ ਉੱਤੇ ਸੁਕਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ। asīṁ kappṛe chhatt utte sukāunde hāṁ. We dry the clothes on the roof.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਸੁਕਾਏ, maiṁ kappṛe sukāe.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁਕਾਉਣਾ (sukāuṇā) is the causative of a verb inherited from a reconstructed śuṣkati, "becomes dry"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sukkṇā, "dries up", and its causative sukhāuṇā (CDIAL 12552).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਰੱਸੀ (rassī) — a rope; a washing line.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to dry something, then the Punjabi for to hang up.

You'll want to know: ਰੱਸੀ.
ਰੱਸੀ — rassī — "a rope; a washing line".
ਗਿੱਲੇ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਰੱਸੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਨ। gille kappṛe rassī utte han. The wet clothes are on the line.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਰੱਸੀ (rassī) is inherited from Sanskrit raśmi, "rope, bridle, ray of light"; CDIAL lists Punjabi rassī, "rope" (CDIAL 10648).
