## You'll want to know: die Girlande
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C309-SCHMUECKEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Girlande** — "the garland, the paper chain".

> Über der Tür hing eine bunte Girlande.
>
> *A colourful garland hung over the door.*

Plural: *die Girlanden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Girlande* was borrowed from French *guirlande* "garland", which came from Italian *ghirlanda* (EtymDB).
