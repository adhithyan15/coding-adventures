## You'll want to know: das Gedicht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C521-GEDICHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Gedicht** — "poem".

> Zum Muttertag hat das Kind ein Gedicht gelernt.
>
> *The child learnt a poem for Mother's Day.*

Plural: *die Gedichte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gedicht* is inherited from Middle High German *getiht*, built on *tihten* "to compose" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *dictāre* "to say often, to dictate" (Lewis & Short).
