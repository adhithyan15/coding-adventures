Arabic, chapter 297: On the radio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
إذاعة (idhāʿa) — a radio station; broadcasting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to edit, then the Arabic for a bulletin.

You'll want to know: إذاعة.
إذاعة — idhāʿa — "a radio station; broadcasting".
تعمل خالتي في إذاعة صغيرة. taʿmalu khālatī fī idhāʿa ṣaghīra. My aunt works at a small radio station.

The word, taken apart.
إذاعة (idhāʿa) is usually traced to the native Arabic root dh-y-ʿ, said of news that spreads. Idhāʿa is the verbal noun of Form IV adhāʿa, "to make known", and now names radio.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قناة (qanāh) — a channel; a canal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bulletin, then the Arabic for a radio station.

You'll want to know: قناة.
قناة — qanāh — "a channel; a canal".
تبث هذه القناة الأخبار صباحا ومساء. tabuththu hādhihi al-qanāh al-akhbār ṣabāḥan wa-masāʾan. This channel broadcasts the news morning and evening.
Plural قنوات. A television channel and a water canal are one word.

The word, taken apart.
قناة (qanāh) is built on the native Arabic root q-n-w (Lane), where qanāh is a straight reed or spear shaft, and then a channel dug for water. The television sense is modern.

Lesson 3 of 5.
مذياع (midhyāʿ) — a radio set.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a radio station, then the Arabic for a channel.

You'll want to know: مذياع.
مذياع — midhyāʿ — "a radio set".
كان جدي يسمع المذياع كل صباح. kāna jaddī yasmaʿu al-midhyāʿ kulla ṣabāḥ. My grandfather used to listen to the radio every morning.
Many speakers say راديو; مذياع (midhyāʿ) is the standard word.

The word, taken apart.
مذياع (midhyāʿ) is usually traced to the native Arabic root dh-y-ʿ, "to spread, to become known". The mifʿāl pattern names a tool, so midhyāʿ is "the spreader": a modern coinage for the radio set.

Lesson 5 of 5.
على الهواء (ʿalā al-hawāʾ) — on air, live.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a radio set, then the Arabic for to put on air.

You'll want to know: على الهواء.
على الهواء — ʿalā al-hawāʾ — "on air, live".
تكلم الوزير على الهواء مساء أمس. takallama al-wazīr ʿalā al-hawāʾ masāʾa ams. The minister spoke on air yesterday evening.
على الهواء (ʿalā al-hawāʾ) مباشرة: live on air.

The word, taken apart.
على الهواء (ʿalā al-hawāʾ) is ʿalā, "on", with hawāʾ, "air", which is built on the native Arabic root h-w-y (Lane). The phrase is usually traced to the English broadcasting phrase on the air.
