## You'll want to know: ゆうり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C532-LIMIT532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうり** — *yūri* — "advantageous; favourable".

> **もし ゆきが ふったら、やまの せんしゅが ゆうりでしょう。** — *moshi yuki ga futtara, yama no senshu ga yūri deshō* — "If it snows, the athletes from the mountains will have the advantage."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうり** (*yūri*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yū*, "possess" and *ri*, "profit" (Middle Chinese *lijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
