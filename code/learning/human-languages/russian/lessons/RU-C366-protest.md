## You'll want to know: протест
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C366-VYBORY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**протест** (*protést*) — "a protest".

> Студенты вышли на протест.
>
> *Studénty výshli na protést.*
>
> The students came out to protest.

Masculine. *В знак протеста* — as a sign of protest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**протест** is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin *prōtestārī* "to declare in public", built on *testis* "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
