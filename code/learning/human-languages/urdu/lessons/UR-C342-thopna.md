## You'll want to know: تھوپنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C342-VOT342-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تھوپنا** — *thopnā* — "to impose; to force (on someone)".

> حکومت نے لوگوں پر نیا ٹیکس تھوپ دیا۔
>
> *hukūmat ne logoṅ par nayā ṭaiks thop diyā.*
>
> The government forced a new tax on people.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنی رائے تھوپی, *us ne apnī rāe thopī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تھوپنا** (*thopnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *thopnā*, "to cover, to heap", under *\*stōpyate*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13723); the sense "to force on someone" is a later one our sources do not trace.
