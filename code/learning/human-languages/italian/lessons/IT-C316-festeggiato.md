## You'll want to know: il festeggiato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C316-CANDELINE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il festeggiato** — "the person being celebrated, the birthday boy or girl".

> Il festeggiato ha aperto i regali.
>
> *The birthday boy opened his presents.*

Built on *festeggiare*; a woman is *la festeggiata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*festeggiato* is the past participle of *festeggiare* "to celebrate", built on *festa*, from Latin *fēsta* "feast days" (Lewis & Short).
