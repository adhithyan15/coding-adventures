## You'll want to know: かなり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C530-ATLEAST530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かなり** — *kanari* — "fairly; quite".

> **もし あめが ふったら、みちは かなり こむでしょう。** — *moshi ame ga futtara, michi wa kanari komu deshō* — "If it rains, the roads will be fairly crowded."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かなり** (*kanari*) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, though that is not certain: JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and lists only rare kanji spellings, and no dictionary we use records its history, so where it comes from is not settled (JMdict).
