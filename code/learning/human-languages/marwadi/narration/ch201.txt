Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 201: Around the Old Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कोट (koṭ) — a fort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a quarter, then the Marwadi for a litre.

You'll want to know: कोट.
कोट — koṭ — "a fort".
कोट कठै है? koṭ kaṭhai hai? Where is the fort?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कोट (koṭ) is inherited from Sanskrit kōṭṭa, "fort"; CDIAL lists Marwari koṭ, "fort" (CDIAL 3500).

मोहल्लो — mohallo — "a neighbourhood".
म्हारो मोहल्लो कोट कनै है। mhāro mohallo koṭ kanai hai. My neighbourhood is near the fort.

मोहल्लो (mohallo) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maḥalla, "a quarter of a town".

Lesson 3 of 5.
थाणो (thāṇo) — a police station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a fort, then the Marwadi for a neighbourhood.

You'll want to know: थाणो.
थाणो — thāṇo — "a police station".
थाणो चौक कनै है। thāṇo chauk kanai hai. The police station is near the square.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
थाणो (thāṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit sthāna, "a place". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi thānā and Gujarati thāṇũ, "police station" (CDIAL 13753).

Lesson 5 of 5.
घुसणो (ghusṇo) — to get in; to enter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a police station, then the Marwadi for an inn.

You'll want to know: घुसणो.
घुसणो — ghusṇo — "to get in; to enter".
बकरी खेत मांय घुसै है। bakrī khet mā̃y ghusai hai. The goat keeps getting into the field.
Past (he …): वो मांय घुस्यो, vo mā̃y ghusyo.

The word, taken apart.
घुसणो (ghusṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ghuss, "thrust in"; CDIAL lists Marwari ghusṇo, "to enter" (CDIAL 4492).
