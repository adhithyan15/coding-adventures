## You'll want to know: la contusion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C299-CHUTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la contusion** — "the bruise, the contusion".

> Il a seulement quelques contusions.
>
> *He only has a few bruises.*

Medical; in everyday French, *un bleu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contusion* is a learned borrowing from Latin *contūsiō* "a bruising" (EtymDB), from *contundere* "to beat, to bruise".
