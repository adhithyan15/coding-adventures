# ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ (ghōṣavākya) — a slogan

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a public festival, then the Kannada for a procession.

## You'll want to know: ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C359-UTSAVA359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ** — *ghōṣavākya* — "a slogan".

> ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಜನ ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ ಕೂಗಿದರು.
>
> *meravaṇigeyalli jana ghōṣavākya kūgidaru.*
>
> People shouted slogans in the procession.

Plural: ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯಗಳು, *ghōṣavākyagaḷu*.

ಘೋಷ, a cry, and ವಾಕ್ಯ, a sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ** (*ghōṣavākya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of *ghoṣa*, "a noise, a cry" (Monier-Williams), and *vākya*, "speech, words" (CDIAL 11468).

## What you've built
