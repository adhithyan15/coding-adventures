# భయపెట్టు (bhayapeṭṭu) — to frighten; to scare

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-05, TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for eagerness, then the Telugu for to be afraid.

## You'll want to know: భయపెట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**భయపెట్టు** — *bhayapeṭṭu* — "to frighten; to scare".

> ఆ కుక్క పిల్లల్ని భయపెట్టింది.
>
> *ā kukka pillalni bhayapeṭṭindi.*
>
> That dog frightened the children.

Past (I …): భయపెట్టాను, *bhayapeṭṭānu*.

-పెట్టు after a noun of feeling makes someone else feel it: భయపడు, to be afraid; భయపెట్టు, to frighten.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భయపెట్టు** (*bhayapeṭṭu*) joins Sanskrit *bhaya*, "fear" (CDIAL 9390), to the native verb *peṭṭu*, "to put, to place": to put fear into someone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
