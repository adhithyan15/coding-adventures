Urdu, chapter 48: To play, To buy, To sell, To give, To put.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کھیلنا (khelnā) — to play.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to walk, then the Urdu for to run.

You'll want to know: کھیلنا.
کھیلنا — khelnā — "to play".

The word, taken apart.
کھیلنا (khelnā) is inherited from a reconstructed khel-, "play" (CDIAL 3918), the verb behind khel, "a game". CDIAL links it with Sanskrit krīḍati, "plays": if that verb has a non-Aryan source, these forms may share it.

You'll want to know: خریدنا.
خریدنا — kharīdnā — "to buy".

The word, taken apart.
خریدنا (kharīdnā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kharīdan, "to buy". Urdu took its stem kharīd- and added the verb ending -nā.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بیچنا (bechnā) — to sell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to play, then the Urdu for to buy.

You'll want to know: بیچنا.
بیچنا — bechnā — "to sell".

The word, taken apart.
بیچنا (bechnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vetyayati, "makes a price", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12100). CDIAL builds it on a word for "hire, price", akin to vetana, "wages", and calls other proposals less probable.
