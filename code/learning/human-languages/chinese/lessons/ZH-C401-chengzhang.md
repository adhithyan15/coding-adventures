## You'll want to know: 成长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C401-FUTURE401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**成长** — *chéngzhǎng* — "to mature (as a person)".

> **孩子成长得很快** *háizi chéngzhǎng de hěn kuài* — Children grow up fast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**成长** (*chéngzhǎng*) is built from 成 'to complete' and 长 'to grow', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'big'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
