## You'll want to know: अनुतापः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C448-BHAVA448-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुतापः** — *anutāpaḥ* — "regret; repentance".

> कलहात् अनन्तरं मम अनुतापः भवति।
>
> *kalahāt anantaraṃ mama anutāpaḥ bhavati.*
>
> After a quarrel I feel regret.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुतापः** (*anutāpaḥ*) is built from *anu*, "after", and *tāpa*, "heat", from the root √tap, "to burn", the root of **तापः**: a burning that comes afterwards; MW gives "repentance" (MW).
