## You'll want to know: प्रवेशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C423-PRAVESHA423-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवेशः** — *praveśaḥ* — "an entrance; entry".

> प्रवेशः वामतः अस्ति।
>
> *praveśaḥ vāmataḥ asti.*
>
> The entrance is on the left.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवेशः** (*praveśaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "forward", and the root √viś, "to enter", which also gives *praviśati*, "enters"; MW gives *praveśa* as entering, entrance (MW).
