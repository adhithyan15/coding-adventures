# ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ — we drink tea every day

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-FORM-MILDE-188, PA-PRON-ASI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I drink tea every day*. Then the familiar **ਅਸੀਂ ਮਿਲਦੇ ਹਾਂ**,
"we meet".

## Grammar Lens: -ਦੇ for more than one
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211]; assesses=[PA-PRON-ASI-01, PA-LEX-YOU-REGISTER-02, PA-FORM-MILDE-188, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211] -->

**ਮਿਲਦੇ** showed the **-ਦੇ** ending for **ਅਸੀਂ**. The vowel stems take it too,
nasal and all: **ਜਾਂਦੇ**, **ਖਾਂਦੇ**, **ਪੀਂਦੇ**. **ਤੁਸੀਂ** takes it as well, with
**ਹੋ**:

> **ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।** — *asīṁ roz chāh pīnde hāṁ.* — We drink tea every day.

> **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹੋ।** — *tusīṁ roz sakūl jānde ho.* — You go to school every day.
