## You'll want to know: جرف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C487-MALJA487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جرف** — *jarafa* — "to sweep away".

> جرف الماء السيارات في الوادي.
>
> *jarafa al-māʾ as-sayyārāt fī al-wādī.*
>
> The water swept the cars away in the valley.

Present: يجرف, *yajruf*.

The noun *jurf* is a bank undercut by water, a cliff.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جرف** (*jarafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-f*, "to sweep away" (Lane). EtymDB lists Syriac relatives.
