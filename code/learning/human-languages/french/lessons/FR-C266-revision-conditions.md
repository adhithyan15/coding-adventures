# Review — if, and it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SI-01, FR-LEX-CA-DEPEND-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if he is tired", with the vowel *si* loses. (*S'il est
fatigué.*) Then "it depends". (*Ça dépend.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SI-PRESENT-01, FR-GRAM-SI-DEMAIN-02, FR-GRAM-DEPEND-DE-02, FR-ETYMON-SI-SIC-02] -->

Three lessons, and a way of talking about what has not happened yet. The
condition, opened by *si*: *s'il pleut*. The result, in the present, the
future with *aller*, or a piece of advice. And the condition not yet named:
*ça dépend*, or named after all: *ça dépend du temps*.

> — *Tu vas à la plage demain ?*
> — *Ça dépend du temps. S'il fait beau, je vais à la plage. S'il pleut, je
> vais rester à la maison.*

[PAUSE 2s] Two branches, one for each kind of weather, and the *si* half in
the present both times, although both are about tomorrow.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SI-01, FR-GRAM-SI-PRESENT-01, FR-LEX-CA-DEPEND-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In *s'il fait beau demain*, which tense is *fait*? (The
**present**.) What does *si* become before *il*? (***S'il.***) And what does
*ça dépend* leave unsaid? (**Which** condition.)
