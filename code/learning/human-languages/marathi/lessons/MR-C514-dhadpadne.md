# धडपडणे (dhaḍpaḍṇe) — to struggle; to scramble

## You'll want to know: धडपडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C514-KAMKAJ514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**धडपडणे** — *dhaḍpaḍṇe* — "to struggle; to scramble".

> नवीन कामात सुरुवातीला मी खूप धडपडलो.
>
> *navīn kāmāt suruvātīlā mī khūp dhaḍpaḍlo.*
>
> At first I struggled a lot in the new job.

Past (he …): तो धडपडला, *to dhaḍpaḍlā*.

A woman says धडपडले.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धडपडणे** (*dhaḍpaḍṇe*) is an expressive Marathi verb built on the sound word धडपड; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
