## You'll want to know: خطاطی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C516-KHATTATI516-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خطاطی** — *khattātī* — "calligraphy".

> میرے استاد خطاطی سکھاتے ہیں۔
>
> *mere ustād khattātī sikhāte haiṅ.*
>
> My teacher teaches calligraphy.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خطاطی** (*khattātī*) is built on *khattāt*, "a calligrapher", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khaṭṭāṭ*, with the noun ending *-ī*.
