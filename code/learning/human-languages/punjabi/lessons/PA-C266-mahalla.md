## You'll want to know: ਮਹੱਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C266-TOWN266-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਹੱਲਾ** — *mahallā* — "a neighbourhood; a quarter of a town".

> ਸਾਡਾ ਮਹੱਲਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਸ਼ਾਂਤ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍā mahallā bahut shā̃t hai.*
>
> Our neighbourhood is very calm.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਹੱਲਾ** (*mahallā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *mahalla*, "a quarter of a town".
