# নাক সিঁটকানো (nāk sĩṭkāno) — to turn up one's nose

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for envious of others' good fortune, then the Bengali for to hate.

## You'll want to know: নাক সিঁটকানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C451-GHRINA451-05]; assesses=[] -->

**নাক সিঁটকানো** — *nāk sĩṭkāno* — "to turn up one's nose".

> সবজি দেখে ছেলেটা নাক সিঁটকাল।
>
> *sôbji dekhe chheleṭā nāk sĩṭkālô.*
>
> The boy turned up his nose at the vegetables.

Past (he/she …): তিনি খাবার দেখে নাক সিঁটকালেন, *tini khābār dekhe nāk sĩṭkālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নাক সিঁটকানো** (*nāk sĩṭkāno*) is নাক (taught), the nose (CDIAL 6909), with *sĩṭkāno*, "to wrinkle up", a verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
