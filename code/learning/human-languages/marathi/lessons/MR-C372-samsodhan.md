## You'll want to know: संशोधन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C372-SANSHODHAN372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संशोधन** — *saṁśodhan* — "research".

> ती पाण्याच्या प्रदूषणावर संशोधन करते.
>
> *tī pāṇyācyā pradūṣṇāvar saṁśodhan karte.*
>
> She does research on water pollution.

Neuter.

संशोधन करणे, to do research; the researcher is संशोधक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संशोधन** (*saṁśodhan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṁśodhana*, "purifying; correcting" (Monier-Williams).
