## You'll want to know: حقیقت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C303-INKAR303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حقیقت** — *haqīqat* — "the reality; the actual truth".

> حقیقت کچھ اور تھی۔
>
> *haqīqat kuchh aur thī.*
>
> The truth was something else.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حقیقت** (*haqīqat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥaqīqa*, "the truth of a thing", a cousin of **حق**. **حقیقت میں** is in reality.
