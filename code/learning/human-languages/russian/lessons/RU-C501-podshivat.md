## You'll want to know: подшивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C501-SEWING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**подшивать** (*podshivát*) — "to hem".

> Брюки слишком длинные, и бабушка их подшивает.
>
> *Bryúki slíshkom dlínnyye, i bábushka ikh podshiváyet.*
>
> The trousers are too long, so Grandma is taking them up.

Imperfective; perfective подшить (подошью).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подшивать* is built on *под-* 'under' and *шить* 'to sew'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
