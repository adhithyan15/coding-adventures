## You'll want to know: adorar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C714-CARINO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**adorar** — "to adore, to worship".

> Adoro el chocolate.
>
> *I adore chocolate.*

Stronger than *encantar*; it also means to worship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adorar* was borrowed from Latin *adōrāre* "to address, to pray to" (EtymDB), built on *ad* "to" and *ōrāre* "to speak, to pray".
