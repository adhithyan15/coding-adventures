## You'll want to know: અલગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C544-TAPHAVAT544-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અલગ** — *alag* — "separate".

> જો બાળકો અલગ ઓરડામાં સૂઈ જશે તો ઘર શાંત રહેશે.
>
> *jo bāḷko alag orḍāmā̃ sūī jaśe to ghar śānt raheśe.*
>
> If the children sleep in a separate room, the house will stay quiet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અલગ** (*alag*) is usually linked to Sanskrit *alagna*, "unconnected" (CDIAL 700), under which CDIAL gives a Gujarati *aḷgũ*, "not touching"; this form is not listed there.
