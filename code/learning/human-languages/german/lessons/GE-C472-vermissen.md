## You'll want to know: vermissen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C472-SEHNSUCHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vermissen** — "to miss (someone or something)".

> Ich vermisse meine Freunde aus der Schule.
>
> *I miss my friends from school.*

Also to find something missing: *Seit gestern vermisse ich meinen Schlüssel.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vermissen* is built on *ver-* and the older verb *missen* "to lack, to do without" (EtymDB), a Germanic word; English *miss* is a cousin.
