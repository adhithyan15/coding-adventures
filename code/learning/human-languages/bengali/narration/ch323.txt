Bengali, chapter 323: Heroes and Villains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নায়ক (nāyôk) — the hero of a story; a protagonist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a curse, then the Bengali for a ghost.

You'll want to know: নায়ক.
নায়ক — nāyôk — "the hero of a story; a protagonist".
গল্পের নায়ক এক গরিব জেলে। gôlper nāyôk ek gôrib jele. The hero of the story is a poor fisherman.

The word, taken apart.
নায়ক (nāyôk) is Sanskrit nāyaka, 'a guide, a leader, a chief' (Monier-Williams); in plays and stories it is the leading man.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for a ghost, then the Bengali for the hero of a story.

You'll want to know: খলনায়ক.
খলনায়ক — khôlnāyôk — "a villain".
শেষে পুলিশ খলনায়ককে গ্রেফতার করল। sheshe pulish khôlnāyôkke grephtār kôrlô. In the end the police arrested the villain.

The word, taken apart.
খলনায়ক (khôlnāyôk) joins Sanskrit khala, 'a mischievous man, a rogue' (Monier-Williams), to নায়ক (nāyôk): the rogue who leads the other side of the story.

নাটকে মীরার ভূমিকা ছিল এক ডাইনির। nāṭôke mīrār bhūmikā chhilô ek ḍāinir. In the play Mira's part was a witch.

The word, taken apart.
ভূমিকা (bhūmikā) is Sanskrit bhūmikā, 'ground, a place', which Monier-Williams also gives as an actor's part and as the preface of a book; Bengali keeps both.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ছদ্মবেশ (chhôdmôbesh) — a disguise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a role, then the Bengali for a holy man.

You'll want to know: ছদ্মবেশ.
ছদ্মবেশ — chhôdmôbesh — "a disguise".
খলনায়ক সন্ন্যাসীর ছদ্মবেশে প্রাসাদে ঢুকল। khôlnāyôk sônnyāsīr chhôdmôbeshe prāsāde ḍhuklô. The villain got into the palace disguised as a holy man.

The word, taken apart.
ছদ্মবেশ (chhôdmôbesh) joins Sanskrit chadman, 'a covering, a deceptive dress' (Monier-Williams), to veśa, 'dress': false clothes.
