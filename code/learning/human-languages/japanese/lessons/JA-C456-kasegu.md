## You'll want to know: かせぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C456-PAY456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かせぐ** — *kasegu* — "to earn (money)".

> **あには みせで はたらいて、おかねを かせいでいます。** — *ani wa mise de hataraite, okane o kaseide imasu* — "My elder brother earns money working in a shop."

Polite **かせぎます**, *kasegimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが おかねを かせぎました。**, *chichi ga okane o kasegimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かせぐ** (*kasegu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
