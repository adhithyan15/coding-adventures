## You'll want to know: el ataque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C530-PELIGRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el ataque** — "the attack".

> El ataque ocurrió de noche.
>
> *The attack happened at night.*

*Un ataque de risa* — a fit of laughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ataque* is built on *atacar* (EtymDB), which was borrowed from Italian *attaccare* 'to attach, to join (battle)'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
