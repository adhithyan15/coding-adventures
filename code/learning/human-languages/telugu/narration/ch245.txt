Telugu, chapter 245: To collect, To broadcast, To print, To download, To type.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సేకరించు (sēkariñcu) — to collect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to wilt, then the Telugu for to bark.

You'll want to know: సేకరించు.
సేకరించు — sēkariñcu — "to collect".

The word, taken apart.
DEDR 2801 lists సేకరించు (sēkariñcu), "to save, collect", with Tamil cēkaram, "acquisition, collection", and notes that the Malayalam forms look Sanskrit though Sanskrit has no such word in this sense.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ప్రసారం చేయు (prasāraṁ cēyu) — to broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to bark, then the Telugu for to collect.

You'll want to know: ప్రసారం చేయు.
ప్రసారం చేయు — prasāraṁ cēyu — "to broadcast".

The word, taken apart.
ప్రసారం చేయు (prasāraṁ cēyu) pairs Sanskrit prasāra, "extension, spreading" (CDIAL 8835), with the Telugu ending -ṁ, and native cēyu, "to do" (DEDR 1957).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ముద్రించు (mudriñcu) — to print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to collect, then the Telugu for to broadcast.

You'll want to know: ముద్రించు.
ముద్రించు — mudriñcu — "to print".

The word, taken apart.
ముద్రించు (mudriñcu) is Sanskrit mudrā, "seal, signet-ring" (CDIAL 10203), made into a verb with the Telugu ending -iñcu: "to stamp, print". CDIAL thinks mudrā probably came from Iranian.
