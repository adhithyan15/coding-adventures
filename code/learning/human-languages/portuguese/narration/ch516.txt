Portuguese, chapter 516: A orquestra.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a orquestra — orchestra.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to get injured, then the Portuguese for cheating.

You'll want to know: a orquestra.
a orquestra — "orchestra".
A orquestra tocou durante duas horas. The orchestra played for two hours.
A musician in it is um músico da orquestra.

The word, taken apart.
orquestra comes from Latin orchēstra, from Greek orkhḗstra (EtymDB), the space in a theatre where the chorus danced, built on orkheîsthai "to dance".

You'll want to know: o maestro.
o maestro — "conductor".
O maestro levantou os braços e a música começou. The conductor raised his arms and the music began.
Not a schoolteacher: in Portuguese, maestro is the conductor.

The word, taken apart.
maestro was borrowed from Italian maestro "master" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin magister "master, teacher".
