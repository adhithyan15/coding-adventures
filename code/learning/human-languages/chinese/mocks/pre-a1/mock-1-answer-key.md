# Chinese pre-A1 mock 1 — examiner key and delivery script

Keep this file away from the learner. Apply the separate
[form-1 rubric](mock-1-rubric.md). Every script below is original project
material and uses taught pre-A1 Mandarin. Record each listening and
dictation item at natural, gentle speed (about 1–3 seconds per cue), play it
**twice**, and leave a pause for the response. Do not add pinyin or English
while delivering a Chinese audio cue.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->
Reading, 5 points each: **1 a** (you), **2 b** (good), **3 c** (hello),
**4 a** (goodbye), **5 b** (please / after you).
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->
The examiner records or speaks exactly these cues. The text and keyed
selection are not printed on the learner paper.

| item | cue (twice) | correct selection | taught source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **mǎ** (third tone; isolated syllable) | **c** | `ZH-C01-tones` |
| 7 | **你好** (*ní hǎo*) | **a**, hello | `ZH-C01-nihao`, `ZH-C01-tone-sandhi` |
| 8 | **再见** (*zàijiàn*) | **a**, 再见 | `ZH-C06-zaijian` |
| 9 | **我** (*wǒ*) | **a**, pointing to self | `ZH-C08-wo` |
| 10 | **请** (*qǐng*) | **a**, 谢谢 | `ZH-C11-qing`, `ZH-C10-xiexie` |

Each correct selection is 5 points, otherwise 0. Do not make a tone-neutral
or English announcement part of the recorded cue.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->
Item 11 card: show **你** alone for ten seconds, then cover it. Expected
handwritten answer: **你**. `ZH-C01-ni` teaches its visible components and
formation. Score 0–5 by the rubric, not by whether the learner can merely
recognize it.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->
Item 12 audio: **你** (*nǐ*); expected pinyin **nǐ** (syllable and third-tone
mark). Item 13 audio: **好** (*hǎo*); expected handwritten simplified **好**.
Each cue is spoken twice without a displayed transcript. Both are taught in
`ZH-C01-ni` and `ZH-C01-hao`; apply the distinct 5-point criteria in the rubric.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
14. Sample: **你好** (a greeting, taught in `ZH-C01-nihao`).
15. Sample: **再见** (farewell, taught in `ZH-C06-zaijian`).

Accept an equally appropriate, independently written taught greeting or
farewell. Do not reveal these models until the sitting is over. Score each
response out of 5 under the form-1 rubric.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->
16. Examiner says **你好**. Expected: **你好** (or another taught greeting).
17. Examiner says **谢谢**. Expected: **不谢** (*bú xiè*).
18. Examiner says in English, “Please say who you are.” Expected: **我是**
    (*wǒ shì*) plus the candidate's own name. Do not penalize an authentic
    personal name outside the taught word list.
19. Show the leaving-person picture; expected **再见**.
20. Examiner says **好** (*hǎo*, third tone) without displaying text; the
    candidate repeats **hǎo** with an intelligible third tone.

Read each prompt once, allowing one slow repeat request. Responses should
fit in one short turn, no more than 15 seconds. Score each out of 5 with
form-1's analytic criteria; retain recording and independent rater marks.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->
