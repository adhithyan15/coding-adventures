## You'll want to know: فخر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C381-KAMYAB381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فخر** — *fakhr* — "pride (in someone)".

> اگر تم جیتے تو ہمیں تم پر فخر ہو گا۔
>
> *agar tum jīte to hameṅ tum par fakhr ho gā.*
>
> If you win, we will be proud of you.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فخر** (*fakhr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fakhr*, "glory, pride".
