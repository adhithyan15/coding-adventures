## You'll want to know: quaestor, quaestōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C383-MAGISTRATUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**quaestor, quaestōris** — "a quaestor, a finance officer".

> Quaestor pecūniam pūblicam servat.
>
> *The quaestor keeps the public money.*

*quaestor, quaestōris* m., built on *quaerō*, to seek, to inquire. The lowest regular office: the treasury and the accounts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quaestor* is built on *quaerō* "to seek, to inquire" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tor* of the one who does it.
