## You'll want to know: గాథ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C370-GATHA370-02]; assesses=[] -->

**గాథ** — *gātha* — "a tale; a saga".

> మా అమ్మమ్మ వీరుల గాథలు చెప్పేది.
>
> *mā ammamma vīrula gāthalu ceppēdi.*
>
> My grandmother used to tell tales of heroes.

Plural: గాథలు, *gāthalu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గాథ** (*gātha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gāthā*, "a song" (CDIAL 4126); in Telugu it is a tale of deeds.
