## You'll want to know: बग़ल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C418-NAZDIK418-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बग़ल** — *baġal* — "the side; beside (with में)".

> बैंक के बग़ल में एक दवाख़ाना है।
>
> *baiṅk ke baġal meṁ ek davākhānā hai.*
>
> There is a pharmacy next to the bank.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बग़ल** (*baġal*) is usually traced to Persian *baġal*, "the armpit, the side of the body": what is in your armpit is right beside you.
