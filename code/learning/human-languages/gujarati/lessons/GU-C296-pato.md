## You'll want to know: પાટો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C296-AMBULANS296-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પાટો** — *pāṭo* — "a bandage".

> નર્સે તેના હાથ પર પાટો બાંધ્યો.
>
> *narse tenā hāth par pāṭo bā̃dhyo.*
>
> The nurse bandaged his arm.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાટો** (*pāṭo*) goes back to Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "cloth, a bandage", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun (CDIAL 7700). A bandage is tied on: **પાટો બાંધ્યો**.
