Kannada, chapter 391: Fairness for All.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಮಾನತೆ (samānate) — equality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for official, then the Kannada for interference.

You'll want to know: ಸಮಾನತೆ.
ಸಮಾನತೆ — samānate — "equality".
ಗಂಡು ಹೆಣ್ಣಿನ ನಡುವೆ ಸಮಾನತೆ ಇರಬೇಕು. gaṇḍu heṇṇina naḍuve samānate irabēku. There must be equality between men and women.
ಸಮಾನ, equal, + -ತೆ, -ness.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಾನತೆ (samānate) is borrowed from Sanskrit samānatā, "equality" (Monier-Williams), from samāna, "equal".

You'll want to know: ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡು.

ಆ ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನು ಗ್ರಾಹಕರಲ್ಲಿ ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. ā aṅgaḍiyavanu grāhakaralli tāratamya māḍuvudilla. That shopkeeper does not discriminate among customers.
Past (I …): ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, tāratamya māḍide.
ತಾರತಮ್ಯ alone is discrimination.

The word, taken apart.
ತಾರತಮ್ಯ ಮಾಡು (tāratamya māḍu) is built from Sanskrit tāratamya, "gradation, difference" (Monier-Williams), with māḍu, "to do": to rank people as more and less.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಶೋಷಿಸು (śōṣisu) — to exploit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for equality, then the Kannada for to discriminate.

You'll want to know: ಶೋಷಿಸು.
ಶೋಷಿಸು — śōṣisu — "to exploit".
ಆ ಕಂಪನಿ ಕೆಲಸಗಾರರನ್ನು ಶೋಷಿಸಿತು. ā kampani kelasagārarannu śōṣisitu. That company exploited its workers.
Past (I …): ಶೋಷಿಸಿದೆ, śōṣiside.
Who is exploited takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಶೋಷಣೆ, exploitation.

The word, taken apart.
ಶೋಷಿಸು (śōṣisu) is built on Sanskrit śoṣaṇa, "drying up, draining" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu: to drain someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to discriminate, then the Kannada for to exploit.

You'll want to know: ಸಬಲೀಕರಣ.
ಸಬಲೀಕರಣ — sabalīkaraṇa — "empowerment".
ಮಹಿಳೆಯರ ಸಬಲೀಕರಣಕ್ಕೆ ಶಿಕ್ಷಣ ಮುಖ್ಯ. mahiḷeyara sabalīkaraṇakke śikṣaṇa mukhya. Education is key to women's empowerment.
ಸಬಲ (strong) + -ಈಕರಣ (making).

The word, taken apart.
ಸಬಲೀಕರಣ (sabalīkaraṇa) is a modern word built on Sanskrit sabala, "powerful, strong" (Monier-Williams), with -īkaraṇa, "making into": making strong.
