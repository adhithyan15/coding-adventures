## You'll want to know: घूमणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C204-OUT204-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घूमणो** — *ghūmṇo* — "to stroll; to go round".

> आथण हूं बाजार मांय घूमूं हूं।
>
> *āthaṇ hū̃ bājār mā̃y ghūmū̃ hū̃.*
>
> In the evening I stroll round the market.

Past (I …): हूं सहर मांय घूम्यो, *hū̃ sahar mā̃y ghūmyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घूमणो** (*ghūmṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ghummati*, "revolves". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *ghūmnā*, "to revolve, wander" (CDIAL 4485).
