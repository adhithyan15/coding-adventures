Hindi, chapter 407: On the Highway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
राजमार्ग (rājmārg) — a highway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a helmet, then the Hindi for a licence.

You'll want to know: राजमार्ग.
राजमार्ग — rājmārg — "a highway".
यह राजमार्ग पहाड़ों से होकर जाता है। yah rājmārg pahāṛoṁ se hokar jātā hai. This highway goes through the mountains.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
राजमार्ग (rājmārg) is a tatsama, Sanskrit rājamārga, "the king's road, a main road" (Monier-Williams), from rāja, "king", and mārga, "road"; the national highways carry it on their signs.

Lesson 3 of 5.
नाका (nākā) — a checkpoint (on a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a highway, then the Hindi for a toll.

You'll want to know: नाका.
नाका — nākā — "a checkpoint (on a road)".
शहर के नाके पर पुलिस गाड़ियाँ रोक रही थी। śahar ke nāke par pulis gāṛiyā̃ rok rahī thī. At the checkpoint into the town the police were stopping cars.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
नाका (nākā) goes back to nākka, "like a nose, projecting", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 7037), which lists Hindi nākā, "the end of a road, a passage, a gap": the narrow point where a road enters a town, and so the post that watches it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
क़ाफ़िला (qāfilā) — a convoy; a caravan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a toll, then the Hindi for a checkpoint.

You'll want to know: क़ाफ़िला.
क़ाफ़िला — qāfilā — "a convoy; a caravan".
राजमार्ग पर ट्रकों का एक लंबा क़ाफ़िला जा रहा था। rājmārg par ṭrakoṁ kā ek lambā qāfilā jā rahā thā. A long convoy of lorries was moving along the highway.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
क़ाफ़िला (qāfilā) is usually traced to Arabic qāfila, "a caravan", which came to Hindi through Persian: once a line of camels and travellers crossing the desert together, now any line of vehicles on the move.

Lesson 5 of 5.
बहाना (bahānā) — to wash away; to make (water) flow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a checkpoint, then the Hindi for a convoy.

You'll want to know: बहाना.
बहाना — bahānā — "to wash away; to make (water) flow".
बाढ़ ने पुराना पुल बहा दिया। bāṛh ne purānā pul bahā diyā. The flood washed away the old bridge.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नाली में पानी बहाया, usne nālī meṁ pānī bahāyā.
A different word, the noun बहाना (bahānā), an excuse, is spelled the same way.

The word, taken apart.
बहाना (bahānā) is the causative partner of बहना, "to flow", from Sanskrit vahati, "carries along" (CDIAL 11453): to make water flow, or to let it carry a thing away.
