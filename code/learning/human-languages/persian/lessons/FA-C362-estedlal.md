## You'll want to know: استدلال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C362-ESTEDLAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**استدلال** (*estedlâl*) — "an argument, a line of reasoning".

> استدلال او خیلی قوی بود.
>
> *estedlâl-e u kheyli qavi bud.*
>
> His argument was very strong.

Formal. Plural *estedlâlhâ*. It is an argument as reasoning, not a quarrel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استدلال** comes from Arabic *istidlāl* "reasoning, inference" (EtymDB), a verbal noun from the same Arabic family as *dalīl* "proof, guide", which gave Persian *dalil*.
