## You'll want to know: آتش بازی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C320-ATISHBAZI320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آتش بازی** — *ātish bāzī* — "fireworks".

> رات کو آسمان پر آتش بازی ہوئی۔
>
> *rāt ko āsmān par ātish bāzī huī.*
>
> There were fireworks in the sky at night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آتش بازی** (*ātish bāzī*) is usually traced to Persian *ātish-bāzī*, built from *ātish*, "fire", and *bāzī*, "play". It is feminine.
