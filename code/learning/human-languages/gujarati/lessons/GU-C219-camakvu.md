## You'll want to know: ચમકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C219-THUNDER219-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચમકવું** — *camakvũ* — "to shine, to flash".

> **આકાશમાં વીજળી ચમકશે.** — *ākāśmā̃ vījḷī camakśe* — "Lightning will flash in the sky."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચમકવું** (*camakvũ*) comes from *\*camakka*, "sudden movement", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to glitter, be startled" (CDIAL 4676).
