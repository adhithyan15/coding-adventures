## You'll want to know: ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C408-PATHYAKRAMA408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ** — *paṭhyakrama* — "a syllabus".

> ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ ಬದಲಾಯಿತು.
>
> *ī varṣa paṭhyakrama badalāyitu.*
>
> The syllabus changed this year.

ಪಠ್ಯ, a text to be read; ಪಠ್ಯಪುಸ್ತಕ, a textbook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ** (*paṭhyakrama*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *paṭhya*, "to be read", from *paṭh*, "to read", and *krama*, "a step, an order" (CDIAL 3577).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
