## You'll want to know: اطمینان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C447-CALM447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اطمینان** — *itmīnān* — "satisfaction; peace of mind".

> کام ختم کر کے مجھے اطمینان ہوا۔
>
> *kām khatm kar ke mujhe itmīnān huā.*
>
> I felt satisfied once the work was finished.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اطمینان** (*itmīnān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iṭmiʾnān*, "being at rest".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
