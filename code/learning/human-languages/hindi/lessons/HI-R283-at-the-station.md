# Review — at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C283-AGLA-01, HI-LEX-C283-KHONA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the next train" and "next week". (*aglī ṭren*, *agle hafte*.) Then "my bag got lost". (*merā thailā kho gayā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C283-AGLA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-BUY-TICKET-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01, HI-LEX-C283-KHONA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-LOST-REPORT-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-MISSED-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01, HI-LEX-C205-THG205-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Excuse me, where is the ticket office? Go straight on, then turn left.]
- [YOU SAY: One ticket for the village, please. How much is it?]
- [YOU SAY: When will the next train come? How long will it take?]
- [YOU SAY: My key got lost. Then the other way: I lost my key.]
- [YOU SAY: I was late, so I missed my train. Please help me.]
- [YOU SAY: Next week I will go by bus.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C283-AGLA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-BUY-TICKET-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01, HI-LEX-C283-KHONA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-LOST-REPORT-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C283-MISSED-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "The next train"? (**अगली ट्रेन**.) "How much is one ticket?" (**एक टिकट कितने का है?**) "What time does it leave?" (**कितने बजे चलेगी?**) "I lost my key"? (**मैंने अपनी चाबी खो दी**: **ने**, and **दी** for the key.) "I missed my train"? (**मेरी ट्रेन छूट गई**.)
