# ठसका लागणे (ṭhaskā lāgṇe) — to choke on food

## You'll want to know: ठसका लागणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C492-UCAKI492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ठसका लागणे** — *ṭhaskā lāgṇe* — "to choke on food".

> घाईत पाणी प्यायल्याने त्याला ठसका लागला.
>
> *ghāīt pāṇī pyāylyāne tyālā ṭhaskā lāglā.*
>
> He choked from drinking water too fast.

Past (to him …): त्याला ठसका लागला, *tyālā ṭhaskā lāglā*.

A coughing fit when food or water goes down the wrong way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठसका लागणे** (*ṭhaskā lāgṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on ठसका, "a choking cough", whose origin is not settled, with लागणे.
