## You'll want to know: かばん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KABAN]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KA-01, JA-SCRIPT-N-01] -->

**かばん** — *kaban* — "a bag" you carry: a school bag, a briefcase, a
handbag.

Three beats: *ka–ba–n*. **か** and **ん** you already write. The middle sign
is **は** with the voicing mark, and on an *h* sign the mark makes a *b*:
*ha* becomes *ba*. The next lesson writes it.
