## You'll want to know: der Applaus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C517-THEATER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Applaus** — "applause".

> Der Applaus dauerte fünf Minuten.
>
> *The applause lasted five minutes.*

No plural. Also *der Beifall*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Applaus* comes from Latin *applausus* "applause" (EtymDB), from *applaudere* "to strike against", built on *plaudere* "to strike".
