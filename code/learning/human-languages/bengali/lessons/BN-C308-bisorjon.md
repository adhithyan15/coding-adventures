## You'll want to know: বিসর্জন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C308-BISORJON308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিসর্জন** — *bisôrjôn* — "the immersion of an idol".

> পুজোর শেষ দিনে প্রতিমার বিসর্জন হয়।
>
> *pujor shesh dine prôtimār bisôrjôn hôy.*
>
> On the last day of the puja comes the immersion of the image.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিসর্জন** (*bisôrjôn*) is Sanskrit *visarjana*, 'sending forth, dismissal' (Monier-Williams), which already names the casting of a deity's image into holy water at the close of a festival.
