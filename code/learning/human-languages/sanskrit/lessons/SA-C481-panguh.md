## You'll want to know: पंगुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C481-ANDHA481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पंगुः** — *paṅguḥ* — "lame".

> पंगुः वृद्धः शनैः चलति।
>
> *paṅguḥ vṛddhaḥ śanaiḥ calati.*
>
> The lame old man walks slowly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पंगुः** (*paṅguḥ*) MW asks, with a question mark, whether it comes from a root √paj, so its origin is uncertain; it gives "lame, halt, crippled in the legs" (MW).
