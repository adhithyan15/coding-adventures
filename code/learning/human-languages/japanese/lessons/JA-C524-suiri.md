## You'll want to know: すいり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C524-IMAGINE524-04]; assesses=[] -->

**すいり** — *suiri* — "reasoning; deduction".

> **もし かれの すいりが あたったら、すごいでしょう。** — *moshi kare no suiri ga atattara, sugoi deshō* — "If his reasoning turns out right, that will be amazing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいり** (*suiri*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sui*, "conjecture" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwij*) and *ri*, "logic" (*liX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
