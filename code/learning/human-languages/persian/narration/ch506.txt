Persian, chapter 506: Family Ties.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ارث بردن (ers bordan) — to inherit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to hug, then the Persian for to caress.

You'll want to know: ارث بردن.
ارث بردن (ers bordan) — "to inherit".
او این خانه را از پدربزرگش ارث برد. u in khâne râ az pedarbozorgash ers bord. He inherited this house from his grandfather.
ers 'inheritance' + bordan 'to carry off'; present stem bar-. The giver takes az.

The word, taken apart.
ers is a loan, usually traced to the Arabic word for 'inheritance'; EtymDB has no entry for it. bordan, 'to carry', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
شباهت داشتن (shebâhat dâshtan) — to resemble, to look like.

You'll want to know: شباهت داشتن.
شباهت داشتن (shebâhat dâshtan) — "to resemble, to look like".
او خیلی به مادرش شباهت دارد. u kheyli be mâdarash shebâhat dârad. She looks a lot like her mother.
be kasi shebâhat dâshtan; shebâhat 'likeness' + dâshtan 'to have'. It shares a root with shabih 'similar'.

The word, taken apart.
shebâhat is a loan, usually traced to Arabic šabāha, 'likeness', from the same root as shabih, 'similar'. EtymDB has no entry for it. dâshtan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سر زدن (sar zadan) — to drop in (on someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to inherit, then the Persian for to resemble.

You'll want to know: سر زدن.
سر زدن (sar zadan) — "to drop in (on someone)".
بعد از کار به مادرم سر میزنم. ba'd az kâr be mâdaram sar mizanam. After work I drop in on my mother.
be kasi sar zadan; literally 'to strike a head'. Present stem zan-.

The word, taken apart.
sar zadan is built on two inherited words: sar, 'head', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and zadan, 'to strike'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
رفت و آمد کردن (raft-o âmad kardan) — to visit each other, to keep in touch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to resemble, then the Persian for to drop in.

You'll want to know: رفت و آمد کردن.
رفت و آمد کردن (raft-o âmad kardan) — "to visit each other, to keep in touch".
ما با همسایه ها زیاد رفت و آمد میکنیم. mâ bâ hamsâyehâ ziyâd raft-o âmad mikonim. We see a lot of our neighbours.
raft 'going' + o 'and' + âmad 'coming' (the past stems of raftan and âmadan) + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
raft-o âmad is built on two inherited past stems, raft from raftan, 'to go', and âmad from âmadan, 'to come': coming and going. kardan is inherited too.

Lesson 5 of 5.
آبرو (âberu) — reputation, good name.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to drop in, then the Persian for to visit each other.

You'll want to know: آبرو.
آبرو (âberu) — "reputation, good name".
این کار آبروی خانواده را برد. in kâr âberu-ye khânevâde râ bord. This act disgraced the family.
âberu bordan — to disgrace; âberu dâshtan — to be respected.

The word, taken apart.
âberu is built on two native Persian words: âb, 'water, lustre', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and ru, 'face': the shine on a face.
