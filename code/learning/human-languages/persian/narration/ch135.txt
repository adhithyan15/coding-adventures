Persian, chapter 135: To drive, To stay, To live, To stick, To spin.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
راندن (rândan) — to drive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to smell, then the Persian for to dig.

You'll want to know: راندن.
راندن — rândan — "to drive".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: rândan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: rândan, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say rândan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for to smell, then the Persian for to dig, then say rândan again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does راندن (rândan) mean? ("To drive".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words and verbs — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to stick and to spin.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: golden, round, kind, simple, calm]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tired, silent, glad, wise, ignorant]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: healthy, coarse, tiny, little, much]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: popular, to pay, to learn, to jump, to fall]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Golden? (طلایی, talâyi.) Round? (گرد, gerd.) Kind? (مهربان, mehrabân.) Simple? (ساده, sâde.) Calm? (آرام, ârâm.) Tired? (خسته, khaste.) Silent? (ساکت, sâkat.) Glad? (شاد, shâd.) Wise? (دانا, dânâ.) Ignorant? (نادان, nâdân.) Healthy? (سالم, sâlem.) Coarse? (درشت, dorosht.) Tiny? (ریز, riz.) Little? (کم, kam.) Much? (زیاد, ziyâd.) Popular? (محبوب, mahbub.) To pay? (پرداختن, pardâkhtan.) To learn? (آموختن, âmukhtan.) To jump? (پریدن, paridan.) To fall? (افتادن, oftâdan.)

To turn? (پیچیدن, pichidan.) To arrive? (رسیدن, residan.) To weep? (گریستن, geristan.) To hit? (زدن, zadan.) To be afraid? (ترسیدن, tarsidan.) To taste? (چشیدن, chashidan.) To smell?
