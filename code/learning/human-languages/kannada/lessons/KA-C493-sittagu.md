# ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು (siṭṭāgu) — to get annoyed; to lose your temper

## You'll want to know: ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C493-KOPA493-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು** — *siṭṭāgu* — "to get annoyed; to lose your temper".

> ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಅಪ್ಪನಿಗೆ ನನ್ನ ಮೇಲೆ ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಯಿತು.
>
> *taḍavāgi bandiddakke appanige nanna mēle siṭṭāyitu.*
>
> Father got annoyed with me for coming late.

Past (it …): ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಯಿತು, *siṭṭāyitu*.

The one who gets annoyed takes -ಗೆ, the target … ಮೇಲೆ. ಸಿಟ್ಟು, a temper, is the everyday word beside ಕೋಪ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು** (*siṭṭāgu*) is built from *siṭṭu*, "a temper, anger", and ಆಗು, "to become"; the origin of *siṭṭu* is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
