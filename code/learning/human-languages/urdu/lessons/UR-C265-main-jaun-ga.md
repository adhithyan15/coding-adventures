# میں جاؤں گا (maiṅ jāūṅ gā) — what will happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C145-MUST145-01, UR-LEX-PARHNA, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You met one future long ago: **میں پڑھوں گا** (*maiṅ paṛhūṅ gā*), "I will read". Say it, then a woman's **پڑھوں گی** (*paṛhūṅ gī*). This lesson gives every person.

## You'll want to know: the future, person by person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-MADAD-KARNA, UR-LEX-JANA, UR-LEX-AAP-TUM-TU, UR-LEX-AAP-TUM-TU, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-03] -->

> **میں جاؤں گا۔**
>
> *maiṅ jāūṅ gā.* — "I will go."

> **وہ جائے گی۔**
>
> *voh jāe gī.* — "She will go."

A short ending follows the person, and **گا** (*gā*), **گی** (*gī*) or **گے** (*ge*) follows the man, the woman or the group. The book writes it apart, as it wrote **پڑھوں گا**:

> **ہم کل جائیں گے۔**
>
> *ham kal jāeṅ ge.* — "We will go tomorrow."

> **میں کام کروں گی۔**
>
> *maiṅ kām karūṅ gī.* — "I will do the work."

> **تم کب آؤ گے؟**
>
> *tum kab āo ge?* — "When will you come?"

| who | کرنا | جانا |
|---|---|---|
| **میں** | **کروں گا** (*karūṅ gā*) | **جاؤں گا** (*jāūṅ gā*) |
| **تم** | **کرو گے** (*karo ge*) | **جاؤ گے** (*jāo ge*) |
| **وہ**, one | **کرے گا** (*kare gā*) | **جائے گا** (*jāe gā*) |
| **ہم**, **آپ**, **وہ** | **کریں گے** (*kareṅ ge*) | **جائیں گے** (*jāeṅ ge*) |

"Won't" puts **نہیں** before it: **میں نہیں جاؤں گا** (*maiṅ nahīṅ jāūṅ gā*).

## You'll want to know: ہو گا, "will be"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-LEX-C57-FOOD57-05, UR-LEX-C192-VAJAH192-02] -->

> **کل بارش ہو گی۔**
>
> *kal bārish ho gī.* — "It will rain tomorrow."

**بارش** (*bārish*) is feminine: **ہو گی**. For a man, **ہو گا**; for a group, **ہوں گے** (*hoṅ ge*):

> **وہ شاید گھر پر ہو گا۔**
>
> *voh shāyad ghar par ho gā.* — "He will probably be at home."
