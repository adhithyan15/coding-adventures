Portuguese, chapter 488: Ficar bom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o repouso — rest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to prescribe, then the Portuguese for treatment.

You'll want to know: o repouso.
o repouso — "rest".
O médico recomendou uma semana de repouso. The doctor recommended a week of rest.
Estar em repouso — to be resting, on doctor's orders.

The word, taken apart.
repouso comes from repousar "to rest" (EtymDB), inherited from Late Latin repausāre "to lay at rest", built on pausāre "to pause".
