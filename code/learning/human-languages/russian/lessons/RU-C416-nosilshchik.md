## You'll want to know: носильщик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C416-PROVODNIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**носильщик** (*nosílshchik*) — "a porter".

> Носильщик помог нам с чемоданами.
>
> *Nosílshchik pomóg nam s chemodánami.*
>
> The porter helped us with the suitcases.

Masculine. Built on *носить* — to carry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*носильщик* is built on *носить* 'to carry' with *-льщик* 'one who does it'. *Носить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *nositi* (EtymDB).
