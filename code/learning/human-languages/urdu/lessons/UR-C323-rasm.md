## You'll want to know: رسم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C323-RIVAJ323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رسم** — *rasm* — "a rite; a ceremony".

> شادی سے پہلے مہندی کی رسم ہوئی۔
>
> *shādī se pehle mehndī kī rasm huī.*
>
> Before the wedding there was the henna ceremony.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رسم** (*rasm*) is usually traced to Persian *rasm*, "a usage, a custom", a word Persian took from Arabic. It is feminine: **رسم ہوئی**.
