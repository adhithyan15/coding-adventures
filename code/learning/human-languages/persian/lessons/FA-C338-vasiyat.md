## You'll want to know: وصیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C338-KHATM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وصیت** (*vasiyat*) — "a will, a last wish".

> در وصیت خود خانه را به دخترش داد.
>
> *Dar vasiyat-e khod khâne râ be dokhtarash dâd.*
>
> In his will he left the house to his daughter.

*Vasiyatnâme* is a written will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vasiyat* is borrowed from Arabic *waṣiyya* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a bequest, a last instruction".
