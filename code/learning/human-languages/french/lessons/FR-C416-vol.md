## You'll want to know: le vol
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C416-AEROGARE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le vol** — "the flight".

> Notre vol part à sept heures du matin.
>
> *Our flight leaves at seven in the morning.*

*Le vol* is also a theft; that sense comes from *voler* 'to steal'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vol* is built on *voler* 'to fly' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *volāre*.
