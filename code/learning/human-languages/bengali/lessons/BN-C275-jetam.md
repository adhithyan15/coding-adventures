# যদি … থাকত, তাহলে … যেতাম (jôdi … thāktô, tāhôle … jetām) — what would be

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-THEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "It depends on the weather". (*eṭā ābôhāoyār opôr nirbhôr kôre*.) Then "If you come, I'll cook". (*jôdi tumi āso, āmi rānnā kôrbô*.) That if can come true.

## You'll want to know: what would be
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C159-KARON159-03, BN-LEX-C218-KARON218-04, BN-LEX-C80-TIME80-04, BN-CONCEPT-C05-THAKA-01, BN-LEX-C64-SCHOOL64-04, BN-LEX-C49-VERBS49-02] -->

> **যদি সময় থাকত, তাহলে আমি যেতাম।**
>
> *jôdi sômôy thāktô, tāhôle āmi jetām.* — "If I had time, I would go."

For an if that is not true, both halves take the **-ত-** form: the stem, then **-ত-**, then the person: **আমি যেতাম** (*āmi jetām*), **তুমি যেতে** (*tumi jete*), **সে যেত** (*she jetô*), **আপনি যেতেন** (*āpni jeten*). **যাওয়া** → **যে-**, **খাওয়া** → **খে-**, **হওয়া** → **হ-**; **করা** → **করতাম**, **থাকা** → **থাকতাম**. The if half may take the **-লে** form instead: **টাকা থাকলে আমি একটা নৌকা কিনতাম** (*ṭākā thākle āmi ekṭā noukā kintām*), "if I had money, I'd buy a boat".

## You'll want to know: what used to be
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C275-USED-TO-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C80-TIME80-02, BN-LEX-C92-TIME92-04, BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-03, BN-LEX-C72-ADJ72-02] -->

The same form tells what happened again and again in the past:

> **আগে আমি রোজ নদীতে যেতাম।**
>
> *āge āmi roj nôdīte jetām.* — "I used to go to the river every day."

**আগে**, "before", here is "in the old days". A word like **রোজ** or **প্রতিদিন** makes the habit clear.
