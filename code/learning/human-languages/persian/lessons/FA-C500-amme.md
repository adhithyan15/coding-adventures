## You'll want to know: عمه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C500-UNCLES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عمه** (*'amme*) — "an aunt (father's sister)".

> عمه من آشپز خوبی است.
>
> *'amme-ye man âshpaz-e khubi ast.*
>
> My aunt is a good cook.

The father's sister; the mother's sister is *khâle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'amme* was borrowed from Arabic *ʿamma*, 'paternal aunt' (EtymDB), the feminine of the word behind *'amu*.
