## You'll want to know: قاعدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C367-QAIDA367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قاعدہ** — *qāidā* — "a rule; a regulation".

> اگر قاعدہ یہی ہے تو ہم کیا کر سکتے ہیں؟
>
> *agar qāidā yihī hai to ham kyā kar sakte haiṅ?*
>
> If that is the rule, what can we do?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قاعدہ** (*qāidā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qāʿida*, "a base, a foundation; a rule".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
