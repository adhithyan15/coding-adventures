## You'll want to know: اطلاع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C300-KHABAR300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اطلاع** — *ittilā* — "an official notice; word sent".

> پولیس کو اطلاع ملی۔
>
> *polīs ko ittilā milī.*
>
> The police were informed.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اطلاع** (*ittilā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iṭṭilāʿ*, "a coming to know". **اطلاع ملی** says word reached someone; **اطلاع دینا** is to inform.
