Hindi, chapter 549: Two Ways to Go.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विकल्प (vikalp) — an option; an alternative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for laziness, then the Hindi for to squander.

You'll want to know: विकल्प.
विकल्प — vikalp — "an option; an alternative".
हमारे पास दो विकल्प हैं: बस या ट्रेन। Hamāre pās do vikalp haiṁ: bas yā ṭren. We have two options: the bus or the train.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विकल्प (vikalp) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit vikalpa, "alternation, option" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दुविधा (duvidhā) — a dilemma; being in two minds.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to squander, then the Hindi for an option.

You'll want to know: दुविधा.
दुविधा — duvidhā — "a dilemma; being in two minds".
दो अच्छे घर देखकर अरुण दुविधा में पड़ गया। Do acche ghar dekhkar Aruṇ duvidhā meṁ paṛ gayā. After seeing two good houses, Arun was in two minds.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
दुविधा (duvidhā) is usually traced to Sanskrit dvividha, "twofold, of two kinds" (Monier-Williams); the sense "being of two minds" is the Hindi one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
असमंजस (asmañjas) — indecision; uncertainty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an option, then the Hindi for a dilemma.

You'll want to know: असमंजस.
असमंजस — asmañjas — "indecision; uncertainty".
नौकरी बदलने को लेकर वह असमंजस में है। Naukrī badalne ko lekar vah asmañjas meṁ hai. He cannot decide whether to change jobs.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
असमंजस (asmañjas) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit asamañjasa, "unfit, unbecoming" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for not knowing which way to turn.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: हिचकना.
हिचकना — hicaknā — "to hesitate, to hold back".
बोलने से पहले वह थोड़ा हिचका। Bolne se pahle vah thoṛā hickā. He hesitated a little before speaking.
Past (he …): वह हिचका, vah hickā.

The word, taken apart.
हिचकना (hicaknā) is a tadbhava, inherited from hicc, "draw back", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 14083).
