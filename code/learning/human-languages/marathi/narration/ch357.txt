Lesson 1 of 6.
प्रशासन (praśāsan) — administration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for public opinion, then the Marathi for to give a mandate.

You'll want to know: प्रशासन.
प्रशासन — praśāsan — "administration".
पुरानंतर प्रशासनाने लोकांना मदत केली. purānantar praśāsnāne lokānnā madat kelī. After the flood the administration helped people.
Neuter.
Neuter; प्रशासकीय, administrative.

The word, taken apart.
प्रशासन (praśāsan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit praśāsana, "instruction, government" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 6.
मंत्री (mantrī) — a cabinet minister; a minister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for administration, then the Marathi for parliament.

You'll want to know: मंत्री.
मंत्री — mantrī — "a cabinet minister; a minister".
मंत्र्यांनी नव्या पुलाचं उद्घाटन केलं. mantryānnī navyā pulācaṁ udghāṭan kelaṁ. The minister opened the new bridge.
Masculine.
Respect takes the plural: मंत्री (mantrī) आले, the minister came.

The word, taken apart.
मंत्री (mantrī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit mantrin, "a counsellor, a minister" (Monier-Williams). Marathi drops the final -n.

नेता (netā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit netṛ, "a leader, a guide" (Monier-Williams). The Sanskrit nominative is netā.

Lesson 5 of 6.
नेमणे (nemṇe) — to appoint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cabinet minister, then the Marathi for a leader.

You'll want to know: नेमणे.
नेमणे — nemṇe — "to appoint".
कंपनीने नवा व्यवस्थापक नेमला. kampnīne navā vyavasthāpak nemlā. The company appointed a new manager.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नेमलं, tyāne nemlaṁ.
X म्हणून नेमणे (nemṇe), to appoint as X; नेमणूक, an appointment.

The word, taken apart.
नेमणे (nemṇe) is built on नेम, "a fixed rule, an aim", usually traced to Sanskrit niyama, "restraint, fixed rule" (Monier-Williams).
