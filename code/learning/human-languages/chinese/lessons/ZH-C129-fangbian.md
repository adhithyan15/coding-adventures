## You'll want to know: fāngbiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C129-PLACE129-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngbiàn* — "convenient; handy".

> *zhèr mǎi dōngxi hěn fāngbiàn* — Shopping here is easy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngbiàn* is built from *fāng* and *biàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjang* and *bjienH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'the four quarters' and 'a method', and the second 'advantageous'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
