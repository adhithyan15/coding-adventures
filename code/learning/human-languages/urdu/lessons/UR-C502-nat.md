## You'll want to know: نٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C502-ZANG502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نٹ** — *naṭ* — "a nut (for a bolt)".

> سائیکل کا ایک نٹ کھو گیا۔
>
> *sāikil kā ek naṭ kho gayā.*
>
> One of the bicycle's nuts has gone missing.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نٹ** (*naṭ*) is borrowed from English *nut*, the small metal piece on a bolt.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
