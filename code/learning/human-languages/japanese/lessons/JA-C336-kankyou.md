## You'll want to know: かんきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C336-KANKYOU336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かんきょう** — *kankyō* — "the environment; surroundings".

> **かんきょうの ために、じてんしゃを つかいます。** — *kankyō no tame ni, jitensha o tsukaimasu* — "I ride a bicycle for the sake of the environment."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんきょう** (*kankyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "ring" (Middle Chinese *hwaen*) and *kyō*, "boundary" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
