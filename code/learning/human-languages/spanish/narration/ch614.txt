Spanish, chapter 614: Riesgos y garantías.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
prevenir — to prevent, to head off; to warn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for likely, then the Spanish for the probability.

You'll want to know: prevenir.
prevenir — "to prevent, to head off; to warn".
Es mejor prevenir los problemas. It is better to prevent problems.
Like venir: prevengo, previne.

The word, taken apart.
prevenir is a learned borrowing from Latin praevenire 'to come before, to anticipate', built from prae and venire 'to come' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: arriesgar.
arriesgar — "to risk".
No quiero arriesgar mi dinero. I do not want to risk my money.
Arriesgarse — to take a chance. Spelling: arriesgué.

The word, taken apart.
arriesgar is built on riesgo 'risk', which comes from Italian rischio (EtymDB); the older origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to prevent, then the Spanish for to risk.

You'll want to know: garantizar.
garantizar — "to guarantee".
No te puedo garantizar nada. I cannot guarantee you anything.
Spelling: garanticé.

The word, taken apart.
garantizar is built on garantía; EtymDB links garantía with Catalan garantia and English guarantee, and the family is usually traced to Old French garant 'guarantor', from a Frankish word for a protector.

la garantía — "the guarantee, the warranty".

garantía comes from garante 'guarantor'; EtymDB links it with Catalan garantia, and the family is usually traced to Old French garant, from a Frankish word for a protector.
