## You'll want to know: débarquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C422-TRAVERSEE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**débarquer** — "to get off (a boat or plane), to land".

> Les passagers débarquent sur le quai.
>
> *The passengers get off onto the quay.*

Informal: *il débarque* — he turns up out of the blue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*débarquer* is built from *dé-* and *barque* 'boat' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *barca* (Lewis & Short).
