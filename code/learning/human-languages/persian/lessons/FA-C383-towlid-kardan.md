## You'll want to know: تولید کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C383-TOWLID-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تولید کردن** (*towlid kardan*) — "to produce, to manufacture".

> این کارخانه کفش تولید میکند.
>
> *in kârkhâne kafsh towlid mikonad.*
>
> This factory produces shoes.

*Towlid* "production" + *kardan* (stem *kon-*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تولید** is usually traced to Arabic *tawlīd* "generating, bringing forth", from the same Arabic family as *tavallod* "birth"; *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
