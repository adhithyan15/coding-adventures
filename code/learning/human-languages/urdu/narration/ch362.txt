Urdu, chapter 362: Perhaps, Probably.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
تخمینہ (takhmīnā) — an estimate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a probability, then the Urdu for to go by.

You'll want to know: تخمینہ.
تخمینہ — takhmīnā — "an estimate".
اگر تخمینہ ٹھیک ہے تو خرچ کم ہو گا۔ agar takhmīnā ṭhīk hai to kharch kam ho gā. If the estimate is right, the cost will be lower.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تخمینہ (takhmīnā) is usually traced to Persian takhmīna, built on Arabic takhmīn, "guessing, reckoning".

One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 6.
بوجھنا (būjhnā) — to work out a riddle; to guess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for often, then the Urdu for conjecture.

You'll want to know: بوجھنا.
بوجھنا — būjhnā — "to work out a riddle; to guess".
اگر آپ نے ٹھیک بوجھا تو آپ جیتیں گے۔ agar āp ne ṭhīk būjhā to āp jīteṅ ge. If you guess right, you will win.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے ٹھیک بوجھا, us ne ṭhīk būjhā.

The word, taken apart.
بوجھنا (būjhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin būjhnā, "to understand, to ascertain", under Sanskrit búdhyate, "wakes; understands" (CDIAL 9279).
