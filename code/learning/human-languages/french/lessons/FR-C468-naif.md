## You'll want to know: naïf
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C468-FRANCHISE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**naïf** — "naive".

> J'étais si naïf que je croyais tout.
>
> *I was so naive that I believed everything.*

Feminine *naïve*. The noun is *la naïveté*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naïf* is inherited from Latin *nātīvus* "inborn, natural" (EtymDB), from *nāscī* "to be born" (Lewis & Short); the sense moved from "natural" to "unspoiled" and then to "naive".
