# Japanese pre-A1 mock 1 — scoring rubric

This project-defined JLPT/JF Standard precursor has 100 points total: 25 per skill. Require at least 60% on each skill independently and at least 60/100 overall. Attempt every section. A higher score elsewhere cannot compensate for a failed skill. A score meeting this form's threshold does not establish readiness, attainment, or equivalence to an external exam.

## Reading — 25 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-kana-and-doorway-words -->
Five keyed meaning selections, 5 points each. Score recognition of the printed known form, not oral performance.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-kana-and-doorway-words -->

## Listening — 25 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-greetings-and-one-turn-details -->
Five keyed selections, 5 points each. Play each 2–8-second file exactly twice, with a response pause. Measure complete file duration including silence. Do not show a written transcript or spelling.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-greetings-and-one-turn-details -->

## Writing — 25 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-kana-recall -->
Five points: recognizable ordered kana 3, independent delayed retrieval 1, legibility 1. Show the card for ten seconds, then remove it before writing.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-kana-recall -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
Two items at 5 points each: mora-to-kana mapping and order 3, independent transcription 1, legibility 1. Each file lasts 2–5 seconds and is played twice. No visible model.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
Two items at 5 points each: fulfills cue with a taught form 2, recognizable Japanese script 2, independent retrieval and legibility 1. A copied response earns zero independent-writing credit.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

## Speaking — 25 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-doorway-exchange -->
Five prompts at 5 points each: communicative purpose 2, intelligibility and mora timing 2, appropriate taught response 1. Accept one chosen name as a complete identity response; do not score an untaught full sentence. Each prompt lasts 2–8 seconds, with a 2–15-second response; one slow repeat is allowed. Record for independent scoring.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-doorway-exchange -->

Before live use, obtain qualified Japanese review of naturalness, register, mora timing, and recordings, followed by book-only human validation. These files claim neither review nor validation.
