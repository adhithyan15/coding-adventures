## You'll want to know: hūnlǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C254-BORN254-04]; assesses=[] -->

*hūnlǐ* — "wedding".

> *wǒmen qù cānjiā tāmen de hūnlǐ* — We're going to their wedding.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hūnlǐ* is built from *hūn* 'marriage' and *lǐ* 'a rite', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwon* and *lejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes an older dictionary for the second: what is done to serve the spirits.
