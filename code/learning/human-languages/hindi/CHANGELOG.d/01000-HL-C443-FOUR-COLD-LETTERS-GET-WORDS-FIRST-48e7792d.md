## HL-C443: four cold letters get words first

Gentle writing asks for every letter to be taken from a word the reader
already says. Four Hindi letter lessons still came before any word holding
their letter. Each now follows a short word lesson that holds it:

| letter | word placed just before it |
|---|---|
| घ | **घी** (*ghī*, ghee) |
| ढ | **ढोल** (*ḍhol*, a drum) |
| ऊ | **ऊन** (*ūn*, wool) |
| ओ | **ओस** (*os*, dew) |

Each word is revisited by its letter lesson and by the lesson after it.

    hindi cold letter lessons   4  ->  0 (ceiling ratcheted)
