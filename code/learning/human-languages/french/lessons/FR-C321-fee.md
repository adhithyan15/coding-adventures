## You'll want to know: la fée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C321-CONTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la fée** — "the fairy".

> La bonne fée a aidé la jeune fille.
>
> *The good fairy helped the girl.*

*Une bonne fée* — a fairy godmother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fée* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *Fāta* "goddess of fate" (EtymDB), built on Latin *fātum* "destiny".
