## You'll want to know: わく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C492-BOIL492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**わく** — *waku* — "to boil; to get hot".

> **やかんの ゆが わいています。** — *yakan no yu ga waiteimasu* — "The water in the kettle is boiling."

Polite **わきます**, *wakimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おふろが わきました。**, *ofuro ga wakimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わく** (*waku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
