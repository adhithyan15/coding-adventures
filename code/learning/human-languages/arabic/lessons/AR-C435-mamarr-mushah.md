## You'll want to know: ممر مشاة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C435-MASAR435-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ممر مشاة** — *mamarr mushāh* — "a pedestrian crossing".

> عبر الأطفال الشارع من ممر المشاة.
>
> *ʿabara al-aṭfāl ash-shāriʿ min mamarr al-mushāh.*
>
> The children crossed the street at the pedestrian crossing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ممر مشاة** (*mamarr mushāh*) is built from two native Arabic words: *mamarr*, "a passage", on the root *m-r-r*, "to pass" (Lane), and *mushāh*, "walkers", on the root *m-sh-y*, "to walk" (Lane).
