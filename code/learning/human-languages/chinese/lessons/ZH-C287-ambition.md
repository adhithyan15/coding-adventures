## Grammar Lens: 以后 and 想, looking ahead
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-AMBITION-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C97-YIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-01, ZH-LEX-C148-JIANGLAI-01, ZH-LEX-C35-WORK35-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YAO-FUTURE-01, ZH-LEX-C287-DANG-01] -->

> **A time word for later, then 想 or 要, says what you want to do or be.**

> **以后我想去中国工作** *yǐhòu wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó gōngzuò* — Later on I want to go and work in China.
> **以后你想当什么** *yǐhòu nǐ xiǎng dāng shénme* — What do you want to be later on?

以后 here is later, some time to come, at the front of the sentence. *jiānglái* reaches further: some day, in the future.

想 is a wish; 要 is a plan you mean to keep, as in 下个月我要去中国.
