## You'll want to know: কিপটে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C419-ABHAB419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**কিপটে** — *kipṭe* — "stingy; a miser".

> কিপটে দোকানদার একটা পয়সাও ছাড়লেন না।
>
> *kipṭe dokāndār ekṭā pôysāo chhāṛlen nā.*
>
> The stingy shopkeeper would not give up a single paisa.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কিপটে** (*kipṭe*) is an everyday, rather rude word for a stingy person; its origin is not settled.
