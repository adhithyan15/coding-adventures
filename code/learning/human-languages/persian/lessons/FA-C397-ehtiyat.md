## You'll want to know: احتیاط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C397-EHTIYAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**احتیاط** (*ehtiyât*) — "caution, care".

> او با احتیاط از پله ها پایین رفت.
>
> *u bâ ehtiyât az pelle hâ pâyin raft.*
>
> He went down the stairs with care.

*Bâ ehtiyât* — carefully. *Ehtiyât kardan* — to be careful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتیاط** is usually traced to Arabic *iḥtiyāṭ* "caution, precaution".
