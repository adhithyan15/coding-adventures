## You'll want to know: украшение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C313-YOLKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**украшение** (*ukrashéniye*) — "a decoration; a piece of jewellery".

> В коробке лежат ёлочные украшения.
>
> *V koróbke lezhát yólochnyye ukrashéniya.*
>
> The tree decorations are in a box.

Neuter. Also jewellery: *золотые украшения*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*украшение* is built on *украшать* "to decorate", from *краса* "beauty", an inherited Slavic word.
