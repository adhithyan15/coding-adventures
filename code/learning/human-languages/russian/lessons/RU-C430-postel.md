## You'll want to know: постель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C430-POSTEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**постель** (*postél*) — "bedding; a bed".

> Постель в номере была чистая и мягкая.
>
> *Postél v nómere bylá chístaya i myágkaya.*
>
> The bed in the room was clean and soft.

Feminine. *Постельное бельё* — bed linen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*постель* is built on the root of *стлать*, *стелить* 'to spread': what is spread out to sleep on. EtymDB lists *постель* with the senses 'bed; bedding'.
