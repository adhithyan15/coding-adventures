## You'll want to know: das Darlehen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C388-KREDIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Darlehen** — "the loan".

> Meine Eltern haben mir ein Darlehen für das Studium gegeben.
>
> *My parents gave me a loan for my studies.*

Plural: *die Darlehen*. Formal and legal; in everyday speech *der Kredit* is more common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Darlehen* joins *dar* "there, forth" to *Lehen* "a loan, a fief", of the family of *leihen* "to lend", Old High German *līhan*, Proto-Germanic *līhwaną* (EtymDB).
