## You'll want to know: வணிகம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C511-VANIKAM511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**வணிகம்** — *vaṇikam* — "business; commerce".

> என் மாமா துணி வணிகம் செய்கிறார்.
>
> *eṉ māmā tuṇi vaṇikam ceykiṟār.*
>
> My uncle is in the cloth business.

A formal word; in speech people often say வியாபாரம் (*viyāpāram*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வணிகம்** (*vaṇikam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vaṇij*, "a trader", also "trade" (CDIAL 11230), with the Tamil noun ending -அம்.
