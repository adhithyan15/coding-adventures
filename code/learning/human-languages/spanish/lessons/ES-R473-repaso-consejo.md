# (aconsejar, recomendar, el estado) — from cold

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-01, ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Three words, no prompts. What somebody does when asked, what they
do about a restaurant, and the thing an object has to be in before anyone will
take it.

## You'll want to know: the three, cold
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-01, ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-02, ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-03] -->

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| to advise | **aconsejar** |
| to recommend | **recomendar** |
| the condition | **el estado** |

## Grammar Lens: none of the three was really new
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-01, ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-03] -->

Each one is a word you held, put through a machine you had already been shown.

| the word you had | what happened to it |
|---|---|
| el consejo | *a-* on the front, *-ar* on the end |
| estar | its Latin root borrowed back as a noun |

*Acercarse* gave the first machine. *El estado* is the subtler case: it looks
like *estar*'s participle and is not derived from it — both come down from
Latin *stare*, and the noun was borrowed from *status* directly.

*Recomendar* is the odd one out: its pieces are Latin rather than Spanish, and
what you already held was the **rule for its stem** — *pensar*'s e breaking
into ie.

So the chapter asked you to recognise three shapes rather than memorise three
words.

## The words, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-02, ES-LEX-C473-CONSEJO-03] -->

Two of the three carry a picture that is still readable.

| word | underneath |
|---|---|
| recomendar | *mandare*, to **entrust** — to put in the hand |
| el estado | *stare*, to **stand** — how a thing is standing |

And *aconsejar* is *consilium*, a **sitting together** — so none of the three
is really about giving orders. Advice hands a thing over, or sits down beside
you, and neither pushes.
