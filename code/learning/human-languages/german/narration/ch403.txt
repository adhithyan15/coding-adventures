German, chapter 403: Der Motor springt nicht an.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
anspringen — to start (of an engine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the petrol pump, then the German for the oil change.

You'll want to know: anspringen.
anspringen — "to start (of an engine)".
Im Winter springt unser Auto morgens schlecht an. In winter our car is hard to start in the mornings.
Separable; Perfekt with sein: der Motor ist nicht angesprungen.

The word, taken apart.
anspringen is built from an 'on' and springen 'to jump', inherited from Proto-Germanic springaną (EtymDB): an engine that jumps into life. English spring is a cousin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Batterie — the battery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the oil change, then the German for to start.

You'll want to know: die Batterie.
die Batterie — "the battery".
Die Batterie ist leer, weil das Licht die ganze Nacht an war. The battery is flat because the light was on all night.
Plural: die Batterien. The battery in a phone is usually der Akku.

The word, taken apart.
Batterie was borrowed from French batterie, first 'a beating', then a row of guns, from battre 'to beat' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin battuere 'to beat' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Starthilfe — the jump start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to start, then the German for the battery.

You'll want to know: die Starthilfe.
die Starthilfe — "the jump start".
Ein Nachbar gibt uns mit seinem Auto Starthilfe. A neighbour gives us a jump start with his car.
No plural in this sense. Das Starthilfekabel — the jump lead.

The word, taken apart.
Starthilfe is built from Start, usually traced to English start (EtymDB lists Start without a source), and Hilfe 'help', from the inherited verb helfen.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Abschleppwagen — the tow truck, the breakdown lorry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the battery, then the German for the jump start.

You'll want to know: der Abschleppwagen.
der Abschleppwagen — "the tow truck, the breakdown lorry".
Der Abschleppwagen bringt das kaputte Auto in die Werkstatt. The tow truck takes the broken-down car to the garage.
Same form in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
Abschleppwagen is built from abschleppen 'to tow away', from schleppen 'to drag', which came in from Middle Low German slēpen (EtymDB), and Wagen 'vehicle', inherited from Proto-Germanic wagnaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Mechaniker — the mechanic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the jump start, then the German for the tow truck.

You'll want to know: der Mechaniker.
der Mechaniker — "the mechanic".
Der Mechaniker findet den Fehler nach zehn Minuten. The mechanic finds the fault after ten minutes.
Same form in the plural. A woman: die Mechanikerin.

The word, taken apart.
Mechaniker is a learned word built on New Latin mechanicus, from Greek mēkhanikós 'of machines' (EtymDB), from mēkhanḗ 'device'.
