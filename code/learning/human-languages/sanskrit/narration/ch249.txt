Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: for the sake of, by which, by that, that, however]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: easy to get, hard to get, complicated, easy to do, hard to do]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mighty, sick, healthy, happy, unhappy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: proper, improper, necessary, other, alone]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
For the sake of? (कृते, kṛte.) By which? (येन, yena.) By that? (तेन, tena.) That? (यत्, yat.) However? (परन्तु, parantu.) Easy to get? (सुलभः, sulabhaḥ.) Hard to get? (दुर्लभः, durlabhaḥ.) Complicated? (जटिलः, jaṭilaḥ.) Easy to do? (सुकरः, sukaraḥ.) Hard to do? (दुष्करः, duṣkaraḥ.) Mighty? (बलवान्, balavān.) Sick? (रुग्णः, rugṇaḥ.) Healthy? (स्वस्थः, svasthaḥ.) Happy? (सुखी, sukhī.) Unhappy? (दुःखी, duḥkhī.) Proper? (उचितः, ucitaḥ.) Improper? (अनुचितः, anucitaḥ.) Necessary? (आवश्यकः, āvaśyakaḥ.) Other? (अन्यः, anyaḥ.) Alone? (एकाकी, ekākī.)
