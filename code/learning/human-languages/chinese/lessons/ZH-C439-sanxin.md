## You'll want to know: sànxīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C439-COUNTRY439-05]; assesses=[] -->

*sànxīn* — "to take one's mind off things; to unwind".

> *lèi de shíhou wǒ xǐhuan qù nóngcūn sànxīn* — When I'm tired, I like to go to the countryside to unwind.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sànxīn* is built from *sàn* 'to scatter' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sanH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to break apart' and 'to spread': cares scattered from the heart.
