Arabic, chapter 148: Pleased, Spacious, Deep, Round, Sharp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مسرور (masrūr) — pleased.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for kind, pleasant, then the Arabic for angry.

You'll want to know: مسرور.
مسرور — masrūr — "pleased".

Wrap-up Recall.
