## You'll want to know: انتشر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C353-MUZAHARA353-05]; assesses=[] -->

**انتشر** — *intashara* — "to spread".

> انتشر الخبر في القرية بسرعة.
>
> *intashara al-khabar fī al-qarya bi-surʿa.*
>
> The news spread through the village quickly.

Present: ينتشر, *yantashir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتشر** (*intashara*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-sh-r* (Lane), "to spread out", the root of the taught *nashara*. It is Form VIII, "to spread itself".
