# ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ — one familiar number becomes a clock time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C95-NUMBERS95-02, PA-ADV-BAHUT-DEGREE-01, PA-GRAMMAR-MULTIWORD-NP-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the familiar phrase **ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ** once. Then say
the familiar number **ਅੱਠ**. This lesson keeps that number and adds one time
word after it.

## Grammar Lens: ਵਜੇ follows the whole hour
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-TIME-VAJE-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C95-NUMBERS95-02] -->

> **ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ** — *aṭṭh vaje* — at eight o'clock

Keep the order number + **ਵਜੇ**. Here **ਵਜੇ** marks the whole-hour time. This
first step does not add half hours, minutes, or a general clock paradigm.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-TIME-VAJE-01, PA-LEX-C95-NUMBERS95-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU HEAR: *aṭṭh vaje*; YOU SAY: **ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ**]
- [YOU SAY: **ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ**]
- [YOU SAY: the whole phrase once more — **ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-TIME-VAJE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *at eight o'clock*. (**ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ**.) Which piece follows the
number? (**ਵਜੇ**.)
