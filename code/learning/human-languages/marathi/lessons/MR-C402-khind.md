## You'll want to know: खिंड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C402-GHAT402-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खिंड** — *khiṇḍ* — "a narrow pass; a gap".

> किल्ल्याच्या मागे एक अरुंद खिंड आहे.
>
> *killyācyā māge ek arund khiṇḍ āhe.*
>
> Behind the fort there is a narrow pass.

Feminine.

A gap between two hills, narrower than a घाट.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खिंड** (*khiṇḍ*) is a Marathi word for a narrow gap between hills; its origin is not settled.
