بستان (bustān) — an orchard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: بستان.
بستان — bustān — "an orchard".

The word, taken apart.
بستان (bustān) is a loan: Lane's sources argued over it, some calling it Arabic and some Greek, but Lane concludes it is arabicized from Persian būstān, "garden".

You'll want to know: خيمة.
خيمة — khayma — "a tent".

The word, taken apart.
خيمة (khayma), "a tent", is an old native noun, filed under the root kh-y-m (Lane). Lane's khayma is a tent or booth.

موقف (mawqif) — a car park; a stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an orchard, then the Arabic for a tent.

You'll want to know: موقف.
موقف — mawqif — "a car park; a stop".

The word, taken apart.
موقف (mawqif) is built on the native Arabic root w-q-f, "to stand still, to stop" (Lane). Mawqif is the ma- place noun, "a place of stopping".

Lesson 4 of 5.
قارة (qārra) — a continent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a tent, then the Arabic for a car park; a stop.

You'll want to know: قارة.
قارة — qārra — "a continent".

The word, taken apart.
قارة (qārra) is built on the native Arabic root q-r-r, "to settle, to be still" (Lane). Qārra is the feminine active participle, "settled, firm"; the continent, the firm land, is a modern sense.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بركان (burkān) — a volcano.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a car park; a stop, then the Arabic for a continent.

You'll want to know: بركان.
بركان — burkān — "a volcano".

The word, taken apart.
بركان (burkān) is usually traced to Italian vulcano, in its Sicilian form vurcanu (EtymDB), named after Vulcan, the Roman god of fire.
