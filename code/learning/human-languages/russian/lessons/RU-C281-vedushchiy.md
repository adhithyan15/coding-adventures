## You'll want to know: ведущий
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C281-EFIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ведущий** (*vedúshchiy*) — "a presenter, a host".

> Ведущий программы очень весёлый.
>
> *Vedúshchiy prográmmy óchen vesyólyy.*
>
> The presenter of the programme is very cheerful.

A participle of *вести* "to lead" used as a noun; a woman is *ведущая*. It declines like an adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ведущий* is the present participle of *вести* "to lead" (EtymDB), an inherited Slavic verb from the same root as *водить*: the one who leads the show.
