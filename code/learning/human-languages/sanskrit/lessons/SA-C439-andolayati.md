## You'll want to know: आन्दोलयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C439-PAVANA439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आन्दोलयति** — *āndolayati* — "sets swaying; swings".

> पवनः शाखाम् आन्दोलयति।
>
> *pavanaḥ śākhām āndolayati.*
>
> The breeze sets the branch swaying.

Past (it …): पवनः शाखाम् आन्दोलितवान्, *pavanaḥ śākhām āndolitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आन्दोलयति** (*āndolayati*) is built on *āndola*, "swinging, a swing", for which MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain; MW gives *āndolayati* as to swing or agitate, with the participle *āndolita*, "shaken, swung" (MW).
