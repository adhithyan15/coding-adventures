# উ (u) — the letter at the start of উচিত

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C269-UCHIT-01, BN-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-DDA-01, BN-SCRIPT-TTA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say **উচিত**. Then read **তু** (*tu*): the sign **ু** under **ত** is the vowel of this lesson.

## Script you'll notice: উ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-C269-U-01]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-DDA-01, BN-SCRIPT-TTA-01, BN-LEX-C269-UCHIT-01] -->

**উ** — *u*. After a consonant you write it as the sign **ু**: **তুমি**. At the start of a word, with nothing to ride on, it is the letter **উ**: **উচিত**. **আ** and **া** are the same pair.

In the print of this book, **উ** is **ড** with the raised hook of **ট** on top. Below the headline it has **ড**'s body: a slanting left arm that is the left side of a round bowl, a short stem hanging from the headline in the middle, and a curve joining that stem to the bowl's right side, which ends in a point. Above the headline it has **ট**'s hook: a curve standing on the headline, its free end a tip at the upper left.

## Writing: উ — copy the printed shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C269-U-01] -->

Put your pen beside **উ** and copy the shape you can see, slowly and large. Check the copy against the print: the hook above the headline, the left arm and bowl, the short middle stem and its curve, the point on the right.

> This book does not yet tell you where to start উ or which way the pen goes. That is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying the printed shape needs no such source, and it is how the shape becomes yours in the meantime.
