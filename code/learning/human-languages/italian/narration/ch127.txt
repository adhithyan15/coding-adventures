Italian, chapter 127: Serious, Right, Wrong, Ready, Safe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
serio — serious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for bad; nasty, then the Italian for kind.

You'll want to know: serio.
serio — "serious".

The word, taken apart.
serio from Latin serius, "earnest, grave" — a learned borrowing, taken from written Latin. (EtymDB 2.1 records the Latin word.)

Lesson 3 of 5.
sbagliato — wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for serious, then the Italian for right.

You'll want to know: sbagliato.
sbagliato — "wrong".

The word, taken apart.
sbagliato is the past participle of the verb for "to make a mistake", which EtymDB 2.1 takes apart as s- plus an old verb bagliare; where bagliare came from is uncertain.
