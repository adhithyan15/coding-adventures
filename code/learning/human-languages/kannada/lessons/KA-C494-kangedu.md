# ಕಂಗೆಡು (kaṅgeḍu) — to lose heart; to be dismayed

## You'll want to know: ಕಂಗೆಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C494-BERAGU494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಂಗೆಡು** — *kaṅgeḍu* — "to lose heart; to be dismayed".

> ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಫೇಲಾದರೂ ಅವನು ಕಂಗೆಡಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *parīkṣeyalli phēlādarū avanu kaṅgeḍalilla.*
>
> Though he failed the exam, he did not lose heart.

Past (I …): ಕಂಗೆಟ್ಟೆ, *kaṅgeṭṭe*.

Often in the negative, as praise: ಕಂಗೆಡಲಿಲ್ಲ, did not lose heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಂಗೆಡು** (*kaṅgeḍu*) is a native Kannada verb, "to be dismayed, to lose heart"; its origin is not settled.
