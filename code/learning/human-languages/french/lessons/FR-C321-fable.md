## You'll want to know: la fable
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C321-CONTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la fable** — "the fable".

> Les enfants apprennent des fables à l'école.
>
> *Children learn fables at school.*

French children learn fables by heart, with animals that talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fable* comes from Old French *fable*, a learned borrowing from Latin *fābula* "a story" (EtymDB), built on *fārī* "to speak".
