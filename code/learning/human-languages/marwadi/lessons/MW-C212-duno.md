## You'll want to know: दूणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C212-AMOUNT212-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दूणो** — *dūṇo* — "double; twice as much".

> आज भाव दूणो है।
>
> *āj bhāv dūṇo hai.*
>
> Today the price is double.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूणो** (*dūṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*duguṇa*, "double". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *dūnā*, "double" (CDIAL 6390).
