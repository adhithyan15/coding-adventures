## Script — the left piece of 很, then a dot over three bars
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WANG-TOWARD-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 往

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, three strokes**, as in 很: two short strokes falling to the left, one under the other, then a vertical down from the second.

**The right piece, five strokes.** A dot on top. A bar, then a second bar. A vertical down through them. Then a long bar across the foot.

3 + 5 = eight.
