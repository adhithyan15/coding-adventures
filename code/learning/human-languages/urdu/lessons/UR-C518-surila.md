## You'll want to know: سریلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C518-RIYAZ518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سریلا** — *surīlā* — "melodious; in tune".

> سارا کی آواز سریلی ہے۔
>
> *Sārā kī āvāz surīlī hai.*
>
> Sara has a lovely singing voice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سریلا** (*surīlā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *surīlā*, "melodious", under Sanskrit *sura*, "sound", a variant of *svara* (CDIAL 13498).
