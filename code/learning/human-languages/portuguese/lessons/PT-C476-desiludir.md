## You'll want to know: desiludir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C476-DESILUSAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**desiludir** — "to disappoint, to let down".

> O filme desiludiu-me: esperava mais.
>
> *The film disappointed me: I expected more.*

*Ficar desiludido* — to be disappointed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desiludir* is built from *des-* and *iludir* "to deceive", a learned borrowing from Latin *illūdere* "to mock" (Lewis & Short), from *lūdere* "to play".
