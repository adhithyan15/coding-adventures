## You'll want to know: মরিয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C539-BIGHNO539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মরিয়া** — *môriyā* — "desperate; ready to risk anything".

> বন্যার জল বাড়ছিল, তাই লোকজন মরিয়া হল।
>
> *Bônyār jôl bāṛchhilô, tāi lokjôn môriyā hôlô.*
>
> The flood water was rising, so people grew desperate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মরিয়া** (*môriyā*) is probably built on মরা, "to die" (CDIAL 9871): ready to face death itself.
