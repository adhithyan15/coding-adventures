## You'll want to know: bànfǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C214-HELP214-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bànfǎ* — "a way to deal with it".

> *wǒ méiyǒu bànfǎ* — There's nothing I can do.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bànfǎ* is built from *bàn* 'to handle' and *fǎ* 'a method', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *beanH* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to provide' and the second 'a rule'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
