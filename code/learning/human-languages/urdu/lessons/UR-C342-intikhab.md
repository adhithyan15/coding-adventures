## You'll want to know: انتخاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C342-VOT342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**انتخاب** — *intikhāb* — "an election; a choice".

> اگلے سال انتخاب ہو گا۔
>
> *agle sāl intikhāb ho gā.*
>
> There will be an election next year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتخاب** (*intikhāb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *intikhāb*, "choosing".
