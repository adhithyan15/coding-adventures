## You'll want to know: भुखमरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C306-AKAL306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भुखमरी** — *bhukhmarī* — "starvation".

> अकाल में बहुत लोग भुखमरी से मरे।
>
> *akāl meṁ bahut log bhukhmarī se mare.*
>
> Many people died of starvation in the famine.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भुखमरी** (*bhukhmarī*) joins **भूख**, hunger, from Sanskrit *bubhukṣā* (CDIAL 9286), with **मरना**, to die: dying of hunger. The vowel of **भूख** shortens inside the word.
