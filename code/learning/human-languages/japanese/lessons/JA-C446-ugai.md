## You'll want to know: うがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C446-CHILL446-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うがい** — *ugai* — "gargling".

> **うちに かえったら、うがいを して ください。** — *uchi ni kaettara, ugai o shite kudasai* — "When you get home, please gargle."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うがい** (*ugai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; JMdict writes it in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
