# ആഹ്ലാദം (āhlādaṁ) — delight; glee

## You'll want to know: ആഹ്ലാദം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C517-VIKARAM517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ആഹ്ലാദം** — *āhlādaṁ* — "delight; glee".

> പരീക്ഷയിൽ ജയിച്ചപ്പോൾ കുട്ടികൾ ആഹ്ലാദത്തോടെ ചാടി.
>
> *parīkṣayil jayiccappōḷ kuṭṭikaḷ āhlādattōṭe cāṭi.*
>
> When they passed the exam, the children jumped for joy.

With -ഓടെ it makes a manner phrase: ആഹ്ലാദത്തോടെ, with delight. A -ം noun takes -ത്ത- before an ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആഹ്ലാദം** (*āhlādaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āhlāda*, "refreshment, joy, delight" (Monier-Williams). Like many Sanskrit nouns in -a, it ends in -ം in Malayalam.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
