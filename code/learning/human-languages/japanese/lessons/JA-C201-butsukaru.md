## You'll want to know: ぶつかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C201-ROAD201-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつかる** — *butsukaru* — "to bump into; to collide with".

> **じてんしゃが くるまに ぶつかりました。** — *jitensha ga kuruma ni butsukarimashita* — "A bicycle bumped into a car."

Polite **ぶつかります**, *butsukarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつかる** (*butsukaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed and usually written in kana (JMdict); its older origin is not settled.
