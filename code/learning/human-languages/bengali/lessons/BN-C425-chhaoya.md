## You'll want to know: ছাওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C425-MEGH425-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ছাওয়া** — *chhāoyā* — "to spread over; to cover over (of clouds); to thatch".

> দুপুরে আকাশ মেঘে ছেয়ে গেল।
>
> *dupure ākāsh meghe chheye gelô.*
>
> At midday clouds spread across the sky.

Past (he/she …): সেই আকাশ মেঘে ছেয়ে গেল, *sei ākāsh meghe chheye gelô*.

Mostly in the -এ form with যাওয়া: ছেয়ে যাওয়া, to be covered all over. Also to thatch a roof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছাওয়া** (*chhāoyā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *chāuyā*, "to cover, to thatch", under Sanskrit *chādáyati*, "covers, veils" (CDIAL 5018), the root of ছই, a boat's hood, as well.
