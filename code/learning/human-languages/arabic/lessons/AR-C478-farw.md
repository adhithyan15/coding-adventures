## You'll want to know: فرو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C478-QATI478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فرو** — *farw* — "fur".

> للثعلب فرو جميل.
>
> *li-th-thaʿlab farw jamīl.*
>
> The fox has beautiful fur.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرو** (*farw*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *f-r-w*, "fur, a fur coat".
