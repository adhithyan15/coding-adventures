## You'll want to know: el forastero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C684-CIUDADANO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el forastero** — "the outsider, the stranger (from out of town)".

> En el pueblo todos saben quién es forastero.
>
> *In the village everyone knows who is from out of town.*

As an adjective: *gente forastera*. It is not quite *extranjero*, a foreigner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*forastero* was borrowed from Catalan *foraster* (EtymDB), built on *fora* "outside", from Latin *forās* "out of doors".
