## You'll want to know: धमकाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C360-APATTI360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धमकाना** — *dhamkānā* — "to threaten".

> दुकानदार ने चोर को धमकाया।
>
> *dukāndār ne cor ko dhamkāyā.*
>
> The shopkeeper threatened the thief.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे धमकाया, *usne mujhe dhamkāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धमकाना** (*dhamkānā*) is inherited from *\*dhammakka*, "a threat", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6736).
