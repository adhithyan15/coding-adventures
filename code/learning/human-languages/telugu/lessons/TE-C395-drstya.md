# దృష్ట్యా (dṛṣṭyā) — in view of; considering

## You'll want to know: దృష్ట్యా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C395-KANUKA395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**దృష్ట్యా** — *dṛṣṭyā* — "in view of; considering".

> వర్షం దృష్ట్యా పాఠశాలకు సెలవు ఇచ్చారు.
>
> *varṣaṁ dṛṣṭyā pāṭhaśālaku selavu iccāru.*
>
> In view of the rain, they gave the school a holiday.

Formal; it follows a noun: వర్షం దృష్ట్యా, in view of the rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దృష్ట్యా** (*dṛṣṭyā*) is borrowed whole from Sanskrit *dṛṣṭyā*, "by the sight (of)", the instrumental case of *dṛṣṭi*, "seeing, sight" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
