## You'll want to know: તીર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C550-NISHAN550-01]; assesses=[] -->

**તીર** — *tīr* — "an arrow".

> જો પવન નહીં હોય તો તીર સીધું જશે.
>
> *jo pavan nahī̃ hoy to tīr sīdhũ jaśe.*
>
> If there's no wind, the arrow will fly straight.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તીર** (*tīr*) is usually traced to Persian *tīr*, "an arrow".
