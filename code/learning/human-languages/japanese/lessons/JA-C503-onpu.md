## You'll want to know: おんぷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C503-SONG503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おんぷ** — *onpu* — "a musical note".

> **この うたは おんぷが おおいです。** — *kono uta wa onpu ga ōi desu* — "This song has a lot of notes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おんぷ** (*onpu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *on*, "sound" (Middle Chinese *'im*) and *pu*, "token" (*bju*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
