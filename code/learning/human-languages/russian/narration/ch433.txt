Russian, chapter 433: Noisy neighbours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
шум (shum) — noise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to flow, then the Russian for a flood.

You'll want to know: шум.
шум (shum) — "noise".
Из-за шума на улице я плохо спал. Iz-za shúma na úlitse ya plókho spal. Because of the noise in the street I slept badly.
Masculine. Шуметь — to make noise.

The word, taken apart.
шум (shum) is inherited from Proto-Slavic šumŭ (EtymDB), the root of шумный 'noisy'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
мешать (meshát) — to disturb, to bother; to stir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a flood, then the Russian for noise.

You'll want to know: мешать.
мешать (meshát) — "to disturb, to bother; to stir".
Музыка соседей мешает нам спать. Múzyka sosédey mesháyet nam spat. The neighbours' music keeps us from sleeping.
Imperfective; perfective помешать. Takes the dative: мешать (meshát) соседям. Also to stir: мешать (meshát) суп.

The word, taken apart.
мешать (meshát) is usually traced to an old Slavic verb meaning 'to mix', which fits мешать (meshát) суп 'to stir soup'; to disturb is to mix things up for someone. EtymDB lists the word without a source.

You'll want to know: таракан.
таракан (tarakán) — "a cockroach".
На кухне мы увидели таракана. Na kúkhne my uvídeli tarakána. We saw a cockroach in the kitchen.
Masculine. Colloquial: тараканы в голове — odd ideas.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of таракан (tarakán) is not settled; it is often traced to a Turkic word. EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
неполадка (nepoládka) — a fault, a problem (with equipment).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cockroach, then the Russian for a safe.

You'll want to know: неполадка.
неполадка (nepoládka) — "a fault, a problem (with equipment)".
В номере была неполадка с душем. V nómere bylá nepoládka s dúshem. There was a problem with the shower in the room.
Feminine; often plural: неполадки.

The word, taken apart.
неполадка (nepoládka) is built from не- 'not' and поладить 'to get on', on лад 'harmony, good order', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic ladŭ (EtymDB).
