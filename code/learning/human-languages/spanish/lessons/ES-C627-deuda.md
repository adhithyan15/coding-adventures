## You'll want to know: la deuda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C627-GASTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la deuda** — "the debt".

> Tengo una deuda con el banco.
>
> *I have a debt with the bank.*

*Estar en deuda con* — to be in debt to, also in a moral sense.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*deuda* is inherited from Latin *debita* 'things owed', through Old Spanish *debda*, from *debere* 'to owe' (EtymDB).
