## You'll want to know: آستین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C500-SILAI500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آستین** — *āstīn* — "a sleeve".

> اس قمیض کی آستین لمبی ہے۔
>
> *is qamīz kī āstīn lambī hai.*
>
> The sleeves of this shirt are long.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آستین** (*āstīn*) is usually traced to Persian *āstīn*, "a sleeve".
