Italian, chapter 425: In aeroporto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il terminal — the terminal (at an airport).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the lifebelt, then the Italian for the nausea.

You'll want to know: il terminal.
il terminal — "the terminal (at an airport)".
Il nostro volo parte dal terminal due. Our flight leaves from terminal two.
Taken from English; masculine, unchanged in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
terminal was borrowed from English terminal, which goes back to Latin terminālis 'of the end', from terminus 'boundary, end' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: lo scalo.
lo scalo — "the stopover; the port of call".
Il volo fa uno scalo di due ore. The flight has a two-hour stopover.
Un volo senza scalo — a direct flight; fare scalo — to stop over.

The word, taken apart.
scalo is built on scala 'ladder, steps', which is inherited from Latin scāla (EtymDB): first the steps where ships landed goods.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la navetta — the shuttle (bus).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the stopover, then the Italian for the boarding.

You'll want to know: la navetta.
la navetta — "the shuttle (bus)".
Una navetta va dall'aeroporto all'albergo. A shuttle goes from the airport to the hotel.
Literally a little ship; also the shuttle of a loom.

The word, taken apart.
navetta is a little nave 'ship', which is inherited from Latin nāvis (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
il fuso orario — the time zone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the boarding, then the Italian for the shuttle.

You'll want to know: il fuso orario.
il fuso orario — "the time zone".
Tra qui e casa ci sono sei ore di fuso orario. There are six hours of time difference between here and home.
Plural i fusi orari. A fuso is a spindle: on a globe the zones look like spindles.

The word, taken apart.
fuso orario is built from fuso 'spindle', inherited from Latin fūsus (EtymDB), and orario 'of the hours', from Latin hōrārius, built on hōra 'hour'.
