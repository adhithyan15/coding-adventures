## You'll want to know: виновный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C294-NAKAZANIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**виновный** (*vinóvnyy*) — "guilty".

> Суд признал его виновным.
>
> *Sud priznál yevó vinóvnym.*
>
> The court found him guilty.

*Виновный в* + prepositional. In speech, of a small fault: *я виноват* — it is my fault.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*виновный* is built on *вина* "guilt", an inherited Slavic word, with the adjective ending *-ный*; EtymDB lists no parent.
