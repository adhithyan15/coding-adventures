## You'll want to know: yìwù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C362-MUST362-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yìwù* — "duty; obligation".

> *wǒ juéde zhè shì dàjiā de yìwù* — I think it's everyone's duty.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìwù* is built from *yì* and *wù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjeH* and *mjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'benevolence and right conduct' and the second 'affairs'.
