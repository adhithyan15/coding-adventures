## You'll want to know: fēnxī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C351-SENSE351-01]; assesses=[] -->

*fēnxī* — "to analyse".

> *wǒmen yīnggāi xiān fēnxī wèntí* — We should analyse the problem first.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēnxī* is built from *fēn* and *xī*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjun* and *sek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to share out' and the second 'to split'.
