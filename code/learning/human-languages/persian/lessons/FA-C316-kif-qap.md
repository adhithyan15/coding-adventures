## You'll want to know: کیف قاپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C316-DOZD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کیف قاپ** (*kif qâp*) — "a bag snatcher".

> کیف قاپ کیف زن را گرفت و دوید.
>
> *Kif qâp kif-e zan râ gereft va david.*
>
> The bag snatcher grabbed the woman's bag and ran.

*kif* "bag" + *qâp*, from *qâpidan* "to snatch".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kif qâp* joins *kif* "bag", whose origin is not settled, and *qâp-*, the stem of *qâpidan* "to snatch", which is usually traced to Turkic *qap-* "to grab". EtymDB lists no entry.
