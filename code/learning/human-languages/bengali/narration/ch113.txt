Lesson 1 of 5.
কুয়ো (kuyo) — a well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a veranda, then the Bengali for a garden.

You'll want to know: কুয়ো.
কুয়ো — kuyo — "a well".

The word, taken apart.
কুয়ো (kuyo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kūpa, "hole, pit, well" (CDIAL 3400). CDIAL writes the Bengali form kuyā.

You'll want to know: ধুলো.
ধুলো — dhulo — "dust".

The word, taken apart.
ধুলো (dhulo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhūḍi, "dust, powder", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6835). Sanskrit has dhūli; CDIAL writes the Bengali forms dhul and dhulā.

বজ্র — bôjrô — "thunder".

The word, taken apart.
বজ্র (bôjrô) is Sanskrit vajra, "thunderbolt", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 11204). The word inherited from it is bāj, which CDIAL lists.

রংধনু — rôngdhônu — "a rainbow".

The word, taken apart.
রংধনু (rôngdhônu) is rông, "colour", which matches Sanskrit raṅga and Persian rang (CDIAL 10560), + dhônu, "bow", Sanskrit dhanus: a bow of colours.
