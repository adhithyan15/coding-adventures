## You'll want to know: chēhuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C296-CRASH296-02]; assesses=[] -->

*chēhuò* — "traffic accident; car crash".

> *tā zài chēhuò lǐ shòushāng le* — He was hurt in a car crash.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chēhuò* is built from 车 'a vehicle' and *huò* 'a disaster', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhae* and *hwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'harm'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
