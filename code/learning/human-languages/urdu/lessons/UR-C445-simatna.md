## You'll want to know: سمٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C445-FEAR445-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سمٹنا** — *simaṭnā* — "to huddle; to shrink into oneself".

> سردی میں بچے کمبل میں سمٹ گئے۔
>
> *sardī meṅ bachche kambal meṅ simaṭ gae.*
>
> In the cold the children huddled under the blanket.

Past (she …): وہ کونے میں سمٹ گئی, *voh kone meṅ simaṭ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سمٹنا** (*simaṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *simaṭnā*, "to shrink, to be gathered in", under Sanskrit *saṃvēṣṭayati*, "wraps up" (CDIAL 13026).
