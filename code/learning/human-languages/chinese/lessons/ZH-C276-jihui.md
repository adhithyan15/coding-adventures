## You'll want to know: 机会
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C276-NEWJOB276-03]; assesses=[] -->

**机会** — *jīhuì* — "chance; opportunity".

> **这是一个好机会** *zhè shì yí ge hǎo jīhuì* — This is a good opportunity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**机会** (*jīhuì*) is built from 机 and 会 'to meet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kj+j* and *hwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a meeting' and 'a trigger', and the second 'to come together'.
