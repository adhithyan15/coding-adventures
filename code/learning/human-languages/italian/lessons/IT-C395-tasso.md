## You'll want to know: il tasso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C395-RISPARMIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il tasso** — "the rate".

> Il tasso di interesse è del due per cento.
>
> *The interest rate is two per cent.*

*Il tasso di cambio* — the exchange rate. A different *tasso* is the badger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tasso* "rate" is built on *tassare* "to assess", a learned borrowing from Latin *taxāre* "to value, to assess" (Lewis & Short). The animal *tasso* is a different word.
