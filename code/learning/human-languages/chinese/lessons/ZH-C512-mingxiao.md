## You'll want to know: 名校
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C512-DREAMS512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**名校** — *míngxiào* — "top school; famous university".

> **我想考上名校** *wǒ xiǎng kǎoshàng míngxiào* — I want to get into a top school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**名校** (*míngxiào*) is built from 名 'a name' and 校 'a school', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjieng* and *haewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the title of an officer; the sense 'a school' is not given there.
