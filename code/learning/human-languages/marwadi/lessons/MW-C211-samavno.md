## You'll want to know: समावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C211-SIZE211-05]; assesses=[] -->

**समावणो** — *samāvṇo* — "to fit (into)".

> म्हारो पग जूती मांय कोनी समावै।
>
> *mhāro pag jūtī mā̃y konī samāvai.*
>
> My foot won't fit in the shoe.

Past (it …): वो सामान पेटी मांय समायो, *vo sāmān peṭī mā̃y samāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समावणो** (*samāvṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sammāti*, "is contained in"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *samāi*, "fits into, is contained in" (CDIAL 12975).
