## You'll want to know: 游船
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C431-OUTING431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**游船** — *yóuchuán* — "pleasure boat".

> *wǒmen zuò yóuchuán qù hú duìmiàn* — We're taking a pleasure boat across the lake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**游船** (*yóuchuán*) is built from 游 'to roam' and 船 'a boat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yuw* and *zywen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
