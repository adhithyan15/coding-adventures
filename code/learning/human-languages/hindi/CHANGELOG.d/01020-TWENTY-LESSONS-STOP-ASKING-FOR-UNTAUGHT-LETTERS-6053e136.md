## Twenty lessons stop asking for untaught letters

- Script closure (a lesson may ask the reader to decode only letters an
  earlier lesson taught, its own romanized headword aside) found twenty Hindi
  violations; there are now none.
- Words whose letters are not yet taught are given in romanization, not in
  Devanagari. These include the Sanskrit and Persian etymons (*namaḥ*,
  *√kṛ*, *kīdṛśa*, *mayā*, *biḍāla*), example sentences that run ahead of the
  letters (*maiṁ ṭhīk hūṁ*, *maiṁ kām kartā hūṁ*, *maiṁ Dillī meṁ rahtā hūṁ*),
  the *Vikram Samvat* month names, and single words such as *bhāī*,
  *prabhāt* and *śubh rātri*.
- The chapter-1 cross-links name sibling lessons in romanization
  (*namaskār*, *dhanyavād*, *shukriyā*, *namaste*).
- `HI-C02-practice` gives its dialogue in romanization until the letters
  arrive.
- The retroflex series is written *ṭ-ṭh-ḍ*, and `HI-C08-kripaya`'s request
  endings are written *-iye* / *-iyegā*.
