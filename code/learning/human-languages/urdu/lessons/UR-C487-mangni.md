## You'll want to know: منگنی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C487-WEDDING487-01]; assesses=[] -->

**منگنی** — *mangnī* — "an engagement to marry".

> میرے بھائی کی منگنی ہو گئی۔
>
> *mere bhāī kī mangnī ho gaī.*
>
> My brother has got engaged.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منگنی** (*mangnī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *maṅgnī*, "betrothal", under Sanskrit *mārgaṇa*, "asking" (CDIAL 10073): the asking for a bride.
