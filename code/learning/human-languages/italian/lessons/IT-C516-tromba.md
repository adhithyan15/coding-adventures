## You'll want to know: la tromba
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C516-STRUMENTI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la tromba** — "trumpet".

> Il vicino suona la tromba la domenica mattina.
>
> *The neighbour plays the trumpet on Sunday mornings.*

Also *la tromba delle scale* — the stairwell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tromba* is usually traced to a Germanic word *trumba* "trumpet", which may imitate the sound.
