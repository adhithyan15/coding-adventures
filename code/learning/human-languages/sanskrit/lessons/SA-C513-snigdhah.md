## You'll want to know: स्निग्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C513-MATHNATI513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्निग्धः** — *snigdhaḥ* — "oily; rich".

> घृतेन ओदनः स्निग्धः भवति।
>
> *ghṛtena odanaḥ snigdhaḥ bhavati.*
>
> Ghee makes the rice rich.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्निग्धः** (*snigdhaḥ*) comes from the root √snih, "to be sticky, to be fond of", as its past participle; MW gives *snigdha* as sticky, unctuous, smooth (MW).
