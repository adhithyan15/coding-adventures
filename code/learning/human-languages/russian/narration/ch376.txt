Lesson 1 of 6.
исключение (isklyuchéniye) — an exception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for necessity, then the Russian for enough.

You'll want to know: исключение.
исключение (isklyuchéniye) — "an exception".
Все пришли, за исключением одного студента. Vse prishlí, za isklyuchéniyem odnogó studénta. Everyone came except for one student.
Neuter. За исключением + genitive — except for. Без исключения — without exception.

The word, taken apart.
исключение (isklyuchéniye) is built on исключить "to exclude", from из- "out" and ключ "a key", inherited from Proto-Slavic ključĭ: to lock someone out.

Lesson 2 of 6.
лишь бы (lish by) — as long as, if only.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: лишь бы.
лишь бы (lish by) — "as long as, if only".
Я согласен на любую работу, лишь бы только она была интересной. Ya soglásen na lyubúyu rabótu, lish by tólko oná bylá interésnoy. I will take any job, as long as it is interesting.
Лишь бы + past tense or infinitive names the one thing that matters: лишь бы (lish by) успеть.

The word, taken apart.
лишь бы (lish by) is built from лишь "only", which is usually related to лишний "extra, too many", and бы, the particle of the conditional.

разве что (rázve chto) — "except perhaps, unless maybe".
Я ничего не хочу, разве что чаю. Ya nichegó ne khochú, rázve chto cháyu. I do not want anything, except perhaps some tea.
It names a small exception: никто не знает, разве что (rázve chto) мама.

The word, taken apart.
разве что (rázve chto) is built from the particle разве and что "what". The origin of разве is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
хотя бы (khotyá by) — at least.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for as long as, then the Russian for except perhaps.

You'll want to know: хотя бы.
хотя бы (khotyá by) — "at least".
Он мог бы хотя бы позвонить. On mog by khotyá by pozvonít. He could at least have called.
It sets a minimum: хотя бы (khotyá by) немного — at least a little. Not the same as хотя, although.

The word, taken apart.
хотя бы (khotyá by) is built from хотя, the old gerund of хотеть "to want", and бы, the particle of the conditional: wanting it to be so, at the very least.

Lesson 5 of 6.
на всякий случай (na vsyákiy slúchay) — in case, to be on the safe side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for except perhaps, then the Russian for at least.

You'll want to know: на всякий случай.
на всякий случай (na vsyákiy slúchay) — "in case, to be on the safe side".
Я возьму зонтик на всякий случай. Ya vozmú zóntik na vsyákiy slúchay. I will take an umbrella, to be on the safe side.
A fixed phrase; всякий means any.

The word, taken apart.
на всякий случай (na vsyákiy slúchay) is "for any case": всякий "any" and случай "a case", inherited from Proto-Slavic sŭlučajĭ (EtymDB).
