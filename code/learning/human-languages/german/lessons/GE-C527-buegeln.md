## You'll want to know: bügeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C527-WAESCHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bügeln** — "to iron".

> Am Sonntagabend bügelt sie ihre Blusen für die Woche.
>
> *On Sunday evening she irons her blouses for the week.*

Regular: *hat gebügelt*. In Switzerland also *glätten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bügeln* is built on *Bügel* "a curved handle, a hanger" (EtymDB); the link is usually explained by the bent handle of early irons.
