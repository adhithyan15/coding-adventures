French, chapter 414: Le train de nuit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la couchette — the berth, the couchette.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the carriage, then the French for to stop at.

You'll want to know: la couchette.
la couchette — "the berth, the couchette".
Dans le train de nuit, j'ai dormi sur la couchette du haut. On the night train I slept on the top berth.
A small couche 'bed'.

The word, taken apart.
couchette is a small couche 'bed', from coucher 'to lay down', which is inherited from Latin collocāre 'to put in place', built on locus 'place' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

le chariot — "the trolley (for luggage or shopping)".
Il pousse un chariot plein de valises. He is pushing a trolley full of suitcases.
In a supermarket, le chariot is the shopping trolley.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la navette — the shuttle (bus or train).

You'll want to know: la navette.
la navette — "the shuttle (bus or train)".
Une navette relie le centre-ville à l'aéroport. A shuttle links the town centre with the airport.
Faire la navette — to go back and forth.

The word, taken apart.
navette is a small nef 'ship', from Latin nāvis 'ship' (Lewis & Short): first the boat-shaped shuttle of a loom that runs back and forth.
