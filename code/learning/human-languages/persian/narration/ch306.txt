Persian, chapter 306: Little by little and meanwhile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
به تدریج (be tadrij) — gradually, little by little.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the time, then the Persian for round the clock.

You'll want to know: به تدریج.
به تدریج (be tadrij) — "gradually, little by little".
هوا به تدریج سرد شد. Havâ be tadrij sard shod. The weather gradually turned cold.
Formal; in speech kam-kam. The adjective is tadriji "gradual".

The word, taken apart.
tadrij is usually traced to Arabic tadrīj, "a gradual progression"; EtymDB lists no entry. be "to, by" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
در همین حال (dar hamin hâl) — meanwhile, at the same time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for round the clock, then the Persian for gradually.

You'll want to know: در همین حال.
در همین حال (dar hamin hâl) — "meanwhile, at the same time".
من ظرف میشستم و در همین حال برادرم تلویزیون تماشا میکرد. Man zarf mishostam va dar hamin hâl barâdaram televiziyon tamâshâ mikard. I was washing the dishes, and meanwhile my brother was watching television.
It joins two background actions, often both in the past continuous. Literally "in this very state".

The word, taken apart.
hâl "state" is borrowed from Arabic ḥāl (EtymDB); dar "in" and hamin "this very" are inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تا اینکه (tâ inke) — until finally, until.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for gradually, then the Persian for meanwhile.

You'll want to know: تا اینکه.
تا اینکه (tâ inke) — "until finally, until".
منتظر ماندیم تا اینکه اتوبوس آمد. Montazer mândim tâ inke otobus âmad. We waited until the bus finally came.
In a story it marks the turn:... tâ inke yek ruz... "... until one day...".

The word, taken apart.
tâ "until" is inherited: EtymDB traces it through Middle Persian to Old Persian yātā, "until, so that". inke is in "this" + ke "that".

Lesson 4 of 5.
طی (tey) — during, in the space of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for meanwhile, then the Persian for until finally.

You'll want to know: طی.
طی (tey) — "during, in the space of".
طی (tey) سفر چند بار باران آمد. Tey-e safar chand bâr bârân âmad. It rained several times during the trip.
Formal, with the ezafe: tey-e do sâl "over two years".

The word, taken apart.
tey is borrowed from Arabic ṭayy (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a folding up, a covering of distance": time folded up as it passes.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مجددا (mojaddadan) — again, anew (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for until finally, then the Persian for during.

You'll want to know: مجددا.
مجددا (mojaddadan) — "again, anew (formal)".
او مجددا به آن شرکت زنگ زد. U mojaddadan be ân sherkat zang zad. She phoned that company again.
Formal and official; in speech dobâre.

The word, taken apart.
mojaddadan is the Arabic adverb of mujaddad, "renewed" (EtymDB lists Arabic mujaddad), made with the ending -an.
