## You'll want to know: tapezieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C529-RENOVIEREN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tapezieren** — "to wallpaper".

> Am Wochenende tapeziert meine Schwester das Kinderzimmer.
>
> *At the weekend my sister is wallpapering the children's room.*

Regular, Perfekt without *ge-*: *hat tapeziert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tapezieren* is built on *Tapete* "wallpaper", from Medieval Latin *tapeta* "wall carpet" (EtymDB).
