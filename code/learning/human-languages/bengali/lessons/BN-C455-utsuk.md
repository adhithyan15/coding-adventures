## You'll want to know: উৎসুক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C455-DHOIRJO455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**উৎসুক** — *utsuk* — "eager; curious to know".

> ফলাফল জানার জন্য ছাত্ররা উৎসুক হয়ে ছিল।
>
> *phôlāphôl jānār jônyô chhātrôrā utsuk hôye chhilô.*
>
> The students were eager to know the results.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উৎসুক** (*utsuk*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *utsuka*, "restless, uneasy" and "anxiously eager" (Monier-Williams).
