## You'll want to know: la collezione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C505-COLLEZIONI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la collezione** — "collection".

> Ha una collezione di dischi in vinile.
>
> *He has a collection of vinyl records.*

*Fare collezione di* — to collect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*collezione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *collēctiō* "a gathering" (EtymDB), from *colligere* "to gather", built on *legere* "to gather".
