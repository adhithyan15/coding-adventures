## You'll want to know: हिस्सा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C430-MOBADLA430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हिस्सा** — *hissā* — "a share; a portion".

> तिघा भावांनी शेताचे तीन हिस्से केले.
>
> *tighā bhāvānnī śetāce tīn hisse kele.*
>
> The three brothers split the field into three shares.

Masculine. Plural: हिस्से, *hisse*.

The plural is हिस्से.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिस्सा** (*hissā*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *ḥiṣṣa*, "a portion, a share".
