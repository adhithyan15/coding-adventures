## You'll want to know: رحالة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C438-SIYAHA438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رحالة** — *raḥḥāla* — "a great traveller; an explorer".

> كتب الرحالة عن رحلته في الصحراء.
>
> *kataba ar-raḥḥāla ʿan riḥlatihi fī aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ.*
>
> The explorer wrote about his journey in the desert.

The ending *-a* here marks a man who does a thing a great deal, not a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رحالة** (*raḥḥāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ḥ-l*, "to set out on a journey" (Lane). It shares its root with *riḥla*, "a journey".
