# મૃત્યુ (mṛtyu) — death

## You'll want to know: મૃત્યુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C298-MRUTYU298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મૃત્યુ** — *mṛtyu* — "death".

> અકસ્માતમાં એક માણસનું મૃત્યુ થયું.
>
> *akasmātmā̃ ek māṇasnũ mṛtyu thayũ.*
>
> One man died in the accident.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મૃત્યુ** (*mṛtyu*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mṛtyu*, "death, dying" (Monier-Williams). It is the word of news reports: **તેનું મૃત્યુ થયું**, he died.
