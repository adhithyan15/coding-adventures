Punjabi, chapter 310: A Slip in the Rain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਫਿਸਲਣਾ (phisalṇā) — to slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a din, then the Punjabi for to die.

You'll want to know: ਫਿਸਲਣਾ.
ਫਿਸਲਣਾ — phisalṇā — "to slip".
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਮਨ ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਫਿਸਲਿਆ। kall mīṁh vich Aman saṛak utte phisliā. Yesterday Aman slipped on the road in the rain.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪੌੜੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਫਿਸਲਿਆ, maiṁ pauṛī utte phisliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਫਿਸਲਣਾ (phisalṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed phiss, "slip"; CDIAL lists Punjabi phisalṇā, "to slip", under it (CDIAL 9080).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਠੋਕਰ (ṭhokar) — a stumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to die, then the Punjabi for to slip.

You'll want to know: ਠੋਕਰ.
ਠੋਕਰ — ṭhokar — "a stumble".
ਹਨੇਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਪੱਥਰ ਨਾਲ ਠੋਕਰ ਖਾਧੀ। hanere vich maiṁ ikk patthar nāl ṭhokar khādhī. In the dark I stumbled over a stone.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਠੋਕਰ (ṭhokar) is inherited from a reconstructed ṭhokk, "knock"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭhokar, "blow", under it (CDIAL 5513). Its use for a stumble is not given there.
