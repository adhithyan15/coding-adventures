Marathi, chapter 386: Stopping Trouble.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रतिबंध करणे (pratibandh karṇe) — to prevent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be averted, then the Marathi for to confront.

You'll want to know: प्रतिबंध करणे.
प्रतिबंध करणे — pratibandh karṇe — "to prevent".
स्वच्छतेमुळे आजारांना प्रतिबंध करता येतो. svacchatemuḷe ājārānnā pratibandh kartā yeto. Cleanliness can prevent illness.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने प्रतिबंध केला, tyāne pratibandh kelā.
What is prevented takes -ला; प्रतिबंधक, preventive.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिबंध करणे (pratibandh karṇe) joins the tatsama प्रतिबंध, Sanskrit pratibandha, "connexion, hindrance" (CDIAL 8580), to करणे, "to do".

The word, taken apart.
रोखणे (rokhṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi rokhṇẽ, "to hinder", under rōkk-, "stop" (CDIAL 10827).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अडवणे (aḍavṇe) — to block; to stop (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to prevent, then the Marathi for to restrain.

You'll want to know: अडवणे.
अडवणे — aḍavṇe — "to block; to stop (someone)".
रस्त्यात एका माणसाने मला अडवलं. rastyāt ekā māṇsāne malā aḍavlaṁ. A man stopped me on the road.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अडवलं, tyāne aḍavlaṁ.
The one stopped takes -ला; अडणे, to get stuck.

The word, taken apart.
अडवणे (aḍavṇe) is built on अडणे, "to be stopped", as its causative; CDIAL lists it as Marathi aḍṇẽ under aḍḍ-, "obstruct, stop" (CDIAL 188).

Lesson 4 of 5.
दखल घेणे (dakhal gheṇe) — to take notice (of).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to restrain, then the Marathi for to block.

You'll want to know: दखल घेणे.
दखल घेणे — dakhal gheṇe — "to take notice (of)".
शेवटी प्रशासनाने आमच्या तक्रारीची दखल घेतली. śevṭī praśāsnāne āmacyā takrārīcī dakhal ghetlī. In the end the administration took notice of our complaint.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दखल घेतली, tyāne dakhal ghetlī.
What is noticed takes -ची: तक्रारीची दखल घेणे (dakhal gheṇe).

The word, taken apart.
दखल घेणे (dakhal gheṇe) joins दखल, "notice, interference", a loan from Persian usually traced to Arabic dakhl, "entering", to घेणे, "to take".

Lesson 5 of 5.
पळवाट (paḷvāṭ) — a loophole; an escape route.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to block, then the Marathi for to take notice.

You'll want to know: पळवाट.
पळवाट — paḷvāṭ — "a loophole; an escape route".
या नियमात एक पळवाट आहे. yā niymāt ek paḷvāṭ āhe. There is a loophole in this rule.
Feminine.
पळ + वाट, flight + path; पळवाट (paḷvāṭ) काढणे, to find a way out.

The word, taken apart.
पळवाट (paḷvāṭ) is a Marathi compound built from the stem of पळणे, "to run away", inherited from Sanskrit palāyate, "flees" (CDIAL 7955), and वाट, "path", inherited from Sanskrit vartman, "track of a wheel, path" (CDIAL 11366): a path to run by.
