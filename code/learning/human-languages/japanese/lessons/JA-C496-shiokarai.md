## You'll want to know: しおからい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C496-SOFT496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しおからい** — *shiokarai* — "salty".

> **この さかなは しおからいです。** — *kono sakana wa shiokarai desu* — "This fish is salty."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しおからい** (*shiokarai*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *shio*, "salt" and *karai*, "hot, spicy" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
