## You'll want to know: उजास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C229-LIGHT229-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उजास** — *ujās* — "daylight; brightness".

> तड़कै उजास आवै है।
>
> *taṛkai ujās āvai hai.*
>
> At daybreak the light comes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उजास** (*ujās*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ujjhāṣa*, "shining"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *ujās*, "brightness, light", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 1678).
