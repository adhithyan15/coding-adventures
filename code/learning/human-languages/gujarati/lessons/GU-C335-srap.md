## You'll want to know: શ્રાપ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C335-SADHU335-02]; assesses=[] -->

**શ્રાપ** — *śrāp* — "a curse".

> સાધુએ રાજાને શ્રાપ આપ્યો.
>
> *sādhue rājāne śrāp āpyo.*
>
> The holy man put a curse on the king.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શ્રાપ** (*śrāp*) goes back to Sanskrit *śāpa*, "a curse" (CDIAL 12393); the Sanskrit word has no *r*, and how the Gujarati form got one is not settled. A curse is given: **શ્રાપ આપ્યો**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
