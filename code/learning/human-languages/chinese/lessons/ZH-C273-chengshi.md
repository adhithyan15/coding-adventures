## You'll want to know: chéngshí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C273-CHAR273-01]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngshí* — "honest".

> *tā shì yí ge chéngshí de rén* — He's an honest person.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngshí* is built from *chéng* and *shí*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *zyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'trustworthy' and the second 'full' and 'sincere'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
