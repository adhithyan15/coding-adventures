Telugu, chapter 478: Storms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అల్పపీడనం (alpapīḍanaṁ) — a low-pressure area.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a shower of rain, then the Telugu for to get soaked.

You'll want to know: అల్పపీడనం.
అల్పపీడనం — alpapīḍanaṁ — "a low-pressure area".
సముద్రంలో అల్పపీడనం ఏర్పడింది. samudraṁlō alpapīḍanaṁ ērpaḍindi. A low-pressure area has formed over the sea.
The weather news says it every monsoon: అల్ప, low, + పీడనం, pressure. A storm often follows.

The word, taken apart.
అల్పపీడనం (alpapīḍanaṁ) is a modern weather word built from two Sanskrit words: alpa, "small, little", and pīḍana, "pressing, squeezing" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
పెనుగాలి (penugāli) — a gale; a strong wind.

You'll want to know: పెనుగాలి.
పెనుగాలి — penugāli — "a gale; a strong wind".
పెనుగాలికి చాలా చెట్లు కూలాయి. penugāliki cālā ceṭlu kūlāyi. Many trees came down in the gale.
పెను, great, + గాలి, wind.

The word, taken apart.
పెనుగాలి (penugāli) is native Dravidian: పెను, "great, big", which DEDR 4411 lists beside Tamil peru, "great", and గాలి, "wind" (DEDR 1499).

Lesson 3 of 5.
సుడిగాలి (suḍigāli) — a whirlwind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a low-pressure area, then the Telugu for a gale.

You'll want to know: సుడిగాలి.
సుడిగాలి — suḍigāli — "a whirlwind".
సుడిగాలికి ఇంటి పైకప్పు ఎగిరిపోయింది. suḍigāliki iṇṭi paikappu egiripōyindi. In the whirlwind the roof of the house blew off.
సుడి, a whirl, + గాలి, wind. A whirlpool in water is a సుడిగుండం.

The word, taken apart.
సుడిగాలి (suḍigāli) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2698 lists Telugu suḍi, "a whirl, a whirlpool, a whirlwind", here joined to గాలి, "wind" (DEDR 1499).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a gale, then the Telugu for a whirlwind.

You'll want to know: గాలివాన.
గాలివాన — gālivāna — "a rainstorm".
నిన్న రాత్రి పెద్ద గాలివాన వచ్చింది. ninna rātri pedda gālivāna vaccindi. There was a big rainstorm last night.
గాలి, wind, + వాన, rain: wind and rain together.

The word, taken apart.
గాలివాన (gālivāna) is native Dravidian: గాలి, "wind" (DEDR 1499), and వాన, "rain" (DEDR 5381).

Lesson 5 of 5.
బలహీనపడు (balahīnapaḍu) — to lose strength; to weaken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a whirlwind, then the Telugu for a rainstorm.

You'll want to know: బలహీనపడు.
బలహీనపడు — balahīnapaḍu — "to lose strength; to weaken".
తీరం దాటిన తరువాత తుపాను బలహీనపడింది. tīraṁ dāṭina taruvāta tupānu balahīnapaḍindi. After crossing the coast the storm weakened.
Past (I …): బలహీనపడ్డాను, balahīnapaḍḍānu.
బలం, strength, + హీన, lacking, + పడు. Said of storms, of the body and of a currency.

The word, taken apart.
బలహీనపడు (balahīnapaḍu) is a Telugu verb built from Sanskrit bala, "strength", and hīna, "lacking, deprived of" (Monier-Williams), with the native పడు, "to undergo" (DEDR 3853).
