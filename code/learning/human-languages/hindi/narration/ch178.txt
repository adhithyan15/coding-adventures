Hindi, chapter 178: To weave, To hit, To fight, To be afraid, To shout.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बुनना (bunnā) — to weave, to knit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to boil, then the Hindi for to fry.

You'll want to know: बुनना.
बुनना — bunnā — "to weave, to knit".

The word, taken apart.
बुनना (bunnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vunāti, "weaves", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 11773). CDIAL explains the u as taken from other forms of Sanskrit vayati, "weaves".

The word, taken apart.
मारना (mārnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mārayati, "kills" (CDIAL 10066). It is the causing form of the verb "to die": to strike was "to make die".
