# എനിക്ക് ബസ് കിട്ടിയില്ല (enikkŭ basŭ kiṭṭiyilla) — I missed the bus

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to get", "to lose", and "it's late". (*kiṭṭuka*, *naṣṭappeṭuka*, *vaiki*.) Then
the cry when something goes wrong. (*ayyō!*)

## Grammar Lens: it did not come to me
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C344-MISSED-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C183-KRIYA183-04, ML-CONCEPT-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-01, ML-LEX-C121-TRAVEL-03, ML-LEX-C158-THINGS158-02, ML-LEX-C127-CARE-02, ML-LEX-C139-TIME-03, ML-LEX-C306-KRIYA306-03] -->

Malayalam does not "miss" a bus. The bus is not got by you, in the dative:

> **എനിക്ക് ബസ് കിട്ടിയില്ല.**
>
> *enikkŭ basŭ kiṭṭiyilla.* — "I missed the bus."

Or the bus went: **ബസ് പോയി** (*basŭ pōyi*), "the bus has gone". Delays and cancellations use verbs
you know:

> **ട്രെയിൻ ഒരു മണിക്കൂർ വൈകി.**
>
> *ṭreyin oru maṇikkūr vaiki.* — "The train was an hour late."

**എന്റെ ടിക്കറ്റ് റദ്ദാക്കി** (*enṟe ṭikkaṟṟŭ ṟaddākki*), "my ticket was cancelled": no one is named, as in English.

## Grammar Lens: lost and broken, and help
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C344-LOST-REPORT-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C183-KRIYA183-05, ML-LEX-C147-THINGS147-03, ML-LEX-C110-PHONE-01, ML-LEX-C271-KRIYA271-04, ML-LEX-C127-CARE-05, ML-CONCEPT-C34-SAHAAYIKKUKA-01, ML-CONCEPT-C09-KSHAMIKKANAM-01] -->

> **അയ്യോ, എന്റെ ബാഗ് നഷ്ടപ്പെട്ടുപോയി.**
>
> *ayyō, enṟe bāgŭ naṣṭappeṭṭupōyi.* — "Oh no, my bag has got lost."

**പോയി** after a past form says it went and is gone, and you are sorry about it:
**ഫോൺ കേടായിപ്പോയി**, "the phone has broken down".

To ask for help, take the **-ആം** of **വിളിക്കാം**, "I'll call", and add **-ഓ**: "could you …?"

> **ക്ഷമിക്കണം, എന്നെ സഹായിക്കാമോ?**
>
> *kṣamikkaṇaṁ, enne sahāyikkāmō?* — "Excuse me, could you help me?"
