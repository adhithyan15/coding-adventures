## You'll want to know: делиться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C342-OBSUZHDAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**делиться** (*delítsya*) — "to share".

> Она делится с нами своими идеями.
>
> *Oná délitsya s námi svoími idéyami.*
>
> She shares her ideas with us.

Imperfective; present *делюсь, делишься*; perfective *поделиться*. *Делиться чем с кем*: the thing shared goes in the instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**делиться** is the reflexive of *делить* "to divide, to share", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*děliti* "to divide", built on *\*dělŭ* "a part" (EtymDB); EtymDB sets Proto-Germanic *\*dailą* "part, deal" beside it.
