# Your own line — the first writing with no model

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SVO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Everything you have written so far had something to reach for — a
shape, a word, a sound.

[PAUSE 1s] This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed:
only what this book has taught you.

## Writing — controlled composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-ET-01, FR-GRAM-SVO-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **one line** about yourself. Subject, verb, then the rest — the
order you already know.

[PAUSE 2s] One thing to decide deliberately rather than by accident before you
start: **the accents are letters, not decoration.**

[PAUSE 2s] An acute on an **e** is not a flourish on the same letter; it makes a
different sound and, often enough, a different word. A hand that writes French
without accents has not written French untidily — it has written something else
and hoped the reader would guess.

[PAUSE 3s] Write the line. Then read it back and check the accents **before** you
check anything else.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SVO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Are French accents decoration? (**No** — they are part of the
letter.)

[PAUSE 2s] And what did you choose here that the earlier lessons chose for you?
(**The words.**)
