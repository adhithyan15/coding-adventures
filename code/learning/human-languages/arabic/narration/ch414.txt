Arabic, chapter 414: Outcomes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نتج (nataja) — to stem from; to result.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to recur, then the Arabic for to endure.

You'll want to know: نتج.
نتج — nataja — "to stem from; to result".
نتج الحريق عن خطأ صغير. nataja al-ḥarīq ʿan khaṭaʾ ṣaghīr. The fire resulted from a small mistake.
Present: ينتج, yantij.
It takes عن: نتج (nataja) عن …. The noun نتيجة is a result.

The word, taken apart.
نتج (nataja) is built on the native Arabic root n-t-j, "to bring forth young" (Lane). Nataja is a Form I verb: to come forth from a thing, to result.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ترتب (tarattaba) — to follow from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to endure, then the Arabic for to stem from.

You'll want to know: ترتب.
ترتب — tarattaba — "to follow from".
ترتب على القرار تغييرات كثيرة. tarattaba ʿalā al-qarār taghyīrāt kathīra. Many changes followed from the decision.
Present: يترتب, yatarattab.
It takes على: ترتب (tarattaba) على ….

The word, taken apart.
ترتب (tarattaba) is built on the native Arabic root r-t-b, "to be firm, fixed in place" (Lane). It is Form V, to fall into rank after a thing: to follow from it. رتب, to tidy, is from the same root.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: تأثير.

تأثير (taʾthīr) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-th-r, "a trace, a track" (Lane). Taʾthīr is the Form II verbal noun, leaving a trace on something: an effect, an influence.
