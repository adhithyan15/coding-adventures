## You'll want to know: स्वादुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C515-SVADA515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वादुः** — *svāduḥ* — "tasty".

> मातुः भोजनं स्वादु अस्ति।
>
> *mātuḥ bhojanaṃ svādu asti.*
>
> Mother's cooking is tasty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वादुः** (*svāduḥ*) comes from the root √svad, "to be sweet"; MW gives *svādu* as sweet, savoury, pleasant to the taste (MW).
