## You'll want to know: stornieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C420-STORNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**stornieren** — "to cancel (a booking)".

> Er storniert das Hotel, weil er krank ist.
>
> *He cancels the hotel because he is ill.*

Past participle without *ge-*: *ich habe den Flug storniert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stornieren* was borrowed from Italian *stornare* 'to turn back, to reverse an entry in the books' (EtymDB), a word from bookkeeping.
