## You'll want to know: l'efficacité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C361-STRESS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'efficacité** — "the efficiency, effectiveness".

> Il travaille avec beaucoup d'efficacité.
>
> *He works very efficiently.*

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*efficacité* is a learned borrowing from Latin *efficācitās* "efficacy" (Lewis & Short), from *efficāx* "effective", built on *facere* "to do".
