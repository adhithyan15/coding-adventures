# ఊరు (ūru) — a village or town; one's home place

## You'll want to know: ఊరు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C320-URU-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C42-ADJ-02, TE-LEX-C64-JOIN-01] -->

**ఊరు** — *ūru* — "a village or town; one's home place".

> **మీది ఏ ఊరు?**
>
> *mīdi ē ūru?* — "Which town are you from?"

> **మా ఊరు చిన్నది, కానీ చాలా బాగుంటుంది.**
>
> *mā ūru cinnadi, kānī cālā bāguṇṭundi.* — "My home town is small, but it is very nice."

**మీది** (*mīdi*, "yours") and **ఏ** (*ē*, "which"): "yours is which town?", the usual way to ask where someone comes from.

**ఊరు** is wider than **గ్రామం** or **పట్టణం**: any place where people live, and most of all the one you come
from. Before an ending its **-ఉ** turns to **-ఇ**: **ఊరికి** (*ūriki*, "to the town, home"), **ఊరిలో** (*ūrilō*, "in the town").
**ఊరికి వెళ్తున్నాను** on its own means "I am going home, to my own place". Many town names end in it, as
**నెల్లూరు** does.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఊరు** (*ūru*) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil and Malayalam *ūr* and Kannada *ūru*, all "village, town"
(DEDR 752). It is one of the words the whole family shares.
