## You'll want to know: قرابة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C532-HAYAT532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قرابة** — *qarāba* — "kinship".

> بيني وبين جاري قرابة بعيدة.
>
> *baynī wa-bayna jārī qarāba baʿīda.*
>
> My neighbour and I are distant relatives.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرابة** (*qarāba*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-b*, "to be near" (Lane). Qarīb, near and a relative, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
