Italian, chapter 502: Sotto le stelle.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
campeggiare — to camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to bite, then the Italian for water bottle.

You'll want to know: campeggiare.
campeggiare — "to camp".
D'estate campeggiamo vicino al fiume. In summer we camp near the river.
Built on il campeggio "campsite, camping".

The word, taken apart.
campeggiare is built on campo "field", inherited from Latin campus "a plain, a field" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 6.
la torcia — torch, flashlight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for water bottle, then the Italian for to camp.

You'll want to know: la torcia.
la torcia — "torch, flashlight".
Di notte usiamo la torcia per trovare la tenda. At night we use the torch to find the tent.
In speech also la pila.

The word, taken apart.
torcia is usually traced to Vulgar Latin torca "something twisted", from Latin torquēre "to twist" (Lewis & Short): a torch was twisted straw or rope.

Lesson 4 of 6.
l'escursione — trip, hike, excursion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for torch, then the Italian for camping stove.

You'll want to know: l'escursione.
l'escursione — "trip, hike, excursion".
Domani facciamo un'escursione in montagna. Tomorrow we are going on a hike in the mountains.
Fare un'escursione — to go on a trip or a hike.

The word, taken apart.
escursione is a learned borrowing from Latin excursiō "a running out, a setting out" (EtymDB), from excurrere "to run out".
