Lesson 1 of 5.
il dialetto — a dialect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for society, then the Italian for the government.

You'll want to know: il dialetto.
il dialetto — "a dialect".

The word, taken apart.
dialetto from Latin dialectos, "a way of speaking", from Greek dialektos, "a conversation, a way of speaking" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
il telegiornale — the TV news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a dialect, then the Italian for a piece of news.

You'll want to know: il telegiornale.
il telegiornale — "the TV news".

The word, taken apart.
telegiornale is tele-, from the word for television, plus giornale, "a newspaper": the news on the screen. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for it; the parts are plain to see.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la pubblicità — advertising.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the TV news, then the Italian for TV.

You'll want to know: la pubblicità.
la pubblicità — "advertising".

The word, taken apart.
pubblicità — built from pubblico, "public", with -ità, after French publicité. pubblico goes back to Latin publicus, "of the people" (Lewis & Short).
