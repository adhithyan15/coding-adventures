- `ML-A1-LEX-54` closes. Malayalam A1 coverage 202/243 -> **203/243 (84%)**, 40
  points unmapped. **The percentage rolls over, 83% -> 84%** -- computed, not
  assumed, after a stale 82% shipped past every check but the full suite in

- **Two words and no new grammar**, and the corpus proves it rather than the
  lesson claiming it. **എളുപ്പം + ആണ് → എളുപ്പമാണ്** is the same welding as
  **സുഖമാണ്** (chapter 3), **ഇഷ്ടമാണ്** (34), **കുടുംബമാണ്** (35) and
  **മോശമാണ്** (89). The pattern sat in the learner's mouth for most of the book
  because until now they had no word of their own to put through it.
- So the lesson can say *"you have been doing this since you first answered that
  you were well"* and point at four attested examples, instead of asserting a
  rule.
- `ML-C96-prayaasam` needs no second explanation: **പ്രയാസം** ends in **ം** as
  well, so it behaves the way the first one did. One word apart and the sentence
  is its own opposite, with everything around it holding still.

  from chapter 95. Romanization was **derived from the track** -- final anusvara

  invented, which is how chapter 95's `th`/`tth` drift happened.
- **A draft line was cut:** it said the learner had been doing this *"since your
  third week"*. Chapters are not weeks and nothing in this repo knows how long a
  learner takes. It names the event instead. Same class as the `chapter 42`
  cross-reference and the lesson-count that rotted from 393 to 405.
- **ബുദ്ധിമുട്ട് was considered for a third lesson and dropped** rather than
  assert register nuances that could not be defended under review. The point asks
  for ease and difficulty; two words close it.

  without being assessed in any body block. Declared where it is actually tested.
