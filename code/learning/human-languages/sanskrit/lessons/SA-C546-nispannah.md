## You'll want to know: निष्पन्नः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C546-VIPAKA546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्पन्नः** — *niṣpannaḥ* — "completed".

> यदि कार्यं निष्पन्नम् अस्ति, तर्हि वयं गृहं गच्छामः।
>
> *yadi kāryaṃ niṣpannam asti, tarhi vayaṃ gṛhaṃ gacchāmaḥ.*
>
> If the work is done, we go home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्पन्नः** (*niṣpannaḥ*) is built on *nis*, "out", and the root √pad, "to go", as its past participle; MW gives *niṣpanna* as brought about, completed, finished, ready (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
