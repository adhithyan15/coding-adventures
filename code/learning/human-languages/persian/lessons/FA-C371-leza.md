## You'll want to know: لذا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C371-DALIL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لذا** (*lezâ*) — "therefore (formal)".

> هوا خیلی بد بود، لذا جلسه برگزار نشد.
>
> *havâ kheyli bad bud, lezâ jalase bargozâr nashod.*
>
> The weather was very bad; therefore the meeting was not held.

Formal and written; in speech *barâye hamin*. A whole clause follows. ذ sounds like z.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لذا** is usually traced to the Arabic phrase *li-dhā* "for this"; Persian says the Arabic *dh* as *z*.
