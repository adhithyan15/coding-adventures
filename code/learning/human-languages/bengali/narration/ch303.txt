Bengali, chapter 303: Long Ago.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সেদিন (sedin) — that day; the other day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to bring about, then the Bengali for gradually.

You'll want to know: সেদিন.
সেদিন — sedin — "that day; the other day".
সেদিন বিকেলে হঠাৎ ঝড় এল। sedin bikele hôṭhāt jhôṛ elô. That day a storm blew up suddenly in the late afternoon.

The word, taken apart.
সেদিন (sedin) joins সে, 'that', to দিন, a day — Sanskrit dina (CDIAL 6328) — into one word: that day, the day the story is about. In talk it also means 'the other day', a few days ago.

Lesson 3 of 5.
পেরোনো (perono) — to pass by; to go past.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for that day, then the Bengali for the past.

You'll want to know: পেরোনো.
পেরোনো — perono — "to pass by; to go past".
বছর পেরোল, কিন্তু রাজকুমার ফিরল না। bôchhôr perolô, kintu rājkumār phirlô nā. Years went by, but the prince did not return.
Past (he/she …): সেই নৌকা নদী পেরোল, sei noukā nôdī perolô.

The word, taken apart.
পেরোনো (perono) is usually explained as built on পার, 'the far bank' — Sanskrit pāra (CDIAL 8100) — with the verb ending: to get to the far side of a river, or of a stretch of time.

Lesson 4 of 5.
কালক্রমে (kālôkrôme) — in the course of time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the past, then the Bengali for to go past.

You'll want to know: কালক্রমে.
কালক্রমে — kālôkrôme — "in the course of time".
কালক্রমে গ্রামটা একটা বড় শহর হল। kālôkrôme grāmṭā ekṭā bôṛo shôhôr hôlô. In the course of time the village became a big town.

The word, taken apart.
কালক্রমে (kālôkrôme) is Sanskrit kālakrama, 'the lapse of time' (Monier-Williams), in the -e form: kāla, 'time', and krama, 'a step'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
স্মরণ করা (smôrôn kôrā) — to commemorate; to call to mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to go past, then the Bengali for in the course of time.

You'll want to know: স্মরণ করা.
স্মরণ করা — smôrôn kôrā — "to commemorate; to call to mind".
আজ শহর সেই ভূমিকম্পের দিনটা স্মরণ করল। āj shôhôr sei bhūmikômper dinṭā smôrôn kôrlô. Today the city commemorated the day of that earthquake.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বাবাকে স্মরণ করলেন, tini bābāke smôrôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
স্মরণ করা (smôrôn kôrā) puts Sanskrit smaraṇa, 'the act of remembering' (Monier-Williams), before করা. It is more formal than মনে রাখা and is the word for honouring a memory in public; স্মৃতি comes from the same root.
