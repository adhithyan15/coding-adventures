Urdu, chapter 519: Drums and Gatherings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ڈھول (ḍhol) — a drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for practice (in music), then the Urdu for to hum.

You'll want to know: ڈھول.
ڈھول — ḍhol — "a drum".
شادی میں لڑکے ڈھول بجا رہے تھے۔ shādī meṅ laṛke ḍhol bajā rahe the. At the wedding the boys were playing the drum.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ڈھول (ḍhol) is inherited from Sanskrit ḍhōla, "a large drum" (CDIAL 5608).

Lesson 3 of 5.
قوالی (qavvālī) — qawwali (devotional song).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a drum, then the Urdu for a musical instrument.

You'll want to know: قوالی.
قوالی — qavvālī — "qawwali (devotional song)".
ہم نے کل رات قوالی سنی۔ ham ne kal rāt qavvālī sunī. Last night we listened to qawwali.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
قوالی (qavvālī) is built on qavvāl, "a qawwali singer", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qawwāl, "one who says much", with the noun ending -ī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نچانا (nachānā) — to make (someone) dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for qawwali, then the Urdu for a gathering (for music or poetry).

You'll want to know: نچانا.
نچانا — nachānā — "to make (someone) dance".
ڈھول نے سب مہمانوں کو نچایا۔ ḍhol ne sab mehmānoṅ ko nachāyā. The drum got all the guests dancing.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچوں کو نچایا, us ne bachchoṅ ko nachāyā.

The word, taken apart.
نچانا (nachānā) is the causative of nāchnā, "to dance", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit nṛtyati, "dances" (CDIAL 7583).
