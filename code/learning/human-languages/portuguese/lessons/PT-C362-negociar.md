## You'll want to know: negociar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C362-ORDENADO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**negociar** — "to negotiate".

> O sindicato negociou um ordenado melhor.
>
> *The union negotiated a better salary.*

Also to trade: *negociar em vinho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*negociar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *negōtiārī* "to carry on business" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *negōtium* "business", from *nec* "not" and *ōtium* "leisure".
