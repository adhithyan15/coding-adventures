## You'll want to know: खेल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C70-LEISURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खेल** (*khel*) — "a game".

Sanskrit खेल (*khela*), "play, sport", from a root that meant shaking or moving
about. The final vowel dropped away by the schwa rule and left the bare
consonant ending Hindi likes.

खेल covers more than a sport with rules. A child's game is a खेल, a match is a
खेल, and a thing somebody is not taking seriously is a खेल too. When somebody
you have just met asks what you do with your evenings, this is the word the
answer starts from.

The **ख** is the aspirated one — a real puff of breath after the *k*, which is
what separates खेल from क, and the difference changes the word.
