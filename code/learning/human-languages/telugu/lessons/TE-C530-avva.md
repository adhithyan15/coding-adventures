## You'll want to know: అవ్వ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C530-BANDHUVU530-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అవ్వ** — *avva* — "a granny; an old woman".

> పల్లెలో మా అవ్వ ఒంటరిగా ఉంటుంది.
>
> *pallelō mā avva oṇṭarigā uṇṭundi.*
>
> Our granny lives alone in the village.

Rural and affectionate; also any old woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అవ్వ** (*avva*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 273 lists Telugu *avva*, "mother, grandmother, an old woman", with Tamil *avvai*.
