# ವಿಪತ್ತು (vipattu) — a disaster; a calamity

## You'll want to know: ವಿಪತ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C332-VIPATTU332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಪತ್ತು** — *vipattu* — "a disaster; a calamity".

> ಪ್ರವಾಹ ಆ ಜಿಲ್ಲೆಗೆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ವಿಪತ್ತು ತಂದಿತು.
>
> *pravāha ā jillege doḍḍa vipattu tanditu.*
>
> The flood brought a great disaster to that district.

Plural: ವಿಪತ್ತುಗಳು, *vipattugaḷu*.

ಪ್ರಕೃತಿ ವಿಕೋಪ is the news phrase for a natural disaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಪತ್ತು** (*vipattu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vipatti*, "misfortune, adversity, disaster" (CDIAL 11788).

One more word for telling what happened.
