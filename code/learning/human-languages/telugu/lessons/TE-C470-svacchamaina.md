## You'll want to know: స్వచ్ఛమైన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C470-KALUSHYAM470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వచ్ఛమైన** — *svacchamaina* — "pure; clean".

> కొండల్లో స్వచ్ఛమైన గాలి దొరుకుతుంది.
>
> *koṇḍallō svacchamaina gāli dorukutundi.*
>
> In the hills you get pure air.

It stands before its noun: స్వచ్ఛమైన నీరు, clean water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వచ్ఛమైన** (*svacchamaina*) is built from Sanskrit *svaccha*, "very clear, transparent" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu adjective ending -మైన.
