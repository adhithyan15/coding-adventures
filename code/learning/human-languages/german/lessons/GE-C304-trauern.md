## You'll want to know: trauern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C304-TRAUER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**trauern** — "to mourn, to grieve".

> Das ganze Dorf trauerte um den alten Arzt.
>
> *The whole village mourned the old doctor.*

*Um jemanden trauern* — to mourn someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trauern* is inherited from Middle High German *trūren*, Old High German *trūrēn* "to mourn" (EtymDB).
