## You'll want to know: تبعیض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C387-MOHAJERAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تبعیض** (*tab'iz*) — "discrimination".

> تبعیض بین دختر و پسر درست نیست.
>
> *tab'iz beyn-e dokhtar o pesar dorost nist.*
>
> Discrimination between girls and boys is wrong.

Formal. *Tab'iz-e nezhâdi* is racial discrimination.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تبعیض** is usually traced to Arabic *tab'īḍ* "dividing into parts": discrimination splits people into groups and treats them apart.
