## You'll want to know: a gravidez
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C309-NASCIMENTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a gravidez** — "pregnancy".

> Durante a gravidez, ela continuou a trabalhar.
>
> *During her pregnancy she went on working.*

Built on *grávida*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gravidez* is built on *grávida* "pregnant", from Latin *gravidus* "laden, heavy", from *gravis* "heavy" (Lewis & Short).
