## You'll want to know: برداشت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C414-ARZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**برداشت کردن** (*bardâsht kardan*) — "to withdraw (money)".

> از خودپرداز صد هزار تومان برداشت کردم.
>
> *az khodpardâz sad hezâr tumân bardâsht kardam.*
>
> I withdrew a hundred thousand tomans from the cash machine.

*Az hesâb bardâsht kardan* — to withdraw from an account. Also to harvest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برداشت کردن** is native Persian, built from *bardâsht* "taking up", from *bar* "up" and *dâsht*, the past stem of *dâshtan* "to hold", with *kardan* "to do".
