# ബാല്യം (bālyaṁ) — early childhood

## You'll want to know: ബാല്യം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C558-VARDHAKYAM558-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ബാല്യം** — *bālyaṁ* — "early childhood".

> എന്റെ ബാല്യം ഗ്രാമത്തിലായിരുന്നു.
>
> *enṟe bālyaṁ grāmattilāyirunnu.*
>
> My early childhood was spent in the village.

A word of writing; in speech one says കുട്ടിക്കാലം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ബാല്യം** (*bālyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bālya*, "boyhood, childhood" (Monier-Williams), the noun of *bāla*, "a child".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
