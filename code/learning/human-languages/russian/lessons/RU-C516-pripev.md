## You'll want to know: припев
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C516-MELODY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**припев** (*pripév*) — "a chorus, a refrain".

> Все в зале пели припев вместе с певцом.
>
> *Vse v zále péli pripév vméste s pevtsóm.*
>
> Everyone in the hall sang the chorus with the singer.

Masculine. The verse is *куплет*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*припев* is built on *при-* and the root of *петь* 'to sing'; EtymDB has no entry.
