## You'll want to know: l'œuvre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C518-EXPOSITION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'œuvre** — "work (of art)".

> Le musée garde les œuvres d'un grand peintre.
>
> *The museum holds the works of a great painter.*

Feminine for a single work: *une œuvre d'art*. *Un chef-d'œuvre* — a masterpiece.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'œuvre* is inherited from Latin *opera* "work, effort" (EtymDB), related to *opus* "a work" (Lewis & Short).
