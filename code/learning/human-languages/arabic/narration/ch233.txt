Arabic, chapter 233: Since, Dawn, Sunrise, Sunset, Birth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منذ (mundhu) — since, ago.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a crutch, then the Arabic for bleeding.

You'll want to know: منذ.
منذ — mundhu — "since, ago".

The word, taken apart.
منذ (mundhu) is an old particle of time. Lane reports that some call it a simple word and others a compound, and its origin is not settled; it is not built on a three-letter root.

Lesson 2 of 5.
فجر (fajr) — dawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for bleeding, then the Arabic for since.

You'll want to know: فجر.
فجر — fajr — "dawn".

The word, taken apart.
فجر (fajr) is built on the native Arabic root f-j-r, "to cleave, to break open" (Lane). Fajr is the dawn that breaks the night.
