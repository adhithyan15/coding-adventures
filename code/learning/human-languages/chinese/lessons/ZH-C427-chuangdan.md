## You'll want to know: chuángdān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C427-COMEGO427-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chuángdān* — "bed sheet".

> *wǒ xiǎng huàn chuángdān* — I'd like the sheets changed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chuángdān* is built from *chuáng* 'a bed' and *dān* 'a single layer', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzrjang* and *tan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
