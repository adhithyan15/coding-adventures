## You'll want to know: ਮੇਜ਼ਬਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C312-VISIT312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੇਜ਼ਬਾਨ** — *mezbān* — "a host".

> ਉਸ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਸਾਡਾ ਮੇਜ਼ਬਾਨ ਬਹੁਤ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ ਸੀ।
>
> *us shām sāḍā mezbān bahut khush sī.*
>
> That evening our host was very happy.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੇਜ਼ਬਾਨ** (*mezbān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *mezbān*, "a host".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
