Lesson 1 of 5.
भोजनम् (bhojanam) — a meal, food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sesame, then the Sanskrit for a chickpea.

You'll want to know: भोजनम्.
भोजनम् — bhojanam — "a meal, food".

The word, taken apart.
bhojana comes from the root √bhuj "enjoy, eat" (CDIAL 9630): "eating", and so "a meal, food", already in the Rigveda. MW compares √bhuj with Latin fungor "perform, enjoy".

अशनम् — aśanam — "eating, a meal".

The word, taken apart.
aśana comes from the root √aś "eat, consume" (MW's second √aś; CDIAL 909): "eating, food", already in the Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa. It is not the same word as āsana "seat", from √ās "sit".

Lesson 5 of 5.
कलशी (kalaśī) — a small pitcher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for food, then the Sanskrit for eating.

You'll want to know: कलशी.
कलशी — kalaśī — "a small pitcher".

The word, taken apart.
kalaśī is the feminine of kalaśa "a water-pot, a jar", already in the Rigveda (CDIAL 2920). MW compares Latin calix "cup", and kalaśa lives on as Hindi kalsā and kalsī "small pot" (CDIAL 2920).
