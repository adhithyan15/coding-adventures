French, chapter 389: L'enquête.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
l'enquête — the investigation, the inquiry; the survey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the prisoner, then the French for to punish.

You'll want to know: l'enquête.
l'enquête — "the investigation, the inquiry; the survey".
La police a ouvert une enquête. The police have opened an investigation.
Also a survey: une enquête sur les jeunes. Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
enquête comes from Old French enqueste (EtymDB), from Latin inquīsīta "things searched for", from inquīrere "to search into", built on quaerere "to seek" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la victime.
la victime — "the victim".
La victime est encore à l'hôpital. The victim is still in hospital.
Always feminine in grammar, even for a man: il est la victime.

The word, taken apart.
victime is a learned borrowing from Latin victima "an animal offered in sacrifice" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
