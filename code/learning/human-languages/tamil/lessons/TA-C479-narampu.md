## You'll want to know: நரம்பு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C479-TACAI479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**நரம்பு** — *narampu* — "a nerve; a vein".

> கையில் உள்ள நரம்பில் ஊசி போட்டார்கள்.
>
> *kaiyil uḷḷa narampil ūci pōṭṭārkaḷ.*
>
> They gave the injection into a vein in the arm.

One word for nerve, sinew and vein.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நரம்பு** (*narampu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2903), covering nerve, tendon and blood vessel.

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
