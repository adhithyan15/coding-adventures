## You'll want to know: zhǐlù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C454-INFO454-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐlù* — "to give directions; to show someone the way".

> *tā gěi wǒ zhǐlù gàosu wǒ wǎng qián zǒu* — He gave me directions and told me to go straight on.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐlù* is built from *zhǐ* 'to point' and 路 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyijX* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a finger and 'to show'.
