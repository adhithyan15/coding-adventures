Gujarati, chapter 413: Which Way?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
તરફ (taraph) — towards; in the direction of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to drive in, then the Gujarati for to spend (time).

You'll want to know: તરફ.
તરફ — taraph — "towards; in the direction of".
અમે નદી તરફ ચાલ્યા. ame nadī taraph cālyā. We walked towards the river.

The word, taken apart.
તરફ (taraph) is usually traced to Arabic ṭaraf, "a side, an edge", which came through Persian; after a noun it means towards it: ઘર તરફ, towards home.

Lesson 2 of 5.
માર્ગ (mārg) — a route; a way (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spend (time), then the Gujarati for towards.

You'll want to know: માર્ગ.
માર્ગ — mārg — "a route; a way (formal)".
શહેરમાં જવાનો સૌથી ટૂંકો માર્ગ કયો છે? śahermā̃ javāno sauthī ṭū̃ko mārg kayo che? What is the shortest route into the city?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
માર્ગ (mārg) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mārga, first the track of a wild animal and then any road or way (Monier-Williams); signs and books use it where speech says રસ્તો.

Lesson 3 of 5.
પાર (pār) — the far side (of a river or a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for towards, then the Gujarati for a route.

You'll want to know: પાર.
પાર — pār — "the far side (of a river or a road)".
નદી પાર એક નાનું ગામ છે. nadī pār ek nānũ gām che. There is a small village on the far side of the river.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પાર (pār) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit pāra, "the further bank, the furthest end"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8100).

Lesson 4 of 5.
દોરવું (dorvũ) — to lead (someone along); to guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a route, then the Gujarati for the far side.

You'll want to know: દોરવું.
દોરવું — dorvũ — "to lead (someone along); to guide".
ભોમિયો અમને પર્વત પર દોરી ગયો. bhomiyo amne parvat par dorī gayo. The guide led us up the mountain.
Past (he …): તે બાળકને ઘરે દોરી ગયો, te bāḷakne ghare dorī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
દોરવું (dorvũ) goes back to dōr, "to look at or for", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to direct" (CDIAL 6581). It also means to draw a line, a sense this lesson leaves for later.

એક માણસે અમને સ્ટેશનનો રસ્તો ચીંધ્યો. ek māṇse amne sṭeśanno rasto cī̃dhyo. A man pointed out the way to the station.

The word, taken apart.
ચીંધવું (cī̃dhvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
