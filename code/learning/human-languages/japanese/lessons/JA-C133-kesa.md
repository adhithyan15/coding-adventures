## You'll want to know: けさ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C133-KESA]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KE-01, JA-SCRIPT-SA-01, JA-LEX-C133-KINOU] -->

**けさ** — *kesa* — "this morning".

The new sign is spent at once. **け** with **さ** after it: two beats, *ke–sa*,
and both signs are ones you write. **きのう** was yesterday; **けさ** is earlier
today.
