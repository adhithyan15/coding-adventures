## You'll want to know: लायक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C288-KAM288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लायक** — *lāyak* — "capable".

> छोरो घणो लायक है।
>
> *chhoro ghaṇo lāyak hai.*
>
> The boy is very capable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लायक** (*lāyak*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *lāyiq*, "worthy, fit".
