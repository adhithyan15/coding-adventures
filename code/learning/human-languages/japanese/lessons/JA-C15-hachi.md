## You'll Want to Know — hear and say

> **はち** — *hachi* — **eight**

Two beats, two old signs: **は** from chapter one and **ち** from chapter two.

And that is the whole lesson, which is the point of it. **Eight has no second
name.** Four had one and seven had one; eight does not, and neither did one, two,
three or five. The pattern you were shown twice is not everywhere — it sits at
two numbers out of the seven you now hold.

A rule you cannot see the edge of is not a rule you can use. This is the edge.
