# मिस्त्री (mistrī) — a mechanic

## You'll want to know: मिस्त्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C165-NOTE165-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C160-AYO-01] -->

**मिस्त्री** — *mistrī* — "a mechanic; a mason": the person who comes to mend
things.

> **मिस्त्री पंखो ठीक करै है।**
>
> *mistrī paṅkho ṭhīk karai hai.* — The mechanic mends the fan.

> **मिस्त्री काल आयो।**
>
> *mistrī kāl āyo.* — The mechanic came yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिस्त्री** (*mistrī*) is a loan, usually traced to Portuguese *mestre*, "a master
(of a trade)".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C165-NOTE165-01, MW-SCRIPT-THIK-01, MW-LEX-C58-VERBS58-02] -->

**ठीक करणो**, *to make right*, is how Marwari says *to mend*: **ठीक करै है**, and
in the past with **म्हैं**, **ठीक कियो**.
