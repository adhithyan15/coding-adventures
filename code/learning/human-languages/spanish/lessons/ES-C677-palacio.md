## You'll want to know: el palacio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C677-MONUMENTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el palacio** — "the palace".

> El rey vivía en este palacio.
>
> *The king lived in this palace.*

*El Palacio de Justicia* — the law courts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*palacio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *palātium* (EtymDB), named after the Palatine hill in Rome, where the emperors had their houses.
