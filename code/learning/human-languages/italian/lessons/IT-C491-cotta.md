## You'll want to know: la cotta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C491-INNAMORATI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la cotta** — "crush (on someone)".

> A quindici anni avevo una cotta per un compagno di classe.
>
> *At fifteen I had a crush on a classmate.*

*Prendersi una cotta per qualcuno* — to get a crush on someone. Informal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cotta* is the feminine past participle of *cuocere* "to cook", from Latin *coquere* (Lewis & Short): someone *cotto* is cooked through with love.
