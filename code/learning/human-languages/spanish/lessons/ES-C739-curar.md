## You'll want to know: curar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C739-CURAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**curar** — "to cure, to heal".

> El tiempo lo cura todo.
>
> *Time heals everything.*

*Curarse* — to get better.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*curar* is inherited from Latin *cūrāre* "to take care of" (EtymDB), built on *cūra* "care".
