## You'll want to know: el lustro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C539-ANYO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el lustro** — "the five-year period".

> En el último lustro la ciudad cambió mucho.
>
> *In the last five years the city changed a lot.*

A formal word, common in the press.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lustro* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lūstrum* (EtymDB), the cleansing sacrifice that Rome held every five years after the census.
