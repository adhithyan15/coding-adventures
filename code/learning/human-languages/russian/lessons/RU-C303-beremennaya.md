## You'll want to know: беременная
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C303-ROZHDENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**беременная** (*berémennaya*) — "pregnant".

> Сестра сейчас беременна.
>
> *Sestrá seychás berémenna.*
>
> My sister is pregnant now.

The feminine form, since it describes a woman; short form *беременна*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*беременная* (EtymDB: "pregnant") is usually traced to *беремя* "a burden, an armful" (EtymDB: Old East Slavic *beremę*), whose Church Slavonic shape is *бремя*: carrying a load. EtymDB lists no parent for *беременная*.
