Russian, chapter 234: To sweep, To plant, To water, To grow, To feed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
подметать (podmetát) — to sweep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to show, then the Russian for to bring.

You'll want to know: подметать.
подметать (podmetát) — "to sweep".

The word, taken apart.
подметать (podmetát) is под-, "under", with -метать, the ongoing partner of мести, "to sweep": to sweep up. Vasmer relates мести to метать, "to throw".

Lesson 4 of 5.
расти (rastí) — to grow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to plant, then the Russian for to water.

You'll want to know: расти.
расти (rastí) — "to grow".

The word, taken apart.
расти (rastí) from Proto-Slavic orsti, "to grow" — inherited, but its а is Church Slavonic, says Vasmer, in place of native рости. Vasmer reports comparisons with Greek órnymi, "I stir", and Latin orior, "I rise".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: кормить.

The word, taken apart.
кормить (kormít) comes from корм, "fodder, food" (Vasmer), which Vasmer compares with Irish cuirm, "beer", and Gaulish kourmi, a kind of beer.
