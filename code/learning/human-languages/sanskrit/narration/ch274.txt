Lesson 1 of 3.
One rental exchange, held as a whole — Is that a rented house? No, I do not pay rent.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Recall गृहम्, “house” or “home,” and क्रीणामि, “I buy.” Then recall the familiar household actions पचति, प्रक्षालयति, and मार्जयति: cooking, washing, and cleaning. This is your known house, buying, and household-work field.

You'll want to know: one complete attested exchange.
किं तत् भाटकितं गृहम्? kiṃ tat bhāṭakitaṃ gṛham? “Is that a rented house?” न, अहं भाटकं न ददामि। na, ahaṃ bhāṭakaṃ na dadāmi. “No, I do not pay rent.”
The direct conversation source supplies this complete attested exchange. A 1954 Saṃskṛtavyavahārapraśnottarī conversation and its Hindi gloss corroborate the rented-house and rent-payment function.
The older volunteer rendering prints भाटं, with an apparent dropped syllable. The Government of India's Commission for Scientific and Technical Terminology gives भाटकम् for “hire, rent” and गृहभाटकम् for “house rent.” The conversation above independently preserves भाटकं, so this lesson uses the cross-checked complete exchange rather than copying that apparent omission.

Grammar Lens: keep the exchange bounded.
Hear the question and answer as one practical pair. The lesson does not turn भाटकितम्, गृहम्, or भाटकम् into a productive participle or case paradigm, and it does not ask you to derive their sandhi.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[pause 4 seconds]
Read the question and answer once slowly. Then say them together as one exchange about whether a house is rented.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say the complete pair once more. Keep the attested wording together.
