# ਪੀਤਾ, ਪੀਤੀ — drank

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KITA-190, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-02, PA-LEX-CHA, PA-LEX-DUDH] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I did the work*. Then *tea* and *milk*.

## Grammar Lens: the thing decides
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KITA-190, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-02, PA-LEX-MAIN-03, PA-LEX-CHA, PA-LEX-DUDH] -->

**ਪੀਣਾ** works like **ਕੀਤਾ**: after **ਮੈਂ**, **ਅਸੀਂ** or **ਤੁਸੀਂ**, its past
agrees with what was drunk. **ਚਾਹ** is a feminine word, **ਦੁੱਧ** a masculine
one:

> **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।** — *maiṁ chāh pītī.* — I drank tea.

> **ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ।** — *maiṁ duddh pītā.* — I drank milk.

A man and a woman say both lines the same way.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-PRON-ASI-01, PA-LEX-YOU-REGISTER-02, PA-LEX-PAANI] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *we drank water*] Say **ਅਸੀਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਤਾ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *you drank tea*] Say **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।**] Point to **ਚਾਹ**, then to the ending that follows it.
- [YOU WRITE: copy **ਪੀਤਾ** and **ਪੀਤੀ** once]
