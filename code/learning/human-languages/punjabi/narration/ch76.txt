Punjabi, chapter 76: Sea, Fire, Snow, Rain, Wind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਮੁੰਦਰ (samundar) — the sea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the sun, then the Punjabi for the moon.

You'll want to know: ਸਮੁੰਦਰ.
ਸਮੁੰਦਰ — samundar — "the sea".
