## Script — 大 inside the frame of 国
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 因

**A frame and what it holds, and the frame is closed last, after its contents, as in 国.**

**The frame, two strokes first**: its left side, then its top turning down the right side.

**大, three strokes**, inside: the bar, the left-falling stroke, the short right-falling stroke.

**The bottom bar**, last, closing the frame.

2 + 3 + 1 = six.
