## You'll want to know: o pretexto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C352-REACAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o pretexto** — "the pretext".

> Ela usou o trabalho como pretexto para não ir.
>
> *She used work as a pretext for not going.*

*Sob o pretexto de* — on the pretext of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pretexto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praetextus* "an outward appearance, a show" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *praetexere* "to weave in front", built on *texere* "to weave".
