## Script — the grain piece, then 火
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QIU-AUTUMN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 秋

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, the grain piece, five strokes,** as on the left of 和: a short stroke falling to the left at the top, a bar, a vertical down through it, a stroke falling to the left from the crossing, and a dot to the right.

**The right piece, 火, four strokes,** as you wrote it: a short stroke on the left, a short stroke on the right falling to the left, then a long stroke falling to the left and a long stroke falling to the right.

5 + 4 = nine.
