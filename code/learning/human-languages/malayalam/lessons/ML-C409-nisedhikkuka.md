# നിഷേധിക്കുക (niṣēdhikkuka) — to deny

## You'll want to know: നിഷേധിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C409-KURRAM409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**നിഷേധിക്കുക** — *niṣēdhikkuka* — "to deny".

> അവൻ ആ വാർത്ത നിഷേധിച്ചു.
>
> *avan ā vārtta niṣēdhiccu.*
>
> He denied that news report.

Past: നിഷേധിച്ചു, *niṣēdhiccu*.

The noun is നിഷേധം, a denial; നിഷേധിക്കുക can also mean to refuse a thing to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നിഷേധിക്കുക** (*niṣēdhikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *niṣedha*, "warding off, prevention, prohibition" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക. To deny is to push a claim away.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
