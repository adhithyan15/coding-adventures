## You'll want to know: عقیدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C345-QADR345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عقیدہ** — *aqīdā* — "a belief; a creed".

> یہ اس کا پرانا عقیدہ ہے۔
>
> *yih us kā purānā aqīdā hai.*
>
> This is an old belief of hers.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقیدہ** (*aqīdā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿaqīda*, "a creed, a firm belief".
