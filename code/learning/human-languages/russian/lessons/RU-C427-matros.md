## You'll want to know: матрос
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C427-MAYAK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**матрос** (*matrós*) — "a sailor, a seaman".

> Старый матрос рассказывал нам о море.
>
> *Stáryy matrós rasskázyval nam o móre.*
>
> The old sailor told us about the sea.

Masculine. *Моряк* is anyone who works at sea; *матрос* is a deckhand or a junior rank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*матрос* was borrowed from Dutch *matroos* 'a sailor, a seaman', which EtymDB traces to French *matelot*.
