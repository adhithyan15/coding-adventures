## You'll want to know: 村长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C440-HILLS440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**村长** — *cūnzhǎng* — "village head".

> *cūnzhǎng dài wǒmen qù tā jiā* — The village head takes us to his house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**村长** (*cūnzhǎng*) is built from 村 'a village' and 长 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshwon* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'big'; in this sense 长 is said zhǎng, not cháng.
