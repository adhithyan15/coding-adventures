## You'll want to know: marchander
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C452-BRADERIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**marchander** — "to haggle, to bargain".

> Au marché aux puces, tout le monde marchande.
>
> *At the flea market everyone haggles.*

*Le marchandage* — haggling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marchander* is built on *marchand* 'trader' (EtymDB), which goes back to a Vulgar Latin form of *mercārī* 'to trade', from *merx* 'goods'.
