# పట్టుబట్టు (paṭṭubaṭṭu) — to insist

## You'll want to know: పట్టుబట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C427-MADHYAVARTI427-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టుబట్టు** — *paṭṭubaṭṭu* — "to insist".

> ఆమె ఆ రోజే వెళ్ళాలని పట్టుబట్టింది.
>
> *āme ā rōjē veḷḷālani paṭṭubaṭṭindi.*
>
> She insisted on going that very day.

Past (I …): పట్టుబట్టాను, *paṭṭubaṭṭānu*.

పట్టు, a grip, + పట్టు, to hold: to keep a firm grip on one’s point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టుబట్టు** (*paṭṭubaṭṭu*) is native Dravidian, a compound of *paṭṭu*, "a hold, a grasp; perseverance", and *paṭṭu*, "to hold, to seize" (DEDR 4034).
