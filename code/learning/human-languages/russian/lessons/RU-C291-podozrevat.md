## You'll want to know: подозревать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C291-SLEDOVATEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**подозревать** (*podozrevát*) — "to suspect".

> Я подозреваю, что он нас обманул.
>
> *Ya podozreváyu, shto on nas obmanúl.*
>
> I suspect that he deceived us.

An imperfective verb. *Подозревать кого-то в* + prepositional: *его подозревают в краже*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подозревать* is built on *под-* "under" and the old root *зр-* "to look", as in *зрение* "sight", inherited through Old East Slavic *zĭrěti* "to see" (EtymDB): to watch from below. EtymDB lists the verb with no parent.
