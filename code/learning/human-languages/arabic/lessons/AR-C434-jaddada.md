## You'll want to know: جدد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C434-RUKHSA434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جدد** — *jaddada* — "to renew".

> جدد أبي رخصته هذا الشهر.
>
> *jaddada abī rukhṣatahu hādhā ash-shahr.*
>
> My father renewed his licence this month.

Present: يجدد, *yujaddid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جدد** (*jaddada*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-d-d*, "to be new" (Lane). It is Form II, "to make new", beside *jadīd*, "new".
