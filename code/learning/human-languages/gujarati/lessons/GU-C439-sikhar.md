## You'll want to know: શિખર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C439-HILL439-02]; assesses=[] -->

**શિખર** — *śikhar* — "a summit; a peak".

> પર્વતના શિખર પર બરફ છે.
>
> *parvatnā śikhar par baraph che.*
>
> There is snow on the summit of the mountain.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શિખર** (*śikhar*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śikhara*, "a point, a peak" (Monier-Williams); it is also the spire of a temple.
