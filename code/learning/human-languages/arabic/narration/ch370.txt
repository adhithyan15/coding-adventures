Arabic, chapter 370: Criticism and excuses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انتقد (intaqada) — to criticize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to hesitate, then the Arabic for an alternative.

You'll want to know: انتقد.
انتقد — intaqada — "to criticize".
انتقد المدرب أداء اللاعبين. intaqada al-mudarrib adāʾ al-lāʿibīn. The coach criticized the players' performance.
Present: ينتقد, yantaqid.
The noun انتقاد, intiqād, criticism.

The word, taken apart.
انتقد (intaqada) is built on the native Arabic root n-q-d, "to sort good coins from bad" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to pick out the good from the bad: to judge, to criticize.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
