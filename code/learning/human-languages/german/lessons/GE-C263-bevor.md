## You'll want to know: bevor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C263-NACHDEM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bevor** — "before (joining two clauses)".

> Bevor er schlafen ging, las er ein Buch.
>
> *Before he went to bed, he read a book.*

The verb goes to the end: *bevor der Film beginnt*. The preposition is *vor*: *vor dem Film*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bevor* is built on *vor* "before", an inherited Germanic word and a cousin of English *fore*, with the old prefix *be-* in front; EtymDB lists no parent for the conjunction.
