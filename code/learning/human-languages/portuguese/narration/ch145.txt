Portuguese, chapter 145: To float, To chase, To insist, To interrupt, To tie.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
flutuar — to float.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to kill, then the Portuguese for to unite.

You'll want to know: flutuar.
flutuar — "to float".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does flutuar mean? ("To float".) Say it once more.
