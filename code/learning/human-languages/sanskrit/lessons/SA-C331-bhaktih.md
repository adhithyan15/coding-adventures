## You'll want to know: भक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C331-NADITIRA331-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भक्तिः** — *bhaktiḥ* — "devotion".

> जनाः भक्त्या नदीं नमन्ति।
>
> *janāḥ bhaktyā nadīṃ namanti.*
>
> People bow to the river with devotion.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भक्तिः** (*bhaktiḥ*) comes from the root √bhaj, "to share, worship", as in *bhajati*; MW gives *bhakti* as a share, and also attachment, devotion, worship, faith (MW).
