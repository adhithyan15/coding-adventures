## You'll want to know: અદબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C467-VARTAN467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અદબ** — *adab* — "respectful manners".

> બાળકોએ દાદા સાથે અદબથી બોલવું જોઈએ.
>
> *bāḷkoe dādā sāthe adabthī bolvũ joīe.*
>
> Children should speak to their grandfather respectfully.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અદબ** (*adab*) is usually traced to Arabic *adab*, "good manners, culture", which reached Gujarati through Persian.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
