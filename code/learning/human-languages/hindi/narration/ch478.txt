Hindi, chapter 478: Inside the Body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फेफड़ा (phephṛā) — a lung.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a leg, then the Hindi for a heel.

You'll want to know: फेफड़ा.
फेफड़ा — phephṛā — "a lung".
गंदी हवा से फेफड़ों को नुक़सान होता है। gandī havā se phephṛoṁ ko nuqsān hotā hai. Dirty air harms the lungs.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
फेफड़ा (phephṛā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi phephṛā, "lungs", under Sanskrit phupphusa, "lungs" (CDIAL 9090).

Lesson 3 of 5.
नस (nas) — a vein; a nerve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a lung, then the Hindi for the brain.

You'll want to know: नस.
नस — nas — "a vein; a nerve".
बूढ़े हाथों पर नसें साफ़ दिखती हैं। būṛhe hāthoṁ par naseṁ sāf dikhtī haiṁ. The veins show clearly on old hands.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
नस (nas) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi nas, "muscle, tendon, vein, nerve", under Sanskrit snasā, "sinew" (CDIAL 13784). Everyday Hindi uses one word for every cord under the skin.
