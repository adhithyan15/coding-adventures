## You'll want to know: l'actualité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C278-PRESSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'actualité** — "the news, current events".

> Mon père suit l'actualité tous les matins.
>
> *My father follows the news every morning.*

*Les actualités* — the news on TV or radio, a little old-fashioned. *D'actualité* — topical.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*actualité* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *āctuālitās* (EtymDB), built on *āctuālis* "active, real", from *agere* "to do" (Lewis & Short): what is happening now.
