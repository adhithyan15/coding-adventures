## You'll want to know: ساعت ساز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C454-KAFFASH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ساعت ساز** (*sâ'atsâz*) — "a watchmaker, a watch repairer".

> ساعت پدربزرگ را پیش ساعت ساز بردیم.
>
> *Sâ'at-e pedarbozorg râ pish-e sâ'atsâz bordim.*
>
> We took grandfather's watch to the watchmaker.

*sâ'at* 'watch, hour' + *sâz* 'maker'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sâ'atsâz* is built from *sâ'at* 'hour, clock', borrowed from Arabic *sāʿa* (EtymDB), and native *sâz*, the present stem of *sâkhtan* 'to make'.
