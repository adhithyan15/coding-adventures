# ડ — retroflex ḍa

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C147-PAST147-01, GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01, GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01] -->

- [YOU RECALL: say *late* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say **ટ**, **ઠ** and **ઢ**, the three tongue-curled letters you already write]

**મોડું** holds the one curled consonant your hand has not made yet.

## You'll want to know: the fourth corner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01, GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01] -->

Gujarati's retroflex row, with the tongue curled back, has a plain and a breathy
letter for each voicing:

| | plain | breathy |
|---|---|---|
| voiceless | **ટ** *ṭa* | **ઠ** *ṭha* |
| voiced | ? | **ઢ** *ḍha* |

The missing corner is the plain voiced one, *ḍa*, and **મોડું** needs it.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C147-DDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ડ** — *ḍa*

Keep **ડ** visible. Begin at the upper right and sweep left across the high
shoulder. Come down through the middle into the right side of the broad lower
bowl, then carry the bowl round its base and finish at its lower-left end. One
stroke, no lift.

**ઠ** starts the same way, but its bowl curls back inward and closes. **ડ**
leaves its bowl open at the lower left. If the end turns back in, you have made
a **ઠ**.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C147-DDA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ડ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice the shoulder, the drop
through the middle, and the open end of the bowl.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C147-DDA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ડ** once. Compare only two things: is the bowl broad, and does it
end open at the lower left? Repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C147-DDA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W47-dda-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-C147-DDA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write retroflex da from memory.","answer":"ડ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ડ — shoulder, drop, and a bowl left open.","incorrect":"Look once at ડ, hide it, and repair only the landmark that differs."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write retroflex *ḍa* once. Uncover **ડ**, compare, and stop
after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall

missing? (**None**: with **ડ** all four are in your hand.)
