## You'll want to know: પરિણામ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C246-MARKS246-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિણામ** — *pariṇām* — "result, outcome".

> **આજે પરિણામ આવે છે.** — *āje pariṇām āve che* — "The results come out today."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિણામ** (*pariṇām*) is Sanskrit *pariṇāma*, "change, outcome", taken over whole as a tatsama; EtymDB lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit, and CDIAL has no entry for it (EtymDB).
