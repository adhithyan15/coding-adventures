## You'll want to know: ഊർജ്ജസ്വലമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C530-USHAR530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഊർജ്ജസ്വലമായ** — *ūrjjasvalamāya* — "energetic; lively".

> ഊർജ്ജസ്വലമായ കുട്ടികൾ മൈതാനത്ത് ഓടിക്കളിച്ചു.
>
> *ūrjjasvalamāya kuṭṭikaḷ maitānattŭ ōṭikkaḷiccu.*
>
> The lively children ran about playing on the field.

From ഊർജ്ജം, energy. Note the doubled ജ്ജ after ർ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഊർജ്ജസ്വലമായ** (*ūrjjasvalamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *ūrjasvala*, "powerful, strong" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam adjective ending -ആയ.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
