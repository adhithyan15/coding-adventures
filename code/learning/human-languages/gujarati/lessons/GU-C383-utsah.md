# ઉત્સાહ (utsāh) — enthusiasm

## You'll want to know: ઉત્સાહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C383-PRAYATNA383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્સાહ** — *utsāh* — "enthusiasm".

> જો ખેલાડીઓમાં ઉત્સાહ હશે તો તેઓ સારું રમશે.
>
> *jo khelāḍīomā̃ utsāh haśe to teo sārũ ramśe.*
>
> If the players are keen, they will play well.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્સાહ** (*utsāh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *utsāha*, "power, effort, zeal" (Monier-Williams).
