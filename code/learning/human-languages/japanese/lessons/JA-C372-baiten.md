## You'll want to know: ばいてん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C372-EKIMAE372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ばいてん** — *baiten* — "a kiosk; a stall".

> **えきの ばいてんで しんぶんを かいました。** — *eki no baiten de shinbun o kaimashita* — "I bought a newspaper at the station kiosk."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばいてん** (*baiten*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bai*, "sell" (Middle Chinese *meaH*) and *ten*, "store" (*temH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
