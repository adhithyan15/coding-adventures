# のぼる (noboru) — to climb; to go up (a slope)

## You'll want to know: のぼる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C213-SKY213-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のぼる** — *noboru* — "to climb; to go up (a slope)".

> **らいしゅう やまに のぼります。** — *raishū yama ni noborimasu* — "Next week I will climb a mountain."

Polite **のぼります**, *noborimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のぼる** (*noboru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
