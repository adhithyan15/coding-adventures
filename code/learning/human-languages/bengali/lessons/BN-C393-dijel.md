## You'll want to know: ডিজেল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C393-PANGCHAR393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ডিজেল** — *ḍijel* — "diesel".

> ট্রাকগুলো ডিজেলে চলে।
>
> *ṭrākgulo ḍijele chôle.*
>
> Lorries run on diesel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডিজেল** (*ḍijel*) is borrowed from English *diesel*, named after the engineer Rudolf Diesel; the English z sound is said with জ.
