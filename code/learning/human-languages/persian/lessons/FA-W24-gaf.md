# گ (gâf) — the letter that opens گفتن

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-YE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-KAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ی and ک once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **گفتن** (*goftan*), "to say".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: گ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-GAF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گ** — *gâf*, the sound *g*.

It opens **گفتن** (*goftan*), "to say". It is **ک**, from the last lesson, with a
**second slash** above the first, the last of Persian's four added letters you
will write here.

## Writing: گ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-GAF-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the independent stem downward
- **2.** continue left through the shallow bowl and final hook without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then draw the long slash down from the upper right toward the stem
- **4.** lift again, then draw the shorter floating slash above the first — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, گ demonstration at 02:24–02:28 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **گفتن** and point at گ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: گ three times, saying *g* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ک, then گ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **گفتن** aloud once more]
