## You'll want to know: लाइन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C399-LAIN399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लाइन** — *lāin* — "a line; a railway line".

> रेल की लाइन नदी के किनारे जाती है।
>
> *rel kī lāin nadī ke kināre jātī hai.*
>
> The railway line runs along the river bank.

Feminine.

Also a queue of people: लाइन में खड़े रहिए, stay in the line.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाइन** (*lāin*) is borrowed from English *line*, used in Hindi for a railway line, a row of people and a line on a page.
