# ರಗ್ಗು (raggu) — a quilt; a thick blanket

## You'll want to know: ರಗ್ಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C545-BATTE545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಗ್ಗು** — *raggu* — "a quilt; a thick blanket".

> ಚಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ರಗ್ಗು ಹೊದ್ದು ಮಲಗಿದರು.
>
> *caḷiyalli makkaḷu raggu hoddu malagidaru.*
>
> In the cold the children slept under a quilt.

Heavier than ಕಂಬಳಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಗ್ಗು** (*raggu*) is a loan from English *rug*, in the older sense of a thick travelling blanket.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
