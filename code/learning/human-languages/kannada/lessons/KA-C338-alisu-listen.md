## You'll want to know: ಆಲಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C338-VADANTI338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಲಿಸು** — *ālisu* — "to listen closely".

> ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಕಥೆಯನ್ನು ಗಮನವಿಟ್ಟು ಆಲಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *ellarū ajjana katheyannu gamanaviṭṭu ālisidaru.*
>
> Everyone listened closely to Grandfather's story.

Past (I …): ಆಲಿಸಿದೆ, *āliside*.

A more careful listening than ಕೇಳು; what one listens to takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಲಿಸು** (*ālisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 383 lists Kannada *ālisu*, *ālaysu*, "to listen, to attend to".
