Russian, chapter 372: Likely or unlikely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
вероятно (veroyátno) — probably, likely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for let us say, then the Russian for a supposition.

You'll want to know: вероятно.
вероятно (veroyátno) — "probably, likely".
Он, вероятно, опоздает. On, veroyátno, opozdáyet. He will probably be late.
More bookish than наверное; set off by commas inside the sentence.

The word, taken apart.
вероятно (veroyátno) is built on вероятный "likely", which is usually explained as вера "belief" with the old verb ять "to take": what may be taken on belief. Вера is inherited from Proto-Slavic věra (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
вероятность (veroyátnost) — probability, likelihood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a supposition, then the Russian for probably.

You'll want to know: вероятность.
вероятность (veroyátnost) — "probability, likelihood".
Вероятность дождя — сорок процентов. Veroyátnost dozhdyá — sórok protséntov. The chance of rain is forty per cent.
Feminine. По всей вероятности — in all likelihood.

The word, taken apart.
вероятность (veroyátnost) is built on вероятный "likely" with the abstract suffix -ость; the root is вера "belief", Proto-Slavic věra (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
маловероятный (maloveroyátnyy) — unlikely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for probably, then the Russian for probability.

You'll want to know: маловероятный.
маловероятный (maloveroyátnyy) — "unlikely".
Это маловероятно, но возможно. Éto maloveroyátno, no vozmózhno. It is unlikely, but possible.
Built from мало, little, and вероятный, likely. Маловероятно — it is unlikely.

The word, taken apart.
маловероятный (maloveroyátnyy) is built from мало "little", from Proto-Slavic malŭ "small" (EtymDB), and вероятный "likely", built on вера "belief".

Lesson 4 of 5.
шанс (shans) — a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for probability, then the Russian for unlikely.

You'll want to know: шанс.
шанс (shans) — "a chance".
У тебя есть шанс получить эту работу. U tebyá yest shans poluchít étu rabótu. You have a chance of getting this job.
Masculine. Шансы — the odds: у него мало шансов.

The word, taken apart.
шанс (shans) was borrowed from French chance "luck, chance", from Vulgar Latin cadentia "a falling", from Latin cadere "to fall, to happen" (EtymDB): how the dice fall.

Lesson 5 of 5.
риск (risk) — a risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for unlikely, then the Russian for a chance.

You'll want to know: риск.
риск (risk) — "a risk".
Это большой риск. Éto bolshóy risk. That is a big risk.
Masculine. На свой риск — at one’s own risk.

The word, taken apart.
риск (risk) is usually traced, through French risque, to Italian risco; the further origin of the Italian word is not settled.
