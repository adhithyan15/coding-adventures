## You'll want to know: cuestionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C635-PROMESA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cuestionar** — "to question, to call into doubt".

> Nadie cuestiona su palabra.
>
> *Nobody questions his word.*

It means to doubt something, not to ask a question: that is *preguntar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cuestionar* is built on *cuestión*, a learned borrowing from Latin *quaestio* 'inquiry, question', from *quaerere* 'to seek, to ask' (EtymDB).
