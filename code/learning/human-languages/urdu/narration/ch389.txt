Urdu, chapter 389: Dozing on the Train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سوار (savār) — on board; riding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a berth (on a train), then the Urdu for to gaze (at).

You'll want to know: سوار.
سوار — savār — "on board; riding".
ہم صبح گاڑی میں سوار تھے۔ ham subah gāṛī meṅ savār the. We were on board the train in the morning.

The word, taken apart.
is usually traced to Persian savār, "a rider, mounted"; in Urdu a passenger who has got on a bus or a train is سوار too.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سرکانا (sarkānā) — to slide (something) along.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for on board, then the Urdu for a bundle (tied in cloth).

You'll want to know: سرکانا.
سرکانا — sarkānā — "to slide (something) along".
میں نے اپنا تھیلا سیٹ کے نیچے سرکایا۔ maiṅ ne apnā thailā sīṭ ke nīche sarkāyā. I slid my bag under the seat.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گٹھری آگے سرکائی, us ne gaṭhrī āge sarkāī.

The word, taken apart.
سرکانا (sarkānā) is built on saraknā, "to slide", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit sarati, "runs, glides" (CDIAL 13250): this is the form for moving something else.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a bundle (tied in cloth), then the Urdu for to slide (something) along.

You'll want to know: جماہی.
جماہی — jamāhī — "a yawn".
رات لمبی تھی اور سارا کو جماہی آئی۔ rāt lambī thī aur sārā ko jamāhī āī. The night was very long and Sara yawned.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
جماہی (jamāhī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jamhāī, "a yawn", under jṛmbhāyita, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5266). A yawn comes to you: سارا کو جماہی آئی.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اونگھنا (ūṅghnā) — to doze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to slide (something) along, then the Urdu for a yawn.

You'll want to know: اونگھنا.
اونگھنا — ūṅghnā — "to doze".
سارا کھڑکی کے پاس بیٹھ کر اونگھ رہی تھی۔ sārā khiṛkī ke pās baiṭh kar ūṅgh rahī thī. Sara sat by the window, dozing.
Past (he …): وہ گاڑی میں اونگھا, voh gāṛī meṅ ūṅghā.

The word, taken apart.
اونگھنا (ūṅghnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ū̃ghnā, "to be drowsy", under uṅghati, "sleeps", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1632).
