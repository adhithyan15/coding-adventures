# Close the writing loop for ब, स and ो

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PERFORMANCE-NUMBERED-PRICE-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-PERFORMANCE-PRICE-FOUR-SKILL-01] -->

[PAUSE 22s] Recall the numbered-price payoff, then recall the four-skill price
payoff from chapter 27 and say what the second one could not do.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-BA-01, MW-SCRIPT-SA-01, MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-BEES-01, MW-SCRIPT-SO-01, MW-SCRIPT-NO-NINE-01, MW-SCRIPT-DO-TWO-01, MW-SCRIPT-COUNTER-OFFER-01, MW-SCRIPT-DAS-01, MW-SCRIPT-PRICE-NUMBER-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Write two single signs and one mark from sound cues: **ब**, **स**, **ो**.

Then write **दो**, **नो**, **सो** as a column, given out of order, and finish
with **बीस** and **दस करो।** from dictation. Repair only the missed sign and
rewrite its word once.
