Arabic, chapter 59: To sell, To give, To put, To open, To close.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
باع (bāʿa) — to sell (he sold).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to play, then the Arabic for to buy.

You'll want to know: باع.
باع — bāʿa — "to sell (he sold)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bāʿa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bāʿa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bāʿa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to play, then the Arabic for to buy, then say bāʿa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does باع (bāʿa) mean? ("To sell (he sold)".) Say it once more.
