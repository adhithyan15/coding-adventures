## You'll want to know: das Gerücht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C274-GERUECHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Gerücht** — "the rumour".

> Im Dorf gab es ein Gerücht über den neuen Nachbarn.
>
> *In the village there was a rumour about the new neighbour.*

Plural: *die Gerüchte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gerücht* is usually traced to Middle Low German *gerüchte* "outcry, talk", built on the root of *rufen* "to call", from Proto-Germanic *hrōpaną* (EtymDB).
