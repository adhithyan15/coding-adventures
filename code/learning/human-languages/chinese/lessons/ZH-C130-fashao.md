## You'll want to know: fāshāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C130-ILL130-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fāshāo* — "to have a fever".

> *wǒ fāshāo le wǒ xiǎng shuìjiào* — I've got a temperature and I want to sleep.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāshāo* is built from *fā* 'to start' and *shāo* 'to burn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *syew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
