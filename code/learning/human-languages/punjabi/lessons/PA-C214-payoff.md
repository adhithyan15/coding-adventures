# Yes or no: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask *are you a traveller?* Then answer it with **ਹਾਂ ਜੀ**.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-YA-01, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *is he a traveller?*] Say **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੈ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *no, he is not a traveller*] Say **ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਉਹ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਕੀ ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ?**] Give its meaning, then point to **ਯ** in **ਯਾਤਰੀ** once more.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ?**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **ਕੀ** asks; **ਹਾਂ ਜੀ** or **ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ** answers. Ask and answer once more.
