Arabic, chapter 431: Traffic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نفق (nafaq) — a tunnel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a dinghy, then the Arabic for to row.

You'll want to know: نفق.
نفق — nafaq — "a tunnel".
دخل القطار نفقا طويلا تحت الجبل. dakhala al-qiṭār nafaqan ṭawīlan taḥta al-jabal. The train went into a long tunnel under the mountain.
Plural: أنفاق, anfāq.

The word, taken apart.
نفق (nafaq) is built on the native Arabic root n-f-q, "to pass away, to run out" (Lane). Lane gives nafaq as a passage under the ground.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.
