## You'll want to know: escroquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C288-ARNAQUE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**escroquer** — "to swindle, to con".

> Il a escroqué des personnes âgées.
>
> *He swindled elderly people.*

*Escroquer quelqu'un de cent euros* — to con someone out of a hundred euros.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escroquer* was borrowed from Italian *scroccare* "to sponge, to get by trickery" (EtymDB).
