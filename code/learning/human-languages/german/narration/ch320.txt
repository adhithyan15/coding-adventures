German, chapter 320: Im Mittelalter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Mittelalter — the Middle Ages.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to conquer, then the German for the victory.

You'll want to know: das Mittelalter.
das Mittelalter — "the Middle Ages".
Im Mittelalter war die Stadt sehr klein. In the Middle Ages the town was very small.
No plural. Mittelalterlich — medieval.

The word, taken apart.
Mittelalter joins mittel- "middle" and Alter "age", from Old High German altar, Proto-Germanic aldrą "life, age" (EtymDB): the age in the middle.

Chronik comes from Latin chronica, from Greek khronikos "of or about time", built on khronos "time" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Vorfahr — the ancestor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for antiquity, then the German for the chronicle.

You'll want to know: der Vorfahr.
der Vorfahr — "the ancestor".
Seine Vorfahren waren Bauern. His ancestors were farmers.
Weak noun, often in the plural: die Vorfahren. Also der Vorfahre.

The word, taken apart.
Vorfahr joins vor "before" and the root of fahren "to travel, to go", an inherited Germanic verb: one who went before. EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Untergang — the downfall, the sinking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the chronicle, then the German for the ancestor.

You'll want to know: der Untergang.
der Untergang — "the downfall, the sinking".
Der Untergang des Schiffes war eine Katastrophe. The sinking of the ship was a disaster.
Plural: die Untergänge. Der Sonnenuntergang — sunset.

The word, taken apart.
Untergang is built on untergehen "to go down", from unter "under" and Gang "a going", from the root of gehen "to go" (EtymDB).
