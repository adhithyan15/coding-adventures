Japanese, chapter 469: Instruments.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
がっき (gakki) — a musical instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a masterpiece, then the Japanese for to assemble.

You'll want to know: がっき.
がっき — gakki — "a musical instrument".
あねは がっきが じょうずです。 — ane wa gakki ga jōzu desu — "My elder sister is good at instruments."

The word, taken apart.
がっき (gakki) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ga, "music" (Middle Chinese ngaewk) and ki, "instrument" (khijH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

ねいろ — neiro — "the tone of an instrument".

The word, taken apart.
ねいろ (neiro) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: ne, "sound" and iro, "colour" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
