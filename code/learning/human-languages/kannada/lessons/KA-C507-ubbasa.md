# ಉಬ್ಬಸ (ubbasa) — asthma; breathlessness

## You'll want to know: ಉಬ್ಬಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C507-SADDU507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಬ್ಬಸ** — *ubbasa* — "asthma; breathlessness".

> ಚಳಿಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಉಬ್ಬಸ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *caḷigāladalli ajjana ubbasa heccāguttade.*
>
> In winter grandfather's asthma gets worse.

Doctors also say ಆಸ್ತಮಾ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಬ್ಬಸ** (*ubbasa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 632 lists Kannada *ubbasa* with Tamil and Kota words for asthma and panting.
