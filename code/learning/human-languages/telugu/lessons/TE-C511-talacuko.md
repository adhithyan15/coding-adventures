# తలచుకో (talacukō) — to think back on; to recall fondly

## You'll want to know: తలచుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C511-GNAPAKAM511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తలచుకో** — *talacukō* — "to think back on; to recall fondly".

> చిన్నప్పటి రోజులను తలచుకుని అమ్మమ్మ నవ్వింది.
>
> *cinnappaṭi rōjulanu talacukuni ammamma navvindi.*
>
> Thinking back on her childhood, grandma smiled.

Past (I …): తలచుకున్నాను, *talacukunnānu*.

Often -ని తలచుకుని, thinking of: fondly, or with regret.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తలచుకో** (*talacukō*) is built on తలచు, "to think of", with the reflexive -కో; the origin of తలచు is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
