## You'll want to know: avarento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C469-TEIMOSIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**avarento** — "miserly; miser".

> O velho avarento guardava o dinheiro debaixo da cama.
>
> *The miserly old man kept his money under the bed.*

The vice is *a avareza*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avarento* is built on *avaro* "greedy", which goes back to Latin *avārus* "greedy, covetous" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
