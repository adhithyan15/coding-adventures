# সঙ্গে (sôngge) — with

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-ALAAP-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "If I had time, I would go". (*jôdi sômôy thāktô, tāhôle āmi jetām*.) Then the line from your first meeting: **আলাপ করে ভালো লাগলো**.

## You'll want to know: সঙ্গে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C275-SONGGE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C11-BONDHU-01, BN-LEX-C225-JINIS225-03, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-ALAAP-01] -->

**সঙ্গে** — *sôngge* — "with".

> **আমি বন্ধুর সঙ্গে সিনেমায় গেলাম।**
>
> *āmi bôndhur sôngge sinemāy gelām.* — "I went to the cinema with a friend."

The one you are with takes the **-র** form: **বন্ধুর সঙ্গে**, **আমার সঙ্গে**, **তোমার সঙ্গে**. Now the greeting of chapter three can say who: **তোমার সঙ্গে আলাপ করে ভালো লাগল**, "it was good to meet you".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সঙ্গে** (*sôngge*) is usually read as **সঙ্গ**, "company", with the locative **-এ**: "in the company of". **সঙ্গ** is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṅga*, which Monier-Williams gives as "contact with" and "association with".
