## You'll want to know: ವರ್ತನೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C500-SVABHAVA500-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರ್ತನೆ** — *vartane* — "attitude; way of behaving".

> ಅತಿಥಿಗಳ ಮುಂದೆ ಮಗುವಿನ ವರ್ತನೆ ವಿಚಿತ್ರವಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *atithigaḷa munde maguvina vartane vicitravāgittu.*
>
> The child's behaviour in front of the guests was strange.

The verb is ವರ್ತಿಸು, to behave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರ್ತನೆ** (*vartane*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vartana*, "turning, rolling" (CDIAL 11354), from the root *vṛt*, "to turn, to be"; in Kannada it is the way a person carries on.
