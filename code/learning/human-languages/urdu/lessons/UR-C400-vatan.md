## You'll want to know: وطن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C400-PARDES400-01]; assesses=[] -->

**وطن** — *vatan* — "a homeland".

> میرا وطن یہاں سے دور ہے۔
>
> *merā vatan yahāṅ se dūr hai.*
>
> My homeland is a long way from here.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وطن** (*vatan*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *waṭan*, "a dwelling place, a homeland".
