# きえる (kieru) — to vanish; to go out (of a fire or light)

## You'll want to know: きえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C214-STORM214-05]; assesses=[] -->

**きえる** — *kieru* — "to vanish; to go out (of a fire or light)".

> **あめで ひが きえます。** — *ame de hi ga kiemasu* — "The fire goes out in the rain."

Polite **きえます**, *kiemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きえる** (*kieru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
