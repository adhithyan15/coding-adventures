Bengali, chapter 370: How Much.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
খুব (khub) — very.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for in spite of, then the Bengali for to determine.

You'll want to know: খুব.
খুব — khub — "very".
তুমি এলে আমি খুব খুশি হব। tumi ele āmi khub khushi hôbô. I'll be very happy if you come.

The word, taken apart.
খুব (khub) is usually traced to Persian khūb, good or fine. In Bengali it became the plain word for 'very'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ভীষণ (bhīshôn) — terribly; extremely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to determine, then the Bengali for very.

You'll want to know: ভীষণ.
ভীষণ — bhīshôn — "terribly; extremely".
রাস্তায় ভীষণ ভিড় থাকলে আমরা হেঁটে যাব। rāstāy bhīshôn bhiṛ thākle āmrā hẽṭe jābô. If the streets are terribly crowded, we'll walk.

The word, taken apart.
ভীষণ (bhīshôn) is Sanskrit bhīṣaṇa, terrifying (Monier-Williams). Bengali speech uses it as English uses 'terribly', for anything extreme, good or bad.

Lesson 3 of 5.
বেশি (beshi) — more; too much.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for very, then the Bengali for extremely.

You'll want to know: বেশি.
বেশি — beshi — "more; too much".
বেশি খেলে পেট ব্যথা করবে। beshi khele peṭ bæthā kôrbe. If you eat too much, you'll get a stomach ache.

The word, taken apart.
বেশি (beshi) is usually traced to Persian bēsh, more. It means 'more' after -এর চেয়ে and 'too much' on its own.

Lesson 5 of 5.
একটু (ekṭu) — a little; a bit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for too much, then the Bengali for less.

You'll want to know: একটু.
একটু — ekṭu — "a little; a bit".
একটু আস্তে বললে আমি বুঝব। ekṭu āste bôlle āmi bujhbô. If you speak a bit more slowly, I'll understand.

The word, taken apart.
একটু (ekṭu) is এক, one (CDIAL 2462 lists Bengali ek), probably with a small-making ending: 'one little bit'. It softens a request: একটু বসো, do sit down.
