## You'll want to know: بقشيش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C446-KHIDMA446-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بقشيش** — *baqshīsh* — "a tip (for service)".

> ترك أبي بقشيشا للنادل.
>
> *taraka abī baqshīshan li-n-nādil.*
>
> My father left a tip for the waiter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بقشيش** (*baqshīsh*) was borrowed from Persian *bakhshish*, "a gift" (EtymDB).
