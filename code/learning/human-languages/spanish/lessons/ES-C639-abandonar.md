## You'll want to know: abandonar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C639-FINAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**abandonar** — "to abandon, to give up; to leave".

> Abandonó los estudios a los dieciocho años.
>
> *He gave up his studies at eighteen.*

*Abandonar un proyecto* — to drop a project.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abandonar* was borrowed from French *abandonner*, from the phrase *à bandon* 'under someone's power', built on a Germanic word for an order, *bannan* (EtymDB).
