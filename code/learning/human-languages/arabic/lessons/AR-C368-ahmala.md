## You'll want to know: أهمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C368-AHAMMIYYA368-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أهمل** — *ahmala* — "to neglect".

> أهمل الرجل حديقته فماتت الزهور.
>
> *ahmala ar-rajul ḥadīqatahu fa-mātat az-zuhūr.*
>
> The man neglected his garden, so the flowers died.

Present: يهمل, *yuhmil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أهمل** (*ahmala*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-m-l*, whose Form I Lane gives for water overflowing (Lane). Form IV is to leave cattle to graze with no herdsman, and so to leave a thing to itself: to neglect.
