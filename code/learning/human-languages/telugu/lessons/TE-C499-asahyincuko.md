# అసహ్యించుకో (asahyiñcukō) — to loathe; to be disgusted by

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be jealous, then the Telugu for to hate.

## You'll want to know: అసహ్యించుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C499-ASUYA499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అసహ్యించుకో** — *asahyiñcukō* — "to loathe; to be disgusted by".

> మురికిగా ఉన్న వంటగదిని ఆమె అసహ్యించుకుంది.
>
> *murikigā unna vaṇṭagadini āme asahyiñcukundi.*
>
> She was disgusted by the dirty kitchen.

Past (I …): అసహ్యించుకున్నాను, *asahyiñcukunnānu*.

The noun is అసహ్యం, disgust: నాకు అసహ్యంగా ఉంది, it disgusts me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అసహ్యించుకో** (*asahyiñcukō*) is built on Sanskrit *asahya*, "unbearable, not to be endured" (Monier-Williams), from *a-*, "not", and *sah*, "to bear"; the Telugu endings -ఇంచు and -కో make it a verb about one's own feeling.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
