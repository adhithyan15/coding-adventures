Tamil, chapter 75: Yes or No.
4 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
-ஆ (-ā) — the question you can build yourself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Read பாலும். Then say: neither milk nor tea.

Grammar lens: one letter on the end.
Every question you have been given so far came ready-made: எப்படி, என்ன, யார், எங்கே, எவ்வளவு. Each one asks about a thing you name in advance.
-ஆ (-ā) asks about the whole sentence, and it is yours to attach to anything.
Take a statement. Put -ஆ (-ā) on its last word. It is now a question that wants yes or no.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
statement: நீங்கள் நலம் — you are well. question: நீங்கள் நலமா? — are you well?
statement: இது பால் — this is milk. question: இது பாலா? — is this milk?
statement: சரி — okay. question: சரியா (sariyā)? — okay?
Word order does not move. Nothing is added at the front. The ending goes on the last word, and the last word is the one being asked about.

What you've built.
Every sentence you already own can now be a question.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sariyā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nīṅgaḷ nalamā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — turn: say idu pāl, then ask idu pālā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: read பாலும், then say pālum tēnīrum illai, then ask sariyā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — return to: say āṇḍu, say tākam and read ஓ — three distances back — then ask about one of them with -ஆ (-ā)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word in the sentence carries -ஆ (-ā)? (The last one — and that is the word the question is about.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
ஆம், இல்லை — and the answer Tamil likes better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask, in Tamil: is this milk? Then say: neither milk nor tea.

Grammar lens: two answers, and a third.
You have had ஆம் and இல்லை since chapter one. Now there is something to answer.
இது பாலா? — idu pālā?
ஆம். / இல்லை.
Both work, and both are correct. But listen to how Tamil speakers actually reply, because they often reach for neither: they repeat the word that was asked about.
இது பாலா? — பால்.
சரியா (sariyā)? — சரி.
The echo is the everyday answer. ஆம் is a shade more formal, and it is the one to use when the question was long and you want to be plain.
இல்லை has no echo version. To deny, you say இல்லை.

What you've built.
You can ask a yes-or-no question and answer one, in the two shapes a speaker will hear.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — answer: idu pālā? — with the echo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: idu pālā? — with இல்லை]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — ask and answer: sariyā? — then sari]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say pālum tēnīrum illai, then ask nīṅgaḷ nalamā?, then answer nalam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — return to: say -um … -um, tūkkam and ōḍu — three distances back — then ask about one of them with -ஆ (-ā)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Somebody asks சரியா (sariyā)? and you agree. What is the everyday reply? (சரி — the echo, not ஆம்.)


Lesson 3 of 4.
சரியா (sariyā) — a question you can see.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: சரியா — the ending is a sign, not a letter and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the page: ask sariyā?, then give the echo answer.

Script you'll notice: சரியா — the ending is a sign, not a letter.
சரி was the third word in this book, and you wrote it in chapter nineteen. The question ending adds one shape to it.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
piece: ச, ca, the letter for a whole family of sounds.
piece: ரி, ri, ர with the ி sign.
piece: யா, yā, ய with the long ா sign.
ச, ரி, யா becomes சரியா (sariyā).
A ய appeared that was not in சரி. That is the same slide you saw in மேசையும்: சரி ends in a vowel, so a light ய் carries the ending on.
And the ending itself is the sign ா, sitting after ய the way it sits after ப in பா. A question mark closes the line on the page, but the question is already in that sign.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — read: ச — then ரி — then யா]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — read: சரி, then சரியா (sariyā) — a statement, then a question]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — point: the shape that does the asking]
[your turn — recall: ask sariyā?, then answer sari, then read சரியா]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — return to: say -um, kāyccal and kaḻuvu — three distances back — then ask about one of them with -ஆ (-ā)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read சரியா (sariyā). Which single shape turned it into a question? (The long ா on the end.)


Lesson 4 of 4.
நான் உள்ளே வரலாமா? (nāṉ uḷḷē varalāmā?) — may I come in?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Read சரியா (sariyā), then answer it.

You'll want to know: வரலாமா?
நான் உள்ளே வரலாமா? (nāṉ uḷḷē varalāmā?) — "may I come in?"
Three pieces you have, and one you half-have. நான் is "I". உள்ளே is "inside", from the six directions. வா is "come".
The half-known piece is -லாம். It has been in your goodbye since chapter four: நாளை பார்க்கலாம் — "we may see each other tomorrow". On a verb, -லாம் means it is allowed.
வா becomes வரலாம் — "may come". Then chapter seventy-five's ending arrives:
வரலாம் becomes வரலாமா? — "may I come?"
Permission is a yes-or-no question, so it takes the yes-or-no ending. Nothing new to learn about the asking.
To grant it, you have both answers already:
ஆம், வரலாம். or, at a doorway, plainly: சரி.

What you've built.
A doorway you can stand in and be let through.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nāṉ uḷḷē varalāmā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — grant: ām, varalām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — grant again: sari]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: answer sariyā?, then read சரியா, then ask varalāmā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — return to: read பாலும், say vali and say viḷaiyāḍu — three distances back — then ask about one of them with -ஆ (-ā)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
You are at a door and want to be let in. Say it. (நான் உள்ளே வரலாமா? (nāṉ uḷḷē varalāmā?))
