## You'll want to know: shōu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C118-SEND118-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shōu* — "to receive; to accept".

> *tā bù shōu qián* — He won't take any money.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shōu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *syuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to gather in' and 'to seize'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
