# एक तिकीट द्या (ek tikīṭ dyā) — at the counter

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C283-TIKIT-01, MR-LEX-DENE, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-HOW-FAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask how far the station is from here. (*ithūn sṭeśan kitī lāmb āhe?*) Then say "I want a ticket to Pune". (*malā puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ havaṁ āhe*.) The polite "please give" is **द्या**.

## You'll want to know: buying a ticket
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C283-BUY-TICKET-01]; assesses=[MR-PHRASE-KRUPAYA, MR-LEX-C154-ACT154-01, MR-QUESTION-KITI, MR-LEX-PAISE] -->

> **पुण्याचं एक तिकीट द्या.**
>
> *puṇyācaṁ ek tikīṭ dyā.* — "One ticket to Pune, please."

**पुण्याचं** is "of Pune": the ticket belongs to where it goes. A return: **परतीचं तिकीट** (*partīcaṁ tikīṭ*), with the **परत**, "back", of **परतणे**. Then the price: **किती पैसे झाले?** (*kitī paise jhāle?*), "How much is it?"

## You'll want to know: when it leaves
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-VAJTA, MR-LEX-C154-ACT154-02, MR-LEX-C153-QUAL153-05, MR-LEX-C236-VASTU236-01] -->

> **पुण्याची गाडी किती वाजता निघते?**
>
> *puṇyācī gāḍī kitī vājtā nighte?* — "What time does the Pune train leave?"

**निघते**, "sets out", is the present of **निघणे**, said of the train. The answers come as times: **सात वाजता** (*sāt vājtā*). And the other end: **गाडी किती वाजता पोहोचते?** (*gāḍī kitī vājtā pohocte?*), "What time does it arrive?" For the platform, ask with **कोणत्या** (*koṇtyā*, "which"): **कोणत्या फलाटावर?** (*koṇtyā phalāṭāvar?*)
