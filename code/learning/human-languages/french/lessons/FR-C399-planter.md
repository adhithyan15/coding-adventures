## You'll want to know: planter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C399-PANNE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**planter** — "to crash (of a computer, informal); to plant".

> Mon ordinateur a planté pendant la réunion.
>
> *My computer crashed during the meeting.*

Informal in this sense. First, to plant: *planter un arbre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*planter* is inherited from Latin *plantāre* "to plant" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *planta* "a sprout, a cutting".
