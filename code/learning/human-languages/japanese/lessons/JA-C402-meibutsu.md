## You'll want to know: めいぶつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C402-KANKOU402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**めいぶつ** — *meibutsu* — "a local speciality".

> **この まちの めいぶつは さかなです。** — *kono machi no meibutsu wa sakana desu* — "Fish is this town's speciality."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めいぶつ** (*meibutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mei*, "noted" (Middle Chinese *mjieng*) and *butsu*, "thing" (*mjut*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
