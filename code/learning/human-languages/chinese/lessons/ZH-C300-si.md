## You'll want to know: 死
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C300-DEATH300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**死** — *sǐ* — "to die".

> **我的花都死了** *wǒ de huā dōu sǐ le* — All my flowers have died.

The new character, in pieces: 死 — six strokes: a long bar on top; under it on the left the evening piece of 外, three strokes; on the right a short sweep and a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up, as on the right of 比. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**死** (*sǐ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *sijX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes an older dictionary for it: 'to be used up', what a person parts from.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
