## You'll want to know: સરકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C352-SARKAR352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સરકાર** — *sarkār* — "a government".

> સરકારે ગામમાં નવી શાળા બનાવી.
>
> *sarkāre gāmmā̃ navī śāḷā banāvī.*
>
> The government built a new school in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સરકાર** (*sarkār*) is usually traced to Persian *sarkār*, "head of affairs", built from *sar*, "head", and *kār*, "work".
