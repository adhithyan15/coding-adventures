## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**aussprechen** is *aus-*, "out", + the verb "to speak", Old High German *sprehhan* ← Proto-Germanic *\*sprekaną*, English *speak* (EtymDB 2.1).
