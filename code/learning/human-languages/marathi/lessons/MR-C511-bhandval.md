## You'll want to know: भांडवल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C511-DHANDA511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भांडवल** — *bhāṇḍval* — "capital; funds for a business".

> दुकान सुरू करायला त्याला भांडवल हवं होतं.
>
> *dukān surū karāylā tyālā bhāṇḍval havaṁ hotaṁ.*
>
> He needed capital to start the shop.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भांडवल** (*bhāṇḍval*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *bhāṇḍa*, "pot, wares, goods" (CDIAL 9440), the stock a trader starts with; the ending is Marathi.
