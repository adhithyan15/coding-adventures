## You'll want to know: ریل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C429-RAHAHAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ریل** (*reyl*) — "a rail (of a railway); the rails".

> قطار از ریل خارج شد.
>
> *Qatâr az reyl khârej shod.*
>
> The train came off the rails.

*Az reyl khârej shodan* — to come off the rails.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reyl* was borrowed from English and is usually traced to English *rail*. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
