# सिकोड़ना (sikoṛnā) — to draw in; to screw up (the face)

## You'll want to know: सिकोड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C481-LETNA481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिकोड़ना** — *sikoṛnā* — "to draw in; to screw up (the face)".

> ठंड में दादी ने रजाई में पैर सिकोड़े।
>
> *ṭhaṇḍ meṁ dādī ne rajāī meṁ pair sikoṛe.*
>
> In the cold, my grandmother drew her feet up under the quilt.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नाक सिकोड़ी, *usne nāk sikoṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिकोड़ना** (*sikoṛnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *sikoṛnā*, "to contract, compress, crumple together", under *\*saṁkōṭayati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12833). *nāk sikoṛnā*, "to screw up one's nose", is to show distaste.

## What you've built
