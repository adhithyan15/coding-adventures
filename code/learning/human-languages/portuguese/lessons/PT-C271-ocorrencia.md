## You'll want to know: a ocorrência
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C271-OCORRER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a ocorrência** — "the incident, the occurrence".

> A polícia respondeu a uma ocorrência na rua de cima.
>
> *The police responded to an incident in the street above.*

A police and news word for any reported incident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ocorrência* is a learned noun built on *ocorrer* "to occur", from Latin *occurrere* "to run to meet" (EtymDB), with the ending *-ência*.
