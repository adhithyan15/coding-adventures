Telugu, chapter 137: Light, Soft, Hard, Clean, Dirty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తేలిక (tēlika) — light (in weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for short, then the Telugu for heavy.

You'll want to know: తేలిక.
తేలిక — tēlika — "light (in weight)".

The word, taken apart.
తేలిక (tēlika) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3464 lists tēlika, "lightness; light, easy", under tēlu, "to float", with Kannada tēlu, "to float".

Lesson 3 of 5.
గట్టి (gaṭṭi) — hard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for light, then the Telugu for soft.

You'll want to know: గట్టి.
గట్టి — gaṭṭi — "hard".

The word, taken apart.
గట్టి (gaṭṭi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1148 lists gaṭṭi, "hard, firm", with Tamil kaṭṭu, "to harden", and Kannada gaṭṭi, "firmness". DEDR compares Sanskrit kaṭhina, "hard" (CDIAL 2650); CDIAL reports the view that kaṭhina came from Dravidian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
శుభ్రం (śubhraṁ) — clean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for soft, then the Telugu for hard.

You'll want to know: శుభ్రం.
శుభ్రం — śubhraṁ — "clean".

The word, taken apart.
శుభ్రం (śubhraṁ) is Sanskrit śubhra, "bright; white", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 12539). The sense "clean" is the one Telugu gives it.
