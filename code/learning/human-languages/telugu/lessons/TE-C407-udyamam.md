## You'll want to know: ఉద్యమం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C407-NIRASANA407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉద్యమం** — *udyamaṁ* — "a movement; a campaign for change".

> ఆ గ్రామంలో నీళ్ళ కోసం ఒక ఉద్యమం మొదలైంది.
>
> *ā grāmaṁlō nīḷḷa kōsaṁ oka udyamaṁ modalaindi.*
>
> A movement for water began in that village.

Plural: ఉద్యమాలు, *udyamālu*.

A long campaign by many people. ఉద్యమకారుడు, an activist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉద్యమం** (*udyamaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *udyama*, "the act of raising", and "undertaking, striving after, zeal" (CDIAL 2048).
