## You'll want to know: ढाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C329-SHASTRA329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ढाल** — *ḍhāl* — "a round shield; a shield".

> सैनिकाने ढालीने वार अडवला.
>
> *sainikāne ḍhālīne vār aḍavlā.*
>
> The soldier blocked the blow with his shield.

Feminine.

ढालीने, with the shield.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढाल** (*ḍhāl*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *ḍhāl*, 'a shield', under Sanskrit *ḍhāla*, 'a shield' (CDIAL 5583).
