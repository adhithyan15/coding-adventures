## You'll want to know: 洗衣店
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C215-DELIVER215-04]; assesses=[] -->

**洗衣店** — *xǐyīdiàn* — "laundry shop".

> **洗衣店在书店东边** *xǐyīdiàn zài shūdiàn dōngbian* — The laundry is to the east of the bookshop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**洗衣店** (*xǐyīdiàn*) is built from 洗 'to wash', 衣 'clothes' and 店 'a shop', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sejX*, *'j+j* and *temH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
