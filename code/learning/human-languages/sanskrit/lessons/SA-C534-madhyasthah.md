## You'll want to know: मध्यस्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C534-SAMVID534-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मध्यस्थः** — *madhyasthaḥ* — "a go-between".

> यदि विवादः अस्ति, तर्हि मध्यस्थः समाधानं ददातु।
>
> *yadi vivādaḥ asti, tarhi madhyasthaḥ samādhānaṃ dadātu.*
>
> If there is a dispute, let a go-between offer a way out.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मध्यस्थः** (*madhyasthaḥ*) is built from *madhya*, "the middle", and *stha*, "standing", from the root √sthā; MW gives *madhyastha* as standing between two parties, a mediator, and as impartial (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
