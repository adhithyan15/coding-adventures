## You'll want to know: احساس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C440-FEEL440-02]; assesses=[] -->

**احساس** — *ehsās* — "a feeling; an awareness".

> مجھے سردی کا احساس ہوا۔
>
> *mujhe sardī kā ehsās huā.*
>
> I became aware of the cold.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احساس** (*ehsās*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iḥsās*, "perceiving, sensing".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
