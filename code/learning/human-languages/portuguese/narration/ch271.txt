Portuguese, chapter 271: O que ocorreu.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ocorrer — to occur, to happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the moment that, then the Portuguese for suddenly.

You'll want to know: ocorrer.
ocorrer — "to occur, to happen".
O problema ocorreu durante a noite. The problem occurred during the night.
Formal and frequent on the news; in speech, acontecer. Ocorre-me uma ideia — an idea occurs to me.

The word, taken apart.
ocorrer was borrowed from Latin occurrere "to run to meet, to come up" (EtymDB), built from ob "towards" and currere "to run".

Lesson 2 of 5.
suceder — to come after; to happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for suddenly, then the Portuguese for to occur.

You'll want to know: suceder.
suceder — "to come after; to happen".
O filho sucedeu ao pai na empresa. The son came after his father in the company.
O que sucedeu? — what happened? With a, it means to take someone’s place.

The word, taken apart.
suceder comes from Latin succēdere "to come up after, to take the place of" (EtymDB), from sub and cēdere "to go".

Lesson 3 of 5.
decorrer — to take place, to go on (over a stretch of time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to occur, then the Portuguese for to come after.

You'll want to know: decorrer.
decorrer — "to take place, to go on (over a stretch of time)".
A festa decorreu no jardim. The party took place in the garden.
No decorrer de means ‘as … goes on’: no decorrer do ano — as the year went on.

The word, taken apart.
decorrer is inherited from Latin dēcurrere "to run down, to run its course" (EtymDB), from dē and currere "to run".

Lesson 4 of 5.
o acontecimento — the event, the happening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to come after, then the Portuguese for to take place.

You'll want to know: o acontecimento.
o acontecimento — "the event, the happening".
O concerto foi o grande acontecimento do verão. The concert was the big event of the summer.
An important happening. Built on acontecer, ‘to happen’.

The word, taken apart.
acontecimento is built on acontecer "to happen", which is usually traced to Latin contingere "to touch, to befall" (Lewis & Short), with the noun ending -mento.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a ocorrência — the incident, the occurrence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to take place, then the Portuguese for the event.

You'll want to know: a ocorrência.
a ocorrência — "the incident, the occurrence".
A polícia respondeu a uma ocorrência na rua de cima. The police responded to an incident in the street above.
A police and news word for any reported incident.

The word, taken apart.
ocorrência is a learned noun built on ocorrer "to occur", from Latin occurrere "to run to meet" (EtymDB), with the ending -ência.
