## You'll want to know: بیمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C422-DALAL422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بیمہ** — *bīmā* — "insurance".

> میری گاڑی کا بیمہ ہے۔
>
> *merī gāṛī kā bīmā hai.*
>
> My car is insured.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیمہ** (*bīmā*) is usually traced to Persian *bīma*, "insurance".
