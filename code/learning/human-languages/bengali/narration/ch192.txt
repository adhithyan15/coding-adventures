Lesson 1 of 5.
হতাশ (hôtāsh) — disappointed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for ancient, then the Bengali for real.

You'll want to know: হতাশ.
হতাশ — hôtāsh — "disappointed".

The word, taken apart.
হতাশ (hôtāsh) is Sanskrit hatāśa, "whose hope is struck down", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. It is Sanskrit hata, "struck", + āśā, "hope".

You'll want to know: আলাদা.
আলাদা — ālādā — "separate, different".

The word, taken apart.
আলাদা (ālādā) is usually traced to Persian ʿalāḥida, "separate", from the Arabic phrase ʿalā ḥida, "by itself", on the root w-ḥ-d, "one" (Lane).
