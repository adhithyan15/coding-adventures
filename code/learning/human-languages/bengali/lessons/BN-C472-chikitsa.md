## You'll want to know: চিকিৎসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C472-OSHUDH472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**চিকিৎসা** — *chikitsā* — "medical treatment".

> শহরের হাসপাতালে দাদুর চিকিৎসা হল।
>
> *shôhôrer hāspātāle dādur chikitsā hôlô.*
>
> Grandfather was treated at the city hospital.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চিকিৎসা** (*chikitsā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *cikitsā*, "medical attendance, the practice of medicine" (Monier-Williams).
