## You'll want to know: ਡੁੱਬਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C318-MISHAP318-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੁੱਬਣਾ** — *ḍubbṇā* — "to sink".

> ਉਸ ਦਿਨ ਝੀਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਕਿਸ਼ਤੀ ਡੁੱਬੀ।
>
> *us din jhīl vich ikk kishtī ḍubbī.*
>
> That day a boat sank in the lake.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਪਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਡੁੱਬਿਆ, *oh patthar pāṇī vich ḍubbiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੁੱਬਣਾ** (*ḍubbṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍubb*, "sink"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍubbṇā*, "to sink", under it (CDIAL 5561).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
