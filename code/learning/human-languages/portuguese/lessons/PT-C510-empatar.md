## You'll want to know: empatar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C510-RESULTADO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**empatar** — "to draw, to tie".

> As duas equipas empataram a zero.
>
> *The two teams drew nil-nil.*

*Empatar a um* — to draw one-all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*empatar* was borrowed from Spanish *empatar* (EtymDB), which goes back to Italian *patta* "a draw".
