## You'll want to know: अंग्रेज़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अंग्रेज़ी** (*angrezī*) — "English", the language.

The route is long and worth following. Portuguese traders said *inglês*; Persian
took it as انگریز (*angrez*), "an Englishman"; Hindi added the ending **-ी**
that turns a people into their language, and अंग्रेज़ी came out. The nuqta under
ज़ is the *z* you learnt with the letters.

Set it beside **हिंदी**, which is built the same way: हिंद, the land, plus the
same ending. भाषा is the word for the class of thing; हिंदी and अंग्रेज़ी are two
members of it.

Both listening papers you will one day sit key on somebody speaking two of
these, so the pair matters more than either word alone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: देश, भारत, शहर, भाषा, अंग्रेज़ी. Enough to say what country you are in, what
city you live in, and what you speak.
