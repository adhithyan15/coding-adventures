## You'll want to know: ಹಿತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C388-NAITIKA388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿತ** — *hita* — "well-being; good".

> ಊರಿನ ಹಿತಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *ūrina hitakkāgi ellarū kelasa māḍidaru.*
>
> Everyone worked for the good of the village.

ಹಿತವಾದ, pleasant; ಅಹಿತ, harmful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿತ** (*hita*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *hita*, "beneficial, good, welfare" (CDIAL 14091).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
