## You'll want to know: かおり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C495-SMELL495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かおり** — *kaori* — "a fragrance; an aroma".

> **この おちゃは かおりが いいです。** — *kono ocha wa kaori ga ii desu* — "This tea has a lovely aroma."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かおり** (*kaori*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
