## You'll want to know: కేసు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C333-KESU333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కేసు** — *kēsu* — "a case (legal)".

> పోలీసులు దొంగ మీద కేసు పెట్టారు.
>
> *pōlīsulu doṅga mīda kēsu peṭṭāru.*
>
> The police filed a case against the thief.

Plural: కేసులు, *kēsulu*.

కేసు పెట్టు, to file a case: literally to put a case on someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కేసు** (*kēsu*) is a loan from English *case*, with the final -ఉ that many loans take, as in బస్సు.
