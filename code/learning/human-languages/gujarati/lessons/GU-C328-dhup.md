## You'll want to know: ધૂપ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C328-PUJA328-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ધૂપ** — *dhūp* — "incense".

> ઓરડામાં ધૂપનો ધુમાડો હતો.
>
> *orḍāmā̃ dhūpno dhumāḍo hato.*
>
> There was incense smoke in the room.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધૂપ** (*dhūp*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dhūpa*, "incense, aromatic smoke" (Monier-Williams). It is masculine: **ધૂપનો ધુમાડો**, the smoke of incense.
