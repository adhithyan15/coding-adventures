## You'll want to know: جادوگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C348-PARI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جادوگر** (*jâdugar*) — "a witch, a wizard".

> جادوگر شاهزاده را به سنگ تبدیل کرد.
>
> *Jâdugar shâhzâde râ be sang tabdil kard.*
>
> The witch turned the prince into stone.

*jâdu* "magic" + *-gar* "one who does".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jâdugar* is built from Persian *jâdu* "magic", a native word, with the suffix *-gar* "one who does".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
