## You'll want to know: der Bürgersteig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C440-BRUNNEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Bürgersteig** — "the pavement (US: sidewalk)".

> Die Kinder fahren mit dem Roller auf dem Bürgersteig.
>
> *The children ride their scooters on the pavement.*

Plural: *die Bürgersteige*. Also *der Gehweg*; in Switzerland *das Trottoir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bürgersteig* is built from *Bürger* 'townsman, citizen', from *Burg* 'castle, town', and *Steig* 'path', inherited from Old High German *stīg* (EtymDB).
