Bengali, chapter 383: Worth the Effort.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
সার্থক (sārthôk) — worthwhile; fruitful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to keep up, then the Bengali for to amend.

You'll want to know: সার্থক.
সার্থক — sārthôk — "worthwhile; fruitful".
তুমি পাস করলে আমার সব পরিশ্রম সার্থক হবে। tumi pās kôrle āmār sôb pôrishrôm sārthôk hôbe. If you pass, all my hard work will have been worth it.

The word, taken apart.
সার্থক (sārthôk) is Sanskrit sārthaka, 'with meaning', significant (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for an effort that turned out to have a point.

Lesson 4 of 7.
আক্ষেপ (ākkhep) — a regret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for in vain, then the Bengali for satisfied.

You'll want to know: আক্ষেপ.
আক্ষেপ — ākkhep — "a regret".
আগে চেষ্টা করলে আজ এই আক্ষেপ থাকত না। āge cheshṭā kôrle āj ei ākkhep thāktô nā. If I had tried earlier, I would not have this regret today.

The word, taken apart.
আক্ষেপ (ākkhep) is Sanskrit ākṣepa, among whose senses Monier-Williams gives reproach and harsh speech. The Bengali sense, regret for what might have been, is not one the dictionary gives.

Lesson 5 of 7.
ব্যর্থ হওয়া (bærthô hôoyā) — to fail; to come to nothing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for satisfied, then the Bengali for a regret.

You'll want to know: ব্যর্থ হওয়া.
ব্যর্থ হওয়া — bærthô hôoyā — "to fail; to come to nothing".
পরিকল্পনা ব্যর্থ হলে আমরা আবার চেষ্টা করব। pôrikôlpônā bærthô hôle āmrā ābār cheshṭā kôrbô. If the plan fails, we'll try again.
Past (he/she …): সেই চেষ্টা ব্যর্থ হল, sei cheshṭā bærthô hôlô.

The word, taken apart.
ব্যর্থ হওয়া (bærthô hôoyā) is Sanskrit vyartha, 'without purpose', useless (Monier-Williams), with হওয়া: to come to nothing. The taught ব্যর্থতা, failure, is its noun.
