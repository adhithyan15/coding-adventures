# పంక్చరు (paṅkcaru) — a puncture

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C444-INDHANAM444-02, TE-LEX-C444-INDHANAM444-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to break down, then the Telugu for to refuse to start.

## You'll want to know: పంక్చరు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C444-INDHANAM444-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పంక్చరు** — *paṅkcaru* — "a puncture".

> సైకిలు టైరుకు పంక్చరు అయింది.
>
> *saikilu ṭairuku paṅkcaru ayindi.*
>
> The bicycle tyre got a puncture.

పంక్చరు అయింది, it got a puncture; the shop that mends it is the పంక్చరు shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పంక్చరు** (*paṅkcaru*) is a loan from English *puncture*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
