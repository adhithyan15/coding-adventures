## You'll want to know: राजते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C444-CANDRA444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**राजते** — *rājate* — "gleams; shines brightly".

> अमावास्यायाः अनन्तरं चन्द्रः पुनः राजते।
>
> *amāvāsyāyāḥ anantaraṃ candraḥ punaḥ rājate.*
>
> After the new moon the moon gleams again.

Past (it …): चन्द्रः पुनः राजितवान्, *candraḥ punaḥ rājitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राजते** (*rājate*) comes from the root √rāj, "to shine, to be splendid", which MW also gives as to reign, and thinks was perhaps two roots at first; MW gives the participle *rājita*, "resplendent" (MW).
