## You'll want to know: der Vorschlag
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C335-DISKUSSION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Vorschlag** — "the suggestion, the proposal".

> Ich habe einen Vorschlag: Wir essen heute draußen.
>
> *I have a suggestion: we eat outside today.*

Plural: *die Vorschläge*. *Einen Vorschlag machen* — to make a suggestion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vorschlag* is the noun of *vorschlagen*, built from *vor* "before, forward" and *schlagen* "to strike": you put an idea forward. EtymDB has no entry for the compound, so this is the plain reading of its parts.
