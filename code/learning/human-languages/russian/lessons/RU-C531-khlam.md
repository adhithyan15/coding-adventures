## You'll want to know: хлам
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C531-CLUTTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**хлам** (*khlam*) — "junk".

> На балконе много старого хлама.
>
> *Na balkóne mnógo stárogo khláma.*
>
> There's a lot of old junk on the balcony.

Masculine, a collective noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
