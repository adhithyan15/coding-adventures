Marathi, chapter 120: Holiday, Music, News, Country, Story.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
सुट्टी (suṭṭī) — a holiday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a colour, then the Marathi for a journey.

You'll want to know: सुट्टी.
सुट्टी — suṭṭī — "a holiday".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: suṭṭī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: suṭṭī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say suṭṭī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a colour, then the Marathi for a journey, then say suṭṭī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सुट्टी (suṭṭī) mean? ("A holiday".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.

[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a country and a story.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: hot, cold, slowly, fast, expensive]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cheap, empty, rich, poor, dirt; dirty]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: clean, sweet, thick, thin, light]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: true, green, yellow, red, blue]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: black, white, brown, pink, young]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: old, a week, a month, a year, an hour]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a minute, today, now, always, sometimes]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: early, lateness; late, before, after, time]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Yellow? (पिवळा, pivḷā.) Red? (लाल, lāl.) Blue? (निळा, niḷā.) Black? (काळा, kāḷā.) White? (पांढरा, pāṇḍhrā.) Brown? (तपकिरी, tapkirī.) Pink? (गुलाबी, gulābī.) Young? (तरुण, taruṇ.) Old? (म्हातारा, mhātārā.) A week? (आठवडा, āṭhavḍā.) A month? (महिना, mahinā.) A year? (वर्ष, varṣa.) An hour? (तास, tās.) A minute? (मिनिट, miniṭ.) Today? (आज, āj.) Now? (आता, ātā.) Always?
