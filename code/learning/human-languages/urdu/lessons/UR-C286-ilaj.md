## You'll want to know: علاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C286-ZAKHMI286-03]; assesses=[] -->

**علاج** — *ilāj* — "medical treatment".

> ہسپتال میں اس کا علاج ہوا۔
>
> *haspatāl meṅ us kā ilāj huā.*
>
> He was treated in hospital.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**علاج** (*ilāj*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿilāj*, "a remedy, a cure". **علاج ہوا** says that someone was treated.
