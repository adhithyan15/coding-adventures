# ਨੁਕਸਾਨ (nuksān) — damage; a loss

## You'll want to know: ਨੁਕਸਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C311-LOST311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੁਕਸਾਨ** — *nuksān* — "damage; a loss".

> ਉਸ ਹਾਦਸੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਨੁਕਸਾਨ ਹੋਇਆ।
>
> *us hādse vich bahut nuksān hoiā.*
>
> There was a lot of damage in that accident.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੁਕਸਾਨ** (*nuksān*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *nuqṣān*, "a decrease, a shortfall".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
