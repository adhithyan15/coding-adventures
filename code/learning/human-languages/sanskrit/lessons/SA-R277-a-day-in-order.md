# Review — a day, in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-ONGOING-PAST-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "she went home" and "when I was reading, Arun came". (*sā gṛhaṃ gatavatī*, *yadā ahaṃ paṭhāmi sma, tadā aruṇaḥ āgatavān*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-THEN-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-THEN-02, SA-LEX-C277-SIGHRAM-01, SA-SKILL-C277-READ-STORY-01, SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-03, SA-LEX-C172-TRV172-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First I went to the station.]
- [YOU SAY: Then I bought a ticket and got on the train.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira came in and sat down.]
- [YOU SAY: Afterwards we ate quickly.]
- [YOU SAY: In the end Arun came home.]
- [YOU SAY: Tell Mira's day in the city in four lines, with *prathamam*, *tataḥ*, *anantaram* and *ante*.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-THEN-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-THEN-02, SA-LEX-C277-SIGHRAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **प्रविश्य** or *praviśtvā*? (**प्रविश्य**: the verb has a prefix.) "Having eaten"? (**खादित्वा**.) Now the other way round: what does **शीघ्रम्** mean? ("Quickly".) And **अन्ते**? ("In the end".)
