Italian, chapter 309: Di padre in figlio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la giovinezza — youth (the time of life).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the godmother, then the Italian for the confirmation.

You'll want to know: la giovinezza.
la giovinezza — "youth (the time of life)".
Nella mia giovinezza ho vissuto al mare. In my youth I lived by the sea.
Built on giovane, ‘young’.

The word, taken apart.
giovinezza is built on giovine, an old form of giovane "young", from Latin iuvenis (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la generazione — the generation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for of age, then the Italian for underage.

You'll want to know: la generazione.
la generazione — "the generation".
Tre generazioni vivono nella stessa casa. Three generations live in the same house.
Di generazione in generazione — from generation to generation.

The word, taken apart.
generazione is a learned borrowing from Latin generātiō "a begetting", from generāre "to beget", built on genus "birth, kind" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
tramandare — to hand down (across generations).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for underage, then the Italian for the generation.

You'll want to know: tramandare.
tramandare — "to hand down (across generations)".
La nonna ci ha tramandato questa ricetta. Grandmother handed this recipe down to us.
For stories, recipes and customs.

The word, taken apart.
tramandare is usually traced to a Latin trānsmandāre, from trāns "across" and mandāre "to entrust" (Lewis & Short gives mandāre): to send across the years.
