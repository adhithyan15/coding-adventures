# ঝুল (jhul) — cobwebs; sooty dust

## You'll want to know: ঝুল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C510-SAPHAI510-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝুল** — *jhul* — "cobwebs; sooty dust".

> সিলিং থেকে আমি ঝুল ঝাড়লাম।
>
> *Siling theke āmi jhul jhāṛlām.*
>
> I brushed the cobwebs off the ceiling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝুল** (*jhul*) probably belongs with *jhulyati*, "swings, hangs" (CDIAL 5406), the root of ঝোলানো: the dust that hangs from the ceiling.
