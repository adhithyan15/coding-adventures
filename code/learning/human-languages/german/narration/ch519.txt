German, chapter 519: In der Galerie.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Galerie — gallery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for easel, then the German for watercolour.

You'll want to know: die Galerie.
die Galerie — "gallery".
In der Galerie hängen Bilder von jungen Künstlern. Pictures by young artists hang in the gallery.
Plural: die Galerien.

The word, taken apart.
Galerie came into German from Italian galleria, through French galerie; the older origin of the Italian word is not settled, and EtymDB lists the German word without a source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ausstellen — to exhibit; to issue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for watercolour, then the German for gallery.

You'll want to know: ausstellen.
ausstellen — "to exhibit; to issue".
Das Museum stellt Fotos aus den Sechzigerjahren aus. The museum is exhibiting photos from the sixties.
Separable: stellt aus, hat ausgestellt. Also to issue a document: einen Pass ausstellen.

The word, taken apart.
ausstellen is built on aus "out" and stellen "to set, to place" (EtymDB), inherited from Old High German stellen, from Proto-Germanic stallijaną.

Skulptur is a learned borrowing from Latin sculptūra, from sculpere "to carve" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Bildhauer — sculptor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to exhibit, then the German for sculpture.

You'll want to know: der Bildhauer.
der Bildhauer — "sculptor".
Der Bildhauer arbeitet mit Holz und Stein. The sculptor works with wood and stone.
Plural: die Bildhauer. A woman is die Bildhauerin.

The word, taken apart.
Bildhauer is built on Bild "image", from Proto-Germanic biliþją, and hauen "to hew", inherited from Old High German houwan (EtymDB): someone who hews images.

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Kunstwerk — work of art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for sculpture, then the German for sculptor.

You'll want to know: das Kunstwerk.
das Kunstwerk — "work of art".
Für mich ist dieser Garten ein echtes Kunstwerk. To me this garden is a real work of art.
Plural: die Kunstwerke.

The word, taken apart.
Kunstwerk is built on Kunst "art, skill" and Werk "work", inherited from Old High German werc, from Proto-Germanic werką (EtymDB).
