## You'll want to know: dēlectātiō, dēlectātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C499-VOLU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dēlectātiō, dēlectātiōnis** — "delight, pleasure".

> Cantāre mihi magna dēlectātiō est.
>
> *Singing is a great delight to me.*

Feminine, third declension, from *dēlectō*. English *delectation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēlectātiō** is the action noun of *dēlectō* "to delight" (Lewis & Short). English *delectation* comes from it.
