# ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ — place the morning before the hour

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-TIME-SAMAA-01, PA-TIME-TON-BAAD-SEQUENCE-01, PA-TIME-VAJE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਸਮਾਂ**. Then retrieve **ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ**.

## Grammar Lens: morning precedes the hour
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-TIME-SAVERE-DAYPART-01]; assesses=[PA-TIME-VAJE-01, PA-LEX-C95-NUMBERS95-02] -->

> **ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ** — *savere aṭṭh vaje* — at eight in the morning

Put **ਸਵੇਰੇ** before the familiar whole-hour phrase. This lesson owns that bounded day-part use.
