# पहले … फिर … (pahle … phir …) — first, then, after that, in the end

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-01, HI-CONCEPT-C04-PHIR-01, HI-LEX-C279-AKHIR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You know **पहले**, "first, earlier", and **फिर**, "again, then". Say both. Then "in the end". (*ākhir meṁ*.)

## You'll want to know: the order words
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C279-FIRST-THEN-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-01, HI-CONCEPT-C04-PHIR-01, HI-LEX-C279-AKHIR-01, HI-LEX-C69-GOING-03, HI-LEX-C69-GOING-04, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01] -->

> **पहले मैं स्टेशन गया, फिर मैंने टिकट लिया।**
>
> *pahle maiṁ sṭeśan gayā, phir maiṁne ṭikaṭ liyā.* — "First I went to the station, then I bought a ticket."

A story marks its steps:

| step | the word |
|---|---|
| the first | **पहले** (*pahle*) |
| the next | **फिर** (*phir*) |
| the one after | **उसके बाद** (*uske bād*) |
| the last | **आख़िर में** (*ākhir meṁ*) |

Each one opens its sentence, and the verb after it is the plain past. **टिकट** is masculine, so **लिया**.

## You'll want to know: उसके बाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C279-USKE-BAAD-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-06, HI-LEX-C84-POSS-01, HI-LEX-C69-GOING-01] -->

**उसके बाद** is "after that": **उस**, the form **वह** takes before a postposition, as in **उसका**, then **के बाद**. **इसके बाद** (*iske bād*), "after this", is built the same way, on **इस**, the form **यह** takes before a postposition.

> **उसके बाद हम गाड़ी में बैठे।**
>
> *uske bād ham gāṛī meṁ baiṭhe.* — "After that we got on the train."
