German, chapter 516: Auf der Bühne.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Bühne — stage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to conduct, then the German for to compose.

You'll want to know: die Bühne.
die Bühne — "stage".
Die Band kommt um neun Uhr auf die Bühne. The band comes on stage at nine.
Plural: die Bühnen.

The word, taken apart.
Bühne is inherited from Middle High German büne "wooden stage, platform, ceiling" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Lampenfieber — stage fright.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for stage, then the German for to rehearse.

You'll want to know: das Lampenfieber.
das Lampenfieber — "stage fright".
Vor dem Konzert hatte die Sängerin Lampenfieber. Before the concert the singer had stage fright.
No plural. Literally lamp fever, after the stage lights.

The word, taken apart.
Lampenfieber is built on Lampe, from Latin lampas "torch, lamp", and Fieber, from Latin febris "fever" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
auftreten — to perform, to appear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to rehearse, then the German for stage fright.

You'll want to know: auftreten.
auftreten — "to perform, to appear".
Die Band tritt heute Abend im Stadtpark auf. The band is performing in the city park tonight.
Separable, Perfekt with sein: Sie ist oft im Fernsehen aufgetreten. Also of problems: Es sind Probleme aufgetreten.

The word, taken apart.
auftreten is built on auf "up, on" and treten "to step", inherited from Old High German tretan, from Proto-Germanic tredaną (EtymDB): to step up on stage.
