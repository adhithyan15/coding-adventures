## You'll want to know: त्वरयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C529-PARIVARTANA529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**त्वरयति** — *tvarayati* — "hurries along".

> यदि समयः अल्पः अस्ति, तर्हि पिता अस्मान् त्वरयति।
>
> *yadi samayaḥ alpaḥ asti, tarhi pitā asmān tvarayati.*
>
> If time is short, my father hurries us along.

Past (he …): सः अस्मान् त्वरितवान्, *saḥ asmān tvaritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्वरयति** (*tvarayati*) comes from the causative of the root √tvar, "to hurry, to make haste"; MW gives *tvarayati* as to cause to hasten, to urge forward (MW).
