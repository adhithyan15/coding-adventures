[PAUSE 3s] Safe? (**సురక్షితం**, *surakṣitaṁ*.) A lie; untrue? (**అబద్ధం**, *abaddhaṁ*.) Grey; ash? (**బూడిద**, *būḍida*.) Golden? (**బంగారు**, *baṅgāru*.) Spoiled? (**పాడైన**, *pāḍaina*.) Sweet? (**తీయని**, *tīyani*.) Bitter-tasting? (**చేదైన**, *cēdaina*.) Spicy? (**కారమైన**, *kāramaina*.) Courage; brave? (**ధైర్యం**, *dhairyaṁ*.) Lazy? (**సోమరి**, *sōmari*.) Hard-working? (**కష్టపడే**, *kaṣṭapaḍē*.) Clever? (**తెలివైన**, *telivaina*.) Terrible? (**భయంకరమైన**, *bhayaṅkaramaina*.) Black? (**నల్లని**, *nallani*.) White? (**తెల్లని**, *tellani*.) Red? (**ఎర్రటి**, *erraṭi*.) To fly? (**ఎగురు**, *egiru*.) To run? (**పరిగెత్తు**, *parigettu*.) To sleep? (**నిద్రపో**, *nidrapō*.) To get up? (**లేచు**, *lēcu*.) To cook? (**వండు**, *vaṇḍu*.) To cut? (**కోయు**, *kōyu*.) To break? (**పగలగొట్టు**, *pagalagoṭṭu*.) To send? (**పంపు**, *pampu*.) To look for? (**వెతుకు**, *vetuku*.)
