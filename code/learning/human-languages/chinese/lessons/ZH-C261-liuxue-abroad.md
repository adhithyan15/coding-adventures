## You'll want to know: liúxué
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C261-EVER261-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liúxué* — "to study abroad".

> *wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó liúxué* — I'd like to go to China to study.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liúxué* is built from *liú* 'to stay' and 学 'to study', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *haewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stay' and 'to stop'.
