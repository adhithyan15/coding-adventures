## You'll want to know: 爱情
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C461-KIND461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**爱情** — *àiqíng* — "romantic love; love between a couple".

> *zhè shì wǒ kàn guo de zuì hǎo de àiqíng diànyǐng* — This is the best love film I've ever seen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**爱情** (*àiqíng*) is built from 爱 'to love' and 情 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'ojH* and *dzjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to cherish'.
