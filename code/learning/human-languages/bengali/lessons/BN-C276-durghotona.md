## You'll want to know: দুর্ঘটনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C276-GHOTONA276-05]; assesses=[] -->

**দুর্ঘটনা** — *durghôṭônā* — "an accident".

> গতকাল সকালে স্টেশনের কাছে একটা বড় দুর্ঘটনা ঘটল।
>
> *gôtôkāl sôkāle sṭeshôner kāchhe ekṭā bôṛo durghôṭônā ghôṭlô.*
>
> There was a bad accident near the station yesterday morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দুর্ঘটনা** (*durghôṭônā*) joins Sanskrit *dur-*, 'bad, ill', to **ঘটনা**, an event; Monier-Williams has both parts. A bad happening is an accident.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
