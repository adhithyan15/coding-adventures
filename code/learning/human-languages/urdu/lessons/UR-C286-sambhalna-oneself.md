## You'll want to know: سنبھلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C286-ZAKHMI286-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سنبھلنا** — *sambhalnā* — "to steady oneself; to recover".

> ایک ہفتے بعد وہ سنبھل گیا۔
>
> *ek hafte bād voh sambhal gayā.*
>
> A week later he had recovered.

Past (he …): وہ سنبھل گیا, *voh sambhal gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sā̃bhalnā*, "to be supported", under Sanskrit *sáṃbharati* (CDIAL 12961). It is the partner of **سنبھالنا**: you **سنبھالنا** something, and you **سنبھلنا** yourself.
