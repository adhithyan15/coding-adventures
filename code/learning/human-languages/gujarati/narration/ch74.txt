Gujarati, chapter 74: Dear, Near, Far, Up and Down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મોંઘું (moṅghũ) — expensive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for hot, then the Gujarati for cheap.

You'll want to know: મોંઘું.
મોંઘું (moṅghũ) — "expensive". The opposite of સસ્તું.

What you've built.
One more word for dear, near, far, up and down.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: moṅghũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: moṅghũ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say moṅghũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for hot, then the Gujarati for cheap, then say moṅghũ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does મોંઘું (moṅghũ) mean? ("Expensive".) Say it once more.
