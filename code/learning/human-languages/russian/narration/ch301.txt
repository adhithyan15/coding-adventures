Russian, chapter 301: Disaster.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
катастрофа (katastrófa) — a disaster, a catastrophe; a crash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a drought, then the Russian for to flood.

You'll want to know: катастрофа.
катастрофа (katastrófa) — "a disaster, a catastrophe; a crash".
Это была настоящая катастрофа. Éto bylá nastoyáshchaya katastrófa. It was a real disaster.
Also a crash: авиакатастрофа — a plane crash.

The word, taken apart.
катастрофа (katastrófa) goes back to Greek katastrophḗ "an overturning", from katastréphō "I overturn", built on katá "down" and stréphō "I turn" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
взрыв (vzryv) — an explosion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to flood, then the Russian for a disaster.

You'll want to know: взрыв.
взрыв (vzryv) — "an explosion".
Мы услышали громкий взрыв. My uslýshali grómkiy vzryv. We heard a loud explosion.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
взрыв (vzryv) is built on взрывать "to blow up", which is usually traced to вз- "up" and рыть "to dig": to throw the earth up. EtymDB lists no parent.

Imperfective; perfective взорваться (vzorvátsya), взорвётся (vzorvyótsya), on another stem.

The word, taken apart.
взрываться (vzryvátsya) is the reflexive of взрывать "to blow up", which is usually traced to вз- "up" and рыть "to dig"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for an explosion, then the Russian for to explode.

You'll want to know: обрушиться.
обрушиться (obrúshitsya) — "to collapse, to cave in".
Старый мост обрушился. Stáryy most obrúshilsya. The old bridge collapsed.
Perfective; imperfective обрушиваться (obrúshivatsya).

The word, taken apart.
обрушиться (obrúshitsya) is built on об- and рушить "to pull down", an old East Slavic verb; EtymDB lists рушить with no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to explode, then the Russian for to collapse.

You'll want to know: паника.
паника (pánika) — "panic".
Люди бежали в панике. Lyúdi bezháli v pánike. People were running in panic.
Feminine. Не паникуй! — don't panic!

The word, taken apart.
паника (pánika) is usually traced through French panique to Greek panikós "of Pan", the god said to bring sudden fear; EtymDB lists no parent.
