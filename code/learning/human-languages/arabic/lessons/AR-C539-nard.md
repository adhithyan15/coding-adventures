## You'll want to know: نرد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C539-LUAB539-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نرد** — *nard* — "dice".

> رمى أخي النرد وفاز.
>
> *ramā akhī an-nard wa-fāza.*
>
> My brother threw the dice and won.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نرد** (*nard*) was borrowed from Persian: it is a Persian word, arabicized, for the game of backgammon and its pieces (Lane). Today it names the dice.
