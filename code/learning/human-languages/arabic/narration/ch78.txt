Arabic, chapter 78: Fire, Snow, Wind, Sky, Horse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نار (nār) — fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the moon, then the Arabic for the sea.

You'll want to know: نار.
نار — nār — "fire".

The word, taken apart.
نار (nār) is built on the native Arabic root n-w-r, "light; fire" (Lane). The same root gives nūr, "light".
