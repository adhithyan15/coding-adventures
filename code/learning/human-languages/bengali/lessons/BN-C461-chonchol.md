## You'll want to know: চঞ্চল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C461-JEDI461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**চঞ্চল** — *chônchôl* — "restless; never still".

> চঞ্চল শিশুটা এক জায়গায় বসে থাকে না।
>
> *chônchôl shishuṭā ek jāygāy bôse thāke nā.*
>
> The fidgety child never stays sitting in one place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চঞ্চল** (*chônchôl*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *cañcala*, "moving to and fro, unsteady, flickering" (Monier-Williams).
