Lesson 1 of 5.
とうげ (tōge) — a mountain pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to surround, then the Japanese for to scoop up.

You'll want to know: とうげ.
とうげ — tōge — "a mountain pass".
くるまで とうげを こえました。 — kuruma de tōge o koemashita — "We crossed the mountain pass by car."

The word, taken apart.
とうげ (tōge) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
