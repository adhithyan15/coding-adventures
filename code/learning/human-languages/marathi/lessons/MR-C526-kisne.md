## You'll want to know: किसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C526-OTA526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**किसणे** — *kisṇe* — "to grate".

> कोशिंबिरीसाठी मीराने काकडी किसली.
>
> *kośimbirīsāṭhī mīrāne kākḍī kislī.*
>
> Meera grated a cucumber for the salad.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने गाजर किसलं, *tyāne gājar kislaṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किसणे** (*kisṇe*) is a Marathi verb for grating vegetables; its origin is not settled.
