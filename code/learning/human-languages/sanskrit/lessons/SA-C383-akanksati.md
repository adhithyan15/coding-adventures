## You'll want to know: आकांक्षति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C383-SANKALPA383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आकांक्षति** — *ākāṅkṣati* — "longs for".

> यदि सः ज्ञानम् आकांक्षति, तर्हि प्रतिदिनं पठेत्।
>
> *yadi saḥ jñānam ākāṅkṣati, tarhi pratidinaṃ paṭhet.*
>
> If he longs for knowledge, he should study every day.

Past (he …): सः ज्ञानम् आकांक्षितवान्, *saḥ jñānam ākāṅkṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकांक्षति** (*ākāṅkṣati*) is built on *ā* and the root √kāṅkṣ, "to desire, to long for"; MW gives *ā-kāṅkṣā* "desire, wish", and KRM gives *kāṅkṣita* for the root (MW).
