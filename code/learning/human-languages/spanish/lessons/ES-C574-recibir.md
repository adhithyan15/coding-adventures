## You'll want to know: recibir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C574-ENTERARSE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**recibir** — "to receive, to get".

> Recibí una carta de mi tía.
>
> *I got a letter from my aunt.*

It also means to welcome: *Nos recibieron muy bien.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recibir* is inherited from Latin *recipere* 'to take back, to receive' (Lewis & Short), built on *re-* and *capere* 'to take'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
