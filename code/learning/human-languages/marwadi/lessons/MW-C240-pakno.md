## You'll want to know: पाकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C240-HARV240-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पाकणो** — *pākṇo* — "to ripen".

> काती मांय बाजरो पाकै है।
>
> *kātī mā̃y bājro pākai hai.*
>
> The millet ripens in autumn.

Past (it …): वो बाजरो पाक्यो, *vo bājro pākyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाकणो** (*pākṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pakva*, "cooked, ripe"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *pākaï*, "becomes ripe" (CDIAL 7621). **पक्को**, settled, comes from the same word.
