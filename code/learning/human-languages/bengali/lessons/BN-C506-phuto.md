# ফুটো (phuṭo) — a hole right through; full of holes

## You'll want to know: ফুটো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C506-TANK506-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ফুটো** — *phuṭo* — "a hole right through; full of holes".

> বালতিতে একটা ফুটো আছে।
>
> *Bāltite ekṭā phuṭo āchhe.*
>
> There's a hole in the bucket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফুটো** (*phuṭo*) belongs with a reconstructed *sphuṭyati*, "bursts open" (CDIAL 13845): a place where something has burst through.
