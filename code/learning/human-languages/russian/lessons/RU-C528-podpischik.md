## You'll want to know: подписчик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C528-GAMING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**подписчик** (*podpíschik*) — "a subscriber, a follower".

> У неё уже миллион подписчиков.
>
> *U neyó uzhé millión podpíschikov.*
>
> She already has a million followers.

Masculine; feminine подписчица.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подписчик* is built on *подписаться* 'to subscribe', from *под-* and *писать* 'to write'; EtymDB has no entry.
