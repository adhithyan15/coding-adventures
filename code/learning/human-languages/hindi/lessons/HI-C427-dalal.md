## You'll want to know: दलाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C427-UDHAR427-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दलाल** — *dalāl* — "a broker, a middleman".

> दलाल ने हमें एक सस्ता मकान दिखाया।
>
> *dalāl ne hamẽ ek sastā makān dikhāyā.*
>
> The broker showed us a cheap house.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दलाल** (*dalāl*) is usually traced to Arabic *dallāl*, "a broker, one who shows the way to a sale", through Persian.
