## You'll want to know: la droguerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C445-BOUTIQUES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la droguerie** — "the household goods shop".

> À la droguerie, elle trouve des produits pour le ménage.
>
> *At the household shop she finds cleaning products.*

Not a chemist's: it sells paint, soap and cleaning products.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*droguerie* is built on *drogue*, first 'dried goods, spices'. The origin of *drogue* is not settled; it is sometimes linked to Dutch *droog* 'dry'.
