## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-HA-01] -->

> **け** — *ke*

Three strokes, two lifts:

1. the left **vertical**, top to bottom; at the foot, turn and **flick** up to
   the right
2. the **bar**, left to right
3. the long **vertical** from the top, through the bar: run straight down, then
   **sweep** away to the lower left

The first two strokes are the ones your hand already makes in **は**. Only the
last stroke differs:

| you write | the last stroke ends |
|---|---|
| **は**, *ha* | in a small loop |
| **け**, *ke* | in a sweep to the lower left |

With **い** in front of it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**いけ**, *ike*, a pond.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KE-01, JA-SCRIPT-HA-01, JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-IKE] -->

1. Trace **け** saying "down and flick, bar, down and sweep".
2. Write **は** and **け** side by side; check that only the first one ties a loop.
3. Write **いけ** from memory and say *ike*.
