## You'll want to know: fouetter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C329-FOUETTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fouetter** — "to whisk, to whip".

> Elle fouette la crème pendant cinq minutes.
>
> *She whips the cream for five minutes.*

*La crème fouettée* — whipped cream.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fouetter* is built on *fouet* "whip, whisk" (EtymDB).
