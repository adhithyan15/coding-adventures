## You'll want to know: どりょくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C340-JOGEN340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**どりょくする** — *doryoku suru* — "to make an effort".

> **しけんが あるから、どりょくした ほうが いいです。** — *shiken ga aru kara, doryoku shita hō ga ii desu* — "There is an exam, so you should make an effort."

Polite **どりょくします**, *doryokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが どりょくしました。**, *gakusei ga doryoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どりょくする** (*doryoku suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *doryoku*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *do*, "toil" (Middle Chinese *nuX*) and *ryoku*, "power" (*lik*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
