Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it whole rather than
inventing smaller pieces. This lesson owns the noun alone; a complete identity
statement comes only after a separate retrieval.

## Across the family
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-VIDIARTHI-194] -->

Wiktionary records the Punjabi noun as a borrowing from Sanskrit
*vidyārthī*. The Punjabi University dictionary defines the Punjabi word as
“student, pupil, learner.” Keep the source history separate from today's
Punjabi pronunciation, *vidiārthī*, and learn the Punjabi form as one whole.
Sources: [Wiktionary: ਵਿਦਿਆਰਥੀ](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%A8%B5%E0%A8%BF%E0%A8%A6%E0%A8%BF%E0%A8%86%E0%A8%B0%E0%A8%A5%E0%A9%80);
[Punjabi University Punjabi-English Dictionary](https://dic.learnpunjabi.org/default.aspx?look=%E0%A8%B5%E0%A8%BF%E0%A8%A6%E0%A8%BF%E0%A8%86%E0%A8%B0%E0%A8%A5%E0%A9%80).
