# سه سال است که (se sâl ast ke) — for three years now, and three years ago

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C153-ZAMAN153-02, FA-LEX-C229-ZAMAN229-01, FA-LEX-C297-MESL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "ago", then "a length of time". (*Pish*, *moddat*.) Then "like
Tehran". (*Mesl-e Tehrân.*)

[PAUSE 1s] An experience has a length. Persian measures it two ways, and
the verb changes with them.

## Grammar Lens: ... ast ke, with the present
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-DURATION-AST-KE-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C234-CHERA234-04, FA-LEX-C217-KONESH217-02, FA-LEX-SE, FA-LEX-DO, FA-LEX-C64-ADJ64-04, FA-GRAMMAR-FEKR-MIKONAM-KE-01] -->

For a stretch that is still going on, name it, add *ast ke*, "it is that",
and keep the verb in the present:

> **سه سال است که در تهران زندگی میکنم.** — *se sâl ast ke dar Tehrân zendegi
> mikonam* — I have lived in Tehran for three years.

Word for word, "it is three years that I live in Tehran". English switches
to "have lived"; Persian stays in the present, because the living has not
stopped. *Zendegi kardan* is "to live", *zendegi*, "life", on *kardan*.

> **دو هفته است که اینجا کار میکنم.** — *do hafte ast ke injâ kâr mikonam* — I've
> been working here for two weeks.

To ask: **چه مدت است که اینجا کار میکنید؟**, *che moddat ast ke injâ kâr
mikonid?*, "how long have you been working here?".

## Grammar Lens: pish, with the plain past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-PISH-AGO-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C153-ZAMAN153-02, FA-LEX-AMADAN, FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01] -->

For a point in the past, the time goes first with *pish* after it, and the
verb is the plain past:

> **سه سال پیش به تهران آمدم.** — *se sâl pish be Tehrân âmadam* — I came to
> Tehran three years ago.

And a stretch that is over takes *barâ-ye*, "for", with the plain past:
*barâ-ye do hafte be Tehrân raftam*, "I went to Tehran for two weeks".

So the first two fit together: *se sâl pish âmadam. se sâl ast ke injâ zendegi
mikonam.*
