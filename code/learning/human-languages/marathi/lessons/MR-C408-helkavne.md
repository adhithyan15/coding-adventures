## You'll want to know: हेलकावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C408-NAVADI408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हेलकावणे** — *helkāvṇe* — "to toss about; to rock".

> वादळात होडी हेलकावत होती.
>
> *vādaḷāt hoḍī helkāvat hotī.*
>
> The boat was tossing about in the storm.

Past (he …): तो हेलकावला, *to helkāvlā*.

The noun हेलकावा is one toss: हेलकावे खाणे, to be tossed about.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हेलकावणे** (*helkāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb for a boat or a load swinging from side to side; its origin is not settled.
