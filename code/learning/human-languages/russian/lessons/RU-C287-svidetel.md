## You'll want to know: свидетель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C287-PRESTUPLENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**свидетель** (*svidétel*) — "a witness".

> Свидетель описал машину.
>
> *Svidétel opisál mashínu.*
>
> The witness described the car.

A woman is *свидетельница*. At a wedding, *свидетель* is also the best man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*свидетель* is inherited from Old East Slavic *sŭvědětelĭ* (EtymDB), built on *съ-* and the root of *ведать* "to know", PIE *weyd-*: one who knows what happened.
