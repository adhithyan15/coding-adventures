## You'll want to know: صيد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C536-SAYD536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صيد** — *ṣayd* — "hunting; fishing".

> يحب خالي الصيد في البحر.
>
> *yuḥibbu khālī aṣ-ṣayd fī al-baḥr.*
>
> My uncle loves fishing in the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صيد** (*ṣayd*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-y-d*, "to hunt, to catch" (Lane). Ṣāda, to hunt, and ṣayyād, a fisherman, which you know, share the root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
