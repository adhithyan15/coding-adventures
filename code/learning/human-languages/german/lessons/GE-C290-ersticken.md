## You'll want to know: ersticken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C290-BEWUSSTLOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ersticken** — "to choke, to suffocate".

> Der Hund erstickte fast an einem Knochen.
>
> *The dog almost choked on a bone.*

Perfekt with *sein*: *Er ist fast erstickt*. *An etwas ersticken* — to choke on something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ersticken* is usually traced to Middle High German *ersticken*, built on an old verb *sticken* "to get stuck, to choke"; EtymDB lists no parent.
