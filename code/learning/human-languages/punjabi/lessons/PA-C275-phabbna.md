## You'll want to know: ਫੱਬਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C275-FIT275-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫੱਬਣਾ** — *phabbṇā* — "to suit; to look good on someone".

> ਨੀਲਾ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਫੱਬਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *nīlā kuṛtā Aman nū̃ bahut phabbdā hai.*
>
> The blue kurta really suits Aman.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਫੱਬਿਆ, *oh kuṛtā Aman nū̃ phabbiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫੱਬਣਾ** (*phabbṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*sparvati*, "releases, succeeds, is fitting"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *phabbṇā*, "to look well, be profitable", under it (CDIAL 13808).
