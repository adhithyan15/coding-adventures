## You'll want to know: ущелье
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C458-DOLINA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ущелье** (*ushchélye*) — "a gorge, a ravine".

> Дорога идёт по узкому ущелью.
>
> *Doróga idyót po úzkomu ushchélyu.*
>
> The road runs through a narrow gorge.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ущелье* is usually traced to *щель* 'a crack, a gap', with *у-* and *-ье*: a deep crack between mountains. EtymDB does not list it, so the line is hedged.
