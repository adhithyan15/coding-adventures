## You'll want to know: la flaque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C465-TEMPETE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la flaque** — "the puddle".

> Le bus est passé dans une flaque et m'a mouillé.
>
> *The bus went through a puddle and got me wet.*

*Une flaque d'eau*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flaque* came into French from Picard *flaque* (EtymDB), a northern dialect word usually linked to *flache* 'hollow, soft ground'; the deeper origin is not settled.
