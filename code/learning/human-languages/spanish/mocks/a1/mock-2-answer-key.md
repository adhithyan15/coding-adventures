Every row carries a **`requires`** column: the lexemes and grammar a candidate
must hold to answer that item. Per `rubric.md` §5.1, an item is scored correct in
a book-bounded sitting **only if every entry in `requires` is taught at or below
A1**. That check is executed mechanically against the corpus. Lexemes are written
as citation forms; a `!` prefix marks a grammar point rather than a lexeme.

## Prueba 3 · Expresión e interacción escritas

Rated 0–3 per tarea; the two tareas weight 50 % / 50 %. Band 2 = the A1
descriptor.

### Tarea 1 — hoja de inscripción (15–25 palabras)

**Band-2 requirement.** All identifying fields completed intelligibly, and both
open questions answered with a short phrase.

`requires:` nombre, apellido, fecha, nacimiento, país, lengua, idioma, hablar,
dirección, teléfono, **correo electrónico**, querer, estudiar, español,
preferir, día, `!mayúsculas-en-nombres-propios`,
`!arroba-y-punto-en-una-dirección-de-correo`, `!formato-de-fecha`

### Tarea 2 — mensaje a un amigo (30–40 palabras)

**Band-2 requirement.** All five content prompts addressed, greeting and
farewell present, intelligible despite errors.

`requires:` saludar, estar, ciudad, describir, casa, habitación, hacer, fin de
semana, ir, `!despedida-epistolar`, `!futuro-o-presente-con-valor-de-futuro`,
`!punto-final`
