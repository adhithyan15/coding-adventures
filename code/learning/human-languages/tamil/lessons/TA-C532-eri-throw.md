## You'll want to know: எறி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C532-SANDAI532-05]; assesses=[] -->

**எறி** — *eṟi* — "to hurl; to throw".

> அவள் கல்லை ஏரியில் எறிந்தாள்.
>
> *avaḷ kallai ēriyil eṟintāḷ.*
>
> She threw the stone into the lake.

Past (I …): எறிந்தேன், *eṟintēṉ*.

Close to வீசு (*vīcu*); எறி stresses a hard throw at something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**எறி** (*eṟi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 859 gives எறி (*eṟi*), "to throw, to discharge, to fling".
