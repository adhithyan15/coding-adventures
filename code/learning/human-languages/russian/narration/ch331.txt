Russian, chapter 331: Dough.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
тесто (tésto) — dough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to stir, then the Russian for a ladle.

You'll want to know: тесто.
тесто (tésto) — "dough".
Тесто должно постоять час. Tésto dolzhnó postoyát chas. The dough has to rest for an hour.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
тесто (tésto) is inherited through Old East Slavic těsto from Proto-Slavic těsto "dough" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
месить (mesít) — to knead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a ladle, then the Russian for dough.

You'll want to know: месить.
месить (mesít) — "to knead".
Бабушка месит тесто. Bábushka mésit tésto. Grandma is kneading the dough.
Imperfective; present мешу, месишь. Perfective замесить (zamesít).

The word, taken apart.
месить (mesít) is inherited from Proto-Slavic měsiti, from PIE meyḱ- "to mix" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
раскатывать (raskátyvat) — to roll out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for dough, then the Russian for to knead.

You'll want to know: раскатывать.
раскатывать (raskátyvat) — "to roll out".
Он раскатал тесто на столе. On raskatál tésto na stolé. He rolled out the dough on a table.
Imperfective; perfective раскатать (raskatát).

The word, taken apart.
раскатывать (raskátyvat) is built on рас- "apart" and катать "to roll", an inherited Slavic verb (EtymDB lists катать "to roll" with no parent).

Lesson 4 of 5.
лепить (lepít) — to shape, to make (by hand); to model.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to knead, then the Russian for to roll out.

You'll want to know: лепить.
лепить (lepít) — "to shape, to make (by hand); to model".
Вся семья лепила пельмени. Vsya semyá lepíla pelméni. The whole family was making dumplings.
Imperfective; present леплю, лепишь. Perfective слепить (slepít). Лепить снеговика — to make a snowman.

The word, taken apart.
лепить (lepít) is inherited from Proto-Slavic lěpiti "to smear, to stick", from PIE leyp- "to stick" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
начинка (nachínka) — a filling, a stuffing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to roll out, then the Russian for to shape.

You'll want to know: начинка.
начинка (nachínka) — "a filling, a stuffing".
Начинка в пирогах была из капусты. Nachínka v pirogákh bylá iz kapústy. The filling in the pies was cabbage.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
начинка (nachínka) is built on начинить "to stuff", which is usually traced to чин "order": to fill in good order. EtymDB lists no parent.
