## Added — ी and ो print stroke-order filmstrips

ी and ो now have a cited pen path, from native writers' pen traces in HP Labs
India's LipiTk Devanagari recognizer (counts and shares only). Each strip
draws the sign alone.

- **New filmstrips (3 lessons, 58 -> 61):** HI-S116-vowel-sign-ii and
  HI-W12-ii-matra (ी: one run from the hook's lower tip, over the top and
  down the stem), HI-S150-vowel-sign-o (ो: the stem down, a lift, then the
  flag from its upper-left tip).
- **The grey bar.** The printed sign carries a short piece of headline that
  the strip leaves grey: in a word it is part of the headline, which writers
  draw last, once, across the whole word.
- **Rewritten:** HI-S116 and HI-S150's Writing blocks no longer say the book
  gives no stroke order; they point at the strip and explain the grey bar.
  Both stay under the duration budget (241s and 226s computed).
- **Not yet:** HI-W128-vowel-sign-i (ि: native writers split three ways),
  HI-S134-vowel-sign-au (ौ) and HI-W12-ai-matra (ै: the traces split).
