## You'll want to know: abboccare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C501-PESCA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**abboccare** — "to bite (the bait); to fall for it".

> Dopo un'ora finalmente un pesce ha abboccato.
>
> *After an hour a fish finally bit.*

Also of people: *ha abboccato* — he fell for it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abboccare* is built on *bocca* "mouth", inherited from Latin *bucca* "cheek, mouth" (Lewis & Short).
