## You'll want to know: همدیگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C504-FRIENDS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**همدیگر** (*hamdigar*) — "each other".

> آنها از کودکی همدیگر را میشناسند.
>
> *ânhâ az kudaki hamdigar râ mishenâsand.*
>
> They've known each other since childhood.

*ham* 'together' + *digar* 'other'. Formal Persian also says *yekdigar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hamdigar* is built on two native Persian words: *ham*, 'together, same', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *digar*, 'other'.
