## You'll want to know: होळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C249-HOLI249-01]; assesses=[] -->

**होळी** — *hoḷī* — "the spring festival of colours, Holi".

> होळी बसंत मांय आवै है।
>
> *hoḷī basant mā̃y āvai hai.*
>
> Holi comes in spring.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**होळी** (*hoḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *holā*, "the spring festival". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *holī* and Gujarati *hoḷī*, with this book's ळ (CDIAL 14182).
