## You'll want to know: कडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कडा** — *kaḍā* — "a cliff; an edge".

> कड्यावरून धबधबा खाली पडतो.
>
> *kaḍyāvarūn dhabdhabā khālī paḍto.*
>
> The waterfall drops down from the cliff.

Masculine.

Before an ending कडा becomes कड्या-: कड्यावरून, from the cliff.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कडा** (*kaḍā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *kaḍā*, "precipice", under Sanskrit *kaḍāra* (CDIAL 2655).
