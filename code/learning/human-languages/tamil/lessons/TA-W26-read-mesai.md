# மேசை — two front-hanging signs in one word

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C70-ROOM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the page: you can say *mēsai* and *kaṭṭil*. Now read the first one.

## Script you'll notice: மேசை — two front-hanging signs in one word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-SCRIPT-READ-MESAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**மேசை** is the first word you have read that carries a front-hanging sign on **each** of its
two letters.

| piece | | |
|---|---|---|
| **மே** | *mē* | ம with the **ே** sign, the one you read twice in மேலே |
| **சை** | *sai* | ச with the **ை** sign, the one you read at the end of சட்டை and கடை |

> **மே · சை → மேசை**

Both signs are drawn to the **left** of their letter and said **after** it. So the eye moves
left-to-right across four marks while the mouth says only two beats, and the order of the two
is not the order they were printed in. Read it slowly once and let that happen on purpose.
