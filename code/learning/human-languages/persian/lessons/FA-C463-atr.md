## You'll want to know: عطر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C463-SOWGHATI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عطر** (*'atr*) — "a perfume, a scent".

> خواهرم برای تولدم یک عطر خرید.
>
> *Khâharam barâ-ye tavallodam yek 'atr kharid.*
>
> My sister bought me a perfume for my birthday.

*'Atr-e gol-e mohammadi* — rose scent, made from the damask rose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'atr* was borrowed from Arabic *ʿiṭr* 'perfume' (EtymDB).
