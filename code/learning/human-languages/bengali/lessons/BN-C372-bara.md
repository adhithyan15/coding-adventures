# বাড়া (bāṛā) — to grow; to go up (in amount)

## You'll want to know: বাড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C372-BARA372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বাড়া** — *bāṛā* — "to grow; to go up (in amount)".

> দাম বাড়লে লোকে কম কেনে।
>
> *dām bāṛle loke kôm kene.*
>
> When prices go up, people buy less.

Past (he/she …): সেই নদীর জল বাড়ল, *sei nôdīr jôl bāṛlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাড়া** (*bāṛā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *várdhatē*, grows (CDIAL 11376 lists Bengali bāṛā). It is the partner of the taught বাড়ানো: prices বাড়ে by themselves; someone বাড়ায় them.
