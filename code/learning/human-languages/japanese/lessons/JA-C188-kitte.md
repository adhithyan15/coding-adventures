## You'll want to know: きって
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C188-POST188-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きって** — *kitte* — "stamp; postage stamp".

> **ゆうびんきょくで きってを かいます。** — *yūbinkyoku de kitte o kaimasu* — "I buy stamps at the post office."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きって** (*kitte*) is native Japanese, built from *kiri*, the stem of *kiru*, "to cut", and *te*, "hand", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
