## You'll want to know: うけもつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C364-SONGAI364-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うけもつ** — *ukemotsu* — "to be in charge of".

> **せんぱいが やめたら、あにが しごとを うけもつでしょう。** — *senpai ga yametara, ani ga shigoto o ukemotsu deshō* — "If my senior leaves, my brother will probably take charge of the work."

Polite **うけもちます**, *ukemochimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが しごとを うけもちました。**, *ani ga shigoto o ukemochimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うけもつ** (*ukemotsu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *uke*, "receiving (ukeru)" and *motsu*, "to hold", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
