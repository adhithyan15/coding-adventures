## You'll want to know: o mergulho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C506-AGUA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o mergulho** — "dive; (quick) swim".

> Demos um mergulho no mar antes do almoço.
>
> *We had a quick swim in the sea before lunch.*

*Dar um mergulho* — to have a dip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mergulho* comes from *mergulhar* "to dive", which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin form built on Latin *mergere* "to plunge, to dip" (Lewis & Short).
