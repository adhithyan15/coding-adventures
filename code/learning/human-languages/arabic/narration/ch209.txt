Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
An injection? (حقنة, ḥuqna.) An operation? (عملية, ʿamaliyya.) A bruise? (كدمة, kadma.) A burn? (حرق, ḥarq.) A stomach cramp? (مغص, maghaṣ.) Usually? (غالبا, ghāliban.) Immediately? (فورا, fawran.) Later? (لاحقا, lāḥiqan.) A date; history? (تاريخ, tārīkh.) A moment? (لحظة, laḥẓa.) Previously? (سابقا, sābiqan.) Finally? (أخيرا, akhīran.) During? (أثناء, athnāʾa.) A century? (قرن, qarn.) Weekly? (أسبوعي, usbūʿī.) Towards? (نحو, naḥwa.) An area? (منطقة, minṭaqa.) A centre? (مركز, markaz.) A capital city? (عاصمة, ʿāṣima.) Where? (أين, ayna.)
