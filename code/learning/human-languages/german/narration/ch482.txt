German, chapter 482: Kleine Verletzungen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
verstauchen — to sprain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to scratch, then the German for to put cream on.

You'll want to know: verstauchen.
verstauchen — "to sprain".
Beim Fußball habe ich mir den Fuß verstaucht. I sprained my foot playing football.
With a dative of the person: sich den Fuß verstauchen. Inseparable: hat verstaucht.

The word, taken apart.
verstauchen is built on ver- and stauchen "to compress, to jolt" (EtymDB): a joint pushed out of shape.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Nach dem Sturz hatte er eine Prellung am Bein. After the fall he had a bruise on his leg.
Plural: die Prellungen. In speech also der blaue Fleck.

The word, taken apart.
Prellung is built on prellen "to strike, to bump" (EtymDB) with -ung; the older origin of prellen is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Verband — bandage, dressing; association.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to sprain, then the German for bruise.

You'll want to know: der Verband.
der Verband — "bandage, dressing; association".
Die Krankenschwester legt einen Verband an. The nurse puts on a bandage.
Plural: die Verbände. Also an association or federation: der Fußballverband.

The word, taken apart.
Verband is built on verbinden "to bind up, to bandage", from binden "to bind", inherited from Old High German bintan, from Proto-Germanic bindaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
desinfizieren — to disinfect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for bruise, then the German for bandage.

You'll want to know: desinfizieren.
desinfizieren — "to disinfect".
Der Arzt desinfiziert die Wunde. The doctor disinfects the wound.
Regular, Perfekt without ge-: hat desinfiziert.

The word, taken apart.
desinfizieren is built on des- "un-" and infizieren "to infect", a learned borrowing from Latin inficere "to stain, to taint" (Lewis & Short).
