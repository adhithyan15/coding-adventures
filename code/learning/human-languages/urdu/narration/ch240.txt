Urdu, chapter 240: To push, To put out, To drive, To halt, To turn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دھکیلنا (dhakelnā) — to push.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to stare, then the Urdu for to search.

You'll want to know: دھکیلنا.
دھکیلنا — dhakelnā — "to push".

The word, taken apart.
دھکیلنا (dhakelnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhakk, "push, strike", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6701).

Lesson 2 of 5.
بجھانا (bujhānā) — to put out (a fire or a light), to extinguish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to search, then the Urdu for to push.

You'll want to know: بجھانا.
بجھانا — bujhānā — "to put out (a fire or a light), to extinguish".

The word, taken apart.
بجھانا (bujhānā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vijjhāpayati, "extinguishes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 11703). It is the causing form of vijjhāyati, "is burnt out"; it is a different word from būjhnā, "to understand" (CDIAL 9279).

Lesson 3 of 5.
چلانا (chalānā) — to drive, to operate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to push, then the Urdu for to put out.

You'll want to know: چلانا.
چلانا — chalānā — "to drive, to operate".

The word, taken apart.
چلانا (chalānā) is the causing form of chalnā, "to move, to go": to drive is to make something go. CDIAL lists chalnā under Sanskrit calati, "moves" (CDIAL 4715).
