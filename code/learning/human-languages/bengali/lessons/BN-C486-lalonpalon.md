## You'll want to know: লালনপালন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C486-LALON486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**লালনপালন** — *lālônpālôn* — "upbringing; raising (a child)".

> বাবার মৃত্যুর পরে মামা অরুণের লালনপালন করলেন।
>
> *bābār mrityur pôre māmā ôruner lālônpālôn kôrlen.*
>
> After his father's death, Arun's uncle brought him up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লালনপালন** (*lālônpālôn*) pairs Sanskrit *lālana*, "caressing, fondling", with *pālana*, "guarding, nourishing" (Monier-Williams); পালা (taught), to rear, is from the second.
