Tamil, chapter 129: Lion, Bear, Monkey, Rabbit, Deer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சிங்கம் (ciṅkam) — a lion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an elephant, then the Tamil for a tiger.

You'll want to know: சிங்கம்.
சிங்கம் — ciṅkam — "a lion".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does சிங்கம் (ciṅkam) mean? ("A lion".) Say it once more.
