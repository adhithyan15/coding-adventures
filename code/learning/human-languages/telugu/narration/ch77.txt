Telugu, chapter 77: Where You Are From.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నుంచి (nunci) — from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: what did kaṣṭaṁ mean?

You'll want to know: నుంచి.
నుంచి (nunci) — "from".
You already have -కు, "to", and -లో, "in". నుంచి (nunci) is the third of the set and the one an introduction needs.
Like them it follows its noun: గ్రామం నుంచి (nunci) — "from the village".

Grammar Lens: the third relation word.
Three now sit behind a noun and do three different jobs: -లో puts you inside, -కు sends you toward, నుంచి (nunci) takes you out of. Telugu has no words in front of nouns at all; everything a preposition would do in English is done by a piece glued to the back.

What you've built.
The direction you came out of.

Lesson 2 of 5.
పట్టణం (paṭṭaṇaṁ) — a town.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: what did nunci mean?

You'll want to know: పట్టణం.
పట్టణం (paṭṭaṇaṁ) — "a town".
You already have గ్రామం, the village. పట్టణం (paṭṭaṇaṁ) is the larger settlement, the one with the shops and the bargaining in it.
నేను పట్టణం (paṭṭaṇaṁ) నుంచి (nunci) — "I am from a town." That sentence needs no verb, exactly like నా పేరు.

Lesson 3 of 5.
దేశం (dēśaṁ) — a country.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: what did paṭṭaṇaṁ mean?

You'll want to know: దేశం.
దేశం (dēśaṁ) — "a country".
Sanskrit tatsama, and the same word behind Hindi desh. It scales the ladder up once more: గ్రామం, పట్టణం (paṭṭaṇaṁ), దేశం (dēśaṁ).
ఏ దేశం (dēśaṁ)? — "which country?" is a question you will be asked in the first minute of any exam interview.

What you've built.
The largest place you can be from.

భారతదేశం (Bhāratadēśaṁ) — "India".
Your first Telugu proper name, and you can already read half of it: భారత plus the దేశం (dēśaṁ) you learned a moment ago.
నేను భారతదేశం (Bhāratadēśaṁ) నుంచి (nunci) — "I am from India." Swap in the name of wherever you are actually from and the sentence still works.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ముప్పై (muppai) — thirty (and the tens above it).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: what did Bhāratadēśaṁ mean?

You'll want to know: ముప్పై.
ముప్పై (muppai) — "thirty".
Your counting stopped at ఇరవై, twenty. The tens above it are built the same way, from the units you already know:
ముప్పై (muppai) 30, నలభై 40, యాభై 50, అరవై 60, డెబ్బై 70, ఎనభై 80, తొంభై 90, వంద 100. Every one but వంద ends in the same -ై twenty does.

Grammar Lens: an age, at last.
You have long been able to ask నీ వయసెంత? and unable to answer it, because the numbers stopped at twenty and no word for a year existed. With the tens and with సంవత్సరం, the answer is now sayable: నాకు ముప్పై (muppai) సంవత్సరాలు — "I am thirty", literally "to me, thirty years". Note the dative నాకు: an age is something that has arrived at you, like every other Telugu experience.

What you've built.
Numbers past twenty: an age, a price, a year.
