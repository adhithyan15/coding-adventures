## You'll want to know: enfadarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C487-ANIMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**enfadarse** — "to get angry".

> Mi hermana se enfada si llego tarde.
>
> *My sister gets angry if I arrive late.*

In much of Latin America people say *enojarse*.
