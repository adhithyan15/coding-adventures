## You'll want to know: волонтёр
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C368-POMOSHCH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**волонтёр** (*volontyór*) — "a volunteer".

> Волонтёры помогают в больнице.
>
> *Volontyóry pomogáyut v bolnítse.*
>
> Volunteers help at the hospital.

Masculine. *Доброволец* means the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**волонтёр** was borrowed from French *volontaire* (EtymDB) "voluntary, a volunteer", from Latin *voluntās* "will, choice" (Lewis & Short).
