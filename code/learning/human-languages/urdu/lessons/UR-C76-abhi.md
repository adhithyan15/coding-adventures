## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ابھی** (*abhī*) is *ab*, "now", with the emphatic *-hī*. CDIAL lists *ab* and *abhī* under Sanskrit *evam eva*, "exactly so", crossed with a pointing *a-* (CDIAL 2528).
