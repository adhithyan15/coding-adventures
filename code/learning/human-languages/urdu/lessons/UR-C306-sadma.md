# صدمہ (sadmā) — a shock (of grief or fright)

## You'll want to know: صدمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C306-DHAMAKA306-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صدمہ** — *sadmā* — "a shock (of grief or fright)".

> ماں کو خبر سے صدمہ ہوا۔
>
> *māṅ ko khabar se sadmā huā.*
>
> The news came as a shock to the mother.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صدمہ** (*sadmā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣadma*, "a blow, a collision"; in Urdu it is the blow of bad news. The one shocked takes **کو**.
