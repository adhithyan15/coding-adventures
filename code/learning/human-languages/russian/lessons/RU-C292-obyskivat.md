## You'll want to know: обыскивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C292-DOPROS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**обыскивать** (*obýskivat*) — "to search (a person or a place)".

> Полицейские обыскали квартиру.
>
> *Politséyskiye obyskáli kvartíru.*
>
> The police searched the flat.

Imperfective; perfective *обыскать* (*obyskát*), *обыщу*. *Искать* is to look for; *обыскивать* goes through a place. *Обыск* — a search.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обыскивать* is built on *об-* "all round" and *искать* "to look for", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *jĭskati*, from PIE *h₂eys-* "to wish, to seek" (EtymDB).
