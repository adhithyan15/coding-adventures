## You'll want to know: yǎnchū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C257-SHOW257-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnchū* — "to perform on stage".

> *jīntiān wǎnshang tāmen yǎnchū* — They're performing tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnchū* is built from *yǎn* and 出 'to come out', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yenX* and *tsyhwit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'wide' and 'water flowing far'. The sense 'to perform' is not given there, so how it came is not settled.
