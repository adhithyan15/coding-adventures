# લલકારવું (lalkārvũ) — to sing out

## You'll want to know: લલકારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C553-NAGARU553-03]; assesses=[] -->

**લલકારવું** — *lalkārvũ* — "to sing out".

> જો મીરા ગીત લલકારશે તો બધા સાથે ગાશે.
>
> *jo Mīrā gīt lalkārśe to badhā sāthe gāśe.*
>
> If Mira sings out a song, everyone will sing along.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગીત લલકાર્યું, *teṇe gīt lalkāryũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લલકારવું** (*lalkārvũ*) is built on *lalkār*, "a challenge, a shout", which CDIAL files under *\*lallakka* (CDIAL 10973): to sing out loud and clear.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
