## You'll want to know: ಹೆದರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C490-BHAYA490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆದರು** — *hedaru* — "to be afraid; to fear".

> ಮಗು ಕತ್ತಲೆಗೆ ಹೆದರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *magu kattalege hedaruttade.*
>
> The child is afraid of the dark.

Past (I …): ಹೆದರಿದೆ, *hedaride*.

What you fear takes -ಗೆ: ಕತ್ತಲೆಗೆ ಹೆದರು, to be afraid of the dark. The noun for the feeling is ಭಯ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆದರು** (*hedaru*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4401 lists Kannada *bedaṟu* and *hedaṟu*, "to be alarmed, to take fright", with a noun meaning "fright" beside them. The b- and h- forms are two shapes of one old verb.
