## You'll want to know: すいえい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-03]; assesses=[] -->

**すいえい** — *suiei* — "swimming; swimming as a sport".

> **なつやすみに すいえいを ならいました。** — *natsuyasumi ni suiei o naraimashita* — "I took swimming lessons in the summer holidays."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいえい** (*suiei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sui*, "water" (Middle Chinese *sywijX*) and *ei*, "swim" (*hjwaengH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
