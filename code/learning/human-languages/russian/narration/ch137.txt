Russian, chapter 137: Recently, for a Long Time, Before, Last, To Happen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
недавно (nedávno) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for (to) nowhere, then the Russian for what for.

You'll want to know: недавно.
недавно (nedávno) — "recently". Russian's past usually ends in -л, with -ла for a woman: Недавно я читал книгу. — nedávno ya chitál knígu — "Recently I read a book." A woman says читала.

Lesson 2 of 5.
давно (davnó) — long ago; for a long time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for what for, then the Russian for recently.

You'll want to know: давно.
давно (davnó) — "long ago; for a long time". With the present it means "for a long time": Я давно здесь живу. — ya davnó zdes zhivú — "I have lived here for a long time."

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: раньше.
раньше (ránshe) — "before, earlier". Раньше здесь был парк. — ránshe zdes byl park — "There used to be a park here." был is the past of быть.

Lesson 4 of 5.
прошлый (próshlyy) — last, past.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for long ago; for a long time, then the Russian for before.

You'll want to know: прошлый.
прошлый (próshlyy) — "last, past". It agrees with its noun: на прошлой неделе — na próshloy nedéle — "last week"; в прошлом году — "last year".

You'll want to know: случиться.
случиться (sluchítsya) — "to happen". Что случилось? — shto sluchílos — "What happened?" случилось is its past with "it".
