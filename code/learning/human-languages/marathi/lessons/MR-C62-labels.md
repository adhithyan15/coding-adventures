# (reading) — six words, no sentence around them

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-GHAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Every word below you have already written. Not one is new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nothing tells you which is coming.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SKILL-READ-LABELS]; assesses=[MR-LEX-KHOLI, MR-LEX-PAANI] -->

> नाव
> शहर
> भाषा
> घर
> खोली
> पाणी

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once. Do not sound out each letter — look at the
whole word and let it arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Again, and notice which came at once and which you had to assemble.
Both are fine. The difference is what practice removes.

## You'll want to know: a word with nothing to lean on
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-GHAR, MR-LEX-KHOLI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The first three are form labels — the ones you filled in when you
wrote your own profile. The last three are things.

[PAUSE 2s] A label gives you no sentence to lean on. There is no **आहे** at the
end to tell you what is going on, and no verb to wait for. One word, and it
carries everything.

[PAUSE 2s] That is why six is enough for one sitting.
