## You'll want to know: یتیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C490-STEP490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**یتیم** — *yatīm* — "an orphan".

> وہ یتیم بچہ چچا کے گھر رہتا ہے۔
>
> *voh yatīm bachchā chachā ke ghar rahtā hai.*
>
> That orphan boy lives at his uncle's house.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**یتیم** (*yatīm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *yatīm*, "an orphan".
