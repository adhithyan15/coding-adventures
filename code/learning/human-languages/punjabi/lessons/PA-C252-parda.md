## You'll want to know: ਪਰਦਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C252-GHR252-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਦਾ** — *pardā* — "a curtain".

> ਖਿੜਕੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਨੀਲਾ ਪਰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *khiṛkī utte ikk nīlā pardā hai.*
>
> There is a blue curtain at the window.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਦਾ** (*pardā*) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian *parda*, "a curtain, a veil".
