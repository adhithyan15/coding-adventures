# ങ (ṅa) — the nasal in നിങ്ങൾ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-148, ML-SCRIPT-C113-SHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ഫ and ശ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **നിങ്ങൾ** (*niṅṅaḷ*), "you (respectful)".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ങ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-NGA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ങ** — *ṅa*, the nasal made where *k* is made.

It is inside **നിങ്ങൾ** (*niṅṅaḷ*), the respectful "you", where it is written
**twice**, one on top of the other, for the long *ṅṅ*.

## Writing: ങ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-NGA-01] -->

Put your pen on ങ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
