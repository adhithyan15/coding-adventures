Lesson 1 of 5.
die Stimmung — an atmosphere, a mood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a worry, then the German for a voice.

You'll want to know: die Stimmung.
die Stimmung — "an atmosphere, a mood".

The word, taken apart.
Stimmung is the verb "to tune", built on Stimme, "voice" (EtymDB 2.1), + -ung: the key a room or a mood is tuned to.
