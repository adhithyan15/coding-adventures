## You'll want to know: દુઆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C489-KUSHAL489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દુઆ** — *duā* — "a prayer for someone; a blessing".

> દાદીની દુઆથી મારો તાવ મટી ગયો.
>
> *dādīnī duāthī māro tāv maṭī gayo.*
>
> Thanks to my grandmother's prayers my fever went away.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દુઆ** (*duā*) is usually traced to Arabic *duʿā*, "a prayer, a call", which reached Gujarati through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
