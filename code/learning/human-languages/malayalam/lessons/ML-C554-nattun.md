# നാത്തൂൻ (nāttūn) — a sister-in-law

## You'll want to know: നാത്തൂൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C554-MATAPITA554-04]; assesses=[] -->

**നാത്തൂൻ** — *nāttūn* — "a sister-in-law".

> നാത്തൂനും ഞാനും ഒരുമിച്ച് ചന്തയിൽ പോയി.
>
> *nāttūnuṁ ñānuṁ orumiccŭ cantayil pōyi.*
>
> My sister-in-law and I went to the market together.

A husband's sister or a brother's wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നാത്തൂൻ** (*nāttūn*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3644 gives it as a sister-in-law, a brother's wife (DEDR 3644).
