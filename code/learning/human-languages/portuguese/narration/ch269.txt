Portuguese, chapter 269: Primeiro de tudo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
primeiramente — firstly, first of all.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a jungle, then the Portuguese for a slipper.

You'll want to know: primeiramente.
primeiramente — "firstly, first of all".
Primeiramente, abrimos as janelas; depois, limpámos a casa. First of all we opened the windows; then we cleaned the house.
A careful word for speeches and written accounts; in speech people usually say primeiro.

The word, taken apart.
primeiramente is built on primeiro "first", which is inherited from Latin prīmārius "of the first rank", from prīmus "first" (Lewis & Short), with the adverb ending -mente.

Lesson 3 of 5.
seguidamente — next, then (in a series).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for firstly, then the Portuguese for initially.

You'll want to know: seguidamente.
seguidamente — "next, then (in a series)".
Lavámos os pratos e, seguidamente, fomos dormir. We washed the dishes and next we went to bed.
Formal, common in reports. In everyday talk, a seguir.

The word, taken apart.
seguidamente is built on seguido "followed", the past participle of seguir, which comes from Latin sequī "to follow" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
posteriormente — later, subsequently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for initially, then the Portuguese for next.

You'll want to know: posteriormente.
posteriormente — "later, subsequently".
O homem foi ao hospital e, posteriormente, voltou para casa. The man went to the hospital and later went back home.
Formal; you hear it on the news. In speech, mais tarde or depois.

The word, taken apart.
posteriormente is built on a Latin comparative, posterior "coming after, later" (EtymDB), from post "after", with the adverb ending -mente.

Lesson 5 of 5.
por fim — finally, in the end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for next, then the Portuguese for later.

You'll want to know: por fim.
por fim — "finally, in the end".
Esperámos duas horas e, por fim, o comboio chegou. We waited two hours and in the end the train arrived.
Close to finalmente; it closes a list of steps or a story.

The word, taken apart.
fim is inherited from Latin fīnis "boundary, end" (EtymDB); por comes from Latin pro "for, in front of".
