## You'll want to know: રેઈનકોટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C434-SHOWER434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રેઈનકોટ** — *reīnkoṭ* — "a raincoat".

> ચોમાસામાં હું રેઈનકોટ પહેરીને નીકળું છું.
>
> *comāsāmā̃ hũ reīnkoṭ paherīne nīkḷũ chũ.*
>
> In the monsoon I put on a raincoat to go out.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રેઈનકોટ** (*reīnkoṭ*) is borrowed from English *raincoat*.
