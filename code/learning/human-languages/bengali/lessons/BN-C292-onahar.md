# অনাহার (ônāhār) — starvation; going without food

## You'll want to know: অনাহার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C292-KHORA292-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অনাহার** — *ônāhār* — "starvation; going without food".

> দুর্ভিক্ষে অনেক মানুষ অনাহারে মরল।
>
> *durbhikkhe ônek mānush ônāhāre môrlô.*
>
> Many people died of starvation in the famine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনাহার** (*ônāhār*) is Sanskrit *anāhāra*, 'not taking food' (Monier-Williams): *an-*, 'not', and *āhāra*, 'food, eating'.
