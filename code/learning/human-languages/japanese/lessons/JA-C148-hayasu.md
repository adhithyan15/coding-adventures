## You'll want to know: はやす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C148-HAIR148-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はやす** — *hayasu* — "to grow a beard; to let grow".

> **ふゆは ひげを はやします。** — *fuyu wa hige o hayashimasu* — "In winter I grow a beard."

Polite **はやします**, *hayashimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はやす** (*hayasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
