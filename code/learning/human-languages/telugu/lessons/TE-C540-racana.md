## You'll want to know: రచన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C540-SAHITYAM540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**రచన** — *racana* — "a piece of writing; a work".

> ఆ రచయిత కొత్త రచన బాగా అమ్ముడుపోయింది.
>
> *ā racayita kotta racana bāgā ammuḍupōyindi.*
>
> That writer's new work sold very well.

Plural: రచనలు, *racanalu*.

Plural రచనలు, a writer's works.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రచన** (*racana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *racanā*, "arrangement; a literary production, a composition" (Monier-Williams).
