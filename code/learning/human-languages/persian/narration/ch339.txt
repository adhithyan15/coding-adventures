Persian, chapter 339: Getting ready for Nowruz.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
عید ('eyd) — a festival, a holiday; the New Year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a will, then the Persian for an inheritance.

You'll want to know: عید.
عید ('eyd) — "a festival, a holiday; the New Year".
برای عید همه لباس نو میخرند. Barâ-ye 'eyd hame lebâs-e now mikharand. For the New Year everyone buys new clothes.
Said alone, 'eyd usually means Nowruz, the Persian New Year. 'Eyd mobârak! "Happy holiday!"

The word, taken apart.
'eyd is borrowed from Arabic 'īd, "holiday, feast" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 6.
خانه تکانی (khâne tekâni) — spring cleaning (before Nowruz).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an inheritance, then the Persian for a festival.

You'll want to know: خانه تکانی.
خانه تکانی (khâne tekâni) — "spring cleaning (before Nowruz)".
قبل از عید مادرم خانه تکانی میکند. Qabl az 'eyd mâdaram khâne tekâni mikonad. Before the New Year my mother does the spring cleaning.
Literally "house-shaking", from tekândan "to shake": the whole house is cleaned before Nowruz.

The word, taken apart.
khâne tekâni is built from Persian khâne "house" and tekâni "shaking", from the native verb tekândan "to shake".

Lesson 3 of 6.
سمنو (samanu) — samanu, a sweet wheat pudding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a festival, then the Persian for spring cleaning.

You'll want to know: سمنو.
سمنو (samanu) — "samanu, a sweet wheat pudding".
سمنو (samanu) یکی از هفت سین است. Samanu yeki az haft sin ast. Samanu is one of the seven things on the haft-sin spread.
A thick brown pudding of sprouted wheat, cooked for many hours; one of the haft sin.

The word, taken apart.
samanu is a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
سنجد (senjed) — oleaster, a dried silver berry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for spring cleaning, then the Persian for samanu.

You'll want to know: سنجد.
سنجد (senjed) — "oleaster, a dried silver berry".
سنجد (senjed) هم روی سفره هفت سین است. Senjed ham ru-ye sofre-ye haft sin ast. Oleaster is on the haft-sin spread too.
The dried fruit of the oleaster tree; on the haft sin it stands for love.

The word, taken apart.
senjed is a native Persian word; EtymDB connects it with the Indo-European root spey-, "thorn-like", fitting a thorny tree.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ماهی قرمز (mâhi qermez) — a goldfish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for samanu, then the Persian for oleaster.

You'll want to know: ماهی قرمز.
ماهی قرمز (mâhi qermez) — "a goldfish".
بچه ها ماهی قرمز را در تنگ آب گذاشتند. Bachche-hâ mâhi qermez râ dar tong-e âb gozâshtand. The children put the goldfish in a bowl of water.
Literally "red fish". A goldfish in a bowl sits on the Nowruz table.

The word, taken apart.
mâhi "fish" is inherited from Middle Persian māhīg (EtymDB). qermez "red" is borrowed from Arabic qirmiz, "crimson, kermes" (EtymDB), named after a red dye.
