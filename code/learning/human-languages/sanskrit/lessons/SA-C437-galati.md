## You'll want to know: गलति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C437-PRAVRSH437-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गलति** — *galati* — "drips; trickles".

> पत्रात् जलं गलति।
>
> *patrāt jalaṃ galati.*
>
> Water drips from the leaf.

Past (it …): जलं पत्रात् गलितवत्, *jalaṃ patrāt galitavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गलति** (*galati*) comes from the root √gal, "to drip, to ooze"; MW gives *galati* as to drip, drop, trickle, with the participle *galita* (MW).
