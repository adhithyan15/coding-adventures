Spanish, chapter 698: Nubes y sol.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
nublado — cloudy, overcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the drizzle, then the Spanish for to drizzle.

You'll want to know: nublado.
nublado — "cloudy, overcast".
Hoy está nublado. It is cloudy today.
Used with estar: está nublado.

The word, taken apart.
nublado is built on nublo "cloud", from Latin nūbilus "cloudy" (EtymDB), from nūbēs "cloud".

Lesson 2 of 5.
despejado — clear (sky); awake, alert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to drizzle, then the Spanish for cloudy.

You'll want to know: despejado.
despejado — "clear (sky); awake, alert".
Mañana el cielo estará despejado. Tomorrow the sky will be clear.
Una noche despejada — a clear night.

The word, taken apart.
despejado is the past form of despejar "to clear", which came from Portuguese despejar (EtymDB), built on des- and a verb meaning "to hinder".

You'll want to know: nublarse.
nublarse — "to cloud over".
Por la tarde se nubló. It clouded over in the afternoon.

nublarse comes from Latin nūbilāre "to be cloudy" (EtymDB), built on nūbilus "cloudy", from nūbēs "cloud".

Lesson 5 of 5.
despejar — to clear (up); to clear (a space).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for sunny, then the Spanish for to cloud over.

You'll want to know: despejar.
despejar — "to clear (up); to clear (a space)".
Después de la lluvia despejó. After the rain it cleared up.
Despejar la mesa — to clear the dishes away.

The word, taken apart.
despejar came from Portuguese despejar (EtymDB), built on des- and pejar "to hinder", from Latin pedica "a fetter for the feet".
