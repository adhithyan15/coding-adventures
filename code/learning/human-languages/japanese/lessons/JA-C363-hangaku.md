## You'll want to know: はんがく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C363-KAIKEI363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はんがく** — *hangaku* — "half price".

> **ばんに なったら、おべんとうが はんがくに なります。** — *ban ni nattara, obentō ga hangaku ni narimasu* — "In the evening, the boxed lunches go half price."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はんがく** (*hangaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *han*, "half" (Middle Chinese *panH*) and *gaku*, "sum" (*ngaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
