# സഹതാപം (sahatāpaṁ) — sympathy; pity

## You'll want to know: സഹതാപം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C520-ANUKAMPA520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**സഹതാപം** — *sahatāpaṁ* — "sympathy; pity".

> അയൽക്കാർ ഞങ്ങളോട് സഹതാപം കാണിച്ചു.
>
> *ayalkkār ñaṅṅaḷōṭŭ sahatāpaṁ kāṇiccu.*
>
> The neighbours showed us sympathy.

സഹ- with + താപം heat, pain: feeling the pain with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സഹതാപം** (*sahatāpaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *saha*, "with", and *tāpa*, "heat, pain" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
