Russian, chapter 504: Draughts, Dice and Cards.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
шашки (sháshki) — draughts, checkers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a crossword, then the Russian for to solve.

You'll want to know: шашки.
шашки (sháshki) — "draughts, checkers".
По вечерам дед играет с соседом в шашки. Po vecherám ded igráyet s sosédom v sháshki. In the evenings Grandad plays draughts with the neighbour.
Plural. Играть в шашки (sháshki) — to play draughts.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists шашки (sháshki) without a source; it is usually traced to шах 'check', from Persian shāh 'king', like шахматы 'chess'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
пешка (péshka) — a pawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to solve, then the Russian for draughts.

You'll want to know: пешка.
пешка (péshka) — "a pawn".
Пешка — самая слабая фигура в шахматах. Péshka — sámaya slábaya figúra v shákhmatakh. The pawn is the weakest piece in chess.
Feminine. Figuratively, a person of no importance.

The word, taken apart.
пешка (péshka) is built on пеший 'on foot', so a pawn is a foot soldier; EtymDB lists пешка (péshka) without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
кубик (kúbik) — a die; a small cube.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for draughts, then the Russian for a pawn.

You'll want to know: кубик.
кубик (kúbik) — "a die; a small cube".
Он бросил кубик, и выпала шестёрка. On brósil kúbik, i výpala shestyórka. He threw the die and got a six.
Masculine. A diminutive of куб — a cube. Кубики are also a child's building blocks.

The word, taken apart.
кубик (kúbik) is a diminutive of куб 'cube', which EtymDB traces through German Kubus and Latin cubus to Greek kýbos 'a cube'; EtymDB has no entry for кубик (kúbik).

Lesson 4 of 5.
колода (kolóda) — a pack of cards; a log.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pawn, then the Russian for a die.

You'll want to know: колода.
колода (kolóda) — "a pack of cards; a log".
В колоде не хватает одной карты. V kolóde ne khvatáyet odnóy kárty. One card is missing from the pack.
Feminine. Its older sense is a log or a block of wood.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists колода (kolóda) without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic kolda 'a log, a block'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
жульничать (zhúlnichat) — to cheat (at a game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a die, then the Russian for a pack of cards.

You'll want to know: жульничать.
жульничать (zhúlnichat) — "to cheat (at a game)".
С ним нельзя играть в карты: он жульничает. S nim nelzyá igrát v kárty: on zhúlnichayet. You can't play cards with him: he cheats.
Imperfective, colloquial; present жульничаю. Built on жулик — a crook.

The word, taken apart.
жульничать (zhúlnichat) is built on жулик 'a crook'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source, and the origin of жулик is not settled.
