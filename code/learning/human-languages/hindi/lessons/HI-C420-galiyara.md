## You'll want to know: गलियारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C420-DHALAN420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गलियारा** — *galiyārā* — "a corridor; a passage".

> ट्रेन के गलियारे में लोग खड़े थे।
>
> *ṭren ke galiyāre meṁ log khaṛe the.*
>
> People were standing in the corridor of the train.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गलियारा** (*galiyārā*) is built on गली, "a lane", from *\*galī*, "a defile, a lane", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4085): a lane inside a building or a train. CDIAL does not list galiyārā itself.
