## You'll want to know: یدک کش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C427-TAMIRGAH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**یدک کش** (*yadakkesh*) — "a tow truck, a breakdown lorry".

> یدک کش ماشین ما را به تعمیرگاه برد.
>
> *Yadakkesh mâshin-e mâ râ be ta'mirgâh bord.*
>
> The tow truck took our car to the garage.

*yadak* 'spare, in tow' + *kesh* 'puller', from *keshidan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yadakkesh* is built from *yadak* 'a led horse, a spare', usually traced to a Turkic word for a horse led on a rope, and *kesh* 'puller', from the native verb *keshidan* 'to pull'. EtymDB lists neither part.
