## Guided Practice: the distant band — every noun in the pantry, two at a time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C38-WA-01, AR-LEX-C38-AW-01, AR-CONCEPT-C33-SHAY-01, AR-CONCEPT-C33-HALIB-01, AR-CONCEPT-C33-SUKKAR-01, AR-CONCEPT-C33-QAHWA-01, AR-CONCEPT-C34-MILH-01, AR-CONCEPT-C34-LAHM-01, AR-CONCEPT-C34-JUBN-01, AR-CONCEPT-C34-TAAM-01, AR-CONCEPT-C36-KUB-01, AR-CONCEPT-C36-MILAQA-01, AR-CONCEPT-C35-JAR-01, AR-CONCEPT-C35-IBN-BINT-01, AR-CONCEPT-C35-SADIQ-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] This is what the chapter actually bought. Chapters thirty-three to
thirty-six taught a pantry and a family that could only ever be named one item at
a time — and every one of them is now combinable. Take them two at a time, cold,
from memory.

- [YOU SAY: the four drinks and their sweetener — *ash-shāy wa-l-qahwa wa-l-ḥalīb wa-s-sukkar*]
- [YOU SAY: the same four as a choice, two at a time — *shāy aw qahwa*, *ḥalīb aw sukkar*]
- [YOU SAY: what is on the table — *al-milḥ wa-l-laḥm wa-l-jubn*, and over it all *aṭ-ṭaʿām*]
- [YOU SAY: what you eat it with — *kūb wa-milʿaqa*]
- [YOU SAY: the people — *ibn wa-bint*, *jār wa-ṣadīq*]
- [YOU SAY: one of each, turned — *ash-shāy lākin lā al-qahwa*]
