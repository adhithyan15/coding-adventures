## You'll want to know: હુમલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C322-HUMLO322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**હુમલો** — *humlo* — "an attack".

> પરોઢે દુશ્મને કિલ્લા પર હુમલો કર્યો.
>
> *paroḍhe duśmane killā par humlo karyo.*
>
> At dawn the enemy attacked the fort.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હુમલો** (*humlo*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥamla*, "a charge, an attack". The place attacked takes **પર**: **કિલ્લા પર હુમલો**.
