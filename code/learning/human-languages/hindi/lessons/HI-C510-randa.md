## You'll want to know: रंदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C510-BADHAI510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रंदा** — *randā* — "a carpenter's plane".

> बढ़ई ने रंदे से दरवाज़ा चिकना किया।
>
> *Baṛhaī ne rande se darvāzā cikanā kiyā.*
>
> The carpenter made the door smooth with a plane.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रंदा** (*randā*) is usually traced to Persian *randa*, "a plane (the tool)".
