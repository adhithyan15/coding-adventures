## You'll want to know: स्याही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C284-BASTO284-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्याही** — *syāhī* — "ink".

> स्याही नीली है।
>
> *syāhī nīlī hai.*
>
> The ink is blue.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्याही** (*syāhī*) is usually traced to Persian *siyāhī*, "blackness", and so "ink".
