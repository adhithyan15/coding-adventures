## You'll want to know: कपास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C512-KARGHA512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कपास** — *kapās* — "cotton (the plant, the raw fibre)".

> किसान ने खेत में कपास बोई।
>
> *Kisān ne khet meṁ kapās boī.*
>
> The farmer sowed cotton in the field.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कपास** (*kapās*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *karpāsa*, "the cotton plant" (CDIAL 2877).
