Marathi, chapter 102: Snake, Peacock, Bear, Camel, Butterfly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साप (sāp) — a snake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an elephant, then the Marathi for a monkey.

You'll want to know: साप.
साप — sāp — "a snake".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sāp]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sāp, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sāp]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for an elephant, then the Marathi for a monkey, then say sāp again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does साप (sāp) mean? ("A snake".) Say it once more.
