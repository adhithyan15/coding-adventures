## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-OPINION-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-JUEDE-01, ZH-LEX-C280-YIJIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C280-HAOXIANG-01, ZH-LEX-C280-ZHEME-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-HUI-FUTURE-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-TONE-SANDHI-BU] -->

> **A:** 明天我们去山上 好不好
>
> *míngtiān wǒmen qù shān shàng, hǎo bu hǎo* — Shall we go up the mountain tomorrow?
>
> **B:** 我觉得这个意见很好
>
> *wǒ juéde zhège yìjiàn hěn hǎo* — I think that's a good suggestion.
>
> **A:** 你们有意见吗
>
> *nǐmen yǒu yìjiàn ma* — Any objections?
>
> **B:** *wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn, tā hǎoxiàng bù xiǎng qù* — None from me. He looks as if he doesn't want to go.
>
> **A:** 为什么
>
> *wèishénme* — Why?
>
> **B:** *wǒ juéde tā tài lèi le* — I think he's too tired.
>
> **A:** 今天这么冷 明天会冷吗
>
> *jīntiān zhème lěng, míngtiān huì lěng ma* — It's so cold today. Will it be cold tomorrow?
>
> **B:** 我觉得不会
>
> *wǒ juéde bú huì* — I don't think so.

我觉得 answers on its own too: 我觉得不会, I don't think it will. The 不 goes after 我觉得, with the verb it denies.

## What you've built — giving an opinion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-OPINION-01, ZH-SCRIPT-YI-IDEA-01, ZH-LEX-C280-YIJIAN-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-JUEDE-01, ZH-LEX-C280-HAOXIANG-01, ZH-LEX-C280-ZHEME-01] -->

Four ways to say how you see it, from sure to unsure:

- **我觉得他说得对** *wǒ juéde tā shuō de duì* — your opinion.
- **我没有意见** *wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn* — no objection.
- *tā hǎoxiàng bìng le* — a guess.
- **今天这么冷** *jīntiān zhème lěng* — so cold, here and now.

**Listen.** *Nǐ juéde míngtiān huì xiàyǔ ma?*

**Speak.** Give your opinion of a plan for the weekend, and one objection.
