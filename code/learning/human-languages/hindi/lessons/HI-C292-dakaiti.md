## You'll want to know: डकैती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C292-DAKAITI292-03]; assesses=[] -->

**डकैती** — *ḍakaitī* — "a robbery (by a gang)".

> अगर फिर डकैती हुई तो गाँव के लोग पुलिस को बुलाएँगे।
>
> *agar phir ḍakaitī huī to gā̃v ke log pulis ko bulāeṅge.*
>
> If there is another robbery, the villagers will call the police.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डकैती** (*ḍakaitī*) is *ḍakait*, a member of a robber gang, with the noun ending *-ī*; CDIAL 5543 lists *ḍakait* beside **डाकू** under *\*ḍākka*, "robbery". English *dacoit* and *dacoity* were taken from these words.
