## You'll want to know: চোষা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C486-LALON486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**চোষা** — *choshā* — "to suck".

> বাচ্চাটা বোতল থেকে দুধ চুষে খাচ্ছে।
>
> *bāchchāṭā botôl theke dudh chushe khāchchhe.*
>
> The baby is sucking milk from its bottle.

Past (he/she …): সেই শিশু বোতল থেকে দুধ চুষল, *sei shishu botôl theke dudh chushlô*.

The stem vowel rises in the verb forms: চুষি, I suck; চুষল, sucked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চোষা** (*choshā*) comes down from Sanskrit *cūṣati*, "sucks" (CDIAL 4898, which lists Bengali *cusā*, "to suck"); the forms চুষি and চুষছে keep the old u.
