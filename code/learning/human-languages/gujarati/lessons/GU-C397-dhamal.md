## You'll want to know: ધમાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C397-DOOR397-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમાલ** — *dhamāl* — "a commotion; a bustle".

> ટ્રેન આવી ત્યારે પ્લેટફોર્મ પર ધમાલ થઈ.
>
> *ṭren āvī tyāre pleṭphorm par dhamāl thaī.*
>
> When the train came in there was a commotion on the platform.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમાલ** (*dhamāl*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
