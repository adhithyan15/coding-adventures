## You'll want to know: der Schweiß
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C479-SCHNUPFEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schweiß** — "sweat".

> Nach dem Joggen war mein T-Shirt nass vor Schweiß.
>
> *After jogging my T-shirt was wet with sweat.*

No plural. The verb is *schwitzen* — to sweat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schweiß* is inherited from Middle High German *sweiz* and Old High German *sweiz*, from Proto-Germanic *swait-* "sweat" (EtymDB); English *sweat* is a cousin.
