## You'll want to know: टिकट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C205-BUS205-01]; assesses=[] -->

**टिकट** — *ṭikaṭ* — "a ticket".

> टिकट कठै मिलै है?
>
> *ṭikaṭ kaṭhai milai hai?*
>
> Where can I get a ticket?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टिकट** (*ṭikaṭ*) is borrowed from English *ticket*.
