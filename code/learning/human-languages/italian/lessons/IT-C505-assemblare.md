## You'll want to know: assemblare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C505-COLLEZIONI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**assemblare** — "to assemble, to put together".

> Ho assemblato il mobile da solo in due ore.
>
> *I put the piece of furniture together by myself in two hours.*

For furniture or parts; *montare* means the same in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assemblare* came from French *assembler* "to bring together", usually traced to Latin *simul* "together" (Lewis & Short).
