## You'll want to know: l'obbligo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C384-NORMA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'obbligo** — "the obligation, the duty".

> Non hai nessun obbligo di venire.
>
> *You are under no obligation to come.*

Plural *gli obblighi*. *Avere l'obbligo di* — to be required to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obbligo* is built on *obbligare* "to oblige", a learned borrowing from Latin *obligāre* "to bind" (Lewis & Short), built on *ligāre* "to tie".
