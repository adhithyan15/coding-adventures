## You'll want to know: ગતિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C206-ARRIVE206-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ગતિ** — *gati* — "speed".

> **ટ્રેનની ગતિ ધીમી હતી.** — *ṭrennī gati dhīmī hatī* — "The train was going slowly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગતિ** (*gati*) is Sanskrit *gáti*, "going, gait", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4009).
