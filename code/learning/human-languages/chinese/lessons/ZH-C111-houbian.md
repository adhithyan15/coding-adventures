## You'll want to know: hòubian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-02]; assesses=[] -->

*hòubian* — "behind; at the back".

> *xuéxiào hòubian yǒu yí ge gōngyuán* — There's a park behind the school.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hòubian* is built from hòu 'behind' and 边 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwX* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
