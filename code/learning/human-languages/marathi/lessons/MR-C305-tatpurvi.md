## You'll want to know: तत्पूर्वी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C305-GATVARSH305-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्पूर्वी** — *tatpūrvī* — "before that".

> कार्यक्रम सहा वाजता होता, तत्पूर्वी सगळे जेवले.
>
> *kāryakram sahā vājtā hotā, tatpūrvī sagḷe jevle.*
>
> The programme was at six; before that everyone ate.

Formal and written; in speech त्याच्या आधी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्पूर्वी** (*tatpūrvī*) is built from Sanskrit *tat*, 'that', and *pūrva*, 'before' (Monier-Williams), with a Marathi locative ending.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
