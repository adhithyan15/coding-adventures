## You'll want to know: ਭਤੀਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C332-KIN332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਤੀਜਾ** — *bhatījā* — "a brother's son".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਭਤੀਜਾ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā bhatījā har roz sakūl jāndā hai.*
>
> My brother's son goes to school every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਤੀਜਾ** (*bhatījā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhrātrīya*, "a brother's son"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhatījā*, "brother's son", under it (CDIAL 9672).
