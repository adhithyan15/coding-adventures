## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಯೋಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C407-SAMSHODHANE407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಯೋಗ** — *prayōga* — "an experiment".

> ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಣ್ಣ ಪ್ರಯೋಗ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *makkaḷu śāleyalli saṇṇa prayōga māḍidaru.*
>
> The children did a small experiment at school.

ಪ್ರಯೋಗಾಲಯ, a laboratory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಯೋಗ** (*prayōga*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prayoga*, "joining, use, application" (Monier-Williams).
