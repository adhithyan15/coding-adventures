## You'll want to know: জড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C539-BIGHNO539-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জড়ানো** — *jôṛāno* — "to get tangled up; to wrap round".

> ঘুড়ির সুতো গাছের ডালে জড়াল।
>
> *Ghuṛir suto gāchher ḍāle jôṛālô.*
>
> The kite string got tangled in the branches of the tree.

Past (he/she …): সেই দড়ি চাকায় জড়াল, *sei dôṛi chākāy jôṛālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জড়ানো** (*jôṛāno*) comes from a reconstructed *jaḍati*, "joins, sets", which Turner took from Dravidian; CDIAL lists Bengali *jaṛāna*, "to wrap round, to entangle" (CDIAL 5091).
