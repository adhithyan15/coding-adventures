Punjabi, chapter 75: Sand, Field, Cloud, Sun, Moon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਰੇਤ (ret) — sand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a lake, then the Punjabi for a stone.

You'll want to know: ਰੇਤ.
ਰੇਤ — ret — "sand".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ret]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ret, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ret]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a lake, then the Punjabi for a stone, then say ret again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਰੇਤ (ret) mean? ("Sand".) Say it once more.
