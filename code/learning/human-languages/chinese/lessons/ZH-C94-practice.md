## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-DESCRIBE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C94-DOU-01, ZH-LEX-C94-YIYANG-01, ZH-LEX-C94-NIANQING-01, ZH-LEX-C94-SHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C94-PANG-01, ZH-LEX-C92-YE-01, ZH-LEX-C33-FAMILY33-02] -->

> **A:** 他们都是你的同学吗
>
> *tāmen dōu shì nǐ de tóngxué ma* — Are they all your classmates?
>
> **B:** 对 我们都是学生
>
> *duì, wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng* — Yes, we're all students.
>
> **A:** *nǐmen dōu hěn niánqīng* — You're all so young.
>
> **B:** *wǒmen yíyàng dà* — We're the same age.
>
> **A:** 那个男人呢
>
> *nàge nánrén ne* — And that man?
>
> **B:** *tā shì wǒmen de lǎoshī, tā hěn gāo, hěn shòu* — He's our teacher. He's tall and thin.
>
> **A:** *nǐ de māo hěn pàng* — Your cat's fat.
>
> **B:** 也很大
>
> *yě hěn dà* — And big.

都 gathers everybody before it into one: 他们都, 我们都, 你们都. *yíyàng* matches them up; *gāo*, *shòu* and *pàng* tell them apart.
