## You'll want to know: 大小
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C108-SHOP108-04]; assesses=[] -->

**大小** — *dàxiǎo* — "size".

> **我试了大小很好** *wǒ shì le dàxiǎo hěn hǎo* — I've tried it on, and the size is fine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**大小** (*dàxiǎo*) is built from 大 'big' and 小 'small', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *daH* and *sjewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): how big or small a thing is.
