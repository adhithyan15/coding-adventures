Kannada, chapter 157: A Timetable, a Notice, the Rent, an Advert, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ವೇಳಾಪಟ್ಟಿ (vēḷāpaṭṭi) — a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to decide, then the Kannada for to expect.

You'll want to know: ವೇಳಾಪಟ್ಟಿ.
