## You'll want to know: 春游
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C431-OUTING431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**春游** — *chūnyóu* — "spring outing".

> **我们坐火车去春游** *wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù chūnyóu* — We're going on a spring outing by train.

The new character, in pieces: 游 — twelve strokes in three pieces: on the left the water piece of 洗, three strokes; in the middle a dot, a bar, a stroke that turns down and hooks, and a stroke falling to the left; on the right a short stroke falling to the left and a bar, with 子 under them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**春游** (*chūnyóu*) is built from 春 'spring' and 游 'to roam', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwin* and *yuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to float' and 'to let loose'; the sense 'to tour' is usually explained as a widening of it.
