# ಅದರ ನಂತರ (adara nantara) — first, after that, in the end

## You'll want to know: ಮೊದಲು … ಅದರ ನಂತರ … ಕೊನೆಗೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C310-FIRST-THEN-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C39-KAAPI-01, KA-LEX-C147-ACT147-01, KA-LEX-C92-WORK-01, KA-LEX-C210-KALA210-01, KA-ETYMON-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-02, KA-ETYMON-C18-GANTE-01, KA-LEX-C103-HOME-01] -->

> **ಮೊದಲು ನಾನು ಕಾಫಿ ಕುಡಿದೆ. ಅದರ ನಂತರ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋದೆ.**
>
> *modalu nānu kāphi kuḍide. adara nantara śālege hōde.* — "First I drank coffee. After that I went to school."

**ಮೊದಲು** (*modalu*, "first, at first") opens a story. **ಅದರ ನಂತರ** is **ನಂತರ**, "later", with **ಅದರ** (*adara*, "of that") in front: "after that". **ಆಮೇಲೆ** does the same job, and you will hear both.

> **ಕೊನೆಗೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹತ್ತು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದೆ.**
>
> *konege rātri hattu gaṇṭege manege bande.* — "In the end I came home at ten at night."

A told story often runs on three posts: **ಮೊದಲು** … **ಅದರ ನಂತರ** (or **ಆಮೇಲೆ**) … **ಕೊನೆಗೆ**.

## You'll want to know: before and after a thing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C310-BEFORE-AFTER-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C40-OOTA-01, KA-LEX-C298-VASTU298-02, KA-LEX-C07-ERADU-01, KA-ETYMON-C23-DINA-01] -->

> **ಊಟದ ನಂತರ ನಾವು ಸಿನಿಮಾಗೆ ಹೋದೆವು.**
>
> *ūṭada nantara nāvu sinimāge hōdevu.* — "After the meal we went to the cinema."

**ಊಟದ** is **ಊಟ** with **-ದ**, "of": "after (of) the meal". **ಮೊದಲು** works for "before", but it takes the "to" ending instead: **ಊಟಕ್ಕೆ ಮೊದಲು** (*ūṭakke modalu*, "before the meal"). Each word keeps its own ending.

With a number, **ನಂತರ** counts time:

> **ಎರಡು ದಿನಗಳ ನಂತರ ಅವನು ಬಂದನು.**
>
> *eraḍu dinagaḷa nantara avanu bandanu.* — "Two days later he came."

**ದಿನಗಳ** (*dinagaḷa*, "of days") is **ದಿನ** with the plural **-ಗಳ**.
