## You'll want to know: il malessere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C487-STOMACO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il malessere** — "feeling unwell; unease".

> Dopo pranzo ho avuto un leggero malessere.
>
> *After lunch I felt a little unwell.*

A vague feeling that something is wrong, in the body or the mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*malessere* is built from *male* "badly", from Latin *male*, and *essere* "to be", from Latin *esse* (Lewis & Short): a being-unwell.
