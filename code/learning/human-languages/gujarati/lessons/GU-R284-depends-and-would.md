# Review — it depends, and what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-FUTURE-01, GU-LEX-C283-NAHINTAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, we'll stay at home". (*jo varsād paḍśe, to ame ghare rahīśũ*.) Then "or else". (*nahī̃tar*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C284-ADHAR-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C284-PAR-ADHAR-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C284-DEPENDS-ANSWER-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C284-T-COUNTERFACTUAL-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C284-T-FORMS-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-JO-PRESENT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-PERFECTIVE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C282-KARTAN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C277-OPINION-ASK-01, GU-LEX-C213-WEATHER213-01, GU-LEX-C79-WEEK2-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: My parents are my support. What do you think?]
- [YOU SAY: It depends on the weather. Everything depends on the time.]
- [YOU SAY: Will we go or not? It depends on the rain. If it doesn't rain, we'll go.]
- [YOU SAY: If you're tired, stay at home. If I miss the train, I'll come by bus: the bus is slower than the train, but it's cheaper.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had had time, I would have come.]
- [YOU SAY: If the train hadn't been late, we would have arrived early.]
