## You'll want to know: احتیاط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C290-AILAN290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**احتیاط** — *ehtiyāt* — "caution; care".

> سڑک پر احتیاط سے چلیے۔
>
> *saṛak par ehtiyāt se chaliye.*
>
> Walk carefully on the road.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتیاط** (*ehtiyāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iḥtiyāṭ*, "caution, taking care". **احتیاط سے** is carefully.
