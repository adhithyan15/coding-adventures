## You'll want to know: la couture
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C496-COUTURE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la couture** — "sewing; fashion design".

> Je fais de la couture le samedi.
>
> *I do sewing on Saturdays.*

*La haute couture* — high fashion. *Une couture* is also a seam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la couture* is inherited, through Old French *cousture*, from Vulgar Latin *cōnsūtūra* "a seam" (EtymDB), built on *cōnsuere* "to sew together", from *suere* (Lewis & Short).
