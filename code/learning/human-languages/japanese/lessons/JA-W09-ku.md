# く — one angled stroke

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-YOKU, JA-SCRIPT-YO-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Write known **よ** and say *yoku*.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **く** — *ku*

One stroke. Begin high on the right, sweep down-left to the point, then turn and
sweep down-right. Keep it narrow and open. The turn is part of the same stroke.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KU-01, JA-LEX-YOKU] -->
1. Look at **く** for five seconds.
2. Hide it and write the one angled stroke.
3. Reveal, check, then join known **よ** + **く** → **よく**.
