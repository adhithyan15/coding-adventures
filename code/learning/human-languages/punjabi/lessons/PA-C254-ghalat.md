# ਗ਼ਲਤ (ghalat) — wrong; mistaken

## You'll want to know: ਗ਼ਲਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C254-FORM254-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗ਼ਲਤ** — *ghalat* — "wrong; mistaken".

> ਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਉੱਤੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਹੈ।
>
> *fāram utte merī umar ghalat hai.*
>
> My age is wrong on the form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗ਼ਲਤ** (*ghalat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ghalat*, "an error".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
