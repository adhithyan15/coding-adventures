## You'll want to know: சுக்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C485-MARUNTU485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**சுக்கு** — *cukku* — "dry ginger".

> சுக்கு போட்ட காபி தொண்டைக்கு நல்லது.
>
> *cukku pōṭṭa kāpi toṇṭaikku nallatu.*
>
> Coffee with dry ginger is good for the throat.

Fresh ginger is இஞ்சி; dried, it is சுக்கு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **சுக்கு** (*cukku*) is not settled; it is sometimes compared with Sanskrit *śuṇṭhī*, "dry ginger" (CDIAL 12515), but the link is uncertain.
