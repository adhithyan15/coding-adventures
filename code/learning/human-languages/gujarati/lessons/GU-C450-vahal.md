## You'll want to know: વહાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C450-VAHAL450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વહાલ** — *vahāl* — "fondness; tender affection".

> દાદીને નાના બાળક પર ઘણું વહાલ છે.
>
> *dādīne nānā bāḷak par ghaṇũ vahāl che.*
>
> Grandmother is very fond of the little child.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વહાલ** (*vahāl*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vallabha*, "dear, desired"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "affection" (CDIAL 11427).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
