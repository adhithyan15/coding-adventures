## You'll want to know: el colega
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C750-CONVIVIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el colega** — "the colleague; mate".

> Voy a cenar con unos colegas.
>
> *I am having dinner with some mates.*

At work it is a colleague; in Spain, colloquially, a mate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*colega* is a learned borrowing from Latin *collēga* "a partner in office" (EtymDB).
