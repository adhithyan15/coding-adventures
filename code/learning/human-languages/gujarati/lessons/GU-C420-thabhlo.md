## You'll want to know: થાંભલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C420-HOUSE420-04]; assesses=[] -->

**થાંભલો** — *thā̃bhlo* — "a post; a pole".

> રસ્તા પરના થાંભલા પર બત્તી છે.
>
> *rastā parnā thā̃bhlā par battī che.*
>
> There is a light on the post by the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થાંભલો** (*thā̃bhlo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *stambha*, "a post, a pillar"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 13682).
