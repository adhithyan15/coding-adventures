Portuguese, chapter 80: Pay, Use, Show, Clean, Cut.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pagar — to pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a thousand, then the Portuguese for zero.

You'll want to know: pagar.
pagar — "to pay".

The word, taken apart.
pagar from Latin pacare, "to pacify" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. Paying a debt pacifies the creditor.
