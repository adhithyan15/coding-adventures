## You'll want to know: विवेक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C394-BHAN394-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विवेक** — *vivek* — "good judgement; discretion".

> रागात माणूस विवेक विसरतो.
>
> *rāgāt māṇūs vivek visarto.*
>
> In anger a person forgets good judgement.

Masculine.

विवेकी, sensible.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विवेक** (*vivek*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *viveka*, "discrimination, judgement" (Monier-Williams).
