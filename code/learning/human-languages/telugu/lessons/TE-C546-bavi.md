## You'll want to know: బావి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C546-INTLO546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**బావి** — *bāvi* — "a dug well".

> మా పెరట్లో ఒక పాత బావి ఉంది.
>
> *mā peraṭlō oka pāta bāvi undi.*
>
> There is an old well in our backyard.

బావి నీళ్ళు, well water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బావి** (*bāvi*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *vāpī*, "a pond, a tank" (CDIAL 11529).
