Persian, chapter 372: Effects and reactions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تاثیر (ta'sir) — an influence, an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for therefore, then the Persian for considering.

You'll want to know: تاثیر.
تاثیر (ta'sir) — "an influence, an effect".
این کتاب تاثیر زیادی روی من داشت. in ketâb ta'sir-e ziyâdi ruye man dâsht. This book had a big influence on me.
Ta'sir dâshtan bar or ruye — to have an effect on. Plural ta'sirât. Many writers put a hamza on its alef; here it is written plain.

The word, taken apart.
تاثیر (ta'sir) is usually traced to Arabic ta'thīr "effect", a verbal noun usually explained as leaving a trace; Persian says the Arabic th as s.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تاثیر گذاشتن (ta'sir gozâshtan) — to influence, to have an impact.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for considering, then the Persian for an influence.

You'll want to know: تاثیر گذاشتن.
تاثیر گذاشتن (ta'sir gozâshtan) — "to influence, to have an impact".
معلمم روی زندگی من تاثیر گذاشت. mo'allemam ruye zendegi-ye man ta'sir gozâsht. My teacher had an impact on my life.
Ta'sir + gozâshtan "to put" (present stem gozâr-). Takes ruye or bar.

The word, taken apart.
تاثیر گذاشتن (ta'sir gozâshtan) is literally "to put an effect": ta'sir is usually traced to Arabic ta'thīr, and gozâshtan "to put, to leave" is a native Persian verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ناشی شدن (nâshi shodan) — to stem from, to arise from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an influence, then the Persian for to influence.

You'll want to know: ناشی شدن.
ناشی شدن (nâshi shodan) — "to stem from, to arise from".
سردرد او از خستگی ناشی میشود. sardard-e u az khastegi nâshi mishavad. His headache comes from tiredness.
Takes az: az chizi nâshi shodan. Formal.

The word, taken apart.
ناشی is usually traced to Arabic nāshi' "arising, growing out of"; shodan "to become" is the inherited Persian verb.

Lesson 4 of 5.
موجب شدن (mojeb shodan) — to give rise to, to cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to influence, then the Persian for to stem from.

You'll want to know: موجب شدن.
موجب شدن (mojeb shodan) — "to give rise to, to cause".
کار زیاد موجب شد که او مریض شود. kâr-e ziyâd mojeb shod ke u mariz shavad. Too much work made him ill.
Mojeb "cause" + shodan; formal, like bâ'es shodan. Ke + subjunctive follows.

The word, taken apart.
موجب is usually traced to Arabic mūjib "that which makes something necessary"; with shodan "to become" it means to be the cause.

Lesson 5 of 5.
واکنش (vâkonesh) — a reaction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to stem from, then the Persian for to give rise to.

You'll want to know: واکنش.
واکنش (vâkonesh) — "a reaction".
واکنش مادرم به این خبر جالب بود. vâkonesh-e mâdaram be in khabar jâleb bud. My mother's reaction to this news was interesting.
A modern Persian coinage: vâ- "back" + konesh "action". Vâkonesh neshân dâdan — to react.

The word, taken apart.
واکنش (vâkonesh) is a modern native coinage built from vâ- "back, again" + konesh "action", built on kon-, the present stem of kardan "to do". A reaction is an action back.
