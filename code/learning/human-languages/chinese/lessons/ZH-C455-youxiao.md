## You'll want to know: yǒuxiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C455-FORMS455-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuxiào* — "valid; effective".

> *zhè zhāng ménpiào jīntiān yǒuxiào* — This ticket is valid today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuxiào* is built from 有 'to have' and *xiào* 'an effect', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuwX* and *haewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to imitate' and 'an effect'.
