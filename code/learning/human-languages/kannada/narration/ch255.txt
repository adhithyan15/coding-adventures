Kannada, chapter 255: Naughty, Calm, Selfish, Serious, Round.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತುಂಟ (tuṇṭa) — naughty, mischievous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for brown, then the Kannada for pink.

You'll want to know: ತುಂಟ.
ತುಂಟ — tuṇṭa — "naughty, mischievous".

The word, taken apart.
ತುಂಟ (tuṇṭa) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil tuṇṭaṉ, Telugu tuṇṭa and Malayalam tuṇṭan (DEDR 3312). DEDR lists tuṇṭa itself, "a mischievous man, a rogue".

ಸ್ವಾರ್ಥಿ (svārthi) is Sanskrit svārthin, "one who seeks his own interest": sva, "own", and artha, "aim, interest". Kannada drops the final -n.
