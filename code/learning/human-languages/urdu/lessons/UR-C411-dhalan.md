## You'll want to know: ڈھلان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C411-DHALAN411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھلان** — *ḍhalān* — "a slope".

> پہاڑی کی ڈھلان پر گھاس ہے۔
>
> *pahāṛī kī ḍhalān par ghās hai.*
>
> There is grass on the slope of the hill.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھلان** (*ḍhalān*) is built on *ḍhalnā*, "to topple, to slope away", which CDIAL lists under *ḍhalati*, "bends over, falls", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5581).
