## You'll want to know: پھیلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C416-KURA416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پھیلانا** — *phailānā* — "to spread (something) around; to strew".

> کچھ لوگ گلی میں کوڑا پھیلاتے ہیں۔
>
> *kuchh log galī meṅ kūṛā phailāte haiṅ.*
>
> Some people strew rubbish about the lane.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے میز پر نقشہ پھیلایا, *us ne mez par naqshā phailāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھیلانا** (*phailānā*) is built on **پھیلنا**, "to spread": CDIAL lists both, as Hindi *phailnā* and *phailānā*, under Sanskrit *prathita*, "spread out" (CDIAL 8651). This is the form for spreading something else.
