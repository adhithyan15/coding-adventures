# より (yori) — than

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C49-ADJ49-04, JA-LEX-C203-FLY203-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C255-SHOULD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "fast" and "a plane". (*hayai*, *hikōki*.) Then "you had better rest". (*yasunda hō ga ii desu*.) Its **ほう** is back today.

## You'll want to know: A は B より
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C259-COMPARE-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C134-DENSHA, JA-LEX-C55-TIME55-02, JA-LEX-C55-TIME55-03, JA-LEX-C53-ADJ53-03] -->

> **ひこうきは でんしゃより はやいです。**
>
> *hikōki wa densha yori hayai desu.* — "A plane is faster than a train."

**より** (*yori*), "than", follows the thing you measure against. The adjective does not change: **はやい** is "faster" here.

> **なつは ふゆより あついです。**
>
> *natsu wa fuyu yori atsui desu.* — "Summer is hotter than winter."

## You'll want to know: B より A の ほうが
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C259-COMPARE-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-03, JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-05, JA-LEX-C42-NATURE42-02] -->

To put the winner last, **の ほうが** (*no hō ga*), "the side of", marks it:

> **ふゆより なつの ほうが すきです。**
>
> *fuyu yori natsu no hō ga suki desu.* — "I like summer more than winter."

> **やまより うみの ほうが すきです。**
>
> *yama yori umi no hō ga suki desu.* — "I like the sea more than the mountains."
