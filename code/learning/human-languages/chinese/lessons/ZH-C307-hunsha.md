## You'll want to know: hūnshā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C307-SPLIT307-05]; assesses=[] -->

*hūnshā* — "wedding dress".

> *xīnniáng de hūnshā hěn piàoliang* — The bride's dress is beautiful.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hūnshā* is built from *hūn* 'marriage' and *shā* 'gauze', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwon* and *srae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a kind of silk'.
