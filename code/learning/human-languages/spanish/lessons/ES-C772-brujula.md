## You'll want to know: la brújula
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C772-MONTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la brújula** — "the compass".

> Sin mapa, usamos la brújula.
>
> *With no map, we used the compass.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brújula* comes from Italian *bussola* 'a compass' (EtymDB), from Late Latin *buxula* 'a little box', built on *buxus* 'boxwood' (Lewis & Short). The needle sat in a small wooden box.
