# വിഷാദം (viṣādaṁ) — melancholy; depression

## You'll want to know: വിഷാദം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C521-VISHADAM521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**വിഷാദം** — *viṣādaṁ* — "melancholy; depression".

> മഴക്കാലത്ത് ചിലർക്ക് വിഷാദം തോന്നാറുണ്ട്.
>
> *maḻakkālattŭ cilarkkŭ viṣādaṁ tōnnāṟuṇṭŭ.*
>
> Some people tend to feel low in the rainy season.

In medicine വിഷാദരോഗം is depression the illness. -ആറുണ്ട് says something tends to happen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിഷാദം** (*viṣādaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *viṣāda*, "drooping, dejection, despondency" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
