German, chapter 375: Die Klausur.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Klausur — the written exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for flexible, then the German for the planning.

You'll want to know: die Klausur.
die Klausur — "the written exam".
Morgen schreiben wir eine Klausur in Mathe. Tomorrow we are sitting a written exam in maths.
Plural: die Klausuren. At school in Austria the written test is die Schularbeit.

The word, taken apart.
Klausur is a learned borrowing from Latin clausūra "a lock, a bar" (Lewis & Short), from claudere "to close": you sit the exam shut away from help.

Lesson 2 of 5.
durchfallen — to fail an exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the planning, then the German for the written exam.

You'll want to know: durchfallen.
durchfallen — "to fail an exam".
Mein Bruder ist bei der Fahrprüfung durchgefallen. My brother failed his driving test.
Separable, Perfekt with sein: er fällt durch, er ist durchgefallen. Durch eine Prüfung fallen — to fail an exam.

The word, taken apart.
durchfallen is built from durch "through" and fallen "to fall", inherited from Old High German fallan and Proto-Germanic fallaną (EtymDB): you fall through instead of getting past.

Mündlich comes from der Mund — the mouth.

mündlich is built with -lich on Mund "mouth", inherited from Proto-Germanic munþaz (EtymDB): by mouth.

Lesson 4 of 5.
schriftlich — written, in writing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to fail an exam, then the German for oral.

You'll want to know: schriftlich.
schriftlich — "written, in writing".
Die Kündigung muss schriftlich sein. The notice must be in writing.
The opposite of mündlich. Schriftlich und mündlich — written and spoken.

The word, taken apart.
schriftlich is built with -lich on Schrift "writing", from Old High German scrift, which goes back to Latin scrīptum "something written" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Zeugnis — the school report; the certificate, the reference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for oral, then the German for written.

You'll want to know: das Zeugnis.
das Zeugnis — "the school report; the certificate, the reference".
Am Ende des Schuljahres bekommen die Kinder ihr Zeugnis. At the end of the school year the children get their reports.
Plural: die Zeugnisse. From an employer: das Arbeitszeugnis — a written reference.

The word, taken apart.
Zeugnis is formed with -nis on zeugen "to testify", from Middle High German ziugen (EtymDB): a paper that bears witness to what you did.
