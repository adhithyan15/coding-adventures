## You'll want to know: ਮੁਰੰਮਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C336-TRADE336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਰੰਮਤ** — *murammat* — "a repair".

> ਪਿਛਲੇ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਘਰ ਦੀ ਮੁਰੰਮਤ ਕੀਤੀ।
>
> *pichhle mahīne asīṁ ghar dī murammat kītī.*
>
> Last month we repaired the house.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਰੰਮਤ** (*murammat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *marammat*, "repair".
