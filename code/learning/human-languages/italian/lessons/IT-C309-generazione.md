## You'll want to know: la generazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C309-GENERAZIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la generazione** — "the generation".

> Tre generazioni vivono nella stessa casa.
>
> *Three generations live in the same house.*

*Di generazione in generazione* — from generation to generation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*generazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *generātiō* "a begetting", from *generāre* "to beget", built on *genus* "birth, kind" (Lewis & Short).
