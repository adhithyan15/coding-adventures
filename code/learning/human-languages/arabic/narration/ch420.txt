Arabic, chapter 420: Responding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استجاب (istajāba) — to respond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for secondary, then the Arabic for comprehensive.

You'll want to know: استجاب.
استجاب — istajāba — "to respond".
استجاب المدير لطلب العمال. istajāba al-mudīr li-ṭalab al-ʿummāl. The manager responded to the workers' request.
Present: يستجيب, yastajīb.
It takes لـ: استجاب (istajāba) لـ ….

The word, taken apart.
استجاب (istajāba) is built on the native Arabic root j-w-b, "to cut through; to answer" (Lane). It is Form X, to give the answer sought: to respond. جواب, an answer, is from the same root.

رشح المدير أختي لوظيفة جديدة. rashshaḥa al-mudīr ukhtī li-waẓīfa jadīda. The manager nominated my sister for a new job.

رشح (rashshaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root r-sh-ḥ, "to sweat, to ooze" (Lane). It is Form II; Lane gives it for training young ones until they are strong: to put someone forward, to nominate.
