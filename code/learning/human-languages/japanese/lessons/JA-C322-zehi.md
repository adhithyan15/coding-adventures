## You'll want to know: ぜひ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C322-SHOUCHI322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜひ** — *zehi* — "by all means; without fail".

> **おもしろいので、ぜひ よんで ください。** — *omoshiroi node, zehi yonde kudasai* — "It is interesting, so do read it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜひ** (*zehi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ze*, "right" (Middle Chinese *dzyeX*) and *hi*, "negative" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
