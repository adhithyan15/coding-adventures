## HL-C326 — Hindi written marks declare their taught names

After the formal-age repair, **विराम** is the highest-ranked independent Hindi
term in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. The Chapter 2 writing sequence
already defines **विराम** and everyday **हलंत** as names for the vowel-killing
mark **्**, practises both names, and assesses them in wrap-up recall. It also
teaches and recalls **पूर्ण विराम** as the name of the sentence stop **।**.

The two writing lesson headwords declared only their glyphs, leaving the taught
Hindi terminology invisible to reports and downstream cards. Declare **् /
विराम / हलंत** and **। / पूर्ण विराम** as the paired mark-and-name headwords,
then regenerate the derived Hindi artifacts.

This is a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim.
It closes the high-frequency **विराम** row together with its explicitly taught
everyday synonym and complete-stop compound.
