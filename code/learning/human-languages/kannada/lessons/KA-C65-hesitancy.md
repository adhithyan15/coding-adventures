## You'll want to know: ಸಂಕೋಚ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C65-MANNERS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಕೋಚ** (*saṅkōca*) — "hesitancy, holding back".

ಸಂಕೋಚ is Sanskrit for a drawing-in, a shrinking — and in Kannada it names the polite reluctance that stops you accepting what you actually want.

It is the one word in this chapter you will hear said to you rather than by you. A host feeding an *atithi* says ಸಂಕೋಚ ಬೇಡ, using the refusal-word from the replies chapter to refuse your refusal. Hearing it is the signal to eat.
