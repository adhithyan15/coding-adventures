Malayalam, chapter 509: Heat and Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
താപനില (tāpanila) — temperature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a raincoat, then the Malayalam for to dry out.

You'll want to know: താപനില.
താപനില — tāpanila — "temperature".
ഇന്ന് താപനില മുപ്പതിന് മുകളിൽ ആണ്. innŭ tāpanila muppatinŭ mukaḷil āṇŭ. Today's temperature is above thirty.
താപം, heat, plus നില, a level: the level of heat.

The word, taken apart.
താപനില (tāpanila) is a Malayalam compound built from താപം, heat, from Sanskrit tāpa (Monier-Williams), and the native നില, a standing or level (DEDR 3675).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ആർദ്രത (ārdrata) — humidity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to dry out, then the Malayalam for temperature.

You'll want to know: ആർദ്രത.
ആർദ്രത — ārdrata — "humidity".
തീരദേശത്ത് ആർദ്രത കൂടുതലാണ്. tīradēśattŭ ārdrata kūṭutalāṇŭ. Humidity is higher in the coastal region.
A formal word from the weather report; people say the air is heavy and you sweat.

The word, taken apart.
ആർദ്രത (ārdrata) is built from Sanskrit ārdra, "wet, moist" (Monier-Williams), with -tā, the ending of abstract nouns.

മഴയ്ക്ക് മുൻപ് വല്ലാത്ത ഉഷ്ണമായിരുന്നു. maḻaykkŭ munpŭ vallātta uṣṇamāyirunnu. Before the rain the heat was oppressive.

Lesson 4 of 5.
കുളിർ (kuḷir) — a chill; coolness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for humidity, then the Malayalam for sultry heat.

You'll want to know: കുളിർ.
കുളിർ — kuḷir — "a chill; coolness".
പുലർച്ചെ മലയിൽ നല്ല കുളിർ ആയിരുന്നു. pularcce malayil nalla kuḷir āyirunnu. At dawn it was very chilly in the hills.
A pleasant coolness, as of a morning breeze.

The word, taken apart.
കുളിർ (kuḷir) is native Dravidian, "coolness, chill" (DEDR 1834).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ചൂടാകുക (cūṭākuka) — to get hot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for sultry heat, then the Malayalam for a chill.

You'll want to know: ചൂടാകുക.
ചൂടാകുക — cūṭākuka — "to get hot".
ഉച്ചയായപ്പോൾ കാറിന്റെ ഉള്ള് വല്ലാതെ ചൂടായി. uccayāyappōḷ kāṟinṟe uḷḷŭ vallāte cūṭāyi. By noon the inside of the car got very hot.
Past: ചൂടായി, cūṭāyi.
ചൂട്, heat, plus ആകുക, to become. A person can also ചൂടാകുക (cūṭākuka): get angry.

The word, taken apart.
ചൂടാകുക (cūṭākuka) is a Malayalam verb built from the native ചൂട്, heat (DEDR 2654), and ആകുക, to become.
