Mandarin Chinese, chapter 350: Facts and Grounds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
shìshí — fact.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the way something is done, then the Mandarin for to argue in favour of.

You'll want to know: shìshí.
shìshí — "fact".
wǒ juéde tā shuō de shì shìshí — I think what he says is true.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shìshí is built from 事 'a matter' and shí, both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzriH and zyit (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'full' and 'sincere'.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons.

Lesson 4 of 5.
pànduàn — to make a judgement; to judge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to prove, then the Mandarin for a basis for a claim.

You'll want to know: pànduàn.
pànduàn — "to make a judgement; to judge".
wǒ bù néng pànduàn tā shuō de shì bu shì shìshí — I can't judge whether what he says is true.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
pànduàn is built from pàn and duàn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, phanH and dwanX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to split' and 'to divide', and the second 'to cut off'; it also lists the second in the departing tone, glossed 'to decide'.
