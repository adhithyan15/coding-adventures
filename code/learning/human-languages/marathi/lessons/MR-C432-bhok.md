## You'll want to know: भोक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C432-BHOK432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भोक** — *bhok* — "a hole".

> माझ्या खिशाला भोक पडलं आहे.
>
> *mājhyā khiśālā bhok paḍlaṁ āhe.*
>
> There is a hole in my pocket.

Neuter.

भोक पडणे, a hole appears; the thing with the hole takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भोक** (*bhok*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *bhok*, "hole", under *\*bhōkka*, "hollow" (CDIAL 9624).
