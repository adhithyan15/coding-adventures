## You'll want to know: 年代
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C323-ERA323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**年代** — *niándài* — "decade; era".

> **他八十年代去过中国** *tā bāshí niándài qù guo Zhōngguó* — He went to China in the eighties.

The new character, in pieces: 代 — five strokes: the person piece 亻 on the left; on the right a bar rising to the right, a long stroke that slants down to the right and flicks up, and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**年代** (*niándài*) is built from 年 'a year' and *dài* 'an age', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nen* and *dojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to take turns' and gives this very word as its example.
