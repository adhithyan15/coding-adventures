## You'll want to know: o umbigo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C482-CORPO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o umbigo** — "navel, belly button".

> O bebé ri quando lhe tocam no umbigo.
>
> *The baby laughs when someone touches his belly button.*

*Olhar para o próprio umbigo* — to think only of yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*umbigo* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *umbiigo*, from Latin *umbilīcus* "navel" (EtymDB).
