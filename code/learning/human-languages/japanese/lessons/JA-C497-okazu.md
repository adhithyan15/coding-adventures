## You'll want to know: おかず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C497-MEAL497-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おかず** — *okazu* — "a side dish (eaten with rice)".

> **ばんごはんの おかずは さかなです。** — *bangohan no okazu wa sakana desu* — "The side dish for dinner is fish."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おかず** (*okazu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
