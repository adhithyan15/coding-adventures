## You'll want to know: उद्यमी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C465-UDYAMIN465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्यमी** — *udyamī* — "industrious".

> उद्यमी कृषकः प्रातः क्षेत्रं गच्छति।
>
> *udyamī kṛṣakaḥ prātaḥ kṣetraṃ gacchati.*
>
> The industrious farmer goes to the field early.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्यमी** (*udyamī*) is built on *udyama*, "exertion", from *ud* and the root √yam, "to hold"; MW gives "persevering, making effort, active" (MW).
