Arabic, chapter 116: Jug, Frying pan, Oven, Fan, Computer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
إبريق (ibrīq) — a jug, a teapot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to ask for, then the Arabic for an intention.
