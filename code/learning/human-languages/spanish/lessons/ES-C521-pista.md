## You'll want to know: la pista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C521-PISTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la pista** — "the clue, the lead; the track".

> La policía no tiene ninguna pista.
>
> *The police do not have any leads.*

It also means a track or court: *una pista de tenis*, *la pista de baile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pista* was borrowed from Italian *pista* 'a track, a beaten trail' (EtymDB). A clue is the trail you follow.
