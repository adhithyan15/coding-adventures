# ਜਗਾਉਣਾ (jagāuṇā) — to wake (someone) up

## You'll want to know: ਜਗਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C225-DIN225-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਗਾਉਣਾ** — *jagāuṇā* — "to wake (someone) up".

> ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਜੇ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਜਗਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ savere chhe vaje bachchiāṁ nū̃ jagāundī hāṁ.*
>
> I wake the children at six in the morning.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਜਗਾਇਆ, *maiṁ Aman nū̃ jagāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਗਾਉਣਾ** (*jagāuṇā*) is the causative partner of ਜਾਗਣਾ, built on a verb inherited from Sanskrit *jāgrati*, "are awake"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jagāuṇā* under it (CDIAL 5175).
