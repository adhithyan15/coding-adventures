## You'll want to know: die Bremse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C399-LENKRAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Bremse** — "the brake".

> Die Bremsen an meinem Fahrrad sind kaputt.
>
> *The brakes on my bike are broken.*

Plural: *die Bremsen*. *Die Bremse* is also a horsefly, a different word that only looks the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Die Bremse* 'brake' is usually traced to Middle Low German *premse* 'a clamp', first one put on a restless horse's nose. EtymDB only lists the look-alike *Bremse* 'horsefly', inherited from Middle Low and Middle High German, so the line for the brake is hedged.
