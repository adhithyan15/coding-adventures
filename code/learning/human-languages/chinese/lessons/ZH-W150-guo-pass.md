## Script — 寸, then the walking piece last
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GUO-PASS-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 过

**Two pieces you own, in the order you know from 这.**

**寸, three strokes**, on the upper right: the bar, the vertical that hooks to the left at its foot, then the dot. It is the right half of 对 and of 时.

**辶, three strokes**, last: the dot at the upper left; the short stroke that turns down, turns again and falls to the left in one movement; then the long base, sweeping out to the right under 寸.

3 + 3 = six. The walking piece goes in last, as in 这 and 进, though it sits under everything.
