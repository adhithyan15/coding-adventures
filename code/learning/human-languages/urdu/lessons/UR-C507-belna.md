## You'll want to know: بیلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C507-ROTI507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بیلنا** — *belnā* — "to roll out (dough)".

> سارا چکلے پر روٹی بیل رہی تھی۔
>
> *Sārā chakle par roṭī bel rahī thī.*
>
> Sara was rolling out a roti on the board.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے روٹی بیلی, *us ne roṭī belī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیلنا** (*belnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *belnā*, "to roll out", under Sanskrit *vēllayati* (CDIAL 12121).
