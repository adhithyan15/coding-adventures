## You'll want to know: رصيد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C452-FAR452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رصيد** — *raṣīd* — "a balance (of an account); credit".

> كان رصيد حسابي صغيرا.
>
> *kāna raṣīd ḥisābī ṣaghīran.*
>
> The balance of my account was small.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رصيد** (*raṣīd*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ṣ-d*, "to watch for; to make ready" (Lane).
