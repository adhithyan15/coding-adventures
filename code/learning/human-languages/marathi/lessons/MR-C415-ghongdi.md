## You'll want to know: घोंगडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C415-BICHANA415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घोंगडी** — *ghoṅgḍī* — "a woollen blanket".

> थंडीत आजोबा घोंगडी अंगावर घेतात.
>
> *thaṇḍīt ājobā ghoṅgḍī aṅgāvar ghetāt.*
>
> In the cold Grandfather wraps himself in a woollen blanket.

Feminine.

A rough blanket of sheep’s wool, woven by shepherds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोंगडी** (*ghoṅgḍī*) is a Marathi word for the coarse wool blanket shepherds weave; its origin is not settled.
