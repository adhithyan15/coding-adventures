Lesson 1 of 5.
かのう (kanou) — possible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for something to look forward to, then the Japanese for love.

You'll want to know: かのう.
かのう — kanou — "possible".

The word, taken apart.
かのう (kanou) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ka, "may, can" (Middle Chinese khaX) and nō, "to be able" (nong) (Unihan, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

You'll want to know: にがて.
にがて — nigate — "poor at".

The word, taken apart.
にがて (nigate) is a native compound, inherited rather than borrowed: niga, "bitter", from nigai, and te, "hand", both kun'yomi of its kanji (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
