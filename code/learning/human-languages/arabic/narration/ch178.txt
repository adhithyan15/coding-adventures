Arabic, chapter 178: Official, Private, In a hurry, Clear, Hard-working.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رسمي (rasmī) — official.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for cooked, then the Arabic for fried.

You'll want to know: رسمي.
رسمي — rasmī — "official".

The word, taken apart.
رسمي (rasmī) is built on the native Arabic root r-s-m, "a trace, a mark" (Lane). Rasmī is rasm, "a mark, a prescribed form", with the adjective ending -ī; "official" is a modern sense.
