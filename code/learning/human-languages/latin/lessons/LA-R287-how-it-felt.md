# Review — how it felt, and what you hope for

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C287-AFFECTUS-01, LA-LEX-C287-SPERO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A woman says "I was worried". (*Sollicita eram.*) Then say "I
hope for peace". (*Pācem spērō.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C287-CUPIO-INF-02, LA-LEX-C284-QUOD-CAUSA-02, LA-LEX-C286-AMITTO-01] -->

Two lessons, and an experience has a before and an after. Before: a
feeling with *eram*, agreeing with the one who felt it, and its cause with
*quod*. After: *spērō* for what you hope for, and *cupiō* or *volō* with
an infinitive for what you long to do.

> *Sarcinam āmīsī, et trīstis eram. Sed nunc laetus sum, et Rōmam vidēre
> cupiō.*
> — I lost my pack, and I was sad. But now I am happy, and I long to see
> Rome.

[PAUSE 2s] An event, a feeling in the past, a feeling now, and a wish.
