## You'll want to know: arreglarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C494-MANANA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**arreglarse** — "to get ready, to get dressed up".

> Se arregla para ir a la boda.
>
> *She gets ready to go to the wedding.*

Without *se*, *arreglar* is to fix.
