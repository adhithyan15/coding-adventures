Portuguese, chapter 341: As provas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a prova — the proof, the evidence; the test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for absurd, then the Portuguese for debatable.

You'll want to know: a prova.
a prova — "the proof, the evidence; the test".
Não há nenhuma prova de que ele mentiu. There is no proof that he lied.
Uma prova can also be a test at school. À prova de água — waterproof.

The word, taken apart.
prova is built on provar "to prove, to try", which is inherited from Latin probāre "to test, to approve, to prove" (Lewis & Short); Old Portuguese already had prova (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: evidente.
evidente — "evident, clear".
É evidente que ele está cansado. It is evident that he is tired.
É evidente que takes the indicative. Evidentemente — evidently.

The word, taken apart.
evidente is a learned borrowing from Latin ēvidēns "visible, apparent, clear" (EtymDB), built on vidēre "to see".

You'll want to know: óbvio.
óbvio — "obvious".
A resposta parecia óbvia a toda a gente. The answer seemed obvious to everyone.
É óbvio! — obviously! Obviamente — obviously.

The word, taken apart.
óbvio is a learned borrowing from Latin obvius "in the way, meeting, easy to find" (EtymDB), built from ob "towards" and via "road": it lies right in your path.

Lesson 4 of 5.
indiscutível — indisputable, beyond question.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for evident, then the Portuguese for obvious.

You'll want to know: indiscutível.
indiscutível — "indisputable, beyond question".
O talento dela é indiscutível. Her talent is beyond question.
The opposite of discutível.

The word, taken apart.
indiscutível is built from the prefix in- "not" and discutível "debatable", on the verb discutir, which comes from Latin discutere "to strike asunder" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
refutar — to refute, to disprove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for obvious, then the Portuguese for indisputable.

You'll want to know: refutar.
refutar — "to refute, to disprove".
A cientista refutou a teoria com dados novos. The scientist refuted the theory with new data.
Formal. Refutar um argumento — to refute an argument.

The word, taken apart.
refutar is a learned borrowing from Latin refūtāre "to check, to drive back, to repress", and so "to disprove" (Lewis & Short).
