# उपदेशः (upadeśaḥ) — a piece of advice

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "you should go to the doctor". (*bhavān vaidyaṃ gacchet*.) That sentence is a piece of advice.

## You'll want to know: उपदेशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C279-UPADESAH-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-MAMA, SA-LEX-HEAR, SA-LEX-C26-ROLE-05] -->

**उपदेशः** — *upadeśaḥ* — "a piece of advice". It is a masculine noun.

> **मम उपदेशः अस्ति।**
>
> *mama upadeśaḥ asti.* — "I have a piece of advice."

> **सः मम उपदेशं न शृणोति।**
>
> *saḥ mama upadeśaṃ na śṛṇoti.* — "He does not listen to my advice."

> **वैद्यः उपदेशं दास्यति।**
>
> *vaidyaḥ upadeśaṃ dāsyati.* — "The doctor will give advice."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपदेशः** (*upadeśaḥ*) is built from **उप** (*upa*), "towards", and the root √diś, "to point out". Monier-Williams gives its first sense as pointing something out to someone, and then instruction, teaching and advice.
