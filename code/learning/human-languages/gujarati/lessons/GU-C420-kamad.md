## You'll want to know: કમાડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C420-HOUSE420-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કમાડ** — *kamāḍ* — "a wooden door; a door leaf".

> પવનથી ઘરનું જૂનું કમાડ ખખડે છે.
>
> *pavanthī gharnũ jūnũ kamāḍ khakhḍe che.*
>
> The old wooden door of the house rattles in the wind.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કમાડ** (*kamāḍ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kavāṭa*, "the leaf of a door"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a door" (CDIAL 2963).
