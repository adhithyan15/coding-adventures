## You'll want to know: سیپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C435-KHALIJ435-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سیپ** — *sīp* — "a seashell".

> میں نے ساحل سے ایک سیپ اٹھائی۔
>
> *maiṅ ne sāhil se ek sīp uṭhāī.*
>
> I picked up a shell on the beach.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سیپ** (*sīp*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sīp*, "a bivalve shell", under *sippī*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13417).
