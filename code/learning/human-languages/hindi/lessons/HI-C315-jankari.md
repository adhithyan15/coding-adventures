## You'll want to know: जानकारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C315-DAVA315-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जानकारी** — *jānkārī* — "information".

> पुलिस ने पत्रकारों को कोई जानकारी नहीं दी।
>
> *pulis ne patrakāroṁ ko koī jānkārī nahīṃ dī.*
>
> The police gave the journalists no information.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is *jānkār*, one who knows, with the noun ending *-ī*; *jānkār* is built on **जानना**, to know, from Sanskrit *jānāti* (CDIAL 5193). **जानकारी** is what a knower has: information.
