Punjabi, chapter 77: Dog, Cat, Cow, Goat, Sheep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੁੱਤਾ (kuttā) — a dog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for rain, then the Punjabi for wind.

You'll want to know: ਕੁੱਤਾ.
ਕੁੱਤਾ — kuttā — "a dog".

The word, taken apart.
ਕੁੱਤਾ (kuttā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kutta, "dog", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3275). CDIAL calls it imitative, comparing Santali kutu kutu, the call to a puppy.
