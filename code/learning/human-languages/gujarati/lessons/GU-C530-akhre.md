## You'll want to know: આખરે
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C530-TAKID530-05]; assesses=[] -->

**આખરે** — *ākhre* — "at long last".

> જો આપણે મહેનત કરીશું તો આખરે આપણે જીતીશું.
>
> *jo āpaṇe mahenat karīśũ to ākhre āpaṇe jītīśũ.*
>
> If we work hard, in the end we will win.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આખરે** (*ākhre*) is usually traced to Arabic *ākhir*, "last", through Persian, with the Gujarati ending *-e* of **ગઈકાલે**: at the last.
