Russian, chapter 287: Crime and its witnesses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
преступление (prestupléniye) — a crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a publication, then the Russian for a print run.

You'll want to know: преступление.
преступление (prestupléniye) — "a crime".
Это было серьёзное преступление. Éto býlo seryóznoye prestupléniye. It was a serious crime.
Neuter. Совершить преступление (prestupléniye) — to commit a crime.

The word, taken apart.
преступление (prestupléniye) is built on преступить "to step over (the law)", from пре-, the Church Slavonic shape of native пере-, and ступить "to step", the root of ступень, Proto-Slavic stǫpenĭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
преступник (prestúpnik) — a criminal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: преступник.
преступник (prestúpnik) — "a criminal".
Преступник убежал через окно. Prestúpnik ubezhál chérez oknó. The criminal ran away through the window.
A woman is преступница.

The word, taken apart.
преступник (prestúpnik) is built on преступить "to step over", Church Slavonic пре- with ступить "to step" (EtymDB lists ступень, Proto-Slavic stǫpenĭ), and the ending -ник for a person.

Lesson 3 of 5.
жертва (zhértva) — a victim; a sacrifice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a crime, then the Russian for a criminal.

You'll want to know: жертва.
жертва (zhértva) — "a victim; a sacrifice".
Жертва ничего не помнила. Zhértva nichevó ne pómnila. The victim remembered nothing.
Feminine, even of a man: он стал жертвой обмана — he fell victim to a fraud.

The word, taken apart.
жертва (zhértva) is inherited from Proto-Slavic žĭrtva "a sacrifice", built on žĭrti "to offer, to sacrifice", which EtymDB traces to PIE gʷerH-.

Lesson 4 of 5.
улика (ulíka) — a clue, a piece of evidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a criminal, then the Russian for a victim.

You'll want to know: улика.
улика (ulíka) — "a clue, a piece of evidence".
Полицейский нашёл важную улику. Politséyskiy nashyól vázhnuyu ulíku. The policeman found an important clue.
Evidence as a whole is plural: улики.

The word, taken apart.
улика (ulíka) is built on уличить "to catch out, to expose", which is usually traced to лик "face": to bring face to face with the facts. EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
свидетель (svidétel) — a witness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a victim, then the Russian for a clue.

You'll want to know: свидетель.
свидетель (svidétel) — "a witness".
Свидетель описал машину. Svidétel opisál mashínu. The witness described the car.
A woman is свидетельница. At a wedding, свидетель (svidétel) is also the best man.

The word, taken apart.
свидетель (svidétel) is inherited from Old East Slavic sŭvědětelĭ (EtymDB), built on съ- and the root of ведать "to know", PIE weyd-: one who knows what happened.
