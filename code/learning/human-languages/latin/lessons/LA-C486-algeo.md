## You'll want to know: algeō, algēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C486-PRUINA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**algeō, algēre** — "to feel cold, to be cold".

> Servus sine palliō alget.
>
> *The slave without a cloak feels cold.*

*algeō, algēre, alsī*, second conjugation. Of a person feeling cold; *frīgus* is the cold itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *algeō* is not settled: Lewis & Short report an old link with Greek *algeō* 'to feel pain', and EtymDB gives no parent.
