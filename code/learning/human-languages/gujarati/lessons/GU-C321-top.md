## You'll want to know: તોપ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C321-TALVAR321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**તોપ** — *top* — "a cannon".

> કિલ્લા પર બે જૂની તોપ છે.
>
> *killā par be jūnī top che.*
>
> There are two old cannons on the fort.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તોપ** (*top*) is usually traced to Turkish *top*, "a ball", and then "a cannon", a word that came with the armies of the Mughal age. It is feminine: **જૂની તોપ**.
