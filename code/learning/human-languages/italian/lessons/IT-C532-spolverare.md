## You'll want to know: spolverare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C532-PULIZIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**spolverare** — "to dust".

> Spolvero i mobili una volta alla settimana.
>
> *I dust the furniture once a week.*

Built on *polvere* "dust".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spolverare* is built from *s-* and *polvere* "dust", inherited from Latin *pulvis, pulveris* (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
