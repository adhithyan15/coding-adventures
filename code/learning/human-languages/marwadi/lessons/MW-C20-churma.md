# चूरमा — the sweet on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-CHURMA-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-CHURMA-01, MW-LEX-BAADAL-01, MW-PERFORMANCE-TRANSPORT-FIVE-FOUR-SKILL-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Recall the five-word transport payoff, then say the sweet crumbled
wheat dessert and cloud.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-CHURMA-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHURMA-01] -->

> **चू** + **र** + **मा** → **चूरमा** — *chūrmā* — sweet crumbled wheat dessert

The **ू** under **च** is the long *ū* met in **मिलसू**, and **मा** closes with
the long **ा** used since the first greeting.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-CHURMA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write **चूरमा**. Check that the **ू** hangs
below **च** and not below **र**.
