## You'll want to know: হাত

**হাত** — *hāt* — "a hand".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হাত** (*hāt*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *hasta*, "hand" (CDIAL 14024).
