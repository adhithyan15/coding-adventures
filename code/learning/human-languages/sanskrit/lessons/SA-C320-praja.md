## You'll want to know: प्रजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C320-VIPLAVA320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रजा** — *prajā* — "the subjects of a king; the people".

> प्रजाः राज्ये सुखेन वसन्ति।
>
> *prajāḥ rājye sukhena vasanti.*
>
> The subjects live happily in the kingdom.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रजा** (*prajā*) is built from *pra* and the root √jan, "to be born"; MW gives *prajā* as offspring, and also as people, the subjects of a prince (MW).
