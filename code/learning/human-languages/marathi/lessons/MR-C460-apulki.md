## You'll want to know: आपुलकी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C460-MAYA460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आपुलकी** — *āpulkī* — "fellow feeling".

> नव्या शाळेत सगळ्यांनी माझं आपुलकीने स्वागत केलं.
>
> *navyā śāḷet sagḷyānnī mājhaṁ āpulkīne svāgat kelaṁ.*
>
> At the new school everyone welcomed me warmly.

Feminine.

Built on आपला, one's own: treating someone as one's own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपुलकी** (*āpulkī*) is usually traced to आपुला, an older form of आपला, "one's own", with the noun ending *-kī*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
