## You'll want to know: поздравление
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C312-POZDRAVLYAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**поздравление** (*pozdravléniye*) — "congratulations, a greeting".

> Она получила много поздравлений.
>
> *Oná poluchíla mnógo pozdravléniy.*
>
> She received a lot of greetings.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поздравление* is built on *поздравлять* "to congratulate", from *здравый* "healthy", the Church Slavonic shape of *здоровый*.
