Gujarati, chapter 175: Full moon and new moon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પૂનમ (pūnam) — full moon day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wall calendar, then the Gujarati for beginning.

You'll want to know: પૂનમ.
પૂનમ — pūnam — "full moon day".
પૂનમે અમે મંદિરે જઈશું. — pūname ame mandire jaīśũ — "We will go to the temple on the full moon day."

The word, taken apart.
પૂનમ (pūnam) is usually traced to Sanskrit pūrṇimā, "full-moon day", and EtymDB derives it from that word, but the path is not settled: CDIAL lists the Sanskrit word with no Gujarati form under it (CDIAL 8340).

Lesson 2 of 5.
અમાસ (amās) — new moon day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for beginning, then the Gujarati for full moon day.

You'll want to know: અમાસ.
અમાસ — amās — "new moon day".
અમાસની રાત્રે અંધારું હશે. — amāsnī rātre andhārũ haśe — "It will be dark on the night of the new moon."

The word, taken apart.
અમાસ (amās) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit amāvāsyā, "the night of the new moon", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati amās with that sense (CDIAL 565).

Lesson 3 of 5.
અંત (ant) — end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for full moon day, then the Gujarati for new moon day.

You'll want to know: અંત.
અંત — ant — "end".
મહિનાના અંતમાં હું આવીશ. — mahinānā antmā̃ hũ āvīś — "I will come at the end of the month."

The word, taken apart.
અંત (ant) is Sanskrit anta, "end, border", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL lists the Sanskrit word (CDIAL 347).

Lesson 4 of 5.
અડધું (aḍdhũ) — half.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for new moon day, then the Gujarati for end.

You'll want to know: અડધું.
અડધું — aḍdhũ — "half".
હું અડધી રોટલી ખાઈશ. — hũ aḍdhī roṭlī khāīś — "I will eat half a rotli."

The word, taken apart.
અડધું (aḍdhũ) is inherited from Sanskrit ardha, "half". CDIAL lists Gujarati adh, "half", in compounds under that word, but not aḍdhũ itself, so the exact path is not settled (CDIAL 644).

Lesson 5 of 5.
સતત (satat) — constantly, nonstop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for end, then the Gujarati for half.

You'll want to know: સતત.
સતત — satat — "constantly, nonstop".
તે સતત કામ કરશે. — te satat kām karśe — "He will work nonstop."

The word, taken apart.
સતત (satat) is Sanskrit satata, "constant, uninterrupted", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama; EtymDB lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit. CDIAL lists the Sanskrit word but no Gujarati form (CDIAL 13104).
