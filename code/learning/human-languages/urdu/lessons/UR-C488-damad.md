## You'll want to know: داماد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C488-INLAWS488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**داماد** — *dāmād* — "a son-in-law".

> میرے ابو نانی کے داماد ہیں۔
>
> *mere abbū nānī ke dāmād haiṅ.*
>
> My father is my grandmother's son-in-law.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**داماد** (*dāmād*) is usually traced to Persian *dāmād*, "son-in-law".
