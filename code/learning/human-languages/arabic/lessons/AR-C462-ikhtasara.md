# اختصر (ikhtaṣara) — to shorten; to cut short

## You'll want to know: اختصر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C462-ISTIDAD462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اختصر** — *ikhtaṣara* — "to shorten; to cut short".

> اختصرنا الرحلة بسبب المطر.
>
> *ikhtaṣarnā ar-riḥla bi-sabab al-maṭar.*
>
> We cut the trip short because of the rain.

Present: يختصر, *yakhtaṣir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختصر** (*ikhtaṣara*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṣ-r*, "the waist" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to take in, to make short", beside *khaṣr*, "a waist".
