## You'll want to know: 半路
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C424-ROADS424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**半路** — *bànlù* — "halfway; on the way".

> **我们在半路停了车** *wǒmen zài bànlù tíng le chē* — We stopped the car on the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**半路** (*bànlù*) is built from 半 'half' and 路 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *panH* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
