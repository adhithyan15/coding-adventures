## You'll want to know: أرجوحة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C539-LUAB539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أرجوحة** — *urjūḥa* — "a swing".

> يلعب الأطفال على الأرجوحة في الحديقة.
>
> *yalʿabu al-aṭfāl ʿalā al-urjūḥa fī al-ḥadīqa.*
>
> The children play on the swing in the park.

Plural: أراجيح, *arājīḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أرجوحة** (*urjūḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-j-ḥ*, "to outweigh, to go down (of a scale)" (Lane). A swing goes up and down like the pans of a scale.
