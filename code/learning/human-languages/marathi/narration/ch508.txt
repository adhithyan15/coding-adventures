Marathi, chapter 508: Wages and Earnings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वेतन (vetan) — a wage; pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a knack, then the Marathi for to join a post.

You'll want to know: वेतन.
वेतन — vetan — "a wage; pay".
या महिन्याचं वेतन उशिरा आलं. yā mahinyācaṁ vetan uśirā ālaṁ. This month's pay came late.
Neuter.
वेतन (vetan) is the formal word beside पगार.

The word, taken apart.
वेतन (vetan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vetana, "wages, hire, salary" (Monier-Williams).

मजुरी — majurī — "daily wages".

The word, taken apart.
मजुरी (majurī) is a loan from Persian muzdūrī, "work for hire", built on muzd, "wage".

Lesson 3 of 5.
मानधन (māndhan) — an honorarium; a fee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a wage, then the Marathi for daily wages.

You'll want to know: मानधन.
मानधन — māndhan — "an honorarium; a fee".
भाषणासाठी लेखकाला मानधन मिळालं. bhāṣṇāsāṭhī lekhakālā māndhan miḷālaṁ. The writer got a fee for the talk.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
मानधन (māndhan) is built from two tatsamas, Sanskrit māna, "honour", and dhana, "wealth" (Monier-Williams): money given as a mark of respect.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पगारवाढ (pagārvāḍh) — a pay rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for daily wages, then the Marathi for an honorarium.

You'll want to know: पगारवाढ.
पगारवाढ — pagārvāḍh — "a pay rise".
या वर्षी सगळ्यांना चांगली पगारवाढ मिळाली. yā varṣī sagḷyānnā cāṅglī pagārvāḍh miḷālī. Everyone got a good pay rise this year.
Feminine.
पगार is masculine, but पगारवाढ (pagārvāḍh) follows वाढ and is feminine.

The word, taken apart.
पगारवाढ (pagārvāḍh) is a Marathi compound of पगार, "salary", a loan from Portuguese pagar, "to pay", and वाढ, "growth", from वाढणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कमावणे (kamāvṇe) — to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an honorarium, then the Marathi for a pay rise.

You'll want to know: कमावणे.
कमावणे — kamāvṇe — "to earn".
अरुणने पहिल्या नोकरीत थोडे पैसे कमावले. aruṇne pahilyā nokrīt thoḍe paise kamāvle. Arun earned a little money in his first job.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पैसे कमावले, tyāne paise kamāvle.
With पैसे the past is plural: पैसे कमावले.

The word, taken apart.
कमावणे (kamāvṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi kamāviṇẽ, "works, earns", under karmāpayati (CDIAL 2897), built on Sanskrit karman, "work".
