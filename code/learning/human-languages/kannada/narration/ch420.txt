Kannada, chapter 420: Facing the Unknown.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಳುಕು (aḷuku) — to waver; to flinch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a crisis, then the Kannada for unavoidable.

You'll want to know: ಅಳುಕು.
ಅಳುಕು — aḷuku — "to waver; to flinch".
ಕತ್ತಲೆಯ ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೋಗಲು ನಾನು ಅಳುಕಿದೆ. kattaleya dāriyalli hōgalu nānu aḷukide. I flinched at going down the dark road.
Past (I …): ಅಳುಕಿದೆ, aḷukide.
Often negative: ಅಳುಕದೆ, without flinching.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಳುಕು (aḷuku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 306 lists Kannada aḷuku, "to fear", beside Tamil aḷukku, "to be frightened".

ಎಚ್ಚೆತ್ತುಕೊ (eccettuko) is native Dravidian: DEDR 851 lists Kannada eccatt-, "to wake up", here with -ko, "for oneself".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to waver, then the Kannada for to wake up to.

You'll want to know: ಬಲವಂತ.
ಬಲವಂತ — balavanta — "compulsion; force".
ಯಾರ ಬಲವಂತವೂ ಇಲ್ಲದೆ ಅವನು ಕೆಲಸ ಬಿಟ್ಟನು. yāra balavantavū illade avanu kelasa biṭṭanu. He left the job without anyone forcing him.
ಬಲವಂತವಾಗಿ, by force; ಬಲವಂತ (balavanta) ಮಾಡು, to press someone.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಲವಂತ (balavanta) is borrowed from Sanskrit balavant, "strong" (CDIAL 9166), from bala, "strength"; Kannada uses it for force put on someone.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಆಪತ್ತು (āpattu) — a calamity; a mishap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for compulsion, then the Kannada for uncertain.

You'll want to know: ಆಪತ್ತು.
ಆಪತ್ತು — āpattu — "a calamity; a mishap".
ಆಪತ್ತಿನ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನೆರೆಯವರು ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡಿದರು. āpattina samayadalli nereyavaru sahāya māḍidaru. In the time of trouble the neighbours helped.
ಆಪತ್ತು (āpattu) ಬರು, for trouble to strike.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಪತ್ತು (āpattu) is borrowed from Sanskrit āpad, "misfortune, calamity" (CDIAL 1196), with the Kannada -u after the final consonant.
