## You'll want to know: rapiō, rapere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C304-COMPREHENDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rapiō, rapere** — "to snatch, to carry off".

> Fūr crumēnam meam rapuit et fūgit.
>
> *The thief snatched my purse and ran away.*

*rapiō, rapere, rapuī, raptum*. English *rapid* and *rapture* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**rapiō** is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*rapiō* (EtymDB). English *rapid* comes from *rapidus*, "snatching, swift", and *rapture* from its participle.
