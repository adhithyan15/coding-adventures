## You'll want to know: كلما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C400-KULLAMA400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**كلما** — *kullamā* — "whenever".

> كلما زرت جدتي، طبخت لي.
>
> *kullamā zurtu jaddatī, ṭabakhat lī.*
>
> Whenever I visit my grandmother, she cooks for me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كلما** (*kullamā*) joins *kull*, "all, every", built on the native Arabic root *k-l-l* (Lane), with *mā*: every time that, whenever.
