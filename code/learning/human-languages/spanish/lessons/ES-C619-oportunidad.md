## You'll want to know: la oportunidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C619-QUIZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la oportunidad** — "the opportunity, the chance".

> Es una buena oportunidad.
>
> *It is a good opportunity.*

*Aprovechar la oportunidad* — to take the chance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*oportunidad* is a learned borrowing from Latin *opportunitas* 'fitness, a good moment' (EtymDB), built on *opportunus* 'fit, timely', literally 'before the harbour', from *portus* 'harbour' (Lewis & Short).
