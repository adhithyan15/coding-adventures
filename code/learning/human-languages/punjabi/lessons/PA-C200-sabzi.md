Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole noun rather
than inventing smaller Punjabi pieces. This lesson owns the word alone; the
complete identity frame comes only after a separate retrieval.

## The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SABZI-200] -->

Punjabi University's Punjabipedia records the present-day Punjabi vegetable
category. It supports the bounded everyday vegetable meaning taught here.
Source: [Punjabi University Punjabipedia](https://punjabipedia.org/topic.aspx?txt=%E0%A8%B8%E0%A8%AC%E0%A9%9B%E0%A9%80%E0%A8%86%E0%A8%82).

The exact origin of modern written **ਸਬਜ਼ੀ** is unresolved in the sources used
here. Its origin remains unknown here. This lesson therefore makes no
etymological claim. Keep the present-day Punjabi noun whole rather than turning
spelling resemblance into a derivation. A Government of Punjab canteen rate
list provides a current food-service use of the singular noun.
Source: [Government of Punjab](https://punjab.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Hospitality-Food-and-Beverages-Rate-List-M.L.A-Hostel-Canteen-Punjab-Chd.pdf).
