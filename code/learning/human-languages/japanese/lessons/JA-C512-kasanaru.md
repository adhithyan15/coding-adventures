## You'll want to know: かさなる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C512-CHANGE512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かさなる** — *kasanaru* — "to clash (of plans); to overlap".

> **もし よていが かさなったら、どちらを えらびますか。** — *moshi yotei ga kasanattara, dochira o erabimasu ka* — "If your plans clash, which will you choose?"

Polite **かさなります**, *kasanarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **しけんと しあいが かさなりました。**, *shiken to shiai ga kasanarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かさなる** (*kasanaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
