## You'll want to know: ابلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C372-JAMNA372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ابلنا** — *ubalnā* — "to come to the boil".

> اگر دودھ ابلا تو وہ گر جائے گا۔
>
> *agar dūdh ublā to voh gir jāe gā.*
>
> If the milk boils, it will spill over.

Past (it …): وہ پانی ابل گیا, *voh pānī ubal gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ابلنا** (*ubalnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ubalnā*, "to boil over", under *\*ubbal*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2339); **ابالنا**, "to boil (something)", is its partner.
