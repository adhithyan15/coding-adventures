## You'll want to know: wàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C132-BIG132-02]; assesses=[] -->

*wàn* — "ten thousand".

> *wǒ xiǎng yǒu yíwàn kuài qián* — I wish I had ten thousand yuan.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The tone of 一 changes: yī is said yí before the fourth tone of wàn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wàn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *mjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses this short way of writing the character 'ten thousands'; under its longer way of writing it gives the name of a dance and of an insect.
