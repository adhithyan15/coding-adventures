# Review — if, and otherwise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C274-LE-FORM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "If there's a storm tomorrow, we won't go", then "if it rains tomorrow, we won't go", with **হলে**. (*jôdi kāl jhôṛ hôy, tāhôle āmrā jābô nā*; *kāl brishṭi hôle āmrā jābô nā*.)

- [YOU SAY: If Mira comes, we'll go to the market.]
- [YOU SAY: We have to leave now, otherwise we'll be late.]
- [YOU SAY: The shop is open from morning until night. She went in and bought a book.]
