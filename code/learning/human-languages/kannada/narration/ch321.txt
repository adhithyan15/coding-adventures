Kannada, chapter 321: In Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ (nyāyālaya) — a court of law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to broadcast, then the Kannada for a headline.

You'll want to know: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ.
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ — nyāyālaya — "a court of law".
ಅವನ ಭವಿಷ್ಯ ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯದ ನಿರ್ಧಾರದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ. avana bhaviṣya nyāyālayada nirdhārada mēle avalambiside. His future depends on the court's decision.
ನ್ಯಾಯ, justice, and ಆಲಯ, a house: the house of justice.

The word, taken apart.
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ (nyāyālaya) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of nyāya, "rule, method, a judicial sentence" (CDIAL 7614), and ālaya, "a house, a dwelling" (CDIAL 1366).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ (nyāyādhīśa) — a judge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a headline, then the Kannada for a court of law.

You'll want to know: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ.
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ — nyāyādhīśa — "a judge".
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಎರಡೂ ಕಡೆಯ ಮಾತು ಕೇಳಿದರು. nyāyādhīśaru eraḍū kaḍeya mātu kēḷidaru. The judge heard both sides.
Plural: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು, nyāyādhīśaru.
People speak of a judge with the respectful plural: ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಬಂದರು.

The word, taken apart.
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶ (nyāyādhīśa) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of nyāya, "justice" (CDIAL 7614), and adhīśa, "a lord, a master" (CDIAL 267).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಆರೋಪಿಸು (ārōpisu) — to accuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a court of law, then the Kannada for a judge.

You'll want to know: ಆರೋಪಿಸು.
ಆರೋಪಿಸು — ārōpisu — "to accuse".
ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನು ಆ ಹುಡುಗ ದುಡ್ಡು ಕದ್ದ ಅಂತ ಆರೋಪಿಸಿದನು. aṅgaḍiyavanu ā huḍuga duḍḍu kadda anta ārōpisidanu. The shopkeeper accused that boy of stealing money.
Past (I …): ಆರೋಪಿಸಿದೆ, ārōpiside.
The charge comes before ಅಂತ. The charge itself is an ಆರೋಪ, and ಆರೋಪಿ is the accused.

The word, taken apart.
ಆರೋಪಿಸು (ārōpisu) is built on Sanskrit āropa, "placing on, charging with", the noun beside āropaṇa, "placing on" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತೀರ್ಪು (tīrpu) — a verdict; a judgement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a judge, then the Kannada for to accuse.

You'll want to know: ತೀರ್ಪು.
ತೀರ್ಪು — tīrpu — "a verdict; a judgement".
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಸೋಮವಾರ ತೀರ್ಪು ಕೊಟ್ಟರು. nyāyādhīśaru sōmavāra tīrpu koṭṭaru. The judge gave the verdict on Monday.
A verdict is given: ತೀರ್ಪು (tīrpu) ಕೊಡು or, more formally, ತೀರ್ಪು (tīrpu) ನೀಡು.

The word, taken apart.
ತೀರ್ಪು (tīrpu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3278 lists Kannada tīrpu, "finishing, concluding", under tīr, "to end, be settled, be decided". A verdict settles a case.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ (śikṣe) — a punishment; a sentence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to accuse, then the Kannada for a verdict.

You'll want to know: ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ.
ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ — śikṣe — "a punishment; a sentence".
ಕಳ್ಳನಿಗೆ ಎರಡು ವರ್ಷದ ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ ಆಯಿತು. kaḷḷanige eraḍu varṣada śikṣe āyitu. The thief got a two-year sentence.
The one punished takes -ಗೆ: ಕಳ್ಳನಿಗೆ ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ (śikṣe) ಆಯಿತು, the thief was punished.

The word, taken apart.
ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ (śikṣe) is borrowed from Sanskrit śikṣā, which meant "learning, study" and also "punishment, correction" (CDIAL 12432). Kannada kept the second sense.
