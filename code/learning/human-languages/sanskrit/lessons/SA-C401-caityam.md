## You'll want to know: चैत्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C401-BHIKSHU401-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चैत्यम्** — *caityam* — "a memorial shrine; a sacred mound".

> विहारस्य समीपे प्राचीनं चैत्यम् अस्ति।
>
> *vihārasya samīpe prācīnaṃ caityam asti.*
>
> Near the monastery there is an ancient shrine.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चैत्यम्** (*caityam*) is built on *citā*, "a funeral pile", or *citi*, "a heap", as MW gives it: MW gives *caitya* as a funeral monument or stūpa, a sacred tree on a mound, and a temple or place of worship (MW).
