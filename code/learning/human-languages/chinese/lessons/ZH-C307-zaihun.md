## You'll want to know: zàihūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C307-SPLIT307-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zàihūn* — "to remarry".

> *tā líhūn yǐhòu, hěn kuài jiù zàihūn le* — After his divorce he soon married again.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zàihūn* is built from 再 'again' and *hūn* 'marriage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsojH* and *xwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'twice'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
