# HL110 — Punjabi A2 `ਨੀਲੀ` feminine-singular prerequisite

Issue: #18534

After PR #18530 merged, the maintained aggregate-only audit of the two
preserved confidential Punjabi A2 mock artifacts selected **ਨੀਲੀ** as the next
dependency-ordered exact form without an instructional owner. It occurs once
in the confidential learner material and twice in the confidential examiner
material. Neither confidential body nor the remaining candidate set belongs
in the public corpus.

Chapter 209 closes that one-form gap through one deliberately narrow frame:
**ਇਹ ਨੀਲੀ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਹੈ** means “This is a blue shoe.” The voice-first opener
retrieves only already-owned **ਨੀਲਾ**, **ਜੁੱਤੀ**, **ਇਹ**, and **ਹੈ**. One
grammar lesson changes the known masculine-singular ending of **ਨੀਲਾ** to own
exact feminine singular **ਨੀਲੀ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound,
meaning, and script, a bounded frame lesson combines it with the familiar
feminine noun, demonstrative, and copula, and a four-skill payoff retrieves the
complete frame again. The target has one direct owner and three later
retrievals. It must not leak into authored, generated-book, or narration copy
before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-FORM-NILI-209`. The direct owner presents **ਨੀਲੀ** only as
the feminine singular direct form of the already-owned inflectable adjective
**ਨੀਲਾ** before the already-owned feminine noun **ਜੁੱਤੀ**. The chapter does not
teach masculine plural, feminine plural, oblique forms, a complete agreement
paradigm, a general predicative-versus-attributive contrast, or any new colour
word. Chapter 177 already owns the bounded adjective-agreement rule; Chapter
209 retrieves that rule rather than introducing a duplicate grammar atom.

Every Gurmukhi codepoint in **ਨੀਲੀ** is already script-taught and already
appears in public headwords. The base **ਨੀਲਾ** is directly owned by
`PA-C84-nila`; **ਜੁੱਤੀ** by `PA-C94-jutti`; **ਇਹ** by `PA-C176-eh`; **ਹੈ** by
`PA-C02-hai`; and the feminine agreement atom by
`PA-C177-adjective-agreement`. This chapter therefore adds no vocabulary or
script owner.

Punjabi University Patiala's *A Reference Grammar of Punjabi*, sections 5.8
and 6.3, divides adjectives into inflectable and uninflectable classes and
states that inflectable adjectives agree with their head noun in gender,
number, and case. Its noun-phrase examples contrast masculine **ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ**
with feminine **ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ**:
<https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/assets/a%20reference%20grammar_final.pdf>.
Chapter 84's existing CDIAL 7563 citation establishes the inherited adjective
**ਨੀਲਾ** and its meaning “blue”; Chapter 209 makes no new etymological claim.

Those sources support the one ending change and the one bounded attributive
frame. They do not justify presenting the wider adjective paradigm in this
tranche, so the course keeps every excluded form out of learner copy and tests
the exclusion explicitly.

This is the thirty-second prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test
case; no shared aggregate is introduced.
