Urdu, chapter 97: Straight, In front, Behind, Middle, Edge.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سیدھا (sīdhā) — straight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to the right, then the Urdu for to the left.

You'll want to know: سیدھا.
سیدھا — sīdhā — "straight".

The word, taken apart.
سیدھا (sīdhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from siddha, "accomplished, perfected" (CDIAL 13401). CDIAL glosses the Hindi word "straight, honest".

Lesson 3 of 5.
پیچھے (pīchhe) — behind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for straight, then the Urdu for in front.

You'll want to know: پیچھے.
پیچھے — pīchhe — "behind".

The word, taken apart.
پیچھے (pīchhe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from paśca, "hinder part", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7990). pīchhe is an old case form that took the place of Sanskrit paścāt, "behind".
