# வந்த போது (vanta pōtu) — when it happened, and what was there

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C79-WHEN-03, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "when you come", with the joining you know. (*nīṅkaḷ varum pōtu*.) Then "I was reading". (*paṭittukkoṇṭiruntēṉ*.)

[PAUSE 1s] **வரும் போது** looks ahead. A story needs the same joint looking back.

## You'll want to know: -த போது, 'when, back then'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C284-WHEN-PAST-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-VEEDU-01, TA-LEX-KUDUMBAM-02, TA-LEX-C150-QUAL150-04, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

> **நான் வீட்டுக்கு வந்த போது அம்மா சமைத்துக்கொண்டிருந்தார்.**
>
> *nāṉ vīṭṭukku vanta pōtu ammā camaittukkoṇṭiruntār.* — "When I came home, Mother was cooking."

**வந்த** is the past with **-அ** in place of the person ending: **வந்தேன்** → **வந்த**. Before **போது**, "time", it is "when (someone) came", the same for every person: **அவள் வந்த போது**. "When (someone) went" is **போன போது**. With **-கொண்டிருந்த** it is "while": **நான் சாப்பிட்டுக்கொண்டிருந்த போது** (*nāṉ cāppiṭṭukkoṇṭirunta pōtu*), "while I was eating".

## You'll want to know: இருந்தது, 'there was'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C284-THERE-WAS-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-UUR-01, TA-LEX-C41-ADJ-02, TA-LEX-C68-SHOP-01, TA-LEX-C41-ADJ-01, TA-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-04, TA-LEX-C94-LOST-01] -->

> **எங்கள் ஊரில் ஒரு சிறிய கடை இருந்தது.**
>
> *eṅkaḷ ūril oru ciṟiya kaṭai iruntatu.* — "There was a small shop in our town."

**எங்கள்** (*eṅkaḷ*, "our"); **ஊரில்** (*ūril*, "in the town"). **இருந்தது**, "it was", is also "there was". Set it against the present:

> **இப்போது அங்கே ஒரு பெரிய கடை இருக்கிறது.**
>
> *ippōtu aṅkē oru periya kaṭai irukkiṟatu.* — "Now there is a big shop there."

The past "did not" is one form for every person: **-வில்லை**, the ending of **புரியவில்லை**, on the verb's to-form, a form the lesson on "must" takes out. **அவன் வரவில்லை** (*avaṉ varavillai*, "he did not come"), **நான் போகவில்லை** (*nāṉ pōkavillai*, "I did not go"), **அவர்கள் சாப்பிடவில்லை** (*avarkaḷ cāppiṭavillai*, "they did not eat").
