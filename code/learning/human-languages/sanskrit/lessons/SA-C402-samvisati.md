## You'll want to know: संविशति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C402-DHARMASHALA402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संविशति** — *saṃviśati* — "lies down to sleep".

> रात्रौ यात्रिकाः धर्मशालायां संविशन्ति।
>
> *rātrau yātrikāḥ dharmaśālāyāṃ saṃviśanti.*
>
> At night the pilgrims lie down to sleep in the rest house.

Past (he …): सः धर्मशालायां संविष्टवान्, *saḥ dharmaśālāyāṃ saṃviṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संविशति** (*saṃviśati*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √viś, "to enter, to settle"; MW gives *saṃ-viś* as to lie down or go to rest, and its participle *saṃviṣṭa* as one who has lain down, sleeping (MW).
