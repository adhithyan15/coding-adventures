## You'll want to know: じんせい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C292-JINSEI292-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じんせい** — *jinsei* — "a life; one's lifetime".

> **日本人の じんせいは ながく なりました。** — *nihonjin no jinsei wa nagaku narimashita* — "Japanese people's lives have grown longer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じんせい** (*jinsei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jin*, "person" (Middle Chinese *nyin*) and *sei*, "life" (*srjaeng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
