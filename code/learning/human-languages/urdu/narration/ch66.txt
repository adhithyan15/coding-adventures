Urdu, chapter 66: Sun, Moon, Sea, Fire, Snow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سورج (sūraj) — the sun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a star, then the Urdu for a cloud.

You'll want to know: سورج.
سورج — sūraj — "the sun".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does سورج (sūraj) mean? ("The sun".) Say it once more.
