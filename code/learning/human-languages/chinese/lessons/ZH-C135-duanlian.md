## You'll want to know: duànliàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C135-SPORT135-05]; assesses=[] -->

*duànliàn* — "to exercise; to work out".

> *wǒ xǐhuan zǎoshang duànliàn* — I like working out in the morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duànliàn* is built from *duàn* 'to forge iron' and *liàn* 'to refine metal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *twanH* and *lenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The pair said of the body is not given there.
