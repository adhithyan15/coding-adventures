Lesson 1 of 6.
ನೆರವೇರಿಸು (neravērisu) — to carry out (a rite); to perform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a victim, then the Kannada for a forewarning.

You'll want to know: ನೆರವೇರಿಸು.
ನೆರವೇರಿಸು — neravērisu — "to carry out (a rite); to perform".
ಅರ್ಚಕರು ಮದುವೆಯ ಪೂಜೆಯನ್ನು ನೆರವೇರಿಸಿದರು. arcakaru maduveya pūjeyannu neravērisidaru. The priest performed the wedding puja.
Past (I …): ನೆರವೇರಿಸಿದೆ, neravēriside.
Rites and ceremonies are carried out with it. A wish or a plan that comes true ನೆರವೇರುತ್ತದೆ. Before a rite is carried out, the house is made ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ, spotless.

The word, taken apart.
ನೆರವೇರಿಸು (neravērisu) is usually traced to the native Dravidian neṟe, "to become complete, to be realized, to occur" (DEDR 3682), with the causative ending -isu.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಸಮಾರಂಭ (samārambha) — a ceremony; a function.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a forewarning, then the Kannada for to carry out.

You'll want to know: ಸಮಾರಂಭ.
ಸಮಾರಂಭ — samārambha — "a ceremony; a function".
ಶಾಲೆಯ ವಾರ್ಷಿಕ ಸಮಾರಂಭದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಹಾಡಿದರು. śāleya vārṣika samārambhadalli makkaḷu hāḍidaru. The children sang at the school's annual function.
Plural: ಸಮಾರಂಭಗಳು, samārambhagaḷu.
Any formal gathering: a wedding, a prize-giving, an opening.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಾರಂಭ (samārambha) is borrowed from Sanskrit samārambha, "an undertaking, an enterprise" (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it became an organised event.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಅರ್ಪಿಸು (arpisu) — to offer; to dedicate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to carry out, then the Kannada for a ceremony.

You'll want to know: ಅರ್ಪಿಸು.
ಅರ್ಪಿಸು — arpisu — "to offer; to dedicate".
ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಹಿರಿಯರಿಗೆ ಹೂವು ಅರ್ಪಿಸಿದರು. makkaḷu hiriyarige hūvu arpisidaru. The children offered flowers to the elders.
Past (I …): ಅರ್ಪಿಸಿದೆ, arpiside.
What is offered takes -ಅನ್ನು or stands bare; to whom takes -ಗೆ. Books are dedicated too: ಪುಸ್ತಕವನ್ನು ಅಮ್ಮನಿಗೆ ಅರ್ಪಿಸು (arpisu).

The word, taken apart.
ಅರ್ಪಿಸು (arpisu) is built on Sanskrit arpaṇa, "procuring, placing upon, handing over" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸು (ērpaḍisu) — to set up; to arrange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ceremony, then the Kannada for to offer.

You'll want to know: ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸು.
ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸು — ērpaḍisu — "to set up; to arrange".
ಅಪ್ಪ ಮದುವೆಗೆ ಊಟ ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸಿದರು. appa maduvege ūṭa ērpaḍisidaru. Father arranged the meal for the wedding.
Past (I …): ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, ērpaḍiside.
ಏರ್ಪಡು is to come about; ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸು (ērpaḍisu) is to make it come about. The noun is ಏರ್ಪಾಡು, an arrangement.

The word, taken apart.
ಏರ್ಪಡಿಸು (ērpaḍisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 905 lists Kannada ērpaḍu, "to come about", and ērpaḍisu, beside Tamil ēl, "to happen, to be suitable".

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
You'll want to know: ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ.
ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ — āmantraṇa — "an invitation card; an invitation".
ಮದುವೆಯ ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ ಅಂಚೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂತು. maduveya āmantraṇa añceyalli bantu. The wedding invitation came by post.
Plural: ಆಮಂತ್ರಣಗಳು, āmantraṇagaḷu.
In full the printed card is ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ (āmantraṇa) ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ; ಆಹ್ವಾನ is the general word for an invitation.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ (āmantraṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit āmantraṇa, "addressing, calling to, summoning" (Monier-Williams).
