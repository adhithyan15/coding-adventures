## You'll want to know: えんか

**えんか** — *enka* — "an enka ballad".

> **ちちは えんかが すきです。** — *chichi wa enka ga suki desu* — "My father likes enka ballads."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんか** (*enka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *en*, "performance" (Middle Chinese *yenX*) and *ka*, "song" (*ka*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
