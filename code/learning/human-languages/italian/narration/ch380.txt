Italian, chapter 380: All'università.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la facoltà — the faculty (of a university); the ability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the school report, then the Italian for the head teacher.

You'll want to know: la facoltà.
la facoltà — "the faculty (of a university); the ability".
Studio alla facoltà di lettere. I study in the arts faculty.
Also a power or ability: la facoltà di scegliere.

The word, taken apart.
facoltà is a learned borrowing from Latin facultās "ability, power, means" (Lewis & Short), related to facilis "easy to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.
la matricola — the first-year student; the registration number.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the head teacher, then the Italian for the faculty.

You'll want to know: la matricola.
la matricola — "the first-year student; the registration number".
Le matricole hanno lezione nell'aula grande. The first-year students have lessons in the big lecture hall.
La matricola is also your student number. One form for men and women.

The word, taken apart.
matricola is a learned borrowing from Late Latin mātrīcula "a public register", diminutive of mātrīx "a register, a womb" (Lewis & Short), built on māter "mother".

Lesson 3 of 5.
iscriversi — to enrol, to sign up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the faculty, then the Italian for the first-year student.

You'll want to know: iscriversi.
iscriversi — "to enrol, to sign up".
Se ti iscrivi entro settembre, paghi meno. If you enrol by September, you pay less.
Iscriversi a — to sign up for: a un corso, in palestra. Past participle iscritto.

The word, taken apart.
iscriversi comes from Latin īnscrībere "to write on, to enter in a list" (Lewis & Short), built from in and scrībere "to write": you put your name on the list.

L'iscrizione costa cento euro. Registration costs a hundred euros.
