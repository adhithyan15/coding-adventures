## You'll want to know: ruir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C302-SISMO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ruir** — "to collapse, to fall down".

> O velho prédio ruiu durante a noite.
>
> *The old building collapsed during the night.*

Mostly in the third person: *ruiu, ruíram*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruir* comes from Latin *ruere* "to rush down, to fall" (EtymDB).
