Marathi, chapter 456: Shyness and Confidence.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आत्मविश्वास (ātmaviśvās) — self-confidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to toss and turn, then the Marathi for to feel stifled.

You'll want to know: आत्मविश्वास.
आत्मविश्वास — ātmaviśvās — "self-confidence".
सरावानंतर तिचा आत्मविश्वास वाढला. sarāvānantar ticā ātmaviśvās vāḍhlā. Her self-confidence grew after practice.
Masculine.
आत्म-, self, + विश्वास, trust. आत्मविश्वासाने, confidently.

The word, taken apart.
आत्मविश्वास (ātmaviśvās) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, ātman, "self", and viśvāsa, "trust" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
संकोच (saṅkoc) — hesitation; reserve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to feel stifled, then the Marathi for self-confidence.

You'll want to know: संकोच.
संकोच — saṅkoc — "hesitation; reserve".
मोठ्या माणसांसमोर बोलायला त्याला संकोच वाटतो. moṭhyā māṇsāṁsamor bolāylā tyālā saṅkoc vāṭto. He feels shy about speaking in front of grown-ups.
Masculine.
संकोच (saṅkoc) वाटणे, to feel shy about something: the dative plus the -ायला form. संकोची, reserved.

The word, taken apart.
संकोच (saṅkoc) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit saṃkoca, "contraction, shrinking together" (Monier-Williams); the shrinking is now of the self.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for self-confidence, then the Marathi for hesitation.

You'll want to know: बिचकणे.
बिचकणे — bicakṇe — "to shrink back".
कुत्रा जवळ आला तेव्हा मुलगी बिचकली. kutrā javaḷ ālā tevhā mulgī bicaklī. The girl shrank back when the dog came near.
Past (he …): तो बिचकला, to bicaklā.
A small start of fear or doubt; बिचकत बिचकत, hesitantly.

The word, taken apart.
बिचकणे (bicakṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
