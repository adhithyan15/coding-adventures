## You'll want to know: लाचार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C551-MAJBUR551-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लाचार** — *lācār* — "helpless".

> पैर टूटने के बाद वह कुछ दिन लाचार था।
>
> *Pair ṭūṭne ke bād vah kuch din lācār thā.*
>
> After breaking his leg he was helpless for a few days.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाचार** (*lācār*) is usually traced to Persian *lāchār*, "without remedy, helpless".
