Telugu, chapter 169: Sore, Blister, Rash, Scar, Sprain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పుండు (puṇḍu) — a sore, an ulcer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for peace, then the Telugu for patience.

You'll want to know: పుండు.
పుండు — puṇḍu — "a sore, an ulcer".

The word, taken apart.
పుండు (puṇḍu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil puṇ and Kannada puṇ (DEDR 4268). DEDR lists Telugu puṇḍu, "sore, ulcer, boil".

బొబ్బ — bobba — "a blister".

The word, taken apart.
బొబ్బ (bobba) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4455 lists bobba, "blister", with Kannada bobbe and Malayalam pokkuḷa, "blister". DEDR compares Nepali phoko, "blister" (CDIAL 8391), a word group CDIAL thinks probably came from Dravidian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
దద్దుర్లు (daddurlu) — a rash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sore, then the Telugu for a blister.

You'll want to know: దద్దుర్లు.
దద్దుర్లు — daddurlu — "a rash".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of దద్దుర్లు (daddurlu) is uncertain. DEDR has no entry for it. Sanskrit dadru, "skin eruption" (CDIAL 6142), is close in shape and sense, but CDIAL lists no Telugu word.
