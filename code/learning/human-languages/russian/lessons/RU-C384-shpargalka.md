## You'll want to know: шпаргалка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C384-UCHYOBA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**шпаргалка** (*shpargálka*) — "a crib sheet".

> На экзамене у него нашли шпаргалку.
>
> *Na ekzámene u negó nashlí shpargálku.*
>
> They found a crib sheet on him during the exam.

Feminine. Colloquial; students often say *шпора*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**шпаргалка** is usually traced to Polish *szpargał* "an old scrap of paper"; the further origin is not settled.
