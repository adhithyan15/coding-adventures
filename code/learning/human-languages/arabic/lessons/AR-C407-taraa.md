## You'll want to know: طرأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C407-TAJANNUB407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طرأ** — *ṭaraʾa* — "to arise unexpectedly".

> طرأ تغيير على الخطة.
>
> *ṭaraʾa taghyīr ʿalā al-khiṭṭa.*
>
> A change came up in the plan.

Present: يطرأ, *yaṭraʾ*.

It takes على: طرأ على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طرأ** (*ṭaraʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-r-ʾ*, "to come upon unexpectedly" (Lane). *Ṭaraʾa* is a Form I verb: to come from far off without warning, to arise.
