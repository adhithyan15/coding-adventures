Portuguese, chapter 174: To cross, To increase, To decrease, To warn, To be enough.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
atravessar — to cross.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to grow old, then the Portuguese for to marry.

You'll want to know: atravessar.
atravessar — "to cross".

The word, taken apart.
atravessar is built on an old word for "across", from Latin transversus, "turned across" (Lewis & Short).
