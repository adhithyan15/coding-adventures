## You'll want to know: el conocimiento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C598-SABER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el conocimiento** — "the knowledge".

> Tiene buenos conocimientos de inglés.
>
> *She has a good knowledge of English.*

In the plural it means what you know about a subject.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conocimiento* is built on *conocer*, inherited from Latin *cognoscere* 'to get to know' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
