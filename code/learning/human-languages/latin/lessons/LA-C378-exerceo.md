## You'll want to know: exerceō, exercēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C378-TEXO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**exerceō, exercēre** — "to train, to practise; to keep busy".

> Pistor artem suam multōs annōs exercuit.
>
> *The baker practised his trade for many years.*

*exerceō, exercēre, exercuī, exercitum*. *Artem exercēre*, to practise a craft; *sē exercēre*, to train. English *exercise*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exerceō* is built from *ex* and *arceō* "to shut in, to keep off" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to drive animals out to work, then to keep anyone busy. English *exercise* comes from it.
