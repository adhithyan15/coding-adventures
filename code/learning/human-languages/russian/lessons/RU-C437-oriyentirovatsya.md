## You'll want to know: ориентироваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C437-ZABLUDITSYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ориентироваться** (*oriyentírovatsya*) — "to find one's way, to get one's bearings".

> В новом городе я плохо ориентируюсь.
>
> *V nóvom górode ya plókho oriyentíruyus.*
>
> I am bad at finding my way in a new town.

Imperfective; perfective *сориентироваться*. Takes *в* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ориентироваться* is usually traced to French *orienter* 'to orient, to set facing north' (EtymDB), on Latin *oriēns* 'rising; the east' (Lewis & Short).
