# పసిగట్టు (pasigaṭṭu) — to sense; to detect

## You'll want to know: పసిగట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C329-DONGATANAM329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పసిగట్టు** — *pasigaṭṭu* — "to sense; to detect".

> కుక్క దొంగను వెంటనే పసిగట్టింది.
>
> *kukka doṅganu veṇṭanē pasigaṭṭindi.*
>
> The dog sensed the thief at once.

Past (I …): పసిగట్టాను, *pasigaṭṭānu*.

A dog senses a stranger, and so does a sharp mind: పసిగట్టు works for both.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పసిగట్టు** (*pasigaṭṭu*) is a Telugu compound whose first part is *pasi*, "smell, scent" (DEDR 3826); the origin of the whole word is not settled.
