## You'll want to know: ಕೇರಂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C531-ATIKE531-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇರಂ** — *kēraṃ* — "carrom".

> ರಜೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ದಿನವೂ ಕೇರಂ ಆಡುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *rajeyalli makkaḷu dinavū kēraṃ āḍuttāre.*
>
> In the holidays the children play carrom every day.

Players flick a striker to pocket the pieces.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇರಂ** (*kēraṃ*) is a loan from English *carrom*, the name of the board game.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
