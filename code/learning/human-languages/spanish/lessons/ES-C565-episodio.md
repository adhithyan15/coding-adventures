## You'll want to know: el episodio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C565-ESCENA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el episodio** — "the episode".

> Fue un episodio triste de su vida.
>
> *It was a sad episode in her life.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*episodio* is usually traced to Greek *epeisódion* 'an added part between the songs of a play', built on *eísodos* 'a way in', from *hodós* 'way'.
