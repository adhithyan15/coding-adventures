# પરોણાગત (paroṇāgat) — hospitality (to a guest)

## You'll want to know: પરોણાગત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C409-LODGE409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પરોણાગત** — *paroṇāgat* — "hospitality (to a guest)".

> આ હોટેલની પરોણાગત ઘણી સારી છે.
>
> *ā hoṭelnī paroṇāgat ghaṇī sārī che.*
>
> The hospitality at this hotel is very good.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરોણાગત** (*paroṇāgat*) is built on પરોણો, a guest, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *prāhuṇa*, "a guest" (CDIAL 8973): the care a house gives to the guest who stays in it.
