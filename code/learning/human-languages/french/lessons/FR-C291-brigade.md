## You'll want to know: la brigade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C291-GENDARMES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la brigade** — "the squad, the brigade".

> Une brigade de police est arrivée en dix minutes.
>
> *A police squad arrived in ten minutes.*

*La brigade des stupéfiants* — the drug squad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brigade* was borrowed from Italian *brigata* "a troop, a company" (EtymDB), built on *briga* "strife, trouble".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
