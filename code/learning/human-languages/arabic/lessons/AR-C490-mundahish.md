# مندهش (mundahish) — astonished

## You'll want to know: مندهش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C490-DAHSHA490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مندهش** — *mundahish* — "astonished".

> كان أبي مندهشا من الخبر.
>
> *kāna abī mundahishan min al-khabar.*
>
> My father was astonished by the news.

After كان it takes the accusative: كان مندهشا.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مندهش** (*mundahish*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-h-sh*, "to be amazed, to be bewildered" (Lane). Mundahish is the participle of Form VII: one who has been struck with amazement.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
