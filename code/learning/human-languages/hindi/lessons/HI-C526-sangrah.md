## You'll want to know: संग्रह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C526-FURSAT526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संग्रह** — *saṅgrah* — "a collection".

> अरुण के पास पुराने सिक्कों का बड़ा संग्रह है।
>
> *Aruṇ ke pās purāne sikkoṁ kā baṛā saṅgrah hai.*
>
> Arun has a big collection of old coins.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संग्रह** (*saṅgrah*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *saṃgraha*, "holding together, gathering" (Monier-Williams).
