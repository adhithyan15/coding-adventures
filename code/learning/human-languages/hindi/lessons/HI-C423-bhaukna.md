## You'll want to know: भौंकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C423-GARD423-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भौंकना** — *bhaũknā* — "to bark".

> रात को गली के कुत्ते बहुत भौंकते हैं।
>
> *rāt ko galī ke kutte bahut bhaũkte haiṁ.*
>
> The dogs in the lane bark a lot at night.

Past (it …): वह कुत्ता रात भर भौंका, *vah kuttā rāt bhar bhaũkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भौंकना** (*bhaũknā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *bukkati*, "barks" (CDIAL 9265), which lists Hindi *bhaũknā*.
