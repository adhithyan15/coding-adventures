## You'll want to know: சாது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C472-MANITAR472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**சாது** — *cātu* — "a meek person; a gentle soul".

> என் தாத்தா ஒரு சாது, அவருக்குக் கோபமே வராது.
>
> *eṉ tāttā oru cātu, avarukkuk kōpamē varātu.*
>
> My grandfather is a gentle soul; he never gets angry.

Also a holy man; சாதுவான மாடு, a docile cow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சாது** (*cātu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sādhu*, "straight, good; a good man" (CDIAL 13337).
