French, chapter 74: Roof, Garden, Lamp, Bathroom, Bedroom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le toit — a roof.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a key, then the French for a wall.

You'll want to know: le toit.
le toit — "a roof".

The word, taken apart.
toit from Latin tectum, "roof", from tegere, "to cover" (EtymDB 2.1 records the Latin word) — inherited: Latin -ct- became French -it, as in lait from lactem.
