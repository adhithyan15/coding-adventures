## You'll want to know: رژه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C342-TAZYIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رژه** (*rezhe*) — "a parade, a march-past".

> روز جشن رژه از جلوی خانه ما رد شد.
>
> *Ruz-e jashn rezhe az jelo-ye khâne-ye mâ radd shod.*
>
> On the day of the celebration the parade passed our house.

Written with ژ. *Rezhe raftan* "to march in a parade".
