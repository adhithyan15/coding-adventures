## You'll want to know: ひきかえす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C392-YORIMICHI392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきかえす** — *hikikaesu* — "to turn back".

> **みちを まちがえたので、ひきかえしました。** — *michi o machigaeta node, hikikaeshimashita* — "I took the wrong road, so I turned back."

Polite **ひきかえします**, *hikikaeshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが えきまで ひきかえしました。**, *chichi ga eki made hikikaeshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきかえす** (*hikikaesu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hiki*, "pulling" (from *hiku*) and *kaesu*, "to send back"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
