## You'll want to know: hǎibiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C227-SEA227-01]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎibiān* — "the seaside; the seashore".

> *shàng ge yuè wǒmen qùle hǎibiān* — Last month we went to the seaside.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎibiān* is built from *hǎi* 'sea' and 边 'edge', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xojX* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as the pool of heaven that takes in a hundred rivers.
