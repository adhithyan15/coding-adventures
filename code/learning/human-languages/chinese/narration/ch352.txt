Mandarin Chinese, chapter 352: Same Here.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
同感 (tónggǎn) — the same feeling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for an ideal to live up to, then the Mandarin for a person's state of mind.

You'll want to know: 同感.
同感 — tónggǎn — "the same feeling".
我也有同感 wǒ yě yǒu tónggǎn — I feel the same.

The word, taken apart.
同感 (tónggǎn) is built from 同 'the same' and 感 'to feel', both inherited from Middle Chinese, duwng and komX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'equal' and 'together', and the second 'to move'.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons.
