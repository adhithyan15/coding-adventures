# ಉಸ್ತುವಾರಿ (ustuvāri) — oversight; charge

## You'll want to know: ಉಸ್ತುವಾರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C521-DUDIME521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಸ್ತುವಾರಿ** — *ustuvāri* — "oversight; charge".

> ಹೊಸ ಕಟ್ಟಡದ ಕೆಲಸದ ಉಸ್ತುವಾರಿ ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣನದು.
>
> *hosa kaṭṭaḍada kelasada ustuvāri nanna aṇṇanadu.*
>
> My elder brother is in charge of the new building work.

ಉಸ್ತುವಾರಿ ವಹಿಸು, to take charge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಸ್ತುವಾರಿ** (*ustuvāri*) came into Kannada with the language of offices; its source is not settled.
