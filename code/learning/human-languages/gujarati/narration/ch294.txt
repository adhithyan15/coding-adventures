Lesson 1 of 5.
લપસવું (lapasvũ) — to slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for serious, then the Gujarati for to panic.

You'll want to know: લપસવું.
લપસવું — lapasvũ — "to slip".
વરસાદમાં મીરા પગથિયાં પર લપસી ગઈ. varsādmā̃ Mīrā pagthiyā̃ par lapsī gaī. In the rain Mira slipped on the steps.
Past (she …): તે લપસી ગઈ, te lapsī gaī.

The word, taken apart.
લપસવું (lapasvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. A wet floor makes you લપસી જવું: ભીની ભોંય પર લપસી ગઈ, she slipped on the wet floor.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઉઝરડો (ujharḍo) — a graze; a scratch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to panic, then the Gujarati for to slip.

You'll want to know: ઉઝરડો.
ઉઝરડો — ujharḍo — "a graze; a scratch".
મીરાના ઘૂંટણ પર મોટો ઉઝરડો હતો. Mīrānā ghū̃ṭaṇ par moṭo ujharḍo hato. Mira had a big graze on her knee.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉઝરડો (ujharḍo) comes from ujjharati, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists Gujarati ujharṛo, "a scratch" (CDIAL 1675). It is skin scraped in a fall: ઘૂંટણ પર ઉઝરડો.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to slip, then the Gujarati for a graze.

You'll want to know: ગબડવું.
ગબડવું — gabaḍvũ — "to tumble".
છોકરો સાઈકલ પરથી ગબડી પડ્યો. chokro sāīkal parthī gabḍī paḍyo. The boy tumbled off his bicycle.
Past (he …): તે છોકરો ગબડી પડ્યો, te chokro gabḍī paḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
ગબડવું (gabaḍvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. It is falling and rolling over: ગબડી પડ્યો, he went tumbling down.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઢાળ (ḍhāḷ) — a slope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a graze, then the Gujarati for to tumble.

You'll want to know: ઢાળ.
ઢાળ — ḍhāḷ — "a slope".
આ રસ્તા પર મોટો ઢાળ છે. ā rastā par moṭo ḍhāḷ che. This road has a steep slope.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઢાળ (ḍhāḷ) probably belongs with ḍhaḷvũ, to slip, to be poured out, which CDIAL sets under ḍhalati (CDIAL 5581): ground that falls away. A bicycle goes fast ઢાળ પર, on a slope.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભાન (bhān) — consciousness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to tumble, then the Gujarati for a slope.

You'll want to know: ભાન.
ભાન — bhān — "consciousness".
અડધા કલાક પછી તે ભાનમાં આવ્યો. aḍdhā kalāk pachī te bhānmā̃ āvyo. Half an hour later he came round.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભાન (bhān) is Sanskrit bhāna, "appearance, perception" (Monier-Williams): in Gujarati, being aware of oneself and the world. ભાનમાં આવ્યો, he came round; બેભાન is its opposite.
