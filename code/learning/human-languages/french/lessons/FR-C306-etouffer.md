## You'll want to know: étouffer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C306-PIQURES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**étouffer** — "to choke (as s'étouffer); to suffocate, to stifle".

> Il s'est étouffé avec un bonbon.
>
> *He choked on a sweet.*

For choking on food, the reflexive *s'étouffer*. Without *se*, to smother or to stifle: *on étouffe ici !* — it is stifling in here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étouffer* comes from Old French *estouffer* (EtymDB); the origin of the root is not settled.
