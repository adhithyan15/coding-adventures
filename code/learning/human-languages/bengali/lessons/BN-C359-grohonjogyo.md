## You'll want to know: গ্রহণযোগ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C359-OJUHAT359-05]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রহণযোগ্য** — *grôhônjogyô* — "acceptable".

> তোমার অজুহাতটা গ্রহণযোগ্য নয়।
>
> *tomār ôjuhātṭā grôhônjogyô nôy.*
>
> Your excuse is not acceptable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রহণযোগ্য** (*grôhônjogyô*) is Sanskrit *grahaṇa*, taking or acceptance, with *yogya*, fit for (Monier-Williams): fit to be accepted. An excuse, a proposal or a reason can be গ্রহণযোগ্য; the compound itself is not in the dictionary.
