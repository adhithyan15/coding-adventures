French, chapter 522: Quel genre de livres ?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la poésie — poetry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for signed message, then the French for to sign.

You'll want to know: la poésie.
la poésie — "poetry".
Mon père lit de la poésie le soir. My father reads poetry in the evening.
Un poème is a single poem.

The word, taken apart.
la poésie comes from Latin poēsis (EtymDB), a borrowing from Greek poíēsis "a making, a poem".

Lesson 2 of 5.
la bande dessinée — comic book, comic strip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to sign, then the French for poetry.

You'll want to know: la bande dessinée.
la bande dessinée — "comic book, comic strip".
Les adultes aussi lisent des bandes dessinées. Adults read comics too.
Informally la BD. A big art form in France and Belgium.

The word, taken apart.
la bande dessinée is "a drawn strip": bande is of Germanic origin, and dessiner comes from Italian disegnare, from Latin dēsignāre "to mark out", built on signum "a sign" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
le polar — crime novel, detective story (informal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for poetry, then the French for comic book.

You'll want to know: le polar.
le polar — "crime novel, detective story (informal)".
En été, je lis surtout des polars. In summer I mostly read crime novels.
Informal for le roman policier. Also a crime film.

The word, taken apart.
le polar is a slang shortening of (roman) policier "detective (novel)" with the familiar ending -ar (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la biographie — biography.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la biographie.
la biographie — "biography".
Je lis la biographie d'un grand musicien. I am reading the biography of a great musician.
Une autobiographie — an autobiography.

The word, taken apart.
la biographie is a learned word built from Greek bíos "life" and gráphein "to write", by way of Medieval Greek biographía.

Lesson 5 of 5.
le conte — tale, story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: le conte.
le conte — "tale, story".
Le soir, la mère lit un conte à ses enfants. In the evening the mother reads her children a story.
Un conte de fées — a fairy tale.

The word, taken apart.
le conte is built on conter "to tell", inherited from Latin computāre "to count, to reckon", built on putāre (Lewis & Short): to recount.
