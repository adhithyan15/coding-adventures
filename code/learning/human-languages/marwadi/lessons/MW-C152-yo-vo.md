# यो and वो — this one here, that one there

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-HE-01, MW-LEX-C101-DEF101-01, MW-CONJ-AR-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ALTERNATIVE-CHOICE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *he is a boy*. Then the near word you know, **यो** (*yo*), "this".
And offer the old choice once more: **चाय अर पाणी?** (*chāy ar pāṇī?*)

## Grammar Lens: वो points away
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-THAT-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C101-DEF101-01, MW-SCRIPT-MHARO-01, MW-SCRIPT-GHAR-01, MW-SCRIPT-DUKAN-01] -->

**वो** also points at a thing away from you: *that*. **यो** is the near one,
*this*.

> **यो म्हारो घर है।**
>
> *yo mhāro ghar hai.* — This is my house.

> **वो दुकान है।**
>
> *vo dukān hai.* — That is a shop.

So **वो** is *he* or *that*: a man, or a thing over there.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-THAT-01, MW-LEX-C101-DEF101-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ALTERNATIVE-CHOICE-01, MW-FUNCTION-ASK-PREFERENCE-ALTERNATIVES-01, MW-CONJ-AR-01, MW-SCRIPT-PAANI-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01] -->

Offer two things the way you offered tea or water: **यो अर वो?** (*yo ar vo?*),
this or that?

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **यो पाणी है।** — *yo pāṇī hai.* — This is water.]
- [YOU SAY: **वो चाय है।** — *vo chāy hai.* — That is tea.]
- [YOU SAY: **यो अर वो?** — *yo ar vo?* — This or that?]
- [YOU SAY: **वो।** — *vo.* — That one.]
