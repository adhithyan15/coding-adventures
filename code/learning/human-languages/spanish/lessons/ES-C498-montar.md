## You'll want to know: montar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C498-DEPORTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**montar** — "to ride; to set up".

> Monto en bici los domingos.
>
> *I ride my bike on Sundays.*

*Montar a caballo* — to ride a horse. In much of Latin America people say *andar en bici*.
