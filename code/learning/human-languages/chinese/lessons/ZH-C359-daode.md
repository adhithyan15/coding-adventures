## You'll want to know: dàodé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C359-RULES359-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dàodé* — "morality; ethics".

> *wǒ juéde zhè shì dàodé wèntí* — I think it's a moral question.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàodé* is built from 道 'a way' and *dé* 'virtue', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dawX* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a principle' and the second 'virtuous conduct'.
