## You'll want to know: entretanto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C270-ENTRETANTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**entretanto** — "meanwhile, in the meantime; however".

> Eu fiz o jantar; entretanto, o meu irmão pôs a mesa.
>
> *I made dinner; meanwhile my brother set out the plates and glasses.*

In formal writing it can also mean ‘however’; in Brazil that is the main sense.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entretanto* joins *entre* "between" and *tanto* "so much", from Latin *inter* "between" and *tantum* "so much" (Lewis & Short): the time in between.
