## You'll want to know: la précision
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C341-MALENTENDU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la précision** — "the precision; an extra detail".

> Merci pour ces précisions.
>
> *Thanks for these extra details.*

In the plural, *des précisions* are extra details: *donner des précisions*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*précision* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praecīsiō* "a cutting off" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *praecīdere* "to cut off in front".
