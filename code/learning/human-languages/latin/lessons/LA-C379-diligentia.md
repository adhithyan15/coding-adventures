## You'll want to know: dīligentia, dīligentiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C379-QUAESTUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dīligentia, dīligentiae** — "care, diligence".

> Opus magnā dīligentiā perfēcit.
>
> *He finished the work with great care.*

*dīligentia, dīligentiae* f., the noun of *dīligēns*, careful, hard-working. English *diligence*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dīligentia* is the noun built on *dīligēns* "careful" (Lewis & Short), the participle of a verb made from *dis-* and *legō* "to pick out". English *diligence* comes from it.
