## You'll want to know: सत्वरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C405-JAVA405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्वरम्** — *satvaram* — "in a hurry; hastily".

> यात्री सत्वरं स्थानकं गच्छति।
>
> *yātrī satvaraṃ sthānakaṃ gacchati.*
>
> The passenger goes to the station in a hurry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्वरम्** (*satvaram*) is built from *sa*, "with", and *tvarā*, "haste", from the root √tvar, "to hurry"; MW gives *satvara* as making haste and its neuter as an adverb (MW).
