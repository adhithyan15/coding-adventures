## You'll want to know: تورم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C508-JILD508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تورم** — *tawarrama* — "to swell (up)".

> تورم كاحلي بعد السقوط.
>
> *tawarrama kāḥilī baʿda as-suqūṭ.*
>
> My ankle swelled up after the fall.

Present: يتورم, *yatawarram*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تورم** (*tawarrama*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-r-m*, "to swell" (Lane). Form V tawarrama is to become swollen.
