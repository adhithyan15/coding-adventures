## You'll want to know: der Wetterbericht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C460-WETTERBERICHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Wetterbericht** — "the weather report, the forecast".

> Laut Wetterbericht regnet es morgen den ganzen Tag.
>
> *According to the weather report it will rain all day tomorrow.*

Plural: *die Wetterberichte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wetterbericht* is built from *Wetter* 'weather', an inherited word and a cousin of English *weather* (EtymDB), and *Bericht* 'report', from Middle High German *beriht* (EtymDB).
