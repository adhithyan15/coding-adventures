## Script — 关 in the walking piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SONG-SEND-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 送

**Two pieces: the inside first, then the piece that wraps it.**

**The inside piece, 关, six strokes,** as you wrote it: two short strokes on top, two bars, a long stroke falling to the left, and last the stroke falling to the right, which here shrinks to a dot.

**The walking piece, three strokes,** as in 这: a dot, a small stroke that zigzags down, then a long sweep along the foot to the right.

6 + 3 = nine.
