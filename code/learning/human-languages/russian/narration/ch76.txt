Russian, chapter 76: Forty, Fifty, Ninety, a Thousand, a Country.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сорок (sórok) — forty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for twelve, then the Russian for zero.

You'll want to know: сорок.
сорок (sórok) — "forty". The odd one out: not built from четыре.
