# വീക്ഷണം (vīkṣaṇaṁ) — an outlook; a perspective

## You'll want to know: വീക്ഷണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C406-VADAM406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വീക്ഷണം** — *vīkṣaṇaṁ* — "an outlook; a perspective".

> ഓരോരുത്തർക്കും ഓരോ വീക്ഷണം ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *ōrōruttarkkuṁ ōrō vīkṣaṇaṁ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> Each person has their own outlook.

From the point of view of is -ന്റെ വീക്ഷണത്തിൽ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വീക്ഷണം** (*vīkṣaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vīkṣaṇa*, "looking at, seeing, inspection" (Monier-Williams). An outlook is the way a person looks at things.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
