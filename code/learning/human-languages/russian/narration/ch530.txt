Russian, chapter 530: Washing Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
стирка (stírka) — laundry, washing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cloth, then the Russian for to wipe.

You'll want to know: стирка.
стирка (stírka) — "laundry, washing".
Стирка занимает у меня целый вечер. Stírka zanimáyet u menyá tsélyy vécher. Doing the laundry takes me a whole evening.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
стирка (stírka) is built on стирать 'to wash clothes', first 'to rub off', from the root of тереть 'to rub'; EtymDB has no entry.
