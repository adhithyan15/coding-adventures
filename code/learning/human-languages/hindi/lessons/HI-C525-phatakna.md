## You'll want to know: फटकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C525-PAUDHA525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फटकना** — *phaṭaknā* — "to winnow (grain)".

> किसान आँगन में अनाज फटक रहा है।
>
> *Kisān āṁgan meṁ anāj phaṭak rahā hai.*
>
> The farmer is winnowing grain in the courtyard.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चावल फटके, *usne cāval phaṭke*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फटकना** (*phaṭaknā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*phaṭ*, "sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9038). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to winnow".
