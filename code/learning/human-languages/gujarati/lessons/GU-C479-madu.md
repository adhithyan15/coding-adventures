## You'll want to know: માંદું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C479-MANDU479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**માંદું** — *mā̃dũ* — "unwell; sick".

> મીરા ત્રણ દિવસથી માંદી છે.
>
> *Mīrā traṇ divasthī mā̃dī che.*
>
> Mira has been unwell for three days.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માંદું** (*mā̃dũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*matthara-*, "defective", beside Sanskrit *manthara*, "slow, dull"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "sick" (CDIAL 9754).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
