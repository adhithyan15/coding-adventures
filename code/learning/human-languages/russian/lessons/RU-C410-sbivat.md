## You'll want to know: сбивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C410-STOLKNOVENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**сбивать** (*sbivát*) — "to knock down; to knock off".

> Сильный ветер сбивал людей с ног.
>
> *Sílnyy véter sbivál lyudéy s nog.*
>
> The strong wind was knocking people off their feet.

Imperfective; perfective *сбить* (*собью, собьёшь*). *Машина сбила собаку* — a car hit a dog.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сбивать* is built from *с-* 'down, off' and *-бивать*, from *бить* 'to beat'. *Бить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *biti* 'to beat' (EtymDB).
