German, chapter 301: Im Beruf.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
kündigen — to quit, to give notice; to cancel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to adopt, then the German for to go through.

You'll want to know: kündigen.
kündigen — "to quit, to give notice; to cancel".
Sie kündigte nach zehn Jahren bei der Firma. She quit after ten years at the company.
Jemandem kündigen — to dismiss someone. Also to cancel a Vertrag.

The word, taken apart.
kündigen is built on kund "known", an inherited word with the Gothic cousin kunþs "known" (EtymDB): to make something known.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with kündigen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for to adopt, then the German for to go through, then say kündigen again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does kündigen mean? ("To quit, to give notice; to cancel".) Say it once more.

The word, taken apart.
Wendepunkt joins wenden "to turn", Old High German wenten, Proto-Germanic wandijaną (EtymDB), and Punkt "point", from Latin punctum.
