## Writing

Task 1 must address Rekha and cover the borrowed tool, observed problem,
action already taken, return time, and requested help in 50–70 independently
produced Bengali words. Task 2 must be a coherent 130–170-word account that
explains evidence, differing views, decision, result, and next change.

## Speaking

Ask neutral follow-ups requiring reasons and examples. In collaborative planning
ensure each candidate proposes, questions, adapts, and helps reach the joint
decision. Preparation notes may contain Bengali keywords but no scripted
sentences. Score the complete recording against the shared criteria.
