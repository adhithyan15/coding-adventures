## You'll want to know: abbauen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C425-CAMPING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**abbauen** — "to take down, to dismantle; to reduce".

> Am letzten Tag bauen wir alles wieder ab.
>
> *On the last day we take everything down again.*

Separable: *wir bauen ab*. *Stress abbauen* — to reduce stress.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abbauen* is built from *ab* 'off, down' and *bauen* 'to build', inherited from Proto-Germanic *būaną* (EtymDB).
