## You'll want to know: 下山
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C435-SUMMIT435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**下山** — *xiàshān* — "to come down a mountain".

> **下山以后我们坐车回酒店** *xiàshān yǐhòu wǒmen zuò chē huí jiǔdiàn* — After coming down the mountain, we take the bus back to the hotel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**下山** (*xiàshān*) is built from 下 'to go down' and 山 'a mountain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *srean* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'low' and 'to come down'.
