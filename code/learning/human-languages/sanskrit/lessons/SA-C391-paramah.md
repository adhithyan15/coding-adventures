## You'll want to know: परमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C391-PARAMA391-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परमः** — *paramaḥ* — "supreme; highest".

> यदि मनः शान्तम्, तर्हि परमं सुखं भवति।
>
> *yadi manaḥ śāntam, tarhi paramaṃ sukhaṃ bhavati.*
>
> If the mind is calm, there is the highest happiness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परमः** (*paramaḥ*) is built on *para*, "far, beyond", with the superlative ending *-ma*; MW gives *parama* "chief, highest, primary, best, most excellent" (MW).
