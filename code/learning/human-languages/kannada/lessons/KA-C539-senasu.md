## You'll want to know: ಸೆಣಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C539-SPARDHE539-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೆಣಸು** — *seṇasu* — "to contend; to fight hard".

> ಎರಡು ತಂಡಗಳು ಕೊನೆಯವರೆಗೂ ಸೆಣಸಿದವು.
>
> *eraḍu taṇḍagaḷu koneyavaregū seṇasidavu.*
>
> The two teams fought hard to the very end.

Past (I …): ಸೆಣಸಿದೆ, *seṇaside*.

With whom: -ಜೊತೆ. ಸೆಣಸಾಟ is a hard fight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೆಣಸು** (*seṇasu*) is a native Kannada verb, "to struggle, to contend"; its origin is not settled.
