## You'll want to know: শর্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C368-SHORTO368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**শর্ত** — *shôrtô* — "a condition; a term (of an agreement)".

> আমার একটা শর্ত আছে: তুমি যদি আসো, তাহলে আমিও যাব।
>
> *āmār ekṭā shôrtô āchhe: tumi jôdi āso, tāhôle āmio jābô.*
>
> I have one condition: if you come, I'll go too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শর্ত** (*shôrtô*) is usually traced to Arabic *sharṭ*, a condition, through Persian: the 'if' a deal depends on.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
