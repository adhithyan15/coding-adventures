## You'll want to know: qiānmíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C189-FORM189-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qiānmíng* — "to sign your name".

> *nǐ qiānmíng le ma* — Have you signed it?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiānmíng* is built from *qiān* 'to sign' and *míng* 'a name'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *mjieng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
