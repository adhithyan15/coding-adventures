## You'll want to know: みわたす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C404-FUUKEI404-03]; assesses=[] -->

**みわたす** — *miwatasu* — "to look out over; to survey".

> **やまの うえから まちを みわたしました。** — *yama no ue kara machi o miwatashimashita* — "From the top of the mountain I looked out over the town."

Polite **みわたします**, *miwatashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが まちを みわたしました。**, *ani ga machi o miwatashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みわたす** (*miwatasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *mi*, "seeing" (from *miru*) and *watasu*, "to carry across"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
