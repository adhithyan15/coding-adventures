## You'll want to know: કળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C535-SHUKAN535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કળવું** — *kaḷvũ* — "to make out (to guess)".

> જો હું તેની વાત પહેલાં કળત તો હું ત્યાં ન જાત.
>
> *jo hũ tenī vāt pahelā̃ kaḷat to hũ tyā̃ na jāt.*
>
> If I had made out what he meant earlier, I wouldn't have gone there.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વાત કળી, *teṇe vāt kaḷī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કળવું** (*kaḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaláyati*, "notices, considers", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to understand, guess" (CDIAL 2918).
