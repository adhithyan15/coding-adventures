# அது (adu) — that one — the thing over there

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You can already name things. This is how you POINT at them.

## You'll want to know: அது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**அது** — *adu* — that one — the thing over there.

Say it, and point while you say it. That is the whole word: it does not mean
anything on its own, it means whatever your finger is on.

Its partner is **இது** *idu*, which you will meet just now. The two of them
differ by one sound at the front — **a-** for far — and that is not a
coincidence. It is the whole system, and the last lesson of this chapter says so.
