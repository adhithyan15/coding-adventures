## You'll want to know: عافية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C512-SHIFA512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عافية** — *ʿāfiya* — "good health, wellbeing".

> الحمد لله، جدي في عافية.
>
> *al-ḥamdu lillāh, jaddī fī ʿāfiya.*
>
> Thank God, my grandfather is in good health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عافية** (*ʿāfiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-f-w*, "to efface; to pardon; to give health" (Lane). ʿĀfiya is health given back, freedom from illness.
