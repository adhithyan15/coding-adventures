## You'll want to know: тюрьма
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C293-SUD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**тюрьма** (*tyurmá*) — "a prison".

> Он провёл в тюрьме пять лет.
>
> *On provyól v tyurmé pyat let.*
>
> He spent five years in prison.

Genitive plural *тюрем*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тюрьма* is traced by EtymDB to Proto-Turkic *türmä* "prison"; others derive it through Polish from German *Turm* "a tower", so the origin is not fully settled.
