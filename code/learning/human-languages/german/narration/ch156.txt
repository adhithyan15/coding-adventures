Lesson 1 of 5.
das Rezept — a prescription, a recipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a traffic jam, then the German for an accident.

You'll want to know: das Rezept.
das Rezept — "a prescription, a recipe".

The word, taken apart.
Rezept from Latin receptum, "(what is) received" (EtymDB 2.1), from recipere, "to take" — borrowed. Doctors' prescriptions began recipe, "take!".

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Erkältung — a cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a prescription, then the German for a pain.

You'll want to know: die Erkältung.
die Erkältung — "a cold".

The word, taken apart.
Erkältung is the noun of the verb "to catch a chill", Middle High German erkelten (EtymDB 2.1): er- + the word for "cold", Old High German kalt, English cold, + -ung.

Grippe from French grippe, "flu, a seizing", from Frankish grīpan, "to seize" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed from French, which took it from Germanic. English grip shares the root.
