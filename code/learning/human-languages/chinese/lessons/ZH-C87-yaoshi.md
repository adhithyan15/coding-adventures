## You'll want to know: yàoshi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C87-YAOSHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yàoshi* — "key".

> *wǒ méiyǒu yàoshi* — I don't have a key.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yàoshi* is built from two words inherited from Middle Chinese, *yak* and *dzye* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a bolt' and 'a spoon'. The Guangyun does not give the pair as 'key', so how the two came together is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C87-MEIYOU-01] -->

*wǒ méiyǒu yàoshi*: 没有 in its 'not have' job, with nothing between it and the thing.
