## You'll want to know: o cabide
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C429-QUARTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o cabide** — "the coat hanger; the coat rack".

> No armário só há dois cabides.
>
> *There are only two hangers in the wardrobe.*

In Brazil: *o cabide* too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *cabide* is not settled; it is sometimes traced to Arabic, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
