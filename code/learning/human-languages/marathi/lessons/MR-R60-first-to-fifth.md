## What you've built this chapter, in one number
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-ORDINAL-VA, MR-GRAMMAR-ORDINAL-SET, MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

Of the five ordinals in this chapter, **one** was built and **four** were
memorised. That is the worst ratio you will ever see in this set, and it is
worth saying out loud, because the next chapter reverses it completely: five
more ordinals, and every single one of them built.

The break sits between **चौथा** and **पाचवा**. Everything before it is a word
Marathi received. Everything after it is a word Marathi makes.
