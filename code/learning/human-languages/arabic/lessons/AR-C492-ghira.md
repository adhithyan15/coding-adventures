## You'll want to know: غيرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C492-GHIRA492-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غيرة** — *ghīra* — "jealousy".

> شعر الطفل بالغيرة من أخيه الصغير.
>
> *shaʿara aṭ-ṭifl bi-l-ghīra min akhīhi aṣ-ṣaghīr.*
>
> The child felt jealous of his little brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غيرة** (*ghīra*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-y-r*, "jealousy, a jealous care for one's own" (Lane). Ghīra is jealousy, often a protective one.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
