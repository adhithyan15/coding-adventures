## You'll want to know: ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C386-SAMAJAYISHI386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕ** — *ākasmika* — "accidental; sudden".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಭೇಟಿ ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕವಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *namma bhēṭi ākasmikavāgittu.*
>
> Our meeting was accidental.

ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕವಾಗಿ, by chance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕ** (*ākasmika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ākasmika*, "causeless, unforeseen" (Monier-Williams), from *akasmāt*, "without a why".
