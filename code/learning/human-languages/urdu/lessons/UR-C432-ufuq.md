## You'll want to know: افق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C432-TULU432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**افق** — *ufuq* — "the horizon".

> سمندر کے افق پر سورج غروب ہوا۔
>
> *samandar ke ufuq par sūraj ghurūb huā.*
>
> The sun went down on the sea's horizon.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افق** (*ufuq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ufuq*, "the horizon".
