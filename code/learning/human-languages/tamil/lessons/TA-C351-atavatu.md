# அதாவது (atāvatu) — that is to say; in other words

## You'll want to know: அதாவது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C351-KURIPPITU351-04]; assesses=[] -->

**அதாவது** — *atāvatu* — "that is to say; in other words".

> அவர் நாளை வருவார், அதாவது திங்கள்கிழமை.
>
> *avar nāḷai varuvār, atāvatu tiṅkaḷkiḻamai.*
>
> He will come tomorrow, that is, on Monday.

It introduces a restatement: அதாவது, that is.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அதாவது** (*atāvatu*) is native: *atu*, "that" (DEDR 1), and *āvatu*, "that which is", a verbal noun of *āku*, "to become" (DEDR 333): "that which it is".
