# Hear *hànzì* — Chinese character

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ZI-02] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Say *zì* and its meaning.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-HANZI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *hànzì* — **Chinese character; Han character**

The second half is your known *zì*, written character. The first half, *hàn*,
labels it as Han Chinese. Hear two clean falling tones: *hàn-zì*. The new first
character stays hidden until the next lesson.
