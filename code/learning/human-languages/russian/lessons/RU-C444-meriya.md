## You'll want to know: мэрия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C444-RATUSHA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**мэрия** (*mériya*) — "a city hall, a mayor's office".

> Документы можно получить в мэрии.
>
> *Dokuménty mózhno poluchít v mérii.*
>
> You can get the documents at the city hall.

Feminine. *Мэр* — a mayor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мэрия* was borrowed from French *mairie* 'a mayor's office' (EtymDB), built on *maire* 'a mayor'.
