## You'll want to know: عمارت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C410-IMARAT410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عمارت** — *imārat* — "a building".

> بینک ایک اونچی عمارت میں ہے۔
>
> *baink ek ū̃chī imārat meṅ hai.*
>
> The bank is in a tall building.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عمارت** (*imārat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿimāra*, "a building, a thing built".
