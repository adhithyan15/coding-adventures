## You'll want to know: تخاصم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C535-RIFAQ535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تخاصم** — *takhāṣama* — "to fall out (with each other)".

> تخاصم الأخوان على المال.
>
> *takhāṣama al-akhawān ʿalā al-māl.*
>
> The two brothers fell out over money.

Present: يتخاصم, *yatakhāṣam*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تخاصم** (*takhāṣama*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṣ-m*, "to dispute, to contend" (Lane). Form VI takhāṣama is to dispute with one another.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
