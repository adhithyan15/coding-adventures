## You'll want to know: जन्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C492-JANMA492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जन्म** — *janma* — "birth".

> मम भ्रातुः जन्म रात्रौ अभवत्।
>
> *mama bhrātuḥ janma rātrau abhavat.*
>
> My brother was born at night.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जन्म** (*janma*) comes from the root √jan, "to be born", the root of **जायते**; MW gives "birth, production, origin" (MW).
