## You'll want to know: le but
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C374-BUT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le but** — "the aim, the goal".

> Mon but, c'est de trouver un travail à l'étranger.
>
> *My aim is to find a job abroad.*

In football, *un but* is a goal. The final *t* is often said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*but* comes from Old French *but* "end, aim" (EtymDB), linked to *butte* "a mound used as a target"; it is usually traced to a Frankish word for a stump or a log.
