# рассказать (rasskazát) — to tell a story, once and to the end

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C134-ACT134-01, RU-LEX-ZATEM-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-CHAIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to tell (a story)". (*Rasskázyvat.*) Then three steps of
a morning, with *snachála*, *potóm* and *zatém*.

## You'll want to know: рассказать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-RASSKAZAT-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C35-FAMILY1-03, RU-LEX-C253-VESHCH253-01, RU-LEX-C64-COLOUR2-05, RU-LEX-C85-TIME85-05] -->

**рассказать** — *rasskazát* — "to tell", once, the whole story. It is
the perfective partner of **рассказывать**.

> **Бабушка часто рассказывала сказки.** — *bábushka chásto
> rasskázyvala skázki* — Grandma often told fairy tales.
> **Вчера бабушка рассказала новую сказку.** — *vcherá bábushka
> rasskazála nóvuyu skázku* — Yesterday Grandma told a new fairy tale.

The habit takes the imperfective; the one tale, told to its end, the
perfective. To ask for a story: **Расскажи!** — *rasskazhí!* — Tell me!
To a stranger, or to more than one: **Расскажите!** — *rasskazhítye!*

## Grammar Lens: scene, steps, end
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-STORY-FRAME-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-IMPERFECTIVE-PROCESS-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-PERFECTIVE-RESULT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BYL-PAST-01, RU-LEX-C59-WEATHER-01, RU-LEX-C70-WEEK1-05] -->

A told story has three parts, and aspect marks each one.

The **scene**, in the imperfective: **Это было в субботу. Шёл дождь.** —
*éto býlo v subbótu. shol dozhd* — It was on a Saturday. It was raining.
Russian rain "goes": *идёт дождь*, and in the past *шёл дождь*.

The **steps**, in the perfective and in order, with *сначала*, *потом*,
*затем*. Then the **end**, with **наконец**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C134-ACT134-01] -->

**рассказать** is *рас-*, "out, apart", with **сказать**, "to say". By the
usual account *сказать* is built on an older *казать*, "to show", which
EtymDB 2.1 traces to Proto-Slavic *\*kazati*; so telling is showing in
words. EtymDB lists *рассказать* itself without taking it apart.
