Persian, chapter 220: To allow, To finish, To pass, To print, To photocopy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اجازه دادن (ejâze dâdan) — to allow, to permit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to kiss, then the Persian for to smile.

You'll want to know: اجازه دادن.
اجازه دادن — ejâze dâdan — "to allow, to permit".

The word, taken apart.
ejâze dâdan pairs ejâze, borrowed from Arabic ijāza, "permission" (root j-w-z, Lane), with the Persian verb dâdan, "to give". dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".

The word, taken apart.
chap kardan pairs chap, "print", with kardan. chap is an Indian loan: EtymDB traces it to Hindi chāp, "stamp". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
کپی گرفتن (kopi gereftan) — to photocopy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pass, then the Persian for to print.

You'll want to know: کپی گرفتن.
کپی گرفتن — kopi gereftan — "to photocopy".

The word, taken apart.
kopi gereftan is "to take a copy": kopi is borrowed from French copie, "copy" (EtymDB), and gereftan, "to take", is native. gereftan, "to take", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root gʰrebh₂-, "to seize".
