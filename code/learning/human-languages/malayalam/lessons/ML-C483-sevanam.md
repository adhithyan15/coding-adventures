## You'll want to know: സേവനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C483-SEVANAM483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**സേവനം** — *sēvanaṁ* — "a service".

> ആ ഹോട്ടലിലെ സേവനം വളരെ നല്ലതായിരുന്നു.
>
> *ā hōṭṭalile sēvanaṁ vaḷare nallatāyirunnu.*
>
> The service at that hotel was very good.

A bus service, a bank service and public service are all സേവനം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സേവനം** (*sēvanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sevana*, "serving, attending on", from the root *sev*, to serve (Monier-Williams).
