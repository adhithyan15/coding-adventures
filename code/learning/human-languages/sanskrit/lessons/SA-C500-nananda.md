## You'll want to know: ननान्दा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C500-DEVARA500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ननान्दा** — *nanāndā* — "a husband's sister".

> ननान्दा मया सह आपणं गच्छति।
>
> *nanāndā mayā saha āpaṇaṃ gacchati.*
>
> My husband's sister goes to the shop with me.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ननान्दा** (*nanāndā*) MW records *nanāndṛ* (Rigveda) and *nanandṛ*, "a husband's sister"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
