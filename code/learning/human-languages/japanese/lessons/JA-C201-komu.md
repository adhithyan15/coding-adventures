## You'll want to know: こむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C201-ROAD201-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こむ** — *komu* — "to be crowded".

> **あさの でんしゃは こみます。** — *asa no densha wa komimasu* — "Morning trains get crowded."

Polite **こみます**, *komimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こむ** (*komu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
