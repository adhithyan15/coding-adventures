## You'll want to know: آداب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C384-TARBIYAT384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آداب** — *ādāb* — "manners; respects".

> آداب سیکھیے، ورنہ لوگ برا کہیں گے۔
>
> *ādāb sīkhiye, varnā log burā kaheṅ ge.*
>
> Learn some manners, or people will speak ill of you.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آداب** (*ādāb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ādāb*, the plural of *adab*, "good manners".
