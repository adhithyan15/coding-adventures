## You'll want to know: comporre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C517-ORCHESTRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**comporre** — "to compose".

> Ha composto una canzone per il compleanno di sua madre.
>
> *He composed a song for his mother's birthday.*

Like *porre*: *compongo*, *componi*; past participle *composto*. Also to dial: *comporre un numero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comporre* is inherited from Latin *compōnere* "to put together, to arrange" (EtymDB), from *pōnere* "to put".
