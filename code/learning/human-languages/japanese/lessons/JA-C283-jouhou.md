## You'll want to know: じょうほう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C283-HAPPYOU283-04]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうほう** — *jōhō* — "information; news".

> **じょうほうが すくないです。** — *jōhō ga sukunai desu* — "There is little to go on."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうほう** (*jōhō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "circumstances" (Middle Chinese *dzjeng*) and *hō*, "report" (*pawH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
