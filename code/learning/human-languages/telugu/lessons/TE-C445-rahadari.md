# రహదారి (rahadāri) — a highway; a main road

## You'll want to know: రహదారి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C445-RAHADARI445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**రహదారి** — *rahadāri* — "a highway; a main road".

> జాతీయ రహదారి మీద వాహనాలు వేగంగా వెళ్తాయి.
>
> *jātīya rahadāri mīda vāhanālu vēgaṅgā veḷtāyi.*
>
> Vehicles go fast on the national highway.

జాతీయ రహదారి is a national highway. దారి alone is any way or path.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రహదారి** (*rahadāri*) is a compound whose second part is దారి, "a way, a road"; the first part is usually traced to Persian *rāh*, "a road", and the whole is read as "the main road".
