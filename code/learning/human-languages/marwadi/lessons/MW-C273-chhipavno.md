# छिपावणो (chhipāvṇo) — to hide (something); to keep secret

## You'll want to know: छिपावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C273-CHUP273-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छिपावणो** — *chhipāvṇo* — "to hide (something); to keep secret".

> वो भेद छिपावै है।
>
> *vo bhed chhipāvai hai.*
>
> He keeps the secret.

Past (I …): म्हैं भेद छिपायो, *mhaĩ bhed chhipāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छिपावणो** (*chhipāvṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*chipp*, "cover, hide"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *chipāvaï*, "hides" (CDIAL 4994).
