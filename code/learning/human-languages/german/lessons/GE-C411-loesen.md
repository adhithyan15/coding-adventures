## You'll want to know: lösen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C411-FAHRSCHEIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**lösen** — "to get a ticket; to solve; to loosen".

> Am Bahnhof löst er eine Fahrkarte nach Hause.
>
> *At the station he gets a ticket home.*

*Eine Fahrkarte lösen* sounds a little formal. *Ein Problem lösen* — to solve a problem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lösen* is inherited through Old High German *lōsen* from Proto-Germanic *lausijaną*, built on *lausaz* 'loose, free' (EtymDB). English *loose* is a cousin.
