Punjabi, chapter 131: Duck, Lizard, Turtle, Crocodile, Mosquito.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਬੱਤਖ਼ (battakh) — a duck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a wolf, then the Punjabi for a pigeon.

You'll want to know: ਬੱਤਖ਼.
ਬੱਤਖ਼ — battakh — "a duck".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: battakh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: battakh, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say battakh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a wolf, then the Punjabi for a pigeon, then say battakh again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਬੱਤਖ਼ (battakh) mean? ("A duck".) Say it once more.
