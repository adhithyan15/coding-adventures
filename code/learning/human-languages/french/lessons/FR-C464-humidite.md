## You'll want to know: l'humidité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C464-CANICULE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'humidité** — "the humidity, the damp".

> Avec l'humidité, mes cheveux frisent.
>
> *With the humidity my hair goes frizzy.*

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*humidité* is a learned borrowing from Latin *hūmiditās* (EtymDB), from *hūmidus* 'damp'.
