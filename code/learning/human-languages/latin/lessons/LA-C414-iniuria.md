## You'll want to know: iniūria, iniūriae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C414-INIURIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**iniūria, iniūriae** — "a wrong, an injustice, an insult".

> Iniūriam accēpit, nōn fēcit.
>
> *He suffered a wrong; he did not do one.*

*iniūria, iniūriae* f., *in-* not + *iūs*: what is against right. *Iniūriā*, wrongly. English *injury*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*iniūria* is built from *in-* "not" and *iūs, iūris* "right, law" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): what goes against right. English *injury* comes from it.
