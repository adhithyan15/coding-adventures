## You'll want to know: rènkě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C352-AGREE352-03]; assesses=[] -->

*rènkě* — "to give your approval; to accept as good".

> *dàjiā dōu rènkě tā de xiǎngfǎ* — Everyone approved of his idea.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rènkě* is built from *rèn* and *kě*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyinH* and *khaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to recognize' and the second 'to permit'.
