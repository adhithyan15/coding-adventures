# ಬದುಕು (baduku) — to live; to survive

## You'll want to know: ಬದುಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C529-JIVANA529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬದುಕು** — *baduku* — "to live; to survive".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ತೊಂಬತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ಬದುಕಿದರು.
>
> *ajja tombattu varṣa badukidaru.*
>
> Grandfather lived to ninety.

Past (I …): ಬದುಕಿದೆ, *badukide*.

Also a noun, life: ಬದುಕು ಕಷ್ಟ, life is hard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬದುಕು** (*baduku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5372 lists Kannada *barduku* and *baduku*, "to live, be alive, make a livelihood".
