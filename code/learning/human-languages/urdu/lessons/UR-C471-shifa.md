## You'll want to know: شفا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C471-HEALTH471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شفا** — *shifā* — "healing; a cure".

> اس دوا سے مریض کو شفا ملی۔
>
> *is davā se marīz ko shifā milī.*
>
> This medicine cured the patient.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شفا** (*shifā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shifāʾ*, "healing".
