## You'll want to know: le portique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C417-DOUANE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le portique** — "the scanner gate (at security); the portico".

> Au contrôle, le portique a sonné à cause de sa ceinture.
>
> *At security the gate beeped because of his belt.*

*Un portique de sécurité* — a walk-through scanner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*portique* is a learned borrowing from Latin *porticus* 'colonnade', built on *porta* 'gate' (EtymDB).
