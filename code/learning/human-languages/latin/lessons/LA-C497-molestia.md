## You'll want to know: molestia, molestiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C497-SOLL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**molestia, molestiae** — "annoyance, trouble".

> Muscae aegrō molestiam afferunt.
>
> *The flies cause the sick man annoyance.*

Feminine, first declension, from *molestus* — annoying. English *molest* comes from the related verb *molestō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**molestia** is built on *molestus* "troublesome", which Lewis & Short connects with *mōlēs* "a heavy mass": a burden. English *molest* comes from the related verb *molestō*.
