## You'll want to know: बैंक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C195-BANK195-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बैंक** — *baiṅk* — "a bank".

> बैंक बाजार कनै है।
>
> *baiṅk bājār kanai hai.*
>
> The bank is near the market.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बैंक** (*baiṅk*) is borrowed from English *bank*.
