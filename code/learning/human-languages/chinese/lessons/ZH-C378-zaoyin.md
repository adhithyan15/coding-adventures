## You'll want to know: zàoyīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C378-NOISE378-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zàoyīn* — "unwanted noise; din".

> *wǎnshang wàibian de zàoyīn tài dà le* — The noise outside is far too loud at night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zàoyīn* is built from *zào* 'clamour' and 音 'a sound', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sawH* and *'im* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a crowd shouting'.
