## You'll want to know: yīguì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C179-WASH179-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yīguì* — "wardrobe".

> *nǐ de dàyī zài yīguì lǐ* — Your coat is in the wardrobe.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yīguì* is built from 衣 'clothes' and *guì* 'a chest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'j+j* and *gwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
