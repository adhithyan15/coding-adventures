## You'll want to know: l'ébullition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C330-MIJOTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ébullition** — "boiling, the boiling point".

> Il met les pâtes quand l'eau arrive à ébullition.
>
> *He puts the pasta in when the water comes to the boil.*

*Porter à ébullition* — to bring to the boil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ébullition* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *ēbullītiō* "a bubbling up" (EtymDB), built on *bulla* "a bubble".
