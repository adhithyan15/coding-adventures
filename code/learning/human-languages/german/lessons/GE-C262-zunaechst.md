## You'll want to know: zunächst
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C262-ZUNAECHST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**zunächst** — "at first, initially; for now".

> Zunächst war alles ruhig.
>
> *At first everything was quiet.*

A little more formal than *zuerst*. It can also mean 'for now': *zunächst bleibe ich zu Hause*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zunächst* is built on *zu* "to" and *nächst* "nearest", the superlative of *nah* "near", which is inherited from Old High German *nāh* "close, near" (EtymDB): the nearest step comes first.
