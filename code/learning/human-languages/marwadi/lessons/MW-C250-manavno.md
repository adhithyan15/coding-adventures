## You'll want to know: मनावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C250-UCHHAV250-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मनावणो** — *manāvṇo* — "to celebrate (a festival); to coax".

> म्हैं दिवाळी घरै मनाई।
>
> *mhaĩ divāḷī gharai manāī.*
>
> I celebrated Diwali at home.

Past (I …): म्हैं होळी मनाई, *mhaĩ hoḷī manāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मनावणो** (*manāvṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *manyate*, "thinks, honours"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *manāvaï*, "persuades", under it. "To celebrate" a feast is probably "to honour" it (CDIAL 9857).
