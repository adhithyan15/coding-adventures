Portuguese, chapter 155: Punctual, Honest, Patient, Generous, Lazy.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
pontual — punctual.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for useless, then the Portuguese for strange.

You'll want to know: pontual.
pontual — "punctual".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: pontual]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pontual, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say pontual]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for useless, then the Portuguese for strange, then say pontual again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does pontual mean? ("Punctual".) Say it once more.
