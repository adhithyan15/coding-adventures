## You'll want to know: موجة حر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C484-HARR484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**موجة حر** — *mawjat ḥarr* — "a heatwave".

> وصلت موجة حر إلى المدينة.
>
> *waṣalat mawjat ḥarr ilā al-madīna.*
>
> A heatwave reached the city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**موجة حر** (*mawjat ḥarr*) is built from two native Arabic words: *mawja*, "a wave", on the root *m-w-j* (Lane), and *ḥarr*, "heat", on *ḥ-r-r* (Lane).
