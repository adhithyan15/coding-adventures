## You'll want to know: うく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C275-TSUNAMI275-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うく** — *uku* — "to float".

> **みずの うえに きが ういていました。** — *mizu no ue ni ki ga uite imashita* — "Wood was floating on the water."

Polite **うきます**, *ukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **きが うきました。**, *ki ga ukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うく** (*uku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
