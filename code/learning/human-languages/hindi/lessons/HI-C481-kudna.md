## You'll want to know: कूदना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C481-LETNA481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कूदना** — *kūdnā* — "to jump".

> बच्चे ख़ुशी से पानी में कूद पड़े।
>
> *bacce khuśī se pānī meṁ kūd paṛe.*
>
> The children jumped into the water with joy.

Past (he …): वह पानी में कूदा, *vah pānī meṁ kūdā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूदना** (*kūdnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *kūdnā*, "to jump", under Sanskrit *kūrdati*, "leaps" (CDIAL 3412).
