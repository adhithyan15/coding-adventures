## You'll want to know: la sierra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C685-PAISAJE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la sierra** — "the mountain range; the saw".

> Pasamos el fin de semana en la sierra.
>
> *We spent the weekend in the mountains.*

A row of peaks looks like the teeth of a saw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sierra* is inherited from Latin *serra* "a saw" (EtymDB); a jagged line of peaks looks like its teeth.
