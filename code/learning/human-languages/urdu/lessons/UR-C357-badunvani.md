## You'll want to know: بدعنوانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C357-BADUNVANI357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بدعنوانی** — *badunvānī* — "corruption".

> حکومت بدعنوانی کے خلاف ہے۔
>
> *hukūmat badunvānī ke khilāf hai.*
>
> The government is against corruption.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بدعنوانی** (*badunvānī*) is built from Persian *bad*, "bad", and *unvān*, "a heading; a manner", which is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿunwān*, with the noun ending *-ī*. Urdu uses it for corruption, bad conduct in office, a sense our sources do not trace.
