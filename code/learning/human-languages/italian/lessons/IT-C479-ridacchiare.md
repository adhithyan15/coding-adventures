## You'll want to know: ridacchiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C479-ALLEGRIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ridacchiare** — "to giggle, to snigger".

> Le ragazze ridacchiavano in fondo alla classe.
>
> *The girls were giggling at the back of the class.*

Built on *ridere* with *-acchiare*, an ending that makes the action small and repeated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ridacchiare* is built on *ridere* "to laugh", inherited from Latin *rīdēre* (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-acchiare* for a small, repeated action.
