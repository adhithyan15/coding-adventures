## You'll want to know: отваривать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C329-KIPYATIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**отваривать** (*otvárivat*) — "to cook in water, to boil (food)".

> Я отварил картошку.
>
> *Ya otvaríl kartóshku.*
>
> I boiled the potatoes.

Imperfective; perfective *отварить* (*otvarít*). *Варить* — to cook, to boil in general.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отваривать* is built on *от-* and *варить* "to boil", inherited from Proto-Slavic *variti*, which EtymDB traces to PIE *wer-* "to burn".
