## You'll want to know: চরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C437-BAGH437-04]; assesses=[] -->

**চরা** — *chôrā* — "to graze".

> মাঠে গরু চরছিল।
>
> *māṭhe gôru chôrchhilô.*
>
> Cows were grazing in the field.

Past (he/she …): সেই গরু মাঠে চরল, *sei gôru māṭhe chôrlô*.

চরানো, to take cattle out to graze, is the causing partner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চরা** (*chôrā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *carā*, "to move about, to graze", under Sanskrit *cárati*, "moves, grazes" (CDIAL 4686).
