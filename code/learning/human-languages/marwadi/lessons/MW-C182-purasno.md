## You'll want to know: पुरसणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C182-JHATKO182-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरसणो** — *purasṇo* — "to serve food".

> मां थाळी मांय रोटी पुरसै है।
>
> *mā̃ thāḷī mā̃y roṭī pursai hai.*
>
> Mother serves rotis onto the plate.

Past (I …): म्हैं खीचड़ो पुरस्यो, *mhaĩ khīchṛo purasyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरसणो** (*purasṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pariveṣati*, "serves food"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *purasaï*, "serves a meal" (CDIAL 7888).
