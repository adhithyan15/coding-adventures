## You'll want to know: लोभः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C336-NITI336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभः** — *lobhaḥ* — "greed".

> लोभः दुःखस्य कारणम् अस्ति।
>
> *lobhaḥ duḥkhasya kāraṇam asti.*
>
> Greed is a cause of sorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभः** (*lobhaḥ*) comes from the root √lubh, "to be perplexed, desire greatly"; MW gives *lobha* as confusion, eager desire, and covetousness, greed (MW).
