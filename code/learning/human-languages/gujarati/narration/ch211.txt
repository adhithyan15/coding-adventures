Gujarati, chapter 211: Boats, pilgrims and rides.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હોડી (hoḍī) — boat, rowing boat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to load, then the Gujarati for to lift.

You'll want to know: હોડી.
હોડી — hoḍī — "boat, rowing boat".
અમે હોડીમાં નદી ઓળંગી. — ame hoḍīmā̃ nadī oḷangī — "We crossed the river in a boat."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હોડી (hoḍī) is listed by CDIAL, glossed "boat", under the lexical Sanskrit hōḍa, "raft, boat", which it takes from Dravidian, as in Kannada ōḍa, "boat" (CDIAL 14174). DEDR also judges the Indo-Aryan words probably Dravidian (DEDR 1039).

Lesson 2 of 5.
સવારી કરવી (savārī karvī) — to ride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to lift, then the Gujarati for boat.

You'll want to know: સવારી કરવી.
સવારી કરવી — savārī karvī — "to ride".
બાળકોએ હાથીની સવારી કરી. — bāḷkoe hāthīnī savārī karī — "The children rode an elephant."

The word, taken apart.
સવારી કરવી (savārī karvī) is built from savārī, "a ride", a loan usually traced to Persian savārī, from savār, "rider", and the light verb karvũ, "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
હંકારવું (hankārvũ) — to steer, to drive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for boat, then the Gujarati for to ride.

You'll want to know: હંકારવું.
હંકારવું — hankārvũ — "to steer, to drive".
તેણે હોડી હંકારી. — teṇe hoḍī hankārī — "He steered the boat."

The word, taken apart.
હંકારવું (hankārvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists hakārvũ, "to drive", under hakkārayati, "calls", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 13941).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to ride, then the Gujarati for to steer.

You'll want to know: યાત્રા.
યાત્રા — yātrā — "pilgrimage, journey".
દાદી ગયા વરસે યાત્રાએ ગઈ. — dādī gayā varse yātrāe gaī — "Grandmother went on a pilgrimage last year."

The word, taken apart.
યાત્રા (yātrā) is Sanskrit yātrā, "going, journey, procession", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
વાહન (vāhan) — vehicle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to steer, then the Gujarati for pilgrimage.

You'll want to know: વાહન.
વાહન — vāhan — "vehicle".
આ વાહન જૂનું હતું. — ā vāhan jūnũ hatũ — "This vehicle was old."
Its inherited cousin is વહાણ, vahāṇ, 'ship'.

The word, taken apart.
વાહન (vāhan) is Sanskrit vā́hana, "any vehicle or draught animal", taken over whole, a tatsama. CDIAL lists its inherited form vahāṇ, "ship" (CDIAL 11610).
