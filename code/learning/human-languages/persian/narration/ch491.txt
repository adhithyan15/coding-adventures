Persian, chapter 491: Aches and Pains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کمردرد (kamardard) — backache, lower back pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sore throat, then the Persian for to run a fever.

You'll want to know: کمردرد.
کمردرد (kamardard) — "backache, lower back pain".
پدرم بعد از کار در باغ کمردرد گرفت. pedaram ba'd az kâr dar bâgh kamardard gereft. My father got backache after working in the garden.
kamar 'lower back, waist' + dard 'pain'. kamardard gereftan — to get backache.

The word, taken apart.
kamardard is built on two inherited words: kamar, 'waist, lower back', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and dard, 'pain'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
دندان درد (dandân dard) — toothache.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to run a fever, then the Persian for backache.

You'll want to know: دندان درد.
دندان درد (dandân dard) — "toothache".
با دندان درد به دندانپزشک رفتم. bâ dandân dard be dandânpezeshk raftam. I went to the dentist with toothache.
dandân 'tooth' + dard 'pain'. In speech also dandun dard.

The word, taken apart.
dandân dard is built on two inherited words. dandân, 'tooth', comes from Middle Persian, and EtymDB lists Latin dens and Greek odous as its cousins (EtymDB); dard is 'pain'.

You'll want to know: درد کشیدن.
درد کشیدن (dard keshidan) — "to suffer pain".
او سالها درد کشید. u sâlhâ dard keshid. She suffered pain for years.
dard 'pain' + keshidan 'to pull, to bear'; present stem kesh-.

The word, taken apart.
dard keshidan is built on two inherited Persian words, dard, 'pain', and keshidan, 'to pull, to bear': to bear pain.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خارش (khâresh) — an itch, itching.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for toothache, then the Persian for to suffer pain.

You'll want to know: خارش.
خارش (khâresh) — "an itch, itching".
دستم خارش دارد. dastam khâresh dârad. My hand itches.
The noun from khâridan 'to itch' (stem khâr-) with -esh; khârândan is 'to scratch'. khâresh dâshtan — to itch.

The word, taken apart.
khâresh is built on khâr-, the present stem of the native verb khâridan, 'to itch', with the noun ending -esh. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سکسکه کردن (sekseke kardan) — to hiccup.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to suffer pain, then the Persian for an itch.

You'll want to know: سکسکه کردن.
سکسکه کردن (sekseke kardan) — "to hiccup".
بچه بعد از غذا سکسکه میکند. bachche ba'd az ghazâ sekseke mikonad. The baby hiccups after eating.
sekseke 'hiccup' + kardan; present stem kon-.

The word, taken apart.
sekseke is usually explained as imitative, built on the sound of a hiccup itself, and kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian. EtymDB has no entry for it.
