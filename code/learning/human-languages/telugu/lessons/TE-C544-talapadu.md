## You'll want to know: తలపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C544-KRIDA544-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తలపడు** — *talapaḍu* — "to clash; to take on".

> రేపు రెండు జట్లు ఫైనల్లో తలపడతాయి.
>
> *rēpu reṇḍu jaṭlu phainallō talapaḍatāyi.*
>
> Tomorrow the two teams clash in the final.

Past (I …): తలపడ్డాను, *talapaḍḍānu*.

-తో తలపడు, to take someone on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తలపడు** (*talapaḍu*) is a native compound: *tala*, "head" (DEDR 3103), and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853): to meet head to head.
