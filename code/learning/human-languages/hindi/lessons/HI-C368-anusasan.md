## You'll want to know: अनुशासन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C368-VIKAS368-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुशासन** — *anuśāsan* — "discipline".

> खिलाड़ियों में अनुशासन बहुत ज़रूरी है।
>
> *khilāṛiyoṁ meṁ anuśāsan bahut zarūrī hai.*
>
> Discipline is very important in players.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुशासन** (*anuśāsan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *anuśāsana*, "instruction, a precept" (Monier-Williams).
