## You'll want to know: کاؤنٹر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C391-QATAR391-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کاؤنٹر** — *kāunṭar* — "a counter; a service desk".

> قطار کاؤنٹر سے دروازے تک تھی۔
>
> *qatār kāunṭar se darvāze tak thī.*
>
> The queue stretched from the counter to the door.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کاؤنٹر** (*kāunṭar*) is borrowed from English *counter*.
