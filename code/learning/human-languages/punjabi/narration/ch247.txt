Punjabi, chapter 247: Inside the Room.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਆਲਾ (ālā) — a wall niche.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a roof gutter, then the Punjabi for a high shelf.

You'll want to know: ਆਲਾ.
ਆਲਾ — ālā — "a wall niche".
ਆਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੀਵਾ ਹੈ। āle vich dīvā hai. There is a lamp in the wall niche.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਆਲਾ (ālā) is inherited from Sanskrit ālaya, "house, receptacle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ālā, "niche in a wall" (CDIAL 1366).
