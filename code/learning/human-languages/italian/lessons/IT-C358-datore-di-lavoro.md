## You'll want to know: il datore di lavoro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C358-DIPENDENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il datore di lavoro** — "the employer".

> Il mio datore di lavoro mi paga il corso di inglese.
>
> *My employer pays for my English lessons.*

Literally the giver of work. In speech people often say *il capo* or *l'azienda*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*datore* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dator* "a giver" (Lewis & Short), from *dare* "to give"; *lavoro* is built on *lavorare*, inherited from Latin *labōrāre* "to toil".
