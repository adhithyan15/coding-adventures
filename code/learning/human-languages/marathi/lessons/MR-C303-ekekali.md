## You'll want to know: एकेकाळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C303-KATHA303-01]; assesses=[] -->

**एकेकाळी** — *ekekāḷī* — "once upon a time; at one time".

> एकेकाळी या डोंगरावर एक मोठा किल्ला होता.
>
> *ekekāḷī yā ḍoṅgrāvar ek moṭhā killā hotā.*
>
> Once upon a time there was a big fort on this hill.

Built from एक, one, and काळ, time, with -ई: at one time. Stories open with it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एकेकाळी** (*ekekāḷī*) is built from *ek*, 'one', and *kāḷ*, 'time', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *kāla*, 'time' (CDIAL 3084), in the locative: at one time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
