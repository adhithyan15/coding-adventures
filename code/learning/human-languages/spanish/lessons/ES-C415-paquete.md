# el paquete — a bundle packed for travel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-IMPRESO] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **el impreso**, the form you fill in. At a post office, the
form may travel with **el paquete**, the package or parcel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-PAQUETE]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish borrowed **paquete** through French **paquet**, a small bundle. English
**packet** travelled from the same word, and **pack** is a close family clue.
Picture several things packed together into one parcel.

## Grammar Lens: what happens to the package?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-PAQUETE] -->

The noun is masculine: **el paquete**, **un paquete**. Use **enviar** for the
action: **enviar el paquete**, to send the package.
