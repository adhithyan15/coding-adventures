## You'll want to know: مال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C424-MAAL424-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مال** — *māl* — "goods (for sale); stock".

> دکان میں نیا مال آیا۔
>
> *dukān meṅ nayā māl āyā.*
>
> New stock has come into the shop.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مال** (*māl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *māl*, "property, wealth"; in a shop it is the goods for sale.
