Telugu, chapter 126: Duck, Pig, Donkey, Camel, Face.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
బాతు (bātu) — a duck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a butterfly, then the Telugu for a crow.

You'll want to know: బాతు.
బాతు — bātu — "a duck".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bātu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bātu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bātu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for a butterfly, then the Telugu for a crow, then say bātu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does బాతు (bātu) mean? ("A duck".) Say it once more.
