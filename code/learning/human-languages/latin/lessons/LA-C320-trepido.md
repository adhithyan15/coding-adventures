## You'll want to know: trepidō, trepidāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C320-PAVOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**trepidō, trepidāre** — "to panic, to be in a flurry".

> Cīvēs in viīs trepidābant, nec quid facerent sciēbant.
>
> *The citizens were rushing about in alarm in the streets and did not know what to do.*

*trepidō, trepidāre, trepidāvī, trepidātum*: anxious hurrying, the bustle of fear. English *trepidation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**trepidō** is built on *trepidus*, "restless, alarmed" (EtymDB). English *trepidation* comes from it.
