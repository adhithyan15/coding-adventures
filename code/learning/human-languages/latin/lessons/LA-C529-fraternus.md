## You'll want to know: frāternus, frāterna, frāternum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C529-NOVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**frāternus, frāterna, frāternum** — "brotherly".

> Amor frāternus semper manet.
>
> *Brotherly love always endures.*

From *frāter*. English *fraternal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**frāternus** is built on *frāter* "brother" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European word for "brother"; English *brother* is a cousin. English *fraternal* comes from it.
