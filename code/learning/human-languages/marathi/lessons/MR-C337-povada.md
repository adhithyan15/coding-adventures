## You'll want to know: पोवाडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C337-DANTKATHA337-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पोवाडा** — *povāḍā* — "a heroic ballad".

> जत्रेत एक गायक शूर सरदाराचा पोवाडा गात होता.
>
> *jatret ek gāyak śūr sardārācā povāḍā gāt hotā.*
>
> At the fair a singer was singing a ballad of a brave chieftain.

Masculine.

A Marathi ballad of heroic deeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोवाडा** (*povāḍā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *povāḍā*, 'a historical ballad', under Sanskrit *pravāda*, 'a speaking forth; talk, report' (CDIAL 8795).
