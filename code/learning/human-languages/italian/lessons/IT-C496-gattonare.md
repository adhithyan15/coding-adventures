## You'll want to know: gattonare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C496-NEONATO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**gattonare** — "to crawl (on all fours)".

> A otto mesi mio figlio gattonava per tutta la casa.
>
> *At eight months my son was crawling all over the house.*

Built on *gatto*: moving like a cat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gattonare* is built on *gatto* "cat", inherited from Late Latin *cattus* (EtymDB): to move on all fours like a cat.
