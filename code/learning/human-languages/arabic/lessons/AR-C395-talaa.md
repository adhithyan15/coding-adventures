## You'll want to know: طالع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C395-MARJI395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**طالع** — *ṭālaʿa* — "to read up on".

> يطالع أبي الجريدة كل صباح.
>
> *yuṭāliʿu abī al-jarīda kulla ṣabāḥ.*
>
> My father reads through the newspaper every morning.

Present: يطالع, *yuṭāliʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طالع** (*ṭālaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-l-ʿ*, "to rise (of the sun)" (Lane). It is Form III, to look into a thing closely: to read up on, to peruse.
