## You'll want to know: shuāngbāotāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C302-BIRTH302-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shuāngbāotāi* — "twins".

> *tāmen shì shuāngbāotāi* — They're twins.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuāngbāotāi* is built from *shuāng* 'a pair', *bāo* 'the womb' and *tāi* 'an unborn child', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sraewng*, *paew* and *thoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'two of a kind' and the third, quoting an older dictionary, 'three months with child', and gives the second only in the pair *bāotāi*, so its sense 'the womb' is not spelled out there: two from one womb.
