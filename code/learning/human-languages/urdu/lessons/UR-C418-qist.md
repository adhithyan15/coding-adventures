## You'll want to know: قسط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C418-HISAB418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قسط** — *qist* — "an instalment".

> میں ہر مہینے موٹر سائیکل کی قسط دیتی ہوں۔
>
> *maiṅ har mahīne moṭar sāikil kī qist detī hūṅ.*
>
> Every month I pay an instalment on the motorbike.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قسط** (*qist*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qisṭ*, "a portion, a share".
