## You'll want to know: せんしゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C140-SENSHUU]; assesses=[] -->

**せんしゅう** — *senshū* — "last week". It sits beside **こんしゅう**,
*konshū*, "this week", which you already know.

To say what happened, the polite verb changes **ます** to **ました**,
*mashita*:

> **せんしゅう はたらきました。** — *senshū hatarakimashita* — "I worked
> last week."

**はたらきます** → **はたらきました**, **ねます** → **ねました**. Every
polite verb makes its past this way.
