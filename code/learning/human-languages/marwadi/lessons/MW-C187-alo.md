## You'll want to know: आळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C187-KOTHO187-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आळो** — *āḷo* — "a wall niche".

> भींत मांय एक आळो है।
>
> *bhī̃t mā̃y ek āḷo hai.*
>
> There's a niche in the wall.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आळो** (*āḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ālaya*, "a house, a receptacle"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ālā*, "a niche in a wall" (CDIAL 1366). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
