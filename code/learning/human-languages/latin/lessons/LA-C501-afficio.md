## You'll want to know: afficiō, afficere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C501-AFFE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**afficiō, afficere** — "to affect, fill with a feeling".

> Nūntius malus omnēs dolōre afficit.
>
> *The bad news fills everyone with grief.*

*afficiō, afficere, affēcī, affectum*. *Aliquem laetitiā afficere* — to fill someone with joy; in the passive, *dolōre afficior* — I am stricken with pain. English *affect*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**afficiō** is built on *ad-* and *faciō* "to do, make" (Lewis & Short): to do something to someone. English *affect* comes from it.
