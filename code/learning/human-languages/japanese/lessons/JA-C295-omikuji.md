## You'll want to know: おみくじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C295-HATSUMOUDE295-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おみくじ** — *omikuji* — "a shrine fortune slip; a paper fortune".

> **じんじゃで おみくじを ひきました。** — *jinja de omikuji o hikimashita* — "I drew a fortune at the shrine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おみくじ** (*omikuji*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *o-*, "the polite prefix", *mi-*, "the honorific prefix", and *kuji*, "a lot drawn" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
