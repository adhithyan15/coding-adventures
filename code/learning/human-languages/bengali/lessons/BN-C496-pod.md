## You'll want to know: পদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C496-PODONNOTI496-01]; assesses=[] -->

**পদ** — *pôd* — "a post; a position".

> তিনি অফিসে একটা বড় পদে আছেন।
>
> *Tini ôphise ekṭā bôṛo pôde āchhen.*
>
> She holds a senior post at the office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পদ** (*pôd*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pada*, "a step", and from that "a place, a rank" (Monier-Williams).
