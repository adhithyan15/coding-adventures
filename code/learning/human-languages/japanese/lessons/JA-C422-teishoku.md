## You'll want to know: ていしょく

**ていしょく** — *teishoku* — "a set meal".

> **しょくどうで さかなの ていしょくを たのみました。** — *shokudō de sakana no teishoku o tanomimashita* — "I ordered the fish set meal at the canteen."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ていしょく** (*teishoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tei*, "fix" (Middle Chinese *dengH*) and *shoku*, "food" (*zyik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
