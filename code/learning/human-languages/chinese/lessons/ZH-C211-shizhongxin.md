## You'll want to know: shìzhōngxīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C211-LOCAL211-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shìzhōngxīn* — "city centre".

> *wǒ jiā zài shìzhōngxīn* — My home is in the city centre.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shìzhōngxīn* is built from *shì* 'a market, a city', 中 'middle' and 心 'heart', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyiX*, *trjuwng* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the place where buying and selling is done.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
