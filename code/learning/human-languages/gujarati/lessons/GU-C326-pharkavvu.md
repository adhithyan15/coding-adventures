# ફરકાવવું (pharkāvvũ) — to hoist (a flag)

## You'll want to know: ફરકાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C326-ZANDO326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફરકાવવું** — *pharkāvvũ* — "to hoist (a flag)".

> આઝાદીના દિવસે ગુરુએ શાળામાં ઝંડો ફરકાવ્યો.
>
> *ājhādīnā divse gurue śāḷāmā̃ jhanḍo pharkāvyo.*
>
> On the day of independence the teacher raised the flag at school.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઝંડો ફરકાવ્યો, *teṇe jhanḍo pharkāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફરકાવવું** (*pharkāvvũ*) is the causative of *pharakvũ*, to flutter, which CDIAL lists under *\*pharati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9050): to make a flag fly. **ઝંડો ફરકાવ્યો**, raised the flag.
