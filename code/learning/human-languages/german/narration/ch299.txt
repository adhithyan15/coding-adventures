German, chapter 299: Die Ehe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Ehe — the marriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the bridegroom, then the German for the bride and groom.

You'll want to know: die Ehe.
die Ehe — "the marriage".
Ihre Ehe hielt fünfzig Jahre. Their marriage lasted fifty years.
Plural: die Ehen. The adjective is verheiratet.

The word, taken apart.
Ehe goes back to Middle High German ē (EtymDB), usually traced to Old High German ēwa "law, eternity": marriage as a lasting bond.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Trauung — the wedding ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the husband, then the German for the wife.

You'll want to know: die Trauung.
die Trauung — "the wedding ceremony".
Die Trauung war um elf Uhr. The wedding ceremony was at eleven.
Plural: die Trauungen. In a church or at the town hall.

The word, taken apart.
Trauung is built on trauen "to marry, to trust", from Old High German trūwēn (EtymDB): the couple trust themselves to each other.

Standesamt joins Stand "standing, status" and Amt "office" with the linking -es- (EtymDB): the office for civil status.
