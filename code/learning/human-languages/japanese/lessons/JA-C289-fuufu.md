## You'll want to know: ふうふ

**ふうふ** — *fūfu* — "a married couple".

> **となりの ふうふは わかいです。** — *tonari no fūfu wa wakai desu* — "The couple next door are young."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふうふ** (*fūfu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fū*, "husband" (Middle Chinese *pju*) and *fu*, "wife" (*bjuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
