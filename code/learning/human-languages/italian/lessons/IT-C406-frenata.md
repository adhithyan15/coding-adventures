## You'll want to know: la frenata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C406-GUIDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la frenata** — "the braking; a sudden stop".

> Ho fatto una frenata e il gatto è scappato.
>
> *I braked hard and the cat ran off.*

*Frenare* is to brake; *una frenata brusca* is slamming on the brakes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frenata* is built on *frenare* 'to brake', from *freno* 'brake', which is inherited from Latin *frēnum* 'bridle' (EtymDB).
