Portuguese, chapter 99: Cup, Bottle, Letterbox, Pasta, Pear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a chávena — a cup.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a lamp, then the Portuguese for a bathroom.

You'll want to know: a chávena.
a chávena — "a cup". In Brazil, a xícara.
