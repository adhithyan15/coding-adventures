## You'll want to know: onerārius, onerāria, onerārium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C436-PUPPIS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**onerārius, onerāria, onerārium** — "for cargo, for carrying loads".

> Nāvēs onerāriae frūmentum ad urbem vehunt.
>
> *Cargo ships carry grain to the city.*

An adjective of three endings. *Nāvis onerāria*, a merchant ship, or *onerāria* on its own; *iūmentum onerārium*, a pack animal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*onerārius* is built on *onus, oneris* 'a load' with the suffix *-ārius* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *onerary* 'fit for loads' is a rare borrowing of it.
