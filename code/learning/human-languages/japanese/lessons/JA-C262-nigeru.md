## You'll want to know: にげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C262-JIKO262-03]; assesses=[] -->

**にげる** — *nigeru* — "to run away; to flee".

> **いぬが きて、ねこが にげました。** — *inu ga kite, neko ga nigemashita* — "A dog came along, and the cat fled."

Polite **にげます**, *nigemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ねこが にげました。**, *neko ga nigemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にげる** (*nigeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
