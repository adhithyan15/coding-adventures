## You'll want to know: पुराणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C324-PURA324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पुराणम्** — *purāṇam* — "an ancient tale; a Purana".

> पितामहः पुराणं कथयति।
>
> *pitāmahaḥ purāṇaṃ kathayati.*
>
> Grandfather tells an ancient tale.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुराणम्** (*purāṇam*) is usually traced to *purā*, "formerly"; MW gives *purāṇa* as belonging to olden times and, as a noun, an ancient tale or legend, old traditional history (MW).
