# (reading) — six words off a counter

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-QUESTO-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Every word below you have met. Not one is new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nobody says them first.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-SKILL-READ-WORDS]; assesses=[IT-LEX-QUESTO-02] -->

> caffè
> pane
> acqua
> libro
> amico
> casa

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and notice that on a real sign each of these would arrive
with a word in front of it.

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-ABBASTANZA-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Italian almost never writes a noun bare. **Il libro**, **l'acqua**,
**un caffè** — the article is part of how the word arrives.

[PAUSE 2s] That is why a list like this one is slightly unusual to an Italian
eye, and why a menu is easier to read than it looks: the article in front tells
you the gender before you have to remember it.

[PAUSE 2s] Six words, and each one is a decision the page will normally make
for you.
