## You'll want to know: क्षुभ्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C456-SAMA456-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षुभ्यति** — *kṣubhyati* — "is shaken".

> वार्तया जनाः क्षुभ्यन्ति।
>
> *vārtayā janāḥ kṣubhyanti.*
>
> People are shaken by the news.

Past (he …): सः वार्तया क्षुभितवान्, *saḥ vārtayā kṣubhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षुभ्यति** (*kṣubhyati*) comes from the root √kṣubh, "to shake, to be agitated"; MW's ppp *kṣubhita* is "agitated, disturbed, frightened" (MW).
