# অন্যথায় (ônyôthāy) — failing that; otherwise (formal)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C369-KINA369-01, BN-LEX-C369-KINA369-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for whether, then the Bengali for even.

## You'll want to know: অন্যথায়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C369-KINA369-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অন্যথায়** — *ônyôthāy* — "failing that; otherwise (formal)".

> যদি সময় থাকে, তাহলে আজ এসো, অন্যথায় কাল এসো।
>
> *jôdi sômôy thāke, tāhôle āj eso, ônyôthāy kāl eso.*
>
> If you have time, come today; failing that, come tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অন্যথায়** (*ônyôthāy*) is Sanskrit *anyathā*, otherwise (Monier-Williams), with the Bengali ending -য়. It is the formal partner of নাহলে, common on notices and in letters.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
