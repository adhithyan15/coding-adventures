## You'll want to know: la gioielleria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C453-NEGOZI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la gioielleria** — "the jeweller's".

> In gioielleria guardiamo gli anelli.
>
> *At the jeweller's we gaze at the rings.*

*Il gioiello* — a jewel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gioielleria* is built on *gioiello* 'jewel', which was borrowed from Old French *joel*, usually traced to Latin *iocus* 'game, joke' (Lewis & Short).
