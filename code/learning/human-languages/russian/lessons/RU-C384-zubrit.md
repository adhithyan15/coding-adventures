## You'll want to know: зубрить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C384-UCHYOBA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**зубрить** (*zubrít*) — "to cram, to learn by rote".

> Перед экзаменом он зубрил всю ночь.
>
> *Péred ekzámenom on zubríl vsyu noch.*
>
> Before the exam he crammed all night.

Imperfective; present *зубрю, зубришь*; perfective *вызубрить*. Colloquial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**зубрить** is usually linked with *зуб* "a tooth", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*zǫbŭ*: to gnaw away at a subject until it is learned.
