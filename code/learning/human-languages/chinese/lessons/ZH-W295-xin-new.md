## Script — two pieces on the left, one on the right
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XIN-NEW-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 新

**Three pieces: two stacked on the left, one on the right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**Top left, five strokes.** The piece on the left of 站: a dot, a bar, a dot falling to the right and a short stroke falling to the left under it, then a bar across the foot.

**Bottom left, four strokes.** A bar, a vertical that hooks to the left at the foot, a short sweep to the lower left and a dot to the lower right: the tree piece of 机, with a hook.

**The right piece, four strokes.** A short sweep down to the left at the top. A long stroke down from under it that curves to the lower left. A bar across to the right. Then a long vertical down to the foot.

5 + 4 + 4 = thirteen.
