## You'll want to know: 门票
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C213-TRIP213-04]; assesses=[] -->

**门票** — *ménpiào* — "admission ticket".

> **门票多少钱** *ménpiào duōshao qián* — How much is it to get in?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**门票** (*ménpiào*) is built from 门 'gate' and 票 'a ticket'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *mwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of 票, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
