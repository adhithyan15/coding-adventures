Russian, chapter 494: Marriage and Break-ups.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
супруг (suprúg) — a spouse, a husband.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for tenderness, then the Russian for affectionate.

You'll want to know: супруг.
супруг (suprúg) — "a spouse, a husband".
На приём она пришла с супругом. Na priyóm oná prishlá s suprúgom. She came to the reception with her husband.
Formal. The wife is супруга; the plural супруги means a married couple.

The word, taken apart.
супруг (suprúg) is inherited from Proto-Slavic sǫprǫgъ (EtymDB), built on sǫ- 'together' and a root usually glossed 'to harness', so spouses are a pair in one harness.

Lesson 2 of 5.
брак (brak) — marriage; a defect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for affectionate, then the Russian for a spouse.

You'll want to know: брак.
брак (brak) — "marriage; a defect".
Их брак длится уже тридцать лет. Ikh brak dlítsya uzhé trídtsat let. Their marriage has lasted thirty years.
Masculine. A second word брак (brak) means faulty goods: бракованный — defective.

The word, taken apart.
брак (brak) 'marriage' was borrowed from Church Slavonic brakъ, the shape with -ра- beside Proto-Slavic borkъ (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
измена (izména) — infidelity; betrayal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a spouse, then the Russian for marriage.

You'll want to know: измена.
измена (izména) — "infidelity; betrayal".
Она не смогла простить мужу измену. Oná ne smoglá prostít múzhu izménu. She couldn't forgive her husband for being unfaithful.
Feminine. Also treason: измена (izména) родине.

The word, taken apart.
измена (izména) is built on из- and the root of менять 'to change'; EtymDB lists измена (izména) without a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
изменять (izmenyát) — to be unfaithful; to change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for marriage, then the Russian for infidelity.

You'll want to know: изменять.
изменять (izmenyát) — "to be unfaithful; to change".
Он никогда не изменял жене. On nikogdá ne izmenyál zhené. He has never been unfaithful to his wife.
Imperfective; perfective изменить. With the dative, to be unfaithful: изменять (izmenyát) жене; with the accusative, to change: изменять (izmenyát) план.

The word, taken apart.
изменять (izmenyát) is built on из- and менять 'to change'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
расставание (rasstavániye) — a parting, a break-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for infidelity, then the Russian for to be unfaithful.

You'll want to know: расставание.
расставание (rasstavániye) — "a parting, a break-up".
После расставания они остались друзьями. Pósle rasstavániya oní ostális druzyámi. After the break-up they stayed friends.
Neuter. The verbs are расставаться / расстаться — to part.

The word, taken apart.
расставание (rasstavániye) is built on расставаться 'to part', from раз- 'apart' and стать 'to stand, to become'; EtymDB has no entry for the noun.
