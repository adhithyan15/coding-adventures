Gujarati, chapter 160: Bedding, swing and light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પથારી (pathārī) — bedding, bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spill, then the Gujarati for floor.

You'll want to know: પથારી.
પથારી — pathārī — "bedding, bed".
મારી પથારી તૈયાર છે. — mārī pathārī taiyār che — "My bed is ready."

The word, taken apart.
પથારી (pathārī) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit prastāra, "spreading out; bed of straw", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "bedding" (CDIAL 8864).

Lesson 2 of 5.
પાથરવું (pātharvũ) — to spread out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for floor, then the Gujarati for bedding.

You'll want to know: પાથરવું.
પાથરવું — pātharvũ — "to spread out".
તે રાત્રે પથારી પાથરે છે. — te rātre pathārī pāthare che — "He spreads out the bedding at night."

The word, taken apart.
પાથરવું (pātharvũ) is a tadbhava, close kin of pathārī. It comes from prastarati, "spreads out", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit prastṛṇoti; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to spread" (CDIAL 8860).

Lesson 3 of 5.
હિંચકો (hincko) — swing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for bedding, then the Gujarati for to spread out.

You'll want to know: હિંચકો.
હિંચકો — hincko — "swing".
ઓસરીમાં હિંચકો છે. — osrīmā̃ hincko che — "There is a swing on the veranda."

The word, taken apart.
હિંચકો (hincko) is built on the inherited verb hĩcvũ, "to swing", which CDIAL lists with hicakvũ under hicc, "draw back", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 14083).

Lesson 4 of 5.
બત્તી (battī) — electric light, lamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spread out, then the Gujarati for swing.

You'll want to know: બત્તી.
બત્તી — battī — "electric light, lamp".
આ બત્તી નવી છે. — ā battī navī che — "This light is new."

The word, taken apart.
બત્તી (battī) is usually traced to Sanskrit varti, "wick", and EtymDB derives it from that word, but its path is not settled. CDIAL lists the kindred Gujarati vāṭ, "lamp", and vātī under that Sanskrit word, but not battī itself (CDIAL 11359).

You'll want to know: હોલવવું.
હોલવવું — holavvũ — "to put out, to switch off".
હું રાત્રે બત્તી હોલવું છું. — hũ rātre battī holavũ chũ — "I switch off the light at night."

The word, taken apart.
હોલવવું (holavvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to extinguish", under Sanskrit avaśrayaṇa, "taking a pot off the fire" (CDIAL 855a).
