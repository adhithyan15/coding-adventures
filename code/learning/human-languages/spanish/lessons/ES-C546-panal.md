## You'll want to know: el pañal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C546-BAUTIZO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el pañal** — "the nappy, the diaper".

> Hay que cambiarle el pañal al bebé.
>
> *The baby's nappy needs changing.*

*Estar en pañales* — to be in its infancy, to know very little about something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pañal* is built on *paño* 'cloth' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *pannus* 'a piece of cloth'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
