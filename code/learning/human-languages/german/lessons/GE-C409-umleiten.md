## You'll want to know: umleiten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C409-UMLEITUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**umleiten** — "to divert (traffic)".

> Die Polizei leitet die Autos über die Landstraße um.
>
> *The police divert the cars along the country road.*

Separable: *sie leitet um*, *sie hat umgeleitet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*umleiten* is built from *um* 'round' and *leiten* 'to lead', an inherited verb from Old High German *leiten* (EtymDB).
