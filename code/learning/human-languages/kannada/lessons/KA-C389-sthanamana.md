# ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ (sthānamāna) — status; standing

## You'll want to know: ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C389-SHLAGHISU389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ** — *sthānamāna* — "status; standing".

> ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅವರಿಗೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ūrinalli avarige oḷḷeya sthānamāna ide.*
>
> They have a good standing in the town.

ಸ್ಥಾನ (place) + ಮಾನ (honour).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ** (*sthānamāna*) is built from Sanskrit *sthāna*, "a place, a position" (CDIAL 13753), paired with *māna*, "honour, respect" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
