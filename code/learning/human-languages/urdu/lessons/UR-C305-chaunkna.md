## You'll want to know: چونکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C305-MUAMMA305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چونکنا** — *chaunknā* — "to be startled".

> خبر سن کر سب چونک گئے۔
>
> *khabar sun kar sab chaunk gae.*
>
> Everyone was startled by the news.

Past (he …): وہ چونکا, *voh chaunkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چونکنا** (*chaunknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *caũknā*, "a sudden start", under *\*camakka* (CDIAL 4676), the entry that also holds **چمکنا**.
