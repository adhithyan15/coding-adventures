## You'll want to know: gracioso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C713-DIVERTIDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**gracioso** — "funny, amusing".

> Tu hermano es muy gracioso.
>
> *Your brother is very funny.*

*Gracioso* is funny in a pleasant way; *chistoso* is close.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gracioso* was borrowed from Latin *grātiōsus* "agreeable, popular" (EtymDB), built on *grātia* "favour, charm", the source of *gracia*.
