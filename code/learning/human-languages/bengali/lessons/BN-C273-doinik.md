## You'll want to know: দৈনিক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C273-DOINIK-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C92-TIME92-04, BN-LEX-C176-KAJ176-04, BN-LEX-C87-NUMBERS87-05, BN-LEX-C64-SCHOOL64-04, BN-LEX-C45-HEALTH45-03] -->

**দৈনিক** — *doinik* — "of each day; a daily paper".

> **আমার দৈনিক খরচ একশো টাকা।**
>
> *āmār doinik khôrôch eksho ṭākā.* — "My daily spending is a hundred taka."

**রোজ** tells you when: "every day". **দৈনিক** describes a thing: **দৈনিক খরচ**, "daily spending", **দৈনিক কাজ**, "daily work". On its own it is a newspaper that comes out every day: **বাবা রোজ একটা দৈনিক পড়েন** (*bābā roj ekṭā doinik pôṛen*), "Father reads a daily every day".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দৈনিক** (*doinik*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *dainika*, "daily" (Monier-Williams). It belongs with *dina*, "day", Bengali **দিন**: CDIAL (6566) gives the related *daina*, "relating to a day", and refers it to *dina*; *dainika* has the same strengthened first vowel, *ai*, with *-ika*, "of, belonging to", added.
