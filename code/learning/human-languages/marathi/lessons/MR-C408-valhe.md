## You'll want to know: वल्हे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C408-NAVADI408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वल्हे** — *valhe* — "an oar".

> होडीत दोन वल्ही होती.
>
> *hoḍīt don valhī hotī.*
>
> There were two oars in the boat.

Neuter. Plural: वल्ही, *valhī*.

A neuter noun: one वल्हे, two वल्ही.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वल्हे** (*valhe*) is a Marathi word for an oar; its origin is not settled.
