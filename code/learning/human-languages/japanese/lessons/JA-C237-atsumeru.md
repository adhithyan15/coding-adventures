## You'll want to know: あつめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C237-PHOTO237-03]; assesses=[] -->

**あつめる** — *atsumeru* — "to collect".

> **きょねん おとうとは きっぷを あつめました。** — *kyonen otōto wa kippu o atsumemashita* — "Last year my younger brother collected tickets."

Polite **あつめます**, *atsumemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あつめる** (*atsumeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
