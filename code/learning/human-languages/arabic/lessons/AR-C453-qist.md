## You'll want to know: قسط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C453-QIST453-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قسط** — *qisṭ* — "an instalment; a share".

> دفع أبي قسط السيارة هذا الشهر.
>
> *dafaʿa abī qisṭ as-sayyāra hādhā ash-shahr.*
>
> My father paid the car instalment this month.

Plural: أقساط, *aqsāṭ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قسط** (*qisṭ*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-s-ṭ*, "a fair share, a portion" (Lane).
