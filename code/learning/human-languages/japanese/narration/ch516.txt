Japanese, chapter 516: Getting Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しゅっせする (shusse suru) — to get ahead (in life).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for grades, then the Japanese for a cram school (for entrance exams).

You'll want to know: しゅっせする.
しゅっせする — shusse suru — "to get ahead (in life)".
もし かれが しゅっせしたら、しゃちょうに なるでしょう。 — moshi kare ga shusseshitara, shachō ni naru deshō — "If he gets ahead, he will probably become company president."
Polite しゅっせします, shusseshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): あにが かいしゃで しゅっせしました。, ani ga kaisha de shusseshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しゅっせする (shusse suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun shusse with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shu, "exit" (Middle Chinese tsyhwit) and se, "generation" (syejH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
のりこえる (norikoeru) — to get over; to overcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a cram school (for entrance exams), then the Japanese for to get ahead (in life).

You'll want to know: のりこえる.
のりこえる — norikoeru — "to get over; to overcome".
もし この しけんを のりこえたら、そつぎょうです。 — moshi kono shiken o norikoetara, sotsugyō desu — "If I get through this exam, I graduate."
Polite のりこえます, norikoemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): かれが びょうきを のりこえました。, kare ga byōki o norikoemashita.

The word, taken apart.
のりこえる (norikoeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from noru, "to ride", and koeru, "to cross"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 5.
やるき (yaruki) — the will to do something; motivation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to get over, then the Japanese for all one's strength.

You'll want to know: やるき.
やるき — yaruki — "the will to do something; motivation".
もし やるきが あったら、かれは しゅっせするでしょう。 — moshi yaruki ga attara, kare wa shusse suru deshō — "If he had the drive, he would get ahead."

The word, taken apart.
やるき (yaruki) mixes the two layers, native and Sino-Japanese: native yaru, "to do", and Sino-Japanese ki, "spirit" (Middle Chinese khj+jH), borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for all one's strength, then the Japanese for the will to do something.

You'll want to know: いどむ.
いどむ — idomu — "to tackle (a challenge); to take on".
もし まけたら、らいねん もう いちど いどみます。 — moshi maketara, rainen mō ichido idomimasu — "If I lose, I will take it on once more next year."
Polite いどみます, idomimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): かれが たかい やまに いどみました。, kare ga takai yama ni idomimashita.

The word, taken apart.
いどむ (idomu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
