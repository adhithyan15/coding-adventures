## You'll want to know: みはる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C315-SURUTO315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**みはる** — *miharu* — "to keep watch; to guard".

> **おまわりさんが みちを みはっています。** — *omawarisan ga michi o mihatte imasu* — "A police officer is keeping watch on the road."

Polite **みはります**, *miharimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おまわりさんが えきを みはりました。**, *omawarisan ga eki o miharimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みはる** (*miharu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *mi*, "seeing" (from *miru*), and *haru*, "to stretch" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
