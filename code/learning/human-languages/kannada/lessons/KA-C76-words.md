# (reading) — six words, and a script shaped by a leaf

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C54-TREE-01, KA-LEX-C59-GROUND-01, KA-LEX-C52-WELCOME-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Almost none. Kannada rounds nearly every letter, and the reason is
the material: the script was cut into palm leaf for a thousand years, and a
straight stroke splits a leaf along its grain.

[PAUSE 2s] So the curves are not decoration. They are what a scribe had to do
to keep the page from tearing, kept long after the page became paper.

[PAUSE 2s] A reader gains from it too. Rounded shapes are easier to tell apart
at a glance than straight ones, which is most of what reading a word cold is.
