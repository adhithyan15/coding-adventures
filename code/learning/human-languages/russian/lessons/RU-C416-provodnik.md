## You'll want to know: проводник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C416-PROVODNIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**проводник** (*provodník*) — "a sleeping-car attendant; a guide".

> Проводник принёс нам чай.
>
> *Provodník prinyós nam chay.*
>
> The attendant brought us tea.

Feminine *проводница*. The stress moves: *проводника* (*provodniká*). Also a conductor of electricity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*проводник* is built on *проводить* 'to lead through' with *-ник* 'one who'. *Водить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *voditi* 'to lead' (EtymDB).
