Gujarati, chapter 363: What the Figures Say.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
આંકડો (ā̃kḍo) — a figure (a number).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a sample, then the Gujarati for to deduce.

You'll want to know: આંકડો.
આંકડો — ā̃kḍo — "a figure (a number)".
આ આંકડા દર્શાવે છે કે ગામમાં વરસાદ ઓછો થયો. ā ā̃kḍā darśāve che ke gāmmā̃ varsād ocho thayo. These figures show that the village has had less rain.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આંકડો (ā̃kḍo) is built on Sanskrit aṅka, "a mark, a numeral" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
તારણ (tāraṇ) — a finding (a conclusion).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a figure, then the Gujarati for a calculation.

You'll want to know: તારણ.
તારણ — tāraṇ — "a finding (a conclusion)".
આ સંશોધનનું તારણ શું છે? ā sanśodhannũ tāraṇ śũ che? What did this research find?
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
તારણ (tāraṇ) CDIAL lists Gujarati tāraṇ, "a taking across, a pledge", under Sanskrit tāraṇa, "crossing, safe passage" (CDIAL 5795); "a conclusion", what is drawn out of the facts, is a sense CDIAL does not give.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સંખ્યા (sankhyā) — a count; a number of people or things.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a calculation, then the Gujarati for a finding.

You'll want to know: સંખ્યા.
સંખ્યા — sankhyā — "a count; a number of people or things".
આ વરસે વિદ્યાર્થીઓની સંખ્યા વધી. ā varse vidyārthīonī sankhyā vadhī. The number of students went up this year.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સંખ્યા (sankhyā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit saṃkhyā, "reckoning, number" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઘટાડવું (ghaṭāḍvũ) — to bring down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a finding, then the Gujarati for a count.

You'll want to know: ઘટાડવું.
ઘટાડવું — ghaṭāḍvũ — "to bring down".
સરકાર પેટ્રોલનો ભાવ ઘટાડશે. sarkār peṭrolno bhāv ghaṭāḍśe. The government will bring down the price of petrol.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ભાવ ઘટાડ્યો, teṇe bhāv ghaṭāḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઘટાડવું (ghaṭāḍvũ) is the causative of ઘટવું (ghaṭvũ), "to go down", from a reconstructed ghaṭṭati, "decreases" (CDIAL 4415).
