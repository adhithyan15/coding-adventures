## You'll want to know: اتفاق افتادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C301-ETTEFAQ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اتفاق افتادن** (*ettefâq oftâdan*) — "to happen".

> دیشب در خیابان ما یک اتفاق عجیب افتاد.
>
> *Dishab dar khiyâbân-e mâ yek ettefâq-e 'ajib oftâd.*
>
> Last night something strange happened in our street.

Literally "an event falls". Present stem *oft-*: *in ziyâd ettefâq mioftad* "this happens a lot". *Che ettefâqi oftâd?* "What happened?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The noun *ettefâq* is borrowed from Arabic *ittifāq* (EtymDB). The verb *oftâdan* "to fall" is inherited Persian, so the phrase is a Persian compound verb built on an Arabic noun.
