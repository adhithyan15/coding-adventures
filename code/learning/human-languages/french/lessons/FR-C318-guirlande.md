## You'll want to know: la guirlande
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C318-ARTIFICE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la guirlande** — "the garland, the string of lights".

> Il y a des guirlandes dans toutes les rues.
>
> *There are strings of lights in every street.*

*Une guirlande électrique* — fairy lights.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guirlande* was borrowed from Italian *ghirlanda* "garland" (EtymDB); the origin of the Italian word is not settled.
