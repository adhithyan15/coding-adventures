Japanese, chapter 456: Pay and Time Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
きゅうりょう (kyūryō) — a salary; pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for being in charge, then the Japanese for to take over (duties).

You'll want to know: きゅうりょう.
きゅうりょう — kyūryō — "a salary; pay".
きゅうりょうが すこし あがりました。 — kyūryō ga sukoshi agarimashita — "My salary went up a little."

The word, taken apart.
きゅうりょう (kyūryō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kyū, "salary" (Middle Chinese kip) and ryō, "fee" (lew) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
かせぐ (kasegu) — to earn (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to take over (duties), then the Japanese for a salary.

You'll want to know: かせぐ.
かせぐ — kasegu — "to earn (money)".
あには みせで はたらいて、おかねを かせいでいます。 — ani wa mise de hataraite, okane o kaseide imasu — "My elder brother earns money working in a shop."
Polite かせぎます, kasegimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ちちが おかねを かせぎました。, chichi ga okane o kasegimashita.

The word, taken apart.
かせぐ (kasegu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

Lesson 5 of 5.
しょくば (shokuba) — a workplace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for time off work, then the Japanese for to hire.

You'll want to know: しょくば.
しょくば — shokuba — "a workplace".
しょくばの みんなは しんせつです。 — shokuba no minna wa shinsetsu desu — "Everyone at my workplace is kind."

The word, taken apart.
しょくば (shokuba) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese shoku, "employment" (Middle Chinese tsyik), borrowed from Chinese, joined to native ba, "place", the kun'yomi of its second kanji (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
