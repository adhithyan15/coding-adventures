## You'll want to know: গম্বুজ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C320-SMRITI320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**গম্বুজ** — *gômbuj* — "a dome".

> পর্যটকরা মসজিদের বড় গম্বুজের ছবি তুললেন।
>
> *pôrjôṭôkrā mosjider bôṛo gômbujer chhôbi tullen.*
>
> The tourists took pictures of the mosque's great dome.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গম্বুজ** (*gômbuj*) is usually traced to Persian *gumbaz*, 'a dome', a word that came with the domed mosques and tombs of Bengal.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
