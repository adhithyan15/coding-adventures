## You'll want to know: 热心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C258-KIND258-02]; assesses=[] -->

**热心** — *rèxīn* — "warm-hearted; eager to help".

> **我们的校长很热心** *wǒmen de xiàozhǎng hěn rèxīn* — Our head teacher is very warm-hearted.

The new character, in pieces: 热 — ten strokes: the hand piece of 打, three strokes; beside it a stroke falling to the left, a bar that turns down and hooks up, and a dot; then four dots along the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**热心** (*rèxīn*) is built from 热 'hot' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyet* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as burning, as with fire.
