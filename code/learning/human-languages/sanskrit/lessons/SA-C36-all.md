## You'll want to know: सर्वम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C36-REPLY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सर्वम्** (*sarvam*) — "all, the whole of it".

**सर्वम्** answers "how much?" with "all of it", and it can stand alone as that answer.

Its cousins lean on *whole* rather than on *every*: Greek *holos*, behind *holistic* and *catholic*; Latin *sollus*, "entire", and *salvus*, "safe and whole", behind *save*, *salvage* and *salute*. **सर्वम्**, *holistic* and *salvage* all start from one idea — nothing missing.
