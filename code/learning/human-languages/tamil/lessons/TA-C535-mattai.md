## You'll want to know: மட்டை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C535-AADUKALAM535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மட்டை** — *maṭṭai* — "a bat".

> அவன் புதிய கிரிக்கெட் மட்டை வாங்கினான்.
>
> *avaṉ putiya kirikkeṭ maṭṭai vāṅkiṉāṉ.*
>
> He bought a new cricket bat.

It first named the stiff stalk of a palm leaf, from which children still make bats.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மட்டை** (*maṭṭai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4663 gives மட்டை (*maṭṭai*), "the stalk of a palm leaf, the stem of a plantain"; the bat is a later use.
