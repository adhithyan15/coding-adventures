## You'll want to know: a medalha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C511-TORNEIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a medalha** — "medal".

> Ganhou uma medalha de ouro.
>
> *She won a gold medal.*

*Medalha de prata*, *de bronze* — silver, bronze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*medalha* comes from Italian *medaglia* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *metallum* "metal", from Greek.
