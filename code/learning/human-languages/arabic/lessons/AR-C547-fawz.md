## You'll want to know: فوز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C547-BUTULA547-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فوز** — *fawz* — "a triumph; a win".

> احتفل اللاعبون بالفوز.
>
> *iḥtafala al-lāʿibūn bi-l-fawz.*
>
> The players celebrated the win.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فوز** (*fawz*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-w-z*, "to win, to escape safely" (Lane). Fāza, to win, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
