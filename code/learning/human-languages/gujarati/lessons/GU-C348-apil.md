# અપીલ (apīl) — an appeal (to a higher court)

## You'll want to know: અપીલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C348-KES348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અપીલ** — *apīl* — "an appeal (to a higher court)".

> અરુણે આ કેસમાં અપીલ કરી.
>
> *Aruṇe ā kesmā̃ apīl karī.*
>
> Arun appealed in this case.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અપીલ** (*apīl*) is borrowed from English *appeal*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
