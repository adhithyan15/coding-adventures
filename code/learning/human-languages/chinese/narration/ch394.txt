Mandarin Chinese, chapter 394: Success or Failure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
chénggōng — to succeed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for at any time at all, then the Mandarin for going smoothly.

You'll want to know: chénggōng.
chénggōng — "to succeed".
rúguǒ nǐ nǔlì jiù huì chénggōng — If you work hard, you'll succeed.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
chénggōng is built from 成 'to complete' and gōng 'merit', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyeng and kuwng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'an achievement'.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what would happen.

Lesson 4 of 5.
báifèi — to be wasted (of effort).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to fail, then the Mandarin for to take a risk.

You'll want to know: báifèi.
báifèi — "to be wasted (of effort)".
yàoshi nǐ xiànzài bú zuò yǐqián de nǔlì jiù báifèi le — If you don't do it now, all your work so far will be wasted.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
báifèi is built from 白 'white, blank' and fèi 'to use up', both inherited from Middle Chinese, baek and phj+jH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the colour of the west'. 白 for 'in vain' is not given there.

Lesson 5 of 5.
不变 (búbiàn) — unchanged; staying the same.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to take a risk, then the Mandarin for to be wasted (of effort).

You'll want to know: 不变.
不变 — búbiàn — "unchanged; staying the same".
时间不变地点改了 shíjiān búbiàn dìdiǎn gǎi le — The time stays the same; the place has changed.
Tone: 不 is said bú here, rising, because biàn after it is a falling tone.

The word, taken apart.
不变 (búbiàn) is built from 不 'not' and 变 'to change', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjut and pjenH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
