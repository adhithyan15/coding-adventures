Portuguese, chapter 118: Rabbit, Tiger, Butterfly, Frog, Tortoise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o coelho — a rabbit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a wood, then the Portuguese for a donkey.

You'll want to know: o coelho.
o coelho — "a rabbit".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o coelho mean? ("A rabbit".) Say it once more.
