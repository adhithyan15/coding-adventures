# A small plan: when, then what I will do

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C28-LEAVE-02, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-02, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall tomorrow, the day after tomorrow, and the three complete
forms “I will go,” “I will do,” and “I will read.”

## Grammar Lens: time first, plan second

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C264-NEAR-TERM-PLAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

Make a near-term plan in two small choices: choose **श्वः** (*śvaḥ*) or
**परश्वः** (*paraśvaḥ*), then choose one complete first-person future form.

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C28-LEAVE-02, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-02, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02, SA-PATTERN-C264-NEAR-TERM-PLAN-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: “Tomorrow, I will read” — *śvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: “The day after tomorrow, I will go” — *paraśvaḥ gamiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: “Tomorrow, I will do the work” — *śvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi*]
