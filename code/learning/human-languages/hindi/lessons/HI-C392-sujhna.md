## You'll want to know: सूझना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C392-TAIYARI392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सूझना** — *sūjhnā* — "to occur to (someone), to strike".

> अगर तुम्हें कोई उपाय सूझेगा तो मुझे बताओ।
>
> *agar tumhẽ koī upāy sūjhegā to mujhe batāo.*
>
> If a way out occurs to you, tell me.

Past (it …): वह उपाय उसे सूझा, *vah upāy use sūjhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूझना** (*sūjhnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *śudhyati*, "becomes clear" (CDIAL 12527).
