# శిశువు (śiśuvu) — a newborn; an infant child

## You'll want to know: శిశువు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C531-SANTANAM531-02]; assesses=[] -->

**శిశువు** — *śiśuvu* — "a newborn; an infant child".

> ఆసుపత్రిలో శిశువు ఆరోగ్యంగా ఉంది.
>
> *āsupatrilō śiśuvu ārōgyaṅgā undi.*
>
> The newborn in the hospital is healthy.

Formal and medical; పాప or బాబు in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శిశువు** (*śiśuvu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śiśu*, "the young of an animal, a child" (CDIAL 12476).
