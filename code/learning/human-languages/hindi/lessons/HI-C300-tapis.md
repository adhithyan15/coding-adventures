## You'll want to know: तपिश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C300-DAHAK300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तपिश** — *tapiś* — "scorching heat".

> आग की तपिश से खिड़की का शीशा टूट गया।
>
> *āg kī tapiś se khiṛkī kā śīśā ṭūṭ gayā.*
>
> The heat of the fire cracked the window glass.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तपिश** (*tapiś*) is usually traced to Persian *tapiš*, "heat, a burning". It is stronger than **गर्मी**: the heat that comes off a fire or a road at noon.
