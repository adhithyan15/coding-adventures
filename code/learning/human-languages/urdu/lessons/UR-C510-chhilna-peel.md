## You'll want to know: چھیلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C510-CHHALNI510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چھیلنا** — *chhīlnā* — "to peel".

> میں نے چھری سے آلو چھیلے۔
>
> *maiṅ ne chhurī se ālū chhīle.*
>
> I peeled the potatoes with a knife.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سیب چھیلا, *us ne seb chhīlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھیلنا** (*chhīlnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *chīlnā*, "to peel", under *\*chilla*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5051).
