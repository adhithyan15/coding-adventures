## You'll want to know: कृपया तूष्णीं भव

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C271-REQUEST-QUIET-02]; assesses=[] -->

> **कृपया तूष्णीं भव।**
>
> *kṛpayā tūṣṇīṃ bhava.*
>
> “Please be quiet.”

The Board of School Education Haryana copy of the NCERT Class 10
[*Śemuṣī* Sanskrit reader](https://bseh.org.in/uploads/ebooks/20_Class_10th_Shemushi_Sansktit_120pp.pdf)
uses the exact command **तूष्णीं भव**. The grammatical commentary on
[Pāṇini 3.4.63](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/3/3.4.63.htm)
also explicitly treats **तूष्णीम्** with √भू. The **कृपया** at the front is
the “please” you already know.

## Grammar Lens: one complete request

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C271-REQUEST-QUIET-02] -->

This lesson owns the complete familiar-person request. It does not teach the
imperative paradigm, and it does not license you to manufacture other command
forms from **भव**.
