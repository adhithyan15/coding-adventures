## You'll want to know: ಹೀಗಾಗಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C410-HIGAGI410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೀಗಾಗಿ** — *hīgāgi* — "thus; for this reason".

> ಬಸ್ ತಡವಾಯಿತು, ಹೀಗಾಗಿ ನಾನು ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದೆ.
>
> *bas taḍavāyitu, hīgāgi nānu taḍavāgi bande.*
>
> The bus was late, so I came late.

ಹೀಗೆ (like this) + ಆಗಿ (being).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೀಗಾಗಿ** (*hīgāgi*) is native Dravidian, a phrase grown into one word: *hīge*, "in this way" (DEDR 410), and *āgi*, "having become" (DEDR 333).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
