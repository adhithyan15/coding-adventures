## You'll want to know: संयोजयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C512-CHURIKA512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**संयोजयति** — *saṃyojayati* — "combines".

> सा दधि शर्करया संयोजयति।
>
> *sā dadhi śarkarayā saṃyojayati.*
>
> She combines the curd with sugar.

Past (he …): सः शर्करां दुग्धेन संयोजितवान्, *saḥ śarkarāṃ dugdhena saṃyojitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संयोजयति** (*saṃyojayati*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the causative of the root √yuj, "to join", which also gives *yojayati*; MW gives *saṃyuj* as to join together, to combine (MW).
