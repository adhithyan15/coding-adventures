## You'll want to know: пристань
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C424-PAROM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**пристань** (*prístan*) — "a landing stage, a jetty".

> Мы ждали паром на пристани.
>
> *My zhdáli paróm na prístani.*
>
> We waited for the ferry at the landing stage.

Feminine, though it ends in -ь: *на пристани*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пристань* is built on *пристать* 'to put in to shore', from *при-* and *стать* 'to stand, to come to a stop'. EtymDB lists *пристать* without a deeper source.
