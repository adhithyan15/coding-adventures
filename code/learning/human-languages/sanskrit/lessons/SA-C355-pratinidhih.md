## You'll want to know: प्रतिनिधिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C355-SANSAD355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिनिधिः** — *pratinidhiḥ* — "a representative".

> प्रतिनिधिः ग्रामस्य मतं वदति।
>
> *pratinidhiḥ grāmasya mataṃ vadati.*
>
> The representative speaks for the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिनिधिः** (*pratinidhiḥ*) is built on *prati*, *ni* and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives *prati-nidhi* "a substitute, a representative, a proxy" (MW).
