German, chapter 411: Fahrkarten kaufen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Fahrkartenautomat — the ticket machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the pedestrian, then the German for the cyclist.

You'll want to know: der Fahrkartenautomat.
der Fahrkartenautomat — "the ticket machine".
Der Fahrkartenautomat nimmt auch Kreditkarten. The ticket machine also takes credit cards.
Plural: die Fahrkartenautomaten; weak noun: den Fahrkartenautomaten.

The word, taken apart.
Fahrkartenautomat is built from Fahrkarte 'ticket', from fahren and Karte 'card' (from Latin charta, Lewis & Short), and Automat 'machine', which goes back to Greek autómatos 'acting by itself' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Fahrschein — the travel ticket (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the cyclist, then the German for the ticket machine.

You'll want to know: der Fahrschein.
der Fahrschein — "the travel ticket (formal)".
Ohne Fahrschein darf man nicht in die Straßenbahn. You may not get on the tram without a ticket.
Plural: die Fahrscheine. The word on official signs; people mostly say die Fahrkarte or das Ticket.

The word, taken apart.
Fahrschein is built from fahren 'to travel' and Schein 'certificate, slip', both inherited: Schein goes back to Proto-Germanic skīnaną 'to shine' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
entwerten — to stamp, to validate (a ticket).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the travel ticket, then the German for the fare.

You'll want to know: entwerten.
entwerten — "to stamp, to validate (a ticket)".
Im Bus entwertet sie ihre Fahrkarte. On the bus she stamps her ticket.
Literally to take the value away: once stamped, the ticket cannot be used again. Inseparable: sie hat entwertet.

The word, taken apart.
entwerten is built from ent- 'away, un-' and Wert 'value, worth', an inherited Germanic word that is a cousin of English worth.

Lesson 5 of 5.
lösen — to get a ticket; to solve; to loosen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the fare, then the German for to stamp.

You'll want to know: lösen.
lösen — "to get a ticket; to solve; to loosen".
Am Bahnhof löst er eine Fahrkarte nach Hause. At the station he gets a ticket home.
Eine Fahrkarte lösen sounds a little formal. Ein Problem lösen — to solve a problem.

The word, taken apart.
lösen is inherited through Old High German lōsen from Proto-Germanic lausijaną, built on lausaz 'loose, free' (EtymDB). English loose is a cousin.
