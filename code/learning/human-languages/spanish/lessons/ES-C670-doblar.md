## You'll want to know: doblar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C670-INDICAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**doblar** — "to turn (a corner); to fold; to dub".

> Dobla a la izquierda en la plaza.
>
> *Turn left at the square.*

Very common for turning in Latin America; Spain also says *girar*. *Una película doblada* is a dubbed film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*doblar* is inherited from Late Latin *duplāre* "to double" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built on *duplus* "double".
