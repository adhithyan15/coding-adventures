## You'll want to know: のぞむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C507-HOPE507-02]; assesses=[] -->

**のぞむ** — *nozomu* — "to hope for; to desire".

> **もし かれが のぞんだら、この しごとを まかせます。** — *moshi kare ga nozondara, kono shigoto o makasemasu* — "If he wants it, I will leave this job to him."

Polite **のぞみます**, *nozomimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かれが ちいさい うちを のぞみました。**, *kare ga chiisai uchi o nozomimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のぞむ** (*nozomu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
