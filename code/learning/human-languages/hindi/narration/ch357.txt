Hindi, chapter 357: What We Value.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मूल्य (mūlya) — a value (moral); worth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for fairness, then the Hindi for to provoke.

You'll want to know: मूल्य.
मूल्य — mūlya — "a value (moral); worth".
सच बोलना हमारे परिवार का सबसे बड़ा मूल्य है। sac bolnā hamāre parivār kā sabse baṛā mūlya hai. Telling the truth is our family's greatest value.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मूल्य (mūlya) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit mūlya, "price, worth" (Monier-Williams). The moral sense, a value one lives by, is not among the senses Monier-Williams gives.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अपनाना (apnānā) — to adopt, to take up as one's own.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for honour, then the Hindi for belief.

You'll want to know: अपनाना.
अपनाना — apnānā — "to adopt, to take up as one's own".
मीरा ने गाँव की भाषा अपना ली। Mīrā ne gā̃v kī bhāṣā apnā lī. Mira took the village's language as her own.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने यह आदत अपनाई, usne yah ādat apnāī.

The word, taken apart.
अपनाना (apnānā) is a verb made on अपना, "one's own", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed ātmanaka, "own" (CDIAL 1135): to make something one's own.
