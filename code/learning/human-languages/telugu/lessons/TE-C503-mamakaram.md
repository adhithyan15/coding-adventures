# మమకారం (mamakāraṁ) — attachment; possessive love

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: మమకారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C503-PREMA503-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మమకారం** — *mamakāraṁ* — "attachment; possessive love".

> ఆ పాత ఇంటి మీద నాన్నకి మమకారం ఎక్కువ.
>
> *ā pāta iṇṭi mīda nānnaki mamakāraṁ ekkuva.*
>
> Dad is very attached to that old house.

What you are attached to takes మీద.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మమకారం** (*mamakāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mamakāra* (Monier-Williams), built from *mama*, "mine", and *kāra*, "making": the feeling that a person or a thing is your own.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
