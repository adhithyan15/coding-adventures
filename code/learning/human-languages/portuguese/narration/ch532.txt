Portuguese, chapter 532: Uma casa acolhedora.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
a lareira — fireplace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for (electric) drill, then the Portuguese for pliers.

You'll want to know: a lareira.
a lareira — "fireplace".
No inverno, jantamos ao pé da lareira. In winter, we have dinner by the fireplace.
Acender a lareira — to light the fire.

The word, taken apart.
lareira is built on lar "hearth, home", which comes from Latin lar, the god of the household (Lewis & Short).

O soalho da casa antiga range. The wooden floor in the old house creaks.

soalho is usually traced to a form built on Latin solum "ground, floor" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 7.
a decoração — decoration, decor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for bookcase, then the Portuguese for wooden floor.

You'll want to know: a decoração.
a decoração — "decoration, decor".
Gosto muito da decoração da tua casa. I really like the decor of your house.
Built on decorar in the sense "to decorate".

The word, taken apart.
decoração is built on decorar "to decorate", a learned borrowing from Latin decorāre "to adorn" (Lewis & Short), built on decus "ornament, grace".
