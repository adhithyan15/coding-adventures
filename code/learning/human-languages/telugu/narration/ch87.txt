Telugu, chapter 87: Right, Wrong, Maybe.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
తప్పు (tappu) — a mistake; wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for one minute, then the Telugu for please wait.

You'll want to know: తప్పు.
తప్పు (tappu) — "a mistake", and as a reply, "wrong".
క్షమించండి, నా తప్పు (tappu) — "sorry, my mistake".
You have met it before without seeing it: తప్పకుండా, "certainly", is built on the verb behind this word, "to slip, to miss". Certainly is without slipping.

What you've built.
A word for a mistake, and the word for certainly finally explained.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tappu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tappu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kṣamin̄caṇḍi, nā tappu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for one minute, then the Telugu for please wait, then say tappu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does తప్పు (tappu) mean? ("A mistake; wrong".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 7.
సరిగ్గా (sariggā) — exactly, properly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for please wait, then the Telugu for a mistake.

You'll want to know: సరిగ్గా.
సరిగ్గా (sariggā) — "exactly, properly".
Said alone, సరిగ్గా (sariggā)! is "exactly!". It shares its start with సరే, "okay": both lean on సరి, "right, even".

What you've built.
Wrong and exactly right, as a pair.

Lesson 3 of 7.
బహుశా (bahuśā) — probably, perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a mistake, then the Telugu for exactly.

You'll want to know: బహుశా.
బహుశా (bahuśā) — "probably, perhaps".
బహుశా రేపు — "probably tomorrow". Compare ఏమో: ēmō shrugs, "who knows", while bahuśā makes a guess.

What you've built.
A guess, next to the shrug you already had.

Lesson 4 of 7.
అలాగా (alāgā) — is that so? I see.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for exactly, then the Telugu for probably.

You'll want to know: అలాగా.
అలాగా (alāgā) — "is that so?", "I see".
It sits one vowel away from అలాగే, "alright then". Alāgā receives news; alāgē agrees to a plan. Say both and hear the difference in the last vowel.

What you've built.
A way to show you are listening.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: alāgā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: alāgā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say alāgā, then alāgē, and hear the last vowel change]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for exactly, then the Telugu for probably, then say alāgā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does అలాగా (alāgā) mean? ("Is that so? I see".) Say it once more.


Lesson 5 of 7.
నిజంగా (nijaṁgā) — really, truly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for probably, then the Telugu for I see.

You'll want to know: నిజంగా.
నిజంగా (nijaṁgā) — "really, truly".
నిజం is "the truth", and -గా turns it into a manner, as it did for nemmadigā. As a question, నిజంగా (nijaṁgā)? is "really?".

What you've built.
Five replies: wrong, exactly, probably, I see, really.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nijaṁgā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nijaṁgā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nijaṁgā? with a rising voice]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for probably, then the Telugu for I see, then say nijaṁgā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does నిజంగా (nijaṁgā) mean? ("Really, truly".) Say it once more.


Lesson 6 of 7.
Keeping a conversation going — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Someone speaks fast and quietly. Ask for three things: slowly, louder, once more.

Grammar Lens: the ending -గా.
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
word: నెమ్మదిగా. built from: slow + -గా.
word: గట్టిగా. built from: firm + -గా.
word: సరిగ్గా (sariggā). built from: right + -గా.
word: నిజంగా (nijaṁgā). built from: truth + -గా.
One ending, four manners. It is the same -గా that made nāku caligā undi.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: ask someone to wait one minute]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: you made a mistake; say sorry and own it]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: agree strongly, then guess "probably tomorrow"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: react to news with "I see", then with "really?"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Slowly? (నెమ్మదిగా.) Once more? (ఇంకోసారి.) Louder? (గట్టిగా.) One minute? (ఒక్క నిమిషం.) Please wait? (ఆగండి.) My mistake? (నా తప్పు (tappu).) Exactly? (సరిగ్గా (sariggā).) Probably? (బహుశా (bahuśā).) I see? (అలాగా (alāgā).) Really? (నిజంగా (nijaṁgā).)


Lesson 7 of 7.
How are you, honestly — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
You are not well today. Say that you have a fever, that your head aches, and that you are tired.

Grammar Lens: two ways a feeling reaches you.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
it comes to you: నాకు జ్వరం వచ్చింది. it is so, to you: నాకు అలసటగా ఉంది.
it comes to you: నాకు కోపం వచ్చింది. it is so, to you: నాకు భయంగా ఉంది.
Both keep the person in the dative నాకు. One says the thing arrived; the other says how it is with you now. చలి and బాధ take the right-hand frame too.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: you are thirsty; ask for water]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: it is cold today; then, it is very hot today]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tell a friend with a stomach ache to rest]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a friend says they are sad; react with "really?", then "I see"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A fever? (జ్వరం.) A pain? (నొప్పి.) Tiredness? (అలసట.) Thirst? (దాహం.) Rest? (విశ్రాంతి.) Cold, and heat? (చలి, వేడి.) Fear, anger, sorrow? (భయం, కోపం, బాధ.) Really? (నిజంగా (nijaṁgā).) I see? (అలాగా (alāgā).)
