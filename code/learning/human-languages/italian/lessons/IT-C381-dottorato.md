## You'll want to know: il dottorato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C381-DOCENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il dottorato** — "the doctorate, the PhD".

> Dopo la laurea vorrei fare un dottorato.
>
> *After my degree I would like to do a PhD.*

*Un dottorato di ricerca* — a research doctorate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dottorato* is built on *dottore*, a learned borrowing from Latin *doctor* "a teacher" (Lewis & Short), from *docēre* "to teach".
