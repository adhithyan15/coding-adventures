## You'll want to know: पुण्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C338-SVARGA338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पुण्यम्** — *puṇyam* — "merit; a good deed".

> दानेन पुण्यं वर्धते।
>
> *dānena puṇyaṃ vardhate.*
>
> Merit grows through giving.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुण्यम्** (*puṇyam*) MW suggests, with a perhaps, the root √puṣ, "to thrive", and gives *puṇya* as good, virtuous and, as a noun, virtue, a good work, religious merit, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
