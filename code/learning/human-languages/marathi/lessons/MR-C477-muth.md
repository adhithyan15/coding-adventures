## You'll want to know: मूठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C477-HAT477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मूठ** — *mūṭh* — "a fist; a handful".

> त्याने मुठीत एक नाणं लपवलं.
>
> *tyāne muṭhīt ek nāṇaṁ lapavlaṁ.*
>
> He hid a coin in his fist.

Feminine.

Oblique मुठी-: मुठीत, in the fist. मूठभर, a handful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूठ** (*mūṭh*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *muṣṭi*, "fist"; CDIAL lists Marathi *mūṭh* (CDIAL 10221).
