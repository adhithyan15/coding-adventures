## You'll want to know: خرافة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C303-USTURA303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خرافة** — *khurāfa* — "a myth, a superstition".

> قال جدي إن القصة خرافة.
>
> *qāla jaddī inna al-qiṣṣa khurāfa.*
>
> My grandfather said the story was a myth.

Plural خرافات. Of a belief it means a superstition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خرافة** (*khurāfa*) comes from a name, as Lane records under the root *kh-r-f* (Lane): Khurāfa, a man of the tribe of ʿUdhra, told what he had seen among the jinn and was called a liar, and *khurāfāt* became tales deemed pretty but untrue.
