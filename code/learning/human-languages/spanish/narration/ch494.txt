Spanish, chapter 494: Por la mañana.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
despertarse — to wake up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to suggest, then the Spanish for the doubt.

You'll want to know: despertarse.
despertarse — "to wake up".
Me despierto a las siete. I wake up at seven.
The e breaks into ie under stress: me despierto.
