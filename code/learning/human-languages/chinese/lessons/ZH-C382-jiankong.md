## You'll want to know: jiānkòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C382-GUARD382-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiānkòng* — "security camera; CCTV".

> *yīnwèi yǒu jiānkòng suǒyǐ xiǎoqū lǐ hěn ānquán* — There are cameras, so the estate is safe.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiānkòng* is built from *jiān* 'to watch over' and *kòng* 'to pull', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaem* and *khuwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to look down on and inspect' and the second 'to draw' and 'to report'. The camera is a modern sense.
