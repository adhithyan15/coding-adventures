## You'll want to know: 亲手
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C513-AMBITION513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**亲手** — *qīnshǒu* — "with one's own hands".

> **我想亲手开一家饭店** *wǒ xiǎng qīnshǒu kāi yì jiā fàndiàn* — I want to open a restaurant with my own hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**亲手** (*qīnshǒu*) is built from 亲 'close' and 手 'a hand', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshin* and *syuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
