## You'll want to know: のうぎょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C489-FARM489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**のうぎょう** — *nōgyō* — "farming; agriculture".

> **そふは のうぎょうを しています。** — *sofu wa nōgyō o shiteimasu* — "My grandfather is a farmer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のうぎょう** (*nōgyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nō*, "agriculture" (Middle Chinese *nowng*) and *gyō*, "business" (*ngjaep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
