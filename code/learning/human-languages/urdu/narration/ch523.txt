Urdu, chapter 523: At the Stadium.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اسٹیڈیم (isṭeḍiyam) — a stadium.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to make (someone) run, then the Urdu for a trophy.

You'll want to know: اسٹیڈیم.
اسٹیڈیم — isṭeḍiyam — "a stadium".
اسٹیڈیم شائقین سے بھرا تھا۔ isṭeḍiyam shāiqīn se bharā thā. The stadium was full of fans.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اسٹیڈیم (isṭeḍiyam) is borrowed from English stadium.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پھاندنا (phāṅdnā) — to leap over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a score, then the Urdu for a cheer.

You'll want to know: پھاندنا.
پھاندنا — phāṅdnā — "to leap over".
گھوڑے نے دیوار پھاندی۔ ghoṛe ne dīvār phāṅdī. The horse leapt over the wall.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دیوار پھاندی, us ne dīvār phāṅdī.

The word, taken apart.
پھاندنا (phāṅdnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phā̃dnā, "to leap", under Sanskrit spandatē, "trembles, kicks" (CDIAL 13806).
