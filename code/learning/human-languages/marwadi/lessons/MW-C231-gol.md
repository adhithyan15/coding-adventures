## You'll want to know: गोळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C231-SHAPE231-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गोळ** — *goḷ* — "round".

> आज चांद गोळ है।
>
> *āj chā̃d goḷ hai.*
>
> Today the moon is round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोळ** (*goḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gola*, "a ball"; CDIAL lists Hindi *gol*, "anything round", and Gujarati *goḷ*, "round". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4321).
