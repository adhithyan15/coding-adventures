## You'll want to know: दर्पः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C458-DARPA458-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्पः** — *darpaḥ* — "haughtiness".

> दर्पेण सः मित्राणि त्यजति।
>
> *darpeṇa saḥ mitrāṇi tyajati.*
>
> In his haughtiness he drops his friends.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्पः** (*darpaḥ*) comes from the root √dṛp, "to be proud, to be wild"; MW gives "pride, arrogance, haughtiness, conceit" (MW).
