## You'll want to know: रूमाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C190-DHOTI190-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रूमाल** — *rūmāl* — "a handkerchief".

> म्हारो रूमाल जेब मांय है।
>
> *mhāro rūmāl jeb mā̃y hai.*
>
> My handkerchief is in my pocket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रूमाल** (*rūmāl*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *rūmāl*, "a handkerchief".
