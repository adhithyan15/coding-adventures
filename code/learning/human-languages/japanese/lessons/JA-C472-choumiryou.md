## You'll want to know: ちょうみりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C472-INGR472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうみりょう** — *chōmiryō* — "a seasoning".

> **しおと しょうゆは ちょうみりょうです。** — *shio to shōyu wa chōmiryō desu* — "Salt and soy sauce are seasonings."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうみりょう** (*chōmiryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "prepare" (Middle Chinese *dew*), *mi*, "taste" (*mj+jH*), and *ryō*, "materials" (*lew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
