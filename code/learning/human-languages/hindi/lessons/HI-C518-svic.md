## You'll want to know: स्विच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C518-TAAR518-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्विच** — *svic* — "a switch".

> स्विच दरवाज़े के पास है।
>
> *Svic darvāze ke pās hai.*
>
> The switch is by the door.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्विच** (*svic*) is a loan from English *switch*.
