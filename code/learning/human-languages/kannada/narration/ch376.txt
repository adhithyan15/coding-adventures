Kannada, chapter 376: Proof and Possibility.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪುರಾವೆ (purāve) — proof; evidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for chalk, then the Kannada for a ruler.

You'll want to know: ಪುರಾವೆ.
ಪುರಾವೆ — purāve — "proof; evidence".
ಅವನ ಮಾತಿಗೆ ಯಾವುದೇ ಪುರಾವೆ ಇರಲಿಲ್ಲ. avana mātige yāvudē purāve iralilla. There was no proof for what he said.
With ಇದೆ and ಇಲ್ಲ: ಪುರಾವೆ (purāve) ಇದೆ, there is proof.

The word, taken apart.
ಪುರಾವೆ (purāve) has no entry in DEDR or CDIAL, so its origin is not settled. Marathi uses a close form, purāvā, for evidence.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು (sābītupaḍisu) — to prove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ruler, then the Kannada for proof.

You'll want to know: ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು.
ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು — sābītupaḍisu — "to prove".
ನನ್ನ ಮಾತು ನಿಜ ಅಂತ ನಾನು ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸಿದೆ. nanna mātu nija anta nānu sābītupaḍiside. I proved that my words were true.
Past (I …): ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, sābītupaḍiside.
ಸಾಬೀತು means proved; -ಪಡಿಸು makes a verb of it. ಸಾಬೀತಾಯಿತು, it was proved.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಬೀತುಪಡಿಸು (sābītupaḍisu) is a loan from Hindustani sābit, "proved, established", which comes from Arabic thābit, "firm", with the Kannada ending -paḍisu that makes a verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ (sādhyate) — a possibility; a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for proof, then the Kannada for to prove.

You'll want to know: ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ.
ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ — sādhyate — "a possibility; a chance".
ನಾಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬರುವ ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ ಇದೆ. nāḷe maḷe baruva sādhyate ide. There is a chance of rain tomorrow.
A verb in -ುವ comes before it: ಬರುವ ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ (sādhyate), the chance of coming.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ (sādhyate) is borrowed from Sanskrit sādhyatā, "feasibility" (Monier-Williams), built on sādhya, "that can be done", with the ending -tā, "-ness".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಹತ್ವ (mahatva) — importance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to prove, then the Kannada for a possibility.

You'll want to know: ಮಹತ್ವ.
ಮಹತ್ವ — mahatva — "importance".
ಈ ಸಭೆಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಮಹತ್ವ ಇದೆ. ī sabhege tumbā mahatva ide. This meeting is very important.
Before a noun it takes -ದ: ಮಹತ್ವದ ವಿಷಯ, an important matter.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಹತ್ವ (mahatva) is borrowed from Sanskrit mahattva, "greatness" (CDIAL 9933), from mahat, "great", with -tva, "-ness".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಆಧರಿಸು (ādharisu) — to base on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a possibility, then the Kannada for importance.

You'll want to know: ಆಧರಿಸು.
ಆಧರಿಸು — ādharisu — "to base on".
ಈ ಸಿನಿಮಾ ಒಂದು ನಿಜ ಘಟನೆಯನ್ನು ಆಧರಿಸಿದೆ. ī sinimā ondu nija ghaṭaneyannu ādhariside. This film is based on a true incident.
Past (I …): ಆಧರಿಸಿದೆ, ādhariside.
What it rests on takes -ಅನ್ನು: ಘಟನೆಯನ್ನು ಆಧರಿಸಿ, based on an incident.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಧರಿಸು (ādharisu) is built on Sanskrit ādhāra, "a support, a basis" (CDIAL 1165), with the Kannada ending -isu that turns a Sanskrit noun into a verb.
