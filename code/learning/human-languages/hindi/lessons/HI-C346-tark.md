## You'll want to know: तर्क
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C346-BAHAS346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्क** — *tark* — "a line of reasoning, logic".

> तुम्हारे तर्क में एक कमज़ोरी है।
>
> *tumhāre tark meṁ ek kamzorī hai.*
>
> There is a weakness in your reasoning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्क** (*tark*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *tarka*, "reasoning, inquiry" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
