## You'll want to know: lo scontro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C299-SCONTRO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scontro** — "the crash, the collision; the clash".

> Sull'autostrada c'è stato uno scontro tra due camion.
>
> *There was a crash between two lorries on the motorway.*

Also a clash of ideas or of teams.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scontro* is built on *scontrare*, a form of *incontrare* "to meet", from Latin *in contrā* "against" (EtymDB).
