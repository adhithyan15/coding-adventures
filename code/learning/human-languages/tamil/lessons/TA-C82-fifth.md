## You'll want to know: ஐந்தாவது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C82-ORDINAL-08]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C82-ORDINAL-04] -->

**ஐந்தாவது** (*aindāvadu*) — "**fifth**". **ஐந்து** (aindu) takes **-ஆவது**,
and nothing else changes.

That is the first five. Count the work: **four of them were built** from
numbers you already count with, and the fifth — **முதல்** — was a word you had
been saying since you learned *mutalil*, without knowing it was a number word.

Nothing in this chapter was a word you had to learn.

**இடது பக்கம் முதல் பாதை** is now sayable in its useful half: **முதல் பாதை**,
the first road, with **இடது** for which side of the street.
