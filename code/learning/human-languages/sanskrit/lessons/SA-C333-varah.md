## You'll want to know: वरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C333-SHAPA333-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वरः** — *varaḥ* — "a boon".

> देवः ऋषये वरं ददाति।
>
> *devaḥ ṛṣaye varaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The god gives the seer a boon.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वरः** (*varaḥ*) comes from the root √vṛ, "to choose", like the *vara* who is chosen as a bridegroom; MW gives *vara* as choosing, a wish, and a boon or gift (MW).
