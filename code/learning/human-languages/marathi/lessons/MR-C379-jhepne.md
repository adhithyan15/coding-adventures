## You'll want to know: झेपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C379-MARYADA379-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झेपणे** — *jhepṇe* — "to be manageable (for someone)".

> एवढं काम मला एकट्याला झेपणार नाही.
>
> *evḍhaṁ kām malā ekaṭyālā jhepṇār nāhī.*
>
> I won't be able to manage so much work alone.

Past (to him …): त्याला झेपलं, *tyālā jheplaṁ*.

Dative: मला झेपतं, I can cope; mostly with नाही.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झेपणे** (*jhepṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
