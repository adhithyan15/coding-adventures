## You'll want to know: ਅਰਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C248-GHR248-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਰਲ** — *aral* — "a door bolt".

> ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ੇ ਦੀ ਅਰਲ ਬੰਦ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *rāt nū̃ darvāze dī aral band hundī hai.*
>
> At night the door bolt is shut.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਰਲ** (*aral*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ararī*, "leaf of a door"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *aral*, "wooden bolt of a door" (CDIAL 605).
