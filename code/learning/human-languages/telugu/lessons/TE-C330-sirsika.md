## You'll want to know: శీర్షిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C330-PATRIKA330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**శీర్షిక** — *śīrṣika* — "a headline; a title".

> పత్రికలో పెద్ద శీర్షిక వచ్చింది.
>
> *patrikalō pedda śīrṣika vaccindi.*
>
> A big headline appeared in the paper.

The line at the head of a page or a story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శీర్షిక** (*śīrṣika*) is a learned word built on Sanskrit *śīrṣa*, "the head" (CDIAL 12497): the words at the head of a story.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
