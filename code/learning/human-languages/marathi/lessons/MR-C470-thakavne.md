## You'll want to know: ठकवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C470-LABAD470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ठकवणे** — *ṭhakavṇe* — "to dupe; to swindle".

> एका माणसाने आजींना ठकवलं आणि पैसे नेले.
>
> *ekā māṇsāne ājīnnā ṭhakavlaṁ āṇi paise nele.*
>
> A man swindled Grandmother and took her money.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मला ठकवलं, *tyāne malā ṭhakavlaṁ*.

The one cheated takes -ला; ठक, a swindler.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठकवणे** (*ṭhakavṇe*) is built on the verb that CDIAL lists as Marathi *ṭhakṇẽ*, "to be deceived", under the reconstructed *\*ṭhagg* (CDIAL 5489), with the causative ending *-avṇe*: to make someone be deceived.
