## You'll want to know: ちょくせつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C347-HOUMON347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょくせつ** — *chokusetsu* — "directly; in person".

> **もし じかんが あったら、ちょくせつ はなして ください。** — *moshi jikan ga attara, chokusetsu hanashite kudasai* — "If you have time, please speak to them in person."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょくせつ** (*chokusetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *choku*, "straightaway" (Middle Chinese *drik*) and *setsu*, "contact" (*tsjep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
