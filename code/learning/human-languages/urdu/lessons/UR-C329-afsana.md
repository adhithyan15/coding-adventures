## You'll want to know: افسانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C329-AFSANA329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**افسانہ** — *afsānā* — "a short story".

> میں نے کل ایک افسانہ پڑھا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne kal ek afsānā paṛhā.*
>
> I read a short story yesterday.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افسانہ** (*afsānā*) is usually traced to Persian *afsāna*, "a tale, a fable"; in Urdu writing it is the short story.
