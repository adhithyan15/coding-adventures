# پھوٹنا (phūṭnā) — to burst; to break out

## You'll want to know: پھوٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C304-FASAD304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پھوٹنا** — *phūṭnā* — "to burst; to break out".

> رات کو اچانک فساد پھوٹ پڑا۔
>
> *rāt ko achānak fasād phūṭ paṛā.*
>
> Suddenly, in the night, a riot broke out.

Past (it …): وہ بوتل پھوٹی, *voh botal phūṭī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھوٹنا** (*phūṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phūṭnā*, "to split, to burst", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13845). Trouble **پھوٹ پڑا** when it broke out all at once.
