Kannada, chapter 360: The Hunt in the Forest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೇಟೆಯಾಡು (bēṭeyāḍu) — to hunt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to parade, then the Kannada for a flag.

You'll want to know: ಬೇಟೆಯಾಡು.
ಬೇಟೆಯಾಡು — bēṭeyāḍu — "to hunt".
ಹಳೆಯ ಕಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ರಾಜರು ಕಾಡಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಬೇಟೆಯಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. haḷeya kāladalli rājaru kāḍinalli bēṭeyāḍuttiddaru. In the old days kings used to hunt in the forest.
Past (I …): ಬೇಟೆಯಾಡಿದೆ, bēṭeyāḍide.
ಬೇಟೆ, a hunt, and ಆಡು, to play: to play the hunt. A hunter is a ಬೇಟೆಗಾರ.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೇಟೆಯಾಡು (bēṭeyāḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5527 lists Kannada bēṭe, "hunting, the chase", and bēṭey āḍu, "to hunt".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಅಲೆದಾಡು (aledāḍu) — to wander; to roam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a flag, then the Kannada for to hunt.

You'll want to know: ಅಲೆದಾಡು.
ಅಲೆದಾಡು — aledāḍu — "to wander; to roam".
ಕಳೆದುಹೋದ ಹುಡುಗ ಇಡೀ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಕಾಡಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅಲೆದಾಡಿದನು. kaḷeduhōda huḍuga iḍī rātri kāḍinalli aledāḍidanu. The lost boy wandered in the forest all night.
Past (I …): ಅಲೆದಾಡಿದೆ, aledāḍide.
To move about with no fixed path. ಸುತ್ತಾಡು is to stroll around for pleasure.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಲೆದಾಡು (aledāḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: ale, "to move about, to wander" (DEDR 240), and āḍu, "to move, to act" (DEDR 347).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು (hoñcu hāku) — to lie in wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to hunt, then the Kannada for to wander.

You'll want to know: ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು.
ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು — hoñcu hāku — "to lie in wait".
ಹುಲಿ ಪೊದೆಯ ಹಿಂದೆ ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕಿತ್ತು. huli podeya hinde hoñcu hākittu. The tiger was lying in wait behind the bush.
Past (I …): ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕಿದೆ, hoñcu hākide.
Said of animals and of people planning an ambush.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು (hoñcu hāku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4596 lists Kannada hoñcu, "to wait for expectantly, to lurk, to lie in wait", here with ಹಾಕು, "to put".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನುಗ್ಗು (nuggu) — to barge in; to rush in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to wander, then the Kannada for to lie in wait.

You'll want to know: ನುಗ್ಗು.
ನುಗ್ಗು — nuggu — "to barge in; to rush in".
ಆನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹೊಲಕ್ಕೆ ನುಗ್ಗಿತು. āne rātri holakke nuggitu. An elephant barged into the field at night.
Past (I …): ನುಗ್ಗಿದೆ, nuggide.
Where one rushes takes -ಗೆ or -ಒಳಗೆ: ಮನೆಯೊಳಗೆ ನುಗ್ಗು (nuggu).

The word, taken apart.
ನುಗ್ಗು (nuggu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3714 lists Kannada nuggu beside Tamil words meaning "to creep through, to penetrate, to get into".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮುರಿ (muri) — to snap; to break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to lie in wait, then the Kannada for to barge in.

You'll want to know: ಮುರಿ.
ಮುರಿ — muri — "to snap; to break".
ಆನೆ ಮರದ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಕೊಂಬೆಯನ್ನು ಮುರಿಯಿತು. āne marada doḍḍa kombeyannu muriyitu. The elephant broke a big branch of the tree.
Past (I …): ಮುರಿದೆ, muride.
Sticks, bones and promises break: ಮಾತು ಮುರಿ (muri), to break one's word. ಮುರಿತ is a fracture.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುರಿ (muri) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5008 lists Kannada muṟi, "to break, to break off, to break down".
