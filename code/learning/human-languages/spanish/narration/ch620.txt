Spanish, chapter 620: Buscar soluciones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la solución — the solution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the prospect, then the Spanish for to predict.

You'll want to know: la solución.
la solución — "the solution".
Tenemos que buscar una solución. We have to look for a solution.
No tiene solución — it cannot be fixed.

The word, taken apart.
solución is a learned borrowing from Latin solutio 'a loosening, a solving', built on solvere 'to loosen' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: plantear.
plantear — "to raise, to bring up; to pose".
Quiero plantear una pregunta. I want to raise a question.
Plantear un problema — to raise a problem. Plantearse — to think about doing.

The word, taken apart.
plantear is built on planta 'sole of the foot; ground plan', a learned borrowing from Latin planta (EtymDB): to set something down so all can see it.
