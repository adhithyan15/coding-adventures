## You'll want to know: નર્સ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C231-MEDICINE231-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નર્સ** — *nars* — "nurse".

> **શું નર્સ દવાખાનામાં છે?** — *śũ nars davākhānāmā̃ che?* — "Is the nurse at the clinic?"

Mostly feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નર્સ** (*nars*) is a loan from English *nurse*.
