## You'll want to know: लय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C532-GEET532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लय** — *lay* — "tempo; rhythm".

> गाने की लय बहुत तेज़ है।
>
> *Gāne kī lay bahut tez hai.*
>
> The song's tempo is very fast.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लय** (*lay*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *laya*, "(in music) time" (Monier-Williams).
