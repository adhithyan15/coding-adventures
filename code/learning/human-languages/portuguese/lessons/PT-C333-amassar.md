## You'll want to know: amassar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C333-BOLO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**amassar** — "to knead; to crush, to dent".

> O padeiro amassa o pão de madrugada.
>
> *The baker kneads the bread before dawn.*

Also to dent a car: *amassar o carro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amassar* is built from *a-* and *massa* "dough", from Latin *massa* "a lump, a mass" (Lewis & Short).
