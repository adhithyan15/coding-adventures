Hindi, chapter 390: Setting a Goal.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
लक्ष्य (lakṣya) — a goal, a target.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for normal, then the Hindi for to brighten.

You'll want to know: लक्ष्य.
लक्ष्य — lakṣya — "a goal, a target".
अगर तुम्हारा लक्ष्य साफ़ होगा तो रास्ता आसान होगा। agar tumhārā lakṣya sāf hogā to rāstā āsān hogā. If your goal is clear, the way will be easy.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
लक्ष्य (lakṣya) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit lakṣya, "what is kept in view; a mark" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 4 of 6.
क़दम (qadam) — a step.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wish, then the Hindi for a resolve.

You'll want to know: क़दम.
क़दम — qadam — "a step".
अगर हम हर दिन एक क़दम आगे बढ़ेंगे तो एक दिन ज़रूर पहुँचेंगे। agar ham har din ek qadam āge baṛheṅge to ek din zarūr pahuñceṅge. If we move one step forward every day, we'll get there one day.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
क़दम (qadam) is usually traced to Arabic qadam, "a foot, a step", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a resolve, then the Hindi for a step.

You'll want to know: कोशिश करना.
कोशिश करना — kośiś karnā — "to try, to make an attempt".
अगर तुम कोशिश करोगे तो ज़रूर कामयाब होगे। agar tum kośiś karoge to zarūr kāmyāb hoge. If you try, you'll certainly succeed.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बहुत कोशिश की, usne bahut kośiś kī.

The word, taken apart.
कोशिश करना (kośiś karnā) joins कोशिश to करना, "to do". कोशिश is usually traced to Persian kōshish, "an effort".
