## You'll want to know: anfrāctus, anfrāctūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C424-COMPITUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**anfrāctus, anfrāctūs** — "a winding, a twisting stretch of road".

> Via per montēs multīs anfrāctibus it.
>
> *The road goes through the mountains with many windings.*

Fourth declension, masculine: *anfrāctus, anfrāctūs m.* Also a long, roundabout way of speaking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anfrāctus* is built on *am(b)-* 'around' and *frangō* 'to break' (Lewis & Short; EtymDB lists *frangō*, from PIE *bʰreg-* 'to break'): a road that keeps breaking round in turns.
