## You'll want to know: सञ्चयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C432-VYAYA432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सञ्चयः** — *sañcayaḥ* — "savings; a store".

> मम सञ्चयः वित्तकोषे अस्ति।
>
> *mama sañcayaḥ vittakoṣe asti.*
>
> My savings are in the bank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सञ्चयः** (*sañcayaḥ*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √ci, "to gather"; MW gives *saṃcaya* as collection, accumulation, a store (MW).
