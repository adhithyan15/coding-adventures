## You'll want to know: jìyì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C344-MEMORY344-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jìyì* — "memory".

> *nà shì wǒ zuì hǎo de jìyì* — That's my best memory.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìyì* is built from *jì* and *yì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kiH* and *'ik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to record' and the second 'to think of'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
