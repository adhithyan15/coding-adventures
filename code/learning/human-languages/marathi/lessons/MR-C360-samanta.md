## You'll want to know: समानता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C360-SAMANTA360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**समानता** — *samāntā* — "equality".

> स्त्री आणि पुरुषांमध्ये समानता असली पाहिजे.
>
> *strī āṇi puruṣāmmadhye samāntā aslī pāhije.*
>
> There should be equality between women and men.

Feminine.

समान, equal, + -ता, -ness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समानता** (*samāntā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *samānatā*, "equality, sameness" (Monier-Williams).
