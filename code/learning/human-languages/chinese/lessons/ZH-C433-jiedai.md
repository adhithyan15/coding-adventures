## You'll want to know: jiēdài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C433-OVERSEAS433-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiēdài* — "to receive visitors; to take care of guests".

> *zhè jiā jiǔdiàn jiēdài le hěn duō wàiguó yóukè* — This hotel has had lots of foreign visitors.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiēdài* is built from *jiē* 'to meet' and *dài* 'to wait on', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsjep* and *dojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to join' and the second 'to wait for'.
