# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, UR-LEX-C266-DAURAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was reading" and "during the meal". (*maiṅ paṛh rahā thā*, *khāne ke daurān*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C267-BAD-01, UR-SCRIPT-C267-AIN-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-02, UR-SKILL-C267-READ-STORY-01, UR-LEX-C102-LOC102-04, UR-LEX-CHAI] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First I had a bath. Then I ate.]
- [YOU SAY: After that I went to the office.]
- [YOU SAY: I finished the work and came home.]
- [YOU SAY: In the end I drank tea and slept.]
- [YOU SAY: Sara's trip, in four lines: Lahore, the train, her friend, the market.]
- [YOU WRITE: **بعد** once, and point at ع inside it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C267-BAD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "After the meal"? (**کھانے کے بعد**.) "Having eaten"? (**کھا کر**.) Now the other way round: in **اس نے کھا کر چائے پی**, which verb decides **نے**? (**پی**, the last.) And the four words that put a story in order? (**پہلے**, **پھر**, **اس کے بعد**, **آخر میں**.)
