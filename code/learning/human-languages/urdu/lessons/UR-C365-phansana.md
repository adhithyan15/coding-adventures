## You'll want to know: پھنسانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C365-HALAT365-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پھنسانا** — *phansānā* — "to land in trouble; to trap".

> اگر تم نے جھوٹ کہا تو مجھے بھی پھنساؤ گے۔
>
> *agar tum ne jhūṭ kahā to mujhe bhī phansāo ge.*
>
> If you tell a lie, you will drag me into trouble too.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجھے پھنسایا, *us ne mujhe phansāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھنسانا** (*phansānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phãsānā*, "to ensnare", under Sanskrit *spāśáyati*, "causes to be bound" (CDIAL 13814).
