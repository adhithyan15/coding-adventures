# તિરસ્કાર (tiraskār) — contempt

## You'll want to know: તિરસ્કાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C457-NAFRAT457-03]; assesses=[] -->

**તિરસ્કાર** — *tiraskār* — "contempt".

> તેની આંખમાં મારા માટે તિરસ્કાર હતો.
>
> *tenī ā̃khmā̃ mārā māṭe tiraskār hato.*
>
> There was contempt for me in his eyes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તિરસ્કાર** (*tiraskār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *tiraskāra*, which Monier-Williams gives as "placing aside" and "abuse, censure".
