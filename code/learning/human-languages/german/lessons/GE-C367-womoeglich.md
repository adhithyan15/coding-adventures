## You'll want to know: womöglich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C367-EVENTUELL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**womöglich** — "perhaps even, quite possibly".

> Der Himmel ist ganz dunkel, womöglich gibt es heute noch ein Gewitter.
>
> *The sky is very dark; there may even be a thunderstorm today.*

Often with a hint of worry: something unwelcome might happen. The verb comes right after it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*womöglich* joins *wo* "where" and *möglich* "possible", of the family of *mögen* and Proto-Germanic *maganą* "to be able" (EtymDB): "if at all possible", then "perhaps even".
