## You'll want to know: बिकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C213-PRICE213-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बिकणो** — *bikṇo* — "to be sold; to sell".

> ये घी जल्दी बिकै है।
>
> *ye ghī jaldī bikai hai.*
>
> This ghee sells quickly.

Past (it …): वो घी बिक्यो, *vo ghī bikyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिकणो** (*bikṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vikrīyate*, "is sold". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *biknā*, "to be sold" (CDIAL 11642).
