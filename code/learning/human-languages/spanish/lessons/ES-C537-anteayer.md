## You'll want to know: anteayer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C537-ENTONCES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**anteayer** — "the day before yesterday".

> Anteayer hubo un accidente en mi calle.
>
> *The day before yesterday there was an accident on my street.*

In Latin America people also say *antier*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anteayer* is built on *ante* 'before' and *ayer* 'yesterday', which comes from Latin *herī* 'yesterday' (Lewis & Short).
