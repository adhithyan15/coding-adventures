Gujarati, chapter 75: Ahead, Behind, In and Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
આગળ (āgaḷ) — ahead, in front.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for up, then the Gujarati for down.

You'll want to know: આગળ.
આગળ (āgaḷ) — "ahead, in front".

The word, taken apart.
આગળ (āgaḷ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit agra, "top, front", among the forms built with an -l- suffix (CDIAL 68).

What you've built.
One more word for ahead, behind, in and out.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for up, then the Gujarati for down, then say āgaḷ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does આગળ (āgaḷ) mean? ("Ahead, in front".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
બહાર (bahār) — outside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for behind, then the Gujarati for inside.

You'll want to know: બહાર.
બહાર (bahār) — "outside".

The word, taken apart.
બહાર (bahār) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bāhira, "external", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9226). It is built on Sanskrit bahis, "outside".
