## You'll want to know: tramandare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C309-GENERAZIONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tramandare** — "to hand down (across generations)".

> La nonna ci ha tramandato questa ricetta.
>
> *Grandmother handed this recipe down to us.*

For stories, recipes and customs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tramandare* is usually traced to a Latin *trānsmandāre*, from *trāns* "across" and *mandāre* "to entrust" (Lewis & Short gives *mandāre*): to send across the years.
