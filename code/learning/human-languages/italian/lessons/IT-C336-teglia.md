## You'll want to know: la teglia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C336-FORNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la teglia** — "the baking tray, the baking tin".

> Metti le patate nella teglia.
>
> *Put the potatoes in the baking tray.*

*La pizza in teglia* — pizza baked in a tray.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*teglia* is inherited from Latin *tēgula* "a tile" (EtymDB), from *tegere* "to cover".
