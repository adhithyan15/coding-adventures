## You'll want to know: la carrera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C595-CARRERA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la carrera** — "the career; the degree; the race".

> Tiene una larga carrera en la empresa.
>
> *She has a long career in the company.*

In Spain *hacer una carrera* is to study for a degree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carrera* is usually traced to Late Latin *(via) carraria* 'cart road', built on *carrus* 'a wagon' (Lewis & Short); a road became a race and then a career.
