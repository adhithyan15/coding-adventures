# ఉసిగొల్పు (usigolpu) — to incite; to egg on

## You'll want to know: ఉసిగొల్పు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C363-TIRUGUBATU363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉసిగొల్పు** — *usigolpu* — "to incite; to egg on".

> ఎవరో ప్రజలను గొడవకు ఉసిగొల్పారు.
>
> *evarō prajalanu goḍavaku usigolpāru.*
>
> Someone incited the people to a quarrel.

Past (I …): ఉసిగొల్పాను, *usigolpānu*.

Whom takes -ను, what to takes -కు: గొడవకు ఉసిగొల్పు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉసిగొల్పు** (*usigolpu*) is a Telugu verb for setting someone on; its origin is not settled.
