# اختتم (ikhtatama) — to conclude, to bring to a close

## You'll want to know: اختتم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C357-KHITAB357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اختتم** — *ikhtatama* — "to conclude, to bring to a close".

> اختتمت المدرسة السنة بحفل كبير.
>
> *ikhtatamat al-madrasa as-sana bi-ḥafl kabīr.*
>
> The school ended the year with a big party.

Present: يختتم, *yakhtatim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختتم** (*ikhtatama*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-t-m* (Lane), "to seal; to finish", the root of the taught *khātam*, a ring that seals. It is Form VIII, "to bring to an end".
