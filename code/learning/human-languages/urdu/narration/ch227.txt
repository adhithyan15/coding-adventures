Lesson 1 of 5.
جانور (jānvar) — an animal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a pumpkin, then the Urdu for spinach.

You'll want to know: جانور.
جانور — jānvar — "an animal".

The word, taken apart.
جانور (jānvar) is a loan, usually traced to Persian jānvar, "animal, living creature". It is built on jān, "life".

Lesson 2 of 5.
بھینس (bhaiṅs) — a buffalo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for spinach, then the Urdu for an animal.

You'll want to know: بھینس.
بھینس — bhaiṅs — "a buffalo".

The word, taken apart.
بھینس (bhaiṅs) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mahiṣī, "buffalo cow" (CDIAL 9964). It is the feminine of mahiṣa, "great; buffalo".

The word, taken apart.
بھیڑیا (bheṛiyā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bheḍriya, "sheep-killer", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9608b). It is built on bheḍra, "ram".
