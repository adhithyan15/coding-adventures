## You'll want to know: સ્વસ્થ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C486-AAROGYA486-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વસ્થ** — *svasth* — "in good health; well".

> ઓપરેશન પછી દાદા હવે સ્વસ્થ છે.
>
> *opreśan pachī dādā have svasth che.*
>
> Since the operation my grandfather is well again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વસ્થ** (*svasth*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *svastha*, "being in oneself, self-abiding", as Monier-Williams records it: a body at ease in itself.
