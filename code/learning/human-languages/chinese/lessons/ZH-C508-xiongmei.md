# xiōngmèi — elder brother and younger sister

## You'll want to know: xiōngmèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C508-SIBLINGS508-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xiōngmèi* — "elder brother and younger sister".

> *tāmen xiōngmèi dōu shì dàxuésheng* — The brother and sister are both at university.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiōngmèi* is built from *xiōng* 'an elder brother' and *mèi* 'a younger sister', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xjwaeng* and *mwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how it was.
