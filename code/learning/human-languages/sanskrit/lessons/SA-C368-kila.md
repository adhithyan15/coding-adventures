## You'll want to know: किल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C368-SHANKA368-05]; assesses=[] -->

**किल** — *kila* — "so they say; indeed".

> सः किल रुग्णः; यदि एवम्, तर्हि अहं गमिष्यामि।
>
> *saḥ kila rugṇaḥ; yadi evam, tarhi ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi.*
>
> He is ill, so they say; if so, I will go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किल** (*kila*) MW gives *kila* as a particle of emphasis, "indeed, verily", and "so said, so reported"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
