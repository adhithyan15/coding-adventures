Kannada, chapter 377: Logic and Objections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತರ್ಕ (tarka) — logic; reasoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for importance, then the Kannada for to base on.

You'll want to know: ತರ್ಕ.
ತರ್ಕ — tarka — "logic; reasoning".
ಅವನ ತರ್ಕ ನನಗೆ ಸರಿ ಅನಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ. avana tarka nanage sari anisalilla. His reasoning did not seem right to me.
ತರ್ಕಬದ್ಧ means reasoned, logical.

The word, taken apart.
ತರ್ಕ (tarka) is borrowed from Sanskrit tarka, "conjecture, reasoning" (CDIAL 5714).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸು.
ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸು — samarthisu — "to justify; to defend".
ಅವಳು ತನ್ನ ನಿರ್ಧಾರವನ್ನು ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸಿದಳು. avaḷu tanna nirdhāravannu samarthisidaḷu. She defended her decision.
Past (I …): ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸಿದೆ, samarthiside.
What you defend takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸಿಕೊ, to defend oneself.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸು (samarthisu) is built on Sanskrit samartha, "fit, able, forcible" (CDIAL 13189), with the Kannada verb ending -isu: to show that something is fit.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ವಿವಾದ (vivāda) — a controversy; a dispute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for logic, then the Kannada for to justify.

You'll want to know: ವಿವಾದ.
ವಿವಾದ — vivāda — "a controversy; a dispute".
ಹೊಸ ನಿಯಮದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ವಿವಾದ ಆಯಿತು. hosa niyamada bagge doḍḍa vivāda āyitu. There was a big controversy about the new rule.
ವಿವಾದಾತ್ಮಕ, controversial.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿವಾದ (vivāda) is borrowed from Sanskrit vivāda, "a dispute, a quarrel, a lawsuit" (Monier-Williams), from vi- "apart" and vad "to speak".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು (ākṣēpisu) — to object.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to justify, then the Kannada for a controversy.

You'll want to know: ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು.
ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು — ākṣēpisu — "to object".
ಊರಿನ ಜನರು ಹೊಸ ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆಗೆ ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸಿದರು. ūrina janaru hosa kārkhānege ākṣēpisidaru. The villagers objected to the new factory.
Past (I …): ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸಿದೆ, ākṣēpiside.
What you object to takes -ಗೆ. ಆಕ್ಷೇಪ, an objection.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಿಸು (ākṣēpisu) is built on Sanskrit ākṣepa, which among other senses means "an objection" and "a reproach" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.
