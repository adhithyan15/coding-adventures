## You'll want to know: anciano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C555-VEJEZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anciano** — "elderly; the elderly person".

> Un anciano me ayudó a encontrar la calle.
>
> *An elderly man helped me find the street.*

More respectful than *viejo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anciano* comes from spoken Latin *\*anteānus* 'one from before' (EtymDB), built on *ante* 'before'.
