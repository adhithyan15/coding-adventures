## You'll want to know: ревность
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C469-JEALOUSY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ревность** (*révnost*) — "jealousy".

> Из-за ревности они часто ссорятся.
>
> *Iz-za révnosti oní chásto ssóryatsya.*
>
> They often quarrel because of jealousy.

Feminine; no plural in everyday use.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ревность* is a noun in *-ость* on the stem *ревн-*, which it shares with *ревновать* and *ревнивый*; EtymDB links it to Church Slavonic *rĭvenie* 'strife, quarrel'. An older sense, 'zeal', is still met in bookish Russian.
