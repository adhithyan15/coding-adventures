## You'll want to know: منعش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C486-TAQALLUB486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منعش** — *munʿish* — "refreshing".

> كان الهواء في الصباح منعشا.
>
> *kāna al-hawāʾ fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ munʿishan.*
>
> The morning air was refreshing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منعش** (*munʿish*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ʿ-sh*, "to raise up, to revive" (Lane). It is the Form IV participle, "that revives".
