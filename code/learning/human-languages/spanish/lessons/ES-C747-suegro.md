## You'll want to know: el suegro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C747-POLITICA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el suegro** — "the father-in-law".

> Mi suegro cocina muy bien.
>
> *My father-in-law cooks very well.*

*Los suegros* — the parents-in-law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suegro* is usually traced to Latin *socer*, *socerum* "father-in-law" (Lewis & Short); the stressed *o* became *ue*.
