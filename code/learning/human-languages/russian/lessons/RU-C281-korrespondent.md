## You'll want to know: корреспондент
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C281-EFIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**корреспондент** (*korrespondént*) — "a correspondent".

> Корреспондент поехал на север.
>
> *Korrespondént poyékhal na séver.*
>
> The correspondent went north.

Also *журналист*. Feminine forms are colloquial, and *корреспондент* serves for a woman too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*корреспондент* is usually traced through German *Korrespondent* or French *correspondant* to Medieval Latin *correspondēre* "to answer together"; EtymDB lists no parent.
