## You'll want to know: lǚtú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C429-HOME429-01]; assesses=[] -->

*lǚtú* — "journey".

> *lǚtú hěn cháng wǒmen dōu hěn lèi* — It was a long journey, and we're all tired.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǚtú* is built from 旅 'to travel' and *tú* 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljoX* and *du* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a body of troops and the second 'a road'.
