## You'll want to know: ēdictum, ēdictī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C409-EDICTUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ēdictum, ēdictī** — "an edict, a proclamation".

> Praetor ēdictum prōposuit.
>
> *The praetor published his edict.*

*ēdictum, ēdictī* n., from *ēdīcō*, to speak out. Each praetor posted an edict saying how he would run the courts. English *edict*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ēdictum* is the participle of *ēdīcō* "to speak out, to proclaim" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *ē* and *dīcō* "to say". English *edict* comes from it.
