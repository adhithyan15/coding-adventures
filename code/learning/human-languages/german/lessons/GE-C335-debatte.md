## You'll want to know: die Debatte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C335-DISKUSSION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Debatte** — "the debate".

> Im Parlament gibt es heute eine Debatte über die Schulen.
>
> *Today there is a debate in parliament about schools.*

Plural: *die Debatten*. More formal and more public than *die Diskussion*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Debatte* was borrowed from French; EtymDB links it to *débattre*, Old French *debatre* "to fight, to contend". That verb is built on *battre* "to beat", from Latin *battuere* "to beat" (Lewis & Short).
