## You'll want to know: હથિયાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C321-TALVAR321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**હથિયાર** — *hathiyār* — "a weapon".

> દુશ્મને હથિયાર નીચે મૂક્યાં.
>
> *duśmane hathiyār nīce mūkyā̃.*
>
> The enemy laid down their weapons.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હથિયાર** (*hathiyār*) comes from *\*hastakāra*, a form CDIAL reconstructs on *hásta*, the hand, where it lists Gujarati *hathiyār*, "a tool" (CDIAL 14027): what the hand works with. In war it is a weapon.
