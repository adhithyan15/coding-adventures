## You'll want to know: доход
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C361-PRIBYL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**доход** (*dokhód*) — "income".

> У них небольшой доход.
>
> *U nikh nebolshóy dokhód.*
>
> They have a small income.

Masculine. *Доходы и расходы* — income and expenditure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**доход** is built from *до-* "up to" and *ход* "a going", from *ходить* "to go", Proto-Slavic *\*xoditi*: what comes up to you.
