Punjabi, chapter 116: Thread, Button, Nail, Bucket, Broom.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਧਾਗਾ (dhāgā) — a thread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for scissors, then the Punjabi for a needle.

You'll want to know: ਧਾਗਾ.
ਧਾਗਾ — dhāgā — "a thread".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dhāgā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dhāgā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dhāgā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for scissors, then the Punjabi for a needle, then say dhāgā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਧਾਗਾ (dhāgā) mean? ("A thread".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Owning and being able — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a bucket and a broom.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: this much, that much, like this, like that, other]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an expert, knowledge, to be able to, near, strength]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: power, a right, authority, a chance, property]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wealth, an owner, experience, a habit, a skill]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
This much? (ਇੰਨਾ, innā.) That much? (ਉੰਨਾ, unnā.) Like this? (ਇਵੇਂ, ivẽ.) Like that? (ਉਵੇਂ, uvẽ.) Other? (ਹੋਰ, hor.) An expert? (ਮਾਹਰ, māhar.) Knowledge? (ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ, jāṇkārī.) To be able to? (ਸਕਣਾ, sakṇā.) Near? (ਕੋਲ, kol.) Strength? (ਤਾਕਤ, tākat.) Power? (ਸ਼ਕਤੀ, shaktī.) A right? (ਹੱਕ, hakk.) Authority? (ਅਧਿਕਾਰ, adhikār.) A chance? (ਮੌਕਾ, maukā.) Property? (ਜਾਇਦਾਦ, jāidād.) Wealth? (ਦੌਲਤ, daulat.) An owner? (ਮਾਲਕ, mālak.) Experience? (ਤਜਰਬਾ, tajarbā.) A habit? (ਆਦਤ, ādat.) A skill? (ਹੁਨਰ, hunar.)

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
An art? (ਕਲਾ, kalā.) A tool? (ਸੰਦ, sand.) Ability? (ਲਿਆਕਤ, liākat.) To win? (ਜਿੱਤਣਾ, jittṇā.) To lose? (ਹਾਰਨਾ, hārnā.) An umbrella? (ਛਤਰੀ, chatrī.) A bowl? (ਕੌਲੀ, kaulī.) A glass? (ਗਲਾਸ, galās.) A cooking pot? (ਪਤੀਲਾ, patīlā.) A comb? (ਕੰਘੀ, kanghī.) Soap? (ਸਾਬਣ, sābaṇ.) A towel? (ਤੌਲੀਆ, tauliā.) A mattress? (ਗੱਦਾ, gaddā.) A bedsheet? (ਚਾਦਰ, cādar.) A blanket? (ਕੰਬਲ, kambal.) A quilt? (ਰਜਾਈ, rajāī.) A stove? (ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ, cullhā.) A vessel? (ਭਾਂਡਾ, bhā̃ḍā.) Scissors? (ਕੈਂਚੀ, kaĩcī.) A needle? (ਸੂਈ, sūī.) A thread? (ਧਾਗਾ, dhāgā.) A button? (ਬਟਨ, baṭan.) A nail? (ਕਿੱਲ, kill.) A bucket? (ਬਾਲਟੀ, bālṭī.) A broom? (ਝਾੜੂ, jhāṛū.)
