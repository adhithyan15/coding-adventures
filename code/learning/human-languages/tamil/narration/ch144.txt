Tamil, chapter 144: Tall, Hot, Cold, Heavy, Light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
உயரமான (uyaramāṉa) — tall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for long, then the Tamil for short.

You'll want to know: உயரமான.
உயரமான — uyaramāṉa — "tall".

The word, taken apart.
உயரமான (uyaramāṉa) is native Dravidian (DEDR 646). It is uyaram, "height", from uyar, "to rise", with -āṉa.

கனமான — kaṉamāṉa — "heavy".

The word, taken apart.
கனமான (kaṉamāṉa) is a loan from Sanskrit ghana, "compact, firm, dense" (CDIAL 4424). Kaṉam, "weight", takes -āṉa.

Lesson 5 of 5.
லேசான (lēcāṉa) — light (in weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for cold, then the Tamil for heavy.

You'll want to know: லேசான.
லேசான — lēcāṉa — "light (in weight)".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of லேசான (lēcāṉa) is uncertain. DEDR has no entry for lēcu, "light, slight", and its initial l- is not native to old Tamil, which points to a loan from a neighbouring language.
