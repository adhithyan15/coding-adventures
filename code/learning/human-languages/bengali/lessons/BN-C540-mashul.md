## You'll want to know: মাশুল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C540-MASHUL540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মাশুল** — *māshul* — "a fee; the price paid (for something)".

> পার্সেলটা ভারী হলে বেশি মাশুল লাগবে।
>
> *Pārselṭā bhārī hôle beshi māshul lāgbe.*
>
> If the parcel is heavy, the fee will be higher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মাশুল** (*māshul*) is usually traced to Arabic *maḥṣūl*, "revenue, produce", through Persian.
