## You'll want to know: کاپوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C422-KAPUT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کاپوت** (*kâput*) — "a bonnet (of a car); a hood".

> مکانیک کاپوت ماشین را باز کرد.
>
> *Mekânik kâput-e mâshin râ bâz kard.*
>
> The mechanic opened the bonnet of the car.

In American English, the hood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kâput* was borrowed from French *capote*, a hooded cloak (EtymDB): the engine wears a hood. EtymDB traces *capote* back to Latin *caput* 'head'.
