## You'll want to know: réngrán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C315-AGAIN315-05]; assesses=[] -->

*réngrán* — "as before; even now".

> *shí nián yǐhòu, tā réngrán zhù zài zhèr* — Ten years later, he still lives here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réngrán* is built from *réng* 'to follow on' and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nying* and *nyen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to follow', 'to repeat' and 'often'.
