## You'll want to know: બોળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C523-FARSAN523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**બોળવું** — *boḷvũ* — "to dunk (in a liquid)".

> મીરાએ ભજિયાં ચટણીમાં બોળ્યાં.
>
> *Mīrāe bhajiyā̃ caṭṇīmā̃ boḷyā̃.*
>
> Mira dipped the fritters in the chutney.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે રોટલી બોળી, *teṇe roṭlī boḷī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બોળવું** (*boḷvũ*) comes from *\*buḍyati*, "sinks", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to dip" (CDIAL 9272).
