## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-AATH-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-AABHAAR-01, MW-LEX-SAAT-01] -->

> *āṭh* — **eight**

It opens on the long *ā* of **आभार**, standing on its own with no consonant in
front of it. Of the ten numbers this slice teaches, eight is the only one that
does that.

The closing consonant is retroflex: the tongue curls back, as in **ठंडी**. Do
not close it at the teeth, or seven and eight begin to sound alike.
