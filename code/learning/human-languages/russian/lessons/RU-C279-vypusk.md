## You'll want to know: выпуск
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C279-IZVESTIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**выпуск** (*výpusk*) — "a bulletin, an issue, an edition".

> В вечернем выпуске новостей говорили о погоде.
>
> *V vechérnem výpuske novostéy govoríli o pogóde.*
>
> In the evening news bulletin they talked about the weather.

*Выпуск новостей* — a news bulletin; also an issue of a magazine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выпуск* is built on *выпускать* "to let out, to issue", from *пускать* "to let go", inherited from Proto-Slavic *pustiti* (EtymDB): what is let out to the public.
