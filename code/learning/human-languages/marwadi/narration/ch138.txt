Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 138: Ox, Calf, Deer, Fox, Jackal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बळद (baḷad) — an ox.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for dates, then the Marwadi for a coconut.

You'll want to know: बळद.
बळद — baḷad — "an ox".

The word, taken apart.
बळद (baḷad) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from balivarda, "ox, bull". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 9176); the match is through Gujarati baḷad and Hindi balad. CDIAL records a view that the Sanskrit word joins balin, "strong", with a non-Aryan word for "ox".

लूंकड़ी — lū̃kṛī — "a fox".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of लूंकड़ी (lū̃kṛī) is not settled. CDIAL lists Hindi lomṛī, "fox", under Sanskrit lomaṭaka, "fox" (CDIAL 11153), but no form with the lū̃k- of लूंकड़ी (lū̃kṛī).
