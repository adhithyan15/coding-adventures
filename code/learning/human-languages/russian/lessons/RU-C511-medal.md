## You'll want to know: медаль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C511-CHAMPIONS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**медаль** (*medál*) — "a medal".

> Спортсмен получил золотую медаль.
>
> *Sportsmén poluchíl zolotúyu medál.*
>
> The athlete won a gold medal.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *медаль*, glossed 'medal', without a source; it is usually traced through French *médaille* to Italian *medaglia*.
