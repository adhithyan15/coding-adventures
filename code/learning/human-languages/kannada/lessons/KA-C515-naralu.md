# ನರಳು (naraḷu) — to groan; to moan

## You'll want to know: ನರಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C515-KURUDA515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನರಳು** — *naraḷu* — "to groan; to moan".

> ನೋವಿನಿಂದ ರೋಗಿ ರಾತ್ರಿಯೆಲ್ಲ ನರಳಿದನು.
>
> *nōvininda rōgi rātriyella naraḷidanu.*
>
> The patient groaned with pain all night.

Past (I …): ನರಳಿದೆ, *naraḷide*.

A low sound of pain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನರಳು** (*naraḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2904 lists Kannada *naral* and *neraḷu*, "to groan, moan".
