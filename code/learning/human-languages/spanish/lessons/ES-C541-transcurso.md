## You'll want to know: el transcurso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C541-DURAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el transcurso** — "the passing, the span (of time)".

> En el transcurso de la noche llegó más gente.
>
> *As the night went on, more people arrived.*

*En el transcurso de* — during, over the span of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transcurso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *trānscursus* 'a running through' (Lewis & Short), built on *trāns* 'across' and *currere* 'to run'.
