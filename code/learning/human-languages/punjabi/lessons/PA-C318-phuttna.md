## You'll want to know: ਫੁੱਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C318-MISHAP318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫੁੱਟਣਾ** — *phuṭṭṇā* — "to burst".

> ਗਰਮੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੀ ਬੋਤਲ ਫੁੱਟੀ।
>
> *garmī vich merī botal phuṭṭī.*
>
> My bottle burst in the heat.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਘੜਾ ਫੁੱਟਿਆ, *oh ghaṛā phuṭṭiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫੁੱਟਣਾ** (*phuṭṭṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*sphuṭyati*, "is burst open"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *phuṭṭṇā*, "to burst", under it (CDIAL 13845).

## What you've built
