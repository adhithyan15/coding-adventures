## You'll want to know: die Hupe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C401-GURT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Hupe** — "the horn (of a car)".

> Die Hupe von unserem alten Auto ist sehr laut.
>
> *The horn on our old car is very loud.*

Feminine: *eine laute Hupe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *Hupe* is not settled. It is usually explained as an imitation of the sound itself; EtymDB does not list the word.
