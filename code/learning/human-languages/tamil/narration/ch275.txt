Tamil, chapter 275: Feather, Tail, Nest, Plant, Creeper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
இறகு (iṟaku) — a feather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for God, then the Tamil for an animal.

You'll want to know: இறகு.
இறகு — iṟaku — "a feather".

The word, taken apart.
இறகு (iṟaku) is native Dravidian. DEDR 2591 lists Malayalam iṟaku, "wing" and Telugu ṟekka.

Before the new one: say the Tamil for an animal, then the Tamil for a feather.

You'll want to know: வால்.
வால் — vāl — "a tail".

The word, taken apart.
வால் (vāl) is an early loan from Indo-Aryan vāla, "tail, hair" (CDIAL 11572). DEDR lists it in its appendix of such loans (DEDR App. 57), with Malayalam vāl and Telugu vālamu.

Lesson 5 of 5.

கொடி (koṭi) is native in both senses: koṭi, "creeper" (DEDR 2050, which lists Malayalam koṭi), and koṭi, "banner, flag" (DEDR 2049).
