## You'll want to know: ग्रामीण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C354-NAGARIK354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्रामीण** — *grāmīṇ* — "rural".

> ग्रामीण भागात अजून चांगले रस्ते नाहीत.
>
> *grāmīṇ bhāgāt ajūn cāṅgle raste nāhīt.*
>
> There are still no good roads in rural areas.

From ग्राम, a village (formal); the opposite is शहरी, urban.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्रामीण** (*grāmīṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *grāmīṇa*, "rural, of a village" (Monier-Williams).
