## You'll want to know: बालिग़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C499-KISHOR499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बालिग़** — *bāliġ* — "of age; adult".

> अठारह साल का होकर अरुण बालिग़ हो गया।
>
> *aṭhārah sāl kā hokar Aruṇ bāliġ ho gayā.*
>
> At eighteen Arun came of age.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बालिग़** (*bāliġ*) is usually traced to Arabic *bāliġ*, "having reached maturity", which reached Hindi through Persian; it is the word of the law.
