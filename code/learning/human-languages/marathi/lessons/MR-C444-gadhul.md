## You'll want to know: गढूळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C444-DABKE444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गढूळ** — *gaḍhūḷ* — "muddy; murky".

> पावसाळ्यात नदीचं पाणी गढूळ होतं.
>
> *pāvsāḷyāt nadīcaṁ pāṇī gaḍhūḷ hotaṁ.*
>
> In the rainy season the river water turns muddy.

It does not change: गढूळ पाणी, गढूळ नदी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गढूळ** (*gaḍhūḷ*) is a Marathi adjective for cloudy, muddy water; its origin is not settled.
