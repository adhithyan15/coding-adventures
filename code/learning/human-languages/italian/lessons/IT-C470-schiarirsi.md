## You'll want to know: schiarirsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C470-PIOGGIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**schiarirsi** — "to clear up, to brighten".

> Nel pomeriggio il cielo si schiarisce.
>
> *In the afternoon the sky clears up.*

*Schiarirsi la voce* — to clear your throat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schiarirsi* is built from *s-* and *chiaro* 'clear', which is inherited from Latin *clārus* (EtymDB).
