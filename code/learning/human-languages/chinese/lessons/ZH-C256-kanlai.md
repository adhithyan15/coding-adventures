## You'll want to know: 看来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C256-THINK256-03]; assesses=[] -->

**看来** — *kànlai* — "it seems; it looks as if".

> **看来他今天不来了** *kànlai tā jīntiān bù lái le* — It looks as if he isn't coming today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**看来** (*kànlai*) is built from 看 'to look' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khanH* and *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). In the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
