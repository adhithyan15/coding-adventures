## You'll want to know: mínzú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C374-SOCIETY374-04]; assesses=[] -->

*mínzú* — "ethnic group; nationality".

> *Zhōngguó yǒu wǔshíliù ge mínzú* — China has fifty-six ethnic groups.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mínzú* is built from *mín* 'the people' and *zú* 'a clan', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjin* and *dzuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the multitude' and the second 'a lineage'.
