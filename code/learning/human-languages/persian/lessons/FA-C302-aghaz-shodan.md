## You'll want to know: آغاز شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C302-AGHAZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**آغاز شدن** (*âghâz shodan*) — "to begin, to start (formal)".

> جشن ساعت هفت شب آغاز شد.
>
> *Jashn sâ'at-e haft-e shab âghâz shod.*
>
> The celebration began at seven in the evening.

The thing itself begins: *âghâz mishavad*. With a doer the verb is *âghâz kardan*, "to begin something".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âghâz shodan* joins *âghâz* "a beginning", a native word whose deeper origin is not settled, with *shodan* "to become", inherited Persian.
