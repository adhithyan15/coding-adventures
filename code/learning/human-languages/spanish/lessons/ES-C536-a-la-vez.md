## You'll want to know: a la vez
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C536-MIENTRAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a la vez** — "at once, at the same time".

> No puedo hacer dos cosas a la vez.
>
> *I can't do two things at once.*

Close to *al mismo tiempo*, and shorter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a la vez* is built on *vez*, which is inherited from Latin *vicem* 'a turn, a change' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
