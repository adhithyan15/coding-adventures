## You'll want to know: আশ্বাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C540-MASHUL540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আশ্বাস** — *āshvās* — "reassurance; an assurance".

> ডাক্তার আমাদের আশ্বাস দিলেন।
>
> *Ḍāktār āmāder āshvās dilen.*
>
> The doctor reassured us.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আশ্বাস** (*āshvās*) is Sanskrit *āśvāsa*, "breathing again freely" (Monier-Williams): what lets you breathe easy.
