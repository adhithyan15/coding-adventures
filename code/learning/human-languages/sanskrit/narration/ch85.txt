Sanskrit, chapter 85: Days of the Week.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सप्ताहः (saptāhaḥ) — a week.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a farmer, then the Sanskrit for a merchant.

You'll want to know: सप्ताहः.
सप्ताहः (saptāhaḥ) — "a week": सप्त, "seven", with अह, "day".

What you've built.
Seven plus day.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: saptāhaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: saptāhaḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say saptāhaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for a farmer, then the Sanskrit for a merchant, then say saptāhaḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सप्ताहः (saptāhaḥ) mean? ("A week".) Say it once more.
