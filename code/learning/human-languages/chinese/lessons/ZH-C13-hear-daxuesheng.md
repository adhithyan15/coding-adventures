# Hear *dàxuésheng* — university student

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-DAXUE-01, ZH-LEX-XUESHENG-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **university**, then **student**.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-DAXUESHENG-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *dàxuésheng* — **university student**

Say three beats: falling *dà*, rising *xué*, light *sheng*. Do not repeat *xué*;
the two familiar words overlap there.
