# ી — the attached long-i vowel sign

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01] -->

Place the nasal dot once from memory. Keep it above the writing line.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ી** visible. It is the attached long-i vowel sign, pronounced *-ī*. This is the long-ī vowel sign. It attaches to the right of a consonant as an upright curl with a trailing tail, replacing the inherent a with long ī.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ી** once, then air-trace it once. Notice right-side curl with a tail; one attached curl and tail.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ી** once. Compare only the landmark named above and repair it once if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W03-ii-matra-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write the attached long-i vowel sign from memory.","answer":"ી","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ી — the taught form is intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ી, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *-ī* once. Uncover **ી**, compare, and stop after one repair.
