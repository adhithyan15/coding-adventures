## You'll want to know: posponer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C616-IMPREV-03]; assesses=[] -->

**posponer** — "to postpone".

> Tuvimos que posponer el viaje.
>
> *We had to postpone the trip.*

Like *poner*: *pospongo, pospuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*posponer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *postponere* 'to put after', built from *post* and *ponere* 'to put' (Lewis & Short).
