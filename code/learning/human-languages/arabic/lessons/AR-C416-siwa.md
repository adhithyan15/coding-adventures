## You'll want to know: سوى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C416-BADAL416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سوى** — *siwā* — "apart from; except".

> لم يحضر سوى ثلاثة طلاب.
>
> *lam yaḥḍur siwā thalāthat ṭullāb.*
>
> No one came apart from three students.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سوى** (*siwā*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-w-y*, "to be equal, level" (Lane). *Siwā* is an old noun of the root, the like of, other than: apart from, except.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
