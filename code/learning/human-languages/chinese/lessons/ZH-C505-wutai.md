## You'll want to know: wǔtái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C505-DANCE505-04]; assesses=[] -->

*wǔtái* — "stage (in a theatre)".

> *nàge wǔtái bǐ wǒmen xuéxiào de dà* — That stage is bigger than our school's.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wǔtái* is built from 舞 'to dance' and *tái* 'a platform', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjuX* and *doj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a raised square of earth.
