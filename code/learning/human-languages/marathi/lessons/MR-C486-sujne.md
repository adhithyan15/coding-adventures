## You'll want to know: सुजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C486-IJA486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सुजणे** — *sujṇe* — "to swell".

> मधमाशी चावल्यामुळे त्याचा हात सुजला.
>
> *madhmāśī cāvlyāmuḷe tyācā hāt sujlā.*
>
> His hand swelled up from a bee sting.

Past (he …): तो सुजला, *to sujlā*.

Usually the swollen part is the subject: त्याचा पाय सुजला. सूज, swelling: सूज येणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुजणे** (*sujṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *śūyate*, "is swollen"; CDIAL lists Marathi *sujṇẽ*, "to swell" (CDIAL 12568).
