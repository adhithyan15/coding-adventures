German, chapter 379: Noten und Bewertungen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bewerten — to rate, to evaluate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the summary, then the German for to sum up.

You'll want to know: bewerten.
bewerten — "to rate, to evaluate".
Die Lehrer bewerten die Aufsätze nach klaren Kriterien. The teachers evaluate the essays by clear criteria.
Inseparable: sie bewertet, sie hat bewertet. Online: ein Hotel bewerten — to rate a hotel.

The word, taken apart.
bewerten is built with be- on werten "to value", from Wert "worth", inherited from Old High German werd and Proto-Germanic werþaz (EtymDB), a cousin of English worth.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Die Klausuren werden erst nächste Woche benotet. The exams will not be graded until next week.

The word, taken apart.
benoten is built with be- on Note "mark, grade", from Latin nota "a mark, a sign, a note" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

Er war krank und muss die Prüfung nächste Woche nachholen. He was ill and has to make up the exam next week.

The word, taken apart.
nachholen is built from nach "after" and holen "to fetch", from Middle High German holen (EtymDB): you fetch what you missed afterwards.

Lesson 5 of 5.
korrigieren — to correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to grade, then the German for to make up.

You'll want to know: korrigieren.
korrigieren — "to correct".
Die Lehrerin korrigiert jeden Abend Hefte. The teacher corrects exercise books every evening.
Perfekt: hat korrigiert. Sich korrigieren — to correct yourself.

The word, taken apart.
korrigieren is a learned borrowing from Latin corrigere "to make straight, to set right" (Lewis & Short), built from con- and regere "to guide, to rule".
