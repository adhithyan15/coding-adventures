## You'll want to know: स्ट्रेचर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C491-DAKHIL491-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्ट्रेचर** — *sṭrecar* — "a stretcher".

> दो आदमी मरीज़ को स्ट्रेचर पर वार्ड तक ले गए।
>
> *do ādmī marīz ko sṭrecar par vārḍ tak le gae.*
>
> Two men carried the patient on a stretcher to the ward.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्ट्रेचर** (*sṭrecar*) is borrowed from English *stretcher*.
