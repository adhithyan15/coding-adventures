## You'll want to know: بورڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C410-IMARAT410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بورڈ** — *borḍ* — "a signboard; a board".

> سڑک کے کنارے ایک بڑا بورڈ ہے۔
>
> *saṛak ke kināre ek baṛā borḍ hai.*
>
> There's a big signboard by the road.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بورڈ** (*borḍ*) is borrowed from English *board*.
