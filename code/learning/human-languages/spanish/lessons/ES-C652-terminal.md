## You'll want to know: la terminal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C652-AEROPUERTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la terminal** — "the terminal".

> Los vuelos internacionales salen de la terminal dos.
>
> *International flights leave from terminal two.*

At an airport it is feminine: *la terminal*; *el terminal* is a device.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terminal* is a learned borrowing from Latin *terminālis* "of a boundary" (EtymDB), from *terminus* "a boundary, an end" (Lewis & Short).
