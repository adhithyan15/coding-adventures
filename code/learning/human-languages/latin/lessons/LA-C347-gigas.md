## You'll want to know: gigās, gigantis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C347-MONSTRUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gigās, gigantis** — "a giant".

> Gigantēs saxa ad caelum iaciēbant.
>
> *The giants were hurling rocks at the sky.*

Masculine, a Greek loan; usually plural. English *giant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**gigās** was borrowed from Greek *gigas*, "giant" (EtymDB). English *giant* comes from it through French.
