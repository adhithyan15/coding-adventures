## You'll want to know: supor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C368-HIPOTESE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**supor** — "to suppose".

> Suponho que ele já saiu de casa.
>
> *I suppose he has already left home.*

Conjugated like *pôr*: *suponho, supões, supõe*. *Suponhamos que…* — let us suppose that…

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*supor* is inherited from Latin *suppōnere* "to put or set under, to substitute" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *sub* and *pōnere* "to put", and reshaped on *pôr*.
