## You'll want to know: દર્શક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C552-MANCH552-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દર્શક** — *darśak* — "a spectator".

> જો દર્શકો ઓછા હશે તો નાટક બંધ થઈ જશે.
>
> *jo darśako ochā haśe to nāṭak bandh thaī jaśe.*
>
> If there are few spectators, the play will be stopped.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દર્શક** (*darśak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *darśaka*, "one who sees" (Monier-Williams).
