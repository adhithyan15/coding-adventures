## You'll want to know: परिवर्तनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C529-PARIVARTANA529-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परिवर्तनम्** — *parivartanam* — "a change; an exchange".

> यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि योजनायां परिवर्तनं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi yojanāyāṃ parivartanaṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If it rains, the plan will change.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिवर्तनम्** (*parivartanam*) is built on *pari*, "around", and the root √vṛt, "to turn", which also gives *parivartayati*; MW gives *parivartana* as turning round, and barter, exchange; "a change of plan" grows from these senses (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
