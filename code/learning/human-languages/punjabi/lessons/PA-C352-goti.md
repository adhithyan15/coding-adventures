## You'll want to know: ਗੋਟੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C352-BOARD352-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੋਟੀ** — *goṭī* — "a game piece; a counter on a board".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਚਾਰ ਲਾਲ ਗੋਟੀਆਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *mere kol chār lāl goṭīāṁ han.*
>
> I have four red pieces.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੋਟੀ** (*goṭī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*goṭṭa*, "something round"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *goṭ*, "piece on a chequer board", under it (CDIAL 4271), but not the form *goṭī* itself.
