## You'll want to know: acuō, acuere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C471-TORNUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**acuō, acuere** — "to sharpen, to whet".

> Mīles gladium ante bellum acuit.
>
> *The soldier sharpens his sword before the war.*

*acuō, acuere, acuī, acūtum*. Its participle *acūtus* 'sharp' gives English *acute*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acuō* is inherited from PIE *h₂eḱ-* 'sharp' (EtymDB), the root of *acus* 'needle'. English *acute* comes from its participle *acūtus*, and *acumen* from a noun built on it.
