## You'll want to know: किशोर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C498-VAY498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**किशोर** — *kiśor* — "a teenager".

> या मासिकात किशोरांसाठी गोष्टी असतात.
>
> *yā māsikāt kiśorāṁsāṭhī goṣṭī astāt.*
>
> This magazine has stories for teenagers.

Masculine.

किशोरी, a teenage girl; किशोरवय, the teenage years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किशोर** (*kiśor*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kiśora*, "a foal; a youth" (CDIAL 3190).
