# সাদাসিধে (sādāsidhe) — plain and simple; unpretentious

## You'll want to know: সাদাসিধে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C464-GOMBHIR464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সাদাসিধে** — *sādāsidhe* — "plain and simple; unpretentious".

> দাদুর অনেক টাকা, কিন্তু তিনি সাদাসিধে জীবন কাটান।
>
> *dādur ônek ṭākā, kintu tini sādāsidhe jībôn kāṭān.*
>
> Grandfather has plenty of money, but he lives a plain and simple life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাদাসিধে** (*sādāsidhe*) pairs সাদা (taught), white and so plain, with *sidhe*, the Bengali *sidhā*, "straight, honest", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *siddha*, "accomplished" (CDIAL 13401).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
