# ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ (mārgadarśi) — a guide

## You'll want to know: ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C455-PRAVASA455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ** — *mārgadarśi* — "a guide".

> ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ ನಮಗೆ ಕೋಟೆಯ ಇತಿಹಾಸ ಹೇಳಿದನು.
>
> *mārgadarśi namage kōṭeya itihāsa hēḷidanu.*
>
> The guide told us the history of the fort.

Plural: ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿಗಳು, *mārgadarśigaḷu*.

ಮಾರ್ಗ, a way, plus -ದರ್ಶಿ, one who shows; a guidebook can be one too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ** (*mārgadarśi*) is built from Sanskrit *mārga*, "a way" (CDIAL 10071), and *-darśin*, "one who shows", from *dṛś*, "to see"; Sanskrit also has *mārgadarśaka*, "a guide" (Monier-Williams).
