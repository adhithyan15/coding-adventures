Russian, chapter 358: Looking for a job.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
вакансия (vakánsiya) — a vacancy, a job opening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to manage, then the Russian for a team.

You'll want to know: вакансия.
вакансия (vakánsiya) — "a vacancy, a job opening".
В этой компании есть вакансия бухгалтера. V étoy kompánii yest vakánsiya bukhgáltera. This company has a vacancy for an accountant.
Feminine. Открытая вакансия (vakánsiya) — an open position.

The word, taken apart.
вакансия (vakánsiya) was borrowed from French vacance, from Latin vacantia "emptiness, vacancy" (EtymDB), built on vacāre "to be empty" (Lewis & Short). A vacancy is an empty place.

Lesson 2 of 5.
собеседование (sobesédovaniye) — a job interview.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a team, then the Russian for a vacancy.

You'll want to know: собеседование.
собеседование (sobesédovaniye) — "a job interview".
Завтра у меня собеседование в банке. Závtra u menyá sobesédovaniye v bánke. Tomorrow I have a job interview at the bank.
Neuter. Пройти собеседование (sobesédovaniye) — to get through an interview.

The word, taken apart.
собеседование (sobesédovaniye) is built on собеседник "the person you talk with", from со- "together" and беседа "a conversation". The origin of беседа is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
кандидат (kandidát) — a candidate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a vacancy, then the Russian for a job interview.

You'll want to know: кандидат.
кандидат (kandidát) — "a candidate".
На эту должность пять кандидатов. Na étu dólzhnost pyat kandidátov. There are five candidates for this post.
Masculine. Кандидат наук is also a Russian academic degree, close to a doctorate.

The word, taken apart.
кандидат (kandidát) was borrowed from Polish kandydat, from Latin candidātus (EtymDB) "clothed in white", since Roman candidates for office wore a white toga (Lewis & Short); the root is candidus "white".

Lesson 4 of 5.
нанимать (nanimát) — to hire, to take on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a job interview, then the Russian for a candidate.

You'll want to know: нанимать.
нанимать (nanimát) — "to hire, to take on".
Фирма нанимает новых сотрудников. Fírma nanimáyet nóvykh sotrúdnikov. The firm is hiring new employees.
Imperfective; perfective нанять (найму, наймёшь). An older use is to rent: нанять квартиру.

The word, taken apart.
нанимать (nanimát) is built from на- and -нимать, from an old verb "to take" that also stands in снимать and занимать. To hire someone is to take them on.

Lesson 5 of 5.
стаж (stazh) — length of service, work experience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a candidate, then the Russian for to hire.

You'll want to know: стаж.
стаж (stazh) — "length of service, work experience".
У него большой стаж работы. U negó bolshóy stazh rabóty. He has many years of work experience.
Masculine. Стаж работы — the years someone has worked, counted for a job or a pension.

The word, taken apart.
стаж (stazh) was borrowed from French stage "a period of training", from Medieval Latin stagium, from Latin stāre "to stand" (EtymDB).
