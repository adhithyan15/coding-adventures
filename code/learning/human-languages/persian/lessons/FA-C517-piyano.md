## You'll want to know: پیانو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C517-INSTRUMENTS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پیانو** (*piyâno*) — "a piano".

> دخترم از هفت سالگی پیانو میزند.
>
> *dokhtaram az haft sâlegi piyâno mizanad.*
>
> My daughter has played the piano since she was seven.

Persian strikes an instrument: *piyâno zadan* 'to play the piano'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piyâno* is a modern loan, usually traced to French or Italian *piano*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
