## You'll want to know: గెంతు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C537-ATALU537-05]; assesses=[] -->

**గెంతు** — *gentu* — "to hop; to skip".

> వర్షంలో పిల్లలు సంతోషంగా గెంతారు.
>
> *varṣaṁlō pillalu santōṣaṅgā gentāru.*
>
> The children hopped happily in the rain.

Past (I …): గెంతాను, *gentānu*.

Jumping for joy or in play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గెంతు** (*gentu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1212 lists Telugu *gentu*, "to hop, to jump", with Tamil *kenti*, "to hop, to skip".
