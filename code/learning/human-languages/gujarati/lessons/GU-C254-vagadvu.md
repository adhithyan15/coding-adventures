## You'll want to know: વગાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C254-MUSIC254-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વગાડવું** — *vagāḍvũ* — "to play an instrument, to ring".

> **તેણે ઢોલ વગાડ્યો.** — *teṇe ḍhol vagāḍyo* — "He played the drum."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વગાડવું** (*vagāḍvũ*), to make something sound, is the causative of the inherited *vāgvũ*, "to sound". CDIAL lists *vāgvũ* under Sanskrit *vādyatē*, "is made to sound" (CDIAL 11513).
