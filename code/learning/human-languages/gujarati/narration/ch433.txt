Gujarati, chapter 433: Outfits and Bags.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પોશાક (pośāk) — an outfit; a costume.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rub, then the Gujarati for to make shine.

You'll want to know: પોશાક.
પોશાક — pośāk — "an outfit; a costume".
લગ્નમાં તેણે નવો પોશાક પહેર્યો. lagnamā̃ teṇe navo pośāk paheryo. He wore a new outfit at the wedding.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પોશાક (pośāk) is usually traced to Persian pōšāk, "clothing, dress".

દાદાએ ઝોળીમાં ફળ ભર્યાં. dādāe jhoḷīmā̃ phaḷ bharyā̃. Grandfather filled his shoulder bag with fruit.

ઝોળી (jhoḷī) goes back to jhōla, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a bag" (CDIAL 5415).

Lesson 4 of 5.
શોભવું (śobhvũ) — to suit; to look good (on someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a shoulder bag, then the Gujarati for a sack.

You'll want to know: શોભવું.
શોભવું — śobhvũ — "to suit; to look good (on someone)".
આ લાલ સાડી તને ઘણી શોભે છે. ā lāl sāḍī tane ghaṇī śobhe che. This red sari suits you very well.
Past (it …): તે સાડી તેને શોભી, te sāḍī tene śobhī.

The word, taken apart.
શોભવું (śobhvũ) is built on Sanskrit śobhā, "splendour, beauty, grace" (Monier-Williams): to look beautiful in something.
