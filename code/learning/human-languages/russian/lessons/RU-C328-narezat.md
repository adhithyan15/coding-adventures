## You'll want to know: нарезать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C328-NAREZAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**нарезать** (*narézat*) — "to slice, to cut up".

> Мама нарезала хлеб.
>
> *Máma narézala khleb.*
>
> Mum sliced the bread.

Perfective, stressed on *-ре-*: *нарежу*. The imperfective is *нарезать* (*narezát*), stressed on the ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*нарезать* is built on *на-* and *резать* "to cut", inherited from Proto-Slavic *rězati* (EtymDB).
