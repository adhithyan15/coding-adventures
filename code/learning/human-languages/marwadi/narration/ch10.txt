Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 10: Four Family Words: Meaning First, One New Sign.
10 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 10.
Hear mā̃ — mother.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say the line that promises another meeting and give its two meaning parts. Then write े and the known word थारो once.

You'll want to know.
mā̃ — mother.
Listen twice. The vowel is long and the ending is nasal. Say the whole word once; do not spell it yet. Families and the names people use for relatives vary, so this lesson teaches one source-attested everyday label, not one family model.

Guided Practice.
Point to the mother cue when you hear mā̃, then say the word once from the meaning alone.

Wrap-up Recall.
Say mā̃ once, then stop.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4 and Society for Endangered Languages, Marwari Swadesh list.

Lesson 2 of 10.
मां (mā̃) — a new word from known signs.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Say mā̃ from its meaning. Write म, ा, and ं; then write ि and say the known question word kāĩ.

You'll want to know.
म + ा + ं becomes मां — mā̃ — mother.
Nothing on the page is new. Join three earned units and read the complete word.

Guided Practice.
Look at मां (mā̃) for five seconds. Cover it, wait five seconds, then write the word once. Uncover and compare the long-vowel sign and nasal mark separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Read and write मां (mā̃) once without romanization.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.

Lesson 3 of 10.
Hear bāp — father.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Say and write mother, then say water. Write familiar ल and क once each.

You'll want to know.
bāp — father.
Hear one long middle vowel: bāp. Say the word once without looking for its spelling. A respectful form such as bāpūjī can come later; this step keeps one meaning and one sound small.

Guided Practice.
Hear mā̃ and bāp in either order. Point to mother or father, then say only the word you heard.

Wrap-up Recall.
Say mā̃ and bāp once each, then stop.

Lesson 4 of 10.
ब (ba) — one new consonant.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one sign until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say father and mother from meaning cues. Write पाछे मिलसू once, then familiar प; today's voiced sound gets a different sign.

Script — one sign.
ब — ba.
Keep the model visible. Trace the lower turn, rise to the upright, and add the headline. Say ba after the hand stops.

Guided Practice.
Copy ब (ba) once. Cover it, wait five seconds, and write it once. Then say the family word bāp that this sign will begin.

Wrap-up Recall.
Read ब (ba) once, then stop.
Source: Unicode Devanagari chart.

Lesson 5 of 10.
बाप (bāp) — father on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Write ब (ba), ा, and प separately. Say mother and father, write कांई, then give the earlier see-you-later four-skill response once.

You'll want to know.
ब (ba) + ा + प becomes बाप — bāp — father.
Only the first sign is newly learned. Read the whole word without romanization.

Guided Practice.
Look for five seconds, cover बाप (bāp), wait five seconds, and write it. Check that ब (ba) did not turn into प.

Wrap-up Recall.
Read मां (mā̃) — बाप (bāp) once, then stop.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.

Lesson 7 of 10.

Lesson 9 of 10.
बहन (bahan) — sister on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Write ब (ba), ह, and न separately. Say brother, then give the heard wellbeing answer with hū̃ and ṭhīk.

You'll want to know.
ब + ह + न becomes बहन — bahan — sister.
All three signs are earned. Read the word as two sound groups: ba-han.

Guided Practice.
Hear bahan, wait five seconds, and write बहन without a visible model. Check the middle ह rather than guessing from the meaning.

Wrap-up Recall.
Read बहन (bahan) — भाई (bhāī) once, then stop.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.

Lesson 10 of 10.
Checkpoint — four close-family words.

Warm-up.
[pause 8 seconds]
Write ू once and say its long vowel. Then close the earlier page.

You'll want to know.
मां — बाप — भाई — बहन (mā̃ — bāp — bhāī — bahan).
The four labels name relationships; they do not prescribe one family structure.

Guided Practice.
Listen: identify all four heard labels.
Speak: produce two different labels from meanings.
Read: match all four printed words.
Write: write two heard labels without a model.
Score each skill separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Stop after one independently accurate pass.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.
[question — say your answer, then pause 40 seconds]
Complete the four-skill check, then write mother, father, brother, and sister in order.
