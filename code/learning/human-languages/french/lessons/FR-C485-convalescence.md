## You'll want to know: la convalescence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C485-GUERIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la convalescence** — "convalescence, recovery period".

> Pendant sa convalescence, il a lu beaucoup de livres.
>
> *During his recovery he read a lot of books.*

*Être en convalescence* — to be convalescing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la convalescence* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *convalēscentia* "regaining of health" (EtymDB), from *convalēscere* "to grow strong", built on *valēre* "to be strong" (Lewis & Short).
