## You'll want to know: ころがる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C274-JISHIN274-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ころがる** — *korogaru* — "to roll; to lie about".

> **いしが みちに ころがっていました。** — *ishi ga michi ni korogatte imashita* — "Stones were lying all over the road."

Polite **ころがります**, *korogarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **いしが ころがりました。**, *ishi ga korogarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ころがる** (*korogaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
