# かぶる (kaburu) — to wear on the head; to put on (a hat)

## You'll want to know: かぶる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C149-DRESS149-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かぶる** — *kaburu* — "to wear on the head; to put on (a hat)".

> **なつは ぼうしを かぶります。** — *natsu wa bōshi o kaburimasu* — "In summer I wear a hat."

Polite **かぶります**, *kaburimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かぶる** (*kaburu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
