## You'll want to know: けつえき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C442-INSIDE442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**けつえき** — *ketsueki* — "blood (in medicine)".

> **びょういんで けつえきを しらべました。** — *byōin de ketsueki o shirabemashita* — "They tested my blood at the hospital."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けつえき** (*ketsueki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ketsu*, "blood" (Middle Chinese *xwet*) and *eki*, "fluid" (*yek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
