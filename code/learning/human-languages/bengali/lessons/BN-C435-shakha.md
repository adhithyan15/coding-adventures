## You'll want to know: শাখা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C435-GHAS435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**শাখা** — *shākhā* — "a branch (of a tree, a bank or a river)".

> আমাদের ব্যাংকের একটা শাখা স্টেশনের কাছে।
>
> *āmāder bængker ekṭā shākhā sṭeshôner kāchhe.*
>
> Our bank has a branch near the station.

On a tree people mostly say ডাল, which sounds like ডাল, lentils; শাখা is the careful word, and the only one for a bank or a river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শাখা** (*shākhā*) is Sanskrit *śākhā*, "a branch" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole; a bank's branch is the same image.
