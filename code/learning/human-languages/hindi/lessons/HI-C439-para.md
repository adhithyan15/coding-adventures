## You'll want to know: पारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C439-TAP439-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पारा** — *pārā* — "mercury; the temperature".

> आज पारा बहुत ऊपर चला गया।
>
> *āj pārā bahut ūpar calā gayā.*
>
> The temperature shot up today.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पारा** (*pārā*) comes from Sanskrit *pārada*, "quicksilver" (Monier-Williams); the mercury in a thermometer gave the weather news its पारा चढ़ा, the temperature rose.
