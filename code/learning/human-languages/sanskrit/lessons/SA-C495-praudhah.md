# प्रौढः (prauḍhaḥ) — grown up

## You'll want to know: प्रौढः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C495-YAUVANA495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रौढः** — *prauḍhaḥ* — "grown up".

> मम पुत्री इदानीं प्रौढा अस्ति।
>
> *mama putrī idānīṃ prauḍhā asti.*
>
> My daughter is grown up now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रौढः** (*prauḍhaḥ*) is built from *pra* and *ūḍha*, the ppp of the root √vah of **वहति**; MW gives "grown up, full-grown, mature" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
