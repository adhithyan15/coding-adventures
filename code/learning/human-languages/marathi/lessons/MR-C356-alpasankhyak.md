## You'll want to know: अल्पसंख्याक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C356-LOKSHAHI356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अल्पसंख्याक** — *alpasaṅkhyāk* — "a minority (group)".

> अल्पसंख्याकांच्या हक्कांचं रक्षण झालं पाहिजे.
>
> *alpasaṅkhyākāñcyā hakkāñcaṁ rakṣaṇ jhālaṁ pāhije.*
>
> The rights of minorities must be protected.

Masculine.

Also an adjective: अल्पसंख्याक समाज; the opposite is बहुसंख्याक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अल्पसंख्याक** (*alpasaṅkhyāk*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *alpa*, "small", and *saṁkhyā*, "number" (Monier-Williams).
