## You'll want to know: শিলাবৃষ্টি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C429-KALBOISHAKHI429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**শিলাবৃষ্টি** — *shilābrishṭi* — "hail; a hailstorm".

> ঝড়ের সঙ্গে শিলাবৃষ্টি হল।
>
> *jhôṛer sôngge shilābrishṭi hôlô.*
>
> Hail came with the storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শিলাবৃষ্টি** (*shilābrishṭi*) joins Sanskrit *śilā*, "a stone", to *vṛṣṭi*, "rain" (Monier-Williams for both): a rain of stones.
