Gujarati, chapter 374: Obstacles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અવરોધ (avrodh) — an obstruction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a difficulty, then the Gujarati for to get in the way.

You'll want to know: અવરોધ.
અવરોધ — avrodh — "an obstruction".
જો રસ્તામાં કોઈ અવરોધ હશે તો આપણે બીજા રસ્તે જઈશું. jo rastāmā̃ koī avrodh haśe to āpaṇe bījā raste jaīśũ. If there is something blocking the road, we will take another way.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અવરોધ (avrodh) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit avarodha, "hindrance, blockade" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: મુસીબત.
મુસીબત — musībat — "trouble".
જો તમે મોડું કરશો તો મુસીબતમાં પડશો. jo tame moḍũ karśo to musībatmā̃ paḍśo. If you are late, you will land in trouble.
A feminine noun.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ખાળવું (khāḷvũ) — to keep in check.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a hindrance, then the Gujarati for trouble.

You'll want to know: ખાળવું.
ખાળવું — khāḷvũ — "to keep in check".
જો આપણે નદીનું પાણી ખાળીશું તો ગામ બચી જશે. jo āpaṇe nadīnũ pāṇī khāḷīśũ to gām bacī jaśe. If we hold back the river water, the village will be saved.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આંસુ ખાળ્યાં, teṇe ā̃su khāḷyā̃.

The word, taken apart.
ખાળવું (khāḷvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit skhālayati, "causes to falter, stops"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to hinder, to stop" (CDIAL 13664).

Lesson 5 of 5.
નિવારવું (nivārvũ) — to avert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for trouble, then the Gujarati for to keep in check.

You'll want to know: નિવારવું.
નિવારવું — nivārvũ — "to avert".
જો આપણે હાથ ધોઈશું તો ઘણા રોગ નિવારી શકીશું. jo āpaṇe hāth dhoīśũ to ghaṇā rog nivārī śakīśũ. If we wash our hands, we can prevent many illnesses.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મોટી આપત્તિ નિવારી, teṇe moṭī āpatti nivārī.

The word, taken apart.
નિવારવું (nivārvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit nivārayati, "wards off, restrains"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to turn back, to avert" (CDIAL 7419).
