Bengali, chapter 324: Riddles and Traps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ধাঁধা (dhā̃dhā) — a riddle; a puzzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a holy man, then the Bengali for a disguise.

You'll want to know: ধাঁধা.
ধাঁধা — dhā̃dhā — "a riddle; a puzzle".
রাক্ষস রাজকুমারকে একটা ধাঁধা জিজ্ঞাসা করল। rākkhôs rājkumārke ekṭā dhā̃dhā jigyāsā kôrlô. The ogre asked the prince a riddle.

The word, taken apart.
ধাঁধা (dhā̃dhā) is a riddle or a puzzle; its origin is not settled. ধাঁধায় পড়া is to be puzzled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
কৌশল (koushôl) — a trick; a clever plan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a disguise, then the Bengali for a riddle.

You'll want to know: কৌশল.
কৌশল — koushôl — "a trick; a clever plan".
শিয়াল একটা কৌশল করে কুমিরকে বোকা বানাল। shiyāl ekṭā koushôl kôre kumirke bokā bānālô. The jackal played a trick and made a fool of the crocodile.

The word, taken apart.
কৌশল (koushôl) is Sanskrit kauśala, 'welfare; skilfulness, cleverness' (Monier-Williams); in Bengali it is the clever plan itself. Its ৌ is read ou.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ফাঁদ (phā̃d) — a trap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a riddle, then the Bengali for a trick.

You'll want to know: ফাঁদ.
ফাঁদ — phā̃d — "a trap".
লোকেরা ইঁদুর ধরতে একটা ফাঁদ বানাল। lokerā ĩdur dhôrte ekṭā phā̃d bānālô. The people made a trap to catch the mice.

The word, taken apart.
ফাঁদ (phā̃d) comes down from Sanskrit spāśa, 'a fetter, a noose' (CDIAL 13813, which lists Bengali phā̃d, 'a net'); ফাঁসানো, to frame, is its neighbour.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ভোলানো (bholāno) — to coax; to beguile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a trick, then the Bengali for a trap.

You'll want to know: ভোলানো.
ভোলানো — bholāno — "to coax; to beguile".
শিয়াল প্রশংসা করে কাককে ভোলাল। shiyāl prôshôngsā kôre kākke bholālô. The jackal flattered the crow and beguiled it.
Past (he/she …): সেই শিয়াল কাককে ভোলাল, sei shiyāl kākke bholālô.

The word, taken apart.
ভোলানো (bholāno) is ভোলা, to forget — CDIAL 9538, a Middle Indo-Aryan bhull-, 'err, forget' — with the -ānō ending: to make someone forget themselves, to coax them.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পার পাওয়া (pār pāoyā) — to get away with it; to escape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a trap, then the Bengali for to coax.

You'll want to know: পার পাওয়া.
পার পাওয়া — pār pāoyā — "to get away with it; to escape".
চোরটা অনেক কৌশল করল, কিন্তু পার পেল না। chorṭā ônek koushôl kôrlô, kintu pār pelô nā. The thief tried many tricks, but he didn't get away with it.
Past (he/she …): সেই চোর পার পেল, sei chor pār pelô.

The word, taken apart.
পার পাওয়া (pār pāoyā) puts পার, 'the far bank' — CDIAL 8100, Sanskrit pāra — before পাওয়া: to reach the far side, to get away. পার হওয়া is to cross.
