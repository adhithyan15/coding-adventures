## You'll want to know: acudir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C483-VIDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**acudir** — "to go, to come (to a place or to someone for help)".

> Acuda a la oficina con su pasaporte.
>
> *Come to the office with your passport.*

Notices use it where speech would use *ir* or *venir*.
