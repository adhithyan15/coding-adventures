## You'll want to know: क्रिकेट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C70-LEISURE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रिकेट** (*krikeṭ*) — "cricket".

English, borrowed whole, and written with two things you have already met. The
opening क्र is क stacked on र by the same vowel-killer ् that stacks स्ट in
स्टेशन. The final ट is retroflex, because an English *t* always lands there.

The ि after the stack is the short *i* matra, and it sits to the **left** of the
whole cluster even though it is sounded after it — the same preposed matra you
first met in a name.

If you learn one game word in Hindi, learn this one. **मैं क्रिकेट खेलता हूँ**
puts three of this chapter's ideas into a single sentence.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Three: खेल, खेलना, क्रिकेट. A game, playing it, and one to name.
