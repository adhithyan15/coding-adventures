## You'll want to know: सोळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-SOLA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **सोळा** — *soḷā* — **16**

Sanskrit **षोडश** (*ṣoḍaśa*), *six-ten*. The **सो-** is the **सहा** you learned
first in this run, and the **-ळा** is the ten again — but written with **ळ**,
the retroflex *l* that Marathi has and Hindi does not.
