## You'll want to know: पराजयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C319-VIJAYA319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पराजयः** — *parājayaḥ* — "a defeat".

> शत्रोः पराजयः स्पष्टः अस्ति।
>
> *śatroḥ parājayaḥ spaṣṭaḥ asti.*
>
> The enemy's defeat is clear.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पराजयः** (*parājayaḥ*) is built from *parā*, "away", and *jaya*, "victory", from the root √ji; MW gives *parājaya* as the being deprived of something, loss, defeat (MW).
