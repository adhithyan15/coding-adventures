## You'll want to know: nánguò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C125-FEEL125-05]; assesses=[] -->

*nánguò* — "sad; upset".

> *tā de gǒu bìng le tā hěn nánguò* — His dog is ill, and he's very upset.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nánguò* is built from *nán* 'hard' and *guò* 'to pass', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nan* and *kwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): hard to get through. The pair is not given there.
