Bengali, chapter 211: To shout, To get angry, To feel shy, To quarrel, To hug.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
চেঁচানো (chẽchāno) — to shout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to marry, then the Bengali for to survive.

You'll want to know: চেঁচানো.
চেঁচানো — chẽchāno — "to shout".

The word, taken apart.
চেঁচানো (chẽchāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cicc, "scream", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4789). CDIAL lists Bengali cẽcāna, "to cry out", and calls the word imitative.

জড়িয়ে ধরা (jôṛiye dhôrā) is jôṛiye, "wrapping round", a form of the verb CDIAL lists as Bengali jaṛāna, "to wrap round", under jaḍati, "joins", which it takes from Dravidian (CDIAL 5091), + dhôrā, "to hold", from Sanskrit dharati, "holds" (CDIAL 6747).
