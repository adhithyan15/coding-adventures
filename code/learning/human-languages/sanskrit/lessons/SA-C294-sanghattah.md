## You'll want to know: संघट्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C294-SANGHATTA294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संघट्टः** — *saṅghaṭṭaḥ* — "a collision; a crash".

> मार्गे एकः संघट्टः घटितः।
>
> *mārge ekaḥ saṅghaṭṭaḥ ghaṭitaḥ.*
>
> A collision happened on the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संघट्टः** (*saṅghaṭṭaḥ*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √ghaṭṭ, "to rub, touch, shake"; MW gives *saṃghaṭṭa* as a rubbing or clashing together, a collision (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
