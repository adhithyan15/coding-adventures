## You'll want to know: খেয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C397-GHAT397-03]; assesses=[] -->

**খেয়া** — *kheyā* — "a ferry (across a river)".

> আমরা খেয়ায় নদী পার হলাম।
>
> *āmrā kheyāy nôdī pār hôlām.*
>
> We crossed the river by ferry.

A খেয়া is a short crossing back and forth; a লঞ্চ makes longer runs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খেয়া** (*kheyā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *kheyā*, "a ferry", under Sanskrit *kṣēpa*, "a sending, a throw" (CDIAL 3738); the step from sending to the boat that sends you across is the Bengali side's own.
