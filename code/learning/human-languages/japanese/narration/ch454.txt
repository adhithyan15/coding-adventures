Lesson 1 of 5.
とりくむ (torikumu) — to tackle; to work hard on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to hand in, then the Japanese for to stay up all night.

You'll want to know: とりくむ.
とりくむ — torikumu — "to tackle; to work hard on".
みんなで もんだいに とりくみました。 — minna de mondai ni torikumimashita — "We all tackled the problem together."
Polite とりくみます, torikumimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): せんせいが もんだいに とりくみました。, sensei ga mondai ni torikumimashita.

The word, taken apart.
とりくむ (torikumu) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: tori, "taking (toru)" and kumu, "putting together" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 5 of 5.
しっぱいする (shippai suru) — to fail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to take on, then the Japanese for to do over.

You'll want to know: しっぱいする.
しっぱいする — shippai suru — "to fail".
かれは しけんに しっぱいしました。 — kare wa shiken ni shippai shimashita — "He failed the exam."
Polite しっぱいします, shippaishimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): せんしゅが しっぱいしました。, senshu ga shippai shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しっぱいする (shippai suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) shippai with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shi, "lose" (Middle Chinese syit) and pai, "failure" (baejH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
