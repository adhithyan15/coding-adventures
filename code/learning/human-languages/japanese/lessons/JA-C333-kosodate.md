## You'll want to know: こそだて
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C333-WAKAMONO333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**こそだて** — *kosodate* — "bringing up children".

> **こそだては じかんが かかると おもいます。** — *kosodate wa jikan ga kakaru to omoimasu* — "I think bringing up children takes time."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こそだて** (*kosodate*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *ko*, "child" and *sodate*, "raising (sodateru)", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
