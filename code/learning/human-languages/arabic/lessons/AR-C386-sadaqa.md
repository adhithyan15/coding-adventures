## You'll want to know: صادق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C386-TAAWUN386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صادق** — *ṣādaqa* — "to ratify".

> صادق البرلمان على الاتفاق.
>
> *ṣādaqa al-barlamān ʿalā al-ittifāq.*
>
> Parliament ratified the agreement.

Present: يصادق, *yuṣādiq*.

It takes على: صادق على …. A friend, صديق, is from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صادق** (*ṣādaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-d-q*, "to be truthful" (Lane). It is Form III; *ṣādaqa ʿalā* is to confirm a thing as true: to ratify.
