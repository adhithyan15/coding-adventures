## You'll want to know: vulgō, vulgāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C301-SUSURRUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vulgō, vulgāre** — "to spread abroad, to make public".

> Rūmor dē pāce celeriter vulgātus est.
>
> *The rumour about peace was quickly spread abroad.*

*vulgō, vulgāre, vulgāvī, vulgātum*, also written *volgō*. Its compound *dīvulgō* gives English *divulge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vulgō** is built on *vulgus*, "the common people, the crowd" (EtymDB): to put something among the crowd. English *vulgar* comes from *vulgus*.
