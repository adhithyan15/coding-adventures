Japanese, chapter 209: Spring, Autumn and Cherry Blossom.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
はる (haru) — spring (the season).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the sky, then the Japanese for a typhoon.

You'll want to know: はる.
はる — haru — "spring (the season)".
らいげつは はるです。 — raigetsu wa haru desu — "Next month is spring."

The word, taken apart.
はる (haru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 3 of 6.
きせつ (kisetsu) — a season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for spring, then the Japanese for autumn.

You'll want to know: きせつ.
きせつ — kisetsu — "a season".
はるは いい きせつです。 — haru wa ii kisetsu desu — "Spring is a good season."

The word, taken apart.
きせつ (kisetsu) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ki, "season" (Middle Chinese kjwijH) and setsu, "joint, period" (tset) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
