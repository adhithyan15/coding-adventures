Hindi, chapter 313: Reporters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पत्रकार (patrakār) — a journalist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a headline, then the Hindi for to be printed.

You'll want to know: पत्रकार.
पत्रकार — patrakār — "a journalist".
मीरा पत्रकार बनना चाहती है। Mīrā patrakār bannā cāhtī hai. Mira wants to become a journalist.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
पत्रकार (patrakār) joins पत्र, a letter or a paper, from Sanskrit pattra, also spelt patra, with -kāra, "maker, doer" (Monier-Williams): a modern word for one who makes the papers, a journalist.

घटनास्थल के लिए पुल के बाद बाएँ मुड़िए। ghaṭnāsthal ke liye pul ke bād bāẽ muṛiye. For the scene of the accident, turn left after the bridge.

The word, taken apart.
घटनास्थल (ghaṭnāsthal) joins घटना, an event, with Sanskrit sthala, "a place" (Monier-Williams): the place where it happened. It is the word of news reports.

Lesson 3 of 5.
छापना (chāpnā) — to print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a journalist, then the Hindi for the scene (of an incident).

You'll want to know: छापना.
छापना — chāpnā — "to print".
अख़बार ने हादसे की तस्वीर छापी। akhbār ne hādse kī tasvīr chāpī. The newspaper printed a picture of the accident.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ख़बर छापी, usne khabar chāpī.

The word, taken apart.
छापना (chāpnā) is inherited from chapp, a pressing, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4994): printing first meant pressing a mark on to cloth or paper.

संपादक ने ख़बर को दो बार पढ़ा। sampādak ne khabar ko do bār paṛhā. The editor read the story twice.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संपादक (sampādak) is Sanskrit sampādaka, "one who brings about, who accomplishes" (Monier-Williams). The sense of editor is modern: the one who gets the paper ready.
