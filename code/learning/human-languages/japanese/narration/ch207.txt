Japanese, chapter 207: Tomorrow's Rain and Snow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
てんき (tenki) — the weather; fine weather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a passenger, then the Japanese for a booking.

You'll want to know: てんき.
てんき — tenki — "the weather; fine weather".
あしたの てんきは いいでしょう。 — ashita no tenki wa ii deshō — "The weather will probably be good tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
てんき (tenki) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ten, "heaven, sky" (Middle Chinese then) and ki, "air, breath" (khj+jH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ふる (furu) — to fall (of rain or snow).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a booking, then the Japanese for the weather.

You'll want to know: ふる.
ふる — furu — "to fall (of rain or snow)".
あした あめが ふります。 — ashita ame ga furimasu — "It will rain tomorrow."
Polite ふります, furimasu; a godan verb.

The word, taken apart.
ふる (furu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
