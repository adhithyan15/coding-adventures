## You'll want to know: رقصنده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C527-TALENT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رقصنده** (*raqsande*) — "a dancer".

> رقصنده ها با لباس محلی روی صحنه آمدند.
>
> *raqsandehâ bâ lebâs-e mahalli ru-ye sahne âmadand.*
>
> The dancers came on stage in local dress.

From *raqsidan* 'to dance' (stem *raqs-*) + *-ande* 'one who'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raqsande* is built in Persian on *raqs*, 'dance', a loan usually traced to Arabic *raqṣ*, with the native ending *-ande*, 'one who'.
