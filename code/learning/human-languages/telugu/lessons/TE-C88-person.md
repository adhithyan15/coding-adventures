## You'll want to know: మనిషి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C88-PEOPLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మనిషి** (*maniṣi*) — "a person, a human being".

**మంచి మనిషి** — "a good person", with the **మంచి** you already have. The plural changes shape:
**మనుషులు** (*manuṣulu*), "people".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A word for anyone at all, and your first plural that changes its stem.
