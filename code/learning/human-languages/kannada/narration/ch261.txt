Kannada, chapter 261: Pet, Calf, Squirrel, Lizard, Cockroach.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಸಾಕುಪ್ರಾಣಿ (sākuprāṇi) — a pet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a vehicle, then the Kannada for an autorickshaw.

You'll want to know: ಸಾಕುಪ್ರಾಣಿ.
ಸಾಕುಪ್ರಾಣಿ — sākuprāṇi — "a pet".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಕುಪ್ರಾಣಿ (sākuprāṇi) joins native sāku, "to bring up, rear", which DEDR 2427 lists, to Sanskrit prāṇin, "a living creature" (CDIAL 8932): "a creature you rear".

ಹಲ್ಲಿ (halli) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil palli, Telugu palli and Malayalam palli (DEDR 3994). DEDR lists older palli, "house lizard": the p- has become h-. DEDR 3994 compares Sanskrit pallī.
