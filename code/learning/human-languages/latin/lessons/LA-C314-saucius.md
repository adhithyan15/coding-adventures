## You'll want to know: saucius, saucia, saucium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C314-OFFENDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**saucius, saucia, saucium** — "wounded, hurt".

> Medicus mīlitēs sauciōs cūrābat.
>
> *The doctor was looking after the wounded soldiers.*

Wounded but alive; *vulnus* (taught) is the wound itself. Caesar counts the *sauciī* after a battle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**saucius** is a Latin word whose origin is not settled (Lewis & Short, EtymDB).
