## Grammar Lens: 最, the most of all
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-ZUI-MOST-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-04, ZH-LEX-C153-BI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-BI-COMPARE-01, ZH-LEX-C38-EAT38-05, ZH-LEX-C190-TOWN190-01, ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-01, ZH-SKILL-READ-STORY-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-JIA-MEASURE-01] -->

> **最 before 喜欢 or an adjective picks out the top of the group.**

> **我最喜欢中国菜** *wǒ zuì xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài* — I like Chinese food best.
> **这家书店最大** *zhè jiā shūdiàn zuì dà* — This bookshop is the biggest.

*zuì* you have said since the chapter on order; now it prints. 比 sets two things side by side, 我比他大; 最 needs no second thing: it is the most of all.

The story on the mountain ended on it: 那天是我最开心的一天.
