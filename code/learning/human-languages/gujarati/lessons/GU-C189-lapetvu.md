## You'll want to know: લપેટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C189-ORDER189-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લપેટવું** — *lapeṭvũ* — "to wrap".

> **દુકાનદાર ભેટ કાગળમાં લપેટે છે.** — *dukāndār bheṭ kāgaḷmā̃ lapeṭe che* — "The shopkeeper wraps the gift in paper."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લપેટવું** (*lapeṭvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*lappēṭṭ*, "wrap", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to wrap, fold, wind" (CDIAL 10942).
