## You'll want to know: el privilegio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C603-DERECHO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el privilegio** — "the privilege".

> Estudiar en esta universidad es un privilegio.
>
> *Studying at this university is a privilege.*

*Privilegiado* — privileged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*privilegio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *privilegium* 'a law made for one person' (EtymDB), from *privus* 'one's own' and *lex* 'law'.
