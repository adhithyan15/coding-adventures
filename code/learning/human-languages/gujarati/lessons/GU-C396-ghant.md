## You'll want to know: ઘંટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C396-GUARD396-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘંટ** — *ghanṭ* — "a large bell".

> સ્ટેશન પર ઘંટ વાગ્યો અને ટ્રેન ઊપડી.
>
> *sṭeśan par ghanṭ vāgyo ane ṭren ūpḍī.*
>
> The bell rang at the station and the train set off.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘંટ** (*ghanṭ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *ghaṇṭā*, "a bell" (CDIAL 4421): the big bell of a temple or a station. ઘંટડી is the small one.
