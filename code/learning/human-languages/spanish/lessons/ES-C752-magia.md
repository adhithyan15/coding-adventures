## You'll want to know: la magia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C752-JUEGOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la magia** — "magic".

> Le encantan los trucos de magia.
>
> *He loves magic tricks.*

*Por arte de magia* — as if by magic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*magia* comes from Late Latin *magīa*, from Greek *mageía* (EtymDB), built on *mágos*, a Persian priest.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
