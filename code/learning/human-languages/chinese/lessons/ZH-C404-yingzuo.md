## You'll want to know: yìngzuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C404-TICKET404-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yìngzuò* — "hard seat".

> *wǒ zuò yìngzuò qù Běijīng* — I'm going to Beijing in a hard seat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìngzuò* is built from *yìng* 'hard' and *zuò* 'a seat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngeangH* and *dzwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'firm' and the second as the seat of a bed.
