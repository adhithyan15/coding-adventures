## You'll want to know: استلقى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C515-NAWM515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**استلقى** — *istalqā* — "to lie down".

> استلقى الولد على العشب.
>
> *istalqā al-walad ʿalā al-ʿushb.*
>
> The boy lay down on the grass.

Present: يستلقي, *yastalqī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استلقى** (*istalqā*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-q-y*, "to meet; to throw" (Lane). Form X istalqā is to throw oneself down on one's back.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
