# Review — what happened, and what was going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-STEM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "they will go" and "I will see the city". (*te gamiṣyanti*, *ahaṃ nagaraṃ drakṣyāmi*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-ONGOING-PAST-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-ONGOING-PAST-02, SA-LEX-C276-GHATATE-01, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-226, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-04, SA-LEX-ANNAM-FOOD, SA-LEX-C35-HOUSE-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday Mira went to the shop.]
- [YOU SAY: Arun ate the food, and Mira read the book.]
- [YOU SAY: The children were playing.]
- [YOU SAY: When I was reading, Mira came.]
- [YOU SAY: What happened yesterday?]
- [YOU READ: **घटते** and **किं घटितम्?** aloud]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-ONGOING-PAST-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-ONGOING-PAST-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **गतवती**: a man or a woman? (A woman.) **सः पठति स्म**: one event, or the scene? (The scene.) Now the other way round: "she read the book"? (**सा पुस्तकं पठितवती**.) "What did he say?" (**सः किम् उक्तवान्?**)
