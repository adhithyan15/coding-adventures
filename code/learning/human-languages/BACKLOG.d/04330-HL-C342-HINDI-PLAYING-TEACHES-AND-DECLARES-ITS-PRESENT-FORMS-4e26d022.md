## HL-C342 — Hindi playing teaches and declares its present forms

After the asking-form repair, **खेलता** is the highest-ranked independent
modern Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 77 already
models **मैं खेलता हूँ**, and the following cricket lesson reuses that sentence,
but the play lesson declares only infinitive **खेलना** and does not derive the
gendered or respectful present family.

Derive **खेलता**, **खेलती**, and **खेलते** from **खेलना**, practise the gendered
first-person forms and respectful **आप**, recall the set, and declare the
complete family as the lesson headword. This closes the reporting gap before
the later reuse and makes every taught form visible to reports and downstream
cards without increasing the gentle-ramp debt.
