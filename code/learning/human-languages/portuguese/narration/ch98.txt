Portuguese, chapter 98: Hammer, Glue, Scissors, Lamp, Bathroom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o martelo — a hammer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a rope, then the Portuguese for a nail.

You'll want to know: o martelo.
o martelo — "a hammer".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o martelo mean? ("A hammer".) Say it once more.
