# यतः · कारणात् (yataḥ · kāraṇāt) — because, and because of

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think that it will rain". (*ahaṃ manye yat varṣā bhaviṣyati*.) Now give the reason for a choice.

## You'll want to know: यतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-05, SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-02, SA-LEX-C157-QUAL157-04, SA-LEX-ASTI] -->

**यतः** (*yataḥ*), "because", opens the reason, and the reason can follow what you did:

> **अहं न आगमिष्यामि,**
>
> *ahaṃ na āgamiṣyāmi,*
>
> **यतः अहं रुग्णः अस्मि।**
>
> *yataḥ ahaṃ rugṇaḥ asmi.* — "I will not come, because I am ill."

> **सः न पठति,**
>
> *saḥ na paṭhati,*
>
> **यतः सः श्रान्तः अस्ति।**
>
> *yataḥ saḥ śrāntaḥ asti.* — "He is not reading, because he is tired."

**आगमिष्यामि** (*āgamiṣyāmi*) is **गमिष्यामि** with the prefix **आ-** of **आगच्छति**: "I will come".

## You'll want to know: कारणात्, हेतुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-04, SA-LEX-C98-WHY98-03, SA-LEX-C76-WEATHER-01] -->

**कारणात्** (*kāraṇāt*) is **कारणम्** (*kāraṇam*), "a reason", with *-āt*, "from": "because of". The cause goes in front of it, joined to it as one word:

> **वर्षाकारणात् सः न आगतवान्।**
>
> *varṣākāraṇāt saḥ na āgatavān.* — "Because of the rain he did not come."

**हेतुः** (*hetuḥ*), "a cause", names the reason as a thing:

> **हेतुः किम्?**
>
> *hetuḥ kim?* — "What is the cause?"

> **हेतुः वर्षा अस्ति।**
>
> *hetuḥ varṣā asti.* — "The cause is the rain."
