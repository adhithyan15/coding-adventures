[your turn — say: sunset, tonight, the day after tomorrow, the day before yesterday, every morning]

[your turn — say: every week, the weekend, the end, on the way, half past]

[your turn — say: every year, this year, this week, the year before last, oversleeping]

[your turn — say: midnight, a whole week, one whole day, half a day, the first of the month]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
The afternoon? (ごご, gogo.) The middle of the night? (よなか, yonaka.) Early morning? (あさがた, asagata.) The evening meal? (ゆうはん, yuuhan.) The morning sun? (あさひ, asahi.) Sunset? (ひのいり, hinoiri.) Tonight? (こんや, konya.) The day after tomorrow? (あさって, asatte.) The day before yesterday? (おととい, ototoi.) Every morning? (まいあさ, maiasa.) Every week? (まいしゅう, maishuu.) The weekend? (しゅうまつ, shuumatsu.) The end? (おわり, owari.) On the way? (とちゅう, tochuu.) Half past? (はん, han.) Every year? (まいとし, maitoshi.) This year? (ことし, kotoshi.) This week? (こんしゅう, konshuu.) The year before last? (おととし, ototoshi.) Oversleeping? (あさねぼう, asanebou.) Midnight? (まよなか, mayonaka.) A whole week? (いっしゅうかん, isshuukan.) One whole day? (いちにち, ichinichi.) Half a day? (はんにち, hannichi.) The first of the month? (ついたち, tsuitachi.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Type the Japanese for 'between'.
