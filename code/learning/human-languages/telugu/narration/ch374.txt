Lesson 1 of 5.
వ్యాఖ్యానం (vyākhyānaṁ) — a commentary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a burrakatha ballad, then the Telugu for to make a start.

You'll want to know: వ్యాఖ్యానం.
వ్యాఖ్యానం — vyākhyānaṁ — "a commentary".
రేడియోలో క్రికెట్ వ్యాఖ్యానం వినిపిస్తోంది. rēḍiyōlō krikeṭ vyākhyānaṁ vinipistōndi. Cricket commentary is playing on the radio.

The word, taken apart.
వ్యాఖ్యానం (vyākhyānaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit vyākhyāna, "comment, narration" (CDIAL 12188).

Lesson 2 of 5.
వ్యాఖ్యాత (vyākhyāta) — a commentator; a presenter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to make a start, then the Telugu for a commentary.

You'll want to know: వ్యాఖ్యాత.
వ్యాఖ్యాత — vyākhyāta — "a commentator; a presenter".
ఆ వ్యాఖ్యాత మాటలు అందరికీ నచ్చాయి. ā vyākhyāta māṭalu andarikī naccāyi. Everyone liked that commentator's words.
Plural: వ్యాఖ్యాతలు, vyākhyātalu.
The person behind a వ్యాఖ్యానం (vyākhyānaṁ), a commentary, on radio or TV.

The word, taken apart.
వ్యాఖ్యాత (vyākhyāta) is borrowed from Sanskrit vyākhyātṛ, "an explainer, a commentator" (Monier-Williams), from the same root as వ్యాఖ్యానం (vyākhyānaṁ).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a commentator, then the Telugu for a listener.

You'll want to know: తిలకించు.
తిలకించు — tilakiñcu — "to behold; to watch".
వేలాది మంది ఆ ఉత్సవాన్ని తిలకించారు. vēlādi mandi ā utsavānni tilakiñcāru. Thousands watched that celebration.
Past (I …): తిలకించాను, tilakiñcānu.
A news word for watching an event or a show.

The word, taken apart.
తిలకించు (tilakiñcu) is a written-style Telugu verb for watching a spectacle; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ముఖాముఖి (mukhāmukhi) — a face-to-face interview.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a listener, then the Telugu for to behold.

You'll want to know: ముఖాముఖి.
ముఖాముఖి — mukhāmukhi — "a face-to-face interview".
టీవీలో ఆ నటుడితో ముఖాముఖి వచ్చింది. ṭīvīlō ā naṭuḍitō mukhāmukhi vaccindi. A face-to-face interview with that actor was on TV.
Literally face to face; ఇంటర్వ్యూ says the same.

The word, taken apart.
ముఖాముఖి (mukhāmukhi) is built on Sanskrit mukha, "the mouth, the face" (CDIAL 10158), said twice: face to face.
