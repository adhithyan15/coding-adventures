## You'll want to know: qiúyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C499-TEAM499-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúyuán* — "a player in a ball game".

> *tā dāng le shí nián qiúyuán* — He was a player for ten years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiúyuán* is built from 球 'a ball' and *yuán* 'a member', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjuw* and *hjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a count of things'; the sense 'a member' is not given there.
