## You'll want to know: a costura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C500-COSTURA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a costura** — "sewing; seam".

> A minha avó aprendeu costura com a mãe.
>
> *My grandmother learned sewing from her mother.*

Also a seam: *a costura rasgou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*costura* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *cōnsūtūra*, built on Latin *cōnsuere* "to sew together" (Lewis & Short), from *suere* "to sew".
