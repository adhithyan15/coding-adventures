## You'll want to know: embaucher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C356-CHOMAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**embaucher** — "to hire, to take on".

> Le restaurant embauche des serveurs pour l'été.
>
> *The restaurant is taking on waiters for the summer.*

*Le restaurant embauche* — the restaurant is hiring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embaucher* is usually explained as built from *en-* and an old word *bauche* "workshop": to bring someone into the workshop. The origin of *bauche* is not settled.
