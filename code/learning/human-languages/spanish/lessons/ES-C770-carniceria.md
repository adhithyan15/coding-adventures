## You'll want to know: la carnicería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C770-COMERCIOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la carnicería** — "the butcher's".

> Compro en esta carnicería porque la carne es muy buena.
>
> *I shop at this butcher's because the meat is very good.*

*El carnicero* is the butcher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carnicería* is built on *carnicero* 'a butcher' with the ending *-ería* (EtymDB). The root is *carne*, which comes from Latin *carō*, *carnem* 'flesh'.
