## You'll want to know: ઉધાર લેવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C190-BANK190-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉધાર લેવું** — *udhār levũ* — "to borrow".

> **તે બેંકમાંથી પૈસા ઉધાર લે છે.** — *te bẽkmā̃thī paisā udhār le che* — "He borrows money from the bank."

Past **તેણે ઉધાર લીધું**, *teṇe udhār līdhũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉધાર લેવું** (*udhār levũ*) is built from *udhār*, "credit, loan", and *levũ*, "to take". *udhār* is a tadbhava from Sanskrit *uddhārá*, "raising; a loan", and CDIAL lists Gujarati *udhāro*, "credit account" (CDIAL 2018).
