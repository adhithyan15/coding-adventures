Arabic, chapter 415: Leaving a mark.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
إنجاز (injāz) — an achievement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a consequence, then the Arabic for an effect.

You'll want to know: إنجاز.
إنجاز — injāz — "an achievement".
كان الجسر الجديد إنجازا كبيرا. kāna al-jisr al-jadīd injāzan kabīran. The new bridge was a great achievement.

The word, taken apart.
إنجاز (injāz) is built on the native Arabic root n-j-z, "to be accomplished, finished" (Lane). Injāz is the Form IV verbal noun of anjaza, to accomplish: an achievement.

تأثر (taʾaththara) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-th-r, "a trace, a track" (Lane). It is Form V, to take a trace from a thing: to be affected, to be moved.
