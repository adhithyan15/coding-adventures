## You'll want to know: il rubinetto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C434-BAGNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il rubinetto** — "the tap, the faucet".

> Il rubinetto dell'acqua calda non funziona.
>
> *The hot tap does not work.*

*Aprire il rubinetto* — to turn on the tap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rubinetto* was borrowed from French *robinet* 'tap' (EtymDB).
