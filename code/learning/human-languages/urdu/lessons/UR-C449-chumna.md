## You'll want to know: چومنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C449-LOVE449-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چومنا** — *chūmnā* — "to kiss".

> ماں نے بچے کا ماتھا چوما۔
>
> *māṅ ne bachche kā māthā chūmā.*
>
> The mother kissed the child's forehead.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو چوما, *us ne bachche ko chūmā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چومنا** (*chūmnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cúmbati*, "kisses" (CDIAL 4870).
