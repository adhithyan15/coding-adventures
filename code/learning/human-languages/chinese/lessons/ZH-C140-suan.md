## You'll want to know: suàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-05]; assesses=[] -->

*suàn* — "to calculate; to work out".

> *wǒ suàn le yígòng shì sānshí kuài* — I've worked it out: thirty yuan in all.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suàn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *swanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the number of things'. The regular sound change would give a third tone, *suǎn*, not *suàn*; why is not settled.
