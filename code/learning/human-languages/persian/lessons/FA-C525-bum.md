## You'll want to know: بوم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C525-PAINTING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بوم** (*bum*) — "a canvas (for painting)".

> نقاش یک بوم بزرگ خرید.
>
> *naqqâsh yek bum-e bozorg kharid.*
>
> The painter bought a large canvas.

Other words are spelled the same: *bum* 'land' in *marz-o-bum* 'homeland', and *bum* 'owl'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB traces Persian *bum*, 'land, ground', to Middle Persian and on to Old Persian (EtymDB). The painter's *bum*, the ground a picture is laid on, is usually linked with it, but the link is not settled.
