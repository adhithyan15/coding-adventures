Lesson 1 of 6.
ஆராய்ச்சி (ārāycci) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a good match, then the Tamil for a connection.

You'll want to know: ஆராய்ச்சி.
ஆராய்ச்சி — ārāycci — "research".
என் அக்கா செடிகளைப் பற்றி ஆராய்ச்சி செய்கிறாள். eṉ akkā ceṭikaḷaip paṟṟi ārāycci ceykiṟāḷ. My elder sister does research on plants.
ஆராய்ச்சி (ārāycci) செய், to do research. Its verb is ஆராய், to investigate.

The word, taken apart.
ஆராய்ச்சி (ārāycci) is native Dravidian: DEDR 377 lists Tamil ārāy, "to investigate, examine, seek", and ārāycci, "research, investigation".

Lesson 3 of 6.
தரவு (taravu) — data.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for research, then the Tamil for a study.

You'll want to know: தரவு.
தரவு — taravu — "data".
இந்தத் தரவுகளை எங்கிருந்து பெற்றீர்கள்? intat taravukaḷai eṅkiruntu peṟṟīrkaḷ? Where did you get this data?
Plural: தரவுகள், taravukaḷ.
Plural தரவுகள், the pieces of data.

The word, taken apart.
தரவு (taravu) is a modern Tamil coinage for "data", usually read as built on native taru, "to give" (DEDR 3098): "what is given", which is also what Latin data means.

ஆசிரியர் தேர்வு முடிவுகளைப் பகுப்பாய்வு செய்தார். āciriyar tērvu muṭivukaḷaip pakuppāyvu ceytār. The teacher analysed the exam results.

The word, taken apart.
பகுப்பாய்வு (pakuppāyvu) is a modern native coinage: pakuppu, "division", from paku, "to divide" (DEDR 3808), and āyvu, "examination" (DEDR 363): taking apart and examining.

Lesson 5 of 6.
அலசு (alacu) — to analyse closely; to rinse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for data, then the Tamil for an analysis.

You'll want to know: அலசு.
அலசு — alacu — "to analyse closely; to rinse".
மாணவர்கள் இந்தப் பிரச்சினையை விரிவாக அலசினார்கள். māṇavarkaḷ intap piracciṉaiyai virivāka alaciṉārkaḷ. The students went over this problem in detail.
Past (I …): அலசினேன், alaciṉēṉ.
The first sense is to rinse clothes; a question is gone over again and again in the same way.

The word, taken apart.
அலசு (alacu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 246 lists Tamil alampu, alacu, "to wash, rinse". Going over a question closely is its newer sense.
