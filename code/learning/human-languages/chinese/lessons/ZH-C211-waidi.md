## You'll want to know: 外地
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C211-LOCAL211-02]; assesses=[] -->

**外地** — *wàidì* — "from out of town; another part of the country".

> **他是外地人每天开车一百公里** *tā shì wàidì rén měitiān kāichē yìbǎi gōnglǐ* — He's from out of town and drives a hundred kilometres every day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**外地** (*wàidì*) is built from 外 'outside' and 地 'place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwajH* and *dijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
