5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il doganiere — the customs officer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the scales, then the Italian for to pack.

You'll want to know: il doganiere.
il doganiere — "the customs officer".
Il doganiere apre la mia valigia. The customs officer opens my case.
La dogana is customs.

The word, taken apart.
doganiere is built on dogana 'customs', which came from Arabic dīwān 'register, office' through a southern form like Sicilian doana (EtymDB).

dichiarare is inherited from Latin dēclārāre 'to make clear' (EtymDB), built on clārus 'clear': Latin cl- became chi-, as in chiaro.

Lesson 3 of 5.
perquisire — to search (a person or a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the customs officer, then the Italian for to declare.

You'll want to know: perquisire.
perquisire — "to search (a person or a place)".
Al controllo mi hanno perquisito. At security they searched me.
Conjugated like finire: perquisisco.

The word, taken apart.
perquisire is a learned borrowing from Latin perquīrere 'to search everywhere', built on quaerere 'to seek' (Lewis & Short).
