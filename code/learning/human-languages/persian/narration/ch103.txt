Persian, chapter 103: Hill, Island, Waterfall, Wave, Root.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تپه (tappe) — a hill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sweet drink, then the Persian for a plain.

You'll want to know: تپه.
تپه — tappe — "a hill".

The word, taken apart.
tappe is borrowed from Turkic: EtymDB gives Turkish tepe, "hill", and an Old Turkic word for "height".

Lesson 3 of 5.
آبشار (âbshâr) — a waterfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a hill, then the Persian for an island.

You'll want to know: آبشار.
آبشار — âbshâr — "a waterfall".

The word, taken apart.
âbshâr is âb, "water", with -shâr, "pouring" (from shâridan, "to pour"): pouring water. EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian ābšār. Both parts are native.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ریشه (rishe) — a root.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a waterfall, then the Persian for a wave.

You'll want to know: ریشه.
ریشه — rishe — "a root".

The word, taken apart.
rishe is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian rēšag, "root". EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European word for "root" behind Latin radix and English wort.
