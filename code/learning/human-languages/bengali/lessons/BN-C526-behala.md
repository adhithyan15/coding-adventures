## You'll want to know: বেহালা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C526-BAJNA526-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বেহালা** — *behālā* — "a violin".

> মেয়েটা স্কুলে বেহালা বাজাতে শেখে।
>
> *Meyeṭā skule behālā bājāte shekhe.*
>
> The girl is learning to play the violin at school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেহালা** (*behālā*) is usually traced to Portuguese *viola*, a stringed instrument.
