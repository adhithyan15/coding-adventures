Telugu, chapter 283: To charge, To remove, To recognize, To oppose, To argue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఛార్జ్ చేయు (chārj cēyu) — to charge (a battery).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to share out, then the Telugu for to visit.

You'll want to know: ఛార్జ్ చేయు.
ఛార్జ్ చేయు — chārj cēyu — "to charge (a battery)".

The word, taken apart.
ఛార్జ్ చేయు (chārj cēyu) pairs chārj, a loan from English charge, with native cēyu, "to do" (DEDR 1957).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: తొలగించు.
తొలగించు — tolagiñcu — "to remove, to delete".

The word, taken apart.
తొలగించు (tolagiñcu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3519 lists a nasalised tolagiñcu, "to remove", with Kannada tolagisu, "to cause to go away".
