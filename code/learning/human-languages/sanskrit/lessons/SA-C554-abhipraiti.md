## You'll want to know: अभिप्रैति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C554-BHAVI554-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिप्रैति** — *abhipraiti* — "intends".

> यदि भवान् गन्तुम् अभिप्रैति, तर्हि अहम् अपि आगच्छामि।
>
> *yadi bhavān gantum abhipraiti, tarhi aham api āgacchāmi.*
>
> If you intend to go, I will come too.

Past (he …): सः गन्तुम् अभिप्रेतवान्, *saḥ gantum abhipretavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिप्रैति** (*abhipraiti*) is built on *abhi*, "towards", *pra*, "forth", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *abhipre* as to approach in mind, to aim at, to intend; its past participle is *abhipreta* (MW).

## What you've built
