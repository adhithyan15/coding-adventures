## You'll want to know: yóuhuà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C502-PAINT502-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yóuhuà* — "oil painting".

> *wǒ xué le liǎng nián yóuhuà* — I studied oil painting for two years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóuhuà* is built from *yóu* 'oil' and *huà* 'a painting', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yuw* and *hweaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second, after an older book, 'to hang': to hang up the shapes of things in the five colours.
