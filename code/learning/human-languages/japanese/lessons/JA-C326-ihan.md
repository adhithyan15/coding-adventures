## You'll want to know: いはん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C326-KISOKU326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いはん** — *ihan* — "a violation; breaking the rules".

> **ここに くるまを とめたら、いはんです。** — *koko ni kuruma o tometara, ihan desu* — "Parking your car here is against the rules."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いはん** (*ihan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *i*, "differ" (Middle Chinese *hjw+j*) and *han*, "anti-" (*pjonX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
