Malayalam, chapter 303: To weigh, To count, To add, To save, To bargain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
തൂക്കുക (tūkkuka) — to weigh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to chop, then the Malayalam for to grind.

You'll want to know: തൂക്കുക.
തൂക്കുക — tūkkuka — "to weigh".

The word, taken apart.
തൂക്കുക (tūkkuka) is native Dravidian, the causative of the verb "to hang, be weighed"; DEDR 3376 lists the root with Tamil tūṅku and Kannada tūgu.
