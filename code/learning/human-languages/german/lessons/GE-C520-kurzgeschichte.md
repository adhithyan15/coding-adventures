## You'll want to know: die Kurzgeschichte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C520-BUECHER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kurzgeschichte** — "short story".

> In der Schule lesen wir eine Kurzgeschichte.
>
> *At school we are reading a short story.*

Plural: *die Kurzgeschichten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kurzgeschichte* is built on *kurz* "short", an early borrowing from Latin *curtus*, and *Geschichte* "story", from Middle High German *geschicht* "event, story" (EtymDB).
