Sanskrit, chapter 296: Fire!.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दावाग्निः (dāvāgniḥ) — a forest fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an emergency, then the Sanskrit for leans on.

You'll want to know: दावाग्निः.
दावाग्निः — dāvāgniḥ — "a forest fire".
पर्वते दावाग्निः ज्वलति। parvate dāvāgniḥ jvalati. A forest fire is burning on the hill.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दावाग्निः (dāvāgniḥ) is built from dāva, "a blaze, a forest blaze", and agni, "fire"; MW gives dāvāgni as fire in a forest (MW).

प्रज्वलति — prajvalati — "blazes up; catches fire".
शुष्कं तृणं सहसा प्रज्वलति। śuṣkaṃ tṛṇaṃ sahasā prajvalati. The dry grass suddenly blazes up.
Past (it …): तृणं सहसा प्रज्वलितवत्, tṛṇaṃ sahasā prajvalitavat.

The word, taken apart.
प्रज्वलति (prajvalati) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √jval, "to burn, blaze"; MW gives pra-jval as to begin to burn, to flame up (MW).

स्फुलिंगः (sphuliṅgaḥ) MW gives sphuliṅga as a spark of fire and suggests, with a perhaps, that it is built from sphulam and ga, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for blazes up, then the Sanskrit for a spark.

You'll want to know: गाढः.
गाढः — gāḍhaḥ — "dense; thick".
सर्वत्र गाढः धूमः अस्ति। sarvatra gāḍhaḥ dhūmaḥ asti. There is thick smoke everywhere.

The word, taken apart.
गाढः (gāḍhaḥ) comes from the root √gāh, "to plunge into": MW gives gāḍha as deeply entered, close, and also thick or dense (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रसरति (prasarati) — spreads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a spark, then the Sanskrit for dense.

You'll want to know: प्रसरति.
प्रसरति — prasarati — "spreads".
वायुना अग्निः शीघ्रं प्रसरति। vāyunā agniḥ śīghraṃ prasarati. With the wind the fire spreads quickly.
Past (it …): अग्निः सर्वत्र प्रसृतवान्, agniḥ sarvatra prasṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसरति (prasarati) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √sṛ, "to run, flow"; MW gives pra-sṛ as to move forwards and spread, and to break out, as a fire does (MW).
