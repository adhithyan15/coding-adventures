## You'll want to know: さかん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C296-SAKAN296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さかん** — *sakan* — "thriving; popular".

> **今も その まつりは さかんです。** — *ima mo sono matsuri wa sakan desu* — "The festival is still going strong today."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さかん** (*sakan*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
