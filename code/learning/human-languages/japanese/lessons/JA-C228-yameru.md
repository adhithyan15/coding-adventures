# やめる (yameru) — to quit; to resign

## You'll want to know: やめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C228-LEARN228-04]; assesses=[] -->

**やめる** — *yameru* — "to quit; to resign".

> **あには らいげつ しごとを やめます。** — *ani wa raigetsu shigoto o yamemasu* — "My elder brother quits his job next month."

Polite **やめます**, *yamemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やめる** (*yameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
