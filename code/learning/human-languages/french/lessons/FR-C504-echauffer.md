## You'll want to know: échauffer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C504-GYM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**échauffer** — "to warm up; (s'échauffer) to warm up (before sport)".

> Les joueurs s'échauffent avant le match.
>
> *The players warm up before the match.*

Mostly reflexive in sport: *s'échauffer*. *L'échauffement* — the warm-up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*échauffer* is inherited from Old French *eschaufer* (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *excalefāre*, a form of Latin *calefacere* "to make warm", built on *calēre* "to be warm" (Lewis & Short).
