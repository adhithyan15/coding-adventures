# طالما (ṭālamā) — as long as

## You'll want to know: طالما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C399-SHART399-04]; assesses=[] -->

**طالما** — *ṭālamā* — "as long as".

> سأبقى هنا طالما أنت هنا.
>
> *sa-abqā hunā ṭālamā anta hunā.*
>
> I will stay here as long as you are here.

With the past it also means often: طالما حلمت بذلك, I often dreamed of that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طالما** (*ṭālamā*) joins *ṭāla*, "it was long", built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-w-l*, "to be long" (Lane), with *mā*: for as long as.

## What you've built
