Arabic, chapter 365: Fact and fancy.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
حقيقة (ḥaqīqa) — a fact.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to specify, then the Arabic for to distinguish.

You'll want to know: حقيقة.
حقيقة — ḥaqīqa — "a fact".
هذه حقيقة لا يعرفها كثير من الناس. hādhihi ḥaqīqa lā yaʿrifuhā kathīr min an-nās. This is a fact that many people do not know.
في الحقيقة, fī al-ḥaqīqa, in fact.

The word, taken apart.
حقيقة (ḥaqīqa) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-q-q, "to be true, to be due" (Lane). A ḥaqīqa is what is truly so: a fact, the truth.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: واقع.
واقع — wāqiʿ — "reality".
الواقع مختلف عن الأفلام. al-wāqiʿ mukhtalif ʿan al-aflām. Reality is different from the films.
في الواقع, fī al-wāqiʿ, in reality, actually.

The word, taken apart.
واقع (wāqiʿ) is built on the native Arabic root w-q-ʿ, "to fall, to happen" (Lane). Wāqiʿ is the active participle, what has fallen, what happens: reality.

You'll want to know: خيال.
خيال — khayāl — "imagination".
عند ابنتي خيال واسع. ʿinda ibnatī khayāl wāsiʿ. My daughter has a vivid imagination.
خيال علمي, khayāl ʿilmī, science fiction.

The word, taken apart.
خيال (khayāl) is built on the native Arabic root kh-y-l, "to think, to fancy" (Lane). Khayāl is an image or phantom in the mind: imagination.

فند — fannada — "to refute".
فند المحامي كلام الشاهد. fannada al-muḥāmī kalām ash-shāhid. The lawyer refuted the witness's statement.
Present: يفند, yufannid.

The word, taken apart.
فند (fannada) is built on the native Arabic root f-n-d, "to lie, to say what is untrue" (Lane). It is Form II, to show to be untrue: to refute.

Lesson 5 of 6.
اقتنع (iqtanaʿa) — to be convinced.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for imagination, then the Arabic for to refute.

You'll want to know: اقتنع.
اقتنع — iqtanaʿa — "to be convinced".
اقتنع أبي بالفكرة بعد ساعة. iqtanaʿa abī bi-l-fikra baʿda sāʿa. My father was convinced by the idea after an hour.
Present: يقتنع, yaqtaniʿ.
It takes بـ: اقتنع (iqtanaʿa) بـ …. Compare أقنع, to convince.

The word, taken apart.
اقتنع (iqtanaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root q-n-ʿ, "to be content, satisfied" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to become content with: to be convinced.
