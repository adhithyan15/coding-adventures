## You'll want to know: そんがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C364-SONGAI364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**そんがい** — *songai* — "damage; a loss".

> **たいふうが きたら、そんがいが あるでしょう。** — *taifū ga kitara, songai ga aru deshō* — "If the typhoon comes, there will probably be damage."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そんがい** (*songai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *son*, "loss" (Middle Chinese *swonX*) and *gai*, "harm" (*hajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
