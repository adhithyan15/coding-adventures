## You'll want to know: റൺവേ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C463-VIMANAM463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**റൺവേ** — *ṟaṇvē* — "a runway".

> വിമാനം റൺവേയിലൂടെ വേഗത്തിൽ ഓടി.
>
> *vimānaṁ ṟaṇvēyilūṭe vēgattil ōṭi.*
>
> The plane ran fast along the runway.

The ending -ലൂടെ means along or through: റൺവേയിലൂടെ, along the runway.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റൺവേ** (*ṟaṇvē*) was borrowed from English *runway*, the strip where planes land and take off.
