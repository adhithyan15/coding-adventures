## You'll want to know: le vernissage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C518-EXPOSITION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le vernissage** — "private view, opening (of an exhibition)".

> Elle nous a invités au vernissage de son exposition.
>
> *She invited us to the opening of her exhibition.*

First the last coat of varnish, put on the day before the show.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le vernissage* is built on *vernir* "to varnish" with *-age* (EtymDB): painters once varnished their works on the eve of the show. The origin of *vernis* is usually traced to Medieval Latin *veronix*.
