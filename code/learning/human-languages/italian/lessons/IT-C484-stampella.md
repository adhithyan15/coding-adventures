## You'll want to know: la stampella
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C484-GESSO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la stampella** — "crutch".

> Cammino con le stampelle da due settimane.
>
> *I have been walking on crutches for two weeks.*

In some regions it is also a clothes hanger; elsewhere that is *la gruccia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *stampella* is not settled.
