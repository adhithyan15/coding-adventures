## You'll want to know: bǎozhèng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C382-GUARD382-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎozhèng* — "to promise; to guarantee".

> *wǒ bǎozhèng yǐhòu bú zhème chǎo le* — I promise I won't be this noisy again.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎozhèng* is built from *bǎo* 'to keep' and *zhèng* 'to prove', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *tsyingH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to answer for' and the second 'to test'.
