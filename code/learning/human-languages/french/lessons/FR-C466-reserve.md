## You'll want to know: réservé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C466-TIMIDITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**réservé** — "reserved, quiet (of a person); booked".

> Mon frère est un garçon réservé ; il parle peu de lui.
>
> *My brother is a reserved boy; he says little about himself.*

Of a table or a seat it means booked: *une table réservée*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réservé* is the past participle of *réserver* "to keep back", which comes from Latin *reservāre* (Lewis & Short), built on *servāre* "to keep, to watch over".
