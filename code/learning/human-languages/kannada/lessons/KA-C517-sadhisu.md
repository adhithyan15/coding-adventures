## You'll want to know: ಸಾಧಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C517-PRAGATI517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಧಿಸು** — *sādhisu* — "to achieve".

> ಕಷ್ಟಪಟ್ಟು ಅವಳು ತನ್ನ ಕನಸನ್ನು ಸಾಧಿಸಿದಳು.
>
> *kaṣṭapaṭṭu avaḷu tanna kanasannu sādhisidaḷu.*
>
> Through hard work she achieved her dream.

Past (I …): ಸಾಧಿಸಿದೆ, *sādhiside*.

The noun is ಸಾಧನೆ, an achievement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಧಿಸು** (*sādhisu*) is built on Sanskrit *sādh-*, "to accomplish", whose noun *sādhana* means "furthering, completion" (CDIAL 13334), with the verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
