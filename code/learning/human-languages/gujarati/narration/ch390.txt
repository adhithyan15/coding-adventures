Gujarati, chapter 390: Making It Grow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પાક (pāk) — a crop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hesitate, then the Gujarati for to waver.

You'll want to know: પાક.
પાક — pāk — "a crop".
જો સમયસર વરસાદ પડશે તો આ વરસે પાક સારો થશે. jo samaysar varsād paḍśe to ā varse pāk sāro thaśe. If the rain comes on time, the crop will be good this year.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પાક (pāk) shares its source with પાકવું (pākvũ), "to ripen": CDIAL traces both to Sanskrit pakvá, "cooked, ripe", which in the Atharvaveda already meant "ripe corn" (CDIAL 7621). CDIAL glosses the Gujarati noun only as "sugar boiled for conserves", so "a crop" is a sense it does not give.

જો તે થોડો વધારે પરિપક્વ હોત તો આવી ભૂલ ન કરત. jo te thoḍo vadhāre paripakva hot to āvī bhūl na karat. If he were a little more mature, he would not make such a mistake.

પરિપક્વ (paripakva) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit paripakva, "completely cooked, fully ripe" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઉગાડવું (ugāḍvũ) — to grow plants.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for mature, then the Gujarati for barren.

You'll want to know: ઉગાડવું.
ઉગાડવું — ugāḍvũ — "to grow plants".
જો તમે આંગણામાં ટામેટાં ઉગાડશો તો બજારમાંથી ખરીદવાં નહીં પડે. jo tame ā̃gṇāmā̃ ṭāmeṭā̃ ugāḍśo to bajārmā̃thī kharīdvā̃ nahī̃ paḍe. If you grow tomatoes in the courtyard, you will not have to buy them at the market.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બગીચામાં ફૂલ ઉગાડ્યાં, teṇe bagīcāmā̃ phūl ugāḍyā̃.

The word, taken apart.
ઉગાડવું (ugāḍvũ) is the causative of ઊગવું (ūgvũ), "to rise, to sprout", from a reconstructed udgāti, "rises, comes forth" (CDIAL 1954).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઊપજવું (ūpajvũ) — to be produced (as a crop).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for barren, then the Gujarati for to grow plants.

You'll want to know: ઊપજવું.
ઊપજવું — ūpajvũ — "to be produced (as a crop)".
જો ખેતરમાં પૂરતું પાણી હશે તો ઘણા ઘઉં ઊપજશે. jo khetarmā̃ pūrtũ pāṇī haśe to ghaṇā ghaũ ūpajśe. If the field has enough water, it will yield a lot of wheat.
Past (it …): તે ખેતરમાં બાજરી ઊપજી, te khetarmā̃ bājrī ūpjī.

The word, taken apart.
ઊપજવું (ūpajvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit útpadyatē, "arises, originates"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "is produced" (CDIAL 1814).
