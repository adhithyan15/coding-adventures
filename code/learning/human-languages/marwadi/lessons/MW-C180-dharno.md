## You'll want to know: धरणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C180-DHARO180-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धरणो** — *dharṇo* — "to set down; to place".

> हूं घड़ो भींत कनै धरूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ ghaṛo bhī̃t kanai dharū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I set the water pot down by the wall.

Past (I …): म्हैं घड़ो भींत कनै धर्यो, *mhaĩ ghaṛo bhī̃t kanai dharyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धरणो** (*dharṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dharati*, "holds, keeps"; CDIAL lists Marwari *dharṇo*, "to keep, place" (CDIAL 6747).
