## You'll want to know: evacuar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C306-RESGATE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**evacuar** — "to evacuate".

> Evacuaram a escola por causa do fumo.
>
> *They evacuated the school because of the smoke.*

Takes a direct object: *evacuar o prédio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*evacuar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēvacuāre* "to empty out" (EtymDB), built on *vacuus* "empty".
