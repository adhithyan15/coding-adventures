Spanish, chapter 540: A finales de año.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a principios de — at the beginning of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the millennium, then the Spanish for the period.

You'll want to know: a principios de.
a principios de — "at the beginning of".
Llegaron a principios de junio. They arrived at the beginning of June.
Used with months, years and centuries: a principios del siglo XX.

The word, taken apart.
a principios de is built on principio, from Latin prīncipium 'a beginning' (Lewis & Short), which comes from prīnceps 'first'.

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for at the beginning of, then the Spanish for in the middle of.

You'll want to know: a finales de.
a finales de — "at the end of".
Se jubiló a finales del año pasado. He retired at the end of last year.
A fines de is also common in Latin America.

The word, taken apart.
a finales de is built on final, a learned word from Latin fīnālis, which comes from fīnis 'end, boundary' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
semanal — weekly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for in the middle of, then the Spanish for at the end of.

You'll want to know: semanal.
semanal — "weekly".
El mercado semanal es los jueves. The weekly market is on Thursdays.

The word, taken apart.
semanal is built on semana 'week' with the ending -al (EtymDB). Semana is inherited from Late Latin septimāna 'a set of seven', built on septem 'seven'.
