## You'll want to know: आधार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C345-NISHKARSH345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आधार** — *ādhār* — "a basis; support".

> तुमच्या मताला काय आधार आहे?
>
> *tumacyā matālā kāy ādhār āhe?*
>
> What basis does your view have?

Masculine.

X ला आधार देणे, to support X; च्या आधारावर, on the basis of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आधार** (*ādhār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ādhāra*, "support, base" (CDIAL 1165); the same entry lists an inherited Marathi *āhārā*, a ring of grass that holds a pot steady.
