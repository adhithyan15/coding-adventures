# రాణి (rāṇi) — a queen

## You'll want to know: రాణి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C327-RAJYAM327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**రాణి** — *rāṇi* — "a queen".

> రాణి రాజుతో అడవికి వెళ్ళింది.
>
> *rāṇi rājutō aḍaviki veḷḷindi.*
>
> The queen went to the forest with the king.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రాణి** (*rāṇi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rājñī*, "a queen" (CDIAL 10692), the feminine of *rājan*, "a king".
