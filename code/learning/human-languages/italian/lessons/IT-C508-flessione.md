## You'll want to know: la flessione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C508-PALESTRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la flessione** — "push-up; bending".

> Ogni mattina faccio venti flessioni.
>
> *Every morning I do twenty push-ups.*

Also *il piegamento*. In grammar, *la flessione* is inflection.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flessione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *flexiō* "a bending" (Lewis & Short), from *flectere* "to bend".
