# そして (soshite) — and; joining two sentences

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "after that, I went out". (*sorekara, dekakemashita*.)

## You'll want to know: そして
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C252-SOSHITE-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C42-NATURE42-02, JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-02] -->

**そして** — *soshite* — "and", at the start of a sentence. **それから** says what came next in time; **そして** adds the next thing.

> **うみに いきました。**
>
> *umi ni ikimashita.* — "I went to the sea."

> **そして、およぎました。**
>
> *soshite, oyogimashita.* — "And I swam."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そして** (*soshite*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. It is usually explained as **そ** (*so*), "that way", the same *so-* as in **それ**, with **して** (*shite*), the **-て** form of **する**. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana (JMdict).
