## You'll want to know: さんぽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C239-PASTIME239-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さんぽ** — *sanpo* — "a walk; a stroll".

> **けさ いぬと こうえんを さんぽしました。** — *kesa inu to kōen o sanpo shimashita* — "This morning I took the dog for a walk in the park."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さんぽ** (*sanpo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *san*, "scatter" (Middle Chinese *sanX*) and *po*, "step, walk" (*buH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
