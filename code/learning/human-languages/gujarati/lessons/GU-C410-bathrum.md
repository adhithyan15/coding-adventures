## You'll want to know: બાથરૂમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C410-BATH410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બાથરૂમ** — *bāthrūm* — "a bathroom".

> અમારા ઓરડામાં બાથરૂમ નથી.
>
> *amārā orḍāmā̃ bāthrūm nathī.*
>
> There is no bathroom in our room.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બાથરૂમ** (*bāthrūm*) is borrowed from English *bathroom*.
