## You'll want to know: انتقم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C344-HIKAYA344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقم** — *intaqama* — "to take revenge".

> انتقم البطل من الساحر الشرير.
>
> *intaqama al-baṭal min as-sāḥir ash-shirrīr.*
>
> The hero took revenge on the wicked magician.

Present: ينتقم, *yantaqim*.

It takes من: انتقم من.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقم** (*intaqama*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-m* (Lane), "to take vengeance, to punish". It is Form VIII, "to punish for oneself".
