## You'll want to know: gemitus, gemitūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C491-LACR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gemitus, gemitūs** — "a groan, moan".

> Ē cubiculō gemitūs audīmus.
>
> *We hear groans from the bedroom.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *gemō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**gemitus** is the action noun of *gemō* "to groan", with the fourth-declension ending *-tus* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB derives it from *gemō*).
