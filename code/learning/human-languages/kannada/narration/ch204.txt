Kannada, chapter 204: To publish, To arrange, To measure, To have fun, To compete.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸು (prakaṭisu) — to publish, to announce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to blow, then the Kannada for to melt.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸು.
ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸು — prakaṭisu — "to publish, to announce".

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸು (prakaṭisu) is Sanskrit prakaṭa, "manifest, visible" (CDIAL 8428), made into a Kannada verb with -isu: "to make manifest".

The word, taken apart.
ಜೋಡಿಸು (jōḍisu) is jōḍi, "a pair, a set", made into a verb with -isu. Jōḍi came through Hindustani jōṛ, "a joining"; its deeper origin is not settled.

ಅಳೆ — aḷe — "to measure".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಳೆ (aḷe) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil aḷa and Telugu alavi (DEDR 295). DEDR lists aḷe itself, "to measure".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಜಾ ಮಾಡು (majā māḍu) — to have fun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಮಜಾ ಮಾಡು.
ಮಜಾ ಮಾಡು — majā māḍu — "to have fun".

The word, taken apart.
ಮಜಾ ಮಾಡು (majā māḍu) is majā, "fun, enjoyment", from Persian maza, "taste, relish", which came through Hindustani, and native māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).
