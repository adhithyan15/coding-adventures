## You'll want to know: o orientador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C377-DOCENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o orientador** — "the thesis supervisor".

> O meu orientador lê tudo o que escrevo.
>
> *My supervisor reads everything I write.*

Feminine *a orientadora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orientador* is built on *orientar* "to guide", on *oriente* "east", from Latin *oriēns* "rising (sun)", on *orīrī* "to rise" (Lewis & Short): to find the east is to find your way.
