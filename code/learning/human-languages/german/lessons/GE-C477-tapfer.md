## You'll want to know: tapfer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C477-MUT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**tapfer** — "brave".

> Das Kind war beim Arzt sehr tapfer.
>
> *The child was very brave at the doctor's.*

The noun: *die Tapferkeit* — bravery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tapfer* is inherited from Middle High German *tapfer* and Old High German *tapfar*, from Proto-Germanic *dapraz* "stout, solid, heavy" (EtymDB); the sense moved from firm to brave.
