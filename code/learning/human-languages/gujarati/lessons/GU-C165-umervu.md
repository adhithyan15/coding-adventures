## You'll want to know: ઉમેરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C165-STIR165-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉમેરવું** — *umervũ* — "to add".

> **તે ચામાં ખાંડ ઉમેરે છે.** — *te cāmā̃ khā̃ḍ umere che* — "He adds sugar to his tea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉમેરવું** (*umervũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*ummēr*, "join", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to add, augment" (CDIAL 2347).
