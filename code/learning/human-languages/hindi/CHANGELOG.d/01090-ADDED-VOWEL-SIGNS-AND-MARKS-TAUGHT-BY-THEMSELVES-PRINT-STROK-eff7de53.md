## Added — vowel signs and marks taught by themselves print stroke-order filmstrips

ु, ू, े, ं, ृ, ँ, ् and ़ now have a cited pen path, and the book prints a
stroke-order filmstrip for the lessons that teach one of them by itself. How
many strokes each sign takes, where it starts and which way it moves come from
native writers' pen traces in HP Labs India's LipiTk Devanagari recognizer;
the paths themselves are fitted to the book's Noto Sans Devanagari letters.
The underlying data is licensed for research only, so only counts and shares
are cited.

- **One sign at a time.** Each strip draws the sign alone, as the writers
  wrote it: no consonant, and no headline the printed sign does not carry.
- **New filmstrips (10 lessons, 44 → 54):** HI-S05-sign-virama (्),
  HI-S112-vowel-sign-e (े), HI-S118-sign-candrabindu (ँ), HI-S120-vowel-sign-u
  (ु), HI-S140-nuqta (़), HI-S144-vowel-sign-vocalic-r (ृ),
  HI-S148-sign-anusvara (ं), HI-S149-vowel-sign-uu (ू), HI-W12-chandrabindu
  (ँ), HI-W12-u-matra (ु).
- **Not yet:** HI-S06-vowel-sign-aa (ा), HI-W128-vowel-sign-i (ि),
  HI-S116-vowel-sign-ii and HI-W12-ii-matra (ी), HI-S150-vowel-sign-o (ो),
  HI-S134-vowel-sign-au (ौ) and HI-W12-ai-matra (ै) still print none;
  HI-W03-preposed-i has no Writing or Script block to hold one. The bundled
  Noto Sans Devanagari prints ा, ि, ी, ो and ः with a short piece of headline
  that the native traces never draw, so a path that follows the traces leaves
  too much of the printed sign untraced; ै and ौ have no clear majority among
  the writers.
- **No words.** The writers wrote each sign alone, with no consonant and no
  headline, so the source does not say when the sign is written against its
  consonant or the headline. A sign on a consonant (कि, कु) and every word
  with a sign still print no filmstrip.
- Lesson prose, narration and durations do not change: filmstrips are printed
  in the book only.
