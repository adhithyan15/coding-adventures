Lesson 2 of 5.
ટેવ (ṭev) — a habit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an owner, then the Gujarati for experience.

You'll want to know: ટેવ.
ટેવ (ṭev) — "a habit".

The word, taken apart.
ટેવ (ṭev) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ṭev, "appoint, fix", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5475). CDIAL lists Gujarati ṭev, "habit", under it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
આદત (ādat) — a habit, a custom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for experience, then the Gujarati for a habit.

You'll want to know: આદત.
આદત (ādat) — "a habit, a custom".

The word, taken apart.
આદત (ādat) is a loan from Arabic ʿāda, "custom, habit", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is ʿ-w-d (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
લાયકાત (lāyakāt) — a qualification.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a habit, then the Gujarati for a habit.

You'll want to know: લાયકાત.
લાયકાત (lāyakāt) — "a qualification".

The word, taken apart.
લાયકાત (lāyakāt) is a loan, usually traced to Persian liyāqat, "fitness, worth", from Arabic, beside lāyak, "fit". The Arabic root is l-y-q (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
કળા (kaḷā) — an art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a habit, then the Gujarati for a qualification.

You'll want to know: કળા.
કળા (kaḷā) — "an art".

The word, taken apart.
કળા (kaḷā) is Sanskrit kalā, "any practical art", lightly reshaped with Gujarati ḷ, a semi-tatsama. CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word from Dravidian. The only Gujarati word it lists there is kaḷ, "door-handle, gadget, scheme" (CDIAL 2927).
