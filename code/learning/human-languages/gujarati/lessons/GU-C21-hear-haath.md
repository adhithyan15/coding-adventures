# હાથ — hear hand first

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-HAATH-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-GHAR-01, GU-PERFORMANCE-PLACES-THREE-FOUR-SKILL-01] -->

Say home. Now listen twice: **hāth** means **hand**. Keep its long vowel and
final puff distinct from *ghar*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-HAATH-01] -->

Touch your hand when you hear *hāth*, then say *hāth* once from the meaning.
