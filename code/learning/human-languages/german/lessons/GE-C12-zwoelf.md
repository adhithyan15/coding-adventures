# zwölf — "twelve"

## You'll want to know: zwölf
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-ZWOELF-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-ZWEI-01, GE-LEX-ELF-01] -->

> **zwölf** — "twelve."

The second number to refuse the pattern, and the last. After this one German
turns regular and stays regular.

You can hear *zwei* inside it if you listen for the opening: *zw-* is the same
start.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-SOUND-UMLAUT-OE-01, GE-SOUND-W-AS-V-01, GE-LEX-ZWOELF-01] -->

- `z-ts` — German *z* is **ts**.
- `w-as-v` — German *w* is a **v**, as on *Winter* and *Wasser*.
- `umlaut-oe` — and *ö* is the rounded vowel from *schön*: shape your lips for
  *oh* and say *eh*.

So the word opens *tsv-* and runs *tsvölf*. Three rules, one syllable, nothing
new.
