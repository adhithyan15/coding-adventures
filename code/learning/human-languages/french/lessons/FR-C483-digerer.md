## You'll want to know: digérer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C483-NAUSEE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**digérer** — "to digest".

> Je ne digère pas bien le lait.
>
> *I do not digest milk well.*

Also to take in bad news: *il n'a pas digéré la défaite*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*digérer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dīgerere* "to divide, to arrange, to digest", built on *gerere* "to carry" (Lewis & Short).
