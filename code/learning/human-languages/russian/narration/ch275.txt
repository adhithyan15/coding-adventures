Russian, chapter 275: Delays and coincidences.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
затягиваться (zatyágivatsya) — to drag on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for later on, then the Russian for at the very end.

You'll want to know: затягиваться.
затягиваться (zatyágivatsya) — "to drag on".
Собрание затянулось до вечера. Sobrániye zatyanúlos do véchera. The meeting dragged on until the evening.
Imperfective; perfective затянуться (zatyanútsya). Of a wound it means "to heal over".

The word, taken apart.
затягиваться (zatyágivatsya) is built on за- and тянуть "to pull", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic tęgnǫti, from PIE tengʰ- "to pull" (EtymDB): to be pulled out long.

Lesson 2 of 5.
откладывать (otkládyvat) — to put off, to postpone; to save up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for at the very end, then the Russian for to drag on.

You'll want to know: откладывать.
откладывать (otkládyvat) — "to put off, to postpone; to save up".
Они откладывали поездку три раза. Oní otkládyvali poyézdku tri ráza. They put off the trip three times.
Imperfective; perfective отложить (otlozhít). Also "to put money aside": откладывать (otkládyvat) деньги.

The word, taken apart.
откладывать (otkládyvat) is built on от- "away" and the stem клад- of класть "to put", an inherited Slavic verb (Proto-Slavic klasti): to put to one side. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

Папа сегодня задерживается на работе. Pápa sevódnya zadérzhivayetsya na rabóte. Dad is staying late at work today.

Lesson 4 of 5.
повторяться (povtoryátsya) — to happen again, to recur.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to put off, then the Russian for to be delayed.

You'll want to know: повторяться.
повторяться (povtoryátsya) — "to happen again, to recur".
Такая история больше не повторится. Takáya istóriya bólshe ne povtorítsya. A story like this will not happen again.
Imperfective; perfective повториться (povtorítsya). Повторять is to repeat something; with -ся the event itself comes back.

The word, taken apart.
повторяться (povtoryátsya) is built on по- and второй "second", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic vŭtorŭ "second" (EtymDB): to happen a second time.

Наш отпуск совпал с их отпуском. Nash ótpusk sovpál s ikh ótpuskom. Our holiday coincided with theirs.
Imperfective; perfective совпасть (sovpást), past совпал. It takes с + instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
совпадать (sovpadát) is built on со- "together" and падать "to fall", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic padati (EtymDB): to fall together.
