## You'll want to know: derreter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C464-GEADA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**derreter** — "to melt".

> Com o sol, a neve derrete depressa.
>
> *In the sun, the snow melts quickly.*

Ice cream *derrete* too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derreter* is usually traced to Latin *dēterere* 'to rub away' (Lewis & Short), but the origin is not settled; EtymDB has no entry for it.
