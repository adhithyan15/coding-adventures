## You'll want to know: ગરબો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C261-KITES261-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગરબો** — *garbo* — "garba, a circle dance".

> **અમે રાત્રે ગરબો ગાયો.** — *ame rātre garbo gāyo* — "We sang a garba at night."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ગરબો** (*garbo*) is not settled. A link with Sanskrit *gárbha*, "womb, inside", is often proposed, but CDIAL lists no Gujarati *garbo* under that word (CDIAL 4055).
