# ಉಣಿಸು (uṇisu) — to feed (a child or the sick)

## You'll want to know: ಉಣಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C513-CHETARIKE513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಣಿಸು** — *uṇisu* — "to feed (a child or the sick)".

> ಅಮ್ಮ ಜ್ವರ ಬಂದ ಮಗನಿಗೆ ಮೆತ್ತಗಿನ ಅನ್ನ ಉಣಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *amma jvara banda maganige mettagina anna uṇisidaru.*
>
> Mother fed her feverish son soft rice.

Past (I …): ಉಣಿಸಿದೆ, *uṇiside*.

The one fed takes -ಗೆ. It is the causative of the old verb ಉಣ್, to eat a meal, as in ಊಟ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಣಿಸು** (*uṇisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 600 lists Kannada *uṇ*, *uṇṇu*, "to eat a meal; of children, to take mother's milk"; -*isu* makes "to make someone eat".
