## You'll want to know: ਗਾਹੁਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C305-HARVEST305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਹੁਣਾ** — *gāhuṇā* — "to thresh; to tread out grain".

> ਬਲਦ ਕਣਕ ਗਾਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *balad kaṇak gāhunde han.*
>
> The oxen thresh the wheat.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਣਕ ਗਾਹੀ, *maiṁ kaṇak gāhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਹੁਣਾ** (*gāhuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gāhatē*, "dives into"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gāhṇā*, "to tread out", under it (CDIAL 4152).
