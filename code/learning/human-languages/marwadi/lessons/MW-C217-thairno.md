## You'll want to know: ठैरणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C217-CROSS217-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ठैरणो** — *ṭhairṇo* — "to halt; to stay for a while".

> हूं परसूं तांई गांव मांय ठैरसूं।
>
> *hū̃ parsū̃ tā̃ī gā̃v mā̃y ṭhairsū̃.*
>
> I'll stop in the village until the day after tomorrow.

Past (he …): वो गांव मांय ठैर्यो, *vo gā̃v mā̃y ṭhairyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठैरणो** (*ṭhairṇo*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*stabhira*, "fixed, firm"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ṭhaharnā*, "to stop", and a Gujarati verb "to stay" under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 13680).
