## You'll want to know: いっぴき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C137-IPPIKI]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01, JA-SCRIPT-KI-01] -->

**いっぴき** — *ippiki* — "one small animal": one cat, one dog, one fish.
Japanese counts animals with a word of their own, the way it counts long thin
things with **いっぽん**.

The new sign is spent at once. **い**, a small **っ**, **ぴ** and **き**: four
beats, *i–p–pi–ki*. The small **っ** holds a beat before the *p*, as it does
in **いっぱい**. You write every sign in it now.
