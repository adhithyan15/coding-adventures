## You'll want to know: छल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C339-RAJKUMAR339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छल** — *chal* — "trickery; deceit".

> राक्षस ने छल से राजकुमार को पकड़ा।
>
> *rākṣas ne chal se rājkumār ko pakṛā.*
>
> The demon caught the prince by trickery.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छल** (*chal*) is Sanskrit *chala*, "fraud, a trick" (Monier-Williams). It belongs to tales and to writing: **छल से**, by a trick.
