## You'll want to know: سہارا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C374-SAHARA374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سہارا** — *sahārā* — "a support; someone to lean on".

> اگر آپ کا سہارا نہ ہوتا تو میں گر جاتا۔
>
> *agar āp kā sahārā na hotā to maiṅ gir jātā.*
>
> If you had not held me up, I would have fallen.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سہارا** (*sahārā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sahārā*, "help", under Sanskrit *sahakāra*, "assistance" (CDIAL 13299).
