# दुख (dukh) — sorrow, grief

## You'll want to know: दुख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C109-MOOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दुख** (*dukh*) — "sorrow, grief".

**मुझे दुख है** — "I am sorry", said in sympathy, when someone tells you bad news.
It is not the **माफ़ कीजिए** of an apology.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The sympathy sorry, apart from the apology sorry.
