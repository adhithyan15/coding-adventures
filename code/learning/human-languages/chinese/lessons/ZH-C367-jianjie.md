## You'll want to know: 见解

**见解** — *jiànjiě* — "insight; considered view".

> **他的见解很有意思** *tā de jiànjiě hěn yǒu yìsi* — His views are interesting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**见解** (*jiànjiě*) is built from 见 'to see' and 解 'to untie', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kenH* and *keaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to see' and the second 'to explain'.
