## You'll want to know: کھاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C512-KYARI512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کھاد** — *khād* — "manure; fertiliser".

> مالی نے کیاری میں کھاد ملائی۔
>
> *mālī ne kyārī meṅ khād milāī.*
>
> The gardener mixed manure into the flower bed.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھاد** (*khād*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *khād*, "manure", under Sanskrit *khāta*, "dug up" (CDIAL 3862).
