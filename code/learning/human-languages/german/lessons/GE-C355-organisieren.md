## You'll want to know: organisieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C355-PROJEKT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**organisieren** — "to organize".

> Meine Kollegin organisiert das Fest für die ganze Firma.
>
> *My colleague is organizing the party for the whole firm.*

Perfekt: *hat organisiert*, with no *ge-*. *Sich organisieren* — to get organized.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*organisieren* was borrowed from French *organiser* (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *organizāre*, built on Latin *organum* "instrument, tool", from Greek *órganon*.
