Russian, chapter 434: Making a complaint.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
жалоба (zháloba) — a complaint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a safe, then the Russian for a fault.

You'll want to know: жалоба.
жалоба (zháloba) — "a complaint".
Туристы подали жалобу на шум. Turísty podáli zhálobu na shum. The tourists made a complaint about the noise.
Feminine. Takes на + accusative: жалоба (zháloba) на соседей.

The word, taken apart.
жалоба (zháloba) goes with жалеть 'to pity' and жаль 'a pity': to complain is to call for pity. EtymDB lists жалоба (zháloba) with the sense 'complaint' but without a source.

Lesson 2 of 6.
жаловаться (zhálovatsya) — to complain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fault, then the Russian for a complaint.

You'll want to know: жаловаться.
жаловаться (zhálovatsya) — "to complain".
Дед всё время жалуется на погоду. Ded vsyo vrémya zháluyetsya na pogódu. Grandad complains about the weather all the time.
Imperfective; the perfective is пожаловаться. Present жалуюсь, жалуешься. На + accusative for what, the dative for whom.

The word, taken apart.
жаловаться (zhálovatsya) goes with жалоба (zháloba) 'a complaint' and жалеть 'to pity'. EtymDB lists жалоба (zháloba) and жалеть without a deeper source.

Lesson 3 of 6.
претензия (preténziya) — a formal complaint, a claim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a complaint, then the Russian for to complain.

You'll want to know: претензия.
претензия (preténziya) — "a formal complaint, a claim".
Мы отправили претензию в турфирму. My otprávili preténziyu v turfírmu. We sent a formal complaint to the travel agency.
Feminine. У меня к вам претензия (preténziya) — I have a complaint to make to you.

The word, taken apart.
претензия (preténziya) is usually traced, probably through German, to Medieval Latin praetēnsiō 'a claim', from praetendere 'to stretch out, to put forward' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it, so the route is hedged.

Lesson 4 of 6.
недовольный (nedovólnyy) — dissatisfied, displeased.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to complain, then the Russian for a formal complaint.

You'll want to know: недовольный.
недовольный (nedovólnyy) — "dissatisfied, displeased".
Недовольный клиент долго спорил с кассиром. Nedovólnyy kliyént dólgo spóril s kassírom. The dissatisfied customer argued with the cashier for a long time.
Takes the instrumental: недоволен номером — unhappy with the room.

The word, taken apart.
недовольный (nedovólnyy) is built from не- 'not' and довольный 'content, satisfied' (EtymDB lists both); довольный goes with довольно 'enough'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
неисправный (neisprávnyy) — out of order, faulty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a formal complaint, then the Russian for dissatisfied.

You'll want to know: неисправный.
неисправный (neisprávnyy) — "out of order, faulty".
В номере был неисправный холодильник. V nómere byl neisprávnyy kholodílnik. There was a fridge in the room that was out of order.
The opposite is исправный — in working order.

The word, taken apart.
неисправный (neisprávnyy) is built from не- 'not' and исправный 'in good order', from исправить 'to put right', on править 'to set right'. EtymDB lists неисправный (neisprávnyy) without a deeper source.
