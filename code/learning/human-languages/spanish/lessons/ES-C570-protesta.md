## You'll want to know: la protesta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C570-PROTESTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la protesta** — "the protest".

> Hubo protestas en todo el país.
>
> *There were protests all over the country.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protesta* is built on *protestar*, from Latin *prōtestārī* 'to declare in public, to bear witness' (Lewis & Short), built on *testis* 'a witness'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
