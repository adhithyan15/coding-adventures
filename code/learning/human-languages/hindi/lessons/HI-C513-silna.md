## You'll want to know: सिलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C513-DARZI513-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिलना** — *silnā* — "to be stitched; to get sewn".

> मेरी नई कमीज़ कल तक सिल जाएगी।
>
> *Merī naī kamīz kal tak sil jāegī.*
>
> My new shirt will be stitched by tomorrow.

Past (it …): वह कुर्ता सिल गया, *vah kurtā sil gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिलना** (*silnā*) is the intransitive partner of *sīnā*, "to sew": CDIAL files both under Sanskrit *sīvyati*, "sews" (CDIAL 13444).
