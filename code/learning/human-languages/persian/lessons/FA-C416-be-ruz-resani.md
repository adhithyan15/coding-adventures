## You'll want to know: به روز رسانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C416-KARBAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**به روز رسانی** (*be ruz resâni*) — "an update".

> کامپیوتر من یک به روز رسانی تازه گرفت.
>
> *kâmpyuter-e man yek be ruz resâni-ye tâze gereft.*
>
> My computer got a new update.

*Be ruz* "up to date" + *resâni* "bringing", from *resândan*. *Be ruz resâni kardan* — to update.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به روز رسانی** is a modern native coinage, literally "bringing to the day": *ruz* "day" is traced by EtymDB through Old Persian, and *resâni* is built on *resân-*, the causative stem of *residan* "to arrive".
