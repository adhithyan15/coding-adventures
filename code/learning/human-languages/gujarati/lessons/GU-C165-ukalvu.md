# ઉકાળવું (ukāḷvũ) — to boil

## You'll want to know: ઉકાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C165-STIR165-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉકાળવું** — *ukāḷvũ* — "to boil".

> **માતા સવારે દૂધ ઉકાળે છે.** — *mātā savāre dūdh ukāḷe che* — "Mother boils the milk in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉકાળવું** (*ukāḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to cause to boil", under Sanskrit *utkālayati*, "drives out", in the entry for *\*utkalati*, "goes out or up", a reconstructed form that gives the intransitive *ukaḷvũ*, "to boil" (CDIAL 1716).
