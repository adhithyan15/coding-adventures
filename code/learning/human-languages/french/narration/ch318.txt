French, chapter 318: Le feu d'artifice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le feu d'artifice — the fireworks (display).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the float, then the French for to dress up.

You'll want to know: le feu d'artifice.
le feu d'artifice — "the fireworks (display)".
Le feu d'artifice a commencé à vingt-trois heures. The fireworks started at eleven at night.
Plural: les feux d'artifice.

The word, taken apart.
feu is inherited from Latin focus "hearth" (EtymDB), and artifice is a learned borrowing from Latin artificium "craft, skill", from ars and facere (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the fireworks, then the French for the firecracker.

You'll want to know: le bal.
le bal — "the dance, the ball".
Les jeunes dansent au bal du village. The young people dance at the village dance.
Le bal populaire — the open-air public dance, a summer tradition.

The word, taken apart.
bal is built on Old French baller "to dance", which is inherited from Late Latin ballāre (EtymDB).

Il y a des guirlandes dans toutes les rues. There are strings of lights in every street.
