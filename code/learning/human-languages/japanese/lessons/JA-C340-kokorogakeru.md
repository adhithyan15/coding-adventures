## You'll want to know: こころがける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C340-JOGEN340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こころがける** — *kokorogakeru* — "to keep in mind; to make a point of".

> **けんこうの ために、はやく ねる ことを こころがけて ください。** — *kenkō no tame ni, hayaku neru koto o kokorogakete kudasai* — "For your health, make a point of going to bed early."

Polite **こころがけます**, *kokorogakemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あには あさの うんどうを こころがけました。**, *ani wa asa no undō o kokorogakemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こころがける** (*kokorogakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *kokoro*, "heart" and *kakeru*, "to hang", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
