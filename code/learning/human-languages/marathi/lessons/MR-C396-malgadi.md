## You'll want to know: मालगाडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C396-AAGGADI396-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मालगाडी** — *mālgāḍī* — "a goods train".

> रात्री एक लांब मालगाडी गेली.
>
> *rātrī ek lāmb mālgāḍī gelī.*
>
> A long goods train went past at night.

Feminine.

It carries freight, not people; a passenger train is a प्रवासी गाडी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मालगाडी** (*mālgāḍī*) is a Marathi compound of **माल**, "goods", a loan usually traced to Arabic *māl*, "property, goods", and **गाडी**, "a cart, a train" (CDIAL 4116).
