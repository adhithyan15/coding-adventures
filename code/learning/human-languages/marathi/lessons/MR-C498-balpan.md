## You'll want to know: बालपण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C498-VAY498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बालपण** — *bālpaṇ* — "childhood".

> माझं बालपण गावात गेलं.
>
> *mājhaṁ bālpaṇ gāvāt gelaṁ.*
>
> I spent my childhood in the village.

Neuter.

बाल, a child, + -पण, -hood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बालपण** (*bālpaṇ*) is a Marathi derivation: बाल, "child", a tatsama of Sanskrit *bāla* (CDIAL 9216), with *-paṇ*, "-hood".
