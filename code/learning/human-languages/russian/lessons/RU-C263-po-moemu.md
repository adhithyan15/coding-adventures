# по-моему and мне кажется — an opinion, three ways

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-DUMAYU-CHTO-01, RU-LEX-C169-VESHCH169-03, RU-LEX-RASSKAZAT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think that you understand". (*Ya dúmayu, shto ty
ponimáyesh.*) Then the word for "an opinion". (*Mnéniye.*)

[PAUSE 1s] A story told with *рассказать* says what happened. Giving
reasons starts one step earlier, with what you think of it.

## Grammar Lens: three frames
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-PO-MOEMU-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-DUMAT, RU-GRAMMAR-CHTO-COMPLEMENTISER-01, RU-LEX-C64-COLOUR2-03, RU-LEX-C113-THINGS113-02, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01] -->

**Я думаю, что …** is the plain frame, and it needs **что** and its comma.

**По-моему, …** — *po-móyemu* — "in my view". It takes no *что*; a comma
follows it, and then the opinion:

> **По-моему, это хороший фильм.** — *po-móyemu, éto khoróshiy film* — In
> my view, it's a good film.

## Grammar Lens: мне кажется, что …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-KAZHETSYA-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C126-QUAL126-05, RU-GRAMMAR-CHTO-COMPLEMENTISER-01] -->

**Мне кажется, что …** — *mne kázhetsya, shto* — "it seems to me that".
It is softer than *я думаю*: less sure, and politer when you disagree.

> **Мне кажется, что фильм скучный.** — *mne kázhetsya, shto film
> skúchnyy* — It seems to me the film is boring.

**Мне** is "to me", a form of *я*. The person something seems to is not
the subject here: the opinion is.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PO-MOEMU-01, RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-KAZHETSYA-01, RU-LEX-TOZHE-01] -->

> **— По-моему, это хороший фильм. А ты как думаешь?**
> **— Я тоже так думаю.**

*A ty kak dúmayesh?* — "And what do you think?" *Ya tózhe tak dúmayu* —
"I think so too." To disagree gently, the second voice says instead:
**А мне кажется, что он скучный.** *Он*, "he", stands for *фильм*, a
masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C169-VESHCH169-03] -->

**по-моему** is *по*, "by, after", with *моему*, a form of **мой**, "my",
which EtymDB 2.1 traces to Proto-Slavic *\*mojĭ*: "after my fashion".
**кажется** is *казаться*, "to show oneself, to seem", the *-ся* form of an
older *казать*, "to show". So *мне кажется* says "it shows itself to me".
