## You'll want to know: آغاز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C302-AGHAZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آغاز** (*âghâz*) — "a beginning, a start".

> آغاز کلاس ساعت هشت است.
>
> *Âghâz-e kelâs sâ'at-e hasht ast.*
>
> The start of the class is at eight o'clock.

More formal than *shoru'*. *Dar âghâz* "at the beginning".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âghâz* is a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent, and its deeper origin is not settled.
