## You'll want to know: मोटरसाइकिल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C404-TRAIFIK404-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मोटरसाइकिल** — *moṭarsāikil* — "a motorcycle".

> अरुण मोटरसाइकिल से दफ़्तर जाता है।
>
> *Aruṇ moṭarsāikil se daftar jātā hai.*
>
> Arun goes to the office by motorcycle.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोटरसाइकिल** (*moṭarsāikil*) is borrowed from English *motorcycle*; like साइकिल it is feminine in Hindi.
