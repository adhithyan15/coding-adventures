Marathi, chapter 105: Cheap, Empty, Rich, Poor, Dirt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्वस्त (svasta) — cheap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for fast, then the Marathi for expensive.

You'll want to know: स्वस्त.
स्वस्त — svasta — "cheap".
