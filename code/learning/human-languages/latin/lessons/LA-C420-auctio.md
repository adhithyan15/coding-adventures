## You'll want to know: auctiō, auctiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C420-AUCTIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**auctiō, auctiōnis** — "an auction".

> Bona eius in auctiōne vendita sunt.
>
> *His goods were sold at an auction.*

*auctiō, auctiōnis* f., built on *augeō*, to increase: the price goes up and up. English *auction*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*auctiō* is the noun built on *augeō* "to increase" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): a sale where the price keeps rising. English *auction* comes from it.
