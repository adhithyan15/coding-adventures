## You'll want to know: گھسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C391-QATAR391-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گھسنا** — *ghusnā* — "to push in; to squeeze in".

> ایک آدمی قطار میں آگے گھس گیا۔
>
> *ek ādmī qatār meṅ āge ghus gayā.*
>
> A man pushed in near the front of the queue.

Past (he …): وہ ڈبے میں گھس گیا, *voh ḍabbe meṅ ghus gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گھسنا** (*ghusnā*) is inherited from *ghuss*, "thrust in", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4492).
