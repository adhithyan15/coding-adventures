## You'll want to know: o poeta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C524-POESIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o poeta** — "poet".

> O poeta leu os seus poemas na biblioteca.
>
> *The poet read his poems in the library.*

A woman is *a poetisa* or *a poeta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poeta* comes from Latin *poēta* "a poet" (EtymDB), from Greek *poiētḗs* "a maker", built on *poieîn* "to make".
