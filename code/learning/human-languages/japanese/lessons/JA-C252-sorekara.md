# それから (sorekara) — and then; after that

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-KARA-01, JA-LEX-C132-SORE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "after eating breakfast, I went out". (*asagohan o tabete kara, dekakemashita*.) Then say "that", a thing near the listener. (*sore*.)

## You'll want to know: それから
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C252-SOREKARA-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C152-READY152-03, JA-LEX-C35-FOOD35-04] -->

**それから** — *sorekara* — "and then", "after that". It opens the next sentence, and **、** follows it.

> **あさごはんを たべました。**
>
> *asagohan o tabemashita.* — "I ate breakfast."

> **それから、でかけました。**
>
> *sorekara, dekakemashita.* — "After that, I went out."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**それから** (*sorekara*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: **それ** (*sore*), "that", and **から** (*kara*), "from", so "from that point on". JMdict lists it as an expression, "and then; after that" (JMdict).
