## You'll want to know: യാദൃച്ഛികമായി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C360-YADRCCHIKAM360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**യാദൃച്ഛികമായി** — *yādṛcchikamāyi* — "by chance".

> ചന്തയിൽ വെച്ച് ഞാൻ യാദൃച്ഛികമായി എന്റെ പഴയ അധ്യാപകനെ കണ്ടു.
>
> *cantayil veccŭ ñān yādṛcchikamāyi enṟe paḻaya adhyāpakane kaṇṭu.*
>
> At the market I met my old teacher by chance.

The -ആയി ending makes an adverb from the adjective യാദൃച്ഛികം, chance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**യാദൃച്ഛികമായി** (*yādṛcchikamāyi*) is built from Sanskrit *yādṛcchika*, "spontaneous, accidental, unexpected" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam adverb ending -ആയി.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
