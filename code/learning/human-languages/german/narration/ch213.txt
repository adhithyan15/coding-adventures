Lesson 1 of 5.
eifersüchtig — jealous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for urgent, then the German for lonely.

You'll want to know: eifersüchtig.
eifersüchtig — "jealous".

The word, taken apart.
eifersüchtig is the word for "zeal" + the word for "sickness, craving", Old High German suht (EtymDB 2.1), + -ig: sick with zeal, jealous. English sick is a cousin of that word.

The word, taken apart.
erfolgreich is the word for "success", from a verb "to follow, to result", + reich, "rich" (EtymDB 2.1): rich in success. The "follow" verb is the same as English follow.
