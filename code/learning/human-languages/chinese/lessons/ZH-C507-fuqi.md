## You'll want to know: fūqī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C507-FATHER507-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fūqī* — "married couple; husband and wife".

> *tāmen zuò le sìshí nián fūqī* — They've been married for forty years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fūqī* is built from *fū* 'a grown man' and *qī* 'a wife', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pju* and *tshej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a grown man' and the second with a word of like sound meaning 'level'.
