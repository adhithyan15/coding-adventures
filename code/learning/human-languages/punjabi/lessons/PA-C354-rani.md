## You'll want to know: ਰਾਣੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C354-STORY354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਣੀ** — *rāṇī* — "a queen".

> ਕਹਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਸਿਆਣੀ ਰਾਣੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *kahāṇī vich ikk siāṇī rāṇī hai.*
>
> There is a wise queen in the story.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਣੀ** (*rāṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rājñī*, "queen"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *rāṇī*, "queen", under it (CDIAL 10692).
