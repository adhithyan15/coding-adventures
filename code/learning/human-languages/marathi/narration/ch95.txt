Marathi, chapter 95: Word, Game, Letter, Picture, Tree.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शब्द (shabda) — a word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a question, then the Marathi for an answer.

You'll want to know: शब्द.
शब्द — shabda — "a word".

The word, taken apart.
शब्द (shabda) is Sanskrit śabda, "articulate sound, noise", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 12298). Marathi uses it for "a word".

Lesson 2 of 5.
खेळ (kheḷ) — a game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an answer, then the Marathi for a word.

You'll want to know: खेळ.
खेळ — kheḷ — "a game".

The word, taken apart.
खेळ (kheḷ) is inherited from a reconstructed khēl-, "play" (CDIAL 3918), the word inside खेळणे, "to play". CDIAL links it with Sanskrit krīḍati, "plays": if that verb has a non-Aryan source, these forms may share it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
झाड (jhāḍ) — a tree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a letter, then the Marathi for a picture.

You'll want to know: झाड.
झाड — jhāḍ — "a tree".

The word, taken apart.
झाड (jhāḍ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit jhāṭa, "forest, arbour", a word known from the old word-lists (CDIAL 5362). CDIAL reports Kuiper's view that it comes from Munda.
