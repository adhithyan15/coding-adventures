# సెలవు (selavu) — leave, a holiday

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-02, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for soon, then the Telugu for home.

## You'll want to know: సెలవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సెలవు** (*selavu*) — "leave", both a holiday and permission to go.

**ఇవాళ సెలవు** — "today is a holiday". At a formal parting, **సెలవు** alone asks leave to go,
the way English once said "by your leave".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A holiday, and the most formal way out of a room.
