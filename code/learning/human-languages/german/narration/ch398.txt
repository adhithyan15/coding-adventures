German, chapter 398: Über Land.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Landstraße — the country road, the main road (outside towns).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for at least, then the German for during.

You'll want to know: die Landstraße.
die Landstraße — "the country road, the main road (outside towns)".
Wir fahren lieber auf der Landstraße als auf der Autobahn. We prefer to drive on the country road rather than on the motorway.
Plural: die Landstraßen. Slower than the Autobahn, often with villages on the way.

The word, taken apart.
Landstraße is a compound of Land 'land, open country', inherited from Proto-Germanic landą (EtymDB), and Straße 'street', an early borrowing from Latin (via) strāta 'paved road' (Lewis & Short) that reached Middle High German as strāze (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Ausfahrt — the exit (from a motorway or a garage).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for during, then the German for the country road.

You'll want to know: die Ausfahrt.
die Ausfahrt — "the exit (from a motorway or a garage)".
Wir nehmen die nächste Ausfahrt und fahren in die Stadt. We take the next exit and drive into town.
Plural: die Ausfahrten. Ausfahrt freihalten — keep the exit clear, a sign on many garage doors.

The word, taken apart.
Ausfahrt is built from aus 'out' and Fahrt 'journey, drive', which comes from the inherited verb fahren 'to travel', Proto-Germanic faraną (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.

An der Auffahrt zur Autobahn steht ein langer Stau. There is a long traffic jam at the slip road onto the motorway.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Kreisverkehr — the roundabout (US: traffic circle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the exit, then the German for the slip road.

You'll want to know: der Kreisverkehr.
der Kreisverkehr — "the roundabout (US: traffic circle)".
Im Kreisverkehr nimmt der Bus die zweite Ausfahrt. At the roundabout the bus takes the second exit.
Plural: die Kreisverkehre. In everyday speech also der Kreisel.

The word, taken apart.
Kreisverkehr is a compound of Kreis 'circle', inherited through Middle and Old High German kreiz (EtymDB), and Verkehr 'traffic', built on kehren 'to turn'. EtymDB does not list the compound itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
abbiegen — to turn off (left or right).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the slip road, then the German for the roundabout.

You'll want to know: abbiegen.
abbiegen — "to turn off (left or right)".
Nach der Kirche biegt der Bus links ab. After the church the bus turns left.
Separable: er biegt ab. Perfekt with sein: wir sind links abgebogen.

The word, taken apart.
abbiegen is built from ab 'off, away' and biegen 'to bend', which is inherited through Middle and Old High German (biegen, biogan) from Proto-Germanic beuganą (EtymDB). English bow 'to bend' is a cousin.
