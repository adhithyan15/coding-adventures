## You'll want to know: सर्वथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C381-VISHESHATAH381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सर्वथा** — *sarvathā* — "in every way; altogether".

> यदि भवान् आगच्छति, तर्हि अहं सर्वथा साहाय्यं करिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi bhavān āgacchati, tarhi ahaṃ sarvathā sāhāyyaṃ kariṣyāmi.*
>
> If you come, I will help in every way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सर्वथा** (*sarvathā*) is built on *sarva*, "all", with the ending *-thā*, "in the manner of"; MW gives *sarvathā* "in every way, by all means, altogether" (MW).
