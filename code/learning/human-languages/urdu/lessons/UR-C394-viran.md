## You'll want to know: ویران
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C394-GARHA394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ویران** — *vīrān* — "deserted; desolate".

> رات کو یہ سڑک ویران ہوتی ہے۔
>
> *rāt ko yih saṛak vīrān hotī hai.*
>
> At night this road is deserted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *vīrān*, "ruined, laid waste"; in Urdu an empty road or an abandoned house is **ویران**.
