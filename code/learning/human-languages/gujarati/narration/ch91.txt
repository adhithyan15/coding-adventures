Gujarati, chapter 91: Welcome, Congratulations, Sorry.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
સ્વાગત (svāgat) — welcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a field, then the Gujarati for a merchant.

You'll want to know: સ્વાગત.
સ્વાગત (svāgat) — "welcome".

What you've built.
One more word for welcome, congratulations, sorry.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: svāgat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: svāgat, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say svāgat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a field, then the Gujarati for a merchant, then say svāgat again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does સ્વાગત (svāgat) mean? ("Welcome".) Say it once more.
