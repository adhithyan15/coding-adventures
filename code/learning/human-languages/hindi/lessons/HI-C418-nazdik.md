## You'll want to know: नज़दीक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C418-NAZDIK418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नज़दीक** — *nazdīk* — "close by; near".

> मेरा घर स्टेशन के नज़दीक है।
>
> *merā ghar sṭeśan ke nazdīk hai.*
>
> My house is close to the station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नज़दीक** (*nazdīk*) is usually traced to Persian *nazdīk*, "near"; like पास it follows के: घर के नज़दीक.
