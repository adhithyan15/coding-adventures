## You'll want to know: خیمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C297-RAHAT297-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خیمہ** — *khaimā* — "a tent".

> ہر خاندان کو ایک خیمہ ملا۔
>
> *har khāndān ko ek khaimā milā.*
>
> Each family got a tent.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خیمہ** (*khaimā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khayma*, "a tent".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
