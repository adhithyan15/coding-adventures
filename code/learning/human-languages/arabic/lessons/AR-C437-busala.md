## You'll want to know: بوصلة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C437-BUSALA437-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بوصلة** — *būṣala* — "a compass".

> حمل أخي بوصلة في رحلته إلى الصحراء.
>
> *ḥamala akhī būṣala fī riḥlatihi ilā aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ.*
>
> My brother took a compass on his trip to the desert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بوصلة** (*būṣala*) was borrowed from Italian *bussola* (EtymDB).
