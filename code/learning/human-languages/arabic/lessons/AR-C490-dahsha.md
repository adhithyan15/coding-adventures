## You'll want to know: دهشة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C490-DAHSHA490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دهشة** — *dahsha* — "astonishment".

> فتح الولد الهدية بدهشة.
>
> *fataḥa al-walad al-hadiyya bi-dahsha.*
>
> The boy opened the present in astonishment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دهشة** (*dahsha*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-h-sh*, "to be amazed, to be bewildered" (Lane). Dahsha is that amazed state.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
