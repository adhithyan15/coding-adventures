French, chapter 158: So, However, Anyway, On the contrary, Although.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
donc — so, therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for behind, then the French for beside.

You'll want to know: donc.
donc — "so, therefore".

The word, taken apart.
donc — its origin is uncertain. The usual account takes it to Late Latin dunc, a blend of tunc, "then", and dum, "while". (EtymDB 2.1 has its Middle French form.)

You'll want to know: pourtant.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 3 of 5.
de toute façon — anyway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for so, then the French for however.

You'll want to know: de toute façon.
de toute façon — "anyway".

The phrase, taken apart.
de toute façon — de, "in, of", toute, "every", from Latin totus, and façon, "a way", from Latin factio, factionis, "a making" (EtymDB 2.1): in every way.
