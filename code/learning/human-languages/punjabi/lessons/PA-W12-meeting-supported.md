# A plan before the goodbye

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-JALDI] -->

The old reading said that Aman and Manan will meet soon. The closing says
they will meet again. This lesson writes only the plan, not both sentences.

## Writing — supported meeting line
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-JALDI, PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **ਅਸੀਂ ਜਲਦੀ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ।** once. Point to **ਅਸੀਂ** for the two people and
**ਜਲਦੀ** for when. Keep the sentence mark after the plan.

## Guided Practice — place the line
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

In a note to Manan, this line goes after Aman's facts and before
**ਧੰਨਵਾਦ। ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ।** Do not write the whole note yet; just point to
where the line belongs.
