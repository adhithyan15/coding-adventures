## You'll want to know: चषक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C530-PADAK530-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चषक** — *caṣak* — "a trophy cup".

> जिंकलेल्या संघाला मोठा चषक मिळाला.
>
> *jiṅklelyā saṅghālā moṭhā caṣak miḷālā.*
>
> The winning team got a big trophy.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चषक** (*caṣak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *caṣaka*, "a cup, a goblet" (Monier-Williams).
