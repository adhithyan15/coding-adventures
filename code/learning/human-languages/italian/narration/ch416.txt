Italian, chapter 416: Su due ruote.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il motorino — the moped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the logbook, then the Italian for the insurance.

You'll want to know: il motorino.
il motorino — "the moped".
Vado a scuola in motorino. I go to school by moped.
A small motore; in many towns it is a teenager's first vehicle.

The word, taken apart.
motorino is a little motore 'engine', a learned borrowing from Latin mōtor 'mover', from movēre 'to move' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
il parchimetro — the parking meter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the scooter, then the Italian for to pedal.

You'll want to know: il parchimetro.
il parchimetro — "the parking meter".
Il parchimetro accetta solo monete. The parking meter takes coins only.
You pay and leave the ticket behind the windscreen: dietro il parabrezza.

The word, taken apart.
parchimetro is built from parcheggio 'parking' and -metro, a learned element that goes back through Latin metrum to Greek métron 'measure' (EtymDB): it measures your parking time.

Lesson 5 of 5.
sostare — to stop briefly, to park (for a short time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: sostare.
sostare — "to stop briefly, to park (for a short time)".
Qui è vietato sostare. No stopping here.
Divieto di sosta — no parking; una sosta is a short stop.

The word, taken apart.
sostare is inherited from Latin substāre 'to stand firm, to hold out', built from sub and stāre 'to stand' (Lewis & Short).
