## You'll want to know: o reino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C327-REINO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o reino** — "the kingdom".

> O reino era pequeno, mas rico.
>
> *The kingdom was small but rich.*

*O reino animal* — the animal kingdom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reino* goes back to Latin *rēgnum* "royal power, a kingdom" (EtymDB), from *rēx* "a king".
