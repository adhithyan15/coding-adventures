# Two familiar nouns, now with their gender

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-EXISTENTIAL-LOCATION-01, PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-03, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] From the last chapter, say **ਕਿਤਾਬ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ** once. Now say the
two familiar people words: **ਮੁੰਡਾ** (*muṇḍā*, boy), then **ਕੁੜੀ** (*kuṛī*,
girl). Nothing about their meaning or spelling is new.

## Grammar Lens: masculine and feminine nouns
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-03, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-04] -->

Punjabi nouns have grammatical gender. For this first bounded pair:

| noun | meaning | gender |
|---|---|---|
| **ਮੁੰਡਾ** — *muṇḍā* | boy | masculine |
| **ਕੁੜੀ** — *kuṛī* | girl | feminine |

The endings help with this pair, but do not turn them into a universal guessing
rule. Punjabi has masculine and feminine nouns whose endings do not advertise
their gender. Learn the gender with the noun.
