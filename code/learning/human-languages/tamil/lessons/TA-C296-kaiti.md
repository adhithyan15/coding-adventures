## You'll want to know: கைதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C296-KAVAL296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கைதி** — *kaiti* — "a prisoner".

> கைதி இரவில் சிறையிலிருந்து தப்பினான்.
>
> *kaiti iravil ciṟaiyiliruntu tappiṉāṉ.*
>
> The prisoner escaped from jail at night.

Plural கைதிகள்; it goes with கைது, an arrest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கைதி** (*kaiti*) goes with கைது, "arrest": it is usually traced to Arabic *qaydī*, "a captive", through Hindustani.
