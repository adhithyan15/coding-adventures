## You'll want to know: ਚਰਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C307-BUFF307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਰਨਾ** — *charnā* — "to graze".

> ਗਾਂ ਦਰਿਆ ਦੇ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਚਰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *gāṁ dariā de kināre chardī hai.*
>
> The cow grazes on the bank of the river.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬੱਕਰੀ ਚਰੀ, *oh bakkrī charī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਰਨਾ** (*charnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *carati*, "moves, grazes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *carnā*, "to graze", under it (CDIAL 4686).
