## You'll want to know: జాగ్రత్తపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C421-APAYAM421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**జాగ్రత్తపడు** — *jāgrattapaḍu* — "to take care; to be on guard".

> డబ్బు పోకుండా నేను జాగ్రత్తపడ్డాను.
>
> *ḍabbu pōkuṇḍā nēnu jāgrattapaḍḍānu.*
>
> I took care not to lose the money.

Past (I …): జాగ్రత్తపడ్డాను, *jāgrattapaḍḍānu*.

What you guard against takes -కుండా: పోకుండా, so that it is not lost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జాగ్రత్తపడు** (*jāgrattapaḍu*) is built from జాగ్రత్త, "care", from Sanskrit *jāgrat*, "waking" (CDIAL 5174), and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to undergo" (DEDR 3853).
