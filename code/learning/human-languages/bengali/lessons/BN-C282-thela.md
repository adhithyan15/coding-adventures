## You'll want to know: ঠেলা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C282-CHURI282-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠেলা** — *ṭhelā* — "to push; to shove".

> চোর আমাকে ঠেলে দরজা দিয়ে পালাল।
>
> *chor āmāke ṭhele dôrjā diye pālālô.*
>
> The thief shoved me aside and escaped through the door.

Past (he/she …): সেই চোর আমাকে ঠেলল, *sei chor āmāke ṭhellô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠেলা** (*ṭhelā*) is filed in CDIAL 5512 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *ṭhēḍḍ-*, 'push', which lists Bengali *ṭhelā*, 'to push'. A ঠেলাগাড়ি is a handcart, a cart you push.
