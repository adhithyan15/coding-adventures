## You'll want to know: quotiēns
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C294-IDENTIDEM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**quotiēns** — "as often as; how often".

> Quotiēns in urbem veniēbat, amīcōs vīsitābat.
>
> *As often as he came into the city, he visited his friends.*

Its partner is *totiēns*, 'so often'. English *quotient* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**quotiēns** is built on *quot*, "how many" (Lewis & Short): how many times. English *quotient* was formed from *quotiēns* (EtymDB).
