# હીંચોળવું (hī̃coḷvũ) — to rock a cradle; to rock a swing

## You'll want to know: હીંચોળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C496-DHAVVU496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હીંચોળવું** — *hī̃coḷvũ* — "to rock a cradle; to rock a swing".

> દાદી ગીત ગાઈને બાળકને હિંચકામાં હીંચોળે છે.
>
> *dādī gīt gāīne bāḷakne hinckāmā̃ hī̃coḷe che.*
>
> Grandmother sings a song and rocks the baby on the swing.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને હીંચોળ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne hī̃coḷyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હીંચોળવું** (*hī̃coḷvũ*) is built on the stem that CDIAL lists as Gujarati *hĩcvũ*, "to swing", under a reconstructed *\*hicc-* (CDIAL 14083); **હિંચકો** (*hincko*), "a swing", is its cousin.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
