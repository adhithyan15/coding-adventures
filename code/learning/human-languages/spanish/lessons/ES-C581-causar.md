## You'll want to know: causar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C581-CAUSA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**causar** — "to cause".

> La lluvia causó muchos problemas.
>
> *The rain caused a lot of problems.*

Regular -ar verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*causar* is built on *causa*, a learned borrowing from Latin *causa* 'reason, cause' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
