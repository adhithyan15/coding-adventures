# ਖ਼ਬਰ (khabar) — a piece of news

## You'll want to know: ਖ਼ਬਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C309-NEWS309-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖ਼ਬਰ** — *khabar* — "a piece of news".

> ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਚੰਗੀ ਖ਼ਬਰ ਦਿੱਤੀ।
>
> *Aman ne mainū̃ ikk chaṅgī khabar dittī.*
>
> Aman gave me a piece of good news.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖ਼ਬਰ** (*khabar*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *khabar*, "news, a report".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
