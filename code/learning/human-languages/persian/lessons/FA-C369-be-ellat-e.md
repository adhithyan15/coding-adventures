## You'll want to know: به علت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C369-ELLAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**به علت** (*be 'ellat-e*) — "owing to, because of".

> مدرسه به علت برف تعطیل شد.
>
> *madrese be 'ellat-e barf ta'til shod.*
>
> School was closed because of the snow.

Takes a noun with ezafe; for a clause, *be 'ellat-e inke* … . Formal; in speech *be khâter-e*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به علت** joins the Persian preposition *be* to *'ellat*, from Arabic *'illa* "cause" (EtymDB): by reason of.
