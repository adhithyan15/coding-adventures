## You'll want to know: वेतन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C508-NAUKRI508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वेतन** — *vetan* — "a salary, pay (formal)".

> इस नौकरी में वेतन अच्छा है, लेकिन काम बहुत है।
>
> *Is naukrī meṁ vetan acchā hai, lekin kām bahut hai.*
>
> This job pays well, but there is a lot of work.

Masculine.

A formal word; everyday speech also says तनख़्वाह.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेतन** (*vetan*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *vetana*, "wages, hire, salary" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
