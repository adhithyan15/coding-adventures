## You'll want to know: りそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C507-HOPE507-04]; assesses=[] -->

**りそう** — *risō* — "an ideal".

> **もし りそうの うちが みつかったら、かいます。** — *moshi risō no uchi ga mitsukattara, kaimasu* — "If I find my ideal house, I will buy it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りそう** (*risō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ri*, "logic" (Middle Chinese *liX*) and *sō*, "concept" (*sjangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
