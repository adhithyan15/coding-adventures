## You'll want to know: കാഴ്ച
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C484-VELLAM484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**കാഴ്ച** — *kāḻca* — "a view; a sight".

> മലമുകളിൽ നിന്നുള്ള കാഴ്ച വളരെ സുന്ദരമായിരുന്നു.
>
> *malamukaḷil ninnuḷḷa kāḻca vaḷare sundaramāyirunnu.*
>
> The view from the hilltop was very beautiful.

The noun of കാണുക, to see. It also means eyesight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കാഴ്ച** (*kāḻca*) is native Dravidian, the noun of കാണുക, to see; DEDR lists it as eyesight and a spectacle (DEDR 1443).
