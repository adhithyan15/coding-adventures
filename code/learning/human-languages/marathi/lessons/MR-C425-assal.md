## You'll want to know: अस्सल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C425-TARAJU425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्सल** — *assal* — "genuine".

> हे अस्सल सोनं आहे.
>
> *he assal sonaṁ āhe.*
>
> This is genuine gold.

It does not change: अस्सल रेशीम, अस्सल पुणेरी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्सल** (*assal*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *aṣl*, "root, origin": something true to its origin.
