## You'll want to know: die Narbe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C482-VERLETZUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Narbe** — "scar".

> Seit dem Unfall hat er eine Narbe am Knie.
>
> *Since the accident he has had a scar on his knee.*

Plural: *die Narben*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Narbe* goes back to Middle High German *narwe*; its older origin is not settled, and EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
