## You'll want to know: निशाण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C327-PRAJA327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निशाण** — *niśāṇ* — "a banner; a flag".

> किल्ल्यावर उंच निशाण फडकत होतं.
>
> *killyāvar uñc niśāṇ phaḍkat hotaṁ.*
>
> A tall banner was flying over the fort.

Neuter.

निशाण फडकणे, a flag flies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निशाण** (*niśāṇ*) is a loan from Persian *nišān*, 'a sign, a mark, a standard'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
