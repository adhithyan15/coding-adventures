## You'll want to know: ಘಾಟಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C442-HEDDARI442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘಾಟಿ** — *ghāṭi* — "a mountain pass; a ghat road".

> ಘಾಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ತಿರುವುಗಳಿವೆ.
>
> *ghāṭiyalli tumbā tiruvugaḷive.*
>
> There are many bends on the ghat road.

ಘಾಟಿ ರಸ್ತೆ, a ghat road, winds up through the hills.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘಾಟಿ** (*ghāṭi*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *ghaṭṭa*, "a landing place, a ferry", whose basic sense, Turner notes, is a passage through mountains or over a river (CDIAL 4414).
