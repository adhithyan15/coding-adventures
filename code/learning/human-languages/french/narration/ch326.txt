French, chapter 326: La libération.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
libérer — to free, to set free.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the archaeologist, then the French for the dig.

You'll want to know: libérer.
libérer — "to free, to set free".
Les soldats ont libéré la ville. The soldiers freed the town.
Also of a room: libérer la chambre — to check out.

The word, taken apart.
libérer is a learned borrowing from Latin līberāre "to set free" (EtymDB), built on līber "free".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

résister is a learned borrowing from Latin resistere "to stand back, to withstand" (EtymDB), built on sistere, from stāre "to stand".
