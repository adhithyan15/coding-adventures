## You'll want to know: ઝાડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C443-TREE443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાડ** — *jhāḍ* — "a tree".

> અમારા આંગણામાં લીમડાનું મોટું ઝાડ છે.
>
> *amārā ā̃gṇāmā̃ līmḍānũ moṭũ jhāḍ che.*
>
> There is a big neem tree in our courtyard.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાડ** (*jhāḍ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *jhāṭa*, "a thicket, an arbour"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a tree" (CDIAL 5362).
