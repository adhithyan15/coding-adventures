## You'll want to know: बाधते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C372-VIGHNA372-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बाधते** — *bādhate* — "hinders; troubles".

> यदि शब्दः मां बाधते, तर्हि अहम् अन्यत्र पठामि।
>
> *yadi śabdaḥ māṃ bādhate, tarhi aham anyatra paṭhāmi.*
>
> If the noise troubles me, I study elsewhere.

Past (it …): शब्दः मां बाधितवान्, *śabdaḥ māṃ bādhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाधते** (*bādhate*) comes from the root √bādh, "to press, to harass"; MW gives *bādhita* "pressed, oppressed" (MW).
