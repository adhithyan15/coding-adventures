# வண்டி — a dot joining two letters

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C69-GOING-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the page: you can say *vaṇḍi* and *pērundu*. Now look at the first one on the page.

## Script you'll notice: வண்டி — a dot joining two letters
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-SCRIPT-READ-VANDI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**வண்டி** brings no new shape either. What it brings is the புள்ளி doing its job **between**
two different letters, where சட்டை showed it between two of the same one.

| piece | | |
|---|---|---|
| **வ** | *va* | the letter with its built-in *a* |
| **ண்** | ண with a **புள்ளி** | closed — nothing said after it |
| **டி** | *ḍi* | ட with the **ி** sign |

> **வ · ண் · டி → வண்டி**

Read the middle two as one movement rather than two beats. That is what the புள்ளி is asking
for: ண் has no vowel of its own left, so it has nowhere to go except onto the letter after it.

- [YOU RECALL: say *vaṇḍi*, then say *pērundu*, then read **வண்டி**]
