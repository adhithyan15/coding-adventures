## You'll want to know: preiswert
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C390-VERSCHWENDUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**preiswert** — "good value, inexpensive".

> Das kleine Hotel war sauber und preiswert.
>
> *The small hotel was clean and good value.*

*Preiswert* is a compliment: worth its price. *Billig* can sound cheap and poor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preiswert* joins *Preis* "price", from Old French *pris* and Latin *pretium* "price, value" (Lewis & Short), to *wert* "worth", Proto-Germanic *werþaz* (EtymDB): worth its price.
