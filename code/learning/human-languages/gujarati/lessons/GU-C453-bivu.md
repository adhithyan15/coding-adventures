# બીવું (bīvũ) — to be scared

## You'll want to know: બીવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C453-BIK453-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બીવું** — *bīvũ* — "to be scared".

> નાની બહેન અંધારામાં બીએ છે.
>
> *nānī bahen andhārāmā̃ bīe che.*
>
> My little sister is scared of the dark.

Past (he …): તે બીધો, *te bīdho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બીવું** (*bīvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bibhēti*, "fears"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 9241). The noun **બીક** (*bīk*), "fear", belongs to the same family.
