## You'll want to know: aes aliēnum, aeris aliēnī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C418-AESALIENUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**aes aliēnum, aeris aliēnī** — "debt".

> Aere aliēnō premitur.
>
> *He is weighed down by debt.*

*aes aliēnum, aeris aliēnī* n.: other people's bronze, money that belongs to someone else. *In aere aliēnō esse*, to be in debt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aes aliēnum* is built from *aes* "bronze, money" and *aliēnus* "belonging to another" (Lewis & Short): money that is someone else's.
