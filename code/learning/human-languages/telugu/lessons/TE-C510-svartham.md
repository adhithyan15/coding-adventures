# స్వార్థం (svārthaṁ) — selfishness

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C509-MANISHI509-04, TE-LEX-C509-MANISHI509-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for laziness, then the Telugu for high spirits.

## You'll want to know: స్వార్థం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C510-GUNAM510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వార్థం** — *svārthaṁ* — "selfishness".

> అతను స్వార్థంతో అన్నీ తనకే తీసుకున్నాడు.
>
> *atanu svārthantō annī tanakē tīsukunnāḍu.*
>
> Out of selfishness he took everything for himself.

స్వార్థపరుడు, a selfish man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వార్థం** (*svārthaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *svārtha*, "one's own affair, self-interest" (Monier-Williams), from *sva*, "own", and *artha*, "aim, advantage".

## What you've built
