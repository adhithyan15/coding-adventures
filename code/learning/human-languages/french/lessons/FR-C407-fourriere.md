## You'll want to know: la fourrière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C407-STATIONNEMENT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la fourrière** — "the car pound".

> Sa voiture était mal garée et elle est partie à la fourrière.
>
> *Her car was badly parked and it was taken to the pound.*

Also the pound for stray dogs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fourrière* is usually traced to Old French *feurre* 'fodder', of Germanic origin: first the place where seized animals were kept and fed. EtymDB does not list it.
