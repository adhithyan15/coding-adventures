## You'll want to know: la tela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C521-PITTURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la tela** — "canvas; cloth".

> Ho dipinto un paesaggio su una tela grande.
>
> *I painted a landscape on a large canvas.*

Also cloth: *una borsa di tela*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tela* is inherited from Latin *tēla* "a web, a loom" (EtymDB), from *texere* "to weave".
