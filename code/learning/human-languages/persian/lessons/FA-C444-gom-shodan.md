## You'll want to know: گم شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C444-NESHANI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گم شدن** (*gom shodan*) — "to get lost; to go missing".

> در بازار بزرگ گم شدیم.
>
> *Dar bâzâr-e bozorg gom shodim.*
>
> We got lost in the big bazaar.

*gom* 'lost' + *shodan*. *Gom kardan* is to lose something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gom shodan* is built from native *gom* 'lost, missing', which EtymDB lists, and the native verb *shodan* 'to become'.
