## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**träumen** ← Old High German *troumen*, the verb of the word for "a dream", Proto-Germanic *\*draumaz* (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited, the same word as English *dream*.
