## You'll want to know: اچنبھا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C442-SURPRISE442-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اچنبھا** — *achambhā* — "an amazing thing; astonishment".

> اس کے لیے سمندر ایک اچنبھا تھا۔
>
> *us ke liye samandar ek achambhā thā.*
>
> For her the sea was an amazing sight.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اچنبھا** (*achambhā*) is inherited from *ācchambha*, a form CDIAL reconstructs for "stiffness" and so "astonishment", the state of being frozen with surprise (CDIAL 1079).
