## You'll want to know: उष्णता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C436-UKADA436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उष्णता** — *uṣṇatā* — "great heat; heat".

> दुपारी उष्णता खूप वाढते.
>
> *dupārī uṣṇatā khūp vāḍhte.*
>
> In the afternoon the heat rises a lot.

Feminine.

A formal word, also heat in science.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उष्णता** (*uṣṇatā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *uṣṇatā*, "heat", from *uṣṇa*, "hot" (Monier-Williams).
