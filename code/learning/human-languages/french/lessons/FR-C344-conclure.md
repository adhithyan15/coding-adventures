## You'll want to know: conclure
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C344-RAISONNEMENT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**conclure** — "to conclude".

> Après la réunion, nous avons conclu qu'il fallait attendre.
>
> *After the meeting, we concluded that we had to wait.*

Past participle *conclu*. *Conclure un accord* — to close a deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conclure* came into Old French as *conclure*, a borrowing from Latin *conclūdere* "to shut up, to bring to an end" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *claudere* "to shut".
