## You'll want to know: ಖಡ್ಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C349-KHADGA349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಡ್ಗ** — *khaḍga* — "a sword".

> ಆ ಯೋಧ ಖಡ್ಗ ಹಿಡಿದು ಮುಂದೆ ನಡೆದನು.
>
> *ā yōdha khaḍga hiḍidu munde naḍedanu.*
>
> That warrior walked ahead, sword in hand.

Plural: ಖಡ್ಗಗಳು, *khaḍgagaḷu*.

The everyday word is ಕತ್ತಿ; ಖಡ್ಗ belongs to stories and history.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಡ್ಗ** (*khaḍga*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *khaḍga*, "a sword, a scimitar" (Monier-Williams).
