## You'll want to know: വിധി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C369-VIDHI369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**വിധി** — *vidhi* — "a verdict; fate".

> കോടതിയുടെ വിധി ഇന്ന് വരും.
>
> *kōṭatiyuṭe vidhi innŭ varuṁ.*
>
> The court's verdict will come today.

Also fate, destiny: അത് എന്റെ വിധി ആയിരുന്നു, that was my fate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിധി** (*vidhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vidhi*, "rule", and later "fate" (CDIAL 11755). A verdict is the rule a court lays down for one case.
