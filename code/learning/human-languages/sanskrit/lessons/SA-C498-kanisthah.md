# कनिष्ठः (kaniṣṭhaḥ) — youngest

## You'll want to know: कनिष्ठः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C498-AGRAJA498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कनिष्ठः** — *kaniṣṭhaḥ* — "youngest".

> कनिष्ठा भगिनी मम प्रिया अस्ति।
>
> *kaniṣṭhā bhaginī mama priyā asti.*
>
> My youngest sister is my favourite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कनिष्ठः** (*kaniṣṭhaḥ*) MW gives "the youngest, younger born", opposed to *jyeṣṭha*, without naming a root, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
