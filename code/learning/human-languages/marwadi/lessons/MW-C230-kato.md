# कांटो (kā̃ṭo) — a thorn

## You'll want to know: कांटो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C230-SUN230-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कांटो** — *kā̃ṭo* — "a thorn".

> पग मांय कांटो है।
>
> *pag mā̃y kā̃ṭo hai.*
>
> There's a thorn in my foot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कांटो** (*kā̃ṭo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaṇṭaka*, "a thorn"; CDIAL lists Hindi *kā̃ṭā* and a Gujarati cousin, "a thorn". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2668).
