## You'll want to know: ظالم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C456-KIND456-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ظالم** — *zālim* — "cruel; oppressive".

> پرانا بادشاہ بڑا ظالم تھا۔
>
> *purānā bādshāh baṛā zālim thā.*
>
> The old king was very cruel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ظالم** (*zālim*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ẓālim*, "a wrongdoer".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
