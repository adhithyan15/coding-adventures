## You'll want to know: अकरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-AKRA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **अकरा** — *akrā* — **11**

Sanskrit **एकादश** (*ekādaśa*), literally *one-ten*. Say **अकरा** slowly: the
**अ-** at the front is the **एक** you have counted with since chapter thirteen,
and the **-रा** at the end is what is left of **दश**.

That is the pattern for everything up to eighteen. Each one is a unit in front
and a worn-down ten behind, and the **-रा** is the tell.
