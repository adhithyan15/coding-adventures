# वरना (varnā) — otherwise, or else

## You'll want to know: वरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C286-VARNA-01]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C286-NAHIN-TO-01, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-03, HI-LEX-C229-ACT229-05, HI-LEX-C64-MEASURE-03, HI-LEX-C159-QUAL159-03, HI-CONCEPT-C39-BACCHA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

**वरना** — *varnā* — "otherwise, or else".

> **जल्दी उठो, वरना ट्रेन छूट जाएगी।**
>
> *jaldī uṭho, varnā ṭren chūṭ jāegī.* — "Get up early, or you'll miss the train."

It does the work of **नहीं तो** in one word, and stands in the same place, before the warning.

- **धीरे बोलो, वरना बच्चा जाग जाएगा।** (*dhīre bolo, varnā baccā jāg jāegā.*) — Speak softly, or the child will wake up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वरना** (*varnā*) is usually traced to Persian *varna*, "otherwise". It ends like a Hindi verb in **-ना**, but it is not one, and it never changes.
