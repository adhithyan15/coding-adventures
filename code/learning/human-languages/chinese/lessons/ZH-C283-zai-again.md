## You'll want to know: 再
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C283-ZAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**再** — *zài* — "again; once more", for what is still to come.

> **我明天再来** *wǒ míngtiān zài lái* — I'll come again tomorrow.

> *qǐng zài shuō yí cì* — Please say it once more.

It is the 再 of 再见: see you again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**再** (*zài*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'twice; a second time'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ZAIJIAN, ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01, ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-03] -->

再 goes before the verb, after the person, and it looks forward: the again has not happened yet.

It is not the 在 of 我在家: the same sound, a different word, a different character.
