## You'll want to know: ખમણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C523-FARSAN523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ખમણ** — *khamaṇ* — "khaman (a steamed gram-flour snack)".

> ખમણ ચણાના લોટમાંથી બને છે.
>
> *khamaṇ caṇānā loṭmā̃thī bane che.*
>
> Khaman is made from gram flour.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખમણ** (*khamaṇ*) is a soft steamed cake of gram flour, cousin to **ઢોકળાં**. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
