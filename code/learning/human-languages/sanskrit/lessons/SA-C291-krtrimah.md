## You'll want to know: कृत्रिमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C291-VANCANA291-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कृत्रिमः** — *kṛtrimaḥ* — "fake; artificial".

> एतत् आभरणं कृत्रिमम् अस्ति।
>
> *etat ābharaṇaṃ kṛtrimam asti.*
>
> This jewellery is fake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कृत्रिमः** (*kṛtrimaḥ*) comes from the root √kṛ, "to make": MW gives *kṛtrima* as made by art, artificial, and also falsified (MW).
