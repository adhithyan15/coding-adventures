## You'll want to know: नंबर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C197-PHONE197-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नंबर** — *nambar* — "a phone number".

> थारो नंबर कांई है?
>
> *thāro nambar kāĩ hai?*
>
> What's your number?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नंबर** (*nambar*) is borrowed from English *number*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
