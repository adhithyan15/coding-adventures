## You'll want to know: ನಾಲ್ಕನೆಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C74-ORDINAL-06]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-NAALKU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C74-ORDINAL-02] -->

**ನಾಲ್ಕನೆಯ** (*nālkaneya*) — "**fourth**".

**ನಾಲ್ಕು** was the first word in this book to carry a stacked consonant, the
**ಲ್ಕ** riding under the line. The ending does not disturb it: the stack stays
exactly where it was and **-ಅನೆಯ** is added after it.

| cardinal | ordinal |
|---|---|
| **ನಾಲ್ಕು** (*nālku*) | **ನಾಲ್ಕನೆಯ** (*nālkaneya*) |

**ನಾಲ್ಕನೆಯ ಕುರ್ಚಿ** (*nālkaneya kurci*), the fourth chair.
