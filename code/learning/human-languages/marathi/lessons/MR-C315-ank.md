## You'll want to know: अंक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C315-ANK315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अंक** — *aṅk* — "a magazine issue; an issue".

> मासिकाचा नवा अंक मला खूप आवडला.
>
> *māsikācā navā aṅk malā khūp āvaḍlā.*
>
> I really liked the magazine's new issue.

Masculine.

It also means a numeral: अंक म्हणजे आकडा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंक** (*aṅk*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *aṅka*, 'a hook, a mark, a numeral' (Monier-Williams); Marathi also names a numbered issue with it.
