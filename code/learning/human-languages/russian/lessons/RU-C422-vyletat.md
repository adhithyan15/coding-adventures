## You'll want to know: вылетать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C422-VYLET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**вылетать** (*vyletát*) — "to fly out, to depart by plane".

> Мы вылетаем рано утром.
>
> *My vyletáyem ráno útrom.*
>
> We fly out early in the morning.

Imperfective; perfective *вылететь* (*вылечу, вылетишь*). *Взлетать* is the take-off itself; *вылетать* is leaving by air.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вылетать* is built from *вы-* 'out' and *летать* 'to fly'. EtymDB lists *взлёт* from the same root, Proto-Slavic *vŭzletŭ*.
