Sanskrit, chapter 458: Pride and Modesty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दर्पः (darpaḥ) — haughtiness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a loud laugh, then the Sanskrit for amusement.

You'll want to know: दर्पः.
दर्पः — darpaḥ — "haughtiness".
दर्पेण सः मित्राणि त्यजति। darpeṇa saḥ mitrāṇi tyajati. In his haughtiness he drops his friends.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दर्पः (darpaḥ) comes from the root √dṛp, "to be proud, to be wild"; MW gives "pride, arrogance, haughtiness, conceit" (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for haughtiness, then the Sanskrit for boasts.

You'll want to know: आडम्बरः.
आडम्बरः — āḍambaraḥ — "bombast".
तस्य भाषणे केवलम् आडम्बरः अस्ति। tasya bhāṣaṇe kevalam āḍambaraḥ asti. His speech is mere bombast.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आडम्बरः (āḍambaraḥ) MW's first sense is "a kind of drum", then "a great noise" and "noisy behaviour, bombast"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

[pause 2 seconds]

उद्धतः — uddhataḥ — "arrogant".
उद्धतः छात्रः अध्यापकं न नमति। uddhataḥ chātraḥ adhyāpakaṃ na namati. The arrogant student does not bow to the teacher.

The word, taken apart.
उद्धतः (uddhataḥ) is built from ud, "up", and the root √han, "to strike": raised up; MW gives "lifted up" and then "puffed up, haughty, arrogant, rude" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
विनीतः (vinītaḥ) — modest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for bombast, then the Sanskrit for arrogant.

You'll want to know: विनीतः.
विनीतः — vinītaḥ — "modest".
विद्वान् अपि सः विनीतः अस्ति। vidvān api saḥ vinītaḥ asti. Though learned, he is modest.

The word, taken apart.
विनीतः (vinītaḥ) is built from vi and the root √nī, "to lead", the root of नयति: led and trained; MW gives "trained, well-behaved, humble, modest" (MW).
