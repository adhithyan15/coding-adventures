## You'll want to know: برد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C483-BARD483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**برد** — *barada* — "to get cold; to cool".

> برد الشاي قبل أن أشربه.
>
> *barada ash-shāy qabla an ashrabahu.*
>
> The tea got cold before I drank it.

Present: يبرد, *yabrud*.

The noun *bard* is cold, and *barad* is hail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برد** (*barada*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-d*, "to be cold" (Lane). Lane gives *barada* and *baruda*, with the present *yabrudu*.
