Persian, chapter 304: Back then and since.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
در آن زمان (dar ân zamân) — at that time, back then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for at the very end, then the Persian for ultimately.

You'll want to know: در آن زمان.
در آن زمان (dar ân zamân) — "at that time, back then".
در آن زمان (dar ân zamân) ما در یک روستا زندگی میکردیم. Dar ân zamân mâ dar yek rustâ zendegi mikardim. At that time we lived in a village.
It sets the scene, so the verb is often the past continuous: zendegi mikardim. In speech un moqe'.

The word, taken apart.
dar "in" and ân "that" are inherited Persian. zamân "time" is found in Middle Persian and in Arabic alike; EtymDB lists it with no parent, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قدیم (qadim) — the old days, olden times.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for ultimately, then the Persian for at that time.

You'll want to know: قدیم.
قدیم (qadim) — "the old days, olden times".
در قدیم مردم با اسب سفر میکردند. Dar qadim mardom bâ asb safar mikardand. In the old days people travelled on horseback.
Qadim-hâ "long ago" in speech. The adjective for old things is qadimi.

The word, taken apart.
qadim is borrowed from Arabic qadīm, "old, ancient" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
بعدها (ba'dhâ) — later on, afterwards.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for at that time, then the Persian for the old days.

You'll want to know: بعدها.
بعدها (ba'dhâ) — "later on, afterwards".
بعدها (ba'dhâ) فهمیدم که او راست میگفت. Ba'dhâ fahmidam ke u râst migoft. Later on I realised that he had been telling the truth.
It looks forward from a point in the story, often by years. Built on ba'd "after" with the plural -hâ.

The word, taken apart.
ba'dhâ adds the Persian plural ending -hâ to ba'd "after", which is borrowed from Arabic ba'd (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
از آن پس (az ân pas) — from then on, after that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the old days, then the Persian for later on.

You'll want to know: از آن پس.
از آن پس (az ân pas) — "from then on, after that".
از آن پس (az ân pas) هر روز ورزش میکرد. Az ân pas har ruz varzesh mikard. From then on he exercised every day.
Formal; in speech az un be ba'd. With the past continuous it describes a new habit.

The word, taken apart.
az "from", ân "that" and pas "after" are all inherited Persian; pas continues Middle Persian pas, "then, afterwards, behind" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
اخیرا (akhiran) — recently, lately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for later on, then the Persian for from then on.

You'll want to know: اخیرا.
اخیرا (akhiran) — "recently, lately".
اخیرا (akhiran) یک کتاب جدید خریدم. Akhiran yek ketâb-e jadid kharidam. I recently bought a new book.
Formal; in speech tâzegi-hâ.

The word, taken apart.
akhiran is usually traced to Arabic akhīran, "lately", the adverb of akhīr "last" with the ending -an; EtymDB lists no entry for it.
