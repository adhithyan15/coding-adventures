# ਪਾਉਣਾ (pāuṇā) — to pour in; to put in

## You'll want to know: ਪਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C243-KAM243-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਉਣਾ** — *pāuṇā* — "to pour in; to put in".

> ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੁੱਧ ਪਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ chāh vich dudh pāundī hāṁ.*
>
> I pour milk into the tea.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪਾਇਆ, *maiṁ dudh pāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਉਣਾ** (*pāuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pātayati*, "throws, pours out"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pāuṇā*, "to pour" (CDIAL 8053).
