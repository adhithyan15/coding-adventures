Mandarin Chinese, chapter 108: Trying It On.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
试 (shì) — to try; to try on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to stop, then the Mandarin for to leave a place.

You'll want to know: 试.
试 — shì — "to try; to try on".
这个能试吗 zhège néng shì ma — Can I try this one on?
The new character, in pieces: 试 (shì) — the speech piece of 说 on the left; on the right six strokes: a horizontal across the top, a small three-stroke piece low under it, and a long stroke that slants down and hooks, with a dot at its top right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
试 (shì) is inherited from Middle Chinese syiH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to use'. Trying a thing by putting it to use is the usual explanation, but the step is not given there.

What you've built.
One more word for asking for what I want.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for to stop, then the Mandarin for to leave a place, then say shì again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.

Script — the speech piece, then a slanting hook.
试 (shì).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The speech piece, two strokes, as in 说: a dot, then a stroke across that turns down and rises.
The right piece, six strokes: a horizontal across the top; then, low on the left, a short horizontal, a vertical and a stroke that rises to the right; then one long stroke that slants down to the right and hooks up; and last a dot at the top right.
2 + 6 = eight.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 试 (shì), all eight, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many pieces in 试 (shì)? (Two — the speech piece, then the right piece.) Which characters you know start with the same piece? (说, 请, 课.) Which stroke comes last? (The dot at the top right.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 试 (shì), pinned stroke paths and medians.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to exchange, then the Mandarin for a pair of.

You'll want to know: 大小.
大小 — dàxiǎo — "size".
我试了大小很好 wǒ shì le dàxiǎo hěn hǎo — I've tried it on, and the size is fine.

The word, taken apart.
大小 (dàxiǎo) is built from 大 'big' and 小 'small', both inherited from Middle Chinese, daH and sjewX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): how big or small a thing is.
