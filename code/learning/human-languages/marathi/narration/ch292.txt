Marathi, chapter 292: A Fire Breaks Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ठिणगी (ṭhiṇgī) — a spark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for missing, then the Marathi for to comb through.

You'll want to know: ठिणगी.
ठिणगी — ṭhiṇgī — "a spark".
एक ठिणगी पडल्यास सगळं गवत पेटेल. ek ṭhiṇgī paḍalyās sagḷaṁ gavat peṭel. Should a single spark fall, all the grass will catch fire.
Feminine. Plural: ठिणग्या, ṭhiṇagyā.
पडल्यास, should it fall, says जर … पडली तर in one word; plural ठिणग्या.

The word, taken apart.
ठिणगी (ṭhiṇgī) is the everyday Marathi word for a spark; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
भडकणे (bhaḍakṇe) — to flare up; to blaze up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to comb through, then the Marathi for a spark.

You'll want to know: भडकणे.
भडकणे — bhaḍakṇe — "to flare up; to blaze up".
तेल पडल्यावर आग भडकली. tel paḍalyāvar āg bhaḍaklī. When oil fell on it, the fire flared up.
Past (he …): तो भडकला, to bhaḍaklā.
Said of fire and of tempers: तो भडकला, he flared up in anger.

The word, taken apart.
भडकणे (bhaḍakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi bhaḍakṇe, 'to blaze, take fright', under bhaṭ, 'a sudden movement or noise' (CDIAL 9365).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ज्वाळा (jvāḷā) — a tongue of flame; a blaze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a spark, then the Marathi for to flare up.

You'll want to know: ज्वाळा.
ज्वाळा — jvāḷā — "a tongue of flame; a blaze".
इमारतीच्या खिडक्यांतून ज्वाळा बाहेर येत होत्या. imārtīcyā khiḍakyāntūn jvāḷā bāher yet hotyā. Flames were coming out of the windows of the building.
Feminine.
ज्वाळा (jvāḷā) keeps its form in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
ज्वाळा (jvāḷā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit jvālā, 'a flame' (Monier-Williams), with Marathi ळ for the Sanskrit l.

Lesson 4 of 5.
राख (rākh) — ash; ashes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to flare up, then the Marathi for a tongue of flame.

You'll want to know: राख.
राख — rākh — "ash; ashes".
आग विझल्यावर तिथे फक्त राख उरली. āg vijhalyāvar tithe phakta rākh urlī. When the fire went out, only ash was left there.
Feminine.
राख (rākh) होणे, to burn to ashes.

The word, taken apart.
राख (rākh) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit rakṣā, 'ashes' (CDIAL 10552).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अग्निशमन दल (agniśaman dal) — a fire brigade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tongue of flame, then the Marathi for ash.

You'll want to know: अग्निशमन दल.
अग्निशमन दल — agniśaman dal — "a fire brigade".
अग्निशमन दलाच्या गाड्या दहा मिनिटांत आल्या. agniśaman dalācyā gāḍyā dahā miniṭānt ālyā. The fire brigade arrived within ten minutes.
Neuter.
दल is neuter, and so is the whole: अग्निशमन दलाला फोन करणे, to call the fire brigade.

The word, taken apart.
अग्निशमन दल (agniśaman dal) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit agni, 'fire', śamana, 'quelling', and dala, 'a troop' (Monier-Williams).
