## You'll want to know: बीजळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C221-RAIN221-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बीजळी** — *bījḷī* — "lightning".

> बादल मांय बीजळी है।
>
> *bādal mā̃y bījḷī hai.*
>
> There's lightning in the clouds.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बीजळी** (*bījḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vidyullatā*, "forked lightning", built from *vidyut*, "lightning", and *latā*, "a creeper"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bījalī* (CDIAL 11745).
