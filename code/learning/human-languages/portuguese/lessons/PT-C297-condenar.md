## You'll want to know: condenar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C297-TRIBUNAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**condenar** — "to sentence, to condemn".

> O juiz condenou o homem a três anos de cadeia.
>
> *The judge sentenced the man to three years in jail.*

*Condenar a* + the punishment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condenar* comes from Latin *condemnāre* "to sentence, to condemn", built on *damnāre*, from *damnum* "loss, harm" (Lewis & Short).
