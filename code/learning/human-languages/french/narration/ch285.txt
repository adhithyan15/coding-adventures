French, chapter 285: On dit que….
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la rumeur — the rumour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to recount, then the French for to comment on.

You'll want to know: la rumeur.
la rumeur — "the rumour".
Une rumeur court dans le village. A rumour is going round the village.
La rumeur court — the rumour is going round. Also a low noise: la rumeur de la ville.

The word, taken apart.
rumeur is inherited from Latin rūmor "common talk, hearsay" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la désinformation — disinformation, false information.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the rumour, then the French for the scoop.

You'll want to know: la désinformation.
la désinformation — "disinformation, false information".
Il y a beaucoup de désinformation sur internet. There is a lot of false information on the internet.
People also say les fausses nouvelles, or les fake news in speech.

The word, taken apart.
désinformation is built on information, a learned borrowing from Latin īnfōrmātiō "an outline, an idea", built on fōrma "shape" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la censure — censorship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the scoop, then the French for disinformation.

You'll want to know: la censure.
la censure — "censorship".
Le film a été interdit par la censure. The film was banned by the censors.
The verb is censurer.

The word, taken apart.
censure is a learned borrowing from Latin cēnsūra "the office of the censor", from cēnsēre "to assess, to judge" (Lewis & Short).

polémique comes from Greek polemikós "warlike" (EtymDB), built on pólemos "war": a war of words.
