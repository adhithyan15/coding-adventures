## You'll want to know: l'auditeur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C280-TELE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'auditeur** — "the listener".

> Les auditeurs peuvent appeler la radio.
>
> *Listeners can phone in to the radio.*

Feminine: *l'auditrice*. In a firm, *un auditeur* also checks the accounts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*auditeur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *audītor* "hearer" (EtymDB), from *audīre* "to hear".
