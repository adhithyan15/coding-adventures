## You'll want to know: अंगारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C299-AAG299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अंगारा** — *aṅgārā* — "an ember; a live coal".

> चूल्हे से एक अंगारा गिरा।
>
> *cūlhe se ek aṅgārā girā.*
>
> An ember fell out of the stove.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from Sanskrit *aṅgāra*, charcoal, burning or not (Monier-Williams). One small **अंगारा** is enough to start a fire.
