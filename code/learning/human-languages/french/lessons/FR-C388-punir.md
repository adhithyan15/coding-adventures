## You'll want to know: punir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C388-PEINE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**punir** — "to punish".

> Ses parents l'ont puni parce qu'il a cassé la fenêtre.
>
> *His parents punished him because he broke the window.*

A *-ir* verb like *finir*: *ils punissent*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*punir* comes through Old French *punir* (EtymDB) from Latin *pūnīre* "to inflict a penalty on" (Lewis & Short), from *poena* "penalty".
