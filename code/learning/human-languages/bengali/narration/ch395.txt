Bengali, chapter 395: Flights and Runways.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ফ্লাইট (phlāiṭ) — a flight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a steering wheel, then the Bengali for to rub.

You'll want to know: ফ্লাইট.
ফ্লাইট — phlāiṭ — "a flight".
আমাদের ফ্লাইট রাত দশটায় ছাড়বে। āmāder phlāiṭ rāt dôshṭāy chhāṛbe. Our flight leaves at ten at night.

The word, taken apart.
ফ্লাইট (phlāiṭ) is borrowed from English flight, with ফ for f and the hard ট for t.

Lesson 3 of 5.
কাস্টমস (kāsṭôms) — customs (at a border or airport).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a flight, then the Bengali for a runway.

You'll want to know: কাস্টমস.
কাস্টমস — kāsṭôms — "customs (at a border or airport)".
কাস্টমসে আমাদের ব্যাগ খুলতে হল। kāsṭômse āmāder byāg khulte hôlô. At customs we had to open our bags.
-তে হল is the past of -তে হবে: খুলতে হল, we had to open.

The word, taken apart.
কাস্টমস (kāsṭôms) is borrowed from English customs, the office at a border that checks goods and collects duty.

Lesson 4 of 5.
বিদেশ (bidesh) — abroad; a foreign country.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a runway, then the Bengali for customs.

You'll want to know: বিদেশ.
বিদেশ — bidesh — "abroad; a foreign country".
মীরার ভাই বিদেশে থাকে। mīrār bhāi bideshe thāke. Mira's brother lives abroad.
বিদেশে, abroad (being there); বিদেশে যাওয়া, to go abroad. বিদেশি, foreign, is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
বিদেশ (bidesh) is Sanskrit videśa, "another country, abroad" (Monier-Williams), from vi-, apart, and deśa, a country: the land away from home.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পাড়ি দেওয়া (pāṛi deoyā) — to make a crossing (of sea or river); to set out on a long way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for customs, then the Bengali for abroad.

You'll want to know: পাড়ি দেওয়া.
পাড়ি দেওয়া — pāṛi deoyā — "to make a crossing (of sea or river); to set out on a long way".
জাহাজটা সাত দিনে সমুদ্র পাড়ি দিল। jāhājṭā sāt dine sômudrô pāṛi dilô. The ship crossed the sea in seven days.
Past (he/she …): সেই জাহাজ সমুদ্র পাড়ি দিল, sei jāhāj sômudrô pāṛi dilô.
A word with distance in it: you পাড়ি দাও across an ocean, or to a far city.

The word, taken apart.
পাড়ি দেওয়া (pāṛi deoyā) joins পাড়ি, "a crossing, a long passage", to দেওয়া, to give; the origin of পাড়ি is not settled.
