## You'll want to know: la fouille
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C325-PASSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la fouille** — "the dig, the excavation; a search".

> Les fouilles ont duré trois ans.
>
> *The dig went on for three years.*

Also a search, by the police or at an airport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fouille* is built on *fouiller* "to dig, to search", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *fodiculāre* (EtymDB lists no parent), built on Latin *fodere* "to dig" (Lewis & Short).
