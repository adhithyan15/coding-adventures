Tamil, chapter 529: Puzzles and Old Games.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
புதிர் (putir) — a puzzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to print and publish, then the Tamil for to compile.

You'll want to know: புதிர்.
புதிர் — putir — "a puzzle".
தாத்தா ஒவ்வொரு நாளும் நாளிதழில் வரும் புதிருக்கு விடை கண்டுபிடிக்கிறார். tāttā ovvoru nāḷum nāḷitaḻil varum putirukku viṭai kaṇṭupiṭikkiṟār. Every day grandfather solves the puzzle in the newspaper.
To set someone a puzzle is புதிர் போடு (putir pōṭu); to solve one is to find its answer: புதிருக்கு விடை கண்டுபிடி.

The word, taken apart.
புதிர் (putir) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4163 gives பிதிர் and புதிர் (putir), "a riddle, a puzzle".

Lesson 2 of 6.
விடுகதை (viṭukatai) — a riddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to compile, then the Tamil for a puzzle.

You'll want to know: விடுகதை.
விடுகதை — viṭukatai — "a riddle".
பாட்டி எங்களுக்கு ஒரு விடுகதை சொன்னாள். pāṭṭi eṅkaḷukku oru viṭukatai coṉṉāḷ. Grandmother told us a riddle.
A spoken riddle, given as a little story; the listener must find the answer.

The word, taken apart.
விடுகதை (viṭukatai) ends in கதை (katai), "a story", from Sanskrit kathā (CDIAL 2704); how the first part, விடு, joins it is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.
தாயம் (tāyam) — a dice board game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a puzzle, then the Tamil for a riddle.

You'll want to know: தாயம்.
தாயம் — tāyam — "a dice board game".
விடுமுறையில் நாங்கள் தாயம் விளையாடினோம். viṭumuṟaiyil nāṅkaḷ tāyam viḷaiyāṭiṉōm. In the holidays we played the dice game.
A board game played with dice; the game takes விளையாடு (viḷaiyāṭu) like any other.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of தாயம் (tāyam) is not settled; one suggestion is Sanskrit dāya, "a share, a portion" (CDIAL 6288).

Lesson 5 of 6.
பல்லாங்குழி (pallāṅkuḻi) — a seed-and-pit board game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a dice board game, then the Tamil for a die.

You'll want to know: பல்லாங்குழி.
பல்லாங்குழி — pallāṅkuḻi — "a seed-and-pit board game".
பாட்டியும் பேத்தியும் பல்லாங்குழி விளையாடுகிறார்கள். pāṭṭiyum pēttiyum pallāṅkuḻi viḷaiyāṭukiṟārkaḷ. Grandmother and granddaughter are playing the pit game.
Two players move seeds or shells through rows of small pits in a wooden board.

The word, taken apart.
பல்லாங்குழி (pallāṅkuḻi) ends in the native குழி (kuḻi), "a pit", for the hollows of the board; the origin of the first part is not settled.
