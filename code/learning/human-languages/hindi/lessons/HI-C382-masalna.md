## You'll want to know: मसलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C382-JALDBAZI382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मसलना** — *masalnā* — "to rub hard, to crush".

> आँखें मत मसलो, वरना वे और लाल हो जाएँगी।
>
> *āṅkheṁ mat maslo, varnā ve aur lāl ho jāeṅgī.*
>
> Don't rub your eyes, or they'll get even redder.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने फूल मसला, *usne phūl maslā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मसलना** (*masalnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *maṣati*, "rubs, crushes" (CDIAL 9919).
