## You'll want to know: इनाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C275-SABAS275-03]; assesses=[] -->

**इनाम** — *inām* — "a prize".

> छोरी इनाम जीतै है।
>
> *chhorī inām jītai hai.*
>
> The girl wins a prize.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इनाम** (*inām*) is usually traced, through Persian *inʿām*, to Arabic *inʿām*, "a gift, a favour".
