## You'll want to know: નગારું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C553-NAGARU553-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નગારું** — *nagārũ* — "a kettledrum".

> જો તહેવાર હશે તો અરુણ મંદિરમાં નગારું વગાડશે.
>
> *jo tahevār haśe to Aruṇ mandirmā̃ nagārũ vagāḍśe.*
>
> If there is a festival, Arun will play the kettledrum at the temple.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નગારું** (*nagārũ*) is usually traced to Arabic *naqqāra*, "a kettledrum", through Persian.
