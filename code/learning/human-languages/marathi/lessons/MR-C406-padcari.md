## You'll want to know: पादचारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C406-PADPATH406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पादचारी** — *pādcārī* — "a pedestrian".

> पादचारी पदपथावरून चालतात.
>
> *pādcārī padpathāvarūn cāltāt.*
>
> Pedestrians walk on the pavement.

Masculine.

The plural is the same: एक पादचारी, खूप पादचारी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पादचारी** (*pādcārī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pādacārin*, "going on foot, walking" (Monier-Williams).
