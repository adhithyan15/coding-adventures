## You'll want to know: పల్లవి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C539-PADYAM539-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పల్లవి** — *pallavi* — "a refrain; a chorus".

> ఆ పాట పల్లవి అందరికీ గుర్తుంది.
>
> *ā pāṭa pallavi andarikī gurtundi.*
>
> Everyone remembers the refrain of that song.

The opening line a song keeps returning to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పల్లవి** (*pallavi*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *pallava*, "a sprout, a twig, a blossom" (CDIAL 7969).
