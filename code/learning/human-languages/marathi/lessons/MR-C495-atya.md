## You'll want to know: आत्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C495-NATU495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्या** — *ātyā* — "a father's sister".

> माझी आत्या गावात राहते.
>
> *mājhī ātyā gāvāt rāhte.*
>
> My father's sister lives in the village.

Feminine.

A father's sister; a mother's sister is मावशी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्या** (*ātyā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *āt*, *ātū* and *āte*, "father's sister", under Sanskrit *attā*, "mother, elder sister" (CDIAL 222), a word usually traced to Dravidian, as CDIAL does: Tamil *attai*, "aunt".

## What you've built
