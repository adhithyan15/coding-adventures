## You'll want to know: おわりました
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C140-OWARIMASHITA]; assesses=[] -->

**おわりました** — *owarimashita* — "it finished", "it is over". It is the
polite past of **おわる**, *owaru*, "to come to an end", which follows the
*-u* pattern: **る** becomes **り**, then **ました**.

> **しごとが おわりました。** — *shigoto ga owarimashita* — "Work is over;
> I have finished work."

Now you can say what you did, and when:

> **せんしゅう はたらきました。** — "I worked last week."
>
> **きょねん どこにも いきませんでした。** — "Last year I didn't go
> anywhere."
