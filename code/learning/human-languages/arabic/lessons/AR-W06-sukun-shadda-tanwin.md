# ْ ّ ً — stop, double, or add *-an*

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You already know the three short-vowel marks. Keep those shapes in
mind. This lesson adds exactly three more marks, one job at a time.

## Script — Three marks, three instructions
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-CONCEPT-W06-SUKUN-01, AR-CONCEPT-W06-SHADDA-02, AR-CONCEPT-W06-FATHATAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

Each mark sits around a consonant. It changes how you read that consonant; it is
not another full letter.

| mark | name | instruction |
|---|---|---|
| **ْ** | *sukūn* | stop: no vowel follows this consonant |
| **ّ** | *shadda* | hold or double this consonant |
| **ً** | *fatḥatan* | add the ending *-an* |

Look at the last mark in **شكرًا** (*shukran*, “thank you”). The small doubled
stroke above the final letter supplies *-an*. You have seen the word before;
today you are learning what that one mark tells the reader.

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU WRITE: draw **ْ** once — a small circle above the line; say “no vowel”]
- [YOU WRITE: draw **ّ** once — a small *w* above the line; say “double”]
- [YOU WRITE: draw **ً** once — two light slashes above the line; say “-an”]
- [YOU SAY: point in order — *sukūn*, *shadda*, *fatḥatan*]

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-W06-SUKUN-01, AR-CONCEPT-W06-SHADDA-02, AR-CONCEPT-W06-FATHATAN-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which mark means “no vowel”? (**ْ**, *sukūn*.) Which doubles a
