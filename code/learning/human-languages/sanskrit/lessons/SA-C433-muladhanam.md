## You'll want to know: मूलधनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C433-AYA433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मूलधनम्** — *mūladhanam* — "trading capital".

> वणिक् मूलधनेन व्यापारं करोति।
>
> *vaṇik mūladhanena vyāpāraṃ karoti.*
>
> The merchant trades with his capital.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूलधनम्** (*mūladhanam*) is built from *mūla*, "a root, the base", and *dhana*, "wealth"; MW gives the compound as principal, capital (MW).
