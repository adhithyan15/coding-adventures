Telugu, chapter 152: A Word for a Stream — Then Its First Letter.
3 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ఝరి (jhari) — a stream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Find the familiar రి at the end: ర carries the short ి sign. Now picture a narrow stream moving down a hillside. This lesson gives that stream a name before it asks you to study the uncommon first letter.

You'll want to know: ఝరి.
ఝరి — jhari — "a stream" or "mountain stream".
Say it in two gentle pieces: jha, ri. Read the word as ఝ-రి. The first sound is a breathy jh; the second piece is the familiar రి.
ఝరి (jhari) is an authentic learned or literary word shared with Sanskrit. It is not an everyday word you must force into conversation. Keep its concrete image today: a stream running down a slope.

What you've built.
Meaning comes first. You can recognise and say the whole word before the next lesson asks your hand to take its first shape apart.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jha, ri, slowly]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jhari, as one word]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — read: ఝరి (jhari) — a stream]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — find: the familiar రి at the end]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
What Telugu word can mean a stream or mountain stream?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ఝరి (jhari) mean? ("A stream" or "mountain stream.") Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 3.
ఝ — the breathy first letter in ఝరి (jhari).

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ఝ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
One gentle look back first: వాఙ్మయం means "literature," and its uncommon letter is ఙ. Point to ఙ without copying it. Today you will keep that shape distinct from a different uncommon letter.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say ఝరి (jhari) once. What does it mean? ("A stream" or "mountain stream.")
[pause 1 second]
Now slow the first piece: jha. That breathy jh base letter is today's whole task.

Script you'll notice: ఝ.
ఝ — jha.
In ఝరి (jhari), the first base letter is ఝ. The rest, రి, is already familiar. Read the whole word in two pieces: ఝ-రి.
This aspirated letter is uncommon in everyday Telugu vocabulary. That is why the book gave you the authentic word ఝరి (jhari) before asking you to practise the shape.

Writing: ఝ — five movements, five runs.
Follow the filmstrip slowly. Movement 1 circles the broad left bowl. Lift. Movement 2 circles the middle bowl. Lift again. Movement 3 circles the right bowl. Movement 4 sweeps through the separate upper flourish. Movement 5 draws the separate stem downward below the middle.
The numbered route is one attested school-style order; Telugu handwriting can vary. Keep all five pen-down runs distinct and let the three bowls stay round.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: ఝ (jha) once, following the five numbered movements]
[your turn — copy: ఝ (jha) twice without tracing]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — build: ఝ + రి = ఝరి (jhari)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — read: ఝరి (jhari)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which base letter begins ఝరి (jhari)?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — ఝ? What word gave it to you? (ఝరి (jhari).)


Lesson 3 of 3.
ఝరి (jhari) — meaning and shape together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Picture a stream running down a hillside. Say the Telugu word for it. (ఝరి (jhari).)

Guided Practice.
Read ఝరి (jhari) once. Cover it, then write only its first base letter: ఝ.
Now uncover the word and check the three rounded bowls, the upper flourish, and the separate downward stem. Keep the five pen-down runs distinct.
[once you have stopped driving — write: ఝ (jha) once from memory]
[your turn — build: ఝ + రి = ఝరి (jhari)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “a stream”]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Write the first base letter of ఝరి (jhari), then say what the whole word means.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ఝరి (jhari) mean, and which base letter begins it? ("A stream"; ఝ.)
