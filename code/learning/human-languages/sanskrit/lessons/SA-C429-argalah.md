## You'll want to know: अर्गलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C429-UDGHATANA429-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्गलः** — *argalaḥ* — "a door bolt".

> रात्रौ अहं द्वारे अर्गलं स्थापयामि।
>
> *rātrau ahaṃ dvāre argalaṃ sthāpayāmi.*
>
> At night I put the bolt on the door.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्गलः** (*argalaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a wooden bolt or pin that fastens a door, and for a bar or check; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
