Telugu, chapter 366: Treasure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఖజానా (khajānā) — a treasury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to order, then the Telugu for to banish.

You'll want to know: ఖజానా.
ఖజానా — khajānā — "a treasury".
రాజు ఖజానా బంగారంతో నిండి ఉండేది. rāju khajānā baṅgārantō niṇḍi uṇḍēdi. The king's treasury used to be full of gold.
Today also the public purse: ప్రభుత్వ ఖజానా (khajānā).

The word, taken apart.
ఖజానా (khajānā) is a loan from Arabic khizāna, "a storehouse, a treasury".

In the villages దొర (dora) was the landlord, and దొరసాని his wife.

The word, taken apart.
దొర (dora) is a Telugu word for a lord or master; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఆక్రమించు (ākramiñcu) — to occupy; to invade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a quest, then the Telugu for a lord.

You'll want to know: ఆక్రమించు.
ఆక్రమించు — ākramiñcu — "to occupy; to invade".
శత్రువులు కోటను ఆక్రమించారు. śatruvulu kōṭanu ākramiñcāru. The enemies occupied the fort.
Past (I …): ఆక్రమించాను, ākramiñcānu.
Also to take land unlawfully: స్థలం ఆక్రమించు (ākramiñcu).

The word, taken apart.
ఆక్రమించు (ākramiñcu) is built on Sanskrit ākramaṇa, "stepping on, approaching" (CDIAL 1016), from kram, "to step", with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.
