# ಕರುಳು (karuḷu) — the intestine; the gut

## You'll want to know: ಕರುಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C508-DEHA508-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರುಳು** — *karuḷu* — "the intestine; the gut".

> ಹಸಿವಾದಾಗ ಕರುಳು ಸದ್ದು ಮಾಡುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *hasivādāga karuḷu saddu māḍuttade.*
>
> When you are hungry, the gut rumbles.

Also the seat of tender feeling: ಕರುಳಿನ ಸಂಬಂಧ, a blood tie.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರುಳು** (*karuḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1274 lists Kannada *karuḷ*, "an entrail, the bowels; love", beside Malayalam *karaḷ*.
