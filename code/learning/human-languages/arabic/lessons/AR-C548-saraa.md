## You'll want to know: صارع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C548-HALABA548-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صارع** — *ṣāraʿa* — "to wrestle (with)".

> صارع اللاعب بطل البلد.
>
> *ṣāraʿa al-lāʿib baṭal al-balad.*
>
> The player wrestled the national champion.

Present: يصارع, *yuṣāriʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صارع** (*ṣāraʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-r-ʿ*, "to throw down" (Lane). Form III ṣāraʿa is to try to throw one another down.
