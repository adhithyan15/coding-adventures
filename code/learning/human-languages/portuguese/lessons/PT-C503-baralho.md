## You'll want to know: o baralho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C503-JOGOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o baralho** — "pack of cards, deck".

> Temos um baralho de cartas na gaveta.
>
> *We have a pack of cards in the drawer.*

*Baralhar* — to shuffle; also to muddle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *baralho* is not settled; it may be linked to *baralhar* "to mix up", but the source of both is uncertain.
