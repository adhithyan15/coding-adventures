## You'll want to know: આખું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C173-WEEK173-05]; assesses=[] -->

**આખું** — *ākhũ* — "whole".

> **હું આખો દિવસ કામ કરીશ.** — *hũ ākho divas kām karīś* — "I will work the whole day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આખું** (*ākhũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *akṣata*, "unhurt, not crushed", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "whole" (CDIAL 28).
