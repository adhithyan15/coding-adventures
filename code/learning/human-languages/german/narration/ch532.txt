German, chapter 532: Wohnen in der WG.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Wohngemeinschaft — shared flat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to wash up, then the German for to dry.

You'll want to know: die Wohngemeinschaft.
die Wohngemeinschaft — "shared flat".
Während des Studiums wohnte ich in einer Wohngemeinschaft. While I was a student I lived in a shared flat.
Plural: die Wohngemeinschaften. Mostly shortened to die WG.

The word, taken apart.
Wohngemeinschaft is built on wohnen "to live", inherited from Old High German wonēn, and Gemeinschaft "community", from gemein "common" (EtymDB).

Hausordnung is built on Haus "house" and Ordnung "order", inherited from Old High German ordinunga (EtymDB), built on a verb borrowed from Latin ōrdināre.

Hausmeister is built on Haus "house" and Meister "master", which came through Old High German meistar from Latin magister (EtymDB).
