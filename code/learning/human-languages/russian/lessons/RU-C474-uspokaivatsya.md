## You'll want to know: успокаиваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C474-WORRY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**успокаиваться** (*uspokáivatsya*) — "to calm down".

> После чашки чая она быстро успокаивается.
>
> *Pósle cháshki cháya oná býstro uspokáivayetsya.*
>
> After a cup of tea she soon calms down.

Imperfective; perfective успокоиться. Built like *спокойный* — calm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*успокаиваться* is the reflexive of *успокаивать*, the partner of *успокоить* 'to calm', built on *покой* 'rest', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pokojь* (EtymDB).
