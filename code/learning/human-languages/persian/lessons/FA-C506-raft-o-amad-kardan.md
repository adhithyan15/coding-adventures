## You'll want to know: رفت و آمد کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C506-TIES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رفت و آمد کردن** (*raft-o âmad kardan*) — "to visit each other, to keep in touch".

> ما با همسایه ها زیاد رفت و آمد میکنیم.
>
> *mâ bâ hamsâyehâ ziyâd raft-o âmad mikonim.*
>
> We see a lot of our neighbours.

*raft* 'going' + *o* 'and' + *âmad* 'coming' (the past stems of *raftan* and *âmadan*) + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raft-o âmad* is built on two inherited past stems, *raft* from *raftan*, 'to go', and *âmad* from *âmadan*, 'to come': coming and going. *kardan* is inherited too.
