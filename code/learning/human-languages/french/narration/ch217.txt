Lesson 1 of 5.
presque — almost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for up there, then the French for the suburbs.

You'll want to know: presque.
presque — "almost".

The word, taken apart.
presque — près, "near", and que, "that": nearly. Près from Latin presse, "closely" (EtymDB 2.1).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for the suburbs, then the French for almost.

You'll want to know: environ.
environ — "about, approximately".

The word, taken apart.
environ — en, "in", and Old French viron, "a circuit", from the verb for "to turn": in the round of. En from Latin in; the verb behind viron is of uncertain origin.

The word, taken apart.
cependant — ce, "this", and the word for "during", from the verb for "to hang", from Latin pendere (EtymDB 2.1): "during this".
