# છેતરવું (chetarvũ) — to deceive, to cheat

## You'll want to know: છેતરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C268-QUARREL268-03]; assesses=[] -->

**છેતરવું** — *chetarvũ* — "to deceive, to cheat".

> **શું તેણે તમને છેતર્યા?** — *śũ teṇe tamne chetaryā?* — "Did he cheat you?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **છેતરવું** (*chetarvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this shape; its Gujarati *chaḷvũ*, "to deceive" (CDIAL 5003), is a different word.
