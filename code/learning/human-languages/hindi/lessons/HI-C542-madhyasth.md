## You'll want to know: मध्यस्थ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C542-SAMJHAUTA542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मध्यस्थ** — *madhyasth* — "a mediator; a go-between".

> दादा जी ने दोनों भाइयों के बीच मध्यस्थ का काम किया।
>
> *Dādā jī ne donõ bhāiyoṁ ke bīc madhyasth kā kām kiyā.*
>
> Grandfather acted as mediator between the two brothers.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मध्यस्थ** (*madhyasth*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *madhyastha*, "being in the middle, between" (Monier-Williams).
