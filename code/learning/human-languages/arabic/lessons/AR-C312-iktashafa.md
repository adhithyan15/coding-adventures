## You'll want to know: اكتشف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C312-KASHF312-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اكتشف** — *iktashafa* — "to discover".

> اكتشف العلماء نجما جديدا.
>
> *iktashafa al-ʿulamāʾ najman jadīdan.*
>
> The scientists discovered a new star.

Present: يكتشف, *yaktashif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اكتشف** (*iktashafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-sh-f* (Lane), "to uncover". It is Form VIII, "to uncover for oneself": to find out.
