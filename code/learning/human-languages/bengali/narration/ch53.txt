Bengali, chapter 53: To wear, To leave, To throw, To fly, To teach.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পরা (pôrā) — to wear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to look for, then the Bengali for to lose.

You'll want to know: পরা.
পরা — pôrā — "to wear".

The word, taken apart.
পরা (pôrā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from paridadhāti, "sets round, puts on clothes" (CDIAL 7835). CDIAL notes that Chatterji preferred the passive paridhīyate.

The word, taken apart.

শেখানো — shekhāno — "to teach".

The word, taken apart.
শেখানো (shekhāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śikṣate, "learns" (CDIAL 12430). Shekhāno is the causing form of shekhā, "to learn"; CDIAL lists the pair as sikhā and sikhāna.
