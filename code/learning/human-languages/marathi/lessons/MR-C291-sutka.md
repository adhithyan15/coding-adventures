## You'll want to know: सुटका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C291-SUTKA291-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सुटका** — *suṭkā* — "a rescue; a release".

> तीन तासांनंतर मुलाची सुटका झाली.
>
> *tīn tāsānnantar mulācī suṭkā jhālī.*
>
> After three hours the child was rescued.

Feminine.

सुटका होणे, to be rescued or set free; सुटका करणे, to rescue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुटका** (*suṭkā*) is built on the stem *suṭ-*, 'to come loose, be set free': CDIAL lists Marathi *suṭṇẽ*, 'to get loose', and *suṭī*, 'remission', under *\*kṣuṭyate*, 'is released' (CDIAL 3707).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
