## You'll want to know: xīnqíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C245-MASK245-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnqíng* — "mood; frame of mind".

> *shēngbìng le xīnqíng bù hǎo* — Being ill puts me in a bad mood.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnqíng* is built from 心 'heart' and *qíng* 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim* and *dzjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the second, after an older book, as what a person desires.
