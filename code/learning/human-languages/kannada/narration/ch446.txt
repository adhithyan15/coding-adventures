Kannada, chapter 446: Shortcuts and Detours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಾಲುದಾರಿ (kāludāri) — a footpath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to move, then the Kannada for to approach.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಲುದಾರಿ.
ಕಾಲುದಾರಿ — kāludāri — "a footpath".
ಹೊಲಗಳ ನಡುವೆ ಒಂದು ಕಾಲುದಾರಿ ಇದೆ. holagaḷa naḍuve ondu kāludāri ide. There is a footpath between the fields.
ಕಾಲು, foot, plus ದಾರಿ, way: a way for feet only.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಲುದಾರಿ (kāludāri) is a native Kannada compound of kāl, "foot, leg" (DEDR 1479), and ದಾರಿ, "a way" (DEDR 3170).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪು (dāri tappu) — to lose one's way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a shortcut, then the Kannada for a detour.

You'll want to know: ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪು.
ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪು — dāri tappu — "to lose one's way".
ಕತ್ತಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವು ಕಾಡಿನಲ್ಲಿ ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪಿದೆವು. kattaleyalli nāvu kāḍinalli dāri tappidevu. In the dark we lost our way in the forest.
Past (I …): ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪಿದೆ, dāri tappide.
Word for word the way slips: ತಪ್ಪು is to slip or to go wrong.

The word, taken apart.
ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪು (dāri tappu) joins two native Dravidian words: ದಾರಿ, "a way" (DEDR 3170), and ತಪ್ಪು, "to slip, to miss, to go wrong" (DEDR 3071).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹೆಜ್ಜೆ (hejje) — a footstep; a step.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a detour, then the Kannada for to lose one's way.

You'll want to know: ಹೆಜ್ಜೆ.
ಹೆಜ್ಜೆ — hejje — "a footstep; a step".
ಮರಳಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಆನೆಯ ಹೆಜ್ಜೆಗಳು ಕಂಡವು. maraḷina mēle āneya hejjegaḷu kaṇḍavu. Elephant footprints could be seen on the sand.
Plural: ಹೆಜ್ಜೆಗಳು, hejjegaḷu.
ಹೆಜ್ಜೆ (hejje) ಹಾಕು, to take steps, to walk along.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆಜ್ಜೆ (hejje) "a footprint, a step" is listed, beside older pajje, in the appendix of Indo-Aryan loans in DEDR (DEDR App. 6), which compares Sanskrit padyā, "footsteps" (CDIAL 7778); the exact path is not settled.
