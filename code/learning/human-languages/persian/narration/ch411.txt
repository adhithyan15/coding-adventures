Persian, chapter 411: Borrowing and lending.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قرض گرفتن (qarz gereftan) — to borrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a document, then the Persian for citizenship.

You'll want to know: قرض گرفتن.
قرض گرفتن (qarz gereftan) — "to borrow".
از دوستم کمی پول قرض گرفتم. az dustam kami pul qarz gereftam. I borrowed a little money from my friend.
Qarz "loan" + gereftan "to take" (stem gir-). Az kasi qarz gereftan — to borrow from someone.

The word, taken apart.
قرض گرفتن (qarz gereftan) is literally "to take a loan": qarz was borrowed from Arabic qarḍ "a loan" (EtymDB), and gereftan "to take" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قرض دادن (qarz dâdan) — to lend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for citizenship, then the Persian for to borrow.

You'll want to know: قرض دادن.
قرض دادن (qarz dâdan) — "to lend".
برادرم ماشینش را به من قرض داد. barâdaram mâshinash râ be man qarz dâd. My brother lent me his car.
Qarz + dâdan "to give" (stem deh-). Be kasi qarz dâdan — to lend to someone.

The word, taken apart.
قرض دادن (qarz dâdan) is literally "to give a loan": qarz comes from Arabic qarḍ "a loan" (EtymDB), and dâdan "to give" is traced by EtymDB to PIE deh₃-.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بدهی (bedehi) — a debt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to borrow, then the Persian for to lend.

You'll want to know: بدهی.
بدهی (bedehi) — "a debt".
او همه بدهیهایش را پرداخت. u hame-ye bedehihâyash râ pardâkht. He paid off all his debts.
From bedeh "owing", built on dâdan "to give", + -i.

The word, taken apart.
بدهی (bedehi) is native Persian, built on bedeh "owing", from deh-, the present stem of dâdan "to give", with the noun suffix -i: what you still have to give.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: بدهکار.
بدهکار (bedehkâr) — "in debt, owing; a debtor".
من هنوز به تو بدهکارم. man hanuz be to bedehkâram. I still owe you.
Be kasi bedehkâr budan — to owe someone. The opposite is talabkâr.

The word, taken apart.
بدهکار (bedehkâr) is native Persian, built from bedeh "owing", built on deh-, the present stem of dâdan "to give", + the agent suffix -kâr.

Lesson 5 of 5.
طلبکار (talabkâr) — a creditor; owed money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a debt, then the Persian for in debt.

You'll want to know: طلبکار.
طلبکار (talabkâr) — "a creditor; owed money".
طلبکارها هر روز به او زنگ میزنند. talabkârhâ har ruz be u zang mizanand. His creditors call him every day.
Talab "claim" + -kâr. Of a person who acts as if everyone owes him: talabkâr ast.

The word, taken apart.
طلبکار (talabkâr) joins talab "claim", from Arabic ṭalab "seeking", to the Persian agent suffix -kâr: the one who has a claim.
