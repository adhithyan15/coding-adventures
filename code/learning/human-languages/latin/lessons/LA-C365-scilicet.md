## You'll want to know: scīlicet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C365-ETENIM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**scīlicet** — "to be sure, of course".

> Tū scīlicet omnia scīs!
>
> *You know everything, to be sure!*

*Scīlicet* = *scīre licet*, one may know. Often ironic: to be sure! English *scilicet*, short *sc.*, means namely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scīlicet* is contracted from *scīre licet* "one may know" (Lewis & Short): it is plain to know. English *scilicet* "namely" was borrowed from it.
