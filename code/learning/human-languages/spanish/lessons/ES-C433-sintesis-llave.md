# (dejar una llave) — read it, then do what it says

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-02, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *la portera* and *el contador*. Both appear in the notice you are
about to read, and the notice asks you to do something.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-01, ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-02, ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-04, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-02] -->

Read it once straight through, then answer.

> **NORMAS DEL PORTAL**
> El jueves vendrán a leer los **contadores** entre las nueve y las dos. Es
> necesario que haya alguien en casa. Quien no pueda estar debe dejar una llave
> a la **portera**.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: what day they are coming and what for]
- [YOU SAY: what you must do if you cannot be at home]
- [YOU SAY: who you leave the key with]

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-03, ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-05, ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-01] -->

Now produce it yourself. Say aloud:

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: I cannot be at home on Thursday]
- [YOU SAY: I am leaving the key with the concierge]
- [YOU SAY: my car is in the garage, next to the door]

**This is the level's real test, and it is not a conversation.** A2 asks you to
read a short official notice and act on it correctly. Three chapters ago the
words for the notice did not exist for you; the grammar never changed.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-02, ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-01, ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Who do you leave the key with? (**la portera**.) Where do you wait?
(**en el portal**.) What is the heading on the wall? (**NORMAS**.)
