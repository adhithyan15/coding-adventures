## You'll want to know: योगासन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C533-TANDURUSTI533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**योगासन** — *yogāsan* — "a yoga posture".

> आजी रोज सकाळी योगासनं करते.
>
> *ājī roj sakāḷī yogāsanaṁ karte.*
>
> Grandmother does yoga every morning.

Neuter. Plural: योगासनं, *yogāsanaṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**योगासन** (*yogāsan*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *yoga* and *āsana*, "a seat, a posture" (Monier-Williams).
