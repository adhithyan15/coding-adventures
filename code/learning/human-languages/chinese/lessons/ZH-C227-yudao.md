## You'll want to know: yùdào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C227-SEA227-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yùdào* — "to run into; to come across".

> *zuótiān wǒ yùdàole yí ge lǎo tóngxué* — Yesterday I ran into an old classmate.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùdào* is built from *yù* 'to meet by chance' and 到 'to arrive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjuH* and *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to meet without arranging it'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
