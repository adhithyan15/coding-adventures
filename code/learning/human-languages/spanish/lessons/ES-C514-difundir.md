## You'll want to know: difundir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C514-REPORTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**difundir** — "to spread, to broadcast".

> La radio difundió la noticia en pocos minutos.
>
> *The radio spread the news in a few minutes.*

Used for news, ideas and images: *difundir una foto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*difundir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *diffundere* 'to pour out, to spread' (EtymDB), built on *dis-* 'apart' and *fundere* 'to pour'.
