## You'll want to know: dōngxi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C42-SHOP42-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dōngxi* — "thing; things".

> *nǐ mǎi shénme dōngxi* — What are you buying?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dōngxi* is built from dōng 'east' and xī 'west', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tuwng* and *sej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How east-and-west came to mean a thing is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for asking politely.
