## You'll want to know: పురాతన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C367-TAVVAKALU367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పురాతన** — *purātana* — "ancient; very old".

> అది ఒక పురాతన దేవాలయం.
>
> *adi oka purātana dēvālayaṁ.*
>
> That is an ancient temple.

An adjective that goes before its noun: పురాతన కట్టడం, an ancient building.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పురాతన** (*purātana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *purātana*, "ancient" (CDIAL 8285).
