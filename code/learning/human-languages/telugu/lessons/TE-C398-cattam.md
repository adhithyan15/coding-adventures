# చట్టం (caṭṭaṁ) — a law; an act

## You'll want to know: చట్టం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C398-CATTAM398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**చట్టం** — *caṭṭaṁ* — "a law; an act".

> ఆ పని చట్టం ప్రకారం తప్పు.
>
> *ā pani caṭṭaṁ prakāraṁ tappu.*
>
> That act is wrong under the law.

Plural: చట్టాలు, *caṭṭālu*.

చట్టం ప్రకారం, according to the law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చట్టం** (*caṭṭaṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2304 lists Telugu *caṭṭamu*, "a frame, a plan, a system, a law", with Tamil *caṭṭam*, "a frame; a rule, a regulation".
