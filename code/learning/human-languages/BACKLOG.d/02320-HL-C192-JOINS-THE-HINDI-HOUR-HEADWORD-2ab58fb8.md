## HL-C192 — बजना joins the Hindi hour headword

After the book-register repair, **बजना** is the next independent
learner-facing form in the ranked Hindi queue. The adjacent **बज** row is the
same verb's stem inside inflected clock sentences, but the infinitive itself is
explicitly defined and assessed rather than being a morphology fragment.

Chapter 18 teaches **घंटा** as both “hour” and “bell,” then defines **बजना** as
“to strike, to toll” and as the verb Hindi uses to tell time. Guided practice
asks learners to produce singular and plural clock sentences, and wrap-up
recall asks for the verb and its literal meaning. The old headword declared
only **घंटा**, hiding the lesson's second concept.

The headword is now **घंटा / बजना**, with a gloss that names both the noun and
the time-telling verb. The stem **बज** remains correctly classified as
morphology rather than a standalone vocabulary claim. Rerun the ranked report
next and continue the evidence-first audit after the explicitly classified
script, morphology, and historical-source rows.
