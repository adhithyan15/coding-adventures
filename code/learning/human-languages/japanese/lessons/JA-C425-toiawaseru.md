## You'll want to know: といあわせる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C425-TSUUYAKU425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**といあわせる** — *toiawaseru* — "to make an enquiry".

> **でんわで えきに といあわせました。** — *denwa de eki ni toiawasemashita* — "I phoned the station to ask."

Polite **といあわせます**, *toiawasemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが りょかんに といあわせました。**, *chichi ga ryokan ni toiawasemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**といあわせる** (*toiawaseru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *toi*, "asking" (from *tou*) and *awaseru*, "to bring together"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
