Mandarin Chinese, chapter 55: What You Love.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
想 (xiǎng) — to want to; to think; to miss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to ask, then the Mandarin for lesson.

You'll want to know: 想.
想 — xiǎng — "to want to; to think; to miss".
我想说中国话 wǒ xiǎng shuō Zhōngguó huà — I'd like to speak Chinese.
The new character, in pieces: 想 (xiǎng) — a tree piece and an eye piece side by side, over a heart piece. The next lesson puts it in your hand.
xiǎng before another verb says what you would like to do: wǒ xiǎng shuō.

The word, taken apart.
想 (xiǎng) is inherited from Middle Chinese sjangX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to think'.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.
想 (xiǎng) — a tree and an eye, over a heart.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — a tree and an eye, over a heart and Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The word first, then the character inside it.
[your turn — recall: say xiǎng — R1, one lesson back, before it decays]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
看 说.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — kàn, shuō]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — a tree and an eye, over a heart.
想 (xiǎng).
Three pieces: the tree, then the eye, then the heart below. Count three, not thirteen.
The tree, four strokes, narrow on the top left: a horizontal, a vertical through it, a left-falling stroke, and a dot.
The eye, five strokes, on the top right: a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, and three short horizontals inside, the last one closing it. It is the box at the bottom of 看.
The heart, four strokes, wide underneath: a dot, a long curve that sweeps right and hooks up, then two more dots.
4 + 5 + 4 = thirteen.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 想 (xiǎng), all thirteen, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many pieces in 想 (xiǎng)? (Three — the tree, the eye, the heart.) Which character you know has the same eye in it? (看.) What does the heart begin with? (A dot.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 想 (xiǎng), pinned stroke paths and medians.
