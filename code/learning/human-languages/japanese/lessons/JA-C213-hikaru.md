## You'll want to know: ひかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C213-SKY213-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ひかる** — *hikaru* — "to shine; to glitter".

> **こんや ほしが ひかります。** — *kon'ya hoshi ga hikarimasu* — "The stars will shine tonight."

Polite **ひかります**, *hikarimasu*; a godan verb. The noun **ひかり**, *hikari*, means "light".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひかる** (*hikaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
