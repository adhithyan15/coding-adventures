## You'll want to know: ふで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C465-PAINT465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふで** — *fude* — "a brush; a writing brush".

> **ふでで なまえを かきました。** — *fude de namae o kakimashita* — "I wrote my name with a brush."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふで** (*fude*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
