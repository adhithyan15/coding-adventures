# செயலிழ (ceyaliḻa) — to stop working; to fail

## You'll want to know: செயலிழ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C411-KOLARU411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**செயலிழ** — *ceyaliḻa* — "to stop working; to fail".

> நடுவழியில் எங்கள் காரின் இயந்திரம் செயலிழந்தது.
>
> *naṭuvaḻiyil eṅkaḷ kāriṉ iyantiram ceyaliḻantatu.*
>
> Halfway, the engine of our car stopped working.

Past (it …): செயலிழந்தது, *ceyaliḻantatu*.

செயல், an action, + இழ, to lose: to lose the power to act. Said of machines and of organs of the body.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**செயலிழ** (*ceyaliḻa*) is built from செயல், "an action", the noun of செய், "to do" (DEDR 1957), and இழ, "to lose" (DEDR 501): to lose the power to act. Like இழ, it makes its past in -ந்த-.
