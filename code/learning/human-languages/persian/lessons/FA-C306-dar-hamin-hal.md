## You'll want to know: در همین حال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C306-TADRIJ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**در همین حال** (*dar hamin hâl*) — "meanwhile, at the same time".

> من ظرف میشستم و در همین حال برادرم تلویزیون تماشا میکرد.
>
> *Man zarf mishostam va dar hamin hâl barâdaram televiziyon tamâshâ mikard.*
>
> I was washing the dishes, and meanwhile my brother was watching television.

It joins two background actions, often both in the past continuous. Literally "in this very state".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hâl* "state" is borrowed from Arabic *ḥāl* (EtymDB); *dar* "in" and *hamin* "this very" are inherited Persian.
