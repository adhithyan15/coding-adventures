## You'll want to know: despegar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C654-AVION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**despegar** — "to take off; to unstick".

> El avión despega a las seis.
>
> *The plane takes off at six.*

The noun is *el despegue*, take-off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*despegar* is built on *pegar* "to stick" with *des-* "un-"; *pegar* is inherited from Latin *picāre* "to smear with pitch" (EtymDB). A plane unsticks itself from the ground.
