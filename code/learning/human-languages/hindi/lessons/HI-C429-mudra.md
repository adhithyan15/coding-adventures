## You'll want to know: मुद्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C429-PARCHI429-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुद्रा** — *mudrā* — "a currency".

> हर देश की अपनी मुद्रा होती है।
>
> *har deś kī apnī mudrā hotī hai.*
>
> Every country has its own currency.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुद्रा** (*mudrā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mudrā*, "a seal, a stamp" (Monier-Williams); a stamped coin led to money in general, and modern Hindi uses it for a currency.
