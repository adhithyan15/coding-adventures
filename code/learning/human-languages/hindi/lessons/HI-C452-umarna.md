## You'll want to know: उमड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C452-BHAVNA452-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उमड़ना** — *umaṛnā* — "to well up; to surge".

> बेटी को देखकर माँ की आँखों में आँसू उमड़ आए।
>
> *beṭī ko dekhkar māṁ kī ā̃khoṁ meṁ ā̃sū umaṛ āe.*
>
> When she saw her daughter, tears welled up in the mother's eyes.

Past (it …): वह नदी उमड़ पड़ी, *vah nadī umaṛ paṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उमड़ना** (*umaṛnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *umaḍnā*, "to swell, heave", under *\*ummaḍ*, a form it reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2344). Feelings, tears, rivers and crowds all well up with it.

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
