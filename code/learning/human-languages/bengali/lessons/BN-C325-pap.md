## You'll want to know: পাপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C325-UPODESH325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পাপ** — *pāp* — "a sin".

> গল্পে বলে যে মিথ্যা বলা পাপ।
>
> *gôlpe bôle je mitthā bôlā pāp.*
>
> The story says that telling lies is a sin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাপ** (*pāp*) is Sanskrit *pāpa*, 'bad, evil', and as a noun 'a sin' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
