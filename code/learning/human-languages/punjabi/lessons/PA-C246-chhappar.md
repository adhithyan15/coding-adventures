## You'll want to know: ਛੱਪਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C246-GHR246-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੱਪਰ** — *chhappar* — "a thatched roof; a shed".

> ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਛੋਟਾ ਛੱਪਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *khet vich ikk chhoṭā chhappar hai.*
>
> There is a small thatched shed in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੱਪਰ** (*chhappar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *chattvara*, "house, bower"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chappar*, "thatched roof" (CDIAL 4976).
