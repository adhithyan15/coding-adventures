## You'll want to know: понятие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C386-DOKLAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**понятие** (*ponyátiye*) — "a concept, a notion".

> Я не имею понятия, где он.
>
> *Ya ne iméyu ponyátiya, gde on.*
>
> I have no idea where he is.

Neuter. *Не иметь понятия* — to have no idea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**понятие** is built on *понять* "to understand", from *по-* and the old verb "to take" that also stands in *снимать* and *занимать*: a concept is what the mind takes hold of.
