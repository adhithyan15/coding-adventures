## You'll want to know: عمق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C471-KHALIJ471-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عمق** — *ʿumq* — "depth".

> عمق البحيرة كبير.
>
> *ʿumq al-buḥayra kabīr.*
>
> The depth of the lake is great.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عمق** (*ʿumq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-m-q*, "to be deep" (Lane). It is the noun beside *ʿamīq*, "deep".
