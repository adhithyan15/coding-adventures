## You'll want to know: इमारत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C419-TIRAHA419-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इमारत** — *imārat* — "a building".

> वह सफ़ेद इमारत अस्पताल है।
>
> *vah safed imārat aspatāl hai.*
>
> That white building is the hospital.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इमारत** (*imārat*) is usually traced to Arabic *imāra*, "a building", through Persian, which gave it its final t; it is feminine.
