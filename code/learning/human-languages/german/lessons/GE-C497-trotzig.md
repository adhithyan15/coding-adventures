## You'll want to know: trotzig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C497-TROTZIG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**trotzig** — "defiant".

> Der Kleine ist müde und deshalb trotzig.
>
> *The little one is tired and so he's being defiant.*

*Die Trotzphase* — the terrible twos.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trotzig* is built on *Trotz* "defiance", from Middle High German *traz* (EtymDB), with *-ig*.
