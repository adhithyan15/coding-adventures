## You'll want to know: निकलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C49-LEAVE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निकलना** (*nikalnā*) — "to set out, to leave".

What you say when you are on the point of walking out of a door. It is the going-out kind of action, where the thing leaves under its own steam, and it has a partner, *nikālnā*, with a long *ā*, which means to take something out.

Lengthen the vowel and the doer changes: that pairing is one of Hindi's tidiest machines, and it runs through dozens of everyday words. Sanskrit *niṣkal-*, 'to go forth', stands behind both.
