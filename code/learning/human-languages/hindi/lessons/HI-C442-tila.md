## You'll want to know: टीला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टीला** — *ṭīlā* — "a mound; a dune".

> रेगिस्तान में रेत के ऊँचे टीले हैं।
>
> *registān meṁ ret ke ū̃ce ṭīle haiṁ.*
>
> There are high sand dunes in the desert.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टीला** (*ṭīlā*) goes back to *\*ṭalla*, *\*ṭilla*, "a heap", forms CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5451), which lists Hindi *ṭīlā*, "a hillock, a mound".
