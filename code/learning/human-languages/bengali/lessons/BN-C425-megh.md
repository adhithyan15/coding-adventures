## You'll want to know: মেঘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C425-MEGH425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মেঘ** — *megh* — "a cloud".

> আকাশে কালো মেঘ দেখে আমরা ছাতা নিলাম।
>
> *ākāshe kālo megh dekhe āmrā chhātā nilām.*
>
> Seeing dark clouds in the sky, we took umbrellas.

Thunder is said to be the clouds calling: মেঘ ডাকে.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেঘ** (*megh*) is Sanskrit *megha*, "a cloud" (Monier-Williams), a word Monier-Williams links with *mih*, to sprinkle: the cloud is the sprinkler.
