# Hide, wait, then fill the language field

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LANGUAGE-CUE-MAP-01] -->

Read A — **ਪੰਜਾਬੀ**, B — **ਹਿੰਦੀ** once. Hide the bank and count slowly to ten.

## Writing — short delayed entry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-LANGUAGE-DELAYED-ENTRY-01]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-LANGUAGE-01, PA-FORM-LANGUAGE-CUE-MAP-01] -->

With the bank hidden, complete **ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: __________** for B. Finish the attempt
before revealing the bank.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LANGUAGE-DELAYED-ENTRY-01] -->

Reveal and compare selection, spelling, spacing, then placement. Repair one
dimension only.
