## You'll want to know: શતરંજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C260-GAMES260-02]; assesses=[] -->

**શતરંજ** — *śatranj* — "chess".

> **દાદાએ મને શતરંજ શીખવી.** — *dādāe mane śatranj śīkhavī* — "Grandfather taught me chess."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શતરંજ** (*śatranj*) is a loan from Persian *shatranj*, "chess" (EtymDB); Lane records the Arabic *shiṭranj* as a Persian word taken into Arabic (Lane). The Persian word comes from Sanskrit *cáturaṅga*, "having four limbs", the four parts of an army, which CDIAL also glosses "a kind of chess" (CDIAL 4596).
