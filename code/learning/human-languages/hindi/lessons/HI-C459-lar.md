## You'll want to know: लाड़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C459-SNEH459-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लाड़** — *lāṛ* — "fond pampering; fondness".

> नाना के लाड़ में मीरा का बचपन बीता।
>
> *nānā ke lāṛ meṁ Mīrā kā bacpan bītā.*
>
> Mira's childhood was spent in her grandfather's fond care.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाड़** (*lāṛ*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *lāṛ*, "affection", under *\*lāḍya*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11013).
