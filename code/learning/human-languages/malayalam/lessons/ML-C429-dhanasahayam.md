# ധനസഹായം (dhanasahāyaṁ) — financial aid

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: ധനസഹായം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C429-KSEMAM429-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ധനസഹായം** — *dhanasahāyaṁ* — "financial aid".

> വീട് നഷ്ടപ്പെട്ടവർക്ക് സർക്കാർ ധനസഹായം നൽകി.
>
> *vīṭŭ naṣṭappeṭṭavarkkŭ sarkkār dhanasahāyaṁ nalki.*
>
> The government gave financial aid to those who lost their homes.

സഹായം alone is help.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ധനസഹായം** (*dhanasahāyaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *dhana*, "property, wealth" (CDIAL 6717), and *sahāya*, "a companion, a helper" (CDIAL 13309): help in money.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
