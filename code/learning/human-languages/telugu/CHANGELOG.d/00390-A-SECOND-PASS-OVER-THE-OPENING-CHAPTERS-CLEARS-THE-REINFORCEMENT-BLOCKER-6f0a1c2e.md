## A second pass over the opening chapters, and the reinforcement blocker is gone

Nine `review` lessons. Telugu's pre-A1 rung was held by two of §3.1's five
criteria; it is now held by one.

```
before   telugu: vocabulary 79, reinforcement 50
after    telugu: vocabulary 79
```

#### What the fifty atoms were

Chapters 1 to 5 each end with a `practice` lesson. **Chapters 6 to 31 have
none.** So every atom in the opening lexicon was revisited exactly once — by its
own chapter's recap — and never again. The gate wants two. Forty-seven of the
fifty needed a single further pass; three needed two.

| where | atoms |
|---|---|
| chapters 1-5, the opening lexicon | 42 |
| script recognition (శ ఫ ఐ ఒ ఆ బ ఞ ◌ౌ) | 7 |
| భోజనం | 1 |

#### The nine lessons

Each sits where the material it revisits has a reason to come back, not at the
end of the track:

- **TE-C06-second-pass-greetings** — the opening five words, sorted by the job
  each does rather than the order they arrived.
- **TE-C07-second-pass-names** — the name exchange rebuilt from four pieces,
  with ఒ · ఐ · ఆ, the three letters that open ఒకటి, ఐదు and ఆరు.
- **TE-C09-second-pass-wellbeing** — how-are-you and its answer, plus
  **పరవాలేదు**, which is the reply to that chapter's **క్షమించండి**.
- **TE-C10-second-pass-farewells** — the three goodbyes, in the chapter that
  teaches the days, because every one of them names a return.
- **TE-C12-second-pass-verbs** — the first three verbs and their shared ‑ాను,
  with **లేదు** taught as ఉండు's negative twin.
- **TE-S168-script-recall-split-sounds** — శ and ఫ, two letters that exist to
  keep a heard difference visible.
- **TE-C40-second-pass-meal** — టీ, పాలు and భోజనం, asked for with the ‑ండి
  ending.
- **TE-S169** and **TE-S170** — ఞ and ◌ౌ, recall then dictation, because those
  two atoms had **no** later revisit at all and needed two passes.

#### Why review lessons rather than more words

`CONTENT_TYPES` is `{word, phrase}`. A `review` lesson is outside it, so these
nine add **no headword** — the vocabulary shortfall is untouched, deliberately.
They introduce no atoms either, so they cost nothing against the chapter atom
budget and create no new reinforcement debt of their own.

The opposite trade was measured first: 79 new headwords would have been roughly
237 new atoms and taken reinforcement from 50 to about 130, leaving the track
further from pre-A1 while the headline number read *finished*.

#### What it does not do

No exam item moves, because none has ever been scored against Telugu. One of
five corpus criteria, at one level, on one track. The vocabulary rung is still
79 words away.
