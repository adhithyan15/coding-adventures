# A full message to Mira under a timer

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-GREETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-NAME, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-MEETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-KNOW-MARATHI, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-READ-MARATHI, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-WRITE-NAME, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SPEAK, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THINK, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-UNDERSTAND-MARATHI, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THANKS, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-AGAIN, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-TOMORROW] -->

Mira is reading. Introduce Arun, tell her the six Marathi skills you have
practised, thank her, and close with tomorrow's meeting.

## Writing — full task timer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-GREETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-NAME, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-MEETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-KNOW-MARATHI, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-READ-MARATHI, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-WRITE-NAME, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SPEAK, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THINK, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-UNDERSTAND-MARATHI, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THANKS, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-AGAIN, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-TOMORROW] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

Set **three minutes**. Write **30–40 words** in Devanagari from
reader and purpose alone. No line bank, romanization, or visible model. Stop
at the bell and preserve the first attempt before checking it.

Score **task fulfilment** (named reader, purpose, 30–40 words),
**organisation** (opening, skills, closing), **Marathi control** (the
practised forms), and **orthographic control** (Devanagari spelling and
spacing) separately. Make one 20-second **repair** on another line; the
stopped attempt remains the evidence. This is not a full A1 mock or a
readiness claim.
