## You'll want to know: режиссёр
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C520-SCREENACTING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**режиссёр** (*rezhissyór*) — "a director (of a film or play)".

> Режиссёр долго выбирал актёров.
>
> *Rezhissyór dólgo vybirál aktyórov.*
>
> The director took a long time choosing the actors.

Masculine; also of a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*режиссёр* was borrowed from French *régisseur* 'a manager, a stage manager' (EtymDB).
