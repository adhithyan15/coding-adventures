# (el día de la mudanza) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-02, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **la mudanza** and **estropeado**. Both are in the note below,
and one of them is doing the work of a warning.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-02, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-03, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-04] -->

A note left on the kitchen counter for whoever moves in next. Read it, then
answer.

> Hola. Nos **mudamos** hoy y te dejo unas cosas por escrito.
>
> El timbre está **estropeado** desde enero. El portero dice que lo arregla,
> pero lleva así medio año, así que llama por teléfono.
>
> En el armario hay un cristal **roto**. Lo he tapado con cartón, pero ten
> cuidado al abrirlo.
>
> La **mudanza** ha sido **dura** y quedan tres cajas en el trastero. Las
> recojo el domingo.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: which two things in the flat are not in working order]
- [YOU SAY: which of the two came apart, and how you know]
- [YOU SAY: what is still in the storage room, and when it goes]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-04, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-05, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-01] -->

The note uses both words for broken within four lines, and each one is the
only correct choice.

The doorbell is **estropeado**. Nothing about it is in pieces; it has stopped
doing its job, and the fault is invisible. The pane of glass is **roto**,
which is why it needed covering with cardboard — there is now a hole where
glass used to be. Swap the two words and a Spanish reader would picture a
smashed doorbell and a window that mysteriously fails to be a window.

Then look at **nos mudamos hoy**. Present tense, for something happening
today, exactly as in English. And **dura**, agreeing with *la mudanza*: the
day resisted, all day long.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-01, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-02, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-03, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-04, ES-LEX-C448-MUDA-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Leave a note of four
lines for the next tenant: say you are moving today, name one thing that is
out of order, name one thing that is broken, and say the move has been hard.

Then check yourself: did each of *roto* and *estropeado* go to the right
object, and did *duro* agree with what it describes?
