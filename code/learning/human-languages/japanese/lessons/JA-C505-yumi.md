## You'll want to know: ゆみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C505-SUMO505-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆみ** — *yumi* — "a bow (for arrows); archery".

> **おとうとは ゆみを ならっています。** — *otōto wa yumi o naratteimasu* — "My younger brother is learning archery."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆみ** (*yumi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
