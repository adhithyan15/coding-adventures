## You'll want to know: پرس و جو کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C444-NESHANI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پرس و جو کردن** (*pors-o ju kardan*) — "to ask around, to make inquiries".

> در محله پرس و جو کردیم و خانه را پیدا کردیم.
>
> *Dar mahalle pors-o ju kardim va khâne râ peydâ kardim.*
>
> We asked around the neighbourhood and found the house.

*pors* 'ask' (from *porsidan*) + *o* 'and' + *ju* 'seek' (from *jostan*) + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pors-o ju kardan* is built from two native present stems, *pors* of *porsidan* 'to ask', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root *preḱ-* 'to ask', and *ju* of *jostan* 'to seek', with *kardan* 'to do'.
