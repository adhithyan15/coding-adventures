Persian, chapter 180: To mix, To stir, To boil, To fry, To chop.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مخلوط کردن (makhlut kardan) — to mix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for "to look at", then the Persian for "to listen".

You'll want to know: مخلوط کردن.
مخلوط کردن — makhlut kardan — "to mix".

The word, taken apart.
makhlut kardan pairs makhlut, borrowed from Arabic maḫlūṭ, "mixed" (root ḫ-l-ṭ, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
هم زدن (ham zadan) — to stir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to listen, then the Persian for to mix.

You'll want to know: هم زدن.
هم زدن — ham zadan — "to stir".

The word, taken apart.
ham zadan pairs ham, "together", with zadan, "to strike": to beat together. ham is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian ham and Old Persian hama-, "same, together". zadan, "to strike", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian zadan.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: جوشاندن.
جوشاندن — jushândan — "to boil".

The word, taken apart.
jushândan is the causative of jushidan, "to boil": to make boil. The verb is native, usually traced to Middle Persian jōšīdan.
