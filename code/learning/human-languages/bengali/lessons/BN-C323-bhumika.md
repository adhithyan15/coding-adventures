# ভূমিকা (bhūmikā) — a role; a preface

## You'll want to know: ভূমিকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C323-NAYOK323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভূমিকা** — *bhūmikā* — "a role; a preface".

> নাটকে মীরার ভূমিকা ছিল এক ডাইনির।
>
> *nāṭôke mīrār bhūmikā chhilô ek ḍāinir.*
>
> In the play Mira's part was a witch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভূমিকা** (*bhūmikā*) is Sanskrit *bhūmikā*, 'ground, a place', which Monier-Williams also gives as an actor's part and as the preface of a book; Bengali keeps both.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
