Urdu, chapter 344: Fair and Unfair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
برابر (barābar) — equal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an instruction, then the Urdu for to suggest.

You'll want to know: برابر.
برابر — barābar — "equal".
سب انسان برابر ہیں۔ sab insān barābar haiṅ. All human beings are equal.

The word, taken apart.
برابر (barābar) is usually traced to Persian barābar, "equal, level".

Lesson 5 of 5.
اکسانا (uksānā) — to provoke; to stir up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for favouritism, then the Urdu for oppression.

You'll want to know: اکسانا.
اکسانا — uksānā — "to provoke; to stir up".
اس نے لوگوں کو ظلم کے خلاف اکسایا۔ us ne logoṅ ko zulm ke khilāf uksāyā. He stirred people up against oppression.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بھائی کو اکسایا, us ne bhāī ko uksāyā.

The word, taken apart.
اکسانا (uksānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin uksānā, "to draw out (a wick), to excite", under Sanskrit útkasati, "opens" (CDIAL 1718). As a wick is drawn up to make a flame burn higher.
