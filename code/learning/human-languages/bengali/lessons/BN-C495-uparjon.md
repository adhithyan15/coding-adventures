## You'll want to know: উপার্জন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C495-BETON495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উপার্জন** — *upārjôn* — "earnings; what one earns".

> দোকান থেকে তার উপার্জন ভালো।
>
> *Dokān theke tār upārjôn bhālo.*
>
> Her earnings from the shop are good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপার্জন** (*upārjôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *upārjana*, "the act of gaining" (Monier-Williams).
