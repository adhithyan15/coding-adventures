## You'll want to know: fundus, fundī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C473-RUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**fundus, fundī** — "a farm, an estate; the bottom".

> Fundus noster parvus est sed bonus.
>
> *Our farm is small but good.*

Second declension, masculine: *fundus, fundī m.* First the bottom of a thing, then the ground itself, a piece of land. English *fund* and *foundation* go back to the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fundus* is inherited from PIE *bʰudʰmḗn* 'bottom' (EtymDB), with Greek *pythmēn* 'bottom' as a cousin. English *fund* and *profound* come from it.
