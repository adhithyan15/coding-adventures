## You'll want to know: സർവേ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C436-PARIKSANAM436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**സർവേ** — *sarvē* — "a survey".

> വിദ്യാർത്ഥികൾ ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ ഒരു സർവേ നടത്തി.
>
> *vidyārtthikaḷ grāmattil oru sarvē naṭatti.*
>
> The students carried out a survey in the village.

A survey is നടത്തുക, carried out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സർവേ** (*sarvē*) was borrowed from English *survey*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
