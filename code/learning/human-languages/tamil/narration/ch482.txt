Tamil, chapter 482: Sprains and Breaks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சுளுக்கு (cuḷukku) — a sprain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to trip, then the Tamil for to falter.

You'll want to know: சுளுக்கு.
சுளுக்கு — cuḷukku — "a sprain".
விளையாடும்போது எனக்குக் காலில் சுளுக்கு ஏற்பட்டது. viḷaiyāṭumpōtu eṉakkuk kālil cuḷukku ēṟpaṭṭatu. I sprained my foot while playing.
Also a verb: கால் சுளுக்கியது, the foot got sprained.

The word, taken apart.
சுளுக்கு (cuḷukku) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2702); the entry also has உளுக்கு uḷukku for the same.

Lesson 2 of 5.
பிசகு (picaku) — to be dislocated; to go wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to falter, then the Tamil for a sprain.

You'll want to know: பிசகு.
பிசகு — picaku — "to be dislocated; to go wrong".
விழுந்ததில் அவருடைய தோள் பிசகியது. viḻuntatil avaruṭaiya tōḷ picakiyatu. His shoulder was dislocated in the fall.
Past (it …): பிசகியது, picakiyatu.
Said of the joint, so the past is பிசகியது. Of plans too: கணக்கு பிசகியது, the sums went wrong.

The word, taken apart.
பிசகு (picaku) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4131), from a false step to a slipped joint.

Lesson 3 of 5.
அடிபடு (aṭipaṭu) — to get hurt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a sprain, then the Tamil for to be dislocated.

You'll want to know: அடிபடு.
அடிபடு — aṭipaṭu — "to get hurt".
விளையாடும்போது அவனுக்குத் தலையில் அடிபட்டது. viḷaiyāṭumpōtu avaṉukkut talaiyil aṭipaṭṭatu. He hurt his head while playing.
Past (I …): அடிபட்டேன், aṭipaṭṭēṉ.
Often with the dative: அவனுக்கு அடிபட்டது, he got hurt.

The word, taken apart.
அடிபடு (aṭipaṭu) is built from native words: அடி aṭi, "to beat; a blow" (DEDR 77), and படு, "to undergo".

Lesson 4 of 5.
முறி (muṟi) — to snap; to break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to be dislocated, then the Tamil for to get hurt.

You'll want to know: முறி.
முறி — muṟi — "to snap; to break".
மரத்திலிருந்து விழுந்து அவனுடைய கை முறிந்தது. marattiliruntu viḻuntu avaṉuṭaiya kai muṟintatu. He fell from a tree and broke his arm.
Past (it …): முறிந்தது, muṟintatu.
Of bones and branches: கிளை முறிந்தது, the branch snapped.

The word, taken apart.
முறி (muṟi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4975 lists the verb, spelled muri, "to break off, to snap".

Lesson 5 of 5.
கசி (kaci) — to ooze; to seep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to get hurt, then the Tamil for to snap.

You'll want to know: கசி.
கசி — kaci — "to ooze; to seep".
காயத்திலிருந்து இரத்தம் கசிந்தது. kāyattiliruntu irattam kacintatu. Blood oozed from the wound.
Past (it …): கசிந்தது, kacintatu.
Also of damp walls and of tears.

The word, taken apart.
கசி (kaci) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1091), of moisture seeping through a wall or eyes welling up.
