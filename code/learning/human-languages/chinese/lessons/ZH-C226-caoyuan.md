## You'll want to know: cǎoyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C226-LAKE226-04]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎoyuán* — "grassland; prairie".

> *wǒ xiǎng qù cǎoyuán qí mǎ* — I'd love to go riding on the grasslands.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎoyuán* is built from 草 'grass' and *yuán* 'a plain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshawX* and *ngjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'broad and flat'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
