## You'll want to know: एकाग्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C527-DHANURDHARA527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**एकाग्रः** — *ekāgraḥ* — "focused; intent".

> धनुर्धरः एकाग्रः तिष्ठति।
>
> *dhanurdharaḥ ekāgraḥ tiṣṭhati.*
>
> The archer stands focused.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एकाग्रः** (*ekāgraḥ*) is built from *eka*, "one", and *agra*, "a point, a tip"; MW gives *ekāgra* as fixing one's attention on one point, intent (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
