## You'll want to know: شوط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C546-MUBARA546-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شوط** — *shawṭ* — "a half (of a match)".

> سجل الفريق هدفا في الشوط الأول.
>
> *sajjala al-farīq hadafan fī ash-shawṭ al-awwal.*
>
> The team scored a goal in the first half.

Plural: أشواط, *ashwāṭ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شوط** (*shawṭ*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-w-ṭ*, "a run, a single course" (Lane). Lane gives shawṭ as one run of a course: one stretch of a race.
