# Chapter 5 — The first verbs

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-VADAMI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. Three verbs, one engine — and Sanskrit's count-to-three.

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-I-SPEAK-SANSKRIT, SA-LEX-VASAMI, SA-LEX-KAROMI] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: "I speak Sanskrit" — *ahaṁ saṁskṛtaṁ vadāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: "I live in the city" — *ahaṁ nagare vasāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: "I work" — *ahaṁ kāryaṁ karomi*]
- [YOU SAY: "we two speak" — *vadāvaḥ* (the dual!)]

## How to answer: the engine

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-SINGULAR-DUAL-PLURAL, SA-GRAMMAR-LOCATIVE-CASE] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: the ending that means "I" (*-mi*, as in *asmi*, *vadāmi*)]
- [YOU SAY: Sanskrit's three numbers (singular / **dual** / plural)]
- [YOU SAY: where the verb sits, and how "in" is shown (verb last; a case-ending, the locative *-e*)]

## Guided Practice — the roots under the verbs

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-ETYMON-VAD-TO-SPEAK, SA-ETYMON-VAS-TO-DWELL, SA-ETYMON-KR-TO-DO, SA-ETYMON-SAMSKRTA-PERFECTED, SA-GRAMMAR-SANDHI-JOINS-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: the root of *vadāmi* and its Chapter-1 cousin (*vad*, "to speak"; *dhanyavāda*)]
- [YOU SAY: the root of *vasāmi* and its English cousin (*vas*, PIE *\*wes-*; **was**)]
- [YOU SAY: the root of *karomi* and three words built on it (*kṛ*, PIE *\*kʷer-*; *namaskāra*, *karma*, *saṁskṛta*)]
- [YOU SAY: what *saṁskṛta* means, and its opposite (*sam* "together" + *kṛta* "made", "perfected"; *prākṛta*, "natural")]
- [YOU SAY: *aham* + *saṁskṛtam* run together, and the name for that joining (*ahaṁ saṁskṛtam*; sandhi)]

## Script — the chapter's two shapes

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-204, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-205] -->

> ◌ृ · य

- [YOU POINT: *ya*, then the *ṛ*-sign]
- [YOU SAY: what each does, and a word with each (**य**, *ya*, in *dhanyavādaḥ*; **◌ृ**, a hook below that puts *ṛ* on and takes the *a* off, in *saṁskṛtam*)]

## What you've built: the roots you now carry

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-HISTORY-VERB-ROOTS-BOTH-WAYS]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *vad* "to speak" (→ *dhanyavāda*)
- *vas* "to dwell" ← PIE *\*wes-* → English **was**
- *kṛ* "to do" ← *\*kʷer-* → the root of *namaskāra*, *karma*, *saṁskṛta*; *kāryam* "work"
- *saṁskṛta* = *sam* ("together," English *same*) + *kṛta* ("made") = "perfected"

## Wrap-up Recall

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-HISTORY-VERB-ROOTS-BOTH-WAYS] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In one breath, say your language, your city, and your work in Sanskrit —
then say "we two speak." (*Ahaṁ saṁskṛtaṁ vadāmi. Ahaṁ nagare vasāmi. Ahaṁ kāryaṁ
karomi. Vadāvaḥ.*)
