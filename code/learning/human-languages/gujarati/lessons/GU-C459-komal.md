## You'll want to know: કોમળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C459-KOMAL459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કોમળ** — *komaḷ* — "gentle; tender".

> નર્સનો અવાજ ઘણો કોમળ હતો.
>
> *narsano avāj ghaṇo komaḷ hato.*
>
> The nurse's voice was very gentle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કોમળ** (*komaḷ*) goes back to Sanskrit *kōmala*, "tender, soft"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "soft" (CDIAL 3523).
