Russian, chapter 490: Getting Better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
выздоравливать (vyzdorávlivat) — to recover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an X-ray, then the Russian for a surgeon.

You'll want to know: выздоравливать.
выздоравливать (vyzdorávlivat) — "to recover".
Мой брат медленно выздоравливает после гриппа. Moy brat médlenno vyzdorávlivayet pósle gríppa. My brother is slowly recovering from the flu.
Imperfective; perfective выздороветь (выздоровею).

The word, taken apart.
выздоравливать (vyzdorávlivat) is built on вы- and здоровый 'healthy'; EtymDB lists выздороветь and здоровый without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
поправляться (popravlyátsya) — to get better; to put on weight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to recover, then the Russian for recovery.

You'll want to know: поправляться.
поправляться (popravlyátsya) — "to get better; to put on weight".
После болезни она быстро поправляется. Pósle bolézni oná býstro popravlyáyetsya. After her illness she is getting better quickly.
Imperfective; perfective поправиться (поправлюсь). Also to put on weight.

The word, taken apart.
поправляться (popravlyátsya) is the reflexive of поправлять, the partner of поправить 'to set right', built on править 'to put right' and правый 'right', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic pravъ 'right, correct'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
глотать (glotát) — to swallow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: глотать.
глотать (glotát) — "to swallow".
Мне больно глотать. Mne bólno glotát. It hurts to swallow.
Imperfective; perfective проглотить (проглочу).

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists глотать (glotát), glossed 'to swallow', without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic glъtati.

Lesson 5 of 5.
сироп (siróp) — syrup.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: сироп.
сироп (siróp) — "syrup".
Врач выписал сироп от кашля. Vrach výpisal siróp ot káshlya. The doctor prescribed cough syrup.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
сироп (siróp) was borrowed from French sirop, which comes from Arabic šarāb 'drink' (EtymDB).
