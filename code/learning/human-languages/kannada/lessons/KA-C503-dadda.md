## You'll want to know: ದಡ್ಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C503-BUDDHI503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಡ್ಡ** — *daḍḍa* — "slow-witted; dull".

> ಗಣಿತದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ದಡ್ಡ.
>
> *gaṇitadalli nānu svalpa daḍḍa.*
>
> I am a bit slow at mathematics.

It can sting; ಜಾಣ is the opposite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಡ್ಡ** (*daḍḍa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2314 lists Kannada *daḍḍa*, "a dull, ignorant man", beside Kodagu *daḍḍë*, "a dull man".
