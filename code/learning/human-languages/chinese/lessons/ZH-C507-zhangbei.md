## You'll want to know: zhǎngbèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C507-FATHER507-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎngbèi* — "one's elders; the older generation".

> *zài zhǎngbèi miànqián wǒ gǎnjué hěn jǐnzhāng* — I feel nervous in front of my elders.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎngbèi* is built from *zhǎng* 'elder' and *bèi* 'a generation', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjangX* and *pwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'great' and the second 'people of one rank'.
