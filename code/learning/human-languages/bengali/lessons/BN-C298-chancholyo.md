# চাঞ্চল্য (chānchôlyô) — a sensation; a stir

## You'll want to know: চাঞ্চল্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C298-UTTEJONA298-05]; assesses=[] -->

**চাঞ্চল্য** — *chānchôlyô* — "a sensation; a stir".

> দুর্ঘটনার খবরে শহরে চাঞ্চল্য ছড়াল।
>
> *durghôṭônār khôbôre shôhôre chānchôlyô chhôṛālô.*
>
> News of the accident caused a stir in the town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চাঞ্চল্য** (*chānchôlyô*) is Sanskrit *cāñcalya*, 'unsteadiness, restlessness' (Monier-Williams); a town in a stir is a restless town.
