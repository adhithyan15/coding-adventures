Lesson 1 of 7.
ગ્રંથ (granth) — a tome; a great book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the hero (of a story), then the Gujarati for a reader.

You'll want to know: ગ્રંથ.
ગ્રંથ — granth — "a tome; a great book".
પુસ્તકાલયમાં એક પ્રાચીન ગ્રંથ છે. pustakālaymā̃ ek prācīn granth che. There is an ancient tome in the library.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગ્રંથ (granth) is a tatsama, Sanskrit grantha, "a knot", and then "a book", once leaves tied together (CDIAL 4350). It is a large or important book.

સામયિક — sāmayik — "a magazine".
આ સામયિક દરેક મહિને આવે છે. ā sāmayik darek mahine āve che. This magazine comes out every month.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સામયિક (sāmayik) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sāmayika, "seasonable, timely" (Monier-Williams); the sense of a magazine that comes out at set times is the modern language's.

Lesson 3 of 7.
છપાવવું (chapāvvũ) — to get (something) printed; to publish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a tome, then the Gujarati for a magazine.

You'll want to know: છપાવવું.
છપાવવું — chapāvvũ — "to get (something) printed; to publish".
લેખકે પોતાની નવલકથા છપાવી. lekhke potānī navalkathā chapāvī. The author had his novel published.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પુસ્તક છપાવ્યું, teṇe pustak chapāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
છપાવવું (chapāvvũ) is the causative of છાપવું, to print, from chapp, "press, cover", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4994): to have a printer print it. The long ā of છાપવું shortens in the longer verb.

અરુણે નાટકમાં રાજાનું પાત્ર ભજવ્યું. Aruṇe nāṭakmā̃ rājānũ pātra bhajavyũ. Arun played the king in the play.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નાટક ભજવ્યું, teṇe nāṭak bhajavyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ભજવવું (bhajavvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. Actors નાટક ભજવે છે, put on a play, and each પાત્ર ભજવે છે, plays a part.

Lesson 5 of 7.
અદ્ભુત (adbhut) — wonderful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to publish, then the Gujarati for to perform.

You'll want to know: અદ્ભુત.
અદ્ભુત — adbhut — "wonderful".
આ નવલકથા અદ્ભુત છે. ā navalkathā adbhut che. This novel is wonderful.

The word, taken apart.
અદ્ભુત (adbhut) is a tatsama, Sanskrit adbhuta, "wonderful, marvellous" (CDIAL 240). It does not change for gender: અદ્ભુત નવલકથા, અદ્ભુત ગીત.
