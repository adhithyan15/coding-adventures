Telugu, chapter 243: To solve, To reduce, To raise, To inform, To transfer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పరిష్కరించు (pariṣkariñcu) — to solve, to settle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to begin, then the Telugu for to iron.

You'll want to know: పరిష్కరించు.
పరిష్కరించు — pariṣkariñcu — "to solve, to settle".

The word, taken apart.
పరిష్కరించు (pariṣkariñcu) is Sanskrit pariṣkāra, which CDIAL 7894 glosses "ornament", made into a verb with the Telugu ending -iñcu.

The word, taken apart.
తగ్గించు (taggiñcu) is native Dravidian, made from taggu, "to decrease, be reduced", which DEDR 3178 lists with Tamil tāḻ, "to fall low, decrease".

Lesson 3 of 5.
పెంచు (peñcu) — to raise, to increase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to solve, then the Telugu for to reduce.

You'll want to know: పెంచు.
పెంచు — peñcu — "to raise, to increase".

The word, taken apart.
పెంచు (peñcu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4436 lists peñcu, "to nourish, rear, increase", with Tamil pēṇu, "to cherish, foster". DEDR 4385 pencu, "potsherd", is a different word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
తెలియజేయు (teliyajēyu) — to inform, to let know.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to reduce, then the Telugu for to raise.

You'll want to know: తెలియజేయు.
తెలియజేయు — teliyajēyu — "to inform, to let know".

The word, taken apart.
తెలియజేయు (teliyajēyu) is native Dravidian: teliya, from teliyu, "to be known" (DEDR 3433), and -jēyu, from cēyu, "to do" (DEDR 1957): "to make known".

Lesson 5 of 5.
బదిలీ చేయు (badilī cēyu) — to transfer.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: బదిలీ చేయు.
బదిలీ చేయు — badilī cēyu — "to transfer".

The word, taken apart.
బదిలీ చేయు (badilī cēyu) pairs badilī, "transfer", from Arabic badal, "exchange", through Persian and Hindustani, with native cēyu, "to do" (DEDR 1957).
