## You'll want to know: el concejal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C601-GOBIERNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el concejal** — "the town councillor".

> La concejala abrió la nueva biblioteca.
>
> *The councillor opened the new library.*

A woman is *la concejala*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*concejal* is built on *concejo* 'town council', inherited from Latin *concilium* 'assembly, meeting' (Lewis & Short).
