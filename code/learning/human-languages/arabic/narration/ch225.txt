Arabic, chapter 225: To pull, To prepare, To repair, To use, To bring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سحب (saḥaba) — to pull; to withdraw (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to change, then the Arabic for to hurry.

You'll want to know: سحب.
سحب — saḥaba — "to pull; to withdraw (money)".

The word, taken apart.
سحب (saḥaba) is built on the native Arabic root s-ḥ-b, "to drag, to draw along" (Lane). Saḥaba is the plain verb, "he pulled".
