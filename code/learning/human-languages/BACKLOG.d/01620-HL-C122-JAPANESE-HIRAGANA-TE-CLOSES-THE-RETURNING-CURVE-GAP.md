## HL-C122 — Japanese hiragana te closes the returning-curve gap

After hiragana **せ** landed, Japanese hiragana **て** led the measured queue at
**3 affected realizations**.

Sirgazil's CC0 28-frame Wikimedia Commons animation supplies the one-run order:
carry the high horizontal left to right, turn back down and left through the
diagonal, then round the broad lower curve and sweep right to the finish. The
learner filmstrip fits that order to the bundled Noto Sans JP outline without
presenting print geometry as an independent handwriting source.

This closes all 3 affected realizations for **て** and reranks Japanese hiragana
**な** first at **3**.
