## You'll want to know: ようきゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C369-JUUYOU369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ようきゅう** — *yōkyū* — "a demand; a firm request".

> **ようきゅうが あったら、かいしゃに 言って ください。** — *yōkyū ga attara, kaisha ni itte kudasai* — "If you have any demands, please tell the company."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ようきゅう** (*yōkyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yō*, "need" (Middle Chinese *'jiewH*) and *kyū*, "request" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
