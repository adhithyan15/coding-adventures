## You'll want to know: 鬼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C332-GHOST332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**鬼** — *guǐ* — "ghost".

> **那个房子里有鬼** *nàge fángzi lǐ yǒu guǐ* — There's a ghost in that house.

The new character, in pieces: 鬼 — nine strokes: a short stroke falling left over a box with a bar inside; from the box two long strokes, one falling to the left, one bending right along the foot and hooking up; and on the right a small turning stroke and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**鬼** (*guǐ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *kjw+jX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the word by one of like sound, 'to return home'.
