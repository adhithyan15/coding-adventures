# Review — advice and reasons

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C319-OTU-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a vote". (*ōṭu*.)
- [YOU WRITE: **ఓ** once from memory; then **ఈ**, from **ఈలోగా**]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C319-OBLIGATIVE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-PROHIBITIVE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-BETTER-TO-01, TE-LEX-C319-OTU-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-ADAM-VALLA-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-REASON-ORDER-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C318-THINK-THAT-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C318-KABATTI-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C318-ANDUKE-RESULT-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C318-IN-MY-OPINION-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: You must not go to school today. You had better stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: I think everyone must vote.]
- [YOU SAY: Because of reading every day, my Telugu is good.]
- [YOU SAY: I did not come, because I had a fever.]
- [YOU SAY: I had a fever. That is why I did not come.]
- [YOU WRITE: **ఓటు**, then **ఈ ఓటు**, "this vote"]

## Wrap-up Recall
