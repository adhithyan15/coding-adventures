# ಎಟಿಎಂ (eṭieṃ) — a cash machine; an ATM

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C439-GADI439-03, KA-LEX-C439-GADI439-04] -->

## You'll want to know: ಎಟಿಎಂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C439-GADI439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಎಟಿಎಂ** — *eṭieṃ* — "a cash machine; an ATM".

> ನಾನು ಎಟಿಎಂನಿಂದ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ದುಡ್ಡು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡೆ.
>
> *nānu eṭieṃninda svalpa duḍḍu tegedukoṇḍe.*
>
> I took some money out of the cash machine.

Said letter by letter, as in English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಎಟಿಎಂ** (*eṭieṃ*) is a loan from English *ATM*, the letters read out one by one.
