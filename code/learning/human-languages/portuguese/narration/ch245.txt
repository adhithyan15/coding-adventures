Portuguese, chapter 245: Dialect, Piece of news, Advertising, Screen, Keyboard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o dialeto — a dialect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for temperature, then the Portuguese for a fly.

You'll want to know: o dialeto.
o dialeto — "a dialect".

The word, taken apart.
dialeto from Latin dialectus, "way of speaking" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing from Latin. Latin took it from Greek dialektos.
