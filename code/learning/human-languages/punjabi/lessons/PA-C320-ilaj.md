## You'll want to know: ਇਲਾਜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C320-CLINIC320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਲਾਜ** — *ilāj* — "medical treatment".

> ਉਸ ਹਸਪਤਾਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਇਲਾਜ ਮੁਫ਼ਤ ਹੈ।
>
> *us haspatāl vich ilāj mufat hai.*
>
> Treatment is free at that hospital.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਲਾਜ** (*ilāj*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ilāj*, "a remedy, treatment".
