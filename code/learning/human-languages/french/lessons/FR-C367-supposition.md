## You'll want to know: la supposition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C367-HYPOTHESE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la supposition** — "the supposition, the assumption".

> Ce n'est qu'une supposition : je n'en suis pas sûr.
>
> *It is only an assumption: I am not sure of it.*

*Faire une supposition* — to make an assumption.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*supposition* is a learned borrowing from Latin *suppositiō* "a putting under, a substitution" (Lewis & Short), from *suppōnere*, built on *pōnere* "to put".
