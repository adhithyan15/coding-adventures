## You'll want to know: नकेल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C233-GEAR233-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नकेल** — *nakel* — "a camel's nose-peg and rein".

> नकेल ढीली है।
>
> *nakel ḍhīlī hai.*
>
> The nose-rein is loose.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नकेल** (*nakel*) is built from a reconstructed *\*nakka*, "nose", and Sanskrit *kīla*, "a peg"; CDIAL lists Hindi *nakel*, the pin fixed in a camel's nose. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6910).
