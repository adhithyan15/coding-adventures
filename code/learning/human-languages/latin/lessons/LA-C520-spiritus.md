## You'll want to know: spīritus, spīritūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C520-SPIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**spīritus, spīritūs** — "breath; spirit".

> Post cursum spīritum vix dūcō.
>
> *After the run I can hardly draw breath.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *spīrō*. Breath first, then the spirit. English *spirit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**spīritus** is the action noun of *spīrō* "to breathe" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *spirit* comes from it.
