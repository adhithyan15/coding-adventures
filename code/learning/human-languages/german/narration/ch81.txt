German, chapter 81: School, Card, Picture, Bag, Money.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Schule — a school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a pen, then the German for paper.

You'll want to know: die Schule.
die Schule — "a school".

The word, taken apart.
Schule from Old High German scuola from Latin schola from Greek scholḗ (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed, like English school. The Greek word first meant "leisure".

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Karte — a card; a map; a ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for paper, then the German for a school.

You'll want to know: die Karte.
die Karte — "a card; a map; a ticket".

The word, taken apart.
Karte from Latin charta from Greek chártēs, "sheet of papyrus" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed, by way of French. English card and chart come from the same word.

The word, taken apart.
Geld from Old High German gelt, "payment" from Proto-Germanic geldą, "reward, payment" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) English yield, first "to pay", shares its root.
