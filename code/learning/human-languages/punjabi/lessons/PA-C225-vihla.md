## You'll want to know: ਵਿਹਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C225-DIN225-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਹਲਾ** — *vihlā* — "free; not busy".

> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਵਿਹਲਾ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *shām nū̃ maiṁ vihlā hundā hāṁ.*
>
> In the evening I am free.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਹਲਾ** (*vihlā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*vēlya*, "at leisure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vihlā*, "vacant, at leisure" (CDIAL 12118).
