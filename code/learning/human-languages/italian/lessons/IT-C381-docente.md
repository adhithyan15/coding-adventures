## You'll want to know: il docente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C381-DOCENTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il docente** — "the teacher, the lecturer".

> Il docente risponde alle domande dopo la lezione.
>
> *The lecturer answers questions after the lesson.*

Formal; one form for a man or a woman. In speech, *il professore*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*docente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *docēns*, present participle of *docēre* "to teach" (Lewis & Short).
