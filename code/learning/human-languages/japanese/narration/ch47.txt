Japanese, chapter 47: Crab, Octopus, Squid, Turtle, Wolf.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
かに (kani) — a crab.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a sheep, then the Japanese for a deer.

You'll want to know: かに.
かに — kani — "a crab".

The word, taken apart.
かに (kani) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana; its kanji, when used, is a kun'yomi attached later (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
