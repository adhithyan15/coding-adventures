## You'll want to know: استأجر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C427-TAKHTIT427-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استأجر** — *istaʾjara* — "to hire; to rent (from someone)".

> استأجرنا سيارة صغيرة في المطار.
>
> *istaʾjarnā sayyāra ṣaghīra fī al-maṭār.*
>
> We hired a small car at the airport.

Present: يستأجر, *yastaʾjir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استأجر** (*istaʾjara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-j-r*, "a wage, a reward" (Lane). It is Form X, "to take something for a payment"; *ījār*, "rent", shares the root.
