## You'll want to know: उबारना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C380-HAL380-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उबारना** — *ubārnā* — "to pull out of trouble, to rescue".

> अगर मीरा नहीं होती तो मुझे कौन उबारता?
>
> *agar Mīrā nahīṃ hotī to mujhe kaun ubārtā?*
>
> If Mira hadn't been there, who would have pulled me out?

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे मुसीबत से उबारा, *usne mujhe musībat se ubārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उबारना** (*ubārnā*) is inherited from *\*udvārayati*, "opens", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2082). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to release".
