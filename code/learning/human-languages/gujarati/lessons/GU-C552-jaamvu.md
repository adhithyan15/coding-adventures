## You'll want to know: જામવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C552-MANCH552-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જામવું** — *jāmvũ* — "to get going (of a show or a party)".

> જો સંગીત સારું હશે તો કાર્યક્રમ જામશે.
>
> *jo sangīt sārũ haśe to kāryakram jāmśe.*
>
> If the music is good, the show will really get going.

Past (it …): તે કાર્યક્રમ જામી ગયો, *te kāryakram jāmī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જામવું** (*jāmvũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *yamyátē*, "is fixed", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to collect, to set" (CDIAL 10428): milk sets into curd, and a show "sets" when it takes hold.
