## You'll want to know: o bibliotecário
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C525-BIBLIOTECA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o bibliotecário** — "librarian".

> O bibliotecário ajudou-me a encontrar o livro.
>
> *The librarian helped me find the book.*

A woman is *a bibliotecária*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bibliotecário* is built on *biblioteca*, a learned borrowing through Latin *bibliothēca* "a library" (Lewis & Short) from Greek *bibliothḗkē*, from *biblíon* "book" and *thḗkē* "a store".
