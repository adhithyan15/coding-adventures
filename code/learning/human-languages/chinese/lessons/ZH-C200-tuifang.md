## You'll want to know: tuìfáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C200-HOTEL200-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìfáng* — "to check out (of a hotel)".

> *míngtiān jǐ diǎn tuìfáng* — What time is check-out tomorrow?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìfáng* is built from *tuì* 'to give back' and 房 'room', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thwojH* and *bjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to draw back'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
