German, chapter 363: Die Umwelt schützen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Umweltschutz — environmental protection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to donate, then the German for to get involved.

You'll want to know: der Umweltschutz.
der Umweltschutz — "environmental protection".
Für den Umweltschutz fahren wir mit dem Fahrrad zur Arbeit. We cycle to work to protect the environment.
No plural. Built from die Umwelt — the environment — and der Schutz — protection.

The word, taken apart.
Umweltschutz joins Umwelt "environment", from um "around" and Welt "world", Middle High German welt (EtymDB), to Schutz "protection", Middle High German schuz (EtymDB). Umwelt is usually said to render Danish omverden "the world around".

Der Klimawandel macht die Sommer immer heißer. Climate change is making the summers hotter and hotter.
No plural. Das Klima — the climate — and der Wandel — change.

The word, taken apart.
Klimawandel joins Klima, from Latin clima and Greek klíma "slope, zone of latitude", from klínein "to slope" (EtymDB), to Wandel "change", inherited from Old High German wandil (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
nachhaltig — sustainable; lasting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for environmental protection, then the German for climate change.

You'll want to know: nachhaltig.
nachhaltig — "sustainable; lasting".
Wir kaufen nur noch nachhaltige Produkte. We only buy sustainable products now.
Die Nachhaltigkeit — sustainability. Ein nachhaltiger Eindruck — a lasting impression.

The word, taken apart.
nachhaltig is built on Nachhalt "something held back, a reserve", from nach and halten "to hold", Proto-Germanic haldaną (EtymDB). It is usually said to have been first a word of forestry, for felling no more trees than grow back.

Wir recyceln Papier, Glas und Plastik. We recycle paper, glass and plastic.
Said the English way: ri-SAI-keln. Perfekt: hat recycelt.

The word, taken apart.
recyceln was borrowed from English recycle (EtymDB), built from re- "again" and cycle "a round", which goes back through Latin cyclus to Greek kýklos "a ring".

Lesson 5 of 5.
verbrauchen — to consume, to use up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for sustainable, then the German for to recycle.

You'll want to know: verbrauchen.
verbrauchen — "to consume, to use up".
Unser alter Kühlschrank verbraucht zu viel Strom. Our old fridge uses too much electricity.
Inseparable: er verbraucht, er hat verbraucht. Der Verbrauch — consumption.

The word, taken apart.
verbrauchen is built with ver- on brauchen "to need, to use", inherited from Old High German brūhhan and Proto-Germanic brūkaną "to use, to consume" (EtymDB).
