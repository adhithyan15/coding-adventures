## You'll want to know: अवस्था
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C370-STHITI370-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवस्था** — *avasthā* — "a state; a condition".

> यदि वृद्धस्य अवस्था उत्तमा, तर्हि सः श्वः गमिष्यति।
>
> *yadi vṛddhasya avasthā uttamā, tarhi saḥ śvaḥ gamiṣyati.*
>
> If the old man's condition is good, he will go tomorrow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवस्था** (*avasthā*) is built on *ava*, "down", and the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives *ava-sthā* "state, condition, situation" (MW).
