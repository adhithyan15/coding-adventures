## You'll want to know: o ministro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C380-POLITICA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o ministro** — "the minister".

> Se eu fosse ministro, construía mais escolas.
>
> *If I were a minister, I would build more schools.*

Feminine *a ministra*. *O primeiro-ministro* — the prime minister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ministro* comes from Latin *minister* "servant, attendant" (EtymDB), built on *minus* "less" (Lewis & Short): first a servant of the state.
