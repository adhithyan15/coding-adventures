## You'll want to know: arrastrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C668-EQUIPAJE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**arrastrar** — "to drag, to pull along".

> Arrastro la maleta por la calle.
>
> *I drag my suitcase along the street.*

*Arrastrar los pies* — to shuffle your feet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arrastrar* is built from *a-* and *rastrar*, on *rastro* "trail" (EtymDB), which comes from Latin *rāstrum* "a rake", from *rādere* "to scrape".
