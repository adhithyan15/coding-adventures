## You'll want to know: विव्हळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C482-DUKH482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विव्हळणे** — *vivhaḷṇe* — "to moan in pain".

> पाय मोडल्यामुळे कुत्रा विव्हळत होता.
>
> *pāy moḍlyāmuḷe kutrā vivhaḷat hotā.*
>
> The dog was moaning with a broken leg.

Past (he …): तो विव्हळला, *to vivhaḷlā*.

Louder than कण्हणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विव्हळणे** (*vivhaḷṇe*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *vihvala*, "agitated" (CDIAL 12038), with the verb ending *-ṇe*; CDIAL gives no Marathi form there.
