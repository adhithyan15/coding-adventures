# हिचकिचाना (hickicānā) — to hesitate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C461-SANKOCH461-01, HI-LEX-C461-SANKOCH461-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Hindi for ashamed, then the Hindi for to shrink back shyly.

## You'll want to know: हिचकिचाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C461-SANKOCH461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हिचकिचाना** — *hickicānā* — "to hesitate".

> सवाल पूछने से पहले अरुण थोड़ा हिचकिचाया।
>
> *savāl pūchne se pahle Aruṇ thoṛā hickicāyā.*
>
> Arun hesitated a little before asking his question.

Past (he …): वह हिचकिचाया, *vah hickicāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिचकिचाना** (*hickicānā*) is probably built on *hicaknā*, "to draw back", which CDIAL lists under *\*hicc*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 14083); the doubled *-cki-* paints the stop-and-start of hesitation.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
