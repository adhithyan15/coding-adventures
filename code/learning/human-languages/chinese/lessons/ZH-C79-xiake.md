## You'll want to know: 下课

**下课** — *xiàkè* — "to finish class".

> **十二点前我们下课** *shí'èr diǎn qián wǒmen xiàkè* — We finish class before twelve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**下课** (*xiàkè*) is built from 下 'to go down from' and 课 'lesson', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *khwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); lesson is not among the senses the dictionary gives the second.
