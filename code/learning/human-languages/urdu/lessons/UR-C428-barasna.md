## You'll want to know: برسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C428-BOOND428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**برسنا** — *barasnā* — "to pour (of rain)".

> رات بھر بادل برسے۔
>
> *rāt bhar bādal barse.*
>
> The clouds poured down all night.

Past (it …): وہ بادل رات بھر برسا, *voh bādal rāt bhar barsā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برسنا** (*barasnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *barasnā*, "to rain", under Sanskrit *várṣati*, "it rains" (CDIAL 11394).
