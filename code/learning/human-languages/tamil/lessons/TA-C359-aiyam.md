## You'll want to know: ஐயம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C359-TIRI359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஐயம்** — *aiyam* — "uncertainty; a doubt".

> இதில் எனக்கு எந்த ஐயமும் இல்லை.
>
> *itil eṉakku enta aiyamum illai.*
>
> I have no doubt about this.

A formal word; the everyday one is சந்தேகம். ஐயமின்றி, without doubt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஐயம்** (*aiyam*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 190 lists Tamil *ayir*, "to suspect", and *aiyam*, "doubt, uncertainty", with Malayalam *ayyam*, "doubt".
