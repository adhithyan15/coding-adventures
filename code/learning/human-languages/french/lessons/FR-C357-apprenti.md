## You'll want to know: l'apprenti
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C357-STAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'apprenti** — "the apprentice".

> Le boulanger a un apprenti de seize ans.
>
> *The baker has a sixteen-year-old apprentice.*

Feminine *apprentie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apprenti* comes from Middle French *apprentis* (EtymDB), built on *apprendre* "to learn", which comes from Latin *apprehendere* "to seize, to grasp" (Lewis & Short).
