Telugu, chapter 403: Running Things.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పరిపాలన (paripālana) — administration; governance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to govern, then the Telugu for to approve.

You'll want to know: పరిపాలన.
పరిపాలన — paripālana — "administration; governance".
మంచి పరిపాలన వల్ల జనం సంతోషంగా ఉన్నారు. mañci paripālana valla janaṁ santōṣaṅgā unnāru. Thanks to good administration the people are happy.
From the same word as పాలించు, with పరి-, all round.

The word, taken apart.
పరిపాలన (paripālana) is borrowed from Sanskrit paripālana, "the act of guarding; fostering" (Monier-Williams), pari-, "all round", added to pālana.

ప్రభుత్వం పేదల కోసం కొత్త పథకం తెచ్చింది. prabhutvaṁ pēdala kōsaṁ kotta pathakaṁ teccindi. The government brought in a new scheme for the poor.

Plural పథకాలు.

The word, taken apart.
పథకం (pathakaṁ) has an origin that is not settled: it looks like a Sanskrit loan, but neither CDIAL nor Monier-Williams gives a source with this sense.

Lesson 5 of 5.
సంక్షేమం (saṅkṣēmaṁ) — welfare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for coordination, then the Telugu for a scheme.

You'll want to know: సంక్షేమం.
సంక్షేమం — saṅkṣēmaṁ — "welfare".
పిల్లల సంక్షేమం కోసం ఆ సంస్థ పని చేస్తుంది. pillala saṅkṣēmaṁ kōsaṁ ā saṁstha pani cēstundi. That organisation works for the welfare of children.
సం-, wholly, + క్షేమం, well-being, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
సంక్షేమం (saṅkṣēmaṁ) is built from Sanskrit saṁ-, "together, wholly", and kṣema, "ease, security, prosperity" (Monier-Williams), the word Telugu has as క్షేమం.
