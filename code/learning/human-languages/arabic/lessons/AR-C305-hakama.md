## You'll want to know: حكم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C305-MAHKAMA305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حكم** — *ḥakama* — "to pass judgment; to rule".

> حكم القاضي على اللص بالسجن.
>
> *ḥakama al-qāḍī ʿalā al-liṣṣ bi-s-sijn.*
>
> The judge sentenced the thief to prison.

Present: يحكم, *yaḥkum*.

حكم على someone بـ: to sentence; حكم البلد: to rule the country. The noun حكم is a verdict.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حكم** (*ḥakama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-m* (Lane), first "to restrain from wrong", then "to judge" and "to rule"; *ḥikma*, wisdom, shares it.
