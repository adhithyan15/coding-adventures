## You'll want to know: a víbora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C461-PEQUENOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a víbora** — "the viper, the adder".

> Na serra há víboras, por isso andamos com cuidado.
>
> *There are vipers in the hills, so we walk carefully.*

Figurative: a spiteful person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*víbora* comes from Latin *vīpera* 'viper' (EtymDB).
