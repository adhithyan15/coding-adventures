# quién — “who?” in one gentle step

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-TU, ES-LEX-USTED, ES-REGISTER-TU-USTED] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You can choose **tú** or **usted** for the person in front of you.
Now add one tiny question about a person: **¿Quién?** — “Who?”

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-QUIEN]; assesses=[] -->

Say **quién** like *kyen*, one smooth beat. It asks for a **person**:

> — **¿Quién?** — Who?
> — **Ana.**

Hear the word as one piece for now. The next micro-lesson slows down and
explains the written accent that Spanish question words carry.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-QUIEN] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **¿Quién?** — then answer with one name]
- [YOU SAY: **tú**, **usted** — you; **¿Quién?** — who]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-TU, ES-LEX-USTED, ES-REGISTER-TU-USTED, ES-LEX-QUIEN] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which two words mean “you”? (**Tú, usted**.) Which word asks
“who”? (**Quién**.) Say it once more, slowly. Stop there.
