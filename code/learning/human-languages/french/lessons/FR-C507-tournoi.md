## You'll want to know: le tournoi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C507-TOURNOI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le tournoi** — "tournament".

> Notre école organise un tournoi de basket.
>
> *Our school is organising a basketball tournament.*

In the Middle Ages, a contest between knights.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le tournoi* is built on *tournoyer* "to wheel about", from *tourner* "to turn", which comes from Latin *tornāre* "to turn on a lathe" (Lewis & Short).
