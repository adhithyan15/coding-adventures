## You'll want to know: چیرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C478-CUT478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**چیرا** — *chīrā* — "an incision; a slit".

> ڈاکٹر نے ہاتھ پر ایک چھوٹا چیرا دیا۔
>
> *ḍākṭar ne hāth par ek chhoṭā chīrā diyā.*
>
> The doctor made a small incision in the hand.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چیرا** (*chīrā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cīrā*, "a cut, a slit", under Sanskrit *cīra*, "a strip of bark or cloth" (CDIAL 4843).
