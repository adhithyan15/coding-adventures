## You'll want to know: آنگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C303-EBTEDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آنگاه** (*ângâh*) — "then, at that point".

> شام خوردیم و آنگاه به پارک رفتیم.
>
> *Shâm khordim va ângâh be pârk raftim.*
>
> We had dinner and then went to the park.

Literary; in speech *ba'd*. In writing it can answer an *agar* clause: "if ..., then ...".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ângâh* is built from Persian *ân* "that" and *gâh* "time, place", both native words: "that time".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
