Lesson 1 of 5.
मधुमेह (madhumeh) — diabetes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a virus, then the Marathi for an epidemic.

You'll want to know: मधुमेह.
मधुमेह — madhumeh — "diabetes".
मधुमेहामुळे आजोबा साखर खात नाहीत. madhumehāmuḷe ājobā sākhar khāt nāhīt. Grandfather does not eat sugar because of his diabetes.
Masculine.
मधु, honey, + मेह, urine: an old name for the sugar sickness.

The word, taken apart.
मधुमेह (madhumeh) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit madhumeha, "honey-like urine, diabetes" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
रक्तदाब (raktadāb) — blood pressure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an epidemic, then the Marathi for diabetes.

You'll want to know: रक्तदाब.
रक्तदाब — raktadāb — "blood pressure".
दवाखान्यात त्यांनी माझा रक्तदाब तपासला. davākhānyāt tyānnī mājhā raktadāb tapāslā. At the clinic they checked my blood pressure.
Masculine.
रक्त + दाब, pressure, from दाबणे.

The word, taken apart.
रक्तदाब (raktadāb) is a modern Marathi compound of रक्त, "blood", a tatsama of Sanskrit rakta (CDIAL 10539), and दाब, "pressure", the noun of दाबणे.

कावीळ — kāvīḷ — "jaundice".
काविळीत डोळे पिवळे दिसतात. kāviḷīt ḍoḷe pivḷe distāt. In jaundice the eyes look yellow.
Feminine.
Feminine; oblique काविळी-: काविळीत, in jaundice.

The word, taken apart.
कावीळ (kāvīḷ) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit kāmalā; CDIAL lists Marathi kāvīḷ, "jaundice" (CDIAL 3044).
