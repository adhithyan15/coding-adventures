## You'll want to know: படை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C332-POR332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**படை** — *paṭai* — "an army; a force".

> மன்னரின் படை ஆற்றைக் கடந்தது.
>
> *maṉṉariṉ paṭai āṟṟaik kaṭantatu.*
>
> The king's army crossed the river.

Also a band of people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**படை** (*paṭai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3860): army, crowd, weapons, battle.
