## You'll want to know: friggere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C334-SOFFRITTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**friggere** — "to fry".

> Ho fritto il pesce in tanto olio.
>
> *I fried the fish in plenty of oil.*

Past participle *fritto*; *il fritto misto* is a plate of mixed fried food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*friggere* is inherited from Latin *frīgere* "to roast, to fry" (EtymDB).
