## You'll want to know: gūdān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C459-MIND459-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gūdān* — "lonely; on one's own".

> *wǒ yí ge rén zhù le liǎng nián yǒudiǎnr gūdān* — I've lived on my own for two years, and I'm a bit lonely.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gūdān* is built from *gū* 'an orphan' and *dān* 'single', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ku* and *tan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a fatherless child' and the second as single against double.
