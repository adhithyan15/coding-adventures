## You'll want to know: tricoter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C496-COUTURE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tricoter** — "to knit".

> Mon père tricote une écharpe pour l'hiver.
>
> *My father is knitting a scarf for the winter.*

*Le tricot* — knitting; also a knitted jumper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tricoter* goes back to Old French *tricoter* (EtymDB), which once meant "to beat with sticks". Its origin is not settled; a Germanic source has been proposed.
