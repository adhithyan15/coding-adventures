## You'll want to know: el consumo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C607-AMBIENTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el consumo** — "the consumption".

> El consumo de agua es muy alto.
>
> *Water consumption is very high.*

*La sociedad de consumo* — the consumer society.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consumo* is built on *consumir*, a learned borrowing from Latin *consumere* 'to use up', from *sumere* 'to take' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
