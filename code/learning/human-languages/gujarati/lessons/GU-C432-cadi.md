# ચાંદી (cā̃dī) — silver

## You'll want to know: ચાંદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C432-GOLD432-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાંદી** — *cā̃dī* — "silver".

> આ વાટકી ચાંદીની છે.
>
> *ā vāṭkī cā̃dīnī che.*
>
> This little bowl is made of silver.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાંદી** (*cā̃dī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *candrikā*, "moonlight"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "silver" (CDIAL 4669): the metal with the moon's colour.
