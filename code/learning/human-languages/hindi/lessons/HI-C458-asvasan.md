## You'll want to know: आश्वासन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C458-TANAV458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्वासन** — *āśvāsan* — "an assurance; comforting words".

> डाक्टर के आश्वासन से मीरा की चिंता कम हुई।
>
> *ḍākṭar ke āśvāsan se Mīrā kī cintā kam huī.*
>
> The doctor's assurance eased Mira's worry.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आश्वासन** (*āśvāsan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āśvāsana*, "reviving; consoling, encouraging" (Monier-Williams).
