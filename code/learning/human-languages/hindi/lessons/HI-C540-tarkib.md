## You'll want to know: तरकीब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C540-KOSHISH540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तरकीब** — *tarkīb* — "a trick; a method (for doing something)".

> मीरा के पास हर समस्या की कोई तरकीब होती है।
>
> *Mīrā ke pās har samasyā kī koī tarkīb hotī hai.*
>
> Mira always has some trick for every problem.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तरकीब** (*tarkīb*) is usually traced to Arabic *tarkīb*, "putting together", through Persian.
