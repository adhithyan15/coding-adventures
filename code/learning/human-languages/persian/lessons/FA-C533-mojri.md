## You'll want to know: مجری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C533-MEDIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مجری** (*mojri*) — "a presenter, a host (on TV or radio)".

> مجری این برنامه خیلی شوخ است.
>
> *mojri-ye in barnâme kheyli shukh ast.*
>
> The presenter of this programme is very witty.

Plural *mojriyân* or *mojrihâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mojri* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *mujrī*, 'one who carries something out'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
