Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: اکاؤنٹ.
اکاؤنٹ — akāunṭ — "a bank account".
میرا اکاؤنٹ اس بینک میں ہے۔ merā akāunṭ is baink meṅ hai. My account is with this bank.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اکاؤنٹ (akāunṭ) is borrowed from English account.

Lesson 2 of 6.
رقم (raqam) — a sum of money; an amount.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to spread (something) around, then the Urdu for a bank account.

You'll want to know: رقم.
رقم — raqam — "a sum of money; an amount".
اکاؤنٹ میں رقم کم ہے۔ akāunṭ meṅ raqam kam hai. There isn't much money in the account.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
رقم (raqam) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic raqam, "a figure, a written mark"; in Urdu it is a sum of money.

Lesson 5 of 6.
نکلوانا (nikalvānā) — to withdraw (money); to have (something) taken out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for cash, then the Urdu for a cheque.

You'll want to know: نکلوانا.
نکلوانا — nikalvānā — "to withdraw (money); to have (something) taken out".
سارا نے بینک سے پیسے نکلوائے۔ sārā ne baink se paise nikalvāe. Sara took some money out of the bank.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رقم نکلوائی, us ne raqam nikalvāī.

The word, taken apart.
نکلوانا (nikalvānā) is built on نکلنا, "to come out", with the ending -vā- for getting someone else to do it: you have the bank take your money out. نکلنا comes from niṣkalati, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 7478).

One more word for getting around.
