## You'll want to know: membrāna, membrānae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C551-CAPS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**membrāna, membrānae** — "parchment; skin".

> Poēta versūs in membrānā scrībit.
>
> *The poet writes the verses on parchment.*

Feminine, first declension, from *membrum*. Thin skin, then parchment for writing. English *membrane*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**membrāna** is built on *membrum* "limb" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB): the skin over the limbs, then parchment. English *membrane* comes from it.
