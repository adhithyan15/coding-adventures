## You'll want to know: ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C290-WEEK290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਣਾ** — *khullhṇā* — "to clear up; to come open".

> ਦੁਪਹਿਰ ਤੱਕ ਮੌਸਮ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹੇਗਾ।
>
> *dupahir tak mausam khullhegā.*
>
> The weather will clear up by noon.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਿਆ, *oh darvāzā khullhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਣਾ** (*khullhṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khull*, "to be open"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khullhṇā*, "to be open", under it (CDIAL 3945).
