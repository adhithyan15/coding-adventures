## You'll want to know: भीषयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C463-PARAKRAMA463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भीषयते** — *bhīṣayate* — "frightens".

> सिंहः गर्जनेन मृगान् भीषयते।
>
> *siṃhaḥ garjanena mṛgān bhīṣayate.*
>
> The lion frightens the deer with its roar.

Past (it …): सिंहः मृगान् भीषितवान्, *siṃhaḥ mṛgān bhīṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भीषयते** (*bhīṣayate*) is built on the causative of the root √bhī, "to fear", the root of **बिभेति** and **भयम्**: to make afraid; MW records this causative *bhīṣayate*, citing Pāṇini 1.3.68 (MW).
