## You'll want to know: ರಕ್ತಸ್ರಾವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C512-ROGA512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಕ್ತಸ್ರಾವ** — *raktasrāva* — "bleeding; a haemorrhage".

> ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನಿಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ರಕ್ತಸ್ರಾವ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *apaghātadalli avanige tumbā raktasrāva āyitu.*
>
> He bled heavily in the accident.

ಸ್ರಾವ is a flow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಕ್ತಸ್ರಾವ** (*raktasrāva*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *rakta*, "blood" (CDIAL 10539), and *srāva*, "a flowing" (Monier-Williams).
