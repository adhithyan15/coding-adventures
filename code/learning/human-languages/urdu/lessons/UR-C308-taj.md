## You'll want to know: تاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C308-SHAHENSHAH308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تاج** — *tāj* — "a crown".

> اس نے تاج پہنا۔
>
> *us ne tāj pahnā.*
>
> He put on the crown.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تاج** (*tāj*) is usually traced to Persian *tāj*, "a crown". It is masculine: **تاج پہنا**.
