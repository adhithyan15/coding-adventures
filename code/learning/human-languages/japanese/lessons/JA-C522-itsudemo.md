## You'll want to know: いつでも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C522-SOME522-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いつでも** — *itsudemo* — "at any time".

> **もし こまったら、いつでも でんわして ください。** — *moshi komattara, itsudemo denwa shite kudasai* — "If you are in trouble, call me any time."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いつでも** (*itsudemo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from *itsu*, "when", and *demo*, "even" (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
