# यथेष्टम् (yatheṣṭam) — as much as one likes

## You'll want to know: यथेष्टम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C380-MATRA380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**यथेष्टम्** — *yatheṣṭam* — "as much as one likes".

> यदि भवान् क्षुधितः, तर्हि यथेष्टं खादतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān kṣudhitaḥ, tarhi yatheṣṭaṃ khādatu.*
>
> If you are hungry, eat as much as you like.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यथेष्टम्** (*yatheṣṭam*) is built from *yathā*, "as", and *iṣṭa*, "wished"; MW gives *yatheṣṭam* "according to wish, at pleasure" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
