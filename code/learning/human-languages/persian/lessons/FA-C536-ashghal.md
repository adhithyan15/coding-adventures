## You'll want to know: آشغال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C536-CHORES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**آشغال** (*âshghâl*) — "rubbish, trash".

> پدرم شبها آشغالها را بیرون میبرد.
>
> *pedaram shabhâ âshghâlhâ râ birun mibarad.*
>
> My father takes the rubbish out at night.

Everyday; *zobâle* is more formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âshghâl* is the everyday word for rubbish, but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled.
