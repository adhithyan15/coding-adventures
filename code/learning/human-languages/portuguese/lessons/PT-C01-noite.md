# noite / boa noite — "night," and the *-ct-* → *-it-* rule

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] "Night" — and a sound-rule shared with French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-ETYMON-NOITE-02, PT-LEX-NOITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a noite** (fem.) ← Latin **noctem** (*noct-*) → English **noct**urnal,
equi**nox**. Portuguese turned Latin *-ct-* into **-it-** (*noite*) — the same
change French made (*nuit*), while Spanish went *-ch-* (*noche*) and Italian
*-tt-* (*notte*):

| Latin | Portuguese | Spanish | French | Italian |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *noctem* | no**it**e | no**ch**e | nu**it** | no**tt**e |
| *factum* | fe**it**o | he**ch**o | fa**it** | fa**tt**o |

**boa noite** = "good night" — used both as an evening greeting and the farewell
for bed.
