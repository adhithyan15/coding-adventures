## You'll want to know: نمونہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C351-MUTALAA351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نمونہ** — *namūnā* — "a sample; a model".

> نرس نے خون کا نمونہ لیا۔
>
> *nars ne khūn kā namūnā liyā.*
>
> The nurse took a blood sample.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نمونہ** (*namūnā*) is usually traced to Persian *namūna*, "a sample, a model".
