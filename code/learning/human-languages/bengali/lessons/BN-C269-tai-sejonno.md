# তাই / সেজন্য (tāi / sejônyô) — so, that is why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-LEX-C159-KARON159-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I had to walk". (*āmāke hẽṭe jete hôlô*.) Then **তাই**, "so", from chapter one hundred and fifty-nine.

## You'll want to know: so
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TAI-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C159-KARON159-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-LEX-C59-FOOD59-04] -->

**কারণ** puts the result first and the reason after it. **তাই** turns that round: the reason first, then "so", then the result.

> **বাইরে ঝড় হচ্ছিল, তাই আমরা বাড়িতে থাকলাম।**
>
> *bāire jhôṛ hôchchhilô, tāi āmrā bāṛite thāklām.* — "There was a storm outside, so we stayed at home."

## You'll want to know: that is why
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C269-SEJONNO-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C44-BODY44-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-JONNO-01] -->

**সেজন্য** (*sejônyô*), "for that, that is why", is **সে**, "that", and the **জন্য** of the last chapter. It usually opens a new sentence:

> **আমার জ্বর ছিল। সেজন্য আমাকে বাড়িতে থাকতে হল।**
>
> *āmār jbôr chhilô. sejônyô āmāke bāṛite thākte hôlô.* — "I had a fever. That's why I had to stay at home."

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "There was a storm, so we stayed at home" — *jhôṛ hôchchhilô, tāi āmrā bāṛite thāklām*]
- [YOU SAY: "I had a fever. That's why I had to stay at home." — *āmār jbôr chhilô. sejônyô āmāke bāṛite thākte hôlô.*]
- [YOU SAY: "The train was late, so I took a bus" — *ṭren derite elô, tāi āmi bāse gelām*]
