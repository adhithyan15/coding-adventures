## You'll want to know: कार्यशाळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C513-KARYASHALA513-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कार्यशाळा** — *kāryaśāḷā* — "a workshop; a training session".

> शिक्षकांसाठी तीन दिवसांची कार्यशाळा झाली.
>
> *śikṣakāṁsāṭhī tīn divsāñcī kāryaśāḷā jhālī.*
>
> There was a three-day workshop for teachers.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कार्यशाळा** (*kāryaśāḷā*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *kārya*, "work", and *śālā*, "hall" (Monier-Williams).
