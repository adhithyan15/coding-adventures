## You'll want to know: পরশ্রীকাতর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C451-GHRINA451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পরশ্রীকাতর** — *pôrôshrīkātôr* — "envious of others' good fortune".

> প্রতিবেশীর নতুন গাড়ি দেখে লোকটা পরশ্রীকাতর হল।
>
> *prôtibeshīr nôtun gāṛi dekhe lokṭā pôrôshrīkātôr hôlô.*
>
> The man grew envious when he saw his neighbour's new car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরশ্রীকাতর** (*pôrôshrīkātôr*) puts together Sanskrit *para*, "another", *śrī*, "good fortune", and *kātara*, "despairing, agitated" (Monier-Williams): pained by someone else's good luck.
