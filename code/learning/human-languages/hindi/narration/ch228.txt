Lesson 1 of 5.
उगना (ugnā) — to grow, to sprout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to split, then the Hindi for to sow.

You'll want to know: उगना.
उगना — ugnā — "to grow, to sprout".

The word, taken apart.
उगना (ugnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from udgāti, "rises, comes forth", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 1954).

पकड़ाना — pakṛānā — "to hand over".

The word, taken apart.
पकड़ाना (pakṛānā) is the causing form of pakaṛnā, "to hold": to hand over is to make someone hold. CDIAL lists pakaṛnā under a reconstructed pakkaḍ, "seize" (CDIAL 7619).
