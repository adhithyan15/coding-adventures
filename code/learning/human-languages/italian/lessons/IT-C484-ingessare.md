## You'll want to know: ingessare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C484-GESSO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ingessare** — "to put in a plaster cast".

> In ospedale mi hanno ingessato la gamba.
>
> *At the hospital they put my leg in a cast.*

*Ingessato* — in a cast: *ho il braccio ingessato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingessare* is built on *gesso* "plaster", inherited from Latin *gypsum*, from Greek *gýpsos* (EtymDB).
