## You'll want to know: lājī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C142-CHORE142-02]; assesses=[] -->

*lājī* — "rubbish".

> *ménkǒu yǒu lājī* — There's rubbish by the door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lājī* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
