## You'll want to know: فرش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C552-TARMIM552-05]; assesses=[] -->

**فرش** — *farasha* — "to furnish; to spread".

> فرشنا الشقة الجديدة.
>
> *farashnā ash-shaqqa al-jadīda.*
>
> We furnished the new flat.

Present: يفرش, *yafrush*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرش** (*farasha*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-sh*, "to spread out" (Lane). Firāsh, a mattress, which you know, is from the same root: a thing spread out.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
