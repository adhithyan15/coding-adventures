## You'll want to know: o protesto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C364-PROTESTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o protesto** — "the protest".

> Houve um protesto em frente à câmara.
>
> *There was a protest in front of the town hall.*

*Em protesto contra* — in protest against.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protesto* is built on *protestar* "to protest", from Latin *prōtestārī* "to declare in public, to bear witness", on *testis* "witness" (Lewis & Short).
