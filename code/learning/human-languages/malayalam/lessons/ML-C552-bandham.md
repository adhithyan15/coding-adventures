## You'll want to know: ബന്ധം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C552-BANDHAM552-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ബന്ധം** — *bandhaṁ* — "a relationship; a tie".

> സഹപ്രവർത്തകരുമായി എനിക്ക് നല്ല ബന്ധമാണ്.
>
> *sahapravarttakarumāyi enikkŭ nalla bandhamāṇŭ.*
>
> I have a good relationship with my colleagues.

ബന്ധു, a relative, shares its root; ബന്ധപ്പെടുക is to get in touch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ബന്ധം** (*bandhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bandha*, "a bond, a tie" (CDIAL 9136).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
