### Changed — French's forward-reference pin falls from 156 to 52

- **`FORWARD_REFERENCE_CEILINGS` pins French at 52**, down from the 156 it was
  pinned at when the table was introduced. The French lessons were reworded so
  they stop leaning on words a later lesson teaches (see the French changelog).
  No detector rule changed and no exemption was added.
- **The ceiling comment names every hit left.** Most are a phrase the track
  teaches whole, used before a late vocabulary lesson gives one of its words a
  headword of its own: de rien, à plus tard, au revoir, et vous ? / et toi ?,
  et quart / et demie, au printemps, du pain / de l'eau, j'ai vingt ans, passé
  simple, les lettres que j'ai écrites, j'ai mal au ventre, C'est combien ? /
  combien de, est-ce que, la pomme de terre, remplir un formulaire, offrir un
  cadeau, and the être / ser · estar comparison headword. Eight are not French
  borrowing at all: the Roman war-god Mars against FR-C09-mars, "March", and
  the English gloss "to vote" against FR-C248's le vote.
- **The comment's preamble names one more shape.** A word a grammar lesson
  teaches registers no matcher, so its credit falls to a later word lesson:
  au, taught by FR-C35-au-aux, is credited to FR-C157-au-dessus, whose
  hyphenated headword the splitter reads as au plus dessus.
