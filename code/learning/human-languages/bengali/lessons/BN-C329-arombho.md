## You'll want to know: আরম্ভ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C329-SHESHPATA329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**আরম্ভ** — *ārômbhô* — "a start; a commencement".

> উপন্যাসটা একটা ঝড়ের রাতে আরম্ভ হয়।
>
> *upônyāsṭā ekṭā jhôṛer rāte ārômbhô hôy.*
>
> The novel begins on a stormy night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আরম্ভ** (*ārômbhô*) is Sanskrit *ārambha*, 'undertaking, beginning' (Monier-Williams). It is more formal than **শুরু**.
