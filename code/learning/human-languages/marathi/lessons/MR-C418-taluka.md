## You'll want to know: तालुका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C418-NAGAR418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तालुका** — *tālukā* — "a taluka".

> आमचा तालुका डोंगराळ आहे.
>
> *āmcā tālukā ḍoṅgrāḷ āhe.*
>
> Our taluka is hilly.

Masculine.

A part of a district, with its own town office; the plural is the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तालुका** (*tālukā*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *taʿalluqa*, "a dependency, an estate".
