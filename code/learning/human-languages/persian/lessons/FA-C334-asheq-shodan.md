## You'll want to know: عاشق شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C334-ASHEQ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عاشق شدن** (*'âsheq shodan*) — "to fall in love".

> او در دانشگاه عاشق همکلاسی اش شد.
>
> *U dar dâneshgâh 'âsheq-e hamkelâsi-ash shod.*
>
> He fell in love with his classmate at university.

The loved one follows with the ezafe: *'âsheq-e u shod*. Present *'âsheq mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'âsheq* "in love, a lover" is borrowed from Arabic *'āshiq*, "lover" (EtymDB). *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
