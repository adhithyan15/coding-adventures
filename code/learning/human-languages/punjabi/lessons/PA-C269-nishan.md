## You'll want to know: ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C269-WAY269-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ** — *nishān* — "a mark; a sign".

> ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਲਾਲ ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ ਹੈ।
>
> *saṛak utte lāl nishān hai.*
>
> There is a red mark on the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ** (*nishān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *nishān*, "a mark, a sign".
