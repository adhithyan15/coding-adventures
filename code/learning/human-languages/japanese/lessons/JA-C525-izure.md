## You'll want to know: いずれ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C525-REAL525-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いずれ** — *izure* — "sooner or later; some day".

> **いずれ おとなに なったら、むすこも わかるでしょう。** — *izure otona ni nattara, musuko mo wakaru deshō* — "Some day, when he grows up, my son will understand too."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いずれ** (*izure*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
