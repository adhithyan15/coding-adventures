## Guided Practice: not, and yes or no
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-PRESENT-E-01, GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01, GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-PRESENT-IE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-PRESENT-O-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NEGATE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NA-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NAHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-KOI-NATHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-KAI-NATHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-KYAREY-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POLAR-SHUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POLAR-RISE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-KE-NAHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-KHARUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-SG-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-PL-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-AGREE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-SG-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-PL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *they*; *she speaks Gujarati*; *we speak Gujarati*; *you speak Gujarati*]
- [YOU SAY: *I don't speak Gujarati*; *don't worry*; *not now*]
- [YOU SAY: *nobody is here*; *I'm not eating anything*; *she never drinks tea*]
- [YOU SAY: *do you speak Gujarati?*, with and without **શું**; *are you coming or not?*; *you live here, right?*]
- [YOU SAY: *I have a fever*; *they like Gujarati*; *my sister*; *her name is Mira*; *our village*, yours and mine]

- [YOU SAY: *I was there*; *the house was small*; *yesterday, at home*]
- [YOU SAY: *I went*; *she came*; *I got worried*; *I came late*]
