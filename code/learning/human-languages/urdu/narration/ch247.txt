Urdu, chapter 247: Short, Possible, Impossible, Useful, Dangerous.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ناٹا (nāṭā) — short (of a person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a border, then the Urdu for a government.

You'll want to know: ناٹا.
ناٹا — nāṭā — "short (of a person)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nāṭā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nāṭā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nāṭā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for a border, then the Urdu for a government, then say nāṭā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ناٹا (nāṭā) mean? ("Short (of a person)".) Say it once more.

(بجھانا, bujhānā.) To drive? (چلانا, chalānā.) To halt? (رکنا, ruknā.) To turn? (مڑنا, muṛnā.) To bring up? (پالنا, pālnā.) To sow? (بونا, bonā.) To cultivate? (اگانا, ugānā.) To sweep? (جھاڑو لگانا, jhāṛū lagānā.) To wipe? (پونچھنا, ponchhnā.) To complain? (شکایت کرنا, shikāyat karnā.) To compete? (مقابلہ کرنا, muqābalā karnā.) To try? (کوشش کرنا, koshish karnā.) To finish?
