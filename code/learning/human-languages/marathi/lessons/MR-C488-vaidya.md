## You'll want to know: वैद्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C488-RUGNA488-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वैद्य** — *vaidya* — "a physician".

> गावातल्या वैद्यांनी आजीची नाडी पाहिली.
>
> *gāvātlyā vaidyānnī ājīcī nāḍī pāhilī.*
>
> The village physician felt Grandmother's pulse.

Masculine.

Traditionally an Ayurvedic doctor; दंतवैद्य, a dentist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वैद्य** (*vaidya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vaidya*, "learned; a physician" (CDIAL 12140).
