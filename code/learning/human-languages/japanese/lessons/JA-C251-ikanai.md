# いかない / たべない (ikanai / tabenai) — the plain "not"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C251-FUTURE-02, JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-03, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-TABERU] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say, to a friend, "I will go tomorrow". (*ashita iku*.) Then say "I will not go", politely. (*ikimasen*.) This lesson gives the plain "not".

## You'll want to know: -ない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-05, JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-04, JA-LEX-C27-VERBS27-04, JA-LEX-KOOHII, JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-02] -->

A friend asks whether you are going tomorrow. The plain answer:

> **いかない。**
>
> *ikanai.* — "I won't go."

> **あした いかない。**
>
> *ashita ikanai.* — "I won't go tomorrow."

**いかない** (*ikanai*) is **いきません** (*ikimasen*) without the politeness. A verb that drops **る** for **た** drops it for **ない** too: **たべる** (*taberu*) has **たべない** (*tabenai*).

> **にくは たべない。**
>
> *niku wa tabenai.* — "I do not eat meat."

The other verbs take the sound of their last kana in the **a** row: **いく** has **いかない**, **のむ** (*nomu*) has **のまない** (*nomanai*), **まつ** (*matsu*) has **またない** (*matanai*). A verb in **う** takes **わ**: **かう** (*kau*) has **かわない** (*kawanai*).

> **コーヒーは のまない。**
>
> *kōhī wa nomanai.* — "I do not drink coffee."

Three are short: **する** has **しない** (*shinai*), **くる** has **こない** (*konai*), and **ある** (*aru*), "there is", has plain **ない** (*nai*).

> **じかんが ない。**
>
> *jikan ga nai.* — "I have no time."

## You'll want to know: -なかった
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C35-FOOD35-04, JA-LEX-C133-KINOU] -->

For "did not", the last **い** of **ない** becomes **かった**: **いかなかった** (*ikanakatta*), **たべなかった** (*tabenakatta*). It is **ませんでした** without the politeness.

> **きのう いかなかった。**
>
> *kinō ikanakatta.* — "I did not go yesterday."

> **あさごはんを たべなかった。**
>
> *asagohan o tabenakatta.* — "I did not eat breakfast."

Like the plain past, the plain "not" has a second job that later lessons give it: it goes in front of the word that keeps a sentence polite.
