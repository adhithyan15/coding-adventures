## You'll want to know: दिलेर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C470-SAHAS470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलेर** — *diler* — "bold; fearless".

> मीरा बहुत दिलेर है, चोर को देखकर भी नहीं डरी।
>
> *Mīrā bahut diler hai, cor ko dekhkar bhī nahīṃ ḍarī.*
>
> Mira is very bold; she wasn't afraid even when she saw the thief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलेर** (*diler*) is usually traced to Persian *dilēr*, "bold", built on *dil*, "heart".
