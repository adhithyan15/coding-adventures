## You'll want to know: استبيان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C391-BAHTH391-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استبيان** — *istibyān* — "a questionnaire".

> ملأ الطلاب استبيانا قصيرا.
>
> *malaʾa aṭ-ṭullāb istibyānan qaṣīran.*
>
> The students filled in a short questionnaire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استبيان** (*istibyān*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-y-n*, "to be apart; to become clear" (Lane). *Istibyān* is a Form X verbal noun, seeking to make a thing clear: a questionnaire.
