## You'll want to know: بغاوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C312-BAGHAVAT312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بغاوت** — *baghāvat* — "a rebellion; a revolt".

> شہر میں بغاوت ہوئی۔
>
> *shahr meṅ baghāvat huī.*
>
> There was a rebellion in the city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بغاوت** (*baghāvat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *baghy*, "a revolt". It is feminine: **بغاوت ہوئی**.
