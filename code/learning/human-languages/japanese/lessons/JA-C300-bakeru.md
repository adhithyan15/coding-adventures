## You'll want to know: ばける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C300-KITSUNE300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ばける** — *bakeru* — "to take the form of; to disguise oneself".

> **むかしばなしで、たぬきが おじいさんに ばけました。** — *mukashibanashi de, tanuki ga ojiisan ni bakemashita* — "In an old tale, a raccoon dog turned itself into an old man."

Polite **ばけます**, *bakemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **きつねが おんなに ばけました。**, *kitsune ga onna ni bakemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばける** (*bakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
