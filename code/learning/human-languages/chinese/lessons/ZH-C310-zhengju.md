## You'll want to know: zhèngjù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C310-SUSPECT310-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngjù* — "evidence; proof".

> *jǐngchá zhǎodào le zhèngjù* — The police found the evidence.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngjù* is built from *zhèng* 'to verify' and *jù* 'to lean on', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyingH* and *kjoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to rely on' and 'to hold': what a proof can lean on.
