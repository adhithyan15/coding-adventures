## You'll want to know: जामो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C190-DHOTI190-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जामो** — *jāmo* — "a long robe".

> बाप लाल जामो पैरै है।
>
> *bāp lāl jāmo pairai hai.*
>
> Father wears a red robe.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जामो** (*jāmo*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *jāma*, "a garment".
