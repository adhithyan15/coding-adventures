## You'll want to know: অগত্যা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C537-MONOSTHIR537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অগত্যা** — *ôgôtyā* — "with no other choice; perforce".

> বাস এল না, অগত্যা আমরা হেঁটে গেলাম।
>
> *Bās elô nā, ôgôtyā āmrā hẽṭe gelām.*
>
> The bus didn't come, so we had no choice but to walk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অগত্যা** (*ôgôtyā*) is Sanskrit *agatyā*, "unavoidably" (Monier-Williams), literally "for lack of a way".
