## You'll want to know: નિવૃત્ત થવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C252-DUTY252-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નિવૃત્ત થવું** — *nivṛtt thavũ* — "to retire".

> **મારા પિતા આ વરસે નિવૃત્ત થાય છે.** — *mārā pitā ā varse nivṛtt thāy che* — "My father retires this year."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિવૃત્ત થવું** (*nivṛtt thavũ*) is built from *nivṛtt*, Sanskrit *nivṛtta*, "turned back, ceased", taken over as a tatsama, and the verb *thavũ*, "to become". CDIAL has no entry for *nivṛtta*.
