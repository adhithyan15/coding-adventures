## You'll want to know: اجتهاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C421-FURSA421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اجتهاد** — *ijtihād* — "diligence".

> نجح الطالب باجتهاده.
>
> *najaḥa aṭ-ṭālib bi-jtihādih.*
>
> The student succeeded through hard work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجتهاد** (*ijtihād*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-h-d*, "to strive, to exert oneself" (Lane). *Ijtihād* is the Form VIII verbal noun, exerting oneself: diligence. مجتهد, hard-working, is from the same root.
