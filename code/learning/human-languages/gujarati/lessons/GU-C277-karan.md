# કારણ (kāraṇ) — a reason; a cause

## You'll want to know: કારણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C277-KARAN-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C102-CAN102-02, GU-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

**કારણ** — *kāraṇ* — "a reason; a cause". It is neuter: **તેનું કારણ**, "the reason for it".

> **તેનું કારણ શું છે?**
>
> *tenũ kāraṇ śũ che?* — "What is the reason for it?"

> **મારી પાસે બે કારણ છે.**
>
> *mārī pāse be kāraṇ che.* — "I have two reasons."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કારણ** (*kāraṇ*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *kāraṇa*, "cause" (CDIAL 3057), "what makes a thing happen", from the root *kṛ-*, "to do", that also stands behind **કરવું**.
