## Script — a frame, two dots and a long bar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-MU-MOTHER-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 母

**The frame, two strokes.** One that runs down the left side and turns right along the bottom. Then one that runs along the top, turns down the right side and hooks to the left at the foot.

**A dot** inside the top half.

**A long bar** right across the middle, wider than the frame on both sides.

**A dot** inside the bottom half.

2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = five.
