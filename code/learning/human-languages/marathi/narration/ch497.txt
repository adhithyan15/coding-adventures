Marathi, chapter 497: Elder and Younger.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धाकटा (dhākṭā) — younger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a brother's wife, then the Marathi for a child's father-in-law.

You'll want to know: धाकटा.
धाकटा — dhākṭā — "younger".
माझा धाकटा भाऊ शाळेत जातो. mājhā dhākṭā bhāū śāḷet jāto. My younger brother goes to school.
Agrees: धाकटा (dhākṭā) भाऊ, धाकटी बहीण.

The word, taken apart.
धाकटा (dhākṭā) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
चुलत (culat) — of a paternal uncle; cousin on the father's side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for elder, then the Marathi for the only one.

You'll want to know: चुलत.
चुलत — culat — "of a paternal uncle; cousin on the father's side".
माझा चुलत भाऊ आणि मी एकाच वर्गात आहोत. mājhā culat bhāū āṇi mī ekāc vargāt āhot. My cousin and I are in the same class.
Unchanging: चुलत (culat) भाऊ, चुलत (culat) बहीण, the children of a father's brother.

The word, taken apart.
चुलत (culat) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi cultā, "father's brother", under the reconstructed culla, "small" (CDIAL 4877).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सावत्र (sāvatra) — step-.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the only one, then the Marathi for of a paternal uncle.

You'll want to know: सावत्र.
सावत्र — sāvatra — "step-".
त्याची सावत्र आई त्याच्यावर खूप प्रेम करते. tyācī sāvatra āī tyācyāvar khūp prem karte. His stepmother loves him very much.
Unchanging: सावत्र (sāvatra) आई, a stepmother; सावत्र (sāvatra) भाऊ, a half-brother.

The word, taken apart.
सावत्र (sāvatra) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi sāvatra, "born of a co-wife", under Sanskrit sāpatna, "of a rival" (CDIAL 13342).
