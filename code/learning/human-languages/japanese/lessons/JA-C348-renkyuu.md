## You'll want to know: れんきゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C348-KESSEKI348-05]; assesses=[] -->

**れんきゅう** — *renkyū* — "consecutive holidays".

> **れんきゅうに なったら、いなかに かえります。** — *renkyū ni nattara, inaka ni kaerimasu* — "When the long weekend comes, I will go home to the country."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**れんきゅう** (*renkyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ren*, "connect" (Middle Chinese *ljen*) and *kyū*, "rest" (*xjuw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
