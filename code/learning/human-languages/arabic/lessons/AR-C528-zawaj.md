## You'll want to know: زواج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C528-ZAWAJ528-02]; assesses=[] -->

**زواج** — *zawāj* — "marriage".

> احتفل جدي وجدتي بخمسين سنة من الزواج.
>
> *iḥtafala jaddī wa-jaddatī bi-khamsīna sana min az-zawāj.*
>
> My grandparents celebrated fifty years of marriage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زواج** (*zawāj*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-w-j*, "a pair, one of a pair" (Lane). Zawj, a husband, and tazawwaja, to marry, which you know, are from the same root.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
