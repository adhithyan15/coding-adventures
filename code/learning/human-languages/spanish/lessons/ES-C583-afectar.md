## You'll want to know: afectar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C583-INFLUYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**afectar** — "to affect".

> La crisis afecta a todos.
>
> *The crisis affects everyone.*

With *a* before people: *afecta a los jóvenes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afectar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *affectare*, a form of *afficere* 'to act upon', built from *ad* and *facere* 'to do' (EtymDB).
