## You'll want to know: લૂંટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C202-THEFT202-03]; assesses=[] -->

**લૂંટવું** — *lū̃ṭvũ* — "to rob".

> **કોઈએ વેપારીને લૂંટ્યો.** — *koīe vepārīne lū̃ṭyo* — "Someone robbed the merchant."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લૂંટવું** (*lū̃ṭvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "robs", under *\*luṭṭati*, "plunders", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 11078).
