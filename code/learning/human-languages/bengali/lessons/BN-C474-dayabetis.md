## You'll want to know: ডায়াবেটিস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C474-HANPANI474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ডায়াবেটিস** — *ḍāyābeṭis* — "diabetes".

> ডায়াবেটিসের জন্য দাদু রসগোল্লা খান না।
>
> *ḍāyābeṭiser jônyô dādu rôsôgollā khān nā.*
>
> Because of his diabetes my grandfather doesn't eat rasgullas.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডায়াবেটিস** (*ḍāyābeṭis*) is borrowed from English *diabetes*.
