Bengali, chapter 374: Keeping Danger Away.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
হুঁশিয়ার (hũshiyār) — watchful; on one's guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for nearly, then the Bengali for partial.

You'll want to know: হুঁশিয়ার.
হুঁশিয়ার — hũshiyār — "watchful; on one's guard".
রাতে বাইরে গেলে হুঁশিয়ার থেকো। rāte bāire gele hũshiyār theko. Be on your guard if you go out at night.

The word, taken apart.
হুঁশিয়ার (hũshiyār) is usually traced to Persian hushyār, alert or wide awake. As a single word it is also a warning: হুঁশিয়ার! Watch out!

Lesson 2 of 5.
প্রতিরোধ করা (prôtirodh kôrā) — to resist; to prevent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for partial, then the Bengali for on one's guard.

You'll want to know: প্রতিরোধ করা.
প্রতিরোধ করা — prôtirodh kôrā — "to resist; to prevent".
নিয়মিত হাত ধুলে তুমি অনেক রোগ প্রতিরোধ করতে পারবে। niyômitô hāt dhule tumi ônek rog prôtirodh kôrte pārbe. If you wash your hands regularly, you can ward off many diseases.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শত্রুকে প্রতিরোধ করলেন, tini shôtruke prôtirodh kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
প্রতিরোধ করা (prôtirodh kôrā) is Sanskrit pratirodha, opposition or obstruction (Monier-Williams), with করা: to stand against an attack, a disease or a change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for on one's guard, then the Bengali for to resist.

You'll want to know: ঠেকানো.
ঠেকানো — ṭhekāno — "to stop; to ward off".
দরজা বন্ধ না করলে তুমি মশা ঠেকাতে পারবে না। dôrjā bôndhô nā kôrle tumi môshā ṭhekāte pārbe nā. If you don't close the door, you won't keep the mosquitoes out.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বলটা ঠেকালেন, tini bôlṭā ṭhekālen.

The word, taken apart.
ঠেকানো (ṭhekāno) is built on the reconstructed ṭhēkk, to fix; CDIAL 5509 lists Bengali ṭhek, a prop, and ṭhekāna, to obstruct: to put something in the way so a thing cannot get through.

নজর (nôjôr) — watch; a close eye (on something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to ward off, then the Bengali for to control.

You'll want to know: নজর.
নজর — nôjôr — "watch; a close eye (on something)".
বাজারে গেলে ব্যাগের ওপর নজর রাখো। bājāre gele byāger opôr nôjôr rākho. If you go to the market, keep an eye on your bag.

The word, taken apart.
নজর (nôjôr) is usually traced to Arabic naẓar, a look, through Persian. নজর রাখা is to keep watch on something.
