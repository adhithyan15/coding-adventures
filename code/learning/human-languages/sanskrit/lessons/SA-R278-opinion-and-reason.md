# Review — what I think, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-THEN-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "first Mira went to the shop". (*prathamaṃ mīrā āpaṇaṃ gatavatī*.) Then "she came in and sat down". (*sā praviśya upaviśati*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-02, SA-LEX-C278-MANYATE-01, SA-LEX-C278-DRDHAH-01, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-227, SA-LEX-C89-DESCRIBE-01, SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: In my opinion the city is beautiful.]
- [YOU SAY: I think that Mira will come tomorrow.]
- [YOU SAY: I will not go, because I am tired.]
- [YOU SAY: Because of the rain the children did not play.]
- [YOU SAY: What does Arun think? His decision is firm.]
- [YOU READ: **दृढः** and **मम दृढः विश्वासः अस्ति** aloud]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C278-EXPLANATION-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **मन्ये** or **मन्यते**, for "I think"? (**मन्ये**.) **यतः** or **कारणात्**, before a whole reason? (**यतः**.) Now the other way round: what does **हेतुः किम्?** ask? ("What is the cause?")
