## You'll want to know: pertenecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C600-SOCIEDAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pertenecer** — "to belong".

> Este libro pertenece a la biblioteca.
>
> *This book belongs to the library.*

Like *conocer*: *pertenezco*. Always with *a*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pertenecer* is inherited from Latin *pertinere* 'to stretch to, to belong', built from *per* and *tenere* 'to hold', with the ending *-ecer* (EtymDB).
