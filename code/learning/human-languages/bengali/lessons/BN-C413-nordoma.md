## You'll want to know: নর্দমা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C413-NORDOMA413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**নর্দমা** — *nôrdômā* — "a drain; a gutter".

> আমাদের গলির নর্দমা খোলা।
>
> *āmāder gôlir nôrdômā kholā.*
>
> The drain in our lane is open.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নর্দমা** (*nôrdômā*) is the open drain along a lane; its origin is not settled, and CDIAL gives no Bengali form for it.
