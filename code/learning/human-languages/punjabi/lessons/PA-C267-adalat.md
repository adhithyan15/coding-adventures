## You'll want to know: ਅਦਾਲਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C267-OFFICE267-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਦਾਲਤ** — *adālat* — "a court of law".

> ਅਦਾਲਤ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੇ ਵਿਚਕਾਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *adālat shahir de vichkār hai.*
>
> The court is in the middle of the town.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਦਾਲਤ** (*adālat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *adāla*, "justice".
