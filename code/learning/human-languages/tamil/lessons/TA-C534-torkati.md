# தோற்கடி (tōṟkaṭi) — to beat a rival; to defeat

## You'll want to know: தோற்கடி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C534-VETRI534-01]; assesses=[] -->

**தோற்கடி** — *tōṟkaṭi* — "to beat a rival; to defeat".

> எங்கள் அணி பலமான அணியைத் தோற்கடித்தது.
>
> *eṅkaḷ aṇi palamāṉa aṇiyait tōṟkaṭittatu.*
>
> Our team defeated a strong team.

Past (I …): தோற்கடித்தேன், *tōṟkaṭittēṉ*.

The side beaten takes -ஐ. To be the side that loses is தோற்றுப்போ (*tōṟṟuppō*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தோற்கடி** (*tōṟkaṭi*) is usually read as a native compound: தோல் (*tōl*), "to be defeated" (DEDR 3558), in its stem தோற்-, and அடி (*aṭi*), "to strike": to strike someone into defeat.
