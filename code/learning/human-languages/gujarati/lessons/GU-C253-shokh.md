## You'll want to know: શોખ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C253-HOBBY253-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શોખ** — *śokh* — "hobby, keen interest".

> **મારા દાદાને ગાવાનો શોખ હતો.** — *mārā dādāne gāvāno śokh hato* — "My grandfather was fond of singing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શોખ** (*śokh*) is a loan from Arabic *shawq*, "longing, desire", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *sh-w-q* (Lane).
