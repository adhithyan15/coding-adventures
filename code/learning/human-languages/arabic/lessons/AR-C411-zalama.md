## You'll want to know: ظلم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C411-IJAZA411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ظلم** — *ẓalama* — "to wrong".

> ظلم المدير العامل الجديد.
>
> *ẓalama al-mudīr al-ʿāmil al-jadīd.*
>
> The manager wronged the new worker.

Present: يظلم, *yaẓlim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ظلم** (*ẓalama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ẓ-l-m*, "to put a thing in the wrong place" (Lane). *Ẓalama* is a Form I verb: to do someone wrong. ظالم, unjust, is its active participle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
