## You'll want to know: indicium, indiciī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C310-IURO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**indicium, indiciī** — "information, evidence; a sign".

> Servus cōnsulī indicium dedit.
>
> *A slave gave information to the consul.*

Neuter. *Indicium facere*: to give information. A clue in a story is also an *indicium*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**indicium** is built on *index*, "one who points out, an informer" (EtymDB), and so on the verb meaning "to point out": a pointing out.
