## You'll want to know: la gripe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C733-GRIPE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la gripe** — "the flu".

> Estoy en cama con gripe.
>
> *I am in bed with the flu.*

In Mexico and Colombia people say *la gripa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gripe* was borrowed from French *grippe* (EtymDB), from *gripper* "to seize", which comes from Frankish *grīpan*: an illness that grips you.
