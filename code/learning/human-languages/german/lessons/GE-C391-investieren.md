## You'll want to know: investieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C391-STEUER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**investieren** — "to invest".

> Sie hat ihr Geld in eine kleine Firma investiert.
>
> *She invested her money in a small firm.*

Perfekt: *hat investiert*. *Zeit investieren* — to invest time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investieren* goes back to Latin *investīre* "to clothe, to cover" (Lewis & Short), built on *vestis* "garment"; the money sense is usually said to have come later, through Italian *investire*.
