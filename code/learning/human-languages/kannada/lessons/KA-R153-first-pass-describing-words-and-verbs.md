[PAUSE 3s] Bitter? (**ಕಹಿ**, *kahi*.) Sour? (**ಹುಳಿ**, *huḷi*.) Sweet? (**ಸಿಹಿ**, *sihi*.) Lazy? (**ಸೋಮಾರಿ**, *sōmāri*.) Clever? (**ಜಾಣ**, *jāṇa*.) Bad? (**ಕೆಟ್ಟ**, *keṭṭa*.) Terrible? (**ಭಯಂಕರ**, *bhayaṅkara*.) Brave? (**ಧೈರ್ಯವಂತ**, *dhairyavanta*.) Golden? (**ಚಿನ್ನದ**, *cinnada*.) Silver? (**ಬೆಳ್ಳಿಯ**, *beḷḷiya*.) To drink? (**ಕುಡಿ**, *kuḍi*.) To run? (**ಓಡು**, *ōḍu*.) To sleep? (**ಮಲಗು**, *malagu*.) To cut? (**ಕತ್ತರಿಸು**, *kattarisu*.) To break? (**ಒಡೆ**, *oḍe*.) To send? (**ಕಳಿಸು**, *kaḷisu*.) To look for? (**ಹುಡುಕು**, *huḍuku*.) To learn? (**ಕಲಿ**, *kali*.) To teach? (**ಕಲಿಸು**, *kalisu*.) To jump? (**ಜಿಗಿ**, *jigi*.)
