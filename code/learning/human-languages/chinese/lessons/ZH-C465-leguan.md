## You'll want to know: lèguān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C465-FUN465-03]; assesses=[] -->

*lèguān* — "optimistic".

> *wǒ nǎinai shì wǒmen jiā zuì lèguān de rén* — My grandmother is the most optimistic person in our family.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lèguān* is built from 乐 'joy' and *guān* 'to view', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lak* and *kwan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'joy' and the second 'to look': looking at things with joy.
