## Added — the A2 core: chapters 147-157, the future, practical texts and connected composition

- Eleven hand-authored chapters, 78 lessons (sequences 12000-12770), stage A2. They
  realize the two A2 spine nodes Chinese had not reached and prove the A2 writing
  stage:
  - **The future** (`SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE`, chapters 147-148): 会 for what will
    happen (VERB-FUTURE, on its own node), 后天, *kěnéng*, *yídìng*, *tiānqì yùbào*;
    要 before a verb for going to, and 要 … 了 for about to; 下个月, 下星期, 上个月,
    *dǎsuàn*, *jiānglái*.
  - **The grammar the A2 vocabulary needs** (chapters 149-154): 在 and 正在 before a
    verb, 正好, 一边 … 一边; 过 after a verb for experience, 过去, 后来, *ránhòu*,
    *cónglái* (and *de shíhou* in one pinyin sentence); a verb and its result, 看到,
    学会, *tīngdǒng*, *kàndǒng*, *zuòwán*; 得 for how well, *liúlì*, *búcuò*, *qīngchu*;
    比 with the difference last, *gèng*, *gēn* … *yíyàng*, 没有 … 那么; 还 and 还没,
    还是 in questions, *huòzhě* in statements, *bié*.
  - **Practical texts** (`SPINE-READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS`, chapters 155-157): a station
    timetable and a bookshop's opening hours with 从 … 到, *chūfā*, 开门, *guānmén*;
    a price list and an advert with 元, *jiàqián*, *miǎnfèi*, *guǎnggào*; a notice at
    work and a friend's message with 开会, *tōngzhī*, *xiāoxi*, *huífù*.
- 45 new headwords (22 in characters, 23 by ear with pinyin headwords), 12 verbs.
  Eight grammar lessons, one point each; three readings printed one clause a line,
  every character one the reader writes.
- Ten characters written, one a chapter (147-156), each directly after the word
  whose headword first shows it: 后 月 正 过 到 得 比 还 从 元, with a stroke-by-stroke
  strip each, every one taught as pieces the reader already writes.
- ZH-W157-reply proves **connected composition**: four to six lines answering the
  friend's message (a named reader, a real purpose), held together by time words at
  the front, the subject said once, and 因为 tying a reason to the line before; words
  whose characters are not written go in pinyin inside the reply. A final review,
  ZH-R157-review-final-157, is chapter 157's payoff.
- Spine: VERB-FUTURE sits on ZH-C147-hui-future, so the TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE omits
  empty. Four concept aliases in `curriculum.d/_meta.json` (COMPARISON-BASIC ->
  ZH-GRAMMAR-BI-COMPARE, CONNECTIVE-THEN -> ZH-PAST150-RANHOU, VERB-TAKE ->
  ZH-VERB-NA, VERB-ANSWER -> ZH-VERB-HUIDA), and the omits of SAY-WHAT-I-DO,
  NARRATE-EVENTS and DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE drop those four.
- Origins from the Guangyun in Baxter's transcription, every sense the gloss does
  not give hedged, irregular modern readings named (期 qī, 打 dǎ, 算 suàn, 完 wán,
  还 hái, 复 fù), and "not settled" where the Guangyun has no reading (懂, 做). Each
  corpus root slug is reused for its own etymon; 價 'price' takes the sense-tagged
  `middle-chinese-kaeh-price`, because the bare `kaeh` is 假 'holiday'.
- A2 headwords 645 -> 690 and verbs 173 -> 185; all five A2 spine nodes realized and
  no A2 writing stage missing. A2 is not attained: 1,200 headwords are needed.
