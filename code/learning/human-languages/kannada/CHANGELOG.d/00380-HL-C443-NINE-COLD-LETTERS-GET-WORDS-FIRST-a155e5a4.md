## HL-C443: nine cold letters get words first

Gentle writing asks for every letter to be taken from a word the reader
already says. Nine Kannada letter lessons came before any word holding their
letter. Each now follows a short word lesson that holds it:

| letter | word placed just before it |
|---|---|
| ಉ | **ಉತ್ತರ** (*uttara*, an answer) |
| ಊ | **ಊರುಗೋಲು** (*ūrugōlu*, a walking stick) |
| ಝ | **ಝರಿ** (*jhari*, a stream) |
| ಥ | **ಕಥೆ** (*kathe*, a story) |
| ಈ | **ಈಜು** (*īju*, swimming) |
| ಓ | **ಓಟ** (*ōṭa*, running) |
| ೃ | **ಕೃಷಿ** (*kṛṣi*, farming) |
| ಃ | **ನಮಃ** (*namaḥ*, a salutation) |
| ಞ | **ಜ್ಞಾನ** (*jñāna*, knowledge) |

Each word is revisited by its letter lesson and by the lesson after it. The
ten digit lessons stay cold: no word headword holds a digit.

    kannada cold letter lessons   19  ->  10 (all ten are digits; ceiling ratcheted)
