## You'll want to know: juésài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C500-MATCH500-03]; assesses=[] -->

*juésài* — "final (of a competition)".

> *wǒmen de qiúduì jìn le juésài* — Our team got into the final.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juésài* is built from *jué* 'to decide' and 赛 'a contest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwet* and *sojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to cut' and 'to break through'.
