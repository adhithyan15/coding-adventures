## You'll want to know: मूर्छा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C485-CHAKKAR485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मूर्छा** — *mūrchā* — "a faint; a swoon".

> गर्मी से एक आदमी को सड़क पर मूर्छा आ गई।
>
> *garmī se ek ādmī ko saṛak par mūrchā ā gaī.*
>
> A man fainted in the street from the heat.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूर्छा** (*mūrchā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mūrchā*, "fainting, a swoon, stupor" (Monier-Williams). *mūrchā ānā* is to faint; everyday speech often says *behoś honā*.
