# ः — a small breath after a vowel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You already know the greeting *namaskār* and its older first piece,
*namaḥ*, "a bow." The final breath in *namaḥ* has its own mark.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-VISARGA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ः

The **visarga** is two dots written vertically to the right of a syllable. It
marks a quiet breath after the vowel. For this first encounter, place the upper
dot, lift, then place the lower dot. Stop after this one sign.

## Writing — visible model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-VISARGA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Keep **ः** visible. Point to the upper dot, then the lower dot. Finger-trace the
two positions once and copy the sign once.
