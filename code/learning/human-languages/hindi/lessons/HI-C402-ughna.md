## You'll want to know: ऊँघना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C402-GATHRI402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊँघना** — *ū̃ghnā* — "to doze".

> बस में बहुत लोग ऊँघ रहे थे।
>
> *bas meṁ bahut log ū̃gh rahe the.*
>
> A lot of people on the bus were dozing.

Past (he …): वह थोड़ी देर ऊँघा, *vah thoṛī der ū̃ghā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊँघना** (*ū̃ghnā*) goes back to *\*uṅghati*, "sleeps", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1632), which lists Hindi *ū̃ghnā*, "to be drowsy".
