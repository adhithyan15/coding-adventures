## You'll want to know: die Stellenanzeige
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C349-STELLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Stellenanzeige** — "the job advertisement".

> In der Zeitung habe ich eine interessante Stellenanzeige gefunden.
>
> *I found an interesting job advertisement in the newspaper.*

Plural: *die Stellenanzeigen*. Online it is often *das Stellenangebot*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stellenanzeige* joins *Stelle* "place, job", from the family of *stellen* "to put" (Proto-Germanic *stallijaną*, EtymDB), and *Anzeige* "notice, advertisement", from *anzeigen* "to show".
