## You'll want to know: la tabaccheria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C452-EDICOLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la tabaccheria** — "the tobacconist's".

> In tabaccheria compro i biglietti dell'autobus.
>
> *At the tobacconist's I buy bus tickets.*

Marked by a big T sign; it sells tickets, stamps and phone credit too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tabaccheria* is built on *tabacco* 'tobacco', which was borrowed from Spanish *tabaco*, a word of Caribbean origin.
