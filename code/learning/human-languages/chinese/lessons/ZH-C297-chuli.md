## You'll want to know: chǔlǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C297-FAULT297-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chǔlǐ* — "to deal with".

> *jǐngchá lái chǔlǐ le zhège shìgù* — The police came to deal with the accident.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: chǔ is said with a rising tone before lǐ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chǔlǐ* is built from *chǔ* 'to settle' and *lǐ* 'to put in order', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhoX* and *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to dwell' and 'to settle' and the second 'to arrange' and 'reason'.
