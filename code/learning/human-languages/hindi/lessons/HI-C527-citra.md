## You'll want to know: चित्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C527-KALA527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चित्र** — *citra* — "a picture, a painting".

> दीवार पर एक सुंदर चित्र है।
>
> *Dīvār par ek sundar citra hai.*
>
> There is a beautiful painting on the wall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चित्र** (*citra*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *citra*, "bright, bright-coloured; a picture" (Monier-Williams).
