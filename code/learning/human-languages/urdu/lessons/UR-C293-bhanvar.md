## You'll want to know: بھنور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C293-SAILAB293-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بھنور** — *bhanvar* — "a whirlpool".

> دریا میں ایک بھنور تھا۔
>
> *daryā meṅ ek bhanvar thā.*
>
> There was a whirlpool in the river.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھنور** (*bhanvar*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bhãwar*, "a whirlpool", under Sanskrit *bhramara* (CDIAL 9650), a word for what turns round and round.
