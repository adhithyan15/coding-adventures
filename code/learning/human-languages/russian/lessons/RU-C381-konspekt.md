## You'll want to know: конспект
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C381-SESSIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**конспект** (*konspékt*) — "lecture notes, a summary".

> Я потерял конспект лекций.
>
> *Ya poteryál konspékt léktsiy.*
>
> I have lost my lecture notes.

Masculine. *Конспект лекций* — notes from lectures.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**конспект** is usually traced to Latin *cōnspectus* "a view, a survey" (Lewis & Short), from *cōnspicere* "to catch sight of", built on *specere* "to see": a summary that lets you take in a subject at a glance.
