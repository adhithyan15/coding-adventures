## You'll want to know: または
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C528-OR528-02]; assesses=[] -->

**または** — *matawa* — "or; or else".

> **もし あめが ふったら、でんしゃ または くるまで いきます。** — *moshi ame ga futtara, densha matawa kuruma de ikimasu* — "If it rains, I will go by train or by car."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**または** (*matawa*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
