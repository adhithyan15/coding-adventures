Gujarati, chapter 366: On One Condition.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શરત (śarat) — a stipulation; a condition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for actual, then the Gujarati for to link.

You'll want to know: શરત.
શરત — śarat — "a stipulation; a condition".
જો તમે મારી શરત માનશો તો હું આ કામ કરીશ. jo tame mārī śarat mānśo to hũ ā kām karīś. If you accept my condition, I will do this job.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શરત (śarat) is usually traced to Arabic sharṭ, "a condition", through Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ચાહવું (cāhvũ) — to want.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a likelihood, then the Gujarati for a conjecture.

You'll want to know: ચાહવું.
ચાહવું — cāhvũ — "to want".
જો તમે ચાહો છો તો આપણે આજે નીકળીશું. jo tame cāho cho to āpaṇe āje nīkḷīśũ. If you want, we will set off today.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મદદ ચાહી, teṇe madad cāhī.

The word, taken apart.
ચાહવું (cāhvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to long for, to love", under a reconstructed cāh, "see, look for, desire" (CDIAL 4775); "to want" is its everyday sense.
