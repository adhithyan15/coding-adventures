Telugu, chapter 121: Thunder, Lightning, Storm, Wave, Hill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఉరుము (urumu) — thunder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a leaf, then the Telugu for soil.

You'll want to know: ఉరుము.
ఉరుము — urumu — "thunder".
