Hindi, chapter 154: Hot, Wet, Dry, Heavy, Light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गरम (garam) — hot, warm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an office, then the Hindi for a bank.
[pause 2 seconds]
Walk one दिन: दोपहर, two pahars, now the whole afternoon, then रात, and आधी रात, half the night.

You'll want to know: गरम.
गरम — garam — "hot, warm".
