Bengali, chapter 103: To get down, Swimming, To jump, To hit, Share.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নামা (nāmā) — to get down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to wake someone, then the Bengali for to save.

You'll want to know: নামা.
নামা — nāmā — "to get down".

The word, taken apart.
নামা (nāmā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from namra, "bent, submissive" (CDIAL 6964). CDIAL lists Bengali nāmā, "to descend", under it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
লাফানো (lāphāno) — to jump.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to get down, then the Bengali for swimming.

You'll want to know: লাফানো.
লাফানো — lāphāno — "to jump".

The word, taken apart.
লাফানো (lāphāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from lappha, "sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10939). Lāphāno is a verb from lāph, "a leap", which CDIAL lists.
