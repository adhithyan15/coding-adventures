# ചെരുപ്പ് (ceruppŭ) — a sandal

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C100-SARI-01, ML-LEX-C100-MUNDU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *a sari*. Then say *a mundu*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C100-SANDAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ചെരുപ്പ്** — *ceruppŭ* — **a sandal**, the everyday open footwear.

> **ഇത് പഴയ ചെരുപ്പാണ്.** — *itŭ paḻaya ceruppāṇŭ* — "This is an old sandal."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C100-SANDAL-01, ML-LEX-C100-MUNDU-01, ML-LEX-AANU-01] -->

**This word is not a garment, and the book wants both.** **മുണ്ട്** and
**സാരി** go on the body; **ചെരുപ്പ്** goes on the feet.

Its ending you have already handled twice. **ചെരുപ്പ്** finishes in the
breathed half-u, like **മുണ്ട്**, so **ആണ്** lands straight on it:
**ചെരുപ്പാണ്**.

| word | joined to **ആണ്** |
|---|---|
| **മുണ്ട്** | **മുണ്ടാണ്** (*muṇṭāṇŭ*) |
| **ചെരുപ്പ്** | **ചെരുപ്പാണ്** (*ceruppāṇŭ*) |
