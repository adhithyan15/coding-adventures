## You'll want to know: અર્પવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C499-MARAN499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**અર્પવું** — *arpavũ* — "to offer with respect".

> બેસણામાં બધાએ દાદાના ફોટાને ફૂલ અર્પ્યાં.
>
> *besṇāmā̃ badhāe dādānā phoṭāne phūl arpyā̃.*
>
> At the prayer meeting everyone offered flowers before my grandfather's photo.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ફૂલ અર્પ્યું, *teṇe phūl arpyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અર્પવું** (*arpavũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *arpayati*, "places upon, offers", the causative of *ṛ*, "to go" (Monier-Williams).
