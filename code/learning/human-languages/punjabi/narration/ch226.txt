Punjabi, chapter 226: Ready for the Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਤਿਆਰ (tiār) — ready.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to lie down, then the Punjabi for in a hurry.

You'll want to know: ਤਿਆਰ.
ਤਿਆਰ — tiār — "ready".
ਮੈਂ ਸੱਤ ਵਜੇ ਤਿਆਰ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ satt vaje tiār hundī hāṁ. I am ready at seven.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਿਆਰ (tiār) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian tayyār, "ready".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਕੰਬਣਾ (kambṇā) — to shiver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for ready, then the Punjabi for a toothbrush.

You'll want to know: ਕੰਬਣਾ.
ਕੰਬਣਾ — kambṇā — "to shiver".
ਸਰਦੀ ਦੀ ਸਵੇਰ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਕੰਬਦਾ ਹਾਂ। sardī dī saver nū̃ maiṁ kambdā hāṁ. On winter mornings I shiver.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਕੰਬਿਆ, maiṁ bahut kambiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੰਬਣਾ (kambṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit kampatē, "trembles, shivers"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kambṇā, "trembles, shivers" (CDIAL 2767).

ਭੇੜਨਾ — bheṛnā — "to shut a door".
ਮੈਂ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਭੇੜਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ rāt nū̃ darvāzā bheṛdī hāṁ. At night I shut the door.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਭੇੜਿਆ, maiṁ darvāzā bheṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਭੇੜਨਾ (bheṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed bhiṭ, "meet, throng"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bheṛnā, "to close, compare" (CDIAL 9490).
