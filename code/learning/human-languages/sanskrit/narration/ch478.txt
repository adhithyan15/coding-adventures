Sanskrit, chapter 478: Aches and Pains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वेदना (vedanā) — agony; acute pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a burning feeling, then the Sanskrit for runs a fever.

You'll want to know: वेदना.
वेदना — vedanā — "agony; acute pain".
रात्रौ मम दन्ते वेदना अभवत्। rātrau mama dante vedanā abhavat. In the night I had a terrible toothache.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वेदना (vedanā) comes from the root √vid, "to know, to feel": what is felt; MW gives "pain, torture, agony" (MW).
