# مستحيل (mustaḥīl) — impossible

## You'll want to know: مستحيل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C403-IRTIYAB403-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مستحيل** — *mustaḥīl* — "impossible".

> من المستحيل أن ننهي العمل اليوم.
>
> *min al-mustaḥīl an nunhiya al-ʿamal al-yawm.*
>
> It is impossible for us to finish the work today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستحيل** (*mustaḥīl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-l*, "to change, to turn" (Lane). *Mustaḥīl* is the Form X active participle, what has turned away from being: impossible.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
