Bengali, chapter 366: Likely or Not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সম্ভাবনা (sômbhābônā) — a likelihood; a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to talk round, then the Bengali for unanimous.

You'll want to know: সম্ভাবনা.
সম্ভাবনা — sômbhābônā — "a likelihood; a chance".
আজ ঝড়ের সম্ভাবনা আছে, তাই বাইরে না গেলে ভালো। āj jhôṛer sômbhābônā āchhe, tāi bāire nā gele bhālo. There's a chance of a storm today, so it's better not to go out.

The word, taken apart.
সম্ভাবনা (sômbhābônā) is Sanskrit sambhāvanā, supposing or assuming (Monier-Williams), from the same root as সম্ভব, possible. Bengali uses it for how likely a thing is.

সম্ভবত — sômbhôbôtô — "probably".
অরুণ যদি বাস না পায়, তাহলে সম্ভবত দেরি করবে। ôrun jôdi bās nā pāy, tāhôle sômbhôbôtô deri kôrbe. If Arun doesn't get a bus, he will probably be late.

The word, taken apart.
সম্ভবত (sômbhôbôtô) is Sanskrit sambhava, arising or possible (Monier-Williams), with the adverb ending -tas, in its Bengali form: 'in all likelihood'. It sits beside the taught সম্ভব.

Lesson 3 of 5.
অনিবার্য (ônibārjô) — inevitable; unavoidable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a likelihood, then the Bengali for probably.

You'll want to know: অনিবার্য.
অনিবার্য — ônibārjô — "inevitable; unavoidable".
নিয়ম না মানলে দুর্ঘটনা অনিবার্য। niyôm nā mānle durghôṭônā ônibārjô. If people don't follow the rules, an accident is inevitable.

The word, taken apart.
অনিবার্য (ônibārjô) is Sanskrit anivārya, not to be warded off, unavoidable (Monier-Williams), with the negative a- at its head: what cannot be held back. It marks the likeliest outcome of all, the one that will surely follow.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ধরে নেওয়া (dhôre neoyā) — to assume; to take for granted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for inevitable, then the Bengali for rare.

You'll want to know: ধরে নেওয়া.
ধরে নেওয়া — dhôre neoyā — "to assume; to take for granted".
যদি ধরে নিই যে কাল ছুটি, তাহলে আমরা কোথায় যাব? jôdi dhôre nii je kāl chhuṭi, tāhôle āmrā kothāy jābô? If we assume tomorrow is a holiday, where shall we go?
Past (he/she …): তিনি ধরে নিলেন যে সবাই আসবে, tini dhôre nilen je sôbāi āsbe.

The word, taken apart.
ধরে নেওয়া (dhôre neoyā) is ধরে, the -এ form of ধরা 'to hold' (CDIAL 6747), with নেওয়া 'to take' (CDIAL 6966): to take a thing as held, to assume it. What is assumed follows যে.
