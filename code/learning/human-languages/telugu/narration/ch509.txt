Telugu, chapter 509: What Kind of Person.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
పిరికితనం (pirikitanaṁ) — cowardice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for daring, then the Telugu for timid.

You'll want to know: పిరికితనం.
పిరికితనం — pirikitanaṁ — "cowardice".
నిజం చెప్పకపోవడం పిరికితనం. nijaṁ ceppakapōvaḍaṁ pirikitanaṁ. Not telling the truth is cowardice.
పిరికి, timid, + -తనం.

The word, taken apart.
పిరికితనం (pirikitanaṁ) is native Dravidian: piṟiki, "timid, cowardly" (DEDR 4200), with -తనం, the ending that makes abstract nouns.

Lesson 2 of 6.
అమాయకుడు (amāyakuḍu) — an innocent man; a naive man.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for timid, then the Telugu for cowardice.

You'll want to know: అమాయకుడు.
అమాయకుడు — amāyakuḍu — "an innocent man; a naive man".
ఆ అమాయకుడిని దొంగలు మోసం చేశారు. ā amāyakuḍini doṅgalu mōsaṁ cēśāru. Thieves cheated that naive man.
Feminine: అమాయకురాలు. అమాయకంగా, innocently.

The word, taken apart.
అమాయకుడు (amāyakuḍu) is usually traced to Sanskrit a-, "not", and māyā, which CDIAL 10060 glosses as "illusion" and "fraud", with the Telugu masculine ending -డు: a man without guile.

Lesson 3 of 6.
మూర్ఖుడు (mūrkhuḍu) — a fool; a stubborn fool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for cowardice, then the Telugu for an innocent man.

You'll want to know: మూర్ఖుడు.
మూర్ఖుడు — mūrkhuḍu — "a fool; a stubborn fool".
ఆ మూర్ఖుడు ఎవరి మాటా వినడు. ā mūrkhuḍu evari māṭā vinaḍu. That fool listens to no one.
Someone foolish and obstinate. Feminine: మూర్ఖురాలు.

The word, taken apart.
మూర్ఖుడు (mūrkhuḍu) is borrowed from Sanskrit mūrkha, "stupid, foolish; a fool" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu masculine ending -డు.

ఆదివారం ఉదయం లేవడానికి నాకు బద్ధకం. ādivāraṁ udayaṁ lēvaḍāniki nāku baddhakaṁ. On Sunday mornings I feel too lazy to get up.
నాకు బద్ధకంగా ఉంది, I feel lazy. బద్ధకస్తుడు, a lazy man.

The word, taken apart.
బద్ధకం (baddhakaṁ) is a Telugu word for sluggishness and laziness; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
హుషారు (huṣāru) — high spirits; liveliness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a fool, then the Telugu for laziness.

You'll want to know: హుషారు.
హుషారు — huṣāru — "high spirits; liveliness".
పండుగ రోజు పిల్లలు హుషారుగా ఉన్నారు. paṇḍuga rōju pillalu huṣārugā unnāru. On the festival day the children were full of beans.
హుషారుగా, cheerfully, full of energy.

The word, taken apart.
హుషారు (huṣāru) is usually traced to Persian hushyār, "alert, awake", which came into Telugu through Hindustani; in Telugu it is the bright energy of someone in good spirits.
