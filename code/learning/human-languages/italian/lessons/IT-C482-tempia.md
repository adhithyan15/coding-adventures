## You'll want to know: la tempia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C482-VISO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la tempia** — "temple (side of the head)".

> Ho un dolore alla tempia sinistra.
>
> *I have a pain in my left temple.*

The plural is *le tempie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tempia* comes from Latin *tempora* "the temples of the head" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), through a Vulgar Latin form *tempula*.
