## You'll want to know: छांव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C168-SURAJ168-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छांव** — *chhā̃v* — "shade".

> गाय छांव मांय बैठै है।
>
> *gāy chhā̃v mā̃y baiṭhai hai.*
>
> The cow sits in the shade.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छांव** (*chhā̃v*) is inherited from Sanskrit *chāyā*, "shade, shadow"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *chāvalī*, "shade" (CDIAL 5027).
