## You'll want to know: zūnshǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C359-RULES359-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zūnshǒu* — "to keep to; to abide by".

> *wǒmen yīnggāi zūnshǒu guīzé* — We should keep to the rules.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zūnshǒu* is built from *zūn* and *shǒu*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tswin* and *syuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to follow' and the second 'to guard'.
