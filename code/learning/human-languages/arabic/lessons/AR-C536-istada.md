## You'll want to know: اصطاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C536-SAYD536-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اصطاد** — *iṣṭāda* — "to go fishing; to hunt".

> اصطاد أخي سمكة كبيرة.
>
> *iṣṭāda akhī samaka kabīra.*
>
> My brother caught a big fish.

Present: يصطاد, *yaṣṭād*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اصطاد** (*iṣṭāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-y-d*, "to hunt, to catch" (Lane). Form VIII iṣṭāda, with t turning into ṭ, is to catch for oneself.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
