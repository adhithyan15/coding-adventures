## You'll want to know: la excusa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C586-JUSTIF-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la excusa** — "the excuse".

> Tiene una excusa para todo.
>
> *He has an excuse for everything.*

*Poner excusas* — to make excuses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*excusa* is built on *excusar*, inherited from Latin *excusare* 'to free from blame', *ex* + *causa* 'case, charge' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
