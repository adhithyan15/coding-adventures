# (reading) — the whole bargain

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-KAPDA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Six words, then six lines. Now the whole thing, start to finish.

[PAUSE 1s] It is longer than anything you have read. Nothing in it is new.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-BEES-01, MW-SCRIPT-MAHANGO-01] -->

> ये कपड़ा दिखावो।
> ये कितणे कू है?
> बीस।
> ये घणो महंगो है। दस करो।
> कोनी।
> पंदरा करो।
> ठीक। पैसा ले लो।
> पाछे मिलसू।

[PAUSE 3s] Read it through once without stopping. Do not translate as you go.

[PAUSE 3s] Now again, and watch the number move: बीस, दस, पंदरा. That is the
whole negotiation, and it is carried by three words you learned as counting.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-PANDARA-01, MW-SCRIPT-KONI-01, MW-SKILL-READ-NOTICE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **कोनी** is the hinge. Before it, the price is falling because you
pushed. After it, the price settles because they pushed back.

[PAUSE 2s] Notice what the passage never says. Nobody explains that पंदरा is
between दस and बीस — the bargain assumes you know, and you do.

[PAUSE 2s] This is the first time the book has asked you to hold a whole
exchange at once, and the reason it can is that every line was paid for, one
chapter at a time.
