## You'll want to know: el origen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C571-DESCUBRIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el origen** — "the origin".

> Nadie conoce el origen del incendio.
>
> *Nobody knows the origin of the fire.*

Plural: *los orígenes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*origen* is a learned borrowing from Latin *orīgō* 'a beginning, a source' (EtymDB), from *orīrī* 'to rise'.
