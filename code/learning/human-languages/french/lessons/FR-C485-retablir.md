## You'll want to know: rétablir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C485-GUERIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rétablir** — "to restore; (se rétablir) to recover".

> Après l'opération, elle s'est vite rétablie.
>
> *After the operation, she recovered quickly.*

Mostly reflexive for health: *se rétablir*. *Rétablir le courant* — to restore the power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rétablir* is built from *re-* and *établir* "to set up", inherited from Latin *stabilīre* "to make firm", from *stabilis*, built on *stāre* "to stand" (Lewis & Short).
