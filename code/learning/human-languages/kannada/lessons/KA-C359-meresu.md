# ಮೆರೆಸು (meresu) — to parade; to carry in procession

## You'll want to know: ಮೆರೆಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C359-UTSAVA359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆರೆಸು** — *meresu* — "to parade; to carry in procession".

> ಜನರು ಗೆದ್ದ ಆಟಗಾರನನ್ನು ಬೀದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮೆರೆಸಿದರು.
>
> *janaru gedda āṭagāranannu bīdiyalli meresidaru.*
>
> The people paraded the winning player through the streets.

Past (I …): ಮೆರೆಸಿದೆ, *mereside*.

Someone honoured is carried or led through the streets with music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆರೆಸು** (*meresu*) is native Dravidian: the causative of *mere*, "to shine, to show off", which DEDR 5074 lists with Kannada *merugu*, "to shine".

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
