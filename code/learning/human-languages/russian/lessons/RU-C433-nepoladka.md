## You'll want to know: неполадка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C433-SHUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**неполадка** (*nepoládka*) — "a fault, a problem (with equipment)".

> В номере была неполадка с душем.
>
> *V nómere bylá nepoládka s dúshem.*
>
> There was a problem with the shower in the room.

Feminine; often plural: *неполадки*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*неполадка* is built from *не-* 'not' and *поладить* 'to get on', on *лад* 'harmony, good order', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *ladŭ* (EtymDB).
