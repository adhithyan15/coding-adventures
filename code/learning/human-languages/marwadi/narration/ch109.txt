Lesson 6 of 7.
Places, this and that, and actions — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to lose and to hit.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a gate, a gateway, a mansion, a fair, a weekly market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: this, like this, like that, which, who]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: all, another, our own, someone, each]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to bathe, to cook, to make, to break, to join]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to buy, to uncover, to call, to ask for, to look]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A gate? (दरवाजो, darvājo.) A gateway? (पोळ, poḷ.) A mansion? (हवेली, havelī.) A fair? (मेळो, meḷo.) A weekly market? (हाट, hāṭ.) This? (यो, yo.) Like this? (इसो, iso.) Like that? (उसो, uso.) Which? (किसो, kiso.) Who? (कुण, kuṇ.) All? (सगळा, sagḷā.) Another? (दूजो, dūjo.) Our own? (आपणो, āpṇo.) Someone? (कोई, koī.) Each? (हरेक, harek.) To bathe? (न्हावणो, nhāvṇo.) To cook? (पकावणो, pakāvṇo.) To make? (बणावणो, baṇāvṇo.) To break? (तोड़णो, toṛṇo.) To join? (जोड़णो, joṛṇo.) To buy? (खरीदणो, kharīdṇo.) To uncover? (उघाड़णो, ughāṛṇo.) To call? (बुलावणो, bulāvṇo.) To ask for? (मांगणो, māṅgṇo.) To look? (जोवणो, jovṇo.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Type the Marwadi for 'to lose (a game)'.
