## You'll want to know: たけのこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C494-VEG494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たけのこ** — *takenoko* — "a bamboo shoot".

> **はるに たけのこを たべます。** — *haru ni takenoko o tabemasu* — "We eat bamboo shoots in spring."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たけのこ** (*takenoko*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
