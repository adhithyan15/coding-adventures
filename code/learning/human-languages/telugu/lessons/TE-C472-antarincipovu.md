# అంతరించిపోవు (antariñcipōvu) — to die out; to vanish

## You'll want to know: అంతరించిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C472-VANYAPRANI472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతరించిపోవు** — *antariñcipōvu* — "to die out; to vanish".

> చాలా రకాల పక్షులు అంతరించిపోయాయి.
>
> *cālā rakāla pakṣulu antariñcipōyāyi.*
>
> Many kinds of birds have died out.

Past (it …): అంతరించిపోయింది, *antariñcipōyindi*.

Said of species, languages and old customs that disappear for good. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: అంతరించిపోయింది, it died out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతరించిపోవు** (*antariñcipōvu*) is a Telugu verb built from Sanskrit *antarita*, "gone within, hidden, vanished" (Monier-Williams), with -ఇంచు and the native పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
