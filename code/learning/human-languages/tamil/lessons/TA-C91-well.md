## You'll want to know: நன்றாக
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C91-WEATHER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**நன்றாக** (*naṉṟāka*) — "well, fine".

**நான் நன்றாக இருக்கிறேன்** — "I am well", the everyday answer to how are you.
