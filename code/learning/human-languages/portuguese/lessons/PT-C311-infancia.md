## You'll want to know: a infância
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C311-IDADES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a infância** — "childhood".

> Passei a infância numa aldeia.
>
> *I spent my childhood in a village.*

*Amigo de infância* — childhood friend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*infância* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnfantia* "inability to speak, childhood", from *īnfāns* "not speaking", built on *fārī* "to speak" (Lewis & Short).
