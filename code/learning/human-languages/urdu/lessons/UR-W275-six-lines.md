# Six lines about a trip

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SKILL-C267-READ-STORY-01, UR-LEX-C266-DAURAN-01, UR-LEX-C269-CHUNANCHE-01, UR-LEX-C270-ZARA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You read Sara's trip to Lahore. Now you write one: a trip of your own, real or invented, for a friend to read.

[PAUSE 1s] Say **دوران** (*daurān*) and **بعد** (*bād*) aloud; one of them will go in.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A told story is not a list. It has a **scene**, with **رہا تھا** or **جب … تو …**; **events** in the plain past, kept in order with **پہلے**, **پھر**, **اس کے بعد**, **آخر میں**, and joined with **کر**; a **reason**, with **کیونکہ**, **اس لیے** or **کی وجہ سے**; and a **feeling**, with **مجھے … لگا** or **میں خوش ہوا**. It can end on what might have been: **اگر … ہوتا، تو …**.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-02, UR-LEX-C267-BAD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-01, UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C271-PROBLEM-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-02, UR-LEX-C272-DAR-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-02, UR-SCRIPT-C265-WAW-HAMZA-01, UR-SCRIPT-C267-AIN-01, UR-SCRIPT-C268-GHAIN-01, UR-SCRIPT-C270-ZAL-01, UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C272-DDAL-01, UR-SCRIPT-C273-SUAD-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] On paper, in Urdu script, write **six lines**, one for each frame. Each line is one short sentence of your own; the frame gives its job, not its words.

1. where you went, and when: **کل میں … گیا** (or **گئی**), or **پچھلے ہفتے** …;
2. the scene as you set off: **… رہا تھا**, or **جب …، تو …**;
3. what happened first and next: **پہلے** …، **پھر** …، or a **کر** form; one thing done with **نے**;
4. one thing that went wrong, why, and whom you told: **کھو گیا**, **چھوٹ گئی** or **حادثہ**, with **کیونکہ** or **کی وجہ سے**, and **… کو بتایا**;
5. how it ended and how you felt: **آخر میں** …، **مجھے … لگا** or **میں خوش ہوا**, or what you hope now: **مجھے امید ہے کہ** …;
6. what might have been: **اگر … ہوتا، تو …**, with one word that holds a letter you wrote in these chapters: ع, غ, ذ, ث, ڈ, ص or ؤ.

[PAUSE 3s] There is no model in this lesson. Use only words you already write.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, UR-SCRIPT-C267-AIN-01, UR-SCRIPT-C268-GHAIN-01, UR-SCRIPT-C270-ZAL-01, UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C272-DDAL-01, UR-SCRIPT-C273-SUAD-01, UR-SCRIPT-C265-WAW-HAMZA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read your six lines aloud. Mark each past verb **s** for scene and **e** for event: line two mostly **s**, lines three and four mostly **e**.

[PAUSE 2s] Then three checks. A plain past with an object has **نے** on its doer and follows the object: **میں نے ٹکٹ لیا**, **میں نے چائے پی**. A **رہا تھا** verb takes no **نے**. In line six, do both halves end in **-تا**?
