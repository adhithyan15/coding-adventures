## You'll want to know: کانال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C531-SCREEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کانال** (*kânâl*) — "a TV channel; a canal".

> او کانال تلویزیون را عوض کرد.
>
> *u kânâl-e televiziyon râ 'avaz kard.*
>
> He changed the TV channel.

Also a waterway, and a channel online.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kânâl* was borrowed from French *canal* (EtymDB).
