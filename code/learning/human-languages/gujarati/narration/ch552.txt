Gujarati, chapter 552: If the Show Goes On.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મંચ (manc) — a stage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to make (someone) run, then the Gujarati for a trophy.

You'll want to know: મંચ.
મંચ — manc — "a stage".
જો મંચ તૈયાર હશે તો નાટક સમયસર શરૂ થશે. jo manc taiyār haśe to nāṭak samaysar śarū thaśe. If the stage is ready, the play will start on time.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મંચ (manc) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit mañca, "a raised platform, a stage" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ગજાવવું (gajāvvũ) — to make ring (with noise).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for acting, then the Gujarati for a spectator.

You'll want to know: ગજાવવું.
ગજાવવું — gajāvvũ — "to make ring (with noise)".
જો આપણી ટીમ જીતશે તો આપણે આખું મેદાન ગજાવીશું. jo āpṇī ṭīm jītśe to āpaṇe ākhũ medān gajāvīśũ. If our team wins, we'll make the whole ground ring.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઓરડો ગજાવ્યો, teṇe orḍo gajāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
ગજાવવું (gajāvvũ) is built from ગાજવું, "to thunder", with the causative -āv- and a shortened vowel: to make a place thunder. CDIAL files ગાજવું under Sanskrit garjati, "roars" (CDIAL 4046).

Lesson 5 of 5.
જામવું (jāmvũ) — to get going (of a show or a party).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a spectator, then the Gujarati for to make ring.

You'll want to know: જામવું.
જામવું — jāmvũ — "to get going (of a show or a party)".
જો સંગીત સારું હશે તો કાર્યક્રમ જામશે. jo sangīt sārũ haśe to kāryakram jāmśe. If the music is good, the show will really get going.
Past (it …): તે કાર્યક્રમ જામી ગયો, te kāryakram jāmī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
જામવું (jāmvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit yamyátē, "is fixed", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to collect, to set" (CDIAL 10428): milk sets into curd, and a show "sets" when it takes hold.
