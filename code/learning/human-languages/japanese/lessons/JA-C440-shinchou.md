## You'll want to know: しんちょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C440-CAREFUL440-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しんちょう** — *shinchō* — "cautious; prudent".

> **ははは うんてんが しんちょうです。** — *haha wa unten ga shinchō desu* — "My mother is a careful driver."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんちょう** (*shinchō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "be careful" (Middle Chinese *dzyinH*) and *chō*, "important" (*drjowngX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
