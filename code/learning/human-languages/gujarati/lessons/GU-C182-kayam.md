## You'll want to know: કાયમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C182-NOW182-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કાયમ** — *kāyam* — "permanently, for good".

> **આ ઘર કાયમ અમારું રહેશે.** — *ā ghar kāyam amārũ raheśe* — "This house will be ours for good."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાયમ** (*kāyam*) is a loan from Arabic *qāʾim*, "standing, lasting", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *q-w-m*, "to stand" (Lane).
