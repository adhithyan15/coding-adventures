## You'll want to know: क्षेमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C307-VIYOGA307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षेमः** — *kṣemaḥ* — "safety; well-being".

> अद्य सर्वे क्षेमेण गृहं प्रत्यागच्छन्ति।
>
> *adya sarve kṣemeṇa gṛhaṃ pratyāgacchanti.*
>
> Today everyone comes home in safety.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षेमः** (*kṣemaḥ*) comes from the root √kṣi, "to dwell, abide"; MW gives *kṣema* as giving rest or ease and as safety, security (MW).
