## You'll want to know: बाँधना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C292-DAKAITI292-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बाँधना** — *bā̃dhnā* — "to tie up; to bind".

> डाकुओं ने चौकीदार को रस्सी से बाँधा।
>
> *ḍākuoṁ ne caukīdār ko rassī se bā̃dhā.*
>
> The bandits tied the watchman up with rope.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने रस्सी बाँधी, *usne rassī bā̃dhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाँधना** (*bā̃dhnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *bandhati*, "binds" (CDIAL 9139). A person tied up takes **को** and the verb stays masculine: **चौकीदार को बाँधा**; a thing tied agrees: **रस्सी बाँधी**.
