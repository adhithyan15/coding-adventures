Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 263: The Priest, the Gardener and the Goldsmith.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पुजारी (pujārī) — a temple priest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a village headman, then the Marwadi for a craftsman.

You'll want to know: पुजारी.
पुजारी — pujārī — "a temple priest".
पुजारी मंदिर मांय पूजा करै है। pujārī mandir mā̃y pūjā karai hai. The priest performs worship in the temple.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पुजारी (pujārī) is inherited from Sanskrit pūjākara, "performing worship". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi pujerī, "temple priest" (CDIAL 8317a).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: धोबी.
धोबी — dhobī — "a washerman".
धोबी नदी कनै रैवै है। dhobī nadī kanai raivai hai. The washerman lives near the river.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धोबी (dhobī) is inherited from a reconstructed dhauvati, "washes"; CDIAL lists Marwari dhoṇo, "washes", and Hindi dhobī, "washerman", under it (CDIAL 6886).
