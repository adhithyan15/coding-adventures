## You'll want to know: आराम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C70-LEISURE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आराम** (*ārām*) — "rest, ease".

Persian آرام (*ārām*), "quiet, calm, repose". It came in with the Persian half of
the vocabulary and settled deep: **आरामकुर्सी** is an easy chair, built from
आराम and the **कुर्सी** you already own.

आराम is not sleep. **नींद** is sleep, and you have that word. आराम is the state
of not being pushed — sitting down, breathing out, nothing owed to anybody for an
hour. **आराम करना** is what you say for taking that hour.

It is also an answer. When somebody asks **आप कैसे हैं?**, one ordinary reply is
that you are at आराम, and it says more warmly than **ठीक** does.
