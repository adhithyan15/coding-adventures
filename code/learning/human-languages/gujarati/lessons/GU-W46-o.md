# ઓ — the standing vowel o

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01, GU-SCRIPT-AA-01, GU-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01] -->

- [YOU RECALL: say *they* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say the standing vowel **આ**, and the sign **ો** that gives a consonant its *o*]

**તેઓ** ends on a vowel that stands alone. After a consonant, *o* is a sign:
**તો**, **જો**. At the start of a syllable there is no consonant to carry it, so
*o* needs a letter of its own.

## You'll want to know: આ with an arc above
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-AA-01, GU-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01] -->

**ઓ** is built on **આ**. Keep the whole of **આ**, the body and its stems on the
right, and set one high arc over the top.

| letter | sound | what it is made of |
|---|---|---|
| આ | ā | the body and its stems |
| ઓ | o | આ, then a high arc |

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ઓ** — *o*

Keep **ઓ** visible. Write the joined body of **આ** first: the open curve on the
left, the broad lower body, the small shoulder and arch. Lift, and bring the first
stem down the right side to its foot. Lift again and bring the trailing stem
down beside it. Lift a last time and sweep the high arc from left to right over
the top. Four movements, three lifts.

The arc sits high and clear of the stems. Without it the letter reads **આ**.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઓ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice the body of **આ**, the two
stems, and the arc that rides above them.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઓ** once. Compare only two things: are both stems there, and does
the arc clear their tops? Repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W46-o-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write the standing vowel o from memory.","answer":"ઓ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ઓ — the body of આ, two stems, and the high arc.","incorrect":"Look once at ઓ, hide it, and repair only the landmark that differs."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write the standing vowel *o* once. Uncover **ઓ**, compare, and
stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01, GU-SCRIPT-AA-01, GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **આ ઓ તેઓ** aloud. What turns **આ** into **ઓ**? (**The high
arc.**)
