Persian, chapter 404: Rules.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مقررات (moqarrarât) — regulations, rules.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a bachelor's degree, then the Persian for a doctorate.

You'll want to know: مقررات.
مقررات (moqarrarât) — "regulations, rules".
مقررات این ساختمان خیلی سخت است. moqarrarât-e in sâkhtemân kheyli sakht ast. The rules of this building are very strict.
A plural noun with the Arabic ending -ât; one rule is qâ'ede or qânun.

The word, taken apart.
مقررات (moqarrarât) is usually traced to Arabic muqarrarāt "things decided, laid down", from the same Arabic family as qarār "a settled decision".

رعایت کردن (re'âyat kardan) — to observe, to comply with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for forbidden, then the Persian for allowed.

You'll want to know: رعایت کردن.
رعایت کردن (re'âyat kardan) — "to observe, to comply with".
همه باید قوانین را رعایت کنند. hame bâyad qavânin râ re'âyat konand. Everyone must obey the rules.
Re'âyat "observance, care" + kardan. For rules and laws, and for hygiene too.

The word, taken apart.
رعایت is usually traced to Arabic ri'āya "care, guarding, observance"; with kardan "to do" it means to keep a rule.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نقض کردن (naqz kardan) — to break, to violate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for allowed, then the Persian for to observe.

You'll want to know: نقض کردن.
نقض کردن (naqz kardan) — "to break, to violate".
آن شرکت قانون را نقض کرد. ân sherkat qânun râ naqz kard. That company broke the law.
For laws, rules and agreements; formal.

The word, taken apart.
نقض is usually traced to Arabic naqḍ "undoing, breaking"; Persian says the Arabic ḍ as z.
