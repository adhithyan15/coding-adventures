## You'll want to know: die Baustelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C409-UMLEITUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Baustelle** — "the road works; the building site".

> Wegen der Baustelle fährt der Bus heute langsam.
>
> *Because of the road works the bus is slow today.*

Plural: *die Baustellen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Baustelle* is built from *Bau* 'building', from *bauen* 'to build', inherited from Proto-Germanic *būaną* (EtymDB), and *Stelle* 'place'.

## What you've built
