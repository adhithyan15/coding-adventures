## You'll want to know: cloāca, cloācae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C458-ANGIPORTUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**cloāca, cloācae** — "a sewer, a drain".

> Aqua sordida per cloācam in flūmen it.
>
> *Dirty water runs through the sewer into the river.*

First declension, feminine: *cloāca, cloācae f.* The great sewers of old towns still carry water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cloāca* is built on an old verb *cluō* 'to cleanse, to purge' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), so it is a channel that washes the city clean. English keeps *cloaca* as a term in zoology.
