## You'll want to know: i posteri
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C312-ANTENATI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**i posteri** — "posterity, future generations".

> Questa casa resterà ai posteri.
>
> *This house will be left to posterity.*

Plural only; formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*posteri* is a learned borrowing from Latin *posterī* "those coming after", from *posterus* "following" (Lewis & Short).
