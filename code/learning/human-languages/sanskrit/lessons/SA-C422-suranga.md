## You'll want to know: सुरंगा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C422-PRAKARA422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सुरंगा** — *suraṅgā* — "a tunnel; an underground passage".

> दुर्गस्य अधः सुरंगा अस्ति।
>
> *durgasya adhaḥ suraṅgā asti.*
>
> There is a tunnel beneath the fort.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुरंगा** (*suraṅgā*) is the word MW gives for a hole cut in a wall and an underground passage; MW cites the view that it is Greek *syrinx*, "a pipe", so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Hindi *suraṅg*, a burglar's hole or a mine (MW; CDIAL 13506).
