## You'll want to know: la liberté
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C362-CITOYEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la liberté** — "the freedom, liberty".

> Ces gens se battent pour leur liberté.
>
> *These people are fighting for their freedom.*

*En liberté* — free, at large.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liberté* comes through Old French *liberté* from Latin *lībertās* "freedom" (EtymDB), from *līber* "free" (Lewis & Short).
