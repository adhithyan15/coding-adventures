## You'll want to know: অল্প
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C371-JOTHESHTO371-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অল্প** — *ôlpô* — "a little; a small amount".

> অল্প নুন দিলে তরকারি ভালো হবে।
>
> *ôlpô nun dile tôrkāri bhālo hôbe.*
>
> If you add a little salt, the curry will be good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অল্প** (*ôlpô*) is Sanskrit *alpa*, small or little (Monier-Williams). It is a little more formal than একটু and can stand before a noun.
