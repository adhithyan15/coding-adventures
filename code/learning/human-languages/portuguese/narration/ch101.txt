Portuguese, chapter 101: Necklace, Photograph, Candle, Calendar, Message.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o colar — a necklace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a wallet, then the Portuguese for a ring.

You'll want to know: o colar.
o colar — "a necklace".
