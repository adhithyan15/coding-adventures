## You'll want to know: o acidente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C299-ACIDENTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o acidente** — "the accident".

> Houve um acidente na autoestrada.
>
> *There was an accident on the motorway.*

*Ter um acidente* — to have an accident; *por acidente* — by chance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acidente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *accidēns* "happening, chance" (EtymDB), from *accidere* "to fall upon, to happen", built on *cadere* "to fall".
