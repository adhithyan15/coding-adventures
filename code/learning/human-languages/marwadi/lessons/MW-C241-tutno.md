# टूटणो (ṭūṭṇo) — to get broken; to break by itself

## You'll want to know: टूटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C241-FALL241-04]; assesses=[] -->

**टूटणो** — *ṭūṭṇo* — "to get broken; to break by itself".

> म्हारी चूड़ी टूटी।
>
> *mhārī chūṛī ṭūṭī.*
>
> My bangle broke.

Past (it …): वो प्यालो टूट्यो, *vo pyālo ṭūṭyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टूटणो** (*ṭūṭṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *truṭyati*, "is broken"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *tūṭaï*, "is broken", under it (CDIAL 6065).
