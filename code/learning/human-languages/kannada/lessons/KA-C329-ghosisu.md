# ಘೋಷಿಸು (ghōṣisu) — to announce; to declare

## You'll want to know: ಘೋಷಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C329-MADHYAMA329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘೋಷಿಸು** — *ghōṣisu* — "to announce; to declare".

> ಸರ್ಕಾರ ನಾಳೆ ರಜೆ ಅಂತ ಘೋಷಿಸಿತು.
>
> *sarkāra nāḷe raje anta ghōṣisitu.*
>
> The government announced that tomorrow is a holiday.

Past (I …): ಘೋಷಿಸಿದೆ, *ghōṣiside*.

What is announced comes before ಎಂದು or ಅಂತ: ರಜೆ ಅಂತ ಘೋಷಿಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘೋಷಿಸು** (*ghōṣisu*) is built on Sanskrit *ghoṣa*, "a noise, the cry of a crowd", whose derivative *ghoṣaṇā* means "a proclamation" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
