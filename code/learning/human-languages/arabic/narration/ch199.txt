Arabic, chapter 199: To shake, To bite, To blow, To whisper, To harvest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
هز (hazza) — to shake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to fall, then the Arabic for to dig.

You'll want to know: هز.
هز — hazza — "to shake".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: hazza]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hazza, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to fall, then the Arabic for to dig, then say hazza again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does هز (hazza) mean? ("To shake".) Say it once more.
