## You'll want to know: चाळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C419-VASTI419-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चाळ** — *cāḷ* — "a tenement".

> शहरात अजून काही जुन्या चाळी आहेत.
>
> *śahrāt ajūn kāhī junyā cāḷī āhet.*
>
> There are still some old tenements in the city.

Feminine. Plural: चाळी, *cāḷī*.

A long building of one-room homes along a shared balcony; the plural is चाळी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चाळ** (*cāḷ*) is a Marathi word for a long tenement block; its origin is not settled.
