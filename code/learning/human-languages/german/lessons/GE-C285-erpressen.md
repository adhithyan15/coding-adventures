## You'll want to know: erpressen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C285-BETRUG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**erpressen** — "to blackmail, to extort".

> Ein Unbekannter erpresste die Firma.
>
> *A stranger blackmailed the company.*

Built on *pressen* 'to press': to squeeze money out of someone. *Die Erpressung* — blackmail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erpressen* is built on *pressen* "to press", Old High German *pressōn* (EtymDB), from Latin *pressāre*, built on *premere* "to press" (Lewis & Short).
