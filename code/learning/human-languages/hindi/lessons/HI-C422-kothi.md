## You'll want to know: कोठी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C422-KOTHI422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कोठी** — *koṭhī* — "a large house, a bungalow".

> उस बड़ी कोठी में एक अमीर परिवार रहता है।
>
> *us baṛī koṭhī meṁ ek amīr parivār rahtā hai.*
>
> A rich family lives in that big bungalow.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोठी** (*koṭhī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *koṣṭha*, "a granary, a storeroom" (CDIAL 3546), which lists Hindi *koṭhī*, "granary, large house": the strong room grew into the whole mansion.
