## You'll want to know: नाते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C494-NATE494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नाते** — *nāte* — "a relationship; kinship".

> आमचं आणि त्यांचं जवळचं नातं आहे.
>
> *āmcaṁ āṇi tyāñcaṁ javaḷcaṁ nātaṁ āhe.*
>
> We and they are closely related.

Neuter. Plural: नाती, *nātī*.

Sentences write the neuter as नातं. नातं जोडणे, to form a tie.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाते** (*nāte*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *nātẽ*, "kinship, kinsman", under the reconstructed *\*jñātra*, "kinship" (CDIAL 5279).
