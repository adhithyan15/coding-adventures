## You'll want to know: la rocca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C446-BORGO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la rocca** — "the fortress, the stronghold".

> La rocca domina il paese.
>
> *The fortress looks down on the village.*

Usually on a rock above the town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rocca* goes back to Vulgar Latin *rocca* 'rock, crag'; the deeper origin is not settled, and it may be older than Latin.
