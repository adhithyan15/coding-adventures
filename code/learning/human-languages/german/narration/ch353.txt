German, chapter 353: Im Betrieb.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
der Betrieb — the firm, the business; operation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the trial period, then the German for to train in.

You'll want to know: der Betrieb.
der Betrieb — "the firm, the business; operation".
Mein Onkel hat einen kleinen Betrieb mit acht Mitarbeitern. My uncle has a small business with eight employees.
Plural: die Betriebe. In Betrieb — in operation; außer Betrieb — out of order.

The word, taken apart.
Betrieb is the noun of betreiben "to run, to operate", built with be- on treiben "to drive", inherited from Proto-Germanic drībaną (EtymDB), a cousin of English drive.

Lesson 2 of 6.
das Unternehmen — the company, the enterprise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to train in, then the German for the firm.

You'll want to know: das Unternehmen.
das Unternehmen — "the company, the enterprise".
Das Unternehmen verkauft Möbel in vielen Ländern. The company sells furniture in many countries.
Plural: die Unternehmen. Also the verb unternehmen — to undertake, to do: Was unternehmen wir heute?

The word, taken apart.
Unternehmen is the verb unternehmen "to undertake" made into a noun; the verb is built from unter and nehmen "to take", Old High German neman, Proto-Germanic nemaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 6.

die Abteilung — "the department".
Sie arbeitet in der Abteilung für Marketing. She works in the marketing department.
Plural: die Abteilungen. In a hospital, a ward: die Kinderabteilung.

The word, taken apart.
Abteilung is the noun of abteilen "to divide off", built on Teil "part", Old High German teil, Proto-Germanic dailą (EtymDB): one part of the whole.

Lesson 4 of 6.
der Vorgesetzte — the superior, the boss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the company, then the German for the department.

You'll want to know: der Vorgesetzte.
der Vorgesetzte — "the superior, the boss".
Mein Vorgesetzter ist sehr freundlich. My superior is very friendly.
Declined like an adjective: der Vorgesetzte, ein Vorgesetzter, and die Vorgesetzte for a woman.

The word, taken apart.
Vorgesetzte is the participle of vorsetzen "to set before, to set over", built on setzen "to set", Proto-Germanic satjaną (EtymDB), made into a noun: the person set over you.

Lesson 5 of 6.
leiten — to run, to lead (a team, a firm).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the department, then the German for the superior.

You'll want to know: leiten.
leiten — "to run, to lead (a team, a firm)".
Meine Mutter leitet eine Schule mit vierhundert Kindern. My mother runs a school with four hundred children.
Die Leitung — the management. Der Leiter, die Leiterin — the head.

The word, taken apart.
leiten is inherited from Middle High German leiten, Old High German leiten and Proto-Germanic laidijaną "to lead", built on līþaną "to go" (EtymDB); English lead is a cousin.
