## You'll want to know: جالا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C505-ATARI505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جالا** — *jālā* — "a cobweb".

> چھت کے کونے میں جالا ہے۔
>
> *chhat ke kone meṅ jālā hai.*
>
> There is a cobweb in the corner of the ceiling.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جالا** (*jālā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jālā*, "a net, a web", under Sanskrit *jāla*, "a net" (CDIAL 5213).
