## You'll want to know: il calzolaio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C455-ARTIGIANI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il calzolaio** — "the cobbler, the shoemaker".

> Il calzolaio ripara le mie scarpe vecchie.
>
> *The cobbler repairs my old shoes.*

*Le calze* — socks: both go back to Latin *calceus*, shoe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*calzolaio* is built on *calzare* 'to put on shoes', which is inherited from Latin *calceāre* (EtymDB), from *calceus* 'shoe' (Lewis & Short).
