French, chapter 455: En randonnée.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la randonnée — the hike, the long walk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the mountain pass, then the French for the mountainside.

You'll want to know: la randonnée.
la randonnée — "the hike, the long walk".
Le dimanche, nous faisons une randonnée en montagne. On Sundays we go for a hike in the mountains.
In speech, la rando.

The word, taken apart.
randonnée is built on randonner 'to walk a long way' (EtymDB), from Old French randonner 'to run headlong', usually traced to a Germanic word.

Lesson 5 of 6.
trébucher — to stumble, to trip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the slope, then the French for to climb.

You'll want to know: trébucher.
trébucher — "to stumble, to trip".
Sur le sentier, elle a trébuché sur une pierre. On the path she tripped over a stone.
Trébucher sur un mot — to stumble over a word.

The word, taken apart.
trébucher comes from Old French trebuchier 'to topple' (EtymDB), from tres-, from Latin trāns 'across', and a Frankish word for 'trunk, body'.
