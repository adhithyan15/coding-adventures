## You'll want to know: विजय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C323-VIJAY323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विजय** — *vijay* — "a victory".

> विजय के बाद शहर में जुलूस निकला।
>
> *vijay ke bād śahar meṁ julūs niklā.*
>
> After the victory a procession went through the city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विजय** (*vijay*) is Sanskrit *vijaya*, "victory" (Monier-Williams), the formal word beside **जीत**. Hindi makes it feminine.
