## You'll want to know: dōngtiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C131-SEASON131-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dōngtiān* — "winter".

> *dōngtiān wǒ bù xiǎng chūmén* — In winter I don't feel like going out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dōngtiān* is built from *dōng* 'winter' and 天 'sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *towng* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'the last of the four seasons'.
