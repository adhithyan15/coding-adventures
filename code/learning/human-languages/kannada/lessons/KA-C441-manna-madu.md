# ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು (mannā māḍu) — to waive; to write off

## You'll want to know: ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C441-SHULKA441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು** — *mannā māḍu* — "to waive; to write off".

> ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಮಕ್ಕಳ ಶುಲ್ಕವನ್ನು ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡಿತು.
>
> *hōṭel makkaḷa śulkavannu mannā māḍitu.*
>
> The hotel waived the fee for the children.

Past (I …): ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *mannā māḍide*.

Said of fees, fines and debts: ದಂಡ ಮನ್ನಾ, a fine waived.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು** (*mannā māḍu*) joins *mannā*, "waived, remitted", which came into Kannada through Hindustani and whose earlier source is not settled, and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.
