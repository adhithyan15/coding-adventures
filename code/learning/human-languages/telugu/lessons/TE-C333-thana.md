## You'll want to know: ఠాణా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C333-KESU333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఠాణా** — *ṭhāṇā* — "a police station".

> వాళ్ళు దొంగతనం గురించి ఠాణాలో చెప్పారు.
>
> *vāḷḷu doṅgatanaṁ guriñci ṭhāṇālō ceppāru.*
>
> They reported the theft at the police station.

An older word for the పోలీసు స్టేషన్; the news still uses it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఠాణా** (*ṭhāṇā*) is a loan from Hindustani *thānā*, "a post, a station", which goes back to Sanskrit *sthāna*, "a place" (CDIAL 13753).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
