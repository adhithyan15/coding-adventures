## You'll want to know: rùkǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C112-EXIT112-02]; assesses=[] -->

*rùkǒu* — "entrance; way in".

> *rùkǒu zài nàr* — The way in is over there.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rùkǒu* is built from rù 'to go in' and 口 'opening', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyip* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'inside, to take in' and 'mouth'.
