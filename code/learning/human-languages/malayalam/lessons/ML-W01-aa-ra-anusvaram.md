# ാ · ര · ം — finish the sound inventory

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-SA-01, ML-SCRIPT-CHANDRAKKALA-01, ML-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **സ്ക** once: *ska*. The greeting needs only three more
written pieces.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, ML-SCRIPT-RA-01, ML-SCRIPT-ANUSVARAM-01]; assesses=[] -->

- **ാ** is the long-*ā* vowel sign. Attach it to known **ക**: **കാ**, *kā*.
- **ര** is *ra*.
- **ം** is the *anusvaram*, the final nasal *m* here. With **ര**: **രം**, *ram*.

These are the last new shapes in the greeting.

## Writing — observe and trace the last three
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, ML-SCRIPT-RA-01, ML-SCRIPT-ANUSVARAM-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **ാ**, **ര**, and **ം** once each while the models remain visible. Then
trace **കാ** and **രം** once. Say *kā-ram* after your finger stops.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-KA-01, ML-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, ML-SCRIPT-RA-01, ML-SCRIPT-ANUSVARAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What makes **ക** into **കാ**? (**The long-*ā* sign, ാ.**) What does
the final circle **ം** contribute here? (**A final nasal *m*.**) How many new
shapes remain before reading the whole greeting? (**None.**)
