## You'll want to know: ಕರಕುಶಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C533-KASUTI533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರಕುಶಲ** — *karakuśala* — "handicraft".

> ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕರಕುಶಲ ವಸ್ತುಗಳನ್ನು ಮಾರುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು.
>
> *jātreyalli karakuśala vastugaḷannu māruttiddaru.*
>
> Handicrafts were being sold at the fair.

ಕರ, hand + ಕುಶಲ, skilful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರಕುಶಲ** (*karakuśala*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *kara*, "the hand", and *kuśala*, "skilful, clever" (Monier-Williams).
