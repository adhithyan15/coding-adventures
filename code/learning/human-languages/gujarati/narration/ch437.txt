Gujarati, chapter 437: Frost and the Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઠાર (ṭhār) — a hard frost; a biting chill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to scorch, then the Gujarati for to bake (in the heat).

You'll want to know: ઠાર.
ઠાર — ṭhār — "a hard frost; a biting chill".
ગઈકાલે રાત્રે ઘણો ઠાર પડ્યો. gaīkāle rātre ghaṇo ṭhār paḍyo. There was a hard frost last night.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઠાર (ṭhār) goes back to sthāra, "firm, firmness", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "frost" (CDIAL 13765): the cold that makes things set hard.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ટાઢ (ṭāḍh) — the cold; cold weather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a biting chill, then the Gujarati for heavy dew.

You'll want to know: ટાઢ.
ટાઢ — ṭāḍh — "the cold; cold weather".
આજે ઘણી ટાઢ છે. āje ghaṇī ṭāḍh che. It is very cold today.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટાઢ (ṭāḍh) goes back to Sanskrit stabdhi, "fixedness, numbness"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word under it (CDIAL 13677): the cold that makes your hands stiff.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for heavy dew, then the Gujarati for the cold.

You'll want to know: થીજવું.
થીજવું — thījvũ — "to congeal; to freeze solid".
રાત્રે ઠારમાં ઘી થીજી ગયું. rātre ṭhārmā̃ ghī thījī gayũ. In the night's frost the ghee set solid.
Past (it …): તે પાણી થીજી ગયું, te pāṇī thījī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
થીજવું (thījvũ) goes back to sthiryati, "becomes firm", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to thicken from cold" (CDIAL 13772).

Lesson 5 of 5.
થરથરવું (thartharvũ) — to shiver; to tremble all over.

You'll want to know: થરથરવું.
થરથરવું — thartharvũ — "to shiver; to tremble all over".
ટાઢમાં બાળકો થરથરતાં હતાં. ṭāḍhmā̃ bāḷko tharthartā̃ hatā̃. The children were shivering in the cold.
Past (he …): તે ટાઢમાં થરથર્યો, te ṭāḍhmā̃ thartharyo.

The word, taken apart.
થરથરવું (thartharvũ) goes back to thar, "to tremble", a form CDIAL reconstructs and lists doubled in this verb; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tremble" (CDIAL 6092).
