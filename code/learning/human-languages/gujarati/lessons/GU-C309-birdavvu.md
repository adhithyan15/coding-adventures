# બિરદાવવું (birdāvvũ) — to applaud; to praise publicly

## You'll want to know: બિરદાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C309-INAM309-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બિરદાવવું** — *birdāvvũ* — "to applaud; to praise publicly".

> બધાએ અરુણના સાહસને બિરદાવ્યું.
>
> *badhāe Aruṇnā sāhasne birdāvyũ.*
>
> Everyone applauded Arun's courage.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે તેને બિરદાવ્યો, *teṇe tene birdāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is built on *birad*, praise, from Sanskrit *viruda*, "a laudatory poem", which Monier-Williams also gives as *birada*. **વખાણવું** is to praise; **બિરદાવવું** is to praise in public, before others.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
