## Script — a dot and a bar, then the body
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YI-CLOTHES-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 衣

**One piece, six strokes.**

**The top, two strokes.** A dot. A long horizontal under it.

**The body, four strokes.** A long stroke falling to the lower left. A short vertical inside it that flicks up to the right at its foot. A short stroke on the right falling to the left. Last, a long stroke falling to the lower right.

2 + 4 = six.
