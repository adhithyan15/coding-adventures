## You'll want to know: ゆだん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C350-YOUJIN350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆだん** — *yudan* — "carelessness; letting one's guard down".

> **ゆだんしたら、けがを します。** — *yudan shitara, kega o shimasu* — "If you let your guard down, you will get hurt."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆだん** (*yudan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yu*, "oil" (Middle Chinese *yuw*) and *dan*, "severance" (*dwanX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
