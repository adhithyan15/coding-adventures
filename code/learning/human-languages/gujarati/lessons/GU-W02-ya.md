# ય — upper turn, lower body, separate spine, ya

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **વ** once from memory. Name its two runs, then move on.

## Script — meet the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-YA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ય** visible. It is **ya**. Begin at the upper left, circle through the
rounded upper turn, and sweep around the broad lower body into the long right
shoulder. Lift once; descend the tall right spine and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-YA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace the body once with a finger, lift, then trace the spine. Repeat once in the air.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-YA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ય** once. Check upper turn, lower body, shoulder, spine.
