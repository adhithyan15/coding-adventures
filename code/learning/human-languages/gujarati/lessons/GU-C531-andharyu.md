# અણધાર્યું (aṇdhāryũ) — unexpected

## You'll want to know: અણધાર્યું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C531-JHANJHAT531-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અણધાર્યું** — *aṇdhāryũ* — "unexpected".

> જો કંઈક અણધાર્યું બનશે તો મને તરત ફોન કરો.
>
> *jo kaīk aṇdhāryũ banśe to mane tarat phon karo.*
>
> If something unexpected happens, phone me at once.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અણધાર્યું** (*aṇdhāryũ*) is built from *aṇ-*, "un-", and **ધાર્યું**, the past of **ધારવું**, "to suppose": not supposed, unexpected. CDIAL files **ધારવું** under Sanskrit *dhāráyati*, "holds" (CDIAL 6791).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
