## You'll want to know: سارق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C316-DOZD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سارق** (*sâreq*) — "a robber, a burglar (formal)".

> پلیس به دنبال سارق است.
>
> *Polis be donbâl-e sâreq ast.*
>
> The police are looking for the robber.

Formal, in the news; in speech *dozd*. Plural *sâreqân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sâreq* is borrowed from Arabic *sāriq* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), the active participle "one who steals".
