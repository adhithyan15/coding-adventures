## You'll want to know: yǒuqù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C277-LIKES277-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuqù* — "fun; amusing".

> *zhège gùshi hěn yǒuqù* — This story is a lot of fun.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǒuqù* is built from 有 'to have' and *qù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuwX* and *tshjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to tend towards'. How the pair came to mean amusing is not settled.
