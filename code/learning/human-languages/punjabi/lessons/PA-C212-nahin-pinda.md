# ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ — I don't drink tea

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-NEG-CLAUSE-01, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-LEX-CHA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I drink tea every day*, with the ending that fits you.

## Grammar Lens: ਨਹੀਂ before the verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212]; assesses=[PA-NEG-CLAUSE-01, PA-LEX-HAAN-NAHIN-01, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05] -->

Put **ਨਹੀਂ** directly before **ਪੀਂਦਾ**. Everything else stays where it was,
the helper **ਹਾਂ** included:

> **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** — *maiṁ chāh nahīṁ pīndā hāṁ.* — I don't drink tea (a man speaking).

> **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।** — *maiṁ chāh nahīṁ pīndī hāṁ.* — I don't drink tea (a woman speaking).

> **ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।** — *uh roṭī nahīṁ khāndā hai.* — He doesn't eat roti.

This book keeps the helper after **ਨਹੀਂ** in every sentence it prints.
