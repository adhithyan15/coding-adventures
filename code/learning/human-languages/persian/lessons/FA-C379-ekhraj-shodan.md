# اخراج شدن (ekhrâj shodan) — to be dismissed, to be fired

## You'll want to know: اخراج شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C379-ESTEFA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اخراج شدن** (*ekhrâj shodan*) — "to be dismissed, to be fired".

> او به خاطر دیر آمدن اخراج شد.
>
> *u be khâter-e dir âmadan ekhrâj shod.*
>
> He was fired for coming late.

The passive of *ekhrâj kardan*, with *shodan* (stem *shav-*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اخراج شدن** joins *ekhrâj*, usually traced to Arabic *ikhrāj* "putting out", to the inherited Persian verb *shodan* "to become".
