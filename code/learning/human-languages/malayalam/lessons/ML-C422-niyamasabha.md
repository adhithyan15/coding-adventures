# നിയമസഭ (niyamasabha) — a legislative assembly

## You'll want to know: നിയമസഭ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C422-SABHA422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**നിയമസഭ** — *niyamasabha* — "a legislative assembly".

> നിയമസഭയിൽ ഇന്ന് വലിയ ചർച്ച നടന്നു.
>
> *niyamasabhayil innŭ valiya carcca naṭannu.*
>
> There was a big discussion in the assembly today.

A member of it is നിയമസഭാംഗം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നിയമസഭ** (*niyamasabha*) is a Malayalam compound built from Sanskrit *niyama*, "a fixed law" (CDIAL 7250), and *sabhā*, "an assembly, a hall" (CDIAL 13167): the assembly that makes laws.
