## You'll want to know: નિભાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C358-SEVA358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નિભાવવું** — *nibhāvvũ* — "to keep up (a promise or a duty)".

> અરુણ હંમેશા પોતાનું વચન નિભાવે છે.
>
> *Aruṇ hammeśā potānũ vacan nibhāve che.*
>
> Arun always keeps his word.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાની ફરજ નિભાવી, *teṇe potānī pharaj nibhāvī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિભાવવું** (*nibhāvvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nírvahati*, "leads out of, accomplishes"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 7397).
