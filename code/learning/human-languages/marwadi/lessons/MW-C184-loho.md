## You'll want to know: लोहो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C184-OKHLI184-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लोहो** — *loho* — "iron".

> लोहो भारी है।
>
> *loho bhārī hai.*
>
> Iron is heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोहो** (*loho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *loha*, first "copper", later "iron"; CDIAL lists Hindi *lohā* (CDIAL 11158). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
