## You'll want to know: જણાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C311-SAMACHAR311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જણાવવું** — *jaṇāvvũ* — "to inform".

> પોલીસે લોકોને જણાવ્યું કે રસ્તો બંધ છે.
>
> *polīse lokone jaṇāvyũ ke rasto bandh che.*
>
> The police told people that the road was closed.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધાને જણાવ્યું, *teṇe badhāne jaṇāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જણાવવું** (*jaṇāvvũ*) belongs with Sanskrit *jānāti*, "knows", where CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "informs" (CDIAL 5193): to make someone know. It sits beside **જાણવું**, to know.
