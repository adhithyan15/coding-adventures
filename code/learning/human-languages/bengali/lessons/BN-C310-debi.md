## You'll want to know: দেবী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C310-SHONKHO310-04]; assesses=[] -->

**দেবী** — *debī* — "a goddess".

> গ্রামের মানুষ নদীর দেবীকে ভয় পায়।
>
> *grāmer mānush nôdīr debīke bhôy pāy.*
>
> The villagers are afraid of the river goddess.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দেবী** (*debī*) is Sanskrit *devī*, 'a female deity, a goddess' (Monier-Williams), the feminine of *deva*, a god.
