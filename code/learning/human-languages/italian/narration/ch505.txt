Italian, chapter 505: Collezionisti.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
collezionare — to collect (as a hobby).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for bet, then the Italian for crossword.

You'll want to know: collezionare.
collezionare — "to collect (as a hobby)".
Da bambino collezionavo francobolli. As a child I collected stamps.
For things kept as a hobby; to pick up, say raccogliere.

The word, taken apart.
collezionare is built on collezione "collection", a learned borrowing from Latin collēctiō "a gathering" (EtymDB), from colligere "to gather".

Said as in English; the plural is i puzzle.

The word, taken apart.
puzzle was borrowed from English puzzle (EtymDB); the origin of the English word is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
il modellismo — model making.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for collection, then the Italian for jigsaw puzzle.

You'll want to know: il modellismo.
il modellismo — "model making".
La sua passione è il modellismo: costruisce treni in miniatura. His passion is model making: he builds miniature trains.
The person is il modellista.

The word, taken apart.
modellismo is built on modello "model", from Vulgar Latin modellus (EtymDB), a little form of Latin modulus "a small measure" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
assemblare — to assemble, to put together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for jigsaw puzzle, then the Italian for model making.

You'll want to know: assemblare.
assemblare — "to assemble, to put together".
Ho assemblato il mobile da solo in due ore. I put the piece of furniture together by myself in two hours.
For furniture or parts; montare means the same in speech.

The word, taken apart.
assemblare came from French assembler "to bring together", usually traced to Latin simul "together" (Lewis & Short).
