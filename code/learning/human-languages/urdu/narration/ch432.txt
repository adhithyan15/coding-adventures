Urdu, chapter 432: Sunrise and Sunset.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
طلوع (tulū) — the rising (of the sun).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for mist, then the Urdu for to let up.

You'll want to know: طلوع.
طلوع — tulū — "the rising (of the sun)".
سورج چھ بجے طلوع ہوتا ہے۔ sūraj chhe baje tulū hotā hai. The sun rises at six.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
طلوع (tulū) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṭulūʿ, "rising"; with ہونا it means the sun comes up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

صبح چھ بجے اجالا ہوتا ہے۔ subah chhe baje ujālā hotā hai. It gets light at six in the morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اجالا (ujālā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ujālā, "bright", under ujjvālaka, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1673).
