## You'll want to know: парикмахерская
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C449-STRIZHKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**парикмахерская** (*parikmákherskaya*) — "a hairdresser's, a barber's".

> Парикмахерская работает до восьми.
>
> *Parikmákherskaya rabótayet do vosmí.*
>
> The hairdresser's is open until eight.

An adjective used as a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*парикмахерская* is built on *парикмахер* 'a hairdresser' with an adjective ending; *парикмахер* was borrowed from German *Perückenmacher* 'a wig-maker' (EtymDB).
