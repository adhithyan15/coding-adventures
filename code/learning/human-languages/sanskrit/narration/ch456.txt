Sanskrit, chapter 456: Calm and Composure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क्षुभ्यति (kṣubhyati) — is shaken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for attachment, then the Sanskrit for tenderness for a child.

You'll want to know: क्षुभ्यति.
क्षुभ्यति — kṣubhyati — "is shaken".
वार्तया जनाः क्षुभ्यन्ति। vārtayā janāḥ kṣubhyanti. People are shaken by the news.
Past (he …): सः वार्तया क्षुभितवान्, saḥ vārtayā kṣubhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
क्षुभ्यति (kṣubhyati) comes from the root √kṣubh, "to shake, to be agitated"; MW's ppp kṣubhita is "agitated, disturbed, frightened" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
प्रसादयति (prasādayati) — appeases.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tenderness for a child, then the Sanskrit for is shaken.

You'll want to know: प्रसादयति.
प्रसादयति — prasādayati — "appeases".
पुत्रः कुपितं पितरं प्रसादयति। putraḥ kupitaṃ pitaraṃ prasādayati. The son calms his angry father.
Past (he …): सः कुपितं पितरं प्रसादितवान्, saḥ kupitaṃ pitaraṃ prasāditavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसादयति (prasādayati) is built on pra and the root √sad, "to sit": pra-√sad is "to become placid, to be pleased", and this causative is "to make calm"; MW's ppp prasādita is "pleased, conciliated" (MW).

You'll want to know: समाहितः.
समाहितः — samāhitaḥ — "composed; collected".
परीक्षायाम् अपि छात्रः समाहितः अस्ति। parīkṣāyām api chātraḥ samāhitaḥ asti. Even in the exam the student stays composed.

The word, taken apart.
समाहितः (samāhitaḥ) is built from sam, ā and hita, "put", from the root √dhā, "to put": put together; MW gives "put or held together" and then "composed, collected, concentrated" (MW).

माता बालस्य पादौ मर्दयति। mātā bālasya pādau mardayati. Mother rubs the child's feet.

The word, taken apart.
मर्दयति (mardayati) is built on the causative of the root √mṛd, "to press, to crush"; MW gives "to press or squeeze hard" and "to rub" (MW).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: एकान्तः.

एकान्ते मनः शान्तं भवति। ekānte manaḥ śāntaṃ bhavati. In a quiet place the mind grows calm.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
एकान्तः (ekāntaḥ) is built from eka, "one", and anta, "an end, a side": a place set to one side; MW gives "a lonely or retired or secret place" (MW).
