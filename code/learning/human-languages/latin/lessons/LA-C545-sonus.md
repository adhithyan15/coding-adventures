## You'll want to know: sonus, sonī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C545-SONU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**sonus, sonī** — "a sound".

> Sonus tubae per castra it.
>
> *The sound of the trumpet goes through the camp.*

Masculine, second declension. English *sound* comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sonus** belongs with *sonō* "to sound", inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to sound" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *sound* comes from it through French.
