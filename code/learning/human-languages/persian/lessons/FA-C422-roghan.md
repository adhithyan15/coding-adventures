## You'll want to know: روغن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C422-KAPUT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**روغن** (*roghan*) — "oil (for an engine or for cooking)".

> مکانیک گفت ماشین روغن لازم دارد.
>
> *Mekânik goft mâshin roghan lâzem dârad.*
>
> The mechanic said the car needs oil.

*Roghan-e motor* — engine oil; *roghan-e zeytun* — olive oil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*roghan* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian *rōghn* 'oil; butter'.
