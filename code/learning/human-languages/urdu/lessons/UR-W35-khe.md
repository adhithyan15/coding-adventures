# خ (khe) — the letter that opens خدا

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-JIM-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-BARI-HE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ج and ح once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **خدا** (*khudā*), "God".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: خ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-KHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خ** — *khe*, a *kh* scraped at the back of the throat.

It opens **خدا** (*khudā*), the first half of **خدا حافظ**. The same skeleton as
**ح**, with **one dot above**.

## Writing: خ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-KHE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the short upper head from left to right
- **2.** without lifting, continue down and around the deep bowl
- **3.** lift once, then place the dot above — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Khe, ze, zāl, swād, and zwād, independent خ handwriting animation and Khe instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-JIM-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-BARI-HE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-KHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **خدا** and point at خ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: خ three times, saying *kh* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ح, then خ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **خدا** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-JIM-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-BARI-HE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-KHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — خ? (**khe**, *kh*.) Which word did it
come from? (**خدا**, *khudā*, "God".)
