Persian, chapter 303: First, then and in the end.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ابتدا (ebtedâ) — at first, at the start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to come to an end, then the Persian for finally.

You'll want to know: ابتدا.
ابتدا (ebtedâ) — "at first, at the start".
ابتدا (ebtedâ) همه ساکت بودند. Ebtedâ hame sâket budand. At first everyone was quiet.
Formal; in speech avval. Also a noun: dar ebtedâ-ye râh "at the start of the road".

The word, taken apart.
ebtedâ is borrowed from Arabic ibtidā', "a beginning" (EtymDB lists the Arabic source).

You'll want to know: نخست.

نخست (nakhost) نامه را خواند و بعد گریه کرد. Nakhost nâme râ khând va ba'd gerye kard. First she read the letter, and then she cried.
Literary; nakhost vazir "prime minister". In speech avval.

Lesson 3 of 5.
آنگاه (ângâh) — then, at that point.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for at first, then the Persian for first.

You'll want to know: آنگاه.
آنگاه (ângâh) — "then, at that point".
شام خوردیم و آنگاه به پارک رفتیم. Shâm khordim va ângâh be pârk raftim. We had dinner and then went to the park.
Literary; in speech ba'd. In writing it can answer an agar clause: "if..., then...".

The word, taken apart.
ângâh is built from Persian ân "that" and gâh "time, place", both native words: "that time".

Lesson 4 of 5.
دست آخر (dast-e âkhar) — at the very end, in the end (colloquial).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for first, then the Persian for then.

You'll want to know: دست آخر.
دست آخر (dast-e âkhar) — "at the very end, in the end (colloquial)".
دست آخر (dast-e âkhar) همه با هم شام خوردیم. Dast-e âkhar hame bâ ham shâm khordim. In the end we all had dinner together.
Conversational. Literally "the last hand", as in a card game. Written without the ezafe sign, read dast-e.

The word, taken apart.
dast "hand" is inherited, from Old Persian dasta- (EtymDB); âkhar "last" is borrowed from Arabic ākhir (EtymDB). The phrase is the last hand of a game.

Lesson 5 of 5.
در نهایت (dar nahâyat) — ultimately, in the end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for then, then the Persian for at the very end.

You'll want to know: در نهایت.
در نهایت (dar nahâyat) — "ultimately, in the end".
در نهایت (dar nahâyat) تصمیم گرفتیم در خانه بمانیم. Dar nahâyat tasmim gereftim dar khâne bemânim. Ultimately we decided to stay at home.
nahâyat alone means "the utmost": nahâyat-e lotf "the height of kindness".

The word, taken apart.
nahâyat is borrowed from Arabic nihāya, "end, limit" (EtymDB lists the Arabic source); dar "in" is inherited Persian.
