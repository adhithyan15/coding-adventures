Sanskrit, chapter 167: Turtle, She-buffalo, Calf, She-goat, Mare.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
कच्छपः (kacchapaḥ) — a turtle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a banyan tree, then the Sanskrit for a palm tree.
