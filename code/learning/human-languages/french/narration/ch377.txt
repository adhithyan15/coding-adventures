French, chapter 377: Avant l'examen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
réviser — to revise, to study for (an exam); to service (a car).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the semester, then the French for the lecture hall.

You'll want to know: réviser.
réviser — "to revise, to study for (an exam); to service (a car)".
Je révise mes cours tous les soirs. I revise my notes every evening.
Belgium and Québec: étudier. Faire réviser sa voiture — to have the car serviced.

The word, taken apart.
réviser is a learned borrowing from Latin revīsere "to view again, to revisit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on vīsere, from vidēre "to see".

manuel comes from Late Latin manuāle "a handbook" (EtymDB), from manuālis "of the hand", built on manus "hand" (Lewis & Short).
