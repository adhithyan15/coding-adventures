## You'll want to know: さらいしゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C170-NEWYEAR170-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さらいしゅう** — *saraishū* — "the week after next".

> **さらいしゅう ひっこします。** — *saraishū hikkoshimasu* — "I will move house the week after next."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さらいしゅう** (*saraishū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "again" (Middle Chinese *tsojH*), *rai*, "coming" (*loj*) and *shū*, "week", whose Middle Chinese reading the sources here do not give (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
