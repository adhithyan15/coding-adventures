## You'll want to know: складывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C531-CLUTTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**складывать** (*skládyvat*) — "to fold; to put together".

> Папа аккуратно складывает рубашки.
>
> *Pápa akkurátno skládyvayet rubáshki.*
>
> Dad folds his shirts neatly.

Imperfective; perfective сложить. Also to add up numbers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*складывать* is built on *с-* 'together' and the root of *класть* 'to put'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
