Marathi, chapter 514: Getting It Done.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कार्य (kārya) — a task; a function.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to present, then the Marathi for to host a programme.

You'll want to know: कार्य.
कार्य — kārya — "a task; a function".
हे कार्य आज संध्याकाळपर्यंत पूर्ण झालं. he kārya āj sandhyākāḷparyant pūrṇa jhālaṁ. This task was finished by this evening.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
कार्य (kārya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kārya, "what is to be done, work" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
कामकाज (kāmkāj) — the day's business; proceedings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to host a programme, then the Marathi for a task.

You'll want to know: कामकाज.
कामकाज — kāmkāj — "the day's business; proceedings".
सुट्टीमुळे कार्यालयाचं कामकाज बंद होतं. suṭṭīmuḷe kāryālayācaṁ kāmkāj band hotaṁ. Because of the holiday the office's business was closed.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
कामकाज (kāmkāj) is a Marathi pairing of काम, "work", and काज, "business", which is inherited from Sanskrit kārya (CDIAL 3078).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उरकणे (urakṇe) — to get done; to finish off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the day's business, then the Marathi for a shift.

You'll want to know: उरकणे.
उरकणे — urakṇe — "to get done; to finish off".
दुपारच्या आधी त्याने सगळी कामं उरकली. dupārcyā ādhī tyāne sagḷī kāmaṁ uraklī. He got all the jobs done before noon.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने काम उरकलं, tyāne kām uraklaṁ.
With a plural neuter object the past ends -ली: कामं उरकली.

The word, taken apart.
उरकणे (urakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi urakṇẽ, "to be over, be finished", under urvara, "left over" (CDIAL 2356).

Lesson 5 of 5.
धडपडणे (dhaḍpaḍṇe) — to struggle; to scramble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a shift, then the Marathi for to get done.

You'll want to know: धडपडणे.
धडपडणे — dhaḍpaḍṇe — "to struggle; to scramble".
नवीन कामात सुरुवातीला मी खूप धडपडलो. navīn kāmāt suruvātīlā mī khūp dhaḍpaḍlo. At first I struggled a lot in the new job.
Past (he …): तो धडपडला, to dhaḍpaḍlā.
A woman says धडपडले.

The word, taken apart.
धडपडणे (dhaḍpaḍṇe) is an expressive Marathi verb built on the sound word धडपड; its origin is not settled.
