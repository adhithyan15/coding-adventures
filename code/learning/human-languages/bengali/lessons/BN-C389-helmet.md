# হেলমেট (helmeṭ) — a helmet

## You'll want to know: হেলমেট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C389-CHALOK389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**হেলমেট** — *helmeṭ* — "a helmet".

> মোটরসাইকেলে হেলমেট পরতে হবে।
>
> *moṭôrsāikele helmeṭ pôrte hôbe.*
>
> On a motorbike you have to wear a helmet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হেলমেট** (*helmeṭ*) is borrowed from English *helmet*, with the final t said as ট.
