Persian, chapter 41: Fruit, Apple, Banana, Tomato, Carrot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
میوه (miveh) — fruit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for rice, then the Persian for meat.

You'll want to know: میوه.
میوه — miveh — "fruit".

The word, taken apart.
miveh is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian mēwag, "fruit" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
گوجه (gowje) — a tomato.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an apple, then the Persian for a banana.

You'll want to know: گوجه.
گوجه — gowje — "a tomato".

The word, taken apart.
gowje is short for gowje farangi, "European plum": the tomato was named after the plum, gowje, and labelled farangi, "Frankish, European". Where gowje itself comes from is not settled.
