## You'll want to know: রাজ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C313-RAJYO313-01]; assesses=[] -->

**রাজ্য** — *rājyô* — "a kingdom; a state".

> অনেক দিন আগে এখানে একটা ছোট রাজ্য ছিল।
>
> *ônek din āge ekhāne ekṭā chhoṭo rājyô chhilô.*
>
> Long ago there was a small kingdom here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রাজ্য** (*rājyô*) is Sanskrit *rājya*, 'kingship, sovereignty; a kingdom' (Monier-Williams), the abstract of *rājan*, a king — **রাজা**. Today it is also a state of India.
