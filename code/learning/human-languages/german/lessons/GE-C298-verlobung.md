## You'll want to know: die Verlobung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C298-VERLOBUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Verlobung** — "the engagement (to marry)".

> Zur Verlobung schenkte er ihr einen Ring.
>
> *For the engagement he gave her a ring.*

Plural: *die Verlobungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Verlobung* is built on *verloben* "to get engaged", from *loben*, Proto-Germanic *lubōną* "to praise, to vow" (EtymDB).
