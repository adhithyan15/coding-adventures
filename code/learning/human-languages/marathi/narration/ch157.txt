Marathi, chapter 157: To taste, To sow, To grow, To get tired, To stay awake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चाखणे (cākhṇe) — to taste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to bite, then the Marathi for to swallow.

You'll want to know: चाखणे.
चाखणे — cākhṇe — "to taste".

The word, taken apart.
चाखणे (cākhṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cakṣati, "tastes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4557).

Lesson 2 of 5.
पेरणे (perṇe) — to sow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to swallow, then the Marathi for to taste.

You'll want to know: पेरणे.
पेरणे — perṇe — "to sow".

The word, taken apart.
पेरणे (perṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from prakirati, "scatters" (CDIAL 8443). CDIAL glosses the Marathi verb "to scatter, strew, sow", and calls Bloch's source pradara wrong.
