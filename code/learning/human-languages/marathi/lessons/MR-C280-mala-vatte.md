# मला वाटतं की … (malā vāṭtaṁ kī …) — what I think

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-VATNE, MR-JOIN-KII] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I feel fine", with the verb that puts you in the dative. (*malā baraṁ vāṭtaṁ*.) Then "Meera says that Marathi is easy". (*mīrā mhaṇte kī marāṭhī sopaṁ āhe*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Put the two together and you have an opinion.

## You'll want to know: मला वाटतं की …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C280-VATTE-KI-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-FEELING, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-LEX-C99-NATURE99-01] -->

> **मला वाटतं की उद्या पाऊस पडेल.**
>
> *malā vāṭtaṁ kī udyā pāūs paḍel.* — "I think it will rain tomorrow."

Word for word: "to me it seems that tomorrow rain will fall". The thinker stands in the dative, as with **आवडणे**; **की** opens the opinion, and the opinion is a whole sentence. To ask: **तुम्हाला काय वाटतं?** (*tumhālā kāy vāṭtaṁ?*), "What do you think?" In the past: **मला वाटलं की …** (*malā vāṭlaṁ kī …*), "I thought that …". "I don't think so": **मला असं वाटत नाही** (*malā asaṁ vāṭat nāhī*), with **असं** (*asaṁ*, "so, like this").

## You'll want to know: माझ्या मते, 'in my opinion'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C280-MAJHYA-MATE-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C275-VASTU275-03, MR-LEX-CHANGLA, MR-GRAMMAR-PRON-OBLIQUE] -->

> **माझ्या मते हा चित्रपट चांगला आहे.**
>
> *mājhyā mate hā citrapaṭ cāṅglā āhe.* — "In my opinion this film is good."

**मते** is **मत**, "an opinion", the word inside **मत देणे**, "to vote", with an ending; **माझ्या** is **माझा** bent to fit it. It sits at the front of the sentence, and it sounds firmer than **मला वाटतं**.
