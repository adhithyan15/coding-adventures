## You'll want to know: आरोप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C348-PRAMAN348-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आरोप** — *ārop* — "an accusation, a charge".

> पुलिस का आरोप है कि ड्राइवर ने नियम तोड़ा।
>
> *pulis kā ārop hai ki ḍrāivar ne niyam toṛā.*
>
> The police allege that the driver broke the rules.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आरोप** (*ārop*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āropa*, "imposing a burden, charging with" (Monier-Williams).
