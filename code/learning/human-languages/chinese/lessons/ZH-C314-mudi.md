## You'll want to know: mùdì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C314-FUNERAL314-04]; assesses=[] -->

*mùdì* — "cemetery; graveyard".

> *mùdì lí zhèr hěn yuǎn* — The cemetery is a long way from here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mùdì* is built from *mù* 'a grave' and 地 'ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *muH* and *dijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a grave mound'.
