Marathi, chapter 255: To insist, To interrupt, To mix, To pray, To recommend.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आग्रह करणे (āgrah karṇe) — to insist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to bear, then the Marathi for to dream.

You'll want to know: आग्रह करणे.
आग्रह करणे — āgrah karṇe — "to insist".

The word, taken apart.
आग्रह is Sanskrit āgraha, "seizing; insistence", a tatsama built on grah, "to seize". करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 3 of 5.
मिसळणे (misaḷṇe) — to mix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to insist, then the Marathi for to interrupt.

You'll want to know: मिसळणे.
मिसळणे — misaḷṇe — "to mix".

The word, taken apart.
मिसळणे (misaḷṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from miśrala, "mixed", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10138). It is built on Sanskrit miśra, "mixed".
