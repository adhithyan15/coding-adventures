## You'll want to know: die Leinwand
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C518-MALEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Leinwand** — "canvas; screen".

> Der Maler stellt eine neue Leinwand auf.
>
> *The painter sets up a new canvas.*

Plural: *die Leinwände*. Also a cinema screen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Leinwand* is inherited from Middle High German *līnwāt* "linen" (EtymDB), built on *lein* "flax" and *wāt* "cloth"; the second part was later reshaped like *Wand* "wall".
