## You'll want to know: die Steuer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C391-STEUER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Steuer** — "the tax".

> Auf Zigaretten zahlt man hohe Steuern.
>
> *You pay high taxes on cigarettes.*

Plural: *die Steuern*. Careful: *das Steuer* is the steering wheel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Steuer* "tax" is usually traced to Old High German *stiura* "a prop, a support", and so a contribution; EtymDB lists English *steer* as a cousin of the word.
