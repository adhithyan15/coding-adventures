## You'll want to know: सताना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C381-KASH381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सताना** — *satānā* — "to torment, to harass".

> अगर तुम उसे नहीं सताते तो वह नहीं रोता।
>
> *agar tum use nahīṃ satāte to vah nahīṃ rotā.*
>
> If you hadn't tormented him, he wouldn't have cried.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने छोटे भाई को सताया, *usne choṭe bhāī ko satāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सताना** (*satānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *saṃtāpayati*, "inflames; torments" (CDIAL 12886).
