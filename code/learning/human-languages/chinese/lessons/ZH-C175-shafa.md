## You'll want to know: shāfā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C175-FURN175-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shāfā* — "sofa".

> *wǒmen de shāfā hěn shūfu* — Our sofa is really comfortable.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shāfā* is usually traced to English *sofa*: the two characters were chosen for their sound, not for what they mean.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
