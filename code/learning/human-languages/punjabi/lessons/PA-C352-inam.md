## You'll want to know: ਇਨਾਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C352-BOARD352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਨਾਮ** — *inām* — "a prize; a reward".

> ਸਕੂਲ ਹਰ ਸਾਲ ਚੰਗੇ ਵਿਦਿਆਰਥੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਇਨਾਮ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *sakūl har sāl chaṅge vidiārthīāṁ nū̃ inām dindā hai.*
>
> Every year the school gives prizes to good students.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਨਾਮ** (*inām*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *inʿām*, "favour".
