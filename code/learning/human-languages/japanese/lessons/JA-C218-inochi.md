## You'll want to know: いのち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C218-HOSPITAL218-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いのち** — *inochi* — "life".

> **いのちは たいせつです。** — *inochi wa taisetsu desu* — "Life is precious."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いのち** (*inochi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
