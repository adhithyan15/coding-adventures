## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घमंडी** (*ghamaṇḍī*) is *ghamaṇḍ*, "pride", with the ending *-ī*. The origin of *ghamaṇḍ* is uncertain, and CDIAL has no entry for it.
