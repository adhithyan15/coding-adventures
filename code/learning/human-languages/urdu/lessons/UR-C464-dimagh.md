## You'll want to know: دماغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C464-ORGAN464-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دماغ** — *dimāgh* — "the brain; the mind".

> دماغ جسم کو چلاتا ہے۔
>
> *dimāgh jism ko chalātā hai.*
>
> The brain runs the body.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دماغ** (*dimāgh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dimāgh*, "brain".
