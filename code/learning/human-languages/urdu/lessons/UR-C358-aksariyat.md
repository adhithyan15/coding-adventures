## You'll want to know: اکثریت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C358-JALSA358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اکثریت** — *aksariyat* — "a majority".

> اکثریت نے اس تجویز کی حمایت کی۔
>
> *aksariyat ne is tajvīz kī himāyat kī.*
>
> The majority backed this proposal.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکثریت** (*aksariyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *aktharīya*, "the greater part", built on *akthar*, "more, most".
