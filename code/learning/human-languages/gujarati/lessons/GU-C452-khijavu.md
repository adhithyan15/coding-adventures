## You'll want to know: ખીજાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C452-KHIJ452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ખીજાવું** — *khījāvũ* — "to get cross; to lose one's temper".

> દાદા જલદી ખીજાઈ જાય છે.
>
> *dādā jaldī khījāī jāy che.*
>
> Grandfather gets cross quickly.

Past (he …): તે ખીજાઈ ગયો, *te khījāī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખીજાવું** (*khījāvũ*) is the *-āvũ* form of the verb that CDIAL lists as Gujarati *khījvũ*, "to take offence", under Sanskrit *kṣīyatē*, "is worn away, is injured" (CDIAL 3695).
