Urdu, chapter 453: Laughter and Tears.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ہنسانا (hãsānā) — to make (someone) laugh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fussiness, then the Urdu for haughtiness.

You'll want to know: ہنسانا.
ہنسانا — hãsānā — "to make (someone) laugh".
میرا بھائی سب کو ہنساتا ہے۔ merā bhāī sab ko hãsātā hai. My brother makes everyone laugh.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچوں کو ہنسایا, us ne bachchoṅ ko hãsāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ہنسانا (hãsānā) is the causative of ہنسنا, "to laugh", inherited from Sanskrit hásati, "laughs" (CDIAL 14021).

Lesson 2 of 5.
رلانا (rulānā) — to make (someone) cry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for haughtiness, then the Urdu for to make (someone) laugh.

You'll want to know: رلانا.
رلانا — rulānā — "to make (someone) cry".
اس فلم نے سب کو رلایا۔ is film ne sab ko rulāyā. This film made everyone cry.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بہن کو رلایا, us ne bahan ko rulāyā.

The word, taken apart.
رلانا (rulānā) is the causative of رونا, "to cry", inherited from Sanskrit rōdati, "howls, weeps" (CDIAL 10840).

Lesson 3 of 5.
قہقہہ (qahqahā) — a burst of laughter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to make (someone) laugh, then the Urdu for to make (someone) cry.

You'll want to know: قہقہہ.
قہقہہ — qahqahā — "a burst of laughter".
مجھے اس کا قہقہہ بہت اچھا لگتا ہے۔ mujhe us kā qahqahā bahut achchhā lagtā hai. I really like her loud laugh.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
قہقہہ (qahqahā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qahqaha, "loud laughter", a word that imitates the sound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سسکنا (sisaknā) — to sob.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to make (someone) cry, then the Urdu for a burst of laughter.

You'll want to know: سسکنا.
سسکنا — sisaknā — "to sob".
لڑکی کونے میں بیٹھ کر سسک رہی تھی۔ laṛkī kone meṅ baiṭh kar sisak rahī thī. The girl was sitting in the corner, sobbing.
Past (she …): وہ دیر تک سسکی, voh der tak siskī.

The word, taken apart.
سسکنا (sisaknā) is probably inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi siskārī, "sobbing", under śuṣati, "hisses, pants", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12545).

Lesson 5 of 5.
مذاق (mazāq) — teasing; fun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a burst of laughter, then the Urdu for to sob.

You'll want to know: مذاق.
مذاق — mazāq — "teasing; fun".
میں نے یہ بات مذاق میں کہی۔ maiṅ ne yih bāt mazāq meṅ kahī. I said it in fun.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مذاق (mazāq) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic madhāq, "taste"; the sense "fun, teasing" is the one Urdu gives it.
