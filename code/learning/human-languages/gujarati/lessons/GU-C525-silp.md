## You'll want to know: શિલ્પ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C525-SHILP525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શિલ્પ** — *śilp* — "a sculpture".

> મંદિરમાં પથ્થરનાં ઘણાં સુંદર શિલ્પ છે.
>
> *mandirmā̃ paththarnā̃ ghaṇā̃ sundar śilp che.*
>
> There are many beautiful stone sculptures in the temple.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શિલ્પ** (*śilp*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śilpa*, "an art, a craft" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is a work of sculpture.
