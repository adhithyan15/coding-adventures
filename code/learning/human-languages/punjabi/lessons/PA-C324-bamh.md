## You'll want to know: ਬਾਂਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C324-BREATH324-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਂਹ** — *bāṁh* — "an arm".

> ਭਾਰੀ ਥੈਲੇ ਤੋਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਬਾਂਹ ਦੁਖਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *bhārī thaile ton merī bāṁh dukhdī hai.*
>
> My arm aches from the heavy bag.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਂਹ** (*bāṁh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bāhu*, "an arm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bā̃h*, "arm", under it (CDIAL 9229).
