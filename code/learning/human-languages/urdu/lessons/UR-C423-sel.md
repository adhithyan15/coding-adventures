## You'll want to know: سیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C423-SAUDA423-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سیل** — *sel* — "a sale (at low prices)".

> اس ہفتے کپڑوں کی سیل ہے۔
>
> *is hafte kapṛoṅ kī sel hai.*
>
> There's a sale on clothes this week.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سیل** (*sel*) is borrowed from English *sale*.
