## You'll want to know: perdoar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C479-PERDAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**perdoar** — "to forgive".

> A minha irmã perdoou-me o atraso.
>
> *My sister forgave me for being late.*

*Perdoar alguma coisa a alguém* — to forgive someone for something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perdoar* is inherited from Old Portuguese *perdõar* "to forgive" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Late Latin *perdōnāre*, built on *dōnāre* "to give".
