## You'll want to know: رقیب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C383-TOWLID-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رقیب** (*raqib*) — "a rival, a competitor".

> رقیب ما قیمتها را پایین آورد.
>
> *raqib-e mâ qeymathâ râ pâyin âvard.*
>
> Our rival brought prices down.

Plural *raqibân*. In sport too: *raqib-e mâ*, our opponent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رقیب** comes from Arabic *raqīb* (EtymDB), a word usually explained as one who watches: a rival keeps an eye on you.
