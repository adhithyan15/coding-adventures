# ਹੋਵੇਗੀ — it will be, with a feminine subject

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186, PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਹੋਵੇਗਾ**, then say **ਯਾਤਰਾ**.

## Grammar Lens: change only the agreement ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-HOVEGI-186]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186] -->

> **ਹੋਵੇਗੀ** — *hovegii* — it will be or happen, feminine singular

The ending changes from **ਾ** to **ੀ**. The time stays future; the form now
agrees with a feminine singular subject.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGI-186, PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186, PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01] -->

- [YOU READ: **ਯਾਤਰਾ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ**]
- [YOU WRITE: copy **ਹੋਵੇਗੀ** once and check the final **ੀ**]
