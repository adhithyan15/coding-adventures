## You'll want to know: īrācundia, īrācundiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C493-IRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**īrācundia, īrācundiae** — "a hot temper, irritability".

> Īrācundia eius omnēs amīcōs terret.
>
> *His hot temper frightens all his friends.*

Feminine, first declension, from *īrācundus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**īrācundia** is built on *īrācundus* "hot-tempered", itself from *īra* "anger" (Lewis & Short).
