## You'll want to know: ぎろん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C317-SANSEI317-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎろん** — *giron* — "a discussion; an argument".

> **ぎろんが ながく なったので、つかれました。** — *giron ga nagaku natta node, tsukaremashita* — "The discussion dragged on, so I got tired."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎろん** (*giron*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gi*, "debate" (Middle Chinese *ngjeH*) and *ron*, "argument" (*lwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
