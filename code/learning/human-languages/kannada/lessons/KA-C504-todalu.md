## You'll want to know: ತೊದಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C504-MATU504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೊದಲು** — *todalu* — "a stammer; baby talk".

> ಮಗುವಿನ ತೊದಲು ಮಾತು ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಇಷ್ಟ.
>
> *maguvina todalu mātu ellarigū iṣṭa.*
>
> Everyone loves the baby's lisping talk.

ತೊದಲು ಮಾತು, baby talk. It is a verb as well: ತೊದಲುತ್ತಾ ಮಾತಾಡು, to speak stammering.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೊದಲು** (*todalu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3503 lists Kannada *todal(u)*, "faltering, stammering, speaking indistinctly".
