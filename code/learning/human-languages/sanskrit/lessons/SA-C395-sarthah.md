## You'll want to know: सार्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C395-SARTHA395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सार्थः** — *sārthaḥ* — "a caravan".

> सार्थः ग्रामात् ग्रामं गच्छति।
>
> *sārthaḥ grāmāt grāmaṃ gacchati.*
>
> The caravan goes from village to village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सार्थः** (*sārthaḥ*) is built from *sa*, "with", and *artha*, "an aim, business": MW gives *sārtha* first as having a purpose, then as a travelling company of traders or pilgrims, a caravan (MW).
