Russian, chapter 80: Hotel, Pharmacy, Church, Park, Taxi.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
гостиница (gostínitsa) — a hotel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a car, then the Russian for a bridge.

You'll want to know: гостиница.
гостиница (gostínitsa) — "a hotel". Built on гость, guest.

What you've built.
One more word for hotel, pharmacy, church, park, taxi.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gostínitsa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gostínitsa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gostínitsa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for a car, then the Russian for a bridge, then say gostínitsa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does гостиница (gostínitsa) mean? ("A hotel".) Say it once more.
