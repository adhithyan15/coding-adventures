[PAUSE 3s] A birth? (**జననం**, *jananaṁ*.) A newborn baby? (**పసికందు**, *pasikandu*.) A naming ceremony? (**నామకరణం**, *nāmakaraṇaṁ*.) To take birth? (**జన్మించు**, *janmiñcu*.) To bless? (**దీవించు**, *dīviñcu*.) A crown? (**కిరీటం**, *kirīṭaṁ*.) A throne? (**సింహాసనం**, *siṁhāsanaṁ*.) A coronation? (**పట్టాభిషేకం**, *paṭṭābhiṣēkaṁ*.) To enthrone? (**పట్టం కట్టు**, *paṭṭaṁ kaṭṭu*.) A reign? (**పాలన**, *pālana*.) A rumour? (**పుకారు**, *pukāru*.) Hearsay? (**వదంతి**, *vadanti*.) An exaggeration? (**అతిశయోక్తి**, *atiśayōkti*.) An uproar? (**కలకలం**, *kalakalaṁ*.) To disclose? (**వెల్లడించు**, *vellaḍiñcu*.) To arrest? (**అరెస్టు చేయు**, *aresṭu cēyu*.) To get caught? (**పట్టుబడు**, *paṭṭubaḍu*.) A prisoner? (**ఖైదీ**, *khaidī*.) Shackles? (**సంకెళ్ళు**, *saṅkeḷḷu*.) The accused? (**నిందితుడు**, *nindituḍu*.) Gradually? (**క్రమంగా**, *kramaṅgā*.) As time went by? (**రానురాను**, *rānurānu*.) Previously? (**మునుపు**, *munupu*.) The past? (**గతం**, *gataṁ*.) In those days? (**అప్పట్లో**, *appaṭlō*.) An army? (**సైన్యం**, *sainyaṁ*.) A soldier? (**సైనికుడు**, *sainikuḍu*.) A commander? (**సేనాధిపతి**, *sēnādhipati*.) A march? (**కవాతు**, *kavātu*.) To march against? (**దండెత్తు**, *daṇḍettu*.) Instantly? (**తక్షణం**, *takṣaṇaṁ*.) Unexpectedly? (**అనుకోకుండా**, *anukōkuṇḍā*.) Out of the blue? (**ఉన్నట్టుండి**, *unnaṭṭuṇḍi*.) In the end? (**తీరా**, *tīrā*.) To wake up? (**మేల్కొను**, *mēlkonu*.) The main points? (**ముఖ్యాంశాలు**, *mukhyāṁśālu*.) An editor? (**సంపాదకుడు**, *sampādakuḍu*.) An editorial? (**సంపాదకీయం**, *sampādakīyaṁ*.) A reader? (**పాఠకుడు**, *pāṭhakuḍu*.) To certify? (**ధ్రువీకరించు**, *dhruvīkariñcu*.)
