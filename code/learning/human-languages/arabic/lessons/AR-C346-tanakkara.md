## You'll want to know: تنكر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C346-JASUS346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تنكر** — *tanakkara* — "to disguise oneself".

> تنكر الأمير في ثياب قديمة.
>
> *tanakkara al-amīr fī thiyāb qadīma.*
>
> The prince disguised himself in old clothes.

Present: يتنكر, *yatanakkar*.

A fancy-dress party is حفلة تنكرية.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنكر** (*tanakkara*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-k-r* (Lane), "not to know a thing". It is Form V, "to make oneself unknown".
