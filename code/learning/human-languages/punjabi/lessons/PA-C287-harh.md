## You'll want to know: ਹੜ੍ਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C287-WET287-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੜ੍ਹ** — *haṛh* — "a flood".

> ਜੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਰੁਕੇਗਾ ਤਾਂ ਹੜ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ।
>
> *je mīṁh nahīṁ rukegā tāṁ haṛh āvegā.*
>
> If the rain does not stop, there will be a flood.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੜ੍ਹ** (*haṛh*) has no settled source: CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, and the Punjabi origin remains unknown in the sources used here.
