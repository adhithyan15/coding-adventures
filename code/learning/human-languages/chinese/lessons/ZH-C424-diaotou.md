# diàotóu — to turn round (in a car); to make a U-turn

## You'll want to know: diàotóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C424-ROADS424-02]; assesses=[] -->

*diàotóu* — "to turn round (in a car); to make a U-turn".

> *zhèr bù néng diàotóu wǎng qián kāi* — You can't turn round here: drive on.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diàotóu* is built from *diào* 'to turn' and 头 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dewH* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to shake'; the sense 'to turn' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
