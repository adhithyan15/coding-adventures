Telugu, chapter 89: About Yourself.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఉద్యోగం (udyōgaṁ) — a job.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a person, then the Telugu for a relative.

You'll want to know: ఉద్యోగం.
ఉద్యోగం (udyōgaṁ) — "a job".
మీ ఉద్యోగం (udyōgaṁ) ఏమిటి? — "what is your job?". It is built exactly like మీ పేరు ఏమిటి?, with the job where the name was.

What you've built.
The second question of an introduction, on the frame of the first.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: udyōgaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: udyōgaṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask mī udyōgaṁ ēmiṭi?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for a person, then the Telugu for a relative, then say udyōgaṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ఉద్యోగం (udyōgaṁ) mean? ("A job".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
భాష (bhāṣa) — a language.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a relative, then the Telugu for a job.

You'll want to know: భాష.
భాష (bhāṣa) — "a language".
మీకు ఏ భాషలు వచ్చు? — "which languages do you know?". The knower sits in the dative, as in నాకు తెలుగు వచ్చు: a language is something that comes to you.

What you've built.
A way to ask what someone speaks, in the frame you learned for Telugu itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
పెళ్ళి (peḷḷi) — a wedding, marriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a language, then the Telugu for English.

You'll want to know: పెళ్ళి.
పెళ్ళి (peḷḷi) — "a wedding", and "marriage".
మీకు పెళ్ళి (peḷḷi) అయిందా? — "are you married?", literally "has a wedding happened to you?". The person sits in the dative again, and the marriage is something that happened.

What you've built.
A question you will be asked early and often, and the word it turns on.
