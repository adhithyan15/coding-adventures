# Review — at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-ASK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-LOST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask for a ticket. (*ekṭā ṭikiṭ deben?*) Then say "My wallet has gone missing". (*āmār mānibyāg hāriye gechhe*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C271-NOUKA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-ASK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-FARE-TIME-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-LOST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-MISSED-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C270-ASK-WAY-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-SEJONNO-01, BN-LEX-C198-JINIS198-05, BN-LEX-C217-JAYGA217-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Could I have a ticket to the village, please? What's the fare?]
- [YOU SAY: When will the boat leave?]
- [YOU SAY: Excuse me, my bag has gone missing. I was sitting on the platform.]
- [YOU SAY: Our train has left. That's why we have to take the bus.]
- [YOU SAY: How do I get to the airport? How far is it?]
- [YOU WRITE: **নৌকা**, then **দূর**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C271-NOUKA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-ASK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-FARE-TIME-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-LOST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C271-MISSED-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] The polite ask? (**দেবেন?**) "Has gone missing"? (**হারিয়ে গেছে**.) "Has left"? (**ছেড়ে গেছে**.) Which sign is around the **ন** of **নৌকা**? (**ৌ**.)
