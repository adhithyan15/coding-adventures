5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸು (vistarisu) — to widen; to expand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bakery, then the Kannada for a home delivery.

You'll want to know: ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸು.
ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸು — vistarisu — "to widen; to expand".
ಪುರಸಭೆ ನಮ್ಮ ಬೀದಿಯನ್ನು ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸಿತು. purasabhe namma bīdiyannu vistarisitu. The town council widened our street.
Past (I …): ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸಿದೆ, vistariside.
Of roads, shops and towns; ವಿಸ್ತಾರ is extent or breadth.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸು (vistarisu) is built from Sanskrit vistara, "spreading, extension, expansion" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರವೇಶದ್ವಾರ.
ಪ್ರವೇಶದ್ವಾರ — pravēśadvāra — "an entrance gate".
ವಸ್ತುಸಂಗ್ರಹಾಲಯದ ಪ್ರವೇಶದ್ವಾರದಲ್ಲಿ ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. vastusaṅgrahālayada pravēśadvāradalli ṭikeṭ koḍuttāre. Tickets are given out at the entrance gate of the museum.
ಪ್ರವೇಶ, entry, plus ದ್ವಾರ, a door or gate.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರವೇಶದ್ವಾರ (pravēśadvāra) is built from two Sanskrit words: pravēśa, "an entrance" (CDIAL 8809), and dvāra, "a door" (CDIAL 6663).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕಡಿತ (kaḍita) — a power cut; a cut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to widen, then the Kannada for an entrance gate.

You'll want to know: ಕಡಿತ.
ಕಡಿತ — kaḍita — "a power cut; a cut".
ಬೇಸಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ಎರಡು ಗಂಟೆ ಕರೆಂಟ್ ಕಡಿತ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ. bēsigeyalli pratidina eraḍu gaṇṭe kareṇṭ kaḍita iruttade. In summer there is a two-hour power cut every day.
ಕರೆಂಟ್ ಕಡಿತ (kaḍita), a power cut; ಸಂಬಳ ಕಡಿತ (kaḍita), a pay cut.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಡಿತ (kaḍita) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1125 lists Kannada kaḍi, "to cut, to chop", with the noun kaḍita, "a cut, a portion cut off".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸರಬರಾಜು (sarabarāju) — supplies; a supply.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an entrance gate, then the Kannada for a power cut.

You'll want to know: ಸರಬರಾಜು.
ಸರಬರಾಜು — sarabarāju — "supplies; a supply".
ಮಂಗಳವಾರ ನಮ್ಮ ಬಡಾವಣೆಗೆ ನೀರಿನ ಸರಬರಾಜು ಇರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. maṅgaḷavāra namma baḍāvaṇege nīrina sarabarāju iruvudilla. On Tuesday there will be no water supply to our area.
ಸರಬರಾಜು (sarabarāju) ಮಾಡು, to supply.

The word, taken apart.
ಸರಬರಾಜು (sarabarāju) is usually traced to Persian sarbarāh, "a manager, one who provides", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ದೂರವಾಣಿ (dūravāṇi) — a telephone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a power cut, then the Kannada for supplies.

You'll want to know: ದೂರವಾಣಿ.
ದೂರವಾಣಿ — dūravāṇi — "a telephone".
ಕರೆಂಟ್ ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೂ ದೂರವಾಣಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. kareṇṭ illadiddarū dūravāṇi kelasa māḍuttittu. Even without power the telephone was working.
ದೂರ, far, plus ವಾಣಿ, voice: a coined word; in speech people say ಫೋನ್.

The word, taken apart.
ದೂರವಾಣಿ (dūravāṇi) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: dūra, "distant" (CDIAL 6495), and vāṇī, "voice, sound" (CDIAL 11490).
