## You'll want to know: ज़िम्मेदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C343-KARAN343-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िम्मेदार** — *zimmedār* — "responsible".

> दुर्घटना के लिए ड्राइवर ज़िम्मेदार था।
>
> *durghaṭnā ke liye ḍrāivar zimmedār thā.*
>
> The driver was responsible for the accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िम्मेदार** (*zimmedār*) is usually traced to Persian *zimmadār*, "answerable, in charge". The ending *-dār* is the Persian "holding".
