## You'll want to know: दौड़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C535-DAUR535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दौड़ाना** — *dauṛānā* — "to make run; to send running".

> हर सुबह हमारे शिक्षक हमें मैदान में दौड़ाते हैं।
>
> *Har subah hamāre śikṣak hamẽ maidān meṁ dauṛāte haiṁ.*
>
> Every morning our teacher makes us run on the field.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने घोड़ा दौड़ाया, *usne ghoṛā dauṛāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दौड़ाना** (*dauṛānā*) is the causative of *dauṛnā*, "to run", which comes from Sanskrit *dravati*, "runs" (CDIAL 6624).
