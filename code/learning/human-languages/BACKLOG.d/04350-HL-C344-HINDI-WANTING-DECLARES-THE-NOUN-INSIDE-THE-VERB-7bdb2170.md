## HL-C344 — Hindi wanting declares the noun inside the verb

After the agreeing-how repair, **चाह** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi word in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 70 already identifies
**चाह** as the noun "longing" inside **चाहना**, and its etymology hook repeats
that relationship, but the lesson neither declares nor practises the noun.

Declare **चाह** beside **चाहना / चाहता**, explicitly rebuild the verb from the
noun, practise the pair, and recall the noun's meaning. This turns the existing
word-family explanation into reusable vocabulary without introducing the
separate **चाहिए** construction or increasing the gentle-ramp debt.
