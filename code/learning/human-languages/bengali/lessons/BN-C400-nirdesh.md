## You'll want to know: নির্দেশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C400-THIKANA400-04]; assesses=[] -->

**নির্দেশ** — *nirdesh* — "an instruction; directions".

> পুলিশের নির্দেশে আমরা গাড়ি থামালাম।
>
> *pulisher nirdeshe āmrā gāṛi thāmālām.*
>
> On the police's instruction we stopped the car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নির্দেশ** (*nirdesh*) is Sanskrit *nirdeśa*, "pointing out, directing, an order" (Monier-Williams), from *diś*, to point.
