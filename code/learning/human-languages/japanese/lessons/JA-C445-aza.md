## You'll want to know: あざ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C445-ACHE445-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あざ** — *aza* — "a bruise".

> **ひざに あおい あざが あります。** — *hiza ni aoi aza ga arimasu* — "There is a blue bruise on my knee."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あざ** (*aza*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
