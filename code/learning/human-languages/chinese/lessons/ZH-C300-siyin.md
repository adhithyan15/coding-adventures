## You'll want to know: 死因
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C300-DEATH300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**死因** — *sǐyīn* — "cause of death".

> **他的死因是什么** *tā de sǐyīn shì shénme* — What did he die of?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**死因** (*sǐyīn*) is built from 死 'to die' and 因 'a cause', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sijX* and *'jin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to rely on' and 'a reason'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
