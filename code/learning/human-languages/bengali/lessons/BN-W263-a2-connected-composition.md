# A connected message about your familiar day

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C17-AR-01, BN-LEX-C18-KINTU-01, BN-LEX-C18-KENONA-01, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-03, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, BN-LEX-C91-TIME91-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A connected message is not a pile of vocabulary and not one long
sentence. It is a short route for one reader. Before writing, draw eight empty
boxes and give them these jobs:

1. reader and greeting;
2. reason for writing;
3. where or when your day begins;
4. two familiar actions;
5. what happens next and a second time anchor;
6. one preference and one reason;
7. one direct question about tomorrow;
8. a close.

Write only one or two English cue words in each box. Do not draft Bengali yet.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C01-NOMOSHKAR-01, BN-LEX-C17-AR-01, BN-LEX-C18-KINTU-01, BN-LEX-C18-KENONA-01, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-03, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, BN-LEX-C91-TIME91-01, BN-LEX-C07-JAOWA-01, BN-LEX-C07-KHAOWA-01, BN-LEX-C08-PORA-01, BN-CONCEPT-C05-KAJKORA-01, BN-LEX-C09-BHALOLAGA-01, BN-LEX-C26-KOTHAY-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

There is no model, Bengali sentence bank, or copyable answer on this page.

**Reader:** your friend Rina. **Purpose:** help Rina understand your familiar day
and choose a good time to meet tomorrow.

Write **70–90 words in Bengali script**. Use every planning box. Include at
least four familiar actions, two time anchors, one sequence link, one
preference, one reason, and one direct question for Rina. Make every detail
help with tomorrow's meeting. The instructions give content, not sentences:
choose only words and forms this book has taught, and connect them yourself.

This first connected attempt is untimed. Put away romanization, dictionaries,
grammar references, translators, generative assistants, and spell-checkers.
Do not copy from an earlier lesson. Ordinary legible Bengali handwriting is
enough.

When the whole message exists, count its words once. If it is short, add one
useful taught detail to a planning box. If it is long, remove a detail that
does not help Rina. Do not replace the message with a memorised model.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C17-AR-01, BN-LEX-C18-KINTU-01, BN-LEX-C18-KENONA-01, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-03, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, BN-LEX-C91-TIME91-01, BN-LEX-C26-KOTHAY-01] -->

Review the same attempt in four separate passes:

1. **Task fulfilment and relevant content** — Rina, the familiar day,
   tomorrow's meeting, and every requested content point are present.
2. **Comprehensibility and organisation** — the eight jobs form a route rather
   than a list of unrelated words or lines.
3. **Bengali vocabulary and grammar** — every form and connector comes from
   the taught standard-colloquial Bengali track; a simpler familiar sentence
   is better than an invented structure.
4. **Bengali-script control** — letter shapes, vowel signs, conjuncts, word
   boundaries, and punctuation remain readable.

[PAUSE 2s] Mark one strength and one next step. Repair only the next step; keep
the original attempt so the next 70–90-word message can show real growth.

This is one untimed curriculum practice attempt, not the complete 30-minute A2
writing paper and not evidence that either timed mock has been passed. Closing
the machine-measured connected-composition stage does not prove A2 attainment
or readiness, learner performance, independent review, standard setting, mock
passage, or human validation.
