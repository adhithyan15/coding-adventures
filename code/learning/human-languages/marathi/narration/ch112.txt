Marathi, chapter 112: Far, Inside, Outside, Behind, Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दूर (dūr) — far.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for after, then the Marathi for time.

You'll want to know: दूर.
दूर — dūr — "far".

The word, taken apart.
दूर (dūr) is Sanskrit dūra, "distant", which has lost only its final vowel (CDIAL 6495).

Lesson 2 of 5.
आत (āt) — inside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for time, then the Marathi for far.

You'll want to know: आत.
आत — āt — "inside".

The word, taken apart.
आत (āt) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from antar, "inside" (CDIAL 356).

Lesson 3 of 5.
बाहेर (bāher) — outside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for far, then the Marathi for inside.

You'll want to know: बाहेर.
बाहेर — bāher — "outside".

The word, taken apart.
बाहेर (bāher) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bāhira, "external", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9226). CDIAL builds it on Sanskrit bahis, "outside".
