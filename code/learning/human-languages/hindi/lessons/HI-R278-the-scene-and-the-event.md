# Review — the scene and the event

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C278-PICHLA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was reading a book". (*maiṁ kitāb paṛh rahā thā*.) Then "last week" and "last time". (*pichle hafte*, *pichlī bār*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C278-SCENE-EVENT-01, HI-LEX-C278-PICHLA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-02, HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Last week I went to the village.]
- [YOU SAY: What were you doing yesterday evening? I was cooking.]
- [YOU SAY: When we were drinking tea, Arun came.]
- [YOU SAY: It was raining, and suddenly the power went.]
- [YOU SAY: Last year Mira read many books.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C278-SCENE-EVENT-01, HI-LEX-C278-PICHLA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which past is the scene? (**रहा था**: **बारिश हो रही थी**.) Which is the event? (The plain past: **मीरा आई**.) "Last year she read many books"? (**पिछले साल उसने बहुत किताबें पढ़ीं**: **ने**, and **पढ़ीं** for the books.)
