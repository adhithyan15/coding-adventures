## You'll want to know: der Pinsel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C518-MALEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Pinsel** — "paintbrush".

> Für die kleinen Details nimmt sie einen dünnen Pinsel.
>
> *For the small details she uses a thin brush.*

Plural: *die Pinsel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pinsel* comes from Middle High German *bensel*, *pensel*, from Old French *pincel*, from Latin *pēniculus* "little tail, brush" (EtymDB).
