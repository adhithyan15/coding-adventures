## You'll want to know: भटकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C310-BICHHUD310-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भटकना** — *bhaṭaknā* — "to wander; to lose one's way".

> बच्चा भटककर नदी के किनारे पहुँचा।
>
> *baccā bhaṭakkar nadī ke kināre pahũcā.*
>
> The child wandered off and ended up on the riverbank.

Past (he …): वह रास्ता भटक गया, *vah rāstā bhaṭak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भटकना** (*bhaṭaknā*) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit *bhraṣṭa*, "fallen, ruined" (CDIAL 9655). It is wandering without knowing the way: **रास्ता भटकना**, to lose one's way.
