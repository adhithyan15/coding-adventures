## You'll want to know: ازدحام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C431-MURUR431-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ازدحام** — *izdiḥām* — "congestion; a traffic jam".

> تأخرنا بسبب الازدحام في الشارع.
>
> *taʾakhkharnā bi-sabab al-izdiḥām fī ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> We were late because of the congestion in the street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ازدحام** (*izdiḥām*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-ḥ-m*, "to crowd, to press" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form VIII, beside *muzdaḥim*, "crowded".
