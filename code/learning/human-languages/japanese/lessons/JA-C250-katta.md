# かった / のんだ / かいた (katta / nonda / kaita) — the past by the last kana

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C250-PAST-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C250-PAST-02, JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I ate" and "I did", to a friend. (*tabeta*, *shita*.) Then say "I bought shoes", politely. (*kutsu o kaimashita*.) Most verbs do not drop a **る**: their **-た** depends on their last kana.

## You'll want to know: った and んだ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C250-TA-SOUND-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C27-VERBS27-04, JA-LEX-C138-KAERIMASU, JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-05, JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-01, JA-LEX-C58-PLACE58-05] -->

**かう** (*kau*), "to buy", ends in **う**. Its plain past is **かった** (*katta*): a small **っ**, a short catch, then **た**.

> **くつを かった。**
>
> *kutsu o katta.* — "I bought shoes."

**つ** and **る** go the same way. **まつ** (*matsu*), "to wait", has **まった** (*matta*). **かえります** (*kaerimasu*) is the polite form of **かえる** (*kaeru*), "to go home", and its past is **かえった** (*kaetta*). (A different かえる, "to change", drops its る like **みる**.)

> **えきで まった。**
>
> *eki de matta.* — "I waited at the station."

> **はやく うちに かえった。**
>
> *hayaku uchi ni kaetta.* — "I went home early."

**む** and **ぶ**, and the rare verbs in **ぬ**, end in **んだ**. **のむ** (*nomu*), "to drink", has **のんだ** (*nonda*); **あそぶ** (*asobu*), "to play", has **あそんだ** (*asonda*).

> **おちゃを のんだ。**
>
> *ocha o nonda.* — "I drank tea."

> **こうえんで あそんだ。**
>
> *kōen de asonda.* — "I played in the park."

## You'll want to know: いた, いだ, した, and いった
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C250-TA-SOUND-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C27-VERBS27-02, JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-03, JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-02, JA-LEX-C27-VERBS27-03, JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-03] -->

A verb in **く** ends in **いた**: **かく** (*kaku*), "to write", has **かいた** (*kaita*); **きく** (*kiku*), "to listen", has **きいた** (*kiita*). One in **ぐ** ends in **いだ**: **およぐ** (*oyogu*), "to swim", has **およいだ** (*oyoida*).

> **てがみを かいた。**
>
> *tegami o kaita.* — "I wrote a letter."

> **うみで およいだ。**
>
> *umi de oyoida.* — "I swam in the sea."

A verb in **す** ends in **した**: **はなす** (*hanasu*), "to speak", has **はなした** (*hanashita*).

> **ともだちと はなした。**
>
> *tomodachi to hanashita.* — "I talked with a friend."

And one verb in **く** goes its own way: **いく** (*iku*), "to go", has **いった** (*itta*), like **かった**.

> **きのう やまに いった。**
>
> *kinō yama ni itta.* — "I went to the mountains yesterday."
