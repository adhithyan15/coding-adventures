French, chapter 244: Silent, Clean, Hard, Smooth, Rough.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
silencieux / silencieuse — silent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a medical appointment, then the French for a diet.

You'll want to know: silencieux / silencieuse.
silencieux / silencieuse — "silent".

The word, taken apart.
silencieux — built from the word for "silence", from Latin silentium (Lewis & Short), with -eux, "full of".
