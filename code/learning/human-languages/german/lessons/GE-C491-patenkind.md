## You'll want to know: das Patenkind
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C491-PATEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Patenkind** — "godchild".

> Zum Geburtstag schenke ich meinem Patenkind ein Buch.
>
> *I'm giving my godchild a book for their birthday.*

Plural: *die Patenkinder*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Patenkind* is built on *Pate* "godparent", from Middle High German *pate* (EtymDB), and *Kind* "child".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
