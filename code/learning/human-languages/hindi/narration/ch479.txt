Hindi, chapter 479: The Mouth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मुँह (mũh) — the mouth; the face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a rib, then the Hindi for a kidney.

You'll want to know: मुँह.
मुँह — mũh — "the mouth; the face".
खाने से पहले मुँह और हाथ धो लो। khāne se pahle mũh aur hāth dho lo. Wash your face and hands before eating.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मुँह (mũh) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi mũh, "mouth, face", under Sanskrit múkha, "mouth, face" (CDIAL 10158); the kh wore down to h and left a nasal vowel.
