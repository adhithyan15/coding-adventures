## You'll want to know: il bouquet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C311-SPOSARSI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il bouquet** — "the bouquet".

> Alla fine la sposa lancia il bouquet.
>
> *At the end the bride throws the bouquet.*

From French; said *bu-KEH*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bouquet* was borrowed from French *bouquet* "a little wood, a bunch"; EtymDB traces the French word to Old French *bochet*, a little wood, and has no Italian entry.
