# ਫ · ਗ · ਿ — three pieces met inside a word you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-MA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-RA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-LAVA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-KA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-NANNA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BIHARI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DULAVA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TAINKA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TTHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDA-01, PA-LEX-THIK-03, PA-PHRASE-I-AM-FINE-03, PA-PHRASE-NO-PROBLEM-03, PA-ETYMON-NEGATIVE-NE-03] -->

- [YOU SAY: *ṭhīk* · *koī gall nahīṁ*]

## Script you'll notice: ਫ · ਗ · ਿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SIHARI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ਫ

**phapphā** — *pha*. A consonant with a puff of breath after it — *p* plus an audible *h*, not the *f* of English *phone*.

> ਗ

**gaggā** — *ga*. A consonant, the voiced *g* of *go*, with no breath after it.

> ਿ

**sihārī** — *-i*. A mātrā for short *i*, and the one that surprises everybody: it is **written before** the consonant it is **spoken after**.

Now put them where you have already heard them:

> **ਫ** + **ਿ** + **ਰ** → **ਫਿਰ** (*phir*)

> **ਫਿਰ** — *phir* — **again, then**

You are not being asked to write any of this. You are being asked to
**recognise** it the next time it goes past.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SIHARI-01] -->

Keep ਫ · ਗ · ਿ in front of you and do not cover any of them. Follow each shape once with a
finger, then copy it once with a pencil, larger than it is printed. Say its
sound as your hand stops.

> This is **observe-and-trace with the model visible**. Nothing in these
> chapters asks you to write a Punjabi word from memory; that ladder starts
> later, and it starts from these same shapes.
