## You'll want to know: せんぱい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C230-BOSS230-03]; assesses=[] -->

**せんぱい** — *senpai* — "a senior; an older schoolmate or colleague".

> **せんぱいは よく しゅくだいを てつだいます。** — *senpai wa yoku shukudai o tetsudaimasu* — "My senior often helps with my homework."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんぱい** (*senpai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "before" (Middle Chinese *sen*) and *pai*, "fellows" (*pwojH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
