## You'll want to know: половник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C330-SMESHIVAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**половник** (*polóvnik*) — "a ladle".

> Бабушка налила суп половником.
>
> *Bábushka nalilá sup polóvnikom.*
>
> Grandma ladled out the soup.

Masculine; genitive *половника*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *половник* is not settled; EtymDB lists no parent.
