Arabic, chapter 96: Belt, Six, Seven, Eight, Nine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حزام (ḥizām) — a belt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a shoe, then the Arabic for a coat.

You'll want to know: حزام.
حزام — ḥizām — "a belt".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ḥizām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḥizām, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ḥizām]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a shoe, then the Arabic for a coat, then say ḥizām again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does حزام (ḥizām) mean? ("A belt".) Say it once more.
