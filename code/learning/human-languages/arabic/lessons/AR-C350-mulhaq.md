## You'll want to know: ملحق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C350-NASHIR350-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ملحق** — *mulḥaq* — "a supplement; an annex".

> نشرت الصحيفة ملحقا عن الرياضة.
>
> *nasharat aṣ-ṣaḥīfa mulḥaqan ʿan ar-riyāḍa.*
>
> The newspaper published a sports supplement.

At an embassy, a ملحق is an attaché.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملحق** (*mulḥaq*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-ḥ-q* (Lane), "to overtake, to join". *Mulḥaq* is the Form IV passive participle, "a thing joined on".
