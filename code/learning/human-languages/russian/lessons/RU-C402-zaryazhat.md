## You'll want to know: заряжать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C402-SVYAZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**заряжать** (*zaryazhát*) — "to charge (a battery)".

> Я заряжаю телефон каждую ночь.
>
> *Ya zaryazháyu telefón kázhduyu noch.*
>
> I charge my phone every night.

Imperfective; perfective *зарядить*. *Зарядка* is a charger, and also morning exercises.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**заряжать** is built from *за-* and the root of *ряд* "a row, an order", as in *снаряжать* "to equip": first to load a gun, now to charge a battery.
