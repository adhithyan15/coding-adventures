## You'll want to know: खोदणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C238-SOW238-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खोदणो** — *khodṇo* — "to dig".

> हूं खेत खोदूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ khet khodū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I dig the field.

Past (I …): म्हैं खेत खोद्यो, *mhaĩ khet khodyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खोदणो** (*khodṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khōdd*, "dig"; CDIAL lists Marwari *khodṇo*, "to dig" (CDIAL 3934).
