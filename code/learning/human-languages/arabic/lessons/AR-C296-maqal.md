## You'll want to know: مقال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C296-SAHAFA296-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مقال** — *maqāl* — "an article, an essay".

> نشرت الصحيفة مقالا عن البحر.
>
> *nasharat aṣ-ṣaḥīfa maqālan ʿan al-baḥr.*
>
> The newspaper published an article about the sea.

Plural مقالات. مقالة is the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقال** (*maqāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-w-l* (Lane), "to say", the root of *qāla*. *Maqāl* is first "a saying"; in the modern press it is a written piece.
