Tamil, chapter 167: To continue, To operate, To stick, To hang, To roll.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தொடர் (toṭar) — to continue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to make peace, then the Tamil for to consider.

You'll want to know: தொடர்.
தொடர் — toṭar — "to continue".

The word, taken apart.
தொடர் (toṭar) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3480 lists Malayalam toṭuka, "to touch". The entry lists toṭar, "to follow, to be connected", under toṭu, "to touch".
