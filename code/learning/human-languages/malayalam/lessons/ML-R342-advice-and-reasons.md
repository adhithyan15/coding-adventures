# Review — advice and reasons

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C342-AUDYOGIKAMAYA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C342-AU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "official". (*audyōgikamāya*.) Write **ഔ** once from memory.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ANAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ENDATANU-01, ML-LEX-C342-AUDYOGIKAMAYA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C342-AU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ATUKONDU-CLAUSE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-PLAN-REASON-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C341-SO-RESULT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C341-THINK-THAT-01, ML-SCRIPT-C341-AI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: "I must go tomorrow", then "I want to go tomorrow"]
- [YOU SAY: "You ought to sleep early. You must not be late."]
- [YOU SAY: "This is an official document."]
- [YOU SAY: "Because it rained, I did not come."]
- [YOU SAY: next year's plan, with its reason after *kāraṇaṁ*]
- [YOU SAY: "I think you must come. So I am calling."]
- [YOU WRITE: **ഔദ്യോഗിക ഭാഷ**, then **ഐക്യം**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ANAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ENDATANU-01, ML-LEX-C342-AUDYOGIKAMAYA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C342-AU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ATUKONDU-CLAUSE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-PLAN-REASON-01] -->
