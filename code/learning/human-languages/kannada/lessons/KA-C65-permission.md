## You'll want to know: ಅನುಮತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C65-MANNERS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅನುಮತಿ** (*anumati*) — "permission".

ಅನುಮತಿ is Sanskrit for a mind that goes along with you — agreement given rather than merely not refused.

It belongs with ಹೊರಡು from the leave-taking chapter. In a Kannada house you do not simply set out; you ask ಅನುಮತಿ of whoever is host, and only then do you say ಹೋಗಿ ಬರುತ್ತೇನೆ. The farewell you learned early has this word standing in front of it.
