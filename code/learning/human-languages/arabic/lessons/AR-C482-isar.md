## You'll want to know: إعصار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C482-RIH482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**إعصار** — *iʿṣār* — "a hurricane; a whirlwind".

> وصل إعصار قوي إلى الساحل.
>
> *waṣala iʿṣār qawī ilā as-sāḥil.*
>
> A strong hurricane reached the coast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إعصار** (*iʿṣār*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṣ-r*, "to press, to wring" (Lane). Lane gives *iʿṣār* as a whirlwind that raises the dust.
