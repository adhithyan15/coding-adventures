Persian, chapter 366: Discussion and debate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بحث (bahs) — a discussion, a debate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to exaggerate, then the Persian for to justify.

You'll want to know: بحث.
بحث (bahs) — "a discussion, a debate".
بعد از جلسه یک بحث طولانی داشتیم. ba'd az jalase yek bahs-e tulâni dâshtim. After the meeting we had a long discussion.
Plural bahshâ, or formal mabâhes. In Persian, ث sounds like s.

The word, taken apart.
بحث (bahs) was borrowed from Arabic baḥth "search, examination, discussion" (EtymDB): a debate is a search for the answer.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بحث کردن (bahs kardan) — to discuss, to argue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to justify, then the Persian for a discussion.

You'll want to know: بحث کردن.
بحث کردن (bahs kardan) — "to discuss, to argue".
آنها درباره پول بحث میکنند. ânhâ darbâre-ye pul bahs mikonand. They are arguing about money.
Bahs kardan darbâre-ye … — to argue about …; with someone: bâ kasi.

The word, taken apart.
بحث کردن (bahs kardan) joins bahs, from Arabic baḥth "discussion" (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
مذاکره (mozâkere) — a negotiation, talks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a discussion, then the Persian for to discuss.

You'll want to know: مذاکره.
مذاکره (mozâkere) — "a negotiation, talks".
مذاکره دو کشور سه روز طول کشید. mozâkere-ye do keshvar se ruz tul keshid. The talks between the two countries lasted three days.
Formal talks are often plural: mozâkerât. Mozâkere kardan — to negotiate.

The word, taken apart.
مذاکره (mozâkere) comes from Arabic mudhākara (EtymDB), a mutual verbal noun usually explained as reminding one another; in Persian ذ sounds like z.

اعتراض او فایده نداشت. e'terâz-e u fâyede nadâsht. His objection was no use.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مثال زدن (mesâl zadan) — to give an example.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a negotiation, then the Persian for an objection.

You'll want to know: مثال زدن.
مثال زدن (mesâl zadan) — "to give an example".
معلم برای هر کلمه یک مثال زد. mo'allem barâye har kalame yek mesâl zad. The teacher gave an example for each word.
Mesâl "example" + zadan "to strike" (present stem zan-). Masalan "for example" is the adverb.

The word, taken apart.
مثال is usually traced to Arabic mithāl "example, likeness"; zadan "to strike" is the inherited Persian verb that also builds harf zadan "to talk".
