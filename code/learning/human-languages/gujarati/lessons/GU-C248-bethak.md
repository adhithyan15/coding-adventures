## You'll want to know: બેઠક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C248-OFFICE248-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બેઠક** — *beṭhak* — "meeting; sitting room".

> **આજે બપોરે બેઠક છે.** — *āje bapore beṭhak che* — "There is a meeting this afternoon."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બેઠક** (*beṭhak*), a sitting together, is built on *beṭh-*, a stem of the taught *besvũ*, "to sit", with *-ak*. CDIAL derives *besvũ* and *beṭho* from Sanskrit *úpaviśati*, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245).
