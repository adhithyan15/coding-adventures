German, chapter 387: Auf dem Konto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Kontostand — the bank balance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to be liable, then the German for liability.

You'll want to know: der Kontostand.
der Kontostand — "the bank balance".
Jeden Morgen schaut sie auf ihren Kontostand. Every morning she checks her bank balance.
Plural: die Kontostände.

The word, taken apart.
Kontostand joins Konto "account", borrowed from Italian conto "reckoning" (EtymDB), to Stand "state, standing", from the family of stehen "to stand": where your account stands.

Lesson 3 of 5.
überziehen — to overdraw (an account); to overrun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the bank balance, then the German for the credit.

You'll want to know: überziehen.
überziehen — "to overdraw (an account); to overrun".
Er hat sein Konto um zweihundert Euro überzogen. He has overdrawn his account by two hundred euros.
In this sense inseparable: er überzieht, er hat überzogen. Separable when you pull something on over: Er zieht eine Jacke über.

The word, taken apart.
überziehen is built from über "over" and ziehen "to pull, to draw", inherited from Old High German ziohan and Proto-Germanic teuhaną (EtymDB): you draw out more than is there.

Lesson 4 of 5.
abbuchen — to debit, to take (from an account).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the credit, then the German for to overdraw.

You'll want to know: abbuchen.
abbuchen — "to debit, to take (from an account)".
Die Miete wird jeden Monat automatisch abgebucht. The rent is taken from the account automatically every month.
Separable: die Bank hat das Geld abgebucht. Die Abbuchung — the debit.

The word, taken apart.
abbuchen is built from ab "off" and buchen "to enter in a book", made from Buch "book", Old High German buoh and Proto-Germanic bōks (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Betrag — the amount, the sum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to overdraw, then the German for to debit.

You'll want to know: der Betrag.
der Betrag — "the amount, the sum".
Der Betrag ist bis Ende des Monats fällig. The amount is due by the end of the month.
Plural: die Beträge. Der Gesamtbetrag — the total.

The word, taken apart.
Betrag is the noun of betragen "to amount to", built with be- on tragen "to carry", Proto-Germanic draganą (EtymDB): what a bill carries.
