## You'll want to know: प्राचीरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C323-STUPA323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राचीरम्** — *prācīram* — "a rampart; an enclosing wall".

> दुर्गस्य प्राचीरं जीर्णम् अस्ति।
>
> *durgasya prācīraṃ jīrṇam asti.*
>
> The fort's rampart is crumbling.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राचीरम्** (*prācīram*) is built on *pra-cīra*, by MW's account; MW gives *prācīra* as an enclosure, hedge, fence, wall (MW).
