# హాయిగా (hāyigā) — comfortably, pleasantly

## You'll want to know: హాయిగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C93-FINE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**హాయిగా** (*hāyigā*) — "comfortably, pleasantly".

**హాయిగా ఉంది** — "it is pleasant", said of a cool breeze, a good rest, an easy day.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A word for when things are good.
