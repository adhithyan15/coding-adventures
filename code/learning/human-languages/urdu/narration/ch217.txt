Urdu, chapter 217: Beard, Moustache, Good health, Toothache, Itch.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
داڑھی (dāṛhī) — a beard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a dream, then the Urdu for weakness.

You'll want to know: داڑھی.
داڑھی — dāṛhī — "a beard".

The word, taken apart.
داڑھی (dāṛhī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dāṃṣṭra, "fang, tusk, beard", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6250). It is built on Sanskrit daṃṣṭrā, "tusk, fang"; CDIAL finds a change of sense from "fang" through "tusk" to "beard" possible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: کھجلی.
کھجلی — khujlī — "an itch".

The word, taken apart.
کھجلی (khujlī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kharju, "itching, scab" (CDIAL 3827). CDIAL takes the word from Dravidian. The Sanskrit word is known only from the old word-lists.
