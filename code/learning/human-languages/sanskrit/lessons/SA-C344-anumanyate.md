## You'll want to know: अनुमन्यते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C344-PAKSHA344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमन्यते** — *anumanyate* — "consents; approves".

> पिता अनुमन्यते, अतः अहं गमिष्यामि।
>
> *pitā anumanyate, ataḥ ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi.*
>
> Father consents, so I will go.

Past (he …): सः अनुमतवान्, *saḥ anumatavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमन्यते** (*anumanyate*) is built on *anu*, "after, along", and the root √man, "to think"; MW gives *anu-mata* "approved, assented to, permitted" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
