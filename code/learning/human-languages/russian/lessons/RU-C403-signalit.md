## You'll want to know: сигналить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C403-RUL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сигналить** (*signálit*) — "to sound the horn, to honk; to signal".

> Возле дома водитель такси долго сигналил.
>
> *Vózle dóma vodítel taksí dólgo signálil.*
>
> Outside the house the taxi driver kept sounding his horn.

Imperfective; perfective *просигналить*. Built on *сигнал* — a signal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сигналить* is built on *сигнал* 'a signal'. *Сигнал* is usually traced to French *signal*, which EtymDB derives from Latin *signālis*, from *signum* 'a sign'.
