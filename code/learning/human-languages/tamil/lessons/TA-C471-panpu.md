## You'll want to know: பண்பு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C471-KUNAM471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**பண்பு** — *paṇpu* — "a virtue; good character".

> உண்மை பேசுவது ஒரு நல்ல பண்பு.
>
> *uṇmai pēcuvatu oru nalla paṇpu.*
>
> Speaking the truth is a good quality.

பண்பான, well-mannered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பண்பு** (*paṇpu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3904), a word for quality, nature and good character.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
