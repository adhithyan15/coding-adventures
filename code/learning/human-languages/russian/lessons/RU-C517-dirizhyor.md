## You'll want to know: дирижёр
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C517-ORCHESTRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**дирижёр** (*dirizhyór*) — "a conductor (of an orchestra)".

> Дирижёр поднял палочку, и в зале стало тихо.
>
> *Dirizhyór pódnyal pálochku, i v zále stálo tíkho.*
>
> The conductor raised his baton and the hall went quiet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дирижёр* is usually explained as built on French *diriger* 'to direct'; EtymDB has no entry.
