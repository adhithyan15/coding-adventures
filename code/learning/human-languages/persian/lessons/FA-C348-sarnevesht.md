## You'll want to know: سرنوشت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C348-PARI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سرنوشت** (*sarnevesht*) — "fate, destiny".

> سرنوشت آنها را دوباره به هم رساند.
>
> *Sarnevesht ânhâ râ dobâre be ham resând.*
>
> Fate brought them together again.

Literally "what is written on the head": *sar* "head" + *nevesht* "writing".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarnevesht* joins *sar* "head" and *nevesht*, the past stem of *neveshtan* "to write"; EtymDB glosses it "destiny, fate". Both parts are native Persian.
