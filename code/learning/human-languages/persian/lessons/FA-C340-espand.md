## You'll want to know: اسپند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C340-EYDI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اسپند** (*espand*) — "wild rue (seeds burned for luck)".

> مادربزرگم برای ما اسپند دود کرد.
>
> *Mâdarbozorgam barâ-ye mâ espand dud kard.*
>
> My grandmother burned wild rue for us.

The seeds are burned on hot coals against the evil eye, at weddings and at the New Year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*espand* "wild rue" is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian *spand*; EtymDB lists no entry.
