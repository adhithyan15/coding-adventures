Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 140: Mouse, Fly, Mosquito, Ant, Scorpion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उंदरो (undro) — a mouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a koel, then the Marwadi for a kite (the bird).

You'll want to know: उंदरो.
उंदरो — undro — "a mouse".

The word, taken apart.
उंदरो (undro) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from undura, "rat, mouse". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2095); the match is through Gujarati ũdar and Hindi ũdur. CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word itself from an Austroasiatic language.

The word, taken apart.
मच्छर (machchhar) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from matsara, "mosquito, fly".
