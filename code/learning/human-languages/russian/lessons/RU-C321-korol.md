## You'll want to know: король
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C321-KOROL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**король** (*koról*) — "a king".

> Король жил в большом замке.
>
> *Koról zhil v bolshóm zámke.*
>
> The king lived in a big castle.

Genitive *короля*. A Russian ruler was a *царь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*король* is inherited through Old East Slavic *korolĭ* from Proto-Slavic *korľĭ* "king" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to the name of a great Frankish emperor.
