Portuguese, chapter 92: Motorbike, Ship, Boat, Bus, Train.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
a mota — a motorbike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for holidays, then the Portuguese for a bicycle.

You'll want to know: a mota.
a mota — "a motorbike". In Brazil, a moto.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: a mota]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a mota, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say a mota]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for holidays, then the Portuguese for a bicycle, then say a mota again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does a mota mean? ("A motorbike".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Can, want, why and places — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a bus and a train.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: to be able to, to have to, a need, a trade, to take]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to want, I would like, to prefer, to wish, a wish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a reason, a reason, so, that is why, a cause]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an airport, a stop, the post office, a cinema, a town hall]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a supermarket, a petrol station, a swimming pool, a stadium, a factory]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To be able to? (poder.) To have to? (dever.) A need? (a necessidade.) A trade? (o ofício.) To take? (levar.) To want? (querer.) I would like? (gostaria.) To prefer? (preferir.) To wish? (desejar.) A wish? (o desejo.) A reason? (a razão.) A reason? (o motivo.) So? (então.) That is why? (por isso.) A cause? (a causa.) An airport? (o aeroporto.) A stop? (a paragem.) The post office? (os correios.) A cinema? (o cinema.) A town hall? (a câmara municipal.) A supermarket? (o supermercado.) A petrol station? (a bomba de gasolina.) A swimming pool? (a piscina.) A stadium? (o estádio.) A factory? (a fábrica.)
