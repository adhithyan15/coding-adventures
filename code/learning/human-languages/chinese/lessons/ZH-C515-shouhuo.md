## You'll want to know: shōuhuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C515-SEASONS515-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shōuhuò* — "to bring in the harvest; to reap".

> *qiūtiān shì shōuhuò de jìjié* — Autumn is the season for bringing in the harvest.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shōuhuò* is built from *shōu* 'to gather in' and *huò* 'to reap', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuw* and *hwak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to collect' and the second, written with the grain piece, 'to cut down the crop'. The falling tone of huò is not the regular outcome of *hwak*, which would give huó.
