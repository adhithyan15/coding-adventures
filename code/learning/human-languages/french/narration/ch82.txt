French, chapter 82: Sea, Stone, Sand, Beach, Island.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la mer — the sea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a mountain, then the French for a river.

You'll want to know: la mer.
la mer — "the sea".
