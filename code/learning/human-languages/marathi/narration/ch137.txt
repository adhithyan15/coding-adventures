Marathi, chapter 137: Flattened rice, Ladoo, Cucumber, Garlic, Ginger.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पोहे (pohe) — flattened rice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a vegetable dish, then the Marathi for plain dal.

You'll want to know: पोहे.
पोहे — pohe — "flattened rice".

The word, taken apart.
पोहे (pohe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pṛthuka, "flattened rice or grain" (CDIAL 8359). It is built on pṛthu, "broad, flat".

लाडू — lāḍū — "a ladoo, a sweet ball".

The word, taken apart.
लाडू (lāḍū) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit laḍḍu, "a kind of sweetmeat" (CDIAL 10926). CDIAL takes it as probably the same word as laḍḍu, "lump", and notes Kuiper's link with Munda words for "lump".
