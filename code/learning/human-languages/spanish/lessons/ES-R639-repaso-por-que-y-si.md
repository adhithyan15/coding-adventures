# Why, and what if — back to the first reasons

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-EXPLICAR-07, ES-LEX-DEBER-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Two verbs from much earlier in the book: say the Spanish for "to explain" and for "must, should".

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-EXPLICAR-07, ES-GRAMMAR-REASON-CHAIN-09, ES-LEX-DEBER-05, ES-GRAMMAR-POR-THROUGH-BECAUSE, ES-GRAMMAR-IMPERFECT-SUBJUNCTIVE-STRONG, ES-LEX-DEPENDE, ES-SEMANTIC-CONDITION-UNSTATED] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *Te lo explico.* — I'll explain it to you.]
- [YOU SAY: a chain of reasons: *Creo que va a llover, así que debo volver a casa.*]
- [YOU SAY: the cause, pointed at with *por*: *No salimos por la lluvia.* — We didn't go out because of the rain.]
- [YOU SAY: a world that is not real: *Si tuviera tiempo, te lo explicaría.* Then the same with *hiciera*: *Si hiciera buen tiempo, saldríamos.*]
- [YOU SAY: an answer that keeps its condition hidden: *¿Vienes mañana? — Depende.* Then name it: *Depende del tiempo.*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-EXPLICAR-07, ES-GRAMMAR-REASON-CHAIN-09, ES-LEX-DEBER-05, ES-GRAMMAR-POR-THROUGH-BECAUSE, ES-GRAMMAR-IMPERFECT-SUBJUNCTIVE-STRONG, ES-LEX-DEPENDE, ES-SEMANTIC-CONDITION-UNSTATED] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which word points back at a cause, *por* or *para*? (**Por**.) Which form says "if I had": *tenía* or *tuviera*? (**Tuviera**.) What does *depende* leave unsaid? (The condition; add *de* to name it.) Say one reason chain of your own with *creo que*, *porque* and *así que*.
