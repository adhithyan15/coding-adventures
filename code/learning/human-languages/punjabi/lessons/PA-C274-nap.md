## You'll want to know: ਨਾਪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C274-SIZE274-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਾਪ** — *nāp* — "a size; a measurement".

> ਦਰਜ਼ੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਪ ਲੈਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *darzī merā nāp laindā hai.*
>
> The tailor takes my measurements.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਾਪ** (*nāp*) goes with *nāpṇā*, "to measure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nāpṇā* under Sanskrit *jñāpyate*, "is made known" (CDIAL 5283).
