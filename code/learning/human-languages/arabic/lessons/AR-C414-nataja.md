## You'll want to know: نتج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C414-NATIJA414-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نتج** — *nataja* — "to stem from; to result".

> نتج الحريق عن خطأ صغير.
>
> *nataja al-ḥarīq ʿan khaṭaʾ ṣaghīr.*
>
> The fire resulted from a small mistake.

Present: ينتج, *yantij*.

It takes عن: نتج عن …. The noun نتيجة is a result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نتج** (*nataja*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-t-j*, "to bring forth young" (Lane). *Nataja* is a Form I verb: to come forth from a thing, to result.
