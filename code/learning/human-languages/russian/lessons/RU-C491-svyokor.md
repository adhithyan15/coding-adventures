## You'll want to know: свёкор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C491-INLAWS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**свёкор** (*svyókor*) — "a husband's father, a father-in-law".

> Свёкор помог нам с ремонтом.
>
> *Svyókor pomóg nam s remóntom.*
>
> My father-in-law helped us with the renovation.

Masculine; genitive свёкра (the vowel drops). The husband's father.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*свёкор* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *svekrъ* through Old East Slavic *svekrъ*, which EtymDB traces to PIE *swéḱuros* 'father-in-law'; *свекровь* goes back to the related PIE *sweḱrúh₂* (EtymDB).
