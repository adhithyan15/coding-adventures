## Grammar Lens: the length after the verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-DURATION-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C47-DATE47-01, ZH-LEX-C88-LIANG-01, ZH-ORTHO-HANYU-01, ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-01, ZH-LEX-C78-AGE78-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-COMPLETION-01, ZH-LEX-C35-WORK35-05] -->

> **How long something lasted goes after the verb and its 了; with an object, the object comes after the length.**

> **我学了两年汉语** *wǒ xué le liǎng nián Hànyǔ* — I studied Chinese for two years.
> *tā zài Zhōngguó zhù le yì nián* — He lived in China for a year.

When it happened goes before the verb: 上个月. How long it lasted goes after: 一个月.

To ask, put 多 before 长, as you put it before 大 in 多大: **你学了多长时间** *nǐ xué le duō cháng shíjiān*?
