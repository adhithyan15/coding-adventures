Punjabi, chapter 106: Somewhere, Ever, Each, Such, Every.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਿਤੇ (kite) — somewhere.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for Magh, then the Punjabi for Phaggan.

You'll want to know: ਕਿਤੇ.
ਕਿਤੇ — kite — "somewhere".

The word, taken apart.
Where ਕਿਤੇ (kite) comes from is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it. It has the question k- of kitthe, "where".

The word, taken apart.
Where ਕਦੇ (kade) comes from is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it. It lists Hindi kabhī, "ever", under Sanskrit evam eva, "exactly so", crossed with the question stem ka- (CDIAL 2528).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਹਰੇਕ (harek) — each.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for somewhere, then the Punjabi for ever.

You'll want to know: ਹਰੇਕ.
ਹਰੇਕ — harek — "each".

The word, taken apart.
ਹਰੇਕ (harek) joins words from two sources: har, "every", usually traced to Persian har, and ek, "one", from Sanskrit eka (CDIAL 2462).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for ever, then the Punjabi for each.

You'll want to know: ਅਜਿਹਾ.
ਅਜਿਹਾ — ajihā — "such".

The word, taken apart.
Where ਅਜਿਹਾ (ajihā) comes from is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it, though it lists the close Punjabi ihā and ehā, "such, like this", under Sanskrit īdṛśa, "such, like this" (CDIAL 1611).
