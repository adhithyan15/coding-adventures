## You'll want to know: educar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C597-APRENDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**educar** — "to educate; to bring up".

> Es difícil educar a los hijos.
>
> *Bringing up children is difficult.*

*Educar a los hijos* — to bring up your children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*educar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *educare* 'to bring up, to rear' (EtymDB).
