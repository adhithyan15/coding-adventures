Marathi, chapter 167: To breathe, To give up, To exchange, To slip, To suspect.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
श्वास घेणे (śvās gheṇe) — to breathe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to carry, then the Marathi for to hit; to kill.

You'll want to know: श्वास घेणे.
श्वास घेणे — śvās gheṇe — "to breathe".

The word, taken apart.
श्वास is Sanskrit śvāsa, "breath, breathing", a tatsama (CDIAL 12769). घेणे comes from Sanskrit gṛbhāyati, "takes, seizes" (CDIAL 4236).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सोडून देणे (soḍūn deṇe) — to give up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to hit; to kill, then the Marathi for to breathe.

You'll want to know: सोडून देणे.
सोडून देणे — soḍūn deṇe — "to give up".

The word, taken apart.
सोडून is "having let go", from सोडणे, which comes from Sanskrit kṣoṭayati, "throws" (CDIAL 3747). देणे comes from Sanskrit dadāti, "gives" (CDIAL 6141).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अदलाबदल करणे (adlābadal karṇe) — to exchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to breathe, then the Marathi for to give up.

You'll want to know: अदलाबदल करणे.
अदलाबदल करणे — adlābadal karṇe — "to exchange".

The word, taken apart.
अदलाबदल is a rhyming pair built on badal, "exchange", a loan from Arabic badal, "a substitute", through Persian; its root is b-d-l (Lane). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
