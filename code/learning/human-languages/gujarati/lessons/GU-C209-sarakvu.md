## You'll want to know: સરકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C209-BREAKDOWN209-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સરકવું** — *sarakvũ* — "to slide, to slip".

> **ભીના રસ્તા પર સાઈકલ સરકી.** — *bhīnā rastā par saikal sarakī* — "The bicycle slid on the wet road."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સરકવું** (*sarakvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to move, slip, slide", under Sanskrit *sárati*, "runs, flows, glides", extended with *-kk-* (CDIAL 13250).
