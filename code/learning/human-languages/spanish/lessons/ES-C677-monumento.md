## You'll want to know: el monumento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C677-MONUMENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el monumento** — "the monument, the sight".

> Visitamos el monumento más famoso.
>
> *We visited the most famous monument.*

*Monumentos* are the sights of a town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*monumento* is a learned borrowing from Latin *monumentum* "that which keeps a memory, a memorial" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *monēre* "to remind".
