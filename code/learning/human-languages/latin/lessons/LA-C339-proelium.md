## You'll want to know: proelium, proeliī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C339-PROELIUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**proelium, proeliī** — "a battle".

> Proelium ab hōrā prīmā ad vesperum dūrābat.
>
> *The battle lasted from the first hour until evening.*

Neuter. A single battle; *bellum* (taught) is the whole war. *Proelium committere*: to join battle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**proelium** is a word whose origin Lewis & Short call doubtful; they suggest, with hesitation, a link with *duellum*, the old form of *bellum*.
