## You'll want to know: ঠান্ডা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C426-THANDA426-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠান্ডা** — *ṭhānḍā* — "cold; chilly; cold weather".

> ঠান্ডা জলে স্নান করে অরুণ কাঁপছিল।
>
> *ṭhānḍā jôle snān kore ôrun kā̃pchhilô.*
>
> After bathing in cold water Arun was shivering.

Also a cold you catch: ঠান্ডা লাগা. শীত is the cold season and the cold you feel; ঠান্ডা is cold things too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠান্ডা** (*ṭhānḍā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ṭhāṇḍā*, "cold", under Sanskrit *stabdha*, "stiffened, numb" (CDIAL 13676): cold is what makes you stiff.
