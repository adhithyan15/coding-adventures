Gujarati, chapter 245: Homework and spelling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગૃહકાર્ય (gṛhkārya) — homework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to explain, then the Gujarati for to solve.

You'll want to know: ગૃહકાર્ય.
ગૃહકાર્ય — gṛhkārya — "homework".
હું સાંજે ગૃહકાર્ય કરું છું. — hũ sā̃je gṛhkārya karũ chũ — "I do my homework in the evening."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગૃહકાર્ય (gṛhkārya) is a modern learned compound built from two tatsamas: Sanskrit gṛha, "house" (CDIAL 4240), and kārya, "what is to be done, business" (CDIAL 3078).

Lesson 2 of 5.
જોડણી (joḍṇī) — spelling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to solve, then the Gujarati for homework.

You'll want to know: જોડણી.
જોડણી — joḍṇī — "spelling".
આ શબ્દની જોડણી અઘરી છે. — ā śabdanī joḍṇī agharī che — "The spelling of this word is hard."

The word, taken apart.
જોડણી (joḍṇī), the joining of letters, is built on the inherited verb joḍvũ, "to join", with the suffix -aṇī. CDIAL lists joḍvũ under yōṭayati, "joins", a reconstructed form in its entry yuṭati, "is joined", and calls the history of the group obscure (CDIAL 10496).

You'll want to know: ભૂંસવું.
ભૂંસવું — bhū̃svũ — "to rub out, to erase".
તે ખોટો શબ્દ ભૂંસે છે. — te khoṭo śabda bhū̃se che — "He rubs out the wrong word."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ભૂંસવું (bhū̃svũ) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this form and meaning, and our other sources do not trace it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઉચ્ચારવું (uccārvũ) — to pronounce, to utter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rub out, then the Gujarati for eraser.

You'll want to know: ઉચ્ચારવું.
ઉચ્ચારવું — uccārvũ — "to pronounce, to utter".
તે દરેક શબ્દ ધીમે ઉચ્ચારે છે. — te darek śabda dhīme uccāre che — "She pronounces every word slowly."

The word, taken apart.
ઉચ્ચારવું (uccārvũ) comes from Sanskrit uccārayati, "utters", the causative of úccarati. CDIAL lists the inherited Gujarati ucārvũ, "to pronounce, speak"; the written cc follows the Sanskrit, a semi-tatsama (CDIAL 1641).
