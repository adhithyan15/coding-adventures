## You'll want to know: ছিদ্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C507-PHATOL507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ছিদ্র** — *chhidrô* — "a hole; a small opening".

> নৌকার তলায় একটা ছিদ্র ছিল।
>
> *Noukār tôlāy ekṭā chhidrô chhilô.*
>
> There was a hole in the bottom of the boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছিদ্র** (*chhidrô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *chidra*, "torn asunder", and so "a hole, an opening" (Monier-Williams).
