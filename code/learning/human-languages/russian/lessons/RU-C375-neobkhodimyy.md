## You'll want to know: необходимый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C375-NEOBKHODIMO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**необходимый** (*neobkhodímyy*) — "necessary, essential".

> У нас есть всё необходимое.
>
> *U nas yest vsyo neobkhodímoye.*
>
> We have everything we need.

*Необходимо* + infinitive — it is necessary to. More formal than *нужный*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**необходимый** is built from *не-* "not" and *обходить* "to go round", from *ходить* "to go", Proto-Slavic *\*xoditi*: what cannot be got round.
