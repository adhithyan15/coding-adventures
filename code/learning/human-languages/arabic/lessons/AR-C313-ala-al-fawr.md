# على الفور (ʿalā al-fawr) — on the spot, at once

## You'll want to know: على الفور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C313-FAWR313-01]; assesses=[] -->

**على الفور** — *ʿalā al-fawr* — "on the spot, at once".

> اتصلت بالطبيب على الفور.
>
> *ittaṣaltu bi-ṭ-ṭabīb ʿalā al-fawr.*
>
> I called the doctor at once.

The taught فورا is the same word as an adverb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**على الفور** (*ʿalā al-fawr*) is "on the boiling": *fawr* is built on the native Arabic root *f-w-r*, "to boil, to gush" (Lane), the root of the taught *fawran*. What is done at the boil is done at once.
