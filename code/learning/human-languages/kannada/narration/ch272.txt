Kannada, chapter 272: To spill, To pour, To mix, To stir, To roast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚೆಲ್ಲು (cellu) — to spill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an organisation, then the Kannada for a government department.

You'll want to know: ಚೆಲ್ಲು.
ಚೆಲ್ಲು — cellu — "to spill".

The word, taken apart.
ಚೆಲ್ಲು (cellu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil callu and Telugu callu (DEDR 2384). DEDR lists callu, cellu, "to scatter, pour out, spill".
