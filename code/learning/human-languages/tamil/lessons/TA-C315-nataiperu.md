# நடைபெறு (naṭaipeṟu) — to take place

## You'll want to know: நடைபெறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C315-NADAIPERU315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**நடைபெறு** — *naṭaipeṟu* — "to take place".

> விழா நேற்று பள்ளியில் நடைபெற்றது.
>
> *viḻā nēṟṟu paḷḷiyil naṭaipeṟṟatu.*
>
> The ceremony took place at the school yesterday.

Past (it …): நடைபெற்றது, *naṭaipeṟṟatu*.

A formal way to say நட, to happen, for planned events.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நடைபெறு** (*naṭaipeṟu*) is a Tamil compound built from நடை, "walk, course, conduct" (DEDR 3582), and பெறு, "to get, obtain" (DEDR 4422): to get its course.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
