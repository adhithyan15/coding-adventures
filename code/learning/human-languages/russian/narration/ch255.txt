Lesson 1 of 5.
фамилия (famíliya) — a surname.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for parents, then the Russian for a nephew.

You'll want to know: фамилия.
фамилия (famíliya) — "a surname".

The word, taken apart.
фамилия (famíliya) — first "a family", from the early 1700s; it came through Polish familia or German Familie from Latin familia, "a household" (Vasmer).

Lesson 3 of 5.
номер (nómer) — a number; a hotel room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: номер.
номер (nómer) — "a number; a hotel room".

The word, taken apart.
номер (nómer) — нумер in 1704; the form with -о- comes from German Nummer, from Latin numerus, "a number" (Vasmer). A hotel room is a numbered room.

Lesson 4 of 5.
сообщение (soobshchéniye) — a message.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an age, then the Russian for a number; a hotel room.

You'll want to know: сообщение.
сообщение (soobshchéniye) — "a message".

The word, taken apart.
сообщение (soobshchéniye) comes from сообщить, "to share, to report", from общий, "common", Proto-Slavic obĭtjĭ, "what is around" (Vasmer): to make something common. общий itself is the Church Slavonic form, with щ; the native Old Russian form was обчий (Vasmer).
