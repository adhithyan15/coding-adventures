## You'll want to know: मुकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C549-SANRAKSHAN549-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मुकणे** — *mukṇe* — "to miss out on".

> आजारपणामुळे तो अंतिम सामन्याला मुकला.
>
> *ājārpaṇāmuḷe to antim sāmnyālā muklā.*
>
> Because of illness he missed the final match.

Past (he …): तो संधीला मुकला, *to sandhīlā muklā*.

What is missed takes -ला: संधीला मुकणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुकणे** (*mukṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *mukṇẽ*, "to lose", under *\*mukna*, "loosed" (CDIAL 10157).

## What you've built
