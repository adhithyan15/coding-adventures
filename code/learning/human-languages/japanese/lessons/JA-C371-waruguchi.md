## You'll want to know: わるぐち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C371-JOUDAN371-03]; assesses=[] -->

**わるぐち** — *waruguchi* — "speaking ill of someone; abuse".

> **わるぐちを 言ったら、ともだちが おこるでしょう。** — *waruguchi o ittara, tomodachi ga okoru deshō* — "If you speak ill of people, your friends will be angry."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わるぐち** (*waruguchi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *waru*, "bad", the stem of *warui*, and *guchi*, "mouth", the native *kuchi* voiced, and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
