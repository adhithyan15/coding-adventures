## You'll want to know: पात्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C340-UPANYAS340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पात्र** — *pātra* — "a character (in a story)".

> इस कहानी में सिर्फ़ तीन पात्र हैं।
>
> *is kahānī meṁ sirf tīn pātra haiṁ.*
>
> There are only three characters in this story.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पात्र** (*pātra*) is Sanskrit *pātra* (Monier-Williams), first a vessel and then a worthy person and an actor in a play; in Hindi it is any character in a story.
