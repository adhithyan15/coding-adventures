# ഇടയ്ക്കിടെ (iṭaykkiṭe) — now and then

## You'll want to know: ഇടയ്ക്കിടെ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C359-VAIKATHE359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടയ്ക്കിടെ** — *iṭaykkiṭe* — "now and then".

> അവൻ ഇടയ്ക്കിടെ മുത്തശ്ശിയെ കാണാൻ പോകും.
>
> *avan iṭaykkiṭe muttaśśiye kāṇān pōkuṁ.*
>
> He goes to see grandmother now and then.

ഇട, an interval, said twice: at one interval after another.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇടയ്ക്കിടെ** (*iṭaykkiṭe*) is native Dravidian, built by repeating ഇട (*iṭa*), "an interval, a space between" (DEDR 448), the word inside ഇടയിൽ, "between".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
