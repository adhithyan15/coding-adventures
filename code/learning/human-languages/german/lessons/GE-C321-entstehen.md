## You'll want to know: entstehen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C321-ERFINDEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**entstehen** — "to come into being, to arise".

> Die Stadt entstand im Mittelalter.
>
> *The town came into being in the Middle Ages.*

Strong, with *sein*: *entstand*, *ist entstanden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entstehen* is built on *stehen* "to stand", from Proto-Germanic *stāną* (EtymDB), with *ent-*: to come to stand.
