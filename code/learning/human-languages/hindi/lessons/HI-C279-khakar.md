# खाना खाकर (khānā khākar) — one action, then the next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-KAR-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You met **-कर** with **देखकर** and **करके**: **मैं किताब देखकर आता हूँ**, "I'll look at the book and come". Say it once. Then "she came". (*vah āī*.)

[PAUSE 1s] This lesson takes **-कर** into the past, where stories are told.

## You'll want to know: -कर in a story
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C279-KAR-SEQUENCE-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C160-ACT160-04, HI-CONCEPT-C13-HAATH-01, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-03, HI-LEX-C160-ACT160-05, HI-CONCEPT-C28-SUBAH-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01] -->

> **मैंने हाथ धोकर खाना खाया।**
>
> *maiṁne hāth dhokar khānā khāyā.* — "I washed my hands and ate."

One doer, two actions, in the order they happened. The first drops its **-ना** and takes **-कर**; only the last verb carries the past. **करना** still gives **करके**.

The last verb decides **ने**. If it takes a thing, the doer takes **ने**: **मैंने हाथ धोकर खाना खाया**. If it does not, there is no **ने**, though something was eaten on the way: **मैं खाना खाकर सो गया** (*maiṁ khānā khākar so gayā*), "I ate and fell asleep".

- **वह सुबह उठकर नहाया।** (*vah subah uṭhkar nahāyā.*) — He got up in the morning and bathed.
- **मीरा ने घर आकर चाय पी।** (*Mīrā ne ghar ākar cāy pī.*) — Mira came home and drank tea.

## You'll want to know: after doing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C279-NE-KE-BAAD-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-06, HI-LEX-C93-REL-07, HI-LEX-C73-EATING-02, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-04] -->

> **नाश्ते के बाद हम बाज़ार गए।**
>
> *nāśte ke bād ham bāzār gae.* — "After breakfast we went to the market."

**के बाद**, "after", follows a noun in its oblique form: **नाश्ते के बाद**. It follows a verb too, as **के लिए** does in **करने के लिए**: the **-ना** becomes **-ने**.

- **खाना खाने के बाद** (*khānā khāne ke bād*) — after eating
- **काम करने के बाद मैं घर आया।** (*kām karne ke bād maiṁ ghar āyā.*) — After work I came home.

**-कर** needs one doer for both actions. When the doers differ, tell it in two sentences, or with **जब … तब …**.
