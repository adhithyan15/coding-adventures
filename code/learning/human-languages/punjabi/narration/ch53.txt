Punjabi, chapter 53: Sad, Father, Mother, Son, Daughter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉਦਾਸ (udās) — sad.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for hunger, then the Punjabi for happy.
