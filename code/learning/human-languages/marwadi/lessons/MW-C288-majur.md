## You'll want to know: मजूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C288-KAM288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मजूर** — *majūr* — "a labourer".

> मजूर खेत मांय मेहनत करै है।
>
> *majūr khet mā̃y mehnat karai hai.*
>
> The labourer works hard in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मजूर** (*majūr*) is usually traced to Persian *mazdūr*, "a labourer".
