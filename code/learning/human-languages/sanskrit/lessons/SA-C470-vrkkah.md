## You'll want to know: वृक्कः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C470-PHUPPHUSA470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वृक्कः** — *vṛkkaḥ* — "a kidney".

> मम मित्रस्य वृक्कः रुग्णः अस्ति।
>
> *mama mitrasya vṛkkaḥ rugṇaḥ asti.*
>
> My friend's kidney is diseased.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृक्कः** (*vṛkkaḥ*) MW gives "the kidneys" in the dual, from the Atharvaveda on; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
