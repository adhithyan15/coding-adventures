Telugu, chapter 333: At the Police Station.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ఠాణా (ṭhāṇā) — a police station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a moment, then the Telugu for to yell.

You'll want to know: ఠాణా.
ఠాణా — ṭhāṇā — "a police station".
వాళ్ళు దొంగతనం గురించి ఠాణాలో చెప్పారు. vāḷḷu doṅgatanaṁ guriñci ṭhāṇālō ceppāru. They reported the theft at the police station.
An older word for the పోలీసు స్టేషన్; the news still uses it.

The word, taken apart.
ఠాణా (ṭhāṇā) is a loan from Hindustani thānā, "a post, a station", which goes back to Sanskrit sthāna, "a place" (CDIAL 13753).

Lesson 2 of 6.
కేసు (kēsu) — a case (legal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to yell, then the Telugu for a police station.

You'll want to know: కేసు.
కేసు — kēsu — "a case (legal)".
పోలీసులు దొంగ మీద కేసు పెట్టారు. pōlīsulu doṅga mīda kēsu peṭṭāru. The police filed a case against the thief.
Plural: కేసులు, kēsulu.
కేసు (kēsu) పెట్టు, to file a case: literally to put a case on someone.

The word, taken apart.
కేసు (kēsu) is a loan from English case, with the final -ఉ that many loans take, as in బస్సు.

Lesson 4 of 6.
జాడ (jāḍa) — a trace; a sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a case, then the Telugu for an investigation.

You'll want to know: జాడ.
జాడ — jāḍa — "a trace; a sign".
దొంగ జాడ ఎక్కడా దొరకలేదు. doṅga jāḍa ekkaḍā dorakalēdu. No trace of the thief was found anywhere.
జాడ (jāḍa) తెలియదు, the trace is not known: nobody knows where someone went.

The word, taken apart.
జాడ (jāḍa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2442 lists Telugu jāḍa, "a sign, trace, track", with Tamil cāṭai, "a hint, a feature".

Lesson 5 of 6.
వేలిముద్ర (vēlimudra) — a fingerprint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an investigation, then the Telugu for a trace.

You'll want to know: వేలిముద్ర.
వేలిముద్ర — vēlimudra — "a fingerprint".
తలుపు మీద దొంగ వేలిముద్రలు ఉన్నాయి. talupu mīda doṅga vēlimudralu unnāyi. The thief's fingerprints are on the door.
Plural: వేలిముద్రలు, vēlimudralu.
వేలు, a finger (వేలి- before another word), with ముద్ర, a seal or a print.

The word, taken apart.
వేలిముద్ర (vēlimudra) is a Telugu compound: native vēlu, "a finger" (DEDR 5409), and Sanskrit mudrā, "a seal, a signet" (CDIAL 10203).
