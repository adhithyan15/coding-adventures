## You'll want to know: द्वेष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C462-HEVA462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वेष** — *dveṣ* — "hatred".

> मनात द्वेष ठेवू नये.
>
> *manāt dveṣ ṭhevū naye.*
>
> One should not keep hatred in one's heart.

Masculine.

द्वेष करणे, to hate, with the genitive: त्याचा द्वेष करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वेष** (*dveṣ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *dveṣa*, "hatred" (CDIAL 6696).
