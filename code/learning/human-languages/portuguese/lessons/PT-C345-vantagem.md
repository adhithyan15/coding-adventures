## You'll want to know: a vantagem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C345-VANTAGEM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a vantagem** — "the advantage".

> A grande vantagem desta casa é o jardim.
>
> *The great advantage of this house is the garden.*

*Tirar vantagem de* — to take advantage of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vantagem* is usually traced to Old French *avantage*, built on *avant* "before", which EtymDB derives from Late Latin *abante* "before, in front", from *ab* and *ante* "before" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short); Portuguese dropped the first *a-*.
