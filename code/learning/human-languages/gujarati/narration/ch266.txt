Gujarati, chapter 266: Fear, tears and shyness.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
લાગણી (lāgṇī) — feeling, emotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for daughter-in-law, then the Gujarati for trust.

You'll want to know: લાગણી.
લાગણી — lāgṇī — "feeling, emotion".
આ લાગણી સારી નથી. — ā lāgṇī sārī nathī — "This feeling is not good."

The word, taken apart.
લાગણી (lāgṇī), what touches you, is built on the inherited lāgvũ, "to come into contact with", with -aṇī. CDIAL derives lāgvũ from Sanskrit lagyati, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895).

સુખ (sukh) is Sanskrit sukhá, "pleasant; comfort, happiness", taken over as a tatsama (CDIAL 13451). The same entry lists the inherited sahelũ, "easy".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for feeling, then the Gujarati for contentment.

હું અંધારાથી નથી ડરતો. — hũ andhārāthī nathī ḍarto — "I am not afraid of the dark."

The word, taken apart.
ડરવું (ḍarvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit darati, "is frightened"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to fear" (CDIAL 6190).

શરમાવું (śarmāvũ) — to feel shy, to be embarrassed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be afraid, then the Gujarati for to cry.

You'll want to know: શરમાવું.
શરમાવું — śarmāvũ — "to feel shy, to be embarrassed".
નાની છોકરી શરમાતી નથી. — nānī chokrī śarmātī nathī — "The little girl is not shy."

The word, taken apart.
શરમાવું (śarmāvũ) is built on śaram, "shame, shyness", with -ā-. Śaram is a loan usually traced to Persian sharm, "shame"; CDIAL has no entry for it.
