## You'll want to know: tīngzhòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C292-RADIO292-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tīngzhòng* — "listeners; the people listening to the radio".

> *rúguǒ yǒu tīngzhòng dǎ diànhuà, zhǔchírén jiù huì huídá* — If a listener calls in, the host will answer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tīngzhòng* is built from *tīng* 'to listen' and *zhòng* 'a crowd', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *theng* and *tsyuwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to listen' and the second 'many'.
