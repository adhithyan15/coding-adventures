## You'll want to know: костыль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C484-PLASTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**костыль** (*kostýl*) — "a crutch".

> После операции бабушка ходила на костылях.
>
> *Pósle operátsii bábushka khodíla na kostylyákh.*
>
> After the operation Grandma walked on crutches.

Masculine; genitive костыля. *Ходить на костылях* — to walk on crutches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*костыль* is derived from *кость* 'bone' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *kostь*.
