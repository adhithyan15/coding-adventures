## You'll want to know: zuìchū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C295-AGAIN295-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zuìchū* — "at the very start; initially".

> *zuìchū wǒ bù xǐhuan zhèr* — At first I didn't like it here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuìchū* is built from 最 'most' and *chū* 'the start', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tswajH* and *tsrhjo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the beginning': the very start.
