Japanese, chapter 372: In Front of the Station.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
えきまえ (ekimae) — the area in front of a station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for serious, then the Japanese for selfish.

You'll want to know: えきまえ.
えきまえ — ekimae — "the area in front of a station".
えきまえに ゆうびんきょくが あります。 — ekimae ni yūbinkyoku ga arimasu — "There is a post office in front of the station."

The word, taken apart.
えきまえ (ekimae) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese eki, "station" (Middle Chinese yek), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) mae, "front", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 5.
まちあわせる (machiawaseru) — to meet up (at an agreed place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for in front of a station, then the Japanese for a kiosk.

You'll want to know: まちあわせる.
まちあわせる — machiawaseru — "to meet up (at an agreed place)".
ともだちと えきまえで まちあわせました。 — tomodachi to ekimae de machiawasemashita — "I met up with my friend in front of the station."
Polite まちあわせます, machiawasemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): ちちと ははが えきで まちあわせました。, chichi to haha ga eki de machiawasemashita.

The word, taken apart.
まちあわせる (machiawaseru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from machi, "waiting" (from matsu) and awaseru, "to bring together"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

まちあいしつ — machiaishitsu — "a waiting room".
まちあいしつで でんしゃを まちました。 — machiaishitsu de densha o machimashita — "I waited for the train in the waiting room."

The word, taken apart.
まちあいしつ (machiaishitsu) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the native Japanese (wago) machi, "waiting" (from matsu), inherited rather than borrowed; the native Japanese (wago) ai, "meeting" (from au), inherited rather than borrowed; and the Sino-Japanese shitsu, "room" (Middle Chinese syit), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

The word, taken apart.
じどうはんばいき (jidōhanbaiki) is Sino-Japanese (kango): five Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese.
