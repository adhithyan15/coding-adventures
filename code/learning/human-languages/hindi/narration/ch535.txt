Hindi, chapter 535: Training Hard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दौर (daur) — a round, a phase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a winner, then the Hindi for a prize, an award.

You'll want to know: दौर.
दौर — daur — "a round, a phase".
पहले दौर में हमारी टीम जीती। Pahle daur meṁ hamārī ṭīm jītī. Our team won in the first round.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
दौर (daur) is usually traced to Arabic dawr, "a turn, a round", through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
फुर्ती (phurtī) — quickness; agility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a prize, an award, then the Hindi for a round, a phase.

You'll want to know: फुर्ती.
फुर्ती — phurtī — "quickness; agility".
खिलाड़ी ने बड़ी फुर्ती से गेंद पकड़ी। Khilāṛī ne baṛī phurtī se gẽd pakṛī. The player caught the ball with great quickness.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
फुर्ती (phurtī) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit sphurati, "darts, flashes" (CDIAL 13849). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "activity"; quickness is how Hindi uses it now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a round, a phase, then the Hindi for quickness.

You'll want to know: फाँदना.
फाँदना — phā̃dnā — "to leap over; to vault".
घोड़ा ऊँची दीवार फाँद गया। Ghoṛā ū̃cī dīvār phā̃d gayā. The horse leapt over the high wall.
Past (he …): वह नाली फाँद गया, vah nālī phā̃d gayā.

The word, taken apart.
फाँदना (phā̃dnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit spandatē, "quivers, throbs" (CDIAL 13806). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to leap".

Lesson 5 of 5.
दौड़ाना (dauṛānā) — to make run; to send running.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to leap over, then the Hindi for a medal.

You'll want to know: दौड़ाना.
दौड़ाना — dauṛānā — "to make run; to send running".
हर सुबह हमारे शिक्षक हमें मैदान में दौड़ाते हैं। Har subah hamāre śikṣak hamẽ maidān meṁ dauṛāte haiṁ. Every morning our teacher makes us run on the field.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने घोड़ा दौड़ाया, usne ghoṛā dauṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
दौड़ाना (dauṛānā) is the causative of dauṛnā, "to run", which comes from Sanskrit dravati, "runs" (CDIAL 6624).
