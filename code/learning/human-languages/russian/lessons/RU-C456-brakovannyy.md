## You'll want to know: бракованный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C456-OBMENNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**бракованный** (*brakóvannyy*) — "faulty, defective (of goods)".

> Мы вернули бракованный чайник в магазин.
>
> *My vernúli brakóvannyy cháynik v magazín.*
>
> We returned the faulty kettle to the shop.

Built on *браковать* — to reject as faulty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бракованный* is built on *браковать* 'to reject as faulty', from *брак* 'a defect'. That *брак* is usually traced to Low German *brak* 'damaged'; it is a different word from *брак* 'a marriage'. EtymDB lists *браковать* without a source.
