# HL105 — Punjabi A2 `ਸਭ` prerequisite

Issue: #18286

After PR #18271 merged, an aggregate-only audit of the preserved confidential
Punjabi A2 mock drafts against exact merged main
`f0869280230044dbbc88f4e3323f2275e9eeac11` finds **ਸਭ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs three times in the confidential learner material
and zero times in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential body
nor the remaining candidate set belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 205 closes that one-word gap through one deliberately narrow meaning
and frame: **ਸਭ** means “all” here, and **ਸਭ ਠੀਕ ਹੈ** means “everything is
fine.” The voice-first opener retrieves only the already-owned **ਠੀਕ ਹੈ**. One
lesson directly owns exact **ਸਭ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound,
meaning, and script, a bounded frame lesson places it before familiar **ਠੀਕ
ਹੈ**, and a four-skill payoff retrieves the complete frame again. The target
has one direct owner and three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored,
generated-book, or narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-LEX-SABH-205`. The direct owner keeps **ਸਭ** whole and
teaches only the beginner meaning “all / everyone / everything” needed for the
bounded frame. It does not claim every syntactic use, teach a broad indefinite-
pronoun or numeral survey, introduce distributive nuance, reopen number or case
paradigms, or add alternate forms.

Punjabi University Patiala's lexical statistics lists **ਸਭ** as an indefinite
pronoun with the meanings “all / every / whole”:
<https://www.learnpunjabi.org/statistics.html>. Its pronoun grammar independently
lists **ਸਭ** among plural indefinite pronouns and glosses it “all”:
<https://www.learnpunjabi.org/Pronoun.html>. Its number introduction also lists
**ਸਭ** as “all” among forms that can serve an indefinite numeral role:
<https://www.learnpunjabi.org/numbers_intro.html>.

Those sources support the bounded modern meaning but do not establish the
word's deeper origin. The course therefore records that the Punjabi origin
remains unknown in the sources used here, makes no historical derivation claim,
and keeps the form whole.

This is the twenty-eighth prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
