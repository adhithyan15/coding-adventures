## You'll want to know: 点头
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C228-ACHE228-05]; assesses=[] -->

**点头** — *diǎntóu* — "to nod".

> **我问他头疼不疼他点头了** *wǒ wèn tā tóu téng bu téng tā diǎntóu le* — I asked if his head hurt, and he nodded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**点头** (*diǎntóu*) is built from 点 'to dot' and 头 'head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *temX* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'dots and strokes'; 'to dip' is not given there.
