## You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C322-HURT322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ** — *zakham* — "a wound".

> ਚਾਕੂ ਨਾਲ ਮੇਰੀ ਉਂਗਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਹੋਇਆ।
>
> *chākū nāl merī uṅgal utte zakham hoiā.*
>
> I got a cut on my finger from the knife.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ** (*zakham*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *zakhm*, "a wound".
