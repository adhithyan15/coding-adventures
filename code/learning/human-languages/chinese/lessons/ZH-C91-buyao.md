## You'll want to know: 不要
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C91-BUYAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**不要** — *bú yào* — "don't; do not want".

> **我不要** *wǒ bú yào* — I don't want it.

> **不要说** *bú yào shuō* — Don't say it.

不 before the fall of 要 rises: *bú yào*, as in *bú shì*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**不要** (*bú yào*) is built from 不 'not' and 要 'to want', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjut* and *'jiewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-TONE-SANDHI-BU] -->

Two jobs. 我不要: I don't want it. 不要 + a verb: don't do it. The second is how Mandarin tells somebody not to.
