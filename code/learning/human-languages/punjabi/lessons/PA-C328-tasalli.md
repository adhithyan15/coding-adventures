## You'll want to know: ਤਸੱਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C328-CARE328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਸੱਲੀ** — *tasallī* — "reassurance".

> ਡਾਕਟਰ ਦੇ ਜਵਾਬ ਤੋਂ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤਸੱਲੀ ਹੋਈ।
>
> *ḍākṭar de javāb ton mainū̃ tasallī hoī.*
>
> The doctor's answer reassured me.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਸੱਲੀ** (*tasallī*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *tasallī*, "consolation".
