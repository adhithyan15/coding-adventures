## You'll want to know: табло
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C414-PERRON-03]; assesses=[] -->

**табло** (*tabló*) — "a departures board, a display board".

> На табло уже есть наш поезд.
>
> *Na tabló uzhé yest nash póyezd.*
>
> Our train is already on the board.

Neuter and indeclinable: *на табло*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*табло* was borrowed from French *tableau* 'a board, a picture' (EtymDB).
