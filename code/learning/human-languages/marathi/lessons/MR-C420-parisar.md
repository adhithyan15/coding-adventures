## You'll want to know: परिसर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C420-DUTAVAS420-05]; assesses=[] -->

**परिसर** — *parisar* — "the surroundings; an area".

> मंदिराचा परिसर खूप हिरवा आहे.
>
> *mandirācā parisar khūp hirvā āhe.*
>
> The area around the temple is very green.

Masculine.

शाळेचा परिसर, the school grounds and what is around them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिसर** (*parisar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *parisara*, "neighbourhood, environs" (Monier-Williams).
