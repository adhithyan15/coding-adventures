## You'll want to know: روایت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C346-MAJARA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**روایت** (*revâyat*) — "an account, a narrative".

> روایت او با روایت پلیس فرق داشت.
>
> *Revâyat-e u bâ revâyat-e polis farq dâsht.*
>
> His account differed from the police account.

*Be revâyat-e ...* "according to ...". Also a traditional report of a saying.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revâyat* is usually traced to Arabic *riwāya*, "narration, transmission"; EtymDB lists no entry.
