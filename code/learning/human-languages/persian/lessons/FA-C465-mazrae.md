## You'll want to know: مزرعه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C465-MAZRAE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مزرعه** (*mazra'e*) — "a farm; a field (of crops)".

> پدربزرگم یک مزرعه کوچک در روستا دارد.
>
> *Pedarbozorgam yek mazra'e-ye kuchek dar rustâ dârad.*
>
> My grandfather has a small farm in the village.

Arabic plural *mazâre'*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mazra'e* was borrowed from Arabic *mazraʿa* (EtymDB), a place where seed is sown.
