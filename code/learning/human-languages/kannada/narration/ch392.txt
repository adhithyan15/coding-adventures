Kannada, chapter 392: Elections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚುನಾವಣೆ (cunāvaṇe) — an election.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for empowerment, then the Kannada for commitment.

You'll want to know: ಚುನಾವಣೆ.
ಚುನಾವಣೆ — cunāvaṇe — "an election".
ಮುಂದಿನ ತಿಂಗಳು ನಮ್ಮ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಚುನಾವಣೆ ಇದೆ. mundina tiṅgaḷu namma ūrinalli cunāvaṇe ide. There is an election in our town next month.
ಚುನಾವಣೆಗೆ ನಿಲ್ಲು, to stand for election.

The word, taken apart.
ಚುನಾವಣೆ (cunāvaṇe) is a loan from Hindustani cunāv, "a choosing, an election", from cunnā, "to pick", with a Kannada noun ending.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮತ (mata) — a vote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for commitment, then the Kannada for an election.

You'll want to know: ಮತ.
ಮತ — mata — "a vote".
ಹದಿನೆಂಟು ವರ್ಷ ತುಂಬಿದವರಿಗೆ ಮತ ಹಾಕುವ ಹಕ್ಕು ಇದೆ. hadineṇṭu varṣa tumbidavarige mata hākuva hakku ide. Those who have turned eighteen have the right to vote.
ಮತ (mata) ಹಾಕು, to cast a vote. It also means an opinion or a creed.

The word, taken apart.
ಮತ (mata) is borrowed from Sanskrit mata, "thought, opinion" (Monier-Williams), from man, "to think".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬಹುಮತ (bahumata) — a majority.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an election, then the Kannada for a vote.

You'll want to know: ಬಹುಮತ.
ಬಹುಮತ — bahumata — "a majority".
ಚುನಾವಣೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅವರಿಗೆ ಬಹುಮತ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. cunāvaṇeyalli avarige bahumata sikkitu. They won a majority in the election.
ಬಹು (many) + ಮತ (mata) (votes).

The word, taken apart.
ಬಹುಮತ (bahumata) is built from Sanskrit bahu, "many", and mata, "opinion" (Monier-Williams); the sense "majority" is modern.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡು.
ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡು — pracāra māḍu — "to campaign; to publicise".
ಅವಳು ಮನೆ ಮನೆಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡಿದಳು. avaḷu mane manege hōgi pracāra māḍidaḷu. She campaigned from door to door.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, pracāra māḍide.
ಪ್ರಚಾರ alone is publicity or a campaign.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಚಾರ ಮಾಡು (pracāra māḍu) is built from Sanskrit pracāra, "coming forth, showing oneself" (Monier-Williams), with māḍu, "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಂಬೋಧಿಸು (sambōdhisu) — to address a crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a majority, then the Kannada for to campaign.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಬೋಧಿಸು.
ಸಂಬೋಧಿಸು — sambōdhisu — "to address a crowd".
ಚುನಾವಣೆಗೆ ಮೊದಲು ಅವಳು ಊರಿನ ಜನರನ್ನು ಸಂಬೋಧಿಸಿದಳು. cunāvaṇege modalu avaḷu ūrina janarannu sambōdhisidaḷu. Before the election she addressed the townspeople.
Past (I …): ಸಂಬೋಧಿಸಿದೆ, sambōdhiside.
The audience takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಸಂಬೋಧನೆ, an address.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಬೋಧಿಸು (sambōdhisu) is built on Sanskrit saṃbodhana, "calling to, addressing" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
