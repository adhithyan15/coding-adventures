Lesson 1 of 7.
ग्रहः (grahaḥ) — a planet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bower, then the Sanskrit for a meadow.

You'll want to know: ग्रहः.
ग्रहः — grahaḥ — "a planet".

The word, taken apart.
graha is built on the root √grah, "to seize" (MW), which you met in gṛhṇāti: a planet is a "seizer", believed to grip people's fortunes. In the Rigveda it means "as much as can be taken" (CDIAL 4363), and Punjabi garauh is "the influence of the planets".

Lesson 2 of 7.
धूमकेतुः (dhūmaketuḥ) — a comet.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: धूमकेतुः.
धूमकेतुः — dhūmaketuḥ — "a comet".

The word, taken apart.
dhūmaketu is a compound of dhūma, "smoke", and ketu, "bright sign, banner" (MW): a star with a banner of smoke. MW compares dhūma with Latin fūmus, "smoke", and dhūma lives on as the Hindi word for smoke (CDIAL 6849).

Lesson 3 of 7.
पशुः (paśuḥ) — an animal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a planet, then the Sanskrit for a comet.

You'll want to know: पशुः.
पशुः — paśuḥ — "an animal".

The word, taken apart.
paśu is a Rigvedic word for a domestic or sacrificial animal (CDIAL 7984). It is inherited from Indo-European: MW compares Latin pecu, "cattle", and English fee, from the time when wealth was counted in livestock. Hindi pasū means "animal".

The word, taken apart.
jantu is built on the root √jan, "to be born, beget" (MW): first "offspring", then any creature (CDIAL 5110). MW compares √jan with Latin gignere, "to beget", and Greek gignomai, "I become".

Lesson 5 of 7.
पक्षी (pakṣī) — a winged creature, a bird.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an animal, then the Sanskrit for a creature.

You'll want to know: पक्षी.
पक्षी — pakṣī — "a winged creature, a bird".

The word, taken apart.
pakṣī is the nominative of pakṣin, "winged", built on pakṣa, "wing", with -in, "having" (MW): a bird is the winged one. It lives on as Gujarati and Punjabi paṅkhī, "bird" (CDIAL 7636).
