## You'll want to know: જોગાનુજોગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C534-JUGAR534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જોગાનુજોગ** — *jogānujog* — "a chance meeting; a coincidence".

> જો હું મોડો આવત તો આ જોગાનુજોગ ન બનત.
>
> *jo hũ moḍo āvat to ā jogānujog na banat.*
>
> If I had come late, this coincidence would never have happened.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જોગાનુજોગ** (*jogānujog*) is built from *jog*, "a meeting, a joining", from Sanskrit *yoga*, "joining" (Monier-Williams), with *anu*, "after": one meeting falling after another.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
