## You'll want to know: بيطري
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C518-MIHNA518-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بيطري** — *bayṭarī* — "a vet".

> عالج البيطري الحصان المريض.
>
> *ʿālaja al-bayṭarī al-ḥiṣān al-marīḍ.*
>
> The vet treated the sick horse.

The full phrase is طبيب بيطري.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بيطري** (*bayṭarī*) is built on *bayṭār*, an old word for a horse doctor, which was borrowed from Greek *hippiatros*, "horse doctor" (EtymDB). The ending *-ī* makes it "of the bayṭār".

## What you've built
