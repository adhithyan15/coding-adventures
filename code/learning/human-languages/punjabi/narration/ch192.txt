Punjabi, chapter 192: People Before Postpositions.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Return to people and three postpositions — retrieve people and three familiar postpositions.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: lok; YOU SAY: ਲੋਕ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens.
Keep the familiar word for “people” ready. Then retrieve the three familiar postpositions ਨਾਲ, ਲਈ, and ਤੋਂ without joining them to the noun yet.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: lok; YOU SAY: ਲੋਕ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: naal; laii; ton; YOU SAY: ਨਾਲ; ਲਈ; ਤੋਂ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say “people,” then “with,” “for,” and “from.” This opener owns no new form.


Lesson 2 of 7.
Change one familiar noun before a postposition — people before a postposition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਲੋਕ, then the familiar companion postposition ਨਾਲ.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨਾਲ (lokaan naal) — lokā̃ nāl — with people.
The standalone form is ਲੋਕ. Before a postposition, this noun becomes ਲੋਕਾਂ (lokaan). Keep the step narrow: this lesson follows one noun and does not say that every Punjabi noun changes in the same way.

Grammar Lens: source boundary.
Johal explains the oblique case before a postposition and shows that this class of masculine plural adds the nasal ending -ਾਂ. Here that source rule is applied only to ਲੋਕ. Source: Ranbir Johal, Speaking and Writing Punjabi (2018), pp. 76–78, oblique nouns before postpositions.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: lokaan naal; YOU SAY: ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨਾਲ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨਾਲ (lokaan naal)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਲੋਕਾਂ (lokaan) once, then check the final ਾਂ]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Produce the form of “people” used before ਨਾਲ.

Lesson 5 of 7.

Grammar Lens: keep the noun form.
ਲੋਕਾਂ ਲਈ (lokaan laii) — lokā̃ laī — for people.
Only the familiar postposition changes. The noun remains ਲੋਕਾਂ (lokaan).

Lesson 7 of 7.
A bounded people-and-postpositions payoff — contrast standalone people with three postposition frames.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Recall standalone ਲੋਕ, then the form used before a postposition.

Guided Practice: four skills.
[your turn — hear: lok; lokaan naal; lokaan laii; lokaan ton; YOU SAY: ਲੋਕ; ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨਾਲ; ਲੋਕਾਂ ਲਈ; ਲੋਕਾਂ ਤੋਂ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਲੋਕ, ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨਾਲ (lokaan naal), ਲੋਕਾਂ ਲਈ (lokaan laii), ਲੋਕਾਂ ਤੋਂ (lokaan ton)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: cover the line and write ਲੋਕਾਂ (lokaan) once, then one complete postposition phrase]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Produce the standalone form, then all three postposition phrases.
