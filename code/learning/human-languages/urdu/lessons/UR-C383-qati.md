## You'll want to know: قطعی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C383-MANZURI383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قطعی** — *qatī* — "absolute; (with نہیں) not at all".

> اگر وہ آیا تو میں قطعی بات نہیں کروں گا۔
>
> *agar voh āyā to maiṅ qatī bāt nahīṅ karūṅ gā.*
>
> If he comes, I will not speak to him at all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قطعی** (*qatī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qaṭʿī*, "decisive", built on *qaṭʿ*, "cutting".
