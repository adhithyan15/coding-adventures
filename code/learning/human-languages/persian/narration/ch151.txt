Lesson 1 of 5.
تاول (tâval) — a blister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a wound, then the Persian for a pill.

You'll want to know: تاول.
تاول — tâval — "a blister".

The word, taken apart.
tâval is the word for a blister, but no source used here gives its origin, which is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
جوش (jush) — a pimple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pill, then the Persian for a blister.

You'll want to know: جوش.
جوش — jush — "a pimple".

The word, taken apart.
jush is the stem of jushidan, "to boil": a boil on the skin. The verb is native, usually traced to Middle Persian jōšīdan.

Lesson 4 of 5.
قطره (qatre) — a drop; drops (medicine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pimple, then the Persian for an ointment.

You'll want to know: قطره.
قطره — qatre — "a drop; drops (medicine)".

The word, taken apart.
qatre is borrowed from Arabic qaṭra, "drop". Its Arabic root is q-ṭ-r (Lane).
