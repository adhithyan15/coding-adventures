## You'll want to know: बातूनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C473-BATUNI473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बातूनी** — *bātūnī* — "talkative".

> मेरी मौसी बहुत बातूनी हैं, घंटों बात करती हैं।
>
> *merī mausī bahut bātūnī haiṁ, ghaṇṭoṁ bāt kartī haiṁ.*
>
> My aunt is very talkative; she talks for hours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बातूनी** (*bātūnī*) is *bāt*, "talk", with the ending *-ūnī*; CDIAL lists Hindi *bāt*, "talk, matter, thing", under Sanskrit *vārtta* (CDIAL 11564).
