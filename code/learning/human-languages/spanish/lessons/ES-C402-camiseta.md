# la camiseta — the smaller shirt

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-CAMISA, ES-LEX-COSTAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *the shirt* and ask what it costs. (*La camisa. ¿Cuánto
cuesta?*)

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *ca-mi-SE-ta*: keep all four vowels; the stress moves to *se*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-CAMISETA]; assesses=[] -->

**la camiseta** — "the T-shirt."

The build is visible: **camisa** plus **-eta**, a suffix that can make something
smaller. The lighter, smaller shirt became the ordinary T-shirt. Keep the two
shop labels distinct: **camisa** for a shirt, **camiseta** for a T-shirt.
