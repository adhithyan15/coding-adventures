## You'll want to know: फुर्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C535-DAUR535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**फुर्ती** — *phurtī* — "quickness; agility".

> खिलाड़ी ने बड़ी फुर्ती से गेंद पकड़ी।
>
> *Khilāṛī ne baṛī phurtī se gẽd pakṛī.*
>
> The player caught the ball with great quickness.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फुर्ती** (*phurtī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *sphurati*, "darts, flashes" (CDIAL 13849). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "activity"; quickness is how Hindi uses it now.
