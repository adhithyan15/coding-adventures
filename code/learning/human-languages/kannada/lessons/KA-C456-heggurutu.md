## You'll want to know: ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C456-SMARAKA456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು** — *heggurutu* — "a landmark".

> ಆ ಗೋಪುರ ನಗರದ ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು.
>
> *ā gōpura nagarada heggurutu.*
>
> That tower is the landmark of the city.

ಹೆ-, big, plus ಗುರುತು, a mark: the big sign people steer by.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು** (*heggurutu*) is a native Kannada compound of *per*, *hē-*, "big" (DEDR 4411), and ಗುರುತು, "a mark, a sign" (DEDR 1847).
