## You'll want to know: फलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C555-GIRAVAT555-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फलना** — *phalnā* — "to bear fruit; to pay off".

> आँगन का पेड़ बहुत फला।
>
> *Āṁgan kā peṛ bahut phalā.*
>
> The tree in the courtyard bore a lot of fruit.

Past (it …): वह पेड़ फला, *vah peṛ phalā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फलना** (*phalnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *phalati*, "bears fruit" (CDIAL 9057).
