## You'll want to know: der Riese
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C315-RITTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Riese** — "the giant".

> Der Riese war so groß wie ein Haus.
>
> *The giant was as big as a house.*

Weak noun: *den Riesen*. *Riesig* — huge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Riese* is inherited from Old High German *riso*, from Proto-Germanic *risiz* "giant" (EtymDB).
