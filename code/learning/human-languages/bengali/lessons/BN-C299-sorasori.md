## You'll want to know: সরাসরি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C299-SOMPROCHAR299-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সরাসরি** — *sôrāsôri* — "live; directly".

> টিভিতে খেলাটা সরাসরি দেখাল।
>
> *ṭibhite khelāṭā sôrāsôri dekhālô.*
>
> The match was shown live on television.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সরাসরি** (*sôrāsôri*) is usually traced to Persian *sarāsar*, 'from end to end, throughout'; Bengali uses it for 'straight, directly' and for a broadcast shown live.
