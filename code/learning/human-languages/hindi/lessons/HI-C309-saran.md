## You'll want to know: शरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C309-RAHAT309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शरण** — *śaraṇ* — "refuge; shelter".

> पीड़ितों ने पास के मंदिर में शरण ली।
>
> *pīṛitoṁ ne pās ke mandir meṁ śaraṇ lī.*
>
> The victims took refuge in a nearby temple.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शरण** (*śaraṇ*) is Sanskrit *śaraṇa*, "a shelter, a protection" (Monier-Williams), feminine in Hindi. **शरण लेना** is to take refuge.
