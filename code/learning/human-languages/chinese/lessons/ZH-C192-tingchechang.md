## You'll want to know: tíngchēchǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C192-SIGN192-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tíngchēchǎng* — "car park".

> *shāngchǎng xiàbian yǒu yí ge tíngchēchǎng* — There's a car park under the shopping centre.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tíngchēchǎng* is built from *tíng* 'to stop', 车 'vehicle' and *chǎng* 'an open ground', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *deng*, *tsyhae* and *drjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
