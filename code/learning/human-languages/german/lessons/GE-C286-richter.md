## You'll want to know: der Richter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C286-URTEIL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Richter** — "the judge".

> Der Richter hörte beiden Seiten zu.
>
> *The judge listened to both sides.*

Plural: *die Richter*. Feminine *die Richterin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Richter* is built on *richten* "to judge, to put right", from Old High German *rihten*, Proto-Germanic *rihtijaną* (EtymDB).
