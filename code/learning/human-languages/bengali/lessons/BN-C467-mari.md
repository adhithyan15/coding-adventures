## You'll want to know: মাড়ি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C467-THOT467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মাড়ি** — *māṛi* — "the gums".

> অরুণের মাড়ি থেকে রক্ত পড়ছে।
>
> *ôruner māṛi theke rôkto pôṛchhe.*
>
> Arun's gums are bleeding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মাড়ি** (*māṛi*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under a reconstructed *māṁsapuṭaka*, "the gums" (CDIAL 9984), a compound of the words for flesh and for a fold.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
