Urdu, chapter 404: Washrooms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غسل خانہ (ghusl khānā) — a washroom; a bathroom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to spread out (a sheet), then the Urdu for a lift.

You'll want to know: غسل خانہ.
غسل خانہ — ghusl khānā — "a washroom; a bathroom".
کمرے کے ساتھ ایک غسل خانہ ہے۔ kamre ke sāth ek ghusl khānā hai. There is a washroom next to the room.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
غسل خانہ (ghusl khānā) is built from ghusl, "washing", usually traced to Arabic ghusl, and Persian khāna, "a house, a room".

غسل خانے کا نل ٹپکتا ہے۔ ghusl khāne kā nal ṭapaktā hai. The tap in the washroom drips.
Past (it …): وہ نل رات بھر ٹپکا, voh nal rāt bhar ṭapkā.

The word, taken apart.
ٹپکنا (ṭapaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭapaknā, "to drip", under ṭapp, "drip, drop", an imitative form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5444).
