# ઉગારવું (ugārvũ) — to rescue

## You'll want to know: ઉગારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C307-RUNI307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉગારવું** — *ugārvũ* — "to rescue".

> અરુણે નદીમાંથી એક છોકરીને ઉગારી.
>
> *Aruṇe nadīmā̃thī ek chokrīne ugārī.*
>
> Arun rescued a girl from the river.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને ઉગાર્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne ugāryũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉગારવું** (*ugārvũ*) is the causative of *ugarvũ*, to escape, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *udgirati*, "spits out, gives out" (CDIAL 1960): to make someone escape a danger. The person saved takes **ને**: **છોકરીને ઉગારી**.
