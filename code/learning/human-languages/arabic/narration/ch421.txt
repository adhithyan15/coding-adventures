Arabic, chapter 421: Chances and discoveries.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
فرصة (furṣa) — a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to be equal, then the Arabic for shared.

You'll want to know: فرصة.
فرصة — furṣa — "a chance".
كانت الرحلة فرصة جيدة للتعلم. kānat ar-riḥla furṣa jayyida li-t-taʿallum. The trip was a good chance to learn.
Plural: فرص, furaṣ.

The word, taken apart.
فرصة (furṣa) is built on the native Arabic root f-r-ṣ, "a turn, an opportunity" (Lane). A furṣa is one's turn, the right moment: a chance.

Lesson 2 of 6.
لعل (laʿalla) — it may be that; perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for shared, then the Arabic for a chance.

You'll want to know: لعل.
لعل — laʿalla — "it may be that; perhaps".
لعل الطقس يتحسن غدا. laʿalla aṭ-ṭaqs yataḥassanu ghadan. Perhaps the weather will improve tomorrow.
Like إن, it is followed by a noun or a pronoun: لعله, perhaps he.

The word, taken apart.
لعل (laʿalla) is an old native Arabic particle of hope or expectation, perhaps. Grammarians often trace it to ʿalla with la-; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for it may be that, then the Arabic for a discovery.

You'll want to know: ذكاء.
ذكاء — dhakāʾ — "intelligence".
الذكاء وحده لا يكفي. adh-dhakāʾ waḥdahu lā yakfī. Intelligence alone is not enough.

The word, taken apart.
ذكاء (dhakāʾ) is built on the native Arabic root dh-k-w, usually traced to the sense "to be bright, sharp of mind". Dhakāʾ is sharpness of mind: intelligence. ذكي, clever, is from the same root.

You'll want to know: اجتهاد.
اجتهاد — ijtihād — "diligence".
نجح الطالب باجتهاده. najaḥa aṭ-ṭālib bi-jtihādih. The student succeeded through hard work.

The word, taken apart.
اجتهاد (ijtihād) is built on the native Arabic root j-h-d, "to strive, to exert oneself" (Lane). Ijtihād is the Form VIII verbal noun, exerting oneself: diligence. مجتهد, hard-working, is from the same root.
