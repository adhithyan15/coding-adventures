## You'll want to know: o vândalo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C286-DELITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o vândalo** — "the vandal".

> Um vândalo pintou a estátua do parque.
>
> *A vandal painted the statue in the park.*

Named after an ancient Germanic people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vândalo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *Vandalus* (EtymDB), the name of an ancient Germanic people.
