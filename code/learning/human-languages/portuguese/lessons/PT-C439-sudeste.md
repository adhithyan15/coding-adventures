## You'll want to know: o sudeste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C439-CARDEAIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o sudeste** — "the south-east".

> A tempestade vai para sudeste.
>
> *The storm is moving south-east.*

Also spelled *sueste* in old texts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sudeste* came from French *sud-est* (EtymDB), from *sud* 'south' and *est* 'east', both of Germanic origin.
