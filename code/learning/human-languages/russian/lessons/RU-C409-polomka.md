## You'll want to know: поломка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C409-POLOMKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поломка** (*polómka*) — "a breakdown, a fault".

> Из-за поломки мы приехали домой поздно.
>
> *Iz-za polómki my priyékhali domóy pózdno.*
>
> Because of the breakdown we got home late.

Feminine. Built on *поломать* — to break.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поломка* is built on *поломать* 'to break', from *ломать* 'to break'; EtymDB lists *ломать* without a deeper source.
