Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 116: Virtue, Good sense, Knowledge, Right, Chance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गुण (guṇ) — a virtue, a good quality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for earnings, then the Marwadi for a job.

You'll want to know: गुण.
गुण — guṇ — "a virtue, a good quality".

The word, taken apart.
गुण (guṇ) is Sanskrit guṇa, "strand of a cord; quality", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4190). The inherited form is Hindi gun, "rope"; गुण (guṇ) keeps the Sanskrit sense "quality".

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a job, then the Marwadi for a virtue.

You'll want to know: अकल.
अकल — akal — "good sense, intelligence".

The word, taken apart.
अकल (akal) is a loan from Arabic ʿaql, "reason, intelligence", which reached Marwadi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ʿ-q-l (Lane). Marwadi splits the final consonants with a vowel.

You'll want to know: ग्यान.
ग्यान — gyān — "knowledge".

The word, taken apart.
ग्यान (gyān) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit jñāna, "knowledge", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped (CDIAL 5281). Marwadi says the jñ as gy.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मौको (mauko) — a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for knowledge, then the Marwadi for a right.

You'll want to know: मौको.
मौको — mauko — "a chance".

The word, taken apart.
मौको (mauko) is a loan from Arabic mawqiʿ, "place, occasion", which reached Marwadi through Persian. Its Arabic root is w-q-ʿ (Lane). Persian says it mauqa, "occasion"; the sense moved to "chance".
