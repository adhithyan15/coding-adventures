## You'll want to know: wūyún
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C238-SKY238-03]; assesses=[] -->

*wūyún* — "dark clouds".

> *wūyún lái le yào xiàyǔ le* — Dark clouds are coming in; it's about to rain.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wūyún* is built from *wū* 'black as a crow' and 云 'cloud'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjun* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary explains the first, *'u* there, after an older book, as the crow that is all black.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
