**frangō, frangere** — "to break".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**frangō** is inherited, usually traced to Proto-Indo-European *\*bhreg-*, "to break" (EtymDB 2.1); English *break* is a cousin (Lewis & Short).
