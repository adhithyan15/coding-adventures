## You'll want to know: दंतकथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C337-DANTKATHA337-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दंतकथा** — *dantkathā* — "a legend".

> या तलावाबद्दल एक जुनी दंतकथा आहे.
>
> *yā talāvābaddal ek junī dantkathā āhe.*
>
> There is an old legend about this lake.

Feminine.

A tale passed on by word of mouth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दंतकथा** (*dantkathā*) is built from Sanskrit *danta*, 'a tooth', and *kathā*, 'a story' (Monier-Williams); it is usually explained as a tale passed from mouth to mouth.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
