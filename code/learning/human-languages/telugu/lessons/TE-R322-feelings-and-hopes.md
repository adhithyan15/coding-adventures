# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C322-DUHKHAM-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-178, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-177, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "grief", "a remedy" and "one's home place". (*duḥkhaṁ*, *auṣadhaṁ*, *ūru*.)
- [YOU WRITE: **ః**, **ఔ** and **ఊ**, once each from memory]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C322-DUHKHAM-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-178, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-177, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, TE-GRAMMAR-C322-FEELING-GA-UNDI-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C322-FELT-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C322-ALANI-UNDI-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C322-MY-WISH-01, TE-LEX-C321-SITU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-BUY-TICKET-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-WHEN-LEAVES-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-MISSED-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-LOST-REPORT-01, TE-LEX-C321-AUSADHAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I am very happy. I would like to go to my home town.]
- [YOU SAY: When the bus went, I felt angry.]
- [YOU SAY: My bag was lost. I felt sad.]
- [YOU SAY: My dream is to write a book. My hope is that I will get that job.]
- [YOU WRITE: **దుఃఖం**, **ఔషధం**, **ఊరు**, **ఓటు**, **ఈలోగా**]
