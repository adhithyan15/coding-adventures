## You'll want to know: సాగు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C493-TIRUGU493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సాగు** — *sāgu* — "to go on; to proceed".

> మా ప్రయాణం హాయిగా సాగింది.
>
> *mā prayāṇaṁ hāyigā sāgindi.*
>
> Our journey went on pleasantly.

Past (I …): సాగాను, *sāgānu*.

Said of a journey, a meeting or a life: సాఫీగా సాగింది, it went smoothly. As a noun సాగు is farming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సాగు** (*sāgu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2430 lists Telugu *sāgu*, "to go on, to proceed, to continue".
