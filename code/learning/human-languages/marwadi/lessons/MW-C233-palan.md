## You'll want to know: पलाण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C233-GEAR233-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पलाण** — *palāṇ* — "a camel saddle; a pack-saddle".

> ऊंट माथै नुवो पलाण है।
>
> *ū̃ṭ māthai nuvo palāṇ hai.*
>
> The camel has a new saddle.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पलाण** (*palāṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *palyāṇa*, "a saddle"; CDIAL lists Hindi *palān* and Gujarati *palāṇ*, "a pack-saddle". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 7966).
