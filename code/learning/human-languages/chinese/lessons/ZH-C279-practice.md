## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-THATDAY-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-SHIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C279-TURAN-01, ZH-LEX-C279-DIERTIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C279-YIXIAZI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-YIHOU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-SEQUENCE-01, ZH-LEX-C234-RAIN234-04, ZH-ORTHO-TONGXUE-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01] -->

> **A:** 上个星期六你做什么了
>
> *shàng ge xīngqīliù nǐ zuò shénme le* — What did you do last Saturday?
>
> **B:** 我和两个同学去山上了
>
> *wǒ hé liǎng ge tóngxué qù shān shàng le* — Two classmates and I went up the mountain.
>
> **A:** 天气好吗
>
> *tiānqì hǎo ma* — Was the weather good?
>
> **B:** 吃了饭以后 突然下雨了
>
> *chī le fàn yǐhòu, tūrán xiàyǔ le* — After we'd eaten, it suddenly rained.
>
> **A:** 你们有雨衣吗
>
> *nǐmen yǒu yǔyī ma* — Did you have raincoats?
>
> **B:** 没有 我们找车的时候 一个老人来了
>
> *méiyǒu, wǒmen zhǎo chē de shíhou, yí ge lǎorén lái le* — No. While we were looking for the car, an old man came.
>
> **A:** 第二天呢
>
> *dì'èr tiān ne* — And the next day?
>
> **B:** 天气一下子好了 我们看了花 然后回家了
>
> *tiānqì yíxiàzi hǎo le, wǒmen kàn le huā, ránhòu huí jiā le* — The weather was fine in no time. We looked at the flowers, then went home.

B tells the story from the inside: 我们, not they. The time words still lead each turn.

## What you've built — telling a day
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-THATDAY-01, ZH-SCRIPT-DI-01, ZH-SCRIPT-TU-01, ZH-LEX-C279-DIERTIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C279-TURAN-01, ZH-LEX-C279-YIXIAZI-01, ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGZAI-01] -->

A story needs a moment, a scene, a turn and a next day:

- **他来的时候 我正在看书** *tā lái de shíhou, wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū* — the moment and the scene.
- **突然下雨了** *tūrán xiàyǔ le* — the turn.
- **天气一下子冷了** *tiānqì yíxiàzi lěng le* — a change that took no time.
- **第二天** *dì'èr tiān* — the day after, inside the story.

**Listen.** *Nà tiān nǐ zuò shénme le?*

**Speak.** Tell a day of your own in six lines: a time, what you were doing, what happened, the next day.
