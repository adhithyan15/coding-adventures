German, chapter 158: An offer, A bill, A bag, A parcel, A stamp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Angebot — an offer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a credit card, then the German for a price.

You'll want to know: das Angebot.
das Angebot — "an offer".

The word, taken apart.
Angebot is the noun of the verb "to offer": an- + a verb meaning "to offer, to bid", which EtymDB 2.1 lists beside English bid and Dutch bieden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an offer, then the German for a bill.

You'll want to know: die Tüte.
die Tüte — "a bag (paper or plastic)".

The word, taken apart.
Tüte from Middle Low German tūte (EtymDB 2.1) — a northern word; EtymDB lists Dutch tuit, "spout", beside it. A paper bag was rolled into a cone.
