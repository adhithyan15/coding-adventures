## You'll want to know: આર્થિક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C355-VERO355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**આર્થિક** — *ārthik* — "economic; financial".

> નોકરી ગયા પછી અરુણની આર્થિક પરિસ્થિતિ નબળી થઈ.
>
> *nokrī gayā pachī Aruṇnī ārthik paristhiti nabḷī thaī.*
>
> After he lost his job, Arun's finances grew weak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આર્થિક** (*ārthik*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ārthika*, built on *artha*, "aim, wealth" (Monier-Williams).
