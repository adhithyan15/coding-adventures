## You'll want to know: झेलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C367-TARIF367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झेलना** — *jhelnā* — "to endure, to put up with".

> उसने बहुत आलोचना झेली।
>
> *usne bahut ālocnā jhelī.*
>
> He put up with a lot of criticism.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दुख झेला, *usne dukh jhelā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झेलना** (*jhelnā*) is inherited from *\*jhēlati*, "undergoes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5413).
