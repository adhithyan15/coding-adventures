# તણાવું (taṇāvũ) — to be swept away

## You'll want to know: તણાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C304-PUR304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**તણાવું** — *taṇāvũ* — "to be swept away".

> પૂરમાં અમારી ગાય તણાઈ ગઈ.
>
> *pūrmā̃ amārī gāy taṇāī gaī.*
>
> Our cow was swept away in the flood.

Past (it …): તે ઝૂંપડું તણાઈ ગયું, *te jhū̃pḍũ taṇāī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તણાવું** (*taṇāvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *tanṓti*, "stretches", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be dragged" (CDIAL 5659): a flood drags things along with it. **તણાઈ ગયું**, it was swept away.

One more word for telling what happened.
