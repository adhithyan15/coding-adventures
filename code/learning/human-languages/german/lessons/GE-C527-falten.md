## You'll want to know: falten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C527-WAESCHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**falten** — "to fold".

> Nach dem Bügeln faltet er die Hemden.
>
> *After ironing he folds the shirts.*

Regular: *hat gefaltet*. *Die Hände falten* — to fold your hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falten* is inherited from Old High German *faltan*, from Proto-Germanic *falþaną* "to fold" (EtymDB); English *fold* is a cousin.
