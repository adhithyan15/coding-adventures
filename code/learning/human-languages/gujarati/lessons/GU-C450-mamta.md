## You'll want to know: મમતા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C450-VAHAL450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મમતા** — *mamtā* — "a mother's love".

> માતાની મમતા સૌથી મોટી છે.
>
> *mātānī mamtā sauthī moṭī che.*
>
> A mother's love is the greatest of all.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મમતા** (*mamtā*) is a tatsama: Monier-Williams gives Sanskrit *mamatā* as the state of feeling "mine", from *mama*, "my"; Gujarati uses it for a mother's tender love.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
