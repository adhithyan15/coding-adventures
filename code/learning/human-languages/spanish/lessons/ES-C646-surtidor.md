## You'll want to know: el surtidor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C646-GASOLINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el surtidor** — "the petrol pump; the jet (of a fountain)".

> Pare junto al surtidor número tres.
>
> *Stop next to pump number three.*

At a petrol station each pump is *un surtidor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*surtidor* is built on *surtir* "to supply; to spout". The origin of *surtir* is not settled.
