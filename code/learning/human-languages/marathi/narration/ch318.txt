Marathi, chapter 318: A New Baby.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जन्म (janma) — birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a shehnai, then the Marathi for a rhyming couplet.

You'll want to know: जन्म.
जन्म — janma — "birth".
माझ्या भावाचा जन्म पुण्यात झाला. mājhyā bhāvācā janma puṇyāt jhālā. My brother was born in Pune.
Masculine.
X-चा जन्म (janma) होणे, X is born.

The word, taken apart.
जन्म (janma) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit janman, 'birth' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बारसे (bārse) — a naming ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rhyming couplet, then the Marathi for birth.

You'll want to know: बारसे.
बारसे — bārse — "a naming ceremony".
बाळाचं बारसं बाराव्या दिवशी झालं. bāḷācaṁ bārsaṁ bārāvyā divśī jhālaṁ. The baby's naming ceremony was on the twelfth day.
Neuter.
Neuter, written बारसं in a sentence like मोठं; held on the twelfth day after a birth, from बारा, twelve.

The word, taken apart.
बारसे (bārse) is built on bārā, 'twelve', the day the rite falls on; CDIAL lists Marathi bārāvā, 'twelfth', under Sanskrit dvādaśama (CDIAL 6660).

Before the new one: say the Marathi for birth, then the Marathi for a naming ceremony.

वाढदिवस — vāḍhdivas — "a birthday".
काल माझ्या मुलीचा पाचवा वाढदिवस होता. kāl mājhyā mulīcā pācvā vāḍhdivas hotā. Yesterday was my daughter's fifth birthday.
Masculine.
वाढदिवसाच्या शुभेच्छा, happy birthday.

The word, taken apart.
वाढदिवस (vāḍhdivas) is built from vāḍh, 'increase, growth', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit vardha (CDIAL 11373), and divas, 'a day': the day one grows a year.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मुंज (muñj) — a thread ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a naming ceremony, then the Marathi for a birthday.

You'll want to know: मुंज.
मुंज — muñj — "a thread ceremony".
माझ्या भावाच्या मुलाची मुंज पुढच्या आठवड्यात आहे. mājhyā bhāvācyā mulācī muñj puḍhacyā āṭhvaḍyāt āhe. My brother's son's thread ceremony is next week.
Feminine.
The Marathi name for the upanayana, the rite at which a boy is given the sacred thread; it is kept in some communities.

The word, taken apart.
मुंज (muñj) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi mũjī, 'the sacred thread', under Sanskrit mauñjī, the cord of muñja grass worn by a Brahman (CDIAL 10367; muñja is CDIAL 10184). The rite is named after that cord.

The word, taken apart.
पत्रिका (patrikā) is built on Sanskrit patra, 'a leaf, a letter' (Monier-Williams), with the small-thing ending -ikā.
