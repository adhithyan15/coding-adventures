## You'll want to know: ساکھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C531-QAUL531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ساکھ** — *sākh* — "a good name (for honesty); credit".

> اگر دکاندار جھوٹ بولتا تو اس کی ساکھ جاتی۔
>
> *agar dukāndār jhūṭ boltā to us kī sākh jātī.*
>
> If the shopkeeper told lies, his good name would be gone.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ساکھ** (*sākh*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sākh*, "evidence, reputation", under Sanskrit *sākṣya*, "testimony" (CDIAL 13322).
