## You'll want to know: rhētorica, rhētoricae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C404-RHETORICA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**rhētorica, rhētoricae** — "rhetoric, the art of speaking".

> Rhētoricam in scholā didicit.
>
> *He learned rhetoric at school.*

*rhētorica, rhētoricae* f., also *ars rhētorica*. English *rhetoric*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rhētorica* was borrowed from Greek *rhētorikē* "the art of speaking" (EtymDB), built on *rhētōr* "a speaker". English *rhetoric* comes from it.
