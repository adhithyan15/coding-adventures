## You'll want to know: praetor, praetōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C383-MAGISTRATUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**praetor, praetōris** — "a praetor, a judge and magistrate".

> Praetor iūs dīcit in forō.
>
> *The praetor gives judgement in the Forum.*

*praetor, praetōris* m. Next in rank below the consuls, he ran the law courts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*praetor* is usually taken as *prae* "before" and the root of *eō* "to go" (Lewis & Short): one who goes in front, an old word for a leader.
