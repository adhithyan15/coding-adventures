## You'll want to know: le relais
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C508-COURSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le relais** — "relay; relay race".

> Notre équipe a gagné le relais quatre fois cent mètres.
>
> *Our team won the four by one hundred metres relay.*

*Prendre le relais* — to take over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le relais* comes from Old French *relaier* "to leave behind" (EtymDB), built from *re-* and *laier* "to leave": fresh horses were left waiting along the road.
