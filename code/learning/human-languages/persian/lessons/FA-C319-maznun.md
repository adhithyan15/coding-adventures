## You'll want to know: مظنون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C319-MAMUR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مظنون** (*maznun*) — "a suspect; suspected".

> پلیس با دو مظنون صحبت کرد.
>
> *Polis bâ do maznun sohbat kard.*
>
> The police spoke with two suspects.

As an adjective: *maznun be dozdi* "suspected of theft".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maznun* is borrowed from Arabic *maẓnūn*, "suspected, imagined" (EtymDB).
