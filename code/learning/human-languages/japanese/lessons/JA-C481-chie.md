## You'll want to know: ちえ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C481-SKILL481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちえ** — *chie* — "wisdom; know-how".

> **そふは ちえが あります。** — *sofu wa chie ga arimasu* — "My grandfather is wise."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちえ** (*chie*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chi*, "know" (Middle Chinese *trje*) and *e*, "blessing" (*hwejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
