## You'll want to know: प्रसारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C309-VRITTANTA309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसारयति** — *prasārayati* — "broadcasts; spreads out".

> आकाशवाणी वार्तां प्रसारयति।
>
> *ākāśavāṇī vārtāṃ prasārayati.*
>
> The radio broadcasts the news.

Past (it …): आकाशवाणी वार्तां प्रसारितवती, *ākāśavāṇī vārtāṃ prasāritavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसारयति** (*prasārayati*) is built from *pra* and the causative of the root √sṛ, "to run, flow"; MW gives *pra-sārayati* as to stretch out, extend, spread out, and to broadcast is its modern use (MW).
