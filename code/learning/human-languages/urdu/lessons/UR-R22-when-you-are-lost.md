## Guided Practice: the distant band — the first conversation, rescued
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-SALAM, UR-LEX-SHUKRIYA, UR-LEX-JI-HAN, UR-LEX-NAME-QUESTION, UR-LEX-NICE-TO-MEET, UR-LEX-WELLBEING-QUESTION, UR-LEX-WELLBEING-REPLY, UR-LEX-KHUDA-HAFIZ, UR-LEX-MAIN, UR-LEX-HUN, UR-LEX-THIK, UR-LEX-AAP-TUM-TU, UR-DIALOGUE-NAME-EXCHANGE, UR-DIALOGUE-WELLBEING, UR-GRAMMAR-AAP-HAIN, UR-GRAMMAR-MAIN-HUN-FRAME, UR-GRAMMAR-AAP-KA, UR-PRAGMATICS-YOU-REGISTER, UR-PRAGMATICS-STANDARD-FAREWELL, UR-REGISTER-JI-RESPECT, UR-FUNC-KYA-KAHA, UR-LEX-ARAM-SE] -->

[PAUSE 4s] The exchange this book opened with was taught between eighty and a
hundred lessons ago and has not been asked for since. It is also the exact place
a beginner gets lost — so run it now, cold, and put the repair kit inside it.

- [YOU SAY: the opening, from memory — *salām … āp kā nām kyā hai?*]
- [YOU SAY: your answer, and the courtesy after it — *merā nām … hai. āp se mil kar khushī huī*]
- [YOU SAY: the wellbeing question respectfully, choosing for a man or a woman — *āp kaise haiṅ? / āp kaisī haiṅ?*]
- [YOU SAY: the reply — *maiṅ ṭhīk hūṅ, shukriyā*]
- [YOU SAY: now lose the thread, and rescue it — *maiṅ nahīṅ samajhtā. kyā kahā? ārām se kaheṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: and close — *khudā hāfiz*]
- [YOU RECALL: which of *āp*, *tum* and *tū* you used, and why]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-FUNC-KYA-KAHA, UR-LEX-ARAM-SE, UR-GRAMMAR-KYA-POLAR, UR-GRAMMAR-NAHIN-VERB, UR-LEX-KAUN, UR-LEX-JI-HAN] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Four chapters ago this book could not deny a sentence, ask a
yes-or-no question, join two nouns, give a reason, or say that it had not
understood. Say the sentence chapter one promised and never delivered.
(***maiṅ nahīṅ samajhtā***.) Say the question that asks for a repeat.
(**کیا کہا؟**) And say the four members of the **k-** family.
(***kyā, kahāṅ, kyūṅ, kaun***.)
