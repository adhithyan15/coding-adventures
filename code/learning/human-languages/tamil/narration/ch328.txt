Tamil, chapter 328: Kings and Queens.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மன்னர் (maṉṉar) — a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a long time, then the Tamil for ancestral roots.

You'll want to know: மன்னர்.
மன்னர் — maṉṉar — "a king".
மன்னர் தன் மக்களை நன்றாகக் கவனித்துக்கொண்டார். maṉṉar taṉ makkaḷai naṉṟākak kavaṉittukkoṇṭār. The king took good care of his people.
A respectful form; மன்னன் is the plain one.

The word, taken apart.
மன்னர் (maṉṉar) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4774 gives மன் and மன்னன், "king, lord, chief", and மன்னர் is its respectful form.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ராணி (rāṇi) — a queen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for ancestral roots, then the Tamil for a king.

You'll want to know: ராணி.
ராணி — rāṇi — "a queen".
ராணி அழகான தோட்டத்தில் நடந்தாள். rāṇi aḻakāṉa tōṭṭattil naṭantāḷ. The queen walked in a beautiful garden.
Also the queen in chess and in cards.

The word, taken apart.
ராணி (rāṇi) is borrowed from Sanskrit rājñī, "queen" (CDIAL 10692), through a Prakrit form.

Lesson 3 of 5.
இளவரசன் (iḷavaracaṉ) — a prince.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a king, then the Tamil for a queen.

You'll want to know: இளவரசன்.
இளவரசன் — iḷavaracaṉ — "a prince".
இளவரசன் குதிரையில் காட்டுக்குப் போனான். iḷavaracaṉ kutiraiyil kāṭṭukkup pōṉāṉ. The prince rode to the forest on a horse.
இள, young + அரசன், king.

The word, taken apart.
இளவரசன் (iḷavaracaṉ) is a Tamil compound built from இள, "young" (DEDR 513), and அரசன், "king", which DEDR 201 traces to Sanskrit rājan.

Lesson 4 of 5.
இளவரசி (iḷavaraci) — a princess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a queen, then the Tamil for a prince.

You'll want to know: இளவரசி.
இளவரசி — iḷavaraci — "a princess".
இளவரசி அரண்மனையில் வாழ்ந்தாள். iḷavaraci araṇmaṉaiyil vāḻntāḷ. The princess lived in the palace.
The -இ marks a woman, as in அரசன் and அரசி.

The word, taken apart.
இளவரசி (iḷavaraci) is a Tamil compound built from இள, "young" (DEDR 513), and அரசி, "queen", the feminine of அரசன், which DEDR 201 traces to Sanskrit rājan.

Lesson 5 of 5.
அரியணை (ariyaṇai) — a throne.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a prince, then the Tamil for a princess.

You'll want to know: அரியணை.
அரியணை — ariyaṇai — "a throne".
புதிய மன்னர் அரியணையில் அமர்ந்தார். putiya maṉṉar ariyaṇaiyil amarntār. The new king sat on the throne.
அரியணை (ariyaṇai) ஏறு, to come to the throne.

The word, taken apart.
அரியணை (ariyaṇai) is a Tamil compound built from அரி, "lion", and அணை, "support, prop" (DEDR 123): a lion seat.
