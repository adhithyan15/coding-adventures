## You'll want to know: el obstáculo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C620-SOLUCION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el obstáculo** — "the obstacle".

> Hay muchos obstáculos en el camino.
>
> *There are many obstacles in the way.*

*Superar un obstáculo* — to overcome an obstacle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obstáculo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obstaculum* (EtymDB), built on *obstare* 'to stand in the way', from *stare* 'to stand'.

## What you've built
