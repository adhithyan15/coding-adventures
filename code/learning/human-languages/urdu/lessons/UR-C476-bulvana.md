## You'll want to know: بلوانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C476-DOCTOR476-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بلوانا** — *bulvānā* — "to have (someone) sent for".

> امی نے ڈاکٹر کو گھر بلوایا۔
>
> *ammī ne ḍākṭar ko ghar bulvāyā.*
>
> Mum had the doctor called to the house.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے حکیم کو بلوایا, *us ne hakīm ko bulvāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بلوانا** (*bulvānā*) is built on **بلانا**, "to call", which CDIAL lists under *bōll*, "speak", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9321): the form for having someone else do the calling.
