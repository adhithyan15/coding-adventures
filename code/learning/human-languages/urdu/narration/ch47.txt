Urdu, chapter 47: To sit, To sleep, To get up, To walk, To run.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بیٹھنا (baiṭhnā) — to sit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to see, then the Urdu for to hear.

You'll want to know: بیٹھنا.
بیٹھنا — baiṭhnā — "to sit".

The word, taken apart.
بیٹھنا (baiṭhnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from upaviśati, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245). The -ṭh- goes back to its past form upaviṣṭa, "seated".
