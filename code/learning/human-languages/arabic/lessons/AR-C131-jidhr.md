## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جذر** (*jidhr*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-dh-r*, "a root, a base" (Lane). *Jidhr* is the root of a plant or of a number.
