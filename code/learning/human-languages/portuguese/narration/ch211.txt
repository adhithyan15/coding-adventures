paciência from Latin patientia, "endurance" (EtymDB 2.1), from pati, "to suffer" — a learned borrowing from Latin.

The word, taken apart.
dor de cabeça is "pain of head": dor from Latin dolor (EtymDB 2.1), and cabeça from Vulgar Latin capitia (EtymDB 2.1), from Latin caput, "head".
