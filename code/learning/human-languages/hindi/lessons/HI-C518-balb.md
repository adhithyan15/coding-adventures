## You'll want to know: बल्ब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C518-TAAR518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बल्ब** — *balb* — "a light bulb".

> रसोई का बल्ब नहीं जल रहा है।
>
> *Rasoī kā balb nahīṃ jal rahā hai.*
>
> The kitchen bulb is not lighting up.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बल्ब** (*balb*) is a loan from English *bulb*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
