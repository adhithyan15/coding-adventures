## You'll want to know: a quinzena
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C276-QUINZENA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a quinzena** — "the fortnight, two weeks".

> Passámos uma quinzena na praia.
>
> *We spent a fortnight at the beach.*

*Na primeira quinzena de agosto* — in the first half of August.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quinzena* is built on *quinze* "fifteen", from Latin *quīndecim* (Lewis & Short): a stretch of fifteen days.
