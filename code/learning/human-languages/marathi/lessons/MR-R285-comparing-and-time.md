# Review — comparing and time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-DATIVE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C284-AYCHA-AHE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C284-VHAYCHA-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I got angry" and "I want to become a teacher". (*malā rāg ālā*, *malā śikṣak vhāycaṁ āhe*.) Ask what time the Pune train leaves. (*puṇyācī gāḍī kitī vājtā nighte?*) Then **ऋतू** and **ज्ञान**, and the letters **ऋ** and **ञ**.

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: In my opinion the train is better than the bus.]
- [YOU SAY: Which season do you like most? I like the monsoon most.]
- [YOU SAY: I have lived in Pune for three years.]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday it rained all day. I read from morning until evening.]
- [YOU SAY: how long you have been learning Marathi, with **-पासून**]
