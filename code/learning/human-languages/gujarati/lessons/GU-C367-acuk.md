# અચૂક (acūk) — without fail

## You'll want to know: અચૂક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C367-SAMBHAV367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અચૂક** — *acūk* — "without fail".

> જો તમે આ દવા લેશો તો તાવ અચૂક મટશે.
>
> *jo tame ā davā leśo to tāv acūk maṭśe.*
>
> If you take this medicine, the fever will go without fail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અચૂક** (*acūk*) is built from *a-*, "not", and *cūk*, "a slip, a miss", from a reconstructed *cukk*, "to fall short" (CDIAL 4848): with no miss.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
