## You'll want to know: мостовая
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C443-BRODIT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**мостовая** (*mostováya*) — "a roadway; a paved street".

> Машины едут по мостовой, а люди идут по тротуару.
>
> *Mashíny yédut po mostovóy, a lyúdi idút po trotuáru.*
>
> The cars drive on the roadway, and people walk on the pavement.

An adjective used as a noun: *по мостовой*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мостовая* is built on *мостить* 'to pave', from *мост* 'a bridge', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *mostŭ* (EtymDB).
