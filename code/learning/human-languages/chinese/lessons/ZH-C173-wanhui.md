## You'll want to know: 晚会
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C173-INVITE173-03]; assesses=[] -->

**晚会** — *wǎnhuì* — "evening party; evening show".

> **晚上我有事不能去晚会** *wǎnshang wǒ yǒushì bù néng qù wǎnhuì* — I've got something on tonight, so I can't come to the party.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**晚会** (*wǎnhuì*) is built from 晚 'evening' and 会 'to meet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjonX* and *hwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to come together'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
