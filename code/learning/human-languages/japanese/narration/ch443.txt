Lesson 1 of 6.
てくび (tekubi) — a wrist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the spine, then the Japanese for a joint.

You'll want to know: てくび.
てくび — tekubi — "a wrist".
ころんで、てくびが いたいです。 — koronde, tekubi ga itai desu — "I fell over, and my wrist hurts."

The word, taken apart.
てくび (tekubi) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: te, "hand" and kubi, "neck" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
