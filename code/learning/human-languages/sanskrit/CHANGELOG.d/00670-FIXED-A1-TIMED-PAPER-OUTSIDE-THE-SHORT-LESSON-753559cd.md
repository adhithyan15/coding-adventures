## Fixed — A1 timed paper outside the short lesson

The chapter 65 writing checkpoint now asks for one three-minute independent
form and a bounded one-minute reflection. The authentic twenty-minute form and
named-reader message remain in the separate A1 mock assessment, linked from
the lesson, so a learner can finish the lesson within its 280-second envelope
without losing full-paper exam readiness. A focused corpus test measures the
directed clock, summed subtask times, and review budget rather than trusting
the duration metadata alone.
