Hindi, chapter 531: Drums and Pipes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ढोलक (ḍholak) — a small two-headed drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a raga, then the Hindi for to hum.

You'll want to know: ढोलक.
ढोलक — ḍholak — "a small two-headed drum".
शादी में औरतें ढोलक बजाकर गाती हैं। Śādī meṁ aurteṁ ḍholak bajākar gātī haiṁ. At weddings the women play the dholak and sing.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ढोलक (ḍholak) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit ḍhōla, "a drum" (CDIAL 5608).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a small two-headed drum, then the Hindi for a shehnai (a reed pipe).

You'll want to know: घुँघरू.
घुँघरू — ghũghrū — "ankle bells".
उसके पैरों के घुँघरू बज रहे थे। Uske pairoṁ ke ghũghrū baj rahe the. The bells on her feet were ringing.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
घुँघरू (ghũghrū) is a tadbhava, inherited from ghuṅghura, "a string of bells", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4477).
