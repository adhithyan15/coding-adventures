## You'll want to know: うさぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C211-ANIMAL211-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うさぎ** — *usagi* — "a rabbit".

> **うさぎは くさを たべます。** — *usagi wa kusa o tabemasu* — "Rabbits eat grass."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うさぎ** (*usagi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed and usually written in kana; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
