## You'll want to know: fú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C301-ESCAPE301-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fú* — "to help up; to hold up with a hand".

> *wǒ fú tā qǐlai* — I helped him up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fú* is inherited from Middle Chinese *bju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to hold up' and 'to help'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
