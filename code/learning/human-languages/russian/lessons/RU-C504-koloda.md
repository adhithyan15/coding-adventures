## You'll want to know: колода
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C504-BOARDGAMES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**колода** (*kolóda*) — "a pack of cards; a log".

> В колоде не хватает одной карты.
>
> *V kolóde ne khvatáyet odnóy kárty.*
>
> One card is missing from the pack.

Feminine. Its older sense is a log or a block of wood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *колода* without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *kolda* 'a log, a block'.
