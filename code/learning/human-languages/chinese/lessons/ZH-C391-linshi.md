## You'll want to know: línshí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C391-PLANS391-02]; assesses=[] -->

*línshí* — "at the last minute; for the time being".

> *wǒ línshí yǒushì bù néng qù le* — Something's come up at the last minute and I can't go.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*línshí* is built from *lín* 'to come close' and 时 'the time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lim* and *dzyi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be present at': when the moment is upon you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
