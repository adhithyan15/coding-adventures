## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-LEX-C288-MAIOR-MELIOR-03, LA-LEX-C288-ABHINC-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C288-ACC-DURATION-02, LA-GRAMMAR-C287-AFFECTUS-01] -->

Two chapters, and an experience can be told whole. How it felt, with
*eram* and *quod*. What you hope for, with *spērō* and *cupiō*. What was
bigger, faster or better, with *-ior*, *quam*, *maior*, *minor* and
*melior*. And time counted out: the accusative for how long, *abhinc* for
how long ago.

> *Abhinc duōs annōs Rōmam vēnī. Decem diēs ibi mānsī. Rōma maior erat quam
> putāvī, et laetus eram.*
> — I came to Rome two years ago. I stayed there ten days. Rome was bigger
> than I thought, and I was happy.
