## You'll want to know: نسیه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C461-KARTKHAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نسیه** (*nesye*) — "on credit, on account (buying now and paying later)".

> بقال محله به ما نسیه میدهد.
>
> *Baqqâl-e mahalle be mâ nesye midahad.*
>
> The local grocer lets us buy on credit.

*Nesye kharidan* — to buy on credit. The opposite is *naqd* 'cash'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nesye* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *nasīʾa* 'deferment, sale on credit'. EtymDB does not list it.
