## You'll want to know: a mangueira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C404-BOMBA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a mangueira** — "the hose".

> O empregado pôs a mangueira no carro.
>
> *The attendant put the hose into the car.*

Also a garden hose. *A mangueira* is the mango tree too, a different word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mangueira* is built on *manga* 'sleeve' with *-eira*: a long cloth tube. *Manga* is inherited from Latin *manica* 'sleeve' (Lewis & Short).
