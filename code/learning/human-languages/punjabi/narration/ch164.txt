Punjabi, chapter 164: Return to Waking, Driving, and Saying.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
An old word for waking — to wake up, recalled after a long gap.

One familiar action returns after a long gap. Hear the meaning, pause, and recall its Punjabi word before the model. No new word or scored mock is added.

Warm-up.
Hear to wake up.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਜਾਗਣਾ (jāgṇā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਜਾਗਣਾ (jāgṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to wake up. Now hear to wake up again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਜਾਗਣਾ (jāgṇā) before the model. One old word is enough.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to wake up.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਜਾਗਣਾ (jāgṇā). Listen and retry once if the word does not come back.


Lesson 2 of 3.
An old word for driving — to drive, recalled after a long gap.

Retrieve the familiar driving action. The learner only needs the familiar word, not a sentence, a new meaning, or an A1 mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to drive.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā) before the model.

Guided Practice.
Hear ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall to drive. Then hear to drive again.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā). Only this old meaning and word are needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to drive once more.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā). If needed, listen to the model and try again once.


Lesson 3 of 3.
An old word for saying — to say, then a return to three familiar actions.

First retrieve to say alone. Then hear three familiar actions in a small shuffled mix. Pause before each model. This is not scored A1 writing or a complete mock.

Warm-up.
Hear to say.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਕਹਿਣਾ (kahiṇā).

Guided Practice.
Pause after each familiar meaning, then hear its model:
to drive —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā).
to say —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਕਹਿਣਾ (kahiṇā).
to wake up —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਜਾਗਣਾ (jāgṇā).
Only the order of these old words has changed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to wake up, to say, then to drive. Pause after each cue and recall its Punjabi word before the model: ਜਾਗਣਾ (jāgṇā), ਕਹਿਣਾ (kahiṇā), ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā). Revisit only the old word you missed.
