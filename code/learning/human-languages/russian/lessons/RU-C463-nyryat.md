## You'll want to know: нырять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C463-PRILIV-05]; assesses=[] -->

**нырять** (*nyryát*) — "to dive".

> Мальчики ныряют с камня в море.
>
> *Málchiki nyryáyut s kámnya v móre.*
>
> The boys dive from a rock into the sea.

Imperfective; perfective *нырнуть*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*нырять* is usually linked with *нора* 'a burrow': to dive is to go down into a hole. EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the line is hedged.
