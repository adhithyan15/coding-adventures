## You'll want to know: محقق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C308-TAHQIQ308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**محقق** — *muḥaqqiq* — "an investigator, a detective".

> سأل المحقق الجيران عن الحريق.
>
> *saʾala al-muḥaqqiq al-jīrān ʿan al-ḥarīq.*
>
> The investigator asked the neighbours about the fire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محقق** (*muḥaqqiq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-q-q* (Lane), the root of *ḥaqq*, truth. *Muḥaqqiq* is the Form II participle, "one who makes sure of the truth".
