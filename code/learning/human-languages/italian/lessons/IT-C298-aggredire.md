## You'll want to know: aggredire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C298-BANDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aggredire** — "to attack, to assault".

> Due uomini lo hanno aggredito di notte.
>
> *Two men attacked him at night.*

Present like *capire*: *aggredisce*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aggredire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aggredī* "to go at, to attack" (EtymDB), from *ad* and *gradī* "to step".
