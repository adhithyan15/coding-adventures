Gujarati, chapter 453: A Sudden Fright.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બીવું (bīvũ) — to be scared.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to seethe, then the Gujarati for strife.

You'll want to know: બીવું.
બીવું — bīvũ — "to be scared".
નાની બહેન અંધારામાં બીએ છે. nānī bahen andhārāmā̃ bīe che. My little sister is scared of the dark.
Past (he …): તે બીધો, te bīdho.

The word, taken apart.
બીવું (bīvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit bibhēti, "fears"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 9241). The noun બીક (bīk), "fear", belongs to the same family.

ધાક — dhāk — "awe; dread of someone".
ગામમાં તે સાહેબની ઘણી ધાક હતી. gāmmā̃ te sāhebnī ghaṇī dhāk hatī. The whole village stood in awe of that official.

The word, taken apart.
ધાક (dhāk) goes back to a reconstructed dhākka-, "pomp, boast"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the sense "awe" (CDIAL 6769).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for awe, then the Gujarati for to shudder.

You'll want to know: ત્રાસ.
ત્રાસ — trās — "torment; harassment".
મચ્છરનો ત્રાસ આખી રાત ચાલ્યો. maccharno trās ākhī rāt cālyo. The mosquitoes tormented us all night.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ત્રાસ (trās) is inherited from Sanskrit trāsa, "fear"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "fear, vexation" (CDIAL 6013), and today it is mostly the vexation.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભીતિ (bhīti) — apprehension; fear of what may come.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to shudder, then the Gujarati for torment.

You'll want to know: ભીતિ.
ભીતિ — bhīti — "apprehension; fear of what may come".
ભૂકંપ પછી ગામમાં ઘણી ભીતિ હતી. bhūkamp pachī gāmmā̃ ghaṇī bhīti hatī. After the earthquake there was great apprehension in the village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભીતિ (bhīti) is a tatsama, Sanskrit bhīti, "fear, alarm, dread", as Monier-Williams records it; it shares its root with બીવું (bīvũ), from the start of the chapter.
