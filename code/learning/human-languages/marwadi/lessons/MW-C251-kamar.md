## You'll want to know: कमर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C251-KANDHO251-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कमर** — *kamar* — "the waist; the lower back".

> म्हारी कमर पतळी है।
>
> *mhārī kamar patḷī hai.*
>
> My waist is slim.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कमर** (*kamar*) is usually traced to Persian *kamar*, "waist".
