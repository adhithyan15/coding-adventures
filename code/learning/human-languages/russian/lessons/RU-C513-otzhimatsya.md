## You'll want to know: отжиматься
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C513-TRAINING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**отжиматься** (*otzhimátsya*) — "to do press-ups".

> Каждое утро он отжимается двадцать раз.
>
> *Kázhdoye útro on otzhimáyetsya dvádtsat raz.*
>
> Every morning he does twenty press-ups.

Imperfective; perfective отжаться (отожмусь).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отжиматься* is built on *от-* and *жать* 'to press, to squeeze'; EtymDB has no entry.
