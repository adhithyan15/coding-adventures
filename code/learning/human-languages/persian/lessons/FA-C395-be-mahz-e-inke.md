# به محض اینکه (be mahz-e inke) — as soon as

## You'll want to know: به محض اینکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C395-BASTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**به محض اینکه** (*be mahz-e inke*) — "as soon as".

> به محض اینکه رسیدم، به تو زنگ میزنم.
>
> *be mahz-e inke residam, be to zang mizanam.*
>
> As soon as I arrive, I will call you.

It takes the past even for a future event: *be mahz-e inke residam* — as soon as I arrive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به محض اینکه** is literally "at the mere fact that": *mahz* is usually traced to Arabic *maḥḍ* "pure, mere", and *be* and *inke* "that" are Persian.
