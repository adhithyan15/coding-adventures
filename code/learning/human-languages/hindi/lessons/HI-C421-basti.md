## You'll want to know: बस्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C421-BASTI421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बस्ती** — *bastī* — "a settlement; a poor quarter".

> नदी के पार एक छोटी बस्ती है।
>
> *nadī ke pār ek choṭī bastī hai.*
>
> There is a small settlement across the river.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बस्ती** (*bastī*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *vasati*, "staying, a dwelling-place" (Monier-Williams), from the root *vas*, "to dwell". A बस्ती can be a hamlet or a crowded poor quarter of a city.
