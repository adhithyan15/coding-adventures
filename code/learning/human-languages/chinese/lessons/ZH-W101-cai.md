## Script — the grass piece, a hand, the tree piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 菜

**Three pieces, top to bottom.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The grass piece, three strokes**: a horizontal across the top, then two short verticals through it, left before right.

**The hand, four strokes**: a left-falling stroke across, two dots under it, left then middle, and a short left-falling stroke on the right.

**The tree piece, four strokes**, full width at the foot: a horizontal, a vertical down through it, a left-falling stroke and a long right-falling one.

3 + 4 + 4 = eleven.
