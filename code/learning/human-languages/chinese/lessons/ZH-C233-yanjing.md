## You'll want to know: yǎnjìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C233-LOOKS233-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnjìng* — "glasses; spectacles".

> *wǒ de yǎnjìng ne* — Where are my glasses?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnjìng* is built from *yǎn* 'eye' and *jìng* 'mirror', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngeanX* and *kjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the eye'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
