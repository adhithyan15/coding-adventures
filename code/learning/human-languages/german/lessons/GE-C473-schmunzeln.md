## You'll want to know: schmunzeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C473-KICHERN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**schmunzeln** — "to smile quietly, to smirk".

> Der Lehrer schmunzelte über die Antwort.
>
> *The teacher smiled to himself at the answer.*

A quiet, amused smile with the lips closed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schmunzeln* is traced in EtymDB to Middle High German *smutzen* "to smile"; how the modern form arose is not settled.
