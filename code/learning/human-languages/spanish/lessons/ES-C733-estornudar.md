## You'll want to know: estornudar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C733-GRIPE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**estornudar** — "to sneeze".

> El polvo me hace estornudar.
>
> *Dust makes me sneeze.*

People say *¡Salud!* or *¡Jesús!* when someone sneezes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estornudar* is inherited from Latin *sternūtāre* "to sneeze" (EtymDB).
