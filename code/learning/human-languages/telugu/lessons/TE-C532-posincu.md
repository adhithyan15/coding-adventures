# పోషించు (pōṣiñcu) — to support; to provide for

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C532-PENPAKAM532-01, TE-LEX-C532-PENPAKAM532-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for upbringing, then the Telugu for pampering.

## You'll want to know: పోషించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C532-PENPAKAM532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పోషించు** — *pōṣiñcu* — "to support; to provide for".

> అన్నయ్యే కుటుంబాన్ని పోషించాడు.
>
> *annayyē kuṭumbānni pōṣiñcāḍu.*
>
> It was my elder brother who provided for the family.

Past (I …): పోషించాను, *pōṣiñcānu*.

Also to play a part: పాత్ర పోషించు, to play a role.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పోషించు** (*pōṣiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *poṣaṇa*, "nourishing, fostering, supporting" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
