## You'll want to know: खोडकर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C473-KHODKAR473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खोडकर** — *khoḍkar* — "naughty".

> खोडकर मुलगा वर्गात सारखा बोलत असतो.
>
> *khoḍkar mulgā vargāt sārkhā bolat asto.*
>
> The naughty boy keeps talking in class.

Built on खोड, a bad habit or prank (not the tree trunk): खोडी काढणे, to play a prank. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खोडकर** (*khoḍkar*) is a Marathi adjective, खोड, "mischief", with the ending *-kar*; the origin of खोड is not settled.

## What you've built
