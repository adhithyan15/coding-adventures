## You'll want to know: ਕੰਢਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C299-WATER299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੰਢਾ** — *kaṇḍhā* — "a riverbank; a shore".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਦਰਿਆ ਦੇ ਕੰਢੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਬੈਠਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *asīṁ dariā de kaṇḍhe utte baiṭhde hāṁ.*
>
> We sit on the bank of the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੰਢਾ** (*kaṇḍhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaṇṭha*, "throat, neck", also "border"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaṇḍhā*, "bank, shore", under it (CDIAL 2680).
