## You'll want to know: der Lappen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C526-PUTZEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Lappen** — "cloth, rag".

> Sie putzt das Fenster mit einem feuchten Lappen.
>
> *She cleans the window with a damp cloth.*

Plural: *die Lappen*. Informal for a driving licence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Lappen* is inherited from Middle High German *lappe* and Old High German *lappo*, from Proto-Germanic *lappa-* (EtymDB).
