## You'll want to know: じしょ

**じしょ** — *jisho* — "a dictionary".

> **がくせいは いつも じしょを つかいます。** — *gakusei wa itsumo jisho o tsukaimasu* — "The students always use a dictionary."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じしょ** (*jisho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "words" (Middle Chinese *zi*) and *sho*, "write" (*syo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
