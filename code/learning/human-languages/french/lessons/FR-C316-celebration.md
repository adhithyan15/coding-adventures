## You'll want to know: la célébration
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C316-CELEBRER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la célébration** — "the celebration".

> La célébration a duré toute la nuit.
>
> *The celebration went on all night.*

Often for a religious or official event.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*célébration* is a learned borrowing from Latin *celebrātiō* "a crowd, a festival", from *celebrāre* (Lewis & Short).
