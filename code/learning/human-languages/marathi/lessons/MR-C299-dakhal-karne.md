# दाखल करणे (dākhal karṇe) — to file; to admit

## You'll want to know: दाखल करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C299-TAPAS299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दाखल करणे** — *dākhal karṇe* — "to file; to admit".

> तिने पोलीस ठाण्यात तक्रार दाखल केली.
>
> *tine polīs ṭhāṇyāt takrār dākhal kelī.*
>
> She filed a complaint at the police station.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दाखल केलं, *tyāne dākhal kelaṁ*.

रुग्णालयात दाखल करणे, to admit to hospital; गुन्हा दाखल करणे, to register a case.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाखल करणे** (*dākhal karṇe*) joins *dākhal*, 'entered, admitted', a loan from Persian *dāxil*, usually traced to Arabic *dākhil*, 'entering', to *karṇe*, 'to do'.
