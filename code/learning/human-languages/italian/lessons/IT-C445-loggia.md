## You'll want to know: la loggia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C445-PIAZZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la loggia** — "the loggia, the open gallery".

> Dalla loggia del palazzo si vede la piazza.
>
> *The square is in view from the loggia of the palace.*

A covered space open on one side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*loggia* was borrowed from Old French *loge* 'arbour, covered walk' (EtymDB), from Frankish *laubja* 'arbour'.
