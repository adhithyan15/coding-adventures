German, chapter 144: To change, To phone, To stop, To go out, To look.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ändern — to change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for opening hours, then the German for a receipt.

You'll want to know: ändern.
ändern — "to change".

The word, taken apart.
ändern from Middle High German endern (EtymDB 2.1), from Old High German antarōn, "to make other", built on the word for "other", a cousin of English other.

You'll want to know: aufhören.
aufhören — "to stop".

The word, taken apart.
aufhören from Middle High German ūfhœren (EtymDB 2.1): auf + hören, "to hear". On the usual account, to stop was first to look up and listen.
