Lesson 1 of 5.
പാഠ്യപദ്ധതി (pāṭhyapaddhati) — a curriculum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a semester, then the Malayalam for a syllabus.

You'll want to know: പാഠ്യപദ്ധതി.
പാഠ്യപദ്ധതി — pāṭhyapaddhati — "a curriculum".
സർക്കാർ പുതിയ പാഠ്യപദ്ധതി കൊണ്ടുവന്നു. sarkkār putiya pāṭhyapaddhati koṇṭuvannu. The government brought in a new curriculum.
പദ്ധതി alone is a plan or a scheme.

The word, taken apart.
പാഠ്യപദ്ധതി (pāṭhyapaddhati) is a Malayalam compound built from Sanskrit pāṭhya, "to be recited, to be taught" (Monier-Williams), and paddhati, "a way, a path" (Monier-Williams): the path of what is taught.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ഹാജർ (hājar) — attendance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a syllabus, then the Malayalam for a curriculum.

You'll want to know: ഹാജർ.
ഹാജർ — hājar — "attendance".
ക്ലാസിൽ ഇന്ന് ഹാജർ കുറവായിരുന്നു. klāsil innŭ hājar kuṟavāyirunnu. Attendance in class was low today.
ഹാജരാകുക, to be present, is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
ഹാജർ (hājar) came from Arabic ḥāḍir, "present", and was borrowed from Hindustani hāzir; it is the noun inside ഹാജരാകുക.

Lesson 5 of 5.
പാസാകുക (pāsākuka) — to pass an exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a hostel, then the Malayalam for a scholarship.

You'll want to know: പാസാകുക.
പാസാകുക — pāsākuka — "to pass an exam".
അവൾ എല്ലാ വിഷയങ്ങളിലും പാസായി. avaḷ ellā viṣayaṅṅaḷiluṁ pāsāyi. She passed in all subjects.
Past: പാസായി, pāsāyi.
Its past is പാസായി; the causative പാസാക്കുക was taught a few chapters earlier.

The word, taken apart.
പാസാകുക (pāsākuka) is a Malayalam compound: the first part was borrowed from English pass, and ആകുക, "to become", is native Dravidian (DEDR 333).
