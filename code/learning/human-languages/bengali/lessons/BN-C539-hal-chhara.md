# হাল ছাড়া (hāl chhāṛā) — to give up hope; to stop trying

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C539-BIGHNO539-02, BN-LEX-C539-BIGHNO539-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for persistent, then the Bengali for desperate.

## You'll want to know: হাল ছাড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C539-BIGHNO539-04]; assesses=[] -->

**হাল ছাড়া** — *hāl chhāṛā* — "to give up hope; to stop trying".

> হাল না ছাড়লে তোমার দল জিতবে।
>
> *Hāl nā chhāṛle tomār dôl jitbe.*
>
> If your team doesn't give up, it will win.

Past (he/she …): তিনি হাল ছাড়লেন না, *tini hāl chhāṛlen nā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হাল ছাড়া** (*hāl chhāṛā*) joins হাল, "the helm, the rudder", to ছাড়া, "to let go": to let go of the helm. Where হাল in this sense comes from is not settled.
