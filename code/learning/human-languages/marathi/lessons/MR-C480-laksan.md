## You'll want to know: लक्षण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C480-TABYET480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लक्षण** — *lakṣaṇ* — "a symptom; a sign".

> ताप आणि सर्दी ही कशाची लक्षणं आहेत?
>
> *tāp āṇi sardī hī kaśācī lakṣaṇaṁ āhet?*
>
> What are fever and a cold symptoms of?

Neuter.

Neuter; plural लक्षणं. A good sign is चांगलं लक्षण.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लक्षण** (*lakṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *lakṣaṇa*, "line, mark" (CDIAL 10882).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
