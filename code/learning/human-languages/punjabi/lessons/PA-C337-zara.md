# ਜ਼ਰਾ (zarā) — a bit (to soften a request); a moment

## You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C337-ASK337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਰਾ** — *zarā* — "a bit (to soften a request); a moment".

> ਜ਼ਰਾ ਰੁਕੋ ਅਤੇ ਹੁਣ ਨਾ ਬੋਲੋ।
>
> *zarā ruko ate huṇ nā bolo.*
>
> Wait a moment and don't speak now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਰਾ** (*zarā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *dharra*, "a tiny particle".
