## You'll want to know: সেল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C424-DAM424-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সেল** — *sel* — "a sale (at reduced prices)".

> পুজোর আগে দোকানে সেল চলে।
>
> *pujor āge dokāne sel chôle.*
>
> Before the Pujas the shops have sales on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সেল** (*sel*) is borrowed from English *sale*, in the shop-window sense of prices cut for a time.
