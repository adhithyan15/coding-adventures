## You'll want to know: মতভেদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C365-SOMJHOTA365-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মতভেদ** — *môtbhed* — "a difference of opinion".

> এই বিষয়ে আমাদের মতভেদ আছে।
>
> *ei bishôye āmāder môtbhed āchhe.*
>
> We disagree on this subject.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মতভেদ** (*môtbhed*) is Sanskrit *mata*, an opinion, with *bheda*, a split or difference (Monier-Williams): where opinions part.
