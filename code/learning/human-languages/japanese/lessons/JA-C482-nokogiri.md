## You'll want to know: のこぎり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C482-NAIL482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**のこぎり** — *nokogiri* — "a saw".

> **ちちは のこぎりを つかいます。** — *chichi wa nokogiri o tsukaimasu* — "My father uses a saw."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のこぎり** (*nokogiri*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
