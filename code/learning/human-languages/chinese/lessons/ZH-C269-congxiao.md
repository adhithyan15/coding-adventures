## You'll want to know: 从小
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C269-YOUNG269-01]; assesses=[] -->

**从小** — *cóngxiǎo* — "since childhood; since I was little".

> **我从小喜欢看书** *wǒ cóngxiǎo xǐhuan kànshū* — I've loved books since I was little.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**从小** (*cóngxiǎo*) is built from 从 'from' and 小 'small', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjowng* and *sjewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
