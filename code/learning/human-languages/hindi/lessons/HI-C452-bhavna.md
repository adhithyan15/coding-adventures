## You'll want to know: भावना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C452-BHAVNA452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भावना** — *bhāvnā* — "a feeling; an emotion".

> हर इंसान की भावना अलग होती है।
>
> *har insān kī bhāvnā alag hotī hai.*
>
> Every person's feelings are different.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भावना** (*bhāvnā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *bhāvanā*, which Monier-Williams gives with senses such as imagining and a feeling of devotion (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for any feeling.
