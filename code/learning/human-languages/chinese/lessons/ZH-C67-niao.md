## You'll want to know: niǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C67-ANIMALS67-04]; assesses=[] -->

*niǎo* — "bird".

> *wǒ xǐhuan kàn niǎo* — I love watching birds.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed, through an older book, as the name for all long-tailed birds. Why the modern word begins with n is not settled.
