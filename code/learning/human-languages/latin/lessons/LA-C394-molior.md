## You'll want to know: mōlior, mōlīrī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C394-APPARO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**mōlior, mōlīrī** — "to set about (with effort), to undertake; to plot".

> Magnam rem mōlītur.
>
> *He is setting about a great undertaking.*

Deponent: *mōlior, mōlīrī, mōlītus sum*, built on *mōlēs*, a mass: to heave a heavy thing. English *demolish* comes from *dēmōlior*, to pull down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mōlior* is built on *mōlēs* "a mass, a heavy weight" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to move a great weight. English *demolish* comes from its compound *dēmōlior*.
