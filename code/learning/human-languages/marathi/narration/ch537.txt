Marathi, chapter 537: On Stage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अभिनय (abhinay) — acting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to hum, then the Marathi for to practise music.

You'll want to know: अभिनय.
अभिनय — abhinay — "acting".
त्या अभिनेत्रीचा अभिनय सगळ्यांना आवडला. tyā abhinetrīcā abhinay sagḷyānnā āvaḍlā. Everyone liked that actress's acting.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अभिनय (abhinay) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit abhinaya, "expressing a feeling on stage, acting" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

You'll want to know: वेशभूषा.
वेशभूषा — veśbhūṣā — "a costume".
नाटकासाठी मुलांनी जुन्या काळची वेशभूषा केली. nāṭkāsāṭhī mulānnī junyā kāḷcī veśbhūṣā kelī. For the play the children dressed in costumes of the old days.
Feminine.
वेशभूषा (veśbhūṣā) करणे, to put on a costume.

The word, taken apart.
वेशभूषा (veśbhūṣā) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit veśa, "dress", and bhūṣā, "ornament" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
साकारणे (sākārṇe) — to portray; to bring to life.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rehearsal, then the Marathi for a costume.

You'll want to know: साकारणे.
साकारणे — sākārṇe — "to portray; to bring to life".
तिने आईची भूमिका खूप छान साकारली. tine āīcī bhūmikā khūp chān sākārlī. She portrayed the mother beautifully.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भूमिका साकारली, tyāne bhūmikā sākārlī.

The word, taken apart.
साकारणे (sākārṇe) is built on Sanskrit sākāra, "having form" (Monier-Williams): to give a role a shape.
