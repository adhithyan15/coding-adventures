Punjabi, chapter 279: Taking Something Back.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਖ਼ਰਾਬ (kharāb) — faulty; spoilt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a packet, then the Punjabi for to add up.

You'll want to know: ਖ਼ਰਾਬ.
ਖ਼ਰਾਬ — kharāb — "faulty; spoilt".
ਮੇਰਾ ਨਵਾਂ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਖ਼ਰਾਬ ਹੈ। merā navāṁ fon kharāb hai. My new phone is faulty.

The word, taken apart.
ਖ਼ਰਾਬ (kharāb) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic kharāb, "ruin".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਵਟਾਉਣਾ (vaṭāuṇā) — to exchange; to swap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for fake, then the Punjabi for genuine.

You'll want to know: ਵਟਾਉਣਾ.
ਵਟਾਉਣਾ — vaṭāuṇā — "to exchange; to swap".
ਮੈਂ ਖ਼ਰਾਬ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਵਟਾਵਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ kharāb fon kall vaṭāvāṅgā. I will exchange the faulty phone tomorrow.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਵਟਾਈ, maiṁ juttī vaṭāī.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਟਾਉਣਾ (vaṭāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit vartayati, "causes to turn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi baṭāuṇā, "to cause to be twisted, change", under it (CDIAL 11356).
