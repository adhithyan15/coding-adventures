## You'll want to know: kǒuzhào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C245-MASK245-03]; assesses=[] -->

*kǒuzhào* — "face mask".

> *nǐ yǒu kǒuzhào ma* — Have you got a face mask?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kǒuzhào* is built from 口 'mouth' and *zhào* 'a cover', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khuwX* and *traewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a bamboo basket for catching fish, so how it came to mean a cover is not settled.
