Telugu, chapter 155: Next, From Now On, the Future, To Hope, To Look Forward To.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వచ్చే (vaccē) — next, coming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for once, then the Telugu for to spend.

You'll want to know: వచ్చే.
వచ్చే — vaccē — "next, coming". The future for "I" ends in -తాను (-tānu): వచ్చే వారం నేను విజయవాడ వెళ్తాను — vaccē vāraṁ nēnu vijayavāḍa veḷtānu — "Next week I will go to Vijayawada."

The word, taken apart.
వచ్చే (vaccē) is native Dravidian: the "which is coming" form of vaccu, "to come" (DEDR 5270).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఇకపై (ikapai) — from now on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to spend, then the Telugu for next.

You'll want to know: ఇకపై.
ఇకపై — ikapai — "from now on". ఇకపై నేను త్వరగా లేస్తాను — ikapai nēnu tvaragā lēstānu — "From now on I will get up early."

The word, taken apart.
ఇకపై (ikapai) is native Dravidian: ika, "hereafter, henceforth", which DEDR 410 lists, and pai, "upon; in future" (DEDR 4451).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for next, then the Telugu for from now on.

You'll want to know: భవిష్యత్తు.
భవిష్యత్తు — bhaviṣyattu — "the future". భవిష్యత్తులో నేను ఉపాధ్యాయుడిని అవుతాను — bhaviṣyattulō nēnu upādhyāyuḍini avutānu — "In the future I will become a teacher."

The word, taken apart.
భవిష్యత్తు (bhaviṣyattu) is Sanskrit bhaviṣyat, "that which will be", with Telugu's added -u.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఆశించు (āśiñcu) — to hope, to expect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for from now on, then the Telugu for the future.

You'll want to know: ఆశించు.
ఆశించు — āśiñcu — "to hope, to expect". మీరు బాగున్నారని ఆశిస్తున్నాను — mīru bāgunnārani āśistunnānu — "I hope you are well."

The word, taken apart.
ఆశించు (āśiñcu) is Sanskrit āśā, "wish, expectation" (CDIAL 1456), made into a verb with the Telugu ending -iñcu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఎదురుచూడు (edurucūḍu) — to look forward to, to wait for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the future, then the Telugu for to hope.

You'll want to know: ఎదురుచూడు.
ఎదురుచూడు — edurucūḍu — "to look forward to, to wait for". నేను మీ ఉత్తరం కోసం ఎదురుచూస్తున్నాను — nēnu mī uttaraṁ kōsaṁ edurucūstunnānu — "I am looking forward to your letter."

The word, taken apart.
ఎదురుచూడు (edurucūḍu) is native Dravidian: eduru, "the front, the point opposite", one word with Tamil etir (DEDR 795), and cūḍu, "to look" (DEDR 2735).
