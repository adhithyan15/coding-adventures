## You'll want to know: آمادگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C397-EHTIYAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آمادگی** (*âmâdegi*) — "readiness, preparation".

> تیم ما آمادگی کامل دارد.
>
> *tim-e mâ âmâdegi-ye kâmel dârad.*
>
> Our team is fully prepared.

From *âmâde* "ready" + *-gi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آمادگی** is native Persian, built on *âmâde* "ready" with the noun suffix *-gi*.
