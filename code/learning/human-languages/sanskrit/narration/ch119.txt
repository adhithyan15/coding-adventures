Sanskrit, chapter 119: Ladle, Frying pan, Vessel, Bottle, Small umbrella.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
दर्वी (darvī) — a ladle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a chair, then the Sanskrit for a board.

You'll want to know: दर्वी.
दर्वी — darvī — "a ladle".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does दर्वी (darvī) mean? ("A ladle".) Say it once more.
