# ప్రత్యక్షమవు (pratyakṣamavu) — to appear suddenly

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C353-ADRSYAM353-02, TE-LEX-C353-ADRSYAM353-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to vanish, then the Telugu for to fade from view.

## You'll want to know: ప్రత్యక్షమవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C353-ADRSYAM353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రత్యక్షమవు** — *pratyakṣamavu* — "to appear suddenly".

> మాయమైన కుక్క మరుసటి రోజు ప్రత్యక్షమైంది.
>
> *māyamaina kukka marusaṭi rōju pratyakṣamaindi.*
>
> The dog that had vanished turned up the next day.

Past (I …): ప్రత్యక్షమయ్యాను, *pratyakṣamayyānu*.

ప్రత్యక్షం, before the eyes, with అవు, to become.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రత్యక్షమవు** (*pratyakṣamavu*) is built on Sanskrit *pratyakṣa*, "before the eyes, visible" (CDIAL 8635), with the Telugu verb *avu*, "to become".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
