## You'll want to know: きんがく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C359-SHAKKIN359-05]; assesses=[] -->

**きんがく** — *kingaku* — "a sum of money; an amount".

> **きんがくが かわったら、れんらくします。** — *kingaku ga kawattara, renraku shimasu* — "If the amount changes, I will let you know."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きんがく** (*kingaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kin*, "gold" (Middle Chinese *kim*) and *gaku*, "sum" (*ngaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
