# ਹਟਣਾ (haṭṇā) — to move out of the way; to step back

## You'll want to know: ਹਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C253-RULE253-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਟਣਾ** — *haṭṇā* — "to move out of the way; to step back".

> ਲੋਕ ਬੱਸ ਲਈ ਸੜਕ ਤੋਂ ਹਟਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *lok bass laī saṛak ton haṭde han.*
>
> People move off the road for the bus.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸੜਕ ਤੋਂ ਹਟਿਆ, *maiṁ saṛak ton haṭiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਟਣਾ** (*haṭṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*haṭṭ*, "move"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *haṭṇā*, "to move back or out of the way", under it (CDIAL 13943).
