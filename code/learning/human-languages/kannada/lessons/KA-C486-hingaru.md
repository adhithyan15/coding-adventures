# ಹಿಂಗಾರು (hiṅgāru) — the late monsoon; the retreating rains

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C485-MUNGARU485-04, KA-LEX-C485-MUNGARU485-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a downpour, then the Kannada for to get soaked.

## You'll want to know: ಹಿಂಗಾರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C486-HINGARU486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಂಗಾರು** — *hiṅgāru* — "the late monsoon; the retreating rains".

> ಹಿಂಗಾರು ಮಳೆ ಮುಂಗಾರಿನ ನಂತರ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *hiṅgāru maḷe muṅgārina nantara baruttade.*
>
> The late monsoon rain comes after the early monsoon.

Its partner is ಮುಂಗಾರು, the early rains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಂಗಾರು** (*hiṅgāru*) is a native Kannada compound of *pin*, *him*, "behind, later" (DEDR 4205), and *kār*, "the rainy season" (DEDR 1278).
