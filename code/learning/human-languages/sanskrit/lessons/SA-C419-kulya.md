## You'll want to know: कुल्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C419-VAPI419-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुल्या** — *kulyā* — "a water channel; a canal".

> कुल्या क्षेत्रेषु जलं वहति।
>
> *kulyā kṣetreṣu jalaṃ vahati.*
>
> The channel carries water to the fields.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुल्या** (*kulyā*) is the word MW gives for a small river, a canal or a channel for water; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
