## You'll want to know: کارت پرواز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C431-PARVAZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کارت پرواز** (*kârt-e parvâz*) — "a boarding pass, a boarding card".

> کارت پرواز را در موبایلم دارم.
>
> *Kârt-e parvâz râ dar mobâylam dâram.*
>
> I have the boarding pass on my phone.

*kârt* 'card' + *parvâz* 'flight'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kârt-e parvâz* is built from *kârt* 'card', which EtymDB takes from French *carte*, and native *parvâz* 'flight'.
