Arabic, chapter 135: Gazelle, Fox, Pigeon, Duck, Turtle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غزال (ghazāl) — a gazelle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a cloud, then the Arabic for a rabbit.

You'll want to know: غزال.
غزال — ghazāl — "a gazelle".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ghazāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ghazāl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ghazāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a cloud, then the Arabic for a rabbit, then say ghazāl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does غزال (ghazāl) mean? ("A gazelle".) Say it once more.
