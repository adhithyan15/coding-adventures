# ఆశ్రయించు (āśrayiñcu) — to turn to; to resort to

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C426-ARAMBHAM426-01, TE-LEX-C426-ARAMBHAM426-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hold back, then the Telugu for to be willing.

## You'll want to know: ఆశ్రయించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C426-ARAMBHAM426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆశ్రయించు** — *āśrayiñcu* — "to turn to; to resort to".

> న్యాయం కోసం వాళ్ళు కోర్టును ఆశ్రయించారు.
>
> *nyāyaṁ kōsaṁ vāḷḷu kōrṭunu āśrayiñcāru.*
>
> They turned to the court for justice.

Past (I …): ఆశ్రయించాను, *āśrayiñcānu*.

For turning to someone for help or shelter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆశ్రయించు** (*āśrayiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *āśraya*, "resting place, refuge" (CDIAL 1466), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; విమానాశ్రయం, an airport, ends in the same word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
