Japanese, chapter 44: Island, Wave, Soil, Seed, Rain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しま (shima) — an island.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a cloud; a spider, then the Japanese for the sun.

You'll want to know: しま.
しま — shima — "an island".

The word, taken apart.
しま (shima) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 5 of 5.
あめ (ame) — rain; candy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for soil, then the Japanese for a seed.

You'll want to know: あめ.
あめ — ame — "rain; candy".

The word, taken apart.
あめ (ame) is two native words that sound alike: ame, "rain", and ame, "candy", each with its own kanji (JMdict). Both are inherited rather than borrowed, and are told apart by their pitch.
