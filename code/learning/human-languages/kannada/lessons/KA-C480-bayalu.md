## You'll want to know: ಬಯಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C480-BAYALU480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಯಲು** — *bayalu* — "an open plain; open ground".

> ಊರಿನ ಹೊರಗೆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಬಯಲು ಇದೆ.
>
> *ūrina horage doḍḍa bayalu ide.*
>
> Outside the town there is a big open plain.

ಬಯಲುಸೀಮೆ, the plains country, as against the hills.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಯಲು** (*bayalu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5258 lists Kannada *bayal*, *bayil*, "open space, a field, a plain".
