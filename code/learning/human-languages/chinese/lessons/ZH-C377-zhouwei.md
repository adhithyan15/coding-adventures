## You'll want to know: zhōuwéi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C377-AREA377-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōuwéi* — "surroundings; the area around".

> *xiǎoqū zhōuwéi yǒu fàndiàn yě yǒu chāoshì* — Round the estate there are restaurants, and a supermarket too.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōuwéi* is built from *zhōu* 'all round' and *wéi* 'to surround', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyuw* and *hjw+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go all the way round' and the second 'to encircle'.
