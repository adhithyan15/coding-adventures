# વળગવું (vaḷagvũ) — to cling to; to hug tight

## You'll want to know: વળગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C451-VALAG451-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વળગવું** — *vaḷagvũ* — "to cling to; to hug tight".

> બાળક બીકથી માતાને વળગી પડ્યું.
>
> *bāḷak bīkthī mātāne vaḷgī paḍyũ.*
>
> The child clung to its mother in fear.

Past (he …): તે માતાને વળગી પડ્યો, *te mātāne vaḷgī paḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વળગવું** (*vaḷagvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*vilagyati*, "hangs on to"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to adhere to, seize" (CDIAL 11881).
