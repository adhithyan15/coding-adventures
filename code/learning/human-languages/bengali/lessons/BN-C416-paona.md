## You'll want to know: পাওনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C416-KISTI416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পাওনা** — *pāonā* — "money owed to one; one's dues".

> কাজ শেষ করে মিস্ত্রি তার পাওনা চাইলেন।
>
> *kāj shesh kore mistri tār pāonā chāilen.*
>
> When the job was done, the mechanic asked for what he was owed.

The pair দেনা and পাওনা, what you owe and what you are owed, often go together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাওনা** (*pāonā*) is built on **পাওয়া**, "to get", from Sanskrit *prāpayati* (CDIAL 8943): what one is due to get.
