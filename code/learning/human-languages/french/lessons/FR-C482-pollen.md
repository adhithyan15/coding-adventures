## You'll want to know: le pollen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C482-ALLERGIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le pollen** — "pollen".

> En mai, il y a beaucoup de pollen dans l'air.
>
> *In May there is a lot of pollen in the air.*

The final *n* is sounded: *pol-lène*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le pollen* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pollen* "fine flour, powder" (Lewis & Short).
