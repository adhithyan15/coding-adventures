## You'll want to know: commémorer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C326-LIBERATION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**commémorer** — "to commemorate".

> Chaque année, la ville commémore la fin de la guerre.
>
> *Every year, the town commemorates the end of the war.*

The noun is *la commémoration*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*commémorer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commemorāre* "to call to mind" (EtymDB), from *con-* and *memor* "mindful".
