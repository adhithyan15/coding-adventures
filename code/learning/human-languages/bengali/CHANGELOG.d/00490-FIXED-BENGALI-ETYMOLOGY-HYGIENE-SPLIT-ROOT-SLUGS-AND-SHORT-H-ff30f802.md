## Fixed — Bengali etymology hygiene: split root slugs and short hooks

- **BN-C207-dokandar** moves from `persian-dar` to `persian-dar-holder`, the slug 17 lessons use for *-dār* "holder"; `persian-dar` also named *dar* "door" and *dar* "in".
- **BN-C97-dabi** moves from `arabic-dawa` to a new `arabic-dawa-claim`. *Dābi* is Arabic *daʿwā* "claim", while every other `arabic-dawa` lesson is *dawāʾ* "medicine".
- **`indo-aryan-bolla` retired.** BN-C05-ami-bangla-boli, BN-C05-bola and BN-C19-abar-bolben now use `bol-speak`, the slug Gujarati, Hindi, Marathi, Punjabi and Urdu use for the same *\*boll-* (CDIAL 9321).
- **আসা (BN-C07-asha).** The block said Hindi *ānā* is *ā-* on *yā-*; CDIAL files *ānā* under *āpayati* (CDIAL 1200), so that sentence and the wrap-up answer that leaned on it are gone. The *ā-* "hither" in *āsā* and its CDIAL and Chatterji accounts stay.
- **Hooks cut to 120 characters.** BN-C06-numbers-1-5, BN-C07-asha, BN-C18-ekmot, BN-C19-jokhon and BN-C20-arekta, each keeping its origin claim and its CDIAL number.
