# ಝಾಡಿಸು (jhāḍisu) — to shake out; to dust off

## You'll want to know: ಝಾಡಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C545-BATTE545-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಝಾಡಿಸು** — *jhāḍisu* — "to shake out; to dust off".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಜಮಖಾನವನ್ನು ಹೊರಗೆ ಝಾಡಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *ajji jamakhānavannu horage jhāḍisidaru.*
>
> Grandmother shook out the rug outside.

Past (I …): ಝಾಡಿಸಿದೆ, *jhāḍiside*.

To shake or beat the dust out of something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಝಾಡಿಸು** (*jhāḍisu*) is usually traced to Hindustani *jhāṛnā*, "to dust, to shake off", with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
