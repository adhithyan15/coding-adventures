## You'll want to know: تابعیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C410-LAYEHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تابعیت** (*tâbe'iyat*) — "citizenship, nationality".

> او تابعیت دو کشور را دارد.
>
> *u tâbe'iyat-e do keshvar râ dârad.*
>
> He has the citizenship of two countries.

*Tâbe'iyyat gereftan* — to become a citizen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تابعیت** is usually traced to Arabic *tābi'iyya*, built on *tābi'* "following, subject to": a citizen belongs to a state.
