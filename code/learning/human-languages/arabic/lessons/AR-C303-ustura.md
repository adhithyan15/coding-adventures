## You'll want to know: أسطورة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C303-USTURA303-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أسطورة** — *usṭūra* — "a legend".

> سمعنا أسطورة عن جبل قديم.
>
> *samiʿnā usṭūra ʿan jabal qadīm.*
>
> We heard a legend about an old mountain.

Plural: أساطير, *asāṭīr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أسطورة** (*usṭūra*) is filed by Lane under the root *s-ṭ-r*, "a line of writing", and its plural *asāṭīr* is old. It was borrowed from Greek *historia*, "an inquiry, a story" (EtymDB); others see a native word, so the origin is not settled.
