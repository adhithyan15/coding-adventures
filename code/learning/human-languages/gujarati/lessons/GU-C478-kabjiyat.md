## You'll want to know: કબજિયાત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C478-KAPH478-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કબજિયાત** — *kabjiyāt* — "constipation".

> દાક્તરે કહ્યું કે કબજિયાત માટે વધારે પાણી પીઓ.
>
> *dāktare kahyũ ke kabjiyāt māṭe vadhāre pāṇī pīo.*
>
> The doctor said to drink more water for constipation.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કબજિયાત** (*kabjiyāt*) is usually traced to Arabic *qabḍ*, "contraction, seizing", through Persian, with an abstract ending.
