## You'll want to know: दिसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C203-STEPS203-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दिसा** — *disā* — "a direction".

> टेसण किसी दिसा मांय है?
>
> *ṭesaṇ kisī disā mā̃y hai?*
>
> Which way is the station?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिसा** (*disā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *diśā*, "direction, region". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *dis*, "direction, side" (CDIAL 6339).
