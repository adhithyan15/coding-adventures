# ઓસરવું (osarvũ) — to recede, to go down

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to overflow, then the Gujarati for flood.

## You'll want to know: ઓસરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C220-RIVER220-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓસરવું** — *osarvũ* — "to recede, to go down".

> **બે દિવસમાં પાણી ઓસરશે.** — *be divasmā̃ pāṇī osarśe* — "The water will go down in two days."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓસરવું** (*osarvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *apasarati*, "slips off from, goes away", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to recede, decrease, subside" (CDIAL 465).
