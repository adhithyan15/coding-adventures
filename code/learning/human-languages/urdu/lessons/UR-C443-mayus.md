# مایوس (māyūs) — disappointed; without hope

## You'll want to know: مایوس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C443-SAD443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مایوس** — *māyūs* — "disappointed; without hope".

> امتحان کا نتیجہ دیکھ کر وہ مایوس ہوا۔
>
> *imtihān kā natījā dekh kar voh māyūs huā.*
>
> He was disappointed when he saw the exam result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مایوس** (*māyūs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maʾyūs*, "given up as hopeless".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
