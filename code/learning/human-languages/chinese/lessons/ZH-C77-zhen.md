## You'll want to know: zhēn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C77-THANKS77-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhēn* — "really; truly".

> *nǐ zhēn hǎo* — You're so kind.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhēn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'true, as against false'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for thanking and answering thanks.
