## You'll want to know: कोठो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C187-KOTHO187-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कोठो** — *koṭho* — "a storeroom".

> म्हारो कोठो छोटो है।
>
> *mhāro koṭho chhoṭo hai.*
>
> My storeroom is small.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोठो** (*koṭho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *koṣṭha*, "a granary, a storeroom"; CDIAL lists Marwari *koṭho*, "a room" (CDIAL 3546).
