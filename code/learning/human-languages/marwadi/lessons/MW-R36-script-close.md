<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Everything this slice added, from dictation, with no model in view.

1. The letter: **ए**, and the mark **े** beside it.
2. The twelve words, shuffled, from **एक** to **सो**.
3. The ten figures, shuffled, from **०** to **९**.
4. The two turns: **बीस।** and **दस करो।**

Repair only what missed. Then say what the whole slice cost the hand: one
letter and ten figures, against twelve numbers and a bargaining turn that
finally has a figure in it.
