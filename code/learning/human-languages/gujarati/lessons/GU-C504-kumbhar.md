## You'll want to know: કુંભાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C504-KUMBHAR504-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કુંભાર** — *kumbhār* — "a potter".

> અમારા ગામમાં એક કુંભાર રહે છે.
>
> *amārā gāmmā̃ ek kumbhār rahe che.*
>
> A potter lives in our village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કુંભાર** (*kumbhār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kumbhakāra*, "a pot-maker", built from *kumbha*, "a pot", and *kāra*, "maker"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 3310).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
