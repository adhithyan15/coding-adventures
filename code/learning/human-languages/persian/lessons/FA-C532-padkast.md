## You'll want to know: پادکست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C532-WATCHING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پادکست** (*pâdkast*) — "a podcast".

> او در راه کار پادکست گوش میدهد.
>
> *u dar râh-e kâr pâdkast gush midahad.*
>
> He listens to podcasts on the way to work.

Plural *pâdkasthâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pâdkast* was borrowed from English *podcast* (EtymDB).
