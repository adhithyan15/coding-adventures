## You'll want to know: કિશોર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C494-BALPAN494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કિશોર** — *kiśor* — "a teenage boy".

> તે કિશોર ઘણો હોશિયાર છે.
>
> *te kiśor ghaṇo hośiyār che.*
>
> That teenage boy is very clever.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કિશોર** (*kiśor*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kiśora*, which Monier-Williams gives first as "a colt" and then as a youth.
