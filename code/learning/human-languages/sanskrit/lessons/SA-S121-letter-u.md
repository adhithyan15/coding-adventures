# उ — one character, met inside words you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-120] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: ज — what does it do?

[PAUSE 2s] One character this time. One only — and you have been saying it
for pages without knowing which mark on the page it was.

## Script you'll notice: उ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-121]; assesses=[] -->

**उ** — *u*.

It is an **independent vowel** — the shape the vowel *u* takes when a word begins with it, rather than the sign it becomes inside a word.

What it is made of:

- a joined upper bowl and lower loop
- the top shirorekhā

You already say these, and every one of them has उ somewhere inside it:

- **उत्तम** *uttama* — good, best
- **उदक** *udaka* — water — the other word for it
- **उपसर्ग** *upasarga* — a prefix, the piece that turns गच्छति into आगच्छति

You have read the *u* sound many times without ever meeting this shape, because
inside a word it is a small hook tucked under the consonant instead. उ is what it
looks like when a word opens on it and there is no consonant to sit under.

## Writing: उ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-121] -->

- **1.** start at the headline junction, curve down and left around the upper bowl, then sweep back through the waist and around the lower loop without lifting
- **2.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 1.** The pen comes up 1 times and no more.

> Verified modern printed teaching form; everyday handwriting may simplify the curves.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari उ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–2, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.
