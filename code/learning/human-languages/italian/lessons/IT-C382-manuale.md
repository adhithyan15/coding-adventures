## You'll want to know: il manuale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C382-RIPASSO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il manuale** — "the textbook, the manual".

> Il manuale di storia ha cinquecento pagine.
>
> *The history textbook has five hundred pages.*

As an adjective: *lavoro manuale* — work with your hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manuale* is a learned borrowing from Latin *manuālis* "of the hand, held in the hand" (Lewis & Short), from *manus* "hand": a book to keep at hand.
