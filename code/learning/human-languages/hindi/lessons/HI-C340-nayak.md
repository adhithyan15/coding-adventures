## You'll want to know: नायक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C340-UPANYAS340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नायक** — *nāyak* — "a hero (of a story); a leader".

> उपन्यास का नायक एक ग़रीब किसान है।
>
> *upanyās kā nāyak ek ġarīb kisān hai.*
>
> The hero of the novel is a poor farmer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नायक** (*nāyak*) is Sanskrit *nāyaka*, "a leader", and already in Sanskrit drama the hero (Monier-Williams).
