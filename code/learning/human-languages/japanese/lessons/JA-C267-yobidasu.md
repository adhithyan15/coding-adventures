## You'll want to know: よびだす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C267-MAIGO267-02]; assesses=[] -->

**よびだす** — *yobidasu* — "to call (someone) out; to page".

> **えきいんが ははを よびだしました。** — *ekiin ga haha o yobidashimashita* — "A station attendant paged my mother."

Polite **よびだします**, *yobidashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが ははを よびだしました。**, *sensei ga haha o yobidashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よびだす** (*yobidasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yobi*, "calling" (from *yobu*), and *dasu*, "to put out" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
