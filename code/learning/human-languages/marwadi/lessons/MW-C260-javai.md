# जंवाई (jãvāī) — a son-in-law

## You'll want to know: जंवाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C260-BAHU260-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जंवाई** — *jãvāī* — "a son-in-law".

> जंवाई काल आयो।
>
> *jãvāī kāl āyo.*
>
> Our son-in-law came yesterday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जंवाई** (*jãvāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jāmātṛ*, "a daughter's husband". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *jãwāī* (CDIAL 5198).
