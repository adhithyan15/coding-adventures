## You'll want to know: بوق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C421-BUGH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بوق** (*buq*) — "a horn (of a car)".

> بوق این ماشین خیلی بلند است.
>
> *Buq-e in mâshin kheyli boland ast.*
>
> This car's horn is very loud.

Also an old trumpet made of horn or brass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*buq* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *būq* 'a trumpet'. EtymDB does not list the word, so the line is hedged.
