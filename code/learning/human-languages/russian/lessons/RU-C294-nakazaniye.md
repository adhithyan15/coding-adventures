## You'll want to know: наказание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C294-NAKAZANIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**наказание** (*nakazániye*) — "a punishment".

> Наказание было справедливым.
>
> *Nakazániye býlo spravedlívym.*
>
> The punishment was fair.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наказание* is built on *наказать*, from *на-* and *казать* "to show, to tell", inherited from Proto-Slavic *kazati* (EtymDB): first "an instruction", then a punishment.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
