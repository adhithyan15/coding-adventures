## You'll want to know: பல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C45-BODY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**பல்** (*pal*) — "a tooth".

Proto-Dravidian **\*pal**, and the sisters pulled it three ways. Malayalam പല്ല്, Kannada ಹಲ್ಲು, Telugu పన్ను. Kannada is the odd one again, with the same *p-* to *h-* shift the fruit-word showed; Telugu turned the *l* into an *n*. Tamil stops the word dead on its *l* and adds nothing.

In everyday speech it often comes out *pallu*, with the *l* doubled — which is exactly the shape Malayalam and Kannada put on the page.
