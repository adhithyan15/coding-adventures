# ર — one new shape, ra

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-NAMASTE-READ-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the inherent-vowel rule once: a bare Gujarati consonant carries
short *a*. Keep that rule; add only one shape.

## Script — meet the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-RA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ર** visible. It is **ra**: *r* plus the inherent *a*. Begin at the upper
left, circle clockwise around the rounded upper body, continue through the
small middle loop, and descend into the lower-right tail without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ર** once. Then trace it once in the air. Say *ra* only after the
motion stops.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **ર** visible and copy it once. Compare only three landmarks: rounded top,
small middle loop, descending tail. If one differs, repair that part.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W02-ra-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-RA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model. Write the Gujarati consonant ra from memory.","answer":"ર","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ર — rounded top, small loop, descending tail.","incorrect":"Look once at ર, hide it again, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Cover the model. Write *ra* once from memory, then uncover **ર** and check the
three landmarks. Stop after one honest repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What vowel comes free with **ર**? (**Short a.**) Name the three
landmarks without writing again. (**Rounded top, small loop, descending tail.**)
