## You'll want to know: مظاهرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C353-MUZAHARA353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مظاهرة** — *muẓāhara* — "a demonstration, a march".

> سار الطلاب في مظاهرة سلمية.
>
> *sāra aṭ-ṭullāb fī muẓāhara silmiyya.*
>
> The students walked in a peaceful march.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مظاهرة** (*muẓāhara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ẓ-h-r* (Lane), the root of *ẓahr*, the back, and *ẓahara*, to appear. Form III *ẓāhara* is "to back someone up"; a public show of support is the modern sense.
