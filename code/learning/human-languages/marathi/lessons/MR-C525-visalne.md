## You'll want to know: विसळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C525-BHANDI525-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विसळणे** — *visaḷṇe* — "to rinse".

> चहा पिऊन झाल्यावर त्याने कप विसळला.
>
> *cahā piūn jhālyāvar tyāne kap visaḷlā.*
>
> After drinking his tea he rinsed the cup.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कप विसळला, *tyāne kap visaḷlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विसळणे** (*visaḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *visaḷṇẽ*, "to rinse", under *\*vikṣalati*, "rinses" (CDIAL 11645).
