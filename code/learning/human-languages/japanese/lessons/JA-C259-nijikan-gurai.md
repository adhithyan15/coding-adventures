# にじかんぐらい / くじから ごじまで (nijikan gurai / kuji kara goji made) — how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-02, JA-LEX-C55-TIME55-01, JA-LEX-C185-SALE185-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "time, an hour" and "a minute". (*jikan*, *fun*.) Then "to take", of time. (*kakaru*.)

## You'll want to know: 〜じかんぐらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C259-DURATION-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C203-FLY203-02, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-EKI, JA-LEX-C138-ARUKIMASU] -->

> **くうこうまで にじかん かかります。**
>
> *kūkō made nijikan kakarimasu.* — "It takes two hours to the airport."

A number before **じかん** counts hours: **にじかん** (*nijikan*), two hours. Before **ふん** it counts minutes: **ごふん** (*gofun*), five minutes. **ぐらい** (*gurai*), "about", follows the time:

> **くうこうまで にじかんぐらい かかります。**
>
> *kūkō made nijikan gurai kakarimasu.* — "It takes about two hours to the airport."

> **えきまで ごふんぐらい あるきます。**
>
> *eki made gofun gurai arukimasu.* — "I walk about five minutes to the station."

## You'll want to know: 〜から 〜まで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C259-DURATION-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C138-HATARAKIMASU, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-GETSUYOUBI, JA-LEX-C164-WEEK164-04] -->

**から** (*kara*) after a time or a place is "from", and **まで** is "until", "as far as":

> **くじから ごじまで はたらきます。**
>
> *kuji kara goji made hatarakimasu.* — "I work from nine to five."

> **げつようびから きんようびまで はたらきます。**
>
> *getsuyōbi kara kin'yōbi made hatarakimasu.* — "I work from Monday to Friday."
