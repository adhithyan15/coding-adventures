## You'll want to know: बुदबुदाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C496-NEEND496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बुदबुदाना** — *budbudānā* — "to mumble".

> बुख़ार में दादा कुछ बुदबुदा रहे थे।
>
> *bukhār meṁ dādā kuch budbudā rahe the.*
>
> In his fever my grandfather was mumbling something.

Past (he …): वह कुछ बुदबुदाया, *vah kuch budbudāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुदबुदाना** (*budbudānā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *budbudānā*, "to mutter", under *\*budabuda*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9274); the doubled syllable is the sound of lips moving with no clear words.
