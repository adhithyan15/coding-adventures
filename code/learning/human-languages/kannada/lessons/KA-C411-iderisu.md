# ಈಡೇರಿಸು (īḍērisu) — to fulfil

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for an agreement, then the Kannada for a pact.

## You'll want to know: ಈಡೇರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C411-SHARATTU411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಈಡೇರಿಸು** — *īḍērisu* — "to fulfil".

> ಅಪ್ಪ ನನ್ನ ಆಸೆಯನ್ನು ಈಡೇರಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *appa nanna āseyannu īḍērisidaru.*
>
> Father fulfilled my wish.

Past (I …): ಈಡೇರಿಸಿದೆ, *īḍēriside*.

ಈಡೇರು, to come true: ಆಸೆ ಈಡೇರಿತು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಈಡೇರಿಸು** (*īḍērisu*) is built as the causative of *īḍēru*, "to be fulfilled"; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
