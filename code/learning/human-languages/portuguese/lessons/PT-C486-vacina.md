## You'll want to know: a vacina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C486-LIGADURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a vacina** — "vaccine, jab".

> As crianças levaram a vacina da gripe.
>
> *The children had the flu jab.*

*Levar a vacina* — to get vaccinated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vacina* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vaccīna* (EtymDB), from *vaccīnus* "of cows", built on *vacca* "cow": the first vaccine came from cowpox.
