## You'll want to know: sànbù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C141-FUN141-04]; assesses=[] -->

*sànbù* — "to take a walk".

> *wǎnfàn yǐhòu wǒmen qù sànbù* — After dinner we go for a walk.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sànbù* is built from *sàn* 'to scatter, to spread out' and *bù* 'step', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sanH* and *buH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): steps let loose.
