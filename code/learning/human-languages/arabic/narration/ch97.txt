Arabic, chapter 97: Ten, Thirty, Forty, Fifty, Thousand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عشرة (ʿashara) — ten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for eight, then the Arabic for nine.

You'll want to know: عشرة.
عشرة — ʿashara — "ten".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does عشرة (ʿashara) mean? ("Ten".) Say it once more.
