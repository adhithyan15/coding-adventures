## You'll want to know: l'intrigue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C519-ROMAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'intrigue** — "plot (of a story); intrigue".

> L'intrigue du livre se passe dans un petit village.
>
> *The plot of the book takes place in a small village.*

Feminine. Also a secret scheme.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'intrigue* was borrowed from Italian *intrigo* (EtymDB), from *intrigare*, from Latin *intrīcāre* "to entangle, to perplex" (Lewis & Short).
