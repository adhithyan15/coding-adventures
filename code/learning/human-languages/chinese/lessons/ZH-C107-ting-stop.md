## You'll want to know: tíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C107-MOVE107-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tíng* — "to stop; to park".

> *chē tíng le* — The car has stopped.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tíng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *deng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to rest', 'to settle' and 'to stop'.
