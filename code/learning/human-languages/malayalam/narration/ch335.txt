Malayalam, chapter 335: Degree, Subject, Mathematics, Homework, Letter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ബിരുദം (birudaṁ) — a degree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a company, then the Malayalam for a meeting.

You'll want to know: ബിരുദം.
ബിരുദം — birudaṁ — "a degree".

The word, taken apart.
ബിരുദം (birudaṁ) comes from Sanskrit biruda, "a title, a panegyric". DEDR 5414 compares that Sanskrit word with Dravidian ones, Tamil virutu and Kannada biruda, "badge of honour".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for mathematics, then the Malayalam for homework.

You'll want to know: അക്ഷരം.
അക്ഷരം — akṣaraṁ — "a letter (of the alphabet)".

The word, taken apart.
അക്ഷരം (akṣaraṁ) from Sanskrit akṣara, "indestructible; syllable", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 38). A syllable was "the imperishable", and became a letter.
