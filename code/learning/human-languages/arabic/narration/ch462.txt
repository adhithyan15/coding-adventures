Arabic, chapter 462: Getting ready to go.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
استعد (istaʿadda) — to get ready.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to miss, then the Arabic for to waste time.

You'll want to know: استعد.
استعد — istaʿadda — "to get ready".
استعد المسافرون للرحلة. istaʿadda al-musāfirūn li-r-riḥla. The travellers got ready for the trip.
Present: يستعد, yastaʿidd.
It takes li-: istaʿadda li-, "to get ready for".

The word, taken apart.
استعد (istaʿadda) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-d-d, "to count" (Lane). It is Form X, "to make oneself ready".
