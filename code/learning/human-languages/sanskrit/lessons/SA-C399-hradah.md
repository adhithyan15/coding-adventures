## You'll want to know: ह्रदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C399-HRADA399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ह्रदः** — *hradaḥ* — "a deep pool; a lake".

> वनस्य मध्ये गभीरः ह्रदः अस्ति।
>
> *vanasya madhye gabhīraḥ hradaḥ asti.*
>
> In the middle of the forest there is a deep pool.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ह्रदः** (*hradaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a large or deep piece of water, a lake or pool; MW connects it doubtfully with √hlād, "to refresh", so its origin is uncertain (MW).
