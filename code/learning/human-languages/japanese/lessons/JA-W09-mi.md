# み — one looped bridge

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-SUMIMASEN, JA-SCRIPT-MA-01, JA-SCRIPT-NA-01] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Say *sumimasen* once, then write known **ま** and **な**. The new
sign shares the *m* opening but carries a different vowel.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-MI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **み** — *mi*

Write it in two strokes. First make the bent left body, ending in a small turn.
Then begin at the upper right, sweep down through the middle, and finish by
lifting to the right. Say *mi* only when the second stroke ends.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-MI-01] -->
1. Trace **み** once with a finger.
2. Trace it once with a pencil.
3. Point to **み** inside **すみません**; do not decode the other signs yet.
