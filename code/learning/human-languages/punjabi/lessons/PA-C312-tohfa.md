## You'll want to know: ਤੋਹਫ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C312-VISIT312-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੋਹਫ਼ਾ** — *tohfā* — "a gift".

> ਮਹਿਮਾਨ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਲਈ ਤੋਹਫ਼ੇ ਲਿਆਏ।
>
> *mahimān bachchiāṁ laī tohfe liāe.*
>
> The guests brought gifts for the children.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੋਹਫ਼ਾ** (*tohfā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *tuḥfa*, "a gift, a rarity".
