## You'll want to know: झमेला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C541-RUKAVAT541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**झमेला** — *jhamelā* — "a hassle, a tiresome business".

> दफ़्तर में काग़ज़ों का बड़ा झमेला है।
>
> *Daftar meṁ kāġzoṁ kā baṛā jhamelā hai.*
>
> There is a big hassle with papers at the office.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झमेला** (*jhamelā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*jhamm*, "entanglement", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5340). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "wrangling".
