German, chapter 409: Bauarbeiten.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Baustelle — the road works; the building site.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the oncoming traffic, then the German for to swerve.

You'll want to know: die Baustelle.
die Baustelle — "the road works; the building site".
Wegen der Baustelle fährt der Bus heute langsam. Because of the road works the bus is slow today.
Plural: die Baustellen.

The word, taken apart.
Baustelle is built from Bau 'building', from bauen 'to build', inherited from Proto-Germanic būaną (EtymDB), and Stelle 'place'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
sperren — to close (a road); to block.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the diversion, then the German for to divert.

You'll want to know: sperren.
sperren — "to close (a road); to block".
Nach dem Unfall sperrt die Polizei die Brücke. After the accident the police close the bridge.
Gesperrt — closed, on signs. Eine Karte sperren — to block a card.

The word, taken apart.
sperren is an inherited Germanic verb; EtymDB links it with English spar 'to bolt, to bar', so it is usually explained as first meaning to shut something with a bar.

The word, taken apart.
Raststätte is built from Rast 'rest, a break on a journey' and Stätte 'place', inherited from Middle High German stat (EtymDB).
