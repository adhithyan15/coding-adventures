Kannada, chapter 373: The Rise and Fall of a Kingdom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉದಯ (udaya) — a rise; a dawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for former, then the Kannada for theft.

You'll want to know: ಉದಯ.
ಉದಯ — udaya — "a rise; a dawn".
ಆ ರಾಜ್ಯದ ಉದಯ ಒಂದು ಸಣ್ಣ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಯಿತು. ā rājyada udaya ondu saṇṇa haḷḷiyalli āyitu. The rise of that kingdom began in a small village.
ಸೂರ್ಯೋದಯ is sunrise. Of a kingdom or a leader, its rise.

The word, taken apart.
ಉದಯ (udaya) is borrowed from Sanskrit udaya, "rise, rising of the sun" (CDIAL 1931).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಪತನ (patana) — a downfall; a fall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for theft, then the Kannada for a rise.

You'll want to know: ಪತನ.
ಪತನ — patana — "a downfall; a fall".
ಸೋಮಾರಿ ರಾಜರಿಂದ ಆ ಸಾಮ್ರಾಜ್ಯದ ಪತನ ಆಯಿತು. sōmāri rājarinda ā sāmrājyada patana āyitu. That empire fell because of lazy kings.
The opposite of ಉದಯ (udaya): the fall of a kingdom, a government or a price.

The word, taken apart.
ಪತನ (patana) is borrowed from Sanskrit patana, "the act of flying or coming down, falling" (CDIAL 7724).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಗತಕಾಲ (gatakāla) — the past; bygone days.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a rise, then the Kannada for a downfall.

You'll want to know: ಗತಕಾಲ.
ಗತಕಾಲ — gatakāla — "the past; bygone days".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ ಗತಕಾಲದ ವೈಭವದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಮಾತಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. ajja yāvāgalū gatakālada vaibhavada bagge mātāḍuttāre. Grandfather always talks about the glory of the past.
ಗತ, gone, and ಕಾಲ, time.

The word, taken apart.
ಗತಕಾಲ (gatakāla) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of gata, "gone, past" (CDIAL 4008), and kāla, "time" (Monier-Williams).

ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ ತನ್ನ ಪ್ರಯಾಣದ ವೃತ್ತಾಂತ ಬರೆದನು. pravāsi tanna prayāṇada vṛttānta baredanu. The traveller wrote an account of his journey.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ವಿಜಯ (vijaya) — a triumph; a victory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the past, then the Kannada for a narrative.

You'll want to know: ವಿಜಯ.
ವಿಜಯ — vijaya — "a triumph; a victory".
ಯುದ್ಧದಲ್ಲಿ ವಿಜಯದ ನಂತರ ರಾಜ ಊರಿಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗಿದನು. yuddhadalli vijayada nantara rāja ūrige hintirugidanu. After the victory in the war the king returned to the town.
ವಿಜಯ (vijaya) is the grand word; ಗೆಲುವು is the everyday one.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಜಯ (vijaya) is borrowed from Sanskrit vijaya, "contest for victory, victory, conquest, triumph" (Monier-Williams), from ji, "to win".
