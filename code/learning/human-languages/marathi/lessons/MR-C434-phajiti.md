## You'll want to know: फजिती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C434-GAIRSOY434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फजिती** — *phajitī* — "a fiasco; an embarrassment".

> पावसात छत्री उलटली आणि माझी फजिती झाली.
>
> *pāvsāt chatrī ulaṭlī āṇi mājhī phajitī jhālī.*
>
> In the rain my umbrella turned inside out and I was embarrassed.

Feminine.

फजिती होणे, to be made to look foolish; the person takes -ची.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फजिती** (*phajitī*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *faḍīḥat*, "disgrace".
