## You'll want to know: fretum, fretī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C443-FLUCTUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fretum, fretī** — "a strait, a narrow channel; (in poetry) the sea".

> Fretum angustum est, sed flūctūs magnī sunt.
>
> *The strait is narrow, but the waves are big.*

Second declension, neuter: *fretum, fretī n.* A narrow sea between two coasts, where the water runs hard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fretum* is inherited. Lewis & Short connect it with a root for restless, seething movement, seen in *ferveō* 'to boil', since water churns in a strait; EtymDB gives no parent, so the link is hedged.
