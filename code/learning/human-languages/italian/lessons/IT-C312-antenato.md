## You'll want to know: l'antenato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C312-ANTENATI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'antenato** — "the ancestor".

> I miei antenati erano contadini.
>
> *My ancestors were farmers.*

Plural *gli antenati*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*antenato* is usually traced to Late Latin *antenātus* "born before", a learned word built on *ante* "before" and *nātus* "born" (Lewis & Short).
