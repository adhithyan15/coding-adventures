## You'll want to know: निदानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C482-CIKITSA482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निदानम्** — *nidānam* — "the cause of an illness".

> वैद्यः रोगस्य निदानं जानाति।
>
> *vaidyaḥ rogasya nidānaṃ jānāti.*
>
> The physician knows the cause of the illness.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निदानम्** (*nidānam*) is built from *ni* and the root √dā, "to bind": MW's first sense is "a band, a halter", then "a first cause" and "the cause of a disease and enquiry into it, pathology" (MW).
