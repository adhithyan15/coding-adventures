## You'll want to know: редактор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C284-INTERVYU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**редактор** (*redáktor*) — "an editor".

> Редактор прочитал статью и улыбнулся.
>
> *Redáktor prochitál statyú i ulybnúlsya.*
>
> The editor read the article and smiled.

*Главный редактор* — the editor-in-chief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB derives *редактор* from French *rédacteur* "editor", given a Latin-style ending *-ор*; the French word is built on Latin *redāctus*, the participle of *redigere* "to drive back, to bring back" (Lewis & Short).
