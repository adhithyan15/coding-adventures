## You'll want to know: تزیین کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C342-TAZYIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تزیین کردن** (*tazyin kardan*) — "to decorate".

> ما خانه را با گل تزیین کردیم.
>
> *Mâ khâne râ bâ gol tazyin kardim.*
>
> We decorated the house with flowers.

Also spelled تزئین. Present *tazyin mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tazyin* is usually traced to Arabic *tazyīn*, "adornment"; EtymDB lists no entry. *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
