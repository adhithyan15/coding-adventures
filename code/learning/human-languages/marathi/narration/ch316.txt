Marathi, chapter 316: A Wedding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लग्न (lagna) — a wedding; a marriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to register, then the Marathi for to wander.

You'll want to know: लग्न.
लग्न — lagna — "a wedding; a marriage".
माझ्या मावशीचं लग्न पुढच्या महिन्यात आहे. mājhyā māvśīcaṁ lagna puḍhacyā mahinyāt āhe. My aunt's wedding is next month.
Neuter.
लग्न (lagna) होणे, to get married; X-शी लग्न (lagna) करणे, to marry X.

The word, taken apart.
लग्न (lagna) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit lagna, 'attached, joined, fixed' (Monier-Williams): a joining of two people and two families.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to wander, then the Marathi for a wedding.

You'll want to know: साखरपुडा.
साखरपुडा — sākharpuḍā — "an engagement".
त्यांचा साखरपुडा काल घरीच झाला. tyāñcā sākharpuḍā kāl gharīc jhālā. Their engagement took place at home yesterday.
Masculine.
A packet of sugar is exchanged: साखर + पुडा.

The word, taken apart.
साखरपुडा (sākharpuḍā) is built from sākhar, 'sugar', inherited from Sanskrit śarkarā (CDIAL 12338), and puḍā, 'a packet', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit puṭa, 'a fold, a small container' (CDIAL 8253).

Lesson 3 of 5.
मेंदी (mendī) — henna.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a wedding, then the Marathi for an engagement.

You'll want to know: मेंदी.
मेंदी — mendī — "henna".
लग्नाआधी नवरीच्या हातांवर मेंदी काढतात. lagnāādhī navrīcyā hātāṁvar mendī kāḍhtāt. Before the wedding, henna is put on the bride's hands.
Feminine.
मेंदी (mendī) काढणे, to put on henna in patterns.

The word, taken apart.
मेंदी (mendī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi mẽdhī, 'the henna plant', under Sanskrit mendhī or mendhikā, 'Lawsonia alba' (CDIAL 10328).

Lesson 4 of 5.
वरात (varāt) — a wedding procession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an engagement, then the Marathi for henna.

You'll want to know: वरात.
वरात — varāt — "a wedding procession".
वरात वाजत गाजत मंडपात पोहोचली. varāt vājat gājat maṇḍpāt pohoclī. The wedding procession reached the marquee with music and fanfare.
Feminine.
The groom and his party come in it.

The word, taken apart.
वरात (varāt) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi varāt, 'the groom's procession', under Sanskrit varayātrā, 'the procession of a suitor or bridegroom' (CDIAL 11322).
