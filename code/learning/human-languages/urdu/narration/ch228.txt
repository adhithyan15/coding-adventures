Urdu, chapter 228: Swimming, Team, Player, Film, Play.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تیراکی (tairākī) — swimming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a wolf, then the Urdu for a squirrel.

You'll want to know: تیراکی.
تیراکی — tairākī — "swimming".

The word, taken apart.
تیراکی (tairākī) is built on tairnā, "to swim", with the ending -ākī. CDIAL lists tairnā under Sanskrit tirate, "crosses over", and explains its ai as a blend with another verb (CDIAL 5821).
