## You'll want to know: ಪಾಲಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C444-NIYAMA444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾಲಿಸು** — *pālisu* — "to obey; to keep (a rule)".

> ನಾವು ಸಂಚಾರ ನಿಯಮಗಳನ್ನು ಪಾಲಿಸಿದೆವು.
>
> *nāvu sañcāra niyamagaḷannu pālisidevu.*
>
> We kept to the traffic rules.

Past (I …): ಪಾಲಿಸಿದೆ, *pāliside*.

Also to look after or rear; ನಿಯಮ ಪಾಲಿಸು is the usual pair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾಲಿಸು** (*pālisu*) is built from Sanskrit *pālayati*, "guards, protects" (CDIAL 8129), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*: to keep a rule is to guard it.
