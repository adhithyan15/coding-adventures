## You'll want to know: आक्रमण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C328-LADHAI328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्रमण** — *ākramaṇ* — "an invasion".

> शत्रूच्या आक्रमणानंतर राज्य कमकुवत झालं.
>
> *śatrūcyā ākramaṇānantar rājya kamkuvat jhālaṁ.*
>
> After the enemy's invasion the kingdom grew weak.

Neuter.

X-वर आक्रमण करणे, to invade X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्रमण** (*ākramaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ākramaṇa*, 'a stepping upon, an attack' (Monier-Williams).
