## You'll want to know: क्षतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C298-KSHATI298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षतिः** — *kṣatiḥ* — "damage; harm".

> अग्निना ग्रामस्य क्षतिः भवति।
>
> *agninā grāmasya kṣatiḥ bhavati.*
>
> The fire does damage to the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षतिः** (*kṣatiḥ*) MW gives *kṣati* as injury, loss and damage, beside *kṣata*, "wounded", but gives no root in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
