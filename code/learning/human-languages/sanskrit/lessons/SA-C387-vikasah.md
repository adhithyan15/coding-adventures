## You'll want to know: विकासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C387-UNNATI387-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विकासः** — *vikāsaḥ* — "development; growth".

> यदि मार्गाः भवन्ति, तर्हि ग्रामस्य विकासः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi mārgāḥ bhavanti, tarhi grāmasya vikāsaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If there are roads, the village will develop.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विकासः** (*vikāsaḥ*) is built on *vi* and the root √kas, "to open"; MW gives *vi-kāsa* "expanding, budding" and "expansion, development, growth" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
