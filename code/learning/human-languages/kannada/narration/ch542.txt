Kannada, chapter 542: Rooms of a House.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮಾಳಿಗೆ (māḷige) — an upper storey; a flat roof.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for archery, then the Kannada for to cheer on.

You'll want to know: ಮಾಳಿಗೆ.
ಮಾಳಿಗೆ — māḷige — "an upper storey; a flat roof".
ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಮಾಳಿಗೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಗಾಳಿಪಟ ಹಾರಿಸಿದರು. makkaḷu māḷigeya mēle gāḷipaṭa hārisidaru. The children flew kites on the roof.
ಮಾಳಿಗೆ (māḷige) ಮನೆ, a house with an upper floor.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾಳಿಗೆ (māḷige) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4796 lists Kannada māḍa, māḷa and māḷige, "a large building, an upstair house".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ (nelamāḷige) — a basement; a cellar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a threshold, then the Kannada for a hall.

You'll want to know: ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ.
ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ — nelamāḷige — "a basement; a cellar".
ಹಳೆಯ ಸಾಮಾನುಗಳು ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇವೆ. haḷeya sāmānugaḷu nelamāḷigeyalli ive. The old things are in the basement.
ನೆಲ, the ground + ಮಾಳಿಗೆ (māḷige), a storey: the storey under the ground.

The word, taken apart.
ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ (nelamāḷige) is a native Kannada compound: nela, "the ground", and māḷige, "a storey" (DEDR 4796).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಪಾಟು (kapāṭu) — a cabinet; a shelved cupboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a hall, then the Kannada for a basement.

You'll want to know: ಕಪಾಟು.
ಕಪಾಟು — kapāṭu — "a cabinet; a shelved cupboard".
ಪುಸ್ತಕಗಳು ಗಾಜಿನ ಕಪಾಟಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಇವೆ. pustakagaḷu gājina kapāṭinalli ive. The books are in the glass cabinet.
ಬೀರು is a big wardrobe; a ಕಪಾಟು (kapāṭu) is often set into the wall.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಪಾಟು (kapāṭu) is usually traced to Sanskrit kapāṭa, "a door, the panel of a door" (Monier-Williams): a cupboard is what has the doors. The path is not settled.
