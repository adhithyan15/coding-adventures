## You'll want to know: ઊણપ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C431-STOCK431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊણપ** — *ūṇap* — "a shortfall; a lack".

> ઉનાળામાં ગામમાં પાણીની ઊણપ રહે છે.
>
> *unāḷāmā̃ gāmmā̃ pāṇīnī ūṇap rahe che.*
>
> In summer the village runs short of water.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊણપ** (*ūṇap*) is built on ઊણું, "wanting, short", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *ūna*, "deficient" (CDIAL 2405): what is missing from the full amount.
