Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 143: Hot, Salty, Sour, Bitter, Spicy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तातो (tāto) — hot (to the touch).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for wet, then the Marwadi for dry.

You'll want to know: तातो.
तातो — tāto — "hot (to the touch)".

The word, taken apart.
तातो (tāto) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tapta, "heated, hot". CDIAL lists Marwari tāto under it (CDIAL 5679).

The word, taken apart.
खाटो (khāṭo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khaṭṭa, "sour". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 3777); the match is through Gujarati khāṭũ. CDIAL asks whether it is connected with kaṭu, "pungent".
