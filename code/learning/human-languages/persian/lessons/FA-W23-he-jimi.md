# ح (he-ye jimi) — the letter that opens حال

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-DAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ز and د once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **حال** (*hâl*), "state, how you are".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-HE-JIMI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ح** — *he-ye jimi*, the sound *h*.

It opens **حال** (*hâl*), the "how you are" in **حالت چطور است؟**. Persian already
has an *h*, **ه**, which you write; this second one came in with Arabic words and
sounds the same. Its name, *he-ye jimi*, means "the *h* shaped like *jim*": a short
head and a deep bowl, with **no dots**.

## Writing: ح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-HE-JIMI-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the short upper head from left to right
- **2.** continue down and around the deep bowl without lifting — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 0.** The pen never leaves the paper.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ح demonstration at 00:42–00:46 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).
