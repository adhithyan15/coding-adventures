## You'll want to know: ફિલ્મ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C255-STORY255-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફિલ્મ** — *philm* — "film, movie".

> **ગઈકાલે મેં નવી ફિલ્મ જોઈ.** — *gaīkāle mẽ navī philm joī* — "Yesterday I saw a new film."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફિલ્મ** (*philm*) is a loan from English *film*.
