## You'll want to know: posteriormente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C538-ANTES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**posteriormente** — "subsequently, later".

> Posteriormente la policía detuvo al hombre.
>
> *The police later arrested the man.*

A formal word, common in the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*posteriormente* is built on *posterior* and the ending *-mente* (EtymDB). *Posterior* is a learned word from Latin *posterior* 'later', from *posterus*, built on *post* 'after' (Lewis & Short).
