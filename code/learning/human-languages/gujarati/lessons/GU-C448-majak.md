# મજાક (majāk) — a joke; good-natured teasing

## You'll want to know: મજાક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C448-HASYA448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મજાક** — *majāk* — "a joke; good-natured teasing".

> અરુણે મજાક કરી અને બધા હસ્યા.
>
> *Aruṇe majāk karī ane badhā hasyā.*
>
> Arun made a joke and everyone laughed.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મજાક** (*majāk*) is usually traced to Arabic *madhāq*, "taste, relish", which reached Gujarati through Persian, where it is said *mazāq*; the sense "a joke" is said to have grown up on the way.
