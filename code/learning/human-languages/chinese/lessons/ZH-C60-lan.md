## You'll want to know: lán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C60-COLOR60-05]; assesses=[] -->

*lán* — "blue".

> *tā de yǎnjing hěn lán* — Her eyes are very blue.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lán* is inherited from Middle Chinese *lam* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a plant for dye'. The colour is not given there; blue as the colour of that dye is the likely step, but it is not settled.
