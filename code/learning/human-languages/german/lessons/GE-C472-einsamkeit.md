## You'll want to know: die Einsamkeit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C472-SEHNSUCHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Einsamkeit** — "loneliness; solitude".

> Nach dem Umzug litt sie unter Einsamkeit.
>
> *After the move she suffered from loneliness.*

No plural. Built on *einsam* — lonely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Einsamkeit* is built on *einsam* "lonely" with *-keit* (EtymDB lists both); *einsam* joins *ein* "one", from Proto-Germanic *ainaz*, to the ending *-sam*.
