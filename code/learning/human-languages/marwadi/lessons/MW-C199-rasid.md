## You'll want to know: रसीद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C199-SEAL199-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रसीद** — *rasīd* — "a receipt".

> आप रसीद राखो।
>
> *āp rasīd rākho.*
>
> Please keep the receipt.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रसीद** (*rasīd*) is usually traced to Persian *rasīd*, "received; a receipt".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
