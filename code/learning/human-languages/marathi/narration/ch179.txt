Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Rarely? (क्वचित, kvacit.) Then? (मग, mag.) Finally? (शेवटी, śevṭī.) Still? (अजून, ajūn.) Suddenly? (अचानक, acānak.) Around? (भोवती, bhovtī.) Everywhere? (सगळीकडे, sagḷīkaḍe.) Elsewhere? (इतरत्र, itaratra.) Over there? (तिकडे, tikaḍe.) In the middle? (मध्यभागी, madhyabhāgī.) Even so? (तरीही, tarīhī.) Although? (जरी, jarī.) Despite? (असूनही, asūnhī.) Unfortunately? (दुर्दैवाने, durdaivāne.) Luckily? (सुदैवाने, sudaivāne.) Comfortable? (आरामदायक, ārāmdāyak.) Polite? (सभ्य, sabhya.) Rude? (उद्धट, uddhaṭ.) Unpleasant? (अप्रिय, apriya.) Enthusiastic? (उत्साही, utsāhī.)
