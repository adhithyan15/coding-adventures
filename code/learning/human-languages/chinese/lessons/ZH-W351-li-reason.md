## Script — the jade piece, then lǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-LI-REASON-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 理

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the jade piece, four strokes**, narrow: a horizontal, a second horizontal, a vertical down through them, then a bar at the foot that rises to the right.

**The right piece, 里, seven strokes**: a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, a horizontal across the middle, a horizontal closing the box, a vertical down through the middle, a short horizontal, then the long horizontal at the foot.

4 + 7 = eleven, and the last seven your hand already knows.
