# હસાવવું (hasāvvũ) — to make someone laugh

## You'll want to know: હસાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C448-HASYA448-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હસાવવું** — *hasāvvũ* — "to make someone laugh".

> અરુણની વાર્તાઓ બધાને હસાવે છે.
>
> *Aruṇnī vārtāo badhāne hasāve che.*
>
> Arun's stories make everyone laugh.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને હસાવ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne hasāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હસાવવું** (*hasāvvũ*) is the causative of **હસવું** (*hasvũ*), "to laugh", which is inherited from Sanskrit *hasati*, "laughs" (CDIAL 14021).
