## You'll want to know: garantizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C614-RIESGO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**garantizar** — "to guarantee".

> No te puedo garantizar nada.
>
> *I cannot guarantee you anything.*

Spelling: *garanticé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garantizar* is built on *garantía*; EtymDB links *garantía* with Catalan *garantia* and English *guarantee*, and the family is usually traced to Old French *garant* 'guarantor', from a Frankish word for a protector.
