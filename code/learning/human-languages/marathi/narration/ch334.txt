Lesson 1 of 6.
क्रांती (krāntī) — a revolution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to set free, then the Marathi for to drive out.

You'll want to know: क्रांती.
क्रांती — krāntī — "a revolution".
नव्या बियाण्यांमुळे शेतीत मोठी क्रांती झाली. navyā biyāṇyāmmuḷe śetīt moṭhī krāntī jhālī. New seeds brought about a revolution in farming.
Feminine.
क्रांती (krāntī) होणे, a revolution happens.

The word, taken apart.
क्रांती (krāntī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit krānti, 'a step, a going; an overcoming' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a revolution' is modern.

You'll want to know: स्थापन करणे.
स्थापन करणे — sthāpan karṇe — "to found; to establish".
आजोबांनी गावात पहिली शाळा स्थापन केली. ājobānnī gāvāt pahilī śāḷā sthāpan kelī. Grandfather founded the first school in the village.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने स्थापन केलं, tyāne sthāpan kelaṁ.
What is founded agrees: शाळा स्थापन केली, संघ स्थापन केला.

The word, taken apart.
स्थापन करणे (sthāpan karṇe) joins sthāpan, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit sthāpana, 'a setting up, an establishing' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.

लोकांनी चौकात वीराचा पुतळा उभारला. lokānnī caukāt vīrācā putḷā ubhārlā. People put up the hero's statue in the square.

उभारणे (ubhārṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi ubhārṇe, 'to raise', under Sanskrit udbharati, 'raises' (CDIAL 2038).

Lesson 4 of 6.
बखर (bakhar) — a chronicle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to found, then the Marathi for to erect.

You'll want to know: बखर.
बखर — bakhar — "a chronicle".
या बखरीत त्या लढाईचं वर्णन आहे. yā bakhrīt tyā laḍhāīcaṁ varṇan āhe. This chronicle describes that battle.
Feminine.
Old Marathi prose histories are called बखरी.

The word, taken apart.
बखर (bakhar) is usually traced to Arabic khabar, 'news, a report', through Persian; the Marathi form has moved its sounds round.

मंदिराच्या भिंतीवर एक जुना शिलालेख आहे. mandirācyā bhintīvar ek junā śilālekh āhe. There is an old inscription on the temple wall.
Masculine.
शिला, a stone, + लेख, a writing.

The word, taken apart.
शिलालेख (śilālekh) is built from Sanskrit śilā, 'a stone', and lekha, 'a writing' (Monier-Williams): writing in stone.
