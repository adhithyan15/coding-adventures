## You'll want to know: ઝબકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C435-STORM435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝબકવું** — *jhabakvũ* — "to flash (of lightning); to flicker".

> રાત્રે આકાશમાં વીજળી ઝબકી.
>
> *rātre ākāśmā̃ vījḷī jhabkī.*
>
> At night lightning flashed in the sky.

Past (it …): તે વીજળી ઝબકી, *te vījḷī jhabkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝબકવું** (*jhabakvũ*) goes back to *\*jhamm*, "a flash", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to flash" (CDIAL 5341).
