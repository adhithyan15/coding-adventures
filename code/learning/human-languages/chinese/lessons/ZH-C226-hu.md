## You'll want to know: hú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C226-LAKE226-02]; assesses=[] -->

*hú* — "lake".

> *gōngyuán lǐ yǒu yí ge hú* — There's a lake in the park.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hú* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hu* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains it as a broad body of water.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
