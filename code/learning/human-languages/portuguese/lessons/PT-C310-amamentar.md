## You'll want to know: amamentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C310-BATIZADO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**amamentar** — "to breastfeed".

> Ela amamentou o filho durante seis meses.
>
> *She breastfed her son for six months.*

Built on *a mama*, ‘the breast’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amamentar* is built from *a-* and *mama* "breast" (EtymDB), from Latin *mamma* "breast".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
