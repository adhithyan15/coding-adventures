## You'll want to know: ખબર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C313-AFVA313-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ખબર** — *khabar* — "knowledge (of something); word".

> મીરાને ચોરીની ખબર સવારે પડી.
>
> *Mīrāne corīnī khabar savāre paḍī.*
>
> Mira found out about the theft in the morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખબર** (*khabar*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khabar*, "news, a report". In Gujarati it is knowing about something: **ખબર પડી**, came to know; **મને ખબર છે**, I know.
