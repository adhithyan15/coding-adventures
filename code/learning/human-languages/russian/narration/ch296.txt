Russian, chapter 296: Rescue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
спасатель (spasátel) — a rescuer, a lifeguard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to catch fire, then the Russian for a fire extinguisher.

You'll want to know: спасатель.
спасатель (spasátel) — "a rescuer, a lifeguard".
Спасатели приехали через десять минут. Spasáteli priyékhali chérez désyat minút. The rescuers arrived ten minutes later.
Genitive спасателя.

The word, taken apart.
спасатель (spasátel) is built on спасать (spasát) "to save", from спасти, Old East Slavic sŭpasti (EtymDB), the verb also behind спасибо, with the ending -тель for a person.

Lesson 2 of 5.
спасать (spasát) — to save, to rescue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fire extinguisher, then the Russian for a rescuer.

You'll want to know: спасать.
спасать (spasát) — "to save, to rescue".
Пожарный спас ребёнка. Pozhárnyy spas rebyónka. The firefighter saved a child.
Imperfective; perfective спасти (spastí), past спас, спасла. Спасите! — help!

The word, taken apart.
спасать (spasát) is the imperfective of спасти, inherited through Old East Slavic sŭpasti, from sŭ- "with" and pasti "to pasture, to herd", PIE peh₂- "to guard, to feed" (EtymDB); the same verb stands behind спасибо.

Lesson 4 of 5.
эвакуировать (evakuírovat) — to evacuate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to save, then the Russian for rescue.

You'll want to know: эвакуировать.
эвакуировать (evakuírovat) — "to evacuate".
Жителей эвакуировали из дома. Zhíteley evakuírovali iz dóma. The residents were evacuated from the building.
One form for both aspects. It takes the accusative.

The word, taken apart.
эвакуировать (evakuírovat) goes back to Latin ēvacuāre "to empty out", built on vacuus "empty" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the Russian verb with no parent.
