Telugu, chapter 186: Grandson, Granddaughter, Parents, Twins, Baby.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
మనవడు (manavaḍu) — a grandson.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the post, then the Telugu for a pencil.

You'll want to know: మనవడు.
మనవడు — manavaḍu — "a grandson".

The word, taken apart.
మనవడు (manavaḍu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4715 lists the older manumāḍu, "grandson", with Kannada mommaga, "grandson".

తల్లిదండ్రులు (tallidaṇḍrulu) is native Dravidian: talli, "mother", one word with Tamil taḷḷai (DEDR 3136), and taṇḍri, "father", one word with Tamil tantai (DEDR 3067), with the plural ending -lu.
