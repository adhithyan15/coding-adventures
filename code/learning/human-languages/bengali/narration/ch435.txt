Bengali, chapter 435: Grass, Creepers and Wood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘাস (ghās) — grass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to spoil, then the Bengali for to conserve.

You'll want to know: ঘাস.
ঘাস — ghās — "grass".
সকালে মাঠের ঘাস শিশিরে ভেজা। sôkāle māṭher ghās shishire bhejā. In the morning the grass on the field is wet with dew.

The word, taken apart.
ঘাস (ghās) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali ghās, "grass", under Sanskrit ghāsá, "fodder, grass" (CDIAL 4471).

Lesson 3 of 5.
শাখা (shākhā) — a branch (of a tree, a bank or a river).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for grass, then the Bengali for a creeper.

You'll want to know: শাখা.
শাখা — shākhā — "a branch (of a tree, a bank or a river)".
আমাদের ব্যাংকের একটা শাখা স্টেশনের কাছে। āmāder bængker ekṭā shākhā sṭeshôner kāchhe. Our bank has a branch near the station.
On a tree people mostly say ডাল, which sounds like ডাল, lentils; শাখা (shākhā) is the careful word, and the only one for a bank or a river.

The word, taken apart.
শাখা (shākhā) is Sanskrit śākhā, "a branch" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole; a bank's branch is the same image.

[pause 2 seconds]

কাঠ — kāṭh — "wood; timber".
শীতে আমরা কাঠ জ্বালালাম। shīte āmrā kāṭh jvālālām. In winter we burned wood.
ঠ, the breathy partner of ট, closes the word.

The word, taken apart.
কাঠ (kāṭh) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali kāṭh, "wood", under Sanskrit kāṣṭhá, "a piece of wood" (CDIAL 3120).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ছাঁটা (chhā̃ṭā) — to trim; to prune; to cut back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a branch, then the Bengali for wood.

You'll want to know: ছাঁটা.
ছাঁটা — chhā̃ṭā — "to trim; to prune; to cut back".
মালি বাগানের গাছগুলো ছাঁটলেন। māli bāgāner gāchhgulo chhā̃ṭlen. The gardener trimmed the plants in the garden.
Past (he/she …): তিনি গাছ ছাঁটলেন, tini gāchh chhā̃ṭlen.
Also of hair and of staff: চুল ছাঁটা (chhā̃ṭā), to trim hair.

The word, taken apart.
ছাঁটা (chhā̃ṭā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali chā̃ṭā, "to lop", under the reconstructed chāṇṭ, "cut" (CDIAL 5013).
