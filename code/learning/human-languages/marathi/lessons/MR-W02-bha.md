# भ — bha, with a puff of breath

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Make room for one consonant and one careful copy.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-BHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> भ

Say *ba*, then *bha* with a small puff against your palm. For the shape, sweep
the upper loop into the trunk, lower bowl, and crossbar without lifting; descend
the right stem; finish the headline left to right.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-BHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **भ** visible. Copy one run at a time, looking back before each. Say *bha*
once after the headline is complete.
