## HL-C343 — Hindi how declares its agreeing forms

After the playing-form repair, **कैसा** is the highest-ranked independent
modern Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 3 already
explains the masculine **कैसा**, feminine **कैसी**, and respectful or masculine
plural **कैसे** agreement pattern, but its headword declares only **कैसे** and
guided practice does not rehearse the complete set.

Declare all three agreeing forms as the lesson headword, practise them as a
set, and recall which agreement each form marks. This makes the existing lesson
explicit for learners, reports, and generated study artifacts without adding a
new construction or increasing the gentle-ramp debt.
