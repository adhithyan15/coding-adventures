## You'll want to know: факт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C282-ISTOCHNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**факт** (*fakt*) — "a fact".

> Это известный факт.
>
> *Éto izvéstnyy fakt.*
>
> This is a well-known fact.

Masculine. *На самом деле* or *фактически* — in fact.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*факт* goes back to Latin *factum* "a deed, a thing done", from *facere* "to do" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
