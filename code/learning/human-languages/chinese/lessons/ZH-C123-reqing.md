## You'll want to know: rèqíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C123-PEOPLE123-04]; assesses=[] -->

*rèqíng* — "warm; welcoming".

> *wǒ de lǎoshī hěn rèqíng* — My teacher is very warm.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rèqíng* is built from *rè* 'hot' and *qíng* 'feeling', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyet* and *dzjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary explains the second, after an older book, as the breath in a person that has desires.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
