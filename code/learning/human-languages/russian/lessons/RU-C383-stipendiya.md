## You'll want to know: стипендия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C383-POSTUPLENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**стипендия** (*stipéndiya*) — "a student grant, a scholarship".

> Он получает стипендию, потому что хорошо учится.
>
> *On polucháyet stipéndiyu, potomú chto khoroshó úchitsya.*
>
> He gets a grant because he does well at his studies.

Feminine. A monthly payment to students.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**стипендия** is usually traced to Latin *stīpendium* "a tax, a soldier’s pay" (Lewis & Short).
