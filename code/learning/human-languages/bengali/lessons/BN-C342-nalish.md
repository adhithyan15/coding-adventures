## You'll want to know: নালিশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C342-BICHAR342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**নালিশ** — *nālish* — "a formal complaint".

> আমি থানায় নালিশ জানালাম।
>
> *āmi thānāy nālish jānālām.*
>
> I lodged a complaint at the police station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নালিশ** (*nālish*) is usually traced to Persian *nālish*, a lament or complaint. It is made to someone in authority, with জানানো or করা.
