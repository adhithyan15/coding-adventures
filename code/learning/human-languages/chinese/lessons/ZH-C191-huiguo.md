## You'll want to know: 回国
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C191-BACK191-03]; assesses=[] -->

**回国** — *huíguó* — "to go home from abroad; to return to one's own country".

> **他下星期回国** *tā xià xīngqī huíguó* — He's going back to his own country next week.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**回国** (*huíguó*) is built from 回 'to turn back' and 国 'country', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwoj* and *kwok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
