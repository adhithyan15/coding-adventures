## You'll want to know: die Vermutung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C338-VERMUTUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Vermutung** — "the guess, the supposition".

> Meine Vermutung war richtig: Er hatte den Schlüssel im Auto vergessen.
>
> *My guess was right: he had left the key in the car.*

Plural: *die Vermutungen*. The noun of *vermuten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vermutung* is the noun of *vermuten* "to suppose", which is usually traced to *muten*, from *Mut* in its old sense "mind, feeling". EtymDB has no entry for the verb, so the account is hedged.
