## You'll want to know: незаконный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C388-ZAKON-03]; assesses=[] -->

**незаконный** (*nezakónnyy*) — "illegal, unlawful".

> Продавать это лекарство без рецепта незаконно.
>
> *Prodavát éto lekárstvo bez retsépta nezakónno.*
>
> Selling this medicine without a prescription is illegal.

*Незаконно* — illegally.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**незаконный** is *не-* "not" in front of *законный* "lawful", from *закон* "a law".
