# ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ (nelamāḷige) — a basement; a cellar

## You'll want to know: ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C542-MANE542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ** — *nelamāḷige* — "a basement; a cellar".

> ಹಳೆಯ ಸಾಮಾನುಗಳು ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇವೆ.
>
> *haḷeya sāmānugaḷu nelamāḷigeyalli ive.*
>
> The old things are in the basement.

ನೆಲ, the ground + ಮಾಳಿಗೆ, a storey: the storey under the ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೆಲಮಾಳಿಗೆ** (*nelamāḷige*) is a native Kannada compound: *nela*, "the ground", and *māḷige*, "a storey" (DEDR 4796).
