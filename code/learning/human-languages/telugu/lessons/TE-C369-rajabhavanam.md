# రాజభవనం (rājabhavanaṁ) — a palace

## You'll want to know: రాజభవనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C369-RAJABHAVANAM369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజభవనం** — *rājabhavanaṁ* — "a palace".

> రాజభవనంలో వంద గదులు ఉండేవి.
>
> *rājabhavanaṁlō vanda gadulu uṇḍēvi.*
>
> The palace had a hundred rooms.

రాజ, king, with భవనం, a building.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజభవనం** (*rājabhavanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *rājan*, "a king" (CDIAL 10679), and *bhavana*, "a place of abode" (CDIAL 9417).
