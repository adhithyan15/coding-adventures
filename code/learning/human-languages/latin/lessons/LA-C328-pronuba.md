## You'll want to know: prōnuba, prōnubae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C328-FLAMMEUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**prōnuba, prōnubae** — "a matron of honour".

> Prōnuba dextrās spōnsī et spōnsae iūnxit.
>
> *The matron of honour joined the right hands of groom and bride.*

Feminine. A woman married only once led the bride and joined the couple's hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**prōnuba** is built on *nūbō*, "to marry", with *prō* (Lewis & Short): she who helps on the marrying.
