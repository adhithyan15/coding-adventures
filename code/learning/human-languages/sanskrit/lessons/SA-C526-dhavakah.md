## You'll want to know: धावकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C526-ABHYASYATI526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धावकः** — *dhāvakaḥ* — "a runner".

> धावकः क्षेत्रं परितः धावति।
>
> *dhāvakaḥ kṣetraṃ paritaḥ dhāvati.*
>
> The runner runs round the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धावकः** (*dhāvakaḥ*) comes from the root √dhāv, "to run", which also gives *dhāvati*, with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *dhāvaka* as running, a forerunner (MW).
