Punjabi, chapter 269: Finding the Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਛਾਣਨਾ (pachhāṇnā) — to recognize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a crowd, then the Punjabi for to stay put.

You'll want to know: ਪਛਾਣਨਾ.
ਪਛਾਣਨਾ — pachhāṇnā — "to recognize".
ਮੈਂ ਇਹ ਗਲੀ ਪਛਾਣਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ eh galī pachhāṇdā hāṁ. I recognize this lane.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਰਸਤਾ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ, maiṁ rastā pachhāṇiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਛਾਣਨਾ (pachhāṇnā) is inherited from Sanskrit pratyabhijānāti, "recognizes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pachāṇnā, "recognizes", under it (CDIAL 8637).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਨਕਸ਼ਾ (nakshā) — a map.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to stay put, then the Punjabi for to recognize.

You'll want to know: ਨਕਸ਼ਾ.
ਨਕਸ਼ਾ — nakshā — "a map".
ਮੇਰੇ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਨਕਸ਼ਾ ਹੈ। mere fon vich shahir dā nakshā hai. There is a map of the town on my phone.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਕਸ਼ਾ (nakshā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian naqsha, "a map, a plan", itself from Arabic.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਢੁੱਕਣਾ (ḍhukkṇā) — to draw near; to approach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a map, then the Punjabi for a mark.

You'll want to know: ਢੁੱਕਣਾ.
ਢੁੱਕਣਾ — ḍhukkṇā — "to draw near; to approach".
ਬੱਸ ਹੌਲੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਅੱਡੇ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਢੁੱਕਦੀ ਹੈ। bass haulī haulī aḍḍe de neṛe ḍhukkdī hai. The bus slowly draws near the stand.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬੱਸ ਅੱਡੇ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਢੁੱਕੀ, oh bass aḍḍe de neṛe ḍhukkī.

The word, taken apart.
ਢੁੱਕਣਾ (ḍhukkṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍhukyati, "approaches"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍhukkṇā, "to approach", under it (CDIAL 5592).
