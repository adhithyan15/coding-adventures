## You'll want to know: でんごん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C307-DENWA307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**でんごん** — *dengon* — "a message (passed on)".

> **あにから でんごんが あります。** — *ani kara dengon ga arimasu* — "There is a message from my brother."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**でんごん** (*dengon*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *den*, "transmit" (Middle Chinese *drjwen*) and *gon*, "word" (*ngjon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
