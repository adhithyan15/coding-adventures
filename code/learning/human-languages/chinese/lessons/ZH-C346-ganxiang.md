## You'll want to know: 感想
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C346-THINK346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**感想** — *gǎnxiǎng* — "impressions afterwards; thoughts".

> **看了这本书你有什么感想** *kàn le zhè běn shū nǐ yǒu shénme gǎnxiǎng* — What did you make of the book once you'd read it?

Two third tones meet in this word: gǎn is said with a rising tone before xiǎng, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**感想** (*gǎnxiǎng*) is built from 感 'to feel' and 想 'to think', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *komX* and *sjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move' and the second 'to think'.
