## You'll want to know: आरोहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C547-UTTHANA547-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आरोहः** — *ārohaḥ* — "a climb; an ascent".

> यदि पर्वतस्य आरोहः कठिनः अस्ति, तर्हि मन्दं गच्छतु।
>
> *yadi parvatasya ārohaḥ kaṭhinaḥ asti, tarhi mandaṃ gacchatu.*
>
> If the climb up the mountain is hard, go slowly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आरोहः** (*ārohaḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √ruh, "to rise, to grow", which also gives *ārohati*; MW gives *āroha* as one who mounts, and as rising, an ascent (MW).
