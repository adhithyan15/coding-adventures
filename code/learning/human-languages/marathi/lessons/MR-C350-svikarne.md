## You'll want to know: स्वीकारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C350-SVIKAR350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वीकारणे** — *svīkārṇe* — "to accept".

> त्याने नवीन नोकरी आनंदाने स्वीकारली.
>
> *tyāne navīn nokrī ānandāne svīkārlī.*
>
> He gladly accepted the new job.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने स्वीकारलं, *tyāne svīkārlaṁ*.

Transitive: the past agrees with what is accepted, नोकरी स्वीकारली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वीकारणे** (*svīkārṇe*) is built on the tatsama स्वीकार, Sanskrit *svīkāra*, "making one's own, accepting" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -णे.
