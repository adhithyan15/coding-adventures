## You'll want to know: मात करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C529-SAMNA529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मात करणे** — *māt karṇe* — "to overcome; to beat".

> आमच्या संघाने मोठ्या संघावर मात केली.
>
> *āmcyā saṅghāne moṭhyā saṅghāvar māt kelī.*
>
> Our team beat a bigger team.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अडचणींवर मात केली, *tyāne aḍcaṇīṁvar māt kelī*.

The one beaten takes -वर: संघावर मात करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मात करणे** (*māt karṇe*) is मात, a loan from Persian *māt*, "checkmated", usually traced to Arabic, with करणे.
