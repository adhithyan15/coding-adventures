## You'll want to know: સન્માન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C309-INAM309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સન્માન** — *sanmān* — "an honour".

> સભામાં અરુણનું સન્માન થયું.
>
> *sabhāmā̃ Aruṇnũ sanmān thayũ.*
>
> Arun was honoured at the gathering.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સન્માન** (*sanmān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sammāna*, "honour, respect, homage" (Monier-Williams). Someone is honoured with **થવું**: **તેનું સન્માન થયું**.
