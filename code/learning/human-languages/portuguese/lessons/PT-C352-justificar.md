## You'll want to know: justificar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C352-REACAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**justificar** — "to justify".

> Ele tentou justificar a decisão.
>
> *He tried to justify the decision.*

*Justificar-se* — to explain yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*justificar* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *iūstificāre* "to act justly, to justify" (EtymDB), built on *iūstus* "fair, righteous" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
