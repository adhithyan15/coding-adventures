## You'll want to know: le règne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C323-REGNE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le règne** — "the reign".

> Pendant son règne, le pays a connu la paix.
>
> *During his reign, the country knew peace.*

Also a kingdom in nature: *le règne animal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*règne* comes from Old French *regne*, from Latin *rēgnum* "kingship, authority" (EtymDB).
