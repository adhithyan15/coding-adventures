## You'll want to know: وقود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C432-WAQUD432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**وقود** — *waqūd* — "fuel".

> اشترينا الوقود من محطة صغيرة.
>
> *ishtaraynā al-waqūd min maḥaṭṭa ṣaghīra.*
>
> We bought fuel at a small station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وقود** (*waqūd*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-q-d*, "to burn, to blaze" (Lane). A *waqūd* is what is burned.
