## You'll want to know: مسافت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C446-JAHAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مسافت** (*masâfat*) — "a distance (to be travelled)".

> مسافت خانه تا مدرسه زیاد نیست.
>
> *Masâfat-e khâne tâ madrese ziyâd nist.*
>
> The distance from home to school isn't great.

Close to *fâsele*, but used for a journey's length.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*masâfat* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *masāfa* 'distance, the length of a journey'. EtymDB does not list it.
