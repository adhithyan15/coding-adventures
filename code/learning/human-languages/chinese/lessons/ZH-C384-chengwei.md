## You'll want to know: 成为
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C384-BECOME384-02]; assesses=[] -->

**成为** — *chéngwéi* — "to become".

> **他想成为一个医生** *tā xiǎng chéngwéi yí ge yīshēng* — He wants to become a doctor.

The new character, in pieces: 成 — six strokes: a short bar, a long stroke falling to the left, a small turning hook, the long slanting hook of 我, a short fall and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**成为** (*chéngwéi*) is built from 成 'to complete' and 为 'to make', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *hjwe* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to finish' and the second 'to do'.
