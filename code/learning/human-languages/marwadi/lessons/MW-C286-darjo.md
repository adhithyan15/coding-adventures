## You'll want to know: दरजो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C286-IMTIHAN286-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दरजो** — *darjo* — "a class at school".

> यो दरजो बड़ो है।
>
> *yo darjo baṛo hai.*
>
> This class is big.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरजो** (*darjo*) is usually traced, through Persian *darja*, to Arabic *daraja*, "a step, a degree"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
