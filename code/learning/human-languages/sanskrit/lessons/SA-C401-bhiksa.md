## You'll want to know: भिक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C401-BHIKSHU401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भिक्षा** — *bhikṣā* — "alms".

> भिक्षुः गृहे गृहे भिक्षां याचते।
>
> *bhikṣuḥ gṛhe gṛhe bhikṣāṃ yācate.*
>
> The monk asks for alms from house to house.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भिक्षा** (*bhikṣā*) comes from the root √bhikṣ, "to beg"; MW gives *bhikṣā* as begging and as the alms or food a beggar receives (MW).
