## You'll want to know: નીપજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C339-ASAR339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નીપજવું** — *nīpajvũ* — "to result".

> ગરમીને કારણે ઘણા રોગ નીપજે છે.
>
> *garmīne kāraṇe ghaṇā rog nīpje che.*
>
> Many illnesses come from the heat.

Past (it …): તે રોગ ગરમીથી નીપજ્યો, *te rog garmīthī nīpajyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નીપજવું** (*nīpajvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *níṣpadyatē*, "falls out, comes forth"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be produced" (CDIAL 7511).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
