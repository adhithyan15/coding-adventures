## You'll want to know: স্থপতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C501-BISHESHOGGO501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**স্থপতি** — *sthôpôti* — "an architect".

> এই সেতুর স্থপতি একজন মহিলা।
>
> *Ei setur sthôpôti ekjôn môhilā.*
>
> The architect of this bridge is a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্থপতি** (*sthôpôti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sthapati*, which Monier-Williams gives both as "a chief" and as "an architect, a master builder".
