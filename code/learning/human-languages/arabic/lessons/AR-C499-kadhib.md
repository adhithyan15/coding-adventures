# كاذب (kādhib) — untruthful, false

## You'll want to know: كاذب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C499-AMANA499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**كاذب** — *kādhib* — "untruthful, false".

> كان الخبر كاذبا.
>
> *kāna al-khabar kādhiban.*
>
> The news was false.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كاذب** (*kādhib*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-dh-b*, "to lie" (Lane). Kādhib is the active participle of kadhaba, to lie, which you know.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
