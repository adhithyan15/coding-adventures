## You'll want to know: die Präsentation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C354-BESPRECHUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Präsentation** — "the presentation".

> Ihre Präsentation war kurz und sehr klar.
>
> *Her presentation was short and very clear.*

Plural: *die Präsentationen*. *Eine Präsentation halten* — to give a presentation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Präsentation* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *praesentātiō* "a showing", from *praesentāre* "to show, to present", built on *praesēns* "present" (Lewis & Short).
