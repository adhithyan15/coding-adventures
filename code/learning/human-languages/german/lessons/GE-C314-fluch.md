## You'll want to know: der Fluch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C314-ZAUBERN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Fluch** — "the curse".

> Ein Fluch lag auf der alten Burg.
>
> *A curse lay on the old castle.*

Plural: *die Flüche*. Also a swear word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fluch* is inherited from Old High German *fluoh* (EtymDB), related to the verb *fluchen* "to curse, to swear".
