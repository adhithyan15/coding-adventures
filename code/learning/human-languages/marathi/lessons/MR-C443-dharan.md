## You'll want to know: धरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C443-ODHA443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धरण** — *dharaṇ* — "a dam".

> पावसाळ्यात धरण भरतं.
>
> *pāvsāḷyāt dharaṇ bhartaṁ.*
>
> In the rainy season the dam fills up.

Neuter.

A noun of its own: धरण भरणे, a dam fills up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धरण** (*dharaṇ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *dharaṇ*, "dam", under Sanskrit *dharaṇa*, "supporting, a prop" (CDIAL 6742).
