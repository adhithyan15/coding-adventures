Bengali, chapter 78: Night, Evening, Morning, Week, Month.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
রাত (rāt) — night.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for beautiful, then the Bengali for a day.

You'll want to know: রাত.
রাত — rāt — "night".

The word, taken apart.
রাত (rāt) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from rātrī, "night" (CDIAL 10702).

Lesson 2 of 5.
সন্ধ্যা (sôndhyā) — evening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a day, then the Bengali for night.

You'll want to know: সন্ধ্যা.
সন্ধ্যা — sôndhyā — "evening".

The word, taken apart.
সন্ধ্যা (sôndhyā) is Sanskrit saṁdhyā, "twilight", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 12918). The word inherited from it is sāṁjh, "evening", which CDIAL lists.
