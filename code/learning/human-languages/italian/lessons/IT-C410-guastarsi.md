## You'll want to know: guastarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C410-GUASTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**guastarsi** — "to break down; to go off (food)".

> La lavatrice si è guastata ieri.
>
> *The washing machine broke down yesterday.*

Food too: *il latte si è guastato* — the milk has gone off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guastarsi* is the reflexive of *guastare* 'to spoil', which is inherited from Latin *vāstāre* 'to lay waste' (EtymDB).
