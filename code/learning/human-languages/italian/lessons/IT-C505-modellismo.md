## You'll want to know: il modellismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C505-COLLEZIONI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il modellismo** — "model making".

> La sua passione è il modellismo: costruisce treni in miniatura.
>
> *His passion is model making: he builds miniature trains.*

The person is *il modellista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modellismo* is built on *modello* "model", from Vulgar Latin *modellus* (EtymDB), a little form of Latin *modulus* "a small measure" (Lewis & Short).
