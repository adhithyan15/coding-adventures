## You'll want to know: भेग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C521-DURUSTI521-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भेग** — *bheg* — "a fissure; a crack in the ground".

> उन्हाळ्यात शेतात मोठ्या भेगा पडल्या.
>
> *unhāḷyāt śetāt moṭhyā bhegā paḍlyā.*
>
> In the summer big cracks opened in the field.

Feminine. Plural: भेगा, *bhegā*.

भेग पडणे, a crack opens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भेग** (*bheg*) is the word for a split in dry earth or a wall; its origin is not settled.
