Urdu, chapter 297: The Relief Camp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کیمپ (kaimp) — a relief camp; a camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to dart, then the Urdu for safe and sound.

You'll want to know: کیمپ.
کیمپ — kaimp — "a relief camp; a camp".
شہر کے باہر ایک کیمپ تھا۔ shahr ke bāhar ek kaimp thā. There was a camp outside the city.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
is borrowed from English camp. After a flood or an earthquake, people live in a کیمپ (kaimp) for a time.

Lesson 2 of 5.
راحت (rāhat) — relief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for safe and sound, then the Urdu for a relief camp.

You'll want to know: راحت.
راحت — rāhat — "relief".
کیمپ میں لوگوں کو راحت ملی۔ kaimp meṅ logoṅ ko rāhat milī. People found relief in the camp.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
راحت (rāhat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic rāḥa, "rest, ease". It is feminine: راحت ملی.

Lesson 5 of 5.
متاثر (mutāsir) — affected (by a disaster).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a tent, then the Urdu for refuge.

You'll want to know: متاثر.
متاثر — mutāsir — "affected (by a disaster)".
سیلاب سے دو گاؤں متاثر ہوئے۔ sailāb se do gāoṅ mutāsir hue. Two villages were affected by the flood.

The word, taken apart.
متاثر (mutāsir) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mutaʾaththir, "one that has taken an effect", a word from the same family as اثر.
