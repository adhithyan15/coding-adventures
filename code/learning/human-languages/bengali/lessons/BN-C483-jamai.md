## You'll want to know: জামাই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C483-JAMAI483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**জামাই** — *jāmāi* — "a son-in-law".

> জামাই এলে শাশুড়ি মাছ রান্না করেন।
>
> *jāmāi ele shāshuṛi māchh rānnā kôren.*
>
> When the son-in-law visits, his mother-in-law cooks fish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জামাই** (*jāmāi*) comes from Sanskrit *jāmātṛ*, "a daughter's husband" (CDIAL 5198), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form.
