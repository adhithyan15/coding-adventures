## You'll want to know: alerter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C301-ALERTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**alerter** — "to alert, to warn".

> Un voisin a alerté les pompiers.
>
> *A neighbour alerted the fire brigade.*

*Alerter la police* — to call the police.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alerter* is built on *alerte* "alert" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Italian *all'erta* "on the lookout".
