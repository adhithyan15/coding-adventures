## You'll want to know: 保护
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C443-CLEAN443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**保护** — *bǎohù* — "to protect; to keep from harm".

> **我们应该保护河水** *wǒmen yīnggāi bǎohù héshuǐ* — We should keep the river water clean.

The new character, in pieces: 护 — seven strokes in two pieces: on the left the hand piece of 打, three strokes; on the right a dot over a frame and a long stroke falling to the left, four strokes, as on top of 房. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**保护** (*bǎohù*) is built from 保 'to keep' and 护 'to guard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *huH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to keep safe' and the second 'to rescue' and 'to help'.
