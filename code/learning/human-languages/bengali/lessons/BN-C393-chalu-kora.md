## You'll want to know: চালু করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C393-PANGCHAR393-05]; assesses=[] -->

**চালু করা** — *chālu kôrā* — "to start (an engine); to switch on".

> চালক ইঞ্জিন চালু করলেন।
>
> *chālôk injin chālu kôrlen.*
>
> The driver started the engine.

Past (he/she …): তিনি পাখা চালু করলেন, *tini pākhā chālu kôrlen*.

চালু on its own is running, in use: দোকানটা চালু, the shop is open for business.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চালু করা** (*chālu kôrā*) joins চালু, "going, in working order", probably a Bengali formation on চাল, "gait, motion", which CDIAL lists under the reconstructed *calyā* (CDIAL 4722), to করা, to do.
