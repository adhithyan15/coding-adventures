## You'll want to know: die Armut
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C359-GERECHTIGKEIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Armut** — "poverty".

> In vielen Ländern leben Kinder in großer Armut.
>
> *In many countries children live in great poverty.*

No plural. Built on *arm* — poor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Armut* is inherited from Middle High German *armuot* (EtymDB), built on *arm* "poor" with an old noun ending; EtymDB lists Dutch *armoede* as a cousin.
