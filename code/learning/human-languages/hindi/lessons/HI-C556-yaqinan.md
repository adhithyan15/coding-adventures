## You'll want to know: यक़ीनन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C556-SANDEH556-02]; assesses=[] -->

**यक़ीनन** — *yaqīnan* — "certainly, surely".

> यक़ीनन वह कल आएगा।
>
> *Yaqīnan vah kal āegā.*
>
> He will certainly come tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यक़ीनन** (*yaqīnan*) is usually traced to Arabic *yaqīnan*, "certainly", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
