ওপরে — opôre — "above, upstairs".

The word, taken apart.
ওপরে (opôre) is opôr, "the top", which CDIAL lists as Bengali upar under uppari, "above" (CDIAL 2333), + -e, "at".

Lesson 5 of 5.
কোথাও (kothāo) — somewhere.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for all around, then the Bengali for above.

You'll want to know: কোথাও.
কোথাও — kothāo — "somewhere".

The word, taken apart.
কোথাও (kothāo) is kothā, "where", + -o, "also, even". Its head is the question stem ka- (CDIAL 2574); CDIAL has no entry for kothā, so the rest is not settled.
