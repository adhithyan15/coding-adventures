# ಹೇಡಿ (hēḍi) — a coward

## You'll want to know: ಹೇಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C504-MATU504-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೇಡಿ** — *hēḍi* — "a coward".

> ಹೇಡಿ ಆಗಬೇಡ ಅಂತ ಅಪ್ಪ ಹೇಳಿದರು.
>
> *hēḍi āgabēḍa anta appa hēḷidaru.*
>
> Father said, do not be a coward.

The opposite is ಧೈರ್ಯವಂತ, brave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೇಡಿ** (*hēḍi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4434 sets Kannada *hēḍi* beside Tamil *pēṭi*, where fear is one of the senses.
