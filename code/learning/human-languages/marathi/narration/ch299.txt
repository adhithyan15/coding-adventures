Marathi, chapter 299: The Investigation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
झडती घेणे (jhaḍtī gheṇe) — to search a place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a mob, then the Marathi for to whisper.

You'll want to know: झडती घेणे.
झडती घेणे — jhaḍtī gheṇe — "to search a place".
पोलिसांनी संशयिताच्या घराची झडती घेतली. polisānnī saṁśayitācyā gharācī jhaḍtī ghetlī. The police searched the suspect's house.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने झडती घेतली, tyāne jhaḍtī ghetlī.
What is searched takes -ची: घराची झडती घेणे (jhaḍtī gheṇe). झडती is feminine.

The word, taken apart.
झडती घेणे (jhaḍtī gheṇe) joins jhaḍtī, 'a search', to gheṇe, 'to take'; the origin of jhaḍtī is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पंचनामा (pañcnāmā) — a scene report; an official record of a scene.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to whisper, then the Marathi for to search a place.

You'll want to know: पंचनामा.
पंचनामा — pañcnāmā — "a scene report; an official record of a scene".
हवालदाराने घटनास्थळी पंचनामा केला. havāldārāne ghaṭnāsthaḷī pañcnāmā kelā. The constable drew up an official report at the scene.
Masculine.
पंचनामा (pañcnāmā) करणे, to draw one up, signed by witnesses.

The word, taken apart.
पंचनामा (pañcnāmā) is built from pañc, 'five; the panel of witnesses', from Sanskrit pañca (Monier-Williams), and Persian nāma, 'a writing, a document'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
जप्त करणे (japta karṇe) — to confiscate; to seize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to search a place, then the Marathi for a scene report.

You'll want to know: जप्त करणे.
जप्त करणे — japta karṇe — "to confiscate; to seize".
पोलिसांनी चोरीची गाडी जप्त केली. polisānnī corīcī gāḍī japta kelī. The police seized the stolen car.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने जप्त केलं, tyāne japta kelaṁ.
What is seized agrees with the past: गाडी जप्त केली, पैसे जप्त केले.

The word, taken apart.
जप्त करणे (japta karṇe) joins japta, 'seized', a loan from Persian zabt, usually traced to Arabic ḍabṭ, 'a seizing', to karṇe, 'to do'.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: कबूल करणे.
कबूल करणे — kabūl karṇe — "to confess; to admit".
चौकशीत त्याने चोरी कबूल केली. caukśīt tyāne corī kabūl kelī. During questioning he confessed to the theft.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कबूल केलं, tyāne kabūl kelaṁ.
What is admitted agrees: गुन्हा कबूल केला, चूक कबूल केली.

The word, taken apart.
कबूल करणे (kabūl karṇe) joins kabūl, 'accepted, admitted', a loan from Persian qabūl, usually traced to Arabic qabūl, 'acceptance', to karṇe, 'to do'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दाखल करणे (dākhal karṇe) — to file; to admit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to confiscate, then the Marathi for to confess.

You'll want to know: दाखल करणे.
दाखल करणे — dākhal karṇe — "to file; to admit".
तिने पोलीस ठाण्यात तक्रार दाखल केली. tine polīs ṭhāṇyāt takrār dākhal kelī. She filed a complaint at the police station.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दाखल केलं, tyāne dākhal kelaṁ.
रुग्णालयात दाखल करणे (dākhal karṇe), to admit to hospital; गुन्हा दाखल करणे (dākhal karṇe), to register a case.

The word, taken apart.
दाखल करणे (dākhal karṇe) joins dākhal, 'entered, admitted', a loan from Persian dāxil, usually traced to Arabic dākhil, 'entering', to karṇe, 'to do'.
