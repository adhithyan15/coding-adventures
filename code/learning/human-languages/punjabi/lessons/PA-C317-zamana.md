## You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਮਾਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C317-PAST317-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਮਾਨਾ** — *zamānā* — "an era".

> ਉਸ ਜ਼ਮਾਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੋਕ ਪੈਦਲ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਸਨ।
>
> *us zamāne vich lok paidal jānde san.*
>
> In those days people went on foot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਮਾਨਾ** (*zamānā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *zamān*, "time, a period".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
