## You'll want to know: superar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C572-CAMBIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**superar** — "to overcome, to get over; to beat".

> Le costó mucho superar la muerte de su padre.
>
> *It was very hard for him to get over his father's death.*

It also means to exceed: *superar un récord*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*superar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *superāre* 'to go over, to rise above' (EtymDB), built on *super* 'over'.
