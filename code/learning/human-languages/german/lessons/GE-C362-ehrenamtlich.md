## You'll want to know: ehrenamtlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C362-EHRENAMT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ehrenamtlich** — "unpaid, voluntary".

> Mein Großvater arbeitet ehrenamtlich in der Bibliothek.
>
> *My grandfather works as a volunteer in the library.*

*Ehrenamtlich arbeiten* — to do voluntary work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ehrenamtlich* is built with *-lich* on *Ehrenamt*, from *Ehre* "honour", Old High German *ēra*, and *Amt* "office", Middle High German *ambahte* (EtymDB).
