## You'll want to know: 后妈
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C509-PARENTS509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**后妈** — *hòumā* — "stepmother".

> *tā xiǎoshíhou gēn hòumā zhù le wǔ nián* — As a child he lived with his stepmother for five years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**后妈** (*hòumā*) is built from 后 'after' and 妈 'mum', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwX* and *muX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'after' and the second 'a mother'. It belongs to everyday speech.
