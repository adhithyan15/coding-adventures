## You'll want to know: diàntī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C192-SIGN192-02]; assesses=[] -->

*diàntī* — "lift; elevator".

> *diàntī huài le* — The lift is out of order.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diàntī* is built from 电 'electricity' and *tī* 'a ladder', the *tī* of *lóutī*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *thej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as wooden steps.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
