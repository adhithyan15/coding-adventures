## You'll want to know: a cela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C296-ALGEMAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a cela** — "the cell".

> A cela era pequena e fria.
>
> *The cell was small and cold.*

Also a monk’s cell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cela* is inherited from Latin *cella* "a small room, a storeroom" (EtymDB).
