## HL-C286 — Gujarati's remaining retrieval debt is its opening seven chapters

Found while adding Gujarati chapters 29-34 (49 lessons, 20 new pre-A1
headwords, 52 -> 72). This tranche moved the numbers the right way and, unlike
the one before it, moved the reinforcement figure the right way too: whole-track
misses **339 -> 283** on a track that grew 179 -> 228, R4 misses **101 -> 43**,
atoms never revisited at all **5 -> 1**, ear-drivable share **56% -> 65%**, and
the three script zeros held at zero. HL-C271's fifth-return slab is built
(chapter 30) and its oral-only time words are written (chapter 29).

What is left is now concentrated rather than diffuse, and worth naming precisely.

**1. The remaining 43 R4 misses are almost all chapters 1-7 and 8-11.**
The core-verb slab serviced positions 82-98. It did not touch positions 0-81,
which are the greeting, the courtesy words, the twenty-odd script forms of the
first-use and final-closure chapters, and the introduction and wellbeing
exchanges. The R4 bridges of chapters 19-23 placed those returns at distance 61,
which was R3 at the time and is not R4 now that the track is long enough to
measure them. The fix is the same shape as chapter 30 — a second fifth-return
slab, roughly eight zero-new-atom lessons, targeting positions 0-81 — and it is
now the single largest item in the figure.

**2. R3 rose 115 -> 117 because the last two chapters cannot service themselves.**
A chapter's own atoms need a return 20 to 60 positions later. The atoms of
chapters 33 and 34 sit inside the last twenty lessons of the book, so their third
window opens past the end of it for some and only just inside it for others. This
is not a defect in those chapters; it is what "third window" means near a
boundary. It clears itself the moment another chapter is added after them, which
is a reason to keep the next tranche's first chapter cheap and returns-heavy.

**3. Two facts about the vocabulary target that authoring cannot move.**

- **The pre-A1 verb-vocabulary blocker is still structural.** Gujarati teaches
  nineteen verb headwords and exactly **one** counts, because the rest hang off
  `SPINE-SAY-WHAT-I-DO`, `SPINE-NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS` and
  `SPINE-SAY-WHAT-I-LIKE`, which the *shared* spine stages at A1/A2. The gate
  wants five at or below pre-A1. HL-C271 said no Gujarati-only change moves this;
  that is still true, and **Tamil has since hit the identical wall and reached
  the identical conclusion**. It is therefore no longer a Gujarati item at all —
  it is a cross-track decision about where those three spine nodes sit, and it
  should be filed and taken as one.
- **The next 228 headwords will cost script lessons, and this tranche did not.**
  Every word in chapters 31-34 was chosen so its spelling uses only the 43 forms
  the book already teaches, which is why twenty words cost zero script debt. The
  set of common Gujarati words spellable inside those 43 is not inexhaustible:
  **ડ**, **ઠ**, **ઝ**, **ફ**, **ષ**, the independent **ઇ/ઊ/ઓ/ઔ** and the **ૌ**
  sign are all untaught, and they block ordinary words — *āṭh* (eight), *ṭhaṇḍũ*
  (cold), *phūl* (flower), *jhāḍ* (tree), *phaḷ* (fruit). Teaching **ઠ** alone
  unlocks the numbers six to ten, which is the highest-value blocked set in the
  language. Whoever takes the next tranche should plan one or two glyph lessons
  into it rather than steering around them, and must place each one **before**
  first use in reading order, because closure is measured in reading order and
  a glyph taught late cannot retire an earlier violation.

**Two smaller things, both deliberate, both left open.**

- Four Gujarati lessons remain measurement-blind and three atom spikes remain
  (two lesson, one chapter). This tranche added none of either, and cleared none
  of them; they are all in chapters 1-18.
- Chapter 29 wrote six words that had been oral. Chapters 31-34 deliberately did
  not: sixteen of the twenty new headwords are still ear-only, which is the
  gloss-first-then-glyph order rather than an omission. Their writing return is
  a later chapter's work and needs no new glyph.
