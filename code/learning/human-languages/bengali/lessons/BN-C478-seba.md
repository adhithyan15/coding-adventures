## You'll want to know: সেবা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C478-JOTNO478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সেবা** — *sebā* — "nursing; service".

> মেয়ের সেবায় বাবা তাড়াতাড়ি সুস্থ হলেন।
>
> *meyer sebāy bābā tāṛātāṛi susthô hôlen.*
>
> With his daughter's nursing, the father soon got well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সেবা** (*sebā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sevā*, "attendance on, homage" (CDIAL 13595).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
