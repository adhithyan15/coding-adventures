## You'll want to know: crēbrō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C294-IDENTIDEM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**crēbrō** — "frequently, often".

> Ad vīllam avī crēbrō veniēbāmus.
>
> *We often used to come to our grandfather's villa.*

The adverb of *crēber* (taught), 'thick, close-packed, frequent'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**crēbrō** is the ablative of *crēber*, "close-packed, frequent", used as an adverb (EtymDB).
