## You'll want to know: のぞく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C304-ONGAESHI304-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のぞく** — *nozoku* — "to peep; to peek".

> **おばあさんは へやの なかを のぞきました。** — *obāsan wa heya no naka o nozokimashita* — "The old woman peeped into the room."

Polite **のぞきます**, *nozokimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが へやを のぞきました。**, *otoko ga heya o nozokimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のぞく** (*nozoku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
