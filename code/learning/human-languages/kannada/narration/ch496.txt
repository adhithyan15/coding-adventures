Kannada, chapter 496: Hope and Longing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಂಬಲಿಸು (hambalisu) — to long for; to yearn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for motherly love, then the Kannada for care.

You'll want to know: ಹಂಬಲಿಸು.
ಹಂಬಲಿಸು — hambalisu — "to long for; to yearn".
ವಿದೇಶದಲ್ಲಿದ್ದ ಮಗ ಅಮ್ಮನ ಅಡುಗೆಗಾಗಿ ಹಂಬಲಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. vidēśadallidda maga ammana aḍugegāgi hambalisuttiddanu. The son living abroad used to long for his mother's cooking.
Past (I …): ಹಂಬಲಿಸಿದೆ, hambaliside.
What you long for takes -ಗಾಗಿ. The noun is ಹಂಬಲ, a longing.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಂಬಲಿಸು (hambalisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3931 lists Kannada pambal, "ardent desire", and pambalisu, "to desire ardently". Old Kannada p- became h- in the modern language.

ಮಾರಿದ ಹಳೆಯ ಮನೆಗಾಗಿ ಅಜ್ಜ ಹಳಹಳಿಸಿದರು. mārida haḷeya manegāgi ajja haḷahaḷisidaru. Grandfather pined for the old house that had been sold.
Past (I …): ಹಳಹಳಿಸಿದೆ, haḷahaḷiside.
A longing mixed with regret; ಹಳಹಳಿಕೆ is the feeling.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಳಹಳಿಸು (haḷahaḷisu) is a native Kannada verb for longing after something gone; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಭರವಸೆ (bharavase) — hope; assurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to long for, then the Kannada for to rue a loss.

You'll want to know: ಭರವಸೆ.
ಭರವಸೆ — bharavase — "hope; assurance".
ವೈದ್ಯರ ಮಾತಿನಿಂದ ನಮಗೆ ಭರವಸೆ ಬಂದಿತು. vaidyara mātininda namage bharavase banditu. The doctor's words gave us hope.
ಭರವಸೆ (bharavase) ಕೊಡು, to give an assurance, to promise.

The word, taken apart.
ಭರವಸೆ (bharavase) is usually traced to Hindustani bharosā, "trust, reliance"; the path into Kannada is not settled. In Kannada it is both hope and a promise you can lean on.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕಾತರ (kātara) — eagerness; anxious longing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to rue a loss, then the Kannada for hope.

You'll want to know: ಕಾತರ.
ಕಾತರ — kātara — "eagerness; anxious longing".
ಫಲಿತಾಂಶಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳು ಕಾತರದಿಂದ ಕಾಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. phalitāṃśakkāgi vidyārthigaḷu kātaradinda kāyuttiddaru. The students were waiting eagerly for the results.
ಕಾತರದಿಂದ, eagerly, impatiently.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾತರ (kātara) is borrowed from Sanskrit kātara, "confused, discouraged, cowardly" (CDIAL 3027). In Kannada the nervous edge stayed and turned into impatient longing.
