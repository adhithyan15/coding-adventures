# घाऊक (ghāūk) — wholesale

## You'll want to know: घाऊक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C424-VIKRI424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घाऊक** — *ghāūk* — "wholesale".

> तो घाऊक बाजारातून कांदे आणतो.
>
> *to ghāūk bājārātūn kānde āṇto.*
>
> He brings onions from the wholesale market.

It does not change: घाऊक बाजार, घाऊक खरेदी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घाऊक** (*ghāūk*) is a Marathi word for buying and selling in bulk; its origin is not settled.
