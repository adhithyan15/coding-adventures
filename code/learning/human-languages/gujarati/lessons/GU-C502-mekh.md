## You'll want to know: મેખ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C502-SUTHAR502-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મેખ** — *mekh* — "a nail (for wood or a wall)".

> દીવાલમાં એક મેખ છે.
>
> *dīvālmā̃ ek mekh che.*
>
> There is a nail in the wall.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મેખ** (*mekh*) is usually traced to Persian *mīkh*, "a nail, a peg".
