Persian, chapter 78: Journey, Music, News, Country, Story.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
سفر (safar) — a journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a colour, then the Persian for a song.

You'll want to know: سفر.
سفر — safar — "a journey".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: safar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: safar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say safar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for a colour, then the Persian for a song, then say safar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does سفر (safar) mean? ("A journey".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: a minute, today, yesterday, tomorrow, now]

[your turn — say: always, sometimes, early, late, before]

[your turn — say: morning, night, a day, far, near]

(صبح, sobh.) Night? (شب, shab.) A day? (روز, ruz.) Far? (دور, dur.) Near? (نزدیک, nazdik.) Inside? (داخل, dâkhel.) Outside? (بیرون, birun.) Up? (بالا, bâlâ.) Down? (پایین, pâyin.) In front? (جلو, jolow.) Here? (اینجا, injâ.) There? (آنجا, ânjâ.) Right; true? (راست, râst.) Left? (چپ, chap.) A street? (خیابان, khiyâbân.)
