## You'll want to know: دلسوز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C488-CHARACTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دلسوز** (*delsuz*) — "caring, compassionate".

> پرستارهای این بیمارستان خیلی دلسوز هستند.
>
> *parastârhâ-ye in bimârestân kheyli delsuz hastand.*
>
> The nurses at this hospital are very caring.

*del* 'heart' + *suz* 'burning' (stem of *sukhtan*): a heart that burns for others.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delsuz* is built on two native Persian pieces: *del*, 'heart', and *suz*, which EtymDB derives from *sukhtan*, 'to burn' (EtymDB).
