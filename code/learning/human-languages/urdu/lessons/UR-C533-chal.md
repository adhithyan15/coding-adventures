## You'll want to know: چال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C533-JUA533-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چال** — *chāl* — "a move (in a game)".

> اگر وہ یہ چال سمجھ گیا تو جیت جائے گا۔
>
> *agar voh yih chāl samajh gayā to jīt jāe gā.*
>
> If he sees through this move, he will win.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چال** (*chāl*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cāl*, "behaviour", under *\*calyā*, "gait, conduct" (CDIAL 4722); the sense "a move in a game" is a later one that CDIAL does not record.
