Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 220: A Settled Plan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पक्को (pakko) — settled; firm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the Teej festival, then the Marwadi for in a hurry.

You'll want to know: पक्को.
पक्को — pakko — "settled; firm".
बात पक्की है, हूं काल जासूं। bāt pakkī hai, hū̃ kāl jāsū̃. It's settled: I'm going tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
पक्को (pakko) goes back to Sanskrit pakva, "cooked, ripe". Its doubled k matches Hindi pakkā, "ripe, mature, real", which CDIAL takes from Panjabi; the Old Marwari word CDIAL lists in the same entry is pāka, "ripe" (CDIAL 7621), the plain inherited shape. From ripe it has come to mean firm, settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.

त्यार — tyār — "ready".
हूं अबार त्यार हूं। hū̃ abār tyār hū̃. I'm ready now.

The word, taken apart.
त्यार (tyār) is usually traced to Persian taiyār, "ready", which Persian took from Arabic; Marwadi has worn it down to one syllable.

Lesson 3 of 5.
टालणो (ṭālṇo) — to put off; to avoid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for settled, then the Marwadi for ready.

You'll want to know: टालणो.
टालणो — ṭālṇo — "to put off; to avoid".
हूं जातरा कोनी टालसूं। hū̃ jātrā konī ṭālsū̃. I won't put off the journey.
Past (I …): म्हैं जातरा टाली, mhaĩ jātrā ṭālī.

The word, taken apart.
टालणो (ṭālṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit ṭalati, "moves aside", through its causative ṭālayati; CDIAL lists Marwari ṭālṇo, "to pass (time)", and Hindi and Gujarati verbs for averting and avoiding under it (CDIAL 5450). To move a plan aside is to put it off.

हूं काल गेलो बदळसूं। hū̃ kāl gelo badaḷsū̃. Tomorrow I'll change my route.
Past (I …): म्हैं गेलो बदळ्यो, mhaĩ gelo badaḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
बदळणो (badaḷṇo) is usually traced to Persian badal, "an exchange, a substitute", which Persian took from Arabic; Marwadi made a verb of it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सोधणो (sodhṇo) — to find out; to search out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to put off, then the Marwadi for to change.

You'll want to know: सोधणो.
सोधणो — sodhṇo — "to find out; to search out".
हूं डूंगर कनै नुवो गेलो सोधसूं। hū̃ ḍū̃gar kanai nuvo gelo sodhsū̃. I'll search out a new path near the hill.
Past (I …): म्हैं गेलो सोध्यो, mhaĩ gelo sodhyo.

The word, taken apart.
सोधणो (sodhṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit śoddhum, "to purify"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb sodhaï, "finds out", and a Gujarati verb "to search" under it (CDIAL 12626).
