# たちよる (tachiyoru) — to stop by; to drop in on the way

## You'll want to know: たちよる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C392-YORIMICHI392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たちよる** — *tachiyoru* — "to stop by; to drop in on the way".

> **えきの まえの きっさてんに たちよりました。** — *eki no mae no kissaten ni tachiyorimashita* — "I stopped by a cafe in front of the station."

Polite **たちよります**, *tachiyorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あねが はなやに たちよりました。**, *ane ga hanaya ni tachiyorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たちよる** (*tachiyoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tachi*, "standing" (from *tatsu*) and *yoru*, "to drop in"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
