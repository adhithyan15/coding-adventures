## You'll want to know: la promotion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C360-CARRIERE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la promotion** — "the promotion; the special offer".

> Elle a eu une promotion : elle dirige maintenant l'équipe.
>
> *She got a promotion: she now runs the team.*

In shops, *en promotion* — on special offer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*promotion* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōmōtiō* "advancement" (EtymDB), from *prōmovēre* "to move forward", built on *movēre* "to move" (Lewis & Short).
