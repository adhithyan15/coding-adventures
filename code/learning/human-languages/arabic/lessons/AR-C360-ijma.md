## You'll want to know: إجماع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C360-JADAL360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إجماع** — *ijmāʿ* — "consensus".

> وصل الفريق إلى إجماع بعد نقاش طويل.
>
> *waṣala al-farīq ilā ijmāʿ baʿda niqāsh ṭawīl.*
>
> The team reached a consensus after a long discussion.

بالإجماع, *bi-l-ijmāʿ*, unanimously.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إجماع** (*ijmāʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-m-ʿ*, "to gather, to bring together" (Lane). *Ijmāʿ* is the Form IV verbal noun, a bringing together: everyone gathered on one view.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
