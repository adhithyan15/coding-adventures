## You'll want to know: રકમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C192-BILL192-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રકમ** — *rakam* — "amount, sum".

> **આ રકમ સાચી છે.** — *ā rakam sācī che* — "This amount is correct."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રકમ** (*rakam*) is a loan from Arabic *raqam*, "mark, written figure", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *r-q-m*, "to write, to mark" (Lane).
