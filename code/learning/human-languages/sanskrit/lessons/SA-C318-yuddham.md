## You'll want to know: युद्धम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C318-SENA318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**युद्धम्** — *yuddham* — "a war; a battle".

> सीमायां युद्धं भवति।
>
> *sīmāyāṃ yuddhaṃ bhavati.*
>
> There is a war on the border.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**युद्धम्** (*yuddham*) comes from the root √yudh, "to fight"; MW gives *yuddha* as fought and, as a neuter noun, a battle, fight, war (MW).
