## You'll want to know: かみさま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C302-YUUREI302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かみさま** — *kamisama* — "a god; a deity".

> **そふは やまの かみさまに いのりました。** — *sofu wa yama no kamisama ni inorimashita* — "Grandfather prayed to the god of the mountain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かみさま** (*kamisama*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *kami*, "god", and *sama*, "the honorific" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
