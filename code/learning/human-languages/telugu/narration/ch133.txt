Telugu, chapter 133: Clothes, Moustache, Tears, Boat, Train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
గుడ్డలు (guḍḍalu) — clothes, cloth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a blouse, then the Telugu for a lotus.

You'll want to know: గుడ్డలు.
గుడ్డలు — guḍḍalu — "clothes, cloth".

The word, taken apart.
గుడ్డలు (guḍḍalu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kuṭṭai and Kannada guḍḍe (DEDR 1681). DEDR lists Telugu guḍḍa, "cloth, a piece of cloth".

The word, taken apart.
కంటినీరు (kaṇṭinīru) is native Dravidian: kaṇṭi, "of the eye", from kannu (DEDR 1159), and nīru, "water" (DEDR 3690). DEDR compares Sanskrit nīra, "water", with nīru (CDIAL 7552).
