## You'll want to know: क़सम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C543-VACHAN543-02]; assesses=[] -->

**क़सम** — *qasam* — "an oath; a vow".

> मीरा ने क़सम खाई कि वह फिर कभी देर से नहीं आएगी।
>
> *Mīrā ne qasam khāī ki vah phir kabhī der se nahīṃ āegī.*
>
> Mira swore that she would never be late again.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़सम** (*qasam*) is usually traced to Arabic *qasam*, "an oath", through Persian.
