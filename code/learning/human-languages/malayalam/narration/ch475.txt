Malayalam, chapter 475: Ships and Boats.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കപ്പൽ (kappal) — a ship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to hang on, then the Malayalam for to speed along.

You'll want to know: കപ്പൽ.
കപ്പൽ — kappal — "a ship".
തുറമുഖത്ത് ഒരു വലിയ കപ്പൽ വന്നു. tuṟamukhattŭ oru valiya kappal vannu. A big ship came into the harbour.
Plural: കപ്പലുകൾ, kappalukaḷ.
A small boat is a തോണി; a കപ്പൽ (kappal) crosses the sea.

The word, taken apart.
കപ്പൽ (kappal) is native Dravidian, "a ship, a sailing vessel" (DEDR 1219).

Lesson 2 of 5.
നാവികൻ (nāvikan) — a sailor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to speed along, then the Malayalam for a ship.

You'll want to know: നാവികൻ.
നാവികൻ — nāvikan — "a sailor".
നാവികൻ കപ്പലിൽ നിന്ന് കരയിലേക്ക് ഇറങ്ങി. nāvikan kappalil ninnŭ karayilēkkŭ iṟaṅṅi. The sailor came down from the ship onto the land.
The navy is the നാവികസേന, the sailors' army.

The word, taken apart.
നാവികൻ (nāvikan) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāvika, "belonging to a boat", and so a boatman or sailor (Monier-Williams), with the masculine ending -ൻ.

ജെട്ടി — jeṭṭi — "a jetty".
ഞങ്ങൾ ജെട്ടിയിൽ തോണിക്കായി കാത്തുനിന്നു. ñaṅṅaḷ jeṭṭiyil tōṇikkāyi kāttuninnu. We stood waiting for the boat at the jetty.
Boats on the backwaters stop at a ജെട്ടി (jeṭṭi) the way a bus stops at a stop.

The word, taken apart.
ജെട്ടി (jeṭṭi) was borrowed from English jetty, a landing stage for boats.

വള്ളം — vaḷḷaṁ — "a country boat".
രാവിലെ ആ വള്ളം കായലിലൂടെ പോയി. rāvile ā vaḷḷaṁ kāyalilūṭe pōyi. In the morning that boat went along the backwaters.
You met it inside വള്ളംകളി, the boat race. A boatman is a വള്ളക്കാരൻ.

The word, taken apart.
വള്ളം (vaḷḷaṁ) is native Dravidian; DEDR gives the Malayalam word as a canoe hollowed from one trunk (DEDR 5315).

Lesson 5 of 5.
തുഴ (tuḻa) — an oar; a paddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a jetty, then the Malayalam for a country boat.

You'll want to know: തുഴ.
തുഴ — tuḻa — "an oar; a paddle".
വള്ളക്കാരൻ രണ്ട് തുഴകൾ എടുത്തു. vaḷḷakkāran raṇṭŭ tuḻakaḷ eṭuttu. The boatman picked up two oars.
Plural: തുഴകൾ, tuḻakaḷ.
The verb is തുഴയുക, to row: a തുഴ (tuḻa) is what you row with.

The word, taken apart.
തുഴ (tuḻa) is native Dravidian, the noun of the root of തുഴയുക, to row or paddle (DEDR 3356).
