## You'll want to know: అవునా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C78-YESNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అవునా** (*avunā*) — "is it so?".

Every question you have asked so far needed a question *word*. This one needs none: take అవును, "yes", and add **-ఆ**.

That **-ఆ** is the whole yes-or-no machine. Hang it on the end of any statement and it becomes a question.

## Grammar Lens: -ఆ makes any statement a question
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C78-YESNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**మీరు వెళ్తారు** "you go" becomes **మీరు వెళ్తారా?** "do you go?". **తెలుగు కష్టం** becomes **తెలుగు కష్టమా?**. Nothing moves and no word is added: the last vowel lengthens into ఆ and the sentence is a question. This is the commonest question type in any listening paper, and it is one ending.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A question with no question word in it.
