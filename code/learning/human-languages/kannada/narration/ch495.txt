Kannada, chapter 495: Love and Care.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುದ್ದಿಸು (muddisu) — to cuddle; to fondle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to lose heart, then the Kannada for bewilderment.

You'll want to know: ಮುದ್ದಿಸು.
ಮುದ್ದಿಸು — muddisu — "to cuddle; to fondle".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಮೊಮ್ಮಗನನ್ನು ಮುದ್ದಿಸಿದರು. ajji mommaganannu muddisidaru. Grandmother cuddled her grandson.
Past (I …): ಮುದ್ದಿಸಿದೆ, muddiside.
The noun ಮುದ್ದು is fondness, and a pet word for a little one: ಮುದ್ದು ಮಗು, a darling child.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುದ್ದಿಸು (muddisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4960 lists Kannada muttu and muddu, "a kiss", with words for caressing and fondling; -isu makes the verb.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮುತ್ತಿಡು (muttiḍu) — to kiss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for bewilderment, then the Kannada for to cuddle.

You'll want to know: ಮುತ್ತಿಡು.
ಮುತ್ತಿಡು — muttiḍu — "to kiss".
ಅಮ್ಮ ಮಗುವಿನ ಕೆನ್ನೆಗೆ ಮುತ್ತಿಟ್ಟಳು. amma maguvina kennege muttiṭṭaḷu. The mother kissed the baby's cheek.
Past (I …): ಮುತ್ತಿಟ್ಟೆ, muttiṭṭe.
ಮುತ್ತು, a kiss + ಇಡು, to put: what is kissed takes -ಗೆ. ಮುತ್ತು is also a pearl.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುತ್ತಿಡು (muttiḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: muttu, "a kiss" (DEDR 4960), and iḍu, "to put, to place" (DEDR 442). DEDR also records muddiḍu, "to kiss".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to cuddle, then the Kannada for to kiss.

You'll want to know: ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ.
ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ — vātsalya — "tender affection".
ಅಜ್ಜನ ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ ನನಗೆ ಇನ್ನೂ ನೆನಪಿದೆ. ajjana vātsalya nanage innū nenapide. I still remember grandfather's tender love.
The love of an elder for a little one; Sanskrit vatsa is a calf.

The word, taken apart.
ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ (vātsalya) is borrowed from Sanskrit vātsalya, "affection" (CDIAL 11506), built on vatsala, "fond", from vatsa, "a calf".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಮತೆ (mamate) — motherly love; deep attachment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to kiss, then the Kannada for tender affection.

You'll want to know: ಮಮತೆ.
ಮಮತೆ — mamate — "motherly love; deep attachment".
ಅಮ್ಮನ ಮಮತೆಗೆ ಬೆಲೆ ಕಟ್ಟಲು ಆಗುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. ammana mamatege bele kaṭṭalu āguvudilla. A mother's love cannot be priced.
From Sanskrit ಮಮ, mine: the feeling that someone is your very own.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಮತೆ (mamate) is borrowed from Sanskrit mamatā, "the state of mine, a sense of ownership, attachment" (Monier-Williams), built on mama, "mine".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಾಳಜಿ (kāḷaji) — care; concern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for tender affection, then the Kannada for motherly love.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಳಜಿ.
ಕಾಳಜಿ — kāḷaji — "care; concern".
ದಾದಿಗೆ ರೋಗಿಗಳ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಕಾಳಜಿ ಇದೆ. dādige rōgigaḷa bagge tumbā kāḷaji ide. The nurse cares a great deal about the patients.
Care for someone: … ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಕಾಳಜಿ (kāḷaji).

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಳಜಿ (kāḷaji) is usually traced to Marathi kāḷjī, "care, anxious concern"; its earlier history is not settled.
