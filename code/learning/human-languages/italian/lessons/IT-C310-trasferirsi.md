## You'll want to know: trasferirsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C310-TRASFERIRSI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**trasferirsi** — "to move (house or town)".

> Ci siamo trasferiti in campagna.
>
> *We moved to the country.*

For a job too: *trasferirsi per lavoro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trasferirsi* is built on *trasferire* "to transfer", from Latin *trānsferre* "to carry across" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
