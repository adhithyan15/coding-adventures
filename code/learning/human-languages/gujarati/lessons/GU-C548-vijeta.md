## You'll want to know: વિજેતા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C548-TEAM548-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વિજેતા** — *vijetā* — "a winner".

> જો તું છેલ્લી બાજી જીતીશ તો તું વિજેતા બનીશ.
>
> *jo tũ chellī bājī jītīś to tũ vijetā banīś.*
>
> If you win the last round, you'll be the winner.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિજેતા** (*vijetā*) is a tatsama, from Sanskrit *vijetṛ*, "a conqueror, a victor" (Monier-Williams).
