## You'll want to know: ಕೇಂದ್ರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C465-PATTANA465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇಂದ್ರ** — *kēndra* — "a centre; a hub".

> ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ನಗರದ ಕೇಂದ್ರದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *bas nildāṇa nagarada kēndradalli ide.*
>
> The bus station is in the centre of the city.

ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಕೇಂದ್ರ, an information centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇಂದ್ರ** (*kēndra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kendra*, "the centre of a circle", which Sanskrit itself took from Greek *kentron* (Monier-Williams).
