## You'll want to know: डसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C337-KHAZANA337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डसना** — *ḍasnā* — "to bite (of a snake)".

> नाग ने चोर को डसा।
>
> *nāg ne cor ko ḍasā.*
>
> The cobra bit the thief.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने किसान को डसा, *usne kisān ko ḍasā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डसना** (*ḍasnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *daśati*, "bites, stings" (CDIAL 6230). It is the bite of a snake or a scorpion, not of a dog.
