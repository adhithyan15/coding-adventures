Mandarin Chinese, chapter 370: The Key Point.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
yīnsù — factor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for secondly, then the Mandarin for in addition.

You'll want to know: yīnsù.
yīnsù — "factor".
wǒ juéde tiānqì shì yí ge zhòngyào de yīnsù — I think the weather is an important factor.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yīnsù is built from 因 'a cause' and sù, both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jin and suH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rely on' and 'a cause', and the second 'empty' and 'silk'. How the pair came to mean a factor is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
guānjiàn — key; crucial point.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a factor, then the Mandarin for all things considered.

You'll want to know: guānjiàn.
guānjiàn — "key; crucial point".
wǒ juéde guānjiàn shì shíjiān — I think the key thing is time.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
guānjiàn is built from 关 'to shut' and jiàn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwaen and gjen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, quoting an older dictionary, 'a bar across a door', and the second 'a lock'. Middle Chinese gjen would regularly give qián; the dictionary also lists the character in the rising tone, which fits jiàn.

Lesson 4 of 5.
作用 (zuòyòng) — effect; function.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for all things considered, then the Mandarin for the crucial point.

You'll want to know: 作用.
作用 — zuòyòng — "effect; function".
我说的话没有作用 wǒ shuō de huà méiyǒu zuòyòng — What I said had no effect.

The word, taken apart.
作用 (zuòyòng) is built from 作 'to do' and 用 'to use', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsak and yowngH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to do' and 'to rise', and the second 'to use'.
