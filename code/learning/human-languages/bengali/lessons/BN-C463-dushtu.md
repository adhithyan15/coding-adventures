# দুষ্টু (dushṭu) — naughty; mischievous

## You'll want to know: দুষ্টু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C463-DUSHTU463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**দুষ্টু** — *dushṭu* — "naughty; mischievous".

> দুষ্টু ছেলেটা খাতায় ছবি আঁকল।
>
> *dushṭu chheleṭā khātāy chhôbi ā̃klô.*
>
> The naughty boy drew pictures in his exercise book.

দুষ্টুমি, mischief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দুষ্টু** (*dushṭu*) comes from Sanskrit *duṣṭa*, "defiled, bad" (CDIAL 6472); Bengali softened it into a fond word for a mischievous child.
