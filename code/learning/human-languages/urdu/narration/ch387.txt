Urdu, chapter 387: Porters and Loads.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قلی (qulī) — a porter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a whistle, then the Urdu for to sound.

You'll want to know: قلی.
قلی — qulī — "a porter".
اسٹیشن پر قلی لال قمیض پہنتے ہیں۔ isṭeshan par qulī lāl qamīz pahante haiṅ. At the station the porters wear red shirts.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
قلی (qulī) is often traced to Tamil kūli, "wages, hire" (DEDR 1905): the porter is the man paid by the load. Other sources have been proposed, so the link is likely rather than certain.

Lesson 3 of 5.
لادنا (lādnā) — to load.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a porter, then the Urdu for a trunk.

You'll want to know: لادنا.
لادنا — lādnā — "to load".
مزدور ٹرک پر سامان لادتے ہیں۔ mazdūr ṭrak par sāmān lādte haiṅ. The labourers load the goods onto the lorry.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گاڑی پر سامان لادا, us ne gāṛī par sāmān lādā.

The word, taken apart.
لادنا (lādnā) is inherited from Sanskrit lardayati, "loads" (CDIAL 10966).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ڈھونا (ḍhonā) — to haul; to carry (a heavy load).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a trunk, then the Urdu for to load.

You'll want to know: ڈھونا.
ڈھونا — ḍhonā — "to haul; to carry (a heavy load)".
قلی دن بھر بھاری صندوق ڈھوتے ہیں۔ qulī din bhar bhārī sandūq ḍhote haiṅ. Porters haul heavy trunks all day long.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سامان ڈھویا, us ne sāmān ḍhoyā.

The word, taken apart.
ڈھونا (ḍhonā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ḍhonā, "to convey", under Sanskrit ḍhaukayati, "brings near" (CDIAL 5610).

Lesson 5 of 5.
بٹھانا (biṭhānā) — to seat (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to load, then the Urdu for to haul.

You'll want to know: بٹھانا.
بٹھانا — biṭhānā — "to seat (someone)".
قلی نے سارا کو کھڑکی کے پاس بٹھایا۔ qulī ne sārā ko khiṛkī ke pās biṭhāyā. The porter gave Sara a seat by the window.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو بٹھایا, us ne bachche ko biṭhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
بٹھانا (biṭhānā) is built on بیٹھنا, "to sit", whose Hindi cousin baiṭhnā CDIAL lists under Sanskrit upaviśati, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245): this is the form for making someone sit.
