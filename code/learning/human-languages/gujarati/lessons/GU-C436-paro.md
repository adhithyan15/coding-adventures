## You'll want to know: પારો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C436-HEAT436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પારો** — *pāro* — "mercury; the temperature (on a thermometer)".

> આજે પારો ચાળીસ ડિગ્રી પર પહોંચ્યો.
>
> *āje pāro cāḷīs ḍigrī par pahõcyo.*
>
> Today the temperature reached forty degrees.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પારો** (*pāro*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pārada*, "quicksilver"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8104). The mercury in a thermometer gave the news its પારો.
