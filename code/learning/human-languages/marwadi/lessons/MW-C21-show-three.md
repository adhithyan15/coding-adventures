# Payoff — one request, three counters

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 24s] Recall the seven-word shopping payoff, the five-word transport
payoff, and the seven-word food payoff.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-PERFORMANCE-SHOW-FOUR-SKILL-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-SHOW-REQUEST-01, MW-SCRIPT-SHOW-REQUEST-01, MW-LEX-DIKHAVO-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIKHAVO-01, MW-LEX-YE-01, MW-SCRIPT-YE-01, MW-LEX-KAPDA-01, MW-SCRIPT-KAPDA-01, MW-LEX-SABJI-01, MW-SCRIPT-SABJI-01, MW-LEX-GAADI-01, MW-SCRIPT-GAADI-01] -->

The middle word is the only one that changes.

1. Identify which of the three lines you heard.
2. Produce all three from meaning cues alone.
3. Read three printed lines and say which counter each belongs at.
4. Write all three from dictation, without a model.

Pass each skill separately. Asking to see something is one move; asking what it
costs, saying it is too dear, and settling on a price are still untaught.
