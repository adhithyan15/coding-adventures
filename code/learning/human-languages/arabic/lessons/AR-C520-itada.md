## You'll want to know: اعتاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C520-DAWAM520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتاد** — *iʿtāda* — "to get used to".

> اعتادت الممرضة على العمل في الليل.
>
> *iʿtādat al-mumarriḍa ʿalā al-ʿamal fī al-layl.*
>
> The nurse got used to working at night.

Present: يعتاد, *yaʿtād*.

It takes على, or a direct object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتاد** (*iʿtāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-w-d*, "to return" (Lane). ʿĀda, a habit, which you know, is from the same root: a thing that keeps coming back.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
