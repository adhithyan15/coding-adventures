## You'll want to know: द्रुह्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C461-SATPURUSA461-05]; assesses=[] -->

**द्रुह्यति** — *druhyati* — "bears malice".

> खलः सज्जनाय द्रुह्यति।
>
> *khalaḥ sajjanāya druhyati.*
>
> The scoundrel bears malice towards the good man.

Past (he …): सः मित्राय द्रुग्धवान्, *saḥ mitrāya drugdhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**द्रुह्यति** (*druhyati*) comes from the root √druh, which takes the dative of the person harmed (MW).
