# ঝিমানো (jhimāno) — to doze; to nod off

## You'll want to know: ঝিমানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C479-GHUM479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝিমানো** — *jhimāno* — "to doze; to nod off".

> বাসে বসে দাদু ঝিমাচ্ছিলেন।
>
> *bāse bôse dādu jhimāchchhilen.*
>
> Grandfather was nodding off on the bus.

Past (he/she …): তিনি চেয়ারে ঝিমালেন, *tini cheyāre jhimālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝিমানো** (*jhimāno*) is a Bengali verb for drowsy nodding whose origin is not settled.
