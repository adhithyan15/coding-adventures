## You'll want to know: وراثت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C492-OLDAGE492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**وراثت** — *virāsat* — "an inheritance".

> یہ گھر ہمیں وراثت میں ملا۔
>
> *yih ghar hameṅ virāsat meṅ milā.*
>
> We inherited this house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وراثت** (*virāsat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wirātha*, "inheritance".
