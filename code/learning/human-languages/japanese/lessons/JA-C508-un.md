## You'll want to know: うん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C508-LUCK508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**うん** — *un* — "luck; fortune".

> **もし うんが よかったら、あには しあいに かつでしょう。** — *moshi un ga yokattara, ani wa shiai ni katsu deshō* — "If he is lucky, my elder brother will probably win the match."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うん** (*un*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *un*, "luck" (Middle Chinese *hjunH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
