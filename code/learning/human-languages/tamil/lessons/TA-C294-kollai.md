# கொள்ளை (koḷḷai) — a robbery; plunder

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for an incident, then the Tamil for to steal.

## You'll want to know: கொள்ளை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C294-NIKAZHVU294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கொள்ளை** — *koḷḷai* — "a robbery; plunder".

> அந்த வங்கியில் பெரிய கொள்ளை நடந்தது.
>
> *anta vaṅkiyil periya koḷḷai naṭantatu.*
>
> A big robbery took place at that bank.

A theft by force or on a large scale; கொள்ளை நடந்தது, a robbery took place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கொள்ளை** (*koḷḷai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2151 lists it beside கொள், "to seize, take", so a robbery is at root a seizing.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
