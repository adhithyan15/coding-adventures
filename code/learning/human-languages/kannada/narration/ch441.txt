Kannada, chapter 441: Paying Charges.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸರತಿ (sarati) — a turn in line; a queue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an embassy, then the Kannada for international.

You'll want to know: ಸರತಿ.
ಸರತಿ — sarati — "a turn in line; a queue".
ಬಿಲ್ ಕಟ್ಟಲು ನಾವು ಸರತಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಂತೆವು. bil kaṭṭalu nāvu saratiyalli nintevu. We stood in the queue to pay the bill.
ಸರತಿ (sarati) ಸಾಲು, a queue; ನನ್ನ ಸರತಿ (sarati), my turn.

The word, taken apart.
ಸರತಿ (sarati) is a Kannada word for a turn in a line whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು (mannā māḍu) — to waive; to write off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for international, then the Kannada for a turn in line.

You'll want to know: ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು.
ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು — mannā māḍu — "to waive; to write off".
ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಮಕ್ಕಳ ಶುಲ್ಕವನ್ನು ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡಿತು. hōṭel makkaḷa śulkavannu mannā māḍitu. The hotel waived the fee for the children.
Past (I …): ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, mannā māḍide.
Said of fees, fines and debts: ದಂಡ ಮನ್ನಾ, a fine waived.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನ್ನಾ ಮಾಡು (mannā māḍu) joins mannā, "waived, remitted", which came into Kannada through Hindustani and whose earlier source is not settled, and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ದೇಶೀಯ (dēśīya) — domestic; of the country.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a turn in line, then the Kannada for to waive.

You'll want to know: ದೇಶೀಯ.
ದೇಶೀಯ — dēśīya — "domestic; of the country".
ದೇಶೀಯ ವಿಮಾನಗಳು ಒಂದನೇ ಟರ್ಮಿನಲ್ನಿಂದ ಹೊರಡುತ್ತವೆ. dēśīya vimānagaḷu ondanē ṭarminalninda horaḍuttave. Domestic flights leave from terminal one.
Its partner on airport signs is ಅಂತರರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ, international.

The word, taken apart.
ದೇಶೀಯ (dēśīya) is borrowed from Sanskrit dēśīya, "of the country", built on dēśa, "a region, a country" (CDIAL 6547), the source of ದೇಶ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಗದು (nagadu) — cash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to waive, then the Kannada for domestic.

You'll want to know: ನಗದು.
ನಗದು — nagadu — "cash".
ಈ ಅಂಗಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಗದು ಮಾತ್ರ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾರೆ. ī aṅgaḍiyalli nagadu mātra tegedukoḷḷuttāre. This shop takes cash only.
ನಗದು (nagadu) ಕೊಡು, to pay in cash.

The word, taken apart.
ನಗದು (nagadu) is usually traced to Arabic naqd, "ready money", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.
