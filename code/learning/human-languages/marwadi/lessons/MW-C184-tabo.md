## You'll want to know: तांबो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C184-OKHLI184-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तांबो** — *tā̃bo* — "copper".

> तांबो लाल है।
>
> *tā̃bo lāl hai.*
>
> Copper is red.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तांबो** (*tā̃bo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tāmra*, "dark red; copper"; CDIAL lists Hindi *tā̃bā* (CDIAL 5779). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
