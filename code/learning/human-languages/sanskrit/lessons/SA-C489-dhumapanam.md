## You'll want to know: धूमपानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C489-VYASANA489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धूमपानम्** — *dhūmapānam* — "smoking".

> धूमपानं फुप्फुसं पीडयति।
>
> *dhūmapānaṃ phupphusaṃ pīḍayati.*
>
> Smoking harms the lungs.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूमपानम्** (*dhūmapānam*) is built from **धूमः**, "smoke", and *pāna*, "drinking", from the root √pā of **पिबति**; MW gives "inhalation of smoke or vapour" and "tobacco-smoking" (MW).
