## You'll want to know: xiāngxìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C270-AGREE270-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāngxìn* — "to believe; to trust".

> *wǒ xiāngxìn nǐ* — I believe you.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāngxìn* is built from *xiāng* 'each other' and *xìn* 'to trust', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjang* and *sinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'together' and the second 'faithful'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
