Punjabi, chapter 161: Return to Four More Familiar Actions.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
An old word for swimming — to swim, recalled after a long gap.

This is a return to one word you have already met. You need not swim or perform any movement. Hear a meaning, pause, and recall the word aloud.

Warm-up.
Hear to swim.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say the earlier Punjabi word before the model: ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall its old meaning, to swim. Now hear to swim and say ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā) before the spoken model. No sentence or new grammar is needed.

Wrap-up Recall.
One last cue: to swim.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā). Listen and retry once if it does not come back. This is untimed vocabulary retrieval, not scored A1 writing or mock evidence.


Lesson 2 of 4.
An old word for climbing — to climb, recalled after a long gap.

Keep the previous word for swimming separate. Retrieve just one other familiar action from its meaning; no movement is required.

Warm-up.
Hear to climb.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say the earlier Punjabi word before the model: ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ (caṛhnā).

Guided Practice.
Hear ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ (caṛhnā).
[pause 4 seconds]
Give its old meaning, to climb. Then hear to climb and say ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ (caṛhnā) before the model. This is the same word you already learned, not a new form.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to climb once more.
[pause 4 seconds]
Recall ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ (caṛhnā). If it slips away, hear the model and retry once. This untimed return is separate from scored A1 production.

Lesson 4 of 4.
An old word for using — to use, then a return to four familiar actions.

First retrieve to use on its own. Then hear four already-taught action meanings in a small shuffled mix. Pause before each model. There is no new Punjabi and no scored mock here.

Warm-up.
Hear to use.
[pause 4 seconds]
Say the old word before the model: ਵਰਤਣਾ (vartṇā).

Guided Practice.
Pause after each familiar meaning, then hear its model:
to call —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਬੁਲਾਉਣਾ (bulāuṇā).
to swim —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā).
to use —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਵਰਤਣਾ (vartṇā).
to climb —.
[pause 4 seconds]
ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ (caṛhnā).
Only the order of these four old words has changed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear to climb, to use, to call, then to swim. Pause after each cue and recall its Punjabi word before the model: ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ (caṛhnā), ਵਰਤਣਾ (vartṇā), ਬੁਲਾਉਣਾ (bulāuṇā), ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā). Revisit only the old word you missed.
