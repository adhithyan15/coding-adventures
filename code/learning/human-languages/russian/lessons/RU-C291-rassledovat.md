## You'll want to know: расследовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C291-SLEDOVATEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**расследовать** (*rasslédovat*) — "to investigate".

> Журналисты расследовали эту историю.
>
> *Zhurnalísty rasslédovali étu istóriyu.*
>
> The journalists investigated this story.

One form for both aspects. It takes the accusative. Present *расследую*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*расследовать* is built on *рас-* and *следовать* "to follow", from *след* "a track" (EtymDB lists the verb with no further parent).
