The ā sign now has a cited pen path, and a cited place in a word (its stem
after its consonant's body, before the headline, as the cited आ draws the
same bar). Four lessons gain a stroke-order strip:

- **SA-S06-vowel-sign-aa:** ◌ा by itself: the stem, then the piece of
  headline the printed sign carries.
- **SA-W03-mama-nama-guided-copy, -delayed-copy, -dictation:** the phrase
  मम नाम, word by word: मम's two bodies and its headline, then नाम's bodies
  (न, the stem of ◌ा, म) and its own headline. The space between the words
  breaks the headline, as on the page.

  stroke order. SA-W03-mama-nama-guided-copy told the learner to join the
  two letter headlines of मम; it now follows the strip: the bodies first,
  then one headline across each word.
