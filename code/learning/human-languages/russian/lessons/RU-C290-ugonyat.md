## You'll want to know: угонять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C290-MOSHENNIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**угонять** (*ugonyát*) — "to hijack, to steal (a vehicle)".

> Ночью у соседа угнали машину.
>
> *Nóchyu u soséda ugnáli mashínu.*
>
> During the night the neighbour's car was stolen.

Imperfective; perfective *угнать* (*ugnát*), *угоню*. For a car or a plane; *угон* — a car theft.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*угонять* is built on *у-* "away" and *гнать* "to drive", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *gŭnati*, from PIE *gʷʰen-* "to strike" (EtymDB): to drive away.
