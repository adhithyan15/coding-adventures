## You'll want to know: രീതി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C448-PRATISANDHI448-05]; assesses=[] -->

**രീതി** — *rīti* — "a method; a manner".

> ഓരോരുത്തർക്കും പഠിക്കാൻ ഓരോ രീതിയുണ്ട്.
>
> *ōrōruttarkkuṁ paṭhikkān ōrō rītiyuṇṭŭ.*
>
> Everyone has their own way of learning.

In that way: ആ രീതിയിൽ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**രീതി** (*rīti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rīti*, "a going, a course; a stream" (CDIAL 10751; Monier-Williams). A method is the path a thing follows.
