## You'll want to know: سہمنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C445-FEAR445-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سہمنا** — *sahamnā* — "to cower (in fright)".

> کتے کو دیکھ کر لڑکی سہم گئی۔
>
> *kutte ko dekh kar laṛkī saham gaī.*
>
> The girl cowered when she saw the dog.

Past (she …): وہ سہم گئی, *voh saham gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سہمنا** (*sahamnā*) is usually traced to Persian *sahm*, "fear, dread", made into an Urdu verb with *-nā*.
