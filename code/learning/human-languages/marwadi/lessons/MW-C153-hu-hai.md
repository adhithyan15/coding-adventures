# हूं and है — am and is

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HOON-01, MW-SCRIPT-THIK-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-HE-01, MW-LEX-C47-HEALTH47-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I am fine*, then *he is ill*. You have said *am* and *is* since
the greeting; now they get a name.

## Grammar Lens: हूं … हूं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HOON-01, MW-SCRIPT-THIK-01] -->

In **हूं ठीक हूं।** the first **हूं** is *I* and the last one is *am*: the same
word, at both ends.

> **हूं अठै हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ aṭhai hū̃.* — I am here.

## Grammar Lens: है
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HAI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-HE-01, MW-LEX-RAAM-01] -->

Anyone or anything you talk about takes **है**, *is*: **वो**, **राम**, the tea.

> **राम बठै है।**
>
> *rām baṭhai hai.* — Ram is there.

> **चाय गरम है।**
>
> *chāy garam hai.* — The tea is hot.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01, MW-CONJ-PAN-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ADVERSATIVE-PAN-01, MW-LEX-C117-WHY117-05, MW-GRAMMAR-CAUSAL-KYUNKAI-01, MW-LEX-C48-HEALTH48-01, MW-LEX-C48-HEALTH48-02, MW-LEX-C74-ADJ74-01, MW-LEX-C82-PLACE82-01, MW-LEX-C82-PLACE82-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **हूं अठै हूं पण राम बठै है।** — *hū̃ aṭhai hū̃ paṇ rām baṭhai hai.* — I am here, but Ram is there.]
- [YOU SAY: **हूं राजी हूं क्यूंकै चाय गरम है।** — *hū̃ rājī hū̃ kyū̃kai chāy garam hai.* — I am happy because the tea is hot.]
- [YOU SAY: **वो उदास है।** — *vo udās hai.* — He is sad.]
