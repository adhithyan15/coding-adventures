## You'll want to know: proponer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C585-PROPONE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**proponer** — "to propose, to suggest".

> Propongo salir más temprano.
>
> *I propose leaving earlier.*

Like *poner*: *propongo, propuse, propuesto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proponer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *proponere* 'to put forward', *pro* + *ponere* 'to put', and rebuilt on *poner* (EtymDB).
