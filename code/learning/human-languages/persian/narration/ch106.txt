Persian, chapter 106: Parrot, Pigeon, Peacock, Duck, Bee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طوطی (tuti) — a parrot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fox, then the Persian for a pig.

You'll want to know: طوطی.
طوطی — tuti — "a parrot".

The word, taken apart.
tuti is the word for a parrot; it is sometimes linked with Hindi totā, "parrot", but its origin is not settled.

tâvus is borrowed from Arabic ṭāwūs, "peacock" (EtymDB), a word usually traced to Greek taōs, "peacock".
