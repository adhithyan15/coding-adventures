## You'll want to know: подавать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C393-DOKUMENTY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**подавать** (*podavát*) — "to submit, to hand in; to serve".

> Документы подают до первого июля.
>
> *Dokuménty podayút do pérvogo iyúlya.*
>
> Documents must be submitted by the first of July.

Imperfective; present *подаю, подаёшь*; perfective *подать*. *Подавать заявление* — to put in an application. *Подавать в суд* — to take someone to court. It also means to serve food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**подавать** is built from *под-* "up to, under" and *давать* "to give", the ongoing partner of *дать*: to hand something up to someone.
