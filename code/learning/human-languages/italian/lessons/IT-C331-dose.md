## You'll want to know: la dose
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C331-RICETTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la dose** — "the amount, the quantity (in a recipe); the dose".

> Le dosi sono per quattro persone.
>
> *The amounts are for four people.*

In recipes: *dosi per quattro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dose* is usually traced to Greek *dósis* "a giving", through Medieval Latin *dosis* (EtymDB has no Italian entry).
