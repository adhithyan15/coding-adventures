# Review — tomorrow, and what I will not do

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C251-FUTURE-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I will go tomorrow", politely, then "I will not go", to a friend. (*ashita ikimasu*, *ikanai*.)

- [YOU SAY: I will work next week.]
- [YOU SAY: I will see a film tomorrow. — to a friend]
- [YOU SAY: It will probably snow tomorrow.]
- [YOU SAY: I do not eat meat, and I do not drink coffee. — to a friend]
- [YOU SAY: My younger brother became a teacher.]
