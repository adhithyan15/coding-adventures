Italian, chapter 260: Writing --- My Own Line.
1 lesson. It needs your eyes or your hands, so save it for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 1.
Your own line — the first writing with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Everything you have written so far had something to reach for. You traced ciao, copied it, wrote it with the model covered, and wrote it from the sound.
[pause 1 second]
This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed: only what this book has taught you.

Writing — controlled composition.
[pause 2 seconds]
Write one line about yourself, on paper: your name, then one more fact about you. You hold two ways to give a name — the reflexive verb, and my name is with its article kept. Choose one; nobody is choosing for you.
[pause 2 seconds]
One thing to decide deliberately rather than by accident before you start: the accent on è is a letter, not decoration.
[pause 2 seconds]
With its accent, è is is. Without it, e is and. A hand that drops the accent has not written Italian untidily: il mio nome e Marco says "my name and Marco", and leaves the reader to guess the verb you meant.
[pause 3 seconds]
Write the line. Then read it back and check every e — does it mean is or and? — before you check anything else.

Wrap-up recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Is the accent on è decoration? (No — without it the word is and.)
[pause 2 seconds]
And what did you choose here that the earlier lessons chose for you? (The words.)
