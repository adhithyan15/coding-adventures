## You'll want to know: ひかえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C328-KOUKYOU328-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ひかえる** — *hikaeru* — "to refrain from; to cut down on".

> **ねつが あるから、おふろは ひかえて ください。** — *netsu ga aru kara, ofuro wa hikaete kudasai* — "You have a fever, so please skip the bath."

Polite **ひかえます**, *hikaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちは おさけを ひかえました。**, *chichi wa osake o hikaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひかえる** (*hikaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
