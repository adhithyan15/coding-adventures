## You'll want to know: পরিবেশন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C515-PORIBESHON515-01]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিবেশন** — *pôribeshôn* — "serving food".

> বিয়েতে ওয়েটাররা খাবার পরিবেশন করল।
>
> *Biyete oyeṭārrā khābār pôribeshôn kôrlô.*
>
> At the wedding the waiters served the food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিবেশন** (*pôribeshôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pariveṣaṇa*, "waiting at table, serving up a meal" (Monier-Williams).
