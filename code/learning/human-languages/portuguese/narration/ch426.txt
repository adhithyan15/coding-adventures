Portuguese, chapter 426: No parque de campismo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
acampar — to camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the guesthouse, then the Portuguese for camping.

You'll want to know: acampar.
acampar — "to camp".
Acampámos perto de um lago. We camped near a lake.
Acampar in the wild is not allowed everywhere.

The word, taken apart.
acampar was borrowed from Italian accampare 'to set up camp' (EtymDB), built on campo 'field', from Latin campus.

Lesson 3 of 5.

a autocaravana — "the camper van, the motorhome".
Viajámos pelo país de autocaravana. We travelled round the country in a camper van.
In Brazil: o motorhome.

The word, taken apart.
autocaravana is built from auto- 'self-moving', from Greek autós 'self', and caravana, which comes from Persian kārvān (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
a estaca — the tent peg; the stake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the caravan, then the Portuguese for the camper van.

You'll want to know: a estaca.
a estaca — "the tent peg; the stake".
O vento arrancou as estacas da tenda. The wind pulled out the tent pegs.
A thicker estaca holds up a young tree or a fence.

The word, taken apart.
estaca is usually traced to a Germanic word for a pointed stick; EtymDB lists English stake and Old French estaque as its cousins.

You'll want to know: o mosquiteiro.
o mosquiteiro — "the mosquito net".
Sem mosquiteiro, ninguém dorme no verão. Without a mosquito net, nobody sleeps in summer.
Built on o mosquito.

The word, taken apart.
mosquiteiro is built on mosquito, a small form of mosca 'fly', which is inherited from Latin musca (Lewis & Short).
