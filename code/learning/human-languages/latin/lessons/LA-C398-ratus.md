## You'll want to know: ratus, rata, ratum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C398-PRAESTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ratus, rata, ratum** — "fixed, settled, valid".

> Pactum ratum est.
>
> *The pact is valid.*

*ratus, rata, ratum*, the participle of *reor*, to reckon. *Ratum habēre*, to ratify; *prō ratā parte*, in proportion, gives English *pro rata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ratus* is the participle of *reor* "to reckon, to think" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): reckoned, so settled. English *pro rata* and *rate* come from it.
