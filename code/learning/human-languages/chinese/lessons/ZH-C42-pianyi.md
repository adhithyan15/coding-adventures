## You'll want to know: piányi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C42-SHOP42-04]; assesses=[] -->

*piányi* — "cheap".

> *zhège hěn piányi* — This one's cheap.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piányi* is built from two old words, both inherited from Middle Chinese: *bjien* 'at ease' and *ngje* 'fitting' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The sense 'cheap' is not given there, so how the pair came to mean it is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
