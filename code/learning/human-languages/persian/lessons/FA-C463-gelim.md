## You'll want to know: گلیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C463-SOWGHATI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گلیم** (*gelim*) — "a kilim, a flat-woven rug".

> روی زمین اتاق یک گلیم قرمز بود.
>
> *Ru-ye zamin-e otâq yek gelim-e qermez bud.*
>
> There was a red kilim on the floor of the room.

A *gelim* has no pile, unlike a *farsh* or *qâli*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gelim* is a Persian word that EtymDB links with Greek *kálymma* 'a cover, a covering'.
