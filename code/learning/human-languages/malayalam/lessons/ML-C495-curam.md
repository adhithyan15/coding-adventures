## You'll want to know: ചുരം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C495-MALA495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ചുരം** — *curaṁ* — "a mountain pass; a ghat road".

> ബസ് പതുക്കെ ചുരം കയറി.
>
> *basŭ patukke curaṁ kayaṟi.*
>
> The bus slowly climbed the ghat road.

The road that bends up through the hills; its hairpin bends are ഹെയർപിൻ വളവുകൾ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചുരം** (*curaṁ*) is native Dravidian, "a narrow and difficult path, a pass" (DEDR 2683).
