## You'll want to know: वाहन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C400-VAHAN400-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहन** — *vāhan* — "a vehicle".

> या रस्त्यावर मोठ्या वाहनांना बंदी आहे.
>
> *yā rastyāvar moṭhyā vāhanānnā bandī āhe.*
>
> Large vehicles are banned on this road.

Neuter.

A formal, general word; the plural before an ending is वाहनां-: वाहनांना.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहन** (*vāhan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vāhana*, "conveying; a vehicle, a mount" (Monier-Williams).
