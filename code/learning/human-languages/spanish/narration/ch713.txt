Spanish, chapter 713: Pasarlo bien.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la diversión — fun, entertainment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to cheer up, then the Spanish for relief.

You'll want to know: la diversión.
la diversión — "fun, entertainment".
Para los niños, la playa es pura diversión. For children, the beach is pure fun.
¡Qué diversión! — What fun!

The word, taken apart.
diversión is built on divertir, from Latin dīvertere "to turn aside" (EtymDB), from vertere "to turn": fun is what turns you away from your cares.

Lesson 4 of 5.
aburrirse — to get bored.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for entertaining, then the Spanish for funny.

You'll want to know: aburrirse.
aburrirse — "to get bored".
Me aburro en las reuniones largas. I get bored in long meetings.
Reflexive: me aburro. Aburrir without se is to bore someone.

The word, taken apart.
aburrirse is inherited from Latin abhorrēre "to shrink from in horror", through a spoken form abhorrīre (EtymDB); English abhor is the same word.
