Russian, chapter 429: Checking in and out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
администратор (administrátor) — a receptionist (at a hotel); an administrator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to spend the night, then the Russian for a booking.

You'll want to know: администратор.
администратор (administrátor) — "a receptionist (at a hotel); an administrator".
Администратор дал нам ключ от номера. Administrátor dal nam klyuch ot nómera. The receptionist gave us the key to the room.
Masculine, for a woman too. The desk itself is often called ресепшен.

The word, taken apart.
администратор (administrátor) is usually traced to Latin administrātor 'a manager' (EtymDB lists the Latin word), from administrāre 'to attend to, to manage'. EtymDB does not list the Russian word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
заселяться (zaselyátsya) — to check into a hotel; to move in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a booking, then the Russian for a receptionist.

You'll want to know: заселяться.
заселяться (zaselyátsya) — "to check into a hotel; to move in".
В гостиницу можно заселяться после двух часов. V gostínitsu mózhno zaselyátsya pósle dvukh chasóv. You can check in to the hotel after two o'clock.
Imperfective; perfective заселиться. Takes в + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
заселяться (zaselyátsya) is built from за-, селить 'to settle' and -ся. Селить goes with село 'a village', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic selo (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
выселяться (vyselyátsya) — to check out; to move out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a receptionist, then the Russian for to check into a hotel.

You'll want to know: выселяться.
выселяться (vyselyátsya) — "to check out; to move out".
Из номера надо выселяться до двенадцати. Iz nómera nádo vyselyátsya do dvenádtsati. You have to check out of the room before twelve.
Imperfective; the perfective is выселиться. In hotels people also say выезжать.

The word, taken apart.
выселяться (vyselyátsya) is built from вы- 'out', селить 'to settle' and -ся. Селить goes with село 'a village', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic selo (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
залог (zalóg) — a deposit (security).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to check into a hotel, then the Russian for to check out.

You'll want to know: залог.
залог (zalóg) — "a deposit (security)".
При заселении мы оставили залог. Pri zaselénii my ostávili zalóg. When we checked in we left a deposit.
Masculine. Под залог (zalóg) — against a deposit; also on bail.

The word, taken apart.
залог (zalóg) is built from за- and -лог, the root of положить 'to lay': something laid down as a pledge. EtymDB lists the word with the senses 'pledge, deposit, security'.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: продлевать.
продлевать (prodlevát) — "to extend (a stay, a visa)".
Каждый месяц он продлевает визу. Kázhdyy mésyats on prodleváyet vízu. Every month he extends his visa.
Imperfective; perfective продлить (продлю, продлишь).

The word, taken apart.
продлевать (prodlevát) is built from про- and длить 'to make long', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic dĭliti (EtymDB).
