## You'll want to know: धाप लागणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C492-UCAKI492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धाप लागणे** — *dhāp lāgṇe* — "to get breathless".

> जिना चढल्यावर आजीला धाप लागली.
>
> *jinā caḍhlyāvar ājīlā dhāp lāglī.*
>
> Grandmother was out of breath after climbing the stairs.

Past (to him …): त्याला धाप लागली, *tyālā dhāp lāglī*.

Dative: मला धाप लागली; धापा टाकणे, to pant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धाप लागणे** (*dhāp lāgṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on धाप, "panting", which CDIAL lists under the expressive *\*dhapp* (CDIAL 6729), with लागणे.
