Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 179: Grain and Spice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सिल (sil) — a grinding stone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to scatter, then the Marwadi for to hand over.

You'll want to know: सिल.
सिल — sil — "a grinding stone".
रसोई मांय एक भारी सिल है। rasoī mā̃y ek bhārī sil hai. There is a heavy grinding stone in the kitchen.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सिल (sil) is inherited from Sanskrit śilā, "a rock", also "the lower millstone"; CDIAL lists Hindi sil, "a flat grinding stone" (CDIAL 12459). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

दादी सिल माथै मिरच कूटै है। dādī sil māthai mirach kūṭai hai. Grandmother pounds chillies on the grinding stone.

कूटणो (kūṭṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit kuṭṭayati, "crushes, pounds"; CDIAL lists Hindi kūṭnā and Gujarati kuṭvũ (CDIAL 3241). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a grinding stone, then the Marwadi for to pound.

You'll want to know: दळणो.
दळणो — daḷṇo — "to grind (grain)".
मां चक्की माथै बाजरो दळै है। mā̃ chakkī māthai bājro daḷai hai. Mother grinds millet on the hand mill.
Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो दळ्यो, mhaĩ bājro daḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
दळणो (daḷṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit dalati, "cracks, splits"; CDIAL lists Hindi dalnā, "to grind coarsely", and Gujarati daḷvũ (CDIAL 6216). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
छीलणो (chhīlṇo) — to peel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to pound, then the Marwadi for to grind.

You'll want to know: छीलणो.
छीलणो — chhīlṇo — "to peel".
मां आलू छीलै है। mā̃ ālū chhīlai hai. Mother peels the potatoes.
Past (I …): म्हैं केळो छील्यो, mhaĩ keḷo chhīlyo.

The word, taken apart.
छीलणो (chhīlṇo) comes from a reconstructed chilla, "torn, cut"; CDIAL lists Hindi chīlnā and Gujarati chilvũ, "to peel" (CDIAL 5051). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
