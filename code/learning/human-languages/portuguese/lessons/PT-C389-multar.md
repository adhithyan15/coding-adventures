## You'll want to know: multar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C389-CRIME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**multar** — "to fine".

> A polícia multou-me por excesso de velocidade.
>
> *The police fined me for speeding.*

*Ser multado* — to be fined; *a multa* — the fine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*multar* comes from Latin *multāre* "to punish, to fine" (EtymDB), on *multa* "a fine" (Lewis & Short).
