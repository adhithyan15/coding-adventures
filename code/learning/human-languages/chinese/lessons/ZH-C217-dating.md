## You'll want to know: dǎting
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C217-FINE217-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎting* — "to ask around; to inquire".

> *wǒ dǎtingle shūdiàn jīntiān bù kāimén* — I asked around, and the bookshop is closed today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎting* is built from 打 'to strike' and *tīng* 'to listen', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *theng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); in the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
