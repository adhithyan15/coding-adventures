## You'll want to know: એરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C508-LUHAR508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**એરણ** — *eraṇ* — "an anvil".

> લુહાર એરણ પર ગરમ લોખંડ મૂકે છે.
>
> *luhār eraṇ par garam lokhanḍ mūke che.*
>
> The blacksmith puts the hot iron on the anvil.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**એરણ** (*eraṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *adhikaraṇī*, "an anvil", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 252).
