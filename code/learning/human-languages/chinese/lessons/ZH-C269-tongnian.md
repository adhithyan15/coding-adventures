## You'll want to know: tóngnián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C269-YOUNG269-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tóngnián* — "childhood".

> *wǒ de tóngnián hěn kuàilè* — I had a very happy childhood.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tóngnián* is built from *tóng* 'a child' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duwng* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a young person who does not yet have a household.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
