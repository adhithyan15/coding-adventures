# ૈ (ai sign) — the ai sign in પૈસા

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-C45-GHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ઠ and ઘ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **પૈસા** (*paisā*), "money".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ૈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C45-AI-SIGN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ૈ** — the *ai* sign.

It sits above **પ** in **પૈસા** (*paisā*), "money". You already write **ે**, one
stroke above a consonant, for *e*. Two strokes, **ૈ**, make it *ai*.

## Writing: ૈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C45-AI-SIGN-01] -->

Put your pen on ૈ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-C45-GHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-C45-AI-SIGN-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **પૈસા** and point at ૈ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ૈ three times, saying *ai* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ઘ, then ૈ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **પૈસા** aloud once more]
