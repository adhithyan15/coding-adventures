## You'll want to know: भ्रष्टाचार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C363-SATTA363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रष्टाचार** — *bhraṣṭācār* — "corruption".

> भ्रष्टाचारामुळे रस्त्यांचं काम खराब झालं.
>
> *bhraṣṭācārāmuḷe rastyāñcaṁ kām kharāb jhālaṁ.*
>
> Because of corruption the road work was poor.

Masculine.

भ्रष्ट, corrupt, + आचार, conduct.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रष्टाचार** (*bhraṣṭācār*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *bhraṣṭa*, "fallen, ruined", and *ācāra*, "conduct" (Monier-Williams).
