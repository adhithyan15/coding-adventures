## You'll want to know: ماجستير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C397-SHAHADA397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ماجستير** — *mājistīr* — "a master's degree".

> تدرس أختي الماجستير في الأدب.
>
> *tadrusu ukhtī al-mājistīr fī al-adab.*
>
> My sister is studying for a master's degree in literature.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ماجستير** (*mājistīr*) is a modern loan, usually traced to Latin *magister*, "a master, a teacher", through European names for the degree.
