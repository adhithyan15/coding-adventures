Bengali, chapter 529: Torn Between Two.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মনঃপূত (mônôppūtô) — pleasing; to one's liking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to suffice, then the Bengali for to scrape together.

You'll want to know: মনঃপূত.
মনঃপূত — mônôppūtô — "pleasing; to one's liking".
জামাটা মনঃপূত হলে আমি সেটা কিনব। Jāmāṭā mônôppūtô hôle āmi seṭā kinbô. If the shirt is to my liking, I'll buy it.

The word, taken apart.
মনঃপূত (mônôppūtô) is Sanskrit manaḥpūta, "pure in mind" (Monier-Williams): manas, "mind", and pūta, "purified"; the sense "to one's liking" is Bengali's own.

দোটানা (doṭānā) — a dilemma; being torn two ways.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to scrape together, then the Bengali for pleasing to oneself.

You'll want to know: দোটানা.
দোটানা — doṭānā — "a dilemma; being torn two ways".
আমি দোটানায় আছি: শহরে থাকব, না গ্রামে ফিরব? Āmi doṭānāy āchhi: shôhôre thākbô, nā grāme phirbô? I'm torn: shall I stay in the city or go back to the village?

The word, taken apart.
দোটানা (doṭānā) joins দো, "two", usually traced to Persian do, to টানা, "a pull": being pulled two ways at once.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ঝোঁকা (jhõkā) — to lean; to stoop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a dilemma, then the Bengali for personal taste.

You'll want to know: ঝোঁকা.
ঝোঁকা — jhõkā — "to lean; to stoop".
সামনে বেশি ঝুঁকলে তুমি পড়ে যাবে। Sāmne beshi jhũkle tumi pôṛe jābe. If you lean too far forward, you'll fall.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সামনে ঝুঁকলেন, tini sāmne jhũklen.
The stem vowel rises in the past: ঝুঁকলেন, he leaned.

The word, taken apart.
ঝোঁকা (jhõkā) comes from a reconstructed jhukkati, "stoops"; CDIAL lists Bengali jhũkā, "to stoop" (CDIAL 5399). ঝুঁকি, a risk, is probably from the same root.
