# Review — a journey by sea

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-LEX-C286-NAVIS-01, LA-LEX-C286-NAULUM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a ship", then "the fare". (*Nāvis. Naulum.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-LEX-C286-AMITTO-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C286-QUID-ACCIDIT-02, LA-LEX-C285-ITER-FACIO-02, LA-GRAMMAR-C285-QUA-VIA-02] -->

Two chapters, and a whole journey. The way through the town, *quā viā …*,
*rēctā viā*, *ad dextram*. The ship, the fare and the master who takes it.
And the trouble: *quid accidit?*, answered in the perfect.

> *Iter Rōmam faciēbam. Prīmum naulum solvī. Deinde sarcinam āmīsī.
> Postrēmō nāvis discessit!*
> — I was making a journey to Rome. First I paid the fare. Then I lost my
> pack. And last of all, the ship left!

[PAUSE 2s] One line of scene, *faciēbam*, and three events.
