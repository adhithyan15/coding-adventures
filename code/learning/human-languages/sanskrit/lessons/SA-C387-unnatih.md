## You'll want to know: उन्नतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C387-UNNATI387-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्नतिः** — *unnatiḥ* — "advancement; a rise".

> यदि शिक्षा उत्तमा, तर्हि समाजस्य उन्नतिः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi śikṣā uttamā, tarhi samājasya unnatiḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If education is good, society will advance.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्नतिः** (*unnatiḥ*) is built on *ut*, "up", and the root √nam, "to bend"; MW gives *un-nati* "rising, ascending" and "elevation, height", and "advancement" is how modern Sanskrit uses it (MW).
