## You'll want to know: ぼんち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C413-HEIYA413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼんち** — *bonchi* — "a basin among mountains".

> **ぼんちの なつは むしあついです。** — *bonchi no natsu wa mushiatsui desu* — "Summers in the basin are hot and humid."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼんち** (*bonchi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bon*, "basin" (Middle Chinese *bwon*) and *chi*, "ground" (*dijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
