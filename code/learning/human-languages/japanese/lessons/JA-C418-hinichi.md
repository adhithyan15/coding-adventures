## You'll want to know: ひにち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C418-SHOMEI418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ひにち** — *hinichi* — "a date (for something planned)".

> **よやくの ひにちを きめました。** — *yoyaku no hinichi o kimemashita* — "We decided on the date of the booking."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひにち** (*hinichi*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the native Japanese (wago) *hi*, "day", inherited rather than borrowed, and the Sino-Japanese *nichi*, "day" (Middle Chinese *nyit*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
