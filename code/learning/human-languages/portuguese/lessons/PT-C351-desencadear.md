## You'll want to know: desencadear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C351-IMPACTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**desencadear** — "to trigger, to unleash".

> Uma pequena discussão desencadeou uma grande zanga.
>
> *A small argument triggered a big row.*

Literally to unchain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desencadear* is built from *des-* and *encadear* "to chain", on *cadeia* "chain", inherited from Latin *catēna* "a chain" (Lewis & Short): to let loose from the chain.
