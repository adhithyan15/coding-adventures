## You'll want to know: la censura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C286-EDIZIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la censura** — "censorship".

> Il film ha avuto problemi con la censura.
>
> *The film had trouble with the censors.*

Also a formal rebuke: *un voto di censura*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*censura* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cēnsūra* "the office of the censor" (Lewis & Short), from *cēnsēre* "to assess, to judge".
