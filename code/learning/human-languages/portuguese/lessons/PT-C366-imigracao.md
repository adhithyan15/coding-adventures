## You'll want to know: a imigração
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C366-SOCIEDADE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a imigração** — "the immigration".

> A imigração mudou muito as cidades.
>
> *Immigration has changed the cities a lot.*

*Imigrante* — immigrant; *a emigração* — emigration, very common in Portuguese history.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imigração* is built on *imigrar* "to immigrate", a learned borrowing from Latin *immigrāre* "to remove or go into" (Lewis & Short), from *in* and *migrāre* "to move".
