## You'll want to know: хирург
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C489-CHECKUP-05]; assesses=[] -->

**хирург** (*khirúrg*) — "a surgeon".

> Хирург сказал, что операция прошла хорошо.
>
> *Khirúrg skazál, chto operátsiya proshlá khoroshó.*
>
> The surgeon said that the operation had gone well.

Masculine; also of a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хирург* was borrowed from Greek *kheirourgós* 'surgeon' (EtymDB), a compound of *kheír* 'hand' and *érgon* 'work'.
