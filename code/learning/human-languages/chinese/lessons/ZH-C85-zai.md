## You'll want to know: 在
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**在** — *zài* — "to be at; in, at".

> **妈妈在家吗** *māma zài jiā ma* — Is Mum at home?

> **我在这儿** *wǒ zài zhèr* — I'm here.

The next lesson puts the character in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**在** (*zài*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to dwell' and 'to exist'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The place comes straight after 在: 妈妈, 在, 家. English needs *is* and *at*; Mandarin needs 在 alone.

That is the whole sentence pattern of the chapter: a person or a thing, then 在, then the place.
