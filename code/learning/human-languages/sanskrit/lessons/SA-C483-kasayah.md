## You'll want to know: कषायः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C483-GUTIKA483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कषायः** — *kaṣāyaḥ* — "a decoction".

> पितामही आर्द्रकस्य कषायं पचति।
>
> *pitāmahī ārdrakasya kaṣāyaṃ pacati.*
>
> Grandmother boils a ginger decoction.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कषायः** (*kaṣāyaḥ*) MW gives "astringent" and, in medicine, "a decoction" boiled down to a quarter; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
