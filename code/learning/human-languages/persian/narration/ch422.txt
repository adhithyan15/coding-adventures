Persian, chapter 422: Under the bonnet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کاپوت (kâput) — a bonnet (of a car); a hood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a windscreen wiper, then the Persian for a side mirror.

You'll want to know: کاپوت.
کاپوت (kâput) — "a bonnet (of a car); a hood".
مکانیک کاپوت ماشین را باز کرد. Mekânik kâput-e mâshin râ bâz kard. The mechanic opened the bonnet of the car.
In American English, the hood.

The word, taken apart.
kâput was borrowed from French capote, a hooded cloak (EtymDB): the engine wears a hood. EtymDB traces capote back to Latin caput 'head'.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Persian for a side mirror, then the Persian for a bonnet.

You'll want to know: موتور.
موتور (motor) — "an engine, a motor; a motorbike (colloquial)".
موتور (motor) این ماشین قدیمی هنوز خوب کار میکند. Motor-e in mâshin-e qadimi hanuz khub kâr mikonad. This old car's engine still works well.
In everyday speech motor is also a motorbike.

The word, taken apart.
motor was borrowed from French moteur, which EtymDB derives from Latin motor 'a mover', built on movere 'to move' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
استارت زدن (estârt zadan) — to start (an engine), to turn the key.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an engine, then the Persian for oil.

You'll want to know: استارت زدن.
استارت زدن (estârt zadan) — "to start (an engine), to turn the key".
صبح سرد بود و من سه بار استارت زدم. Sobh sard bud va man se bâr estârt zadam. The morning was cold and I turned the key three times.
estârt (from English) + zadan. Present stem zan-: estârt mizanam.

The word, taken apart.
estârt zadan is built from estârt, a loan usually traced to English start, and the native verb zadan 'to strike'. EtymDB does not list the loan.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خاموش شدن (khâmush shodan) — to stall, to cut out; to go out (of a light).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for oil, then the Persian for to start.

You'll want to know: خاموش شدن.
خاموش شدن (khâmush shodan) — "to stall, to cut out; to go out (of a light)".
ماشین وسط خیابان خاموش شد. Mâshin vasat-e khiyâbân khâmush shod. The car stalled in the middle of the street.
khâmush (off, silent) + shodan (to become): the engine or a light goes off by itself. Khâmush kardan is to switch something off.

The word, taken apart.
khâmush shodan is built from khâmush 'silent, off', inherited from Middle Persian xāmōš 'silent' (EtymDB), and the native verb shodan 'to become'.
