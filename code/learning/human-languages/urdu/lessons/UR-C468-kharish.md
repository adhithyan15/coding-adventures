## You'll want to know: خارش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C468-SMELL468-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خارش** — *khārish* — "a rash; an itch (on the skin)".

> اس کی جلد پر خارش ہے۔
>
> *us kī jild par khārish hai.*
>
> She has a rash on her skin.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خارش** (*khārish*) is usually traced to Persian *khārish*, "itching".
