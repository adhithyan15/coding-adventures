## You'll want to know: فرود آمدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C431-PARVAZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فرود آمدن** (*forud âmadan*) — "to land (of a plane or a bird)".

> هواپیما در باران فرود آمد.
>
> *Havâpeymâ dar bârân forud âmad.*
>
> The plane landed in the rain.

*forud* 'down' + *âmadan* 'to come'. Present: *forud miâyad*. Taking off is *boland shodan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*forud âmadan* is built from native *forud* 'down, below' and *âmadan* 'to come', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root *gʷem-* 'to step'.
