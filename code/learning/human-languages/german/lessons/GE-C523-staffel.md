## You'll want to know: die Staffel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C523-FERNSEHEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Staffel** — "season (of a series); relay team".

> Die zweite Staffel ist noch spannender als die erste.
>
> *The second season is even more exciting than the first.*

Plural: *die Staffeln*. In sport, a relay team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Staffel* first meant "step, rung"; EtymDB traces it to Proto-Germanic *stapulaz* "post". The sense of a season of episodes is recent.
