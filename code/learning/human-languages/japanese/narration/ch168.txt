Japanese, chapter 168: Deadlines and Putting Things Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
きまる (kimaru) — to be decided; to be settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to become different, then the Japanese for calling off.

You'll want to know: きまる.
きまる — kimaru — "to be decided; to be settled".
らいしゅう よていが きまります。 — raishū yotei ga kimarimasu — "The plan will be decided next week."
Polite きまります, kimarimasu; a godan verb.

The word, taken apart.
きまる (kimaru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 2 of 5.
きめる (kimeru) — to decide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for calling off, then the Japanese for to be decided.

You'll want to know: きめる.
きめる — kimeru — "to decide".
あした ははと よていを きめます。 — ashita haha to yotei o kimemasu — "Tomorrow I will decide the plan with my mother."
Polite きめます, kimemasu; an ichidan verb. Its intransitive partner is きまる, kimaru.

The word, taken apart.
きめる (kimeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as kimaru, "to be decided"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 3 of 5.
きげん (kigen) — a deadline; a time limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be decided, then the Japanese for to decide.

You'll want to know: きげん.
きげん — kigen — "a deadline; a time limit".
しゅくだいの きげんは あしたです。 — shukudai no kigen wa ashita desu — "The homework deadline is tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
きげん (kigen) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ki, "period, time" (Middle Chinese gi) and gen, "limit" (heanX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
