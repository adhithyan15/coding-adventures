## You'll want to know: ритм
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C516-MELODY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ритм** (*ritm*) — "rhythm".

> Танцоры двигались в ритме музыки.
>
> *Tantsóry dvígalis v rítme múzyki.*
>
> The dancers moved to the rhythm of the music.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ритм* was borrowed from Latin *rhythmus*, from Greek *rhythmós* 'measured flow', built on *rhéō* 'to flow' (EtymDB).
