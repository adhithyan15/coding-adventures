## You'll want to know: ರೆಸಾರ್ಟ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C463-SEVE463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ರೆಸಾರ್ಟ್** — *resārṭ* — "a resort".

> ನಾವು ಕಾಡಿನ ಹತ್ತಿರದ ಒಂದು ರೆಸಾರ್ಟ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ತಂಗಿದೆವು.
>
> *nāvu kāḍina hattirada ondu resārṭnalli taṅgidevu.*
>
> We stayed at a resort near the forest.

A place to stay for a holiday, often out of town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರೆಸಾರ್ಟ್** (*resārṭ*) is a loan from English *resort*.
