Sanskrit, chapter 350: Release.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बन्धनम् (bandhanam) — captivity; a bond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for restrains, then the Sanskrit for an oath.

You'll want to know: बन्धनम्.
बन्धनम् — bandhanam — "captivity; a bond".
पक्षी बन्धनं न इच्छति। pakṣī bandhanaṃ na icchati. A bird does not want captivity.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
बन्धनम् (bandhanam) comes from the root √bandh, "to bind"; MW gives bandhana "the act of binding, tying, fettering" (MW).

You'll want to know: निर्धारयति.
निर्धारयति — nirdhārayati — "settles (a question); determines".
न्यायाधीशः दण्डं निर्धारयति। nyāyādhīśaḥ daṇḍaṃ nirdhārayati. The judge settles the punishment.
Past (he …): सः दण्डं निर्धारितवान्, saḥ daṇḍaṃ nirdhāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
निर्धारयति (nirdhārayati) is built on nir, "out", and the root √dhṛ, "to hold", in its causative form; MW gives nir-dhārita "determined, ascertained, settled" (MW).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: प्रत्यर्पयति.
प्रत्यर्पयति — pratyarpayati — "gives back; restores".
चौरः धनं प्रत्यर्पयति। cauraḥ dhanaṃ pratyarpayati. The thief gives back the money.
Past (he …): सः स्यूतं प्रत्यर्पितवान्, saḥ syūtaṃ pratyarpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रत्यर्पयति (pratyarpayati) is built on prati, "back", and the root √ṛ, "to go", in its causative form arpaya, "to hand over"; MW gives praty-arpita "restored" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for settles (a question), then the Sanskrit for gives back.

You'll want to know: प्रायश्चित्तम्.
प्रायश्चित्तम् — prāyaścittam — "atonement; amends".
दोषस्य प्रायश्चित्तं करोतु। doṣasya prāyaścittaṃ karotu. Make amends for the fault.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रायश्चित्तम् (prāyaścittam) is built from prāyas, "for the most part", and citta, "thought" (MW records the explanations "predominant thought" and "thought of death"); MW gives prāyaś-citta "atonement, expiation, amends" (MW).
