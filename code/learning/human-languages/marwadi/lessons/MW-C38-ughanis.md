# उघणीस — nineteen on the page

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-UGHANIS-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-U-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-GHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-NNA-01, MW-SCRIPT-BEES-01] -->

> **उ** + **घ** + **णी** + **स** → **उघणीस** — *ughaṇīs* — nineteen

Four pieces and no new sign: **उ** from the last lesson, **घ** from chapter 10,
the retroflex **ण** from chapter 4, and **स** from the first page of the book.

Write **बीस** under it. The **ीस** at the end of nineteen is the **ीस** of
twenty, with a retroflex **ण** in front of it instead of **ब**. What the ear was
told, the page shows.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-UGHANIS-01, MW-SCRIPT-BEES-01, MW-SCRIPT-ATHARA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **उघणीस** once while looking, then write **बीस** beneath it and ring the
**ीस** in both. Finish by writing **अठारा** above the pair, so eighteen,
nineteen and twenty stand in a column.
