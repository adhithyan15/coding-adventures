## You'll want to know: обычай
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C318-TRADITSIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**обычай** (*obýchay*) — "a custom".

> По русскому обычаю гостей встречают хлебом и солью.
>
> *Po rússkomu obýchayu gostéy vstrecháyut khlébom i sólyu.*
>
> By Russian custom, guests are welcomed with bread and salt.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обычай* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *obvyčajĭ* (EtymDB), built on *vyknǫti* "to get used to", the root of *привыкать*.
