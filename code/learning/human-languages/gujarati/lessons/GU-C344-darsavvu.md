# દર્શાવવું (darśāvvũ) — to indicate

## You'll want to know: દર્શાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C344-SABITI344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**દર્શાવવું** — *darśāvvũ* — "to indicate".

> આ ફોટો દર્શાવે છે કે દુકાન રાત્રે ખુલ્લી હતી.
>
> *ā phoṭo darśāve che ke dukān rātre khullī hatī.*
>
> This photo shows that the shop was open at night.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સાબિતી દર્શાવી, *teṇe sābitī darśāvī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દર્શાવવું** (*darśāvvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *darśana*, "seeing, showing" (Monier-Williams), with the Gujarati causative ending *-āvvũ*: to make something seen.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
