German, chapter 354: Die Besprechung.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Besprechung — the meeting (at work).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the superior, then the German for to run.

You'll want to know: die Besprechung.
die Besprechung — "the meeting (at work)".
Die Besprechung dauert bis elf Uhr. The meeting lasts until eleven o'clock.
Plural: die Besprechungen. In einer Besprechung sein — to be in a meeting.

The word, taken apart.
Besprechung is the noun of besprechen "to discuss", built with be- on sprechen "to speak", Old High German sprehhan and Proto-Germanic sprekaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Sitzung — the session, the formal meeting.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for to run, then the German for the meeting.

You'll want to know: die Sitzung.
die Sitzung — "the session, the formal meeting".
Der Stadtrat hat jeden Montag eine Sitzung. The town council has a session every Monday.
Plural: die Sitzungen. More formal than die Besprechung; with a therapist, a session.

The word, taken apart.
Sitzung is formed from sitzen "to sit", inherited from Proto-Germanic sitjaną (EtymDB), with -ung: a sitting, as in English.

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Protokoll — the minutes, the record; protocol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the meeting, then the German for the session.

You'll want to know: das Protokoll.
das Protokoll — "the minutes, the record; protocol".
Heute führt mein Kollege das Protokoll. Today my colleague is taking the minutes.
Plural: die Protokolle. Protokoll führen — to take the minutes.

The word, taken apart.
Protokoll came through Medieval Latin prōtocollum from Byzantine Greek prōtókollon (EtymDB), the first sheet glued onto a roll of papers, from prôtos "first" and kólla "glue".

Lesson 4 of 5.
präsentieren — to present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the session, then the German for the minutes.

You'll want to know: präsentieren.
präsentieren — "to present".
Morgen präsentiert sie ihre Ideen vor der ganzen Abteilung. Tomorrow she is presenting her ideas to the whole department.
Perfekt: hat präsentiert, with no ge-.

The word, taken apart.
präsentieren was borrowed in Middle High German as prēsentieren from Old French présenter (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin praesentāre "to show, to present", from praesēns "present" (Lewis & Short).

Ihre Präsentation war kurz und sehr klar. Her presentation was short and very clear.
Plural: die Präsentationen. Eine Präsentation halten — to give a presentation.

The word, taken apart.
Präsentation is a learned borrowing from Late Latin praesentātiō "a showing", from praesentāre "to show, to present", built on praesēns "present" (Lewis & Short).
