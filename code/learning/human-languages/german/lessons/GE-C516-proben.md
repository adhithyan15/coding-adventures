## You'll want to know: proben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C516-BUEHNE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**proben** — "to rehearse".

> Die Band probt jeden Freitag im Keller.
>
> *The band rehearses every Friday in the cellar.*

Regular: *hat geprobt*. The noun *die Probe* — rehearsal; also a test or a sample.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proben* goes back to Latin *probāre* "to test, to try" (EtymDB).
