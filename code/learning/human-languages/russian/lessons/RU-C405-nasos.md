## You'll want to know: насос
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C405-SHINA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**насос** (*nasós*) — "a pump".

> Хорошо, что у нас был с собой насос.
>
> *Khoroshó, chto u nas byl s sobóy nasós.*
>
> It was a good thing we had a pump with us.

Masculine. *Велосипедный насос* — a bicycle pump.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*насос* is built from *на-* and the root of *сосать* 'to suck': a pump sucks up air or water. EtymDB lists the word without a source.
