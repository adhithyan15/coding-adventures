Telugu, chapter 505: Kindness and Comfort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
జాలి (jāli) — pity; sympathy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to appreciate, then the Telugu for acclaim.

You'll want to know: జాలి.
జాలి — jāli — "pity; sympathy".
ఆకలితో ఉన్న కుక్కపిల్లను చూసి నాకు జాలి వేసింది. ākalitō unna kukkapillanu cūsi nāku jāli vēsindi. Seeing the hungry puppy, I felt pity.
జాలి (jāli) వేయు or జాలి (jāli) కలుగు, to feel pity; -మీద జాలిపడు, to pity someone.

The word, taken apart.
జాలి (jāli) is a Telugu word for the pity you feel for someone weaker; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: ఆదరించు.
ఆదరించు — ādariñcu — "to welcome warmly; to cherish".
కొత్తగా వచ్చిన కోడలిని అత్త ఆదరించింది. kottagā vaccina kōḍalini atta ādariñcindi. The mother-in-law warmly welcomed her new daughter-in-law.
Past (I …): ఆదరించాను, ādariñcānu.
The noun ఆదరణ is a warm reception or support.

The word, taken apart.
ఆదరించు (ādariñcu) is built on Sanskrit ādara, "respect" (CDIAL 1142), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for compassion, then the Telugu for to welcome warmly.

You'll want to know: ఆదుకో.
ఆదుకో — ādukō — "to stand by; to help in need".
కష్టకాలంలో స్నేహితులు మమ్మల్ని ఆదుకున్నారు. kaṣṭakālaṁlō snēhitulu mammalni ādukunnāru. In hard times our friends stood by us.
Past (I …): ఆదుకున్నాను, ādukunnānu.
Help given when someone is in trouble, not everyday help.

The word, taken apart.
ఆదుకో (ādukō) is a Telugu verb ending in the reflexive -కో; the origin of its stem is not settled.
