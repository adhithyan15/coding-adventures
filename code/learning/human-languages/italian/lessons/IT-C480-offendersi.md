## You'll want to know: offendersi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C480-RANCORE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**offendersi** — "to take offence".

> Si è offeso perché non l'abbiamo invitato.
>
> *He took offence because we did not invite him.*

The past participle is *offeso*. To offend someone: *offendere qualcuno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*offendersi* is the reflexive of *offendere*, a learned borrowing from Latin *offendere* "to strike against, to displease" (Lewis & Short).
