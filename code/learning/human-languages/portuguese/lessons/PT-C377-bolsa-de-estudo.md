## You'll want to know: a bolsa de estudo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C377-DOCENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a bolsa de estudo** — "the scholarship, the grant".

> Ela recebeu uma bolsa de estudo para estudar fora.
>
> *She got a scholarship to study abroad.*

*O bolseiro* — the grant holder (in Brazil *o bolsista*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bolsa* "purse" goes back through Old Portuguese *bolssa* to Late Latin *bursa* "oxhide, purse" (EtymDB), usually traced to Greek *býrsa* "hide"; *estudo* is inherited from Latin *studium* "zeal, eagerness, study" (Lewis & Short).
