## You'll want to know: a travagem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C300-CAPOTAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a travagem** — "the braking".

> Ouvimos uma travagem e depois um barulho enorme.
>
> *We heard someone brake hard and then a huge bang.*

European Portuguese; in Brazil, *a freada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*travagem* is built on *travar* "to brake, to hold back", from *trave* "a beam", which comes from Latin *trabs* "a beam" (Lewis & Short).
