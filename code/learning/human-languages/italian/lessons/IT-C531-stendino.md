## You'll want to know: lo stendino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C531-BUCATO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lo stendino** — "clothes horse, drying rack".

> Metto lo stendino sul balcone.
>
> *I put the clothes horse out on the balcony.*

Built on *stendere* "to spread out, to hang out".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stendino* is built on *stendere* "to spread out", inherited from Latin *extendere* "to stretch out" (Lewis & Short), from *tendere*.
