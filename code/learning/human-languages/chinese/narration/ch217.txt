Mandarin Chinese, chapter 217: Never Mind.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
méi guānxi — it doesn't matter; never mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for police officer, then the Mandarin for to call the police.

You'll want to know: méi guānxi.
méi guānxi — "it doesn't matter; never mind".
méi guānxi wǒmen míngtiān qù — Never mind, we'll go tomorrow.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
méi guānxi is built from 没 'not have' and guānxi 'a connection', all three parts inherited from Middle Chinese, mwot, kwaen and kejH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): no connection to worry about.

What you've built.
One more word for saying no and asking.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: méi guānxi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: méi guānxi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: méi guānxi wǒmen míngtiān qù — Never mind, we'll go tomorrow.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for police officer, then the Mandarin for to call the police, then say méi guānxi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does méi guānxi mean? ("It doesn't matter; never mind".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 7.
jiějué — to solve; to settle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to remember, then the Mandarin for to ask around.

You'll want to know: jiějué.
jiějué — "to solve; to settle".
zhège wèntí wǒmen néng jiějué — We can sort this problem out.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jiějué is built from jiě 'to untie' and jué 'to break through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, keaX and kwet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to explain' and 'to loosen', and the second 'to break'.
