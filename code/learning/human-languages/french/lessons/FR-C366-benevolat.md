## You'll want to know: le bénévolat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C366-BENEVOLAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le bénévolat** — "the volunteering, unpaid work".

> Le bénévolat prend tous ses samedis.
>
> *Volunteering takes up all her Saturdays.*

*Faire du bénévolat* — to do voluntary work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bénévolat* is built from *bénévole* and *-at*. *bénévole* comes from Latin *benevolus* "well-wishing", from *bene* "well" and *velle* "to want" (Lewis & Short).
