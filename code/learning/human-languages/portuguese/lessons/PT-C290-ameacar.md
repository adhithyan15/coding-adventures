## You'll want to know: ameaçar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C290-ARMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ameaçar** — "to threaten".

> O homem ameaçou o empregado com uma faca.
>
> *The man threatened the employee with a knife.*

*Ameaçar de morte* — to threaten to kill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ameaçar* comes down from Old Portuguese *amẽaçar* (EtymDB), built on Vulgar Latin *minācia* "a threat", from Latin *mināx* "threatening".
