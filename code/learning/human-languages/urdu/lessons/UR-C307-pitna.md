## You'll want to know: پیٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C307-JHAGRA307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پیٹنا** — *pīṭnā* — "to beat".

> لوگوں نے چور کو پیٹا۔
>
> *logoṅ ne chor ko pīṭā.*
>
> People beat the thief.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دروازہ پیٹا, *us ne darvāzā pīṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pīṭnā*, "to beat", under *piṭṭayati*, "pounds" (CDIAL 8165). You can **پیٹنا** a person, a drum or a door.
