# ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ (pahunchāuṇā) — to deliver; to take something to a place

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a postman, then the Punjabi for a parcel.

## You'll want to know: ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C256-DAK256-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ** — *pahunchāuṇā* — "to deliver; to take something to a place".

> ਡਾਕੀਆ ਚਿੱਠੀਆਂ ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ḍākīā chiṭṭhīāṁ sāḍe ghar pahunchāundā hai.*
>
> The postman delivers the letters to our house.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਾਰਸਲ ਪਹੁੰਚਾਇਆ, *maiṁ pārsal pahunchāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ** (*pahunchāuṇā*) is the causative partner of *pahũcṇā*, "to arrive"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pahũcṇā* under Sanskrit *prabhūta*, "much, great" (CDIAL 8716).
