## The frame, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-JOIN-SOCHDA-KI-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-KI-01, PA-GRAMMAR-MAIN-HAAN-03] -->

> **ਮੈਂ ਸੋਚਦਾ ਹਾਂ ਕਿ …** — *maiṁ sochdā hāṁ ki …* — "I think that …"

Three of the four pieces are old: **ਮੈਂ**, the habitual ending you met with
*bolda*, and **ਹਾਂ**. Only **ਕਿ** is new, and it opens the space your opinion
goes into.

**A woman says ਸੋਚਦੀ** — *sochdī* — with the feminine ending, exactly as with
every habitual verb this book has taught. The sentence you use is the one that
matches you.

Everything you can already say can now be said **as an opinion** rather than as
a fact. That is a large change for one small word, and it is the difference
between reporting the world and having a view about it.
