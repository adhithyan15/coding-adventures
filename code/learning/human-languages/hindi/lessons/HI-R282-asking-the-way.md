# Review — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01, HI-LEX-C282-DUR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask "How do I get to the station?" (*sṭeśan kaise jāte haiṁ?*) Then "How far is it?" (*kitnī dūr hai?*)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01, HI-LEX-C282-BATANA-01, HI-LEX-C282-DUR-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, HI-LEX-C107-DOCTOR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Excuse me, how do I get to the market?]
- [YOU SAY: Is it far? No, it's ten minutes away.]
- [YOU SAY: Go straight on, then turn right at the crossroads.]
- [YOU SAY: Can you tell me the way to the hospital?]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday a man told me the way.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01, HI-LEX-C282-BATANA-01, HI-LEX-C282-DUR-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "Please tell me the way"? (**मुझे रास्ता बताइए**.) "How far?" (**कितनी दूर?**) "Turn right"? (**दाएँ मुड़िए**.) "A man told me the way"? (**एक आदमी ने मुझे रास्ता बताया**: **ने**, and **बताया** for **रास्ता**.)
