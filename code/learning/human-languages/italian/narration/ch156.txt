You'll want to know: comodo.

The word, taken apart.
comodo from Latin commodus, "of due measure, fit, suitable" (Lewis & Short) — a learned borrowing; EtymDB 2.1 has no row for comodo.

The word, taken apart.
cortese — built from corte, "a court", with -ese: having the manners of a court. corte from Latin cortem, "an enclosed yard". (EtymDB 2.1).

maleducato — rude.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Italian for uncomfortable, then the Italian for polite.

You'll want to know: maleducato.
maleducato — "rude".

The word, taken apart.
maleducato is male, "badly", plus educato, "brought up": badly brought up. educato goes back to Latin educatus, "reared" (Lewis & Short). EtymDB 2.1 has no row for it.
