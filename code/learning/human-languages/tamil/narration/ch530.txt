Tamil, chapter 530: Children's Games.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கண்ணாமூச்சி (kaṇṇāmūcci) — hide and seek.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a die, then the Tamil for a seed-and-pit board game.

You'll want to know: கண்ணாமூச்சி.
கண்ணாமூச்சி — kaṇṇāmūcci — "hide and seek".
குழந்தைகள் தோட்டத்தில் கண்ணாமூச்சி விளையாடினார்கள். kuḻantaikaḷ tōṭṭattil kaṇṇāmūcci viḷaiyāṭiṉārkaḷ. The children played hide and seek in the garden.
The seeker covers the eyes while the others hide.

The word, taken apart.
கண்ணாமூச்சி (kaṇṇāmūcci) begins with the native கண் (kaṇ), "an eye" (DEDR 1159), since the seeker shuts the eyes; the formation of the rest is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
பம்பரம் (pamparam) — a spinning top.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a seed-and-pit board game, then the Tamil for hide and seek.

You'll want to know: பம்பரம்.
பம்பரம் — pamparam — "a spinning top".
என் தம்பி தரையில் பம்பரம் சுற்றினான். eṉ tampi taraiyil pamparam cuṟṟiṉāṉ. My little brother spun a top on the floor.
One spins a top with சுற்று (cuṟṟu) or விடு: பம்பரம் (pamparam) விடு.

The word, taken apart.
பம்பரம் (pamparam) is listed in DEDR 3930 as the Tamil word for a spinning top; the article marks a possible source in Sanskrit bhramaraka, "a humming top", with a question mark, so the origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
கேரம் (kēram) — carrom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a spinning top, then the Tamil for marbles.

You'll want to know: கேரம்.
கேரம் — kēram — "carrom".
மழைக்காலத்தில் நாங்கள் வீட்டில் கேரம் விளையாடுவோம். maḻaikkālattil nāṅkaḷ vīṭṭil kēram viḷaiyāṭuvōm. In the rainy season we play carrom at home.
A board game: players flick a striker to knock pieces into the four corner pockets.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of கேரம் (kēram) is not settled: it is the same word as English carrom, but which way the name travelled is unclear.

Lesson 5 of 5.
காற்றாடி (kāṟṟāṭi) — a pinwheel; a paper windmill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for marbles, then the Tamil for carrom.

You'll want to know: காற்றாடி.
காற்றாடி — kāṟṟāṭi — "a pinwheel; a paper windmill".
திருவிழாவில் அப்பா எனக்கு ஒரு காற்றாடி வாங்கினார். tiruviḻāvil appā eṉakku oru kāṟṟāṭi vāṅkiṉār. At the festival Father bought me a pinwheel.
Word for word "wind-dancer": காற்று (kāṟṟu) + ஆடி, "that which swings".

The word, taken apart.
காற்றாடி (kāṟṟāṭi) is a native compound: காற்று (kāṟṟu), "wind" (DEDR 1481), and ஆடி (āṭi), "that which moves or swings", from ஆடு, "to move, to dance".
