Italian, chapter 294: Le forze dell'ordine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il carabiniere — the carabiniere, the military police officer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the alarm, then the Italian for the vandal.

You'll want to know: il carabiniere.
il carabiniere — "the carabiniere, the military police officer".
Ho chiesto aiuto a un carabiniere. I asked a carabiniere for help.
One of the two main police forces; the other is la polizia.

The word, taken apart.
carabiniere is usually traced to French carabinier, a soldier armed with a carabine, a short gun. EtymDB derives carabine from carabin "a dragoon", whose own origin is not settled.

commissariato is built on commissario "commissioner", usually traced to Medieval Latin commissārius, built on Latin committere "to entrust" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la telecamera — the video camera, the CCTV camera.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the police station, then the Italian for the patrol.

You'll want to know: la telecamera.
la telecamera — "the video camera, the CCTV camera".
Le telecamere hanno ripreso tutto. The cameras filmed everything.
For photos, la macchina fotografica.

The word, taken apart.
telecamera joins Greek tēle "far" and camera, from Latin camera "a vault, a room" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB links it with English television camera.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: l'inseguimento.
l'inseguimento — "the chase, the pursuit".
Dopo un lungo inseguimento, la polizia l'ha preso. After a long chase, the police caught him.
Built on inseguire, ‘to chase’.

The word, taken apart.
inseguimento is built on inseguire "to chase", from in- and seguire "to follow", from Latin sequī (Lewis & Short).
