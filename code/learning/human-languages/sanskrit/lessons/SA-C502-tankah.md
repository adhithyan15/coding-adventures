## You'll want to know: टंकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C502-KARMASALA502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**टंकः** — *ṭaṅkaḥ* — "a chisel".

> शिल्पी टंकेन शिलां तक्षति।
>
> *śilpī ṭaṅkena śilāṃ takṣati.*
>
> The sculptor shapes the stone with a chisel.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टंकः** (*ṭaṅkaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a spade, a hatchet and a stone-cutter's chisel; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
