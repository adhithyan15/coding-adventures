Arabic, chapter 523: Getting it done.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خطط (khaṭṭaṭa) — to make plans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for retired, then the Arabic for unemployed.

You'll want to know: خطط.
خطط — khaṭṭaṭa — "to make plans".
يخطط أخي لرحلة في الصيف. yukhaṭṭiṭu akhī li-riḥla fī aṣ-ṣayf. My brother is planning a trip in the summer.
Present: يخطط, yukhaṭṭiṭ.
It takes لـ for what is planned.

The word, taken apart.
خطط (khaṭṭaṭa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-ṭ-ṭ, "to draw a line" (Lane). Khuṭṭa, a plan, which you know, is from the same root: a plan is lines drawn ahead.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تفرغ (tafarragha) — to devote oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to make plans, then the Arabic for to complete.

You'll want to know: تفرغ.
تفرغ — tafarragha — "to devote oneself".
تفرغ أبي لأسرته بعد التقاعد. tafarragha abī li-usratihi baʿda at-taqāʿud. My father devoted himself to his family after he retired.
Present: يتفرغ, yatafarragh.
It takes لـ for what you give your time to.

The word, taken apart.
تفرغ (tafarragha) is built on the native Arabic root f-r-gh, "to be empty, free" (Lane). Fārigh, empty, which you know, is from the same root: Form V is to empty one's time for a thing.
