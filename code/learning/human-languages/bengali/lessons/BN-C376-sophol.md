## You'll want to know: সফল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C376-LOKKHYO376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সফল** — *sôphôl* — "successful".

> চেষ্টা চালিয়ে গেলে তুমি সফল হবে।
>
> *cheshṭā chāliye gele tumi sôphôl hôbe.*
>
> If you keep trying, you will succeed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সফল** (*sôphôl*) is Sanskrit *saphala*, 'with fruit', fruitful (Monier-Williams): an effort that bears fruit. সফল হওয়া is to succeed.
