## You'll want to know: il palloncino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C316-CANDELINE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il palloncino** — "the balloon".

> Abbiamo comprato cinquanta palloncini.
>
> *We bought fifty balloons.*

A little *pallone*, ‘ball’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*palloncino* is a little *pallone* "big ball", built on *palla*, which EtymDB traces to a Longobard word for a ball.
