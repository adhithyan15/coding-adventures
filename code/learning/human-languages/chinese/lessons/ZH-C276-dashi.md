## You'll want to know: 大事
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C276-NEWJOB276-05]; assesses=[] -->

**大事** — *dàshì* — "big deal; important matter".

> **找工作是大事** *zhǎo gōngzuò shì dàshì* — Finding a job is a big deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**大事** (*dàshì*) is built from 大 'big' and 事 'an affair', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *daH* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
