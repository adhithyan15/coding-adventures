## You'll want to know: إنذار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C324-KHATAR324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إنذار** — *indhār* — "an alarm; a warning".

> انطلق إنذار الحريق في المدرسة.
>
> *inṭalaqa indhār al-ḥarīq fī al-madrasa.*
>
> The fire alarm went off in the school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إنذار** (*indhār*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-dh-r* (Lane), "to vow; to warn". *Indhār* is the verbal noun of Form IV *andhara*, "to warn of danger".
