# عقوبة (ʿuqūba) — a penalty, a punishment

## You'll want to know: عقوبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C348-TUHMA348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عقوبة** — *ʿuqūba* — "a penalty, a punishment".

> كانت العقوبة سنة في السجن.
>
> *kānat al-ʿuqūba sana fī as-sijn.*
>
> The penalty was a year in prison.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقوبة** (*ʿuqūba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-b* (Lane), "to come after, to follow at the heel". A punishment is what comes after the deed.
