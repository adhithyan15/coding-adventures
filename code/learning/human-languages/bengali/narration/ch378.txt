Bengali, chapter 378: Before It Is Too Late.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আয়োজন (āyojôn) — preparations (for an event); the organising.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to surpass, then the Bengali for to accomplish.

You'll want to know: আয়োজন.
আয়োজন — āyojôn — "preparations (for an event); the organising".
বিয়ের আয়োজন সময়মতো শেষ না হলে খুব ঝামেলা হবে। biyer āyojôn sômôymôto shesh nā hôle khub jhāmelā hôbe. If the wedding preparations aren't finished in time, there will be a lot of trouble.

The word, taken apart.
আয়োজন (āyojôn) is Sanskrit āyojana, a joining or collecting (Monier-Williams). The Bengali sense, all the preparations and arrangements for an event, is not one the dictionary gives; it goes with করা: বিয়ের আয়োজন করা.

Lesson 2 of 5.
অবিলম্বে (ôbilômbe) — immediately; without delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to accomplish, then the Bengali for preparations (for an event).

You'll want to know: অবিলম্বে.
অবিলম্বে — ôbilômbe — "immediately; without delay".
জ্বর বাড়লে অবিলম্বে ডাক্তার ডাকো। jbôr bāṛle ôbilômbe ḍāktār ḍāko. If the fever rises, call a doctor at once.

The word, taken apart.
অবিলম্বে (ôbilômbe) is Sanskrit a- 'without' with vilamba, delay (Monier-Williams), and the Bengali ending -এ: 'without delay'. It is the word of notices and orders.

Lesson 3 of 5.
মেয়াদ (meyād) — a term; a period of validity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for preparations (for an event), then the Bengali for without delay.

You'll want to know: মেয়াদ.
মেয়াদ — meyād — "a term; a period of validity".
ভিসার মেয়াদ শেষ হলে তোমাকে দেশে ফিরতে হবে। bhisār meyād shesh hôle tomāke deshe phirte hôbe. When your visa runs out, you will have to go back home.

The word, taken apart.
মেয়াদ (meyād) is usually traced to Arabic mīʿād, an appointed time, which would have come into Bengali through Persian: the time a visa, a lease or a loan runs for. When it ends, মেয়াদ শেষ হয়.

Lesson 4 of 5.
স্থগিত করা (sthôgitô kôrā) — to postpone; to suspend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for without delay, then the Bengali for a period of validity.

You'll want to know: স্থগিত করা.
স্থগিত করা — sthôgitô kôrā — "to postpone; to suspend".
আবহাওয়া খারাপ হলে স্কুল খেলাটা স্থগিত করবে। ābôhāoyā khārāp hôle skul khelāṭā sthôgitô kôrbe. If the weather turns bad, the school will postpone the match.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সভাটা স্থগিত করলেন, tini sôbhāṭā sthôgitô kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
স্থগিত করা (sthôgitô kôrā) is Sanskrit sthagita, covered or hidden (Monier-Williams), with করা. Monier-Williams also gives closed and stopped or interrupted, the sense Bengali keeps: to put a match, a meeting or a plan on hold.

Lesson 5 of 5.
তাগাদা দেওয়া (tāgādā deoyā) — to chase up; to press (someone) for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a period of validity, then the Bengali for to postpone.

You'll want to know: তাগাদা দেওয়া.
তাগাদা দেওয়া — tāgādā deoyā — "to chase up; to press (someone) for".
কাজটা না হলে ম্যানেজারকে তাগাদা দাও। kājṭā nā hôle mænejārke tāgādā dāo. If the work isn't done, chase up the manager.
Past (he/she …): তিনি টাকার জন্য তাগাদা দিলেন, tini ṭākār jônyô tāgādā dilen.

The word, taken apart.
তাগাদা দেওয়া (tāgādā deoyā) is usually traced to Arabic taqāḍā, a pressing demand, through Persian, with দেওয়া: to keep reminding someone until it gets done.
