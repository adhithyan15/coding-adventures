## You'll want to know: innocent

**innocent** — "innocent".

> Il dit qu'il est innocent.
>
> *He says he is innocent.*

Feminine *innocente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*innocent* is a learned borrowing from Latin *innocēns* "harmless, blameless" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from *in-* "not" and *nocēre* "to harm".
