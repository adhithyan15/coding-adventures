## You'll want to know: গ্রাহক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C422-PORAKH422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রাহক** — *grāhôk* — "a customer; a subscriber".

> দোকানে নতুন গ্রাহকদের জন্য ছাড় আছে।
>
> *dokāne nôtun grāhôkder jônyô chhāṛ āchhe.*
>
> The shop has a discount for new customers.

খদ্দের is the everyday word; গ্রাহক is what banks, shops and the post office say.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রাহক** (*grāhôk*) is Sanskrit *grāhaka*, "one who receives, a purchaser" (Monier-Williams), from *grah*, to take.
