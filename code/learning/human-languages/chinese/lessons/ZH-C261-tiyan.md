## You'll want to know: tǐyàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C261-EVER261-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐyàn* — "to experience for yourself".

> *tāmen qù nóngcūn tǐyàn shēnghuó* — They're going to the countryside to experience life there.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐyàn* is built from 体 'the body' and *yàn* 'to verify', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thejX* and *ngjemH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the body' and the second 'to verify'.
