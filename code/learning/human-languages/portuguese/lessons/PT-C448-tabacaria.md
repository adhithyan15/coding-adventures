## You'll want to know: a tabacaria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C448-TABACARIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a tabacaria** — "the tobacconist, the newsagent".

> Compro o jornal na tabacaria da esquina.
>
> *I buy the paper at the newsagent on the corner.*

It sells newspapers, magazines and lottery tickets too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tabacaria* is built on *tabaco* with *-aria*, a shop (EtymDB); *tabaco* was borrowed from Spanish *tabaco*.
