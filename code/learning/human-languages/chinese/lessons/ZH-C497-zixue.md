## You'll want to know: 自学
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C497-SELFSTUDY497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**自学** — *zìxué* — "to teach oneself".

> **我自学了两年汉语现在是中级** *wǒ zìxué le liǎng nián Hànyǔ xiànzài shì zhōngjí* — I taught myself Chinese for two years, and now I'm at intermediate level.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**自学** (*zìxué*) is built from 自 'oneself' and 学 'to learn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH* and *haewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from'; the sense 'oneself' is not given there.
