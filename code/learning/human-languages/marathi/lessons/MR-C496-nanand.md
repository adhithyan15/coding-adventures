# नणंद (naṇand) — a husband's sister

## You'll want to know: नणंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C496-SASAR496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नणंद** — *naṇand* — "a husband's sister".

> सुनेची आणि नणंदेची चांगली मैत्री आहे.
>
> *sunecī āṇi naṇandecī cāṅglī maitrī āhe.*
>
> The daughter-in-law and her husband's sister are good friends.

Feminine.

Oblique नणंदे-.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नणंद** (*naṇand*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *nanāndṛ*, "husband's sister"; CDIAL lists Marathi *naṇãd* (CDIAL 6946).
