## You'll want to know: عداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C453-QIST453-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عداد** — *ʿaddād* — "a meter (that counts)".

> كان العداد في التاكسي معطلا.
>
> *kāna al-ʿaddād fī at-tāksī muʿaṭṭalan.*
>
> The meter in the taxi was out of order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عداد** (*ʿaddād*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-d*, "to count" (Lane). The pattern *faʿʿāl* names a thing that does a job: the counter.
