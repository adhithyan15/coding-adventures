## You'll want to know: prahlen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C475-ANGEBER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**prahlen** — "to boast, to brag".

> Er prahlt gern mit seinem teuren Auto.
>
> *He likes to brag about his expensive car.*

*Prahlen mit* + dative.
