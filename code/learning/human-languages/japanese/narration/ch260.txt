Japanese, chapter 260: If It Rains.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
ふったら (futtara) — if it rains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say, plainly, "it rained". (ame ga futta.) Then "I won't go", politely. (ikimasen.)

You'll want to know: -たら, if.
あめが ふったら、いきません。 ame ga futtara, ikimasen. — "If it rains, I won't go."
Take the plain past and add ら: ふった becomes ふったら (futtara), "if it rains". The condition comes first; what follows it comes second, and that last verb says when.
じかんが あったら、えいがを みます。 jikan ga attara, eiga o mimasu. — "If I have time, I will see a film."
An i-adjective does the same from its -かった: さむかった gives さむかったら (samukattara).
さむかったら、うちに います。 samukattara, uchi ni imasu. — "If it is cold, I will stay home."

You'll want to know: -たら, once.
When the first thing is sure to happen, -たら is "once", "when":
えきに ついたら、きっぷを かいます。 eki ni tsuitara, kippu o kaimasu. — "Once I get to the station, I will buy a ticket."
うちに かえったら、ねます。 uchi ni kaettara, nemasu. — "When I get home, I will go to bed."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "If it rains, I won't go" — ame ga futtara, ikimasen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If it snows, I won't go" — yuki ga futtara, ikimasen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If it is cold, I won't go" — samukattara, ikimasen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that if it rains, you won't go.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If it rains"? (ふったら (futtara).) "If I have time"? (じかんが あったら.) "Once I get home"? (かえったら.) How is -たら made? (The plain past, then ら.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
もし (moshi) — if; supposing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say "if it rains, I won't go". (ame ga futtara, ikimasen.) Then say "hello", on the telephone. (moshimoshi.)

You'll want to know: もし.
もし — moshi — "if", "supposing". It opens the sentence and warns the listener that a condition is coming; the -たら at the end of the clause still carries the "if".
もし あめが ふったら、いきません。 moshi ame ga futtara, ikimasen. — "If it rains, I won't go."
It is not the もし (moshi) of もしもし, the telephone "hello".

The word, taken apart.
もし (moshi) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and glosses it "if; in case; supposing" (JMdict).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: moshi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If it rains, I won't go" — moshi ame ga futtara, ikimasen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If it snows, I won't go" — moshi yuki ga futtara, ikimasen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Japanese for 'if, supposing'.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If", at the start? (もし, moshi.) Which part still carries the "if"? (The -たら.)


Lesson 3 of 4.
もし … たら (moshi … tara) — if … then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "if it rains, I won't go", with もし. (moshi ame ga futtara, ikimasen.) Then "what do you think?" (dō omoimasu ka.)

You'll want to know: もし … -たら.
もし はれたら、やまに いきます。 moshi haretara, yama ni ikimasu. — "If it clears up, I will go to the mountains."
The frame holds the whole condition: もし (moshi) opens it, -たら closes it, and 、 comes after it. Everything between is the condition:
もし あした はれたら、やまに いきます。 moshi ashita haretara, yama ni ikimasu. — "If it is fine tomorrow, I will go to the mountains."

You'll want to know: もし … -たら、どう しますか.
To ask what someone will do if, どう しますか (dō shimasu ka), "what will you do", follows the frame:
もし あめが ふったら、どう しますか。 moshi ame ga futtara, dō shimasu ka. — "If it rains, what will you do?"
うちで えいがを みます。 uchi de eiga o mimasu. — "I will see a film at home."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "If it is fine tomorrow, I will go to the mountains" — moshi ashita haretara, yama ni ikimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If it is fine tomorrow, I will go to the sea" — moshi ashita haretara, umi ni ikimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If it is fine tomorrow, I will go to the river" — moshi ashita haretara, kawa ni ikimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Ask someone what they will do if it rains.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word opens the condition? (もし (moshi).) Which closes it? (-たら.) "If it rains, what will you do?" (もし (moshi) あめが ふったら (futtara)、どう しますか.)

Review — if it rains.
