## You'll want to know: अंथरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C415-BICHANA415-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अंथरणे** — *antharṇe* — "to spread out bedding".

> आईने जमिनीवर चादर अंथरली.
>
> *āīne jaminīvar cādar antharlī.*
>
> Mother spread a sheet out on the floor.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अंथरलं, *tyāne antharlaṁ*.

The thing spread is the object: चादर अंथरली, गादी अंथरली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंथरणे** (*antharṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ā̃tharṇẽ*, "to spread out", under Sanskrit *āstarati*, "spreads" (CDIAL 1507).
