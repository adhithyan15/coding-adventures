## You'll want to know: こさめ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C415-YUUDACHI415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こさめ** — *kosame* — "light rain; drizzle".

> **こさめの なかを あるきました。** — *kosame no naka o arukimashita* — "I walked in the light rain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こさめ** (*kosame*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ko*, "small" and *same*, "rain" (from *ame*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
