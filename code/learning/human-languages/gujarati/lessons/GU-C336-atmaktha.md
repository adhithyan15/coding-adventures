# આત્મકથા (ātmakthā) — an autobiography

## You'll want to know: આત્મકથા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C336-LEKHAK336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આત્મકથા** — *ātmakthā* — "an autobiography".

> મારા દાદાએ પોતાની આત્મકથા લખી.
>
> *mārā dādāe potānī ātmakthā lakhī.*
>
> My grandfather wrote his autobiography.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આત્મકથા** (*ātmakthā*) is built from Sanskrit *ātman*, "self", and *kathā*, "a story" (Monier-Williams): the story of one's own life.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
