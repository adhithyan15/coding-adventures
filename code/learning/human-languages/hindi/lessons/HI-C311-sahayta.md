## You'll want to know: सहायता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C311-MADAD311-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सहायता** — *sahāytā* — "aid; assistance".

> कई देशों ने सहायता भेजी।
>
> *kaī deśoṁ ne sahāytā bhejī.*
>
> Many countries sent aid.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहायता** (*sahāytā*) is Sanskrit *sahāyatā* (Monier-Williams), from *sahāya*, a companion who goes along with you. It is the formal word beside **मदद**.
