## You'll want to know: huàichu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C373-PROSCONS373-03]; assesses=[] -->

*huàichu* — "harm; downside".

> *xīyān yǒu shénme huàichu* — What harm does smoking do?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huàichu* is built from *huài* and *chù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hweajH* and *tsyhoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to fall apart of itself' and the second 'a place'. The second is said light here.
