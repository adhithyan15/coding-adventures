6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
das Risiko — the risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to do without, then the German for to replace.

You'll want to know: das Risiko.
das Risiko — "the risk".
Das Risiko ist mir zu groß. The risk is too great for me.
Plural: die Risiken. Ein Risiko eingehen — to take a risk.

The word, taken apart.
Risiko was borrowed from Italian risico, rischio, a word of the merchants; EtymDB traces French risque to Old Italian risco. Further back the origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.
absichern — to protect, to safeguard (mostly reflexive, sich absichern).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the risk, then the German for to risk.

You'll want to know: absichern.
absichern — "to protect, to safeguard (mostly reflexive, sich absichern)".
Bevor ich unterschreibe, will ich mich gut absichern. Before I sign, I want to protect myself properly.
Separable: er sichert sich ab, er hat sich abgesichert.

The word, taken apart.
absichern is built on sichern "to make safe", from sicher "safe", inherited from Old High German sihhur, which goes back to Latin sēcūrus "free from care" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 6.
die Garantie — the guarantee, the warranty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to risk, then the German for to protect.

You'll want to know: die Garantie.
die Garantie — "the guarantee, the warranty".
Auf den Fernseher gibt es zwei Jahre Garantie. The television comes with a two-year warranty.
Plural: die Garantien. Das ist noch in der Garantie — that is still under warranty.

The word, taken apart.
Garantie was borrowed from French garantie (EtymDB), from Old French guarantie "protection", from guarantir "to vouch for", which goes back to a Frankish word; EtymDB links German gewähren "to grant" to the same family.
