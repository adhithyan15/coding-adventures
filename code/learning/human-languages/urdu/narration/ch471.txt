Urdu, chapter 471: Health.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ڈاکٹر (ḍākṭar) — a doctor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a faint, then the Urdu for frailty.

You'll want to know: ڈاکٹر.
ڈاکٹر — ḍākṭar — "a doctor".
ڈاکٹر نے مجھے دوا دی۔ ḍākṭar ne mujhe davā dī. The doctor gave me some medicine.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ڈاکٹر (ḍākṭar) is borrowed from English doctor.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: ḍākṭar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḍākṭar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḍākṭar ne mujhe davā dī. — The doctor gave me some medicine.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for a faint, then the Urdu for frailty, then say ḍākṭar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
