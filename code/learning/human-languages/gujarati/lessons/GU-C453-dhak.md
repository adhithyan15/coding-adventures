## You'll want to know: ધાક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C453-BIK453-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ધાક** — *dhāk* — "awe; dread of someone".

> ગામમાં તે સાહેબની ઘણી ધાક હતી.
>
> *gāmmā̃ te sāhebnī ghaṇī dhāk hatī.*
>
> The whole village stood in awe of that official.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધાક** (*dhāk*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*dhākka-*, "pomp, boast"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the sense "awe" (CDIAL 6769).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
