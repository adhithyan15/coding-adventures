## You'll want to know: ਗਾਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C235-KAM235-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਰਾ** — *gārā* — "mud plaster".

> ਗਾਰਾ ਗਿੱਲਾ ਅਤੇ ਭਾਰੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *gārā gillā ate bhārī hai.*
>
> The mud plaster is wet and heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਰਾ** (*gārā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*gāra*, "dripping, oozing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gārā*, "kneaded potter's clay, mud used for mortar" (CDIAL 4137).
