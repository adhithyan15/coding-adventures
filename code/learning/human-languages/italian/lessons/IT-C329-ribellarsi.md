## You'll want to know: ribellarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C329-RIVOLTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ribellarsi** — "to rebel".

> I contadini si sono ribellati.
>
> *The peasants rebelled.*

*Ribellarsi a* qualcuno.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ribellarsi* is built on *ribelle* "rebel", from Latin *rebellis*, from *rebellāre* "to make war again", built on *bellum* "war" (Lewis & Short).
