# வந்திருந்தால் (vantiruntāl) — if it had, it would have

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C292-CONDITIONAL-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C285-VERBAL-PARTICIPLE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C293-DEPENDS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "having come" (*vantu*) and "if (it) is" (*iruntāl*). Then "It depends on the weather." (*atu vāṉilaiyaip poṟuttatu*.)

## You'll want to know: -இருந்தால் … -இருப்பேன்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C293-UNREAL-IF-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C48-LEAVE-02, TA-GRAMMAR-C285-VERBAL-PARTICIPLE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C292-CONDITIONAL-01, TA-LEX-VAA-01] -->

> **எனக்கு நேரம் இருந்திருந்தால், நான் வந்திருப்பேன்.**
>
> *eṉakku nēram iruntiruntāl, nāṉ vantiruppēṉ.* — "If I had had time, I would have come."

Two pieces you know make the if-clause: the **-உ** form and **இருந்தால்**, "if (it) is". **வந்து** + **இருந்தால்** → **வந்திருந்தால்**, "if (someone) had come"; the **-உ** drops before the **இ**. With **இரு** itself: **இருந்திருந்தால்**, "if (there) had been".

The main clause takes **இருப்பேன்**, "I will be", on the same **-உ** form: **வந்திருப்பேன்**, "I would have come". After **போய்** the **இ** stays whole: **போயிருப்பேன்**, "I would have gone".

Both halves are about what did **not** happen: there was no time, and nobody came.

## You'll want to know: would have, for everyone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C293-WOULD-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C02-NII-NIINGAL-01, TA-LEX-C85-NAAM-NAANGAL-01, TA-LEX-C125-THINGS125-05, TA-LEX-PO-01, TA-LEX-C91-WEATHER-04, TA-LEX-C85-AVAN-AVAL-01] -->

> **நீங்கள் வந்திருந்தால், நாம் சேர்ந்து கடலுக்குப் போயிருப்போம்.**
>
> *nīṅkaḷ vantiruntāl, nām cērntu kaṭalukkup pōyiruppōm.* — "If you had come, we would have gone to the sea together."

The ending of **இருப்பேன்** changes for the person, as every future does: **போயிருப்போம்**, "we would have gone"; **வந்திருப்பான்**, "he would have come". For "it", **இருக்கும்**:

> **அது நன்றாக இருந்திருக்கும்.**
>
> *atu naṉṟāka iruntirukkum.* — "That would have been nice."

The same form also makes a guess about the past: **அவன் வந்திருப்பான்** can mean "he must have come (by now)". The rest of the sentence tells you which.
