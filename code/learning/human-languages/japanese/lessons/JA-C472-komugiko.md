## You'll want to know: こむぎこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C472-INGR472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こむぎこ** — *komugiko* — "wheat flour".

> **こむぎこと たまごを まぜます。** — *komugiko to tamago o mazemasu* — "I mix the flour and the eggs."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こむぎこ** (*komugiko*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ko*, "small", *mugi*, "wheat", and *ko*, "powder" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
