Persian, chapter 412: Investing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سرمایه (sarmâye) — capital, an investment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for in debt, then the Persian for a creditor.

You'll want to know: سرمایه.
سرمایه (sarmâye) — "capital, an investment".
او با سرمایه کمی یک فروشگاه باز کرد. u bâ sarmâye-ye kami yek forushgâh bâz kard. He opened a shop with a little capital.
Sar "head" + mâye "substance, stock".

The word, taken apart.
سرمایه (sarmâye) is a native Persian compound built from sar "head" + mâye "substance, stock", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian "essence, substance". Capital is the head stock of a business.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سرمایه گذاری کردن (sarmâye gozâri kardan) — to invest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a creditor, then the Persian for capital.

You'll want to know: سرمایه گذاری کردن.
سرمایه گذاری کردن (sarmâye gozâri kardan) — "to invest".
پدرم در یک شرکت کوچک سرمایه گذاری کرد. pedaram dar yek sherkat-e kuchek sarmâye gozâri kard. My father invested in a small company.
Sarmâye + gozâri "placing", from gozâshtan "to put", + kardan. Takes dar.

The word, taken apart.
سرمایه گذاری کردن (sarmâye gozâri kardan) is native Persian, built from sarmâye "capital" + gozâri "placing", built on gozâr-, the present stem of gozâshtan "to put", + kardan. To invest is to place capital.

او سهام یک شرکت بزرگ را خرید. u sahâm-e yek sherkat-e bozorg râ kharid. She bought shares in a big company.
The Arabic broken plural of sahm "a share, a portion".

The word, taken apart.
سهام (sahâm) is an Arabic broken plural of sahm "part, portion", which EtymDB lists as the source of Persian sahm.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بورس (burs) — the stock exchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to invest, then the Persian for shares.

You'll want to know: بورس.
بورس (burs) — "the stock exchange".
قیمت سهام در بورس پایین آمد. qeymat-e sahâm dar burs pâyin âmad. Share prices fell on the stock exchange.
A French loan. It also turns up in bursiye "scholarship".

The word, taken apart.
بورس (burs) is usually traced to French bourse "stock exchange; purse", from Late Latin bursa "purse".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ورشکسته (varshekaste) — bankrupt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for shares, then the Persian for the stock exchange.

You'll want to know: ورشکسته.
ورشکسته (varshekaste) — "bankrupt".
آن شرکت سال گذشته ورشکسته شد. ân sherkat sâl-e gozashte varshekaste shod. That company went bankrupt last year.
Var + shekaste "broken". Varshekast shodan — to go bankrupt.

The word, taken apart.
ورشکسته (varshekaste) is native Persian, built on shekaste "broken", the participle of shekastan "to break", which EtymDB traces to PIE skeyd- "to break". A bankrupt business is broken.
