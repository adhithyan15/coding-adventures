Tamil, chapter 97: Definitely, and Why Not.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
கண்டிப்பாக (kaṇṭippāka) — definitely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for that's right, then the Tamil for I don't know.

You'll want to know: கண்டிப்பாக.
கண்டிப்பாக (kaṇṭippāka) — "definitely", "for sure".
கண்டிப்பாக (kaṇṭippāka) வருகிறேன் — "I'll definitely come".

What you've built.
A firm yes.

Lesson 2 of 3.
அப்படித்தான் (appaṭittāṉ) — that's how it is.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for I don't know, then the Tamil for definitely.

You'll want to know: அப்படித்தான்.
அப்படித்தான் (appaṭittāṉ) — "that's how it is", "quite so".
அப்படி, "like that", with -தான், the same "that and no other" as சரிதான்.

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 3.
ஏன் இல்லை? (ēṉ illai?) — why not?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for definitely, then the Tamil for that's how it is.

You'll want to know: ஏன் இல்லை?
ஏன் இல்லை? (ēṉ illai?) — "why not?".
ஏன் is "why"; with இல்லை, "why not?" — as a question, or as a cheerful yes.

What you've built.
Definitely, that's how it is, why not.
