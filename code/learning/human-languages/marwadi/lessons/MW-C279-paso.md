## You'll want to know: पासो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C279-GOTHIYO279-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पासो** — *pāso* — "a die for games".

> म्हारो पासो लाल है।
>
> *mhāro pāso lāl hai.*
>
> My die is red.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पासो** (*pāso*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pāśa*, "a die"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *pāsā*, "die" (CDIAL 8132).
