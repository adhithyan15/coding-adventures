## You'll want to know: هواشناسی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C473-HAVASHENASI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**هواشناسی** (*havâshenâsi*) — "the weather service; meteorology".

> هواشناسی برای فردا باران اعلام کرد.
>
> *Havâshenâsi barâ-ye fardâ bârân e'lâm kard.*
>
> The weather service forecast rain for tomorrow.

*havâ* 'air' + *shenâsi* 'knowing, the study of'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*havâshenâsi* is built from *havâ* 'air', borrowed from Arabic *hawāʾ* (EtymDB), and native *shenâsi* 'knowing, the study of', from *shenâkhtan* 'to know'.
