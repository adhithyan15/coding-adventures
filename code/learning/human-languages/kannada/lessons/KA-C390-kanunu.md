## You'll want to know: ಕಾನೂನು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C390-KANUNU390-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾನೂನು** — *kānūnu* — "a law; the law".

> ಈ ಕಾನೂನು ಮುಂದಿನ ತಿಂಗಳಿನಿಂದ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ī kānūnu mundina tiṅgaḷininda baruttade.*
>
> This law comes in from next month.

ಕಾನೂನುಬದ್ಧ, legal; ಕಾನೂನುಬಾಹಿರ, illegal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾನೂನು** (*kānūnu*) is a loan from Arabic *qānūn*, "a rule, a canon", through Persian and Hindustani; Arabic took it from Greek *kanōn*, "a rule".
