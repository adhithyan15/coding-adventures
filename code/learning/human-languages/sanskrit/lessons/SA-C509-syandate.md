## You'll want to know: स्यन्दते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C509-JARJARA509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्यन्दते** — *syandate* — "trickles".

> रन्ध्रात् जलं स्यन्दते।
>
> *randhrāt jalaṃ syandate.*
>
> Water trickles from the hole.

Past (it …): जलं स्यन्नवत्, *jalaṃ syannavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्यन्दते** (*syandate*) comes from the root √syand, "to move on, to flow, to trickle"; its past participle is *syanna* (MW).
