## Chapters 87-108: 105 pre-A1 words, a script lesson back under budget, and Tamil attains pre-A1

Tamil meets every HL09 §3.1 criterion at pre-A1, the fourth track to attain any
level (after Spanish at A1, Telugu and Hindi at pre-A1).

```
before   tamil: working pre-A1, vocabulary 208/300 — 2 blockers (atom-budget 1, vocabulary 92)
after    tamil: complete pre-A1, working A1
```

**Atom budget.** `TA-W01-abugida-va-ka` introduced four atoms against a budget
of three. The abugida idea itself ("every consonant carries a built-in *a*",
`TA-SCRIPT-ABUGIDA-VA-KA-01`) moved one lesson earlier, into
`TA-W01-curves-va-ka`, which is already about how Tamil writing works; the
va-ka lesson now recalls it and introduces the two letters and க's positional
sounds. No renumbering, no new segment.

Chained like the Telugu and Hindi tranches; chapter 108 closes on two reviews.
Two older atoms surfaced as under-revisited once the track grew (the same
effect recorded in lessons.d for Hindi) and now get a real revisit: the
thing-without-**-ஐ** rule in **குடியுங்கள்** ("தண்ணீர் குடியுங்கள்"), and
**உன்னை** in "see you later". நிஜமா was dropped for **அடடா** because ஜ is not
a letter this book teaches.

Not a claim about learners: no exam item has been scored against Tamil.
