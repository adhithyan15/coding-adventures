Japanese, chapter 308: A Wrong Number.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
まちがい (machigai) — a mistake; an error.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a mobile phone, then the Japanese for to call back.

You'll want to know: まちがい.
まちがい — machigai — "a mistake; an error".
まちがいの でんわが ありました。 — machigai no denwa ga arimashita — "Someone rang the wrong number."

The word, taken apart.
まちがい (machigai) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the noun of machigau, "to be mistaken", the intransitive partner of machigaeru (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
じゅわき (juwaki) — a telephone receiver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a mistake, then the Japanese for to be heard.

You'll want to know: じゅわき.
じゅわき — juwaki — "a telephone receiver".
じゅわきから こえが きこえました。 — juwaki kara koe ga kikoemashita — "A voice came from the receiver."

The word, taken apart.
じゅわき (juwaki) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ju, "accept" (Middle Chinese dzyuwX), wa, "talk" (hwaejH) and ki, "instrument" (khijH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.
はなしかける (hanashikakeru) — to speak to (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be heard, then the Japanese for a telephone receiver.

You'll want to know: はなしかける.
はなしかける — hanashikakeru — "to speak to (someone)".
えきで おばあさんに はなしかけました。 — eki de obāsan ni hanashikakemashita — "At the station I spoke to an old woman."
Polite はなしかけます, hanashikakemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): おとこが こどもに はなしかけました。, otoko ga kodomo ni hanashikakemashita.

The word, taken apart.
はなしかける (hanashikakeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from hanashi, "speaking" (from hanasu), and kakeru, "to set about" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
