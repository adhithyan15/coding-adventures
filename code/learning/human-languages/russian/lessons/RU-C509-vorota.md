## You'll want to know: ворота
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C509-FOOTBALL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ворота** (*voróta*) — "gates; the goal (in football)".

> Мяч пролетел мимо ворот.
>
> *Myach proletél mímo vorót.*
>
> The ball flew past the goal.

Plural only; genitive ворот.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ворота* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *vorta* (EtymDB), with the native East Slavic *-оро-*.
