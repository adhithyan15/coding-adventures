## You'll want to know: realizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C511-HACER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**realizar** — "to carry out, to do".

> Realizamos obras en la calle.
>
> *We are carrying out works on the street.*

A false friend: to realise is *darse cuenta*.
