## You'll want to know: पसरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C496-NEEND496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पसरना** — *pasarnā* — "to sprawl; to stretch out".

> काम से लौटकर अरुण बिस्तर पर पसर गया।
>
> *kām se lauṭkar Aruṇ bistar par pasar gayā.*
>
> Back from work, Arun sprawled out on the bed.

Past (he …): वह बिस्तर पर पसर गया, *vah bistar par pasar gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पसरना** (*pasarnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *pasarnā*, "to spread out", under Sanskrit *prásarati*, "spreads out" (CDIAL 8825): a tired body spreads out across the bed.
