# すぎる (sugiru) — to go past; to pass, to exceed

## You'll want to know: すぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C195-CORNER195-04]; assesses=[] -->

**すぎる** — *sugiru* — "to go past; to pass, to exceed".

> **あの しんごうを すぎます。** — *ano shingō o sugimasu* — "We go past that traffic light."

Polite **すぎます**, *sugimasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すぎる** (*sugiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi; JMdict marks at least one of its senses as usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
