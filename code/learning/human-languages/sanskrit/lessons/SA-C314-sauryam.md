## You'll want to know: शौर्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C314-SHAURYA314-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शौर्यम्** — *śauryam* — "valour; heroism".

> शौर्येण सः आहताय औषधम् आनयति।
>
> *śauryeṇa saḥ āhatāya auṣadham ānayati.*
>
> With great valour he brings medicine to the injured man.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शौर्यम्** (*śauryam*) is usually traced to *śūra*, "a hero", with the lengthened first vowel; MW gives *śaurya* as heroism, valour, prowess (MW).
