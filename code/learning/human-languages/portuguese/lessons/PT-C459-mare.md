## You'll want to know: a maré
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C459-ROCHEDOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a maré** — "the tide".

> Com a maré baixa, podemos andar até à ilha.
>
> *At low tide, we can walk to the island.*

*Maré alta* — high tide; *maré baixa* — low tide.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maré* came from French *marée* 'tide', built on *mer* 'sea', from Latin *mare* (EtymDB).
