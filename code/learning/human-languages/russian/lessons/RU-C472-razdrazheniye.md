## You'll want to know: раздражение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C472-ANGER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**раздражение** (*razdrazhéniye*) — "irritation".

> Он ответил с раздражением.
>
> *On otvétil s razdrazhéniyem.*
>
> He answered irritably.

Neuter. *С раздражением* — irritably.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*раздражение* is built on *раздражать* 'to irritate' with *-ение*; the verb's stem is usually linked to *дразнить* 'to tease', and EtymDB has no entry for the noun.
