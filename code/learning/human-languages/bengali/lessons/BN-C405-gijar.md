## You'll want to know: গিজার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C405-BATHRUM405-03]; assesses=[] -->

**গিজার** — *gijār* — "a water heater; a geyser".

> গিজারটা চালু করলে গরম জল পাবে।
>
> *gijārṭā chālu kôrle gôrôm jôl pābe.*
>
> Switch on the water heater and you will get hot water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গিজার** (*gijār*) is borrowed from English *geyser*, the Indian English name for a water heater in a bathroom, from the hot springs of Iceland.
