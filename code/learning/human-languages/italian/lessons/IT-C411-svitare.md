## You'll want to know: svitare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C411-COFANO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**svitare** — "to unscrew".

> Svita la ruota con la chiave.
>
> *Unscrew the wheel with the wrench.*

The opposite is *avvitare*, to screw in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*svitare* is built from *s-* and *vite* 'screw', which is inherited from Latin *vītis* 'vine' (EtymDB): the thread of a screw winds like a vine.
