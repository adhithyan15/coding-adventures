## You'll want to know: tunc
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C291-PRIMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tunc** — "then, at that time".

> Tunc urbs parva erat, sed mūrī altī erant.
>
> *At that time the city was small, but the walls were high.*

A stronger *tum* (taught): at that very time. Livy and later writers use it freely in stories.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**tunc** is built on *tum*, "then", with the pointing particle *-ce* that is also seen in *hic* (Lewis & Short): then, at that point.
