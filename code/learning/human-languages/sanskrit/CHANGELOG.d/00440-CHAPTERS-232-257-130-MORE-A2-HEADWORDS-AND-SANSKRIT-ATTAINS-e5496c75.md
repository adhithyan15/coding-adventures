## Chapters 232-257: 130 more A2 headwords, and Sanskrit attains A2

The last of four Sanskrit A2 vocabulary tranches. Twenty-six chapters of five
words each, in three runs of at most nine chapters, and each run closes with
two reviews.

- **Chapters 232-235: twenty verbs**, including **जायते** (is born),
  **म्रियते** (dies), **वर्षति** (rains), **प्रतीक्षते** (waits),
  **अनुगच्छति** (follows), **आरभते** (begins), **समापयति** (finishes) and
  **आलिंगति** (embraces). **वर्तते** is glossed "is present, exists", because
  "is" already belongs to **भवति**.
- **Chapters 236-238: people**: **शिशुः**, **कुमारः**, **युवतिः**, the crafts
  (**कुम्भकारः**, **स्वर्णकारः**, **तन्तुवायः**), and **परिचारिका**,
  **अभियन्ता**, **वैज्ञानिकः**.
- **Chapter 239: feeling for others** (**आशा**, **प्रेम**, **स्नेहः**, **दया**,
  **करुणा**).
- **Chapter 240: time** (**शनैः**, **प्रतिदिनम्**, **नित्यम्**).
- **Chapter 241: joining clauses** (**कृते**, **येन**, **तेन**, **यत्**,
  **परन्तु**).
- **Chapters 242-244: fifteen qualities**, such as **सुलभः**, **दुर्लभः**,
  **जटिलः**, **रुग्णः**, **स्वस्थः**, **उचितः** and **आवश्यकः**.
- **Chapters 245-257: sixty-five nouns**:
  - food (**तक्रम्**, **एला**, **मिष्टान्नम्**, **प्रातराशः**);
  - arms (**बाणः**, **खड्गः**, **ध्वजः**);
  - money (**निधिः**, **रूप्यकम्**, **शुल्कम्**);
  - news and stories (**वार्ता**, **नाटकम्**, **काव्यम्**);
  - buildings (**दुर्गम्**, **प्रासादः**, **कुटीरम्**);
  - the world (**पृथिवी**, **प्रकृतिः**);
  - plants and small animals (**तुलसी**, **कीटः**, **लूता**);
  - study (**शिक्षा**, **ज्ञानम्**, **गृहकार्यम्**, **उत्तरम्**);
  - thought (**निर्णयः**, **विचारः**, **मतम्**);
  - travel (**आगमनम्**, **प्रस्थानम्**).
- **Sanskrit attains A2.** Chapters 139-257 realize the last two A2 spine nodes
  and teach 595 headwords, ninety-five of them verbs. At or below A2 the track
  now teaches 1,226 distinct headwords (target 1,200) and 167 verbs (target
  120). The level gate reports no blocker below B1, and the attainment pin moves
  from A1 to A2.
- **Pins:** the Sanskrit lesson-content budget goes from 1255 to 1391.
