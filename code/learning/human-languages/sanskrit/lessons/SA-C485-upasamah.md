## You'll want to know: उपशमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C485-SUSRUSA485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उपशमः** — *upaśamaḥ* — "relief".

> औषधेन वेदनायाः उपशमः भवति।
>
> *auṣadhena vedanāyāḥ upaśamaḥ bhavati.*
>
> The medicine brings relief from the pain.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपशमः** (*upaśamaḥ*) is built from *upa* and the root √śam, "to be calm"; MW gives "assuagement, alleviation, cessation" (MW).
