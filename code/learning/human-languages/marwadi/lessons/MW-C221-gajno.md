## You'll want to know: गाजणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C221-RAIN221-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गाजणो** — *gājṇo* — "to thunder; to roar".

> आज रात बादल गाजै है।
>
> *āj rāt bādal gājai hai.*
>
> Tonight the clouds are thundering.

Past (it …): वो बादल गाज्यो, *vo bādal gājyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गाजणो** (*gājṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *garjati*, "thunders, roars"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *gājaï*, "thunders" (CDIAL 4046).
