## You'll want to know: 出现
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C236-ODD236-01]; assesses=[] -->

**出现** — *chūxiàn* — "to appear; to turn up".

> **草地里出现了一个人** *cǎodì lǐ chūxiànle yí ge rén* — Someone appeared on the lawn.

The new character, in pieces: 现 — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left two bars, a vertical and a stroke rising to the right, then 见 on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出现** (*chūxiàn*) is built from 出 'to come out' and 现 'to show', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *henH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to reveal', noting the character as an everyday form.
