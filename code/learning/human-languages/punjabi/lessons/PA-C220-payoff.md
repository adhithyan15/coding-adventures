# Who did what: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220, PA-GRAMMAR-US-NE-220] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *Aman bought a book*, then *she drank tea*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220, PA-GRAMMAR-US-NE-220, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-LIA-DITTA-220, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *Aman gave money*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *did he drink milk?*] Say **ਕੀ ਉਸ ਨੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ?**
- [YOU READ: **ਉਸ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਲਈ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।**] Give its meaning: he paid money for the book.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ।**]
- Check: **ਨੇ** after the named doer, and the ending after the thing.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220, PA-GRAMMAR-US-NE-220, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-LIA-DITTA-220] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Tell what Aman did yesterday: he went to the market, bought a book,
and drank tea.
