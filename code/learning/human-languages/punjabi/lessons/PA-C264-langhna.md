## You'll want to know: ਲੰਘਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C264-CROSS264-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੰਘਣਾ** — *laṅghṇā* — "to pass through; to go past".

> ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਤੋਂ ਲੰਘਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ roz bāzār ton laṅghdā hāṁ.*
>
> I pass through the market every day.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪੁਲ ਤੋਂ ਲੰਘਿਆ, *maiṁ pul ton laṅghiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੰਘਣਾ** (*laṅghṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *laṅghayati*, "leaps over"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *laṅghṇā*, "to pass", under it (CDIAL 10905).
