## You'll want to know: स्वयंचलित
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C518-YANTRA518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंचलित** — *svayañcalit* — "automatic".

> इमारतीचे दरवाजे स्वयंचलित आहेत.
>
> *imārtīce darvāje svayañcalit āhet.*
>
> The building's doors are automatic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंचलित** (*svayañcalit*) is a modern tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *svayam*, "by itself", and *calita*, "moving" (Monier-Williams).
