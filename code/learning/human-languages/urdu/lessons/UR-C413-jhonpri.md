## You'll want to know: جھونپڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C413-KOTHI413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جھونپڑی** — *jhoṅpṛī* — "a hut".

> دریا کے کنارے ایک جھونپڑی ہے۔
>
> *daryā ke kināre ek jhoṅpṛī hai.*
>
> There is a hut on the riverbank.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھونپڑی** (*jhoṅpṛī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhõpṛī*, "a hut", under *jhuppa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5403).
