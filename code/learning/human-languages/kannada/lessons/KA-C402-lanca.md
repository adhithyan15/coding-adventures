## You'll want to know: ಲಂಚ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C402-LANCHA402-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಂಚ** — *lañca* — "a bribe".

> ಆ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ ಲಂಚ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಸಿಕ್ಕಿಬಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *ā adhikāri lañca tegedukoṇḍu sikkibiddanu.*
>
> That official was caught taking a bribe.

ಲಂಚ ಕೊಡು, to bribe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಂಚ** (*lañca*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lañcā*, "a present, a bribe" (CDIAL 10914).

## What you've built
