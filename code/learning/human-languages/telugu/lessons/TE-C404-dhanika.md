## You'll want to know: ధనిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C404-PEDARIKAM404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ధనిక** — *dhanika* — "rich; wealthy".

> ధనిక దేశాలు పేద దేశాలకు సహాయం చేయాలి.
>
> *dhanika dēśālu pēda dēśālaku sahāyaṁ cēyāli.*
>
> Rich countries should help poor countries.

Before a noun: ధనిక కుటుంబం, a rich family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ధనిక** (*dhanika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dhanika*, "wealthy, opulent" (Monier-Williams), from *dhana*, "wealth".
