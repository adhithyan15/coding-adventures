Portuguese, chapter 399: Na rede.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a rede — the network; the net.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the attachment, then the Portuguese for to attach.

You'll want to know: a rede.
a rede — "the network; the net".
Não há rede aqui na aldeia. There is no signal here in the village.
Estar na rede — to be online; as redes sociais — social media. Also a fishing net.

The word, taken apart.
rede is inherited from Latin rēte "a net" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese rede.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: o motor de busca.
o motor de busca — "the search engine".
Escrevi o nome do restaurante no motor de busca. I typed the name of the restaurant into the search engine.
Also o motor de pesquisa.

The word, taken apart.
motor comes through French moteur from Latin mōtor "a mover" (EtymDB), on movēre "to move" (Lewis & Short); busca "search" is built on buscar "to look for", whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a hiperligação — the web link, the hyperlink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to search, then the Portuguese for the search engine.

You'll want to know: a hiperligação.
a hiperligação — "the web link, the hyperlink".
Cliquei na hiperligação e abriu uma página nova. I clicked on the link and a new page opened.
European Portuguese, formal; in speech most people say o link.

The word, taken apart.
hiperligação is built from Greek hypér "over, beyond" and ligação "connection", on ligar, inherited from Latin ligāre "to tie" (Lewis & Short); it translates English hyperlink.
