# పెద్దమ్మ (peddamma) — a mother's elder sister

## You'll want to know: పెద్దమ్మ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C529-MENAMAMA529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పెద్దమ్మ** — *peddamma* — "a mother's elder sister".

> పెద్దమ్మ మాకు అమ్మలాగే.
>
> *peddamma māku ammalāgē.*
>
> My mother's elder sister is like a mother to us.

Also the wife of పెద్దనాన్న.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పెద్దమ్మ** (*peddamma*) is built from native *pedda*, "great, big, elder" (DEDR 4411), and అమ్మ, "mother".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
