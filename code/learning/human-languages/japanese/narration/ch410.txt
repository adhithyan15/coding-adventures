Japanese, chapter 410: Where I Come From.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
じもと (jimoto) — one's home area; the local area.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a boundary, then the Japanese for a dialect.

You'll want to know: じもと.
じもと — jimoto — "one's home area; the local area".
じもとの まつりは なつです。 — jimoto no matsuri wa natsu desu — "The festival back home is in summer."

The word, taken apart.
じもと (jimoto) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese ji, "ground" (Middle Chinese dijH), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) moto, "origin", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
しゅっしん (shusshin) — one's place of origin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a dialect, then the Japanese for one's home area.

You'll want to know: しゅっしん.
しゅっしん — shusshin — "one's place of origin".
せんせいは どこの しゅっしんですか。 — sensei wa doko no shusshin desu ka — "Where are you from, teacher?"

The word, taken apart.
しゅっしん (shusshin) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shutsu, "exit" (Middle Chinese tsyhwit) and shin, "somebody" (syin), joined as shusshin (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
しげる (shigeru) — to grow thick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a field (for crops), then the Japanese for a meadow.

You'll want to know: しげる.
しげる — shigeru — "to grow thick".
なつは くさが しげります。 — natsu wa kusa ga shigerimasu — "In summer the grass grows thick."
Polite しげります, shigerimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): にわの くさが しげりました。, niwa no kusa ga shigerimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しげる (shigeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
