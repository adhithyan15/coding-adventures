## You'll want to know: उधाण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C409-KHALASHI409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उधाण** — *udhāṇ* — "a sea surge; a spring tide".

> आज समुद्राला मोठं उधाण आलं.
>
> *āj samudrālā moṭhaṁ udhāṇ ālaṁ.*
>
> Today the sea surged high.

Neuter.

उधाण येणे, a surge comes; it also names a rush of joy: आनंदाला उधाण आलं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उधाण** (*udhāṇ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *udhāṇ*, "spring-tide", under *uddhāna*, "rising up" (CDIAL 2015).
