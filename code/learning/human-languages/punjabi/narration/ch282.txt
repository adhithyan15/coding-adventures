Punjabi, chapter 282: Bags for the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਅਟੈਚੀ (aṭaichī) — a suitcase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to put off, then the Punjabi for at once.

You'll want to know: ਅਟੈਚੀ.
ਅਟੈਚੀ — aṭaichī — "a suitcase".
ਮੇਰੀ ਅਟੈਚੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਭਾਰੀ ਹੈ। merī aṭaichī bahut bhārī hai. My suitcase is very heavy.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਅਟੈਚੀ (aṭaichī) is borrowed from English attaché, as in an attaché case.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what comes next.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਬਿਸਤਰਾ (bisatrā) — a bedding roll; bedding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for at once, then the Punjabi for a suitcase.

You'll want to know: ਬਿਸਤਰਾ.
ਬਿਸਤਰਾ — bisatrā — "a bedding roll; bedding".
ਅਸੀਂ ਬਿਸਤਰਾ ਰੇਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖਾਂਗੇ। asīṁ bisatrā rel vich rakkhāṅge. We will put the bedding roll in the train.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਬਿਸਤਰਾ (bisatrā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian bistar, "bedding".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਝੋਲਾ (jholā) — a cloth shoulder bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a suitcase, then the Punjabi for a bedding roll.

You'll want to know: ਝੋਲਾ.
ਝੋਲਾ — jholā — "a cloth shoulder bag".
ਝੋਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਣੀ ਦੀ ਬੋਤਲ ਹੈ। jhole vich pāṇī dī botal hai. There is a bottle of water in the shoulder bag.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਝੋਲਾ (jholā) is inherited from a reconstructed jhōla, "bag"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jholā under it (CDIAL 5415).

ਮੈਂ ਟਿਕਟਾਂ ਸਾਂਭਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ ṭikṭāṁ sāmbhāṅgā. I will keep the tickets safe.
