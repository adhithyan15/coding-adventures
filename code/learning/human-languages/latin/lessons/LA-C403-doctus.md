## You'll want to know: doctus, docta, doctum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C403-DOCTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**doctus, docta, doctum** — "learned, skilled".

> Vir doctus et prūdēns est.
>
> *He is a learned and wise man.*

*doctus, docta, doctum*, the participle of *doceō*: taught.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*doctus* is the participle of *doceō* "to teach" (Lewis & Short): one who has been taught.
