## You'll want to know: अधूनमधून
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C306-KRAMASH306-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अधूनमधून** — *adhūnmadhūn* — "now and then".

> आजोबा अधूनमधून जुन्या गोष्टी सांगतात.
>
> *ājobā adhūnmadhūn junyā goṣṭī sāṅgtāt.*
>
> Grandpa tells old stories now and then.

A rhyming pair; कधीकधी means much the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधूनमधून** (*adhūnmadhūn*) is a rhyming pair built on *madhūn*, 'from the middle'; the origin of the first half, *adhūn*, is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
