German, chapter 113: Shower, Bathtub, Shelf, Curtain, Cushion.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
die Dusche — a shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a fridge, then the German for a cooker.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read the two sentences aloud, as one run: Das Hotel ist gegenüber. Das Kino ist hinten.

You'll want to know: die Dusche.
die Dusche — "a shower".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Dusche]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Dusche, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say die Dusche]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a fridge, then the German for a cooker, then say die Dusche again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Dusche mean? ("A shower".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: a restaurant, a bakery, a cinema, a theatre, a town hall]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a supermarket, a petrol station, a swimming pool, a stadium, a factory]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an office, a corner, a crossroads, traffic lights, a car park]

[your turn — say: the north, the south, the east, the west, straight on]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

A stadium? (das Stadion.) A factory? (die Fabrik.) An office? (das Büro.) A corner? (die Ecke.) A crossroads? (die Kreuzung.) Traffic lights? (die Ampel.) A car park? (der Parkplatz.) The north? (der Norden.) The south? (der Süden.) The east? (der Osten.) The west? (der Westen.) Straight on? (geradeaus.)
