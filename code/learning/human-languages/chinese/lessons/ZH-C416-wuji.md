## You'll want to know: wùjī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C416-STORM416-03]; assesses=[] -->

*wùjī* — "to miss a flight".

> *wǒmen zuò chūzūchē qù jīchǎng bùrán huì wùjī* — Let's take a taxi to the airport, or we'll miss the plane.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wùjī* is built from *wù* 'to miss' and 机 'a plane', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nguH* and *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'an error'.
