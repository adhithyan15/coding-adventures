Urdu, chapter 390: Departures and Arrivals.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آمد (āmad) — an arrival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a yawn, then the Urdu for to doze.

You'll want to know: آمد.
آمد — āmad — "an arrival".
گاڑی کی آمد شام کو ہے۔ gāṛī kī āmad shām ko hai. The train gets in this evening.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
آمد (āmad) is usually traced to Persian āmad, "a coming", from the verb for "to come"; station boards list arrivals under it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تاخیر (tākhīr) — lateness; a delay (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an arrival, then the Urdu for setting off.

You'll want to know: تاخیر.
تاخیر — tākhīr — "lateness; a delay (formal)".
بارش کی وجہ سے گاڑی کی آمد میں تاخیر ہے۔ bārish kī vajah se gāṛī kī āmad meṅ tākhīr hai. The train is arriving late because of the rain.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تاخیر (tākhīr) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taʾkhīr, "a putting off"; station announcements use it for a late train, where everyday talk says دیر.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اتارنا (utārnā) — to take down; to drop off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for lateness, then the Urdu for cancelled.

You'll want to know: اتارنا.
اتارنا — utārnā — "to take down; to drop off".
قلی نے ڈبے سے ہمارا سامان اتارا۔ qulī ne ḍabbe se hamārā sāmān utārā. The porter took our luggage down from the carriage.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے صندوق اتارا, us ne sandūq utārā.

The word, taken apart.
اتارنا (utārnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi utārnā, "to take across, to make descend", under Sanskrit úttarati and its causative uttārayati (CDIAL 1770). It is the causing partner of اترنا, "to get off".
