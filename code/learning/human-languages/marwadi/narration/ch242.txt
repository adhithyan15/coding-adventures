Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 242: Fire, Water and a Broken Pot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जळणो (jaḷṇo) — to get burnt; to burn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to get broken, then the Marwadi for to slip.

You'll want to know: जळणो.
जळणो — jaḷṇo — "to get burnt; to burn".
रसोई मांय म्हारो हाथ जळ्यो। rasoī mā̃y mhāro hāth jaḷyo. My hand got burnt in the kitchen.
Past (it …): वो पापड़ जळ्यो, vo pāpaṛ jaḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
जळणो (jaḷṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit jvalati, "burns brightly"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari jalaï, "burns", under it (CDIAL 5306).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to slip, then the Marwadi for to get burnt.

You'll want to know: बुझणो.
बुझणो — bujhṇo — "to go out (of a flame)".
रात मांय दीयो बुझ्यो। rāt mā̃y dīyo bujhyo. The lamp went out in the night.
Past (it …): वो चूल्हो बुझ्यो, vo chūlho bujhyo.

The word, taken apart.
बुझणो (bujhṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed vijjhāyati, "is burnt out, is extinguished"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari partner bujhāvaï, "to extinguish", under it (CDIAL 11703).

Lesson 3 of 5.
फूटणो (phūṭṇo) — to crack open; to burst.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to get burnt, then the Marwadi for to go out (of a flame).

You'll want to know: फूटणो.
फूटणो — phūṭṇo — "to crack open; to burst".
म्हारो मटको फूट्यो। mhāro maṭko phūṭyo. My big clay pot cracked.
Past (it …): वो नारेळ फूट्यो, vo nāreḷ phūṭyo.

The word, taken apart.
फूटणो (phūṭṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed sphuṭyati, "is burst open". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi phūṭnā, "to split, burst" (CDIAL 13845).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ठीकरो (ṭhīkro) — a potsherd; a shard of a broken pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to go out (of a flame), then the Marwadi for to crack open.

You'll want to know: ठीकरो.
ठीकरो — ṭhīkro — "a potsherd; a shard of a broken pot".
ठीकरो तीखो है। ṭhīkro tīkho hai. The shard of the pot is sharp.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ठीकरो (ṭhīkro) is inherited from a reconstructed ṭhīkkara, "potsherd"; CDIAL lists Marwari ṭhīkro, "potsherd", under it (CDIAL 5504).
