## You'll want to know: o lucro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C395-LUCRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o lucro** — "the profit".

> A loja teve lucro pela primeira vez.
>
> *The shop made a profit for the first time.*

*Dar lucro* — to make a profit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lucro* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lucrum* "gain, profit" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
