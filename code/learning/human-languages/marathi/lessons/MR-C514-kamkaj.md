## You'll want to know: कामकाज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C514-KAMKAJ514-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कामकाज** — *kāmkāj* — "the day's business; proceedings".

> सुट्टीमुळे कार्यालयाचं कामकाज बंद होतं.
>
> *suṭṭīmuḷe kāryālayācaṁ kāmkāj band hotaṁ.*
>
> Because of the holiday the office's business was closed.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कामकाज** (*kāmkāj*) is a Marathi pairing of काम, "work", and काज, "business", which is inherited from Sanskrit *kārya* (CDIAL 3078).
