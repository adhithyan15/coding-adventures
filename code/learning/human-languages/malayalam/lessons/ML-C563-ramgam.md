## You'll want to know: രംഗം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C563-RANGAM563-02]; assesses=[] -->

**രംഗം** — *raṁgaṁ* — "a stage scene; a scene".

> സിനിമയിലെ അവസാന രംഗം എല്ലാവരെയും കരയിച്ചു.
>
> *sinimayile avasāna raṁgaṁ ellāvareyuṁ karayiccu.*
>
> The last scene of the film made everyone cry.

Also a field of work: കലാരംഗം, the world of the arts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**രംഗം** (*raṁgaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *raṅga*, "dancing, a place of public amusement, a stage" (CDIAL 10561).
