## You'll want to know: anticipar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C616-IMPREV-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anticipar** — "to bring forward; to anticipate".

> Anticipamos la reunión al viernes.
>
> *We brought the meeting forward to Friday.*

*Adelantar* is more everyday. *Anticiparse a* — to get ahead of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anticipar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *anticipare* 'to take beforehand', built from *ante* and *capere* 'to take' (EtymDB).
