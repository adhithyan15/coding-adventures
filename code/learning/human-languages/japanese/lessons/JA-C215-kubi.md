## You'll want to know: くび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C215-BODY215-04]; assesses=[] -->

**くび** — *kubi* — "a neck".

> **くびが いたいですか。** — *kubi ga itai desu ka* — "Does your neck hurt?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くび** (*kubi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
