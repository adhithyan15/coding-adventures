## You'll want to know: madurar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C731-MADUREZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**madurar** — "to mature, to ripen".

> Has madurado mucho este año.
>
> *You have grown up a lot this year.*

For people and fruit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*madurar* comes from Latin *mātūrāre* "to ripen" (EtymDB), built on *mātūrus*.
