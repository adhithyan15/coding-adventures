Arabic, chapter 454: City buildings.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
عمارة (ʿimāra) — a block of flats; architecture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to get back, then the Arabic for a meter.

You'll want to know: عمارة.
عمارة — ʿimāra — "a block of flats; architecture".
نسكن في عمارة قديمة في وسط المدينة. naskunu fī ʿimāra qadīma fī wasaṭ al-madīna. We live in an old block of flats in the city centre.

The word, taken apart.
عمارة (ʿimāra) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-m-r, "to inhabit, to build up" (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ناطحة سحاب (nāṭiḥat saḥāb) — a skyscraper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a meter, then the Arabic for a block of flats.

You'll want to know: ناطحة سحاب.
ناطحة سحاب — nāṭiḥat saḥāb — "a skyscraper".
رأينا ناطحة سحاب من نافذة الفندق. raʾaynā nāṭiḥat saḥāb min nāfidhat al-funduq. We saw a skyscraper from the hotel window.

The word, taken apart.
ناطحة سحاب (nāṭiḥat saḥāb) is a modern coinage built from two native Arabic words (Lane gives both roots): nāṭiḥa, "one that butts", on n-ṭ-ḥ, "to butt with the horns", and saḥāb, "clouds". It is a calque of English skyscraper.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a block of flats, then the Arabic for a skyscraper.

You'll want to know: ضاحية.
ضاحية — ḍāḥiya — "a suburb".
يسكن عمي في ضاحية بعيدة عن المركز. yaskunu ʿammī fī ḍāḥiya baʿīda ʿan al-markaz. My uncle lives in a suburb far from the centre.

The word, taken apart.
ضاحية (ḍāḥiya) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-ḥ-w, "to be in the sun; to come into view" (Lane). Lane gives ḍāḥiya as the outer, exposed side or tract of a place; ḍuḥā, "late morning", shares the root.

مسبح — masbaḥ — "a swimming pool".
سبح الأطفال في مسبح الفندق. sabaḥa al-aṭfāl fī masbaḥ al-funduq. The children swam in the hotel pool.

The word, taken apart.
مسبح (masbaḥ) is built on the native Arabic root s-b-ḥ, "to swim" (Lane). The pattern mafʿal makes it the place of swimming.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a suburb, then the Arabic for a swimming pool.

You'll want to know: مجمع تجاري.
مجمع تجاري — mujammaʿ tijārī — "a shopping centre".
اشترينا الملابس من مجمع تجاري كبير. ishtaraynā al-malābis min mujammaʿ tijārī kabīr. We bought the clothes at a big shopping centre.

The word, taken apart.
مجمع تجاري (mujammaʿ tijārī) is built from two native Arabic words: mujammaʿ, "a place where things are gathered", on the root j-m-ʿ, "to gather" (Lane), and tijārī, "of trade", on t-j-r (Lane).
