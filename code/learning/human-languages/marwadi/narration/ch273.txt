Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 273: Hush, a Secret.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चुप (chup) — silent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for of course, then the Marwadi for agreed.

You'll want to know: चुप.
चुप — chup — "silent".
चुप रैवो। chup raivo. Keep quiet.

The word, taken apart.
चुप (chup) is inherited from a reconstructed cuppa, "silent"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi cup, "silent" (CDIAL 4864).

Lesson 2 of 5.
इसारो (isāro) — a gesture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for agreed, then the Marwadi for silent.

You'll want to know: इसारो.
इसारो — isāro — "a gesture".
मास्टर इसारो करै है अर छोरा चुप है। māsṭar isāro karai hai ar chhorā chup hai. The teacher makes a sign and the boys are quiet.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
इसारो (isāro) is usually traced, through Persian ishāra, to Arabic ishāra, "a sign"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
भेद (bhed) — a secret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for silent, then the Marwadi for a gesture.

You'll want to know: भेद.
भेद — bhed — "a secret".
यो भेद है, चुप रैवो। yo bhed hai, chup raivo. This is a secret: keep quiet.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भेद (bhed) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit bheda, "breaking open", and so "disclosing (a secret)" (Monier-Williams). Here it names the secret itself, a sense Monier-Williams does not list.

छिपावणो — chhipāvṇo — "to hide (something); to keep secret".
वो भेद छिपावै है। vo bhed chhipāvai hai. He keeps the secret.
Past (I …): म्हैं भेद छिपायो, mhaĩ bhed chhipāyo.

The word, taken apart.
छिपावणो (chhipāvṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed chipp, "cover, hide"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari chipāvaï, "hides" (CDIAL 4994).

Lesson 5 of 5.
वचन (vachan) — a promise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a secret, then the Marwadi for to keep secret.

You'll want to know: वचन.
वचन — vachan — "a promise".
यो म्हारो वचन है। yo mhāro vachan hai. This is my promise.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वचन (vachan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vacana, "speaking; a word" (Monier-Williams), which does not list the sense "a promise". CDIAL gives no Marwari form under vacana, but lists the inherited Gujarati veṇ, "word, promise" (CDIAL 11199): a word given is a promise.
