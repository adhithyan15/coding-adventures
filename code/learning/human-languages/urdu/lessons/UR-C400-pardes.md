## You'll want to know: پردیس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C400-PARDES400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پردیس** — *pardes* — "a foreign land".

> میری بہن پردیس میں کام کرتی ہے۔
>
> *merī bahan pardes meṅ kām kartī hai.*
>
> My sister works abroad.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پردیس** (*pardes*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pares*, "a foreign country", under Sanskrit *paradeśa* (CDIAL 7799a), built from *para*, "other", and *deśa*, "a country".
