Arabic, chapter 241: Polite, Patient, Modest, Funny, Serious.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مهذب (muhadhdhab) — polite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for suitable, then the Arabic for excellent.

You'll want to know: مهذب.
مهذب — muhadhdhab — "polite".

The word, taken apart.
مهذب (muhadhdhab) is built on the native Arabic root h-dh-b, "to prune, to refine" (Lane). Muhadhdhab is the Form II passive participle, "refined", hence "polite".
