## You'll want to know: খাটা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C332-SUTORANG332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**খাটা** — *khāṭā* — "to hold good; to apply (of a rule); to toil".

> এই নিয়মটা এখানে খাটে না।
>
> *ei niyômṭā ekhāne khāṭe nā.*
>
> This rule doesn't apply here.

Past (he/she …): সেই নিয়মটা খাটল না, *sei niyômṭā khāṭlô nā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খাটা** (*khāṭā*) is listed by CDIAL 3779 (*khaṭṭáyati*) with the senses 'to work' and 'to apply'. A person খাটে when working hard; a rule or a reason খাটে when it holds in a case.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
