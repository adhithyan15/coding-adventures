## You'll want to know: конкурент
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C363-PEREGOVORY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**конкурент** (*konkurént*) — "a competitor".

> Наш главный конкурент снизил цены.
>
> *Nash glávnyy konkurént snízil tsény.*
>
> Our main competitor has cut its prices.

Masculine. *Конкуренция* — competition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**конкурент** is usually traced, through German, to Latin *concurrēns*, the participle of *concurrere* "to run together, to clash" (Lewis & Short), built on *currere* "to run".
