## You'll want to know: 好喝
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C71-TASTE71-01]; assesses=[] -->

**好喝** — *hǎohē* — "nice to drink".

> *zhèr de chá hěn hǎohē* — The tea here is lovely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**好喝** (*hǎohē*) is built from 好 'good' and 喝 'to drink', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xawX* and *xat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as 'to shout at', and the drinking sense is not given there.
