## You'll want to know: 没事
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C167-PAST167-03]; assesses=[] -->

**没事** — *méishì* — "it's nothing; to be all right".

> **没事我很好** *méishì wǒ hěn hǎo* — It's nothing. I'm fine.

The new character, in pieces: 事 — eight strokes: a horizontal, a small 口, a horizontal that turns down, a long horizontal, a shorter one, and last a vertical with a hook down through them all. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**没事** (*méishì*) is built from 没 'not to have' and 事 'matter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mwot* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to sink' and the second 'to employ' and 'to set up'; 'a matter' is the everyday sense, not the gloss. *Mwot* would regularly give mò, not méi.
