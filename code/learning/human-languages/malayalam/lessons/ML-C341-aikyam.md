# ഐക്യം (aikyaṁ) — unity

## You'll want to know: ഐക്യം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C341-AIKYAM-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C135-TOWN-05, ML-CONCEPT-C35-KUDUMBAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C341-THINK-THAT-01] -->

**ഐക്യം** — *aikyaṁ* — "unity, togetherness": people holding together as one.

> **ഞങ്ങളുടെ ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ നല്ല ഐക്യം ഉണ്ട്.**
>
> *ñaṅṅaḷuṭe grāmattil nalla aikyaṁ uṇṭŭ.* — "There is real unity in our village."

> **കുടുംബത്തിൽ ഐക്യം വേണം എന്ന് ഞാൻ കരുതുന്നു.**
>
> *kuṭumbattil aikyaṁ vēṇaṁ ennŭ ñān karutunnu.* — "I think a family needs unity."

It is a word for speeches and newspapers as much as for kitchens, and it opens with a letter
you have not written yet. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഐക്യം** (*aikyaṁ*) ← Sanskrit *aikya*, "oneness", taken over whole as a tatsama with Malayalam's
noun ending (CDIAL 2533). *aikya* is built on *eka*, "one": Sanskrit lengthens the first vowel,
*e* to *ai*, to make "the state of being one". Hindi *ek*, "one", is the same root.
