## You'll want to know: 客车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C408-BUS408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**客车** — *kèchē* — "coach; long-distance bus".

> **我们坐客车去那儿** *wǒmen zuò kèchē qù nàr* — We're going there by coach.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**客车** (*kèchē*) is built from 客 'a guest' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaek* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a vehicle that carries people, not goods.
