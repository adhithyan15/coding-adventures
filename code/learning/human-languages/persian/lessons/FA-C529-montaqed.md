## You'll want to know: منتقد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C529-READERS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منتقد** (*montaqed*) — "a critic".

> منتقدها از فیلم تازه او تعریف کردند.
>
> *montaqedhâ az film-e tâze-ye u ta'rif kardand.*
>
> Critics praised his new film.

Plural *montaqedân* or *montaqedhâ*. *naqd* is criticism.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*montaqed* comes from Arabic *muntaqid* (EtymDB), a word from the same root as *naqd*, 'criticism'.
