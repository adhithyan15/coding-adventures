## You'll want to know: relatar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C563-RELATO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**relatar** — "to relate, to recount".

> El testigo relató lo que vio.
>
> *The witness recounted what he saw.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*relatar* is built on *relato* 'an account', which comes from Latin *relātus* 'reported', the participle of *referre* 'to carry back' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
