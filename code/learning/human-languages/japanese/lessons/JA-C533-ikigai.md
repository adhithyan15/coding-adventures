## You'll want to know: いきがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C533-WORTH533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**いきがい** — *ikigai* — "a purpose in life".

> **もし いきがいが みつかったら、まいにち たのしいでしょう。** — *moshi ikigai ga mitsukattara, mainichi tanoshii deshō* — "If you find a purpose in life, every day will surely be enjoyable."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いきがい** (*ikigai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
