Gujarati, chapter 208: Out on the highway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સડક (saḍak) — main road, paved road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for petrol, then the Gujarati for to hire.

You'll want to know: સડક.
સડક — saḍak — "main road, paved road".
સડક પહોળી હતી. — saḍak pahoḷī hatī — "The road was wide."

The word, taken apart.
સડક (saḍak) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "road", under Sanskrit sṛtí, "road, path", extended with the suffix -kk- (CDIAL 13577).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ફાટક (phāṭak) — level crossing, gate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for traffic, then the Gujarati for wheel.

You'll want to know: ફાટક.
ફાટક — phāṭak — "level crossing, gate".
ફાટક બંધ હતું. — phāṭak bandh hatũ — "The level-crossing gate was closed."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફાટક (phāṭak) is usually traced to phāṭṭakka, "gate, door", a reconstructed form whose own source CDIAL leaves in doubt. CDIAL lists Hindi phāṭak, "gate", but for Gujarati only phaṭkiyũ bārṇũ, "a single shutter to close a door" (CDIAL 9069).
