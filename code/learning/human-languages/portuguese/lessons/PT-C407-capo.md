## You'll want to know: o capô
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C407-CAPO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o capô** — "the bonnet (of a car)".

> O mecânico abriu o capô para ver o motor.
>
> *The mechanic opened the bonnet to check the engine.*

In Brazil also *o capô*; in the US, the hood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capô* was borrowed from French *capot* 'bonnet, cover' (EtymDB).
