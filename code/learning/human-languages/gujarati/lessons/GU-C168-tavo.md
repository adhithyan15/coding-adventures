**તવો** — *tavo* — "griddle".

> **તવો ગરમ છે.** — *tavo garam che* — "The griddle is hot."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તવો** (*tavo*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*tapaka*, "frying pan", a reconstructed form on the root *tap*, "to heat", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "griddle" (CDIAL 5670).
