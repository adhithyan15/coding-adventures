## You'll want to know: la transferencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C628-BANCO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la transferencia** — "the bank transfer; the transfer".

> Te hago una transferencia mañana.
>
> *I will send you a bank transfer tomorrow.*

*Hacer una transferencia* — to make a transfer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transferencia* is a learned word built on Latin *transferre* 'to carry across', from *trans* and *ferre* 'to carry' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
