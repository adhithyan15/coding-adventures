## You'll want to know: अवघड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C395-UPAYUKTA395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवघड** — *avghaḍ* — "tricky; hard".

> गणिताचा पेपर अवघड होता.
>
> *gaṇitācā pepar avghaḍ hotā.*
>
> The maths paper was hard.

Invariable; अवघड जागी, in an awkward spot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवघड** (*avghaḍ*) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
