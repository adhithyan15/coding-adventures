## You'll want to know: damit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C346-ZWECK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**damit** — "in order that, so that; with it".

> Ich spreche langsam, damit alle mich verstehen.
>
> *I speak slowly so that everyone understands me.*

As a conjunction *damit* sends the verb to the end and names an aim. As an adverb it means with it: *Was machst du damit?*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*damit* joins *da* "there" and *mit* "with", from Old High German *miti* (EtymDB). It first meant "with that"; the sense "so that" grew out of it.
