# கூட்டணி (kūṭṭaṇi) — an alliance; a coalition

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C374-TERTAL374-05, TA-LEX-C375-KATCI375-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for a campaign, then the Tamil for a political party.

## You'll want to know: கூட்டணி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C375-KATCI375-02]; assesses=[] -->

**கூட்டணி** — *kūṭṭaṇi* — "an alliance; a coalition".

> இரண்டு கட்சிகளும் கூட்டணி அமைத்தன.
>
> *iraṇṭu kaṭcikaḷum kūṭṭaṇi amaittaṉa.*
>
> The two parties formed an alliance.

கூட்டணி அமை, to form an alliance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கூட்டணி** (*kūṭṭaṇi*) is native: *kūṭṭu*, "union, joining" (DEDR 1882), and *aṇi*, "array of an army, row" (DEDR 117): ranks drawn up together.
