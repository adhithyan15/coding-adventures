## You'll want to know: तड़पना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C458-TANAV458-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तड़पना** — *taṛapnā* — "to writhe; to be in anguish".

> दाँत के दर्द से अरुण पूरी रात तड़पा।
>
> *dā̃t ke dard se Aruṇ pūrī rāt taṛpā.*
>
> Arun writhed all night with toothache.

Past (he …): वह दर्द से तड़पा, *vah dard se taṛpā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तड़पना** (*taṛapnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *taṛapnā*, "to toss, roll about, be anxious", under *\*taḍapphaḍ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5634).
