## You'll want to know: o lagarto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C461-PEQUENOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o lagarto** — "the lizard".

> Um lagarto apanha sol numa pedra.
>
> *A lizard is sunbathing on a stone.*

A small one is *a lagartixa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lagarto* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *lacartus*, from Latin *lacertus* 'lizard' (EtymDB).
