## You'll want to know: ਸਿੱਟਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C304-GROW304-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿੱਟਾ** — *siṭṭā* — "an ear of grain".

> ਕਣਕ ਦਾ ਸਿੱਟਾ ਭਾਰਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *kaṇak dā siṭṭā bhārā hai.*
>
> The ear of wheat is heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿੱਟਾ** (*siṭṭā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*siṭṭa*, "ear of corn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *siṭṭā* under it (CDIAL 13396).
