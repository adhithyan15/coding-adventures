## You'll want to know: l'avvenimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C276-VICENDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'avvenimento** — "the event, the happening".

> Il concerto è stato l'avvenimento dell'estate.
>
> *The concert was the event of the summer.*

An important happening. Built on *avvenire*, ‘to take place’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avvenimento* is built on *avvenire* "to take place", from Latin *advenīre* "to come to" (EtymDB), with the noun ending *-mento*.
