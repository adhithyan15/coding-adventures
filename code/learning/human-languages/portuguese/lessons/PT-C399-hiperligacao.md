## You'll want to know: a hiperligação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C399-REDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a hiperligação** — "the web link, the hyperlink".

> Cliquei na hiperligação e abriu uma página nova.
>
> *I clicked on the link and a new page opened.*

European Portuguese, formal; in speech most people say *o link*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hiperligação* is built from Greek *hypér* "over, beyond" and *ligação* "connection", on *ligar*, inherited from Latin *ligāre* "to tie" (Lewis & Short); it translates English *hyperlink*.
