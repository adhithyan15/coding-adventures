## You'll want to know: scippare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C291-SCIPPO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**scippare** — "to snatch (a bag) from someone".

> Un ragazzo in moto le ha scippato la borsa.
>
> *A boy on a motorbike snatched her bag.*

*Scippare qualcosa a qualcuno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *scippare* is not settled; it is the verb behind *scippo* and is usually traced to southern dialects (EtymDB has no entry).
