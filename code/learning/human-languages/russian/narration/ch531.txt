Russian, chapter 531: Clutter and Clearing Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
беспорядок (besporyádok) — a mess, disorder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to hang out, then the Russian for to wash out.

You'll want to know: беспорядок.
беспорядок (besporyádok) — "a mess, disorder".
В комнате сына вечный беспорядок. V kómnate sýna véchnyy besporyádok. My son's room is permanently in a mess.
Masculine; genitive беспорядка.

The word, taken apart.
беспорядок (besporyádok) is built on без 'without' and порядок 'order', from the root of ряд 'a row'; EtymDB lists беспорядок (besporyádok) without a source.

кладовка (kladóvka) — "a storeroom".
Пылесос стоит в кладовке. Pylesós stoít v kladóvke. The vacuum cleaner is in the storeroom.
Feminine, colloquial for кладовая.

кладовка (kladóvka) is built on кладовая 'a storeroom', from the root of класть 'to put'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for junk, then the Russian for a storeroom.

You'll want to know: складывать.
складывать (skládyvat) — "to fold; to put together".
Папа аккуратно складывает рубашки. Pápa akkurátno skládyvayet rubáshki. Dad folds his shirts neatly.
Imperfective; perfective сложить. Also to add up numbers.

The word, taken apart.
складывать (skládyvat) is built on с- 'together' and the root of класть 'to put'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
