(**au fur et à mesure**.) In the long run? (**à la longue**.) Sooner or later? (**tôt ou tard**.) Long ago? (**jadis**.) Not long ago? (**naguère**.) Before? (**auparavant**.) Until then? (**jusqu'alors**.) Since then? (**depuis lors**.) The day before? (**la veille**.) The next day? (**le lendemain**.) Two days later? (**le surlendemain**.) Lately? (**dernièrement**.) Soon? (**prochainement**.) To occur? (**survenir**.) To unfold? (**dérouler**.) To take place? (**avoir lieu**.) To precede? (**précéder**.) To last? (**durer**.) To go by? (**écouler**.) To postpone? (**ajourner**.) To succeed? (**aboutir**.) The hitch? (**le contretemps**.) The deadline? (**l'échéance**.)
