Telugu, chapter 143: Red, To fly, To run, To sleep, To get up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎర్రటి (erraṭi) — red, reddish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for black, then the Telugu for white.

You'll want to know: ఎర్రటి.
ఎర్రటి — erraṭi — "red, reddish".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ఎర్రటి (erraṭi) mean? ("Red, reddish".) Say it once more.
