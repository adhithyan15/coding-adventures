## You'll want to know: उन्मूलयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C301-CAKRAVATA301-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्मूलयति** — *unmūlayati* — "uproots".

> चक्रवातः वृक्षान् उन्मूलयति।
>
> *cakravātaḥ vṛkṣān unmūlayati.*
>
> The whirlwind uproots trees.

Past (it …): वायुः वृक्षान् उन्मूलितवान्, *vāyuḥ vṛkṣān unmūlitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्मूलयति** (*unmūlayati*) is built from *ud*, "out", and *mūla*, "a root"; MW gives *unmūlayati* as to pull up by the roots (MW).
