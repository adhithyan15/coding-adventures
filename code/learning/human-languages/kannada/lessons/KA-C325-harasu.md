## You'll want to know: ಹರಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C325-VIVAHA325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹರಸು** — *harasu* — "to bless".

> ಹಿರಿಯರು ಹೊಸ ಜೋಡಿಯನ್ನು ಹರಸಿದರು.
>
> *hiriyaru hosa jōḍiyannu harasidaru.*
>
> The elders blessed the newly married couple.

Past (I …): ಹರಸಿದೆ, *haraside*.

The person blessed takes -ಅನ್ನು. A blessing or a vow is a ಹರಕೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹರಸು** (*harasu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3951 lists Kannada *parasu*, "to utter a benediction, to bless"; old *p-* became *h-*.
