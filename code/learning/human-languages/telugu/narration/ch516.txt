Telugu, chapter 516: Senses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చూపు (cūpu) — eyesight; a look.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the spine, then the Telugu for breath.

You'll want to know: చూపు.
చూపు — cūpu — "eyesight; a look".
వయసుతో తాతయ్య చూపు తగ్గింది. vayasutō tātayya cūpu taggindi. With age grandpa's eyesight has weakened.
Also a glance: ఒక చూపు (cūpu), one look. The noun of చూడు, to see.

The word, taken apart.
చూపు (cūpu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2735 lists Telugu cūcu, "to see", and cūpu, "sight, vision, look, glance".

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: చెవుడు.
చెవుడు — cevuḍu — "deafness".
ముసలితనంలో ఆయనకు కొంచెం చెవుడు వచ్చింది. musalitanaṁlō āyanaku koñceṁ cevuḍu vaccindi. In old age he became a little deaf.
Built on చెవి, ear. చెవిటివాడు, a deaf man.

The word, taken apart.
చెవుడు (cevuḍu) is built on the native చెవి, "ear" (DEDR 1977, with Tamil cevi), with a noun ending; DEDR does not list cevuḍu itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: నిద్రలేమి.
నిద్రలేమి — nidralēmi — "sleeplessness; insomnia".
పరీక్షల ముందు చాలామందికి నిద్రలేమి వస్తుంది. parīkṣala mundu cālāmandiki nidralēmi vastundi. Before exams many people cannot sleep.
నిద్ర, sleep, + లేమి, lack.

The word, taken apart.
నిద్రలేమి (nidralēmi) is built from నిద్ర, from Sanskrit nidrā, "sleep" (CDIAL 7200) and the Telugu noun లేమి, "lack", the noun of లేదు.
