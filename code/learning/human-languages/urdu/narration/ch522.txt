Urdu, chapter 522: Quick on Your Feet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پھرتی (phurtī) — agility; quickness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a winner, then the Urdu for a defeat.

You'll want to know: پھرتی.
پھرتی — phurtī — "agility; quickness".
اس کھلاڑی میں بڑی پھرتی ہے۔ is khilāṛī meṅ baṛī phurtī hai. This player is very quick on his feet.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
پھرتی (phurtī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phurtī, "activity", under Sanskrit sphurati, "darts, springs" (CDIAL 13849).

Lesson 2 of 5.
لچکنا (lachaknā) — to bend without breaking; to flex.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a defeat, then the Urdu for agility.

You'll want to know: لچکنا.
لچکنا — lachaknā — "to bend without breaking; to flex".
اس کھلاڑی کا جسم بہت لچکتا ہے۔ is khilāṛī kā jism bahut lachaktā hai. This player's body is very supple.
Past (it …): وہ شاخ ہوا میں لچکی, voh shākh hawā meṅ lachkī.

The word, taken apart.
لچکنا (lachaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lacaknā, "to be bent", under lacc, "bend, sway", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10907).

Lesson 3 of 5.
لڑھکانا (luṛhkānā) — to roll (something) along.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for agility, then the Urdu for to bend without breaking.

You'll want to know: لڑھکانا.
لڑھکانا — luṛhkānā — "to roll (something) along".
بچے نے گیند میری طرف لڑھکائی۔ bachche ne gend merī taraf luṛhkāī. The child rolled the ball towards me.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پتھر لڑھکایا, us ne patthar luṛhkāyā.

The word, taken apart.
لڑھکانا (luṛhkānā) is the causative of luṛhaknā, "to roll", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit luṭhati, "rolls, wallows" (CDIAL 11079).

Lesson 4 of 5.
دوڑانا (dauṛānā) — to make (someone) run.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to bend without breaking, then the Urdu for to roll (something) along.

You'll want to know: دوڑانا.
دوڑانا — dauṛānā — "to make (someone) run".
استاد نے لڑکوں کو میدان میں دوڑایا۔ ustād ne laṛkoṅ ko maidān meṅ dauṛāyā. The teacher made the boys run round the field.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گھوڑا دوڑایا, us ne ghoṛā dauṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
دوڑانا (dauṛānā) is the causative of dauṛnā, "to run", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit dravati, "runs" (CDIAL 6624).
