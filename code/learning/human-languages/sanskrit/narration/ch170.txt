Lesson 1 of 5.
कार्यम् (kāryam) — work, a task.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a dictionary, then the Sanskrit for writing.

You'll want to know: कार्यम्.
कार्यम् — kāryam — "work, a task".

The word, taken apart.
kārya is the gerundive of the root √kṛ "do, make" (CDIAL 3078): "to be done", and so "a task, work, business" (MW). It lives on as Hindi and Bengali kāj "work" (CDIAL 3078).

Lesson 2 of 5.
कर्म (karma) — an action, work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for writing, then the Sanskrit for work.

You'll want to know: कर्म.
कर्म — karma — "an action, work".

The word, taken apart.
karma is the nominative of karman "act, action, work", from the root √kṛ "do, make" (MW; CDIAL 2892). It wore down into Hindi kām "work" (CDIAL 2892), and it is the karma that English has borrowed.

Lesson 3 of 5.
उद्योगः (udyogaḥ) — an occupation, industry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for work, then the Sanskrit for an action.

You'll want to know: उद्योगः.
उद्योगः — udyogaḥ — "an occupation, industry".

The word, taken apart.
udyoga comes from ud-√yuj "exert oneself, set to work", the root √yuj "yoke, join" with ud "up" (MW; CDIAL 2055): "exertion, persevering effort", and so "an occupation, industry".

Lesson 4 of 5.
वृत्तिः (vṛttiḥ) — a livelihood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an action, then the Sanskrit for an occupation.

You'll want to know: वृत्तिः.
वृत्तिः — vṛttiḥ — "a livelihood".

The word, taken apart.
vṛtti comes from the root √vṛt "turn, proceed, live" (CDIAL 12070): "the way one goes on", a mode of life, a livelihood. MW sets √vṛt beside Latin vertere "turn" and the English -ward of toward.
