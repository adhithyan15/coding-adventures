## You'll want to know: منطق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C334-BAHAS334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**منطق** — *mantiq* — "logic".

> اس کی بات میں کوئی منطق نہیں ہے۔
>
> *us kī bāt meṅ koī mantiq nahīṅ hai.*
>
> There is no logic in what he says.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منطق** (*mantiq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *manṭiq*, "speech; logic".
