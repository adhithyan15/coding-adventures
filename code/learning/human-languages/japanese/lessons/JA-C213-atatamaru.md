## You'll want to know: あたたまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C213-SKY213-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あたたまる** — *atatamaru* — "to get warm; to warm up (by itself)".

> **ごごは へやが あたたまります。** — *gogo wa heya ga atatamarimasu* — "The room gets warm in the afternoon."

Polite **あたたまります**, *atatamarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あたたまる** (*atatamaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed and built on the same stem as *atatakai*, "warm"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
