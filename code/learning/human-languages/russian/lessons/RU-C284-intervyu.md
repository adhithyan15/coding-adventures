## You'll want to know: интервью
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C284-INTERVYU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**интервью** (*intervyú*) — "an interview".

> Певица дала интервью газете.
>
> *Pevítsa dalá intervyú gazéte.*
>
> The singer gave an interview to a newspaper.

Neuter and indeclinable: *в интервью*. *Брать интервью у* + genitive — to interview someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*интервью* was borrowed from English *interview* (EtymDB), which comes from French *entrevue* "a meeting", from the reflexive verb *entrevoir* "to see each other".
