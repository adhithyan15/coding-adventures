## You'll want to know: ずれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C352-KOBORERU352-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ずれる** — *zureru* — "to slip out of place".

> **めがねが ずれたら、なおして ください。** — *megane ga zuretara, naoshite kudasai* — "If your glasses slip, push them back."

Polite **ずれます**, *zuremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **めがねが ずれました。**, *megane ga zuremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ずれる** (*zureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled; JMdict gives it no kanji spelling (JMdict).
