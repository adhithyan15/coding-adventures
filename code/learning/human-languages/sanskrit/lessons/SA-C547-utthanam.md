## You'll want to know: उत्थानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C547-UTTHANA547-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्थानम्** — *utthānam* — "a rise".

> यदि देशस्य उत्थानं भवति, तर्हि जनाः प्रसन्नाः भविष्यन्ति।
>
> *yadi deśasya utthānaṃ bhavati, tarhi janāḥ prasannāḥ bhaviṣyanti.*
>
> If the country rises, the people will be glad.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्थानम्** (*utthānam*) is built on *ud*, "up", and the root √sthā, "to stand", which also gives *uttiṣṭhati*; MW gives *utthāna* as standing up, rising (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
