Malayalam, chapter 154: Elbow, Knee, Tongue, Skin, Forehead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കൈമുട്ട് (kaimuṭṭŭ) — an elbow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a mosquito, then the Malayalam for a shoulder.

You'll want to know: കൈമുട്ട്.
കൈമുട്ട് — kaimuṭṭŭ — "an elbow".

The word, taken apart.
കൈമുട്ട് (kaimuṭṭŭ) is native Dravidian, "the arm's joint": kai, "hand, arm" (DEDR 2023), and muṭṭŭ, "joint, knee" (DEDR 4935, with Tamil muṭṭu).
