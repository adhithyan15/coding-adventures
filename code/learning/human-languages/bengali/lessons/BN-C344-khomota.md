## You'll want to know: ক্ষমতা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C344-RASHTRO344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষমতা** — *khômôtā* — "power; capacity".

> নির্বাচনের পরে নতুন দল ক্ষমতায় এল।
>
> *nirbāchôner pôre nôtun dôl khômôtāy elô.*
>
> After the election a new party came to power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষমতা** (*khômôtā*) is Sanskrit *kṣamatā*, ability or fitness (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for any capacity and for political power: ক্ষমতায় আসা, to come to power.
