## You'll want to know: गाड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C69-GOING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गाड़ी** (*gāṛī*) — "a vehicle".

From Sanskrit गर्त (*garta*), a cart, through the Prakrits. It began as the thing
oxen pulled and it has taken in everything since: a car is a गाड़ी, a train is a
गाड़ी, a bullock cart is a गाड़ी.

That width is the reason to learn it first. **बस** you already own, and बस names
one thing only. गाड़ी names whatever is carrying you, so it is the word that
never leaves you stuck — point at anything with wheels and गाड़ी is right.

The ड़ in the middle is the flapped retroflex, made by flicking the tongue down
off the roof of the mouth. It is ड with the nuqta underneath.
