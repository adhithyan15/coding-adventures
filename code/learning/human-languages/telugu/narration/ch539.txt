Telugu, chapter 539: Songs and Verse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పల్లవి (pallavi) — a refrain; a chorus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a mridangam drum, then the Telugu for a reed pipe.

You'll want to know: పల్లవి.
పల్లవి — pallavi — "a refrain; a chorus".
ఆ పాట పల్లవి అందరికీ గుర్తుంది. ā pāṭa pallavi andarikī gurtundi. Everyone remembers the refrain of that song.
The opening line a song keeps returning to.

The word, taken apart.
పల్లవి (pallavi) is usually traced to Sanskrit pallava, "a sprout, a twig, a blossom" (CDIAL 7969).

Lesson 2 of 5.
చరణం (caraṇaṁ) — a stanza; a verse of a song.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a reed pipe, then the Telugu for a refrain.

You'll want to know: చరణం.
చరణం — caraṇaṁ — "a stanza; a verse of a song".
రెండో చరణం నాకు గుర్తులేదు. reṇḍō caraṇaṁ nāku gurtulēdu. I do not remember the second verse.
The verses that come between the refrains.

The word, taken apart.
చరణం (caraṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit caraṇa, "a foot; a line of a stanza" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
పద్యం (padyaṁ) — a metrical verse; a poem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a refrain, then the Telugu for a stanza.

You'll want to know: పద్యం.
పద్యం — padyaṁ — "a metrical verse; a poem".
తాతయ్య చాలా పద్యాలు నోటికి చెబుతారు. tātayya cālā padyālu nōṭiki cebutāru. Grandpa can recite many verses by heart.
Plural: పద్యాలు, padyālu.
Classical Telugu verse in a fixed metre; పాట is a song.

The word, taken apart.
పద్యం (padyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit padya, "relating to a foot; made of the parts of verses" (Monier-Williams).
