## You'll want to know: かのじょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C242-FRIENDS242-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かのじょ** — *kanojo* — "she; a girlfriend".

> **かのじょは いま きょうしつに いません。** — *kanojo wa ima kyōshitsu ni imasen* — "She is not in the classroom now."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かのじょ** (*kanojo*) is a mixed word: native *kano*, "that", joined to Sino-Japanese *jo*, "woman" (Middle Chinese *nrjoX*), borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
