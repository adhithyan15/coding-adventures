## You'll want to know: بہو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C488-INLAWS488-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بہو** — *bahū* — "a daughter-in-law".

> ہماری بہو ڈاکٹر ہے۔
>
> *hamārī bahū ḍākṭar hai.*
>
> Our daughter-in-law is a doctor.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بہو** (*bahū*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vadhū*, "bride, young wife", already "son's wife" in later texts (CDIAL 11250).
