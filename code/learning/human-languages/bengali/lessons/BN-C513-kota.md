## You'll want to know: কোটা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C513-KOTA513-01]; assesses=[] -->

**কোটা** — *koṭā* — "to chop (vegetables or fish)".

> আমি রাতের জন্য সবজি কুটলাম।
>
> *Āmi rāter jônyô sôbji kuṭlām.*
>
> I chopped vegetables for dinner.

Past (he/she …): তিনি মাছ কুটলেন, *tini māchh kuṭlen*.

The stem vowel rises in the past: কুটলাম, I chopped.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কোটা** (*koṭā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *kuṭā*, "to cut up for cooking", and *koṭā* under Sanskrit *kuṭṭayati*, "crushes, pounds" (CDIAL 3241).
