## You'll want to know: yāoqǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C173-INVITE173-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yāoqǐng* — "to invite; an invitation".

> *tā yāoqǐng wǒmen qù tā jiā* — He's invited us to his place.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yāoqǐng* is built from *yāo* and 请 'to ask', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jiew* and *tshjengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'to stop someone on the way'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
