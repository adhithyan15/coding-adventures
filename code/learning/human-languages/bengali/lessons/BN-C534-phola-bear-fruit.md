## You'll want to know: ফলা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C534-BAJI534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ফলা** — *phôlā* — "to come true; to bear fruit".

> জ্যোতিষীর কথা ফলল।
>
> *Jyotishīr kôthā phôllô.*
>
> The astrologer's words came true.

Past (he/she …): সেই গাছে আম ফলল, *sei gāchhe ām phôllô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফলা** (*phôlā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *phalā*, "to bear fruit", under Sanskrit *phalati* (CDIAL 9057); a prediction that comes true has borne fruit.
