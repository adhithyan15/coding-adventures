Marathi, chapter 356: Democracy and Majorities.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लोकशाही (lokśāhī) — democracy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for canvassing, then the Marathi for to be elected.

You'll want to know: लोकशाही.
लोकशाही — lokśāhī — "democracy".
लोकशाहीत जनता सरकार निवडते. lokśāhīt jantā sarkār nivaḍte. In a democracy, the people choose the government.
Feminine.
लोक + शाही, people + rule; the opposite is हुकूमशाही, dictatorship.

The word, taken apart.
लोकशाही (lokśāhī) is a Marathi compound built from लोक, "people", from Sanskrit loka (Monier-Williams), and शाही, "rule", a loan from Persian shāhī, "kingship".

Lesson 5 of 5.
कौल देणे (kaul deṇe) — to give a mandate; to give a verdict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a minority, then the Marathi for public opinion.

You'll want to know: कौल देणे.
कौल देणे — kaul deṇe — "to give a mandate; to give a verdict".
लोकांनी तरुण उमेदवाराला कौल दिला. lokānnī taruṇ umedvārālā kaul dilā. The people gave their mandate to the young candidate.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कौल दिला, tyāne kaul dilā.
The one chosen takes -ला: उमेदवाराला कौल देणे (kaul deṇe).

The word, taken apart.
कौल देणे (kaul deṇe) joins कौल, "a verdict, a word given", a loan usually traced to Arabic qaul, "saying, word", through Persian, to देणे, "to give".
