Punjabi, chapter 15: The First Form Line: Name.
8 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
ਅ — a vowel that can stand first.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Point to the first known piece in ਨਮਸਤੇ: ਨ. A consonant can carry an unwritten a. Today a vowel stands by itself at the beginning of a word.

Script.
ਅ (a).
Read it as short a. It is one complete letter, not a mark attached to a consonant. That is why it can begin the practice name in the next lesson.

Writing — observe and trace.
Keep ਅ visible. Follow one printed model with your finger, then trace it once. Compare only three things: the top line, the upright, and the lower turn.

Wrap-up recall.
Cover the model for two seconds. Point to ਅ when it appears beside ਨ.
[question — say your answer, then pause 8 seconds]
Which Gurmukhi letter is the independent short a?


Lesson 2 of 8.
ਅਮਨ (Aman) — one new piece, two old pieces.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — assemble the practice name until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Read the three separate pieces: ਅ, ਮ, ਨ.

Script — assemble the practice name.
ਅ + ਮ + ਨ becomes ਅਮਨ (Aman).
This book uses ਅਮਨ (Aman) as one closed-bank practice identity. It is not asking you to spell your own name yet.

Writing — guided copy.
Keep ਅਮਨ (Aman) visible. Copy it once, saying each piece as your hand reaches it. Compare left to right. Repair only the first differing piece.

Wrap-up recall.
Point to the only piece that was new today: ਅ.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Read the practice name ਅਮਨ (Aman) and type the same Gurmukhi name.


Lesson 3 of 8.
ਮਨਨ (Manan) — two familiar pieces, one repeated.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — assemble the second practice name until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Write ਮ once and ਨ once. No new letter appears today.

Script — assemble the second practice name.
ਮ + ਨ + ਨ becomes ਮਨਨ (Manan).
This is the second and final value in the first name-field bank. Notice the two adjacent ਨ letters; neither may disappear.

Writing — guided copy.
Keep ਮਨਨ (Manan) visible. Copy it once. Tap three positions after writing: ਮ | ਨ | ਨ. If there are only two positions, restore the final ਨ.

Wrap-up recall.
Contrast once: ਅਮਨ (Aman) begins with ਅ; ਮਨਨ (Manan) begins with ਮ.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Read the practice name ਮਨਨ (Manan) and type the same three-letter Gurmukhi name.


Lesson 4 of 8.
ਨਾਂ (nāṁ) — the first form label.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — read the label until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Build the known pieces aloud: ਨ + ਾ + ਂ.

Script — read the label.
ਨਾਂ (nāṁ): __________ (name field).
On a form, ਨਾਂ (nāṁ) asks for a name. The colon belongs to the form; it is not a Gurmukhi letter. Point to the label, then point to the empty answer line.

Writing — one label.
Copy ਨਾਂ (nāṁ) once. Check the base ਨ, then the long-vowel mark, then the nasal dot. Do not add a name value yet.

Wrap-up recall.
Find ਨਾਂ (nāṁ) among ਹਾਂ, ਨਾਂ (nāṁ), ਨਹੀਂ without writing the other two words.
[question — say your answer, then pause 8 seconds]
A form shows ਨਾਂ (nāṁ). What information belongs on that line?


Lesson 5 of 8.
Two people, two already-known values — select one known name value from a nonverbal identity cue.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — attach one nonverbal cue to each person until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Read both names. Point to the first differing letter:
ਅਮਨ (Aman), ਮਨਨ (Manan).

Script — attach one nonverbal cue to each person.
For this closed practice bank only:
☀ becomes ਅਮਨ (Aman).
★ becomes ਮਨਨ (Manan).
Look for five seconds. Cover the Gurmukhi names. Point to the sun and recall its name; point to the star and recall its name. Uncover and compare.

Guided Practice — value selection.
With the bank visible, choose but do not copy: which value belongs to ★? Which belongs to ☀? The skill today is selecting the requested known value.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after the two-way contrast. No third name enters this field bank.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
In this practice bank, select the Gurmukhi name paired with ★.


Lesson 6 of 8.
Fill one field with the bank visible — complete one name field with a visible two-value bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read the label ਨਾਂ (nāṁ). Recall the visible bank: ☀ ਅਮਨ (Aman), ★ ਮਨਨ (Manan).

Guided Practice — supported entry.
Keep the bank visible. The sun card requests its known value:
☀ &nbsp;&nbsp; ਨਾਂ (nāṁ): __________ (name field).
Select ਅਮਨ (Aman) and copy it on the line. Read the completed field from label to value. This is supported entry, not independent writing evidence.

Writing — separate spelling check.
Check only spelling: ਅ | ਮ | ਨ. Do not check spacing or selection in this pass. If one piece differs, repair that one piece.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one supported field.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
With the two-value bank visible, complete the name field for ☀.


Lesson 7 of 8.
Hide the bank, then fill one line — complete one requested name field after the answer bank is hidden.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Look once: ☀ ਅਮਨ (Aman), ★ ਮਨਨ (Manan). Cover the complete bank and wait ten seconds.

Writing — delayed requested entry.
Keep the bank hidden. Complete only this field:
★ &nbsp;&nbsp; ਨਾਂ (nāṁ): __________ (name field).
The requested value is chosen from memory; there is no copyable answer beside the line. Reveal the bank only after writing.

Guided Practice — separate repair passes.
Compare in order: (1) selected person, (2) three written positions, (3) any extra space inside the name. Repair the first differing dimension and stop.

Wrap-up recall.
This is one delayed choice, not a six-field form.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
After hiding the bank for ten seconds, complete ਨਾਂ (nāṁ) for ★. Do not reveal the bank until the attempt is finished.


Lesson 8 of 8.
One no-model name field — choose and write one known name from a nonverbal cue with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Close the previous lesson. Keep only the two learned identity symbols in memory. There is no value bank, support-language name, or romanized answer below.

Writing — independent controlled choice.
Complete the requested field:
☀ &nbsp;&nbsp; ਨਾਂ (nāṁ): __________ (name field).
Choose the known value attached to the sun and write it from memory. Do not open an earlier lesson until the attempt is complete.

Guided Practice — separate repair passes.
After writing, compare in four bounded passes: requested person, spelling, spacing, then label/value placement. Repair only the first differing dimension.

Wrap-up recall.
You have completed one field pattern. Five more field families remain before the published six-field form can be attempted.
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
With no bank, romanization, or copyable answer, complete the name field for ☀.
