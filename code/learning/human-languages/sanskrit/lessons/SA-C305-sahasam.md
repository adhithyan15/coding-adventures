## You'll want to know: साहसम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C305-UDDHARANA305-03]; assesses=[] -->

**साहसम्** — *sāhasam* — "daring; boldness".

> तस्य साहसं सर्वे प्रशंसन्ति।
>
> *tasya sāhasaṃ sarve praśaṃsanti.*
>
> Everyone praises his daring.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साहसम्** (*sāhasam*) is built on *sahas*, "might, force"; MW gives *sāhasa* as boldness, daring, rashness, and also as violence (MW).
