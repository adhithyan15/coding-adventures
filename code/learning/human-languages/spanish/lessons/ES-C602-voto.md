## You'll want to know: el voto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C602-VOTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el voto** — "the vote".

> Cada voto es importante.
>
> *Every vote matters.*

*Votar* — to vote.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*voto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *votum* 'a vow, a wish', from *vovere* 'to vow' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
