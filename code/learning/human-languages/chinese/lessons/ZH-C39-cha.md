## You'll want to know: chá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C39-DRINK39-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chá* — "tea".

> *nǐ hē chá ma* — Would you like some tea?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chá* is inherited from Middle Chinese *drae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as leaves gathered in spring and made into a drink.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
