# ڈ (ḍāl) — the letter that opens ڈر

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01, UR-LEX-C272-DAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ث once, and name it. (*se*.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ڈر** (*ḍar*), "fear". This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C272-DDAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈ** — *ḍāl*, the sound *ḍ*, made with the tongue curled back, as for **ٹ** (*ṭe*) and **ڑ** (*ṛe*).

It opens **ڈر** (*ḍar*). You already write **د**: **ڈ** is that shape wearing the small mark that **ٹ** and **ڑ** wear above them. Like **د**, it does not join the letter after it.

## Writing: ڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C272-DDAL-01] -->

Put your pen on ڈ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C271-SE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C272-DDAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **ڈر** and point at ڈ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ڈ three times, saying *ḍ* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: **ڈر** once, with the small mark above its first letter]
- [YOU READ: **ڈر** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C272-DDAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ڈ? (**ḍāl**, *ḍ*.) What tells it from د? (The small mark that ٹ wears.) Which word did it come from? (**ڈر**, *ḍar*, "fear".)
