## You'll want to know: la puntata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C528-SERIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la puntata** — "instalment, episode (of a series)".

> Stasera c'è l'ultima puntata.
>
> *Tonight is the last episode.*

Built on *punto*; *a puntate* — in instalments.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*puntata* is built on *punto* "point", inherited from Latin *punctum* (Lewis & Short), from *pungere* "to prick".
