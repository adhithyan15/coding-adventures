## You'll want to know: llenar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C496-HOGAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**llenar** — "to fill".

> Llena la botella de agua.
>
> *Fill the bottle with water.*

The verb behind *lleno*, full.
