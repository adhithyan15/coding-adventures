Punjabi, chapter 243: Mixing and Tasting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਾਉਣਾ (pāuṇā) — to pour in; to put in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to sprinkle, then the Punjabi for to get burnt.

You'll want to know: ਪਾਉਣਾ.
ਪਾਉਣਾ — pāuṇā — "to pour in; to put in".
ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੁੱਧ ਪਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ chāh vich dudh pāundī hāṁ. I pour milk into the tea.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪਾਇਆ, maiṁ dudh pāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਾਉਣਾ (pāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit pātayati, "throws, pours out"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pāuṇā, "to pour" (CDIAL 8053).
