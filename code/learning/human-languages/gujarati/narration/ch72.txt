Gujarati, chapter 72: Big and Small, Long and Short.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મોટું (moṭũ) — big.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for beautiful, then the Gujarati for bad.

You'll want to know: મોટું.
મોટું (moṭũ) — "big".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists મોટું (moṭũ) under mōṭṭa, one member of a large group of reconstructed forms it heads muṭṭa, "defective" (CDIAL 10187).

What you've built.
One more word for big and small, long and short.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: moṭũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: moṭũ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say moṭũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for beautiful, then the Gujarati for bad, then say moṭũ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does મોટું (moṭũ) mean? ("Big".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
નાનું (nānũ) — small.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for bad, then the Gujarati for big.

You'll want to know: નાનું.
નાનું (nānũ) — "small".

The word, taken apart.
નાનું (nānũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit ślakṣṇa, "slippery, smooth, soft, tender", which the old word-lists also gloss "thin, small" (CDIAL 12732).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભારે (bhāre) — heavy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for long, then the Gujarati for short.

You'll want to know: ભારે.
ભારે (bhāre) — "heavy".

The word, taken apart.
ભારે (bhāre) is built on Gujarati bhār, "load, weight", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit bhāra, "burden, weight" (CDIAL 9459). It lists Gujarati bhārī, "heavy", under bhārika, "forming a load, heavy" (CDIAL 9465).
