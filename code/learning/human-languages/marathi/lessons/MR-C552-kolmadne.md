# कोलमडणे (kolmaḍṇe) — to break down; to collapse

## You'll want to know: कोलमडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C552-BIGHADNE552-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कोलमडणे** — *kolmaḍṇe* — "to break down; to collapse".

> वडील आजारी पडले आणि घराचं सगळं गणित कोलमडलं.
>
> *vaḍīl ājārī paḍle āṇi gharācaṁ sagḷaṁ gaṇit kolmaḍlaṁ.*
>
> Father fell ill and the family's whole budget collapsed.

Past (he …): तो कोलमडला, *to kolmaḍlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोलमडणे** (*kolmaḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb for a person or a plan that gives way; its origin is not settled.
