# సాహిత్యం (sāhityaṁ) — literary works; literature

## You'll want to know: సాహిత్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C540-SAHITYAM540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సాహిత్యం** — *sāhityaṁ* — "literary works; literature".

> తెలుగు సాహిత్యం చాలా పాతది.
>
> *telugu sāhityaṁ cālā pātadi.*
>
> Telugu literature is very old.

The everyday word; వాఙ్మయం is more learned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సాహిత్యం** (*sāhityaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sāhitya*, "association, union; literary composition" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
