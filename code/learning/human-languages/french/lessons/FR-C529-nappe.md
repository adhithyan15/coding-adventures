## You'll want to know: la nappe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C529-TABLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la nappe** — "tablecloth".

> Pour la fête, ma mère sort la belle nappe blanche.
>
> *For the party my mother gets out the nice white tablecloth.*

Also a sheet of water or fog: *une nappe de brouillard*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la nappe* is inherited from Latin *mappa* "a napkin, a cloth" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with its first sound changed.
