## You'll want to know: عنوان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C528-BOOKS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عنوان** (*'onvân*) — "a title; a heading".

> عنوان این کتاب خیلی جالب است.
>
> *'onvân-e in ketâb kheyli jâleb ast.*
>
> The title of this book is very interesting.

Plural *'anâvin* (Arabic). *be 'onvân-e* — as, in the role of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'onvân* was borrowed from Arabic *ʿunwān* (EtymDB), a word for the title or address on a letter.
