Lesson 1 of 6.
kàndài — to regard (in a certain way).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the other party, then the Mandarin for all of one mind.

You'll want to know: kàndài.
kàndài — "to regard (in a certain way)".
nǐ zěnme kàndài zhège wèntí — How do you view this question?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kàndài is built from 看 and dài, both inherited from Middle Chinese, khan and dojX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to view' and the second 'to wait'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
想法 (xiǎngfǎ) — idea; way of thinking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for all of one mind, then the Mandarin for to regard.

You'll want to know: 想法.
想法 — xiǎngfǎ — "idea; way of thinking".
我觉得你的想法很好 wǒ juéde nǐ de xiǎngfǎ hěn hǎo — I think your idea is a good one.
The new character, in pieces: 法 (fǎ) — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left the water piece, three strokes, as on the left of 汉; on the right 去 qù, five strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.
Two third tones meet in this word: xiǎng is said with a rising tone before fǎ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
想法 (xiǎngfǎ) is built from 想 'to think' and 法 (fǎ) 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sjangX and pjop (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to think' and the second 'a rule' and 'a norm'.

Script — the water piece, then qù.
法 (fǎ).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, the water piece, three strokes, as on the left of 汉: a dot, a second dot under it, then a stroke that rises from the bottom left.
The right piece, 去, five strokes: a short horizontal, a vertical down through it, a long horizontal, then a stroke that falls left and turns right, and a dot.
3 + 5 = eight.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 法 (fǎ), all eight, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many pieces in 法 (fǎ)? (Two.) Which character you know has the same left piece? (汉, or 洗.) What is the right piece read? (qù.)

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to regard, then the Mandarin for a way of thinking.

You'll want to know: 说法.
说法 — shuōfa — "version of events; way of putting it".
我觉得这个说法不对 wǒ juéde zhège shuōfa bú duì — I don't think that's the right way to put it.

The word, taken apart.
说法 (shuōfa) is built from 说 'to say' and 法 (fǎ) 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sywet and pjop (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tell' and the second 'a rule'. The second is said light here.

Lesson 5 of 6.
做法 (zuòfǎ) — the way something is done; method.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a way of thinking, then the Mandarin for one version of events.

You'll want to know: 做法.
做法 — zuòfǎ — "the way something is done; method".
我觉得这个做法不好 wǒ juéde zhège zuòfǎ bù hǎo — I don't think that's a good way to go about it.

The word, taken apart.
做法 (zuòfǎ) is built from 做 'to do' and 法 (fǎ) 'a rule'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese pjop (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a rule'. No dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.

zhǔzhāng is built from zhǔ and zhāng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsyuX and trjang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be in charge' and the second 'to spread out'. How the pair came to mean arguing for something is not settled.
