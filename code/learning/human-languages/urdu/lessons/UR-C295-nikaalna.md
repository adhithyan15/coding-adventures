## You'll want to know: نکالنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C295-PHANSA295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نکالنا** — *nikālnā* — "to take out; to pull out".

> لوگوں نے انہیں باہر نکالا۔
>
> *logoṅ ne unheṅ bāhar nikālā.*
>
> People pulled them out.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو نکالا, *us ne bachche ko nikālā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *nikālnā*, "to drive out, to take out", under Sanskrit *niṣkālayati* (CDIAL 7484). It is the partner of **نکلنا**: a thing **نکلنا** by itself, and you **نکالنا** it.
