## You'll want to know: вмятина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C410-STOLKNOVENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**вмятина** (*vmyátina*) — "a dent".

> На двери машины теперь большая вмятина.
>
> *Na dverí mashíny tepér bolsháya vmyátina.*
>
> There is now a big dent in the car door.

Feminine. Built on *вмять* — to press in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вмятина* is built on *вмять* 'to press in', from *в-* 'into' and *мять* 'to crumple, to knead' (EtymDB lists *мять* with that sense).
