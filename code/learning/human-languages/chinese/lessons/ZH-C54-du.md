## You'll want to know: dú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C54-LEARN54-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dú* — "to read aloud; to study".

> *wǒ xǐhuan dú shū* — I love reading.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dú* is inherited from Middle Chinese *duwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to recite'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
