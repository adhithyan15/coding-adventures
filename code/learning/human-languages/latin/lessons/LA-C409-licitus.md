## You'll want to know: licitus, licita, licitum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C409-EDICTUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**licitus, licita, licitum** — "allowed, lawful".

> Quod nōn licitum est, nōn faciō.
>
> *What is not allowed, I do not do.*

*licitus, licita, licitum*, the participle of *licet*, it is allowed. Its opposite is *illicitus*. English *licit* and *illicit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*licitus* is the participle of *licet* "it is allowed" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *licit* and *illicit* come from it.
