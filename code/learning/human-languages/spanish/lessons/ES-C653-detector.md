## You'll want to know: el detector
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C653-EMBARQUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el detector** — "the detector; the metal detector".

> Pasa por el detector sin el cinturón.
>
> *Go through the detector without your belt.*

*El detector de metales* — the metal detector.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*detector* came from English *detector*, a word built on Latin *dētegere* "to uncover", from *tegere* "to cover".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
