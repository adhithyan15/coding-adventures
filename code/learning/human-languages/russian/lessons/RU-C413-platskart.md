## You'll want to know: плацкарт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C413-PROYEZD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**плацкарт** (*platskárt*) — "an open sleeping car (on a train)".

> Студенты ехали на море в плацкарте.
>
> *Studénty yékhali na móre v platskárte.*
>
> The students travelled to the seaside in an open sleeping car.

Masculine; colloquial for *плацкартный вагон*, a cheap carriage of open bunks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*плацкарт* is usually traced to German *Platzkarte* 'a seat reservation', built from *Platz* 'place' and *Karte* 'card'. EtymDB lists the German word but not the Russian, so the route is hedged.
