## You'll want to know: la prua
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C423-TRAGHETTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la prua** — "the bow (of a ship), the prow".

> Dalla prua si vede il porto.
>
> *The harbour is in sight from the bow.*

*A prua* — at the front of the ship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prua* is inherited from Latin *prōra* 'prow' (EtymDB), from Greek *prōira*, probably through a dialect of the coast.
