## You'll want to know: castigar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C623-SANCION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**castigar** — "to punish".

> Lo castigaron sin salir.
>
> *They grounded him.*

*Castigar sin salir* — to keep a child in. Spelling: *castigué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*castigar* is inherited from Latin *castigare* 'to correct, to chasten', built on *castus* 'pure' (EtymDB).
