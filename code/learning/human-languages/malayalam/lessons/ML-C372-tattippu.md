# തട്ടിപ്പ് (taṭṭippŭ) — a fraud; a scam

## You'll want to know: തട്ടിപ്പ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C372-THATTIPPU372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**തട്ടിപ്പ്** — *taṭṭippŭ* — "a fraud; a scam".

> ഫോണിലൂടെയുള്ള തട്ടിപ്പ് ഇപ്പോൾ കൂടുതലാണ്.
>
> *phōṇilūṭeyuḷḷa taṭṭippŭ ippōḷ kūṭutalāṇŭ.*
>
> Fraud over the phone is more common now.

A scam takes your money by a trick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തട്ടിപ്പ്** (*taṭṭippŭ*) is native Dravidian, a noun built on തട്ടുക (*taṭṭuka*), "to knock, to strike" (DEDR 3039), which in everyday speech also means to cheat someone of money.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
