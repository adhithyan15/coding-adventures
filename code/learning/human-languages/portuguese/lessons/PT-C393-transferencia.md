## You'll want to know: a transferência
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C393-BANCO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a transferência** — "the transfer".

> A transferência demora um dia.
>
> *The transfer takes a day.*

*Transferência bancária* — bank transfer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transferência* is built on *transferir* "to transfer", from Latin *trānsferre* "to carry across", on *ferre* "to carry" (Lewis & Short).
