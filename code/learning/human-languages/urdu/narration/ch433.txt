Urdu, chapter 433: Mounds, Heaps and Caves.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ٹیلا (ṭīlā) — a mound; a dune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the glow of sunset, then the Urdu for daylight.

You'll want to know: ٹیلا.
ٹیلا — ṭīlā — "a mound; a dune".
ریگستان میں ریت کے ٹیلے ہیں۔ registān meṅ ret ke ṭīle haiṅ. There are sand dunes in the desert.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ٹیلا (ṭīlā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭīlā, "a hillock, a mound", under ṭalla and its variant ṭilla, "a heap" (CDIAL 5451).
