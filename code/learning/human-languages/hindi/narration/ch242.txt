Hindi, chapter 242: Ordinary, Special, Famous, Useless, Successful.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
साधारण (sādhāraṇ) — ordinary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for valuable, then the Hindi for unusual.

You'll want to know: साधारण.
साधारण — sādhāraṇ — "ordinary".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sādhāraṇ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sādhāraṇ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sādhāraṇ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for valuable, then the Hindi for unusual, then say sādhāraṇ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does साधारण (sādhāraṇ) mean? ("Ordinary".) Say it once more.
