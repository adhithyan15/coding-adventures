## You'll want to know: सबूत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C271-SAHI271-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सबूत** — *sabūt* — "proof".

> सबूत कठै है?
>
> *sabūt kaṭhai hai?*
>
> Where's the proof?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सबूत** (*sabūt*) is usually traced, through Persian *subūt*, to Arabic *thubūt*, "firmness; proof".
