## You'll want to know: résister
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C326-LIBERATION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**résister** — "to resist, to hold out".

> Le petit village a résisté pendant des mois.
>
> *The little village held out for months.*

*Résister à* — to resist: *résister à l'ennemi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*résister* is a learned borrowing from Latin *resistere* "to stand back, to withstand" (EtymDB), built on *sistere*, from *stāre* "to stand".
