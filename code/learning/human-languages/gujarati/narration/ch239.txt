Gujarati, chapter 239: Down by the pond.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
દેડકો (deḍko) — frog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ant, then the Gujarati for to hang.

You'll want to know: દેડકો.
દેડકો — deḍko — "frog".
રાત્રે તળાવમાં દેડકો બોલ્યો. — rātre taḷāvmā̃ deḍko bolyo — "A frog croaked in the pond at night."

The word, taken apart.
દેડકો (deḍko) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dardura, "frog" (CDIAL 6198).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ડૂબવું (ḍūbvũ) — to sink, to drown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for frog, then the Gujarati for mud.

You'll want to know: ડૂબવું.
ડૂબવું — ḍūbvũ — "to sink, to drown".
મારો બૂટ કાદવમાં ડૂબ્યો. — māro būṭ kādavmā̃ ḍūbyo — "My shoe sank into the mud."

The word, taken apart.
ડૂબવું (ḍūbvũ) comes from ḍubb, "sink", a reconstructed form that CDIAL reads as a reshuffled Middle Indo-Aryan buḍḍaï; it glosses the Gujarati verb "to sink" (CDIAL 5561).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for mud, then the Gujarati for to sink.

You'll want to know: ડંખવું.
ડંખવું — ḍankhvũ — "to sting".
મને મધમાખી ડંખી. — mane madhmākhī ḍankhī — "A bee stung me."

The word, taken apart.
ડંખવું (ḍankhvũ) is built on ḍankh, "a sting". It is usually linked to Sanskrit daṁśa, "stinging insect", where CDIAL lists Gujarati ḍā̃s, "gadfly" (CDIAL 6110), but CDIAL does not list ḍankh, so the link is not settled.
