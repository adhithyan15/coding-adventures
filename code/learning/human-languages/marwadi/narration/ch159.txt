Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 159: Went Home.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
घरै (gharai) — at home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say here, there and on top: अठै, बठै, माथै. Then a house: घर.

Grammar Lens: घर with -ै.
घर with the -ै of अठै, बठै and माथै is घरै (gharai): at home, or home as the place you go to.
हूं घरै हूं। hū̃ gharai hū̃. — I am at home.
वो रोज आथण घरै आवै है। vo roj āthaṇ gharai āvai hai. — He comes home every evening.
Old Marwari shows a place ending like it: āgai, "before", beside āgo, "front" (CDIAL 68).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: मां घरै है। — mā̃ gharai hai. — Mother is at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: आप रोज घरै जावो हो? — āp roj gharai jāvo ho? — Do you go home every day?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हां सा, हूं रोज घरै जाऊं हूं। — hā̃ sā, hū̃ roj gharai jāū̃ hū̃. — Yes, I go home every day.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
At home? (घरै (gharai).) Here? (अठै.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say that you are at home.


Lesson 2 of 3.
गयो, गई, गया — went.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I go to the market every day. Then yesterday, काल, and a boy, a girl.

Grammar Lens: one word for the past.
गयो is the past of जाणो, all on its own: no हूं and no है after it.
हूं काल बाजार गयो। hū̃ kāl bājār gayo. — I went to the market yesterday.
It ends like छोरो, and changes like it, to agree with whoever went: गयो for a man, गई for a woman, गया for more than one.
मां घरै गई। mā̃ gharai gaī. — Mother went home.
सगळा घरै गया। sagḷā gharai gayā. — Everyone went home.
With a past, काल is yesterday. CDIAL lists Old Marwari gayaü, "gone" (CDIAL 4008).

Guided Practice.
A woman says हूं काल बाजार गई। (hū̃ kāl bājār gaī.)
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: राम काल बाजार गयो। — rām kāl bājār gayo. — Ram went to the market yesterday.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: मां घरै गई। — mā̃ gharai gaī. — Mother went home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो कोनी गयो। — vo konī gayo. — He didn't go.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
He went? (गयो.) She went, of mother? (गई.) Everyone went? (गया.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that mother went home.


Lesson 3 of 3.
Went home — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say I am at home.

Guided Practice.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
who went: राम, a man. went: गयो.
who went: मां, a woman. went: गई.
who went: सगळा, more than one. went: गया.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I went to the market yesterday, as a man and then as a woman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mother went home; everyone went home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: he didn't go]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: do you go home every day?; yes, I go home every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
At home? (घरै (gharai).) Went, for a man, a woman, and more than one? (गयो, गई, गया.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that everyone went home.
