Malayalam, chapter 195: Every day, Date, Weekend, Quickly, In advance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
എന്നും (ennuṁ) — every day, always.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for health, then the Malayalam for an eyelid.

You'll want to know: എന്നും.
എന്നും — ennuṁ — "every day, always".

The word, taken apart.
എന്നും (ennuṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5151 lists it, "always", under the old question stem, with Tamil yā: "on whatever day".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for every day, then the Malayalam for a date.

You'll want to know: വാരാന്ത്യം.
വാരാന്ത്യം — vārāntyaṁ — "the weekend".

The word, taken apart.
വാരാന്ത്യം (vārāntyaṁ) is built from Sanskrit parts: vāra, "day of the week" (CDIAL 11547), and antya, "last", from anta, "end" (CDIAL 347): the week's end.

പെട്ടെന്ന് (peṭṭennŭ) is an expressive sound for suddenness followed by ennŭ, "saying" (DEDR 868): "with a peṭṭ".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: മുൻകൂട്ടി.

The word, taken apart.
മുൻകൂട്ടി (munkūṭṭi) is native Dravidian, "put together beforehand": mun, "before" (DEDR 5020), and kūṭṭi, from the verb "to bring together" (DEDR 1882, with Tamil kūṭu).
