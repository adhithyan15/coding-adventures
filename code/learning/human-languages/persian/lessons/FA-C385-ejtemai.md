## You'll want to know: اجتماعی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C385-JAMEE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اجتماعی** (*ejtemâ'i*) — "social".

> او آدم خیلی اجتماعی است.
>
> *u âdam-e kheyli ejtemâ'i ast.*
>
> He is a very sociable person.

From *ejtemâ'* "gathering, society" + *-i*. *Masâ'el-e ejtemâ'i* — social issues.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجتماعی** was borrowed from Arabic *ijtimā'ī* "social" (EtymDB), the adjective of *ijtimā'* "gathering".
