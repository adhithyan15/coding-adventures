## You'll want to know: प्रमाण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C348-PRAMAN348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाण** — *pramāṇ* — "proof (formal), evidence".

> तुम्हारे पास क्या प्रमाण है?
>
> *tumhāre pās kyā pramāṇ hai?*
>
> What evidence do you have?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाण** (*pramāṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pramāṇa*, "a measure, a standard; proof" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
