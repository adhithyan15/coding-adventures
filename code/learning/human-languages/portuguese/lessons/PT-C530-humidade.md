## You'll want to know: a humidade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C530-SENHORIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a humidade** — "damp, humidity".

> Há humidade nas paredes do quarto.
>
> *There is damp on the bedroom walls.*

In Brazil: *a umidade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*humidade* is built on *húmido* "damp", a learned borrowing from Latin *ūmidus* "moist" (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
