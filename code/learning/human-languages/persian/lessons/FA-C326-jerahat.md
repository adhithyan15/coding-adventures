## You'll want to know: جراحت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C326-MASDUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جراحت** (*jerâhat*) — "a wound, an injury".

> جراحت او عمیق نبود.
>
> *Jerâhat-e u 'amiq nabud.*
>
> His wound was not deep.

Formal; in speech *zakhm*. The same root gives *jarrâh* "a surgeon".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jerâhat* is usually traced to Arabic *jirāḥa*, "wounds, wounding"; EtymDB lists no entry.
