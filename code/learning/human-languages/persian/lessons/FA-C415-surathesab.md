## You'll want to know: صورتحساب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C415-MAKHAREJ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صورتحساب** (*surathesâb*) — "a bill, a statement".

> بعد از شام صورتحساب را آوردند.
>
> *ba'd az shâm surathesâb râ âvardand.*
>
> After dinner they brought the bill.

*Surat* "form, list" + *hesâb* "account". Also a bank statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صورتحساب** is a Persian compound of two words from Arabic: *surat*, from *ṣūra* "form" (EtymDB), and *hesâb*, from *ḥisāb* "reckoning, account".
