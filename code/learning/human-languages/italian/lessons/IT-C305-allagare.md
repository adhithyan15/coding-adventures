## You'll want to know: allagare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C305-ALLUVIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**allagare** — "to flood".

> La pioggia ha allagato la cantina.
>
> *The rain flooded the cellar.*

*Allagato* — flooded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allagare* is built on *lago* "lake", from Latin *lacus* (Lewis & Short): to turn into a lake.
