## You'll want to know: die Mücke
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C430-MUECKE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Mücke** — "the mosquito, the gnat".

> Abends kommen die Mücken durch das offene Fenster.
>
> *In the evening the mosquitoes come in through the open window.*

Plural: *die Mücken*. In Austria: *die Gelse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Mücke* is an inherited Germanic word; EtymDB lists English *midge*, Dutch *mug* and Swedish *mygg* among its cousins.
