Bengali, chapter 433: Lakes, Ditches and Wetlands.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঝিল (jhil) — a lake; a marshy pool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a desert, then the Bengali for a jungle.

You'll want to know: ঝিল.
ঝিল — jhil — "a lake; a marshy pool".
শীতে ঝিলে অনেক পাখি আসে। shīte jhile ônek pākhi āse. In winter many birds come to the lake.
হ্রদ is a lake in geography; ঝিল (jhil) is the pool or marsh lake near a village.

The word, taken apart.
ঝিল (jhil) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali jhil, "a lake, a pool", under the reconstructed jhilla (CDIAL 5392).

জলাভূমিতে অনেক পাখি বাসা বাঁধে। jôlābhūmite ônek pākhi bāsā bā̃dhe. Many birds nest in the wetland.

The word, taken apart.
জলাভূমি (jôlābhūmi) joins জলা, which CDIAL lists as Bengali jalā, "a marsh", beside jal, water (CDIAL 5155), to bhūmi, "land" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
দোল খাওয়া (dol khāoyā) — to sway; to rock; to swing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a ditch, then the Bengali for a wetland.

You'll want to know: দোল খাওয়া.
দোল খাওয়া — dol khāoyā — "to sway; to rock; to swing".
হাওয়ায় ঝিলের নৌকাটা দোল খেল। hāoyāy jhiler noukāṭā dol khelô. The boat on the lake rocked in the breeze.
Past (he/she …): সেই নৌকা দোল খেল, sei noukā dol khelô.
দোল is a swing; খাওয়া here is to undergo, as in ধাক্কা খাওয়া.

The word, taken apart.
দোল খাওয়া (dol khāoyā) joins দোল, "a swing, a swaying", from Sanskrit dolā, "a swing, a litter" (Monier-Williams), to খাওয়া, to eat, here to undergo.

Lesson 5 of 5.
কলকল করা (kôlkôl kôrā) — to babble; to murmur (of running water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a wetland, then the Bengali for to sway.

You'll want to know: কলকল করা.
কলকল করা — kôlkôl kôrā — "to babble; to murmur (of running water)".
ঝরনার জল কলকল করছিল। jhôrnār jôl kôlkôl kôrchhilô. The water of the waterfall was babbling.
Past (he/she …): সেই জল কলকল করল, sei jôl kôlkôl kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
কলকল করা (kôlkôl kôrā) joins কলকল, an imitative word for the sound of running water, to করা, to do. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
