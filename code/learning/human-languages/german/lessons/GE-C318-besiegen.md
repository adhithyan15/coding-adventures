## You'll want to know: besiegen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C318-KRIEG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**besiegen** — "to defeat, to beat".

> Die kleine Armee besiegte die große.
>
> *The small army defeated the big one.*

Regular: *besiegte*, *hat besiegt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*besiegen* is built on *Sieg* "victory", Old High German *sigu*, Proto-Germanic *segaz* (EtymDB), with *be-*.
