## You'll want to know: રાણી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C318-RAJA318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**રાણી** — *rāṇī* — "a queen".

> રાણીએ ગરીબ લોકોને અનાજ આપ્યું.
>
> *rāṇīe garīb lokone anāj āpyũ.*
>
> The queen gave grain to the poor.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રાણી** (*rāṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rājñī*, "a queen", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 10692). With **એ** she is the doer: **રાણીએ અનાજ આપ્યું**.
