## You'll want to know: a proa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C419-NAVIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a proa** — "the bow (of a ship)".

> Da proa, vemos as ilhas ao longe.
>
> *From the bow, we can see islands in the distance.*

*À proa* — at the front of the boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proa* comes from Latin *prōra* 'bow of a ship', from Greek *prōira* (EtymDB).
