Marathi, chapter 520: Pipes and Leaks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नळ (naḷ) — a faucet; a water pipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for darkness, then the Marathi for to light up.

You'll want to know: नळ.
नळ — naḷ — "a faucet; a water pipe".
आज सकाळपासून नळाला पाणी नाही. āj sakāḷpāsūn naḷālā pāṇī nāhī. There has been no water in the tap since this morning.
Masculine.
The tap takes -ला: नळाला पाणी येतं, water comes in the tap.

The word, taken apart.
नळ (naḷ) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi naḷ, "lotus stalk; pipe, conduit", under Sanskrit naḍa, "a reed" (CDIAL 6936): a hollow stalk and a pipe share one shape.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गळणे (gaḷṇe) — to drip; to leak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to light up, then the Marathi for a faucet.

You'll want to know: गळणे.
गळणे — gaḷṇe — "to drip; to leak".
तो नळ रात्रभर गळत होता. to naḷ rātrabhar gaḷat hotā. That tap was dripping all night.
Past (he …): तो नळ गळला, to naḷ gaḷlā.

The word, taken apart.
गळणे (gaḷṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit galati, "drips, trickles" (CDIAL 4074).

गळती — gaḷtī — "a leak".
छपरातून पाण्याची गळती होते. chaprātūn pāṇyācī gaḷtī hote. Water leaks through the roof.
Feminine.
Built on गळणे (gaḷṇe): गळती (gaḷtī) होणे, there is a leak.

The word, taken apart.
गळती (gaḷtī) is built on गळणे (gaḷṇe), "to drip", inherited from Sanskrit galati, "drips" (CDIAL 4074).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: तुंबणे.
तुंबणे — tumbṇe — "to get blocked; to back up".
खूप पाऊस पडला आणि गटार तुंबलं. khūp pāūs paḍlā āṇi gaṭār tumblaṁ. It rained heavily and the drain got blocked.
Past (he …): तो नळ तुंबला, to naḷ tumblā.

The word, taken apart.
तुंबणे (tumbṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi tũbṇẽ, "to accumulate through being obstructed", under Sanskrit tumbati (CDIAL 5870).
