## You'll want to know: समझ नहीं आया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**समझ नहीं आया** (*samajh nahīṁ āyā*) — "I didn't understand", literally "understanding did not
come".

**समझ** is "understanding", from **समझना**. It comes, or it doesn't.
