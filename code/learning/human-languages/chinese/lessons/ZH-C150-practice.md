## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-EVER-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C150-GUOQU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-GUO-EXPERIENCE-01, ZH-LEX-C150-HOULAI-01, ZH-LEX-C150-RANHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C150-CONGLAI-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01] -->

> **A:** 你去过中国吗
>
> *nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma* — Have you ever been to China?
>
> **B:** 去过
>
> *qù guo* — I have.
>
> **B:** 过去我家在中国
>
> *guòqù wǒ jiā zài Zhōngguó* — My family used to live there.
>
> **A:** 后来呢
>
> *hòulái ne* — And afterwards?
>
> **B:** 后来我们来这儿了
>
> *hòulái wǒmen lái zhèr le* — Afterwards we came here.
>
> **B:** 你呢 你去过吗
>
> *nǐ ne, nǐ qù guo ma* — And you? Have you been?
>
> **A:** 我没去过
>
> *wǒ méi qù guo* — I've not been.
>
> **B:** 你开过车吗
>
> *nǐ kāi guo chē ma* — Have you ever driven a car?
>
> **A:** *wǒ cónglái méi kāi guo* — Never once.
>
> **B:** *wǒ xiān qù Zhōngguó, ránhòu qù Běijīng* — I'll go to China first, then to Beijing.

去过 answers 你去过中国吗 on its own: the verb and 过, nothing else. The no is 没去过, with 过 kept.

## What you've built — ever, and what came next
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-EVER-01, ZH-SCRIPT-GUO-PASS-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-GUO-EXPERIENCE-01, ZH-LEX-C150-HOULAI-01, ZH-LEX-C150-RANHOU-01] -->

过 after a verb asks about a whole life; 后来 and *ránhòu* put events in order:

- **你去过中国吗** *nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma* — have you ever been to China?
- **我没去过** *wǒ méi qù guo* — I've not been.
- **后来我们来这儿了** *hòulái wǒmen lái zhèr le* — afterwards we came here.
- *wǒ xiān chī fàn, ránhòu kàn shū* — first I eat, then I read.

**Listen.** *Nǐ qù guo Běijīng ma?*

**Speak.** Say one place you have been, and one you have not.
