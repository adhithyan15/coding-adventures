Malayalam, chapter 173: Laugh, Look, Study, Teach, Begin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ചിരിക്കുക (cirikkuka) — to laugh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to call, then the Malayalam for to cry.
[pause 1 second]
Say "him," as the one it happens to. (Avane.)

You'll want to know: ചിരിക്കുക.
ചിരിക്കുക — cirikkuka — "to laugh".
