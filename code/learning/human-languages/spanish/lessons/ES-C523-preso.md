## You'll want to know: el preso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C523-PRESO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el preso** — "the prisoner".

> El preso salió de la cárcel ayer.
>
> *The prisoner came out of prison yesterday.*

A woman is *la presa*. As an adjective: *Está preso* — he is in prison.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preso* is inherited from Latin *prēnsus*, a shorter form of *prehēnsus* 'seized' (EtymDB), the participle of *prehendere* 'to take hold of'. A prisoner is a person who has been seized.
