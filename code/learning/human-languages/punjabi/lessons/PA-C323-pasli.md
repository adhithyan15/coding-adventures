## You'll want to know: ਪਸਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C323-BODY323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਸਲੀ** — *paslī* — "a rib".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਖੱਬੀ ਪਸਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī khabbī paslī vich darad hai.*
>
> I have a pain in a rib on my left side.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਸਲੀ** (*paslī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *parśu*, "a rib"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *paslī*, "rib", under it (CDIAL 7948).
