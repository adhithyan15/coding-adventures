## You'll want to know: زیرنویس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C531-SCREEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**زیرنویس** (*zirnevis*) — "subtitles".

> من فیلمهای خارجی را با زیرنویس میبینم.
>
> *man filmhâ-ye khâreji râ bâ zirnevis mibinam.*
>
> I watch foreign films with subtitles.

*zir* 'under' + *nevis* 'writing'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zirnevis* is built on native Persian *zir*, 'under', usually traced to Middle Persian *azēr*, and *nevis*, the present stem of *neveshtan*, 'to write': under-writing.
