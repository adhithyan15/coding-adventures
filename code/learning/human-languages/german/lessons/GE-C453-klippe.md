## You'll want to know: die Klippe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C453-GIPFEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Klippe** — "the cliff; the rock (in the sea)".

> Von der Klippe sieht man weit über das Meer.
>
> *From the cliff you can see far out over the sea.*

Plural: *die Klippen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Klippe* goes back to medieval German *klippe* 'rock, cliff' (EtymDB) and came into the standard language from Low German; Dutch *klip* 'cliff' is a cousin.
