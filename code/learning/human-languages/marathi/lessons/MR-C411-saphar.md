## You'll want to know: सफर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C411-SAFAR411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सफर** — *saphar* — "a trip; a tour".

> आम्ही होडीने बेटाची सफर केली.
>
> *āmhī hoḍīne beṭācī saphar kelī.*
>
> We took a trip to the island by boat.

Feminine.

सफर करणे, to make a trip; it sounds light and happy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सफर** (*saphar*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *safar*, "a journey".
