## You'll want to know: परिशीलयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C552-PRAYASA552-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परिशीलयति** — *pariśīlayati* — "practises regularly".

> यदि त्वं प्रतिदिनं परिशीलयसि, तर्हि त्वं कुशलः भविष्यसि।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ pratidinaṃ pariśīlayasi, tarhi tvaṃ kuśalaḥ bhaviṣyasi.*
>
> If you practise every day, you will become good at it.

Past (he …): सः संगीतं परिशीलितवान्, *saḥ saṅgītaṃ pariśīlitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिशीलयति** (*pariśīlayati*) is built on *pari* and the root √śīl, "to practise, to do repeatedly"; MW gives *pariśīlayati* as to practise, to use frequently (MW).
