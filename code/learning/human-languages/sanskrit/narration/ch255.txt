Lesson 1 of 5.
शिक्षा (śikṣā) — education.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for dinner, then the Sanskrit for cooked food.

You'll want to know: शिक्षा.
शिक्षा — śikṣā — "education".

The word, taken apart.
śikṣā is built on śikṣ, "to learn", which MW calls the desiderative of √śak, "to be able": learning is wanting to be able. MW's first sense is "desire of being able", then "learning, study". The verb śikṣate lives on as Hindi sīkhnā, "to learn" (CDIAL 12430).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अभ्यासः (abhyāsaḥ) — practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for education, then the Sanskrit for knowledge.

You'll want to know: अभ्यासः.
अभ्यासः — abhyāsaḥ — "practice".

The word, taken apart.
abhyāsa is built on abhi, "towards", and the root √as, "to throw" (MW): "throwing towards, adding", and then "repetition" and "practice". Practice is doing a thing again and again; Hindi took the word as abhyās.

Lesson 4 of 5.
गृहकार्यम् (gṛhakāryam) — homework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for knowledge, then the Sanskrit for practice.

You'll want to know: गृहकार्यम्.
गृहकार्यम् — gṛhakāryam — "homework".

The word, taken apart.
gṛhakārya is a compound of gṛha, "house", and kārya, "what is to be done", from the root √kṛ (MW; CDIAL 3078). MW glosses it "a domestic affair"; its use for school homework is modern. Kārya lives on as Hindi kāj, "work".

Lesson 5 of 5.
उत्तरम् (uttaram) — an answer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for practice, then the Sanskrit for homework.

You'll want to know: उत्तरम्.
उत्तरम् — uttaram — "an answer".

The word, taken apart.
uttara is the comparative of ud, "up": "higher", and also "following, later" (MW). The answer is the later word: CDIAL 1767 explains uttaram as uttaram vākyam, "the following speech". The same word means "north".
