# నీరసం (nīrasaṁ) — weakness, lethargy

## You'll want to know: నీరసం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**నీరసం** (*nīrasaṁ*) — "weakness, lethargy", the drained feeling after a fever.

**నాకు నీరసంగా ఉంది** — "I feel weak", in the frame of **అలసటగా** and **దాహంగా**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A cold, medicine, a hospital, a wound, weakness: enough for a visit to the doctor.
