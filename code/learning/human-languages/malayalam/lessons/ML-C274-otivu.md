## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഒടിവ്** (*oṭivŭ*) is native Dravidian, the noun of the verb "to break"; DEDR 946 lists the root with Tamil *oṭi* and Kannada *oḍi*.
