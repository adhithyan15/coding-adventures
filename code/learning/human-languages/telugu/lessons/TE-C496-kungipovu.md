# కుంగిపోవు (kuṅgipōvu) — to be crushed; to sink in spirit

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to feel sad, then the Telugu for to hurt.

## You'll want to know: కుంగిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C496-BADHA496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**కుంగిపోవు** — *kuṅgipōvu* — "to be crushed; to sink in spirit".

> ఉద్యోగం పోయాక అతను కుంగిపోయాడు.
>
> *udyōgaṁ pōyāka atanu kuṅgipōyāḍu.*
>
> After losing his job, he was crushed.

Past (I …): కుంగిపోయాను, *kuṅgipōyānu*.

Literally to sink and go: a -పోవు compound, like వెళ్ళిపోవు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కుంగిపోవు** (*kuṅgipōvu*) is a native compound: DEDR 1767 lists Telugu *k(r)uṅgu*, "to sink, to fall down", with Kannada *kuggu*; *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572) completes it. Someone who sinks in spirit is crushed.
