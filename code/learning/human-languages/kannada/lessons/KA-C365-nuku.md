## You'll want to know: ನೂಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C365-HODEDATA365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೂಕು** — *nūku* — "to shove; to push".

> ಜನಸಂದಣಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾರೋ ನನ್ನನ್ನು ನೂಕಿದರು.
>
> *janasandaṇiyalli yārō nannannu nūkidaru.*
>
> Someone shoved me in the crowd.

Past (I …): ನೂಕಿದೆ, *nūkide*.

Who is pushed takes -ಅನ್ನು. ನೂಕುನುಗ್ಗಲು is a crush of people pushing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೂಕು** (*nūku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3722 lists Kannada *nūku*, "to shove, to push, to thrust aside", beside Telugu *nūku*.
