## You'll want to know: mài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C42-SHOP42-02]; assesses=[] -->

*mài* — "to sell".

> *tāmen mài shénme* — What do they sell?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mài* is inherited from Middle Chinese *meaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to put goods out'. It differs from the word for buy only in its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
