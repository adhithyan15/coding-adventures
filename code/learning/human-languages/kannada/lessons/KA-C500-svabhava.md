## You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಭಾವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C500-SVABHAVA500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಭಾವ** — *svabhāva* — "temperament; nature".

> ನನ್ನ ತಮ್ಮನ ಸ್ವಭಾವ ತುಂಬಾ ಶಾಂತ.
>
> *nanna tammana svabhāva tumbā śānta.*
>
> My younger brother's nature is very calm.

A person's own way of being: ಸ್ವ, own, joined to the Sanskrit word for being.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವಭಾವ** (*svabhāva*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *svabhāva*, "one's own way of being, natural disposition" (Monier-Williams): *sva*, "own", and *bhāva*, "being".
