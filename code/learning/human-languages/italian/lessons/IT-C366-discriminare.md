## You'll want to know: discriminare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C366-SOCIETA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**discriminare** — "to discriminate (against)".

> Non si può discriminare una persona per la sua religione.
>
> *You cannot discriminate against a person because of their religion.*

Stress: *discrìmino*. The old sense is to tell apart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discriminare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *discrīmināre* "to divide, to separate" (Lewis & Short), from *discrīmen* "a dividing line", built on *cernere* "to sift".
