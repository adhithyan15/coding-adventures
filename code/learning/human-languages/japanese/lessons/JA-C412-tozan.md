## You'll want to know: とざん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C412-TOUGE412-05]; assesses=[] -->

**とざん** — *tozan* — "mountain climbing".

> **しゅうまつに ちちと とざんに いきました。** — *shūmatsu ni chichi to tozan ni ikimashita* — "At the weekend I went mountain climbing with my father."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とざん** (*tozan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *to*, "ascend" (Middle Chinese *tong*) and *zan*, "mountain" (*srean*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
