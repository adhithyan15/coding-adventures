## You'll want to know: めぐまれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C510-CHANCE510-05]; assesses=[] -->

**めぐまれる** — *megumareru* — "to be blessed (with)".

> **もし てんきに めぐまれたら、やまに のぼります。** — *moshi tenki ni megumaretara, yama ni noborimasu* — "If we are blessed with good weather, we will climb the mountain."

Polite **めぐまれます**, *megumaremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **かれが こどもに めぐまれました。**, *kare ga kodomo ni megumaremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めぐまれる** (*megumareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
