Tamil, chapter 189: Glue, Candle, Website, App, Screen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பசை (pacai) — glue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a neem tree, then the Tamil for a coconut palm.

You'll want to know: பசை.
பசை — pacai — "glue".

The word, taken apart.
பசை (pacai) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3827 lists Malayalam paca, "gum, glue".

The word, taken apart.
மெழுகுவர்த்தி (meḻukuvartti) is meḻuku, "wax", which DEDR 5082 files with words for smearing and glossing, and vartti, "wick", from Sanskrit varti (CDIAL 11359): "the wax wick".

Lesson 3 of 5.
இணையதளம் (iṇaiyataḷam) — a website.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for glue, then the Tamil for a candle.

You'll want to know: இணையதளம்.
இணையதளம் — iṇaiyataḷam — "a website".

The word, taken apart.
இணையதளம் (iṇaiyataḷam) is a modern coinage: iṇaiyam, "the internet", from native iṇai, "to join, to link" (DEDR 457), and taḷam, "surface, platform", from Sanskrit tala, "surface" (CDIAL 5731).

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: செயலி.
செயலி — ceyali — "an app".

The word, taken apart.
செயலி (ceyali) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1957). It is a modern coinage on ceyal, "action", from cey, "to do", with -i: "the thing that does".
