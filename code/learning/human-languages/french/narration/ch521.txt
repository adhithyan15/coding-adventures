French, chapter 521: Auteurs et lecteurs.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
le lecteur — reader; player (for discs).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for paperback, then the French for to devour.

You'll want to know: le lecteur.
le lecteur — "reader; player (for discs)".
Ce journal a beaucoup de lecteurs jeunes. This newspaper has a lot of young readers.
A woman is la lectrice. Un lecteur de DVD — a DVD player.

The word, taken apart.
le lecteur is a learned borrowing from Latin lēctor "a reader" (EtymDB), from legere "to gather, to read" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 6.

You'll want to know: le héros.
le héros — "hero; main character".
Le héros du livre est un vieux marin. The hero of the book is an old sailor.
The h is aspirated: le héros, les héros with no liaison.

The word, taken apart.
le héros comes from Latin hērōs, a borrowing from Greek hḗrōs "a demigod, a hero" (EtymDB).
