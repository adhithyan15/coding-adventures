## You'll want to know: lamentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C434-RECLAMACAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lamentar** — "to regret, to be sorry about".

> O gerente lamentou muito o problema.
>
> *The manager was very sorry about the problem.*

*Lamento muito* — I am very sorry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lamentar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lāmentārī* 'to wail, to lament' (EtymDB), on *lāmentum* 'wailing'.
