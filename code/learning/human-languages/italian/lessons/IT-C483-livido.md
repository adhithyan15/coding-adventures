## You'll want to know: il livido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C483-FERITE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il livido** — "bruise".

> Sono caduto e ho un livido sul ginocchio.
>
> *I fell and I have a bruise on my knee.*

Also an adjective: *livido* — bluish, or pale with anger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*livido* is a learned borrowing from Latin *līvidus* "bluish, black and blue" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *līvēre* "to be bluish".
