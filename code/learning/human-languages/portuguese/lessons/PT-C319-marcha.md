## You'll want to know: a marcha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C319-SANTOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a marcha** — "the march; a neighbourhood parade".

> A marcha do nosso bairro foi a mais bonita.
>
> *Our neighbourhood’s parade was the most beautiful.*

In Lisbon in June, neighbourhoods parade in *marchas populares*, with songs and colourful clothes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marcha* came from French *marche*, built on *marcher* "to walk", which is usually traced to Frankish *markōn* "to mark, to tread" (EtymDB has no entry).
