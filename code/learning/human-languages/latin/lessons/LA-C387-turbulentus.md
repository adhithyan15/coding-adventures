## You'll want to know: turbulentus, turbulenta, turbulentum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C387-SEDITIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**turbulentus, turbulenta, turbulentum** — "stormy, turbulent; disorderly".

> Tempora turbulenta erant.
>
> *The times were turbulent.*

*turbulentus, turbulenta, turbulentum*, built on the word for a crowd or an uproar. English *turbulent*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*turbulentus* is built on *turba* "an uproar, a crowd" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-ulentus* "full of". English *turbulent* comes from it.
