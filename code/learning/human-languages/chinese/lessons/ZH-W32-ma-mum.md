## Script — the woman piece, then a horse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-MA-MUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 妈

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, 女, three strokes**, as you wrote it alone, but narrow: the fold that drops and turns, the long left-falling stroke, and last the horizontal, which here rises to the right so it does not cross into the next piece.

**The right piece, the horse, three strokes.** A horizontal that turns down. A vertical that folds right, folds down again and hooks at the foot. Then a rising stroke across the bottom.

3 + 3 = six. The same horse stands on the right of 吗, which is why the two sound alike.
