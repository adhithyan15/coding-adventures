## You'll want to know: le gazole
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C403-POMPE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le gazole** — "the diesel (fuel)".

> Ma voiture roule au gazole.
>
> *My car runs on diesel.*

Also spelled *le gasoil*; many people say *le diesel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gazole* is usually explained as a French spelling of English *gas oil*; EtymDB does not list it.
