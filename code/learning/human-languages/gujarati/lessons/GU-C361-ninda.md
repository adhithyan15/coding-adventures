# નિંદા (nindā) — speaking ill of someone

## You'll want to know: નિંદા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C361-TIKA361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નિંદા** — *nindā* — "speaking ill of someone".

> બીજાની નિંદા કરવી સારી વાત નથી.
>
> *bījānī nindā karvī sārī vāt nathī.*
>
> It isn't good to speak ill of others.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિંદા** (*nindā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nindā*, "blame, censure, reproach" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
