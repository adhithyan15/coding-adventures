## You'll want to know: el robo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C518-ROBO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el robo** — "the robbery, the theft".

> Hubo un robo en la tienda de la esquina.
>
> *There was a robbery at the shop on the corner.*

*¡Esto es un robo!* — this is daylight robbery! — about a high price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*robo* is built on the verb *robar* (EtymDB), which comes from a Germanic verb *\*raubōn* 'to rob, to plunder'.
