Gujarati, chapter 251: Words and their meanings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અર્થ (arth) — meaning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to keep safe, then the Gujarati for to print.

You'll want to know: અર્થ.
અર્થ — arth — "meaning".
આ શબ્દનો અર્થ શું છે? — ā śabdano arth śũ che? — "What is the meaning of this word?"

The word, taken apart.
અર્થ (arth) is Sanskrit ártha, "aim, thing" and later "meaning", taken over whole as a tatsama (CDIAL 638). The same entry lists an inherited Gujarati āth, "store of money".

Lesson 3 of 5.
શબ્દકોશ (śabdkoś) — dictionary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for meaning, then the Gujarati for translation.

You'll want to know: શબ્દકોશ.
શબ્દકોશ — śabdkoś — "dictionary".
હું શબ્દકોશમાં અર્થ શોધું છું. — hũ śabdkośmā̃ arth śodhũ chũ — "I look up the meaning in the dictionary."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શબ્દકોશ (śabdkoś), a store of words, is a learned compound of two tatsamas: Sanskrit śabda, "sound, word" (CDIAL 12298), and kōśa, "case, storeroom" (CDIAL 3539).

You'll want to know: ધ્યાન આપવું.
ધ્યાન આપવું — dhyān āpvũ — "to pay attention".
વિદ્યાર્થી વર્ગમાં ધ્યાન આપે છે. — vidyārthī vargmā̃ dhyān āpe che — "The students pay attention in class."

The word, taken apart.
ધ્યાન આપવું (dhyān āpvũ), to give attention, is built from dhyān, Sanskrit dhyāna, "meditation", taken over as a tatsama (CDIAL 6889), and the inherited verb āpvũ, "to give".

હું રોજ દસ નવા શબ્દ યાદ કરું છું. — hũ roj das navā śabda yād karũ chũ — "I memorize ten new words every day."

The word, taken apart.
યાદ કરવું (yād karvũ) is built from yād, "memory", a loan usually traced to Persian yād, and the verb karvũ, "to do".
