## You'll want to know: a comichão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C483-ALERGIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a comichão** — "itch".

> A picada do mosquito deu-me comichão.
>
> *The mosquito bite made me itch.*

In Brazil: *a coceira*. *Ter comichão* — to itch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comichão* is usually traced to Latin *comēstiō* "a consuming", from *comedere* "to eat up" (Lewis & Short): an itch feels as if the skin were being eaten.
