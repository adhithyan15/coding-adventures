Urdu, chapter 460: Courage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دلیر (diler) — bold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for miserly, then the Urdu for alms.

You'll want to know: دلیر.
دلیر — diler — "bold".
وہ دلیر لڑکی اکیلی پہاڑ پر چڑھی۔ voh diler laṛkī akelī pahāṛ par chaṛhī. That bold girl climbed the mountain alone.

The word, taken apart.
دلیر (diler) is usually traced to Persian dilēr, "bold", a word built on dil, "heart" (دل).

Lesson 3 of 5.
جوکھم (jokham) — a risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for bold, then the Urdu for daring.

You'll want to know: جوکھم.
جوکھم — jokham — "a risk".
پہاڑ پر چڑھنا جوکھم کا کام ہے۔ pahāṛ par chaṛhnā jokham kā kām hai. Climbing a mountain is risky work.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
جوکھم (jokham) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jokham, "risk, danger", under Sanskrit yōgakṣēmá, "the keeping of one's property" (CDIAL 10527): the sense probably moved through the charge paid against loss to the risk itself.
