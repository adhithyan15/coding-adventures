# हादरणे (hādarṇe) — to shake; to be shaken

## You'll want to know: हादरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C293-BHUKAMP293-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हादरणे** — *hādarṇe* — "to shake; to be shaken".

> भूकंपात आमचं संपूर्ण घर हादरलं.
>
> *bhūkampāt āmcaṁ sampūrṇa ghar hādarlaṁ.*
>
> Our whole house shook in the earthquake.

Past (he …): तो हादरला, *to hādarlā*.

Also of people: ती बातमी ऐकून तो हादरला, the news shook him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हादरणे** (*hādarṇe*) is a Marathi verb of violent shaking; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
