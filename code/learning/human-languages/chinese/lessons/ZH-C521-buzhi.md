## You'll want to know: bùzhì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C521-CHORES521-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bùzhì* — "to lay out a room; to decorate a room".

> *xīn fángzi wǒmen bùzhì le liǎng tiān* — We spent two days arranging the new flat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bùzhì* is built from *bù* 'to spread' and *zhì* 'to place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *puH* and *triH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to spread everywhere' and the second 'to set in place'.
