## HL-C347 — Hindi reading declares its stem, present forms, and word family

After the introduction-formula repair, Chapter 34's reading lesson contains the
largest clear cluster of modern Hindi forms that it teaches but never declares.
It explicitly derives and practises **पढ़-**, **पढ़ता**, **पढ़ती**, and
**पढ़ते**, then defines the everyday relatives **पढ़ाई** and **पढ़ाना**, while
the headword exposes only **पढ़ना**.

Declare the stem, three practised present forms, and both taught derivatives
beside the infinitive. This makes the lesson's full working family visible to
reports and downstream study artifacts without adding any new material.
