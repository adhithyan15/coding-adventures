## You'll want to know: triunfar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C595-CARRERA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**triunfar** — "to succeed, to triumph".

> Quiere triunfar en su profesión.
>
> *He wants to succeed in his profession.*

*Triunfar en la vida* — to be a success in life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*triunfar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *triumphare* 'to hold a victory procession', built on *triumphus* 'triumph' (EtymDB).
