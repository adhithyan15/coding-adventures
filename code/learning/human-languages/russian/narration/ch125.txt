Russian, chapter 125: Free, Open, Closed, Quiet, Noisy.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
свободный (svobódnyy) — free.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for orange, then the Russian for busy.

You'll want to know: свободный.
свободный (svobódnyy) — "free".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: svobódnyy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: svobódnyy, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say svobódnyy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for orange, then the Russian for busy, then say svobódnyy again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does свободный (svobódnyy) mean? ("Free".) Say it once more.
