## You'll want to know: ارتياح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C496-HAMAS496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتياح** — *irtiyāḥ* — "relief".

> كان الارتياح واضحا على وجهها.
>
> *kāna al-irtiyāḥ wāḍiḥan ʿalā wajhihā.*
>
> Relief was clear on her face.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتياح** (*irtiyāḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-w-ḥ*, "wind, breath, rest" (Lane). Irtiyāḥ is the verbal noun of Form VIII: breathing easy again.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
