## You'll want to know: le témoin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C389-ENQUETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le témoin** — "the witness".

> Un témoin a tout vu depuis sa fenêtre.
>
> *A witness saw everything from his window.*

At a wedding, *le témoin* is the best man or a witness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*témoin* comes from Old French *tesmoing* (EtymDB), from Latin *testimōnium* "evidence, testimony", built on *testis* "witness" (Lewis & Short).
