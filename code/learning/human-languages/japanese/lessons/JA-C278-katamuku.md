## You'll want to know: かたむく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C278-INAZUMA278-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かたむく** — *katamuku* — "to lean; to tilt".

> **じしんで たなが かたむいています。** — *jishin de tana ga katamuite imasu* — "The shelves are leaning since the earthquake."

Polite **かたむきます**, *katamukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **きが かたむきました。**, *ki ga katamukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かたむく** (*katamuku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
