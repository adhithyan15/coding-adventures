# నిశ్చయం (niścayaṁ) — certainty; a firm resolve

## You'll want to know: నిశ్చయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C388-SANDEHAM388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**నిశ్చయం** — *niścayaṁ* — "certainty; a firm resolve".

> అతను వస్తాడని నాకు నిశ్చయం లేదు.
>
> *atanu vastāḍani nāku niścayaṁ lēdu.*
>
> I am not certain that he will come.

A family word too: పెళ్ళి నిశ్చయం, the fixing of a wedding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిశ్చయం** (*niścayaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *niścaya*, "inquiry, conviction" (CDIAL 7439), which Monier-Williams also glosses "certainty; a fixed resolve".
