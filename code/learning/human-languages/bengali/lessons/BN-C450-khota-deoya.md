# খোঁটা দেওয়া (khõṭā deoyā) — to taunt; to throw something in someone's face

## You'll want to know: খোঁটা দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C450-OPOMAN450-04]; assesses=[] -->

**খোঁটা দেওয়া** — *khõṭā deoyā* — "to taunt; to throw something in someone's face".

> প্রতিবেশী মহিলাটি রোজ মীরাকে খোঁটা দিতেন।
>
> *prôtibeshī môhilāṭi roj mīrāke khõṭā diten.*
>
> The neighbour woman used to taunt Mira every day.

Past (he/she …): তিনি বোনকে খোঁটা দিলেন, *tini bonke khõṭā dilen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খোঁটা দেওয়া** (*khõṭā deoyā*) is *khõṭā*, "a jibe", whose origin is not settled, with *deoyā*, "to give" (CDIAL 6141).
