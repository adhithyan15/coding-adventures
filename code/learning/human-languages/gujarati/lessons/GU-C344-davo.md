## You'll want to know: દાવો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C344-SABITI344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**દાવો** — *dāvo* — "a claim".

> દુકાનદારનો દાવો છે કે માલ અસલી છે.
>
> *dukāndārno dāvo che ke māl aslī che.*
>
> The shopkeeper claims the goods are genuine.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાવો** (*dāvo*) is usually traced to Arabic *daʿwā*, "a claim, a lawsuit", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
