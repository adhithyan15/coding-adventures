## You'll want to know: atropellar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C527-CHOQUE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**atropellar** — "to run over, to knock down".

> Un coche atropelló a una señora en el paso de cebra.
>
> *A car knocked down a woman on the zebra crossing.*

*Me atropelló un coche* — I was hit by a car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atropellar* is built on *tropel* 'a rushing crowd' (EtymDB), with *a-*. *Tropel* is usually said to have come into Spanish from Occitan or French, and its root is usually traced to Germanic.
