## You'll want to know: шалить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C497-UPBRINGING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**шалить** (*shalít*) — "to be naughty".

> На перемене мальчики шалят в коридоре.
>
> *Na pereméne málchiki shalyát v koridóre.*
>
> At break the boys get up to mischief in the corridor.

Imperfective; perfective пошалить. The noun is *шалость* — a prank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *шалить* without a source, and the origin is not settled.
