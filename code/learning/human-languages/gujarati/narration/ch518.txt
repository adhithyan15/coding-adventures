Gujarati, chapter 518: Clearing Out the Loft.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પસ્તી (pastī) — waste paper (sold by weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a light bulb, then the Gujarati for to push in (firmly).

You'll want to know: પસ્તી.
પસ્તી — pastī — "waste paper (sold by weight)".
અમે મહિને એક વાર પસ્તી વેચીએ છીએ. ame mahine ek vār pastī vecīe chīe. Once a month we sell our old newspapers.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પસ્તી (pastī) is old newspaper and paper, sold to a dealer by weight. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
જાળું (jāḷũ) — a cobweb.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to push in (firmly), then the Gujarati for waste paper.

You'll want to know: જાળું.
જાળું — jāḷũ — "a cobweb".
છતના ખૂણામાં કરોળિયાનું જાળું છે. chatnā khūṇāmā̃ karoḷiyānũ jāḷũ che. There is a spider's web in the corner of the ceiling.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
જાળું (jāḷũ) is inherited from Sanskrit jāla, "a net", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a net, a cobweb" (CDIAL 5213).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઝાડુ (jhāḍu) — a long broom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for waste paper, then the Gujarati for a cobweb.

You'll want to know: ઝાડુ.
ઝાડુ — jhāḍu — "a long broom".
મીરાએ ઝાડુથી આખો ઓરડો સાફ કર્યો. Mīrāe jhāḍuthī ākho orḍo sāph karyo. Mira swept the whole room clean with the broom.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઝાડુ (jhāḍu) is perhaps related to jhāṛvũ, "to shake, to sift", which CDIAL files under a reconstructed jhaṭati with Panjabi jhāṛṇā, "to shake off, to sweep" (CDIAL 5328); CDIAL does not list the broom itself, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ફૂગ (phūg) — mould (a fungus).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a cobweb, then the Gujarati for a long broom.

You'll want to know: ફૂગ.
ફૂગ — phūg — "mould (a fungus)".
ભીની દીવાલ પર ફૂગ આવી ગઈ છે. bhīnī dīvāl par phūg āvī gaī che. Mould has appeared on the damp wall.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફૂગ (phūg) grows where it is damp; it "comes" on a thing: ફૂગ આવી ગઈ. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ચોંટાડવું (cõṭāḍvũ) — to stick (something) on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a long broom, then the Gujarati for mould.

You'll want to know: ચોંટાડવું.
ચોંટાડવું — cõṭāḍvũ — "to stick (something) on".
મીરાએ પેટી પર કાગળ ચોંટાડ્યો. Mīrāe peṭī par kāgaḷ cõṭāḍyo. Mira stuck a piece of paper on the box.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કાગળ ચોંટાડ્યો, teṇe kāgaḷ cõṭāḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
ચોંટાડવું (cõṭāḍvũ) is built from ચોંટવું, "to stick", with the causative -āḍ-: to make something stick. The history of ચોંટવું itself is not settled.
