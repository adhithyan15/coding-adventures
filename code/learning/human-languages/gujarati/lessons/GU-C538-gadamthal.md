## You'll want to know: ગડમથલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C538-SANSAY538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગડમથલ** — *gaḍamthal* — "inner turmoil".

> જો હું તેને સાચું કહેત તો મારી ગડમથલ ઓછી થાત.
>
> *jo hũ tene sācũ kahet to mārī gaḍamthal ochī thāt.*
>
> If I had told him the truth, my inner turmoil would have been less.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગડમથલ** (*gaḍamthal*) is an expressive word for the churning of a mind that cannot settle. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
