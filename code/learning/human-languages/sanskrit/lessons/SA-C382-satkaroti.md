## You'll want to know: सत्करोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C382-HITA382-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्करोति** — *satkaroti* — "receives with honour".

> यदि अतिथिः आगच्छति, तर्हि माता तं सत्करोति।
>
> *yadi atithiḥ āgacchati, tarhi mātā taṃ satkaroti.*
>
> If a guest comes, mother receives him with honour.

Past (he …): सः अतिथिं सत्कृतवान्, *saḥ atithiṃ satkṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्करोति** (*satkaroti*) is built from *sat*, "good", and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives *sat-√kṛ* "to treat well or with respect, to honour, to receive hospitably" (MW).
