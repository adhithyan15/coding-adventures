## You'll want to know: मसुदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C544-AARAKHDA544-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मसुदा** — *masudā* — "a draft".

> पत्राचा पहिला मसुदा तयार आहे.
>
> *patrācā pahilā masudā tayār āhe.*
>
> The first draft of the letter is ready.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मसुदा** (*masudā*) is a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic *musawwada*, "a rough copy".
