Spanish, chapter 513: En el periódico.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la redacción — the newsroom; the writing, the essay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to come about, then the Spanish for to take place.

You'll want to know: la redacción.
la redacción — "the newsroom; the writing, the essay".
La redacción del periódico está en el centro. The paper's newsroom is in the centre.
At school una redacción is a written essay.

The word, taken apart.
redacción is built on redactar 'to write up, to draw up', which is usually traced to Latin redāctus, the participle of redigere 'to bring back, to reduce to order' (Lewis & Short), built on agere 'to drive'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
el titular — the headline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to take place, then the Spanish for the newsroom.

You'll want to know: el titular.
el titular — "the headline".
El titular habla del accidente. The headline is about the accident.
Los titulares are the headlines. As an adjective, titular means holding a post: el profesor titular.

The word, taken apart.
titular comes from Late Latin titulāre 'to give a title to' (EtymDB), built on titulus 'an inscription, a label' (Lewis & Short), the word behind título. A headline is the title printed over a story.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la prensa — the press.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the newsroom, then the Spanish for the headline.

You'll want to know: la prensa.
la prensa — "the press".
La prensa habló mucho del caso. The press talked a lot about the case.
La prensa is the newspapers and the journalists together. Una rueda de prensa is a press conference.

The word, taken apart.
prensa comes from Catalan premsa 'a press' (EtymDB), a word usually traced to Latin premere 'to press'. The printing press gave its name to the papers it printed.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el corresponsal — the correspondent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the headline, then the Spanish for the press.

You'll want to know: el corresponsal.
el corresponsal — "the correspondent".
El corresponsal vive en Roma desde hace años. The correspondent has lived in Rome for years.
A reporter who sends news from another city or country. The same form for a woman: la corresponsal.

The word, taken apart.
corresponsal is usually traced to corresponder 'to correspond, to match', from co- and responder, which comes from Latin respondēre 'to answer', built on spondēre 'to promise' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
publicar — to publish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the press, then the Spanish for the correspondent.

You'll want to know: publicar.
publicar — "to publish".
El periódico publicó la noticia el domingo. The newspaper published the news on Sunday.
It takes qu before e: publiqué. Publicar en internet is to post online.

The word, taken apart.
publicar is a learned borrowing from Latin pūblicāre 'to make public' (EtymDB), built on pūblicus 'of the people'. English publish comes from the same verb through French.
