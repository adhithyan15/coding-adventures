## You'll want to know: عانى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C381-MUJTAMA381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عانى** — *ʿānā* — "to suffer".

> يعاني جدي من ألم في الظهر.
>
> *yuʿānī jaddī min alam fī aẓ-ẓahr.*
>
> My grandfather suffers from back pain.

Present: يعاني, *yuʿānī*.

It takes من: عانى من ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عانى** (*ʿānā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-n-y*, "to concern; to toil" (Lane). It is Form III, to wrestle with a hardship: to suffer.
