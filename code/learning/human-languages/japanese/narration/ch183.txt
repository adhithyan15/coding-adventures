Japanese, chapter 183: Customers and Shop Staff.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
きゃく (kyaku) — customer; guest, visitor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for banknote, then the Japanese for small coins.

You'll want to know: きゃく.
きゃく — kyaku — "customer; guest, visitor".
この みせは きゃくが おおいです。 — kono mise wa kyaku ga ōi desu — "This shop has many customers."

The word, taken apart.
きゃく (kyaku) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character, "guest, customer", read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese khaek) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

あの てんいんは しんせつです。 — ano ten'in wa shinsetsu desu — "That shop assistant is kind."

The word, taken apart.
てんいん (ten'in) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ten, "shop" (Middle Chinese temH) and in, "member, staff" (hjwen) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 6.
うりば (uriba) — sales floor; counter, department.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: うりば.
うりば — uriba — "sales floor; counter, department".
くつの うりばは どこですか。 — kutsu no uriba wa doko desu ka — "Where is the shoe department?"

The word, taken apart.
うりば (uriba) is native Japanese, built from uri, the stem of uru, "to sell", and ba, "place" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 6.
えらぶ (erabu) — to choose; to pick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for shop assistant, then the Japanese for sales floor.

You'll want to know: えらぶ.
えらぶ — erabu — "to choose; to pick".
あには いつも やすい くつを えらびます。 — ani wa itsumo yasui kutsu o erabimasu — "My older brother always chooses cheap shoes."
Polite えらびます, erabimasu; a godan verb.

The word, taken apart.
えらぶ (erabu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
