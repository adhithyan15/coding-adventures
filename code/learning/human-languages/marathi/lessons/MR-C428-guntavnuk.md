## You'll want to know: गुंतवणूक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C428-SHILLAK428-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गुंतवणूक** — *guntavṇūk* — "an investment".

> घर ही चांगली गुंतवणूक आहे.
>
> *ghar hī cāṅglī guntavṇūk āhe.*
>
> A house is a good investment.

Feminine.

गुंतवणूक करणे, to invest; a feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुंतवणूक** (*guntavṇūk*) is built on **गुंतवणे**, "to entangle; to invest", with the noun ending -णूक; the origin of the verb is not settled.
