## You'll want to know: сыпь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C487-RASH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сыпь** (*syp*) — "a rash".

> После клубники у ребёнка появилась сыпь.
>
> *Pósle klubníki u rebyónka poyavílas syp.*
>
> After the strawberries the child came out in a rash.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сыпь* is built on *сыпать* 'to pour, to strew', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sypati* (EtymDB): a rash is strewn over the skin.
