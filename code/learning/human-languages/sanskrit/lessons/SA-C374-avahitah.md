## You'll want to know: अवहितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C374-PRAMADA374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अवहितः** — *avahitaḥ* — "attentive; careful".

> यदि भवान् अवहितः, तर्हि दोषः न भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān avahitaḥ, tarhi doṣaḥ na bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you are attentive, there will be no mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवहितः** (*avahitaḥ*) is built on *ava*, "down", and *hita*, "put", the participle of √dhā; MW gives *ava-hita* "attentive" (MW).
