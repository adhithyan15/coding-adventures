## You'll want to know: がっかりする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C429-DOWN429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**がっかりする** — *gakkari suru* — "to be disappointed".

> **しあいに まけて、がっかりしました。** — *shiai ni makete, gakkari shimashita* — "We lost the match, and I was disappointed."

Polite **がっかりします**, *gakkarishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが がっかりしました。**, *senshu ga gakkari shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がっかりする** (*gakkari suru*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, *gakkari*, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with *suru*, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
