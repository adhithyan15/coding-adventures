Russian, chapter 369: The environment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
экология (ekológiya) — ecology, the environment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to donate, then the Russian for to need.

You'll want to know: экология.
экология (ekológiya) — "ecology, the environment".
Мой брат изучает экологию. Moy brat izucháyet ekológiyu. My brother studies ecology.
Feminine. In everyday speech, the state of the environment: плохая экология (ekológiya).

The word, taken apart.
экология (ekológiya) is usually traced to German Ökologie, a learned coinage from Greek oîkos "a house, a household" and lógos "word, study": the study of the home we live in.

Lesson 2 of 5.
загрязнение (zagryaznéniye) — pollution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to need, then the Russian for ecology.

You'll want to know: загрязнение.
загрязнение (zagryaznéniye) — "pollution".
Загрязнение воздуха — серьёзная проблема. Zagryaznéniye vózdukha — seryóznaya probléma. Air pollution is a serious problem.
Neuter. Загрязнение воды — water pollution.

The word, taken apart.
загрязнение (zagryaznéniye) is built on загрязнить "to pollute", from за- and грязь "dirt, mud", the root of грязный "dirty".

Lesson 4 of 5.
перерабатывать (pererabátyvat) — to recycle, to process.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for pollution, then the Russian for to pollute.

You'll want to know: перерабатывать.
перерабатывать (pererabátyvat) — "to recycle, to process".
В нашем городе перерабатывают пластик. V náshem górode pererabátyvayut plástik. In our town they recycle plastic.
Imperfective; perfective переработать. It also means to work overtime.

The word, taken apart.
перерабатывать (pererabátyvat) is built from пере- "over again" and работать "to work", from работа, Proto-Slavic orbota: to work a material over into something new.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: беречь.
беречь (beréch) — "to look after, to protect; to save".
Нужно беречь природу. Núzhno beréch priródu. We must look after nature.
Imperfective; present берегу, бережёшь, берегут; past берёг, берегла. Беречь себя — to take care of oneself.

The word, taken apart.
беречь (beréch) is inherited from Proto-Slavic berťi, from PIE bʰergʰ- "to defend, to protect" (EtymDB).
