## You'll want to know: a bilheteira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C414-BILHETEIRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a bilheteira** — "the ticket office, the box office".

> A bilheteira da estação abre às seis.
>
> *The station ticket office opens at six.*

In Brazil: *a bilheteria*. Theatres and stadiums have one too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bilheteira* is built on *bilhete* 'ticket' with *-eira*, a place; *bilhete* came from French *billet* 'note', from Old French *billette* (EtymDB).
