## You'll want to know: rentable
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C637-FAVOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rentable** — "profitable, worthwhile".

> El negocio es rentable.
>
> *The business is profitable.*

Built on *la renta* — income.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rentable* is built on *renta* 'income', which is usually traced to spoken Latin *rendita* 'what is given back', from *reddere* 'to give back' (Lewis & Short).
