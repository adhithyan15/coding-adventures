# ਅਜੇ (aje) — not yet; still

## You'll want to know: ਅਜੇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-AJE-213]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03, PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-REQUEST-NA-213] -->

**ਅਜੇ** — *aje* — "not yet; still".

> **ਮੈਂ ਅਜੇ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਂ।** — *maiṁ aje ghar vichch hāṁ.* — I am still at home.

> **ਅਜੇ ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ।** — *aje nā uṭṭho.* — Don't get up yet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਜੇ** (*aje*) is inherited from Sanskrit *adyaiva*, "even today": CDIAL lists
Punjabi *aje*, "hitherto" (CDIAL 245). "Up to today" is where both of its
senses come from: *still*, and with a no, *not yet*.

One more word for saying no and asking.
