## You'll want to know: 电工
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C259-JOB259-05]; assesses=[] -->

**电工** — *diàngōng* — "electrician".

> **他是电工** *tā shì diàngōng* — He's an electrician.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**电工** (*diàngōng*) is built from 电 'electricity' and 工 'a worker', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *kuwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'lightning'.
