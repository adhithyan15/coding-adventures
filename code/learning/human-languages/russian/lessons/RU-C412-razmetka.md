## You'll want to know: разметка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C412-PESHEKHOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**разметка** (*razmétka*) — "road markings".

> Зимой разметки на дороге почти не видно.
>
> *Zimóy razmétki na doróge pochtí ne vídno.*
>
> In winter the road markings are hard to make out.

Feminine and singular for all the lines: *дорожная разметка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разметка* is built on *разметить* 'to mark out', from *раз-* and *метить* 'to mark', the root also found in *замечать* 'to notice'. EtymDB does not list the word.
