Marathi, chapter 109: Old, Week, Month, Year, Hour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
म्हातारा (mhātārā) — old (people).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for pink, then the Marathi for young.
