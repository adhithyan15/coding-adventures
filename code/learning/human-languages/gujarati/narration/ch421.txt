Gujarati, chapter 421: Noise and Stray Cattle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઘોંઘાટ (ghõghāṭ) — noise; a din.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a post, then the Gujarati for to thatch.

You'll want to know: ઘોંઘાટ.
ઘોંઘાટ — ghõghāṭ — "noise; a din".
શહેરમાં આખો દિવસ ઘોંઘાટ રહે છે. śahermā̃ ākho divas ghõghāṭ rahe che. In the city there is noise all day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઘોંઘાટ (ghõghāṭ) sounds like the noise it names. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઢોર (ḍhor) — cattle; livestock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a din, then the Gujarati for an uproar.

You'll want to know: ઢોર.
ઢોર — ḍhor — "cattle; livestock".
રસ્તા પર ઢોર બેઠાં છે, એટલે ગાડીઓ ધીમે ચાલે છે. rastā par ḍhor beṭhā̃ che, eṭle gāḍīo dhīme cāle che. Cattle are sitting on the road, so the cars move slowly.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઢોર (ḍhor) CDIAL lists Hindi ḍhor, "cattle", under Sanskrit dhaurēya, "a beast of burden" (CDIAL 6884), the word behind ધોરી in ધોરીમાર્ગ; it gives no Gujarati form for ઢોર, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઘુરકવું (ghurakvũ) — to growl; to snarl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an uproar, then the Gujarati for cattle.

You'll want to know: ઘુરકવું.
ઘુરકવું — ghurakvũ — "to growl; to snarl".
રાત્રે શેરીમાં કૂતરો ઘુરકે છે. rātre śerīmā̃ kūtro ghurke che. At night a dog growls in the street.
Past (it …): તે કૂતરો ઘુરક્યો, te kūtro ghurakyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઘુરકવું (ghurakvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit ghurati, "cries out frightfully"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to growl" (CDIAL 4487).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભગાડવું (bhagāḍvũ) — to chase away; to drive off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for cattle, then the Gujarati for to growl.

You'll want to know: ભગાડવું.
ભગાડવું — bhagāḍvũ — "to chase away; to drive off".
ચોકીદાર ઢોરને દરવાજા પાસેથી ભગાડે છે. cokīdār ḍhorne darvājā pāsethī bhagāḍe che. The watchman chases the cattle away from the gate.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કૂતરાને ભગાડ્યો, teṇe kūtrāne bhagāḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
ભગાડવું (bhagāḍvũ) is the causative partner of ભાગવું, "to run away", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit bhajyátē, "is broken" (CDIAL 9361): to make someone run off.
