## You'll want to know: wèntí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C214-HELP214-01]; assesses=[] -->

*wèntí* — "question; problem".

> *wǒ yǒu yí ge wèntí* — I've got a question.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wèntí* is built from 问 'to ask' and *tí* 'a heading', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjunH* and *dej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the forehead' and 'the title of a book'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
