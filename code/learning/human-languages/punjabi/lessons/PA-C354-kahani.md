## You'll want to know: ਕਹਾਣੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C354-STORY354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਹਾਣੀ** — *kahāṇī* — "a story".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੁਰਾਣੀਆਂ ਕਹਾਣੀਆਂ ਪਸੰਦ ਹਨ।
>
> *mainū̃ purāṇīāṁ kahāṇīāṁ pasand han.*
>
> I like old stories.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਹਾਣੀ** (*kahāṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kathānaka*, "a little tale"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kahāṇī* under it (CDIAL 2705).
