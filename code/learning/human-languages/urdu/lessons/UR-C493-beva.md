## You'll want to know: بیوہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C493-MOURNING493-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بیوہ** — *bevā* — "a widow".

> وہ بیوہ اپنے بیٹے کو اکیلی پالتی ہے۔
>
> *voh bevā apne beṭe ko akelī pāltī hai.*
>
> That widow is bringing up her son on her own.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیوہ** (*bevā*) is usually traced to Persian *bēva*, "widow".
