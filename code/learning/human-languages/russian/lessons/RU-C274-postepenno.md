## You'll want to know: постепенно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C274-POSTEPENNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**постепенно** (*postépenno*) — "gradually, little by little".

> Постепенно все поняли правила игры.
>
> *Postépenno vse pónyali právila igrý.*
>
> Gradually everyone understood the rules of the game.

The adverb of *постепенный* (*postépennyy*) "gradual".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*постепенно* is built on *по* and *степень* "a degree, a step", inherited from Proto-Slavic *stepenĭ* (EtymDB): step by step.
