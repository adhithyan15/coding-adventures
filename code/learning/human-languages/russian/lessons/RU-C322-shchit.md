## You'll want to know: щит
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C322-RYTSAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**щит** (*shchit*) — "a shield".

> На щите был нарисован лев.
>
> *Na shchité byl narisóvan lev.*
>
> A lion was painted on the shield.

Genitive *щита*. Also a board: *электрический щит* — a fuse box.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*щит* is inherited through Old East Slavic *ščitŭ* from Proto-Slavic *ščitŭ* "shield", which EtymDB traces to PIE *skey-* "to cut, to split": a split board.
