# تسلل (tasallala) — to sneak in, to slip in

## You'll want to know: تسلل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C346-JASUS346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلل** — *tasallala* — "to sneak in, to slip in".

> تسلل اللص إلى البيت من النافذة.
>
> *tasallala al-liṣṣ ilā al-bayt min an-nāfidha.*
>
> The thief sneaked into the house through the window.

Present: يتسلل, *yatasallal*.

In football, تسلل is offside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلل** (*tasallala*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-l-l* (Lane), "to draw a thing out gently", as a sword from its sheath. It is Form V, "to slip away or in unseen".
