## You'll want to know: турбаза
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C428-NOCHLEG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**турбаза** (*turbáza*) — "a holiday camp, a tourist centre".

> Летом мы отдыхали на турбазе у озера.
>
> *Létom my otdykháli na turbáze u ózera.*
>
> In summer we stayed at a holiday camp by the lake.

Feminine; short for *туристическая база*: simple cabins in the countryside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*турбаза* is a short form of *туристическая база* 'a tourist base', from *турист* 'a tourist' and *база* 'a base'. EtymDB lists *база* and *турист* without a source; *турист* is usually traced through French *touriste* and *tour* to Latin *tornāre* 'to turn on a lathe' (Lewis & Short).
