Arabic, chapter 81: Camel, Donkey, Hen, Big, Small.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جمل (jamal) — a camel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a snake, then the Arabic for a bear.

You'll want to know: جمل.
جمل — jamal — "a camel".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jamal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jamal, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say jamal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a snake, then the Arabic for a bear, then say jamal again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does جمل (jamal) mean? ("A camel".) Say it once more.
