Gujarati, chapter 356: Rights and the Constitution.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બંધારણ (bandhāraṇ) — a constitution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a grant, then the Gujarati for to collect money.

You'll want to know: બંધારણ.
બંધારણ — bandhāraṇ — "a constitution".
આપણું બંધારણ દરેક નાગરિકને સમાન હક આપે છે. āpṇũ bandhāraṇ darek nāgrikne samān hak āpe che. Our constitution gives every citizen equal rights.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
બંધારણ (bandhāraṇ) CDIAL lists Gujarati bandhāraṇ, "arrangement, construction", under Sanskrit bandhakaraṇa, "binding" (CDIAL 9138); "a constitution" is its modern sense, the frame a state is built on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for independent, then the Gujarati for society.

You'll want to know: માન્ય.
માન્ય — mānya — "valid; accepted".
આ પરવાનો ફક્ત એક વરસ માટે માન્ય છે. ā parvāno phakt ek varas māṭe mānya che. This licence is valid for one year only.

The word, taken apart.
માન્ય (mānya) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit mānya, "to be respected, to be honoured" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati uses it for what an office accepts as valid.
