## You'll want to know: अधूरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C385-BIKHAR385-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अधूरा** — *adhūrā* — "unfinished, incomplete".

> अगर तुम आज नहीं रुकोगे तो काम अधूरा रह जाएगा।
>
> *agar tum āj nahīṃ rukoge to kām adhūrā rah jāegā.*
>
> If you don't stay today, the work will be left unfinished.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधूरा** (*adhūrā*) is inherited from *\*ardhapūraka*, "half-full", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 664).
