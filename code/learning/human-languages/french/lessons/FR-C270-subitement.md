## You'll want to know: subitement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C270-SOUDAIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**subitement** — "suddenly, without warning".

> Le vent a subitement changé.
>
> *The wind suddenly changed.*

Built on *subit*, 'sudden': *une mort subite*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*subitement* is built on *subit* "sudden", a learned borrowing from Latin *subitus* "sudden", from *subīre* "to come up, to steal upon" (Lewis & Short).
