Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 164: A Notice at the Dairy.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खुलो (khulo) — open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say to open, खोलणो. Then a house and a shop.

You'll want to know: खुलो.
खुलो — khulo — "open".
घर खुलो है। ghar khulo hai. — The house is open.

The word, taken apart.
खुलो (khulo) is inherited: CDIAL lists Old Marwari khulo, "open", in the same entry as खोलणो, under a reconstructed khōll, "to open" (CDIAL 3945).

What you've built.
खुलो (khulo) ends like छोरो and changes the same way, to agree with what is open: घर खुलो (khulo) है।, but दुकान खुली है। (dukān khulī hai.), the shop is open.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: khulo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: दुकान आज खुली है? — dukān āj khulī hai? — Is the shop open today?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हां सा, दुकान खुली है। — hā̃ sā, dukān khulī hai. — Yes, the shop is open.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does खुलो (khulo) mean? ("Open".) The shop is open? (दुकान खुली है।).
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Marwadi for 'open'.


Lesson 2 of 5.
बंद (band) — closed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the shop is open. Then Monday and Sunday: सोमवार, रविवार.

You'll want to know: बंद.
बंद — band — "closed".
सोमवार दुकान बंद है। somvār dukān band hai. — On Monday the shop is closed.

The word, taken apart.
बंद (band) is a loan, usually traced to Persian band, "shut, bound".

What you've built.
बंद (band) does not change: घर बंद (band) है।, दुकान बंद (band) है। Its opposite does: खुलो (khulo), खुली.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: band]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: दुकान बंद है पण घर खुलो है। — dukān band hai paṇ ghar khulo hai. — The shop is closed, but the house is open.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: दुकान आज बंद कोनी। — dukān āj band konī. — The shop isn't closed today.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does बंद (band) mean? ("Closed".) Does it change for a shop? (No.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Marwadi for 'closed'.


Lesson 3 of 5.
तांई (tā̃ī) — until.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say he comes from the market. Then the shop is closed.

You'll want to know: तांई.
तांई — tā̃ī — "until".
दुकान दिनूगै ६ सूं १० तांई खुली है। dukān dinūgai chha sū̃ das tā̃ī khulī hai. — The shop is open in the morning from six until ten.
सूं opens the stretch of time, and तांई (tā̃ī) closes it. The hours are digits, read as numbers: ६ is chha, १० is das.

The word, taken apart.
तांई (tā̃ī) is probably inherited from Sanskrit tāvat, "so long". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but it lists an Old Gujarati word for "up till" there (CDIAL 5804).

What you've built.
सूं … तांई (tā̃ī) frames any stretch: आथण ५ सूं ८ तांई (tā̃ī), in the evening from five until eight.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tā̃ī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: आथण ५ सूं ८ तांई — āthaṇ pāṅch sū̃ āṭh tā̃ī — in the evening, from five until eight]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say from the market, then tā̃ī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does तांई (tā̃ī) mean? ("Until".) From six until ten? (६ सूं १० तांई (tā̃ī).)
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Marwadi for 'until'.


Lesson 4 of 5.
(reading) (dukān roj dinūgai chha sū̃ das tā̃ī khulī hai.) — a notice at the dairy.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, to read the printed text yourself. The questions are about what you read, so save the whole lesson for when you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say open, closed and until.
[pause 1 second]
Nobody is talking to anybody here. A notice says what you may do, and when.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Reading.
दूध, दही अर छाछ दुकान रोज दिनूगै ६ सूं १० तांई (tā̃ī) खुली है। आथण दुकान ५ सूं ८ तांई (tā̃ī) खुली है। रविवार दुकान दिनूगै ६ सूं १२ तांई (tā̃ī) खुली है। दोपार अर रात दुकान बंद (band) है। सोमवार दुकान बंद (band) है। भाव: दूध ५४। आज घी कोनी। दही घणो आछो है! आभार।.
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: the notice once, straight through, without translating]
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: it a second time, and find every line that says when]

You'll want to know.
[pause 2 seconds]
Most lines open on a when: रोज, आथण, रविवार, दोपार, सोमवार. Each stretch of hours runs सूं … तांई (tā̃ī). भाव is the price, written in digits. The line with! is an advert, set into the notice to bring you in.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — answer: is the shop open on Monday? (No: सोमवार दुकान बंद (band) है।)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: until when is it open on Sunday morning? (Until twelve: १२ तांई (tā̃ī).)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: is there ghee today? (No: आज घी कोनी।)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Which day is the shop closed all day? (सोमवार, Monday.) When is it closed every day? (दोपार अर रात, midday and night.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
On Tuesday you work from seven in the morning until four in the afternoon. When can you buy milk that day?


Lesson 5 of 5.
A notice at the dairy — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the shop is open.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: the house is open; the shop is open; the shop is closed]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: from six until ten; in the evening, from five until eight]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: on Monday the shop is closed; there is no ghee today]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: in the notice, when can you buy milk on a Sunday morning? (From six until twelve.)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Open? (खुलो (khulo), खुली.) Closed? (बंद (band).) Until? (तांई (tā̃ī).)
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say: on Monday the shop is closed, but on Sunday it is open.
