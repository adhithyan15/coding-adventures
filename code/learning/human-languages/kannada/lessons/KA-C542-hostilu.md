# ಹೊಸ್ತಿಲು (hostilu) — a threshold; a doorstep

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C541-VYAYAMA541-05, KA-LEX-C542-MANE542-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to cheer on, then the Kannada for an upper storey.

## You'll want to know: ಹೊಸ್ತಿಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C542-MANE542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಸ್ತಿಲು** — *hostilu* — "a threshold; a doorstep".

> ಅತಿಥಿ ಹೊಸ್ತಿಲು ದಾಟಿ ಒಳಗೆ ಬಂದರು.
>
> *atithi hostilu dāṭi oḷage bandaru.*
>
> The guest stepped over the threshold and came in.

In many homes a rangoli is drawn before it each morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಸ್ತಿಲು** (*hostilu*) is a native Kannada word for the threshold of a door; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
