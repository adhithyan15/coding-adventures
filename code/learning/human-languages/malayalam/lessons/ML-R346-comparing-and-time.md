# Review — comparing and time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C346-RTU-01, ML-SCRIPT-C346-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a season". (*ṛtu*.) Write **ഋ** once from memory, then **ഋതു**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C346-EKKAL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-ETTAVUM-01, ML-LEX-C346-RTU-01, ML-SCRIPT-C346-VOCALIC-R-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-SPAN-AAYI-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-SINCE-MUTAL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C345-FELT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C345-AAN-AGRAHAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C345-ENNANU-AGRAHAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: "This is bigger than that. The train goes faster than the bus."]
- [YOU SAY: "I like coffee more than tea."]
- [YOU SAY: "This is the biggest house in the village."]
- [YOU SAY: "The rainy season is my favourite season."]
- [YOU SAY: "I have been learning Malayalam for three years. I would like to see Kerala."]
- [YOU SAY: "I have had a fever since yesterday. I feel tired."]
- [YOU SAY: "My dream is to see Kerala." — *kēraḷaṁ kāṇaṇaṁ ennāṇŭ enṟe svapnaṁ.*]
