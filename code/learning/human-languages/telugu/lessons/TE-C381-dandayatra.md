# దండయాత్ర (daṇḍayātra) — an invasion; a military campaign

## You'll want to know: దండయాత్ర
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C381-SAHASAM381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**దండయాత్ర** — *daṇḍayātra* — "an invasion; a military campaign".

> ఆ చక్రవర్తి చాలా దండయాత్రలు చేశాడు.
>
> *ā cakravarti cālā daṇḍayātralu cēśāḍu.*
>
> That emperor led many military campaigns.

దండయాత్ర చేయు, to invade, to lead a campaign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దండయాత్ర** (*daṇḍayātra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *daṇḍayātrā*, "a procession; a military expedition" (Monier-Williams), from *daṇḍa*, "a stick, a rod" (CDIAL 6128), and *yātrā*, "a journey" (CDIAL 10456).
