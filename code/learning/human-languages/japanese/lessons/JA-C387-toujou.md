## You'll want to know: とうじょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C387-DEMUKAERU387-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とうじょう** — *tōjō* — "boarding (a plane or ship)".

> **とうじょうの じかんは くじです。** — *tōjō no jikan wa kuji desu* — "Boarding time is nine o'clock."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とうじょう** (*tōjō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tō*, "board" (Middle Chinese *thap*) and *jō*, "ride" (*zying*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
