## You'll want to know: die Drogerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C443-FEINKOST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Drogerie** — "the chemist (for toiletries), the drugstore".

> In der Drogerie kauft sie Shampoo und Zahnpasta.
>
> *At the chemist she buys shampoo and toothpaste.*

Plural: *die Drogerien*. Medicine is sold at *die Apotheke*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Drogerie* was borrowed from French *droguerie* (EtymDB), built on *drogue* 'dried goods, drug'; the deeper origin of *drogue* is not settled.
