## You'll want to know: der Spaten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C504-SAEEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Spaten** — "spade".

> Mit dem Spaten gräbt er ein Loch für den Baum.
>
> *He digs a hole for the tree with the spade.*

Plural: *die Spaten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Spaten* is usually traced to Low German *spade*; English *spade* is a cousin. EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
