Persian, chapter 192: Yearly, Gradually, Again, Immediately, Soon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سالانه (sâlâne) — yearly, annual.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for Nowruz, then the Persian for Yalda.

سالانه — sâlâne — "yearly, annual".
