## You'll want to know: ઢાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C294-LAPAS294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢાળ** — *ḍhāḷ* — "a slope".

> આ રસ્તા પર મોટો ઢાળ છે.
>
> *ā rastā par moṭo ḍhāḷ che.*
>
> This road has a steep slope.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢાળ** (*ḍhāḷ*) probably belongs with *ḍhaḷvũ*, to slip, to be poured out, which CDIAL sets under *\*ḍhalati* (CDIAL 5581): ground that falls away. A bicycle goes fast **ઢાળ પર**, on a slope.
