Urdu, chapter 304: Trouble in the Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فساد (fasād) — a riot; strife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for information, then the Urdu for the reality.

You'll want to know: فساد.
فساد — fasād — "a riot; strife".
شہر میں فساد ہوا۔ shahr meṅ fasād huā. There was a riot in the city.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
فساد (fasād) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic fasād, "corruption, disorder"; in Urdu it is most often violence between groups.

ہنگامہ (hangāmā) is usually traced to Persian hangāma, "a crowd, a tumult". It is noise and disorder, which need not turn violent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پھوٹنا (phūṭnā) — to burst; to break out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a riot, then the Urdu for an uproar.

You'll want to know: پھوٹنا.
پھوٹنا — phūṭnā — "to burst; to break out".
رات کو اچانک فساد پھوٹ پڑا۔ rāt ko achānak fasād phūṭ paṛā. Suddenly, in the night, a riot broke out.
Past (it …): وہ بوتل پھوٹی, voh botal phūṭī.

The word, taken apart.
پھوٹنا (phūṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phūṭnā, "to split, to burst", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13845). Trouble پھوٹ پڑا when it broke out all at once.

The word, taken apart.
لاٹھی (lāṭhī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lāṭhī, "a staff, a club", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10991). It is the long bamboo stick of the police.
