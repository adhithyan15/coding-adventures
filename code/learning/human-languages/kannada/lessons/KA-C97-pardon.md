# ಕ್ಷಮೆ (kṣame) — pardon, forgiveness

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C96-HELLO-04, KA-LEX-C96-HELLO-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for hello, then the Kannada for are you well.

## You'll want to know: ಕ್ಷಮೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C97-GRATEFUL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕ್ಷಮೆ** (*kṣame*) — "pardon, forgiveness". You know its verb from **ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ**
(*kṣamisi*), "excuse me".

**ಕ್ಷಮೆ ಇರಲಿ** (*kṣame irali*) — "please forgive me".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The noun behind excuse me.
