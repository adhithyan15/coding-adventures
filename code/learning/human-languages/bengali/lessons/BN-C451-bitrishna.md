## You'll want to know: বিতৃষ্ণা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C451-GHRINA451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিতৃষ্ণা** — *bitrishnā* — "aversion; distaste".

> রোজ একই খাবার খেয়ে অরুণের বিতৃষ্ণা এল।
>
> *roj eki khābār kheye ôruner bitrishnā elô.*
>
> Eating the same food every day, Arun grew sick of it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিতৃষ্ণা** (*bitrishnā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vitṛṣṇā*, which Monier-Williams gives as freedom from desire, *vi-* "without" on *tṛṣṇā* "thirst"; Bengali has pushed it on to distaste, a sense the dictionary does not give.
