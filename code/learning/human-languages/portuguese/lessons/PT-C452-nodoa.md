## You'll want to know: a nódoa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C452-CUIDADOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a nódoa** — "the stain".

> Tenho uma nódoa de vinho na camisa.
>
> *I have a wine stain on my shirt.*

In Brazil: *a mancha*. *Tirar uma nódoa* — to get a stain out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nódoa* is inherited from Latin *notula* 'little mark' (EtymDB), a small form of *nota* 'mark'.
