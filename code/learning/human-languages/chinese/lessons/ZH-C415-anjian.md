## You'll want to know: 安检
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C415-TERMINAL415-04]; assesses=[] -->

**安检** — *ānjiǎn* — "security check".

> **我们先去安检** *wǒmen xiān qù ānjiǎn* — We'll go through security first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**安检** (*ānjiǎn*) is built from *ān* 'safe' and 检 'to check', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'an* and *kjemX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); it is a short form of a longer name, a safety check.
