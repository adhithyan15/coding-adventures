Persian, chapter 330: Storms and warnings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طوفان (tufân) — a storm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to leak, then the Persian for ash.

You'll want to know: طوفان.
طوفان (tufân) — "a storm".
طوفان (tufân) درخت های زیادی را انداخت. Tufân derakht-hâ-ye ziyâdi râ andâkht. The storm brought down a lot of trees.
Also tufân-e shen "a sandstorm".

The word, taken apart.
tufân is borrowed from Arabic ṭūfān, "storm, typhoon" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
غرق شدن (gharq shodan) — to drown; to sink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for ash, then the Persian for a storm.

You'll want to know: غرق شدن.
غرق شدن (gharq shodan) — "to drown; to sink".
قایق در دریا غرق شد. Qâyeq dar daryâ gharq shod. The boat sank in the sea.
Of people "to drown", of boats "to sink". Present gharq mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
gharq "drowning, sinking" is borrowed from Arabic, of the root of gharaqa "to drown" (EtymDB lists the Arabic verb). shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سقوط کردن (soqut kardan) — to crash down, to fall (from a height).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a storm, then the Persian for to drown.

You'll want to know: سقوط کردن.
سقوط کردن (soqut kardan) — "to crash down, to fall (from a height)".
هواپیما در کوه سقوط کرد. Havâpeymâ dar kuh soqut kard. The plane crashed in the mountains.
For planes, buildings and governments. Present soqut mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
soqut is borrowed from Arabic suqūṭ, "a fall" (EtymDB). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
هشدار (hoshdâr) — a warning, an alert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to drown, then the Persian for to crash down.

You'll want to know: هشدار.
هشدار (hoshdâr) — "a warning, an alert".
رادیو هشدار طوفان داد. Râdiyo hoshdâr-e tufân dâd. The radio gave a storm warning.
hosh "mind, attention" + dâr "holding": keep your mind on it.

The word, taken apart.
hoshdâr joins hosh "mind, attention", inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and dâr-, the present stem of dâshtan "to hold".

Lesson 5 of 5.
هشدار دادن (hoshdâr dâdan) — to warn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to crash down, then the Persian for a warning.

You'll want to know: هشدار دادن.
هشدار دادن (hoshdâr dâdan) — "to warn".
پلیس به مردم هشدار داد که از خانه بیرون نروند. Polis be mardom hoshdâr dâd ke az khâne birun naravand. The police warned people not to go out of their homes.
The person warned takes be; the warning follows in a ke clause with the subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
hoshdâr dâdan joins the native noun hoshdâr "a warning" with dâdan "to give", inherited Persian.
