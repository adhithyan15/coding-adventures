## You'll want to know: おいわい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C288-NYUUGAKU288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おいわい** — *oiwai* — "a celebration; a present for a happy occasion".

> **おいわいに みんなで しょくじを しました。** — *oiwai ni minna de shokuji o shimashita* — "We all had a meal to celebrate."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おいわい** (*oiwai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *o-*, "the polite prefix", and *iwai*, "celebrating" (from *iwau*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
