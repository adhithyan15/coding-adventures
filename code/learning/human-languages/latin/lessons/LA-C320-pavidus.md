## You'll want to know: pavidus, pavida, pavidum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C320-PAVOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pavidus, pavida, pavidum** — "frightened, trembling".

> Pavida ancilla in angulō sedēbat.
>
> *The frightened maid was sitting in the corner.*

From the same verb as *pavor*. Plautus pairs it with *timidus* (taught).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pavidus** is built on *paveō*, "to tremble with fear", with the adjective ending *-idus* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
