## You'll want to know: la caserma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C447-UFFICI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la caserma** — "the barracks; the police station (of the carabinieri)".

> La caserma dei carabinieri è in fondo alla strada.
>
> *The carabinieri station is at the end of the street.*

*La caserma dei vigili del fuoco* — the fire station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caserma* is usually traced through French *caserne* and Provençal to Latin *quaterna* 'a set of four': first a small hut for four soldiers. It came into Italian as a borrowing.
