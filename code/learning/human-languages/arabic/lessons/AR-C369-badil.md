## You'll want to know: بديل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C369-NASIHA369-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بديل** — *badīl* — "an alternative".

> الدراجة بديل جيد عن السيارة.
>
> *ad-darrāja badīl jayyid ʿan as-sayyāra.*
>
> A bicycle is a good alternative to a car.

بديل عن or بديل لـ: an alternative to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بديل** (*badīl*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-d-l*, "to change, to put in place of" (Lane). A *badīl* is what is put in place of another: a substitute, an alternative.
