## You'll want to know: il candidato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C356-CANDIDATURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il candidato** — "the candidate, the applicant".

> Ci sono venti candidati per un solo posto.
>
> *There are twenty candidates for a single job.*

A woman is *la candidata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*candidato* is a learned borrowing from Latin *candidātus* "dressed in white" (Lewis & Short), from *candidus* "shining white", because people seeking office in Rome wore a white toga.
