## You'll want to know: presumir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C368-HIPOTESE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**presumir** — "to presume, to assume".

> Presumo que a reunião começa às nove.
>
> *I presume the meeting starts at nine.*

Formal. *Presumível* — presumed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*presumir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praesūmere* "to take before, to take beforehand" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *prae* and *sūmere* "to take".
