## You'll want to know: 如愿
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C535-WILLING535-03]; assesses=[] -->

**如愿** — *rúyuàn* — "to get what one wished for".

> *zhǐyào nǐ nǔlì jiù néng rúyuàn* — As long as you work hard, you'll get what you hoped for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**如愿** (*rúyuàn*) is built from 如 'as, like' and 愿 'a wish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyo* and *ngjwonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): as one wished.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
