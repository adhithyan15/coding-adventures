Kannada, chapter 415: Suspensions and Exemptions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಮಾನತು (amānatu) — suspension.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to fix, then the Kannada for to restrict.

You'll want to know: ಅಮಾನತು.
ಅಮಾನತು — amānatu — "suspension".
ಲಂಚ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಯನ್ನು ಅಮಾನತು ಮಾಡಿದರು. lañca tegedukoṇḍa adhikāriyannu amānatu māḍidaru. The official who took a bribe was suspended.
ಅಮಾನತು (amānatu) ಮಾಡು, to suspend from a job.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಮಾನತು (amānatu) is usually traced to Arabic amānat, "a trust, a deposit", through Hindustani: a post held in trust, not used.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹೊರಗಿಡು (horagiḍu) — to exclude; to leave out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to restrict, then the Kannada for suspension.

You'll want to know: ಹೊರಗಿಡು.
ಹೊರಗಿಡು — horagiḍu — "to exclude; to leave out".
ಗಾಯದಿಂದ ಅವನನ್ನು ತಂಡದಿಂದ ಹೊರಗಿಟ್ಟರು. gāyadinda avanannu taṇḍadinda horagiṭṭaru. Because of an injury they left him out of the team.
Past (I …): ಹೊರಗಿಟ್ಟೆ, horagiṭṭe.
What is left out takes -ಅನ್ನು, the group -ಇಂದ.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊರಗಿಡು (horagiḍu) is native Dravidian, built from horage, "outside" (DEDR 4333), and iḍu, "to put" (DEDR 442).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಂಜೂರು ಮಾಡು (mañjūru māḍu) — to sanction; to grant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to exclude, then the Kannada for an exemption.

You'll want to know: ಮಂಜೂರು ಮಾಡು.
ಮಂಜೂರು ಮಾಡು — mañjūru māḍu — "to sanction; to grant".
ಬ್ಯಾಂಕ್ ನನಗೆ ಸಾಲ ಮಂಜೂರು ಮಾಡಿತು. byāṅk nanage sāla mañjūru māḍitu. The bank granted me a loan.
Past (I …): ಮಂಜೂರು ಮಾಡಿದೆ, mañjūru māḍide.
ಮಂಜೂರಾಗು, to be sanctioned.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಂಜೂರು ಮಾಡು (mañjūru māḍu) is built from mañjūru, a loan from Hindustani manzūr, "approved", which comes from Arabic, with māḍu, "to do".
