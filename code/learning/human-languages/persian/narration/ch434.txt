Persian, chapter 434: Underground and buses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پله برقی (pelle-barqi) — an escalator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to meet, then the Persian for to hurry.

You'll want to know: پله برقی.
پله برقی (pelle-barqi) — "an escalator".
در ایستگاه مترو پله برقی خراب بود. Dar istgâh-e metro pelle-barqi kharâb bud. The escalator at the metro station was out of order.
Literally 'electric stairs': pelle 'step' + barqi 'electric'.

The word, taken apart.
pelle-barqi is built from pelle 'step, stair', whose origin is not settled, and barqi 'electric', from barq 'electricity', borrowed from Arabic barq 'lightning, flashing' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
مسیر (masir) — a route, a way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to hurry, then the Persian for an escalator.

You'll want to know: مسیر.
مسیر (masir) — "a route, a way".
مسیر (masir) این اتوبوس خیلی طولانی است. Masir-e in otobus kheyli tulâni ast. This bus's route is very long.
Dar masir — on the way, en route.

The word, taken apart.
masir was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic masīr 'going, the way of a journey'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
جا گرفتن (jâ gereftan) — to get a seat; to take up room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a route, then the Persian for a fare.

You'll want to know: جا گرفتن.
جا گرفتن (jâ gereftan) — "to get a seat; to take up room".
در مترو جا گرفتم و نشستم. Dar metro jâ gereftam va neshastam. In the metro I got a seat and sat down.
jâ 'place' + gereftan 'to take'. Barâ-ye kasi jâ gereftan — to save someone a seat.

The word, taken apart.
jâ gereftan is built from jâ 'place', inherited from Middle Persian gyāg (EtymDB), and the native verb gereftan 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian grab- 'to seize'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
جا ماندن (jâ mândan) — to miss (a bus, a train); to be left behind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fare, then the Persian for to get a seat.

You'll want to know: جا ماندن.
جا ماندن (jâ mândan) — "to miss (a bus, a train); to be left behind".
دیر رسیدم و از اتوبوس جا ماندم. Dir residam va az otobus jâ mândam. I arrived late and missed the bus.
jâ 'place' + mândan 'to stay'. Things can be left behind too: kifam dar tâksi jâ mând.

The word, taken apart.
jâ mândan is built from jâ 'place', inherited from Middle Persian gyāg (EtymDB), and mândan 'to remain', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian: to stay behind in one's place.
