Russian, chapter 455: Paying at the till.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
расплачиваться (raspláchivatsya) — to settle up, to pay (the bill).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to haggle, then the Russian for to overpay.

You'll want to know: расплачиваться.
расплачиваться (raspláchivatsya) — "to settle up, to pay (the bill)".
Мы расплачиваемся и уходим. My raspláchivayemsya i ukhódim. We settle up and leave.
Imperfective; the perfective is расплатиться. Takes за + accusative: расплатиться за ужин.

The word, taken apart.
расплачиваться (raspláchivatsya) is built from рас-, -плачивать, from платить 'to pay', and -ся. EtymDB takes платить from Proto-Slavic platiti.

Lesson 2 of 5.
оплата (opláta) — payment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to overpay, then the Russian for to settle up.

You'll want to know: оплата.
оплата (opláta) — "payment".
Оплата только наличными. Opláta tólko nalíchnymi. Payment is in cash only.
Feminine. Оплата картой — payment by card.

The word, taken apart.
оплата (opláta) is built from о- and плата 'pay', the root of платить 'to pay', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic platiti.

Lesson 3 of 5.
карточка (kártochka) — a bank card; a small card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to settle up, then the Russian for payment.

You'll want to know: карточка.
карточка (kártochka) — "a bank card; a small card".
Можно заплатить карточкой? Mózhno zaplatít kártochkoy? Can I pay by card?
Feminine; a diminutive of карта. In speech карточка (kártochka) is a bank card.

The word, taken apart.
карточка (kártochka) is a diminutive of карта 'a card', which EtymDB traces through Polish karta and German Karte; the source is Latin charta 'paper' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
разменивать (razménivat) — to break (a note), to change (money into smaller notes).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for payment, then the Russian for a bank card.

You'll want to know: разменивать.
разменивать (razménivat) — "to break (a note), to change (money into smaller notes)".
В кассе не разменивают крупные деньги. V kásse ne razménivayut krúpnyye déngi. They do not change large notes at the till.
Imperfective; perfective разменять. Разменять тысячу — to break a thousand-rouble note.

The word, taken apart.
разменивать (razménivat) is built from раз- and -менивать, from менять 'to change', on мена 'an exchange', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic měna (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
обсчитывать (obschítyvat) — to shortchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bank card, then the Russian for to break.

You'll want to know: обсчитывать.
обсчитывать (obschítyvat) — "to shortchange".
Этот продавец часто обсчитывает покупателей. Étot prodavéts chásto obschítyvayet pokupáteley. This seller often shortchanges customers.
Imperfective; perfective обсчитать.

The word, taken apart.
обсчитывать (obschítyvat) is built from об- and -считывать, from считать 'to count': to count to someone's loss. EtymDB lists считать without a deeper source.
