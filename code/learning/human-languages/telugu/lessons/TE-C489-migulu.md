## You'll want to know: మిగులు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C489-KALTI489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మిగులు** — *migulu* — "to be left over".

> పెళ్ళి తరువాత చాలా భోజనం మిగిలింది.
>
> *peḷḷi taruvāta cālā bhōjanaṁ migilindi.*
>
> After the wedding a lot of food was left over.

Past (I …): మిగిలాను, *migilānu*.

What is left takes no ending; the one who keeps it takes -కి: నాకు వంద రూపాయలు మిగిలాయి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మిగులు** (*migulu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4838 lists Telugu *migulu*, "to remain, to be left over, to be saved", with Tamil *miku*, "to exceed".
