## You'll want to know: سیٹی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C386-DABBA386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سیٹی** — *sīṭī* — "a whistle".

> میں نے صبح انجن کی سیٹی سنی۔
>
> *maiṅ ne subah injan kī sīṭī sunī.*
>
> In the morning I heard the engine's whistle.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سیٹی** (*sīṭī*) is inherited from *sīṭṭa*, "a whistle", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as an imitation of the sound (CDIAL 13427).
