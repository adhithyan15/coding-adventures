# ج (jim) — the letter that ends پنج

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-DAL-01, FA-SCRIPT-C23-HE-JIMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write د and ح once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **پنج** (*panj*), "five".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-JIM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ج** — *jim*, the sound *j*.

It ends **پنج** (*panj*), "five". It is **ح**, from the last lesson, with **one dot
below** the head.

## Writing: ج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-JIM-01] -->

Put your pen on ج and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
