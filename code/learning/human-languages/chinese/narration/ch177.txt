Mandarin Chinese, chapter 177: Full Already.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
tuījiàn — to recommend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to topple over, then the Mandarin for to catch fire.

You'll want to know: tuījiàn.
tuījiàn — "to recommend".
fúwùyuán nǐ tuījiàn shénme cài — Waiter, which dish do you recommend?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tuījiàn is built from tuī 'to push' and jiàn 'to put forward', both inherited from Middle Chinese, thwoj and tsenH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to present' and 'to put forward'.

What you've built.
One more word for asking for what I want.

Lesson 2 of 5.
dòufu — tofu; bean curd.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to catch fire, then the Mandarin for to recommend.

You'll want to know: dòufu.
dòufu — "tofu; bean curd".
wǒmen yào yí fèn dòufu — One portion of tofu for us, please.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dòufu is built from dòu 'beans' and fu, both inherited from Middle Chinese, duwH and bjuX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'rotten' and 'broken down': beans broken down into curd. In the word the second loses its tone.

Lesson 5 of 5.
nánchī — tasting bad; horrible to eat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for full after eating, then the Mandarin for fresh.

You'll want to know: nánchī.
nánchī — "tasting bad; horrible to eat".
zhège cài bù xīnxiān hěn nánchī — This dish isn't fresh, and it tastes horrible.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
nánchī is built from nán 'hard' and 吃 'to eat': hard to eat. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese nan (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); for the second the dictionary has khek, glossed 'to eat', under another way of writing it, though khek would regularly give qì.

What you've built.
One more word for asking for what I want.
