Lesson 1 of 6.
cǎifǎng — to interview someone for the news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the presenter of a show, then the Mandarin for what everyone is talking about.

You'll want to know: cǎifǎng.
cǎifǎng — "to interview someone for the news".
jìzhě cǎifǎng le wǒmen — A reporter interviewed us.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Two third tones meet in this word: cǎi is said with a rising tone before fǎng, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
cǎifǎng is built from cǎi 'to pick' and fǎng 'to ask about', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tshojX and phjangH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to take' and the second 'to consult': to gather news by asking. phjangH would regularly give fàng; the dipping tone of fǎng is irregular.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.

Lesson 2 of 6.
发明 (fāmíng) — to invent; an invention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for what everyone is talking about, then the Mandarin for to interview someone for the news.

You'll want to know: 发明.
发明 — fāmíng — "to invent; an invention".
他发明了一个小东西 tā fāmíng le yí ge xiǎo dōngxi — He invented a little gadget.
The new character, in pieces: 发 (fā) — five strokes in three small groups: a short stroke that drops and turns to the right, and a long sweep down to the left from the top; then a fork of two strokes crossing below; then a dot at the top right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
发明 (fāmíng) is built from 发 (fā) 'to send out' and 明 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjot and mjaeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to start' and 'to make clear' and the second 'light' and 'clear': to bring something to light.

What you've built.

Script — a bent stroke, a sweep, a fork and a dot.
发 (fā).
One shape, three small groups. Count the groups, not the strokes.
The frame, two strokes. A short stroke that drops and turns to run across to the right, rising a little. Then a long sweep from the top middle, down to the lower left, crossing it.
The fork, two strokes. A bar that turns into a sweep down to the lower left. Then a long stroke falling to the right from under the bar, out to the foot.
The dot, one stroke, high on the right.
2 + 2 + 1 = five.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 发 (fā), all five, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 发 (fā)? (Five.) Which stroke comes first? (The short one that drops and turns right.) What comes last? (The dot, high on the right.)
