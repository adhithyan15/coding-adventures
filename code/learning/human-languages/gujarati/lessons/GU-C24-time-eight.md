**Listening — meaning from sound.** Eight items, shuffled, once through.

**Speaking — sound from meaning.** Six items from English cues, no Gujarati
model in view.

**Reading — a cold pair.** Only the two words this chapter put on the page are
read; the other six stay oral until a later chapter writes them.
