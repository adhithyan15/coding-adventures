## You'll want to know: отвлекаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C384-UCHYOBA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**отвлекаться** (*otvlekátsya*) — "to get distracted".

> Когда я работаю, я часто отвлекаюсь на телефон.
>
> *Kogdá ya rabótayu, ya chásto otvlekáyus na telefón.*
>
> When I work, I often get distracted by my phone.

Imperfective; perfective *отвлечься*. *Отвлекаться на* + accusative — to be distracted by.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**отвлекаться** is built from *от-* "away" and *влечь* "to drag, to pull", an inherited Slavic verb: to be pulled away from your work.
