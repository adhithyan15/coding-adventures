## You'll want to know: जान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C491-DAKHIL491-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जान** — *jān* — "a life; the life in a body".

> डाक्टरों ने बच्चे की जान बचाई।
>
> *ḍākṭaroṁ ne bacce kī jān bacāī.*
>
> The doctors saved the child's life.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जान** (*jān*) is usually traced to Persian *jān*, "life, soul". *jān bacānā* is to save a life; *jīvan* is life as a span of years, *jān* the life in a body.
