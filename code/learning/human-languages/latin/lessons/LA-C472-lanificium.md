## You'll want to know: lānificium, lānificiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C472-COLUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**lānificium, lānificiī** — "wool-working, spinning and weaving".

> Lānificium in vīllā labor fēminārum est.
>
> *Wool-working in the house is women's work.*

Second declension, neuter: *lānificium, lānificiī n.*, from *lāna* 'wool' and *faciō* 'to make'. A Roman tomb often praised a woman for it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lānificium* is built on *lānificus* 'wool-working', from *lāna* 'wool' and *faciō* 'to make' (Lewis & Short).
