## You'll want to know: छलम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C335-MAYA335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छलम्** — *chalam* — "a trick; deceit".

> छलेन सः जनान् वञ्चयति।
>
> *chalena saḥ janān vañcayati.*
>
> By a trick he cheats people.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छलम्** (*chalam*) comes from the root √skhal, "to stumble", as MW explains it, as in *skhalati*; MW gives *chala* as fraud, deceit, sham, guise, a trick (MW).
