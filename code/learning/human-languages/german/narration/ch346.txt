German, chapter 346: Wozu?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Zweck — the purpose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to back up, then the German for to prove.

You'll want to know: der Zweck.
der Zweck — "the purpose".
Der Zweck der Reise war ein Besuch bei meiner Tante. The purpose of the trip was a visit to my aunt.
Plural: die Zwecke. Das hat keinen Zweck — there is no point.

The word, taken apart.
Zweck is inherited from Old High German zwec (EtymDB), a word for a wooden peg or nail. It is usually explained as the peg in the middle of a target: the thing you aim at.

Lesson 3 of 5.
damit — in order that, so that; with it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the purpose, then the German for the intention.

You'll want to know: damit.
damit — "in order that, so that; with it".
Ich spreche langsam, damit alle mich verstehen. I speak slowly so that everyone understands me.
As a conjunction damit sends the verb to the end and names an aim. As an adverb it means with it: Was machst du damit?

The word, taken apart.
damit joins da "there" and mit "with", from Old High German miti (EtymDB). It first meant "with that"; the sense "so that" grew out of it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Anlass — the occasion; the cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for in order that, then the German for on purpose.

You'll want to know: der Anlass.
der Anlass — "the occasion; the cause".
Zu diesem Anlass trägt er einen Anzug. For this occasion he is wearing a suit.
Plural: die Anlässe. Aus diesem Anlass — on this occasion, for this reason. In Switzerland der Anlass is also an event.

The word, taken apart.
Anlass is built from an and lassen "to let, to let go", inherited from Old High German lāzan and Proto-Germanic lētaną (EtymDB): something that lets events start.
