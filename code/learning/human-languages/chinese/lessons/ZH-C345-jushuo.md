## You'll want to know: jùshuō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C345-LOOKBACK345-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jùshuō* — "it is said that; reportedly".

> *jùshuō zhège chuánshuō yǒu liǎngqiān nián le* — They say this legend is two thousand years old.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jùshuō* is built from *jù* 'to rely on' and 说 'to say', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjoH* and *sywet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to lean on' and 'to cite'.
