## You'll want to know: ದವಡೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C510-NOVU510-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ದವಡೆ** — *davaḍe* — "the jaw".

> ಆನೆಯ ದವಡೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಬಲವಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *āneya davaḍe tumbā balavāgide.*
>
> An elephant's jaw is very strong.

ದವಡೆ ಹಲ್ಲು, a molar, a jaw tooth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದವಡೆ** (*davaḍe*) is a native Kannada word for the jaw; its origin is not settled.
