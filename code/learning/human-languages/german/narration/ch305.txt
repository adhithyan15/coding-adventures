German, chapter 305: Die Feier.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Feier — the celebration, the party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the widow, then the German for to inherit.

You'll want to know: die Feier.
die Feier — "the celebration, the party".
Die Feier ging bis Mitternacht. The celebration went on until midnight.
Plural: die Feiern. The verb is feiern.

The word, taken apart.
Feier comes from Middle High German vire, Old High German fīra, which came from Latin fēriae "holidays, feast days" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Feiertag — the public holiday.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for the celebration, then the German for solemn.

You'll want to know: der Feiertag.
der Feiertag — "the public holiday".
Am Feiertag sind alle Geschäfte geschlossen. On the public holiday all the shops are closed.
Plural: die Feiertage. Frohe Feiertage! — happy holidays!

The word, taken apart.
Feiertag joins Feier, from Latin fēriae "holidays" (EtymDB), and the inherited Tag "day".

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Zeremonie — the ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for solemn, then the German for the public holiday.

You'll want to know: die Zeremonie.
die Zeremonie — "the ceremony".
Die Zeremonie dauerte eine Stunde. The ceremony lasted an hour.
Plural: die Zeremonien. Stress at the end: Ze-re-mo-NIE.

The word, taken apart.
Zeremonie is a learned borrowing from Latin caerimōnia "a sacred rite" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
einweihen — to open officially, to inaugurate; to let in on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the public holiday, then the German for the ceremony.

You'll want to know: einweihen.
einweihen — "to open officially, to inaugurate; to let in on".
Am Sonntag weihte die Stadt die neue Brücke ein. On Sunday the town officially opened the new bridge.
Separable: sie weihen ein. Also to let someone in on a secret: jemanden einweihen.

The word, taken apart.
einweihen is built on weihen "to make holy", Middle High German wīhen, from Proto-Germanic wīhijaną "to hallow" (EtymDB).
