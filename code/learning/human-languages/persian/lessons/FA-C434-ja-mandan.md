## You'll want to know: جا ماندن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C434-PELLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جا ماندن** (*jâ mândan*) — "to miss (a bus, a train); to be left behind".

> دیر رسیدم و از اتوبوس جا ماندم.
>
> *Dir residam va az otobus jâ mândam.*
>
> I arrived late and missed the bus.

*jâ* 'place' + *mândan* 'to stay'. Things can be left behind too: *kifam dar tâksi jâ mând*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jâ mândan* is built from *jâ* 'place', inherited from Middle Persian *gyāg* (EtymDB), and *mândan* 'to remain', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian: to stay behind in one's place.
