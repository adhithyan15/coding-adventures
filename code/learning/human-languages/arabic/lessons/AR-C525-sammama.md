## You'll want to know: صمم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C525-TASMIM525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صمم** — *ṣammama* — "to design".

> صممت أختي فستان العروس.
>
> *ṣammamat ukhtī fustān al-ʿarūs.*
>
> My sister designed the bride's dress.

Present: يصمم, *yuṣammim*.

With على it means to be determined: صمم على السفر.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صمم** (*ṣammama*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ṣ-m-m*, "to be solid, closed up". Its older sense is to be firmly resolved; the sense of designing is modern.
