## You'll want to know: a tarefa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C359-DEPARTAMENTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a tarefa** — "the task, the job (to be done)".

> Hoje tenho três tarefas importantes.
>
> *Today I have three important tasks.*

*Tarefas domésticas* — housework.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tarefa* was borrowed from Arabic *ṭarīḥa* "endeavour, a task set" (EtymDB); Spanish *tarea* has the same source.
