## You'll want to know: le trac
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C515-SCENE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le trac** — "stage fright, nerves".

> Avant chaque concert, elle a le trac.
>
> *Before every concert she gets stage fright.*

*Avoir le trac* — to have nerves, also before an exam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *le trac* is not settled; it appeared in French in the nineteenth century.
