## You'll want to know: خزانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C327-KHAZANA327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خزانہ** — *khazānā* — "a treasure; a treasury".

> زمین کے نیچے ایک خزانہ تھا۔
>
> *zamīn ke nīche ek khazānā thā.*
>
> There was a treasure under the ground.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خزانہ** (*khazānā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khizāna*, "a storehouse". **خزانچی**, a cashier, is the one who keeps it.
