## You'll want to know: صوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C378-INTIKHAB378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صوت** — *ṣawwata* — "to vote".

> صوتت أمي في الصباح الباكر.
>
> *ṣawwatat ummī fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ al-bākir.*
>
> My mother voted early in the morning.

Present: يصوت, *yuṣawwit*.

صوت لـ, voted for. The noun صوت, *ṣawt*, voice, also means a vote.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صوت** (*ṣawwata*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-w-t*, "to make a sound" (Lane). It is Form II, to raise one's voice: to cast a vote.
