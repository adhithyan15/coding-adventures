6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
કફ (kaph) — phlegm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to throw up, then the Gujarati for to stomach.

You'll want to know: કફ.
કફ — kaph — "phlegm".
શરદીને લીધે મારી છાતીમાં કફ છે. śardīne līdhe mārī chātīmā̃ kaph che. My chest is full of phlegm because of the cold.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કફ (kaph) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kapha, "phlegm", one of the three humours of the old medicine, as Monier-Williams records it.
