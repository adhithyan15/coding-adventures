## You'll want to know: はか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C291-SOUSHIKI291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はか** — *haka* — "a grave".

> **まいとし そふの はかに いきます。** — *maitoshi sofu no haka ni ikimasu* — "Every year we visit grandfather's grave."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はか** (*haka*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
