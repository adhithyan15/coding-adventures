## You'll want to know: संसद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C353-SHASAN353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संसद** — *saṁsad* — "parliament".

> संसद में आज नए क़ानून पर बहस हुई।
>
> *saṁsad meṁ āj nae qānūn par bahas huī.*
>
> Parliament debated the new law today.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संसद** (*saṁsad*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃsad*, "an assembly" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
