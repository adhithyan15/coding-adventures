## You'll want to know: culpa, culpae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C311-DAMNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**culpa, culpae** — "fault, blame; guilt".

> Nōn mea culpa erat, sed tua.
>
> *It was not my fault, but yours.*

Feminine. *In culpā esse*: to be to blame. English *culpable* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**culpa** is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*kʷolpā*, "a wrong, a mistake" (EtymDB). English *culpable* and *culprit* come from its family.
