## You'll want to know: el rencor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C708-CELOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el rencor** — "resentment, grudge".

> No le guardo rencor.
>
> *I do not hold a grudge against him.*

*Guardar rencor* — to bear a grudge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rencor* comes from Old Spanish *rancor*, from Latin *rancor* "a rancid smell, a grudge" (EtymDB); English *rancour* is the same word.
