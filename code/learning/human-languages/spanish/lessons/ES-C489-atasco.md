## You'll want to know: el atasco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C489-CIUDAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el atasco** — "the traffic jam".

> Hay un atasco a la entrada de la ciudad.
>
> *There is a traffic jam at the entrance to the city.*

In much of Latin America people say *el embotellamiento* or *el tráfico*.
