## You'll want to know: wèikǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C477-INSIDE477-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wèikǒu* — "appetite".

> *wǒ bìng le sān tiān wèikǒu bù hǎo* — I've been ill for three days and have no appetite.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wèikǒu* is built from *wèi* 'the stomach' and 口 'mouth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjw+jH* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary explains the first, after an older book, as the storehouse of grain.
