## You'll want to know: cōnstāns, cōnstantis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C509-FORT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnstāns, cōnstantis** — "steadfast, consistent".

> Amīcus cōnstāns in perīculīs nōn fugit.
>
> *A steadfast friend does not run away in danger.*

One ending. The present participle of *cōnstō* — to stand firm. English *constant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnstāns** is the present participle of *cōnstō* "to stand firm", built on *con-* and *stō* "to stand" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *constant* comes from it.
