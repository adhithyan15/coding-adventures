# അസ്വസ്ഥത (asvasthata) — unease; restlessness

## You'll want to know: അസ്വസ്ഥത
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C520-ANUKAMPA520-04]; assesses=[] -->

**അസ്വസ്ഥത** — *asvasthata* — "unease; restlessness".

> ചൂട് കാരണം രാത്രി മുഴുവൻ എനിക്ക് അസ്വസ്ഥത തോന്നി.
>
> *cūṭŭ kāraṇaṁ rātri muḻuvan enikkŭ asvasthata tōnni.*
>
> Because of the heat I felt restless all night.

From അസ്വസ്ഥം, not at ease, with -ത; അ- turns a Sanskrit word into its opposite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അസ്വസ്ഥത** (*asvasthata*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *asvasthatā*, "illness" (Monier-Williams), the opposite of *svastha*, "being in oneself, well".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
