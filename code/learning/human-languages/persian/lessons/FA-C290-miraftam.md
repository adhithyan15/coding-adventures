# میرفتم (miraftam) — the past that is still running

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-MI-01, FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm going", then "I went". (*Miravam*, *raftam*.)

[PAUSE 1s] *Raftam* has an edge: the going happened, and it is over. Persian
has a second past with no edge on it, and a story needs both.

## Grammar Lens: mi- on the past stem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-PAST-CONTINUOUS-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C49-SCHOOL49-03, FA-GRAMMAR-NEGATIVE-NE-01] -->

Put the *mi-* of the present onto the past stem, and keep the past endings:

> mi- + raft + -am → **میرفتم** — *miraftam* — I was going; I used to go

So *mikhândam*, I was reading; *mikhordam*, I was eating; *nemiraftam*, I
wasn't going. "She" again takes no ending: *miraft*, she was going.

It answers two English shapes. "Was ‑ing": the action was under way.
"Used to": it happened again and again.

> **هر روز به مدرسه میرفتم.** — *har ruz be madrese miraftam* — Every day I
> used to go to school.

*Budan* normally takes no *mi-* in the past. *Bud* is already the background: **هوا
سرد بود**, *havâ sard bud*, it was cold.

## Grammar Lens: the scene, and the events
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-SCENE-EVENT-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C159-CHERA159-04, FA-LEX-C110-THINGS110-02, FA-LEX-C258-KONESH258-02, FA-LEX-C184-KONESH184-04, FA-LEX-KHORSHID] -->

A told story splits its verbs in two. The *mi-* past paints the **scene**:
what was going on, what things were like. The plain past drops the
**events** into it: what happened next.

> **در حالی که کتاب میخواندم، تلفن زنگ زد.** — *dar hâli ke ketâb mikhândam,
> telefon zang zad* — While I was reading a book, the phone rang.

The reading is scene, still running when the ring cuts into it. The ring is
an event, over in a moment. *Dar hâli ke*, "while", is the joint between them.

> **هوا سرد بود و خورشید میتابید.** — *havâ sard bud va khorshid mitâbid* — It
> was cold, and the sun was shining.

Two pieces of scene, and nothing has happened yet. That is how a story opens.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-PAST-CONTINUOUS-01, FA-GRAMMAR-SCENE-EVENT-01, FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] How do you build "I was reading"? (*Mi-* on the past stem:
***mikhândam***.) In the phone sentence, which verb is the scene? (***Mikhândam***,
still running.) Which is the event? (***Zang zad***, over in a moment.) Why is
there no *mi-* in *havâ sard bud*? (*Budan* takes none: ***bud*** is already
background.)
