## You'll want to know: o disfarce
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C321-CARNAVAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o disfarce** — "the disguise; a fancy-dress outfit".

> Fiz o meu disfarce com papel e cola.
>
> *I made my fancy-dress outfit out of paper and glue.*

For carnival, people also say *a fantasia* in Brazil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disfarce* is built on *disfarçar* "to disguise"; the origin of the verb is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry.
