## You'll want to know: تفتیش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C282-TAFTISH282-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تفتیش** — *taftīsh* — "an investigation".

> تفتیش میں ایک مہینہ لگا۔
>
> *taftīsh meṅ ek mahīnā lagā.*
>
> The investigation took a month.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفتیش** (*taftīsh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taftīsh*, "a searching, an inspection". It is the word of police reports and the news.
