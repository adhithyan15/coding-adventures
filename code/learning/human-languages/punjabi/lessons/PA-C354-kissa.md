## You'll want to know: ਕਿੱਸਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C354-STORY354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿੱਸਾ** — *kissā* — "a tale; a long story".

> ਦਾਦਾ ਇੱਕ ਲੰਮਾ ਕਿੱਸਾ ਸੁਣਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādā ikk lammā kissā suṇāundā hai.*
>
> Grandfather tells a long tale.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿੱਸਾ** (*kissā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *qiṣṣa*, "story".
