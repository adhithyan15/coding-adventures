## You'll want to know: रोमाञ्चः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C449-VISMAYA449-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रोमाञ्चः** — *romāñcaḥ* — "a thrill; the hair standing on end".

> सिंहस्य गर्जनेन मम रोमाञ्चः भवति।
>
> *siṃhasya garjanena mama romāñcaḥ bhavati.*
>
> The lion's roar gives me a thrill.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोमाञ्चः** (*romāñcaḥ*) is built from *roman*, "the hair of the body", and *añca*, from the root √añc, "to bend, to rise"; MW gives "thrill of the hair" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
