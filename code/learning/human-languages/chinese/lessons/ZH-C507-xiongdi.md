## You'll want to know: xiōngdì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C507-FATHER507-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xiōngdì* — "brothers".

> *xiōngdì liǎng ge gēge bǐ dìdi gāo* — Of the two brothers, the elder is taller.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiōngdì* is built from *xiōng* 'an elder brother' and *dì* 'a younger brother', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xjwaeng* and *dejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older dictionary, 'a man born first' and the second 'a man born after'.
