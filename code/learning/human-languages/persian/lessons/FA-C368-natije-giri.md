## You'll want to know: نتیجه گیری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C368-TAHLIL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نتیجه گیری** (*natije giri*) — "a conclusion (drawn)".

> نتیجه گیری او منطقی بود.
>
> *natije giri-ye u manteqi bud.*
>
> His conclusion was logical.

*Natije* "result" + *giri* "taking", from *gereftan*. Also the last part of an essay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نتیجه گیری** joins *natije*, from Arabic *natīja* "result" (EtymDB), to *giri* "taking", built on *gir-*, the present stem of the inherited Persian verb *gereftan* "to take".
