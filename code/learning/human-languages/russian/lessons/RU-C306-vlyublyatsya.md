## You'll want to know: влюбляться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C306-POMOLVKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**влюбляться** (*vlyublyátsya*) — "to fall in love".

> Он влюбился в неё с первого взгляда.
>
> *On vlyubílsya v neyó s pérvovo vzglyáda.*
>
> He fell in love with her at first sight.

Imperfective; perfective *влюбиться* (*vlyubítsya*), *влюблюсь*. It takes *в* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*влюбляться* is built on *в-* "into" and *любить* "to love", inherited from Proto-Slavic *ľubiti*, from PIE *lewbʰ-* (EtymDB); English *love* is a cousin.
