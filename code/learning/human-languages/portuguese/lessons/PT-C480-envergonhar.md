## You'll want to know: envergonhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C480-REMORSO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**envergonhar** — "to embarrass, to shame".

> As notas más envergonharam o rapaz.
>
> *The bad marks made the boy ashamed.*

*Envergonhar-se* — to be ashamed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*envergonhar* is built on *vergonha* "shame", which is inherited from Latin *verēcundia* "modesty, shame" (Lewis & Short).
