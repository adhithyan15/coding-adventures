Kannada, chapter 467: At the Market.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ (mārukaṭṭe) — a marketplace; a market.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a streetlight, then the Kannada for a drain.

You'll want to know: ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ.
ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ — mārukaṭṭe — "a marketplace; a market".
ಭಾನುವಾರ ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಜನ ಇರುತ್ತಾರೆ. bhānuvāra mārukaṭṭeyalli tumbā jana iruttāre. On Sundays there are a lot of people in the market.
ಸಂತೆ is a weekly fair; ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ (mārukaṭṭe) is a standing market.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ (mārukaṭṭe) is a native Kannada compound of māṟu, "to sell, to barter" (DEDR 4834), and ಕಟ್ಟೆ, "a raised platform".

You'll want to know: ಪೇಟೆ.
ಪೇಟೆ — pēṭe — "a bazaar; a market street".
ಹಳೆಯ ಪೇಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಅಗ್ಗವಾಗಿ ಸಿಗುತ್ತದೆ. haḷeya pēṭeyalli baṭṭe aggavāgi siguttade. Cloth is cheap in the old bazaar.
Also the market quarter of a town: ಪೇಟೆಗೆ ಹೋಗು, to go into town.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೇಟೆ (pēṭe) is a Kannada word for a market street whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮಳಿಗೆ (maḷige) — a store; a shop unit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a marketplace, then the Kannada for a bazaar.

You'll want to know: ಮಳಿಗೆ.
ಮಳಿಗೆ — maḷige — "a store; a shop unit".
ಈ ಬೀದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಟ್ಟೆಯ ಮಳಿಗೆಗಳು ಇವೆ. ī bīdiyalli baṭṭeya maḷigegaḷu ive. There are clothes stores on this street.
Plural: ಮಳಿಗೆಗಳು, maḷigegaḷu.
A large shop or a showroom; ಅಂಗಡಿ is any shop.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಳಿಗೆ (maḷige) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4757 lists Kannada maḷige, "a warehouse, a shop".

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಮಾರಾಟ.
ಮಾರಾಟ — mārāṭa — "a sale; selling".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ಮುಂಚೆ ಸೀರೆಗಳ ಮಾರಾಟ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗುತ್ತದೆ. habbada muñce sīregaḷa mārāṭa heccāguttade. Before a festival the sale of saris goes up.
ಮಾರಾಟಕ್ಕೆ ಇದೆ, it is for sale; ಮಾರಾಟಗಾರ, a seller.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾರಾಟ (mārāṭa) is native Dravidian, a noun built on māṟu, "to sell, to barter" (DEDR 4834).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ (koṇḍuko) — to buy for oneself; to purchase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a store, then the Kannada for a sale.

You'll want to know: ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ.
ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ — koṇḍuko — "to buy for oneself; to purchase".
ನಾನು ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೊಸ ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ ಕೊಂಡುಕೊಂಡೆ. nānu mārukaṭṭeyalli hosa cappali koṇḍukoṇḍe. I bought new sandals at the market.
Past (I …): ಕೊಂಡುಕೊಂಡೆ, koṇḍukoṇḍe.
ಕೊಂಡು is the past stem of ಕೊಳ್ಳು, to take or buy; ಕೊ adds for oneself.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ (koṇḍuko) is native Dravidian: koṇḍu, the past stem of koḷ, koḷḷu, "to take, to obtain, to buy" (DEDR 2151), with the reflexive -ko.
