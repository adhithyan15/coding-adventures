## You'll want to know: ਤਰੇੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C295-CRACK295-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਰੇੜ** — *tareṛ* — "a crack".

> ਕੰਧ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਲੰਮੀ ਤਰੇੜ ਹੈ।
>
> *kandh vich ikk lammī tareṛ hai.*
>
> There is a long crack in the wall.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਰੇੜ** (*tareṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*tṛdi*, "crack, fissure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tareṛ*, "crack, crevice", under it (CDIAL 5926).
