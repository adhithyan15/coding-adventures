## You'll want to know: したぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C149-DRESS149-05]; assesses=[] -->

**したぎ** — *shitagi* — "underwear".

> **まいにち したぎを あらいます。** — *mainichi shitagi o araimasu* — "I wash my underwear every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**したぎ** (*shitagi*) is native Japanese, built from *shita*, "under", and *ki*, "wearing", from *kiru*, voiced to *gi*; both parts are inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
