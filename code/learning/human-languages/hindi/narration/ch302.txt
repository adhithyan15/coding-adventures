Hindi, chapter 302: An Announcement.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आपदा (āpdā) — a disaster; a calamity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to melt, then the Hindi for a blister.

You'll want to know: आपदा.
आपदा — āpdā — "a disaster; a calamity".
पिछले साल गाँव में बाढ़ की बड़ी आपदा आई। pichle sāl gā̃v meṁ bāṛh kī baṛī āpdā āī. Last year a great flood disaster struck the village.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
is Sanskrit āpad, "misfortune, distress" (Monier-Williams), with the feminine ending Hindi gives it. It is the word of the news and of government: a flood, a storm or an earthquake is an आपदा (āpdā).

चेतावनी के बाद लोगों को गाँव छोड़ना पड़ा। cetāvnī ke bād logoṁ ko gā̃v choṛnā paṛā. After the warning people had to leave the village.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
चेतावनी (cetāvnī) is built on cetānā, to make someone aware, probably from the Sanskrit family of cetana, "awareness" (Monier-Williams). It is feminine: चेतावनी दी, gave a warning.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ऐलान (ailān) — an announcement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a disaster, then the Hindi for a warning.

You'll want to know: ऐलान.
ऐलान — ailān — "an announcement".
गाँव के मंदिर से ऐलान हुआ कि नदी में पानी ऊपर आ रहा है। gā̃v ke mandir se ailān huā ki nadī meṁ pānī ūpar ā rahā hai. An announcement came from the village temple that the river was rising.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ऐलान (ailān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iʿlān, "a making known". It begins with the letter ऐ, the vowel of ऐसा. ऐलान करना is to announce.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सावधान (sāvdhān) — careful; on guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a warning, then the Hindi for an announcement.

You'll want to know: सावधान.
सावधान — sāvdhān — "careful; on guard".
ऐलान के बाद सब लोग सावधान हो गए। ailān ke bād sab log sāvdhān ho gae. After the announcement everyone was on their guard.

The word, taken apart.
सावधान (sāvdhān) is Sanskrit sāvadhāna, "attentive, heedful" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole. Said alone on a sign it means "Beware!"

Past (it …): वह ख़तरा टल गया, vah khatrā ṭal gayā.

The word, taken apart.
टलना (ṭalnā) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit ṭalati, "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450). A danger that टल गया moved away from you; a meeting that टल गई was put off.
