Lesson 1 of 5.
कलम (kalam) — a clause; a section (of a law).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a proportion, then the Marathi for a level.

You'll want to know: कलम.
कलम — kalam — "a clause; a section (of a law)".
करारातील हे कलम मला मान्य नाही. karārātīl he kalam malā mānya nāhī. I do not accept this clause in the contract.
Neuter.
Neuter: हे कलम (kalam).

The word, taken apart.
कलम (kalam) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic qalam, "pen": an item set down in writing.

Lesson 2 of 5.
वाटप करणे (vāṭap karṇe) — to allocate; to distribute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a level, then the Marathi for a clause.

You'll want to know: वाटप करणे.
वाटप करणे — vāṭap karṇe — "to allocate; to distribute".
संस्थेने पूरग्रस्तांना अन्नाचं वाटप केलं. saṁsthene pūragrastānnā annācaṁ vāṭap kelaṁ. The organization distributed food to the flood victims.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वाटप केलं, tyāne vāṭap kelaṁ.
What is shared out takes -चं: अन्नाचं वाटप.

The word, taken apart.
वाटप करणे (vāṭap karṇe) joins वाटप, "a sharing out", built on the verb वाटणे, "to share out" (CDIAL 11238 lists Marathi vā̃ṭṇẽ under Sanskrit vaṇṭati, "shares"), to करणे.

निवड करताना त्यांनी अनुभवी लोकांना डावललं. nivaḍ kartānā tyānnī anubhvī lokānnā ḍāvallaṁ. While choosing, they passed over experienced people.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने डावललं, tyāne ḍāvallaṁ.
The one passed over takes -ला.

डावलणे (ḍāvalṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनुसरणे (anusarṇe) — to follow (a method).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to allocate, then the Marathi for to pass over.

You'll want to know: अनुसरणे.
अनुसरणे — anusarṇe — "to follow (a method)".
आम्ही शिक्षकांनी सांगितलेली पद्धत अनुसरली. āmhī śikṣakānnī sāṅgitlelī paddhat anusarlī. We followed the method the teacher described.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अनुसरलं, tyāne anusarlaṁ.
Transitive: the past agrees with what is followed, पद्धत अनुसरली.

The word, taken apart.
अनुसरणे (anusarṇe) is a learned verb built on Sanskrit anusarati, "runs to, runs after" (CDIAL 338), with the Marathi ending -णे.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: अंगीकारणे.
अंगीकारणे — aṅgīkārṇe — "to embrace; to adopt".
गावाने स्वच्छतेचा नवा मार्ग अंगीकारला. gāvāne svacchatecā navā mārga aṅgīkārlā. The village embraced a new way of keeping clean.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अंगीकारलं, tyāne aṅgīkārlaṁ.
Formal; अंगीकार, adoption.

The word, taken apart.
अंगीकारणे (aṅgīkārṇe) is built on the tatsama अंगीकार, Sanskrit aṅgīkāra, "agreement, promise" (Monier-Williams), built on aṅga, "limb, body", with the verb ending -णे.
