## You'll want to know: گاؤں
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C293-SAILAB293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گاؤں** — *gāoṅ* — "a village".

> ہمارا گاؤں دریا کے کنارے ہے۔
>
> *hamārā gāoṅ daryā ke kināre hai.*
>
> Our village is on the bank of the river.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گاؤں** (*gāoṅ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *grāma*, "a village" (CDIAL 4368), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gā̃u*. Urdu writes it with **ؤ** and the nasal **ں**.
