## You'll want to know: एयरपोर्ट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C409-UDAN409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**एयरपोर्ट** — *eyarporṭ* — "an airport".

> एयरपोर्ट शहर से काफ़ी दूर है।
>
> *eyarporṭ śahar se kāfī dūr hai.*
>
> The airport is quite far from the city.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एयरपोर्ट** (*eyarporṭ*) is borrowed from English *airport*, the word most people use in speech.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
