## You'll want to know: おやゆび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C443-WRIST443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おやゆび** — *oyayubi* — "a thumb".

> **おやゆびに きずが あります。** — *oyayubi ni kizu ga arimasu* — "There is a cut on my thumb."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おやゆび** (*oyayubi*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *oya*, "parent" and *yubi*, "finger" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
