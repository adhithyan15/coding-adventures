## You'll want to know: 改变
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C392-NEWPLAN392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**改变** — *gǎibiàn* — "to make different (a plan or a habit)".

> *rúguǒ tiānqì bù hǎo wǒmen jiù gǎibiàn jìhuà* — If the weather's bad, we'll change our plans.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**改变** (*gǎibiàn*) is built from 改 'to alter' and 变 'to change', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kojX* and *pjenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to change' and the second 'to transform'.
