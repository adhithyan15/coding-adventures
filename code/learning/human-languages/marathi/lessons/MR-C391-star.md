## You'll want to know: स्तर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C391-GHATNE391-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्तर** — *star* — "a level".

> नदीतील पाण्याचा स्तर वाढला.
>
> *nadītīl pāṇyācā star vāḍhlā.*
>
> The water level in the river rose.

Masculine.

Also a layer, a tier: समाजाचे स्तर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्तर** (*star*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *stara*, "layer" (CDIAL 13685); the same entry lists an inherited Marathi *thar*, "layer".
