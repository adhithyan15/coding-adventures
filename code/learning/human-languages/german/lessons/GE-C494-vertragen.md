## You'll want to know: vertragen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C494-STREIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vertragen** — "to get along (sich vertragen); to tolerate".

> Meine Katze und mein Hund vertragen sich gut.
>
> *My cat and my dog get along well.*

Reflexive for to get along: *sich vertragen*. Without *sich* it means to tolerate: *Ich vertrage keine Milch.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vertragen* is inherited from Middle High German *vertragen* and Old High German *fartragan* "to bear, to stand" (EtymDB), built on *tragen* "to carry", from Proto-Germanic *draganą*.
