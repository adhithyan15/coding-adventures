## You'll want to know: ingrātus, ingrāta, ingrātum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C512-GRAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ingrātus, ingrāta, ingrātum** — "ungrateful; unpleasant".

> Fīlius ingrātus parentēs nōn vīsitat.
>
> *The ungrateful son does not visit his parents.*

*in-* 'not' + *grātus*. English *ingrate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ingrātus** is built on *in-* "not" and *grātus* "pleasing, grateful" (Lewis & Short). English *ingratitude* and *ingrate* come from it.
