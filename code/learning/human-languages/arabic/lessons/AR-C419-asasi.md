## You'll want to know: أساسي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C419-IJABI419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أساسي** — *asāsī* — "basic".

> الخبز طعام أساسي هنا.
>
> *al-khubz ṭaʿām asāsī hunā.*
>
> Bread is a basic food here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أساسي** (*asāsī*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-s-s*, "to lay a foundation" (Lane). *Asāsī* is the adjective of *asās*, a foundation: basic, fundamental.
