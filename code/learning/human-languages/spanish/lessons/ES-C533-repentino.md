## You'll want to know: repentino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C533-CASUAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**repentino** — "sudden".

> El cambio de tiempo fue repentino.
>
> *The change in the weather was sudden.*

*De repente* — suddenly — comes from the same family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*repentino* is a learned borrowing from Latin *repentīnus* 'sudden' (EtymDB), built on *repēns* 'sudden, unexpected'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
