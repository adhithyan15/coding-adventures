# 语 — speech, five, box

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-YAN, ZH-LEX-HANYU-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Air-write 讠, then say *hànyǔ*.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YU-01]; assesses=[] -->

Write **讠** first. On the right, build **五** from top to bottom, then close
**口** at the base. Nine strokes; eight lifts. Keep the speech side narrow so
the right side has room.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-YU-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **语** once. Say **speech, five, box** as your hand crosses the three
parts.
