Bengali, chapter 386: Porters and Luggage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
কুলি (kuli) — a porter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to wait, then the Bengali for a bench.

You'll want to know: কুলি.
কুলি — kuli — "a porter".
কুলি আমার ভারী মালপত্র তুলল। kuli āmār bhārī mālpôtrô tullô. The porter lifted my heavy luggage.
At a big station the porters wear red shirts and carry loads on the head.

The word, taken apart.
কুলি (kuli) has an origin that is not settled: it is often traced to the South Indian word for wages, Tamil kūli (DEDR 1905), so that a porter is someone paid by the job.

Lesson 4 of 5.
নামানো (nāmāno) — to take down; to unload.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a suitcase, then the Bengali for a trolley.

You'll want to know: নামানো.
নামানো — nāmāno — "to take down; to unload".
অরুণ তাক থেকে বাক্সটা নামাল। ôrun tāk theke bāksôṭā nāmālô. Arun took the box down from the shelf.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বাক্সটা নামালেন, tini bāksôṭā nāmālen.
The causing partner of নামা, to get down: you নামো from a bus, and you নামাও a box from a shelf.

The word, taken apart.
নামানো (nāmāno) is the causing form of নামা, "to get down", which CDIAL lists as Bengali nāmā, "to descend", under Sanskrit namrá, "bent, bowed" (CDIAL 6964).

Lesson 5 of 5.
বহন করা (bôhôn kôrā) — to carry (a load); to bear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a trolley, then the Bengali for to unload.

You'll want to know: বহন করা.
বহন করা — bôhôn kôrā — "to carry (a load); to bear".
ট্রলিটা আমাদের সব মালপত্র বহন করল। ṭrôliṭā āmāder sôb mālpôtrô bôhôn kôrlô. The trolley carried all our luggage.
Past (he/she …): সেই ট্রাক মালপত্র বহন করল, sei ṭrāk mālpôtrô bôhôn kôrlô.
A careful word, common in writing and on signs; in talk people say নিয়ে যাওয়া, to take along.

The word, taken apart.
বহন করা (bôhôn kôrā) joins Sanskrit vahana, "bearing, carrying, conveying" (Monier-Williams), to করা, to do.
