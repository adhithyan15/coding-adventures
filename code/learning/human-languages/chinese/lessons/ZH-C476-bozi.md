## You'll want to know: bózi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C476-NECK476-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bózi* — "neck".

> *wǒ kàn le sān ge xiǎoshí diànnǎo bózi hěn téng* — I spent three hours at the computer, and my neck really hurts.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bózi* is built from *bó* and 子, a noun ending, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bwot* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the navel; how it came to mean the neck is not settled.
