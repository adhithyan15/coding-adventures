## Guided Practice: the same ladder, two more fields

- [YOU WRITE: the residence label and a value, with nothing to copy]
- [YOU WRITE: the work label and a value, with nothing to copy]

Both fields climbed the same three rungs the name and language fields did. What
changes between fields is the vocabulary, not the ladder.

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-RESIDENCE-REPAIR-01] -->

**Repair is its own skill, and only the residence field teaches it.** The move is
not *fill the field* — it is *one dimension of a completed field is wrong, change
that dimension and leave the rest standing*.

That is a harder thing than it sounds, and it is the thing a real form actually
demands: a clerk points at one line, you correct one line. A learner who can only
write a field from scratch will rewrite the whole row and introduce two new
errors doing it.

dimension that is wrong.**) Which field in this book taught repair?
