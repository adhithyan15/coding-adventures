Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 202: Turn and Go Back.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मुड़णो (muṛṇo) — to turn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an inn, then the Marwadi for to get in.

You'll want to know: मुड़णो.
मुड़णो — muṛṇo — "to turn".
आप मोड़ माथै मुड़ो। āp moṛ māthai muṛo. Turn at the bend.
Past (he …): वो मुड़्यो, vo muṛyo.

The word, taken apart.
मुड़णो (muṛṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit muṭati, "twists". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi muṛnā, "to be turned" (CDIAL 10186). मोड़, "a bend", belongs to the same family.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पाछो (pāchho) — back again; back the way one came.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to get in, then the Marwadi for to turn.

You'll want to know: पाछो.
पाछो — pāchho — "back again; back the way one came".
हूं काल पाछो आसूं। hū̃ kāl pāchho āsū̃. I'll come back tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
पाछो (pāchho) is inherited from Sanskrit paścāt, "from behind"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pācha, "behind" (CDIAL 7999).

उलटो — ulṭo — "reversed; the wrong way round".
म्हारो कुरतो उलटो है। mhāro kurto ulṭo hai. My kurta is inside out.

The word, taken apart.
उलटो (ulṭo) is inherited from a reconstructed ullaṭati, "turns over"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ulaṭo, "reversed" (CDIAL 2368).
