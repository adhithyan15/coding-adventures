## You'll want to know: o resgate
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C306-RESGATE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o resgate** — "the rescue; the ransom".

> O resgate durou toda a noite.
>
> *The rescue went on all night.*

In a kidnapping, the money: *pedir um resgate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*resgate* was borrowed from Spanish *rescate* (EtymDB), from *rescatar*, which goes back to Vulgar Latin *recaptāre*, from Latin *captāre* "to catch".
