## You'll want to know: yuē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C180-VISIT180-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yuē* — "to arrange to meet; to make a date with".

> *wǒ yuē le péngyou xiàwǔ jiànmiàn* — I've arranged to meet a friend this afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuē* is inherited from Middle Chinese *'jak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to bind' and 'sparing': to bind someone to a time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
