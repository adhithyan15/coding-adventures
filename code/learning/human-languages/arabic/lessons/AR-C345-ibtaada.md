# ابتعد (ibtaʿada) — to move away, to keep away

## You'll want to know: ابتعد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C345-MUWAJAHA345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ابتعد** — *ibtaʿada* — "to move away, to keep away".

> ابتعد الناس عن البيت المحترق.
>
> *ibtaʿada an-nās ʿan al-bayt al-muḥtariq.*
>
> People moved away from the burning house.

Present: يبتعد, *yabtaʿid*.

It takes عن: ابتعد عن.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ابتعد** (*ibtaʿada*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-ʿ-d* (Lane), "to be far", the root of the taught *baʿīd*. It is Form VIII, "to go far off".
