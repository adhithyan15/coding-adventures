## You'll want to know: دف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C518-PLAYING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دف** (*daf*) — "a daf (a frame drum)".

> زنها در جشن دف میزدند.
>
> *zanhâ dar jashn daf mizadand.*
>
> The women were playing frame drums at the celebration.

*daf zadan* — to play the daf.
