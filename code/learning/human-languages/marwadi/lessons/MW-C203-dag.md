## You'll want to know: डग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C203-STEPS203-02]; assesses=[] -->

**डग** — *ḍag* — "a step; a pace".

> मंदिर दस डग आगै है।
>
> *mandir das ḍag āgai hai.*
>
> The temple is ten paces ahead.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डग** (*ḍag*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍag*, "step, pace"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ḍaga*, "step, stride" (CDIAL 5523).
