## You'll want to know: la nostalgia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C477-NOSTALGIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la nostalgia** — "nostalgia, homesickness".

> Quando vivevo all'estero avevo nostalgia di casa.
>
> *When I lived abroad I was homesick.*

*Avere nostalgia di* — to miss (a place, a time).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nostalgia* comes from New Latin *nostalgia* (EtymDB), a modern coinage from Greek *nóstos* "return home" and *álgos* "pain".
