# Four lines, and the comma you cannot hear

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-I-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson asked for writing **finished on time**. This one asks
for writing that **holds together**.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one person who will read it.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-NO-01, RU-LEX-A-01, RU-GRAMMAR-NO-VS-A-01, RU-LEX-POTOMU-CHTO-01, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Connected writing is not **longer** writing. Four short lines can be
connected; one long line can fail to be. What makes it connected is that a
sentence stops standing on its own.

[PAUSE 2s] You hold four joiners. **и** adds. **но** denies. **а** stands one
thing beside another. **потому что** gives the reason.

[PAUSE 2s] And here is the Russian thing to notice. Said aloud, a join is just a
word. Written down, three of these four carry a **mark**: a comma before **но**,
before **а**, and before **потому что** — before **потому**; at this level,
never inside the pair.

[PAUSE 2s] **и** between two halves about the same person takes none.

[PAUSE 2s] So the ear never hears the comma, and the examiner always sees it. It
is the one piece of this lesson a reader can mark wrong without any argument.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-I-01, RU-GRAMMAR-I-TWO-CLAUSES-01, RU-LEX-NO-01, RU-LEX-A-01, RU-GRAMMAR-NO-VS-A-01, RU-LEX-POTOMU-CHTO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-POTOMU-CHTO-CLAUSE-01, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **four lines** by hand to the person you picked.

At least one must use **и**, joining two things the same person does. At
least one must use **но** or **а**. At least one must use **потому что** —
both words, spelled out.

[PAUSE 3s] Use only words you already write. This lesson adds no vocabulary;
the whole exercise is the joining.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01, RU-LEX-I-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Cover your first line and read the second alone. Does it still make
sense without the first? If it does, you wrote a **list** — which is where you
were one lesson ago.

[PAUSE 2s] Then find every joiner and look just in front of it. A comma before
**но**, **а** and **потому**; none before an **и** that joins two things one
person does. Each comma's tail below the line.
