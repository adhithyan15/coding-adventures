Bengali, chapter 57: Carrot, Salt, Butter, Honey, Oil.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গাজর (gājôr) — a carrot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a banana, then the Bengali for a potato.

You'll want to know: গাজর.
গাজর — gājôr — "a carrot".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gājôr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gājôr, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gājôr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for a banana, then the Bengali for a potato, then say gājôr again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does গাজর (gājôr) mean? ("A carrot".) Say it once more.
