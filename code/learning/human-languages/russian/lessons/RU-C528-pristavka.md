## You'll want to know: приставка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C528-GAMING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**приставка** (*pristávka*) — "a games console; a prefix".

> На день рождения сын получил игровую приставку.
>
> *Na den rozhdéniya syn poluchíl igrovúyu pristávku.*
>
> For his birthday my son got a games console.

Feminine. *Игровая приставка* — a games console. In grammar, a prefix.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*приставка* is built on *приставить* 'to put next to, to attach', from *ставить* 'to stand, to put'; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
