# Cover the six old values

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SIX-CUE-01] -->

Look once at the old B and ਖ values: **ਮਨਨ · ਹਿੰਦੀ · ਸ਼ਹਿਰ · ਨੌਕਰੀ**,
then **੨੫ · ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫**. Cover this complete bank and wait ten seconds.

## Writing — delayed entry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-SIX-DELAYED-01]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SIX-CUE-01, PA-FORM-SIX-ORDER-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

With the bank still hidden, fill the six lines for B · ਖ. Recall one
value at a time. Pause after the residence line. Do not uncover the bank
until all six attempts are complete.

> ਨਾਂ: ______
>
> ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: ______
>
> ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: ______
>
> ਕੰਮ: ______
>
> ਉਮਰ: ______
>
> ਫ਼ੋਨ: ______

## Guided Practice — compare after the attempt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SIX-DELAYED-01] -->

Uncover the old bank. Compare only the first differing line. Check its
selection, then spelling or digit order, then the space after the colon.
Correct that line without erasing lines that were already right.
