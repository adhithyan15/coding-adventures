## You'll want to know: おもいだす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C227-THINK227-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいだす** — *omoidasu* — "to recall; to remember".

> **ははは よく なつやすみを おもいだします。** — *haha wa yoku natsuyasumi o omoidashimasu* — "My mother often remembers the summer holidays."

Polite **おもいだします**, *omoidashimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいだす** (*omoidasu*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *omoi*, from *omou*, "to think", and *dasu*, "to take out" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
