# ا and م — finish **سلام** without a cliff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01, AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **س** and **ل**. Add just two shapes, then the spoken word
*salām* becomes readable and writable.

## Script — observe two new shapes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-WRITE-SALAM-03]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01, AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02] -->

- **ا** is *alif*: one clean upright carrying the long *ā* here.
- **م** is *mīm*: a small round head and a tail; it gives the final *m*.

Arabic letters join. In **سلام**, *sīn* reaches into *lām*; *lām* and *alif*
form **لا**; then *alif* forces a pen lift before final *mīm*.

## Writing — guided copy, then delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01, AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02, AR-SCRIPT-WRITE-SALAM-03] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

1. Point right to left: **س · ل · ا · م**. Say *s · l · ā · m*.
2. Trace **سلام** once: teeth, tall hook, upright, lift, round-headed *mīm*.
3. Copy **سلام** once while looking.
4. Cover it. Write it once from memory, then compare and correct one feature.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-C01-SALAM-01, AR-SCRIPT-SIN-LAM-02, AR-SCRIPT-WRITE-SALAM-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **سلام**. (**salām, peace.**) Where does the pen lift? (**After
alif.**) Write the word once without looking. This is delayed recall, not
penmanship perfection.
