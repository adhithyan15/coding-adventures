## You'll want to know: il fumetto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C527-GENERI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il fumetto** — "comic (strip)".

> Da piccolo leggevo fumetti tutto il giorno.
>
> *As a child I read comics all day.*

Literally "a little puff of smoke": the speech balloon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fumetto* is built on *fumo* "smoke" (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *fūmus*, with *-etto*: the speech balloon looks like a puff of smoke.
