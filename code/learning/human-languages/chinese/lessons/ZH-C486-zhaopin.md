## You'll want to know: zhāopìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C486-JOBHUNT486-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāopìn* — "to recruit; to advertise a job".

> *zhè jiā gōngsī zhèngzài zhāopìn* — This company is hiring.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāopìn* is built from *zhāo* 'to beckon' and *pìn* 'to engage by invitation', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyew* and *phjiengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to call to' and the second 'a formal visit of inquiry'; the sense 'to hire' is not given there.
