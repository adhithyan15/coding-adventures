Telugu, chapter 369: Palaces and Chariots.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
రాజభవనం (rājabhavanaṁ) — a palace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a civilisation, then the Telugu for an era.

You'll want to know: రాజభవనం.
రాజభవనం — rājabhavanaṁ — "a palace".
రాజభవనంలో వంద గదులు ఉండేవి. rājabhavanaṁlō vanda gadulu uṇḍēvi. The palace had a hundred rooms.
రాజ, king, with భవనం, a building.

The word, taken apart.
రాజభవనం (rājabhavanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit: rājan, "a king" (CDIAL 10679), and bhavana, "a place of abode" (CDIAL 9417).

Lesson 3 of 5.
వారసత్వం (vārasatvaṁ) — heritage; inheritance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a palace, then the Telugu for a chariot.

You'll want to know: వారసత్వం.
వారసత్వం — vārasatvaṁ — "heritage; inheritance".
ఆ పురాతన కట్టడాలు మన వారసత్వం. ā purātana kaṭṭaḍālu mana vārasatvaṁ. Those ancient buildings are our heritage.
వారసత్వంగా, by inheritance: వారసత్వంగా వచ్చిన ఇల్లు, an inherited house.

The word, taken apart.
వారసత్వం (vārasatvaṁ) is built with the Sanskrit ending -tva, "-ness", on vārasu, "an heir"; the origin of vārasu is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
కూల్చు (kūlcu) — to demolish; to bring down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a chariot, then the Telugu for heritage.

You'll want to know: కూల్చు.
కూల్చు — kūlcu — "to demolish; to bring down".
వాళ్ళు పాత భవనాన్ని కూల్చారు. vāḷḷu pāta bhavanānni kūlcāru. They demolished the old building.
Past (I …): కూల్చాను, kūlcānu.
The causative of కూలు, to fall: to make something fall.

The word, taken apart.
కూల్చు (kūlcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1907 lists Telugu kūlcu, "to cause to fall, knock down", beside kūlu, "to fall down".

Lesson 5 of 5.
నిర్మించు (nirmiñcu) — to build; to construct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for heritage, then the Telugu for to demolish.

You'll want to know: నిర్మించు.
నిర్మించు — nirmiñcu — "to build; to construct".
ఆ రాజు ఒక పెద్ద కోట నిర్మించాడు. ā rāju oka pedda kōṭa nirmiñcāḍu. That king built a great fort.
Past (I …): నిర్మించాను, nirmiñcānu.
A formal partner of కట్టు; the noun is నిర్మాణం, construction.

The word, taken apart.
నిర్మించు (nirmiñcu) is built on Sanskrit nirmāṇa, "measuring; making" (CDIAL 7368), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.
