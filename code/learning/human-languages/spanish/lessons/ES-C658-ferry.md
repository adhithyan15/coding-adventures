## You'll want to know: el ferry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C658-BARCO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el ferry** — "the ferry".

> Tomamos el ferry a la isla.
>
> *We took the ferry to the island.*

Also *el transbordador*. Plural *los ferris*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferry* was borrowed from English *ferry* (EtymDB), from Old English *ferian* "to carry, to convey".
