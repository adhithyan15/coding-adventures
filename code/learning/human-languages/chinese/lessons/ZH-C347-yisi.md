## You'll want to know: 意思
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C347-MEANING347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**意思** — *yìsi* — "meaning; what something means".

> **这个字是什么意思** *zhège zì shì shénme yìsi* — What does this character mean?

The new character, in pieces: 思 — nine strokes in two pieces: on top a box with a cross inside it, five strokes; under it 心 *xīn*, the heart, four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**意思** (*yìsi*) is built from 意 'intent' and 思 'to think', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'iH* and *si* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'will' and the second 'to think of'. The second is said light here.
