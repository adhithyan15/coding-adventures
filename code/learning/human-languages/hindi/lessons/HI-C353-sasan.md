## You'll want to know: शासन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C353-SHASAN353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शासन** — *śāsan* — "rule, governance".

> राजा का शासन चालीस साल चला।
>
> *rājā kā śāsan cālīs sāl calā.*
>
> The king's rule lasted forty years.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शासन** (*śāsan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *śāsana*, "governing; instruction" (Monier-Williams).
