Urdu, chapter 298: Lost and Found.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بچھڑنا (bichhaṛnā) — to be separated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for refuge, then the Urdu for affected.

You'll want to know: بچھڑنا.
بچھڑنا — bichhaṛnā — "to be separated".
میلے میں بچہ ماں سے بچھڑ گیا۔ mele meṅ bachchā māṅ se bichhaṛ gayā. At the fair the child got separated from his mother.
Past (she …): وہ بچھڑ گئی, voh bichhaṛ gaī.

The word, taken apart.
بچھڑنا (bichhaṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bichuṛnā, "is separated", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11651). The person you lose takes سے: ماں سے بچھڑ گیا.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گمشدہ (gumshudā) — missing; lost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be separated, then the Urdu for to lose one's way.

You'll want to know: گمشدہ.
گمشدہ — gumshudā — "missing; lost".
گمشدہ بچہ صبح مل گیا۔ gumshudā bachchā subah mil gayā. The missing child was found in the morning.

The word, taken apart.
گمشدہ (gumshudā) is usually traced to Persian gum-shuda, built from gum, "lost", and shuda, "become". It is the word on notices for missing people.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تسلی (tasallī) — comfort; reassurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to lose one's way, then the Urdu for missing.

You'll want to know: تسلی.
تسلی — tasallī — "comfort; reassurance".
ماں کو پولیس سے تسلی ملی۔ māṅ ko polīs se tasallī milī. The police reassured the mother.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تسلی (tasallī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tasallī, "consolation". تسلی دینا is to comfort someone.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ملاپ (milāp) — a reunion; a coming together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for missing, then the Urdu for reassurance.

You'll want to know: ملاپ.
ملاپ — milāp — "a reunion; a coming together".
شام کو ماں بیٹے کا ملاپ ہوا۔ shām ko māṅ beṭe kā milāp huā. In the evening mother and son were reunited.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ملاپ (milāp) is built on ملنا, to meet, which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit miláti, "meets" (CDIAL 10133): a meeting again after a parting.
