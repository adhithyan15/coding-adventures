## You'll want to know: はなみず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C446-CHILL446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はなみず** — *hanamizu* — "a runny nose".

> **はなみずが とまりません。** — *hanamizu ga tomarimasen* — "My nose will not stop running."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はなみず** (*hanamizu*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *hana*, "nose" and *mizu*, "water" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
