## You'll want to know: गाँठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C512-KARGHA512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गाँठ** — *gā̃ṭh* — "a knot".

> धागे में एक गाँठ पड़ गई।
>
> *Dhāge meṁ ek gā̃ṭh paṛ gaī.*
>
> A knot has formed in the thread.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गाँठ** (*gā̃ṭh*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *granthi*, "a knot" (CDIAL 4354).
