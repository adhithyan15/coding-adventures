## You'll want to know: つる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C239-PASTIME239-02]; assesses=[] -->

**つる** — *tsuru* — "to go fishing; to catch (a fish)".

> **せんしゅう かわで さかなを つりました。** — *senshū kawa de sakana o tsurimashita* — "Last week I caught fish in the river."

Polite **つります**, *tsurimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つる** (*tsuru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
