## You'll want to know: पर्यटति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C424-PARYATANA424-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्यटति** — *paryaṭati* — "tours; travels around".

> पर्यटकः नगरे पर्यटति।
>
> *paryaṭakaḥ nagare paryaṭati.*
>
> The tourist tours the city.

Past (he …): सः नगरे पर्यटितवान्, *saḥ nagare paryaṭitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्यटति** (*paryaṭati*) is built from *pari*, "around", and the root √aṭ, "to roam", which also gives *paryaṭaka*, "a tourist"; MW gives *pary-aṭ* as to roam about, travel over, with the participle *paryaṭita* (MW).
