Lesson 1 of 6.
会 (huì) — can, having learned how.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

[your turn — recall: say sījī, a driver — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 会.
会 — huì — "can (having learned how)".
我会开车 wǒ huì kāichē — I can drive.
我会说汉语 wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ — I can speak Chinese.

The word, taken apart.
会 (huì) is inherited from Middle Chinese hwajH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to come together'. The sense 'can' is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.

What you've built.
会 + a verb says you have learned to do it: driving, a language, a skill. A sījī huì kāichē: a driver can drive. You have written 会 since 一会儿; this is another word in the same character.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say sījī, then say a driver can drive: sījī huì kāichē]

You'll want to know: 能.
能 — néng — "can; to be able to; to be allowed to".
我能去吗 wǒ néng qù ma — Can I go?
wǒ bìng le, bù néng qù — I'm ill, I can't go.
The next lesson puts the new character in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
能 (néng) is inherited from Middle Chinese nong (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'skilled; good at work'.

What you've built.
会 (huì) is a skill you have learned. 能 (néng) is being able right now, or being allowed: you 会 (huì) drive, but ill you 不能.

Script — 厶 over the moon piece, then two 匕.
能 (néng).
The whole left side first, top to bottom, then the right.
厶, two strokes: a stroke that falls to the left and turns right, then a dot.
The moon piece 月, four strokes, as it stands in 明 and 前: the left side, the top that turns down and hooks, two short bars inside.
Two 匕, two strokes each, the upper one first: a short left-falling stroke, then a vertical that bends right along its foot and hooks up.
2 + 4 + 4 = ten.

Lesson 4 of 6.
kěyǐ — may; can, with permission.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: kěyǐ.
kěyǐ — "may; can (with permission)".
wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma — May I sit here?
Two dips in a row: spoken, the first one rises, kéyǐ. It is written kěyǐ.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kěyǐ is built from kě 'may' and yǐ 'to use', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khaX and yiX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to permit' and 'to use'.

What you've built.
会 (huì) learned, 能 (néng) able or allowed, kěyǐ permitted. Asking whether you may, kěyǐ is the polite one; the answer is kěyǐ, or bù kěyǐ.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kěyǐ — yes, you may]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say gōngrén, then kěyǐ again]

You'll want to know: yóuyǒng.
yóuyǒng — "to swim".
nǐ huì yóuyǒng ma — Can you swim?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yóuyǒng is built from yóu 'to float' and yǒng 'to swim under water', both inherited from Middle Chinese, yuw and hjwaengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). hjwaengH would regularly give yòng, with a fall; why the modern word dips is not settled.

What you've built.
Swimming is learned, so it takes 会: nǐ huì yóuyǒng ma. 能 asks whether you are able today: jīntiān nǐ néng yóuyǒng ma.

Lesson 6 of 6.
我会开车 (wǒ huì kāichē) — three ways to say can: learned, able, allowed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The chapter closes here, with three ways to say can in one conversation.
[your turn — recall: say yóuyǒng, to swim — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say shàngbān, to go to work — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say gōngsī, a company — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

The exchange.
A: 你会开车吗 nǐ huì kāichē ma — Can you drive? B: 会 huì — I can. A: 你明天能开车吗 nǐ míngtiān néng kāichē ma — Can you drive tomorrow? B: bù néng, wǒ míngtiān shàngbān — No, I'm at work tomorrow. A: nǐ huì yóuyǒng ma — Can you swim? B: 不会 bú huì — I can't. A: wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma — May I sit here? B: kěyǐ — Yes, please.
The answer to each is the verb alone: 会 (huì), 不能, 不会, kěyǐ. Mandarin answers yes or no by saying the verb again.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 13 seconds]
Why can't B drive tomorrow?

What you've built — three cans.
我会开车 wǒ huì kāichē — I have learned to drive.
我明天不能开车 wǒ míngtiān bù néng kāichē — tomorrow I'm not able to.
wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma — may I sit here?
Listen. Nǐ huì shuō Hànyǔ ma?
Speak. Answer it, then say one thing you cannot do today, and ask permission for something.
