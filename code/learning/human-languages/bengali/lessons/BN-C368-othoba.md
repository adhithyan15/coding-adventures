# অথবা (ôthôbā) — or else; or otherwise

## You'll want to know: অথবা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C368-SHORTO368-05]; assesses=[] -->

**অথবা** — *ôthôbā* — "or else; or otherwise".

> কাল ছুটি হলে আমরা সিনেমায় যাব, অথবা বাড়িতে থাকব।
>
> *kāl chhuṭi hôle āmrā sinemāy jābô, ôthôbā bāṛite thākbô.*
>
> If tomorrow is a holiday, we'll go to the cinema or else stay at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অথবা** (*ôthôbā*) is Sanskrit *athavā*, 'or' (Monier-Williams). It is more weighty than বা and often offers a second plan.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
