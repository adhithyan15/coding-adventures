## You'll want to know: riferire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C284-INCHIESTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**riferire** — "to report, to pass on (what was said)".

> Ti riferisco quello che ha detto il medico.
>
> *I am passing on what the doctor said.*

Present like *capire*: *riferisco*. *Riferirsi a* — to refer to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*riferire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *referre* "to carry back, to report" (EtymDB), built on *ferre* "to carry".
