## You'll want to know: لنگوٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C520-AKHARA520-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لنگوٹ** — *langoṭ* — "a loincloth (a wrestler's)".

> پہلوان لنگوٹ پہن کر میدان میں اترے۔
>
> *pahalvān langoṭ pahan kar maidān meṅ utre.*
>
> The wrestlers put on their loincloths and stepped into the ring.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لنگوٹ** (*langoṭ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lãgoṭ*, "a loincloth", under *\*laṅgapaṭṭa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10903).
