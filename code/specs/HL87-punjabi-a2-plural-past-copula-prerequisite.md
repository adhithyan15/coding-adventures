# HL87 — Punjabi A2 plural past-copula prerequisite

Issue: #17977

Punjabi A2 mock drafting exposed `ਸਨ` six times: four times in the learner
paper and twice in the confidential examiner master. A direct audit then
found that the authored course does not own standalone singular past `ਸੀ`
either. The legacy substring rule had mistaken the `ਸੀ` inside `ਤੁਸੀਂ` for
the standalone copula. Chapter 187 therefore closes the two-form past-copula
prerequisite in dependency order and does not edit the mock.

The ramp first retrieves familiar `ਹੋਣਾ`, teaches `ਸੀ` with familiar singular
descriptions, and gives that form a short retrieval step. It then introduces
`ਸਨ` in a transparent plural description, contrasts the two forms, and moves
`ਸਨ` into familiar “they used to …” language. Each target has one direct owner
and at least two later retrieval lessons before a four-skill payoff. All other
words and inflections in learner-facing Punjabi must already occur in earlier
lessons.

This remains a prerequisite tranche, not mock-readiness evidence. The separate
perfective family (`ਕੀਤਾ`, `ਮਿਲਿਆ`, `ਗਏ`, `ਦੱਸਿਆ`, and `ਬਦਲਿਆ`) and
first-person future forms remain out of scope. All authored and generated
ownership stays sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case; no
corpus-wide aggregate is introduced.
