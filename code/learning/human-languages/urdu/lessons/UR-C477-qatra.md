## You'll want to know: قطرہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C477-MEDICINE477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قطرہ** — *qatrā* — "a droplet; a drop (of medicine)".

> دوا کے دو قطرے پانی میں ملائیے۔
>
> *davā ke do qatre pānī meṅ milāiye.*
>
> Mix two drops of the medicine into some water.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قطرہ** (*qatrā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qaṭra*, "a drop".
