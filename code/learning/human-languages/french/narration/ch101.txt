Lesson 1 of 5.
le restaurant — a restaurant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a church, then the French for a museum.

You'll want to know: le restaurant.
le restaurant — "a restaurant".

The word, taken apart.
restaurant is the present participle of the verb for "to restore", a learned borrowing of Latin restaurare (EtymDB 2.1): first a restoring broth, then the place that served it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'hôtel — a hotel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a museum, then the French for a restaurant.

You'll want to know: l'hôtel.
l'hôtel — "a hotel".

The word, taken apart.
hôtel from Old French hostel from Late Latin hospitale, "a guest house", from hospes, "guest" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) hôpital is the same Latin word taken in by scholars; hôtel is the one worn down in speech.
