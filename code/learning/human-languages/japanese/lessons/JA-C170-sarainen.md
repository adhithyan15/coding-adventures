## You'll want to know: さらいねん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C170-NEWYEAR170-02]; assesses=[] -->

**さらいねん** — *sarainen* — "the year after next".

> **さらいねん いなかに すむ つもりです。** — *sarainen inaka ni sumu tsumori desu* — "I plan to live in the countryside the year after next."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さらいねん** (*sarainen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "again" (Middle Chinese *tsojH*), *rai*, "coming" (*loj*) and *nen*, "year" (*nen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
