## You'll want to know: నెగ్గు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C544-KRIDA544-04]; assesses=[] -->

**నెగ్గు** — *neggu* — "to prevail; to win through".

> చివరి బంతికి మా జట్టు నెగ్గింది.
>
> *civari bantiki mā jaṭṭu neggindi.*
>
> Our team won on the last ball.

Past (I …): నెగ్గాను, *neggānu*.

Also in arguments and elections; గెలుచు is the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నెగ్గు** (*neggu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3661 lists Telugu *neggu*, "to be successful, to succeed, to thrive".
