## You'll want to know: శ్రమించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C525-SAMPADANA525-05]; assesses=[] -->

**శ్రమించు** — *śramiñcu* — "to toil; to labour".

> ఆ పుస్తకం కోసం రచయిత రెండేళ్ళు శ్రమించారు.
>
> *ā pustakaṁ kōsaṁ racayita reṇḍēḷḷu śramiñcāru.*
>
> The writer toiled for two years on that book.

Past (I …): శ్రమించాను, *śramiñcānu*.

The noun is శ్రమ, toil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శ్రమించు** (*śramiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *śrama*, "labour; fatigue" (CDIAL 12682), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
