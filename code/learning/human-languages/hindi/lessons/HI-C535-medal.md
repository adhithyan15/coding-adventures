## You'll want to know: मेडल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C535-DAUR535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मेडल** — *meḍal* — "a medal".

> दौड़ में मीरा को सोने का मेडल मिला।
>
> *Dauṛ meṁ Mīrā ko sone kā meḍal milā.*
>
> Mira won a gold medal in the race.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेडल** (*meḍal*) is a loan from English *medal*.
