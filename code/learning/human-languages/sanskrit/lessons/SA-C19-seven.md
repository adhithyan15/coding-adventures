# सप्त (sapta) — seven

## You'll want to know: सप्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C19-NUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सप्त** (*sapta*) — "seven."

Latin *septem*. Greek *hepta*. English *seven*. Line them up and the shape is one
shape: *s-p-t* under the vowels, with Greek turning the initial *s* into a rough
breathing and English softening the *-pt-* to *-v-*.

The Sanskrit form is not the ancestor of the others — that is a common
misunderstanding worth heading off. All four descend from a **shared parent
nobody wrote down**. Sanskrit is simply an old, well-recorded cousin.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two numbers, and one correction that matters: Sanskrit is a cousin of Latin and
English, not their ancestor.
