## You'll want to know: ēloquor, ēloquī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C368-ORATOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ēloquor, ēloquī** — "to speak out, to utter".

> Tandem quod sentiēbat ēlocūtus est.
>
> *At last he spoke out what he felt.*

Deponent: *ēloquor, ēloquī, ēlocūtus sum*. *Ē* + *loquor*: to speak out. English *eloquent*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ēloquor* is built from *ē* "out" and *loquor* "to speak" (Lewis & Short). English *eloquent* comes from its present participle.
