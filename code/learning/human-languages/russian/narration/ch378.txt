Russian, chapter 378: Making arrangements.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
договариваться (dogovárivatsya) — to arrange, to come to an agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to set, then the Russian for to be going to.

You'll want to know: договариваться.
договариваться (dogovárivatsya) — "to arrange, to come to an agreement".
Мы договорились встретиться у метро. My dogovorílis vstrétitsya u metró. We agreed to meet by the metro.
Imperfective; perfective договориться. Договариваться с кем о чём, or + infinitive.

The word, taken apart.
договариваться (dogovárivatsya) is built from до- "through to the end" and говорить "to speak", on the old Slavic noun govorŭ "talk" (EtymDB): to talk a matter through.

Lesson 3 of 5.
вариант (variánt) — an option, a version.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to arrange, then the Russian for an arrangement.

You'll want to know: вариант.
вариант (variánt) — "an option, a version".
Есть два варианта: поезд или самолёт. Yest dva variánta: póyezd íli samolyót. There are two options: the train or the plane.
Masculine. Лучший вариант (variánt) — the best option. It is also a version of a text.

The word, taken apart.
вариант (variánt) was borrowed from French variante (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin variāns, the participle of variāre "to change, to vary", built on varius "various" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
альтернатива (alternatíva) — an alternative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an arrangement, then the Russian for an option.

You'll want to know: альтернатива.
альтернатива (alternatíva) — "an alternative".
У нас нет альтернативы. U nas net alternatívy. We have no alternative.
Feminine. Альтернатива + dative, or для: альтернатива (alternatíva) машине.

The word, taken apart.
альтернатива (alternatíva) is usually traced to French alternative, built on Latin alternus "by turns", from alter "the other of two" (Lewis & Short).
