## You'll want to know: امیدوار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C342-VOT342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**امیدوار** — *ummīdvār* — "a candidate (for a post or a seat)".

> اس انتخاب میں تین امیدوار ہیں۔
>
> *is intikhāb meṅ tīn ummīdvār haiṅ.*
>
> There are three candidates in this election.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امیدوار** (*ummīdvār*) is usually traced to Persian *ummīdvār*, "hopeful", built on **امید**, "hope", with *-vār*, "having": the one who hopes to be chosen.
