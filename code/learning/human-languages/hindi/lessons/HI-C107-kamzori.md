## You'll want to know: कमज़ोरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C107-DOCTOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कमज़ोरी** (*kamzorī*) — "weakness".

It opens on the **कम** you know, "little": weakness is having little strength.
**कमज़ोरी लग रही है** — "I feel weak".
