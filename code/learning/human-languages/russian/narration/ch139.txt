Russian, chapter 139: A Timetable, an Advert, an Entrance, an Exit, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
расписание (raspisániye) — a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to plan, then the Russian for to hope.

You'll want to know: расписание.
расписание (raspisániye) — "a timetable".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the board]
The board says Автобус: 8:00. The bus leaves at eight, so be there before eight.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Nobody? (никто.) Nothing? (ничего.) Nowhere? (нигде.) (to) nowhere? (никуда.) What for? (зачем.) Recently? (недавно.) Long ago; for a long time? (давно.) Before? (раньше.) Last? (прошлый.) To happen? (случиться.)
