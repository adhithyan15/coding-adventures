Hindi, chapter 496: Sleep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अनिद्रा (anidrā) — insomnia; sleeplessness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for nourishing, then the Hindi for to be digested.

You'll want to know: अनिद्रा.
अनिद्रा — anidrā — "insomnia; sleeplessness".
तनाव से अरुण को अनिद्रा की शिकायत है। tanāv se Aruṇ ko anidrā kī śikāyat hai. Because of stress, Arun suffers from insomnia.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
अनिद्रा (anidrā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit a-nidrā, "sleeplessness", built on a-, "not", and nidrā, "sleep" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उनींदा (unīndā) — drowsy; heavy with sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be digested, then the Hindi for insomnia.

You'll want to know: उनींदा.
उनींदा — unīndā — "drowsy; heavy with sleep".
सुबह छह बजे बच्चे उनींदे थे। subah chah baje bacce unīnde the. At six in the morning the children were still drowsy.

The word, taken apart.
उनींदा (unīndā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi unī̃dā, with the sense "sleepless, with eyes open", under Sanskrit unnidra, "sleepless" (CDIAL 2108). Today it means eyes heavy from too little sleep, a turn CDIAL does not give.

You'll want to know: पसरना.
पसरना — pasarnā — "to sprawl; to stretch out".
काम से लौटकर अरुण बिस्तर पर पसर गया। kām se lauṭkar Aruṇ bistar par pasar gayā. Back from work, Arun sprawled out on the bed.
Past (he …): वह बिस्तर पर पसर गया, vah bistar par pasar gayā.

The word, taken apart.
पसरना (pasarnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi pasarnā, "to spread out", under Sanskrit prásarati, "spreads out" (CDIAL 8825): a tired body spreads out across the bed.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: बुदबुदाना.
बुदबुदाना — budbudānā — "to mumble".
बुख़ार में दादा कुछ बुदबुदा रहे थे। bukhār meṁ dādā kuch budbudā rahe the. In his fever my grandfather was mumbling something.
Past (he …): वह कुछ बुदबुदाया, vah kuch budbudāyā.

The word, taken apart.
बुदबुदाना (budbudānā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi budbudānā, "to mutter", under budabuda, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9274); the doubled syllable is the sound of lips moving with no clear words.
