## You'll want to know: थक्क होणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C465-NAVAL465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**थक्क होणे** — *thakka hoṇe* — "to be amazed".

> त्याचं चित्र पाहून आम्ही थक्क झालो.
>
> *tyācaṁ citra pāhūn āmhī thakka jhālo.*
>
> We were amazed to see his painting.

Past (he …): तो थक्क झाला, *to thakka jhālā*.

थक्क, struck still, + होणे; stronger than नवल वाटणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थक्क होणे** (*thakka hoṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on थक्क, "struck still", whose origin is not settled, with होणे, "to become".

## What you've built
