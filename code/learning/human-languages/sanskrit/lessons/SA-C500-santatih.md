## You'll want to know: सन्ततिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C500-DEVARA500-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्ततिः** — *santatiḥ* — "offspring".

> माता पिता च सन्ततेः सुखं चिन्तयतः।
>
> *mātā pitā ca santateḥ sukhaṃ cintayataḥ.*
>
> Parents think of their children's happiness.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्ततिः** (*santatiḥ*) is built from *sam* and the root √tan, "to stretch": a line stretched on; MW gives "uninterrupted succession, lineage, progeny, offspring" (MW).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
