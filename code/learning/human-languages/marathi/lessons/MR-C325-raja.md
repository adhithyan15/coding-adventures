## You'll want to know: राजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C325-RAJA325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राजा** — *rājā* — "a king".

> एकेकाळी या प्रदेशात एक दयाळू राजा होता.
>
> *ekekāḷī yā pradeśāt ek dayāḷū rājā hotā.*
>
> Once upon a time there was a kind king in this region.

Masculine. Plural: राजे, *rāje*.

Plural राजे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राजा** (*rājā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *rājan*, 'a king', in its nominative form *rājā* (Monier-Williams).
