## You'll want to know: rènshi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C34-CALLED34-04]; assesses=[] -->

*rènshi* — "to know someone; to recognise".

> *wǒ rènshi tā* — I know him.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rènshi* is built from two old words for knowing, both inherited from Middle Chinese: *nyinH*, which the Guangyun glosses with the second, and *syik*, 'to know' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
