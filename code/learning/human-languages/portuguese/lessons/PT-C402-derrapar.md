## You'll want to know: derrapar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C402-VOLANTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**derrapar** — "to skid".

> Com a chuva, o carro derrapou na estrada.
>
> *In the rain, the car skidded on the road.*

The noun is *a derrapagem*, a skid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derrapar* was borrowed from French *déraper* 'to skid; to weigh anchor' (EtymDB).
