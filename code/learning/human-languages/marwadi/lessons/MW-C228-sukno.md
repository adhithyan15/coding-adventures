## You'll want to know: सूकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C228-HEAT228-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सूकणो** — *sūkṇo* — "to dry up; to wither".

> जेठ मांय खेत सूकै है।
>
> *jeṭh mā̃y khet sūkai hai.*
>
> The field dries up in early summer.

Past (it …): वो खेत सूक्यो, *vo khet sūkyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूकणो** (*sūkṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*śuṣkati*, "becomes dry"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *sūkaï*, "dries up" (CDIAL 12552). **सूको**, dry, is its relative.
