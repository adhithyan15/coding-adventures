# ゆ — open yukkuri

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-YUKKURI, JA-SCRIPT-YO-01, JA-SCRIPT-MO-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Tap *yu | hold | ku | ri*, then write known **よ** and **も**.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-YU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ゆ** — *yu*

Two strokes. First a short curved vertical on the left. Second, begin at the
upper right, sweep left through the middle, loop around, and finish to the right.
