## You'll want to know: предприятие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C361-PRIBYL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**предприятие** (*predpriyátiye*) — "an enterprise, a business; a plant".

> Отец работает на большом предприятии.
>
> *Otéts rabótayet na bolshóm predpriyátii.*
>
> My father works at a large plant.

Neuter. *На предприятии* — at a plant. *Малое предприятие* — a small business.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**предприятие** is built on *предпринять* "to undertake", from *пред-* "before" and the root of *принять* "to take", from the old verb "to take" seen in *снимать*: an undertaking.
