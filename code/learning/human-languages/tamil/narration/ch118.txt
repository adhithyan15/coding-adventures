Lesson 3 of 5.
தட்டு (taṭṭu) — a plate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a stove, then the Tamil for a vessel.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count with care: oṉṟu, mūṉṟu, with the hard ṉṟ; onpatu, one short of pattu; then பதினொன்று to இருபது, two tens.

You'll want to know: தட்டு.
