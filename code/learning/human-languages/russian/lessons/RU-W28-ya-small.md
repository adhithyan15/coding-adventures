# я (ya) — the word for I, written small

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-Y-SHORT-01, RU-SCRIPT-YU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write й and ю once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **я** (*ya*), "I".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: я
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SCRIPT-C28-YA-SMALL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**я** — small *ya*.

**я** (*ya*) is also a whole word: "I". You wrote the capital **Я** in the letter
chapters, but Russian writes "I" with the **small** letter: inside a sentence it is
never capitalised, unlike English "I". So this is the shape you will write most.

## Writing: я
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-C28-YA-SMALL-01] -->

- **1.** start here: climb the right stem from the baseline to the top
- **2.** without lifting, curve counterclockwise around the upper bowl
- **3.** without lifting, sweep left through the bowl's lower join
- **4.** without lifting, descend the diagonal leg to the lower-left tip — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 0.** The pen never leaves the paper.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: RussianIrina, ‘Learning Russian - Alphabet letters, handwriting’, lowercase я demonstration, 12:13–12:21, YouTube, 5 February 2013.

## Guided Practice

- [YOU LOOK: at **я** and point at я inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: я three times, saying *ya* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ю, then я, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **я** aloud once more]
