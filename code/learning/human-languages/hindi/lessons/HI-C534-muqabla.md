## You'll want to know: मुक़ाबला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C534-MAICH534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक़ाबला** — *muqāblā* — "a contest; a competition".

> दोनों टीमों के बीच बहुत कठिन मुक़ाबला था।
>
> *Donõ ṭīmoṁ ke bīc bahut kaṭhin muqāblā thā.*
>
> It was a very hard contest between the two teams.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक़ाबला** (*muqāblā*) is usually traced to Arabic *muqābala*, "facing each other", through Persian.
