## You'll want to know: مصور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C356-JUMHUR356-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مصور** — *muṣawwir* — "a photographer; a camera operator".

> التقط المصور صورا للعروسين.
>
> *iltaqaṭa al-muṣawwir ṣuwaran li-l-ʿarūsayn.*
>
> The photographer took pictures of the couple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصور** (*muṣawwir*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-w-r* (Lane), the root of the taught *ṣūra*, a picture. *Muṣawwir* is the participle of *ṣawwara*, "one who makes pictures".
