## You'll want to know: प्रतिभा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C504-AVEDANA504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिभा** — *pratibhā* — "talent; a gift".

> सर्वे तस्य प्रतिभां प्रशंसन्ति।
>
> *sarve tasya pratibhāṃ praśaṃsanti.*
>
> Everyone praises his talent.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिभा** (*pratibhā*) is built on *prati*, "towards", and the root √bhā, "to shine, to appear": what lights up in the mind; MW gives *pratibhā* as light, then intelligence, presence of mind, genius (MW).
