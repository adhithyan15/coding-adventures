## You'll want to know: تلوا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C466-LIMB466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تلوا** — *talvā* — "the sole of the foot".

> گرم فرش پر اس کے تلوے جل گئے۔
>
> *garm farsh par us ke talve jal gae.*
>
> The hot floor burnt the soles of his feet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تلوا** (*talvā*) is inherited from *talapāda*, "the lower foot", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5738).
