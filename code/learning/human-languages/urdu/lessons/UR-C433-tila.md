## You'll want to know: ٹیلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C433-TILA433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹیلا** — *ṭīlā* — "a mound; a dune".

> ریگستان میں ریت کے ٹیلے ہیں۔
>
> *registān meṅ ret ke ṭīle haiṅ.*
>
> There are sand dunes in the desert.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹیلا** (*ṭīlā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭīlā*, "a hillock, a mound", under *ṭalla* and its variant *ṭilla*, "a heap" (CDIAL 5451).
