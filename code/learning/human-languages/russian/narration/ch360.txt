6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
совещание (soveshchániye) — a meeting (at work).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to carry out, then the Russian for to cope.

You'll want to know: совещание.
совещание (soveshchániye) — "a meeting (at work)".
Совещание начнётся в девять часов. Soveshchániye nachnyótsya v dévyat chasóv. The meeting will begin at nine o’clock.
Neuter. A meeting at work; a meeting of friends is встреча.

The word, taken apart.
совещание (soveshchániye) is built on совещаться "to confer", on вещать "to proclaim", the Church Slavonic form of the root of ответ, Old Russian větŭ "counsel". The щ is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian has ч.

You'll want to know: отчёт.
отчёт (otchyót) — "a report".
Я пишу отчёт о командировке. Ya pishú otchyót o komandiróvke. I am writing a report on the business trip.
Masculine. Сдать отчёт (otchyót) — to hand in a report. Отдавать себе отчёт (otchyót) — to realise.

The word, taken apart.
отчёт (otchyót) is built from от- and the root of счёт "a count, an account" and считать "to count": a report is an account given back.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: график.
график (gráfik) — "a schedule; a graph".
У меня свободный график работы. U menyá svobódnyy gráfik rabóty. I have flexible working hours.
Masculine. Гибкий график (gráfik) — flexible hours. It is also a chart of figures.

The word, taken apart.
график (gráfik) is usually traced, through German, to Greek graphikós "of drawing or writing", built on gráphein "to scratch, to draw, to write". A schedule is a time plan drawn out.

You'll want to know: смена.
смена (sména) — "a shift (at work); a change".
Сегодня у медсестры ночная смена. Segódnya u medsestrý nochnáya sména. Today the nurse has a night shift.
Feminine. Работать в две смены — to work in two shifts. Смена погоды — a change in the weather.

The word, taken apart.
смена (sména) is built on менять "to change", inherited from Proto-Slavic měniti: a shift is one team changing places with another.

Lesson 5 of 6.
отчитываться (otchítyvatsya) — to report, to account for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a schedule, then the Russian for a shift.

You'll want to know: отчитываться.
отчитываться (otchítyvatsya) — "to report, to account for".
Каждую пятницу мы отчитываемся перед начальником. Kázhduyu pyátnitsu my otchítyvayemsya péred nachálnikom. Every Friday we report to the boss.
Imperfective; perfective отчитаться. Отчитываться перед кем о чём. Without -ся, отчитывать means to tell off.

The word, taken apart.
отчитываться (otchítyvatsya) is built from от- and читать in its old sense "to count", the root of число "number", Proto-Slavic čislo (EtymDB): to give back an account.
