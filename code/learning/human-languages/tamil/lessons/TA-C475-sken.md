## You'll want to know: ஸ்கேன்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C475-MARUTTUVAR475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஸ்கேன்** — *skēṉ* — "a scan".

> மருத்துவர் வயிற்றுக்கு ஒரு ஸ்கேன் எடுக்கச் சொன்னார்.
>
> *maruttuvar vayiṟṟukku oru skēṉ eṭukkac coṉṉār.*
>
> The doctor asked for a scan of the stomach.

ஸ்கேன் எடு, to have a scan taken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஸ்கேன்** (*skēṉ*) is a loan from English *scan*, written with the grantha letter ஸ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
