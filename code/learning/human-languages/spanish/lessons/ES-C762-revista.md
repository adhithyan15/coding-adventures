## You'll want to know: la revista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C762-PRENSA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la revista** — "the magazine".

> Compro una revista de cocina.
>
> *I buy a cooking magazine.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revista* is built on *re-* and *vista* "view", from Latin *vidēre* "to see"; it was probably modelled on English *review* and French *revue*.
