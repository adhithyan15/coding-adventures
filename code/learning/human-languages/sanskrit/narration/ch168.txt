Lesson 1 of 5.
कक्षा (kakṣā) — a class, a classroom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a she-goat, then the Sanskrit for a mare.

You'll want to know: कक्षा.
कक्षा — kakṣā — "a class, a classroom".

The word, taken apart.
kakṣā is built on kakṣa "hiding place, armpit", as its feminine; MW gives it as "a walled enclosure, a private room". Using it for a school class and its room is modern.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अक्षरम् (akṣaram) — a syllable, a letter.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a class, then the Sanskrit for a chapter.

You'll want to know: अक्षरम्.
अक्षरम् — akṣaram — "a syllable, a letter".

The word, taken apart.
akṣara is built from a- "not" and kṣara "perishing, melting away" (MW; CDIAL 38): "imperishable", and so "a syllable, a letter". It lives on as Hindi ākhar "letter" (CDIAL 38).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पदम् (padam) — a word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a syllable, then the Sanskrit for a colour.

You'll want to know: पदम्.
पदम् — padam — "a word".

The word, taken apart.
pada first meant "a step, a footstep, a track" (CDIAL 7747), built on pad "foot", and then a step of speech, "a word". It is inherited from Indo-European, cousin of Greek pedon "ground" (EtymDB).
