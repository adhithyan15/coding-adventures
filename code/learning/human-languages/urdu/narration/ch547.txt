Urdu, chapter 547: Looking Ahead.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
تصور (tasavvur) — imagination; a picture in the mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to grapple with (a hard task), then the Urdu for a yardstick.

You'll want to know: تصور.
تصور — tasavvur — "imagination; a picture in the mind".
اگر تصور نہ ہوتا تو کوئی نئی چیز نہ بنتی۔ agar tasavvur na hotā to koī naī chīz na bantī. Without imagination nothing new would ever be made.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تصور (tasavvur) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taṣawwur, "forming an image in the mind".

Lesson 3 of 7.
مہم (muhim) — a campaign; a venture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for imagination, then the Urdu for an intention (in one's heart).

You'll want to know: مہم.
مہم — muhim — "a campaign; a venture".
اگر مہم ختم ہوئی تو سب گھر لوٹیں گے۔ agar muhim khatm huī to sab ghar lauṭeṅ ge. If the campaign ends, everyone will go home.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
مہم (muhim) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muhimm, "an important matter"; the sense "a campaign" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.

Lesson 5 of 7.
سنورنا (sanvarnā) — to take shape nicely; to be put right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a campaign, then the Urdu for an offer.

You'll want to know: سنورنا.
سنورنا — sanvarnā — "to take shape nicely; to be put right".
اگر بچوں نے اچھا سیکھا تو کل سنور جائے گا۔ agar bachchoṅ ne achchhā sīkhā to kal sanvar jāe gā. If the children learn well, tomorrow will turn out well.
Past (it …): وہ کمرہ سنور گیا, voh kamrā sanvar gayā.

The word, taken apart.
سنورنا (sanvarnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins sãwārṇā, "to arrange, to deck", and its intransitive sãwarnā, "to be arranged", under Sanskrit saṃvārayati, "puts in order" (CDIAL 13021).
