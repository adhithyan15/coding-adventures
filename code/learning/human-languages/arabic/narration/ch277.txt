Arabic, chapter 277: Ball, Match, Team, Race, Sport.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
كرة (kura) — a ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a giraffe, then the Arabic for a whale.

You'll want to know: كرة.
كرة — kura — "a ball".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kura]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kura, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kura]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a giraffe, then the Arabic for a whale, then say kura again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does كرة (kura) mean? ("A ball".) Say it once more.

Words from chapters 272-274 — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a race and sport.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: amazing, sure, exciting, complicated, reasonable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: chocolate, sweets, pasta, flour, spices]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a printer, ink, a blackboard, a dictionary, a file]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Amazing? (مدهش, mudhish.) Sure? (متأكد, mutaʾakkid.) Exciting? (مثير, muthīr.) Complicated? (معقد, muʿaqqad.) Reasonable? (معقول, maʿqūl.) Chocolate? (شوكولاتة, shūkūlāta.) Sweets? (حلوى, ḥalwā.) Pasta? (معكرونة, maʿkarūna.) Flour? (دقيق, daqīq.) Spices? (توابل, tawābil.) A printer? (طابعة, ṭābiʿa.) Ink? (حبر, ḥibr.) A blackboard? (سبورة, sabbūra.) A dictionary? (قاموس, qāmūs.) A file? (ملف, malaff.)
