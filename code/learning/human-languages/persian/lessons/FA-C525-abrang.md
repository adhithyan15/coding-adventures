## You'll want to know: آبرنگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C525-PAINTING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آبرنگ** (*âbrang*) — "watercolour".

> دخترم با آبرنگ یک دریا کشید.
>
> *dokhtaram bâ âbrang yek daryâ keshid.*
>
> My daughter painted a sea in watercolour.

*âb* 'water' + *rang* 'colour'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âbrang* is built on two inherited words: *âb*, 'water', and *rang*, 'colour, dye', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
