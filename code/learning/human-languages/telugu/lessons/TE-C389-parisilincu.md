# పరిశీలించు (pariśīliñcu) — to examine closely; to look into

## You'll want to know: పరిశీలించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C389-VISLESANA389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిశీలించు** — *pariśīliñcu* — "to examine closely; to look into".

> అధికారి నా దరఖాస్తును పరిశీలించాడు.
>
> *adhikāri nā darakhāstunu pariśīliñcāḍu.*
>
> The officer examined my application.

Past (I …): పరిశీలించాను, *pariśīliñcānu*.

Careful, official checking: దరఖాస్తు పరిశీలించు, to go through an application.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిశీలించు** (*pariśīliñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *pariśīlana*, "contact; constant occupation, study" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
