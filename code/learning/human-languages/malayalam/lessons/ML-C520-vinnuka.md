# വിങ്ങുക (viṅṅuka) — to throb; to swell with grief

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for compassion, then the Malayalam for sympathy.

## You'll want to know: വിങ്ങുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C520-ANUKAMPA520-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വിങ്ങുക** — *viṅṅuka* — "to throb; to swell with grief".

> സുഹൃത്ത് പോയപ്പോൾ അവളുടെ മനസ്സ് വിങ്ങി.
>
> *suhṛttŭ pōyappōḷ avaḷuṭe manassŭ viṅṅi.*
>
> When her friend left, her heart ached.

Past: വിങ്ങി, *viṅṅi*.

Of a wound it means to throb and swell; of the heart, to ache with held-back grief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിങ്ങുക** (*viṅṅuka*) is native Dravidian and seems to be a short-vowel form of വീങ്ങുക, "to swell": DEDR 5448 lists that verb with വീക്കം, a swelling, and the throbbing of a wound (DEDR 5448).
