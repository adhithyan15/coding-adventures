## You'll want to know: પસંદગી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C537-PASANDGI537-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પસંદગી** — *pasandgī* — "a choice".

> જો પસંદગી મારી હોત તો હું પર્વત પર જાત.
>
> *jo pasandgī mārī hot to hũ parvat par jāt.*
>
> If the choice were mine, I would go to the mountains.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પસંદગી** (*pasandgī*) is usually traced to Persian *pasand*, "approved, liked", with the ending *-gī*; **પસંદ કરવું** is to choose.
