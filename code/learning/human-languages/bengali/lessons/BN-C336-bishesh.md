## You'll want to know: বিশেষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C336-GURUTTO336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিশেষ** — *bishesh* — "special; particular".

> আজ একটা বিশেষ দিন।
>
> *āj ekṭā bishesh din.*
>
> Today is a special day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিশেষ** (*bishesh*) is Sanskrit *viśeṣa*, a distinguishing mark (Monier-Williams). Before a noun it means special or particular.
