## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**острый** ← Proto-Slavic *\*ostrŭ* — inherited. Vasmer lists Lithuanian *aštrùs*, "sharp", Greek *ákros*, "topmost", and Latin *ācer*, "sharp", from PIE *\*h₂eḱ-*, "sharp".
