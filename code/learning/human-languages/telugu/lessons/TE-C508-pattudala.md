# పట్టుదల (paṭṭudala) — determination; persistence

## You'll want to know: పట్టుదల
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C508-DHAIRYAM508-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టుదల** — *paṭṭudala* — "determination; persistence".

> పట్టుదలతో చదివి ఆమె డాక్టరు అయింది.
>
> *paṭṭudalatō cadivi āme ḍākṭaru ayindi.*
>
> By studying with determination, she became a doctor.

పట్టుదలతో, with grit; పట్టు, to hold.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టుదల** (*paṭṭudala*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4034 lists Telugu *paṭṭu*, "to hold, to seize", also as a noun, "grip, perseverance", and *paṭṭudala* itself.
