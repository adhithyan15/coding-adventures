Urdu, chapter 326: The Curse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لعنت (lānat) — a curse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a ghost, then the Urdu for a witch.

You'll want to know: لعنت.
لعنت — lānat — "a curse".
سب نے اس پر لعنت بھیجی۔ sab ne us par lānat bhejī. Everyone cursed him.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
لعنت (lānat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic laʿna, "a curse". A curse is sent: لعنت بھیجی.

Lesson 2 of 5.
کوسنا (kosnā) — to curse; to rail at.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a witch, then the Urdu for a curse.

You'll want to know: کوسنا.
کوسنا — kosnā — "to curse; to rail at".
دادی نے بادشاہ کو کوسا۔ dādī ne bādshāh ko kosā. Grandmother cursed the king.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اسے کوسا, us ne use kosā.

The word, taken apart.
کوسنا (kosnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kosnā, "to curse", under Sanskrit krṓśati, "cries out" (CDIAL 3612).

The word, taken apart.
پچھتانا (pachhtānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin pachtānā under paścōttāpa, a burning afterwards, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8010): the pain that comes after the deed.

روٹھنا (rūṭhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin rūṭhnā, "to be offended", under Sanskrit ruṣṭa, "angry" (CDIAL 10791). The person you sulk with takes سے.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عذاب (azāb) — torment; a punishing suffering.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to regret, then the Urdu for to sulk.

You'll want to know: عذاب.
عذاب — azāb — "torment; a punishing suffering".
یہ قحط ایک عذاب تھا۔ yih qahat ek azāb thā. This famine was a torment.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
عذاب (azāb) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿadhāb, "torment, punishment". Urdu says its middle letter as z.
