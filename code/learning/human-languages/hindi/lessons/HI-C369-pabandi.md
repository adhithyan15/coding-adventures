## You'll want to know: पाबंदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C369-SANSTHA369-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पाबंदी** — *pābandī* — "a restriction, a ban".

> सरकार ने प्लास्टिक पर पाबंदी का प्रस्ताव रखा।
>
> *sarkār ne plāsṭik par pābandī kā prastāv rakhā.*
>
> The government proposed a ban on plastic.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाबंदी** (*pābandī*) is usually traced to Persian *pābandī*, "a fettering".
