## You'll want to know: tíxǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C364-ALLOW364-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tíxǐng* — "to remind".

> *qǐng tíxǐng wǒ míngtiān dài shū* — Please remind me to bring the book tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tíxǐng* is built from *tí* and *xǐng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dej* and *sengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to carry by the hand' and the second 'to sober up'.
