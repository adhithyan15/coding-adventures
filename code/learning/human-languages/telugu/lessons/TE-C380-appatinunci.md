# అప్పటినుంచి (appaṭinuñci) — from then on; ever since

## You'll want to know: అప్పటినుంచి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C380-KRITAM380-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అప్పటినుంచి** — *appaṭinuñci* — "from then on; ever since".

> అప్పటినుంచి ఆ ఇంట్లో ఎవరూ ఉండటం లేదు.
>
> *appaṭinuñci ā iṇṭlō evarū uṇḍaṭaṁ lēdu.*
>
> Since then nobody has lived in that house.

అప్పటి, of that time, and నుంచి, from.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అప్పటినుంచి** (*appaṭinuñci*) is native Telugu: *appaṭi*, "of that time", from *appuḍu* (DEDR 4559), with *nuñci*, "from".
