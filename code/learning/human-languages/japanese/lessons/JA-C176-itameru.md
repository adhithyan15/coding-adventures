## You'll want to know: いためる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C176-FRY176-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いためる** — *itameru* — "to stir-fry; to fry".

> **あぶらで やさいを いためます。** — *abura de yasai o itamemasu* — "I stir-fry vegetables in oil."

Polite **いためます**, *itamemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いためる** (*itameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
