# ज़ फ़ ख़ क़ — the same dot, a different job

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-140] -->

[PAUSE 1s] The mark from before: where does it sit, and what does it change?

[PAUSE 2s] On **ड** and **ढ** it made a flap. On these four it does something
else — it lets the letter carry a sound that came into Hindi from outside it.

## Script you'll notice: ज़ फ़ ख़ क़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-141]; assesses=[] -->

| letter | with the dot | sound |
|---|---|---|
| ज | ज़ | *za* |
| फ | फ़ | *fa* |
| ख | ख़ | *xa* |
| क | क़ | *qa* |

**ज़** is the *z* of English *zoo*. **फ़** is the *f* of *far*. **ख़** is made
further back than **ख**, at the soft palate. **क़** is made further back than
**क**, at the very top of the throat.

Many speakers say the plain letter for all four and are understood. The dot is
still printed, so a reader has to know it.

## Script: words you already say
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

- **ज़रूर** — certainly · **मेज़** — table · **दरवाज़ा** — door
- **सफ़ेद** — white · **सफ़र** — a journey · **तोहफ़ा** — a gift
- **ख़ुशी** — happiness · **ख़ुशबू** — fragrance
- **मुलाक़ात** — a meeting

**One pair you have met already.** **खाना** and **ख़ाना** were taught together,
and the dot between them was named there as a fact about those two words. It is
the same dot, and it is doing the same job in every word on this page. That
lesson also told you the truth about it: **the dot is often left off in print**,
and a reader sorts the two out from context.

## Writing: the dotted four — copy what you see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

The letter is drawn exactly as you draw it bare. One dot goes below, clear of
the line.

Put your pen on **ज़** and follow it, then **फ़**. Copy slowly and larger than
it is printed.

> This book does not tell you **where to start the character or which way to
> travel**. That is a real thing, taught with real variation from school to
> school, and it is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source.
