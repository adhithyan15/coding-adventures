# ਚੱਲਣਾ (challṇā) — to set off; to go, of a bus or train

## You'll want to know: ਚੱਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C261-TRAIN261-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੱਲਣਾ** — *challṇā* — "to set off; to go, of a bus or train".

> ਪਹਿਲੀ ਬੱਸ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਜੇ ਚੱਲਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *pahilī bass savere chhe vaje challdī hai.*
>
> The first bus sets off at six in the morning.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬੱਸ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਚੱਲੀ, *oh bass aṭṭh vaje challī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੱਲਣਾ** (*challṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*calyati*, "goes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *callṇā*, "to go, move", under it (CDIAL 4721).
