## You'll want to know: verzollen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C417-ZOLL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**verzollen** — "to declare (goods at customs); to pay duty on".

> Wir haben nichts zu verzollen.
>
> *We have nothing to declare.*

*Nichts zu verzollen* — nothing to declare, the green exit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verzollen* is built from *ver-* and *Zoll* 'customs duty', from Old High German *zol* (EtymDB), a word usually traced to Late Latin *tolōneum*.
