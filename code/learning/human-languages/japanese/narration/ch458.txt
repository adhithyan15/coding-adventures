Japanese, chapter 458: Jobs People Do.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
べんごし (bengoshi) — a lawyer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to apply, then the Japanese for recruitment.

You'll want to know: べんごし.
べんごし — bengoshi — "a lawyer".
あには べんごしに なりたいです。 — ani wa bengoshi ni naritai desu — "My elder brother wants to become a lawyer."

The word, taken apart.
べんごし (bengoshi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ben, "speech" (Middle Chinese bjenX), go, "protect" (huH), and shi, "gentleman" (dzriX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
