## Fixed — Arabic driving lesson defers drawing a new letter

- AR-C03-kayfa no longer tells a listener to draw **ف** in bare narrated prose. The voice-drivable lesson asks learners to hear its *f* sound now and leaves the written shape to a later lesson. This keeps the first encounter gentle without inviting a writing task while driving (issue #12070).
- AR-C04-ila-liqaa similarly replaces “read them for now and draw them later” with a listening-first introduction and leaves the two unfamiliar shapes to later writing lessons.
