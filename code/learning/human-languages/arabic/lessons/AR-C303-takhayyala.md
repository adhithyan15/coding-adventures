## You'll want to know: تخيل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C303-USTURA303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تخيل** — *takhayyala* — "to imagine".

> تخيلت البنت أنها تطير فوق المدينة.
>
> *takhayyalat al-bint annahā taṭīru fawqa al-madīna.*
>
> The girl imagined that she was flying over the city.

Present: يتخيل, *yatakhayyal*.

It often takes أن: تخيلت أنها …, she imagined that she ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تخيل** (*takhayyala*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-y-l* (Lane), "to think, to fancy", the root of *khayāl*, imagination. It is Form V, "to picture to oneself".
