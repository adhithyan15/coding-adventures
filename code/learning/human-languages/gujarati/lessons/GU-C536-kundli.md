# કુંડળી (kunḍḷī) — a birth chart

## You'll want to know: કુંડળી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C536-MUHURT536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કુંડળી** — *kunḍḷī* — "a birth chart".

> જો બંનેની કુંડળી મળશે તો લગ્ન નક્કી થશે.
>
> *jo bannenī kunḍḷī maḷśe to lagna nakkī thaśe.*
>
> If both their birth charts match, the wedding will be fixed.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કુંડળી** (*kunḍḷī*) goes back to Sanskrit *kuṇḍala*, "a ring, a circle" (Monier-Williams); the chart of the heavens at one's birth is drawn as a figure.
