## You'll want to know: rènlǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C456-LOST456-05]; assesses=[] -->

*rènlǐng* — "to claim lost property".

> *shīzhǔ kěyǐ qù fúwùtái rènlǐng qiánbāo* — The owner can claim the wallet at the information desk.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rènlǐng* is built from *rèn* 'to recognise' and *lǐng* 'to receive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyinH* and *ljengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to know' and the second 'to take charge of'; the sense 'to receive' is not given there.
