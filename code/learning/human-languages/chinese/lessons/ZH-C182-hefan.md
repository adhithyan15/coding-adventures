## You'll want to know: héfàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C182-EATOUT182-05]; assesses=[] -->

*héfàn* — "boxed meal; packed lunch".

> *wǒmen zhōngwǔ chī héfàn* — We have boxed lunches at midday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*héfàn* is built from *hé* 'box' and 饭 'meal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hop* and *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a dish with a lid.
