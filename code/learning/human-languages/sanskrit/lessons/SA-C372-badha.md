## You'll want to know: बाधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C372-VIGHNA372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बाधा** — *bādhā* — "a hindrance; harm".

> यदि मार्गे बाधा अस्ति, तर्हि अन्यं मार्गं गच्छतु।
>
> *yadi mārge bādhā asti, tarhi anyaṃ mārgaṃ gacchatu.*
>
> If there is a hindrance on the road, take another road.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाधा** (*bādhā*) comes from the root √bādh, "to press, to harass"; MW gives *bādha* "annoyance, obstacle, trouble" and the feminine *bādhā* "injury, hurt" (MW).
