Spanish, chapter 764: Recuerdos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
inolvidable — unforgettable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the painter, then the Spanish for to photograph.

You'll want to know: inolvidable.
inolvidable — "unforgettable".
Fue un viaje inolvidable. It was an unforgettable trip.

The word, taken apart.
inolvidable is built on olvidar "to forget", with in- and -able; olvidar is inherited from a spoken Latin oblītāre (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
el olvido — forgetting; oblivion; oversight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to photograph, then the Spanish for unforgettable.

You'll want to know: el olvido.
el olvido — "forgetting; oblivion; oversight".
Perdón, fue un olvido. Sorry, I forgot.
Caer en el olvido — to be forgotten.

The word, taken apart.
olvido is built on olvidar, which is inherited from a spoken Latin oblītāre (EtymDB), from oblītus, the participle of oblīvīscī "to forget".

Tengo que memorizar estos números. I have to learn these numbers by heart.

The word, taken apart.
memorizar is built on memoria "memory" with the ending -izar; memoria is a learned borrowing from Latin memoria, from memor "mindful".

Lesson 5 of 5.
el diario — the diary; the daily paper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to memorize, then the Spanish for to relive.

You'll want to know: el diario.
el diario — "the diary; the daily paper".
De pequeña escribía un diario. As a girl I kept a diary.
Also a daily newspaper, and the adjective daily: la vida diaria. A diario — every day.

The word, taken apart.
diario comes from Latin diārium "a daily allowance" (EtymDB), built on diēs "day".
