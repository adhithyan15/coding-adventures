## You'll want to know: لایحه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C410-LAYEHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لایحه** (*lâyehe*) — "a draft law, a bill (in parliament)".

> دولت یک لایحه تازه به مجلس فرستاد.
>
> *dowlat yek lâyehe-ye tâze be majles ferestâd.*
>
> The government sent a new bill to parliament.

A government bill; once passed it becomes a *qânun*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لایحه** is usually traced to Arabic *lā'iḥa* "a written statement, a memorandum".
