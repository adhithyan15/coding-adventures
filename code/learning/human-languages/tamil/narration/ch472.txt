Tamil, chapter 472: Kinds of People.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சாது (cātu) — a meek person; a gentle soul.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for maturity, then the Tamil for negligence.

You'll want to know: சாது.
சாது — cātu — "a meek person; a gentle soul".
என் தாத்தா ஒரு சாது, அவருக்குக் கோபமே வராது. eṉ tāttā oru cātu, avarukkuk kōpamē varātu. My grandfather is a gentle soul; he never gets angry.
Also a holy man; சாதுவான மாடு, a docile cow.

The word, taken apart.
சாது (cātu) is borrowed from Sanskrit sādhu, "straight, good; a good man" (CDIAL 13337).

Lesson 2 of 5.
வெகுளி (vekuḷi) — a naive person; an innocent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for negligence, then the Tamil for a meek person.

You'll want to know: வெகுளி.
வெகுளி — vekuḷi — "a naive person; an innocent".
அவள் ஒரு வெகுளி, எல்லோரையும் நம்புவாள். avaḷ oru vekuḷi, ellōraiyum nampuvāḷ. She is naive; she trusts everyone.
வெகுளித்தனம், naivety.

The word, taken apart.
வெகுளி (vekuḷi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5464 gives it the older sense "anger". How it came to mean an artless person is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ஏமாளி (ēmāḷi) — a gullible person; a dupe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a meek person, then the Tamil for a naive person.

You'll want to know: ஏமாளி.
ஏமாளி — ēmāḷi — "a gullible person; a dupe".
நான் ஏமாளி இல்லை, உன் பொய்யை நம்ப மாட்டேன். nāṉ ēmāḷi illai, uṉ poyyai nampa māṭṭēṉ. I am no fool; I will not believe your lie.
From the same root as ஏமாற்று "to cheat"; -ஆளி means one who is.

The word, taken apart.
ஏமாளி (ēmāḷi) is native Dravidian (DEDR 898), in the entry of ஏமாறு "to be deceived".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a naive person, then the Tamil for a gullible person.

You'll want to know: கஞ்சன்.
கஞ்சன் — kañcaṉ — "a miser".
அந்தப் பணக்காரன் ஒரு கஞ்சன், யாருக்கும் உதவ மாட்டான். antap paṇakkāraṉ oru kañcaṉ, yārukkum utava māṭṭāṉ. That rich man is a miser; he will help nobody.
கஞ்சத்தனம், stinginess.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of கஞ்சன் (kañcaṉ) is not settled; it has the -அன் ending of a person, but the stem is not in our sources.
