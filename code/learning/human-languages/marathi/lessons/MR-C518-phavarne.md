## You'll want to know: फवारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C518-YANTRA518-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फवारणे** — *phavārṇe* — "to spray".

> यंत्राने शेतावर औषध फवारलं.
>
> *yantrāne śetāvar auṣadh phavārlaṁ.*
>
> The machine sprayed the field with pesticide.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने औषध फवारलं, *tyāne auṣadh phavārlaṁ*.

The noun is फवारा, a spray or a fountain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फवारणे** (*phavārṇe*) is built on फवारा, "a spray, a fountain", a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic *fawwāra*, "fountain".
