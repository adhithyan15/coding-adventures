Bengali, chapter 51: To learn, To forget, To want, To bring, To count.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
শেখা (shekhā) — to learn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sing, then the Bengali for to dance.

You'll want to know: শেখা.
শেখা — shekhā — "to learn".

The word, taken apart.
শেখা (shekhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śikṣate, "learns" (CDIAL 12430).

Lesson 3 of 5.
চাওয়া (chāowā) — to want.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to learn, then the Bengali for to forget.

You'll want to know: চাওয়া.
চাওয়া — chāowā — "to want".

The word, taken apart.
চাওয়া (chāowā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cāh, "see, look for, desire", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4775). Its source is uncertain: CDIAL reports J. Bloch's view that it is a deformed form of a verb from Sanskrit cakṣate, "sees", and leaves the question open.
