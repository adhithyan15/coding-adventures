Kannada, chapter 485: The Monsoon Arrives.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುಂಗಾರು (muṅgāru) — the monsoon; the early rains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for dampness, then the Kannada for to worsen.

You'll want to know: ಮುಂಗಾರು.
ಮುಂಗಾರು — muṅgāru — "the monsoon; the early rains".
ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಮುಂಗಾರು ಒಂದು ವಾರ ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದಿತು. ī varṣa muṅgāru ondu vāra taḍavāgi banditu. This year the monsoon came a week late.
The June rains that end the summer heat.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಂಗಾರು (muṅgāru) is a native Kannada compound of mun, "front, first" (DEDR 5020), and kār, "blackness, the rainy season" (DEDR 1278).

ಜಡಿಮಳೆ — jaḍimaḷe — "a downpour; heavy rain".
ಜಡಿಮಳೆಯಿಂದ ರಸ್ತೆಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ನೀರು ನಿಂತಿತು. jaḍimaḷeyinda rastegaḷalli nīru nintitu. Because of the downpour water stood on the roads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for drizzle, then the Kannada for a downpour.

You'll want to know: ತೊಯ್ಯು.
ತೊಯ್ಯು — toyyu — "to get soaked; to get drenched".
ಕೊಡೆ ಇಲ್ಲದೆ ನಾನು ಪೂರ್ತಿ ತೊಯ್ದೆ. koḍe illade nānu pūrti toyde. Without an umbrella I got soaked through.
Past (I …): ತೊಯ್ದೆ, toyde.
ಒದ್ದೆ is wet; ತೊಯ್ಯು (toyyu) is to become wet through.

The word, taken apart.
ತೊಯ್ಯು (toyyu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3555 lists Kannada tuy, toy, toyyu, "to be or become wet or moist".
