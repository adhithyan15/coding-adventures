## You'll want to know: ಹೊದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C506-NIDDE506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊದೆ** — *hode* — "to cover oneself; to wrap".

> ಚಳಿಗೆ ಅಜ್ಜ ಕಂಬಳಿ ಹೊದ್ದು ಮಲಗಿದರು.
>
> *caḷige ajja kambaḷi hoddu malagidaru.*
>
> In the cold, grandfather slept wrapped in a blanket.

Past (I …): ಹೊದ್ದೆ, *hodde*.

The participle is ಹೊದ್ದು: ಕಂಬಳಿ ಹೊದ್ದು, wrapped in a blanket. ಹೊದ್ದುಕೊ, to wrap yourself up. Keep it apart from ಹೋದೆ, I went, with a long ō.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊದೆ** (*hode*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4509 lists Kannada *pode*, "to put over, put on, wrap round", and *podisu*, "to cover". Old p- became h-.
