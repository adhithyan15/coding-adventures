Arabic, chapter 493: Regret and pity.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ندم (nadima) — to regret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to envy, then the Arabic for to be jealous.

You'll want to know: ندم.
ندم — nadima — "to regret".
ندم الولد لأنه كذب على أمه. nadima al-walad li-annahu kadhaba ʿalā ummihi. The boy was sorry because he had lied to his mother.
Present: يندم, yandam.
It takes على: ندم (nadima) على ما قال.

The word, taken apart.
ندم (nadima) is built on the native Arabic root n-d-m, "to repent, to regret" (Lane). The noun nadam is regret.

نادم (nādim) is built on the native Arabic root n-d-m, "to repent, to regret" (Lane). Nādim is the active participle: one who regrets.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: شفقة.

شعرت بالشفقة على الحصان المتعب. shaʿartu bi-sh-shafaqa ʿalā al-ḥiṣān al-mutʿab. I felt pity for the tired horse.

The word, taken apart.
شفقة (shafaqa) is built on the native Arabic root sh-f-q, "to be anxious for, to pity" (Lane). Shafaqa is a tender worry for someone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
عاتب (ʿātaba) — to reproach (gently).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for regretful, then the Arabic for pity.

You'll want to know: عاتب.
عاتب — ʿātaba — "to reproach (gently)".
عاتبت الأم ابنها لأنه لم يتصل بها. ʿātabat al-umm ibnahā li-annahu lam yattaṣil bihā. The mother reproached her son because he had not phoned her.
Present: يعاتب, yuʿātib.
It is a gentle, loving complaint, not anger.

The word, taken apart.
عاتب (ʿātaba) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-t-b, "to blame, to be angry with" (Lane). Lane gives yaʿtibu for Form I; Form III ʿātaba is to reproach someone face to face.
