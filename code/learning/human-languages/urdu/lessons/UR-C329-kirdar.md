## You'll want to know: کردار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C329-AFSANA329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کردار** — *kirdār* — "a character (in a story)".

> اس ناول میں دس کردار ہیں۔
>
> *is nāvil meṅ das kirdār haiṅ.*
>
> There are ten characters in this novel.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کردار** (*kirdār*) is usually traced to Persian *kirdār*, "a deed, conduct"; the sense of a person in a story is thought to have come from the part one plays.
