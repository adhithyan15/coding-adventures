## You'll want to know: હિજરાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C407-ABROAD407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હિજરાવું** — *hijrāvũ* — "to pine for home; to grieve inwardly".

> પરદેશમાં દાદી વતન માટે હિજરાતી હતી.
>
> *pardeśmā̃ dādī vatan māṭe hijrātī hatī.*
>
> Abroad, grandmother used to pine for her home town.

Past (she …): તે ઘણી હિજરાઈ, *te ghaṇī hijrāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હિજરાવું** (*hijrāvũ*) is usually traced to Persian *hijrān*, "separation", a word of Arabic origin: the ache of being far from people and places you love.
