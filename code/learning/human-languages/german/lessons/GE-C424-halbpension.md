## You'll want to know: die Halbpension
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C424-GASTHOF-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Halbpension** — "the half board".

> Mit Halbpension bekommen wir Frühstück und Abendessen.
>
> *With half board we get breakfast and dinner.*

No plural. *Die Vollpension* — full board.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Halbpension* is built from *halb* 'half', inherited from Proto-Germanic *halbaz* (EtymDB), and *Pension* 'board and lodging', borrowed from French *pension* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *pēnsiō* 'payment' (Lewis & Short).
