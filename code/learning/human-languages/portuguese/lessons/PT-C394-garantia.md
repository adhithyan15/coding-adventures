## You'll want to know: a garantia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C394-GARANTIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a garantia** — "the guarantee, the warranty".

> A máquina de lavar tem dois anos de garantia.
>
> *The washing machine has a two-year warranty.*

*Estar na garantia* — to be under warranty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garantia* is usually traced to French *garantie*, which EtymDB takes back to Old French *garantie* "protection, defence", on *garantir* and *garant* "guarantor", a word with Germanic cousins such as Old High German *werento* "guarantor" (EtymDB).
