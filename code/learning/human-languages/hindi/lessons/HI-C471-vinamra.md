## You'll want to know: विनम्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C471-VINAMRA471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विनम्र** — *vinamra* — "polite; humble".

> मीरा बड़ों से हमेशा विनम्र होकर बात करती है।
>
> *Mīrā baṛoṁ se hameśā vinamra hokar bāt kartī hai.*
>
> Mira always speaks politely to her elders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विनम्र** (*vinamra*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vinamra*, "bent down, submissive, humble, modest" (Monier-Williams): someone who bows a little.
