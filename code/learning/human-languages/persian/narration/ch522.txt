Persian, chapter 522: Writing Verse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قافیه (qâfiye) — a rhyme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a quatrain, then the Persian for a couplet.

You'll want to know: قافیه.
قافیه (qâfiye) — "a rhyme".
این دو کلمه با هم قافیه دارند. in do kalame bâ ham qâfiye dârand. These two words rhyme.
qâfiye dâshtan — to rhyme. Plural qavâfi (Arabic).

The word, taken apart.
qâfiye is a loan, usually traced to Arabic qāfiya, 'rhyme'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سرودن (sorudan) — to compose (poetry).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a couplet, then the Persian for a rhyme.

You'll want to know: سرودن.
سرودن (sorudan) — "to compose (poetry)".
او این شعر را در جوانی سرود. u in she'r râ dar javâni sorud. He composed this poem in his youth.
Literary; present stem sorâ-: misorâyad. In speech people say she'r goftan.

The word, taken apart.
sorudan is a native Persian verb, with the present stem sorâ-, but EtymDB lists it without a source, and its older history is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
دکلمه کردن (dekleme kardan) — to recite (poetry).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a rhyme, then the Persian for to compose.

You'll want to know: دکلمه کردن.
دکلمه کردن (dekleme kardan) — "to recite (poetry)".
شاگردها در جشن شعر دکلمه کردند. shâgerdhâ dar jashn she'r dekleme kardand. The pupils recited poems at the celebration.
dekleme 'recitation' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
dekleme is a modern loan, usually traced to French déclamer, 'to declaim'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
الهام گرفتن (elhâm gereftan) — to be inspired, to draw inspiration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to compose, then the Persian for to recite.

You'll want to know: الهام گرفتن.
الهام گرفتن (elhâm gereftan) — "to be inspired, to draw inspiration".
شاعر از دریا الهام گرفت. shâ'er az daryâ elhâm gereft. The poet was inspired by the sea.
az chizi elhâm gereftan; elhâm 'inspiration' + gereftan 'to take'.

The word, taken apart.
elhâm was borrowed from Arabic ilhām, 'inspiration' (EtymDB). gereftan, 'to take', is inherited Persian.

تخیل (takhayyol) — "imagination".
