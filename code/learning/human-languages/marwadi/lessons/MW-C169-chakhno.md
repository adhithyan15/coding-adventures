## You'll want to know: चाखणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C169-ROTI169-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चाखणो** — *chākhṇo* — "to taste".

> हूं दाल चाखूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ dāl chākhū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I taste the dal.

Past (I …): म्हैं दाल चाखी, *mhaĩ dāl chākhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चाखणो** (*chākhṇo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*cakṣati*, "tastes"; CDIAL lists Hindi *cākhnā* and Gujarati *cākhvũ* (CDIAL 4557). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
