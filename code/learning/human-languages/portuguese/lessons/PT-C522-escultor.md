## You'll want to know: o escultor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C522-GALERIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o escultor** — "sculptor".

> O escultor trabalha com madeira.
>
> *The sculptor works with wood.*

A woman is *a escultora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escultor* comes from Latin *sculptor* "a carver" (EtymDB), from *sculpere* "to carve".
