Urdu, chapter 83: Ring, Watch, Eleven, Twelve, Thirteen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انگوٹھی (angūṭhī) — a ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sock, then the Urdu for a pocket.

You'll want to know: انگوٹھی.
انگوٹھی — angūṭhī — "a ring".

The word, taken apart.
انگوٹھی (angūṭhī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aṅguṣṭhiya, "of the thumb", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 138). It is built on aṅguṣṭha, "thumb".
