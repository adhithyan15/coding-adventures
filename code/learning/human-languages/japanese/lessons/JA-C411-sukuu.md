## You'll want to know: すくう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C411-OGAWA411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**すくう** — *sukuu* — "to scoop up".

> **いずみの みずを てで すくいました。** — *izumi no mizu o te de sukuimashita* — "I scooped up water from the spring with my hands."

Polite **すくいます**, *sukuimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが みずを すくいました。**, *kodomo ga mizu o sukuimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すくう** (*sukuu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
