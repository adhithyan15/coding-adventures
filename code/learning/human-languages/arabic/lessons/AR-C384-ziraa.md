# زراعة (zirāʿa) — agriculture

## You'll want to know: زراعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C384-TANMIYA384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**زراعة** — *zirāʿa* — "agriculture".

> يعيش أهل القرية من الزراعة.
>
> *yaʿīshu ahl al-qarya min az-zirāʿa.*
>
> The village people live from farming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زراعة** (*zirāʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-r-ʿ*, "to sow, to plant" (Lane). *Zirāʿa* is the work of sowing: agriculture.
