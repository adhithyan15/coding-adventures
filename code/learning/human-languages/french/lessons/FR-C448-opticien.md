## You'll want to know: l'opticien
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C448-OPTICIEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'opticien** — "the optician".

> L'opticien a réparé mes lunettes tout de suite.
>
> *The optician fixed my glasses straight away.*

A masculine noun; feminine *l'opticienne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*opticien* is built on *optique* 'optics', a learned word from Latin *opticus*, from Greek *optikós* 'of seeing' (EtymDB). EtymDB lists *opticien* itself without a source.
