## You'll want to know: अहंकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C463-GARVA463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अहंकार** — *ahaṅkār* — "ego; arrogance".

> त्याच्या अहंकारामुळे मित्र दूर गेले.
>
> *tyācyā ahaṅkārāmuḷe mitra dūr gele.*
>
> His friends drifted away because of his ego.

Masculine.

अहं, I, + कार, making: the sense of I.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अहंकार** (*ahaṅkār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ahaṃkāra*, "conception of one's individuality" (Monier-Williams), built on *aham*, "I".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
