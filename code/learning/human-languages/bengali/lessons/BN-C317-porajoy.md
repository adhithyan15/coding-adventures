## You'll want to know: পরাজয়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C317-BIR317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পরাজয়** — *pôrājôy* — "defeat".

> পরাজয়ের পরে রাজা দেশ ছেড়ে পালালেন।
>
> *pôrājôyer pôre rājā desh chheṛe pālālen.*
>
> After the defeat the king fled the country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরাজয়** (*pôrājôy*) is Sanskrit *parājaya*, 'loss, defeat' (Monier-Williams), with *parā-*, 'away', before *jaya*, victory — **জয়**.
