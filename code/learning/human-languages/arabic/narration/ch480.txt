Arabic, chapter 480: The forecast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نشرة جوية (nashra jawwiyya) — a weather forecast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to exchange, then the Arabic for to pack.

You'll want to know: نشرة جوية.
نشرة جوية — nashra jawwiyya — "a weather forecast".
شاهدنا النشرة الجوية في التلفاز. shāhadnā an-nashra al-jawwiyya fī at-tilfāz. We watched the weather forecast on television.

The word, taken apart.
نشرة جوية (nashra jawwiyya) is built from two native Arabic words: nashra, "a bulletin", on the root n-sh-r, "to spread out" (Lane), and jawwiyya, "of the air", on j-w-w (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
درجة (daraja) — a degree; a class (of travel).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pack, then the Arabic for a weather forecast.

You'll want to know: درجة.
درجة — daraja — "a degree; a class (of travel)".
كانت درجة (daraja) الحرارة مرتفعة اليوم. kānat darajat al-ḥarāra murtafiʿa al-yawm. The temperature was high today.
Plural: درجات, darajāt.
darajat al-ḥarāra is the temperature; ad-daraja al-ūlā is first class.

The word, taken apart.
درجة (daraja) is built on the native Arabic root d-r-j, "to go step by step" (Lane). A daraja is a step.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ارتفع (irtafaʿa) — to increase; to rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a weather forecast, then the Arabic for a degree.

You'll want to know: ارتفع.
ارتفع — irtafaʿa — "to increase; to rise".
ارتفعت درجة (daraja) الحرارة عند الظهر. irtafaʿat darajat al-ḥarāra ʿinda aẓ-ẓuhr. The temperature rose at noon.
Present: يرتفع, yartafiʿ.

The word, taken apart.
ارتفع (irtafaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root r-f-ʿ, "to raise" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to rise".
