## You'll want to know: exsistō, exsistere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C375-EXSISTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**exsistō, exsistere** — "to arise, to come forth; to exist".

> Ex parvīs initiīs magnum bellum exstitit.
>
> *From small beginnings a great war arose.*

*exsistō, exsistere, exstitī*. *Ex* + *sistō*, to set, to stand: to stand out, to appear. English *exist*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exsistō* is built from *ex* "out" and *sistō* "to stand, to set" (Lewis & Short): to step forth. English *exist* comes from it.
