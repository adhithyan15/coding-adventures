## You'll want to know: प्रतिध्वनिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C315-VISPHOTA315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिध्वनिः** — *pratidhvaniḥ* — "an echo".

> पर्वतेषु विस्फोटस्य प्रतिध्वनिः भवति।
>
> *parvateṣu visphoṭasya pratidhvaniḥ bhavati.*
>
> The blast echoes in the hills.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिध्वनिः** (*pratidhvaniḥ*) is built from *prati*, "back, against", and *dhvani*, "a sound", from the root √dhvan; MW gives *pratidhvani* as an echo (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
