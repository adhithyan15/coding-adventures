Punjabi, chapter 241: Cutting Vegetables.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਛਿੱਲਣਾ (chhillṇā) — to peel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a pestle, then the Punjabi for to pound.

You'll want to know: ਛਿੱਲਣਾ.
ਛਿੱਲਣਾ — chhillṇā — "to peel".
ਮੈਂ ਚਾਕੂ ਨਾਲ ਆਲੂ ਛਿੱਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ chākū nāl ālū chhilldī hāṁ. I peel the potatoes with a knife.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਲੂ ਛਿੱਲੇ, maiṁ ālū chhille.

The word, taken apart.
ਛਿੱਲਣਾ (chhillṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed chilla, "torn, cut"; CDIAL lists Punjabi chillṇā, "to cut with a knife, peel" (CDIAL 5051).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਕਤਰਨਾ (katarnā) — to chop finely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to pound, then the Punjabi for to peel.

You'll want to know: ਕਤਰਨਾ.
ਕਤਰਨਾ — katarnā — "to chop finely".
ਮੈਂ ਸਾਗ ਕਤਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ sāg katardī hāṁ. I chop the greens finely.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਾਗ ਕਤਰਿਆ, maiṁ sāg katriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਤਰਨਾ (katarnā) is inherited, built on Sanskrit kartari, "scissors, knife"; CDIAL lists Punjabi katarnā, "to clip, shear" (CDIAL 2858), and does not give the kitchen sense "chop finely".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਮਿਰਚ (mirach) — a chilli; pepper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to chop finely, then the Punjabi for to split.

You'll want to know: ਮਿਰਚ.
ਮਿਰਚ — mirach — "a chilli; pepper".
ਇਹ ਹਰੀ ਮਿਰਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਤਿੱਖੀ ਹੈ। eh harī mirach bahut tikkhī hai. This green chilli is very hot.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਿਰਚ (mirach) is inherited from Sanskrit marīca, "peppercorn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi mirc, "black pepper" (CDIAL 9875). CDIAL does not give the everyday sense "chilli", and it notes that the Sanskrit word may itself be Austroasiatic.

The word, taken apart.
ਵੇਸਣ (vesaṇ) is inherited from Sanskrit vēsana, "flour of a particular vegetable product"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vesaṇ, "gram flour" (CDIAL 12133).
