## You'll want to know: せっけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C147-WASH147-05]; assesses=[] -->

**せっけん** — *sekken* — "soap".

> **せっけんで てを あらいます。** — *sekken de te o araimasu* — "I wash my hands with soap."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せっけん** (*sekken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *seki*, "stone" (Middle Chinese *dzyek*) and *ken*, "alkaline salt" (*keamX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
