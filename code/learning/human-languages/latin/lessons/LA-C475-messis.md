## You'll want to know: messis, messis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C475-MESSIS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**messis, messis** — "the harvest, the crop".

> Aestāte messis est, et omnēs in agrīs labōrant.
>
> *In summer it is harvest time, and everyone works in the fields.*

Third declension, feminine: *messis, messis f.*, accusative often *messem*. From *metō* 'to reap'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*messis* is built on *metō* 'to reap, to mow' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from PIE *h₂meh₁-* 'to mow'. English *mow* and *meadow* are Germanic cousins.
