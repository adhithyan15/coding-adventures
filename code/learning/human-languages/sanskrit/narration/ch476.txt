Sanskrit, chapter 476: Strength and Build.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शक्तिः (śaktiḥ) — strength.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for scratches, then the Sanskrit for a hiccup.

You'll want to know: शक्तिः.
शक्तिः — śaktiḥ — "strength".
व्यायामेन शक्तिः वर्धते। vyāyāmena śaktiḥ vardhate. Exercise builds strength.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
शक्तिः (śaktiḥ) comes from the root √śak, "to be able", the root of शक्नोति; MW gives "power, ability, strength" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: पोषयति.
पोषयति — poṣayati — "nourishes".
माता शिशुं क्षीरेण पोषयति। mātā śiśuṃ kṣīreṇa poṣayati. The mother nourishes her baby with milk.
Past (she …): सा शिशुं पोषितवती, sā śiśuṃ poṣitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
पोषयति (poṣayati) is built on the causative of the root √puṣ, "to thrive": to make thrive; MW gives "to rear, nourish, feed" (MW).
