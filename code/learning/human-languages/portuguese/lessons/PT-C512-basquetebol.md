## You'll want to know: o basquetebol
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C512-PAVILHAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o basquetebol** — "basketball".

> Ele é alto e joga basquetebol.
>
> *He is tall and plays basketball.*

In Brazil: *o basquete*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*basquetebol* was borrowed from English *basketball* (EtymDB).
