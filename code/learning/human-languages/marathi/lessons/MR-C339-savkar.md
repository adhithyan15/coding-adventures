## You'll want to know: सावकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C339-NAYAK339-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सावकार** — *sāvkār* — "a moneylender".

> गोष्टीतला सावकार गरीब लोकांना फार त्रास द्यायचा.
>
> *goṣṭītlā sāvkār garīb lokānnā phār trās dyāycā.*
>
> The moneylender in the story used to give poor people a lot of trouble.

Masculine.

सावकाराकडून कर्ज घेणे, to borrow from a moneylender.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सावकार** (*sāvkār*) is built on *sāv*, 'a banker', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *sādhu*, 'good' (CDIAL 13337), with the ending *-kār*, 'one who does'.
