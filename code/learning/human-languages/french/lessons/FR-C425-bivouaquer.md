## You'll want to know: bivouaquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C425-CAMPING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bivouaquer** — "to bivouac, to sleep out in the open".

> En montagne, ils ont bivouaqué sous les étoiles.
>
> *In the mountains they slept out under the stars.*

*Un bivouac* is one night in the open, often in the mountains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bivouaquer* is built on *bivouac*, which is usually traced to Swiss or Alsatian German *Biwacht* 'extra night watch'. EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
