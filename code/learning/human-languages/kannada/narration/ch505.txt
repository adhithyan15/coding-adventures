Kannada, chapter 505: Effort and Neglect.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಶ್ರದ್ಧೆ (śraddhe) — dedication; devotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a coward, then the Kannada for a stammer.

You'll want to know: ಶ್ರದ್ಧೆ.
ಶ್ರದ್ಧೆ — śraddhe — "dedication; devotion".
ಅವಳು ಶ್ರದ್ಧೆಯಿಂದ ಸಂಗೀತ ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಾಳೆ. avaḷu śraddheyinda saṅgīta kaliyuttāḷe. She learns music with dedication.
ಶ್ರದ್ಧೆಯಿಂದ, wholeheartedly.

The word, taken apart.
ಶ್ರದ್ಧೆ (śraddhe) is borrowed from Sanskrit śraddhā, "trust, faith" (CDIAL 12678); in Kannada it is steady devotion to a task.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ನಿರ್ಲಕ್ಷ್ಯ (nirlakṣya) — neglect; carelessness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a stammer, then the Kannada for dedication.

You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ಲಕ್ಷ್ಯ.
ನಿರ್ಲಕ್ಷ್ಯ — nirlakṣya — "neglect; carelessness".
ಚಾಲಕನ ನಿರ್ಲಕ್ಷ್ಯದಿಂದ ಅಪಘಾತ ಆಯಿತು. cālakana nirlakṣyadinda apaghāta āyitu. The accident happened through the driver's carelessness.
The verb is ನಿರ್ಲಕ್ಷಿಸು, to ignore.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರ್ಲಕ್ಷ್ಯ (nirlakṣya) is built from Sanskrit nir-, "without", and lakṣya, "what is to be marked or watched"; Monier-Williams lists nirlakṣya as "unobservable" (Monier-Williams). Kannada uses it for failing to watch.

You'll want to know: ಶ್ರಮಿಸು.
ಶ್ರಮಿಸು — śramisu — "to strive; to work hard".
ರೈತರು ಬೆಳೆಗಾಗಿ ಹಗಲು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಶ್ರಮಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ. raitaru beḷegāgi hagalu rātri śramisuttāre. Farmers toil day and night for the crop.
Past (I …): ಶ್ರಮಿಸಿದೆ, śramiside.
For what: -ಗಾಗಿ. ಶ್ರಮ is toil.

The word, taken apart.
ಶ್ರಮಿಸು (śramisu) is built on Sanskrit śrama, "labour, fatigue" (CDIAL 12682), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಬೇಡು (bēḍu) — to beg; to plead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an addiction, then the Kannada for to strive.

You'll want to know: ಬೇಡು.
ಬೇಡು — bēḍu — "to beg; to plead".
ಮುದುಕ ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಊಟಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಬೇಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. muduka dāriyalli ūṭakkāgi bēḍuttiddanu. The old man was begging for food by the road.
Past (I …): ಬೇಡಿದೆ, bēḍide.
What you beg for takes -ಗಾಗಿ or -ಅನ್ನು. ಭಿಕ್ಷೆ ಬೇಡು (bēḍu), to beg for alms.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೇಡು (bēḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5528 lists Kannada bēḍu, "to wish, desire, beg, entreat, request", in the same article as ಬೇಕು and ಬೇಡ.
