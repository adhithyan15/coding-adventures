# dēmēns, dēmentis — out of one's mind, crazy

## You'll want to know: dēmēns, dēmentis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C510-INSA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dēmēns, dēmentis** — "out of one's mind, crazy".

> Dēmēns es, sī in tempestāte nāvigās.
>
> *You are out of your mind if you sail in a storm.*

One ending. *dē-* 'away from' + *mēns* 'mind'. English *demented*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēmēns** is built on *dē-* "away from" and *mēns* "mind" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *demented* comes from the related Late Latin verb *dēmentō*.
