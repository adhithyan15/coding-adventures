## You'll want to know: انتظام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C383-MANZURI383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**انتظام** — *intizām* — "an arrangement".

> اگر آپ آئیں گے تو کھانے کا انتظام ہو گا۔
>
> *agar āp āeṅ ge to khāne kā intizām ho gā.*
>
> If you come, there will be food laid on.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتظام** (*intizām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *intiẓām*, "being put in order".
