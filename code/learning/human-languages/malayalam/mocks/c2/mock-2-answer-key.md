## Writing

Task 1 must synthesise the three maintenance sources for a finance committee without
turning uncertainty into indecision, preserve the worker and resident evidence, and
produce an independent 220–280-word Malayalam briefing. Task 2 must sustain a
420–520-word formal address in a contrasting register, distinguish remembering from
freezing practice, integrate a serious counter-position, and formulate a reversible
decision rule.

## Speaking

Allow keyword and relationship-diagram notes only. The presentation must mediate
access, livelihood, and ecological time rather than rank sources mechanically.
Negotiation must expose hidden costs, repair audience mismatch, and reach a conditional
joint resolution. Use all six follow-ups, naming the second audience in each, to test
defence and reformulation, then score the complete recording against the shared criteria.
