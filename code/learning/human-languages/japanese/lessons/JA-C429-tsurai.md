## You'll want to know: つらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C429-DOWN429-04]; assesses=[] -->

**つらい** — *tsurai* — "hard to bear; painful".

> **にゅういんは つらかったです。** — *nyūin wa tsurakatta desu* — "Being in hospital was hard to bear."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つらい** (*tsurai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
