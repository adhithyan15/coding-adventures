## You'll want to know: निवाडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C359-KHATLA359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निवाडा** — *nivāḍā* — "a verdict; a settlement".

> न्यायाधीशांनी शेवटी निवाडा दिला.
>
> *nyāyādhīśānnī śevṭī nivāḍā dilā.*
>
> The judge finally gave the verdict.

Masculine.

निवाडा देणे or करणे, to give a ruling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवाडा** (*nivāḍā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *nivāḍā*, "settlement", under *\*nirvarati*, "restrains" (CDIAL 7392).
