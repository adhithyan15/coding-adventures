Lesson 2 of 5.
कदी (kadī) — sometimes, ever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for now, then the Marwadi for always.

You'll want to know: कदी.
कदी — kadī — "sometimes, ever".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL has no entry for कदी (kadī), so its history is not settled. Its k- is the old question stem: CDIAL lists Hindi kabhī, "ever", under Sanskrit evam eva, "exactly so", crossed with the stem ka- (CDIAL 2528), but nothing with the shape of कदी (kadī).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पैलां (pailā̃) — before.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for quickly, then the Marwadi for a delay; late.

You'll want to know: पैलां.
पैलां — pailā̃ — "before".

The word, taken apart.
पैलां (pailā̃) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from prathila, "first", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari pahalo under it (CDIAL 8652). पैलां (pailā̃), "before", is a form of the word for "first", built on Sanskrit prathama, "first".
