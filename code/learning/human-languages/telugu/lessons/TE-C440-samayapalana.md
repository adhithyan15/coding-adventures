# సమయపాలన (samayapālana) — punctuality

## You'll want to know: సమయపాలన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C440-ALASYAM440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సమయపాలన** — *samayapālana* — "punctuality".

> మా డ్రైవరుకు సమయపాలన చాలా ముఖ్యం.
>
> *mā ḍraivaruku samayapālana cālā mukhyaṁ.*
>
> Punctuality matters a great deal to our driver.

సమయం, time, + పాలన, keeping: keeping time. పాలన alone is rule or administration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమయపాలన** (*samayapālana*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *samaya*, "an appointed time" (CDIAL 13185), and *pālana*, "guarding, keeping" (CDIAL 8128).
