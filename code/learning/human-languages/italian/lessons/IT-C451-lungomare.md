## You'll want to know: il lungomare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C451-PASSEGGIATA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il lungomare** — "the seafront, the promenade".

> La sera passeggiamo sul lungomare.
>
> *In the evening we stroll on the seafront.*

By a lake it is *il lungolago*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lungomare* is built from *lungo* 'along', inherited from Latin *longus* 'long', and *mare* 'sea', inherited from Latin *mare* (EtymDB).
