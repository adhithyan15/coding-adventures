## You'll want to know: てじな
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C239-PASTIME239-04]; assesses=[] -->

**てじな** — *tejina* — "a magic trick; conjuring".

> **おじさんは まつりで てじなを しました。** — *ojisan wa matsuri de tejina o shimashita* — "My uncle did magic tricks at the festival."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てじな** (*tejina*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *te*, "hand", and *shina*, "goods, article", with *sh* voiced to *j* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
