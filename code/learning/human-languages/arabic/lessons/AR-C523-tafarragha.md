## You'll want to know: تفرغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C523-INJAZ523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تفرغ** — *tafarragha* — "to devote oneself".

> تفرغ أبي لأسرته بعد التقاعد.
>
> *tafarragha abī li-usratihi baʿda at-taqāʿud.*
>
> My father devoted himself to his family after he retired.

Present: يتفرغ, *yatafarragh*.

It takes لـ for what you give your time to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفرغ** (*tafarragha*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-gh*, "to be empty, free" (Lane). Fārigh, empty, which you know, is from the same root: Form V is to empty one's time for a thing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
