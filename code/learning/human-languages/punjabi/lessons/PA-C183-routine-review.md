## Guided Practice: a short routine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KARDA-183, PA-FORM-KARDE-183, PA-FORM-UTTHDA-183, PA-FORM-DEKHDA-183, PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05] -->

Read slowly, with the English meanings beside the line:

- **ਉਹ ਉੱਠਦਾ ਹੈ** — he gets up
- **ਉਹ ਦੇਖਦਾ ਹੈ** — he watches
- **ਉਹ ਸੌਂਦਾ ਹੈ** — he sleeps
- **ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ** — I work, said by a man
- **ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਹਨ** — they work, or respectful he works
