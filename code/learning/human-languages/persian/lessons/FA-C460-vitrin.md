## You'll want to know: ویترین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C460-VITRIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ویترین** (*vitrin*) — "a shop window; a display case".

> دختر جلوی ویترین ایستاد و کفشها را نگاه کرد.
>
> *Dokhtar jolow-ye vitrin istâd va kafsh-hâ râ negâh kard.*
>
> The girl stopped in front of the shop window and looked at the shoes.

Also a glass cabinet at home for the best china.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vitrin* was borrowed from French *vitrine* 'shop window', which EtymDB derives from *vitre* 'pane of glass'; that goes back to Latin *vitrum* 'glass' (Lewis & Short).
