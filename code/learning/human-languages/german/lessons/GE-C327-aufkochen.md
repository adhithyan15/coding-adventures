## You'll want to know: aufkochen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C327-KOECHELN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aufkochen** — "to bring to the boil".

> Sie ließ die Milch kurz aufkochen.
>
> *She brought the milk briefly to the boil.*

Separable: *etwas aufkochen lassen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aufkochen* is built on *kochen* "to cook", Old High German *kochōn*, an early loan from Latin *coquus* "a cook" (EtymDB), with *auf* "up".
