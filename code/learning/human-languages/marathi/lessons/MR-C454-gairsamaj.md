## You'll want to know: गैरसमज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C454-RUSVA454-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गैरसमज** — *gairsamaj* — "a misunderstanding".

> एका लहान गैरसमजामुळे दोन मित्र भांडले.
>
> *ekā lahān gairsamjāmuḷe don mitra bhāṇḍle.*
>
> Two friends quarrelled over a small misunderstanding.

Masculine.

गैरसमज होणे, to misunderstand: माझा गैरसमज झाला, I got it wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गैरसमज** (*gairsamaj*) is a Marathi compound built from *gair-*, "not, other", a loan from Persian *ghair* usually traced to Arabic, and समज, "understanding", the noun of समजणे.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
