# راضی (rāzī) — willing; agreed

## You'll want to know: راضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C530-MARZI530-02]; assesses=[] -->

**راضی** — *rāzī* — "willing; agreed".

> اگر ابو راضی ہوئے تو میں جاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar abbū rāzī hue to maiṅ jāūṅ gā.*
>
> If my father agrees, I will go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**راضی** (*rāzī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rāḍī*, "content, pleased".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
