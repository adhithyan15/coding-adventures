## You'll want to know: tepidārium, tepidāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C541-BALN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tepidārium, tepidāriī** — "the warm room".

> Ē caldāriō in tepidārium īmus.
>
> *From the hot room we go into the warm room.*

Neuter, second declension, from *tepidus* — lukewarm, already known.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**tepidārium** is built on *tepidus* "lukewarm" (Lewis & Short).
