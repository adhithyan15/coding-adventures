## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಫಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C416-PRATIJNE416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಫಲ** — *pratiphala* — "a reward; a return".

> ಅವಳ ಶ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಪ್ರತಿಫಲ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *avaḷa śramakke oḷḷeya pratiphala sikkitu.*
>
> Her hard work brought a good reward.

What comes back for what one gave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಫಲ** (*pratiphala*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pratiphala*, "a return, a requital" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
