## You'll want to know: અવરોધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C374-AVRODH374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અવરોધ** — *avrodh* — "an obstruction".

> જો રસ્તામાં કોઈ અવરોધ હશે તો આપણે બીજા રસ્તે જઈશું.
>
> *jo rastāmā̃ koī avrodh haśe to āpaṇe bījā raste jaīśũ.*
>
> If there is something blocking the road, we will take another way.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવરોધ** (*avrodh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *avarodha*, "hindrance, blockade" (Monier-Williams).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
