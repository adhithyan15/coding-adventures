Hindi, chapter 482: How Someone Looks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क़द (qad) — height; stature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to stand on tiptoe, then the Hindi for to jump.

You'll want to know: क़द.
क़द — qad — "height; stature".
अरुण का क़द अपने पिता से लंबा है। Aruṇ kā qad apne pitā se lambā hai. Arun is taller than his father.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
क़द (qad) is usually traced to Arabic qadd, "stature, height", which reached Hindi through Persian.

हुलिया — huliyā — "appearance; looks".
दस साल बाद दोस्त का हुलिया बिलकुल बदल गया था। das sāl bād dost kā huliyā bilkul badal gayā thā. Ten years on, my friend's looks had changed completely.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
हुलिया (huliyā) is usually traced to Arabic ḥulya, "ornament", and so "a description of someone's features", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
साँवला (sā̃vlā) — dusky; dark-skinned.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for height, then the Hindi for appearance.

You'll want to know: साँवला.
साँवला — sā̃vlā — "dusky; dark-skinned".
मेरी बहन का रंग साँवला है। merī bahan kā raṅg sā̃vlā hai. My sister has a dusky complexion.

The word, taken apart.
साँवला (sā̃vlā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi sā̃wlā, "brown, dark-complexioned", under Sanskrit śyāmalá, "dark" (CDIAL 12665). Like gorā, it describes a complexion and nothing more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
गोरा (gorā) — fair-skinned.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for appearance, then the Hindi for dusky.

You'll want to know: गोरा.
गोरा — gorā — "fair-skinned".
मीरा का भाई गोरा है और मीरा साँवली है। Mīrā kā bhāī gorā hai aur Mīrā sā̃vlī hai. Mira's brother is fair and Mira is dusky.

The word, taken apart.
गोरा (gorā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi gorā, "fair", under Sanskrit gaurá, "white, yellowish, pale red" (CDIAL 4345), with the diphthong worn down to o. It describes a complexion, as sā̃vlā does.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दुबला (dublā) — lean; thin and weak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for dusky, then the Hindi for fair-skinned.

You'll want to know: दुबला.
दुबला — dublā — "lean; thin and weak".
बीमारी के बाद दादा बहुत दुबले हो गए। bīmārī ke bād dādā bahut duble ho gae. Grandfather got very thin after his illness.

The word, taken apart.
दुबला (dublā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi dublā, "weak, emaciated", under Sanskrit durbala, "weak", from dur-, "ill", and bala, "strength" (CDIAL 6438). patlā is slim; dublā is thin from weakness.
