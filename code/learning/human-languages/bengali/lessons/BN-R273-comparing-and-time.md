# Review — than, most, and how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C273-CHEYE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C273-DHORE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "The city is bigger than the village", then "for two years". (*shôhôr grāmer cheye bôṛo*; *dui bôchhôr dhôre*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C273-DOINIK-01, BN-SCRIPT-C273-OI-KAR-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C273-CHEYE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C273-SOBCHEYE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C273-DHORE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C273-THEKE-PORJONTO-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-GENITIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C272-WANT-TO-BE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C272-WISH-01, BN-LEX-C44-BODY44-03, BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: The mountains are more beautiful than the city. I'd like to go there.]
- [YOU SAY: This is my favourite book of all.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira has been working at the hospital for three years.]
- [YOU SAY: The market is open from morning until evening.]
- [YOU SAY: Father reads a daily every morning.]
- [YOU WRITE: **দৈনিক**, then **নৌকা**]

## Wrap-up Recall
