## You'll want to know: la borraccia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C501-PESCA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la borraccia** — "water bottle, flask".

> Porto sempre la borraccia piena d'acqua.
>
> *I always carry a full water bottle.*

A bottle you can fill again, for walks and sport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*borraccia* was borrowed from Spanish *borracha* "a leather wine bottle" (EtymDB); the deeper origin is not settled.
