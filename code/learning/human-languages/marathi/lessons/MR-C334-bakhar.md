## You'll want to know: बखर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C334-KRANTI334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बखर** — *bakhar* — "a chronicle".

> या बखरीत त्या लढाईचं वर्णन आहे.
>
> *yā bakhrīt tyā laḍhāīcaṁ varṇan āhe.*
>
> This chronicle describes that battle.

Feminine.

Old Marathi prose histories are called बखरी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बखर** (*bakhar*) is usually traced to Arabic *khabar*, 'news, a report', through Persian; the Marathi form has moved its sounds round.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
