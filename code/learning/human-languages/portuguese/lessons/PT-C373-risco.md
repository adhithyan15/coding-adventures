## You'll want to know: o risco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C373-RISCO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o risco** — "the risk; the line, the scratch".

> Não quero correr o risco de perder o comboio.
>
> *I do not want to run the risk of missing the train.*

*Correr o risco* — to run the risk. Also a line or a scratch: *um risco no carro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *risco* "risk" is not settled; it goes with Italian *rischio* and Spanish *riesgo*, while *risco* "line, scratch" belongs with the verb *riscar* "to scratch" (EtymDB lists *risco* "line").
