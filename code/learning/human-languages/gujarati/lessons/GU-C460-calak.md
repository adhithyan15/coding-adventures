## You'll want to know: ચાલાક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C460-BHOLU460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાલાક** — *cālāk* — "clever; shrewd".

> શિયાળ ઘણું ચાલાક પ્રાણી છે.
>
> *śiyāḷ ghaṇũ cālāk prāṇī che.*
>
> The jackal is a very shrewd animal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાલાક** (*cālāk*) is usually traced to Persian *čālāk*, "quick, nimble, clever".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
