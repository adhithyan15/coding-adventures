## You'll want to know: dēversor, dēversārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C445-DEVERTICULUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dēversor, dēversārī** — "to lodge, to stay as a guest".

> Mercātor apud amīcum in oppidō dēversātur.
>
> *The merchant lodges with a friend in the town.*

Deponent: *dēversor, dēversārī, dēversātus sum*, often with *apud* 'at the house of'. The inn is a *dēversōrium*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēversor* is a frequentative built on *dēvertō* 'to turn aside' (Lewis & Short): to keep turning in, to stay as a guest. *dēversōrium* 'inn' comes from the same verb.
