## You'll want to know: la biografia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C527-GENERI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la biografia** — "biography".

> Sto leggendo la biografia di un famoso pittore.
>
> *I am reading the biography of a famous painter.*

*L'autobiografia* — autobiography.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biografia* is a learned word built from Greek *bíos* "life" and *-graphía* "writing" (EtymDB).
