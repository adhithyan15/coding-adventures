## You'll want to know: धमकी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C244-JHAGRO244-05]; assesses=[] -->

**धमकी** — *dhamkī* — "a threat".

> धमकी मत दो।
>
> *dhamkī mat do.*
>
> Don't make threats.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धमकी** (*dhamkī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhammakka*, "threat". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi and Gujarati *dhamkī* (CDIAL 6736).
