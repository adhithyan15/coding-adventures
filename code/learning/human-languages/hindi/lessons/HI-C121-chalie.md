## You'll want to know: चलिए
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C121-CARE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चलिए** (*calie*) — "let's go", "shall we".

It is **चलना** with the respectful ending of **बोलिए**, the verb inside **चलता हूँ**, "I'll be off".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Take care, carefully, a good journey, come again, let's go.
