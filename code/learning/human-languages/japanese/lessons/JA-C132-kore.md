## You'll want to know: これ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KORE]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KO-01, JA-LEX-KOKO] -->

**これ** — *kore* — "this", a thing near you as you speak.

**ここ** is a place near you; **これ** is a thing near you, the one in your hand or
under your finger. It begins with the same **こ**. The second sign is one you do
not write yet. It turns the pointing word into a thing, and the next lesson
writes it.
