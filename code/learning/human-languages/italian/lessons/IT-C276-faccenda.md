## You'll want to know: la faccenda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C276-VICENDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la faccenda** — "the matter, the business; a chore".

> È una faccenda complicata.
>
> *It is a complicated matter.*

*Le faccende di casa* are the housework.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*faccenda* is inherited from Latin *facienda* "things to be done", from *facere* "to do" (EtymDB).
