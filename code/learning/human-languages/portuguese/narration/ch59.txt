Portuguese, chapter 59: Rain, Wind, Hill, Desert, Fire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a chuva — rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a star, then the Portuguese for a cloud.

You'll want to know: a chuva.
a chuva — "rain". Inside o guarda-chuva.
