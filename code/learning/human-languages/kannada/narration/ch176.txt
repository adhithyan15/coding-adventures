Kannada, chapter 176: For the sake of, Otherwise, Even so, In case, Instead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಲುವಾಗಿ (saluvāgi) — for the sake of, for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for on the other side, then the Kannada for on this side.

You'll want to know: ಸಲುವಾಗಿ.
ಸಲುವಾಗಿ — saluvāgi — "for the sake of, for".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಲುವಾಗಿ (saluvāgi) is saluva, "for, on behalf of", with the adverb ending -āgi. The origin of saluva is uncertain; neither DEDR nor CDIAL records it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ (illadiddare) — otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for on this side, then the Kannada for for the sake of.

You'll want to know: ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ.
ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ — illadiddare — "otherwise".

The word, taken apart.
ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ (illadiddare) is native Dravidian: illade, "not being", which DEDR 2559 lists, and iddare, "if it is", from iru, "to be" (DEDR 480): "if it is not so".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಆದರೂ (ādarū) — even so, nevertheless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for for the sake of, then the Kannada for otherwise.

You'll want to know: ಆದರೂ.
ಆದರೂ — ādarū — "even so, nevertheless".

The word, taken apart.
ಆದರೂ (ādarū) is native Dravidian: the "even if" form of āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333). "Even if it is so" is "even so". DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ (ondu vēḷe) — in case, if by chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for otherwise, then the Kannada for even so.

You'll want to know: ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ.
ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ — ondu vēḷe — "in case, if by chance".

The word, taken apart.
ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ (ondu vēḷe) is ondu, "one" (DEDR 990), native, and vēḷe, "time", from Sanskrit velā, "limit, time" (CDIAL 12115): "at one time", if it so happens.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಬದಲು (badalu) — instead (of).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for even so, then the Kannada for in case.

You'll want to know: ಬದಲು.
ಬದಲು — badalu — "instead (of)".

The word, taken apart.
ಬದಲು (badalu) is a loan from Arabic badal, "substitute, exchange", which came through Persian and Hindustani.
