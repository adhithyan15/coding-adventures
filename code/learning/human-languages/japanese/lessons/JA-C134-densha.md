## You'll want to know: でんしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C134-DENSHA]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-N-01, JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YA-01] -->

**でんしゃ** — *densha* — "a train", the kind that stops at a station.

The new sign is spent at once. **で** and **ん**, then **し** with a small
**ゃ**: three beats, *de–n–sha*. The small **ゃ** joins **し** into one beat,
as it joins **ち** in **おちゃ**. You write every sign in it now.
