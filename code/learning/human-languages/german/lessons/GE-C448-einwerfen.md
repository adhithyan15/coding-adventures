## You'll want to know: einwerfen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C448-POST-03]; assesses=[] -->

**einwerfen** — "to post (a letter); to throw in".

> Auf dem Weg zur Arbeit wirft er den Brief ein.
>
> *On the way to work he posts the letter.*

Separable, strong: *wirft ein*, *warf ein*, *hat eingeworfen*. *Der Briefkasten* — the postbox.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einwerfen* is built from *ein* 'in' and *werfen* 'to throw', inherited from Old High German *werfan* and Proto-Germanic *werpaną* (EtymDB).
