Persian, chapter 148: To touch, To doze, To brake, To get a flat tyre, To get stuck.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
دست زدن (dast zadan) — to touch; to clap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to rub, then the Persian for to sprinkle.

You'll want to know: دست زدن.
دست زدن — dast zadan — "to touch; to clap".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dast zadan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dast zadan, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dast zadan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for to rub, then the Persian for to sprinkle, then say dast zadan again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does دست زدن (dast zadan) mean? ("To touch; to clap".) Say it once more.

(صبر کردن, sabr kardan.) To suggest? (پیشنهاد کردن, pishnahâd kardan.) To choose? (انتخاب کردن, entekhâb kardan.) To catch a cold? (سرما خوردن, sarmâ khordan.) To get hurt? (زخمی شدن, zakhmi shodan.) To swallow? (قورت دادن, qurt dâdan.) To forget? (فراموش کردن, farâmush kardan.) To teach? (یاد دادن, yâd dâdan.) To fill? (پر کردن, por kardan.) To hand in? (تحویل دادن, tahvil dâdan.)
