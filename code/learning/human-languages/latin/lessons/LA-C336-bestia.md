## You'll want to know: bēstia, bēstiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C336-GLADIATOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bēstia, bēstiae** — "a beast, a wild animal".

> Bēstiae ingentēs in harēnam missae sunt.
>
> *Huge beasts were sent into the arena.*

Feminine. A creature without reason, as opposed to people. English *beast*, through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**bēstia** is a Latin word whose origin is not settled; Lewis & Short suggest a link with *fera*, "wild beast". English *beast* comes from it through French.
