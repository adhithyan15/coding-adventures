# కృషి (kṛṣi) — endeavour; hard work

## You'll want to know: కృషి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C433-VYUHAM433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కృషి** — *kṛṣi* — "endeavour; hard work".

> ఆమె కృషి వల్లే ఆ పాఠశాల బాగుపడింది.
>
> *āme kṛṣi vallē ā pāṭhaśāla bāgupaḍindi.*
>
> That school got better because of her hard work.

కృషి చేయు, to strive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కృషి** (*kṛṣi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kṛṣi*, "ploughing, cultivation of the soil" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for patient, hard effort.
