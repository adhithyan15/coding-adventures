# la verdura — the green growth that became vegetables

- *ver-DU-ra*: tap the single **r** once; the stress falls on **du**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-VERDURA]; assesses=[] -->

**la verdura** — “the vegetables” or “the greens.”

The word is built on **verde**, from Latin **viridis**, “green.” The food sense
grew from green plant growth. But **verde** cannot be reduced to a colour in
every context: Spanish can also use it of an indecent or erotic joke, and the
dictionary records a marked secondary sense of **verdura**. Here the context is
plainly food.
