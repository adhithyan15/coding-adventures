## You'll want to know: નણંદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C490-SASRA490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નણંદ** — *naṇand* — "a husband's sister".

> મારી નણંદ દિવાળીમાં અમારા ઘરે આવશે.
>
> *mārī naṇand divāḷīmā̃ amārā ghare āvśe.*
>
> My husband's sister will come to our house at Diwali.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નણંદ** (*naṇand*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nanāndṛ*, "husband's sister"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *naṇãd* (CDIAL 6946).
