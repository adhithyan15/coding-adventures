## You'll want to know: cruciō, cruciāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C497-SOLL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cruciō, cruciāre** — "to torment, torture".

> Dolor dentium senem cruciat.
>
> *Toothache torments the old man.*

*cruciō, cruciāre, cruciāvī, cruciātum*, from *crux* — a cross. In comedy also of worry: *mē cruciō* — I fret. English *excruciating*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cruciō** is built on *crux* "a cross" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB): to torment as on a cross. English *excruciating* comes from the compound *excruciō*.
