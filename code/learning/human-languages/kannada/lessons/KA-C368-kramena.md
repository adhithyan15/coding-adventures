# ಕ್ರಮೇಣ (kramēṇa) — gradually

## You'll want to know: ಕ್ರಮೇಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C368-KRAMA368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕ್ರಮೇಣ** — *kramēṇa* — "gradually".

> ಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಹಳ್ಳಿ ಒಂದು ಪಟ್ಟಣವಾಯಿತು.
>
> *kramēṇa haḷḷi ondu paṭṭaṇavāyitu.*
>
> Gradually the village became a town.

A formal adverb; in talk people say ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ or ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪವಾಗಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕ್ರಮೇಣ** (*kramēṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *krameṇa*, "in regular course, gradually, by degrees" (Monier-Williams), a case form of *krama*, "a step, an order".

## What you've built
