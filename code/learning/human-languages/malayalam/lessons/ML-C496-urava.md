## You'll want to know: ഉറവ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C496-NADI496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉറവ** — *uṟava* — "a water spring".

> മലയുടെ ചുവട്ടിൽ ഒരു തണുത്ത ഉറവ ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *malayuṭe cuvaṭṭil oru taṇutta uṟava uṇṭŭ.*
>
> There is a cold spring at the foot of the hill.

Where water comes up out of the ground; a well fills from its ഉറവ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉറവ** (*uṟava*) is native Dravidian, from a root that means to ooze or spring up (DEDR 761).
