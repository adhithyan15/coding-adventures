## Chapters 107-153: 235 headwords, can, want and why, and Kannada attains A1

Kannada was 227 headwords and 28 verbs short of A1. Six A1 spine nodes had no
segment: ASK-LOCATION, SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO, SAY-WHAT-I-WANT, SAY-WHY,
NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS and DESCRIBE-QUALITIES. Twenty-four atoms were revisited
fewer than twice.

**The words.** Forty-seven chapters of five:

- **places and directions:** **ಬಲ**, **ಎಡ**, **ನೇರ**, **ದೂರ**, **ಮೇಲೆ**, **ಕೆಳಗೆ**,
  **ಹೊರಗೆ**, **ಮುಂದೆ**, **ನಡುವೆ**, the road, the bridge, the park, the temple
  and more (ASK-LOCATION)
- **can:** **ಸಾಮರ್ಥ್ಯ**, **ಕೌಶಲ್ಯ**, **ಹಕ್ಕು**, **ಅವಕಾಶ**, **ಸಾಧನ**, **ಆಸ್ತಿ**,
  **ಯಜಮಾನ**, **ಶಕ್ತಿ** (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **want:** **ಆಸೆ**, **ಕನಸು**, **ಉದ್ದೇಶ**, **ಯೋಜನೆ**, **ಅಗತ್ಯ**, **ನಿರ್ಧಾರ**,
  **ಬಯಸು** (SAY-WHAT-I-WANT)
- **why:** **ಕಾರಣ**, **ಆದುದರಿಂದ**, **ಅದಕ್ಕೇ**, **ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ** (SAY-WHY)
- **things:** 123 of them, covering the house, things you carry and wear,
  food from **ಇಡ್ಲಿ** to **ರಾಗಿ**, nature, animals and the body
  (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** thirty-three describing words (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** thirty-six stems, in the bare-command form the track already uses

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text, with Kannada letter boundaries. Every word uses
only letters the book has already taught the reader to write, so **ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ**
was left out for its **ಛ**. The builder was made from the Marathi one. This
time it was checked for every Marathi literal, including the book label
prefix, before it ran. Forward references stay at 14, and letter anchoring
stays at zero cold letters.

**The revisits.** Twenty-four thin atoms each get a warm-up retrieval:

- the ordinals past eight, and the sign form **೧ನೇ**
- the Kannada digits
- the chapter-76 reading skills
- adjectives before the noun
- the greetings through the day
- the colon, the brackets and **ಠ**
