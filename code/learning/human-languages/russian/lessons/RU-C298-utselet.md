## You'll want to know: уцелеть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C298-NAEZD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**уцелеть** (*utselét*) — "to come out unhurt, to survive intact".

> В аварии все уцелели.
>
> *V avárii vse utseléli.*
>
> Everyone came out of the accident unhurt.

Perfective. *Дом уцелел* — the house was left standing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*уцелеть* is built on *у-* and *целый* "whole, unhurt" (EtymDB glosses *целый* "whole, healthy"): to stay whole.
