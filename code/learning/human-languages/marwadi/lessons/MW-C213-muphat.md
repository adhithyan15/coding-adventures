## You'll want to know: मुफत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C213-PRICE213-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुफत** — *muphat* — "free of charge".

> अठै पाणी मुफत है।
>
> *aṭhai pāṇī muphat hai.*
>
> Water is free here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुफत** (*muphat*) is usually traced to Persian *muft*, "free, gratis".
