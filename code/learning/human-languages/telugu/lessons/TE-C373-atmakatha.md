## You'll want to know: ఆత్మకథ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C373-ATMAKATHA373-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆత్మకథ** — *ātmakatha* — "an autobiography".

> ఆ నాయకుడు తన ఆత్మకథ రాశాడు.
>
> *ā nāyakuḍu tana ātmakatha rāśāḍu.*
>
> That leader wrote his autobiography.

ఆత్మ, self, and కథ: one's own story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆత్మకథ** (*ātmakatha*) is built from Sanskrit *ātman*, "breath; self" (CDIAL 1135), and *kathā*, "a story".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
