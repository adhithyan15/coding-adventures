## You'll want to know: berücksichtigen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C373-UNVORHERGESEHEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**berücksichtigen** — "to take into account".

> Bei der Reise müssen wir auch das Wetter berücksichtigen.
>
> *For the trip we also have to take the weather into account.*

Inseparable: *er berücksichtigt, er hat berücksichtigt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*berücksichtigen* is built on *Rücksicht* "consideration", literally a looking back, from *Rücken* "back" and *Sicht* "sight", of the family of *sehen* (EtymDB). *Rücksicht* is usually explained as a rendering of Latin *respectus* "a looking back" (Lewis & Short).
