# തടിച്ച (taṭicca) — stout

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C101-THIN-01, ML-CONCEPT-C36-KUTTI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *a thin child*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C101-STOUT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**തടിച്ച** — *taṭicca* — **stout**, **heavily built**.

> **തടിച്ച കുട്ടി** — *taṭicca kuṭṭi* — "a stout child."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C101-STOUT-01, ML-LEX-C101-THIN-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C42-ADJ-SYSTEM, ML-LEX-C101-SHORT-01] -->

**This is the far end of the last word**, and the book has done that before: one
quality, then its opposite, so a single axis costs you two words and not four.

Three shapes, one ending, one behaviour:

| adjective | ends |
|---|---|
| **കുറിയ** | **-ിയ** |
| **മെലിഞ്ഞ** | **-ഞ്ഞ** |
| **തടിച്ച** | **-ച്ച** |

Every one of them closes on **-a**, stands in front of the noun, and does not
change. **The shape in front of the -a varies and never matters.**
