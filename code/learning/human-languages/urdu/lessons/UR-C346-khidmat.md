## You'll want to know: خدمت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C346-KHIDMAT346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خدمت** — *khidmat* — "service to others".

> وہ ہسپتال میں مریضوں کی خدمت کرتی ہے۔
>
> *voh haspatāl meṅ marīzoṅ kī khidmat kartī hai.*
>
> She looks after patients at the hospital.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خدمت** (*khidmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khidma*, "service".
