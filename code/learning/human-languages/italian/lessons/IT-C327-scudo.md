## You'll want to know: lo scudo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C327-BATTAGLIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scudo** — "the shield".

> Lo scudo era rosso e oro.
>
> *The shield was red and gold.*

*Fare scudo* — to shield someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scudo* is inherited from Latin *scūtum* "a shield" (EtymDB).
