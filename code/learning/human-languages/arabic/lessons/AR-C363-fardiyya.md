## You'll want to know: فرضية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C363-NAZARIYYA363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فرضية** — *farḍiyya* — "a hypothesis".

> أراد الطالب أن يختبر فرضيته.
>
> *arāda aṭ-ṭālib an yakhtabira farḍiyyatah.*
>
> The student wanted to test his hypothesis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرضية** (*farḍiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-ḍ*, "to make a notch; to make binding" (Lane). *Farḍiyya* is a modern abstract noun from *farḍ*, a supposition: an idea put forward to be tested.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
