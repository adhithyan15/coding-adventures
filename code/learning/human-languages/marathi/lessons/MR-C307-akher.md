## You'll want to know: अखेर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C307-ANT307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अखेर** — *akher* — "at last; finally".

> तीन दिवसांनी अखेर पाऊस थांबला.
>
> *tīn divsānnī akher pāūs thāmblā.*
>
> After three days the rain finally stopped.

Also a noun: महिन्याच्या अखेरीस, at the end of the month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अखेर** (*akher*) is a loan from Persian *āxir*, 'the end', usually traced to Arabic *ākhir*, 'last'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
