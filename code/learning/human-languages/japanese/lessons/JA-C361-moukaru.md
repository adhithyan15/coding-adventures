## You'll want to know: もうかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C361-MOUKERU361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**もうかる** — *mōkaru* — "to be profitable; to pay".

> **みせが もうかったら、てんいんを ふやします。** — *mise ga mōkattara, ten'in o fuyashimasu* — "If the shop does well, we will take on more people."

Polite **もうかります**, *mōkarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **みせが もうかりました。**, *mise ga mōkarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もうかる** (*mōkaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
