Marathi, chapter 442: Peaks and Valleys.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
शिखर (śikhar) — a peak; a summit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to set, then the Marathi for to shine.

You'll want to know: शिखर.
शिखर — śikhar — "a peak; a summit".
आम्ही पहाटे शिखरावर पोहोचलो. āmhī pahāṭe śikharāvar pohoclo. We reached the summit at dawn.
Neuter.
Also the top of a temple, and the height of success: यशाचं शिखर (śikhar).

The word, taken apart.
शिखर (śikhar) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit śikhara, "a peak, a summit, a pinnacle" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 6.
दरी (darī) — a valley; a gorge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to shine, then the Marathi for a peak.

You'll want to know: दरी.
दरी — darī — "a valley; a gorge".
शिखरावरून खाली खोल दरी दिसते. śikharāvarūn khālī khol darī diste. From the summit you can see a deep valley below.
Feminine.
खोल दरी (darī), a deep valley.

The word, taken apart.
दरी (darī) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi darī, "chasm", under Sanskrit dara, darī, "a cleft, a cave" (CDIAL 6188).

कडा — kaḍā — "a cliff; an edge".
कड्यावरून धबधबा खाली पडतो. kaḍyāvarūn dhabdhabā khālī paḍto. The waterfall drops down from the cliff.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 6.
पायथा (pāythā) — a foothill; the foot of a hill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a valley, then the Marathi for a cliff.

You'll want to know: पायथा.
पायथा — pāythā — "a foothill; the foot of a hill".
डोंगराच्या पायथ्याशी एक लहान गाव आहे. ḍoṅgarācyā pāyathyāśī ek lahān gāv āhe. There is a small village at the foot of the hill.
Masculine.
पायथ्याशी, at the foot; the opposite is the शिखर (śikhar).

The word, taken apart.
पायथा (pāythā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi pāythā, "extremity of the feet", under Sanskrit pādānta, "the end at the feet" (CDIAL 8072).

Lesson 5 of 6.
डोंगररांग (ḍoṅgarrāṅg) — a mountain range.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cliff, then the Marathi for a foothill.

You'll want to know: डोंगररांग.
डोंगररांग — ḍoṅgarrāṅg — "a mountain range".
पश्चिमेला एक मोठी डोंगररांग आहे. paścimelā ek moṭhī ḍoṅgarrāṅg āhe. There is a great mountain range to the west.
Feminine.
डोंगर, a hill, and रांग, a row.

The word, taken apart.
डोंगररांग (ḍoṅgarrāṅg) is built from डोंगर, "a hill", and रांग, "a row, a line", both of uncertain origin.
