Punjabi, chapter 335: Gold and Iron.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸੋਨਾ (sonā) — gold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a weaver, then the Punjabi for to dye.

You'll want to know: ਸੋਨਾ.
ਸੋਨਾ — sonā — "gold".
ਵਿਆਹ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਅਸੀਂ ਸੋਨਾ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਿਆ। viāh ton pahilāṁ asīṁ sonā kharīdiā. Before the wedding we bought gold.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੋਨਾ (sonā) is inherited from Sanskrit suvarṇa, "golden; gold"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sonā, "gold", under it (CDIAL 13519).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਘੜਨਾ (ghaṛnā) — to hammer into shape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a goldsmith, then the Punjabi for iron.

You'll want to know: ਘੜਨਾ.
ਘੜਨਾ — ghaṛnā — "to hammer into shape".
ਲੁਹਾਰ ਲੋਹੇ ਤੋਂ ਸੰਦ ਘੜਦਾ ਹੈ। luhār lohe ton sand ghaṛdā hai. The blacksmith hammers tools out of iron.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਸੰਦ ਘੜਿਆ, maiṁ ikk sand ghaṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਘੜਨਾ (ghaṛnā) is inherited from Sanskrit ghaṭate, "is busy with"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ghaṛnā, "to form by hammering", under it (CDIAL 4407).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਮਿਸਤਰੀ (misatrī) — a mechanic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for iron, then the Punjabi for to hammer into shape.

You'll want to know: ਮਿਸਤਰੀ.
ਮਿਸਤਰੀ — misatrī — "a mechanic".
ਮਿਸਤਰੀ ਸਾਡੀ ਗੱਡੀ ਠੀਕ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ। misatrī sāḍī gaḍḍī ṭhīk kardā hai. The mechanic fixes our car.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਿਸਤਰੀ (misatrī) is a loan, usually traced to Portuguese mestre, "a master of a trade".
