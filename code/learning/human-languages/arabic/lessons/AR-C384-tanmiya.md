## You'll want to know: تنمية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C384-TANMIYA384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تنمية** — *tanmiya* — "development".

> تحتاج القرية إلى تنمية.
>
> *taḥtāju al-qarya ilā tanmiya.*
>
> The village needs development.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنمية** (*tanmiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-m-w*, usually traced to the sense "to grow, to increase". *Tanmiya* is the Form II verbal noun, making a thing grow: development.
