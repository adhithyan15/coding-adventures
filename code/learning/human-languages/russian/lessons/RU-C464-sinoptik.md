## You'll want to know: синоптик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C464-PROGNOZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**синоптик** (*sinóptik*) — "a weather forecaster".

> Синоптики обещают тёплую неделю.
>
> *Sinóptiki obeshcháyut tyópluyu nedélyu.*
>
> The forecasters promise a warm week.

Masculine, for a woman too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*синоптик* is usually traced to Greek *synoptikós* 'seeing the whole together at a glance' (EtymDB lists the Greek word but not the Russian): a forecaster reads the whole weather map at once.
