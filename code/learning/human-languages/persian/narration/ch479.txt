Persian, chapter 479: After the rain.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
رنگین کمان (ranginkamân) — a rainbow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to dry up, then the Persian for foggy.

You'll want to know: رنگین کمان.
رنگین کمان (ranginkamân) — "a rainbow".
بعد از باران یک رنگین کمان در آسمان دیدیم. Ba'd az bârân yek ranginkamân dar âsmân didim. After the rain we saw a rainbow in the sky.
Literally 'coloured bow': rangin 'coloured' + kamân 'bow'.

The word, taken apart.
ranginkamân is a native compound of rangin 'coloured', from rang 'colour', and kamân 'bow', inherited from Middle Persian kamān (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 7.
نسیم (nasim) — a breeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for foggy, then the Persian for a rainbow.

You'll want to know: نسیم.
نسیم (nasim) — "a breeze".
عصر نسیم خنکی از دریا میوزید. 'Asr nasim-e khonaki az daryâ mivazid. In the afternoon a cool breeze was blowing from the sea.
Also a given name, both for boys and girls.

The word, taken apart.
nasim was borrowed from Arabic nasīm, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'a gentle breeze'.

You'll want to know: درخشیدن.
درخشیدن (derakhshidan) — "to shine, to sparkle".
بعد از باران خورشید درخشید. Ba'd az bârân khorshid derakhshid. After the rain the sun shone.
Present stem derakhsh-: miderakhshad.

The word, taken apart.
derakhshidan is a native verb, usually traced to Middle Persian draxšīdan 'to shine'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 4 of 7.
غروب کردن (ghorub kardan) — to set (of the sun).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a breeze, then the Persian for to shine.

You'll want to know: غروب کردن.
غروب کردن (ghorub kardan) — "to set (of the sun)".
خورشید پشت کوه غروب کرد. Khorshid posht-e kuh ghorub kard. The sun set behind the mountain.
ghorub 'sunset' + kardan. Ghorub alone is sunset or dusk.

The word, taken apart.
ghorub kardan is built from ghorub 'sunset', borrowed from Arabic ġurūb (EtymDB), and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to shine, then the Persian for to set.

You'll want to know: طلوع کردن.
طلوع کردن (tolu' kardan) — "to rise (of the sun or moon)".
صبح زود خورشید از پشت دریا طلوع کرد. Sobh-e zud khorshid az posht-e daryâ tolu' kard. Early in the morning the sun rose over the sea.
tolu' 'rising' + kardan; the opposite of ghorub kardan.

The word, taken apart.
tolu' kardan is built from tolu' 'rising', borrowed from Arabic ṭulūʿ, which EtymDB lists, and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
