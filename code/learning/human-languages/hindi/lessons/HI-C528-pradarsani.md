## You'll want to know: प्रदर्शनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C528-SHILP528-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रदर्शनी** — *pradarśanī* — "an exhibition".

> संग्रहालय में चित्रों की एक प्रदर्शनी लगी है।
>
> *Saṅgrahālay meṁ citroṁ kī ek pradarśanī lagī hai.*
>
> There is an exhibition of paintings at the museum.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रदर्शनी** (*pradarśanī*) is a modern formation built on Sanskrit *pradarśana*, "look, appearance; showing" (Monier-Williams), with the feminine -ī: a place where things are shown.
