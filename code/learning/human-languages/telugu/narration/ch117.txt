Telugu, chapter 117: Vegetables, Banana, Mango, Coconut, Onion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కూరగాయలు (kūragāyalu) — vegetables.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a fish, then the Telugu for meat.

You'll want to know: కూరగాయలు.
కూరగాయలు — kūragāyalu — "vegetables".

The word, taken apart.
కూరగాయలు (kūragāyalu) is native Dravidian: kūra, "curry-stuff" (DEDR 1760), and kāya, "unripe fruit" (DEDR 1459), with the plural -lu. DEDR compares the Indo-Aryan kacca, "raw, unripe" (CDIAL 2613), with kāya.

Lesson 3 of 5.
మామిడిపండు (māmiḍipaṇḍu) — a mango.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for vegetables, then the Telugu for a banana.

You'll want to know: మామిడిపండు.
మామిడిపండు — māmiḍipaṇḍu — "a mango".

The word, taken apart.
మామిడిపండు (māmiḍipaṇḍu) is native Dravidian: māmiḍi, "mango", which DEDR 4782 lists with Tamil mā and Kannada māvu; and paṇḍu, "fruit" (DEDR 4004). DEDR compares Sanskrit mākanda, "mango tree", with the first and phala, "fruit", with the second.

Lesson 4 of 5.
కొబ్బరి (kobbari) — coconut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a banana, then the Telugu for a mango.

You'll want to know: కొబ్బరి.
కొబ్బరి — kobbari — "coconut".

The word, taken apart.
కొబ్బరి (kobbari) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil koppari, "dried coconut kernel", and Kannada kobbari (DEDR 2105). DEDR compares Sanskrit kūrpasa, "the inner part of a coconut", a word known only from lexicons, and the northern words in CDIAL 3936.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఉల్లిపాయ (ullipāya) — an onion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a mango, then the Telugu for coconut.

You'll want to know: ఉల్లిపాయ.
ఉల్లిపాయ — ullipāya — "an onion".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ulli, "onion", the first part of ఉల్లిపాయ (ullipāya), is uncertain: DEDR does not list it in this sense. The second part is kāya, "unripe fruit, pod" (DEDR 1459), which DEDR compares with the Indo-Aryan kacca, "raw" (CDIAL 2613).
