## You'll want to know: feilschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C451-SCHNAEPPCHEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**feilschen** — "to haggle".

> Auf dem Markt feilscht er um jeden Euro.
>
> *At the market he haggles over every euro.*

With *um*: *um den Preis feilschen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*feilschen* is usually linked to the old adjective *feil* 'for sale', but the details are not settled, and EtymDB does not list the word.
