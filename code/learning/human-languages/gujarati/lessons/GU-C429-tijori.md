## You'll want to know: તિજોરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C429-SAFE429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**તિજોરી** — *tijorī* — "a safe; a strongbox".

> દુકાનદાર રોકડ તિજોરીમાં રાખે છે.
>
> *dukāndār rokaḍ tijorīmā̃ rākhe che.*
>
> The shopkeeper keeps the cash in a safe.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તિજોરી** (*tijorī*) is usually traced to Portuguese *tesouro*, "a treasure, a treasury": the place where the money is locked away.
