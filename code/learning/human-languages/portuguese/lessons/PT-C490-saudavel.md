## You'll want to know: saudável
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C490-FORMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**saudável** — "healthy".

> Tento comer de forma saudável.
>
> *I try to eat in a healthy way.*

Plural *saudáveis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saudável* is built on *saúde* "health", which is inherited from Latin *salūs* "health, safety" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
