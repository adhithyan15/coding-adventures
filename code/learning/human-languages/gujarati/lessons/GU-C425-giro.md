## You'll want to know: ગીરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C425-LOAN425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગીરો** — *gīro* — "a pledge; a pawn (left as security)".

> તેણે ઘરેણાં ગીરો મૂક્યાં.
>
> *teṇe ghareṇā̃ gīro mūkyā̃.*
>
> She pawned her jewellery.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગીરો** (*gīro*) is usually traced to Persian *giraw*, "a pledge, a pawn": what you leave with a lender until you pay.
