## You'll want to know: દોહવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C234-MILK234-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દોહવું** — *dohvũ* — "to milk".

> **સવારે મેં ગાય દોહી.** — *savāre mẽ gāy dohī* — "I milked the cow in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દોહવું** (*dohvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*dohati*, "milks", a form of the Sanskrit root *duh*, "milk" (CDIAL 6592).
