# मोठा (moṭhā) — big

is **like**.

- [YOU RECALL: say *mājhyākaḍe ghar āhe*, then *mī mitrālā pāṇī deto*]

## You'll want to know: मोठा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-MOTHA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-GHAR, MR-LEX-KHOLI, MR-LEX-LAAMB] -->

> **मोठा** — *moṭhā* — **big, large**

> **मोठं घर**
> — *moṭhaṁ ghar* — **a big house**

Start with the ending. The headword is **मोठा**, and the
example says **मोठं**. Nothing is wrong: the adjective took the shape of the
noun it sits in front of, and **घर** is neuter. The next lesson is about
nothing but that.

**मोठा** covers size in every direction — a big house, a big city, a big
brother. Where **लांब** measured a distance, this measures a thing.

One warning worth having early. If you know Hindi you will meet **मोटा**
(*moṭā*) there and reach for it: it means **fat**, not *big*, and the two
words are a genuine trap between the languages. Marathi's **मोठा** is the
neutral, everyday word; nothing about it is about a body.
