## You'll want to know: صبور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C481-PATIENCE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صبور** (*sabur*) — "patient".

> پرستار بچه ها خیلی صبور است.
>
> *parastâr-e bachchehâ kheyli sabur ast.*
>
> The children's nurse is very patient.

An adjective. The noun is *sabr* 'patience', as in *sabr kardan* 'to wait'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sabur* was borrowed from Arabic *ṣabūr*, 'patient, forbearing' (EtymDB). It belongs with *sabr*, 'patience', another Arabic loan.
