# HL102 — Punjabi A2 `ਯਾਤਰੀ` prerequisite

Issue: #18221

After PR #18215 merged, an aggregate-only audit of the preserved confidential
Punjabi A2 mock drafts against merged main
`b07e82802f6e1f17b6b93491c9b7ff11ab9e4a90` finds **ਯਾਤਰੀ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs zero times in the confidential learner material
and three times in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential
body belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 202 closes that single-word gap through one deliberately narrow person
identity frame: **ਇਹ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੈ** — “this person is a traveller.” The
voice-first opener retrieves only familiar **ਇਹ** and **ਹੈ**. One lesson
directly owns exact **ਯਾਤਰੀ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound,
meaning, and script, a bounded frame lesson places it between the two familiar
words, and a four-skill payoff retrieves the complete label again. The target
has one direct owner and three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored,
generated-book, or narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-LEX-YATRI-202`. The lesson keeps the Punjabi form whole and
owns only the concrete person noun “traveller,” meaning a person who travels.
It does not teach gender, number or oblique forms, compounds such as
**ਪੁਲਾੜ-ਯਾਤਰੀ**, transport-specific passenger terminology, synonyms, or a
productive derivation from **ਯਾਤਰਾ**.

The Punjab School Education Board's current Class 11 Punjabi material gives
**ਸੈਲਨੀ — ਯਾਤਰੀ**, supporting the bounded “traveller” meaning:
<https://static.pseb.ac.in/media/1749801072_11%20%20Adhunik%20Punjabi%202025.pdf>.
PSEB's Class 5 Punjabi book independently uses **ਪੁਲਾੜ-ਯਾਤਰੀ** for Kalpana
Chawla, corroborating the same person-who-travels noun in current educational
Punjabi:
<https://static.pseb.ac.in/media/1687845902_Punjabi%20Pustak-5%28Pehli%20Bhasha%29.pdf>.

The exact origin of modern written **ਯਾਤਰੀ** remains unknown in the cited
sources. The lesson therefore makes no etymological or letter-by-letter
derivational claim and keeps the present-day word whole.

This is the twenty-fifth prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
