# ঠিক (ṭhik) — correct; exactly

## You'll want to know: ঠিক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C266-THIK-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-04, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-TOKHON-01] -->

**ঠিক** — *ṭhik* — "correct; exactly".

**তুমি ঠিক** (*tumi ṭhik*) is "you're right", and **ঠিক আছে** (*ṭhik āchhe*), "it's all right", is the everyday "fine, agreed". Before a time or a place, **ঠিক** is "exactly":

> **ঠিক তখন ট্রেন এল।**
>
> *ṭhik tôkhon ṭren elô.* — "Right then the train came."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠিক** (*ṭhik*) goes back to a form *\*ṭhīkka*, "firm, right" (CDIAL 5503), which no text records (hence the star), and which CDIAL derives, probably, from Sanskrit *sthita*, "standing". CDIAL lists the Bengali **ঠিক**, "correct", under it.
