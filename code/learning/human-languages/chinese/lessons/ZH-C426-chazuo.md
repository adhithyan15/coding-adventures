## You'll want to know: chāzuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C426-ROOM426-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chāzuò* — "power socket".

> *fángjiān lǐ de chāzuò zài nǎr* — Where's the socket in the room?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chāzuò* is built from *chā* 'to insert' and *zuò* 'a seat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrheap* and *dzwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the seat of a bed.
