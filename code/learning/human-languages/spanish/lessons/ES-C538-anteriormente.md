## You'll want to know: anteriormente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C538-ANTES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**anteriormente** — "previously, earlier".

> Anteriormente vivía en Madrid.
>
> *Previously she lived in Madrid.*

A formal word; in speech people say *antes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anteriormente* is built on *anterior* and the ending *-mente* (EtymDB). *Anterior* is a learned word from Latin *anterior* 'former', built on *ante* 'before'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
