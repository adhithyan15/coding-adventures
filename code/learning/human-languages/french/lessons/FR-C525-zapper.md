## You'll want to know: zapper
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C525-ECRAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**zapper** — "to channel-hop; to skip".

> Le soir, il zappe d'une chaîne à l'autre.
>
> *In the evening he flicks from one channel to another.*

Informally, to forget or skip: *j'ai zappé le rendez-vous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zapper* was borrowed from English *zap* (EtymDB), an imitative word.
