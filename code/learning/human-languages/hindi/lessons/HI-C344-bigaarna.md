# बिगाड़ना (bigāṛnā) — to spoil, to make worse

## You'll want to know: बिगाड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C344-PARINAM344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बिगाड़ना** — *bigāṛnā* — "to spoil, to make worse".

> बारिश ने फ़सल बिगाड़ दी।
>
> *bāriś ne fasal bigāṛ dī.*
>
> The rain ruined the crop.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने खेल बिगाड़ दिया, *usne khel bigāṛ diyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिगाड़ना** (*bigāṛnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *vighaṭate*, "breaks apart" (CDIAL 11673).
