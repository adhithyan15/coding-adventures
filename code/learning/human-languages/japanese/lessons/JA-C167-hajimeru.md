## You'll want to know: はじめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C167-DONE167-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はじめる** — *hajimeru* — "to start (something)".

> **らいしゅう しごとを はじめます。** — *raishū shigoto o hajimemasu* — "I will start work next week."

Polite **はじめます**, *hajimemasu*; an ichidan verb. Its intransitive partner is **はじまる**, *hajimaru*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はじめる** (*hajimeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *hajimaru*, "to begin"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
