## You'll want to know: la angustia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C719-CONFIANZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la angustia** — "anguish, distress".

> Sentí angustia al no tener noticias.
>
> *I felt anguish at having no news.*

*Me da angustia* — it makes me anxious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*angustia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *angustia* "narrowness, difficulty, distress" (EtymDB), built on *angustus* "narrow": distress is a tightness in the chest.
