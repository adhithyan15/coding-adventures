## You'll want to know: مجرم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C300-JARIMA300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مجرم** — *mujrim* — "a criminal".

> هرب المجرم من الشرطة.
>
> *haraba al-mujrim min ash-shurṭa.*
>
> The criminal ran away from the police.

Plural مجرمون.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجرم** (*mujrim*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-m* (Lane), "to commit a crime". *Mujrim* is the Form IV participle, "one who commits a crime".
