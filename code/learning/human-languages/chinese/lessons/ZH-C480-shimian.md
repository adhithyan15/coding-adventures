## You'll want to know: shīmián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C480-SYMPTOM480-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shīmián* — "to be unable to sleep; to lie awake at night".

> *wǒ shīmián le yí ge xīngqī* — I haven't been able to sleep for a week.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shīmián* is built from *shī* 'to lose' and *mián* 'sleep', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syit* and *men* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to let slip' and the second 'to sleep'.
