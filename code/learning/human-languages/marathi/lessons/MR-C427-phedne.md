## You'll want to know: फेडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C427-VYAJ427-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फेडणे** — *pheḍṇe* — "to repay".

> त्याने पाच वर्षांत कर्ज फेडलं.
>
> *tyāne pāc varṣānt karja pheḍlaṁ.*
>
> He repaid the loan in five years.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने फेडलं, *tyāne pheḍlaṁ*.

कर्ज फेडणे, to pay off a debt; the debt is the object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फेडणे** (*pheḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *pheḍṇẽ*, "to loosen", under *\*sphiṭati*, "gets loose" (CDIAL 13838); paying off a debt is its everyday sense today.
