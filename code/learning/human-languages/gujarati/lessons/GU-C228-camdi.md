## You'll want to know: ચામડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C228-SKIN228-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ચામડી** — *cāmḍī* — "skin".

> **શું તમારી ચામડી સૂકી છે?** — *śũ tamārī cāmḍī sūkī che?* — "Is your skin dry?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચામડી** (*cāmḍī*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *carman*, "hide, skin" (CDIAL 4701).
