## You'll want to know: सांडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C525-BHANDI525-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सांडणे** — *sāṇḍṇe* — "to spill".

> घाईत माझ्याकडून चहा सांडला.
>
> *ghāīt mājhyākaḍūn cahā sāṇḍlā.*
>
> In my hurry I spilled the tea.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दूध सांडलं, *tyāne dūdh sāṇḍlaṁ*.

For an accident Marathi says माझ्याकडून चहा सांडला, the tea spilled from me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सांडणे** (*sāṇḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *sā̃ḍṇẽ*, "to spill, give up", under Sanskrit *chardati*, "ejects, vomits" (CDIAL 4998), whose basic sense is letting something out.
