# Return to a familiar went

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-JANA] -->

[YOU HEAR: *oh gae*] Say **ਉਹ ਗਏ**, "they went". Then *to go*: **ਜਾਣਾ**.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211] -->

**ਗਏ** is the went form for a group, or for one person you respect. Today it
gets two partners: one for a man, one for a woman. Hold **ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ
ਹੈ** beside it: every day, and once, in the past.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *they went*] Say **ਉਹ ਗਏ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *he goes to school every day*] Say **ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *they went* once more. No new form is needed yet.
