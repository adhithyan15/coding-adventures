Marathi, chapter 538: Rhythm and Dance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ताल (tāl) — rhythm; a beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a costume, then the Marathi for to portray.

You'll want to know: ताल.
ताल — tāl — "rhythm; a beat".
ढोलाचा ताल ऐकून सगळे नाचू लागले. ḍholācā tāl aikūn sagḷe nācū lāgle. Hearing the drum's rhythm, everyone began to dance.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ताल (tāl) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit tāla, "clapping the hands, musical time" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
घुंगरू (ghuṅgrū) — ankle bells.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for rhythm, then the Marathi for a dancer.

You'll want to know: घुंगरू.
घुंगरू — ghuṅgrū — "ankle bells".
नाचताना तिच्या घुंगरांचा आवाज येत होता. nāctānā ticyā ghuṅgrāñcā āvāj yet hotā. As she danced the sound of her ankle bells could be heard.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
घुंगरू (ghuṅgrū) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ghũghrū, "bell", under ghuṅghura (CDIAL 4477), a word that copies the ringing.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ठेका धरणे (ṭhekā dharṇe) — to keep the beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a dancer, then the Marathi for ankle bells.

You'll want to know: ठेका धरणे.
ठेका धरणे — ṭhekā dharṇe — "to keep the beat".
गाणं सुरू झालं आणि सगळ्यांनी टाळ्यांनी ठेका धरला. gāṇaṁ surū jhālaṁ āṇi sagḷyānnī ṭāḷyānnī ṭhekā dharlā. The song began and everyone kept the beat with their claps.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ठेका धरला, tyāne ṭhekā dharlā.

The word, taken apart.
ठेका धरणे (ṭhekā dharṇe) is ठेका, inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ṭhekā, "mode of beating a drum", under ṭhēkk, "fix" (CDIAL 5509), with धरणे, "to hold".

Lesson 5 of 5.
डोलणे (ḍolṇe) — to sway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for ankle bells, then the Marathi for to keep the beat.

You'll want to know: डोलणे.
डोलणे — ḍolṇe — "to sway".
संगीत ऐकून आजोबा खुर्चीत डोलू लागले. saṅgīt aikūn ājobā khurcīt ḍolū lāgle. Listening to the music, Grandfather began to sway in his chair.
Past (he …): तो डोलला, to ḍollā.

The word, taken apart.
डोलणे (ḍolṇe) is perhaps kin to Sanskrit dōla, "swinging", under which CDIAL lists Hindi ḍol, "swinging" (CDIAL 6582); CDIAL gives no Marathi verb there, so the link is not settled.
