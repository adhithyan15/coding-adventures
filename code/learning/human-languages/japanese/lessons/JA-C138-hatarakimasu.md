## You'll want to know: はたらきます
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C138-HATARAKIMASU]; assesses=[] -->

**はたらきます** — *hatarakimasu* — "I work": to do a job, at a desk,
in a shop or in a field. The noun for the job itself is **しごと**, which
you already know.

Its dictionary form is **はたらく**, *hataraku*. Verbs that end in *-u*
like this one change that last sound to *-i* before **ます**: *hatarak-u*
becomes *hatarak-i-masu*. So **く** turns into **き**.

> **ぎんこうで はたらきます。** — *ginkō de hatarakimasu* — "I work at a
> bank." The particle **で** marks the place where something is done.
