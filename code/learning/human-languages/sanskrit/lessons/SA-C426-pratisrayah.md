## You'll want to know: प्रतिश्रयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C426-VIDESHA426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिश्रयः** — *pratiśrayaḥ* — "a shelter; a place of refuge".

> विदेशे मित्रस्य गृहं मम प्रतिश्रयः अस्ति।
>
> *videśe mitrasya gṛhaṃ mama pratiśrayaḥ asti.*
>
> Abroad, my friend's house is my shelter.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिश्रयः** (*pratiśrayaḥ*) is built from *prati*, "towards", and the root √śri, "to lean on, to resort to"; MW gives *pratiśraya* as refuge, a place of shelter, a house (MW).
