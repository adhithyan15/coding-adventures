## You'll want to know: प्रयोगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C362-ANUSANDHANA362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोगः** — *prayogaḥ* — "an experiment; a use".

> वैज्ञानिकः नूतनं प्रयोगं करोति।
>
> *vaijñānikaḥ nūtanaṃ prayogaṃ karoti.*
>
> The scientist carries out a new experiment.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोगः** (*prayogaḥ*) is built on *pra* and the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives *pra-yoga* "application, use" and "practice, experiment" (MW).
