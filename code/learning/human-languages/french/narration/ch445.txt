French, chapter 445: Petites boutiques.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la quincaillerie — the hardware shop, the ironmonger’s.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the grocer, then the French for the greengrocer.

You'll want to know: la quincaillerie.
la quincaillerie — "the hardware shop, the ironmonger’s".
Il achète des clous à la quincaillerie. He buys nails at the hardware shop.
Le quincaillier runs it.

The word, taken apart.
quincaillerie is built on quincaille 'small metal goods', which is usually traced to an old word that imitates the clink of metal. EtymDB lists it without a source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la droguerie — the household goods shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the greengrocer, then the French for the hardware shop.

You'll want to know: la droguerie.
la droguerie — "the household goods shop".
À la droguerie, elle trouve des produits pour le ménage. At the household shop she finds cleaning products.
Not a chemist's: it sells paint, soap and cleaning products.

The word, taken apart.
droguerie is built on drogue, first 'dried goods, spices'. The origin of drogue is not settled; it is sometimes linked to Dutch droog 'dry'.

papeterie is built on papier 'paper' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin papyrus, from Greek pápyros.
