## You'll want to know: محدد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C417-WUDUH417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**محدد** — *muḥaddad* — "specific".

> وصل الضيوف في الوقت المحدد.
>
> *waṣala aḍ-ḍuyūf fī al-waqt al-muḥaddad.*
>
> The guests arrived at the set time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محدد** (*muḥaddad*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-d-d*, "to sharpen; to set a limit" (Lane). *Muḥaddad* is the Form II passive participle of *ḥaddada*, to specify: fixed, specific.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
