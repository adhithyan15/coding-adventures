German, chapter 349: Eine Stelle suchen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Stellenanzeige — the job advertisement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the excuse, then the German for that is why.

You'll want to know: die Stellenanzeige.
die Stellenanzeige — "the job advertisement".
In der Zeitung habe ich eine interessante Stellenanzeige gefunden. I found an interesting job advertisement in the newspaper.
Plural: die Stellenanzeigen. Online it is often das Stellenangebot.

The word, taken apart.
Stellenanzeige joins Stelle "place, job", from the family of stellen "to put" (Proto-Germanic stallijaną, EtymDB), and Anzeige "notice, advertisement", from anzeigen "to show".

Lesson 2 of 5.

das Vorstellungsgespräch — "the job interview".
Morgen habe ich ein Vorstellungsgespräch bei einer Bank. Tomorrow I have a job interview at a bank.
Plural: die Vorstellungsgespräche. Sich vorstellen — to introduce yourself — is at its heart.

The word, taken apart.
Vorstellungsgespräch joins Vorstellung "introduction", from vorstellen, built on stellen "to put" (EtymDB), and Gespräch "conversation", from Old High German gisprāhhi (EtymDB): the talk where you introduce yourself.

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Fähigkeit — the ability, the skill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the job advertisement, then the German for the job interview.

You'll want to know: die Fähigkeit.
die Fähigkeit — "the ability, the skill".
Für diese Stelle braucht man die Fähigkeit, im Team zu arbeiten. For this job you need the ability to work in a team.
Plural: die Fähigkeiten. From fähig — able, capable.

The word, taken apart.
Fähigkeit is built on fähig "able", inherited from Middle High German gevæhic, from vāhen "to catch, to seize" (EtymDB): someone who can take hold of a task.

Lesson 4 of 5.
qualifiziert — qualified.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the job interview, then the German for the ability.

You'll want to know: qualifiziert.
qualifiziert — "qualified".
Sie ist für die Stelle sehr gut qualifiziert. She is very well qualified for the job.
The participle of qualifizieren; sich qualifizieren — to qualify, also in sport.

The word, taken apart.
qualifiziert is the participle of qualifizieren, a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin quālificāre "to give a quality to", built on Latin quālis "of what kind" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
beherrschen — to master, to have a good command of; to control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the ability, then the German for qualified.

You'll want to know: beherrschen.
beherrschen — "to master, to have a good command of; to control".
Er beherrscht drei Sprachen. He has a good command of three languages.
Inseparable: er beherrscht, er hat beherrscht. Sich beherrschen — to control yourself.

The word, taken apart.
beherrschen is built with be- on herrschen "to rule", inherited from Middle High German hērschen and Old High German hērisōn (EtymDB): you rule over a skill.
