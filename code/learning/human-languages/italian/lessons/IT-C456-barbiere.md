## You'll want to know: il barbiere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C456-CURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il barbiere** — "the barber".

> Mio nonno va dal barbiere ogni sabato.
>
> *My grandfather goes to the barber every Saturday.*

*Farsi la barba* — to shave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barbiere* is built on *barba* 'beard' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *barba*.
