## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درس** (*dars*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-s*, "to become effaced; to study" (Lane). Lane gives *dars* as a lecture, a lesson.
