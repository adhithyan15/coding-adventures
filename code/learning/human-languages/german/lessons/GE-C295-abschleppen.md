## You'll want to know: abschleppen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C295-PANNE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**abschleppen** — "to tow away".

> Das Auto wurde abgeschleppt.
>
> *The car was towed away.*

Separable: *Sie schleppen das Auto ab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abschleppen* is built on *schleppen* "to drag", Middle High German *slepen*, which came from Middle Low German *slēpen* "to drag" (EtymDB).
