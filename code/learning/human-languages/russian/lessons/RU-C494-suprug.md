## You'll want to know: супруг
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C494-MARRIAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**супруг** (*suprúg*) — "a spouse, a husband".

> На приём она пришла с супругом.
>
> *Na priyóm oná prishlá s suprúgom.*
>
> She came to the reception with her husband.

Formal. The wife is *супруга*; the plural *супруги* means a married couple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*супруг* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sǫprǫgъ* (EtymDB), built on *sǫ-* 'together' and a root usually glossed 'to harness', so spouses are a pair in one harness.
