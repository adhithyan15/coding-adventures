## You'll want to know: накануне
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C272-ZARANEE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**накануне** (*nakanúne*) — "the day before, on the eve".

> Накануне свадьбы она не спала.
>
> *Nakanúne svádby oná ne spalá.*
>
> On the eve of the wedding she did not sleep.

With the genitive: *накануне праздника* — on the eve of the holiday. Alone it means "the day before".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*накануне* is built on *на* "on" and *канун* "the eve of a feast", which is usually traced to Greek *kanṓn* "a rule", the name of a church service sung on the eve; EtymDB lists no parent.
