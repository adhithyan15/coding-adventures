## You'll want to know: épuisé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C361-STRESS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**épuisé** — "exhausted, worn out; sold out".

> Après ce voyage, je suis épuisé.
>
> *After that trip, I am exhausted.*

Of a book or a product: sold out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*épuisé* is the participle of *épuiser* "to drain dry", built on *puits* "a well", which comes from Latin *puteus* "a well" (Lewis & Short): drained like a well.
