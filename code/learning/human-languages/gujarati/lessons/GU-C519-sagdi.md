## You'll want to know: સગડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C519-SAGDI519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સગડી** — *sagḍī* — "a charcoal stove".

> શિયાળામાં દાદી સગડી પાસે બેસતી હતી.
>
> *śiyāḷāmā̃ dādī sagḍī pāse bestī hatī.*
>
> In winter my grandmother used to sit by the charcoal stove.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સગડી** (*sagḍī*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
