## You'll want to know: a continuación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C534-PRONTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a continuación** — "next, then; below".

> A continuación, el ladrón salió por la ventana.
>
> *Next, the thief went out through the window.*

In a text it means below: *Lea las instrucciones que aparecen a continuación.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a continuación* is built on *continuación*, a learned borrowing from Latin *continuātiō* 'an unbroken series, a following of one thing after another' (Lewis & Short), from *continuus* 'joined, unbroken'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
