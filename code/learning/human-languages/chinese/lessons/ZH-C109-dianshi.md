## You'll want to know: diànshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C109-SCREEN109-02]; assesses=[] -->

*diànshì* — "television".

> *wǒ wǎnshang kàn diànshì* — I watch TV in the evening.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diànshì* is built from 电 'electricity' and shì 'to look', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *dzyijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as lightning. A modern word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
