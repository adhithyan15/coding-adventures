## You'll want to know: घबराहट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C125-CALM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घबराहट** (*ghabrāhaṭ*) — "nervousness", a flutter of panic.

**मुझे घबराहट हो रही है** — "I'm feeling nervous".

## What you've built
