## You'll want to know: batizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C310-BATIZADO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**batizar** — "to baptise, to christen; to name".

> Batizaram a menina em junho.
>
> *They christened the little girl in June.*

Also to give a name to anything: *batizaram o barco com o nome da avó*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*batizar* comes down from Old Portuguese *bautizar* (EtymDB), from Latin *baptizāre*, from Greek *baptízein* "to dip".
