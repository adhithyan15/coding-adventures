# સોનું (sonũ) — gold

## You'll want to know: સોનું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C432-GOLD432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સોનું** — *sonũ* — "gold".

> દાદી પાસે સોનાની વીંટી છે.
>
> *dādī pāse sonānī vī̃ṭī che.*
>
> Grandmother has a gold ring.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સોનું** (*sonũ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *suvarṇa*, "of a good colour", and so "gold"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 13519).
