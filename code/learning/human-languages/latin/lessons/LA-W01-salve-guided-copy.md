# salvē — copy one greeting with help

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-PHONO-SALVE-01, LA-ETYMON-SALVE-02] -->

Point to **salvē** and say it once: *SAL-weh*. The model stays on the page.

## Writing — one guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-PHONO-SALVE-01, LA-ETYMON-SALVE-02, LA-GRAMMAR-SALVE-03] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **salvē** once on the line below it. Write **salve** first, then add the
short macron over the final **ē**. Look back at the model whenever you want.

Say the word as you finish. The macron belongs to the vowel: it records the long
sound you already heard; it is not a new letter and not something to guess.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-PHONO-SALVE-01, LA-ETYMON-SALVE-02] -->

Compare your copy with the model. If one mark differs, repair only that mark.
No hiding, no spelling from memory, and no second word today.
