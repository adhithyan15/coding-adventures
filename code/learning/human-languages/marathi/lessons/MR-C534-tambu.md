## You'll want to know: तंबू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C534-BAHER534-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तंबू** — *tambū* — "a tent".

> शिबिरात आम्ही तंबूत झोपलो.
>
> *śibirāt āmhī tambūt jhoplo.*
>
> At the camp we slept in a tent.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तंबू** (*tambū*) is usually traced to Persian; the exact path into Marathi is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
