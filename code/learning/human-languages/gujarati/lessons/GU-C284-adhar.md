# આધાર (ādhār) — a support; what something rests on

## You'll want to know: આધાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C284-ADHAR-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-AGREE-01, GU-LEX-C51-FAMILY1-02, GU-LEX-C51-FAMILY1-01] -->

**આધાર** — *ādhār* — "a support; what something rests on". It is masculine: **મારો આધાર**, "my support".

> **મારાં માતા-પિતા મારો આધાર છે.**
>
> *mārā̃ mātā-pitā māro ādhār che.* — "My parents are my support."

A person can be a **આધાર**, and so can a fact that a plan rests on. The next lesson builds "it depends" on that second sense.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આધાર** (*ādhār*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *ādhāra*, "support, prop", and "the power of holding up" (Monier-Williams), from *ā-* and the root *dhṛ-*, "to hold".
