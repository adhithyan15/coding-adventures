Lesson 1 of 5.
తరగతి (taragati) — a class, a grade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for groceries, then the Telugu for a purchase.

You'll want to know: తరగతి.
తరగతి — taragati — "a class, a grade".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of తరగతి (taragati) is uncertain. DEDR and CDIAL list no word of this shape meaning "class, grade".

Lesson 4 of 5.
విషయం (viṣayaṁ) — a subject, a matter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a lesson, then the Telugu for an answer.

You'll want to know: విషయం.
విషయం — viṣayaṁ — "a subject, a matter".

The word, taken apart.
విషయం (viṣayaṁ) is Sanskrit viṣaya, "scope; sphere, region", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 11973). The sense "subject, matter" is the one Telugu gives it.
