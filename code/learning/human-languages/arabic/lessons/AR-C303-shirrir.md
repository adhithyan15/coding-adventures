## You'll want to know: شرير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C303-USTURA303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شرير** — *shirrīr* — "a villain; wicked".

> كان الملك في الحكاية شريرا.
>
> *kāna al-malik fī al-ḥikāya shirrīran.*
>
> The king in the tale was wicked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرير** (*shirrīr*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-r* (Lane), the root of *sharr*, evil. The *fiʿʿīl* pattern marks a quality held strongly: "very evil".
