## You'll want to know: die Ferienwohnung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C424-GASTHOF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ferienwohnung** — "the holiday flat, the holiday apartment".

> Wir mieten eine Ferienwohnung am See.
>
> *We are renting a holiday flat by the lake.*

Plural: *die Ferienwohnungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ferienwohnung* is built from *Ferien* 'holidays', a learned borrowing from Latin *fēriae* 'days of rest, festivals' (Lewis & Short), and *Wohnung* 'flat', from *wohnen* 'to live', inherited from Old High German *wonēn* (EtymDB).
