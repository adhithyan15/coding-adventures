## You'll want to know: فایل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C418-NARMAFZAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فایل** (*fâyl*) — "a computer file".

> فایلها را برای همکارم فرستادم.
>
> *fâylhâ râ barâye hamkâram ferestâdam.*
>
> I sent the files to my colleague.

An English loan. Plural *fâylhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فایل** was borrowed from English *file*.
