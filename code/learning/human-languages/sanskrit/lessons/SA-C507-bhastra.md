## You'll want to know: भस्त्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C507-BHASTRA507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भस्त्रा** — *bhastrā* — "a bellows".

> लोहकारः भस्त्रां चालयति।
>
> *lohakāraḥ bhastrāṃ cālayati.*
>
> The blacksmith works the bellows.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भस्त्रा** (*bhastrā*) is the word MW records for a leather bottle or bag, and so a bellows; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
