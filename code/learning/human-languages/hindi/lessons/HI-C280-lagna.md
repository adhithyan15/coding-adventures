# लगना (lagnā) — to seem, to be felt

## You'll want to know: लगना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C280-LAGNA-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C63-STATE-01, HI-LEX-C63-STATE-02, HI-LEX-C41-ADJ-03, HI-CONCEPT-C37-KHAANA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C35-PASAND-01] -->

**लगना** — *lagnā* — "to seem; to be felt".

> **मुझे भूख लगी है।**
>
> *mujhe bhūkh lagī hai.* — "I'm hungry."

Word for word, "hunger has struck me". The one who feels it takes **मुझे**, as in **मुझे चाय चाहिए**, and the verb follows the thing felt: **भूख** is feminine, so **लगी**.

> **यह खाना मुझे अच्छा लगता है।**
>
> *yah khānā mujhe acchā lagtā hai.* — "I like this food."

Word for word, "this food seems good to me". **खाना** is masculine, so **लगता**. It says much what **मुझे … पसंद है** says.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लगना** (*lagnā*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL traces it to Sanskrit *lagyati*, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895), and gives "to be attached to" as a Hindi sense: a hunger, a hurt or a liking attaches itself to the one who feels it.
