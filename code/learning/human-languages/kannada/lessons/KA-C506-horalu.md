## You'll want to know: ಹೊರಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C506-NIDDE506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊರಳು** — *horaḷu* — "to roll over; to toss".

> ಮಗು ನಿದ್ದೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೊರಳಿತು.
>
> *magu niddeyalli horaḷitu.*
>
> The baby rolled over in its sleep.

Past (I …): ಹೊರಳಿದೆ, *horaḷide*.

ಹೊರಳಾಡು, to toss and turn all night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊರಳು** (*horaḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4285 lists Kannada *poraḷ*, "to roll, wallow". Old p- became h-.
