Gujarati, chapter 307: You Saved Her Life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઉગારવું (ugārvũ) — to rescue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for grain, then the Gujarati for to throng.

You'll want to know: ઉગારવું.
ઉગારવું — ugārvũ — "to rescue".
અરુણે નદીમાંથી એક છોકરીને ઉગારી. Aruṇe nadīmā̃thī ek chokrīne ugārī. Arun rescued a girl from the river.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને ઉગાર્યું, teṇe bāḷakne ugāryũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઉગારવું (ugārvũ) is the causative of ugarvũ, to escape, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit udgirati, "spits out, gives out" (CDIAL 1960): to make someone escape a danger. The person saved takes ને: છોકરીને ઉગારી.

Lesson 2 of 5.

ઋણી — ṛṇī — "indebted; grateful".
છોકરીની માતાએ અરુણને કહ્યું, હું તમારી ઋણી છું. chokrīnī mātāe Aruṇne kahyũ, hũ tamārī ṛṇī chũ. The girl's mother told Arun that she was in his debt.

The word, taken apart.
ઋણી (ṛṇī) is a tatsama, Sanskrit ṛṇin, "one who is in debt" (Monier-Williams), built on ṛṇa, a debt. It agrees with the speaker: a man says હું તમારો ઋણી છું, a woman તમારી ઋણી.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rescue, then the Gujarati for indebted.

You'll want to know: ઊંડું.
ઊંડું — ū̃ḍũ — "deep".
કૂવો ખૂબ ઊંડો હતો. kūvo khūb ū̃ḍo hato. The well was very deep.

The word, taken apart.
ઊંડું (ū̃ḍũ) comes from ūṇḍa, "deep", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 2402). It agrees like મોટું: ઊંડો કૂવો, ઊંડી નદી, ઊંડું પાણી.

Lesson 4 of 5.
હેમખેમ (hemkhem) — safe and sound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for indebted, then the Gujarati for deep.

You'll want to know: હેમખેમ.
હેમખેમ — hemkhem — "safe and sound".
બાળક હેમખેમ ઘરે પહોંચ્યું. bāḷak hemkhem ghare pahõcyũ. The child got home safe and sound.

The word, taken apart.
હેમખેમ (hemkhem) is an echo pair built on khem, "wellbeing", which CDIAL lists for Gujarati under Sanskrit kṣḗma, "safety, ease" (CDIAL 3745); the rhyming hem in front adds weight. It is said of people who come through danger: હેમખેમ પહોંચ્યું.

હાશ (hāś) — phew (in relief).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for deep, then the Gujarati for safe and sound.

You'll want to know: હાશ.
હાશ — hāś — "phew (in relief)".
બાળક બહાર આવ્યું ત્યારે બધાએ કહ્યું, હાશ! bāḷak bahār āvyũ tyāre badhāe kahyũ, hāś! When the child came out, everyone breathed again: phew!

The word, taken apart.
હાશ (hāś) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. It is the sound of breath let out after fear: હાશ!, thank goodness.
