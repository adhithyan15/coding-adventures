Gujarati, chapter 412: Tents and Camps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ડેરો (ḍero) — a tent; a camp (for a stay).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to roll up, then the Gujarati for to put to sleep.

You'll want to know: ડેરો.
ડેરો — ḍero — "a tent; a camp (for a stay)".
નદીના કિનારે અમે ડેરો નાખ્યો. nadīnā kināre ame ḍero nākhyo. We pitched our tent on the river bank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ડેરો (ḍero) goes back to ḍēra, "a resting place", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a tent, a shelter" (CDIAL 5564).

Lesson 2 of 5.
પડાવ (paḍāv) — a halt for the night; a camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to put to sleep, then the Gujarati for a tent.

You'll want to know: પડાવ.
પડાવ — paḍāv — "a halt for the night; a camp".
સાંજે અમારો પડાવ ગામની બહાર હતો. sā̃je amāro paḍāv gāmnī bahār hato. In the evening our camp was outside the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પડાવ (paḍāv) seems to be built on પડવું, to fall, here to stop and lie down for the night; CDIAL lists that verb under Sanskrit pátati, "falls" (CDIAL 7722), but gives no form for પડાવ itself, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
રાતવાસો (rātvāso) — a night's stay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a tent, then the Gujarati for a halt for the night.

You'll want to know: રાતવાસો.
રાતવાસો — rātvāso — "a night's stay".
અમે ધર્મશાળામાં રાતવાસો કર્યો. ame dharmaśāḷāmā̃ rātvāso karyo. We stayed the night at the rest house.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રાતવાસો (rātvāso) is built from રાત, night, and વાસો, a stay, from Sanskrit vāsa, "a dwelling, an abode" (Monier-Williams): a roof for one night.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ખોડવું (khoḍvũ) — to drive in (a peg); to plant (a pole).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a halt for the night, then the Gujarati for a night's stay.

You'll want to know: ખોડવું.
ખોડવું — khoḍvũ — "to drive in (a peg); to plant (a pole)".
છોકરાએ રેતીમાં લાંબી ડાળી ખોડી. chokrāe retīmā̃ lā̃bī ḍāḷī khoḍī. The boy stuck a long branch into the sand.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ડાળી ખોડી, teṇe ḍāḷī khoḍī.

The word, taken apart.
ખોડવું (khoḍvũ) may belong with a Gujarati word for a large block of wood, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit kṣōḍa, "a post to which an elephant is tied" (CDIAL 3748), beside Marathi khoḍ, "a tree stump": to drive a post into the ground. CDIAL gives no form for ખોડવું itself, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ગાળવું (gāḷvũ) — to spend (time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a night's stay, then the Gujarati for to drive in.

You'll want to know: ગાળવું.
ગાળવું — gāḷvũ — "to spend (time)".
અમે બે દિવસ ગામમાં ગાળ્યા. ame be divas gāmmā̃ gāḷyā. We spent two days in the village.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે રાત ત્યાં ગાળી, teṇe rāt tyā̃ gāḷī.

The word, taken apart.
ગાળવું (gāḷvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit gālayati, "causes to drop, strains, melts"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, which also means to pass time (CDIAL 4144): the days drip away.
