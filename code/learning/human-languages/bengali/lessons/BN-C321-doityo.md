# দৈত্য (doityô) — a giant; a demon

## You'll want to know: দৈত্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C321-KINGBODONTI321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**দৈত্য** — *doityô* — "a giant; a demon".

> রূপকথায় একটা দৈত্য রাজকুমারকে বনে আটকাল।
>
> *rūpkôthāy ekṭā doityô rājkumārke bône āṭkālô.*
>
> In the fairy tale a giant trapped the prince in the forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দৈত্য** (*doityô*) is Sanskrit *daitya*, a son of Diti, one of the demons of the old stories (Monier-Williams); in a Bengali fairy tale it is a huge, rough giant.
