Kannada, chapter 323: A Crime in the Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಪರಾಧ (aparādha) — a crime; an offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a throne, then the Kannada for a crown.

You'll want to know: ಅಪರಾಧ.
ಅಪರಾಧ — aparādha — "a crime; an offence".
ಅಪರಾಧ ಮಾಡಿದರೆ ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ ಸಿಗುತ್ತದೆ. aparādha māḍidare śikṣe siguttade. If you commit a crime, you get punished.
One who commits a crime is an ಅಪರಾಧಿ, a criminal. A crime is "done": ಅಪರಾಧ (aparādha) ಮಾಡು.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಪರಾಧ (aparādha) is borrowed from Sanskrit aparādha, "an offence, a fault" (CDIAL 439).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಬಂಧಿಸು.
ಬಂಧಿಸು — bandhisu — "to arrest".
ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಕಳ್ಳನನ್ನು ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಬಂಧಿಸಿದರು. polīsaru kaḷḷanannu beḷagge bandhisidaru. The police arrested the thief in the morning.
Past (I …): ಬಂಧಿಸಿದೆ, bandhiside.
The person arrested takes -ಅನ್ನು: ಕಳ್ಳನನ್ನು ಬಂಧಿಸು (bandhisu).

The word, taken apart.
ಬಂಧಿಸು (bandhisu) is built on Sanskrit bandha, "a bond", and also "custody" (CDIAL 9136), from the root bandh, "to bind", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಠಾಣೆ (ṭhāṇe) — a police station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a crime, then the Kannada for to arrest.

You'll want to know: ಠಾಣೆ.
ಠಾಣೆ — ṭhāṇe — "a police station".
ನಾನು ಠಾಣೆಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ದೂರು ಕೊಟ್ಟೆ. nānu ṭhāṇege hōgi dūru koṭṭe. I went to the police station and made a complaint.
In full it is ಪೊಲೀಸ್ ಠಾಣೆ (ṭhāṇe).

The word, taken apart.
ಠಾಣೆ (ṭhāṇe) came through Hindustani thānā, "a post, a police station", which goes back to Sanskrit sthāna, "a place, an abode" (CDIAL 13753).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತನಿಖೆ (tanikhe) — an investigation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to arrest, then the Kannada for a police station.

You'll want to know: ತನಿಖೆ.
ತನಿಖೆ — tanikhe — "an investigation".
ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಆ ಘಟನೆಯ ತನಿಖೆ ಶುರು ಮಾಡಿದರು. polīsaru ā ghaṭaneya tanikhe śuru māḍidaru. The police began an investigation into that incident.
ತನಿಖೆ (tanikhe) ಮಾಡು, to investigate. What is investigated takes the possessive -ಅ: ಘಟನೆಯ ತನಿಖೆ (tanikhe).

The word, taken apart.
ತನಿಖೆ (tanikhe) is usually traced to Hindustani tanqīh, "an inquiry, an investigation", a word of Arabic origin; the path into Kannada is not settled.

ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಆ ಕೆಲಸಗಾರನನ್ನು ಶಂಕಿಸಿದರು. polīsaru ā kelasagāranannu śaṅkisidaru. The police suspected that worker.

The word, taken apart.
ಶಂಕಿಸು (śaṅkisu) is built on Sanskrit śaṅkā, "fear, distrust, suspicion" (CDIAL 12258), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
