## You'll want to know: der Bösewicht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C520-BUECHER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Bösewicht** — "villain".

> Im Film ist der Bösewicht am Ende im Gefängnis.
>
> *In the film the villain ends up in prison.*

Plural: *die Bösewichte*. Half joking when said of real people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bösewicht* is built on *böse* "evil", from Proto-Germanic *bausijaz*, and *Wicht* "creature, wretch", inherited from Old High German *wiht* (EtymDB).
