## You'll want to know: ушиб
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C297-TRAVMA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ушиб** (*ushíb*) — "a bruise, a knock".

> У мальчика сильный ушиб ноги.
>
> *U málchika sílnyy ushíb nogí.*
>
> The boy has a bad bruise on his leg.

Masculine; genitive *ушиба*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ушиб* is built on *ушибить* "to knock, to bruise", from the old verb *шибать* "to strike"; EtymDB lists both with no parent.
