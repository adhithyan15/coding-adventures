## You'll want to know: chéngjì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C249-STUDY249-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngjì* — "results; marks".

> *tā de chéngjì hěn hǎo* — His marks are very good.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngjì* is built from *chéng* 'to complete' and *jì* 'a deed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *tsek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to finish' and the second 'merit' and 'a deed'.
