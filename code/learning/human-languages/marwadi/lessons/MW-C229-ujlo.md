## You'll want to know: ऊजळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C229-LIGHT229-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊजळो** — *ūjḷo* — "bright; clear".

> आज रात चांद ऊजळो है।
>
> *āj rāt chā̃d ūjḷo hai.*
>
> Tonight the moon is bright.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊजळो** (*ūjḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ujjvala*, "blazing up, bright"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ujala*, "bright" (CDIAL 1670).
