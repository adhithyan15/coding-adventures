# ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ (allagaḷe) — to deny; to refute

## You'll want to know: ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C383-SANDEHA383-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ** — *allagaḷe* — "to deny; to refute".

> ಅವನು ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಆರೋಪಗಳನ್ನು ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆದನು.
>
> *avanu ellā ārōpagaḷannu allagaḷedanu.*
>
> He denied all the accusations.

Past (I …): ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆದೆ, *allagaḷede*.

What you deny takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಲ್ಲಗಳೆ** (*allagaḷe*) is built from two parts: native *alla*, "it is not so" (DEDR 234), and *kaḷe*, "to cast off": to throw a claim out as untrue.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
