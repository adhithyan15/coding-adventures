## You'll want to know: तपना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C439-TAP439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तपना** — *tapnā* — "to scorch; to be burning hot".

> जून में सड़कें धूप में तपती हैं।
>
> *jūn meṁ saṛkeṁ dhūp meṁ taptī haiṁ.*
>
> In June the roads bake in the sun.

Past (it …): वह छत धूप में तपी, *vah chat dhūp meṁ tapī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तपना** (*tapnā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *tapyati*, "is hot" (CDIAL 5684), the same root as ताप.
