## You'll want to know: xuèyā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C479-BLOOD479-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xuèyā* — "blood pressure".

> *wǒ bàba de xuèyā bǐ wǒ gāo* — My dad's blood pressure is higher than mine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuèyā* is built from 血 'blood' and *yā* 'to press', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwet* and *'aep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to press down'.
