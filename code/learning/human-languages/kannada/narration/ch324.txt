Kannada, chapter 324: Fire in the Market.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ (agniśāmaka) — a firefighter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an investigation, then the Kannada for to suspect.

You'll want to know: ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ.
ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ — agniśāmaka — "a firefighter".
ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ ಬೆಂಕಿ ಹತ್ತಿದರೆ ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು ತಕ್ಷಣ ಬರುತ್ತಾರೆ. ondu vēḷe beṅki hattidare agniśāmakaru takṣaṇa baruttāre. If a fire breaks out, the firefighters come at once.
Plural: ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು, agniśāmakaru.
The fire brigade is ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ (agniśāmaka) ದಳ.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ (agniśāmaka) is built from Sanskrit: a modern compound of agni, "fire" (CDIAL 55), and a noun built on the root śam, "to calm, to put out" (Monier-Williams): one who puts out fire.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ (hottiko) — to catch fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to suspect, then the Kannada for a firefighter.

You'll want to know: ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ.
ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ — hottiko — "to catch fire".
ಅಡುಗೆಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಎಣ್ಣೆ ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡಿತು. aḍugemaneyalli eṇṇe hottikoṇḍitu. The oil caught fire in the kitchen.
Past (it …): ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡಿತು, hottikoṇḍitu.
The thing that burns is the subject: ಮನೆ ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡಿತು, the house caught fire.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊತ್ತಿಕೊ (hottiko) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4517 lists Kannada pottu, "to be kindled, catch fire, flame", and modern Kannada adds -ko, from koḷ "to take" (DEDR 2151).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ (samayaprajñe) — presence of mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a firefighter, then the Kannada for to catch fire.

You'll want to know: ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ.
ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ — samayaprajñe — "presence of mind".
ಹುಡುಗಿಯ ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆಯಿಂದ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಬೆಂಕಿಯಿಂದ ತಪ್ಪಿಸಿಕೊಂಡರು. huḍugiya samayaprajñeyinda ellarū beṅkiyinda tappisikoṇḍaru. Thanks to the girl's presence of mind, everyone escaped the fire.
Quick, calm thinking in danger: ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ (samayaprajñe) ತೋರಿಸು, to show presence of mind.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ (samayaprajñe) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of samaya, "a meeting, an occasion, time" (CDIAL 13185), and prajñā, "wisdom, awareness" (CDIAL 8515).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಂದಿಸು (nandisu) — to extinguish; to put out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to catch fire, then the Kannada for presence of mind.

You'll want to know: ನಂದಿಸು.
ನಂದಿಸು — nandisu — "to extinguish; to put out".
ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು ಒಂದು ಗಂಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬೆಂಕಿಯನ್ನು ನಂದಿಸಿದರು. agniśāmakaru ondu gaṇṭeyalli beṅkiyannu nandisidaru. The firefighters put out the fire in an hour.
Past (I …): ನಂದಿಸಿದೆ, nandiside.
Fire and a lamp are put out: ದೀಪವನ್ನು ನಂದಿಸು (nandisu). A fire going out by itself is ನಂದು.

The word, taken apart.
ನಂದಿಸು (nandisu) is native Dravidian: the causative in -isu of nandu, "to be quenched, to go out", which DEDR 3590 lists for Kannada.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ರಕ್ಷಿಸು (rakṣisu) — to rescue; to protect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for presence of mind, then the Kannada for to extinguish.

You'll want to know: ರಕ್ಷಿಸು.
ರಕ್ಷಿಸು — rakṣisu — "to rescue; to protect".
ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು ಆ ಮನೆಯಿಂದ ಮೂರು ಮಕ್ಕಳನ್ನು ರಕ್ಷಿಸಿದರು. agniśāmakaru ā maneyinda mūru makkaḷannu rakṣisidaru. The firefighters rescued three children from that house.
Past (I …): ರಕ್ಷಿಸಿದೆ, rakṣiside.
Who is rescued takes -ಅನ್ನು; what from takes -ಇಂದ.

The word, taken apart.
ರಕ್ಷಿಸು (rakṣisu) is built on Sanskrit rakṣaṇa, "guarding, tending, protection" (CDIAL 10546), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
