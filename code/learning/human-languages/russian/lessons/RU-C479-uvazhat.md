## You'll want to know: уважать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C479-TRUST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**уважать** (*uvazhát*) — "to respect".

> Студенты уважают этого профессора.
>
> *Studénty uvazháyut étogo proféssora.*
>
> The students respect this professor.

Imperfective. The noun is *уважение* — respect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*уважать* was borrowed from Polish *uważać* 'to consider' (EtymDB).
