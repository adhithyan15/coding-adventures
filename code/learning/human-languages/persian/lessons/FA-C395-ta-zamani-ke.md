# تا زمانی که (tâ zamâni ke) — as long as; until

## You'll want to know: تا زمانی که
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C395-BASTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تا زمانی که** (*tâ zamâni ke*) — "as long as; until".

> تا زمانی که اینجا هستی، میتوانی از اتاق من استفاده کنی.
>
> *tâ zamâni ke injâ hasti, mitavâni az otâq-e man estefâde koni.*
>
> As long as you are here, you can use my room.

With a negative verb it means "until": *tâ zamâni ke nayâyad* — until he comes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تا زمانی که** is literally "until the time that": *tâ* is inherited Persian (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian "until, so that"), and *zamân* comes from Arabic *zamān* "time".
