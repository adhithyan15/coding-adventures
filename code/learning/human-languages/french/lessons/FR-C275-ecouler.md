## You'll want to know: écouler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C275-DELAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**écouler** — "to go by (as s'écouler, of time); to sell off (goods)".

> Trois ans se sont écoulés depuis son départ.
>
> *Three years have gone by since he left.*

Mostly used as *s'écouler*: time goes by, and water flows out (*l'eau s'écoule*). Without *se*, it means to sell off goods: *écouler un stock*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*écouler* is built on *couler* "to flow", which is inherited from Latin *cōlāre* "to strain, to filter" (Lewis & Short).
