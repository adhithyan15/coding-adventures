## You'll want to know: ಅನ್ವಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C428-ONDOMME428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅನ್ವಯ** — *anvaya* — "applicability".

> ಈ ನಿಯಮ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಅನ್ವಯ ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ī niyama ellarigū anvaya āguttade.*
>
> This rule applies to everyone.

ಅನ್ವಯ ಆಗು / ಅನ್ವಯಿಸು, to apply to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅನ್ವಯ** (*anvaya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anvaya*, "connection, being linked to or concerned with" (Monier-Williams).
