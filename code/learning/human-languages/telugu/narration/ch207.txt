Telugu, chapter 207: To cool, To tap, To pluck, To till, To grow.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
చల్లార్చు (callārcu) — to cool (something) down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to tread on, then the Telugu for to hit.

You'll want to know: చల్లార్చు.
చల్లార్చు — callārcu — "to cool (something) down".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: callārcu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: callārcu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say callārcu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for to tread on, then the Telugu for to hit, then say callārcu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does చల్లార్చు (callārcu) mean? ("To cool (something) down".) Say it once more.
