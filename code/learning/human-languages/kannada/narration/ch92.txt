Kannada, chapter 92: School and Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಶಾಲೆ (śāle) — school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for home town, then the Kannada for language.

You'll want to know: ಶಾಲೆ.
ಶಾಲೆ (śāle) — "school".
A ಶಿಕ್ಷಕ (śikṣaka), "teacher", works in one.

What you've built.
Where the teacher you know works.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: śāle]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: śāle, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say śāle]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for home town, then the Kannada for language, then say śāle again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಶಾಲೆ (śāle) mean? ("School".) Say it once more.

ಕಾಲೇಜು (kālēju) — "college", borrowed from English and given a Kannada ending ‑ಉ (-u), the way Kannada rounds off words that end in a consonant.

What you've built.
