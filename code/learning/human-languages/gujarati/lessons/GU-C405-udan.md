## You'll want to know: ઉડાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C405-FLIGHT405-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉડાન** — *uḍān* — "a flight".

> અમારી ઉડાન આવતીકાલે સવારે છે.
>
> *amārī uḍān āvtīkāle savāre che.*
>
> Our flight is tomorrow morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉડાન** (*uḍān*) is built on ઊડવું, "to fly", a tadbhava of Sanskrit *uḍḍīyatē*, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697): the flying itself, and then the journey by air.
