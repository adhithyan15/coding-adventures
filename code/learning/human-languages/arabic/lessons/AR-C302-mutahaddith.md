## You'll want to know: متحدث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C302-HIWAR302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**متحدث** — *mutaḥaddith* — "a spokesperson; a speaker".

> قال متحدث إن المصنع مغلق اليوم.
>
> *qāla mutaḥaddith inna al-maṣnaʿ mughlaq al-yawm.*
>
> A spokesman said that the factory is closed today.

متحدث باسم الشركة: a spokesman for the company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متحدث** (*mutaḥaddith*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-d-th* (Lane), the root of the taught *ḥadīth*, talk. *Mutaḥaddith* is the Form V participle, "one who speaks".
