## You'll want to know: પેન્શન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C427-SAVE427-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પેન્શન** — *penśan* — "a pension".

> નિવૃત્ત થયા પછી દાદાને પેન્શન મળે છે.
>
> *nivṛtt thayā pachī dādāne penśan maḷe che.*
>
> Since he retired, grandfather has received a pension.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પેન્શન** (*penśan*) is borrowed from English *pension*.
