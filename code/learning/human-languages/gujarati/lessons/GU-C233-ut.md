## You'll want to know: ઊંટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C233-WORK233-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંટ** — *ū̃ṭ* — "camel".

> **ઊંટ રણમાં ચાલ્યો.** — *ū̃ṭ raṇmā̃ cālyo* — "The camel walked in the desert."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંટ** (*ū̃ṭ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *uṣṭra*, which meant a buffalo in the oldest texts and a camel later (CDIAL 2387).
