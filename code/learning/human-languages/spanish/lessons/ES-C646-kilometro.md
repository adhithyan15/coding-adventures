## You'll want to know: el kilómetro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C646-GASOLINA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el kilómetro** — "the kilometre".

> Faltan diez kilómetros.
>
> *There are ten kilometres to go.*

Often written *km*. *El kilómetro cero* is the point distances are measured from.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kilómetro* was borrowed from French *kilomètre*, a word made from Greek *khílioi* "a thousand" and *métron* "measure".
