## You'll want to know: бульвар
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C440-PEREKHOD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**бульвар** (*bulvár*) — "a boulevard".

> Вдоль бульвара растут старые деревья.
>
> *Vdol bulvára rastút stáryye derévya.*
>
> Old trees grow along the boulevard.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бульвар* was borrowed from French *boulevard* (EtymDB), which goes back to Middle Dutch *bollewerc* 'a bulwark, a bastion'.
