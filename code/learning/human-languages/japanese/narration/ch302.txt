Japanese, chapter 302: Ghost Stories.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ゆうれい (yūrei) — a ghost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for poor, then the Japanese for greedy.

You'll want to know: ゆうれい.
ゆうれい — yūrei — "a ghost".
その ふるい てらには ゆうれいが います。 — sono furui tera ni wa yūrei ga imasu — "There is a ghost in that old temple."

The word, taken apart.
ゆうれい (yūrei) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are yū, "faint" (Middle Chinese 'jiw) and rei, "spirits" (leng) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
おばけ (obake) — a spook; a monster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for greedy, then the Japanese for a ghost.

You'll want to know: おばけ.
おばけ — obake — "a spook; a monster".
いもうとは おばけが こわいです。 — imōto wa obake ga kowai desu — "My little sister is afraid of ghosts."

The word, taken apart.
おばけ (obake) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from o-, "the polite prefix", and bake, "changing shape" (from bakeru) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 3 of 5.
うらむ (uramu) — to bear a grudge against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a ghost, then the Japanese for a spook.

You'll want to know: うらむ.
うらむ — uramu — "to bear a grudge against".
ゆうれいは その おとこを うらんでいました。 — yūrei wa sono otoko o urande imashita — "The ghost held a grudge against the man."
Polite うらみます, uramimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): おんなが おとこを うらみました。, onna ga otoko o uramimashita.

The word, taken apart.
うらむ (uramu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
