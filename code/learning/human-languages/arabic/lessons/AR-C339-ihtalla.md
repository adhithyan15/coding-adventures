## You'll want to know: احتل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C339-GHAZW339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**احتل** — *iḥtalla* — "to occupy, to take up".

> احتل فريقنا المركز الأول.
>
> *iḥtalla farīqunā al-markaz al-awwal.*
>
> Our team took first place.

Present: يحتل, *yaḥtall*.

Of armies: to occupy a land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتل** (*iḥtalla*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-l-l* (Lane), "to untie; to alight in a place". It is Form VIII, "to take up a place".
