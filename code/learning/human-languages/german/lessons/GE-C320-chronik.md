## You'll want to know: die Chronik
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C320-MITTELALTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Chronik** — "the chronicle".

> In der Chronik der Stadt steht das Jahr des Feuers.
>
> *The year of the fire is in the town chronicle.*

Plural: *die Chroniken*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Chronik* comes from Latin *chronica*, from Greek *khronikos* "of or about time", built on *khronos* "time" (EtymDB).
