## You'll want to know: ざせき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C203-FLY203-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ざせき** — *zaseki* — "a seat".

> **ざせきは まどの ちかくです。** — *zaseki wa mado no chikaku desu* — "The seat is near the window."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ざせき** (*zaseki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *za*, "to sit" (Middle Chinese *dzwaH*) and *seki*, "mat, seat" (*zjek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
