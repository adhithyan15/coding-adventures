## You'll want to know: वर्णयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C289-SAKSHYA289-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्णयति** — *varṇayati* — "describes".

> साक्षी चौरस्य मुखं वर्णयति।
>
> *sākṣī caurasya mukhaṃ varṇayati.*
>
> The witness describes the thief's face.

Past (he …): सः मुखं वर्णितवान्, *saḥ mukhaṃ varṇitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्णयति** (*varṇayati*) is built on *varṇa*, "colour, outward appearance"; MW treats *varṇayati* as a verb made from that noun, to paint and to describe (MW).

One more word for telling what happened.
