## You'll want to know: a alternativa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C371-IMPREVISTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a alternativa** — "the alternative, the other option".

> Não temos alternativa: vamos de táxi.
>
> *We have no alternative: we are going by taxi.*

*Em alternativa* — alternatively.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alternativa* is built on *alternativo* (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *alternātīvus*, on *alternus* "one after the other", from *alter* "the other" (Lewis & Short).
