## You'll want to know: गोळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C254-VAID254-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गोळी** — *goḷī* — "a pill; a tablet".

> म्हैं दिनूगै गोळी खाई।
>
> *mhaĩ dinūgai goḷī khāī.*
>
> I took a pill early in the morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोळी** (*goḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gola*, "a ball". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati *goḷī*, "pill" (CDIAL 4321).
