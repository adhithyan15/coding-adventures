Arabic, chapter 238: Like, Completely, Of course, Almost, Especially.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مثل (mithl) — like, such as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for morals, then the Arabic for personality.

You'll want to know: مثل.
مثل — mithl — "like, such as".

The word, taken apart.
مثل (mithl) is built on the native Arabic root m-th-l, "to be like" (Lane). Mithl is "a like, a match".
