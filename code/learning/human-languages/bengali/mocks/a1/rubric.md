# Bengali A1 mock rubric

Score either parallel form against [task-shapes/a1.json](../../task-shapes/a1.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are four separate 100-point papers.
The learner must reach 60/100 on every skill in the same timed administration;
240/400 overall cannot compensate for a skill below 60. Every required part
must be attempted. This is a project-defined equivalent, not an external
Bengali qualification.

Print each skill as a separate learner sheet. Collect Reading and Listening
before issuing Writing, because their Bengali text could reveal answer models.
Keep the examiner scripts, answer keys, and any productive-task examples out of
learner view. Support-language directions may explain procedure at A1, not
translate the answer-bearing Bengali source.

## Reading — 100 points

- Signs and forms: 35 points across seven selections.
- Short messages: 35 points across seven selections.
- Personal descriptions: 30 points across six selections.

Within each part, multiply the number correct by the part points divided by
its item count; sum the parts and round the paper score to one decimal. An
unanswered item earns zero. Award credit for the selected meaning or detail,
not for a copied Bengali word or an examiner hint.

## Listening — 100 points

- Announcements: 35 points across six selections.
- Short exchanges: 35 points across six selections.
- Personal account: 30 points across five selections.

Use the same part-weight formula as Reading. Read or play each private script
twice, targeting 100 words per minute within the 90–110 band. The first-pass
source lengths correspond to 60–90, 90–120, and 120–180 seconds respectively;
the second pass and short answer pauses fit the 18-minute paper. Do not show a
transcript, answer-bearing written label, translation, or romanization. A
technical restart replaces the affected recording; it is not a learner-chosen
third replay.

## Writing — 100 points

- Practical form: 40 points for seven independently completed fields; six
  correct fields still constitute a complete attempt, while an empty field
  earns no task credit.
- Named-reader message: 60 points for an independently composed 30–40 word
  Bengali message with a clear reader, purpose, place, and time.

For each task, score four equally weighted dimensions from 0 to 5: task
fulfilment and relevant content; comprehensibility and organisation; Bengali
vocabulary and grammar; and Bengali orthographic control. Scale the 20-point
dimension sum to the task maximum. For orthography assess the matra line,
recognizable letter order, vowel-sign side, hasanta placement when the chosen
word requires it, and legibility. Do not require ornamental typeface shapes.
Accept consistent standard Bengali spelling and safe invented personal data.
A romanized, traced, visibly copied, or non-Bengali-language response is not
independent Bengali writing evidence. A support-language instruction is not a
copyable Bengali answer model.

## Speaking — 100 points

- Personal interview: 30 points across five questions.
- Prepared one-minute description: 30 points after five minutes of preparation.
- Transactional role-play: 40 points for greeting, request, price question,
  and polite closing.

For each part, score four equally weighted dimensions from 0 to 5: task
fulfilment and relevant content; comprehensibility, organisation, and
interaction; Bengali vocabulary and grammar; and pronunciation and fluency.
Scale the 20-point dimension sum to the part maximum. Allow one slow repeat or
natural repair without penalty. Do not provide a written answer script or
coach the response. Accept a chosen identity rather than forcing a model name.

## Readiness boundary

The pair, keys, and rubric make the A1 target administrable; they do not prove
that the book has taught the controlled writing ramp or that a book-only learner
can pass. No book-only readiness claim follows from artifact tests. Human
validation still requires clean timed administrations, qualified Bengali
review of script variety and writing, audio review, scoring calibration, and
the planned book-only learner study.
