## You'll want to know: imber, imbris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C485-IMBER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**imber, imbris** — "a downpour, a heavy shower of rain".

> Subitō imber magnus cadit, et viātōrēs ad caupōnam currunt.
>
> *Suddenly a heavy downpour falls, and the travellers run to the inn.*

Third declension, masculine: *imber, imbris m.*, genitive plural *imbrium*. Heavier than *pluvia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imber* is inherited; Lewis & Short list Sanskrit *abhra* 'cloud', Greek *ombros* 'rain' and Latin *umbra* 'shade' as kin. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
