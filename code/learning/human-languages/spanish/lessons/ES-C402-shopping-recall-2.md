# shopping recall two — choose, size, and price

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COSTAR, ES-LEX-CAMISA, ES-LEX-CAMISETA, ES-LEX-TALLA, ES-LEX-GORRO] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Without looking back, name the four shop nouns. (*La camisa, la
camiseta, la talla, el gorro*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COSTAR, ES-LEX-CAMISA, ES-LEX-CAMISETA, ES-LEX-TALLA, ES-LEX-GORRO] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *Busco una camiseta.*]
- [YOU SAY: *¿Qué talla?*]
- [YOU SAY: *También busco un gorro.*]
- [YOU SAY: *¿Cuánto cuesta?*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COSTAR, ES-LEX-CAMISA, ES-LEX-CAMISETA, ES-LEX-TALLA, ES-LEX-GORRO] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Build a two-question shop exchange using a garment, its size, and
its price. (*Busco una camiseta. ¿Qué talla? ¿Cuánto cuesta?*)
