Persian, chapter 495: Inside the Body.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
عضله ('azole) — a muscle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for health, then the Persian for to get better.

You'll want to know: عضله.
عضله ('azole) — "a muscle".
بعد از کوهنوردی عضله ('azole) های پایم درد میکند. ba'd az kuhnavardi 'azolehâ-ye pâyam dard mikonad. After hiking my leg muscles ache.
Plural 'azolehâ, or the Arabic 'azolât in formal Persian.

The word, taken apart.
'azole was borrowed from Arabic ʿaḍala, 'muscle' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a muscle, then the Persian for a lung.

You'll want to know: کبد.
کبد (kabed) — "the liver".
دکتر گفت کبد او سالم است. doktor goft kabed-e u sâlem ast. The doctor said his liver is healthy.
Medical. Everyday Persian also says jegar, which is also the liver in kebab.

The word, taken apart.
kabed is a loan, usually traced to Arabic kabid, 'liver'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 5 of 6.
معده (me'de) — the stomach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the liver, then the Persian for a kidney.

You'll want to know: معده.
معده (me'de) — "the stomach".
بعد از غذای تند معده درد دارم. ba'd az ghazâ-ye tond me'de dard dâram. After spicy food I get a stomach ache.
shekam is the belly seen from outside; me'de is the organ. me'de dard — stomach ache.

The word, taken apart.
me'de is a loan, usually traced to Arabic maʿida, 'stomach'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
