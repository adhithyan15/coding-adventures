## You'll want to know: umgraben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C504-SAEEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**umgraben** — "to dig over".

> Im Frühling graben wir das Beet um.
>
> *In spring we dig over the flower bed.*

Separable: *er gräbt um*, *hat umgegraben*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*umgraben* is built on *um* "around, over" and *graben* "to dig", inherited from Old High German *graban*, from Proto-Germanic *grabaną* (EtymDB).
