## You'll want to know: சர்க்கரை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C59-KITCHEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சர்க்கரை** (*sarkkarai*) — "sugar".

The refined white kind, beside வெல்லம் rather than in place of it. Kannada ಸಕ್ಕರೆ (*sakkare*), Telugu చక్కెర (*cakkera*): one word, lightly redressed three times.

It has travelled further than any other word in this run. Sanskrit *śarkarā* went out through Arabic, Greek and Latin and came back into English as *sugar*, so சர்க்கரை and the English word are one word wearing different clothes.
