## You'll want to know: aggraver
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C353-REMEDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aggraver** — "to make worse, to aggravate".

> Le froid a aggravé sa toux.
>
> *The cold made his cough worse.*

*S'aggraver* — to get worse: *la situation s'aggrave*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aggraver* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aggravāre* "to make heavier" (EtymDB), built on *gravis* "heavy" (Lewis & Short).
