Lesson 1 of 6.
चित्रकला (citrakalā) — painting as an art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tent, then the Marathi for a campfire.

You'll want to know: चित्रकला.
चित्रकला — citrakalā — "painting as an art".
मीराला लहानपणापासून चित्रकलेची आवड आहे. mīrālā lahānpaṇāpāsūn citrakalecī āvaḍ āhe. Meera has loved painting since childhood.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
चित्रकला (citrakalā) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit citra, "picture", and kalā, "art" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 6.
कुंचला (kuñclā) — a paintbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an artist, then the Marathi for a sculpture.

You'll want to know: कुंचला.
कुंचला — kuñclā — "a paintbrush".
कलाकाराने बारीक कुंचल्याने डोळे रंगवले. kalākārāne bārīk kuñclyāne ḍoḷe raṅgavle. The artist painted the eyes with a fine brush.
Masculine.
The oblique is कुंचल्या-: कुंचल्याने.

The word, taken apart.
कुंचला (kuñclā) probably belongs with Marathi kũcā and kũcī, "brush", which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit kūrca, "a bunch of grass", whose derivatives meant a painter's brush (CDIAL 3408). CDIAL lists no कुंचला itself, so the -ला form is not settled.
