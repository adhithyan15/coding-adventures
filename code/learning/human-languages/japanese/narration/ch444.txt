Japanese, chapter 444: Breathing and Pulse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
こきゅうする (kokyū suru) — to breathe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for lips, then the Japanese for an eyelid.

You'll want to know: こきゅうする.
こきゅうする — kokyū suru — "to breathe".
ゆっくり こきゅうして ください。 — yukkuri kokyū shite kudasai — "Please breathe slowly."
Polite こきゅうします, kokyūshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): かんじゃが ゆっくり こきゅうしました。, kanja ga yukkuri kokyū shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
こきゅうする (kokyū suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) kokyū with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ko, "call" (Middle Chinese xu) and kyū, "inhale" (xip) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
