## You'll want to know: しゅっぱつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C199-DEPART199-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっぱつ** — *shuppatsu* — "departure".

> **しゅっぱつは ごごです。** — *shuppatsu wa gogo desu* — "Departure is in the afternoon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっぱつ** (*shuppatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shutsu*, "to go out" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwit*) and *hatsu*, "to set off" (*pjot*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *shup-patsu*.
