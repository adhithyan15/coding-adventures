Kannada, chapter 452: Moving Goods.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಾರಿಗೆ (sārige) — transport; public transport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a motorbike, then the Kannada for a bullock cart.

You'll want to know: ಸಾರಿಗೆ.
ಸಾರಿಗೆ — sārige — "transport; public transport".
ಈ ಊರಿಗೆ ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಸಾರಿಗೆ ಬಸ್ ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ಎರಡು ಸಲ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. ī ūrige sarkāri sārige bas dinakke eraḍu sala baruttade. A government transport bus comes to this town twice a day.
ಸಾರಿಗೆ (sārige) ಇಲಾಖೆ, the transport department.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾರಿಗೆ (sārige) is a Kannada word for transport whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸರಕು (saraku) — goods; cargo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for transport, then the Kannada for a depot.

You'll want to know: ಸರಕು.
ಸರಕು — saraku — "goods; cargo".
ಲಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸರಕು ತುಂಬಿದೆ. lāriyalli saraku tumbide. The lorry is full of goods.
ಸರಕು (saraku) ರೈಲು, a goods train.

The word, taken apart.
ಸರಕು (saraku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2353 lists Kannada saraku, sarku, "goods, things, merchandise, cargo".

You'll want to know: ಸಾಗಿಸು.
ಸಾಗಿಸು — sāgisu — "to transport; to carry off".
ರೈತರು ತಮ್ಮ ಭತ್ತವನ್ನು ಲಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಾಗಿಸಿದರು. raitaru tamma bhattavannu lāriyalli sāgisidaru. The farmers carried their paddy away by lorry.
Past (I …): ಸಾಗಿಸಿದೆ, sāgiside.
ಸಾಗು, to move along, with -ಇಸು: to make go.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಗಿಸು (sāgisu) is native Dravidian: the causative of sāgu, "to go, to move forward, to proceed" (DEDR 2430).
