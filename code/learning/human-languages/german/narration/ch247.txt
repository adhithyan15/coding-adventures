Lesson 1 of 5.
bestehen — to pass (an exam), to insist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to report, then the German for to visit (sights).

You'll want to know: bestehen.
bestehen — "to pass (an exam), to insist".

The word, taken apart.
bestehen from Middle High German bestān (EtymDB 2.1): be- + stehen, "to stand". To pass is to stand the test; to insist is to stand firm.
