## The word, taken apart: Roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C05-MATANADU-01, KA-LEX-C05-IRU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C05-IRU-02, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C05-MAADU-02, KA-LEX-C05-KANNADA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *mātanāḍu* "to speak" ← *mātu* "word"
- *iru* "to be / stay / live"; *-alli* "in" attaches to the noun's end
- *māḍu* "to do" → *kelasa māḍu* "to work" (the twin of *karnā* / *sey* / *cēyu*)
- *kannaḍa* ← *kar-nāḍu*, "the high/black land" (→ *Karnataka*)
