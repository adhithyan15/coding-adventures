# மற்றது (maṟṟadu) — the other one

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-READ-EPPOTHU-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C79-WHEN-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **எப்போது**. Then say: when you come, tell me.

## You'll want to know: மற்றது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C80-OTHER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**மற்றது** (*maṟṟadu*) — "the other one".

இது and அது name a thing by where it stands: near me, over there. மற்றது names it by what it
is not — the one that is *left*, once you have picked one up.

> **இது வேண்டும். மற்றது வேண்டாம்.**

> *idu vēṇḍum. maṟṟadu vēṇḍām.* — "I want this one. Not the other."

The tail of the word is **அது**, the same *that one* that ends அதனால். Tamil builds a
surprising amount out of that small word, and once you hear it inside a longer one you can
usually guess what the longer one is doing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A third way to point at a thing, once two of them are on the table.
