German, chapter 528: Müll und Unordnung.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Unordnung — mess, disorder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to iron, then the German for to fold.

You'll want to know: die Unordnung.
die Unordnung — "mess, disorder".
In seinem Zimmer herrscht immer Unordnung. His room is forever in a mess.
No plural.

The word, taken apart.
Unordnung is built on un- "not" and Ordnung "order", inherited from Old High German ordinunga (EtymDB), built on a verb borrowed from Latin ōrdināre, from ōrdō "row, order".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: entsorgen.

The word, taken apart.
entsorgen is built on ent- "away" and Sorge "care, worry", inherited from Old High German sorga, from Proto-Germanic surgō (EtymDB): to take away a worry.
