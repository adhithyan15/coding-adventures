## You'll want to know: la serie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C761-SERIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la serie** — "the series (TV)".

> Estoy viendo una serie muy buena.
>
> *I am watching a really good series.*

Same form in the plural: *las series*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*serie* comes from Latin *seriēs* "a row, a chain" (EtymDB), from *serere* "to join together".
