## You'll want to know: abwägen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C336-EINIGUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**abwägen** — "to weigh up".

> Sie wägt die Vorteile und Nachteile genau ab.
>
> *She weighs up the advantages and disadvantages carefully.*

Separable: *sie wägt ab*. Perfekt: *sie hat abgewogen* (rarely *abgewägt*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abwägen* is built on *wägen* "to weigh", an older form beside *wiegen*, from Proto-Germanic *weganą* "to move, to carry, to weigh"; EtymDB lists English *weigh* and Dutch *wegen* as its cousins.
