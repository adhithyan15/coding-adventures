## You'll want to know: ਰਬੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C357-SCHOOL357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਬੜ** — *rabaṛ* — "an eraser; rubber".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਇੱਕ ਨਵਾਂ ਰਬੜ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere kol ikk navāṁ rabaṛ hai.*
>
> I have a new eraser.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਬੜ** (*rabaṛ*) is borrowed from English *rubber*.
