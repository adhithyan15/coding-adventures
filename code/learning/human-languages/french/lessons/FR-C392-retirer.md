## You'll want to know: retirer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C392-BANQUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**retirer** — "to take out, to withdraw".

> J'ai retiré cinquante euros au distributeur.
>
> *I took fifty euros out of the cash machine.*

Also to take off: *retirer son manteau*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*retirer* is built from *re-* "back" and *tirer* "to pull" (EtymDB). The origin of *tirer* is not settled.
