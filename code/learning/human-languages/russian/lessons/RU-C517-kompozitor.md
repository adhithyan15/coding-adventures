## You'll want to know: композитор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C517-ORCHESTRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**композитор** (*kompozítor*) — "a composer".

> Этот композитор пишет музыку для кино.
>
> *Étot kompozítor píshet múzyku dlya kinó.*
>
> This composer writes film music.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*композитор* was borrowed from Latin *compositor* 'an arranger' (EtymDB), from *compōnere* 'to put together' (Lewis & Short).
