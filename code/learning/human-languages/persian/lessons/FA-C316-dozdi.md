## You'll want to know: دزدی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C316-DOZD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دزدی** (*dozdi*) — "a theft, stealing".

> دزدی ساعت دو شب اتفاق افتاد.
>
> *Dozdi sâ'at-e do-ye shab ettefâq oftâd.*
>
> The theft happened at two in the morning.

*dozd* "thief" + *-i*. In speech *dozdi kardan* is "to steal".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dozdi* adds the native noun suffix *-i* to *dozd* "thief", a word usually traced to Middle Persian *duzd*.
