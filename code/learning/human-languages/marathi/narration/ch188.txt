Lesson 1 of 7.
दिनदर्शिका (dindarśikā) — a calendar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a piece of jewellery, then the Marathi for a bangle.

You'll want to know: दिनदर्शिका.
दिनदर्शिका — dindarśikā — "a calendar".

The word, taken apart.
दिनदर्शिका (dindarśikā) is a modern compound of Sanskrit parts: dina, "day", and darśikā, "one that shows", from dṛś, "to see".

You'll want to know: गरुड.
गरुड — garuḍ — "an eagle".

The word, taken apart.
गरुड (garuḍ) is Sanskrit garuḍa, "a mythical bird", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4041). Marathi uses the name of Vishnu's bird for the eagle.

कोकिळा — kokiḷā — "a cuckoo".

The word, taken apart.
कोकिळा (kokiḷā) is Sanskrit kokilā, "the Indian cuckoo", with Marathi ळ. CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi forms as koīḷ and koyāḷ (CDIAL 3483).

मधमाशी — madhmāśī — "a bee".

The word, taken apart.
मधमाशी (madhmāśī) is "honey-fly": madh, "honey", from Sanskrit madhu (CDIAL 9784), and māśī, "fly", from Sanskrit makṣikā, "fly, bee" (CDIAL 9696).
