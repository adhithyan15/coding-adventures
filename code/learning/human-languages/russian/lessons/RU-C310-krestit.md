## You'll want to know: крестить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C310-YUBILEY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**крестить** (*krestít*) — "to christen, to baptize".

> Ребёнка крестили в мае.
>
> *Rebyónka krestíli v máye.*
>
> The baby was christened in May.

Both aspects; also perfective *окрестить* (*okrestít*). Present *крещу*, *крестишь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*крестить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *krĭstiti*, built on *krĭstŭ* "baptism" (EtymDB), a word that goes back to Greek *Khristós*.
