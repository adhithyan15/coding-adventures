## You'll want to know: મૂંગું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C475-SENSES475-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂંગું** — *mū̃gũ* — "silent; unable to speak".

> બીકથી બાળક મૂંગું થઈ ગયું.
>
> *bīkthī bāḷak mū̃gũ thaī gayũ.*
>
> The child went silent with fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂંગું** (*mū̃gũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mūka*, "unable to speak"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 10231).
