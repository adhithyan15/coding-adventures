## You'll want to know: ટાયર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C401-TYRE401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાયર** — *ṭāyar* — "a tyre".

> ગાડીના ટાયરમાં હવા ઓછી છે.
>
> *gāḍīnā ṭāyarmā̃ havā ochī che.*
>
> There is too little air in the car's tyre.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાયર** (*ṭāyar*) is borrowed from English *tyre*.
