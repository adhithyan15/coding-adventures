## You'll want to know: प्रयत्न
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C540-KOSHISH540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयत्न** — *prayatn* — "an effort, an endeavour (formal)".

> बहुत प्रयत्न के बाद वह पहाड़ पर पहुँचा।
>
> *Bahut prayatn ke bād vah pahāṛ par pahũcā.*
>
> After a great effort he reached the top of the mountain.

Masculine.

A formal word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयत्न** (*prayatn*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *prayatna*, "persevering effort" (Monier-Williams).
