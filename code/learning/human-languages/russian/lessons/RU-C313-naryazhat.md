## You'll want to know: наряжать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C313-YOLKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**наряжать** (*naryazhát*) — "to dress up; to trim (a tree)".

> В субботу мы нарядили ёлку.
>
> *V subbótu my naryadíli yólku.*
>
> On Saturday we trimmed the tree.

Imperfective; perfective *нарядить* (*naryadít*), *наряжу*. *Наряжаться* — to dress up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наряжать* is usually traced to *на-* and *ряд* "a row, order": to put in order, to array. EtymDB lists no parent.
