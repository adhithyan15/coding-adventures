## You'll want to know: અવાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C249-PHONE249-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અવાજ** — *avāj* — "voice, sound".

> **ફોનમાં મારી માતાનો અવાજ આવે છે.** — *phonmā̃ mārī mātāno avāj āve che* — "My mother's voice comes through the phone."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવાજ** (*avāj*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *āvāz*, "voice, sound". CDIAL has no entry for it.
