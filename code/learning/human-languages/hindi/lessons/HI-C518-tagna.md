## You'll want to know: टाँगना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C518-TAAR518-05]; assesses=[] -->

**टाँगना** — *ṭā̃gnā* — "to hang up".

> मीरा ने अपना छाता खूँटी पर टाँगा।
>
> *Mīrā ne apnā chātā khū̃ṭī par ṭā̃gā.*
>
> Mira hung her umbrella on the peg.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कोट टाँगा, *usne koṭ ṭā̃gā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टाँगना** (*ṭā̃gnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*ṭaṅg*, "hang", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5436).
