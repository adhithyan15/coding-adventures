Persian, chapter 496: Brain, Nerves and Eyes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مغز (maghz) — the brain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a kidney, then the Persian for the stomach.

You'll want to know: مغز.
مغز (maghz) — "the brain".
مغز در خواب هم کار میکند. maghz dar khâb ham kâr mikonad. The brain works even during sleep.
Also the kernel of a nut: maghz-e gerdu 'a walnut kernel'.

The word, taken apart.
maghz is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian, where the word meant 'brain, marrow' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
اعصاب (a'sâb) — nerves.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the stomach, then the Persian for the brain.

You'll want to know: اعصاب.
اعصاب (a'sâb) — "nerves".
صدای بلند روی اعصاب من است. sedâ-ye boland ru-ye a'sâb-e man ast. Loud noise gets on my nerves.
A plural noun. ru-ye a'sâb budan — to get on someone's nerves.

The word, taken apart.
a'sâb is a loan, usually traced to Arabic aʿṣāb, the plural of ʿaṣab, 'nerve, sinew'. The same 'asab is inside 'asabâni. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Plural rag-hâ. Also a streak or vein in stone or wood.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پلک (pelk) — an eyelid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for nerves, then the Persian for a vein.

You'll want to know: پلک.
پلک (pelk) — "an eyelid".
او بدون پلک زدن به من نگاه کرد. u bedun-e pelk zadan be man negâh kard. She looked at me without blinking.
pelk zadan — to blink.

The word, taken apart.
pelk is usually taken as a native Persian word, but EtymDB lists it without any source, and its origin is not settled.

The word, taken apart.
