Gujarati, chapter 469: Habits and Quirks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વિચિત્ર (vicitra) — strange; odd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for boasting, then the Gujarati for to beguile.

You'll want to know: વિચિત્ર.
વિચિત્ર — vicitra — "strange; odd".
મારા કાકાને વિચિત્ર ટેવ છે. mārā kākāne vicitra ṭev che. My uncle has an odd habit.

The word, taken apart.
વિચિત્ર (vicitra) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vicitra, "variegated, many-coloured", as Monier-Williams records it; Gujarati uses it for anything out of the ordinary.

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to beguile, then the Gujarati for odd.

You'll want to know: તરંગી.
તરંગી — tarangī — "whimsical".
તરંગી મામા રોજ નવી યોજના બનાવે છે. tarangī māmā roj navī yojnā banāve che. My whimsical uncle makes a new plan every day.

The word, taken apart.
તરંગી (tarangī) is built on taraṅg, "a wave", from Sanskrit taraṃga, "a wave, a billow" (Monier-Williams), with -ī: a mind that rises and falls like waves.
