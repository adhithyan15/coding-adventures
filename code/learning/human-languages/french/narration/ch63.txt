Lesson 1 of 5.
nettoyer — to clean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to use, then the French for to show.

You'll want to know: nettoyer.
nettoyer — "to clean".

The word, taken apart.
nettoyer from Old French nettoier, built on the adjective for "clean, neat", which goes back to Latin nitidus, "shining". (EtymDB 2.1).
