## You'll want to know: قیاس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C362-QIYAS362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قیاس** — *qiyās* — "a guess; conjecture".

> اگر میرا قیاس ٹھیک ہے تو وہ کل آئے گا۔
>
> *agar merā qiyās ṭhīk hai to voh kal āe gā.*
>
> If my guess is right, he will come tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قیاس** (*qiyās*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qiyās*, "measuring one thing by another".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
