## You'll want to know: sincère
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C468-FRANCHISE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**sincère** — "sincere, honest".

> Elle est toujours sincère avec ses amis.
>
> *She is always honest with her friends.*

The noun is *la sincérité*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sincère* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sincērus* "pure, clean, whole" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
