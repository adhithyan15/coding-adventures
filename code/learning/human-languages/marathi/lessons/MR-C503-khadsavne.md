## You'll want to know: खडसावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C503-MAPH503-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खडसावणे** — *khaḍsāvṇe* — "to rebuke".

> उशिरा आल्याबद्दल शिक्षकांनी त्याला खडसावलं.
>
> *uśirā ālyābaddal śikṣakānnī tyālā khaḍsāvlaṁ.*
>
> The teacher rebuked him for coming late.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मुलाला खडसावलं, *tyāne mulālā khaḍsāvlaṁ*.

Sharper than रागावणे; the one rebuked takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खडसावणे** (*khaḍsāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
