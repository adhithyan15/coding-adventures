## You'll want to know: adottare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C495-FAMIGLIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**adottare** — "to adopt".

> Hanno adottato una bambina di due anni.
>
> *They adopted a two-year-old girl.*

Also for ideas: *adottare un metodo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adottare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adoptāre* "to choose, to adopt" (Lewis & Short), from *ad-* and *optāre* "to choose".
