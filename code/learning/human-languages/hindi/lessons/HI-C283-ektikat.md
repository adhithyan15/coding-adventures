# शहर के लिए दो टिकट दीजिए (śahar ke liye do ṭikaṭ dījie) — at the ticket window

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C69-GOING-04, HI-LEX-C205-THG205-03, HI-LEX-C283-AGLA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have asked for a ticket since you first learned the word: **एक टिकट दीजिए**. Say it, then "the next train". (*aglī ṭren*.) This lesson buys the right ticket for the right train.

## You'll want to know: buying a ticket
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C283-BUY-TICKET-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C69-GOING-04, HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-03, HI-LEX-C84-POSS-06, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-04, HI-LEX-C72-COST-02, HI-LEX-C72-HUNDRED-01] -->

> **शहर के लिए दो टिकट दीजिए।**
>
> *śahar ke liye do ṭikaṭ dījie.* — "Two tickets for the city, please."

The place you are going takes **के लिए**, "for". **टिकट** is masculine in standard Hindi, though you will hear it made feminine too, and it does not change for two.

- **एक टिकट कितने का है?** (*ek ṭikaṭ kitne kā hai?*) — How much is one ticket?
- **सौ रुपये का है।** (*sau rupaye kā hai.*) — It's a hundred rupees.

In **कितने का**, "of how much", the **का** agrees with the ticket.

## You'll want to know: when it leaves, and how long
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C283-WHEN-LEAVES-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C283-AGLA-01, HI-LEX-C88-TIME-01, HI-CONCEPT-C18-GHANTA-01, HI-LEX-C166-READ166-03, HI-CONCEPT-C04-CHALTAHUN-01, HI-LEX-C280-LAGNA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

> **अगली ट्रेन कितने बजे चलेगी?**
>
> *aglī ṭren kitne baje calegī?* — "What time does the next train leave?"

A train **चलती है**, "moves off", and **चलेगी** is its future. "At what time" is **कितने बजे**, and the answer is a time you know: **पाँच बजे**.

How long will it take? **लगना** again, with the time as the thing:

- **कितना समय लगेगा?** (*kitnā samay lagegā?*) — How long will it take?
- **दो घंटे लगेंगे।** (*do ghaṇṭe lageṅge.*) — It will take two hours.

**लगेगा** follows **समय**; **लगेंगे** follows **दो घंटे**.
