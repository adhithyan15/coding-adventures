## You'll want to know: चिडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C453-TAPAT453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चिडणे** — *ciḍṇe* — "to get irritated".

> भाऊ खूप प्रश्न विचारतो, म्हणून ती चिडते.
>
> *bhāū khūp praśna vicārto, mhaṇūn tī ciḍte.*
>
> Her brother asks a lot of questions, so she gets irritated.

Past (he …): तो चिडला, *to ciḍlā*.

The person one is cross with takes -वर: ती माझ्यावर चिडली. चिडवणे, to tease.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिडणे** (*ciḍṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from the reconstructed root *\*ciḍ*, "be angry"; CDIAL lists Marathi *ciḍṇẽ*, "to be provoked" (CDIAL 4794).
