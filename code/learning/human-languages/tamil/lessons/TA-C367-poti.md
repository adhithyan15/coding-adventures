## You'll want to know: போதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C367-NANAM367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**போதி** — *pōti* — "to teach formally; to preach".

> அந்த ஆசிரியர் மாணவர்களுக்கு நேர்மையைப் போதித்தார்.
>
> *anta āciriyar māṇavarkaḷukku nērmaiyaip pōtittār.*
>
> That teacher taught the students honesty.

Past (I …): போதித்தேன், *pōtittēṉ*.

A formal, often moral teach: the person taught takes -க்கு, the lesson -ஐ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**போதி** (*pōti*) is a verb built on Sanskrit *bodha*, "waking, understanding" (CDIAL 9314), from the root *budh*, "to wake, to know": to make someone understand.
