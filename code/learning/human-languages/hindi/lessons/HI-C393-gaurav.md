## You'll want to know: गौरव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C393-SAFALTA393-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरव** — *gaurav* — "pride, a matter of honour".

> अगर तुम जीतोगे तो यह पूरे गाँव के लिए गौरव की बात होगी।
>
> *agar tum jītoge to yah pūre gā̃v ke liye gaurav kī bāt hogī.*
>
> If you win, it will be a matter of pride for the whole village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरव** (*gaurav*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *gaurava*, "weight; importance, dignity" (Monier-Williams).
