Marathi, chapter 499: A New Baby.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गरोदर (garodar) — pregnant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for age, then the Marathi for a teenager.

You'll want to know: गरोदर.
गरोदर — garodar — "pregnant".
माझी मावशी गरोदर आहे. mājhī māvśī garodar āhe. My aunt is pregnant.
Unchanging; the formal word is गर्भवती.

The word, taken apart.
गरोदर (garodar) is usually traced to two Sanskrit words, garbha, "womb", and udara, "belly".

बाळंतपण — bāḷantpaṇ — "childbirth; confinement".
मुलीचं बाळंतपण आईच्या घरी झालं. mulīcaṁ bāḷantpaṇ āīcyā gharī jhālaṁ. The daughter gave birth at her mother's house.
Neuter.
बाळंत, a woman who has recently given birth, + -पण.

The word, taken apart.
बाळंतपण (bāḷantpaṇ) is a Marathi derivation: बाळंत, a woman who has recently given birth, with -paṇ; the origin of बाळंत is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
तान्हा (tānhā) — newborn; infant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for pregnant, then the Marathi for childbirth.

You'll want to know: तान्हा.
तान्हा — tānhā — "newborn; infant".
तान्हं बाळ दिवसभर झोपतं. tānhaṁ bāḷ divasbhar jhoptaṁ. A newborn baby sleeps all day.
Agrees: तान्हं बाळ, a newborn baby.

The word, taken apart.
तान्हा (tānhā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi tānhā, "sucking, of a baby or young animal", under Sanskrit tṛṣṇaka, "thirsty" (CDIAL 5940).

आमचं बाळ आता रांगायला लागलं. āmcaṁ bāḷ ātā rāṅgāylā lāglaṁ. Our baby has started crawling now.
Past (he …): तो रांगला, to rāṅglā.

The word, taken apart.
रांगणे (rāṅgṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पाजणे (pājṇe) — to give to drink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for newborn, then the Marathi for to crawl.

You'll want to know: पाजणे.
पाजणे — pājṇe — "to give to drink".
आईने बाळाला दूध पाजलं. āīne bāḷālā dūdh pājlaṁ. The mother fed the baby milk.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाळाला दूध पाजलं, tyāne bāḷālā dūdh pājlaṁ.
The one given the drink takes -ला: बाळाला पाजणे (pājṇe).

The word, taken apart.
पाजणे (pājṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit pāyayati, "gives to drink"; CDIAL lists Marathi pājṇẽ (CDIAL 8098).
