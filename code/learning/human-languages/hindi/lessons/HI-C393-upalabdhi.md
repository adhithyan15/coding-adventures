## You'll want to know: उपलब्धि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C393-SAFALTA393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपलब्धि** — *upalabdhi* — "an achievement".

> अगर गाँव में बिजली आई तो यह हमारी सबसे बड़ी उपलब्धि होगी।
>
> *agar gā̃v meṁ bijlī āī to yah hamārī sabse baṛī upalabdhi hogī.*
>
> If electricity reaches the village, it will be our greatest achievement.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपलब्धि** (*upalabdhi*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *upalabdhi*, "obtaining, perceiving" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
