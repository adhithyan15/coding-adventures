## You'll want to know: l'invito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C318-RICEVIMENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'invito** — "the invitation".

> Grazie per l'invito!
>
> *Thank you for the invitation!*

Built on *invitare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invito* is built on *invitare* "to invite", from Latin *invītāre* (Lewis & Short).
