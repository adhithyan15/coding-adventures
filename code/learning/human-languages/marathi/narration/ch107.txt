Marathi, chapter 107: True, Green, Yellow, Red, Blue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खरे (khare) — true.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for thin, then the Marathi for light.

You'll want to know: खरे.
खरे — khare — "true".

The word, taken apart.
खरे (khare) is a form of Marathi kharā, "pure, good, firm", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit khara, "hard, sharp; solid" (CDIAL 3819).

Lesson 2 of 5.
हिरवा (hirvā) — green.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for light, then the Marathi for true.

You'll want to know: हिरवा.
हिरवा — hirvā — "green".

The word, taken apart.
हिरवा (hirvā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from hiru, "yellow, green", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 14114). CDIAL builds it on Sanskrit hari, "yellow, green".

Lesson 4 of 5.
लाल (lāl) — red.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for green, then the Marathi for yellow.

You'll want to know: लाल.
लाल — lāl — "red".

The word, taken apart.
लाल (lāl) is a loan, usually traced to Persian lāl, "red". The Persian word is usually tied to laʿl, "a ruby".

Lesson 5 of 5.
निळा (niḷā) — blue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for yellow, then the Marathi for red.

You'll want to know: निळा.
निळा — niḷā — "blue".

The word, taken apart.
निळा (niḷā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nīla, "dark blue, dark green, black" (CDIAL 7563). CDIAL lists Marathi nīḷ, "blue".
