Spanish, chapter 662: Cambios y cancelaciones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el localizador — the booking reference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the stay, then the Spanish for the concierge.

You'll want to know: el localizador.
el localizador — "the booking reference".
Dígame el localizador de su billete, por favor. Tell me the booking reference for your ticket, please.
A short code of letters and numbers.

The word, taken apart.
localizador is built on localizar "to locate", from local, a learned borrowing from Late Latin locālis (EtymDB), from locus "place".

Lesson 2 of 5.
modificar — to change, to modify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the concierge, then the Spanish for the booking reference.

You'll want to know: modificar.
modificar — "to change, to modify".
¿Puedo modificar la fecha de la reserva? Can I change the date of the booking?
The c becomes qu before e: modifiqué. Formal, common on booking sites.

The word, taken apart.
modificar is a learned borrowing from Latin modificāre "to set limits to, to regulate" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built from modus "measure" and facere "to make".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la penalización — the penalty, the charge (for cancelling).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to change, then the Spanish for the confirmation.

You'll want to know: la penalización.
la penalización — "the penalty, the charge (for cancelling)".
Si cancela hoy, no hay penalización. If you cancel today, there is no charge.
Booking terms often say sin penalización — free of charge.

The word, taken apart.
penalización is built on penalizar "to penalise", from penal, a learned borrowing from Latin poenālis, built on poena "penalty, punishment".
