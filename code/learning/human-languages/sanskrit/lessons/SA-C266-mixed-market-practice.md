No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.

[PAUSE 12s] You are speaking to a shopkeeper at a market. Choose one familiar
item. Say that you buy it. Ask the price. The shopkeeper says that the price is
twenty rupees. Request the item politely, then close with thanks.

One complete answer with fruit is **अहं फलं क्रीणामि। मूल्यं कियत्?
विंशतिः रूप्यकाणि। कृपया मह्यं फलं ददातु। धन्यवादः।** Your item may differ;
the five jobs must remain.
