## You'll want to know: वचनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C535-SATYASANDHA535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वचनम्** — *vacanam* — "one's word; a statement".

> यदि भवान् वचनं ददाति, तर्हि तत् प्रतिपालयतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān vacanaṃ dadāti, tarhi tat pratipālayatu.*
>
> If you give your word, keep to it.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वचनम्** (*vacanam*) comes from the root √vac, "to speak", with the ending *-ana*; MW gives *vacana* as an utterance, a statement, a word (MW).
