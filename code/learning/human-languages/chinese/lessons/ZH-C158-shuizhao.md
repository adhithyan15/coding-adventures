## You'll want to know: shuìzháo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C158-WAKE158-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shuìzháo* — "to fall asleep".

> *wǒ zuótiān wǎnshang shí'èr diǎn shuìzháo le* — Last night I fell asleep at twelve.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuìzháo* is built from *shuì* 'to sleep' and *zháo*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyweH* and *drjak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'to attach to': sleep takes hold.
