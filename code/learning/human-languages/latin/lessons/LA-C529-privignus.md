## You'll want to know: prīvignus, prīvignī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C529-NOVE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**prīvignus, prīvignī** — "a stepson".

> Prīvignus novercae epistulam scrībit.
>
> *The stepson writes a letter to his stepmother.*

Masculine; *prīvigna* — a stepdaughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**prīvignus** is built, Lewis & Short explains, on *prīvus* "separate" and the root of *gignō* "to beget": born of another line.
