## You'll want to know: farcire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C337-CONDIRE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**farcire** — "to stuff, to fill".

> Ho farcito i peperoni con riso e carne.
>
> *I stuffed the peppers with rice and meat.*

Cakes too: *farcire una torta con la crema*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*farcire* is inherited from Latin *farcīre* "to cram, to stuff" (EtymDB).
