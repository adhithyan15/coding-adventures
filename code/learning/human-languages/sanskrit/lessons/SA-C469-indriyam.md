## You'll want to know: इन्द्रियम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C469-INDRIYA469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इन्द्रियम्** — *indriyam* — "a sense; an organ of sense".

> नरस्य पञ्च इन्द्रियाणि सन्ति।
>
> *narasya pañca indriyāṇi santi.*
>
> A person has five senses.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इन्द्रियम्** (*indriyam*) is built on *Indra*, the god's name: MW's first sense is "belonging to Indra", then "bodily power" and "a sense, an organ of sense" (MW).
