## You'll want to know: નીંદર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-02]; assesses=[] -->

**નીંદર** — *nī̃dar* — "slumber; sleep (a homely word)".

> થાકને લીધે મને તરત નીંદર આવી ગઈ.
>
> *thākne līdhe mane tarat nī̃dar āvī gaī.*
>
> I was so tired that I fell asleep at once.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નીંદર** (*nī̃dar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nidrā*, "sleep"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *nīdar* (CDIAL 7200).
