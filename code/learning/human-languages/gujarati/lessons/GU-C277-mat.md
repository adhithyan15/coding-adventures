# મત (mat) — an opinion; a view

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-VICHAARUN-KE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-SG-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01] -->

## You'll want to know: મત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C277-MAT-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-HUNGUJARATIBOLUNCHHUN-01, GU-LEX-C133-QUAL133-03] -->

**મત** — *mat* — "an opinion; a view". It is masculine: **તમારો મત**, "your opinion".

> **તમારો મત શું છે?**
>
> *tamāro mat śũ che?* — "What is your opinion?"

The answer often opens on **મારા મતે** (*mārā mate*), "in my opinion": **મત** takes the **-ે** of **સવારે**, and **મારો** turns to **મારા** before it.

> **મારા મતે ગુજરાતી અઘરી નથી.**
>
> *mārā mate gujarātī agharī nathī.* — "In my opinion Gujarati is not hard."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મત** (*mat*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *mata*, "thought, held to be", and as a noun "an opinion" (Monier-Williams), taken into Gujarati as it was. It belongs with the Sanskrit verb *man-*, "to think".
