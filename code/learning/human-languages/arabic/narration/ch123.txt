Arabic, chapter 123: Blanket, Pillow, Ladder, Stove, Vessel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بطانية (baṭṭāniyya) — a blanket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a towel, then the Arabic for a mattress.
