## You'll want to know: la polémique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C285-RUMEUR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la polémique** — "the controversy, the row".

> Ses paroles ont créé une polémique.
>
> *His words caused a row.*

As an adjective: *un ton polémique*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*polémique* comes from Greek *polemikós* "warlike" (EtymDB), built on *pólemos* "war": a war of words.
