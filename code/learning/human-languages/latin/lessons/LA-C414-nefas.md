## You'll want to know: nefās
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C414-INIURIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**nefās** — "a crime against heaven, a wicked thing".

> Nefās est hospitem violāre.
>
> *It is wicked to wrong a guest.*

*nefās* n., indeclinable: *ne-* not + *fās*. English *nefarious* comes from its family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nefās* is built from *ne-* "not" and *fās* "divine law" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): what heaven forbids. English *nefarious* comes from its adjective.
