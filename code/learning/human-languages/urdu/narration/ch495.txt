Urdu, chapter 495: Wages and Skill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اجرت (ujrat) — wages; payment for work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for dismissed from a job, then the Urdu for to recruit.

You'll want to know: اجرت.
اجرت — ujrat — "wages; payment for work".
مزدوروں کو ان کی اجرت شام کو ملتی ہے۔ mazdūroṅ ko un kī ujrat shām ko miltī hai. The labourers get their wages in the evening.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
اجرت (ujrat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ujra, "a wage, a fee".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a strike, then the Urdu for expertise.

You'll want to know: ٹھیکیدار.
ٹھیکیدار — ṭhekedār — "a contractor".
ٹھیکیدار نے مزدوروں کو جلدی بلایا۔ ṭhekedār ne mazdūroṅ ko jaldī bulāyā. The contractor called the labourers early.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھیکیدار (ṭhekedār) is built from ṭhekā, "a contract for piecework", which CDIAL lists under ṭhīkka, "firm, right" (CDIAL 5503), and the ending -dār, usually traced to Persian -dār, "holding".

Lesson 5 of 5.
کھپانا (khapānā) — to use up (time or effort); to toil over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for expertise, then the Urdu for a contractor.

You'll want to know: کھپانا.
کھپانا — khapānā — "to use up (time or effort); to toil over".
اس نے اس کام میں دو سال کھپائے۔ us ne is kām meṅ do sāl khapāe. He spent two whole years toiling over this work.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کام میں تین گھنٹے کھپائے, us ne kām meṅ tīn ghanṭe khapāe.

The word, taken apart.
کھپانا (khapānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin khapānā, "to destroy", under Sanskrit kṣapyatē, "is destroyed, is used up" (CDIAL 3655); CDIAL glosses it "to destroy", and the everyday sense "to use up time or effort" is a later one it does not record.
