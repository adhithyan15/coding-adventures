## You'll want to know: संवर्धयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C494-LALANA494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संवर्धयति** — *saṃvardhayati* — "brings up".

> माता पिता च पुत्रं स्नेहेन संवर्धयतः।
>
> *mātā pitā ca putraṃ snehena saṃvardhayataḥ.*
>
> The mother and father bring up their son with love.

Past (she …): सा पुत्रीं स्नेहेन संवर्धितवती, *sā putrīṃ snehena saṃvardhitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संवर्धयति** (*saṃvardhayati*) is built on *sam* and the causative of the root √vṛdh, "to grow", the root of **वर्धते**; MW's ppp *saṃvardhita* is "brought up, reared" (MW).
