## You'll want to know: ندامت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C451-REGRET451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ندامت** — *nadāmat* — "remorse".

> جھوٹ بول کر اسے ندامت ہوئی۔
>
> *jhūṭ bol kar use nadāmat huī.*
>
> After telling the lie he felt remorse.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ندامت** (*nadāmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nadāma*, "regret".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
