## You'll want to know: प्राध्यापक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C369-VIDYAPITH369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राध्यापक** — *prādhyāpak* — "a professor".

> आमचे प्राध्यापक खूप छान शिकवतात.
>
> *āmce prādhyāpak khūp chān śikavtāt.*
>
> Our professor teaches very well.

Masculine.

Respect takes the plural: प्राध्यापक शिकवतात.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राध्यापक** (*prādhyāpak*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *pra-*, "forth, before", and *adhyāpaka*, "teacher" (CDIAL 278).
