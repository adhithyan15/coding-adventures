## You'll want to know: ভেড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C397-GHAT397-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ভেড়ানো** — *bheṛāno* — "to bring (a boat) alongside; to moor".

> মাঝি নৌকাটা ঘাটে ভেড়াল।
>
> *mājhi noukāṭā ghāṭe bheṛālô.*
>
> The boatman brought the boat in to the ghat.

Past (he/she …): তিনি নৌকা ঘাটে ভেড়ালেন, *tini noukā ghāṭe bheṛālen*.

Not ভেড়া, a sheep: the same first letters, a different word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভেড়ানো** (*bheṛāno*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *bhiṛāna*, "to bring alongside", under the reconstructed *bhiṭ*, "meet, throng" (CDIAL 9490), the same root as ভিড়, a crowd.
