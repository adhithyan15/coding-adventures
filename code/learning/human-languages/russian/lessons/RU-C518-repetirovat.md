## You'll want to know: репетировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C518-REHEARSAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**репетировать** (*repetírovat*) — "to rehearse".

> Актёры репетируют новый спектакль.
>
> *Aktyóry repetíruyut nóvyy spektákl.*
>
> The actors are rehearsing a new play.

Imperfective; present репетирую. Perfective отрепетировать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*репетировать* is built on the stem of *репетиция* 'a rehearsal', borrowed from Polish *repetycja* (EtymDB); EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
