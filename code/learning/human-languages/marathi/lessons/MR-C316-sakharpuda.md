## You'll want to know: साखरपुडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C316-LAGNA316-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साखरपुडा** — *sākharpuḍā* — "an engagement".

> त्यांचा साखरपुडा काल घरीच झाला.
>
> *tyāñcā sākharpuḍā kāl gharīc jhālā.*
>
> Their engagement took place at home yesterday.

Masculine.

A packet of sugar is exchanged: साखर + पुडा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साखरपुडा** (*sākharpuḍā*) is built from *sākhar*, 'sugar', inherited from Sanskrit *śarkarā* (CDIAL 12338), and *puḍā*, 'a packet', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *puṭa*, 'a fold, a small container' (CDIAL 8253).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
