Spanish, chapter 721: Rasgos de personalidad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el rasgo — the trait, the feature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for gratitude, then the Spanish for to appreciate.

You'll want to know: el rasgo.
el rasgo — "the trait, the feature".
La paciencia es su mejor rasgo. Patience is her best trait.
Los rasgos are also facial features: rasgos finos.

The word, taken apart.
rasgo is built on rasgar "to tear", which is usually traced to Latin resecāre "to cut off", from secāre "to cut". A rasgo was first a stroke of the pen, then a feature.

The word, taken apart.
personalidad is a learned borrowing from Late Latin persōnālitās (EtymDB), built on persōna "an actor's mask, a role", the source of persona.

Lesson 3 of 5.
el temperamento — temperament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the trait, then the Spanish for personality.

You'll want to know: el temperamento.
el temperamento — "temperament".
Es un niño de temperamento tranquilo. He is a child with a calm temperament.
Tener temperamento — to be fiery.

The word, taken apart.
temperamento is a learned borrowing from Latin temperāmentum "a due mixture" (EtymDB), from temperāre "to mix in proportion"; old medicine saw character as a mix of body fluids.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el defecto — the fault, the flaw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for personality, then the Spanish for temperament.

You'll want to know: el defecto.
el defecto — "the fault, the flaw".
Su mayor defecto es la impaciencia. His biggest fault is impatience.
The opposite is la virtud or la cualidad.

The word, taken apart.
defecto is a learned borrowing from Latin dēfectus "failure, lack" (EtymDB), from dēficere "to fail", built on dē- and facere "to do".
