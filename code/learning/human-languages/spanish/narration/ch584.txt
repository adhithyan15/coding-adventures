Spanish, chapter 584: Pros y contras.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la ventaja — the advantage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the factor, then the Spanish for to generate.

You'll want to know: la ventaja.
la ventaja — "the advantage".
Vivir cerca tiene muchas ventajas. Living nearby has many advantages.
Sacar ventaja — to gain an advantage, to get ahead.

The word, taken apart.
ventaja was borrowed from French avantage 'a being in front', built on avant, from Latin ab ante 'in front' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: la desventaja.
la desventaja — "the disadvantage".
La desventaja es el precio. The disadvantage is the price.
The opposite of la ventaja.

The word, taken apart.
desventaja is built on ventaja with the prefix des-; ventaja was borrowed from French avantage, from Latin ab ante 'in front' (EtymDB).
