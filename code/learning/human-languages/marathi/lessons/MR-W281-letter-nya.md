# ञ (ña) — the letter hidden inside ज्ञ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C281-JNAN-01, MR-SCRIPT-JA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VIRAMA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ज्ञान**, "knowledge". You have read its first block, **ज्ञ**, as one shape. Name the letter it starts with: **ज**. Then the mark that silences a letter's vowel: **्**.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-JA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VIRAMA-01] -->

> ञ

**ञ** is *ña*, a nasal made where *j* is made. **ज्ञ** is **ज**, the **्**, and **ञ**, joined into one shape; Marathi says the pair *dny*. On its own **ञ** is rare in Marathi words; you will meet it inside **ज्ञ**, in **ज्ञान**.

## Writing: ञ — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **ञ** in front of you for this first pass.

- **1.** start at the open upper-left tip and curve clockwise around the bowl to the open lower-left tip
- **2.** lift at the bowl's right junction, sweep right through the shoulder, then curve upward along the right side to the headline
- **3.** lift at the lower right-side junction and descend the short stem below the bowl
- **4.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 3.** This is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-ञ-order.gif’, strokes 1–4, Wikimedia Commons, 10 May 2009.

[PAUSE 1s] Copy **ञ** twice beside the model, saying *ña* as you finish each one. Then write **ज्ञान** once and point at the **ञ** inside its first block.
