## You'll want to know: পশ্চিম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C401-DIK401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পশ্চিম** — *pôshchim* — "west".

> সন্ধ্যায় আমরা পশ্চিম দিকে হাঁটলাম।
>
> *sôndhyāy āmrā pôshchim dike hā̃ṭlām.*
>
> In the evening we walked westward.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পশ্চিম** (*pôshchim*) is Sanskrit *paścima*, "being behind, later", and so "western" (Monier-Williams): with the sunrise in front, the west lies behind you.
