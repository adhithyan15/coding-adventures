## You'll want to know: پھیلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C332-NUQSAN332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پھیلنا** — *phailnā* — "to spread".

> یہ خبر شہر میں جلدی پھیل گئی۔
>
> *yih khabar shahr meṅ jaldī phail gaī.*
>
> The news spread quickly through the city.

Past (it …): وہ بیماری پھیل گئی, *voh bīmārī phail gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھیلنا** (*phailnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phailnā*, "to spread", under Sanskrit *prathita*, "spread out" (CDIAL 8651).
