Spanish, chapter 516: Su versión.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
asegurar — to assure, to insist; to insure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to declare, then the Spanish for to confirm.

You'll want to know: asegurar.
asegurar — "to assure, to insist; to insure".
Me aseguró que no había visto nada. He assured me that he had not seen anything.
Asegurarse de algo is to make sure of it. Asegurar el coche is to insure the car.

The word, taken apart.
asegurar is built on seguro 'sure, safe' with the prefix a-. Seguro is inherited from Latin sēcūrus (EtymDB), 'free from care', from sē- 'without' and cūra 'care' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
rectificar — to correct, to put right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to confirm, then the Spanish for to assure.

You'll want to know: rectificar.
rectificar — "to correct, to put right".
Un día después, el periódico rectificó la noticia. A day later, the newspaper corrected the story.
It takes qu before e: rectifiqué. Rectificar es de sabios — it is wise to admit a mistake.

The word, taken apart.
rectificar is usually traced to Late Latin rēctificāre 'to make straight', a learned word built on rēctus 'straight' (Lewis & Short) and facere 'to make'. To correct a story is to set it straight.

Lesson 3 of 5.
revelar — to reveal; to develop (a photo).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to assure, then the Spanish for to correct.

You'll want to know: revelar.
revelar — "to reveal; to develop (a photo)".
El periódico reveló el nombre del ladrón. The newspaper revealed the thief's name.
With old film, revelar las fotos is to develop the photos.

The word, taken apart.
revelar is a learned borrowing from Latin revēlāre 'to draw back the veil' (EtymDB), built on re- and vēlum 'a cloth, a veil'. To reveal is to pull the cloth away.

Lesson 4 of 5.
desmentir — to refute, to deny publicly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to correct, then the Spanish for to reveal.

You'll want to know: desmentir.
desmentir — "to refute, to deny publicly".
El club desmintió la noticia. The club denied the story.
Used for rumours and news reports. Stem change: desmiento, desmintió.

The word, taken apart.
desmentir is built on mentir 'to lie' with the prefix des-. Mentir is inherited from Latin mentīrī 'to lie' (EtymDB). To desmentir a story is to call it a lie.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la versión — the version, the account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to reveal, then the Spanish for to refute.

You'll want to know: la versión.
la versión — "the version, the account".
Cada persona tiene su versión de lo que pasó. Each person has their own version of what happened.
En versión original on a cinema listing means not dubbed.

The word, taken apart.
versión is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin versiō 'a turning, a translation' (EtymDB), built on vertere 'to turn'. A version is a story turned one particular way.
