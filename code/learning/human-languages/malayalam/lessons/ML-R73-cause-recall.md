## Grammar Lens: a question and two answers
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C73-WHY-01, ML-LEX-C73-KAARANAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C73-BECAUSE-01] -->

| | |
|---|---|
| to ask | **എന്തുകൊണ്ട്** *entukoṇṭŭ* |
| to answer, speaking | **കാരണം** *kāraṇaṁ* |
| to answer, writing | **എന്തുകൊണ്ടെന്നാൽ** *entukoṇṭennāl* |

**Until this chapter, cause could not be handled in either direction.** There
was no way to ask what was behind something and no way to give it. Three words
close both halves at once, and two of the three are built on material you had:
*entukoṇṭŭ* on the word for *what*, and the written one on *entukoṇṭŭ* itself.

**കാരണം is the one to have in your mouth.** The long one is for reading.
