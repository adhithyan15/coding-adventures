## You'll want to know: थबकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C302-KSHAN302-02]; assesses=[] -->

**थबकणे** — *thabakṇe* — "to stop short".

> रस्त्यात साप दिसल्यावर ती थबकली.
>
> *rastyāt sāp disalyāvar tī thabaklī.*
>
> When she saw a snake on the road, she stopped short.

Past (he …): तो थबकला, *to thabaklā*.

Intransitive: थबकून उभं राहणे, to stand stock-still.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थबकणे** (*thabakṇe*) is a Marathi verb of stopping short; its origin is not settled.
