## You'll want to know: ಪೂರೈಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C421-BEDIKE421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೂರೈಸು** — *pūraisu* — "to supply".

> ಆ ಹಳ್ಳಿ ನಗರಕ್ಕೆ ಹಾಲು ಪೂರೈಸುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ā haḷḷi nagarakke hālu pūraisuttade.*
>
> That village supplies milk to the city.

Past (I …): ಪೂರೈಸಿದೆ, *pūraiside*.

Also to complete: ಓದು ಪೂರೈಸು, to finish one's studies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೂರೈಸು** (*pūraisu*) is built on Sanskrit *pūra*, "filling" (CDIAL 8330), with a Kannada verb ending: to make full.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
