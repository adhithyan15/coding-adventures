## You'll want to know: にぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C180-EATOUT180-04]; assesses=[] -->

**にぎる** — *nigiru* — "to grip; to grasp, to shape (rice)".

> **こどもが ははの てを にぎります。** — *kodomo ga haha no te o nigirimasu* — "The child holds his mother's hand."

Polite **にぎります**, *nigirimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にぎる** (*nigiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
