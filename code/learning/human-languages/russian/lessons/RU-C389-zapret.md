## You'll want to know: запрет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C389-ZAPRET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**запрет** (*zaprét*) — "a ban".

> В нашем городе есть запрет на курение в парках.
>
> *V náshem górode yest zaprét na kuréniye v párkakh.*
>
> In our town there is a ban on smoking in parks.

Masculine. *Запрет на* + accusative — a ban on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**запрет** is formed from *запретить* "to forbid", built from *за-* and the root of *претить*.
