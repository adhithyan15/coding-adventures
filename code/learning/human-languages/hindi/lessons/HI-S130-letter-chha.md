# छ — the puff again, on a letter you already have

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-117, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-126] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: **च** — what sound does it carry?

[PAUSE 2s] You have met this arrangement once before, with **त** and **थ**. It
is about to happen again.

## Script you'll notice: छ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-130]; assesses=[] -->

**छ** — *chha*.

It is the **breathy partner of च**, exactly as **थ** is the breathy partner of
**त**. Say *cha*, then say it again with a puff of air behind it, and you have
the difference.

What it is made of:

- a continuous upper-left loop, lower bowl, outer-right rise and inner loop
- a short upper stem
- the top shirorekhā

## Script: the pattern, a second time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-130, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-117] -->

| character | sound | puff of breath |
|---|---|---|
| च | ca | no |
| छ | chha | yes |
| त | ta | no |
| थ | tha | yes |

Four characters, two pairs, one distinction running through both. **This script
pairs its letters by breath**, and once you have heard the pair once you can
expect it everywhere — there are more pairs waiting, and each will cost you
less than the last.

Unlike **त** and **थ**, the two shapes here look nothing alike. The sound is
paired; the drawing is not.

## Writing: छ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-130] -->

- **1.** start at the upper loop's open right tip, sweep counterclockwise around the upper-left loop, continue clockwise around the broad lower bowl, rise along the outer right side, and curl counterclockwise around the inner loop to its down-right tip without lifting
- **2.** lift and draw the short upper stem top-to-bottom
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.** The pen comes up 2 times and no more.

> Verified three-stroke teaching form fitted to the bundled printed outline;
> everyday handwriting may divide or simplify the loops differently.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-छ-order.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 10 May 2009.
