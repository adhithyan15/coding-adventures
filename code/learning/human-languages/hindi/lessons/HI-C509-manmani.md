## You'll want to know: मनमानी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C509-HADTAL509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मनमानी** — *manmānī* — "high-handedness; doing as one pleases".

> मालिक की मनमानी से सब मज़दूर परेशान हैं।
>
> *Mālik kī manmānī se sab mazdūr pareśān haiṁ.*
>
> All the workers are fed up with the owner's high-handedness.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मनमानी** (*manmānī*) joins *man*, "mind", from Sanskrit *manas* (CDIAL 9822), and a form of *mānnā*, "to respect, obey", from Sanskrit *manyate*, "thinks fit" (CDIAL 9857): doing only what one's own mind will have.
