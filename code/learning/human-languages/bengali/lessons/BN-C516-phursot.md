## You'll want to know: ফুরসত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C516-BINODON516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ফুরসত** — *phursôt* — "spare time; a free moment".

> এই সপ্তাহে আমার ফুরসত নেই।
>
> *Ei sôptāhe āmār phursôt nei.*
>
> I have no spare time this week.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফুরসত** (*phursôt*) is usually traced to Arabic *furṣa*, "an opportunity", through Persian *furṣat*.
