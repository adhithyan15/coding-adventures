# আমার মনে হয় … (āmār mône hôy …) — I think

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FIRST-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-FINALLY-01, BN-LEX-C239-ACT239-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "first", "after that", "in the end". (*prôthôme*, *tārpôr*, *sheshe*.) Then **মনে রাখা**, "to remember": "to keep in the mind". Its **মনে**, "in the mind", is in this lesson too.

## You'll want to know: I think
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C268-I-THINK-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C239-ACT239-05, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMAR-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C19-JE-01, BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

> **আমার মনে হয়, কাল ঝড় হবে।**
>
> *āmār mône hôy, kāl jhôṛ hôbe.* — "I think there will be a storm tomorrow."

Bengali does not say "I think" with **আমি**. It says "to my mind it comes": **আমার মনে হয়**, with the thinker in the **-র** form, as in **আমার নাম**. **যে**, "that", which you know, may follow it: **আমার মনে হয় যে …**. To ask, put the other person in the same form: **তোমার কী মনে হয়?** (*tomār kī mône hôy?*), "what do you think?".

## You'll want to know: I don't think so
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C268-THINK-NOT-01]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-LEX-C23-SHE-01] -->

**না** after **হয়** turns it: **আমার মনে হয় না** (*āmār mône hôy nā*), "I don't think so", and **আমার মনে হয় না সে আসবে**, "I don't think he'll come". In the past, **হয়** becomes **হল**: **আমার মনে হল** (*āmār mône hôlô*), "I thought (then), it seemed to me".

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "I think there will be a storm tomorrow" — *āmār mône hôy, kāl jhôṛ hôbe*]
- [YOU SAY: "What do you think?" — *tomār kī mône hôy?*]
- [YOU SAY: "I don't think the train will come" — *āmār mône hôy nā ṭren āsbe*]
