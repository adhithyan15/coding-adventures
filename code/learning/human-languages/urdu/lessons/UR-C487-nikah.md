## You'll want to know: نکاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C487-WEDDING487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نکاح** — *nikāh* — "the marriage contract".

> مسجد میں ان کا نکاح ہوا۔
>
> *masjid meṅ un kā nikāh huā.*
>
> They were married at the mosque.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نکاح** (*nikāh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nikāḥ*, "marriage".
