## You'll want to know: الجھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C347-MUAMALA347-04]; assesses=[] -->

**الجھنا** — *ulajhnā* — "to get tangled; to get bogged down".

> وہ ایک چھوٹی بات میں الجھ گئے۔
>
> *voh ek chhoṭī bāt meṅ ulajh gae.*
>
> They got bogged down in a small point.

Past (it …): وہ دھاگا الجھ گیا, *voh dhāgā ulajh gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**الجھنا** (*ulajhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ulajhnā* under Sanskrit *uparundhati*, "obstructs" (CDIAL 2221).
