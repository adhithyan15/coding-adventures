## You'll want to know: похороны
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C309-POKHORONY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**похороны** (*pokhoróny*) — "a funeral".

> На похороны пришло много людей.
>
> *Na pokhoróny prishló mnógo lyudéy.*
>
> A lot of people came to the funeral.

Plural only; genitive *похорон*: *на похоронах* — at the funeral.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*похороны* is built on *хоронить* "to bury", an inherited Slavic verb whose older sense was "to keep, to hide away"; EtymDB lists no parent.
