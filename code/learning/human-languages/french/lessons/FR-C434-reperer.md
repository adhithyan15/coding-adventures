## You'll want to know: repérer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C434-ORIENTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**repérer** — "to spot; to find one’s way (se repérer)".

> Elle a repéré un petit café près de la gare.
>
> *She spotted a little café near the station.*

*Se repérer* — to find your way around.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*repérer* is built on *repère* 'mark, landmark' (EtymDB).
