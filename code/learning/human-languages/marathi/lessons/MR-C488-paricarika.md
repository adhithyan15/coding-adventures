## You'll want to know: परिचारिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C488-RUGNA488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**परिचारिका** — *paricārikā* — "a nurse".

> रात्री परिचारिकेने आजोबांची काळजी घेतली.
>
> *rātrī paricārikene ājobāñcī kāḷjī ghetlī.*
>
> At night a nurse looked after Grandfather.

Feminine.

Feminine; oblique परिचारिके-.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिचारिका** (*paricārikā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *paricārikā*, "a female attendant" (Monier-Williams).
