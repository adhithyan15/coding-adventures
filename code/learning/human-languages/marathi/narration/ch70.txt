Marathi, chapter 70: Hand, Foot, Hair, Tooth, Throat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हात (hāt) — a hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for ninth, then the Marathi for tenth.

You'll want to know: हात.
हात — hāt — "a hand".

The word, taken apart.
हात (hāt) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from hasta, "hand" (CDIAL 14024). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "hand, arm, cubit".
