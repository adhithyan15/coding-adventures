## You'll want to know: محقق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C349-TAHQIQ349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**محقق** — *muhaqqiq* — "a researcher".

> محقق نے اپنا نتیجہ لکھا۔
>
> *muhaqqiq ne apnā natījā likhā.*
>
> The researcher wrote up her result.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محقق** (*muhaqqiq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muḥaqqiq*, "one who verifies", of the same family as **تحقیق**, "research".
