## You'll want to know: apetecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C499-MERIENDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**apetecer** — "to feel like, to fancy".

> ¿Te apetece un café?
>
> *Do you feel like a coffee?*

It works like *gustar*: *me apetece*. Used mostly in Spain; elsewhere *tener ganas de*.
