## You'll want to know: आर्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C304-RASHI304-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आर्तः** — *ārtaḥ* — "in distress; afflicted".

> राशेः अधः आर्तः नरः अस्ति।
>
> *rāśeḥ adhaḥ ārtaḥ naraḥ asti.*
>
> There is a man in distress under the heap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आर्तः** (*ārtaḥ*) MW gives *ārta* as fallen into misfortune, afflicted, pained, and discusses its derivation without settling it, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
