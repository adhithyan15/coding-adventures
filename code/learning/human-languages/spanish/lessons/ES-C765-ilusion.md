## You'll want to know: la ilusión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C765-ILUSION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la ilusión** — "excitement, hope; illusion".

> Me hace mucha ilusión verte.
>
> *I am really looking forward to seeing you.*

*Me hace ilusión* — I am excited about it. It is usually a happy word in Spanish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ilusión* is a learned borrowing from Latin *illūsiō* "mockery, deception" (EtymDB), from *illūdere*, built on *lūdere* "to play"; Spanish turned it into hope and excitement.
