## You'll want to know: a disciplina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C375-ESCOLA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a disciplina** — "the school subject; discipline".

> A minha disciplina preferida é a história.
>
> *My favourite subject is history.*

In Portugal *uma disciplina* is a school subject; also discipline, order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disciplina* is a learned borrowing from Latin *disciplīna* "instruction, teaching" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *discipulus* "pupil", from *discere* "to learn".
