## You'll want to know: चुराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C288-CHORI288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चुराना** — *curānā* — "to steal".

> चोर ने मेरी साइकिल चुराई।
>
> *cor ne merī sāikil curāī.*
>
> The thief stole my bicycle.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बटुआ चुराया, *usne baṭuā curāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुराना** (*curānā*) belongs with Sanskrit *corayati*, "steals" (CDIAL 4933), where CDIAL records an older Hindi verb *cornā*. With **ने** the verb agrees with the thing taken: **साइकिल चुराई**, but **बटुआ चुराया**.
