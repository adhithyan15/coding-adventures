# إلا إذا (illā idhā) — unless

## You'll want to know: إلا إذا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C399-SHART399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**إلا إذا** — *illā idhā* — "unless".

> لن نخرج إلا إذا توقف المطر.
>
> *lan nakhruja illā idhā tawaqqafa al-maṭar.*
>
> We will not go out unless the rain stops.

After إذا the verb is usually in the past, even for the future.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إلا إذا** (*illā idhā*) joins the native Arabic particles *illā*, "except", and *idhā*, "if": except if, unless. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
