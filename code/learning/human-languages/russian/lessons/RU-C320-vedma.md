## You'll want to know: ведьма
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C320-VOLSHEBNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ведьма** (*védma*) — "a witch".

> Старая ведьма жила в лесу.
>
> *Stáraya védma zhilá v lesú.*
>
> An old witch lived in the forest.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ведьма* is inherited through Old East Slavic *vědĭma* from Proto-Slavic *vědĭma* (EtymDB), built on the root of *ведать* "to know": a woman who knows.
