## You'll want to know: चयन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C550-CHAYAN550-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चयन** — *cayan* — "a selection; a choice (formal)".

> टीम के लिए ग्यारह खिलाड़ियों का चयन हुआ।
>
> *Ṭīm ke liye gyārah khilāṛiyoṁ kā cayan huā.*
>
> Eleven players were picked for the team.

Masculine.

A formal word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चयन** (*cayan*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *cayana*, "piling up, collecting" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for picking out, a selection.
