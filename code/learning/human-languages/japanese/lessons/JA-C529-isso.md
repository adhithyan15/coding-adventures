## You'll want to know: いっそ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C529-ANYWAY529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いっそ** — *isso* — "would sooner; rather".

> **もし あしたも まけたら、いっそ やめた ほうが いいです。** — *moshi ashita mo maketara, isso yameta hō ga ii desu* — "If we lose tomorrow as well, it would be better to quit altogether."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いっそ** (*isso*) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, though that is not certain: JMdict gives it no kanji spelling at all, and no dictionary we use records its history, so where it comes from is not settled (JMdict).
