Hindi, chapter 318: A Blast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धमाका (dhamākā) — a bang; a blast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be startled, then the Hindi for astonished.

You'll want to know: धमाका.
धमाका — dhamākā — "a bang; a blast".
रात को एक ज़ोर का धमाका हुआ। rāt ko ek zor kā dhamākā huā. There was a loud bang in the night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
धमाका (dhamākā) is probably imitative, built on dham, the sound of a heavy thud. CDIAL reconstructs dhamm, "noise" (CDIAL 6735), and lists Hindi dhamādham, noisily, under it, though not this word itself. धमाका होना is for there to be a bang.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a bang, then the Hindi for a bomb.

You'll want to know: गूँजना.
गूँजना — gū̃jnā — "to echo; to resound".
धमाका पूरे शहर में गूँजा। dhamākā pūre śahar meṁ gū̃jā. The blast echoed through the whole city.
Past (it …): वह घंटा गूँजा, vah ghaṇṭā gū̃jā.

The word, taken apart.
गूँजना (gū̃jnā) goes back to Sanskrit guñjati, "hums, buzzes" (CDIAL 4175). A sound गूँजता है when it fills a place and comes back.

Lesson 4 of 5.
थर्राना (tharrānā) — to quake; to shudder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a bomb, then the Hindi for to echo.

You'll want to know: थर्राना.
थर्राना — tharrānā — "to quake; to shudder".
धमाके से खिड़कियाँ थर्रा गईं। dhamāke se khiṛkiyā̃ tharrā gaīṁ. The windows shook with the blast.
Past (it …): वह दीवार थर्रा गई, vah dīvār tharrā gaī.

The word, taken apart.
थर्राना (tharrānā) is inherited from thar, a form CDIAL reconstructs for trembling (CDIAL 6092). It is a hard shaking, of a building or of a person in fear.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सदमा (sadmā) — a shock (of grief or fright).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to echo, then the Hindi for to quake.

You'll want to know: सदमा.
सदमा — sadmā — "a shock (of grief or fright)".
धमाके के बाद मीरा को गहरा सदमा लगा। dhamāke ke bād Mīrā ko gahrā sadmā lagā. After the blast Mira was in deep shock.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सदमा (sadmā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṣadma, "a blow, a collision": a blow to the mind. A shock लगता है: मीरा को सदमा लगा.
