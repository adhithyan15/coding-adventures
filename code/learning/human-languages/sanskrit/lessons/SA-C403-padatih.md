## You'll want to know: पदातिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C403-PANTHA403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पदातिः** — *padātiḥ* — "a traveller on foot".

> पदातिः मार्गस्य पार्श्वे चलति।
>
> *padātiḥ mārgasya pārśve calati.*
>
> The traveller on foot walks at the side of the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पदातिः** (*padātiḥ*) is built on *pada*, "a foot, a step"; MW gives *padāti* as going on foot, a pedestrian, and divides it, with a query, as *pada* + *āti* (MW).
