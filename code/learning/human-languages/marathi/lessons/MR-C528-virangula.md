## You'll want to know: विरंगुळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C528-CHHAND528-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विरंगुळा** — *viraṅguḷā* — "a pastime; a pleasant break".

> संध्याकाळी बागेत काम करणं हा माझा विरंगुळा आहे.
>
> *sandhyākāḷī bāget kām karṇaṁ hā mājhā viraṅguḷā āhe.*
>
> Working in the garden in the evening is my pastime.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरंगुळा** (*viraṅguḷā*) is a Marathi word for something that takes the mind off work; its origin is not settled.
