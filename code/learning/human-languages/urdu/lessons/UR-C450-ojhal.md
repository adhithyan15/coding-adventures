## You'll want to know: اوجھل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C450-MISS450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اوجھل** — *ojhal* — "out of sight".

> ریل گاڑی دور جا کر اوجھل ہو گئی۔
>
> *rel gāṛī dūr jā kar ojhal ho gaī.*
>
> The train went far off and disappeared from sight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اوجھل** (*ojhal*) is inherited from *ōjjhalla*, "a screen, a veil", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 2542).
