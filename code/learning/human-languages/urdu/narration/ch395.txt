Urdu, chapter 395: Tyres and Punctures.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ٹائر (ṭāyar) — a tyre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to bounce, then the Urdu for deserted.

You'll want to know: ٹائر.
ٹائر — ṭāyar — "a tyre".
گاڑی کا ایک ٹائر پرانا ہے۔ gāṛī kā ek ṭāyar purānā hai. One of the car's tyres is old.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ٹائر (ṭāyar) is borrowed from English tyre.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ٹکر (ṭakkar) — a collision; a knock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a tyre, then the Urdu for a puncture.

You'll want to know: ٹکر.
ٹکر — ṭakkar — "a collision; a knock".
پل پر دو گاڑیوں کی ٹکر ہوئی۔ pul par do gāṛiyoṅ kī ṭakkar huī. Two cars collided on the bridge.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ٹکر (ṭakkar) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi ṭakkar, "a blow", under ṭakkarā, "a blow on the head" (CDIAL 5424).

Lesson 5 of 5.
پچکنا (pichaknā) — to get dented; to be squashed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a collision, then the Urdu for a helmet.

You'll want to know: پچکنا.
پچکنا — pichaknā — "to get dented; to be squashed".
ٹکر میں گاڑی کا دروازہ پچک گیا۔ ṭakkar meṅ gāṛī kā darvāzā pichak gayā. The car door got dented in the collision.
Past (it …): وہ ڈبہ پچک گیا, voh ḍabbā pichak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
پچکنا (pichaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin picaknā, "to be pressed", under Sanskrit piccayati, "presses flat" (CDIAL 8149); the sense "to get dented" is probably a later one.
