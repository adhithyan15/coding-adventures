## You'll want to know: امکان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C361-IMKAN361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**امکان** — *imkān* — "a possibility; a chance".

> اگر بادل ہیں تو بارش کا امکان ہے۔
>
> *agar bādal haiṅ to bārish kā imkān hai.*
>
> If there are clouds, there is a chance of rain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امکان** (*imkān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *imkān*, "possibility", of the same family as **ممکن**, "possible".
