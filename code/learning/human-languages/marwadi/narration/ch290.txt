7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
बही (bahī) — a ledger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a carpenter's plane, then the Marwadi for to paint a picture.

You'll want to know: बही.
बही — bahī — "a ledger".
मालक बही राखै है। mālak bahī rākhai hai. The owner keeps a ledger.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बही (bahī) is inherited from Sanskrit vahikā, found in a word for a king's diary; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi bahī, "account-book, ledger" (CDIAL 11460).

Lesson 3 of 7.
सरू (sarū) — begun; the start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a ledger, then the Marwadi for employment.

You'll want to know: सरू.
सरू — sarū — "begun; the start".
पाठ सरू करो। pāṭh sarū karo. Start the lesson.

The word, taken apart.
सरू (sarū) is usually traced, through Persian shurūʿ, to Arabic shurūʿ, "a beginning". With करो it asks someone to start.

Lesson 5 of 7.
अभ्यास (abhyās) — practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for begun, then the Marwadi for finished.

You'll want to know: अभ्यास.
अभ्यास — abhyās — "practice".
रोज अभ्यास करो। roj abhyās karo. Practise every day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अभ्यास (abhyās) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit abhyāsa, "repetition; repeated exercise" (Monier-Williams).
