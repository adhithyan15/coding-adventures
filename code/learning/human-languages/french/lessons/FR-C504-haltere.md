## You'll want to know: l'haltère
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C504-GYM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'haltère** — "dumbbell, weight".

> Il soulève des haltères devant le miroir.
>
> *He lifts dumbbells in front of the mirror.*

Masculine: *un haltère*. The *h* is silent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'haltère* comes, through Latin, from Greek *haltēres*, weights that athletes held while jumping, built on *hállesthai* "to jump".
