## Script — the sickbed piece around a box
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-BING-ILL-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 病

**Two pieces: the sickbed piece outside, a boxed piece inside.**

**The sickbed piece, five strokes.** A dot at the top, a horizontal under it, a long left-falling stroke down the left side, then on the left a short dot and a short stroke rising to the right.

**The inside piece, five strokes.** A horizontal across the top; a short vertical, the left side of the box; a horizontal that turns down the right side and hooks; then a small 人 from the top bar into the box: its left-falling stroke, then its dot.

5 + 5 = ten.
