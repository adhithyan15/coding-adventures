# Six lines, one trip

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C253-ONGOING-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C258-FEELING-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Four lines once held together by time words and by the person you left out. Six lines can hold together by the joints you now own: **-て**, **それから**, **から** and **ので**, **-たら**.

[PAUSE 1s] The day at the river was thirteen lines of this kind. Pick one **ともだち** who will read about a trip you took.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-02, JA-LEX-C252-SOREKARA-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C254-REASON-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C257-PROBLEM-01, JA-LEX-C257-NORIOKURERU-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C261-WOULD-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Each line has a job, and a frame to hang it on:

1. When and where, and what was going on: **きのう** or **きょねん**, a place, and **-ていました** (**あめが ふっていました**).
2. The first thing that happened: **-て**, or **それから** at the start.
3. A problem: **のりおくれました**, **なくしました**, or **こわれました**.
4. Why: a reason with **から** or **ので**.
5. How it felt: **-かったです**, or **でした** after a na-adjective; **いちばん** if one thing stood out.
6. What you would do next time: **もし** … **-たら** … **と おもいます**.

[PAUSE 2s] Use at least two of the six kanji you have copied: **月日**, **年じゅう**, **言い出す**, **日本人**, **日本中**, **今まで**. Every other word goes in hiragana, spaced as the book spaces it.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C253-ONGOING-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-02, JA-LEX-C252-SOREKARA-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C257-PROBLEM-01, JA-LEX-C257-NORIOKURERU-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C254-REASON-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C254-REASON-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C258-FEELING-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C258-FEELING-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C260-TARA-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C261-WOULD-02, JA-LEX-C260-MOSHI-01, JA-LEX-C252-TSUKIHI-01, JA-LEX-C258-IMAMADE-01, JA-LEX-C256-NIHONJIN-01, JA-LEX-C257-NIHONJUU-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-TSUKI-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-JUU-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-IMA-01, JA-SKILL-READ-STORY-B1-01, JA-LEX-C259-ICHIBAN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 3s] Write **six lines** to the **ともだち** you picked, one job a line, in that order. There is no model to copy: the frames are the whole of the help. Count your signs when you finish; about one hundred is a good length.

[PAUSE 2s] End every line with **。**. A line may open with **それから** or **そして**, and then **、** follows it.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C257-NORIOKURERU-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C261-WOULD-02] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id": "JA-W261-six-lines-would", "kind": "text", "assesses": ["JA-GRAMMAR-C261-WOULD-02"], "prompt": "Give the frame for your sixth line: what you think you would do if you went again.", "answer": "moshi … tara, … to omoimasu", "accepted": ["もし … たら、… と おもいます", "moshi tara to omoimasu", "-tara … to omoimasu"], "feedback": {"correct": "Right: moshi opens it, -tara closes the condition, to omoimasu ends the line.", "incorrect": "The sixth line is moshi … -tara, then … to omoimasu."}, "response_seconds": 20} -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read your fourth line alone. Does it say why, with **から** or **ので**? Does your third line say what went wrong? If each line does its one job, the six lean on each other, and that is what connected means.
