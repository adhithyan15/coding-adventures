## You'll want to know: પરણાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C497-KANKOTRI497-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પરણાવવું** — *parṇāvvũ* — "to marry off; to give in marriage".

> તેઓ આવતા વરસે દીકરીને પરણાવશે.
>
> *teo āvtā varse dīkrīne parṇāvśe.*
>
> They will marry off their daughter next year.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાના સંતાનને પરણાવ્યું, *teṇe potānā santānne parṇāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરણાવવું** (*parṇāvvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pariṇāpayati*, "causes a man to marry"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to give in marriage" (CDIAL 7821).
