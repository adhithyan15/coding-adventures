## You'll want to know: enterrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C315-FUNERAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**enterrar** — "to bury".

> O cão enterrou o osso no quintal.
>
> *The dog buried the bone in the yard.*

For people and for things.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enterrar* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *interrāre* "to put in the earth" (EtymDB), built on *terra* "earth".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
