## You'll want to know: общество
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C364-OBSHCHESTVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**общество** (*óbshchestvo*) — "society".

> В современном обществе много проблем.
>
> *V sovreménnom óbshchestve mnógo problém.*
>
> There are many problems in modern society.

Neuter. It also means a society or club: *общество любителей книги*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**общество** was taken from Church Slavonic *obĭščĭstvo* (EtymDB), built on *общий* "common, shared": society is what people have in common.
