# め — cross, loop, release

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-MI-01, JA-LEX-SUMIMASEN] -->

[PAUSE 10s] Find **み** in **すみません**, then say the five-mora repair opener.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-ME-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **め** — *me*

Two strokes. The short first stroke leans down to the right. The longer second
stroke crosses it, curves into an open loop, and releases at the lower right.
Keep that final opening visible; do not close it into a circle.
