## You'll want to know: ટ્રક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C401-TYRE401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ટ્રક** — *ṭrak* — "a truck; a lorry".

> સડક પર એક મોટી ટ્રક ધીમે ચાલતી હતી.
>
> *saḍak par ek moṭī ṭrak dhīme cāltī hatī.*
>
> A big truck was moving slowly on the road.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટ્રક** (*ṭrak*) is borrowed from English *truck*.
