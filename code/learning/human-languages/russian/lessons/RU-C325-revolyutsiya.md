## You'll want to know: революция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C325-IMPERIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**революция** (*revolyútsiya*) — "a revolution".

> После революции жизнь в стране изменилась.
>
> *Pósle revolyútsii zhizn v strané izmenílas.*
>
> After the revolution life in the country changed.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*революция* came through Polish *rewolucja* from Latin *revolūtiō* (EtymDB), "a revolving" (Lewis & Short), built on *revolvere* "to roll back", from *volvere* "to roll".
