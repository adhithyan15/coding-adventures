## Writing

Task 1 must address the named coordinator, explain the locked room and the
learner's response, request workable arrangements, and ask for confirmation in
50–70 independently produced Sanskrit words. Task 2 must address community
newsletter readers, explain the current problem, develop two benefits and one
risk, and recommend a practical first step in 130–170 words. Apply the shared
rubric's external-sandhi and Devanagari criteria; do not reward copied prompt
language as independent production.

## Speaking

Use all five interview prompts, the three-minute account, the collaborative
planning brief, and all four follow-up questions. Score the candidate's own
contribution, development, interaction and repair, organisation, Sanskrit
vocabulary and grammar, and pronunciation and fluency in the declared
pronunciation tradition. Keyword notes are allowed only for the prepared and
planning parts. Examiner rephrasing beyond one same-pace repeat must be
reflected in interaction scoring.
