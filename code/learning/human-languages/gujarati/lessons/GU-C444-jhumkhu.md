## You'll want to know: ઝૂમખું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C444-BUD444-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂમખું** — *jhūmkhũ* — "a bunch; a cluster".

> આંબા પર કેરીનું ઝૂમખું લટકે છે.
>
> *ā̃bā par kerīnũ jhūmkhũ laṭke che.*
>
> A bunch of mangoes hangs on the mango tree.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂમખું** (*jhūmkhũ*) goes back to *\*jhuppa*, "a tuft, a bunch", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a cluster" (CDIAL 5404).
