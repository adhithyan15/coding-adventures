## You'll want to know: نوزاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C332-BARDAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نوزاد** (*nowzâd*) — "a newborn baby".

> نوزاد تمام شب گریه میکرد.
>
> *Nowzâd tamâm-e shab gerye mikard.*
>
> The newborn baby cried all night.

*now* "new" + *zâd* "born", from *zâdan* "to give birth".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nowzâd* joins *now* "new" and *zâd* "born", the past stem of *zâdan* "to give birth". Both parts are native Persian; EtymDB lists no entry.
