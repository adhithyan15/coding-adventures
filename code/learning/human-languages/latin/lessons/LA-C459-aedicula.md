## You'll want to know: aedicula, aediculae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C459-PORTICUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aedicula, aediculae** — "a shrine, a small temple; a niche".

> In compitō aedicula parva est, et viātōrēs ibi flōrēs pōnunt.
>
> *At the crossroads there is a small shrine, and travellers put flowers there.*

First declension, feminine: *aedicula, aediculae f.*, a diminutive of *aedēs*. A little shrine, or a niche for a statue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aedicula* is a diminutive of *aedēs* 'temple, house' (Lewis & Short), made with *-cula*. English keeps *aedicule* in architecture.
