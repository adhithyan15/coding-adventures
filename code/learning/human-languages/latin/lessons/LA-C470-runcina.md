## You'll want to know: runcīna, runcīnae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C470-ASCIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**runcīna, runcīnae** — "a plane (a carpenter's tool)".

> Faber tabulam runcīnā parat.
>
> *The carpenter prepares the board with a plane.*

First declension, feminine: *runcīna, runcīnae f.* An iron blade in a wooden block, for smoothing wood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*runcīna* was borrowed from Greek *rhykanē* 'plane', as an ancient grammarian already said (Lewis & Short); the form was changed to look like *runcō* 'to weed'.
