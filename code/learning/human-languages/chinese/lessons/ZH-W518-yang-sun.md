## Script — the ear piece on the left, then 日
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YANG-SUN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 阳

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the ear, two strokes**, as on the right of 那 and 都: one stroke that runs right, folds down twice like an ear and hooks, then a long vertical down its left edge.

**The right piece, 日, four strokes**, as you wrote it: a vertical, a bar that turns down the right side, a bar across the middle, then a bar closing the foot.

2 + 4 = six.
