## You'll want to know: प्रतिभूः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C433-AYA433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिभूः** — *pratibhūḥ* — "a guarantor".

> मम मित्रं मम प्रतिभूः भवति।
>
> *mama mitraṃ mama pratibhūḥ bhavati.*
>
> My friend stands as my guarantor.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिभूः** (*pratibhūḥ*) is built from *prati*, "in place of", and the root √bhū, "to be", which also gives *bhavati*, "is": one who stands in for another; MW gives *pratibhū* as a surety, security, bail (MW).
