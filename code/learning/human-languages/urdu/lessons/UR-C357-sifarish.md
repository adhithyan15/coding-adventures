## You'll want to know: سفارش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C357-BADUNVANI357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سفارش** — *sifārish* — "a recommendation; string-pulling".

> اسے سفارش سے نوکری ملی۔
>
> *use sifārish se naukrī milī.*
>
> He got the job through string-pulling.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سفارش** (*sifārish*) is usually traced to Persian *sifārish*, "a recommendation, a commission"; in Urdu a word put in for someone, often unfairly.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
