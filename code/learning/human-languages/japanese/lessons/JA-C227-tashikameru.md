## You'll want to know: たしかめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C227-THINK227-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たしかめる** — *tashikameru* — "to make sure; to check carefully".

> **がくせいは こたえを もういちど たしかめます。** — *gakusei wa kotae o mō ichido tashikamemasu* — "The students check their answers once more."

Polite **たしかめます**, *tashikamemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たしかめる** (*tashikameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built on *tashika*, "certain", with a verb ending (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
