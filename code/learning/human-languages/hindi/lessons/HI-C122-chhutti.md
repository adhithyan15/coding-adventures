# छुट्टी (chuṭṭī) — a holiday, a day off, leave

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C121-CARE-04, HI-LEX-C121-CARE-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Hindi for come again, then the Hindi for let's go.

## You'll want to know: छुट्टी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C122-LATE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छुट्टी** (*chuṭṭī*) — "a holiday", "a day off", "leave from work".

**आज छुट्टी है** — "today is a holiday", with the **आज** you know.

## What you've built

A day off.
