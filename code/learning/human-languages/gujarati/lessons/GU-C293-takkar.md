## You'll want to know: ટક્કર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C293-AKASMAT293-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ટક્કર** — *ṭakkar* — "a collision".

> બસ અને ગાડીની ટક્કર થઈ.
>
> *bas ane gāḍīnī ṭakkar thaī.*
>
> A bus and a car collided.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to *ṭakkarā*, "a blow on the head", and CDIAL lists Gujarati *ṭakkar*, "a blow" (CDIAL 5424). Two vehicles have a **ટક્કર**: **બસ અને ગાડીની ટક્કર થઈ**.
