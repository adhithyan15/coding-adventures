## You'll want to know: ਸਾਉਣੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C300-CROP300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਉਣੀ** — *sāuṇī* — "the autumn harvest; the monsoon-sown crop".

> ਮੱਕੀ ਸਾਉਣੀ ਦੀ ਫ਼ਸਲ ਹੈ।
>
> *makkī sāuṇī dī fasal hai.*
>
> Maize is a crop of the autumn harvest.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਉਣੀ** (*sāuṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śrāvaṇika*, "relating to the month Śrāvaṇa"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sāuṇī*, "the autumn crop", under it (CDIAL 12700).
