## You'll want to know: ಸಾರಾಂಶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C347-GOSHTHI347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರಾಂಶ** — *sārāṃśa* — "a summary; the gist".

> ಸುದ್ದಿಗೋಷ್ಠಿಯ ಸಾರಾಂಶ ರಾತ್ರಿಯ ವಾರ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂತು.
>
> *suddigōṣṭhiya sārāṃśa rātriya vārteyalli bantu.*
>
> A summary of the press conference came on the night news.

ಸಾರ is the essence: the essential part of something said or written.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರಾಂಶ** (*sārāṃśa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *sāra*, "the core, the essence", and *aṃśa*, "a part, a share" (Monier-Williams).
