German, chapter 323: Schälen und schneiden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
schälen — to peel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to measure out, then the German for the bowl.

You'll want to know: schälen.
schälen — "to peel".
Zuerst schälte er die Kartoffeln. First he peeled the potatoes.
Built on die Schale — peel, skin; also a bowl.

The word, taken apart.
schälen is built on Schale "peel, shell", an inherited Germanic word from Old High German scāla "shell, bowl" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
würfeln — to dice; to throw dice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to peel, then the German for to chop.

You'll want to know: würfeln.
würfeln — "to dice; to throw dice".
Dann würfelte er die Karotten. Then he diced the carrots.
To cut into small cubes. With dice, würfeln is to throw them.

The word, taken apart.
würfeln is built on Würfel "a dice", Old High German wurfil, Proto-Germanic wurpilaz (EtymDB), from the root of werfen "to throw".

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: halbieren.
halbieren — "to halve, to cut in half".

Built on halb with the borrowed ending -ieren: hat halbiert.

The word, taken apart.
halbieren is built on halb "half", Old High German halb, Proto-Germanic halbaz (EtymDB), with the ending -ieren.
