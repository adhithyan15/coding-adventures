## The word, taken apart — a textbook cognate
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-ETYMON-DAR]; assesses=[] -->

**در** continues Old Persian **duvar-**, "door, gate," from Indo-European
\**dʰwer-*. Few Indo-European words are this well attested across every
branch of the family at once: English **door**, German **Tür**, Latin
**foris** (→ **foreign**, "outside the door"), Greek **thýra**, and Sanskrit
**dvāra** all continue the identical ancient word. **در** is Persian's
contribution to a cognate set most Indo-European languages still carry.
