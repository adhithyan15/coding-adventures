## You'll want to know: ಜಾಗೃತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C403-SAMAJA403-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಗೃತಿ** — *jāgṛti* — "awareness".

> ಆರೋಗ್ಯದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಜನರಲ್ಲಿ ಜಾಗೃತಿ ಬೇಕು.
>
> *ārōgyada bagge janaralli jāgṛti bēku.*
>
> People need awareness about health.

ಜಾಗೃತಿ ಮೂಡಿಸು, to raise awareness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಗೃತಿ** (*jāgṛti*) is built on Sanskrit *jāgṛ*, "to be awake, to watch" (Monier-Williams): a waking up.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
