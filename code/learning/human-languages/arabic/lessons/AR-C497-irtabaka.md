## You'll want to know: ارتبك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C497-TAWATTUR497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتبك** — *irtabaka* — "to get flustered".

> ارتبك الطالب ونسي الجواب.
>
> *irtabaka aṭ-ṭālib wa-nasiya al-jawāb.*
>
> The student got flustered and forgot the answer.

Present: يرتبك, *yartabik*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتبك** (*irtabaka*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-b-k*, "to be mixed up, entangled" (Lane). Form VIII irtabaka is to become tangled: to lose one's thread.
