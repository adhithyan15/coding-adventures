# *hola* — one supported copy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTHO-HOLA-01, ES-SOUND-H-SILENT] -->

Point to the first letter in **hola**. It is written **h** and it is said as
silence.

Keep the model on the page for the whole of this lesson. You are not being
asked to remember anything today.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTHO-HOLA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **hola** once. You may look back at the model after every single letter —
that is what "guided" means here.

When you finish, touch your own **h-o-l-a** and say *OH-la*.

Your eye checks four letters. Your voice makes three sounds. The hand writes
one letter the mouth never says.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ORTHO-HOLA-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"ES-W00-hola-guided-copy-check","kind":"text","assesses":["ES-ORTHO-HOLA-01"],"prompt":"Copy the visible word hola once, then report how many letters you wrote.","answer":"hola — four letters","accepted":["hola","4","four"],"feedback":{"correct":"Good: one supported four-letter copy is enough today.","incorrect":"Look back at the model and copy h-o-l-a once."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Compare only the order of the four letters and whether the **h** is there.
Repair one letter if you need to, then stop. The model stays visible today.
