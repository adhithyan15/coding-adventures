Urdu, chapter 534: On the Board.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بساط (bisāt) — a game board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a move (in a game), then the Urdu for to turn over.

You'll want to know: بساط.
بساط — bisāt — "a game board".
اگر بساط ہے تو ہم شطرنج کھیلیں گے۔ agar bisāt hai to ham shatranj kheleṅ ge. If you have a board, we'll play chess.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بساط (bisāt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic bisāṭ, "a carpet, something spread out".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a piece (in a board game), then the Urdu for a pawn.

You'll want to know: مات.
مات — māt — "checkmate".

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
مات (māt) is usually traced to Persian māt, "helpless, beaten", as in shāh māt, "the king is helpless".
