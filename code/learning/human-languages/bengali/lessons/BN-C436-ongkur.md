## You'll want to know: অঙ্কুর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C436-PHOTA436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অঙ্কুর** — *ôngkur* — "a sprout; a shoot".

> বৃষ্টির পরে বীজ থেকে অঙ্কুর বেরোল।
>
> *brishṭir pôre bīj theke ôngkur berolô.*
>
> After the rain shoots came up from the seeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অঙ্কুর** (*ôngkur*) is Sanskrit *aṅkurá*, "a sprout, a shoot" (CDIAL 109), taken over whole; CDIAL also lists the inherited Bengali *ã̄kur*.
