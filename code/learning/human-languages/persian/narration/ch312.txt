Persian, chapter 312: On air and in print.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منتشر کردن (montasher kardan) — to publish, to release.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for details, then the Persian for a news agency.

You'll want to know: منتشر کردن.
منتشر کردن (montasher kardan) — "to publish, to release".
روزنامه عکس های جشن را منتشر کرد. Ruznâme 'aks-hâ-ye jashn râ montasher kard. The newspaper published the photos of the celebration.
Present montasher mikonad; the passive is montasher shod. montasher means "spread, published".

The word, taken apart.
montasher is usually traced to Arabic muntashir, "spread out"; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
پخش کردن (pakhsh kardan) — to broadcast; to hand out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a news agency, then the Persian for to publish.

You'll want to know: پخش کردن.
پخش کردن (pakhsh kardan) — "to broadcast; to hand out".
تلویزیون مسابقه را زنده پخش کرد. Televiziyon mosâbeqe râ zende pakhsh kard. The television broadcast the match live.
Also "to hand out, to spread": shirini pakhsh kard "he handed out sweets". Present pakhsh mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
pakhsh "spread, scattered" is a native Persian word whose deeper origin is not settled; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گوینده (guyande) — a newsreader, an announcer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to publish, then the Persian for to broadcast.

You'll want to know: گوینده.
گوینده (guyande) — "a newsreader, an announcer".
گوینده (guyande) اخبار با صدای آرام صحبت میکرد. Guyande-ye akhbâr bâ sedâ-ye ârâm sohbat mikard. The newsreader spoke in a calm voice.
Literally "one who says": gu-, the present stem of goftan, + -ande.

The word, taken apart.
guyande is the present stem gu- of goftan "to say" with the native suffix -ande "one who". EtymDB lists the word with no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پوشش خبری (pushesh-e khabari) — news coverage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a newsreader, then the Persian for a viewer.

You'll want to know: پوشش خبری.
پوشش خبری (pushesh-e khabari) — "news coverage".
این جشن پوشش خبری زیادی داشت. In jashn pushesh-e khabari-ye ziyâdi dâsht. This celebration got a lot of news coverage.
pushesh "covering", from pushidan "to cover, to wear", + khabari "of the news".

The word, taken apart.
pushesh "covering" is native Persian, from pushidan "to cover, to wear". khabari adds the Persian -i to khabar "news", borrowed from Arabic khabar (EtymDB).
