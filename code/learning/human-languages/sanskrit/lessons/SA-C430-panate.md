## You'll want to know: पणते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C430-HATTA430-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पणते** — *paṇate* — "haggles; bargains".

> ग्राहकः हट्टे पणते।
>
> *grāhakaḥ haṭṭe paṇate.*
>
> The customer haggles at the bazaar.

Past (he …): सः हट्टे पणितवान्, *saḥ haṭṭe paṇitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पणते** (*paṇate*) comes from the root √paṇ, "to barter, to bargain", which also gives *paṇa*, "a stake, a coin"; MW gives the participle *paṇita*, "transacted as business" (MW).
