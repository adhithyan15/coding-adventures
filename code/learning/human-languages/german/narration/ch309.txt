German, chapter 309: Alles ist geschmückt.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
schmücken — to decorate, to adorn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the applause, then the German for to applaud.

You'll want to know: schmücken.
schmücken — "to decorate, to adorn".
Wir schmückten den Saal mit Blumen. We decorated the hall with flowers.
Der Schmuck — jewellery or decoration.

The word, taken apart.
schmücken is usually traced to Middle High German smücken, which EtymDB glosses "to dress, to adorn".

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 6.
aufhängen — to hang up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to decorate, then the German for the garland.

You'll want to know: aufhängen.
aufhängen — "to hang up".
Die Kinder hängten Bilder an der Wand auf. The children hung pictures on the wall.
Separable: er hängt auf. On the phone, to hang up.

The word, taken apart.
aufhängen joins auf "up" and hängen "to hang", an inherited Germanic verb and a cousin of English hang; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

das Feuerwerk — "the fireworks, the firework display".
Um Mitternacht gab es ein großes Feuerwerk. At midnight there was a big firework display.
Plural: die Feuerwerke. One rocket is eine Rakete.

The word, taken apart.
Feuerwerk joins Feuer "fire", a cousin of English fire (EtymDB), and Werk "work", both inherited Germanic words.
