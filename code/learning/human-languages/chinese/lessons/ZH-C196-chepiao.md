## You'll want to know: 车票
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C196-TICKET196-01]; assesses=[] -->

**车票** — *chēpiào* — "train or bus ticket".

> **车票多少钱** *chēpiào duōshao qián* — How much is the ticket?

The new character, in pieces: 票 — eleven strokes: on top a bar over a frame with two short verticals; under it 二, a hooked vertical and two dots. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**车票** (*chēpiào*) is built from 车 'vehicle' and 票 'a ticket'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the second, so its history is not settled.
