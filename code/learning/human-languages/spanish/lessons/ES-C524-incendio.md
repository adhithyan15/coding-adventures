## You'll want to know: el incendio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C524-FUEGO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el incendio** — "the fire (big, destructive)".

> El incendio empezó en la cocina.
>
> *The fire started in the kitchen.*

*El fuego* is fire in general; *un incendio* is a fire that burns a building or a forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incendio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *incendium* 'a fire, a blaze' (EtymDB), built on *incendere* 'to set on fire'. English *incendiary* comes from the same verb.
