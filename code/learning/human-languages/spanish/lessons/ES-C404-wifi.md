# el wifi — the connection with a deliberately catchy name

<!-- Calibration: preserve HL24 section 7.2. Do not present Wireless Fidelity
as either the original expansion or a wholly invented myth, and do not attach a
precise duration to the Alliance's marketing line. -->

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-INTERNET] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *internet*. (*Internet*.)

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- In much of the Spanish-speaking world you will hear *WEE-fee*. **El wifi** is
  widely used; **la wifi** also occurs in parts of the Spanish-speaking world.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-WIFI]; assesses=[] -->

**el wifi** — “the Wi-Fi” or “Wi-Fi.”

The name was coined to sound like **Hi-Fi**. The phrase **Wireless Fidelity**
was attached afterward as marketing by the industry alliance and was later
dropped. That history is why treating the phrase as the name's simple original
expansion is misleading, while pretending the Alliance never used it also goes
too far.
