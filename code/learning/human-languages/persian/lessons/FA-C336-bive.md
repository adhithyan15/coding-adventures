## You'll want to know: بیوه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C336-TALAQ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بیوه** (*bive*) — "a widow; widowed".

> مادربزرگم سی سال بیوه بود.
>
> *Mâdarbozorgam si sâl bive bud.*
>
> My grandmother was a widow for thirty years.

Mostly of women; *mard-e bive* is "a widower".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bive* is a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent, so its deeper origin is not settled here.
