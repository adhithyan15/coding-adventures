## You'll want to know: expresar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C604-PROTESTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**expresar** — "to express".

> Quiero expresar mi opinión.
>
> *I want to express my opinion.*

*Expresarse* — to express yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*expresar* is built on *expreso* (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *expressus* 'pressed out, clear', from *exprimere*, built on *premere* 'to press'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
