Portuguese, chapter 408: Peões e radares.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
o peão — the pedestrian; the pawn (in chess).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the screw, then the Portuguese for to unscrew.

You'll want to know: o peão.
o peão — "the pedestrian; the pawn (in chess)".
Esta rua é só para peões. This street is only for pedestrians.
In Brazil: o pedestre.

The word, taken apart.
peão is inherited from Late Latin pedō, pedōnis 'foot soldier', built on pēs 'foot' (EtymDB), which also lists Spanish peón and French pion as its cousins.

Lesson 2 of 6.
a passadeira — the zebra crossing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to unscrew, then the Portuguese for the pedestrian.

You'll want to know: a passadeira.
a passadeira — "the zebra crossing".
O carro parou para nós na passadeira. The car stopped for us at the zebra crossing.
In Brazil: a faixa de pedestres. Uma passadeira is also a long, narrow rug.

The word, taken apart.
passadeira is built on passar 'to cross, to pass' (EtymDB) with -deira, a place for doing it; passar goes back to Latin passus 'step'.

You'll want to know: atropelar.
atropelar — "to run over, to knock down".
Um carro quase atropelou um cão na passadeira. A car almost ran over a dog on the zebra crossing.
Ser atropelado — to be run over.

The word, taken apart.
atropelar is built from a- and tropel 'rush, trampling crowd'. The origin of tropel is not settled; EtymDB has no entry for these words.

Lesson 4 of 6.
o radar — the speed camera; the radar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the zebra crossing, then the Portuguese for to run over.

You'll want to know: o radar.
o radar — "the speed camera; the radar".
Há um radar à entrada da cidade. There is a speed camera at the entrance to the town.
Also the radar of a ship or an airport.

The word, taken apart.
radar was borrowed from English radar, a word made from the first letters of radio detection and ranging (EtymDB lists English radar).

Lesson 5 of 6.
fiscalizar — to inspect, to check (officially).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to run over, then the Portuguese for the speed camera.

You'll want to know: fiscalizar.
fiscalizar — "to inspect, to check (officially)".
A polícia fiscaliza a velocidade nesta estrada. The police check speeds on this road.
A fiscalização — the inspection, the checks.

The word, taken apart.
fiscalizar is built on fiscal 'inspector', from fisco 'treasury', which goes back to Latin fiscus 'basket; treasury' (EtymDB).
