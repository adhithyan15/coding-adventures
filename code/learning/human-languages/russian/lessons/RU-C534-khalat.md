## You'll want to know: халат
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C534-MORNING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**халат** (*khalát*) — "a dressing gown; a white coat".

> Утром папа пьёт кофе в халате.
>
> *Útrom pápa pyot kófe v khaláte.*
>
> In the morning Dad drinks his coffee in his dressing gown.

Masculine. Also a doctor's or a cook's white coat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *халат*, glossed 'dressing gown; robe', without a source; it is usually traced through a Turkic language to Arabic *xilʿa* 'a robe of honour'.
