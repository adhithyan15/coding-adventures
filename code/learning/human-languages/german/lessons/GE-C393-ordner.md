## You'll want to know: der Ordner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C393-DATEI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ordner** — "the folder; the ring binder".

> Alle Fotos sind in einem Ordner auf dem Computer.
>
> *All the photos are in a folder on the computer.*

Plural: *die Ordner*. In an office, *der Ordner* is a ring binder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ordner* is formed with *-er* on *ordnen* "to put in order", inherited from Old High German *ordinōn*, an early borrowing from Latin *ōrdināre* (EtymDB), from *ōrdo* "row, order".
