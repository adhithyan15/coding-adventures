## You'll want to know: lutulentus, lutulenta, lutulentum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C426-LUTULENTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lutulentus, lutulenta, lutulentum** — "muddy, covered with mud".

> Post pluviam via lutulenta est, et plaustra lentē eunt.
>
> *After the rain the road is muddy, and the wagons go slowly.*

An adjective of three endings, from *lutum* 'mud'. Also of a dirty, low person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lutulentus* is built on *lutum* 'mud' with the suffix *-ulentus* 'full of' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the same pattern as *pulverulentus* 'dusty'.
