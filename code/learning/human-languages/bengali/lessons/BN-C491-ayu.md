## You'll want to know: আয়ু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C491-SHRADDHO491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**আয়ু** — *āyu* — "lifespan; length of life".

> ভালো খাবার আর ব্যায়ামে আয়ু বাড়ে।
>
> *bhālo khābār ār bæyāme āyu bāṛe.*
>
> Good food and exercise lengthen your life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আয়ু** (*āyu*) is Sanskrit *āyus*, "life, vigour, length of life" (Monier-Williams), with the final *-s* dropped.
