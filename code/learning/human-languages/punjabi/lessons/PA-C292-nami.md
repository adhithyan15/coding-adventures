## You'll want to know: ਨਮੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C292-DAMP292-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਮੀ** — *namī* — "moisture; humidity".

> ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਨਮੀ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *samundar de neṛe havā vich bahut namī hundī hai.*
>
> Near the sea there is a lot of moisture in the air.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਮੀ** (*namī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *namī*, "dampness", built on *nam*, "damp".
