## You'll want to know: ਕਮਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C258-LOAN258-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਮਾਉਣਾ** — *kamāuṇā* — "to earn".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੰਗੇ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਮਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ daftar vich chaṅge paise kamāundī hai.*
>
> My sister earns good money at the office.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਮਾਏ, *maiṁ bahut paise kamāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਮਾਉਣਾ** (*kamāuṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*karmāpayati*, "works, earns"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kamāuṇā*, "to work, earn", under it (CDIAL 2897).
