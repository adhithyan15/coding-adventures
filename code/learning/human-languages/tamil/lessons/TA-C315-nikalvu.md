## You'll want to know: நிகழ்வு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C315-NADAIPERU315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**நிகழ்வு** — *nikaḻvu* — "an event".

> அந்த நிகழ்வை என்னால் மறக்க முடியாது.
>
> *anta nikaḻvai eṉṉāl maṟakka muṭiyātu.*
>
> I cannot forget that event.

From நிகழ், to happen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நிகழ்வு** (*nikaḻvu*) is native Dravidian: a noun from நிகழ், "to happen, occur" (DEDR 3661).
