## You'll want to know: गुहा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C336-LENI336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गुहा** — *guhā* — "a cave".

> वाघ गुहेत झोपला होता.
>
> *vāgh guhet jhoplā hotā.*
>
> The tiger was asleep in the cave.

Feminine.

The oblique is गुहे-: गुहेत, in the cave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुहा** (*guhā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *guhā*, 'a hiding place, a cave' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
