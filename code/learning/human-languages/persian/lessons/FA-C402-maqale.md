## You'll want to know: مقاله
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C402-TAHQIQ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مقاله** (*maqâle*) — "an article, a paper".

> او یک مقاله درباره آب نوشت.
>
> *u yek maqâle darbâre-ye âb nevesht.*
>
> He wrote an article about water.

Plural *maqâlât* or *maqâlehâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقاله** was borrowed from Arabic *maqāla* "article, essay" (EtymDB).
