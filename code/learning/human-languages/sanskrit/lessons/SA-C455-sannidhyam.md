## You'll want to know: सान्निध्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C455-ANURAGA455-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सान्निध्यम्** — *sānnidhyam* — "nearness; presence".

> मातुः सान्निध्ये शिशुः शान्तः भवति।
>
> *mātuḥ sānnidhye śiśuḥ śāntaḥ bhavati.*
>
> In its mother's presence the baby is calm.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सान्निध्यम्** (*sānnidhyam*) is built on *saṃnidhi*, "nearness", from *sam*, *ni* and the root √dhā, "to put", with the lengthening of its first vowel; MW gives "the being near, nearness, vicinity, presence" (MW).
