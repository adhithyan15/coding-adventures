Spanish, chapter 403: Chicken, Vegetables and Paella.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el pollo — chicken, not dust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the meat. (La carne.)

Sounds you'll need.
PO-yo for most speakers: written ll commonly has the sound of English y. Keep the final vowel round and clear.

The word, taken apart.
el pollo — “the chicken,” especially chicken as food.
Latin pullus meant a young animal. Spanish pollo comes from that word. Keep it distinct from polvo, “dust,” which comes from unrelated Latin pulvis. The useful listening contrast is only pollo / polvo: the first has -ll-, the second has -lv-.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: el pollo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pollo, no polvo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: carne de pollo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the chicken, then contrast it with dust. (El pollo; pollo, no polvo.)
Sources: DLE: pollo and DLE: polvo.


Lesson 2 of 5.
la verdura — the green growth that became vegetables.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Name chicken and fruit. (El pollo, la fruta.)

Sounds you'll need.
ver-DU-ra: tap the single r once; the stress falls on du.

The word, taken apart.
la verdura — “the vegetables” or “the greens.”
The word is built on verde, from Latin viridis, “green.” The food sense grew from green plant growth. But verde cannot be reduced to a colour in every context: Spanish can also use it of an indecent or erotic joke, and the dictionary records a marked secondary sense of verdura. Here the context is plainly food.

[your turn — say: la verdura]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: fruta y verdura]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pollo con verdura]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Give the supermarket pair: fruit and vegetables. (Fruta y verdura.)
Sources: DLE: verdura and DLE: verde.


Lesson 3 of 5.
la paella — the pan that named the dish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say to cook and the vegetables. (Cocinar, la verdura.)

Sounds you'll need.
pa-E-ya for most speakers: keep three clear beats, with the stress on e.

The word, taken apart.
la paella — “paella,” the Valencian rice dish.
The dish took the name of its pan. Valencian paella came through Old French paele from Latin patella, a small pan or dish. Catalan also has native padella; the different middle consonant is evidence that paella made the French detour rather than wearing down inside Catalan.
