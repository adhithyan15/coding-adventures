## You'll want to know: भाण्डागारम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C435-DINARA435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भाण्डागारम्** — *bhāṇḍāgāram* — "a storehouse".

> नगरस्य भाण्डागारे बहु धान्यम् अस्ति।
>
> *nagarasya bhāṇḍāgāre bahu dhānyam asti.*
>
> There is a lot of grain in the city's storehouse.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाण्डागारम्** (*bhāṇḍāgāram*) is built from *bhāṇḍa*, "a vessel, wares", and *āgāra*, "a house, a room"; MW gives the compound as a treasury, a storehouse (MW).
