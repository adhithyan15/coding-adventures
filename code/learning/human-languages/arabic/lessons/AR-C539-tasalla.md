## You'll want to know: تسلى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C539-LUAB539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلى** — *tasallā* — "to have fun".

> تسلينا بلعب الورق في المساء.
>
> *tasallaynā bi-laʿib al-waraq fī al-masāʾ.*
>
> We had fun playing cards in the evening.

Present: يتسلى, *yatasallā*.

لعب الورق is playing cards.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلى** (*tasallā*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-l-w*, "to forget, to be consoled" (Lane). Form V tasallā is to take one's mind off things.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
