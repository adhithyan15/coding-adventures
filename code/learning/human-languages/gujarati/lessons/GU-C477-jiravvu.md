## You'll want to know: જીરવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C477-ODKAR477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જીરવવું** — *jīravvũ* — "to stomach; to digest (food or bad news)".

> આવું દુઃખ જીરવવું અઘરું છે.
>
> *āvũ duḥkh jīravvũ agharũ che.*
>
> Such sorrow is hard to bear.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દુઃખ જીરવ્યું, *teṇe duḥkh jīravyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જીરવવું** (*jīravvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*jīrayati*, "digests"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to digest" (CDIAL 5236).
