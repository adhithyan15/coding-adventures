## You'll want to know: il funerale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C313-LUTTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il funerale** — "the funeral".

> Al funerale c'era tutto il paese.
>
> *The whole village was at the funeral.*

Often plural: *i funerali*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*funerale* is inherited from Late Latin *fūnerālis* (EtymDB), built on *fūnus* "a burial".
