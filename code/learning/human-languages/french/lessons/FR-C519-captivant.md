## You'll want to know: captivant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C519-ROMAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**captivant** — "gripping, captivating".

> J'ai lu un livre captivant pendant les vacances.
>
> *I read a gripping book during the holidays.*

Built on *captiver* "to captivate".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*captivant* is the present participle of *captiver* "to captivate", from Late Latin *captīvāre* "to take prisoner", built on *captīvus* "a captive", from *capere* "to take" (Lewis & Short).
