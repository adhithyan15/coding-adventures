## You'll want to know: आलोचयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C545-ANUKRAMA545-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आलोचयति** — *ālocayati* — "reviews; considers".

> यदि त्वं योजनाम् आलोचयसि, तर्हि कार्यं सम्यक् भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ yojanām ālocayasi, tarhi kāryaṃ samyak bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you go over the plan, the work will go well.

Past (he …): सः योजनाम् आलोचितवान्, *saḥ yojanām ālocitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आलोचयति** (*ālocayati*) is built on *ā* and the causative of the root √loc, "to see"; MW gives *ālocayati* as to behold, to consider, to reflect (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
