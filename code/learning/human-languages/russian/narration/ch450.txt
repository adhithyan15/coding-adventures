Russian, chapter 450: Certificates and forms.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
справка (správka) — a certificate, an official note; information.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to shave, then the Russian for a fringe.

You'll want to know: справка.
справка (správka) — "a certificate, an official note; information".
Для визы нужна справка с работы. Dlya vízy nuzhná správka s rabóty. For the visa you need a letter from your employer.
Feminine. Справочное бюро — an information desk.

The word, taken apart.
справка (správka) is built on справиться 'to inquire', from править 'to set right, to put in order'. EtymDB lists справка (správka) without a deeper source.

Lesson 2 of 6.
бланк (blank) — a form (to fill in).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fringe, then the Russian for a certificate.

You'll want to know: бланк.
бланк (blank) — "a form (to fill in)".
В банке мне дали бланк. V bánke mne dáli blank. At the bank they gave me a form.
Masculine. Заполнить бланк (blank) — to fill in a form.

The word, taken apart.
бланк is usually traced, probably through German, to French blanc 'white, blank' (EtymDB lists the French word): a sheet left blank to be filled in.

очередь (óchered) — "a queue; a turn".
На почте была длинная очередь. Na póchte bylá dlínnaya óchered. There was a long queue at the post office.
Feminine. Стоять в очереди — to stand in a queue; моя очередь (óchered) — my turn.

The word, taken apart.
очередь (óchered) is usually linked with череда 'a row, a turn in order'. EtymDB lists очередь (óchered) without a source, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 4 of 6.
оформлять (oformlyát) — to get (documents) done, to process; to design.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a form, then the Russian for a queue.

You'll want to know: оформлять.
оформлять (oformlyát) — "to get (documents) done, to process; to design".
Мы три дня оформляли визу. My tri dnya oformlyáli vízu. It took us three days to get the visa done.
Imperfective; perfective оформить (оформлю, оформишь).

The word, taken apart.
оформлять (oformlyát) is built from о- and форма 'a form, a shape', which is usually traced to Latin forma 'a shape' (Lewis & Short; EtymDB lists форма without a source): to give a matter its proper form.

Lesson 5 of 6.
заверять (zaveryát) — to certify; to assure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a queue, then the Russian for to get.

You'll want to know: заверять.
заверять (zaveryát) — "to certify; to assure".
Нотариус заверяет копию паспорта. Notárius zaveryáyet kópiyu pásporta. The notary certifies a copy of the passport.
Imperfective; perfective заверить. Заверить копию — to certify a copy.

The word, taken apart.
заверять (zaveryát) is built from за- and -верять, from верить 'to believe', on вера 'faith'. EtymDB lists верить without a deeper source.
