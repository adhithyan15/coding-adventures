## You'll want to know: सांत्वना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C463-SUKUN463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सांत्वना** — *sāntvanā* — "consolation; words of comfort".

> दोस्तों की सांत्वना से मीरा का मन शांत हुआ।
>
> *dostoṁ kī sāntvanā se Mīrā kā man śānt huā.*
>
> Her friends' words of comfort calmed Mira's mind.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सांत्वना** (*sāntvanā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sāntvanā*, which Monier-Williams explains as calming someone with gentle words, or consolation (Monier-Williams). It is what one offers a person in grief: *sāntvanā denā*.
