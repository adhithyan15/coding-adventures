# jiùmìng — help!; the cry for help

## You'll want to know: jiùmìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C298-FIRE298-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiùmìng* — "help!; the cry for help".

> *tā dà hǎn jiùmìng* — He shouted for help.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiùmìng* is built from 救 'to save' and *mìng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjuwH* and *mjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to order' and 'to send'; its sense 'a life' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
