## You'll want to know: der Passant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C436-GASSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Passant** — "the passer-by".

> Ein Passant zeigt uns den Weg zum Museum.
>
> *A passer-by shows us the way to the museum.*

Weak noun: *den Passanten*. A woman: *die Passantin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Passant* was borrowed from French *passant* 'passing (by)' (EtymDB), from *passer* 'to pass', which goes back to Latin *passus* 'step'.
