## You'll want to know: كفى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C406-TATALLUB406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**كفى** — *kafā* — "to suffice".

> يكفي هذا الطعام لثلاثة أشخاص.
>
> *yakfī hādhā aṭ-ṭaʿām li-thalāthat ashkhāṣ.*
>
> This food is enough for three people.

Present: يكفي, *yakfī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كفى** (*kafā*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-f-y*, usually traced to the sense "to be enough". *Kafā* is a Form I verb with a weak last letter: to be enough. Its present is *yakfī*.
