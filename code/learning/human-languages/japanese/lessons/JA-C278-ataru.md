## You'll want to know: あたる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C278-INAZUMA278-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あたる** — *ataru* — "to strike; to hit (a target)".

> **いしが くるまの まどに あたりました。** — *ishi ga kuruma no mado ni atarimashita* — "A stone hit the car window."

Polite **あたります**, *atarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あめが まどに あたりました。**, *ame ga mado ni atarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あたる** (*ataru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
