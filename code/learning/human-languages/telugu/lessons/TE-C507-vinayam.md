## You'll want to know: వినయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C507-SVABHAVAM507-04]; assesses=[] -->

**వినయం** — *vinayaṁ* — "humility; modesty".

> అంత డబ్బు ఉన్నా ఆయనకు వినయం ఎక్కువ.
>
> *anta ḍabbu unnā āyanaku vinayaṁ ekkuva.*
>
> Though he has so much money, he is very humble.

వినయంగా, humbly, politely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వినయం** (*vinayaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vinaya*, "guidance, good breeding" (CDIAL 11767).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
