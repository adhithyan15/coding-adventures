## You'll want to know: प्रदर्शन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C539-DAD539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रदर्शन** — *pradarśan* — "an exhibition; a show".

> शहरात चित्रांचं मोठं प्रदर्शन भरलं होतं.
>
> *śahrāt citrāñcaṁ moṭhaṁ pradarśan bharlaṁ hotaṁ.*
>
> A big exhibition of paintings was on in the city.

Neuter.

प्रदर्शन भरणे, an exhibition is held.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रदर्शन** (*pradarśan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pradarśana*, "appearance, display" (Monier-Williams).
