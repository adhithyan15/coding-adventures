## You'll want to know: обязательно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C375-NEOBKHODIMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**обязательно** (*obyazátelno*) — "definitely, without fail".

> Я обязательно позвоню тебе завтра.
>
> *Ya obyazátelno pozvonyú tebé závtra.*
>
> I will definitely call you tomorrow.

It also means necessarily: *не обязательно* — not necessarily.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**обязательно** is built on *обязать* "to oblige", from *о-* and *вязать* "to tie, to bind", Proto-Slavic *\*vęzati*: bound to happen.
