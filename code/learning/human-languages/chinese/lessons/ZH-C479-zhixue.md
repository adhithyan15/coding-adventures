## You'll want to know: zhǐxuè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C479-BLOOD479-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐxuè* — "to stop the bleeding".

> *yīshēng yòng le shí fēnzhōng cái zhǐxuè* — It took the doctor ten minutes to stop the bleeding.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐxuè* is built from *zhǐ* 'to stop' and 血 'blood', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyiX* and *xwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stop'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how it was.
