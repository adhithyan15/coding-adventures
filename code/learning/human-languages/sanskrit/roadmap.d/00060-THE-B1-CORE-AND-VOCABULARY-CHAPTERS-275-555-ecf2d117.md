## The B1 core and vocabulary, chapters 275–555 *(authored)*

Chapters 275–285 add the future, the -tavat past, the present with स्म,
reasons, travel, feelings and conditions, the track's first running
sentences, with four letters (घ ढ औ ई). Chapters 286–555 add the 1,350-word
B1 vocabulary in five tranches, and Sanskrit attains B1 on the level gate.
Still ahead: the passive, the perfect and the aorist, vowel sandhi across
words, the case forms the track has not yet shown (genitive and dative
plurals, the oblique dual, s- and n-stem obliques), ऊ ऐ झ ळ, and the four B2
spine nodes.
