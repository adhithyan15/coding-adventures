Lesson 1 of 5.
कोमलः (komalaḥ) — tender, soft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for surely, then the Sanskrit for certainly.

You'll want to know: कोमलः.
कोमलः — komalaḥ — "tender, soft".

The word, taken apart.
komala is of uncertain origin. MW offers, with a question mark, ko-, a form of ku- "little", with mala from √mlai "to fade", so "easily fading". It lives on as Gujarati komaḷ, "soft" (CDIAL 3523).

Lesson 2 of 5.
वामनः (vāmanaḥ) — dwarfish, short.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for certainly, then the Sanskrit for tender.

You'll want to know: वामनः.
वामनः — vāmanaḥ — "dwarfish, short".

The word, taken apart.
vāmana is of uncertain origin: MW calls it "of doubtful derivation". It is the name of Viṣṇu's dwarf form, and it lives on as Hindi baunā, "dwarf" (CDIAL 11538).

Lesson 3 of 5.
विशालः (viśālaḥ) — vast, broad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tender, then the Sanskrit for dwarfish.

You'll want to know: विशालः.
विशालः — viśālaḥ — "vast, broad".

The word, taken apart.
viśāla probably comes from the root √viś, "to enter, settle", MW says, though others derive it from vi- with √śṛ. The derivation is not settled. Bengali keeps it as bishāl, "huge".

सूक्ष्मः (sūkṣmaḥ) — fine, subtle.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: सूक्ष्मः.
सूक्ष्मः — sūkṣmaḥ — "fine, subtle".

The word, taken apart.
sūkṣma is probably connected with sūci, "needle", MW says: fine as a needle's point. CDIAL 13546 lists it with Pali sukhuma, "subtle, minute".

Lesson 5 of 5.
स्थिरः (sthiraḥ) — steady.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for vast, then the Sanskrit for fine.

You'll want to know: स्थिरः.
स्थिरः — sthiraḥ — "steady".

The word, taken apart.
sthira is filed by MW under the root √sthā, "to stand", which MW compares with Latin stāre and English stand. CDIAL 13771 links it rather to a related root sthar-. Either way it lives on as Hindi thir, "firm, fixed".
