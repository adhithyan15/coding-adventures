## You'll want to know: نشانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C444-NESHANI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نشانه** (*neshâne*) — "a landmark, a sign".

> مسجد بزرگ نشانه خوبی است.
>
> *Masjed-e bozorg neshâne-ye khubi ast.*
>
> The big mosque is a good landmark.

Also a sign or symptom: *neshâne-ye bimâri*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*neshâne* is built from native *neshân* 'sign, mark', usually traced to Middle Persian *nišān*, with the ending *-e*.
