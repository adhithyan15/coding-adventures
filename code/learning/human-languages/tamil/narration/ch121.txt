Tamil, chapter 121: Clock, Skirt, Sari, Lentils, Curry.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
கடிகாரம் (kaṭikāram) — a clock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a pen, then the Tamil for paper.
[pause 2 seconds]
Tell the time, ஒரு மணி, இரண்டு மணி, then name the afternoon three ways: நடுப்பகல், மதியம், பிற்பகல்.

You'll want to know: கடிகாரம்.
கடிகாரம் — kaṭikāram — "a clock".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kaṭikāram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kaṭikāram, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kaṭikāram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for a pen, then the Tamil for paper, then say kaṭikāram again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does கடிகாரம் (kaṭikāram) mean? ("A clock".) Say it once more.

[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: straight ahead, near, distance; far, behind, in front]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: in the middle, a street, a bridge, a park, a temple]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a library, a bank, a market, a beach, an office]

[your turn — say: a garden, a corner, a turn, can, a skill]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Straight ahead? (நேராக, nērāka.) Near? (அருகில், arukil.) Distance; far? (தூரம், tūram.) Behind? (பின்னால், piṉṉāl.) In front? (முன்னால், muṉṉāl.) In the middle? (நடுவில், naṭuvil.) A street? (தெரு, teru.) A bridge? (பாலம், pālam.) A park? (பூங்கா, pūṅkā.) A temple? (கோயில், kōyil.) A library? (நூலகம், nūlakam.) A bank? (வங்கி, vaṅki.) A market? (சந்தை, cantai.) A beach? (கடற்கரை, kaṭaṟkarai.) An office? (அலுவலகம், aluvalakam.) A garden? (தோட்டம், tōṭṭam.) A corner? (மூலை, mūlai.) A turn? (திருப்பம், tiruppam.) Can? (முடியும், muṭiyum.) A skill? (திறமை, tiṟamai.)
