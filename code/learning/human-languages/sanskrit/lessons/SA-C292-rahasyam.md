## You'll want to know: रहस्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C292-CARA292-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रहस्यम्** — *rahasyam* — "a secret".

> चरः रहस्यं जानाति।
>
> *caraḥ rahasyaṃ jānāti.*
>
> The spy knows the secret.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रहस्यम्** (*rahasyam*) is built on *rahas*, "a lonely place, secrecy"; MW gives *rahasya* as secret and, as a noun, a secret or mystery (MW).
