## You'll want to know: रक्कम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C426-ROKAD426-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रक्कम** — *rakkam* — "a sum of money".

> ही रक्कम खूप मोठी आहे.
>
> *hī rakkam khūp moṭhī āhe.*
>
> This sum is very large.

Feminine.

A feminine noun: मोठी रक्कम.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रक्कम** (*rakkam*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *raqam*, "a written figure".
