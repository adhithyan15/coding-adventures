## You'll want to know: のりば
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C200-CHANGE200-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のりば** — *noriba* — "a boarding point; a bus or taxi stand".

> **のりばは えきの まえです。** — *noriba wa eki no mae desu* — "The boarding point is in front of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のりば** (*noriba*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *nori*, "riding", and *ba*, "place", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict).
