## You'll want to know: ménlíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C522-FIXIT522-04]; assesses=[] -->

*ménlíng* — "doorbell".

> *wǒmen jiā de ménlíng huài le sān tiān le* — Our doorbell has been broken for three days.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ménlíng* is built from 门 'a door' and *líng* 'a bell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mwon* and *leng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as like a bell, but smaller.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how it was.
