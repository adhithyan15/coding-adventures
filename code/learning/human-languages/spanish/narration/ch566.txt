Spanish, chapter 566: Una aventura.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la anécdota — the anecdote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the episode, then the Spanish for the ending.

You'll want to know: la anécdota.
la anécdota — "the anecdote".
Me contó una anécdota muy graciosa. He told me a very funny anecdote.

The word, taken apart.
anécdota was borrowed from French anecdote, which comes from Greek anékdota 'unpublished things' (EtymDB), built on an- 'not' and ékdotos 'given out, published'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la aventura — the adventure; the affair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the ending, then the Spanish for the anecdote.

You'll want to know: la aventura.
la aventura — "the adventure; the affair".
El viaje fue toda una aventura. The trip was quite an adventure.
It can also mean a love affair.

The word, taken apart.
aventura comes from spoken Latin adventūra 'the things that are going to happen' (EtymDB), from advenīre 'to arrive', built on venīre 'to come'.

The word, taken apart.
hazaña comes from Andalusi Arabic ḥasána 'a good deed', from Arabic ḥasana (EtymDB). The good deed grew into a heroic one.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el récord — the record (best result).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the adventure, then the Spanish for the feat.

You'll want to know: el récord.
el récord — "the record (best result)".
Tiene el récord del mundo. He holds the world record.
Tener el récord — to hold the record. A record in writing is un registro.

The word, taken apart.
récord was borrowed from English record (EtymDB), which came from Old French recorder 'to recall', from Latin recordārī 'to call to mind'.
