German, chapter 459: Auf dem Land.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Bauernhof — the farm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the stag, then the German for to chirp.

You'll want to know: der Bauernhof.
der Bauernhof — "the farm".
Im Urlaub wohnen wir auf einem Bauernhof. On holiday we stay on a farm.
Plural: die Bauernhöfe.

The word, taken apart.
Bauernhof is built from Bauer 'farmer', inherited from Old High German būr (EtymDB), and Hof 'yard, farm'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Weide — the pasture (for animals); the willow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to chirp, then the German for the farm.

You'll want to know: die Weide.
die Weide — "the pasture (for animals); the willow".
Die Kühe stehen den ganzen Tag auf der Weide. The cows stand in the pasture all day.
Plural: die Weiden. Die Weide is also a willow tree, a different word.

The word, taken apart.
Weide 'pasture' is inherited from Old High German weida (EtymDB). Weide 'willow' is a separate inherited word, Old High German wīda (EtymDB).
