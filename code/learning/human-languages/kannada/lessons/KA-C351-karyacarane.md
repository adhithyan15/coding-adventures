## You'll want to know: ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C351-KARYA351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ** — *kāryācaraṇe* — "a mission; an operation".

> ಪ್ರವಾಹದಲ್ಲಿ ರಕ್ಷಣಾ ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ ಎರಡು ದಿನ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *pravāhadalli rakṣaṇā kāryācaraṇe eraḍu dina naḍeyitu.*
>
> The rescue operation in the flood went on for two days.

Used of rescue, police and army work: ರಕ್ಷಣಾ ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ, a rescue operation. In a flood it brings food and ಔಷಧ, medicine, to people cut off by the water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ** (*kāryācaraṇe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of *kārya*, "a thing to be done, business" (CDIAL 3078), and *ācaraṇa*, "undertaking, performing" (Monier-Williams).
