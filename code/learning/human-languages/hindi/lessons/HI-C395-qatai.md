## You'll want to know: क़तई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C395-MANZURI395-05]; assesses=[] -->

**क़तई** — *qataī* — "at all, in the least (with नहीं)".

> अगर तुम देर करोगे तो मैं क़तई नहीं रुकूँगा।
>
> *agar tum der karoge to maiṁ qataī nahīṃ rukūṅgā.*
>
> If you're late, I won't wait at all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़तई** (*qataī*) is usually traced to Arabic *qaṭʿī*, "decisive, final", which reached Hindi through Persian.
