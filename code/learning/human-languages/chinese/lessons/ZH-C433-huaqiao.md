# huáqiáo — overseas Chinese; a Chinese person settled abroad

## You'll want to know: huáqiáo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C433-OVERSEAS433-04]; assesses=[] -->

*huáqiáo* — "overseas Chinese; a Chinese person settled abroad".

> *tā shì zhù zài hǎiwài de huáqiáo* — He's one of the Chinese who live overseas.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huáqiáo* is built from *huá* 'Chinese' and *qiáo* 'to live away from home', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwae* and *gjew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'flourishing' and the second 'to lodge' and 'a guest'; the sense 'Chinese' for the first is not given there.
