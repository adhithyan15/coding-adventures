Kannada, chapter 425: Accepting and Rejecting.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸು (svīkarisu) — to accept; to receive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for specific, then the Kannada for an audit.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸು.
ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸು — svīkarisu — "to accept; to receive".
ಅವಳು ಹೊಸ ಕೆಲಸದ ಆಹ್ವಾನವನ್ನು ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸಿದಳು. avaḷu hosa kelasada āhvānavannu svīkarisidaḷu. She accepted the offer of a new job.
Past (I …): ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ, svīkariside.
Formal. ಸ್ವೀಕಾರ, acceptance.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ವೀಕರಿಸು (svīkarisu) is built on Sanskrit svīkāra, "making one's own" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

ವೈದ್ಯರು ನನ್ನ ಆರೋಗ್ಯವನ್ನು ಪ್ರಮಾಣೀಕರಿಸಿದರು. vaidyaru nanna ārōgyavannu pramāṇīkarisidaru. The doctor certified my health.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಮಾಣೀಕರಿಸು (pramāṇīkarisu) is built on Sanskrit pramāṇa, "a standard, a proof" (CDIAL 8723), with -īkarisu, "to make into": to make proof of.

ಮಾನ್ಯತೆ — mānyate — "recognition; validity".
ಈ ಕಾಲೇಜಿಗೆ ಸರ್ಕಾರದ ಮಾನ್ಯತೆ ಇದೆ. ī kālējige sarkārada mānyate ide. This college is recognised by the government.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾನ್ಯತೆ (mānyate) is built on Sanskrit mānya, "to be respected or honoured" (Monier-Williams), with -tā, "-ness".
