# Two sentences after the model closes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-NAMASTE, SA-PATTERN-I-SPEAK-SANSKRIT] -->

Read **नमस्ते। अहं संस्कृतं वदामि।** once. Say its two meanings: “Hello. I
speak Sanskrit.” Notice where each sentence ends.

## Writing — delayed copy of the whole text
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-NAMASTE, SA-PATTERN-I-SPEAK-SANSKRIT, SA-SCRIPT-DANDA-01, SA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Cover the model now. Wait ten seconds without writing. Then write both
sentences from memory, with a danda after each one. There is no visible answer
and no romanization while you write.

Uncover and compare only after one complete attempt:

> **नमस्ते। अहं संस्कृतं वदामि।**

Check in this order: two complete sentences; two danda marks; word spaces;
both anusvāra dots; **◌ृ**; joined **स्त**. Repair only the first difference,
then read the repaired text aloud. A Latin-letter version earns no Devanagari
writing credit.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-DANDA-01, SA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

What support disappeared? (**The visible model; the meaning and the text stayed
the same.**)
