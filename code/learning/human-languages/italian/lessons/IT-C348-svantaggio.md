## You'll want to know: lo svantaggio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C348-VANTAGGIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lo svantaggio** — "the disadvantage".

> Lo svantaggio è che la casa è lontana dalla stazione.
>
> *The disadvantage is that the house is far from the station.*

*S-* in front reverses the meaning, as in *comodo* and *scomodo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*svantaggio* is built from *s-* "un-" and *vantaggio*, which is usually traced to Old French *avantage*, from Late Latin *abante* "ahead", made from *ab* and *ante* (Lewis & Short).
