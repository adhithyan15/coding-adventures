## You'll want to know: ತೊರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C482-TORE482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೊರೆ** — *tore* — "a brook; a stream".

> ಕಾಡಿನ ನಡುವೆ ಒಂದು ಚಿಕ್ಕ ತೊರೆ ಹರಿಯುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *kāḍina naḍuve ondu cikka tore hariyuttade.*
>
> A small stream flows through the forest.

Smaller than a ನದಿ, like a ಝರಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೊರೆ** (*tore*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3370 lists Kannada *toṟe*, "a stream, a river".
