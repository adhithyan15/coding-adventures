# जमा करणे (jamā karṇe) — to deposit; to collect

## You'll want to know: जमा करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C426-ROKAD426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जमा करणे** — *jamā karṇe* — "to deposit; to collect".

> मी पगाराचे पैसे खात्यात जमा केले.
>
> *mī pagārāce paise khātyāt jamā kele.*
>
> I paid my salary into my account.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने जमा केलं, *tyāne jamā kelaṁ*.

The verb agrees with the money: पैसे जमा केले, रक्कम जमा केली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जमा करणे** (*jamā karṇe*) joins **जमा**, "collected, deposited", a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *jamʿ*, "gathering", to the light verb **करणे**.
