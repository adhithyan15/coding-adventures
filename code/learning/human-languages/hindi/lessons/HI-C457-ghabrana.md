## You'll want to know: घबराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C457-BHAY457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घबराना** — *ghabrānā* — "to panic; to get flustered".

> परीक्षा से पहले अरुण घबरा गया।
>
> *parīkṣā se pahle Aruṇ ghabrā gayā.*
>
> Arun panicked before the exam.

Past (he …): वह घबरा गया, *vah ghabrā gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घबराना** (*ghabrānā*) is probably inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *ghābrā*, "startled", under *\*ghabbhara*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4427), and *ghabrānā* belongs with it. *ghabrāhaṭ*, "nervousness", is its noun.
