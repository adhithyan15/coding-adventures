Punjabi, chapter 26: Form Retrieval at R2 and R3.
3 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
Three fields return at R2 — retrieve the three-field no-model task and its bounded repair passes at R2.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
No new labels or values appear. Keep every bank closed and recall the order language, residence, work.

Writing — one no-model form.
Complete the three fields for cues B, A, B. Finish the whole attempt before checking anything.

Guided Practice — one bounded repair pass.
Compare selection, spelling, spacing, then placement. Repair only the first differing dimension. If the work row differs, repair that row only.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one form. This is retrieval, not a longer writing test.
[question — say your answer, then pause 65 seconds]
With every bank closed, complete language, residence, and work for cues B, A, B; then repair only the first differing dimension.


Lesson 2 of 3.
Rebuild one work row at R3 — rebuild the known job value and its work-field mapping at R3.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script and value recall until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Read the three labels in order: ਭਾਸ਼ਾ, ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼, ਕੰਮ. Point to the work label and stop.

Script and value recall.
Trace ੌ once above ਨ, making ਨੌ. Then read the known value ਨੌਕਰੀ once.

Writing — one supported work row.
The old bank is A — ਖੇਤੀ, B — ਨੌਕਰੀ. For B, complete ਕੰਮ: __________. Keep the bank visible for this one attempt.

Wrap-up recall.
One supported row is enough. The next lesson removes the bank after a short delay.
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
After naming the third field as work, use visible-bank cue B to complete ਕੰਮ: ______.


Lesson 3 of 3.
Hide, write, and check one work row at R3 — retrieve one work value after a delay and check its four known controls at R3.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read the old work bank A — ਖੇਤੀ, B — ਨੌਕਰੀ. Select B, hide the bank, and count slowly to ten.

Writing — one delayed row.
With the bank hidden, complete ਕੰਮ: __________ for B.

Guided Practice — four checks, no rewrite.
Reveal the model. Check the value piece by piece, the one space after the colon, and that the job value stayed on the work field. Repair only the first difference.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one delayed row. This closes the bounded R3 retrieval pass without adding a new form field.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
Select B from the work bank, hide it for ten seconds, write the work row, then name the three checks.
