## You'll want to know: 虽然
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C371-THOUGH371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**虽然** — *suīrán* — "although".

> **虽然下雨了但是我们还是去了** *suīrán xiàyǔ le dànshì wǒmen háishi qù le* — Although it rained, we still went.

The new character, in pieces: 虽 — nine strokes in two pieces: 口 *kǒu* on top, and under it an insect piece, six strokes: a small box with a vertical through it, a stroke rising along the foot, and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**虽然** (*suīrán*) is built from *suī* and 然, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *swij* and *nyen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a particle and the second as a particle and 'like this'.
