# … என்று தோன்றுகிறது (eṉṟu tōṉṟukiṟatu) — it seems to me, in my opinion

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-PRAGMATICS-C77-OPINION-03, TA-GRAMMAR-C77-SAY-02, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think this is a good house." (*itu nalla vīṭu eṉṟu niṉaikkiṟēṉ*.) What closes the opinion before the verb? (**என்று**.) Then say "dampness". (*īram*.)

## You'll want to know: … என்று தோன்றுகிறது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C286-SEEMS-01]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-SUBJECT-02, TA-LEX-DATIVE-SUBJECT-01, TA-LEX-C04-NAALAI-01, TA-LEX-NINAI-01] -->

> **எனக்கு நாளை மழை பெய்யும் என்று தோன்றுகிறது.**
>
> *eṉakku nāḷai maḻai peyyum eṉṟu tōṉṟukiṟatu.* — "It seems to me it will rain tomorrow."

**தோன்றுகிறது** (*tōṉṟukiṟatu*, "it appears, it occurs"). The person it occurs to takes **-க்கு**, as in **எனக்குத் தமிழ் தெரியும்**: "to me, '(it) will rain tomorrow', so it appears". **பெய்யும்** (*peyyum*, "it will fall") is the "it" form of the future.

**நினைக்கிறேன்** says you have weighed it; **தோன்றுகிறது** says it is an impression, and sounds softer. Both take the whole opinion first, closed by **என்று**.

Two more shapes of the same **நினை**: **… என்று நினைத்தேன்** (*… eṉṟu niṉaittēṉ*, "I thought that …"), and the negative, **அவன் வருவான் என்று நான் நினைக்கவில்லை** (*avaṉ varuvāṉ eṉṟu nāṉ niṉaikkavillai*, "I don't think he will come"). **வருவான்** (*varuvāṉ*, "he will come").

## You'll want to know: என் கருத்தில், 'in my opinion'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C286-IN-MY-OPINION-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C02-EN-01, TA-LEX-C277-PORUL277-05, TA-LEX-C69-GOING-03, TA-LEX-C48-LEAVE-03, TA-LEX-C41-ADJ-03] -->

> **என் கருத்தில், ரயில் பயணம் நல்லது.**
>
> *eṉ karuttil, rayil payaṇam nallatu.* — "In my opinion, travelling by train is good."

**கருத்து** is "an opinion"; **கருத்தில்** is "in (the) opinion". **நல்லது** (*nallatu*, "a good thing; good") is **நல்ல** made into a thing you can point at. **என் கருத்துப்படி** (*eṉ karuttuppaṭi*, "according to my opinion") says the same with **-படி**, "according to".

This one sits at the front, apart, so the listener knows that what follows is yours.
