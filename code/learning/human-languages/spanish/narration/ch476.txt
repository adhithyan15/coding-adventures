Spanish, chapter 476: En clase.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el alumno — the pupil, the student.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the material, then the Spanish for the gardener.

You'll want to know: el alumno.
el alumno — "the pupil, the student".
Cada alumno tiene un libro. Each pupil has a book.
A girl or woman is la alumna.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: el alumno]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: el alumno, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with el alumno]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the material, then the Spanish for the gardener, then say el alumno again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does el alumno mean? ("The pupil, the student".) Say it once more.

La película es aburrida. The film is boring.
