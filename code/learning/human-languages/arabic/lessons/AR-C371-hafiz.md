## You'll want to know: حافز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C371-WASILA371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حافز** — *ḥāfiz* — "an incentive".

> كان الراتب الجيد حافزا للعمل.
>
> *kāna ar-rātib al-jayyid ḥāfizan li-l-ʿamal.*
>
> The good salary was an incentive to work.

Plural: حوافز, *ḥawāfiz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حافز** (*ḥāfiz*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-f-z*, "to urge on from behind" (Lane). *Ḥāfiz* is the active participle: what urges you on, an incentive.
