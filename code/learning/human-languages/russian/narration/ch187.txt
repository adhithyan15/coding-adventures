Russian, chapter 187: Full, Deep, Even, Square, Sharp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
полный (pólnyy) — full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for generous, then the Russian for greedy.

You'll want to know: полный.
полный (pólnyy) — "full".

The word, taken apart.
полный (pólnyy) from Proto-Slavic pŭlnŭ — inherited. Vasmer lists Lithuanian pìlnas, Sanskrit pūrṇá-, Gothic fulls, Latin plēnus and Greek plḗrēs, all "full": English full is a cousin.

The word, taken apart.
глубокий (glubókiy) from Proto-Slavic glǫbokŭ — inherited. Vasmer thinks it is probably related, with an inserted nasal, to Greek glýphō, "I hollow out", Latin glūbō, "I peel", and Old Norse kljúfa, "to split".

You'll want to know: ровный.

The word, taken apart.
ровный (róvnyy) from Proto-Slavic orvĭnŭ — inherited; East Slavic turned or- into ро-. Vasmer relates it to Old Prussian arwis, "true", Avestan ravah-, "open space", and Irish róe, "a level field".

Lesson 4 of 5.
квадратный (kvadrátnyy) — square.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for deep, then the Russian for even.

You'll want to know: квадратный.
квадратный (kvadrátnyy) — "square".

The word, taken apart.
квадратный (kvadrátnyy) is квадрат, "a square", with -ный. квадрат, recorded from 1720, came through German Quadrat from Latin quadrātum, "a four-cornered figure" (Vasmer).
