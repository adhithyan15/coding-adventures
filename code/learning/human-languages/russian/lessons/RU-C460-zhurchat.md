## You'll want to know: журчать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C460-RUCHEY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**журчать** (*zhurchát*) — "to babble, to murmur (of water)".

> Под мостом тихо журчит ручей.
>
> *Pod mostóm tíkho zhurchít ruchéy.*
>
> Under the bridge a stream babbles quietly.

Imperfective. Present *журчит*, *журчат*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*журчать* is usually taken to imitate the sound of running water; EtymDB lists it without a source.
