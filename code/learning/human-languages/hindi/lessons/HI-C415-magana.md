## You'll want to know: मँगाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C415-RAJISTAR415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मँगाना** — *mãgānā* — "to send for; to order (to be brought)".

> हमने कमरे में चाय मँगाई।
>
> *hamne kamre meṁ cāy mãgāī.*
>
> We ordered tea to our room.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने खाना मँगाया, *usne khānā mãgāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मँगाना** (*mãgānā*) is the causative partner of माँगना, "to ask for", which goes back to Sanskrit *mārgati*, "seeks, requests" (CDIAL 10074): you ask for a thing to be brought to you.
