## You'll want to know: عبور کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C445-TAGHATO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عبور کردن** (*'obur kardan*) — "to cross, to go across".

> بچه ها با دقت از خیابان عبور کردند.
>
> *Bachche-hâ bâ deqqat az khiyâbân 'obur kardand.*
>
> The children crossed the street carefully.

More formal than *rad shodan*. *'Obur mamnu'* — no crossing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'obur kardan* is built from *'obur* 'crossing', a loan usually traced to Arabic *ʿubūr*, and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
