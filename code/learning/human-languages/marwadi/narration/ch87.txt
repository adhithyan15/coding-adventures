Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 87: Eighty, Ninety, Thousand, Hundred thousand, World.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अस्सी (assī) — eighty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for sixty, then the Marwadi for seventy.

You'll want to know: अस्सी.
अस्सी — assī — "eighty".

The word, taken apart.
अस्सी (assī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aśīti, "eighty". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 911); the match is through Hindi assī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दुनिया (duniyā) — the world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a thousand, then the Marwadi for a hundred thousand.

You'll want to know: दुनिया.
दुनिया — duniyā — "the world".

The word, taken apart.
दुनिया (duniyā) is a loan from Arabic dunyā, "the world, this life", which reached Marwadi through Persian. Its Arabic root is d-n-w (Lane).
