## You'll want to know: めでたい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C303-RYUU303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**めでたい** — *medetai* — "auspicious; joyful".

> **あにの けっこんは めでたいです。** — *ani no kekkon wa medetai desu* — "My brother's wedding is a happy event."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めでたい** (*medetai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
