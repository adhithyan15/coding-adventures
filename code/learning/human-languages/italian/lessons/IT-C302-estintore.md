## You'll want to know: l'estintore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C302-INCENDIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'estintore** — "the fire extinguisher".

> C'è un estintore su ogni piano.
>
> *There is an extinguisher on every floor.*

Built on Latin *exstinguere*, ‘to put out’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estintore* is built on Latin *exstinctor* "one who puts out", from *exstinguere* "to quench" (Lewis & Short).
