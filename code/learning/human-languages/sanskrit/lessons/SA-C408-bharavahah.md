## You'll want to know: भारवाहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C408-VAHA408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भारवाहः** — *bhāravāhaḥ* — "a porter".

> भारवाहः यात्रिकस्य भारं वहति।
>
> *bhāravāhaḥ yātrikasya bhāraṃ vahati.*
>
> The porter carries the traveller's load.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भारवाहः** (*bhāravāhaḥ*) is built from *bhāra*, "a load", and *vāha*, "carrying", from the root √vah; MW gives the compound as a porter, a carrier (MW).
