## You'll want to know: ਥੱਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C285-HOME285-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥੱਕਣਾ** — *thakkṇā* — "to get tired".

> ਬੱਚੇ ਲੰਮੇ ਸਫ਼ਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਥੱਕਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *bachche lamme safar vich thakkde han.*
>
> Children get tired on a long journey.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਥੱਕਿਆ, *maiṁ bahut thakkiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥੱਕਣਾ** (*thakkṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*sthakk*, "stop, halt"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *thakkṇā* under it only as "stop, halt"; the sense "to become weary" is the one it gives for Hindi *thaknā* (CDIAL 13737).
