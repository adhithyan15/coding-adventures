Arabic, chapter 140: Palm, Fingernail, Brain, Heel, Lip.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
كف (kaff) — a palm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a waist, then the Arabic for an elbow.

You'll want to know: كف.
كف — kaff — "a palm".

The word, taken apart.
كف (kaff) is built on the native Arabic root k-f-f, "the palm of the hand; to hold back" (Lane). Kaff is the palm.

ظفر — ẓufr — "a fingernail".

The word, taken apart.
ظفر (ẓufr), "a nail, a claw", is an old native noun, filed under the root ẓ-f-r (Lane). Hebrew tsipporen, "fingernail", is usually counted a cousin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
كعب (kaʿb) — a heel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a fingernail, then the Arabic for a brain.

You'll want to know: كعب.
كعب — kaʿb — "a heel".

The word, taken apart.
كعب (kaʿb) is built on the native Arabic root k-ʿ-b, "to swell, to be prominent" (Lane); kaʿb is the ankle-bone. The same root gives the Kaʿba, the cube-shaped shrine at Mecca.
