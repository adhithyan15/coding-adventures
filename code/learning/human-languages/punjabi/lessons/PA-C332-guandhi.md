## You'll want to know: ਗੁਆਂਢੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C332-KIN332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁਆਂਢੀ** — *guāṇḍhī* — "a neighbour".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਗੁਆਂਢੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੇ ਲੋਕ ਹਨ।
>
> *sāḍe guāṇḍhī bahut chaṅge lok han.*
>
> Our neighbours are very good people.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁਆਂਢੀ** (*guāṇḍhī*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*grāmārdha*, "the vicinity of a village", built from *grāma*, "village", and *ardha*, "half, side"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gavā̃ḍhī*, "neighbour", under it (CDIAL 4376).
