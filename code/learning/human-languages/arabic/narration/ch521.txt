Arabic, chapter 521: Pay and rewards.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أجر (ajr) — a wage, pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a task, then the Arabic for to carry out.

You'll want to know: أجر.
أجر — ajr — "a wage, pay".
يأخذ العامل أجره كل أسبوع. yaʾkhudhu al-ʿāmil ajrahu kull usbūʿ. The worker takes his pay every week.
Plural: أجور, ujūr.
راتب is a monthly salary; أجر (ajr) is pay for work done. Read ajara, the same letters are a verb, to reward.

The word, taken apart.
أجر (ajr) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-j-r, "to reward, to hire" (Lane). Ījār, rent, which you know, is from the same root.
