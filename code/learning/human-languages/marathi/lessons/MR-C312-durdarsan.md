## You'll want to know: दूरदर्शन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C312-VAHINI312-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दूरदर्शन** — *dūrdarśan* — "television".

> रात्री आम्ही दूरदर्शनवर बातम्या पाहतो.
>
> *rātrī āmhī dūrdarśanvar bātamyā pāhto.*
>
> At night we watch the news on television.

Neuter.

Also the name of the national channel; in speech टीव्ही.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूरदर्शन** (*dūrdarśan*) is a modern coinage built from Sanskrit *dūra*, 'far', and *darśana*, 'seeing' (Monier-Williams): seeing far.
