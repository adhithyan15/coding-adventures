## You'll want to know: ਮਜ਼ਦੂਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C336-TRADE336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਜ਼ਦੂਰ** — *mazdūr* — "a labourer".

> ਮਜ਼ਦੂਰ ਸਵੇਰ ਤੋਂ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਤੱਕ ਇੱਟਾਂ ਚੁੱਕਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *mazdūr saver ton shām tak iṭṭāṁ chukkde han.*
>
> The labourers carry bricks from morning till evening.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਜ਼ਦੂਰ** (*mazdūr*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *mazdūr*, "a hired labourer".
