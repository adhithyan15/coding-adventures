## You'll want to know: la cattedrale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C445-PIAZZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la cattedrale** — "the cathedral".

> La cattedrale ha tre porte di bronzo.
>
> *The cathedral has three bronze doors.*

The bishop's church; *la cattedra* is the bishop's chair, and also a teacher's desk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cattedrale* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *cathedrālis*, from *cathedra* 'chair', from Greek *kathédra* 'seat' (EtymDB): the church with the bishop's chair.
