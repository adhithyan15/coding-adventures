## You'll want to know: இடறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C481-KAL481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**இடறு** — *iṭaṟu* — "to trip; to stumble".

> இருட்டில் கல்லில் இடறி விழுந்தேன்.
>
> *iruṭṭil kallil iṭaṟi viḻuntēṉ.*
>
> I tripped over a stone in the dark and fell.

Past (I …): இடறினேன், *iṭaṟiṉēṉ*.

What you trip over takes -இல்: கல்லில் இடறு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இடறு** (*iṭaṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 437); as a noun it means an obstacle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
