# अठारा — eighteen on the page

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-ATHARA-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-A-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AATH-01, MW-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

> **अ** + **ठा** + **रा** → **अठारा** — *aṭhārā* — eighteen

Here is the standing **अ** doing the one thing it exists for. Write **आठ** and
**अठारा** one above the other:

- **आठ** opens on the long **आ**;
- **अठारा** opens on the short **अ**, and moves the length onto **ठा**.

Two letters that differ by one stroke, at the front of two numbers ten apart.
This is the pair most worth writing twice.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-ATHARA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AATH-01, MW-SCRIPT-A-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Write **आठ**, cover it, write **अठारा** from memory, then uncover and compare the
first letter of each. Say which is long and which is short.
