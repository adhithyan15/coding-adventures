Tamil, chapter 458: Crying Aloud.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கதறு (kataṟu) — to wail; to cry out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for pitifulness, then the Tamil for concern.

You'll want to know: கதறு.
கதறு — kataṟu — "to wail; to cry out".
அந்தச் செய்தியைக் கேட்டு அவள் கதறி அழுதாள். antac ceytiyaik kēṭṭu avaḷ kataṟi aḻutāḷ. Hearing the news, she wailed and wept.
Past (I …): கதறினேன், kataṟiṉēṉ.
Often கதறி அழு, to cry aloud.

The word, taken apart.
கதறு (kataṟu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1189); Malayalam has the same verb for roaring.

Lesson 2 of 5.
விம்மு (vimmu) — to sob.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for concern, then the Tamil for to wail.

You'll want to know: விம்மு.
விம்மு — vimmu — "to sob".
குழந்தை அம்மாவைத் தேடி விம்மியது. kuḻantai ammāvait tēṭi vimmiyatu. The child sobbed, looking for its mother.
Past (I …): விம்மினேன், vimmiṉēṉ.
Past with -இன்-: விம்மினேன். விம்மல், a sob.

The word, taken apart.
விம்மு (vimmu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 5402). DEDR keeps a second விம்மு, "to swell", apart as its own entry (5397).

Lesson 3 of 5.
விசும்பு (vicumpu) — to whimper; to sob softly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to wail, then the Tamil for to sob.

You'll want to know: விசும்பு.
விசும்பு — vicumpu — "to whimper; to sob softly".
தோற்ற பிறகு அவன் தனியாக விசும்பினான். tōṟṟa piṟaku avaṉ taṉiyāka vicumpiṉāṉ. After losing, he whimpered on his own.
Past (I …): விசும்பினேன், vicumpiṉēṉ.
Often doubled: விசும்பி விசும்பி அழுதாள், she sobbed and sobbed.

The word, taken apart.
விசும்பு (vicumpu) is a Tamil verb whose origin is not settled; DEDR has no entry for this sense.

Lesson 4 of 5.
புலம்பு (pulampu) — to lament; to moan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to sob, then the Tamil for to whimper.

You'll want to know: புலம்பு.
புலம்பு — pulampu — "to lament; to moan".
அவர் இரவு முழுவதும் புலம்பினார். avar iravu muḻuvatum pulampiṉār. He moaned all night long.
Past (I …): புலம்பினேன், pulampiṉēṉ.
Also of a feverish person talking aloud without sense.

The word, taken apart.
புலம்பு (pulampu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4304); the entry runs from speaking foolishly to wailing in grief.

Lesson 5 of 5.
அலறு (alaṟu) — to scream; to shriek.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to whimper, then the Tamil for to lament.

You'll want to know: அலறு.
அலறு — alaṟu — "to scream; to shriek".
பாம்பைப் பார்த்ததும் அவள் அலறினாள். pāmpaip pārttatum avaḷ alaṟiṉāḷ. She screamed when she saw the snake.
Past (I …): அலறினேன், alaṟiṉēṉ.
The verbal participle: அலறி ஓடினான், he ran off screaming.

The word, taken apart.
அலறு (alaṟu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 245), in an entry of words for noisy talk and loud cries.
