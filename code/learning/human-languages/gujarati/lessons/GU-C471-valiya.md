## You'll want to know: વળિયાં
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C471-VAN471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વળિયાં** — *vaḷiyā̃* — "wrinkles".

> દાદીના ચહેરા પર વળિયાં છે.
>
> *dādīnā caherā par vaḷiyā̃ che.*
>
> There are wrinkles on my grandmother's face.

A neuter noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વળિયાં** (*vaḷiyā̃*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vali*, "a fold of skin"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "wrinkles in a garment" (CDIAL 11412), and Gujarati uses it for the folds of the skin too.
