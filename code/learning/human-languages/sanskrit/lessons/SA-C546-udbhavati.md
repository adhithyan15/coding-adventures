## You'll want to know: उद्भवति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C546-VIPAKA546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्भवति** — *udbhavati* — "arises".

> यदि प्रश्नः उद्भवति, तर्हि अध्यापकं पृच्छतु।
>
> *yadi praśnaḥ udbhavati, tarhi adhyāpakaṃ pṛcchatu.*
>
> If a question comes up, ask the teacher.

Past (it …): प्रश्नः उद्भूतवान्, *praśnaḥ udbhūtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्भवति** (*udbhavati*) is built on *ud*, "up", and the root √bhū, "to become", which also gives *bhavati*; MW gives *udbhū* as to come forth, to arise, to spring from; its past participle is *udbhūta* (MW).
