## You'll want to know: کھوپڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C465-BONE465-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کھوپڑی** — *khopṛī* — "a skull".

> سر کے اندر کھوپڑی ہوتی ہے۔
>
> *sar ke andar khopṛī hotī hai.*
>
> Inside the head is the skull.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھوپڑی** (*khopṛī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khopṛī*, "skull, shell", under *khōppa*, "a coconut shell", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3936).
