## You'll want to know: il ritratto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C523-MOSTRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il ritratto** — "portrait".

> Nel salotto c'è un ritratto della nonna da giovane.
>
> *In the living room there is a portrait of grandmother as a young woman.*

Also a description: *un ritratto della città*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ritratto* is the past participle of *ritrarre* "to portray", from Latin *retrahere* "to draw back" (EtymDB), built on *trahere* "to draw".
