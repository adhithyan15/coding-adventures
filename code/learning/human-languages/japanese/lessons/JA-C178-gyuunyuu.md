## You'll want to know: ぎゅうにゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C178-MEAT178-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎゅうにゅう** — *gyūnyū* — "milk; cow's milk".

> **まいあさ ぎゅうにゅうを のみます。** — *maiasa gyūnyū o nomimasu* — "I drink milk every morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎゅうにゅう** (*gyūnyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gyū*, "cow" (Middle Chinese *ngjuw*) and *nyū*, "milk" (*nyuX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
