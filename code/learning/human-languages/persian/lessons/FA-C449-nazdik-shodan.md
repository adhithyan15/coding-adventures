## You'll want to know: نزدیک شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C449-MASIRYAB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نزدیک شدن** (*nazdik shodan*) — "to approach, to get near".

> قطار به ایستگاه نزدیک شد.
>
> *Qatâr be istgâh nazdik shod.*
>
> The train approached the station.

*nazdik* 'near' + *shodan*; the place takes *be*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nazdik shodan* is built from native *nazdik* 'near', usually traced to Middle Persian *nazdīk*, and the native verb *shodan* 'to become'.
