# अहं प्रसन्नः अस्मि (ahaṃ prasannaḥ asmi) — how I feel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-02, SA-LEX-C37-COURTESY-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I am ill". (*ahaṃ rugṇaḥ asmi*.) And a woman? (*ahaṃ rugṇā asmi*.) Feelings work the same way.

## You'll want to know: the feeling follows the speaker
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-FEELING-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C37-COURTESY-03, SA-LEX-C157-QUAL157-02, SA-LEX-C157-QUAL157-04, SA-LEX-C157-QUAL157-01] -->

> **अहं प्रसन्नः अस्मि।**
>
> *ahaṃ prasannaḥ asmi.* — "I am pleased."

> **अहं प्रसन्ना अस्मि।**
>
> *ahaṃ prasannā asmi.* — "I am pleased."

The first is a man speaking, the second a woman: the word for the feeling takes the speaker's gender, *-aḥ* or *-ā*.

> **मीरा भीता अस्ति।**
>
> *mīrā bhītā asti.* — "Mira is afraid."

> **अरुणः श्रान्तः अस्ति।**
>
> *aruṇaḥ śrāntaḥ asti.* — "Arun is tired."

> **ते कुपिताः सन्ति।**
>
> *te kupitāḥ santi.* — "They are angry."

**सन्ति** (*santi*) is "are", for several.

## You'll want to know: happy, and what I like
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-FEELING-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-04, SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-05, SA-FRAME-ROCATE-01, SA-LEX-C228-SCH228-03, SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-05] -->

**सुखी** (*sukhī*), "happy", and **दुःखी** (*duḥkhī*), "unhappy", end in *-ī* for a man.

> **अद्य अहं सुखी अस्मि।**
>
> *adya ahaṃ sukhī asmi.* — "Today I am happy."

What you like keeps the frame you know, with **मह्यम्** (*mahyam*), "to me":

> **मह्यं संगीतं रोचते।**
>
> *mahyaṃ saṅgītaṃ rocate.* — "I like music."

A feeling with its reason:

> **अहं प्रसन्नः अस्मि,**
>
> *ahaṃ prasannaḥ asmi,*
>
> **यतः अरुणः आगतवान्।**
>
> *yataḥ aruṇaḥ āgatavān.* — "I am pleased, because Arun has come."
