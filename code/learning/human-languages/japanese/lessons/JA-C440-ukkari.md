## You'll want to know: うっかり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C440-CAREFUL440-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うっかり** — *ukkari* — "thoughtlessly; inadvertently".

> **うっかり かさを わすれました。** — *ukkari kasa o wasuremashita* — "Without thinking, I left my umbrella behind."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うっかり** (*ukkari*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
