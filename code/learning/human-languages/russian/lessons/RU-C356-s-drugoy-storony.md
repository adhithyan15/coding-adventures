## You'll want to know: с другой стороны
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C356-NESMOTRYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**с другой стороны** (*s drugóy storoný*) — "on the other hand".

> С другой стороны, зарплата маленькая.
>
> *S drugóy storoný, zarpláta málenkaya.*
>
> On the other hand, the pay is low.

It follows *с одной стороны*, or stands alone to bring in a second view.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**с другой стороны** is "from the other side": *другой* "other" and *сторона* "a side", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*storna*.
