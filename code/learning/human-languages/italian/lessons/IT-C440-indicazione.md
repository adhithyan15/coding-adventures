## You'll want to know: l'indicazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C440-INDICAZIONI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'indicazione** — "the direction (to a place); the sign".

> Mi può dare un'indicazione per la stazione?
>
> *Can you give me directions to the station?*

Often plural: *chiedere indicazioni* — to ask for directions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indicazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *indicātiō* 'a pointing out' (EtymDB), from *indicāre* 'to point out', built on *index* 'pointer'.
