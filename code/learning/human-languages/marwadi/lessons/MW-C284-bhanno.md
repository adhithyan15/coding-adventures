## You'll want to know: भणणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C284-BASTO284-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भणणो** — *bhaṇṇo* — "to study".

> हूं स्कूल मांय भणूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ skūl mā̃y bhaṇū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I study at school.

Past (I …): म्हैं किताब भणी, *mhaĩ kitāb bhaṇī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भणणो** (*bhaṇṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhaṇati*, "calls aloud, speaks"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bhaṇaï*, "recites", and Gujarati *bhaṇvũ*, "to say, repeat, study" (CDIAL 9383). It is the Marwadi word for studying.
