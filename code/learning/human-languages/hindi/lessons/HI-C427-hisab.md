## You'll want to know: हिसाब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C427-UDHAR427-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हिसाब** — *hisāb* — "an account; a reckoning".

> दुकानदार सब ग्राहकों का हिसाब रखता है।
>
> *dukāndār sab grāhakoṁ kā hisāb rakhtā hai.*
>
> The shopkeeper keeps an account for every customer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिसाब** (*hisāb*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥisāb*, "counting, reckoning", through Persian. हिसाब करना is to settle up.
