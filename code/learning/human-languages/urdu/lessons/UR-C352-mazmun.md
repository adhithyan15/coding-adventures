## You'll want to know: مضمون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C352-MAZMUN352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مضمون** — *mazmūn* — "an article; an essay".

> میں نے اخبار میں اس کا مضمون پڑھا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne akhbār meṅ us kā mazmūn paṛhā.*
>
> I read her article in the newspaper.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مضمون** (*mazmūn*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maḍmūn*, "contents, what is contained".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
