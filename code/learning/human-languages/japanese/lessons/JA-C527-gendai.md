## You'll want to know: げんだい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C527-TIME527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**げんだい** — *gendai* — "the present day; modern times".

> **もし そふの そふが げんだいに きたら、おどろくでしょう。** — *moshi sofu no sofu ga gendai ni kitara, odoroku deshō* — "If my grandfather's grandfather came to the present day, he would be amazed."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**げんだい** (*gendai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gen*, "present" (Middle Chinese *henH*) and *dai*, "age" (*dojH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
