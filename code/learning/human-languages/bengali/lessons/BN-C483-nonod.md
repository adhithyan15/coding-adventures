# ননদ (nônôd) — a husband's sister

## You'll want to know: ননদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C483-JAMAI483-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ননদ** — *nônôd* — "a husband's sister".

> মীরা ননদের সঙ্গে বাজারে গেল।
>
> *mīrā nônôder sôngge bājāre gelô.*
>
> Mira went to the market with her husband's sister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ননদ** (*nônôd*) comes from Sanskrit *nanāndṛ*, "a husband's sister" (CDIAL 6946), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form.
