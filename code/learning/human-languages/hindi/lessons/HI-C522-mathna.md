## You'll want to know: मथना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C522-CHAACH522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मथना** — *mathnā* — "to churn".

> दादी दही मथकर मक्खन निकालती हैं।
>
> *Dādī dahī mathkar makkhan nikāltī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother churns yoghurt and takes out the butter.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दही मथा, *usne dahī mathā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मथना** (*mathnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *mathnāti*, "stirs, churns" (CDIAL 9771).
