## You'll want to know: la preocupación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C719-CONFIANZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la preocupación** — "worry, concern".

> Mi mayor preocupación es la salud.
>
> *My biggest worry is health.*

The verb *preocuparse* is to worry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preocupación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praeoccupātiō* "a seizing beforehand" (EtymDB); a worry takes hold of the mind before anything else can.
