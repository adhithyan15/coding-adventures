# Retrieve the body in small blocks

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

Close the four supported pages. Take a short pause. Keep three English cues:
who Aman is, how the two people are and what Aman likes, and when they meet.

## Writing — delayed blocks, not a whole letter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-HOW-ARE-YOU-03, PA-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-LIKING, PA-LEX-JALDI] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Write the old name-and-language pair. Pause. Write the old reader question
and Aman's wellbeing reply. Pause. Write the two old liking sentences and
the one meeting plan. Leave the opening and closing for later. Do not reopen
the supported pages until every block has an attempt.

## Guided Practice — compare one block at a time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

Reopen one earlier lesson for each block. Check order and meaning first,
then endings, word spaces, spelling, and punctuation in separate passes.
Repair only the first difference found on a pass.
