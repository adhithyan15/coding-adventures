Gujarati, chapter 418: Societies and Flats.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સોસાયટી (sosāyṭī) — a housing society; a residential colony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a small gated lane, then the Gujarati for to shut (a door).

You'll want to know: સોસાયટી.
સોસાયટી — sosāyṭī — "a housing society; a residential colony".
અમારી સોસાયટીમાં ચાળીસ ઘર છે. amārī sosāyṭīmā̃ cāḷīs ghar che. There are forty houses in our housing society.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સોસાયટી (sosāyṭī) is borrowed from English society; in Gujarat a સોસાયટી is a planned group of houses with its own gate and committee.

Lesson 3 of 5.
બંગલો (banglo) — a bungalow; a large house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a housing society, then the Gujarati for a flat.

You'll want to know: બંગલો.
બંગલો — banglo — "a bungalow; a large house".
નદી કિનારે એક સુંદર બંગલો છે. nadī kināre ek sundar banglo che. There is a beautiful bungalow on the river bank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બંગલો (banglo) is borrowed from English bungalow, a word English itself took from India: it is usually traced to Hindi baṅglā, "in the Bengal style".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ટાંકી (ṭā̃kī) — a water tank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a flat, then the Gujarati for a bungalow.

You'll want to know: ટાંકી.
ટાંકી — ṭā̃kī — "a water tank".
છત પરની ટાંકીમાં પાણી નથી. chat parnī ṭā̃kīmā̃ pāṇī nathī. There is no water in the tank on the roof.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટાંકી (ṭā̃kī) goes back to ṭaṅka, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a cistern" (CDIAL 5430).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bungalow, then the Gujarati for a water tank.

You'll want to know: ભાડૂત.
ભાડૂત — bhāḍūt — "a tenant".
ભાડૂત મહિનાના અંતે ભાડું ચૂકવે છે. bhāḍūt mahinānā ante bhāḍũ cūkve che. The tenant pays the rent at the end of the month.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ભાડૂત (bhāḍūt) is built on ભાડું, rent, which CDIAL lists under bhārta, "pertaining to servants" (CDIAL 9468): the one who pays the rent.
