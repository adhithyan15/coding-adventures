## You'll want to know: donnern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C462-UNWETTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**donnern** — "to thunder".

> Es blitzt und donnert die ganze Nacht.
>
> *There is thunder and lightning all night.*

With *es*: *es donnert*. *Der Donner* — the thunder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*donnern* is built on *Donner* 'thunder' (EtymDB lists the verb), inherited from Proto-Germanic *þunraz*; English *thunder* is a cousin.
