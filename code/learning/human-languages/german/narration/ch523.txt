German, chapter 523: Vor dem Fernseher.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Fernbedienung — remote control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for publisher, then the German for translation.

You'll want to know: die Fernbedienung.
die Fernbedienung — "remote control".
Die Fernbedienung liegt unter dem Sofa. The remote control is under the sofa.
Plural: die Fernbedienungen.

The word, taken apart.
Fernbedienung is built on fern "far", from Old High German ferrana (EtymDB), and Bedienung "operation, service", from bedienen.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Staffel — season (of a series); relay team.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to flick through channels, then the German for series.

You'll want to know: die Staffel.
die Staffel — "season (of a series); relay team".
Die zweite Staffel ist noch spannender als die erste. The second season is even more exciting than the first.
Plural: die Staffeln. In sport, a relay team.

The word, taken apart.
Staffel first meant "step, rung"; EtymDB traces it to Proto-Germanic stapulaz "post". The sense of a season of episodes is recent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Einschaltquote — ratings, viewing figures.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for series, then the German for season.

You'll want to know: die Einschaltquote.
die Einschaltquote — "ratings, viewing figures".
Die Show hatte eine hohe Einschaltquote. The show had high ratings.
Plural: die Einschaltquoten. Built on einschalten — to switch on.

The word, taken apart.
Einschaltquote is built on einschalten "to switch on" and Quote "share", which goes back to Latin quotus "how many" (EtymDB).
