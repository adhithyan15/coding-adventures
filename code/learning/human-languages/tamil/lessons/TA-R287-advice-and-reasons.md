# Review — advice and reasons

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C287-OBLIGATIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-PROHIBITIVE-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I must go" and "You must not go alone at night." Then read **ஈரம்** aloud. (*īram*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C287-OBLIGATIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-PROHIBITIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-BECAUSE-DID-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-REASON-ORDER-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C286-SO-RESULT-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C286-THEREFORE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C286-IN-MY-OPINION-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: You must not eat now. Because you have a fever.]
- [YOU SAY: Because the bus did not come, we walked.]
- [YOU SAY: In my opinion, you must see a doctor.]
- [YOU SAY: You don't need to come tomorrow. So you may stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: one piece of advice for a friend, with வேண்டும் or கூடாது, and the reason, with -ததால் or ஏனென்றால்]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C287-OBLIGATIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-PROHIBITIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-BECAUSE-DID-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-REASON-ORDER-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "Must"? (**to-form + வேண்டும்**.) "Must not"? (**to-form + கூடாது**.) "Because it rained"? (**மழை பெய்ததால்**.) "I did not come, because …"? (**நான் வரவில்லை. ஏனென்றால் …**)
