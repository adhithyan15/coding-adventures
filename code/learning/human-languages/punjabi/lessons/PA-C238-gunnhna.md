# ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਣਾ (gunnhṇā) — to knead

## You'll want to know: ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C238-KAM238-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਣਾ** — *gunnhṇā* — "to knead".

> ਮੈਂ ਪਰਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਟਾ ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ parāt vich āṭā gunnhdī hāṁ.*
>
> I knead the flour in the tray.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਟਾ ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਿਆ, *maiṁ āṭā gunnhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਣਾ** (*gunnhṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *guphati*, "strings together"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gunnhṇā*, "to braid, knead" (CDIAL 4205).
