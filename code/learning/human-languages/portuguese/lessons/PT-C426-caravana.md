## You'll want to know: a caravana
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C426-TENDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a caravana** — "the caravan".

> Os vizinhos passam o verão numa caravana.
>
> *The neighbours spend the summer in a caravan.*

Also a line of travellers, as in the desert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caravana* comes from Persian *kārvān* 'group of travellers' (EtymDB).
