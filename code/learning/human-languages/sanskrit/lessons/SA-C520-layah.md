## You'll want to know: लयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C520-SVARA520-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लयः** — *layaḥ* — "a tempo".

> एतस्य गीतस्य लयः मन्दः अस्ति।
>
> *etasya gītasya layaḥ mandaḥ asti.*
>
> This song has a slow tempo.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लयः** (*layaḥ*) comes from the root √lī, "to cling, to rest"; MW gives *laya* as rest, and in music the time, of three kinds, quick, middle and slow (MW).
