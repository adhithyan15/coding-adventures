## You'll want to know: ತೂಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C99-BODY2-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೂಕ** (*tūka*) — "weight".

**ನಿಮ್ಮ ತೂಕ ಎಷ್ಟು?** (*nimma tūka eṣṭu?*) — "what is your weight?", a doctor's
question, with **ಎಷ್ಟು**, "how much".
