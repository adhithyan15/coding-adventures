## You'll want to know: تأثير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C414-NATIJA414-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تأثير** — *taʾthīr* — "an effect".

> للنوم تأثير كبير على الصحة.
>
> *li-n-nawm taʾthīr kabīr ʿalā aṣ-ṣiḥḥa.*
>
> Sleep has a big effect on health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأثير** (*taʾthīr*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-th-r*, "a trace, a track" (Lane). *Taʾthīr* is the Form II verbal noun, leaving a trace on something: an effect, an influence.

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
