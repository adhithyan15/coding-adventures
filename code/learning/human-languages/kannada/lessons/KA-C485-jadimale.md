# ಜಡಿಮಳೆ (jaḍimaḷe) — a downpour; heavy rain

## You'll want to know: ಜಡಿಮಳೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C485-MUNGARU485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಡಿಮಳೆ** — *jaḍimaḷe* — "a downpour; heavy rain".

> ಜಡಿಮಳೆಯಿಂದ ರಸ್ತೆಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ನೀರು ನಿಂತಿತು.
>
> *jaḍimaḷeyinda rastegaḷalli nīru nintitu.*
>
> Because of the downpour water stood on the roads.

ಜಡಿ, to pound, plus ಮಳೆ, rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಡಿಮಳೆ** (*jaḍimaḷe*) is a native Kannada compound of *jaḍi*, "to beat, to pound" (DEDR 2300), and ಮಳೆ, "rain" (DEDR 4753).
