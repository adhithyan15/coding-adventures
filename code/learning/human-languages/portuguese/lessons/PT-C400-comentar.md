## You'll want to know: comentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C400-PUBLICAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**comentar** — "to comment (on)".

> Toda a gente comentou a fotografia.
>
> *Everyone commented on the photo.*

*Comentar alguma coisa* — no preposition needed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comentar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *commentārī* "to think over, to make notes on" (EtymDB), a verb formed on *comminīscī* "to devise, to recall" (Lewis & Short).
