## You'll want to know: l'impronta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C295-INDAGINE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'impronta** — "the footprint; the fingerprint".

> Sulla neve ci sono impronte di scarpe.
>
> *There are shoe prints in the snow.*

*Le impronte digitali* — fingerprints.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impronta* is built on *improntare* "to stamp, to press a mark into" (EtymDB); where *improntare* comes from is not settled.
