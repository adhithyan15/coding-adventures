## You'll want to know: ਚੋਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C307-BUFF307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੋਣਾ** — *choṇā* — "to milk an animal".

> ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੱਝ ਚੋਵਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ savere majjh chovāṅgā.*
>
> I will milk the buffalo in the morning.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝ ਚੋਈ, *maiṁ majjh choī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੋਣਾ** (*choṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cyōtati*, "flows, trickles"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *coṇā*, "to leak, milk", under it (CDIAL 4948).
