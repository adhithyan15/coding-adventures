## You'll want to know: പ്രവൃത്തിദിനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C486-OPHIS486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രവൃത്തിദിനം** — *pravṛttidinaṁ* — "a working day".

> ശനിയാഴ്ച ഇവിടെ പ്രവൃത്തിദിനമല്ല.
>
> *śaniyāḻca iviṭe pravṛttidinamalla.*
>
> Saturday is not a working day here.

Its opposite is an അവധിദിനം, a holiday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രവൃത്തിദിനം** (*pravṛttidinaṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from two words borrowed from Sanskrit, *pravṛtti*, "activity", and *dina*, "a day" (Monier-Williams).
