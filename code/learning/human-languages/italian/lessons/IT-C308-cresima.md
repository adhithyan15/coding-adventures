## You'll want to know: la cresima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C308-BATTESIMO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cresima** — "the confirmation (sacrament)".

> A dodici anni ho fatto la cresima.
>
> *I was confirmed at twelve.*

*Fare la cresima* — to be confirmed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cresima* comes from Church Latin *chrisma* "anointing oil", from Greek *khrîsma* "an ointment" (Lewis & Short).
