## You'll want to know: il cervo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C464-SELVATICI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il cervo** — "the deer, the stag".

> Nel parco abbiamo visto un cervo.
>
> *In the park we saw a deer.*

*Il capriolo* is the smaller roe deer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cervo* is inherited from Latin *cervus* 'deer' (EtymDB).
