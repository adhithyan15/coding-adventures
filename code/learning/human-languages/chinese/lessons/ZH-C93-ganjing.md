## You'll want to know: gānjìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C93-GANJING-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gānjìng* — "clean".

> *fángjiān hěn gānjìng* — The room is clean.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gānjìng* is built from gān 'dry' and jìng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kan* and *dzjengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'dry' and 'without dirt'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-HEN-01] -->

An adjective takes a plain 很 where English has *is*: *fángjiān hěn gānjìng*. No 是.
