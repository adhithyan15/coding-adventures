## You'll want to know: verser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C329-FOUETTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**verser** — "to pour; to pay in".

> Il verse le lait dans le saladier.
>
> *He pours the milk into the bowl.*

Also to pay money in: *verser un salaire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verser* is inherited from Latin *versāre* "to turn often" (EtymDB), built on *vertere* "to turn".
