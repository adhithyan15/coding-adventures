## You'll want to know: खळबळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C313-AFVA313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खळबळ** — *khaḷbaḷ* — "a sensation; a stir".

> त्या बातमीने शहरात खळबळ उडाली.
>
> *tyā bātmīne śahrāt khaḷbaḷ uḍālī.*
>
> That news caused a sensation in the city.

Feminine.

खळबळ उडणे, a stir breaks out; खळबळजनक, sensational.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खळबळ** (*khaḷbaḷ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *khaḷbaḷṇe*, 'to agitate', under Sanskrit *khalati*, 'shakes' (CDIAL 3837); the noun names the stir.
