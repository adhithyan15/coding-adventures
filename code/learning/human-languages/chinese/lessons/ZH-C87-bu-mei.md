# 不 — 没: two words for not

[PAUSE 2s] Two words for 'not', and this lesson sorts them.

- [YOU READ: 没有, aloud — *méiyǒu*]
- [YOU RECALL: say 不 — *bù*, not]

## Grammar Lens: 不 for now and for habit, 没 for what did not happen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-BU-MEI-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-BU, ZH-LEX-C87-MEIYOU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-V-NOT-V-01, ZH-TONE-SANDHI-BU] -->

> **不 says no about now, about habit, and about what you will or won't do. 没 says something did not happen.**

> **我不去** *wǒ bú qù* — I'm not going; I don't go.
> **我没去** *wǒ méi qù* — I didn't go.

Same verb, same person; the negative alone moves the sentence from a decision to a report. 不 before the fall of 去 is *bú*, as in *bú shì*.

And 有 takes 没 every time, which is how the question works:

> **你有没有手机** *nǐ yǒu méiyǒu shǒujī* — Do you have a phone?

It is the same yes-or-no shape as 好不好: the verb, its negative, the verb again. The answer is 有 or 没有.
