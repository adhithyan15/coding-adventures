## You'll want to know: le bal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C318-ARTIFICE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le bal** — "the dance, the ball".

> Les jeunes dansent au bal du village.
>
> *The young people dance at the village dance.*

*Le bal populaire* — the open-air public dance, a summer tradition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bal* is built on Old French *baller* "to dance", which is inherited from Late Latin *ballāre* (EtymDB).
