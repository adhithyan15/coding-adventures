## You'll want to know: ਥਾਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C267-OFFICE267-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥਾਣਾ** — *thāṇā* — "a police station".

> ਥਾਣਾ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *thāṇā bāzār de neṛe hai.*
>
> The police station is near the market.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥਾਣਾ** (*thāṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sthāna*, "firm stance, place of abode"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *thāṇā*, "place, police station", under it (CDIAL 13753).
