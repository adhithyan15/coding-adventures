# ವಿರಹ (viraha) — the pain of parting; separation from a loved one

## You'll want to know: ವಿರಹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿರಹ** — *viraha* — "the pain of parting; separation from a loved one".

> ಗಂಡ ವಿದೇಶಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋದಾಗ ಅವಳಿಗೆ ವಿರಹದ ನೋವು ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *gaṇḍa vidēśakke hōdāga avaḷige virahada nōvu āyitu.*
>
> When her husband went abroad, she felt the pain of separation.

A favourite theme of songs and poems.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿರಹ** (*viraha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *viraha*, "desertion, separation, especially of lovers" (CDIAL 11851).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
