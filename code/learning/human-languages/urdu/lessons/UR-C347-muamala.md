## You'll want to know: معاملہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C347-MUAMALA347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**معاملہ** — *muāmalā* — "a matter to be settled; an affair".

> یہ معاملہ بڑا مشکل ہے۔
>
> *yih muāmalā baṛā mushkil hai.*
>
> This is a very difficult affair.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معاملہ** (*muāmalā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muʿāmala*, "dealing, a transaction".
