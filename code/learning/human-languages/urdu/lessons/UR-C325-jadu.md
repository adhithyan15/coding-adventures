## You'll want to know: جادو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C325-DEV325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جادو** — *jādū* — "magic".

> اس نے جادو سے پتھر کو پھول بنایا۔
>
> *us ne jādū se patthar ko phūl banāyā.*
>
> She turned the stone into a flower by magic.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جادو** (*jādū*) is usually traced to Persian *jādū*, "magic, a sorcerer". **جادوگر** is a magician.
