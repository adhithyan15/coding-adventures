## You'll want to know: abiēs, abietis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C484-FAGUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**abiēs, abietis** — "a fir tree; fir wood".

> In monte altō abietēs crēscunt.
>
> *Fir trees grow on the high mountain.*

Third declension, feminine: *abiēs, abietis f.* Its wood was light and good for ships and oars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abiēs* is inherited from Proto-Italic *abiets* 'fir' (EtymDB); Lewis & Short call its further origin uncertain.
