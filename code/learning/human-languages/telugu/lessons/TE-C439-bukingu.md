# బుకింగు (bukiṅgu) — a booking

## You'll want to know: బుకింగు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C439-BOGI439-02]; assesses=[] -->

**బుకింగు** — *bukiṅgu* — "a booking".

> మా బుకింగు నిన్ననే అయిపోయింది.
>
> *mā bukiṅgu ninnanē ayipōyindi.*
>
> Our booking was done yesterday itself.

A ticket counter for advance tickets is called the బుకింగు office in many towns.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బుకింగు** (*bukiṅgu*) is a loan from English *booking*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
