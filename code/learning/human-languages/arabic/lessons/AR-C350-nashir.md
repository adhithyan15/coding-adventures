## You'll want to know: ناشر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C350-NASHIR350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ناشر** — *nāshir* — "a publisher".

> قبل الناشر كتاب أختي.
>
> *qabila an-nāshir kitāb ukhtī.*
>
> The publisher accepted my sister’s book.

A publishing house is دار نشر.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ناشر** (*nāshir*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-sh-r* (Lane), "to spread out", the root of the taught *nashara*. *Nāshir* is the active participle, "one who publishes".
