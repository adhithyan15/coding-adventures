## You'll want to know: la dédicace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C521-AUTEURS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la dédicace** — "signed message, inscription (in a book); dedication".

> L'auteur a écrit une dédicace dans mon livre.
>
> *The author wrote an inscription in my book.*

*Une séance de dédicaces* — a book signing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la dédicace* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēdicātiō* "a consecration", from *dēdicāre* "to dedicate" (Lewis & Short).
