# Chinese pre-A1 mock 1 — examiner rubric

This rubric applies **only to form 1** and its separate [learner paper](mock-1-paper.md)
and [answer key](mock-1-answer-key.md). The target is a project-defined HSK
precursor, not an official HSK award. Do not infer calibration or learner
readiness from a complete artifact set.

Administer reading for 5 minutes, listening for 5, writing for 10, then
speaking for 5 after 1 minute of preparation. The first three sections run
continuously. A full timed mock is not a five-minute lesson. Record speaking;
retain both the candidate sheet and audio. Play each listening/dictation cue
exactly twice, without showing the key. Permit one slow spoken repeat in
speaking, but no written answer script. No dictionary, translator, pinyin
input aid, tracing guide, or model visible during writing.

Award **25 per skill / 100 total**. Passing requires **at least 15/25 in each
of reading, listening, writing, and speaking and 60/100 overall in the same
sitting**, with every section attempted. An unattempted part earns zero; do not
average it away. A strong skill cannot compensate for a weak one.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->
Reading 1–5: five objective selections, **5 points each**. Compare with this
form's key. No partial credit; do not reward pinyin supplied over a character.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->
Listening 6–10: five objective selections, **5 points each**. Play the exact
keyed Mandarin cue twice at a natural, gentle pace; do not paraphrase a cue
or add English. Score the chosen response, not the candidate's handwriting.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->
Writing 11: show the key's card for ten seconds, remove it, then score the
independently recalled simplified character out of **5**: recognizable
identity 3, complete shape 1, legibility 1. A trace or visible model is not
independent recall.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->
Writing 12: **5** = correct pinyin syllable 3 + correctly placed tone mark 2.
Writing 13: **5** = correct simplified-character identity 4 + legibility 1.
No transcript or copyable character is visible. Accept a consistent, legible
handwritten shape; do not require a particular font or invisible stroke order.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
Writing 14–15: **5 each** = fulfils picture-supported communicative task 2,
retrieves a taught Chinese chunk without a model 1, writes its recognizable
simplified form 1, and is legible 1. The key gives sample answers, not the
only possible taught answer. Do not award a copied model or English fallback.
One short chunk suffices; extra length earns nothing.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->
Speaking 16–20: **5 each** = responds to the prompt 2, intelligible Mandarin
syllables 1, appropriate taught tone contrast 1, and interaction/turn-taking
1. For item 18, any authentic personal name is accepted and its spelling is
not marked; score the taught **wǒ shì** frame. For item 20, the third-tone
target is **hǎo**; do not replace it with a fourth-tone word. A slow repeat
request does not reduce a mark. A memorized answer unrelated to the prompt
does not earn task-completion points.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->

Two trained raters independently score writing and speaking. Preserve their
item marks; if a skill total differs by more than 2 points or one rater would
pass and the other fail, a third rater adjudicates before reporting. Respect
documented regional pronunciation when intelligible; the target remains
standard Mandarin with simplified-character writing. Both mocks passed can
support only a **mock-ready** claim; a preregistered book-only human study is
still required for **learner-proven** readiness.
