## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੌਂ** (*nauṁ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *nava*, "nine" (CDIAL 6984).
