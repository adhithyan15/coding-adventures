## You'll want to know: la diffusion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C283-ANTENNE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la diffusion** — "the broadcasting, the showing; the spread".

> La diffusion du match commence à vingt heures.
>
> *The broadcast of the match starts at eight in the evening.*

*En diffusion* — being shown, on air.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diffusion* is a learned borrowing from Latin *diffūsiō* "a spreading out" (EtymDB), from *diffundere* "to pour out".
