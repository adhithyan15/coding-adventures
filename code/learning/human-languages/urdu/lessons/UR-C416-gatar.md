## You'll want to know: گٹر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C416-KURA416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گٹر** — *gaṭar* — "a sewer; a covered drain".

> سڑک کے نیچے ایک بڑا گٹر ہے۔
>
> *saṛak ke nīche ek baṛā gaṭar hai.*
>
> There's a big sewer under the road.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گٹر** (*gaṭar*) is borrowed from English *gutter*; in Urdu it names a sewer.
