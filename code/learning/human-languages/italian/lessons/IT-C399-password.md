## You'll want to know: la password
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C399-ACCOUNT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la password** — "the password".

> Se dimentichi la password, clicca qui.
>
> *If you forget your password, click here.*

Feminine in Italian; less often *la parola d'ordine*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*password* was borrowed from English *password* (EtymDB), built from *pass* and *word*: the word that lets you pass a guard.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
