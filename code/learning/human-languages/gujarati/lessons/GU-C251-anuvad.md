## You'll want to know: અનુવાદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C251-MEANING251-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અનુવાદ** — *anuvād* — "translation".

> **તે પત્રનો અનુવાદ કરે છે.** — *te patrano anuvād kare che* — "She translates the letter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અનુવાદ** (*anuvād*) is Sanskrit *anuvāda*, built from *anu*, "after", and *vāda*, "speaking", taken over whole as a tatsama. CDIAL has no entry for it.
