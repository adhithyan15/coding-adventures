## You'll want to know: ふるい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C162-REPAIR162-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるい** — *furui* — "old (of things)".

> **この いすは ふるいです。** — *kono isu wa furui desu* — "This chair is old."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるい** (*furui*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
