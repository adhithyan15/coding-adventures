## You'll want to know: हलवाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C424-DHABA424-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हलवाई** — *halvāī* — "a sweet-maker; a sweet shop".

> हलवाई की दुकान से मैंने लड्डू ख़रीदे।
>
> *halvāī kī dukān se maiṁne laḍḍū kharīde.*
>
> I bought laddus from the sweet shop.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हलवाई** (*halvāī*) is built on हलवा, which is usually traced to Arabic *ḥalwā*, "a sweetmeat", through Persian; the ending -āī makes the person who makes it.
