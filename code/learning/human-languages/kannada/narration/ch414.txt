Kannada, chapter 414: Limits.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಟ್ಟುನಿಟ್ಟು (kaṭṭuniṭṭu) — strictness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to estimate, then the Kannada for an average.

You'll want to know: ಕಟ್ಟುನಿಟ್ಟು.
ಕಟ್ಟುನಿಟ್ಟು — kaṭṭuniṭṭu — "strictness".
ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಯ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಯಮಗಳು ಕಟ್ಟುನಿಟ್ಟಾಗಿ ಇರುತ್ತವೆ. parīkṣeya samayadalli niyamagaḷu kaṭṭuniṭṭāgi iruttave. During exams the rules are strict.
ಕಟ್ಟುನಿಟ್ಟಾದ, strict; ಕಟ್ಟುನಿಟ್ಟಾಗಿ, strictly.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಟ್ಟುನಿಟ್ಟು (kaṭṭuniṭṭu) is native Kannada, a pair-word built on kaṭṭu, "to bind, a restraint" (DEDR 1147); the second half, niṭṭu, is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಗರಿಷ್ಠ (gariṣṭha) — maximum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an average, then the Kannada for strictness.

You'll want to know: ಗರಿಷ್ಠ.
ಗರಿಷ್ಠ — gariṣṭha — "maximum".
ಈ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಗರಿಷ್ಠ ವೇಗ ನಲವತ್ತು. ī rasteyalli gariṣṭha vēga nalavattu. The maximum speed on this road is forty.
ಗರಿಷ್ಠ (gariṣṭha) ಅಂಕ, the top mark.

The word, taken apart.
ಗರಿಷ್ಠ (gariṣṭha) is borrowed from Sanskrit gariṣṭha, "heaviest", the superlative of guru, "heavy" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಿಗದಿಪಡಿಸು (nigadipaḍisu) — to fix; to set.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for maximum, then the Kannada for minimum.

You'll want to know: ನಿಗದಿಪಡಿಸು.
ನಿಗದಿಪಡಿಸು — nigadipaḍisu — "to fix; to set".
ನಾವು ಮದುವೆಯ ದಿನಾಂಕವನ್ನು ನಿಗದಿಪಡಿಸಿದೆವು. nāvu maduveya dināṅkavannu nigadipaḍisidevu. We fixed the date of the wedding.
Past (I …): ನಿಗದಿಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, nigadipaḍiside.
ನಿಗದಿತ, fixed: ನಿಗದಿತ ಸಮಯ, the set time.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಗದಿಪಡಿಸು (nigadipaḍisu) is built from nigadi, "fixed", with -paḍisu, which makes a verb; the origin of nigadi is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನಿರ್ಬಂಧಿಸು (nirbandhisu) — to restrict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for minimum, then the Kannada for to fix.

You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ಬಂಧಿಸು.
ನಿರ್ಬಂಧಿಸು — nirbandhisu — "to restrict".
ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಪ್ಲಾಸ್ಟಿಕ್ ಬಳಕೆಯನ್ನು ನಿರ್ಬಂಧಿಸಿತು. sarkāra plāsṭik baḷakeyannu nirbandhisitu. The government restricted the use of plastic.
Past (I …): ನಿರ್ಬಂಧಿಸಿದೆ, nirbandhiside.
ನಿರ್ಬಂಧ, a restriction.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರ್ಬಂಧಿಸು (nirbandhisu) is built on Sanskrit nirbandha, "perseverance, insistence" (CDIAL 7355), from bandh, "to bind", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
