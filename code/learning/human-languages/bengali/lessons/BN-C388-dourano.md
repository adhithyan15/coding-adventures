## You'll want to know: দৌড়ানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C388-TRAM388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**দৌড়ানো** — *douṛāno* — "to race; to run fast".

> অরুণ দৌড়ে বাসে উঠল।
>
> *ôrun douṛe bāse uṭhlô.*
>
> Arun ran and got on the bus.

Past (he/she …): তিনি দৌড়ালেন, *tini douṛālen*.

The -এ form দৌড়ে is the one you hear most: দৌড়ে এল, came running.

The first vowel sign is ৌ, the vowel ou.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দৌড়ানো** (*douṛāno*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *dauṛā* and *dauṛāna*, "to run", under Sanskrit *drávati*, "runs" (CDIAL 6624), and notes that the word probably came east from the languages to the west.
