German, chapter 416: Am Flughafen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
einchecken — to check in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to ride without a ticket, then the German for to queue.

You'll want to know: einchecken.
einchecken — "to check in".
Zwei Stunden vor dem Abflug checken wir ein. Two hours before departure we check in.
Separable: ich checke ein, ich habe eingecheckt. Also at a hotel.

The word, taken apart.
einchecken is built from ein- 'in' and checken, which was borrowed from English check (EtymDB), on the model of English check in.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: die Bordkarte.
die Bordkarte — "the boarding pass".
Die Bordkarte ist auf ihrem Handy. The boarding pass is on her phone.
Plural: die Bordkarten. An Bord — on board.

The word, taken apart.
Bordkarte is built from Bord 'board, side of a ship', inherited from Old High German bort 'edge, rim' (EtymDB), and Karte 'card', which goes back to Latin charta 'sheet of paper' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

der Flugsteig — "the gate (at an airport)".
Der Flugsteig für unseren Flug ist ganz am Ende. The gate for our flight is right at the end.
Plural: die Flugsteige. People also say das Gate.

Flugsteig is built from Flug 'flight', inherited from Proto-Germanic flugiz (EtymDB), and Steig 'path, way up', from steigen 'to climb'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
aufgeben — to give up; to check in (a bag); to post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the gate, then the German for the hand luggage.

You'll want to know: aufgeben.
aufgeben — "to give up; to check in (a bag); to post".
Den großen Koffer gibt sie auf, den kleinen nimmt sie mit. She checks in the big suitcase and takes the small one with her.
Separable, strong: gibt auf, gab auf, hat aufgegeben. The everyday sense is to give up: Er gibt nie auf.

The word, taken apart.
aufgeben is built from auf 'up' and geben 'to give', inherited from Proto-Germanic gebaną (EtymDB). EtymDB pairs it with the rare English upgive.
