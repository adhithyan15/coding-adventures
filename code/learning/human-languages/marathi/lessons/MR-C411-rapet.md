## You'll want to know: रपेट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C411-SAFAR411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रपेट** — *rapeṭ* — "a ride; an outing".

> सकाळी आम्ही सायकलवरून रपेट मारली.
>
> *sakāḷī āmhī sāykalvarūn rapeṭ mārlī.*
>
> In the morning we went for a ride on our bicycles.

Feminine.

रपेट मारणे, to go for a quick ride or round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रपेट** (*rapeṭ*) is a Marathi word for a short ride or round trip; its origin is not settled.
