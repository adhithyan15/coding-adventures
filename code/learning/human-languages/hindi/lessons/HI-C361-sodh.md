## You'll want to know: शोध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C361-SHODH361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शोध** — *śodh* — "research".

> मीरा पानी पर शोध कर रही है।
>
> *Mīrā pānī par śodh kar rahī hai.*
>
> Mira is doing research on water.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोध** (*śodh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *śodha*, "purification, cleansing" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams does not give the sense of research, which is usually taken as a modern Hindi one.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
