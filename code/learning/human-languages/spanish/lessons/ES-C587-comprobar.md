## You'll want to know: comprobar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C587-EVIDENTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**comprobar** — "to check, to verify".

> Comprueba el precio antes de pagar.
>
> *Check the price before paying.*

Stem change o→ue: *compruebo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comprobar* is inherited from Latin *comprobare* 'to confirm, to approve', built on *probare* 'to test' (EtymDB).
