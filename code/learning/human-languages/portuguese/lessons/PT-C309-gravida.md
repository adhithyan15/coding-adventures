## You'll want to know: grávida
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C309-NASCIMENTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**grávida** — "pregnant".

> A minha irmã está grávida de seis meses.
>
> *My sister is six months pregnant.*

*Estar grávida de* + the months.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grávida* was borrowed from Latin *gravida* "pregnant" (EtymDB), from *gravis* "heavy".
