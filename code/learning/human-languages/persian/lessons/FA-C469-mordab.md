## You'll want to know: مرداب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C469-TALAB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مرداب** (*mordâb*) — "a marsh, a swamp".

> پرنده های زیادی در مرداب زندگی میکنند.
>
> *Parande-hâ-ye ziyâdi dar mordâb zendegi mikonand.*
>
> Many birds live in the marsh.

Literally 'dead water': *mord* 'dead' + *âb* 'water'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mordâb* is usually analysed as native *mord* 'dead', from *mordan* 'to die', and *âb* 'water', from Middle Persian *āb* (EtymDB): still, standing water.
