# ભગાડવું (bhagāḍvũ) — to chase away; to drive off

## You'll want to know: ભગાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C421-NOISE421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભગાડવું** — *bhagāḍvũ* — "to chase away; to drive off".

> ચોકીદાર ઢોરને દરવાજા પાસેથી ભગાડે છે.
>
> *cokīdār ḍhorne darvājā pāsethī bhagāḍe che.*
>
> The watchman chases the cattle away from the gate.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કૂતરાને ભગાડ્યો, *teṇe kūtrāne bhagāḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભગાડવું** (*bhagāḍvũ*) is the causative partner of ભાગવું, "to run away", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *bhajyátē*, "is broken" (CDIAL 9361): to make someone run off.
