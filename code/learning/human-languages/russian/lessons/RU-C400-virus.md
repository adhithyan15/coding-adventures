## You'll want to know: вирус
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C400-BEZOPASNOST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**вирус** (*vírus*) — "a virus".

> На компьютере вирус.
>
> *Na kompyútere vírus.*
>
> There is a virus on the computer.

Masculine. For computers and for illness alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вирус** is usually traced to Latin *vīrus* "slime, poison" (Lewis & Short); the computer sense came through English.
