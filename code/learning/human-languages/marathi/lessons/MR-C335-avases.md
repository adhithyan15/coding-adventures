## You'll want to know: अवशेष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C335-UTKHANAN335-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवशेष** — *avaśeṣ* — "remains; ruins".

> टेकडीवर एका जुन्या शहराचे अवशेष आहेत.
>
> *ṭekḍīvar ekā junyā śahrāce avaśeṣ āhet.*
>
> There are the remains of an old city on the hill.

Masculine.

Mostly in the plural: अवशेष सापडणे, remains are found.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवशेष** (*avaśeṣ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *avaśeṣa*, 'a remainder, what is left' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
