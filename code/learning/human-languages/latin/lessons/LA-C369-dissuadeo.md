## You'll want to know: dissuādeō, dissuādēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C369-HORTOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dissuādeō, dissuādēre** — "to advise against, to dissuade".

> Amīcī mihi hoc iter dissuāsērunt.
>
> *My friends advised me against this journey.*

*dissuādeō, dissuādēre, dissuāsī, dissuāsum*, with the dative of the person: the opposite of *suādeō*. English *dissuade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dissuādeō* is built from *dis-* "apart, away" and *suādeō* "to advise" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *dissuade* comes from it.
