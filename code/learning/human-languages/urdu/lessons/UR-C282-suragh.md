## You'll want to know: سراغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C282-TAFTISH282-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سراغ** — *surāgh* — "a clue; a trace".

> اگر سراغ ملا تو پولیس آئے گی۔
>
> *agar surāgh milā to polīs āe gī.*
>
> If a clue turns up, the police will come.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سراغ** (*surāgh*) is usually traced to Persian *surāgh*, "a trace, a track". **سراغ ملا** says a clue was found; **سراغ لگانا** is to track something down.
