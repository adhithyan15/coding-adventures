# ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ (mokaddame) — a lawsuit

## You'll want to know: ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C358-VICHARANE358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ** — *mokaddame* — "a lawsuit".

> ನೆರೆಯವರ ನಡುವೆ ಜಮೀನಿನ ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *nereyavara naḍuve jamīnina mokaddame hattu varṣa naḍeyitu.*
>
> A land lawsuit between the neighbours went on for ten years.

Plural: ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆಗಳು, *mokaddamegaḷu*.

A lawsuit is brought against someone: ಅವನ ಮೇಲೆ ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೊಕದ್ದಮೆ** (*mokaddame*) is a loan from Arabic *muqaddama*, "a beginning, a case at law", which came through Persian and Hindustani.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
