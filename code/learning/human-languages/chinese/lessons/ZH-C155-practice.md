## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-TIMETABLE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C155-CONG-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-CONG-DAO-01, ZH-LEX-C155-CHUFA-01, ZH-LEX-C155-KAIMEN-01, ZH-LEX-C155-GUANMEN-01, ZH-LEX-C48-CLOCK48-04, ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-02] -->

> **A:** *qǐngwèn, huǒchē jǐ diǎn chūfā* — Excuse me, what time does the train leave?
>
> **B:** 早上八点开
>
> *zǎoshang bā diǎn kāi* — At eight in the morning.
>
> **A:** *jǐ diǎn dào dàxué* — What time does it get to the university?
>
> **B:** 十点半到
>
> *shí diǎn bàn dào* — At half past ten.
>
> **A:** 书 星期天开门吗
>
> *shū, xīngqītiān kāimén ma* — The bookshop: is it open on Sunday?
>
> **B:** 星期天不开门
>
> *xīngqītiān bù kāimén* — It's closed on Sundays.
>
> **A:** 星期六呢
>
> *xīngqīliù ne* — And Saturday?
>
> **B:** 上午十点到下午五点开门
>
> *shàngwǔ shí diǎn dào xiàwǔ wǔ diǎn kāimén* — Open from ten to five.
>
> **A:** *xiàwǔ wǔ diǎn guānmén* — So it closes at five.

The questions come in pinyin, because *jǐ* is not a character you write yet; the answers come off the notices, in characters.

## What you've built — reading the times off a notice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-TIMETABLE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-CONG-FROM-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-CONG-DAO-01, ZH-LEX-C155-GUANMEN-01, ZH-LEX-C48-CLOCK48-03] -->

A start, an end, and a verb for what happens:

- **早上八点开 十点半到** *zǎoshang bā diǎn kāi, shí diǎn bàn dào* — leaves at eight, arrives at half past ten.
- **从八点到十点** *cóng bā diǎn dào shí diǎn* — from eight to ten.
- **星期一到星期五** *xīngqīyī dào xīngqīwǔ* — Monday to Friday.
- *wǎnshang bā diǎn guānmén* — closes at eight in the evening.

**Listen.** *Huǒchē jǐ diǎn dào?*

**Speak.** Give the hours of a shop you know, from opening to closing.

[YOU WRITE: 十点半到 — then 从, one 人 following another]
