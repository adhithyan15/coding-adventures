Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮನಸ್ಸು (manassu) — the mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an illness, then the Kannada for a patient.

You'll want to know: ಮನಸ್ಸು.
ಮನಸ್ಸು — manassu — "the mind".

The word, taken apart.
ಮನಸ್ಸು (manassu) is Sanskrit manas, "mind", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with Kannada's added -u (CDIAL 9822). The final -s is doubled before Kannada's -u.

ನಿರಾಸೆ (nirāse) comes from Sanskrit nirāśa, "hopeless", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 7278). The ś became s, and the word ends in Kannada's -e: "hopelessness".
