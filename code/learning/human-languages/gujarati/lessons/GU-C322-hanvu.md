# હણવું (haṇvũ) — to slay

## You'll want to know: હણવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C322-HUMLO322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**હણવું** — *haṇvũ* — "to slay".

> યોદ્ધાએ એક વાઘને હણ્યો.
>
> *yoddhāe ek vāghne haṇyo.*
>
> The warrior slew a tiger.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દુશ્મનને હણ્યો, *teṇe duśmanne haṇyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હણવું** (*haṇvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hánati*, "strikes, kills", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to strike, kill" (CDIAL 13963). It is a word of old stories; **મારવું** is the everyday verb.

One more word for telling what happened.
