## You'll want to know: 父女
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C507-FATHER507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**父女** — *fùnǚ* — "father and daughter".

> **他们父女一起去了舞会** *tāmen fùnǚ yìqǐ qù le wǔhuì* — Father and daughter went to the dance together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**父女** (*fùnǚ*) is built from 父 'a father' and 女 'a daughter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjuX* and *nrjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
