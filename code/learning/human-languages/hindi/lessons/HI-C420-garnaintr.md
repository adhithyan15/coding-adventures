## You'll want to know: गड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C420-DHALAN420-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गड़ना** — *gaṛnā* — "to dig into; to sink in (of something pointed)".

> जूते की कील मेरे पैर में गड़ रही थी।
>
> *jūte kī kīl mere pair meṁ gaṛ rahī thī.*
>
> The nail in my shoe was digging into my foot.

Past (it …): वह काँटा पैर में गड़ गया, *vah kā̃ṭā pair meṁ gaṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गड़ना** (*gaṛnā*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *gaḍati*, "drops, runs as liquid" (CDIAL 3973), which lists Hindi *gaṛnā*, "to sink into"; CDIAL does not say how the one sense led to the other, so take the link as probable.
