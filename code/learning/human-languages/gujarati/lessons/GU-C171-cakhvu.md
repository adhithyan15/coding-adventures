## You'll want to know: ચાખવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C171-TASTE171-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાખવું** — *cākhvũ* — "to taste".

> **માતા શાક ચાખે છે.** — *mātā śāk cākhe che* — "Mother tastes the curry."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાખવું** (*cākhvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*cakṣati*, "tastes", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to taste" (CDIAL 4557).
