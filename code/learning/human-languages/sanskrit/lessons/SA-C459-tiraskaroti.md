## You'll want to know: तिरस्करोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C459-ADARA459-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तिरस्करोति** — *tiraskaroti* — "insults".

> सः अतिथिं तिरस्करोति, तत् अनुचितम्।
>
> *saḥ atithiṃ tiraskaroti, tat anucitam.*
>
> He insults the guest; that is improper.

Past (he …): सः अतिथिं तिरस्कृतवान्, *saḥ atithiṃ tiraskṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिरस्करोति** (*tiraskaroti*) is built from *tiras*, "across, aside", and the root √kṛ, "to do", the root of **करोति**: to set aside; MW gives "to set aside" and later senses of slighting (MW).
