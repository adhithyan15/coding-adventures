## You'll want to know: গতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C377-UNNOTI377-03]; assesses=[] -->

**গতি** — *gôti* — "speed; pace".

> গতি না কমালে দুর্ঘটনা হতে পারে।
>
> *gôti nā kômāle durghôṭônā hôte pāre.*
>
> If you don't slow down, there could be an accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গতি** (*gôti*) is Sanskrit *gati*, going or motion (Monier-Williams): how fast something goes.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
