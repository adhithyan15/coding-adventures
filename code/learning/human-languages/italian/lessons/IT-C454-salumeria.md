## You'll want to know: la salumeria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C454-ALIMENTARI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la salumeria** — "the delicatessen (for cured meats)".

> In salumeria compro prosciutto e formaggio.
>
> *At the deli I buy ham and cheese.*

*I salumi* — cured meats.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*salumeria* is built on *salume* 'cured meat', from *sale* 'salt', which is inherited from Latin *sāl* (EtymDB).
