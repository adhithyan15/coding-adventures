Kannada, chapter 136: Thumb, Fingernail, Chest, Waist, Eyebrow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹೆಬ್ಬೆರಳು (hebberaḷu) — a thumb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an elbow, then the Kannada for a wrist.

You'll want to know: ಹೆಬ್ಬೆರಳು.
ಹೆಬ್ಬೆರಳು — hebberaḷu — "a thumb".

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆಬ್ಬೆರಳು (hebberaḷu) is native Dravidian, usually explained as "the big finger": heb-, a form of the root for "great", which DEDR 4411 lists as Kannada per, pēr; then beraḷu, "finger" (DEDR 5409).
