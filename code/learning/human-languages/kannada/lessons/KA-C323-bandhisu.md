# ಬಂಧಿಸು (bandhisu) — to arrest

## You'll want to know: ಬಂಧಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C323-APARADHA323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಧಿಸು** — *bandhisu* — "to arrest".

> ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಕಳ್ಳನನ್ನು ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಬಂಧಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *polīsaru kaḷḷanannu beḷagge bandhisidaru.*
>
> The police arrested the thief in the morning.

Past (I …): ಬಂಧಿಸಿದೆ, *bandhiside*.

The person arrested takes -ಅನ್ನು: ಕಳ್ಳನನ್ನು ಬಂಧಿಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಧಿಸು** (*bandhisu*) is built on Sanskrit *bandha*, "a bond", and also "custody" (CDIAL 9136), from the root *bandh*, "to bind", with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
