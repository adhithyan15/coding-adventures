## You'll want to know: تراشنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C482-GROOM482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تراشنا** — *tarāshnā* — "to trim; to pare".

> دادا نے اپنی داڑھی تراشی۔
>
> *dādā ne apnī dāṛhī tarāshī.*
>
> My grandfather trimmed his beard.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مونچھ تراشی, *us ne mūṅchh tarāshī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تراشنا** (*tarāshnā*) is usually traced to Persian *tarāsh*, "cutting", from *tarāshīdan*, "to shave, to carve", made into an Urdu verb.
