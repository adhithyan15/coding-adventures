## You'll want to know: صاحبخانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C537-CHANGES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صاحبخانه** (*sâhebkhâne*) — "a landlord, a landlady; the host".

> صاحبخانه اجاره را بالا برد.
>
> *sâhebkhâne ejâre râ bâlâ bord.*
>
> The landlord put the rent up.

*sâheb* 'owner' + *khâne* 'house'. Also the host at a party.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sâhebkhâne* is built in Persian on *sâheb*, 'owner', from Arabic *ṣāḥib*, 'companion' (EtymDB), and native *khâne*, 'house'.
