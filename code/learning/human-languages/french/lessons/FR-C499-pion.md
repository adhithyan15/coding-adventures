## You'll want to know: le pion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C499-JEUX-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le pion** — "pawn, counter (in a game)".

> Elle avance son pion de trois cases.
>
> *She moves her counter forward three squares.*

In school slang, *le pion* is also a supervisor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le pion* is inherited from Late Latin *pedō* "a foot soldier" (EtymDB), built on *pēs* "foot" (Lewis & Short).
