## You'll want to know: ઉઝરડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C294-LAPAS294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઝરડો** — *ujharḍo* — "a graze; a scratch".

> મીરાના ઘૂંટણ પર મોટો ઉઝરડો હતો.
>
> *Mīrānā ghū̃ṭaṇ par moṭo ujharḍo hato.*
>
> Mira had a big graze on her knee.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઝરડો** (*ujharḍo*) comes from *\*ujjharati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists Gujarati *ujharṛo*, "a scratch" (CDIAL 1675). It is skin scraped in a fall: **ઘૂંટણ પર ઉઝરડો**.
