## You'll want to know: премьера
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C519-CURTAIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**премьера** (*premyéra*) — "a premiere".

> Премьера спектакля будет в субботу.
>
> *Premyéra spektáklya búdet v subbótu.*
>
> The premiere of the play is on Saturday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*премьера* was borrowed from French *première* 'first' (EtymDB).
