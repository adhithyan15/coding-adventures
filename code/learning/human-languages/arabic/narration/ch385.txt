Arabic, chapter 385: Organizations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منظمة (munaẓẓama) — an organization.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for agriculture, then the Arabic for development.

You'll want to know: منظمة.
منظمة — munaẓẓama — "an organization".
تساعد المنظمة الأطفال. tusāʿidu al-munaẓẓama al-aṭfāl. The organization helps children.

The word, taken apart.
منظمة (munaẓẓama) is built on the native Arabic root n-ẓ-m, "to string pearls, to put in order" (Lane). Munaẓẓama is a Form II passive participle, feminine, something put in order: an organization.

Lesson 3 of 5.
نقابة (niqāba) — a trade union.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an organization, then the Arabic for an association.

You'll want to know: نقابة.
نقابة — niqāba — "a trade union".
دافعت النقابة عن العمال. dāfaʿat an-niqāba ʿan al-ʿummāl. The union defended the workers.

The word, taken apart.
نقابة (niqāba) is built on the native Arabic root n-q-b, "to bore through; a chief" (Lane). A naqīb is the chief of a group; niqāba is his office, and now a trade union.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عضو (ʿuḍw) — a member.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a trade union, then the Arabic for a council.

You'll want to know: عضو.
عضو — ʿuḍw — "a member".
أخي عضو في نادي الشطرنج. akhī ʿuḍw fī nādī ash-shaṭranj. My brother is a member of the chess club.
Plural: أعضاء, aʿḍāʾ.

The word, taken apart.
عضو (ʿuḍw) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ḍ-w, "a limb" (Lane). A ʿuḍw is a limb of the body, and so a member of any body of people.
