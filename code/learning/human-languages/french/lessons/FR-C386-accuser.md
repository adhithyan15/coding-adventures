## You'll want to know: accuser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C386-ACCUSER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**accuser** — "to accuse".

> Ils l'accusent d'avoir volé un vélo.
>
> *They accuse him of stealing a bike.*

*Accuser quelqu'un de quelque chose*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accuser* comes through Old French *acuser* (EtymDB) from Latin *accūsāre* "to call to account, to accuse", built from *ad* and *causa* "cause, lawsuit" (Lewis & Short).
