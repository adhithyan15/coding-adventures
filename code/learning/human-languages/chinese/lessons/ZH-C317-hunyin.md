## You'll want to know: hūnyīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C317-LOOKBACK317-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hūnyīn* — "marriage; the bond of marriage".

> *tāmen de hūnyīn yǒu wèntí* — Their marriage is in trouble.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hūnyīn* is built from *hūn* and *yīn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwon* and *'jin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses each with the pair itself, 'marriage', and explains *hūn* by the evening hour of old weddings.
