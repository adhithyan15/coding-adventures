## You'll want to know: なきごえ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C267-MAIGO267-04]; assesses=[] -->

**なきごえ** — *nakigoe* — "a cry; the sound of crying".

> **こどもの なきごえが しました。** — *kodomo no nakigoe ga shimashita* — "We heard a child crying."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なきごえ** (*nakigoe*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *naki*, "crying" (from *naku*), and *goe*, "voice" (from *koe*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
