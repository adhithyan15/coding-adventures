Lesson 1 of 7.
lóudào — stairwell; corridor (in a block of flats).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a stranger, then the Mandarin for the property management office.

You'll want to know: lóudào.
lóudào — "stairwell; corridor (in a block of flats)".
lóudào lǐ de dēng huài le — The light in the stairwell is broken.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
lóudào is built from lóu 'a building of several floors' and dào 'a way', both inherited from Middle Chinese, luw and dawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a house on a house' and the second 'a road'.

安心 — ānxīn — "with a mind at rest; settled".
这个社区很好我很安心 zhège shèqū hěn hǎo wǒ hěn ānxīn — This is a good neighbourhood, and my mind is at rest.
The new character, in pieces: 安 (ān) — six strokes: the roof of 家 on top, three strokes, and 女 under it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
安心 (ānxīn) is built from 安 (ān) 'calm' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'an and sim (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'peaceful' and 'to come to rest'.

Script — the roof of 家, then 女.
安 (ān).
Two pieces, top before bottom.
The roof, three strokes, as on top of 家 and 字: a dot at the top, a short stroke down on the left, then a bar across that turns down at the right end and hooks.
Under the roof, 女, three strokes, as you wrote it: a stroke that falls to the left and turns to the right, a long stroke falling to the left, then a long bar across.
3 + 3 = six.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 安 (ān), all six, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 安 (ān)? (Six.) What is on top? (The roof of 家.) What sits under it? (女.)

Lesson 4 of 7.
不安 (bù'ān) — uneasy; worried.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the stairwell of a block, then the Mandarin for with a mind at rest.

You'll want to know: 不安.
不安 — bù'ān — "uneasy; worried".
我一个人在家的时候有点儿不安 wǒ yí ge rén zài jiā de shíhou yǒudiǎnr bù'ān — When I'm at home on my own, I feel a little uneasy.
Tone: 不 keeps its own falling tone here, bù, because ān after it is a level tone, not a falling one.

The word, taken apart.
不安 (bù'ān) is built from 不 'not' and 安 'calm', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjut and 'an (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 7.
ménjìn — door entry system; a card-entry door.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for with a mind at rest, then the Mandarin for uneasy (ill at ease).

You'll want to know: ménjìn.
ménjìn — "door entry system; a card-entry door".
wǒmen xiǎoqū yǒu ménjìn suǒyǐ hěn ānquán — Our estate has a door entry system, so it's safe.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
ménjìn is built from 门 'a gate' and jìn 'to forbid', both inherited from Middle Chinese, mwon and kimH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to restrain' and 'to stop'. The card-and-lock system is a modern use.

zhì'ān — "law and order; public order".

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhì'ān is built from zhì 'to govern' and 安 'calm', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dri and 'an (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to put in order' and also lists it twice in the departing tone, drijH and driH, with the same gloss. Mandarin zhì follows a departing reading.
