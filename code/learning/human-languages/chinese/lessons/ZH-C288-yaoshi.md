## You'll want to know: 要是
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C288-YAOSHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**要是** — *yàoshi* — "if".

> **要是明天下雨 我就不去了** *yàoshi míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le* — If it rains tomorrow, I won't go.

It sounds like *yàoshi*, a key: the same sound, a different word. Every character in it is one you write.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**要是** (*yàoshi*) is built from 要 and 是, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jiewH* and *dzyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to bind by agreement' and 'right, as against wrong'. 'If' for the pair is the modern word's own sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C288-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-03, ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-05] -->

要是 opens the condition; 就 opens what follows, after its subject: **我就不去了** *wǒ jiù bú qù le*.
