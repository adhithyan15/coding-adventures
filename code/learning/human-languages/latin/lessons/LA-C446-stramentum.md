## You'll want to know: strāmentum, strāmentī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C446-LECTULUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**strāmentum, strāmentī** — "straw bedding, straw; a covering".

> Pauper in strāmentō dormit, nōn in lectō.
>
> *The poor man sleeps on straw, not in a bed.*

Second declension, neuter: *strāmentum, strāmentī n.* Straw spread out for sleeping on, or for animals.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*strāmentum* is built on *sternō* 'to spread' (Lewis & Short), from its participle *strātus*, with the suffix *-mentum*: something spread out to lie on.
