## You'll want to know: सिलवाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C513-DARZI513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिलवाना** — *silvānā* — "to have (clothes) stitched".

> मैंने दर्ज़ी से एक नया कुर्ता सिलवाया।
>
> *Maiṁne darzī se ek nayā kurtā silvāyā.*
>
> I had a new kurta stitched by the tailor.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने एक कमीज़ सिलवाई, *usne ek kamīz silvāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिलवाना** (*silvānā*) is the second causative of *sīnā*, "to sew": CDIAL files *sīnā* and its causative *silānā* under Sanskrit *sīvyati*, "sews" (CDIAL 13444). It means getting someone else to do the sewing.
