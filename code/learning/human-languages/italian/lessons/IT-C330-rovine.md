## You'll want to know: le rovine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C330-SCAVI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le rovine** — "the ruins".

> Abbiamo visitato le rovine di un tempio.
>
> *We visited the ruins of a temple.*

*Andare in rovina* — to go to ruin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rovina* is inherited from Latin *ruīna* "a falling down", from *ruere* "to rush, to fall" (Lewis & Short).
