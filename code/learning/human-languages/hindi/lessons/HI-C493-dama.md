## You'll want to know: दमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C493-DAMA493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दमा** — *damā* — "asthma".

> दादी को दमा है, इसलिए वह ठंडी हवा से दूर रहती हैं।
>
> *dādī ko damā hai, isliye vah ṭhaṇḍī havā se dūr rahtī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother has asthma, so she keeps away from cold air.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दमा** (*damā*) is usually traced to Persian *dama*, "asthma", a word beside *dam*, "breath": the illness of the breath.
