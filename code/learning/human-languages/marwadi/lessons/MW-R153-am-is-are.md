# Am, is, are — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *I am here*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HO-01, MW-LEX-C153-ANIMAL153-01, MW-SCRIPT-C153-UU-01, MW-CONJ-PAN-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ADVERSATIVE-PAN-01, MW-LEX-C117-WHY117-05, MW-GRAMMAR-CAUSAL-KYUNKAI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ALTERNATIVE-CHOICE-01, MW-FUNCTION-ASK-PREFERENCE-ALTERNATIVES-01] -->

The three forms side by side, now that each one is taught:

| who | to be |
|---|---|
| **हूं** | **हूं** |
| **आप** | **हो** |
| **वो**, **राम**, **ऊंट** | **है** |

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *I am here*; *you are there*; *Ram is there*]
- [YOU SAY: *that is a camel*; *the camel is here*]
- [YOU SAY: *I am happy because the tea is hot*]
- [YOU SAY: *you are there, but I am here*]
- [YOU SAY: *tea or water?*, and answer *tea*]
- [YOU READ: **ऊंट**, and name its first letter]
