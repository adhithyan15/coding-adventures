## You'll want to know: a garrafeira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C450-MERCEARIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a garrafeira** — "the wine shop; the wine cellar".

> Comprámos um vinho velho na garrafeira.
>
> *We bought an old wine at the wine shop.*

*Vinho garrafeira* — a long-aged wine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garrafeira* is built on *garrafa* 'bottle' (EtymDB) with *-eira*, a place for them; the origin of *garrafa* is not settled.
