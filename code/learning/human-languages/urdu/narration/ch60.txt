Urdu, chapter 60: Key, Cup, Spoon, Knife, Fork.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چابی (chābī) — a key.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a mirror, then the Urdu for a light.

چابی — chābī — "a key".
