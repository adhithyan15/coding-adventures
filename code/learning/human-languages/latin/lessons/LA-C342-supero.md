## You'll want to know: superō, superāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C342-CLADES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**superō, superāre** — "to overcome, to defeat; to surpass".

> Parva manus magnum exercitum superāvit.
>
> *A small band defeated a large army.*

*superō, superāre, superāvī, superātum*, from *super*, 'above'. Also to climb over: *montēs superāre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**superō** is built on *super*, "above, over" (Lewis & Short): to go over, to get the upper hand.
