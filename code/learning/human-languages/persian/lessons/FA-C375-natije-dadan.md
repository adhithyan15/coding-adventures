## You'll want to know: نتیجه دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C375-MAZIYYAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نتیجه دادن** (*natije dâdan*) — "to pay off, to work".

> تلاش او بالاخره نتیجه داد.
>
> *talâsh-e u bâlâkhare natije dâd.*
>
> Her effort finally paid off.

*Natije* "result" + *dâdan* "to give" (stem *deh-*): literally to give a result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نتیجه دادن** is literally "to give a result": *natije* comes from Arabic *natīja* "result" (EtymDB), and *dâdan* "to give" is traced by EtymDB to PIE *deh₃-*.
