## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**geduldig** ← Middle High German *gedultic* (EtymDB 2.1): the word for "patience", from a verb "to endure", + *-ig*. EtymDB lists Old English *geþyldig*, "patient", beside it.
