## You'll want to know: сокровище
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C327-KLAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**сокровище** (*sokróvishche*) — "a treasure".

> Пираты спрятали сокровище на острове.
>
> *Piráty spryátali sokróvishche na óstrove.*
>
> The pirates hid the treasure on an island.

Neuter; often plural *сокровища*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сокровище* is usually traced to *сокровенный* "hidden", built on *со-* and the root of *крыть* "to cover": what is kept hidden. EtymDB lists no parent.
