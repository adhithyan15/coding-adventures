## You'll want to know: బలపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C520-KOLUKO520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**బలపడు** — *balapaḍu* — "to grow strong".

> వ్యాయామంతో నా కాళ్ళు బలపడ్డాయి.
>
> *vyāyāmantō nā kāḷḷu balapaḍḍāyi.*
>
> With exercise my legs grew strong.

Past (I …): బలపడ్డాను, *balapaḍḍānu*.

బలం, strength, + పడు. Also of friendships: స్నేహం బలపడింది.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బలపడు** (*balapaḍu*) is built from బలం, from Sanskrit *bala*, "power, strength" (CDIAL 9161) and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
