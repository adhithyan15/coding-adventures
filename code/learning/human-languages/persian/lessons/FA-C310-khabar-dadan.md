## You'll want to know: خبر دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C310-TAKZIB-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خبر دادن** (*khabar dâdan*) — "to let someone know, to tell the news".

> دوستم به من خبر داد که برادرش ازدواج کرده است.
>
> *Dustam be man khabar dâd ke barâdarash ezdevâj karde ast.*
>
> My friend let me know that his brother had got married.

The person told takes *be*: *be mâdaram khabar dâdam*. Present stem *dah-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khabar* "news" is borrowed from Arabic *khabar* (EtymDB). *dâdan* "to give" is inherited Persian: to give the news.
