## You'll want to know: el granizo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C699-TORMENTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el granizo** — "the hail".

> El granizo rompió los cristales.
>
> *The hail broke the windows.*

One hailstone is *un grano de granizo* or *una piedra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*granizo* is built on *grano* "grain", which is inherited from Latin *grānum* (EtymDB): hail falls like grains of ice.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
