## You'll want to know: संभव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C373-MUMKIN373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संभव** — *sambhav* — "possible (formal)".

> अगर सब मदद करेंगे तो यह काम संभव है।
>
> *agar sab madad kareṅge to yah kām sambhav hai.*
>
> If everyone helps, this job can be done.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संभव** (*sambhav*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sambhava*, "coming into being" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it as an adjective: what can come about.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
