## You'll want to know: चुकामूक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C434-GAIRSOY434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चुकामूक** — *cukāmūk* — "missing each other".

> स्टेशनवर आमची चुकामूक झाली.
>
> *sṭeśanvar āmcī cukāmūk jhālī.*
>
> We missed each other at the station.

Feminine.

चुकामूक होणे, two people fail to meet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुकामूक** (*cukāmūk*) is an echo compound built on **चुकणे**, "to miss", whose origin is not settled.
