Lesson 1 of 7.
प्रेरणा (preraṇā) — inspiration; urging.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the time ahead, then the Sanskrit for a start.

You'll want to know: प्रेरणा.
प्रेरणा — preraṇā — "inspiration; urging".
यदि गुरोः प्रेरणा अस्ति, तर्हि शिष्यः उत्तमं कार्यं करोति। yadi guroḥ preraṇā asti, tarhi śiṣyaḥ uttamaṃ kāryaṃ karoti. With a teacher's urging, a pupil does fine work.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रेरणा (preraṇā) is built on pra, "forth", and the root √īr, "to set in motion"; MW gives preraṇa, also preraṇā, as setting in motion, urging, inciting; "inspiration" is its modern use (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: प्रत्युत.
प्रत्युत — pratyuta — "on the contrary".
यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि हानिः न, प्रत्युत लाभः भवति। yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi hāniḥ na, pratyuta lābhaḥ bhavati. If it rains, it is no loss; on the contrary, it is a gain.

The word, taken apart.
प्रत्युत (pratyuta) is built from prati, "back, against", and uta, "and, even"; MW gives pratyuta as on the contrary, rather (MW).

कर्मठः — karmaṭhaḥ — "hard-working".
यदि त्वं कर्मठः असि, तर्हि तव कार्यं सम्यक् भविष्यति। yadi tvaṃ karmaṭhaḥ asi, tarhi tava kāryaṃ samyak bhaviṣyati. If you are hard-working, your work will go well.

Lesson 5 of 7.
अध्यवस्यति (adhyavasyati) — resolves.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for on the contrary, then the Sanskrit for hard-working.

You'll want to know: अध्यवस्यति.
अध्यवस्यति — adhyavasyati — "resolves".
यदि सः दृढम् अध्यवस्यति, तर्हि सः अवश्यं जयति। yadi saḥ dṛḍham adhyavasyati, tarhi saḥ avaśyaṃ jayati. If he resolves firmly, he surely wins.
Past (he …): सः गन्तुम् अध्यवसितवान्, saḥ gantum adhyavasitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अध्यवस्यति (adhyavasyati) is built on adhi, ava and the root √so, "to bring to an end"; MW gives adhyavaso as to undertake, to accomplish, to determine; its past participle is adhyavasita (MW).
