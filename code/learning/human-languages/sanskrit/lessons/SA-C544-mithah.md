## You'll want to know: मिथः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C544-PARASPARA544-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मिथः** — *mithaḥ* — "together; mutually".

> यदि बालाः मिथः क्रीडन्ति, तर्हि ते प्रसन्नाः सन्ति।
>
> *yadi bālāḥ mithaḥ krīḍanti, tarhi te prasannāḥ santi.*
>
> If the children play together, they are happy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिथः** (*mithaḥ*) is the word MW gives for together, mutually, alternately; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
