## You'll want to know: उद्वेगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C452-UDVEGA452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्वेगः** — *udvegaḥ* — "anxiety".

> परीक्षायाः पूर्वं छात्रस्य उद्वेगः भवति।
>
> *parīkṣāyāḥ pūrvaṃ chātrasya udvegaḥ bhavati.*
>
> A student feels anxious before an exam.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्वेगः** (*udvegaḥ*) is built from *ud*, "up", and the root √vij, "to tremble"; MW gives "trembling" and "agitation, anxiety" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
