## You'll want to know: पर्ची
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C429-PARCHI429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्ची** — *parcī* — "a slip (of paper), a chit".

> बैंक में पहले एक पर्ची भरिए।
>
> *baiṅk meṁ pahle ek parcī bhariye.*
>
> At the bank, fill in a slip first.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्ची** (*parcī*) is usually traced to Persian *parča*, "a piece, a scrap", with the Hindi ending -ī for a small one: a slip for a bank, a doctor or a lottery.
