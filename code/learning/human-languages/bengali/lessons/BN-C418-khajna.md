## You'll want to know: খাজনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C418-MUNAFA418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**খাজনা** — *khājnā* — "land tax; rent paid on land".

> আগে গ্রামের লোকেরা রাজাকে খাজনা দিত।
>
> *āge grāmer lokerā rājāke khājnā ditô.*
>
> In the old days the villagers paid land tax to the king.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খাজনা** (*khājnā*) is usually traced to Arabic *khazāna*, "a treasury, a store", through Persian: the money that went into the treasury.
