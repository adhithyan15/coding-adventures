## You'll want to know: les fiançailles
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C489-NOCES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**les fiançailles** — "engagement (to marry)".

> Ils ont fêté leurs fiançailles avec toute la famille.
>
> *They celebrated their engagement with the whole family.*

Always plural and feminine: *des fiançailles*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*les fiançailles* is built on *fiancer* "to promise in marriage", from old *fiance* "trust, promise", which goes back to Vulgar Latin *fīdāre*, from Latin *fīdere* "to trust" (Lewis & Short).
