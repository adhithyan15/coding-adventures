Russian, chapter 59: Rain, Snow, Wind, Weather, World.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
дождь (dozhd) — rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a star, then the Russian for a cloud.

You'll want to know: дождь.
дождь (dozhd) — "rain".

What you've built.
One more word for rain, snow, wind, weather, world.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does дождь (dozhd) mean? ("Rain".) Say it once more.
