Marathi, chapter 294: The Flood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पूर (pūr) — a flood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to cave in, then the Marathi for a landslide.

You'll want to know: पूर.
पूर — pūr — "a flood".
नदीला पूर आल्यास हा पूल बंद होईल. nadīlā pūr ālyās hā pūl band hoīl. Should the river flood, this bridge will be closed.
Masculine.
नदीला पूर (pūr) येणे, the river floods; आल्यास, should it come.

The word, taken apart.
पूर (pūr) goes back to Sanskrit pūra, 'filling; a flood': CDIAL lists the Marathi word, as purā, 'filling; flood', under it (CDIAL 8330).

Lesson 2 of 5.
साचणे (sācṇe) — to collect; to pile up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a landslide, then the Marathi for a flood.

You'll want to know: साचणे.
साचणे — sācṇe — "to collect; to pile up".
रस्त्यावर गुडघाभर पाणी साचलं. rastyāvar guḍghābhar pāṇī sāclaṁ. Knee-deep water collected on the road.
Past (he …): तो साचला, to sāclā.
Of water, dust or work: काम साचलं, the work piled up.

The word, taken apart.
साचणे (sācṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi sācṇe, 'to accumulate', under saṁcayayati, 'piles up' (CDIAL 12867).

Lesson 3 of 5.
मदतकार्य (madatkārya) — relief work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a flood, then the Marathi for to collect.

You'll want to know: मदतकार्य.
मदतकार्य — madatkārya — "relief work".
पूर ओसरल्यावर मदतकार्य सुरू झालं. pūr osralyāvar madatkārya surū jhālaṁ. Once the flood went down, relief work began.
Neuter.
Built from मदत, help, and Sanskrit kārya, work.

The word, taken apart.
मदतकार्य (madatkārya) is a Marathi compound built from madat, 'help', a loan usually traced to Arabic madad, and Sanskrit kārya, 'work, a task' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
स्वयंसेवक (svayaṁsevak) — a volunteer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to collect, then the Marathi for relief work.

You'll want to know: स्वयंसेवक.
स्वयंसेवक — svayaṁsevak — "a volunteer".
शंभर स्वयंसेवकांनी लोकांना जेवण वाटलं. śambhar svayaṁsevakānnī lokānnā jevaṇ vāṭlaṁ. A hundred volunteers handed out food to people.
Masculine.
A woman volunteer is स्वयंसेविका.

The word, taken apart.
स्वयंसेवक (svayaṁsevak) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit svayam, 'of oneself', and sevaka, 'one who serves' (Monier-Williams): one who serves of his own accord.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अडकणे (aḍakṇe) — to get stuck; to be caught.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for relief work, then the Marathi for a volunteer.

You'll want to know: अडकणे.
अडकणे — aḍakṇe — "to get stuck; to be caught".
पाण्यात दोन मुलं अडकली होती. pāṇyāt don mulaṁ aḍaklī hotī. Two children were stuck in the water.
Past (he …): तो अडकला, to aḍaklā.
The place takes -त: गर्दीत अडकणे (aḍakṇe), to be stuck in a crowd.

The word, taken apart.
अडकणे (aḍakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi aḍakṇe, 'to stick fast', under aḍ, 'obstruct, stop' (CDIAL 187).
