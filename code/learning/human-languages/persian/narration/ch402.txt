Persian, chapter 402: Research and papers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تحقیق (tahqiq) — research, an investigation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for lecture notes, then the Persian for homework.

You'll want to know: تحقیق.
تحقیق (tahqiq) — "research, an investigation".
او درباره زبانهای قدیمی تحقیق میکند. u darbâre-ye zabânhâ-ye qadimi tahqiq mikonad. She does research on ancient languages.
Tahqiq kardan — to research. Plural tahqiqât.

The word, taken apart.
تحقیق (tahqiq) comes from Arabic taḥqīq (EtymDB), a verbal noun from the same Arabic family as ḥaqīqa "truth": research finds out what is true.

Lesson 2 of 5.
پژوهش (pazhuhesh) — scholarly research, a study.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for homework, then the Persian for research.

You'll want to know: پژوهش.
پژوهش (pazhuhesh) — "scholarly research, a study".
این پژوهش پنج سال طول کشید. in pazhuhesh panj sâl tul keshid. This study took five years.
A native, formal near-synonym of tahqiq. Pazhuheshgar — a researcher.

The word, taken apart.
پژوهش (pazhuhesh) is native Persian, built on pazhuh-, the stem of pazhuhidan "to search, to inquire", with the noun suffix -esh.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پایان نامه (pâyân nâme) — a thesis, a dissertation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for scholarly research, then the Persian for an article.

You'll want to know: پایان نامه.
پایان نامه (pâyân nâme) — "a thesis, a dissertation".
خواهرم پایان نامه اش را تمام کرد. khâharam pâyân nâme ash râ tamâm kard. My sister finished her thesis.
Pâyân "end" + nâme "letter, book": the book at the end of a degree.

The word, taken apart.
پایان نامه (pâyân nâme) is a native Persian compound built from pâyân "end" + nâme "letter, book", a word EtymDB lists without a parent and usually traced to Middle Persian nāmag. It is the book that ends a degree.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سخنرانی (sokhanrâni) — a lecture, a talk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an article, then the Persian for a thesis.

You'll want to know: سخنرانی.
سخنرانی (sokhanrâni) — "a lecture, a talk".
سخنرانی استاد یک ساعت طول کشید. sokhanrâni-ye ostâd yek sâ'at tul keshid. The professor's lecture lasted an hour.
Sokhan "speech" + rân "driving", from rândan, + -i. Sokhanrâni kardan — to give a talk.

The word, taken apart.
سخنرانی (sokhanrâni) is native Persian, built from sokhan "speech, word" + rân-, the present stem of rândan "to drive", + -i. A lecturer drives the speech forward.
