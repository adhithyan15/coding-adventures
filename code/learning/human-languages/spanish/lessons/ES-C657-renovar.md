## You'll want to know: renovar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C657-PAPELES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**renovar** — "to renew".

> Tengo que renovar el pasaporte.
>
> *I have to renew my passport.*

The o becomes ue under stress: *renuevo*, *renueva*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*renovar* comes from Latin *renovāre* "to renew, to restore" (Lewis & Short), built on *novus* "new" (EtymDB).
