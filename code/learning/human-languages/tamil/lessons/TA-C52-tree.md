## You'll want to know: மரம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C52-TREE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**மரம்** (*maram*) — "a tree".

Proto-Dravidian **\*mar-**, and Malayalam did not lay a finger on it: മരം, the same word letter for letter. Telugu went somewhere else entirely, to చెట్టు (*ceṭṭu*) — the ordinary arrangement in this family, where the two closest sisters agree and the third has its own.

*maram* is the tree standing and the timber cut out of it. A carpenter works in *maram*; so does a forest.
