## You'll want to know: ਸ਼ਰਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C345-DECIDE345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ਰਤ** — *sharat* — "a condition".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਇੱਕ ਸ਼ਰਤ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਉਹ ਔਖੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī ikk sharat hai ate oh aukhī nahīṁ hai.*
>
> I have one condition, and it is not hard.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ਰਤ** (*sharat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *sharṭ*, "condition".
