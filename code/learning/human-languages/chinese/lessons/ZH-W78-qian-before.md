## Script — horns, a bar, the moon and a blade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QIAN-BEFORE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 前

**Two horns, two strokes**: a dot on the left, a short left-falling stroke on the right.

**A long horizontal** under them.

**The moon piece, four strokes, read yuè** as in the word for month: a left side that falls down, a top that turns down the right side and hooks, and two short bars inside.

**The blade, two strokes**: a short vertical, then a long vertical that hooks to the left.

2 + 1 + 4 + 2 = nine.
