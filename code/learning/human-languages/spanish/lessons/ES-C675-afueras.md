## You'll want to know: las afueras
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C675-AFUERAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**las afueras** — "the outskirts".

> Vivimos en las afueras de la ciudad.
>
> *We live on the outskirts of the city.*

Always plural: *las afueras*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afueras* is built on *afuera* and *fuera* "outside", which come from Latin *forās* "out of doors" (EtymDB).
