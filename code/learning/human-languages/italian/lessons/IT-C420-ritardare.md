## You'll want to know: ritardare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C420-RITARDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ritardare** — "to be late, to delay".

> Il volo ritarda di due ore.
>
> *The flight is two hours late.*

*In ritardo* — late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ritardare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *retardāre* 'to hold back' (EtymDB), built on *tardus* 'slow'.
