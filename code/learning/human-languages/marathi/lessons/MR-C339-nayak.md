## You'll want to know: नायक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C339-NAYAK339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नायक** — *nāyak* — "a protagonist; the hero of a story".

> चित्रपटाचा नायक एक साधा शिक्षक आहे.
>
> *citrapaṭācā nāyak ek sādhā śikṣak āhe.*
>
> The hero of the film is an ordinary teacher.

Masculine.

A heroine is नायिका.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नायक** (*nāyak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nāyaka*, 'a guide, a leader; the hero of a drama' (Monier-Williams).
