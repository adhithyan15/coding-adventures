## You'll want to know: برادرزاده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C501-INLAWS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**برادرزاده** (*barâdarzâde*) — "a brother's child, a nephew or niece".

> برادرزاده من تازه راه رفتن یاد گرفته است.
>
> *barâdarzâde-ye man tâze râh raftan yâd gerefte ast.*
>
> My nephew has recently learned to walk.

*barâdar* 'brother' + *zâde* 'born of'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barâdarzâde* is built on two inherited words: *barâdar*, 'brother', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *zâde*, 'born of'.
