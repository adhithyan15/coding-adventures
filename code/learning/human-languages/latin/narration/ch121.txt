Lesson 1 of 5.
cochlear, cochleāris — a spoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: cochlear, cochleāris.
cochlear, cochleāris — "a spoon".

The word, taken apart.
cochlear is built on cochlea, "a snail" (EtymDB 2.1), a word Latin took from Greek kokhlias, "snail, spiral": the spoon was first for eating snails, its pointed end for picking them out.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ōlla, ōllae — a cooking pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for a napkin, then the Latin for a spoon.

You'll want to know: ōlla, ōllae.
ōlla, ōllae — "a cooking pot".

The word, taken apart.
ōlla is inherited, usually traced through Proto-Italic aukslā, "pot", to Proto-Indo-European Hukwsleh2, "cooking pot" (EtymDB 2.1). An older Latin spelling was aula (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
patina, patinae — a dish, a pan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for a spoon, then the Latin for a cooking pot.

You'll want to know: patina, patinae.
patina, patinae — "a dish, a pan".

The word, taken apart.
patina was probably borrowed from Greek patanē, "a flat dish" (EtymDB 2.1); Lewis & Short's link with pateō, "to lie open", is less likely. English patina is the same word.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: catīnus, catīnī.
catīnus, catīnī — "a bowl, a deep dish".

The word, taken apart.
catīnus has no sure origin; Varro compared a Sicilian Greek katinon, "dish" (Lewis & Short), and it may be a loan from there.

Lesson 5 of 5.
candēla, candēlae — a candle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for a dish, then the Latin for a bowl.
[pause 2 seconds]
Put three hours in order, first to last: prīma hōra, sexta hōra, nōna hōra. Which of them is ante merīdiem?

You'll want to know: candēla, candēlae.
candēla, candēlae — "a candle".

The word, taken apart.
candēla is built on candeō, "to shine, to glow white" (Lewis & Short), with -ēla, an ending for a tool. candeō is usually traced to Proto-Indo-European (s)kand-, "to shine" (EtymDB 2.1). English candle comes from candēla.
