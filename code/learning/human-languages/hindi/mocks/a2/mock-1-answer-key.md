## Writing

Task 1 must name the broken zip, request a remedy such as replacement or
repair/refund, and ask when to visit. Task 2 must contrast an old routine with a
change, give two results, and state a recommendation. Apply the rubric rather
than matching a fixed script. Scorer-only reference openings:

- **नमस्ते। मैंने कल आपकी दुकान से एक बैग खरीदा था, लेकिन उसकी ज़िप टूट गई है…**
- **पहले मैं देर से सोता था और सुबह थका रहता था। पिछले महीने मैंने…**

Do not show these stems to the learner.

## Speaking

The interview needs relevant answers and reasons. The account must sustain two
minutes and make its sequence clear. The role-play must identify two booking
problems, obtain information, compare two solutions, and reach an explicit
agreement. Score communicative development, interaction/repair, Hindi control,
and intelligibility with the rubric; no single wording is required.
