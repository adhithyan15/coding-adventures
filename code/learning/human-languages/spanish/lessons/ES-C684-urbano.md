## You'll want to know: urbano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C684-CIUDADANO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**urbano** — "urban, city (as an adjective)".

> El autobús urbano es muy barato.
>
> *The city bus is very cheap.*

*Transporte urbano* — city transport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*urbano* is a learned borrowing from Latin *urbānus* "of the city" (EtymDB), from *urbs* "city".
