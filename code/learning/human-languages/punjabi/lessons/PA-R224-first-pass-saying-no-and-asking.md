# Looking back: saying no and asking

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I don't drink tea*. Then ask *do you drink tea?*

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212, PA-GRAMMAR-REQUEST-O-213, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-REQUEST-NA-213, PA-LEX-AJE-213, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-HABITUAL-215, PA-GRAMMAR-ASK-AND-ANSWER-215] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU HEAR: *I go to school every day*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *we eat roti every day*] Say **ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *the garden is not open*] Say **ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *please sit here; don't get up yet*] Say **ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠੋ। ਅਜੇ ਨਾ ਉੱਠੋ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *are you a traveller? No.*] Say **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ? ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹਾਂ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ?**] Give its meaning.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-REQUEST-NA-213, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **ਨਹੀਂ** tells, **ਨਾ** asks someone not to, **ਕੀ** at the front asks
yes or no. Say one sentence for each.
