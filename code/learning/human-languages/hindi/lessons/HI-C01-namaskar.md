# Namaskār — the more formal bow

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASTE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The dressier sibling of *namaste* — same root, one new piece, and
the letters that finish *namaste* on the page.

## You'll want to know first
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASKAR-01]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASTE-01] -->

- [*namaste*](./HI-C01-namaste.md) — you already know **न**, the syllable *te*, and
  how consonants carry "a."

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASKAR-01] -->

- **म** = "ma" · **स** = "sa."
- **्**, the *halant*, **kills a consonant's vowel**: **स्** = a bare "s,"
  which leans onto the next consonant as a **conjunct**.

So **नमस्** (*namas*, "a bow") reads **न·म·स्**. Add the last lesson's *te*
and every piece of *namaste* is yours. Three facts did it: consonants carry
*a*, a *mātrā* changes the vowel, a *halant* removes it.

The second half, *kār*, needs three more shapes; the whole written word waits
for the end of this chapter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASKAR-01] -->

*Namaskār* = *namaḥ* ("a bow," from root **नम्** *nam*, as in
*namaste*) + *kāra* ("the making or doing of" — from the Sanskrit root
*kṛ*, "to do, make"). So *namaskār* is literally **"the making of a
bow," "the act of homage."** Where *namaste* says "bow to *you*," *namaskār*
names the bow itself — a touch more formal and ceremonial.

That piece *-kāra* ("the making/doing of") is a Sanskrit building
block you'll see again — e.g. in *ahaṃkāra* ("the making of 'I'" → ego). Learn
it once as "the act of ___."

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASKAR-01] -->

*namaskār* is a notch more **formal** than *namaste* — common in respectful,
official, or ceremonial settings, and as a courteous greeting to elders or in
writing. Both are "hello"; *namaskār* wears a tie.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASKAR-01] -->

New: म ma, स sa, and the *halant* ् (स् = s). Together न·म·स् spell
*namas*. [YOU READ: **न·म·स्**] Say *namaskār* in its beats: na · ma · s · kā · r.
