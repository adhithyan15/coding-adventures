# જથ્થાબંધ (jaththābandh) — wholesale; in bulk

## You'll want to know: જથ્થાબંધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C431-STOCK431-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જથ્થાબંધ** — *jaththābandh* — "wholesale; in bulk".

> વેપારી જથ્થાબંધ ભાવે માલ વેચે છે.
>
> *vepārī jaththābandh bhāve māl vece che.*
>
> The trader sells goods at wholesale prices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જથ્થાબંધ** (*jaththābandh*) is built from જથ્થો, "a quantity, a bulk", and -બંધ, "bound, tied", usually traced to Persian *band*: goods sold tied up in bulk.
