## You'll want to know: ಪುರಭವನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C456-SMARAKA456-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಭವನ** — *purabhavana* — "a town hall".

> ಪುರಭವನದ ಮುಂದೆ ಒಂದು ದೊಡ್ಡ ಗಡಿಯಾರ ಇದೆ.
>
> *purabhavanada munde ondu doḍḍa gaḍiyāra ide.*
>
> There is a big clock in front of the town hall.

ಪುರ, a town, plus ಭವನ, a building or mansion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಭವನ** (*purabhavana*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: *pura*, "a fortress, a town" (CDIAL 8278), and *bhavana*, "a place of abode" (CDIAL 9417).
