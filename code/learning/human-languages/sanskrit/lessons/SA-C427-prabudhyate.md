## You'll want to know: प्रबुध्यते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C427-VASATI427-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबुध्यते** — *prabudhyate* — "wakes up".

> पथिकः प्रातः प्रबुध्यते।
>
> *pathikaḥ prātaḥ prabudhyate.*
>
> The wayfarer wakes up in the morning.

Past (he …): सः प्रातः प्रबुद्धवान्, *saḥ prātaḥ prabuddhavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबुध्यते** (*prabudhyate*) is built from *pra*, "forth", and the root √budh, "to wake, to know", which also gives *budhyate*, "understands"; MW gives *pra-budh* as to wake up, with the participle *prabuddha*, "awakened" (MW).
