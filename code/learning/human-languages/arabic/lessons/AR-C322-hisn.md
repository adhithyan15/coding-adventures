## You'll want to know: حصن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C322-QALA322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حصن** — *ḥiṣn* — "a fort, a stronghold".

> بنى الجنود حصنا قرب البحر.
>
> *banā al-junūd ḥiṣnan qurba al-baḥr.*
>
> The soldiers built a fort near the sea.

Plural: حصون, *ḥuṣūn*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حصن** (*ḥiṣn*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ṣ-n* (Lane), "to be inaccessible, well guarded"; the taught *ḥiṣān*, a horse, is the same root.
