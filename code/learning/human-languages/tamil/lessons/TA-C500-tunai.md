## You'll want to know: துணை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C500-VITU500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**துணை** — *tuṇai* — "a companion; a partner".

> பயணத்தில் எனக்குத் துணையாக என் தம்பி வந்தான்.
>
> *payaṇattil eṉakkut tuṇaiyāka eṉ tampi vantāṉ.*
>
> My younger brother came along to keep me company on the journey.

துணையாக, as company; வாழ்க்கைத் துணை, a life partner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**துணை** (*tuṇai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3308), for company, help and a companion.
