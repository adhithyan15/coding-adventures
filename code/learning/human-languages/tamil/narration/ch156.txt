Tamil, chapter 156: To wipe, To sweep, To learn, To win, To find.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
துடை (tuṭai) — to wipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to tie, then the Tamil for to wash.

You'll want to know: துடை.
துடை — tuṭai — "to wipe".
