## You'll want to know: வாடகை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C160-PATI160-03]; assesses=[] -->

**வாடகை** — *vāṭakai* — "rent; a fare". The money for a room, or for a ride: **வீட்டு வாடகை** — *vīṭṭu vāṭakai* — "the rent for a house".
