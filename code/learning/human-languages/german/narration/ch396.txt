German, chapter 396: Am Computer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Tastatur — the keyboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for data protection, then the German for to encrypt.

You'll want to know: die Tastatur.
die Tastatur — "the keyboard".
Auf der deutschen Tastatur sind Y und Z vertauscht. On the German keyboard Y and Z have swapped places.
Plural: die Tastaturen. Die Taste — the key.

The word, taken apart.
Tastatur is built on Taste "key", borrowed from Italian tasto "key", from tastare "to touch, to feel", which goes back to Latin taxitāre (EtymDB), with the Latin-style ending -atur.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: der Drucker.
der Drucker — "the printer".
Der Drucker hat kein Papier mehr. The printer has run out of paper.
Plural: die Drucker. Also a printer by trade.

The word, taken apart.
Drucker is formed with -er on drucken "to print", of the family of drücken "to press", from Proto-Germanic þrukjaną "to press" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ausdrucken — to print out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the keyboard, then the German for the printer.

You'll want to know: ausdrucken.
ausdrucken — "to print out".
Ich drucke die Fahrkarte zu Hause aus. I print out the ticket at home.
Separable: ich drucke aus, ich habe ausgedruckt. Careful: ausdrücken, with an umlaut, means to express.

The word, taken apart.
ausdrucken is built from aus "out" and drucken "to print", from Proto-Germanic þrukjaną "to press" (EtymDB): you press the text out onto paper.

Lesson 4 of 5.
installieren — to install.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the printer, then the German for to print out.

You'll want to know: installieren.
installieren — "to install".
Mein Sohn hat die App auf meinem Handy installiert. My son installed the app on my mobile.
Perfekt: hat installiert. Also of wiring: eine Lampe installieren.

The word, taken apart.
installieren is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin installāre (EtymDB), built on stallum "a stall, a seat": usually said to have meant first to place someone in a church seat or an office.

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Programm — the program; the programme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to print out, then the German for to install.

You'll want to know: das Programm.
das Programm — "the program; the programme".
Mit diesem Programm kann man Fotos bearbeiten. You can edit photos with this program.
Plural: die Programme. Also a TV channel or a schedule: das Fernsehprogramm.

The word, taken apart.
Programm is a learned borrowing from Late Latin programma "a proclamation, an edict" (Lewis & Short), from Greek prógramma "a public notice", built from pro "before" and gráphein (EtymDB).
