## You'll want to know: dernièrement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C273-VEILLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dernièrement** — "lately, recently".

> Dernièrement, je dors mal.
>
> *Lately, I have been sleeping badly.*

Close to *récemment*; it goes with the present or the passé composé.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dernièrement* is built on *dernier* "last", from Old French *derrenier*, usually traced to Latin *dē retrō* "from behind" (Lewis & Short).
