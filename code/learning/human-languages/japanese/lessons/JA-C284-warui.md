## You'll want to know: わるい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C284-SENKYO284-05]; assesses=[] -->

**わるい** — *warui* — "bad".

> **あしたは てんきが わるいでしょう。** — *ashita wa tenki ga warui deshō* — "The weather will probably be poor tomorrow."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わるい** (*warui*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
