# मी जाईन (mī jāīn) — the future, person by person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-UDYA, MR-GRAMMAR-WE-LIVES-IN-THE-VERB, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "see you tomorrow": *udyā bheṭū*. Then "I went": *mī gelo*.

[PAUSE 1s] **भेटू**, "we'll meet", has been a future all along. Here are the others.

## You'll want to know: the future, person by person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-JANE, MR-LEX-MI, MR-LEX-TU-TUMHI, MR-LEX-TO-TI-TE] -->

> **उद्या मी पुण्याला जाईन.**
>
> *udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn.* — "Tomorrow I will go to Pune."

- **मी** **जाईन** (*jāīn*) — I will go
- **तू** **जाशील** (*jāśīl*) — you will go, to a friend
- **तो**, **ती**, **ते** **जाईल** (*jāīl*) — he, she, it will go
- **आम्ही** **जाऊ** (*jāū*) — we will go: the **-ऊ** of **भेटू**
- **तुम्ही** **जाल** (*jāl*) — you will go, with respect
- **ते** **जातील** (*jātīl*) — they will go

There is no gender here: a man and a woman both say **मी जाईन**.

## You'll want to know: more verbs in the future
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-STEMS-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-YENE, MR-LEX-KARNE, MR-LEX-VACHNE, MR-LEX-C84-VERBS84-04, MR-LEX-C159-PAS159-05] -->

The endings stay; the stem in front changes:

- **येणे** → **मी येईन** (*mī yeīn*), **तो येईल** (*to yeīl*), **तुम्ही याल** (*tumhī yāl*)
- **करणे** → **मी करीन** (*mī karīn*), **तो करेल** (*to karel*), **आम्ही करू** (*āmhī karū*)
- **वाचणे** → **मी वाचीन** (*mī vācīn*), **ती वाचेल** (*tī vācel*)
- **पडणे** → **पाऊस पडेल** (*pāūs paḍel*), "it will rain"
- **असणे** → **मी घरी असेन** (*mī gharī asen*), "I will be at home"; **तो असेल** (*to asel*)
- **होणे** → **काय होईल?** (*kāy hoīl?*), "What will happen?"

Many people say **करेन** (*karen*) and **वाचेन** (*vācen*) for "I will"; both are heard.
