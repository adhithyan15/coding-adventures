## You'll want to know: कमाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C454-ACHAMBHA454-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कमाल** — *kamāl* — "a marvel; something amazing".

> मीरा ने एक दिन में पूरी किताब पढ़ ली, कमाल है!
>
> *Mīrā ne ek din meṁ pūrī kitāb paṛh lī, kamāl hai!*
>
> Mira read the whole book in a day — amazing!

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कमाल** (*kamāl*) is usually traced to Arabic *kamāl*, "perfection", which reached Hindi through Persian. *kamāl hai!* is "that's amazing!".
