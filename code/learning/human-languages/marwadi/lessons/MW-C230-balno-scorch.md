# बळणो (baḷṇo) — to be scorching; to burn (by itself)

## You'll want to know: बळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C230-SUN230-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बळणो** — *baḷṇo* — "to be scorching; to burn (by itself)".

> दोपार मांय रेत बळै है।
>
> *dopār mā̃y ret baḷai hai.*
>
> At midday the sand is scorching.

Past (it …): वो दीयो बळ्यो, *vo dīyo baḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बळणो** (*baḷṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dvalati*, "burns"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *balaï*, "burns" (CDIAL 6654). **बाळणो**, to burn something, is its partner.
