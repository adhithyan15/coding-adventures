## You'll want to know: राई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C185-MASALO185-02]; assesses=[] -->

**राई** — *rāī* — "mustard seed".

> मां तेल मांय राई घालै है।
>
> *mā̃ tel mā̃y rāī ghālai hai.*
>
> Mother puts mustard seed in the oil.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राई** (*rāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rājikā*, "mustard"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *rāī* (CDIAL 10688). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
