## You'll want to know: das Heimweh
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C472-SEHNSUCHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Heimweh** — "homesickness".

> In der ersten Woche im Ferienlager hatte er Heimweh.
>
> *In the first week at summer camp he was homesick.*

No plural. The opposite is *das Fernweh* — the urge to travel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Heimweh* is built on *Heim* "home" and *Weh* "pain, woe", which goes back to Middle High German *we* (EtymDB); the word is usually said to have spread from Swiss German.
