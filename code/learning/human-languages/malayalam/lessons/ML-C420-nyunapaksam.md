# ന്യൂനപക്ഷം (nyūnapakṣaṁ) — a minority

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C420-VIJAYAM420-02, ML-LEX-C420-VIJAYAM420-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a manifesto, then the Malayalam for a majority.

## You'll want to know: ന്യൂനപക്ഷം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C420-VIJAYAM420-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ന്യൂനപക്ഷം** — *nyūnapakṣaṁ* — "a minority".

> ആ ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ അവർ ന്യൂനപക്ഷമാണ്.
>
> *ā grāmattil avar nyūnapakṣamāṇŭ.*
>
> In that village they are a minority.

Plural with -ങ്ങൾ: ന്യൂനപക്ഷങ്ങൾ, minorities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ന്യൂനപക്ഷം** (*nyūnapakṣaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *nyūna*, "less, deficient" (Monier-Williams), and *pakṣa*, "a side" (CDIAL 7627): the smaller side.
