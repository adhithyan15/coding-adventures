Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 274: Help in Trouble.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तकलीफ (taklīph) — trouble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to keep secret, then the Marwadi for a promise.

You'll want to know: तकलीफ.
तकलीफ — taklīph — "trouble".
कोई तकलीफ है? koī taklīph hai? Is anything the matter?
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तकलीफ (taklīph) is usually traced, through Persian taklīf, to Arabic taklīf, "a burden laid on someone".

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पुकार (pukār) — a cry for help.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a promise, then the Marwadi for trouble.

You'll want to know: पुकार.
पुकार — pukār — "a cry for help".
हूं पुकार सुणूं हूं। hū̃ pukār suṇū̃ hū̃. I hear a cry for help.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पुकार (pukār) is inherited from an imitative pukkār, "shout"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pukāra, "complaint" (CDIAL 8246).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सहारो (sahāro) — support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for trouble, then the Marwadi for a cry for help.

You'll want to know: सहारो.
सहारो — sahāro — "support".
मां म्हारो सहारो है। mā̃ mhāro sahāro hai. Mother is my support.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सहारो (sahāro) is inherited from Sanskrit sahakāra, "assistance"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi sahārā, "help" (CDIAL 13299).

Lesson 4 of 5.
दया (dayā) — mercy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a cry for help, then the Marwadi for support.

You'll want to know: दया.
दया — dayā — "mercy".
दया करो सा। dayā karo sā. Have mercy, please.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दया (dayā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit dayā, "sympathy, compassion" (Monier-Williams). दयाळु, kind, is built on it.
