## You'll want to know: चिता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C505-SHOK505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चिता** — *citā* — "a funeral pyre".

> बेटे ने पिता की चिता को आग दी।
>
> *beṭe ne pitā kī citā ko āg dī.*
>
> The son lit his father's funeral pyre.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिता** (*citā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *citā*, "a layer, a pile of wood, a funeral pile" (Monier-Williams).
