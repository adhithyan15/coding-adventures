## You'll want to know: braquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C401-CONDUITE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**braquer** — "to turn the wheel (hard); to aim".

> Elle a braqué à gauche pour éviter le chat.
>
> *She turned the wheel hard to the left to avoid the cat.*

*Braquer une banque* is slang for to hold up a bank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *braquer* is not fully settled. It is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin verb built on Latin *bracchium* 'arm' (Lewis & Short): first to point or turn something with the arm.
