## You'll want to know: वळीव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C438-VALIV438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वळीव** — *vaḷīv* — "a pre-monsoon storm".

> उन्हाळ्याच्या शेवटी वळवाचा पाऊस येतो.
>
> *unhāḷyācyā śevṭī vaḷvācā pāūs yeto.*
>
> At the end of summer the pre-monsoon storms come.

Masculine.

Before an ending वळीव becomes वळवा-: वळवाचा पाऊस.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वळीव** (*vaḷīv*) names the sudden thunderstorm of late summer before the monsoon; its origin is not settled.
