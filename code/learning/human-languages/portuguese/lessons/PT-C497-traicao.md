## You'll want to know: a traição
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C497-SEPARACAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a traição** — "betrayal".

> Para ela, a mentira foi uma traição.
>
> *For her, the lie was a betrayal.*

Built like *trair* on Latin *trādere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*traição* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *traiçon*, from Latin *trāditiōnem* "a handing over" (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
