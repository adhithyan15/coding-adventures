## You'll want to know: జయించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C361-YUDDHABHUMI361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**జయించు** — *jayiñcu* — "to conquer".

> ఆ రాజు చాలా రాజ్యాలను జయించాడు.
>
> *ā rāju cālā rājyālanu jayiñcāḍu.*
>
> That king conquered many kingdoms.

Past (I …): జయించాను, *jayiñcānu*.

Also to overcome: భయాన్ని జయించు, to conquer fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జయించు** (*jayiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *jaya*, "victory" (CDIAL 5141), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.
