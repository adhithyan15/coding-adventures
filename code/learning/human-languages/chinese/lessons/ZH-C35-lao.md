## You'll want to know: lǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C35-WORK35-02]; assesses=[] -->

*lǎo* — "old".

> *tā shì wǒ de lǎo tóngxué* — He's an old classmate of mine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *lawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'aged'.
