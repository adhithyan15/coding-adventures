# ಆರೋಪಿಸು (ārōpisu) — to accuse

## You'll want to know: ಆರೋಪಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C321-NYAYA321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರೋಪಿಸು** — *ārōpisu* — "to accuse".

> ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನು ಆ ಹುಡುಗ ದುಡ್ಡು ಕದ್ದ ಅಂತ ಆರೋಪಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *aṅgaḍiyavanu ā huḍuga duḍḍu kadda anta ārōpisidanu.*
>
> The shopkeeper accused that boy of stealing money.

Past (I …): ಆರೋಪಿಸಿದೆ, *ārōpiside*.

The charge comes before ಅಂತ. The charge itself is an ಆರೋಪ, and ಆರೋಪಿ is the accused.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರೋಪಿಸು** (*ārōpisu*) is built on Sanskrit *āropa*, "placing on, charging with", the noun beside *āropaṇa*, "placing on" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
