Arabic, chapter 174: Reason, Result, Therefore, So, So that.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سبب (sabab) — a reason, a cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a continent, then the Arabic for a volcano.

You'll want to know: سبب.
سبب — sabab — "a reason, a cause".
