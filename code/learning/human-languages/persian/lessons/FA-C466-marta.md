## You'll want to know: مرتع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C466-CHUPAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مرتع** (*marta'*) — "a pasture, grazing land".

> در بهار مرتع سبز است.
>
> *Dar bahâr marta' sabz ast.*
>
> In spring the pasture is green.

Arabic plural *marâte'*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marta'* was borrowed from Arabic *martaʿ* 'pasture', which EtymDB derives from *ratʿ* 'grazing'.
