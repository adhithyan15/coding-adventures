## You'll want to know: कुजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C448-DURGANDHI448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुजणे** — *kujṇe* — "to rot".

> पावसात लाकूड कुजलं.
>
> *pāvsāt lākūḍ kujlaṁ.*
>
> The wood rotted in the rain.

Past (he …): तो कुजला, *to kujlā*.

Wood, leaves and fruit कुजतात slowly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुजणे** (*kujṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *kuj̈ṇẽ*, "to rot", under *\*kujjati*, "rots" (CDIAL 3222).
