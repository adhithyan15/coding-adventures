Lesson 1 of 6.
ছিদ্র (chhidrô) — a hole; a small opening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to bail out water, then the Bengali for to seep.

You'll want to know: ছিদ্র.
ছিদ্র — chhidrô — "a hole; a small opening".
নৌকার তলায় একটা ছিদ্র ছিল। Noukār tôlāy ekṭā chhidrô chhilô. There was a hole in the bottom of the boat.

The word, taken apart.
ছিদ্র (chhidrô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit chidra, "torn asunder", and so "a hole, an opening" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ঠোকা (ṭhokā) — to hammer in; to knock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for glue, then the Bengali for to plaster.

You'll want to know: ঠোকা.
ঠোকা — ṭhokā — "to hammer in; to knock".
মিস্ত্রি দেওয়ালে একটা পেরেক ঠুকল। Mistri deoyāle ekṭā perek ṭhuklô. The mechanic hammered a nail into the wall.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দরজায় ঠুকলেন, tini dôrjāy ṭhuklen.
The stem vowel rises in the past: ঠুকল, he hammered.

The word, taken apart.
ঠোকা (ṭhokā) comes from a reconstructed root ṭhōkk, "knock"; CDIAL lists Bengali ṭhokā, "to strike" (CDIAL 5513).
