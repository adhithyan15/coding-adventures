## You'll want to know: बरसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C438-VALIV438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बरसणे** — *barasṇe* — "to pour down".

> ढग गडगडले आणि पाऊस बरसला.
>
> *ḍhag gaḍgaḍle āṇi pāūs baraslā.*
>
> The clouds rumbled and the rain poured down.

Past (he …): तो बरसला, *to baraslā*.

Rain बरसतो; it sounds more poetic than पडणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बरसणे** (*barasṇe*) belongs with Sanskrit *varṣati*, "it rains", where CDIAL lists Marathi *varasṇẽ* (CDIAL 11394); the form with b- matches Hindi *barasnā*, and how it reached Marathi is not settled.
