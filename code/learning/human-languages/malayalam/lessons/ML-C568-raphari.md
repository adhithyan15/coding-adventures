## You'll want to know: റഫറി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C568-KAYIKAM568-04]; assesses=[] -->

**റഫറി** — *ṟaphaṟi* — "a referee".

> റഫറി കളി പത്ത് മിനിറ്റ് നിർത്തി.
>
> *ṟaphaṟi kaḷi pattŭ miniṟṟŭ nirtti.*
>
> The referee stopped the game for ten minutes.

Malayalam writes the English f with ഫ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റഫറി** (*ṟaphaṟi*) was borrowed from English *referee*.
