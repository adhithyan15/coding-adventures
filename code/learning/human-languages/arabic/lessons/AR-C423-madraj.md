## You'll want to know: مدرج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C423-BAWWABA423-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مدرج** — *madraj* — "a runway".

> كانت الرحلة تنتظر على المدرج.
>
> *kānat ar-riḥla tantaẓiru ʿalā al-madraj.*
>
> The flight was waiting on the runway.

Plural: مدارج, *madārij*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مدرج** (*madraj*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-j*, "to go step by step" (Lane). Lane gives *madraj* as a way or route one goes along; modern Arabic gave the word the runway.
