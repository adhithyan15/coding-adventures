Gujarati, chapter 187: Prices that rise and fall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વધવું (vadhvũ) — to go up, to grow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for banknote, then the Gujarati for receipt.

You'll want to know: વધવું.
વધવું — vadhvũ — "to go up, to grow".
દૂધનો ભાવ વધે છે. — dūdhno bhāv vadhe che — "The price of milk is going up."

The word, taken apart.
વધવું (vadhvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to advance", under Sanskrit várdhatē, "grows, increases" (CDIAL 11376).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઘટવું (ghaṭvũ) — to go down, to decrease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for receipt, then the Gujarati for to go up.

You'll want to know: ઘટવું.
ઘટવું — ghaṭvũ — "to go down, to decrease".
શિયાળામાં શાકનો ભાવ ઘટે છે. — śiyāḷāmā̃ śākno bhāv ghaṭe che — "In winter the price of vegetables goes down."

The word, taken apart.
ઘટવું (ghaṭvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from ghaṭṭati, "decreases, is wanting", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to decrease" (CDIAL 4415).

Lesson 3 of 5.
વધારે (vadhāre) — more, too much.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to go up, then the Gujarati for to go down.

You'll want to know: વધારે.
વધારે — vadhāre — "more, too much".
આ દુકાનમાં વધારે ગ્રાહક છે. — ā dukānmā̃ vadhāre grāhak che — "This shop has more customers."

The word, taken apart.
વધારે (vadhāre) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vṛddhatara, "larger", the comparative of vṛddha, "grown", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "more, greater" (CDIAL 12074).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ટકો (ṭako) — per cent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to go down, then the Gujarati for more.

You'll want to know: ટકો.
ટકો — ṭako — "per cent".
પગાર પાંચ ટકા વધે છે. — pagār pā̃c ṭakā vadhe che — "The salary goes up by five per cent."
After a number it takes the plural ટકા, ṭakā.

The word, taken apart.
ટકો (ṭako) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit ṭaṅka, "a weight; a stamped coin", with the Gujarati sense "two-paisā coin"; the sense "per cent" is not in CDIAL (CDIAL 5426).

Lesson 5 of 5.
છૂટ (chūṭ) — discount; freedom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for more, then the Gujarati for per cent.

You'll want to know: છૂટ.
છૂટ — chūṭ — "discount; freedom".
આ દુકાનમાં દસ ટકા છૂટ છે. — ā dukānmā̃ das ṭakā chūṭ che — "This shop gives ten per cent off."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
છૂટ (chūṭ) is a tadbhava from the family that CDIAL lists under kṣuṭyatē, "is released", a reconstructed form; its Gujarati relatives there are chuṭvũ, "to get loose", and chuṭũ, "loosened" (CDIAL 3707).
