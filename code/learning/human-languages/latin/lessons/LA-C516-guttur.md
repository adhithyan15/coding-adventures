## You'll want to know: guttur, gutturis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C516-GUTT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**guttur, gutturis** — "throat, gullet".

> Guttur mihi dolet.
>
> *My throat hurts.*

Neuter, third declension. English *guttural* — of the throat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**guttur** is inherited; EtymDB traces it to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "a swelling, rounding". English *guttural* comes from it.
