## You'll want to know: بسته به
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C395-BASTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بسته به** (*baste be*) — "depending on".

> قیمت بسته به فصل فرق میکند.
>
> *qeymat baste be fasl farq mikonad.*
>
> The price varies depending on the season.

*Baste* "bound" + *be* "to". It takes a noun or a clause: *baste be inke* … .

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بسته به** is native Persian, literally "bound to": *baste* is the participle of *bastan* "to bind", which EtymDB traces to PIE *bʰendʰ-* "to bind".
