Marathi, chapter 204: Serious, Shy, Valiant, Fearful, Curious.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गंभीर (gambhīr) — serious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for about, then the Marathi for only.

You'll want to know: गंभीर.
गंभीर — gambhīr — "serious".

The word, taken apart.
गंभीर (gambhīr) is Sanskrit gambhīra, "deep", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4031). CDIAL lists the Marathi word as gãbhīr, "deep (of water), serious".

Lesson 4 of 5.
भित्रा / भित्री (bhitrā / bhitrī) — fearful, cowardly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for shy, then the Marathi for valiant.

You'll want to know: भित्रा / भित्री.
भित्रा / भित्री — bhitrā / bhitrī — "fearful, cowardly".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of भित्रा (bhitrā) is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it; under Sanskrit bhīta, "frightened", it lists only Old Marathi bheḍa, "timid" (CDIAL 9511).
