## You'll want to know: der Ruhestand
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C302-RUHESTAND-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ruhestand** — "the retirement".

> Im Ruhestand reiste sie viel.
>
> *In retirement she travelled a lot.*

Formal. *In den Ruhestand gehen* — to retire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ruhestand* joins *Ruhe* "rest", from Proto-Germanic *rōwō* "quiet, rest" (EtymDB), and *Stand* "standing, state": the state of rest.
