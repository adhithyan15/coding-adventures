## You'll want to know: il preavviso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C357-DIMISSIONI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il preavviso** — "the notice (period), the advance warning".

> Devo dare un mese di preavviso prima di andare via.
>
> *I have to give a month's notice before leaving.*

*Senza preavviso* — without warning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preavviso* is built from *pre-* "before" and *avviso* "notice", from *avvisare*, usually traced to Old French *aviser* "to perceive, to inform", built on Late Latin *vīsāre* "to view, to behold" (EtymDB), from the family of *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
