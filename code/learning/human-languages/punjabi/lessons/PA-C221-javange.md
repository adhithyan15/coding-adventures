# ਅਸੀਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ — we will go

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *Aman will go home tomorrow*. Then *we will meet again*.

## Grammar Lens: the ending of ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04, PA-PRON-ASI-01, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221] -->

**ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** has been "we will meet" since the early lessons. Its ending,
**-ਾਂਗੇ**, is the one for **ਅਸੀਂ**:

> **ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।** — *asīṁ kallh bāzār jāvāṅge.* — We will go to the market tomorrow.

> **ਅਸੀਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।** — *asīṁ savere jāvāṅge.* — We will go in the morning.
