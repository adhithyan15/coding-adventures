# (reading) — six labels, no blank after them

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-NAME-01, PA-FORM-LABEL-AGE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have written every one of these into a form. Not one is new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that there is no blank after it, and nothing telling
you which is coming.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SKILL-READ-LABELS]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-WORK-01, PA-FORM-LABEL-RESIDENCE-01] -->

> ਨਾਂ
> ਉਮਰ
> ਕੰਮ
> ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼
> ਭਾਸ਼ਾ
> ਫ਼ੋਨ

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once. Do not assemble each one letter by letter —
look at the whole word and let it arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Again, and notice which came at once and which you had to build.
Both are fine. The difference is what practice removes.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-LANGUAGE-01, PA-FORM-LABEL-PHONE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Filling a form, the label is the part you can ignore. It sits there,
you know what goes next to it, and your attention is on the blank.

[PAUSE 2s] Reading one, the label is the whole message. **ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼** on its own
tells you what a stranger is about to be asked.

[PAUSE 2s] That is why six is enough for one sitting: the same six signs, doing
the opposite job.
