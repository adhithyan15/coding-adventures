Telugu, chapter 297: Stubborn, Stingy, Active, Naughty, Honesty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
మొండి (moṇḍi) — stubborn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for useful, then the Telugu for local.

You'll want to know: మొండి.
మొండి — moṇḍi — "stubborn".

The word, taken apart.
మొండి (moṇḍi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5114 lists moṇḍi, "maimed, blunt, stubborn", with Kannada moṇḍa, "blunt, maimed". DEDR compares Sanskrit muṇḍa, "hornless, blunt".

చురుకైన (curukaina) is native Dravidian: curuku, "activity, briskness", one word with Tamil cuṟukku, "quickness" (DEDR 2713), with aina, "which is" (DEDR 333).
