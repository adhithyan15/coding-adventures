## You'll want to know: ammirare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C473-SINCERITA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ammirare** — "to admire".

> Ammiro molto il coraggio di mia madre.
>
> *I really admire my mother's courage.*

*Ammirare il panorama* — to take in the view.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ammirare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *admīrārī* "to wonder at" (EtymDB), built on *mīrārī* "to wonder" (Lewis & Short).
