Russian, chapter 501: Needle and Thread.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
нитка (nítka) — a thread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to embroider, then the Russian for lace.

You'll want to know: нитка.
нитка (nítka) — "a thread".
Нужна чёрная нитка, чтобы пришить пуговицу. Nuzhná chyórnaya nítka, chtóby prishít púgovitsu. I need black thread to sew the button on.
Feminine; genitive plural ниток. A diminutive of нить.

The word, taken apart.
нитка (nítka) is a diminutive of нить 'thread'; EtymDB lists нитка (nítka) without a source, and нить is inherited from Proto-Slavic nitь (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
иголка (igólka) — a needle.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for lace, then the Russian for a thread.

You'll want to know: иголка.
иголка (igólka) — "a needle".
Я укололся иголкой. Ya ukolólsya igólkoy. I pricked myself with a needle.
Feminine. Everyday word beside the bookish игла.

The word, taken apart.
иголка (igólka) is a diminutive of игла 'needle'; EtymDB lists иголка (igólka) without a source, and игла is inherited from Proto-Slavic jьgъla (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
напёрсток (napyórstok) — a thimble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a thread, then the Russian for a needle.

You'll want to know: напёрсток.
напёрсток (napyórstok) — "a thimble".
Без напёрстка я всегда колю палец. Bez napyórstka ya vsegdá kolyú pálets. Without a thimble I always prick my finger.
Masculine; genitive напёрстка.

The word, taken apart.
напёрсток (napyórstok) is inherited from Proto-Slavic napьrstъkъ, derived from pьrstъ 'finger' (EtymDB): a cap on the finger. The old word перст 'finger', which EtymDB marks as a Church Slavonic borrowing, is usually linked to перчатки 'gloves'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
выкройка (výkroyka) — a sewing pattern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a needle, then the Russian for a thimble.

You'll want to know: выкройка.
выкройка (výkroyka) — "a sewing pattern".
Мама сшила платье по старой выкройке. Máma sshíla plátye po stároy výkroyke. Mum made a dress from an old pattern.
Feminine. Шить по выкройке — to sew from a pattern.

The word, taken apart.
выкройка (výkroyka) is built on выкроить 'to cut out', from кроить 'to cut cloth'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
подшивать (podshivát) — to hem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a thimble, then the Russian for a sewing pattern.

You'll want to know: подшивать.
подшивать (podshivát) — "to hem".
Брюки слишком длинные, и бабушка их подшивает. Bryúki slíshkom dlínnyye, i bábushka ikh podshiváyet. The trousers are too long, so Grandma is taking them up.
Imperfective; perfective подшить (подошью).

The word, taken apart.
подшивать (podshivát) is built on под- 'under' and шить 'to sew'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
