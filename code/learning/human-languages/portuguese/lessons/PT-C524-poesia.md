## You'll want to know: a poesia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C524-POESIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a poesia** — "poetry; poem".

> A minha avó sabe muita poesia de cor.
>
> *My grandmother knows a lot of poetry by heart.*

*Uma poesia* can also mean one poem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poesia* comes from Latin *poēsis* (EtymDB), from Greek *poíēsis* "a making, a composing", built on *poieîn* "to make".
