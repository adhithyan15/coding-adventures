# స్తంభించు (stambhiñcu) — to seize up; to come to a standstill

## You'll want to know: స్తంభించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C449-ADDANKI449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**స్తంభించు** — *stambhiñcu* — "to seize up; to come to a standstill".

> భారీ వర్షంతో నగరంలో ట్రాఫిక్ స్తంభించింది.
>
> *bhārī varṣantō nagaraṁlō ṭrāphik stambhiñcindi.*
>
> With the heavy rain the traffic in the city came to a standstill.

Past (it …): స్తంభించింది, *stambhiñcindi*.

స్తంభం is a pillar: a road that stands still like a pillar. The news uses it of traffic and of normal life. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: స్తంభించింది, it came to a standstill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**స్తంభించు** (*stambhiñcu*) is built from Sanskrit *stambh*, "to become stiff, to be fixed", whose noun *stambha* is "a pillar, a post" (CDIAL 13682), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
