## You'll want to know: ऊभो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C231-SHAPE231-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊभो** — *ūbho* — "standing; upright".

> ऊंट ऊभो है।
>
> *ū̃ṭ ūbho hai.*
>
> The camel is standing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊभो** (*ūbho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ūrdhva*, "erect"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ūbhau*, "upright" (CDIAL 2426).
