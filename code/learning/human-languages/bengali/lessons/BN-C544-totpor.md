## You'll want to know: তৎপর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C544-SOMOY544-01]; assesses=[] -->

**তৎপর** — *tôtpôr* — "prompt; quick to act".

> যদি দমকল তৎপর না হত, তাহলে আগুন ছড়াত।
>
> *Jôdi dômkôl tôtpôr nā hôtô, tāhôle āgun chhôṛātô.*
>
> If the fire brigade hadn't been quick, the fire would have spread.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তৎপর** (*tôtpôr*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *tatpara*, "following on that, intent on it" (Monier-Williams).
