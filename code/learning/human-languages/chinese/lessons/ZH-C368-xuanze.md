## You'll want to know: xuǎnzé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C368-KEEPGOING368-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xuǎnzé* — "to make a choice".

> *wǒ juéde nǐ yīnggāi zìjǐ xuǎnzé* — I think you should make the choice yourself.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuǎnzé* is built from *xuǎn* and *zé*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjwenX* and *draek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses each of them 'to choose'.
