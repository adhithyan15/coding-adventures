## You'll want to know: നീറുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C539-AHARAM539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**നീറുക** — *nīṟuka* — "to smart; to sting".

> മുറിവിൽ മരുന്ന് വച്ചപ്പോൾ നീറി.
>
> *muṟivil marunnŭ vaccappōḷ nīṟi.*
>
> It stung when the medicine went on the wound.

Past: നീറി, *nīṟi*.

The sting of a cut or of smoke in the eyes; a grief can also നീറുക inside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നീറുക** (*nīṟuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3693 lists it with words for burning to ashes and for a grief that burns (DEDR 3693).
