## You'll want to know: sur le coup
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C270-SOUDAIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sur le coup** — "at the time, on the spot".

> Sur le coup, je n'ai rien compris.
>
> *At the time, I did not understand anything.*

The feeling of the very moment; later you see things differently.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coup* is inherited from Late Latin *colpus* "a blow", from Latin *colaphus* "a punch" (EtymDB): at the moment of the blow.
