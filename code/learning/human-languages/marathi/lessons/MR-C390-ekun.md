## You'll want to know: एकूण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C390-EKUN390-01]; assesses=[] -->

**एकूण** — *ekūṇ* — "in total; overall".

> वर्गात एकूण तीस मुलं आहेत.
>
> *vargāt ekūṇ tīs mulaṁ āhet.*
>
> There are thirty children in the class in all.

Also an adjective: एकूण खर्च, the total cost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एकूण** (*ekūṇ*) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
