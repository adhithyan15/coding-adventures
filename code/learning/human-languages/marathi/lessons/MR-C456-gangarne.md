## You'll want to know: गांगरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C456-SANKOC456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गांगरणे** — *gāṅgarṇe* — "to get flustered".

> प्रश्न ऐकून तो गांगरला आणि गप्प बसला.
>
> *praśna aikūn to gāṅgarlā āṇi gappa baslā.*
>
> He got flustered at the question and fell silent.

Past (he …): तो गांगरला, *to gāṅgarlā*.

Of nerves before people: रंगमंचावर गांगरणे, to get stage fright.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गांगरणे** (*gāṅgarṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
