## You'll want to know: കൊഞ്ചുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C558-VARDHAKYAM558-03]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊഞ്ചുക** — *koñcuka* — "to prattle; to talk baby talk".

> കുഞ്ഞ് അമ്മയോട് കൊഞ്ചി.
>
> *kuññŭ ammayōṭŭ koñci.*
>
> The baby babbled sweetly to its mother.

Past: കൊഞ്ചി, *koñci*.

Also the cooing of grown-ups to a baby; കൊഞ്ചൽ is the noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊഞ്ചുക** (*koñcuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2043 gives the Malayalam verb as to prattle and to fondle, as with a child (DEDR 2043).
