## You'll want to know: वारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C495-YAUVANA495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वारयति** — *vārayati* — "holds back; forbids".

> वैद्यः शर्करां वारयति।
>
> *vaidyaḥ śarkarāṃ vārayati.*
>
> The physician forbids sugar.

Past (he …): सः शर्करां वारितवान्, *saḥ śarkarāṃ vāritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वारयति** (*vārayati*) is built on the causative of the root √vṛ, "to cover, to ward off"; MW's ppp *vārita* is "warded off, prevented, restrained" (MW).
