## You'll want to know: siccome
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C350-SICCOME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**siccome** — "since, as (at the start of a sentence)".

> Siccome piove, prendiamo la macchina.
>
> *Since it is raining, we will take the car.*

*Siccome* opens the sentence; *perché* comes after the main clause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*siccome* is built from *sì* "so", inherited from Latin *sīc* "thus", and *come* "as", inherited from Latin *quōmodo* "in what way" (Lewis & Short).
