## You'll want to know: লুডো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C518-GHOROA518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**লুডো** — *luḍo* — "ludo".

> বৃষ্টির দিনে আমরা লুডো খেলি।
>
> *Brishṭir dine āmrā luḍo kheli.*
>
> On rainy days we play ludo.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লুডো** (*luḍo*) is borrowed from English *ludo*, the name of the game.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
