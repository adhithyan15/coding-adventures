## You'll want to know: la lavandería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C680-TIENDAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la lavandería** — "the launderette, the laundry".

> En el barrio hay una lavandería.
>
> *There is a launderette in the neighbourhood.*

*Lavandería automática* — self-service launderette.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lavandería* is built on *lavandero* "washerman", from *lavar* "to wash", which is inherited from Latin *lavāre* (EtymDB).
