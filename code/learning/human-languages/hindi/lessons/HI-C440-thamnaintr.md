## You'll want to know: थमना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C440-GHIRNA440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**थमना** — *thamnā* — "to let up; to stop (of rain or wind)".

> दो घंटे बाद बारिश थमी।
>
> *do ghaṇṭe bād bāriś thamī.*
>
> The rain let up after two hours.

Past (it …): वह आँधी थम गई, *vah ā̃dhī tham gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थमना** (*thamnā*) belongs with Sanskrit *stambhate*, "supports, arrests" (CDIAL 13683), which lists Hindi *thāmnā*, "to prop, stop"; थमना is its intransitive: the rain stops itself.
