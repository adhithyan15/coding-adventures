# متردد (mutaraddid) — hesitant; in doubt

## You'll want to know: متردد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C538-TAZABZUB538-02]; assesses=[] -->

**متردد** — *mutaraddid* — "hesitant; in doubt".

> اگر وہ متردد ہے تو ہم انتظار کریں گے۔
>
> *agar voh mutaraddid hai to ham intizār kareṅ ge.*
>
> If he is unsure, we will wait.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متردد** (*mutaraddid*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mutaraddid*, "going back and forth".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
