# поэтому and так что — the reason, turned round

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-POTOMU-CHTO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-POTOMU-CHTO-CLAUSE-01, RU-LEX-C154-PRICH154-02, RU-GRAMMAR-PO-MOEMU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "because" and "since". (*Potomú shto. Tak kak.*) Then an
opinion with *po-móyemu*. An opinion needs a reason, and a reason has a
direction.

## Grammar Lens: back to the cause, forward to the result
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-POETOMU-RESULT-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C154-PRICH154-01, RU-LEX-C134-ACT134-02, RU-LEX-C59-WEATHER-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-IMPERFECTIVE-PROCESS-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-LI-PLURAL-01, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01] -->

One rainy day, said two ways:

> **Мы не гуляли, потому что шёл дождь.** — *my ne gulyáli, potomú shto
> shol dozhd* — We didn't go for a walk, because it was raining.
> **Шёл дождь, поэтому мы не гуляли.** — *shol dozhd, poétomu my ne
> gulyáli* — It was raining, so we didn't go for a walk.

**Потому что** stands in front of the cause. **Поэтому** stands in front
of the result. The facts are the same; the order flips, and the comma
moves with the seam. **Поэтому** can also open a new sentence: *Шёл
дождь. Поэтому мы не гуляли.*

## You'll want to know: так что
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-TAK-CHTO-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C242-MESTO242-03, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01] -->

**так что** — *tak shto* — "so, so then". It also points to the result,
and it sounds more like talk:

> **Шёл дождь, так что мы сидели в кафе.** — *shol dozhd, tak shto my
> sidéli v kafé* — It was raining, so we sat in a café.

It usually sits inside one sentence, after a comma.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C154-PRICH154-01] -->

**так**, "in that way, so", goes back to Old East Slavic *tako* (EtymDB
2.1), on the pointing stem *\*to-* that also gives *тот*, "that". So
**так что** is "that way, so that". **Поэтому** is *по*, "by", with
*этому*, a form of *этот*: "for this".
