Marathi, chapter 241: Flowerpot, Seedling, Trunk, Lawn, Footpath.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कुंडी (kuṇḍī) — a flowerpot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a seat, then the Marathi for a wheel.

You'll want to know: कुंडी.
कुंडी — kuṇḍī — "a flowerpot".

The word, taken apart.
कुंडी (kuṇḍī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kuṇḍa, "bowl, waterpot" (CDIAL 3264). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "small open-mouthed jar". It takes the Sanskrit word from Dravidian.

पायवाट — pāyvāṭ — "a footpath".

The word, taken apart.
पायवाट (pāyvāṭ) is "foot-path": pāy, "foot", from Sanskrit pāda (CDIAL 8056), and vāṭ, "path", from Sanskrit vartman, "track of a wheel, path" (CDIAL 11366).
