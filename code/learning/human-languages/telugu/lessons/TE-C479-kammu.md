## You'll want to know: కమ్ము
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C479-PIDUGU479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**కమ్ము** — *kammu* — "to close in; to envelop".

> ఆకాశంలో నల్లని మబ్బులు కమ్మాయి.
>
> *ākāśaṁlō nallani mabbulu kammāyi.*
>
> Dark clouds closed in across the sky.

Past (it …): కమ్మింది, *kammindi*.

Said of clouds, smoke or darkness that cover everything. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: కమ్మింది, it closed in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కమ్ము** (*kammu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1221 lists Telugu *kammu*, "to cover, to surround, to overspread", beside *kappu*, "to spread over in a thick covering, as clouds".
