## You'll want to know: बावजूद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C371-HALANKI371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बावजूद** — *bāvjūd* — "despite (के बावजूद)".

> बीमारी के बावजूद वह काम पर आया।
>
> *bīmārī ke bāvjūd vah kām par āyā.*
>
> Despite being ill, he came to work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बावजूद** (*bāvjūd*) is usually traced to Persian *bāvujūd*, "with the existence (of)". Its second part is Arabic *wujūd*, "existence".
