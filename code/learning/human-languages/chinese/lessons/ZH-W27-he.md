## Script — 禾 first, then 口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 和

**Eight strokes**, five on the left and three on the right — and the three on the
right are 口, which you have written since the fourth chapter.

The left half is 禾 *hé*, standing grain:

1. a short **piě** at the very top, falling left
2. the **héng** beneath it
3. the long **shù** straight down the middle
4. a **piě** falling to the lower left
5. a **diǎn** closing to the lower right

Then 口, in its usual order:

6. the **shù** down its left side
7. the **héngzhé** across the top and down the right
8. the **héng** closing the floor

**Left half complete before the right one starts**, as always.

This is a **phono-semantic compound**, the kind the 请 lesson named: one part
carries meaning and the other carries sound. Here the sounding half is
unusually generous — 禾 is itself said *hé*, so the character tells you its own
pronunciation outright.
