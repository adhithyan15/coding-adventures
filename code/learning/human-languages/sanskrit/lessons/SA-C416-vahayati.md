## You'll want to know: वाहयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C416-NAVIKA416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहयति** — *vāhayati* — "rows (a boat); drives".

> नाविकः अरित्रेण नौकां वाहयति।
>
> *nāvikaḥ aritreṇa naukāṃ vāhayati.*
>
> The boatman rows the boat with an oar.

Past (he …): सः नौकां वाहितवान्, *saḥ naukāṃ vāhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाहयति** (*vāhayati*) is built on the root √vah, "to carry", which gives *vahati*, "carries"; MW gives its causative as to make carry, to drive a chariot and to propel a boat, and KRM gives the participle *vāhita* (MW).
