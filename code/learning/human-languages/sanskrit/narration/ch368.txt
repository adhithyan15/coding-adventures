Sanskrit, chapter 368: Doubts and Hopes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शंका (śaṅkā) — a misgiving; a fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for swiftly, then the Sanskrit for expects.

You'll want to know: शंका.
शंका — śaṅkā — "a misgiving; a fear".
यदि शंका अस्ति, तर्हि प्रश्नं पृच्छतु। yadi śaṅkā asti, tarhi praśnaṃ pṛcchatu. If you have a misgiving, ask a question.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
शंका (śaṅkā) comes from the root √śaṅk, "to doubt, to fear"; MW gives śaṅkā "apprehension, fear, distrust" and "doubt, hesitation" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
तर्कयति (tarkayati) — conjectures; guesses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for expects, then the Sanskrit for a misgiving.

You'll want to know: तर्कयति.
तर्कयति — tarkayati — "conjectures; guesses".
यदि दीपः न ज्वलति, तर्हि तर्कयामि यत् सः गतः। yadi dīpaḥ na jvalati, tarhi tarkayāmi yat saḥ gataḥ. If no lamp is burning, I guess that he has gone.
Past (he …): सः कारणं तर्कितवान्, saḥ kāraṇaṃ tarkitavān.

The word, taken apart.
तर्कयति (tarkayati) is built on tarka, "conjecture, reasoning"; MW gives √tark "to conjecture, to guess, to suspect, to infer" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
आशंसते (āśaṃsate) — hopes for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a misgiving, then the Sanskrit for conjectures.

You'll want to know: आशंसते.
आशंसते — āśaṃsate — "hopes for".
यदि सः प्रयत्नं करोति, तर्हि सफलताम् आशंसते। yadi saḥ prayatnaṃ karoti, tarhi saphalatām āśaṃsate. If he makes an effort, he hopes for success.
Past (he …): सः सफलताम् आशंसितवान्, saḥ saphalatām āśaṃsitavān.

The word, taken apart.
आशंसते (āśaṃsate) is built on ā and the root √śaṃs, "to declare, to praise"; MW gives ā-√śaṃs "to hope for, to expect, to wish to attain" and ā-śaṃsita "hoped, expected" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनुमानम् (anumānam) — an inference; a guess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for conjectures, then the Sanskrit for hopes for.

You'll want to know: अनुमानम्.
अनुमानम् — anumānam — "an inference; a guess".
यदि मम अनुमानं सत्यम्, तर्हि सः श्वः गमिष्यति। yadi mama anumānaṃ satyam, tarhi saḥ śvaḥ gamiṣyati. If my guess is right, he will go tomorrow.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अनुमानम् (anumānam) is built on anu, "after", and the root √mā, "to measure"; MW gives anu-māna "inference, consideration" and "guess, conjecture" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
किल (kila) — so they say; indeed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for hopes for, then the Sanskrit for an inference.

You'll want to know: किल.
किल — kila — "so they say; indeed".
सः किल रुग्णः; यदि एवम्, तर्हि अहं गमिष्यामि। saḥ kila rugṇaḥ; yadi evam, tarhi ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi. He is ill, so they say; if so, I will go.

The word, taken apart.
किल (kila) MW gives kila as a particle of emphasis, "indeed, verily", and "so said, so reported"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
