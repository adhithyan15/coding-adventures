## You'll want to know: फरशी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C522-KHOLI522-04]; assesses=[] -->

**फरशी** — *pharśī* — "a floor tile; a stone floor".

> नवीन घरात पांढरी फरशी आहे.
>
> *navīn gharāt pāṇḍhrī pharśī āhe.*
>
> The new house has a white tiled floor.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फरशी** (*pharśī*) is usually traced to Persian *farš*, "carpet, floor", which goes back to Arabic *farš*.
