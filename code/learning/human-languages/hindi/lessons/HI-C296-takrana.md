## You'll want to know: टकराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C296-TAKKAR296-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टकराना** — *ṭakrānā* — "to collide; to crash into".

> ट्रक एक बस से टकराया।
>
> *ṭrak ek bas se ṭakrāyā.*
>
> The lorry crashed into a bus.

Past (it …): वह ट्रक पेड़ से टकराया, *vah ṭrak peṛ se ṭakrāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टकराना** (*ṭakrānā*) is the verb made on *ṭakkar*, a blow or a crash, which CDIAL 5424 lists for Hindi under *ṭakkarā*, "a blow on the head". What you hit takes **से**: **बस से टकराया**.
