Spanish, chapter 621: Lo hace posible o no.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
impedir — to stop, to prevent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for complicated, then the Spanish for to raise.

You'll want to know: impedir.
impedir — "to stop, to prevent".
La lluvia nos impidió salir. The rain stopped us going out.
Like pedir: impido, impidió.

The word, taken apart.
impedir is a learned borrowing from Latin impedire 'to hinder, to entangle the feet', built on pes, pedis 'foot' (EtymDB).
