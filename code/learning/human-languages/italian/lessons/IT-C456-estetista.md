## You'll want to know: l'estetista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C456-CURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'estetista** — "the beautician".

> L'estetista lavora vicino al parrucchiere.
>
> *The beautician works near the hairdresser's.*

One form for a man or a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estetista* is built on *estetica* 'beauty care', a learned borrowing from Greek *aisthētikós* 'of the senses'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
