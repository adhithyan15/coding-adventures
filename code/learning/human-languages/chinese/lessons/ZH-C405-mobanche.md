## You'll want to know: mòbānchē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C405-GATES405-04]; assesses=[] -->

*mòbānchē* — "last bus or train".

> *wǒmen zuò mòbānchē qù jīchǎng* — We're taking the last train to the airport.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mòbānchē* is built from *mò* 'last', *bān* 'a shift' and 车 'vehicle', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *mat*, *paen* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the tip of a tree and 'the end', and the second as dividing jade tokens, so the sense 'a shift, a run' is not given there.
