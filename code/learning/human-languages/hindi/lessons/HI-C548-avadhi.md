## You'll want to know: अवधि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C548-AVADHI548-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधि** — *avadhi* — "a period; a time limit".

> टिकट की अवधि एक हफ़्ता है।
>
> *Ṭikaṭ kī avadhi ek haftā hai.*
>
> The ticket is valid for a week.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधि** (*avadhi*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *avadhi*, "a term, a limit" (Monier-Williams).
