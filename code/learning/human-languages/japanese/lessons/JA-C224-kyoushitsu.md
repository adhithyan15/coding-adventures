## You'll want to know: きょうしつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C224-SCHOOL224-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうしつ** — *kyōshitsu* — "a classroom".

> **がくせいは きょうしつで べんきょうします。** — *gakusei wa kyōshitsu de benkyō shimasu* — "The students study in the classroom."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうしつ** (*kyōshitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyō*, "teach" (Middle Chinese *kaewH*) and *shitsu*, "room" (*syit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
