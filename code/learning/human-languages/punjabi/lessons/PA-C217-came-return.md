# Return to come

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AUNA, PA-NEG-NOT-UNDERSTOOD-01] -->

[YOU HEAR: *āuṇā*] Say **ਆਉਣਾ**, "to come". Then **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ**.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-NEG-NOT-UNDERSTOOD-01, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216] -->

Listen to the last word of **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ**: *āī*. It is a came form, and
you have been saying it since the early lessons. Next to it, hold the went forms
**ਗਿਆ · ਗਈ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AUNA, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-NEG-NOT-UNDERSTOOD-01] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *to come*] Say **ਆਉਣਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *Aman went home*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *I did not understand*] Say **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AUNA, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *went*, for a man and a woman, once more.
