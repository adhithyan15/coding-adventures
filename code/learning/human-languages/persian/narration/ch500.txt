Persian, chapter 500: Uncles, Aunts and Cousins.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عمو ('amu) — an uncle (father's brother).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for sleepy, then the Persian for to oversleep.

You'll want to know: عمو.
عمو ('amu) — "an uncle (father's brother)".
عموی من در روستا زندگی میکند. 'amu-ye man dar rustâ zendegi mikonad. My uncle lives in a village.
The father's brother; the mother's brother is dâyi. Children also call any friendly older man 'amu.

The word, taken apart.
'amu comes from Arabic ʿamm, 'paternal uncle' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
پسرعمو (pesar'amu) — a paternal cousin (son of a father's brother).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an uncle, then the Persian for an aunt.

You'll want to know: پسرعمو.
پسرعمو (pesar'amu) — "a paternal cousin (son of a father's brother)".
پسرعمویم با من همکلاسی است. pesar'amuyam bâ man hamkelâsi ast. My cousin is in my class.
pesar 'son' + 'amu. Persian names each cousin by the parent: dokhtar'amme, pesardâyi and so on.

The word, taken apart.
pesar'amu is built in Persian on native pesar, 'son', and 'amu, which comes from Arabic ʿamm, 'paternal uncle' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
خواهرزاده (khâharzâde) — a sister's child, a nephew or niece.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a paternal cousin, then the Persian for a maternal cousin.

You'll want to know: خواهرزاده.
خواهرزاده (khâharzâde) — "a sister's child, a nephew or niece".
خواهرزاده من سه ساله است. khâharzâde-ye man se sâle ast. My nephew is three.
khâhar 'sister' + zâde 'born of'. One word for a sister's son or daughter.

The word, taken apart.
khâharzâde is built on two inherited words: khâhar, 'sister', a cousin of Latin soror (EtymDB), and zâde, 'born of', which EtymDB derives from zâdan, 'to give birth' (EtymDB).
