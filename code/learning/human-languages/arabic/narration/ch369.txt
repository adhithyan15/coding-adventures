Arabic, chapter 369: Advice and hesitation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نصح (naṣaḥa) — to advise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to neglect, then the Arabic for to care about.

You'll want to know: نصح.
نصح — naṣaḥa — "to advise".
نصحني الطبيب بالراحة. naṣaḥanī aṭ-ṭabīb bi-r-rāḥa. The doctor advised me to rest.
Present: ينصح, yanṣaḥ.
The advice comes after بـ or أن: نصحه بـ …, نصحه أن ….

The word, taken apart.
نصح (naṣaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root n-ṣ-ḥ, "to be sincere" (Lane). Naṣaḥa is a Form I verb: to give sincere counsel, to advise.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تردد (taraddada) — to hesitate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to hint, then the Arabic for to encourage.

You'll want to know: تردد.
تردد — taraddada — "to hesitate".
تردد الولد قبل أن يقفز في الماء. taraddada al-walad qabla an yaqfiza fī al-māʾ. The boy hesitated before jumping into the water.
Present: يتردد, yataraddad.
Also to go often: تردد (taraddada) على السوق, went to the market often.

The word, taken apart.
تردد (taraddada) is built on the native Arabic root r-d-d, "to send back" (Lane). It is Form V, to turn back and forth: to waver, to hesitate.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بديل (badīl) — an alternative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to encourage, then the Arabic for to hesitate.

You'll want to know: بديل.
بديل — badīl — "an alternative".
الدراجة بديل جيد عن السيارة. ad-darrāja badīl jayyid ʿan as-sayyāra. A bicycle is a good alternative to a car.
بديل (badīl) عن or بديل (badīl) لـ: an alternative to.

The word, taken apart.
بديل (badīl) is built on the native Arabic root b-d-l, "to change, to put in place of" (Lane). A badīl is what is put in place of another: a substitute, an alternative.
