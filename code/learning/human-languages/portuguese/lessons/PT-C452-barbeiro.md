## You'll want to know: o barbeiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C452-CUIDADOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o barbeiro** — "the barber".

> O barbeiro corta o cabelo ao meu avô há trinta anos.
>
> *The barber has cut my grandfather's hair for thirty years.*

Built on *a barba*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barbeiro* is built from *barba* 'beard' and *-eiro* (EtymDB); *barba* is inherited from Latin *barba*.
