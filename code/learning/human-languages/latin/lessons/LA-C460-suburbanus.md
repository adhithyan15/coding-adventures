## You'll want to know: suburbānus, suburbāna, suburbānum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C460-TURBA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**suburbānus, suburbāna, suburbānum** — "near the city, suburban".

> Dīves vīllam suburbānam habet et saepe ibi cēnat.
>
> *The rich man has a villa near the city and often dines there.*

An adjective of three endings, from *sub* 'near, under' and *urbs* 'city'. *Suburbānum*, a villa near the city. English *suburban* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suburbānus* is built from *sub* 'under, close to' and *urbānus* 'of the city', from *urbs* (Lewis & Short). English *suburban* comes from it.
