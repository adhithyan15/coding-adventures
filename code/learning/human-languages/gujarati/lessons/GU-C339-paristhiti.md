## You'll want to know: પરિસ્થિતિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C339-ASAR339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિસ્થિતિ** — *paristhiti* — "a situation".

> પૂર પછી ગામની પરિસ્થિતિ ખરાબ હતી.
>
> *pūr pachī gāmnī paristhiti kharāb hatī.*
>
> After the flood the village was in a bad way.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિસ્થિતિ** (*paristhiti*) is built from Sanskrit *pari-*, "around", and *sthiti*, "standing, a state" (Monier-Williams): the state of things all around.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
