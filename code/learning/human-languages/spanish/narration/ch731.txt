Spanish, chapter 731: Crecer por dentro.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
maduro — mature; ripe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for beauty, then the Spanish for elegance.

You'll want to know: maduro.
maduro — "mature; ripe".
Es muy maduro para su edad. He is very mature for his age.
With estar it is ripe fruit: el plátano está maduro.

The word, taken apart.
maduro is inherited from Latin mātūrus "ripe, mature" (EtymDB); the Latin -t- softened to -d-.
