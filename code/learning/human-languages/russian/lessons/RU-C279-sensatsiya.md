## You'll want to know: сенсация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C279-IZVESTIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сенсация** (*sensátsiya*) — "a sensation".

> Эта новость стала настоящей сенсацией.
>
> *Éta nóvost stála nastoyáshchey sensátsiyey.*
>
> This news became a real sensation.

Feminine. The adjective is *сенсационный*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сенсация* was borrowed from French *sensation*, built on Latin *sensus* "feeling", from *sentīre* "to feel" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
