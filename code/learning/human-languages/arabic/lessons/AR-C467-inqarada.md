## You'll want to know: انقرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C467-MAHMIYYA467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**انقرض** — *inqaraḍa* — "to die out; to become extinct".

> انقرض هذا الحيوان قبل قرون.
>
> *inqaraḍa hādhā al-ḥayawān qabla qurūn.*
>
> This animal died out centuries ago.

Present: ينقرض, *yanqariḍ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انقرض** (*inqaraḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-ḍ*, "to cut, to gnaw" (Lane). It is Form VII, "to be cut off".
