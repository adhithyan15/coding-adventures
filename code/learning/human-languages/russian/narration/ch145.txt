Russian, chapter 145: To sign, To print, To keep, To download, To be beaten.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
подписывать (podpísyvat) — to sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to praise, then the Russian for to scold.

You'll want to know: подписывать.
подписывать (podpísyvat) — "to sign".

The word, taken apart.
подписывать (podpísyvat) is под-, "under", with -писывать, from писать, "to write": you sign at the foot of a page.

Before the new one: say the Russian for to sign, then the Russian for to print.

You'll want to know: сохранять.
сохранять (sokhranyát) — "to keep; to save (a file)".

The word, taken apart.
сохранять (sokhranyát) is с- with хранять, from хранить, "to keep", a Church Slavonic word, says Vasmer, beside the native хоронить, now "to bury".

Lesson 4 of 5.
скачивать (skáchivat) — to download.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to print, then the Russian for to keep; to save.

You'll want to know: скачивать.
скачивать (skáchivat) — "to download".

The word, taken apart.
скачивать (skáchivat) is с-, "off, down", with -качивать, from качать, "to swing, to pump", which Vasmer derives from катать, "to roll". Downloading is pumping data off.

проигрывать (proígryvat) is про-, "through, away", with -игрывать, from играть, "to play": to play your stake away, to lose.
