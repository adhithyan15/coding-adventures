## You'll want to know: મોડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C147-PAST147-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C94-TIME94-02] -->

**મોડું** (*moḍũ*) — "late".

It is the partner of **વહેલું**, *early*, and like the possessives it bends to
agree:

> **હું મોડો આવ્યો.** — *hũ moḍo āvyo.* — I came late. (a man)
> **તે મોડી આવી.** — *te moḍī āvī.* — She came late.
> **મોડું થયું.** — *moḍũ thayũ.* — It got late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મોડું** (*moḍũ*) is an old word whose origin is not settled: CDIAL lists no
Gujarati form for it, and no Sanskrit source has been shown. Its **-ું** is the
neuter ending of the adjective, which is why it turns into **મોડો** and **મોડી**.
