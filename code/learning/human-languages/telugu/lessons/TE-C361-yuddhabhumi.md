## You'll want to know: యుద్ధభూమి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C361-YUDDHABHUMI361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**యుద్ధభూమి** — *yuddhabhūmi* — "a battlefield".

> యుద్ధభూమిలో రెండు సైన్యాలు ఎదురయ్యాయి.
>
> *yuddhabhūmilō reṇḍu sainyālu edurayyāyi.*
>
> Two armies met on the battlefield.

యుద్ధం, war, and భూమి, ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**యుద్ధభూమి** (*yuddhabhūmi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *yuddha*, "battle" (CDIAL 10499), and *bhūmi*, "earth, ground" (CDIAL 9557).
