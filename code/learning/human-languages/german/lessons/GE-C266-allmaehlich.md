## You'll want to know: allmählich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C266-PLOETZLICH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**allmählich** — "gradually".

> Allmählich wurde es dunkel.
>
> *Gradually it got dark.*

Close to *nach und nach*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allmählich* comes from Middle High German *almehlich* (EtymDB), usually traced to *gemach* "slow, easy": little by little.
