Lesson 1 of 5.
چاه (châh) — a well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an embassy, then the Persian for customs.

You'll want to know: چاه.
چاه — châh — "a well".

The word, taken apart.
châh is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian čāh, "well".

Lesson 3 of 5.
آسیاب (âsiyâb) — a mill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a well, then the Persian for a hut.

You'll want to know: آسیاب.
آسیاب — âsiyâb — "a mill".

The word, taken apart.
âsiyâb is usually explained as âs, "millstone", with âb, "water": the water-driven mill. EtymDB derives it from âb, which is inherited (Middle Persian āb).

You'll want to know: دکان.

The word, taken apart.
dokkân is borrowed from Arabic dukkān, "shop", which EtymDB traces through Aramaic dukkānā, "platform, bench", back to Akkadian.
