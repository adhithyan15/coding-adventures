Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 208: Offices in Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अस्पताल (aspatāl) — a hospital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a big gate, then the Marwadi for a tower.

You'll want to know: अस्पताल.
अस्पताल — aspatāl — "a hospital".
अस्पताल नदी कनै है। aspatāl nadī kanai hai. The hospital is near the river.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अस्पताल (aspatāl) is borrowed from English hospital, reshaped in everyday speech.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तहसील (tahsīl) — a sub-district office; a tehsil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a tower, then the Marwadi for a hospital.

You'll want to know: तहसील.
तहसील — tahsīl — "a sub-district office; a tehsil".
तहसील सोमवार बंद है। tahsīl somvār band hai. The tehsil office is closed on Monday.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तहसील (tahsīl) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taḥṣīl, "collecting": probably the office where land revenue was collected.

Lesson 5 of 5.
खुलणो (khulṇo) — to come open; to open (of a shop or office).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a factory, then the Marwadi for a court of law.

You'll want to know: खुलणो.
खुलणो — khulṇo — "to come open; to open (of a shop or office)".
दफ्तर दिनूगै खुलै है। daphtar dinūgai khulai hai. The office opens early in the morning.
Past (it …): वो दफ्तर खुल्यो, vo daphtar khulyo.

The word, taken apart.
खुलणो (khulṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed khull-, "to be open"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari khulo, "open", and Hindi khulnā, "to be open" (CDIAL 3945). खोलणो, "to open", is its transitive partner.
