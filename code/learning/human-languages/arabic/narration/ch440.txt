Arabic, chapter 440: Breaks and souvenirs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نافورة (nāfūra) — a fountain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to explore, then the Arabic for to take along.

You'll want to know: نافورة.
نافورة — nāfūra — "a fountain".
لعب الأطفال حول النافورة. laʿiba al-aṭfāl ḥawla an-nāfūra. The children played around the fountain.

The word, taken apart.
نافورة (nāfūra) names a fountain; the origin of the word is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تذكار (tidhkār) — a souvenir; a keepsake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to take along, then the Arabic for a fountain.

You'll want to know: تذكار.
تذكار — tidhkār — "a souvenir; a keepsake".
اشتريت تذكارا صغيرا لأختي. ishtaraytu tidhkāran ṣaghīran li-ukhtī. I bought a small souvenir for my sister.

The word, taken apart.
تذكار (tidhkār) is built on the native Arabic root dh-k-r, "to remember" (Lane). A tidhkār is a thing that makes one remember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a fountain, then the Arabic for a souvenir.

You'll want to know: استراحة.
استراحة — istirāḥa — "a rest stop; a break".
وقفت الحافلة عند استراحة على الطريق. waqafat al-ḥāfila ʿinda istirāḥa ʿalā aṭ-ṭarīq. The bus stopped at a rest stop on the road.

The word, taken apart.
استراحة (istirāḥa) is built on the native Arabic root r-w-ḥ, "to be at ease; breath, wind" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form X istarāḥa, "to rest".

Lesson 4 of 5.
رافق (rāfaqa) — to accompany.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a souvenir, then the Arabic for a rest stop.

You'll want to know: رافق.
رافق — rāfaqa — "to accompany".
رافقتنا جدتي في الرحلة. rāfaqatnā jaddatī fī ar-riḥla. My grandmother came with us on the trip.
Present: يرافق, yurāfiq.

The word, taken apart.
رافق (rāfaqa) is built on the native Arabic root r-f-q, "to be gentle; a companion" (Lane). It is Form III, "to be a companion to"; a rafīq is a companion.
