## You'll want to know: ચાંપવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C451-VALAG451-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાંપવું** — *cā̃pvũ* — "to press close; to clasp".

> માતાએ બાળકને છાતીએ ચાંપ્યું.
>
> *mātāe bāḷakne chātīe cā̃pyũ.*
>
> The mother clasped the child to her chest.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને છાતીએ ચાંપ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne chātīe cā̃pyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાંપવું** (*cā̃pvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*capp-*, "press"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to press" (CDIAL 4674).
