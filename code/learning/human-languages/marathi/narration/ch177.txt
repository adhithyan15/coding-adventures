Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रेमळ (premaḷ) — affectionate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for impossible, then the Marathi for main.

You'll want to know: प्रेमळ.
प्रेमळ — premaḷ — "affectionate".

The word, taken apart.
प्रेमळ (premaḷ) is Sanskrit preman, "love", with the Marathi ending -aḷ, "full of".
