## You'll want to know: арендовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C398-ARENDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**арендовать** (*arendovát*) — "to rent, to lease".

> Мы арендуем маленький офис.
>
> *My arendúyem málenkiy ófis.*
>
> We rent a small office.

Imperfective and perfective; present *арендую, арендуешь*. For a flat, people usually say *снимать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**арендовать** is built on *аренда* "a lease" with the verb suffix *-овать*; the noun came through Polish *arenda* from Medieval Latin (EtymDB).
