Lesson 1 of 5.
bekannt — well known.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for full, then the German for old-fashioned.

You'll want to know: bekannt.
bekannt — "well known".

The word, taken apart.
bekannt is a past participle built on kennen, "to know" from Old High German kennan from Proto-Germanic kannijaną (EtymDB 2.1): known.

Lesson 5 of 5.
wütend — angry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for nervous, then the German for proud.

You'll want to know: wütend.
wütend — "angry".

The word, taken apart.
wütend is the present participle of the verb "to rage", built on the word for "fury", Old High German wuot from Proto-Germanic wōdaz, "mad" (EtymDB 2.1). The god's name Wodan shares the root.
