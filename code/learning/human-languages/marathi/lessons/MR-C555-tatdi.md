## You'll want to know: तातडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C555-GHAI555-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तातडी** — *tātḍī* — "urgency".

> कामाची तातडी होती, म्हणून तो रविवारीही कार्यालयात गेला.
>
> *kāmācī tātḍī hotī, mhaṇūn to ravivārīhī kāryālayāt gelā.*
>
> The work was urgent, so he went to the office even on Sunday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तातडी** (*tātḍī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *tā̃tḍī*, "urgency, flurry", under Sanskrit *tānti*, "suffocation" (CDIAL 5764).
