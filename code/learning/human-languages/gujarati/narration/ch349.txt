Gujarati, chapter 349: Fair and Unfair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વાજબી (vājbī) — reasonable; fair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a ruling, then the Gujarati for to hit hard.

You'll want to know: વાજબી.
વાજબી — vājbī — "reasonable; fair".
દુકાનદારે વાજબી ભાવ માગ્યો. dukāndāre vājbī bhāv māgyo. The shopkeeper asked a fair price.

The word, taken apart.
વાજબી (vājbī) is usually traced to Arabic wājib, "due, proper", through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ભેદભાવ (bhedbhāv) — discrimination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hit hard, then the Gujarati for reasonable.

You'll want to know: ભેદભાવ.
ભેદભાવ — bhedbhāv — "discrimination".
શાળામાં છોકરા અને છોકરી વચ્ચે ભેદભાવ ન હોવો જોઈએ. śāḷāmā̃ chokrā ane chokrī vacce bhedbhāv na hovo joīe. There should be no discrimination between boys and girls at school.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભેદભાવ (bhedbhāv) is built from Sanskrit bheda, "a difference", and bhāva, "a state, a feeling" (Monier-Williams): treating people as different.

Lesson 4 of 5.
તટસ્થ (taṭasth) — neutral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for discrimination, then the Gujarati for equal.

You'll want to know: તટસ્થ.
તટસ્થ — taṭasth — "neutral".
આ વિવાદમાં હું તટસ્થ રહીશ. ā vivādmā̃ hũ taṭasth rahīś. I will stay neutral in this dispute.

The word, taken apart.
તટસ્થ (taṭasth) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit taṭastha, "standing on the bank" (Monier-Williams): one who watches the river from the bank. Gujarati uses it for taking neither side.

Lesson 5 of 5.
વર્તવું (vartvũ) — to behave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for equal, then the Gujarati for neutral.

You'll want to know: વર્તવું.
વર્તવું — vartvũ — "to behave".
તમારે બધા સાથે સમાન રીતે વર્તવું જોઈએ. tamāre badhā sāthe samān rīte vartvũ joīe. You should treat everyone equally.
Past (he …): તે મહેમાનો સાથે સારી રીતે વર્ત્યો, te mahemāno sāthe sārī rīte vartyo.

The word, taken apart.
વર્તવું (vartvũ) goes back to Sanskrit vártatē, "turns, moves; takes place" (CDIAL 11352). CDIAL lists no Gujarati form; "to behave", to carry oneself, is the sense Gujarati gives it.
