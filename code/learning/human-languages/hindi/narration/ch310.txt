Hindi, chapter 310: Lost and Found.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बिछुड़ना (bichuṛnā) — to be separated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a victim, then the Hindi for refuge.

You'll want to know: बिछुड़ना.
बिछुड़ना — bichuṛnā — "to be separated".
मेले में एक बच्चा अपनी माँ से बिछुड़ गया। mele meṁ ek baccā apnī māṁ se bichuṛ gayā. At the fair a child got separated from his mother.
Past (he …): वह बच्चा बिछुड़ गया, vah baccā bichuṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
बिछुड़ना (bichuṛnā) is inherited from vikṣuṭati, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11651). The one you are parted from takes से: माँ से बिछुड़ गया.

Lesson 2 of 5.
भटकना (bhaṭaknā) — to wander; to lose one's way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for refuge, then the Hindi for to be separated.

You'll want to know: भटकना.
भटकना — bhaṭaknā — "to wander; to lose one's way".
बच्चा भटककर नदी के किनारे पहुँचा। baccā bhaṭakkar nadī ke kināre pahũcā. The child wandered off and ended up on the riverbank.
Past (he …): वह रास्ता भटक गया, vah rāstā bhaṭak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
भटकना (bhaṭaknā) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit bhraṣṭa, "fallen, ruined" (CDIAL 9655). It is wandering without knowing the way: रास्ता भटकना, to lose one's way.

Lesson 3 of 5.
तलाश (talāś) — a search.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be separated, then the Hindi for to wander.

You'll want to know: तलाश.
तलाश — talāś — "a search".
दो दिन तक बच्चे की तलाश चली। do din tak bacce kī talāś calī. The search for the child went on for two days.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तलाश (talāś) is usually traced to Persian talāš, "a search, an effort", which may itself come from Turkic. की तलाश में is in search of.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सलामत (salāmat) — safe and sound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to wander, then the Hindi for a search.

You'll want to know: सलामत.
सलामत — salāmat — "safe and sound".
आख़िर में बच्चा सलामत मिला। ākhir meṁ baccā salāmat milā. In the end the child was found safe and sound.

The word, taken apart.
सलामत (salāmat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic salāma, "safety". It describes a person who has come through unhurt.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दिलासा (dilāsā) — comfort; consolation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a search, then the Hindi for safe and sound.

You'll want to know: दिलासा.
दिलासा — dilāsā — "comfort; consolation".
पड़ोसियों ने माँ को दिलासा दिया। paṛosiyoṁ ne māṁ ko dilāsā diyā. The neighbours comforted the mother.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
दिलासा (dilāsā) is usually traced to Persian dilāsā, "a consoling", whose first part is dil, the heart, as in दिल. दिलासा देना is to comfort someone.
