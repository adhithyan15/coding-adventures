## You'll want to know: ময়লা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C413-NORDOMA413-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ময়লা** — *môylā* — "dirt; rubbish; dirty".

> ময়লা ডাস্টবিনে ফেলতে হবে।
>
> *môylā ḍāsṭbine phelte hôbe.*
>
> Rubbish has to go in the dustbin.

Also the adjective dirty, of clothes and water: ময়লা জামা, a dirty shirt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ময়লা** (*môylā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *mayalā*, "dirt", under the reconstructed *malin-*, "dirty", beside Sanskrit *malina* (CDIAL 9904).
