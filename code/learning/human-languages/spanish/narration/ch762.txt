5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el crucigrama — the crossword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to yawn, then the Spanish for to record.

You'll want to know: el crucigrama.
el crucigrama — "the crossword".
Hago el crucigrama del periódico. I do the newspaper crossword.
Masculine, though it ends in -a.

The word, taken apart.
crucigrama is built on cruz "cross", from Latin crux, and Greek grámma "letter", a twentieth-century coinage for crossed words.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la portada — the cover; the front page.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the magazine, then the Spanish for the reader.

You'll want to know: la portada.
la portada — "the cover; the front page".
Me gusta la portada de esta revista. I like the cover of this magazine.
En portada — on the front page.

The word, taken apart.
portada is usually traced to Latin porta "door, gate", the source of puerta: it named the front of a building, then the cover of a book.

Lesson 5 of 5.
hojear — to leaf through, to flick through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the reader, then the Spanish for the cover.

You'll want to know: hojear.
hojear — "to leaf through, to flick through".
Hojeo una revista en el tren. I flick through a magazine on the train.
Not to be confused with ojear, to glance at.

The word, taken apart.
hojear is built on hoja "leaf, page" with the ending -ear; hoja is inherited from Latin folia "leaves" (EtymDB), with f- turned to h-.
