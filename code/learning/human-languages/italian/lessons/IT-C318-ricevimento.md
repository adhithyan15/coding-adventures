## You'll want to know: il ricevimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C318-RICEVIMENTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il ricevimento** — "the reception".

> Il ricevimento è in un giardino in collina.
>
> *The reception is in a garden in the hills.*

In a hotel, the front desk is also *il ricevimento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ricevimento* is built on *ricevere* "to receive", from Latin *recipere* (Lewis & Short).
