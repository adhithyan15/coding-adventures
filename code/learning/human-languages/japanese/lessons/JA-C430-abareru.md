## You'll want to know: あばれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C430-ANGER430-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あばれる** — *abareru* — "to run wild; to act violently".

> **うまが あばれて、おとこが けがを しました。** — *uma ga abarete, otoko ga kega o shimashita* — "A horse ran wild and a man was hurt."

Polite **あばれます**, *abaremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **うしが あばれました。**, *ushi ga abaremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あばれる** (*abareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
