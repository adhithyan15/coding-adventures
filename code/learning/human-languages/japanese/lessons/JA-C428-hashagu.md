## You'll want to know: はしゃぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C428-JOY428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はしゃぐ** — *hashagu* — "to frolic; to make merry".

> **こどもが こうえんで はしゃいでいます。** — *kodomo ga kōen de hashaide imasu* — "The children are frolicking in the park."

Polite **はしゃぎます**, *hashagimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが はしゃぎました。**, *kodomo ga hashagimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はしゃぐ** (*hashagu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; JMdict writes it in kana (JMdict).
