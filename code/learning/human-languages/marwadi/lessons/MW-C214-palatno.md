# पलटणो (palaṭṇo) — to flip over; to change

## You'll want to know: पलटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C214-RETURN214-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पलटणो** — *palaṭṇo* — "to flip over; to change".

> आप थैली पलटो।
>
> *āp thailī palṭo.*
>
> Please turn the bag over.

Past (I …): म्हैं थैली पलटी, *mhaĩ thailī palṭī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पलटणो** (*palaṭṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*pallaṭṭ*, "turn, overturn", whose origin CDIAL calls very doubtful; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *palaṭaï*, "alters", and Hindi *palaṭnā*, "to overturn" (CDIAL 7968).
