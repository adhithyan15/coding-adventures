## You'll want to know: याददाश्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C504-BUDHAPA504-01]; assesses=[] -->

**याददाश्त** — *yāddāśt* — "memory (the power to remember)".

> बुढ़ापे में दादी की याददाश्त कमज़ोर हो गई है।
>
> *buṛhāpe meṁ dādī kī yāddāśt kamzor ho gaī hai.*
>
> In her old age my grandmother's memory has grown weak.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**याददाश्त** (*yāddāśt*) is usually traced to Persian *yāddāsht*, "a memorandum, memory", built on *yād*, "remembrance", and a word for "holding": holding things in mind.
