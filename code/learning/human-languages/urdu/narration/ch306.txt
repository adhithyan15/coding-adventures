Urdu, chapter 306: A Blast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دھماکا (dhamākā) — a bang; a blast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be startled, then the Urdu for a revelation.

You'll want to know: دھماکا.
دھماکا — dhamākā — "a bang; a blast".
دھماکا ذرا دور ہوا۔ dhamākā zarā dūr huā. The blast happened a little way off.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
دھماکا (dhamākā) is probably imitative, built on the sound dham; CDIAL sets Hindi dhamādham, "noisily", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6735).

Lesson 3 of 5.
گونجنا (gūnjnā) — to echo; to resound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a blast, then the Urdu for a bomb.

You'll want to know: گونجنا.
گونجنا — gūnjnā — "to echo; to resound".
دھماکے سے گلی گونج اٹھی۔ dhamāke se galī gūnj uṭhī. The lane rang with the blast.
Past (it …): وہ گلی گونجی, voh galī gūnjī.

The word, taken apart.
گونجنا (gūnjnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gū̃jnā, "to hum", under Sanskrit gúñjati (CDIAL 4175); in Urdu a sound that fills a place گونجتی ہے.

Lesson 5 of 5.
صدمہ (sadmā) — a shock (of grief or fright).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to echo, then the Urdu for to shudder.

You'll want to know: صدمہ.
صدمہ — sadmā — "a shock (of grief or fright)".
ماں کو خبر سے صدمہ ہوا۔ māṅ ko khabar se sadmā huā. The news came as a shock to the mother.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
صدمہ (sadmā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṣadma, "a blow, a collision"; in Urdu it is the blow of bad news. The one shocked takes کو.
