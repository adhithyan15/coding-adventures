Mandarin Chinese, chapter 247: Back From a Trip.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
jīnglì — an experience (something lived through).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a pond, then the Mandarin for to row a boat.

You'll want to know: jīnglì.
jīnglì — "an experience (something lived through)".
zhè cì lǚyóu shì yí cì hěn hǎo de jīnglì — This trip was a great experience.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jīnglì is built from jīng 'to pass through' and lì 'to go through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, keng and lek (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to pass' and 'to go'.

What you've built.
One more word for talking about the past.

You'll want to know: jìniànpǐn.
jìniànpǐn — "souvenir".
wǒ mǎile liǎng ge jìniànpǐn — I bought two souvenirs.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jìniànpǐn is built from jì 'to record', niàn 'to think of' and pǐn 'an article', all inherited from Middle Chinese, kiX, nemH and phimX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to think' and the third 'a kind' and 'a rank'.

Lesson 5 of 7.
huíyì — to recall; to think back on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a local speciality, then the Mandarin for a souvenir.

You'll want to know: huíyì.
huíyì — "to recall; to think back on".
wǒ chángcháng huíyì nà cì lǚyóu — I often think back to that trip.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huíyì is built from 回 'to return' and yì 'to remember'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese hwoj (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second, 'ik there, 'to think of'.
