Portuguese, chapter 428: Pernoitar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o alojamento — the accommodation, the lodging.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to bring forward, then the Portuguese for to rebook.

You'll want to know: o alojamento.
o alojamento — "the accommodation, the lodging".
O alojamento perto da praia é caro. Accommodation near the beach is expensive.
O alojamento local — short holiday lets in Portugal.

The word, taken apart.
alojamento is built on alojar 'to lodge', usually traced to French loger, on loge 'hut, shelter', which EtymDB traces to Frankish laubija 'shelter, roof'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
pernoitar — to spend the night, to stay overnight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to rebook, then the Portuguese for the accommodation.

You'll want to know: pernoitar.
pernoitar — "to spend the night, to stay overnight".
Pernoitámos numa aldeia a meio do caminho. We spent the night in a village halfway there.
A little formal; people also say passar a noite.

The word, taken apart.
pernoitar comes from Latin pernoctāre 'to spend the night' (EtymDB), reshaped on noite 'night', from Latin noctem.

Lesson 4 of 5.
hospedar — to put up, to host (guests).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to spend the night, then the Portuguese for the overnight stay.

You'll want to know: hospedar.
hospedar — "to put up, to host (guests)".
Os meus tios hospedaram-nos durante uma semana. My aunt and uncle put us up for a week.
Hospedar-se — to stay: hospedámo-nos num hotel.

The word, taken apart.
hospedar is inherited from Late Latin hospitāre 'to receive as a guest' (EtymDB), built on hospes 'host, guest'.

O porteiro do hotel chamou um táxi. The hotel doorman called a taxi.
In a block of flats, o porteiro looks after the entrance.

The word, taken apart.
porteiro is inherited (EtymDB) from Latin portārius 'doorkeeper', built on porta 'door' (Lewis & Short).
