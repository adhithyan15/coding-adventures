Marathi, chapter 116: Shirt, Sari, Sock, Pocket, Ring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सदरा (sadrā) — a shirt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a village, then the Marathi for clothes.

You'll want to know: सदरा.
सदरा — sadrā — "a shirt".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sadrā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sadrā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sadrā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a village, then the Marathi for clothes, then say sadrā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सदरा (sadrā) mean? ("A shirt".) Say it once more.
