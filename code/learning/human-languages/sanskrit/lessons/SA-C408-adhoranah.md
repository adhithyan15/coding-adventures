## You'll want to know: आधोरणः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C408-VAHA408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आधोरणः** — *ādhoraṇaḥ* — "a mahout; an elephant driver".

> आधोरणः गजस्य उपरि उपविशति।
>
> *ādhoraṇaḥ gajasya upari upaviśati.*
>
> The mahout sits on top of the elephant.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आधोरणः** (*ādhoraṇaḥ*) is the word MW gives for the rider or driver of an elephant; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
