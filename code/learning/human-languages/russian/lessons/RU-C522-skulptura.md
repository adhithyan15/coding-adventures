## You'll want to know: скульптура
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C522-SCULPTURE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**скульптура** (*skulptúra*) — "a sculpture".

> В музее много древних скульптур.
>
> *V muzéye mnógo drévnikh skulptúr.*
>
> There are many ancient sculptures in the museum.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*скульптура* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sculptūra* 'carving', from *sculpere* 'to carve' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source.
