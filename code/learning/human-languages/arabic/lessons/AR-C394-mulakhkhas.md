## You'll want to know: ملخص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C394-ITQAN394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ملخص** — *mulakhkhaṣ* — "a summary".

> قرأت ملخص الكتاب.
>
> *qaraʾtu mulakhkhaṣ al-kitāb.*
>
> I read the summary of the book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملخص** (*mulakhkhaṣ*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-kh-ṣ*, "to make clear, to explain" (Lane). *Mulakhkhaṣ* is the Form II passive participle: what has been summed up, a summary.

## What you've built
