Marathi, chapter 412: Finding the Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दिशा (diśā) — a direction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to roam, then the Marathi for to linger.

You'll want to know: दिशा.
दिशा — diśā — "a direction".
पुणे कोणत्या दिशेला आहे? puṇe koṇatyā diśelā āhe? In which direction is Pune?
Feminine.
Before an ending दिशा (diśā) becomes दिशे-: दिशेला, दिशेने.

The word, taken apart.
दिशा (diśā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit diśā, "direction, quarter of the compass" (Monier-Williams).

Before the new one: say the Marathi for to linger, then the Marathi for a direction.

You'll want to know: होकायंत्र.
होकायंत्र — hokāyantra — "a compass".
जंगलात होकायंत्र उपयोगी पडतं. jaṅgalāt hokāyantra upyogī paḍtaṁ. In the forest a compass comes in useful.
Neuter.
The needle of a compass points north.

The word, taken apart.
होकायंत्र (hokāyantra) ends in यंत्र, "a device", a tatsama from Sanskrit yantra (Monier-Williams); where the first part comes from is not settled.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for a compass, then the Marathi for a mark.

You'll want to know: भरकटणे.
भरकटणे — bharkaṭṇe — "to stray; to drift".
धुक्यात आम्ही वाटेवरून भरकटलो. dhukyāt āmhī vāṭevarūn bharkaṭlo. In the fog we strayed from the path.
Past (he …): तो भरकटला, to bharkaṭlā.
For people who lose their way, and for a mind that wanders.

The word, taken apart.
भरकटणे (bharkaṭṇe) is a Marathi verb for drifting off the right path; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: घुटमळणे.
घुटमळणे — ghuṭmaḷṇe — "to hang about; to hesitate".
तो दुकानाबाहेर बराच वेळ घुटमळला. to dukānābāher barāc veḷ ghuṭmaḷlā. He hung about outside the shop for quite a while.
Past (he …): तो घुटमळला, to ghuṭmaḷlā.
Moving a little but not leaving, as if unsure.

The word, taken apart.
घुटमळणे (ghuṭmaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for hovering in one place, unsure whether to go; its origin is not settled.
