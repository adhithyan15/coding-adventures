## Added — Portuguese's B1 core realizes all five B1 spine nodes

Ten chapters, 259-268 (sequences 15400-15760), 37 lessons: two chapters per B1
spine node, each closing on a review that is its payoff. Before them the track
had no *pretérito imperfeito* and no *condicional* at all, the event past was
taught only for *-ar* verbs, and all five B1 nodes were declared omissions in
`curriculum.d/spine/`. Those omission ledgers are now empty, and the level gate
no longer lists `spine-nodes` among Portuguese's B1 blockers; vocabulary remains
the open B1 criterion. The track writes European Portuguese throughout
(*comboio*, *autocarro*, *estar a* + infinitive, *ajudar-me*), with a short note
wherever Brazil differs.

- **`SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS`, chapters 259-260.** The imperfeito on all three verb
  families (`PT-C259-falava`); *tinha* and *havia* beside the *era* chapter 16
  already taught; the weather with *fazia frio* and *chovia*; the *-i* event past
  of *-er/-ir* verbs and the scene-against-events division of labour
  (`PT-C259-dormia-dormi`, `ASPECT-ONGOING-PAST`); the European progressive
  *estava a* + infinitive with *quando* for the event that cuts in. Then
  *primeiro, depois, no fim* (`CONNECTIVE-THEN`), the backbone of a told story
  under *contar uma história*, with *mas* for the turn (`NARRATIVE-SEQUENCE`),
  and the track's first B1 reading, a 91-word journey in both pasts whose every
  word is taught earlier (`PT-C260-leitura`), retold aloud in the payoff.
- **`SPINE-GIVE-REASONS`, chapters 261-262.** *acho que* and *para mim*
  (`OPINION-I-THINK`); *porque*, the word for "because" the writing chapters
  noted was missing, with the European *porque é que…?* and *porquê*; *porque*
  pointing back against *por isso*/*então* pointing forward (`CONNECTIVE-SO`);
  *devia* and *deveria* (`MODAL-SHOULD`); and the four moves of an explanation
  under *explicar*, a learned *explicāre* whose inherited twin is *chegar*
  (`EXPLANATION-GIVE`).
- **`SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL`, chapters 263-264.** *Desculpe, como se vai para…?* and
  *onde fica…?* (`DIRECTION-ASK`); *à direita, à esquerda* and understanding
  *siga em frente, vire à direita*; *ida e volta*, *só de ida* and *queria um
  bilhete para…* (`TRANSPORT-TICKET`); *perdi o comboio*, kept apart from the
  iterative *tenho perdido*, and a three-part problem report (`PROBLEM-REPORT`).
- **`SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE`, chapters 265-266.** Feelings with *estar* in the
  imperfeito as scene, with agreement and *contente com* (`ADJECTIVE-FEELING`);
  *espero* + bare infinitive and *o meu sonho é* (`AMBITION-EXPRESS`); *mais … do
  que*, *menos … do que*, *o mais*, *melhor* and *pior* (`COMPARISON-BASIC`); *há
  … que* with the present against *há* + perfeito for "ago", *desde*, and
  *durante* for a finished stretch (`TIME-DURATION`).
- **`SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION`, chapters 267-268.** *se*, kept apart from the
  reflexive *se* (`CONNECTIVE-IF`); the real condition with both halves in the
  present, and *se chover* recognised for one future occasion
  (`CONDITIONAL-REAL`); *depende* and *depende de* (`CONDITION-DEPENDS`); the
  condicional on the whole infinitive with *poderia, gostaria, deveria*
  (`MODAL-WOULD`) and *seria*. The chapter ends on `PT-W268-seis-linhas`, six
  connected lines to one reader about a journey, a second connected-composition
  piece of evidence.

New headwords (word and phrase lessons): *tinha, havia*; *fazia frio, fazia
calor*; *porque*; *devia, deveria*; *desculpe, como se vai para…?*; *à direita,
à esquerda*; *ida e volta, só de ida*; *perdi o comboio*; *espero voltar*; *mais
… do que*; *há três anos que*; *depende*; *seria*. Where an item was already
taught (*era, primeiro, depois, a seguir, fim, contar, história, pensar, por
isso, então, portanto, dever, gostaria, explicar, em frente, virar, bilhete,
comboio, perder, contente, esperar, sonho, desde, depender*) the concept lesson
builds on it. Etymologies cite EtymDB 2.1 and Lewis & Short, and say so where
neither covers a step: the loss of *-b-* in *comia*, the *-nh-* of *tinha*,
*fim*, *menos*, *direita*, *desculpar*, *ida*, the folding-sails story behind
*chegar*, and the condicional's *-ia* from *habēbat*. Every new atom is
practised at least twice later; no forward reference, idiom, sense or culture
claim is added. `tests/corpus/portuguese.test.ts` pins the lesson count at 1336
and the second connected composition.
