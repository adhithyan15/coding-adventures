## You'll want to know: переговоры
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C363-PEREGOVORY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**переговоры** (*peregovóry*) — "negotiations, talks".

> Переговоры шли всю ночь.
>
> *Peregovóry shli vsyu noch.*
>
> The talks went on all night.

Plural only. *Вести переговоры* — to hold talks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**переговоры** is built from *пере-* "across" and *говор* "speech", the root of *говорить*: talk that goes back and forth across a table.
