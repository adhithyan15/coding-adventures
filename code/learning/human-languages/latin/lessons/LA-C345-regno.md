## You'll want to know: rēgnō, rēgnāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C345-REX-02]; assesses=[] -->

**rēgnō, rēgnāre** — "to reign, to be king".

> Senex rēx multōs annōs rēgnāvit.
>
> *The old king reigned for many years.*

*rēgnō, rēgnāre, rēgnāvī, rēgnātum*, from *rēgnum* (taught). English *reign*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**rēgnō** is built on *rēgnum*, "kingship, kingdom" (EtymDB). English *reign* comes from its family through French.
