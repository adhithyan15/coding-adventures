## Script — a bar, a turning hook, a long fall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WAN-MYRIAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 万

**Three strokes, top to bottom.**

**A long bar** across the top.

**A turning hook:** from under the middle of the bar, a stroke that runs right, turns down and hooks to the left, as in 为 and 月.

**A long stroke falling to the left,** from the bar down to the lower left.

One, two, three.
