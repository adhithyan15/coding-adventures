## You'll want to know: a vaidade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C467-ORGULHO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a vaidade** — "vanity".

> Ela passa horas ao espelho por pura vaidade.
>
> *She spends hours at the mirror out of pure vanity.*

*Por vaidade* — out of vanity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vaidade* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *vãidade*, from Latin *vānitās* "emptiness" (EtymDB), built on *vānus* "empty".
