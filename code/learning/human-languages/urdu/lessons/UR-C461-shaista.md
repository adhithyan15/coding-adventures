## You'll want to know: شائستہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C461-MANNERS461-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شائستہ** — *shāistā* — "refined; courteous".

> وہ بڑی شائستہ لڑکی ہے۔
>
> *voh baṛī shāistā laṛkī hai.*
>
> She is a very courteous girl.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شائستہ** (*shāistā*) is usually traced to Persian *shāyista*, "fitting, worthy".
