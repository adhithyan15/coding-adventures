## You'll want to know: il romanziere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C524-ROMANZO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il romanziere** — "novelist".

> Il romanziere presenta il suo nuovo libro in libreria.
>
> *The novelist is presenting his new book in the bookshop.*

A woman is *la romanziera*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*romanziere* is built on *romanzo* "novel", from Old French *romanz* "a story told in the common tongue", from Vulgar Latin *rōmānicē* "in the Roman manner" (EtymDB).
