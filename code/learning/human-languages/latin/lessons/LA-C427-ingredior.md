## You'll want to know: ingredior, ingredī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C427-MANSIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ingredior, ingredī** — "to set out on, to enter upon; to go in".

> Prīmā lūce viātōrēs iter longum ingrediuntur.
>
> *At first light the travellers set out on the long journey.*

Deponent: *ingredior, ingredī, ingressus sum*, with an accusative: *iter ingredī*, to begin a journey. English *ingredient* comes from its participle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingredior* is built from *in-* 'in, on' and *gradior* 'to step' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *ingredient* comes from its present participle *ingrediēns*.
