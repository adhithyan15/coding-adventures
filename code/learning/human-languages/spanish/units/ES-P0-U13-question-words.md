# Unit 13: Question Words

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Say *¿cómo estás?* from Chapter 1. *cómo* is one of seven core
question words — today gets the rest.

## New Material

**qué** — what ← Latin *quid*.
**dónde** — where ← Latin *de unde* ("from where").
**cómo** — how ← Latin *quomodo* (already met).
**cuándo** — when ← Latin *quando*.
**por qué** — why (literally "for what") — two words, unlike *porque*
("because"), one word.
**quién** — who ← Latin *quis/quem*.
**cuánto** — how much/many ← Latin *quantum* (→ English *quantum*,
*quantity*).

## Grammar Lens

Every one of these carries a **written accent** when used as a question
word — *qué* vs. plain *que* ("that"), *cómo* vs. *como* ("like/as"),
*dónde* vs. *donde* ("where," as a relative pronoun, not a question).
Spanish uses the accent mark to visibly distinguish "asking" from
"describing" — *"Sé que vienes"* ("I know that you're coming") vs. *"¿Qué
sabes?"* ("What do you know?"). English has no equivalent marker: "that"
and "what" look the same whether they're part of a question or not; only
context and word order disambiguate. Spanish put a visible flag on it.
