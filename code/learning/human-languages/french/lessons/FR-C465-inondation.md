## You'll want to know: l'inondation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C465-TEMPETE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'inondation** — "the flood".

> Après l'inondation, la cave était pleine d'eau.
>
> *After the flood the cellar was full of water.*

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inondation* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inundātiō* (EtymDB), from *inundāre* 'to overflow', built on *unda* 'wave'.
