# अमलात आणणे (amlāt āṇṇe) — to implement; to put into effect

## You'll want to know: अमलात आणणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C388-UDDISHTA388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अमलात आणणे** — *amlāt āṇṇe* — "to implement; to put into effect".

> समितीने ठरलेली योजना लगेच अमलात आणली.
>
> *samitīne ṭharlelī yojnā lagec amlāt āṇlī.*
>
> The committee put the agreed plan into effect at once.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अमलात आणलं, *tyāne amlāt āṇlaṁ*.

अमलात येणे, to come into effect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अमलात आणणे** (*amlāt āṇṇe*) joins अमलात, "into effect", from अमल, a loan from Persian usually traced to Arabic *ʿamal*, "action", to आणणे, "to bring".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
