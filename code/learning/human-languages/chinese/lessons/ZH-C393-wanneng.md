## You'll want to know: 万能
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C393-BESURE393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**万能** — *wànnéng* — "able to do everything; all-purpose".

> *méiyǒu wànnéng de bànfǎ* — There's no way that works for everything.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**万能** (*wànnéng*) is built from 万 'ten thousand' and 能 'able', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjonH* and *nong* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'skilled': able in ten thousand things.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
