## You'll want to know: كنة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C529-AHL529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**كنة** — *kanna* — "a daughter-in-law".

> تحب جدتي كنتها كثيرا.
>
> *tuḥibbu jaddatī kannatahā kathīran.*
>
> My grandmother loves her daughter-in-law very much.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كنة** (*kanna*) is an old native Arabic word for a son's or a brother's wife, usually traced to the root *k-n-n*, "to cover, to shelter"; how the sense grew is not settled.
