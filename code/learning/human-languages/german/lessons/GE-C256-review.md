# Review — the way there

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WIE-KOMME-ICH-01, GE-GRAMMAR-ZUM-ZUR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the way to the pharmacy. (*Wie komme ich zur Apotheke?*)
*Die Apotheke*, so *zur*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-LINKS-01, GE-GRAMMAR-SIE-IMPERATIV-02, GE-LEX-C108-RICHTUNG108-05, GE-LEX-C95-WO-05] -->

Two lessons, and a whole exchange in the street. You ask with *wie komme
ich*, and the gender of the place picks *zum* or *zur*. The answer comes
back as a *Sie* command with the verb first, and three short words do much of
the work: *geradeaus*, *rechts*, *links*.

[PAUSE 2s] One exchange, both halves:

> *Entschuldigung, wie komme ich zum Museum?*
> *Gehen Sie geradeaus bis zur Kreuzung, dann rechts. Das Museum ist gleich
> gegenüber.*
