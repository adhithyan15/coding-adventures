# اعتنى (iʿtanā) — to look after

## You'll want to know: اعتنى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C533-RIAYA533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتنى** — *iʿtanā* — "to look after".

> تعتني أختي بجدتي المريضة.
>
> *taʿtanī ukhtī bi-jaddatī al-marīḍa.*
>
> My sister looks after my sick grandmother.

Present: يعتني, *yaʿtanī*.

It takes بـ for the person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتنى** (*iʿtanā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-n-y*, "to concern, to occupy" (Lane). ʿĀnā, to suffer, is from the same root; Form VIII iʿtanā is to make a person your concern.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
