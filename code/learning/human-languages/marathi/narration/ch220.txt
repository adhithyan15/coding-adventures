Lesson 2 of 5.
पापणी (pāpṇī) — an eyelid; eyelashes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a customer, then the Marathi for a heel.

You'll want to know: पापणी.
पापणी — pāpṇī — "an eyelid; eyelashes".

The word, taken apart.
पापणी (pāpṇī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pakṣman, "eyelashes" (CDIAL 7638). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "eyelid".

You'll want to know: भुवई.
भुवई — bhuvaī — "an eyebrow".

The word, taken apart.
भुवई (bhuvaī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhrū, "eyebrow" (CDIAL 9688). CDIAL lists the Marathi forms as bhãvaī and bhõvaī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अश्रू (aśrū) — a tear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an eyebrow, then the Marathi for sweat.

You'll want to know: अश्रू.
अश्रू — aśrū — "a tear".

The word, taken apart.
अश्रू (aśrū) is Sanskrit aśru, "tear", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 919). CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as āsū.
