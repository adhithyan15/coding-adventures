Marathi, chapter 313: Rumour and Sensation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अफवा (aphvā) — a rumour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a newsreader, then the Marathi for to broadcast.

You'll want to know: अफवा.
अफवा — aphvā — "a rumour".
गावात भूकंपाची खोटी अफवा पसरली. gāvāt bhūkampācī khoṭī aphvā pasarlī. A false rumour of an earthquake spread through the village.
Feminine.
अफवा (aphvā) पसरणे, a rumour spreads; plural अफवा (aphvā).

The word, taken apart.
अफवा (aphvā) is a loan from Persian afvāh, usually traced to Arabic afwāh, 'mouths': talk that goes from mouth to mouth.

Lesson 2 of 5.
खळबळ (khaḷbaḷ) — a sensation; a stir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to broadcast, then the Marathi for a rumour.

You'll want to know: खळबळ.
खळबळ — khaḷbaḷ — "a sensation; a stir".
त्या बातमीने शहरात खळबळ उडाली. tyā bātmīne śahrāt khaḷbaḷ uḍālī. That news caused a sensation in the city.
Feminine.
खळबळ (khaḷbaḷ) उडणे, a stir breaks out; खळबळजनक, sensational.

The word, taken apart.
खळबळ (khaḷbaḷ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi khaḷbaḷṇe, 'to agitate', under Sanskrit khalati, 'shakes' (CDIAL 3837); the noun names the stir.

घोषणा — ghoṣṇā — "an announcement; a slogan".
स्टेशनवर गाडी उशिरा येण्याची घोषणा झाली. sṭeśanvar gāḍī uśirā yeṇyācī ghoṣṇā jhālī. At the station it was announced that the train would be late.
Feminine.
घोषणा (ghoṣṇā) करणे, to announce.

The word, taken apart.
घोषणा (ghoṣṇā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit ghoṣaṇā, 'a proclaiming aloud' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ठळक (ṭhaḷak) — prominent; bold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sensation, then the Marathi for an announcement.

You'll want to know: ठळक.
ठळक — ṭhaḷak — "prominent; bold".
निवेदकाने आजच्या ठळक बातम्या वाचल्या. nivedakāne ājacyā ṭhaḷak bātamyā vācalyā. The newsreader read out today's main headlines.
It keeps one form: ठळक (ṭhaḷak) अक्षरं, bold letters; ठळक (ṭhaḷak) बातम्या, the headlines.

The word, taken apart.
ठळक (ṭhaḷak) is a Marathi word for what stands out clearly; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पुकारणे (pukārṇe) — to proclaim; to call a strike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an announcement, then the Marathi for prominent.

You'll want to know: पुकारणे.
पुकारणे — pukārṇe — "to proclaim; to call a strike".
कामगारांनी उद्यापासून संप पुकारला. kāmgārānnī udyāpāsūn samp pukārlā. The workers called a strike from tomorrow.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पुकारलं, tyāne pukārlaṁ.
संप पुकारणे (pukārṇe), to call a strike; it also means to call out to someone.

The word, taken apart.
पुकारणे (pukārṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi pukārṇe, 'to shout', under pukkār, 'shout', a word made from the sound (CDIAL 8246).
