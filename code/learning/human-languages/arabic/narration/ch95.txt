Arabic, chapter 95: Pocket, Ring, Hat, Shoe, Coat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جيب (jayb) — a pocket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for trousers, then the Arabic for a sock.

You'll want to know: جيب.
جيب — jayb — "a pocket".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jayb]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jayb, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say jayb]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for trousers, then the Arabic for a sock, then say jayb again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does جيب (jayb) mean? ("A pocket".) Say it once more.
