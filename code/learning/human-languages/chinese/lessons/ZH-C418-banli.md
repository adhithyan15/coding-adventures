## You'll want to know: bànlǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C418-CHECKIN418-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bànlǐ* — "to deal with the paperwork; to go through a procedure".

> *wǒmen qù nà jiā bīnguǎn bànlǐ rùzhù* — We're going to that hotel to check in.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bànlǐ* is built from *bàn* 'to see to' and *lǐ* 'to put in order', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *beanH* and *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to prepare' and the second 'to manage'.
