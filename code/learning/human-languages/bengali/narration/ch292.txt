Bengali, chapter 292: Drought and Hunger.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
খরা (khôrā) — a drought.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to hand out, then the Bengali for to donate.

You'll want to know: খরা.
খরা — khôrā — "a drought".
এই বছর খরার জন্য ধান হল না। ei bôchhôr khôrār jônyô dhān hôlô nā. This year the rice failed because of the drought.

The word, taken apart.
খরা (khôrā) is filed in CDIAL 3819 under Sanskrit khara, 'hard, sharp', also 'hot' of a wind, which lists Bengali kharā, 'hot and dry', and as a verb 'to parch'. The noun 'a drought', the parching season, is the Bengali use.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ফাটা (phāṭā) — to crack; to burst.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a famine, then the Bengali for starvation.

You'll want to know: ফাটা.
ফাটা — phāṭā — "to crack; to burst".
খরায় জমির মাটি ফেটে গেল। khôrāy jômir māṭi pheṭe gelô. In the drought the soil of the fields cracked.
Past (he/she …): সেই কলসি ফাটল, sei kolsi phāṭlô.

The word, taken apart.
ফাটা (phāṭā) comes down from Sanskrit sphāṭayati, 'causes to split' (CDIAL 13825, which lists Bengali phāṭā, 'to be split'). The -এ form is ফেটে: ফেটে গেল, it cracked.

ঝলসে যাওয়া (jhôlse jāoyā) — to be scorched; to wither in the heat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for starvation, then the Bengali for to crack.

You'll want to know: ঝলসে যাওয়া.
ঝলসে যাওয়া — jhôlse jāoyā — "to be scorched; to wither in the heat".
কড়া রোদে গাছের পাতা ঝলসে গেল। kôṛā rode gāchher pātā jhôlse gelô. The leaves of the trees were scorched in the fierce sun.
Past (he/she …): সেই গাছ ঝলসে গেল, sei gāchh jhôlse gelô.

The word, taken apart.
ঝলসে যাওয়া (jhôlse jāoyā) is the -e form of ঝলসানো, to scorch — CDIAL 5352 lists Bengali jhalsā, 'scorched', under a Middle Indo-Aryan jhal-, 'flash' — before যাওয়া.
