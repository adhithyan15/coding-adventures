Marathi, chapter 401: Fuel and Tyres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
इंधन (indhan) — fuel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a truck, then the Marathi for to stuff in.

You'll want to know: इंधन.
इंधन — indhan — "fuel".
गाडीतलं इंधन कमी झालं आहे. gāḍītlaṁ indhan kamī jhālaṁ āhe. The fuel in the car has run low.
Neuter.
Any fuel: wood, gas or petrol; इंधन (indhan) भरणे, to fill up.

The word, taken apart.
इंधन (indhan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit indhana, "kindling; fuel" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
पेट्रोल (peṭrol) — petrol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to stuff in, then the Marathi for fuel.

You'll want to know: पेट्रोल.
पेट्रोल — peṭrol — "petrol".
पुढच्या गावात पेट्रोल मिळेल. puḍhacyā gāvāt peṭrol miḷel. We will get petrol in the next village.
Neuter.
पेट्रोल (peṭrol) भरणे, to fill up with petrol; मिळणे takes the dative: मला पेट्रोल (peṭrol) मिळालं, I got petrol.

The word, taken apart.
पेट्रोल (peṭrol) is a loan from English petrol.

Lesson 5 of 5.
संपणे (sampṇe) — to run out; to come to an end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tyre, then the Marathi for a puncture.

You'll want to know: संपणे.
संपणे — sampṇe — "to run out; to come to an end".
रस्त्यात पेट्रोल संपलं. rastyāt peṭrol samplaṁ. The petrol ran out on the way.
Past (he …): तो संपला, to samplā.
What runs out is the subject: पैसे संपले, the money ran out; सुट्टी संपली, the holiday ended.

The word, taken apart.
संपणे (sampṇe) is a common Marathi verb, "to be finished"; its origin is not settled.
