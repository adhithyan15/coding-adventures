## You'll want to know: مصرف کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C388-ALUDEGI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مصرف کردن** (*masraf kardan*) — "to consume, to use up".

> این ماشین بنزین زیادی مصرف میکند.
>
> *in mâshin benzin-e ziyâdi masraf mikonad.*
>
> This car uses a lot of petrol.

*Masraf* + *kardan*. For food, fuel, medicine and electricity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصرف کردن** joins *masraf*, from Arabic *maṣraf* (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do".
