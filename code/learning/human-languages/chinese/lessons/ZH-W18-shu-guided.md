# 书 — copy the compact route

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHU-01, ZH-ORTHO-KANJIAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Read **看见** and give its meaning. Then air-write **fold, hooked
fold, upright, dot**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHU-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **书** twice beside the model. On the second copy, pause before the upright
and check that both folds leave a clear central route.

## Guided Practice — Spaced Return
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-ORTHO-KANJIAN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Hear **see** and write 看见 once without a model.
