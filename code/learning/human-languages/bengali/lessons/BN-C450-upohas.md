## You'll want to know: উপহাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C450-OPOMAN450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**উপহাস** — *upôhās* — "mockery; derision".

> মীরার হাসিতে উপহাস ছিল।
>
> *mīrār hāsite upôhās chhilô.*
>
> There was mockery in Mira's laugh.

উপহাস করা, to mock, to make fun of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপহাস** (*upôhās*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *upahāsa*, "sport, laughter" (CDIAL 2282), on the root *has*, to laugh, the হাসা (taught) of Bengali; Bengali uses it for laughter aimed at someone.
