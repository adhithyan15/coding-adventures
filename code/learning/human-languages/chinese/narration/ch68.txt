Mandarin Chinese, chapter 68: Out of Town.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
山 (shān) — mountain; hill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for bird, then the Mandarin for fish.

You'll want to know: 山.
山 — shān — "mountain; hill".
我喜欢山 wǒ xǐhuan shān — I love the mountains.
The new character, in pieces: 山 (shān) — three peaks standing on one base. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
山 (shān) is inherited from Middle Chinese srean (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an old book saying the mountain brings forth all things.

What you've built.
One more word for everyday answers.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: shān]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shān, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wǒ xǐhuan shān — I love the mountains.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for bird, then the Mandarin for fish, then say shān again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does 山 (shān) mean? ("Mountain; hill".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 6.
山 (shān) — three peaks on a base.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — three peaks on a base and Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — suì, zhōng]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — three peaks on a base.
山 (shān).
One piece, three strokes, the middle first.
The tall vertical in the middle.
A vertical down the left that turns and runs right along the bottom.
A vertical down the right, standing on the base.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 山 (shān), all three, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 山 (shān)? (Three.) Which stroke comes first? (The middle one.) Which character you know has it on top? (岁.)

The word, taken apart.
星星 (xīngxing) is built from 星 'star' said twice, the second time light. 星 is inherited from Middle Chinese seng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 6 of 6.
tàiyang — sun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: tàiyang.
tàiyang — "sun".
jīntiān de tàiyang hěn dà — The sun is strong today.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tàiyang is built from tài 'great' and yang, both inherited from Middle Chinese, thajH and yang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second as the yang of yin and yang, 'high and bright': the great bright one.
