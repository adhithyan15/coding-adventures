## You'll want to know: निमित्तम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C340-KARANA340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निमित्तम्** — *nimittam* — "an occasion; a cause".

> विवाहः मम आगमनस्य निमित्तम् अस्ति।
>
> *vivāhaḥ mama āgamanasya nimittam asti.*
>
> A wedding is the occasion of my visit.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निमित्तम्** (*nimittam*) MW gives *nimitta* the senses "a mark", "a sign" and "a cause, a motive, a ground", and says it is possibly connected with *ni* and the root √mā, "to measure"; so its origin is uncertain (MW).
