Urdu, chapter 359: Even So.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
باوجود (bāvujūd) — despite (کے باوجود).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a majority, then the Urdu for to spurn.

You'll want to know: باوجود.
باوجود — bāvujūd — "despite (کے باوجود)".
بارش کے باوجود وہ دفتر گیا۔ bārish ke bāvujūd voh daftar gayā. He went to the office despite the rain.

The word, taken apart.
باوجود (bāvujūd) is usually traced to Persian bāvujūd, "with the existence of": bā, "with", and vujūd, "existence", a word Persian took from Arabic.

البتہ (albattā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic al-battata, "decidedly, by all means"; in Urdu it brings in a reservation.
