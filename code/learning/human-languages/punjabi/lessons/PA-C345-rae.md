## You'll want to know: ਰਾਏ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C345-DECIDE345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਏ** — *rāe* — "an opinion; a view".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਰਾਏ ਮੰਨਦੇ ਹੋ?
>
> *kī tusī̃ merī rāe mannde ho?*
>
> Do you go along with my view?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਏ** (*rāe*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *raʾy*, "opinion".
