## You'll want to know: ਕਿੱਲੋ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C273-WEIGH273-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿੱਲੋ** — *killo* — "a kilo".

> ਮੈਂ ਦੋ ਕਿੱਲੋ ਸੇਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ do killo seb kharīdāṅgā.*
>
> I will buy two kilos of apples.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿੱਲੋ** (*killo*) is borrowed from English *kilo*.
