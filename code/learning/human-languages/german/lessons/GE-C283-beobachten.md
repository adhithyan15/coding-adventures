## You'll want to know: beobachten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C283-ZEUGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**beobachten** — "to observe, to watch".

> Sie beobachtete den Mann vom Fenster aus.
>
> *She watched the man from the window.*

Regular: *beobachtete*, *hat beobachtet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beobachten* is usually traced to a Low German verb built on *achten* "to pay heed", from Old High German *ahtōn* (EtymDB): to keep an eye on.
