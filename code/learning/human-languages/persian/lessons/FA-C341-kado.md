## You'll want to know: کادو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C341-MEHMANI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کادو** (*kâdo*) — "a present (everyday word)".

> برای دوستم یک کادوی کوچک خریدم.
>
> *Barâ-ye dustam yek kâdo-ye kuchak kharidam.*
>
> I bought a small present for my friend.

Everyday; *hediye* is more formal. *Kâdo kardan* is "to gift-wrap".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kâdo* is borrowed from French *cadeau*, "gift" (EtymDB).

## What you've built
