## You'll want to know: le violoncelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C511-INSTRUMENTS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le violoncelle** — "cello".

> Ma sœur joue du violoncelle depuis l'âge de huit ans.
>
> *My sister has played the cello since she was eight.*

*Jouer du violoncelle*. The player is *le* or *la violoncelliste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le violoncelle* was borrowed from Italian *violoncello*, a small form of *violone* "a large viol", itself built on *viola*.
