## You'll want to know: फाटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C431-BIGHAD431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फाटणे** — *phāṭṇe* — "to rip; to tear".

> गर्दीत माझी पिशवी फाटली.
>
> *gardīt mājhī piśvī phāṭlī.*
>
> My bag ripped in the crowd.

Past (he …): तो फाटला, *to phāṭlā*.

Cloth and paper फाटतात; to tear something is फाडणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फाटणे** (*phāṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *phāṭṇẽ*, "to be split", under Sanskrit *sphāṭayati*, "splits" (CDIAL 13825).
