# چ (che) — the letter inside سوچنا

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-BARI-HE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-KHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ح and خ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **سوچنا** (*sochnā*), "to think".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: چ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-CHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چ** — *che*, the sound *ch*.

It is inside **سوچنا** (*sochnā*), "to think". The same skeleton once more, with
**three dots below**: one letter, four sounds, and the dots decide which.

## Writing: چ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-CHE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: sweep left through the pointed hooked head
- **2.** continue down and around the bowl without lifting
- **3.** after one lift, place the lower-left dot
- **4.** after another lift, place the lower-right dot
- **5.** after a third lift, place the lower-center dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 3.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Te, mīm, jīm, che, and more diacritics, independent چ calligraphic and handwriting animations and Che instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C35-BARI-HE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-KHE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C35-CHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **سوچنا** and point at چ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: چ three times, saying *ch* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: خ, then چ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **سوچنا** aloud once more]
