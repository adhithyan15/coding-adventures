Italian, chapter 60: Come Back, Study, Travel, Pay, Use.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
tornare — to come back, to return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a knife, then the Italian for a fork.

You'll want to know: tornare.
tornare — "to come back, to return".

What you've built.
