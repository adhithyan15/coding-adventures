## You'll want to know: কুচোনো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C513-KOTA513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**কুচোনো** — *kuchono* — "to chop finely; to dice".

> আমি তরকারির জন্য পেঁয়াজ কুচোলাম।
>
> *Āmi tôrkārir jônyô pẽyāj kucholām.*
>
> I chopped the onions finely for the curry.

Past (he/she …): তিনি আদা কুচোলেন, *tini ādā kucholen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কুচোনো** (*kuchono*) comes from a reconstructed *kucyate*, "contracts, shrinks"; CDIAL lists Bengali *kucāna*, "to shrivel, to cut into bits" (CDIAL 3221).
