## You'll want to know: फिरकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C414-ATITHI414-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फिरकणे** — *phirakṇe* — "to drop by".

> सुट्टीत तो आमच्याकडे फिरकलाच नाही.
>
> *suṭṭīt to āmcyākaḍe phiraklāc nāhī.*
>
> In the holidays he did not even drop by our place.

Past (he …): तो फिरकला, *to phiraklā*.

Mostly with a negative: इकडे फिरकला नाही, he never came this way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फिरकणे** (*phirakṇe*) is a Marathi verb that looks built on *phir-*, "to turn" (CDIAL 9078); how it came to mean dropping in is not settled.
