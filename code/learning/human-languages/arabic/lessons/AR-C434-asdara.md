## You'll want to know: أصدر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C434-RUKHSA434-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أصدر** — *aṣdara* — "to issue".

> أصدرت السفارة التأشيرة بعد أسبوع.
>
> *aṣdarat as-sifāra at-taʾshīra baʿda usbūʿ.*
>
> The embassy issued the visa after a week.

Present: يصدر, *yuṣdir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أصدر** (*aṣdara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-d-r*, "to come forth" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to send forth".
