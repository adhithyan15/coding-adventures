## You'll want to know: সহিষ্ণুতা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C350-ADORSHO350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সহিষ্ণুতা** — *sôhishnutā* — "tolerance".

> সমাজে সহিষ্ণুতা দরকার।
>
> *sômāje sôhishnutā dôrkār.*
>
> Society needs tolerance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সহিষ্ণুতা** (*sôhishnutā*) is Sanskrit *sahiṣṇutā*, the abstract of *sahiṣṇu*, patient and forbearing (Monier-Williams): the patience to live with views you do not share.
