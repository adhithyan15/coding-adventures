Bengali, chapter 186: Rib, Navel, Palm, Brain, Muscle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পাঁজর (pā̃jôr) — a rib.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a blister, then the Bengali for a bandage.

You'll want to know: পাঁজর.
পাঁজর — pā̃jôr — "a rib".

The word, taken apart.
পাঁজর (pā̃jôr) (pāṁjôr) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pañjara, "cage; skeleton, ribs" (CDIAL 7685). CDIAL lists Bengali pāṁjar, "the ribs, a rib".

Lesson 3 of 5.
তালু (tālu) — a palm; the palate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a rib, then the Bengali for a navel.

You'll want to know: তালু.
তালু — tālu — "a palm; the palate".

The word, taken apart.
তালু (tālu) is inherited from Sanskrit tālu, "palate", and has come down with almost no change (CDIAL 5803). CDIAL lists Bengali tālu as "palate, top of the head"; it does not list the sense "palm".
