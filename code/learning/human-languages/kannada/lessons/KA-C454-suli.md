# ಸುಳಿ (suḷi) — a whirlpool; an eddy

## You'll want to know: ಸುಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C454-TEPPA454-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಳಿ** — *suḷi* — "a whirlpool; an eddy".

> ನದಿಯ ಮಧ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಪಾಯಕಾರಿ ಸುಳಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *nadiya madhyadalli apāyakāri suḷi ide.*
>
> There is a dangerous whirlpool in the middle of the river.

ಸುಳಿಗಾಳಿ, a whirlwind; ಸುಳಿ also names the whorl of hair on the head.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಳಿ** (*suḷi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2698 lists older Kannada *suḻi*, "to turn round; a whirl, a whirlpool, an eddy", beside Tamil *cuḻi*.
