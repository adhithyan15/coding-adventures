Arabic, chapter 492: Envy and jealousy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غيرة (ghīra) — jealousy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for grateful, then the Arabic for to impress.

You'll want to know: غيرة.
غيرة — ghīra — "jealousy".
شعر الطفل بالغيرة من أخيه الصغير. shaʿara aṭ-ṭifl bi-l-ghīra min akhīhi aṣ-ṣaghīr. The child felt jealous of his little brother.

The word, taken apart.
غيرة (ghīra) is built on the native Arabic root gh-y-r, "jealousy, a jealous care for one's own" (Lane). Ghīra is jealousy, often a protective one.

Lesson 5 of 5.
غار (ghāra) — to be jealous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for envious, then the Arabic for to envy.

You'll want to know: غار.
غار — ghāra — "to be jealous".
تغار أختي عندما يلعب أبي مع أخي. taghāru ukhtī ʿindamā yalʿabu abī maʿa akhī. My sister gets jealous when my father plays with my brother.
Present: يغار, yaghār.
It takes من or على: غار (ghāra) منه. The same letters also spell غار (ghāra), ghār, a cave, from another root.

The word, taken apart.
غار (ghāra) is built on the native Arabic root gh-y-r, "jealousy, a jealous care for one's own" (Lane). It is the verb that goes with ghīra and ghayūr.
