Marathi, chapter 326: Emperors and Princesses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सम्राट (samrāṭ) — an emperor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a royal court, then the Marathi for to reign.

You'll want to know: सम्राट.
सम्राट — samrāṭ — "an emperor".
तो सम्राट फार शूर आणि दयाळू होता. to samrāṭ phār śūr āṇi dayāḷū hotā. That emperor was very brave and kind.
Masculine.
साम्राज्य, an empire, is built on the same word.

The word, taken apart.
सम्राट (samrāṭ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit samrāj, 'a supreme ruler', in its nominative form samrāṭ (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to reign, then the Marathi for an emperor.

You'll want to know: सिंहासन.
सिंहासन — siṁhāsan — "a throne".
नवा राजा सिंहासनावर बसला. navā rājā siṁhāsanāvar baslā. The new king sat on the throne.
Neuter.
सिंहासनावर बसणे, to take the throne.

The word, taken apart.
सिंहासन (siṁhāsan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit siṁhāsana, 'a lion-seat, a throne' (Monier-Williams): siṁha, 'a lion', and āsana, 'a seat'.
