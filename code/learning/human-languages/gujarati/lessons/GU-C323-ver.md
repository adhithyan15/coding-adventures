## You'll want to know: વેર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C323-SHAHID323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વેર** — *ver* — "enmity; a feud".

> બંને રાજાઓ વચ્ચે જૂનું વેર હતું.
>
> *banne rājāo vacce jūnũ ver hatũ.*
>
> There was an old feud between the two kings.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વેર** (*ver*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vaíra*, "enmity", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "enmity, revenge" (CDIAL 12142). It is neuter: **જૂનું વેર**, an old feud.
