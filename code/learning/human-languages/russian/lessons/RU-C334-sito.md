## You'll want to know: сито
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C334-FARSH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сито** (*síto*) — "a sieve".

> Бабушка просеяла муку через сито.
>
> *Bábushka proséyala múku chérez síto.*
>
> Grandma sifted the flour through a sieve.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сито* is inherited through Old East Slavic *sito* from Proto-Slavic *sito* "sieve" (EtymDB).
