# ಸ್ನೇಹ (snēha) — friendship

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C527-POSHANE527-04, KA-LEX-C527-POSHANE527-05] -->

## You'll want to know: ಸ್ನೇಹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C528-SNEHA528-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ನೇಹ** — *snēha* — "friendship".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಸ್ನೇಹ ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಶುರುವಾಯಿತು.
>
> *namma snēha śāleyalli śuruvāyitu.*
>
> Our friendship began at school.

ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ, a friend, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ನೇಹ** (*snēha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sneha*, "oiliness, smoothness", and from there "tenderness, friendship" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
