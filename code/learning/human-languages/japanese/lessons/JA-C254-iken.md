# いけん (iken) — an opinion

## You'll want to know: いけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C254-IKEN-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-03, JA-LEX-CHICHI] -->

**いけん** — *iken* — "an opinion", what someone thinks.

> **いけんは ありますか。**
>
> *iken wa arimasu ka.* — "Do you have an opinion?"

> **ちちの いけんを ききました。**
>
> *chichi no iken o kikimashita.* — "I asked my father's opinion."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いけん** (*iken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *i*, "idea" (Middle Chinese *'iH*) and *ken*, "see" (*kenH*), which KANJIDIC2 also glosses "opinion" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict glosses the word "opinion; view; comment" (JMdict).
