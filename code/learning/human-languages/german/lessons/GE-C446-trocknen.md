## You'll want to know: trocknen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C446-REINIGUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**trocknen** — "to dry".

> Im Sommer trocknet die Wäsche im Garten.
>
> *In summer the washing dries in the garden.*

Perfekt with *sein* when something dries by itself: *die Wäsche ist getrocknet*; with *haben* when someone dries it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trocknen* is built on *trocken* 'dry', inherited from Middle High German *trucken* (EtymDB).
