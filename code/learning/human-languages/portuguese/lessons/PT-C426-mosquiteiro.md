## You'll want to know: o mosquiteiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C426-TENDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o mosquiteiro** — "the mosquito net".

> Sem mosquiteiro, ninguém dorme no verão.
>
> *Without a mosquito net, nobody sleeps in summer.*

Built on *o mosquito*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mosquiteiro* is built on *mosquito*, a small form of *mosca* 'fly', which is inherited from Latin *musca* (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
