## Chapters 139-140: the future and reading practical texts

Sanskrit had realized three of its five A2 spine nodes. These two chapters
realize the last two. Sanskrit's A2 gate now reports no missing spine node;
vocabulary and verb vocabulary remain.

- **Chapter 139 — the future** (`SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE`): **भविष्यत्**
  (the future), **श्वः** (tomorrow), **आगामी** (coming, next), **भविष्यति**
  (will be) and **गमिष्यति** (will go). Sanskrit puts the future inside the
  verb, with **-ष्य-** before the ending: पठति → पठिष्यति. The time word stands
  first (**श्वः सः पठिष्यति।**). **आगामी** takes the ending of the time word it
  stands before (**आगामिनि सप्ताहे**, next week).
- **Chapter 140 — reading practical texts** (`SPINE-READ-PRACTICAL-TEXTS`):
  **सूचना** (a notice), **समयसारणी** (a timetable), **विज्ञापनम्** (an advert),
  **देयकम्** (a bill) and **प्राप्तिपत्रम्** (a receipt). Each lesson reads a
  short text built only from words the book already teaches, then says what to
  do about it. For example, **अद्य विश्रामः।** on a shop door means come back
  tomorrow.
- Two reviews close chapter 140.
