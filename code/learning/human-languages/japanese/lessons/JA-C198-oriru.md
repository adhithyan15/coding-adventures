# おりる (oriru) — to get off; to go down

## You'll want to know: おりる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C198-RIDE198-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おりる** — *oriru* — "to get off; to go down".

> **ここで でんしゃを おります。** — *koko de densha o orimasu* — "I get off the train here."

Polite **おります**, *orimasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おりる** (*oriru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
