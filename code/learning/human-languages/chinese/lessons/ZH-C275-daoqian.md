## You'll want to know: dàoqiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C275-SORRY275-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dàoqiàn* — "to apologise".

> *tā dàoqiàn le* — He apologised.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàoqiàn* is built from *dào* and *qiàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dawX* and *kheamH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a road' and 'a principle'. It gives the second other senses; how the pair came to mean an apology is not settled.

## What you've built
