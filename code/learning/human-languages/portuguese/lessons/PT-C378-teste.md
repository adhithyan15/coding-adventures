## You'll want to know: o teste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C378-REVER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o teste** — "the test".

> Amanhã temos um teste de português.
>
> *Tomorrow we have a Portuguese test.*

At school, a written test during the term; *o exame* is bigger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*teste* was borrowed from English *test* (EtymDB), which goes back through Old French *test* "pot for melting metal" to Latin *testum* "an earthen vessel" (Lewis & Short): metals were tested in such pots.
