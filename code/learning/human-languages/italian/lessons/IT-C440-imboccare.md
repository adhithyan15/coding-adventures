## You'll want to know: imboccare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C440-INDICAZIONI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**imboccare** — "to take (a road), to turn into".

> Imbocchiamo la prima strada a sinistra.
>
> *We take the first road on the left.*

Literally to put into the mouth: it also means to spoon-feed a child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imboccare* is built from *in-* and *bocca* 'mouth', which is inherited from Latin *bucca* 'cheek' (EtymDB): going into the mouth of a street.
