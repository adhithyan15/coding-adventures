# చుట్టం (cuṭṭaṁ) — a kinsman; a relation

## You'll want to know: చుట్టం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C530-BANDHUVU530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**చుట్టం** — *cuṭṭaṁ* — "a kinsman; a relation".

> పెళ్ళికి చుట్టాలంతా వచ్చారు.
>
> *peḷḷiki cuṭṭālantā vaccāru.*
>
> All the relations came to the wedding.

Plural: చుట్టాలు, *cuṭṭālu*.

Plural చుట్టాలు; చుట్టరికం, kinship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చుట్టం** (*cuṭṭaṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2715 lists Telugu *cuṭṭamu*, "relation, kinsman", beside *cuṭṭu*, "to go round, to encircle": the people around you.
