## You'll want to know: xuéfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C304-SCHOOL304-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xuéfèi* — "tuition fees".

> *dàxué de xuéfèi hěn guì* — University fees are high.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuéfèi* is built from 学 'to study' and *fèi* 'to spend', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haewk* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to use up'.
