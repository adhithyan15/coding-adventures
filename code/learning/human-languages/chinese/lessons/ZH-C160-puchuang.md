## You'll want to know: pūchuáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C160-CHORE160-03]; assesses=[] -->

*pūchuáng* — "to make the bed".

> *qǐchuáng yǐhòu bié wàng le pūchuáng* — Don't forget to make your bed when you get up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pūchuáng* is built from *pū* 'to spread out' and *chuáng* 'bed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *phu* and *dzrjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to spread the bedding out.
