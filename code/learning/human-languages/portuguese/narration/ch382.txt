Portuguese, chapter 382: Pode-se ou não.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
proibido — forbidden, not allowed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for optional, then the Portuguese for the obligation.

You'll want to know: proibido.
proibido — "forbidden, not allowed".
É proibido fumar no restaurante. Smoking is not allowed in the restaurant.
The participle of proibir. Entrada proibida — no entry.

The word, taken apart.
proibido is the participle of proibir, from Latin prohibēre "to hold back, to forbid", built on habēre "to have, to hold" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 6.
violar — to break, to violate (a rule, a law).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to tolerate, then the Portuguese for to authorise.

You'll want to know: violar.
violar — "to break, to violate (a rule, a law)".
O motorista violou as regras de trânsito. The driver broke the traffic rules.
Also, in a grave sense, to rape; for rules many speakers prefer infringir or desrespeitar.

The word, taken apart.
violar comes from Latin violāre "to treat with violence, to violate" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese violar; it is built on vīs "strength, force".

Lesson 5 of 6.
a violação — the violation, the breach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to authorise, then the Portuguese for to break.

You'll want to know: a violação.
a violação — "the violation, the breach".
Isso é uma violação da lei. That is a breach of the law.
In a grave sense also rape; violação de direitos — breach of rights.

The word, taken apart.
violação is a learned borrowing from Latin violātiō "injury, profanation" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from violāre "to treat with violence", on vīs "force".
