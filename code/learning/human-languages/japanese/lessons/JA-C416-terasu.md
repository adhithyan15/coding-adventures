## You'll want to know: てらす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C416-YOAKE416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**てらす** — *terasu* — "to shine on; to light up".

> **つきが みちを てらしています。** — *tsuki ga michi o terashite imasu* — "The moon is lighting up the road."

Polite **てらします**, *terashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あさひが やまを てらしました。**, *asahi ga yama o terashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てらす** (*terasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
