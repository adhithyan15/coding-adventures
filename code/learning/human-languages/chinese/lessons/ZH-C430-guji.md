## You'll want to know: gǔjì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C430-SIGHTS430-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔjì* — "historic site".

> *wǒmen zuò gōngjiāo qù kàn gǔjì* — We're taking the bus to visit the historic sites.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔjì* is built from *gǔ* 'ancient' and *jì* 'a trace', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *tsjek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'former' and the second 'a footprint'.
