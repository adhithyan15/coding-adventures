## You'll want to know: のろい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C300-KITSUNE300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のろい** — *noroi* — "a curse; a spell".

> **おばあさんの のろいで、おうさまは ねこに なりました。** — *obāsan no noroi de, ōsama wa neko ni narimashita* — "The old woman's curse turned the king into a cat."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のろい** (*noroi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
