# Asking, and being able to say no

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-NAHIN-VERB, UR-GRAMMAR-KYA-POLAR, UR-GRAMMAR-NA-NA] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Three items landed in this chapter, and between them they let the
book ask a question and let you refuse it. Name all three, and say what each one
stands in front of.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-NAHIN-VERB, UR-GRAMMAR-KYA-POLAR, UR-GRAMMAR-NA-NA, UR-LEX-THIK, UR-LEX-SAMAJHNA, UR-LEX-JI-HAN, UR-LEX-MAIN, UR-LEX-PANI] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the sentence chapter one deferred — *maiṅ nahīṅ samajhtā*]
- [YOU SAY: a question the book can now ask — *kyā āp ṭhīk haiṅ?*]
- [YOU SAY: both answers — *jī hāṅ* … *nahīṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: a double refusal — *na pānī, na hawā*]
- [YOU SAY: a negative asked back, which is how you check something you suspect — *kyā āp nahīṅ samajhte?*]
- [YOU RECALL: the reply from chapter four, then deny it — *maiṅ ṭhīk hūṅ* … *maiṅ ṭhīk nahīṅ hūṅ*]
