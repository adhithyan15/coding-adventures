## You'll want to know: ઝાંખું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C438-DUSK438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાંખું** — *jhā̃khũ* — "dim; faint (of light)".

> ઓરડામાં ઝાંખી બત્તી હતી.
>
> *orḍāmā̃ jhā̃khī battī hatī.*
>
> There was a dim light in the room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાંખું** (*jhā̃khũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
