Russian, chapter 419: Checking in for a flight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
регистрация (registrátsiya) — check-in; registration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to go past, then the Russian for to see off.

You'll want to know: регистрация.
регистрация (registrátsiya) — "check-in; registration".
Регистрация начинается в шесть утра. Registrátsiya nachináyetsya v shest utrá. Check-in opens at six in the morning.
Feminine. Стойка регистрации — the check-in desk.

The word, taken apart.
регистрация (registrátsiya) is a learned word built on регистр 'a register', usually traced to Medieval Latin registrum 'a list'. EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 2 of 5.
регистрироваться (registrírovatsya) — to check in; to register.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to see off, then the Russian for check-in.

You'll want to know: регистрироваться.
регистрироваться (registrírovatsya) — "to check in; to register".
Сейчас все регистрируются на сайте. Seychás vse registríruyutsya na sáyte. Nowadays everyone checks in on the website.
Imperfective; present регистрируюсь. Takes на + accusative for a flight. The perfective is зарегистрироваться.

The word, taken apart.
регистрироваться (registrírovatsya) is built on регистр 'a register' with -ировать and -ся. Регистр is usually traced to Medieval Latin registrum 'a list'; EtymDB lists регистрировать without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
рейс (reys) — a flight; a trip (of a bus or a ship).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for check-in, then the Russian for to check in.

You'll want to know: рейс.
рейс (reys) — "a flight; a trip (of a bus or a ship)".
Наш рейс в два часа дня. Nash reys v dva chasá dnya. Our flight is at two in the afternoon.
Masculine. Рейс отменили — the flight was cancelled.

The word, taken apart.
рейс (reys) was borrowed from Dutch reis 'a journey, travel' (EtymDB); in Russian it names one trip of a plane, a ship or a bus.

Lesson 4 of 5.
кладь (klad) — luggage (in ручная кладь: hand luggage).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to check in, then the Russian for a flight.

You'll want to know: кладь.
кладь (klad) — "luggage (in ручная кладь: hand luggage)".
В самолёт можно взять одну сумку ручной клади. V samolyót mózhno vzyat odnú súmku ruchnóy kládi. You can take one bag of hand luggage onto the plane.
Feminine; formal, and mostly in ручная кладь (klad) — hand luggage.

The word, taken apart.
кладь (klad) is built on the root of класть 'to put, to lay': the things one loads. EtymDB lists кладь (klad) without a deeper source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
посадочный (posádochnyy) — boarding (adjective).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a flight, then the Russian for luggage.

You'll want to know: посадочный.
посадочный (posádochnyy) — "boarding (adjective)".
Без посадочного талона в самолёт не пустят. Bez posádochnogo talóna v samolyót ne pústyat. They will not let you onto the plane without a boarding pass.
Посадочный талон — a boarding pass. Посадка is boarding, and also landing.

The word, taken apart.
посадочный (posádochnyy) is built on посадка 'boarding; landing', from по- and садить 'to seat'. EtymDB lists садить without a deeper source.
