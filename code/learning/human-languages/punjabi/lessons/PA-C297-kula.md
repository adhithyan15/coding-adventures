## You'll want to know: ਕੂਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C297-TOUCH297-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੂਲਾ** — *kūlā* — "tender; soft".

> ਬੱਚੇ ਦੇ ਹੱਥ ਕੂਲੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *bachche de hatth kūle hunde han.*
>
> A child's hands are soft.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੂਲਾ** (*kūlā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kōmala*, "tender, soft"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kūlā* under it (CDIAL 3523).
