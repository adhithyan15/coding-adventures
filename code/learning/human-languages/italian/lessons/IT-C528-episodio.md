## You'll want to know: l'episodio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C528-SERIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'episodio** — "episode; incident".

> Ho visto tre episodi di fila.
>
> *I watched three episodes in a row.*

Also an incident: *un brutto episodio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*episodio* is a learned borrowing from Greek *epeisódion* "a part added between choral songs" (EtymDB, under English *episode*), from *eísodos* "an entrance".
