Russian, chapter 393: Applications and wills.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
заявление (zayavléniye) — an application (in writing); a statement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an offender, then the Russian for innocent.

You'll want to know: заявление.
заявление (zayavléniye) — "an application (in writing); a statement".
Он подал заявление на отпуск. On pódal zayavléniye na ótpusk. He put in a request for leave.
Neuter. A written request to an office or a boss: заявление (zayavléniye) на отпуск. Also a public statement.

The word, taken apart.
заявление (zayavléniye) is built on заявить "to declare", from за- and явить "to show", from Proto-Slavic aviti "to make visible" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
подавать (podavát) — to submit, to hand in; to serve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for innocent, then the Russian for an application.

You'll want to know: подавать.
подавать (podavát) — "to submit, to hand in; to serve".
Документы подают до первого июля. Dokuménty podayút do pérvogo iyúlya. Documents must be submitted by the first of July.
Imperfective; present подаю, подаёшь; perfective подать. Подавать заявление (zayavléniye) — to put in an application. Подавать в суд — to take someone to court. It also means to serve food.

The word, taken apart.
подавать (podavát) is built from под- "up to, under" and давать "to give", the ongoing partner of дать: to hand something up to someone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
нотариус (notárius) — a notary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an application, then the Russian for to submit.

You'll want to know: нотариус.
нотариус (notárius) — "a notary".
Мы ходили к нотариусу с документами. My khodíli k notáriusu s dokuméntami. We went to the notary with our documents.
Masculine, also of a woman. In Russia a notary certifies documents, wills and powers of attorney.

The word, taken apart.
нотариус (notárius) is usually traced to Latin notārius "a shorthand clerk, a secretary" (Lewis & Short), built on nota "a mark, a sign".

Lesson 4 of 5.
доверенность (dovérennost) — a power of attorney.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to submit, then the Russian for a notary.

You'll want to know: доверенность.
доверенность (dovérennost) — "a power of attorney".
По доверенности сестра может получить мою посылку. Po dovérennosti sestrá mózhet poluchít moyú posýlku. With a power of attorney my sister can collect my parcel.
Feminine. A paper that lets someone act for you: по доверенности.

The word, taken apart.
доверенность (dovérennost) is built on доверить "to entrust", from до- and верить "to believe", on вера "faith", Proto-Slavic věra (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
завещание (zaveshchániye) — a will (a testament).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a notary, then the Russian for a power of attorney.

You'll want to know: завещание.
завещание (zaveshchániye) — "a will (a testament)".
Дед написал завещание. Ded napisál zaveshchániye. Grandfather made a will.
Neuter. Оставить завещание (zaveshchániye) — to leave a will.

The word, taken apart.
завещание (zaveshchániye) is built on завещать "to bequeath", on вещать "to proclaim", the Church Slavonic form of the root of ответ, Old Russian větŭ "counsel": a will is a last word spoken for the future.
