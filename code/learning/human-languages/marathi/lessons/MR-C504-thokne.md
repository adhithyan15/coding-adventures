## You'll want to know: ठोकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C504-KAMGAR504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकणे** — *ṭhokṇe* — "to hammer in; to knock".

> सुताराने भिंतीत एक खिळा ठोकला.
>
> *sutārāne bhintīt ek khiḷā ṭhoklā.*
>
> The carpenter hammered a nail into the wall.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने खिळा ठोकला, *tyāne khiḷā ṭhoklā*.

The place takes -त: भिंतीत खिळा ठोकणे, to drive a nail into a wall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकणे** (*ṭhokṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ṭhokṇẽ*, "to strike", under *\*ṭhōkk*, "knock" (CDIAL 5513), a sound word shared with Hindi *ṭhoknā*.
