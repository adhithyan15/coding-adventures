# कब (*kab*) — when?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-JOIN-KYON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C40-DEIXIS-SYSTEM] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Run the k- words you now hold: **क्या**, **कहाँ**, **क्यों**, and
*kaun*. One question a traveller asks more than any of those is still missing.

## You'll want to know: कब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-JOIN-KAB-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-SAKNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C04-KALMILTEHAIN-01] -->

> **कब** — *kab* — **when?**

> **आप कब आ सकते हैं?** — *āp kab ā sakte haiṁ?* — **When can you come?**

Like every Hindi question word, **कब** stays in the sentence's own slot in front
of the verb; nothing is fronted and nothing is reordered. Two letters, both of
which you can already write.

You have had **कल** since chapter four — *tomorrow*, and also *yesterday*, the
same word for both. **कब** is how you ask which day somebody means, and it is
the question the whole timetable half of a listening paper is made of.

**कब** is Sanskrit **कदा**, worn down. Its two partners are coming in the next
lesson, and they are the same word with different first letters.
