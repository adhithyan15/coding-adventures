## You'll want to know: лепить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C331-TESTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**лепить** (*lepít*) — "to shape, to make (by hand); to model".

> Вся семья лепила пельмени.
>
> *Vsya semyá lepíla pelméni.*
>
> The whole family was making dumplings.

Imperfective; present *леплю*, *лепишь*. Perfective *слепить* (*slepít*). *Лепить снеговика* — to make a snowman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*лепить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *lěpiti* "to smear, to stick", from PIE *leyp-* "to stick" (EtymDB).
