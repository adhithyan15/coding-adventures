# அடடா (aṭaṭā) — oh dear!, wow!

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for not at all, then the Tamil for is that so.

## You'll want to know: அடடா
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C96-NOTATALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**அடடா** (*aṭaṭā*) — "oh dear!", or "wow!", by tone.

Said to bad news it is sympathy; said to good news it is delight. **அடடா, அப்படியா?** — "oh
dear, is that so?".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A cry of surprise that can go either way.
