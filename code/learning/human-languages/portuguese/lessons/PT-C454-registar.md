## You'll want to know: registar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C454-CORREIOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**registar** — "to register; to record".

> Registei a carta para ter um recibo.
>
> *I registered the letter to get a receipt.*

*Uma carta registada* — a registered letter. In Brazil: *registrar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*registar* is built on *registo* 'register', from Late Latin *regesta* 'things recorded', on *regerere* 'to carry back, to record' (Lewis & Short).
