## You'll want to know: आर्थिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C361-ARTHIK361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आर्थिक** — *ārthik* — "economic; financial".

> त्यांना सध्या आर्थिक अडचण आहे.
>
> *tyānnā sadhyā ārthik aḍcaṇ āhe.*
>
> They are in financial difficulty at the moment.

Invariable; built on अर्थ, wealth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आर्थिक** (*ārthik*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ārthika* (Monier-Williams), built on *artha*, "wealth, aim"; Marathi uses it for money matters.
