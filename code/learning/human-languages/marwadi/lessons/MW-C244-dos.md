## You'll want to know: दोस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C244-JHAGRO244-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दोस** — *dos* — "a fault; blame".

> यो म्हारो दोस कोनी।
>
> *yo mhāro dos konī.*
>
> This is not my fault.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दोस** (*dos*) is inherited from Sanskrit *doṣa*, "fault"; CDIAL lists Marwari *dos*, "fault, blame", under it (CDIAL 6587).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
