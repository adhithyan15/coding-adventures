Portuguese, chapter 36: Fear, Anger, Sadness, Happy, Sad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o medo — fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for tiredness, then the Portuguese for joy.

You'll want to know: o medo.
o medo — "fear". tenho medo: I am afraid.
