## You'll want to know: convencido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C335-OPINIAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**convencido** — "convinced, sure".

> Estou convencido de que é a melhor solução.
>
> *I am convinced that it is the best solution.*

*Estar convencido de que…* — to be convinced that… A woman says *convencida*. It is the past participle of *convencer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convencido* is the past participle of *convencer*, which comes from Latin *convincere* "to overcome, to convict of error" (Lewis & Short), built on *vincere* "to conquer".
