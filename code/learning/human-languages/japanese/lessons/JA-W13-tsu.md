# つ — one full-size curve

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01, JA-PERFORMANCE-BODY-FOURTEEN-01, JA-ETYMON-ARIGATASHI, JA-LEX-ARIGATOU, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN] -->

[PAUSE 35s] Write **ありがとう**, name its older *arigatashi* family, and point
to the dakuten. Then write small **っ** and retrieve one word from the body map.

## Script — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-TSU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **つ** — *tsu*

This is the full-size sign. Trace its one stroke: begin at the upper left, sweep
right, turn downward, and finish toward the lower left.

## Writing — copy, then recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-TSU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01] -->

Copy **つ** once. Hide it for ten seconds, write it once, then place **つ** beside
small **っ** and check that the full-size sign is clearly larger.
