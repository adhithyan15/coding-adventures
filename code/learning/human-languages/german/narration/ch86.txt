German, chapter 86: Pig, Bird, Mouse, Fox, Snake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Schwein — a pig.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a horse, then the German for a cow.

You'll want to know: das Schwein.
das Schwein — "a pig".

What you've built.
