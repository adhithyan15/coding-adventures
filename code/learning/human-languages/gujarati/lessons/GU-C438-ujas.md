## You'll want to know: ઉજાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C438-DUSK438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજાસ** — *ujās* — "the light of daybreak; brightness".

> સવારે બારીમાંથી ઓરડામાં ઉજાસ આવ્યો.
>
> *savāre bārīmā̃thī orḍāmā̃ ujās āvyo.*
>
> In the morning light came into the room through the window.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજાસ** (*ujās*) goes back to *\*ujjhāṣa*, "shining", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "brightness, light" (CDIAL 1678).
