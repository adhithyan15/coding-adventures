## You'll want to know: la désinformation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C285-RUMEUR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la désinformation** — "disinformation, false information".

> Il y a beaucoup de désinformation sur internet.
>
> *There is a lot of false information on the internet.*

People also say *les fausses nouvelles*, or *les fake news* in speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*désinformation* is built on *information*, a learned borrowing from Latin *īnfōrmātiō* "an outline, an idea", built on *fōrma* "shape" (Lewis & Short).
