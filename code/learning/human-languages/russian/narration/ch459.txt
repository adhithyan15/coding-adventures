Russian, chapter 459: Going hiking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поход (pokhód) — a hike, a trek; a campaign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a summit, then the Russian for a gorge.

You'll want to know: поход.
поход (pokhód) — "a hike, a trek; a campaign".
Летом мы ходили в поход в горы. Létom my khodíli v pokhód v góry. In summer we went on a hike in the mountains.
Masculine. Идти в поход (pokhód) — to go hiking or camping.

The word, taken apart.
поход (pokhód) is built from по- and ход 'a going', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic xodŭ 'motion' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
тропа (tropá) — a path, a trail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a gorge, then the Russian for a hike.

You'll want to know: тропа.
тропа (tropá) — "a path, a trail".
Узкая тропа ведёт к озеру. Úzkaya tropá vedyót k ózeru. A narrow path leads to the lake.
Feminine; plural тропы. Тропинка — a little path.

The word, taken apart.
тропа (tropá) is an old Slavic word, usually linked with a root for stamping the feet: a path trodden by feet. EtymDB lists it without a source, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
привал (privál) — a rest stop (on a hike), a halt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a hike, then the Russian for a path.

You'll want to know: привал.
привал (privál) — "a rest stop (on a hike), a halt".
Через два часа мы сделали привал. Chérez dva chasá my sdélali privál. After two hours we stopped for a rest.
Masculine. Сделать привал (privál) — to stop for a rest.

The word, taken apart.
привал (privál) is built on привалить 'to lean against; to pull in to shore', from при- and валить 'to tumble'. EtymDB does not list it, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 5 of 5.
спотыкаться (spotykátsya) — to stumble, to trip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a rest stop, then the Russian for to scramble.

You'll want to know: спотыкаться.
спотыкаться (spotykátsya) — "to stumble, to trip".
В темноте я часто спотыкаюсь о камни. V temnoté ya chásto spotykáyus o kámni. In the dark I often trip over stones.
Imperfective; perfective споткнуться. Takes о + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
спотыкаться (spotykátsya) is built from с-, по-, тыкать 'to poke, to knock' and -ся (EtymDB): to knock one's foot against something.
