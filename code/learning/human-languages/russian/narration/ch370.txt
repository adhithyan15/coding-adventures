Russian, chapter 370: On one condition.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
условие (uslóviye) — a condition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to recycle, then the Russian for to look after.

You'll want to know: условие.
условие (uslóviye) — "a condition".
У меня одно условие: мы вернёмся до десяти. U menyá odnó uslóviye: my vernyómsya do desyatí. I have one condition: we come back before ten.
Neuter. The plural условия also means terms or circumstances: условия работы.

The word, taken apart.
условие (uslóviye) is built on the old verb условиться "to agree on terms", from у- and слово "word", inherited from Proto-Slavic slovo (EtymDB). A condition is something settled in words.

Lesson 2 of 5.
при условии (pri uslóvii) — on condition, provided (that).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to look after, then the Russian for a condition.

You'll want to know: при условии.
при условии (pri uslóvii) — "on condition, provided (that)".
Я пойду при условии, что ты пойдёшь со мной. Ya poydú pri uslóvii, chto ty poydyósh so mnoy. I will go on condition that you come with me.
При условии, что… takes an ordinary indicative clause. With a noun it takes the genitive: при условии (pri uslóvii) оплаты.

The word, taken apart.
при условии (pri uslóvii) is при "at, under" with the prepositional of условие (uslóviye) "a condition", which is built on слово "word", Proto-Slavic slovo (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
в случае (v slúchaye) — in case of, in the event of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a condition, then the Russian for on condition.

You'll want to know: в случае.
в случае (v slúchaye) — "in case of, in the event of".
В случае дождя концерт будет в зале. V slúchaye dozhdyá kontsért búdet v zále. In case of rain the concert will be in the hall.
В случае + genitive: в случае (v slúchaye) дождя. Before a clause, в случае (v slúchaye) если…

The word, taken apart.
в случае (v slúchaye) is в "in" with the prepositional of случай "an event, a chance", inherited from Proto-Slavic sŭlučajĭ, from lučiti, tentatively "to find, to reach" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
в противном случае (v protívnom slúchaye) — otherwise, if not.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for on condition, then the Russian for in case of.

You'll want to know: в противном случае.
в противном случае (v protívnom slúchaye) — "otherwise, if not".
Нужно выйти сейчас, в противном случае мы опоздаем. Núzhno výyti seychás, v protívnom slúchaye my opozdáyem. We need to leave now, otherwise we will be late.
Formal; in conversation people say иначе or а то.

The word, taken apart.
в противном случае (v protívnom slúchaye) is "in the opposite case": противный "opposite", built on против "against", and случай "a case", from Proto-Slavic sŭlučajĭ (EtymDB). Today противный on its own usually means nasty.

Lesson 5 of 5.
даже если (dázhe yésli) — even if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for in case of, then the Russian for otherwise.

You'll want to know: даже если.
даже если (dázhe yésli) — "even if".
Я приду, даже если будет дождь. Ya pridú, dázhe yésli búdet dozhd. I will come even if it rains.
Like если, it takes an ordinary clause in any tense: даже если (dázhe yésli) он позвонит. With бы it turns hypothetical: даже если (dázhe yésli) бы он позвонил.

The word, taken apart.
даже если (dázhe yésli) is built from даже "even", made of да and the particle же, and если "if", which is usually explained as есть "is" with the particle ли.
