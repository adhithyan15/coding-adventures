## You'll want to know: гражданство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C391-ZAKONODATELSTVO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**гражданство** (*grazhdánstvo*) — "citizenship".

> У неё двойное гражданство.
>
> *U neyó dvoynóye grazhdánstvo.*
>
> She has dual citizenship.

A neuter noun. *Двойное гражданство* — dual citizenship. From *гражданин*, a citizen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**гражданство** is built on *гражданин* "a citizen", on *град*, the Church Slavonic form of native *город* "a town", Proto-Slavic *\*gordŭ*. The *-ра-* is the Church Slavonic shape, *-оро-* the native one.
