## You'll want to know: तरक़्क़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C393-SAFALTA393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तरक़्क़ी** — *taraqqī* — "progress; a promotion".

> अगर तुम अच्छा काम करोगे तो तुम्हें तरक़्क़ी मिलेगी।
>
> *agar tum acchā kām karoge to tumhẽ taraqqī milegī.*
>
> If you do good work, you'll get a promotion.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तरक़्क़ी** (*taraqqī*) is usually traced to Arabic *taraqqī*, "rising, ascent", which reached Hindi through Persian.
