# सुवर्णसंधी (suvarṇasandhī) — a golden chance

## You'll want to know: सुवर्णसंधी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C545-BHAVISHYA545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सुवर्णसंधी** — *suvarṇasandhī* — "a golden chance".

> ही नोकरी माझ्यासाठी सुवर्णसंधी आहे.
>
> *hī nokrī mājhyāsāṭhī suvarṇasandhī āhe.*
>
> This job is a golden chance for me.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुवर्णसंधी** (*suvarṇasandhī*) is a compound built from Sanskrit *suvarṇa*, "gold" (Monier-Williams), and संधी, "a chance".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
