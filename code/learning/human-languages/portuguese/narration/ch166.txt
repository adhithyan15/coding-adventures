Portuguese, chapter 166: Connection, Return, Arrival, Departure, Destination.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a ligação — a connection; a phone call.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a sack, then the Portuguese for a thread.

You'll want to know: a ligação.
a ligação — "a connection; a phone call".

The word, taken apart.
ligação from Latin ligatio, ligationis, "a binding" (EtymDB 2.1), from ligare, "to bind" — a learned borrowing from Latin.
