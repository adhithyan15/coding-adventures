## You'll want to know: गृहस्थी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C503-PIDHI503-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहस्थी** — *gṛhasthī* — "a household; family life".

> शादी के बाद मीरा ने अपनी गृहस्थी सँभाली।
>
> *śādī ke bād Mīrā ne apnī gṛhasthī sãbhālī.*
>
> After her marriage Mira took charge of her household.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहस्थी** (*gṛhasthī*) is Sanskrit *gṛhastha*, "a householder", built on *gṛha*, "house", and *stha*, "standing in" (Monier-Williams), with the ending *-ī*: the life and business of a household.
