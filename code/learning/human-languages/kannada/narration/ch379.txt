Kannada, chapter 379: Agreeing and Disagreeing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸು (sammatisu) — to consent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for bias, then the Kannada for to advocate.

You'll want to know: ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸು.
ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸು — sammatisu — "to consent".
ಅಪ್ಪ ನನ್ನ ಪ್ರವಾಸಕ್ಕೆ ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸಿದರು. appa nanna pravāsakke sammatisidaru. Father agreed to my trip.
Past (I …): ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸಿದೆ, sammatiside.
What you consent to takes -ಗೆ. ಸಮ್ಮತಿ, consent.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸು (sammatisu) is built on Sanskrit sammati, "agreement, approval" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ (bhinnābhiprāya) — a difference of opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to advocate, then the Kannada for to consent.

You'll want to know: ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ.
ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ — bhinnābhiprāya — "a difference of opinion".
ಅಣ್ಣ ತಮ್ಮನ ನಡುವೆ ಸಣ್ಣ ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ ಇತ್ತು. aṇṇa tammana naḍuve saṇṇa bhinnābhiprāya ittu. There was a small difference of opinion between the brothers.
ಭಿನ್ನ, different, + ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ, opinion; the two vowels merge into ಆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಭಿನ್ನಾಭಿಪ್ರಾಯ (bhinnābhiprāya) is built from Sanskrit bhinna, "split, different" (CDIAL 9498), and abhiprāya, "aim, opinion" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಒಮ್ಮತ (ommata) — a consensus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to consent, then the Kannada for a difference of opinion.

You'll want to know: ಒಮ್ಮತ.
ಒಮ್ಮತ — ommata — "a consensus".
ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಎಲ್ಲರಲ್ಲೂ ಒಮ್ಮತ ಮೂಡಿತು. konege ellarallū ommata mūḍitu. At last a consensus formed among everyone.
ಒಮ್ಮತದಿಂದ, unanimously.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಮ್ಮತ (ommata) is built from the native base for "one", or- / om- (DEDR 990), joined to Sanskrit mata, "an opinion" (Monier-Williams): one opinion.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪರಸ್ಪರ (paraspara) — mutual; each other.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a consensus, then the Kannada for to oppose.

You'll want to know: ಪರಸ್ಪರ.
ಪರಸ್ಪರ — paraspara — "mutual; each other".
ಅವರು ಪರಸ್ಪರ ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. avaru paraspara sahāya māḍuttāre. They help each other.
Before a verb it means each other: ಪರಸ್ಪರ (paraspara) ಮಾತಾಡು, to talk to each other.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಸ್ಪರ (paraspara) is borrowed from Sanskrit paraspara, "mutual, each other" (Monier-Williams), a doubling of para, "other".
