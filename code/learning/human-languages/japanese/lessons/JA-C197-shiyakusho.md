## You'll want to know: しやくしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C197-CITY197-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しやくしょ** — *shiyakusho* — "city hall; municipal office".

> **しやくしょは えきの みなみに あります。** — *shiyakusho wa eki no minami ni arimasu* — "City hall is south of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しやくしょ** (*shiyakusho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "market, city" (Middle Chinese *dzyiX*), *yaku*, "duty, office" (*ywek*) and *sho*, "place" (*srjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
