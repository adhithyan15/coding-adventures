## You'll want to know: escalar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C686-CIMA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**escalar** — "to climb (a mountain, a wall)".

> Mi hermana escala montañas.
>
> *My sister climbs mountains.*

*La escalada* is climbing as a sport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escalar* is built on *escala* "ladder" (EtymDB), from Latin *scāla* "a flight of steps, a ladder", from *scandere* "to climb".
