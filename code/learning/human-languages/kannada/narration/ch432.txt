Kannada, chapter 432: Pressure and Objections.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು (hattikku) — to suppress; to stifle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a formal resolution, then the Kannada for to include.

You'll want to know: ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು.
ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು — hattikku — "to suppress; to stifle".
ಅವಳು ತನ್ನ ಕೋಪವನ್ನು ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕಿದಳು. avaḷu tanna kōpavannu hattikkidaḷu. She suppressed her anger.
Past (I …): ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕಿದೆ, hattikkide.
Of feelings and of protests.

The word, taken apart.
ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು (hattikku) is a Kannada verb whose origin is not settled: DEDR does not list it.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

[your turn — say: hattikku]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hattikku, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: avaḷu tanna kōpavannu hattikkidaḷu. — She suppressed her anger.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a formal resolution, then the Kannada for to include, then say hattikku again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಹತ್ತಿಕ್ಕು (hattikku) mean? ("To suppress; to stifle".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 7.
ತಲೆಬಾಗು (talebāgu) — to bow down; to give in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to include, then the Kannada for to suppress.

You'll want to know: ತಲೆಬಾಗು.
ತಲೆಬಾಗು — talebāgu — "to bow down; to give in".
ಒತ್ತಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಲೆಬಾಗಿ ಕಂಪನಿ ಸಂಬಳ ಹೆಚ್ಚಿಸಿತು. ottaḍakke talebāgi kampani sambaḷa heccisitu. Giving in to pressure, the company raised pay.
Past (I …): ತಲೆಬಾಗಿದೆ, talebāgide.
ತಲೆ (head) + ಬಾಗು (to bend). What one gives in to takes -ಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ತಲೆಬಾಗು (talebāgu) is native Dravidian, built from tale, "head" (DEDR 3103), and bāgu, "to bend, to bow" (DEDR 5335).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 7.
ತಕರಾರು (takarāru) — an objection; a dispute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to suppress, then the Kannada for to bow down.

You'll want to know: ತಕರಾರು.
ತಕರಾರು — takarāru — "an objection; a dispute".
ಈ ಹೊಸ ಯೋಜನೆಗೆ ಯಾರಿಗೂ ತಕರಾರು ಇಲ್ಲ. ī hosa yōjanege yārigū takarāru illa. No one has any objection to this new scheme.
ತಕರಾರು (takarāru) ಮಾಡು, to raise objections.

The word, taken apart.
ತಕರಾರು (takarāru) is usually traced to Arabic takrār, "repetition, altercation", through Hindustani.
