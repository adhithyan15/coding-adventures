## You'll want to know: расследование
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C291-SLEDOVATEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**расследование** (*rasslédovaniye*) — "an investigation".

> Расследование продолжалось два месяца.
>
> *Rasslédovaniye prodolzhálos dva mésyatsa.*
>
> The investigation went on for two months.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*расследование* is built on *расследовать* "to investigate", from *рас-* and *след* "a track" (EtymDB): following the footsteps to the end.
