## You'll want to know: shībài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C394-TRY394-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shībài* — "to fail".

> *shībài le yě méi guānxi wǒmen zài lái* — It doesn't matter if we fail; we'll try again.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shībài* is built from *shī* 'to lose' and *bài* 'to fall apart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syit* and *baejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to break of itself'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
