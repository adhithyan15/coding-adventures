# વાંધો નહીં (vāndho nahĩ) — "no problem"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AABHAAR-01, GU-CONCEPT-C01-HAANAA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] When someone thanks you — or apologises — this is the easy, gracious
reply.

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-VANDHONAHI-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AABHAAR-01, GU-CONCEPT-C01-HAANAA-01] -->

**વાંધો** (*vāndho*, "objection, problem" — with the nasal on **વાં**) + **નહીં**
(*nahĩ*, "not") → *vāndho nahĩ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-AABHAAR-01, GU-CONCEPT-C01-HAANAA-01, GU-CONCEPT-C03-VANDHONAHI-01] -->

**વાંધો નહીં** literally means "**[there is] no objection**" — *vāndho*
("objection, difficulty, harm") + **નહીં** (*nahĩ*, "not / no"). It serves as
"it's okay / no worries / no problem / you're welcome." The negative **નહીં**
(*nahĩ*) is the fuller form of the **ના** (*nā*) from the earlier yes/no lesson — both from
the ancient **na-**, Proto-Indo-European **\*ne**, behind English **no**,
**not**, and Hindi *nahīṁ*.
