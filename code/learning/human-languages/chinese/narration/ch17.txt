Mandarin Chinese, chapter 17: Language or country?.
17 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 17.
Hear zhōngwén — Chinese.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
From the last chapter, say 汉语, 汉字, 字 for Chinese language, Chinese character, written character. Then say the sound of 中: zhōng.

You'll want to know.
zhōngwén — Chinese; the Chinese language.
Keep the level first tone in zhōng. Let wén rise in second tone. The new second character stays hidden for now; this step is only sound and meaning.

Lesson 2 of 17.
文 (wén) — watch four open strokes.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — the shape until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 15 seconds]
Say go to school as shàngxué and write 谢 once from memory. Then say Chinese language as zhōngwén.

Script — the shape.
Write 文 (wén) in four separate strokes: top dot, horizontal bar, long fall left, long fall right. The last two strokes begin near the centre and open in opposite directions. Four strokes; three lifts.

Guided Practice.
Trace 文 (wén) once. Say dot, bar, left, right as your hand moves.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 8 of 17.
国 (guó) — frame, inside, close.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — the shape until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 14 seconds]
Say China. Put 请 before a familiar request, then air-write the opening two strokes of a box without closing it.

Script — the shape.
Write 国 (guó) in eight strokes. Open the outer frame with its left side, then top-and-right. Finish every stroke of 玉 inside. Only then add the bottom bar that closes the frame. Eight strokes; seven lifts.

Guided Practice.
Trace 国 (guó) once. Say frame, three bars, upright, dot, close. Keep the bottom open until the inner dot is finished.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 17 of 17.
Payoff — five identity words.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Keep four scores. Repair one missed skill without erasing the other passes.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Write 家, 文 (wén), and 国 (guó) without a model, then name each aloud.

Guided Practice.
Listening, /5: hear all five words in mixed order and choose each meaning.
Speaking, /5: produce all five from meaning cards, with tones audible.
Reading, /5: read 中国 (zhōngguó), 汉语, 字, 中文 (zhōngwén), 汉字 cold, without pinyin.
Writing, /5: hear all five meanings and write each form without a model.
Pass each skill separately. A miss in one column does not cancel the other three. For repair, revisit only the missed form and retry that skill.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Official HSK 3.0 syllabus, Level 1 vocabulary.
[question — say your answer, then pause 28 seconds]
Without pinyin or a model, write character, Chinese character, Chinese language with 汉, Chinese language with 中, and China.
