## You'll want to know: साम्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C348-SAMBANDH348-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साम्य** — *sāmya* — "a similarity".

> दोन्ही भावांच्या आवाजात खूप साम्य आहे.
>
> *donhī bhāvāñcyā āvājāt khūp sāmya āhe.*
>
> The two brothers' voices are very alike.

Neuter.

Neuter; X आणि Y मध्ये साम्य असणे, X and Y are alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साम्य** (*sāmya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sāmya*, "equality, likeness" (Monier-Williams).
