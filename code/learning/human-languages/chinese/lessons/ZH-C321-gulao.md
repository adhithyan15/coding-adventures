## You'll want to know: 古老
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C321-ANCIENT321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**古老** — *gǔlǎo* — "ancient; age-old".

> **他们家的房子很古老** *tāmen jiā de fángzi hěn gǔlǎo* — Their family's house is ancient.

Two third tones meet in this word: gǔ is said with a rising tone before lǎo, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**古老** (*gǔlǎo*) is built from 古 'ancient' and 老 'old', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *lawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'former' and the second 'aged'.
