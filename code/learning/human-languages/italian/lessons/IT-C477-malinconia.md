## You'll want to know: la malinconia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C477-NOSTALGIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la malinconia** — "melancholy, sadness".

> La pioggia d'autunno mi mette malinconia.
>
> *Autumn rain makes me melancholy.*

The adjective is *malinconico*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*malinconia* comes from Late Latin *melancholia* (EtymDB), from Greek *melankholía* "black bile", which old medicine blamed for sadness.
