Punjabi, chapter 318: A Day of Mishaps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉੱਡਣਾ (uḍḍṇā) — to fly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to regret, then the Punjabi for to spend time.

You'll want to know: ਉੱਡਣਾ.
ਉੱਡਣਾ — uḍḍṇā — "to fly".
ਤੇਜ਼ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੀ ਟੋਪੀ ਉੱਡੀ। tez havā vich merī ṭopī uḍḍī. My cap blew away in the strong wind.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੰਛੀ ਰੁੱਖ ਤੋਂ ਉੱਡਿਆ, oh panchhī rukkh ton uḍḍiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਡਣਾ (uḍḍṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit uḍḍīyate, "flies up"; CDIAL lists Punjabi uḍḍṇā, "flies", under it (CDIAL 1697).

ਟੱਕਰ — ṭakkar — "a collision".
ਪੁਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਦੋ ਗੱਡੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਟੱਕਰ ਹੋਈ। pul utte do gaḍḍīāṁ dī ṭakkar hoī. Two cars crashed into each other on the bridge.
A feminine noun.

ਟੱਕਰ (ṭakkar) is inherited from Sanskrit ṭakkarā, "a blow on the head"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭakkar, "pushing, knocking", under it (CDIAL 5424).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

The word, taken apart.
