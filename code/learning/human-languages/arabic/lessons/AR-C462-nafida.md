## You'll want to know: نفد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C462-ISTIDAD462-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نفد** — *nafida* — "to run out".

> نفد الماء في منتصف الطريق.
>
> *nafida al-māʾ fī muntaṣaf aṭ-ṭarīq.*
>
> The water ran out halfway along the road.

Present: ينفد, *yanfad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نفد** (*nafida*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-f-d*, "to come to an end" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
