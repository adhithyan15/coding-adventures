## You'll want to know: गुणः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C460-SVABHAVA460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गुणः** — *guṇaḥ* — "a virtue".

> दया महान् गुणः अस्ति।
>
> *dayā mahān guṇaḥ asti.*
>
> Compassion is a great virtue.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुणः** (*guṇaḥ*) is traced to the root √grah, "to seize", in the Uṇādi list that MW follows; its first sense is "a strand of a cord", and "a good quality, virtue" comes later (MW).
