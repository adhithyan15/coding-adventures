## You'll want to know: भड़काना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C314-AFVAH314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भड़काना** — *bhaṛkānā* — "to stir up; to incite".

> कुछ लोगों ने अफ़वाह से भीड़ को भड़काया।
>
> *kuch logoṁ ne afvāh se bhīṛ ko bhaṛkāyā.*
>
> A few people used the rumour to stir up the crowd.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने लोगों को भड़काया, *usne logoṁ ko bhaṛkāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भड़काना** (*bhaṛkānā*) is the causative of **भड़कना**, to flare up, from *\*bhaṭ* (CDIAL 9365): to make a fire, or a crowd, blaze.
