## You'll want to know: указатель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C438-UKAZATEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**указатель** (*ukazátel*) — "a signpost; an index".

> По указателю до озера три километра.
>
> *Po ukazátelyu do ózera tri kilométra.*
>
> According to the signpost it is three kilometres to the lake.

Masculine. *Дорожный указатель* — a road sign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*указатель* is built on *указать* 'to show the way' with *-тель*, from *казать* 'to show', the root of *сказать* 'to say'. EtymDB lists *указатель* without a deeper source.
