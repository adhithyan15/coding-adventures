## You'll want to know: पायपीट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C407-VEG407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पायपीट** — *pāypīṭ* — "a long trudge; a long walk".

> बस नव्हती म्हणून आमची खूप पायपीट झाली.
>
> *bas navhatī mhaṇūn āmcī khūp pāypīṭ jhālī.*
>
> There was no bus, so we had a long trudge on foot.

Feminine.

पायपीट होणे or पायपीट करणे; it sounds tiring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पायपीट** (*pāypīṭ*) is built from **पाय**, "a foot", and *pīṭ*, a beating, from the family of Sanskrit *piṭṭayati*, "beats" (CDIAL 8165): beating the road with your feet.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
