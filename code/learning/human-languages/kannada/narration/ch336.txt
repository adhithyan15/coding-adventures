Kannada, chapter 336: The Army Marches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸೈನ್ಯ (sainya) — an army.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a leaflet, then the Kannada for a publisher.

You'll want to know: ಸೈನ್ಯ.
ಸೈನ್ಯ — sainya — "an army".
ರಾಜನ ಸೈನ್ಯ ನದಿಯನ್ನು ದಾಟಿತು. rājana sainya nadiyannu dāṭitu. The king's army crossed the river.
A soldier is a ಸೈನಿಕ; the army is the ಸೈನ್ಯ (sainya).

The word, taken apart.
ಸೈನ್ಯ (sainya) is borrowed from Sanskrit sainya, "belonging to an army", and as a noun "an army" (CDIAL 13602), from senā, "an army".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸೇನಾಪತಿ (sēnāpati) — a commander; a general.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a publisher, then the Kannada for an army.

You'll want to know: ಸೇನಾಪತಿ.
ಸೇನಾಪತಿ — sēnāpati — "a commander; a general".
ಸೇನಾಪತಿ ಸೈನಿಕರಿಗೆ ಮುಂದೆ ಹೋಗಲು ಹೇಳಿದನು. sēnāpati sainikarige munde hōgalu hēḷidanu. The general told the soldiers to go forward.
Plural: ಸೇನಾಪತಿಗಳು, sēnāpatigaḷu.
ಸೇನೆ, an army, and ಪತಿ, a lord.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೇನಾಪತಿ (sēnāpati) is borrowed from Sanskrit senāpati, "the general of an army" (Monier-Williams), from senā "an army" and pati "a lord, a master".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಯೋಧ (yōdha) — a warrior.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an army, then the Kannada for a commander.

You'll want to know: ಯೋಧ.
ಯೋಧ — yōdha — "a warrior".
ಆ ಯೋಧ ಯುದ್ಧದಲ್ಲಿ ಊರನ್ನು ಕಾಪಾಡಿದನು. ā yōdha yuddhadalli ūrannu kāpāḍidanu. That warrior protected the town in the war.
Plural: ಯೋಧರು, yōdharu.
Today it is also a respectful word for a soldier: ನಮ್ಮ ಯೋಧರು.

The word, taken apart.
ಯೋಧ (yōdha) is borrowed from Sanskrit yodha, "a fighter, a warrior, a soldier" (Monier-Williams), from the root yudh, "to fight", as in ಯುದ್ಧ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಹೋರಾಡು (hōrāḍu) — to fight; to struggle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a commander, then the Kannada for a warrior.

You'll want to know: ಹೋರಾಡು.
ಹೋರಾಡು — hōrāḍu — "to fight; to struggle".
ರೈತರು ತಮ್ಮ ನೆಲಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಹೋರಾಡಿದರು. raitaru tamma nelakkāgi hōrāḍidaru. The farmers fought for their land.
Past (I …): ಹೋರಾಡಿದೆ, hōrāḍide.
What one fights for takes -ಆಗಿ: ನ್ಯಾಯಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಹೋರಾಡು (hōrāḍu), to fight for justice.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೋರಾಡು (hōrāḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: pōr, "to fight, to wrestle" (DEDR 4540), whose p- became h-, and āḍu, "to move, to act" (DEDR 347).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು (himmeṭṭu) — to retreat; to fall back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a warrior, then the Kannada for to fight.

You'll want to know: ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು.
ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು — himmeṭṭu — "to retreat; to fall back".
ದೊಡ್ಡ ಸೈನ್ಯ ಬರುವುದನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಅವರು ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟಿದರು. doḍḍa sainya baruvudannu nōḍi avaru himmeṭṭidaru. Seeing a large army coming, they fell back.
Past (I …): ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟಿದೆ, himmeṭṭide.
ಹಿಂ, back, and ಮೆಟ್ಟು, to step: to step back.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು (himmeṭṭu) is a native Dravidian compound: him, "behind, back" (DEDR 4205), and meṭṭu, "to put the foot down, to step" (DEDR 5057).
