Japanese, chapter 460: Goals and Success.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
もくひょう (mokuhyō) — a goal; a target.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for one's field of study, then the Japanese for a career path.

You'll want to know: もくひょう.
もくひょう — mokuhyō — "a goal; a target".
ことしの もくひょうは しあいで かつ ことです。 — kotoshi no mokuhyō wa shiai de katsu koto desu — "My goal this year is to win a match."

The word, taken apart.
もくひょう (mokuhyō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are moku, "eye" (Middle Chinese mjuwk) and hyō, "mark" (pjiew) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
