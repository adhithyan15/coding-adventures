## You'll want to know: глохнуть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C404-KAPOT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**глохнуть** (*glókhnut*) — "to stall (of an engine); to go deaf".

> Старый мотор часто глохнет на светофоре.
>
> *Stáryy motór chásto glókhnet na svetofóre.*
>
> The old engine often stalls at the traffic lights.

Imperfective; perfective *заглохнуть*. Past *глох, глохла*: the -ну- drops out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*глохнуть* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *glŭxnǫti* (EtymDB), the verb that goes with *глухой* 'deaf': an engine that stalls goes silent.
