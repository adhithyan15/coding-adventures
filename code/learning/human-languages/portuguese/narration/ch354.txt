Portuguese, chapter 354: Melhor ou pior.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a influência — the influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to make difficult, then the Portuguese for to favour.

You'll want to know: a influência.
a influência — "the influence".
A avó teve muita influência na minha vida. My grandmother had a lot of influence on my life.
Ter influência sobre — to have influence over.

The word, taken apart.
influência is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin īnfluentia (EtymDB), built on Latin īnfluere "to flow or run into" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

compensar — "to make up for, to compensate; to be worth it".

compensar is a learned borrowing from Latin compēnsāre "to weigh several things with one another, to balance" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on pēnsāre "to weigh", from pendere.
