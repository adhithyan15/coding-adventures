## You'll want to know: ذبل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C468-NABAT468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ذبل** — *dhabala* — "to wilt; to wither".

> ذبلت الزهرة في الشمس.
>
> *dhabalat az-zahra fī ash-shams.*
>
> The flower wilted in the sun.

Present: يذبل, *yadhbul*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذبل** (*dhabala*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *dh-b-l*, "to wither". It is the plain verb.
