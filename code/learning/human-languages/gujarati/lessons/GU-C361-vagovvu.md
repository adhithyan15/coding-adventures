## You'll want to know: વગોવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C361-TIKA361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વગોવવું** — *vagovvũ* — "to run down (to defame)".

> તે બધાની સામે પોતાના ભાઈને વગોવે છે.
>
> *te badhānī sāme potānā bhāīne vagove che.*
>
> He runs his brother down in front of everyone.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાના પડોશીને વગોવ્યો, *teṇe potānā paḍośīne vagovyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વગોવવું** (*vagovvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to revile", under a reconstructed *vigrōcati*, "robs" (CDIAL 11671): to rob someone of a good name.
