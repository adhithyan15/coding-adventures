## You'll want to know: ಶಿಖರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C459-CARANA459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಖರ** — *śikhara* — "a peak; a summit".

> ನಾವು ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಶಿಖರ ತಲುಪಿದೆವು.
>
> *nāvu beḷagge śikhara talupidevu.*
>
> We reached the summit in the morning.

The top of a mountain, and the top of a temple tower.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಖರ** (*śikhara*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śikhara*, "a point, a peak" (CDIAL 12435).
