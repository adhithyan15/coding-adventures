## You'll want to know: viser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C374-BUT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**viser** — "to aim (at)".

> Elle vise la première place.
>
> *She is aiming for first place.*

*Viser à* + infinitive — to aim to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viser* is inherited through Old French from a Vulgar Latin *vīsāre* (EtymDB), from Latin *vīsere* "to view closely" (Lewis & Short), built on *vidēre* "to see".
