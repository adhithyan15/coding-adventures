## You'll want to know: इस्तीफ़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C509-HADTAL509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इस्तीफ़ा** — *istīfā* — "a resignation".

> मीरा ने अपना इस्तीफ़ा मालिक को दे दिया।
>
> *Mīrā ne apnā istīfā mālik ko de diyā.*
>
> Mira handed her resignation to the owner.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इस्तीफ़ा** (*istīfā*) is usually traced to Arabic *istiʿfā*, "asking to be let off", through Persian.
