## You'll want to know: o parto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C309-NASCIMENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o parto** — "the delivery, childbirth".

> O parto correu bem.
>
> *The delivery went well.*

*Estar em trabalho de parto* — to be in labour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parto* is inherited from Latin *partus* "a birth" (EtymDB), from *parere* "to give birth".
