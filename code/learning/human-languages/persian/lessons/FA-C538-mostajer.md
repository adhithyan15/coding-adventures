## You'll want to know: مستاجر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C538-APPLIANCES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مستاجر** (*mosta'jer*) — "a tenant".

> مستاجر طبقه بالا خیلی ساکت است.
>
> *mosta'jer-e tabaqe-ye bâlâ kheyli sâkat ast.*
>
> The tenant upstairs is very quiet.

Plural *mosta'jerân*. The opposite is *sâhebkhâne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mosta'jer* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *mustaʾjir*, 'one who rents', from the root of *ejâre*, 'rent'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
