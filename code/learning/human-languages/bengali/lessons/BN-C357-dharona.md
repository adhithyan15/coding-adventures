## You'll want to know: ধারণা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C357-PROBONDHO357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ধারণা** — *dhārônā* — "an idea; a notion".

> এই বিষয়ে আমার কোনো ধারণা নেই।
>
> *ei bishôye āmār kono dhārônā nei.*
>
> I have no idea about this subject.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধারণা** (*dhārônā*) is Sanskrit *dhāraṇā*, holding, keeping in mind (Monier-Williams): what you hold in your head about something, a notion.

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
