## You'll want to know: ગોઠવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C157-PLACE157-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોઠવવું** — *goṭhavvũ* — "to arrange, to set out".

> **તે પુસ્તકો ગોઠવે છે.** — *te pustako goṭhave che* — "He arranges the books."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ગોઠવવું** (*goṭhavvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
