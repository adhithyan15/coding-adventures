> *aitvār* — Sunday

Keep this word oral for now. Its first printed letter comes next.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Aitvār* is inherited from Sanskrit *ādityavāra*, "Sunday". Its history is older than the transparent weekday compounds beside it.
