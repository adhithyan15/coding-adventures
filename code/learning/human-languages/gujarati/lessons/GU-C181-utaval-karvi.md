# ઉતાવળ કરવી (utāvaḷ karvī) — to hurry

## You'll want to know: ઉતાવળ કરવી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C181-DELAY181-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉતાવળ કરવી** — *utāvaḷ karvī* — "to hurry".

> **આજે તે ઉતાવળ કરશે.** — *āje te utāvaḷ karśe* — "Today she will be in a hurry."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉતાવળ કરવી** (*utāvaḷ karvī*) is built from the feminine noun *utāvaḷ*, "haste", and *karvī*, the feminine of *karvũ*, "to do". CDIAL lists *utāvaḷ*, glossed "impetuous", under *\*uttāpala*, a reconstructed form on the root *tap*, "to heat" (CDIAL 1788).
