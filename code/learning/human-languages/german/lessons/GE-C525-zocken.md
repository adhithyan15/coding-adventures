## You'll want to know: zocken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C525-STREAMEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**zocken** — "to play video games; to gamble".

> Die Jungen zocken den ganzen Nachmittag.
>
> *The boys are gaming all afternoon.*

Informal. It first meant to gamble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zocken* was borrowed from Yiddish *tsokn* "to gamble" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Hebrew *ṣāḥaq* "to laugh, to play".
