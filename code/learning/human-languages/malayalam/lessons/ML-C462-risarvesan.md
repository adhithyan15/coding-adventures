# റിസർവേഷൻ (ṟisarvēṣan) — a reservation

## You'll want to know: റിസർവേഷൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C462-BOOKING462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**റിസർവേഷൻ** — *ṟisarvēṣan* — "a reservation".

> റിസർവേഷൻ ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ സീറ്റ് കിട്ടില്ല.
>
> *ṟisarvēṣan illeṅkil sīṟṟŭ kiṭṭilla.*
>
> If you have no reservation, you won't get a seat.

A reserved seat on a train is a റിസർവേഷൻ സീറ്റ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റിസർവേഷൻ** (*ṟisarvēṣan*) was borrowed from English *reservation*, a seat or room kept for someone in advance.
