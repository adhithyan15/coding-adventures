# దోపిడీ (dōpiḍī) — a robbery; plunder

## You'll want to know: దోపిడీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C348-DOPIDI348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**దోపిడీ** — *dōpiḍī* — "a robbery; plunder".

> రాత్రి బ్యాంకులో దోపిడీ జరిగింది.
>
> *rātri byāṅkulō dōpiḍī jarigindi.*
>
> There was a robbery at the bank at night.

You know దోచుకో, to rob; దోపిడీ is the deed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దోపిడీ** (*dōpiḍī*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3541 lists Telugu *dōpiḍi*, "plunder, pillage", with *dōcu*, "to plunder, rob".
