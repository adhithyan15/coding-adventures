Arabic, chapter 98: Colour, Song, Journey, Holiday, Love.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لون (lawn) — a colour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for fifty, then the Arabic for a thousand.

You'll want to know: لون.
لون — lawn — "a colour".

The word, taken apart.
لون (lawn) is built on the native Arabic root l-w-n, "colour" (Lane). An old noun with its own root in Lane.
