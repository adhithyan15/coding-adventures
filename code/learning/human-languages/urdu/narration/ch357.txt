Urdu, chapter 357: Corruption.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بدعنوانی (badunvānī) — corruption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to put right, then the Urdu for to polish a skill.

You'll want to know: بدعنوانی.
بدعنوانی — badunvānī — "corruption".
حکومت بدعنوانی کے خلاف ہے۔ hukūmat badunvānī ke khilāf hai. The government is against corruption.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بدعنوانی (badunvānī) is built from Persian bad, "bad", and unvān, "a heading; a manner", which is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿunwān, with the noun ending -ī. Urdu uses it for corruption, bad conduct in office, a sense our sources do not trace.
