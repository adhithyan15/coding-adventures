# ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು (hoñcu hāku) — to lie in wait

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to hunt, then the Kannada for to wander.

## You'll want to know: ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C360-BETE360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು** — *hoñcu hāku* — "to lie in wait".

> ಹುಲಿ ಪೊದೆಯ ಹಿಂದೆ ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕಿತ್ತು.
>
> *huli podeya hinde hoñcu hākittu.*
>
> The tiger was lying in wait behind the bush.

Past (I …): ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕಿದೆ, *hoñcu hākide*.

Said of animals and of people planning an ambush.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಂಚು ಹಾಕು** (*hoñcu hāku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4596 lists Kannada *hoñcu*, "to wait for expectantly, to lurk, to lie in wait", here with ಹಾಕು, "to put".
