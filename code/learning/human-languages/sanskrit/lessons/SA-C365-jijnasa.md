## You'll want to know: जिज्ञासा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C365-SIDDHANTA365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जिज्ञासा** — *jijñāsā* — "curiosity; the wish to know".

> बालेषु बहु जिज्ञासा भवति।
>
> *bāleṣu bahu jijñāsā bhavati.*
>
> Children have a great deal of curiosity.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिज्ञासा** (*jijñāsā*) comes from the root √jñā, "to know", in its desiderative form, "to wish to know"; MW gives *jijñāsā* as the desire of knowing (MW).
