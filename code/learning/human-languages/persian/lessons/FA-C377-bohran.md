## You'll want to know: بحران
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C377-BOHRAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بحران** (*bohrân*) — "a crisis".

> کشور در یک بحران بزرگ است.
>
> *keshvar dar yek bohrân-e bozorg ast.*
>
> The country is in a big crisis.

Plural *bohrânhâ*. *Bohrâni* — critical.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بحران** was borrowed from Arabic *buḥrān* "crisis" (EtymDB); Persian also forms the adjective *bohrâni* "critical".
