## You'll want to know: ਸਿਹਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C319-HEALTH319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਹਤ** — *sihat* — "health".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਾਦਾ ਜੀ ਦੀ ਸਿਹਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere dādā jī dī sihat chaṅgī nahīṁ hai.*
>
> My grandfather's health is not good.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਹਤ** (*sihat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ṣiḥḥa*, "health, soundness".
