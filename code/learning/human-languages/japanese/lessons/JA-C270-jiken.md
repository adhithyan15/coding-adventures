## You'll want to know: じけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C270-HANNIN270-02]; assesses=[] -->

**じけん** — *jiken* — "an incident; a case".

> **この まちで じけんが ありました。** — *kono machi de jiken ga arimashita* — "There was an incident in this town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じけん** (*jiken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "matter" (Middle Chinese *dzriH*) and *ken*, "affair" (*gjenX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
