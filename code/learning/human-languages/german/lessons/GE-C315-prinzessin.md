## You'll want to know: die Prinzessin
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C315-RITTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Prinzessin** — "the princess".

> Die Prinzessin wollte nicht heiraten.
>
> *The princess did not want to marry.*

Plural: *die Prinzessinnen*. *Der Prinz* — the prince.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Prinzessin* is built after French *princesse* (EtymDB), from Latin *prīnceps* "first, chief", from *prīmus* "first" and *capere* "to take".
