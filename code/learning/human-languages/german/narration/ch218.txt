German, chapter 218: Tasty, Kind, Relaxed, Thin, Brave.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
lecker — tasty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for strong, then the German for single.

You'll want to know: lecker.
lecker — "tasty".

The word, taken apart.
lecker from Middle High German lecker (EtymDB 2.1), from the verb "to lick", Old High German lecchōn, English lick: food worth licking your lips for.

The word, taken apart.
lieb from Middle High German liep from Old High German liob from Proto-Germanic leubaz, "dear" — inherited, the same word as English lief. (EtymDB 2.1) English love shares the root.

The word, taken apart.
mutig from Middle High German muotic from Old High German muotag (EtymDB 2.1): the word for "courage, spirit" + -ig. That word, Old High German muot, is the same as English mood.
