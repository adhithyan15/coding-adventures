# Head and na return separately at R4

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SIR] -->

[PAUSE 7s] Say the Punjabi word for **head** from memory. Then read **ਸਿਰ** once.

## Guided Practice — meaning and history
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SIR, PA-ETYMON-SIR-HORN] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"PA-R28-head-na-r4-word","kind":"text","assesses":["PA-LEX-SIR","PA-ETYMON-SIR-HORN"],"prompt":"Say head in Punjabi. Keep the two claims together: **sir** means head, and **horn** is its surprising English cousin. No new etymology follows.

## Guided Practice — recognize na
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-NA-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"PA-R28-head-na-r4-script","kind":"text","assesses":["PA-SCRIPT-NA-01"],"prompt":"Which already-taught Gurmukhi consonant is na: ਨ or ਸ?","answer":"ਨ","accepted":["ਨ na","na is ਨ"],"feedback":{"correct":"ਨ is the old na shape.","incorrect":"Choose ਨ. Read **ਨ** once and say **na**. Do not copy it in this lesson.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SIR, PA-ETYMON-SIR-HORN, PA-SCRIPT-NA-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Say **sir — head — horn**, then point to **ਨ**. This lesson measures listening, speech, and recognition. It does not award independent Gurmukhi writing evidence.
