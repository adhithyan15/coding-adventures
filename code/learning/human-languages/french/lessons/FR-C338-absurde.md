## You'll want to know: absurde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C338-DOUTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**absurde** — "absurd".

> C'est absurde de payer deux fois pour le même billet.
>
> *It is absurd to pay twice for the same ticket.*

One form for both genders: *une idée absurde*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*absurde* is a learned borrowing from Latin *absurdus* "out of tune, harsh; senseless" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *surdus* "deaf, dull-sounding".
