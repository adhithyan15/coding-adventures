## You'll want to know: ਬੂੰਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C287-WET287-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੂੰਦ** — *būnd* — "a drop".

> ਛੱਤ ਤੋਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਬੂੰਦ ਡਿੱਗੀ।
>
> *chhatt ton pāṇī dī ikk būnd ḍiggī.*
>
> A drop of water fell from the roof.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੂੰਦ** (*būnd*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bindu*, "drop, spot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bund*, "drop", under it (CDIAL 9240).
