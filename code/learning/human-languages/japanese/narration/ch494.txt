Japanese, chapter 494: Onions and Mushrooms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
たまねぎ (tamanegi) — an onion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the washing-up, then the Japanese for to freeze (food).

You'll want to know: たまねぎ.
たまねぎ — tamanegi — "an onion".
たまねぎを きざんだ とき、なきました。 — tamanegi o kizanda toki, nakimashita — "When I chopped the onion, I cried."

The word, taken apart.
たまねぎ (tamanegi) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from tama, "ball" and negi, "leek" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
