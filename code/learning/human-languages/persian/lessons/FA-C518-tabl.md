## You'll want to know: طبل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C518-PLAYING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**طبل** (*tabl*) — "a drum".

> پسر کوچک طبل میزند و همه را بیدار میکند.
>
> *pesar-e kuchek tabl mizanad va hame râ bidâr mikonad.*
>
> The little boy bangs a drum and wakes everyone up.

*tabl zadan* — to beat a drum.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tabl* comes from Arabic *ṭabl*, 'drum' (EtymDB); Hindi *tabla* goes back to the same word.
