## You'll want to know: intonato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C518-CANTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**intonato** — "in tune".

> Hai una voce molto intonata.
>
> *You sing very much in tune.*

Also matching: *una borsa intonata alle scarpe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*intonato* comes from *intonare* "to tune, to strike up", built on *tono* "tone", from Latin *tonus*, from Greek *tónos* "a stretching, a pitch" (EtymDB).
