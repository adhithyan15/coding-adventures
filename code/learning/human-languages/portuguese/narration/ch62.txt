Portuguese, chapter 62: Snake, Hen, Duck, Sheep, Goat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a cobra — a snake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a bear, then the Portuguese for a fox.

You'll want to know: a cobra.
a cobra — "a snake".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does a cobra mean? ("A snake".) Say it once more.
