## You'll want to know: સંજોગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C339-ASAR339-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સંજોગ** — *sanjog* — "a circumstance".

> આવા સંજોગોમાં આપણે શું કરવું જોઈએ?
>
> *āvā sanjogomā̃ āpaṇe śũ karvũ joīe?*
>
> What should we do in circumstances like these?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંજોગ** (*sanjog*) goes back to Sanskrit *saṃyoga*, "a joining, a connection" (Monier-Williams), with *y* become *j* as in many words Gujarati inherited. "A circumstance" is its Gujarati sense: the way things happen to come together.
