# پ (pe) — the letter that opens پرسیدن

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-KHE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C23-CHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write خ and چ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **پرسیدن** (*porsidan*), "to ask".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: پ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پ** — *pe*, the sound *p*.

It opens **پرسیدن** (*porsidan*), "to ask". It is **ب** with **three dots below**
instead of one, another letter Persian added to the Arabic alphabet, because
Arabic has no *p*.

## Writing: پ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: sweep the shallow bowl from right to left
- **2.** lift, then place the left dot below
- **3.** lift again and place the right dot below
- **4.** lift again and place the lower-center dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 3.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, پ demonstration at 00:16–00:21 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-KHE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C23-CHE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **پرسیدن** and point at پ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: پ three times, saying *p* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: چ, then پ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **پرسیدن** aloud once more]
