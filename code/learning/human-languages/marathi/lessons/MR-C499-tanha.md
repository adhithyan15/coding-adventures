## You'll want to know: तान्हा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C499-BAL499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तान्हा** — *tānhā* — "newborn; infant".

> तान्हं बाळ दिवसभर झोपतं.
>
> *tānhaṁ bāḷ divasbhar jhoptaṁ.*
>
> A newborn baby sleeps all day.

Agrees: तान्हं बाळ, a newborn baby.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तान्हा** (*tānhā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *tānhā*, "sucking, of a baby or young animal", under Sanskrit *tṛṣṇaka*, "thirsty" (CDIAL 5940).
