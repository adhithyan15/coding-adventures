## You'll want to know: تحصیلات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C403-TAHSILAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تحصیلات** (*tahsilât*) — "education, schooling".

> پدرم تحصیلات دانشگاهی ندارد.
>
> *pedaram tahsilât-e dâneshgâhi nadârad.*
>
> My father does not have a university education.

The Arabic-style plural of *tahsil* "study".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحصیلات** is the Arabic-style plural of *tahsil* "study", usually traced to Arabic *taḥṣīl* "obtaining, acquiring": education is what you have acquired.
