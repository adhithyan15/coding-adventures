## You'll want to know: liángkuai
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C136-WEATHER136-05]; assesses=[] -->

*liángkuai* — "pleasantly cool".

> *qiūtiān hěn liángkuai* — Autumn is nice and cool.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liángkuai* is built from *liáng* 'cool' and 快, glossed 'to one's liking', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljang* and *khwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): cool and pleasant.
