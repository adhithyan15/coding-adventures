# اختطف (ikhtaṭafa) — to kidnap; to snatch

## You'll want to know: اختطف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C347-KHATF347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اختطف** — *ikhtaṭafa* — "to kidnap; to snatch".

> اختطف رجلان ابن التاجر.
>
> *ikhtaṭafa rajulān ibn at-tājir.*
>
> Two men kidnapped the merchant’s son.

Present: يختطف, *yakhtaṭif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختطف** (*ikhtaṭafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-f* (Lane), "to snatch away quickly", as a bird snatches its prey. It is Form VIII.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
