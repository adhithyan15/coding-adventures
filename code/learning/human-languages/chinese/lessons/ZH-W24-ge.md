## Script — 人, and a line under it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 个

**Three strokes**, and the first two are a character you have written since the
opening chapter.

1. the **piě**, falling from the peak to the lower left
2. the **nà**, falling from the same peak to the lower right
3. the **shù**, straight down from under the junction

Strokes 1 and 2 are **人** — the same pair, from the same peak, in the same
order. All that is new is a vertical hung beneath it.

Keep the roof wide and the vertical dead centre. If the roof narrows, 个 starts
to look like 人 with an accident underneath.

This is the cheapest character in the chapter and it does the most work in the
one after it.
