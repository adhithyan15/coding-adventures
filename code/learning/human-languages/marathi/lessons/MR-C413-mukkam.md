## You'll want to know: मुक्काम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C413-NIVAS413-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक्काम** — *mukkām* — "an overnight stay; a halt".

> आज रात्री आमचा मुक्काम एका लहान गावात आहे.
>
> *āj rātrī āmcā mukkām ekā lahān gāvāt āhe.*
>
> Tonight we halt in a small village.

Masculine.

मुक्काम करणे, to stop for the night; मुक्कामी, staying over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक्काम** (*mukkām*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *muqām*, "a place where one stays".
