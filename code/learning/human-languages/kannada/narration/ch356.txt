Kannada, chapter 356: Ballads and Vassal Kings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಜಾನಪದ (jānapada) — folklore; folk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an inscription, then the Kannada for a manuscript.

You'll want to know: ಜಾನಪದ.
ಜಾನಪದ — jānapada — "folklore; folk".
ಅಜ್ಜಿಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಜಾನಪದ ಹಾಡುಗಳು ಗೊತ್ತು. ajjige tumbā jānapada hāḍugaḷu gottu. Grandmother knows many folk songs.
Before a noun it means "folk": ಜಾನಪದ (jānapada) ಕಥೆ, a folk tale.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಾನಪದ (jānapada) is borrowed from Sanskrit jānapada, "living in the country", and as a noun "a country person" (Monier-Williams), from janapada, "a country, a people".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಾಮಂತ (sāmanta) — a vassal king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a manuscript, then the Kannada for folklore.

You'll want to know: ಸಾಮಂತ.
ಸಾಮಂತ — sāmanta — "a vassal king".
ಆ ಸಾಮಂತ ಚಕ್ರವರ್ತಿಗೆ ಪ್ರತಿ ವರ್ಷ ಕಪ್ಪ ಕೊಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. ā sāmanta cakravartige prati varṣa kappa koḍuttiddanu. That vassal king paid tribute to the emperor every year.
Plural: ಸಾಮಂತರು, sāmantaru.
A local ruler under a greater king.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಮಂತ (sāmanta) is borrowed from Sanskrit sāmanta, "being on all sides", and as a noun "a neighbour", later "a vassal" (CDIAL 13348).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸಮರ (samara) — a battle; combat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for folklore, then the Kannada for a vassal king.

You'll want to know: ಸಮರ.
ಸಮರ — samara — "a battle; combat".
ಆ ಸಮರದಲ್ಲಿ ಎರಡು ಸಾಮ್ರಾಜ್ಯಗಳು ಕಾದಾಡಿದವು. ā samaradalli eraḍu sāmrājyagaḷu kādāḍidavu. In that battle two empires fought each other.
Plural: ಸಮರಗಳು, samaragaḷu.
A bookish word; ಸಮರ (samara) ಸಾರು, to declare war.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮರ (samara) is borrowed from Sanskrit samara, "coming together", and so "a hostile encounter, a battle" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಲಾವಣಿ (lāvaṇi) — a ballad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a vassal king, then the Kannada for a battle.

You'll want to know: ಲಾವಣಿ.
ಲಾವಣಿ — lāvaṇi — "a ballad".
ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಕಲಾವಿದ ಒಂದು ಹಳೆಯ ಲಾವಣಿ ಹಾಡಿದನು. haḷḷiya kalāvida ondu haḷeya lāvaṇi hāḍidanu. The village artist sang an old ballad.
Plural: ಲಾವಣಿಗಳು, lāvaṇigaḷu.
A sung story of a hero, a battle or a famous event.

The word, taken apart.
ಲಾವಣಿ (lāvaṇi) is usually traced to Marathi lāvaṇī, a kind of folk song; how the word came into Kannada is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನೀತಿ (nīti) — a moral; a principle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a battle, then the Kannada for a ballad.

You'll want to know: ನೀತಿ.
ನೀತಿ — nīti — "a moral; a principle".
ಈ ಕಥೆಯ ನೀತಿ ಏನು? ī katheya nīti ēnu? What is the moral of this story?
ನೀತಿ (nīti) ಕಥೆ is a fable. ನೀತಿ (nīti) also means a policy: ಸರ್ಕಾರದ ನೀತಿ (nīti).

The word, taken apart.
ನೀತಿ (nīti) is borrowed from Sanskrit nīti, "conduct, guidance, wise behaviour" (CDIAL 7546), from nī, "to lead".
