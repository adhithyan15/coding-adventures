নিঃশ্বাস নেওয়া (niḥshvās neoyā) — to breathe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to water, then the Bengali for to print.

You'll want to know: নিঃশ্বাস নেওয়া.
নিঃশ্বাস নেওয়া — niḥshvās neoyā — "to breathe".

The phrase, taken apart.
নিঃশ্বাস নেওয়া (niḥshvās neoyā) is niḥshvās, "breath", Sanskrit niḥśvāsa taken over whole, + neoyā, "to take", from Sanskrit nayati, "leads, carries off" (CDIAL 6966).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ভেজা (bhejā) — to get wet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ভেজা.
ভেজা — bhejā — "to get wet".

The word, taken apart.
ভেজা (bhejā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhiyajyate, "is smeared, gets wet", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9502). CDIAL writes the Bengali verb bhijā.

বোঝানো — bojhāno — "to explain".

The word, taken apart.
বোঝানো (bojhāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from budhyate, "understands" (CDIAL 9279). CDIAL lists Bengali bojhā, "to understand", and its causing form bujhāna, "to explain".
