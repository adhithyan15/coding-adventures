## You'll want to know: ਮੋਹਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C240-KAM240-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਹਲਾ** — *mohlā* — "a pestle".

> ਉੱਖਲੀ ਅਤੇ ਮੋਹਲਾ ਰਸੋਈ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਨ।
>
> *ukkhlī ate mohlā rasoī vich han.*
>
> The mortar and pestle are in the kitchen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋਹਲਾ** (*mohlā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *musala*, "pestle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *mohlā*, "pestle" (CDIAL 10223). CDIAL notes that the Sanskrit word is probably not Indo-Aryan in origin.
