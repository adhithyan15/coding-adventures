## You'll want to know: वेगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C287-ANUDHAVANA287-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वेगः** — *vegaḥ* — "speed".

> यामिकः वेगेन धावति।
>
> *yāmikaḥ vegena dhāvati.*
>
> The watchman runs at speed.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेगः** (*vegaḥ*) comes from the root √vij, "to move with a quick, darting motion"; MW gives *vega* as a violent rush and also as speed or velocity (MW).
