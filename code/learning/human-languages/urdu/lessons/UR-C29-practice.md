# Payoff — counting aloud to ten

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-FIVE, UR-REGISTER-TWO-ROADS-ONE-ROOT] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Count one to five, then say which of the two roads all five came by.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-TEN]; assesses=[UR-LEX-EK, UR-LEX-DO, UR-LEX-TEEN, UR-LEX-CHAR, UR-LEX-PANCH, UR-LEX-CHHE, UR-LEX-SAAT, UR-LEX-AATH, UR-LEX-NAU, UR-LEX-DAS, UR-ETYMON-EK-INHERITED, UR-ETYMON-DO-TWO-PIE, UR-ETYMON-TEEN-THREE-PIE, UR-ETYMON-CHAR-FOUR-PIE, UR-ETYMON-PANCH-FIVE-PIE, UR-ETYMON-CHHE-SIX-PIE, UR-ETYMON-SAAT-SEVEN-PIE, UR-ETYMON-AATH-EIGHT-PIE, UR-ETYMON-NAU-NINE-PIE, UR-ETYMON-DAS-TEN-PIE] -->

1. **Count aloud**, one to ten, without a model — the whole of it, in one
   breath if you can.
2. Count backwards from **das** to **ek**.
3. Hear all ten named singly and out of order, and say each.
4. Read the ten printed shuffled and say each.
5. Write all ten from dictation. The tranche has added **no new letter**: every
   sign in all ten words was already on the page.

Then say the English cousin of each. All ten have one.
