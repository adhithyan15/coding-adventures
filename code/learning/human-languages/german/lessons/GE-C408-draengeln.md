## You'll want to know: drängeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C408-VORFAHRT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**drängeln** — "to tailgate; to push in, to jostle".

> Hinter uns drängelt ein schwarzes Auto.
>
> *A black car is tailgating us.*

*Drängeln* is also pushing in a queue: *sich vordrängeln*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*drängeln* is built on *drängen* 'to push, to press', inherited from Old High German *drengen* and Proto-Germanic *þrangwijaną* (EtymDB). English *throng* is usually counted as a cousin.
