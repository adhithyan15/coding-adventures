## You'll want to know: سرد شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C538-APPLIANCES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سرد شدن** (*sard shodan*) — "to get cold, to cool down".

> چای من سرد شد.
>
> *chây-e man sard shod.*
>
> My tea has gone cold.

*sard* 'cold' + *shodan*. Also of feelings: *ân do az ham sard shodand* 'those two grew cold to each other'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sard shodan* is built on two inherited words: *sard*, 'cold', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *shodan*, 'to become'.
