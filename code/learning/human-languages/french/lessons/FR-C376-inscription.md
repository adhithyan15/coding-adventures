## You'll want to know: l'inscription
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C376-FAC-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'inscription** — "the registration, the enrolment".

> Les inscriptions ferment le trente septembre.
>
> *Registration closes on the thirtieth of September.*

Also words carved in stone. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inscription* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnscrīptiō* "a writing on, an inscription" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *īnscrībere* "to write on".
