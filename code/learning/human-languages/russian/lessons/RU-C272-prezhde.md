## You'll want to know: прежде
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C272-ZARANEE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**прежде** (*prézhde*) — "before, formerly".

> Прежде здесь был маленький парк.
>
> *Prézhde zdes byl málenkiy park.*
>
> Formerly there was a small park here.

*Прежде чем* + infinitive — before doing: *прежде чем уйти*. *Прежде всего* — first of all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прежде* is a Church Slavonic word, *prěžde* "before" (EtymDB), with the *-ре-* of the church language; the native Russian shape of the same root is *перед* "in front of".
