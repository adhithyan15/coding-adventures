## You'll want to know: el ladrón
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C495-NOTICIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el ladrón** — "the thief".

> La policía busca al ladrón.
>
> *The police are looking for the thief.*

A woman is *la ladrona*.
