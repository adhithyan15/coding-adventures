## You'll want to know: それ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C132-SORE]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SO-01, JA-SCRIPT-RE-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KORE, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-SOKO, JA-LEX-KOKO] -->

**それ** — *sore* — "that", a thing near the person you are talking to.

You have met both halves. **そ** points at the listener, as in **そこ**; **れ** makes
it a thing, as in **これ**. Put side by side, the four words line up:

| near you | near the listener |
|---|---|
| **ここ**, here | **そこ**, there |
| **これ**, this | **それ**, that |
