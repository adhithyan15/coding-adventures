## You'll want to know: خطر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C482-RIH482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خطر** — *khaṭar* — "danger".

> كان الخطر كبيرا على الساحل.
>
> *kāna al-khaṭar kabīran ʿalā as-sāḥil.*
>
> The danger was great on the coast.

Plural: أخطار, *akhṭār*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خطر** (*khaṭar*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-r*, "peril; to be of great weight" (Lane). It shares its root with *khaṭīr*, "dangerous".
