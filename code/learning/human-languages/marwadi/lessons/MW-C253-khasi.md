## You'll want to know: खांसी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C253-SARDI253-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खांसी** — *khā̃sī* — "a cough".

> रात मांय घणी खांसी आवै है।
>
> *rāt mā̃y ghaṇī khā̃sī āvai hai.*
>
> I cough a lot at night.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खांसी** (*khā̃sī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kāsā*, "a cough". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi and Gujarati *khā̃sī* (CDIAL 3138).
