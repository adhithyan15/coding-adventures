Gujarati, chapter 198: Signs in the street.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પાટિયું (pāṭiyũ) — signboard, plank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for closed, then the Gujarati for to open up.

You'll want to know: પાટિયું.
પાટિયું — pāṭiyũ — "signboard, plank".
દુકાનનું પાટિયું નવું છે. — dukānnũ pāṭiyũ navũ che — "The shop's signboard is new."

The word, taken apart.
પાટિયું (pāṭiyũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "plank", under Sanskrit paṭṭa, "slab, tablet" (CDIAL 7699).

પાટિયા પર સૂચના છે. — pāṭiyā par sūcnā che — "There is a notice on the board."

Lesson 3 of 5.
જાહેરાત (jāherāt) — advertisement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for signboard, then the Gujarati for announcement.

You'll want to know: જાહેરાત.
જાહેરાત — jāherāt — "advertisement".
દીવાલ પર મોટી જાહેરાત છે. — dīvāl par moṭī jāherāt che — "There is a big advert on the wall."
Built on જાહેર, jāher, 'public'.

The word, taken apart.
જાહેરાત (jāherāt) is a loan built on Arabic ẓāhir, "manifest, public", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is ẓ-h-r, "to appear" (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
મનાઈ (manāī) — ban, prohibition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for announcement, then the Gujarati for advertisement.

You'll want to know: મનાઈ.
મનાઈ — manāī — "ban, prohibition".
મંદિરમાં બૂટની મનાઈ છે. — mandirmā̃ būṭnī manāī che — "Shoes are not allowed in the temple."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મનાઈ (manāī) is a loan that reached Gujarati from Arabic through Persian, but its Arabic source is not settled. It may rest on manʿ, "prevention, forbidding", root m-n-ʿ, or on the root n-h-y, "to forbid"; Lane gives both roots (Lane).
