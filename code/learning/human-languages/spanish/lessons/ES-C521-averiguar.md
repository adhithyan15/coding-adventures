## You'll want to know: averiguar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C521-PISTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**averiguar** — "to find out".

> Quiero averiguar qué pasó.
>
> *I want to find out what happened.*

It takes *ü* before *e*: *averigüé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*averiguar* is usually traced to Late Latin *verificāre* 'to show to be true', built on *vērus* 'true'. To find out is to get at the truth.
