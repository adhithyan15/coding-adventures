## You'll want to know: اختراع کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C354-KASHF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اختراع کردن** (*ekhterâ' kardan*) — "to invent".

> او یک قفل جدید اختراع کرد.
>
> *U yek qofl-e jadid ekhterâ' kard.*
>
> He invented a new kind of lock.

Present *ekhterâ' mikonad*. *Ekhterâ'* alone is "an invention".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ekhterâ'* is borrowed from Arabic *ikhtirā'*, "invention" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
