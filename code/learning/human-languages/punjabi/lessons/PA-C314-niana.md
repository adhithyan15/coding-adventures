## You'll want to know: ਨਿਆਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C314-BABY314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਆਣਾ** — *niāṇā* — "a small child; a little one".

> ਨਿਆਣਾ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਰੋਇਆ।
>
> *niāṇā rāt nū̃ bahut roiā.*
>
> The little one cried a lot at night.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਆਣਾ** (*niāṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *na jānant*, "not knowing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *niāṇā*, "of immature judgement, young, little", under it (CDIAL 6931).
