## HL-C194 — महिला and स्त्री join the Hindi woman headword

After the parent-register repair, **महिला** is the next independent
learner-facing form in the ranked Hindi queue. Its owning lesson teaches a
three-register set, including lower-frequency **स्त्री**, so the metadata gap
is broader than a single hidden synonym.

Chapter 39 defines everyday **औरत**, respectful public **महिला**, and older
literary **स्त्री** as three Hindi words for “woman.” The register comparison
introduces a dedicated concept, and wrap-up recall asks learners which form
belongs on a public sign and which belongs in conversation. The old headword
named only **औरत**, leaving both alternatives invisible to reports and
downstream cards.

The headword is now **औरत / महिला / स्त्री**, with a gloss that names the
register of each form. This repairs metadata for an existing taught contrast
without adding vocabulary or moving its teaching point. Rerun the ranked report
next and continue the evidence-first audit after the explicitly classified
script, morphology, and historical-source rows.
