## You'll want to know: la pescheria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C454-ALIMENTARI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la pescheria** — "the fishmonger's".

> La pescheria apre presto la mattina.
>
> *The fishmonger's opens early in the morning.*

*Il pescivendolo* — the fishmonger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pescheria* is built on *pesce* 'fish', which is inherited from Latin *piscis* (EtymDB).
