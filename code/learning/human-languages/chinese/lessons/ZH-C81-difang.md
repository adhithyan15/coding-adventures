## You'll want to know: dìfang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C81-CITY81-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dìfang* — "place".

> *zhège dìfang hěn hǎo* — This is a nice place.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìfang* is built from dì 'land' and fāng 'quarter, region', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *pjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
