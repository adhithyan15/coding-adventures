# よこぎる (yokogiru) — to cut across; to cross (a road)

## You'll want to know: よこぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C201-ROAD201-03]; assesses=[] -->

**よこぎる** — *yokogiru* — "to cut across; to cross (a road)".

> **こどもが どうろを よこぎります。** — *kodomo ga dōro o yokogirimasu* — "A child cuts across the road."

Polite **よこぎります**, *yokogirimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よこぎる** (*yokogiru*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *yoko*, "side", and *kiru*, "to cut", both inherited rather than borrowed; the *k* is voiced to *g* (JMdict).
