## You'll want to know: ફૂટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C443-TREE443-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂટવું** — *phūṭvũ* — "to sprout; to burst out".

> વસંતમાં ઝાડ પર નવાં પાન ફૂટે છે.
>
> *vasantmā̃ jhāḍ par navā̃ pān phūṭe che.*
>
> In spring new leaves sprout on the trees.

Past (it …): તે પાન ફૂટ્યું, *te pān phūṭyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂટવું** (*phūṭvũ*) goes back to *\*sphuṭyati*, "splits open", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to split, to burst" (CDIAL 13845): a bud that bursts into leaf.
