## You'll want to know: うかぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C303-RYUU303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うかぶ** — *ukabu* — "to float up; to come to mind".

> **ぬまに とりが うかんでいます。** — *numa ni tori ga ukande imasu* — "Birds are floating on the marsh."

Polite **うかびます**, *ukabimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ふねが うかびました。**, *fune ga ukabimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うかぶ** (*ukabu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
