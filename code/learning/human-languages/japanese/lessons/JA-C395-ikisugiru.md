# いきすぎる (ikisugiru) — to go past one's turning; to go too far

## You'll want to know: いきすぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C395-KOSHOU395-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いきすぎる** — *ikisugiru* — "to go past one's turning; to go too far".

> **しんごうを いきすぎて、みちに まよいました。** — *shingō o ikisugite, michi ni mayoimashita* — "I went past the traffic light and got lost."

Polite **いきすぎます**, *ikisugimasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが かどを いきすぎました。**, *chichi ga kado o ikisugimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いきすぎる** (*ikisugiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *iki*, "going" (from *iku*) and *sugiru*, "to go beyond"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
