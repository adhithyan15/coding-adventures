Telugu, chapter 453: On the Water.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ఒడ్డు (oḍḍu) — a riverbank; a shore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an anchor, then the Telugu for a lighthouse.

You'll want to know: ఒడ్డు.
ఒడ్డు — oḍḍu — "a riverbank; a shore".
పిల్లలు నది ఒడ్డున ఆడుకుంటున్నారు. pillalu nadi oḍḍuna āḍukuṇṭunnāru. The children are playing on the riverbank.
ఒడ్డున, on the bank, is the usual form. The bank of a pond, a river or a sea.

The word, taken apart.
ఒడ్డు (oḍḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 965 lists Telugu oḍḍu, "a bank", with Kannada oḍḍu, "a bank, a causeway".

Lesson 2 of 6.
వాగు (vāgu) — a rivulet; a stream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a lighthouse, then the Telugu for a riverbank.

You'll want to know: వాగు.
వాగు — vāgu — "a rivulet; a stream".
వర్షాకాలంలో ఆ వాగు నిండుగా పారుతుంది. varṣākālaṁlō ā vāgu niṇḍugā pārutundi. In the rainy season that stream runs full.
A small stream that fills in the rains and dries in summer.

The word, taken apart.
వాగు (vāgu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5334 lists Telugu vāgu, "a rivulet, a streamlet", in the family of Tamil vākku, "to pour".

Lesson 4 of 6.
తేలిపోవు (tēlipōvu) — to float away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a rivulet, then the Telugu for a raft.

You'll want to know: తేలిపోవు.
తేలిపోవు — tēlipōvu — "to float away".
గాలికి నా టోపీ నీటిలో తేలిపోయింది. gāliki nā ṭōpī nīṭilō tēlipōyindi. In the wind my cap floated away on the water.
Past (it …): తేలిపోయింది, tēlipōyindi.
తేలు, to float, + పోవు, to go: carried off on the surface. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: తేలిపోయింది, it floated away.

The word, taken apart.
తేలిపోవు (tēlipōvu) is native Dravidian: తేలు, "to float" (DEDR 3464), with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 5 of 6.
కొట్టుకుపోవు (koṭṭukupōvu) — to be swept away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a raft, then the Telugu for to float away.

You'll want to know: కొట్టుకుపోవు.
కొట్టుకుపోవు — koṭṭukupōvu — "to be swept away".
వరదలో చాలా పశువులు కొట్టుకుపోయాయి. varadalō cālā paśuvulu koṭṭukupōyāyi. Many cattle were swept away in the flood.
Past (it …): కొట్టుకుపోయింది, koṭṭukupōyindi.
Said of anything a current carries off: a bridge, a road, a crop. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: కొట్టుకుపోయింది, it was swept away.

The word, taken apart.
కొట్టుకుపోవు (koṭṭukupōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2065 lists Telugu koṭṭukonipōvu, "to be carried along by a stream or a current", from koṭṭu, "to pour forth".
