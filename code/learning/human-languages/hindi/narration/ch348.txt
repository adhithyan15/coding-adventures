Hindi, chapter 348: Proof.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रमाण (pramāṇ) — proof (formal), evidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to support, then the Hindi for to agree.

You'll want to know: प्रमाण.
प्रमाण — pramāṇ — "proof (formal), evidence".
तुम्हारे पास क्या प्रमाण है? tumhāre pās kyā pramāṇ hai? What evidence do you have?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रमाण (pramāṇ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit pramāṇa, "a measure, a standard; proof" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
साबित करना (sābit karnā) — to prove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to agree, then the Hindi for proof (formal).

You'll want to know: साबित करना.
साबित करना — sābit karnā — "to prove".
मीरा ने अपनी बात साबित की। Mīrā ne apnī bāt sābit kī. Mira proved her point.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपनी बात साबित की, usne apnī bāt sābit kī.

The word, taken apart.
साबित करना (sābit karnā) joins साबित to करना, "to do". साबित is usually traced to Arabic thābit, "firm, established", which reached Hindi through Persian.
