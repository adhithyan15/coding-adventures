## You'll want to know: 雨衣
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C234-RAIN234-04]; assesses=[] -->

**雨衣** — *yǔyī* — "raincoat".

> **下雨了风也很大我有雨衣** *xiàyǔ le fēng yě hěn dà wǒ yǒu yǔyī* — It's raining and it's windy too; good thing I've got a raincoat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**雨衣** (*yǔyī*) is built from 雨 'rain' and 衣 'garment', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuX* and *'j+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
