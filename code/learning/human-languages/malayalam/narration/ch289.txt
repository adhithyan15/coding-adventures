Malayalam, chapter 289: Boy, Aunt, Niece, Mother-in-law, Father-in-law.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ആൺകുട്ടി (āṇkuṭṭi) — a boy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an amount, then the Malayalam for a form.

You'll want to know: ആൺകുട്ടി.
ആൺകുട്ടി — āṇkuṭṭi — "a boy".

The word, taken apart.
ആൺകുട്ടി (āṇkuṭṭi) is native Dravidian, "male child": āṇ, "male" (DEDR 399, with Tamil āḷ), and kuṭṭi, "child" (DEDR 1670).
