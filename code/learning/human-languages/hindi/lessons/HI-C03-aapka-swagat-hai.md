# आपका स्वागत है (āpkā svāgat hai) — "you're welcome"

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] When someone thanks you, this is what you say back — and it hides a
word you may meet again in Sanskrit.

## The letters in this word

The key word is **स्वागत** (*svāgat*): the conjunct **स्व** (**स्** *s* +
**व** *va* → *sva*) + **ग** (*ga*) + **त** (*ta*). Add **आपका** (*āpkā*, "your,"
from Chapter 2) and **है** (*hai*, "is").

## The phrase, taken apart

**आपका स्वागत है** = "your welcome is" → "**you are welcome**." The heart of it,
**स्वागत** (*svāgat*, "welcome"), is Sanskrit **सु** (*su-*, "well, good") +
**आगत** (*āgata*, "come") — literally "**well come**," exactly like English
*welcome*. Two deep roots hide inside:

- *su-* "good" is the twin of Greek **eu-** (*euphoria*, *eulogy*).
- *āgata* is from the root **गम्** (*gam*, "to go/come"), cousin of English
  **come**.

You will meet *svāgatam* again, unchanged, in the Sanskrit track — this is the
same word, worn into everyday Hindi.
