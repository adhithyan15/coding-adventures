Russian, chapter 335: Cooling and thawing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
остывать (ostyvát) — to cool down, to get cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a colander, then the Russian for a sieve.

You'll want to know: остывать.
остывать (ostyvát) — "to cool down, to get cold".
Чай уже остыл. Chay uzhé ostýl. The tea has already gone cold.
Imperfective; perfective остыть (ostýt), остынет.

The word, taken apart.
остывать (ostyvát) is built on о- and стыть "to grow cold", the Slavic root of студ "cold" and простуда "a cold"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
переворачивать (perevoráchivat) — to turn over, to flip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to chill, then the Russian for to melt.

You'll want to know: переворачивать.
переворачивать (perevoráchivat) — "to turn over, to flip".
Он переворачивает блины. On perevoráchivayet bliný. He flips the pancakes.
Imperfective; perfective перевернуть (perevernút).

The word, taken apart.
переворачивать (perevoráchivat) is the imperfective partner of перевернуть "to turn over"; its stem -ворач- belongs with воротить "to turn back", Proto-Slavic vortiti, and -вернуть with вертеть "to turn", Proto-Slavic vĭrtěti, PIE wert- (EtymDB); the two are usually seen as grades of one root.
