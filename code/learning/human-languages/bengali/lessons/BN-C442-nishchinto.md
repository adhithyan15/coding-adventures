# নিশ্চিন্ত (nishchintô) — free of worry; at ease

## You'll want to know: নিশ্চিন্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C442-UDBEG442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**নিশ্চিন্ত** — *nishchintô* — "free of worry; at ease".

> ফলাফল দেখে বাবা নিশ্চিন্ত হলেন।
>
> *phôlāphôl dekhe bābā nishchintô hôlen.*
>
> Seeing the result, my father stopped worrying.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নিশ্চিন্ত** (*nishchintô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *niścinta*, "unconcerned, careless" (Monier-Williams): *niḥ-* "without" on *cintā*, the চিন্তা (taught) that means both a thought and a worry.
