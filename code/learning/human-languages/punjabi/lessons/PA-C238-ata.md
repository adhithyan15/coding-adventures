## You'll want to know: ਆਟਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C238-KAM238-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਆਟਾ** — *āṭā* — "flour".

> ਆਟਾ ਡੱਬੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *āṭā ḍabbe vich hai.*
>
> The flour is in the box.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਆਟਾ** (*āṭā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ārta*, "flour"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *āṭṭā*, "flour" (CDIAL 1338).
