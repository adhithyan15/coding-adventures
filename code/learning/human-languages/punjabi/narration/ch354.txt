Punjabi, chapter 354: Once Upon a Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਹਾਣੀ (kahāṇī) — a story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to make something fly, then the Punjabi for to play with a child.

You'll want to know: ਕਹਾਣੀ.
ਕਹਾਣੀ — kahāṇī — "a story".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੁਰਾਣੀਆਂ ਕਹਾਣੀਆਂ ਪਸੰਦ ਹਨ। mainū̃ purāṇīāṁ kahāṇīāṁ pasand han. I like old stories.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਹਾਣੀ (kahāṇī) is inherited from Sanskrit kathānaka, "a little tale"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kahāṇī under it (CDIAL 2705).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਸੁਣਾਉਣਾ (suṇāuṇā) — to tell a story; to recite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to play with a child, then the Punjabi for a story.

You'll want to know: ਸੁਣਾਉਣਾ.
ਸੁਣਾਉਣਾ — suṇāuṇā — "to tell a story; to recite".
ਦਾਦੀ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਕਹਾਣੀ ਸੁਣਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। dādī rāt nū̃ kahāṇī suṇāundī hai. Grandmother tells a story at night.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਕਹਾਣੀ ਸੁਣਾਈ, maiṁ ikk kahāṇī suṇāī.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁਣਾਉਣਾ (suṇāuṇā) is the causative partner of suṇnā, "to hear", which goes back to Sanskrit śṛṇoti, "hears"; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Lahnda suṇāvaṇ, "informs", under it (CDIAL 12598).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a story, then the Punjabi for to tell a story.

You'll want to know: ਕਿੱਸਾ.
ਕਿੱਸਾ — kissā — "a tale; a long story".
ਦਾਦਾ ਇੱਕ ਲੰਮਾ ਕਿੱਸਾ ਸੁਣਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। dādā ikk lammā kissā suṇāundā hai. Grandfather tells a long tale.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਿੱਸਾ (kissā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic qiṣṣa, "story".
