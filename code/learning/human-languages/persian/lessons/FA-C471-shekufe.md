## You'll want to know: شکوفه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C471-SHEKAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شکوفه** (*shekufe*) — "a blossom".

> در بهار درختهای سیب پر از شکوفه است.
>
> *Dar bahâr derakht-hâ-ye sib por az shekufe ast.*
>
> In spring the apple trees are full of blossom.

Built on the stem of *shekoftan* 'to bloom'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shekufe* is a native word built on the stem of *shekoftan* 'to bloom, to open'. EtymDB does not list it.
