## You'll want to know: flūctus, flūctūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C443-FLUCTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**flūctus, flūctūs** — "a billow, a wave; a surge".

> Magnī flūctūs in lītus cadunt.
>
> *Great waves fall on the shore.*

Fourth declension, masculine: *flūctus, flūctūs m.* A bigger word than *unda*: the wave as it rolls and breaks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flūctus* is built on *fluō* 'to flow' with the noun suffix *-tus* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): a flowing, a surge of water.
