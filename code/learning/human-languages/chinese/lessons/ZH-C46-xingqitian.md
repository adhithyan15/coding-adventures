## You'll want to know: 星期天
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-05]; assesses=[] -->

**星期天** — *xīngqītiān* — "Sunday".

> **我喜欢星期天** *wǒ xǐhuan xīngqītiān* — Sunday is the day I like best.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**星期天** (*xīngqītiān*) is built from 星期 'week' and 天 'day'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
