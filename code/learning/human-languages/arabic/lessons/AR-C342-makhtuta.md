## You'll want to know: مخطوطة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C342-TURATH342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مخطوطة** — *makhṭūṭa* — "a manuscript".

> وجد الباحث مخطوطة قديمة في المكتبة.
>
> *wajada al-bāḥith makhṭūṭa qadīma fī al-maktaba.*
>
> The researcher found an old manuscript in the library.

Plural مخطوطات.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخطوطة** (*makhṭūṭa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-ṭ* (Lane), "to draw a line; to write", the root of *khaṭṭ*, handwriting. *Makhṭūṭa* is "a thing written by hand".
