# Review — how it felt, and what comes next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-COUNTER-QUESTIONS-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-ASK-HELP-01, AR-SCRIPT-C288-HAMZA-YAA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-PROBLEM-PAST-01, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Buy a return ticket and ask the price. (*Tadhkirat dhahāb
wa-iyāb, min faḍlik. Bi-kam at-tadhkira?*) Then report a problem and ask for
the person in charge. (*Fātanī al-qiṭār. Urīdu an atakallama maʿa al-masʾūl*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C289-KANA-ACCUSATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-SHAARTU-BI-01, AR-LEX-C289-TAJRIBA289-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-WISH-AN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-BECOME-01] -->

Three lessons, and an experience can be told from both ends. Looking back:
*kuntu saʿīdan*, *kānat ḥazīna*, *shaʿartu bi-l-khawf*, *kānat tajribatan
jadīda*. Looking ahead: *atamannā an*, *aḥlumu bi-*, *urīdu an uṣbiḥa*.

[PAUSE 2s] Both ends in one breath:

> **كانت تجربة صعبة، لكن أتمنى أن أسافر قريبا.** — *kānat tajribatan
> ṣaʿba, lākin atamannā an usāfira qarīban* — It was a hard
> experience, but I hope to travel again soon.

- [YOU SAY: "I told my grandmother how the trip was" — *ḥakaytu li-jaddatī kayfa kānat ar-riḥla*]
- [YOU SAY: how your last trip felt, and one thing you hope for next time]
