## You'll want to know: कपास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C239-CROP239-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कपास** — *kapās* — "cotton (the plant)".

> हूं कपास बेचूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ kapās bechū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I sell cotton.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कपास** (*kapās*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karpāsa*, "the cotton plant", which CDIAL takes, perhaps, from Austroasiatic; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *kapās*. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2877).
