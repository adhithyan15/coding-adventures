Russian, chapter 453: Trying things on.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
примерочная (primérochnaya) — a fitting room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a shop assistant, then the Russian for to weigh.

You'll want to know: примерочная.
примерочная (primérochnaya) — "a fitting room".
Примерочная слева от кассы. Primérochnaya sléva ot kássy. The fitting room is to the left of the till.
An adjective used as a noun.

The word, taken apart.
примерочная (primérochnaya) is built on примерка 'a fitting', from примерять (primeryát) 'to try on', on мера 'a measure'. EtymDB builds мерить on Proto-Slavic měra.

размер (razmér) — "a size".
Эти брюки мне не подошли по размеру. Éti bryúki mne ne podoshlí po razméru. These trousers were not my size.
Masculine. Какой у вас размер (razmér)? — what size are you?

The word, taken apart.
размер (razmér) is built from раз- and мера 'a measure'. EtymDB lists размер (razmér) without a deeper source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
жать (zhat) — to pinch, to be too tight; to press.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to try on, then the Russian for a size.

You'll want to know: жать.
жать (zhat) — "to pinch, to be too tight; to press".
Новые туфли немного жмут. Nóvyye túfli nemnógo zhmut. The new shoes pinch a little.
Imperfective. Present жму, жмёшь, жмут. Жать руку — to shake hands. A different verb жать (zhat) (жну) means to reap.

The word, taken apart.
жать (zhat) 'to press, to squeeze' is usually counted an inherited Slavic verb. EtymDB lists it without a source, so its deeper origin is left open here.

Lesson 5 of 5.
обменивать (obménivat) — to exchange (goods), to swap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a size, then the Russian for to pinch.

You'll want to know: обменивать.
обменивать (obménivat) — "to exchange (goods), to swap".
В этом магазине легко обменивают товар. V étom magazíne legkó obménivayut továr. In this shop they exchange goods without any fuss.
Imperfective; perfective обменять. Takes на + accusative: обменять свитер на другой размер (razmér).

The word, taken apart.
обменивать (obménivat) is built from об- and -менивать, from менять 'to change', on мена 'an exchange', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic měna (EtymDB).
