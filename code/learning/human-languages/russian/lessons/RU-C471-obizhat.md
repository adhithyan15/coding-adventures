## You'll want to know: обижать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C471-HURT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**обижать** (*obizhát*) — "to hurt (someone's feelings)".

> Старший брат никогда не обижал младшего.
>
> *Stárshiy brat nikogdá ne obizhál mládshego.*
>
> The older brother never picked on the younger one.

Imperfective; perfective обидеть. Takes the accusative: *обижать младшего*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обижать* is the imperfective partner of *обидеть* 'to offend' (EtymDB lists both without a source); they share the root of *обида*, from Proto-Slavic *obvida* (EtymDB).
