## You'll want to know: ਪਰਹੇਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C325-BETTER325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਹੇਜ਼** — *parhez* — "keeping off certain food".

> ਡਾਕਟਰ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ ਕਿ ਮਿੱਠੇ ਤੋਂ ਪਰਹੇਜ਼ ਕਰੋ।
>
> *ḍākṭar kahindā hai ki miṭṭhe ton parhez karo.*
>
> The doctor says to keep off sweet things.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਹੇਜ਼** (*parhez*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *parhez*, "abstinence".
