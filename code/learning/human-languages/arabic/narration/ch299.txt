Arabic, chapter 299: Meanwhile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
في هذه الأثناء (fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ) — meanwhile, in the meantime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to burn down, then the Arabic for a firefighter.

You'll want to know: في هذه الأثناء.
في هذه الأثناء — fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ — "meanwhile, in the meantime".
في هذه الأثناء كان أخي ينتظر في السيارة. fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ kāna akhī yantaẓiru fī as-sayyāra. Meanwhile my brother was waiting in the car.
With كان and the present it sets a second scene beside the first.

The word, taken apart.
في هذه الأثناء (fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ) is "in these folds": athnāʾ is a plural built on the native Arabic root th-n-y, "to fold, to double" (Lane), the root of ithnān, two.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Arabic for a firefighter, then the Arabic for meanwhile.

You'll want to know: فيما بعد.
فيما بعد — fīmā baʿd — "later on, afterwards".
عرفت السبب فيما بعد. ʿaraftu as-sabab fīmā baʿd. I found out the reason later on.

The word, taken apart.
فيما بعد (fīmā baʿd) is fī, "in", mā, "what", and baʿd, "after", which is built on the native Arabic root b-ʿ-d, "to be far" (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
في اليوم التالي (fī al-yawm at-tālī) — the next day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for meanwhile, then the Arabic for later on.

You'll want to know: في اليوم التالي.
في اليوم التالي — fī al-yawm at-tālī — "the next day".
في اليوم التالي عاد المسافر إلى بيته. fī al-yawm at-tālī ʿāda al-musāfir ilā baytihi. The next day the traveller went back home.
At night: في الليلة التالية, the following night.

The word, taken apart.
في اليوم التالي (fī al-yawm at-tālī) is "on the following day": tālī is the participle of talā, "to follow", built on the native Arabic root t-l-w (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ذكر (dhakara) — to mention; to remember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for later on, then the Arabic for the next day.

You'll want to know: ذكر.
ذكر — dhakara — "to mention; to remember".
ذكر المراسل اسم القرية. dhakara al-murāsil ism al-qarya. The correspondent mentioned the name of the village.
Present: يذكر, yadhkur.
The taught ذكرى, a memory, is the same root.

The word, taken apart.
ذكر (dhakara) is usually traced to the native Arabic root dh-k-r, which joins keeping a thing in mind and saying it aloud: dhikrā, a memory, and tadhakkara, to recall, share it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نقل (naqala) — to pass on, to report; to carry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the next day, then the Arabic for to mention.

You'll want to know: نقل.
نقل — naqala — "to pass on, to report; to carry".
نقلت الصحيفة كلام المدير. naqalat aṣ-ṣaḥīfa kalām al-mudīr. The newspaper reported what the manager said.
Present: ينقل, yanqul.
It also moves people and goods: نقل (naqala) الأثاث, to move the furniture.

The word, taken apart.
نقل (naqala) is built on the native Arabic root n-q-l (Lane), "to move a thing from one place to another". Words moved from one mouth to another are reported.
