# ਯਾਤਰਾ — journey or travel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-YA-01, PA-TRAVEL-TICKET-01, PA-TRAVEL-RAIL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Retrieve **ਰੇਲ ਦੀ ਟਿਕਟ**, then point to **ਯ** and say *ya*.

## Grammar Lens: one journey word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-YA-01, PA-TRAVEL-RAIL-01] -->

> **ਯਾਤਰਾ** — *yātrā* — journey; travel

> **ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਯਾਤਰਾ** — *rel nāl yātrā* — travel by train

Treat the second line as one useful travel chunk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਯਾਤਰਾ** (*yātrā*) is Sanskrit *yātrā*, “journey, procession,” taken over as a learned loan.
