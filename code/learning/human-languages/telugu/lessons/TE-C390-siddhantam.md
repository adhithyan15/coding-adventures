## You'll want to know: సిద్ధాంతం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C390-SIDDHANTAM390-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సిద్ధాంతం** — *siddhāntaṁ* — "a theory; a doctrine".

> ఆ సిద్ధాంతాన్ని ఇంకా ఎవరూ నిరూపించలేదు.
>
> *ā siddhāntānni iṅkā evarū nirūpiñcalēdu.*
>
> No one has proved that theory yet.

Plural: సిద్ధాంతాలు, *siddhāntālu*.

Also a principle a person lives by.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సిద్ధాంతం** (*siddhāntaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *siddhānta*, "established end, doctrine" (CDIAL 13404), from *siddha*, "established", and *anta*, "end".

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
