## You'll want to know: वर्धयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C379-ADHIKA379-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्धयति** — *vardhayati* — "increases (something)".

> यदि वणिक् मूल्यं वर्धयति, तर्हि जनाः अल्पं क्रीणन्ति।
>
> *yadi vaṇik mūlyaṃ vardhayati, tarhi janāḥ alpaṃ krīṇanti.*
>
> If the merchant raises the price, people buy less.

Past (he …): सः मूल्यं वर्धितवान्, *saḥ mūlyaṃ vardhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्धयति** (*vardhayati*) comes from the root √vṛdh, "to grow", in its causative form; MW gives *vardhita* "increased, grown, augmented" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
