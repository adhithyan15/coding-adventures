## You'll want to know: vetustās, vetustātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C344-MAIORES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vetustās, vetustātis** — "great age, antiquity".

> Templum vetustāte collāpsum erat.
>
> *The temple had fallen in from age.*

Feminine, 3rd declension, from *vetus* (taught). *Vetustāte*: through long age.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vetustās** is built on *vetustus*, "aged", from *vetus*, "old" (Lewis & Short): long existence. English *veteran* belongs to the same family.
