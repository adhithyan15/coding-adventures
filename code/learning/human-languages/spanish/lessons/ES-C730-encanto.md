## You'll want to know: el encanto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C730-ENCANTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el encanto** — "charm".

> El pueblo tiene mucho encanto.
>
> *The village has a lot of charm.*

*Es un encanto* — he or she is a sweetheart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*encanto* is built on *encantar* "to enchant", from Latin *incantāre* (EtymDB); charm was first a spell.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
