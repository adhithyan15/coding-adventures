## You'll want to know: ਪਹੁ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C285-HOME285-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੁ** — *pahu* — "dawn; first light".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਪਹੁ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਘਰ ਪਹੁੰਚਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ pahu ton pahilāṁ ghar pahunchāṅge.*
>
> We will reach home before dawn.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੁ** (*pahu*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prabhā*, "light"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pauh*, "dawn", under it (CDIAL 8705).
