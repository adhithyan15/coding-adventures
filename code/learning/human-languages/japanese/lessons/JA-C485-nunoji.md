## You'll want to know: ぬのじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C485-CLOTH485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬのじ** — *nunoji* — "fabric; dress material".

> **この ぬのじで ふくを つくります。** — *kono nunoji de fuku o tsukurimasu* — "I make clothes from this fabric."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬのじ** (*nunoji*) mixes the two layers, native and Sino-Japanese: native *nuno*, "linen" and Sino-Japanese *ji*, "ground" (Middle Chinese *dijH*), borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
