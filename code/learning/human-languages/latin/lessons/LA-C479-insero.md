## You'll want to know: īnserō, īnserere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C479-POMARIUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**īnserō, īnserere** — "to graft, to plant in".

> Agricola rāmum pirī in mālum īnserit.
>
> *The farmer grafts a pear branch onto an apple tree.*

*īnserō, īnserere, īnsēvī, īnsitum*, from *serō, serere, sēvī* 'to sow'. A different verb *īnserō, īnserere, īnseruī, īnsertum* 'to put in' gives English *insert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*īnserō* 'to graft' is built from *in-* 'in' and *serō, serere, sēvī* 'to sow, to plant' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). It must be kept apart from *īnserō* 'to put in', from *serō, seruī* 'to join'.
