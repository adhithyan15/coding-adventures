# સંભળાવું (sambhḷāvũ) — to be heard; to be audible

## You'll want to know: સંભળાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C399-DELAY399-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભળાવું** — *sambhḷāvũ* — "to be heard; to be audible".

> દૂરથી ટ્રેનની સીટી સંભળાય છે.
>
> *dūrthī ṭrennī sīṭī sambhḷāy che.*
>
> The train's whistle can be heard from far away.

Past (it …): તે અવાજ સંભળાયો, *te avāj sambhḷāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભળાવું** (*sambhḷāvũ*) is the intransitive partner of સાંભળવું, "to listen, to hear", which goes back to *\*saṁbhalati*, "attends to", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 12962): what you hear, સાંભળવું; what can be heard, સંભળાવું.
