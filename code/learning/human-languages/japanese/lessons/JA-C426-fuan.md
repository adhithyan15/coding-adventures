## You'll want to know: ふあん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C426-FEEL426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふあん** — *fuan* — "anxious; uneasy".

> **しけんの まえは ふあんでした。** — *shiken no mae wa fuan deshita* — "I felt uneasy before the exam."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふあん** (*fuan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fu*, "negative" (Middle Chinese *pjuw*) and *an*, "peaceful" (*'an*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
