## You'll want to know: le garrot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C300-SECOURS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le garrot** — "the tourniquet".

> Un médecin a posé un garrot sur sa jambe.
>
> *A doctor put a tourniquet on his leg.*

Also the withers of a horse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *garrot* is not settled; it is sometimes linked to Provençal *garra* "leg" (EtymDB).
