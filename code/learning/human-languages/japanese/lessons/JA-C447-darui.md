## You'll want to know: だるい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C447-TIRED447-03]; assesses=[] -->

**だるい** — *darui* — "sluggish; listless".

> **かぜで からだが だるいです。** — *kaze de karada ga darui desu* — "My cold makes my body feel sluggish."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だるい** (*darui*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
