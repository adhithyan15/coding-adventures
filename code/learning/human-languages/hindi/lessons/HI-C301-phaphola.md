## You'll want to know: फफोला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C301-BUJHI301-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फफोला** — *phapholā* — "a blister".

> आग से उसके हाथ पर फफोले पड़ गए।
>
> *āg se uske hāth par phaphole paṛ gae.*
>
> The fire left blisters on his hand.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फफोला** (*phapholā*) is inherited from *\*prasphōṭa*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8883), which gathers words for bubbles and blisters. Blisters **पड़ते हैं**: they come up on the skin.
