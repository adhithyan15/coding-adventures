Arabic, chapter 525: Design and code.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أنتج (antaja) — to produce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to be promoted, then the Arabic for headquarters.

You'll want to know: أنتج.
أنتج — antaja — "to produce".
ينتج المصنع ألف سيارة كل شهر. yuntiju al-maṣnaʿ alf sayyāra kull shahr. The factory produces a thousand cars every month.
Present: ينتج, yuntij.

The word, taken apart.
أنتج (antaja) is built on the native Arabic root n-t-j, "to bring forth young" (Lane). Natīja, a result, which you know, is from the same root: what a thing brings forth.

Lesson 2 of 5.
صمم (ṣammama) — to design.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for headquarters, then the Arabic for to produce.

You'll want to know: صمم.
صمم — ṣammama — "to design".
صممت أختي فستان العروس. ṣammamat ukhtī fustān al-ʿarūs. My sister designed the bride's dress.
Present: يصمم, yuṣammim.
With على it means to be determined: صمم (ṣammama) على السفر.

The word, taken apart.
صمم (ṣammama) is usually traced to the native Arabic root ṣ-m-m, "to be solid, closed up". Its older sense is to be firmly resolved; the sense of designing is modern.

Lesson 4 of 5.
برمج (barmaja) — to program (a computer).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to design, then the Arabic for a designer.

You'll want to know: برمج.
برمج — barmaja — "to program (a computer)".
برمج أخي لعبة على الحاسوب. barmaja akhī luʿba ʿalā al-ḥāsūb. My brother programmed a game on the computer.
Present: يبرمج, yubarmij.

The word, taken apart.
برمج (barmaja) is a modern Arabic verb built from barnāmaj, "a programme", which you know; barnāmaj is usually traced to Persian barnāma. Arabic made a new four-letter root b-r-m-j from it.
