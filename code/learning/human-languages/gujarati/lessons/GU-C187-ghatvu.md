## You'll want to know: ઘટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C187-PRICE187-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘટવું** — *ghaṭvũ* — "to go down, to decrease".

> **શિયાળામાં શાકનો ભાવ ઘટે છે.** — *śiyāḷāmā̃ śākno bhāv ghaṭe che* — "In winter the price of vegetables goes down."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘટવું** (*ghaṭvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*ghaṭṭati*, "decreases, is wanting", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to decrease" (CDIAL 4415).
