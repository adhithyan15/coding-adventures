Telugu, chapter 388: Doubt and Certainty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సందేహం (sandēhaṁ) — a misgiving; a doubt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to comment, then the Telugu for to find fault with.

You'll want to know: సందేహం.
సందేహం — sandēhaṁ — "a misgiving; a doubt".
నాకు ఒక చిన్న సందేహం ఉంది. nāku oka cinna sandēhaṁ undi. I have a small doubt.
Plural: సందేహాలు, sandēhālu.
In class it is the polite word for a question you are unsure about. అనుమానం, which you know, leans to suspicion.

The word, taken apart.
సందేహం (sandēhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁdeha, "doubt, uncertainty" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
సందేహించు (sandēhiñcu) — to have misgivings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to find fault with, then the Telugu for a misgiving.

You'll want to know: సందేహించు.
సందేహించు — sandēhiñcu — "to have misgivings".
అతను నిజం చెప్పాడా అని నేను సందేహించాను. atanu nijaṁ ceppāḍā ani nēnu sandēhiñcānu. I doubted whether he had told the truth.
Past (I …): సందేహించాను, sandēhiñcānu.
What you doubt is often a question with అని: చెప్పాడా అని సందేహించు (sandēhiñcu).

The word, taken apart.
సందేహించు (sandēhiñcu) is built on Sanskrit saṁdeha, "doubt" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; the noun is సందేహం (sandēhaṁ).

Lesson 3 of 5.
విశ్వసించు (viśvasiñcu) — to believe in; to have faith in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a misgiving, then the Telugu for to have misgivings.

You'll want to know: విశ్వసించు.
విశ్వసించు — viśvasiñcu — "to believe in; to have faith in".
నేను నా స్నేహితులను పూర్తిగా విశ్వసిస్తాను. nēnu nā snēhitulanu pūrtigā viśvasistānu. I trust my friends completely.
Past (I …): విశ్వసించాను, viśvasiñcānu.
Formal beside నమ్ము. The noun is విశ్వాసం, trust.

The word, taken apart.
విశ్వసించు (viśvasiñcu) is built on Sanskrit viśvāsa, "trust, confidence" (CDIAL 11966), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 5.
నిశ్చయం (niścayaṁ) — certainty; a firm resolve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to have misgivings, then the Telugu for to believe in.

You'll want to know: నిశ్చయం.
నిశ్చయం — niścayaṁ — "certainty; a firm resolve".
అతను వస్తాడని నాకు నిశ్చయం లేదు. atanu vastāḍani nāku niścayaṁ lēdu. I am not certain that he will come.
A family word too: పెళ్ళి నిశ్చయం (niścayaṁ), the fixing of a wedding.

The word, taken apart.
నిశ్చయం (niścayaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit niścaya, "inquiry, conviction" (CDIAL 7439), which Monier-Williams also glosses "certainty; a fixed resolve".

Lesson 5 of 5.
అనిశ్చితి (aniściti) — uncertainty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to believe in, then the Telugu for certainty.

You'll want to know: అనిశ్చితి.
అనిశ్చితి — aniściti — "uncertainty".
ఉద్యోగాల విషయంలో చాలా అనిశ్చితి ఉంది. udyōgāla viṣayaṁlō cālā aniściti undi. There is a lot of uncertainty about jobs.
అ- means not: అనిశ్చితి (aniściti) is the lack of నిశ్చయం (niścayaṁ).

The word, taken apart.
అనిశ్చితి (aniściti) is built from Sanskrit a-, "not", and niściti, "ascertainment, settling" (Monier-Williams), a word of the same family as నిశ్చయం (niścayaṁ).
