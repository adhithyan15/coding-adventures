## You'll want to know: తరం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C357-VAMSAM357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**తరం** — *taraṁ* — "a generation".

> మా తరం పిల్లలు టీవీ చూస్తూ పెరిగారు.
>
> *mā taraṁ pillalu ṭīvī cūstū perigāru.*
>
> The children of our generation grew up watching TV.

Plural: తరాలు, *tarālu*.

తరతరాలుగా, for generations.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తరం** (*taraṁ*) is the Telugu word for a generation; its origin is not settled.
