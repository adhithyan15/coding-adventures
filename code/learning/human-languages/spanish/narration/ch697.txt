Spanish, chapter 697: Lluvia.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
escampar — to stop raining, to clear up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for humid, then the Spanish for the humidity.

You'll want to know: escampar.
escampar — "to stop raining, to clear up".
Esperamos en el bar hasta que escampó. We waited in the bar until it stopped raining.
Ya ha escampado — it has stopped raining.

The word, taken apart.
escampar is usually explained as es- and campo "field", inherited from Latin campus: first to clear a field, then for the sky to clear.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: el chubasco.
el chubasco — "the shower (of rain)".
Por la tarde habrá chubascos. There will be showers in the afternoon.
A forecast word for a short heavy shower.

The word, taken apart.
chubasco comes from Portuguese chuvasco, built on chuva "rain" (EtymDB), from Latin pluvia.

la llovizna — "the drizzle".
