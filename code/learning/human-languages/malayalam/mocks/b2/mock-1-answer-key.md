## Writing

Task 1 must address Leena and integrate a position, two pieces of evidence, a
risk, safeguard, and measurable outcome in 100–130 independently produced
Malayalam words. Task 2 must sustain a developed 220–280-word argument with
two examples, counterargument, conditions, limitations, and balanced conclusion.

## Speaking

Allow keyword notes only. In collaboration, require genuine comparison,
challenge, repair, and a qualified joint recommendation. Follow-up questions
must probe reasons and conditions without supplying language. Score the complete
recording against the shared criteria.
