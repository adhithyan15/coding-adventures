## You'll want to know: assus, assa, assum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C354-ASSUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**assus, assa, assum** — "roasted".

> Carō assa iūcunda erat.
>
> *The roast meat was delicious.*

*Assus* is roasted, *ēlixus* boiled: Horace and Celsus set the two side by side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**assus** is inherited (EtymDB); Lewis & Short connect it with *āreō*, "to be dry", and *ārdeō*, "to burn".
