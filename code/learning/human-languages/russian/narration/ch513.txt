Russian, chapter 513: Warming Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
разминка (razmínka) — a warm-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fall behind, then the Russian for a distance.

You'll want to know: разминка.
разминка (razmínka) — "a warm-up".
Перед бегом обязательно нужна разминка. Péred bégom obyazátelno nuzhná razmínka. A warm-up is essential before a run.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
разминка (razmínka) is built on размять 'to loosen, to knead', from мять 'to crumple'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Russian for a distance, then the Russian for a warm-up.

You'll want to know: отжиматься.
отжиматься (otzhimátsya) — "to do press-ups".
Каждое утро он отжимается двадцать раз. Kázhdoye útro on otzhimáyetsya dvádtsat raz. Every morning he does twenty press-ups.
Imperfective; perfective отжаться (отожмусь).

The word, taken apart.
отжиматься (otzhimátsya) is built on от- and жать 'to press, to squeeze'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
приседать (prisedát) — to squat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a warm-up, then the Russian for to do press-ups.

You'll want to know: приседать.
приседать (prisedát) — "to squat".
Тренер сказал нам приседать медленно. Tréner skazál nam prisedát médlenno. The coach told us to squat slowly.
Imperfective; perfective присесть (присяду), which also means to sit down for a moment.

The word, taken apart.
приседать (prisedát) is built on при- and the root of сесть 'to sit down'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Дома у меня есть гантели и коврик. Dóma u menyá yest gantéli i kóvrik. At home I have dumbbells and a mat.
Plural of гантель (feminine).

гантели (gantéli) is the plural of гантель, borrowed from German Hantel 'dumbbell' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
выносливость (vynóslivost) — stamina, endurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to squat, then the Russian for dumbbells.

You'll want to know: выносливость.
выносливость (vynóslivost) — "stamina, endurance".
Для марафона нужна выносливость. Dlya marafóna nuzhná vynóslivost. You need stamina for a marathon.
Feminine. Built on выносливый — tough, hardy.

The word, taken apart.
выносливость (vynóslivost) is built on выносливый 'hardy', from выносить 'to bear, to endure' and носить 'to carry'; EtymDB has no entry.
