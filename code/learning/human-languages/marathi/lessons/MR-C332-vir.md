## You'll want to know: वीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C332-SHAURYA332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वीर** — *vīr* — "a hero; a warrior".

> गावात त्या वीराचं स्मारक आहे.
>
> *gāvāt tyā vīrācaṁ smārak āhe.*
>
> There is a memorial to that hero in the village.

Masculine.

As an adjective, brave: वीर सैनिक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वीर** (*vīr*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vīra*, 'a man, a hero' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
