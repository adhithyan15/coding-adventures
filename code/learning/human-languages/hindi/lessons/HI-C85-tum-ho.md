# तुम हो — the copula that was hiding inside one question

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C84-POSS-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *tumhārā*. You can name the *tum* level and command at it. Say
"you are" at that level, and notice you have never been given the word.

## You'll want to know: तुम हो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C85-IMP-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तुम हो** (*tum ho*) — "you are," at the *tum* level.

**हो** is the copula that goes with **तुम**, and nothing else takes it.

## Grammar Lens: the copula, completed
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C85-IMP-03, HI-LEX-C84-POSS-03] -->

You have had the others for a long time. Here they are together, which is the
first time the set has been complete:

| subject | copula |
|---|---|
| मैं | हूँ |
| तुम | **हो** |
| आप | हैं |
| वह | है |

Four forms and no irregularity beyond having to learn them. **हो** is the one
that was missing, and it was missing in an awkward way: the track has been
saying it inside the age question all along, as a lump, without the form ever
standing on its own.

Now it can. *Tum acchhe ho*, *tum śarmīle ho* — any of the character adjectives
you have, at the familiar level.

## Grammar Lens: matching the level all the way through
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C85-IMP-03, HI-LEX-C85-IMP-01] -->

A sentence has to keep one level from end to end:

| level | copula | command |
|---|---|---|
| तुम | **हो** | बोलो |
| आप | हैं | बोलिए |

Mixing them — *āp ho*, or *tum boliye* — is the error a listener notices at
once, in the way an English speaker notices *you was*.
