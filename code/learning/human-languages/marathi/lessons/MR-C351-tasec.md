## You'll want to know: तसेच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C351-JODNE351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तसेच** — *tasec* — "likewise; as well as".

> आम्ही पुस्तकं वाचली, तसेच गाणी ऐकली.
>
> *āmhī pustakaṁ vāclī, tasec gāṇī aiklī.*
>
> We read books and also listened to songs.

Formal; तसंच in speech. Built from तसे, in that way, and -च.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तसेच** (*tasec*) is built from तसे, "in that way", and the stressing ending -च. तसा is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *tasā*, "suchlike", under Sanskrit *tādṛśa*, "suchlike" (CDIAL 5760).
