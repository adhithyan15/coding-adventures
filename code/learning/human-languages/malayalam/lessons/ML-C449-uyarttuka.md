# ഉയർത്തുക (uyarttuka) — to lift up; to raise

## You'll want to know: ഉയർത്തുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C449-VARDDHANA449-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉയർത്തുക** — *uyarttuka* — "to lift up; to raise".

> ആ ചോദ്യം അവൾ യോഗത്തിൽ ഉയർത്തി.
>
> *ā cōdyaṁ avaḷ yōgattil uyartti.*
>
> She raised that question at the meeting.

Past: ഉയർത്തി, *uyartti*.

The causative of ഉയരുക: to make a thing rise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉയർത്തുക** (*uyarttuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 646 lists Malayalam *uyarttuka*, "to raise, to elevate", the causative of ഉയരുക.
