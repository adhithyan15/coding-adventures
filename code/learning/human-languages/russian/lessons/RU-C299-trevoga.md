## You'll want to know: тревога
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C299-SIRENA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**тревога** (*trevóga*) — "an alarm; anxiety".

> Ночью в школе зазвенел сигнал тревоги.
>
> *Nóchyu v shkóle zazvenél signál trevógi.*
>
> At night the alarm went off in the school.

Also "worry": *с тревогой* — anxiously. *Пожарная тревога* — a fire alarm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тревога* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *trĭvoga* (EtymDB); its deeper origin is not settled.
