## You'll want to know: غامض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C417-WUDUH417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غامض** — *ghāmiḍ* — "vague".

> كان جوابه غامضا.
>
> *kāna jawābuhu ghāmiḍan.*
>
> His answer was vague.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غامض** (*ghāmiḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-m-ḍ*, "to be hidden, unapparent" (Lane). *Ghāmiḍ* is the active participle: what is hidden, obscure, vague.
