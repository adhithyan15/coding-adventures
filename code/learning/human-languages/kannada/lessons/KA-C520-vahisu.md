# ವಹಿಸು (vahisu) — to take charge of; to take on

## You'll want to know: ವಹಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C520-TANDA520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಹಿಸು** — *vahisu* — "to take charge of; to take on".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ಊಟದ ಜವಾಬ್ದಾರಿಯನ್ನು ಮಾವ ವಹಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *habbada ūṭada javābdāriyannu māva vahisidaru.*
>
> Uncle took charge of the festival meal.

Past (I …): ವಹಿಸಿದೆ, *vahiside*.

Often with ಜವಾಬ್ದಾರಿ or ಕಾಳಜಿ: ಕಾಳಜಿ ವಹಿಸು, to take care.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಹಿಸು** (*vahisu*) is built on the Sanskrit root *vah*, "to carry, to bear" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*: to shoulder a task.
