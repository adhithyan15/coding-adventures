Portuguese, chapter 324: Era uma vez.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
o conto — the short story, the tale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the Easter bread, then the Portuguese for the sugared almonds.

You'll want to know: o conto.
o conto — "the short story, the tale".
O avô contava-nos um conto antes de dormir. Grandfather used to tell us a story before bed.
Built on contar, ‘to tell’: contar um conto.

The word, taken apart.
conto is built on contar "to count, to tell", which is inherited from Latin computāre "to reckon" (Lewis & Short): to tell events one by one, like counting.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
