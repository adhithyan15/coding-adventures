## You'll want to know: ઝવેરાત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C432-GOLD432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝવેરાત** — *jhaverāt* — "jewellery; gems".

> લગ્ન માટે તેમણે ઝવેરાત ખરીદ્યું.
>
> *lagna māṭe temṇe jhaverāt kharīdyũ.*
>
> They bought jewellery for the wedding.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝવેરાત** (*jhaverāt*) is usually traced to Arabic *jawāhirāt*, "jewels", which came through Persian.
