## You'll want to know: rivelare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C288-ANNUNCIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rivelare** — "to reveal".

> Il giornale ha rivelato il nome del vincitore.
>
> *The paper revealed the winner's name.*

*Rivelarsi* — to turn out: *si è rivelato un errore*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rivelare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *revēlāre* "to unveil" (Lewis & Short), built on *vēlum* "veil".
