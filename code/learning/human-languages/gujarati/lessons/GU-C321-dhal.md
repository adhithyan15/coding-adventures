## You'll want to know: ઢાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C321-TALVAR321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢાલ** — *ḍhāl* — "a shield".

> તેણે ઢાલથી પોતાને બચાવ્યો.
>
> *teṇe ḍhālthī potāne bacāvyo.*
>
> He protected himself with his shield.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢાલ** (*ḍhāl*) goes back to *ḍhāla*, "a shield", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun (CDIAL 5583). It is feminine: **ઢાલથી**, with a shield.
