## You'll want to know: le remboursement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C421-ANNULATION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le remboursement** — "the refund".

> Elle attend le remboursement de son billet.
>
> *She is waiting for the refund on her ticket.*

Built on *rembourser*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remboursement* is built on *rembourser* 'to pay back', from *bourse* 'purse', which comes from Old French *borse* (EtymDB) and is usually traced to Late Latin *bursa*, from Greek *býrsa* 'hide'. EtymDB does not list the noun.
