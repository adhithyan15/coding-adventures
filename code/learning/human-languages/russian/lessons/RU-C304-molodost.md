## You'll want to know: молодость
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C304-DETSTVO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**молодость** (*mólodost*) — "one's young years, youth".

> В молодости бабушка много путешествовала.
>
> *V mólodosti bábushka mnógo puteshéstvovala.*
>
> In her youth Grandma travelled a lot.

Feminine. *Молодость* reaches into the twenties and thirties; *юность* is the teens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*молодость* is built on *молодой* "young" and the ending *-ость* (EtymDB); *молодой* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *moldŭ* "young".
