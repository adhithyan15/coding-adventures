## You'll want to know: वाञ्छति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C531-VANCHATI531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वाञ्छति** — *vāñchati* — "yearns for".

> यदि सः सुखं वाञ्छति, तर्हि सः परिश्रमं करोतु।
>
> *yadi saḥ sukhaṃ vāñchati, tarhi saḥ pariśramaṃ karotu.*
>
> If he wants happiness, let him work hard.

Past (he …): सः सुखं वाञ्छितवान्, *saḥ sukhaṃ vāñchitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाञ्छति** (*vāñchati*) comes from the root √vāñch, "to desire, to wish"; MW compares German *wünschen* and English *wish* (MW).
