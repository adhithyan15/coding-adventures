## You'll want to know: ప్రహరీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C467-ANTASTU467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రహరీ** — *praharī* — "a compound wall".

> బడి చుట్టూ ఎత్తైన ప్రహరీ గోడ ఉంది.
>
> *baḍi cuṭṭū ettaina praharī gōḍa undi.*
>
> There is a high compound wall round the school.

Mostly ప్రహరీ గోడ: the wall round a house, a school or an office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రహరీ** (*praharī*) is a Telugu word for a boundary wall whose origin is not settled.
