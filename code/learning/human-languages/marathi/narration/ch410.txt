Marathi, chapter 410: Along the Coast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
किनारपट्टी (kinārpaṭṭī) — a coastline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to grow rough, then the Marathi for to slosh about.

You'll want to know: किनारपट्टी.
किनारपट्टी — kinārpaṭṭī — "a coastline".
इथली किनारपट्टी खूप सुंदर आहे. ithlī kinārpaṭṭī khūp sundar āhe. The coastline here is very beautiful.
Feminine.
Built on किनारा, a shore, and पट्टी, a strip: a long strip of shore.

The word, taken apart.
किनारपट्टी (kinārpaṭṭī) is a Marathi compound of किनारा, "a shore", a loan usually traced to Persian kinār, and पट्टी, "a strip".

खाडीत मच्छीमारांच्या होड्या उभ्या आहेत. khāḍīt macchīmārāñcyā hoḍyā ubhyā āhet. Fishermen’s boats are standing in the creek.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
खाडी (khāḍī) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi khāḍī, "creek, inlet", under khaḍḍa, "hole, pit" (CDIAL 3790).

Lesson 3 of 5.
दीपगृह (dīpgṛh) — a lighthouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a coastline, then the Marathi for a creek.

You'll want to know: दीपगृह.
दीपगृह — dīpgṛh — "a lighthouse".
रात्री दीपगृहाचा दिवा दूरवर दिसतो. rātrī dīpgṛhācā divā dūrvar disto. At night the lighthouse lamp can be seen far away.
Neuter.
दीप is a lamp and गृह a house: a lamp house.

The word, taken apart.
दीपगृह (dīpgṛh) is a modern word built from Sanskrit dīpa, "a lamp", and gṛha, "a house" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
द्वीपकल्प (dvīpkalpa) — a peninsula.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a creek, then the Marathi for a lighthouse.

You'll want to know: द्वीपकल्प.
द्वीपकल्प — dvīpkalpa — "a peninsula".
द्वीपकल्पाच्या तीन बाजूंना समुद्र असतो. dvīpkalpācyā tīn bājūnnā samudra asto. A peninsula has sea on three sides.
Masculine.
द्वीप is an island and -कल्प means almost: almost an island.

The word, taken apart.
द्वीपकल्प (dvīpkalpa) is a modern word built from Sanskrit dvīpa, "an island", and kalpa, "like, almost" (Monier-Williams): land that is almost an island.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for a lighthouse, then the Marathi for a peninsula.

You'll want to know: चौपाटी.
चौपाटी — caupāṭī — "a beachfront; a seafront".
रविवारी चौपाटीवर खूप गर्दी असते. ravivārī caupāṭīvar khūp gardī aste. On Sunday the beachfront is very crowded.
Feminine.
A town beach with food stalls and evening walkers.

The word, taken apart.
चौपाटी (caupāṭī) names an open town beach where people walk and eat in the evening; its origin is not settled.
