## You'll want to know: temerārius, temerāria, temerārium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C509-FORT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**temerārius, temerāria, temerārium** — "rash, reckless".

> Iuvenis temerārius in flūmen altum salit.
>
> *The rash young man jumps into the deep river.*

From the adverb *temere* — blindly, by chance. English *temerity* comes from the related *temeritās*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**temerārius** is built on the adverb *temere* "blindly, by chance" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *temerity* comes from the related *temeritās*.
