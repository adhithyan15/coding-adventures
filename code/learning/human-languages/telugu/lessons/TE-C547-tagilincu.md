# తగిలించు (tagiliñcu) — to hang up; to hook on

## You'll want to know: తగిలించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C547-INTIPANI547-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తగిలించు** — *tagiliñcu* — "to hang up; to hook on".

> అతను చొక్కాను మేకుకు తగిలించాడు.
>
> *atanu cokkānu mēkuku tagiliñcāḍu.*
>
> He hung his shirt on a nail.

Past (I …): తగిలించాను, *tagiliñcānu*.

-కి తగిలించు, to hang on a peg or a nail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తగిలించు** (*tagiliñcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3004 lists Telugu *tagulu*, "to touch, to catch", and *tagilincu*, "to cause to touch, to catch or to stick".
