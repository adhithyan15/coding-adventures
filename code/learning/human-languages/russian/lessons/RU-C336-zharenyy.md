## You'll want to know: жареный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C336-GOTOVO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**жареный** (*zhárenyy*) — "fried, roast".

> На ужин была жареная рыба.
>
> *Na úzhin bylá zhárenaya rýba.*
>
> There was fried fish for dinner.

One *н*, as a plain adjective; *жаренный на масле* takes two.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*жареный* is the old participle of *жарить* "to fry", from *жар* "heat", the same Slavic root as *гореть* "to burn"; EtymDB lists no parent.
