## You'll want to know: ਲਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C231-DIN231-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਟ** — *laṭ* — "a lock of hair".

> ਉਸ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਲਟ ਮੱਥੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *us dī ikk laṭ matthe utte hai.*
>
> A lock of her hair is on her forehead.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਟ** (*laṭ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*laṭṭa*, "hanging, tangled"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *laṭ*, "lock of hair" (CDIAL 10918).
