## You'll want to know: जबकि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C552-BAHARHAAL552-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जबकि** — *jabki* — "whereas; while".

> मुझे चाय पसंद है, जबकि मीरा को दूध पसंद है।
>
> *Mujhe cāy pasand hai, jabki Mīrā ko dūdh pasand hai.*
>
> I like tea, whereas Mira likes milk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जबकि** (*jabki*) joins *jab*, "when", and *ki*, "that", which is usually traced to Persian *ki*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
