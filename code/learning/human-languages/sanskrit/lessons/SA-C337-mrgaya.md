## You'll want to know: मृगया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C337-VANAVASA337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मृगया** — *mṛgayā* — "hunting; the chase".

> राजा मृगयायै वनं गच्छति।
>
> *rājā mṛgayāyai vanaṃ gacchati.*
>
> The king goes to the forest to hunt.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मृगया** (*mṛgayā*) is usually traced to *mṛga*, "a wild animal, a deer"; MW gives *mṛgayā* as hunting, the chase (MW).
