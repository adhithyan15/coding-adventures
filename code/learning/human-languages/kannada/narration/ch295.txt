Kannada, chapter 295: Cotton, Silk, Envelope, Postage stamp, Parcel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹತ್ತಿ (hatti) — cotton.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for money, then the Kannada for small change.

You'll want to know: ಹತ್ತಿ.
ಹತ್ತಿ — hatti — "cotton".

The word, taken apart.
ಹತ್ತಿ (hatti) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil parutti, Telugu patti and Malayalam parutti (DEDR 3976). DEDR lists older parti, patti, "cotton": the p- has become h-.

ರೇಷ್ಮೆ (rēṣme) is Persian rēsham, "silk", which came into Kannada through Hindustani, with a final -e added.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಅಂಚೆಚೀಟಿ (añcecīṭi) — a postage stamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for silk, then the Kannada for an envelope.

You'll want to know: ಅಂಚೆಚೀಟಿ.
ಅಂಚೆಚೀಟಿ — añcecīṭi — "a postage stamp".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂಚೆಚೀಟಿ (añcecīṭi) is native añce, "the post" (DEDR 54), with cīṭi, "a slip, a note", which probably came from Hindi cīṭhī, ciṭṭhī, "a note, a letter"; CDIAL 4832 traces that to a reconstructed ciṣṭa, "message".
