## You'll want to know: huài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C103-USE103-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huài* — "broken; bad".

> *wǒ de shǒujī huài le* — My phone's broken.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huài* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hweajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to break of itself'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
