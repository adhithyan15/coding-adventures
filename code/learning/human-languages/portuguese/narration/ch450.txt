Portuguese, chapter 450: Comida na loja.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a mercearia — the grocer's, the corner shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the herbalist's shop, then the Portuguese for the jeweller's shop.

You'll want to know: a mercearia.
a mercearia — "the grocer's, the corner shop".
A mercearia do bairro abre ao domingo. The local grocer's opens on Sundays.
In Brazil: a mercearia or o armazém.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB links mercearia with Italian merceria; the family goes back to Latin merx, mercis 'goods' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
a charcutaria — the deli (cured meats and cheese).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: a charcutaria.
a charcutaria — "the deli (cured meats and cheese)".
Na charcutaria compro presunto e chouriço. At the deli I buy cured ham and chouriço.
Also the deli counter in a supermarket.

The word, taken apart.
charcutaria was borrowed from French charcuterie, from chair cuite 'cooked flesh'; chair is inherited from Latin carō 'flesh' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.

a frutaria — "the fruit shop, the greengrocer".
Na frutaria, as laranjas estão baratas. At the fruit shop, oranges are cheap.
Built on a fruta.

The word, taken apart.
frutaria is built on fruta 'fruit', which goes back to Latin frūctus 'enjoyment, fruit' (Lewis & Short), through a Late Latin plural form frūcta.

Lesson 4 of 5.
a garrafeira — the wine shop; the wine cellar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the deli, then the Portuguese for the fruit shop.

You'll want to know: a garrafeira.
a garrafeira — "the wine shop; the wine cellar".
Comprámos um vinho velho na garrafeira. We bought an old wine at the wine shop.
Vinho garrafeira — a long-aged wine.

The word, taken apart.
garrafeira is built on garrafa 'bottle' (EtymDB) with -eira, a place for them; the origin of garrafa is not settled.
