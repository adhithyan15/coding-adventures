## You'll want to know: খসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C542-BHESTE542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**খসা** — *khôsā* — "to come loose; to fall off".

> জামার একটা বোতাম খসল।
>
> *Jāmār ekṭā botām khôslô.*
>
> A button came off my shirt.

Past (he/she …): সেই ইট খসল, *sei iṭ khôslô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খসা** (*khôsā*) comes from a reconstructed *khasati*, "slips"; CDIAL lists Bengali *khasā*, "to fall off, to slip away" (CDIAL 3856).
