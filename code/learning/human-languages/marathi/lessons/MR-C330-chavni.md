# छावणी (chāvṇī) — an army camp; a camp

## You'll want to know: छावणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C330-SENAPATI330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छावणी** — *chāvṇī* — "an army camp; a camp".

> नदीकाठी सैन्याची छावणी पडली होती.
>
> *nadīkāṭhī sainyācī chāvṇī paḍlī hotī.*
>
> The army's camp had been pitched on the riverbank.

Feminine.

छावणी पडणे, a camp is pitched.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छावणी** (*chāvṇī*) is the Marathi word for an army camp; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
