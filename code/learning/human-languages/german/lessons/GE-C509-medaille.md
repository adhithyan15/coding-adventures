## You'll want to know: die Medaille
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C509-WETTKAMPF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Medaille** — "medal".

> Sie gewann eine Medaille im Weitsprung.
>
> *She won a medal in the long jump.*

Plural: *die Medaillen*. Said roughly *me-DAL-ye*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Medaille* came from French *médaille*, from Italian *medaglia* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *metallum* "metal".
