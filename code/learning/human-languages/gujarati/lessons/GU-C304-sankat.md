## You'll want to know: સંકટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C304-PUR304-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકટ** — *sankaṭ* — "a crisis; danger".

> પૂર પછી ગામમાં પાણીનું સંકટ હતું.
>
> *pūr pachī gāmmā̃ pāṇīnũ sankaṭ hatũ.*
>
> After the flood the village faced a water crisis.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકટ** (*sankaṭ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṁkaṭá*, "narrow", and as a noun "a difficulty, a danger" (CDIAL 12817). It is neuter: **મોટું સંકટ**, a grave crisis.
