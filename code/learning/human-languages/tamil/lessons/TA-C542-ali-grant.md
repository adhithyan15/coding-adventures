## You'll want to know: அளி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C542-UTHTHARAVATHAM542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அளி** — *aḷi* — "to grant; to give formally".

> அரசு ஏழைக் குடும்பங்களுக்கு உதவி அளித்தது.
>
> *aracu ēḻaik kuṭumpaṅkaḷukku utavi aḷittatu.*
>
> The government gave help to poor families.

Past (I …): அளித்தேன், *aḷittēṉ*.

A formal verb beside கொடு (*koṭu*). The receiver takes -க்கு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அளி** (*aḷi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 301 gives அளி (*aḷi*), "to protect, to show favour", and a Malayalam cognate that means "to grant, to bestow", the sense the Tamil verb has today.
