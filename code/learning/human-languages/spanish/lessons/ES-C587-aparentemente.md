## You'll want to know: aparentemente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C587-EVIDENTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**aparentemente** — "apparently".

> Aparentemente, todo va bien.
>
> *Apparently, everything is going well.*

*Aparente* + *-mente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aparentemente* is built on *aparente* and *-mente*; *aparente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *apparens*, from *apparere* 'to appear' (EtymDB).
