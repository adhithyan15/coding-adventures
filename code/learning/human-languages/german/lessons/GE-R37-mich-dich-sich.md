## Guided Practice: point every verb at somebody
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-MICH-01, GE-LEX-DICH-01, GE-LEX-IHN-01, GE-LEX-UNS-01, GE-LEX-EUCH-01, GE-LEX-SEHEN-01, GE-ETYMON-SEHEN-SEE-02, GE-LEX-HOEREN-09, GE-ETYMON-HOEREN-10, GE-LEX-VERSTEHEN-05, GE-ETYMON-VERSTEHEN-06, GE-LEX-FRAGEN-05, GE-ETYMON-FRAGEN-06, GE-LEX-HELFEN-07, GE-ETYMON-HELFEN-08, GE-LEX-LESEN-07, GE-ETYMON-LESEN-08, GE-LEX-SCHREIBEN-10, GE-ETYMON-SCHREIBEN-11] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Six verbs of address, six pronouns. Run the grid by ear, not by
reading it.

- [YOU SAY: "Ich sehe dich. Ich sehe ihn. Ich sehe euch."]
- [YOU SAY: "Ich höre dich. Er hört uns. Wir hören euch."]
- [YOU SAY: "Ich verstehe dich." and "Verstehst du mich?"]
- [YOU SAY: "Ich frage dich." and "Ich helfe dir." — one takes the done-to
  shape, one takes the to-whom]
- [YOU SAY: the two verbs of the page — "Ich lese es. Ich schreibe es."]

## Guided Practice: the distant band — the row that moves
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-IHN-01, GE-LEX-DEN-01, GE-LEX-EINEN-01, GE-LEX-KEINEN-01, GE-LEX-MEIN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-AKKUSATIV-NUR-MASKULIN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-EIN-WOERTER-01, GE-LEX-HUND-02, GE-LEX-KAFFEE-02, GE-LEX-TEE-02, GE-LEX-WEIN-01, GE-LEX-BRUDER-01, GE-LEX-VATER-01, GE-LEX-FREUND-02, GE-LEX-ARM-02, GE-LEX-FUSS-02, GE-LEX-FINGER-02, GE-LEX-KOPF-02, GE-LEX-MUND-02, GE-LEX-SIE-SHE-02, GE-LEX-ES-01, GE-LEX-KATZE-04, GE-LEX-MILCH-02, GE-LEX-BROT-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Six words now move together on one row. Say the article, then the
whole family, then the pronoun that replaces it.

- [YOU SAY: "der Hund → den Hund, einen Hund, keinen Hund, meinen Hund → ihn"]
- [YOU SAY: "der Kaffee → den Kaffee → ihn"; "der Tee → den Tee → ihn"; "der Wein
  → den Wein → ihn"]
- [YOU SAY: the people — "den Bruder, den Vater, den Freund → ihn"]
- [YOU SAY: the body — "den Arm, den Fuß, den Finger, den Kopf, den Mund → ihn"]
- [YOU SAY: the two rows that stand still — "die Katze → sie", "die Milch → sie",
  "das Brot → es"]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-DICH-01, GE-LEX-IHN-01, GE-LEX-UNS-01, GE-LEX-EUCH-01, GE-LEX-SICH-01, GE-GRAMMAR-REFLEXIV-01, GE-GRAMMAR-AKKUSATIV-NUR-MASKULIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Which pronoun replaces a *der*-word being done to? (***Ihn***.) Which
two pronouns never change shape? (***Sie*** and ***es***.) How many English words
does *sich* cover? (**Four or more**.) And which English pronoun is *euch*?
(***You*** — the object form that took the whole job.)
