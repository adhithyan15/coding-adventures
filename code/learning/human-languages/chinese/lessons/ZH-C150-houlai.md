## You'll want to know: 后来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C150-HOULAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**后来** — *hòulái* — "afterwards; later (in the past)".

> **后来我们去了大学** *hòulái wǒmen qù le dàxué* — Afterwards we went to the university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**后来** (*hòulái*) is built from 后 'after' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwX* and *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): what came after.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C97-YIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-03, ZH-LEX-C78-AGE78-05] -->

后来 looks back: it follows something that already happened and says what came next. For what will come later, Mandarin says *yǐhòu*: *yí ge xiǎoshí yǐhòu*.

One more way to set a time in the past: *de shíhou* after a clause turns it into a when, the shíhou of *xiǎoshíhou*:

> *wǒ xiǎo de shíhou, wǒ jiā zài Zhōngguó, hòulái wǒmen lái zhèr le* — When I was small my family lived in China; later we came here.
