## You'll want to know: مستوى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C415-INJAZ415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مستوى** — *mustawā* — "a level".

> مستوى الطلاب جيد هذه السنة.
>
> *mustawā aṭ-ṭullāb jayyid hādhihi as-sana.*
>
> The students' level is good this year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستوى** (*mustawā*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-w-y*, "to be equal, level" (Lane). *Mustawā* is a noun of place from Form VIII, a level place: a level, a standard.
