## You'll want to know: आपाकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C505-MRD505-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आपाकः** — *āpākaḥ* — "a kiln".

> कुम्भकारः पात्राणि आपाके स्थापयति।
>
> *kumbhakāraḥ pātrāṇi āpāke sthāpayati.*
>
> The potter puts the pots in the kiln.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपाकः** (*āpākaḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √pac, "to cook, to bake"; MW gives *āpāka* as a baking-oven, a potter's kiln (MW).
