## You'll want to know: آپلود کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C418-NARMAFZAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آپلود کردن** (*âplod kardan*) — "to upload".

> او ویدیو را در اینترنت آپلود کرد.
>
> *u vide'o râ dar internet âplod kard.*
>
> He uploaded the video to the internet.

An English loan + *kardan*. Persian also says *bârgozâri kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آپلود کردن** joins the English loan *upload* to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do", the usual way Persian takes in new verbs.
