## You'll want to know: il palio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C320-FIERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il palio** — "the palio, the traditional horse race".

> Il palio si corre in piazza due volte l'anno.
>
> *The palio is run in the square twice a year.*

Named after the prize, a painted banner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*palio* is inherited from Latin *pallium* "a cloak, a cloth" (Lewis & Short): the race is run for a precious cloth.
