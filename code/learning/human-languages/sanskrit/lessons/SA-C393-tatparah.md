## You'll want to know: तत्परः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C393-VIJAYA393-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्परः** — *tatparaḥ* — "intent on; fully engaged".

> यदि छात्रः अध्ययने तत्परः, तर्हि सः सफलतां प्राप्नोति।
>
> *yadi chātraḥ adhyayane tatparaḥ, tarhi saḥ saphalatāṃ prāpnoti.*
>
> If a student is intent on study, he succeeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्परः** (*tatparaḥ*) is built from *tat*, "that", and *para*, "highest"; MW gives *tat-para* "having that as one's highest object, totally devoted to, eagerly engaged in" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
