Urdu, chapter 503: Damp and Cracks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سیلن (sīlan) — damp (in a wall).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for rust, then the Urdu for a metal spring.

You'll want to know: سیلن.
سیلن — sīlan — "damp (in a wall)".
برسات میں دیواروں میں سیلن آ جاتی ہے۔ barsāt meṅ dīvāroṅ meṅ sīlan ā jātī hai. In the rainy season damp gets into the walls.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سیلن (sīlan) is built on sīl, "cool, moist", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit sītala, "cold" (CDIAL 12487).

Lesson 4 of 5.
رنگوانا (rangvānā) — to have (something) painted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a brick, then the Urdu for plaster (on a wall).

You'll want to know: رنگوانا.
رنگوانا — rangvānā — "to have (something) painted".
ہم نے برسات کے بعد دیواریں رنگوائیں۔ ham ne barsāt ke bād dīvāreṅ rangvāīṅ. We had the walls painted after the rainy season.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دروازہ رنگوایا, us ne darvāzā rangvāyā.

The word, taken apart.
رنگوانا (rangvānā) is the causative of rangnā, "to colour": to have someone colour a thing. CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin rãgānā, "to be coloured", under raṅgayati, "dyes", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10570).

دادی نے آنگن کو مٹی سے لیپا۔ dādī ne āṅgan ko miṭṭī se līpā. Grandmother smeared the courtyard with fresh mud.
