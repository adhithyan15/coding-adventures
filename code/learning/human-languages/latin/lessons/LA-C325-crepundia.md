## You'll want to know: crepundia, crepundiōrum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C325-BULLA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**crepundia, crepundiōrum** — "a rattle; childhood toys".

> Nūtrīx īnfantī crepundia dedit.
>
> *The nurse gave the baby a rattle.*

Neuter, plural only. In comedies a lost child is recognised by the little toys kept in a box.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**crepundia** is built on *crepō*, "to rattle, to creak" (Lewis & Short): things that rattle.
