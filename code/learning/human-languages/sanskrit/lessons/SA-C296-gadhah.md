## You'll want to know: गाढः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C296-DAVAGNI296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गाढः** — *gāḍhaḥ* — "dense; thick".

> सर्वत्र गाढः धूमः अस्ति।
>
> *sarvatra gāḍhaḥ dhūmaḥ asti.*
>
> There is thick smoke everywhere.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गाढः** (*gāḍhaḥ*) comes from the root √gāh, "to plunge into": MW gives *gāḍha* as deeply entered, close, and also thick or dense (MW).
