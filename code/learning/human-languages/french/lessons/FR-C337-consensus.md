## You'll want to know: le consensus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C337-COMPROMIS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le consensus** — "the consensus, broad agreement".

> Après deux heures, nous avons trouvé un consensus.
>
> *After two hours, we reached a consensus.*

The final *s* is said: *consensuss*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consensus* was borrowed whole from Latin *cōnsēnsus* "agreement, accord" (EtymDB), from *cōnsentīre* "to agree", built on *sentīre* "to feel" (Lewis & Short).
