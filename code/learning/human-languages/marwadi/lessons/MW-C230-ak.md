## You'll want to know: आक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C230-SUN230-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आक** — *āk* — "swallow-wort (a desert shrub)".

> रेत मांय आक है।
>
> *ret mā̃y āk hai.*
>
> There's swallow-wort in the sand.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आक** (*āk*) is inherited from Sanskrit *arka*, the name of the plant *Calotropis*; CDIAL lists Marwari *āk* (CDIAL 625). It grows wild in the sand.
