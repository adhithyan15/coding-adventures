## You'll want to know: いつのまにか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C314-SUKKARI314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**いつのまにか** — *itsunomanika* — "before one knows it".

> **いつのまにか ゆうがたに なっていました。** — *itsunomanika yūgata ni natte imashita* — "Before we knew it, evening had come."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いつのまにか** (*itsunomanika*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *itsu*, "when", and *ma*, "an interval" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
