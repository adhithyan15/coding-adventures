## You'll want to know: schuldig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C286-URTEIL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**schuldig** — "guilty; owing".

> Das Gericht fand den Mann schuldig.
>
> *The court found the man guilty.*

Also 'owing': *Du bist mir zehn Euro schuldig*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schuldig* is inherited from Old High German *sculdig* (EtymDB), built on *Schuld* "guilt, debt".
