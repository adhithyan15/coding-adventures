Kannada, chapter 221: Apple, Orange, Papaya, Watermelon, Chapati.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸೇಬು (sēbu) — an apple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a fan, then the Kannada for rubbish.

You'll want to know: ಸೇಬು.
ಸೇಬು — sēbu — "an apple".

The word, taken apart.
ಸೇಬು (sēbu) is a loan from Persian sēb, "apple", which came through Hindustani. Kannada adds -u at the end.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಿತ್ತಳೆ (kittaḷe) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kiccili, Telugu kiccili and Malayalam kiccili (DEDR 1512). DEDR lists kittaḷe, "an orange".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ಪಪ್ಪಾಯಿ.
ಪಪ್ಪಾಯಿ — pappāyi — "a papaya".

The word, taken apart.
ಪಪ್ಪಾಯಿ (pappāyi) comes from papaya, a word from the Caribbean that the Portuguese and Spanish carried, with the fruit, around the world.

[pause 2 seconds]

The word, taken apart.
ಕಲ್ಲಂಗಡಿ (kallaṅgaḍi) probably goes back to Sanskrit kāliṅga, "watermelon" (CDIAL 3100), whose Marathi form is very close to the Kannada one; CDIAL does not list the Kannada word.
