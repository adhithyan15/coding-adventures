## You'll want to know: jīngyàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C261-EVER261-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jīngyàn* — "experience; know-how".

> *tā hěn yǒu jīngyàn* — He's very experienced.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīngyàn* is built from *jīng* and *yàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keng* and *ngjemH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'constant' and 'the warp of cloth', and the second 'to verify'. How the pair came to mean experience is not settled.
