## You'll want to know: धोबी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C263-TELI263-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धोबी** — *dhobī* — "a washerman".

> धोबी नदी कनै रैवै है।
>
> *dhobī nadī kanai raivai hai.*
>
> The washerman lives near the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धोबी** (*dhobī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhauvati*, "washes"; CDIAL lists Marwari *dhoṇo*, "washes", and Hindi *dhobī*, "washerman", under it (CDIAL 6886).
