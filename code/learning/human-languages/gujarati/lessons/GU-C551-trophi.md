## You'll want to know: ટ્રોફી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C551-TALIM551-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ટ્રોફી** — *ṭrophī* — "a trophy".

> જો અમારી ટીમ જીતશે તો શાળાને ટ્રોફી મળશે.
>
> *jo amārī ṭīm jītśe to śāḷāne ṭrophī maḷśe.*
>
> If our team wins, the school will get a trophy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટ્રોફી** (*ṭrophī*) is borrowed from English *trophy*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
