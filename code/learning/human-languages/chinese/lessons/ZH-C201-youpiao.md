## You'll want to know: yóupiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C201-POST201-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yóupiào* — "postage stamp".

> *wǒ yào mǎi liǎng zhāng yóupiào* — I'd like two stamps, please.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóupiào* is built from *yóu* 'post' and 票 'a slip'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as a lodge at a border; no dictionary we use records an older form of 票, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
