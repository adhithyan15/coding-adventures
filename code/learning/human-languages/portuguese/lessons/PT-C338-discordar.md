## You'll want to know: discordar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C338-DESACORDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**discordar** — "to disagree".

> Discordo de ti: o filme não é nada aborrecido.
>
> *I disagree with you: the film is not boring at all.*

*Discordar de alguém* — to disagree with someone. Politely: *permite-me discordar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discordar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *discordāre* "to be at variance, to differ" (Lewis & Short), from *discors*, built from *dis-* "apart" and *cor, cordis* "heart".
