Italian, chapter 328: Imperi e sovrani.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
l'impero — the empire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the shield, then the Italian for to conquer.

You'll want to know: l'impero.
l'impero — "the empire".
L'impero è durato cinque secoli. The empire lasted five centuries.
Built on Latin imperium, ‘command’.

The word, taken apart.
impero is inherited from Latin imperium "command, power, empire" (EtymDB), from imperāre "to command".
