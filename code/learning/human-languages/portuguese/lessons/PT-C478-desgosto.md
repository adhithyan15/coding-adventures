## You'll want to know: o desgosto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C478-SOLIDAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o desgosto** — "grief, sorrow; heartbreak".

> A morte do cão foi um grande desgosto para as crianças.
>
> *The dog's death was a great sorrow for the children.*

Stronger than *tristeza*. *Ter um desgosto* — to suffer a heartbreak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desgosto* is built from *des-* and *gosto* "taste, pleasure", which is inherited from Latin *gustus* "a tasting, taste" (Lewis & Short).
