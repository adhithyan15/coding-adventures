Portuguese, chapter 421: No aeroporto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a escala — the stopover; the scale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for seasickness, then the Portuguese for to feel sick.

You'll want to know: a escala.
a escala — "the stopover; the scale".
O voo tem uma escala de três horas. The flight has a three-hour stopover.
Um voo sem escalas — a direct flight. On a map, a escala is the scale.

The word, taken apart.
escala is a learned borrowing from Latin scāla 'ladder, flight of steps' (EtymDB), from scandere 'to climb'. A stopover is a step on the way.

o embarque — "the boarding".
O embarque começa meia hora antes do voo. Boarding starts half an hour before the flight.
A porta de embarque — the gate.

The word, taken apart.
embarque is built on embarcar 'to board', on barca 'boat', inherited from Late Latin barca (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
o vaivém — the shuttle (bus); the coming and going.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the stopover, then the Portuguese for the boarding.

You'll want to know: o vaivém.
o vaivém — "the shuttle (bus); the coming and going".
Um vaivém leva os passageiros até ao avião. A shuttle bus takes the passengers to the plane.
Literally 'goes-comes'. O vaivém of a street is its bustle.

The word, taken apart.
vaivém is built from vai 'goes' and vem 'comes' (EtymDB): something that keeps going back and forth.

Lesson 4 of 5.
a pista — the runway; the track; the clue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the boarding, then the Portuguese for the shuttle.

You'll want to know: a pista.
a pista — "the runway; the track; the clue".
O avião está parado na pista. The plane is standing on the runway.
A pista de dança — the dance floor. Uma pista in a mystery is a clue.

The word, taken apart.
pista was borrowed from Italian pista 'beaten track', which is usually linked with Latin pinsere 'to pound' (Lewis & Short), but that is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
o fuso — the time zone (o fuso horário); the spindle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the shuttle, then the Portuguese for the runway.

You'll want to know: o fuso.
o fuso — "the time zone (o fuso horário); the spindle".
Mudamos de fuso horário e perdemos uma hora. We change time zones and lose an hour.
O fuso horário — the time zone. A spindle for spinning wool is also um fuso.

The word, taken apart.
fuso is inherited from Latin fūsus 'spindle' (EtymDB): the time zones on a globe have the shape of a spindle.
