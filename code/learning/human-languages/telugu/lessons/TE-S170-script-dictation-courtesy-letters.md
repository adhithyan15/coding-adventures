# ఞ · ◌ౌ — from the sound, with nothing to copy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-132, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-133] -->

Cover both courtesy words and the page before this one. Nothing to copy and
nothing to uncover.

## Writing — dictation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-132, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-133] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Say *gau* aloud and write it: the letter **గ**, then the sign that makes it
*gau*. Once.

Then say *gnya* aloud and write the block it is spelled with. This is the harder
of the two, because your ear is being asked for one thing and your hand for
another — the mouth says *gnya* and the page wants **జ్ఞ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-132, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-133] -->

Uncover the two words and compare. Then notice what each of the two asked of
you. The sign was a matter of hearing one sliding vowel and putting one mark in
the right place. The stack was not a matter of hearing at all — the sound your
mouth makes and the letters the page keeps have drifted apart, and only the
spelling remembers where the word came from.
