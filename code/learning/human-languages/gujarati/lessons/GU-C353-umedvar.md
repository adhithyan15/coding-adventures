## You'll want to know: ઉમેદવાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C353-MATDAN353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉમેદવાર** — *umedvār* — "a candidate".

> આ વખતે ત્રણ ઉમેદવાર છે.
>
> *ā vakhte traṇ umedvār che.*
>
> There are three candidates this time.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉમેદવાર** (*umedvār*) is usually traced to Persian *umīdvār*, "hopeful", from *umīd*, "hope": the one who hopes to be chosen.
