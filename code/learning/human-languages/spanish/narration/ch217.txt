Spanish, chapter 217: Synthesis --- Carrying a Conversation.
1 lesson. All 1 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 1.
(synthesis) — carrying a whole conversation to somebody who was not there — four lines of live speech, one steady account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Here is a very short conversation. Two people, four lines.
— ¿Dónde trabajas? — Trabajo en casa. — ¿Estás cansado? — Sí.
[pause 1 second]
You were there. Somebody else was not.

What you've built.
Carry it across, line by line. Every line needs a joint, and the joint is different every time.
[pause 2 seconds]
The first line was a question with an asking word. (Preguntó dónde trabajaba.)
[pause 2 seconds]
The second was a statement. (Dijo que trabajaba en casa.)
[pause 2 seconds]
The third was a yes-or-no question. (Preguntó si estaba cansado.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: all three reports, one after another, without stopping]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[repeat that twice]
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Report the first line of the conversation.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Report the second line.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Report the third line.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Read your three sentences back and name what is not in them. (No question marks — and not one verb left in the present.)
[pause 2 seconds]
Four lines of live speech went in; one steady account came out. What is new is not that you said something in Spanish — it is that you carried somebody else's words to a person who was not there, and marked every seam where they stopped being your own.
