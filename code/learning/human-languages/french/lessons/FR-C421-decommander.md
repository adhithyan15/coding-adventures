## You'll want to know: décommander
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C421-ANNULATION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**décommander** — "to cancel (a booking, an order)".

> Après l'annulation du vol, nous avons décommandé le taxi.
>
> *After the flight was cancelled, we cancelled the taxi.*

*Se décommander* — to cry off, to cancel at the last minute.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*décommander* is built from *dé-* and *commander* 'to order', which goes back through Vulgar Latin to Latin *commendāre* 'to entrust', built on *mandāre* (EtymDB lists *commander*).
