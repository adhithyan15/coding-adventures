# కొనియాడు (koniyāḍu) — to extol; to praise highly

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C370-GATHA370-02, TE-LEX-C370-GATHA370-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a tale, then the Telugu for fame.

## You'll want to know: కొనియాడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C370-GATHA370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కొనియాడు** — *koniyāḍu* — "to extol; to praise highly".

> అందరూ ఆ వీరుడి ధైర్యాన్ని కొనియాడారు.
>
> *andarū ā vīruḍi dhairyānni koniyāḍāru.*
>
> Everyone praised that hero's courage.

Past (I …): కొనియాడాను, *koniyāḍānu*.

A written-style word: the news uses it of speeches and tributes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కొనియాడు** (*koniyāḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2151 lists Telugu *koniyāḍu*, "to praise", under *konu*, "to take, hold".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
