## You'll want to know: برهان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C358-HUJJA358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برهان** — *burhān* — "a proof".

> هذه الصورة برهان على كلامه.
>
> *hādhihi aṣ-ṣūra burhān ʿalā kalāmih.*
>
> This photo is proof of what he said.

Plural: براهين, *barāhīn*.

It takes على: برهان على …, proof of ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برهان** (*burhān*) is a native Arabic word that Lane files under *b-r-h-n*, "to bring clear proof" (Lane). Some early grammarians built it on a three-letter root instead, so the shape of its root is disputed.
