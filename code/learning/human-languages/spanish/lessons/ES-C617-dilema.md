## You'll want to know: el dilema
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C617-DECIDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el dilema** — "the dilemma".

> Tengo un dilema: trabajar o estudiar.
>
> *I have a dilemma: to work or to study.*

*Estar ante un dilema* — to face a dilemma.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dilema* comes from Latin *dilemma*, taken from Greek *dilēmma* 'a double premise', from *dis* 'twice' and *lēmma* 'premise' (EtymDB).
