Bengali, chapter 89: Music, News, Country, Story, Toy.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
সংগীত (sôṅgit) — music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a song, then the Bengali for a holiday.

You'll want to know: সংগীত.
সংগীত — sôṅgit — "music".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sôṅgit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sôṅgit, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sôṅgit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for a song, then the Bengali for a holiday, then say sôṅgit again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does সংগীত (sôṅgit) mean? ("Music".) Say it once more.
