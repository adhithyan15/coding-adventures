## You'll want to know: મિસ્ત્રી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C511-MAJUR511-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મિસ્ત્રી** — *mistrī* — "a mechanic".

> ગાડી બગડી ગઈ એટલે અમે મિસ્ત્રીને બોલાવ્યો.
>
> *gāḍī bagḍī gaī eṭle ame mistrīne bolāvyo.*
>
> The car broke down, so we called a mechanic.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મિસ્ત્રી** (*mistrī*) is usually traced to Portuguese *mestre*, "a master of a trade". In Gujarati it names a skilled hand: a mechanic, or a master carpenter or mason.
