Russian, chapter 356: Despite everything.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
несмотря на (nesmotryá na) — despite, in spite of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for inevitable, then the Russian for to arise.

You'll want to know: несмотря на.
несмотря на (nesmotryá na) — "despite, in spite of".
Несмотря на дождь, мы пошли в парк. Nesmotryá na dozhd, my poshlí v park. Despite the rain, we went to the park.
It takes the accusative: несмотря на (nesmotryá na) погоду. Before a clause, несмотря на (nesmotryá na) то, что… — although. Несмотря is written as one word.

The word, taken apart.
несмотря на (nesmotryá na) is built from не "not" and смотря, the gerund of смотреть "to look", from Proto-Slavic motriti "to watch": not looking at the obstacle.

Lesson 2 of 5.
тем не менее (tem ne méneye) — nevertheless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to arise, then the Russian for despite.

You'll want to know: тем не менее.
тем не менее (tem ne méneye) — "nevertheless".
Было трудно, тем не менее мы всё сделали. Býlo trúdno, tem ne méneye my vsyo sdélali. It was hard; nevertheless, we did everything.
Formal. It may open the clause or follow но: но тем не менее (tem ne méneye).

The word, taken apart.
тем не менее (tem ne méneye) is built from тем "by that", не "not" and менее "less", the old comparative: not the less for that.

Lesson 3 of 5.
впрочем (vpróchem) — anyway, mind you.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for despite, then the Russian for nevertheless.

You'll want to know: впрочем.
впрочем (vpróchem) — "anyway, mind you".
Я не люблю мясо, впрочем, сегодня попробую. Ya ne lyublyú myáso, vpróchem, segódnya popróbuyu. I do not like meat; still, today I will try some.
It softens or partly takes back what was said: Впрочем, это неважно — anyway, it does not matter.

The word, taken apart.
впрочем (vpróchem) is fused from в "in" and прочем, a form of прочий "other, the rest": as for the rest.

Lesson 4 of 5.
с одной стороны (s odnóy storoný) — on the one hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for nevertheless, then the Russian for anyway.

You'll want to know: с одной стороны.
с одной стороны (s odnóy storoný) — "on the one hand".
С одной стороны, работа интересная. S odnóy storoný, rabóta interésnaya. On the one hand, the work is interesting.
It sets up a first view, and a second view follows. Сторона is a side.

The word, taken apart.
с одной стороны (s odnóy storoný) is "from one side": сторона "a side" is inherited from Proto-Slavic storna.
