## You'll want to know: વ્રત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C331-UPVAS331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્રત** — *vrat* — "a vow (of worship)".

> માતાએ દીકરા માટે વ્રત રાખ્યું.
>
> *mātāe dīkrā māṭe vrat rākhyũ.*
>
> The mother kept a vow for her son.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્રત** (*vrat*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vrata*, "a vow, a religious observance" (Monier-Williams). It is kept, **વ્રત રાખ્યું**, and often means fasting on set days.
