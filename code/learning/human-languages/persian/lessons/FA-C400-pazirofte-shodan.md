## You'll want to know: پذیرفته شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C400-DANESHKADE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پذیرفته شدن** (*pazirofte shodan*) — "to be admitted, to be accepted".

> او در رشته پزشکی پذیرفته شد.
>
> *u dar reshte-ye pezeshki pazirofte shod.*
>
> She was admitted to study medicine.

*Pazirofte*, the participle of *paziroftan* "to accept" (present stem *pazir-*), + *shodan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پذیرفته شدن** is native Persian, built from *pazirofte*, the participle of *paziroftan* "to accept", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian, and *shodan* "to become", which makes it passive.
