## You'll want to know: दुविधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C549-VIKALP549-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दुविधा** — *duvidhā* — "a dilemma; being in two minds".

> दो अच्छे घर देखकर अरुण दुविधा में पड़ गया।
>
> *Do acche ghar dekhkar Aruṇ duvidhā meṁ paṛ gayā.*
>
> After seeing two good houses, Arun was in two minds.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुविधा** (*duvidhā*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *dvividha*, "twofold, of two kinds" (Monier-Williams); the sense "being of two minds" is the Hindi one.
