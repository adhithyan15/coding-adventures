## You'll want to know: بوند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C428-BOOND428-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بوند** — *būṅd* — "a drop".

> بارش کی پہلی بوند میرے ہاتھ پر گری۔
>
> *bārish kī pehlī būṅd mere hāth par girī.*
>
> The first drop of rain fell on my hand.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بوند** (*būṅd*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bū̃d*, "a drop", under Sanskrit *bindú*, "a drop, a spot" (CDIAL 9240).
