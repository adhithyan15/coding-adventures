Spanish, chapter 559: El desfile.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
el desfile — the parade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the tribute, then the Spanish for to open.

You'll want to know: el desfile.
el desfile — "the parade".
Vimos el desfile desde la ventana. We watched the parade from the window.
Un desfile de moda — a fashion show.

The word, taken apart.
desfile is built on desfilar (EtymDB), from French défiler 'to march in a line', built on fil 'thread', from Latin fīlum.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 6.
la procesión — the procession.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for to open, then the Spanish for the parade.

You'll want to know: la procesión.
la procesión — "the procession".
La procesión sale de la iglesia a las ocho. The procession leaves the church at eight.
Very important in Holy Week, la Semana Santa.

The word, taken apart.
procesión is a learned borrowing from Latin prōcessiō 'a marching forward' (EtymDB), from prōcēdere 'to go forward', built on cēdere 'to go'.

la carroza — "the float (in a parade); the carriage".
Los niños saludaban desde las carrozas. The children waved from the floats.
La carroza de los Reyes Magos — the Three Kings' float on 5 January.

The word, taken apart.
carroza was borrowed from Italian carrozza 'a carriage', built on carro 'a cart', which comes from Latin carrus (Lewis & Short).
