Gujarati, chapter 260: Cards, chess and toys.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
પત્તું (pattũ) — playing card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for exercise, then the Gujarati for wrestling.

You'll want to know: પત્તું.
પત્તું — pattũ — "playing card".
અમે રાત્રે પત્તાં રમ્યા. — ame rātre pattā̃ ramyā — "We played cards at night."
The plural is પત્તાં, pattā̃, "cards".

The word, taken apart.
પત્તું (pattũ), a leaf, then a playing card, is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit páttra, "wing-feather; leaf; leaf of a book"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati pātũ, "leaf", but not the form pattũ (CDIAL 7733).

Lesson 2 of 6.
શતરંજ (śatranj) — chess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wrestling, then the Gujarati for playing card.

You'll want to know: શતરંજ.
શતરંજ — śatranj — "chess".
દાદાએ મને શતરંજ શીખવી. — dādāe mane śatranj śīkhavī — "Grandfather taught me chess."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શતરંજ (śatranj) is a loan from Persian shatranj, "chess" (EtymDB); Lane records the Arabic shiṭranj as a Persian word taken into Arabic (Lane). The Persian word comes from Sanskrit cáturaṅga, "having four limbs", the four parts of an army, which CDIAL also glosses "a kind of chess" (CDIAL 4596).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: રમકડું.
રમકડું — ramakḍũ — "toy".
મેં બાળક માટે રમકડું ખરીદ્યું. — mẽ bāḷak māṭe ramakḍũ kharīdyũ — "I bought a toy for the child."

The word, taken apart.
રમકડું (ramakḍũ), a thing to play with, is built on the taught ramvũ, "to play", with -kḍũ. CDIAL derives ramvũ from Sanskrit rámatē, "takes pleasure in" (CDIAL 10628), but lists no ramakḍũ.

You'll want to know: ધારવું.
ધારવું — dhārvũ — "to guess, to suppose".
મેં સાચો જવાબ ધાર્યો. — mẽ sāco javāb dhāryo — "I guessed the right answer."

The word, taken apart.
ધારવું (dhārvũ), to hold a thought, is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit dhāráyati, "holds, keeps"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to consider, guess" (CDIAL 6791).

Lesson 5 of 6.
પસંદ કરવું (pasand karvũ) — to choose, to pick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for toy, then the Gujarati for to guess.

You'll want to know: પસંદ કરવું.
પસંદ કરવું — pasand karvũ — "to choose, to pick".
તેણે લાલ રમકડું પસંદ કર્યું. — teṇe lāl ramakḍũ pasand karyũ — "She chose the red toy."
પસંદ, pasand, alone means liked or chosen.

The word, taken apart.
પસંદ કરવું (pasand karvũ) is built from pasand, "liked, approved", a loan usually traced to Persian pasand, and the verb karvũ, "to do".
