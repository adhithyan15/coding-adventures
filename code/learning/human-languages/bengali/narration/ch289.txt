Bengali, chapter 289: The Water Rises.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
জলোচ্ছ্বাস (jôlochchhvās) — a tidal surge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to uproot, then the Bengali for electricity.

You'll want to know: জলোচ্ছ্বাস.
জলোচ্ছ্বাস — jôlochchhvās — "a tidal surge".
ঘূর্ণিঝড়ের সঙ্গে একটা বড় জলোচ্ছ্বাস এল। ghūrnijhôṛer sôngge ekṭā bôṛo jôlochchhvās elô. A great tidal surge came with the cyclone.

The word, taken apart.
জলোচ্ছ্বাস (jôlochchhvās) joins জল, water, to Sanskrit ucchvāsa, 'a breathing out, a deep breath' (Monier-Williams): the sea heaving up over the land.

Lesson 2 of 5.
তলিয়ে যাওয়া (tôliye jāoyā) — to go under; to be submerged.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for electricity, then the Bengali for a tidal surge.

You'll want to know: তলিয়ে যাওয়া.
তলিয়ে যাওয়া — tôliye jāoyā — "to go under; to be submerged".
জলোচ্ছ্বাসে নিচু গ্রামগুলো তলিয়ে গেল। jôlochchhvāse nichu grāmgulo tôliye gelô. The low-lying villages went under in the surge.
Past (he/she …): সেই নৌকা তলিয়ে গেল, sei noukā tôliye gelô.

The word, taken apart.
তলিয়ে যাওয়া (tôliye jāoyā) is the -e form of তলানো, to sink to the bottom, built on তল — Sanskrit tala (CDIAL 5731, which lists Bengali tal, 'bottom, floor') — before যাওয়া. তলা, a storey, is from the same word.

উঁচু (ũchu) comes down from Sanskrit ucca, 'tall, lofty' (CDIAL 1634, which lists Bengali uc and ũc); its opposite is নিচু, low.

Lesson 5 of 5.
প্লাবিত হওয়া (plābitô hôoyā) — to be flooded; to be inundated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for high, then the Bengali for a raft.

You'll want to know: প্লাবিত হওয়া.
প্লাবিত হওয়া — plābitô hôoyā — "to be flooded; to be inundated".
বন্যায় উপকূলের অনেক গ্রাম প্লাবিত হল। bônyāy upôkūler ônek grām plābitô hôlô. Many villages on the coast were flooded.
Past (he/she …): সেই শহর প্লাবিত হল, sei shôhôr plābitô hôlô.

The word, taken apart.
প্লাবিত হওয়া (plābitô hôoyā) puts Sanskrit plāvita, 'made to overflow, deluged' (Monier-Williams), before হওয়া: to be under the flood. It is a word of the news; talk says জলে ডুবে গেল.
