# దారితీయు (dāritīyu) — to lead to; to result in

## You'll want to know: దారితీయు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C392-MULAM392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**దారితీయు** — *dāritīyu* — "to lead to; to result in".

> చిన్న అభిప్రాయభేదం పెద్ద గొడవకు దారితీసింది.
>
> *cinna abhiprāyabhēdaṁ pedda goḍavaku dāritīsindi.*
>
> A small difference of opinion led to a big quarrel.

Past (it …): దారితీసింది, *dāritīsindi*.

What it leads to takes -కి: గొడవకు దారితీయు. Word for word, to take the road. Events lead to things, so its past is shown as దారితీసింది, it led to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దారితీయు** (*dāritīyu*) is native Dravidian, a compound of *dāri*, "a way, a road" (DEDR 3170), and *tīyu*, "to take, to draw" (DEDR 3407).
