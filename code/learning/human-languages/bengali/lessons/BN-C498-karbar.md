## You'll want to know: কারবার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C498-UDYOG498-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কারবার** — *kārbār* — "a trade; a line of business".

> দাদুর কাপড়ের কারবার ছিল।
>
> *Dādur kāpṛer kārbār chhilô.*
>
> My grandfather was in the cloth trade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কারবার** (*kārbār*) is usually traced to Persian *kārbār*, "business, affairs", built on *kār*, "work".
