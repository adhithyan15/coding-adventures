# ಹೋರಾಡು (hōrāḍu) — to fight; to struggle

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a commander, then the Kannada for a warrior.

## You'll want to know: ಹೋರಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C336-SAINYA336-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೋರಾಡು** — *hōrāḍu* — "to fight; to struggle".

> ರೈತರು ತಮ್ಮ ನೆಲಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಹೋರಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *raitaru tamma nelakkāgi hōrāḍidaru.*
>
> The farmers fought for their land.

Past (I …): ಹೋರಾಡಿದೆ, *hōrāḍide*.

What one fights for takes -ಆಗಿ: ನ್ಯಾಯಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಹೋರಾಡು, to fight for justice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೋರಾಡು** (*hōrāḍu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *pōr*, "to fight, to wrestle" (DEDR 4540), whose *p-* became *h-*, and *āḍu*, "to move, to act" (DEDR 347).
