# লাফদড়ি (lāphdôṛi) — a skipping rope; skipping

## You'll want to know: লাফদড়ি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C517-KHELA517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**লাফদড়ি** — *lāphdôṛi* — "a skipping rope; skipping".

> মেয়েরা উঠোনে লাফদড়ি খেলছিল।
>
> *Meyerā uṭhone lāphdôṛi khelchhilô.*
>
> The girls were skipping in the courtyard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লাফদড়ি** (*lāphdôṛi*) joins লাফ, "a leap", from a reconstructed *lappa* (CDIAL 10939), to দড়ি, "a rope".
