# मम मतेन (mama matena) — in my opinion

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C256-WRK256-05, SA-LEX-MAMA, SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-STEM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "an opinion". (*matam*.) And "my". (*mama*.) This chapter gives your opinion, and a reason for it.

## You'll want to know: मम मतेन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C256-WRK256-05, SA-LEX-MAMA, SA-LEX-C89-DESCRIBE-01, SA-LEX-C27-REPLY-04] -->

> **मम मतेन एतत् सुन्दरम्।**
>
> *mama matena etat sundaram.* — "In my opinion this is beautiful."

**मतेन** (*matena*) is **मतम्** (*matam*), "an opinion", with *-ena*, "by": "by my opinion", that is, "in my opinion".

> **मम मतेन सः सत्यं वदति।**
>
> *mama matena saḥ satyaṃ vadati.* — "In my opinion he is telling the truth."

> **तव मतेन किम्?**
>
> *tava matena kim?* — "What is your opinion?"

## You'll want to know: अहं मन्ये
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C278-OPINION-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C241-WHY241-04, SA-JOIN-ITI-01, SA-LEX-C76-WEATHER-01, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-04] -->

**मन्ये** (*manye*) is "I think". It ends in *-e*, the "I" ending of verbs like **रोचते** (*rocate*). **यत्** (*yat*), "that", opens what you think:

> **अहं मन्ये यत् वर्षा भविष्यति।**
>
> *ahaṃ manye yat varṣā bhaviṣyati.* — "I think that it will rain."

Or say the thought first and close it with **इति** (*iti*), as you close what someone said:

> **वर्षा भविष्यति इति मन्ये।**
>
> *varṣā bhaviṣyati iti manye.* — "It will rain, I think."
