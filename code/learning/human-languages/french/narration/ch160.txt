French, chapter 160: Deep, Low, Unknown, Dangerous, Safe.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
profond / profonde — deep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for rude, then the French for unpleasant.

You'll want to know: profond / profonde.
profond / profonde — "deep".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: profond / profonde]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: profond / profonde, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say profond / profonde]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the French for rude, then the French for unpleasant, then say profond / profonde again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does profond / profonde mean? ("Deep".) Say it once more.
