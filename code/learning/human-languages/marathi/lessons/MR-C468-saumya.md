## You'll want to know: सौम्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C468-SAUMYA468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सौम्य** — *saumya* — "mild".

> आजोबांचा स्वभाव सौम्य आहे.
>
> *ājobāñcā svabhāv saumya āhe.*
>
> Grandfather has a mild nature.

Unchanging: of a nature, a medicine or the weather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौम्य** (*saumya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saumya*, "gentle and placid" (CDIAL 13620).
