## You'll want to know: तलवार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C329-SHASTRA329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तलवार** — *talvār* — "a sword".

> संग्रहालयात एक जुनी तलवार ठेवली आहे.
>
> *saṅgrahālayāt ek junī talvār ṭhevlī āhe.*
>
> An old sword is kept in the museum.

Feminine.

Plural तलवारी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तलवार** (*talvār*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *tarvār*, 'a one-edged sword', under Sanskrit *taravāri* (CDIAL 5706); standard Marathi writes it with l for r.
