## You'll want to know: extraviarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C652-AEROPUERTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**extraviarse** — "to go missing, to get lost (of things)".

> Mi maleta se extravió en el viaje.
>
> *My suitcase went missing on the trip.*

Formal; airports talk about *equipaje extraviado*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*extraviarse* is built from *extra* "outside" and *vía* "way" (EtymDB), from Latin *via*: to go off the road.
