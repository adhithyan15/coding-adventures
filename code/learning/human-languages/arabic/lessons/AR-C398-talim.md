## You'll want to know: تعليم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C398-TALIM398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعليم** — *taʿlīm* — "education".

> التعليم مجاني في هذا البلد.
>
> *at-taʿlīm majjānī fī hādhā al-balad.*
>
> Education is free in this country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعليم** (*taʿlīm*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-l-m*, "to know" (Lane). *Taʿlīm* is the Form II verbal noun, making someone know: teaching, education.
