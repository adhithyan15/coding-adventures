## You'll want to know: うけとる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C189-PARCEL189-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うけとる** — *uketoru* — "to accept; to receive, to take delivery of".

> **うけつけで にもつを うけとります。** — *uketsuke de nimotsu o uketorimasu* — "I collect the package at reception."

Polite **うけとります**, *uketorimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うけとる** (*uketoru*) is native Japanese, built from *uke*, the stem of *ukeru*, "to receive", and *toru*, "to take", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
