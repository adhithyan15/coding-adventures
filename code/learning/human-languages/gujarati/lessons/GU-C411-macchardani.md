## You'll want to know: મચ્છરદાની
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C411-QUILT411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મચ્છરદાની** — *macchardānī* — "a mosquito net".

> રાત્રે અમે પલંગ પર મચ્છરદાની બાંધીએ છીએ.
>
> *rātre ame palaṅg par macchardānī bā̃dhīe chīe.*
>
> At night we hang a mosquito net over the bed.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મચ્છરદાની** (*macchardānī*) is built from મચ્છર, a mosquito, and -દાની, "a holder, a container", an ending usually traced to Persian *-dān*: a holder that keeps the mosquitoes out.
