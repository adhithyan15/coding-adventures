## You'll want to know: ふだん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C154-HABIT154-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ふだん** — *fudan* — "usually; everyday".

> **ふだんは うちで ごはんを たべます。** — *fudan wa uchi de gohan o tabemasu* — "I usually eat at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふだん** (*fudan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fu*, "general, widely" (Middle Chinese *phuX*) and *dan*, "step, grade" (*dwanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
