Lesson 1 of 6.
सर्वेक्षण (sarvekṣaṇ) — a survey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a thesis, then the Marathi for a theory.

You'll want to know: सर्वेक्षण.
सर्वेक्षण — sarvekṣaṇ — "a survey".
संस्थेने गावात आरोग्याचं सर्वेक्षण केलं. saṁsthene gāvāt ārogyācaṁ sarvekṣaṇ kelaṁ. The organization carried out a health survey in the village.
Neuter.
सर्व + ईक्षण, all + looking.

The word, taken apart.
सर्वेक्षण (sarvekṣaṇ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit sarva, "all", and īkṣaṇa, "look, view" (Monier-Williams): a view over everything.

You'll want to know: आकडेवारी.
आकडेवारी — ākḍevārī — "statistics; figures".
सर्वेक्षणाची आकडेवारी उद्या जाहीर होईल. sarvekṣaṇācī ākḍevārī udyā jāhīr hoīl. The survey's figures will be announced tomorrow.
Feminine.
आकडा, a figure, + -वारी: figures taken together.

The word, taken apart.
आकडेवारी (ākḍevārī) is built on आकडा, "a figure, a number", with the ending -वारी; the origin of आकडा is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
पद्धत (paddhat) — a method; a way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for statistics, then the Marathi for an analysis.

You'll want to know: पद्धत.
पद्धत — paddhat — "a method; a way".
शिकवण्याची त्यांची पद्धत वेगळी आहे. śikvaṇyācī tyāñcī paddhat vegḷī āhe. Their way of teaching is different.
Feminine.
Feminine: X ची पद्धत (paddhat), the way of X.

The word, taken apart.
पद्धत (paddhat) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit paddhati, "a path, a track, a way" (Monier-Williams). Marathi drops the final -i.
