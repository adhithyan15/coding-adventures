## You'll want to know: うんめい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C508-LUCK508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うんめい** — *unmei* — "fate; destiny".

> **もし あねが まちで かれに あったら、うんめいでしょう。** — *moshi ane ga machi de kare ni attara, unmei deshō* — "If my elder sister met him in town, it would be fate."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うんめい** (*unmei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *un*, "luck" (Middle Chinese *hjunH*) and *mei*, "fate" (*mjaengH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
