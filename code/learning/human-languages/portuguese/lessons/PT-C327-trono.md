## You'll want to know: o trono
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C327-REINO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o trono** — "the throne".

> O rei sentou-se no trono.
>
> *The king sat down on the throne.*

*Subir ao trono* — to come to the throne.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trono* comes down from Old Portuguese (EtymDB) and goes back to Latin *thronus*, from Greek *thrónos* "a seat, a chair" (Lewis & Short).
