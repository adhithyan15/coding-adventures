Portuguese, chapter 103: Jumper, Tie, Gloves, Scarf, Boots.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
a camisola — a jumper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a boy, then the Portuguese for a rainbow.

You'll want to know: a camisola.
a camisola — "a jumper".

What you've built.
