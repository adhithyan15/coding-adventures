## You'll want to know: جادل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C360-JADAL360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جادل** — *jādala* — "to argue".

> جادل الولد أمه حول وقت النوم.
>
> *jādala al-walad ummahu ḥawla waqt an-nawm.*
>
> The boy argued with his mother about bedtime.

Present: يجادل, *yujādil*.

You argue with someone directly, with no preposition: جادله, argued with him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جادل** (*jādala*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-d-l*, "to twist firmly" (Lane). It is Form III, to twist against someone: to argue with him.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
