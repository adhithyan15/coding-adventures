German, chapter 204: Leather, Wood, Metal, Plastic, The body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Leder — leather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for wool, then the German for silk.

You'll want to know: das Leder.
das Leder — "leather".

The word, taken apart.
Leder from Old High German ledar from Proto-Germanic leþrą — inherited, the same word as English leather. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Kunststoff — plastic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for wood, then the German for metal.

You'll want to know: der Kunststoff.
der Kunststoff — "plastic".

The word, taken apart.
Kunststoff is the word for "art, skill" + Stoff, "material": material made by skill, not found in nature. Kunst is built on können, "to be able", inherited from Proto-Germanic kunnaną (EtymDB 2.1).
