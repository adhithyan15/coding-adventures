## You'll want to know: arguō, arguere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C310-IURO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**arguō, arguere** — "to prove; to accuse".

> Haec epistula eum mentītum esse arguit.
>
> *This letter proves that he lied.*

*arguō, arguere, arguī, argūtum*: to make clear, and so to prove or to charge. English *argue*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**arguō** first meant "to make clear"; Lewis & Short connect it with the root of *argentum*, "silver", and Greek *argos*, "bright". English *argue* comes from it through French.
