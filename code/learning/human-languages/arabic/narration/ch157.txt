Arabic, chapter 157: Next, From Now On, a Programme, To Decide, To Intend.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
القادم (al-qādim) — next, coming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for once, then the Arabic for to spend.

You'll want to know: القادم.
القادم — al-qādim — "next, coming". The future puts sa- in front of the present: سأسافر الأسبوع القادم — sa-usāfiru al-usbūʿa al-qādim — "I will travel next week."
