## You'll want to know: ばんごはん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C172-MEALS172-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ばんごはん** — *bangohan* — "dinner; evening meal".

> **ばんごはんは いつも うちで たべます。** — *bangohan wa itsumo uchi de tabemasu* — "I always eat dinner at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばんごはん** (*bangohan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ban*, "evening" (Middle Chinese *mjonX*), *go*, "honourable" (*ngjoH*) and *han*, "cooked rice" (*bjonH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
