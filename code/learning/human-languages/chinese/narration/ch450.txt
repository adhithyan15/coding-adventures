Lesson 1 of 6.
hǎixiān — seafood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for light and mild (of food), then the Mandarin for greasy (of food).

You'll want to know: hǎixiān.
hǎixiān — "seafood".
zhè jiā fànguǎn de hǎixiān hěn xīnxiān — The seafood at this restaurant is very fresh.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
hǎixiān is built from 海 'the sea' and xiān 'fresh', both inherited from Middle Chinese, xojX and sjen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'fresh' and 'clean'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
特点 (tèdiǎn) — characteristic; a distinguishing trait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for greasy (of food), then the Mandarin for seafood.

You'll want to know: 特点.
特点 — tèdiǎn — "characteristic; a distinguishing trait".
这儿的菜有什么特点 zhèr de cài yǒu shénme tèdiǎn — What's special about the food here?
The new character, in pieces: 特 (tè) — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left the cow piece, four strokes; on the right the earth piece of 地 over the right piece of 对. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
特点 (tèdiǎn) is built from 特 (tè) 'single, special' and 点 'a point', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dok and temX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a bull and 'alone' and the second as a dot made with the brush.

Script — the cow piece, then two pieces stacked.
特 (tè).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, the cow piece, four strokes. A short stroke falling to the left. A bar. A long vertical down through the bar. Then a stroke that rises to the right.
The right piece, six strokes, in two parts. On top, the earth piece of 地 with its last bar long: a short bar, a vertical, and a long bar under them. Under it, the right piece of 对: a bar, a vertical with a hook, and a dot.
4 + 6 = ten.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 4 of 6.
特意 (tèyì) — specially; for that very purpose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for seafood, then the Mandarin for a distinguishing trait.

You'll want to know: 特意.
特意 — tèyì — "specially; for that very purpose".
我们特意去吃这儿的风味小吃 wǒmen tèyì qù chī zhèr de fēngwèi xiǎochī — We went specially to try the local snacks here.

The word, taken apart.
特意 (tèyì) is built from 特 (tè) 'single' and 意 'an intention', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dok and 'iH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the will'.
