## You'll want to know: la bussola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C439-ORIENTARSI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la bussola** — "the compass".

> In montagna porto la bussola.
>
> *In the mountains I carry a compass.*

*Perdere la bussola* — to lose your head.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bussola* is inherited from Late Latin *buxula* 'little box' (EtymDB), from *buxus* 'boxwood': the needle sat in a small wooden box.
