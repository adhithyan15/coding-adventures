## You'll want to know: cānjiā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C84-TOGETHER84-04]; assesses=[] -->

*cānjiā* — "to take part in; to join".

> *nǐ xiǎng cānjiā ma* — Would you like to join in?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cānjiā* is built from cān 'to attend' and jiā 'to add', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshom* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
