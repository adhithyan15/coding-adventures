## You'll want to know: cōnventiō, cōnventiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C397-PACTUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnventiō, cōnventiōnis** — "an agreement, a covenant; an assembly".

> Cōnventiō inter eōs facta est.
>
> *An agreement was made between them.*

*cōnventiō, cōnventiōnis* f., built on *conveniō*, to come together: *convenit*, it is agreed. English *convention*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnventiō* is the noun built on *conveniō* "to come together, to agree" (Lewis & Short). English *convention* comes from it.
