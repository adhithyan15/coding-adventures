## You'll want to know: शीलम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C358-ACHARA358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शीलम्** — *śīlam* — "character; disposition".

> बालस्य शीलं सुन्दरम् अस्ति।
>
> *bālasya śīlaṃ sundaram asti.*
>
> The boy has a fine character.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शीलम्** (*śīlam*) MW gives *śīla* "habit, custom, way of living, conduct, disposition" and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
