## You'll want to know: o assalto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C288-ASSALTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o assalto** — "the hold-up, the robbery with force".

> O assalto ao banco durou dez minutos.
>
> *The bank hold-up lasted ten minutes.*

*Assalto à mão armada* — armed robbery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assalto* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *adsaltāre* "to jump at", from Latin *saltāre* "to jump, to leap" (Lewis & Short).
