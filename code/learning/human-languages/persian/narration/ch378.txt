Persian, chapter 378: Earning a living.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دستمزد (dastmozd) — a wage, pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for prevention, then the Persian for to matter.

You'll want to know: دستمزد.
دستمزد (dastmozd) — "a wage, pay".
دستمزد کارگرها کم است. dastmozd-e kârgarhâ kam ast. The workers' wages are low.
Dast "hand" + mozd "wage". For daily or hourly pay; a monthly salary is hoquq.

The word, taken apart.
دستمزد (dastmozd) is a native Persian compound built from dast "hand", which EtymDB traces to PIE ǵʰes- "hand", and mozd "wage", usually traced to Middle Persian mizd: pay for the work of your hands.

Lesson 2 of 5.
درآمد (darâmad) — an income.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to matter, then the Persian for a wage.

You'll want to know: درآمد.
درآمد (darâmad) — "an income".
درآمد او از کار تازه بیشتر است. darâmad-e u az kâr-e tâze bishtar ast. His income from the new job is higher.
Dar "in" + âmad "came": what comes in. Plural darâmadhâ.

The word, taken apart.
درآمد (darâmad) is native Persian, built from dar "in" + âmad, the past stem of âmadan "to come", which EtymDB traces to PIE gʷem- "to step". Income is what comes in.

Lesson 3 of 5.
اضافه کاری (ezâfe kâri) — overtime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a wage, then the Persian for an income.

You'll want to know: اضافه کاری.
اضافه کاری (ezâfe kâri) — "overtime".
این ماه خیلی اضافه کاری کردم. in mâh kheyli ezâfe kâri kardam. This month I did a lot of overtime.
Ezâfe "extra" + kâri "working". Ezâfe kâri kardan — to work overtime.

The word, taken apart.
اضافه کاری (ezâfe kâri) joins ezâfe, from Arabic iḍāfa "addition, adjunct" (EtymDB), to kâri, from the inherited Persian kâr "work" (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian): added work.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پاداش (pâdâsh) — a bonus, a reward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an income, then the Persian for overtime.

You'll want to know: پاداش.
پاداش (pâdâsh) — "a bonus, a reward".
آخر سال همه کارمندها پاداش گرفتند. âkhar-e sâl hame-ye kârmandhâ pâdâsh gereftand. At the end of the year all the employees got a bonus.
Also a reward in a story; its opposite is mojâzât, a punishment.

The word, taken apart.
پاداش (pâdâsh) is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian pādāšn "reward, retribution", what is given back for a deed.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مزایا (mazâyâ) — benefits, perks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for overtime, then the Persian for a bonus.

You'll want to know: مزایا.
مزایا (mazâyâ) — "benefits, perks".
این شغل حقوق خوب و مزایای زیادی دارد. in shoghl hoquq-e khub va mazâyâ-ye ziyâdi dârad. This job has a good salary and a lot of benefits.
The Arabic broken plural of maziyat "advantage"; at work, insurance, bonuses and leave.

The word, taken apart.
مزایا (mazâyâ) is usually traced to Arabic mazāyā, the broken plural of maziyya "merit, advantage", the word behind maziyat.
