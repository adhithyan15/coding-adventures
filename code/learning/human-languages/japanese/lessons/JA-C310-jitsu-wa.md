## You'll want to know: じつは
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C310-HENJI310-02]; assesses=[] -->

**じつは** — *jitsu wa* — "actually; to tell the truth".

> **じつは、きのう びょういんに いきました。** — *jitsu wa, kinō byōin ni ikimashita* — "To tell the truth, I went to the hospital yesterday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じつは** (*jitsu wa*) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: *jitsu*, "truth", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *zyit*), and *wa*, "the topic particle", is native Japanese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
