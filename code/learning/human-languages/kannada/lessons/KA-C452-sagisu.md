# ಸಾಗಿಸು (sāgisu) — to transport; to carry off

## You'll want to know: ಸಾಗಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C452-SARIGE452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಗಿಸು** — *sāgisu* — "to transport; to carry off".

> ರೈತರು ತಮ್ಮ ಭತ್ತವನ್ನು ಲಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಾಗಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *raitaru tamma bhattavannu lāriyalli sāgisidaru.*
>
> The farmers carried their paddy away by lorry.

Past (I …): ಸಾಗಿಸಿದೆ, *sāgiside*.

ಸಾಗು, to move along, with -ಇಸು: to make go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಗಿಸು** (*sāgisu*) is native Dravidian: the causative of *sāgu*, "to go, to move forward, to proceed" (DEDR 2430).
