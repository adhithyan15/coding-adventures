# (reading) — the answers, on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson gave you six words. These are six you would answer
with.

[PAUSE 1s] Nothing here is new. What is new is meeting them where nobody is
speaking.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SKILL-READ-ANSWERS]; assesses=[FA-LEX-BALE-01, FA-LEX-NA-01] -->

> بله
> نه
> ممنون
> سلام
> اول
> سوم

[PAUSE 3s] Read down once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and notice the list changes halfway. The first four are
things you say to a person. The last two are things you read off a door.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-AVVAL, FA-LEX-SEVVOM, FA-SKILL-READ-ANSWERS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **اول** and **سوم** are the two you are most likely to meet written
before you ever meet them spoken: a floor, a platform, a queue.

[PAUSE 2s] That is the argument for reading them now. An answer you can only
recognise by ear is one you can only give when somebody asks out loud. The same
words on a sign do not wait for you.

[PAUSE 2s] Six lines, and the last two are the ones a street will hand you
first.
