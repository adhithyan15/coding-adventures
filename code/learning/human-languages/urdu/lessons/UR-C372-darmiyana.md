## You'll want to know: درمیانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C372-JAMNA372-05]; assesses=[] -->

**درمیانہ** — *darmiyānā* — "medium; moderate".

> اگر آگ درمیانی رکھیں گے تو کھانا مزیدار بنے گا۔
>
> *agar āg darmiyānī rakheṅ ge to khānā mazedār bane gā.*
>
> If you keep the heat medium, the food will turn out tasty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درمیانہ** (*darmiyānā*) is usually traced to Persian *darmiyāna*, "middle", built on *darmiyān*, "between".
