Marathi, chapter 158: To wipe, To sweep, To quarrel, To obtain, To save.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
पुसणे (pusṇe) — to wipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get tired, then the Marathi for to stay awake.

You'll want to know: पुसणे.
पुसणे — pusṇe — "to wipe".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: pusṇe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pusṇe, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say pusṇe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for to get tired, then the Marathi for to stay awake, then say pusṇe again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does पुसणे (pusṇe) mean? ("To wipe".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: stale, raw, calm, lazy, hard-working]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: honest, brave, timid, foolish, famous]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: safe, dangerous, false, purple, orange]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: grey, golden, to climb, to get down, to arrive]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Stale? (शिळा, śiḷā.) Raw? (कच्चा, kaccā.) Calm? (शांत, śānt.) Lazy? (आळशी, āḷśī.) Hard-working? (मेहनती, mehantī.) Honest? (प्रामाणिक, prāmāṇik.) Brave? (धाडसी, dhāḍsī.) Timid? (भित्रा, bhitrā.) Foolish? (मूर्ख, mūrkh.) Famous? (प्रसिद्ध, prasiddh.) Safe? (सुरक्षित, surakṣit.) Dangerous? (धोकादायक, dhokādāyak.) False? (खोटे, khoṭe.) Purple? (जांभळा, jāmbhaḷā.) Orange? (नारिंगी, nāriṅgī.) Grey? (करडा, karḍā.) Golden? (सोनेरी, sonerī.) To climb? (चढणे, caḍhṇe.) To get down? (उतरणे, utarṇe.) To arrive? (पोहोचणे, pohocṇe.)
