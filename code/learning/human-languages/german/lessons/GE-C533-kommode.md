## You'll want to know: die Kommode
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C533-GEMUETLICH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kommode** — "chest of drawers".

> Die Socken liegen in der Kommode im Schlafzimmer.
>
> *The socks are in the chest of drawers in the bedroom.*

Plural: *die Kommoden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kommode* was borrowed from French *commode* "chest of drawers" (EtymDB), from the adjective *commode* "handy", from Latin *commodus* "convenient".
