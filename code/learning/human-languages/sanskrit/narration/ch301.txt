Lesson 1 of 6.
मेघः (meghaḥ) — a cloud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for cracks open, then the Sanskrit for a pit.

You'll want to know: मेघः.
मेघः — meghaḥ — "a cloud".
आकाशे कृष्णाः मेघाः सन्ति। ākāśe kṛṣṇāḥ meghāḥ santi. There are dark clouds in the sky.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मेघः (meghaḥ) comes from the root √mih (MW writes it √migh here), "to sprinkle": MW explains megha as the sprinkler, a cloud (MW).

Lesson 2 of 6.
चक्रवातः (cakravātaḥ) — a whirlwind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a pit, then the Sanskrit for a cloud.

You'll want to know: चक्रवातः.
चक्रवातः — cakravātaḥ — "a whirlwind".
समुद्रात् चक्रवातः आगच्छति। samudrāt cakravātaḥ āgacchati. A whirlwind is coming from the sea.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चक्रवातः (cakravātaḥ) is built from cakra, "a wheel", and vāta, "wind"; MW gives cakravāta as a whirlwind, and a cyclone is its modern use (MW).

स्फुरति — sphurati — "flashes; flickers".
मेघेषु विद्युत् स्फुरति। megheṣu vidyut sphurati. Lightning flashes in the clouds.

The word, taken apart.
स्फुरति (sphurati) comes from the root √sphur, "to dart, quiver, flash"; MW compares English spur and spurn (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
उन्मूलयति (unmūlayati) — uproots.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for whirls, then the Sanskrit for flashes.

You'll want to know: उन्मूलयति.
उन्मूलयति — unmūlayati — "uproots".
चक्रवातः वृक्षान् उन्मूलयति। cakravātaḥ vṛkṣān unmūlayati. The whirlwind uproots trees.
Past (it …): वायुः वृक्षान् उन्मूलितवान्, vāyuḥ vṛkṣān unmūlitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उन्मूलयति (unmūlayati) is built from ud, "out", and mūla, "a root"; MW gives unmūlayati as to pull up by the roots (MW).
