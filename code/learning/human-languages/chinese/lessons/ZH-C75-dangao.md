## You'll want to know: dàngāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C75-PARTY75-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dàngāo* — "cake".

> *māma de dàngāo hěn hǎochī* — Mum's cake is delicious.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàngāo* is built from dàn 'egg', the dàn of jīdàn, and gāo 'cake'. No dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled; the second is probably the *kaw* that the Guangyun writes with another form of the character (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed with a word for gruel.
