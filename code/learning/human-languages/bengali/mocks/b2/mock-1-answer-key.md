## Writing

Task 1 must address Lina, identify who loses access under the library's revised
hours, give one concrete example, and propose a testable alternative in 100–130
independently produced Bengali words. Task 2 must sustain a developed
220–280-word argument about resident and expert authority over small public
spaces, considering both sides, evidence, an implementation condition, and a
reasoned conclusion.

## Speaking

Allow keyword notes only. The presentation must develop an inclusive local
service proposal with an objection and success measure. In the market-renewal
collaboration, require genuine comparison, challenge, repair, and a qualified
joint recommendation. The five follow-ups must probe exclusion, evidence,
counterargument, revision, and failure without supplying language.
