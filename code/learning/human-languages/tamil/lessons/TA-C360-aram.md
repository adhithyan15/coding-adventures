## You'll want to know: அறம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C360-NIYAYAM360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**அறம்** — *aṟam* — "ethics; virtue".

> அறம் இல்லாத வாழ்க்கை பயனற்றது.
>
> *aṟam illāta vāḻkkai payaṉaṟṟatu.*
>
> A life without virtue is worthless.

An old Tamil word; அறவழி, the path of virtue. நீதி is justice between people, அறம் the good in a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அறம்** (*aṟam*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 311 lists Tamil *aṟam*, "moral duty, virtue", and *aṟavaṉ*, "one who is virtuous".
