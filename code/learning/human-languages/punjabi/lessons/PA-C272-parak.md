## You'll want to know: ਪਾਰਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C272-CROWD272-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਰਕ** — *pārak* — "a park".

> ਬੱਚੇ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਪਾਰਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੇਡਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *bachche shām nū̃ pārak vich kheḍde han.*
>
> The children play in the park in the evening.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਰਕ** (*pārak*) is borrowed from English *park*.
