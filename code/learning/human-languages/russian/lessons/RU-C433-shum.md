## You'll want to know: шум
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C433-SHUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**шум** (*shum*) — "noise".

> Из-за шума на улице я плохо спал.
>
> *Iz-za shúma na úlitse ya plókho spal.*
>
> Because of the noise in the street I slept badly.

Masculine. *Шуметь* — to make noise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*шум* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *šumŭ* (EtymDB), the root of *шумный* 'noisy'.
