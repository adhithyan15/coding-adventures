## You'll want to know: がけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C276-OOAME276-04]; assesses=[] -->

**がけ** — *gake* — "a cliff; a steep slope".

> **おおあめの あと、がけが くずれました。** — *ōame no ato, gake ga kuzuremashita* — "After the downpour, the slope gave way."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がけ** (*gake*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
