## You'll want to know: गुलिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C517-CATURANGA517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गुलिका** — *gulikā* — "a marble; a small ball".

> बालः उद्याने गुलिकया क्रीडति।
>
> *bālaḥ udyāne gulikayā krīḍati.*
>
> The boy plays marbles in the park.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुलिका** (*gulikā*) is the word MW records, the same as *guḍikā*, for a ball, a small globule, and a ball to play with; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
