## You'll want to know: تجول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C438-SIYAHA438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تجول** — *tajawwala* — "to wander around; to tour".

> تجولنا في السوق حتى المساء.
>
> *tajawwalnā fī as-sūq ḥattā al-masāʾ.*
>
> We wandered around the market until the evening.

Present: يتجول, *yatajawwal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجول** (*tajawwala*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-w-l*, "to go round, to roam" (Lane). It is Form V.
