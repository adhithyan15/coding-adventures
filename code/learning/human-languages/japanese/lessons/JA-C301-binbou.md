## You'll want to know: びんぼう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C301-TAKARA301-04]; assesses=[] -->

**びんぼう** — *binbō* — "poor; poverty".

> **その おじいさんは びんぼうでした。** — *sono ojiisan wa binbō deshita* — "The old man was poor."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**びんぼう** (*binbō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bin*, "poor" (Middle Chinese *bin*) and *bō*, "scarce" (*bjop*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
