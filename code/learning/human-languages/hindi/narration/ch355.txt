Hindi, chapter 355: Offices and Officials.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विभाग (vibhāg) — a department.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for democracy, then the Hindi for to impose.

You'll want to know: विभाग.
विभाग — vibhāg — "a department".
मेरा भाई बिजली विभाग में काम करता है। merā bhāī bijlī vibhāg meṁ kām kartā hai. My brother works in the electricity department.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विभाग (vibhāg) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit vibhāga, "a division, a share" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुझाना (sujhānā) — to suggest, to put (an idea) to someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an official, then the Hindi for a command.

You'll want to know: सुझाना.
सुझाना — sujhānā — "to suggest, to put (an idea) to someone".
अधिकारी ने हमें एक नया रास्ता सुझाया। adhikārī ne hamẽ ek nayā rāstā sujhāyā. The official suggested a new way to us.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने एक उपाय सुझाया, usne ek upāy sujhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
सुझाना (sujhānā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit śudhyati, "becomes clear" (CDIAL 12527). CDIAL glosses the Hindi sujhānā "to explain"; the sense of suggesting is not among its glosses, though it is close: making an idea clear to someone.
