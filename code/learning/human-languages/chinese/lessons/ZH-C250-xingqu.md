## You'll want to know: xìngqù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C250-HOBBY250-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xìngqù* — "interest; interest in something".

> *wǒ méiyǒu xìngqù* — I'm not interested.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìngqù* is built from *xìng* and *qù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xingH* and *tshjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'to tend towards'; it gives no sense for the first in this tone, and its level-tone reading means 'to rise'. How the pair came to mean an interest is not settled.
