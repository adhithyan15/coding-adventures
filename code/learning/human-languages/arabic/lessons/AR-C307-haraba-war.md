# حارب (ḥāraba) — to wage war; to fight against

## You'll want to know: حارب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C307-JAYSH307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حارب** — *ḥāraba* — "to wage war; to fight against".

> حارب الأمير جيشا كبيرا.
>
> *ḥāraba al-amīr jayshan kabīran.*
>
> The prince fought a large army.

Present: يحارب, *yuḥārib*.

It also fights a problem: حاربت المدينة التلوث.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حارب** (*ḥāraba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-b* (Lane), the root of *ḥarb*, war. It is Form III, "to make war with someone".
