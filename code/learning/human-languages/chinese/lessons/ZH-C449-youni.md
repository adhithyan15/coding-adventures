## You'll want to know: yóunì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C449-TASTE449-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yóunì* — "greasy; oily".

> *yèshì de dōngxi tài yóunì le* — The food at the night market is too greasy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóunì* is built from *yóu* 'oil' and *nì* 'fatty', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yuw* and *nrijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as oil and fat and the second as fat and greasy.
