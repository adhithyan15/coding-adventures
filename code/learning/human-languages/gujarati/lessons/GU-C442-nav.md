## You'll want to know: નાવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C442-SEA442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નાવ** — *nāv* — "a river boat".

> નાવમાં બેસીને અમે નદી પાર કરી.
>
> *nāvmā̃ besīne ame nadī pār karī.*
>
> We crossed the river in a boat.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નાવ** (*nāv*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *nāvā*, "a boat"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 7081). Songs and stories prefer it to હોડી.
