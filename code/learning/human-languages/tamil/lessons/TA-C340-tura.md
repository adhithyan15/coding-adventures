## You'll want to know: துற
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C340-VEEZHCHI340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**துற** — *tuṟa* — "to renounce; to give up".

> மன்னர் அரியணையைத் துறந்து காட்டுக்குச் சென்றார்.
>
> *maṉṉar ariyaṇaiyait tuṟantu kāṭṭukkuc ceṉṟār.*
>
> The king gave up the throne and went to the forest.

Past (I …): துறந்தேன், *tuṟantēṉ*.

What is given up takes -ஐ; துறவி, a monk, is one who has renounced.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**துற** (*tuṟa*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3365): to leave, relinquish, forsake; it takes its past in -ந்த-, துறந்தேன்.
