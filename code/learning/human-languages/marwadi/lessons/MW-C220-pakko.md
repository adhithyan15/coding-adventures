## You'll want to know: पक्को
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C220-PLAN220-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्को** — *pakko* — "settled; firm".

> बात पक्की है, हूं काल जासूं।
>
> *bāt pakkī hai, hū̃ kāl jāsū̃.*
>
> It's settled: I'm going tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्को** (*pakko*) goes back to Sanskrit *pakva*, "cooked, ripe". Its doubled *k* matches Hindi *pakkā*, "ripe, mature, real", which CDIAL takes from Panjabi; the Old Marwari word CDIAL lists in the same entry is *pāka*, "ripe" (CDIAL 7621), the plain inherited shape. From *ripe* it has come to mean *firm*, *settled*.
