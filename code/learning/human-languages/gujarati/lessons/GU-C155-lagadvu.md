## You'll want to know: લગાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C155-READY155-05]; assesses=[] -->

**લગાડવું** — *lagāḍvũ* — "to apply, to put on".

> **તે વાળમાં તેલ લગાડે છે.** — *te vāḷmā̃ tel lagāḍe che* — "She puts oil in her hair."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લગાડવું** (*lagāḍvũ*) is built on the inherited *lāgvũ*, "to touch, to come into contact", with the causative *-āḍ-*. CDIAL traces *lāgvũ* to Sanskrit *lagyati*, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895).
