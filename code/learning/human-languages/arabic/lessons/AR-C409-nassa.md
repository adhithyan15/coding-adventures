# نص (naṣṣa) — to stipulate in writing

## You'll want to know: نص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C409-QANUN409-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نص** — *naṣṣa* — "to stipulate in writing".

> ينص القانون على حق التعليم.
>
> *yanuṣṣu al-qānūn ʿalā ḥaqq at-taʿlīm.*
>
> The law stipulates the right to education.

Present: ينص, *yanuṣṣ*.

It takes على: ينص على …. The noun نص, *naṣṣ*, means a text.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نص** (*naṣṣa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ṣ-ṣ*, "to raise, to set up" (Lane). *Naṣṣa* is a Form I verb; from setting a thing up plainly comes stating it in writing: to stipulate.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
