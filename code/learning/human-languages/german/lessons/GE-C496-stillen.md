## You'll want to know: stillen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C496-BABY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**stillen** — "to breastfeed; to quench".

> Sie stillt ihr Baby alle drei Stunden.
>
> *She breastfeeds her baby every three hours.*

Also to quench: *den Durst stillen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stillen* is built on *still* "quiet, calm" (EtymDB): first to make quiet, then to calm a baby at the breast.
