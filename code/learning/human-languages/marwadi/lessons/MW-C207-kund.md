## You'll want to know: कुंड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C207-GARDEN207-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंड** — *kuṇḍ* — "a water tank".

> कुंड मांय पाणी कोनी।
>
> *kuṇḍ mā̃y pāṇī konī.*
>
> There's no water in the tank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंड** (*kuṇḍ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kuṇḍa*, "bowl, basin of water". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *kū̃ḍ*, "tub", and Gujarati *kũḍ*, "basin" (CDIAL 3264).
