## You'll want to know: అద్దె
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C156-CADUVU156-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అద్దె** — *adde* — "rent". The money you pay to live in a house: **ఇంటి అద్దె** — *iṇṭi adde* — "the rent for the house".
