Persian, chapter 349: Betrayal and battle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خیانت کردن (khiyânat kardan) — to betray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a treasure, then the Persian for fate.

You'll want to know: خیانت کردن.
خیانت کردن (khiyânat kardan) — "to betray".
برادرش به او خیانت کرد. Barâdarash be u khiyânat kard. His brother betrayed him.
The person betrayed takes be. Present khiyânat mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
khiyânat is borrowed from Arabic khiyāna, "treason, betrayal" (EtymDB). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
انتقام گرفتن (enteqâm gereftan) — to take revenge, to avenge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for fate, then the Persian for to betray.

You'll want to know: انتقام گرفتن.
انتقام گرفتن (enteqâm gereftan) — "to take revenge, to avenge".
او سال ها بعد انتقام پدرش را گرفت. U sâl-hâ ba'd enteqâm-e pedarash râ gereft. Years later he avenged his father.
Enteqâm-e kasi râ gereftan "to avenge someone"; az kasi enteqâm gereftan "to take revenge on someone".

The word, taken apart.
enteqâm is borrowed from Arabic intiqām, "revenge" (EtymDB). gereftan "to take" is inherited Persian, from the Old Persian root grab-.

جنگیدن (jangidan) — "to fight".
سربازها تا صبح جنگیدند. Sarbâz-hâ tâ sobh jangidand. The soldiers fought until morning.
jang "war" + the verb suffix -idan. Present mijangad.

The word, taken apart.
jangidan is built from Persian jang "war", a native noun, turned into a verb with the suffix -idan.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کشتن (koshtan) — to kill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to take revenge, then the Persian for to fight.

You'll want to know: کشتن.
کشتن (koshtan) — "to kill".
پهلوان اژدها را کشت. Pahlavân ezhdehâ râ kosht. The champion killed the dragon.
Present stem kosh-: mikoshad. Koshte shodan is "to be killed".

The word, taken apart.
koshtan is a native Persian verb with the present stem kosh-; EtymDB lists it without a parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اسیر شدن (asir shodan) — to be taken prisoner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

اسیر شدن (asir shodan) — "to be taken prisoner".
سربازهای زیادی در جنگ اسیر شدند. Sarbâz-hâ-ye ziyâdi dar jang asir shodand. Many soldiers were taken prisoner in the war.
Asir "a captive". Present asir mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
asir is borrowed from Arabic asīr, "captive, prisoner" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.
