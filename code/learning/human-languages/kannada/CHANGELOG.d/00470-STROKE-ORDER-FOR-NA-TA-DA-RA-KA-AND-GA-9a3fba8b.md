## Stroke order for na, ta, da, ra, ka and ga

Six consonants the book has taught since chapters 1 to 7 now have a cited
stroke order: ನ (KA-S01), ಗ (KA-S112), ತ (KA-S05), ದ (KA-S06), ರ (KA-S07) and
ಕ (KA-S116). Their writing blocks stop saying "copy what you see" and list
the movements, the pen lifts and the source, in the form the vowel lessons of
chapter 78 already use, and nine lessons now print a stroke-order filmstrip:
the six letter lessons and KA-S01's guided copy, delayed copy and dictation.

- **Source.** Gopala Krishna A's `Kannada-alphabet-<slug>.gif` animations on
  Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0, 25 May 2016), the same series the vowels
  cite. Every animation draws the body first and the hooked top bar last.
  ನ, ತ, ದ, ರ and ಗ take one lift, ಕ three: the bowl, the lower bar, the
  short link and the upper bar are separate runs.
- **ತ and ದ are filed as "tha" and "dha".** That series spells the dental
  letters with an *h* and uses "ta" and "da" for ಟ and ಡ. The KA-S05 and
  KA-S06 writing blocks say so next to the citation.
- KA-S135 (ಅ, chapter 68) said that *every* earlier character had only asked
  the reader to copy what they saw. That is no longer true, so it now says
  *many*.
