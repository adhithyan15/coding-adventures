## You'll want to know: தஞ்சம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C303-KOOCHAL303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தஞ்சம்** — *tañcam* — "refuge; shelter".

> மக்கள் பள்ளியில் தஞ்சம் புகுந்தார்கள்.
>
> *makkaḷ paḷḷiyil tañcam pukuntārkaḷ.*
>
> People took refuge in the school.

தஞ்சம் புகு, to take refuge; தஞ்சம் கொடு, to give shelter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தஞ்சம்** (*tañcam*), "refuge, shelter", is a Tamil word whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
