## You'll want to know: दिमाग़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C478-PHEPHDA478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दिमाग़** — *dimāġ* — "the brain; the mind".

> पढ़ाई से दिमाग़ तेज़ होता है।
>
> *paṛhāī se dimāġ tez hotā hai.*
>
> Studying sharpens the mind.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिमाग़** (*dimāġ*) is usually traced to Arabic *dimāġ*, "brain", which reached Hindi through Persian.
