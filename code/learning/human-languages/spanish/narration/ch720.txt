Spanish, chapter 720: Admirar y agradecer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la admiración — admiration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for worry, then the Spanish for worried.

You'll want to know: la admiración.
la admiración — "admiration".
Siento admiración por los médicos. I feel admiration for doctors.
Signo de admiración — exclamation mark.

The word, taken apart.
admiración is a learned borrowing from Latin admīrātiō (EtymDB), built on admīrārī "to wonder at", from mīrārī "to marvel".

Lesson 3 of 5.
agradecido — grateful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for admiration, then the Spanish for to admire.

You'll want to know: agradecido.
agradecido — "grateful".
Te estoy muy agradecido. I am very grateful to you.
Estar agradecido por algo. Muy agradecido — much obliged.

The word, taken apart.
agradecido is the participle of agradecer, built on old gradecer, from grado "goodwill", which is usually traced to Latin grātum "pleasing".

Lesson 5 of 5.
apreciar — to appreciate, to value.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for grateful, then the Spanish for gratitude.

You'll want to know: apreciar.
apreciar — "to appreciate, to value".
Aprecio mucho tu ayuda. I really appreciate your help.
Aprecio a mis compañeros — I am fond of my colleagues.

The word, taken apart.
apreciar is a learned borrowing from Late Latin appretiāre "to put a price on" (EtymDB), built on pretium "price", the source of precio.
