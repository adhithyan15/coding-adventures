## You'll want to know: அகதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C381-TECAM381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**அகதி** — *akati* — "a refugee".

> போரால் பல குடும்பங்கள் அகதிகளாக இங்கே வந்தன.
>
> *pōrāl pala kuṭumpaṅkaḷ akatikaḷāka iṅkē vantaṉa.*
>
> Because of the war, many families came here as refugees.

Plural: அகதிகள், *akatikaḷ*.

அகதியாக, as a refugee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அகதி** (*akati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *agati*, "not going; without resource, helpless" (Monier-Williams): one who has no place to go.
