## You'll want to know: शपति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C332-AKHYANA332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शपति** — *śapati* — "curses".

> कुपितः ऋषिः तं शपति।
>
> *kupitaḥ ṛṣiḥ taṃ śapati.*
>
> The angry seer curses him.

Past (he …): सः तं शप्तवान्, *saḥ taṃ śaptavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शपति** (*śapati*) comes from the root √śap, "to curse, swear" (MW).
