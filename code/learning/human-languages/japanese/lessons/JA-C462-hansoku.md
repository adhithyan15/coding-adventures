## You'll want to know: はんそく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C462-CONTEST462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はんそく** — *hansoku* — "a foul (in sport)".

> **せんしゅが はんそくを しました。** — *senshu ga hansoku o shimashita* — "A player committed a foul."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はんそく** (*hansoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *han*, "anti-" (Middle Chinese *pjonX*) and *soku*, "rule" (*tsok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
