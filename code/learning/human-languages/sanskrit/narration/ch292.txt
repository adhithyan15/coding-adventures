Lesson 1 of 5.
चरः (caraḥ) — a spy; a secret agent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for fraud, then the Sanskrit for fake.

You'll want to know: चरः.
चरः — caraḥ — "a spy; a secret agent".
राजा चरं प्रेषयति। rājā caraṃ preṣayati. The king sends a spy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चरः (caraḥ) comes from the root √car, "to move, go about"; MW gives cara as a spy or secret agent (MW).

You'll want to know: प्रमाणम्.
प्रमाणम् — pramāṇam — "proof; evidence".
चरः प्रमाणम् अन्विष्यति। caraḥ pramāṇam anviṣyati. The spy searches for proof.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रमाणम् (pramāṇam) is built from pra and the root √mā, "to measure": a pramāṇa is first a measure or standard, and MW also gives it as any proof or evidence (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a spy, then the Sanskrit for proof.

You'll want to know: परीक्षते.
परीक्षते — parīkṣate — "examines".
चरः चिह्नं परीक्षते। caraḥ cihnaṃ parīkṣate. The spy examines the mark.
Past (he …): सः चिह्नं परीक्षितवान्, saḥ cihnaṃ parīkṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
परीक्षते (parīkṣate) is built from pari, "around", and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives parīkṣ as to look round, inspect carefully and examine (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रकटयति (prakaṭayati) — reveals.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for examines, then the Sanskrit for a secret.

You'll want to know: प्रकटयति.
प्रकटयति — prakaṭayati — "reveals".
यदि प्रमाणं भविष्यति, तर्हि सः प्रकटयिष्यति। yadi pramāṇaṃ bhaviṣyati, tarhi saḥ prakaṭayiṣyati. If there is proof, then he will reveal it.
Past (he …): सः रहस्यं प्रकटितवान्, saḥ rahasyaṃ prakaṭitavān.
Future: प्रकटयिष्यति, prakaṭayiṣyati.

The word, taken apart.
प्रकटयति (prakaṭayati) is built on prakaṭa, "evident, manifest"; MW gives prakaṭayati as a verb made from it, to manifest or disclose (MW).
