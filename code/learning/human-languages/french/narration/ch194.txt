Lesson 1 of 5.
la vallée — a valley.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a saucepan, then the French for a frying pan.

You'll want to know: la vallée.
la vallée — "a valley".

The word, taken apart.
vallée — built in Old French from val, "a valley", from Latin vallis, with -ée. (EtymDB 2.1).
