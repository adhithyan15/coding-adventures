## You'll want to know: मेंदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C316-LAGNA316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मेंदी** — *mendī* — "henna".

> लग्नाआधी नवरीच्या हातांवर मेंदी काढतात.
>
> *lagnāādhī navrīcyā hātāṁvar mendī kāḍhtāt.*
>
> Before the wedding, henna is put on the bride's hands.

Feminine.

मेंदी काढणे, to put on henna in patterns.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेंदी** (*mendī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *mẽdhī*, 'the henna plant', under Sanskrit *mendhī* or *mendhikā*, 'Lawsonia alba' (CDIAL 10328).
