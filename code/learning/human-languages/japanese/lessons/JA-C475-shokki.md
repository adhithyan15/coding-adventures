## You'll want to know: しょっき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C475-TABLE475-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しょっき** — *shokki* — "tableware; the dishes".

> **しょくじの あとで、しょっきを あらいました。** — *shokuji no ato de, shokki o araimashita* — "After the meal I washed the dishes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょっき** (*shokki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sho*, "eat" (Middle Chinese *zyik*) and *ki*, "utensil" (*khijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
