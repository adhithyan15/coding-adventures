## You'll want to know: la mention
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C379-DIPLOME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la mention** — "the distinction, the honours (on an exam); the mention".

> Elle a eu son diplôme avec mention.
>
> *She got her diploma with honours.*

*Mention bien*, *mention très bien* — with honours, with high honours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mention* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mentiō* "a calling to mind, a mention" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), related to *mēns* "mind".
