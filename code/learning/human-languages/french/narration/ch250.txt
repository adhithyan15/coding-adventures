French, chapter 250: Foreigner, Tourist, Adult, Elderly person, Twin.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
l'étranger — a foreigner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a writer, then the French for a painter.

You'll want to know: l'étranger.
l'étranger — "a foreigner".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: l'étranger]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: l'étranger, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say l'étranger]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the French for a writer, then the French for a painter, then say l'étranger again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does l'étranger mean? ("A foreigner".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To lose weight? (maigrir.) To feed? (nourrir.) To go on holiday? (partir en vacances.) To joke? (plaisanter.) To tidy up? (ranger.) A plaster? (le pansement.) The emergency department? (les urgences.) An emergency? (l'urgence.) A medical appointment? (la consultation.) A diet? (le régime.) Silent? (silencieux / silencieuse.) Clean? (propre.) Hard? (dur / dure.) Smooth? (lisse.) Rough? (rugueux / rugueuse.) Similar? (semblable.) Fair? (juste.) False? (faux / fausse.) Ready? (prêt / prête.) Free of charge? (gratuit / gratuite.)
