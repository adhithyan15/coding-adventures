## You'll want to know: festivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C681-COMERCIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**festivo** — "holiday (of a day: día festivo); festive".

> El museo cierra los días festivos.
>
> *The museum is closed on public holidays.*

On timetables: *domingos y festivos* — Sundays and public holidays.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*festivo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *fēstīvus* "festive, merry" (Lewis & Short), from *fēstum* "a holiday".
