## You'll want to know: le coureur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C508-COURSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le coureur** — "runner; rider (in a race)".

> Le coureur a battu son propre record.
>
> *The runner beat his own record.*

A woman is *la coureuse*. Also a cyclist in a race: *un coureur cycliste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le coureur* is built on *courir* "to run", inherited from Latin *currere* "to run" (Lewis & Short).
