## You'll want to know: ураган
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C466-BURYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ураган** (*uragán*) — "a hurricane".

> Ураган сломал много деревьев.
>
> *Uragán slomál mnógo derévyev.*
>
> The hurricane broke a lot of trees.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ураган* was borrowed from French *ouragan*, which came from Spanish *huracán* (EtymDB).
