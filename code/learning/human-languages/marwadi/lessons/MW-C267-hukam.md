## You'll want to know: हुकम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C267-RAJA267-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हुकम** — *hukam* — "an order".

> बाप हुकम देवै है।
>
> *bāp hukam devai hai.*
>
> Father gives an order.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुकम** (*hukam*) is usually traced, through Persian *hukm*, to Arabic *ḥukm*, "judgement, command".
