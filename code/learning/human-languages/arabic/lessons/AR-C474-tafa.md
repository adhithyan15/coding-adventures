## You'll want to know: طفا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C474-SAQR474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طفا** — *ṭafā* — "to float".

> طفت ريشة على الماء.
>
> *ṭafat rīsha ʿalā al-māʾ.*
>
> A feather floated on the water.

Present: يطفو, *yaṭfū*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طفا** (*ṭafā*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ṭ-f-w*, "to rise to the surface of water". It is the plain verb.
