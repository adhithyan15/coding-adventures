## You'll want to know: ઝાલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C394-PORTER394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાલવું** — *jhālvũ* — "to catch hold of; to grip".

> ભીડમાં મીરાએ બાળકનો હાથ ઝાલ્યો.
>
> *bhīḍmā̃ Mīrāe bāḷakno hāth jhālyo.*
>
> In the crowd Mira caught hold of the child's hand.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હાથ ઝાલ્યો, *teṇe hāth jhālyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાલવું** (*jhālvũ*) goes back to *\*jhal*, "a sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to catch hold of" (CDIAL 5351): a quick grab.
