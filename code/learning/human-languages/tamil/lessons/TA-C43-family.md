**குடும்பம்** (*kuṭumbam*) — "family".

The collective word the family lesson never needed, because அப்பா, அம்மா and the four sibling words each named one particular person. It comes from Sanskrit *kuṭumba*, 'household' — which Sanskrit itself appears to have taken from a Dravidian source before handing it back — and the *-am* on the end is what Tamil gives such borrowings.
