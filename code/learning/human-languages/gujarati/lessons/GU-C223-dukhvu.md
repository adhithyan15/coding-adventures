# દુખવું (dukhvũ) — to ache, to hurt

## You'll want to know: દુખવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C223-FEEL223-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દુખવું** — *dukhvũ* — "to ache, to hurt".

> **શું તમારું માથું દુખે છે?** — *śũ tamārũ māthũ dukhe che?* — "Does your head ache?"

The noun for the pain is **દુખાવો**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દુખવું** (*dukhvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *duḥkhati*, "pains", built on *duḥkha*, "pain" (CDIAL 6376).
