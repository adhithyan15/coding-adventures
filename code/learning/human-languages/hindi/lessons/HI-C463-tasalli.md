## You'll want to know: तसल्ली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C463-SUKUN463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तसल्ली** — *tasallī* — "reassurance; comfort".

> डाक्टर की बात सुनकर परिवार को तसल्ली हुई।
>
> *ḍākṭar kī bāt sunkar parivār ko tasallī huī.*
>
> The family was reassured by what the doctor said.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तसल्ली** (*tasallī*) is usually traced to Arabic *tasallī*, "consolation", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
