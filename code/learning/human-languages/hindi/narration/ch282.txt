Hindi, chapter 282: Asking the Way.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
बताना (batānā) — to tell, to let someone know.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "he said that he would come tomorrow". (usne kahā ki vah kal āegā.) कहना reports words. This lesson's verb hands someone a piece of information.

You'll want to know: बताना.
बताना — batānā — "to tell, to let someone know".
मुझे रास्ता बताइए। mujhe rāstā batāie. — "Please tell me the way."
The one who is told takes को or its short form: मुझे, उसे. What is told is the thing, and in the past the verb follows it:
उसने मुझे अपना नाम बताया। (usne mujhe apnā nām batāyā.) — She told me her name.
क्या आप मुझे बता सकते हैं? (kyā āp mujhe batā sakte haiṁ?) — Can you tell me?
कहना reports what someone said: उसने कहा कि …. बताना (batānā) gives someone news or directions: उसने मुझे बताया कि … (usne mujhe batāyā ki …), "she told me that …".

The word, taken apart.
बताना (batānā) has no row in CDIAL, and its origin is not settled. Its ending is the -आना of दिखाना, "to show", and समझाना, "to explain": verbs of passing something on to someone.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: batānā, then mujhe rāstā batāie]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "She told me her name" — usne mujhe apnā nām batāyā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Can you tell me?" — kyā āp mujhe batā sakte haiṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does बताना (batānā) mean? ("To tell, to let someone know".) "Please tell me the way"? (मुझे रास्ता बताइए.) Which verb reports what someone said? (कहना.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
दूर (dūr) — far.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "near the house". (घर के पास.) This lesson's word is its opposite.

You'll want to know: दूर.
दूर — dūr — "far, far away".
स्टेशन यहाँ से कितनी दूर है? sṭeśan yahā̃ se kitnī dūr hai? — "How far is the station from here?"
Hindi asks कितनी दूर (dūr), with the feminine कितनी, whatever the place. दूर (dūr) itself never changes: गाँव दूर (dūr) है, नदी दूर (dūr) है. The place you measure from takes से, as in यहाँ से.
बहुत दूर नहीं है। (bahut dūr nahīṁ hai.) — It's not very far.
मेरा घर स्टेशन से दूर है। (merā ghar sṭeśan se dūr hai.) — My house is far from the station.

The word, taken apart.
दूर (dūr) comes from Sanskrit dūra, "distant" (CDIAL 6495), and has kept its shape almost unchanged.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dūr, then kitnī dūr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "How far is the station from here?" — sṭeśan yahā̃ se kitnī dūr hai?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It's not very far" — bahut dūr nahīṁ hai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does दूर (dūr) mean? ("Far".) "How far is it?" (कितनी दूर (dūr) है?) "From here"? (यहाँ से.)


Lesson 3 of 4.
स्टेशन कैसे जाते हैं? (sṭeśan kaise jāte haiṁ?) — asking the way, and following it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "Please tell me the way" and "How far is it?" (mujhe rāstā batāie, kitnī dūr hai?)

You'll want to know: asking the way.
माफ़ कीजिए, स्टेशन कैसे जाते हैं? māf kījiye, sṭeśan kaise jāte haiṁ? — "Excuse me, how do I get to the station?"
जाते हैं with no one named is "people go": it asks for the way, not for anyone's plans. Or ask where it is: स्टेशन कहाँ है? (sṭeśan kahā̃ hai?)
The answer often comes in minutes: पाँच मिनट का रास्ता है (pā̃c minaṭ kā rāstā hai), "it's five minutes away".

You'll want to know: following the way.
सीधे जाइए, फिर बाएँ मुड़िए। sīdhe jāie, phir bāeṁ muṛiye. — "Go straight on, then turn left."
मुड़ना (muṛnā) is "to turn", and मुड़िए asks it politely, as बोलिए does. सीधे, बाएँ, दाएँ (sīdhe, bāeṁ, dāeṁ) are the forms of सीधा, बायाँ and दायाँ that say which way.
चौराहे से दाएँ मुड़िए। (caurāhe se dāeṁ muṛiye.) — Turn right at the crossroads.
पुल के बाद दूसरी गली में बाएँ मुड़िए। (pul ke bād dūsrī galī meṁ bāeṁ muṛiye.) — After the bridge, take the second side street on the left. गली is feminine, so दूसरी.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Excuse me, where is the post office?" — māf kījiye, ḍākghar kahā̃ hai?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Ask how to get to the station, then give the answer: go straight on, then turn right at the crossroads.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How do you ask the way to the station? (स्टेशन कैसे जाते हैं? (sṭeśan kaise jāte haiṁ?)) Then say "go straight on" and "turn left". (सीधे जाइए, बाएँ मुड़िए.)


Lesson 4 of 4.
Review — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask "How do I get to the station?" (sṭeśan kaise jāte haiṁ?) Then "How far is it?" (kitnī dūr hai?)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Excuse me, how do I get to the market?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Go straight on, then turn right at the crossroads.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Can you tell me the way to the hospital?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Yesterday a man told me the way.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Please tell me the way"? (मुझे रास्ता बताइए.) "How far?" (कितनी दूर (dūr)?) "Turn right"? (दाएँ मुड़िए.) "A man told me the way"? (एक आदमी ने मुझे रास्ता बताया: ने, and बताया for रास्ता.)
