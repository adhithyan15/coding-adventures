Bengali, chapter 441: A Sudden Fright.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আতঙ্ক (ātôngkô) — terror; panic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be surprised, then the Bengali for to be startled.

You'll want to know: আতঙ্ক.
আতঙ্ক — ātôngkô — "terror; panic".
আগুন দেখে গ্রামে আতঙ্ক ছড়াল। āgun dekhe grāme ātôngkô chhôṛālô. Seeing the fire, terror spread through the village.
আতঙ্কে, in terror: আতঙ্কে কাঁপা, to shake with terror.

The word, taken apart.
আতঙ্ক (ātôngkô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit ātaṅka, which Monier-Williams gives for sickness and pain and also for fear; Bengali keeps the fear.

Lesson 2 of 5.
আঁতকে ওঠা (ā̃tke oṭhā) — to recoil in fright; to gasp with alarm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be startled, then the Bengali for terror.

You'll want to know: আঁতকে ওঠা.
আঁতকে ওঠা — ā̃tke oṭhā — "to recoil in fright; to gasp with alarm".
সাপটা দেখে মীরা আঁতকে উঠল। sāpṭā dekhe mīrā ā̃tke uṭhlô. Mira recoiled in fright at the sight of the snake.
Past (he/she …): তিনি খবর শুনে আঁতকে উঠলেন, tini khôbôr shune ā̃tke uṭhlen.

The word, taken apart.
আঁতকে ওঠা (ā̃tke oṭhā) is ā̃tke, the -এ form of a verb of sudden fright whose origin is not settled, with oṭhā, "to rise" (CDIAL 1793).

Lesson 3 of 5.
শিউরে ওঠা (shiure oṭhā) — to shudder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for terror, then the Bengali for to recoil in fright.

You'll want to know: শিউরে ওঠা.
শিউরে ওঠা — shiure oṭhā — "to shudder".
গল্পটা শুনে আমি শিউরে উঠলাম। gôlpôṭā shune āmi shiure uṭhlām. I shuddered when I heard the story.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ছবিটা দেখে শিউরে উঠলেন, tini chhôbiṭā dekhe shiure uṭhlen.

The word, taken apart.
শিউরে ওঠা (shiure oṭhā) is shiure, the -এ form of a verb that is probably a softened form of the Bengali siharā, of hair standing on end, which CDIAL files with siharāna, "to startle", under Sanskrit śikhara, "a peak", a word the lexicographers also use for bristling hair (CDIAL 12435), with oṭhā, "to rise" (CDIAL 1793).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ঘাবড়ানো (ghābṛāno) — to get flustered; to lose one's nerve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to recoil in fright, then the Bengali for to shudder.

You'll want to know: ঘাবড়ানো.
ঘাবড়ানো — ghābṛāno — "to get flustered; to lose one's nerve".
প্রশ্নটা শুনে অরুণ ঘাবড়ে গেল। prôshnôṭā shune ôrun ghābṛe gelô. Arun got flustered when he heard the question.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ঘাবড়ালেন, tini ghābṛālen.
Usually in its -এ form with যাওয়া: ঘাবড়ে যাওয়া.

The word, taken apart.
ঘাবড়ানো (ghābṛāno) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as being confused and flustered, under a reconstructed ghabbhara, "confused" (CDIAL 4427), the entry where CDIAL also files the Hindi word for being startled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ধড়ফড় করা (dhôṛphôṛ kôrā) — to pound; to flutter (of the heart).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to shudder, then the Bengali for to get flustered.

You'll want to know: ধড়ফড় করা.
ধড়ফড় করা — dhôṛphôṛ kôrā — "to pound; to flutter (of the heart)".
ভয়ে আমার বুক ধড়ফড় করছিল। bhôye āmār buk dhôṛphôṛ kôrchhilô. My heart was pounding with fear.
Past (he/she …): সেই রোগীর বুক ধড়ফড় করল, sei rogīr buk dhôṛphôṛ kôrlô.
The heart is বুক (taught), the chest: বুক ধড়ফড় করা (dhôṛphôṛ kôrā).

The word, taken apart.
ধড়ফড় করা (dhôṛphôṛ kôrā) is dhôṛphôṛ, an imitative word for a quick, fluttering beat, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
