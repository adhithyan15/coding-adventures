## You'll want to know: છાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C444-BUD444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**છાલ** — *chāl* — "bark; peel".

> આ ઝાડની છાલ ઘણી કઠણ છે.
>
> *ā jhāḍnī chāl ghaṇī kaṭhaṇ che.*
>
> This tree's bark is very hard.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છાલ** (*chāl*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *challi*, "bark"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "bark, peel" (CDIAL 5005). છોલવું, to peel, is its neighbour.
