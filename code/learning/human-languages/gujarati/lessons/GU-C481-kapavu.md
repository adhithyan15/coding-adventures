# કપાવું (kapāvũ) — to get cut

## You'll want to know: કપાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C481-KAPAVU481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કપાવું** — *kapāvũ* — "to get cut".

> છરીથી મારી આંગળી કપાઈ ગઈ.
>
> *charīthī mārī ā̃gaḷī kapāī gaī.*
>
> I cut my finger on the knife.

Past (it …): તે આંગળી કપાઈ ગઈ, *te ā̃gaḷī kapāī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કપાવું** (*kapāvũ*) is the intransitive partner of **કાપવું** (*kāpvũ*), "to cut", which goes back to Sanskrit *kalpayati*, "trims, cuts" (CDIAL 2944).
