## You'll want to know: あそぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-01]; assesses=[] -->

**あそぶ** — *asobu* — "to play; to have fun".

> **きのう こうえんで ともだちと あそびました。** — *kinō kōen de tomodachi to asobimashita* — "Yesterday I played with friends in the park."

Polite **あそびます**, *asobimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あそぶ** (*asobu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
