# Review — ask, listen, check, thank

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-DIREITA-ESQUERDA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "on the left", then "on the right". (*À esquerda, à direita.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-DESCULPE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-COMO-SE-VAI-02, PT-GRAMMAR-VIRE-A-DIREITA-02, PT-GRAMMAR-DEPOIS-ORDEM-01, PT-ETYMON-DEPOIS-03] -->

Asking the way has four steps, and you now have Portuguese for every one.

[PAUSE 2s] **Ask**: *Desculpe, como se vai para a estação?* **Listen**: *Siga
em frente e depois vire à esquerda no cruzamento.* **Check**, by saying the
answer back as a question: *Em frente, depois à esquerda?* **Thank**:
*Obrigado!*, or *Obrigada!*

The check is the step travellers skip, and it is the one that saves the walk.
A stranger who hears their own directions said back will correct the one
piece you got wrong. And every *depois* in the answer is Latin *post*, "after":
one step, then the next.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-DESCULPE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-VIRE-A-DIREITA-02, PT-GRAMMAR-ACHO-QUE-01, PT-LEX-DEVIA-01, PT-GRAMMAR-EXPLICACAO-01, PT-ETYMON-EXPLICAR-02, PT-LEX-PORQUE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What are the four steps? (**Ask**, **listen**, **check**,
**thank**.) Which one do travellers skip? (**The check.**) If a friend is lost,
advise them to ask, and explain why. (*Acho que **devias** perguntar, porque a
estação é longe.*) And what does *explicar* do, word for word? (It
**unfolds**.)
