# حادثہ (hādsā) — an accident

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01, UR-LEX-C79-PLACE79-04] -->

## You'll want to know: حادثہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C271-HADSA-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C79-PLACE79-04, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-02] -->

**حادثہ** — *hādsā* — "an accident". It is masculine.

> **ایک حادثہ ہوا۔**
>
> *ek hādsā huā.* — "There was an accident."

Word for word, "an accident happened": **ہوا** (*huā*), masculine.

> **سڑک پر حادثہ ہوا۔**
>
> *saṛak par hādsā huā.* — "There was an accident on the road."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حادثہ** (*hādsā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥāditha*, "an event": something that happens. Its fourth letter is new to your hand; the next lesson takes it out.
