## You'll want to know: उपवासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C328-VRATA328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपवासः** — *upavāsaḥ* — "a fast".

> सा सर्वं दिनम् उपवासं करोति।
>
> *sā sarvaṃ dinam upavāsaṃ karoti.*
>
> She fasts the whole day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपवासः** (*upavāsaḥ*) is built from *upa* and the root √vas, "to dwell, stay"; MW gives *upavāsa* as a fast, fasting as a religious act (MW).
