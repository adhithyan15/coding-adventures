You'll want to know: പ്രേരണ.
പ്രേരണ — prēraṇa — "a motivation, an urging".

The word, taken apart.
പ്രേരണ (prēraṇa) from Sanskrit prēraṇa, "driving on, urging", taken over whole as a tatsama (CDIAL 9000).
