## You'll want to know: अदालत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C208-OFFICES208-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अदालत** — *adālat* — "a court of law".

> अदालत तहसील कनै है।
>
> *adālat tahsīl kanai hai.*
>
> The court is near the tehsil office.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अदालत** (*adālat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿadāla*, "justice".
