Arabic, chapter 518: Careers and ambitions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مهنة (mihna) — a profession, a trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to get hungry, then the Arabic for to be full.

You'll want to know: مهنة.
مهنة — mihna — "a profession, a trade".
مهنة (mihna) أبي التدريس. mihnat abī at-tadrīs. My father's profession is teaching.
Plural: مهن, mihan.

The word, taken apart.
مهنة (mihna) is built on the native Arabic root m-h-n, "to serve, to work" (Lane). Mihna is the work one serves at.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سكرتير (sikritīr) — a secretary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: سكرتير.
سكرتير — sikritīr — "a secretary".
تعمل أختي سكرتيرة في شركة كبيرة. taʿmalu ukhtī sikritīra fī sharika kabīra. My sister works as a secretary in a big company.
The feminine is سكرتيرة.

The word, taken apart.
سكرتير (sikritīr) is a modern loan, usually traced to Italian segretario or French secrétaire, words that go back to Latin secretum, "a secret".

Lesson 3 of 5.
بيطري (bayṭarī) — a vet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a profession, then the Arabic for a secretary.

You'll want to know: بيطري.
بيطري — bayṭarī — "a vet".
عالج البيطري الحصان المريض. ʿālaja al-bayṭarī al-ḥiṣān al-marīḍ. The vet treated the sick horse.
The full phrase is طبيب بيطري (bayṭarī).

The word, taken apart.
بيطري (bayṭarī) is built on bayṭār, an old word for a horse doctor, which was borrowed from Greek hippiatros, "horse doctor" (EtymDB). The ending -ī makes it "of the bayṭār".

Lesson 5 of 5.
طمح (ṭamaḥa) — to aspire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a vet, then the Arabic for an adviser.

You'll want to know: طمح.
طمح — ṭamaḥa — "to aspire".
يطمح ابني إلى أن يصبح طيارا. yaṭmaḥu ibnī ilā an yuṣbiḥa ṭayyāran. My son aspires to become a pilot.
Present: يطمح, yaṭmaḥ.
It takes إلى. Ṭumūḥ, ambition, which you know, is its noun.

The word, taken apart.
طمح (ṭamaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-m-ḥ, "to raise the eyes towards" (Lane). Lane gives ṭamaḥa of the sight, raised towards a thing.
