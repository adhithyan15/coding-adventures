Marathi, chapter 148: Heavy, Soft, Hard, Lukewarm, Dry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जड (jaḍ) — heavy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for narrow, then the Marathi for thin.

You'll want to know: जड.
जड — jaḍ — "heavy".

The word, taken apart.
जड (jaḍ) is probably the word CDIAL lists as Marathi jaḍ, "cold, stupid", under Sanskrit jaḍa, "stupid, dumb; cold", a word it calls certainly of non-Aryan origin (CDIAL 5090). CDIAL gives the sense "heavy" only for Konkani jaḍa.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मऊ (maū) — soft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for thin, then the Marathi for heavy.

You'll want to know: मऊ.
मऊ — maū — "soft".

The word, taken apart.
मऊ (maū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mṛdu, "soft" (CDIAL 10292).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कोमट (komaṭ) — lukewarm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for soft, then the Marathi for hard.

You'll want to know: कोमट.
कोमट — komaṭ — "lukewarm".

The word, taken apart.
कोमट (komaṭ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from koṣma, "warm", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3552). CDIAL compares Sanskrit koṣṇa, "warm".
