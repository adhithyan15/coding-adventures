## Guided Practice: the distant band — the whole family, talked about
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C23-SHE-01, BN-LEX-C23-TINI-01, BN-LEX-C23-O-PRON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C23-TAR-01, BN-LEX-C21-EI-01, BN-LEX-C21-OI-01, BN-LEX-C21-ETA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C21-DEM-ORDER-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C22-ANIMACY-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C22-PLURAL-RA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-CLASSIFIER-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TUMIAPNI-01, BN-CONCEPT-C03-AMI-01, BN-LEX-C11-BHAI-01, BN-LEX-C11-BON-01, BN-LEX-C11-BONDHU-01, BN-LEX-C11-PORIBAR-01, BN-LEX-C15-JAMA-01, BN-LEX-C12-MUKH-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C17-O-CLITIC-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Until this chapter every sentence in this book was about the speaker
or the listener. The family of chapter twenty-one has been sitting in the book
for a hundred lessons and could never once be the subject of a sentence. Take
them cold.

- [YOU SAY: the six persons in order — *āmi*, *tumi*, *āpni*, *she*, *tini*, *o*]
- [YOU SAY: the three possessives — *āmār*, *tomār*, *tār*]
- [YOU SAY: a brother, pointed at and then talked about — *ei bhāiṭi* … *tini bhālo*]
- [YOU SAY: a friend, plainly — *oi bôndhuṭā* … *she bhālo*]
- [YOU SAY: whose face — *tār mukhṭā*]
- [YOU SAY: his family, and his brothers — *tār pôribārṭā*, *tār bhāirā*]
- [YOU SAY: whose shirt, with everything in the queue — *tār lāl jāmāṭā*]
- [YOU SAY: the three jobs of one syllable, once more — *bhāi o bôn*; *oi jāmā*; *o bhālo*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C23-SHE-01, BN-LEX-C23-TINI-01, BN-LEX-C23-O-PRON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C23-TAR-01, BN-CONCEPT-C03-BHALO-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Does Bengali mark *he* against *she*? (**No.**) How many rungs of
respect does the third person have? (**Two** — **সে** and **তিনি**.) What sorts
**এ**, **ও** and **সে** from each other? (**How far away the person is**.) And
what ending makes a possessive? (**-র**.)
