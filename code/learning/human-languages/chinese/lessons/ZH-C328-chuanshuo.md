## You'll want to know: 传说
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C328-LEGEND328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**传说** — *chuánshuō* — "legend; folk tale".

> *zhège dìfang yǒu yí ge gǔlǎo de chuánshuō* — This place has an old legend.

The new character, in pieces: 传 — six strokes: the person piece 亻 on the left; on the right two bars, a stroke that runs down, turns right and sweeps down to the left, and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**传说** (*chuánshuō*) is built from 传 'to pass on' and 说 'to tell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjwen* and *sywet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn over, to pass along' and the second 'to tell'.
