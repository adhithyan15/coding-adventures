Lesson 1 of 5.
der Verkehr — traffic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a cause, then the German for behaviour.

You'll want to know: der Verkehr.
der Verkehr — "traffic".

The word, taken apart.
Verkehr is ver- + the verb "to turn", Old High German kēran (EtymDB 2.1): turning to and fro, dealings, then traffic.
