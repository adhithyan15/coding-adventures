# ప్రసంగం (prasaṅgaṁ) — a speech

## You'll want to know: ప్రసంగం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C342-SABHA342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రసంగం** — *prasaṅgaṁ* — "a speech".

> సభలో అతని ప్రసంగం చాలా బాగుంది.
>
> *sabhalō atani prasaṅgaṁ cālā bāgundi.*
>
> His speech at the meeting was very good.

Plural: ప్రసంగాలు, *prasaṅgālu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రసంగం** (*prasaṅgaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prasaṅga* (Monier-Williams), a word whose first sense is "attachment, connection"; in Telugu it means a public talk.
