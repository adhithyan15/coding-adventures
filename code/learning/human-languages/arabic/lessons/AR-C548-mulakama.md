## You'll want to know: ملاكمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C548-HALABA548-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ملاكمة** — *mulākama* — "boxing".

> يشاهد أبي الملاكمة على التلفاز.
>
> *yushāhidu abī al-mulākama ʿalā at-tilfāz.*
>
> My father watches boxing on television.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملاكمة** (*mulākama*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-k-m*, "to strike with the fist" (Lane). Mulākama is the verbal noun of Form III: hitting one another with the fist.
