# ശബ്ദം (śabdaṁ) — a sound; a voice

## You'll want to know: ശബ്ദം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C381-IRUTTU381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ശബ്ദം** — *śabdaṁ* — "a sound; a voice".

> രാത്രി അടുക്കളയിൽ ഒരു ശബ്ദം കേട്ടു.
>
> *rātri aṭukkaḷayil oru śabdaṁ kēṭṭu.*
>
> At night a sound was heard in the kitchen.

Also a voice: അവളുടെ ശബ്ദം, her voice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശബ്ദം** (*śabdaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śabda*, "an articulate sound, a noise" (CDIAL 12298).
