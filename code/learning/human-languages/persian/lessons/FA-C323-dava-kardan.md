## You'll want to know: دعوا کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C323-KOLAHBARDAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دعوا کردن** (*da'vâ kardan*) — "to quarrel, to have a fight".

> دو راننده در خیابان دعوا کردند.
>
> *Do rânande dar khiyâbân da'vâ kardand.*
>
> Two drivers had a fight in the street.

The other side takes *bâ*: *bâ hamsâye da'vâ kard*. Present *da'vâ mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*da'vâ* is borrowed from Arabic *da'wā*, "a claim, an assertion" (EtymDB); in Persian it became an everyday word for a quarrel. *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
