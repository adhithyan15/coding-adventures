## You'll want to know: a entorse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C486-LIGADURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a entorse** — "sprain".

> Fiz uma entorse no tornozelo a jogar à bola.
>
> *I sprained my ankle playing football.*

Feminine in Portugal: *uma entorse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entorse* is usually traced to French *entorse* "a twist", built on a verb that goes back to Latin *torquēre* "to twist" (Lewis & Short).
