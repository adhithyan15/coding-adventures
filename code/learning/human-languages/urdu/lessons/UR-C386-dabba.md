## You'll want to know: ڈبہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C386-DABBA386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈبہ** — *ḍabbā* — "a compartment (of a train); a box".

> ہمارا ڈبہ انجن کے پیچھے ہے۔
>
> *hamārā ḍabbā injan ke pīchhe hai.*
>
> Our carriage is right behind the engine.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈبہ** (*ḍabbā*) comes from the word CDIAL files as *ḍabba*, "a box", of non-Aryan, perhaps Dravidian, origin (CDIAL 5528); Urdu also calls a railway carriage a box.
