Lesson 1 of 5.
مقامی (maqāmī) — local.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for rough, then the Urdu for round.

You'll want to know: مقامی.
مقامی — maqāmī — "local".

The word, taken apart.
مقامی (maqāmī) is maqām, "place", with the ending -ī, "of". maqām is Arabic, "a place of standing", and reached Urdu through Persian (Lane, root q-w-m).

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for local, then the Urdu for English.

You'll want to know: پاکستانی.
پاکستانی — pākistānī — "Pakistani".

The word, taken apart.
پاکستانی (pākistānī) is Pākistān with the ending -ī, "of". The name was coined in 1933, and is read as Persian pāk, "pure", and -stān, "land".

ہندوستانی (hindustānī) is Hindūstān, "the land of Hind", with the ending -ī, "of". The name is Persian: Hindū, "of Hind, India", and -stān, "land".
