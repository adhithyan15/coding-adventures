## You'll want to know: صدفة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C343-MUGHAMARA343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صدفة** — *ṣudfa* — "a coincidence, chance".

> التقيت بصديقي القديم صدفة في السوق.
>
> *iltaqaytu bi-ṣadīqī al-qadīm ṣudfatan fī as-sūq.*
>
> I met my old friend by chance in the market.

صدفة or بالصدفة: by chance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صدفة** (*ṣudfa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-d-f* (Lane), where Form III *ṣādafa* is "to light upon someone without meaning to".
