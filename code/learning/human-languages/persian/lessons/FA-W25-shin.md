# ش (shin) — the letter that opens شما

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-KAF-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-GAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ک and گ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **شما** (*shomâ*), "you (respectful)".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-SHIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ش** — *shin*, the sound *sh*.

It opens **شما** (*shomâ*), the respectful "you". It is **س**, which you already
write, with **three dots above**.

## Writing: ش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-SHIN-01] -->

- **1.** start here: form the three teeth from right to left
- **2.** flow into the final bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift, then place the lower-left dot
- **4.** lift again and place the lower-right dot
- **5.** lift again and place the centered upper dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 3.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ش demonstration at 01:29–01:35 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-KAF-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-GAF-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-SHIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **شما** and point at ش inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ش three times, saying *sh* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: گ, then ش, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **شما** aloud once more]

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ش? (**shin**, *sh*.) Which word did it
come from? (**شما**, *shomâ*, "you (respectful)".)
