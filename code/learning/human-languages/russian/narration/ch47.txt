Russian, chapter 47: Sugar, Honey, Rice, Mushrooms and a Glass.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сахар (sákhar) — sugar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for porridge, then the Russian for a pie.

You'll want to know: сахар.
сахар (sákhar) — "sugar".

What you've built.
One more word for sugar, honey, rice, mushrooms and a glass.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sákhar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sákhar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sákhar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for porridge, then the Russian for a pie, then say sákhar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does сахар (sákhar) mean? ("Sugar".) Say it once more.
