Gujarati, chapter 100: Something, Anything, Oneself, One's own, Both.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
કંઈક (kaīk) — something.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for every, then the Gujarati for someone.

You'll want to know: કંઈક.
કંઈક (kaīk) — "something".

Lesson 6 of 7.

[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for one's own and both.

[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: midnight, dawn, the day after tomorrow, a year, a moment]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a season, summer, winter, the monsoon, spring]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: autumn, early, every day, a date, right now]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: still, immediately, January, February, March]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

(પાનખર.) Early? (વહેલું.) Every day? (રોજ.) A date? (તારીખ.) Right now? (હમણાં.) Still? (હજી.) Immediately? (તરત.) January? (જાન્યુઆરી.) February? (ફેબ્રુઆરી.) March? (માર્ચ.)
