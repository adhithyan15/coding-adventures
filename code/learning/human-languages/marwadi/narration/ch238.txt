Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 238: Sowing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बोवणो (bovṇo) — to sow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an egg, then the Marwadi for a frog.

You'll want to know: बोवणो.
बोवणो — bovṇo — "to sow".
हूं काल खेत मांय बाजरो बोसूं। hū̃ kāl khet mā̃y bājro bosū̃. Tomorrow I'll sow millet in the field.
Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो बोयो, mhaĩ bājro boyo.

The word, taken apart.
बोवणो (bovṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vapati, "scatters, sows"; CDIAL lists Hindi boyā, "sows", and names a Gujarati verb beside it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11282).

Lesson 3 of 5.
रोपणो (ropṇo) — to plant (a seedling or a tree).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to sow, then the Marwadi for to dig.

You'll want to know: रोपणो.
रोपणो — ropṇo — "to plant (a seedling or a tree)".
हूं काल खेत कनै रूंख रोपसूं। hū̃ kāl khet kanai rū̃kh ropsū̃. Tomorrow I'll plant a tree by the field.
Past (I …): म्हैं रूंख रोप्यो, mhaĩ rū̃kh ropyo.

The word, taken apart.
रोपणो (ropṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed rupati, "makes a hole, plants", beside Sanskrit ropayati, "plants"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb ropaï, "plants" (CDIAL 10783).

Lesson 4 of 5.
हाळी (hāḷī) — a ploughman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to dig, then the Marwadi for to plant (a seedling or a tree).

You'll want to know: हाळी.
हाळी — hāḷī — "a ploughman".
हाळी तड़कै खेत जावै है। hāḷī taṛkai khet jāvai hai. The ploughman goes to the field at daybreak.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
हाळी (hāḷī) is inherited from Sanskrit hālika, "a ploughman", from hala, "a plough"; CDIAL lists Hindi hālī and Gujarati hāḷi, "a ploughman". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 14067).

Lesson 5 of 5.
बीज (bīj) — a seed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to plant (a seedling or a tree), then the Marwadi for a ploughman.

You'll want to know: बीज.
बीज — bīj — "a seed".
म्हारो बीज आछो है। mhāro bīj āchho hai. My seed is good.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बीज (bīj) is a tatsama, taken over from Sanskrit bīja, "a seed" (Monier-Williams); the inherited forms CDIAL lists, such as Hindi biyā, have lost the j (CDIAL 9250).
