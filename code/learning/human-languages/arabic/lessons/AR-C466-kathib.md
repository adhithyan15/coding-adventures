## You'll want to know: كثيب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C466-GHUBAR466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**كثيب** — *kathīb* — "a sand dune".

> صعدنا كثيبا كبيرا في الصحراء.
>
> *ṣaʿidnā kathīban kabīran fī aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ.*
>
> We climbed a big sand dune in the desert.

Plural: كثبان, *kuthbān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كثيب** (*kathīb*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-th-b*, "to collect together" (Lane). A dune is sand gathered into a heap.
