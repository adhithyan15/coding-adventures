## You'll want to know: कंडक्टर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C405-GATI405-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कंडक्टर** — *kaṇḍakṭar* — "a conductor; a bus conductor".

> कंडक्टर ने किराया माँगा।
>
> *kaṇḍakṭar ne kirāyā mā̃gā.*
>
> The conductor asked for the fare.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कंडक्टर** (*kaṇḍakṭar*) is borrowed from English *conductor*, the person who sells tickets on a bus.
