# ફૂંકાવું (phū̃kāvũ) — to blow

## You'll want to know: ફૂંકાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C217-WIND217-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂંકાવું** — *phū̃kāvũ* — "to blow".

> **રાત્રે ઠંડો પવન ફૂંકાશે.** — *rātre ṭhanḍo pavan phū̃kāśe* — "A cold wind will blow at night."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂંકાવું** (*phū̃kāvũ*) is built on *phū̃kvũ*, "to blow", with the *-ā-* that makes a verb happen by itself, as wind does. CDIAL lists *phū̃kvũ* under *\*phūtka*, "blowing", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9102).
