## You'll want to know: دہشت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C302-AFWAH302-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دہشت** — *dahshat* — "terror; panic".

> لوگوں میں دہشت تھی۔
>
> *logoṅ meṅ dahshat thī.*
>
> People were terrified.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دہشت** (*dahshat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dahsha*, "bewilderment, alarm". It is a fear that grips a whole crowd.
