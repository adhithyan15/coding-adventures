Italian, chapter 435: Caldo e freddo in camera.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il riscaldamento — the heating.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the drain, then the Italian for to unblock.

You'll want to know: il riscaldamento.
il riscaldamento — "the heating".
Il riscaldamento è spento e fa freddo. The heating is off and it is cold.
Il riscaldamento globale — global warming.

The word, taken apart.
riscaldamento is built on scaldare 'to warm', which is inherited from Late Latin excaldāre (EtymDB), built on calidus 'hot'.

What you've built.

L'aria condizionata is air conditioning.

condizionatore is built on condizionare 'to condition', from condizione, a learned borrowing from Latin condiciō 'agreement, state' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
rinfrescare — to cool down, to freshen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the radiator, then the Italian for the air conditioner.

You'll want to know: rinfrescare.
rinfrescare — "to cool down, to freshen".
La sera la stanza si rinfresca un po'. In the evening the room cools down a bit.
Rinfrescarsi — to freshen up.

The word, taken apart.
rinfrescare is built on fresco 'cool, fresh', which was borrowed through Medieval Latin friscus from a Germanic word (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
lo scaldabagno — the water heater.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: lo scaldabagno.
lo scaldabagno — "the water heater".
Lo scaldabagno è rotto: niente acqua calda. The water heater is broken: no hot water.
In speech also il boiler. Plural unchanged.

The word, taken apart.
scaldabagno is built from scaldare 'to warm', inherited from Late Latin excaldāre, and bagno 'bath', inherited from Latin balneum (EtymDB).
