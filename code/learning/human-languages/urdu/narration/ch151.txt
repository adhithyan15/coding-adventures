Lesson 1 of 5.
تیار کرنا (tayyār karnā) — to prepare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to sign, then the Urdu for to phone.

You'll want to know: تیار کرنا.
تیار کرنا — tayyār karnā — "to prepare".

The phrase, taken apart.
تیار کرنا (tayyār karnā) joins tayyār, "ready", and karnā, "to do". tayyār reached Urdu through Persian, which uses it in the same sense; its Arabic source is not settled. karnā comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

The word, taken apart.
کھانسنا (khāṅsnā) is the verb belonging to khāṅsī, "a cough", which comes from Sanskrit kāsa, "cough" (CDIAL 3138). CDIAL notes the kh- as imitating the sound.

You'll want to know: سانس لینا.
سانس لینا — sāṅs lenā — "to breathe".

The phrase, taken apart.
سانس لینا (sāṅs lenā) is "to take breath". sāṅs comes from Sanskrit śvāsa, "breath, breathing" (CDIAL 12769), and lenā from Sanskrit labhate, "catches, takes" (CDIAL 10948).
