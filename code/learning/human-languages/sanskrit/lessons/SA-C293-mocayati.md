## You'll want to know: मोचयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C293-NYAYALAYA293-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मोचयति** — *mocayati* — "sets free".

> न्यायाधीशः बालं मोचयति।
>
> *nyāyādhīśaḥ bālaṃ mocayati.*
>
> The judge sets the boy free.

Past (he …): सः बालं मोचितवान्, *saḥ bālaṃ mocitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोचयति** (*mocayati*) comes from the root √muc, "to loose, release", in its causative form, to set free (MW).
