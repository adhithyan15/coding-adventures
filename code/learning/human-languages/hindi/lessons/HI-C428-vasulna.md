## You'll want to know: वसूलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C428-KAMANA428-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वसूलना** — *vasūlnā* — "to collect (money owed); to recover (a debt)".

> दुकानदार हर हफ़्ते अपना उधार वसूलता है।
>
> *dukāndār har hafte apnā udhār vasūltā hai.*
>
> The shopkeeper collects what he is owed every week.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना क़र्ज़ वसूला, *usne apnā qarz vasūlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वसूलना** (*vasūlnā*) is a Hindi verb built on *vasūl*, "received, recovered", which is usually traced to Arabic *wuṣūl*, "arrival, receipt", through Persian.
