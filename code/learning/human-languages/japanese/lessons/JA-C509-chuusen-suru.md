# ちゅうせんする (chūsen suru) — to hold a draw; to draw lots

## You'll want to know: ちゅうせんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C509-LOTTERY509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうせんする** — *chūsen suru* — "to hold a draw; to draw lots".

> **もし せんにん きたら、ちゅうせんします。** — *moshi sennin kitara, chūsenshimasu* — "If a thousand people come, we will hold a draw."

Polite **ちゅうせんします**, *chūsenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **みせが ちゅうせんしました。**, *mise ga chūsenshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうせんする** (*chūsen suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *chūsen* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chū*, "pluck" (Middle Chinese *trhjuw*) and *sen*, "elect" (*sjwenX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
