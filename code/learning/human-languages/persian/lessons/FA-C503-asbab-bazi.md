## You'll want to know: اسباب بازی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C503-PLAY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اسباب بازی** (*asbâb bâzi*) — "a toy".

> اتاق بچه پر از اسباب بازی است.
>
> *otâq-e bachche por az asbâb bâzi ast.*
>
> The child's room is full of toys.

*asbâb* 'things, gear' (an Arabic plural) + *bâzi* 'play'. Plural *asbâb bâzihâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*asbâb bâzi* is built in Persian on *asbâb*, 'things, gear', a loan usually traced to the Arabic plural *asbāb*, and native *bâzi*, 'play': play things.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
