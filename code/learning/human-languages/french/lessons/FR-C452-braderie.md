## You'll want to know: la braderie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C452-BRADERIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la braderie** — "the street sale, the clearance market".

> Chaque septembre, la ville organise une grande braderie.
>
> *Every September the town holds a big street sale.*

Very popular in the north of France and in Belgium.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*braderie* is built on *brader* 'to sell off cheap' (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Dutch *braden* 'to roast'.
