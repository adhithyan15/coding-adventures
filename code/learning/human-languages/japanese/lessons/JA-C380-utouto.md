## You'll want to know: うとうと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C380-EKIBEN380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うとうと** — *utōto* — "dozing off".

> **でんしゃで うとうとしました。** — *densha de utōto shimashita* — "I dozed off on the train."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うとうと** (*utōto*) is a native mimetic word that pictures someone nodding off into a light sleep: an imitative Japanese form, inherited rather than borrowed, whose older origin is not settled (JMdict).
