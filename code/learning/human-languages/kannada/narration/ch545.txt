Kannada, chapter 545: Laundry Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಜಮಖಾನ (jamakhāna) — a rug; a woven floor carpet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tube, then the Kannada for a wire.

You'll want to know: ಜಮಖಾನ.
ಜಮಖಾನ — jamakhāna — "a rug; a woven floor carpet".
ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ನೆಲದ ಮೇಲೆ ಜಮಖಾನ ಹಾಸಿದರು. habbakke nelada mēle jamakhāna hāsidaru. They spread a rug on the floor for the festival.
Thick and woven, spread for guests to sit on.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಮಖಾನ (jamakhāna) is usually traced to a Persian word that reached Kannada through Hindustani; the exact source is not settled.

You'll want to know: ಝಾಡಿಸು.
ಝಾಡಿಸು — jhāḍisu — "to shake out; to dust off".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಜಮಖಾನವನ್ನು ಹೊರಗೆ ಝಾಡಿಸಿದರು. ajji jamakhānavannu horage jhāḍisidaru. Grandmother shook out the rug outside.
Past (I …): ಝಾಡಿಸಿದೆ, jhāḍiside.
To shake or beat the dust out of something.

The word, taken apart.
ಝಾಡಿಸು (jhāḍisu) is usually traced to Hindustani jhāṛnā, "to dust, to shake off", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a quilt, then the Kannada for to shake out.

You'll want to know: ಒಗೆ.
ಒಗೆ — oge — "to wash clothes".
ಭಾನುವಾರ ಅಮ್ಮ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಒಗೆಯುತ್ತಾರೆ. bhānuvāra amma ellā baṭṭe ogeyuttāre. On Sunday mother washes all the clothes.
Past (I …): ಒಗೆದೆ, ogede.
For clothes; dishes and hands take ತೊಳೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಗೆ (oge) is native Dravidian: DEDR 933 lists Kannada oge, "to wash".
