## You'll want to know: la intención
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C615-PLAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la intención** — "the intention".

> No tengo intención de mudarme.
>
> *I have no intention of moving house.*

*Tener la intención de* + infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*intención* is a learned borrowing from Latin *intentio* 'a stretching, a purpose', built on *intendere* and *tendere* 'to stretch' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
