## You'll want to know: avaro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C472-DIFETTI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**avaro** — "mean, stingy; miser".

> Non è avaro, ma non spende per cose inutili.
>
> *He is not stingy, but he does not spend on useless things.*

Also a noun: *un avaro* — a miser.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avaro* is inherited from Latin *avārus* "greedy" (EtymDB), from *avēre* "to long for, to crave" (Lewis & Short).
