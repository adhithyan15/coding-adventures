## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-NO-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-HI-01] -->

> **の** — *no*

One stroke. KanjiVG's data holds a single path: start high and to the right of
centre, run down and left, round the bottom, up the left side and across the
top, finishing by crossing what you have just drawn.

**ひ**, from the start of this chapter, was also one stroke and never closed.
**の** closes and then keeps going.

This is the fifth sign this chapter has bought, and the last. Everything after
it is words.
