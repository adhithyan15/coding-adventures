## You'll want to know: 自愿
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C535-WILLING535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**自愿** — *zìyuàn* — "of one's own free will; voluntary".

> **我们自愿在这儿工作** *wǒmen zìyuàn zài zhèr gōngzuò* — We work here of our own free will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**自愿** (*zìyuàn*) is built from 自 'self' and 愿 'to wish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH* and *ngjwonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from' and 'by way of'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
