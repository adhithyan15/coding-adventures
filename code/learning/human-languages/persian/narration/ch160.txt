Lesson 1 of 5.
انگیزه (angize) — a motive, motivation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for while, then the Persian for especially.

You'll want to know: انگیزه.
انگیزه — angize — "a motive, motivation".

The word, taken apart.
angize is built on angiz-, the present stem of angikhtan, "to stir up", with the noun ending -e: what stirs you. It is native Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
منظور (manzur) — a meaning, a purpose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for especially, then the Persian for a motive.

You'll want to know: منظور.
منظور — manzur — "a meaning, a purpose".

The word, taken apart.
manzur is borrowed from Arabic manẓūr, "looked at, kept in view". Its Arabic root is n-ẓ-r (Lane). Persian uses it for what one has in view: a meaning or a purpose.
