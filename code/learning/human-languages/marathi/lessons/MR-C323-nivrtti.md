## You'll want to know: निवृत्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C323-NIROP323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निवृत्ती** — *nivṛttī* — "retirement".

> निवृत्तीनंतर बाबा रोज बागेत काम करतात.
>
> *nivṛttīnantar bābā roj bāget kām kartāt.*
>
> Since retiring Dad works in the garden every day.

Feminine.

निवृत्तीनंतर, after retirement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवृत्ती** (*nivṛttī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nivṛtti*, 'a turning back, ceasing from work' (Monier-Williams).
