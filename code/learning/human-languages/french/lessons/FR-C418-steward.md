## You'll want to know: le steward
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C418-BORD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le steward** — "the steward (on a plane)".

> Le steward aide une dame avec son sac.
>
> *The steward helps a lady with her bag.*

Pronounced more or less as in English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*steward* was borrowed from English *steward* (EtymDB).
