## You'll want to know: pondō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C465-MODIUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pondō** — "by weight, in weight; a pound".

> Pistor duo pondō farīnae emit.
>
> *The baker buys two pounds of flour.*

An old ablative of *pondus* 'weight', used as an adverb and as an undeclined noun: *decem pondō*, ten pounds. English *pound* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pondō* is an old ablative of *pondus* 'weight' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from the root of *pendō* 'to weigh'. English *pound* comes from it, borrowed early into Germanic.
