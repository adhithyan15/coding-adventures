## You'll want to know: māo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C67-ANIMALS67-03]; assesses=[] -->

*māo* — "cat".

> *wǒ jiā yǒu māo* — We've got a cat at home.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*māo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *mjew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the animal that catches mice', though māo is not the sound *mjew* would regularly give.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
