## You'll want to know: मुरजः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C521-VADAKA521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुरजः** — *murajaḥ* — "a tambourine".

> उत्सवे जनाः मुरजं वादयन्ति।
>
> *utsave janāḥ murajaṃ vādayanti.*
>
> At the festival people play the tambourine.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुरजः** (*murajaḥ*) is the word MW records for a kind of drum, a tambourine; MW suggests *mura* and *ja*, "born", with a query, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
