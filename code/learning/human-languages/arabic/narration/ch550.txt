Arabic, chapter 550: Keeping the house.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ثريا (thurayyā) — a chandelier.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bolt, then the Arabic for a drain.

You'll want to know: ثريا.
ثريا — thurayyā — "a chandelier".
في غرفة الجلوس ثريا كبيرة. fī ghurfat al-julūs thurayyā kabīra. There is a big chandelier in the sitting room.
الثريا is also the Pleiades, a cluster of stars.

The word, taken apart.
ثريا (thurayyā) is built on the native Arabic root th-r-w, "to be many, to abound" (Lane). Lane gives al-thurayyā as the Pleiades, a cluster of many stars, and a cluster of lamps.

You'll want to know: مزهرية.
مزهرية — mazhariyya — "a vase".
وضعت أمي الورد في المزهرية. waḍaʿat ummī al-ward fī al-mazhariyya. My mother put the roses in the vase.

The word, taken apart.
مزهرية (mazhariyya) is built on the native Arabic root z-h-r, "to shine; a flower" (Lane). Zahra, a flower, which you know, is from the same root: a mazhariyya is a thing for flowers.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

نفض — nafaḍa — "to shake out (dust)".
نفضت أمي السجادة في الشرفة. nafaḍat ummī as-sajjāda fī ash-shurfa. My mother shook out the carpet on the balcony.
Present: ينفض, yanfuḍ.

The word, taken apart.
نفض (nafaḍa) is built on the native Arabic root n-f-ḍ, "to shake a thing to clear it" (Lane). Lane gives nafaḍa, aor. yanfuḍu, as to shake a cloth so that what is on it falls off.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لمع (lammaʿa) — to polish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: لمع.
لمع — lammaʿa — "to polish".
لمع أبي حذاءه قبل الحفلة. lammaʿa abī ḥidhāʾahu qabla al-ḥafla. My father polished his shoes before the party.
Present: يلمع, yulammiʿ.

The word, taken apart.
لمع (lammaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root l-m-ʿ, "to shine, to gleam" (Lane). Lāmiʿ, shiny, which you know, is from the same root; Form II is to make a thing shine.

Lesson 5 of 5.
صان (ṣāna) — to maintain, to look after (a thing).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to shake out, then the Arabic for to polish.

You'll want to know: صان.
صان — ṣāna — "to maintain, to look after (a thing)".
يصون جدي ساعته القديمة. yaṣūnu jaddī sāʿatahu al-qadīma. My grandfather takes care of his old watch.
Present: يصون, yaṣūn.
The noun صيانة is maintenance.

The word, taken apart.
صان (ṣāna) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-w-n, "to preserve, to guard" (Lane). Lane gives ṣāna, aor. yaṣūnu, as to keep a thing safe.
