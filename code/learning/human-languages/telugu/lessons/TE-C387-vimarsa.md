# విమర్శ (vimarśa) — criticism; a critique

## You'll want to know: విమర్శ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C387-VIMARSA387-01]; assesses=[] -->

**విమర్శ** — *vimarśa* — "criticism; a critique".

> మంచి విమర్శ మనకు సహాయం చేస్తుంది.
>
> *mañci vimarśa manaku sahāyaṁ cēstundi.*
>
> Good criticism helps us.

Plural: విమర్శలు, *vimarśalu*.

Not only blame: a review of a book or a film is also a విమర్శ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**విమర్శ** (*vimarśa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vimarśa*, "consideration, deliberation, critical test" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
