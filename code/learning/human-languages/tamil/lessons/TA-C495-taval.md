## You'll want to know: தவழ்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C495-KUZHANTAI495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**தவழ்** — *tavaḻ* — "to crawl".

> எட்டு மாதத்தில் குழந்தை தவழ்ந்தது.
>
> *eṭṭu mātattil kuḻantai tavaḻntatu.*
>
> At eight months the baby crawled.

Past (I …): தவழ்ந்தேன், *tavaḻntēṉ*.

Past with -ந்த-: தவழ்ந்தது.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தவழ்** (*tavaḻ*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3109), of infants, lizards and the creep of waves.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
