## You'll want to know: こくさい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C385-KAIGAI385-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こくさい** — *kokusai* — "international".

> **この くうこうは こくさい くうこうです。** — *kono kūkō wa kokusai kūkō desu* — "This airport is an international airport."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こくさい** (*kokusai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *koku*, "country" (Middle Chinese *kwok*) and *sai*, "edge" (*tsjejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
