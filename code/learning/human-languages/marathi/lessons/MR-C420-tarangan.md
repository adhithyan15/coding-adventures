## You'll want to know: तारांगण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C420-DUTAVAS420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तारांगण** — *tārāṅgaṇ* — "a planetarium".

> शाळेची सहल तारांगणात गेली.
>
> *śāḷecī sahal tārāṅgaṇāt gelī.*
>
> The school trip went to the planetarium.

Neuter.

तारा, a star, and अंगण, a yard: a courtyard of stars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तारांगण** (*tārāṅgaṇ*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *tārā*, "a star", and *aṅgaṇa*, "a courtyard" (Monier-Williams).
