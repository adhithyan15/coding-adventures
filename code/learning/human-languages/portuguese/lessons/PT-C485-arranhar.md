## You'll want to know: arranhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C485-ARRANHAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**arranhar** — "to scratch (and leave a mark)".

> O gato arranhou-me a mão.
>
> *The cat scratched my hand.*

*Coçar* is for an itch; *arranhar* leaves a mark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arranhar* is an old Portuguese verb (EtymDB), but its origin is not settled.
