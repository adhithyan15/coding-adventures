Urdu, chapter 149: To cross, To wander, To go for a stroll, To jump, To slip.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پار کرنا (pār karnā) — to cross.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to lend, then the Urdu for to save.

You'll want to know: پار کرنا.
پار کرنا — pār karnā — "to cross".

The phrase, taken apart.
پار کرنا (pār karnā) is "to do the far side". pār comes from Sanskrit pāra, "the further bank" (CDIAL 8100), and karnā from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

The word, taken apart.
گھومنا (ghūmnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ghummati, "revolves", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4485).

Lesson 3 of 5.
سیر کرنا (sair karnā) — to go for a stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to cross, then the Urdu for to wander.

You'll want to know: سیر کرنا.
سیر کرنا — sair karnā — "to go for a stroll".

The phrase, taken apart.
سیر کرنا (sair karnā) joins sair, "an outing", and karnā, "to do". sair is Arabic sayr, "a going, a journey", which reached Urdu through Persian (Lane, root s-y-r); karnā comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 4 of 5.
کودنا (kūdnā) — to jump.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to wander, then the Urdu for to go for a stroll.

You'll want to know: کودنا.
کودنا — kūdnā — "to jump".

The word, taken apart.
کودنا (kūdnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kūrdati, "leaps, jumps" (CDIAL 3412). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit verb as probably borrowed from Dravidian.
