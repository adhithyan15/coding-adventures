## You'll want to know: けついする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C517-DECIDE517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**けついする** — *ketsui suru* — "to resolve (to do)".

> **もし けついしたら、さいごまで がんばって ください。** — *moshi ketsuishitara, saigo made ganbatte kudasai* — "If you make up your mind, please keep going to the end."

Polite **けついします**, *ketsuishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かれが ひっこしを けついしました。**, *kare ga hikkoshi o ketsuishimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けついする** (*ketsui suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *ketsui* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ketsu*, "decide" (Middle Chinese *kwet*) and *i*, "idea" (*'iH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
