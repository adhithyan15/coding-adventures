## You'll want to know: অভ্যাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C456-CHORITRO456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অভ্যাস** — *ôbhyās* — "a habit".

> রোজ ভোরে হাঁটা দাদুর পুরনো অভ্যাস।
>
> *roj bhore hā̃ṭā dādur purôno ôbhyās.*
>
> Walking at dawn every day is an old habit of my grandfather's.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অভ্যাস** (*ôbhyās*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *abhyāsa*, "repetition, practice, habit" (Monier-Williams): what you do again and again.
