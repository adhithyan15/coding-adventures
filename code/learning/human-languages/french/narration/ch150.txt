French, chapter 150: To move away, To come near, To chat, To plug in, To deliver.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
s'éloigner — to move away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to suffer, then the French for to dream.

You'll want to know: s'éloigner.
s'éloigner — "to move away".

The word, taken apart.
s'éloigner from Old French esloignier, built on loing, "far", the old spelling of loin (EtymDB 2.1): to put far off.
