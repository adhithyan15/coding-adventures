## You'll want to know: सेठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C209-SHOP209-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सेठ** — *seṭh* — "a merchant".

> सेठ हवेली मांय रैवै है।
>
> *seṭh havelī mā̃y raivai hai.*
>
> The merchant lives in a mansion.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेठ** (*seṭh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śreṣṭhin*, "a distinguished man; the head of a guild". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *seṭh*, "merchant, banker" (CDIAL 12726).
