## You'll want to know: अप्रमत्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C539-AVAHITA539-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अप्रमत्तः** — *apramattaḥ* — "heedful".

> यदि त्वम् अप्रमत्तः असि, तर्हि त्वं न पतसि।
>
> *yadi tvam apramattaḥ asi, tarhi tvaṃ na patasi.*
>
> If you are heedful, you will not fall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अप्रमत्तः** (*apramattaḥ*) is built on *a*, "not", and *pramatta*, "careless", from *pra* and the root √mad, "to be drunk"; MW gives *apramatta* as not careless, careful, attentive, vigilant (MW).
