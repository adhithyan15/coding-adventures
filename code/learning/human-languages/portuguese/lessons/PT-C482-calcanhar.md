## You'll want to know: o calcanhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C482-CORPO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o calcanhar** — "heel".

> Os sapatos novos fizeram-me uma ferida no calcanhar.
>
> *The new shoes gave me a sore on my heel.*

The heel of a shoe is *o salto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*calcanhar* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin forms built on Latin *calcāneum* "heel" (Lewis & Short), from *calx* "heel".
