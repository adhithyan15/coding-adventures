## You'll want to know: शिष्टाचार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C471-VINAMRA471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शिष्टाचार** — *śiṣṭācār* — "etiquette; correct manners".

> मेहमान के आने पर खड़े होना शिष्टाचार है।
>
> *mehmān ke āne par khaṛe honā śiṣṭācār hai.*
>
> Standing up when a guest arrives is good manners.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिष्टाचार** (*śiṣṭācār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *śiṣṭācāra*, "the conduct of the learned, good manners" (Monier-Williams), built on *śiṣṭa*, "well taught", and *ācāra*, "conduct".
