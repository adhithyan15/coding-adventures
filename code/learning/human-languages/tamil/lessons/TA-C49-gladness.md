## You'll want to know: சந்தோஷம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C49-COURTESY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்தோஷம்** (*santōṣam*) — "gladness".

Sanskrit *santoṣa*, 'contentment', sitting beside the native *magiḻcci* you met when somebody was pleased to see you. Both are in daily use and neither has driven the other out — the same arrangement as *nalam* and *sugam*.

*santōṣam* is what you reach for when you accept an invitation. On its own, in reply, it means "gladly".
