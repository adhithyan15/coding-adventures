## You'll want to know: مختار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C539-MUKHTAR539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مختار** — *mukhtār* — "free to decide; empowered".

> اگر آپ مختار ہیں تو آپ کو کون روکے گا؟
>
> *agar āp mukhtār haiṅ to āp ko kaun roke gā?*
>
> If the choice is yours, who will stop you?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مختار** (*mukhtār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mukhtār*, "chosen; free to choose".
