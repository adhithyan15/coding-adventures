## You'll want to know: évacuer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C301-ALERTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**évacuer** — "to evacuate, to clear".

> La police a évacué le bâtiment.
>
> *The police evacuated the building.*

Also of water: *évacuer l'eau de pluie* — to drain away rainwater.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*évacuer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēvacuāre* "to empty out" (EtymDB), built on *vacuus* "empty".
