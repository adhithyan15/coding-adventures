## You'll want to know: ਵਧਾਈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C313-WED313-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਧਾਈ** — *vadhāī* — "congratulations".

> ਮੈਂ ਲਾੜੇ ਨੂੰ ਵਧਾਈ ਦਿੱਤੀ।
>
> *maiṁ lāṛe nū̃ vadhāī dittī.*
>
> I congratulated the bridegroom.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਧਾਈ** (*vadhāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vardhāpayati*, "increases, gladdens, congratulates"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vadhāī*, "blessing", under it (CDIAL 11383).
