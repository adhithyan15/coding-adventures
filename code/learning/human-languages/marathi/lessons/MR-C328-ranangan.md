## You'll want to know: रणांगण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C328-LADHAI328-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रणांगण** — *raṇāṅgaṇ* — "a battlefield".

> रणांगणावर दोन्ही सैन्यं समोरासमोर आली.
>
> *raṇāṅgaṇāvar donhī sainyaṁ samorāsamor ālī.*
>
> On the battlefield the two armies came face to face.

Neuter.

रण, battle, + अंगण, a yard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रणांगण** (*raṇāṅgaṇ*) is built from Sanskrit *raṇa*, 'battle', and *aṅgaṇa*, 'a courtyard' (Monier-Williams): the yard of battle.
