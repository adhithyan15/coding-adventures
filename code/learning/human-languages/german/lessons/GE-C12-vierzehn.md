> **vierzehn** — "fourteen." *vier* + *zehn*.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-SOUND-V-AS-F-01, GE-LEX-VIERZEHN-01] -->

- `v-as-f` — the *V* is an **f**, exactly as in *vier* and *Vater*.
- `z-ts` — and *-TSAYN* again.

*FEER-tsayn*. Third time for that *v*: a number, a father, and now a teen.
