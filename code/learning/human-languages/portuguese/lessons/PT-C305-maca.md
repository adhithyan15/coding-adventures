## You'll want to know: a maca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C305-SOCORRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a maca** — "the stretcher".

> Levaram o homem numa maca.
>
> *They carried the man on a stretcher.*

Not to be confused with *a maçã*, the apple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maca* came from Spanish *hamaca* "a hammock" (EtymDB), a word taken from Taíno *hamaka*.
