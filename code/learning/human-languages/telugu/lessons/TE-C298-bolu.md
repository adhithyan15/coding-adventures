## You'll want to know: బోలు

**బోలు** — *bōlu* — "hollow".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బోలు** (*bōlu*) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4604 lists *bōlu*, "hollow", with Tamil *pōr*, "hollow of a tree", and Kannada *pōr*, "hole".
