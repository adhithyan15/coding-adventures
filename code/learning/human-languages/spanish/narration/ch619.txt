Spanish, chapter 619: A lo mejor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a lo mejor — maybe, perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for possibly, then the Spanish for most likely.

You'll want to know: a lo mejor.
a lo mejor — "maybe, perhaps".
A lo mejor vamos al cine. Maybe we will go to the cinema.
Followed by the indicative, while quizás often takes the subjunctive. Very common in Spain; parts of Latin America also say capaz que.

The word, taken apart.
a lo mejor is built on mejor, inherited from Latin melior 'better' (EtymDB); literally the phrase is 'at the best'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la perspectiva — the prospect; the perspective.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for predictable, then the Spanish for the opportunity.

You'll want to know: la perspectiva.
la perspectiva — "the prospect; the perspective".
Las perspectivas de empleo son buenas. The job prospects are good.
In the plural it often means future chances.

The word, taken apart.
perspectiva is a learned word usually traced to Medieval Latin perspectiva 'the science of optics'; it is built on Latin perspicere 'to look through' (Lewis & Short), from specere 'to look'.
