## You'll want to know: shài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C142-CHORE142-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shài* — "to dry in the sun".

> *jīntiān shì qíngtiān wǒmen shài yīfu* — It's a sunny day, so we're putting the washing out to dry.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shài* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sreaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to sun, to expose to the sun'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
