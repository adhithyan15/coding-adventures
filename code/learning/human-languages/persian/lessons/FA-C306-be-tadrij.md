## You'll want to know: به تدریج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C306-TADRIJ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**به تدریج** (*be tadrij*) — "gradually, little by little".

> هوا به تدریج سرد شد.
>
> *Havâ be tadrij sard shod.*
>
> The weather gradually turned cold.

Formal; in speech *kam-kam*. The adjective is *tadriji* "gradual".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tadrij* is usually traced to Arabic *tadrīj*, "a gradual progression"; EtymDB lists no entry. *be* "to, by" is inherited Persian.
