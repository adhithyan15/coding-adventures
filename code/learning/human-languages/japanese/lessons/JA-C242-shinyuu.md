## You'll want to know: しんゆう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C242-FRIENDS242-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しんゆう** — *shin'yū* — "a close friend; a best friend".

> **かれは あにの しんゆうですか。** — *kare wa ani no shin'yū desu ka* — "Is he my elder brother's best friend?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんゆう** (*shin'yū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "intimate" (Middle Chinese *tshin*) and *yū*, "friend" (*hjuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
