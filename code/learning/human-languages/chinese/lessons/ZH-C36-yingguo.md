## You'll want to know: Yīngguó
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C36-WHERE36-03]; assesses=[] -->

*Yīngguó* — "Britain; the United Kingdom".

> *Yīngguó hěn xiǎo* — Britain is quite small.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Yīngguó* is built from yīng 'flower, fine' and 国 'country', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jaeng* and *kwok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). Yīng is usually explained as standing for the first syllable of England.
