## You'll want to know: 船长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C411-FERRY411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**船长** — *chuánzhǎng* — "ship's captain".

> **我想当船长** *wǒ xiǎng dāng chuánzhǎng* — I want to be a ship's captain.

The new character, in pieces: 船 — eleven strokes in two pieces: on the left the boat piece, six strokes, a short stroke falling to the left, a long one down the left, a bar that turns down and hooks, a dot, a bar rising across and a last dot; on the right a short stroke falling to the left and a bar that turns down and bends right, then 口 under them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**船长** (*chuánzhǎng*) is built from 船 'a boat' and 长 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zywen* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'big'.
