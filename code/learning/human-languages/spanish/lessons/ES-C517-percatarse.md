## You'll want to know: percatarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C517-TESTIGO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**percatarse** — "to notice, to realise".

> No me percaté de nada aquella noche.
>
> *I didn't notice anything that night.*

*Percatarse de algo* — to notice something. A little formal; in speech, *darse cuenta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*percatarse* is built on old Spanish *catar* 'to see, to examine' with *per-*. *Catar* is usually traced to Latin *captāre* 'to try to catch', from *capere* 'to take'.
