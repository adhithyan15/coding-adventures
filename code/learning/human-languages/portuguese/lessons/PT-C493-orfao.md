## You'll want to know: o órfão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C493-GERACOES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o órfão** — "orphan".

> O rapaz ficou órfão muito novo e foi viver com os avós.
>
> *The boy was orphaned very young and went to live with his grandparents.*

Also an adjective: *ficar órfão* — to lose your parents. A girl is *a órfã*; the plural is *órfãos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*órfão* is inherited from Late Latin *orphanus* "an orphan" (Lewis & Short), which was taken from Greek *orphanós* "without parents" (EtymDB).
