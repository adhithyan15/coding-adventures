# নাম, and two pieces from the signs chapter

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 5s] Say *name*, then *my*, then put them together.

## Guided Practice — the distant band
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-AMAR-01, BN-SCRIPT-NAAM-READ-01] -->

- [YOU SAY: *name* — *nām*]
- [YOU SAY: *my* — *āmār*]
- [YOU SAY: "my name is —" with your own name in it]
- [YOU SAY: what Bengali does *not* put in that sentence (**no "is"**)]

The missing verb is the part that decays first, because English keeps insisting
it belongs there. Say the sentence once more without it.

## Writing — from sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-TA-01, BN-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-NAAM-READ-01] -->

Cover every model. Write the dental consonant, then the sign that hangs beneath a
letter, then the word for *name*.

- [YOU WRITE: ত]
- [YOU WRITE: ু]
- [YOU WRITE: নাম]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-TA-01, BN-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Is Bengali's **ত** made at the teeth or behind them? (**At the
teeth**.) And how many pieces are in **নাম**? (**Three**.)
