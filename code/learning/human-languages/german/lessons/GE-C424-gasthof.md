## You'll want to know: der Gasthof
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C424-GASTHOF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Gasthof** — "the inn, the country hotel".

> Im Dorf gibt es einen alten Gasthof mit gutem Essen.
>
> *In the village there is an old inn with good food.*

Plural: *die Gasthöfe*. Common in the south and in Austria.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gasthof* is built from *Gast* 'guest', inherited from Proto-Germanic *gastiz* and a cousin of English *guest* (EtymDB), and *Hof* 'yard, farm, court', also an inherited word.
