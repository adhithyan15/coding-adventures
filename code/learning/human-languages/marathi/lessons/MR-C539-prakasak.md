## You'll want to know: प्रकाशक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C539-DAD539-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकाशक** — *prakāśak* — "a publisher".

> प्रकाशकाने माझ्या मावशीचं पुस्तक छापलं.
>
> *prakāśakāne mājhyā māvśīcaṁ pustak chāplaṁ.*
>
> The publisher printed my aunt's book.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकाशक** (*prakāśak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prakāśaka*, "bringing to light" (Monier-Williams).
