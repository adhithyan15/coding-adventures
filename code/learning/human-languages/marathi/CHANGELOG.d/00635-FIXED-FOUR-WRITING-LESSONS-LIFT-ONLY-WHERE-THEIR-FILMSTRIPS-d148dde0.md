## Fixed — four writing lessons lift only where their filmstrips lift

The Devanagari native-writer refit (#16751) kept these letters' bodies in
one run, but the Script prose still lifted inside them.

- **द MR-W03-da, ध MR-W03-dha, य MR-W03-ya:** "lift for the outer body" and
  "lift for the lower bowl" become "without lifting"; each now lifts once,
  before the headline, and names the climb up the right stem before it is
  drawn down (ध, य), as the record and strip do.
- **आ MR-W02-aa-independent:** the prose named one lift and asked the
  learner to finger-trace "the five runs". The record has three lifts, so
  four runs: the joined body; the middle shoulder with the inner stem, up
  and down; the trailing stem; the headline.
- Narration and modality records regenerate from the new prose.
