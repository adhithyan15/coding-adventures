## You'll want to know: hacken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C323-SCHAELEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**hacken** — "to chop".

> Sie hackte die Zwiebeln ganz klein.
>
> *She chopped the onions very fine.*

Also to chop wood: *Holz hacken*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hacken* is usually traced to an inherited Germanic verb, a cousin of English *hack*; EtymDB lists no parent.
