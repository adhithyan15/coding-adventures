German, chapter 307: Der Gastgeber.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Gastgeber — the host.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to give presents to, then the German for to unwrap.

You'll want to know: der Gastgeber.
der Gastgeber — "the host".
Der Gastgeber begrüßte jeden Gast an der Tür. The host greeted every guest at the door.
Plural: die Gastgeber. Feminine die Gastgeberin.

The word, taken apart.
Gastgeber joins Gast "guest" and Geber "giver", from geben "to give", Proto-Germanic gebaną (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: bewirten.
bewirten — "to treat guests, to serve food and drink to".
Sie bewirtete ihre Gäste mit Kaffee und Kuchen. She treated her guests to coffee and cake.
Built on der Wirt, the landlord of an inn.

The word, taken apart.
bewirten is inherited from Middle High German bewirten (EtymDB), built on Wirt "host", Old High German wirt, Proto-Germanic werduz "host".

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Ansprache — the speech, the address.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to treat guests, then the German for the guest of honour.

You'll want to know: die Ansprache.
die Ansprache — "the speech, the address".
Der Vater der Braut hielt eine kurze Ansprache. The bride's father gave a short speech.
Plural: die Ansprachen. Eine Ansprache halten. More personal than eine Rede.

The word, taken apart.
Ansprache is built on Sprache "speech", from Old High German sprāhha, Proto-Germanic sprēkijō (EtymDB), with an: words spoken to someone.
