## You'll want to know: تنازل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C373-MAWQIF373-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تنازل** — *tanāzala* — "to concede; to give up".

> تنازل الأخ عن حقه في البيت.
>
> *tanāzala al-akh ʿan ḥaqqihi fī al-bayt.*
>
> The brother gave up his right to the house.

Present: يتنازل, *yatanāzal*.

It takes عن: تنازل عن ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنازل** (*tanāzala*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-z-l*, "to come down, to descend" (Lane). It is Form VI, to come down from a claim: to concede, to give a thing up.
