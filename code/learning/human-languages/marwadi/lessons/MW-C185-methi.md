## You'll want to know: मेथी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C185-MASALO185-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मेथी** — *methī* — "fenugreek".

> मेथी कड़वी है।
>
> *methī kaṛvī hai.*
>
> Fenugreek is bitter.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेथी** (*methī*) comes from a reconstructed *\*metthī*, "fenugreek"; CDIAL lists Hindi, Gujarati and Sindhi *methī* (CDIAL 10313). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
