## You'll want to know: दिशा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C412-DISHA412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दिशा** — *diśā* — "a direction".

> पुणे कोणत्या दिशेला आहे?
>
> *puṇe koṇatyā diśelā āhe?*
>
> In which direction is Pune?

Feminine.

Before an ending दिशा becomes दिशे-: दिशेला, दिशेने.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिशा** (*diśā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *diśā*, "direction, quarter of the compass" (Monier-Williams).
