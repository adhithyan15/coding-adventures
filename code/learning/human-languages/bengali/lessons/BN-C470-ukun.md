## You'll want to know: উকুন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C470-TAK470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উকুন** — *ukun* — "a louse; lice".

> স্কুল থেকে ফিরে মেয়েটার মাথায় উকুন হল।
>
> *skul theke phire meyeṭār māthāy ukun hôlô.*
>
> After coming home from school, the girl had lice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উকুন** (*ukun*) comes from Sanskrit *utkuṇa*, "a louse" (CDIAL 1728), which CDIAL lists in this Bengali form.
