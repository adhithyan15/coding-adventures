## You'll want to know: ઘસડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C401-TYRE401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘસડવું** — *ghasaḍvũ* — "to drag".

> તે ભારે પટારો રસ્તા પર ઘસડે છે.
>
> *te bhāre paṭāro rastā par ghasḍe che.*
>
> He drags the heavy trunk along the road.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પટારો ઘસડ્યો, *teṇe paṭāro ghasaḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘસડવું** (*ghasaḍvũ*) seems to be built on the root of the Gujarati verb *ghasvũ*, "to rub", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *gharṣati*, "rubs" (CDIAL 4450): what you drag rubs along the ground. CDIAL gives no form for ઘસડવું itself, so the link is hedged.
