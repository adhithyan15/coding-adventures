## You'll want to know: पञ्चांगम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C528-AYOJAYATI528-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पञ्चांगम्** — *pañcāṅgam* — "an almanac".

> यदि पञ्चांगं शुभं दिनं वदति, तर्हि विवाहः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi pañcāṅgaṃ śubhaṃ dinaṃ vadati, tarhi vivāhaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the almanac gives a lucky day, the wedding will take place.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पञ्चांगम्** (*pañcāṅgam*) is built from *pañca*, "five", and *aṅga*, "a part"; MW gives *pañcāṅga* as a calendar or almanac, which treats of five things, among them the days and the lunar days (MW).
