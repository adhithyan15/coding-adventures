## You'll want to know: शरारत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C499-KISHOR499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शरारत** — *śarārat* — "mischief".

> बचपन में मीरा बहुत शरारत करती थी।
>
> *bacpan meṁ Mīrā bahut śarārat kartī thī.*
>
> As a child Mira got up to a lot of mischief.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शरारत** (*śarārat*) is usually traced to Arabic *sharāra*, "wickedness", which reached Hindi through Persian and softened to a child's mischief.
