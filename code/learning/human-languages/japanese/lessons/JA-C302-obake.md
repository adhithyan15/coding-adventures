## You'll want to know: おばけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C302-YUUREI302-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おばけ** — *obake* — "a spook; a monster".

> **いもうとは おばけが こわいです。** — *imōto wa obake ga kowai desu* — "My little sister is afraid of ghosts."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おばけ** (*obake*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *o-*, "the polite prefix", and *bake*, "changing shape" (from *bakeru*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
