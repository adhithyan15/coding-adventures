## You'll want to know: किनारो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C204-OUT204-04]; assesses=[] -->

**किनारो** — *kināro* — "an edge; a shore".

> किनारो अठै सूं दूर कोनी।
>
> *kināro aṭhai sū̃ dūr konī.*
>
> The shore isn't far from here.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किनारो** (*kināro*) is usually traced to Persian *kinār*, "edge, shore".
