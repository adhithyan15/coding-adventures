# తలదాచుకో (taladācukō) — to take shelter

## You'll want to know: తలదాచుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C358-JIVITAM358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తలదాచుకో** — *taladācukō* — "to take shelter".

> వర్షం వచ్చి మేము ఒక గుడిలో తలదాచుకున్నాము.
>
> *varṣaṁ vacci mēmu oka guḍilō taladācukunnāmu.*
>
> It rained, and we took shelter in a temple.

Past (I …): తలదాచుకున్నాను, *taladācukunnānu*.

Literally to hide one's head: తల, head, and దాచుకో, to hide for oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తలదాచుకో** (*taladācukō*) is a native compound: *tala*, "the head" (DEDR 3103), and *dācu*, "to hide" (DEDR 63), with the self-ending *-kō*.
