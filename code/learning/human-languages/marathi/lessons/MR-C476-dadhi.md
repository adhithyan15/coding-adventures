## You'll want to know: दाढी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C476-CEHRA476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दाढी** — *dāḍhī* — "a beard".

> आजोबांची पांढरी दाढी लांब आहे.
>
> *ājobāñcī pāṇḍhrī dāḍhī lāmb āhe.*
>
> Grandfather's white beard is long.

Feminine.

दाढी करणे, to shave; दाढी वाढवणे, to grow a beard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाढी** (*dāḍhī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *dāḍhī*, "fang, tusk, beard", under *\*dāṁṣṭra* (CDIAL 6250).
