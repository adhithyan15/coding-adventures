[PAUSE 3s] A quantity? (**मात्रा**, *mātrā*.) An amount? (**परिमाणम्**, *parimāṇam*.) Moderate? (**मध्यमः**, *madhyamaḥ*.) As much as one likes? (**यथेष्टम्**, *yatheṣṭam*.) Expands? (**विस्तारयति**, *vistārayati*.) Generally? (**सामान्यतः**, *sāmānyataḥ*.) Especially? (**विशेषतः**, *viśeṣataḥ*.) In every way? (**सर्वथा**, *sarvathā*.) Repeatedly? (**बहुशः**, *bahuśaḥ*.) Sums up? (**संक्षिपति**, *saṅkṣipati*.) Benefit? (**हितम्**, *hitam*.) Welfare? (**कल्याणम्**, *kalyāṇam*.) The better course? (**श्रेयः**, *śreyaḥ*.) Rests? (**विश्राम्यति**, *viśrāmyati*.) Receives with honour? (**सत्करोति**, *satkaroti*.) A resolve? (**संकल्पः**, *saṅkalpaḥ*.) An ambition? (**आकांक्षा**, *ākāṅkṣā*.) Longs for? (**आकांक्षति**, *ākāṅkṣati*.) A heartfelt wish? (**अभिलाषः**, *abhilāṣaḥ*.) More and more? (**उत्तरोत्तरम्**, *uttarottaram*.) Perseverance? (**उद्यमः**, *udyamaḥ*.) Constancy? (**धृतिः**, *dhṛtiḥ*.) As far as one can? (**यथाशक्ति**, *yathāśakti*.) Carries out? (**अनुतिष्ठति**, *anutiṣṭhati*.) Encourages? (**प्रोत्साहयति**, *protsāhayati*.) Competes? (**स्पर्धते**, *spardhate*.) A rival? (**प्रतिद्वन्द्वी**, *pratidvandvī*.) A winner? (**जेता**, *jetā*.) Rewards (with a prize)? (**पुरस्करोति**, *puraskaroti*.) Excellence? (**उत्कर्षः**, *utkarṣaḥ*.) An accomplishment? (**सिद्धिः**, *siddhiḥ*.) Comes off? (**सिध्यति**, *sidhyati*.) Earns? (**अर्जयति**, *arjayati*.) A rise in fortune? (**अभ्युदयः**, *abhyudayaḥ*.) Prosperity? (**समृद्धिः**, *samṛddhiḥ*.)
