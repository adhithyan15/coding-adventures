## You'll want to know: garantieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C369-RISIKO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**garantieren** — "to guarantee".

> Ich kann dir garantieren, dass das Essen dort gut ist.
>
> *I can guarantee you that the food there is good.*

Perfekt: *hat garantiert*. *Garantiert!* — definitely!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garantieren* was borrowed from French *garantir* "to guarantee", from Old French *guarantir* (EtymDB), a verb built on a Frankish word.
