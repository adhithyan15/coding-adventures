## You'll want to know: दिशा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C417-DISHA417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दिशा** — *diśā* — "a direction".

> स्टेशन किस दिशा में है?
>
> *sṭeśan kis diśā meṁ hai?*
>
> Which direction is the station in?

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिशा** (*diśā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *diśā*, "a direction, a quarter of the sky" (CDIAL 6339); the same entry lists Hindi *dis*, the form worn down in speech.
