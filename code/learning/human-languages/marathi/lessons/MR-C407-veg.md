## You'll want to know: वेग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C407-VEG407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वेग** — *veg* — "speed".

> घाटात बसचा वेग कमी असतो.
>
> *ghāṭāt bascā veg kamī asto.*
>
> On the ghat road the bus goes at a low speed.

Masculine.

वेगाने, at speed, fast; वेग वाढवणे, to speed up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेग** (*veg*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vega*, "violent agitation, rush, speed" (Monier-Williams).
