## You'll want to know: قلل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C412-IZDIYAD412-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قلل** — *qallala* — "to reduce".

> قلل الطبيب كمية السكر.
>
> *qallala aṭ-ṭabīb kammiyyat as-sukkar.*
>
> The doctor reduced the amount of sugar.

Present: يقلل, *yuqallil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قلل** (*qallala*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-l-l*, "to be few, little" (Lane). It is Form II, to make little: to reduce. قليل and أقل are from the same root.
