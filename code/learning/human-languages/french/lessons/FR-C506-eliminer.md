## You'll want to know: éliminer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C506-RESULTATS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**éliminer** — "to knock out, to eliminate".

> Notre équipe a été éliminée en demi-finale.
>
> *Our team was knocked out in the semi-final.*

Passive in sport: *être éliminé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*éliminer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēlīmināre* "to turn out of doors" (EtymDB), built on *līmen* "threshold" (Lewis & Short).
