## You'll want to know: जंघा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C473-ANGA473-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जंघा** — *jaṅghā* — "the shin".

> मम जंघायां पीडा अस्ति।
>
> *mama jaṅghāyāṃ pīḍā asti.*
>
> I have a pain in my shin.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जंघा** (*jaṅghā*) is traced to a root √jaṃh by MW, which gives "the shank, from the ankle to the knee" (MW).
