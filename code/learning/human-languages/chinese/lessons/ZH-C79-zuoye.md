## You'll want to know: zuòyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòyè* — "homework".

> *jīntiān de zuòyè shì dú hànzì* — Today's homework is reading characters aloud.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòyè* is built from zuò 'to do' and yè 'task', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsak* and *ngjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
