## You'll want to know: dēfēnsiō, dēfēnsiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C412-COGNITIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dēfēnsiō, dēfēnsiōnis** — "a defence".

> Dēfēnsiō reī longa fuit.
>
> *The defence of the accused was long.*

*dēfēnsiō, dēfēnsiōnis* f., the noun of *dēfendō*. English *defence* comes through French from the same verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēfēnsiō* is the noun built on *dēfendō* "to ward off, to defend" (Lewis & Short). English *defence* comes through French from the same verb.
