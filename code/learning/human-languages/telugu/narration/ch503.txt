Telugu, chapter 503: Love and Fondness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అనురాగం (anurāgaṁ) — tender love; affection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to yearn, then the Telugu for to waste away.

You'll want to know: అనురాగం.
అనురాగం — anurāgaṁ — "tender love; affection".
అక్కాచెల్లెళ్ళ మధ్య అనురాగం ఎక్కువ. akkācelleḷḷa madhya anurāgaṁ ekkuva. There is deep affection between the sisters.
A warm, slightly literary word: అనురాగంతో, lovingly.

The word, taken apart.
అనురాగం (anurāgaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit anurāga, "attachment, love" (CDIAL 331).

Lesson 2 of 5.
మమకారం (mamakāraṁ) — attachment; possessive love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to waste away, then the Telugu for tender love.

You'll want to know: మమకారం.
మమకారం — mamakāraṁ — "attachment; possessive love".
ఆ పాత ఇంటి మీద నాన్నకి మమకారం ఎక్కువ. ā pāta iṇṭi mīda nānnaki mamakāraṁ ekkuva. Dad is very attached to that old house.
What you are attached to takes మీద.

The word, taken apart.
మమకారం (mamakāraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit mamakāra (Monier-Williams), built from mama, "mine", and kāra, "making": the feeling that a person or a thing is your own.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఆప్యాయత (āpyāyata) — warmth; loving care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for tender love, then the Telugu for attachment.

You'll want to know: ఆప్యాయత.
ఆప్యాయత — āpyāyata — "warmth; loving care".
అమ్మమ్మ ఆప్యాయతతో అన్నం వడ్డించింది. ammamma āpyāyatatō annaṁ vaḍḍiñcindi. Grandma served the rice with loving care.
ఆప్యాయంగా, affectionately, is the adverb.

The word, taken apart.
ఆప్యాయత (āpyāyata) is usually traced to Sanskrit āpyāyana, "causing fullness; gladdening" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త: the warmth that makes others feel cared for.

You'll want to know: లాలించు.
లాలించు — lāliñcu — "to fondle; to cuddle".
ఏడుస్తున్న బాబును అమ్మ లాలించింది. ēḍustunna bābunu amma lāliñcindi. Mum cuddled the crying baby boy.
Past (I …): లాలించాను, lāliñcānu.
Also to soothe and humour a child.

The word, taken apart.
లాలించు (lāliñcu) is built on Sanskrit lālana, "caressing" (CDIAL 11025), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 5 of 5.
అభిమానం (abhimānaṁ) — fondness; regard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for warmth, then the Telugu for to fondle.

You'll want to know: అభిమానం.
అభిమానం — abhimānaṁ — "fondness; regard".
ఆ నటుడంటే నాకు చాలా అభిమానం. ā naṭuḍaṇṭē nāku cālā abhimānaṁ. I am very fond of that actor.
Also self-respect or pride, depending on the sentence.

The word, taken apart.
అభిమానం (abhimānaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit abhimāna (CDIAL 524), which meant pride and self-regard; in Telugu it is also the warm regard you feel for a person.
