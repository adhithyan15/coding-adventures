## You'll want to know: ज्वाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C300-DAHAK300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वाला** — *jvālā* — "a blaze; a flame".

> आग की ज्वाला दूर से दिख रही थी।
>
> *āg kī jvālā dūr se dikh rahī thī.*
>
> The blaze could be seen from far away.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वाला** (*jvālā*) is Sanskrit *jvālā*, "a flame" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole. It is a word for writing and for big fires; the everyday word is **लपट**.
