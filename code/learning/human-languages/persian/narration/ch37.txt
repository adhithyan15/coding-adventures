Persian, chapter 37: To give, To close, To cook, To wash, To laugh.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دادن (dâdan) — to give.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to buy, then the Persian for to sell.

You'll want to know: دادن.
دادن — dâdan — "to give".

The word, taken apart.
dâdan is a native Persian verb, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian dādan. EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give", the root of Latin dare.
