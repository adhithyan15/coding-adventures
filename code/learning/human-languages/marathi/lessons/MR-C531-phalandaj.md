## You'll want to know: फलंदाज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C531-KHELADU531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फलंदाज** — *phalandāj* — "a batsman; a batter".

> फलंदाजाने चेंडू मैदानाबाहेर मारला.
>
> *phalandājāne ceṇḍū maidānābāher mārlā.*
>
> The batsman hit the ball out of the ground.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फलंदाज** (*phalandāj*) is a Marathi coinage made on the pattern of गोलंदाज, the bowler, whose -अंदाज is Persian *andāz*, "thrower". The first part is probably Sanskrit *phala*, glossed also as a blade (Monier-Williams): the flat face of the bat. That reading is not settled.
