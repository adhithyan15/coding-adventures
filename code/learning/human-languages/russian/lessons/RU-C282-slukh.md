## You'll want to know: слух
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C282-ISTOCHNIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**слух** (*slukh*) — "a rumour; hearing".

> По городу ходил странный слух.
>
> *Po górodu khodíl stránnyy slukh.*
>
> A strange rumour was going round the town.

*Ходят слухи* — rumours are going round. Also an ear for music: *у неё хороший слух* (*u neyó khoróshiy slukh*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*слух* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sluxŭ* "hearing", from PIE *ḱlew-* "to hear" (EtymDB): what people have heard.
