Lesson 1 of 5.
پڑھانا (paṛhānā) — to tutor, to teach (lessons).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an advert, then the Urdu for a receipt.

You'll want to know: پڑھانا.
پڑھانا — paṛhānā — "to tutor, to teach (lessons)".

The word, taken apart.
پڑھانا (paṛhānā) is the causing form of paṛhnā, "to read": to teach is to make someone read. CDIAL lists both under Sanskrit paṭhati, "repeats aloud, reads" (CDIAL 7712).

یاد کرنا — yād karnā — "to memorise; to remember".

The phrase, taken apart.
یاد کرنا (yād karnā) joins yād, "memory", usually traced to Persian yād, and karnā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 4 of 5.
مسکرانا (muskurānā) — to smile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to memorise; to remember, then the Urdu for to scream.

You'll want to know: مسکرانا.
مسکرانا — muskurānā — "to smile".

The word, taken apart.
مسکرانا (muskurānā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from muss, "smile, sob", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10227). CDIAL notes an added -k-.
