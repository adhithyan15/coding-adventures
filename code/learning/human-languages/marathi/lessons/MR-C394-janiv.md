## You'll want to know: जाणीव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C394-BHAN394-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जाणीव** — *jāṇīv* — "a realization; awareness".

> मला माझ्या चुकीची जाणीव झाली.
>
> *malā mājhyā cukīcī jāṇīv jhālī.*
>
> I realized my mistake.

Feminine.

X ची जाणीव होणे, to become aware of X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जाणीव** (*jāṇīv*) is a noun built on जाणणे, "to know", with the ending -ईव; CDIAL lists the verb under Sanskrit *jānāti*, "knows" (CDIAL 5193).
