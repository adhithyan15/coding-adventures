Lesson 1 of 5.
بهتر (behtar) — better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for broken, then the Persian for different.

You'll want to know: بهتر.
بهتر — behtar — "better".

The word, taken apart.
behtar is beh, "good", with the comparative ending -tar. beh is native, usually traced to Middle Persian weh. English better only looks alike: it is a Germanic word, and no source links it to beh.

Lesson 3 of 5.
کند (kond) — blunt; slow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for better, then the Persian for successful.

You'll want to know: کند.
کند — kond — "blunt; slow".

The word, taken apart.
kond is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: EtymDB takes it back to an Indo-Iranian word for "blunt, dull". (It also lists qand, "sugar candy", a different word.)

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for blunt, then the Persian for rough.

You'll want to know: خلوت.
خلوت — khalvat — "uncrowded".

The word, taken apart.
khalvat is borrowed from Arabic ḫalwa, "solitude, privacy". Its Arabic root is ḫ-l-w (Lane). Persian uses it for an uncrowded place.
