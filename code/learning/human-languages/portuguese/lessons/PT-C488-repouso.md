## You'll want to know: o repouso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C488-CURA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o repouso** — "rest".

> O médico recomendou uma semana de repouso.
>
> *The doctor recommended a week of rest.*

*Estar em repouso* — to be resting, on doctor's orders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*repouso* comes from *repousar* "to rest" (EtymDB), inherited from Late Latin *repausāre* "to lay at rest", built on *pausāre* "to pause".
