## You'll want to know: qǐyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C345-LOOKBACK345-03]; assesses=[] -->

*qǐyuán* — "origin".

> *nǐ zhīdào Chūnjié de qǐyuán ma* — Do you know how the lunar new year began?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qǐyuán* is built from 起 'to rise' and *yuán* 'a source', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khiX* and *ngjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says of the second that the head of a river is called so.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
