## You'll want to know: હેલ્મેટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C400-SCOOTER400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હેલ્મેટ** — *helmeṭ* — "a helmet".

> તમારે હેલ્મેટ પહેરવી જોઈએ.
>
> *tamāre helmeṭ pahervī joīe.*
>
> You should wear a helmet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હેલ્મેટ** (*helmeṭ*) is borrowed from English *helmet*.
