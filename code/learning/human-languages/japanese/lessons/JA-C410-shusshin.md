## You'll want to know: しゅっしん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C410-JIMOTO410-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっしん** — *shusshin* — "one's place of origin".

> **せんせいは どこの しゅっしんですか。** — *sensei wa doko no shusshin desu ka* — "Where are you from, teacher?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっしん** (*shusshin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shutsu*, "exit" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwit*) and *shin*, "somebody" (*syin*), joined as *shusshin* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
