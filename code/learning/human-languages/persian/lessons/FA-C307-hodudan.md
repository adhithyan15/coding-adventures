## You'll want to know: حدودا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C307-AVAYEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حدودا** (*hodudan*) — "roughly, approximately".

> حدودا صد نفر در جشن بودند.
>
> *Hodudan sad nafar dar jashn budand.*
>
> Roughly a hundred people were at the celebration.

Also *dar hodud-e* "around": *dar hodud-e sâ'at-e dah* "around ten o'clock".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hodudan* is built on *hodud* "limits", the Arabic plural of *ḥadd* "limit, edge", with the Arabic adverb ending *-an*. EtymDB lists Arabic *ḥudūd*, "boundaries", though not the adverb.
