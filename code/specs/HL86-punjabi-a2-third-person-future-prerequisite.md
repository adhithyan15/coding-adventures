# HL86 — Punjabi A2 third-person future prerequisite

Issue: #17962

Punjabi A2 mock drafting exposed four reusable third-person future forms that
the authored course had not yet printed in exact Gurmukhi: `ਹੋਵੇਗਾ`, `ਹੋਵੇਗੀ`,
`ਆਵੇਗਾ`, and `ਆਵੇਗੀ`. Together they account for 14 occurrences in the
preserved draft. Chapter 186 closes only that prerequisite and does not edit
the mock.

The ramp first retrieves the already-taught infinitives `ਹੋਣਾ` and `ਆਉਣਾ`.
It then teaches the masculine and feminine future forms of `ਹੋਣਾ`, reviews
that agreement contrast, and transfers the same contrast to `ਆਉਣਾ`. Each
measured form has one direct owner and at least two later retrieval lessons.
A mixed review and a four-skill payoff close the chapter without introducing
first-person future forms or the separate past system.

This remains a prerequisite tranche, not mock-readiness evidence. All authored
and generated ownership stays sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test
case; no corpus-wide aggregate is introduced.
