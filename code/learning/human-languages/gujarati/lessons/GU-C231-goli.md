# ગોળી (goḷī) — pill, tablet

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-04, GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to suffer, then the Gujarati for to faint.

## You'll want to know: ગોળી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C231-MEDICINE231-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોળી** — *goḷī* — "pill, tablet".

> **હું રાત્રે ગોળી નથી લેતો.** — *hũ rātre goḷī nathī leto* — "I don't take the pill at night."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોળી** (*goḷī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *gola*, "ball", through *golikā*, "little ball", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "pill" (CDIAL 4321).
