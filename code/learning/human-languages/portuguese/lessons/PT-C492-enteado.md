## You'll want to know: o enteado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C492-FAMILIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o enteado** — "stepson".

> O meu enteado tem doze anos.
>
> *My stepson is twelve.*

*A enteada* — stepdaughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enteado* goes back to Old Portuguese *entẽado* (EtymDB) and is usually traced to Latin *ante nātum* "born before": a child from an earlier marriage.
