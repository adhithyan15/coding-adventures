# ഘ (gha) — the letter inside ആഘോഷം

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-132, ML-SCRIPT-C113-KHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **ഗ** and **ഖ** once each, and name them. (*ga*, voiced; *kha*,
with a puff of breath.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ആഘോഷം** (*āghōṣaṁ*), "a celebration". This lesson takes its second consonant
out and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ഘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C348-GHA-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C348-AGHOSHAM-01] -->

**ഘ** — *gha*: a *g* with a puff of breath after it. It is to **ഗ** what **ഖ** is to **ക**.

In **ആഘോഷം** it wears the *ō* sign, **ഘോ**. Most words with **ഘ** come from Sanskrit.

## Writing: ഘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C348-GHA-01] -->

Put your pen on **ഘ** and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see, slowly, and larger than it is
printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to travel**. It is not
> written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what is in front of you
> needs no such source, and it is how the shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-132, ML-LEX-C348-AGHOSHAM-01, ML-SCRIPT-C348-GHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ഘ? (**gha**, *g* with breath.) Which word did it come from?
(**ആഘോഷം**, *āghōṣaṁ*, "a celebration".) Which letter is it the breathy partner of? (**ഗ**.)
