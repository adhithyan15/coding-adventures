## You'll want to know: کارمزد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C415-MAKHAREJ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کارمزد** (*kârmozd*) — "a fee, a commission".

> بانک برای هر انتقال کارمزد میگیرد.
>
> *bânk barâye har enteqâl kârmozd migirad.*
>
> The bank charges a fee for every transfer.

*Kâr* "work" + *mozd* "wage": pay for a service.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کارمزد** is a native Persian compound built from *kâr* "work" + *mozd* "wage", usually traced to Middle Persian *mizd*: the wage for a piece of work.
