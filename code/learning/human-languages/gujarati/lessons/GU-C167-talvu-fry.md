## You'll want to know: તળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C167-OKRA167-05]; assesses=[] -->

**તળવું** — *taḷvũ* — "to fry".

> **માતા તેલમાં ભજિયાં તળે છે.** — *mātā telmā̃ bhajiyā̃ taḷe che* — "Mother fries bhajiyas in oil."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તળવું** (*taḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*talati*, "fries", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit *talita*, "fried", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to fry, cook in ghee" (CDIAL 5736).
