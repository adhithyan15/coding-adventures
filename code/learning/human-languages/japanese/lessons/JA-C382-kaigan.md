## You'll want to know: かいがん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C382-KAIGAN382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かいがん** — *kaigan* — "the coast; the seashore".

> **なつに かいがんを あるきました。** — *natsu ni kaigan o arukimashita* — "In summer I walked along the coast."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいがん** (*kaigan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "sea" (Middle Chinese *xojX*) and *gan*, "beach" (*nganH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
