## You'll want to know: فریب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C328-SHAHZADA328-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فریب** — *fareb* — "deceit; a false show".

> لوگ اس کے فریب میں آ گئے۔
>
> *log us ke fareb meṅ ā gae.*
>
> People fell for his deceit.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فریب** (*fareb*) is usually traced to Persian *farēb*, "deceit". **فریب میں آنا** is to be taken in.
