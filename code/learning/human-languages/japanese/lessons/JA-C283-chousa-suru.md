## You'll want to know: ちょうさする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C283-HAPPYOU283-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうさする** — *chōsa suru* — "to investigate; to survey".

> **けいさつが じこの ばしょを ちょうさしています。** — *keisatsu ga jiko no basho o chōsa shite imasu* — "The police are examining the scene of the accident."

Polite **ちょうさします**, *chōsashimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **けいさつが ちょうさしました。**, *keisatsu ga chōsa shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうさする** (*chōsa suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "prepare" (Middle Chinese *dewH*) and *sa*, "investigate" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The native verb *suru*, "to do", makes it a verb.
