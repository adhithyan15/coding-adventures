## You'll want to know: বিশৃঙ্খল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C542-BHESTE542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বিশৃঙ্খল** — *bishringkhôl* — "disorderly; in chaos".

> ঝড়ের পরে বাজারটা বিশৃঙ্খল ছিল।
>
> *Jhôṛer pôre bājārṭā bishringkhôl chhilô.*
>
> After the storm the market was in chaos.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিশৃঙ্খল** (*bishringkhôl*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *viśṛṅkhala*, "unfettered", from *śṛṅkhala*, "a chain" (Monier-Williams): with nothing holding it in order.
