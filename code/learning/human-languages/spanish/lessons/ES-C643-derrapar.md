## You'll want to know: derrapar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C643-VOLANTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**derrapar** — "to skid".

> Con la lluvia, el coche derrapó.
>
> *The car skidded in the rain.*

*Un derrape* is a skid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derrapar* was borrowed from French *déraper* (EtymDB), first said of an anchor that comes loose from the sea bed.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
