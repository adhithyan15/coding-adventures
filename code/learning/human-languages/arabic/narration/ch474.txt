Arabic, chapter 474: Birds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صقر (ṣaqr) — a falcon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a swamp, then the Arabic for barren.

You'll want to know: صقر.
صقر — ṣaqr — "a falcon".
طار صقر فوق الوادي. ṭāra ṣaqr fawqa al-wādī. A falcon flew over the valley.
Plural: صقور, ṣuqūr.

The word, taken apart.
صقر (ṣaqr) is usually traced to the native Arabic root ṣ-q-r, "a bird of prey". It is an old native name.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

طفا — ṭafā — "to float".
طفت ريشة على الماء. ṭafat rīsha ʿalā al-māʾ. A feather floated on the water.
Present: يطفو, yaṭfū.

The word, taken apart.
طفا (ṭafā) is usually traced to the native Arabic root ṭ-f-w, "to rise to the surface of water". It is the plain verb.
