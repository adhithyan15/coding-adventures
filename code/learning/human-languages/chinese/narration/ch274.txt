Mandarin Chinese, chapter 274: Free Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
huábīng — to ice-skate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for careless, then the Mandarin for careful with details.

You'll want to know: huábīng.
huábīng — "to ice-skate".
dōngtiān wǒmen qù huábīng — In winter we go skating.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huábīng is built from huá 'slippery' and bīng 'ice', both inherited from Middle Chinese, hweat and ping (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'smooth' and quotes an older dictionary for the second, 'frozen water'.

What you've built.
One more word for what I like.

Lesson 3 of 5.
热门 (rèmén) — popular; all the rage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to ice-skate, then the Mandarin for to collect things.

You'll want to know: 热门.
热门 — rèmén — "popular; all the rage".
这本小说很热门 zhè běn xiǎoshuō hěn rèmén — This novel is all the rage.

The word, taken apart.
热门 (rèmén) is built from 热 'hot' and 门 'a door', both inherited from Middle Chinese, nyet and mwon (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How a hot door came to mean something popular is not settled.
