## You'll want to know: மீள்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C486-VAITTIYAM486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மீள்** — *mīḷ* — "to pull through; to recover".

> அவர் நோயிலிருந்து மெதுவாக மீண்டார்.
>
> *avar nōyiliruntu metuvāka mīṇṭār.*
>
> He slowly recovered from the illness.

Past (I …): மீண்டேன், *mīṇṭēṉ*.

From an illness or a shock: -இலிருந்து மீள். மீண்டும் "again" shares the root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மீள்** (*mīḷ*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4883): to return, to be cured, to be rescued.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
