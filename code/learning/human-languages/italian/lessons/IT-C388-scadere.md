## You'll want to know: scadere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C388-VALIDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**scadere** — "to expire, to run out; to fall due".

> Il mio passaporto scade a marzo.
>
> *My passport expires in March.*

Conjugated like *cadere*, with *essere*: *è scaduto*. Of food, *scaduto* means past its date.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scadere* is built from *s-*, from Latin *ex-*, and *cadere* "to fall", inherited from Latin *cadere* (Lewis & Short): the date falls due.
