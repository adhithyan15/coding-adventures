## You'll want to know: ঘুমানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C321-KINGBODONTI321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুমানো** — *ghumāno* — "to sleep".

> জাদুর জন্য রাজকুমার একশো বছর ঘুমালেন।
>
> *jādur jônyô rājkumār eksho bôchhôr ghumālen.*
>
> Because of the magic the prince slept for a hundred years.

Past (he/she …): সেই শিশু ঘুমাল, *sei shishu ghumālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুমানো** (*ghumāno*) is filed in CDIAL 4485 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *ghummati*, 'revolves', which lists Bengali *ghumā*, 'to doze'; how the sense moved is not set out there.
