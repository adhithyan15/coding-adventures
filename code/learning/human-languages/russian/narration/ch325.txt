Russian, chapter 325: Empires and uprisings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
империя (impériya) — an empire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to die, then the Russian for to free.

You'll want to know: империя.
империя (impériya) — "an empire".
Империя существовала тысячу лет. Impériya sushchestvovála týsyachu let. The empire lasted a thousand years.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
империя (impériya) goes back to Latin imperium "command, rule" (EtymDB), from imperāre "to command".

Lesson 2 of 5.
революция (revolyútsiya) — a revolution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to free, then the Russian for an empire.

You'll want to know: революция.
революция (revolyútsiya) — "a revolution".
После революции жизнь в стране изменилась. Pósle revolyútsii zhizn v strané izmenílas. After the revolution life in the country changed.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
революция (revolyútsiya) came through Polish rewolucja from Latin revolūtiō (EtymDB), "a revolving" (Lewis & Short), built on revolvere "to roll back", from volvere "to roll".

Lesson 3 of 5.
восстание (vosstániye) — an uprising, a revolt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an empire, then the Russian for a revolution.

You'll want to know: восстание.
восстание (vosstániye) — "an uprising, a revolt".
Восстание началось на юге страны. Vosstániye nachalós na yúge straný. The uprising began in the south of the country.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
восстание (vosstániye) is built on восстать "to rise up", from вос- "up" and стать "to stand", inherited from Proto-Slavic stati, PIE steh₂- (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
завоёвывать (zavoyóvyvat) — to conquer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a revolution, then the Russian for an uprising.

You'll want to know: завоёвывать.
завоёвывать (zavoyóvyvat) — "to conquer".
Они завоевали много земель. Oní zavoyeváli mnógo zemél. They conquered many lands.
Imperfective; the perfective is завоевать (zavoyevát), завоюю. Also завоевать сердце — to win a heart.

The word, taken apart.
завоёвывать (zavoyóvyvat) is built on за- and воевать "to wage war", from воин "a warrior", Proto-Slavic vojinŭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
править (právit) — to rule, to govern; to correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an uprising, then the Russian for to conquer.

You'll want to know: править.
править (právit) — "to rule, to govern; to correct".
Царь правил страной тридцать лет. Tsar právil stranóy trídtsat let. The tsar ruled the country for thirty years.
Imperfective. It takes the instrumental: править (právit) страной. Also to correct a text: править (právit) статью.

The word, taken apart.
править (právit) (EtymDB: "to govern") is built on правый "right, straight", an inherited Slavic word: to keep things straight.
