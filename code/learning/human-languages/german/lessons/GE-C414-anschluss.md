## You'll want to know: der Anschluss
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C414-PENDELN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Anschluss** — "the connection (to another train)".

> Wegen der Verspätung verpassen wir den Anschluss.
>
> *Because of the delay we miss our connection.*

Plural: *die Anschlüsse*. Also a connection for water, gas or the internet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Anschluss* is built on *anschließen* 'to connect, to join on' (EtymDB), from *schließen* 'to close, to lock', inherited from Old High German *sliozan* (EtymDB).
