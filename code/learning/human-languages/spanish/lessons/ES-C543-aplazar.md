## You'll want to know: aplazar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C543-RETRASO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aplazar** — "to postpone, to put off".

> Aplazaron el partido por la lluvia.
>
> *They postponed the match because of the rain.*

It takes *c* before *e*: *aplacé*. *Aplazar algo hasta el lunes* — to put something off until Monday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aplazar* is usually explained as built on *plazo* 'a time limit' with *a-* and *-ar*. *Plazo* comes from Latin *placitum* 'what has been agreed', from *placēre* 'to please' (Lewis & Short).
