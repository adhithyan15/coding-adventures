मक्खण (makkhaṇ) — butter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for sesame, then the Marwadi for jaggery.

You'll want to know: मक्खण.
मक्खण — makkhaṇ — "butter".

The word, taken apart.
मक्खण (makkhaṇ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mrakṣaṇa, "rubbing in; ointment, oil". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10378); the match is through Hindi mākhan and Gujarati mākhaṇ.

The word, taken apart.
कचोरी (kachorī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kaccapūra, "a wheaten cake", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2614); the match is through Hindi kacaurī and Gujarati kacorī. CDIAL builds it on pūra, "cake", with a first part it queries.

भुजिया (bhujiyā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhṛjjita, "fried, parched", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants.
