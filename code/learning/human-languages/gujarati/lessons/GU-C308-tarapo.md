## You'll want to know: તરાપો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C308-SAHAS308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**તરાપો** — *tarāpo* — "a raft".

> પૂરમાં ગામના લોકોએ એક તરાપો બનાવ્યો.
>
> *pūrmā̃ gāmnā lokoe ek tarāpo banāvyo.*
>
> During the flood the villagers built a raft.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તરાપો** (*tarāpo*) comes from *\*tarpa*, "a raft", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, a raft of crossed timbers (CDIAL 5726). It is masculine: **તરાપો બનાવ્યો**, built a raft; **તરાપા પર**, on the raft.
