## You'll want to know: भाकीत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C383-BHAKIT383-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भाकीत** — *bhākīt* — "a prediction; a forecast".

> हवामान खात्याने पावसाचं भाकीत केलं.
>
> *havāmān khātyāne pāvsācaṁ bhākīt kelaṁ.*
>
> The weather department forecast rain.

Neuter.

भाकीत करणे, to predict; भाकीत खरं होणे, to come true.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाकीत** (*bhākīt*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
