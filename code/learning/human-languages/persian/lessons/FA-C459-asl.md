## You'll want to know: اصل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C459-JENS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اصل** (*asl*) — "genuine, original (of goods); the origin".

> این فرش اصل است.
>
> *In farsh asl ast.*
>
> This carpet is genuine.

Arabic plural *osul* 'principles'. *Dar asl* — originally.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*asl* was borrowed from Arabic *aṣl* 'origin, original' (EtymDB). Said of goods, it means the real thing.
