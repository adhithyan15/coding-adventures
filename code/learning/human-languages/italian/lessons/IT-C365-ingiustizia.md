## You'll want to know: l'ingiustizia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C365-GIUSTIZIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ingiustizia** — "the injustice".

> Questa è una grande ingiustizia.
>
> *This is a great injustice.*

*Subire un'ingiustizia* — to suffer an injustice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingiustizia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iniūstitia* "injustice" (Lewis & Short), from *iniūstus*, built on *iūstus* "fair, right".
