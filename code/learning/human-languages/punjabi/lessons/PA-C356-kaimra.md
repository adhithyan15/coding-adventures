## You'll want to know: ਕੈਮਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C356-PHOTO356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੈਮਰਾ** — *kaimrā* — "a camera".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਇੱਕ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਕੈਮਰਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere kol ikk purāṇā kaimrā hai.*
>
> I have an old camera.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੈਮਰਾ** (*kaimrā*) is borrowed from English *camera*.
