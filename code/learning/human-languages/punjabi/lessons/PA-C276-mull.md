## You'll want to know: ਮੁੱਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C276-PRICE276-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁੱਲ** — *mull* — "a price".

> ਕਮੀਜ਼ ਦਾ ਮੁੱਲ ਕੀ ਹੈ?
>
> *kamīz dā mull kī hai?*
>
> What is the price of the shirt?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁੱਲ** (*mull*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mūlya*, "original value, price"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *mull*, "price", under it (CDIAL 10257).
