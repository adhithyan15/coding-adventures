Portuguese, chapter 134: Nothing, None, Nobody, Which One, Not Even.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
nada — nothing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to tell, then the Portuguese for to walk.

You'll want to know: nada.
nada — "nothing". With a verb, Portuguese keeps não in front as well: Não vejo nada. — "I don't see anything." It is the same word as in de nada: "it's nothing", so "you're welcome".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: nenhum / nenhuma.
nenhum / nenhuma — "none, not a single". It agrees with its noun, and it also keeps não: Não há nenhum comboio ao sábado. — "There is no train at all on Saturdays."

You'll want to know: qual.
qual — "which, which one". A question that picks from a set: Qual é a tua mala? — "Which is your suitcase?" For more than one, quais: Quais? — "Which ones?"

Lesson 5 of 5.
nem — nor, not even.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for nobody, then the Portuguese for which.

You'll want to know: nem.
nem — "nor, not even". nem... nem is "neither... nor": Não bebo nem café nem chá. — "I drink neither coffee nor tea." After a no, nem eu means "me neither".
