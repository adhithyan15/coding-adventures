Bengali, chapter 116: Watermelon, Aubergine, Garlic, Ginger, Tomato.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
তরমুজ (tôrmuj) — a watermelon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pineapple, then the Bengali for a papaya.

You'll want to know: তরমুজ.
তরমুজ — tôrmuj — "a watermelon".

The word, taken apart.
তরমুজ (tôrmuj) is a loan, usually traced to Persian tarbuz, "watermelon".

Lesson 2 of 5.
বেগুন (begun) — an aubergine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a papaya, then the Bengali for a watermelon.

You'll want to know: বেগুন.
বেগুন — begun — "an aubergine".

The word, taken apart.
বেগুন (begun) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vātiṅgaṇa, "egg-plant" (CDIAL 11503). CDIAL thinks it probably shares a source with other names for the plant, and reports a view that it is Dravidian.
