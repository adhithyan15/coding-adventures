# മറിയുക (maṟiyuka) — to overturn; to tip over

## You'll want to know: മറിയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C379-KOOTTAM379-04]; assesses=[] -->

**മറിയുക** — *maṟiyuka* — "to overturn; to tip over".

> വളവിൽ ബസ് മറിഞ്ഞു.
>
> *vaḷavil basŭ maṟiññu.*
>
> The bus overturned on the bend.

Past: മറിഞ്ഞു, *maṟiññu*.

Of vehicles, boats and pots.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മറിയുക** (*maṟiyuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4761 lists Malayalam *maṟiyuka*, "to turn back, to turn over, to be upset".
