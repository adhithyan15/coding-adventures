Spanish, chapter 609: Progresar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el progreso — the progress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the growth, then the Spanish for the trend.

You'll want to know: el progreso.
el progreso — "the progress".
Has hecho muchos progresos. You have made a lot of progress.
Hacer progresos — to make progress; the plural is usual here.

The word, taken apart.
progreso is a learned borrowing from Latin progressus 'an advance', built on progredi 'to go forward', from gradi 'to step' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
el desafío — the challenge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the progress, then the Spanish for to perfect.

You'll want to know: el desafío.
el desafío — "the challenge".
Aprender un idioma es un desafío. Learning a language is a challenge.
The verb is desafiar (desafío, desafías).

The word, taken apart.
desafío is built on desafiar, which is des- + afiar (EtymDB), an old verb for pledging faith, from fiar, inherited from spoken Latin fidare, from fidere 'to trust'.
