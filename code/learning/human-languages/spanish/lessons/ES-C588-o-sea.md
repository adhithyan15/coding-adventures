## You'll want to know: o sea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C588-ORDENA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o sea** — "I mean, that is".

> Llego el lunes, o sea, dentro de tres días.
>
> *I arrive on Monday, that is, in three days.*

Very common in speech to explain or correct what you said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*o sea* 'or let it be' is built on *o*, inherited from Latin *aut* 'or' (EtymDB), and *sea*, the subjunctive of *ser*, usually traced to Latin *sedeat*, from *sedere* 'to sit'.
