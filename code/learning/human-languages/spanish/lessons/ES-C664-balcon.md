## You'll want to know: el balcón
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C664-HABITACION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el balcón** — "the balcony".

> La habitación tiene un balcón al mar.
>
> *The room has a balcony over the sea.*

*Salir al balcón* — to go out on the balcony.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*balcón* was borrowed from Italian *balcone*, which comes from Langobardic *balko* "beam", a Germanic word (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
