## You'll want to know: sūdor, sūdōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C517-VENA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sūdor, sūdōris** — "sweat".

> Sūdor dē fronte agricolae fluit.
>
> *Sweat flows from the farmer's brow.*

Masculine, third declension, from *sūdō* — to sweat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sūdor** is built on *sūdō* "to sweat", inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to sweat"; English *sweat* is a cousin (EtymDB).
