## You'll want to know: taufen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C296-GEBURT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**taufen** — "to baptise, to christen".

> Das Kind wurde in der alten Kirche getauft.
>
> *The child was baptised in the old church.*

Also to name a ship: *ein Schiff taufen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taufen* is inherited from Old High German *toufen*, from Proto-Germanic *daupijaną* "to dip", from a root meaning "deep" (EtymDB): baptism by dipping.
