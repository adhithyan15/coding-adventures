## You'll want to know: مطالعہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C351-MUTALAA351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مطالعہ** — *mutāliā* — "a study (a piece of research); close reading".

> یہ مطالعہ بچوں کی نیند پر ہے۔
>
> *yih mutāliā bachchoṅ kī nīṅd par hai.*
>
> This study is about children's sleep.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مطالعہ** (*mutāliā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muṭālaʿa*, "perusal, close reading".
