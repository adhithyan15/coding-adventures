## You'll want to know: अवकाशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C406-ADHIROHA406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अवकाशः** — *avakāśaḥ* — "room; free space".

> लोकयाने अवकाशः न अस्ति।
>
> *lokayāne avakāśaḥ na asti.*
>
> There is no room in the bus.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवकाशः** (*avakāśaḥ*) is built from *ava* and the root √kāś, "to appear, to shine"; MW gives *avakāśa* as place, space, room, and also an occasion or opportunity (MW).
