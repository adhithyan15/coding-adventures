Urdu, chapter 376: More and Less.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعداد (tādād) — a number (of people or things); a count.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for tremendous, then the Urdu for severe.

You'll want to know: تعداد.
تعداد — tādād — "a number (of people or things); a count".
اگر تعداد زیادہ ہوئی تو کمرہ چھوٹا پڑے گا۔ agar tādād zyādā huī to kamrā chhoṭā paṛe gā. If there are too many people, the room will be too small.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تعداد (tādād) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taʿdād, "counting".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لگ بھگ (lag-bhag) — about; approximately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an amount, then the Urdu for minor.

You'll want to know: لگ بھگ.
لگ بھگ — lag-bhag — "about; approximately".
اگر ابھی چلے تو لگ بھگ دو گھنٹے لگیں گے۔ agar abhī chale to lag-bhag do ghanṭe lageṅ ge. If we leave now, it will take about two hours.

The word, taken apart.
لگ بھگ (lag-bhag) is built on lag, "near, up to", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lag under Sanskrit lagna, "attached" (CDIAL 10893); bhag is a rhyming partner with no meaning of its own, as far as our sources show.
