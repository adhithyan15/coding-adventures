## You'll want to know: પાંસળી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C229-LIMBS229-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પાંસળી** — *pā̃sḷī* — "rib".

> **તેની પાંસળીમાં દુખાવો નથી.** — *tenī pā̃sḷīmā̃ dukhāvo nathī* — "There is no pain in his rib."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાંસળી** (*pā̃sḷī*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *parśu*, "rib" (CDIAL 7948).
