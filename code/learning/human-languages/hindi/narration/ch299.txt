Hindi, chapter 299: Fire!.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अंगारा (aṅgārā) — an ember; a live coal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to crush, then the Hindi for to steady oneself.

You'll want to know: अंगारा.
अंगारा — aṅgārā — "an ember; a live coal".
चूल्हे से एक अंगारा गिरा। cūlhe se ek aṅgārā girā. An ember fell out of the stove.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
comes from Sanskrit aṅgāra, charcoal, burning or not (Monier-Williams). One small अंगारा (aṅgārā) is enough to start a fire.

Lesson 2 of 5.
सुलगना (sulagnā) — to smoulder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to steady oneself, then the Hindi for an ember.

You'll want to know: सुलगना.
सुलगना — sulagnā — "to smoulder".
अंगारे से लकड़ी सुलग रही थी। aṅgāre se lakṛī sulag rahī thī. The wood was smouldering from the ember.
Past (it …): वह लकड़ी सुलगी, vah lakṛī sulgī.

The word, taken apart.
सुलगना (sulagnā) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit saṁlagna, "stuck together" (CDIAL 12999), where the Hindi verb means to catch fire. It is the slow burning that comes before the flames.

Lesson 3 of 5.
शोला (śolā) — a flame; a burst of fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an ember, then the Hindi for to smoulder.

You'll want to know: शोला.
शोला — śolā — "a flame; a burst of fire".
खिड़की से आग के शोले निकल रहे थे। khiṛkī se āg ke śole nikal rahe the. Flames were leaping out of the window.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शोला (śolā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic shuʿla, "a flame, a blaze". A burning house sends out शोले; so, in speech, do angry eyes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to smoulder, then the Hindi for a flame.

You'll want to know: भड़कना.
भड़कना — bhaṛaknā — "to flare up".
हवा से आग और भड़क गई। havā se āg aur bhaṛak gaī. The wind made the fire flare up even more.
Past (it …): वह आग भड़की, vah āg bhaṛkī.

The word, taken apart.
भड़कना (bhaṛaknā) is inherited from bhaṭ, a sudden jerk or crackle, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9365). A fire भड़कती है, and so can anger or a crowd.

Lesson 5 of 5.
फूस (phūs) — dry straw; thatch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a flame, then the Hindi for to flare up.

You'll want to know: फूस.
फूस — phūs — "dry straw; thatch".
फूस की छत पल भर में जल गई। phūs kī chat pal bhar meṁ jal gaī. The thatched roof burned in a moment.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
फूस (phūs) is inherited from phussa, "defective", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9100), under which it gathers words for old dry straw, chaff and rubbish. फूस की छत is a thatched roof.
