## You'll want to know: la béquille
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C480-PLATRE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la béquille** — "crutch".

> Elle marche avec des béquilles depuis son accident.
>
> *She has been walking on crutches since her accident.*

Also the kickstand of a bike or scooter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la béquille* is usually traced to *bec* "beak", for the curved handle; the origin is not fully settled.
