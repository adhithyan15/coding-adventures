## You'll want to know: جراثیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C474-SPREAD474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جراثیم** — *jarāsīm* — "germs".

> گندے ہاتھوں پر جراثیم ہوتے ہیں۔
>
> *gande hāthoṅ par jarāsīm hote haiṅ.*
>
> There are germs on dirty hands.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جراثیم** (*jarāsīm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jarāthīm*, "germs, seeds of a thing".
