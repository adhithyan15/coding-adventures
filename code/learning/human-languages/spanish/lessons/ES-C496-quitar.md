## You'll want to know: quitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C496-HOGAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**quitar** — "to take away, to remove".

> Quita la mesa después de comer.
>
> *Clear the table after eating.*

*Quitarse la chaqueta* — to take off your jacket.
