# बाहुयुद्धम् (bāhuyuddham) — wrestling

## You'll want to know: बाहुयुद्धम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C525-MALLA525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बाहुयुद्धम्** — *bāhuyuddham* — "wrestling".

> सायं ग्रामे बाहुयुद्धं भवति।
>
> *sāyaṃ grāme bāhuyuddhaṃ bhavati.*
>
> There is wrestling in the village in the evening.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाहुयुद्धम्** (*bāhuyuddham*) is built from *bāhu*, "an arm", and *yuddha*, "a fight"; MW gives *bāhuyuddha* as an arm-fight, a close fight (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
