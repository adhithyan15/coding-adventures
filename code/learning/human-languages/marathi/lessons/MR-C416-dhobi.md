## You'll want to know: धोबी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C416-HAMAL416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धोबी** — *dhobī* — "a washerman".

> धोबी दर आठवड्याला कपडे नेतो.
>
> *dhobī dar āṭhvaḍyālā kapḍe neto.*
>
> The washerman takes the clothes every week.

Masculine.

The Marathi word परीट names the same trade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धोबी** (*dhobī*) is a loan from Hindi *dhobī*, "washerman": CDIAL lists it under *\*dhauvati*, "washes", and notes that the Hindi word spread to the neighbouring languages, Marathi among them (CDIAL 6886).
