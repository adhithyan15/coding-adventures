Arabic, chapter 92: Train, Bridge, Mosque, Hotel, Office.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قطار (qiṭār) — a train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a car, then the Arabic for a bus.

You'll want to know: قطار.
