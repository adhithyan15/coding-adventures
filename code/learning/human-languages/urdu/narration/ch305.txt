Urdu, chapter 305: The Truth Comes Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
معمہ (muammā) — a mystery; a puzzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a police baton, then the Urdu for a curfew.

You'll want to know: معمہ.
معمہ — muammā — "a mystery; a puzzle".
یہ ایک معمہ تھا۔ yih ek muammā thā. This was a mystery.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
معمہ (muammā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muʿammā, "a thing made obscure, a riddle".

The word, taken apart.
چونکنا (chaunknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin caũknā, "a sudden start", under camakka (CDIAL 4676), the entry that also holds چمکنا.
