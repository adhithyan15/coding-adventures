## You'll want to know: ਸਹਿਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C328-CARE328-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹਿਣਾ** — *sahiṇā* — "to put up with".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਦਰਦ ਸਹਿੰਦੀ ਹੈ ਪਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਰੋਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī bahut darad sahindī hai par nahīṁ rondī hai.*
>
> Grandmother puts up with a lot of pain, but she does not cry.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਦਰਦ ਸਹਿਆ, *maiṁ bahut darad sahiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹਿਣਾ** (*sahiṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sahate*, "conquers; bears, suffers"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sahiṇā*, "to bear, put up with", under it (CDIAL 13304).
