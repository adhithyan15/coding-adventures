## You'll want to know: かぜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KAZE]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->

**かぜ** — *kaze* — "a wind", the air that moves. The same two signs are
also the word for "a cold", the illness you catch in winter. Kanji tell the
two apart; in kana they look the same.

Two beats: *ka–ze*. **か** you already write. The second sign is **せ** with
the voicing mark, so *se* becomes *ze*. The next lesson writes it.
