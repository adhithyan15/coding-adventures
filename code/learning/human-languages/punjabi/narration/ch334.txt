Punjabi, chapter 334: Wool and Cloth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉੱਨ (unn) — wool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a cobbler, then the Punjabi for a gardener.

You'll want to know: ਉੱਨ.
ਉੱਨ — unn — "wool".
ਸਰਦੀ ਲਈ ਦਾਦੀ ਉੱਨ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਦੀ ਹੈ। sardī laī dādī unn kharīddī hai. Grandmother buys wool for the winter.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਨ (unn) is inherited from Sanskrit ūrṇā, "wool"; CDIAL lists Punjabi unn, "wool", under it (CDIAL 2424).

You'll want to know: ਕੱਤਣਾ.
ਕੱਤਣਾ — kattṇā — "to spin thread".
ਦਾਦੀ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਉੱਨ ਕੱਤਦੀ ਹੈ। dādī shām nū̃ unn kattdī hai. Grandmother spins wool in the evening.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਉੱਨ ਕੱਤੀ, maiṁ unn kattī.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੱਤਣਾ (kattṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed kartati, "spins"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kattṇā, "spins", under it (CDIAL 2855).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for wool, then the Punjabi for to spin thread.

You'll want to know: ਬੁਣਨਾ.
ਬੁਣਨਾ — buṇnā — "to knit".
ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਮੇਰੇ ਲਈ ਸਵੈਟਰ ਬੁਣਦੇ ਹਨ। mātā jī mere laī savaiṭar buṇde han. Mother knits a sweater for me.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਸਵੈਟਰ ਬੁਣਿਆ, maiṁ ikk savaiṭar buṇiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਬੁਣਨਾ (buṇnā) is inherited from a reconstructed vināti, "weaves"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bunṇā, "to weave", under it (CDIAL 11773).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਜੁਲਾਹਾ (julāhā) — a weaver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to spin thread, then the Punjabi for to knit.

You'll want to know: ਜੁਲਾਹਾ.
ਜੁਲਾਹਾ — julāhā — "a weaver".
ਜੁਲਾਹਾ ਸਾਰਾ ਦਿਨ ਖੇਸ ਬੁਣਦਾ ਹੈ। julāhā sārā din khes buṇdā hai. The weaver weaves thick cotton sheets all day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਜੁਲਾਹਾ (julāhā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian jūlāh, "a weaver".

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੀ ਚੁੰਨੀ ਪੀਲੀ ਰੰਗੀ, maiṁ āpṇī chunnī pīlī raṅgī.

ਰੰਗਣਾ (raṅgṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed raṅgayati, "dyes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi raṅgṇā, "to dye, paint", under it (CDIAL 10570).
