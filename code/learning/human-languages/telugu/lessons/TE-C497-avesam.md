# ఆవేశం (āvēśaṁ) — fury; passion

## You'll want to know: ఆవేశం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C497-KOPAM497-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆవేశం** — *āvēśaṁ* — "fury; passion".

> ఆవేశంలో అతను తలుపు గట్టిగా వేశాడు.
>
> *āvēśaṁlō atanu talupu gaṭṭigā vēśāḍu.*
>
> In a fury he slammed the door.

ఆవేశంలో, in the heat of the moment; ఆవేశంగా, passionately.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆవేశం** (*āvēśaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āveśa*, "entering, taking possession; frenzy, wrath" (Monier-Williams): a feeling that takes hold of you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
