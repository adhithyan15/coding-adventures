Gujarati, chapter 143: To run away, To go around, To stop, To believe, To change.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ભાગવું (bhāgvũ) — to run away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to save, then the Gujarati for to sow.

You'll want to know: ભાગવું.
ભાગવું (bhāgvũ) — "to run away".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bhāgvũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bhāgvũ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bhāgvũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for to save, then the Gujarati for to sow, then say bhāgvũ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ભાગવું (bhāgvũ) mean? ("To run away".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to believe and to change.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: empty, full, true, false, rich]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: poor, young, strong, weak, necessary]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to jump, to swim, to climb, to open, to call]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to send, to forget, to look for, to use, to make]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Empty? (ખાલી.) Full? (ભરેલું.) True? (સાચું.) False? (ખોટું.) Rich? (અમીર.) Poor? (ગરીબ.) Young? (જુવાન.) Strong? (મજબૂત.) Weak? (નબળું.) Necessary? (જરૂરી.) To jump? (કૂદવું.) To swim? (તરવું.) To climb? (ચઢવું.) To open? (ખોલવું.) To call? (બોલાવવું.) To send? (મોકલવું.) To forget? (ભૂલવું.) To look for? (શોધવું.) To use? (વાપરવું.) To make? (બનાવવું.)
