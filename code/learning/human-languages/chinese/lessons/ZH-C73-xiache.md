## You'll want to know: 下车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-01]; assesses=[] -->

**下车** — *xiàchē* — "to get off (a bus or train)".

> **我们下车吃饭** *wǒmen xiàchē chī fàn* — Let's get off and eat.

The new character, in pieces: 下 — a bar, a vertical hanging from it, and a dot to the right: 上 turned over. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**下车** (*xiàchē*) is built from 下 'to go down' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
