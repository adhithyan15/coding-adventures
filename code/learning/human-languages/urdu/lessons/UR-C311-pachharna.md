## You'll want to know: پچھاڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C311-FATEH311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پچھاڑنا** — *pachhāṛnā* — "to throw down; to overpower".

> اس نے سپاہی کو زمین پر پچھاڑا۔
>
> *us ne sipāhī ko zamīn par pachhāṛā.*
>
> He threw the soldier to the ground.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اسے پچھاڑا, *us ne use pachhāṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پچھاڑنا** (*pachhāṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pachāṛnā*, "to knock down", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8493).
