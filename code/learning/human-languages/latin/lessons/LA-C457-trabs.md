## You'll want to know: trabs, trabis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C457-INSULA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**trabs, trabis** — "a beam, a timber; (in poetry) a ship".

> Magnae trabēs in tēctō templī sunt.
>
> *There are great beams in the roof of the temple.*

Third declension, feminine: *trabs, trabis f.* Bigger than a *tignum*. English *architrave* comes, through Italian *trave*, from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trabs* is inherited from PIE *treb-* 'wooden beam' (EtymDB), and EtymDB lists English *thorp* 'village' as a cousin. English *architrave* comes from it through Italian *trave*.
