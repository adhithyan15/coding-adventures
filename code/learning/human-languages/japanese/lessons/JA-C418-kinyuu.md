## You'll want to know: きにゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C418-SHOMEI418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きにゅう** — *kinyū* — "filling in a form; an entry".

> **ようしに じゅうしょを きにゅうして ください。** — *yōshi ni jūsho o kinyū shite kudasai* — "Please fill in your address on the form."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きにゅう** (*kinyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "account" (Middle Chinese *kiH*) and *nyū*, "enter" (*nyip*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
