## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੰਨ** (*chann*) is inherited from Sanskrit *candra*, "shining; moon". CDIAL lists Punjabi *cand* and *candar* under it, and the matching Lahnda *cann* (CDIAL 4661).
