## You'll want to know: けいさんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C362-HIYOU362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**けいさんする** — *keisan suru* — "to calculate".

> **かいものが おわったら、けいさんして ください。** — *kaimono ga owattara, keisan shite kudasai* — "When the shopping is done, please add it up."

Polite **けいさんします**, *keisanshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **てんいんが けいさんしました。**, *ten'in ga keisan shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けいさんする** (*keisan suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *keisan*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *kei*, "plan" (Middle Chinese *kejH*) and *san*, "calculate" (*swanX*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
