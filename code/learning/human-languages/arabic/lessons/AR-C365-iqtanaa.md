## You'll want to know: اقتنع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C365-HAQIQA365-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتنع** — *iqtanaʿa* — "to be convinced".

> اقتنع أبي بالفكرة بعد ساعة.
>
> *iqtanaʿa abī bi-l-fikra baʿda sāʿa.*
>
> My father was convinced by the idea after an hour.

Present: يقتنع, *yaqtaniʿ*.

It takes بـ: اقتنع بـ …. Compare أقنع, to convince.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتنع** (*iqtanaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-n-ʿ*, "to be content, satisfied" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to become content with: to be convinced.
