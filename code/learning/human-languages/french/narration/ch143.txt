French, chapter 143: To switch on, To switch off, To jump, To smile, To shout.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
allumer — to switch on, to light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for an advert, an announcement, then the French for a bill, an invoice.

You'll want to know: allumer.
allumer — "to switch on, to light".

The word, taken apart.
allumer from Old French allumer (EtymDB 2.1) from Vulgar Latin alluminare, from Latin lumen, "light" (Lewis & Short) — inherited.
