## You'll want to know: खाड़ो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C192-BOARD192-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खाड़ो** — *khāṛo* — "a pit; a pothole".

> सड़क मांय बड़ो खाड़ो है।
>
> *saṛak mā̃y baṛo khāṛo hai.*
>
> There's a big hole in the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खाड़ो** (*khāṛo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khāḍa*, "a hollow"; CDIAL lists Marwari *khāṛo*, "hole" (CDIAL 3860).
