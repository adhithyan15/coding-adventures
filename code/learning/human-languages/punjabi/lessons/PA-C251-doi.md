## You'll want to know: ਡੋਈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C251-GHR251-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੋਈ** — *ḍoī* — "a wooden spoon".

> ਡੋਈ ਲੱਕੜ ਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *ḍoī lakkaṛ dī hai.*
>
> The spoon is made of wood.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੋਈ** (*ḍoī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍōva*, "spoon"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍoī*, "spoon" (CDIAL 5573).
