## You'll want to know: 变脸
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C472-FACE472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**变脸** — *biànliǎn* — "to turn hostile suddenly; to change one's attitude abruptly".

> **我没说什么他怎么突然变脸了** *wǒ méi shuō shénme tā zěnme tūrán biànliǎn le* — I didn't say anything, so why has he suddenly turned nasty?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**变脸** (*biànliǎn*) is built from 变 'to change' and 脸 'face', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjenH* and *leamX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to change'. The sense of a sudden change of attitude is not given there.
