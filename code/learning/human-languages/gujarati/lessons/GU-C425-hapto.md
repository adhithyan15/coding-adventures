## You'll want to know: હપતો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C425-LOAN425-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હપતો** — *hapto* — "an instalment".

> મારે લોનનો હપતો દસમી તારીખે ભરવો પડે છે.
>
> *māre lonno hapto dasmī tārīkhe bharvo paḍe che.*
>
> I have to pay the loan instalment on the tenth.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હપતો** (*hapto*) is usually traced to Persian *hafta*, "a week": a sum paid at regular times, once by the week, now often by the month.
