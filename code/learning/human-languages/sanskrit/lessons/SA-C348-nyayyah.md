# न्याय्यः (nyāyyaḥ) — fair; right

## You'll want to know: न्याय्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C348-AUCITYA348-02]; assesses=[] -->

**न्याय्यः** — *nyāyyaḥ* — "fair; right".

> सः निर्णयः न्याय्यः न अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ nirṇayaḥ nyāyyaḥ na asti.*
>
> That decision is not fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**न्याय्यः** (*nyāyyaḥ*) is built on *nyāya*, "rule, right manner"; MW gives *nyāyya* "regular, correct, right, fit, proper" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
