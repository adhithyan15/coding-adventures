## You'll want to know: shōudào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C201-POST201-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shōudào* — "to get something sent; to receive".

> *nǐ shōudào wǒ de yóujiàn le ma* — Did you get my email?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shōudào* is built from *shōu* 'to gather in' and 到 'to arrive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuw* and *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): taking in what has arrived.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
