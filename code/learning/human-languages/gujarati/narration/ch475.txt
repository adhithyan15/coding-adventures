Gujarati, chapter 475: Seeing and Hearing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અંધ (andh) — blind (the respectful word).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spit, then the Gujarati for the palate.

You'll want to know: અંધ.
અંધ — andh — "blind (the respectful word)".
અરુણે એક અંધ માણસને રસ્તા પર મદદ કરી. Aruṇe ek andh māṇasne rastā par madad karī. Arun helped a blind man in the street.

The word, taken apart.
is a tatsama, Sanskrit andha, "blind", as Monier-Williams records it; CDIAL lists the everyday inherited Gujarati forms ā̃dhũ and ā̃dhḷũ under the same word (CDIAL 385), but for a person the careful word is અંધ (andh).

દાદા એક કાને બહેરા છે. dādā ek kāne baherā che. Grandfather is deaf in one ear.

Lesson 4 of 5.
નજર (najar) — eyesight; a glance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for deaf, then the Gujarati for silent.

You'll want to know: નજર.
નજર — najar — "eyesight; a glance".
ઉંમર સાથે દાદીની નજર નબળી થઈ. ummar sāthe dādīnī najar nabḷī thaī. With age my grandmother's eyesight has grown weak.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નજર (najar) is usually traced to Arabic naẓar, "sight, a look", which reached Gujarati through Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
અડકવું (aḍakvũ) — to touch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for silent, then the Gujarati for eyesight.

You'll want to know: અડકવું.
અડકવું — aḍakvũ — "to touch".
ગરમ તપેલીને અડકશો નહીં. garam tapelīne aḍakśo nahī̃. Don't touch the hot pot.
Past (he …): તે ગરમ તપેલીને અડક્યો, te garam tapelīne aḍakyo.

The word, taken apart.
અડકવું (aḍakvũ) goes back to a reconstructed aḍ-, "obstruct, stop", which CDIAL connects with Dravidian; CDIAL lists the Gujarati aṛakvũ, "to touch" (CDIAL 187).
