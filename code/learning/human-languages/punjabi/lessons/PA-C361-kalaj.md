## You'll want to know: ਕਾਲਜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C361-COLLEGE361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਲਜ** — *kālaj* — "a college".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਕਾਲਜ ਵਿੱਚ ਪੜ੍ਹਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ kālaj vich paṛhdī hai.*
>
> My sister studies at a college.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਲਜ** (*kālaj*) is borrowed from English *college*.
