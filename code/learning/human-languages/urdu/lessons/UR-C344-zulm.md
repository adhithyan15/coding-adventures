## You'll want to know: ظلم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C344-BARABAR344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ظلم** — *zulm* — "oppression; cruelty".

> کمزوروں پر ظلم نہیں ہونا چاہیے۔
>
> *kamzoroṅ par zulm nahīṅ honā chāhiye.*
>
> The weak should not be oppressed.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ظلم** (*zulm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ẓulm*, "wrongdoing, injustice".
