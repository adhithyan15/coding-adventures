## You'll want to know: रेटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C365-VATAGHATI365-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रेटणे** — *reṭṇe* — "to push through".

> विरोध असूनही त्याने आपला मुद्दा रेटला.
>
> *virodh asūnhī tyāne āplā muddā reṭlā.*
>
> Despite opposition he pushed his point through.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मुद्दा रेटला, *tyāne muddā reṭlā*.

Also to shove a load: गाडी रेटणे, to push a cart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेटणे** (*reṭṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
