## You'll want to know: ഭീരു
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C530-USHAR530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭീരു** — *bhīru* — "a coward".

> ഞാൻ ഒരു ഭീരുവല്ല, ഞാൻ പോകും.
>
> *ñān oru bhīruvalla, ñān pōkuṁ.*
>
> I am no coward; I will go.

The opposite is ധൈര്യമുള്ള, brave. Before an ending it takes -വ-: ഭീരുവല്ല.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭീരു** (*bhīru*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bhīru*, "timid", from the root *bhī*, "to fear", the root of ഭയം too (CDIAL 9516).
