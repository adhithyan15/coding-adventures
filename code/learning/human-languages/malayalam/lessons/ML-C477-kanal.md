## You'll want to know: കനാൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C477-KATAVU477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**കനാൽ** — *kanāl* — "a canal".

> ഗ്രാമത്തിലൂടെ ഒരു ചെറിയ കനാൽ ഒഴുകുന്നു.
>
> *grāmattilūṭe oru ceṟiya kanāl oḻukunnu.*
>
> A small canal flows through the village.

Canals join the backwaters and carry boats and water for the fields.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കനാൽ** (*kanāl*) was borrowed from English *canal*, a channel dug for water or boats.
