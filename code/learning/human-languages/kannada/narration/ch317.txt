Kannada, chapter 317: If It Rains.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ಬಂದರೆ (bandare) — if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "but" (ādare), "in that case" (hāgādare) and "otherwise" (illadiddare). Then "I have been learning Kannada for two months." (nānu eraḍu tiṅgaḷinda kannaḍa kaliyuttiddēne.)
[pause 1 second]
Those three words share an ending. This lesson takes it out.

You'll want to know: the -ಅರೆ form.
ನೀನು ಬಂದರೆ ನಾವು ಒಟ್ಟಿಗೆ ಊಟ ಮಾಡೋಣ. nīnu bandare nāvu oṭṭige ūṭa māḍōṇa. — "If you come, let's eat together."
ಬಂದರೆ (bandare) is the past without its ending, ಬಂದ-, with -ಅರೆ: "if (you) come". It stays the same for every person: ನಾನು ಬಂದರೆ (bandare), ಅವಳು ಬಂದರೆ (bandare), ನಾವು ಬಂದರೆ (bandare). Though it is built on the past, it points ahead: "if you come (tomorrow)". The same build on other verbs: ಮಾಡಿದ- becomes ಮಾಡಿದರೆ, ಹೋದ- becomes ಹೋದರೆ, ಇದ್ದ- becomes ಇದ್ದರೆ, ಆದ- becomes ಆದರೆ.
ಮಾಡೋಣ, "let's do", has the ending of ನಾಳೆ ಸಿಗೋಣ.
Now the three words. ಆದರೆ is "if it becomes so", and Kannada uses it for "but". ಹಾಗಾದರೆ is ಹಾಗೆ + ಆದರೆ, "if it is that way": "in that case". ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ is "if it is not": "otherwise". Even ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ holds one: "if (you) say why", so "because".

You'll want to know: ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ, if by chance.
ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ ಬಸ್ ತಡವಾದರೆ, ನನಗೆ ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ. ondu vēḷe bas taḍavādare, nanage phōn māḍi. — "If by chance the bus is late, phone me."
ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ, "in case, if by chance", opens the clause; -ಅರೆ still closes it. ತಡವಾದರೆ is ತಡ (taḍa, "delay") + ಆದರೆ: "if it becomes late". ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ says you do not expect it, but you are ready for it.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "If you come, let's eat together" — nīnu bandare nāvu oṭṭige ūṭa māḍōṇa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If she comes, I will tell her" — avaḷu bandare nānu hēḷuttēne]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If by chance the bus is late, phone me" — ondu vēḷe bas taḍavādare, nanage phōn māḍi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If (you) come"? (ಬಂದರೆ (bandare).) "If (you) go"? (ಹೋದರೆ.) "If there is"? (ಇದ್ದರೆ.) Which of the words you knew is "if it becomes"? (ಆದರೆ, "but".) "If by chance"? (ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ … -ಅರೆ.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
ಮಳೆ ಬಂದರೆ (maḷe bandare) — if it rains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "If you come, let's eat together." (nīnu bandare nāvu oṭṭige ūṭa māḍōṇa.) Then "otherwise". (illadiddare.)

You'll want to know: if this, then that.
ನಾಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬಂದರೆ ನಾನು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಇರುತ್ತೇನೆ. nāḷe maḷe bandare nānu maneyallē iruttēne. — "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home."
The if-clause comes first and the result after it, in the -ಉತ್ತೇನೆ that says "will" as well as "do". For something that holds every time, the same shape:
ಬಿಸಿಲು ಇದ್ದರೆ ನಾವು ಕಡಲತೀರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇವೆ. bisilu iddare nāvu kaḍalatīrakke hōguttēve. — "When it is sunny, we go to the beach."
ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇವೆ (hōguttēve, "we go"), the "we" of ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ.

You'll want to know: if not.
ನೀನು ಬೇಗ ಬರದಿದ್ದರೆ ಬಸ್ ಹೋಗುತ್ತದೆ. nīnu bēga baradiddare bas hōguttade. — "If you don't come quickly, the bus will go."
ಬರದಿದ್ದರೆ is ಬರದೆ (barade, "without coming") with ಇದ್ದರೆ: "if (you) are without coming". It is built the way ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ is, ಇಲ್ಲದೆ + ಇದ್ದರೆ. More of them: ಮಾಡದಿದ್ದರೆ, "if (you) don't do it"; ಹೋಗದಿದ್ದರೆ, "if (you) don't go".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home" — nāḷe maḷe bandare nānu maneyallē iruttēne]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "When it is sunny, we go to the beach" — bisilu iddare nāvu kaḍalatīrakke hōguttēve]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If you don't come quickly, the bus will go" — nīnu bēga baradiddare bas hōguttade]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: one thing you will do if it rains tomorrow, and one if it does not]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If it rains"? (ಮಳೆ ಬಂದರೆ (maḷe bandare).) Which form says what will happen? (-ಉತ್ತೇನೆ, the form that says "do" and "will".) "If you don't come"? (ಬರದಿದ್ದರೆ.) Which word you knew is built the same way? (ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — if, and otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "a medicine" and "clean". (auṣadha, svaccha.) Which letter opens the first, and which sits small inside the second? (ಔ; ಛ.)
[once you have stopped driving — write: ಔಷಧ and ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ, once each from memory]

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: If you come tomorrow, let's go to the cinema.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
