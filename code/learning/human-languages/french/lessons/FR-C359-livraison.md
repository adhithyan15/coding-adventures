## You'll want to know: la livraison
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C359-TACHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la livraison** — "the delivery".

> La livraison est gratuite le samedi.
>
> *Delivery is free on Saturdays.*

From *livrer*. *Livraison à domicile* — home delivery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*livraison* is built from *livrer* and *-aison* (EtymDB). *livrer* is inherited from Latin *līberāre* "to set free" (Lewis & Short).
