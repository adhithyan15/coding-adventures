# mayo — Maia's month

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-QUEDARSE, ES-LEX-MONTHS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall the months and **quedarse**, to stay. The hotel dates in this
chapter fall in **mayo**, May.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-MAYO]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish **mayo** continues Latin **Maius**, the Roman month associated with
**Maia**. English **May** preserves the same name. Keep it separate from
**mayor**, “bigger” or “older”: their similar spelling does not make them the
same word.

## Grammar Lens: dates use de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-MAYO, ES-LEX-QUEDARSE] -->

Spanish links a day to its month with **de**: **el quince de mayo**, the
fifteenth of May. A range uses **del ... al ...**: **del quince al dieciocho de
mayo**. The month name stays lowercase.
