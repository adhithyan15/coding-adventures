## You'll want to know: ざしき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C400-TOKONOMA400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ざしき** — *zashiki* — "a tatami room".

> **ざしきで みんなと ごはんを たべました。** — *zashiki de minna to gohan o tabemashita* — "We all had a meal in the tatami room."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ざしき** (*zashiki*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese *za*, "seat" (Middle Chinese *dzwaH*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) *shiki*, "spreading" (from *shiku*), inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
