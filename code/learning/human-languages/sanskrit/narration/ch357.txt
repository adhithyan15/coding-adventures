Sanskrit, chapter 357: A Committee Meets.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
समितिः (samitiḥ) — a committee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a department, then the Sanskrit for a proposal.

You'll want to know: समितिः.
समितिः — samitiḥ — "a committee".
समितिः श्वः निर्णयं करिष्यति। samitiḥ śvaḥ nirṇayaṃ kariṣyati. The committee will decide tomorrow.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
समितिः (samitiḥ) is built on sam, "together", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives sam-iti "a meeting, an assembly, a council", and "a committee" is its modern sense (MW).

[pause 2 seconds]

परिषद् — pariṣad — "a council".
ग्रामस्य परिषत् जलस्य व्यवस्थां करोति। grāmasya pariṣat jalasya vyavasthāṃ karoti. The village council arranges the water supply.
A feminine noun.
The stem ends in -d; said on its own, as here, the word ends in -t: परिषत्, pariṣat.

The word, taken apart.
परिषद् (pariṣad) is built on pari, "around", and the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives pari-ṣad "an assembly, a meeting, an audience, a council" (MW).

सचिवः सभायाः कार्यं करोति। sacivaḥ sabhāyāḥ kāryaṃ karoti. The secretary does the assembly's work.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सचिवः (sacivaḥ) MW gives saciva "an associate, a companion" and "a king's counsellor, a minister", and "a secretary" is its modern sense; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

अध्यक्षः सदस्यान् निर्दिशति। adhyakṣaḥ sadasyān nirdiśati. The president directs the members.
Past (he …): सः सदस्यान् निर्दिष्टवान्, saḥ sadasyān nirdiṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
निर्दिशति (nirdiśati) is built on nir, "out", and the root √diś, "to point"; MW gives nir-√diś "to point to, to show", and nir-diṣṭa "pointed out, declared, enjoined" (MW).
