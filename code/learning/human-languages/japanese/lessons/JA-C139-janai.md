## You'll want to know: じゃない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C139-JANAI]; assesses=[] -->

**じゃない** — *ja nai* — "is not". It is the plain way, among friends,
to say that something is not so. The polite way is **じゃありません**,
*ja arimasen*.

> **みずじゃない。** — *mizu ja nai* — "It isn't water."

Verbs say *not* with their ending. In the polite form, **ます** becomes
**ません**, *masen*:

> **はたらきます** → **はたらきません** — *hatarakimasen* — "I don't work"
>
> **ねます** → **ねません** — *nemasen* — "I don't go to bed"
