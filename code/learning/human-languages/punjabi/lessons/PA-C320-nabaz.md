## You'll want to know: ਨਬਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C320-CLINIC320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਬਜ਼** — *nabaz* — "the pulse".

> ਡਾਕਟਰ ਹਰ ਮਰੀਜ਼ ਦੀ ਨਬਜ਼ ਵੇਖਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ḍākṭar har marīz dī nabaz vekhdā hai.*
>
> The doctor checks every patient's pulse.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਬਜ਼** (*nabaz*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *nabḍ*, "a pulse, a throbbing".
