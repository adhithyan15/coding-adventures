# ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ (phōn māḍi) — please phone

## You'll want to know: ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C106-SOON-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ** (*phōn māḍi*) — "please phone (me)", literally "please do a phone".
**ಮಾಡಿ** is the polite form of **ಮಾಡು**, "do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A way to stay in touch after goodbye.
