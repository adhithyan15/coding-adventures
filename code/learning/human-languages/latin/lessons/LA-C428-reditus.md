## You'll want to know: reditus, reditūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C428-PROFECTIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**reditus, reditūs** — "a return, a coming back".

> Uxor nautae reditum marītī exspectat.
>
> *The sailor's wife waits for her husband's return.*

Fourth declension, masculine: *reditus, reditūs m.* Also income, what comes back from land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reditus* is built on *redeō* 'to go back' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), itself from *red-* 'back' and *eō* 'to go'.
