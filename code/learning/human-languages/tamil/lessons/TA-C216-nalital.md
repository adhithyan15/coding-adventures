## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நாளிதழ்** (*nāḷitaḻ*) is native: *nāḷ*, "day" (DEDR 3656), and *itaḻ*, "petal, leaf" (DEDR 459): "the daily leaf".
