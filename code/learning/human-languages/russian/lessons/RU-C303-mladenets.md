## You'll want to know: младенец
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C303-ROZHDENIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**младенец** (*mladénets*) — "a baby, an infant".

> Младенец спал в кроватке.
>
> *Mladénets spal v krovátke.*
>
> The baby was asleep in the cot.

Genitive *младенца*. Bookish; in speech *малыш* or *ребёнок* (*rebyónok*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*младенец* is a Church Slavonic word, *mladĭnĭcĭ* (EtymDB), from Proto-Slavic *moldŭ* "young"; the native Russian shape of the same root is *молодой*.
