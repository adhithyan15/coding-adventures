## You'll want to know: a cãibra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C489-SONO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a cãibra** — "cramp".

> A meio da noite, acordei com uma cãibra na perna.
>
> *In the middle of the night, I woke up with a cramp in my leg.*

In Brazil also *câimbra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cãibra* is usually traced to a Germanic word meaning "bent, crooked", the same source as English *cramp*; the path into Portuguese is not settled.
