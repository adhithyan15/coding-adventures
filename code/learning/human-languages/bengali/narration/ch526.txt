Bengali, chapter 526: Instruments.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সেতার (setār) — a sitar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to resound, then the Bengali for to whistle.

You'll want to know: সেতার.
সেতার — setār — "a sitar".
আমার কাকা সেতার বাজান। Āmār kākā setār bājān. My uncle plays the sitar.

The word, taken apart.
সেতার (setār) is usually traced to Persian sitār, "three strings": si, "three", and tār, "string".

Lesson 5 of 5.
টুংটাং করা (ṭungṭāng kôrā) — to strum; to tinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a harmonium, then the Bengali for a violin.

You'll want to know: টুংটাং করা.
টুংটাং করা — ṭungṭāng kôrā — "to strum; to tinkle".
সন্ধ্যায় মেয়েটা গিটারে টুংটাং করছিল। Sôndhyāy meyeṭā giṭāre ṭungṭāng kôrchhilô. In the evening the girl was strumming on the guitar.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সেতারে টুংটাং করলেন, tini setāre ṭungṭāng kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
টুংটাং করা (ṭungṭāng kôrā) joins টুংটাং, an imitative word for the light sound of plucked strings or a small bell, to করা, "to do". No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
