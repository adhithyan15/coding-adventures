Russian, chapter 263: What I Think, and So.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
по-моему and мне кажется — an opinion, three ways.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I think that you understand". (Ya dúmayu, shto ty ponimáyesh.) Then the word for "an opinion". (Mnéniye.)
[pause 1 second]
A story told with рассказать says what happened. Giving reasons starts one step earlier, with what you think of it.

Grammar Lens: three frames.
Я думаю, что … is the plain frame, and it needs что and its comma.
По-моему, … — po-móyemu — "in my view". It takes no что; a comma follows it, and then the opinion:
По-моему, это хороший фильм. — po-móyemu, éto khoróshiy film — In my view, it's a good film.

Grammar Lens: мне кажется, что …
Мне кажется, что … — mne kázhetsya, shto — "it seems to me that". It is softer than я думаю: less sure, and politer when you disagree.
Мне кажется, что фильм скучный. — mne kázhetsya, shto film skúchnyy — It seems to me the film is boring.
Мне is "to me", a form of я. The person something seems to is not the subject here: the opinion is.

The exchange.
— По-моему, это хороший фильм. А ты как думаешь? — Я тоже так думаю.
A ty kak dúmayesh? — "And what do you think?" Ya tózhe tak dúmayu — "I think so too." To disagree gently, the second voice says instead: А мне кажется, что он скучный. Он, "he", stands for фильм, a masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
по-моему is по, "by, after", with моему, a form of мой, "my", which EtymDB 2.1 traces to Proto-Slavic mojĭ: "after my fashion". кажется is казаться, "to show oneself, to seem", the -ся form of an older казать, "to show". So мне кажется says "it shows itself to me".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: Po-móyemu, éto khoróshiy film.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Mne kázhetsya, shto film skúchnyy.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: one opinion of yours three ways: ya dúmayu, shto; po-móyemu; mne kázhetsya, shto]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: A ty kak dúmayesh?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"In my view": which frame needs no что? (По-моему.) The softer one, for "it seems to me"? (Мне кажется, что …) "I think so too"? (Я тоже так думаю.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
поэтому and так что — the reason, turned round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "because" and "since". (Potomú shto. Tak kak.) Then an opinion with po-móyemu. An opinion needs a reason, and a reason has a direction.

Grammar Lens: back to the cause, forward to the result.
One rainy day, said two ways:
Мы не гуляли, потому что шёл дождь. — my ne gulyáli, potomú shto shol dozhd — We didn't go for a walk, because it was raining. Шёл дождь, поэтому мы не гуляли. — shol dozhd, poétomu my ne gulyáli — It was raining, so we didn't go for a walk.
Потому что stands in front of the cause. Поэтому stands in front of the result. The facts are the same; the order flips, and the comma moves with the seam. Поэтому can also open a new sentence: Шёл дождь. Поэтому мы не гуляли.

You'll want to know: так что.
так что — tak shto — "so, so then". It also points to the result, and it sounds more like talk:
Шёл дождь, так что мы сидели в кафе. — shol dozhd, tak shto my sidéli v kafé — It was raining, so we sat in a café.
It usually sits inside one sentence, after a comma.

The word, taken apart.
так, "in that way, so", goes back to Old East Slavic tako (EtymDB 2.1), on the pointing stem to- that also gives тот, "that". So так что is "that way, so that". Поэтому is по, "by", with этому, a form of этот: "for this".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: My ne gulyáli, potomú shto shol dozhd.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: turned round: Shol dozhd, poétomu my ne gulyáli.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Shol dozhd, tak shto my sidéli v kafé.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a reason of your own, first with potomú shto, then turned round with poétomu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word stands in front of the cause? (Потому что.) And in front of the result? (Поэтому, or так что.) Which of the two opens a new sentence more easily? (Поэтому.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — what I think, and so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "in my view, it's a good film". (Po-móyemu, éto khoróshiy film.) Then softer: "it seems to me the film is boring". (Mne kázhetsya, shto film skúchnyy.)

What you've built.
Two lessons, and an opinion now comes with its reason. Three frames: я думаю, что, по-моему, мне кажется, что. And two directions: потому что before the cause, поэтому or так что before the result.
[pause 2 seconds]
All of it in two sentences:
По-моему, музей интересный, потому что он старый. — po-móyemu, muzéy interésnyy, potomú shto on stáryy — In my view the museum is interesting, because it's old. Музей старый, поэтому, мне кажется, он интересный. — the same reason, pointed forward, and said more softly.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: Po-móyemu, muzéy interésnyy, potomú shto on stáryy.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Shol dozhd, tak shto my sidéli v kafé.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: A ty kak dúmayesh? about a film you know, with mne kázhetsya, shto]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a short Saturday story: a scene, then snachála, zatém, and a result with poétomu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"So", in front of a result, two ways? (Поэтому, так что.) "In my view", with no что? (По-моему.)
