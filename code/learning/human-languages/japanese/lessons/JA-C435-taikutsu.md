## You'll want to know: たいくつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C435-CONTENT435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**たいくつ** — *taikutsu* — "bored; tedious".

> **あめの しゅうまつは たいくつです。** — *ame no shūmatsu wa taikutsu desu* — "A rainy weekend is boring."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいくつ** (*taikutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "withdraw" (Middle Chinese *thwojH*) and *kutsu*, "bend" (*khjut*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
