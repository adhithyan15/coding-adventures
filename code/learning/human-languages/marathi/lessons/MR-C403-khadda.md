## You'll want to know: खड्डा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C403-KHADDA403-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खड्डा** — *khaḍḍā* — "a pothole; a pit".

> पावसाळ्यात रस्त्यावर खूप खड्डे पडतात.
>
> *pāvsāḷyāt rastyāvar khūp khaḍḍe paḍtāt.*
>
> In the rainy season lots of potholes form on the road.

Masculine. Plural: खड्डे, *khaḍḍe*.

खड्डा पडणे, a pothole forms; the plural is खड्डे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खड्डा** (*khaḍḍā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *khaḍḍā*, "rough hole, pit", under *\*khaḍḍa*, "hole, pit" (CDIAL 3790).
