## You'll want to know: قاضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C406-QAZI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قاضی** (*qâzi*) — "a judge".

> قاضی به حرفهای هر دو طرف گوش داد.
>
> *qâzi be harfhâ-ye har do taraf gush dâd.*
>
> The judge listened to both sides.

Plural *qozât*, an Arabic broken plural, or *qâzihâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قاضی** comes from Arabic *qāḍin* "judge" (EtymDB); Persian says the Arabic *ḍ* as *z*.
