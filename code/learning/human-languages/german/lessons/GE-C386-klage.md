## You'll want to know: die Klage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C386-KLAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Klage** — "the lawsuit; the complaint".

> Das Gericht hat die Klage abgewiesen.
>
> *The court dismissed the lawsuit.*

Plural: *die Klagen*. *Klage erheben* — to file a lawsuit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Klage* is inherited from Middle High German *klage* and Old High German *klaga* "a lament" (EtymDB), the noun of *klagen*.
