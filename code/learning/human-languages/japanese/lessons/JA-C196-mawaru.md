## You'll want to know: まわる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C196-ADDR196-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まわる** — *mawaru* — "to go round; to make the rounds, to spin".

> **しょうてんがいの みせを まわります。** — *shōtengai no mise o mawarimasu* — "I go round the shops on the shopping street."

Polite **まわります**, *mawarimasu*; a godan verb. Its transitive partner **まわす** is taught already.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まわる** (*mawaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
