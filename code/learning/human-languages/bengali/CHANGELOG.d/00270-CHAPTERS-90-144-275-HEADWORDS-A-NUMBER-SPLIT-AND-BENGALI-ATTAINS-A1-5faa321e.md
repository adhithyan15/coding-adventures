## Chapters 90-144: 275 headwords, a number lesson split, and Bengali attains A1

Bengali had four kinds of A1 gap:

- four A1 spine nodes had no lesson: time of day, describing qualities,
  have-and-can, and want;
- it taught 337 headwords against 600;
- **এক দুই তিন চার পাঁচ** (*one to five*) introduced six atoms against a budget
  of three;
- five atoms were revisited fewer than twice.

**The words.** Fifty-five chapters of five, closed by twelve review lessons:

- **time:** **বিকেল**, **ভোর**, **মাঝরাত**, **পরশু**, the days from
  **সোমবার** to **রবিবার** (all but **মঙ্গলবার**, which needs **ঙ**), **বসন্ত**, **হেমন্ত**, **প্রায়ই**,
  **সবসময়**, **ইদানীং** and more (TIME-OF-DAY)
- **want:** **ইচ্ছা**, **আশা**, **স্বপ্ন**, **দরকার**, **শখ** and more
  (SAY-WHAT-I-WANT)
- **have and can:** **পারা**, **শক্তি**, **সুযোগ**, **বুদ্ধি**,
  **জ্ঞান**, **সাহস** and more (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **actions:** thirty, from **রান্না** to **চালানো**
- **things:** 135 of them, covering the kitchen, the house, nature, fruit and
  vegetables, Bengali food (**রসগোল্লা**, **পায়েস**, **লুচি**, **মুড়ি**),
  clothes, animals, the body, and the people of a town
- **qualities:** fifty-five, from **বেঁটে** to **খসখসে** (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text. Every word uses only signs the book already
teaches, so no word needs **ঘ**, **ঙ**, **ঠ**, **ঢ**, **ণ**, **ষ**, **ূ**,
**ৈ**, **ৌ** or **ৎ**, and **ঘর**, **মাঠ**, **বৃষ্টি** and **সূর্য** wait.
Script closure is unchanged: no new lesson prints an untaught glyph.

**The split.** *One to five* keeps the five numbers, the chandrabindu and the
missing inherent *ô*. Its section on **দুই** keeping its old vowel moves to a
continuation lesson straight after it, together with the questions about it.

**The revisits.** Seven warm-up lines in the new chapters cover the five thin
atoms:

- the chapter-11 sentences (*āmi bānglā bôli*);
- reading connected lines of the chapter-40 passage;
- the two digits that lie (**৪** and **৭**);
- reading lines;
- reading words.

      bengali headwords at or below A1   337  ->  612
      level gate        pre-A1  ->  A1
