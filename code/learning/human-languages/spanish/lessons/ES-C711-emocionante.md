## You'll want to know: emocionante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C711-EMOCION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**emocionante** — "exciting; moving".

> Fue un partido emocionante.
>
> *It was an exciting match.*

*Emocionante* describes the thing; *emocionado* describes the person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emocionante* is built on *emocionar* with the ending *-ante*; the verb is made from *emoción*, borrowed from French *émotion* (EtymDB).
