## You'll want to know: отвёртка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C532-DIY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**отвёртка** (*otvyórtka*) — "a screwdriver".

> Папа ищет отвёртку в ящике.
>
> *Pápa íshchet otvyórtku v yáshchike.*
>
> Dad is looking for the screwdriver in the drawer.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отвёртка* is built on *отвертеть*, *отвернуть* 'to unscrew', from *вертеть* 'to turn'; EtymDB has no entry.
