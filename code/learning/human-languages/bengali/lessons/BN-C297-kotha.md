## You'll want to know: কথা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C297-KOTHA297-01]; assesses=[] -->

**কথা** — *kôthā* — "words; talk; what someone said".

> সাক্ষীর কথা শুনে দারোগা কিছু বললেন না।
>
> *sākkhīr kôthā shune dārogā kichhu bôllen nā.*
>
> The inspector listened to the witness and said nothing.

কথা বলা, to talk; কথা দেওয়া, to give your word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কথা** (*kôthā*) is Sanskrit *kathā*, 'talk, conversation; a story' (Monier-Williams), and it keeps both senses in Bengali.
