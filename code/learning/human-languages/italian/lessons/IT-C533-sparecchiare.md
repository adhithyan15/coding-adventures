## You'll want to know: sparecchiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C533-TAVOLA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sparecchiare** — "to clear (a table)".

> Chi sparecchia stasera?
>
> *Who is clearing the dinner table tonight?*

The opposite of *apparecchiare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sparecchiare* is built from *s-* "un-" and *apparecchiare* "to lay a table", which goes back to Latin *apparāre* "to prepare" (Lewis & Short).
