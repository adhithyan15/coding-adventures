Persian, chapter 140: To open, To wipe, To switch on, To switch off, To wake up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
باز کردن (bâz kardan) — to open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an advert, then the Persian for a receipt.

You'll want to know: باز کردن.
باز کردن — bâz kardan — "to open".

The word, taken apart.
bâz kardan pairs bâz, "open", a native Persian word, with the verb kardan, "to do, to make": "to make open". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
