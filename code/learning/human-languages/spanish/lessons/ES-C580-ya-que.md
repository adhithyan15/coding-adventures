## You'll want to know: ya que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C580-MOTIVO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ya que** — "since, as".

> Ya que estás aquí, ayúdame.
>
> *Since you are here, help me.*

Gives a reason the listener already knows; it often goes first in the sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ya que* is built on *ya*, inherited from Latin *iam* 'already', and *que*, from Latin *quid* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
