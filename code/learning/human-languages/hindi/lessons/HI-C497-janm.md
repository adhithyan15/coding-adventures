## You'll want to know: जन्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C497-JANM497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जन्म** — *janm* — "birth".

> मीरा का जन्म एक छोटे गाँव में हुआ था।
>
> *Mīrā kā janm ek choṭe gā̃v meṁ huā thā.*
>
> Mira was born in a small village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जन्म** (*janm*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *janman*, "birth, origin" (Monier-Williams). *janmdin*, "birthday", is built on it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
