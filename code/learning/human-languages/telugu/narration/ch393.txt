Telugu, chapter 393: Gains and Losses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ప్రయోజనం (prayōjanaṁ) — a benefit; a purpose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to arise, then the Telugu for to crop up.

You'll want to know: ప్రయోజనం.
ప్రయోజనం — prayōjanaṁ — "a benefit; a purpose".
ఆ కొత్త రోడ్డు వల్ల ఎవరికి ప్రయోజనం? ā kotta rōḍḍu valla evariki prayōjanaṁ? Who benefits from that new road?
Plural: ప్రయోజనాలు, prayōjanālu.
ప్రయోజనం (prayōjanaṁ) లేదు, it is no use.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రయోజనం (prayōjanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit prayojana, "occasion, motive, purpose, aim" (Monier-Williams); in Telugu it is also the good a thing does.

Lesson 2 of 5.
లాభపడు (lābhapaḍu) — to benefit; to gain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to crop up, then the Telugu for a benefit.

You'll want to know: లాభపడు.
లాభపడు — lābhapaḍu — "to benefit; to gain".
ఆ వ్యాపారంలో మా మామయ్య బాగా లాభపడ్డాడు. ā vyāpāraṁlō mā māmayya bāgā lābhapaḍḍāḍu. My uncle gained a lot from that business.
Past (I …): లాభపడ్డాను, lābhapaḍḍānu.
లాభం, profit, which you know, + పడు: to come into profit.

The word, taken apart.
లాభపడు (lābhapaḍu) is built from లాభం, from Sanskrit lābha, "gain" (CDIAL 11018), and the native verb paḍu, "to feel, to undergo" (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 4 of 5.
హానికరమైన (hānikaramaina) — harmful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to benefit, then the Telugu for to suffer a loss.

You'll want to know: హానికరమైన.
హానికరమైన — hānikaramaina — "harmful".
పొగ తాగడం ఆరోగ్యానికి హానికరమైన అలవాటు. poga tāgaḍaṁ ārōgyāniki hānikaramaina alavāṭu. Smoking is a habit that harms your health.
హాని, harm, + -కర, causing, + -మైన.

The word, taken apart.
హానికరమైన (hānikaramaina) is built on Sanskrit hānikara, "injurious, detrimental" (Monier-Williams), from hāni, "loss, decrease", with Telugu -మైన for an adjective.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఉపయోగపడు (upayōgapaḍu) — to be useful; to come in handy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to suffer a loss, then the Telugu for harmful.

You'll want to know: ఉపయోగపడు.
ఉపయోగపడు — upayōgapaḍu — "to be useful; to come in handy".
మీ సలహా నాకు చాలా ఉపయోగపడింది. mī salahā nāku cālā upayōgapaḍindi. Your advice was very useful to me.
Past (it …): ఉపయోగపడింది, upayōgapaḍindi.
The person helped takes -కి, and the thing that helps is the subject: its past is shown as ఉపయోగపడింది, it came in handy.

The word, taken apart.
ఉపయోగపడు (upayōgapaḍu) is built from Sanskrit upayoga, "use, application" (Monier-Williams), the word in ఉపయోగించు, and the native verb paḍu (DEDR 3853).
