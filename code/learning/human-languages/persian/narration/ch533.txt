Persian, chapter 533: Shows and Presenters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مجری (mojri) — a presenter, a host (on TV or radio).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a podcast, then the Persian for headphones.

You'll want to know: مجری.
مجری (mojri) — "a presenter, a host (on TV or radio)".
مجری این برنامه خیلی شوخ است. mojri-ye in barnâme kheyli shukh ast. The presenter of this programme is very witty.
Plural mojriyân or mojrihâ.

The word, taken apart.
mojri is a loan, usually traced to Arabic mujrī, 'one who carries something out'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
طنز (tanz) — satire, humour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for headphones, then the Persian for a presenter.

You'll want to know: طنز.
طنز (tanz) — "satire, humour".
این نویسنده داستانهای طنز مینویسد. in nevisande dâstânhâ-ye tanz minevisad. This writer writes humorous stories.
tanz — satire, comic writing. tanznevis — a humorist.

The word, taken apart.
tanz comes from Arabic ṭanz (EtymDB), a word for mockery; Persian made it the name of satire.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پخش شدن (pakhsh shodan) — to be broadcast; to spread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for satire, then the Persian for a comedy.

You'll want to know: پخش شدن.
پخش شدن (pakhsh shodan) — "to be broadcast; to spread".
این سریال هر شب از تلویزیون پخش میشود. in seriyâl har shab az televiziyon pakhsh mishavad. This series is broadcast on TV every night.
pakhsh 'spreading' + shodan. pakhsh kardan — to broadcast; to hand out.

The word, taken apart.
pakhsh is usually taken as a native Persian word for spreading, but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled. shodan is inherited.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خیره شدن (khire shodan) — to stare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a comedy, then the Persian for to be broadcast.

You'll want to know: خیره شدن.
خیره شدن (khire shodan) — "to stare".
او ساعتها به صفحه نمایش خیره شد. u sâ'athâ be safhe-ye namâyesh khire shod. He stared at the screen for hours.
be chizi khire shodan; khire 'dazzled, fixed' + shodan.

The word, taken apart.
khire means 'dazzled, fixed', but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled. shodan, 'to become', is inherited.
