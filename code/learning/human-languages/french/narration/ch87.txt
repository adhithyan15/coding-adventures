French, chapter 87: Sheep, Goat, Lion, Monkey, Bee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le mouton — a sheep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a hen, then the French for a duck.

You'll want to know: le mouton.
le mouton — "a sheep".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
