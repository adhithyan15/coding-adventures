Bengali, chapter 519: The Match.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গ্যালারি (gælāri) — the stands (at a stadium); a gallery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a crossword, then the Bengali for carrom.

You'll want to know: গ্যালারি.
গ্যালারি — gælāri — "the stands (at a stadium); a gallery".
স্টেডিয়ামের গ্যালারি দর্শকে ভর্তি ছিল। Sṭeḍiyāmer gælāri dôrshôke bhôrti chhilô. The stands at the stadium were full of spectators.

The word, taken apart.
গ্যালারি (gælāri) is borrowed from English gallery, used for the stands around a ground.

Lesson 3 of 5.
অধিনায়ক (ôdhināyôk) — a team captain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the stands, then the Bengali for a match.

You'll want to know: অধিনায়ক.
অধিনায়ক — ôdhināyôk — "a team captain".
আমার বন্ধু দলের অধিনায়ক। Āmār bôndhu dôler ôdhināyôk. My friend is the captain of the team.

The word, taken apart.
অধিনায়ক (ôdhināyôk) joins Sanskrit adhi, "over, above", to nāyaka, "a guide, a leader" (Monier-Williams): the leader set over the rest.

Lesson 5 of 5.
হারা (hārā) — to lose (a game); to be beaten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a team captain, then the Bengali for a referee.

You'll want to know: হারা.
হারা — hārā — "to lose (a game); to be beaten".
কাল আমাদের দল হারল। Kāl āmāder dôl hārlô. Our team lost yesterday.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দাবায় হারলেন, tini dābāy hārlen.

The word, taken apart.
হারা (hārā) is the plain partner of হারানো, "to lose (a thing)"; CDIAL lists Bengali hārā, "to lose, to be defeated" (CDIAL 14061).
