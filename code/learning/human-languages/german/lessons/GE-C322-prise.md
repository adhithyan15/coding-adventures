## You'll want to know: die Prise
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C322-ZUTAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Prise** — "the pinch (of salt or spice)".

> Dann kommt eine Prise Salz dazu.
>
> *Then a pinch of salt goes in.*

Plural: *die Prisen*. *Eine Prise Zucker*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Prise* was borrowed from French *prise* "a taking, a hold" (EtymDB): as much as you take between two fingers.
