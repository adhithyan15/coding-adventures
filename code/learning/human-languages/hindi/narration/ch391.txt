Hindi, chapter 391: Effort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रयास (prayās) — an effort (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a step, then the Hindi for to try.

You'll want to know: प्रयास.
प्रयास — prayās — "an effort (formal)".
अगर सब मिलकर प्रयास करेंगे तो गाँव बदलेगा। agar sab milkar prayās kareṅge to gā̃v badlegā. If everyone makes an effort together, the village will change.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रयास (prayās) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit prayāsa, "exertion, effort" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
दोहराना (dohrānā) — to repeat, to go over again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for enthusiasm, then the Hindi for without a break.

You'll want to know: दोहराना.
दोहराना — dohrānā — "to repeat, to go over again".
अगर तुम यह पाठ दो बार दोहराओगे तो याद हो जाएगा। agar tum yah pāṭh do bār dohrāoge to yād ho jāegā. If you go over this lesson twice, you'll remember it.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सवाल दोहराया, usne savāl dohrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
दोहराना (dohrānā) is a verb made on दोहरा, "double", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed dudhāra (CDIAL 6407): to do a thing twice over.
