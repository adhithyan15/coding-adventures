## You'll want to know: સૈનિક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C320-YUDDH320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સૈનિક** — *sainik* — "a soldier".

> સેનાનો સૌથી બહાદુર સૈનિક ઘાયલ થયો.
>
> *senāno sauthī bahādur sainik ghāyal thayo.*
>
> The bravest soldier in the army was wounded.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૈનિક** (*sainik*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sainika*, "belonging to an army" (Monier-Williams), built on *sēnā*, the word behind **સેના**: one who serves in it.
