## You'll want to know: ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C360-EXAM360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ** — *imtihān* — "an examination; a test".

> ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਸਾਡਾ ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ ਹੈ।
>
> *agle hafte sāḍā imtihān hai.*
>
> We have an exam next week.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ** (*imtihān*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *imtiḥān*, "examination".
