## You'll want to know: stīpendium, stīpendiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C379-QUAESTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**stīpendium, stīpendiī** — "pay, wages (especially a soldier's)".

> Mīlitēs stīpendium accēpērunt.
>
> *The soldiers received their pay.*

*stīpendium, stīpendiī* n. A soldier's pay, then a year of service: *vīgintī stīpendia*, twenty campaigns. English *stipend*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stīpendium* is contracted from *stipipendium*, built from *stips* "a small coin" and *pendō* "to weigh out, to pay" (Lewis & Short): coins weighed out. English *stipend* comes from it.
