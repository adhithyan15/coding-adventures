## You'll want to know: ਲਾੜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C313-WED313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਾੜਾ** — *lāṛā* — "a bridegroom".

> ਲਾੜਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ ਸੀ ਅਤੇ ਸਭ ਨਾਲ ਨੱਚਿਆ।
>
> *lāṛā bahut khush sī ate sabh nāl nachchiā.*
>
> The bridegroom was very happy and danced with everyone.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਾੜਾ** (*lāṛā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*lāḍa*, "fondling"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *lāṛā*, "bridegroom", under it (CDIAL 11012).
