## You'll want to know: डमरुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C521-VADAKA521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**डमरुः** — *ḍamaruḥ* — "a hand drum".

> बालः डमरुं वादयति।
>
> *bālaḥ ḍamaruṃ vādayati.*
>
> The child plays a hand drum.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डमरुः** (*ḍamaruḥ*) is the word MW records for a sacred drum shaped like an hourglass, the drum of Śiva; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
