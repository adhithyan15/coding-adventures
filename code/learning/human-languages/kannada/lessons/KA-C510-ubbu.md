# ಉಬ್ಬು (ubbu) — to swell; to bulge

## You'll want to know: ಉಬ್ಬು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C510-NOVU510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಬ್ಬು** — *ubbu* — "to swell; to bulge".

> ಜೇನುನೊಣ ಕಚ್ಚಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನ ಕೈ ಉಬ್ಬಿತು.
>
> *jēnunoṇa kaccida mēle avana kai ubbitu.*
>
> After the bee stung him, his hand swelled.

Past (it …): ಉಬ್ಬಿತು, *ubbitu*.

ಊತ is a swelling; ಉಬ್ಬು is the swelling up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಬ್ಬು** (*ubbu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 666 lists Kannada *ubbu*, "to swell, rise up", with the noun *ubbuvike*, "swelling".
