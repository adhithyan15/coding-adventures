## You'll want to know: браузер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C399-SET-04]; assesses=[] -->

**браузер** (*bráuzer*) — "a web browser".

> Этот сайт не открывается в моём браузере.
>
> *Étot sayt ne otkryváyetsya v moyóm bráuzere.*
>
> This website does not open in my browser.

Masculine. From English *browser*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**браузер** was borrowed from English *browser* (EtymDB) in the 1990s, keeping the English sound.
