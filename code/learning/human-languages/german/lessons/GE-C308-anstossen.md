## You'll want to know: anstoßen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C308-ANSTOSSEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**anstoßen** — "to clink glasses, to drink to".

> Um Mitternacht stießen alle an.
>
> *At midnight everyone clinked glasses.*

Separable: *wir stoßen an*. *Auf jemanden anstoßen* — to drink to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anstoßen* is built on *stoßen* "to push, to bump", from Proto-Germanic *stautaną* (EtymDB), with *an*: glass knocks on glass.
