## You'll want to know: 长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C61-LONG61-01]; assesses=[] -->

**长** — *cháng* — "long".

> *jīntiān de diànyǐng hěn cháng* — Today's film is a long one.

The new character, in pieces: 长 — four strokes: a short fall, a long bar, a vertical that flicks up at the foot, and a sweep down to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**长** (*cháng*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *drjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'lasting', 'far' and 'for ever'.
