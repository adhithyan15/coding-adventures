## You'll want to know: બુઝુર્ગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C493-PEDHI493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બુઝુર્ગ** — *bujhurg* — "a respected elder".

> ગામના બુઝુર્ગો સાંજે મંદિર પાસે બેસે છે.
>
> *gāmnā bujhurgo sā̃je mandir pāse bese che.*
>
> In the evenings the village elders sit near the temple.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બુઝુર્ગ** (*bujhurg*) is usually traced to Persian *buzurg*, "great, elder".
