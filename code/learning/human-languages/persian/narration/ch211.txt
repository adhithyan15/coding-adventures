Persian, chapter 211: Cotton, Wool, Silk, Brick, Metal.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
پنبه (panbe) — cotton.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the internet, then the Persian for a mobile phone.

You'll want to know: پنبه.
پنبه — panbe — "cotton".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
