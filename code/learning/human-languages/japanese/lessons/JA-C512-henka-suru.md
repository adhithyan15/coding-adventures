## You'll want to know: へんかする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C512-CHANGE512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**へんかする** — *henka suru* — "to change; to vary".

> **もし てんきが へんかしたら、しあいは ちゅうしでしょう。** — *moshi tenki ga henkashitara, shiai wa chūshi deshō* — "If the weather changes, the match will probably be called off."

Polite **へんかします**, *henkashimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **まちが へんかしました。**, *machi ga henkashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へんかする** (*henka suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *henka* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hen*, "change" (Middle Chinese *pjenH*) and *ka*, "change" (*xwaeH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
