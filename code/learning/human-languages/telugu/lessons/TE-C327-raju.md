# రాజు (rāju) — a king

## You'll want to know: రాజు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C327-RAJYAM327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజు** — *rāju* — "a king".

> ఆ రాజు చాలా మంచివాడు.
>
> *ā rāju cālā mañcivāḍu.*
>
> That king is a very good man.

Plural: రాజులు, *rājulu*.

The capital, రాజధాని, is the seat of the king: the same Sanskrit word sits inside it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజు** (*rāju*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rājan*, "a chieftain, a king" (CDIAL 10679). Telugu gives it the native ending -ఉ.
