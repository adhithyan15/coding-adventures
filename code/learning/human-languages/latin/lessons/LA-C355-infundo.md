## You'll want to know: īnfundō, īnfundere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C355-CONDIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**īnfundō, īnfundere** — "to pour in, to pour on".

> Garum et oleum in patinam īnfūdit.
>
> *He poured fish sauce and oil into the pan.*

*īnfundō, īnfundere, īnfūdī, īnfūsum*: *in* + *fundō*, 'to pour'. English *infuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**īnfundō** is built on *fundō*, "to pour", with *in*, "into" (Lewis & Short). English *infuse* comes from its participle.
