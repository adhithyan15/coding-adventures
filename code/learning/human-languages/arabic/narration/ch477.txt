Arabic, chapter 477: Under the sea.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قرش (qirsh) — a shark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a claw, then the Arabic for to prey on.

You'll want to know: قرش.
قرش — qirsh — "a shark".
رأى الصيادون قرشا بجانب القارب. raʾā aṣ-ṣayyādūn qirshan bi-jānib al-qārib. The fishermen saw a shark beside the boat.
qirsh is also an old coin, a piastre.

The word, taken apart.
قرش (qirsh) is usually traced to the native Arabic root q-r-sh, "to gather, to scrape together".
