## You'll want to know: der Schaffner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C412-BAHNSTEIG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schaffner** — "the conductor, the ticket inspector (on a train)".

> Der Schaffner kommt und will unsere Fahrkarten sehen.
>
> *The conductor comes and wants to see our tickets.*

Same form in the plural. A woman: *die Schaffnerin*. Today the official word is *der Zugbegleiter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schaffner* is built on *schaffen* in the old sense 'to arrange, to manage', inherited from Middle High German *schaffen* (EtymDB); the word is usually said to have first named a steward who ran a household.
