## You'll want to know: ぼうさい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C350-YOUJIN350-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼうさい** — *bōsai* — "disaster preparedness".

> **もし じしんが きたら、ぼうさいの きそくに したがって ください。** — *moshi jishin ga kitara, bōsai no kisoku ni shitagatte kudasai* — "If an earthquake comes, follow the disaster rules."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼうさい** (*bōsai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bō*, "ward off" (Middle Chinese *bjang*) and *sai*, "disaster" (*tsoj*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
