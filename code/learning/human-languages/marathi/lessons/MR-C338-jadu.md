## You'll want to know: जादू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C338-RAKSHAS338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जादू** — *jādū* — "magic".

> जादूने भोपळ्याची गाडी झाली.
>
> *jādūne bhopaḷyācī gāḍī jhālī.*
>
> By magic the pumpkin turned into a carriage.

Feminine.

जादू करणे, to cast a spell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जादू** (*jādū*) is a loan from Persian *jādū*, 'magic, sorcery'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
