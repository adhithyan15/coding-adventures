## You'll want to know: बदबू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C425-HALLA425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बदबू** — *badbū* — "a bad smell, a stink".

> गटर से बहुत बदबू आ रही थी।
>
> *gaṭar se bahut badbū ā rahī thī.*
>
> There was a terrible smell coming from the sewer.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बदबू** (*badbū*) is usually traced to Persian *badbū*, from *bad*, "bad", and *bū*, "smell": the opposite of ख़ुशबू.
