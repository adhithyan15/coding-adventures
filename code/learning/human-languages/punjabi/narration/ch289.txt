Punjabi, chapter 289: The Cold Season Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਧੁੰਦ (dhund) — fog; mist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the hot season, then the Punjabi for a drought.

You'll want to know: ਧੁੰਦ.
ਧੁੰਦ — dhund — "fog; mist".
ਪੋਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਧੁੰਦ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ। poh vich savere dhund hovegī. In Poh there will be fog in the morning.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਧੁੰਦ (dhund) is inherited from a reconstructed dhūmāndha, "dim-sighted"; CDIAL lists Punjabi dhund, "mist, haziness", under it (CDIAL 6858).
