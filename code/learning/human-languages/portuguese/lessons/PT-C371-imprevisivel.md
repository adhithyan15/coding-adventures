## You'll want to know: imprevisível
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C371-IMPREVISTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**imprevisível** — "unpredictable".

> O tempo na montanha é imprevisível.
>
> *The weather in the mountains is unpredictable.*

The opposite of *previsível*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprevisível* is built from *in-* "not" and *previsível* "predictable", on *prever*, which rests on Latin *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
