## You'll want to know: जिव्हाळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C460-MAYA460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जिव्हाळा** — *jivhāḷā* — "warmth of feeling".

> आमच्या गल्लीतल्या लोकांमध्ये खूप जिव्हाळा आहे.
>
> *āmcyā gallītlyā lokāmmadhye khūp jivhāḷā āhe.*
>
> There is a lot of warmth among the people in our lane.

Masculine.

Masculine; जिव्हाळ्याचा, close to the heart: जिव्हाळ्याचा मित्र.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिव्हाळा** (*jivhāḷā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
