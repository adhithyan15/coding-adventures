## You'll want to know: નક્કી કરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C179-PLAN179-02]; assesses=[] -->

**નક્કી કરવું** — *nakkī karvũ* — "to decide, to fix".

> **અમે કાલે તારીખ નક્કી કરીશું.** — *ame kāle tārīkh nakkī karīśũ* — "We will fix the date tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નક્કી કરવું** (*nakkī karvũ*) is built from *nakkī*, "settled, fixed", and the inherited *karvũ*, "to do". The origin of *nakkī* is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form, and EtymDB has no entry.
