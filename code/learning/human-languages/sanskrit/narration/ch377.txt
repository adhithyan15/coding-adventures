Sanskrit, chapter 377: Very and Extremely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अतीव (atīva) — exceedingly; very.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for control, then the Sanskrit for takes refuge in.

You'll want to know: अतीव.
अतीव — atīva — "exceedingly; very".
यदि भवान् आगच्छति, तर्हि अहम् अतीव प्रसन्नः भविष्यामि। yadi bhavān āgacchati, tarhi aham atīva prasannaḥ bhaviṣyāmi. If you come, I will be very pleased.

The word, taken apart.
अतीव (atīva) MW gives atīva "exceedingly, very" and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

You'll want to know: अत्यन्तम्.
अत्यन्तम् — atyantam — "extremely".
यदि ग्रीष्मः अत्यन्तम् उष्णः, तर्हि जनाः बहिः न गच्छन्ति। yadi grīṣmaḥ atyantam uṣṇaḥ, tarhi janāḥ bahiḥ na gacchanti. If the summer is extremely hot, people do not go out.

The word, taken apart.
अत्यन्तम् (atyantam) is built from ati, "beyond", and anta, "an end"; MW gives aty-anta "excessive, very great, very strong" and "endless" (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: नितराम्.
नितराम् — nitarām — "completely; wholly".
यदि भवान् नितरां श्रान्तः, तर्हि विश्रामं करोतु। yadi bhavān nitarāṃ śrāntaḥ, tarhi viśrāmaṃ karotu. If you are completely worn out, take a rest.

The word, taken apart.
नितराम् (nitarām) is built on ni, "down", with the comparative ending -tarām; MW gives ni-tarām "completely, wholly, entirely" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अतिक्रामति (atikrāmati) — exceeds; goes beyond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for intense, then the Sanskrit for completely.

You'll want to know: अतिक्रामति.
अतिक्रामति — atikrāmati — "exceeds; goes beyond".
यदि नदी सीमाम् अतिक्रामति, तर्हि ग्रामे भयं भविष्यति। yadi nadī sīmām atikrāmati, tarhi grāme bhayaṃ bhaviṣyati. If the river goes beyond its banks, there will be fear in the village.
Past (it …): नदी सीमाम् अतिक्रान्तवती, nadī sīmām atikrāntavatī.

The word, taken apart.
अतिक्रामति (atikrāmati) is built on ati, "beyond", and the root √kram, "to step"; MW gives ati-√kram "to step or go beyond, to pass" and ati-krānta "having passed or transgressed" (MW).
