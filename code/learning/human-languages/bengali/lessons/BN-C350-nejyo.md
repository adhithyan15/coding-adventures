## You'll want to know: ন্যায্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C350-ADORSHO350-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ন্যায্য** — *næjyô* — "fair; rightful".

> আমি ন্যায্য দাম চাই।
>
> *āmi næjyô dām chāi.*
>
> I want a fair price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ন্যায্য** (*næjyô*) is Sanskrit *nyāyya*, right or proper (Monier-Williams), from *nyāya*, what is right. A price, a demand or a share can be ন্যায্য.
