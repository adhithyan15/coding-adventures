## You'll want to know: ਗਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C274-SIZE274-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਜ਼** — *gaz* — "a yard".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਕਮਰਾ ਪੰਜ ਗਜ਼ ਲੰਮਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā kamrā panj gaz lammā hai.*
>
> My room is five yards long.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਜ਼** (*gaz*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *gaz*, "a yard, an ell".
