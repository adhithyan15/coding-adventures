## You'll want to know: orgulhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C467-ORGULHO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**orgulhar** — "to be proud (orgulhar-se de); to make proud".

> A avó orgulha-se muito dos netos.
>
> *Grandma is very proud of her grandchildren.*

Mostly reflexive, with the pronoun after the verb: *orgulho-me de ti* — I am proud of you. In Brazil: *me orgulho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orgulhar* is built on *orgulho* "pride", which came into Portuguese from Catalan *orgull* (EtymDB), a borrowing from Frankish.
