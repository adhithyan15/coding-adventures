## You'll want to know: manifestar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C364-PROTESTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**manifestar** — "to show, to express (a feeling); (manifestar-se) to demonstrate".

> Os estudantes manifestaram-se no centro da cidade.
>
> *The students demonstrated in the city centre.*

*Manifestar-se* — to demonstrate; *manifestar uma opinião* — to express an opinion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manifestar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *manifēstāre* "to show, to make public" (EtymDB), on *manifēstus* "clear, evident" (Lewis & Short).
