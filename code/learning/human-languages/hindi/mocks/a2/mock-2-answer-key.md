## Writing

Task 1 must state the morning registration, give a reason, request the evening
class, and ask for confirmation. Task 2 must identify an old problem, explain a
feedback-led change, give two effects, and name one limitation. Apply the
analytic rubric; do not require a fixed script. Scorer-only opening examples:

- **नमस्ते। मैंने सुबह की कक्षा के लिए पंजीकरण किया था, लेकिन अब मेरे काम का
  समय बदल गया है…**
- **हमारे इलाके का बस-स्टॉप पहले बहुत असुविधाजनक था। यात्रियों ने नगर कार्यालय
  को बताया कि…**

## Speaking

The interview requires relevant reasons. The prepared account must sustain two
minutes with a clear problem–action–result sequence. The role-play must obtain
new time and place, explain a difficulty, compare two alternatives, and confirm
one plan. Score interactional adaptation rather than memorized wording.
