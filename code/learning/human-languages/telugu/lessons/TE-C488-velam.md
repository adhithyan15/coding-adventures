## You'll want to know: వేలం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C488-TOKU488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**వేలం** — *vēlaṁ* — "an auction".

> పాత బస్సులను వేలంలో అమ్మారు.
>
> *pāta bassulanu vēlaṁlō ammāru.*
>
> The old buses were sold at auction.

వేలం వేయు, to auction; వేలంపాట, the bidding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వేలం** (*vēlaṁ*) is usually traced to Portuguese *leilão*, "an auction", from the trade of the Portuguese on the Indian coasts.
