## You'll want to know: まんぷく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C423-GOCHISOU423-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まんぷく** — *manpuku* — "a full stomach".

> **ごちそうを たべて、まんぷくに なりました。** — *gochisō o tabete, manpuku ni narimashita* — "I ate the feast and was full."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まんぷく** (*manpuku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *man*, "full" (Middle Chinese *manX*) and *fuku*, "belly" (*pjuwk*), joined as *manpuku* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
