## You'll want to know: ausmisten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C528-MUELL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ausmisten** — "to clear out".

> Am Wochenende haben wir den Keller ausgemistet.
>
> *At the weekend we cleared out the cellar.*

Separable: *wir misten aus*. It first meant to muck out a stable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ausmisten* is built on *aus* "out" and *misten* "to muck out", from *Mist* "manure", inherited from Old High German *mist* (EtymDB).
