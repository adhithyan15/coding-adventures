## You'll want to know: le chômeur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C356-CHOMAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le chômeur** — "the unemployed person".

> Ce chômeur cherche du travail tous les jours.
>
> *This unemployed man looks for work every day.*

Feminine *chômeuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chômeur* is built from *chômer* "to stop work" and *-eur*. The verb is usually traced to Late Latin *caumāre* "to rest during the heat", from Greek *kaûma* "burning heat".
