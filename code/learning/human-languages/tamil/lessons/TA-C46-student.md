## You'll want to know: மாணவன்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C46-ROLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**மாணவன்** (*māṇavaṉ*) — "a student".

Sanskrit *māṇava*, 'a young person', with the *-aṉ* Tamil puts on a male — the same *-aṉ* as *makaṉ* and *naṇbaṉ*.

Change the ending and the person changes with it: *māṇavi* is a girl studying, *māṇavar* is said with respect or of more than one. One stem, three endings, and you already had all three.

## What you've built

Two: the one who teaches and the one who is taught.
