## You'll want to know: tranquillitās, tranquillitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C444-MALACIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tranquillitās, tranquillitātis** — "calm weather, a calm; stillness".

> Post tempestātem tranquillitās venit.
>
> *After the storm calm weather comes.*

Third declension, feminine: *tranquillitās, tranquillitātis f.* Also calm of mind. English *tranquillity* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tranquillitās* is built on the adjective *tranquillus* 'calm, still' with the suffix *-itās* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *tranquillity* comes from it.
