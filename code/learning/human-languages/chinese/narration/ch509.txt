6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
当家 (dāngjiā) — to run the household.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to pick on (someone weaker), then the Mandarin for sensible (of a child).

You'll want to know: 当家.
当家 — dāngjiā — "to run the household".
我们家是我妈妈当家 wǒmen jiā shì wǒ māma dāngjiā — In our family, it's my mum who runs things.

The word, taken apart.
当家 (dāngjiā) is built from 当 'to be in charge' and 家 'a home', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tang and kae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be in charge' and the second 'a dwelling'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
父母 (fùmǔ) — parents; one's mother and father.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for sensible (of a child), then the Mandarin for to run the household.

You'll want to know: 父母.
父母 — fùmǔ — "parents; one's mother and father".
wǒ gēn fùmǔ zhù le èrshí nián — I lived with my parents for twenty years.
The new character, in pieces: 母 (mǔ) — five strokes: a frame of two, one down the left that turns right along the bottom and one along the top that turns down the right side and hooks; a dot inside the top half; a long bar across; a dot inside the bottom half. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
父母 (fùmǔ) is built from 父 'a father' and 母 (mǔ) 'a mother', both inherited from Middle Chinese, bjuX and muwX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a mother' and, after an older book, reads its two dots as a picture of a nursing mother's breasts.

Script — a frame, two dots and a long bar.
母 (mǔ).
The frame, two strokes. One that runs down the left side and turns right along the bottom. Then one that runs along the top, turns down the right side and hooks to the left at the foot.
A dot inside the top half.
A long bar right across the middle, wider than the frame on both sides.
A dot inside the bottom half.
2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = five.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes does 母 (mǔ) have? (Five.) How many dots? (Two, one above the long bar and one below.) Which stroke is widest? (The long bar across the middle.)

Lesson 4 of 6.
母女 (mǔnǚ) — mother and daughter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to run the household, then the Mandarin for one's mother and father.

You'll want to know: 母女.
母女 — mǔnǚ — "mother and daughter".
母女两个人都喜欢去舞会 mǔnǚ liǎng ge rén dōu xǐhuan qù wǔhuì — Mother and daughter both love going to dances.
Two third tones meet in this word: mǔ is said with a rising tone before nǚ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
母女 (mǔnǚ) is built from 母 (mǔ) 'a mother' and 女 'a daughter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, muwX and nrjoX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

后妈 — hòumā — "stepmother".

后妈 (hòumā) is built from 后 'after' and 妈 'mum', both inherited from Middle Chinese, huwX and muX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'after' and the second 'a mother'. It belongs to everyday speech.
