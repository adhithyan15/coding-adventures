## You'll want to know: causare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C349-CAUSA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**causare** — "to cause".

> Il fumo può causare molte malattie.
>
> *Smoking can cause many illnesses.*

Neutral and frequent; the phrase *a causa di* means because of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*causare* is built on *causa* "cause", a learned borrowing from Latin *causa* "cause, reason, lawsuit" (EtymDB). The everyday word *cosa* "thing" is the same Latin word, inherited and worn down.
