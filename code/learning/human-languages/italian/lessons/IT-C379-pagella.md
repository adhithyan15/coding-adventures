## You'll want to know: la pagella
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C379-LICEO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la pagella** — "the school report".

> Ho preso un bel voto in pagella.
>
> *I got a good mark on my school report.*

Given at the end of each term.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pagella* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pāgella* "a little page" (Lewis & Short), diminutive of *pāgina* "a page".
