## You'll want to know: a sequência
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C273-ETAPA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a sequência** — "the sequence, the series".

> O filme tem uma sequência muito bonita no mar.
>
> *The film has a very beautiful sequence at sea.*

*Em sequência* — one after the other.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sequência* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *sequentia* "a following, a series", from *sequī* "to follow" (Lewis & Short).
