## You'll want to know: أسعد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C498-ATHAR498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أسعد** — *asʿada* — "to make happy".

> أسعدت الهدية جدتي كثيرا.
>
> *asʿadat al-hadiyya jaddatī kathīran.*
>
> The present made my grandmother very happy.

Present: يسعد, *yusʿid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أسعد** (*asʿada*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-a-d*, "to be lucky, to be happy" (Lane). It is Form IV: to make someone saʿīd.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
