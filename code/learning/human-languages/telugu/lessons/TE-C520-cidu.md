# చీదు (cīdu) — to blow one's nose

## You'll want to know: చీదు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C520-KOLUKO520-04]; assesses=[] -->

**చీదు** — *cīdu* — "to blow one's nose".

> జలుబుతో అతను పదే పదే ముక్కు చీదాడు.
>
> *jalubutō atanu padē padē mukku cīdāḍu.*
>
> With his cold he blew his nose again and again.

Past (I …): చీదాను, *cīdānu*.

With ముక్కు, nose: ముక్కు చీదు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చీదు** (*cīdu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2618 lists the Telugu verb with Tamil *cīntu*, "to blow, as the nose".
