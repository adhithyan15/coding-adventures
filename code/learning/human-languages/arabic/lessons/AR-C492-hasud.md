## You'll want to know: حسود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C492-GHIRA492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حسود** — *ḥasūd* — "envious".

> لا أحب الجار الحسود.
>
> *lā uḥibbu al-jār al-ḥasūd.*
>
> I do not like the envious neighbour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حسود** (*ḥasūd*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-s-d*, "to envy" (Lane). Ḥasūd is the pattern of a lasting trait: an envious person.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
