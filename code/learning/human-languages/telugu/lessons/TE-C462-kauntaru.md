# కౌంటరు (kauṇṭaru) — a counter

## You'll want to know: కౌంటరు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C462-KAUNTARU462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కౌంటరు** — *kauṇṭaru* — "a counter".

> టిక్కెట్టు కౌంటరు దగ్గర పెద్ద వరుస ఉంది.
>
> *ṭikkeṭṭu kauṇṭaru daggara pedda varusa undi.*
>
> There is a long queue at the ticket counter.

Any service counter: in a bank, a station, a shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కౌంటరు** (*kauṇṭaru*) is a loan from English *counter*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
