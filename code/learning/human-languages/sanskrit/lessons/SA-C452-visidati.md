## You'll want to know: विषीदति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C452-UDVEGA452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विषीदति** — *viṣīdati* — "loses heart".

> कार्यं कठिनम् अस्ति, किन्तु अरुणः न विषीदति।
>
> *kāryaṃ kaṭhinam asti, kintu aruṇaḥ na viṣīdati.*
>
> The work is hard, but Arun does not lose heart.

Past (he …): सः विषण्णवान्, *saḥ viṣaṇṇavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विषीदति** (*viṣīdati*) is built from *vi* and the root √sad, "to sit, to sink", the verb behind **विषादः**; MW gives "to be dejected, despond, despair" (MW).

## What you've built
