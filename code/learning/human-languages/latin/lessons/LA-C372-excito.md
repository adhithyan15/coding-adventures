## You'll want to know: excitō, excitāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C372-AUCTOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**excitō, excitāre** — "to rouse, to wake; to stir up".

> Clāmor mē ē somnō excitāvit.
>
> *The shouting woke me from sleep.*

*excitō, excitāre, excitāvī, excitātum*. To call out and set moving: a sleeper, a fire, a feeling. English *excite*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*excitō* is the frequentative of *exciō* "to call out, to rouse" (Lewis & Short), from *ex* "out" and *cieō* "to set moving", the verb behind *citō*. English *excite* comes from it.
