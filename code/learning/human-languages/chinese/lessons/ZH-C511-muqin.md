## You'll want to know: 母亲
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C511-KIN511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**母亲** — *mǔqīn* — "mother (the formal word)".

> **我母亲比我父亲大两岁** *wǒ mǔqīn bǐ wǒ fùqīn dà liǎng suì* — My mother is two years older than my father.

Many speakers make the second syllable light: mǔqin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**母亲** (*mǔqīn*) is built from 母 'a mother' and 亲 'close', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *muwX* and *tshin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
