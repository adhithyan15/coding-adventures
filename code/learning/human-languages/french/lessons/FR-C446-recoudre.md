## You'll want to know: recoudre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C446-CORDONNIER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**recoudre** — "to sew back on, to stitch up".

> La dame a recousu le bouton de ma veste.
>
> *The lady sewed the button back on my jacket.*

Irregular: *je recouds, il a recousu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recoudre* is built from *re-* 'again' and *coudre* 'to sew', which goes back to Latin *consuere*, from *suere* 'to sew' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
