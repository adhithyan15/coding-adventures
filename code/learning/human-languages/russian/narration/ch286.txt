Lesson 1 of 6.
транслировать (translírovat) — to broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a summary, then the Russian for the result.

You'll want to know: транслировать.
транслировать (translírovat) — "to broadcast".
Матч транслировали по телевизору. Match translírovali po televízoru. The match was broadcast on television.
One form for both aspects. Трансляция — a broadcast.

The word, taken apart.
транслировать (translírovat) is usually traced to Latin trānslātus "carried across", the participle of trānsferre "to carry over" (Lewis & Short), with the Russian ending -ировать; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 2 of 6.
освещать (osveshchát) — to report on, to cover (in the news); to light up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the result, then the Russian for to broadcast.

You'll want to know: освещать.
освещать (osveshchát) — "to report on, to cover (in the news); to light up".
Газеты широко освещали эту историю. Gazéty shirokó osveshcháli étu istóriyu. The newspapers covered this story widely.
Imperfective; perfective осветить (osvetít), я освещу. First sense: лампа освещает комнату — the lamp lights the room.

The word, taken apart.
освещать (osveshchát) is built on о- and свет "light", an inherited Slavic word (Proto-Slavic světŭ), with -щ-, the Church Slavonic shape -свещ- (native Russian has ч, as in свеча): to throw light on.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for to broadcast, then the Russian for to report on.

You'll want to know: раскрывать.
раскрывать (raskryvát) — "to reveal, to uncover; to solve (a case)".
Журналист раскрыл тайну компании. Zhurnalíst raskrýl táynu kompánii. The journalist uncovered the company's secret.
Imperfective; perfective раскрыть (raskrýt), я раскрою. Раскрыть тайну — to reveal a secret.

The word, taken apart.
раскрывать (raskryvát) is built on рас- "apart" and крыть "to cover", an inherited Slavic verb: to take the cover off. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

Lesson 5 of 6.
тираж (tirázh) — a print run, a circulation; a lottery draw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to reveal, then the Russian for a publication.

You'll want to know: тираж.
тираж (tirázh) — "a print run, a circulation; a lottery draw".
У этой газеты большой тираж. U étoy gazéty bolshóy tirázh. This newspaper has a large circulation.
Masculine; the stress moves to the ending: тиража, тиражи. Выиграть в тираже — to win in a lottery draw.

The word, taken apart.
тираж (tirázh) was borrowed from French tirage "a printing; a drawing of lots" (EtymDB). The French noun is usually traced to tirer "to draw, to pull", as copies are drawn off a press and numbers out of a drum; EtymDB lists no parent for tirage itself.
