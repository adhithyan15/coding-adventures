## You'll want to know: جٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C380-MASHQ380-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جٹنا** — *juṭnā* — "to get down to (a task); to apply oneself".

> اگر سب جٹ گئے تو کام جلدی ختم ہو گا۔
>
> *agar sab juṭ gae to kām jaldī khatm ho gā.*
>
> If everyone gets down to work, it will soon be done.

Past (he …): وہ کام میں جٹ گیا, *voh kām meṅ juṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جٹنا** (*juṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *juṭnā*, "to come together", under *\*yuṭati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10496); the sense "to throw oneself into a task" is a later one our sources do not trace.
