## You'll want to know: چمن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C467-BAGH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چمن** (*chaman*) — "grass, a lawn".

> بچه ها روی چمن فوتبال بازی کردند.
>
> *Bachche-hâ ru-ye chaman futbâl bâzi kardand.*
>
> The children played football on the grass.

Also an old word for a meadow in poetry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chaman* is a native word, which EtymDB lists with the senses 'lawn, meadow'. Its older form is not settled.
