# Review — it depends, and what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C318-AVALAMBISU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C318-DEPENDS-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-175] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to depend" (*avalambisu*) and "It depends on the weather". (*adu havāmānada mēle avalambiside*.)
- [YOU WRITE: **ಛ** and **ಔ**, once each]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C318-AVALAMBISU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C318-DEPENDS-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C318-UNREAL-IF-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C318-WOULD-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C317-ARE-CONDITIONAL-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C317-REAL-CONDITION-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C317-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C317-ONDU-VELE-01, KA-LEX-C215-KARANA215-02, KA-GRAMMAR-C316-FROM-UNTIL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Will you come tomorrow? It depends on the rain. If it rains from morning until evening, I won't come.]
- [YOU SAY: According to the timetable, the bus leaves at six. If you don't come quickly, it will go.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had had time, I would have come.]
- [YOU SAY: If you had come, we would have gone to the cinema together.]
- [YOU SAY: read your six lines aloud to your reader]

## Wrap-up Recall
