## You'll want to know: کیف پول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C453-PASGAH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کیف پول** (*kif-e pul*) — "a wallet, a purse".

> کیف پولم را در اتوبوس گم کردم.
>
> *Kif-e pulam râ dar otobus gom kardam.*
>
> I lost my wallet on the bus.

*kif* 'bag' + *pul* 'money'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kif-e pul* is built from *kif* 'bag', whose origin is not settled, and *pul* 'money', which EtymDB glosses 'change, small money'.
