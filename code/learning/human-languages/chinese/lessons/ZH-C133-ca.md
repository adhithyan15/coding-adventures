## You'll want to know: cā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C133-HOUSE133-04]; assesses=[] -->

*cā* — "to wipe".

> *wǒ cā zhuōzi nǐ cā yǐzi* — I'll wipe the table; you wipe the chairs.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cā* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
