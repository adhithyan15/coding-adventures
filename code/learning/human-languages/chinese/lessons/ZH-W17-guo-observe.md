# 国 — frame, inside, close

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ZHONGGUO-01, ZH-SCRIPT-KOU, ZH-SCRIPT-NUM-SI, ZH-DIALOGUE-POLITE] -->

[PAUSE 14s] Say **China**. Put 请 before a familiar request, then air-write the
opening two strokes of a box without closing it.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GUO-01]; assesses=[] -->

Write **国** in eight strokes. Open the outer frame with its left side, then
top-and-right. Finish every stroke of **玉** inside. Only then add the bottom
bar that closes the frame. Eight strokes; seven lifts.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-GUO-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **国** once. Say **frame, three bars, upright, dot, close**. Keep the
bottom open until the inner dot is finished.
