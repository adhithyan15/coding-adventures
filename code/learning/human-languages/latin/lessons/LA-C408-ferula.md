## You'll want to know: ferula, ferulae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C408-MENDUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ferula, ferulae** — "a cane (for punishing pupils); a fennel stalk".

> Magister ferulam in manū tenēbat.
>
> *The teacher held the cane in his hand.*

*ferula, ferulae* f.: the stalk of the giant fennel, light and stinging, the schoolmaster's cane. English *ferule*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *ferula* is not settled: EtymDB derives it from *feriō* "to strike", but Lewis & Short give no origin for it. English *ferule* comes from it.
