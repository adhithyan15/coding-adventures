## You'll want to know: લિફ્ટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C196-STOREY196-03]; assesses=[] -->

**લિફ્ટ** — *lipht* — "elevator, lift".

> **આ મકાનમાં લિફ્ટ નથી.** — *ā makānmā̃ lipht nathī* — "This building has no lift."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લિફ્ટ** (*lipht*) is a loan from English *lift*.
