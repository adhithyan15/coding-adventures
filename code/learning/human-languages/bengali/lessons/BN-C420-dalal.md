## You'll want to know: দালাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C420-HAT420-03]; assesses=[] -->

**দালাল** — *dālāl* — "a broker; a middleman".

> দালাল বাসার জন্য অগ্রিম চাইল।
>
> *dālāl bāsār jônyô ôgrim chāilô.*
>
> The broker asked for an advance for the flat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দালাল** (*dālāl*) is usually traced to Arabic *dallāl*, "a broker, a guide", through Persian; the word often carries a hint of distrust.
