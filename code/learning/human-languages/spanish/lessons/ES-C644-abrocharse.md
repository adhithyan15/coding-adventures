## You'll want to know: abrocharse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C644-COCHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**abrocharse** — "to fasten (a belt, a button)".

> Abróchense el cinturón, por favor.
>
> *Please fasten your seat belts.*

Without *se*, *abrochar* is to fasten something: *abrochar el abrigo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abrocharse* is built on *broche* "clasp, brooch", which was borrowed from French *broche* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
