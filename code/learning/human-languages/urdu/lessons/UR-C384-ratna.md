## You'll want to know: رٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C384-TARBIYAT384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رٹنا** — *raṭnā* — "to learn by rote; to cram".

> اگر تم صرف رٹو گے تو کچھ نہیں سمجھو گے۔
>
> *agar tum sirf raṭo ge to kuchh nahīṅ samjho ge.*
>
> If you only learn by rote, you will understand nothing.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سبق رٹا, *us ne sabaq raṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رٹنا** (*raṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *raṭnā*, "to cry out, to repeat", under Sanskrit *ráṭati*, "cries out" (CDIAL 10590): saying a thing over and over.
