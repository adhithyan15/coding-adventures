## HL-C333 — Hindi thinking declares its taught present forms

After the speaking-stem repair, **सोचता** is the highest-ranked independent
modern Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 34 already
derives **सोचता**, **सोचती**, and **सोचते** from **सोचना**, models each in a
complete sentence, and asks learners to produce all three in guided practice.

The old metadata declared only infinitive **सोचना** and noun or stem **सोच**,
leaving the explicitly taught present forms invisible to reports and
downstream cards.

Declare the full taught family as the lesson headword, print the Devanagari
sentences in guided practice, and regenerate the derived Hindi artifacts. This
is a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim.
