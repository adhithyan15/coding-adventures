## You'll want to know: ਪਲੰਘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C234-DIN234-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਲੰਘ** — *palaṅgh* — "a wooden bed".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਪਲੰਘ ਖਿੜਕੀ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā palaṅgh khiṛkī de neṛe hai.*
>
> My bed is near the window.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਲੰਘ** (*palaṅgh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *palyaṅka*, "bed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *palaṅgh*, "bed" (CDIAL 7964).
