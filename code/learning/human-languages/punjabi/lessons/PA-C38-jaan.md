# ਜਾਂ (jāṁ) — or

- [YOU RECALL: **ਅਤੇ**]

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-JOIN-JAAN-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-ATE-01, PA-LEX-DUDH] -->

**ਅਤੇ** offers you both. **ਜਾਂ** offers you one of two:

> **ਦੁੱਧ ਜਾਂ ਰੋਟੀ** — *dudh jāṁ roṭī* — **milk or bread**

Same position, different job: it stands between the two things and asks the
listener to choose. The bindi on it is not decoration — the vowel goes through
the nose, and this book has been marking that since its earliest script lessons.

This is the word a shopkeeper uses on you, and the one you need to answer with.
