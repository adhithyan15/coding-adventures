Kannada, chapter 132: Peacock, Butterfly, Ant, Mosquito, Spider.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನವಿಲು (navilu) — a peacock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pigeon, then the Kannada for an owl.

You'll want to know: ನವಿಲು.
ನವಿಲು — navilu — "a peacock".

The word, taken apart.
ನವಿಲು (navilu) is native Dravidian, one word with Telugu nemali (DEDR 2902). DEDR lists Kannada navil(u).
