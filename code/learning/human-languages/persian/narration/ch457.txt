Persian, chapter 457: In the bazaar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
راسته (râste) — a row (of the bazaar given to one trade).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an estate agency, then the Persian for to get in touch.

You'll want to know: راسته.
راسته (râste) — "a row (of the bazaar given to one trade)".
در راسته فرش فروشها قدم زدیم. Dar râste-ye farsh-forush-hâ qadam zadim. We walked along the carpet sellers' row.
Each râste of an old bazaar belongs to one trade: gold, copper, cloth, spices.

The word, taken apart.
râste is a native word, which EtymDB lists; it is built on râst 'straight', usually traced to Middle Persian rāst: a straight line of shops.

Lesson 2 of 5.
حجره (hojre) — a shop or office in the bazaar; a small room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get in touch, then the Persian for a row.

You'll want to know: حجره.
حجره (hojre) — "a shop or office in the bazaar; a small room".
پدربزرگم در بازار یک حجره داشت. Pedarbozorgam dar bâzâr yek hojre dâsht. My grandfather had a shop in the bazaar.
Arabic plural hojarât. Also a student's cell in an old school.

The word, taken apart.
hojre was borrowed from Arabic ḥujra, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'a room, a chamber'.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for a row, then the Persian for a shop or office in the bazaar.

You'll want to know: بازارچه.
بازارچه (bâzârche) — "a small bazaar, a local market".
نزدیک خانه ما یک بازارچه قدیمی هست. Nazdik-e khâne-ye mâ yek bâzârche-ye qadimi hast. There is an old little market near our house.
bâzâr + -che 'little'.

The word, taken apart.
bâzârche is built from bâzâr 'market', inherited from Middle Persian wāzār (EtymDB), and the native ending -che 'little'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تیمچه (timche) — a covered hall in the bazaar, an arcade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a shop or office in the bazaar, then the Persian for a small bazaar.

You'll want to know: تیمچه.
تیمچه (timche) — "a covered hall in the bazaar, an arcade".
فرشها را در تیمچه میفروشند. Farsh-hâ râ dar timche miforushand. They sell the carpets in the covered hall.
A timche is often domed and finely built, a hall for wholesale trade.

The word, taken apart.
timche is built from tim 'a trading hall, a caravanserai' and the ending -che 'little'. The origin of tim is not settled, and EtymDB lists neither word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
کاروانسرا (kârvânsarâ) — a caravanserai, a roadside inn for caravans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a small bazaar, then the Persian for a covered hall in the bazaar.

You'll want to know: کاروانسرا.
کاروانسرا (kârvânsarâ) — "a caravanserai, a roadside inn for caravans".
مسافران قدیم شب را در کاروانسرا میگذراندند. Mosâferân-e qadim shab râ dar kârvânsarâ migozarândand. Travellers in the old days spent the night in a caravanserai.
kârvân 'caravan' + sarâ 'house'. English caravanserai comes from the Persian word.

The word, taken apart.
kârvânsarâ is a native compound of kârvân 'caravan', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian kārawān 'caravan; an army on the march', and sarâ 'house, palace'. EtymDB gives the Persian word as the source of English caravanserai.
