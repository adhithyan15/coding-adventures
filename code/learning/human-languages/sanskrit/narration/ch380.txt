Sanskrit, chapter 380: How Much.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मात्रा (mātrā) — a quantity; a measure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for ample, then the Sanskrit for increases (something).

You'll want to know: मात्रा.
मात्रा — mātrā — "a quantity; a measure".
यदि औषधस्य मात्रा अधिका, तर्हि हानिः भविष्यति। yadi auṣadhasya mātrā adhikā, tarhi hāniḥ bhaviṣyati. If the dose of medicine is too large, it will do harm.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मात्रा (mātrā) comes from the root √mā, "to measure"; MW gives mātrā "measure, quantity, size, duration, degree" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
परिमाणम् (parimāṇam) — an amount; a size.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for increases (something), then the Sanskrit for a quantity.

You'll want to know: परिमाणम्.
परिमाणम् — parimāṇam — "an amount; a size".
यदि जलस्य परिमाणं न्यूनम्, तर्हि वृक्षाः शुष्यन्ति। yadi jalasya parimāṇaṃ nyūnam, tarhi vṛkṣāḥ śuṣyanti. If the amount of water is low, the trees dry up.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
परिमाणम् (parimāṇam) is built on pari, "around", and the root √mā, "to measure"; MW gives pari-māṇa "measure of any kind, size, weight, number" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
यथेष्टम् (yatheṣṭam) — as much as one likes.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an amount, then the Sanskrit for moderate.

You'll want to know: यथेष्टम्.
यथेष्टम् — yatheṣṭam — "as much as one likes".
यदि भवान् क्षुधितः, तर्हि यथेष्टं खादतु। yadi bhavān kṣudhitaḥ, tarhi yatheṣṭaṃ khādatu. If you are hungry, eat as much as you like.

The word, taken apart.
यथेष्टम् (yatheṣṭam) is built from yathā, "as", and iṣṭa, "wished"; MW gives yatheṣṭam "according to wish, at pleasure" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Past (he …): सः व्यापारं विस्तारितवान्, saḥ vyāpāraṃ vistāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
विस्तारयति (vistārayati) is built on vi and the root √stṛ, "to spread", in its causative form; MW gives vi-stārita "spread, extended" (MW).
