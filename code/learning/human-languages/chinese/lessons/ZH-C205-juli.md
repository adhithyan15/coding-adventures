## You'll want to know: jùlí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C205-FAR205-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jùlí* — "distance".

> *liǎng ge chēzhàn de jùlí shì yì gōnglǐ* — The two stations are a kilometre apart.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jùlí* is built from *jù* and 离 'away from', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjoX* and *lje* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the spur on a cock's leg; how it came to mean being apart is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
