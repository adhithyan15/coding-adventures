## You'll want to know: носилки
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C299-SIRENA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**носилки** (*nosílki*) — "a stretcher".

> Раненого несли на носилках.
>
> *Ránenovo neslí na nosílkakh.*
>
> The wounded man was carried on a stretcher.

Plural only: *носилки*, genitive *носилок*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*носилки* is built on *носить* "to carry", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *nositi*, from PIE *h₁neḱ-* "to carry" (EtymDB).
