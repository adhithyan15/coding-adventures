## You'll want to know: دسترسی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C417-SHABAKE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دسترسی** (*dastresi*) — "access".

> در روستا دسترسی به اینترنت کم است.
>
> *dar rustâ dastresi be internet kam ast.*
>
> In the village there is little access to the internet.

*Dast* "hand" + *res-*, from *residan* "to reach", + *-i*. *Dastresi dâshtan be* — to have access to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دسترسی** is native Persian, built from *dast* "hand" + *res-*, the present stem of *residan* "to reach, to arrive", + *-i*. Access is what your hand can reach.
