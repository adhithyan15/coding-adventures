## You'll want to know: ānquán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C209-RULE209-02]; assesses=[] -->

*ānquán* — "safe; safety".

> *wǎnshang yí ge rén zǒulù bù ānquán* — It isn't safe to walk on your own at night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ānquán* is built from *ān* 'calm' and *quán* 'whole', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'an* and *dzjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
