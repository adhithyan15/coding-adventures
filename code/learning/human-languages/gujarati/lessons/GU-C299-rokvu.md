# રોકવું (rokvũ) — to bring to a stop

## You'll want to know: રોકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C299-BUM299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રોકવું** — *rokvũ* — "to bring to a stop".

> સિપાઈએ ગાડી રોકી.
>
> *sipāīe gāḍī rokī.*
>
> The constable stopped the car.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગાડી રોકી, *teṇe gāḍī rokī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રોકવું** (*rokvũ*) comes from *\*rōkk*, "stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 10827). It is the doing partner of **રોકાવું**, to stay on: **સિપાઈએ ગાડી રોકી**.
