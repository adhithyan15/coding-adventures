## You'll want to know: மிதவை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C552-PATHUKAPPU552-03]; assesses=[] -->

**மிதவை** — *mitavai* — "a buoy; a float".

> நீச்சல் தெரியாவிட்டால் மிதவையைப் பிடித்துக்கொள்ள வேண்டும்.
>
> *nīccal teriyāviṭṭāl mitavaiyaip piṭittukkoḷḷa vēṇṭum.*
>
> If you can't swim, you must hold on to the float.

From மித (*mita*), to float: the thing that floats.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மிதவை** (*mitavai*) is built on the native மித (*mita*), "to float"; the formation with -வை is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
