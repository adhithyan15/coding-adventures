Arabic, chapter 453: Paying up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قسط (qisṭ) — an instalment; a share.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to change money, then the Arabic for to deposit.

You'll want to know: قسط.
قسط — qisṭ — "an instalment; a share".
دفع أبي قسط السيارة هذا الشهر. dafaʿa abī qisṭ as-sayyāra hādhā ash-shahr. My father paid the car instalment this month.
Plural: أقساط, aqsāṭ.

The word, taken apart.
قسط (qisṭ) is built on the native Arabic root q-s-ṭ, "a fair share, a portion" (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
نقدا (naqdan) — in cash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to deposit, then the Arabic for an instalment.

You'll want to know: نقدا.
نقدا — naqdan — "in cash".
دفعت ثمن التذكرة نقدا. dafaʿtu thaman at-tadhkira naqdan. I paid for the ticket in cash.
The noun naqd is cash; the ending -an makes the adverb.

The word, taken apart.
نقدا (naqdan) is built on the native Arabic root n-q-d, "to pick out good coins" (Lane). Naqd is ready money.

Lesson 3 of 5.
صراف آلي (ṣarrāf ālī) — a cash machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an instalment, then the Arabic for in cash.

You'll want to know: صراف آلي.
صراف آلي — ṣarrāf ālī — "a cash machine".
سحبت مالا من الصراف الآلي. saḥabtu mālan min aṣ-ṣarrāf al-ālī. I took money out of the cash machine.

The word, taken apart.
صراف آلي (ṣarrāf ālī) is a modern coinage built from two native Arabic words (Lane gives both roots): ṣarrāf, "a money changer", on ṣ-r-f, and ālī, "mechanical", from āla, "a machine", on ʾ-w-l.

Lesson 4 of 5.
استرد (istaradda) — to get back (money); to recover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for in cash, then the Arabic for a cash machine.

You'll want to know: استرد.
استرد — istaradda — "to get back (money); to recover".
استرددت ثمن التذكرة. istaradadtu thaman at-tadhkira. I got the price of the ticket back.
Present: يسترد, yastaridd.
A doubled verb: istaradda, "he got back", but istaradadtu, "I got back".

The word, taken apart.
استرد (istaradda) is built on the native Arabic root r-d-d, "to send back, to return" (Lane). It is Form X, "to ask for back".

Lesson 5 of 5.
عداد (ʿaddād) — a meter (that counts).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a cash machine, then the Arabic for to get back.

You'll want to know: عداد.
عداد — ʿaddād — "a meter (that counts)".
كان العداد في التاكسي معطلا. kāna al-ʿaddād fī at-tāksī muʿaṭṭalan. The meter in the taxi was out of order.

The word, taken apart.
عداد (ʿaddād) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-d-d, "to count" (Lane). The pattern faʿʿāl names a thing that does a job: the counter.
