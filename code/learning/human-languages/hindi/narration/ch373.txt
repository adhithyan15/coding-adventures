Hindi, chapter 373: Is It Possible?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मुमकिन (mumkin) — possible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a sin, then the Hindi for to hold on to.

You'll want to know: मुमकिन.
मुमकिन — mumkin — "possible".
अगर हम जल्दी निकलेंगे तो दोपहर तक पहुँचना मुमकिन है। agar ham jaldī nikleṅge to dopahar tak pahũcnā mumkin hai. If we leave early, we can get there by noon.

The word, taken apart.
मुमकिन (mumkin) is usually traced to Arabic mumkin, "possible", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
संभव (sambhav) — possible (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to hold on to, then the Hindi for possible.

You'll want to know: संभव.
संभव — sambhav — "possible (formal)".
अगर सब मदद करेंगे तो यह काम संभव है। agar sab madad kareṅge to yah kām sambhav hai. If everyone helps, this job can be done.

The word, taken apart.
संभव (sambhav) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sambhava, "coming into being" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it as an adjective: what can come about.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for possible (formal), then the Hindi for a likelihood.

You'll want to know: चूकना.
चूकना — cūknā — "to miss (a chance, a mark)".
अगर तुम तेज़ नहीं दौड़ोगे तो गेंद पकड़ने से चूक जाओगे। agar tum tez nahīṃ dauṛoge to gẽd pakaṛne se cūk jāoge. If you don't run fast, you'll fail to catch the ball.
Past (he …): वह चूक गया, vah cūk gayā.

The word, taken apart.
चूकना (cūknā) is inherited from cukk, "fall short", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4848).
