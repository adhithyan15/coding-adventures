## You'll want to know: грабить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C289-OGRABLENIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**грабить** (*grábit*) — "to rob".

> Они грабили магазины в центре.
>
> *Oní grábili magazíny v tséntre.*
>
> They robbed shops in the centre.

Imperfective; perfective *ограбить* (*ográbit*): *его ограбили* — he was robbed. Present *граблю*, *грабишь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*грабить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *grabiti* (EtymDB), usually glossed in its old sense "to grab, to rake together".
