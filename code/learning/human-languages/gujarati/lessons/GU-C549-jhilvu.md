## You'll want to know: ઝીલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C549-GOAL549-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝીલવું** — *jhīlvũ* — "to catch a ball".

> જો તું દડો ઝીલત તો આપણે જીતી જાત.
>
> *jo tũ daḍo jhīlat to āpaṇe jītī jāt.*
>
> If you had caught the ball, we would have won.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દડો ઝીલ્યો, *teṇe daḍo jhīlyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝીલવું** (*jhīlvũ*) is to catch something thrown or falling. CDIAL lists Marathi *jhelṇẽ*, "to catch", and Hindi *jhelnā*, "to undergo; to take", under a reconstructed *\*jhēlati*, "undergoes" (CDIAL 5413); it gives no Gujarati form, so its history is not settled.
