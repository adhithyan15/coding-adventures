## You'll want to know: うらぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C519-GIVEUP519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うらぎる** — *uragiru* — "to betray; to let down".

> **もし かれが ともだちを うらぎったら、ともだちは おこるでしょう。** — *moshi kare ga tomodachi o uragittara, tomodachi wa okoru deshō* — "If he betrays his friends, they will be angry."

Polite **うらぎります**, *uragirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かれが ともだちを うらぎりました。**, *kare ga tomodachi o uragirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うらぎる** (*uragiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
