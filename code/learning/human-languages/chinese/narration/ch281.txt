Lesson 1 of 8.
比如 (bǐrú) — for instance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
A new chapter, and it opens on the word that brings in an example.
[your turn — recall: say 我觉得这个意见很好 — wǒ juéde zhège yìjiàn hěn hǎo — R1, the chapter you have finished]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 这么 — zhème, so — R1]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 比 — bǐ, than — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 比如.
比如 — bǐrú — "for instance; such as".
我喜欢中国菜 比如白菜 wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài, bǐrú báicài — I like Chinese food, cabbage for one.
我喜欢喝茶 比如中国茶 wǒ xǐhuan hē chá, bǐrú Zhōngguó chá — I like tea, Chinese tea for instance.
The next lesson puts the second character in your hand. The first is the 比 you write.

The word, taken apart.
比如 (bǐrú) is built from 比 'to compare' and 如 (rú) 'like', both inherited from Middle Chinese. The Guangyun lists 比 under five readings, of which pjijX is glossed 'to compare', and 如 (rú) under two, nyo and nyoH; it glosses nyo with several words, among them 'to resemble' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). 'For instance' for the pair is the word's own sense.

What you've built.
比如 (bǐrú) comes after the general point and before the example: 中国菜, then 比如白菜.
It is the 比 of comparing, 比 as in 我比他大, with an example set beside the point.

[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — hǎo, mā, kǒu]

Script — 女, then 口.
如 (rú).
Two pieces, and you write both already.
女, three strokes, narrow, on the left, as in 好 and 妈: down to the left and turning down to the right without lifting; a left-falling stroke from the upper right; and a horizontal that rises as it crosses.
口, three strokes, on the right: the left side, the top that turns down the right side, the bottom bar.
3 + 3 = six.

Lesson 3 of 8.
因为…所以… (yīnwèi … suǒyǐ …) — because, so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Two words you have had for a long time, joined into one frame.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 比如, aloud — bǐrú]
[your turn — recall: say 因为 — yīnwèi, because — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say suǒyǐ, so — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 为什么 — wèishénme, why — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens: 因为 … 所以 …, a reason and what follows.
因为 opens the reason, 所以 opens what follows from it, and together they make one sentence.
因为下雨了 所以我们没去 yīnwèi xiàyǔ le, suǒyǐ wǒmen méi qù — Because it rained, we didn't go. 因为他病了 所以没来上课 yīnwèi tā bìng le, suǒyǐ méi lái shàngkè — He was ill, so he didn't come to class.
You have had 因为 and suǒyǐ as separate words; now they frame one sentence, and the next lesson puts 所 (suǒ) in your hand so the frame prints whole.
English keeps one of the two words; Mandarin is happy with both.
To answer 为什么, 因为 alone is enough: 为什么没去 — 因为下雨了 wèishénme méi qù — yīnwèi xiàyǔ le.

[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — fáng, yǐ]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — two four-stroke sides.
所 (suǒ).
Two sides of four strokes each, and both are new in this shape.
The left side, four strokes: a short, flat left-falling stroke at the top; a long left-falling stroke down the left side; a horizontal that turns down at its right end; and a horizontal under it. It looks like the top of 房, but 房 opens with a dot, and this opens with a short left-falling stroke.
The right side, four strokes: a flat left-falling stroke at the top; a long left-falling stroke that drops down and then curves to the lower left; a horizontal across it; and a long vertical down to the foot.
4 + 4 = eight.

Guided Practice.

[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 所 (suǒ)? (Eight.) How does the left side begin? (With a short left-falling stroke, not the dot of 房.) Which stroke comes last? (The long vertical on the right.)

Lesson 5 of 8.
yīncǐ — for this reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: the character you wrote last, and the word it sits in.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 所以, aloud — suǒyǐ]
[your turn — recall: say 因为下雨了 所以我们没去 — yīnwèi xiàyǔ le, suǒyǐ wǒmen méi qù — R1, two lessons back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: yīncǐ.
yīncǐ — "for this reason; and so".
tiānqì bù hǎo, yīncǐ wǒmen méi qù — The weather was bad, and so we didn't go.
Its second character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yīncǐ is built from 因 and cǐ 'this', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jin and tshjeX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to rely on' and 'a cause'; 'for this reason' for the pair is the word's own sense.

What you've built.
suǒyǐ is the everyday so. yīncǐ belongs to writing and careful speech, and it usually stands on its own, with no 因为 in front.
It opens the result, after the reason: tiānqì bù hǎo, yīncǐ wǒmen méi qù.

You'll want to know: lìzi.
lìzi — "an example".
qǐng shuō yí ge lìzi — Please give an example.
Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
lìzi is built from lì 'a precedent' and the ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, ljejH and tsiX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to compare' and 'all of a kind'.

What you've built.
比如 (bǐrú) brings an example in; lìzi is the example itself, a noun you can count: yí ge lìzi.
So a teacher asks qǐng shuō yí ge lìzi, and you answer with 比如 (bǐrú) ….

The exchange.
A: 你为什么学汉语 nǐ wèishénme xué Hànyǔ — Why are you learning Chinese? B: 因为我喜欢中国 所以学汉语 yīnwèi wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó, suǒyǐ xué Hànyǔ — Because I like China, so I'm learning Chinese. A: 你喜欢中国的什么 nǐ xǐhuan Zhōngguó de shénme — What do you like about China? B: 中国菜 中国茶 Zhōngguó cài, Zhōngguó chá — Chinese food, Chinese tea. A: nǐ néng shuō yí ge lìzi ma — Can you give an example? B: 比如白菜 我很喜欢 bǐrú báicài, wǒ hěn xǐhuan — Cabbage, for one. I love it. A: wǒ yě shì, yīncǐ wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó — Me too, and so I want to go to China.
因为 … 所以 … gives the reason and the result in one breath; 比如 (bǐrú) then backs it up. The line with yīncǐ sounds a little careful, as that word does.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]

Why is B learning Chinese?

tiānqì bù hǎo, yīncǐ wǒmen méi qù — the careful version.
中国菜 比如白菜 Zhōngguó cài, bǐrú báicài — an example brought in.
qǐng shuō yí ge lìzi — asking for one.
Listen. Nǐ wèishénme xué Hànyǔ?
