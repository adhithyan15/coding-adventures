## You'll want to know: ملکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C350-PADESHAH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ملکه** (*malake*) — "a queen".

> ملکه یک دختر زیبا داشت.
>
> *Malake yek dokhtar-e zibâ dâsht.*
>
> The queen had a beautiful daughter.

From the Arabic feminine of *malek* "king".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*malake* is borrowed from Arabic *malika*, "queen" (EtymDB).
