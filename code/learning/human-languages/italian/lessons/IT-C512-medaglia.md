## You'll want to know: la medaglia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C512-TORNEO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la medaglia** — "medal".

> Ha vinto la medaglia d'oro.
>
> *She won the gold medal.*

*D'oro*, *d'argento*, *di bronzo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*medaglia* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *metallea* "of metal" (EtymDB lists *medalia*), from Latin *metallum* "metal".
