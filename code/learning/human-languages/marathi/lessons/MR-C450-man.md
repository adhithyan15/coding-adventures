## You'll want to know: मन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C450-MAN450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मन** — *man* — "the mind; the heart, as the seat of feeling".

> आज माझं मन शांत आहे.
>
> *āj mājhaṁ man śānt āhe.*
>
> My mind is calm today.

Neuter.

Neuter: माझं मन. मनात, in the mind; मनापासून, from the heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मन** (*man*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *manas*, "mind" (CDIAL 9822); Marathi keeps the stem and drops the final *-s*.
