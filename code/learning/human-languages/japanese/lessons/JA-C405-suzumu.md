## You'll want to know: すずむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C405-TEIEN405-05]; assesses=[] -->

**すずむ** — *suzumu* — "to cool off (in the shade or a breeze)".

> **ていえんの えんがわで すずみました。** — *teien no engawa de suzumimashita* — "I cooled off on the veranda by the garden."

Polite **すずみます**, *suzumimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おじいさんが えんがわで すずみました。**, *ojiisan ga engawa de suzumimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すずむ** (*suzumu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
