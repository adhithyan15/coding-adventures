## You'll want to know: स्वर्गः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C338-SVARGA338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वर्गः** — *svargaḥ* — "heaven".

> पुण्येन जनः स्वर्गं गच्छति।
>
> *puṇyena janaḥ svargaṃ gacchati.*
>
> Through merit a person goes to heaven.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वर्गः** (*svargaḥ*) is built from *svar*, "light, the sky", and *ga*, "going", from the root √gam; MW gives *svarga* as leading to heaven and, as a noun, heaven (MW).
