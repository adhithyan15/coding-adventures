## You'll want to know: વ્યવસાય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C501-KARIGAR501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યવસાય** — *vyavsāy* — "a profession".

> મારા કાકાનો વ્યવસાય સુથારનો છે.
>
> *mārā kākāno vyavsāy suthārno che.*
>
> My uncle is a carpenter by profession.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યવસાય** (*vyavsāy*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vyavasāya*, "strenuous effort; resolve", which Monier-Williams also gives as "business, trade" (Monier-Williams).
