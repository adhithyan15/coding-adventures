Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 11: Grandparents and Family: Two New Signs.
12 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 12.
Hear dādā — paternal grandfather.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Recall the four-word family checkpoint, say the known word for name, then write familiar ठ once.

You'll want to know.
dādā — paternal grandfather.
Hear the same long beat twice: dā-dā. Say it once. This chapter follows the source's dādā form; other documented Marwari materials also record dādo. That variation is real and is not an error to erase. Writing waits one step.

Guided Practice.
Choose among brother, sister, and paternal grandfather from three heard words. Repeat only dādā.

Wrap-up Recall.
Say dā-dā once, then stop.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4 and Marwari Pathshala, D glossary.

Lesson 3 of 12.
दादा (dādā) — the repeated word.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Ask and answer wellbeing once. Write द (da) and familiar ा, then say sister from memory.

You'll want to know.
दा + दा becomes दादा — dādā — paternal grandfather.
The spelling repeats exactly what the ear heard: two matching long beats.

Guided Practice.
Look at दादा (dādā), cover it, wait five seconds, and write it once. Point to both copies of ा before reading the whole word.

Wrap-up Recall.
Read दादा (dādā) once, then stop.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4 and Marwari Pathshala, D glossary.

Lesson 5 of 12.
दादी (dādī) — change the final vowel.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Recall the four-word family checkpoint, write छ, then write दा and दी. Say sister once.

You'll want to know.
दा + दी becomes दादी — dādī — paternal grandmother.
Compare दादा (dādā) and दादी (dādī). Only the final vowel sign changes.

Guided Practice.
Hear dādā or dādī. Write the matching word without a model, then check the final vowel sign.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 6 of 12.
Hear parivār — family.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]

You'll want to know.
parivār — family.
Hear three small groups: pa-ri-vār. Say them smoothly once. The word names a family group; it does not require every family to have the same members.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 8 of 12.
परिवार (parivār) — family on the page.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Write प, रि, व (va), and ार as four small units. Write paternal grandfather and ल, then say paternal grandmother.

You'll want to know.
प + रि + वा + र becomes परिवार — parivār — family.
Remember that ि is written before र but read after it: ri.

Guided Practice.
Look at परिवार (parivār), cover it, wait eight seconds, and write the four chunks. Uncover and check रि and व (va) separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Read परिवार (parivār) once without romanization.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
From parivār, write the taught word and give its group meaning.

Lesson 11 of 12.
Payoff — seven family labels in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Read the seven-word line once. Cover it before the scored pass.

You'll want to know.
मां — बाप — भाई — बहन — दादा — दादी — परिवार (mā̃ — bāp — bhāī — bahan — dādā — dādī — parivār).
These are labels for understanding and describing relationships, not a required family structure. Regional and household choices remain valid when documented.

Guided Practice.
Listen: identify four randomly heard labels.
Speak: produce four different labels from relation cues.
Read: match all seven printed words to meanings.
Write: write three heard labels without a model.
Score all four skills separately. A perfect spoken map does not replace missing reading or writing.

Wrap-up Recall.
Stop after one independently accurate pass.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 4, Marwari Pathshala, D glossary, and SIL International, Marwari–English Dictionary archive.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
