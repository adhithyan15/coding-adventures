## You'll want to know: द्वीपकल्प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C410-KHADI410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वीपकल्प** — *dvīpkalpa* — "a peninsula".

> द्वीपकल्पाच्या तीन बाजूंना समुद्र असतो.
>
> *dvīpkalpācyā tīn bājūnnā samudra asto.*
>
> A peninsula has sea on three sides.

Masculine.

द्वीप is an island and -कल्प means almost: almost an island.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वीपकल्प** (*dvīpkalpa*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *dvīpa*, "an island", and *kalpa*, "like, almost" (Monier-Williams): land that is almost an island.
