## You'll want to know: 河
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C442-RIVER442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**河** — *hé* — "river".

> *cūnzi qiánbian yǒu yì tiáo hé* — There's a river in front of the village.

The new character, in pieces: 河 — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left the water piece of 洗, three strokes; on the right 可 kě, five strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**河** (*hé*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *ha* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), where it is the name of one river, the one said to rise in the far west and run to the sea; the sense 'any river' is usually explained as a widening of that name.
