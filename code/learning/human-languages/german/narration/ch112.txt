German, chapter 112: Rug, Television, Computer, Fridge, Cooker.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Teppich — a rug, a carpet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a lift, then the German for to fly.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read der Flughafen, das Hotel, das Kino aloud as one line, without stopping between the words.

You'll want to know: der Teppich.
der Teppich — "a rug, a carpet".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: der Teppich]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: der Teppich, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say der Teppich]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a lift, then the German for to fly, then say der Teppich again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does der Teppich mean? ("A rug, a carpet".) Say it once more.
