## You'll want to know: தொகுப்பாளர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C305-NERALAI305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தொகுப்பாளர்** — *tokuppāḷar* — "a presenter; a host".

> தொகுப்பாளர் நிருபரிடம் ஒரு கேள்வி கேட்டார்.
>
> *tokuppāḷar nirupariṭam oru kēḷvi kēṭṭār.*
>
> The presenter asked the reporter a question.

-ஆளர் marks one who does a job: the one who puts a programme together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தொகுப்பாளர்** (*tokuppāḷar*) is built from தொகுப்பு, "a putting together", from native தொகு, "to collect, assemble" (DEDR 3476), with -ஆளர், "one who".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
