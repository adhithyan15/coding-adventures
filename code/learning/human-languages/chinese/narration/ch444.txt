Mandarin Chinese, chapter 444: An Island.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
qiánshuǐ — to go diving; to dive under water.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to escort someone, then the Mandarin for to catch fish.

You'll want to know: qiánshuǐ.
qiánshuǐ — "to go diving; to dive under water".
xiàtiān wǒmen qù hǎibiān qiánshuǐ — In summer we go diving at the seaside.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qiánshuǐ is built from qián 'to go under' and 水 'water', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzjem and sywijX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as water flowing hidden and 'to hide'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
岛 (dǎo) — island.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to catch fish, then the Mandarin for to dive under water.

You'll want to know: 岛.
岛 — dǎo — "island".
我们坐船去那个岛 wǒmen zuò chuán qù nàge dǎo — We're taking a boat to that island.
The new character, in pieces: 岛 (dǎo) — seven strokes in two pieces: on top a short stroke falling to the left, a small bar that turns down and hooks, a dot inside it, and a long stroke that runs down, turns right, turns down again and hooks; tucked under it 山 shān, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
岛 (dǎo) is inherited from Middle Chinese tawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it, after an older dictionary, as the hills here and there in the sea where one can stop and rest.

What you've built.

Script — a top piece of four strokes, then 山.
岛 (dǎo).
Two pieces, top before bottom. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The top piece, four strokes. A short stroke falling to the left. A small bar that turns down and hooks. A dot inside it. Then a long stroke that runs down the left, turns right along the foot, turns down again and ends in a hook.
The bottom piece, 山 shān, three strokes, tucked into the corner: the middle vertical first, then the left one, which turns right along the foot, then the right one.
4 + 3 = seven.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 岛 (dǎo), all seven, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 岛 (dǎo)? (Seven.) Which written character sits at the bottom? (山.) Which stroke of 山 comes first? (The middle vertical.)

这个半岛有很多酒店 zhège bàndǎo yǒu hěn duō jiǔdiàn — There are lots of hotels on this peninsula.
