## You'll want to know: gültig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C384-GENEHMIGUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**gültig** — "valid".

> Die Fahrkarte ist nur heute gültig.
>
> *The ticket is only valid today.*

The opposite is *ungültig* — invalid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gültig* is inherited from Middle High German *gültic* (EtymDB), from *gülte* "income, payment", of the family of *gelten* "to be worth", Old High German *geltan* "to pay".
