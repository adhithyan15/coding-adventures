## You'll want to know: ઉપક્રમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C388-UPAKRAM388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપક્રમ** — *upakram* — "an initiative".

> જો આ ઉપક્રમ સફળ થશે તો બીજાં ગામો પણ તે અપનાવશે.
>
> *jo ā upakram saphaḷ thaśe to bījā̃ gāmo paṇ te apnāvśe.*
>
> If this initiative succeeds, other villages will take it up too.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપક્રમ** (*upakram*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *upakrama*, "approach, beginning, undertaking" (Monier-Williams).
