## You'll want to know: enfeitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C322-FOGUETE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**enfeitar** — "to decorate, to adorn".

> As crianças enfeitaram a sala com balões.
>
> *The children decorated the room with balloons.*

*Enfeitar-se* — to dress up, to make oneself pretty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enfeitar* is usually traced to *en-* and *feito* "made", the past participle of *fazer*, from Latin *factus* (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has the word with no source.
