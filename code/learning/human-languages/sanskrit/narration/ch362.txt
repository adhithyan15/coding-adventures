Sanskrit, chapter 362: Research.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अनुसन्धानम् (anusandhānam) — research; investigation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for ungrateful, then the Sanskrit for gives one's word.

You'll want to know: अनुसन्धानम्.
अनुसन्धानम् — anusandhānam — "research; investigation".
वैज्ञानिकः रोगस्य अनुसन्धानं करोति। vaijñānikaḥ rogasya anusandhānaṃ karoti. The scientist is doing research on the disease.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अनुसन्धानम् (anusandhānam) is built on anu, sam and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives anu-saṃdhāna "investigation, inquiry, searching into, close inspection" (MW).

अन्वेषणम् — anveṣaṇam — "an inquiry; a search".
आरक्षकः चौरस्य अन्वेषणं करोति। ārakṣakaḥ caurasya anveṣaṇaṃ karoti. The policeman searches for the thief.

The word, taken apart.
अन्वेषणम् (anveṣaṇam) is built on anu, "after", and the root √iṣ, "to seek"; MW gives anv-√iṣ "to seek, to search, to aim at", and anv-eṣaṇa the seeking itself (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अवेक्षते (avekṣate) — inspects; looks into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an experiment, then the Sanskrit for an inquiry.

You'll want to know: अवेक्षते.
अवेक्षते — avekṣate — "inspects; looks into".
अधिकारी कार्यालयम् अवेक्षते। adhikārī kāryālayam avekṣate. The official inspects the office.
Past (he …): सः कार्यम् अवेक्षितवान्, saḥ kāryam avekṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अवेक्षते (avekṣate) is built on ava, "down", and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives avekṣ (ava with √īkṣ) "to perceive, observe" and "to take into consideration"; KRM gives īkṣita, īkṣitavat for the root, so avekṣita (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उद्भावयति (udbhāvayati) — devises; brings into being.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an inquiry, then the Sanskrit for inspects.

You'll want to know: उद्भावयति.
उद्भावयति — udbhāvayati — "devises; brings into being".
वैज्ञानिकः नूतनं यन्त्रम् उद्भावयति। vaijñānikaḥ nūtanaṃ yantram udbhāvayati. The scientist devises a new machine.
Past (he …): सः नूतनम् उपायम् उद्भावितवान्, saḥ nūtanam upāyam udbhāvitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उद्भावयति (udbhāvayati) is built on ut, "up", and the root √bhū, "to be", in its causative form, "to cause to exist"; MW gives ud-bhāvita "caused to exist, created, produced" (MW).
