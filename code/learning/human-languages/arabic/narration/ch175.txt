Arabic, chapter 175: Friendship, Society, Hospitality, Despite, Also.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صداقة (ṣadāqa) — friendship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for so, then the Arabic for so that.

You'll want to know: صداقة.
صداقة — ṣadāqa — "friendship".

The word, taken apart.
صداقة (ṣadāqa) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-d-q, "to be truthful, sincere" (Lane). Ṣadāqa is friendship, sincere affection.

Lesson 4 of 5.
رغم (raghma) — despite, in spite of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for society, then the Arabic for hospitality.

You'll want to know: رغم.
رغم — raghma — "despite, in spite of".

The word, taken apart.
رغم (raghma) is built on the native Arabic root r-gh-m, "dust; to be abased" (Lane). Lane's raghm is the nose cleaving to the dust, abasement; raghma, "despite", grew from it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
أيضا (ayḍan) — also, too.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for hospitality, then the Arabic for despite.

You'll want to know: أيضا.
أيضا — ayḍan — "also, too".

The word, taken apart.
أيضا (ayḍan) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-y-ḍ, "to return" (Lane). Ayḍan is ayḍ, "returning", with the adverb ending -an: "once more", hence "also".
