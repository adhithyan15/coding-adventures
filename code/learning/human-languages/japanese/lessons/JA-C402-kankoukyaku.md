## You'll want to know: かんこうきゃく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C402-KANKOU402-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かんこうきゃく** — *kankōkyaku* — "a tourist".

> **なつは かんこうきゃくが おおいです。** — *natsu wa kankōkyaku ga ōi desu* — "There are a lot of tourists in summer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんこうきゃく** (*kankōkyaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "view" (Middle Chinese *kwan*), *kō*, "light" (*kwang*) and *kyaku*, "guest" (*khaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
