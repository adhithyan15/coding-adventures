Hindi, chapter 508: Looking for a Job.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
इंटरव्यू (iṇṭarvyū) — an interview (for a job or a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for skill, then the Hindi for talent.

You'll want to know: इंटरव्यू.
इंटरव्यू — iṇṭarvyū — "an interview (for a job or a place)".
कल सुबह दस बजे मेरा इंटरव्यू है। Kal subah das baje merā iṇṭarvyū hai. My interview is at ten tomorrow morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
इंटरव्यू (iṇṭarvyū) is a loan from English interview.

Lesson 3 of 5.
आवेदन (āvedan) — an application (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an interview (for a job or a place), then the Hindi for an appointment (to a post).

You'll want to know: आवेदन.
आवेदन — āvedan — "an application (formal)".
मैंने नौकरी के लिए आवेदन भेजा। Maiṁne naukrī ke liye āvedan bhejā. I sent an application for the job.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आवेदन (āvedan) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit āvedana, "announcing, informing" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for a written request, an application.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an appointment (to a post), then the Hindi for an application (formal).

You'll want to know: दाख़िला.
दाख़िला — dākhilā — "admission (to a school or course)".
मेरे भाई को बड़े स्कूल में दाख़िला मिला। Mere bhāī ko baṛe skūl meṁ dākhilā milā. My brother got a place at the big school.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
दाख़िला (dākhilā) is usually traced to Arabic dākhila, "an entry", through Persian; Hindi uses it for being let into a school or a course.

Lesson 5 of 5.
वेतन (vetan) — a salary, pay (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an application (formal), then the Hindi for admission (to a school or course).

You'll want to know: वेतन.
वेतन — vetan — "a salary, pay (formal)".
इस नौकरी में वेतन अच्छा है, लेकिन काम बहुत है। Is naukrī meṁ vetan acchā hai, lekin kām bahut hai. This job pays well, but there is a lot of work.
Masculine.
A formal word; everyday speech also says तनख़्वाह.

The word, taken apart.
वेतन (vetan) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit vetana, "wages, hire, salary" (Monier-Williams).
