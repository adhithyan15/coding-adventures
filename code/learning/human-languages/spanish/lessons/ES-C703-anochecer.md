## You'll want to know: anochecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C703-ANOCHECER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anochecer** — "to get dark; nightfall".

> En invierno anochece muy pronto.
>
> *In winter it gets dark very early.*

*Al anochecer* — at nightfall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anochecer* is built from *a-* and Latin *noctēscere* "to grow dark" (EtymDB), from *nox, noctis* "night".
