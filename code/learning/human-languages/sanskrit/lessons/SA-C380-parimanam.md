## You'll want to know: परिमाणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C380-MATRA380-02]; assesses=[] -->

**परिमाणम्** — *parimāṇam* — "an amount; a size".

> यदि जलस्य परिमाणं न्यूनम्, तर्हि वृक्षाः शुष्यन्ति।
>
> *yadi jalasya parimāṇaṃ nyūnam, tarhi vṛkṣāḥ śuṣyanti.*
>
> If the amount of water is low, the trees dry up.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिमाणम्** (*parimāṇam*) is built on *pari*, "around", and the root √mā, "to measure"; MW gives *pari-māṇa* "measure of any kind, size, weight, number" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
