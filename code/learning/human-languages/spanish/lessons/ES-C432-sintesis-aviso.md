# (un aviso a los vecinos) — the notice on the door

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-04, ES-LEX-AGUA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *la tubería* and *el agua*. You are about to read the notice
those two words exist for.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-04, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-02, ES-LEX-AGUA-01] -->

Read it once straight through, then answer.

> **AVISO A LOS VECINOS**
> El martes cortaremos el **agua** entre las nueve y las dos para reparar una
> **tubería** del garaje. El miércoles vendrán a leer los **contadores**.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: what happens on Tuesday and for how long]
- [YOU SAY: what is being repaired]
- [YOU SAY: what happens the following day]

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-01, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-04, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-05, ES-LEX-C431-PEDIDO-03] -->

Now say these aloud, as if telling somebody who has not read the notice:

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: on Tuesday there is no water in the morning]
- [YOU SAY: they are repairing a pipe in the garage]
- [YOU SAY: I cannot put the washing machine on until the afternoon]
- [YOU SAY: so I am reading on the sofa until they finish]

**A notice like this one is where a beginner's Spanish usually stops.** Every
word in it is ordinary and none of it is in a textbook dialogue. You can read it
now, and you could not two chapters ago.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C432-CASA-04, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-02, ES-LEX-C432-CASA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What are they repairing? (**una tubería**.) What are they coming to
read? (**los contadores**.) What can you not use that morning? (**la
lavadora**.)
