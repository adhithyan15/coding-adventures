## You'll want to know: el mirador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C676-TURISMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el mirador** — "the viewpoint; the glazed balcony".

> Desde el mirador se ve toda la ciudad.
>
> *The whole city can be seen from the viewpoint.*

Built on *mirar*: a place for looking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mirador* is built on *mirar* "to look", which is inherited from Latin *mīrārī* "to wonder at".
