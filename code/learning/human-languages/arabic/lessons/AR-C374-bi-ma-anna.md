# بما أن (bi-mā anna) — seeing that; since

## You'll want to know: بما أن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C374-RABT374-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بما أن** — *bi-mā anna* — "seeing that; since".

> بما أن اليوم عطلة، سننام متأخرين.
>
> *bi-mā anna al-yawm ʿuṭla, sa-nanāmu mutaʾakhkhirīn.*
>
> Seeing that today is a holiday, we will sleep late.

It gives a reason both speakers already accept.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بما أن** (*bi-mā anna*) is built from three native Arabic function words: *bi-*, "by", *mā*, "what", and *anna*, "that". The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
