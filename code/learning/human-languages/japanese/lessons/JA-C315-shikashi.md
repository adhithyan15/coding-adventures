## You'll want to know: しかし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C315-SURUTO315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しかし** — *shikashi* — "but; however".

> **しかし、だれも いませんでした。** — *shikashi, daremo imasen deshita* — "But there was nobody there."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しかし** (*shikashi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
