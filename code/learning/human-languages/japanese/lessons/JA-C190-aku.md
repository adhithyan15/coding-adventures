## You'll want to know: あく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C190-TOWN190-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あく** — *aku* — "to be open; to open, to become vacant".

> **ぎんこうは くじに あきます。** — *ginkō wa kuji ni akimasu* — "The bank opens at nine."

Polite **あきます**, *akimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あく** (*aku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
