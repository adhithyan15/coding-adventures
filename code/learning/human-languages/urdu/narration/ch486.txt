Urdu, chapter 486: Growing Up.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
عمر (umr) — age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be brought up, then the Urdu for to dress (someone).

You'll want to know: عمر.
عمر — umr — "age".
میری بہن کی عمر دس سال ہے۔ merī bahan kī umr das sāl hai. My sister is ten years old.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
عمر (umr) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿumr, "life, a lifetime".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

اکلوتا — iklautā — "only (of a child)".
وہ اپنے ماں باپ کا اکلوتا بیٹا ہے۔ voh apne māṅ bāp kā iklautā beṭā hai. He is his parents' only son.

The word, taken apart.
اکلوتا (iklautā) is inherited from ēkkalaputra, "an only son", a form CDIAL reconstructs from the words for "alone" and "son" (CDIAL 2507).

Lesson 4 of 6.
گزرنا (guzarnā) — to pass (of time); to go by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for of age, then the Urdu for only (of a child).

You'll want to know: گزرنا.
گزرنا — guzarnā — "to pass (of time); to go by".
بچپن کے دن جلدی گزر گئے۔ bachpan ke din jaldī guzar gae. The days of childhood went by quickly.
Past (it …): وہ سال جلدی گزرا, voh sāl jaldī guzrā.

The word, taken apart.
گزرنا (guzarnā) is usually traced to Persian guzar, "a passing", made into an Urdu verb; گزشتہ, "last, previous", is the time that has gone by.
