Lesson 2 of 5.
बोली (bolī) — a dialect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a river valley, then the Marathi for a tradition.

You'll want to know: बोली.
बोली — bolī — "a dialect".

The word, taken apart.
बोली (bolī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bōll, "speak", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9321). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "language".

संकेतस्थळ (saṅketsthaḷ) is a modern coinage of Sanskrit parts: saṅketa, "sign, appointment", and sthala, "ground, place" (CDIAL 13744).

कळफलक (kaḷphalak) is kaḷ, "key, little contrivance", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit kalā, "any practical art", a word it takes from Dravidian (CDIAL 2927), and Sanskrit phalaka, "board" (CDIAL 9053).
