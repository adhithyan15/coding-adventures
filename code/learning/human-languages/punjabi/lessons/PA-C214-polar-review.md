# Ask yes or no again

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214] -->

[YOU HEAR: *are you a traveller?*] Say **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ?**

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-YA-01, PA-LEX-YATRI-202, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *is she a traveller?*] Say **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੈ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *is the garden open?*] Say **ਕੀ ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ?**
- [YOU READ: **ਯਾਤਰੀ**] Point to **ਯ**, say *ya*, then say the whole word.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੈ?** once]
