## You'll want to know: पद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C509-BADHATI509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पद** — *pad* — "a position; a post".

> तिला व्यवस्थापकाचं पद मिळालं.
>
> *tilā vyavasthāpakācaṁ pad miḷālaṁ.*
>
> She got the manager's post.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पद** (*pad*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pada*, "step, place, position" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
