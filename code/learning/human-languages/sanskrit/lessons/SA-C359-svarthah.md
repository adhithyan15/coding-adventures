## You'll want to know: स्वार्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C359-TYAGA359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वार्थः** — *svārthaḥ* — "self-interest".

> सः केवलं स्वार्थं चिन्तयति।
>
> *saḥ kevalaṃ svārthaṃ cintayati.*
>
> He thinks only of his own interest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वार्थः** (*svārthaḥ*) is built from *sva*, "own", and *artha*, "aim, advantage"; MW gives *svārtha* "one's own affair, personal advantage, self-interest" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
