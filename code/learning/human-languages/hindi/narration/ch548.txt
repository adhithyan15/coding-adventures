Hindi, chapter 548: Time Running Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अवधि (avadhi) — a period; a time limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an inkling, then the Hindi for an instruction.

You'll want to know: अवधि.
अवधि — avadhi — "a period; a time limit".
टिकट की अवधि एक हफ़्ता है। Ṭikaṭ kī avadhi ek haftā hai. The ticket is valid for a week.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
अवधि (avadhi) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit avadhi, "a term, a limit" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
लुटाना (luṭānā) — to squander; to give away freely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the end, then the Hindi for laziness.

You'll want to know: लुटाना.
लुटाना — luṭānā — "to squander; to give away freely".
उसने अपने पैसे जुए में लुटा दिए। Usne apne paise jue meṁ luṭā diye. He squandered his money on gambling.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पैसे लुटाए, usne paise luṭāe.

The word, taken apart.
लुटाना (luṭānā) is the causative of lūṭnā, "to plunder", which CDIAL derives from luṭṭati, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 11078): to let one's goods be carried off.
