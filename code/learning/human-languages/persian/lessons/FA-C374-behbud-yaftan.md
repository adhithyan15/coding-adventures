## You'll want to know: بهبود یافتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C374-BEHBUD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بهبود یافتن** (*behbud yâftan*) — "to improve, to recover".

> بعد از دو هفته حالش بهبود یافت.
>
> *ba'd az do hafte hâlash behbud yâft.*
>
> After two weeks he recovered.

Formal; *yâftan* has the stem *yâb-*. In speech, *behtar shodan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بهبود یافتن** is native Persian, built from *behbud* "improvement" with the verb *yâftan* "to find", a formal way to say that something got better.
