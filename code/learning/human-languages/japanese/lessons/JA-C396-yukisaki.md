## You'll want to know: ゆきさき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C396-YUKISAKI396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆきさき** — *yukisaki* — "a destination".

> **でんしゃの ゆきさきを たしかめました。** — *densha no yukisaki o tashikamemashita* — "I made sure of the train's destination."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆきさき** (*yukisaki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yuki*, "going" (from *yuku, another form of iku*) and *saki*, "ahead"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
