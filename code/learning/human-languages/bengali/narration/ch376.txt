6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
লক্ষ্য (lôkkhyô) — a goal; an aim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to impose, then the Bengali for to relax a rule.

You'll want to know: লক্ষ্য.
লক্ষ্য — lôkkhyô — "a goal; an aim".
যদি তোমার লক্ষ্য বড় হয়, তাহলে তোমাকে বেশি কাজ করতে হবে। jôdi tomār lôkkhyô bôṛo hôy, tāhôle tomāke beshi kāj kôrte hôbe. If your goal is big, you will have to work harder.

The word, taken apart.
লক্ষ্য (lôkkhyô) is Sanskrit lakṣya, the mark aimed at (Monier-Williams). It is a target in archery and a goal in life.

চেষ্টা — cheshṭā — "an attempt; an effort".
চেষ্টা করলে তুমি পারবে। cheshṭā kôrle tumi pārbe. If you try, you'll manage.

The word, taken apart.
চেষ্টা (cheshṭā) is Sanskrit ceṣṭā, effort or exertion (Monier-Williams). With করা it is the everyday verb 'to try'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
অর্জন করা (ôrjôn kôrā) — to achieve; to earn (a result).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for hard work, then the Bengali for successful.

You'll want to know: অর্জন করা.
অর্জন করা — ôrjôn kôrā — "to achieve; to earn (a result)".
পরিশ্রম করলে তুমি বড় লক্ষ্যও অর্জন করতে পারবে। pôrishrôm kôrle tumi bôṛo lôkkhyôo ôrjôn kôrte pārbe. With hard work you can achieve even a big goal.
Past (he/she …): তিনি অনেক সাফল্য অর্জন করলেন, tini ônek sāphôlyô ôrjôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
অর্জন করা (ôrjôn kôrā) is Sanskrit arjana, acquiring or earning (Monier-Williams), with করা: to win a goal, a prize or a reputation by effort.
