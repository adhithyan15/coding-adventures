# માઠું (māṭhũ) — unwelcome; bad (of news or feelings)

## You'll want to know: માઠું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C456-DUBHA456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**માઠું** — *māṭhũ* — "unwelcome; bad (of news or feelings)".

> મારા જવાબનું તેને માઠું લાગ્યું.
>
> *mārā javābnũ tene māṭhũ lāgyũ.*
>
> He took offence at my answer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માઠું** (*māṭhũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*maṭṭa-*, "defective"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "vicious, bad, unfortunate" (CDIAL 9723).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
