## You'll want to know: فوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C461-ILGHA461-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فوت** — *fawwata* — "to miss (a chance); to let slip".

> فوت أخي الحفلة بسبب العمل.
>
> *fawwata akhī al-ḥafla bi-sabab al-ʿamal.*
>
> My brother missed the party because of work.

Present: يفوت, *yufawwit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فوت** (*fawwata*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-w-t*, "to pass by, to escape" (Lane). It is Form II, "to let pass".
