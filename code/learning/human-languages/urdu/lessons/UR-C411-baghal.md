## You'll want to know: بغل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C411-DHALAN411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بغل** — *baghal* — "beside; the side (of someone)".

> ہوٹل کی بغل میں ایک دکان ہے۔
>
> *hoṭal kī baghal meṅ ek dukān hai.*
>
> There's a shop next to the hotel.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بغل** (*baghal*) is usually traced to Persian *baghal*, "the armpit, the side"; **کی بغل میں** is "right beside".
