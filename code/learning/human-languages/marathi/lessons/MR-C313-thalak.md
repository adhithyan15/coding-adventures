## You'll want to know: ठळक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C313-AFVA313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ठळक** — *ṭhaḷak* — "prominent; bold".

> निवेदकाने आजच्या ठळक बातम्या वाचल्या.
>
> *nivedakāne ājacyā ṭhaḷak bātamyā vācalyā.*
>
> The newsreader read out today's main headlines.

It keeps one form: ठळक अक्षरं, bold letters; ठळक बातम्या, the headlines.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठळक** (*ṭhaḷak*) is a Marathi word for what stands out clearly; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
