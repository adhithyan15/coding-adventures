## You'll want to know: desinstalar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C397-INSTALAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**desinstalar** — "to uninstall".

> Desinstalei a aplicação porque não funcionava.
>
> *I uninstalled the app because it did not work.*

The opposite of *instalar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desinstalar* is built from *des-* and *instalar* "to install", which was borrowed from French *installer* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
