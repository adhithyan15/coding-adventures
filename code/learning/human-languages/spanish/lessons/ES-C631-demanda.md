## You'll want to know: la demanda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C631-REBAJAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la demanda** — "the demand; the lawsuit".

> Hay mucha demanda de pisos.
>
> *There is a lot of demand for flats.*

*La oferta y la demanda* — supply and demand. In law, a lawsuit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*demanda* is built on *demandar*, inherited from Latin *demandare* 'to entrust', from *mandare* 'to give into someone's hand, to order' (EtymDB).
