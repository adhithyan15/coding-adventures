## You'll want to know: શેખી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C468-JUTHU468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**શેખી** — *śekhī* — "boasting".

> અરુણ પોતાની નવી ગાડીની શેખી મારે છે.
>
> *Aruṇ potānī navī gāḍīnī śekhī māre che.*
>
> Arun boasts about his new car.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શેખી** (*śekhī*) is usually traced to Persian *šēxī*, "pride, boasting", built on *šēx*, "an elder, a chief".

## What you've built
