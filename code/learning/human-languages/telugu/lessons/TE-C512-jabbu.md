## You'll want to know: జబ్బు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C512-JABBU512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**జబ్బు** — *jabbu* — "a sickness; an illness".

> ఆయనకు గుండె జబ్బు ఉంది.
>
> *āyanaku guṇḍe jabbu undi.*
>
> He has heart trouble.

An everyday word: గుండె జబ్బు, heart disease. జబ్బుగా ఉంది, (he) is unwell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జబ్బు** (*jabbu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2338 lists Telugu *jabbu*, "illness, sickness, weakness", in the article of Tamil *cappai*, "that which is weak".
