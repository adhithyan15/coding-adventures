## You'll want to know: 社会
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C375-HELP375-02]; assesses=[] -->

**社会** — *shèhuì* — "society".

> *wǒmen de shèhuì xūyào rèxīn de rén* — Our society needs warm-hearted people.

The new character, in pieces: 社 — seven strokes in two pieces: on the left the sign piece, four strokes; on the right a short bar, a vertical and a long bar, the earth piece at the foot of 在. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**社会** (*shèhuì*) is built from 社 'the altar of the soil' and 会 'to meet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyaeX* and *hwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the first with the god of grain and glosses the second 'to come together'. People meeting at the village altar is usually given as the start; the sense 'society' is not given there.
