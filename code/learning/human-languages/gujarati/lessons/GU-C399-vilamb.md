## You'll want to know: વિલંબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C399-DELAY399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વિલંબ** — *vilamb* — "a delay (formal)".

> ધુમ્મસને લીધે ટ્રેનમાં બે કલાકનો વિલંબ છે.
>
> *dhummasne līdhe ṭrenmā̃ be kalākno vilamb che.*
>
> Because of the fog the train is two hours late.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિલંબ** (*vilamb*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vilamba*, "hanging down", then "slowness, delay" (Monier-Williams); station announcements use it where speech says મોડું.
