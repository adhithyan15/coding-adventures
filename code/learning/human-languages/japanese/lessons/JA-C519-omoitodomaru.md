## You'll want to know: おもいとどまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C519-GIVEUP519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいとどまる** — *omoitodomaru* — "to think better of (doing)".

> **もし ははの いけんを きいたら、かれも おもいとどまるでしょう。** — *moshi haha no iken o kiitara, kare mo omoitodomaru deshō* — "If he listens to his mother's opinion, he too will probably think better of it."

Polite **おもいとどまります**, *omoitodomarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが ひっこしを おもいとどまりました。**, *ani ga hikkoshi o omoitodomarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいとどまる** (*omoitodomaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *omou*, "to think", and *todomaru*, "to stay"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
