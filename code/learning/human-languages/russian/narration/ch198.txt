Lesson 1 of 5.
пассажир (passazhír) — a passenger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a guitar, then the Russian for a piano.

You'll want to know: пассажир.
пассажир (passazhír) — "a passenger".

The word, taken apart.
пассажир (passazhír) — first пасажера, in 1706; it came through German Passagier or Dutch passagier from French passager, Italian passaggiere, "one passing through" (Vasmer).

The word, taken apart.
вагон (vagón) came from German Waggon or French wagon, which go back to English waggon; German Wagen and Russian воз, "a cart", are cousins of the English word (Vasmer).

Lesson 4 of 5.
пробка (próbka) — a traffic jam; a cork.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a carriage, then the Russian for a transfer.

You'll want to know: пробка.
пробка (próbka) — "a traffic jam; a cork".

The word, taken apart.
пробка (próbka) apparently came through Low German propke, prop, "a stopper", since Russia long imported corks from Bremen (Vasmer). A traffic jam corks the road.

The word, taken apart.
авария (aváriya) comes from Italian avaria or French avarie, "damage to a ship or cargo", from Arabic ʿawār, "damage, a flaw" (Vasmer).
