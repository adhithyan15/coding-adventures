## You'll want to know: بدبو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C440-SARSEDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بدبو** (*badbu*) — "smelly, foul-smelling".

> آب لوله بدبو بود.
>
> *Âb-e lule badbu bud.*
>
> The tap water smelled bad.

*bad* 'bad' + *bu* 'smell'; the opposite of *khoshbu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*badbu* is a native compound of *bad* 'bad' and *bu* 'smell', the word behind *buyidan* 'to smell'. EtymDB does not list the compound.
