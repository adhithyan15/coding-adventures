# છોલાવું (cholāvũ) — to get grazed

## You'll want to know: છોલાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C481-KAPAVU481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**છોલાવું** — *cholāvũ* — "to get grazed".

> અરુણ પડી ગયો અને તેનો ઘૂંટણ છોલાઈ ગયો.
>
> *Aruṇ paḍī gayo ane teno ghū̃ṭaṇ cholāī gayo.*
>
> Arun fell and grazed his knee.

Past (it …): તે ઘૂંટણ છોલાઈ ગયો, *te ghū̃ṭaṇ cholāī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છોલાવું** (*cholāvũ*) is the intransitive partner of **છોલવું** (*cholvũ*), "to peel", which goes back to a reconstructed *\*chōll-* (CDIAL 5073): skin that peels off.
