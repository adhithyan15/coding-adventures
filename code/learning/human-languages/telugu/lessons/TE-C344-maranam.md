## You'll want to know: మరణం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C344-SANTAPAM344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మరణం** — *maraṇaṁ* — "death".

> ఆయన మరణం గురించి పత్రికలో వార్త వచ్చింది.
>
> *āyana maraṇaṁ guriñci patrikalō vārta vaccindi.*
>
> The news of his death appeared in the paper.

A formal word: in speech people say చనిపోవు, to die.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మరణం** (*maraṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *maraṇa*, "dying, death" (CDIAL 9869), from the root *mṛ*, "to die".
