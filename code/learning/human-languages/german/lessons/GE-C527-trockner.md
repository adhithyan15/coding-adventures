## You'll want to know: der Trockner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C527-WAESCHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Trockner** — "tumble dryer".

> Die Handtücher kommen in den Trockner.
>
> *The towels go in the dryer.*

Plural: *die Trockner*. Built on *trocknen* — to dry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Trockner* is built on *trocknen* "to dry", from *trocken* "dry", which is inherited from Middle High German *trucken* (EtymDB).
