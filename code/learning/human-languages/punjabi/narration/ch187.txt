Lesson 4 of 9.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਸੀ (sii), then ਫੁੱਲ and ਲਾਲ.

Grammar Lens: the plural and polite past form.
ਸਨ — san — were.
Use this past copula with a plural subject here. It also serves respectful singular reference, matching Punjabi’s familiar plural-for-respect pattern.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 7 of 9.
Put ਸਨ (san) after a familiar plural habit — they used to work, with a familiar habitual predicate and were.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ, then retrieve ਸਨ (san).

Grammar Lens: a familiar habit moved into the past.
ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਸਨ — oh kamm karde san — they used to work.
The familiar plural habitual predicate stays before ਸਨ (san). The final copula moves the routine into the past.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: oh kamm karde san; YOU SAY: ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਸਨ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਸਨ (oh kamm karde san)]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

This step reuses the already-owned habitual predicate and introduces no new perfective form.
