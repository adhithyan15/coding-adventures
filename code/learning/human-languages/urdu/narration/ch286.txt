Urdu, chapter 286: The Injured.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بے ہوش (be hosh) — unconscious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be grazed, then the Urdu for a scratch.

You'll want to know: بے ہوش.
بے ہوش — be hosh — "unconscious".
وہ آدمی بے ہوش تھا۔ voh ādmī be hosh thā. That man was unconscious.

The word, taken apart.
بے ہوش (be hosh) is usually traced to Persian bē-hōsh, built from bē, "without", and hōsh, "the senses, awareness", the word inside ہوشیار.

Lesson 2 of 5.
لہولہان (lahūluhān) — covered in blood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a scratch, then the Urdu for unconscious.

You'll want to know: لہولہان.
لہولہان — lahūluhān — "covered in blood".
اس کا چہرہ لہولہان تھا۔ us kā chehrā lahūluhān thā. His face was covered in blood.

The word, taken apart.
لہولہان (lahūluhān) is lahū, blood, which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit lṓhita, "red" (CDIAL 11165), followed by an expressive echo, -luhān, that makes the picture stronger.

Lesson 3 of 5.
علاج (ilāj) — medical treatment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for unconscious, then the Urdu for covered in blood.

You'll want to know: علاج.
علاج — ilāj — "medical treatment".
ہسپتال میں اس کا علاج ہوا۔ haspatāl meṅ us kā ilāj huā. He was treated in hospital.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
علاج (ilāj) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿilāj, "a remedy, a cure". علاج ہوا says that someone was treated.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کچلنا (kuchalnā) — to crush; to run over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for covered in blood, then the Urdu for medical treatment.

You'll want to know: کچلنا.
کچلنا — kuchalnā — "to crush; to run over".
ٹرک نے سائیکل کو کچل دیا۔ ṭrak ne sāikil ko kuchal diyā. The lorry ran over the bicycle.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پھول کچلا, us ne phūl kuchlā.

The word, taken apart.
کچلنا (kuchalnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kucalnā, "to trample on", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3221). کچل دیا crushes it flat, done once and for all.

Past (he …): وہ سنبھل گیا, voh sambhal gayā.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sā̃bhalnā, "to be supported", under Sanskrit sáṃbharati (CDIAL 12961). It is the partner of سنبھالنا: you سنبھالنا something, and you سنبھلنا (sambhalnā) yourself.
