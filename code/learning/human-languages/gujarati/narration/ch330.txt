Gujarati, chapter 330: Nine Nights of Dance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મંડપ (manḍap) — a pavilion (for a festival).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a garland, then the Gujarati for a procession.

You'll want to know: મંડપ.
મંડપ — manḍap — "a pavilion (for a festival)".
ચોકમાં મોટો મંડપ હતો. cokmā̃ moṭo manḍap hato. There was a big pavilion in the square.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મંડપ (manḍap) is a tatsama, Sanskrit maṇḍapa, "an open temporary shed, a pavilion" (CDIAL 9740). It is put up for weddings and festivals.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઘૂમવું (ghūmvũ) — to whirl round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a procession, then the Gujarati for a pavilion.

You'll want to know: ઘૂમવું.
ઘૂમવું — ghūmvũ — "to whirl round".
રાત્રે લોકો મંડપમાં ગરબે ઘૂમ્યા. rātre loko manḍapmā̃ garbe ghūmyā. At night people whirled round in the garba in the pavilion.
Past (he …): તે ગરબે ઘૂમ્યો, te garbe ghūmyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઘૂમવું (ghūmvũ) comes from ghummati, a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati ghumvũ, glossed "to revolve in the mind" (CDIAL 4485). In the dance it is moving round and round: ગરબે ઘૂમ્યા, they danced the garba.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઝૂલવું (jhūlvũ) — to swing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a pavilion, then the Gujarati for to whirl round.

You'll want to know: ઝૂલવું.
ઝૂલવું — jhūlvũ — "to swing".
બાળકો હિંચકા પર ઝૂલતાં હતાં. bāḷko hinckā par jhūltā̃ hatā̃. The children were swinging on the swing.
Past (she …): તે છોકરી ઝૂલી, te chokrī jhūlī.

The word, taken apart.
ઝૂલવું (jhūlvũ) comes from jhulyati, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to swing, hang" (CDIAL 5406). People swing on a હિંચકો; leaves swing on a branch.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ભજન (bhajan) — a hymn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to whirl round, then the Gujarati for to swing.

You'll want to know: ભજન.
ભજન — bhajan — "a hymn".
રાત્રે મારા દાદાએ ભજન ગાયાં. rātre mārā dādāe bhajan gāyā̃. At night my grandfather sang hymns.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભજન (bhajan) is a tatsama, Sanskrit bhajana, "worship, adoration" (Monier-Williams): a song of devotion. It is neuter: ભજન ગાયાં, sang hymns.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ભક્ત (bhakt) is a tatsama, Sanskrit bhakta, "devoted to, attached to" (Monier-Williams). The same Sanskrit word, in the sense of a share of food, gave ભાત, rice.
