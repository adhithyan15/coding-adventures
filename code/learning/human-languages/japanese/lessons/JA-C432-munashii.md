## You'll want to know: むなしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C432-WORRY432-04]; assesses=[] -->

**むなしい** — *munashii* — "empty; futile".

> **しあいに まけて、むなしかったです。** — *shiai ni makete, munashikatta desu* — "We lost the match, and it felt empty."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むなしい** (*munashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
