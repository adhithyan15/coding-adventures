## You'll want to know: ōsculum, ōsculī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C534-OSCU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ōsculum, ōsculī** — "a kiss".

> Māter fīliō ōsculum dat.
>
> *The mother gives her son a kiss.*

Neuter, a diminutive of *ōs* — mouth: a little mouth. *Ōsculor*, already known, is to kiss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ōsculum** is a diminutive of *ōs* "mouth" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *osculate* comes from the verb *ōsculor*.
