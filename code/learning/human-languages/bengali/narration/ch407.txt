6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
বাজা (bājā) — to ring; to sound; to strike (the hour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a place to stay, then the Bengali for to tap (on a door).

You'll want to know: বাজা.
বাজা — bājā — "to ring; to sound; to strike (the hour)".
স্টেশনে ঘণ্টা বাজল, আর ট্রেন ছাড়ল। sṭeshône ghônṭā bājlô, ār ṭren chhāṛlô. The bell rang at the station, and the train left.
Past (he/she …): সেই ঘণ্টা বাজল, sei ghônṭā bājlô.
Also of the hour: দশটা বাজল, it struck ten. বাজানো, to play an instrument, is the causing partner.

The word, taken apart.
বাজা (bājā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali bājā, "to sound", of a musical instrument, under Sanskrit vādyatē, "is sounded, is played" (CDIAL 11513).

Lesson 2 of 6.
বেয়ারা (beyārā) — a bearer; a waiter (in a hotel).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to tap (on a door), then the Bengali for to ring.

You'll want to know: বেয়ারা.
বেয়ারা — beyārā — "a bearer; a waiter (in a hotel)".
বেয়ারা আমাদের ঘরে চা নিয়ে এল। beyārā āmāder ghôre chā niye elô. The bearer brought tea to our room.

The word, taken apart.
বেয়ারা (beyārā) is usually traced to English bearer, the old word in Indian hotels and clubs for a servant who carries and serves.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bearer, then the Bengali for a menu.

You'll want to know: বকশিশ.
বকশিশ — bôkshish — "a tip".
আমরা ওয়েটারকে কুড়ি টাকা বকশিশ দিলাম। āmrā oyeṭārke kuṛi ṭākā bôkshish dilām. We gave the waiter a twenty-taka tip.

The word, taken apart.
বকশিশ (bôkshish) is usually traced to Persian bakhshish, "a gift, a present", from bakhshīdan, to give; English baksheesh is the same word.
