Spanish, chapter 489: Por la ciudad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
aparcar — to park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to say goodbye, then the Spanish for to greet.

You'll want to know: aparcar.
aparcar — "to park".
No se puede aparcar aquí. You cannot park here.
The verb behind el aparcamiento, the car park. In Latin America people say estacionar.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.
