Italian, chapter 321: Le feste d'inverno.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il presepe — the nativity scene, the crib.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the name day, then the Italian for the saint.

You'll want to know: il presepe.
il presepe — "the nativity scene, the crib".
Ogni anno facciamo il presepe in salotto. Every year we set up the nativity scene in the living room.
Also il presepio.

The word, taken apart.
presepe is a learned borrowing from Latin praesaepe "a stall, a manger" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: il panettone.
il panettone — "the panettone, the tall festive cake".
Il panettone ha l'uvetta e i canditi. Panettone has raisins and candied peel.
A sweet bread from the north, now eaten everywhere in winter.

The word, taken apart.
panettone comes from Lombard dialect panaton (EtymDB), built on pane "bread", from Latin pānis.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the nativity scene, then the Italian for the panettone.

You'll want to know: il cenone.
il cenone — "the big festive dinner".
Il cenone di fine anno è a casa nostra. The big New Year's Eve dinner is at our house.
The ending -one makes things big: cena, cenone.

The word, taken apart.
cenone is cena "dinner" with -one "big"; cena is inherited from Latin cēna (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
addobbare — to decorate (a room, a tree).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the panettone, then the Italian for the big festive dinner.

You'll want to know: addobbare.
addobbare — "to decorate (a room, a tree)".
Abbiamo addobbato la casa con le luci. We decorated the house with lights.
Gli addobbi are the decorations.

The word, taken apart.
addobbare was borrowed from Old French adober "to equip" (EtymDB), from a Frankish verb meaning "to strike", as in dubbing a knight.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la tombola — the tombola, the family bingo game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the big festive dinner, then the Italian for to decorate.

You'll want to know: la tombola.
la tombola — "the tombola, the family bingo game".
Dopo cena giochiamo a tombola. After dinner we play tombola.
Numbers from 1 to 90; fare tombola is to fill the whole card.

The word, taken apart.
tombola is usually traced to tombolare "to tumble", from the numbers tumbling out of the bag; tombolare itself is usually explained as a sound word (EtymDB has no entry).
