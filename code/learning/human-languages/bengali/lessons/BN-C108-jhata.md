## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

CDIAL lists **ঝাঁটা** (*jhāṁṭā*), "broom", under *\*jhāṇṭa*, a variant of Sanskrit *jhāṭa*, "forest, arbour", which it reports may come from Munda (CDIAL 5362).
