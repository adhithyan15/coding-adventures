## You'll want to know: l'épicier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C444-COMMERCES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'épicier** — "the grocer".

> L'épicier du quartier ferme à neuf heures.
>
> *The local grocer closes at nine.*

*L'épicerie* — the grocer's shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*épicier* is built on *épice* 'spice', which is inherited from Latin *speciēs* 'kind', later 'goods, spices' (Lewis & Short).
