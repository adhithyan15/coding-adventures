## You'll want to know: میانبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C448-MIYANBOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**میانبر** (*miyânbor*) — "a shortcut".

> راننده از یک میانبر رفت و زود رسیدیم.
>
> *Rânande az yek miyânbor raft va zud residim.*
>
> The driver took a shortcut and we arrived early.

*miyân* 'middle' + *bor* 'cutting'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miyânbor* is a native compound of inherited words: *miyân* 'middle', usually traced to Middle Persian *mayān*, and *bor*, the present stem of *boridan* 'to cut': a way that cuts through the middle.
