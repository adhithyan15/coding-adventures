Arabic, chapter 99: Music, News, Country, Language, Story.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
موسيقى (mūsīqā) — music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a holiday, then the Arabic for love.

You'll want to know: موسيقى.
موسيقى — mūsīqā — "music".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mūsīqā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mūsīqā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mūsīqā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a holiday, then the Arabic for love, then say mūsīqā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does موسيقى (mūsīqā) mean? ("Music".) Say it once more.
