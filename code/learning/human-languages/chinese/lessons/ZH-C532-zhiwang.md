## You'll want to know: zhǐwàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C532-HOPE532-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐwàng* — "to count on someone".

> *nǐ búyào zhǐwàng tā bùrán nǐ huì shīwàng* — Don't count on him, or you'll be disappointed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐwàng* is built from *zhǐ* 'to point to' and 望 'to look out for', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyijX* and *mjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a finger' and 'to show'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
