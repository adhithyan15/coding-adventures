German, chapter 267: Es dauert lange.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dauern — to last, to take (time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for in time, then the German for at once.

You'll want to know: dauern.
dauern — "to last, to take (time)".
Der Film dauerte drei Stunden. The film lasted three hours.
Regular: es dauert, es dauerte, es hat gedauert. Wie lange dauert das? — how long does it take?

The word, taken apart.
dauern comes from Middle High German dūren, which was borrowed from Latin dūrāre "to last, to endure" (EtymDB), itself built on dūrus "hard" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
vergehen — to pass, to go by (of time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for at once, then the German for to last.

You'll want to know: vergehen.
vergehen — "to pass, to go by (of time)".
Die Zeit verging sehr schnell. The time went by very quickly.
Perfekt with sein: Die Zeit ist schnell vergangen. Vergangen also means 'past'.

The word, taken apart.
vergehen is built on gehen "to go", inherited from Proto-Germanic gāną (EtymDB), with the prefix ver- "away": to go away, to pass.

You'll want to know: fortsetzen.
fortsetzen — "to continue, to carry on with".
Nach der Pause setzten wir die Reise fort. After the break we continued the journey.
Separable: wir setzen die Arbeit fort, wir haben sie fortgesetzt.

The word, taken apart.
fortsetzen joins fort "forth, onward" and setzen "to set", a cousin of English set from Proto-Germanic satjaną (EtymDB): to set forward.

Lesson 5 of 5.
unterbrechen — to interrupt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to go on, then the German for to continue.

You'll want to know: unterbrechen.
unterbrechen — "to interrupt".
Ein Anruf unterbrach das Gespräch. A phone call interrupted the conversation.
Not separable: er unterbricht, er hat unterbrochen.

The word, taken apart.
unterbrechen joins unter "under, between" and brechen "to break", from Old High German brehhan, Proto-Germanic brekaną (EtymDB): to break in between.
