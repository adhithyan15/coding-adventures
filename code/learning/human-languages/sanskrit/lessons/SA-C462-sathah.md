# शठः (śaṭhaḥ) — deceitful

## You'll want to know: शठः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C462-SATHA462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शठः** — *śaṭhaḥ* — "deceitful".

> शठः वणिक् मिथ्या वदति।
>
> *śaṭhaḥ vaṇik mithyā vadati.*
>
> The deceitful merchant tells lies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शठः** (*śaṭhaḥ*) MW gives "false, deceitful, fraudulent" and "a cheat, a rogue"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
