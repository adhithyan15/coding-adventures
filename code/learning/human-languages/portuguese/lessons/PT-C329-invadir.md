## You'll want to know: invadir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C329-BATALHA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**invadir** — "to invade".

> O exército invadiu o país vizinho.
>
> *The army invaded the neighbouring country.*

Also of crowds: *os adeptos invadiram o campo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invadir* was borrowed from Latin *invādere* "to go into, to attack" (EtymDB), built on *vādere* "to go".
