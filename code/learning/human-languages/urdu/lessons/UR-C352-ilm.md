## You'll want to know: علم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C352-MAZMUN352-04]; assesses=[] -->

**علم** — *ilm* — "knowledge; learning".

> کتابوں سے علم ملتا ہے۔
>
> *kitāboṅ se ilm miltā hai.*
>
> Books give us knowledge.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**علم** (*ilm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿilm*, "knowledge".
