## You'll want to know: பணம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C68-SHOP-03]; assesses=[] -->

**பணம்** (*paṇam*) — "money".

Money, in the plain everyday sense — what is in your hand, not what is in a bank.

The ண in the middle is the curled-back one, the same ண you have been saying since வணக்கம் on
the very first page. And it closes with **ம்**, the ending that finishes so many Tamil nouns:
வணக்கம், தூக்கம், and now பணம்.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Three: the shop, the price, and what you pay it with.
