## You'll want to know: वृत्तपत्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C310-VRUTTAPATRA310-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वृत्तपत्र** — *vṛttapatra* — "a newspaper".

> आजोबा रोज सकाळी वृत्तपत्र वाचतात.
>
> *ājobā roj sakāḷī vṛttapatra vāctāt.*
>
> Grandpa reads the newspaper every morning.

Neuter.

Also वर्तमानपत्र, and in speech पेपर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृत्तपत्र** (*vṛttapatra*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *vṛtta*, 'what has happened, an event', and *patra*, 'a leaf, a letter' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
