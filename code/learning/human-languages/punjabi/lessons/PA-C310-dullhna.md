## You'll want to know: ਡੁੱਲ੍ਹਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C310-SLIP310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੁੱਲ੍ਹਣਾ** — *ḍullhṇā* — "to get spilt".

> ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਦੁੱਧ ਡੁੱਲ੍ਹਿਆ।
>
> *farsh utte merā dudh ḍullhiā.*
>
> My milk spilt on the floor.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪਾਣੀ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਡੁੱਲ੍ਹਿਆ, *oh pāṇī kamre vich ḍullhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੁੱਲ੍ਹਣਾ** (*ḍullhṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhulati*, "bends, falls, flows"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍullhaṇā*, "to be poured out", under it (CDIAL 5593).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
