Kannada, chapter 431: Reaching a Decision.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸು (tīrmānisu) — to conclude; to resolve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a stipulation, then the Kannada for a requirement.

You'll want to know: ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸು.
ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸು — tīrmānisu — "to conclude; to resolve".
ಕೊನೆಗೆ ನಾನು ಊರಿಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗಲು ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸಿದೆ. konege nānu ūrige hintirugalu tīrmāniside. In the end I resolved to go back to my town.
Past (I …): ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸಿದೆ, tīrmāniside.
ತೀರ್ಮಾನ, a decision or a conclusion.

The word, taken apart.
ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸು (tīrmānisu) is built on tīrmāna, "a settlement, a conclusion", from native tīr, "to be finished, to be settled" (DEDR 3278).

You'll want to know: ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸು.
ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸು — samālōcisu — "to confer; to consult".
ನಿರ್ಧಾರ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳುವ ಮೊದಲು ನಾನು ಅಪ್ಪನ ಜೊತೆ ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸಿದೆ. nirdhāra tegedukoḷḷuva modalu nānu appana jote samālōciside. Before deciding I conferred with Father.
Past (I …): ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸಿದೆ, samālōciside.
With whom: -ಜೊತೆ. ಸಮಾಲೋಚನೆ, a consultation.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಾಲೋಚಿಸು (samālōcisu) is built on Sanskrit sam-, "together", and ālocana, "seeing, considering" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಠರಾವು (ṭharāvu) — a formal resolution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to confer, then the Kannada for a dialogue.

You'll want to know: ಠರಾವು.
ಠರಾವು — ṭharāvu — "a formal resolution".
ಸಭೆ ಹೊಸ ಶಾಲೆಯ ಪರವಾಗಿ ಠರಾವು ಮಾಡಿತು. sabhe hosa śāleya paravāgi ṭharāvu māḍitu. The meeting passed a resolution in favour of a new school.
ಠರಾವು (ṭharāvu) ಮಾಡು / ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು, to pass a resolution.

The word, taken apart.
ಠರಾವು (ṭharāvu) is usually traced to Hindustani or Marathi ṭharāv, "a fixing, a resolution"; DEDR and CDIAL do not list the Kannada form.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು (oḷagoḷḷu) — to include.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a dialogue, then the Kannada for a formal resolution.

You'll want to know: ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು.
ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು — oḷagoḷḷu — "to include".
ಪ್ರವಾಸದ ಬೆಲೆ ಊಟವನ್ನೂ ಒಳಗೊಂಡಿತ್ತು. pravāsada bele ūṭavannū oḷagoṇḍittu. The price of the trip included meals.
Past (it …): ಒಳಗೊಂಡಿತು, oḷagoṇḍitu.
Mostly of things: ಒಳಗೊಂಡಿದೆ, it includes.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು (oḷagoḷḷu) is native Dravidian, built from oḷ, "the inside" (DEDR 698), and koḷ, "to take" (DEDR 2151): to take inside.
