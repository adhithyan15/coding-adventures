Tamil, chapter 263: Traffic, Footpath, Aeroplane, Airport, Ship.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
போக்குவரத்து (pōkkuvarattu) — traffic; transport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for finally, at last, then the Tamil for these days, nowadays.

You'll want to know: போக்குவரத்து.
போக்குவரத்து — pōkkuvarattu — "traffic; transport".

The word, taken apart.
போக்குவரத்து (pōkkuvarattu) is native: pōkku, "going", from pō, "to go" (DEDR 4572), and varattu, "coming in" (DEDR 5270): "goings and comings".

Lesson 3 of 5.
விமானம் (vimāṉam) — an aeroplane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for traffic; transport, then the Tamil for a footpath.

You'll want to know: விமானம்.
விமானம் — vimāṉam — "an aeroplane".

The word, taken apart.
விமானம் (vimāṉam) is a loan from Sanskrit vimāna, "a chariot of the gods" (CDIAL 11823), reused for the aeroplane.
