Lesson 1 of 5.
das Hobby — a hobby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for weight, then the German for a habit.

You'll want to know: das Hobby.
das Hobby — "a hobby".

The word, taken apart.
Hobby from English hobby (EtymDB 2.1), short for hobby-horse — borrowed.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for a hobby, then the German for hope.

You'll want to know: der Humor.
der Humor — "humour".

The word, taken apart.
Humor from Latin (h)ūmor, "moisture, fluid" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed: the body's fluids, the humours, were thought to set your temper.
