# ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ (śīrṣike) — a headline; a title

## You'll want to know: ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C320-VARADI320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ** — *śīrṣike* — "a headline; a title".

> ಇವತ್ತಿನ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಯ ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ ಆ ಯುದ್ಧದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *ivattina patrikeya śīrṣike ā yuddhada bagge ittu.*
>
> Today's newspaper headline was about that war.

Plural: ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆಗಳು, *śīrṣikegaḷu*.

A book, a film and a song have a ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ too: its title.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ** (*śīrṣike*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śīrṣaka*, built on *śīrṣa*, "head, top" (CDIAL 12497). A headline sits at the head of a story.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
