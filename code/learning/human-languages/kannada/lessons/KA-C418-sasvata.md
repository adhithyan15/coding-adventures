## You'll want to know: ಶಾಶ್ವತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C418-PARYAYA418-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಶ್ವತ** — *śāśvata* — "permanent".

> ಅವನಿಗೆ ಶಾಶ್ವತ ಕೆಲಸ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *avanige śāśvata kelasa sikkitu.*
>
> He got a permanent job.

ಶಾಶ್ವತವಾಗಿ, for good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಶ್ವತ** (*śāśvata*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śāśvata*, "eternal, constant" (Monier-Williams).
