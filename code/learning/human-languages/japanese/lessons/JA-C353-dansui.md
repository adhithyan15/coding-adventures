## You'll want to know: だんすい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C353-TENKEN353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**だんすい** — *dansui* — "a water outage".

> **だんすいに なったら、おふろの みずを つかいます。** — *dansui ni nattara, ofuro no mizu o tsukaimasu* — "If the water is cut off, we use the water in the bath."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だんすい** (*dansui*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dan*, "severance" (Middle Chinese *dwanX*) and *sui*, "water" (*sywijX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
