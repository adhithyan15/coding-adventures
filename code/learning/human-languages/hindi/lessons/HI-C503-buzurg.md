## You'll want to know: बुज़ुर्ग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C503-PIDHI503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बुज़ुर्ग** — *buzurg* — "an elder; elderly".

> गाँव के बुज़ुर्ग पेड़ के नीचे बैठकर बात करते हैं।
>
> *gā̃v ke buzurg peṛ ke nīce baiṭhkar bāt karte haiṁ.*
>
> The village elders sit under the tree and talk.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुज़ुर्ग** (*buzurg*) is usually traced to Persian *buzurg*, "great", and so "an elder". It is a respectful word for anyone old.
