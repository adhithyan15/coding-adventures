## You'll want to know: анализ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C489-CHECKUP-03]; assesses=[] -->

**анализ** (*análiz*) — "a medical test; analysis".

> Медсестра взяла у меня анализ крови.
>
> *Medsestrá vzyalá u menyá análiz króvi.*
>
> The nurse took a blood sample from me.

Masculine. *Сдать анализы* — to have tests done.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*анализ* is a learned borrowing, usually traced through French or German to Greek *análysis* 'a loosening'; EtymDB has no entry for the Russian word.
