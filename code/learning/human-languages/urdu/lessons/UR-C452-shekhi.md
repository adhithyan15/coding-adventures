## You'll want to know: شیخی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C452-PRIDE452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شیخی** — *shekhī* — "boasting".

> اس کی شیخی سے سب تنگ ہیں۔
>
> *us kī shekhī se sab tang haiṅ.*
>
> Everyone is tired of his boasting.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شیخی** (*shekhī*) is usually traced to Persian *shēkhī*, "boasting", built on *shaikh*, "a chief, an elder".
