# పీడకల (pīḍakala) — a nightmare

## You'll want to know: పీడకల
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పీడకల** — *pīḍakala* — "a nightmare".

> నిన్న రాత్రి నాకు ఒక పీడకల వచ్చింది.
>
> *ninna rātri nāku oka pīḍakala vaccindi.*
>
> Last night I had a nightmare.

A dream comes to you: నాకు కల వచ్చింది. Plural: పీడకలలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పీడకల** (*pīḍakala*) is a compound: Sanskrit *pīḍā*, "pain, damage" (CDIAL 8227), borrowed as పీడ, "trouble", and the Telugu word కల, "a dream": a dream that torments.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
