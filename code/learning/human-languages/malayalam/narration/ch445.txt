Lesson 1 of 5.
അനിവാര്യമായ (anivāryamāya) — inevitable; essential.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for as per, then the Malayalam for a role.

You'll want to know: അനിവാര്യമായ.
അനിവാര്യമായ — anivāryamāya — "inevitable; essential".
ആ മാറ്റം അനിവാര്യമായിരുന്നു. ā māṟṟaṁ anivāryamāyirunnu. That change was inevitable.
As a predicate: അത് അനിവാര്യമാണ്.

The word, taken apart.
അനിവാര്യമായ (anivāryamāya) is built from Sanskrit anivārya, "not to be warded off, unavoidable" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ആയ.

You'll want to know: അടിസ്ഥാനപരമായ.
അടിസ്ഥാനപരമായ — aṭisthānaparamāya — "fundamental; basic".
അവർ തമ്മിൽ അടിസ്ഥാനപരമായ ഒരു വ്യത്യാസം ഉണ്ട്. avar tammil aṭisthānaparamāya oru vyatyāsaṁ uṇṭŭ. There is a fundamental difference between them.
Built on അടിസ്ഥാനം, a basis, with -പരമായ.

The word, taken apart.
അടിസ്ഥാനപരമായ (aṭisthānaparamāya) is built on അടിസ്ഥാനം, "a basis", which joins native aṭi, "sole, bottom" (DEDR 72), to Sanskrit sthāna, "place" (CDIAL 13753), with -പരമായ, "concerned with".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for crucial, then the Malayalam for fundamental.

You'll want to know: ഗുരുതരമായ.
ഗുരുതരമായ — gurutaramāya — "serious; grave".
അത് ഗുരുതരമായ ഒരു തെറ്റായിരുന്നു. atŭ gurutaramāya oru teṟṟāyirunnu. That was a serious mistake.
Said of an illness or a mistake, not of a person's mood.

The word, taken apart.
ഗുരുതരമായ (gurutaramāya) is built from Sanskrit gurutara, "heavier, very heavy" (Monier-Williams), the comparative of guru, "heavy", with the adjective ending -ആയ: a grave matter weighs heavy.

സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ (saṅkīrṇṇamāya) — complex; complicated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for fundamental, then the Malayalam for serious.

You'll want to know: സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ.
സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ — saṅkīrṇṇamāya — "complex; complicated".
ഇത് വളരെ സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ ഒരു പ്രശ്നമാണ്. itŭ vaḷare saṅkīrṇṇamāya oru praśnamāṇŭ. This is a very complex problem.
The opposite of ലളിതമായ, simple.

The word, taken apart.
സങ്കീർണ്ണമായ (saṅkīrṇṇamāya) is built from Sanskrit saṁkīrṇa, "poured together, mixed, confused" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ആയ: a complex thing has many parts mixed.
