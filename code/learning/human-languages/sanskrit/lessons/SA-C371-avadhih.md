## You'll want to know: अवधिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C371-VIKALPA371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधिः** — *avadhiḥ* — "a time limit; a term".

> यदि अवधिः श्वः अस्ति, तर्हि अद्य एव कार्यं करोतु।
>
> *yadi avadhiḥ śvaḥ asti, tarhi adya eva kāryaṃ karotu.*
>
> If the deadline is tomorrow, do the work today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधिः** (*avadhiḥ*) is built on *ava*, "down", and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives *ava-dhi* "a term, a limit" and "a conclusion" (MW).
