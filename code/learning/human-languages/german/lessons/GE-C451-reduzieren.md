## You'll want to know: reduzieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C451-SCHNAEPPCHEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**reduzieren** — "to reduce".

> Im Januar reduziert das Kaufhaus alle Preise.
>
> *In January the department store reduces all its prices.*

Past participle without *ge-*: *reduziert* — reduced, on price tags.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reduzieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *redūcere* 'to lead back, to bring back' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the German word.
