## You'll want to know: એકાંત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C458-VIRAH458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**એકાંત** — *ekā̃t* — "solitude".

> દાદાને સાંજે એકાંત ગમે છે.
>
> *dādāne sā̃je ekā̃t game che.*
>
> Grandfather likes solitude in the evenings.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**એકાંત** (*ekā̃t*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ekānta*, "a lonely or retired place", as Monier-Williams records it.
