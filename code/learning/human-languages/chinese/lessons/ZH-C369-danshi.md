# 但是 (dànshì) — but; however

## You'll want to know: 但是
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C369-CLEAR369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**但是** — *dànshì* — "but; however".

> **我觉得他很好但是我不喜欢他** *wǒ juéde tā hěn hǎo dànshì wǒ bù xǐhuan tā* — I think he's nice, but I don't like him.

The new character, in pieces: 但 — seven strokes in two pieces: the person piece on the left, two strokes; on the right 日 *rì*, four strokes, over a long bar, five strokes in all. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**但是** (*dànshì*) is built from *dàn* and 是, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *danX* and *dzyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a particle and 'only', and the second 'right, as against wrong'. How the pair came to mean but is not settled.
