## You'll want to know: સાધુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C335-SADHU335-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સાધુ** — *sādhu* — "a holy man".

> ગામ બહાર એક સાધુ રહેતો હતો.
>
> *gām bahār ek sādhu raheto hato.*
>
> A holy man lived outside the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાધુ** (*sādhu*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sādhu*, "straight, good", and as a noun "a holy man" (Monier-Williams). He gives up the world and wanders, or lives apart.
