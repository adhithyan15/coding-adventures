## You'll want to know: o milénio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C276-QUINZENA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o milénio** — "the millennium".

> Esta igreja tem quase um milénio.
>
> *This church is almost a thousand years old.*

In Brazil: *milênio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*milénio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *millennium* (EtymDB), a word made from *mīlle* "a thousand" and *annus* "year".
