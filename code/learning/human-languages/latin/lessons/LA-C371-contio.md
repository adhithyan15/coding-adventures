## You'll want to know: cōntiō, cōntiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C371-ROSTRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cōntiō, cōntiōnis** — "a public meeting; a speech made to it".

> Cōnsul cōntiōnem habuit.
>
> *The consul held a public meeting.*

*cōntiō, cōntiōnis* f. A meeting of the people called to hear a magistrate, where no vote was taken; also the speech made there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōntiō* is contracted from an old form *coventiō* "a coming together" (Lewis & Short), built from *con-* and *veniō* "to come".
