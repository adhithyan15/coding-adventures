## You'll want to know: നിർണ്ണായകമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C445-GAURAVAM445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**നിർണ്ണായകമായ** — *nirṇṇāyakamāya* — "crucial; decisive".

> നാളത്തെ യോഗം വളരെ നിർണ്ണായകമാണ്.
>
> *nāḷatte yōgaṁ vaḷare nirṇṇāyakamāṇŭ.*
>
> Tomorrow's meeting is very crucial.

As a predicate: നിർണ്ണായകമാണ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നിർണ്ണായകമായ** (*nirṇṇāyakamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *nirṇāyaka*, "settling, conclusive" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ആയ: what settles the matter.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
