## You'll want to know: ತೋಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C509-ELUBU509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೋಳು** — *tōḷu* — "an arm; the upper arm".

> ಅಪ್ಪ ಮಗುವನ್ನು ತೋಳಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಎತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡರು.
>
> *appa maguvannu tōḷinalli ettikoṇḍaru.*
>
> Father picked the baby up in his arms.

Plural: ತೋಳುಗಳು, *tōḷugaḷu*.

ಕೈ is the hand or the whole arm; ತೋಳು is from shoulder to elbow, and also a sleeve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೋಳು** (*tōḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3564 lists Kannada *tōḷ(u)*, "the arm", beside Tamil *tōḷ*, "shoulder, arm".
