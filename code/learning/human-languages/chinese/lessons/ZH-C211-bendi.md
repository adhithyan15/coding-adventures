## You'll want to know: 本地
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C211-LOCAL211-01]; assesses=[] -->

**本地** — *běndì* — "local".

> **我们找一个本地人问路** *wǒmen zhǎo yí ge běndì rén wènlù* — Let's find someone local and ask the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**本地** (*běndì*) is built from 本 'root, one's own' and 地 'place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pwonX* and *dijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the root of a tree and 'the beginning'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
