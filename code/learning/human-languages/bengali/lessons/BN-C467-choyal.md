## You'll want to know: চোয়াল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C467-THOT467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**চোয়াল** — *choyāl* — "the jaw".

> শক্ত মাংস চিবিয়ে আমার চোয়াল ব্যথা করছে।
>
> *shôktô māngsô chibiye āmār choyāl bæthā kôrchhe.*
>
> Chewing the tough meat has made my jaw ache.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চোয়াল** (*choyāl*) is a Bengali word for the jaw whose origin is not settled.
