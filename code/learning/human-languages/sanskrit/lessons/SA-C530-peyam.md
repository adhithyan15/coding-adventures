## You'll want to know: पेयम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C530-BHOJYA530-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पेयम्** — *peyam* — "a drink".

> यदि ग्रीष्मः अस्ति, तर्हि शीतलं पेयं ददातु।
>
> *yadi grīṣmaḥ asti, tarhi śītalaṃ peyaṃ dadātu.*
>
> If it is summer, please give a cold drink.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेयम्** (*peyam*) comes from the root √pā, "to drink", which also gives *pibati*; MW gives *peya* as to be drunk, drinkable (MW).
