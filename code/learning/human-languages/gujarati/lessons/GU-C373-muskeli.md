## You'll want to know: મુશ્કેલી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C373-UPAY373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મુશ્કેલી** — *muśkelī* — "a difficulty".

> જો કોઈ મુશ્કેલી આવશે તો મને ફોન કરો.
>
> *jo koī muśkelī āvśe to mane phon karo.*
>
> If any difficulty comes up, phone me.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુશ્કેલી** (*muśkelī*) is usually traced to Persian *mushkil*, "difficult", a word of Arabic origin, with the Gujarati ending *-ī*.
