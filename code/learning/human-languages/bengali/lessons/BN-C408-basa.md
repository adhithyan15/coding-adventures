## You'll want to know: বাসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C408-BASA408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বাসা** — *bāsā* — "a rented home; lodgings; a nest".

> শহরে মীরা একটা ছোট বাসা ভাড়া নিল।
>
> *shôhôre mīrā ekṭā chhoṭo bāsā bhāṛā nilô.*
>
> In the city Mira rented a small flat.

Also a bird's nest: পাখির বাসা. বাড়ি is the family home; বাসা is where you live now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাসা** (*bāsā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *bāsā*, "a lodging-house, a nest, a lair", under Sanskrit *vāsá*, "a dwelling" (CDIAL 11591).
