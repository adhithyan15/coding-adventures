## You'll want to know: ついでに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C347-HOUMON347-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ついでに** — *tsuideni* — "while you are at it".

> **ゆうびんきょくに いったら、ついでに きってを かって ください。** — *yūbinkyoku ni ittara, tsuideni kitte o katte kudasai* — "If you go to the post office, buy some stamps while you are at it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ついでに** (*tsuideni*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
