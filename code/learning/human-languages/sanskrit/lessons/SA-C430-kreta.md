## You'll want to know: क्रेता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C430-HATTA430-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रेता** — *kretā* — "a buyer".

> क्रेता हट्टे मूल्यं पृच्छति।
>
> *kretā haṭṭe mūlyaṃ pṛcchati.*
>
> The buyer asks the price at the bazaar.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रेता** (*kretā*) is built on the root √krī, "to buy", which also gives *krīṇāti*, "buys", with the agent ending *-tṛ*; MW gives *kretṛ* as a buyer, a purchaser (MW).
