## You'll want to know: aciēs, aciēī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C339-PROELIUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aciēs, aciēī** — "a battle line; a sharp edge".

> Dux aciem ante castra posuit.
>
> *The leader drew up the battle line in front of the camp.*

Feminine, 5th declension, like *diēs*. First a sharp edge, then the sharp front of an army.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**aciēs** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "sharp" (EtymDB): the edge of a blade, then the front edge of an army. English *acid* comes from the same root.
