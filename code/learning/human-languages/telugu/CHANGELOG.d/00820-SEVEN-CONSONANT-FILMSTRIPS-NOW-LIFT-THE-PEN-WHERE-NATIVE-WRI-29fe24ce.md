## Seven consonant filmstrips now lift the pen where native writers do

- The filmstrips for భ (TE-S128), థ (TE-S130), ఞ (TE-S132), ఢ (TE-S139),
  మ (TE-S150), ధ (TE-S151) and ట (TE-S152) had a pen lift between every
  numbered movement of their tracing guide. They now lift only where native
  writers do, between the body and its detached parts: ఞ, థ and ఢ in three
  strokes, and మ, ట, ధ and భ in two. In HP Labs India's native-writer
  samples, 78% to 94% of writers use those counts. Every movement and its
  direction is still shown, frame by frame, and each caption fits on two
  lines.
