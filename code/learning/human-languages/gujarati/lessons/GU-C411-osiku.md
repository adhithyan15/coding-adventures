## You'll want to know: ઓશીકું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C411-QUILT411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓશીકું** — *ośīkũ* — "a pillow; a cushion for the head".

> મને નરમ ઓશીકું ગમે છે.
>
> *mane naram ośīkũ game che.*
>
> I like a soft pillow.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓશીકું** (*ośīkũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *ucchīrṣaka*, "a pillow", from a form for raising the head; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 1853).
