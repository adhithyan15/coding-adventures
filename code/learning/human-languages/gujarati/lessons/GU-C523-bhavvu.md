## You'll want to know: ભાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C523-FARSAN523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાવવું** — *bhāvvũ* — "to be to one's taste".

> મને ઊંધિયું ઘણું ભાવે છે.
>
> *mane ū̃dhiyũ ghaṇũ bhāve che.*
>
> I really love undhiyu.

Past (it …): તે શાક તેને ભાવ્યું, *te śāk tene bhāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાવવું** (*bhāvvũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *bhāti*, "shines", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to please, be agreeable to" (CDIAL 9445). Said of food, the eater takes **ને** and the dish is the subject: **મને ઊંધિયું ભાવે છે**.
