## You'll want to know: म्लायति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C303-DURBHIKSHA303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**म्लायति** — *mlāyati* — "withers; fades".

> वर्षां विना पुष्पाणि म्लायन्ति।
>
> *varṣāṃ vinā puṣpāṇi mlāyanti.*
>
> Without rain the flowers wither.

Past (it …): पुष्पं म्लानवत्, *puṣpaṃ mlānavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**म्लायति** (*mlāyati*) comes from the root √mlai, "to fade, wither, decay", which MW also gives as to be languid or exhausted (MW).
