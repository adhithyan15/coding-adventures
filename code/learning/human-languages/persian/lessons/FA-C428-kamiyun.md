## You'll want to know: کامیون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C428-KAMIYUN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کامیون** (*kâmiyun*) — "a lorry (US: a truck)".

> کامیونها شب در جاده حرکت میکنند.
>
> *Kâmiyun-hâ shab dar jâdde harekat mikonand.*
>
> The lorries travel on the road at night.

A lorry driver is a *kâmiyundâr* or *rânande-ye kâmiyun*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kâmiyun* was borrowed from French *camion* 'truck, lorry' (EtymDB).
