# ਪਾਰ (pār) — across; on the far side

## You'll want to know: ਪਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C264-CROSS264-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਰ** — *pār* — "across; on the far side".

> ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਪੁਲ ਦੇ ਪਾਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *saṭeshan pul de pār hai.*
>
> The station is across the bridge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਰ** (*pār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pāra*, "further bank, furthest end"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pār*, "beyond", under it (CDIAL 8100).
