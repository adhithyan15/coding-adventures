## You'll want to know: zhèngfǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C374-SOCIETY374-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngfǔ* — "government".

> *zhèngfǔ yīnggāi guānxīn lǎo rén* — The government should look after old people.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngfǔ* is built from *zhèng* 'governing' and *fǔ* 'an office', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyengH* and *pjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to set right' and the second 'an office of state' and 'a store of documents'.
