Mandarin Chinese, chapter 117: Which Way?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dōngbian — the east; on the east side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for umbrella, then the Mandarin for to lose.

You'll want to know: dōngbian.
dōngbian — "the east; on the east side".
chēzhàn zài dōngbian — The station is on the east side.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dōngbian is built from dōng 'east' and 边 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tuwng and pen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary calls the east the direction of spring, and repeats an old reading of the character as the sun inside a tree.

What you've built.
One more word for asking where things are.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dōngbian]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dōngbian, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: chēzhàn zài dōngbian — The station is on the east side.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for umbrella, then the Mandarin for to lose, then say dōngbian again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does dōngbian mean? ("The east; on the east side".) Say it once more.

fāngxiàng — "direction; way".

The word, taken apart.
fāngxiàng is built from fāng 'side, region' and xiàng 'to face', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjang and xjangH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the side you face.
