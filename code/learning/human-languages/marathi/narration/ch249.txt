Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for toys, then the Marathi for a doll.

You'll want to know: पतंग.
पतंग — pataṅg — "a kite".

The word, taken apart.
पतंग (pataṅg) is usually traced to Sanskrit pataṅga, "bird, flying insect" (CDIAL 7721), a word CDIAL thinks was crossed with a non-Aryan one. CDIAL lists no Marathi form.

Lesson 5 of 5.
भोवरा (bhovrā) — a spinning top.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a doll, then the Marathi for a kite.

You'll want to know: भोवरा.
भोवरा — bhovrā — "a spinning top".

The word, taken apart.
भोवरा (bhovrā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhramara, "moving unsteadily, revolving; spinning top" (CDIAL 9650). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "whorl of hair".
