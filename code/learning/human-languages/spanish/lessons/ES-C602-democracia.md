## You'll want to know: la democracia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C602-VOTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la democracia** — "the democracy".

> La democracia necesita participación.
>
> *Democracy needs participation.*

*Democrático* is the adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*democracia* is a learned borrowing from Greek *dēmokratia*, built from *dēmos* 'the people' and *kratos* 'power' (EtymDB).
