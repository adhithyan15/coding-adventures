Hindi, chapter 539: Wishes and Prayers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तीर्थ (tīrth) — a place of pilgrimage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be decorated, then the Hindi for to pick out, to pick over (grain).

You'll want to know: तीर्थ.
तीर्थ — tīrth — "a place of pilgrimage".
दादी हर साल तीर्थ पर जाती हैं। Dādī har sāl tīrth par jātī haiṁ. Every year my grandmother goes on a pilgrimage.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
तीर्थ (tīrth) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit tīrtha, "a ford; a place of pilgrimage" (Monier-Williams): a holy place where one crosses over.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ठानना (ṭhānnā) — to resolve; to make up one's mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a prayer, then the Hindi for a wish (for someone's good).

You'll want to know: ठानना.
ठानना — ṭhānnā — "to resolve; to make up one's mind".
मीरा ने ठान लिया कि वह तैरना सीखेगी। Mīrā ne ṭhān liyā ki vah tairnā sīkhegī. Mira made up her mind that she would learn to swim.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ठान लिया, usne ṭhān liyā.

The word, taken apart.
ठानना (ṭhānnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit sthāna, "a firm stance, a place" (CDIAL 13753). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to settle, determine".
