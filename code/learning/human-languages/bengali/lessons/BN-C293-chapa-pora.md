# চাপা পড়া (chāpā pôṛā) — to be trapped underneath; to be buried under

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for to collapse, then the Bengali for destruction.

## You'll want to know: চাপা পড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C293-BHUMIKOMPO293-05]; assesses=[] -->

**চাপা পড়া** — *chāpā pôṛā* — "to be trapped underneath; to be buried under".

> ভাঙা বাড়ির নিচে কিছু মানুষ চাপা পড়ল।
>
> *bhāngā bāṛir niche kichhu mānush chāpā pôṛlô.*
>
> Some people were trapped under the collapsed house.

Past (he/she …): সেই লোক দেয়ালের নিচে চাপা পড়ল, *sei lok deyāler niche chāpā pôṛlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চাপা পড়া** (*chāpā pôṛā*) puts চাপা, 'pressed down' — CDIAL 4674, a Middle Indo-Aryan *capp-*, which lists Bengali *cāpā*, 'to press' — before পড়া: to fall under a weight.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
