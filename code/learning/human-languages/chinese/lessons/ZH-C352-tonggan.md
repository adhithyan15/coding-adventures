## You'll want to know: 同感
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C352-AGREE352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**同感** — *tónggǎn* — "the same feeling".

> **我也有同感** *wǒ yě yǒu tónggǎn* — I feel the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**同感** (*tónggǎn*) is built from 同 'the same' and 感 'to feel', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duwng* and *komX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'equal' and 'together', and the second 'to move'.
