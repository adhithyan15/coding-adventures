## You'll want to know: বিস্ফোরণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C279-SHIKHA279-05]; assesses=[] -->

**বিস্ফোরণ** — *bisphorôn* — "an explosion".

> রাতে কারখানায় একটা বিস্ফোরণ হল।
>
> *rāte kārkhānāy ekṭā bisphorôn hôlô.*
>
> There was an explosion at the factory last night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিস্ফোরণ** (*bisphorôn*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *vi-*, 'apart', and *sphoraṇa*, 'throbbing, quivering' (Monier-Williams); Sanskrit itself has the near word *visphoṭa*, 'a crash'. বিস্ফোরণ হওয়া, an explosion happens.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
