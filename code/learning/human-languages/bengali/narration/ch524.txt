Lesson 2 of 5.
জলরং (jôlrông) — watercolour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to swim across, then the Bengali for an easel.

You'll want to know: জলরং.
জলরং — jôlrông — "watercolour".
শিল্পী জলরঙে একটা নদীর ছবি আঁকলেন। Shilpī jôlrônge ekṭā nôdīr chhôbi ā̃klen. The artist painted a river in watercolour.
Before a vowel ending ং is written ঙ: জলরঙে, in watercolour.

The word, taken apart.
জলরং (jôlrông) joins জল, "water", to রং, "colour", a word that matches both Sanskrit raṅga (CDIAL 10560) and Persian rang: colour mixed with water.

Lesson 4 of 5.
খোদাই করা (khodāi kôrā) — to carve; to engrave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for watercolour, then the Bengali for a pattern.

You'll want to know: খোদাই করা.
খোদাই করা — khodāi kôrā — "to carve; to engrave".
কারিগর কাঠে একটা ফুল খোদাই করল। Kārigôr kāṭhe ekṭā phul khodāi kôrlô. The craftsman carved a flower into the wood.
Past (he/she …): তিনি পাথরে নাম খোদাই করলেন, tini pāthôre nām khodāi kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
খোদাই করা (khodāi kôrā) joins খোদাই, "carving", built on a verb CDIAL lists as Bengali khodā, "to dig, to carve", from a reconstructed khōdd, "dig" (CDIAL 3934), to করা, "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
রাঙানো (rāngāno) — to colour; to dye.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pattern, then the Bengali for to carve.

You'll want to know: রাঙানো.
রাঙানো — rāngāno — "to colour; to dye".
শিল্পী নতুন বাড়ির দেওয়াল রাঙালেন। Shilpī nôtun bāṛir deoyāl rāngālen. The artist coloured the walls of the new house.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কাপড়টা রাঙালেন, tini kāpṛṭā rāngālen.

The word, taken apart.
রাঙানো (rāngāno) comes from a reconstructed raṅgayati, "dyes", built on Sanskrit raṅga, "colour"; CDIAL lists Bengali rāṅgāna, "to redden" (CDIAL 10570).
