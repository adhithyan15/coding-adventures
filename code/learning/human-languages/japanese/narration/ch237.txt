Japanese, chapter 237: Photos and Collections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しゃしん (shashin) — a photo; a photograph.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for music, then the Japanese for a musical performance.

You'll want to know: しゃしん.
しゃしん — shashin — "a photo; a photograph".
きのう かぞくの しゃしんを みました。 — kinō kazoku no shashin o mimashita — "Yesterday I looked through family photos."

The word, taken apart.
しゃしん (shashin) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are sha, "copy" (Middle Chinese sjaeX) and shin, "true" (tsyin) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
とる (toru) — to photograph; to take (a photo).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a musical performance, then the Japanese for a photo.

You'll want to know: とる.
とる — toru — "to photograph; to take (a photo)".
せんげつ やまで しゃしんを とりました。 — sengetsu yama de shashin o torimashita — "Last month I took photos in the mountains."
Polite とります, torimasu; a godan verb.

The word, taken apart.
とる (toru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
