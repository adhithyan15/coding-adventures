## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-PAY-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-PAISA-01, MW-LEX-LO-01] -->

> *paisā le lo* — **take the money**

*paisā* has been yours since Chapter 14 and has had nothing to do until now.
Here it is what you are handing over, and *le lo* is the taking, said as two
short beats that run together in ordinary speech.

Say the three turns you already have, then this one. That is a whole purchase,
from asking to see a thing to paying for it, and every word in it was taught
before this chapter except *lo*.
