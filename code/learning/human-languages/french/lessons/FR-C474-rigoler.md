## You'll want to know: rigoler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C474-GAIETE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**rigoler** — "to laugh, to have fun (informal)".

> On a bien rigolé pendant la soirée.
>
> *We had a good laugh at the party.*

Informal. *Tu rigoles !* — you are joking!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rigoler* goes back to Old French *rigoler* "to amuse oneself". The origin is not settled; a link with *rire* is often suggested.
