## You'll want to know: おれい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C304-ONGAESHI304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おれい** — *orei* — "thanks; a thank-you gift".

> **ははに おれいを いいました。** — *haha ni orei o iimashita* — "I said thank you to my mother."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おれい** (*orei*) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: *o-*, "the polite prefix", is native Japanese, and *rei*, "thanks", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *lejX*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
