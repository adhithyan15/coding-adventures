## You'll want to know: لمع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C550-SIYANA550-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لمع** — *lammaʿa* — "to polish".

> لمع أبي حذاءه قبل الحفلة.
>
> *lammaʿa abī ḥidhāʾahu qabla al-ḥafla.*
>
> My father polished his shoes before the party.

Present: يلمع, *yulammiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لمع** (*lammaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-m-ʿ*, "to shine, to gleam" (Lane). Lāmiʿ, shiny, which you know, is from the same root; Form II is to make a thing shine.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
