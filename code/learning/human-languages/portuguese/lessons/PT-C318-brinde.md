## You'll want to know: o brinde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C318-FESTEJAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o brinde** — "the toast (with drinks); a free gift".

> No fim do jantar, fizemos um brinde aos noivos.
>
> *At the end of dinner we drank a toast to the bride and groom.*

In shops, *um brinde* is a free gift.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brinde* goes back to a German toast, *ich bring dir’s* "I bring it to you" (EtymDB), which reached Portuguese through Spanish and Italian.
