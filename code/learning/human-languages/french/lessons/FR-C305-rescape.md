## You'll want to know: le rescapé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C305-NAUFRAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le rescapé** — "the survivor (of a disaster)".

> Les rescapés ont raconté leur nuit en mer.
>
> *The survivors told the story of their night at sea.*

Someone saved from a disaster: *les rescapés du naufrage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rescapé* is usually explained as a northern dialect form of *réchappé* "escaped", built on *échapper* "to escape", which comes from Old French *eschaper* (EtymDB).
