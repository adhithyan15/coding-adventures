## You'll want to know: ゆびわ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C150-GLOVES150-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆびわ** — *yubiwa* — "a ring (for a finger)".

> **ははの ゆびわは うつくしいです。** — *haha no yubiwa wa utsukushii desu* — "My mother's ring is beautiful."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆびわ** (*yubiwa*) is native Japanese, built from *yubi*, "finger", and *wa*, "ring, circle", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
