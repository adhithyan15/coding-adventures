## You'll want to know: さえぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C391-OUDAN391-05]; assesses=[] -->

**さえぎる** — *saegiru* — "to block; to obstruct".

> **くるまが みちを さえぎっていました。** — *kuruma ga michi o saegitte imashita* — "A car was blocking the road."

Polite **さえぎります**, *saegirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **くるまが どうろを さえぎりました。**, *kuruma ga dōro o saegirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さえぎる** (*saegiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
