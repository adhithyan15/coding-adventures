## You'll want to know: પરબીડિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C200-POST200-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પરબીડિયું** — *parbīḍiyũ* — "envelope".

> **પત્ર પરબીડિયામાં છે.** — *patra parbīḍiyāmā̃ che* — "The letter is in the envelope."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **પરબીડિયું** (*parbīḍiyũ*) is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it, and EtymDB lists no source.
