## You'll want to know: قرعہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C532-QISMAT532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قرعہ** — *qurā* — "a draw (of lots)".

> اگر قرعے میں میرا نام آیا تو میں جاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar qure meṅ merā nām āyā to maiṅ jāūṅ gā.*
>
> If my name comes up in the draw, I'll go.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرعہ** (*qurā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qurʿa*, "a lot drawn to decide something".
