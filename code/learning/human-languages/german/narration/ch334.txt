German, chapter 334: Zustimmen und zugeben.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
zustimmen — to agree (with).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to object, then the German for to deny.

You'll want to know: zustimmen.
zustimmen — "to agree (with)".
Ich stimme dir zu: Wir sollten früher losfahren. I agree with you: we should set off earlier.
Separable, with the dative: ich stimme dir zu, sie hat zugestimmt. Einem Plan zustimmen — to approve a plan.

The word, taken apart.
zustimmen is built from zu "to" and stimmen, a verb made from Stimme "voice, vote" (EtymDB): you add your voice to an idea.

Lesson 3 of 5.
einverstanden — agreed, in agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to agree, then the German for the approval.

You'll want to know: einverstanden.
einverstanden — "agreed, in agreement".
Meine Eltern sind einverstanden: Ich darf am Samstag zur Party gehen. My parents agree: I am allowed to go to the party on Saturday.
Mit etwas einverstanden sein — to agree to something. On its own, Einverstanden! means OK, agreed.

The word, taken apart.
einverstanden is the old participle of sich einverstehen "to reach an understanding", built on verstehen "to understand", from Old High German firstān and Proto-Germanic frastāną (EtymDB), and so on stehen "to stand". EtymDB lists the parts, not the compound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
zugeben — to admit (a fault).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the approval, then the German for agreed.

You'll want to know: zugeben.
zugeben — "to admit (a fault)".
Er gibt zu, dass er den Termin vergessen hat. He admits that he forgot the appointment.
Separable and strong: er gibt zu, er hat zugegeben. Zugegeben, … — admittedly, …

The word, taken apart.
zugeben is built from zu and geben "to give", inherited from Old High German geban and Proto-Germanic gebaną (EtymDB): you give a point to the other side.

Lesson 5 of 5.
einräumen — to concede; to put away (into a cupboard).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for agreed, then the German for to admit.

You'll want to know: einräumen.
einräumen — "to concede; to put away (into a cupboard)".
Der Trainer räumt ein, dass seine Mannschaft schlecht gespielt hat. The coach concedes that his team played badly.
Separable: er räumt ein, er hat eingeräumt. Literally to give room to something: die Teller in den Schrank einräumen.

The word, taken apart.
einräumen is built on räumen "to make room", inherited from Old High German rūmen and Proto-Germanic rūmijaną (EtymDB), from Raum "room": you give the other side some room.
