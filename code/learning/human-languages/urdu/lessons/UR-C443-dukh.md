## You'll want to know: دکھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C443-SAD443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دکھ** — *dukh* — "sorrow; distress".

> دوست کے جانے کا مجھے دکھ ہے۔
>
> *dost ke jāne kā mujhe dukh hai.*
>
> I am sad that my friend has gone.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دکھ** (*dukh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *duḥkhá*, "difficulty, pain" (CDIAL 6375).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
