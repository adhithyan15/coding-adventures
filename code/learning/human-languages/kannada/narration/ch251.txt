Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗಡಿ (gaḍi) — a frontier, a boundary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a post office, then the Kannada for a valley.

You'll want to know: ಗಡಿ.
ಗಡಿ — gaḍi — "a frontier, a boundary".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ಗಡಿ (gaḍi) is uncertain. DEDR has no entry for it in the sense "boundary", CDIAL does not record it, and no source for it is settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕಾಲುವೆ (kāluve) — a canal, a channel.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಲುವೆ.
ಕಾಲುವೆ — kāluve — "a canal, a channel".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಲುವೆ (kāluve) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kāl (DEDR 1480). DEDR lists kāluve, "water-course, channel". DEDR 1480 compares Sanskrit khalla.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸುರಂಗ (suraṅga) — a tunnel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a frontier, then the Kannada for a canal.

You'll want to know: ಸುರಂಗ.
ಸುರಂಗ — suraṅga — "a tunnel".

The word, taken apart.
ಸುರಂಗ (suraṅga) is usually traced to Sanskrit suruṅgā, "an underground passage" (CDIAL 13506), a word often thought to be borrowed from Greek syrinx, "pipe, channel".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಇಳಿಜಾರು (iḷijāru) — a downward slope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a canal, then the Kannada for a tunnel.

You'll want to know: ಇಳಿಜಾರು.
ಇಳಿಜಾರು — iḷijāru — "a downward slope".

The word, taken apart.
ಇಳಿಜಾರು (iḷijāru) is native Dravidian: iḷi, "to come down" (DEDR 502), and jāru, "to slip, slide", which DEDR 2360 lists as jari, jaragu: "a sliding down".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹಿತ್ತಿಲು (hittilu) — a backyard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tunnel, then the Kannada for a downward slope.

You'll want to know: ಹಿತ್ತಿಲು.
ಹಿತ್ತಿಲು — hittilu — "a backyard".

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿತ್ತಿಲು (hittilu) is native Dravidian, built on the root for "behind", which DEDR 4205 lists as older Kannada pin, pindu: the yard behind the house. DEDR does not list hittilu itself.
