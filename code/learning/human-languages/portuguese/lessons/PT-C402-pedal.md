## You'll want to know: o pedal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C402-VOLANTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o pedal** — "the pedal".

> Carrego no pedal do travão devagar.
>
> *I press the brake pedal gently.*

*Carregar no pedal* — to press the pedal. A bicycle has two pedals too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pedal* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pedālis* 'of the foot' (EtymDB), built on *pēs* 'foot' (Lewis & Short).
