Tamil, chapter 93: How Do You Say It?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அர்த்தம் (arttam) — meaning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for one minute, then the Tamil for please wait.

You'll want to know: அர்த்தம்.
அர்த்தம் (arttam) — "meaning".
இதன் அர்த்தம் (arttam) என்ன? — "what does this mean?", the question that turns anyone into a teacher.

What you've built.
The question that unlocks a word.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: arttam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: arttam, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask itaṉ arttam eṉṉa?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for one minute, then the Tamil for please wait, then say arttam again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does அர்த்தம் (arttam) mean? ("Meaning".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
தமிழில் (tamiḻil) — in Tamil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for please wait, then the Tamil for meaning.

You'll want to know: தமிழில்.
தமிழில் (tamiḻil) — "in Tamil".
-இல் is "in", added to the name of the language. ஆங்கிலத்தில் would be "in English".

What you've built.
A way to name the language you want.

Lesson 3 of 5.
எப்படிச் சொல்வது? (eppaṭic colvatu?) — how do you say it?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for meaning, then the Tamil for in Tamil.

You'll want to know: எப்படிச் சொல்வது?
எப்படிச் சொல்வது? (eppaṭic colvatu?) — "how does one say it?".
தமிழில் (tamiḻil) இதை எப்படிச் சொல்வது? (eppaṭic colvatu?) — "how do you say this in Tamil?". எப்படி is the "how" you know.

What you've built.
The question that lets you learn from anyone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
காட்டுங்கள் (kāṭṭuṅkaḷ) — please show me.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for in Tamil, then the Tamil for how do you say it.

You'll want to know: காட்டுங்கள்.
காட்டுங்கள் (kāṭṭuṅkaḷ) — "please show me".
When a word will not come, point and say காட்டுங்கள் (kāṭṭuṅkaḷ).

What you've built.
A request for when words run out.

Lesson 5 of 5.
கேட்கவில்லை (kēṭkavillai) — I did not hear it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for how do you say it, then the Tamil for please show me.

You'll want to know: கேட்கவில்லை.
கேட்கவில்லை (kēṭkavillai) — "I did not hear (it)".
It ends in இல்லை, the "no" of your first chapter. Pair it with சத்தமாக.

What you've built.
