## You'll want to know: रिवाज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C332-PARAMPARA332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रिवाज** — *rivāj* — "a custom".

> हमारे गाँव में त्योहार पर नए कपड़े पहनने का रिवाज है।
>
> *hamāre gā̃v meṁ tyohār par nae kapṛe pahanne kā rivāj hai.*
>
> In our village it is the custom to wear new clothes for a festival.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रिवाज** (*rivāj*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rawāj*, "currency, being in use": what is current in a place. **का रिवाज है** is it is the custom to.
