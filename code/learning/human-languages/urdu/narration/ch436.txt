Urdu, chapter 436: Nature.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قدرت (qudrat) — nature; the natural world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a seashell, then the Urdu for a dip (under water).

You'll want to know: قدرت.
قدرت — qudrat — "nature; the natural world".
یہ وادی قدرت کا تحفہ ہے۔ yih vādī qudrat kā tohfā hai. This valley is a gift of nature.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
قدرت (qudrat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qudra, "power, might"; in Urdu it is also the natural world.

پھولوں کی خوشبو کمرے تک آتی ہے۔ phūloṅ kī khushbū kamre tak ātī hai. The scent of the flowers reaches the room.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
خوشبو (khushbū) is built from Persian khush, "pleasant", and bū, "a smell": it is usually traced to Persian khushbū; بدبو is its opposite.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for nature, then the Urdu for a fragrance.

You'll want to know: کونپل.
کونپل — koṅpal — "a new shoot; an opening bud".
بہار میں درختوں پر کونپلیں آتی ہیں۔ bahār meṅ darakhtoṅ par koṅpaleṅ ātī haiṅ. In spring new shoots appear on the trees.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
کونپل (koṅpal) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kõpal, "an opening bud", under Sanskrit kuḍmalá, "a bud" (CDIAL 3250).

Lesson 5 of 5.
مرجھانا (murjhānā) — to wilt; to fade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a new shoot, then the Urdu for a petal.

You'll want to know: مرجھانا.
مرجھانا — murjhānā — "to wilt; to fade".
گرمی میں پودے مرجھا گئے۔ garmī meṅ paude murjhā gae. The plants wilted in the heat.
Past (it …): وہ پھول مرجھا گیا, voh phūl murjhā gayā.

The word, taken apart.
مرجھانا (murjhānā) is an everyday word whose source the dictionaries we use do not give: CDIAL has no entry for it. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
