## You'll want to know: acusar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C386-ACUSACAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**acusar** — "to accuse".

> Acusaram o vizinho de roubar a bicicleta.
>
> *They accused the neighbour of stealing the bicycle.*

*Acusar alguém de* + noun or infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acusar* is inherited from Latin *accūsāre* "to call to account, to accuse" (EtymDB), built from *ad* and *causa* "cause, lawsuit" (Lewis & Short).
