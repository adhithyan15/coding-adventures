# ಪ್ರಸಂಗ (prasaṅga) — an episode; an incident

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಸಂಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C343-NIRUPANE343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಸಂಗ** — *prasaṅga* — "an episode; an incident".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿ ತಮ್ಮ ಬಾಲ್ಯದ ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಸಂಗ ಹೇಳಿದರು.
>
> *ajji tamma bālyada ondu prasaṅga hēḷidaru.*
>
> Grandmother told an episode from her childhood.

Plural: ಪ್ರಸಂಗಗಳು, *prasaṅgagaḷu*.

A short happening worth telling, from life or from a story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಸಂಗ** (*prasaṅga*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prasaṅga*, "attachment, connection, an occasion" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
