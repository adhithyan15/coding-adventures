## You'll want to know: el resfriado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C733-GRIPE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el resfriado** — "the cold (illness)".

> Tengo un resfriado fuerte.
>
> *I have a bad cold.*

In Spain also *el catarro*; in parts of South America *el resfrío*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*resfriado* is built on *resfriarse* "to catch cold", from *frío* "cold", which is usually traced to Latin *frīgidus*.
