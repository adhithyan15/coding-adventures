# લહેર (laher) — a wave; a ripple

## You'll want to know: લહેર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C442-SEA442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લહેર** — *laher* — "a wave; a ripple".

> દરિયાની લહેરો કિનારા પર આવે છે.
>
> *dariyānī lahero kinārā par āve che.*
>
> The sea's waves come up on the shore.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લહેર** (*laher*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *laharī*, "a wave"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 10999).
