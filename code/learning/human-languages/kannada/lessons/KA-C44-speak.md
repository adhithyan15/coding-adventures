## You'll want to know: ಮಾತಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-SPEAK]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾತಾಡು** (*mātāḍu*) — "speak".

Built from *māta*, 'word' — itself from Sanskrit — plus *āḍu*, 'to play'. To speak is to play with words.
