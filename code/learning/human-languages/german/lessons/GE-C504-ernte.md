## You'll want to know: die Ernte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C504-SAEEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ernte** — "harvest".

> Die Ernte war dieses Jahr sehr gut.
>
> *The harvest was very good this year.*

Plural: *die Ernten*. The verb: *ernten* — to harvest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ernte* is usually traced to Old High German *arnōt* "harvest", built on an old Germanic word for the harvest season; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
