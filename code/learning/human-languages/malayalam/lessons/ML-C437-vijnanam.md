## You'll want to know: വിജ്ഞാനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C437-GANITAM437-04]; assesses=[] -->

**വിജ്ഞാനം** — *vijñānaṁ* — "knowledge; learning".

> പുസ്തകങ്ങൾ വിജ്ഞാനത്തിന്റെ ഉറവിടമാണ്.
>
> *pustakaṅṅaḷ vijñānattinṟe uṟaviṭamāṇŭ.*
>
> Books are a source of knowledge.

An encyclopedia is വിജ്ഞാനകോശം, a treasury of knowledge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിജ്ഞാനം** (*vijñānaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vijñāna*, "the act of discerning, understanding" (CDIAL 11707), from the root *jñā*, "to know".
