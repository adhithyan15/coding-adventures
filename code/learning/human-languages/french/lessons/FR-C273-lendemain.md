## You'll want to know: le lendemain
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C273-VEILLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le lendemain** — "the next day, the day after".

> Le lendemain, il a téléphoné pour s'excuser.
>
> *The next day, he phoned to apologise.*

It looks forward from a day in the past; from today, say *demain*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lendemain* grew out of *l'endemain*, built on *demain*, which is inherited from Latin *dē māne* "from the morning" (Lewis & Short).
