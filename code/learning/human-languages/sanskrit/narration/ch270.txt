Sanskrit, chapter 270: Two modern-technology words, retrieved twice.
2 lessons. Both can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 2.
Two modern-technology words: round one — independently retrieve computer and internet without a visible model.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Prepare to retrieve two familiar nouns from their English meanings.

Guided Practice: independent retrieval.
No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.
Say the Sanskrit for these meanings, in order: a computer, then the internet.
[pause 8 seconds]

What you've built: check after answering.
Now check: संगणकम् (saṅgaṇakam) is “a computer,” and अन्तर्जालम् (antarjālam) is “the internet.”

Wrap-up Recall: boundary.
You have completed one two-word retrieval. It shows recall of two nouns, not a claim about using either technology.


Lesson 2 of 2.
Two modern-technology words: round two — independently retrieve internet and computer in reverse order.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Prepare to retrieve the same two meanings in a fresh order.

Guided Practice: independent retrieval in a fresh order.
No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.
Say the Sanskrit for these meanings in the reverse order: the internet, then a computer.
[pause 8 seconds]

What you've built: check after answering.
Now check: अन्तर्जालम् (antarjālam) is “the internet,” and संगणकम् (saṅgaṇakam) is “a computer.”

Wrap-up Recall: boundary.
You can retrieve two modern-technology nouns twice without a visible model. This is not evidence of device use, internet navigation, digital literacy, technological competence, human validation, learner attainment, or exam readiness.
