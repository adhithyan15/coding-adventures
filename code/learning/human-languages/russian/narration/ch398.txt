Russian, chapter 398: Renting and insurance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
аренда (arénda) — rent, a lease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for paid, then the Russian for profitable.

You'll want to know: аренда.
аренда (arénda) — "rent, a lease".
Аренда квартиры в центре очень дорогая. Arénda kvartíry v tséntre óchen dorogáya. Renting a flat in the centre is very expensive.
Feminine. Сдавать в аренду — to let; брать в аренду — to rent.

The word, taken apart.
аренда (arénda) was borrowed from Polish arenda, from Medieval Latin arrenda (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin reddere "to give back, to pay".

Lesson 2 of 5.
арендовать (arendovát) — to rent, to lease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for profitable, then the Russian for rent.

You'll want to know: арендовать.
арендовать (arendovát) — "to rent, to lease".
Мы арендуем маленький офис. My arendúyem málenkiy ófis. We rent a small office.
Imperfective and perfective; present арендую, арендуешь. For a flat, people usually say снимать.

The word, taken apart.
арендовать (arendovát) is built on аренда (arénda) "a lease" with the verb suffix -овать; the noun came through Polish arenda from Medieval Latin (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ипотека (ipotéka) — a mortgage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for rent, then the Russian for to rent.

You'll want to know: ипотека.
ипотека (ipotéka) — "a mortgage".
Они купили квартиру в ипотеку. Oní kupíli kvartíru v ipotéku. They bought a flat with a mortgage.
Feminine. Взять ипотеку — to take out a mortgage.

The word, taken apart.
ипотека (ipotéka) is usually traced to Greek hypothḗkē "a pledge, a deposit", from hypotithénai "to put under, to pledge": the flat is put down as a pledge.

Lesson 4 of 5.
страховать (strakhovát) — to insure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to rent, then the Russian for a mortgage.

You'll want to know: страховать.
страховать (strakhovát) — "to insure".
Мы страхуем дом от пожара. My strakhúyem dom ot pozhára. We insure the house against fire.
Imperfective; present страхую, страхуешь; perfective застраховать. Страховать от + genitive.

The word, taken apart.
страховать (strakhovát) is built on страх "fear", inherited from Proto-Slavic: to insure is to guard against what you fear.
