## You'll want to know: die Auswirkung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C344-EINFLUSS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Auswirkung** — "the impact, the effect (on something)".

> Die neue Straße hat Auswirkungen auf das ganze Dorf.
>
> *The new road has an impact on the whole village.*

Mostly plural: *die Auswirkungen auf* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Auswirkung* is the noun of *sich auswirken*, built on *wirken* "to work", inherited from Old High German *wirken* and Proto-Germanic *wirkijaną* (EtymDB).
