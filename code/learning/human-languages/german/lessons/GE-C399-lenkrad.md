## You'll want to know: das Lenkrad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C399-LENKRAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Lenkrad** — "the steering wheel".

> Mein Vater hält das Lenkrad mit beiden Händen.
>
> *My father holds the steering wheel with both hands.*

Plural: *die Lenkräder*. Also *das Steuer*: *am Steuer sitzen* — to be at the wheel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Lenkrad* is a compound of *lenken* 'to steer' and *Rad* 'wheel' (EtymDB lists it under *Rad*). Both parts are inherited Germanic words.
