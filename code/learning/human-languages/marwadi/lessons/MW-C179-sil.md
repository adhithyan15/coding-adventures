## You'll want to know: सिल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C179-PISO179-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिल** — *sil* — "a grinding stone".

> रसोई मांय एक भारी सिल है।
>
> *rasoī mā̃y ek bhārī sil hai.*
>
> There is a heavy grinding stone in the kitchen.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिल** (*sil*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śilā*, "a rock", also "the lower millstone"; CDIAL lists Hindi *sil*, "a flat grinding stone" (CDIAL 12459). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
