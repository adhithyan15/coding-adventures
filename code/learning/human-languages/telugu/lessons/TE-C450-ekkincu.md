# ఎక్కించు (ekkiñcu) — to put aboard; to load

## You'll want to know: ఎక్కించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C450-PARKINGU450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎక్కించు** — *ekkiñcu* — "to put aboard; to load".

> నాన్న పిల్లలను బస్సు ఎక్కించాడు.
>
> *nānna pillalanu bassu ekkiñcāḍu.*
>
> Father put the children on the bus.

Past (I …): ఎక్కించాను, *ekkiñcānu*.

The causative of ఎక్కు, to climb on, to board: to make someone or something get on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎక్కించు** (*ekkiñcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 766 lists Telugu *ekkincu*, "to cause to ascend", beside *ekku*, "to climb, to get on".
