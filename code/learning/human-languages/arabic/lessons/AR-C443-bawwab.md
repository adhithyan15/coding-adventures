## You'll want to know: بواب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C443-MULAA443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بواب** — *bawwāb* — "a doorman; a caretaker".

> حمل البواب أمتعتنا إلى المصعد.
>
> *ḥamala al-bawwāb amtiʿatanā ilā al-miṣʿad.*
>
> The doorman carried our luggage to the lift.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بواب** (*bawwāb*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-w-b*, "a door" (Lane). The trade pattern *faʿʿāl* makes him the man of the door.
