Portuguese, chapter 71: Never, Now, Afterwards, Early, Slowly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
nunca — never.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a minute, then the Portuguese for always.

You'll want to know: nunca.
nunca — "never".

The word, taken apart.
nunca from Latin numquam, "never" (EtymDB 2.1), from ne, "not", and umquam, "ever" — inherited.

Lesson 5 of 5.
devagar — slowly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for afterwards, then the Portuguese for early.

You'll want to know: devagar.
devagar — "slowly". Ask for it when someone speaks fast.

The word, taken apart.
devagar is built from de, "at", and an old noun for leisure, from Latin vacare, "to be free, to be idle" (Lewis & Short): "at leisure".
