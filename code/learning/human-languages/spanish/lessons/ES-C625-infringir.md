## You'll want to know: infringir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C625-LEGAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**infringir** — "to break, to infringe".

> Infringió las normas del club.
>
> *He broke the club rules.*

Formal, for rules and laws. Spelling: *infrinjo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*infringir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *infringere* 'to break, to weaken', built from *in* and *frangere* 'to break' (EtymDB).
