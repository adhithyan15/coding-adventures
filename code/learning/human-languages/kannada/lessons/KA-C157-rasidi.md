## You'll want to know: ರಸೀದಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C157-ODU157-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಸೀದಿ** — *rasīdi* — "a receipt". Ask for it when you pay: **ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ರಸೀದಿ ಕೊಡಿ** — *dayaviṭṭu rasīdi koḍi* — "The receipt, please."
