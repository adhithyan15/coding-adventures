Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 261: Aunts, Nephews and Grandsons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मासी (māsī) — a mother's sister; an aunt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a sister's husband, then the Marwadi for a wife's brother.

You'll want to know: मासी.
मासी — māsī — "a mother's sister; an aunt".
म्हारी मासी गांव मांय रैवै है। mhārī māsī gā̃v mā̃y raivai hai. My mother's sister lives in the village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मासी (māsī) is inherited from Sanskrit mātuḥṣvasṛ, "a mother's sister". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi māsī (CDIAL 10001).
