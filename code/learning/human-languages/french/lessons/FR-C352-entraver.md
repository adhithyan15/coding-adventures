## You'll want to know: entraver
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C352-FREIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**entraver** — "to hinder, to hamper".

> La neige entrave le travail des pompiers.
>
> *The snow is hampering the firefighters.*

Formal. *Une entrave* was a chain on an animal's legs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entraver* is usually explained as built from *en-* and Old French *tref* "beam", from Latin *trabs* "a beam" (Lewis & Short): a piece of wood tied to the legs. The details are not settled.
