# সান্ত্বনা (sāntvônā) — consolation; comfort

## You'll want to know: সান্ত্বনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C446-SANTVONA446-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সান্ত্বনা** — *sāntvônā* — "consolation; comfort".

> বন্ধুরা এসে মীরাকে সান্ত্বনা দিল।
>
> *bôndhurā ese mīrāke sāntvônā dilô.*
>
> Her friends came and consoled Mira.

সান্ত্বনা দেওয়া, to console.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সান্ত্বনা** (*sāntvônā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sāntvanā*, "appeasing, soothing with kind words" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
