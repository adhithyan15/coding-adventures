## You'll want to know: नख़रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C462-ABHIMAN462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नख़रा** — *nakhrā* — "fussiness; airs and graces".

> मेरी बहन खाने में बहुत नख़रे करती है।
>
> *merī bahan khāne meṁ bahut nakhre kartī hai.*
>
> My sister is very fussy about food.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नख़रा** (*nakhrā*) is usually traced to Persian *nakhra*, "coquetry, airs". *nakhre karnā* is to make a fuss.
