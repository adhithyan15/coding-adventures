## You'll want to know: خنده دار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C530-STORIES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خنده دار** (*khandedâr*) — "funny".

> این فیلم خیلی خنده دار بود.
>
> *in film kheyli khandedâr bud.*
>
> This film was very funny.

*khande* 'laughter' + *dâr* 'having'. *bâmaze* is 'cute, witty'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khandedâr* is built on native Persian *khande*, 'laughter', from *khandidan*, 'to laugh', and *dâr*, the stem of *dâshtan*, 'to have'.
