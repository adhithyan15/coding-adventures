## You'll want to know: probābilis, probābile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C359-PONDERO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**probābilis, probābile** — "likely, credible; worth approving".

> Tua causa mihi probābilis vidētur.
>
> *Your case seems credible to me.*

*probābilis, probābile*: two endings. Built on *probō*, to approve, to test: a thing you can approve. For a view that is likely but not certain. English *probable*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*probābilis* is built on *probō* "to approve, to test" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which comes from *probus* "good, upright". English *probable* comes from it.
