## You'll want to know: гореть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C295-POZHAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**гореть** (*gorét*) — "to burn, to be on fire".

> В лесу горела трава.
>
> *V lesú goréla travá.*
>
> Grass was burning in the forest.

Imperfective; present *горю*, *горишь*. Perfective *сгореть* (*sgorét*) — to burn down. Of a light: *свет горит* — the light is on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гореть* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *gorěti* "to burn", which EtymDB traces to PIE *gʷʰer-* "warm, hot".
