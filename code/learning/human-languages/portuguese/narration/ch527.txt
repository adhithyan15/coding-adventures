Portuguese, chapter 527: Atrás das câmaras.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o realizador — (film) director.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for subtitled, then the Portuguese for dubbing.

You'll want to know: o realizador.
o realizador — "(film) director".
O realizador ganhou um prémio pelo filme. The director won a prize for the film.
In Brazil: o diretor.

The word, taken apart.
realizador is built on realizar "to carry out, to make", which is usually traced to real "real" and so to Latin rēs "a thing".

Lesson 3 of 5.
o guião — script, screenplay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for (film) director, then the Portuguese for to film.

You'll want to know: o guião.
o guião — "script, screenplay".
O guião do filme é baseado num romance. The film's script is based on a novel.
In Brazil: o roteiro.

The word, taken apart.
guião is linked in EtymDB with guia "a guide": the text that guides the film. The verb guiar is usually traced to a Germanic word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
o documentário — documentary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to film, then the Portuguese for script.

You'll want to know: o documentário.
o documentário — "documentary".
Vimos um documentário sobre os oceanos. We watched a documentary about the oceans.
Um documentário sobre — a documentary about.

The word, taken apart.
documentário is built on documento "a document", from Latin documentum "a lesson, a proof" (Lewis & Short), from docēre "to teach".

Lesson 5 of 5.
o elenco — cast (of a film or play).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for script, then the Portuguese for documentary.

You'll want to know: o elenco.
o elenco — "cast (of a film or play)".
O elenco do filme é quase todo português. The cast of the film is almost all Portuguese.
Also a list or a squad: o elenco da equipa.

The word, taken apart.
elenco comes from Late Latin elenchus (EtymDB), from Greek élenkhos "a scrutiny, a test"; the sense "a list" grew up later.
