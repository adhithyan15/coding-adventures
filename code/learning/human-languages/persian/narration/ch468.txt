Persian, chapter 468: Mountains and caves.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غار (ghâr) — a cave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for grass, then the Persian for to prune.

You'll want to know: غار.
غار (ghâr) — "a cave".
در کوه یک غار تاریک پیدا کردیم. Dar kuh yek ghâr-e târik peydâ kardim. We found a dark cave in the mountains.
Note the letter غ, said gh.

The word, taken apart.
ghâr was borrowed from Arabic ġār 'grotto, cave' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
دامنه (dâmane) — a slope, a mountainside; a range (of a thing).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to prune, then the Persian for a cave.

You'll want to know: دامنه.
دامنه (dâmane) — "a slope, a mountainside; a range (of a thing)".
روستا در دامنه کوه است. Rustâ dar dâmane-ye kuh ast. The village is on the mountainside.
Built on dâman 'skirt, hem': the skirt of the mountain.

The word, taken apart.
dâmane is built on native dâman 'skirt, hem', with the ending -e: the hem of a mountain. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گردنه (gardane) — a mountain pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cave, then the Persian for a slope.

You'll want to know: گردنه.
گردنه (gardane) — "a mountain pass".
در زمستان گردنه پر از برف است. Dar zemestân gardane por az barf ast. In winter the pass is full of snow.
Built on gardan 'neck': the neck between two peaks.

The word, taken apart.
gardane is a native word, which EtymDB lists; it is built on gardan 'neck', the narrow neck of land between two heights.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کوهپایه (kuhpâye) — the foothills.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a slope, then the Persian for a mountain pass.

You'll want to know: کوهپایه.
کوهپایه (kuhpâye) — "the foothills".
شهر ما در کوهپایه است و هوایش خنک است. Shahr-e mâ dar kuhpâye ast va havâyash khonak ast. Our town is in the foothills and its air is cool.
kuh 'mountain' + pâye 'base, foot'.

The word, taken apart.
kuhpâye is a native compound of kuh 'mountain', from Middle Persian kōf (EtymDB), and pâye 'foot, base', from pâ 'foot'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
صعود کردن (so'ud kardan) — to climb, to ascend (a mountain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a mountain pass, then the Persian for the foothills.

You'll want to know: صعود کردن.
صعود کردن (so'ud kardan) — "to climb, to ascend (a mountain)".
کوهنوردها صبح زود به قله صعود کردند. Kuhnavard-hâ sobh-e zud be qolle so'ud kardand. The climbers climbed to the summit early in the morning.
More formal than bâlâ raftan; the top takes be.

The word, taken apart.
so'ud kardan is built from so'ud 'ascent', a loan usually traced to Arabic ṣuʿūd, and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
