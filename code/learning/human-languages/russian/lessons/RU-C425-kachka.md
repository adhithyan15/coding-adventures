## You'll want to know: качка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C425-KACHKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**качка** (*káchka*) — "the rolling (of a ship), the swell".

> Из-за качки мама не могла спать.
>
> *Iz-za káchki máma ne moglá spat.*
>
> Because of the rolling Mum could not sleep.

Feminine. Built on *качать* — to rock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*качка* is built on *качать* 'to rock, to swing'; EtymDB lists both words without a deeper source.
