# অভিমান (ôbhimān) — hurt pride; a loving sulk

## You'll want to know: অভিমান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C444-OBHIMAN444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিমান** — *ôbhimān* — "hurt pride; a loving sulk".

> বোনের জন্মদিনে আমি ফোন করলাম না, তাই তার অভিমান হল।
>
> *boner jônmôdine āmi phon kôrlām nā, tāi tār ôbhimān hôlô.*
>
> I didn't phone on my sister's birthday, so she was hurt.

অভিমান is felt between people who love each other: the sulk of a child, a sister or a wife, waiting to be coaxed round. অভিমানী, quick to feel it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিমান** (*ôbhimān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *abhimāna*, "a high opinion of oneself, pride" (Monier-Williams); Bengali turned it into the wounded pride of someone slighted by a person they love.
