Persian, chapter 364: Facts and rumours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
واقعیت (vâqe'iyat) — a fact; reality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be proven, then the Persian for evidence.

You'll want to know: واقعیت.
واقعیت (vâqe'iyat) — "a fact; reality".
این یک واقعیت است، نه یک نظر. in yek vâqe'iyat ast, na yek nazar. This is a fact, not an opinion.
Plural vâqe'iyathâ. Dar vâqe' "in fact" shares its base.

The word, taken apart.
واقعیت (vâqe'iyat) is built on Arabic wāqi' "happening, real" with the abstract ending -iyat; it is usually traced to Arabic wāqi'iyya "reality".

Lesson 2 of 5.
حقیقت (haqiqat) — the truth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for evidence, then the Persian for a fact.

You'll want to know: حقیقت.
حقیقت (haqiqat) — "the truth".
بالاخره حقیقت را به ما گفت. bâlâkhare haqiqat râ be mâ goft. In the end she told us the truth.
Dar haqiqat — in truth. Plural haqâyeq, an Arabic broken plural.

The word, taken apart.
حقیقت (haqiqat) comes from Arabic ḥaqīqa "truth, reality" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as ḥaqq "right, true", the source of Persian haq.

Lesson 3 of 5.
در واقع (dar vâqe') — in fact, actually.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fact, then the Persian for the truth.

You'll want to know: در واقع.
در واقع (dar vâqe') — "in fact, actually".
در واقع او برادر من نیست، پسرعموی من است. dar vâqe' u barâdar-e man nist, pesar'amu-ye man ast. In fact he is not my brother; he is my cousin.
Dar "in" + vâqe' "real". It corrects or sharpens what came before, and takes a whole clause.

The word, taken apart.
در واقع (dar vâqe') joins the inherited Persian preposition dar "in" to vâqe', usually traced to Arabic wāqi' "happening, real": in what really happens.

Lesson 4 of 5.
شایعه (shâye'e) — a rumour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the truth, then the Persian for in fact.

You'll want to know: شایعه.
شایعه (shâye'e) — "a rumour".
این فقط یک شایعه است. in faqat yek shâye'e ast. This is only a rumour.
Plural shâye'ât or shâye'ehâ. Shâye'e dorost kardan — to start a rumour.

The word, taken apart.
شایعه (shâye'e) was borrowed from Arabic shā'i'a "hearsay, rumour" (EtymDB), literally something that spreads.

Lesson 5 of 5.
انکار کردن (enkâr kardan) — to deny.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for in fact, then the Persian for a rumour.

You'll want to know: انکار کردن.
انکار کردن (enkâr kardan) — "to deny".
او همه چیز را انکار کرد. u hame chiz râ enkâr kard. He denied everything.
Enkâr "denial" + kardan (stem kon-). The object takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
انکار was borrowed from Arabic inkār "denial" (EtymDB); joined to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do", it makes a compound verb.
