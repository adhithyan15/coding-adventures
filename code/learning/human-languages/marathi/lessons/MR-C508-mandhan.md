## You'll want to know: मानधन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C508-PAGAR508-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मानधन** — *māndhan* — "an honorarium; a fee".

> भाषणासाठी लेखकाला मानधन मिळालं.
>
> *bhāṣṇāsāṭhī lekhakālā māndhan miḷālaṁ.*
>
> The writer got a fee for the talk.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मानधन** (*māndhan*) is built from two tatsamas, Sanskrit *māna*, "honour", and *dhana*, "wealth" (Monier-Williams): money given as a mark of respect.
