## You'll want to know: bricoler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bricoler** — "to do DIY, to tinker".

> Ma tante aime bricoler dans son garage.
>
> *My aunt likes tinkering in her garage.*

*Un bricoleur*, *une bricoleuse* — a handy person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bricoler* is built on *bricole*, once a catapult and later a small job. The earlier origin is not settled.
