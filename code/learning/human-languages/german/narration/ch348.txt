German, chapter 348: Wer ist verantwortlich?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Verantwortung — the responsibility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to avoid, then the German for why.

You'll want to know: die Verantwortung.
die Verantwortung — "the responsibility".
Die Eltern tragen die Verantwortung für ihre Kinder. Parents bear responsibility for their children.
Die Verantwortung übernehmen — to take responsibility.

The word, taken apart.
Verantwortung is the noun of verantworten "to answer for", built on Antwort "answer", from Old High German antwurti and Proto-Germanic andawurdiją (EtymDB), which is built on Wort "word".

Lesson 3 of 5.
rechtfertigen — to justify (oneself).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the responsibility, then the German for responsible.

You'll want to know: rechtfertigen.
rechtfertigen — "to justify (oneself)".
Er musste sich vor seinem Chef rechtfertigen. He had to justify himself to his boss.
Inseparable, although it looks like a compound: er rechtfertigt sich, er hat sich gerechtfertigt.

The word, taken apart.
rechtfertigen is built from recht "right", from Proto-Germanic rehtaz (EtymDB), and fertigen "to make ready", from fertig: to set something right.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: die Ausrede.
die Ausrede — "the excuse".
Das ist doch nur eine Ausrede! That is only an excuse!
Plural: die Ausreden. Eine Ausrede finden — to find an excuse.

The word, taken apart.
Ausrede is built from aus "out" and Rede "speech", from the family of reden "to talk", Old High German redōn (EtymDB): talk that gets you out of trouble.

Lesson 5 of 5.
deswegen — that is why, for that reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to justify, then the German for the excuse.

You'll want to know: deswegen.
deswegen — "that is why, for that reason".
Ich habe verschlafen. Deswegen bin ich zu spät gekommen. I overslept. That is why I was late.
The verb comes right after it, as after deshalb: deswegen bin ich ….

The word, taken apart.
deswegen joins des, the old genitive "of that", to wegen "because of", which grew from von … wegen "on the part of". EtymDB has the word but no history for it.
