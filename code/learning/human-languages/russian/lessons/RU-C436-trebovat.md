## You'll want to know: требовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C436-VOZVRAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**требовать** (*trébovat*) — "to demand".

> Клиент требует вернуть деньги.
>
> *Kliyént trébuyet vernút déngi.*
>
> The customer demands his money back.

Imperfective; perfective *потребовать*. Present *требую*. Takes the genitive or an infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*требовать* is usually linked with the old word *треба* 'a need; an offering'. EtymDB lists the verb without a source, so the link is hedged; it takes *треба* from Church Slavonic *trěba*.
