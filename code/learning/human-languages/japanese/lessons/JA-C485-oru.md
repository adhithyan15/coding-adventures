## You'll want to know: おる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C485-CLOTH485-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おる** — *oru* — "to weave".

> **そぼは きものの ぬのを おります。** — *sobo wa kimono no nuno o orimasu* — "My grandmother weaves cloth for kimono."

Polite **おります**, *orimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そぼが ぬのを おりました。**, *sobo ga nuno o orimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おる** (*oru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
