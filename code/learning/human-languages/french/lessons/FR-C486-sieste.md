## You'll want to know: la sieste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C486-NUITS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la sieste** — "nap, siesta".

> Après le déjeuner, mon grand-père fait la sieste.
>
> *After lunch, my grandfather has a nap.*

*Faire la sieste* — to have a nap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la sieste* was borrowed from Spanish *siesta* (EtymDB), from Latin *sexta* (*hōra*) "the sixth hour", that is noon.
