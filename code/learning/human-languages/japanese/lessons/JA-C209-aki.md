## You'll want to know: あき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C209-SEASON209-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あき** — *aki* — "autumn".

> **らいねんの あきに りょこうに いきます。** — *rainen no aki ni ryokō ni ikimasu* — "I will go on a trip next autumn."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あき** (*aki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
