Marathi, chapter 487: Burns and Rashes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चटका बसणे (caṭkā basṇe) — to get a burn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sprain, then the Marathi for to swell.

You'll want to know: चटका बसणे.
चटका बसणे — caṭkā basṇe — "to get a burn".
गरम तव्याला हात लागला आणि चटका बसला. garam tavyālā hāt lāglā āṇi caṭkā baslā. My hand touched the hot griddle and I got burnt.
Past (to him …): त्याला चटका बसला, tyālā caṭkā baslā.
Dative: मला चटका बसला. Also a sudden pang of grief.

The word, taken apart.
चटका बसणे (caṭkā basṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on चटका, which CDIAL lists as Marathi caṭkā, "glow", under caṭa (CDIAL 4570), with बसणे.

Lesson 4 of 5.
भाजणे (bhājṇe) — to roast; to get burnt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an abscess, then the Marathi for a rash.

You'll want to know: भाजणे.
भाजणे — bhājṇe — "to roast; to get burnt".
स्वयंपाक करताना ती भाजली. svayampāk kartānā tī bhājlī. She got burnt while cooking.
Past (he …): तो भाजला, to bhājlā.
Of people, to get burnt; of food, to roast: शेंगदाणे भाजणे (bhājṇe).

The word, taken apart.
भाजणे (bhājṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi bhājṇẽ, "fries, parches", under Sanskrit bhṛjjati (CDIAL 9583).

Lesson 5 of 5.
खाजवणे (khājavṇe) — to scratch (an itch).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rash, then the Marathi for to roast.

You'll want to know: खाजवणे.
खाजवणे — khājavṇe — "to scratch (an itch)".
डास चावल्यावर तो पाय खाजवत होता. ḍās cāvlyāvar to pāy khājavat hotā. After the mosquito bit him he was scratching his leg.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाय खाजवला, tyāne pāy khājavlā.
खाज, an itch: खाज येणे, to itch, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
खाजवणे (khājavṇe) is a Marathi verb built on खाज, "itch", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit kharju, "itching, scab" (CDIAL 3827).
