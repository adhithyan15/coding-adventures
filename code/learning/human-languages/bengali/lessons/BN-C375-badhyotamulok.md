## You'll want to know: বাধ্যতামূলক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C375-ONUMOTI375-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বাধ্যতামূলক** — *bādhyôtāmūlôk* — "compulsory".

> গাড়ি চালালে লাইসেন্স রাখা বাধ্যতামূলক।
>
> *gāṛi chālāle lāisens rākhā bādhyôtāmūlôk.*
>
> If you drive, you must carry a licence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাধ্যতামূলক** (*bādhyôtāmūlôk*) is a modern Bengali formation: Sanskrit *bādhya*, 'to be pressed or constrained', made abstract with *-tā*, and *mūlaka*, 'rooted in' (Monier-Williams): rooted in obligation, required.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
