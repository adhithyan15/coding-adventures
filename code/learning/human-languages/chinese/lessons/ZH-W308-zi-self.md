## Script — a short sweep over a tall frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZI-SELF-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 自

**Two pieces: a short sweep on top, a tall frame under it.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The sweep, one stroke**: short, down to the left, at the top middle.

**The frame, five strokes**, built like 日: a vertical down the left; a bar across the top that turns down the right side; two bars inside; then the bar that closes the foot.

1 + 5 = six.
