## You'll want to know: گزارش دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C308-GOZARESH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گزارش دادن** (*gozâresh dâdan*) — "to report".

> راننده تصادف را به پلیس گزارش داد.
>
> *Rânande tasâdof râ be polis gozâresh dâd.*
>
> The driver reported the crash to the police.

Present stem *dah-*: *gozâresh midahad*. The thing reported takes *râ*; the person told takes *be*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gozâresh dâdan* joins the native noun *gozâresh* "a report" with *dâdan* "to give", which EtymDB takes back to the Indo-European root *deh₃-*: to give a report.
