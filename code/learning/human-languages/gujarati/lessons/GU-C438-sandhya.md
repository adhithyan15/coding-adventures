# સંધ્યા (sandhyā) — dusk; the evening twilight

## You'll want to know: સંધ્યા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C438-DUSK438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સંધ્યા** — *sandhyā* — "dusk; the evening twilight".

> સંધ્યાનું આકાશ ગુલાબી છે.
>
> *sandhyānũ ākāś gulābī che.*
>
> The sky at dusk is pink.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંધ્યા** (*sandhyā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṁdhyā*, "a joining", the meeting of day and night (Monier-Williams).
