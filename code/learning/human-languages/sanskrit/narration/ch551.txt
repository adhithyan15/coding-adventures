Sanskrit, chapter 551: Giving Something Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जहाति (jahāti) — leaves behind; abandons.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for puts to use, then the Sanskrit for a handing out.

You'll want to know: जहाति.
जहाति — jahāti — "leaves behind; abandons".
यदि सः आलस्यं जहाति, तर्हि सः सुखी भविष्यति। yadi saḥ ālasyaṃ jahāti, tarhi saḥ sukhī bhaviṣyati. If he leaves laziness behind, he will be happy.
Past (he …): सः गृहं हीनवान्, saḥ gṛhaṃ hīnavān.

The word, taken apart.
जहाति (jahāti) comes from the root √hā, "to leave, to abandon", whose present is jahāti; its past participle is hīna (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अनुशोचति (anuśocati) — regrets.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a handing out, then the Sanskrit for leaves behind.

You'll want to know: अनुशोचति.
अनुशोचति — anuśocati — "regrets".
यदि त्वं न पठसि, तर्हि पश्चात् अनुशोचसि। yadi tvaṃ na paṭhasi, tarhi paścāt anuśocasi. If you don't study, you will regret it later.
Past (he …): सः पश्चात् अनुशोचितवान्, saḥ paścāt anuśocitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुशोचति (anuśocati) is built on anu, "after", and the root √śuc, "to grieve", which also gives śoka, "grief"; MW gives anuśuc as to mourn over, to regret (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: पश्चात्तापः.
पश्चात्तापः — paścāttāpaḥ — "remorse".
यदि त्वं सत्यं वदसि, तर्हि पश्चात्तापः न भविष्यति। yadi tvaṃ satyaṃ vadasi, tarhi paścāttāpaḥ na bhaviṣyati. If you tell the truth, there will be no remorse.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पश्चात्तापः (paścāttāpaḥ) is built from paścāt, "after", and tāpa, "heat, pain", from the root √tap; MW gives paścāttāpa as after-pain, sorrow, regret, repentance (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विरक्तः (viraktaḥ) — detached; indifferent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for regrets, then the Sanskrit for remorse.

You'll want to know: विरक्तः.
विरक्तः — viraktaḥ — "detached; indifferent".
यदि सः धनात् विरक्तः अस्ति, तर्हि सः सुखेन जीवति। yadi saḥ dhanāt viraktaḥ asti, tarhi saḥ sukhena jīvati. If he does not care for wealth, he lives happily.

The word, taken apart.
विरक्तः (viraktaḥ) is built on vi, "away", and the root √rañj, "to be coloured, to be attached", as its past participle; MW gives virakta as changed in feeling, averse, indifferent (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उत्सृजति (utsṛjati) — lets go of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for remorse, then the Sanskrit for detached.

You'll want to know: उत्सृजति.
उत्सृजति — utsṛjati — "lets go of".
यदि त्वं क्रोधम् उत्सृजसि, तर्हि मनः शान्तं भविष्यति। yadi tvaṃ krodham utsṛjasi, tarhi manaḥ śāntaṃ bhaviṣyati. If you let go of your anger, your mind will be calm.
Past (he …): सः कपोतम् उत्सृष्टवान्, saḥ kapotam utsṛṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
उत्सृजति (utsṛjati) is built on ud, "out", and the root √sṛj, "to let go, to emit"; MW gives utsṛj as to let loose, to let go, to set free; its past participle is utsṛṣṭa (MW).
