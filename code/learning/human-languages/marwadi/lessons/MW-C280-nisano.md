## You'll want to know: निसाणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C280-MAIDAN280-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निसाणो** — *nisāṇo* — "a target; an aim".

> निसाणो दूर है।
>
> *nisāṇo dūr hai.*
>
> The target is far away.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निसाणो** (*nisāṇo*) is usually traced to Persian *nishāna*, "a mark, a target", built on *nishān*, "a sign"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
