Gujarati, chapter 242: Harvest time.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
પાકવું (pākvũ) — to ripen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to uproot, then the Gujarati for to die.

You'll want to know: પાકવું.
પાકવું — pākvũ — "to ripen".
ખેતરમાં ઘઉં પાક્યા. — khetarmā̃ ghaũ pākyā — "The wheat ripened in the field."
The adjective is પાકું, ripe.

The word, taken apart.
પાકવું (pākvũ) is a tadbhava. It is built on Sanskrit pakva, "cooked, ripe", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to become ripe" (CDIAL 7621).

Lesson 3 of 7.

You'll want to know: ચૂંટવું.
ચૂંટવું — cū̃ṭvũ — "to pick, to pluck".
મેં બગીચામાં ફૂલ ચૂંટ્યાં. — mẽ bagīcāmā̃ phūl cū̃ṭyā̃ — "I picked flowers in the garden."

The word, taken apart.
ચૂંટવું (cū̃ṭvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to pluck out, select", under cuṇṭati, a by-form of Sanskrit cuṭati, "cuts off", in a family it takes to be of non-Aryan origin (CDIAL 4857).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pick, then the Gujarati for to dig.

You'll want to know: ગધેડો.
ગધેડો — gadheḍo — "donkey".
સાંજે ગધેડો ગામમાં પાછો આવ્યો. — sā̃je gadheḍo gāmmā̃ pācho āvyo — "In the evening the donkey came back to the village."

The word, taken apart.
ગધેડો (gadheḍo) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit gardabha, "ass", where CDIAL lists Gujarati gaddho, "ass"; gadheḍo adds the suffix -ḍo (CDIAL 4054).
