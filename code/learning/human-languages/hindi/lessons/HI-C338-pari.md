## You'll want to know: परी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C338-PARI338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परी** — *parī* — "a fairy".

> कहानी में एक परी आसमान से आई।
>
> *kahānī meṁ ek parī āsmān se āī.*
>
> In the story a fairy came down from the sky.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced to Persian *parī*, "a fairy", a winged spirit of Persian tales. A very beautiful girl is also called a **परी**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
