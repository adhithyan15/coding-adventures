## You'll want to know: ڈاکٹر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C471-HEALTH471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈاکٹر** — *ḍākṭar* — "a doctor".

> ڈاکٹر نے مجھے دوا دی۔
>
> *ḍākṭar ne mujhe davā dī.*
>
> The doctor gave me some medicine.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈاکٹر** (*ḍākṭar*) is borrowed from English *doctor*.
