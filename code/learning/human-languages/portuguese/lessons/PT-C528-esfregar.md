## You'll want to know: esfregar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C528-LIMPEZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**esfregar** — "to scrub, to rub".

> Esfreguei o chão da casa de banho.
>
> *I scrubbed the bathroom floor.*

*Esfregar os olhos* — to rub your eyes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esfregar* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *exfricāre* (EtymDB), built on Latin *fricāre* "to rub".
