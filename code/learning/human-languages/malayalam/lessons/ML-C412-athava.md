## You'll want to know: അഥവാ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C412-CURUKKAM412-03]; assesses=[] -->

**അഥവാ** — *athavā* — "or; in other words".

> ചായ അഥവാ കാപ്പി, ഏതെങ്കിലും മതി.
>
> *cāya athavā kāppi, ēteṅkiluṁ mati.*
>
> Tea or coffee, either will do.

A bookish or. It can also mean if by chance: അഥവാ അവൻ വന്നാൽ, if he happens to come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അഥവാ** (*athavā*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *athavā*, "or" (CDIAL 231), from *atha*, "then", and *vā*, "or".
