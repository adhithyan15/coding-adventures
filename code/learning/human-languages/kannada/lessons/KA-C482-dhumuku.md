# ಧುಮುಕು (dhumuku) — to plunge; to leap down

## You'll want to know: ಧುಮುಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C482-TORE482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಧುಮುಕು** — *dhumuku* — "to plunge; to leap down".

> ಜಲಪಾತದ ನೀರು ಬಂಡೆಯಿಂದ ಕೆಳಗೆ ಧುಮುಕುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *jalapātada nīru baṇḍeyinda keḷage dhumukuttade.*
>
> The water of the falls plunges down from the rock.

Past (I …): ಧುಮುಕಿದೆ, *dhumukide*.

Of water falling and of a diver: ನೀರಿಗೆ ಧುಮುಕು, to dive into water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಧುಮುಕು** (*dhumuku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3326 lists Kannada *dumuku*, "to leap or jump down from above"; the modern spelling writes it with the aspirate ಧ.
