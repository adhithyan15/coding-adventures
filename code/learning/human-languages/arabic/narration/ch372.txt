Arabic, chapter 372: Obstacles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عقبة (ʿaqaba) — an obstacle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to contribute, then the Arabic for an incentive.

You'll want to know: عقبة.
عقبة — ʿaqaba — "an obstacle".
كانت اللغة أكبر عقبة أمامه. kānat al-lugha akbar ʿaqaba amāmah. Language was the biggest obstacle in his way.

The word, taken apart.
عقبة (ʿaqaba) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-q-b, "to come after, to follow" (Lane). ʿAqaba first named a steep mountain road; in modern use it is any obstacle.

Lesson 2 of 5.
صعوبة (ṣuʿūba) — a difficulty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an incentive, then the Arabic for an obstacle.

You'll want to know: صعوبة.
صعوبة — ṣuʿūba — "a difficulty".
وجدت صعوبة في فهم الدرس. wajadtu ṣuʿūba fī fahm ad-dars. I had difficulty understanding the lesson.
بصعوبة, bi-ṣuʿūba, with difficulty.

The word, taken apart.
صعوبة (ṣuʿūba) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ʿ-b, "to be hard, difficult" (Lane). Ṣuʿūba is the verbal noun of the root of ṣaʿb, difficult: difficulty.

Lesson 3 of 5.
معضلة (muʿḍila) — a dilemma.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an obstacle, then the Arabic for a difficulty.

You'll want to know: معضلة.
معضلة — muʿḍila — "a dilemma".
واجهت الأسرة معضلة صعبة. wājahat al-usra muʿḍila ṣaʿba. The family faced a hard dilemma.

The word, taken apart.
معضلة (muʿḍila) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ḍ-l, "to hinder, to make hard" (Lane). Muʿḍila is a Form IV active participle, feminine: a matter that baffles, a dilemma. عضلة, a muscle, is from the same root.

Lesson 4 of 5.
أخفق (akhfaqa) — to fall short.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a difficulty, then the Arabic for a dilemma.

You'll want to know: أخفق.
أخفق — akhfaqa — "to fall short".
أخفق الفريق في المباراة الأخيرة. akhfaqa al-farīq fī al-mubārā al-akhīra. The team fell short in the last match.
Present: يخفق, yukhfiq.
It takes في: أخفق (akhfaqa) في …. Compare فشل, failure.

The word, taken apart.
أخفق (akhfaqa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-f-q, "to flap, to beat" (Lane). Lane gives Form IV for one who sought a thing and did not get it: to fall short.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تمكن (tamakkana) — to manage to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a dilemma, then the Arabic for to fall short.

You'll want to know: تمكن.
تمكن — tamakkana — "to manage to".
تمكن الطالب من حل المسألة. tamakkana aṭ-ṭālib min ḥall al-masʾala. The student managed to solve the problem.
Present: يتمكن, yatamakkan.
It takes من: تمكن (tamakkana) من ….

The word, taken apart.
تمكن (tamakkana) is built on the native Arabic root m-k-n, "to be firm, to have power" (Lane). It is Form V, to become firmly able: to manage to do a thing.
