## You'll want to know: bǐfēn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C500-MATCH500-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐfēn* — "the score of a game".

> *xiànzài bǐfēn shì duōshao* — What's the score now?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐfēn* is built from 比 'to compare' and *fēn* 'a part', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjijX* and *pjun* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to collate' and the second 'to divide'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how it was.
