# घटणो (ghaṭṇo) — to decrease; to come down

## You'll want to know: घटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C213-PRICE213-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घटणो** — *ghaṭṇo* — "to decrease; to come down".

> आजकाल भाव घटै है।
>
> *ājkāl bhāv ghaṭai hai.*
>
> Prices are coming down these days.

Past (it …): वो भाव घट्यो, *vo bhāv ghaṭyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घटणो** (*ghaṭṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ghaṭṭati*, "decreases"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *ghaṭai*, "decreases" (CDIAL 4415).
