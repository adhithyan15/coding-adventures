## You'll want to know: la lancha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C659-BARCA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la lancha** — "the launch, the motorboat".

> Fuimos a la cueva en lancha.
>
> *We went to the cave by motorboat.*

*La lancha* has a motor; *la barca* is often rowed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lancha* came through Portuguese *lancha* and is usually traced to a Malay word meaning "swift".
