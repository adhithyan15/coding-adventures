# A multiple-choice item must include the keyed answer among its options

A listening master told learners to bring one pen, while the learner paper
offered a book, fruit, or nothing and the key selected “nothing.” Counts and
timing all passed, but the item had no correct response.

Review each objective item as a three-part unit: stimulus, options, and key.
When a correction pins an exact fact, add a focused assertion for that item so
later wording edits cannot silently separate the three parts again.
