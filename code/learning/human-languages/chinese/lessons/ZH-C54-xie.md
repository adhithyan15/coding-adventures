## You'll want to know: xiě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C54-LEARN54-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xiě* — "to write".

> *wǒ xiě hànzì* — I'm practising characters.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiě* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjaeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to remove' and 'to copy out a book'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
