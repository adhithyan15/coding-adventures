## You'll want to know: 外语
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C110-SKILL110-01]; assesses=[] -->

**外语** — *wàiyǔ* — "foreign language".

> **我上网学外语** *wǒ shàngwǎng xué wàiyǔ* — I learn foreign languages online.

The new character, in pieces: 外 — on the left the three-stroke top half of 多; on the right a vertical with a dot beside it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**外语** (*wàiyǔ*) is built from 外 'outside' and 语 'speech', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwajH* and *ngjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the speech from outside.
