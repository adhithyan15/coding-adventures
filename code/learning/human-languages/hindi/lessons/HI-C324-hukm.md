## You'll want to know: हुक्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C324-VIDROH324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हुक्म** — *hukm* — "an order; a command".

> राजा के हुक्म से सिपाही गाँव में आए।
>
> *rājā ke hukm se sipāhī gā̃v meṁ āe.*
>
> On the king's orders, soldiers came to the village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुक्म** (*hukm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥukm*, "a judgement, a ruling". **हुक्म देना** is to give an order.
