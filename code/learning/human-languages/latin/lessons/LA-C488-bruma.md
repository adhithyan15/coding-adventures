## You'll want to know: brūma, brūmae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C488-BRUMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**brūma, brūmae** — "midwinter, the winter solstice, the shortest day".

> Brūmā diēs brevissimus est et nox longissima.
>
> *At midwinter the day is shortest and the night longest.*

First declension, feminine: *brūma, brūmae f.* Shortened from *brevissima (diēs)*, the shortest day. English *brumal* 'wintry' comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brūma* comes from *brevima*, an old form of *brevissima* 'shortest', as in *brevissima diēs* 'the shortest day' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); it is built on *brevis* 'short'.
