- [YOU SAY: *the mechanic came yesterday*; *the mechanic mends the fan*]
- [YOU SAY: *perhaps I'll come again tomorrow*; *the day after tomorrow I'll go to the market*]
- [YOU SAY: *I won't go tomorrow*; *don't go now*]
- [YOU SAY: *the shop is open from six until ten*; *on Monday the shop is closed*]
- [YOU SAY: *I heated the water*; *I took milk*]
