German, chapter 87: Duck, Sheep, Goat, Lion, Monkey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Ente — a duck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a fox, then the German for a snake; a queue.

You'll want to know: die Ente.
die Ente — "a duck".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Ente]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Ente, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say die Ente]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a fox, then the German for a snake; a queue, then say die Ente again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Ente mean? ("A duck".) Say it once more.
