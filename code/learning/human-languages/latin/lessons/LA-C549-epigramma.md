## You'll want to know: epigramma, epigrammatis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C549-POEM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**epigramma, epigrammatis** — "an epigram, a short poem".

> Hoc epigramma breve et facētum est.
>
> *This epigram is short and witty.*

Neuter, third declension, from Greek: first an inscription, then a short, pointed poem. English *epigram*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**epigramma** was borrowed from Greek *epigramma* "inscription", from *epigrapho* "to write upon" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *epigram* comes from it.
