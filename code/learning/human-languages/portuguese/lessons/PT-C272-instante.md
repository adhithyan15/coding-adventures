## You'll want to know: o instante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C272-DURAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o instante** — "the instant, the moment".

> Num instante, o céu ficou escuro.
>
> *In an instant the sky went dark.*

*Um instante!* — one moment!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*instante* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnstāns* "pressing, present" (EtymDB), from *īnstāre* "to stand upon, to press on", built on *stāre* "to stand".
