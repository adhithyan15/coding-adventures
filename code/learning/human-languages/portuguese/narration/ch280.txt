Portuguese, chapter 280: Nos jornais.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
imprimir — to print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the correspondent, then the Portuguese for the headline.

You'll want to know: imprimir.
imprimir — "to print".
O jornal imprime cem mil exemplares por dia. The paper prints a hundred thousand copies a day.
Also on a home printer: imprimir um bilhete. The past participle is impresso.

The word, taken apart.
imprimir is a learned borrowing from Latin imprimere "to press into, to stamp", built on premere "to press" (Lewis & Short).
