## You'll want to know: ढीलो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C231-SHAPE231-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ढीलो** — *ḍhīlo* — "loose; slack".

> रस्सी ढीली है।
>
> *rassī ḍhīlī hai.*
>
> The rope is loose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढीलो** (*ḍhīlo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhila*, "loose, slack" (CDIAL 5590, which lists Marwari *ḍhīlo*).
