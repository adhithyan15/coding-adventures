## You'll want to know: la paupière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C478-VISAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la paupière** — "eyelid".

> Le soir, mes paupières deviennent lourdes.
>
> *In the evening my eyelids get heavy.*

*Fermer les paupières* — to close one's eyes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la paupière* is inherited from Latin *palpebra* "eyelid" (Lewis & Short), with a reshaped ending.
