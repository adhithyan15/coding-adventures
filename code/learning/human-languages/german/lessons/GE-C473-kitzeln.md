## You'll want to know: kitzeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C473-KICHERN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**kitzeln** — "to tickle".

> Der Vater kitzelt seine Tochter, und sie lacht laut.
>
> *The father tickles his daughter, and she laughs out loud.*

*Das kitzelt!* — That tickles!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kitzeln* is inherited from Old High German *kizzilōn* "to tickle" (EtymDB).
