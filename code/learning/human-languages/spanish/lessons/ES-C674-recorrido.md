## You'll want to know: el recorrido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C674-PASEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el recorrido** — "the route, the tour".

> El recorrido dura dos horas.
>
> *The tour takes two hours.*

*Un recorrido guiado* — a guided tour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recorrido* is the past form of *recorrer*, which is inherited from Latin *recurrere* "to run back", built on *currere* "to run".
