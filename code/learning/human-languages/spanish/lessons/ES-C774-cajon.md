## You'll want to know: el cajón
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C774-DESVAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el cajón** — "the drawer".

> Mi abuela guardaba las cartas en un cajón.
>
> *My grandmother kept the letters in a drawer.*

*De cajón* — obvious, of course.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cajón* is built on *caja* 'box' with the ending *-ón*. *Caja* comes from Latin *capsa* 'a box, a case for books' (Lewis & Short), most likely through Catalan *caixa*.
