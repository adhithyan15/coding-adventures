## You'll want to know: しょくにん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C458-CAREER458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくにん** — *shokunin* — "a craftsman; an artisan".

> **そふは たたみの しょくにんでした。** — *sofu wa tatami no shokunin deshita* — "My grandfather was a tatami craftsman."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくにん** (*shokunin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shoku*, "employment" (Middle Chinese *tsyik*) and *nin*, "person" (*nyin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
