# ಅವಲಂಬಿಸು (avalambisu) — to depend, to rely

## You'll want to know: ಅವಲಂಬಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C318-AVALAMBISU-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C91-ABOUTYOU-04, KA-LEX-C107-LOC107-05, KA-LEX-C55-ROAD-04, KA-LEX-C55-ROAD-02, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01] -->

**ಅವಲಂಬಿಸು** — *avalambisu* — "to depend (on), to rely (on)".

> **ನಮ್ಮ ಊರು ಮಳೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ.**
>
> *namma ūru maḷeya mēle avalambiside.* — "Our town depends on the rain."

What you depend on takes **ಮೇಲೆ**, "on": **ಮಳೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ** (*maḷeya mēle*, "on the rain"). **ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ** is **ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿ**, the **-ಇ** form, with **ಇದೆ**: "it is, having come to rest on", so "it depends". Written the same, **ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ** is also "I depended"; the sentence tells you which.

For "it depended", **ಇದೆ** becomes **ಇತ್ತು**:

> **ಆ ಹಳ್ಳಿ ಒಂದು ಕೆರೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿತ್ತು.**
>
> *ā haḷḷi ondu kereya mēle avalambisittu.* — "That village depended on one tank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C33-YOCISU-01] -->

**ಅವಲಂಬಿಸು** (*avalambisu*) is Sanskrit *avalamba* made into a Kannada verb with **-ಇಸು**, as **ಯೋಚಿಸು** is. CDIAL 826 gives *avalamba* as "hanging down" and, as a noun, "stay, support"; Monier-Williams adds "depending, resting upon". To depend on something is to hang from it, which is also where English *depend* comes from: Latin *dēpendēre*, "to hang down".
