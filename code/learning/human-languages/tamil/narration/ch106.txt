Tamil, chapter 106: Take Care, Go Safely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பார்த்துக்கொள்ளுங்கள் (pārttukkoḷḷuṅkaḷ) — take care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a college, then the Tamil for a school.

You'll want to know: பார்த்துக்கொள்ளுங்கள்.
பார்த்துக்கொள்ளுங்கள் (pārttukkoḷḷuṅkaḷ) — "take care", literally "look after (yourself)".
It is built on பார், "to see, to look".

What you've built.
The kindest goodbye.
