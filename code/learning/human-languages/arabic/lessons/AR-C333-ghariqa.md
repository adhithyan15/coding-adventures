# غرق (ghariqa) — to go under; to drown

## You'll want to know: غرق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C333-BAHR333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غرق** — *ghariqa* — "to go under; to drown".

> غرقت السفينة قرب الجزيرة.
>
> *ghariqat as-safīna qurba al-jazīra.*
>
> The ship sank near the island.

Present: يغرق, *yaghraq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غرق** (*ghariqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-r-q* (Lane), "to sink in water, to drown"; *gharīq* is one who has drowned.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
