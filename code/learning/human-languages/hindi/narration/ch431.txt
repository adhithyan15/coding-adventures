Hindi, chapter 431: Rates and Deals.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भाव (bhāv) — the going rate; a price per unit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be paid off, then the Hindi for to collect (dues).

You'll want to know: भाव.
भाव — bhāv — "the going rate; a price per unit".
आज आलू का भाव क्या है? āj ālū kā bhāv kyā hai? What is the price of potatoes today?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
भाव (bhāv) is Sanskrit bhāva, "being, a state, a condition" (Monier-Williams); in Hindi markets it came to mean the state of prices on the day, a sense the dictionary does not give. The same word also means a feeling.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मोल (mol) — worth; a price (old-fashioned).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to collect (dues), then the Hindi for the going rate.

You'll want to know: मोल.
मोल — mol — "worth; a price (old-fashioned)".
माँ के प्यार का कोई मोल नहीं होता। māṁ ke pyār kā koī mol nahīṃ hotā. A mother's love is beyond price.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मोल (mol) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit maulya, "a price" (CDIAL 10373), which lists Hindi mol. It survives in sayings and in मोलभाव, bargaining.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सौदा (saudā) — a deal; shopping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the going rate, then the Hindi for worth.

You'll want to know: सौदा.
सौदा — saudā — "a deal; shopping".
हमने दुकानदार से अच्छा सौदा किया। hamne dukāndār se acchā saudā kiyā. We got a good deal from the shopkeeper.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सौदा (saudā) is usually traced to Persian saudā, "trade, a bargain"; सौदा-सुलफ़ is the day's shopping.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सेल (sel) — a sale (at reduced prices).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for worth, then the Hindi for a deal.

You'll want to know: सेल.
सेल — sel — "a sale (at reduced prices)".
कपड़ों की दुकान में सेल लगी है। kapṛoṁ kī dukān meṁ sel lagī hai. There is a sale on at the clothes shop.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सेल (sel) is borrowed from English sale, a time of cut prices; it is feminine: सेल लगी है.

Lesson 5 of 5.
किफ़ायती (kifāyatī) — economical; good value.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a deal, then the Hindi for a sale.

You'll want to know: किफ़ायती.
किफ़ायती — kifāyatī — "economical; good value".
यह होटल किफ़ायती है और साफ़ भी। yah hoṭal kifāyatī hai aur sāf bhī. This hotel is good value, and clean too.

The word, taken apart.
किफ़ायती (kifāyatī) is built on kifāyat, "thrift", usually traced to Arabic kifāya, "what is enough", through Persian: spending no more than is enough.
