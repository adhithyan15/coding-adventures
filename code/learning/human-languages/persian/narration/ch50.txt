Persian, chapter 50: Map, Lesson, Answer, Word, Game.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نقشه (naghshe) — a map.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a bag, then the Persian for money.

You'll want to know: نقشه.
نقشه — naghshe — "a map".

The word, taken apart.
naghshe is naghsh, "design, picture", with the ending -e. naghsh is borrowed from Arabic naqš, "engraving, painting" (root n-q-š, Lane).

بازی — bâzi — "a game".

The word, taken apart.
bâzi is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian wāzīg, "play, game".
