# もし … たら (moshi … tara) — if … then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C260-MOSHI-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C260-TARA-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C254-OPINION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, I won't go", with **もし**. (*moshi ame ga futtara, ikimasen*.) Then "what do you think?" (*dō omoimasu ka*.)

## You'll want to know: もし … -たら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C260-IF-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-04, JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-05, JA-LEX-C54-TIME54-02, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-FUTURE-01] -->

> **もし はれたら、やまに いきます。**
>
> *moshi haretara, yama ni ikimasu.* — "If it clears up, I will go to the mountains."

The frame holds the whole condition: **もし** opens it, **-たら** closes it, and **、** comes after it. Everything between is the condition:

> **もし あした はれたら、やまに いきます。**
>
> *moshi ashita haretara, yama ni ikimasu.* — "If it is fine tomorrow, I will go to the mountains."

## You'll want to know: もし … -たら、どう しますか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C260-IF-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C44-NATURE44-05, JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-01] -->

To ask what someone will do if, **どう しますか** (*dō shimasu ka*), "what will you do", follows the frame:

> **もし あめが ふったら、どう しますか。**
>
> *moshi ame ga futtara, dō shimasu ka.* — "If it rains, what will you do?"

> **うちで えいがを みます。**
>
> *uchi de eiga o mimasu.* — "I will see a film at home."
