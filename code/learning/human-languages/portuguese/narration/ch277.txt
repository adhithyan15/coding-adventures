Portuguese, chapter 277: De vez em quando.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
frequentemente — frequently, often.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the millennium, then the Portuguese for subsequent.

You'll want to know: frequentemente.
frequentemente — "frequently, often".
Vou frequentemente ao cinema. I often go to the cinema.
Muitas vezes is the everyday way to say it.

The word, taken apart.
frequentemente is built on frequente, a learned borrowing from Latin frequēns "crowded, frequent" (Lewis & Short), with the ending -mente.

previamente is built on prévio "prior" (EtymDB), from Latin praevius "going before", from prae "before" and via "way" (Lewis & Short).
