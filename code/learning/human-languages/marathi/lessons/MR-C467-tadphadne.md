## You'll want to know: तडफडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C467-YATANA467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तडफडणे** — *taḍphaḍṇe* — "to writhe".

> पाण्याबाहेर काढलेला मासा तडफडत होता.
>
> *pāṇyābāher kāḍhlelā māsā taḍphaḍat hotā.*
>
> The fish taken out of the water was writhing.

Past (he …): तो तडफडला, *to taḍphaḍlā*.

Of pain, or of fierce impatience: भेटायला तडफडणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तडफडणे** (*taḍphaḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *taḍphaḍṇẽ*, "to toss about", under the expressive *\*taḍapphaḍ* (CDIAL 5634).
