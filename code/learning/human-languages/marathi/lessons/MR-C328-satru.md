## You'll want to know: शत्रू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C328-LADHAI328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शत्रू** — *śatrū* — "an enemy".

> शत्रू रात्रीच्या अंधारात गावात शिरला.
>
> *śatrū rātrīcyā andhārāt gāvāt śirlā.*
>
> The enemy crept into the village in the dark of night.

Masculine.

Plural शत्रू.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शत्रू** (*śatrū*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śatru*, 'an enemy, a rival' (Monier-Williams).
