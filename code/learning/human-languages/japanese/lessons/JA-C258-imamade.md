## You'll want to know: 今まで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C258-IMAMADE-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C78-LOC78-02, JA-LEX-C29-VERBS29-03, JA-GRAMMAR-C258-TA-KOTO-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C258-TA-KOTO-02] -->

**今まで** — *imamade* — "until now", "so far": **いま**, "now", with **まで**, "as far as".

> **今まで いちども いった ことが ありません。**
>
> *imamade ichido mo itta koto ga arimasen.* — "I have never been there until now."

> **今まで ちかくに すんでいました。**
>
> *imamade chikaku ni sunde imashita.* — "Until now I lived nearby."

The first sign is new: the kanji for **いま**, read **いま** (*ima*) here. The next lesson writes it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**今まで** (*imamade*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: **いま** (*ima*), "now", written with its kanji and read the native way, and **まで** (*made*), "as far as". JMdict glosses it "until now; so far", and KANJIDIC2 gives いま as the kanji's native reading (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
