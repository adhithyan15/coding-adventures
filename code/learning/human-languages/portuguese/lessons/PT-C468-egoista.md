## You'll want to know: egoísta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C468-INVEJA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**egoísta** — "selfish; selfish person".

> Ele é egoísta e nunca partilha nada.
>
> *He is selfish and never shares anything.*

The same form for a man and a woman: *um egoísta*, *uma egoísta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*egoísta* was borrowed from French *égoïste* (EtymDB), a modern word built on Latin *ego* "I".
