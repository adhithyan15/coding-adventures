# حاك (ḥāka) — to knit, to weave

## You'll want to know: حاك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C538-IBRA538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حاك** — *ḥāka* — "to knit, to weave".

> حاكت جدتي وشاحا من الصوف.
>
> *ḥākat jaddatī wishāḥan min aṣ-ṣūf.*
>
> My grandmother knitted a woollen scarf.

Present: يحوك, *yaḥūk*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حاك** (*ḥāka*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-k*, "to weave" (Lane). Lane gives yaḥūku as its present.
