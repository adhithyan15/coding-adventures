# मेरी ट्रेन छूट गई (merī ṭren chūṭ gaī) — when something goes wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C283-KHONA-01, HI-LEX-C229-ACT229-05, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-ISLIYE-RESULT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "my bag got lost". (*merā thailā kho gayā*.) Then **छूटना**, "to be left behind". (*chūṭnā*.)

## You'll want to know: reporting a loss
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C283-LOST-REPORT-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C283-KHONA-01, HI-LEX-C133-THINGS133-03, HI-LEX-C190-THG190-01, HI-LEX-C273-THG273-01, HI-LEX-C187-THG187-02, HI-LEX-C282-BATANA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01] -->

> **मेरा थैला खो गया। उसमें मेरा बटुआ था।**
>
> *merā thailā kho gayā. usmeṁ merā baṭuā thā.* — "My bag got lost. My wallet was in it."

**उसमें** is **उस** with **में**: "in it". Then ask for help, and say whom you told:

- **मेरी मदद कीजिए।** (*merī madad kījiye.*) — Please help me. **मदद** is feminine: **मेरी**.
- **मैंने पुलिस को बताया।** (*maiṁne pulis ko batāyā.*) — I told the police.
- **थाना कहाँ है?** (*thānā kahā̃ hai?*) — Where is the police station?

## You'll want to know: missing a train
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C283-MISSED-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C229-ACT229-05, HI-LEX-C94-SPAN-04, HI-LEX-C122-LATE-02, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-02, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-ISLIYE-RESULT-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-PADNA-MUST-01] -->

> **मेरी ट्रेन छूट गई।**
>
> *merī ṭren chūṭ gaī.* — "I missed my train."

Word for word, "my train got left behind". The train is the subject, so **छूट गई** follows it, and there is no **मैंने**. With a bus, the same: **मेरी बस छूट गई** (*merī bas chūṭ gaī*).

The cause goes first, with **इसलिए**:

> **मुझे देर हो गई, इसलिए मेरी ट्रेन छूट गई।**
>
> *mujhe der ho gaī, isliye merī ṭren chūṭ gaī.* — "I was late, so I missed my train."

And what it cost you, with **पड़ना**: **मुझे बस से जाना पड़ा** (*mujhe bas se jānā paṛā*), "I had to go by bus".
