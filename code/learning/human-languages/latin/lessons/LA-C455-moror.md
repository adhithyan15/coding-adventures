## You'll want to know: moror, morārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C455-CONSPICOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**moror, morārī** — "to delay, to linger; to hold up".

> Viātor in caupōnā nōn morātur, quod nox venit.
>
> *The traveller does not linger at the inn, because night is coming.*

Deponent: *moror, morārī, morātus sum*, from *mora* 'delay'. *Nōlī morārī*, don't hang about. English *moratorium* comes from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*moror* is built on *mora* 'delay' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), a regular first-conjugation deponent made from the noun.
