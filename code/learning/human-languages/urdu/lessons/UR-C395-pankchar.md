## You'll want to know: پنکچر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C395-TAYAR395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پنکچر** — *pankchar* — "a puncture".

> راستے میں ہماری گاڑی کا ٹائر پنکچر ہو گیا۔
>
> *rāste meṅ hamārī gāṛī kā ṭāyar pankchar ho gayā.*
>
> On the way our car got a flat tyre.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پنکچر** (*pankchar*) is borrowed from English *puncture*; with **ہونا** it means a tyre goes flat.
