## Fixed — Gujarati je hedged against CDIAL

- **GU-C36-je** and **GU-C36-je-te:** CDIAL 10391 lists no modern Gujarati form under the relative *ya-*. It gives Old Gujarati *ju* "if" and says in a note that *je* took its *-e* after *te* and *e*. The lessons now say *je* is usually traced to *ya-* and that CDIAL names it only in that note.
