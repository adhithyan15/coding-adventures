Hindi, chapter 489: Medicines.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घोलना (gholnā) — to dissolve; to stir in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to have (something) looked at, then the Hindi for to have something done.

You'll want to know: घोलना.
घोलना — gholnā — "to dissolve; to stir in".
मीरा ने पानी में दवा घोली। Mīrā ne pānī meṁ davā gholī. Mira dissolved the medicine in water.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दवा घोली, usne davā gholī.

The word, taken apart.
घोलना (gholnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi gholnā, "to mix, dissolve, melt", under Sanskrit ghōlayati (CDIAL 4526).

Lesson 2 of 5.
चटाना (caṭānā) — to give (a baby) something to lick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to have something done, then the Hindi for to dissolve.

You'll want to know: चटाना.
चटाना — caṭānā — "to give (a baby) something to lick".
माँ ने बच्चे को थोड़ा शहद चटाया। māṁ ne bacce ko thoṛā śahad caṭāyā. Mother gave the baby a little honey to lick.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को दवा चटाई, usne bacce ko davā caṭāī.

The word, taken apart.
चटाना (caṭānā) is the causative partner of cāṭnā, "to lick", which CDIAL lists under caṭṭ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4573). Medicine for small children is often given this way, mixed with honey.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ख़ुराक (khurāk) — a dose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to dissolve, then the Hindi for to give (a baby) something to lick.

You'll want to know: ख़ुराक.
ख़ुराक — khurāk — "a dose".
दवा की पहली ख़ुराक सुबह लेनी है। davā kī pahlī khurāk subah lenī hai. The first dose of the medicine is to be taken in the morning.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ख़ुराक (khurāk) is usually traced to Persian khurāk, "food, diet"; Hindi uses it for a measured amount of food or medicine, a dose.

Lesson 5 of 5.
काढ़ा (kāṛhā) — a herbal brew; a decoction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a dose, then the Hindi for an ointment.

You'll want to know: काढ़ा.
काढ़ा — kāṛhā — "a herbal brew; a decoction".
ज़ुकाम में दादी अदरक का काढ़ा बनाती हैं। zukām meṁ dādī adrak kā kāṛhā banātī haiṁ. When someone has a cold, my grandmother makes a ginger brew.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
काढ़ा (kāṛhā) is probably the noun of kāṛhnā, "to pull out", which CDIAL lists under kaḍḍhati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2660): what boiling draws out of herbs. The link of senses is not given by CDIAL.
