## You'll want to know: کاهش دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C373-AFZAYESH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کاهش دادن** (*kâhesh dâdan*) — "to reduce, to cut".

> شرکت ساعت کار را کاهش داد.
>
> *sherkat sâ'at-e kâr râ kâhesh dâd.*
>
> The company cut working hours.

*Kâhesh* + *dâdan* "to give" (present stem *deh-*). The object takes *râ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کاهش دادن** is native Persian, built from *kâhesh* "decrease" with *dâdan* "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE *deh₃-* "to give".
