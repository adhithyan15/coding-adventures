Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਾਲਜ (kālaj) — a college.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a result, then the Punjabi for passed.

You'll want to know: ਕਾਲਜ.
ਕਾਲਜ — kālaj — "a college".
ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਕਾਲਜ ਵਿੱਚ ਪੜ੍ਹਦੀ ਹੈ। merī bhaiṇ kālaj vich paṛhdī hai. My sister studies at a college.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਾਲਜ (kālaj) is borrowed from English college.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ.
ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ — vazīfā — "a scholarship; a grant".
ਹੁਸ਼ਿਆਰ ਵਿਦਿਆਰਥੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ ਮਿਲਦਾ ਹੈ। hushiār vidiārthīāṁ nū̃ vazīfā mildā hai. Clever students get a scholarship.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਜ਼ੀਫ਼ਾ (vazīfā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic waẓīfa, "duty".
