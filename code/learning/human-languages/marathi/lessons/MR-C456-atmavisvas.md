## You'll want to know: आत्मविश्वास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C456-SANKOC456-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्मविश्वास** — *ātmaviśvās* — "self-confidence".

> सरावानंतर तिचा आत्मविश्वास वाढला.
>
> *sarāvānantar ticā ātmaviśvās vāḍhlā.*
>
> Her self-confidence grew after practice.

Masculine.

आत्म-, self, + विश्वास, trust. आत्मविश्वासाने, confidently.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्मविश्वास** (*ātmaviśvās*) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, *ātman*, "self", and *viśvāsa*, "trust" (Monier-Williams).
