## You'll want to know: けいこく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C340-JOGEN340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**けいこく** — *keikoku* — "a warning".

> **けいこくを きいたので、みんな うちに かえりました。** — *keikoku o kiita node, minna uchi ni kaerimashita* — "Everyone heard the warning, so they went home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けいこく** (*keikoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kei*, "admonish" (Middle Chinese *kjaengX*) and *koku*, "tell" (*kowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
