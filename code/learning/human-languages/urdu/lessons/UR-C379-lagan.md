## You'll want to know: لگن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C379-MEHNAT379-03]; assesses=[] -->

**لگن** — *lagan* — "dedication; devotion (to a task)".

> اگر اس میں لگن ہوتی تو وہ آج ماہر ہوتا۔
>
> *agar us meṅ lagan hotī to voh āj māhir hotā.*
>
> If he had been dedicated, he would be an expert today.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لگن** (*lagan*) is built on **لگنا**, which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lagnā*, "to be attached to", under Sanskrit *lagyati*, "adheres" (CDIAL 10895). Sticking to a task.
