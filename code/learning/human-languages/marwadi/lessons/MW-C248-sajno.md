## You'll want to know: सजणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C248-MEHDI248-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सजणो** — *sajṇo* — "to dress up; to be adorned".

> ब्याव मांय सगळा सजै है।
>
> *byāv mā̃y sagḷā sajai hai.*
>
> At a wedding everyone dresses up.

Past (he …): वो छोरो सज्यो, *vo chhoro sajyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सजणो** (*sajṇo*) is probably inherited from Sanskrit *sajjyate*, "is made ready"; for a bride or a house, being made ready is being adorned. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *sājnā*, "to be prepared" (CDIAL 13093).
