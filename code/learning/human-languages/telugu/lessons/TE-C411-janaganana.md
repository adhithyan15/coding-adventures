## You'll want to know: జనగణన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C411-SATAM411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**జనగణన** — *janagaṇana* — "a census".

> పది సంవత్సరాలకు ఒకసారి జనగణన జరుగుతుంది.
>
> *padi saṁvatsarālaku okasāri janagaṇana jarugutundi.*
>
> A census takes place once every ten years.

జన, people, + గణన, counting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జనగణన** (*janagaṇana*) is built from Sanskrit *jana*, "a person, people" (CDIAL 5098), and *gaṇana*, "counting" (CDIAL 3992).
