# わかりません — I do not understand

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-YOKU, JA-LEX-SUMIMASEN, JA-SCRIPT-KU-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Read **よく**, then open a repair with *sumimasen*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-WAKARIMASEN]; assesses=[] -->

> **わかりません。** — *wa-ka-ri-ma-se-n* — I do not understand

Six morae. The *wakari-* part carries “understand”; polite **-masen** makes the
predicate negative. Japanese often leaves out “I” when the situation already
makes the experiencer obvious.

Combine the two known pieces only in sound for now:

> *yoku wakarimasen* — I do not understand well / fully
