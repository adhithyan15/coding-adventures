Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 21: Bus, Vehicle, and Cart: No New Signs.
9 lessons. All 9 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 9.
Hear bas — bus.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Recall the seven-word shopping payoff, then say expensive and price.

You'll want to know.
bas — bus.
Hear it twice, point to a bus picture, and say it once. Keep the spelling covered until the sound and meaning are easy.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 8 seconds]
What vehicle does bas name?


Lesson 2 of 9.
बस (bas) — bus on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say bus and maternal grandfather, then write expensive and price.

You'll want to know.
ब + स becomes बस — bas — bus.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write बस (bas).

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
From bas, write bus.


Lesson 3 of 9.
Hear gāṛī — vehicle.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say bus and clothes, write bus and maternal grandfather, then write ड़.

You'll want to know.
gāṛī — vehicle.
Use vehicle as the broad meaning here. Hear the curled middle sound, then say the word once from a vehicle picture.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What does gāṛī name here?


Lesson 4 of 9.
गाड़ी (gāṛī) — vehicle on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say vehicle and maternal grandmother, write clothes, then recall the three-word shopping payoff.

You'll want to know.
गा + ड़ी becomes गाड़ी — gāṛī — vehicle.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write गाड़ी (gāṛī). Keep the dot below ड and the long-ī mark after it.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From gāṛī, write vehicle.

ṭhelā — cart; handcart.

Wrap-up Recall.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write ठेला (ṭhelā).

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.

Lesson 7 of 9.
Retrieve three transport words — bus, vehicle, and cart.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Recall the seven-word shopping payoff, then write त, cheap, and ड़.

Guided Practice.
Hear the three words in a mixed order, give each meaning, read three shuffled cards, then write all three from sound.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 28 seconds]
Write bus, vehicle, and cart in order.


Lesson 8 of 9.
Payoff — three transport words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Write husband, then say goods.

Guided Practice.
Identify three heard words.
Produce three words from meaning cues.
Match three printed cards to meanings.
Write all three heard words without a model.
Pass each skill separately. Timetables, tickets, and location phrases remain later work.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 36 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write the three words.


Lesson 9 of 9.
Close the first transport loop — delayed independent recall of three transport words.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Recall the ten-label family payoff, then write goods.

Guided Practice.
With every model hidden, identify, say, read, and write बस (bas), गाड़ी (gāṛī), and ठेला (ṭhelā) in a shuffled order.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Which four skills must pass independently?
