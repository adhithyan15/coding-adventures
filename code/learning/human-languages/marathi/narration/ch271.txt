Lesson 1 of 5.
कचेरी (kacerī) — a government office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a dhoti, then the Marathi for a kurta.

You'll want to know: कचेरी.
कचेरी — kacerī — "a government office".

The word, taken apart.
कचेरी (kacerī) is a loan from Hindustani kacahrī, "office, court", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed kṛtyagharikā, "court-house" (CDIAL 3429).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ओसरी (osrī) — a veranda.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a bus station, then the Marathi for a blanket.

You'll want to know: ओसरी.
ओसरी — osrī — "a veranda".

The word, taken apart.
ओसरी (osrī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from avaśara, "protection, shelter", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 848). CDIAL compares the sense change in śaraṇa, "shelter", toward "roof, veranda".
