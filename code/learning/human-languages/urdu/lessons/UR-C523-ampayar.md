## You'll want to know: امپائر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C523-STADIUM523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**امپائر** — *ampāyar* — "an umpire".

> امپائر نے کھیل روکا۔
>
> *ampāyar ne khel rokā.*
>
> The umpire stopped the game.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امپائر** (*ampāyar*) is borrowed from English *umpire*.
