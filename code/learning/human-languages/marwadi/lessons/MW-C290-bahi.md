## You'll want to know: बही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C290-SARU290-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बही** — *bahī* — "a ledger".

> मालक बही राखै है।
>
> *mālak bahī rākhai hai.*
>
> The owner keeps a ledger.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बही** (*bahī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vahikā*, found in a word for a king's diary; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *bahī*, "account-book, ledger" (CDIAL 11460).
