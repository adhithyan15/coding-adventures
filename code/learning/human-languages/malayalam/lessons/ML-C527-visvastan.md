## You'll want to know: വിശ്വസ്തൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C527-SVABHAVAM527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വിശ്വസ്തൻ** — *viśvastan* — "a loyal person; a trusted one".

> അവൻ മുതലാളിയുടെ വിശ്വസ്തനാണ്.
>
> *avan mutalāḷiyuṭe viśvastanāṇŭ.*
>
> He is the boss's trusted man.

A woman is വിശ്വസ്ത; the quality is വിശ്വസ്തത, loyalty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിശ്വസ്തൻ** (*viśvastan*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *viśvasta*, "trusted, faithful" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ. It shares its root with വിശ്വാസം.
