## You'll want to know: o arguido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C387-TRIBUNAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o arguido** — "the accused, the formal suspect; the defendant".

> O arguido não disse nada ao juiz.
>
> *The accused said nothing to the judge.*

A European legal term for someone formally named as a suspect in a criminal case, through to the trial; in Brazil *o réu* or *o acusado*. Feminine *a arguida*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arguido* is the participle of *arguir* "to accuse, to argue" (EtymDB), from Latin *arguere* "to make clear, to prove, to accuse" (Lewis & Short).
