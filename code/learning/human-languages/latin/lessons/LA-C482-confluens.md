# cōnfluēns, cōnfluentis — a confluence, the place where two rivers meet

## You'll want to know: cōnfluēns, cōnfluentis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C482-AMNIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnfluēns, cōnfluentis** — "a confluence, the place where two rivers meet".

> Ad cōnfluentem duōrum flūminum oppidum est.
>
> *At the confluence of two rivers there is a town.*

Third declension, masculine: *cōnfluēns, cōnfluentis m.*, also plural *cōnfluentēs*. Many towns stand at such a place. English *confluence* comes from the same verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnfluēns* is the present participle of *cōnfluō* 'to flow together', from *con-* and *fluō* 'to flow' (Lewis & Short). English *confluence* comes from the same verb.
