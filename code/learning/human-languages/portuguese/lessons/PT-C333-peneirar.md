## You'll want to know: peneirar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C333-BOLO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**peneirar** — "to sift".

> A avó peneira a farinha duas vezes.
>
> *Grandmother sifts the flour twice.*

Built on *a peneira*, ‘the sieve’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peneirar* is built on *peneira* "a sieve", which is inherited from Latin *pānāria* (EtymDB), akin to *pānārium* "a bread basket", from *pānis* "bread" (Lewis & Short).
