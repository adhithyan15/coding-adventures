## You'll want to know: دور زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C426-RAHBANDAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دور زدن** (*dowr zadan*) — "to turn round, to make a U-turn; to go round".

> راه را اشتباه رفتیم و دور زدیم.
>
> *Râh râ eshtebâh raftim va dowr zadim.*
>
> We went the wrong way and turned round.

*dowr* 'turn, round' + *zadan*. *Dowr-e meydân* — round the square.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dowr zadan* is built from *dowr* 'a turn, a round', borrowed from Arabic *dawr* 'turn' (EtymDB), and the native verb *zadan* 'to strike'.
