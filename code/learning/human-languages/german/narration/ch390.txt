German, chapter 390: Nicht verschwenden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
verschwenden — to waste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the expense, then the German for the income.

You'll want to know: verschwenden.
verschwenden — "to waste".
Er verschwendet viel Geld für Kleidung. He wastes a lot of money on clothes.
Inseparable: er verschwendet, er hat verschwendet. Zeit verschwenden — to waste time.

The word, taken apart.
verschwenden is inherited from Middle High German verswenden and Old High German firswenten (EtymDB), "to make vanish", of the family of schwinden "to dwindle".

Lesson 3 of 5.
preiswert — good value, inexpensive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to waste, then the German for the waste.

You'll want to know: preiswert.
preiswert — "good value, inexpensive".
Das kleine Hotel war sauber und preiswert. The small hotel was clean and good value.
Preiswert is a compliment: worth its price. Billig can sound cheap and poor.

The word, taken apart.
preiswert joins Preis "price", from Old French pris and Latin pretium "price, value" (Lewis & Short), to wert "worth", Proto-Germanic werþaz (EtymDB): worth its price.

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Vermögen — the fortune, the assets.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the waste, then the German for good value.

You'll want to know: das Vermögen.
das Vermögen — "the fortune, the assets".
Die Familie hat ein großes Vermögen. The family has a large fortune.
Plural: die Vermögen. In formal German also ability: nach bestem Vermögen — to the best of your ability.

The word, taken apart.
Vermögen is the old verb vermögen "to be able" made into a noun, built on mögen, from Proto-Germanic maganą "to be able" (EtymDB): what your means let you do.
