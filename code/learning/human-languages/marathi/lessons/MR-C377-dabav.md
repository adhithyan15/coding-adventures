## You'll want to know: दबाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C377-PRABHAV377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दबाव** — *dabāv* — "outside pressure; pressure".

> घरच्यांच्या दबावामुळे तिने नोकरी सोडली.
>
> *gharacyāñcyā dabāvāmuḷe tine nokrī soḍlī.*
>
> Under pressure from her family, she left her job.

Masculine.

दबाव आणणे, to put pressure on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दबाव** (*dabāv*) is built on दाबणे, "to press", with the noun ending -आव; CDIAL lists Marathi *dābṇẽ*, "to press", under *\*dabb-*, "press" (CDIAL 6173).
