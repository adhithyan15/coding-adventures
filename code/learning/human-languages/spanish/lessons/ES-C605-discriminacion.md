## You'll want to know: la discriminación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C605-PROBSOC-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la discriminación** — "the discrimination".

> La discriminación en el trabajo va contra la ley.
>
> *Discrimination at work is against the law.*

*Discriminar* — to discriminate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discriminación* is built on *discriminar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *discriminare* 'to divide', from *discrimen* 'a dividing line', from *cernere* 'to sift' (EtymDB).
