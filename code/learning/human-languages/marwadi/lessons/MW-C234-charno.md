# चरणो (charṇo) — to graze

## You'll want to know: चरणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C234-HERD234-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चरणो** — *charṇo* — "to graze".

> भेड़ खेत मांय चरै है।
>
> *bheṛ khet mā̃y charai hai.*
>
> The sheep graze in the field.

Past (it …): वो ऊंट चर्यो, *vo ū̃ṭ charyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरणो** (*charṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *carati*, "moves, grazes"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *caraï*, "grazes" (CDIAL 4686).
