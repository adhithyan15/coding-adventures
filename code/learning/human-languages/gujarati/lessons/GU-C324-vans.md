## You'll want to know: વંશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C324-SAMRAT324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વંશ** — *vanś* — "a dynasty; a lineage".

> આ વંશનો છેલ્લો રાજા ખૂબ નબળો હતો.
>
> *ā vanśno chello rājā khūb nabḷo hato.*
>
> The last king of this dynasty was very weak.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વંશ** (*vanś*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vaṁśa*, "a bamboo cane", and then "a family line", one joint after another (CDIAL 11175). It is masculine: **આ વંશનો રાજા**.
