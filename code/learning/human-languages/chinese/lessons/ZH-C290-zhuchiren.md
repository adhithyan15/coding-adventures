## You'll want to know: zhǔchírén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C290-NEWS290-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔchírén* — "presenter; host of a show".

> *zhège jiémù de zhǔchírén hěn yǒuyìsi* — The host of this show is a lot of fun.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔchírén* is built from *zhǔ* 'to be in charge', *chí* 'to hold' and 人 'a person', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyuX*, *dri* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be in charge of' and the second 'to hold': the person who holds the show together.
