## You'll want to know: वचन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C273-CHUP273-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वचन** — *vachan* — "a promise".

> यो म्हारो वचन है।
>
> *yo mhāro vachan hai.*
>
> This is my promise.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वचन** (*vachan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vacana*, "speaking; a word" (Monier-Williams), which does not list the sense "a promise". CDIAL gives no Marwari form under *vacana*, but lists the inherited Gujarati *veṇ*, "word, promise" (CDIAL 11199): a word given is a promise.
