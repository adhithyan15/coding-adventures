## You'll want to know: شرکت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C314-RUYDAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شرکت کردن** (*sherkat kardan*) — "to take part, to participate".

> صد نفر در مسابقه شرکت کردند.
>
> *Sad nafar dar mosâbeqe sherkat kardand.*
>
> A hundred people took part in the competition.

With *dar*: *dar jalase sherkat kard*. The noun *sherkat* alone is "a company".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sherkat* is borrowed from Arabic *shirka* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "partnership, sharing". *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
