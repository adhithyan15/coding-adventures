## You'll want to know: jiānchí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C366-ADVICE366-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiānchí* — "to keep at it; to persist".

> *wǒ juéde xué Hànyǔ yào jiānchí* — I think learning Chinese takes perseverance.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiānchí* is built from *jiān* and *chí*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ken* and *dri* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'firm' and the second 'to hold'.
