# der Mai — "May"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-APRIL-01, GE-SOUND-EI-AS-EYE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One syllable, and its vowel is one you were given for a number.

## You'll want to know: der Mai
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-MAI-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-MONAT-01] -->

> **der Mai** — "May."

Latin *Maius*, the month of **Maia** — a goddess of growth and increase, which
is a reasonable thing to call the month when everything is putting on size.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-SOUND-EI-AS-EYE-01, GE-LEX-MAI-01] -->

- `ai-as-eye` — *ai* is the vowel of English **eye**.

You already have that vowel under its commoner German spelling, *ei*, from the
numbers. German writes the same sound two ways, and *Mai* is the rarer one:
*ei* and *ai* both say **eye**.

So *Mai* rhymes with English *my*.
