Arabic, chapter 259: To publish, To broadcast, To follow, To jump, To press.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نشر (nashara) — to publish, to post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to take part, then the Arabic for to disagree.

You'll want to know: نشر.
نشر — nashara — "to publish, to post".

The word, taken apart.
نشر (nashara) is built on the native Arabic root n-sh-r, "to spread out" (Lane). Nashara is "to spread out", to publish.
