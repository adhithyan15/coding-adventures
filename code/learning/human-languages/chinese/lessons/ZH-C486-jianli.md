## You'll want to know: jiǎnlì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C486-JOBHUNT486-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎnlì* — "CV; résumé".

> *zhè shì wǒ de jiǎnlì* — Here's my CV.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎnlì* is built from *jiǎn* 'a writing slip; brief' and *lì* 'to pass through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keanX* and *lek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a writing tablet' and the second 'to pass through'. The sense 'a CV' is modern and not given there.
