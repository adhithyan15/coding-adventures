Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 262: People of the Village.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जणो (jaṇo) — a person (when counting).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a son's son, then the Marwadi for a daughter's son.

You'll want to know: जणो.
जणो — jaṇo — "a person (when counting)".
घर मांय पांच जणा है। ghar mā̃y pāṅch jaṇā hai. There are five people in the house.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जणो (jaṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit jana, "person"; CDIAL lists Marwari jaṇo under it (CDIAL 5098).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बणजारो (baṇjāro) — a travelling trader.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a daughter's son, then the Marwadi for a person (when counting).

You'll want to know: बणजारो.
बणजारो — baṇjāro — "a travelling trader".
बणजारो ऊंट माथै लूण लावै है। baṇjāro ū̃ṭ māthai lūṇ lāvai hai. The travelling trader brings salt on a camel.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बणजारो (baṇjāro) is inherited from a reconstructed vaṇijyākāra, "trader". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati vaṇjāro, "travelling trader", with the same ण (CDIAL 11234).

सहेली — sahelī — "a woman's friend".
म्हारी सहेली मेंहदी लगावै है। mhārī sahelī mẽhdī lagāvai hai. My friend is putting on henna.

Lesson 4 of 5.
चौधरी (chaudhrī) — a village headman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a travelling trader, then the Marwadi for a woman's friend.

You'll want to know: चौधरी.
चौधरी — chaudhrī — "a village headman".
चौधरी घणो भलो आदमी है। chaudhrī ghaṇo bhalo ādmī hai. The headman is a very kind man.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चौधरी (chaudhrī) is inherited from Sanskrit caturdhara, a family name. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi caudharī, "the headman of a village" (CDIAL 4612).
