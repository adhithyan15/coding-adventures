## You'll want to know: il pubblico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C289-PUBBLICO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il pubblico** — "the audience, the public".

> Il pubblico ha applaudito per dieci minuti.
>
> *The audience clapped for ten minutes.*

As an adjective, public: *un giardino pubblico*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pubblico* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pūblicus* "of the people, public" (Lewis & Short).
