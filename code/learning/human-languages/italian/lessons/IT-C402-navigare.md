## You'll want to know: navigare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C402-RETE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**navigare** — "to browse, to surf; to sail".

> Passo troppo tempo a navigare su internet.
>
> *I spend too much time browsing the internet.*

First to sail: *navigare sul fiume*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navigare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nāvigāre* "to sail" (Lewis & Short), built on *nāvis* "a ship".
