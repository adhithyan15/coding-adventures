## You'll want to know: аплодировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C519-CURTAIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**аплодировать** (*aplodírovat*) — "to applaud".

> Публика долго аплодировала актёрам.
>
> *Públika dólgo aplodírovala aktyóram.*
>
> The audience applauded the actors for a long time.

Imperfective; present аплодирую. Takes the dative. The noun is *аплодисменты* — applause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*аплодировать* is a learned borrowing, usually traced through French *applaudir* to Latin *applaudere* 'to applaud' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
