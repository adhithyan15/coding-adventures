## You'll want to know: ठीकरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C242-FIRE242-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ठीकरो** — *ṭhīkro* — "a potsherd; a shard of a broken pot".

> ठीकरो तीखो है।
>
> *ṭhīkro tīkho hai.*
>
> The shard of the pot is sharp.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठीकरो** (*ṭhīkro*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭhīkkara*, "potsherd"; CDIAL lists Marwari *ṭhīkro*, "potsherd", under it (CDIAL 5504).
