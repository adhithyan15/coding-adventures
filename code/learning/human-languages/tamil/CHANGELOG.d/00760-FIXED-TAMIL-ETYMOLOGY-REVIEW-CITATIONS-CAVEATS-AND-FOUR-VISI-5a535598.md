## Fixed — Tamil etymology review: citations, caveats and four visible origins

A full accuracy review of the 1,200 etymology blocks and hooks added in the previous entry. Every DEDR, DEDR-appendix and CDIAL number was set beside its article in the moli-mandala/data files. The review checked each cognate, each hedge and each "no entry" claim, and checked forward references in reading order. No source text is copied.

- **Wrong entry (1).** TA-C256-tavaravitu cited DEDR 3068 for *tavaṟu*. In that entry *tavaṟu* means only "to die". The "slip, fail, err" verb is DEDR 3071. Block and hook now cite 3071.
- **Source misreported (12).**
  - TA-C43-family's hook credited CDIAL 3233 with a Dravidian origin it does not state. It now cites DEDR 1655, which sets *kuṭumba* beside native *kuṭi*.
  - TA-C47-perhaps now adds CDIAL 12115's own note that *velā* probably came from Dravidian.
  - TA-C140-kunru named Kannada *koṇḍa*, which DEDR 1864 marks as borrowed from Telugu. It now names Telugu *koṇḍa*.
  - TA-C147-comperi glossed Kannada *jompisu* "to doze". DEDR has "to be stupefied". *Sōmāri*, "sluggard", was added.
  - TA-C181-pitittamana gave *piṭittam* a gloss it does not have. The "pleasing" sense belongs to the verb *piṭi*.
  - TA-C278-murukku said DEDR glosses the Tamil noun "a twisted cake". That gloss is on Malayalam *muṟukku*.
  - TA-C101-aunt and TA-C250-kutaram named only the Prakrit word. DEDR also compares Sanskrit (lexicon) *attā* and *kuṭaru*.
  - In four hooks a query in DEDR had dropped out. TA-C228-tattaccu-cey and TA-C256-accitu: *accu* and Sanskrit *sañcaka*. TA-C253-oyvutiyam: *ūtiyam*, whose DEDR gloss is "profit, benefit", not "pay". TA-C233-nerru-muntinam: *nēṟṟu* is "probably" short for *nerunaṟṟu*.
- **"Uncertain / no entry" when a source records the word (5).**
  - TA-C278-karumpu is native: DEDR 1288 lists Ma. *karimpu*, Ka. *kabbu* and Tu. *karmbu*.
  - TA-C220-vicaippalakai: *vicai*, "spring, lever", is native (DEDR 5450).
  - Probable Indo-Aryan sources, still hedged:
    - TA-C244-pottalam: Prakrit *poṭṭala*, "bundle" (CDIAL 8396).
    - TA-C201-tati: Prakrit *dāḍhiā*, "beard" (CDIAL 6250).
    - TA-C181-mocamana: Sanskrit *mōṣa*, "theft" (CDIAL 10358).
- **Possible sources added, origin still uncertain (3).** TA-C231-camali: Indo-Aryan *sambhāḷ-* (CDIAL 12962). TA-C232-kayam: Prakrit *ghāya*, "wound" (CDIAL 4460). TA-C96-adada: the nearest entry is the exclamation *aṭā* (DEDR 70).
- **DEDR comparisons restored (74 lessons).** In each case DEDR compares the word with a Sanskrit or Indo-Aryan form of the same sense, and the block had left the comparison out. The comparison now appears in the block, or in the hook of a lesson that has only a hook. A query stays a query. Examples:
  - *ām* and Sanskrit *ām*; *tamiḻ* and *draviḍa*; *kuḻantai* and *kuḍaka*; *makaḷ* and *mālikā*.
  - *paḻam* and *phala* (7 lessons); *kāy* and *\*kacca* (5); *kaṭṭil* and *khaṭvā*; *kaḻuttu* and *kaṇṭha*.
  - *kākkai* and *kāka*, with DEDR's note on reborrowing; *peṭṭi* and *peṭaka*; *viḷaiyāṭu* and *?vilāsa*.

  Comparisons were left out when they belong to a different word or sense in the same entry. Examples: *uṇḍeraka* goes with *uṇṭai* "ball", *piṇyāka* with *piṇṭi* "oilcake", and *kalā* with *kalai* "arts", not the verb *kal*. They were also left out when they are Marathi, Prakrit (Deśī) or other Indo-Aryan words borrowed from Dravidian, or proper names.
- **Hedges, forms and donors.**
  - TA-C222-tallupati: *paṭi*, "measure", is now "probably".
  - TA-C272-vari: the "tax" sense is now "probably" the line in a register.
  - TA-C163-tayar-cey: Arabic *ṭayyār* means "flying, swift". "Ready" is the Persian and Hindustani sense.
  - TA-C108-longtime: *āccu*, not *ācci*.
  - TA-C169-itayam: *ṛ* became *i*; it was not dropped.
  - TA-C200-urincu: Malayalam *uṟiñcuka*, not *uṛiñcuka*.
- **Four visible origins.** TA-C06-dative-subject, TA-C37-ivar, TA-C37-ivar-en-nanbar and TA-C39-evvalavu each gained one origin sentence in an existing section. Redundant prose was trimmed to make room. Every Tamil word lesson now shows its origin in the body.
- **Durations.** One raise: TA-C39-evvalavu 294 → 298 s. Every other edited lesson still estimates at or under its declared length. TA-C86-rational stays at 280 s because its block was reworded.
- **Not changed.** Roots and `hl-knowledge` lines are untouched. Some romanizations used across a whole lesson are off, and the blocks copy them. They are left for a separate fix: *ottakam* (TA-C142-ottakam), *poṭuvāka* (TA-C207-potuvaka) and *cataraṅkam* for *caturaṅkam* (TA-C249-catarankam).
