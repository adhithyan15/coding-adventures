## You'll want to know: वादयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C520-SVARA520-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वादयति** — *vādayati* — "plays an instrument".

> सः वेणुं वादयति।
>
> *saḥ veṇuṃ vādayati.*
>
> He plays the flute.

Past (he …): सः वीणां वादितवान्, *saḥ vīṇāṃ vāditavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वादयति** (*vādayati*) comes from the causative of the root √vad, "to speak"; MW gives *vādayati* as to make sound, to strike, to play an instrument (MW).
