## You'll want to know: der Pate
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C491-PATEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Pate** — "godfather; godparent".

> Mein Onkel ist der Pate meiner Tochter.
>
> *My uncle is my daughter's godfather.*

Weak noun: *den Paten*. A godmother is *die Patin* or *die Patentante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pate* comes from Middle High German *pate* (EtymDB), usually traced to Church Latin *pater* "father", as in *pater spiritualis* "spiritual father".
