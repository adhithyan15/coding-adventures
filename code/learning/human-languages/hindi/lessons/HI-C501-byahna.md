## You'll want to know: ब्याहना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C501-NATA501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्याहना** — *byāhnā* — "to marry; to marry off".

> पिता ने बेटी को अच्छे परिवार में ब्याहा।
>
> *pitā ne beṭī ko acche parivār meṁ byāhā.*
>
> The father married his daughter into a good family.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बेटी को ब्याहा, *usne beṭī ko byāhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्याहना** (*byāhnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *byāhnā*, "to marry", under Sanskrit *vivāhayati* (CDIAL 11923), the verb beside *vivāha*, "marriage".
