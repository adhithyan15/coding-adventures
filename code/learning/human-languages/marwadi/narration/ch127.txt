Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 127: Nail, Hammer, Axe, Plough, Bullock cart.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खीलो (khīlo) — a nail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a rope, then the Marwadi for a peg.

You'll want to know: खीलो.
खीलो — khīlo — "a nail".

The word, taken apart.
खीलो (khīlo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kīla, "stake, peg". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 3202); the match is through Gujarati khīlo. CDIAL notes that the k/kh variation points to a non-Aryan source.

You'll want to know: हथोड़ो.
हथोड़ो — hathoṛo — "a hammer".

The word, taken apart.
हथोड़ो (hathoṛo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from hastakūṭa, "hand hammer", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 14028); the match is through Hindi hathauṛā and Gujarati hathoṛo. It is built on hasta, "hand", and kūṭa, "hammer".

The word, taken apart.
बैलगाड़ी (bailgāṛī) is an ox cart: bail, "ox", which CDIAL lists as Hindi bail under balilla, "ox" (CDIAL 9175), and gāṛī, "cart", from gāḍḍa (CDIAL 4116).
