## You'll want to know: al mismo tiempo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C536-MIENTRAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**al mismo tiempo** — "at the same time".

> Los dos hablaron al mismo tiempo.
>
> *They both spoke at the same time.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*al mismo tiempo* is built on *mismo*, from spoken Latin *\*metipsimus* 'the very same', and *tiempo*, inherited from Latin *tempus* 'time' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
