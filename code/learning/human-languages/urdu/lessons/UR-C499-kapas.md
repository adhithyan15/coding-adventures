## You'll want to know: کپاس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C499-CHARKHA499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کپاس** — *kapās* — "cotton (the plant)".

> کسان نے اس سال کپاس بوئی۔
>
> *kisān ne is sāl kapās boī.*
>
> The farmer sowed cotton this year.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کپاس** (*kapās*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karpāsa*, "the cotton plant" (CDIAL 2877).
