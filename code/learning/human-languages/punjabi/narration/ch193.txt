Lesson 2 of 8.
Change first inside one familiar room frame — first in one masculine oblique room frame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਪਹਿਲਾ, then ਕਮਰਾ, then ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਪਹਿਲਾ ਕਮਰਾ — pahilā kamrā — the first room.
ਪਹਿਲੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ — pahile kamre vicc — in the first room.
The familiar direct form has the final -aa sound. In this masculine singular phrase, the noun is oblique before ਵਿੱਚ, and the ordinal takes final -e.

Grammar Lens: source boundary.
Gill and Gleason's masculine adjective table contrasts the singular direct and singular oblique patterns. Here that contrast is ਪਹਿਲਾ beside ਪਹਿਲੇ (pahile). Their following numeral section lists first ਪਹਿਲਾ and second ਦੂਜਾ among Punjabi ordinals. This chapter follows these two ordinals, not a claim that every Punjabi adjective changes this way. Source: Harjeet Singh Gill and Henry A. Gleason, Jr., A Reference Grammar of Punjabi (1969), pp. 71–73, adjectives and numerals.

Lesson 4 of 8.
Apply the same bounded change to second — second in one masculine oblique room frame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਦੂਜਾ, then the room-frame change you just practised.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਦੂਜਾ ਕਮਰਾ — dūjā kamrā — the second room.
ਦੂਜੇ (duuje) ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ — dūje kamre vicc — in the second room.
The boundary stays narrow: the familiar direct ordinal changes from final -aa to -e in this same masculine singular oblique room frame.
