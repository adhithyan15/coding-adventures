# இலக்கு (ilakku) — a goal, a target

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C290-ACAI-01, TA-LEX-C46-ROLE-03, TA-LEX-C46-ROLE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I have a wish." (*eṉakku oru ācai irukkiṟatu*.) Then the words for a doctor and a teacher. (*maruttuvar*, *āciriyar*.)

## You'll want to know: இலக்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C290-ILAKKU-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C02-EN-01, TA-LEX-C46-ROLE-03, TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-05, TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-04, TA-LEX-C44-ASK-05] -->

**இலக்கு** — *ilakku* — "a goal, a target".

> **என் இலக்கு மருத்துவர் ஆவது.**
>
> *eṉ ilakku maruttuvar āvatu.* — "My goal is to become a doctor."

**ஆவது** (*āvatu*, "becoming"). Where **ஆசை** is a longing, **இலக்கு** is a mark you aim at and work towards. The same word names the target an arrow is shot at.

> **இந்த ஆண்டு என் இலக்கு: மாதம் ஒரு புத்தகம்.**
>
> *inta āṇṭu eṉ ilakku: mātam oru puttakam.* — "My goal this year: a book a month."

**இந்த** (*inta*, "this"): the **i-** of **இது** and **இங்கே**, in front of a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இலக்கு** (*ilakku*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *lakṣya* or *lakṣa*, "mark, aim": CDIAL 10881 lists *lakṣá* "mark, sign" and "aim", and *lakṣya-* "aim". The **இ** in front is the vowel older Tamil set before a borrowed word that began with *l*.
