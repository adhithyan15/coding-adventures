## You'll want to know: a autocaravana
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C426-TENDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a autocaravana** — "the camper van, the motorhome".

> Viajámos pelo país de autocaravana.
>
> *We travelled round the country in a camper van.*

In Brazil: *o motorhome*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*autocaravana* is built from *auto-* 'self-moving', from Greek *autós* 'self', and *caravana*, which comes from Persian *kārvān* (EtymDB).
