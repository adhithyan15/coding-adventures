## You'll want to know: शिकायत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C290-THANA290-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शिकायत** — *śikāyat* — "a complaint".

> पुलिस क्या करेगी, यह आपकी शिकायत पर निर्भर करता है।
>
> *pulis kyā karegī, yah āpkī śikāyat par nirbhar kartā hai.*
>
> What the police will do depends on your complaint.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shikāya*, "a complaint". **शिकायत करना** is to complain; at a police station, **शिकायत** is the formal complaint.
