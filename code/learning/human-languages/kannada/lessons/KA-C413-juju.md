# ಜೂಜು (jūju) — gambling

## You'll want to know: ಜೂಜು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C413-PANA413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೂಜು** — *jūju* — "gambling".

> ಜೂಜಿನಿಂದ ಅವನು ಮನೆ ಕಳೆದುಕೊಂಡನು.
>
> *jūjininda avanu mane kaḷedukoṇḍanu.*
>
> He lost his house through gambling.

ಜೂಜಾಡು, to gamble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೂಜು** (*jūju*) is usually traced to Prakrit *jūa*, "gambling", from Sanskrit *dyūta*, "gambling" (CDIAL 6608); Kannada is not among the forms CDIAL lists.
