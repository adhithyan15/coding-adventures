## You'll want to know: طارد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C346-JASUS346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طارد** — *ṭārada* — "to chase, to pursue".

> طاردت الشرطة السيارة في الشوارع.
>
> *ṭāradat ash-shurṭa as-sayyāra fī ash-shawāriʿ.*
>
> The police chased the car through the streets.

Present: يطارد, *yuṭārid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طارد** (*ṭārada*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-r-d* (Lane), "to drive away". It is Form III, "to drive at someone, to hunt him".
