## You'll want to know: ūmidus, ūmida, ūmidum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C95-CALIDUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ūmidus, ūmida, ūmidum** — "wet, damp". English *humid*.
