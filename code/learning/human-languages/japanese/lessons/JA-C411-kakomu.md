## You'll want to know: かこむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C411-OGAWA411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かこむ** — *kakomu* — "to surround".

> **もりが いずみを かこんでいます。** — *mori ga izumi o kakonde imasu* — "Forest surrounds the spring."

Polite **かこみます**, *kakomimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **みんなが せんせいを かこみました。**, *minna ga sensei o kakomimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かこむ** (*kakomu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
