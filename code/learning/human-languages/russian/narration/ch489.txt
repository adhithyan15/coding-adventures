Russian, chapter 489: A Check-up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обследовать (obslédovat) — to examine (medically).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a nightmare, then the Russian for to doze.

You'll want to know: обследовать.
обследовать (obslédovat) — "to examine (medically)".
Врач внимательно обследовал пациента. Vrach vnimátelno obslédoval patsiyénta. The doctor examined the patient carefully.
Imperfective and perfective (one form for both). Present обследую.

The word, taken apart.
обследовать (obslédovat) is built on об- and следовать 'to follow', from след 'track, trail' (EtymDB lists all three without a source).

Lesson 2 of 5.
обследование (obslédovaniye) — a medical examination, a check-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to doze, then the Russian for to examine.

You'll want to know: обследование.
обследование (obslédovaniye) — "a medical examination, a check-up".
Каждый год я прохожу обследование. Kázhdyy god ya prokhozhú obslédovaniye. Every year I have a check-up.
Neuter. Проходить обследование (obslédovaniye) — to have a check-up.

The word, taken apart.
обследование (obslédovaniye) is built on обследовать (obslédovat) 'to examine' with -ание; the root is that of след 'track, trail', which EtymDB lists without a source, and EtymDB has no entry for the noun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
анализ (análiz) — a medical test; analysis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to examine, then the Russian for a medical examination.

You'll want to know: анализ.
анализ (análiz) — "a medical test; analysis".
Медсестра взяла у меня анализ крови. Medsestrá vzyalá u menyá análiz króvi. The nurse took a blood sample from me.
Masculine. Сдать анализы — to have tests done.

The word, taken apart.
анализ (análiz) is a learned borrowing, usually traced through French or German to Greek análysis 'a loosening'; EtymDB has no entry for the Russian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
рентген (rentgén) — an X-ray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a medical examination, then the Russian for a medical test.

You'll want to know: рентген.
рентген (rentgén) — "an X-ray".
Мне сделали рентген руки. Mne sdélali rentgén rukí. They X-rayed my arm.
Masculine. Сделать рентген (rentgén) — to have an X-ray.

The word, taken apart.
рентген (rentgén) was borrowed from German Röntgen 'X-ray' (EtymDB), a word made from the surname of the physicist who found the rays.

Lesson 5 of 5.
хирург (khirúrg) — a surgeon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: хирург.

Хирург сказал, что операция прошла хорошо. Khirúrg skazál, chto operátsiya proshlá khoroshó. The surgeon said that the operation had gone well.
Masculine; also of a woman.

The word, taken apart.
хирург (khirúrg) was borrowed from Greek kheirourgós 'surgeon' (EtymDB), a compound of kheír 'hand' and érgon 'work'.
