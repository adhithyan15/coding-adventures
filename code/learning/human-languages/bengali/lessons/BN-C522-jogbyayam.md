## You'll want to know: যোগব্যায়াম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C522-BYAYAM522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**যোগব্যায়াম** — *jogbyāyām* — "yoga (as exercise)".

> দাদু রোজ সকালে যোগব্যায়াম করেন।
>
> *Dādu roj sôkāle jogbyāyām kôren.*
>
> My grandfather does yoga every morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যোগব্যায়াম** (*jogbyāyām*) joins Sanskrit *yoga*, "joining, discipline", and *vyāyāma*, "exertion, struggle" (Monier-Williams); ব্যায়াম on its own is exercise.
