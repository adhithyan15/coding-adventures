## You'll want to know: ঘর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C403-GHOR403-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘর** — *ghôr* — "a room (in a house); a home".

> হোটেলে আমাদের ঘরটা ছোট, কিন্তু পরিচ্ছন্ন।
>
> *hoṭele āmāder ghôrṭā chhoṭo, kintu pôrichchhônnô.*
>
> Our room at the hotel is small but clean.

ঘর is any room, and home as well: ঘরে ফেরা, to come home. রুম is the hotel word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘর** (*ghôr*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ghar*, "a house", under *ghara*, "a house" (CDIAL 4428), and notes that the usual link with Sanskrit *gṛha* is hard to make work by sound.
