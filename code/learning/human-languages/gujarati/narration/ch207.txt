Gujarati, chapter 207: Taking a taxi.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગાડી (gāḍī) — car, cart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for stopping place, then the Gujarati for speed.

You'll want to know: ગાડી.
ગાડી — gāḍī — "car, cart".
અમારી ગાડી નવી હતી. — amārī gāḍī navī hatī — "Our car was new."

The word, taken apart.
ગાડી (gāḍī) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "carriage", under gāḍḍa, "cart", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 4116).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભાડે લેવું (bhāḍe levũ) — to hire, to rent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for driver, then the Gujarati for petrol.

You'll want to know: ભાડે લેવું.
ભાડે લેવું — bhāḍe levũ — "to hire, to rent".
અમે એક ગાડી ભાડે લીધી. — ame ek gāḍī bhāḍe līdhī — "We hired a car."
ભાડે, bhāḍe, 'on hire', is a form of ભાડું, 'rent'; with લેવું it means to hire.

The word, taken apart.
ભાડે લેવું (bhāḍe levũ) is built from bhāḍe, "on hire", and levũ, "to take". CDIAL traces the hire word, Prakrit bhāḍaya, "hire", to bhārta, "pertaining to servants", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9468).
