## You'll want to know: सीमाशुल्क
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C405-VIMAN405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सीमाशुल्क** — *sīmāśulka* — "border customs duty".

> विमानतळावर आम्ही सीमाशुल्क भरलं.
>
> *vimāntaḷāvar āmhī sīmāśulka bharlaṁ.*
>
> We paid customs duty at the airport.

Neuter.

The official word on forms and boards; the older word is जकात.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सीमाशुल्क** (*sīmāśulka*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *sīmā*, "boundary", and *śulka*, "toll, price" (Monier-Williams): the toll paid at the border.
