## You'll want to know: emocionarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C704-ALEGRIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**emocionarse** — "to be moved, to get emotional".

> Mi madre se emociona con las bodas.
>
> *My mother gets emotional at weddings.*

Reflexive: *me emociono*, *te emocionas*. It is about being touched, not about being angry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emocionarse* is built on *emoción*, which came from French *émotion* (EtymDB); the French word goes back to Latin *ēmovēre* "to move out, to stir up", from *movēre* "to move".
