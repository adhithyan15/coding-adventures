# નાટક (nāṭak) — drama, play

## You'll want to know: નાટક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C255-STORY255-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નાટક** — *nāṭak* — "drama, play".

> **અમે શનિવારે નાટક જોયું.** — *ame śanivāre nāṭak joyũ* — "We saw a play on Saturday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નાટક** (*nāṭak*) is Sanskrit *nāṭaka*, "dancer, actor" and later "drama", taken over as a tatsama (CDIAL 7045).
