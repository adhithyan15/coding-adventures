## You'll want to know: चिल्लर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C426-ROKAD426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चिल्लर** — *cillar* — "small change".

> माझ्याकडे चिल्लर नाही.
>
> *mājhyākaḍe cillar nāhī.*
>
> I have no small change.

Feminine.

Coins and small notes; it also means petty, trivial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिल्लर** (*cillar*) is a Marathi word for loose coins; its origin is not settled.
