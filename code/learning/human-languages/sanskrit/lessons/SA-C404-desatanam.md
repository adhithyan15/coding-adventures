# देशाटनम् (deśāṭanam) — travel through the country

## You'll want to know: देशाटनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C404-PRANTARA404-03]; assesses=[] -->

**देशाटनम्** — *deśāṭanam* — "travel through the country".

> देशाटनेन ज्ञानं वर्धते।
>
> *deśāṭanena jñānaṃ vardhate.*
>
> Knowledge grows by travelling through the country.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देशाटनम्** (*deśāṭanam*) is built from *deśa*, "a country", and *aṭana*, "roaming", from the root √aṭ, "to roam", which also gives *paryaṭaka*, "a tourist"; MW gives the compound as roaming through a land, travelling (MW).
