## You'll want to know: निष्कर्ष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C345-NISHKARSH345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कर्ष** — *niṣkarṣa* — "a conclusion".

> शेवटी आम्ही एका निष्कर्षावर आलो.
>
> *śevṭī āmhī ekā niṣkarṣāvar ālo.*
>
> In the end we came to a conclusion.

Masculine.

निष्कर्ष काढणे, to draw a conclusion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कर्ष** (*niṣkarṣa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *niṣkarṣa*, "drawing out, extracting" (Monier-Williams). A conclusion is what is drawn out of the facts.
