## You'll want to know: उछाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C250-UCHHAV250-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उछाव** — *uchhāv* — "festive joy; a celebration".

> गांव मांय घणो उछाव है।
>
> *gā̃v mā̃y ghaṇo uchhāv hai.*
>
> There is great festive joy in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उछाव** (*uchhāv*) is inherited from Sanskrit *utsāha*, "power, joy"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *uchāha*, "merriment, festival", under it (CDIAL 1882).
