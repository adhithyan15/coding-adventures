## You'll want to know: a reação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C352-REACAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a reação** — "the reaction".

> A reação dele foi uma surpresa para todos.
>
> *His reaction was a surprise to everyone.*

AO90 *reação*; older *reacção*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reação* is a learned word modelled on French *réaction* (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *reactiō*, built on *re-* "back" and *agere* "to do, to act" (Lewis & Short).
