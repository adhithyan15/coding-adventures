## You'll want to know: せきにん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C364-SONGAI364-05]; assesses=[] -->

**せきにん** — *sekinin* — "responsibility".

> **もし こわしたら、せきにんを とって ください。** — *moshi kowashitara, sekinin o totte kudasai* — "If you break it, please take responsibility."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せきにん** (*sekinin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *seki*, "blame" (Middle Chinese *tsreak*) and *nin*, "duty" (*nyim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
