## You'll want to know: fǎlǜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C363-PUBLIC363-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fǎlǜ* — "law; the law".

> *wǒmen bìxū zūnshǒu fǎlǜ* — We must obey the law.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fǎlǜ* is built from 法 'a rule' and *lǜ*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjop* and *lwit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a rule' and the second 'the pitch pipes' and 'a law'.
