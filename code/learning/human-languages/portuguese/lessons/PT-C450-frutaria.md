## You'll want to know: a frutaria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C450-MERCEARIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a frutaria** — "the fruit shop, the greengrocer".

> Na frutaria, as laranjas estão baratas.
>
> *At the fruit shop, oranges are cheap.*

Built on *a fruta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frutaria* is built on *fruta* 'fruit', which goes back to Latin *frūctus* 'enjoyment, fruit' (Lewis & Short), through a Late Latin plural form *frūcta*.
