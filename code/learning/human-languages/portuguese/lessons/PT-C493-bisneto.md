## You'll want to know: o bisneto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C493-GERACOES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o bisneto** — "great-grandson".

> A senhora de noventa anos já tem dois bisnetos.
>
> *The ninety-year-old lady already has two great-grandchildren.*

*A bisneta* — great-granddaughter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bisneto* is built from *bis-* "twice" and *neto* "grandson", which goes back to Latin *nepōs* "grandson" (Lewis & Short).
