## You'll want to know: مستهلك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C459-MIZANIYYA459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مستهلك** — *mustahlik* — "a consumer".

> يحب المستهلك الأسعار الرخيصة.
>
> *yuḥibbu al-mustahlik al-asʿār ar-rakhīṣa.*
>
> The consumer likes cheap prices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستهلك** (*mustahlik*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-l-k*, "to perish" (Lane). It is the Form X participle, "one who uses things up".
