## You'll want to know: まどぐち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C205-TICKET205-01]; assesses=[] -->

**まどぐち** — *madoguchi* — "a ticket window; a service counter".

> **まどぐちで きっぷを かいます。** — *madoguchi de kippu o kaimasu* — "I buy a ticket at the ticket window."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まどぐち** (*madoguchi*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *mado*, "window", and *kuchi*, "mouth, opening", both inherited rather than borrowed; *kuchi* is voiced to *guchi* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
