Spanish, chapter 530: En peligro.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
arriesgado — risky, daring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the rubble, then the Spanish for to destroy.

You'll want to know: arriesgado.
arriesgado — "risky, daring".
Fue una idea muy arriesgada. It was a very risky idea.
Arriesgarse is to take a risk.

The word, taken apart.
arriesgado is built on the participle of arriesgar 'to risk', from riesgo 'a risk' (EtymDB). EtymDB takes riesgo back to Italian rischio; where that word came from is not settled.

amenaza comes from spoken Latin mināciā 'a threat' (EtymDB), built on mināx 'threatening', from minārī 'to threaten'.
