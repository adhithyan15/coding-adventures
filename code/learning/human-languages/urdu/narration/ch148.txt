Lesson 1 of 5.
چننا (chunnā) — to choose, to pick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to smile, then the Urdu for to panic.

You'll want to know: چننا.
چننا — chunnā — "to choose, to pick".

The word, taken apart.
چننا (chunnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cinoti, "heaps up, collects" (CDIAL 4814). CDIAL explains the u as taken from other verbs.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بدلنا (badalnā) — to change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to panic, then the Urdu for to choose.

You'll want to know: بدلنا.
بدلنا — badalnā — "to change".

The word, taken apart.
بدلنا (badalnā) is a loan from Arabic badal, "a substitute, an exchange", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is b-d-l (Lane). Urdu made a verb of it with -nā.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ادھار لینا (udhār lenā) — to borrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to choose, then the Urdu for to change.

You'll want to know: ادھار لینا.
ادھار لینا — udhār lenā — "to borrow".

The phrase, taken apart.
ادھار لینا (udhār lenā) joins udhār, "a debt, credit", and lenā, "to take". udhār comes from Sanskrit uddhāra, "a raising; a loan" (CDIAL 2018), and lenā from Sanskrit labhate, "catches, takes" (CDIAL 10948).

قرض دینا (qarz denā) — to lend (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to change, then the Urdu for to borrow.

You'll want to know: قرض دینا.
قرض دینا — qarz denā — "to lend (money)".

The phrase, taken apart.
قرض دینا (qarz denā) joins qarz, "a loan", and denā, "to give". qarz is Arabic qarḍ, "a loan", which reached Urdu through Persian (Lane, root q-r-ḍ); denā comes from Sanskrit dadāti, "gives" (CDIAL 6141).

Lesson 5 of 5.
بچانا (bachānā) — to save, to rescue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to borrow, then the Urdu for to lend.

You'll want to know: بچانا.
بچانا — bachānā — "to save, to rescue".

The word, taken apart.
بچانا (bachānā) is the causing form of bachnā, "to escape": to save is to make someone escape. CDIAL lists bachnā under Sanskrit vañcati, "moves; escapes from" (CDIAL 11208).
