## You'll want to know: મરામત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C152-READ152-02]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-THASHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-JAISH-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01] -->

**મરામત** (*marāmat*) — "a repair, repair work".

It is the job of putting something right: a window, a door, a road.

> **આવતીકાલે મરામત થશે.** — *āvtīkāle marāmat thaśe.* — The repair will be done tomorrow.
> **તે મરામત કરશે.** — *te marāmat karśe.* — He will do the repair.

**થશે** and **કરશે** split the work the way English *be done* and *do* do: **થશે**
when the repair happens, **કરશે** when someone does it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મરામત** (*marāmat*) came into Gujarati through Persian from Arabic *maramma*,
"a repairing". Its root is *r-m-m*, "to repair, to put into a sound state", said
in Lane of a worn rope, a house or a wall (Lane).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for the notes people leave for each other.
