## You'll want to know: మృదంగం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C538-SANGITAM538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మృదంగం** — *mṛdaṅgaṁ* — "a mridangam drum".

> పాటకు తోడుగా ఆయన మృదంగం వాయించారు.
>
> *pāṭaku tōḍugā āyana mṛdaṅgaṁ vāyiñcāru.*
>
> He played the mridangam to accompany the song.

A two-headed barrel drum of South Indian music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మృదంగం** (*mṛdaṅgaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mṛdaṁga*, "a kind of drum" (Monier-Williams).
