## You'll want to know: решаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C379-ZAPASNOY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**решаться** (*reshátsya*) — "to make up one’s mind, to dare".

> Он никак не решается сказать ей правду.
>
> *On nikák ne resháyetsya skazát yey právdu.*
>
> He cannot bring himself to tell her the truth.

Imperfective; perfective *решиться*. *Решаться на* + accusative, or + infinitive. Not the same as *решать*, to solve or decide.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**решаться** is the reflexive of *решать* "to decide", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*rěšiti* "to untie, to loosen": to make up your mind is to untie yourself from doubt.
