## You'll want to know: आकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C505-MRD505-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आकारः** — *ākāraḥ* — "a shape".

> एतस्य पात्रस्य आकारः सुन्दरः अस्ति।
>
> *etasya pātrasya ākāraḥ sundaraḥ asti.*
>
> This pot has a beautiful shape.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकारः** (*ākāraḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √kṛ, "to make"; MW gives *ākāra* as form, figure, shape (MW).
