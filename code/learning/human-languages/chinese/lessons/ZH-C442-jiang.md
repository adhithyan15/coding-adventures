## You'll want to know: jiāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C442-RIVER442-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāng* — "a great river; big river".

> *zhè tiáo jiāng bǐ nà tiáo hé cháng* — This great river is longer than that one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kaewng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it with the pair 'rivers and seas' and lists the great rivers of the south under it.
