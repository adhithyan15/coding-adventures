Marathi, chapter 228: To praise, To go forward, To go back, To defend, To disappear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कौतुक करणे (kautuk karṇe) — to praise, to admire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to float, then the Marathi for to chase.

You'll want to know: कौतुक करणे.
कौतुक करणे — kautuk karṇe — "to praise, to admire".

The word, taken apart.
कौतुक is Sanskrit kautuka, "curiosity, festivity", a tatsama (CDIAL 3562). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: बचाव करणे.
बचाव करणे — bacāv karṇe — "to defend".

The word, taken apart.
बचाव, "defence", is built on the verb stem bac-, "to escape, be saved", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit vañcati (CDIAL 11208). करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 5 of 5.
नाहीसे होणे (nāhīse hoṇe) — to disappear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to go back, then the Marathi for to defend.

You'll want to know: नाहीसे होणे.
नाहीसे होणे — nāhīse hoṇe — "to disappear".

The word, taken apart.
नाहीसे is "not-being", built on नाही, "not", which rests on the old negative na. होणे comes from Sanskrit bhavati, "becomes, is" (CDIAL 9416).
