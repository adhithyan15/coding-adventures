Lesson 1 of 5.
संविधान (saṁvidhān) — a constitution.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: संविधान.
संविधान — saṁvidhān — "a constitution".
संविधानाने प्रत्येक नागरिकाला समान हक्क दिले आहेत. saṁvidhānāne pratyek nāgarikālā samān hakka dile āhet. The constitution has given every citizen equal rights.
Neuter.
Neuter; also राज्यघटना.

The word, taken apart.
संविधान (saṁvidhān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit saṁvidhāna, "arrangement, disposition" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
लागू करणे (lāgū karṇe) — to enforce; to bring into force.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a constitution, then the Marathi for a policy.

You'll want to know: लागू करणे.
लागू करणे — lāgū karṇe — "to enforce; to bring into force".
सरकारने नवा नियम लागू केला. sarkārne navā niyam lāgū kelā. The government brought a new rule into force.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नियम लागू केला, tyāne niyam lāgū kelā.
लागू होणे, to apply: हा नियम सगळ्यांना लागू होतो.

The word, taken apart.
लागू करणे (lāgū karṇe) joins लागू, "in force, applying", built on लागणे, "to attach", which is inherited from Sanskrit lagyati, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895), to करणे.

Lesson 4 of 5.
रद्द करणे (radda karṇe) — to cancel; to repeal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a policy, then the Marathi for to enforce.

You'll want to know: रद्द करणे.
रद्द करणे — radda karṇe — "to cancel; to repeal".
पावसामुळे शाळेने सहल रद्द केली. pāvsāmuḷe śāḷene sahal radda kelī. Because of the rain, the school cancelled the trip.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने रद्द केलं, tyāne radda kelaṁ.
रद्द होणे, to be cancelled.

The word, taken apart.
रद्द करणे (radda karṇe) joins रद्द, "void", a loan from Persian usually traced to Arabic radd, "returning, rejection", to करणे, "to make".

Lesson 5 of 5.
बहाल करणे (bahāl karṇe) — to grant; to confer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to enforce, then the Marathi for to cancel.

You'll want to know: बहाल करणे.
बहाल करणे — bahāl karṇe — "to grant; to confer".
संविधानाने स्त्रियांना मताचा हक्क बहाल केला. saṁvidhānāne striyānnā matācā hakka bahāl kelā. The constitution granted women the right to vote.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बहाल केलं, tyāne bahāl kelaṁ.
The receiver takes -ला: स्त्रियांना हक्क बहाल करणे (bahāl karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
बहाल करणे (bahāl karṇe) joins बहाल, "confirmed, granted", a loan usually traced to Persian ba-ḥāl, "in (its proper) state", built on Arabic ḥāl, "state", to करणे, "to make".
