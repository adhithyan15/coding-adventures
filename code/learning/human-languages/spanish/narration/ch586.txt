Spanish, chapter 586: Dar explicaciones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
justificar — to justify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to warn, then the Spanish for the warning.

You'll want to know: justificar.
justificar — "to justify".
Tienes que justificar los gastos. You have to justify the expenses.
Spelling: justifiqué.

The word, taken apart.
justificar is a learned borrowing from Late Latin iustificare 'to make right', built on iustus 'right, lawful' and facere 'to make' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el pretexto — the pretext.

You'll want to know: el pretexto.
el pretexto — "the pretext".
Usó la lluvia como pretexto. He used the rain as a pretext.
Con el pretexto de — on the pretext of.

The word, taken apart.
pretexto is a learned borrowing from Latin praetextus 'a cover, a show', built on praetexere 'to weave in front', from texere 'to weave' (Lewis & Short).
