## You'll want to know: કાંકરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C439-HILL439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંકરો** — *kā̃kro* — "a pebble".

> બાળકે તળાવમાં કાંકરો ફેંક્યો.
>
> *bāḷke taḷāvmā̃ kā̃kro phẽkyo.*
>
> The child threw a pebble into the pond.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંકરો** (*kā̃kro*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *karkara*, "a stone"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a pebble" (CDIAL 2820).
