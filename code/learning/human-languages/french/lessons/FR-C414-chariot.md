## You'll want to know: le chariot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C414-COUCHETTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le chariot** — "the trolley (for luggage or shopping)".

> Il pousse un chariot plein de valises.
>
> *He is pushing a trolley full of suitcases.*

In a supermarket, *le chariot* is the shopping trolley.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chariot* is built on Old French *char* 'cart', which is inherited from Latin *carrus* 'wagon' (EtymDB).
