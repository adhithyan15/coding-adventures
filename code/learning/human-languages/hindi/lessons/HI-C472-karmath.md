## You'll want to know: कर्मठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C472-PHURTI472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मठ** — *karmaṭh* — "dedicated; diligent in work".

> मीरा की माँ बहुत कर्मठ हैं, सुबह से रात तक काम करती हैं।
>
> *Mīrā kī māṁ bahut karmaṭh haiṁ, subah se rāt tak kām kartī haiṁ.*
>
> Mira's mother is very dedicated; she works from morning till night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मठ** (*karmaṭh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *karmaṭha*, "capable of work; working diligently" (Monier-Williams), from *karman*, "work".
