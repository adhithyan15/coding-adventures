Gujarati, chapter 61: Plate, Bowl and Spoon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
થાળી (thāḷī) — a plate, a thali.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for jalebi, then the Gujarati for puri.

You'll want to know: થાળી.
થાળી (thāḷī) — "a plate, a thali".

The word, taken apart.
થાળી (thāḷī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sthālī, "earthen dish" (CDIAL 13766).

Lesson 5 of 5.
તપેલી (tapelī) — a cooking pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a spoon, then the Gujarati for a cup.

You'll want to know: તપેલી.
તપેલી (tapelī) — "a cooking pot".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of તપેલી (tapelī) is not settled; CDIAL has no entry for it. It matches Gujarati pātelũ, "brass cooking pot", and Hindi patīlā, "copper pot", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit pātra, "drinking vessel, dish" (CDIAL 8055). Whether તપેલી (tapelī) is the same word with two sounds swapped is not known.
