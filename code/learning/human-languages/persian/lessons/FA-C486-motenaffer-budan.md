## You'll want to know: متنفر بودن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C486-JOY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**متنفر بودن** (*motenaffer budan*) — "to hate".

> او از سرما متنفر است.
>
> *u az sarmâ motenaffer ast.*
>
> She hates the cold.

*motenaffer* 'averse' + *budan* 'to be', used as *az chizi motenaffer budan*. *Motenaffer-am* means 'I hate it'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*motenaffer* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *mutanaffir*, 'averse, recoiling'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *budan*, 'to be', is inherited Persian.
