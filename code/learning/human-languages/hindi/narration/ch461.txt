Hindi, chapter 461: Shyness and Shame.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शर्मिंदा (śarmindā) — ashamed; embarrassed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for desolate, then the Hindi for a longing.

You'll want to know: शर्मिंदा.
शर्मिंदा — śarmindā — "ashamed; embarrassed".
अपनी ग़लती पर अरुण शर्मिंदा था। apnī ġaltī par Aruṇ śarmindā thā. Arun was ashamed of his mistake.

The word, taken apart.
शर्मिंदा (śarmindā) is usually traced to Persian sharminda, "ashamed", the adjective beside the noun that gave Hindi śarm.

Lesson 2 of 5.
सकुचाना (sakucānā) — to shrink back shyly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a longing, then the Hindi for ashamed.

You'll want to know: सकुचाना.
सकुचाना — sakucānā — "to shrink back shyly".
पहली मुलाक़ात में मीरा थोड़ा सकुचाई। pahlī mulāqāt meṁ Mīrā thoṛā sakucāī. At the first meeting Mira was a little shy and held back.
Past (she …): वह सकुचाई, vah sakucāī.

The word, taken apart.
सकुचाना (sakucānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi sakucnā, "to shrink, become narrow or tight", under Sanskrit saṁkucyatē, "shrinks" (CDIAL 12824); sakucānā is its common form for a person who shrinks back out of shyness.

Lesson 3 of 5.
हिचकिचाना (hickicānā) — to hesitate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for ashamed, then the Hindi for to shrink back shyly.

You'll want to know: हिचकिचाना.
हिचकिचाना — hickicānā — "to hesitate".
सवाल पूछने से पहले अरुण थोड़ा हिचकिचाया। savāl pūchne se pahle Aruṇ thoṛā hickicāyā. Arun hesitated a little before asking his question.
Past (he …): वह हिचकिचाया, vah hickicāyā.

The word, taken apart.
हिचकिचाना (hickicānā) is probably built on hicaknā, "to draw back", which CDIAL lists under hicc, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 14083); the doubled -cki- paints the stop-and-start of hesitation.

You'll want to know: लजाना.
लजाना — lajānā — "to blush; to be bashful".
सब लोगों के सामने अपना नाम सुनकर मीरा लजा गई। sab logoṁ ke sāmne apnā nām sunkar Mīrā lajā gaī. Mira blushed when she heard her name in front of everyone.
Past (she …): वह लजा गई, vah lajā gaī.

The word, taken apart.
लजाना (lajānā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi lajānā, "to shame, be ashamed", under Sanskrit lajjátē, "is ashamed" (CDIAL 10909).
