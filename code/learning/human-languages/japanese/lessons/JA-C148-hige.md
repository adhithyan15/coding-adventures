## You'll want to know: ひげ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C148-HAIR148-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひげ** — *hige* — "a beard; a moustache".

> **おじいさんは ひげが ながいです。** — *ojiisan wa hige ga nagai desu* — "My grandfather has a long beard."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひげ** (*hige*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana; the kanji JMdict lists for it were attached later (JMdict).
