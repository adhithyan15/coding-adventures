## You'll want to know: くりかえす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C169-OFTEN169-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くりかえす** — *kurikaesu* — "to repeat; to do again".

> **らいしゅう もういちど くりかえします。** — *raishū mō ichido kurikaeshimasu* — "Next week I will do it over again."

Polite **くりかえします**, *kurikaeshimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くりかえす** (*kurikaesu*) is native Japanese, built from *kuri*, "reeling, winding", and *kaesu*, "to send back", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
