## Script — 日, then seven strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 晚

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**日, four strokes**, narrow on the left.

**The right piece, seven strokes.** A short left-falling stroke and a short stroke that runs across and falls, like the top of 多. A vertical, a horizontal that turns down, and a horizontal under it to close a small box. Then a left-falling stroke, and a vertical that bends right along the bottom and hooks up.

4 + 7 = eleven.
