## You'll want to know: 公交
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C408-BUS408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**公交** — *gōngjiāo* — "public transport".

> **我每天坐公交去学校** *wǒ měitiān zuò gōngjiāo qù xuéxiào* — I take public transport to school every day.

The new character, in pieces: 交 — six strokes: a dot and a bar on top; under them a short stroke falling to the left and a short dot falling to the right; then a long stroke falling to the left, crossed by a long stroke falling to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**公交** (*gōngjiāo*) is built from 公 'public' and 交 'to cross', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *kaew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'shared' and the second 'to join'. It is a short form of a longer name for public transport; the city bus is *gōngjiāochē*.
