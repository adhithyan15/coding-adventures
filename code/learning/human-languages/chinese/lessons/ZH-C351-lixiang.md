## You'll want to know: 理想
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C351-SENSE351-04]; assesses=[] -->

**理想** — *lǐxiǎng* — "ideal; aspiration".

> **我的理想是当医生** *wǒ de lǐxiǎng shì dāng yīshēng* — My dream is to be a doctor.

Two third tones meet in this word: lǐ is said with a rising tone before xiǎng, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**理想** (*lǐxiǎng*) is built from 理 'order' and 想 'to think', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *liX* and *sjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to put in order' and the second 'to think'.
