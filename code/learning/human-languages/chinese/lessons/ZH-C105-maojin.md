## You'll want to know: máojīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C105-BATH105-01]; assesses=[] -->

*máojīn* — "towel".

> *wǒ de máojīn hěn gānjìng* — My towel is clean.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*máojīn* is built from máo and jīn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *maw* and *kin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as hair and fur and explains the second as a cloth worn on the head. How the pair came to mean a towel is not settled.
