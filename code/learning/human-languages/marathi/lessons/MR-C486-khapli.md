## You'll want to know: खपली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C486-IJA486-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खपली** — *khaplī* — "a scab".

> जखमेवर आता खपली आली आहे.
>
> *jakhmevar ātā khaplī ālī āhe.*
>
> A scab has formed on the wound now.

Feminine.

खपली धरणे, a scab forms; खपली काढणे, to pick a scab.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खपली** (*khaplī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
