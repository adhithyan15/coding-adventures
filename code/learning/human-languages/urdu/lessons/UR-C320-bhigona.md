## You'll want to know: بھگونا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C320-ATISHBAZI320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بھگونا** — *bhigonā* — "to drench; to soak".

> بارش نے سب کو بھگو دیا۔
>
> *bārish ne sab ko bhigo diyā.*
>
> The rain drenched everyone.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے تولیہ بھگویا, *us ne tauliyā bhigoyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is the causing partner of **بھیگنا**, to get wet, which CDIAL lists for Hindi under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9500): you **بھگونا**, and things **بھیگنا**.
