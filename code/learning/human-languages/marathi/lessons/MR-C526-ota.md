## You'll want to know: ओटा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C526-OTA526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ओटा** — *oṭā* — "a kitchen counter; a raised platform".

> स्वयंपाकघरातला ओटा स्वच्छ आहे.
>
> *svayampākgharātlā oṭā svaccha āhe.*
>
> The kitchen counter is clean.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओटा** (*oṭā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *oṭā*, "a raised mass of earth or bricks", under *\*ōṭṭā*, "shelter, screen" (CDIAL 2544). The kitchen counter is the same raised platform, moved indoors.
