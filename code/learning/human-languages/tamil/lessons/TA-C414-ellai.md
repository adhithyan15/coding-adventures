## You'll want to know: எல்லை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C414-ELLAI414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**எல்லை** — *ellai* — "a border; a limit".

> எல்லையில் நீண்ட வரிசை இருந்தது.
>
> *ellaiyil nīṇṭa varicai iruntatu.*
>
> There was a long queue at the border.

எல்லைக் கோடு is a boundary line.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**எல்லை** (*ellai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 846), "a limit, a border, a boundary".
