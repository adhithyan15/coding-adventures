## You'll want to know: преимущество
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C346-PLYUSY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**преимущество** (*preimúshchestvo*) — "an advantage".

> У этой квартиры есть одно преимущество: она рядом с метро.
>
> *U étoy kvartíry yest odnó preimúshchestvo: oná ryádom s metró.*
>
> This flat has one advantage: it is next to the metro.

Neuter. *Преимущество перед* + instrumental — an advantage over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**преимущество** is built from *пре-* "over, beyond" and *имущий*, the Church Slavonic participle of *иметь* "to have", from Proto-Slavic *\*jĭměti* (EtymDB): what you have over the others. The *щ* is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian has *ч*.
