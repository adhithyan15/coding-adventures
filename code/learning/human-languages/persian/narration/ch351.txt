Persian, chapter 351: War and victory.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سپاه (sepâh) — an army.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a castle, then the Persian for a crown.

You'll want to know: سپاه.
سپاه (sepâh) — "an army".
سپاه (sepâh) شب به شهر رسید. Sepâh shab be shahr resid. The army reached the city at night.
Literary and military; lashkar is a synonym.

The word, taken apart.
sepâh is a native Persian word that EtymDB glosses "army" without a parent; it is usually traced to Middle Persian spāh.

From piruz "victorious". Also a win in sport.

Lesson 4 of 5.
شکست دادن (shekast dâdan) — to defeat, to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a battle, then the Persian for a victory.

You'll want to know: شکست دادن.
شکست دادن (shekast dâdan) — "to defeat, to beat".
سپاه (sepâh) کوچک دشمن را شکست داد. Sepâh-e kuchak doshman râ shekast dâd. The small army defeated the enemy.
shekast "a break, a defeat" + dâdan "to give". Present shekast midahad.

The word, taken apart.
shekast "a break, a defeat" is the past stem of the native verb shekastan "to break", and dâdan "to give" is inherited Persian: to give someone a defeat.

Lesson 5 of 5.
فتح کردن (fath kardan) — to conquer, to capture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a victory, then the Persian for to defeat.

You'll want to know: فتح کردن.
فتح کردن (fath kardan) — "to conquer, to capture".
سپاه (sepâh) شهر را بعد از یک ماه فتح کرد. Sepâh shahr râ ba'd az yek mâh fath kard. The army captured the city after a month.
Also of a summit: qolle râ fath kard "he conquered the peak". Present fath mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
fath is borrowed from Arabic fatḥ (EtymDB), usually glossed "an opening, a conquest". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.
