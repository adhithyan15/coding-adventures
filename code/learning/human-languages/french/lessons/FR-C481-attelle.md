## You'll want to know: l'attelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C481-ENTORSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'attelle** — "splint".

> Le médecin lui a mis une attelle au doigt.
>
> *The doctor put a splint on his finger.*

Feminine. Lighter than *un plâtre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'attelle* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *astella* (EtymDB), from Latin *astula* "a splinter, a chip of wood" (Lewis & Short).
