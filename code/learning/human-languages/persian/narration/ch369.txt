Persian, chapter 369: Causes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
علت ('ellat) — a cause, a reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a conclusion, then the Persian for to mention.

You'll want to know: علت.
علت ('ellat) — "a cause, a reason".
علت بیماری او هنوز معلوم نیست. 'ellat-e bimâri-ye u hanuz ma'lum nist. The cause of his illness is still not known.
Plural 'elal, an Arabic broken plural. Be 'ellat-e — because of.

The word, taken apart.
علت ('ellat) comes from Arabic 'illa (EtymDB), a word for both a cause and an illness; in Persian it means the cause or reason for something.

Lesson 2 of 5.
عامل ('âmel) — a factor; an agent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to mention, then the Persian for a cause.

You'll want to know: عامل.
عامل ('âmel) — "a factor; an agent".
پول تنها عامل خوشبختی نیست. pul tanhâ 'âmel-e khoshbakhti nist. Money is not the only factor in happiness.
Plural 'avâmel, an Arabic broken plural. Also the person who does a deed.

The word, taken apart.
عامل ('âmel) comes from Arabic 'āmil "worker, doer" (EtymDB): a factor is whatever does the work behind an effect.

Lesson 3 of 5.
باعث شدن (bâ'es shodan) — to cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cause, then the Persian for a factor.

You'll want to know: باعث شدن.
باعث شدن (bâ'es shodan) — "to cause".
باران باعث شد که دیر برسیم. bârân bâ'es shod ke dir berasim. The rain made us arrive late.
Bâ'es "cause" + shodan (stem shav-). With a noun: bâ'es-e chizi shodan; with a clause: bâ'es shod ke + subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
باعث is usually traced to Arabic bā'ith "that which sends or prompts"; with the inherited Persian verb shodan "to become" it means to be the cause of something.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سبب شدن (sabab shodan) — to bring about, to cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a factor, then the Persian for to cause.

You'll want to know: سبب شدن.
سبب شدن (sabab shodan) — "to bring about, to cause".
این خبر سبب شد که همه نگران شوند. in khabar sabab shod ke hame negarân shavand. This news made everyone worried.
Like bâ'es shodan, a little more formal; ke + subjunctive follows.

The word, taken apart.
سبب was borrowed from Arabic sabab "reason, cause" (EtymDB); shodan "to become" is the inherited Persian verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
به علت (be 'ellat-e) — owing to, because of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to cause, then the Persian for to bring about.

You'll want to know: به علت.
به علت (be 'ellat-e) — "owing to, because of".
مدرسه به علت برف تعطیل شد. madrese be 'ellat-e barf ta'til shod. School was closed because of the snow.
Takes a noun with ezafe; for a clause, be 'ellat-e inke …. Formal; in speech be khâter-e.

The word, taken apart.
به علت (be 'ellat-e) joins the Persian preposition be to 'ellat, from Arabic 'illa "cause" (EtymDB): by reason of.
