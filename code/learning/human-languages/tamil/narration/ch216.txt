Lesson 1 of 5.
இணையம் (iṇaiyam) — the internet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a letter, then the Tamil for an envelope; a cover.

You'll want to know: இணையம்.
இணையம் — iṇaiyam — "the internet".

The word, taken apart.
இணையம் (iṇaiyam) is native Dravidian (DEDR 457). It is a modern coinage on iṇai, "to join, to link": "the linked thing".

You'll want to know: கடவுச்சொல்.
கடவுச்சொல் — kaṭavuccol — "a password".

The word, taken apart.
கடவுச்சொல் (kaṭavuccol) is a modern coinage from native parts: kaṭavu, "a passing", from kaṭa, "to pass" (DEDR 1109), and col, "word" (DEDR 2855): "the passing word".
