Italian, chapter 334: In padella.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
soffriggere — to fry gently, to sauté.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the lid, then the Italian for the shallow pan.

You'll want to know: soffriggere.
soffriggere — "to fry gently, to sauté".
Soffriggi la cipolla nell'olio. Fry the onion gently in the oil.
Low heat, a few minutes, a little oil.

The word, taken apart.
soffriggere joins so- "under", here "gently", from Latin sub, and friggere "to fry", from Latin frīgere (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
sfumare — to deglaze, to let the wine cook off; to fade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to brown, then the Italian for to fry.

You'll want to know: sfumare.
sfumare — "to deglaze, to let the wine cook off; to fade".
Sfuma con un bicchiere di vino bianco. Deglaze with a glass of white wine.
A risotto word; colours and sounds sfumano too, they fade.

The word, taken apart.
sfumare is built on fumo "smoke", from Latin fūmus (Lewis & Short): to go off like smoke.

Lesson 5 of 5.
il soffritto — the soffritto, the gently fried base of onion and vegetables.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to fry, then the Italian for to deglaze.

You'll want to know: il soffritto.
il soffritto — "the soffritto, the gently fried base of onion and vegetables".
Il soffritto è la base di molte ricette. Soffritto is the base of many recipes.
Onion, carrot and celery, chopped and fried slowly.

The word, taken apart.
soffritto is the past participle of soffriggere "to fry gently", from Latin frīgere "to fry" (EtymDB).
