Marathi, chapter 526: Kitchen Tools.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शेगडी (śegḍī) — a stove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to spill, then the Marathi for to rinse.

You'll want to know: शेगडी.
शेगडी — śegḍī — "a stove".
आईने शेगडीवर दूध ठेवलं. āīne śegḍīvar dūdh ṭhevlaṁ. Mother put the milk on the stove.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
शेगडी (śegḍī) is the word for a cooking stove; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ओटा (oṭā) — a kitchen counter; a raised platform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rinse, then the Marathi for a stove.

You'll want to know: ओटा.
ओटा — oṭā — "a kitchen counter; a raised platform".
स्वयंपाकघरातला ओटा स्वच्छ आहे. svayampākgharātlā oṭā svaccha āhe. The kitchen counter is clean.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ओटा (oṭā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi oṭā, "a raised mass of earth or bricks", under ōṭṭā, "shelter, screen" (CDIAL 2544). The kitchen counter is the same raised platform, moved indoors.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पोळपाट (poḷpāṭ) — a rolling board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a stove, then the Marathi for a kitchen counter.

You'll want to know: पोळपाट.
पोळपाट — poḷpāṭ — "a rolling board".
आजी पोळपाटावर पोळ्या लाटते. ājī poḷpāṭāvar poḷyā lāṭte. Grandmother rolls out chapatis on the rolling board.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पोळपाट (poḷpāṭ) is a Marathi compound of पोळी, "chapati", and पाट, "a flat board", inherited from Sanskrit paṭṭa, "slab" (CDIAL 7699).

Lesson 4 of 5.
मळणे (maḷṇe) — to knead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a kitchen counter, then the Marathi for a rolling board.

You'll want to know: मळणे.
मळणे — maḷṇe — "to knead".
पोळ्यांसाठी आईने पीठ मळलं. poḷyāṁsāṭhī āīne pīṭh maḷlaṁ. Mother kneaded the dough for chapatis.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पीठ मळलं, tyāne pīṭh maḷlaṁ.

The word, taken apart.
मळणे (maḷṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi maḷṇẽ, "to rub", under marati, "crushes, rubs" (CDIAL 9870); kneading is rubbing and pressing.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: किसणे.
किसणे — kisṇe — "to grate".
कोशिंबिरीसाठी मीराने काकडी किसली. kośimbirīsāṭhī mīrāne kākḍī kislī. Meera grated a cucumber for the salad.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने गाजर किसलं, tyāne gājar kislaṁ.

The word, taken apart.
किसणे (kisṇe) is a Marathi verb for grating vegetables; its origin is not settled.
