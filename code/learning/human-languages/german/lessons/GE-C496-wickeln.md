## You'll want to know: wickeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C496-BABY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**wickeln** — "to change (a baby); to wrap".

> Der Vater wickelt das Baby im Bad.
>
> *The father changes the baby in the bathroom.*

Also to wrap: *ein Geschenk in Papier wickeln*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wickeln* is inherited from Middle High German *wickeln* "to wrap around" (EtymDB).
