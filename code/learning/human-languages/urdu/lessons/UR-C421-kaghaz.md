## You'll want to know: کاغذ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C421-KAGHAZ421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کاغذ** — *kāghaz* — "paper; a document".

> بینک والے نے کچھ کاغذ مانگے۔
>
> *baink vāle ne kuchh kāghaz māṅge.*
>
> The man at the bank asked for some papers.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کاغذ** (*kāghaz*) is usually traced to Persian *kāghaz*, "paper"; the word probably reached Persian from further east, along with paper itself.
