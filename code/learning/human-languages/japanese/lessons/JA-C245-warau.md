## You'll want to know: わらう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C245-JOY245-04]; assesses=[] -->

**わらう** — *warau* — "to laugh; to smile".

> **せんせいは じゅぎょうで わらいますか。** — *sensei wa jugyō de waraimasu ka* — "Does the teacher laugh in class?"

Polite **わらいます**, *waraimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わらう** (*warau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
