Lesson 1 of 5.
ಒರಳು (oraḷu) — a grinding stone, a mortar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a team, then the Kannada for cricket.

You'll want to know: ಒರಳು.
ಒರಳು — oraḷu — "a grinding stone, a mortar".

The word, taken apart.
ಒರಳು (oraḷu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil ural (DEDR 651). DEDR lists Kannada oral, oraḷ, "mortar of stone or wood".

The word, taken apart.
ಜರಡಿ (jaraḍi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil cali (DEDR 2370). DEDR lists jaraḍe, jallaḍi, "sieve", beside jallisu, "to sift". DEDR 2370 compares Sanskrit cālana, "a sieve" (CDIAL 4771).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ತುರಿಮಣೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ತುರಿಮಣೆ (turimaṇe) is native Dravidian: turi, "to grate, scrape out" (DEDR 3339), and maṇe, "stool, low bench" (DEDR 4675): the grater is a little stool you sit on to scrape the coconut.
