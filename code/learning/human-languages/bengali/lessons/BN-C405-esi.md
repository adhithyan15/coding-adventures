## You'll want to know: এসি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C405-BATHRUM405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**এসি** — *esi* — "an air conditioner".

> গরমে আমরা এসি চালিয়ে ঘুমালাম।
>
> *gôrôme āmrā esi chāliye ghumālām.*
>
> In the heat we slept with the air conditioner on.

Said as the two English letters A C.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**এসি** (*esi*) is borrowed from English *AC*, the letters of *air conditioning*, said as a word.
