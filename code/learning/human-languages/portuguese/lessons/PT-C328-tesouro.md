## You'll want to know: o tesouro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C328-NAVEGAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o tesouro** — "the treasure".

> Encontraram um tesouro numa gruta.
>
> *They found a treasure in a cave.*

Also a term of affection: *o meu tesouro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tesouro* is inherited from Latin *thēsaurus* "a treasure" (EtymDB), from Greek *thēsaurós* "a storehouse, a treasure".
