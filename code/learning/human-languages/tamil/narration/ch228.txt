Tamil, chapter 228: To type, To translate, To cancel, To hurry, To love.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தட்டச்சு செய் (taṭṭaccu cey) — to type.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to book in advance, then the Tamil for to apply.

You'll want to know: தட்டச்சு செய்.
தட்டச்சு செய் — taṭṭaccu cey — "to type".

The word, taken apart.
தட்டச்சு செய் (taṭṭaccu cey) is a modern coinage from native words: taṭṭu, "to tap, to strike", accu, "mould, type" (DEDR 47, which lists Malayalam accu and Telugu accu 'print'), and sey, "to do" (DEDR 1957).

The word, taken apart.
ரத்து செய் (rattu cey) is rattu, "cancelled", a loan from Arabic radd, "rejection, cancelling", through Hindustani, with native sey, "to do" (DEDR 1957).

Lesson 5 of 5.
நேசி (nēci) — to love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to cancel, then the Tamil for to hurry.

You'll want to know: நேசி.
நேசி — nēci — "to love".

The word, taken apart.
நேசி (nēci) is a verb made from nēcam, "love, affection", from Sanskrit sneha, "oiliness, tenderness, love" (CDIAL 13802). Tamil dropped the first s.
