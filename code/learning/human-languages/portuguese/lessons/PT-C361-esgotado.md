## You'll want to know: esgotado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C361-STRESS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**esgotado** — "worn out, exhausted; sold out".

> Depois de doze horas de trabalho, estou esgotado.
>
> *After twelve hours of work, I am worn out.*

Also sold out: *os bilhetes estão esgotados*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esgotado* is the participle of *esgotar* "to drain to the last drop", built from *es-* and *gota* "drop", inherited from Latin *gutta* "a drop of a fluid" (Lewis & Short).
