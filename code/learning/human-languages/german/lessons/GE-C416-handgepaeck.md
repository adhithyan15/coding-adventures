## You'll want to know: das Handgepäck
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C416-FLUGSTEIG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Handgepäck** — "the hand luggage, the carry-on".

> Im Handgepäck hat er nur ein Buch und eine Jacke.
>
> *In his hand luggage he has only a book and a jacket.*

No plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Handgepäck* is built from *Hand*, inherited from Proto-Germanic *handuz*, and *Gepäck* 'luggage', from *packen* 'to pack'.
