# ज — voice at the palate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Fingers on the throat again. Say *ca*, then *ja*. Same roof, voice on.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-JA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-CHA-01, MR-SCRIPT-CA-01] -->

> ज

**ज** is *ja*, and it shadows **च** exactly: where a word says **च** as *ts*,
it usually says **ज** as *dz*. The two travel together.

You need it for *jāṇe*, "to go", and it hides inside *kāḷjī*, "care", in the
phrase you already use for goodbye. Draw the top stroke, the middle knot, and
the descending stem under the headline.

## Writing — delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-JA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **ज** for five seconds, cover it, and write it once. Then retrieve the
names of the two letters before it in this chapter.
