Telugu, chapter 530: In-Laws and Kin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వియ్యంకుడు (viyyaṅkuḍu) — a co-father-in-law; one's child's father-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a mother's elder sister, then the Telugu for a niece.

You'll want to know: వియ్యంకుడు.
వియ్యంకుడు — viyyaṅkuḍu — "a co-father-in-law; one's child's father-in-law".
పెళ్ళి తరువాత నాన్న, వియ్యంకుడు మంచి స్నేహితులు అయ్యారు. peḷḷi taruvāta nānna, viyyaṅkuḍu mañci snēhitulu ayyāru. After the wedding, dad and his co-father-in-law became good friends.
The fathers of a married couple are each other's వియ్యంకుడు (viyyaṅkuḍu); the mothers are వియ్యపురాలు. వియ్యం is the bond between the two families.

The word, taken apart.
వియ్యంకుడు (viyyaṅkuḍu) is listed in DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 58) beside viyyamu, "a marriage alliance"; the family goes back to Sanskrit vivāha, "marriage" (CDIAL 11920).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ముత్తాత (muttāta) — a great-grandfather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a niece, then the Telugu for a co-father-in-law.

You'll want to know: ముత్తాత.
ముత్తాత — muttāta — "a great-grandfather".
మా ముత్తాత కట్టిన ఇల్లు ఇంకా ఉంది. mā muttāta kaṭṭina illu iṅkā undi. The house my great-grandfather built is still standing.
The father of a grandfather; a great-grandmother is ముత్తవ్వ.

The word, taken apart.
ముత్తాత (muttāta) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4954 lists Telugu muttāta, "great-grandfather", and mutt-avva, "great-grandmother", in the article of Tamil mutu, "old": the old grandfather.

Lesson 3 of 5.
మేనల్లుడు (mēnalluḍu) — a nephew; a sister's son.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a co-father-in-law, then the Telugu for a great-grandfather.

You'll want to know: మేనల్లుడు.
మేనల్లుడు — mēnalluḍu — "a nephew; a sister's son".
నా మేనల్లుడు కాలేజీలో చదువుతున్నాడు. nā mēnalluḍu kālējīlō caduvutunnāḍu. My nephew is studying at college.
A man's sister's son or a woman's brother's son; అల్లుడు alone is a son-in-law.

The word, taken apart.
మేనల్లుడు (mēnalluḍu) joins native alluḍu, "son-in-law" (DEDR 2410), to mēna-, from DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 53).

Lesson 4 of 5.
చుట్టం (cuṭṭaṁ) — a kinsman; a relation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a great-grandfather, then the Telugu for a nephew.

You'll want to know: చుట్టం.
చుట్టం — cuṭṭaṁ — "a kinsman; a relation".
పెళ్ళికి చుట్టాలంతా వచ్చారు. peḷḷiki cuṭṭālantā vaccāru. All the relations came to the wedding.
Plural: చుట్టాలు, cuṭṭālu.
Plural చుట్టాలు; చుట్టరికం, kinship.

The word, taken apart.
చుట్టం (cuṭṭaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2715 lists Telugu cuṭṭamu, "relation, kinsman", beside cuṭṭu, "to go round, to encircle": the people around you.

Lesson 5 of 5.
అవ్వ (avva) — a granny; an old woman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a nephew, then the Telugu for a kinsman.

You'll want to know: అవ్వ.
అవ్వ — avva — "a granny; an old woman".
పల్లెలో మా అవ్వ ఒంటరిగా ఉంటుంది. pallelō mā avva oṇṭarigā uṇṭundi. Our granny lives alone in the village.
Rural and affectionate; also any old woman.

The word, taken apart.
అవ్వ (avva) is native Dravidian: DEDR 273 lists Telugu avva, "mother, grandmother, an old woman", with Tamil avvai.
