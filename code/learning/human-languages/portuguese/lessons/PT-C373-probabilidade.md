## You'll want to know: a probabilidade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C373-RISCO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a probabilidade** — "the probability, the chance".

> Há uma grande probabilidade de nevar.
>
> *There is a good chance of snow.*

*Com toda a probabilidade* — in all likelihood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*probabilidade* is a learned borrowing from Latin *probābilitās* "probability, credibility" (EtymDB), from *probābilis* "likely", on *probāre* "to prove" (Lewis & Short).
