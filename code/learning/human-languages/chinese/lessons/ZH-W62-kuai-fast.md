## Script — the upright heart, then guài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-KUAI-FAST-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 快

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The upright heart piece, three strokes**: a short dot on the left, a short dot on the right, then a long vertical down between them: the heart piece from the foot of 想, standing upright.

**The right piece, four strokes, read guài**: a horizontal that turns down at its right end, a long horizontal across, a long left-falling stroke from the top down through the bars, and a stroke falling to the right.

3 + 4 = seven.
