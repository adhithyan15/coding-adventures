Gujarati, chapter 504: Clay and the Wheel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કુંભાર (kumbhār) — a potter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a crack, then the Gujarati for to split (wood).

You'll want to know: કુંભાર.
કુંભાર — kumbhār — "a potter".
અમારા ગામમાં એક કુંભાર રહે છે. amārā gāmmā̃ ek kumbhār rahe che. A potter lives in our village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કુંભાર (kumbhār) is inherited from Sanskrit kumbhakāra, "a pot-maker", built from kumbha, "a pot", and kāra, "maker"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 3310).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ગારો (gāro) — mud plaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a potter, then the Gujarati for a potter's wheel.

You'll want to know: ગારો.
ગારો — gāro — "mud plaster".
અમારા ગામનાં જૂનાં ઘર ગારાનાં છે. amārā gāmnā̃ jūnā̃ ghar gārānā̃ che. The old houses in our village are made of mud.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગારો (gāro) comes from gāra, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as earth and water mixed for building a wall (CDIAL 4137).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ગૂંદવું (gū̃dvũ) — to knead clay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a potter's wheel, then the Gujarati for mud plaster.

You'll want to know: ગૂંદવું.
ગૂંદવું — gū̃dvũ — "to knead clay".
કુંભારે પગથી માટી ગૂંદી. kumbhāre pagthī māṭī gū̃dī. The potter kneaded the clay with his feet.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે માટી ગૂંદી, teṇe māṭī gū̃dī.

The word, taken apart.
ગૂંદવું (gū̃dvũ) is said of clay or of a stiff dough worked long and hard; the past agrees with what is kneaded: માટી ગૂંદી. CDIAL lists Hindi gū̃dhnā, "to plait, mix, knead", under Sanskrit gupháti, "strings together" (CDIAL 4205), beside a Gujarati verb for plaiting; it gives no Gujarati form for kneading, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for mud plaster, then the Gujarati for to knead clay.

You'll want to know: કોડિયું.
કોડિયું — koḍiyũ — "a clay lamp".
દિવાળીમાં અમે બારીમાં કોડિયાં મૂકીએ છીએ. divāḷīmā̃ ame bārīmā̃ koḍiyā̃ mūkīe chīe. At Diwali we put clay lamps in the windows.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
કોડિયું (koḍiyũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit kuṭa, "a water-pot, a pitcher", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as an earthen cup for oil and a wick (CDIAL 3227). The plural is કોડિયાં.
