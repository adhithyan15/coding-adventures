Lesson 1 of 5.
der Löffel — a spoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a fork, then the German for a knife.

You'll want to know: der Löffel.
der Löffel — "a spoon".

The word, taken apart.
Löffel from Old High German leffil from Proto-Germanic lapilaz, "spoon" — inherited; Dutch lepel is the same word. (EtymDB 2.1) It is related to English lap, "to lick up".
