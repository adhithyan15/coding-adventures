## You'll want to know: efficāx, efficācis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C372-AUCTOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**efficāx, efficācis** — "effective, efficient".

> Hoc remedium efficāx est.
>
> *This remedy is effective.*

*efficāx, efficācis*: one ending in the nominative for all genders. English *efficacious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*efficāx* is built on *efficiō* "to bring about" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-āx* of a lasting tendency. English *efficacious* comes from it.
