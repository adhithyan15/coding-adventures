## You'll want to know: ಕಮ್ಮಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C522-KASUBU522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಮ್ಮಾರ** — *kammāra* — "a blacksmith".

> ಕಮ್ಮಾರ ಬೆಂಕಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಬ್ಬಿಣವನ್ನು ಕಾಯಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *kammāra beṅkiyalli kabbiṇavannu kāyisidanu.*
>
> The blacksmith heated the iron in the fire.

Plural: ಕಮ್ಮಾರರು, *kammāraru*.

His forge is the ಕುಲುಮೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಮ್ಮಾರ** (*kammāra*) goes back to Sanskrit *karmāra*, "blacksmith" (CDIAL 2898), through a Prakrit form in which *rm* became *mm*.
