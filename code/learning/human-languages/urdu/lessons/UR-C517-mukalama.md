## You'll want to know: مکالمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C517-STAGE517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مکالمہ** — *mukālamā* — "a line of dialogue; a dialogue".

> اداکار اپنے مکالمے بھول گیا۔
>
> *adākār apne mukālame bhūl gayā.*
>
> The actor forgot his lines.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مکالمہ** (*mukālamā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mukālama*, "talking together, a conversation".
