## You'll want to know: ٹائر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C395-TAYAR395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹائر** — *ṭāyar* — "a tyre".

> گاڑی کا ایک ٹائر پرانا ہے۔
>
> *gāṛī kā ek ṭāyar purānā hai.*
>
> One of the car's tyres is old.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹائر** (*ṭāyar*) is borrowed from English *tyre*.
