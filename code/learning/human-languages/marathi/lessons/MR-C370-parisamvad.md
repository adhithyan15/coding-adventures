## You'll want to know: परिसंवाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C370-VYAKHYAN370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**परिसंवाद** — *parisaṁvād* — "a symposium; a panel discussion".

> महाविद्यालयात पर्यावरणावर परिसंवाद झाला.
>
> *mahāvidyālyāt paryāvarṇāvar parisaṁvād jhālā.*
>
> A symposium on the environment was held at the college.

Masculine.

परि + संवाद, around + conversation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिसंवाद** (*parisaṁvād*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *pari-*, "around", and *saṁvāda*, "conversation" (Monier-Williams).
