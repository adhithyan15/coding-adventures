## You'll want to know: bollire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C333-PENTOLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**bollire** — "to boil".

> Quando l'acqua bolle, butta la pasta.
>
> *When the water boils, put the pasta in.*

Present *bollo*, *bolli*, *bolle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bollire* is inherited from Latin *bullīre* "to bubble, to boil" (EtymDB), from *bulla* "a bubble".
