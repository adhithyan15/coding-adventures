## You'll want to know: ひきだし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C156-CLOSET156-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきだし** — *hikidashi* — "a drawer".

> **つくえの ひきだしは ちいさいです。** — *tsukue no hikidashi wa chiisai desu* — "The desk drawer is small."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきだし** (*hikidashi*) is native Japanese, built from *hiki*, "pulling", the stem of *hiku*, and *dashi*, "taking out", the stem of *dasu*; both parts are inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
