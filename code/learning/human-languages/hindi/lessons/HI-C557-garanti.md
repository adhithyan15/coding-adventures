## You'll want to know: गारंटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C557-ANDESHA557-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गारंटी** — *gāraṇṭī* — "a guarantee".

> दुकानदार ने घड़ी पर एक साल की गारंटी दी।
>
> *Dukāndār ne ghaṛī par ek sāl kī gāraṇṭī dī.*
>
> The shopkeeper gave a one-year guarantee on the watch.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गारंटी** (*gāraṇṭī*) is a loan from English *guarantee*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
