## You'll want to know: آوارہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C414-GARD414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**آوارہ** — *āvārā* — "stray; wandering".

> گلی میں آوارہ کتے رات کو گھومتے ہیں۔
>
> *galī meṅ āvārā kutte rāt ko ghūmte haiṅ.*
>
> Stray dogs roam the lane at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آوارہ** (*āvārā*) is usually traced to Persian *āvāra*, "wandering, without a home".
