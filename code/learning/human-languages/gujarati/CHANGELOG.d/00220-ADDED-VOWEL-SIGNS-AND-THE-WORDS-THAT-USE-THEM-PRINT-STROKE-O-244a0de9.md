## Added — vowel signs and the words that use them print stroke-order filmstrips

ા, િ, ી, ુ, ૂ, ે, ૈ, ો and ૌ, the anusvara ં and the visarga ઃ now have a cited
pen path, and the book prints a stroke-order filmstrip for the lessons that
teach them and for words and lists that use them. Where each sign starts, which
way it moves and where the pen lifts come from KanoAI's hand-made Gujarati
barakhadi templates; the paths themselves are fitted to the book's Noto Sans
Gujarati letters. KanoAI's licence is ambiguous (MIT in one file, GNU GPL in
another), so only those facts are cited and none of its drawings is copied.

- **Every sign comes after its consonant.** KanoAI writes the consonant first
  and the sign after it in every row, even િ, which sits to the left of the
  consonant. So a word is drawn in the order it is typed: કેમકે is ક, ે, મ, ક,
  ે. The strip calls its groups parts.
- **New filmstrips (22 lessons):** the sign lessons GU-W01-aa-matra (ા),
  GU-W01-e-matra (ે), GU-W03-anusvara (ં), GU-W03-ii-matra (ી),
  GU-W03-o-matra (ો), GU-W03-u-matra (ુ), GU-W04-i-matra (િ),
  GU-W07-uu-matra (ૂ) and GU-W45-ai-sign (ૈ); the words અને (GU-C32), કે
  (GU-C33), કેમકે (GU-C34), તે (GU-C36) and હા (GU-W01 guided
  copy, delayed copy and dictation); and the glyph lists GU-R04-first-four-r1,
  GU-R04-doorway-nine-r2, GU-R07-second-four-r1, GU-R13-doorway-nine-r3,
  GU-R15-u-matra-r4 and GU-R19-doorway-nine-r4.
- **Not yet:** the virama ્ and ૃ have no Gujarati source, so GU-W01-virama,
  GU-W02-vocalic-r and every word that carries one (નમસ્તે, ક્યાં, રસ્તો)
  still print no filmstrip. Words whose consonant and sign the book's font
  joins or reshapes are not composed either: બજાર (જા), જો (GU-C35), and any
  word with ુ or ૂ under ન, મ and twenty other consonants that grow a stem.
- Lesson prose, narration and durations do not change: filmstrips are printed
  in the book only.
