## You'll want to know: ঢেকুর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C476-DOM476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢেকুর** — *ḍhekur* — "a burp; a belch".

> ভারী খাবারের পরে দাদু ঢেকুর তুললেন।
>
> *bhārī khābārer pôre dādu ḍhekur tullen.*
>
> After the heavy meal my grandfather burped.

ঢেকুর তোলা, to burp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢেকুর** (*ḍhekur*) is an imitative word for a belch; it shows the new letter ঢ. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
