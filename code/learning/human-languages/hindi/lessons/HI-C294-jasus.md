## You'll want to know: जासूस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C294-TAFTISH294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जासूस** — *jāsūs* — "a detective; a spy".

> बचपन में अरुण जासूस बनना चाहता था।
>
> *bacpan meṁ Aruṇ jāsūs bannā cāhtā thā.*
>
> As a child Arun wanted to be a detective.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जासूस** (*jāsūs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jāsūs*, "a spy". In stories it is the detective who works out who did it.
