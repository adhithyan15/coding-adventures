## You'll want to know: imperītus, imperīta, imperītum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C403-DOCTUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**imperītus, imperīta, imperītum** — "inexperienced, unskilled".

> Imperītus nauta nāvem frēgit.
>
> *The unskilled sailor wrecked the ship.*

*imperītus, imperīta, imperītum*: the opposite of the word for experienced, with *in-*, not. With the genitive: *bellī imperītus*, without experience of war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imperītus* is built from *in-* "not" and *perītus* "experienced" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which comes from the root of *experior* "to try, to test".
