# Review — what is, what may be, what would be

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C290-SUBIUNCTIVUS-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C290-IRREALIS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I would like to see you". (*Velim tē vidēre.*) Then "if you
were here, I would be happy". (*Sī hīc essēs, laetus essem.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C289-SI-REAL-01, LA-LEX-C289-SI-01, LA-LEX-C289-PENDET-EX-01, LA-ETYMON-C290-OPTATIVUS-02] -->

Two chapters, and three ways to hold a thought. **What is**, and what may
well be: plain verbs, *sī pluit, manēmus*. **What depends**: *ex tempestāte
pendet*. **What would be**: the subjunctive, *velim* and *possim* for the
polite and the possible, *essem* and *habērem* for an "if" that is not
true now.

> *Sī pluit, in vīllā manēmus. Sed sī nōn plueret, nāvigārēmus, et laetus
> essem.*

[PAUSE 2s] The first sentence leaves the weather open. The second knows it
is raining, and wishes it were not.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-GRAMMAR-C290-SUBIUNCTIVUS-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C290-IRREALIS-01, LA-GRAMMAR-C289-SI-REAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say "I could". (***Possim.***) Say "if I had money". (***Sī
pecūniam habērem.***) In *sī pluit, manēmus*, may it rain? (**Yes**, it is
open.) In *sī nōn plueret*, is it raining? (**Yes**: that is why the verb
is subjunctive.) What is the *-i-* of *velim* usually said to be? (An **old
wishing mood**.)
