## You'll want to know: कसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C515-PENCH515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कसना** — *kasnā* — "to tighten".

> अरुण ने पेचकस से सब पेंच कस दिए।
>
> *Aruṇ ne peckas se sab peñc kas diye.*
>
> Arun tightened all the screws with the screwdriver.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पेंच कसा, *usne peñc kasā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसना** (*kasnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *karṣati*, "draws, pulls" (CDIAL 2908). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to tighten".
