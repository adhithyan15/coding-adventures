## You'll want to know: yìnxiàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C324-RECALL324-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yìnxiàng* — "impression".

> *wǒ duì nàge dìfang de yìnxiàng hěn hǎo* — That place made a very good impression on me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìnxiàng* is built from *yìn* 'a seal' and *xiàng* 'an image', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jinH* and *zjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a seal' and describes the second as the long-nosed beast of the south. The sense 'an image' is not given there.
