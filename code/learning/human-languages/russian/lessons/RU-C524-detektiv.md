## You'll want to know: детектив
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C524-GENRES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**детектив** (*detektív*) — "a detective story; a detective".

> Летом я люблю читать детективы.
>
> *Létom ya lyublyú chitát detektívy.*
>
> In the summer I like reading detective stories.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *детектив* without a source; it is usually traced to English *detective*.
