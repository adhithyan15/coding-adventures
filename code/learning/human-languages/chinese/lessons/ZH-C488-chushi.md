## You'll want to know: chúshī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C488-TRADES488-02]; assesses=[] -->

*chúshī* — "chef; professional cook".

> *tā dāng le shí nián chúshī měi tiān dōu hěn píláo* — He was a chef for ten years and was worn out every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chúshī* is built from *chú* 'a kitchen' and *shī* 'a master', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drju* and *srij* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a kitchen' and the second 'a model to follow'.
