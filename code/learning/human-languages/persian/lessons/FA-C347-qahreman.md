## You'll want to know: قهرمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C347-AFSANE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قهرمان** (*qahremân*) — "a hero; a champion".

> قهرمان داستان یک پسر فقیر است.
>
> *Qahremân-e dâstân yek pesar-e faqir ast.*
>
> The hero of the story is a poor boy.

Also a sports champion; *qahremâni* "a championship".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB glosses *qahremân* "champion, hero" and gives no parent; its origin is not settled.
