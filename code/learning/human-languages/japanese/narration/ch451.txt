Japanese, chapter 451: Off to Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しゅっきんする (shukkin suru) — to go in to work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for eye drops, then the Japanese for to nurse the sick.

You'll want to know: しゅっきんする.
しゅっきんする — shukkin suru — "to go in to work".
ちちは どようびも しゅっきんしました。 — chichi wa doyōbi mo shukkin shimashita — "My father went in to work on Saturday too."
Polite しゅっきんします, shukkinshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): ちちが しゅっきんしました。, chichi ga shukkin shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しゅっきんする (shukkin suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) shukkin with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shu, "go out" (Middle Chinese tsyhwit) and kin, "serve" (gj+n) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
