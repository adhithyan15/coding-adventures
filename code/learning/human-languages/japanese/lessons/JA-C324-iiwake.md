## You'll want to know: いいわけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C324-MONKU324-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いいわけ** — *iiwake* — "an excuse".

> **おとうとは おくれたので、いいわけを しました。** — *otōto wa okureta node, iiwake o shimashita* — "My younger brother was late, so he made an excuse."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いいわけ** (*iiwake*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *ii*, "saying (iu)" and *wake*, "reason", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
