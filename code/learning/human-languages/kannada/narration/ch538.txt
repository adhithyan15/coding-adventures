Kannada, chapter 538: Winning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪದಕ (padaka) — a medal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a work of art, then the Kannada for to compose.

You'll want to know: ಪದಕ.
ಪದಕ — padaka — "a medal".
ಓಟದ ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅವಳಿಗೆ ಚಿನ್ನದ ಪದಕ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. ōṭada spardheyalli avaḷige cinnada padaka sikkitu. She won a gold medal in the race.
ಚಿನ್ನ, ಬೆಳ್ಳಿ, ಕಂಚು: gold, silver, bronze.

The word, taken apart.
ಪದಕ (padaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit padaka, which Monier-Williams lists as "a kind of ornament" (Monier-Williams); a medal is an ornament you earn.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕ್ರೀಡಾಪಟು (krīḍāpaṭu) — an athlete.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to compose, then the Kannada for a medal.

You'll want to know: ಕ್ರೀಡಾಪಟು.
ಕ್ರೀಡಾಪಟು — krīḍāpaṭu — "an athlete".
ಆ ಕ್ರೀಡಾಪಟು ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ಹತ್ತು ಕಿಲೋಮೀಟರ್ ಓಡುತ್ತಾನೆ. ā krīḍāpaṭu pratidina hattu kilōmīṭar ōḍuttāne. That athlete runs ten kilometres every day.
Plural: ಕ್ರೀಡಾಪಟುಗಳು, krīḍāpaṭugaḷu.
ಕ್ರೀಡೆ, sport + ಪಟು, a skilled one.

The word, taken apart.
ಕ್ರೀಡಾಪಟು (krīḍāpaṭu) is a modern learned compound built from Sanskrit krīḍā, "sport, play", and paṭu, "clever, skilful" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an athlete, then the Kannada for a winner.

You'll want to know: ಪಂದ್ಯಾವಳಿ.
ಪಂದ್ಯಾವಳಿ — pandyāvaḷi — "a tournament".
ಶಾಲೆಗಳ ಕ್ರಿಕೆಟ್ ಪಂದ್ಯಾವಳಿ ಮುಂದಿನ ವಾರ ಶುರುವಾಗುತ್ತದೆ. śālegaḷa krikeṭ pandyāvaḷi mundina vāra śuruvāguttade. The schools cricket tournament starts next week.
ಪಂದ್ಯ, a match + ಆವಳಿ, a row: a series of matches.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಂದ್ಯಾವಳಿ (pandyāvaḷi) is built from ಪಂದ್ಯ, "a match, a contest", and Sanskrit āvali, "a row, a series" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸೋಲಿಸು (sōlisu) — to defeat; to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a winner, then the Kannada for a tournament.

You'll want to know: ಸೋಲಿಸು.
ಸೋಲಿಸು — sōlisu — "to defeat; to beat".
ನಮ್ಮ ಶಾಲೆಯ ತಂಡ ನಗರದ ತಂಡವನ್ನು ಸೋಲಿಸಿತು. namma śāleya taṇḍa nagarada taṇḍavannu sōlisitu. Our school's team beat the city team.
Past (I …): ಸೋಲಿಸಿದೆ, sōliside.
ಸೋಲು is to lose; ಸೋಲಿಸು (sōlisu) is to make someone lose.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೋಲಿಸು (sōlisu) is native Dravidian: the causative of sōlu, "to be defeated, to lose" (DEDR 3558), so "to make someone lose".
