# વિખેરાવું (vikherāvũ) — to scatter; to disperse

## You'll want to know: વિખેરાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C310-TUKDI310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વિખેરાવું** — *vikherāvũ* — "to scatter; to disperse".

> પોલીસ આવી ત્યારે ટોળું વિખેરાઈ ગયું.
>
> *polīs āvī tyāre ṭoḷũ vikherāī gayũ.*
>
> When the police came, the crowd dispersed.

Past (it …): તે ટોળું વિખેરાઈ ગયું, *te ṭoḷũ vikherāī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિખેરાવું** (*vikherāvũ*) is the intransitive partner of *vikhervũ*, to scatter, which CDIAL lists under *\*viṣkirati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11985). A crowd or a cloud **વિખેરાઈ જાય છે**: it breaks up.
