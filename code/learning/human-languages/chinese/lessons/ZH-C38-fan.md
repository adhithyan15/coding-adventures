## You'll want to know: 饭
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C38-EAT38-02]; assesses=[] -->

**饭** — *fàn* — "cooked rice; meal".

> **你吃饭了吗** *nǐ chī fàn le ma* — Have you eaten?

The new character, in pieces: 饭 — a three-stroke food piece on the left, and a four-stroke piece on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**饭** (*fàn*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'cooked grain'.
