## You'll want to know: मथळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C310-VRUTTAPATRA310-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मथळा** — *mathḷā* — "a headline".

> आजच्या वृत्तपत्राचा मथळा पावसाबद्दल आहे.
>
> *ājacyā vṛttapatrācā mathḷā pāvsābaddal āhe.*
>
> Today's newspaper headline is about the rain.

Masculine. Plural: मथळे, *mathḷe*.

Plural मथळे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मथळा** (*mathḷā*) is the Marathi word for a headline; its origin is not settled.
