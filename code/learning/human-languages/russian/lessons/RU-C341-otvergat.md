## You'll want to know: отвергать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C341-KOMPROMISS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**отвергать** (*otvergát*) — "to reject".

> Начальник отверг наше предложение.
>
> *Nachálnik otvérg náshe predlozhéniye.*
>
> The boss rejected our proposal.

Imperfective; perfective *отвергнуть* (past *отверг, отвергла*). Formal; in conversation people say *не принять*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**отвергать** is built from *от-* "away" and *вергать* "to throw", a bookish verb. EtymDB traces *отвергнуть* to Proto-Slavic *\*vĭrgnǫti* (EtymDB), usually glossed "to throw". To reject is to throw away.
