## You'll want to know: ふざける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C441-PROUD441-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ふざける** — *fuzakeru* — "to fool around; to joke".

> **じゅぎょうで ふざけないで ください。** — *jugyō de fuzakenaide kudasai* — "Please do not fool around in class."

Polite **ふざけます**, *fuzakemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが ふざけました。**, *gakusei ga fuzakemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふざける** (*fuzakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; JMdict writes it in kana (JMdict).
