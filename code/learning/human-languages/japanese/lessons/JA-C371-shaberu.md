## You'll want to know: しゃべる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C371-JOUDAN371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃべる** — *shaberu* — "to chat; to talk".

> **じゅぎょうで しゃべったら、めいわくです。** — *jugyō de shabettara, meiwaku desu* — "Chatting in class is a nuisance to others."

Polite **しゃべります**, *shaberimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **いもうとが しゃべりました。**, *imōto ga shaberimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃべる** (*shaberu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
