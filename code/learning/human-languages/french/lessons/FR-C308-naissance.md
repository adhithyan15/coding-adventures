## You'll want to know: la naissance
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C308-NAISSANCE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la naissance** — "the birth".

> Nous avons fêté la naissance de notre fille.
>
> *We celebrated the birth of our daughter.*

*La date de naissance* — date of birth; *de naissance* — from birth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naissance* is built on the stem of *naître* "to be born", which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *nāscere*, from Latin *nāscī* "to be born" (Lewis & Short).
