## You'll want to know: cōnscendō, cōnscendere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C438-CONSCENDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnscendō, cōnscendere** — "to go on board, to embark; to climb".

> Prīmā lūce mercātōrēs nāvem cōnscendunt.
>
> *At first light the merchants go on board the ship.*

*cōnscendō, cōnscendere, cōnscendī, cōnscēnsum*. *Nāvem cōnscendere*, to embark; *in nāvem* is also found.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnscendō* is built from *con-* and *scandō* 'to climb' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to climb up into a ship.
