## You'll want to know: しゃせん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C390-KOUSOKU390-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃせん** — *shasen* — "a traffic lane".

> **この どうろは しゃせんが みっつ あります。** — *kono dōro wa shasen ga mittsu arimasu* — "This road has three lanes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃせん** (*shasen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sha*, "car" (Middle Chinese *tsyhae*) and *sen*, "line" (*sjenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
