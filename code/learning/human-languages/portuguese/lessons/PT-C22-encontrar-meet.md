# encontrar-se — to meet up

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You know **encontrar**, “to find.” With people, it can also describe
meeting up.

## You'll want to know: Make one plan
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-GRAMMAR-ENCONTRAR-MEET-04]; assesses=[] -->

> **Encontramo-nos no sábado ao meio-dia.** — We are meeting on Saturday at
> midday.

The little **nos** means “each other.” In much of Portugal it commonly attaches
after the verb as shown. In Brazil, **nós nos encontramos** is more usual.

For a first introduction, keep using the **conhecer** family you already know:
**Conheci o Pedro**, “I met Pedro for the first time.” Today’s **encontrar-se**
is for meeting up.
