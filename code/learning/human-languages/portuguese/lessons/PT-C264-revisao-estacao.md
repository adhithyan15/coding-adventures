# Review — one morning at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-PERDI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I've missed the train". (*Perdi o comboio.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-DESCULPE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-COMO-SE-VAI-02, PT-LEX-IDA-E-VOLTA-01, PT-GRAMMAR-QUERIA-UM-BILHETE-02, PT-GRAMMAR-TENHO-UM-PROBLEMA-02] -->

Two chapters of travel, and they make one morning. You find the station, you
buy the ticket, something goes wrong, and you ask your way out of it.

[PAUSE 2s] Every step is a sentence you have said: *Desculpe, como se vai para
a estação?* · *Queria um bilhete para Lisboa, de ida e volta.* · *Desculpe,
tenho um problema: perdi o comboio.* · *Há outro comboio hoje?*

[PAUSE 2s] And when you tell someone about it afterwards, the morning becomes
a story: *Havia muitas pessoas na estação, por isso perdi o comboio.* Scene,
arrow, event.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-IDA-E-VOLTA-01, PT-LEX-PERDI-01, PT-GRAMMAR-TENHO-UM-PROBLEMA-02, PT-GRAMMAR-CENA-EVENTO-01] -->

comboio porque havia muitas pessoas*, which verb is the scene? (***Havia***.)
