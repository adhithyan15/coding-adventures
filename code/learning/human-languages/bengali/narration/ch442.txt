Bengali, chapter 442: Worry and Unease.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উদ্বেগ (udbeg) — anxiety; unease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to get flustered, then the Bengali for to pound.

You'll want to know: উদ্বেগ.
উদ্বেগ — udbeg — "anxiety; unease".
ছেলের জ্বর নিয়ে বাবার উদ্বেগ বাড়ল। chheler jbôr niye bābār udbeg bāṛlô. Father's anxiety over his son's fever grew.

The word, taken apart.
উদ্বেগ (udbeg) is a tatsama, Sanskrit udvega, "trembling, agitation, distress" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
দুর্ভাবনা (durbhābônā) — anxious thoughts; worry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to pound, then the Bengali for anxiety.

You'll want to know: দুর্ভাবনা.
দুর্ভাবনা — durbhābônā — "anxious thoughts; worry".
রাতে দুর্ভাবনায় আমি জেগে ছিলাম। rāte durbhābônāy āmi jege chhilām. I lay awake at night with worry.

The word, taken apart.
দুর্ভাবনা (durbhābônā) is Sanskrit durbhāvanā, "a bad thought" (Monier-Williams), dur- "ill" on bhāvanā "reflection"; Bengali uses it for anxious thoughts. ভাবা (taught), to think, is the same root.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for anxiety, then the Bengali for anxious thoughts.

You'll want to know: অস্থির.
অস্থির — ôsthir — "restless; unsettled".
সারাদিন ফোন না পেয়ে মীরা অস্থির হল। sārādin phon nā peye mīrā ôsthir hôlô. Mira grew restless after a whole day without a call.

The word, taken apart.
অস্থির (ôsthir) is a tatsama, Sanskrit asthira, "unsteady, shaking" (Monier-Williams): a- "not" on sthira "firm", the স্থির of স্থির করা (taught), to decide.

You'll want to know: নিশ্চিন্ত.
নিশ্চিন্ত — nishchintô — "free of worry; at ease".
ফলাফল দেখে বাবা নিশ্চিন্ত হলেন। phôlāphôl dekhe bābā nishchintô hôlen. Seeing the result, my father stopped worrying.

The word, taken apart.
নিশ্চিন্ত (nishchintô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit niścinta, "unconcerned, careless" (Monier-Williams): niḥ- "without" on cintā, the চিন্তা (taught) that means both a thought and a worry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ছটফট করা (chhôṭphôṭ kôrā) — to fidget; to toss and turn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ছটফট করা.
ছটফট করা — chhôṭphôṭ kôrā — "to fidget; to toss and turn".
জ্বরে ছেলেটা সারারাত ছটফট করল। jbôre chheleṭā sārārāt chhôṭphôṭ kôrlô. The boy tossed and turned all night with fever.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ব্যথায় ছটফট করলেন, tini bæthāy chhôṭphôṭ kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
ছটফট করা (chhôṭphôṭ kôrā) is chhôṭphôṭ, an expressive word that paints quick, restless movement, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
