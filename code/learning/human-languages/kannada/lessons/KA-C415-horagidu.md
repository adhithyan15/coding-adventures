# ಹೊರಗಿಡು (horagiḍu) — to exclude; to leave out

## You'll want to know: ಹೊರಗಿಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C415-AMANATU415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊರಗಿಡು** — *horagiḍu* — "to exclude; to leave out".

> ಗಾಯದಿಂದ ಅವನನ್ನು ತಂಡದಿಂದ ಹೊರಗಿಟ್ಟರು.
>
> *gāyadinda avanannu taṇḍadinda horagiṭṭaru.*
>
> Because of an injury they left him out of the team.

Past (I …): ಹೊರಗಿಟ್ಟೆ, *horagiṭṭe*.

What is left out takes -ಅನ್ನು, the group -ಇಂದ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊರಗಿಡು** (*horagiḍu*) is native Dravidian, built from *horage*, "outside" (DEDR 4333), and *iḍu*, "to put" (DEDR 442).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
