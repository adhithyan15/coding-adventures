## You'll want to know: durer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C274-SURVENIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**durer** — "to last".

> Le voyage a duré trois heures.
>
> *The journey lasted three hours.*

*Ça n'a pas duré* — it did not last.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*durer* is inherited from Latin *dūrāre* "to harden, to hold out, to last" (EtymDB), built on *dūrus* "hard".
