## You'll want to know: ਪੰਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C357-SCHOOL357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੰਨਾ** — *pannā* — "a page; a leaf of a book".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੌ ਪੰਨੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *merī kitāb vich sau panne han.*
>
> My book has a hundred pages.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੰਨਾ** (*pannā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *parṇa*, "plumage, foliage"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pannā*, "leaf, page", under it (CDIAL 7918).
