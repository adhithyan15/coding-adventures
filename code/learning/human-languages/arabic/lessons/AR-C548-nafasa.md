## You'll want to know: نافس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C548-HALABA548-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نافس** — *nāfasa* — "to compete with".

> نافست أختي عشر بنات في السباق.
>
> *nāfasat ukhtī ʿashr banāt fī as-sibāq.*
>
> My sister competed with ten girls in the race.

Present: ينافس, *yunāfis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نافس** (*nāfasa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-f-s*, "a soul; to be precious" (Lane). Form III nāfasa is to vie with someone for a precious thing.
