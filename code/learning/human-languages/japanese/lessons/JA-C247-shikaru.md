## You'll want to know: しかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C247-PRAISE247-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しかる** — *shikaru* — "to scold".

> **そふは こどもを しかりません。** — *sofu wa kodomo o shikarimasen* — "My grandfather does not scold the children."

Polite **しかります**, *shikarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しかる** (*shikaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and the word is usually written in kana (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
