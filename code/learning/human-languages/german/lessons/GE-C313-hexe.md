## You'll want to know: die Hexe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C313-MAERCHEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Hexe** — "the witch".

> Die Hexe wohnte in einem kleinen Haus im Wald.
>
> *The witch lived in a small house in the forest.*

Plural: *die Hexen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hexe* comes from Middle High German *hecse* (EtymDB), and EtymDB lists English *hag* as a cousin, from Proto-Germanic *hagatusjōn* "witch".
