## You'll want to know: rènao
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C129-PLACE129-03]; assesses=[] -->

*rènao* — "lively; bustling".

> *Běijīng hěn rènao* — Beijing is a lively place.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rènao* is built from *rè* 'hot' and *nao*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyet* and *nraewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second 'not quiet' and 'to stir up'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
