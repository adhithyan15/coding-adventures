## You'll want to know: آژیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C327-EMDADGAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آژیر** (*âzhir*) — "a siren, an alarm".

> صدای آژیر آمبولانس از دور میآمد.
>
> *Sedâ-ye âzhir-e âmbulâns az dur miâmad.*
>
> The sound of an ambulance siren was coming from far away.

Written with ژ. *Âzhir-e khatar* is an alarm siren.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *âzhir* "a siren, an alarm" is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.
