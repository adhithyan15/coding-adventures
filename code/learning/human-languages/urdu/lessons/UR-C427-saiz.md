## You'll want to know: سائز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C427-POSHAK427-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سائز** — *sāiz* — "a size".

> اس قمیض کا سائز چھوٹا ہے۔
>
> *is qamīz kā sāiz chhoṭā hai.*
>
> This shirt is a small size.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سائز** (*sāiz*) is borrowed from English *size*.
