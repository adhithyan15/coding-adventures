## You'll want to know: tranquillizzarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C475-SPAVENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tranquillizzarsi** — "to calm down".

> Respira piano e tranquillizzati.
>
> *Breathe slowly and calm down.*

Built on *tranquillo*. To calm someone else: *tranquillizzare qualcuno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tranquillizzarsi* is built on *tranquillo* "calm", a learned borrowing from Latin *tranquillus* "calm, still" (Lewis & Short), with the verb ending *-izzare*.
