## You'll want to know: 站长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C403-TRAVELLER403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**站长** — *zhànzhǎng* — "stationmaster; head of a station".

> **旅客去找站长** *lǚkè qù zhǎo zhànzhǎng* — The passenger goes to find the stationmaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**站长** (*zhànzhǎng*) is built from 站 'a station' and 长 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *treamH* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as an everyday word for standing alone and the second 'big'; a station is usually explained as a place where vehicles stand.
