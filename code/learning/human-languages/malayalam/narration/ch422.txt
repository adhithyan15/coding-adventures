Malayalam, chapter 422: The Assembly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
നിയമസഭ (niyamasabha) — a legislative assembly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a chief minister, then the Malayalam for a governor.

You'll want to know: നിയമസഭ.
നിയമസഭ — niyamasabha — "a legislative assembly".
നിയമസഭയിൽ ഇന്ന് വലിയ ചർച്ച നടന്നു. niyamasabhayil innŭ valiya carcca naṭannu. There was a big discussion in the assembly today.
A member of it is നിയമസഭാംഗം.

The word, taken apart.
നിയമസഭ (niyamasabha) is a Malayalam compound built from Sanskrit niyama, "a fixed law" (CDIAL 7250), and sabhā, "an assembly, a hall" (CDIAL 13167): the assembly that makes laws.

Plural: അംഗങ്ങൾ.

The word, taken apart.
അംഗം (aṁgaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit aṅga, "a limb; the body" (CDIAL 114). A member is a limb of a body of people, as in English.

ഓരോ ക്ലാസിൽ നിന്നും ഒരു പ്രതിനിധി യോഗത്തിന് വന്നു. ōrō klāsil ninnuṁ oru pratinidhi yōgattinŭ vannu. A representative came to the meeting from each class.

പ്രതിപക്ഷം — pratipakṣaṁ — "the opposition".
പ്രതിപക്ഷം പുതിയ നികുതിയെ എതിർത്തു. pratipakṣaṁ putiya nikutiye etirttu. The opposition opposed the new tax.
Its members sit across from the government in the assembly.

The word, taken apart.
പ്രതിപക്ഷം (pratipakṣaṁ) is built from Sanskrit prati-, "against, opposite", and pakṣa, "a side" (CDIAL 7627): the side opposite.
