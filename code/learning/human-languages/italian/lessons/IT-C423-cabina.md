## You'll want to know: la cabina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C423-TRAGHETTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la cabina** — "the cabin; the booth".

> La nostra cabina ha una piccola finestra.
>
> *Our cabin has a small window.*

Also a booth: *la cabina telefonica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cabina* was borrowed from French *cabine* (EtymDB); where the French word comes from is less certain.
