## Fixed — Marwadi root slug for -dār and the origin of कंजूस

- **MW-C147-imandar** moves from `persian-dar` to `persian-dar-holder`, the slug the corpus uses for *-dār* "holder"; `persian-dar` also named *dar* "door" and *dar* "in".
- **कंजूस (MW-C147-kanjus)** now says, as the Urdu lesson does, that *kañjūs* is usually traced to Persian *kanjūs* "miserly", and that CDIAL has no entry for it. It had called the origin not settled and only "often said" to be Persian.
