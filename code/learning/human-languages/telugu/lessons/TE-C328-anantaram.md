# అనంతరం (anantaraṁ) — afterwards; after that

## You'll want to know: అనంతరం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C328-KRAMAM328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అనంతరం** — *anantaraṁ* — "afterwards; after that".

> భోజనం అనంతరం అందరూ ఇంటికి వెళ్ళారు.
>
> *bhōjanaṁ anantaraṁ andarū iṇṭiki veḷḷāru.*
>
> After the meal everyone went home.

It follows a noun as తరువాత does, but sounds more formal: news readers use it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అనంతరం** (*anantaraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anantaram*, "immediately after", from *an-antara*, "having no interval" (CDIAL 287).
