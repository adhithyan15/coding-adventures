## You'll want to know: পরিমাণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C372-BARA372-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিমাণ** — *pôrimān* — "an amount; a quantity".

> জলের পরিমাণ কমলে গাছ শুকিয়ে যাবে।
>
> *jôler pôrimān kômle gāchh shukiye jābe.*
>
> If the amount of water drops, the plants will dry up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পরিমাণ** (*pôrimān*) is Sanskrit *parimāṇa*, a measure all round, a quantity (Monier-Williams).
