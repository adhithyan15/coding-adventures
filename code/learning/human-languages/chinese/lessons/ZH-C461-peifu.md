## You'll want to know: pèifú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C461-KIND461-01]; assesses=[] -->

*pèifú* — "to think highly of; to hold in high regard".

> *wǒ zuì pèifú wǒ de lǎoshī* — The person I admire most is my teacher.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pèifú* is built from *pèi* 'to wear at the belt' and *fú* 'to serve', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bwojH* and *bjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as jade worn at the belt and the second 'to serve' and 'clothes'. The sense 'to admire' is not given there.
