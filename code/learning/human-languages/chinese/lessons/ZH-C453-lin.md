## You'll want to know: līn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C453-BAGS453-04]; assesses=[] -->

*līn* — "to carry in the hand (by a handle)".

> *nǐ néng bāng wǒ līn zhège bāo ma* — Could you carry this bag for me?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*līn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *leng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as holding something so it hangs from the hand; today's līn is irregular beside it.
