## You'll want to know: торжество
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C311-PRAZDNOVAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**торжество** (*torzhestvó*) — "a celebration, a festive occasion; triumph".

> Торжество началось в шесть часов.
>
> *Torzhestvó nachalós v shest chasóv.*
>
> The celebration began at six.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*торжество* is inherited from Old East Slavic *tŭržĭstvo* (EtymDB), usually linked with *торг* "a market, a gathering of people".
