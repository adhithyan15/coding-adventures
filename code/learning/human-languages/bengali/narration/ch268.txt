Bengali, chapter 268: What I Think, and Why.
5 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আমার মনে হয় … (āmār mône hôy …) — I think.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "first", "after that", "in the end". (prôthôme, tārpôr, sheshe.) Then মনে রাখা, "to remember": "to keep in the mind". Its মনে, "in the mind", is in this lesson too.

You'll want to know: I think.
আমার মনে হয়, কাল ঝড় হবে। āmār mône hôy, kāl jhôṛ hôbe. — "I think there will be a storm tomorrow."
Bengali does not say "I think" with আমি. It says "to my mind it comes": আমার মনে হয়, with the thinker in the -র form, as in আমার নাম. যে, "that", which you know, may follow it: আমার মনে হয় যে …. To ask, put the other person in the same form: তোমার কী মনে হয়? (tomār kī mône hôy?), "what do you think?".

You'll want to know: I don't think so.
না after হয় turns it: আমার মনে হয় না (āmār mône hôy nā), "I don't think so", and আমার মনে হয় না সে আসবে, "I don't think he'll come". In the past, হয় becomes হল: আমার মনে হল (āmār mône hôlô), "I thought (then), it seemed to me".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I think there will be a storm tomorrow" — āmār mône hôy, kāl jhôṛ hôbe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What do you think?" — tomār kī mône hôy?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I don't think the train will come" — āmār mône hôy nā ṭren āsbe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Say that you think there will be a storm tomorrow, then that you don't think Mira will come.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I think"? (আমার মনে হয়.) Why আমার and not আমি? (The thought comes to me.) "I thought, then"? (আমার মনে হল.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
কারণ (kārôn) — a reason; because.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "I think". (āmār mône hôy.) Then কেননা, "because", which you know.

You'll want to know: কারণ.
কারণ — kārôn — "a reason; because".
তোমার রাগের কারণ কী? tomār rāger kārôn kī? — "What's the reason you're angry?"
As a noun it is "a reason": রাগের কারণ (kārôn), "the reason for the anger". It is also the everyday "because", opening the reason after the result:
আজ আমি বাড়িতে থাকব, কারণ বাইরে ঝড় হচ্ছে। āj āmi bāṛite thākbô, kārôn bāire jhôṛ hôchchhe. — "I'll stay at home today, because there's a storm outside."

The word, taken apart.
কারণ (kārôn) is a tatsama: Sanskrit kāraṇa, "cause", which CDIAL (3057) lists with the Bengali word under it. CDIAL files kāraṇa under the root kṛ, "to do, to make": a cause is what makes a thing happen.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kārôn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What's the reason you're angry?" — tomār rāger kārôn kī?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I'll stay at home, because there's a storm outside" — āmi bāṛite thākbô, kārôn bāire jhôṛ hôchchhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does কারণ (kārôn) mean? ("A reason", and "because".) Which comes first, the result or the reason? (The result; কারণ (kārôn) opens the reason.)


Lesson 3 of 5.
ণ (nô) — the letter at the end of কারণ (kārôn).

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script you'll notice: ণ, Guided Practice and Wrap-up Recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say কারণ (kārôn), "because". Then read ন (nô), the first letter you ever wrote.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script you'll notice: ণ.
ণ — nô. Bengali says ণ and ন alike, so, like ষ beside শ, it is a matter of spelling: most words that carry ণ come from Sanskrit.
Set the print of ণ (nô) beside ন. Both have an upright stem on the right and an arch on the left that ends in a solid knob. In ন, the headline is long and covers the whole letter, and the arch meets the stem low down, near its middle. In ণ (nô), the headline is short and sits only at the stem and to its right; the arch meets the stem near the top, where the headline would be, and the stem's top end stands a little above the short headline, cut on a slant.

Writing: ণ — copy the printed shape.
Put your pen beside ণ (nô) and copy the shape you can see, slowly and large. Check the copy against the print: the short headline right of the stem, the arch joined near the top, the knob, the stem to its foot.
This book does not yet tell you where to start ণ (nô) or which way the pen goes. That is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying the printed shape needs no such source, and it is how the shape becomes yours in the meantime.

Lesson 4 of 5.
… কারণ … / -এর জন্য (… kārôn … / -er jônyô) (kārôn / -er jônyô) — giving the reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I'll stay at home, because there's a storm outside". (āmi bāṛite thākbô, kārôn bāire jhôṛ hôchchhe.)

You'll want to know: because, with a sentence.
আজ আমি যাব না, কারণ আমি ক্লান্ত। āj āmi jābô nā, kārôn āmi klāntô. — "I won't go today, because I'm tired."
A whole reason, with its own verb or a describing word such as ক্লান্ত, follows কারণ (kārôn). কেননা, which you know, does the same job in the same place: আজ আমি যাব না, কেননা আমি ক্লান্ত. To ask for one, কেন? (kenô?), "why?", the word inside কেননা.

You'll want to know: because of, for.
For a reason that is only a noun, Bengali puts the noun in its -র form and adds জন্য (jônyô): -এর জন্য, "because of".
ঝড়ের জন্য ট্রেন দেরিতে এল। jhôṛer jônyô ṭren derite elô. — "The train came late because of the storm."
The same জন্য is "for": এটা তোমার জন্য (eṭā tomār jônyô), "this is for you". After a vowel the ending is -র: মীরার জন্য.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Why? Because I'm tired." — kenô? kārôn āmi klāntô.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "The train came late because of the storm" — jhôṛer jônyô ṭren derite elô]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "This is for you" — eṭā tomār jônyô]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 28 seconds]
Say that you won't go today because you're tired, then that the train came late because of the storm.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What follows কারণ (kārôn)? (A whole reason.) What follows a noun? (-এর জন্য.) "Why?" (কেন?)
