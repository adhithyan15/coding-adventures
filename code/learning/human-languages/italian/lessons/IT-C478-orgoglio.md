## You'll want to know: l'orgoglio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C478-VERGOGNA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'orgoglio** — "pride".

> Mio padre parla con orgoglio del suo lavoro.
>
> *My father speaks with pride about his work.*

The adjective is *orgoglioso*; it can be good or bad, like English *pride*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orgoglio* comes from Frankish *urgol* "pride" (EtymDB), a Germanic word, probably through Old French.
