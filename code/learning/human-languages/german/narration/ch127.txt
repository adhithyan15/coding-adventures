Lesson 1 of 5.
der Beruf — a job, a profession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a till, then the German for a boss.

You'll want to know: der Beruf.
der Beruf — "a job, a profession".

The word, taken apart.
Beruf is be- + the noun of rufen, "to call", Old High German hruof (EtymDB 2.1): a calling, the sense Luther gave the word.

The word, taken apart.
Prüfung is the verb "to test" + -ung. The verb from Middle High German brüeven (EtymDB 2.1), from Old French prover from Latin probāre, "to test" — borrowed, like English prove.
