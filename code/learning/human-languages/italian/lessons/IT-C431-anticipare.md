## You'll want to know: anticipare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C431-CAPARRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**anticipare** — "to bring forward; to pay in advance".

> Possiamo anticipare l'arrivo a venerdì?
>
> *Can we bring our arrival forward to Friday?*

*In anticipo* — early.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anticipare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *anticipāre* 'to take beforehand', built from *ante* 'before' and *capere* 'to take' (Lewis & Short).
