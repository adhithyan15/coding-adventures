## You'll want to know: فلاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C527-MAL527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فلاح** — *fallāḥ* — "a peasant farmer".

> يزرع الفلاح القمح في الربيع.
>
> *yazraʿu al-fallāḥ al-qamḥ fī ar-rabīʿ.*
>
> The farmer sows wheat in the spring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فلاح** (*fallāḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-l-ḥ*, "to split, to till the soil; to prosper" (Lane). A fallāḥ is one who splits the earth to till it.
