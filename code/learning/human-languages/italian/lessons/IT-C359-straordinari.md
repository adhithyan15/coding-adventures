## You'll want to know: gli straordinari
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C359-SCIOPERO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**gli straordinari** — "the overtime".

> Questo mese ho fatto molti straordinari.
>
> *This month I did a lot of overtime.*

Short for *le ore straordinarie*. *Straordinario* also means extraordinary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*straordinari* is the plural of *straordinario*, a learned borrowing from Latin *extraordinārius* "out of the usual order" (Lewis & Short), built from *extrā* "outside" and *ōrdō* "order".
