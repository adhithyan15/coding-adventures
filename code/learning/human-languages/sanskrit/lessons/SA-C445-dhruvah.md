## You'll want to know: ध्रुवः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C445-ULKA445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ध्रुवः** — *dhruvaḥ* — "the pole star".

> ध्रुवः उत्तरायां दिशायां तिष्ठति।
>
> *dhruvaḥ uttarāyāṃ diśāyāṃ tiṣṭhati.*
>
> The pole star stands in the north.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ध्रुवः** (*dhruvaḥ*) comes from the root √dhṛ, "to hold, to bear", as MW gives it: MW gives *dhruva* as fixed, firm, and as the pole star, the star that does not move (MW).
