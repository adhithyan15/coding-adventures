## You'll want to know: કંટાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C253-HOBBY253-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કંટાળવું** — *kanṭāḷvũ* — "to get bored, to be fed up".

> **ગઈકાલે હું ઘરે કંટાળ્યો.** — *gaīkāle hũ ghare kanṭāḷyo* — "Yesterday I got bored at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **કંટાળવું** (*kanṭāḷvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this form and meaning, and our other sources do not trace it.
