## You'll want to know: विषमता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C360-SAMANTA360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विषमता** — *viṣamtā* — "inequality".

> श्रीमंत आणि गरीब यांच्यातली विषमता वाढत आहे.
>
> *śrīmant āṇi garīb yāñcyātlī viṣamtā vāḍhat āhe.*
>
> The inequality between the rich and the poor is growing.

Feminine.

विषम, unequal, + -ता; the opposite is समानता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विषमता** (*viṣamtā*) is a learned noun built from Sanskrit *viṣama*, "unequal, uneven" (CDIAL 11972), with *-tā*, "-ness".
