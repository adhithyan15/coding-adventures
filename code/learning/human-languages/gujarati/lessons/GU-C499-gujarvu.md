# ગુજરવું (gujarvũ) — to pass away

## You'll want to know: ગુજરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C499-MARAN499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુજરવું** — *gujarvũ* — "to pass away".

> દાદી ઊંઘમાં જ ગુજરી ગયાં.
>
> *dādī ū̃ghmā̃ ja gujrī gayā̃.*
>
> Grandmother passed away in her sleep.

Past (he …): તે ગુજરી ગયો, *te gujrī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુજરવું** (*gujarvũ*) is usually traced to Persian *guzar-*, the stem of the verb "to pass, go by".
