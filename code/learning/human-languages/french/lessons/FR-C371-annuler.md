## You'll want to know: annuler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C371-ANNULER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**annuler** — "to cancel".

> Le concert est annulé à cause de la pluie.
>
> *The concert is cancelled because of the rain.*

*Annuler une réservation* — to cancel a booking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*annuler* comes from Late Latin *annūllāre* "to bring to nothing" (EtymDB), built on *nūllus* "none" (Lewis & Short).
