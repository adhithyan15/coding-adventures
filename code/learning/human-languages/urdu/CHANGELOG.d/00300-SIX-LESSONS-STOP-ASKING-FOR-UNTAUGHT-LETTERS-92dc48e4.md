## Six lessons stop asking for untaught letters

- Script closure found six Urdu violations; there are now none.
- `UR-C08-puchhna` romanizes Persian *porsīdan* and *pursān-e-ḥāl*, including
  in its source link text.
- `UR-C15-hawa` romanizes the Arabic *hawāʾ*, and `UR-C13-lal` the Persian
  *lāl*, instead of printing them with Arabic vowel marks the course never
  teaches.
- `UR-C13-kala` spells میرا without the zer in its heading.
- `UR-R22-second-pass-one-mark-apart` points at **ق** in *qamīz* and **تا**
  in *jūtā*, not the whole words, which contain ض and ج.
- `UR-R23-second-pass-three-layers` romanizes *garmī*, whose گ has no
  letter lesson yet.
