## You'll want to know: توج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C309-QASR309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**توج** — *tawwaja* — "to crown".

> توج الأمير ملكا بعد أبيه.
>
> *tuwwija al-amīr malikan baʿda abīhi.*
>
> The prince was crowned king after his father.

Present: يتوج, *yutawwij*.

In sport: توج الفريق بالكأس, the team won the cup.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**توج** (*tawwaja*) comes from *tāj*, "a crown", an Iranian loan (EtymDB). Arabic gave it the Form II pattern: "to put a crown on".
