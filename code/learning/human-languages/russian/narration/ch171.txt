Russian, chapter 171: Singer, Vet, Judge, Soldier, Builder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
певец (pevéts) — a singer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bill; an account, then the Russian for cash.

You'll want to know: певец.
певец (pevéts) — "a singer".

The word, taken apart.
певец (pevéts) comes from петь, "to sing" (Vasmer), with -ец, "one who does it".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a vet, then the Russian for a judge; a referee.

You'll want to know: солдат.
солдат (soldát) — "a soldier".

The word, taken apart.
солдат (soldát) — known from 1515; it came through German Soldat or Dutch soldaat from Italian soldato, "one hired for pay", from soldare, "to hire" (Vasmer), and soldo, "a coin", Latin solidus.
