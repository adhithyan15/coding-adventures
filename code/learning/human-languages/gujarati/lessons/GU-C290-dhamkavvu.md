# ધમકાવવું (dhamkāvvũ) — to threaten

## You'll want to know: ધમકાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C290-DAKU290-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમકાવવું** — *dhamkāvvũ* — "to threaten".

> એક ડાકુએ બંદૂક બતાવીને મુસાફરોને ધમકાવ્યા.
>
> *ek ḍākue bandūk batāvīne musāphrone dhamkāvyā.*
>
> One bandit showed his gun and threatened the passengers.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મુસાફરોને ધમકાવ્યા, *teṇe musāphrone dhamkāvyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમકાવવું** (*dhamkāvvũ*) belongs with **ધમકી**, a threat: CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb in the same entry, *\*dhammakka* (CDIAL 6736). To threaten is to frighten someone with words or a weapon.
