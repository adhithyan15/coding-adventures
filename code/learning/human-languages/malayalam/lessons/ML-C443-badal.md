## You'll want to know: ബദൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C443-PARIDHI443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ബദൽ** — *badal* — "an alternative; a substitute".

> ആ പദ്ധതിക്ക് ഒരു ബദൽ കണ്ടെത്തണം.
>
> *ā paddhatikkŭ oru badal kaṇṭettaṇaṁ.*
>
> An alternative to that plan must be found.

As an adjective it comes first: ബദൽ വഴി, an alternative route.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ബദൽ** (*badal*) is usually traced to Hindustani *badal*, from Arabic *badal*, "a substitute, an exchange".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
