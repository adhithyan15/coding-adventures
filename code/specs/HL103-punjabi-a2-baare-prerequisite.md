# HL103 — Punjabi A2 `ਬਾਰੇ` prerequisite

Issue: #18241

After PR #18225 merged, an aggregate-only audit of the preserved confidential
Punjabi A2 mock drafts against merged main
`895d28d1899bb38123f6396c4f8f51d6a59c6ad3` finds **ਬਾਰੇ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs twice in the confidential learner material and
once in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential body nor the
remaining candidate set belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 203 closes that single-token gap through one deliberately narrow
postpositional frame: **ਕਿਤਾਬ ਬਾਰੇ** — “about the book.” The voice-first opener
retrieves only familiar **ਕਿਤਾਬ**. One lesson directly owns exact **ਬਾਰੇ**, an
immediate lesson retrieves its sound, meaning, and script, a bounded frame
lesson places it after the familiar noun, and a four-skill payoff retrieves the
complete phrase again. The target has one direct owner and three later
retrievals. It must not leak into authored, generated-book, or narration copy
before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-GRAMMAR-BAARE-203`. The lesson keeps the Punjabi form whole
and owns only the invariant postpositional meaning “about / concerning.” It
does not claim new credit for **ਕਿਤਾਬ**, teach a broader postposition survey,
introduce oblique-case theory or an inflectional paradigm, compare alternate
constructions, add synonyms, or teach unrelated meanings.

Punjabi University Patiala's online grammar explains that Punjabi
postpositions follow a noun or pronoun, unlike English prepositions:
<https://www.learnpunjabi.org/postposition.html>. The Punjabi dictionary entry
identifies **ਬਾਰੇ** as a postposition meaning “about, pertaining to,” gives
ordinary usage examples, and cites Bhai Maya Singh's 1895 *The Panjabi
Dictionary*:
<https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%A8%AC%E0%A8%BE%E0%A8%B0%E0%A9%87>.
Punjab School Education Board Class 5 material independently uses **ਬਾਰੇ** in
ordinary school prompts, including questions about a lesson:
<https://static.pseb.ac.in/media/1654685607_5th%20Punjabi-second-language.pdf>.

The exact historical derivation of modern written **ਬਾਰੇ** remains unknown in
the cited sources. The lesson therefore makes no etymological or
letter-by-letter derivational claim and keeps the present-day word whole.

This is the twenty-sixth prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case;
no shared aggregate is introduced.
