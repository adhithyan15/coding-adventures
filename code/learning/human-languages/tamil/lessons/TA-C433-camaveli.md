## You'll want to know: சமவெளி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C433-SIKARAM433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சமவெளி** — *camaveḷi* — "a plain; flat land".

> மலையிலிருந்து கீழே பரந்த சமவெளி தெரிந்தது.
>
> *malaiyiliruntu kīḻē paranta camaveḷi terintatu.*
>
> From the hill a wide plain could be seen below.

சம, level, + வெளி, an open space.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சமவெளி** (*camaveḷi*) is built from *cama*, "level", borrowed from Sanskrit *sama*, "equal, level" (CDIAL 13173), and வெளி, "an open space" (DEDR 5498).
