## You'll want to know: hǎojiǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C185-WHEN185-01]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎojiǔ* — "a long time; ages".

> *wǒmen hǎojiǔ méi jiànmiàn le* — We haven't seen each other for ages.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones: hǎojiǔ is said háojiǔ, the first rising.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hǎojiǔ* is built from 好 'good', here 'very', and *jiǔ* 'long-lasting', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xawX* and *kjuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'lasting long'.
