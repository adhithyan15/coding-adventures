## Added — the pulli and the words that use it print stroke-order filmstrips

The pulli ் now has a cited pen path, and the book prints a stroke-order
filmstrip for the lessons that teach it and for words that use it. It is one
touch of the pen, made after the consonant is complete: the body first, then
the dot above it. The order comes from hand-recorded reference drawings of
all 18 consonants with pulli in Abhinaya Rajarajan's *Varai* app; an
earlier reading of Info-farmer's *Writing Tamil* animations agrees. It is
one writer's drawings, so the record says confidence is medium.

- **New filmstrips (17 lessons):** TA-S08-pulli and TA-W03-pulli-vanakkam
  (the dot by itself); and the words வணக்கம் (TA-W03-write-vanakkam),
  ி, நன்றி (TA-W04-i-sign-write-nandri), ஆம் (TA-W05), இல்லை (TA-W06),
  என் (TA-W08-read-en), பெயர் (TA-W09), நான் (TA-W10), நீங்கள் (TA-W11),
  தமிழ் (TA-W16), ஊர் (TA-W18), சட்டை (TA-W23), பயம் (TA-W27),
  ஆனால் (TA-W31), சொல் (TA-W32) and ஏன் (TA-W33).
- **Order.** Each dot is drawn right after its own consonant; signs written
  to the left (ெ, ே, ை) still come first, so இல்லை is இ, ல, ், ை, ல.
- **Not yet:** ு and ூ have no cited pen path, so TA-S121, TA-S135 and every
  word with one (சொல்லுங்கள், மூன்று, ஒன்று and others) still print no
  filmstrip. Neither do words with the joined டி (எப்படி, வண்டி), or with
  க்ஷ or ஸ்ரீ, which the book's font prints as single shapes.
- Lesson prose, narration and durations do not change: filmstrips are
  printed in the book only.
