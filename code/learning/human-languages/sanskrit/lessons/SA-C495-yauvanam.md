## You'll want to know: यौवनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C495-YAUVANA495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**यौवनम्** — *yauvanam* — "youth (the time of life)".

> यौवने उत्साहः बहु भवति।
>
> *yauvane utsāhaḥ bahu bhavati.*
>
> In youth there is much enthusiasm.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यौवनम्** (*yauvanam*) is built on *yuvan*, "young", the word behind **युवकः**; MW gives "youth, youthfulness, adolescence" (MW).
