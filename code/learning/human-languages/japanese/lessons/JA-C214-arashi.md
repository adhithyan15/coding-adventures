## You'll want to know: あらし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C214-STORM214-01]; assesses=[] -->

**あらし** — *arashi* — "a storm".

> **あしたは あらしでしょう。** — *ashita wa arashi deshō* — "There will probably be a storm tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あらし** (*arashi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
