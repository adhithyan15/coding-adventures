## You'll want to know: ਪੋਟਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C252-GHR252-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਟਲੀ** — *poṭlī* — "a small bundle".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਦੀ ਪੋਟਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੁੜ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī dī poṭlī vich guṛ hai.*
>
> There is jaggery in my grandmother's little bundle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੋਟਲੀ** (*poṭlī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pōṭṭalī*, "bundle", a late word that CDIAL sets under a reconstructed *\*pōṭṭa*, "bundle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *poṭlī*, "small bag" (CDIAL 8396).
