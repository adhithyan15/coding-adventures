## You'll want to know: মগ্ন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C516-BINODON516-04]; assesses=[] -->

**মগ্ন** — *môgnô* — "absorbed (in something)".

> মেয়েটা গল্পের বইয়ে মগ্ন ছিল।
>
> *Meyeṭā gôlper bôiye môgnô chhilô.*
>
> The girl was absorbed in a storybook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মগ্ন** (*môgnô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *magna*, "sunk, plunged", from *majj*, "to sink" (Monier-Williams): sunk deep in what you are doing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
