کھلونا — khilaunā — "a toy".

The word, taken apart.
کھلونا (khilaunā) is built on khel-, "play", with the ending -aunā: a thing to play with. CDIAL lists the verb under a reconstructed khel-, "play" (CDIAL 3918).

Lesson 3 of 5.
گیند (gēnd) — a ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for matches, then the Urdu for a toy.

You'll want to know: گیند.
گیند — gēnd — "a ball".

The word, taken apart.
گیند (gēnd) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from genduka, "ball for play" (CDIAL 4248). CDIAL notes that the related kanduka has been traced to Dravidian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پتنگ (patang) — a kite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a toy, then the Urdu for a ball.

You'll want to know: پتنگ.
پتنگ — patang — "a kite".

The word, taken apart.
پتنگ (patang) is usually traced to Sanskrit pataṅga, "bird; winged insect". CDIAL lists that word, with Hindi patiṅgā, "grasshopper", as its inherited form (CDIAL 7721), but not patang, "kite".

Lesson 5 of 5.
پلنگ (palang) — a bed frame, a bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a ball, then the Urdu for a kite.

You'll want to know: پلنگ.
پلنگ — palang — "a bed frame, a bed".

The word, taken apart.
پلنگ (palang) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from palyaṅka, "bed" (CDIAL 7964). CDIAL notes that this form, with -ṅg, spread from the north-west.
