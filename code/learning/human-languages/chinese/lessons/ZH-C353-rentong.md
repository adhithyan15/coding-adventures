## You'll want to know: rèntóng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C353-POINT353-05]; assesses=[] -->

*rèntóng* — "to share someone's view; to identify with".

> *wǒ hěn rèntóng nǐ de kànfǎ* — I really share your view.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rèntóng* is built from *rèn* and 同 'the same', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyinH* and *duwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to recognize' and the second 'equal'.
