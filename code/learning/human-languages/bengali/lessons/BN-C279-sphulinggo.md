## You'll want to know: স্ফুলিঙ্গ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C279-SHIKHA279-03]; assesses=[] -->

**স্ফুলিঙ্গ** — *sphulinggô* — "a spark".

> একটা ছোট স্ফুলিঙ্গ থেকে বাজারে আগুন লাগল।
>
> *ekṭā chhoṭo sphulinggô theke bājāre āgun lāglô.*
>
> The market caught fire from one small spark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্ফুলিঙ্গ** (*sphulinggô*) is Sanskrit *sphuliṅga*, 'a spark of fire' (Monier-Williams), taken over whole. It is a written word; in talk a spark is often ফুলকি.
