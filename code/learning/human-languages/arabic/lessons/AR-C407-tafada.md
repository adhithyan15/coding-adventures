## You'll want to know: تفادى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C407-TAJANNUB407-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تفادى** — *tafādā* — "to avert".

> تفادى الطفل الكرة.
>
> *tafādā aṭ-ṭifl al-kura.*
>
> The child got out of the way of the ball.

Present: يتفادى, *yatafādā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفادى** (*tafādā*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-d-y*, "to ransom" (Lane). It is Form VI. How its sense, keeping clear of a thing, grew from ransoming is not settled.
