# മൃതദേഹം (mṛtadēhaṁ) — a dead body

## You'll want to know: മൃതദേഹം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C376-JEEVAN376-03]; assesses=[] -->

**മൃതദേഹം** — *mṛtadēhaṁ* — "a dead body".

> നാട്ടുകാർ പുഴയുടെ തീരത്ത് ഒരു മൃതദേഹം കണ്ടു.
>
> *nāṭṭukār puḻayuṭe tīrattŭ oru mṛtadēhaṁ kaṇṭu.*
>
> The locals saw a dead body on the river bank.

The respectful news word for a body; the plain old word is ശവം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മൃതദേഹം** (*mṛtadēhaṁ*) is built from two Sanskrit words, *mṛta*, "dead", and *deha*, "the body" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
