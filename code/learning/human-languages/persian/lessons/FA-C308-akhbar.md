## You'll want to know: اخبار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C308-GOZARESH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اخبار** (*akhbâr*) — "the news (programme)".

> پدرم هر شب ساعت نه اخبار را تماشا میکند.
>
> *Pedaram har shab sâ'at-e noh akhbâr râ tamâshâ mikonad.*
>
> My father watches the news every night at nine.

The Arabic broken plural of *khabar* "a piece of news". *Akhbâr* is the news in general or the news programme.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*akhbâr* is borrowed from Arabic *akhbār* (EtymDB), the broken plural of *khabar* "news", which Persian also has.
