# en medio — in the middle

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-PUERTA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **la puerta**, the door. Now place it at the centre: **la
puerta está en medio**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-MEDIO]; assesses=[] -->

**Medio** comes from Latin **medius**, “middle.” English kept the same root in
**median**, **medium** and **intermediate**. Spanish uses **en medio** for a
position between the edges or between two things.

## Grammar Lens: the frame en medio de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-MEDIO, ES-LEX-PUERTA] -->

Say **en medio** when the reference is understood. Add **de** before the thing
that frames the middle: **en medio de la puerta**, in the middle of the door.
