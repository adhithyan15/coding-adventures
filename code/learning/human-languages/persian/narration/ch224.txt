Persian, chapter 224: To scratch, To press, To connect, To separate, To peel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خاراندن (khârândan) — to scratch (an itch).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to carry, then the Persian for to move.

You'll want to know: خاراندن.
خاراندن — khârândan — "to scratch (an itch)".

The word, taken apart.
khârândan is built from khâr-, the stem of khâridan, "to itch", as its causative: to make the itch go. The verb is native Persian, inherited rather than borrowed.

Lesson 2 of 5.
فشار دادن (feshâr dâdan) — to press, to squeeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to move, then the Persian for to scratch.

You'll want to know: فشار دادن.
فشار دادن — feshâr dâdan — "to press, to squeeze".

The word, taken apart.
feshâr dâdan is "to give pressure": feshâr is built on feshordan, "to press" (EtymDB), a native verb. dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".

Lesson 3 of 5.
وصل کردن (vasl kardan) — to connect, to plug in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to scratch, then the Persian for to press.

You'll want to know: وصل کردن.
وصل کردن — vasl kardan — "to connect, to plug in".

The word, taken apart.
vasl kardan pairs vasl, borrowed from Arabic waṣl, "joining" (root w-ṣ-l, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
جدا کردن (jodâ kardan) — to separate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to press, then the Persian for to connect.

You'll want to know: جدا کردن.
جدا کردن — jodâ kardan — "to separate".

The word, taken apart.
jodâ kardan pairs jodâ, "separate", with kardan. jodâ is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian judāg, "separate, different". (It also lists Arabic jiddan, a different word.) kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to connect, then the Persian for to separate.

You'll want to know: پوست کندن.
پوست کندن — pust kandan — "to peel".

The word, taken apart.
pust kandan is "to dig off the skin": pust, "skin", is usually traced to Middle Persian pōst, and kandan, "to dig", to Middle Persian kandan. Both are native.
