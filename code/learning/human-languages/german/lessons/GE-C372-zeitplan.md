## You'll want to know: der Zeitplan
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C372-VEREINBARUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zeitplan** — "the schedule".

> Wir sind gut im Zeitplan: Das Haus ist im Juni fertig.
>
> *We are well on schedule: the house will be finished in June.*

Plural: *die Zeitpläne*. For trains it is *der Fahrplan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zeitplan* joins *Zeit* "time", Proto-Germanic *tīdiz* (EtymDB), to *Plan*, borrowed from French *plan* and going back to Latin *plānus* "level, flat" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): a drawing laid out flat.
