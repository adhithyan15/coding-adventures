## You'll want to know: சீறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C453-KOPAM453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சீறு** — *cīṟu* — "to flare up; to hiss".

> நான் கேள்வி கேட்டேன், அவர் சீறினார்.
>
> *nāṉ kēḷvi kēṭṭēṉ, avar cīṟiṉār.*
>
> I asked a question and he flared up.

Past (I …): சீறினேன், *cīṟiṉēṉ*.

Its first sense is the hiss of a snake or the snort of a horse; of a person, a sudden angry outburst.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சீறு** (*cīṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2639): the entry joins the hiss of a snake, the snort of a horse and a burst of rage.
