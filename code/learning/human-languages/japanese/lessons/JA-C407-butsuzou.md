## You'll want to know: ぶつぞう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C407-SANPAI407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつぞう** — *butsuzō* — "a statue of the Buddha".

> **てらで ふるい ぶつぞうを みました。** — *tera de furui butsuzō o mimashita* — "I saw an old statue at the temple."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつぞう** (*butsuzō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *butsu*, "Buddha" (Middle Chinese *bjut*) and *zō*, "statue" (*zjangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
