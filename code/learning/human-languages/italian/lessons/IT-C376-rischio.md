## You'll want to know: il rischio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C376-RISCHIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il rischio** — "the risk".

> Se non prenoti, corri il rischio di non trovare posto.
>
> *If you do not book, you run the risk of not finding a seat.*

*Correre il rischio* — to run the risk. *A rischio* — at risk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rischio* is usually traced to Medieval Latin *risicum*, *resicum*, a word of sea trade. The deeper origin is not settled: EtymDB gives no source for *rischio* (EtymDB), and Greek and Arabic sources have both been proposed.
