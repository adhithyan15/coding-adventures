## You'll want to know: la justicia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C603-DERECHO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la justicia** — "the justice".

> Pedimos justicia.
>
> *We ask for justice.*

Also the courts: *acudir a la justicia* — to go to court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*justicia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iustitia* 'justice', built on *iustus* 'lawful, right' (EtymDB).
