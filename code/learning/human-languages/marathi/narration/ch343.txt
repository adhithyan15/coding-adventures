Marathi, chapter 343: Making a Case.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मुद्दा (muddā) — a point at issue; a point in an argument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an explanation, then the Marathi for to come about.

You'll want to know: मुद्दा.
मुद्दा — muddā — "a point at issue; a point in an argument".
हा मुद्दा फार महत्त्वाचा आहे. hā muddā phār mahattvācā āhe. This point is very important.
Masculine. Plural: मुद्दे, mudde.
मुद्द्यावर येणे, to come to the point.

The word, taken apart.
मुद्दा (muddā) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic muddaʿā, "a thing claimed or alleged", from the same Arabic root as daʿwā, "a claim"; in Marathi it is the point under discussion.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तर्क (tarka) — logic; reasoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to come about, then the Marathi for a point at issue.

You'll want to know: तर्क.
तर्क — tarka — "logic; reasoning".
त्याच्या बोलण्यात काहीच तर्क नव्हता. tyācyā bolaṇyāt kāhīc tarka navhatā. There was no logic at all in what he said.
Masculine.
तर्क (tarka) लावणे, to reason something out, to guess.

The word, taken apart.
तर्क (tarka) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit tarka, "conjecture, reasoning" (Monier-Williams). CDIAL lists the same head (CDIAL 5714).

Lesson 3 of 5.
युक्तिवाद (yuktivād) — a reasoned argument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a point at issue, then the Marathi for logic.

You'll want to know: युक्तिवाद.
युक्तिवाद — yuktivād — "a reasoned argument".
त्याचा युक्तिवाद ऐकून सगळे गप्प बसले. tyācā yuktivād aikūn sagḷe gappa basle. Hearing his argument, everyone fell silent.
Masculine.
युक्तिवाद (yuktivād) करणे, to argue a case; a quarrel is भांडण.

The word, taken apart.
युक्तिवाद (yuktivād) is built from two tatsamas, Sanskrit yukti, "contrivance, reasoning", and vāda, "speech, thesis" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
मांडणे (māṇḍṇe) — to put forward; to set out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for logic, then the Marathi for a reasoned argument.

You'll want to know: मांडणे.
मांडणे — māṇḍṇe — "to put forward; to set out".
मीराने बैठकीत आपलं मत मांडलं. mīrāne baiṭhkīt āplaṁ mat māṇḍlaṁ. Meera put forward her view at the meeting.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मुद्दा मांडला, tyāne muddā māṇḍlā.
मत मांडणे (māṇḍṇe), to state one's view; also to set things out in order, as on a table.

The word, taken apart.
मांडणे (māṇḍṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; its first sense is to set things out in order, and from there to set out a view.
