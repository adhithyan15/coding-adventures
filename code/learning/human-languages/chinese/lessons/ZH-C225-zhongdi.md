## You'll want to know: zhòngdì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C225-FARM225-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhòngdì* — "to farm; to work the land".

> *nóngmín měitiān zhòngdì* — Farmers work the land every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhòngdì* is built from *zhòng* 'to plant' and 地 'land', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyowngH* and *dijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to plant in the soil'.
