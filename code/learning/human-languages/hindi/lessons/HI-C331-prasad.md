## You'll want to know: प्रसाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C331-VRAT331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसाद** — *prasād* — "a blessed offering (of food)".

> पुजारी ने सबको प्रसाद बाँटा।
>
> *pujārī ne sabko prasād bā̃ṭā.*
>
> The priest handed out the blessed offering to everyone.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसाद** (*prasād*) is Sanskrit *prasāda*, "grace, favour" (Monier-Williams): food first offered to a god and then shared out as a blessing.
