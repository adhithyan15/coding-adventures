## You'll want to know: dès lors
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C348-GRACE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dès lors** — "from then on; consequently".

> Il a perdu son travail ; dès lors, tout a changé.
>
> *He lost his job; from then on, everything changed.*

Two senses: from that moment on, and so. *Dès lors que* means given that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lors* goes back through Old French to Vulgar Latin *illā hōrā* "at that hour" (EtymDB). *dès* "from" is usually traced to Latin *dē ex* "from out of".
