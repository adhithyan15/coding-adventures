Russian, chapter 131: To begin, To visit, To put, To believe, To drive.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
начинать (nachinát) — to begin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for thin, then the Russian for fat.

You'll want to know: начинать.
начинать (nachinát) — "to begin".

The word, taken apart.
начинать (nachinát) is the ongoing partner of начать, "to begin": на- with -чать. Vasmer finds the same root in конец, "end", and искони, "from the very start", and compares Greek kainós, "new".

The word, taken apart.
посещать (poseshchát) is the ongoing partner of посетить, "to visit", which Vasmer thinks is apparently built on an old Slavic sětĭ, "a guest", a cousin of Lithuanian svẽčias, "guest", and Greek hetaîros, "companion".

Lesson 3 of 5.
класть (klast) — to put (flat).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to begin, then the Russian for to visit.

You'll want to know: класть.
класть (klast) — "to put (flat)".

The word, taken apart.
класть (klast) from Proto-Slavic klasti, present kladǫ — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian klóti, "to spread", and Gothic -hlaþan, German laden, "to load": English lade is a cousin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
верить (vérit) — to believe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to visit, then the Russian for to put.

You'll want to know: верить.
верить (vérit) — "to believe".

The word, taken apart.
верить (vérit) comes from вера, "faith" (Vasmer), Proto-Slavic věra, which Vasmer relates to Avestan varənā-, "faith", and probably to Latin vērus, "true", and German wahr, "true".

Lesson 5 of 5.
водить (vodít) — to drive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to put, then the Russian for to believe.

You'll want to know: водить.
водить (vodít) — "to drive".

The word, taken apart.
водить (vodít) from Proto-Slavic voditi, "to lead", with вести, "to lead" — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian vadýti and Avestan vādayeiti, "leads". Driving a car is "leading" it.
