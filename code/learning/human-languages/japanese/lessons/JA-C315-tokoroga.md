## You'll want to know: ところが
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C315-SURUTO315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ところが** — *tokoroga* — "however; but then".

> **ところが、へんじは きませんでした。** — *tokoroga, henji wa kimasen deshita* — "But no answer came."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ところが** (*tokoroga*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict writes it with the kanji of *tokoro*, "place", and the particle *ga*, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
