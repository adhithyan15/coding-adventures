## You'll want to know: il primogenito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C307-NASCITA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il primogenito** — "the first-born child".

> Il primogenito si chiama come il nonno.
>
> *The first-born is named after his grandfather.*

A daughter is *la primogenita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*primogenito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prīmōgenitus* "first-born" (EtymDB), from *prīmus* "first" and *genitus* "born".
