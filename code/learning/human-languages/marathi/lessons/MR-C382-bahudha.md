## You'll want to know: बहुधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C382-SHAKYA382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बहुधा** — *bahudhā* — "probably; most likely".

> बहुधा उद्या पाऊस पडेल.
>
> *bahudhā udyā pāūs paḍel.*
>
> It will probably rain tomorrow.

Often first in the sentence, before the guess: बहुधा तो येईल.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहुधा** (*bahudhā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bahudhā*, "in many ways" (CDIAL 9191); Marathi uses it for what is most likely.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
