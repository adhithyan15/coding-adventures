## You'll want to know: सौदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C423-KHAREDI423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सौदा** — *saudā* — "a deal; a bargain".

> घराचा सौदा शेवटी पक्का झाला.
>
> *gharācā saudā śevṭī pakkā jhālā.*
>
> In the end the deal for the house was settled.

Masculine.

सौदा पक्का होणे, a deal is closed; सौदा करणे, to strike a deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौदा** (*saudā*) is a loan usually traced to Persian *saudā*, "trade, a deal".
