## You'll want to know: il capitolo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C524-ROMANZO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il capitolo** — "chapter".

> Leggo un capitolo ogni sera prima di dormire.
>
> *I read a chapter every evening before I go to sleep.*

*Nel prossimo capitolo* — in the next chapter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capitolo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *capitulum* "a little head, a heading" (EtymDB), from *caput* "head".
