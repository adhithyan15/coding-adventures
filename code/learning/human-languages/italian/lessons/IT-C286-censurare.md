## You'll want to know: censurare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C286-EDIZIONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**censurare** — "to censor".

> Hanno censurato una scena del film.
>
> *They censored a scene from the film.*

In formal Italian it also means to criticise strongly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*censurare* is built on *censura* "censorship", from Latin *cēnsūra* and *cēnsēre* "to assess, to judge" (Lewis & Short).
