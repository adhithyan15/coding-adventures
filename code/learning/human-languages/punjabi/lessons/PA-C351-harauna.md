## You'll want to know: ਹਰਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C351-BALL351-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਰਾਉਣਾ** — *harāuṇā* — "to defeat; to beat (in a game)".

> ਸਾਡੀ ਟੀਮ ਹਰ ਮੈਚ ਵਿੱਚ ਉਸ ਟੀਮ ਨੂੰ ਹਰਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍī ṭīm har maich vich us ṭīm nū̃ harāundī hai.*
>
> Our team beats that team in every match.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਹਰਾਇਆ, *maiṁ Aman nū̃ harāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਰਾਉਣਾ** (*harāuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hārayati*, "causes to be taken, loses"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *harāuṇā*, "to defeat", under it (CDIAL 14061).
