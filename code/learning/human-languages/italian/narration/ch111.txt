Italian, chapter 111: Nurse, Cook, Police officer, Shop assistant, Farmer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'infermiera — a nurse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a walnut, then the Italian for pepper.

You'll want to know: l'infermiera.
l'infermiera — "a nurse".

The word, taken apart.
infermiera — built from the old word infermo, "sick", with -iera, the ending for a woman who does a job: the woman who tends the sick. infermo from Latin infirmus, "not strong, weak" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: il contadino.
il contadino — "a farmer".

The word, taken apart.
contadino — built from contado, the farmland around a city, with -ino, "one who belongs to it". contado goes back to Latin comitatus, the lands of a count. EtymDB 2.1 records the Latin word but not the Italian steps.
