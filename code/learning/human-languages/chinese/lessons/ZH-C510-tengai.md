## You'll want to know: 疼爱
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C510-GRAND510-04]; assesses=[] -->

**疼爱** — *téng'ài* — "to dote on".

> *wàipó hěn téng'ài wǒ* — Grandma really dotes on me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**疼爱** (*téng'ài*) is built from 疼 'to ache' and *ài* 'to love', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *downg* and *'ojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'pain' and the second 'to pity'; the sense 'to love dearly' is not given for the first there.
