## You'll want to know: বঁড়শি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C523-BAIRE523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বঁড়শি** — *bõṛshi* — "a fish-hook".

> জেলে বঁড়শিতে একটা বড় মাছ ধরল।
>
> *Jele bõṛshite ekṭā bôṛo māchh dhôrlô.*
>
> The fisherman caught a big fish on his hook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বঁড়শি** (*bõṛshi*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *bãṛsi*, "a fish-hook", under Sanskrit *baḍiśa*, which Turner thought might be of Munda origin (CDIAL 9123).
