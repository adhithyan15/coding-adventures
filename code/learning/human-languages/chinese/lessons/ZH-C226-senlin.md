## You'll want to know: sēnlín
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C226-LAKE226-03]; assesses=[] -->

*sēnlín* — "forest".

> *sēnlín lǐ hěn ānjìng* — It's very quiet in the forest.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sēnlín* is built from *sēn* 'dense with trees' and *lín* 'woods'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *lim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'trees'; the dictionary describes the first, *srim* there, as how tall trees look.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
