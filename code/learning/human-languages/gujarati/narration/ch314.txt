Gujarati, chapter 314: The Strike.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હડતાળ (haḍtāḷ) — a strike (stopping work).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for information, then the Gujarati for word (of something).

You'll want to know: હડતાળ.
હડતાળ — haḍtāḷ — "a strike (stopping work)".
હડતાળથી લોકોને ખૂબ દુઃખ થયું. haḍtāḷthī lokone khūb duḥkh thayũ. The strike caused people a lot of hardship.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હડતાળ (haḍtāḷ) is probably built from haṭ, a market, from Sanskrit haṭṭa, where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin haṛtāl, the closing of shops in protest (CDIAL 13944), and તાળું, a lock (CDIAL 5749): the market locked up. Work stops: ડ્રાઈવરોની હડતાળ છે, the drivers are on strike.

Lesson 2 of 5.
મોરચો (morco) — a protest march.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for word (of something), then the Gujarati for a strike.

You'll want to know: મોરચો.
મોરચો — morco — "a protest march".
લોકોએ શહેરમાં મોટો મોરચો કાઢ્યો. lokoe śahermā̃ moṭo morco kāḍhyo. People held a big protest march in the city.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મોરચો (morco) is usually traced to Persian mōrča, "an entrenchment", a soldiers' word: in Gujarati it is a march of protest, which people કાઢે છે, bring out into the street.

Lesson 3 of 5.
સૂત્ર (sūtra) — a slogan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a strike, then the Gujarati for a protest march.

You'll want to know: સૂત્ર.
સૂત્ર — sūtra — "a slogan".
લોકો સૂત્રો બોલતા હતા. loko sūtro boltā hatā. People were shouting slogans.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સૂત્ર (sūtra) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sūtra, "a thread", and then a short rule held in a line (CDIAL 13561). In a march it is the short line everyone shouts together.

Lesson 4 of 5.
પ્રવચન (pravacan) — a talk; a speech.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a protest march, then the Gujarati for a slogan.

You'll want to know: પ્રવચન.
પ્રવચન — pravacan — "a talk; a speech".
સાંજે ગુરુએ ચોકમાં પ્રવચન આપ્યું. sā̃je gurue cokmā̃ pravacan āpyũ. In the evening the teacher gave a speech in the square.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રવચન (pravacan) is a tatsama, Sanskrit pravacana, "speaking, teaching, exposition" (Monier-Williams). A speech is given: પ્રવચન આપ્યું.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ગુંજવું (gunjvũ) — to resound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a slogan, then the Gujarati for a speech.

You'll want to know: ગુંજવું.
ગુંજવું — gunjvũ — "to resound".
આખો ચોક સૂત્રોથી ગુંજતો હતો. ākho cok sūtrothī gunjto hato. The whole square was ringing with slogans.
Past (it …): તે ચોક ગુંજ્યો, te cok gunjyo.

The word, taken apart.
ગુંજવું (gunjvũ) goes back to Sanskrit guñja, "humming", a sound word; CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gū̃j, an echo, but not the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 4173). A place ગુંજે છે, rings, with voices or music.
