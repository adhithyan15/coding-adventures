## You'll want to know: やがて
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C314-SUKKARI314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**やがて** — *yagate* — "before long; in time".

> **やがて あめが やみました。** — *yagate ame ga yamimashita* — "Before long the rain stopped."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やがて** (*yagate*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
