Persian, chapter 40: To become, To build, Fish, Rice, Meat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شدن (shodan) — to become.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to come back, then the Persian for to take away; to win.

You'll want to know: شدن.
شدن — shodan — "to become".

The word, taken apart.
shodan is a native Persian verb, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian šudan, which meant "to go". "To go" turning into "to become" is a shift English go shows too, as in "go bad".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ماهی (mâhi) — a fish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to become, then the Persian for to build.

You'll want to know: ماهی.
ماهی — mâhi — "a fish".

The word, taken apart.
mâhi is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian māhīg, "fish" (EtymDB). EtymDB takes it back to an Indo-Iranian word for "fish", the same word as Sanskrit matsya.

برنج — berenj — "rice".

The word, taken apart.
berenj is usually traced to Middle Persian brinj, "rice", and set beside Sanskrit vrīhi, "rice"; the deeper origin of the rice word is not settled.
