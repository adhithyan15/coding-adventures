# Six lines about a trip

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01, RU-SKILL-READ-NARRATIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The four-line writing step held together with *и*, *но*, *а*
and *потому что*. This time there are six, and they tell a
story, as Anna's rainy Saturday did.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one person who will read it: *друг*, *подруга*, someone in
your family.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-STORY-FRAME-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-IMPERFECTIVE-PROCESS-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-PERFECTIVE-RESULT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-CHAIN-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A told trip is more than a list of events. It has a **scene**,
in the imperfective: *было*, *шёл дождь*, *мы жили в гостинице*. It has
**steps** in order, each one finished, in the perfective. It has a
**feeling**. And a good one ends by looking forward, or wishing.

[PAUSE 2s] A past verb agrees with who did it: *-л*, *-ла*, *-ли*. A few,
such as *мог*, drop the *л* only in the form for a man: *мог*, but
*могла*, *могли*. A verb after *бы* takes the same forms.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-NAZAD-AGO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-POKA-WHILE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-KOGDA-EVENT-01, RU-LEX-ZATEM-01, RU-GRAMMAR-POETOMU-RESULT-01, RU-LEX-TAK-CHTO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-PERFECTIVE-01, RU-LEX-OPOZDAL-NA-POEZD-01, RU-GRAMMAR-RAD-SHORT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-USTAL-STATE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-BYLO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PO-MOEMU-01, RU-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-EE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-CHEM-THAN-01, RU-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-IRREGULAR-01, RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-BY-01, RU-GRAMMAR-U-MENYA-BYLO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-POLITE-01, RU-LEX-MECHTAT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-NADEYUS-CHTO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-EXPLAIN-STEPS-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] On paper, write **six lines** to your reader about a trip, real
or invented, one line for each of these:

1. the scene: when, with *назад* (*год назад*), and what it was like, in
   the imperfective;
2. the first step, with *сначала*, in the perfective;
3. the next step, with *потом* or *затем*, and a reason, with *потому
   что*, *поэтому* or *так что*; a problem works well here: *я опоздал на
   поезд*;
4. something that cut in, with *когда* or *пока*, and how it ended, with
   *наконец*;
5. how you felt, with *я был рад, что …*, *я устал* or *мне было …*, or
   what you thought, with *по-моему* and a comparison: *лучше, чем …*;
6. one line forward, with *если бы у меня было время, я бы …*, or *я бы
   хотел …*, or a hope: *я надеюсь, что …*, *я мечтаю …*.

[PAUSE 3s] Use only words you already write. The exercise is the joining,
and the aspect of each verb.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-L-GENDER-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BYL-PAST-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Now go through your lines and find every verb. In line one,
is it imperfective, a scene? In lines two, three and four, is each step
perfective, one deed done? That is the slip to check first.

[PAUSE 2s] Then the endings: *-л* for a man, *-ла* for a woman, *-ли* for
more than one, after *бы* as well.

[PAUSE 2s] Last, the commas: one before *что*, *потому что* and *чем*,
one before *поэтому* or *так что* when they join inside a sentence, and
one at the seam after a *когда*, *пока* or *если* half. If line six
uses *если бы*, does *бы* stand on both sides of the comma?
