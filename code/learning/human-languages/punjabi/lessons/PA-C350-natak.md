## You'll want to know: ਨਾਟਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C350-SHOW350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਾਟਕ** — *nāṭak* — "a stage play; a drama".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਐਤਵਾਰ ਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਨਾਟਕ ਦੇਖਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ aitvār nū̃ ikk nāṭak dekhāṅge.*
>
> We will watch a play on Sunday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਾਟਕ** (*nāṭak*) is taken over from Sanskrit *nāṭaka*, "acting, dancing", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
