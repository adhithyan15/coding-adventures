Persian, chapter 127: Bitter, Sour, Salty, Purple, Grey.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
تلخ (talkh) — bitter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for dangerous, then the Persian for safe.

You'll want to know: تلخ.
تلخ — talkh — "bitter".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: talkh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: talkh, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say talkh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for dangerous, then the Persian for safe, then say talkh again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does تلخ (talkh) mean? ("Bitter".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Food, clothes and animals — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for purple and grey.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: beans, chickpeas, a coin, a bracelet, a tie]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a jacket, slippers, a pond, a spring, a salt desert]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a buffalo, a beetle, a worm, an insect, a giraffe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a whale, a dolphin, a shark, a squirrel, a bat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Beans? (لوبیا, lubiyâ.) Chickpeas? (نخود, nokhod.) A coin? (سکه, sekke.) A bracelet? (دستبند, dastband.) A tie? (کراوات, kerâvât.) A jacket? (کاپشن, kâpshan.) Slippers? (دمپایی, dampâyi.) A pond? (برکه, barke.) A spring? (چشمه, cheshme.) A salt desert? (کویر, kavir.) A buffalo? (گاومیش, gâvmish.) A beetle? (سوسک, susk.) A worm? (کرم, kerm.) An insect? (حشره, hashare.) A giraffe? (زرافه, zarâfe.) A whale? (نهنگ, nahang.) A dolphin? (دلفین, delfin.) A shark? (کوسه, kuse.) A squirrel? (سنجاب, sanjâb.) A bat? (خفاش, khofâsh.)

(خشک, khoshk.) Soft? (نرم, narm.) Hard? (سفت, seft.) Ugly? (زشت, zesht.) Young? (جوان, javân.) Old? (پیر, pir.) Lazy? (تنبل, tanbal.) Clever? (زرنگ, zerang.) Timid? (ترسو, tarsu.) Famous? (مشهور, mashhur.) Dangerous? (خطرناک, khatarnâk.) Safe? (امن, amn.) Bitter? (تلخ, talkh.) Sour? (ترش, torsh.) Salty? (شور, shur.) Purple? (بنفش, banafsh.) Grey? (خاکستری, khâkestari.)
