Hindi, chapter 442: Peaks and Mounds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शिखर (śikhar) — a peak, a summit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the horizon, then the Hindi for dusk.

You'll want to know: शिखर.
शिखर — śikhar — "a peak, a summit".
हम सुबह तक पहाड़ के शिखर पर पहुँचे। ham subah tak pahāṛ ke śikhar par pahũce. We reached the top of the mountain by morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शिखर (śikhar) is a tatsama, Sanskrit śikhara, "pointed, crested" and so a peak (Monier-Williams); the spire of a temple is its शिखर too.

रेगिस्तान में रेत के ऊँचे टीले हैं। registān meṁ ret ke ū̃ce ṭīle haiṁ. There are high sand dunes in the desert.

The word, taken apart.
टीला (ṭīlā) goes back to ṭalla, ṭilla, "a heap", forms CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5451), which lists Hindi ṭīlā, "a hillock, a mound".

हवा में तिनके उड़ रहे थे। havā meṁ tinke uṛ rahe the. Bits of straw were flying in the wind.

The word, taken apart.
तिनका (tinkā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit tṛṇa, "grass, a blade of grass, a straw" (CDIAL 5906), which lists Hindi tinkā: in sayings it is the smallest thing there is.

Lesson 5 of 5.
लुढ़काना (luṛhkānā) — to roll (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a heap, then the Hindi for a straw.

You'll want to know: लुढ़काना.
लुढ़काना — luṛhkānā — "to roll (something)".
बच्चों ने टीले से पत्थर नीचे लुढ़काए। baccoṁ ne ṭīle se patthar nīce luṛhkāe. The children rolled stones down the mound.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ढोल लुढ़काया, usne ḍhol luṛhkāyā.

The word, taken apart.
लुढ़काना (luṛhkānā) is the causative partner of लुढ़कना, "to roll", from Sanskrit luṭhati, "rolls" (CDIAL 11079): लुढ़कना is to roll, लुढ़काना to roll a thing.
