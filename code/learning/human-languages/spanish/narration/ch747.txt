Spanish, chapter 747: La familia política.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el cuñado — the brother-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to flirt, then the Spanish for the lover.

You'll want to know: el cuñado.
el cuñado — "the brother-in-law".
Mi cuñado vive en Valencia. My brother-in-law lives in Valencia.
La cuñada — sister-in-law.

The word, taken apart.
cuñado is usually traced to Latin cognātus "related by blood" (Lewis & Short); Spanish narrowed it to the in-law.
