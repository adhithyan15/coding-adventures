## You'll want to know: मक्कार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C467-KAPAT467-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मक्कार** — *makkār* — "crooked; a cheat".

> वह दुकानदार मक्कार है, हमेशा कम तोलता है।
>
> *vah dukāndār makkār hai, hameśā kam toltā hai.*
>
> That shopkeeper is a crook; he always gives short weight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मक्कार** (*makkār*) is usually traced to Arabic *makkār*, "crafty, wily", which reached Hindi through Persian.
