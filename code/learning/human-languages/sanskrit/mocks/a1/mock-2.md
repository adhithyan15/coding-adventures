# Sanskrit A1 mock 2 — learner paper

This is an independent parallel form, not a rehearsal copy of mock 1. Give this
paper to the learner and keep
[`mock-2-examiner-script.md`](mock-2-examiner-script.md) confidential. Collect
the Reading and Listening sheets before Writing. The written papers take 58
minutes. The speaking paper takes 10 minutes after five minutes of preparation.
Keep A1 word boundaries unjoined; correctly applied external sandhi remains
acceptable.

## Reading — 20 minutes

Read the sources and choose A, B, or C.

#### Source

## Writing — 20 minutes

Keep one timer running through both tasks. Write only in Devanagari. Do not use
romanization, a dictionary, a translator, a spell-checker, or any visible
Sanskrit model.

1. Practical form — 7 minutes. Complete all seven fields for a neighbourhood
   activity programme. Personal or fictional details are both acceptable. The
   unscored example shows only the response type: “Group size: 8”.

   | field | response |
   |---|---|
   | name | __________ |
   | place you come from | __________ |
   | place you live now | __________ |
   | language spoken | __________ |
   | work or study | __________ |
   | one person you know | __________ |
   | preferred activity | __________ |

2. Named-reader message — 13 minutes. Your reader is Ravi. Write a **30–40
   word message** to return Ravi's book tomorrow. Greet him, identify yourself,
   name the book or subject, explain briefly why you still have it, propose a
   place and time, ask whether that arrangement works, and close politely.

## Speaking — 10 minutes

You have five minutes of preparation for parts 2 and 3. Notes may contain only
keywords, not a written answer script. The examiner conducts the confidential
prompts in Sanskrit and records the complete paper.

1. Personal interview — answer five familiar questions.
2. Prepared description — speak for one minute about a familiar person.
3. Transactional role-play — buy fruit, ask the price, and request two items.
