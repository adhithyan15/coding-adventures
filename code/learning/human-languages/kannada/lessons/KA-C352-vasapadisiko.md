## You'll want to know: ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C352-DANGE352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ** — *vaśapaḍisiko* — "to capture; to take control of".

> ದಂಗೆಕೋರರು ಕೋಟೆಯನ್ನು ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊಂಡರು.
>
> *daṅgekōraru kōṭeyannu vaśapaḍisikoṇḍaru.*
>
> The rebels captured the fort.

Past (I …): ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊಂಡೆ, *vaśapaḍisikoṇḍe*.

ವಶ, control, with -ಪಡಿಸು, to bring, and -ಕೊ, for oneself. The police ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ stolen goods too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಶಪಡಿಸಿಕೊ** (*vaśapaḍisiko*) is built from Sanskrit *vaśa*, "will, authority, power" (CDIAL 11430), with the native verb-makers -*paḍisu* (DEDR 3853) and -*ko*, from *koḷ*, "to take" (DEDR 2151).

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
