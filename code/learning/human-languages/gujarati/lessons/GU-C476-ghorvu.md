## You'll want to know: ઘોરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘોરવું** — *ghorvũ* — "to snore".

> દાદા આખી રાત ઘોરે છે.
>
> *dādā ākhī rāt ghore che.*
>
> Grandfather snores all night.

Past (he …): તે આખી રાત ઘોર્યો, *te ākhī rāt ghoryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘોરવું** (*ghorvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *ghurati*, "cries out frighteningly", probably an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to snore" (CDIAL 4487).
