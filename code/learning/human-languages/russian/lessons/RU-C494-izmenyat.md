## You'll want to know: изменять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C494-MARRIAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**изменять** (*izmenyát*) — "to be unfaithful; to change".

> Он никогда не изменял жене.
>
> *On nikogdá ne izmenyál zhené.*
>
> He has never been unfaithful to his wife.

Imperfective; perfective изменить. With the dative, to be unfaithful: *изменять жене*; with the accusative, to change: *изменять план*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*изменять* is built on *из-* and *менять* 'to change'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
