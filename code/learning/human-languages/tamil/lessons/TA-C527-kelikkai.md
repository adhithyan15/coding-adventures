# கேளிக்கை (kēḷikkai) — entertainment; amusement

## You'll want to know: கேளிக்கை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C527-OYVUNERAM527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**கேளிக்கை** — *kēḷikkai* — "entertainment; amusement".

> இந்த நகரத்தில் இளைஞர்களுக்குக் கேளிக்கை இடங்கள் அதிகம்.
>
> *inta nakarattil iḷaiñarkaḷukkuk kēḷikkai iṭaṅkaḷ atikam.*
>
> In this city there are many places of entertainment for young people.

A rather formal word; it names shows, games and parties together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **கேளிக்கை** (*kēḷikkai*) is not settled; it may be built on Sanskrit *kēli*, "play, sport, pastime" (Monier-Williams), with a Tamil noun ending.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
