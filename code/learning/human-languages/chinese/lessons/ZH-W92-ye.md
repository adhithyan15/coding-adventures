## Script — the right half of 他
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 也

**One piece, and your hand already knows it: the right half of 他, stroke for stroke.**

**A hooked turn**: a horizontal that rises a little, turns down the right side and hooks to the left.

**A short vertical** from above it, down through it.

**The long bend**: down the left from above the horizontal, along the foot to the right, and a hook up at the end.

Three strokes.
