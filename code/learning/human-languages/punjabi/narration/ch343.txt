Punjabi, chapter 343: A Mix-up.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਉਲਝਣਾ (ulajhṇā) — to get tangled; to get muddled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for plain to understand, then the Punjabi for to suggest.

You'll want to know: ਉਲਝਣਾ.
ਉਲਝਣਾ — ulajhṇā — "to get tangled; to get muddled".
ਮੈਂ ਲੰਮੇ ਸਵਾਲਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਨਹੀਂ ਉਲਝਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ lamme savālāṁ vich nahīṁ ulajhdā hāṁ. I don't get muddled by long questions.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਵਾਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਉਲਝਿਆ, maiṁ savāl vich uljhiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਲਝਣਾ (ulajhṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit uparundhati, "obstructs"; CDIAL lists Punjabi uljhaṇā, "to be entangled", under it (CDIAL 2221).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ਉੱਕਣਾ (ukkṇā) — to slip up; to make a mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to suggest, then the Punjabi for to get muddled.

You'll want to know: ਉੱਕਣਾ.
ਉੱਕਣਾ — ukkṇā — "to slip up; to make a mistake".
ਅਮਨ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਦੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਉੱਕਦਾ ਹੈ। Aman hisāb vich kade nahīṁ ukkdā hai. Aman never slips up in his sums.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਜਵਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਉੱਕਿਆ, maiṁ javāb vich ukkiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਕਣਾ (ukkṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit utkramati, "ascends", later "neglects, transgresses"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ukkṇā, "to make a mistake", under it (CDIAL 1737).

ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰਾ ਇਸ਼ਾਰਾ ਸਮਝਦੇ ਹੋ? kī tusī̃ merā ishārā samajhde ho? Do you get my hint?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਇਸ਼ਾਰਾ (ishārā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic ishāra, "sign".
