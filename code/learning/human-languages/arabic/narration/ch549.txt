Arabic, chapter 549: The attic and the door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
علية (ʿulliyya) — an attic, a loft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to compete with, then the Arabic for to punch.

You'll want to know: علية.
علية — ʿulliyya — "an attic, a loft".
وضعنا الصناديق القديمة في العلية. waḍaʿnā aṣ-ṣanādīq al-qadīma fī al-ʿulliyya. We put the old boxes in the attic.

The word, taken apart.
علية (ʿulliyya) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-l-w, "to be high, to rise" (Lane). The ʿulliyya is the room at the top.

Lesson 2 of 5.
جرس (jaras) — a bell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to punch, then the Arabic for an attic.

You'll want to know: جرس.
جرس — jaras — "a bell".
رن جرس الباب في الصباح. ranna jaras al-bāb fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ. The doorbell rang in the morning.
Plural: أجراس, ajrās.
جرس (jaras) الباب is a doorbell.

The word, taken apart.
جرس (jaras) is built on the native Arabic root j-r-s, "a sound, a low voice" (Lane). Lane gives jaras as a sound, and a bell that makes it.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bell, then the Arabic for a handle.

You'll want to know: مزلاج.
مزلاج — mizlāj — "a bolt, a latch".
أغلقت الباب بالمزلاج. aghlaqtu al-bāb bi-l-mizlāj. I shut the door with the bolt.

The word, taken apart.
مزلاج (mizlāj) is built on the native Arabic root z-l-j, "to slide, to slip" (Lane). Lane gives azlaja al-bāb as to make a door fast with the mizlāj. The pattern mifʿāl names a tool.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بالوعة (bālūʿa) — a drain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a handle, then the Arabic for a bolt.

You'll want to know: بالوعة.
بالوعة — bālūʿa — "a drain".
سدت الأوراق البالوعة في الشارع. saddat al-awrāq al-bālūʿa fī ash-shāriʿ. Leaves blocked the drain in the street.

The word, taken apart.
بالوعة (bālūʿa) is built on the native Arabic root b-l-ʿ, "to swallow" (Lane). A bālūʿa swallows the water. Ibtalaʿa, to swallow, which you know, is from the same root.
