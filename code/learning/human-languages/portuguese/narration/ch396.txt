Portuguese, chapter 396: A minha conta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a aplicação — the app, the application (software); the use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to go bankrupt, then the Portuguese for in debt.

You'll want to know: a aplicação.
a aplicação — "the app, the application (software); the use".
Descarreguei uma aplicação para aprender línguas. I downloaded an app for learning languages.
In speech a app. Also the act of applying: a aplicação da lei.

The word, taken apart.
aplicação is a learned borrowing from Latin applicātiō "attachment, application" (EtymDB), from applicāre "to join to", built on plicāre "to fold" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: o utilizador.
o utilizador — "the user".
A aplicação tem um milhão de utilizadores. The app has a million users.
European Portuguese; in Brazil o usuário.

The word, taken apart.
utilizador is built on utilizar "to use", which was borrowed from French utiliser (EtymDB); the French verb rests on Latin ūtilis "useful", on ūtī "to use" (Lewis & Short).

European Portuguese aceder a; in Brazil acessar.

aceder comes from Latin accēdere "to come near, to approach" (EtymDB), built on cēdere "to go" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
a senha — the password; the (queue) ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the user, then the Portuguese for to access.

You'll want to know: a senha.
a senha — "the password; the (queue) ticket".
Esqueci-me da senha outra vez. I forgot the password again.
Also the number ticket in a queue: tirar a senha. A palavra-passe is common in Portugal too.

The word, taken apart.
senha is inherited from Latin signa "signs, marks" (EtymDB), the plural of signum "a sign" (Lewis & Short).

Mudei a foto do meu perfil. I changed my profile photo.

The word, taken apart.
perfil was borrowed from Spanish perfil (EtymDB), from Old Provençal perfil "outline", usually traced to Latin fīlum "a thread" (Lewis & Short).
