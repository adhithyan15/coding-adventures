# Looking back: yesterday and tomorrow

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *Aman went home yesterday*. Then *we will go tomorrow*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-LEX-KALL-216, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217, PA-LEX-AURAT-217, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AGREE-PERSON-217, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-IA-218, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219, PA-LEX-KADDHNA-219, PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220, PA-GRAMMAR-US-NE-220, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-LIA-DITTA-220, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-NEG-QUESTION-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223, PA-LEX-SHAID-223, PA-READ-MESSAGE-224] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU HEAR: *a woman came yesterday*] Say **ਔਰਤ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *I got up early and drank tea*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *Aman bought a book and she gave money*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ ਅਤੇ ਉਸ ਨੇ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *I took out the book*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਕੱਢੀ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *what will you do tomorrow?*] Say **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੀ ਕਰੋਗੇ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *perhaps we will go next week*] Say **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਰੇਲ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ।**] Give its meaning, then say when Aman will be at the station.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the models and write **ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ।** or its woman's form]
