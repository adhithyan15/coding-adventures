## You'll want to know: हिलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C303-BHUKAMP303-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हिलना** — *hilnā* — "to shake (by itself); to move".

> रात को अचानक पूरा घर हिला।
>
> *rāt ko acānak pūrā ghar hilā.*
>
> Suddenly, in the night, the whole house shook.

Past (it …): वह पेड़ हिला, *vah peṛ hilā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिलना** (*hilnā*) is inherited from *\*hillati*, "shakes", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 14120). It is the intransitive partner of **हिलाना**: you **हिलाते हैं** a branch, and the branch **हिलती है**.
