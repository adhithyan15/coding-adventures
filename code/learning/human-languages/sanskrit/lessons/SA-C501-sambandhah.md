## You'll want to know: सम्बन्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C501-SAMBANDHA501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्बन्धः** — *sambandhaḥ* — "a relationship".

> मम मातुलेन सह मम सम्बन्धः मधुरः अस्ति।
>
> *mama mātulena saha mama sambandhaḥ madhuraḥ asti.*
>
> I have a sweet relationship with my uncle.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्बन्धः** (*sambandhaḥ*) is built from *sam* and the root √bandh, "to bind", the root of **बध्नाति**; MW gives "close connection, relation" and "a relative" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
