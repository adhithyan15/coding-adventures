## You'll want to know: છૂટાછેડા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C498-VIDAY498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**છૂટાછેડા** — *chūṭācheḍā* — "a divorce".

> દસ વરસ પછી તેમના છૂટાછેડા થયા.
>
> *das varas pachī temnā chūṭācheḍā thayā.*
>
> After ten years they got divorced.

A masculine noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છૂટાછેડા** (*chūṭācheḍā*) is built from *chūṭā*, "loosened, apart", on the stem of **છૂટવું** (*chūṭvũ*), "to get loose" (CDIAL 3707), and *cheḍā*, "ends": the ends of cloth tied together at a wedding come apart.

## What you've built
