## You'll want to know: la foudre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C462-ORAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la foudre** — "the lightning (that strikes)".

> La foudre est tombée sur un arbre du parc.
>
> *Lightning struck a tree in the park.*

*La foudre* strikes; *un éclair* is the flash you see.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*foudre* is inherited from Latin *fulgur* 'lightning' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the word without a source.
