## You'll want to know: کائی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C437-JHARI437-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کائی** — *kāī* — "moss; green scum (on water)".

> تالاب کے پانی پر کائی ہے۔
>
> *tālāb ke pānī par kāī hai.*
>
> There is green scum on the pond.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کائی** (*kāī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kāī*, "green scum on still water", under *kāvikā*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3109).
