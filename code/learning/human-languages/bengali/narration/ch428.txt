Bengali, chapter 428: Damp and Haze.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আর্দ্র (ārdrô) — humid; moist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for temperature, then the Bengali for heat (warmth).

You'll want to know: আর্দ্র.
আর্দ্র — ārdrô — "humid; moist".
সমুদ্রের কাছে বাতাস আর্দ্র। sômudrer kāchhe bātās ārdrô. Near the sea the air is humid.
A careful word, heard in weather reports.

The word, taken apart.
আর্দ্র (ārdrô) is Sanskrit ārdra, "wet, moist, damp" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.

বর্ষায় ঘরের দেয়াল স্যাঁতসেঁতে হয়। bôrshāy ghôrer deyāl sæ̃tsẽte hôy. In the monsoon the walls of the house get damp.

The word, taken apart.
স্যাঁতসেঁতে (sæ̃tsẽte) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali sẽt-sẽtiyā, "damp, moist", under the reconstructed saṁtimita (CDIAL 12891); the doubled shape is an expressive repetition.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ঢেকে যাওয়া (ḍheke jāoyā) — to be covered over; to be shrouded.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for hazy, then the Bengali for damp (and dank).

You'll want to know: ঢেকে যাওয়া.
ঢেকে যাওয়া — ḍheke jāoyā — "to be covered over; to be shrouded".
কুয়াশায় পুরো গ্রামটা ঢেকে গেল। kuyāshāy puro grāmṭā ḍheke gelô. The whole village was shrouded in fog.
Past (he/she …): সেই পাহাড় মেঘে ঢেকে গেল, sei pāhāṛ meghe ḍheke gelô.
ঢেকে is the -এ form of ঢাকা, to cover: what covers takes -এ, কুয়াশায়, মেঘে.

The word, taken apart.
ঢেকে যাওয়া (ḍheke jāoyā) joins ঢেকে, the -এ form of ঢাকা, "to cover", which CDIAL lists as Bengali ḍhākā under the reconstructed ḍhakk, "cover" (CDIAL 5574), to যাওয়া, to go: to go under a cover.
