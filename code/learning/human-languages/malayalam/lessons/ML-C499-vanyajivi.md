# വന്യജീവി (vanyajīvi) — a wild animal; wildlife

## You'll want to know: വന്യജീവി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C499-VANAM499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**വന്യജീവി** — *vanyajīvi* — "a wild animal; wildlife".

> വനത്തിൽ വന്യജീവികളെ കാണാൻ ഞങ്ങൾ രാവിലെ പോയി.
>
> *vanattil vanyajīvikaḷe kāṇān ñaṅṅaḷ rāvile pōyi.*
>
> We went in the morning to see wild animals in the forest.

Plural: വന്യജീവികൾ, *vanyajīvikaḷ*.

വന്യ, wild, of the forest, plus ജീവി, a living thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വന്യജീവി** (*vanyajīvi*) is a Malayalam compound built from two Sanskrit words, *vanya*, "growing or living in a forest, wild", and *jīvin*, "living" (Monier-Williams).
