## You'll want to know: पोषयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C476-SAKTI476-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पोषयति** — *poṣayati* — "nourishes".

> माता शिशुं क्षीरेण पोषयति।
>
> *mātā śiśuṃ kṣīreṇa poṣayati.*
>
> The mother nourishes her baby with milk.

Past (she …): सा शिशुं पोषितवती, *sā śiśuṃ poṣitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोषयति** (*poṣayati*) is built on the causative of the root √puṣ, "to thrive": to make thrive; MW gives "to rear, nourish, feed" (MW).
