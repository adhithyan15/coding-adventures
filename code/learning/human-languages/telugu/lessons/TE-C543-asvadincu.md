# ఆస్వాదించు (āsvādiñcu) — to savour; to relish

## You'll want to know: ఆస్వాదించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C543-KACERI543-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆస్వాదించు** — *āsvādiñcu* — "to savour; to relish".

> మేము ఆ సంగీతాన్ని హాయిగా ఆస్వాదించాము.
>
> *mēmu ā saṅgītānni hāyigā āsvādiñcāmu.*
>
> We relished that music at our ease.

Past (I …): ఆస్వాదించాను, *āsvādiñcānu*.

Enjoying food, music or a view slowly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆస్వాదించు** (*āsvādiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *āsvādana*, "eating, tasting, enjoying" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
