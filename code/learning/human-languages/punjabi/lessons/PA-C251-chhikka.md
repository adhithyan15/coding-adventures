## You'll want to know: ਛਿੱਕਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C251-GHR251-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਿੱਕਾ** — *chhikkā* — "a hanging net (for pots)".

> ਦੁੱਧ ਦੀ ਹਾਂਡੀ ਛਿੱਕੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *dudh dī hāṇḍī chhikke vich hai.*
>
> The milk pot is in the hanging net.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਿੱਕਾ** (*chhikkā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śikya*, "rope-sling for carrying things"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chikkā*, "a hanging net" (CDIAL 12427).
