## You'll want to know: lectīcārius, lectīcāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C531-IANI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lectīcārius, lectīcāriī** — "a litter-bearer".

> Sex lectīcāriī dominam per urbem portant.
>
> *Six litter-bearers carry their mistress through the city.*

Masculine, second declension, from *lectīca* — a litter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lectīcārius** is built on *lectīca* "litter, sedan chair" with *-ārius* (Lewis & Short); *lectīca* itself comes from *lectus* "couch" (EtymDB).
