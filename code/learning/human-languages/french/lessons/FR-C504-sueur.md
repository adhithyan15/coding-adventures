## You'll want to know: la sueur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C504-GYM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la sueur** — "sweat".

> Après une heure de course, il était tout en sueur.
>
> *After an hour's running he was dripping with sweat.*

*En sueur* — sweating.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la sueur* is inherited, through Old French *suor*, from Latin *sūdor* "sweat" (EtymDB), related to *sūdāre* "to sweat" (Lewis & Short).
