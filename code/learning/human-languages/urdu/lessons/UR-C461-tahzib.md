## You'll want to know: تہذیب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C461-MANNERS461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تہذیب** — *tahzīb* — "refinement; culture".

> اس کی باتوں میں تہذیب ہے۔
>
> *us kī bātoṅ meṅ tahzīb hai.*
>
> There is refinement in the way she speaks.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تہذیب** (*tahzīb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tahdhīb*, "refining, polishing".
