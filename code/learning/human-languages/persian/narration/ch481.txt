Persian, chapter 481: Patience and Temper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صبور (sabur) — patient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be proud, then the Persian for to show off.

You'll want to know: صبور.
صبور (sabur) — "patient".
پرستار بچه ها خیلی صبور است. parastâr-e bachchehâ kheyli sabur ast. The children's nurse is very patient.
An adjective. The noun is sabr 'patience', as in sabr kardan 'to wait'.

The word, taken apart.
sabur was borrowed from Arabic ṣabūr, 'patient, forbearing' (EtymDB). It belongs with sabr, 'patience', another Arabic loan.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بیصبر (bisabr) — impatient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to show off, then the Persian for patient.

You'll want to know: بیصبر.
بیصبر (bisabr) — "impatient".
بچه ها برای باز کردن هدیه ها بیصبر هستند. bachchehâ barâ-ye bâz kardan-e hediyehâ bisabr hastand. The children are impatient to open the presents.
bi- 'without' + sabr 'patience'. In speech people also say howsele nadârad 'he has no patience'.

The word, taken apart.
bisabr is built in Persian from the prefix bi-, 'without', and sabr, 'patience', which was borrowed from Arabic ṣabr: the same sabr as in sabr kardan.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تحمل کردن (tahammol kardan) — to put up with; to bear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for patient, then the Persian for impatient.

You'll want to know: تحمل کردن.
تحمل کردن (tahammol kardan) — "to put up with; to bear".
او صدای بلند را تحمل نمیکند. u sedâ-ye boland râ tahammol nemikonad. She can't stand loud noise.
tahammol 'endurance' + kardan; present stem kon-. The thing you bear takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
tahammol is a loan, usually traced to Arabic taḥammul, 'bearing, endurance'; EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خونسرد (khunsard) — calm, cool-headed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for impatient, then the Persian for to put up with.

You'll want to know: خونسرد.
خونسرد (khunsard) — "calm, cool-headed".
پدرم در تصادف هم خونسرد ماند. pedaram dar tasâdof ham khunsard mând. My father stayed calm even in the accident.
khun 'blood' + sard 'cold'. Unlike English cold-blooded, it is praise: someone who keeps a cool head.

The word, taken apart.
khunsard is built on two inherited words: khun, 'blood', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian xūn, and sard, 'cold', which it traces to Middle Persian as well (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
عصبانی شدن ('asabâni shodan) — to get angry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to put up with, then the Persian for calm.

You'll want to know: عصبانی شدن.
عصبانی شدن ('asabâni shodan) — "to get angry".
وقتی دیر میرسم، مدیرم عصبانی میشود. vaqti dir miresam, modiram 'asabâni mishavad. When I arrive late, my boss gets angry.
'asabâni 'angry, nervous' + shodan 'to become'; present stem shav-. In speech: 'asabâni mishe.

The word, taken apart.
'asabâni is built in Persian on 'asab, 'nerve', a word usually traced to Arabic ʿaṣab, with the adjective ending -âni: 'nervy'. shodan, 'to become', is inherited Persian.
