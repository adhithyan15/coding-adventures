# Review — it depends, and what would have been

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Say "It depends on the weather" (*atu vāṉilaiyaip poṟuttatu*) and "if (you) had come" (*vantiruntāl*).
- [YOU WRITE: **ஈ** and **ஈரம்**, once each]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C293-DEPENDS-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C293-UNREAL-IF-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C293-WOULD-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C292-CONDITIONAL-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C292-ENRAL-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C292-REAL-CONDITION-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C292-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C291-FROM-UNTIL-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Will you come tomorrow? It depends on the rain. If it rains from morning until evening, I will stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: If you don't come soon, the bus will leave.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had had time, I would have come.]
- [YOU SAY: If you had come, we would have gone to the sea together.]
- [YOU SAY: read your six lines aloud to your reader]
