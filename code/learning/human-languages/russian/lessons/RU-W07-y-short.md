# й — и in a hat

## Warm-up

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SCRIPT-Y-SHORT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> й

**No new shape at all.** This is **и**, which you have written since your very
first letters,
with a small curved cap sitting on top of it. The cap is called a *breve*, and
you draw the letter exactly as before and then add it last.

The cap changes the job. **и** is a vowel, the *ee* of *see*. **й** is a
**consonant**: the *y* of English *boy* or *yes*. Its formal name is "short и",
which describes it well — the same sound, clipped so short it stops being a
syllable of its own and leans on the vowel beside it.

That is why it turns up glued to other vowels at the ends of words, and why it
never carries the stress: it cannot, because it is not a syllable.
