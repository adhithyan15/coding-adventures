## You'll want to know: شعر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C521-POETRY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شعر** (*she'r*) — "a poem; poetry".

> او هر شب یک شعر میخواند.
>
> *u har shab yek she'r mikhânad.*
>
> He reads a poem every night.

Plural *she'rhâ*, or the Arabic *ash'âr*. *she'r goftan* — to write poetry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*she'r* comes from Arabic *šiʿr*, 'poem, poetry' (EtymDB).
