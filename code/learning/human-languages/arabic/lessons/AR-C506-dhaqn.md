## You'll want to know: ذقن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C506-WAJH506-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ذقن** — *dhaqan* — "a chin".

> وضع الرجل يده تحت ذقنه.
>
> *waḍaʿa ar-rajul yadahu taḥta dhaqanihi.*
>
> The man put his hand under his chin.

Plural: ذقون, *dhuqūn*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذقن** (*dhaqan*) is built on the native Arabic root *dh-q-n*, "the chin" (Lane). Lane gives two plurals, *adhqān* and *dhuqūn*.
