## You'll want to know: lo scultore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C522-SCULTURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scultore** — "sculptor".

> Lo scultore lavora nel suo studio.
>
> *The sculptor works in his studio.*

A woman is *la scultrice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scultore* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sculptor* (EtymDB), from *sculpere* "to carve".
