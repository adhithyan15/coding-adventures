## You'll want to know: উত্তেজনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C298-UTTEJONA298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উত্তেজনা** — *uttejônā* — "excitement; tension".

> খবরটা শুনে পাড়ায় উত্তেজনা ছড়াল।
>
> *khôbôrṭā shune pāṛāy uttejônā chhôṛālô.*
>
> When the news came, tension spread through the neighbourhood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উত্তেজনা** (*uttejônā*) is Sanskrit *uttejana* (feminine *uttejanā*), 'incitement, stimulation' (Monier-Williams), from *ut-* 'up' and *tejas* 'sharpness, heat': a stirring up.
