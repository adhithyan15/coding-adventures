# صباح (ṣabāḥ) — "morning"

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] The morning word, on the root of *dawn* and *waking*.

## Sounds you'll need

- `emphatic-sad` — the **ص** (ṣ) is an **emphatic** *s*: a heavier, deeper *s*
  made with the tongue lower and the mouth fuller than English *s*. Arabic has
  several such "emphatic" consonants; they genuinely change the word.
- `ha-heavy` — final **ح** (ḥ), the throaty *h* from *marḥaban*. → [reference](../pronunciation-reference.md)

## The word, taken apart

**صباح** (*ṣabāḥ*) — "morning." Root: **ص–ب–ح** (**ṣ–b–ḥ**), the idea of
**dawn / becoming light / waking**:

- **ṣabāḥ** — morning
- **ṣubḥ** — dawn, daybreak
- **aṣbaḥa** — to become, to wake up (to enter the morning) — one of Arabic's
  common "to become" verbs, literally "to reach morning"
