## You'll want to know: اشترك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C540-NADI540-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اشترك** — *ishtaraka* — "to subscribe; to take part".

> اشترك أخي في مجلة رياضية.
>
> *ishtaraka akhī fī majalla riyāḍiyya.*
>
> My brother subscribed to a sports magazine.

Present: يشترك, *yashtarik*.

It takes في.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشترك** (*ishtaraka*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-k*, "to share, to be a partner" (Lane). Sharika, a company, and shāraka, to share, which you know, are from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
