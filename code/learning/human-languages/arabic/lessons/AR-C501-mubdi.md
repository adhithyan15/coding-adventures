## You'll want to know: مبدع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C501-HIKMA501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مبدع** — *mubdiʿ* — "creative".

> ابنتي طالبة مبدعة في الرسم.
>
> *ibnatī ṭāliba mubdiʿa fī ar-rasm.*
>
> My daughter is a creative student in drawing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مبدع** (*mubdiʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-d-ʿ*, "to originate, to make a thing new" (Lane). Mubdiʿ is the participle of Form IV: one who makes a new thing.
