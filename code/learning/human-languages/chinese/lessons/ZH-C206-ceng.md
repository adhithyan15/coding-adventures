## You'll want to know: céng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C206-FLOOR206-02]; assesses=[] -->

*céng* — "floor; storey".

> *wǒmen de lóu yǒu èrshí céng* — Our building has twenty floors.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*céng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzong* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed, like the form of *lóu*, 'a house of more than one storey'.

## What you've built
