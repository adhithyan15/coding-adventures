## You'll want to know: اثاثہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C419-MUNAFA419-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اثاثہ** — *asāsā* — "an asset".

> یہ مکان ہمارا سب سے بڑا اثاثہ ہے۔
>
> *yih makān hamārā sab se baṛā asāsā hai.*
>
> This house is our biggest asset.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اثاثہ** (*asāsā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *athātha*, "household goods"; Urdu says its **ث** as an *s*.
