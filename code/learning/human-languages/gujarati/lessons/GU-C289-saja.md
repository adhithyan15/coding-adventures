## You'll want to know: સજા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C289-ADALAT289-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સજા** — *sajā* — "a punishment".

> જો અદાલત તેને ગુનેગાર માનશે તો તેને સજા થશે.
>
> *jo adālat tene gunegār mānśe to tene sajā thaśe.*
>
> If the court finds him guilty, he will be punished.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સજા** (*sajā*) is usually traced to Persian *sazā*, "what is fitting, what one deserves". It is feminine; a person **સજા થવી**, gets punished, and a court **સજા આપે છે**, passes sentence.
