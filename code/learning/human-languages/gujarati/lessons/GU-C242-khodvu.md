## You'll want to know: ખોદવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C242-HARVEST242-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોદવું** — *khodvũ* — "to dig".

> **મેં બટાકા ખોદ્યા.** — *mẽ baṭākā khodyā* — "I dug up potatoes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોદવું** (*khodvũ*) comes from *\*khodd*, "dig", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to dig, carve" (CDIAL 3934).
