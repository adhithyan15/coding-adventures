Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 154: What I Do Every Day.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
हूं बोलूं हूं (bolū̃ hū̃) — what I do.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say to speak and to hear. (बोलणो, सुणणो.) Then every day, रोज, and I am here.

Grammar Lens: the stem, -ूं and हूं.
Take -णो off बोलणो and बोल is left: the stem. Give it -ूं, then end on हूं:
हूं थोड़ु बोलूं हूं। hū̃ thoṛu bolū̃ hū̃. — I speak a little.
The -ूं sounds like हूं, I, so the sentence says I at both ends. The same two pieces fit any stem that ends in a consonant: सुणूं हूं, देखूं हूं.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: हूं गीत सुणूं हूं। — hū̃ gīt suṇū̃ hū̃. — I listen to songs.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं रोज गीत सुणूं हूं। — hū̃ roj gīt suṇū̃ hū̃. — I listen to songs every day.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं थोड़ु बोलूं हूं। — hū̃ thoṛu bolū̃ hū̃. — I speak a little.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What comes off सुणणो? (-णो.) What goes on, and what ends the sentence? (-ूं, then हूं.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Say that you listen to songs every day.


Lesson 2 of 4.
वो बोलै है (bolai hai) — what he does.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I listen to songs. Then to read and to write: बांचणो, लिखणो.

Grammar Lens: -ै and है.
For वो, for राम, for anyone you talk about, the stem takes -ै and the sentence ends on है:
वो थोड़ु बोलै है। vo thoṛu bolai hai. — He speaks a little.
राम रोज अखबार बांचै है। rām roj akhbār bā̃chai hai. — Ram reads the newspaper every day.
Nothing changes for a woman: मां गीत सुणै है। (mā̃ gīt suṇai hai.), mother listens to songs. Old Marwari wrote the same ending -aï: karaï, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: हूं रोज अखबार बांचूं हूं। — hū̃ roj akhbār bā̃chū̃ hū̃. — I read the newspaper every day.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: राम रोज अखबार बांचै है। — rām roj akhbār bā̃chai hai. — Ram reads it every day.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: मां चिट्ठी लिखै है। — mā̃ chiṭṭhī likhai hai. — Mother writes a letter.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I read? (हूं बांचूं हूं.) He reads? (वो बांचै है.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Say that Ram reads the newspaper every day.


Lesson 3 of 4.
आप बोलो हो (bolo ho) — what you do.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say mother listens to songs. Then you are here, and the request do it, करो.

Grammar Lens: -ो and हो.
After आप, the stem takes -ो, the ending of the requests करो and लावो, and the sentence ends on हो:
आप थोड़ु बोलो हो। āp thoṛu bolo ho. — You speak a little.
आप कांई करो हो? āp kāĩ karo ho? — What do you do?
करो alone asks someone to do something. करो हो after आप says what they do.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: आप गीत सुणो हो। — āp gīt suṇo ho. — You listen to songs.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: आप कांई करो हो? — āp kāĩ karo ho? — What do you do?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं चिट्ठी लिखूं हूं। — hū̃ chiṭṭhī likhū̃ hū̃. — I write letters.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I do? (करूं हूं: the stem कर, -ूं, हूं.) He does? (करै है.) You do, to आप? (करो हो.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Ask politely: what do you do?


Lesson 4 of 4.
Every day — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say I speak a little.

Guided Practice.
Every cell of the present so far, now that each one is taught:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
who: हूं. to speak: बोलूं हूं (bolū̃ hū̃). to read: बांचूं हूं.
who: वो. to speak: बोलै है (bolai hai). to read: बांचै है.
who: आप. to speak: बोलो हो (bolo ho). to read: बांचो हो.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I listen to songs every day; mother listens to songs]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Ram reads the newspaper every day; you read the newspaper]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: what do you do?, to someone you call आप]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I write letters; he writes letters]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I speak? (बोलूं हूं (bolū̃ hū̃).) He speaks? (बोलै है (bolai hai).) You speak? (बोलो हो (bolo ho).)
[once you have stopped driving — question: Say: I write letters, but he reads the newspaper.]
