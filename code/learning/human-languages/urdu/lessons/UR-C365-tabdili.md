## You'll want to know: تبدیلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C365-HALAT365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تبدیلی** — *tabdīlī* — "a change".

> اگر قانون میں تبدیلی آئی تو ٹیکس کم ہو گا۔
>
> *agar qānūn meṅ tabdīlī āī to ṭaiks kam ho gā.*
>
> If the law changes, the tax will come down.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تبدیلی** (*tabdīlī*) is built on *tabdīl*, "changing", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tabdīl*, with the noun ending *-ī*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
