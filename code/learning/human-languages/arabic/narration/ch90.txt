Arabic, chapter 90: Here, There, Right, Shop, Street.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
هنا (hunā) — here.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for in front of, then the Arabic for behind.

You'll want to know: هنا.
هنا — hunā — "here".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hunā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hunā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say hunā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for in front of, then the Arabic for behind, then say hunā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does هنا (hunā) mean? ("Here".) Say it once more.
