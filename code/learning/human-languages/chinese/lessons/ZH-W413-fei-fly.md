## Script — a hooked turn, then two small strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-FEI-FLY-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 飞

**One piece, three strokes.**

**First, a bar to the right that turns and slants down to the right**, then flicks up at the end: one stroke, without lifting the pen.

**Then a short stroke falling to the left**, on the right of the turn.

**Last, a dot** under it, falling to the right.

One long stroke and two short ones: three.
