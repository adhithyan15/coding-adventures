## You'll want to know: অবজ্ঞা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C351-SHRODDHA351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অবজ্ঞা** — *ôbôgyā* — "disdain; contempt".

> গরিব মানুষকে অবজ্ঞা করা অন্যায়।
>
> *gôrib mānushke ôbôgyā kôrā ônyāy.*
>
> It is wrong to look down on poor people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অবজ্ঞা** (*ôbôgyā*) is Sanskrit *avajñā*, *ava-* 'down' with *jñā* 'knowing': to think little of (Monier-Williams). জ্ঞ is said gy, as in জ্ঞান.
