## You'll want to know: ঘুষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C285-HUMKI285-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুষ** — *ghush* — "a bribe".

> চোর দারোগাকে টাকা দিতে চাইল, কিন্তু তিনি ঘুষ নিলেন না।
>
> *chor dārogāke ṭākā dite chāilô, kintu tini ghush nilen nā.*
>
> The thief tried to give the inspector money, but he would not take a bribe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুষ** (*ghush*) is filed in CDIAL 4499 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *ghūssā*, 'a present', which lists Bengali *ghus*, 'a bribe': a present with a purpose.
