## You'll want to know: ぬける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C389-OODOORI389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬける** — *nukeru* — "to get through (to the other side); to come out".

> **ろじを ぬけて、えきに つきました。** — *roji o nukete, eki ni tsukimashita* — "I went through the alley and got to the station."

Polite **ぬけます**, *nukemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **くるまが ろじを ぬけました。**, *kuruma ga roji o nukemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬける** (*nukeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
