## You'll want to know: revivir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C764-RECUERDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**revivir** — "to relive; to revive".

> Al ver las fotos revivo aquel día.
>
> *Looking at the photos, I relive that day.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revivir* is usually traced to Latin *revīvere* "to live again", built on *vīvere* "to live".
