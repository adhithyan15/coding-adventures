Kannada, chapter 104: People at Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ (vyāpāra) — business, trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for country, then the Kannada for state.

You'll want to know: ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ.
ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ (vyāpāra) — "business, trade".
ನನ್ನದು ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ (nannadu vyāpāra) — "mine is business", an answer to ನಿಮ್ಮ ಉದ್ಯೋಗ ಏನು?

What you've built.
One answer to what do you do.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vyāpāra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vyāpāra, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nannadu vyāpāra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for country, then the Kannada for state, then say vyāpāra again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ (vyāpāra) mean? ("Business, trade".) Say it once more.
