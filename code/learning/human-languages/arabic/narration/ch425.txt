Arabic, chapter 425: On board.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مضيف (muḍīf) — a flight attendant; a host.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a seat belt, then the Arabic for a crew.

You'll want to know: مضيف.
مضيف — muḍīf — "a flight attendant; a host".
أحضر المضيف كوب ماء لأمي. aḥḍara al-muḍīf kūb māʾ li-ummī. The flight attendant brought my mother a cup of water.
A woman is muḍīfa.

The word, taken apart.
مضيف (muḍīf) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-y-f, "to be a guest" (Lane). It is the Form IV participle, "one who takes in guests": a host, and on a plane a flight attendant.

Lesson 2 of 5.
راكب (rākib) — a passenger; a rider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a crew, then the Arabic for a flight attendant.

You'll want to know: راكب.
راكب — rākib — "a passenger; a rider".
نام راكب بجانبي أثناء الرحلة. nāma rākib bi-jānibī athnāʾa ar-riḥla. A passenger beside me slept during the flight.
Plural: ركاب, rukkāb.

The word, taken apart.
راكب (rākib) is built on the native Arabic root r-k-b, "to ride" (Lane). It is the active participle of rakiba, "to ride": one who rides.

Lesson 4 of 5.
شد (shadda) — to tighten; to fasten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a passenger, then the Arabic for a destination.

You'll want to know: شد.
شد — shadda — "to tighten; to fasten".
شد الراكب حزامه قبل الهبوط. shadda ar-rākib ḥizāmahu qabla al-hubūṭ. The passenger fastened his belt before landing.
Present: يشد, yashudd.
A doubled verb: shadadtu, "I tightened", unfolds the two d's.

The word, taken apart.
شد (shadda) is built on the native Arabic root sh-d-d, "to be firm; to bind tightly" (Lane). It is the plain verb of a doubled root.
