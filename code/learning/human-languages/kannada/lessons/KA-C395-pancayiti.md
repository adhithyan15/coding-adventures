# ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ (pañcāyiti) — a village council

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C395-ADALITA395-01, KA-LEX-C395-ADALITA395-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for administration, then the Kannada for a ministry.

## You'll want to know: ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C395-ADALITA395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ** — *pañcāyiti* — "a village council".

> ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಹೊಸ ಶಾಲೆ ಕಟ್ಟಿಸಿತು.
>
> *pañcāyiti ūrinalli hosa śāle kaṭṭisitu.*
>
> The village council had a new school built.

ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ ಚುನಾವಣೆ, a council election.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ** (*pañcāyiti*) is a loan from Hindustani *pañcāyat*, "a council", once a body of five elders, from Sanskrit *pañca*, "five" (CDIAL 7655).
