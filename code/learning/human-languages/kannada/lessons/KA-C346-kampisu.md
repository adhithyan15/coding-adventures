# ಕಂಪಿಸು (kampisu) — to tremble; to shake

## You'll want to know: ಕಂಪಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C346-SPHOTA346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಂಪಿಸು** — *kampisu* — "to tremble; to shake".

> ಸ್ಫೋಟದಿಂದ ಹತ್ತಿರದ ಮನೆಗಳು ಕಂಪಿಸಿದವು.
>
> *sphōṭadinda hattirada manegaḷu kampisidavu.*
>
> The houses nearby shook from the blast.

Past (it …): ಕಂಪಿಸಿತು, *kampisitu*.

The ground, a building or a voice trembles: ಭೂಮಿ ಕಂಪಿಸಿತು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಂಪಿಸು** (*kampisu*) is built on Sanskrit *kampa*, "trembling, a tremor" (CDIAL 2765), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

One more word for telling what happened.
