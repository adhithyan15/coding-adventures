Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಂದರು (bandaru) — a port, a harbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for for now, then the Kannada for a period.

You'll want to know: ಬಂದರು.
ಬಂದರು — bandaru — "a port, a harbour".

The word, taken apart.
ಬಂದರು (bandaru) is a loan from Persian bandar, "port, harbour", which came through Hindustani. Kannada adds -u at the end.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ದಿಕ್ಕು.
ದಿಕ್ಕು — dikku — "a direction".

The word, taken apart.
ದಿಕ್ಕು (dikku) comes from Sanskrit diś, "direction, region", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 6339). Kannada doubled the final sound and added -u.
