## You'll want to know: पैसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C72-COST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पैसा** (*paisā*) — "money".

It began as a fraction. Sanskrit पाद (*pāda*) is a quarter and अंश (*aṁśa*) is a
part, and the coin they named was a small share of a larger one. For a long
while a पैसा was one hundredth of a rupee.

Then the small coin ate the whole idea. Today पैसा as one coin is nearly gone,
and पैसा as **money in general** is what people say: *paisā nahīṁ hai* is "there
is no money", about a wallet or about a country.

The ै on the प is the *ai* matra, two strokes above the headline, and it is the
same one inside **कैसे**.
