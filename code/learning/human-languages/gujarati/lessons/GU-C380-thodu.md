## You'll want to know: થોડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C380-PURTU380-03]; assesses=[] -->

**થોડું** — *thoḍũ* — "a little".

> જો તમે થોડી રાહ જોશો તો બસ આવશે.
>
> *jo tame thoḍī rāh jośo to bas āvśe.*
>
> If you wait a little, the bus will come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થોડું** (*thoḍũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *stōka*, "a drop", and as an adverb "a little"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a little, few" (CDIAL 13720).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
