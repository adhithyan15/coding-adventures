## You'll want to know: ग़म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C455-GAM455-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ग़म** — *ġam* — "grief; sorrow at a loss".

> पूरा परिवार दादा की मौत के ग़म में है।
>
> *pūrā parivār dādā kī maut ke ġam meṁ hai.*
>
> The whole family is grieving over my grandfather's death.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ग़म** (*ġam*) is usually traced to Arabic *ġamm*, "grief, distress", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
