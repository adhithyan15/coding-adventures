Lesson 1 of 5.
परिवारः (parivāraḥ) — a household, a family.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for advises, then the Sanskrit for honours.

You'll want to know: परिवारः.
परिवारः — parivāraḥ — "a household, a family".

The word, taken apart.
parivāra is built on pari, "around", and the root √vṛ, "to cover, surround" (MW). It first meant "cover, surroundings", then "followers" (CDIAL 7876), and Hindi took it as parivār, "family".

Lesson 2 of 5.
बन्धुः (bandhuḥ) — a relative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for honours, then the Sanskrit for a household.

You'll want to know: बन्धुः.
बन्धुः — bandhuḥ — "a relative".

The word, taken apart.
bandhu is built on the root √bandh, "to bind, tie" (MW): a relative is someone bound to you. The root is inherited from Indo-European, and MW compares English bind. It is a Rigvedic word for "kinsman" (CDIAL 9144).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सखा (sakhā) — a companion, a (male) friend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a household, then the Sanskrit for a relative.

You'll want to know: सखा.
सखा — sakhā — "a companion, a (male) friend".

The word, taken apart.
sakhā is the nominative of sakhi, "friend, companion", which MW files under the root √sac, "to accompany". MW compares Latin socius, "companion". Its feminine sakhī lives on as Hindi sahelī (CDIAL 13074).

Lesson 4 of 5.
शत्रुः (śatruḥ) — an enemy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a relative, then the Sanskrit for a companion.

You'll want to know: शत्रुः.
शत्रुः — śatruḥ — "an enemy".

The word, taken apart.
śatru is a Rigvedic word for "enemy" (CDIAL 12286). MW reports that it is said to come from √śad, "the overthrower", and compares Greek kotos, "grudge", and English hate; the derivation is only reported, not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रतिवेशी (prativeśī) — a neighbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a companion, then the Sanskrit for an enemy.

You'll want to know: प्रतिवेशी.
प्रतिवेशी — prativeśī — "a neighbour".

The word, taken apart.
prativeśī comes from prativeśa, "a neighbouring house", built on prati, "facing", and veśa, "dwelling" (MW). A related form, prativeśya, lives on as Hindi paṛosī, "neighbour" (CDIAL 8599).
