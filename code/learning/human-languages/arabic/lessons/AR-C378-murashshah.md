## You'll want to know: مرشح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C378-INTIKHAB378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مرشح** — *murashshaḥ* — "a candidate".

> تكلم المرشح عن خطته.
>
> *takallama al-murashshaḥ ʿan khiṭṭatih.*
>
> The candidate talked about his plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرشح** (*murashshaḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-sh-ḥ*, "to sweat, to ooze" (Lane). Lane gives Form II for a mother training her young; *murashshaḥ* is one made ready for a post: a candidate.
