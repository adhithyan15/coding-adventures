## You'll want to know: nénglì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C260-SKILL260-01]; assesses=[] -->

*nénglì* — "ability".

> *tā hěn yǒu nénglì* — He's very capable.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nénglì* is built from 能 'able' and *lì* 'strength', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nong* and *lik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'skilled' and the second 'sinew'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for what I have and can do.
