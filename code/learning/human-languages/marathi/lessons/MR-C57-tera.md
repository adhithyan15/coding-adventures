## You'll want to know: तेरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-TERA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **तेरा** — *terā* — **13**

Sanskrit **त्रयोदश** (*trayodaśa*), *three-ten*. The **त्र-** of three is still
at the front — the same **त्र** you can hear in English *three* — and the
**-रा** is the ten again.
