Urdu, chapter 422: Brokers and Property.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دلال (dallāl) — a broker; a middleman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fee, then the Urdu for a currency.

You'll want to know: دلال.
دلال — dallāl — "a broker; a middleman".
دلال نے ہمیں ایک مکان دکھایا۔ dallāl ne hameṅ ek makān dikhāyā. The broker showed us a house.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
دلال (dallāl) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic dallāl, "a broker, one who points the way to a deal".

Lesson 4 of 5.
وصول کرنا (vusūl karnā) — to collect (money owed); to receive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for property, then the Urdu for insurance.

You'll want to know: وصول کرنا.
وصول کرنا — vusūl karnā — "to collect (money owed); to receive".
دلال نے اپنی فیس وصول کی۔ dallāl ne apnī fīs vusūl kī. The broker collected his fee.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کرایہ وصول کیا, us ne kirāyā vusūl kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
وصول کرنا (vusūl karnā) is built from vusūl, "receipt", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic wuṣūl, "arriving", and کرنا, "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
لوٹانا (lauṭānā) — to give back; to return (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for insurance, then the Urdu for to collect (money owed).

You'll want to know: لوٹانا.
لوٹانا — lauṭānā — "to give back; to return (something)".
دکاندار نے مجھے میرے پیسے لوٹائے۔ dukāndār ne mujhe mere paise lauṭāe. The shopkeeper gave me my money back.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کتاب لوٹائی, us ne kitāb lauṭāī.

The word, taken apart.
لوٹانا (lauṭānā) is built on لوٹنا, "to come back": this is the form for sending something back. No dictionary we use records an older form of لوٹنا, so its history is not settled.
