# बाजणो (bājṇo) — to ring; to sound

## You'll want to know: बाजणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C197-PHONE197-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बाजणो** — *bājṇo* — "to ring; to sound".

> फोन बाजै है।
>
> *phon bājai hai.*
>
> The phone is ringing.

Past (it …): वो फोन बाज्यो, *vo phon bājyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाजणो** (*bājṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vādyate*, "is made to sound"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *bājaï*, "to sound" (CDIAL 11513).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
