Russian, chapter 349: Causes and influence.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
способствовать (sposóbstvovat) — to contribute to, to promote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a motive, then the Russian for origin.

You'll want to know: способствовать.
способствовать (sposóbstvovat) — "to contribute to, to promote".
Спорт способствует здоровому сну. Sport sposóbstvuyet zdoróvomu snu. Sport promotes healthy sleep.
Imperfective; present способствую, способствуешь. It takes the dative: способствовать (sposóbstvovat) успеху. Bookish.

The word, taken apart.
способствовать (sposóbstvovat) is built on способ "a way, a means" with the verb suffix -ствовать: to be a means to something. The further origin of способ is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
приводить (privodít) — to lead to, to result in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for origin, then the Russian for to contribute to.

You'll want to know: приводить.
приводить (privodít) — "to lead to, to result in".
Спешка часто приводит к ошибкам. Spéshka chásto privódit k oshíbkam. Haste often leads to mistakes.
Imperfective; present привожу, приводишь; perfective привести. Приводить к + dative — to lead to. It also means to bring someone along: привести друга.

The word, taken apart.
приводить (privodít) is built from при- "to, towards" and водить "to lead", from Proto-Slavic voditi "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). A cause leads to its result.

Lesson 3 of 5.
влиять (vliyát) — to influence, to affect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to contribute to, then the Russian for to lead to.

You'll want to know: влиять.
влиять (vliyát) — "to influence, to affect".
Погода влияет на моё настроение. Pogóda vliyáyet na moyó nastroyéniye. The weather affects my mood.
Imperfective; perfective повлиять. Влиять на + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
влиять (vliyát) was taken from Church Slavonic vŭlĭjati (EtymDB) "to pour in", built from в- "into" and the root of лить "to pour". To influence someone was to pour something into them.

Lesson 4 of 5.
влияние (vliyániye) — influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to lead to, then the Russian for to influence.

You'll want to know: влияние.
влияние (vliyániye) — "influence".
Родители имеют большое влияние на детей. Rodíteli iméyut bolshóye vliyániye na detéy. Parents have a great influence on their children.
Neuter. Под влиянием + genitive — under the influence of.

The word, taken apart.
влияние (vliyániye) is built on влиять (vliyát) with the noun suffix -ние; the verb comes from Church Slavonic vŭlĭjati "to pour in" (EtymDB), on the root of лить "to pour".

Lesson 5 of 5.
воздействие (vozdéystviye) — an impact, an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to influence, then the Russian for influence.

You'll want to know: воздействие.
воздействие (vozdéystviye) — "an impact, an effect".
Это лекарство оказывает сильное воздействие. Éto lekárstvo okázyvayet sílnoye vozdéystviye. This medicine has a strong effect.
Neuter. Оказывать воздействие (vozdéystviye) на + accusative — to have an effect on. Bookish.

The word, taken apart.
воздействие (vozdéystviye) is built from воз- and действие "action", from действовать "to act"; the root is usually traced to the old verb деять "to do".
