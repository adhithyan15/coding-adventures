## You'll want to know: ઉઠાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C341-DALIL341-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઠાવવું** — *uṭhāvvũ* — "to raise a point; to raise a question".

> મીરાએ બેઠકમાં પગારનો મુદ્દો ઉઠાવ્યો.
>
> *Mīrāe beṭhakmā̃ pagārno muddo uṭhāvyo.*
>
> At the meeting Mira raised the question of pay.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે એક પ્રશ્ન ઉઠાવ્યો, *teṇe ek praśna uṭhāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઠાવવું** (*uṭhāvvũ*) is inherited from a form CDIAL reconstructs as *ut-sthāpayati*, "raises"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 1903).
