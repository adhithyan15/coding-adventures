## You'll want to know: खळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C240-HARV240-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खळो** — *khaḷo* — "a threshing floor".

> खळो खेत कनै है।
>
> *khaḷo khet kanai hai.*
>
> The threshing floor is near the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खळो** (*khaḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *khala*, "a threshing floor"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *khaḷũ*, "a threshing floor". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 3834).
