Hindi, chapter 206: Advice, An opinion, A thought, A lie, A promise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सलाह (salāh) — advice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a message, then the Hindi for a conversation.

You'll want to know: सलाह.
सलाह — salāh — "advice".

The word, taken apart.
सलाह (salāh) is a loan from Arabic ṣalāḥ, "soundness, what is right", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṣ-l-ḥ (Lane). Hindi uses it for advice.

Lesson 2 of 5.
राय (rāy) — an opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a conversation, then the Hindi for advice.

You'll want to know: राय.
राय — rāy — "an opinion".

The word, taken apart.
राय (rāy) is a loan from Arabic raʾy, "opinion, view", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is r-ʾ-y (Lane). It is a different word from the older Hindi rāy, "king", from Sanskrit rājan (CDIAL 10679).

Lesson 4 of 5.
झूठ (jhūṭh) — a lie.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an opinion, then the Hindi for a thought.

You'll want to know: झूठ.
झूठ — jhūṭh — "a lie".

The word, taken apart.
झूठ (jhūṭh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jhūṭṭha, "false", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5407).
