## You'll want to know: asking for a ticket
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C271-TICKET-ASK-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-05, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-EKTA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-LEX-C198-JINIS198-04, BN-LEX-C59-FOOD59-05, BN-LEX-C216-JAYGA216-02] -->

> **একটা টিকিট দেবেন?**
>
> *ekṭā ṭikiṭ deben?* — "Could I have a ticket, please?"

Bengali often asks politely with the respectful future, "will you give?", where English says "could I have". **দেবেন** is **দেওয়া** in that form. To say where to, put the place in the **-র** form: **গ্রামের একটা টিকিট**, "a ticket to the village". To ask for a seat: **জানালার পাশে একটা আসন পাব?** (*jānālār pāshe ekṭā āsôn pābô?*), "can I get a seat by the window?".

## You'll want to know: the fare and the time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C271-FARE-TIME-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C147-PORA147-03, BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-04, BN-LEX-C53-VERBS53-02, BN-LEX-C198-JINIS198-05] -->

> **ভাড়া কত? ট্রেন কখন ছাড়বে?**
>
> *bhāṛā kôtô? ṭren kôkhôn chhāṛbe?* — "What's the fare? When does the train leave?"

**কত**, "how much", asks the fare. **কখন**, "when?", asks the time. **ছাড়বে**, "will leave", is **ছাড়া**, "to leave, to let go", in the future.
