## You'll want to know: おうぼする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C457-JOBHUNT457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おうぼする** — *ōbo suru* — "to apply (for a job or a prize)".

> **かれは みっつの かいしゃに おうぼしました。** — *kare wa mittsu no kaisha ni ōbo shimashita* — "He applied to three companies."

Polite **おうぼします**, *ōboshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが おうぼしました。**, *gakusei ga ōbo shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おうぼする** (*ōbo suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *ōbo* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ō*, "answer" (Middle Chinese *'ingH*) and *bo*, "recruit" (*muH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
