## You'll want to know: うかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C515-EXAM515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うかる** — *ukaru* — "to pass (an exam)".

> **もし しけんに うかったら、みんなで いわいます。** — *moshi shiken ni ukattara, minna de iwaimasu* — "If I pass the exam, we will all celebrate."

Polite **うかります**, *ukarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あねが しけんに うかりました。**, *ane ga shiken ni ukarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うかる** (*ukaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
