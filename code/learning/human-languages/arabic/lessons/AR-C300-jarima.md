## You'll want to know: جريمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C300-JARIMA300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جريمة** — *jarīma* — "a crime".

> وقعت جريمة في الحي أمس.
>
> *waqaʿat jarīma fī al-ḥayy ams.*
>
> A crime took place in the neighbourhood yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جريمة** (*jarīma*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-m* (Lane), first "to cut off (dates from the palm)", then "to commit a crime"; *jurm* is a wrong done.
