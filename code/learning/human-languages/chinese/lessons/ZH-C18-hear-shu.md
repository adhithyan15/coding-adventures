# Hear *shū* — book

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-KAN-01, ZH-LEX-KANJIAN-01, ZH-LEX-HAOKAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **look**, **see**, and **good-looking** in Mandarin.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-SHU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *shū* — **book**

Hold one level first tone. Do not add *kàn* yet; this step is only the object,
**book**. Its four-stroke written form waits until the next lesson.
