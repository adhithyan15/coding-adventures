## You'll want to know: ফ্লাইট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C395-BIMAN395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ফ্লাইট** — *phlāiṭ* — "a flight".

> আমাদের ফ্লাইট রাত দশটায় ছাড়বে।
>
> *āmāder phlāiṭ rāt dôshṭāy chhāṛbe.*
>
> Our flight leaves at ten at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফ্লাইট** (*phlāiṭ*) is borrowed from English *flight*, with ফ for f and the hard ট for t.
