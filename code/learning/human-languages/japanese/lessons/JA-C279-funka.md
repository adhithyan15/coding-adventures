## You'll want to know: ふんか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C279-KAZAN279-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふんか** — *funka* — "an eruption".

> **その かざんが ふんかしました。** — *sono kazan ga funka shimashita* — "That volcano erupted."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふんか** (*funka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fun*, "erupt" (Middle Chinese *phwonH*) and *ka*, "fire" (*xwaX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
