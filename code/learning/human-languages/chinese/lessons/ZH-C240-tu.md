## You'll want to know: tù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C240-SICK240-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tù* — "to throw up; to vomit".

> *wǒ yùnchuán yǒudiǎnr xiǎng tù* — I'm seasick and feel a bit like throwing up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tù* is inherited from Middle Chinese *thuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to vomit'; the dictionary also lists the character with a rising tone for spitting out.
