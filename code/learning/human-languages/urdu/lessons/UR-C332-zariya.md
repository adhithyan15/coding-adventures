## You'll want to know: ذریعہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C332-NUQSAN332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ذریعہ** — *zariyā* — "a means; a way (of doing)".

> پیسہ کمانے کا یہ اچھا ذریعہ ہے۔
>
> *paisā kamāne kā yih achchhā zariyā hai.*
>
> This is a good way of earning money.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذریعہ** (*zariyā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḏarīʿa*, "a means".
