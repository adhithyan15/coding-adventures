## You'll want to know: il podio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C512-TORNEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il podio** — "podium".

> Sono salita sul podio per la prima volta.
>
> *I stood on the podium for the first time.*

*Salire sul podio* — to finish in the top three.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*podio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *podium* "a raised platform" (EtymDB), from Greek *pódion* "a little foot, a base".
