## You'll want to know: shǒushù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C241-WARD241-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒushù* — "surgical operation".

> *tā zuòle yí cì shǒushù* — He had an operation.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒushù* is built from 手 'hand' and *shù* 'skill', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuwX* and *zywit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'skill'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
