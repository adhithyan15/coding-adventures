## You'll want to know: nachhaltig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C363-UMWELT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**nachhaltig** — "sustainable; lasting".

> Wir kaufen nur noch nachhaltige Produkte.
>
> *We only buy sustainable products now.*

*Die Nachhaltigkeit* — sustainability. *Ein nachhaltiger Eindruck* — a lasting impression.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nachhaltig* is built on *Nachhalt* "something held back, a reserve", from *nach* and *halten* "to hold", Proto-Germanic *haldaną* (EtymDB). It is usually said to have been first a word of forestry, for felling no more trees than grow back.
