Persian, chapter 85: Which?, Where?, Beside, Middle, Under.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کدام (kodâm) — which?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for none, then the Persian for oneself.
