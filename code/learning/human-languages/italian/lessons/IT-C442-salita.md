## You'll want to know: la salita
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C442-SALITA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la salita** — "the climb, the uphill slope".

> La salita fino al castello è dura.
>
> *The climb up to the castle is hard.*

*In salita* — uphill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*salita* is built on *salire* 'to go up', which is inherited from Latin *salīre* 'to jump' (EtymDB).
