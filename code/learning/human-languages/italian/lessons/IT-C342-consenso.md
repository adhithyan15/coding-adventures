# il consenso — the consent; the agreement (of many)

## You'll want to know: il consenso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C342-COMPROMESSO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il consenso** — "the consent; the agreement (of many)".

> Non puoi usare la mia foto senza il mio consenso.
>
> *You cannot use my photo without my consent.*

*Dare il proprio consenso* — to give your consent. It can also be broad agreement in a group.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consenso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnsēnsus* "agreement, accord" (Lewis & Short), from *cōnsentīre* "to agree", built from *con-* "together" and *sentīre* "to feel".
