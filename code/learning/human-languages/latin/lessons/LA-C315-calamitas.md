## You'll want to know: calamitās, calamitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C315-NAUFRAGIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**calamitās, calamitātis** — "a disaster, a misfortune".

> Post illam calamitātem multī cīvēs pauperēs erant.
>
> *After that disaster many citizens were poor.*

Feminine, 3rd declension. First harm to crops, then any disaster. English *calamity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**calamitās** first meant "loss, damage", especially to crops (Lewis & Short); its origin is not settled. English *calamity* comes from it through French.
