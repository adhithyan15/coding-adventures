## Script — 文, then the walking piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 这

**Two pieces, and the one underneath goes last.** The walking piece wraps the bottom-left, but it is written after what it carries.

**The top-right piece, 文, four strokes**, as in 中文: a dot, a horizontal, a left-falling stroke and a right-falling stroke crossing it.

**The walking piece, three strokes.** A dot. A stroke that runs across, folds down and folds again, ending in a short fall. Then a long flat sweep along the bottom, under 文, out to the right.

4 + 3 = seven.
