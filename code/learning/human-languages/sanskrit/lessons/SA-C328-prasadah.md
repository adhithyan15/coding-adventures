## You'll want to know: प्रसादः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C328-VRATA328-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसादः** — *prasādaḥ* — "a blessed offering of food; grace".

> उत्सवस्य अनन्तरं सर्वे प्रसादं खादन्ति।
>
> *utsavasya anantaraṃ sarve prasādaṃ khādanti.*
>
> After the festival everyone eats the blessed food.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसादः** (*prasādaḥ*) is built from *pra* and the root √sad, "to sit, settle"; MW gives *prasāda* as clearness, favour, grace, and also the food presented to a deity and then shared (MW).
