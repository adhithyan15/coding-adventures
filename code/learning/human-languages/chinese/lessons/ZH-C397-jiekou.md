## You'll want to know: jièkǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C397-SURELY397-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jièkǒu* — "excuse (a poor reason)".

> *yàoshi nǐ bù xiǎng qù jiù bié zhǎo jièkǒu* — If you don't want to go, don't make excuses.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jièkǒu* is built from *jiè* 'to borrow' and 口 'a mouth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsjaeH* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to borrow': words borrowed to cover the real reason.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
