## You'll want to know: रञ्जकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C506-VAYATI506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रञ्जकः** — *rañjakaḥ* — "a dyer".

> रञ्जकः वस्त्रं नीलं करोति।
>
> *rañjakaḥ vastraṃ nīlaṃ karoti.*
>
> The dyer makes the cloth blue.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रञ्जकः** (*rañjakaḥ*) comes from the root √rañj, "to be coloured, to redden", with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *rañjaka* as a colourist, a dyer, a painter (MW).
