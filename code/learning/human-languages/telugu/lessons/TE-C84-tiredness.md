## You'll want to know: అలసట
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అలసట** (*alasaṭa*) — "tiredness".

**నాకు అలసటగా ఉంది** — "I feel tired", literally "to me it is tired-ly".

The ending **-గా** turns the noun into a manner, and **ఉంది** says "it is". This frame,
*నాకు ... గా ఉంది*, is how Telugu says many of the things English says with *I am*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
