Hindi, chapter 186: Anger, Love, Shame, Hope, A memory.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ग़ुस्सा (ġussā) — anger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a storm, then the Hindi for a leaf.

You'll want to know: ग़ुस्सा.
ग़ुस्सा — ġussā — "anger".

The word, taken apart.
ग़ुस्सा (ġussā) is a loan from Arabic ghuṣṣa, "a choking; grief", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is gh-ṣ-ṣ (Lane). In Hindi it came to mean "anger".

Lesson 2 of 5.
प्यार (pyār) — love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a leaf, then the Hindi for anger.

You'll want to know: प्यार.
प्यार — pyār — "love".

The word, taken apart.
प्यार (pyār) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from priyakāra, "doing a kindness" (CDIAL 8975). It is built from priya, "dear", and kāra, "doing".
