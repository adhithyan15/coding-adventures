Russian, chapter 315: Treats and feasts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
угощение (ugoshchéniye) — treats, refreshments.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a party popper, then the Russian for a balloon.

You'll want to know: угощение.
угощение (ugoshchéniye) — "treats, refreshments".
Угощение было очень вкусным. Ugoshchéniye býlo óchen vkúsnym. The treats were very tasty.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
угощение (ugoshchéniye) is built on угощать (ugoshchát) "to treat", from гость "a guest", inherited from Proto-Slavic gostĭ, PIE gʰóstis (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
угощать (ugoshchát) — to treat (to food or drink).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a balloon, then the Russian for treats.

You'll want to know: угощать.
угощать (ugoshchát) — "to treat (to food or drink)".
Хозяйка угощала гостей пирогами. Khozyáyka ugoshchála gostéy pirogámi. The hostess treated the guests to pies.
Imperfective; perfective угостить (ugostít), угощу. The guest is in the accusative, the food in the instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
угощать (ugoshchát) is built on у- and гость "a guest", inherited from Proto-Slavic gostĭ, from PIE gʰóstis (EtymDB): to treat as a guest.

Lesson 4 of 5.
чокаться (chókatsya) — to clink glasses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to treat, then the Russian for a feast.

You'll want to know: чокаться.
чокаться (chókatsya) — "to clink glasses".
Гости чокались и смеялись. Gósti chókalis i smeyális. The guests clinked glasses and laughed.
Imperfective; perfective чокнуться (chóknutsya). Careful: in slang чокнутый means "crazy".

The word, taken apart.
чокаться (chókatsya) is built on an imitative syllable чок for the ring of glasses; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
шампанское (shampánskoye) — champagne, sparkling wine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a feast, then the Russian for to clink glasses.

You'll want to know: шампанское.
шампанское (shampánskoye) — "champagne, sparkling wine".
В полночь мы открыли шампанское. V pólnoch my otkrýli shampánskoye. At midnight we opened the champagne.
An adjective serving as a noun, neuter: бокал шампанского.

The word, taken apart.
шампанское (shampánskoye) is built on the name of the French wine region where it is made, with the Russian adjective ending -ское; EtymDB lists no parent.
