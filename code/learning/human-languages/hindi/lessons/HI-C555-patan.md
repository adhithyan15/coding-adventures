## You'll want to know: पतन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C555-GIRAVAT555-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पतन** — *patan* — "a downfall; a decline".

> बुरी आदतें इंसान के पतन का कारण बनती हैं।
>
> *Burī ādteṁ insān ke patan kā kāraṇ bantī haiṁ.*
>
> Bad habits bring about a person's downfall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पतन** (*patan*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *patana*, "fall, decline, ruin" (Monier-Williams).
