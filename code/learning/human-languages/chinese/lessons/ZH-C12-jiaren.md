# 家人 — the people of the home

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-JIAREN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-JIA-01, ZH-SCRIPT-REN-01] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Say **family members**, then write 家 and 人 separately.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-JIAREN-01, ZH-LEX-JIA-01, ZH-LEX-REN-01] -->

> **家人** — *jiārén* — **family members; family**

Read from the characters: home + person. The word is plural by meaning here,
but Mandarin adds no plural ending to 人.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-JIAREN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-JIA-01, ZH-SCRIPT-REN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **家人**, cover it, wait five seconds, and write it from **family**.
