## You'll want to know: коллекционер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C503-COLLECTING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**коллекционер** (*kollektsionér*) — "a collector".

> Известный коллекционер купил эту картину.
>
> *Izvéstnyy kollektsionér kupíl étu kartínu.*
>
> A well-known collector bought this painting.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*коллекционер* is built on *коллекция* 'collection', usually traced to Latin *collēctiō* 'a collecting together' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has no entry.
