## You'll want to know: भ्रान्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C414-DIGBHRAMA414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रान्तः** — *bhrāntaḥ* — "astray; wandering".

> भ्रान्तः पथिकः ग्रामं न प्राप्नोति।
>
> *bhrāntaḥ pathikaḥ grāmaṃ na prāpnoti.*
>
> The wayfarer who has gone astray does not reach the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रान्तः** (*bhrāntaḥ*) comes from the root √bhram, "to wander", which also gives *bhramati*, "wanders": it is the participle, "having wandered", and MW gives *bhrānta* as roaming about and as confused (MW).
