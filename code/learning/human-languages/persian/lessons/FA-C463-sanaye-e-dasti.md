## You'll want to know: صنایع دستی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C463-SOWGHATI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صنایع دستی** (*sanâye'-e dasti*) — "handicrafts, crafts".

> در بازار صنایع دستی خریدیم.
>
> *Dar bâzâr sanâye'-e dasti kharidim.*
>
> We bought handicrafts in the bazaar.

*sanâye'* is an Arabic broken plural of *san'at* 'craft, industry'; *dasti* 'by hand'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sanâye'-e dasti* is built from *sanâye'* 'crafts', the Arabic plural *ṣanāʾiʿ* that EtymDB lists, and native *dasti* 'by hand', from *dast* 'hand'.
