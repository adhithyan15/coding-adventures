## You'll want to know: o bailado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C521-GENEROS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o bailado** — "ballet (a dance piece)".

> Fomos ver um bailado no teatro.
>
> *We went to see a ballet at the theatre.*

Also *o ballet*. Built on *bailar* "to dance".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bailado* is built on *bailar* "to dance", which goes back to Late Latin *ballāre* "to dance" (Lewis & Short).
