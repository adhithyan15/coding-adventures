## You'll want to know: استقبل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C336-HADIYYA336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استقبل** — *istaqbala* — "to receive guests; to welcome".

> استقبل الأهل الضيوف عند الباب.
>
> *istaqbala al-ahl aḍ-ḍuyūf ʿinda al-bāb.*
>
> The family welcomed the guests at the door.

Present: يستقبل, *yastaqbil*.

A hotel reception desk is الاستقبال.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استقبل** (*istaqbala*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-l* (Lane), "to be in front of, to face". It is Form X, "to go to meet"; *mustaqbal*, the future, is what comes to meet us.
