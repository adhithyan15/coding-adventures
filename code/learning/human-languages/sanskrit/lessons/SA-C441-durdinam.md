## You'll want to know: दुर्दिनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C441-DURDINA441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्दिनम्** — *durdinam* — "a day of bad weather".

> दुर्दिने यात्रा कठिना भवति।
>
> *durdine yātrā kaṭhinā bhavati.*
>
> On a day of bad weather a journey is hard.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्दिनम्** (*durdinam*) is built from *dur*, "bad, ill", and *dina*, "a day"; MW gives the compound as a rainy or cloudy day, bad weather (MW).
