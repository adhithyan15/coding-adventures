## You'll want to know: سلیقہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C356-ISLAH356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سلیقہ** — *salīqā* — "a knack for doing things well; good taste".

> اسے کھانا پکانے کا سلیقہ ہے۔
>
> *use khānā pakāne kā salīqā hai.*
>
> She has a knack for cooking.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلیقہ** (*salīqā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *salīqa*, "a natural gift, a disposition".
