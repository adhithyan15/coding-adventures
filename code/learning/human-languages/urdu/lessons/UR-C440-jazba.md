## You'll want to know: جذبہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C440-FEEL440-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جذبہ** — *jazbā* — "an emotion; a strong feeling".

> ماں کا پیار ایک گہرا جذبہ ہے۔
>
> *māṅ kā pyār ek gahrā jazbā hai.*
>
> A mother's love is a deep emotion.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جذبہ** (*jazbā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jadhba*, "a pull, an attraction"; the sense "a strong feeling" is the one Urdu gives it.
