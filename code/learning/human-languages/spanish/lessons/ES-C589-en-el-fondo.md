## You'll want to know: en el fondo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C589-MATIZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**en el fondo** — "deep down, at heart".

> En el fondo, es una buena persona.
>
> *Deep down, he is a good person.*

It gives what is true underneath the surface.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*en el fondo* is built on *fondo* 'bottom', inherited from Latin *fundus* 'bottom, base' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
