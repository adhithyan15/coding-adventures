Kannada, chapter 162: To correct, To copy, To warn, To take part, To transfer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತಿದ್ದು (tiddu) — to correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to reserve, then the Kannada for to cancel.

You'll want to know: ತಿದ್ದು.
ತಿದ್ದು — tiddu — "to correct".

The word, taken apart.
ತಿದ್ದು (tiddu) is native Dravidian, one word with Telugu diddu (DEDR 3251). DEDR lists tirdu, tiddu, "to make straight, correct".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ನಕಲು ಮಾಡು (nakalu māḍu) — to copy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to cancel, then the Kannada for to correct.

You'll want to know: ನಕಲು ಮಾಡು.
ನಕಲು ಮಾಡು — nakalu māḍu — "to copy".

The word, taken apart.
ನಕಲು ಮಾಡು (nakalu māḍu) is nakalu, "a copy", from Arabic naql, "a copy, a transfer", which came through Hindustani, and native māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ಎಚ್ಚರಿಸು.
ಎಚ್ಚರಿಸು — eccarisu — "to warn".

The word, taken apart.
ಎಚ್ಚರಿಸು (eccarisu) is native Dravidian, of the root for "to rise, awake", and DEDR 851 lists eccaṟisu, "to awaken, warn", itself; the entry also has Telugu eccarincu.

The word, taken apart.
ಭಾಗವಹಿಸು (bhāgavahisu) is bhāga, "a portion, a share", from Sanskrit bhāga (CDIAL 9430), and vahisu, "to bear", from the Sanskrit verb vah, "to carry": "to carry a share".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ವರ್ಗಾಯಿಸು (vargāyisu) — to transfer (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to warn, then the Kannada for to take part.

You'll want to know: ವರ್ಗಾಯಿಸು.
ವರ್ಗಾಯಿಸು — vargāyisu — "to transfer (money)".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ವರ್ಗಾಯಿಸು (vargāyisu) is uncertain. It is usually linked to Sanskrit varga, "a class, a group" (CDIAL 11331), but no source settles how it came to mean "to transfer".
