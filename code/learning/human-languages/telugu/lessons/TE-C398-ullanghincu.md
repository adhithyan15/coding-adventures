# ఉల్లంఘించు (ullaṅghiñcu) — to violate; to break (a rule)

## You'll want to know: ఉల్లంఘించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C398-CATTAM398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉల్లంఘించు** — *ullaṅghiñcu* — "to violate; to break (a rule)".

> అతను కంపెనీ నియమాలను ఉల్లంఘించాడు.
>
> *atanu kampenī niyamālanu ullaṅghiñcāḍu.*
>
> He broke the company rules.

Past (I …): ఉల్లంఘించాను, *ullaṅghiñcānu*.

Formal: for rules, laws and agreements.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉల్లంఘించు** (*ullaṅghiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ullaṅghana*, "the act of leaping over" (CDIAL 2365), and so "transgression" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
