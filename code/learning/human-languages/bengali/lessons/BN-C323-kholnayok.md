# খলনায়ক (khôlnāyôk) — a villain

## You'll want to know: খলনায়ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C323-NAYOK323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**খলনায়ক** — *khôlnāyôk* — "a villain".

> শেষে পুলিশ খলনায়ককে গ্রেফতার করল।
>
> *sheshe pulish khôlnāyôkke grephtār kôrlô.*
>
> In the end the police arrested the villain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খলনায়ক** (*khôlnāyôk*) joins Sanskrit *khala*, 'a mischievous man, a rogue' (Monier-Williams), to **নায়ক**: the rogue who leads the other side of the story.
