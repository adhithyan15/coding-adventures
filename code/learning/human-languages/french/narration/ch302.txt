French, chapter 302: Au feu !.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'incendie — the fire, the blaze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the emergency exit, then the French for to evacuate.

You'll want to know: l'incendie.
l'incendie — "the fire, the blaze".
Un incendie a brûlé trois maisons. A fire burnt down three houses.
A fire out of control is un incendie; a fire in a fireplace is un feu.

The word, taken apart.
incendie is a learned borrowing from Latin incendium "a burning, a fire", from incendere "to set on fire" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'explosion — the explosion, the blast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: l'explosion.
l'explosion — "the explosion, the blast".
On a entendu l'explosion à deux kilomètres. People heard the blast two kilometres away.
Figurative: une explosion de joie — an outburst of joy.

The word, taken apart.
explosion is a learned borrowing from Latin explōsiō (EtymDB), from explōdere "to drive off by clapping", built on plaudere "to clap".
