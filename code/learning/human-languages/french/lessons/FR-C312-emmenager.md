## You'll want to know: emménager
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C312-DEMENAGEMENT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**emménager** — "to move in".

> Nous avons emménagé dans un nouvel appartement.
>
> *We moved into a new flat.*

The opposite of *déménager*, to move out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emménager* is built on *en-* and *ménage* "household", which goes back to Latin *mānsiō* "a dwelling" (Lewis & Short).
