Telugu, chapter 140: Safe, Lie, Grey, Golden, Spoiled.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సురక్షితం (surakṣitaṁ) — safe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for old (of people), then the Telugu for famous.

You'll want to know: సురక్షితం.
సురక్షితం — surakṣitaṁ — "safe".

The word, taken apart.
సురక్షితం (surakṣitaṁ) is Sanskrit surakṣita, "well protected", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 13499).

Lesson 3 of 5.
బూడిద (būḍida) — grey; ash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for safe, then the Telugu for a lie; untrue.

You'll want to know: బూడిద.
బూడిద — būḍida — "grey; ash".

The word, taken apart.
బూడిద (būḍida) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4316 lists būḍida, "ashes", with Tamil puḻuti, "dust". DEDR compares Sanskrit bhūti, "ashes", and notes that some forms in the entry are probably borrowed from it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
పాడైన (pāḍaina) — spoiled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for grey; ash, then the Telugu for golden.

You'll want to know: పాడైన.
పాడైన — pāḍaina — "spoiled".

The word, taken apart.
పాడైన (pāḍaina) is native Dravidian: pāḍu, "ruin", one word with Tamil pāḻ (DEDR 4110), and ayina, "which has become" (DEDR 333). DEDR compares Prakrit pāla, "worn out".
