## You'll want to know: a moldura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C505-PINTURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a moldura** — "(picture) frame".

> Comprei uma moldura para a fotografia dos avós.
>
> *I bought a frame for my grandparents' photograph.*

The frame of glasses is *a armação*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*moldura* was borrowed from Spanish *moldura* (EtymDB), built on *molde* "a mould", which goes back to Latin *modulus* "a small measure", from *modus*.
