## You'll want to know: alojarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C660-ALOJAMIENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**alojarse** — "to stay (at a hotel or a house), to lodge".

> Nos alojamos en un hotel del centro.
>
> *We are staying at a hotel in the centre.*

Without *se*, *alojar* is to put someone up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alojarse* comes from Occitan *alotjar* "to lodge" (EtymDB), which is built on a Germanic word for a shelter or hut.
