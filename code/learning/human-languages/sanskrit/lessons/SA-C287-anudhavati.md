## You'll want to know: अनुधावति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C287-ANUDHAVANA287-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुधावति** — *anudhāvati* — "runs after; chases".

> यामिकः चौरम् अनुधावति।
>
> *yāmikaḥ cauram anudhāvati.*
>
> The watchman runs after the thief.

Past (he …): सः चौरम् अनुधावितवान्, *saḥ cauram anudhāvitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुधावति** (*anudhāvati*) is built from *anu*, "after", and the root √dhāv, "to run", which also gives *dhāvati*, "runs"; MW gives *anu-dhāv* as to run after or pursue (MW).
