## You'll want to know: ढगफुटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C440-DUSHKAL440-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ढगफुटी** — *ḍhagphuṭī* — "a cloudburst".

> डोंगरावर ढगफुटी झाली.
>
> *ḍoṅgarāvar ḍhagphuṭī jhālī.*
>
> There was a cloudburst on the mountain.

Feminine.

ढग, a cloud, and फुटणे, to burst.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढगफुटी** (*ḍhagphuṭī*) is built from **ढग**, "a cloud", and the stem of **फुटणे**, "to burst" (CDIAL 13845).
