# જ — the consonant ja

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C01-PRACTICE-01] -->

Without looking back, say the leave-taking word aavjo once. Today you meet the letter at its centre.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-JA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **જ** visible. It is the consonant ja, pronounced *ja*. Begin near the upper left and circle the small upper-left loop. Continue diagonally through the crossing body, circle the lower-right loop, and sweep into the long upper-right exit without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-JA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **જ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice two loops joined through a crossing; no pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-JA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **જ** once. Compare only the landmark named above and repair it once if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-JA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W03-ja-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-JA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write the consonant ja from memory.","answer":"જ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"જ — the taught form is intact.","incorrect":"Look once at જ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *ja* once. Uncover **જ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-JA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the form, its position or main landmark, and its pen-lift count.
