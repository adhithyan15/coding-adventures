Portuguese, chapter 32: Back, Hair, Belly, Face, Blood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
as costas — the back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a shoulder, then the Portuguese for a knee.

You'll want to know: as costas.
as costas — "the back". Plural in form, like English "trousers".

The word, taken apart.
costas from Latin costa, "rib, side" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. The plural came to name the whole back.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the back, then the Portuguese for hair.

You'll want to know: a barriga.
a barriga — "the belly".

The word, taken apart.
barriga is probably built from Old Portuguese barrica, "cask" (EtymDB 2.1): a belly shaped like a little barrel. That link is the usual account, not a certain one.
