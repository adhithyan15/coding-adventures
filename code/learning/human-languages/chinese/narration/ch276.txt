Mandarin Chinese, chapter 276: A New Job.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
面试 (miànshì) — to have an interview (for a job); interview.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to accept, then the Mandarin for as you like.

You'll want to know: 面试.
面试 — miànshì — "to have an interview (for a job); interview".
上个月我老公去面试了 shàng ge yuè wǒ lǎogōng qù miànshì le — My husband went for a job interview last month.

The word, taken apart.
面试 (miànshì) is built from 面 'the face' and 试 'a test', both inherited from Middle Chinese, mjienH and syiH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to face' and the second 'to use'.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
cízhí — to resign; to quit a job.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for as you like, then the Mandarin for to have a job interview.

You'll want to know: cízhí.
cízhí — "to resign; to quit a job".
tā shàng ge yuè cízhí le — She quit her job last month.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
cízhí is built from cí and zhí 'a post', both inherited from Middle Chinese, zi and tsyik (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'words' and 'a plea'. The sense 'to take leave' is not given there, so how it came is not settled.
