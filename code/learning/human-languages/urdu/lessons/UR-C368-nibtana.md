## You'll want to know: نبٹانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C368-HAL368-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نبٹانا** — *nibṭānā* — "to deal with; to settle (a matter)".

> اگر آپ معاملہ نبٹائیں گے تو سب خوش ہوں گے۔
>
> *agar āp muāmalā nibṭāeṅ ge to sab khush hoṅ ge.*
>
> If you deal with the matter, everyone will be happy.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کام نبٹایا, *us ne kām nibṭāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نبٹانا** (*nibṭānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *nibṭānā*, "finishes", beside *nibaṭnā*, "to be settled", under Sanskrit *nírvartate* (CDIAL 7395). To bring a matter to its end; Urdu also says *nimṭānā*.
