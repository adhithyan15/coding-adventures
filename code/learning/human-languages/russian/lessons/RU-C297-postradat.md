## You'll want to know: пострадать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C297-TRAVMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**пострадать** (*postradát*) — "to be hurt, to suffer".

> В аварии никто не пострадал.
>
> *V avárii niktó ne postradál.*
>
> Nobody was hurt in the accident.

Perfective. It takes *от* + genitive: *пострадать от пожара*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пострадать* is built on *по-* and *страдать* "to suffer", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists *страдать* "to suffer" with no parent.
