## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-ACTIONS-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C89-GEI-01, ZH-LEX-C89-ZUO-01, ZH-LEX-C89-DAI-01, ZH-LEX-C89-FANG-01, ZH-LEX-C89-ZHAN-01, ZH-LEX-C87-YAOSHI-01, ZH-LEX-C86-SHANGBIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C85-LIBIAN-01] -->

> **A:** 你做什么
>
> *nǐ zuò shénme* — What are you doing?
>
> **B:** *wǒ zhǔnbèi chūmén* — I'm getting ready to go out.
>
> **A:** *nǐ dài yàoshi le ma* — Have you got your key?
>
> **B:** *dài le. shǒujī ne* — Yes. Where's my phone?
>
> **A:** *zài zhuōzi shàngbian. gěi nǐ* — On the table. Here you are.
>
> **B:** *xièxie. fàng zài bāo lǐbian* — Thanks. In the bag it goes.
>
> **A:** *qǐng zhàn zài ménkǒu, wǒ kànkan* — Stand in the doorway, let me look at you.

Five actions in one doorway: do, bring, give, put and stand. 给你, here you are, is 给 handing something over as it is said.

## What you've built — five actions
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-ACTIONS-01, ZH-SCRIPT-GEI-01, ZH-LEX-C89-GEI-01, ZH-LEX-C89-FANG-01, ZH-LEX-C89-ZHAN-01] -->

Five verbs, and the three that take a place take it with 在:

- **给我** *gěi wǒ* — give it to me
- **你做什么** *nǐ zuò shénme* — what are you doing?
- *fàng zài zhèr* — put it here
- *nǐ dài shǒujī le ma* — have you brought your phone?
- *qǐng zhàn zài zhèr* — please stand here

**Listen.** *Gěi wǒ nǐ de shǒujī.*

**Speak.** Hand it over with 给你, then say where you put it.
