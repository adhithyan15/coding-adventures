## You'll want to know: दम्भः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C361-SHRADDHA361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दम्भः** — *dambhaḥ* — "hypocrisy; deceit".

> दम्भः सत्यस्य शत्रुः अस्ति।
>
> *dambhaḥ satyasya śatruḥ asti.*
>
> Hypocrisy is the enemy of truth.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दम्भः** (*dambhaḥ*) comes from the root √dambh, "to deceive, to injure"; MW gives *dambha* "deceit, fraud, feigning, hypocrisy" (MW).
