## You'll want to know: der Fasching
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C310-KARNEVAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Fasching** — "the Shrovetide carnival".

> Im Fasching gibt es in der Schule eine Party.
>
> *At carnival time there is a party at school.*

The word in southern Germany and Austria; it is the season before Lent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fasching* is usually traced to Middle High German *vaschanc* "the serving of drink before the fast"; EtymDB has the word but lists no parent.
