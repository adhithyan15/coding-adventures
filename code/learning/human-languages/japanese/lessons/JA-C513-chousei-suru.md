## You'll want to know: ちょうせいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C513-PLAN513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうせいする** — *chōsei suru* — "to adjust; to coordinate".

> **もし じかんが かわったら、よていを ちょうせいします。** — *moshi jikan ga kawattara, yotei o chōseishimasu* — "If the time changes, I will adjust my schedule."

Polite **ちょうせいします**, *chōseishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんぱいが よていを ちょうせいしました。**, *senpai ga yotei o chōseishimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうせいする** (*chōsei suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *chōsei* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "harmonize" (Middle Chinese *dew*) and *sei*, "organize" (*tsyengX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
