## You'll want to know: transpirer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C464-CANICULE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**transpirer** — "to sweat, to perspire".

> Il transpire beaucoup quand il fait chaud.
>
> *He sweats a lot when it is hot.*

A little more polite than *suer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transpirer* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *transpīrāre* 'to breathe through' (EtymDB), from *trāns* and *spīrāre* 'to breathe'.
