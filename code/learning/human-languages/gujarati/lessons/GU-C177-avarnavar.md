# અવારનવાર (avārnavār) — now and then

## You'll want to know: અવારનવાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C177-OFTEN177-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અવારનવાર** — *avārnavār* — "now and then".

> **અમે અવારનવાર મળીશું.** — *ame avārnavār maḷīśũ* — "We will meet now and then."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવારનવાર** (*avārnavār*) is a rhyming compound built on *vār*, "a turn, a time", from Sanskrit *vāra* (CDIAL 11547); how its first part was formed is not settled.
