## You'll want to know: ਧੋਬੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C333-BARBER333-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੋਬੀ** — *dhobī* — "a washerman".

> ਧੋਬੀ ਹਰ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਸਾਡੇ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਧੋਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *dhobī har hafte sāḍe kappṛe dhondā hai.*
>
> The washerman washes our clothes every week.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੋਬੀ** (*dhobī*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*dhauvati*, "washes", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi *dhobī* under it among the forms that spread from Hindi *dhobī*, "washerman" (CDIAL 6886).
