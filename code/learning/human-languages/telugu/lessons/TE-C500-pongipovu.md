# పొంగిపోవు (poṅgipōvu) — to be overjoyed; to swell with pride

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to enjoy, then the Telugu for to be delighted.

## You'll want to know: పొంగిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C500-ANANDAM500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పొంగిపోవు** — *poṅgipōvu* — "to be overjoyed; to swell with pride".

> బహుమతి గెలిచినప్పుడు ఆమె పొంగిపోయింది.
>
> *bahumati gelicinappuḍu āme poṅgipōyindi.*
>
> When she won the prize, she was overjoyed.

Past (I …): పొంగిపోయాను, *poṅgipōyānu*.

పొంగు is to boil over, like milk: joy that overflows.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పొంగిపోవు** (*poṅgipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4469 lists Telugu *poṅgu*, "to bubble up, to boil; to rejoice, to be elated", with Tamil *poṅku*; *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572) ends it.
