# ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ — Aman bought a book

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210, PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-05, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *Aman did the work*. Then *to buy* and *a book*.

## Grammar Lens: ਨੇ beyond the work
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-LEX-C57-VERBS57-05, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-02, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219] -->

The marker works with any verb that is done to something. The past still
agrees with the thing: **ਕਿਤਾਬ** is feminine, so **ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ**.

> **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ।** — *Aman ne kitāb kharīdī.* — Aman bought a book.

> **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।** — *Aman ne chāh pītī.* — Aman drank tea.

> **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਸੇਬ ਖਾਧਾ।** — *Aman ne seb khādhā.* — Aman ate an apple.

Going and coming are not done to anything, so they take no **ਨੇ**: **ਅਮਨ ਘਰ
ਗਿਆ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NE-NAME-220, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-LEX-ROTI] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *Aman ate a roti*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *Aman went to school*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਿਆ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ।**] Point to **ਨੇ**, then to the thing the ending agrees with.
- [YOU WRITE: copy **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ।** once]
