## You'll want to know: منحوس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C535-SHAGUN535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منحوس** — *manhūs* — "unlucky; ill-omened".

> اگر یہ گھر منحوس ہوتا تو ہم یہاں نہ رہتے۔
>
> *agar yih ghar manhūs hotā to ham yahāṅ na rahte.*
>
> If this house were unlucky, we wouldn't live here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منحوس** (*manhūs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *manḥūs*, "ill-starred, unlucky".
