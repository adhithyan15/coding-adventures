# تعزیت کرنا (tāziyat karnā) — to offer condolences

## You'll want to know: تعزیت کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C493-MOURNING493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تعزیت کرنا** — *tāziyat karnā* — "to offer condolences".

> پڑوسیوں نے آ کر ہم سے تعزیت کی۔
>
> *paṛosiyoṅ ne ā kar ham se tāziyat kī.*
>
> The neighbours came to offer us their condolences.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے ہم سے تعزیت کی, *us ne ham se tāziyat kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعزیت کرنا** (*tāziyat karnā*) is built from **تعزیت**, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taʿziya*, "consolation", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
