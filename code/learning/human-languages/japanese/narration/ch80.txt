5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
こうさてん (kousaten) — a crossroads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for stairs, then the Japanese for a corridor.

You'll want to know: こうさてん.
こうさてん — kousaten — "a crossroads".

The word, taken apart.
こうさてん (kousaten) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kō, "mix, cross" (Middle Chinese kaew), sa, "differ" (tsrhae) and ten, "dot, point" (temX) (Unihan, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict also gives an older spelling with a character for "fork" in place of the second.

ちかみち — chikamichi — "a shortcut".

The word, taken apart.
ちかみち (chikamichi) is a native compound, inherited rather than borrowed: chika, "near", from chikai, and michi, "road", both kun'yomi of its kanji (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
