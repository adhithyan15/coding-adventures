## You'll want to know: ઘેટું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C234-MILK234-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘેટું** — *gheṭũ* — "sheep".

> **ઘેટું ટેકરીથી નીચે આવ્યું.** — *gheṭũ ṭekrīthī nīce āvyũ* — "The sheep came down from the hill."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ઘેટું** (*gheṭũ*) is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it among its words for sheep.
