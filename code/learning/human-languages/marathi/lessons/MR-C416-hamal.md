## You'll want to know: हमाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C416-HAMAL416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हमाल** — *hamāl* — "a porter".

> स्टेशनवर हमालाने आमचं सामान उचललं.
>
> *sṭeśanvar hamālāne āmcaṁ sāmān ucallaṁ.*
>
> At the station a porter lifted our luggage.

Masculine.

At stations they wear a red shirt and a brass badge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हमाल** (*hamāl*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *ḥammāl*, "a carrier of loads".
