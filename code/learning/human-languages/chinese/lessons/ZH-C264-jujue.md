## You'll want to know: jùjué
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C264-REFUSE264-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jùjué* — "to refuse; to turn down".

> *tā jùjuéle wǒ* — He turned me down.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jùjué* is built from *jù* 'to resist' and *jué* 'to cut off', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjoX* and *dzjwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to resist' and the second 'to break off'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
