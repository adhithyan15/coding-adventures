Italian, chapter 240: To push, To pull, To waste, To overtake, To perform.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
spingere — to push.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to give back, then the Italian for to recognise.

You'll want to know: spingere.
spingere — "to push".

The word, taken apart.
spingere from Vulgar Latin expingere, from ex- and pangere, "to fix, to drive in" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.
superare — to overtake, to pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to pull, then the Italian for to waste.

You'll want to know: superare.
superare — "to overtake, to pass".

The word, taken apart.
superare from Latin superare, "to go over, to rise above" — a learned borrowing. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for superare; it derives Spanish and Portuguese superar, borrowed the same way, from superare.

Lesson 5 of 5.
svolgere — to perform, to carry out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to waste, then the Italian for to overtake.

You'll want to know: svolgere.
svolgere — "to perform, to carry out".

The word, taken apart.
svolgere is s-, "out", plus volgere, "to turn": to unroll, to carry out. volgere from Latin volvere, "to roll". (EtymDB 2.1).
