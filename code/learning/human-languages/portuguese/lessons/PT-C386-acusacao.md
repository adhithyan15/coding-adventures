## You'll want to know: a acusação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C386-ACUSACAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a acusação** — "the accusation, the charge; the prosecution".

> Ele negou a acusação.
>
> *He denied the accusation.*

In court, *a acusação* is also the prosecution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acusação* is a learned borrowing from Latin *accūsātiō* "accusation, indictment" (EtymDB), from *accūsāre*, on *causa* "lawsuit" (Lewis & Short).
