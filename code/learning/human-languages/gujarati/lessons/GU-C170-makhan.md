## You'll want to know: માખણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C170-SWEET170-03]; assesses=[] -->

**માખણ** — *mākhaṇ* — "butter".

> **બાળકને માખણ ગમે છે.** — *bāḷakne mākhaṇ game che* — "The child likes butter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માખણ** (*mākhaṇ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *mrakṣaṇa*, "rubbing in; ointment, oil", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "butter, ointment" (CDIAL 10378).
