## You'll want to know: le lavabo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C428-FUITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le lavabo** — "the washbasin".

> Il se lave les mains au lavabo.
>
> *He washes his hands at the basin.*

*Le lavabo* is the basin in the bathroom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lavabo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lavābō* 'I will wash', from *lavāre* 'to wash' (EtymDB), first a word in a church prayer.
