## You'll want to know: まいつき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C169-OFTEN169-04]; assesses=[] -->

**まいつき** — *maitsuki* — "every month".

> **らいねんは まいつき ははに あいます。** — *rainen wa maitsuki haha ni aimasu* — "Next year I will see my mother every month."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まいつき** (*maitsuki*) is a mixed word, built from Sino-Japanese *mai*, "every", borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *mwojX*), and native *tsuki*, "moon, month" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
