## You'll want to know: गोरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C482-KAD482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गोरा** — *gorā* — "fair-skinned".

> मीरा का भाई गोरा है और मीरा साँवली है।
>
> *Mīrā kā bhāī gorā hai aur Mīrā sā̃vlī hai.*
>
> Mira's brother is fair and Mira is dusky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोरा** (*gorā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *gorā*, "fair", under Sanskrit *gaurá*, "white, yellowish, pale red" (CDIAL 4345), with the diphthong worn down to *o*. It describes a complexion, as *sā̃vlā* does.
