## You'll want to know: خسارہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C419-MUNAFA419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خسارہ** — *khasārā* — "a business loss".

> بارش کی وجہ سے کسانوں کو خسارہ ہوا۔
>
> *bārish kī vajah se kisānoṅ ko khasārā huā.*
>
> The farmers made a loss because of the rain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خسارہ** (*khasārā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khasāra*, "a loss".
