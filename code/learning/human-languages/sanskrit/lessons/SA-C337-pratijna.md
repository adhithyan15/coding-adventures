## You'll want to know: प्रतिज्ञा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C337-VANAVASA337-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिज्ञा** — *pratijñā* — "a promise; a vow".

> सः पितुः प्रतिज्ञां पालयति।
>
> *saḥ pituḥ pratijñāṃ pālayati.*
>
> He keeps his father's promise.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिज्ञा** (*pratijñā*) is built from *prati* and the root √jñā, "to know"; MW gives *pratijñā* as admission, agreement, promise, vow (MW).
