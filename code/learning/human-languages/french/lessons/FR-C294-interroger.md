## You'll want to know: interroger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C294-ARRESTATION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**interroger** — "to question, to interrogate".

> Les policiers l'ont interrogé pendant trois heures.
>
> *The police questioned him for three hours.*

At school, *interroger un élève* — to test a pupil orally.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*interroger* is a learned borrowing from Latin *interrogāre* "to ask, to question" (EtymDB), built on *rogāre* "to ask".
