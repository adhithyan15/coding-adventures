# Hear *sastā* — cheap

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 24s] Recall the three-word shopping payoff, say price, write **ु**,
**दुकान**, **वस्तु**, **भाव**, and **भाई**, then say heat.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-SASTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *sastā* — **cheap; inexpensive**

Say the two beats once from a low-price cue. This is the source's dictionary
form; full bargaining phrases remain later work.
