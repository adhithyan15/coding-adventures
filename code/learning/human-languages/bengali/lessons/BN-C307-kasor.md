## You'll want to know: কাঁসর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C307-DHAK307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঁসর** — *kā̃sôr* — "a bell-metal gong".

> ঢাকের সঙ্গে একটা ছেলে কাঁসর বাজাল।
>
> *ḍhāker sôngge ekṭā chhele kā̃sôr bājālô.*
>
> A boy played the gong along with the dhak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঁসর** (*kā̃sôr*) is built on কাঁসা, bell-metal — Sanskrit *kāṁsya*, 'made of bell-metal' (CDIAL 2987, which lists Bengali *kā̃sā*) — and is a flat gong of it, struck beside the drums.
