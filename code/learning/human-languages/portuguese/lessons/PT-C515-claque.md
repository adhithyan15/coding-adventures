## You'll want to know: a claque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C515-LESAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a claque** — "supporters' group, fans".

> A claque cantou durante o jogo todo.
>
> *The supporters sang for the whole match.*

In Portugal, an organised group of fans.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*claque* is usually traced to French *claque*, a group paid to applaud in a theatre, from *claquer* "to clap", an imitative verb.
