Persian, chapter 343: Winning and losing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
برنده شدن (barande shodan) — to win.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a parade, then the Persian for joy.

You'll want to know: برنده شدن.
برنده شدن (barande shodan) — "to win".
تیم ما در مسابقه برنده شد. Tim-e mâ dar mosâbeqe barande shod. Our team won the match.
barande "a winner", from bordan "to carry off, to win". Present barande mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
barande "a winner" is built on bar-, the present stem of the native verb bordan "to carry off, to win", with -ande "one who"; shodan "to become" is inherited too.

Lesson 2 of 5.
باختن (bâkhtan) — to lose (a game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for joy, then the Persian for to win.

You'll want to know: باختن.
باختن (bâkhtan) — "to lose (a game)".
تیم ما دو بر یک باخت. Tim-e mâ do bar yek bâkht. Our team lost two to one.
Present stem bâz-: mibâzad. For losing a thing Persian says gom kardan.

The word, taken apart.
bâkhtan is a native Persian verb with the present stem bâz-, the same shape as in bâzi "game"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تماشاگر (tamâshâgar) — a spectator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to win, then the Persian for to lose.

You'll want to know: تماشاگر.
تماشاگر (tamâshâgar) — "a spectator".
تماشاگران تا آخر بازی ماندند. Tamâshâgarân tâ âkhar-e bâzi mândand. The spectators stayed until the end of the game.
tamâshâ "watching" + -gar. Plural tamâshâgarân.

The word, taken apart.
tamâshâgar adds the Persian suffix -gar to tamâshâ "watching, a show", which EtymDB derives from the Arabic verb mashā "to walk", so first a stroll about, then a show.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تشویق کردن (tashviq kardan) — to cheer (on); to encourage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to lose, then the Persian for a spectator.

You'll want to know: تشویق کردن.
تشویق کردن (tashviq kardan) — "to cheer (on); to encourage".
تماشاگران تیم را تشویق کردند. Tamâshâgarân tim râ tashviq kardand. The spectators cheered the team on.
Also "to encourage": marâ be khândan tashviq kard. Present tashviq mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
tashviq is usually traced to Arabic tashwīq, "a stirring of desire, an encouragement"; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مدال (medâl) — a medal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a spectator, then the Persian for to cheer.

You'll want to know: مدال.
مدال (medâl) — "a medal".
او در مسابقه مدال طلا گرفت. U dar mosâbeqe medâl-e talâ gereft. She won a gold medal in the competition.
Medâl-e noqre is silver, medâl-e boronz bronze.

The word, taken apart.
medâl is usually traced to French médaille, "medal"; EtymDB lists no entry.
