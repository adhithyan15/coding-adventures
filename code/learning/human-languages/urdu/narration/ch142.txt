Urdu, chapter 142: To get off, To fill, To hide, To be saved, To grow.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
اترنا (utarnā) — to get off, to go down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to come out, then the Urdu for to climb.

You'll want to know: اترنا.
اترنا — utarnā — "to get off, to go down".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: utarnā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: utarnā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say utarnā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for to come out, then the Urdu for to climb, then say utarnā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does اترنا (utarnā) mean? ("To get off, to go down".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words — a first pass.

[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to be saved and to grow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: young, old, lovely, soft, hard]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wet, dry, wide, narrow, deep]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: famous, brave, clever, tired, hungry]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: thirsty, fresh, raw, ripe, pink]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: purple, golden, closed, open, to wake up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Young? (جوان, javān.) Old? (بوڑھا, būṛhā.) Lovely? (پیارا, pyārā.) Soft? (نرم, narm.) Hard? (سخت, sakht.) Wet? (گیلا, gīlā.) Dry? (سوکھا, sūkhā.) Wide? (چوڑا, chauṛā.) Narrow? (تنگ, tang.) Deep? (گہرا, gahrā.) Famous? (مشہور, mashhūr.) Brave? (بہادر, bahādur.) Clever? (ہوشیار, hoshiyār.) Tired? (تھکا, thakā.) Hungry? (بھوکا, bhūkā.) Thirsty? (پیاسا, pyāsā.) Fresh? (تازہ, tāzā.) Raw? (کچا, kachchā.) Ripe? (پکا, pakkā.) Pink? (گلابی, gulābī.) Purple? (جامنی, jāmnī.) Golden? (سنہرا, sunahrā.) Closed? (بند, band.) Open? (کھلا, khulā.) To wake up? (جاگنا, jāgnā.)

(جلانا, jalānā.) To run away? (بھاگنا, bhāgnā.) To call? (بلانا, bulānā.) To win? (جیتنا, jītnā.) To lose? (ہارنا, hārnā.) To teach? (سکھانا, sikhānā.) To explain? (سمجھانا, samjhānā.) To ask for? (مانگنا, māṅgnā.) To stay? (ٹھہرنا, ṭhaharnā.) To come back? (لوٹنا, lauṭnā.) To arrive? (پہنچنا, pahuṅchnā.) To come out? (نکلنا, nikalnā.) To climb? (چڑھنا, chaṛhnā.) To get off? (اترنا, utarnā.)
