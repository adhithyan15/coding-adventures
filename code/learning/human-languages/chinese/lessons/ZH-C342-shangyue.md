## You'll want to know: shǎngyuè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C342-AUTUMN342-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shǎngyuè* — "to enjoy the moon".

> *wǎnshang wǒmen yìqǐ zài wàibian shǎngyuè* — In the evening we sat outside together enjoying the moon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shǎngyuè* is built from *shǎng* and 月 'the moon', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syangX* and *ngjwot* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to bestow'. The sense 'to enjoy' is not given there.
