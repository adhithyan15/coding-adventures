## You'll want to know: विशेषज्ञः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C364-VISHESHAJNA364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विशेषज्ञः** — *viśeṣajñaḥ* — "an expert".

> रोगस्य विषये विशेषज्ञं पृच्छतु।
>
> *rogasya viṣaye viśeṣajñaṃ pṛcchatu.*
>
> Ask an expert about the illness.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विशेषज्ञः** (*viśeṣajñaḥ*) is built from *viśeṣa*, "a distinction", and *jña*, "knowing"; MW gives *viśeṣa-jña* "knowing distinctions, judicious", and "an expert" is its modern sense (MW).
