## You'll want to know: ઠોકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C502-SUTHAR502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠોકવું** — *ṭhokvũ* — "to hammer in".

> અરુણે હથોડીથી દીવાલમાં મેખ ઠોકી.
>
> *Aruṇe hathoḍīthī dīvālmā̃ mekh ṭhokī.*
>
> Arun hammered a nail into the wall.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મેખ ઠોકી, *teṇe mekh ṭhokī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠોકવું** (*ṭhokvũ*) comes from *\*ṭhōkk*, "to knock", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to strike" (CDIAL 5513). The past agrees with what is hammered in: **મેખ ઠોકી**.
