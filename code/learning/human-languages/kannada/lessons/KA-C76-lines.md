## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-SKILL-READ-LINES]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-01, KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-03] -->

> ಇದು ಬಾಗಿಲು.
> ಇದು ಕುರ್ಚಿ.
> ಕುರ್ಚಿ ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ.
> ಮರ ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ.
> ನಿಜ.
> ಈಗ.

[PAUSE 3s] Read down once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and count how many different verbs there are.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-04, KA-LEX-C49-REPLY-01, KA-LEX-C50-LEAVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One, and two of the lines have none at all.

[PAUSE 2s] **ಇದು ಬಾಗಿಲು** puts two words side by side and is finished: *this
door* means *this is a door*. Kannada does not say *is* when it is only naming
something.

[PAUSE 2s] But **ಇದೆ** appears the moment you say where a thing is. Naming
needs no verb; locating does. That is the distinction these six lines are for,
and it is visible on the page before anyone names it.
