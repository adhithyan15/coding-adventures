## You'll want to know: wiederherstellen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C397-ABSTURZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**wiederherstellen** — "to restore".

> Der Techniker konnte die alten Fotos wiederherstellen.
>
> *The technician was able to restore the old photos.*

Separable: *er stellt wieder her*, *er hat wiederhergestellt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wiederherstellen* is built from *wieder* "again" and *herstellen* "to make, to produce", from *stellen* "to put", Proto-Germanic *stallijaną* (EtymDB): you put a thing back as it was.
