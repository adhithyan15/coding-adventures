## You'll want to know: ਚਾਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C308-FEED308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਾਰਾ** — *chārā* — "fodder".

> ਮੈਂ ਮੱਝ ਲਈ ਹਰਾ ਚਾਰਾ ਲਿਆਵਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ majjh laī harā chārā liāvāṅgā.*
>
> I will bring green fodder for the buffalo.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਾਰਾ** (*chārā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cāra*, "grazing, fodder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cārā*, "fodder", under it (CDIAL 4755).
