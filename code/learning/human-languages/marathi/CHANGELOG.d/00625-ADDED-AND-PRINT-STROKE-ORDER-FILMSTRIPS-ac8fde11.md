## Added — ी, ो and ः print stroke-order filmstrips

ी, ो and the visarga ः now have a cited pen path, from native writers' pen
traces in HP Labs India's LipiTk Devanagari recognizer (counts and shares
only). Each strip draws the sign alone.

- **New filmstrips (3 lessons, 50 -> 53):** MR-W01-ii-matra (ी: one run from
  the hook's lower tip, over the top and down the stem), MR-W01-o-matra (ो:
  the stem down, a lift, then the flag from its upper-left tip) and
  MR-W02-visarga (ः: the upper dot, a lift, then the lower dot, each a small
  loop from its top, as the lesson's "upper dot, then lower dot" already
  says).
- **The grey bar.** The printed sign carries a short piece of headline that
  the strip leaves grey: it belongs to the word's headline, not to the sign.
- Lesson prose, narration and durations do not change: these lessons never
  said the book gives no stroke order, and the strip is printed in the book
  only.
- **Not yet:** MR-W05-i-matra (ि: native writers split three ways),
  MR-W56-au-matra (ौ) and MR-W59-ai-matra (ै: the traces split).
