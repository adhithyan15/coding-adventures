Punjabi, chapter 332: Nephews, Friends and Neighbours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਭਤੀਜਾ (bhatījā) — a brother's son.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a mother's sister, then the Punjabi for a mother's sister's husband.

You'll want to know: ਭਤੀਜਾ.
ਭਤੀਜਾ — bhatījā — "a brother's son".
ਮੇਰਾ ਭਤੀਜਾ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ। merā bhatījā har roz sakūl jāndā hai. My brother's son goes to school every day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਭਤੀਜਾ (bhatījā) is inherited from Sanskrit bhrātrīya, "a brother's son"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bhatījā, "brother's son", under it (CDIAL 9672).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਗੁਆਂਢੀ (guāṇḍhī) — a neighbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a brother's son, then the Punjabi for a sister's son.

You'll want to know: ਗੁਆਂਢੀ.
ਗੁਆਂਢੀ — guāṇḍhī — "a neighbour".
ਸਾਡੇ ਗੁਆਂਢੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੇ ਲੋਕ ਹਨ। sāḍe guāṇḍhī bahut chaṅge lok han. Our neighbours are very good people.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੁਆਂਢੀ (guāṇḍhī) goes back to a reconstructed grāmārdha, "the vicinity of a village", built from grāma, "village", and ardha, "half, side"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gavā̃ḍhī, "neighbour", under it (CDIAL 4376).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਪੁਕਾਰਨਾ (pukārnā) — to call out to someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a neighbour, then the Punjabi for a woman's female friend.

You'll want to know: ਪੁਕਾਰਨਾ.
ਪੁਕਾਰਨਾ — pukārnā — "to call out to someone".
ਮੇਰਾ ਭਤੀਜਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਗਲੀ ਤੋਂ ਪੁਕਾਰਦਾ ਹੈ। merā bhatījā mainū̃ galī ton pukārdā hai. My brother's son calls out to me from the lane.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਗੁਆਂਢੀ ਨੂੰ ਪੁਕਾਰਿਆ, maiṁ guāṇḍhī nū̃ pukāriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੁਕਾਰਨਾ (pukārnā) is inherited from a reconstructed pukkār, "a shout"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pukārnā, "shout", under it (CDIAL 8246).
