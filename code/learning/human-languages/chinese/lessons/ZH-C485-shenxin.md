## You'll want to know: 身心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C485-MIND485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**身心** — *shēnxīn* — "body and mind".

> **眼前最要紧的是身心都好** *yǎnqián zuì yàojǐn de shì shēnxīn dōu hǎo* — For now, what matters most is being well in body and mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**身心** (*shēnxīn*) is built from 身 'body' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syin* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'oneself'.
