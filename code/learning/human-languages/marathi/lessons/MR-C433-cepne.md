## You'll want to know: चेपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C433-TADA433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चेपणे** — *cepṇe* — "to get dented".

> भिंतीला आपटून गाडीचा दरवाजा चेपला.
>
> *bhintīlā āpṭūn gāḍīcā darvājā ceplā.*
>
> The car door got dented when it hit the wall.

Past (he …): तो चेपला, *to ceplā*.

It also means to press or squeeze something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेपणे** (*cepṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *cepṇẽ*, "to press, squeeze", under *\*capp*, "press" (CDIAL 4674).
