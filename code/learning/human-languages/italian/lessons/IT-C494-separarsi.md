## You'll want to know: separarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C494-SEPARARSI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**separarsi** — "to separate, to split up".

> I miei genitori si sono separati quando ero piccolo.
>
> *My parents separated when I was little.*

It takes *essere*. *Separati* — separated, not yet divorced.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*separarsi* is the reflexive of *separare*, a learned borrowing from Latin *sēparāre* "to part, to divide" (Lewis & Short), from *sē-* "apart" and *parāre*.
