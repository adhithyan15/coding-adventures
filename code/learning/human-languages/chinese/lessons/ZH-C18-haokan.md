# 好看 — good to look at

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-HAOKAN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-HAO, ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **good-looking** as *hǎokàn*. Write 好 and 看 separately.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-ORTHO-HAOKAN-01]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-HAO, ZH-SCRIPT-KAN-01] -->

> **好看** — *hǎokàn* — **good-looking; nice to look at**

No new character is hiding here. Read 好, then 看, and let the literal pieces
carry you to the whole meaning.
