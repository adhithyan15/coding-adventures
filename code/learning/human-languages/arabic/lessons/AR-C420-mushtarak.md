## You'll want to know: مشترك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C420-ISTIJABA420-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مشترك** — *mushtarak* — "shared".

> عندنا أصدقاء مشتركون.
>
> *ʿindanā aṣdiqāʾ mushtarakūn.*
>
> We have friends in common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشترك** (*mushtarak*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-k*, "to share, to be a partner" (Lane). *Mushtarak* is the Form VIII passive participle, what is shared: common, joint.
