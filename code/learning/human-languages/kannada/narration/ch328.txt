Kannada, chapter 328: A Robbery at Night.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದರೋಡೆ (darōḍe) — a robbery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for abruptly, then the Kannada for to be startled.

You'll want to know: ದರೋಡೆ.
ದರೋಡೆ — darōḍe — "a robbery".
ನಿನ್ನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಬ್ಯಾಂಕಿನಲ್ಲಿ ದರೋಡೆ ಆಯಿತು. ninne rātri byāṅkinalli darōḍe āyitu. There was a robbery at the bank last night.
ದರೋಡೆ (darōḍe) is robbery by force, often by a gang.

The word, taken apart.
ದರೋಡೆ (darōḍe) is usually traced to Marathi daroḍā, "a gang robbery"; the earlier history of the word is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ದೋಚು (dōcu) — to loot; to rob.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to be startled, then the Kannada for a robbery.

You'll want to know: ದೋಚು.
ದೋಚು — dōcu — "to loot; to rob".
ಕಳ್ಳರು ಅಂಗಡಿಯನ್ನು ದೋಚಿದರು. kaḷḷaru aṅgaḍiyannu dōcidaru. The robbers looted the shop.
Past (I …): ದೋಚಿದೆ, dōcide.
The place or person robbed takes -ಅನ್ನು: ಮನೆಯನ್ನು ದೋಚು (dōcu).

The word, taken apart.
ದೋಚು (dōcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3541 lists Kannada dōcu, "to plunder, rob", beside Telugu dō̃cu, "to plunder, pillage".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಎಸಗು (esagu) — to commit (a crime).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a robbery, then the Kannada for to loot.

You'll want to know: ಎಸಗು.
ಎಸಗು — esagu — "to commit (a crime)".
ಅವನು ಹಣಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಆ ಅಪರಾಧ ಎಸಗಿದನು. avanu haṇakkāgi ā aparādha esagidanu. He committed that crime for money.
Past (I …): ಎಸಗಿದೆ, esagide.
A formal verb for wrong deeds: ಅಪರಾಧ ಎಸಗು (esagu), ತಪ್ಪು ಎಸಗು (esagu).

The word, taken apart.
ಎಸಗು (esagu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 471 lists Kannada esagu, "to engage in, undertake, do, perform".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪರಾರಿಯಾಗು (parāriyāgu) — to abscond; to flee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to loot, then the Kannada for to commit.

You'll want to know: ಪರಾರಿಯಾಗು.
ಪರಾರಿಯಾಗು — parāriyāgu — "to abscond; to flee".
ದರೋಡೆಯ ನಂತರ ಕಳ್ಳರು ಕಾರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಪರಾರಿಯಾದರು. darōḍeya nantara kaḷḷaru kārinalli parāriyādaru. After the robbery the robbers fled in a car.
Past (I …): ಪರಾರಿಯಾದೆ, parāriyāde.
A news word for an escape from the police: ಆರೋಪಿ ಪರಾರಿಯಾದನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಾರಿಯಾಗು (parāriyāgu) is ಪರಾರಿ, "on the run", with āgu "to become" (DEDR 333). ಪರಾರಿ is usually traced to Hindustani farārī, "a fugitive", from Arabic firār, "flight".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಜಾಡು (jāḍu) — a track; a trail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to commit, then the Kannada for to abscond.

You'll want to know: ಜಾಡು.
ಜಾಡು — jāḍu — "a track; a trail".
ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಕಾರಿನ ಜಾಡು ಹಿಡಿದು ಹೋದರು. polīsaru kārina jāḍu hiḍidu hōdaru. The police followed the car's trail.
ಜಾಡು (jāḍu) ಹಿಡಿ, to follow a trail. Not to be confused with ಜಾಡಿ, a jar.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಾಡು (jāḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2442 lists Kannada jāḍu, "the mark of a footstep or of a cart wheel, a track, a trace".
