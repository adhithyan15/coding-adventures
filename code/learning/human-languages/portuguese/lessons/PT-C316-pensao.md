## You'll want to know: a pensão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C316-HERANCA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a pensão** — "the pension; the guesthouse".

> A minha avó vive da pensão.
>
> *My grandmother lives on her pension.*

Also a small, cheap guesthouse: *ficámos numa pensão*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pensão* was borrowed from Latin *pēnsiō* "a payment, a rent" (EtymDB), from *pendere* "to weigh, to pay".
