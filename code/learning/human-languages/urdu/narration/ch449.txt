Urdu, chapter 449: Affection.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ممتا (mamtā) — a mother's love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for eager, then the Urdu for to feel about for.

You'll want to know: ممتا.
ممتا — mamtā — "a mother's love".
ماں کی ممتا سب سے بڑی ہے۔ māṅ kī mamtā sab se baṛī hai. A mother's love is the greatest of all.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ممتا (mamtā) is inherited from Sanskrit mamatā, "the feeling of mine" (Monier-Williams), built on mama, "my"; Urdu uses it for a mother's love.

چمٹنا (chimaṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin cimaṭnā, "to embrace closely", under cimb, "pinch", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4822).
