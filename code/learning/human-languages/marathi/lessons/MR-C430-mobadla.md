## You'll want to know: मोबदला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C430-MOBADLA430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मोबदला** — *mobadlā* — "a payment in return".

> कामाचा योग्य मोबदला मिळाला पाहिजे.
>
> *kāmācā yogya mobadlā miḷālā pāhije.*
>
> Work should get a fair payment.

Masculine.

A fee or compensation for work or for land taken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोबदला** (*mobadlā*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *mubādala*, "an exchange".
