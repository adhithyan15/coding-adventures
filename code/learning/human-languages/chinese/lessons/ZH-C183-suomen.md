## You'll want to know: suǒmén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C183-INOUT183-04]; assesses=[] -->

*suǒmén* — "to lock the door; to lock up".

> *chūqù yǐqián bié wàng le suǒmén* — Don't forget to lock the door before you go out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suǒmén* is built from *suǒ* 'a lock' and 门 'door', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *swaX* and *mwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'an iron lock'.
