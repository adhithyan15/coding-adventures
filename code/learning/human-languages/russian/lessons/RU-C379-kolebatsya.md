## You'll want to know: колебаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C379-ZAPASNOY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**колебаться** (*kolebátsya*) — "to hesitate, to waver".

> Я долго колебался, но потом согласился.
>
> *Ya dólgo kolebálsya, no potóm soglasílsya.*
>
> I hesitated for a long time, but then I agreed.

Imperfective; present *колеблюсь, колеблешься*. Literally to sway: *пламя колеблется*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**колебаться** is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*kolěbati* (EtymDB), usually glossed "to rock, to sway"; a person who hesitates sways from one side to the other.
