# ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം (gṛhapravēśaṁ) — a housewarming

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to congratulate, then the Malayalam for to bestow.

## You'll want to know: ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C389-GRIHAM389-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം** — *gṛhapravēśaṁ* — "a housewarming".

> അവരുടെ പുതിയ വീടിന്റെ ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം ഞായറാഴ്ച ആണ്.
>
> *avaruṭe putiya vīṭinṟe gṛhapravēśaṁ ñāyaṟāḻca āṇŭ.*
>
> Their new house's housewarming is on Sunday.

Literally entering the house: ഗൃഹം, a house, + പ്രവേശം, entry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം** (*gṛhapravēśaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gṛhapraveśa*, "solemn entrance into a house" (Monier-Williams), from *gṛha*, "a house", and *praveśa*, "entering".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
