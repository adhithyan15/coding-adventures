## You'll want to know: ਪਹੇਲੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C355-RIDDLE355-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੇਲੀ** — *pahelī* — "a riddle".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਾਦੇ ਕੋਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਹੇਲੀਆਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *mere dāde kol bahut pahelīāṁ han.*
>
> My grandfather knows lots of riddles.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਹੇਲੀ** (*pahelī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prahelikā*, "riddle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *parhelī*, "riddle", and *pahelī* under it (CDIAL 8911).
