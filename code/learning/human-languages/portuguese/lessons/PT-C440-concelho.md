## You'll want to know: o concelho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C440-MORADA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o concelho** — "the municipality, the council area".

> O concelho tem dez freguesias.
>
> *The municipality has ten parishes.*

Not *conselho*, advice, which sounds the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*concelho* is inherited from Latin *concilium* 'council, meeting' (EtymDB).
