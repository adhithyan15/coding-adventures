# পৃষ্ঠা (prishṭhā) — a page

## You'll want to know: পৃষ্ঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C328-PRISHTHA328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**পৃষ্ঠা** — *prishṭhā* — "a page".

> বইটার প্রথম পৃষ্ঠায় লেখকের ছবি আছে।
>
> *bôiṭār prothom prishṭhāy lekhôker chhôbi āchhe.*
>
> There is a picture of the writer on the first page of the book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পৃষ্ঠা** (*prishṭhā*) is Sanskrit *pṛṣṭha*, 'the back; the upper side, a surface' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a page', one face of a leaf, is the later one. Everyday speech also says **পাতা**.
