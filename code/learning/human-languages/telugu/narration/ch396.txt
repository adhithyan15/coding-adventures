Telugu, chapter 396: On the Other Hand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అయినప్పటికీ (ayinappaṭikī) — nevertheless; even so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for that is to say, then the Telugu for a footing.

You'll want to know: అయినప్పటికీ.
అయినప్పటికీ — ayinappaṭikī — "nevertheless; even so".
అతను అలసిపోయాడు, అయినప్పటికీ పని పూర్తి చేశాడు. atanu alasipōyāḍu, ayinappaṭikī pani pūrti cēśāḍu. He was tired; even so, he finished the work.
అయిన, having been, + అప్పటికీ, even then. Stronger than అయినా, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
అయినప్పటికీ (ayinappaṭikī) is native Dravidian: ayina, a form of avu, "to be, to become" (DEDR 333), and appaṭikī, "even at that time".

Lesson 2 of 5.
మరోవైపు (marōvaipu) — on the other hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a footing, then the Telugu for nevertheless.

You'll want to know: మరోవైపు.
మరోవైపు — marōvaipu — "on the other hand".
ఒక వైపు డబ్బు కావాలి, మరోవైపు సమయం లేదు. oka vaipu ḍabbu kāvāli, marōvaipu samayaṁ lēdu. On one side we need money; on the other hand there is no time.
ఒక వైపు... మరోవైపు (marōvaipu)..., on one hand... on the other.

The word, taken apart.
మరోవైపు (marōvaipu) is a Telugu phrase of marō, "another", and vaipu, "a side, a direction"; the origin of vaipu is uncertain.

Lesson 3 of 5.
కాగా (kāgā) — whereas; while.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for nevertheless, then the Telugu for on the other hand.

You'll want to know: కాగా.
కాగా — kāgā — "whereas; while".
అన్న వైద్యుడు కాగా, తమ్ముడు రైతు. anna vaidyuḍu kāgā, tammuḍu raitu. Whereas the elder brother is a doctor, the younger is a farmer.
It sets two facts side by side; it is a form of అవు, to be.

The word, taken apart.
కాగా (kāgā) is native Dravidian, a form of avu, "to be, to become" (DEDR 333), whose stem takes the shape kā- in some forms.

Lesson 4 of 5.
విరుద్ధం (viruddhaṁ) — contrary; opposed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for on the other hand, then the Telugu for whereas.

You'll want to know: విరుద్ధం.
విరుద్ధం — viruddhaṁ — "contrary; opposed".
అది నియమాలకు విరుద్ధం. adi niyamālaku viruddhaṁ. That is against the rules.
The thing opposed takes -కి: నియమానికి విరుద్ధం (viruddhaṁ).

The word, taken apart.
విరుద్ధం (viruddhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit viruddha, "opposed, hindered" (CDIAL 11864).

Lesson 5 of 5.
అదేవిధంగా (adēvidhaṅgā) — likewise; in the same way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for whereas, then the Telugu for contrary.

You'll want to know: అదేవిధంగా.
అదేవిధంగా — adēvidhaṅgā — "likewise; in the same way".
అన్న బాగా చదివాడు, అదేవిధంగా చెల్లి కూడా. anna bāgā cadivāḍu, adēvidhaṅgā celli kūḍā. The elder brother studied hard, and likewise the younger sister.
అదే, that very, + విధం, a way, + -గా.

The word, taken apart.
అదేవిధంగా (adēvidhaṅgā) is built from native adē, "that very", on the far-pointing base a- (DEDR 1), and vidhaṁ, "a manner", from Sanskrit vidha (Monier-Williams), with the adverb ending -గా.
