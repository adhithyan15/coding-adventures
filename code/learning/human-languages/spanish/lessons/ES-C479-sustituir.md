## You'll want to know: sustituir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C479-TRAMITE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sustituir** — "to replace, to stand in for".

> Un compañero sustituye al profesor.
>
> *A colleague is standing in for the teacher.*

It takes a *y* in some forms: *sustituyo*, *sustituye*.
