Russian, chapter 377: Changing plans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
переносить (perenosít) — to postpone, to move (to another time); to carry over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for at least, then the Russian for in case.

You'll want to know: переносить.
переносить (perenosít) — "to postpone, to move (to another time); to carry over".
Мы переносим встречу на понедельник. My perenósim vstréchu na ponedélnik. We are moving the meeting to Monday.
Imperfective; present переношу, переносишь; perfective перенести. Переносить на + accusative — to move to. It also means to bear: плохо переносить (perenosít) холод.

The word, taken apart.
переносить (perenosít) is built from пере- "across, over" and носить "to carry", inherited from Proto-Slavic: to carry a meeting over to another day.

You'll want to know: передумывать.
передумывать (peredúmyvat) — "to change one’s mind".
Он часто передумывает в последнюю минуту. On chásto peredúmyvayet v poslédnyuyu minútu. He often changes his mind at the last minute.
Imperfective; perfective передумать: я передумал — I have changed my mind.

The word, taken apart.
передумывать (peredúmyvat) is built from пере- "over again" and думать "to think": to think a thing over again and come out the other way.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: отказываться.
отказываться (otkázyvatsya) — "to refuse, to turn down; to give up".
Она отказывается от сладкого. Oná otkázyvayetsya ot sládkogo. She is giving up sweet things.
Imperfective; perfective отказаться. Отказываться от + genitive (to turn down or give up), or + infinitive: отказался помочь.

The word, taken apart.
отказываться (otkázyvatsya) is built from от- "away" and казать "to show, to say", Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB): to say a thing away from yourself.

назначать (naznachát) — "to set, to fix (a time); to appoint".
Мы назначили встречу на пять часов. My naznáchili vstréchu na pyat chasóv. We set the meeting for five o’clock.
Imperfective; perfective назначить. It also means to appoint someone to a post, or to prescribe a medicine.

The word, taken apart.
назначать (naznachát) is built from на- and знак "a sign, a mark", on the root of знать "to know", Proto-Slavic znati: to mark a time out.

Lesson 5 of 5.
собираться (sobirátsya) — to be going to, to intend; to gather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to refuse, then the Russian for to set.

You'll want to know: собираться.
собираться (sobirátsya) — "to be going to, to intend; to gather".
Летом мы собираемся на море. Létom my sobiráyemsya na móre. In the summer we are planning to go to the seaside.
Imperfective; perfective собраться. With an infinitive, to intend: я собираюсь уехать. It also means to gather: гости собираются.

The word, taken apart.
собираться (sobirátsya) is the reflexive of собирать "to gather", from со- "together" and бирать, from брать "to take": to gather yourself up for a journey or a plan.
