## You'll want to know: حاور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C302-HIWAR302-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حاور** — *ḥāwara* — "to hold a dialogue; to interview".

> حاور المذيع لاعبا مشهورا.
>
> *ḥāwara al-mudhīʿ lāʿiban mashhūran.*
>
> The presenter interviewed a famous player.

Present: يحاور, *yuḥāwir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حاور** (*ḥāwara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-r* (Lane), "to go back". It is Form III, "to answer one another".
