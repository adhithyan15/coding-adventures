## You'll want to know: வஞ்சி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C337-SATHI337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**வஞ்சி** — *vañci* — "to deceive".

> அந்த மந்திரி மன்னரை வஞ்சித்தார்.
>
> *anta mantiri maṉṉarai vañcittār.*
>
> That minister deceived the king.

Past (I …): வஞ்சித்தேன், *vañcittēṉ*.

A written word; the noun is வஞ்சனை, deceit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வஞ்சி** (*vañci*) is a Tamil verb built on Sanskrit *vañc*, "to cheat, deceive" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
