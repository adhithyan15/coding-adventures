## You'll want to know: せんしゅ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C234-MATCH234-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せんしゅ** — *senshu* — "a player; an athlete".

> **きょねん あには がっこうの せんしゅでした。** — *kyonen ani wa gakkō no senshu deshita* — "Last year my elder brother was one of the school's players."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんしゅ** (*senshu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "choose" (Middle Chinese *sjwenX*) and *shu*, "hand" (*syuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
