## You'll want to know: نچانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C519-DHOL519-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نچانا** — *nachānā* — "to make (someone) dance".

> ڈھول نے سب مہمانوں کو نچایا۔
>
> *ḍhol ne sab mehmānoṅ ko nachāyā.*
>
> The drum got all the guests dancing.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچوں کو نچایا, *us ne bachchoṅ ko nachāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نچانا** (*nachānā*) is the causative of *nāchnā*, "to dance", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *nṛtyati*, "dances" (CDIAL 7583).
