Marathi, chapter 335: Digging Up the Past.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उत्खनन (utkhanan) — an excavation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a chronicle, then the Marathi for an inscription.

You'll want to know: उत्खनन.
उत्खनन — utkhanan — "an excavation".
उत्खननात जुनी भांडी सापडली. utkhananāt junī bhāṇḍī sāpaḍlī. Old pots were found in the excavation.
Neuter.
उत्खनन (utkhanan) करणे, to excavate.

The word, taken apart.
उत्खनन (utkhanan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit utkhanana, 'a digging up' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अवशेष (avaśeṣ) — remains; ruins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an inscription, then the Marathi for an excavation.

You'll want to know: अवशेष.
अवशेष — avaśeṣ — "remains; ruins".
टेकडीवर एका जुन्या शहराचे अवशेष आहेत. ṭekḍīvar ekā junyā śahrāce avaśeṣ āhet. There are the remains of an old city on the hill.
Masculine.
Mostly in the plural: अवशेष (avaśeṣ) सापडणे, remains are found.

The word, taken apart.
अवशेष (avaśeṣ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit avaśeṣa, 'a remainder, what is left' (Monier-Williams).

हे गावातलं सर्वात पुरातन मंदिर आहे. he gāvātlaṁ sarvāt purātan mandir āhe. This is the most ancient temple in the village.
पुरातन (purātan) keeps one form: पुरातन (purātan) मंदिर, पुरातन (purātan) वस्तू.

Lesson 4 of 5.
खोदणे (khodṇe) — to dig.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for remains, then the Marathi for age-old.

You'll want to know: खोदणे.
खोदणे — khodṇe — "to dig".
मजुरांनी तिथे नवी विहीर खोदली. majurānnī tithe navī vihīr khodlī. The workers dug a new well there.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने खोदलं, tyāne khodlaṁ.
What is dug agrees with the past: विहीर खोदली, रस्ता खोदला.

The word, taken apart.
खोदणे (khodṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi khodṇe, 'to dig, carve', under khōdd, 'dig' (CDIAL 3934).

Lesson 5 of 5.
कोरणे (korṇe) — to carve; to engrave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for age-old, then the Marathi for to dig.

You'll want to know: कोरणे.
कोरणे — korṇe — "to carve; to engrave".
कारागिरांनी दगडात सुंदर मूर्ती कोरल्या. kārāgirānnī dagḍāt sundar mūrtī koralyā. The craftsmen carved beautiful figures in the stone.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोरलं, tyāne korlaṁ.
The material takes -त: दगडात कोरणे (korṇe).

The word, taken apart.
कोरणे (korṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi korṇe, 'to scoop, engrave', under kōrati, 'scrapes' (CDIAL 3530).
