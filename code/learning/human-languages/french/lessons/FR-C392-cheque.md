## You'll want to know: le chèque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C392-BANQUE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le chèque** — "the cheque".

> Elle a payé le plombier par chèque.
>
> *She paid the plumber by cheque.*

*Un chèque sans provision* — a bounced cheque.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chèque* is usually traced to English *cheque*, *check*, itself linked to *check* "a control": a paper that guards against forgery.
