## You'll want to know: bluffare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C504-CARTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bluffare** — "to bluff".

> Non ha niente in mano, sta bluffando.
>
> *He has nothing in his hand, he is bluffing.*

Also *fare un bluff*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bluffare* is built on *bluff*, which was borrowed from English *bluff* (EtymDB), a word from card games.
