## You'll want to know: l'allarme
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C293-SCASSO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'allarme** — "the alarm".

> Qualcuno ha dato l'allarme.
>
> *Someone raised the alarm.*

*Dare l'allarme* — to raise the alarm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allarme* comes from the cry *all'arme!* "to arms!"; *arme* goes back to Latin *arma* "weapons" (Lewis & Short).
