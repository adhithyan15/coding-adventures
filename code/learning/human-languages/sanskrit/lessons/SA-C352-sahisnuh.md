## You'll want to know: सहिष्णुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C352-AHIMSA352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सहिष्णुः** — *sahiṣṇuḥ* — "tolerant; forbearing".

> सहिष्णुः जनः क्रोधं न करोति।
>
> *sahiṣṇuḥ janaḥ krodhaṃ na karoti.*
>
> A tolerant person does not give way to anger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहिष्णुः** (*sahiṣṇuḥ*) comes from the root √sah, "to bear, to put up with", with the ending *-iṣṇu*; MW gives *sahiṣṇu* "patient, forbearing" and "bearing, enduring", and KRM lists *sahiṣṇu* among the forms of the root (MW).
