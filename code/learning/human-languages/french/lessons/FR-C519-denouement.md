## You'll want to know: le dénouement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C519-ROMAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le dénouement** — "ending, outcome (of a story)".

> Je n'ai pas aimé le dénouement du film.
>
> *I did not like the ending of the film.*

Literally the untying of the knot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le dénouement* is built on *dénouer* "to untie", from *dé-* and *nouer* "to tie", inherited from Latin *nōdāre*, from *nōdus* "a knot" (Lewis & Short).
