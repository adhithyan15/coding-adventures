## You'll want to know: शहारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C452-DHAKKA452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शहारा** — *śahārā* — "a shiver; goosebumps".

> ती गोष्ट ऐकून मला शहारा आला.
>
> *tī goṣṭa aikūn malā śahārā ālā.*
>
> Hearing that story gave me a shiver.

Masculine.

शहारा येणे, to shiver, with the dative. The plural शहारे, goosebumps all over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शहारा** (*śahārā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
