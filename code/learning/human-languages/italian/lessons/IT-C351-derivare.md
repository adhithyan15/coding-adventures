## You'll want to know: derivare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C351-ORIGINE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**derivare** — "to come from, to derive (from)".

> Molti problemi derivano dalla mancanza di soldi.
>
> *Many problems come from a lack of money.*

*Derivare da* takes *essere*: *il problema è derivato da...*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derivare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dērīvāre* "to lead off, to draw off water" (Lewis & Short), built from *dē* "from" and *rīvus* "a stream": a channel flowing out of a river.

## What you've built

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
