## You'll want to know: مستند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C531-SCREEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مستند** (*mostanad*) — "a documentary".

> او مستندهای طبیعت را دوست دارد.
>
> *u mostanadhâ-ye tabi'at râ dust dârad.*
>
> He likes nature documentaries.

Also an adjective 'documented': *film-e mostanad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mostanad* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *mustanad*, 'a document, something relied on'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
