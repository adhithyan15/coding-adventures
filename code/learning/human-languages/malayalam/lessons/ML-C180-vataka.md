## You'll want to know: വാടക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C180-VAYANA180-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വാടക** — *vāṭaka* — "rent, hire charge". The money you pay to live in a house: **വീട്ടുവാടക** — *vīṭṭuvāṭaka* — "the rent for the house".
