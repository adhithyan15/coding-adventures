Telugu, chapter 280: To rub, To receive, To tell, To admit, To deposit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
రుద్దు (ruddu) — to rub, to scrub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to touch, then the Telugu for to peel.

You'll want to know: రుద్దు.
రుద్దు — ruddu — "to rub, to scrub".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: ruddu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ruddu, once more]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does రుద్దు (ruddu) mean? ("To rub, to scrub".) Say it once more.
