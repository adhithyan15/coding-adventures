1. Listen to *tusī̃ kivēṁ ho?* and give its meaning before repeating it.
2. Reply *maiṁ ṭhīk hāṁ* from the English cue alone.
3. Answer thanks with *koī gall nahīṁ*.
4. Name the three small root cues: question *k-*, first-person *m-*, negative
   *n-*.

Romanization is only a machine-readable record of prior speech. It is not
Gurmukhi reading or writing evidence.
