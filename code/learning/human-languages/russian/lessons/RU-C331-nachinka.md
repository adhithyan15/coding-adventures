## You'll want to know: начинка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C331-TESTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**начинка** (*nachínka*) — "a filling, a stuffing".

> Начинка в пирогах была из капусты.
>
> *Nachínka v pirogákh bylá iz kapústy.*
>
> The filling in the pies was cabbage.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*начинка* is built on *начинить* "to stuff", which is usually traced to *чин* "order": to fill in good order. EtymDB lists no parent.
