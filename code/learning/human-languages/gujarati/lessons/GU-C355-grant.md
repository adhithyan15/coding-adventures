# ગ્રાન્ટ (grānṭ) — a grant (of public money)

## You'll want to know: ગ્રાન્ટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C355-VERO355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગ્રાન્ટ** — *grānṭ* — "a grant (of public money)".

> સરકારે શાળાને નવી ગ્રાન્ટ આપી.
>
> *sarkāre śāḷāne navī grānṭ āpī.*
>
> The government gave the school a new grant.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગ્રાન્ટ** (*grānṭ*) is borrowed from English *grant*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
