Mandarin Chinese, chapter 144: Easy and Important.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
重要 (zhòngyào) — important.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for punctual, then the Mandarin for a long while.

You'll want to know: 重要.
重要 — zhòngyào — "important".
每天喝水很重要 měitiān hē shuǐ hěn zhòngyào — Drinking water every day is important.

The word, taken apart.
重要 (zhòngyào) is built from 重 'heavy' and 要, glossed 'to bind, to agree', both inherited from Middle Chinese, drjowngX and 'jiewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How the pair came to mean important is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for describing things.

The word, taken apart.
róngyì is built from róng 'to hold' and yì 'easy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, yowng and yeH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second 'hard and easy' and 'simple'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
juéde — to find (that); to feel; to think (that).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for simple, then the Mandarin for special.

You'll want to know: juéde.
juéde — "to find (that); to feel; to think (that)".
wǒ juéde hànyǔ hěn yǒuyìsi — I find Chinese interesting.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
juéde is built from jué, glossed 'to understand' and 'to wake', and de, from a word glossed 'to get', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kaewk and tok (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
One more word for describing things.
