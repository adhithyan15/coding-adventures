## You'll want to know: أقر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C411-IJAZA411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أقر** — *aqarra* — "to approve formally".

> أقر المجلس الخطة الجديدة.
>
> *aqarra al-majlis al-khiṭṭa al-jadīda.*
>
> The council formally approved the new plan.

Present: يقر, *yuqirr*.

Also to admit: أقر بالخطأ, admitted the mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أقر** (*aqarra*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-r*, "to settle, to be still" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make a thing settled: to approve it formally, or to admit it.
