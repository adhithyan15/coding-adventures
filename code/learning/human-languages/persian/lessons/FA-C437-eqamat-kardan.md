## You'll want to know: اقامت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C437-MOSAFERKHANE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اقامت کردن** (*eqâmat kardan*) — "to stay (somewhere), to reside".

> سه شب در این هتل اقامت کردیم.
>
> *Se shab dar in hotel eqâmat kardim.*
>
> We stayed three nights in this hotel.

*eqâmat* 'stay' + *kardan*: more formal than *mândan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eqâmat kardan* is built from *eqâmat* 'a stay', a loan usually traced to Arabic *iqāma* 'staying, residing', and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
