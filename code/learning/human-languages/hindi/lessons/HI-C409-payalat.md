## You'll want to know: पायलट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C409-UDAN409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पायलट** — *pāyalaṭ* — "a pilot".

> पायलट ने कहा कि मौसम ठीक है।
>
> *pāyalaṭ ne kahā ki mausam ṭhīk hai.*
>
> The pilot said that the weather was fine.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पायलट** (*pāyalaṭ*) is borrowed from English *pilot*.
