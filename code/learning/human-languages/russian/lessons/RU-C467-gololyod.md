## You'll want to know: гололёд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C467-MOROZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**гололёд** (*gololyód*) — "black ice".

> Из-за гололёда машины едут медленно.
>
> *Iz-za gololyóda mashíny yédut médlenno.*
>
> Because of the black ice the cars are going slowly.

Masculine. Built from *голый* — bare — and *лёд* — ice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гололёд* is built from *голый* 'bare' and *лёд* 'ice': bare ice on the road. *Лёд* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *ledŭ* (EtymDB).
