## You'll want to know: apear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C413-PARAGEM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**apear** — "to get off (a vehicle); to dismount".

> Apeei-me na paragem do mercado.
>
> *I got off at the market stop.*

Mostly reflexive and a bit formal: *apear-se do comboio*. Everyday speech also says *sair do autocarro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apear* is built from *a-*, *pé* 'foot' and *-ar* (EtymDB): to put your feet on the ground. *Pé* is inherited from Latin *pedem*.
