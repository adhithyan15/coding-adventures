## You'll want to know: పురోగతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C429-PUROGATI429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పురోగతి** — *purōgati* — "progress; headway".

> ఆ పనిలో మంచి పురోగతి ఉంది.
>
> *ā panilō mañci purōgati undi.*
>
> There is good progress in that work.

Formal; పురోగమించు, to advance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పురోగతి** (*purōgati*) is built from Sanskrit *puras*, "in front, forward", and *gati*, "going, motion" (Monier-Williams; CDIAL 4009).
