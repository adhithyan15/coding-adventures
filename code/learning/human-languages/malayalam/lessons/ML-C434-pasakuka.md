## You'll want to know: പാസാകുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C434-HOSTAL434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പാസാകുക** — *pāsākuka* — "to pass an exam".

> അവൾ എല്ലാ വിഷയങ്ങളിലും പാസായി.
>
> *avaḷ ellā viṣayaṅṅaḷiluṁ pāsāyi.*
>
> She passed in all subjects.

Past: പാസായി, *pāsāyi*.

Its past is പാസായി; the causative പാസാക്കുക was taught a few chapters earlier.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പാസാകുക** (*pāsākuka*) is a Malayalam compound: the first part was borrowed from English *pass*, and ആകുക, "to become", is native Dravidian (DEDR 333).
