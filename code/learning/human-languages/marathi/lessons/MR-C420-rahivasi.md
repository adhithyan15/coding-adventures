## You'll want to know: रहिवासी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C420-DUTAVAS420-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रहिवासी** — *rahivāsī* — "a resident".

> या इमारतीतले रहिवासी शांत आहेत.
>
> *yā imāratītle rahivāsī śānt āhet.*
>
> The residents of this building are quiet.

Masculine.

The plural is the same: एक रहिवासी, सगळे रहिवासी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रहिवासी** (*rahivāsī*) is a Marathi word for someone who lives in a place; it ends like **प्रवासी**, but how it was formed is not settled.
