## You'll want to know: গেট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C412-PANCHIL412-02]; assesses=[] -->

**গেট** — *geṭ* — "a gate".

> দারোয়ান রাতে গেট বন্ধ করেন।
>
> *dāroyān rāte geṭ bôndhô kôren.*
>
> The gatekeeper shuts the gate at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গেট** (*geṭ*) is borrowed from English *gate*, with the final t said as ট.
