## You'll want to know: permanecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C543-RETRASO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**permanecer** — "to remain, to stay".

> Permaneció en el hospital una semana.
>
> *He remained in hospital for a week.*

Formal; in speech *quedarse*. It takes a *z*: *permanezco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*permanecer* comes from Latin *permanēre* 'to stay to the end, to last' (EtymDB), built on *per* and *manēre* 'to stay', with the Spanish ending *-ecer*.
