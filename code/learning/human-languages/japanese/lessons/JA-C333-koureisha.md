## You'll want to know: こうれいしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C333-WAKAMONO333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こうれいしゃ** — *kōreisha* — "an elderly person".

> **こうれいしゃが ふえたと おもいます。** — *kōreisha ga fueta to omoimasu* — "I think the number of elderly people has grown."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうれいしゃ** (*kōreisha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "high" (Middle Chinese *kaw*), *rei*, "age" (*leng*) and *sha*, "someone" (*tsyaeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
