## You'll want to know: کارتخوان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C461-KARTKHAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کارتخوان** (*kârtkhân*) — "a card machine, a card reader".

> کارتخوان نانوایی خراب بود.
>
> *Kârtkhân-e nânvâyi kharâb bud.*
>
> The bakery's card machine was out of order.

*kârt* 'card' + *khân* 'reader', from *khândan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kârtkhân* is built from *kârt* 'card', which EtymDB takes from French *carte*, and native *khân*, the present stem of *khândan* 'to read'.
