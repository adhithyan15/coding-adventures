Italian, chapter 351: Da dove nasce.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'origine — the origin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for therefore, then the Italian for due.

You'll want to know: l'origine.
l'origine — "the origin".
Qual è l'origine di questa tradizione? What is the origin of this tradition?
Avere origine da — to come from. Di origine italiana — of Italian origin.

The word, taken apart.
origine is a learned borrowing from Latin orīgō, orīginis "beginning, source, birth" (Lewis & Short), from orīrī "to rise", as the sun rises.

Lesson 2 of 5.
derivare — to come from, to derive (from).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for due, then the Italian for the origin.

You'll want to know: derivare.
derivare — "to come from, to derive (from)".
Molti problemi derivano dalla mancanza di soldi. Many problems come from a lack of money.
Derivare da takes essere: il problema è derivato da...

The word, taken apart.
derivare is a learned borrowing from Latin dērīvāre "to lead off, to draw off water" (Lewis & Short), built from dē "from" and rīvus "a stream": a channel flowing out of a river.

Lesson 3 of 5.
comportare — to involve, to entail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the origin, then the Italian for to come from.

You'll want to know: comportare.
comportare — "to involve, to entail".
Questo lavoro comporta molti viaggi. This job involves a lot of travel.
Comportarsi is a different use: to behave. Comportare delle spese — to involve costs.

The word, taken apart.
comportare comes from Latin comportāre "to carry together, to bring in" (Lewis & Short), built from com- and portāre "to carry": what a choice carries along with it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
determinare — to determine.

You'll want to know: determinare.
determinare — "to determine".
Il tempo determina il nostro programma per domenica. The weather determines our plan for Sunday.
Determinato also means determined, resolute: una ragazza determinata.

The word, taken apart.
determinare is a learned borrowing from Latin dēterminō "to limit, to set a bound" (EtymDB), built on terminus "a boundary stone".

Lesson 5 of 5.
influire — to influence, to have an effect (on).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to involve, then the Italian for to determine.

You'll want to know: influire.
influire — "to influence, to have an effect (on)".
Il tempo influisce sulle nostre scelte. The weather has an effect on our choices.
Influire su qualcosa. Conjugated with -isc-: influisce.

The word, taken apart.
influire is a learned borrowing from Latin influere "to flow in" (Lewis & Short), built from in and fluere "to flow"; influenza comes from the same verb.
