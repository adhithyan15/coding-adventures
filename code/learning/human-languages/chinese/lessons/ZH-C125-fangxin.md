## You'll want to know: fàngxīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C125-FEEL125-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngxīn* — "to stop worrying; to be at ease".

> *nǐ fàngxīn wǒ hěn hǎo* — Don't worry, I'm fine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngxīn* is built from *fàng*, the word for putting things down, and *xīn* 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjangH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'to drive out' and 'to send away'.
