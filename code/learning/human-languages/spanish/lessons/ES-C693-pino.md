## You'll want to know: el pino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C693-ARBOLES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el pino** — "the pine tree".

> El camino pasa por un bosque de pinos.
>
> *The path goes through a pine forest.*

*La piña* is the pine cone, and also a pineapple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pino* is inherited from Latin *pīnus* "a pine tree" (EtymDB).
