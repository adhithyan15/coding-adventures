# ఆశ్చర్యపరచు (āścaryaparacu) — to surprise; to astonish

## You'll want to know: ఆశ్చర్యపరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C501-NAVVU501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆశ్చర్యపరచు** — *āścaryaparacu* — "to surprise; to astonish".

> ఆమె పాట అందరినీ ఆశ్చర్యపరిచింది.
>
> *āme pāṭa andarinī āścaryaparicindi.*
>
> Her song astonished everyone.

Past (I …): ఆశ్చర్యపరిచాను, *āścaryaparicānu*.

-పరచు makes a causative from a noun: ఆశ్చర్యం, surprise; ఆశ్చర్యపరచు, to surprise someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆశ్చర్యపరచు** (*āścaryaparacu*) is built from ఆశ్చర్యం, from Sanskrit *āścarya*, "wonderful" (CDIAL 1464) and *paracu*, which DEDR 3853 lists as "to cause to feel", the causative partner of *paḍu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
