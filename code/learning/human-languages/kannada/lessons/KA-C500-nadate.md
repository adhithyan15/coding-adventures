# ನಡತೆ (naḍate) — conduct; behaviour

## You'll want to know: ನಡತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C500-SVABHAVA500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಡತೆ** — *naḍate* — "conduct; behaviour".

> ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನ ನಡತೆ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *śāleyalli avana naḍate cennāgide.*
>
> His conduct at school is good.

Built on ನಡೆ, to walk: the way you walk through life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಡತೆ** (*naḍate*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3582 lists Kannada *naḍe*, "to walk, go, happen", and *naḍate*, "conduct, behaviour".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
