Urdu, chapter 259: Dish, Menu, Tangy, Fine white flour, Semolina.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پکوان (pakvān) — a dish (of food).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a tap, then the Urdu for a shower.

You'll want to know: پکوان.
پکوان — pakvān — "a dish (of food)".

The word, taken apart.
پکوان (pakvān) is usually taken from Sanskrit pakvānna, "cooked food", built from pakva, "cooked", and anna, "food". CDIAL has no entry for it.

چٹپٹا (chaṭpaṭā) — tangy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a dish, then the Urdu for a menu.

You'll want to know: چٹپٹا.
چٹپٹا — chaṭpaṭā — "tangy".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of چٹپٹا (chaṭpaṭā) is not settled. CDIAL lists only a Hindi chaṭpaṭī, "restlessness", under a reconstructed chaṭṭa, "sudden movement" (CDIAL 4969), which may or may not be the same word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سوجی (sūjī) — semolina.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for tangy, then the Urdu for fine white flour.

You'll want to know: سوجی.
سوجی — sūjī — "semolina".

The word, taken apart.
سوجی (sūjī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sūjjī, "coarse wheat meal", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13552).
