# কদর (kôdôr) — esteem; appreciation

## You'll want to know: কদর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C351-SHRODDHA351-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কদর** — *kôdôr* — "esteem; appreciation".

> এই দেশে শিল্পীদের কদর নেই।
>
> *ei deshe shilpīder kôdôr nei.*
>
> Artists are not valued in this country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কদর** (*kôdôr*) is usually traced to Arabic *qadr*, worth, through Persian: the worth others see in you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
