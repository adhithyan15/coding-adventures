## You'll want to know: o matrimónio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C312-NOIVADO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o matrimónio** — "matrimony, marriage (formal)".

> Celebraram o matrimónio numa pequena igreja.
>
> *They celebrated their marriage in a small church.*

In Brazil: *matrimônio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*matrimónio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mātrimōnium* "marriage" (EtymDB), built on *māter* "mother".
