## You'll want to know: sbucare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C442-SALITA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sbucare** — "to come out, to pop out".

> Dopo il vicolo siamo sbucati in piazza.
>
> *After the alley we came out in the square.*

*Sbucare all'improvviso* — to pop up suddenly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sbucare* is built from *s-* 'out of' and *buca* 'hole': coming out of a hole. The origin of *buca* is not settled.
