Italian, chapter 289: Chi legge, chi guarda.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il lettore — the reader.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to spread, then the Italian for to go into in depth.

You'll want to know: il lettore.
il lettore — "the reader".
Il giornale risponde alle lettere dei lettori. The paper answers its readers' letters.
A woman is la lettrice. Also a player: il lettore DVD.

The word, taken apart.
lettore is inherited from Latin lēctor "a reader" (EtymDB), from legere "to read".

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'editoriale — the editorial, the leading article.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the viewer, then the Italian for the audience.

You'll want to know: l'editoriale.
l'editoriale — "the editorial, the leading article".
Il direttore scrive l'editoriale ogni domenica. The editor writes the editorial every Sunday.
The opinion piece of the paper's editor, il direttore.

The word, taken apart.
editoriale is usually said to be modelled on English editorial, built on Latin ēditor "one who puts forth", from ēdere "to give out" (Lewis & Short).
