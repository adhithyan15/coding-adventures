Bengali, chapter 381: Making It Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ব্যবস্থা (bæbôsthā) — an arrangement; a system.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for qualified, then the Bengali for to get used to.

You'll want to know: ব্যবস্থা.
ব্যবস্থা — bæbôsthā — "an arrangement; a system".
তুমি এলে থাকার ব্যবস্থা আমি করব। tumi ele thākār bæbôsthā āmi kôrbô. If you come, I'll arrange a place for you to stay.

The word, taken apart.
ব্যবস্থা (bæbôsthā) is Sanskrit vyavasthā, an arrangement or settled order (Monier-Williams). ব্যবস্থা করা is to arrange something.

গাড়ি বিকল হলে আমাকে ফোন করো। gāṛi bikôl hôle āmāke phon kôro. Call me if the car breaks down.

The word, taken apart.
বিকল (bikôl) is Sanskrit vikala, impaired or defective (Monier-Williams). It is the word for a machine or a body part that has stopped working.

Lesson 5 of 5.
অনুশীলন করা (ônushīlôn kôrā) — to practise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for out of order, then the Bengali for skill.

You'll want to know: অনুশীলন করা.
অনুশীলন করা — ônushīlôn kôrā — "to practise".
রোজ অনুশীলন করলে তুমি ভালো খেলবে। roj ônushīlôn kôrle tumi bhālo khelbe. If you practise every day, you'll play well.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সারাদিন অনুশীলন করলেন, tini sārādin ônushīlôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
অনুশীলন করা (ônushīlôn kôrā) is Sanskrit anuśīlana, constant practice or study (Monier-Williams), with করা: to do something again and again to get it right.
