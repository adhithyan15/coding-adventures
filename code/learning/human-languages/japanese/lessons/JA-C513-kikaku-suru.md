## You'll want to know: きかくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C513-PLAN513-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きかくする** — *kikaku suru* — "to plan (an event); to organize".

> **もし かれが りょこうを きかくしたら、たのしいでしょう。** — *moshi kare ga ryokō o kikakushitara, tanoshii deshō* — "If he plans the trip, it will surely be fun."

Polite **きかくします**, *kikakushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かいしゃが りょこうを きかくしました。**, *kaisha ga ryokō o kikakushimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きかくする** (*kikaku suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *kikaku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "undertake" and *kaku*, "brush-stroke" (Middle Chinese *hweak*, the reading of this character for "to plan") (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
