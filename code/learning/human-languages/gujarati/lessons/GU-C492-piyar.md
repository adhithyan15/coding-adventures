## You'll want to know: પિયર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C492-PIYAR492-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પિયર** — *piyar* — "a married woman's parents' home".

> દિવાળીમાં મીરા પિયર જશે.
>
> *divāḷīmā̃ Mīrā piyar jaśe.*
>
> At Diwali Mira will go to her parents' home.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પિયર** (*piyar*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*pitṛghara-*, "father's house"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8180).
