Sanskrit, chapter 83: At School.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विद्यालयः (vidyālayaḥ) — a school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an island, then the Sanskrit for a shore.

You'll want to know: विद्यालयः.
विद्यालयः (vidyālayaḥ) — "a school": विद्या, "knowledge", with आलयः, "home". A school is a home of knowledge.

What you've built.
Knowledge plus home.
