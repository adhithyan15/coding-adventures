## You'll want to know: তীর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C290-DHEU290-02]; assesses=[] -->

**তীর** — *tīr* — "a riverbank; a shore".

> ঢেউ নৌকাটাকে তীরে নিয়ে এল।
>
> *ḍheu noukāṭāke tīre niye elô.*
>
> The waves carried the boat to the shore.

A second তীর, an arrow, is a different word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তীর** (*tīr*) is Sanskrit *tīra*, 'a shore, a bank' (Monier-Williams), unchanged. নদীর তীরে, on the riverbank.
