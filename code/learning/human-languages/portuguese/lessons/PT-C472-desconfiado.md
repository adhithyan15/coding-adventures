## You'll want to know: desconfiado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C472-ESPERTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**desconfiado** — "suspicious, wary".

> O cão fica desconfiado com pessoas novas.
>
> *The dog gets wary of new people.*

The participle of *desconfiar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desconfiado* is the participle of *desconfiar* "to distrust", built on *confiar*, from Latin *cōnfīdere* "to trust fully" (Lewis & Short).
