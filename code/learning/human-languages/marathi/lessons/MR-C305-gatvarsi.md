## You'll want to know: गतवर्षी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C305-GATVARSH305-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गतवर्षी** — *gatvarṣī* — "last year".

> गतवर्षी आमच्या गावात मोठी जत्रा भरली होती.
>
> *gatvarṣī āmacyā gāvāt moṭhī jatrā bharlī hotī.*
>
> Last year a big fair was held in our village.

Formal; in speech मागच्या वर्षी. The letter ञ hides inside ज्ञ: ज्ञान, knowledge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गतवर्षी** (*gatvarṣī*) is built from Sanskrit *gata*, 'gone', and *varṣa*, 'a year' (Monier-Williams), in the Marathi locative: in the year gone by.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
