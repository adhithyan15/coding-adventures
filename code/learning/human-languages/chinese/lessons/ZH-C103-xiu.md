## You'll want to know: xiū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C103-USE103-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiū* — "to repair; to fix".

> *tā huì xiū diànnǎo* — He knows how to fix computers.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiū* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to put in order' and 'to adorn'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
