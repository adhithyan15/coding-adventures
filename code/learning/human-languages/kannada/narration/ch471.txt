Kannada, chapter 471: Crowds and Noise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗದ್ದಲ (gaddala) — a din; a commotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for supplies, then the Kannada for a telephone.

You'll want to know: ಗದ್ದಲ.
ಗದ್ದಲ — gaddala — "a din; a commotion".
ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಗದ್ದಲ ಇತ್ತು. mārukaṭṭeyalli tumbā gaddala ittu. There was a lot of noise in the market.
Many voices at once; ಗಲಾಟೆ is a near twin.

The word, taken apart.
ಗದ್ದಲ (gaddala) is usually traced to the native family of gadaṟu, gaddisu, "to make a loud sound, to roar, to shout" (DEDR 1189).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಜಮಾಯಿಸು (jamāyisu) — to gather; to assemble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a telephone, then the Kannada for a din.

You'll want to know: ಜಮಾಯಿಸು.
ಜಮಾಯಿಸು — jamāyisu — "to gather; to assemble".
ಅಪಘಾತದ ನಂತರ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಜನ ಜಮಾಯಿಸಿದರು. apaghātada nantara rasteyalli jana jamāyisidaru. After the accident people gathered on the road.
Past (I …): ಜಮಾಯಿಸಿದೆ, jamāyiside.
Said of a crowd that forms.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಮಾಯಿಸು (jamāyisu) is usually traced to an Arabic word for gathering, through Hindustani jamā, "gathered, collected", with the Kannada verb ending -isu. ಜಮಾ ಮಾಡು, to deposit, comes from the same word.

You'll want to know: ದಿಟ್ಟಿಸು.
ದಿಟ್ಟಿಸು — diṭṭisu — "to stare; to gaze".
ಆ ಅಪರಿಚಿತ ನನ್ನನ್ನೇ ದಿಟ್ಟಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. ā aparicita nannannē diṭṭisuttiddanu. That stranger kept staring at me.
Past (I …): ದಿಟ್ಟಿಸಿದೆ, diṭṭiside.
The one stared at takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ದಿಟ್ಟಿಸು (diṭṭisu) is built on diṭṭi, "a gaze", usually traced to Sanskrit dṛṣṭi, "seeing, sight" (CDIAL 6520), through Prakrit, with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಜಗ್ಗು (jaggu) — to tug; to pull hard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to gather, then the Kannada for to stare.

You'll want to know: ಜಗ್ಗು.
ಜಗ್ಗು — jaggu — "to tug; to pull hard".
ಗುಂಪಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಮಗು ನನ್ನ ಕೈ ಜಗ್ಗಿತು. gumpinalli magu nanna kai jaggitu. In the crowd the child tugged at my hand.
Past (I …): ಜಗ್ಗಿದೆ, jaggide.
Also to sag under weight: ಹಗ್ಗ ಜಗ್ಗಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಗ್ಗು (jaggu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2360 lists Kannada jaggu, "to bend down, to sink under a weight", in the family of sari, "to slide". How the sense of tugging grew from it is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತುಳುಕು (tuḷuku) — to spill over; to brim over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to stare, then the Kannada for to tug.

You'll want to know: ತುಳುಕು.
ತುಳುಕು — tuḷuku — "to spill over; to brim over".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ದಿನ ಬಸ್ಸುಗಳು ಜನರಿಂದ ತುಳುಕುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು. habbada dina bassugaḷu janarinda tuḷukuttiddavu. On the festival day the buses were overflowing with people.
Past (it …): ತುಳುಕಿತು, tuḷukitu.
Of a full pot, a crowded bus or a full heart.

The word, taken apart.
ತುಳುಕು (tuḷuku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3361 lists Kannada tuḷaku, "to be scattered in drops, to spill, to run over", and tuḷuku.
