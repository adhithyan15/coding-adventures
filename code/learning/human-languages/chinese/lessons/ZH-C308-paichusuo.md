## You'll want to know: pàichūsuǒ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C308-SINCE308-05]; assesses=[] -->

*pàichūsuǒ* — "local police station".

> *wǒmen qù pàichūsuǒ bàojǐng* — We went to the police station to report it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pàichūsuǒ* is built from *pài*, 出 'out' and 所 'a place', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *pheaH*, *tsyhwit* and *srjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a branch of a river'; its sense 'to send' is not given there. The place that sends officers out.
