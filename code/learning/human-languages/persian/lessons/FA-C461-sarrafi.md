## You'll want to know: صرافی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C461-KARTKHAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صرافی** (*sarrâfi*) — "a bureau de change, an exchange office".

> در صرافی دلار را به ریال تبدیل کردیم.
>
> *Dar sarrâfi dolâr râ be riyâl tabdil kardim.*
>
> At the exchange office we changed dollars into rials.

The money changer is the *sarrâf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarrâfi* is built from *sarrâf* 'money changer', a loan usually traced to Arabic *ṣarrāf*, and the Persian ending *-i*. EtymDB does not list it.
