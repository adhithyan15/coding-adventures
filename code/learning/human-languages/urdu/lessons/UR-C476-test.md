## You'll want to know: ٹیسٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C476-DOCTOR476-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹیسٹ** — *ṭesṭ* — "a medical test".

> کل میرے خون کا ٹیسٹ ہو گا۔
>
> *kal mere khūn kā ṭesṭ ho gā.*
>
> I am having a blood test tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹیسٹ** (*ṭesṭ*) is borrowed from English *test*.
