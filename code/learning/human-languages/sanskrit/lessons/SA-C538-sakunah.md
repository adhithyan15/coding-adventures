## You'll want to know: शकुनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C538-SAKUNA538-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शकुनः** — *śakunaḥ* — "an omen".

> यदि शकुनः शुभः अस्ति, तर्हि वयं श्वः यात्रां गच्छामः।
>
> *yadi śakunaḥ śubhaḥ asti, tarhi vayaṃ śvaḥ yātrāṃ gacchāmaḥ.*
>
> If the omen is good, we set out tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शकुनः** (*śakunaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a bird, above all a large bird of good or bad omen, and so an omen; MW reports a derivation from √śak, "to be able", only as "said to be", so its origin is uncertain (MW).
