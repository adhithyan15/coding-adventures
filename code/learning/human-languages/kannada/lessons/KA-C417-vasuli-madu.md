# ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು (vasūli māḍu) — to recover dues

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C417-KANTU417-02, KA-LEX-C417-KANTU417-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for arrears, then the Kannada for savings.

## You'll want to know: ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C417-KANTU417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು** — *vasūli māḍu* — "to recover dues".

> ಬ್ಯಾಂಕ್ ಸಾಲವನ್ನು ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡಿತು.
>
> *byāṅk sālavannu vasūli māḍitu.*
>
> The bank recovered the loan.

Past (I …): ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *vasūli māḍide*.

ವಸೂಲಿ alone is collection of money owed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಸೂಲಿ ಮಾಡು** (*vasūli māḍu*) is built from *vasūli*, a loan from Hindustani *vasūl*, "collected", which comes from Arabic *wuṣūl*, "arrival, receipt", with *māḍu*, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
