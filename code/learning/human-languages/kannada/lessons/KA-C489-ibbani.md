## You'll want to know: ಇಬ್ಬನಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C489-IBBANI489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಬ್ಬನಿ** — *ibbani* — "dew".

> ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಹುಲ್ಲಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಇಬ್ಬನಿ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *beḷagge hullina mēle ibbani ittu.*
>
> In the morning there was dew on the grass.

Drops on leaves at dawn after a cold night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಬ್ಬನಿ** (*ibbani*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 522 lists Kannada *ibbani*, "fog, mist, dew".
