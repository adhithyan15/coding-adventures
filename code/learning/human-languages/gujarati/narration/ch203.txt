Gujarati, chapter 203: Setting off on a trip.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પ્રવાસ (pravās) — trip, tour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be found, then the Gujarati for to take out.

You'll want to know: પ્રવાસ.
પ્રવાસ — pravās — "trip, tour".
અમે ગયા મહિને પ્રવાસે ગયા. — ame gayā mahine pravāse gayā — "We went on a trip last month."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રવાસ (pravās) is Sanskrit pravāsa, "a stay away from home, a journey", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
નીકળવું (nīkaḷvũ) — to set out, to come out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for trip, then the Gujarati for luggage.

You'll want to know: નીકળવું.
નીકળવું — nīkaḷvũ — "to set out, to come out".
અમે સવારે ઘરેથી નીકળ્યા. — ame savāre gharethī nīkaḷyā — "We set out from home in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
નીકળવું (nīkaḷvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from niṣkalati, "comes out", a reconstructed form that CDIAL reads as a back-formation from Sanskrit niṣkālayati (CDIAL 7478).

Lesson 5 of 5.
વળાવવું (vaḷāvvũ) — to see off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to set out, then the Gujarati for to drop off.

You'll want to know: વળાવવું.
વળાવવું — vaḷāvvũ — "to see off".
મારી બહેને મને સ્ટેશન પર વળાવ્યો. — mārī bahene mane sṭeśan par vaḷāvyo — "My sister saw me off at the station."
વળવું is to turn; વળાવવું (vaḷāvvũ) is to see someone off.

The word, taken apart.
વળાવવું (vaḷāvvũ) is built on vaḷvũ, "to turn", with the causative ending -āvvũ: to send someone on his way. vaḷvũ is a tadbhava from Sanskrit válati, "turns" (CDIAL 11405).
