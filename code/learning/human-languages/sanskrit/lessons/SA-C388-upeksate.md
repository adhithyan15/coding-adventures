## You'll want to know: उपेक्षते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C388-VYAVAHARA388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपेक्षते** — *upekṣate* — "neglects; overlooks".

> यदि सः स्वास्थ्यम् उपेक्षते, तर्हि रुग्णः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi saḥ svāsthyam upekṣate, tarhi rugṇaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If he neglects his health, he will fall ill.

Past (he …): सः कार्यम् उपेक्षितवान्, *saḥ kāryam upekṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपेक्षते** (*upekṣate*) is built on *upa* and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives *upekṣ* "to overlook, to disregard, to neglect" (MW).
