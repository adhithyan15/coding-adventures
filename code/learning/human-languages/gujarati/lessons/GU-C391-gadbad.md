## You'll want to know: ગડબડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C391-GADBAD391-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગડબડ** — *gaḍbaḍ* — "a mix-up".

> જો ટિકિટની તારીખમાં ગડબડ હશે તો મુસાફરી રદ થશે.
>
> *jo ṭikiṭnī tārīkhmā̃ gaḍbaḍ haśe to musāpharī rad thaśe.*
>
> If there is a mix-up over the date on the ticket, the trip will be cancelled.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગડબડ** (*gaḍbaḍ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "confused; confusion, disorder", under a reconstructed *gaḍa-baḍa* (CDIAL 3974), a jingle of sounds for a muddle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
