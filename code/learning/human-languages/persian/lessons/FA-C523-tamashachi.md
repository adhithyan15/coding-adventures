## You'll want to know: تماشاچی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C523-THEATRE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تماشاچی** (*tamâshâchi*) — "a spectator, a member of the audience".

> تماشاچی ها برای بازیگرها دست زدند.
>
> *tamâshâchihâ barâ-ye bâzigarhâ dast zadand.*
>
> The audience applauded the actors.

*tamâshâ* 'watching' + the Turkic ending *-chi* 'one who'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tamâshâchi* is built in Persian on *tamâshâ*, 'watching', a loan from Arabic, and the ending *-chi*, 'one who', which was borrowed from Turkic. EtymDB has no entry for it.
