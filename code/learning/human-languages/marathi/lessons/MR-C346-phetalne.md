## You'll want to know: फेटाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C346-KHATRI346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फेटाळणे** — *pheṭāḷṇe* — "to reject (a claim)".

> कंपनीने कामगारांची मागणी फेटाळली.
>
> *kampnīne kāmgārāñcī māgṇī pheṭāḷlī.*
>
> The company rejected the workers' demand.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मागणी फेटाळली, *tyāne māgṇī pheṭāḷlī*.

Of claims, demands and charges; of a person or an offer, नाकारणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फेटाळणे** (*pheṭāḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
