## You'll want to know: ইস্তফা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C497-ISTOPHA497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ইস্তফা** — *istôphā* — "a resignation".

> মামা চাকরি থেকে ইস্তফা দিলেন।
>
> *Māmā chākri theke istôphā dilen.*
>
> My uncle resigned from his job.

With দেওয়া: ইস্তফা দেওয়া, to resign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ইস্তফা** (*istôphā*) is usually traced to Arabic *istiʿfā*, "asking to be let off", which reached Bengali through Persian.
