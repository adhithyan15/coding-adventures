## You'll want to know: நடுவர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C534-VETRI534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**நடுவர்** — *naṭuvar* — "a referee; an umpire".

> நடுவர் விசில் ஊதினார்.
>
> *naṭuvar vicil ūtiṉār.*
>
> The referee blew the whistle.

From நடு, the middle: the one who stands between the two sides.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நடுவர்** (*naṭuvar*) is native Dravidian: நடு (*naṭu*), "the middle, fairness" (DEDR 3584), with the personal ending -அர்.
