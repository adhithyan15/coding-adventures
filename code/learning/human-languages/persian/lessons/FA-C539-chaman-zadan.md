## You'll want to know: چمن زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C539-GARDEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**چمن زدن** (*chaman zadan*) — "to mow the lawn".

> همسایه ما هر هفته چمن میزند.
>
> *hamsâye-ye mâ har hafte chaman mizanad.*
>
> Our neighbour mows the lawn every week.

*chaman* 'lawn, grass' + *zadan* 'to strike'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chaman* is the Persian word for a lawn; EtymDB lists it beside *chamidan*, 'to stroll' (EtymDB), but its origin is not settled. *zadan*, 'to strike', is inherited.
