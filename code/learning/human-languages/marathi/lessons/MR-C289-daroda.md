## You'll want to know: दरोडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C289-GUNHA289-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दरोडा** — *daroḍā* — "a robbery; a gang raid".

> रात्री चार माणसांनी दुकानावर दरोडा टाकला.
>
> *rātrī cār māṇsānnī dukānāvar daroḍā ṭāklā.*
>
> At night four men raided the shop.

Masculine.

दरोडा पडणे, a robbery takes place; दरोडा टाकणे, to carry one out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरोडा** (*daroḍā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *daroḍā*, 'an attack by a band of robbers', under *\*daḍavaḍa*, 'an onrush' (CDIAL 6127).
