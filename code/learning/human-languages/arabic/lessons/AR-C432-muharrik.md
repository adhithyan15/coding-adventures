## You'll want to know: محرك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C432-WAQUD432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**محرك** — *muḥarrik* — "an engine; a motor".

> كان محرك السيارة صاخبا.
>
> *kāna muḥarrik as-sayyāra ṣākhiban.*
>
> The car's engine was noisy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محرك** (*muḥarrik*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-k*, "to move" (Lane). It is the Form II participle, "the thing that sets something moving".
