## You'll want to know: renunciar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C553-EMPLEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**renunciar** — "to resign, to give up".

> Renunció a su puesto en marzo.
>
> *She resigned from her post in March.*

*Renunciar a algo* — to give something up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*renunciar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *renūntiāre* 'to report back, to give up' (EtymDB), built on *nūntius* 'a messenger'.
