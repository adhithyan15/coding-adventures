## You'll want to know: درمان کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C493-DOCTOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**درمان کردن** (*darmân kardan*) — "to treat (an illness)".

> این دکتر بیماری پوست را درمان میکند.
>
> *in doktor bimâri-ye pust râ darmân mikonad.*
>
> This doctor treats skin disease.

*darmân* 'cure, treatment' + *kardan*. *darmângâh* 'clinic' is built on the same noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*darmân* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian, where it meant 'remedy, medicine' (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited too.
