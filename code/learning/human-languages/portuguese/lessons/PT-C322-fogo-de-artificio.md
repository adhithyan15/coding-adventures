## You'll want to know: o fogo de artifício
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C322-FOGUETE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o fogo de artifício** — "the fireworks".

> À meia-noite houve fogo de artifício na praia.
>
> *At midnight there were fireworks on the beach.*

Singular in Portuguese: *o fogo de artifício foi lindo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fogo* is inherited from Latin *focus* "a hearth" (EtymDB); *artifício* is a learned borrowing from Latin *artificium* "a craft, a skill", built on *ars* "art" (Lewis & Short).
