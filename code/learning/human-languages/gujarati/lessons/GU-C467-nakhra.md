## You'll want to know: નખરાં
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C467-VARTAN467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નખરાં** — *nakhrā̃* — "airs; fussy demands".

> ભોજન વખતે નાની બહેન ઘણાં નખરાં કરે છે.
>
> *bhojan vakhte nānī bahen ghaṇā̃ nakhrā̃ kare che.*
>
> At mealtimes my little sister makes a lot of fuss.

A neuter noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નખરાં** (*nakhrā̃*) is usually traced to Persian *naxra*, "coquetry, airs".
