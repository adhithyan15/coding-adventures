## You'll want to know: 希望
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C533-WISH533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**希望** — *xīwàng* — "to hope".

> **我希望明天不下雨** *wǒ xīwàng míngtiān bú xiàyǔ* — I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow.

The new character, in pieces: 希 — seven strokes in three pieces: a small cross of two strokes on top; a long bar with a long stroke falling to the left through it; and at the foot three strokes, a short vertical, a bar that turns down and hooks, and a long vertical through the middle. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**希望** (*xīwàng*) is built from 希 and 望 'to look out for', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xj+j* and *mjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to stop', 'to scatter' and, after an older dictionary, 'rare', and also with the second: 'to hope'.
