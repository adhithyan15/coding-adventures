Punjabi, chapter 114: Soap, Towel, Mattress, Bedsheet, Blanket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਾਬਣ (sābaṇ) — soap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a cooking pot, then the Punjabi for a comb.

You'll want to know: ਸਾਬਣ.
ਸਾਬਣ — sābaṇ — "soap".

The word, taken apart.
ਸਾਬਣ (sābaṇ) is a loan from Arabic ṣābūn, "soap", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṣ-b-n (Lane).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for soap, then the Punjabi for a towel.

You'll want to know: ਗੱਦਾ.
ਗੱਦਾ — gaddā — "a mattress".

The word, taken apart.
ਗੱਦਾ (gaddā) is inherited. CDIAL lists Punjabi gaddī, "cushion, pad, seat", under a reconstructed garda, "seat", which it takes, probably, from Dravidian, and says Hindi gaddā, "quilted mattress", was taken from Punjabi (CDIAL 4053).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਕੰਬਲ (kambal) — a blanket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a mattress, then the Punjabi for a bedsheet.

You'll want to know: ਕੰਬਲ.
ਕੰਬਲ — kambal — "a blanket".

The word, taken apart.
ਕੰਬਲ (kambal) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kambala, "woollen blanket" (CDIAL 2771). CDIAL notes a possible Austroasiatic origin, and says Hindi kambal was taken from Punjabi.
