## You'll want to know: પાસપોર્ટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C210-PAPERS210-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પાસપોર્ટ** — *pāsporṭ* — "passport".

> **મેં પાસપોર્ટ બતાવ્યો.** — *mẽ pāsporṭ batāvyo* — "I showed my passport."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાસપોર્ટ** (*pāsporṭ*) is a loan from English *passport*.
