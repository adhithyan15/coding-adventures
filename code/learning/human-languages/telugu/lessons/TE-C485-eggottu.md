## You'll want to know: ఎగ్గొట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C485-NAGADU485-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎగ్గొట్టు** — *eggoṭṭu* — "to dodge; to skip paying".

> అతను అద్దె ఎగ్గొట్టి వెళ్ళిపోయాడు.
>
> *atanu adde eggoṭṭi veḷḷipōyāḍu.*
>
> He went off without paying the rent.

Past (I …): ఎగ్గొట్టాను, *eggoṭṭānu*.

A colloquial word: to dodge a debt, a class or a duty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎగ్గొట్టు** (*eggoṭṭu*) is a Telugu compound: -గొట్టు, from the native కొట్టు, "to strike" (DEDR 2063), and a first part whose origin is not settled.
