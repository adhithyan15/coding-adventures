## You'll want to know: तिनका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तिनका** — *tinkā* — "a straw; a blade of grass".

> हवा में तिनके उड़ रहे थे।
>
> *havā meṁ tinke uṛ rahe the.*
>
> Bits of straw were flying in the wind.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिनका** (*tinkā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *tṛṇa*, "grass, a blade of grass, a straw" (CDIAL 5906), which lists Hindi *tinkā*: in sayings it is the smallest thing there is.
