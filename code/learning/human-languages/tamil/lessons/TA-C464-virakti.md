## You'll want to know: விரக்தி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C464-CAPALAM464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**விரக்தி** — *virakti* — "despair; disillusionment".

> பல முறை தோற்றதால் அவருக்கு விரக்தி வந்தது.
>
> *pala muṟai tōṟṟatāl avarukku virakti vantatu.*
>
> Having failed many times, he fell into despair.

விரக்தியான பேச்சு, bitter, hopeless talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**விரக்தி** (*virakti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *virakti*, "change of feeling, loss of interest" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
