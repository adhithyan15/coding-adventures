## Grammar Lens: one journey word by ear

> [YOU HEAR: *yātrā*; YOU SAY: *yātrā*] — journey; travel

> [YOU HEAR: *rel nāl yātrā*; YOU SAY: *rel nāl yātrā*] — travel by train

Treat the second line as one useful spoken travel chunk. Its Gurmukhi form
waits until the next lesson takes the new opening letter from this known word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Yātrā* is Sanskrit *yātrā*, “journey, procession,” taken over as a learned loan.
