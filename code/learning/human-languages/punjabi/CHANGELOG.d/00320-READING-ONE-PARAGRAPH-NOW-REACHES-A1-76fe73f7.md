## Reading — one paragraph now reaches A1

Punjabi's first connected passage grows from 43 to 72 words by turning its two
people into a deliberate parallel: Aman speaks Punjabi and thinks it is good;
then Manan does too; then the narrator retrieves the same forms before the
paragraph returns to its question about where Manan is.

Every added Gurmukhi token already appeared in the same paid-for passage. The
extension adds no vocabulary, but it gives the learner repeated subject changes
across known sentence frames. Measured against Punjabi's own task shape, A1
reading moves from **0/3 to 1/3** while pre-A1 remains **3/3**. The new
`punjabi/A1` floor records that gain.
