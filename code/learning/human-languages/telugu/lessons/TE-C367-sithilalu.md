## You'll want to know: శిథిలాలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C367-TAVVAKALU367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**శిథిలాలు** — *śithilālu* — "ruins".

> అడవిలో పాత కోట శిథిలాలు ఉన్నాయి.
>
> *aḍavilō pāta kōṭa śithilālu unnāyi.*
>
> There are ruins of an old fort in the forest.

A plural noun; శిథిల, loose or decayed, makes the adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శిథిలాలు** (*śithilālu*) is built on Sanskrit *śithila*, "loose, slack" (Monier-Williams): what has come loose and fallen.
