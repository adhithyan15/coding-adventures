# इ (i) — the letter inside दिखाइए

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-140, HI-SCRIPT-C128-I-SIGN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ़ and ◌ि once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **दिखाइए** (*dikhāie*), "please show me".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: इ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-C128-I-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इ** — the letter *i*.

It is inside **दिखाइए** (*dikhāie*), "please show me". After **ा** comes another
vowel, and a vowel that follows a vowel has no consonant to ride on, so it is
written as its own letter. **ि**, from the last lesson, is the same vowel as a sign.

## Writing: इ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-C128-I-01] -->

- **1.** start here: descend the upright from the headline
- **2.** turn left and curve around the upper bowl without lifting
- **3.** without lifting, sweep right through the waist and around the lower bowl
- **4.** finish down-right through the tail without lifting
- **5.** lift, then draw the shirorekha left-to-right — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari इ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–2, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.
