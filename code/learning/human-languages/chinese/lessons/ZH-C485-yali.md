## You'll want to know: yālì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C485-MIND485-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yālì* — "pressure; stress".

> *wǒ zuìjìn yālì hěn dà* — I've been under a lot of stress lately.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yālì* is built from *yā* 'to press' and 力 'strength', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'aep* and *lik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to press down' and the second 'sinew'.
