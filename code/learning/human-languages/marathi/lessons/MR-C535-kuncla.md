## You'll want to know: कुंचला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C535-CHITRA535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंचला** — *kuñclā* — "a paintbrush".

> कलाकाराने बारीक कुंचल्याने डोळे रंगवले.
>
> *kalākārāne bārīk kuñclyāne ḍoḷe raṅgavle.*
>
> The artist painted the eyes with a fine brush.

Masculine.

The oblique is कुंचल्या-: कुंचल्याने.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंचला** (*kuñclā*) probably belongs with Marathi *kũcā* and *kũcī*, "brush", which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit *kūrca*, "a bunch of grass", whose derivatives meant a painter's brush (CDIAL 3408). CDIAL lists no कुंचला itself, so the -ला form is not settled.
