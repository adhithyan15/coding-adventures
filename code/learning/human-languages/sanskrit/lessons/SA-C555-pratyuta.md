## You'll want to know: प्रत्युत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C555-PRERANA555-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्युत** — *pratyuta* — "on the contrary".

> यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि हानिः न, प्रत्युत लाभः भवति।
>
> *yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi hāniḥ na, pratyuta lābhaḥ bhavati.*
>
> If it rains, it is no loss; on the contrary, it is a gain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्युत** (*pratyuta*) is built from *prati*, "back, against", and *uta*, "and, even"; MW gives *pratyuta* as on the contrary, rather (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
