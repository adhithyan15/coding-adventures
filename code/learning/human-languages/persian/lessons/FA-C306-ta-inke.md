## You'll want to know: تا اینکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C306-TADRIJ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تا اینکه** (*tâ inke*) — "until finally, until".

> منتظر ماندیم تا اینکه اتوبوس آمد.
>
> *Montazer mândim tâ inke otobus âmad.*
>
> We waited until the bus finally came.

In a story it marks the turn: *... tâ inke yek ruz ...* "... until one day ...".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tâ* "until" is inherited: EtymDB traces it through Middle Persian to Old Persian *yātā*, "until, so that". *inke* is *in* "this" + *ke* "that".
