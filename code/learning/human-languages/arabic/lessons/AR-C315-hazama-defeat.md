## You'll want to know: هزم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C315-NASR315-04]; assesses=[] -->

**هزم** — *hazama* — "to defeat".

> هزم الفريق الصغير الفريق الكبير.
>
> *hazama al-farīq aṣ-ṣaghīr al-farīq al-kabīr.*
>
> The small team beat the big team.

Present: يهزم, *yahzim*.

The passive هزم, *huzima*, is to be defeated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هزم** (*hazama*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-z-m* (Lane), "to rout; to put an army to flight": Lane gives *inhazama*, of an army that was routed.

## What you've built
