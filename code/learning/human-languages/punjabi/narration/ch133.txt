Punjabi, chapter 133: Rooster, Hen, Donkey, Owl, Crow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੁੱਕੜ (kukkaṛ) — a rooster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a spider, then the Punjabi for a squirrel.

You'll want to know: ਕੁੱਕੜ.
ਕੁੱਕੜ — kukkaṛ — "a rooster".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਕੁੱਕੜ (kukkaṛ) mean? ("A rooster".) Say it once more.
