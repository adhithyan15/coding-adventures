## You'll want to know: 话
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C53-SPEAK53-02]; assesses=[] -->

**话** — *huà* — "words; speech".

> **他说中国话** *tā shuō Zhōngguó huà* — He speaks Chinese.

The new character, in pieces: 话 — 讠 on the left; on the right a short fall, a bar and a vertical, standing on 口. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**话** (*huà*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'talk'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
