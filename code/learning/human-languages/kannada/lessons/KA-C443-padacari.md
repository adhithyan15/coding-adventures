# ಪಾದಚಾರಿ (pādacāri) — a pedestrian

## You'll want to know: ಪಾದಚಾರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C443-SANCHARA443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾದಚಾರಿ** — *pādacāri* — "a pedestrian".

> ಪಾದಚಾರಿಗಳು ರಸ್ತೆಯ ಅಂಚಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಡೆಯಬೇಕು.
>
> *pādacārigaḷu rasteya añcinalli naḍeyabēku.*
>
> Pedestrians should walk at the edge of the road.

Plural: ಪಾದಚಾರಿಗಳು, *pādacārigaḷu*.

ಪಾದ, a foot, plus -ಚಾರಿ, one who goes: one who goes on foot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾದಚಾರಿ** (*pādacāri*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pādacārin*, "going on foot, a pedestrian" (Monier-Williams), from *pāda*, "a foot", and *cārin*, "one who moves".
