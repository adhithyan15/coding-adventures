## You'll want to know: अधितिष्ठति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C353-SHASANA353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अधितिष्ठति** — *adhitiṣṭhati* — "presides over; governs".

> मन्त्री सभाम् अधितिष्ठति।
>
> *mantrī sabhām adhitiṣṭhati.*
>
> The minister presides over the assembly.

Past (he …): सः सभाम् अधिष्ठितवान्, *saḥ sabhām adhiṣṭhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधितिष्ठति** (*adhitiṣṭhati*) is built on *adhi*, "over", and the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives *adhi-√ṣṭhā* "to superintend, to govern" (MW).
