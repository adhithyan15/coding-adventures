## You'll want to know: huàzhuāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C164-READY164-04]; assesses=[] -->

*huàzhuāng* — "to put on make-up".

> *wǒ māma měi cì chūmén yǐqián dōu huàzhuāng* — My mum puts on make-up every time before she goes out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huàzhuāng* is built from *huà* 'to change' and *zhuāng* 'to adorn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwaeH* and *tsrjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
