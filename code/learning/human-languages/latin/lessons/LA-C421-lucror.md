## You'll want to know: lucror, lucrārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C421-COMMERCIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**lucror, lucrārī** — "to gain, to make a profit".

> Ille mercātor multum lucrātus est.
>
> *That merchant made a lot of profit.*

Deponent: *lucror, lucrārī, lucrātus sum*, built on *lucrum*, gain. English *lucrative*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lucror* is built on *lucrum* "gain, profit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *lucrative* comes from it.
