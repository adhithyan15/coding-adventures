## You'll want to know: धूजणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C222-COLD222-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धूजणो** — *dhūjṇo* — "to shiver; to tremble".

> रात ठंडी है अर हूं धूजूं हूं।
>
> *rāt ṭhaṇḍī hai ar hū̃ dhūjū̃ hū̃.*
>
> The night is cold and I'm shivering.

Past (he …): वो धूज्यो, *vo dhūjyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूजणो** (*dhūjṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhūyate*, "is shaken"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *dhujvũ*, "to tremble", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6863).
