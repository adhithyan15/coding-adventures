## You'll want to know: たっきゅう

**たっきゅう** — *takkyū* — "table tennis".

> **そふは たっきゅうが すきです。** — *sofu wa takkyū ga suki desu* — "My grandfather loves table tennis."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たっきゅう** (*takkyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ta*, "table" (Middle Chinese *traewk*) and *kyū*, "ball" (*gjuw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
