## You'll want to know: claustrum, claustrī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C304-COMPREHENDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**claustrum, claustrī** — "a bolt, a bar; a barrier".

> Fūr claustra portae nocte remōvit.
>
> *At night the thief removed the bolts of the door.*

Neuter, from *claudō*, 'to shut' (taught); most often plural, *claustra*, the bars of a door or gate. English *cloister*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**claustrum** is built on *claudō*, "to shut" (EtymDB), with a tool ending: the thing that shuts. English *cloister* comes from it through French, and *claustrophobia* is built on it.
