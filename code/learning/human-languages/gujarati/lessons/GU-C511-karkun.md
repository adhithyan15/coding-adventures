## You'll want to know: કારકુન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C511-MAJUR511-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કારકુન** — *kārkun* — "a clerk".

> કચેરીમાં કારકુન આખો દિવસ કાગળો લખે છે.
>
> *kacerīmā̃ kārkun ākho divas kāgḷo lakhe che.*
>
> In the office the clerk writes papers all day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કારકુન** (*kārkun*) is usually traced to Persian *kārkun*, "one who does the work, an agent", built on *kār*, "work".
