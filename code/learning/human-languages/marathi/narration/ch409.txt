Marathi, chapter 409: Sailors and Tides.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खलाशी (khalāśī) — a sailor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to toss about, then the Marathi for an anchor.

You'll want to know: खलाशी.
खलाशी — khalāśī — "a sailor".
जहाजावर वीस खलाशी काम करतात. jahājāvar vīs khalāśī kām kartāt. Twenty sailors work on the ship.
Masculine.
The plural is the same in the direct case: एक खलाशी (khalāśī), वीस खलाशी (khalāśī).

The word, taken apart.
खलाशी (khalāśī) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to khalāṣī, a word for a ship's hand.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ओहोटी (ohoṭī) — low tide; an ebb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an anchor, then the Marathi for a sailor.

You'll want to know: ओहोटी.
ओहोटी — ohoṭī — "low tide; an ebb".
ओहोटीला वाळूवर खेकडे दिसतात. ohoṭīlā vāḷūvar khekaḍe distāt. At low tide you can see crabs on the sand.
Feminine.
Time words take -ला: ओहोटीला, at low tide.

The word, taken apart.
ओहोटी (ohoṭī) is inherited, from the family of avaghaṭṭati, "decreases, recedes", where CDIAL lists Marathi ohaṭṇẽ, "to abate, ebb" (CDIAL 748).

Lesson 3 of 5.
उधाण (udhāṇ) — a sea surge; a spring tide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sailor, then the Marathi for low tide.

You'll want to know: उधाण.
उधाण — udhāṇ — "a sea surge; a spring tide".
आज समुद्राला मोठं उधाण आलं. āj samudrālā moṭhaṁ udhāṇ ālaṁ. Today the sea surged high.
Neuter.
उधाण (udhāṇ) येणे, a surge comes; it also names a rush of joy: आनंदाला उधाण (udhāṇ) आलं.

The word, taken apart.
उधाण (udhāṇ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi udhāṇ, "spring-tide", under uddhāna, "rising up" (CDIAL 2015).

Lesson 4 of 5.
खवळणे (khavaḷṇe) — to grow rough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for low tide, then the Marathi for a sea surge.

You'll want to know: खवळणे.
खवळणे — khavaḷṇe — "to grow rough".
संध्याकाळी समुद्र अचानक खवळला. sandhyākāḷī samudra acānak khavaḷlā. In the evening the sea suddenly grew rough.
Past (he …): तो खवळला, to khavaḷlā.
For the sea and for people: तो खवळला, he flared up.

The word, taken apart.
खवळणे (khavaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb for the sea, or a temper, becoming wild; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
हिंदकळणे (hindkaḷṇe) — to slosh about; to rock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sea surge, then the Marathi for to grow rough.

You'll want to know: हिंदकळणे.
हिंदकळणे — hindkaḷṇe — "to slosh about; to rock".
लाटांवर होडी हिंदकळत होती. lāṭāṁvar hoḍī hindkaḷat hotī. The boat was rocking on the waves.
Past (he …): तो हिंदकळला, to hindkaḷlā.
For water moving in a pot or a tank, and for a boat on waves.

The word, taken apart.
हिंदकळणे (hindkaḷṇe) may belong with Sanskrit hindolayati, "swings", where CDIAL lists Marathi hĩḍaḷṇẽ, "to jog, shake" (CDIAL 14095); the link is not settled.
