## You'll want to know: 面子
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C358-MANNERS358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**面子** — *miànzi* — "face; your standing in others' eyes".

> **他很要面子** *tā hěn yào miànzi* — He cares a lot about saving face.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**面子** (*miànzi*) is built from 面 'the face' and 子, a noun ending, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjienH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to face' and the second 'a child'. The second is said light here.
