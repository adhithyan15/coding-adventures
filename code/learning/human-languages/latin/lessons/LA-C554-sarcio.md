## You'll want to know: sarciō, sarcīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C554-SUBS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sarciō, sarcīre** — "to mend, patch".

> Avia tunicam nepōtis sarcit.
>
> *Grandmother mends her grandson's tunic.*

*sarciō, sarcīre, sarsī, sartum*. Of clothes, roofs and friendships.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sarciō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to mend, make whole" (EtymDB), though Lewis & Short calls it doubtful.
