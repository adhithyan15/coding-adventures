# я был рад, я устал, мне было скучно — how it felt

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C34-FEEL-01, RU-LEX-C216-KACH216-03, RU-LEX-C216-KACH216-04, RU-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-PERFECTIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "joy", "happy" and "sad". (*Rádost. Schastlívyy.
Grústnyy.*) Then "I've missed the train". (*Ya opozdál na póyezd.*) A
trip has problems, and it has feelings.

## Grammar Lens: рад, glad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-RAD-SHORT-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-BYL-PAST-01, RU-GRAMMAR-CHTO-COMPLEMENTISER-01, RU-LEX-C232-ACT232-03] -->

**рад** — *rad* — "glad". It is a short adjective: it does not stand
before a noun, only after the person, and it agrees: **рад** for a man, **рада**
(*ráda*) for a woman, **рады** (*rády*) for more than one.

> **Я рад, что ты позвонил.** — *ya rad, shto ty pozvoníl* — I'm glad
> you rang.
> **Я была рада, что ты позвонил.** — *ya bylá ráda, shto ty pozvoníl* —
> I was glad you rang.

In the present there is no verb; in the past, **был** agrees with it.

## Grammar Lens: я устал, I'm tired
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-USTAL-STATE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-05, RU-LEX-C148-ZDOROV148-05, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-L-GENDER-01] -->

> **Я устал.** — *ya ustál* — I'm tired. A woman: **Я устала.**

This is the perfective past of *устать*, the partner of *уставать*: "I
have got tired". The result is the state you are in now, so a past form
says "I am tired" today. *Усталый* is the adjective for a tired face or a
tired voice.

## Grammar Lens: мне было скучно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-BYLO-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-KAZHETSYA-01, RU-LEX-C126-QUAL126-05, RU-LEX-C126-QUAL126-04, RU-LEX-C127-QUAL127-01, RU-LEX-C114-THINGS114-03] -->

How a thing felt goes on the "to me" form, with an *-о* word and
**было**:

> **На концерте мне было скучно.** — *na kontsérte mne býlo skúchno* — I
> was bored at the concert.
> **Мне было интересно и весело.** — *mne býlo interésno i véselo* — It
> was interesting, and I had fun.

*Скучно* is said *skúshno*: the *ч* is said as *ш* in this word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C34-FEEL-01] -->

**рад** goes back to Proto-Slavic *\*radŭ*, "glad" (EtymDB 2.1). **Радость**,
"joy", is the same root with a noun ending: the gladness itself.
