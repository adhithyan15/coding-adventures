## You'll want to know: रगड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C434-SHUDDH434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रगड़ना** — *ragaṛnā* — "to rub".

> सुनार ने सोने को पत्थर पर रगड़ा।
>
> *sunār ne sone ko patthar par ragaṛā.*
>
> The goldsmith rubbed the gold on a stone.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने हाथ रगड़े, *usne hāth ragaṛe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रगड़ना** (*ragaṛnā*) goes back to *\*ragg*, "rub", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10558), which lists Hindi *ragaṛnā*. Goldsmiths test gold by the streak it leaves when rubbed on a dark stone.
