## You'll want to know: ज्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C527-DHANURDHARA527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्या** — *jyā* — "a bowstring".

> धनुर्धरः ज्यां कर्षति।
>
> *dhanurdharaḥ jyāṃ karṣati.*
>
> The archer draws the bowstring.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्या** (*jyā*) is the word MW records for a bowstring, and MW compares Greek *bios*, "a bow" (MW).
