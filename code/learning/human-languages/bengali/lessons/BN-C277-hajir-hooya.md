## You'll want to know: হাজির হওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C277-SAKSHI277-05]; assesses=[] -->

**হাজির হওয়া** — *hājir hôoyā* — "to turn up; to be present".

> পরের দিন সাক্ষী থানায় হাজির হল।
>
> *pôrer din sākkhī thānāy hājir hôlô.*
>
> The next day the witness turned up at the police station.

Past (he/she …): সেই সাক্ষী থানায় হাজির হল, *sei sākkhī thānāy hājir hôlô*.

With থাকা it means to be there: সবাই হাজির ছিল, everyone was there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হাজির হওয়া** (*hājir hôoyā*) puts হাজির, usually traced to Arabic *ḥāḍir*, 'present, in attendance', which reached Bengali through Persian, before হওয়া, to become: to become present, to turn up.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
