Gujarati, chapter 85: Bus, Train and Plane.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બસ (bas) — a bus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an open ground, then the Gujarati for a bridge.

You'll want to know: બસ.
બસ (bas) — "a bus".

What you've built.
One more word for bus, train and plane.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bas]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bas, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bas]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for an open ground, then the Gujarati for a bridge, then say bas again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does બસ (bas) mean? ("A bus".) Say it once more.
