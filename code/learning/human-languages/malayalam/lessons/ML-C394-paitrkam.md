# പൈതൃകം (paitṛkaṁ) — heritage; an inheritance

## You'll want to know: പൈതൃകം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C394-SVATHTHU394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പൈതൃകം** — *paitṛkaṁ* — "heritage; an inheritance".

> ആ പഴയ വീട് ഞങ്ങളുടെ കുടുംബത്തിന്റെ പൈതൃകം ആണ്.
>
> *ā paḻaya vīṭŭ ñaṅṅaḷuṭe kuṭumbattinṟe paitṛkaṁ āṇŭ.*
>
> That old house is our family's heritage.

What the fathers left: a house, a craft, a custom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പൈതൃകം** (*paitṛkaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paitṛka*, "belonging to a father, paternal, ancestral" (Monier-Williams), from *pitṛ*, "a father".

One more word for telling what happened.
