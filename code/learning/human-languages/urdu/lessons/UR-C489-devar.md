## You'll want to know: دیور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C489-KIN489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دیور** — *devar* — "a husband's younger brother".

> میرا دیور کالج میں پڑھتا ہے۔
>
> *merā devar kālij meṅ paṛhtā hai.*
>
> My husband's younger brother is at college.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دیور** (*devar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dēvara*, "husband's younger brother" (CDIAL 6546).
