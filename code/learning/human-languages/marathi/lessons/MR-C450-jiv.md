## You'll want to know: जीव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C450-MAN450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जीव** — *jīv* — "life; a living being".

> त्या लहान पक्ष्यात अजून जीव होता.
>
> *tyā lahān pakṣyāt ajūn jīv hotā.*
>
> That little bird still had life in it.

Masculine.

Masculine. जीव लावणे, to grow fond of someone; जिवंत, alive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जीव** (*jīv*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *jīva*, "alive, a living being"; CDIAL lists Marathi *jīv*, "living being, energy", under it (CDIAL 5239).
