## You'll want to know: проездной
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C417-MARSHRUTKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**проездной** (*proyezdnóy*) — "a travel pass, a season ticket".

> У мамы проездной на месяц.
>
> *U mámy proyezdnóy na mésyats.*
>
> Mum has a monthly travel pass.

An adjective used as a noun, short for *проездной билет*; it declines like an adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*проездной* is built on *проезд* 'a fare, a passage' with an adjective ending, from *про-* and the root of *ездить* 'to ride'. EtymDB lists *проезд* without a deeper source.
