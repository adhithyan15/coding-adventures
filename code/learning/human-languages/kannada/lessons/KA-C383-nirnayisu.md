# ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸು (nirṇayisu) — to determine; to judge

## You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C383-SANDEHA383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸು** — *nirṇayisu* — "to determine; to judge".

> ಪುರಾವೆಗಳನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಅವರು ಸತ್ಯವನ್ನು ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *purāvegaḷannu nōḍi avaru satyavannu nirṇayisidaru.*
>
> Looking at the evidence, they determined the truth.

Past (I …): ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸಿದೆ, *nirṇayiside*.

ನಿರ್ಣಯ, a decision or a resolution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರ್ಣಯಿಸು** (*nirṇayisu*) is built on Sanskrit *nirṇaya*, "complete ascertainment, decision" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
