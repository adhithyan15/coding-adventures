Marathi, chapter 416: Washermen and Porters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खुंटी (khuṇṭī) — a peg; a hook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to spread out bedding, then the Marathi for to cover oneself.

You'll want to know: खुंटी.
खुंटी — khuṇṭī — "a peg; a hook".
दाराच्या मागे खुंटीवर पिशवी टांगली आहे. dārācyā māge khuṇṭīvar piśvī ṭāṅglī āhe. A bag is hanging on a peg behind the door.
Feminine.
A peg in the wall for clothes and bags; खुंटीवर, on the peg.

The word, taken apart.
खुंटी (khuṇṭī) is a Marathi word for a wall peg; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
धोबी (dhobī) — a washerman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to cover oneself, then the Marathi for a peg.

You'll want to know: धोबी.
धोबी — dhobī — "a washerman".
धोबी दर आठवड्याला कपडे नेतो. dhobī dar āṭhvaḍyālā kapḍe neto. The washerman takes the clothes every week.
Masculine.
The Marathi word परीट names the same trade.

The word, taken apart.
धोबी (dhobī) is a loan from Hindi dhobī, "washerman": CDIAL lists it under dhauvati, "washes", and notes that the Hindi word spread to the neighbouring languages, Marathi among them (CDIAL 6886).

Lesson 3 of 5.
पिळणे (piḷṇe) — to wring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a peg, then the Marathi for a washerman.

You'll want to know: पिळणे.
पिळणे — piḷṇe — "to wring".
तिने ओला सदरा पिळला. tine olā sadrā piḷlā. She wrung out the wet shirt.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पिळलं, tyāne piḷlaṁ.
Also for fruit: लिंबू पिळणे (piḷṇe), to squeeze a lemon.

The word, taken apart.
पिळणे (piḷṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi piḷṇẽ, "to squeeze", under Sanskrit pīḍayati, "presses, squeezes" (CDIAL 8226).

Lesson 4 of 5.
हमाल (hamāl) — a porter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a washerman, then the Marathi for to wring.

You'll want to know: हमाल.
हमाल — hamāl — "a porter".
स्टेशनवर हमालाने आमचं सामान उचललं. sṭeśanvar hamālāne āmcaṁ sāmān ucallaṁ. At the station a porter lifted our luggage.
Masculine.
At stations they wear a red shirt and a brass badge.

The word, taken apart.
हमाल (hamāl) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic ḥammāl, "a carrier of loads".

Lesson 5 of 5.
शिंपडणे (śimpaḍṇe) — to sprinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to wring, then the Marathi for a porter.

You'll want to know: शिंपडणे.
शिंपडणे — śimpaḍṇe — "to sprinkle".
इस्त्रीआधी तो कपड्यांवर पाणी शिंपडतो. istrīādhī to kapaḍyāṁvar pāṇī śimpaḍto. Before ironing he sprinkles water on the clothes.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने शिंपडलं, tyāne śimpaḍlaṁ.
The place sprinkled takes -वर: अंगणात पाणी शिंपडणे (śimpaḍṇe), to sprinkle water in the yard.

The word, taken apart.
शिंपडणे (śimpaḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi śĩpaḍṇẽ, "to sprinkle, scatter", under Sanskrit sikta, "sprinkled, wetted" (CDIAL 13388).
