Tamil, chapter 105: Signature, Work, School.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
கையெழுத்து (kaiyeḻuttu) — a signature; handwriting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an address, then the Tamil for a birthday.

You'll want to know: கையெழுத்து.
கையெழுத்து (kaiyeḻuttu) — "a signature", and "handwriting", literally "hand-writing".
கை is "hand"; எழுத்து is "writing". இங்கே கையெழுத்துப் போடுங்கள் — "please sign here".

What you've built.
A signature, built from hand and writing.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kaiyeḻuttu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say iṅkē kaiyeḻuttup pōṭuṅkaḷ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for an address, then the Tamil for a birthday, then say kaiyeḻuttu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does கையெழுத்து (kaiyeḻuttu) mean? ("A signature; handwriting".) Say it once more.
