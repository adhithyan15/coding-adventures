## You'll want to know: ときどき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C169-OFTEN169-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ときどき** — *tokidoki* — "sometimes".

> **らいねんは ときどき うみに いきます。** — *rainen wa tokidoki umi ni ikimasu* — "Next year I will sometimes go to the sea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ときどき** (*tokidoki*) is native Japanese, built from *toki*, "time", said twice with the second one voiced to *doki*: time and again. It is inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
