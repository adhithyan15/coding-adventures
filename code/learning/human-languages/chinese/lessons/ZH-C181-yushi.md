## You'll want to know: yùshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C181-BED181-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yùshì* — "bathroom".

> *wǒmen jiā yǒu liǎng jiān yùshì* — We have two bathrooms.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùshì* is built from *yù* 'to bathe' and *shì* 'room', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yowk* and *syit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
