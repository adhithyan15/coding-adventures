## You'll want to know: あんがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C356-JOUKEN356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**あんがい** — *angai* — "unexpectedly; surprisingly".

> **はじめたら、あんがい かんたんでしょう。** — *hajimetara, angai kantan deshō* — "Once you start, it will probably be surprisingly easy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あんがい** (*angai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *an*, "plan" (Middle Chinese *'anH*) and *gai*, "outside" (*ngwajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
