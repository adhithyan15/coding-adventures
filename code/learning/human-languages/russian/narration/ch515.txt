Russian, chapter 515: Piano, Cello and Flute.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
рояль (royál) — a grand piano.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for flippers, then the Russian for to splash about.

You'll want to know: рояль.
рояль (royál) — "a grand piano".
В зале стоит большой чёрный рояль. V zále stoít bolshóy chyórnyy royál. There is a big black grand piano in the hall.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists рояль (royál), glossed 'grand piano', without a source; it is usually traced to French royal 'royal'.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: виолончель.
виолончель (violonchél) — "a cello".
Моя сестра играет на виолончели. Moyá sestrá igráyet na violonchéli. My sister plays the cello.
Feminine. Играть на виолончели.

The word, taken apart.
виолончель (violonchél) comes from Italian violoncello (EtymDB), a diminutive of violone 'double bass'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
настраивать (nastráivat) — to tune; to set up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a flute, then the Russian for a string.

You'll want to know: настраивать.
настраивать (nastráivat) — "to tune; to set up".
Перед концертом скрипач настраивает скрипку. Péred kontsértom skripách nastráivayet skrípku. Before the concert the violinist tunes his violin.
Imperfective; perfective настроить. Also to set up a device.

The word, taken apart.
настраивать (nastráivat) is built on на- and строить 'to build, to arrange'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
