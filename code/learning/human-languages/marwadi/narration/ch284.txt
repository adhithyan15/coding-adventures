Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 284: My School Bag.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
बस्तो (basto) — a school bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for embroidery, then the Marwadi for to decorate.

You'll want to know: बस्तो.
बस्तो — basto — "a school bag".
म्हारो बस्तो भारी है। mhāro basto bhārī hai. My school bag is heavy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बस्तो (basto) is usually traced to Persian basta, "a bundle"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.

पाटी — pāṭī — "a slate".
म्हारी पाटी काळी है। mhārī pāṭī kāḷī hai. My slate is black.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पाटी (pāṭī) is inherited from Sanskrit paṭṭikā, "a slab, a tablet"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pāṭī, "plank", and Kumaoni pāṭī, "board, writing board" (CDIAL 7699).

Lesson 5 of 6.
भणणो (bhaṇṇo) — to study.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for ink, then the Marwadi for an inkpot.

You'll want to know: भणणो.
भणणो — bhaṇṇo — "to study".
हूं स्कूल मांय भणूं हूं। hū̃ skūl mā̃y bhaṇū̃ hū̃. I study at school.
Past (I …): म्हैं किताब भणी, mhaĩ kitāb bhaṇī.

The word, taken apart.
भणणो (bhaṇṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit bhaṇati, "calls aloud, speaks"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bhaṇaï, "recites", and Gujarati bhaṇvũ, "to say, repeat, study" (CDIAL 9383). It is the Marwadi word for studying.
