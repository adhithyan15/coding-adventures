## You'll want to know: अपरिचितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C414-DIGBHRAMA414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अपरिचितः** — *aparicitaḥ* — "unfamiliar; strange".

> एतत् नगरं मम अपरिचितम् अस्ति।
>
> *etat nagaraṃ mama aparicitam asti.*
>
> This city is unfamiliar to me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपरिचितः** (*aparicitaḥ*) is built from *a*, "not", and *paricita*, "known, familiar", from *pari* and the root √ci, "to gather"; MW gives *aparicita* as unacquainted, unknown (MW).
