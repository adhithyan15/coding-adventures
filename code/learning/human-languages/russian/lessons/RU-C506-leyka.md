## You'll want to know: лейка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C506-VEGETABLES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**лейка** (*léyka*) — "a watering can".

> Девочка поливает цветы из лейки.
>
> *Dévochka poliváyet tsvetý iz léyki.*
>
> The girl is watering the flowers with a watering can.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*лейка* is built on the root of *лить* 'to pour'; EtymDB has no entry.
