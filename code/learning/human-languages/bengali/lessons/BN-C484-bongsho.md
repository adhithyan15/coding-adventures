## You'll want to know: বংশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C484-SONTAN484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বংশ** — *bôngshô* — "a family line; lineage".

> আমাদের বংশ এই গ্রামে একশো বছর ধরে আছে।
>
> *āmāder bôngshô ei grāme eksho bôchhôr dhôre āchhe.*
>
> Our family line has been in this village for a hundred years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বংশ** (*bôngshô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vaṃśa*, both "a bamboo cane" and "a lineage" (Monier-Williams); বাঁশ (taught), bamboo, is the same word worn down.
