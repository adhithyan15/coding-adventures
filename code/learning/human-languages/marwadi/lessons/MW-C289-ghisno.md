## You'll want to know: घिसणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C289-MASIN289-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घिसणो** — *ghisṇo* — "to rub smooth; to rub".

> काको लकड़ी घिसै है।
>
> *kāko lakṛī ghisai hai.*
>
> My uncle rubs the wood smooth.

Past (I …): म्हैं लकड़ी घिसी, *mhaĩ lakṛī ghisī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घिसणो** (*ghisṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gharṣati*, "rubs"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *ghisnā*, "to rub", and Gujarati *ghasvũ*, "to rub, polish" (CDIAL 4450).
