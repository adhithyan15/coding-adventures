## Script — a cross with two strokes above and two below
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-MI-RICE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 米

**One piece, six strokes, built round a cross.**

**Two short strokes first,** slanting in toward the middle: a dot from the upper left, then a short stroke from the upper right falling to the left.

**The cross.** A long horizontal, then a vertical down through its middle.

**The legs, from the crossing.** A stroke falling to the lower left, then one falling to the lower right.

2 + 2 + 2 = six.
