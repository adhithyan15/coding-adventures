## You'll want to know: дешеветь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C396-TSENY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**дешеветь** (*deshevét*) — "to get cheaper".

> Летом фрукты дешевеют.
>
> *Létom frúkty deshevéyut.*
>
> In summer fruit gets cheaper.

Imperfective; perfective *подешеветь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**дешеветь** is built on *дешёвый* "cheap": to become cheap. *Дешёвый* goes back to Old Russian *deševŭ*, which is usually derived from *десить* "to find, to come upon": what is cheap is easily come by.
