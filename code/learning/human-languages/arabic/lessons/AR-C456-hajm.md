## You'll want to know: حجم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C456-HAJM456-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حجم** — *ḥajm* — "volume; size; bulk".

> حجم الحقيبة صغير جدا.
>
> *ḥajm al-ḥaqība ṣaghīr jiddan.*
>
> The size of the bag is very small.

*maqās* is a clothing size; *ḥajm* is how big a thing is.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حجم** (*ḥajm*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-j-m*, "the bulk of a thing" (Lane).
