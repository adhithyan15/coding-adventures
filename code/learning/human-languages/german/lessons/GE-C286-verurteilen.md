## You'll want to know: verurteilen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C286-URTEIL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**verurteilen** — "to sentence, to condemn".

> Der Richter verurteilte ihn zu einer hohen Strafe.
>
> *The judge sentenced him to a heavy penalty.*

*Zu etwas verurteilen* — to sentence to something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verurteilen* is built on *Urteil* "verdict", from Proto-Germanic *uzdailiją* "judgement" (EtymDB), with the prefix *ver-*.
