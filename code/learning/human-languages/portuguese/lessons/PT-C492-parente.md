## You'll want to know: o parente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C492-FAMILIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o parente** — "relative".

> Nas festas de família juntamos todos os parentes.
>
> *At family parties we get all our relatives together.*

Not English *parent*: it means any relative. Parents are *os pais*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parente* is inherited from Latin *parentem* "a parent" (EtymDB), from *parere* "to give birth"; in Portuguese it widened to any relative.
