## You'll want to know: उद्दिष्ट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C388-UDDISHTA388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्दिष्ट** — *uddiṣṭa* — "an objective; a target".

> या वर्षी शंभर झाडं लावण्याचं उद्दिष्ट आहे.
>
> *yā varṣī śambhar jhāḍaṁ lāvaṇyācaṁ uddiṣṭa āhe.*
>
> The objective this year is to plant a hundred trees.

Neuter.

Neuter: उद्दिष्ट गाठणे, to reach a target.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्दिष्ट** (*uddiṣṭa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *uddiṣṭa*, "mentioned, specified" (Monier-Williams): what has been named as the aim.
