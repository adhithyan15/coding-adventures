Gujarati, chapter 531: When Plans Go Wrong.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઝંઝટ (jhanjhaṭ) — a hassle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to push back, then the Gujarati for at long last.

You'll want to know: ઝંઝટ.
ઝંઝટ — jhanjhaṭ — "a hassle".
જો ટ્રેન મોડી હશે તો ઘણી ઝંઝટ થશે. jo ṭren moḍī haśe to ghaṇī jhanjhaṭ thaśe. If the train is late, there will be a lot of hassle.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઝંઝટ (jhanjhaṭ) sounds like what it means, a tangle of small troubles. CDIAL lists Old Gujarati jhaṁjha, "a quarrel", and Gujarati jhā̃jh, "rage", under Sanskrit jhañjhā, "the noise of rain" (CDIAL 5326); it does not list this word, so its history is not settled.

You'll want to know: અણધાર્યું.
અણધાર્યું — aṇdhāryũ — "unexpected".
જો કંઈક અણધાર્યું બનશે તો મને તરત ફોન કરો. jo kaīk aṇdhāryũ banśe to mane tarat phon karo. If something unexpected happens, phone me at once.

The word, taken apart.
અણધાર્યું (aṇdhāryũ) is built from aṇ-, "un-", and ધાર્યું, the past of ધારવું, "to suppose": not supposed, unexpected. CDIAL files ધારવું under Sanskrit dhāráyati, "holds" (CDIAL 6791).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઓચિંતું (ocintũ) — sudden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a hassle, then the Gujarati for unexpected.

You'll want to know: ઓચિંતું.
ઓચિંતું — ocintũ — "sudden".
જો ઓચિંતો વરસાદ આવશે તો મેચ બંધ થઈ જશે. jo ocinto varsād āvśe to mec bandh thaī jaśe. If a sudden shower comes, the match will be stopped.

The word, taken apart.
ઓચિંતું (ocintũ) goes back to a reconstructed apacitta, "without thought", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "suddenly" (CDIAL 420): what comes without being thought of. It agrees with its noun: ઓચિંતો વરસાદ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ફેરફાર (pherphār) — a change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for unexpected, then the Gujarati for sudden.

You'll want to know: ફેરફાર.
ફેરફાર — pherphār — "a change".
જો યોજનામાં કોઈ ફેરફાર થશે તો હું તમને કહીશ. jo yojnāmā̃ koī pherphār thaśe to hũ tamne kahīś. If there is any change in the plan, I will tell you.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફેરફાર (pherphār) is built on pher, "a turn", which CDIAL files under phirati, "turns" (CDIAL 9078), doubled with a rhyming phār.

You'll want to know: બંદોબસ્ત.
બંદોબસ્ત — bandobast — "arrangements (made in advance)".
જો મહેમાનો રાત્રે રોકાશે તો સૂવાનો બંદોબસ્ત કરવો પડશે. jo mahemāno rātre rokāśe to sūvāno bandobast karvo paḍśe. If the guests stay the night, sleeping arrangements will have to be made.

The word, taken apart.
બંદોબસ્ત (bandobast) is usually traced to Persian band-o-bast, "binding and tying", and so "arrangements".
