# మండిపడు (maṇḍipaḍu) — to flare up; to fume

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C496-BADHA496-05, TE-LEX-C497-KOPAM497-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for despair, then the Telugu for to get angry.

## You'll want to know: మండిపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C497-KOPAM497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మండిపడు** — *maṇḍipaḍu* — "to flare up; to fume".

> బిల్లు చూసి నాన్న మండిపడ్డారు.
>
> *billu cūsi nānna maṇḍipaḍḍāru.*
>
> Seeing the bill, dad flared up.

Past (I …): మండిపడ్డాను, *maṇḍipaḍḍānu*.

Literally to blaze: మండు is to burn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మండిపడు** (*maṇḍipaḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4680 lists Telugu *maṇḍu*, "to burn, to blaze, to be in a fury", with Tamil *maṇṭu*, "to blaze up"; the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853) completes the verb.
