Telugu, chapter 212: Free time, Immediately, Finally, Until, Sankranti.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తీరిక (tīrika) — free time, leisure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for sunstroke, then the Telugu for steam.

You'll want to know: తీరిక.
తీరిక — tīrika — "free time, leisure".

The word, taken apart.
తీరిక (tīrika) looks built on native tīru, "to be finished, completed", one word with Tamil tīr (DEDR 3278); DEDR does not list tīrika itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
సంక్రాంతి (saṅkrānti) — Sankranti, the January harvest festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for finally, then the Telugu for until.

You'll want to know: సంక్రాంతి.
సంక్రాంతి — saṅkrānti — "Sankranti, the January harvest festival".

The word, taken apart.
సంక్రాంతి (saṅkrānti) is Sanskrit saṁkrānti, "going from one place to another; passage of a heavenly body", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 12835). The festival marks the sun's passage into Capricorn.
