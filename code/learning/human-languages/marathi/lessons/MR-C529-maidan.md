## You'll want to know: मैदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C529-SAMNA529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मैदान** — *maidān* — "a playing field; a ground".

> शाळेच्या मैदानावर रोज संध्याकाळी मुलं खेळतात.
>
> *śāḷecyā maidānāvar roj sandhyākāḷī mulaṁ kheḷtāt.*
>
> Children play on the school ground every evening.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मैदान** (*maidān*) is a loan from Persian *maidān*, "open ground, a field".
