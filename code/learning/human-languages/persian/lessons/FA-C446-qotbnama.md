## You'll want to know: قطب نما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C446-JAHAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قطب نما** (*qotbnamâ*) — "a compass".

> در کوه با قطب نما راه را پیدا کردیم.
>
> *Dar kuh bâ qotbnamâ râh râ peydâ kardim.*
>
> In the mountains we found the way with a compass.

Literally 'pole-shower': *qotb* 'pole' + *namâ* 'showing'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qotbnamâ* is built from *qotb* 'pole', borrowed from Arabic *quṭb* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), and native *namâ* 'showing', the stem of *nemudan* 'to show'.
