Sanskrit, chapter 524: The Big Match.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्पर्धा (spardhā) — a contest; rivalry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for backstage, then the Sanskrit for acting.

You'll want to know: स्पर्धा.
स्पर्धा — spardhā — "a contest; rivalry".
श्वः विद्यालये क्रीडायाः स्पर्धा अस्ति। śvaḥ vidyālaye krīḍāyāḥ spardhā asti. Tomorrow there is a sports contest at school.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्पर्धा (spardhā) comes from the root √spardh, "to contend, to vie with"; MW gives spardhā as emulation, rivalry, competition (MW).

You'll want to know: प्रक्षिपति.
प्रक्षिपति — prakṣipati — "hurls".
क्रीडकः कन्दुकं दूरं प्रक्षिपति। krīḍakaḥ kandukaṃ dūraṃ prakṣipati. The player hurls the ball a long way.
Past (he …): सः कन्दुकं प्रक्षिप्तवान्, saḥ kandukaṃ prakṣiptavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रक्षिपति (prakṣipati) is built on pra, "forth", and the root √kṣip, "to throw", which also gives kṣipati; MW gives prakṣip as to cast, to hurl, to fling (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
विजेता (vijetā) — a victor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a contest, then the Sanskrit for hurls.

You'll want to know: विजेता.
विजेता — vijetā — "a victor".
एतस्यां स्पर्धायाम् अरुणः विजेता अस्ति। etasyāṃ spardhāyām aruṇaḥ vijetā asti. Arun is the winner of this contest.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विजेता (vijetā) is built on vi and the root √ji, "to conquer", which also gives jayati, with the agent ending -tṛ; MW gives vijetṛ as a vanquisher, a conqueror (MW).

मीरा पराजिता न अस्ति। mīrā parājitā na asti. Mira is not beaten.

The word, taken apart.
पराजितः (parājitaḥ) is built on parā, "away", and the root √ji, "to conquer", as its past participle; MW gives parājita as conquered, defeated, overthrown (MW).
