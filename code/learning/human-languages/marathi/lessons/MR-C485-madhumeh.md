## You'll want to know: मधुमेह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C485-MADHUMEH485-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मधुमेह** — *madhumeh* — "diabetes".

> मधुमेहामुळे आजोबा साखर खात नाहीत.
>
> *madhumehāmuḷe ājobā sākhar khāt nāhīt.*
>
> Grandfather does not eat sugar because of his diabetes.

Masculine.

मधु, honey, + मेह, urine: an old name for the sugar sickness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मधुमेह** (*madhumeh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *madhumeha*, "honey-like urine, diabetes" (Monier-Williams).
