## You'll want to know: der Dokumentarfilm
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C524-FILM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Dokumentarfilm** — "documentary".

> Im Fernsehen läuft ein Dokumentarfilm über Wale.
>
> *There's a documentary about whales on TV.*

Plural: *die Dokumentarfilme*. In speech also *die Doku*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Dokumentarfilm* is built on *Dokument*, from Latin *documentum* "proof, lesson", and *Film*, borrowed from English *film* (EtymDB).
