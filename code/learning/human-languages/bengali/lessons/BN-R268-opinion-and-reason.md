# Review — what I think, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C268-I-THINK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-KARON-BECAUSE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think there will be a storm tomorrow", then "I won't go, because I'm tired". (*āmār mône hôy, kāl jhôṛ hôbe*; *āmi jābô nā, kārôn āmi klāntô*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C268-I-THINK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-THINK-NOT-01, BN-LEX-C268-KARON-01, BN-SCRIPT-C268-MURDHANYA-NA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-KARON-BECAUSE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-JONNO-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-LEX-C44-BODY44-02, BN-LEX-C83-TOWN83-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: What do you think? I think Mira is right.]
- [YOU SAY: I don't think the bus will come today.]
- [YOU SAY: I stayed at home yesterday, because I had a fever.]
- [YOU SAY: We came late because of the storm.]
- [YOU SAY: What was the reason? I don't know.]
- [YOU WRITE: **কারণ**, then **ঠিক**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C268-I-THINK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-THINK-NOT-01, BN-LEX-C268-KARON-01, BN-SCRIPT-C268-MURDHANYA-NA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-KARON-BECAUSE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-JONNO-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I don't think so"? (**আমার মনে হয় না**.) Which word gives a reason with a verb? (**কারণ**, or **কেননা**.) Which gives one with a noun? (**-এর জন্য**.) Which letter ends **কারণ**? (**ণ**.)
