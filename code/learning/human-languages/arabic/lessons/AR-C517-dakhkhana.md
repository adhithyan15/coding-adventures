# دخن (dakhkhana) — to smoke tobacco

## You'll want to know: دخن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C517-ADAT517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دخن** — *dakhkhana* — "to smoke tobacco".

> لا يدخن أبي في البيت.
>
> *lā yudakhkhinu abī fī al-bayt.*
>
> My father does not smoke at home.

Present: يدخن, *yudakhkhin*.

The noun تدخين is smoking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دخن** (*dakhkhana*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-kh-n*, "smoke" (Lane). Midkhana, a chimney, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
