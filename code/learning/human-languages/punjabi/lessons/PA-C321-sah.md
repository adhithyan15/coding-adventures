## You'll want to know: ਸਾਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C321-COLD321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਹ** — *sāh* — "breath".

> ਪੌੜੀ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਹ ਤੇਜ਼ ਸੀ।
>
> *pauṛī ton bāad merā sāh tez sī.*
>
> After the stairs I was breathing fast.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਹ** (*sāh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvāsa*, "breath, breathing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sāh*, "breath", under it (CDIAL 12769).
