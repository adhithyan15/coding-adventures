## You'll want to know: राक्षस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C335-DEVTA335-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राक्षस** — *rākṣas* — "a demon".

> एक राक्षस जंगल में रहता था।
>
> *ek rākṣas jaṅgal meṁ rahtā thā.*
>
> A demon lived in the forest.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राक्षस** (*rākṣas*) is Sanskrit *rākṣasa*, "a demon" (Monier-Williams): the giant, man-eating enemy of the old tales.
