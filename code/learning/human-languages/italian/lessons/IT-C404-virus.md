## You'll want to know: il virus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C404-BLOCCO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il virus** — "the virus".

> Se apri quell'allegato, prendi un virus.
>
> *If you open that attachment, you will get a virus.*

For computers and for illness. Masculine, invariable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*virus* is taken whole from Latin *vīrus* "slime, poison" (Lewis & Short); the computer sense came from English.
