## You'll want to know: jīròu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C161-RICE161-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jīròu* — "chicken meat; chicken (as food)".

> *nǐ yào niúròu háishi jīròu* — Would you like beef or chicken?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīròu* is built from *jī* 'chicken' and *ròu* 'meat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kej* and *nyuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an older book calling the first the creature that knows the hour.
