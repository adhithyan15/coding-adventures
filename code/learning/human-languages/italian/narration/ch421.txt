Italian, chapter 421: Al porto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il molo — the pier, the jetty (of stone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the refund, then the Italian for to refund.

You'll want to know: il molo.
il molo — "the pier, the jetty (of stone)".
Le barche sono legate al molo. The boats are tied to the pier.
Il molo is built of stone and shelters the harbour.

The word, taken apart.
molo is inherited from Latin mōlēs 'mass, heap, rock' (EtymDB), which could also mean a massive stone structure such as a dam or a pier (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
imbarcarsi — to board (a ship or a plane).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the pier, then the Italian for the landing stage.

You'll want to know: imbarcarsi.
imbarcarsi — "to board (a ship or a plane)".
Ci imbarchiamo alle nove per l'isola. We board at nine for the island.
Without si, imbarcare is to take on board: imbarcare i passeggeri.

The word, taken apart.
imbarcarsi is built from in- and barca 'boat', which is inherited from Late Latin barca (Lewis & Short).
