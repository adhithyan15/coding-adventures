## You'll want to know: ponderar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C345-VANTAGEM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ponderar** — "to weigh up, to consider carefully".

> Ponderei bem antes de mudar de casa.
>
> *I thought it over carefully before moving house.*

*Ponderar os prós e os contras* — to weigh up the pros and cons.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ponderar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ponderāre* "to weigh a thing, to ponder" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *pondus, ponderis* "weight".
