## You'll want to know: vantarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C472-DIFETTI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vantarsi** — "to boast, to show off".

> Il mio collega si vanta sempre della sua macchina nuova.
>
> *My colleague is always boasting about his new car.*

*Vantarsi di qualcosa*: the thing you boast about takes *di*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vantarsi* is usually traced to Late Latin *vānitāre* "to speak emptily", built on Latin *vānus* "empty, vain" (Lewis & Short).
