Lesson 1 of 6.
ಎದುರಾಳಿ (edurāḷi) — an opponent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tournament, then the Kannada for to defeat.

You'll want to know: ಎದುರಾಳಿ.
ಎದುರಾಳಿ — edurāḷi — "an opponent".
ಚದುರಂಗದಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಎದುರಾಳಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಜಾಣ. caduraṅgadalli nanna edurāḷi tumbā jāṇa. My chess opponent is very clever.
Plural: ಎದುರಾಳಿಗಳು, edurāḷigaḷu.
ಎದುರು, facing + ಆಳು, a man: the man facing you.

The word, taken apart.
ಎದುರಾಳಿ (edurāḷi) is a native Dravidian compound: edur, "that which is opposite" (DEDR 795), and āḷ, "a man, a soldier" (DEDR 399).

ಎರಡು ತಂಡಗಳ ಪಂದ್ಯ ಸಮಬಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಮುಗಿಯಿತು. eraḍu taṇḍagaḷa pandya samabaladalli mugiyitu. The match between the two teams ended in a draw.
