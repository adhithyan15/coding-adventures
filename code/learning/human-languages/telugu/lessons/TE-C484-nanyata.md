# నాణ్యత (nāṇyata) — quality

## You'll want to know: నాణ్యత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C484-AMMAKAM484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నాణ్యత** — *nāṇyata* — "quality".

> ఆ దుకాణంలో బట్టల నాణ్యత బాగుంటుంది.
>
> *ā dukāṇaṁlō baṭṭala nāṇyata bāguṇṭundi.*
>
> The quality of the clothes in that shop is good.

నాణ్యమైన, of good quality: నాణ్యమైన సరుకు, good goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నాణ్యత** (*nāṇyata*) is a Telugu word for quality whose origin is not settled.
