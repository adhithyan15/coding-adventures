## You'll want to know: el viudo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C550-DIVORCIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el viudo** — "the widower; widowed".

> Mi abuelo es viudo desde hace años.
>
> *My grandfather has been a widower for years.*

*Quedarse viudo* — to be widowed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viudo* is inherited from Latin *viduus* 'bereaved, widowed' (EtymDB).
