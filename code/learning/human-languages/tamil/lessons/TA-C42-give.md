## You'll want to know: கொடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-GIVE]; assesses=[] -->

**கொடு** (*koṭu*) — "give".

The everyday 'give'. Tamil distinguishes giving away from giving toward the speaker; this is the giving-away one.
