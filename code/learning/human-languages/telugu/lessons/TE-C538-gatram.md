## You'll want to know: గాత్రం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C538-SANGITAM538-02]; assesses=[] -->

**గాత్రం** — *gātraṁ* — "a singing voice".

> ఆమె గాత్రం విని అందరూ ఆశ్చర్యపోయారు.
>
> *āme gātraṁ vini andarū āścaryapōyāru.*
>
> Everyone was amazed to hear her singing voice.

In music, singing as against instruments.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గాత్రం** (*gātraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gātra*, "a limb; the body" (CDIAL 4124); in Telugu music it is the voice, the body's own instrument.
