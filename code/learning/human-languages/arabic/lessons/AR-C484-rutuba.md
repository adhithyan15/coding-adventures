## You'll want to know: رطوبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C484-HARR484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رطوبة** — *ruṭūba* — "humidity; moisture".

> الرطوبة على الساحل مرتفعة.
>
> *ar-ruṭūba ʿalā as-sāḥil murtafiʿa.*
>
> The humidity on the coast is high.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رطوبة** (*ruṭūba*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ṭ-b*, "to be moist" (Lane). It is the noun beside *raṭb*, "humid".
