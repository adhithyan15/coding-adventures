## You'll want to know: निदाघः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C438-NIDAGHA438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निदाघः** — *nidāghaḥ* — "the hot season".

> निदाघे कूपाः शुष्यन्ति।
>
> *nidāghe kūpāḥ śuṣyanti.*
>
> In the hot season the wells dry up.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निदाघः** (*nidāghaḥ*) is built from *ni*, "down", and the root √dah, "to burn", which also gives *dahati*, "burns"; MW gives *nidāgha* as heat, warmth and the hot season (MW).
