6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
नाटक (nāṭak) — a play; a drama.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to prick, to tattoo, then the Hindi for an exhibition.

You'll want to know: नाटक.
नाटक — nāṭak — "a play; a drama".
मेले में बच्चों ने एक नाटक किया। Mele meṁ baccoṁ ne ek nāṭak kiyā. At the fair the children put on a play.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
नाटक (nāṭak) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit nāṭaka, "acting, dancing; a play" (Monier-Williams).

नाटक (nāṭak) में अरुण का किरदार एक बूढ़े किसान का है। Nāṭak meṁ Aruṇ kā kirdār ek būṛhe kisān kā hai. In the play Arun's role is an old farmer.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
किरदार (kirdār) is usually traced to Persian kirdār, "a deed, conduct". The sense "a role in a play" is the one Hindi uses.
