## You'll want to know: zhènyǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C244-STORM244-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhènyǔ* — "a rain shower".

> *míngtiān xiàwǔ yǒu zhènyǔ* — There'll be showers tomorrow afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhènyǔ* is built from *zhèn* 'a spell' and 雨 'rain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drinH* and *hjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the first only as the everyday form of another character, and the sense 'a spell' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
