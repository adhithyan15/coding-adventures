## You'll want to know: തേടുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C378-ABHAYAM378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**തേടുക** — *tēṭuka* — "to seek; to look for".

> ജോലി തേടി അവൻ നഗരത്തിൽ പോയി.
>
> *jōli tēṭi avan nagarattil pōyi.*
>
> He went to the city in search of work.

Past: തേടി, *tēṭi*.

തേടി often means in search of. A more literary word than തിരയുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തേടുക** (*tēṭuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3456 lists Malayalam *tēṭuka*, "to seek, to acquire", beside Tamil *tēṭu*, "to seek, to search for".
