## You'll want to know: shùnlù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C429-HOME429-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shùnlù* — "on one's way".

> *wǒ zhù zài nǐ jiā fùjìn shùnlù sòng nǐ qù jīchǎng* — I live near you, so I'll drop you at the airport on my way.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shùnlù* is built from *shùn* 'to follow' and 路 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zywinH* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to follow'.
