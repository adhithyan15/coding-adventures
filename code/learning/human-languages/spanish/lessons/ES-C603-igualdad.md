## You'll want to know: la igualdad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C603-DERECHO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la igualdad** — "the equality".

> Luchamos por la igualdad.
>
> *We fight for equality.*

*Igual* — equal, the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*igualdad* is inherited from Latin *aequalitas* 'evenness, equality', built on *aequus* 'level, equal' (EtymDB).
