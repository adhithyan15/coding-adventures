## You'll want to know: तापः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C46-LIGHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तापः** (*tāpaḥ*) — "heat".

**तापः** is heat: the warmth of the sun on a back, and by an easy stretch the heat of trouble.

It stands on *tap*, "to be hot, to burn" — the same root under *tapas*, the heat a person is said to build up by hard practice. Sanskrit treats effort and warmth as one thing.

Latin *tepēre*, "to be warm", is that root gone west, and English took *tepid* straight from it. Ends in **-अः**.
