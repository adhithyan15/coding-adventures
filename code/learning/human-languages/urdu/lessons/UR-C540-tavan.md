## You'll want to know: تاوان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C540-GHATA540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تاوان** — *tāvān* — "damages (to be paid)".

> اگر بچوں نے کھڑکی توڑی تو تاوان دینا پڑے گا۔
>
> *agar bachchoṅ ne khiṛkī toṛī to tāvān denā paṛe gā.*
>
> If the children break the window, we'll have to pay for it.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تاوان** (*tāvān*) is usually traced to Persian *tāvān*, "a fine, compensation for loss".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
