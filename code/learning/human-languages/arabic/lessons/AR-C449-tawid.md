## You'll want to know: تعويض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C449-SHAKWA449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تعويض** — *taʿwīḍ* — "compensation".

> طلب المسافرون تعويضا بعد التأخير.
>
> *ṭalaba al-musāfirūn taʿwīḍan baʿda at-taʾkhīr.*
>
> The travellers asked for compensation after the delay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعويض** (*taʿwīḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-w-ḍ*, "to give in exchange" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form II; an *ʿiwaḍ* is a thing given in place of another.
