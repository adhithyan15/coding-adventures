## You'll want to know: yānhuā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C339-NATION339-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yānhuā* — "fireworks".

> *Guóqìng de wǎnshang wǒmen kàn le yānhuā* — On the evening of the national holiday we saw the fireworks.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yānhuā* is built from *yān* 'smoke' and 花 'a flower', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'en* and *xwae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the breath of fire'.
