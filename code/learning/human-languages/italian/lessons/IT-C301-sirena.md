## You'll want to know: la sirena
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C301-SOCCORSO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la sirena** — "the siren".

> Si sente la sirena dell'ambulanza.
>
> *You can hear the ambulance siren.*

In stories, *la sirena* is also a mermaid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sirena* comes through Late Latin *sīrēna* from Greek *Seirḗn* (EtymDB), the singing sea creatures of the old myths.
