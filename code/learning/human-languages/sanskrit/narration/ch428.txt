Sanskrit, chapter 428: Hosts and Guests.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गृहपतिः (gṛhapatiḥ) — the master of the house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for spreads out, then the Sanskrit for wakes up.

You'll want to know: गृहपतिः.
गृहपतिः — gṛhapatiḥ — "the master of the house".
गृहपतिः अतिथिम् आह्वयति। gṛhapatiḥ atithim āhvayati. The master of the house calls in the guest.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गृहपतिः (gṛhapatiḥ) is built from gṛha, "a house", and pati, "a lord, a master"; MW gives the compound as the master of a house, a householder (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अभ्यागतः (abhyāgataḥ) — a visitor; a guest who calls.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for wakes up, then the Sanskrit for the master of the house.

You'll want to know: अभ्यागतः.
अभ्यागतः — abhyāgataḥ — "a visitor; a guest who calls".
अभ्यागतः द्वारे तिष्ठति। abhyāgataḥ dvāre tiṣṭhati. The visitor stands at the door.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अभ्यागतः (abhyāgataḥ) is built from abhi, "towards", ā, "near", and the root √gam, "to go": one who has come up to the house; MW gives abhyāgata as come, arrived and, as a noun, an uninvited guest or any guest (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
निवासयति (nivāsayati) — puts up for the night; lodges (a guest).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the master of the house, then the Sanskrit for a visitor.

You'll want to know: निवासयति.
निवासयति — nivāsayati — "puts up for the night; lodges (a guest)".
गृहपतिः पथिकं गृहे निवासयति। gṛhapatiḥ pathikaṃ gṛhe nivāsayati. The master of the house puts the wayfarer up in his home.
Past (he …): सः पथिकं गृहे निवासितवान्, saḥ pathikaṃ gṛhe nivāsitavān.

The word, taken apart.
निवासयति (nivāsayati) is built from ni, "down, in", and the causative of the root √vas, "to dwell", which gives vasati, "dwells"; MW gives ni-vāsayati as to make stay, to receive as a guest, and KRM gives the causative participle vāsita (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: प्रत्युद्गच्छति.
प्रत्युद्गच्छति — pratyudgacchati — "goes out to meet".
गृहपतिः अतिथिं प्रत्युद्गच्छति। gṛhapatiḥ atithiṃ pratyudgacchati. The master of the house goes out to meet the guest.
Past (he …): सः अतिथिं प्रत्युद्गतवान्, saḥ atithiṃ pratyudgatavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रत्युद्गच्छति (pratyudgacchati) is built from prati, "towards", ud, "up, out", and the root √gam, "to go"; MW gives praty-ud-gam as to go out towards, to advance to meet a friend, with the participle pratyudgata (MW).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: अनुव्रजति.
अनुव्रजति — anuvrajati — "sees a guest off".
गृहपतिः अतिथिम् अनुव्रजति। gṛhapatiḥ atithim anuvrajati. The master of the house sees the guest off.
Past (he …): सः अतिथिम् अनुव्रजितवान्, saḥ atithim anuvrajitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुव्रजति (anuvrajati) is built from anu, "after", and the root √vraj, "to walk, to go"; MW gives anu-vraj as to go along and to follow a departing guest some way as a mark of respect, and vrajita, "gone" (MW).
