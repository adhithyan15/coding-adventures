## You'll want to know: eventuale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C373-PURCHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**eventuale** — "possible, any (that may arise)".

> Per eventuali domande, scrivete alla segreteria.
>
> *For any questions, write to the office.*

It means possible, not final: *eventualmente* is if need be, not eventually.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eventuale* is usually traced to French *éventuel*, built on Latin *ēventus* "an outcome, an event" (Lewis & Short), from *ēvenīre* "to come out, to happen", on *venīre* "to come".
