## You'll want to know: شباهت داشتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C506-TIES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شباهت داشتن** (*shebâhat dâshtan*) — "to resemble, to look like".

> او خیلی به مادرش شباهت دارد.
>
> *u kheyli be mâdarash shebâhat dârad.*
>
> She looks a lot like her mother.

*be kasi shebâhat dâshtan*; *shebâhat* 'likeness' + *dâshtan* 'to have'. It shares a root with *shabih* 'similar'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shebâhat* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *šabāha*, 'likeness', from the same root as *shabih*, 'similar'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *dâshtan* is inherited Persian.
