Hindi, chapter 347: Taking Sides.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पक्ष (pakṣ) — a side (in a dispute).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to interrupt, then the Hindi for but rather.

You'll want to know: पक्ष.
पक्ष — pakṣ — "a side (in a dispute)".
बहस में मैं मीरा के पक्ष में हूँ। bahas meṁ maiṁ Mīrā ke pakṣ meṁ hūṁ. In the argument I'm on Mira's side.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पक्ष (pakṣ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit pakṣa, "a wing, a side" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
विरोध करना (virodh karnā) — to oppose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a side, then the Hindi for against.

You'll want to know: विरोध करना.
विरोध करना — virodh karnā — "to oppose".
किसान नए क़ानून का विरोध कर रहे हैं। kisān nae qānūn kā virodh kar rahe haiṁ. The farmers are opposing the new law.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने क़ानून का विरोध किया, usne qānūn kā virodh kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
विरोध करना (virodh karnā) joins विरोध to करना, "to do". विरोध is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit virodha, "opposition, hostility" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सहमत होना (sahmat honā) — to agree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to oppose, then the Hindi for to support.

You'll want to know: सहमत होना.
सहमत होना — sahmat honā — "to agree".
मैं तुमसे सहमत हूँ, लेकिन मीरा सहमत नहीं है। maiṁ tumse sahmat hūṁ, lekin Mīrā sahmat nahīṃ hai. I agree with you, but Mira doesn't.
Past (he …): वह सहमत हुआ, vah sahmat huā.

The word, taken apart.
सहमत होना (sahmat honā) joins सहमत to होना, "to be, to become". सहमत is built from two Sanskrit words, saha, "together with", and mata, "an opinion" (Monier-Williams): of one opinion with someone.
