## You'll want to know: उबलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C384-BARF384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उबलना** — *ubalnā* — "to come to the boil (of a liquid)".

> अगर दूध उबलेगा तो गैस धीमी कर दो।
>
> *agar dūdh ublegā to gais dhīmī kar do.*
>
> If the milk boils, turn the gas down.

Past (it …): वह दूध उबल गया, *vah dūdh ubal gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उबलना** (*ubalnā*) is inherited from *\*ubbal*, "boil up", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2339).
