## You'll want to know: అరుచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C332-KSHANAM332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అరుచు** — *arucu* — "to yell; to shout".

> పామును చూసి పిల్లవాడు గట్టిగా అరిచాడు.
>
> *pāmunu cūsi pillavāḍu gaṭṭigā aricāḍu.*
>
> Seeing a snake, the boy yelled loudly.

Past (I …): అరిచాను, *aricānu*.

You know its noun, అరుపు, a shout.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అరుచు** (*arucu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 367 lists Telugu *ārcu*, *ārucu*, "to cry aloud, shout", with Tamil *ār*, "to shout, roar".
