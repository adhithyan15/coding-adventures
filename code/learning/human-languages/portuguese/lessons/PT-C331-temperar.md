## You'll want to know: temperar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C331-REFOGAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**temperar** — "to season".

> A avó tempera a carne com alho e sal.
>
> *Grandmother seasons the meat with garlic and salt.*

*Temperar a salada* — to dress the salad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*temperar* comes from Latin *temperāre* "to mix in due proportion" (EtymDB), related to *tempus* "time, the right moment".
