## You'll want to know: скользить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C514-ICE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**скользить** (*skolzít*) — "to slide, to glide".

> Фигуристы легко скользят по льду.
>
> *Figurísty legkó skolzyát po ldu.*
>
> The figure skaters glide easily over the ice.

Imperfective; present скольжу, скользишь (з becomes ж). Perfective скользнуть.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *скользить* without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *skolьzěti*.
