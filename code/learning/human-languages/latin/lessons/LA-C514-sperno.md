## You'll want to know: spernō, spernere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C514-CONT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**spernō, spernere** — "to reject, spurn".

> Puella amōrem iuvenis spernit.
>
> *The girl rejects the young man's love.*

*spernō, spernere, sprēvī, sprētum*. Stronger than *contemnō*: to push away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**spernō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root *sper-* (EtymDB), and English *spurn* is a cousin from the same root (EtymDB).
