## You'll want to know: pariō, parere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C323-PARTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pariō, parere** — "to give birth to; to produce".

> Uxor eius fīliam peperit.
>
> *His wife gave birth to a daughter.*

*pariō, parere, peperī, partum*, short *a*. Not *pāreō, pārēre*, 'to obey' (taught). English *parent* comes from its family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pariō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to bring forth" (EtymDB). English *parent* comes from its participle *parēns*.
