## You'll want to know: 少
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C432-QUIET432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**少** — *shǎo* — "few; not much".

> **今年这家酒店的客人很少** *jīnnián zhè jiā jiǔdiàn de kèrén hěn shǎo* — This hotel has had very few guests this year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**少** (*shǎo*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *syewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'not many'. The dictionary lists a second reading, *syewH*, glossed 'young': the shào of 少年.
