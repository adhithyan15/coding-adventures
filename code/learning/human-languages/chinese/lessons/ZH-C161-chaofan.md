## You'll want to know: chǎofàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C161-RICE161-03]; assesses=[] -->

*chǎofàn* — "fried rice".

> *tā yào le yí fèn chǎofàn* — He ordered a portion of fried rice.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chǎofàn* is built from *chǎo* 'to stir-fry' and 饭 'cooked rice', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrhaewX* and *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to roast dry'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
