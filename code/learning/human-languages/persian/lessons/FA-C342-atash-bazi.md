## You'll want to know: آتش بازی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C342-TAZYIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آتش بازی** (*âtash bâzi*) — "fireworks".

> نیمه شب آتش بازی شروع شد.
>
> *Nime shab âtash bâzi shoru' shod.*
>
> At midnight the fireworks began.

Literally "fire play".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âtash* "fire" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *bâzi* "play, game" is native Persian too: fire play.
