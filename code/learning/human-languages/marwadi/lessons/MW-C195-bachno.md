## You'll want to know: बचणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C195-BANK195-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बचणो** — *bachṇo* — "to be left over; to escape".

> म्हारी तनखा सूं सो रुपिया बचै है।
>
> *mhārī tankhā sū̃ so rupiyā bachai hai.*
>
> A hundred rupees are left over from my salary.

Past (it …): वो सिक्को बच्यो, *vo sikko bachyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बचणो** (*bachṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vañcati*, "moves; escapes from"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *vacaï*, "is saved" (CDIAL 11208). **बचावणो**, "to save", is its partner.
