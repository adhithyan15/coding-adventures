# A greeting that fits the reader

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-NAMASTE-01] -->

Read the two greetings you already know. Today you choose only the opening;
the body of a message comes later.

## Why it's said this way — one clue, one choice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-NAMASTE-01, PA-LEX-SAT-SRI-AKAL-01] -->

**ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ** is specifically a Sikh greeting. **ਨਮਸਤੇ** is the broader
greeting taught here. Do not guess a person's community from a Punjabi name.
For a named reader whose Sikh context is not stated, choose **ਨਮਸਤੇ**.

## Writing — choose before copying
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-NAMASTE-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Imagine a note to Manan. Point to the broader opening, then copy it once in
Gurmukhi: **ਨਮਸਤੇ**. Keep the last vowel sign attached to its letter.
