## You'll want to know: il santo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C320-FIERA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il santo** — "the saint".

> Ogni paese festeggia il suo santo.
>
> *Every village celebrates its own saint.*

Before a man's name it usually becomes *San*, or *Sant'* before a vowel; a woman saint is *santa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*santo* is inherited from Latin *sānctus* "holy" (EtymDB).
