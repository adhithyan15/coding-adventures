## You'll want to know: پایان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C302-AGHAZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پایان** (*pâyân*) — "an end, an ending".

> پایان فیلم را دوست نداشتم.
>
> *Pâyân-e film râ dust nadâshtam.*
>
> I did not like the end of the film.

*Dar pâyân* "in the end"; *be pâyân residan* "to come to an end".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pâyân* is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian *pāyān*, "end"; EtymDB lists no entry.
