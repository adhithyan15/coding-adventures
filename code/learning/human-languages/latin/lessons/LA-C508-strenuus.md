## You'll want to know: strēnuus, strēnua, strēnuum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C508-IGNA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**strēnuus, strēnua, strēnuum** — "vigorous, active".

> Agricola strēnuus ā prīmā lūce labōrat.
>
> *The vigorous farmer works from first light.*

Brisk and energetic, in work or in war. English *strenuous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**strēnuus** is a native Latin adjective; Lewis & Short and EtymDB compare Greek *stereos* "firm, hard", but the link is not settled. English *strenuous* comes from it.
