## You'll want to know: مخيم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C442-MUNTAJA442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مخيم** — *mukhayyam* — "a campsite; a camp".

> كان المخيم بجانب البحيرة.
>
> *kāna al-mukhayyam bi-jānib al-buḥayra.*
>
> The campsite was beside the lake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخيم** (*mukhayyam*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-y-m*, "a tent" (Lane). It is the Form II passive participle: a place set with tents.
