Hindi, chapter 185: Sunshine, Cold, Heat, A storm, A leaf.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धूप (dhūp) — sunshine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a mirror, then the Hindi for rain.

You'll want to know: धूप.
धूप — dhūp — "sunshine".

The word, taken apart.
धूप (dhūp) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhuppā, "sunshine", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6825).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ठंड (ṭhaṇḍ) — cold, the cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for rain, then the Hindi for sunshine.

You'll want to know: ठंड.
ठंड — ṭhaṇḍ — "cold, the cold".

The word, taken apart.
ठंड (ṭhaṇḍ) comes from a reconstructed ṭhaṇḍha, "cold", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit stabdha, "stiff, solidified" (CDIAL 13676). DEDR compares Dravidian words for "cool", such as Tamil taṇ (DEDR 3045).

Lesson 4 of 5.
तूफ़ान (tūfān) — a storm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for cold, then the Hindi for heat.

You'll want to know: तूफ़ान.
तूफ़ान — tūfān — "a storm".

The word, taken apart.
तूफ़ान (tūfān) is a loan from Arabic ṭūfān, "a flood, a deluge", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṭ-w-f (Lane).
