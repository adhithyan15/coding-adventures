Spanish, chapter 481: Problemas y soluciones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
resolver — to solve, to sort out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to promise, then the Spanish for to complain.

You'll want to know: resolver.
resolver — "to solve, to sort out".
Vamos a resolver el problema hoy. We are going to sort out the problem today.
The o breaks into ue under stress: resuelvo, resuelve.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: resolver]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: resolver, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with resolver]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for to promise, then the Spanish for to complain, then say resolver again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does resolver mean? ("To solve, to sort out".) Say it once more.
