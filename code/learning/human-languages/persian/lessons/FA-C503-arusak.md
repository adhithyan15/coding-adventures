## You'll want to know: عروسک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C503-PLAY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عروسک** (*'arusak*) — "a doll".

> دخترم با عروسکش حرف میزند.
>
> *dokhtaram bâ 'arusakash harf mizanad.*
>
> My daughter talks to her doll.

Literally 'little bride': *'arus* 'bride' + the diminutive *-ak*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'arusak* is built in Persian on *'arus*, 'bride', which was borrowed from Arabic *ʿarūs*, 'bride, groom' (EtymDB), with the native diminutive *-ak*.
