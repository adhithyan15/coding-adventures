Malayalam, chapter 377: Earthquake and Landslide.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഭൂകമ്പം (bhūkampaṁ) — an earthquake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to get hurt, then the Malayalam for to drown.

You'll want to know: ഭൂകമ്പം.
ഭൂകമ്പം — bhūkampaṁ — "an earthquake".
ഭൂകമ്പത്തിൽ നഗരത്തിലെ ഒരുപാട് കെട്ടിടങ്ങൾ തകർന്നു. bhūkampattil nagarattile orupāṭŭ keṭṭiṭaṅṅaḷ takarnnu. Many buildings in the city collapsed in the earthquake.
ഭൂ, the earth, + കമ്പം, trembling.

The word, taken apart.
ഭൂകമ്പം (bhūkampaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit bhūkampa, "an earthquake" (CDIAL 9547), made of bhū, "the earth", and kampa, "trembling".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ (uruḷpoṭṭal) — a landslide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to drown, then the Malayalam for an earthquake.

You'll want to know: ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ.
ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ — uruḷpoṭṭal — "a landslide".
മലയിൽ ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ ഉണ്ടായി. malayil uruḷpoṭṭal uṇṭāyi. There was a landslide on the hill.
Common on the hills in the rainy season.

The word, taken apart.
ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ (uruḷpoṭṭal) is a native Dravidian compound of ഉരുൾ, "a rolling" (DEDR 664), and പൊട്ടൽ, "a bursting", from പൊട്ടുക (DEDR 4490): the hillside bursts and rolls down.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ഒലിച്ചുപോകുക (oliccupōkuka) — to be washed away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an earthquake, then the Malayalam for a landslide.

You'll want to know: ഒലിച്ചുപോകുക.
ഒലിച്ചുപോകുക — oliccupōkuka — "to be washed away".
വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കത്തിൽ പാലം ഒലിച്ചുപോയി. veḷḷappokkattil pālaṁ oliccupōyi. The bridge was washed away in the flood.
Past: ഒലിച്ചുപോയി, oliccupōyi.
ഒലിക്കുക, to flow, + പോകുക, to go.

The word, taken apart.
ഒലിച്ചുപോകുക (oliccupōkuka) joins the native verb ഒലിക്കുക, "to flow, to run as water" (DEDR 999), and പോകുക (pōkuka), "to go": to go off with the flowing water.

ഒറ്റപ്പെടുക (oṟṟappeṭuka) — to be cut off; to be isolated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a landslide, then the Malayalam for to be washed away.

You'll want to know: ഒറ്റപ്പെടുക.
ഒറ്റപ്പെടുക — oṟṟappeṭuka — "to be cut off; to be isolated".
മഴ കാരണം ഗ്രാമം ഒറ്റപ്പെട്ടു. maḻa kāraṇaṁ grāmaṁ oṟṟappeṭṭu. The village was cut off because of the rain.
Past: ഒറ്റപ്പെട്ടു, oṟṟappeṭṭu.
From ഒറ്റ, single, alone: also of a lonely person.

The word, taken apart.
ഒറ്റപ്പെടുക (oṟṟappeṭuka) is native Dravidian: ഒറ്റ (oṟṟa), "single, alone", which DEDR 990 sets with the word for one, and -പ്പെടുക, "to come to be".

Lesson 5 of 5.
കാണാതാകുക (kāṇātākuka) — to go missing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to be washed away, then the Malayalam for to be cut off.

You'll want to know: കാണാതാകുക.
കാണാതാകുക — kāṇātākuka — "to go missing".
മൂന്ന് ദിവസം മുൻപ് ഒരു കുട്ടിയെ കാണാതായി. mūnnŭ divasaṁ munpŭ oru kuṭṭiye kāṇātāyi. A child went missing three days ago.
Past: കാണാതായി, kāṇātāyi.
The missing person takes -എ: അവനെ കാണാതായി, literally it became not seeing him.

The word, taken apart.
കാണാതാകുക (kāṇātākuka) is native Dravidian: the negative of കാണുക (kāṇuka), "to see" (DEDR 1443), with ആകുക, "to become": someone comes to be not seen.
