## You'll want to know: लूटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C292-DAKAITI292-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लूटना** — *lūṭnā* — "to rob".

> डाकुओं ने पूरा गाँव लूटा।
>
> *ḍākuoṁ ne pūrā gā̃v lūṭā.*
>
> The bandits robbed the whole village.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दुकान लूटी, *usne dukān lūṭī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लूटना** (*lūṭnā*) is inherited from *\*luṭṭati*, "plunders", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 11078). English *loot* was taken from this family of words.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
