## You'll want to know: ਸਿੱਕਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C258-LOAN258-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿੱਕਾ** — *sikkā* — "a coin".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਜੇਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਸਿੱਕਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī jeb vich ikk purāṇā sikkā hai.*
>
> There is an old coin in my pocket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿੱਕਾ** (*sikkā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *sikka*, "a die for stamping coins".
