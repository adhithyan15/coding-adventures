## You'll want to know: доказывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C338-DOVOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**доказывать** (*dokázyvat*) — "to prove".

> Он долго доказывал, что прав.
>
> *On dólgo dokázyval, chto prav.*
>
> He spent a long time proving that he was right.

Imperfective, with the perfective *доказать* (*докажу, докажешь*). *Доказывать, что…* — to prove that…

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**доказывать** is built from *до-* and *казать* "to show", the verb inside *показывать* and *сказать*. *Казать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*kazati* (EtymDB): to prove is to show a thing through to the end.
