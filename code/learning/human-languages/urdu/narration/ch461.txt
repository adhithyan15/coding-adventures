Urdu, chapter 461: Manners.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لحاظ (lihāz) — consideration; regard (for others).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to fight, then the Urdu for valiant.

You'll want to know: لحاظ.
لحاظ — lihāz — "consideration; regard (for others)".
ہمیں بڑوں کا لحاظ رکھنا چاہیے۔ hameṅ baṛoṅ kā lihāz rakhnā chāhiye. We should show consideration to our elders.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
لحاظ (lihāz) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic laḥāẓ, "a glance", and so "regard" for someone.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 5 of 5.
احترام کرنا (ehtirām karnā) — to show respect (to).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for refinement, then the Urdu for moral conduct.

You'll want to know: احترام کرنا.
احترام کرنا — ehtirām karnā — "to show respect (to)".
ہم بزرگوں کا احترام کرتے ہیں۔ ham buzurgoṅ kā ehtirām karte haiṅ. We show respect to our elders.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے استاد کا احترام کیا, us ne ustād kā ehtirām kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
احترام کرنا (ehtirām karnā) is built from احترام, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iḥtirām, "respect", and کرنا, "to do".
