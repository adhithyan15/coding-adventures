## You'll want to know: قيمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C368-AHAMMIYYA368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قيمة** — *qīma* — "a value".

> هذه الساعة لها قيمة كبيرة عندي.
>
> *hādhihi as-sāʿa lahā qīma kabīra ʿindī.*
>
> This watch has great value for me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قيمة** (*qīma*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-w-m*, "to stand, to rise" (Lane). A *qīma* is what a thing stands at: its worth, its value.
