German, chapter 268: Der Augenblick.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Augenblick — the moment, an instant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to continue, then the German for to interrupt.

You'll want to know: der Augenblick.
der Augenblick — "the moment, an instant".
Im letzten Augenblick sprang er in den Zug. At the last moment he jumped onto the train.
Plural: die Augenblicke. Im Augenblick — at the moment, right now.

The word, taken apart.
Augenblick comes from Middle High German ougenblick "a quick glance" (EtymDB), from Auge "eye" and Blick "look": the time an eye takes to blink.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Zeitpunkt — the point in time, the moment when.

You'll want to know: der Zeitpunkt.
der Zeitpunkt — "the point in time, the moment when".
Zu diesem Zeitpunkt war ich noch in der Schule. At that point in time I was still at school.
Plural: die Zeitpunkte. Zum richtigen Zeitpunkt — at the right moment.

The word, taken apart.
Zeitpunkt joins the inherited Zeit "time" and Punkt "point", which came from Latin punctum "a prick, a point" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Zeitraum — the period, the span of time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the moment, then the German for the point in time.

You'll want to know: der Zeitraum.
der Zeitraum — "the period, the span of time".
In diesem Zeitraum gab es drei Unfälle. In this period there were three accidents.
Plural: die Zeiträume. Common in reports: im Zeitraum von Mai bis Juli.

The word, taken apart.
Zeitraum joins Zeit "time" and Raum "room, space", from Old High German rūm (EtymDB): a stretch of room in time. Both are inherited Germanic words.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Dauer — the length, the duration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the point in time, then the German for the period.

You'll want to know: die Dauer.
die Dauer — "the length, the duration".
Für die Dauer der Reise blieb das Handy aus. For the length of the journey the phone stayed off.
No plural. Auf Dauer — in the long run.

The word, taken apart.
Dauer is built on dauern "to last", which goes back through Middle High German dūren to Latin dūrāre "to last" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Frist — the deadline, the time limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the period, then the German for the length.

You'll want to know: die Frist.
die Frist — "the deadline, the time limit".
Die Frist endet am Freitag. The deadline ends on Friday.
Plural: die Fristen. Innerhalb der Frist — within the time limit.

The word, taken apart.
Frist is inherited from Old High German frist, from Proto-Germanic fristiz (EtymDB), and is a cousin of the old English word frist "a period of grace".
