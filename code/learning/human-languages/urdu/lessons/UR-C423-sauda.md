## You'll want to know: سودا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C423-SAUDA423-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سودا** — *saudā* — "a deal; groceries".

> بازار میں ہم نے سودا خریدا۔
>
> *bāzār meṅ ham ne saudā kharīdā.*
>
> We bought our groceries at the market.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سودا** (*saudā*) is usually traced to Persian *saudā*, "trade, a bargain"; in Urdu it is a deal, and also the day's shopping.
