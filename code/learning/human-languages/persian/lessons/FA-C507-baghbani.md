## You'll want to know: باغبانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C507-HOBBIES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**باغبانی** (*bâghbâni*) — "gardening".

> مادربزرگ در بهار باغبانی میکند.
>
> *mâdarbozorg dar bahâr bâghbâni mikonad.*
>
> Grandmother gardens in spring.

*bâgh* 'garden' + *-bân* 'keeper' + *-i*. A gardener is *bâghbân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâghbâni* is built on native Persian parts: *bâgh*, 'garden', usually traced to Middle Persian *bāg*, the ending *-bân*, 'keeper', and *-i*.
