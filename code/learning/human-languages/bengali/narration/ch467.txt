Bengali, chapter 467: Face and Mouth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঠোঁট (ṭhõṭ) — a lip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the back, then the Bengali for the nape.

You'll want to know: ঠোঁট.
ঠোঁট — ṭhõṭ — "a lip".
শীতে মীরার ঠোঁট শুকনো হয়ে যায়। shīte mīrār ṭhõṭ shukno hôye jāy. In winter Mira's lips get dry.

The word, taken apart.
ঠোঁট (ṭhõṭ) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a bird's beak, under Sanskrit tuṇḍa, "beak, snout" (CDIAL 5853); everyday Bengali uses it for the human lip.

The word, taken apart.
মাড়ি (māṛi) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under a reconstructed māṁsapuṭaka, "the gums" (CDIAL 9984), a compound of the words for flesh and for a fold.
