## You'll want to know: સમાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C349-SAMAN349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાન** — *samān* — "equal".

> કાયદા સામે બધા માણસો સમાન છે.
>
> *kāydā sāme badhā māṇso samān che.*
>
> All people are equal before the law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાન** (*samān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *samāna*, "same, alike, equal" (Monier-Williams).
