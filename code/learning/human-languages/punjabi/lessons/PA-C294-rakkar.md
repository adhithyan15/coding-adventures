## You'll want to know: ਰੱਕੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C294-POOR294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੱਕੜ** — *rakkaṛ* — "hard and barren (of land)".

> ਇਹ ਜ਼ਮੀਨ ਰੱਕੜ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh zamīn rakkaṛ hai.*
>
> This land is hard and barren.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੱਕੜ** (*rakkaṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*rakka*, "defective"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *rakkaṛ*, "hard and barren (of land)", under it (CDIAL 10538).
