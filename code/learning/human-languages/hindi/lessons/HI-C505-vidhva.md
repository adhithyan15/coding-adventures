## You'll want to know: विधवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C505-SHOK505-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विधवा** — *vidhvā* — "a widow".

> मीरा की दादी तीस साल से विधवा हैं।
>
> *Mīrā kī dādī tīs sāl se vidhvā haiṁ.*
>
> Mira's grandmother has been a widow for thirty years.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विधवा** (*vidhvā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vidhavā*, "a widow" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
