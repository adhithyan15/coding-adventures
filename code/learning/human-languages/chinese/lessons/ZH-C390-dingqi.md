## You'll want to know: 定期
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C390-AGREE390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**定期** — *dìngqī* — "at regular intervals; periodically".

> **你最好定期去看医生** *nǐ zuìhǎo dìngqī qù kàn yīshēng* — You'd better see a doctor regularly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**定期** (*dìngqī*) is built from 定 'settled' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dengH* and *gi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a limit'. The level tone of qī is irregular, as in 星期.
