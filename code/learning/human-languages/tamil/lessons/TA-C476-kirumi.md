## You'll want to know: கிருமி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C476-KIRUMI476-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கிருமி** — *kirumi* — "a germ; a microbe".

> கை கழுவினால் கிருமிகள் போகும்.
>
> *kai kaḻuviṉāl kirumikaḷ pōkum.*
>
> If you wash your hands, the germs go away.

Plural: கிருமிகள், *kirumikaḷ*.

Its plural is regular: கிருமிகள்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கிருமி** (*kirumi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kṛmi*, "a worm, an insect" (Monier-Williams).
