## You'll want to know: யுகம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C342-PANDAIYA342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**யுகம்** — *yukam* — "an age; an era".

> இது கணினி யுகம்.
>
> *itu kaṇiṉi yukam.*
>
> This is the computer age.

Also one of the long ages of Indian tradition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**யுகம்** (*yukam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *yuga*, "yoke, a pair" (CDIAL 10482), which also came to mean an age of the world.
