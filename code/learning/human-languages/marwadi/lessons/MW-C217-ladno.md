## You'll want to know: लादणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C217-CROSS217-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लादणो** — *lādṇo* — "to load (an animal or a cart)".

> हूं काल ऊंट माथै बोझ लादसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl ū̃ṭ māthai bojh lādsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll put the load on the camel.

Past (I …): म्हैं बोरो लाद्यो, *mhaĩ boro lādyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लादणो** (*lādṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lardayati*, "loads"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *lādaï*, "loads" (CDIAL 10966).
