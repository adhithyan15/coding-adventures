## You'll want to know: લપસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C294-LAPAS294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લપસવું** — *lapasvũ* — "to slip".

> વરસાદમાં મીરા પગથિયાં પર લપસી ગઈ.
>
> *varsādmā̃ Mīrā pagthiyā̃ par lapsī gaī.*
>
> In the rain Mira slipped on the steps.

Past (she …): તે લપસી ગઈ, *te lapsī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લપસવું** (*lapasvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. A wet floor makes you **લપસી જવું**: **ભીની ભોંય પર લપસી ગઈ**, she slipped on the wet floor.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
