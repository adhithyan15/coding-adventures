## You'll want to know: أنجز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C371-WASILA371-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أنجز** — *anjaza* — "to accomplish".

> أنجز العمال البناء في سنة.
>
> *anjaza al-ʿummāl al-bināʾ fī sana.*
>
> The workers completed the building in a year.

Present: ينجز, *yunjiz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أنجز** (*anjaza*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-j-z*, "to be accomplished, finished" (Lane). It is Form IV, to bring to completion: to accomplish.
