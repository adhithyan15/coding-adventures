## You'll want to know: ಹಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C433-RAIL433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಳಿ** — *haḷi* — "a rail track; a rail".

> ರೈಲು ಹಳಿಯ ಮೇಲೆ ನಡೆಯಬಾರದು.
>
> *railu haḷiya mēle naḍeyabāradu.*
>
> Nobody should walk on the rail track.

Plural: ಹಳಿಗಳು, *haḷigaḷu*.

ಹಳಿ ತಪ್ಪು, to leave the rails, is said of a train and, loosely, of a plan that goes wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಳಿ** (*haḷi*) looks like a native Kannada word, but its origin is not settled: DEDR shows no clear source for *haḷi* in the sense of a rail or a track.
