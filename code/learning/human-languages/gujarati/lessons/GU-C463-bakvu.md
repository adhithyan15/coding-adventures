## You'll want to know: બકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C463-VACAL463-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બકવું** — *bakvũ* — "to babble; to talk nonsense".

> તાવમાં દર્દી કંઈક બકતો હતો.
>
> *tāvmā̃ dardī kaīk bakto hato.*
>
> In his fever the patient was babbling something.

Past (he …): તે ઘણું બક્યો, *te ghaṇũ bakyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બકવું** (*bakvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*bakk-*, "chatter", an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to prattle" (CDIAL 9117).
