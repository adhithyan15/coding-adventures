Lesson 1 of 5.
ऊर्जा (ūrjā) — energy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cellar, then the Marathi for a loft.

You'll want to know: ऊर्जा.
ऊर्जा — ūrjā — "energy".

The word, taken apart.
ऊर्जा (ūrjā) is Sanskrit ūrjā, "strength, vigour", taken over whole, a tatsama. CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कापूस (kāpūs) — cotton.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for energy, then the Marathi for plastic.

You'll want to know: कापूस.
कापूस — kāpūs — "cotton".

The word, taken apart.
कापूस (kāpūs) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from karpāsa, "cotton" (CDIAL 2877). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word from Austro-Asiatic.
