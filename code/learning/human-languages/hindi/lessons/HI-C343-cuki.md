## You'll want to know: चूँकि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C343-KARAN343-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चूँकि** — *cū̃ki* — "since, as (giving a cause)".

> चूँकि बारिश हो रही थी, इसलिए हम घर पर रहे।
>
> *cū̃ki bāriś ho rahī thī, isliye ham ghar par rahe.*
>
> Since it was raining, we stayed at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूँकि** (*cū̃ki*) is usually traced to Persian *chūnki*, "since, because".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
