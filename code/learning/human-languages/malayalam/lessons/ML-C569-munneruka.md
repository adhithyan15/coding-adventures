## You'll want to know: മുന്നേറുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C569-VIJAYAM569-04]; assesses=[] -->

**മുന്നേറുക** — *munnēṟuka* — "to advance; to forge ahead".

> രണ്ടാം പകുതിയിൽ ഞങ്ങളുടെ ടീം മുന്നേറി.
>
> *raṇṭāṁ pakutiyil ñaṅṅaḷuṭe ṭīṁ munnēṟi.*
>
> In the second half our team pulled ahead.

Past: മുന്നേറി, *munnēṟi*.

മുൻ front + ഏറുക to rise, to climb: to move to the front.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മുന്നേറുക** (*munnēṟuka*) is native Dravidian, a compound of മുൻ, "front, before" (DEDR 5020), and ഏറുക, "to rise, to ascend" (DEDR 916).
