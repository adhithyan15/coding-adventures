## You'll want to know: la trompette
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C511-INSTRUMENTS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la trompette** — "trumpet".

> Mon voisin joue de la trompette le dimanche matin.
>
> *My neighbour plays the trumpet on Sunday mornings.*

*Le* or *la trompettiste* — the trumpeter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la trompette* is built on *trompe* "horn" with the small ending *-ette*; *trompe* comes from a Frankish word *trumpa*.
