## You'll want to know: приговор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C293-SUD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**приговор** (*prigovór*) — "a sentence, a verdict".

> Приговор был очень строгим.
>
> *Prigovór byl óchen strógim.*
>
> The sentence was very harsh.

*Вынести приговор* — to pass sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*приговор* is built on *при-* and the root of *говорить* "to speak", inherited from Proto-Slavic *govoriti* (EtymDB): what is pronounced.
