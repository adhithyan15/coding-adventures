# Review — explain yourself

## Warm-up

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-EXPLICACAO-01, PT-GRAMMAR-ACHO-QUE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-DUAS-SETAS-01, PT-GRAMMAR-CENA-EVENTO-01, PT-LEX-FAZIA-FRIO-01, PT-LEX-C73-CIDADE1-04] -->

Two chapters, four moves. You can say what you think, why, what follows from
it, and what ought to be done. And because chapter two hundred and fifty-nine
gave you the scene, your reasons can reach into the past:

> — Porque é que não foste ao mercado?
> — Fazia frio e eu estava constipado, por isso não saí. Acho que devia sair
> mais.

The reason is scene, *fazia* and *estava*; the consequence is an event, *não
saí*; the advice is about now. Three times in one answer.

- [YOU SAY: a different reason — *Chovia, então estudei.*]
- [YOU SAY: the same answer, claim first — *Não saí porque fazia frio.*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-EXPLICACAO-01, PT-ETYMON-EXPLICAR-02, PT-LEX-DEVIA-01, PT-GRAMMAR-DUAS-SETAS-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In *fazia frio, por isso não saí*, which half is the scene and which
the event? (***Fazia frio*** is the scene; ***não saí*** is the event.) What
does *explicar* do, literally? (It **unfolds**.) And what is softer than
*devo*? (***Devia.***)
