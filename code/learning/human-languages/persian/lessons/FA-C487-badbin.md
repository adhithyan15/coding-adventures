## You'll want to know: بدبین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C487-HOPE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بدبین** (*badbin*) — "pessimistic; distrustful".

> او به همه بدبین است.
>
> *u be hame badbin ast.*
>
> He distrusts everyone.

*bad* 'bad' + *bin* 'seeing'. *be kasi badbin budan* — to distrust someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*badbin* is built on native Persian *bad*, 'bad', and *bin*, the present stem of *didan*, 'to see'. Persian *bad* looks like English *bad*, but they are not related.
