# el examen — a weighing of what you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-FRANCES, ES-LEX-ESTUDIANTE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A student of French may take **un examen de francés**, a French
exam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-EXAMEN]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish **examen** is Latin **exāmen**, a weighing or examination. English
**exam** and **examination** are learned relatives. The old weighing image is a
useful memory hook: an exam weighs what has been learned.

## Grammar Lens: singular and plural stress
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-EXAMEN] -->

The singular is **el examen**, with no written accent because the stress falls
naturally on **-xa-**. The plural is **los exámenes**; the accent appears to
keep that stress when another syllable is added.
