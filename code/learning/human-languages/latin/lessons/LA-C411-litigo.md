## You'll want to know: lītigō, lītigāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C411-TRIBUNAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lītigō, lītigāre** — "to go to law, to quarrel".

> Vīcīnī dē fīnibus lītigant.
>
> *The neighbours are going to law about the boundaries.*

*lītigō, lītigāre, lītigāvī, lītigātum*, built on *līs* and *agō*: to drive a suit. English *litigate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lītigō* is built from *līs, lītis* "a lawsuit" and *agō* "to drive, to conduct" (Lewis & Short). English *litigate* comes from it.
