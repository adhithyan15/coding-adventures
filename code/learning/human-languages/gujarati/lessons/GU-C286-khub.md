## You'll want to know: ખૂબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C286-GUNO286-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખૂબ** — *khūb* — "very; a great deal".

> ચોરી પછી મીરા ખૂબ ડરી ગઈ.
>
> *corī pachī Mīrā khūb ḍarī gaī.*
>
> After the theft Mira was badly frightened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખૂબ** (*khūb*) is usually traced to Persian *khūb*, "good, fine". In Gujarati it makes a word stronger: **ખૂબ સારું**, very good; **ખૂબ ડરી ગઈ**, got very frightened.
