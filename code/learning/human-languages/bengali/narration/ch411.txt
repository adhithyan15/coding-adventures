Bengali, chapter 411: Post and Offices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ডাকঘর (ḍākghôr) — a post office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a square (open place), then the Bengali for to pace up and down.

You'll want to know: ডাকঘর.
ডাকঘর — ḍākghôr — "a post office".
আমি ডাকঘর থেকে একটা খাম কিনলাম। āmi ḍākghôr theke ekṭā khām kinlām. I bought an envelope at the post office.
ডাক is the post itself: ডাকে আসা, to come by post.

The word, taken apart.
ডাকঘর (ḍākghôr) joins ডাক, which CDIAL lists as Bengali ḍāk, "a relay, the post, a posting stage" (CDIAL 5519), to ঘর, "a house": the house of the post.

Lesson 4 of 5.
পিয়ন (piyôn) — a postman; an office messenger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an office, then the Bengali for a letter (sent by post).

You'll want to know: পিয়ন.
পিয়ন — piyôn — "a postman; an office messenger".
পিয়ন দুপুরে আমাদের চিঠি দিয়ে গেল। piyôn dupure āmāder chiṭhi diye gelô. The postman left our letters at midday.

The word, taken apart.
পিয়ন (piyôn) is usually traced to Portuguese peão, "a foot-soldier, a man on foot", the same word as English peon.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পাঠানো (pāṭhāno) — to send.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a letter (sent by post), then the Bengali for a postman.

You'll want to know: পাঠানো.
পাঠানো — pāṭhāno — "to send".
আমি ডাকঘর থেকে দিদিমাকে একটা পার্সেল পাঠালাম। āmi ḍākghôr theke didimāke ekṭā pārsel pāṭhālām. I sent my grandmother a parcel from the post office.
Past (he/she …): তিনি চিঠি পাঠালেন, tini chiṭhi pāṭhālen.
The one you send to takes -কে: বাবাকে টাকা পাঠানো (pāṭhāno), to send money to Father. Also for people: ছেলেকে স্কুলে পাঠানো (pāṭhāno), to send a son to school.

The word, taken apart.
পাঠানো (pāṭhāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali paṭhāna, "sends", under Sanskrit prátiṣṭhati, "sets out", and its causing form prásthāpayati, "sends" (CDIAL 8607): to send is to make someone set out.
