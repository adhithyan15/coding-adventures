## You'll want to know: りれきしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C457-JOBHUNT457-03]; assesses=[] -->

**りれきしょ** — *rirekisho* — "a curriculum vitae".

> **りれきしょを かいしゃに おくりました。** — *rirekisho o kaisha ni okurimashita* — "I sent my curriculum vitae to the company."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りれきしょ** (*rirekisho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ri*, "perform" (Middle Chinese *lijX*), *reki*, "curriculum" (*lek*), and *sho*, "write" (*syo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
