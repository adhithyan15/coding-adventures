## You'll want to know: مقبرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C338-MAWTA338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مقبرة** — *maqbara* — "a cemetery".

> زرنا المقبرة صباح العيد.
>
> *zurnā al-maqbara ṣabāḥ al-ʿīd.*
>
> We visited the cemetery on the morning of the holiday.

Plural: مقابر, *maqābir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقبرة** (*maqbara*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-r* (Lane), "to bury". The *mafʿala* pattern names a place: "the place of graves".
