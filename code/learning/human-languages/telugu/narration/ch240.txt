Telugu, chapter 240: To lift, To carry, To pull, To push, To throw.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎత్తు (ettu) — to lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a pension, then the Telugu for an agreement.

You'll want to know: ఎత్తు.
ఎత్తు — ettu — "to lift".

The word, taken apart.
ఎత్తు (ettu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 796 lists ettu, "to lift up, raise", with Kannada ettu, "to lift".

Lesson 5 of 5.
విసురు (visuru) — to throw, to fling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to pull, then the Telugu for to push.

You'll want to know: విసురు.
విసురు — visuru — "to throw, to fling".

The word, taken apart.
విసురు (visuru) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5450 lists visuru, "to throw, fling", with Tamil vīcu, "to throw, fling". DEDR compares Sanskrit vyajana, "fanning; fan" (CDIAL 12043).
