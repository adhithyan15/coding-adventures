## You'll want to know: die Handlung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C316-ERZAEHLER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Handlung** — "the plot, the action".

> Die Handlung des Films war spannend.
>
> *The plot of the film was exciting.*

Plural: *die Handlungen*. Also an act or deed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Handlung* is built on *handeln* "to act", from Old High German *hantalōn*, which EtymDB derives from Proto-Germanic *handuz* "hand".
