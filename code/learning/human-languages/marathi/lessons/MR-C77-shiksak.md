## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिक्षक** (*shikṣak*) is Sanskrit *śikṣaka*, "one who teaches", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. It is built on *śikṣā*, "learning, study".
