## You'll want to know: हड़ताल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C509-HADTAL509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हड़ताल** — *haṛtāl* — "a strike (stopping work)".

> कारख़ाने के मज़दूर तीन दिन से हड़ताल पर हैं।
>
> *Kārkhāne ke mazdūr tīn din se haṛtāl par haiṁ.*
>
> The factory workers have been on strike for three days.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हड़ताल** (*haṛtāl*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *haṭṭa*, "a market, a shop", with the Hindi sense "closing shops in mourning or protest" (CDIAL 13944). Today it is any stopping of work.
