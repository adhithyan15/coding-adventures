## You'll want to know: पासपोर्ट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C410-PASPORT410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पासपोर्ट** — *pāsporṭ* — "a passport".

> अपना पासपोर्ट हमेशा पास रखिए।
>
> *apnā pāsporṭ hameśā pās rakhiye.*
>
> Always keep your passport on you.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पासपोर्ट** (*pāsporṭ*) is borrowed from English *passport*.
