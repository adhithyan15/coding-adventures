## You'll want to know: o criminoso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C389-CRIME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o criminoso** — "the criminal".

> A polícia apanhou o criminoso.
>
> *The police caught the criminal.*

Also an adjective: *um ato criminoso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*criminoso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *crīminōsus* "reproachful, criminal" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *crīmen* "a charge, a crime".
