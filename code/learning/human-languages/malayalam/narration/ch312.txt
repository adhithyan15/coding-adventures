Malayalam, chapter 312: Disease, Patient, Nurse, Injection, Ambulance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
രോഗം (rōgaṁ) — a disease, an illness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to bear fruit, then the Malayalam for to spread.

You'll want to know: രോഗം.
രോഗം — rōgaṁ — "a disease, an illness".

The word, taken apart.
രോഗം (rōgaṁ) from Sanskrit rōga, "disease", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 10829).

Lesson 4 of 5.
കുത്തിവയ്പ്പ് (kuttivayppŭ) — an injection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a patient, then the Malayalam for a nurse.

You'll want to know: കുത്തിവയ്പ്പ്.
കുത്തിവയ്പ്പ് — kuttivayppŭ — "an injection".

The word, taken apart.
കുത്തിവയ്പ്പ് (kuttivayppŭ) is native Dravidian, "a piercing-in": kutti, from the verb "to pierce, prick" (DEDR 1719, with Tamil kuttu), and vayppu, "a putting", from the verb "to place" (DEDR 5549).
