## You'll want to know: বেকার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C494-KAJKHOJA494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বেকার** — *bekār* — "unemployed; out of work".

> আমার ভাই এক বছর ধরে বেকার।
>
> *Āmār bhāi ek bôchhôr dhôre bekār.*
>
> My brother has been out of work for a year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেকার** (*bekār*) is usually traced to Persian *bekār*, "without work": *be-*, "without", and *kār*, "work".
