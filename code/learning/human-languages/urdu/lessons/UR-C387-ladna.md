## You'll want to know: لادنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C387-QULI387-03]; assesses=[] -->

**لادنا** — *lādnā* — "to load".

> مزدور ٹرک پر سامان لادتے ہیں۔
>
> *mazdūr ṭrak par sāmān lādte haiṅ.*
>
> The labourers load the goods onto the lorry.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گاڑی پر سامان لادا, *us ne gāṛī par sāmān lādā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لادنا** (*lādnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lardayati*, "loads" (CDIAL 10966).
