## You'll want to know: अपेक्षते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C367-SAMBHAVA367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अपेक्षते** — *apekṣate* — "expects".

> यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि कृषकः उत्तमं धान्यम् अपेक्षते।
>
> *yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi kṛṣakaḥ uttamaṃ dhānyam apekṣate.*
>
> If it rains, the farmer expects a good harvest.

Past (he …): सः उत्तरम् अपेक्षितवान्, *saḥ uttaram apekṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपेक्षते** (*apekṣate*) is built on *apa*, "away", and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives *apekṣ* "to look for, to wait for, to expect" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
