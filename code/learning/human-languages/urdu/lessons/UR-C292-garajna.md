## You'll want to know: گرجنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C292-TUFAN292-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گرجنا** — *garajnā* — "to thunder; to roar".

> رات کو بادل گرج رہے تھے۔
>
> *rāt ko bādal garaj rahe the.*
>
> The clouds were thundering in the night.

Past (it …): وہ شیر گرج اٹھا, *voh sher garaj uṭhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from Sanskrit *gárjati*, "thunders, roars", where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gājnā* (CDIAL 4046); **گرجنا** keeps the Sanskrit shape closer. Clouds **گرجتے ہیں**, and so does a lion.
