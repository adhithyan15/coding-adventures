Malayalam, chapter 124: Welcome and Good Wishes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
അഭിനന്ദനങ്ങൾ (abhinandanaṅṅaḷ) — congratulations.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for other, then the Malayalam for wrong.

You'll want to know: അഭിനന്ദനങ്ങൾ.
അഭിനന്ദനങ്ങൾ (abhinandanaṅṅaḷ) — "congratulations". The ending ‑ങ്ങൾ is the plural: many good wishes.

The word, taken apart.
അഭിനന്ദനങ്ങൾ (abhinandanaṅṅaḷ) from Sanskrit abhinandana, "rejoicing, congratulation", taken over whole as a tatsama, with the feminine ending -ḷ. Malayalam uses it in the plural, many congratulations.

What you've built.
Good news deserves a word of its own.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ക്ഷണം (kṣaṇaṁ) — an invitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for wrong, then the Malayalam for congratulations.

You'll want to know: ക്ഷണം.
ക്ഷണം (kṣaṇaṁ) — "an invitation". The ക്ഷ is k and ṣ joined into one.

The word, taken apart.
ക്ഷണം (kṣaṇaṁ) is probably Sanskrit kṣaṇa, "a moment; leisure; a festival" (CDIAL 3642); the sense "invitation" grew up in Malayalam, and how is not certain.

Lesson 3 of 5.
സമ്മാനം (sammānaṁ) — a gift, a prize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for congratulations, then the Malayalam for an invitation.

You'll want to know: സമ്മാനം.
സമ്മാനം (sammānaṁ) — "a gift", and also "a prize".

The word, taken apart.
സമ്മാനം (sammānaṁ) from Sanskrit sammāna, "honour, respect", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ. A gift is an honour shown.

Lesson 4 of 5.
സ്വാഗതം (svāgataṁ) — welcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an invitation, then the Malayalam for a gift.

You'll want to know: സ്വാഗതം.
സ്വാഗതം (svāgataṁ) — "welcome", the formal greeting on a signboard or at a door.

The word, taken apart.
സ്വാഗതം (svāgataṁ) from Sanskrit svāgata, "welcome", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 13920). Its parts are su, "well", and āgata, "come".

What you've built.
The formal greeting for a guest.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ആശംസകൾ (āśaṁsakaḷ) — good wishes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a gift, then the Malayalam for welcome.

You'll want to know: ആശംസകൾ.
ആശംസകൾ (āśaṁsakaḷ) — "good wishes". Like അഭിനന്ദനങ്ങൾ (abhinandanaṅṅaḷ), it is said in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
ആശംസകൾ (āśaṁsakaḷ) from Sanskrit āśaṁsā, "hope, wish", taken over whole as a tatsama, with the feminine ending -ḷ. Malayalam uses it in the plural.

What you've built.
Wishes, many of them.
