## You'll want to know: भिक्षुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C401-BHIKSHU401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भिक्षुः** — *bhikṣuḥ* — "a mendicant monk".

> भिक्षुः प्रातः ग्रामं गच्छति।
>
> *bhikṣuḥ prātaḥ grāmaṃ gacchati.*
>
> The monk goes to the village in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भिक्षुः** (*bhikṣuḥ*) comes from the root √bhikṣ, "to beg, to ask for alms"; MW gives *bhikṣu* as a beggar, a religious mendicant and a Buddhist monk (MW).
