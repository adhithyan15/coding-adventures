Lesson 1 of 5.
फलंदाज (phalandāj) — a batsman; a batter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a contestant, then the Marathi for to carry off.

You'll want to know: फलंदाज.
फलंदाज — phalandāj — "a batsman; a batter".
फलंदाजाने चेंडू मैदानाबाहेर मारला. phalandājāne ceṇḍū maidānābāher mārlā. The batsman hit the ball out of the ground.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
फलंदाज (phalandāj) is a Marathi coinage made on the pattern of गोलंदाज (golandāj), the bowler, whose -अंदाज is Persian andāz, "thrower". The first part is probably Sanskrit phala, glossed also as a blade (Monier-Williams): the flat face of the bat. That reading is not settled.

You'll want to know: धावपटू.
धावपटू — dhāvpaṭū — "a runner; an athlete".
तो धावपटू रोज सकाळी मैदानावर धावतो. to dhāvpaṭū roj sakāḷī maidānāvar dhāvto. That runner runs on the ground every morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
धावपटू (dhāvpaṭū) is built from धाव, "a run", from धावणे, and the Sanskrit paṭu, "skilful" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: टोलवणे.
टोलवणे — ṭolavṇe — "to hit away; to fend off".
फलंदाजाने पहिला चेंडू लांब टोलवला. phalandājāne pahilā ceṇḍū lāmb ṭolavlā. The batsman hit the first ball a long way.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चेंडू टोलवला, tyāne ceṇḍū ṭolavlā.

The word, taken apart.
टोलवणे (ṭolavṇe) is a Marathi verb for knocking something away; its origin is not settled.

You'll want to know: झेलणे.
झेलणे — jhelṇe — "to catch a ball".

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चेंडू झेलला, tyāne ceṇḍū jhellā.

The word, taken apart.
झेलणे (jhelṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi jhelṇẽ, "to catch", under jhēlati, "undergoes" (CDIAL 5413).
