Spanish, chapter 485: Las tareas de casa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
barrer — to sweep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to thank for, then the Spanish for to save.

You'll want to know: barrer.
barrer — "to sweep".
Hoy me toca barrer la cocina. Today it is my turn to sweep the kitchen.
Me toca — it is my turn.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: barrer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: barrer, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with barrer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for to thank for, then the Spanish for to save, then say barrer again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does barrer mean? ("To sweep".) Say it once more.
