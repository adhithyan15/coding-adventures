# ところどころ (tokorodokoro) — here and there; in places

## You'll want to know: ところどころ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C315-SURUTO315-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ところどころ** — *tokorodokoro* — "here and there; in places".

> **みちは ところどころ こおっていました。** — *michi wa tokorodokoro kōtte imashita* — "The road was icy in places."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ところどころ** (*tokorodokoro*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tokoro*, "place", and *dokoro*, "place" (from *tokoro*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
