Bengali, chapter 497: Leaving Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ইস্তফা (istôphā) — a resignation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for senior in rank, then the Bengali for to stay in post.

You'll want to know: ইস্তফা.
ইস্তফা — istôphā — "a resignation".
মামা চাকরি থেকে ইস্তফা দিলেন। Māmā chākri theke istôphā dilen. My uncle resigned from his job.
With দেওয়া: ইস্তফা (istôphā) দেওয়া, to resign.

The word, taken apart.
ইস্তফা (istôphā) is usually traced to Arabic istiʿfā, "asking to be let off", which reached Bengali through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ধর্মঘট (dhôrmôghôṭ) — a strike by workers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a resignation, then the Bengali for a job transfer.

You'll want to know: ধর্মঘট.
ধর্মঘট — dhôrmôghôṭ — "a strike by workers".
আজ বাসের ধর্মঘট, তাই আমি হেঁটে অফিসে গেলাম। Āj bāser dhôrmôghôṭ, tāi āmi hẽṭe ôphise gelām. There's a bus strike today, so I walked to the office.

The word, taken apart.
ধর্মঘট (dhôrmôghôṭ) joins Sanskrit dharma, "what is established, duty" (Monier-Williams), and ghaṭa, "a water pot" (CDIAL 4406); how the pair came to mean a strike is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ছাঁটাই করা (chhā̃ṭāi kôrā) — to lay off (workers).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a strike by workers, then the Bengali for dismissed.

You'll want to know: ছাঁটাই করা.
ছাঁটাই করা — chhā̃ṭāi kôrā — "to lay off (workers)".
কারখানাটা পঞ্চাশজন মজুর ছাঁটাই করল। Kārkhānāṭā pônchāshjôn mojur chhā̃ṭāi kôrlô. The factory laid off fifty labourers.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দশজন কর্মী ছাঁটাই করলেন, tini dôshjôn kôrmī chhā̃ṭāi kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
ছাঁটাই করা (chhā̃ṭāi kôrā) joins ছাঁটাই, "a cutting back", built on ছাঁটা, "to trim", to করা, "to do"; CDIAL lists Bengali chā̃ṭā, "to lop", under a reconstructed chāṇṭ, "cut" (CDIAL 5013). Of workers it means to lay them off.
