German, chapter 394: Im Internet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
hochladen — to upload.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the attachment, then the German for to attach.

You'll want to know: hochladen.
hochladen — "to upload".
Sie hat das Video gestern hochgeladen. She uploaded the video yesterday.
Separable and strong: sie lädt hoch, sie hat hochgeladen.

The word, taken apart.
hochladen renders English upload part by part: hoch "up", Old High German hōh (EtymDB), and laden "to load", Old High German hladan (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Webseite — the web page, the website.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to attach, then the German for to upload.

You'll want to know: die Webseite.
die Webseite — "the web page, the website".
Die Öffnungszeiten stehen auf der Webseite. The opening hours are on the website.
Plural: die Webseiten. Also written die Website, said the English way.

The word, taken apart.
Webseite joins English web to Seite "page, side", inherited from Old High German sīta and Proto-Germanic sīdōn (EtymDB), after English web page.

Suchmaschine joins suchen "to search", Proto-Germanic sōkijaną (EtymDB), to Maschine "machine", from Latin māchina and Greek mēkhanḗ (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
posten — to post (online).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the web page, then the German for the search engine.

You'll want to know: posten.
posten — "to post (online)".
Er postet jeden Tag Fotos von seinem Essen. He posts photos of his food every day.
Perfekt: hat gepostet.

The word, taken apart.
posten was borrowed from English post (EtymDB). The English verb is usually traced to putting a notice up on a post, a word that goes back to Latin postis "a doorpost" (Lewis & Short): you put a message up.

Lesson 5 of 5.
kommentieren — to comment on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the search engine, then the German for to post.

You'll want to know: kommentieren.
kommentieren — "to comment on".
Viele Leute haben den Artikel kommentiert. Many people commented on the article.
It takes a direct object: einen Beitrag kommentieren. Der Kommentar — the comment.

The word, taken apart.
kommentieren is a learned borrowing from Latin commentārī "to think over, to make notes on", of the family of comminīscī "to devise, to invent" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
