# വിശദീകരണം (viśadīkaraṇaṁ) — an explanation

## You'll want to know: വിശദീകരണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C409-KURRAM409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വിശദീകരണം** — *viśadīkaraṇaṁ* — "an explanation".

> വൈകിയതിന് അവൻ ഒരു വിശദീകരണവും നൽകിയില്ല.
>
> *vaikiyatinŭ avan oru viśadīkaraṇavuṁ nalkiyilla.*
>
> He gave no explanation for being late.

The verb വിശദീകരിക്കുക, to explain, is the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിശദീകരണം** (*viśadīkaraṇaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *viśada*, "clear, bright" (Monier-Williams), with *-ī-karaṇa*, "a making into": a making clear. It is the noun of വിശദീകരിക്കുക.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
