## You'll want to know: erkälten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C479-SCHNUPFEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**erkälten** — "to catch a cold (sich erkälten)".

> Im Regen habe ich mich erkältet.
>
> *I caught a cold in the rain.*

Reflexive: *sich erkälten*. *Erkältet sein* — to have a cold.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erkälten* comes from Middle High German *erkelten* (EtymDB), built on *kalt* "cold", which is inherited from Old High German *kalt*, from Proto-Germanic *kaldaz*.
