## You'll want to know: разбиваться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C298-NAEZD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**разбиваться** (*razbivátsya*) — "to crash; to break, to smash".

> Чашка упала и разбилась.
>
> *Cháshka upála i razbílas.*
>
> The cup fell and broke.

Imperfective; perfective *разбиться* (*razbítsya*), *разобьётся* (*razobyótsya*). Of a car or a plane: to crash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разбиваться* is built on *раз-* "apart" and *бить* "to beat", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *biti* "to beat", from PIE *bʰeyH-* (EtymDB).
