## You'll want to know: سرانجام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C302-AGHAZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سرانجام** (*saranjâm*) — "finally, at last".

> سرانجام بعد از سه ساعت به خانه رسیدیم.
>
> *Saranjâm ba'd az se sâ'at be khâne residim.*
>
> Finally, after three hours, we reached home.

Written and storytelling Persian; in speech *bel-akhare*. Also a noun, "outcome": *saranjâm-e kâr* "the outcome of the affair".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saranjâm* joins *sar* "head" and *anjâm* "end, completion", both native Persian words. EtymDB lists the compound with no parent.
