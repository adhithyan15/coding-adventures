## You'll want to know: ডাইনি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C322-RAKKHOSH322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ডাইনি** — *ḍāini* — "a witch".

> রূপকথার ডাইনিটা বনের ভিতরে একা থাকত।
>
> *rūpkôthār ḍāiniṭā bôner bhitôre ekā thāktô.*
>
> The witch in the fairy tale lived alone deep in the forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডাইনি** (*ḍāini*) comes down from Sanskrit *ḍākinī*, a female spirit in the train of the goddess Kālī (CDIAL 5542, which lists Bengali *ḍāin* and *ḍāini*, 'a witch').
