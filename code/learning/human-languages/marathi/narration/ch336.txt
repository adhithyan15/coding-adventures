Marathi, chapter 336: Caves and Hidden Treasure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लेणी (leṇī) — rock-cut caves.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to dig, then the Marathi for to carve.

You'll want to know: लेणी.
लेणी — leṇī — "rock-cut caves".
डोंगरात कोरलेली प्राचीन लेणी आम्ही पाहिली. ḍoṅgrāt korlelī prācīn leṇī āmhī pāhilī. We saw the ancient caves carved into the hill.
Neuter.
A neuter plural, the usual form: ही लेणी (leṇī), these caves; one cave is लेणं.

The word, taken apart.
लेणी (leṇī) is usually traced to Sanskrit layana, 'a place of rest, a cell', which Pali has as lēṇa, 'a rock cell used by ascetics', and Prakrit as lēṇa, 'a cave' (CDIAL 10963); CDIAL gives no Marathi form, so the path is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गुहा (guhā) — a cave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to carve, then the Marathi for rock-cut caves.

You'll want to know: गुहा.
गुहा — guhā — "a cave".
वाघ गुहेत झोपला होता. vāgh guhet jhoplā hotā. The tiger was asleep in the cave.
Feminine.
The oblique is गुहे-: गुहेत, in the cave.

The word, taken apart.
गुहा (guhā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit guhā, 'a hiding place, a cave' (Monier-Williams).

गुप्त — gupta — "secret; hidden".
त्याने गुप्त जागी पैसे ठेवले. tyāne gupta jāgī paise ṭhevle. He kept the money in a secret place.
गुप्त (gupta) keeps one form: गुप्त (gupta) मार्ग, गुप्त (gupta) गोष्ट.

The word, taken apart.
गुप्त (gupta) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit gupta, 'guarded, protected, hidden' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
भुयार (bhuyār) — an underground passage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cave, then the Marathi for secret.

You'll want to know: भुयार.
भुयार — bhuyār — "an underground passage".
किल्ल्यातून नदीपर्यंत एक गुप्त भुयार होतं. killyātūn nadīparyant ek gupta bhuyār hotaṁ. There was a secret passage from the fort to the river.
Neuter.
भुयारी मार्ग, a subway.

The word, taken apart.
भुयार (bhuyār) is the Marathi word for a passage under the ground; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
खजिना (khajinā) — a treasure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for secret, then the Marathi for an underground passage.

You'll want to know: खजिना.
खजिना — khajinā — "a treasure".
भुयारात राजाचा खजिना लपवला होता. bhuyārāt rājācā khajinā lapavlā hotā. The king's treasure had been hidden in the passage.
Masculine.
Also a treasury.

The word, taken apart.
खजिना (khajinā) is a loan from Persian xazīna, usually traced to Arabic khazīna, 'a treasury'.
