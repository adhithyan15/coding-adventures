# Return to asking and saying no

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214] -->

[YOU HEAR: *are you a traveller?*] Say **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹੋ?** Then answer:
**ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰੀ ਹਾਂ।**

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211] -->

You can also say **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ** and **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ**. The
next lesson puts **ਕੀ** in front of the second one.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *is the garden open?*] Say **ਕੀ ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *I don't drink tea*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *you drink tea*] Say **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ।**

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Ask one yes-or-no question, then say one thing you don't do.
