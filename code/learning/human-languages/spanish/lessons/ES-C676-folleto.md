## You'll want to know: el folleto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C676-TURISMO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el folleto** — "the leaflet, the brochure".

> Coge un folleto en la oficina de turismo.
>
> *Take a leaflet at the tourist office.*

Hotels and museums give them out for free.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*folleto* came from Italian *foglietto* "a little sheet" (EtymDB), built on *foglio*, from Latin *folium* "leaf".
