## You'll want to know: l'enfance
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C309-BAPTEME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'enfance** — "childhood".

> Il a passé son enfance à la campagne.
>
> *He spent his childhood in the countryside.*

*Un ami d'enfance* — a childhood friend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enfance* comes from Old French *enfance*, from Latin *īnfantia* "childhood" (EtymDB), built on *īnfāns* "unable to speak".
