## You'll want to know: ministrō, ministrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C356-APPONO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ministrō, ministrāre** — "to wait at table, to serve".

> Puerī vīnum ministrābant.
>
> *The slave boys were serving the wine.*

*ministrō, ministrāre, ministrāvī, ministrātum*, from *minister*, 'servant'. English *minister*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ministrō** is built on *minister*, "servant, attendant" (EtymDB), itself from *minus*, "less", as *magister* is from *magis*. English *minister* comes from it.
