## You'll want to know: సమీక్షించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C416-VYASAM416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**సమీక్షించు** — *samīkṣiñcu* — "to review; to assess".

> మంత్రి పథకాల పనిని సమీక్షించారు.
>
> *mantri pathakāla panini samīkṣiñcāru.*
>
> The minister reviewed the work on the schemes.

Past (I …): సమీక్షించాను, *samīkṣiñcānu*.

The noun is సమీక్ష, a review.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమీక్షించు** (*samīkṣiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *samīkṣā*, "thorough or close inspection" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
