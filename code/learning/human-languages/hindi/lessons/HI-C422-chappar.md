## You'll want to know: छप्पर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C422-KOTHI422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छप्पर** — *chappar* — "a thatched roof".

> बारिश में झोंपड़ी का छप्पर टपकता है।
>
> *bāriś meṁ jhõpṛī kā chappar ṭapaktā hai.*
>
> The hut's thatched roof leaks in the rain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छप्पर** (*chappar*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *chattvara*, "a house, a bower" (CDIAL 4976), which lists Hindi *chappar*, "a sloping thatch, a hut".
