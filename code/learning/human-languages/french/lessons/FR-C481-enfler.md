## You'll want to know: enfler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C481-ENTORSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**enfler** — "to swell".

> Sa cheville a enflé pendant la nuit.
>
> *Her ankle swelled during the night.*

*Enflé* — swollen. The everyday word is often *gonfler*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enfler* is inherited from Latin *īnflāre* "to blow into, to puff up" (EtymDB), built on *flāre* "to blow" (Lewis & Short).
