## You'll want to know: ચાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C170-SWEET170-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાળવું** — *cāḷvũ* — "to sift".

> **તે લોટ ચાળે છે.** — *te loṭ cāḷe che* — "She sifts the flour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાળવું** (*cāḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *cālayati*, "shakes, jogs", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to sift" (CDIAL 4772).
