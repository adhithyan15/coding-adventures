## You'll want to know: dirigieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C515-CHOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dirigieren** — "to conduct (music)".

> Heute dirigiert eine junge Frau das Konzert.
>
> *Today a young woman is conducting the concert.*

Regular, Perfekt without *ge-*: *hat dirigiert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dirigieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dīrigere* "to run, to direct" (EtymDB).
