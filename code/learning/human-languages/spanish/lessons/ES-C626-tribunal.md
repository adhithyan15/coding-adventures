## You'll want to know: el tribunal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C626-JUICIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el tribunal** — "the court".

> El caso llegó al tribunal.
>
> *The case reached the court.*

Also an exam board: *el tribunal del examen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tribunal* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tribunal*, the raised seat of the tribunes, built on *tribunus* and *tribus* 'tribe' (EtymDB).
