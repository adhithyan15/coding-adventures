Marathi, chapter 509: Moving Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पदोन्नती (padonnatī) — a promotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pay rise, then the Marathi for to earn.

You'll want to know: पदोन्नती.
पदोन्नती — padonnatī — "a promotion".
मीराला या वर्षी पदोन्नती मिळाली. mīrālā yā varṣī padonnatī miḷālī. Meera got a promotion this year.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
पदोन्नती (padonnatī) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit pada, "step, position", and unnati, "rising" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
झटणे (jhaṭṇe) — to strive; to work hard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for senior, then the Marathi for junior.

You'll want to know: झटणे.
झटणे — jhaṭṇe — "to strive; to work hard".
पदोन्नतीसाठी तो दिवसरात्र झटला. padonnatīsāṭhī to divasrātra jhaṭlā. He strove day and night for the promotion.
Past (he …): तो झटला, to jhaṭlā.
The goal takes -साठी: यशासाठी झटणे (jhaṭṇe).

The word, taken apart.
झटणे (jhaṭṇe) is an expressive Marathi verb. CDIAL lists a Marathi jhaṭṇe, "to collide violently", under jhaṭ, "sudden movement" (CDIAL 5327), but not the sense "to strive", so the link is not settled.
