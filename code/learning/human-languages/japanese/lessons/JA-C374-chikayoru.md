# ちかよる (chikayoru) — to go near; to approach

## You'll want to know: ちかよる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C374-SENRO374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ちかよる** — *chikayoru* — "to go near; to approach".

> **せんろに ちかよらないで ください。** — *senro ni chikayoranaide kudasai* — "Please keep away from the railway line."

Polite **ちかよります**, *chikayorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが せんろに ちかよりました。**, *kodomo ga senro ni chikayorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちかよる** (*chikayoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *chika*, "near" (from *chikai*) and *yoru*, "to draw close"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
