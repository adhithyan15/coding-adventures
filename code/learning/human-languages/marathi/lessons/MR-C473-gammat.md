## You'll want to know: गंमत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C473-KHODKAR473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गंमत** — *gammat* — "amusement; a bit of fun".

> पावसात भिजायला खूप गंमत आली.
>
> *pāvsāt bhijāylā khūp gammat ālī.*
>
> Getting soaked in the rain was great fun.

Feminine.

गंमत येणे, to be fun, with the dative; गंमत म्हणून, as a joke.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गंमत** (*gammat*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
