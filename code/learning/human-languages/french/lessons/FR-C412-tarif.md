## You'll want to know: le tarif
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C412-GUICHET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le tarif** — "the fare, the price (list)".

> Les étudiants paient un tarif réduit.
>
> *Students pay a reduced fare.*

*Plein tarif* — full price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tarif* was borrowed from Italian *tariffa*, which came from Arabic *taʿrīfa* 'notice, list of rates' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
