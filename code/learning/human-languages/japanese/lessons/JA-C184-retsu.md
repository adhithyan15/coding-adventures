## You'll want to know: れつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C184-GOODS184-02]; assesses=[] -->

**れつ** — *retsu* — "queue; line, row".

> **みせの まえに ながい れつが あります。** — *mise no mae ni nagai retsu ga arimasu* — "There is a long queue in front of the shop."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**れつ** (*retsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character, "row, file", read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *ljet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
