## You'll want to know: जर्जरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C509-JARJARA509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जर्जरः** — *jarjaraḥ* — "rickety; worn to pieces".

> मम आसन्दी जर्जरा अस्ति।
>
> *mama āsandī jarjarā asti.*
>
> My chair is rickety.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जर्जरः** (*jarjaraḥ*) is the adjective MW records as infirm, decrepit, decayed, broken in pieces; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
