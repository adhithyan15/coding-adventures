## You'll want to know: मात्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C380-MATRA380-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मात्रा** — *mātrā* — "a quantity; a measure".

> यदि औषधस्य मात्रा अधिका, तर्हि हानिः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi auṣadhasya mātrā adhikā, tarhi hāniḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the dose of medicine is too large, it will do harm.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मात्रा** (*mātrā*) comes from the root √mā, "to measure"; MW gives *mātrā* "measure, quantity, size, duration, degree" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
