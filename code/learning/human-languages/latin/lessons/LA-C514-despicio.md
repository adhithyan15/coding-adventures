## You'll want to know: dēspiciō, dēspicere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C514-CONT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**dēspiciō, dēspicere** — "to look down on, despise".

> Dīves pauperēs dēspicit.
>
> *The rich man looks down on the poor.*

*dēspiciō, dēspicere, dēspexī, dēspectum*: *dē* 'down' + *speciō* 'to look'. English *despise*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēspiciō** is built on *dē-* "down" and *speciō* "to behold" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *despise* comes from it through Old French.
