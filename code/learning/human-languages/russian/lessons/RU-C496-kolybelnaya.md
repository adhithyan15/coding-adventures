## You'll want to know: колыбельная
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C496-BABY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**колыбельная** (*kolybélnaya*) — "a lullaby".

> Бабушка поёт внучке колыбельную.
>
> *Bábushka poyót vnúchke kolybélnuyu.*
>
> Grandma sings her granddaughter a lullaby.

Short for *колыбельная песня*: an adjective used as a noun. Built on *колыбель* — a cradle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*колыбельная* is built on *колыбель* 'cradle', usually linked to *колыхать* 'to sway'; EtymDB has no entry.
