## You'll want to know: zīyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C384-BECOME384-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zīyuán* — "natural resources".

> *shuǐ shì hěn zhòngyào de zīyuán suǒyǐ wǒmen bù yīnggāi làngfèi* — Water is a very important resource, so we shouldn't waste it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zīyuán* is built from *zī* 'goods' and *yuán* 'a source', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsij* and *ngjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'wealth' and 'to help' and the second 'where water rises'.
