# юность (yúnost) — youth, the teenage years

## You'll want to know: юность
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C304-DETSTVO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**юность** (*yúnost*) — "youth, the teenage years".

> В юности он играл на гитаре.
>
> *V yúnosti on igrál na gitáre.*
>
> In his youth he played the guitar.

Feminine. Roughly the years from fifteen to twenty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*юность* is built on *юный* "young", which EtymDB marks as a borrowing from Church Slavonic *junŭ*, from Proto-Slavic *junŭ* and PIE *h₂yuh₁en-* "young"; English *young* is a cousin.
