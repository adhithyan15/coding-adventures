## You'll want to know: o funeral
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C315-FUNERAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o funeral** — "the funeral".

> O funeral foi na sexta-feira de manhã.
>
> *The funeral was on Friday morning.*

*Ir ao funeral* — to go to the funeral.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*funeral* was borrowed from Late Latin *fūnerālis* "of a funeral" (EtymDB), from *fūnus* "a funeral, death".
