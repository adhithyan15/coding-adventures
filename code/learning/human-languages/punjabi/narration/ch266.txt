Punjabi, chapter 266: Around the Town.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਮਹੱਲਾ (mahallā) — a neighbourhood; a quarter of a town.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the distance to travel, then the Punjabi for a kos.

You'll want to know: ਮਹੱਲਾ.
ਮਹੱਲਾ — mahallā — "a neighbourhood; a quarter of a town".
ਸਾਡਾ ਮਹੱਲਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਸ਼ਾਂਤ ਹੈ। sāḍā mahallā bahut shā̃t hai. Our neighbourhood is very calm.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਹੱਲਾ (mahallā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic mahalla, "a quarter of a town".

Lesson 3 of 6.
ਹੱਟੀ (haṭṭī) — a small shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a neighbourhood, then the Punjabi for a small town.

You'll want to know: ਹੱਟੀ.
ਹੱਟੀ — haṭṭī — "a small shop".
ਗਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਹੱਟੀ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਉੱਥੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਮਿਲਦਾ ਹੈ। galī vich ikk haṭṭī hai ate utthe dudh mildā hai. There is a little shop in the lane, and you can get milk there.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਹੱਟੀ (haṭṭī) is inherited from Sanskrit haṭṭa, "market, fair", beside haṭṭī, "petty market"; CDIAL lists Punjabi haṭṭ, "shop", under it (CDIAL 13944).

ਅਸੀਂ ਸੜਕ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਢਾਬੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਖਾਣਾ ਖਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ। asīṁ saṛak de neṛe ḍhābe utte khāṇā khānde hāṁ. We eat at a roadside eatery near the road.
