## You'll want to know: انتقد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C370-NAQD370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقد** — *intaqada* — "to criticize".

> انتقد المدرب أداء اللاعبين.
>
> *intaqada al-mudarrib adāʾ al-lāʿibīn.*
>
> The coach criticized the players' performance.

Present: ينتقد, *yantaqid*.

The noun انتقاد, *intiqād*, criticism.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقد** (*intaqada*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-d*, "to sort good coins from bad" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to pick out the good from the bad: to judge, to criticize.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
