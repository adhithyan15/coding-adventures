## You'll want to know: shěbude
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C313-MOVE313-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shěbude* — "to hate to part with; to hate to leave".

> *wǒ shěbude líkāi zhèr* — I hate to leave this place.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The tone of 不 drops away here: it is said light, without a tone, shěbude.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shěbude* is built from *shě* 'to let go', 不 'not' and 得 'to get', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *syaeX*, *pjut* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to release': not able to let go.
