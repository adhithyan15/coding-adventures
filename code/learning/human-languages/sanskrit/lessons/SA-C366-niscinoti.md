## You'll want to know: निश्चिनोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C366-NISHKARSHA366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चिनोति** — *niścinoti* — "ascertains; makes certain".

> समितिः सत्यं निश्चिनोति।
>
> *samitiḥ satyaṃ niścinoti.*
>
> The committee ascertains the truth.

Past (he …): सः तथ्यं निश्चितवान्, *saḥ tathyaṃ niścitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चिनोति** (*niścinoti*) is built on *niś* and the root √ci, "to gather"; MW gives *niś-cita* "one who has come to a conclusion, determined" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
