## You'll want to know: ほめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C247-PRAISE247-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ほめる** — *homeru* — "to praise".

> **せんせいは よく がくせいを ほめますか。** — *sensei wa yoku gakusei o homemasu ka* — "Does the teacher often praise the students?"

Polite **ほめます**, *homemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほめる** (*homeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
