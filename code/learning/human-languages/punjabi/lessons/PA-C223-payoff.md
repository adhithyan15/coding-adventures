# My plan: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223, PA-LEX-SHAID-223] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *next week*, *tomorrow* and *perhaps*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223, PA-LEX-SHAID-223, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-NEG-QUESTION-222] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *next week we will go home*] Say **ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਘਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *perhaps Aman won't come*] Say **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਮਨ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਵੇਗਾ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਾਂਗੇ।**] Give its meaning: tomorrow morning we will work.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223, PA-LEX-SHAID-223] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Tell your own plan in four lines: tomorrow, then next week, with one
**ਸ਼ਾਇਦ**.
