## You'll want to know: दूखणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C253-SARDI253-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दूखणो** — *dūkhṇo* — "to ache; to hurt".

> म्हारो माथो दूखै है।
>
> *mhāro mātho dūkhai hai.*
>
> My head aches.

Past (it …): वो दांत दूख्यो, *vo dānt dūkhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूखणो** (*dūkhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *duḥkhati*, "pains". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *dūkhnā* (CDIAL 6376).
