German, chapter 224: In love, Different, Careful, Windy, Funny.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
verliebt — in love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for unhappy, then the German for married.

You'll want to know: verliebt.
verliebt — "in love".

The word, taken apart.
verliebt is the participle of ver- + lieben, "to love": fallen in love. lieben is built on Proto-Germanic leubaz, "dear". (EtymDB 2.1).
