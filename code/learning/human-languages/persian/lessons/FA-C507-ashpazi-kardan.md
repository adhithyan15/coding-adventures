## You'll want to know: آشپزی کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C507-HOBBIES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آشپزی کردن** (*âshpazi kardan*) — "to cook, to do the cooking".

> پدرم آخر هفته ها آشپزی میکند.
>
> *pedaram âkhar-e haftehâ âshpazi mikonad.*
>
> My father cooks at weekends.

*âshpaz* 'cook' + *-i* + *kardan*. *pokhtan* is to cook a particular dish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âshpazi* is built on native Persian parts: *âsh*, 'soup', *paz*, the present stem of *pokhtan*, 'to cook', and *-i*: soup-cooking. *kardan* is inherited too.
