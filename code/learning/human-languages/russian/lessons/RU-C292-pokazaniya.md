## You'll want to know: показания
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C292-DOPROS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**показания** (*pokazániya*) — "a statement, testimony".

> Свидетель дал показания.
>
> *Svidétel dal pokazániya.*
>
> The witness gave a statement.

Usually plural in this sense. *Давать показания* — to testify. The same plural also means the readings of a meter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*показания* is built on *показать* "to show", from *казать*, inherited from Proto-Slavic *kazati* (EtymDB): what a witness shows.
