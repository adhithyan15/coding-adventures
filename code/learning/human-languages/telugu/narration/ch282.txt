Telugu, chapter 282: To graze, To plant, To decorate, To share out, To visit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
మేయు (mēyu) — to graze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to get lost, then the Telugu for to love.

You'll want to know: మేయు.
మేయు — mēyu — "to graze".

The word, taken apart.
మేయు (mēyu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil mēy and Kannada mēyu (DEDR 5093). DEDR lists Telugu mēyu, "to graze, feed".

పంచు (pañcu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3936 lists pañcu, "to distribute, divide", with Kannada pañcu, "to share". DEDR 3887 pañcu, "to send", is a different word.
