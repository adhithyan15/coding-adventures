## You'll want to know: aegrōtō, aegrōtāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C521-AEGR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aegrōtō, aegrōtāre** — "to be ill".

> Avus iam trēs diēs aegrōtat.
>
> *Grandfather has been ill for three days now.*

*aegrōtō, aegrōtāre, aegrōtāvī*, from *aegrōtus*. With *iam*, the present means has been.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**aegrōtō** is built on *aegrōtus* "sick" (Lewis & Short).
