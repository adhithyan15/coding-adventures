## You'll want to know: घेरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C321-HAMLA321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घेरना** — *ghernā* — "to surround; to lay siege to".

> दुश्मन की सेना ने क़िले को घेर लिया।
>
> *duśman kī senā ne qile ko gher liyā.*
>
> The enemy army laid siege to the fort.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने गाँव को घेरा, *usne gā̃v ko gherā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घेरना** (*ghernā*) is inherited from *\*ghir*, "go round", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4474): to go all the way round something and close it in.
