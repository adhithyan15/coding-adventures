## You'll want to know: stundenlang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C269-JAHRZEHNT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**stundenlang** — "for hours".

> Wir haben stundenlang auf den Bus gewartet.
>
> *We waited for the bus for hours.*

An adverb: it answers *wie lange?*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stundenlang* joins *Stunden* "hours" and *lang* "long". *Stunde* is inherited and has the old English cousin *stound* "a while" (EtymDB).
