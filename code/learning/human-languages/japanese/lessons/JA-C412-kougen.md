## You'll want to know: こうげん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C412-TOUGE412-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こうげん** — *kōgen* — "a plateau; highlands".

> **なつは こうげんが すずしいです。** — *natsu wa kōgen ga suzushii desu* — "The highlands are cool in summer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうげん** (*kōgen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "high" (Middle Chinese *kaw*) and *gen*, "plain" (*ngjwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
