## You'll want to know: たいじゅう

**たいじゅう** — *taijū* — "one's weight".

> **たいじゅうが すこし ふえました。** — *taijū ga sukoshi fuemashita* — "I have put on a little weight."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいじゅう** (*taijū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "body" (Middle Chinese *thejX*) and *jū*, "heavy" (*drjowngX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
