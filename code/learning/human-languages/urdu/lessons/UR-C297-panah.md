## You'll want to know: پناہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C297-RAHAT297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پناہ** — *panāh* — "refuge; shelter".

> لوگوں نے اسکول میں پناہ لی۔
>
> *logoṅ ne iskūl meṅ panāh lī.*
>
> People took shelter in the school.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پناہ** (*panāh*) is usually traced to Persian *panāh*, "shelter, protection". **پناہ لی** is took refuge.
