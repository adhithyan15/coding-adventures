## You'll want to know: fregar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C485-LIMPIEZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**fregar** — "to wash up; to mop".

> Yo friego los platos y tú los secas.
>
> *I wash the dishes and you dry them.*

The e breaks into ie under stress: *friego*, *friegas*.
