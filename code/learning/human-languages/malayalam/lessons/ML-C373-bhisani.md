# ഭീഷണി (bhīṣaṇi) — a threat

## You'll want to know: ഭീഷണി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C373-AKRAMANAM373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭീഷണി** — *bhīṣaṇi* — "a threat".

> അവന് ഫോണിൽ ഒരു ഭീഷണി വന്നു.
>
> *avanŭ phōṇil oru bhīṣaṇi vannu.*
>
> He received a threat over the phone.

A threat to something takes -ക്ക്: ആരോഗ്യത്തിന് ഭീഷണി, a threat to health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭീഷണി** (*bhīṣaṇi*) comes from Sanskrit *bhīṣaṇa*, "terrifying, frightening" (Monier-Williams), a causative form of *bhī*, "to fear".
