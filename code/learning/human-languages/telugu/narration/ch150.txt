Telugu, chapter 150: A Word for Literature — Then the Letter Inside It.
2 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 2.
వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ) — literature, the whole body of expression in words.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
You already know పుస్తకం, a book, and you have held a short passage without translating every word. This lesson names the larger body that books and spoken works belong to.

You'll want to know: వాఙ్మయం.
వాఙ్మయం — vāṅmayaṁ — "literature", or more broadly, expression made of words.
Say it in three gentle pieces: vāṅ, ma, yaṁ. The first piece ends with the velar nasal heard in English sing. The spelling keeps that sound as ఙ్ before మ: వా-ఙ్-మ-యం.
The word came through Sanskrit: vāc is speech, and maya means made of or filled with. A single పుస్తకం is one book; వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ) is the world of words that books, speeches, and other verbal works make together.

What you've built.
One book, then the wider field it can belong to. The unusual spelling has had a meaning before it becomes a handwriting exercise.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vāṅ, ma, yaṁ, slowly]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vāṅmayaṁ, as one word]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: పుస్తకం, వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ) — one book, then literature]
[once you have stopped driving — find: the small ఙ్ immediately before మ]
[question — say your answer, then pause 14 seconds]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 2 of 2.
ఙ (ṅa) — the quiet letter inside వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ).

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ఙ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach them.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ) once. What does it mean? ("Literature; the wider body of expression in words.")
[pause 1 second]
Now slow the first two pieces: vāṅ, ma. The letter between them is today's whole task.

Script you'll notice: ఙ.
ఙ — ṅa.
It is the velar nasal: the sound at the end of English sing. In వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ), the vowel is suppressed, so ఙ్ leans into the following మ. The base letter you will write is ఙ (ṅa).
This is an uncommon letter in modern Telugu words. That is why the book waited until it had given you a real word that needs it.

Writing: ఙ — five small movements.
Follow the filmstrip slowly. Movements 1–3 stay joined: turn around the compact upper-left lobe, continue around the broad lower bowl, then curl up through the right lobe. Lift for the inner horizontal bar. Lift once more for the short downward headstroke.
The numbered route is one attested school-style order; Telugu handwriting can vary. Keep the three pen-down runs clear rather than racing the shape.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: ఙ (ṅa) once, following the five numbered movements]
[once you have stopped driving — copy: ఙ (ṅa) twice without tracing]
[once you have stopped driving — find: ఙ్ inside వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ)]
[your turn — say: vāṅmayaṁ as you finish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which base letter is hidden in the cluster ఙ్మ?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — ఙ? What sound does it carry? (ṅa.) Which word gave it to you? (వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ).)
