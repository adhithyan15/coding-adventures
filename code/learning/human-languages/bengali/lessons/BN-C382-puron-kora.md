# পূরণ করা (pūrôn kôrā) — to fulfil; to fill in (a form)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C382-NISHTHA382-01, BN-LEX-C382-NISHTHA382-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for dedication, then the Bengali for perseverance.

## You'll want to know: পূরণ করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C382-NISHTHA382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পূরণ করা** — *pūrôn kôrā* — "to fulfil; to fill in (a form)".

> এই ফর্মটা পূরণ করলে তুমি ছাড় পাবে।
>
> *ei phôrmṭā pūrôn kôrle tumi chhāṛ pābe.*
>
> If you fill in this form, you'll get a discount.

Past (he/she …): তিনি তাঁর স্বপ্ন পূরণ করলেন, *tini tā̃r svôpnô pūrôn kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পূরণ করা** (*pūrôn kôrā*) is Sanskrit *pūraṇa*, filling or completing (Monier-Williams), with করা: to fill a form, a need or a dream.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
