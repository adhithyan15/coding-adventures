## You'll want to know: அலறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C458-AZHUKAI458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அலறு** — *alaṟu* — "to scream; to shriek".

> பாம்பைப் பார்த்ததும் அவள் அலறினாள்.
>
> *pāmpaip pārttatum avaḷ alaṟiṉāḷ.*
>
> She screamed when she saw the snake.

Past (I …): அலறினேன், *alaṟiṉēṉ*.

The verbal participle: அலறி ஓடினான், he ran off screaming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அலறு** (*alaṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 245), in an entry of words for noisy talk and loud cries.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
