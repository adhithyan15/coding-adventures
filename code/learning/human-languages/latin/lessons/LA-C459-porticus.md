## You'll want to know: porticus, porticūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C459-PORTICUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**porticus, porticūs** — "a colonnade, a covered walk, a portico".

> Aestāte cīvēs in porticū ambulant, quod ibi umbra est.
>
> *In summer the citizens walk in the colonnade, because there is shade there.*

Fourth declension, feminine: *porticus, porticūs f.* English *portico* and *porch* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*porticus* is built on *porta* 'gate, passage' (EtymDB): a covered passage. English *portico* comes from it through Italian, and *porch* through French.
