## You'll want to know: पाइप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C516-RISNA516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पाइप** — *pāip* — "a pipe".

> रसोई का पाइप टूट गया।
>
> *Rasoī kā pāip ṭūṭ gayā.*
>
> The kitchen pipe has broken.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाइप** (*pāip*) is a loan from English *pipe*.
