## You'll want to know: ежегодный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C277-OCHEREDNOY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ежегодный** (*yezhegódnyy*) — "annual, yearly".

> Ежегодный концерт будет в мае.
>
> *Yezhegódnyy kontsért búdet v máye.*
>
> The annual concert will be in May.

Also *ежедневный* — daily, *еженедельный* — weekly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ежегодный* is built on *еже-* "each" and *год* "year", which is inherited through Old East Slavic *godŭ* from Proto-Slavic *godŭ* "the right time" (EtymDB).
