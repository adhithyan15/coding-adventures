## You'll want to know: تذمر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C449-SHAKWA449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تذمر** — *tadhammara* — "to grumble".

> تذمر أخي من الطعام في الفندق.
>
> *tadhammara akhī min aṭ-ṭaʿām fī al-funduq.*
>
> My brother grumbled about the food in the hotel.

Present: يتذمر, *yatadhammar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تذمر** (*tadhammara*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *dh-m-r*, "to be angry, to growl". It is Form V.
