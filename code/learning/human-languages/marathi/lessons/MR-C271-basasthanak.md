**बसस्थानक** — *basasthānak* — "a bus station".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बसस्थानक** (*basasthānak*) is a modern compound: English *bus*, and Sanskrit *sthānaka*, "position, place" (CDIAL 13753).
