Punjabi, chapter 340: All Right, Agreed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਵਾਅਦਾ (vāadā) — a promise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for by no means, then the Punjabi for to dig one's heels in.

You'll want to know: ਵਾਅਦਾ.
ਵਾਅਦਾ — vāadā — "a promise".
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਵਾਅਦਾ ਕਰਦੇ ਹੋ? kī tusī̃ vāadā karde ho? Do you promise?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਾਅਦਾ (vāadā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic waʿda, "promise".

What you've built.
One more word for asking and answering.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vāadā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vāadā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kī tusī̃ vāadā karde ho? — Do you promise?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for by no means, then the Punjabi for to dig one's heels in, then say vāadā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਵਾਅਦਾ (vāadā) mean? ("A promise".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: ਮਨਾਉਣਾ.
ਮਨਾਉਣਾ — manāuṇā — "to persuade; to talk round".
ਕੀ ਅਮਨ ਪਿਤਾ ਨੂੰ ਮਨਾਵੇਗਾ? kī Aman pitā nū̃ manāvegā? Will Aman talk his father round?
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭੈਣ ਨੂੰ ਮਨਾਇਆ, maiṁ bhaiṇ nū̃ manāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਨਾਉਣਾ (manāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit manyate, "thinks, thinks fit, agrees"; CDIAL lists Punjabi manāuṇā, "to persuade, soothe", under it (CDIAL 9857).

ਕਬੂਲਣਾ — kabūlṇā — "to accept; to admit".
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਬੇਨਤੀ ਕਬੂਲਦੇ ਹੋ? kī tusī̃ merī bentī kabūlde ho? Do you accept my request?

The word, taken apart.
ਕਬੂਲਣਾ (kabūlṇā) is a verb made in Punjabi from a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic qabūl, "acceptance".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸਮਝੌਤਾ (samjhautā) — a compromise; an agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for absolutely, then the Punjabi for to admit.

You'll want to know: ਸਮਝੌਤਾ.
ਸਮਝੌਤਾ — samjhautā — "a compromise; an agreement".
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਸਮਝੌਤਾ ਕਰੋਗੇ? kī tusī̃ samjhautā karoge? Will you agree to a compromise?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਮਝੌਤਾ (samjhautā) is built on samajhṇā, "to understand", which goes back to Sanskrit saṁbudhyate, "wakes up, understands"; CDIAL lists Punjabi samajhṇā under it (CDIAL 12959), but not samjhautā itself.
