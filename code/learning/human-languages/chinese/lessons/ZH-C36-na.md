## You'll want to know: nǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C36-WHERE36-05]; assesses=[] -->

*nǎ* — "which".

> *nǐ shì nǎ guó rén* — Which country are you from?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
