## You'll want to know: निरोगी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C490-PATHYA490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निरोगी** — *nirogī* — "healthy".

> रोज चालल्याने आपण निरोगी राहतो.
>
> *roj cāllyāne āpaṇ nirogī rāhto.*
>
> Walking every day keeps us healthy.

नि-, without, + रोग, disease, + -ई. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निरोगी** (*nirogī*) is a Marathi form built on Sanskrit *nīroga*, "free from sickness, healthy" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending *-ī*.
