## You'll want to know: xiǎoshíhou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C78-AGE78-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎoshíhou* — "in childhood; when one was little".

> *wǒ xiǎoshíhou hěn xǐhuan xiào* — As a child I laughed a lot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎoshíhou* is built from 小 'small' and shíhou 'time', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX*, *dzyi* and *huwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the time when you were small.
