# स्थानकं कुत्र अस्ति? (sthānakaṃ kutra asti?) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-06, SA-LEX-C29-COURTESY-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "where?" and "please". (*kutra*, *kṛpayā*.) You are in a town you do not know, and you need the station.

## You'll want to know: where, how, how far
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-06, SA-LEX-KATHAM, SA-LEX-C185-WHY185-01, SA-LEX-C89-DESCRIBE-02, SA-LEX-C89-DESCRIBE-03, SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-03] -->

> **स्थानकं कुत्र अस्ति?**
>
> *sthānakaṃ kutra asti?* — "Where is the station?"

> **कियत् दूरम् अस्ति?**
>
> *kiyat dūram asti?* — "How far is it?"

**कियत्** (*kiyat*), "how much", with **दूरम्** (*dūram*), "far", asks the distance, and **कथं गच्छामि?** (*kathaṃ gacchāmi?*) asks "how do I get there?" The answer:

> **बहु दूरं न अस्ति।**
>
> *bahu dūraṃ na asti.* — "It is not very far."

## You'll want to know: left, right, straight on
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C93-TIME93-03, SA-LEX-C93-TIME93-04, SA-LEX-C132-QUAL132-03, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-LEX-BHAVAN] -->

**वामतः** (*vāmataḥ*), "on the left", is **वामः**, "left", with *-taḥ*; **दक्षिणतः** (*dakṣiṇataḥ*), "on the right", is **दक्षिणः** with it. **ऋजु** (*ṛju*), "straight", is **ऋजुः** said as an adverb. What to do takes **-तु**, the polite ending of **ददातु** (*dadātu*): **गच्छतु** (*gacchatu*), "please go".

> **ऋजु गच्छतु।**
>
> *ṛju gacchatu.* — "Go straight on."

> **ततः वामतः गच्छतु।**
>
> *tataḥ vāmataḥ gacchatu.* — "Then turn left."

> **स्थानकं दक्षिणतः अस्ति।**
>
> *sthānakaṃ dakṣiṇataḥ asti.* — "The station is on the right."

**-तु** speaks to **भवान्**, or to **भवती** (*bhavatī*), a woman:

> **भवती अत्र तिष्ठतु।**
>
> *bhavatī atra tiṣṭhatu.* — "Please wait here."
