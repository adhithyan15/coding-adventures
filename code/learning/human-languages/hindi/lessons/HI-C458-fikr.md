## You'll want to know: फ़िक्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C458-TANAV458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़िक्र** — *fikr* — "concern; a worry".

> माँ को हमेशा बच्चों की फ़िक्र रहती है।
>
> *māṁ ko hameśā baccoṁ kī fikr rahtī hai.*
>
> Mother always worries about the children.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़िक्र** (*fikr*) is usually traced to Arabic *fikr*, "thought, reflection", which reached Hindi through Persian; in Hindi the thought became a worry.
