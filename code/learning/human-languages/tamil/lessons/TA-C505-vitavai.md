# விதவை (vitavai) — a widow

## You'll want to know: விதவை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C505-MARANAM505-04]; assesses=[] -->

**விதவை** — *vitavai* — "a widow".

> கணவர் இறந்த பிறகு அவள் விதவை ஆனாள்.
>
> *kaṇavar iṟanta piṟaku avaḷ vitavai āṉāḷ.*
>
> After her husband died she became a widow.

Formal; கைம்பெண் is the native word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**விதவை** (*vitavai*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vidhavā*, "widow" (CDIAL 11752); the final -ā becomes Tamil -ஐ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
