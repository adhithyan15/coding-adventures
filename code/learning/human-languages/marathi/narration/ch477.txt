Marathi, chapter 477: Hands and Legs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तळहात (taḷhāt) — the palm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a beard, then the Marathi for a moustache.

You'll want to know: तळहात.
तळहात — taḷhāt — "the palm".
आजीने माझ्या तळहातावर साखर ठेवली. ājīne mājhyā taḷhātāvar sākhar ṭhevlī. Grandmother put some sugar on my palm.
Masculine.
तळ, the base or flat, + हात. The sole of the foot is तळपाय.

The word, taken apart.
तळहात (taḷhāt) is a Marathi compound of तळ, "base, flat", and हात, "hand", a tadbhava of Sanskrit hasta (CDIAL 14024).

Lesson 2 of 5.
मूठ (mūṭh) — a fist; a handful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a moustache, then the Marathi for the palm.

You'll want to know: मूठ.
मूठ — mūṭh — "a fist; a handful".
त्याने मुठीत एक नाणं लपवलं. tyāne muṭhīt ek nāṇaṁ lapavlaṁ. He hid a coin in his fist.
Feminine.
Oblique मुठी-: मुठीत, in the fist. मूठभर, a handful.

The word, taken apart.
मूठ (mūṭh) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit muṣṭi, "fist"; CDIAL lists Marathi mūṭh (CDIAL 10221).
