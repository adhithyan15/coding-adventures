Kannada, chapter 422: Rules and Methods.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸು (parāmarśisu) — to refer to; to consult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for export, then the Kannada for inflation.

You'll want to know: ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸು.
ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸು — parāmarśisu — "to refer to; to consult".
ಉತ್ತರ ಬರೆಯುವ ಮೊದಲು ನಾನು ನಿಘಂಟನ್ನು ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸಿದೆ. uttara bareyuva modalu nānu nighaṇṭannu parāmarśiside. Before writing the answer I consulted the dictionary.
Past (I …): ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸಿದೆ, parāmarśiside.
Of books and records. ಪರಾಮರ್ಶನ ಗ್ರಂಥ, a reference book.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸು (parāmarśisu) is built on Sanskrit parāmarśa, "seizing; consideration" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
