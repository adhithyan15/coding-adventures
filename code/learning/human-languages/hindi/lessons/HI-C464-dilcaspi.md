## You'll want to know: दिलचस्पी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C464-UBNA464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलचस्पी** — *dilcaspī* — "interest; a keen interest".

> मीरा को इतिहास में बहुत दिलचस्पी है।
>
> *Mīrā ko itihās meṁ bahut dilcaspī hai.*
>
> Mira is very interested in history.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलचस्पी** (*dilcaspī*) is usually traced to Persian *dilchaspī*, "interest", built on *dil*, "heart", and a word for "sticking": what the heart sticks to.
