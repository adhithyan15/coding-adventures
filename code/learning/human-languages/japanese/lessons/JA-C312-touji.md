## You'll want to know: とうじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C312-REKISHI312-05]; assesses=[] -->

**とうじ** — *tōji* — "at that time; in those days".

> **とうじ、ちちは まだ がくせいでした。** — *tōji, chichi wa mada gakusei deshita* — "In those days my father was still a student."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とうじ** (*tōji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tō*, "right" (Middle Chinese *tang*) and *ji*, "time" (*dzyi*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
