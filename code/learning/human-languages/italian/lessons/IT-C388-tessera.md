## You'll want to know: la tessera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C388-VALIDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la tessera** — "the card, the membership card, the pass".

> Con la tessera del museo entri gratis.
>
> *With the museum card you get in free.*

Stress on the first syllable: *tèssera*. For buses, a monthly pass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tessera* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tessera* "a square tablet, a token, a ticket" (Lewis & Short), which goes back to Greek *tésseres* "four", for its four corners.
