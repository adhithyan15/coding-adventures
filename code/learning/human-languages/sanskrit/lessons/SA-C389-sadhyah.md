## You'll want to know: साध्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C389-SHAKYA389-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साध्यः** — *sādhyaḥ* — "achievable".

> यदि योजना उत्तमा, तर्हि लक्ष्यं साध्यम्।
>
> *yadi yojanā uttamā, tarhi lakṣyaṃ sādhyam.*
>
> If the plan is good, the goal is achievable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साध्यः** (*sādhyaḥ*) comes from the root √sidh, "to succeed", in the stem *sādh-*; MW gives *sādhya* "to be accomplished" and "practicable, feasible, attainable" (MW).
