# यदि … तर्हि … (yadi … tarhi …) — if, then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C283-DURATION-01, SA-LEX-C154-WHY154-01, SA-LEX-C49-REPLY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "if" and "then, in that case". (*yadi*, *tarhi*.) You have both words; this chapter puts them to work.

## You'll want to know: यदि … तर्हि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-IF-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C154-WHY154-01, SA-LEX-C49-REPLY-01, SA-LEX-C76-WEATHER-01, SA-LEX-ASTI-BHAVATI-BE, SA-LEX-KHADATI-EAT] -->

**यदि** (*yadi*), "if", opens the condition; **तर्हि** (*tarhi*), "then", opens what follows from it:

> **यदि वर्षा भवति,**
>
> *yadi varṣā bhavati,*
>
> **तर्हि अहं गृहे तिष्ठामि।**
>
> *tarhi ahaṃ gṛhe tiṣṭhāmi.* — "If it rains, I stay at home."

> **यदि सः आगच्छति,**
>
> *yadi saḥ āgacchati,*
>
> **तर्हि वयं खादामः।**
>
> *tarhi vayaṃ khādāmaḥ.* — "If he comes, we eat."

## You'll want to know: if not, and the condition second
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-IF-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-NA-NO, SA-LEX-GACCHATI-GO] -->

"If not" puts **न** before the verb of the **यदि** half:

> **यदि सः न आगच्छति,**
>
> *yadi saḥ na āgacchati,*
>
> **तर्हि अहं गच्छामि।**
>
> *tarhi ahaṃ gacchāmi.* — "If he does not come, I go."

The condition can also come second, with no **तर्हि**:

> **अहं गच्छामि, यदि सः न आगच्छति।**
>
> *ahaṃ gacchāmi, yadi saḥ na āgacchati.* — "I go if he does not come."
