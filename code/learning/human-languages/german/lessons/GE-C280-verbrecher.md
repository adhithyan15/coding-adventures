## You'll want to know: der Verbrecher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C280-VERBRECHEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Verbrecher** — "the criminal".

> Die Polizei suchte den Verbrecher drei Wochen lang.
>
> *The police searched for the criminal for three weeks.*

Plural: *die Verbrecher*. Feminine *die Verbrecherin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Verbrecher* is built on *verbrechen* "to do wrong", from *brechen* "to break", Proto-Germanic *brekaną* (EtymDB), with the ending *-er* for a person.
