## You'll want to know: la alternativa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C616-IMPREV-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la alternativa** — "the alternative, the other option".

> No hay otra alternativa.
>
> *There is no other alternative.*

Also an adjective: *una ruta alternativa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alternativa* is built on *alternar* and *alterno*, learned borrowings from Latin *alternare* and *alternus* 'by turns', from *alter* 'the other' (EtymDB).
