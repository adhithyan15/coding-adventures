## You'll want to know: la voie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C413-QUAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la voie** — "the track, the platform number; the way".

> Le train de sept heures part de la voie six.
>
> *The seven o'clock train leaves from platform six.*

*La voie* is the track with its number; *le quai* is where you stand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*voie* is inherited through Old French *voie* from Latin *via* 'road, way' (EtymDB).
