## You'll want to know: scutica, scuticae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C431-HABENA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**scutica, scuticae** — "a whip, a lash".

> Raedārius scuticam habet, sed equōs nōn tangit.
>
> *The coachman has a whip, but he does not touch the horses.*

First declension, feminine: *scutica, scuticae f.* A strap whip of leather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scutica* was borrowed from Greek; EtymDB traces it to *skytos* 'leather, hide'. Lewis & Short gloss it as a lash or whip but give no origin.
