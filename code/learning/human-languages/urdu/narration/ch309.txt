Urdu, chapter 309: The Attack.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حملہ (hamlā) — an attack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a royal court, then the Urdu for an heir.

You'll want to know: حملہ.
حملہ — hamlā — "an attack".
فوج نے رات کو حملہ کیا۔ fauj ne rāt ko hamlā kiyā. The army attacked at night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
حملہ (hamlā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥamla, "a charge, an onset". حملہ کیا is attacked.

Lesson 3 of 5.
گھیرنا (ghernā) — to surround; to lay siege to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an attack, then the Urdu for a fort.

You'll want to know: گھیرنا.
گھیرنا — ghernā — "to surround; to lay siege to".
فوج نے قلعے کو گھیرا۔ fauj ne qile ko gherā. The army surrounded the fort.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے شہر کو گھیرا, us ne shahr ko gherā.

The word, taken apart.
گھیرنا (ghernā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ghernā, "to surround", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4474).

Lesson 4 of 5.
توپ (top) — a cannon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fort, then the Urdu for to surround.

You'll want to know: توپ.
توپ — top — "a cannon".
قلعے پر چار توپیں تھیں۔ qile par chār topeṅ thīṅ. There were four cannons on the fort.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
توپ (top) is usually traced to Turkish top, "a ball", and so a cannon, which came into Urdu through Persian. It is feminine: چار توپیں.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بھڑنا (bhiṛnā) — to clash; to come to blows.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to surround, then the Urdu for a cannon.

You'll want to know: بھڑنا.
بھڑنا — bhiṛnā — "to clash; to come to blows".
دونوں فوجیں میدان میں بھڑ گئیں۔ donoṅ faujeṅ maidān meṅ bhiṛ gaīṅ. The two armies clashed on the field.
Past (he …): وہ بھڑ گیا, voh bhiṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
بھڑنا (bhiṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bhiṛnā, "to come close, to fight", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9490). Who you clash with takes سے.
