Sanskrit, chapter 319: Victory and Defeat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विजयः (vijayaḥ) — a victory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sets out, then the Sanskrit for fights.

You'll want to know: विजयः.
विजयः — vijayaḥ — "a victory".
अन्ते सेनायाः विजयः भवति। ante senāyāḥ vijayaḥ bhavati. In the end the army wins the victory.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विजयः (vijayaḥ) is built from vi and the root √ji, "to conquer, win", as in jayati; MW gives vijaya as a contest for victory, victory, conquest (MW).

शत्रोः सेना शीघ्रम् अपसरति। śatroḥ senā śīghram apasarati. The enemy's army quickly retreats.
Past (it …): सेना शीघ्रम् अपसृतवती, senā śīghram apasṛtavatī.

The word, taken apart.
अपसरति (apasarati) is built from apa, "away", and the root √sṛ, "to go, run"; MW gives apa-sṛ as to slip off from, go away, retreat (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: अभिभवति.
अभिभवति — abhibhavati — "overpowers".
वीरः राजा शत्रुम् अभिभवति। vīraḥ rājā śatrum abhibhavati. The brave king overpowers the enemy.
Past (he …): सः शत्रुम् अभिभूतवान्, saḥ śatrum abhibhūtavān.

The word, taken apart.
अभिभवति (abhibhavati) is built from abhi, "over, against", and the root √bhū, "to be, become", as in bhavati; MW gives abhi-bhū as to overcome, overpower, defeat (MW).
