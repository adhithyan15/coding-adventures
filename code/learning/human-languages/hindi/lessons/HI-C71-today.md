## You'll want to know: आज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आज** (*āj*) — "today".

Sanskrit अद्य (*adya*), "today", built from "this" and "day". Latin did exactly
the same thing with *hōc diē*, "on this day", which wore down to *hodiē* and then
to French *aujourd'hui* and Spanish *hoy*. Two families, the same idea, the same
squeezing.

You already own **कल** and **परसों**, which reach forward and back. आज is the
missing middle of that set, and it is the one you will say most.

**आज आप कैसे हैं?** is the greeting you have been making, with one word added
that makes it about now rather than in general.
