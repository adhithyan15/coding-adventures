Urdu, chapter 477: Taking Medicine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قطرہ (qatrā) — a droplet; a drop (of medicine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a traditional physician, then the Urdu for to have (someone) sent for.

You'll want to know: قطرہ.
قطرہ — qatrā — "a droplet; a drop (of medicine)".
دوا کے دو قطرے پانی میں ملائیے۔ davā ke do qatre pānī meṅ milāiye. Mix two drops of the medicine into some water.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
قطرہ (qatrā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qaṭra, "a drop".

Lesson 2 of 5.
گھولنا (gholnā) — to dissolve; to stir in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (someone) sent for, then the Urdu for a droplet.

You'll want to know: گھولنا.
گھولنا — gholnā — "to dissolve; to stir in".
امی نے دودھ میں چینی گھولی۔ ammī ne dūdh meṅ chīnī gholī. Mum stirred sugar into the milk.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پانی میں نمک گھولا, us ne pānī meṅ namak gholā.

The word, taken apart.
گھولنا (gholnā) is inherited from Sanskrit ghōlayati, "mixes, stirs together" (CDIAL 4526).

[pause 2 seconds]

حکیم نے دوا کی تین پڑیاں دیں۔ hakīm ne davā kī tīn puṛiyāṅ dīṅ. The physician gave three packets of powder.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
پڑیا (puṛiyā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin puṛiyā, "anything wrapped in paper or leaves", under Sanskrit puṭa, "a fold, a small receptacle" (CDIAL 8253).

Lesson 5 of 5.
پھانکنا (phāṅknā) — to toss into the mouth (a dry powder).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a capsule, then the Urdu for a paper packet.

You'll want to know: پھانکنا.
پھانکنا — phāṅknā — "to toss into the mouth (a dry powder)".
اس نے پڑیا کی دوا پھانکی۔ us ne puṛiyā kī davā phāṅkī. He tossed the powder from the packet into his mouth.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چنے پھانکے, us ne chane phāṅke.

The word, taken apart.
پھانکنا (phāṅknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phā̃knā under phakk, "toss food into the mouth", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9034).
