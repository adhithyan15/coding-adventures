## You'll want to know: दुहना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C450-JHADNA450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दुहना** — *duhnā* — "to milk".

> दादी रोज़ सुबह गाय दुहती हैं।
>
> *dādī roz subah gāy duhtī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother milks the cow every morning.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने भैंस दुही, *usne bhaĩs duhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुहना** (*duhnā*) goes back to *\*duhati*, "milks", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6476), which lists Hindi *duhnā*, "to milk".
