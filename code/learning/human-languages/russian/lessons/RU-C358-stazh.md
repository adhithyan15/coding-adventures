## You'll want to know: стаж
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C358-VAKANSIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**стаж** (*stazh*) — "length of service, work experience".

> У него большой стаж работы.
>
> *U negó bolshóy stazh rabóty.*
>
> He has many years of work experience.

Masculine. *Стаж работы* — the years someone has worked, counted for a job or a pension.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**стаж** was borrowed from French *stage* "a period of training", from Medieval Latin *stagium*, from Latin *stāre* "to stand" (EtymDB).
