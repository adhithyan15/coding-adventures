## You'll want to know: заражаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C487-RASH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**заражаться** (*zarazhátsya*) — "to get infected".

> Дети быстро заражаются друг от друга.
>
> *Déti býstro zarazháyutsya drug ot drúga.*
>
> Children catch things from each other very quickly.

Imperfective; perfective заразиться (заражусь). *Заражаться от кого* — to catch it from someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*заражаться* is built on *зараза* 'infection'; EtymDB lists *зараза* and *заразить* without a source, and they are usually traced to *разить* 'to strike'.
