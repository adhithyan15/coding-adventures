## You'll want to know: ਠੋਕਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C310-SLIP310-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠੋਕਰ** — *ṭhokar* — "a stumble".

> ਹਨੇਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਪੱਥਰ ਨਾਲ ਠੋਕਰ ਖਾਧੀ।
>
> *hanere vich maiṁ ikk patthar nāl ṭhokar khādhī.*
>
> In the dark I stumbled over a stone.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਠੋਕਰ** (*ṭhokar*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭhokk*, "knock"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭhokar*, "blow", under it (CDIAL 5513). Its use for a stumble is not given there.

## What you've built
