## You'll want to know: ટાંકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C296-AMBULANS296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાંકો** — *ṭā̃ko* — "a stitch".

> દાક્તરે તેના માથા પર ચાર ટાંકા લીધા.
>
> *dāktare tenā māthā par cār ṭā̃kā līdhā.*
>
> The doctor put four stitches in his head.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટાંકો** (*ṭā̃ko*) goes back to Sanskrit *ṭaṅkáyati*, "ties", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun (CDIAL 5432). A doctor **ટાંકા લે છે**, puts in stitches; a tailor takes them too.
