## You'll want to know: હાશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C307-RUNI307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હાશ** — *hāś* — "phew (in relief)".

> બાળક બહાર આવ્યું ત્યારે બધાએ કહ્યું, હાશ!
>
> *bāḷak bahār āvyũ tyāre badhāe kahyũ, hāś!*
>
> When the child came out, everyone breathed again: phew!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાશ** (*hāś*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. It is the sound of breath let out after fear: **હાશ!**, thank goodness.
