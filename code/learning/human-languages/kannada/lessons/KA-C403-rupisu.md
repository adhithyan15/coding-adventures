## You'll want to know: ರೂಪಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C403-SAMAJA403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ರೂಪಿಸು** — *rūpisu* — "to devise; to shape".

> ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರು ಹೊಸ ಪಾಠಗಳನ್ನು ರೂಪಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *śikṣakaru hosa pāṭhagaḷannu rūpisidaru.*
>
> The teachers devised new lessons.

Past (I …): ರೂಪಿಸಿದೆ, *rūpiside*.

What is shaped takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರೂಪಿಸು** (*rūpisu*) is built on Sanskrit *rūpa*, "form, shape" (CDIAL 10803), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*: to give form to.

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
