Marathi, chapter 402: Mountain Roads.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घाट (ghāṭ) — a ghat road; a mountain pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a puncture, then the Marathi for to run out.

You'll want to know: घाट.
घाट — ghāṭ — "a ghat road; a mountain pass".
बस हळूहळू घाट चढत होती. bas haḷūhaḷū ghāṭ caḍhat hotī. The bus was slowly climbing the ghat.
Masculine.
In the western hills a घाट (ghāṭ) is the steep road up the slope; on a river it is a flight of steps to the water.

The word, taken apart.
घाट (ghāṭ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ghāṭ, "mountain pass, landing place", under Sanskrit ghaṭṭa, "landing place, quay" (CDIAL 4414), and notes a possible Dravidian source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
खिंड (khiṇḍ) — a narrow pass; a gap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to run out, then the Marathi for a ghat road.

You'll want to know: खिंड.
खिंड — khiṇḍ — "a narrow pass; a gap".
किल्ल्याच्या मागे एक अरुंद खिंड आहे. killyācyā māge ek arund khiṇḍ āhe. Behind the fort there is a narrow pass.
Feminine.
A gap between two hills, narrower than a घाट (ghāṭ).

The word, taken apart.
खिंड (khiṇḍ) is a Marathi word for a narrow gap between hills; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
उतार (utār) — a downhill slope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a ghat road, then the Marathi for a narrow pass.

You'll want to know: उतार.
उतार — utār — "a downhill slope".
उतारावरून सायकल लवकर खाली येते. utārāvarūn sāykal lavkar khālī yete. On a downhill slope a bicycle comes down quickly.
Masculine.
The opposite is चढ, the way up; उतारावर, on the slope.

The word, taken apart.
उतार (utār) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi utār, "ford, ferry fare", under Sanskrit uttāra, "crossing over, landing" (CDIAL 1791); in Marathi today it is mostly a slope going down.

Lesson 4 of 5.
नागमोडी (nāgmoḍī) — winding; zigzag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a narrow pass, then the Marathi for a downhill slope.

You'll want to know: नागमोडी.
नागमोडी — nāgmoḍī — "winding; zigzag".
घाटातला नागमोडी रस्ता सुंदर दिसतो. ghāṭātlā nāgmoḍī rastā sundar disto. The winding road through the ghat looks beautiful.
It does not change for gender: नागमोडी (nāgmoḍī) रस्ता, नागमोडी (nāgmoḍī) वाट.

The word, taken apart.
नागमोडी (nāgmoḍī) is a Marathi compound: नाग, "a snake", a tatsama from Sanskrit nāga (Monier-Williams), and moḍī, usually linked with मोडणे, "to bend, to break": a road that bends like a snake. How the second part was formed is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

डोंगरावर एक उंच सुळका दिसतो. ḍoṅgarāvar ek uñc suḷkā disto. A tall rock pinnacle stands out on the hill.
Masculine.
A tall, thin peak of bare rock, loved by rock climbers.

The word, taken apart.
सुळका (suḷkā) is a Marathi word for a tall spike of rock; its origin is not settled.
