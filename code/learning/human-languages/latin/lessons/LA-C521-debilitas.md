# dēbilitās, dēbilitātis — weakness, infirmity

## You'll want to know: dēbilitās, dēbilitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C521-AEGR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dēbilitās, dēbilitātis** — "weakness, infirmity".

> Dēbilitās corporis eum domī tenet.
>
> *Weakness of body keeps him at home.*

Feminine, third declension, from *dēbilis*. English *debility*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēbilitās** is built on *dēbilis* "weak" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *debility* comes from it.
