German, chapter 88: Bee, Ant, Elephant, To fetch, To wish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Biene — a bee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a lion, then the German for a monkey.

You'll want to know: die Biene.
die Biene — "a bee".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Biene]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Biene, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say die Biene]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a lion, then the German for a monkey, then say die Biene again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Biene mean? ("A bee".) Say it once more.
