Italian, chapter 387: Chi può partecipare.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il requisito — the requirement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to break, then the Italian for the exception.

You'll want to know: il requisito.
il requisito — "the requirement".
Il primo requisito è avere la patente. The first requirement is to have a driving licence.
Mostly plural: i requisiti.

The word, taken apart.
requisito is a learned borrowing from Latin requīsītum "a thing needed, a demand" (Lewis & Short), from requīrere "to seek again, to need", built on quaerere "to seek".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: idoneo.
idoneo — "suitable, eligible, fit".
Se il medico ti giudica idoneo, puoi fare la gara. If the doctor finds you fit, you can take part in the race.
Formal: in exams idoneo means passed, without a mark.

The word, taken apart.
idoneo is a learned borrowing from Latin idōneus "fit, suitable" (Lewis & Short); the deeper origin of the Latin word is not settled.

You'll want to know: l'ammissione.
l'ammissione — "the admission, the entry".
L'esame di ammissione è a settembre. The entrance exam is in September.
Built on ammettere. It is also an admission of guilt.

The word, taken apart.
ammissione is a learned borrowing from Latin admissiō "a letting in" (Lewis & Short), from admittere "to let in", built on mittere "to send".

Lesson 4 of 5.
escludere — to exclude, to leave out; to rule out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for suitable, then the Italian for the admission.

You'll want to know: escludere.
escludere — "to exclude, to leave out; to rule out".
Se arrivi tardi, ti escludono dalla gara. If you arrive late, they will exclude you from the race.
Past participle escluso. Lo escludo — I rule it out.

The word, taken apart.
escludere is a learned borrowing from Latin exclūdere "to shut out" (Lewis & Short), built from ex "out" and claudere "to shut", the verb behind chiudere.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la condizione — the condition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the admission, then the Italian for to exclude.

You'll want to know: la condizione.
la condizione — "the condition".
Ti aiuto a una condizione: poi tu aiuti me. I will help you on one condition: then you help me.
A condizione che + subjunctive — on condition that. In buone condizioni — in good shape.

The word, taken apart.
condizione is a learned borrowing from Latin condiciō, later spelled conditiō, "agreement, terms" (EtymDB), from condīcere "to agree upon", built on dīcere "to say".
