## You'll want to know: दया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C372-DAYA372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दया** — *dayā* — "mercy, pity".

> उसे ग़रीबों पर दया आती है।
>
> *use ġarīboṁ par dayā ātī hai.*
>
> He feels pity for the poor.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दया** (*dayā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *dayā*, "sympathy, compassion" (Monier-Williams).
