## You'll want to know: طاقم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C424-IQLA424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طاقم** — *ṭāqam* — "a crew; a set".

> كان طاقم الرحلة لطيفا جدا.
>
> *kāna ṭāqam ar-riḥla laṭīfan jiddan.*
>
> The flight crew was very kind.

It also names a matching set: *ṭāqam shāy*, a tea set.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طاقم** (*ṭāqam*) was borrowed from Ottoman Turkish (EtymDB), where *takım* means a set or a team.
