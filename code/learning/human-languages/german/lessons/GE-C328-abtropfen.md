## You'll want to know: abtropfen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C328-SIEB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**abtropfen** — "to drip dry, to drain off".

> Er ließ den Salat gut abtropfen.
>
> *He let the lettuce drip dry.*

Separable, often with *lassen*. Perfekt with *sein*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abtropfen* is built on *tropfen* "to drip", Old High German *tropfōn* (EtymDB), with *ab* "off".
