# تخفيض (takhfīḍ) — a price cut; a reduction

## You'll want to know: تخفيض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C457-TAKHFID457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تخفيض** — *takhfīḍ* — "a price cut; a reduction".

> اشتريت المعطف بتخفيض كبير.
>
> *ishtaraytu al-miʿṭaf bi-takhfīḍ kabīr.*
>
> I bought the coat with a big price cut.

The plural *takhfīḍāt* is the sales.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تخفيض** (*takhfīḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-f-ḍ*, "to be low; to lower" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form II.
