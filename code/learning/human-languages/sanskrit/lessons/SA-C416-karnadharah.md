## You'll want to know: कर्णधारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C416-NAVIKA416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्णधारः** — *karṇadhāraḥ* — "a helmsman".

> कर्णधारः नौकां सम्यक् नयति।
>
> *karṇadhāraḥ naukāṃ samyak nayati.*
>
> The helmsman steers the boat well.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्णधारः** (*karṇadhāraḥ*) is built from *karṇa*, "an ear", which MW also gives as the helm or rudder of a ship, and *dhāra*, "holding"; MW gives the compound as a helmsman, a pilot (MW).
