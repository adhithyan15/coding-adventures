## You'll want to know: બેદરકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C295-VALANK295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બેદરકાર** — *bedarkār* — "careless".

> બેદરકાર ડ્રાઈવરને લીધે આ અકસ્માત થયો.
>
> *bedarkār ḍrāīvarne līdhe ā akasmāt thayo.*
>
> This accident happened because of a careless driver.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બેદરકાર** (*bedarkār*) is usually traced to Persian *bē-*, "without", and *darkār*, "needed, wanted": someone who does not care what is needed. **બેદરકાર ડ્રાઈવર**, a careless driver.
