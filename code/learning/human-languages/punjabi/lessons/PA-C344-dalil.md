## You'll want to know: ਦਲੀਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C344-ARGUE344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਲੀਲ** — *dalīl* — "an argument; a reason given in support".

> ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਦਲੀਲ ਠੀਕ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *tuhāḍī dalīl ṭhīk nahīṁ hai.*
>
> Your argument is not right.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਲੀਲ** (*dalīl*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *dalīl*, "guide".
