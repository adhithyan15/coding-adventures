## You'll want to know: flirten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C492-VERLIEBT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**flirten** — "to flirt".

> Auf der Party flirtete er mit allen.
>
> *At the party he flirted with everybody.*

Regular: *hat geflirtet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flirten* was borrowed from English *to flirt*; EtymDB has no entry for the German verb.
