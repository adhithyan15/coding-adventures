## You'll want to know: эфир
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C281-EFIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**эфир** (*efír*) — "the air (broadcasting)".

> Программа вышла в эфир в семь часов.
>
> *Prográmma výshla v efír v sem chasóv.*
>
> The programme went on air at seven.

*В прямом эфире* — live. In chemistry it also means ether.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*эфир* was borrowed from Greek *aithḗr* "the upper air" (EtymDB), built on *aíthō* "to burn, to shine".
