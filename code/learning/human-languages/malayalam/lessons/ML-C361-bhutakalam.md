# ഭൂതകാലം (bhūtakālaṁ) — the past

## You'll want to know: ഭൂതകാലം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C361-YUGAM361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭൂതകാലം** — *bhūtakālaṁ* — "the past".

> അവൻ ഭൂതകാലത്തെക്കുറിച്ച് ഒന്നും പറഞ്ഞില്ല.
>
> *avan bhūtakālattekkuṟiccŭ onnuṁ paṟaññilla.*
>
> He said nothing about the past.

In grammar, the past tense. ഭാവി, the future, is its opposite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭൂതകാലം** (*bhūtakālaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bhūtakāla*, "past time; the past tense" (Monier-Williams): *bhūta*, "what has been", and *kāla*, "time" (CDIAL 3084), the word that gave കാലം.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
