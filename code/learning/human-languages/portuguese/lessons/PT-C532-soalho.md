## You'll want to know: o soalho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C532-ACOLHEDORA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o soalho** — "wooden floor, floorboards".

> O soalho da casa antiga range.
>
> *The wooden floor in the old house creaks.*

European Portuguese; in Brazil, *o assoalho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soalho* is usually traced to a form built on Latin *solum* "ground, floor" (Lewis & Short).
