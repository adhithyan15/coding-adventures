# atīt kī tālikā — the past, on one page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-02, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name all three past endings and say who each one belongs to, before
you look at the table.

## Grammar Lens: the three endings
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-02, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-03] -->

| subject | earlier |
|---|---|
| मैं, वह — a man | था |
| मैं, वह — a woman | थी |
| हम, वे, आप, तुम | थे |

Three words, and their endings are **-आ**, **-ई** and **-ए** — the same three
you have been bending adjectives with since the colours.

## Grammar Lens: the swap, in every sentence you own
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-05, HI-LEX-C87-POST-01] -->

| now | earlier |
|---|---|
| मैं घर पर हूँ। | मैं घर पर था। |
| वह स्टेशन पर है। | वह स्टेशन पर था। |
| मैं हिंदी बोलता हूँ। | मैं हिंदी बोलता था। |

One word out, one word in. The place phrase, the postposition **पर** and the
word order all sit still while the tense moves around them.

## Grammar Lens: with नहीं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-PAST-04, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-03] -->

| sentence | meaning |
|---|---|
| हम घर पर नहीं थे। | We were not at home. |
| वे स्टेशन पर नहीं थे। | They were not at the station. |
