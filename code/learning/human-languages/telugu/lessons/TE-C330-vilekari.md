# విలేకరి (vilēkari) — a reporter; a correspondent

## You'll want to know: విలేకరి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C330-PATRIKA330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**విలేకరి** — *vilēkari* — "a reporter; a correspondent".

> విలేకరి సాక్షితో మాట్లాడాడు.
>
> *vilēkari sākṣitō māṭlāḍāḍu.*
>
> The reporter spoke to the witness.

Plural: విలేకరులు, *vilēkarulu*.

A paper's reporter in a town is its విలేకరి there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**విలేకరి** (*vilēkari*) is the Telugu word for a newspaper reporter; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
