## You'll want to know: أذاع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C297-IDHAA297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أذاع** — *adhāʿa* — "to put on air; to make known".

> أذاعت الإذاعة أغنية قديمة.
>
> *adhāʿat al-idhāʿa ughniya qadīma.*
>
> The station broadcast an old song.

Present: يذيع, *yudhīʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أذاع** (*adhāʿa*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *dh-y-ʿ*, said of news that spreads. It is Form IV, "to make known", and the verb of إذاعة.
