## You'll want to know: भाणजो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C261-MASI261-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाणजो** — *bhāṇjo* — "a sister's son; a nephew".

> म्हारो भाणजो सहर सूं आयो।
>
> *mhāro bhāṇjo sahar sū̃ āyo.*
>
> My sister's son came from the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाणजो** (*bhāṇjo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhāgineya*, "a sister's son". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati *bhāṇej* and *bhāṇiyo* (CDIAL 9433).
