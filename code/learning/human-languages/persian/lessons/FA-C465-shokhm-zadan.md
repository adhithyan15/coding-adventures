## You'll want to know: شخم زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C465-MAZRAE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شخم زدن** (*shokhm zadan*) — "to plough (US: to plow)".

> کشاورز زمین را با تراکتور شخم زد.
>
> *Keshâvarz zamin râ bâ terâktor shokhm zad.*
>
> The farmer ploughed the land with a tractor.

*shokhm* 'ploughing' + *zadan*. Present stem *zan-*: *shokhm mizanad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shokhm zadan* is built from *shokhm* 'ploughing', whose origin is not settled, and the native verb *zadan* 'to strike'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
