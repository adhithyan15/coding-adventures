## You'll want to know: ਵੱਛਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C308-FEED308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੱਛਾ** — *vachchhā* — "a calf".

> ਗਾਂ ਦਾ ਵੱਛਾ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੌੜਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *gāṁ dā vachchhā khet vich dauṛdā hai.*
>
> The cow's calf runs in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੱਛਾ** (*vachchhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vatsa*, "calf, child"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vacchā*, "suckling buffalo calf", under it (CDIAL 11239).
