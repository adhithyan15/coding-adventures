## You'll want to know: கலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C536-OVIYAM536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கலை** — *kalai* — "an art; the arts".

> நடனம் ஒரு பழமையான கலை.
>
> *naṭaṉam oru paḻamaiyāṉa kalai.*
>
> Dance is an ancient art.

An artist is a கலைஞர் (*kalaiñar*): கலை + -ஞர், "one who knows". The plural is கலைகள்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கலை** (*kalai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1297 gives கல் (*kal*), "to learn, to practise an art", and beside it கலை (*kalai*), "the arts and sciences, learning".
