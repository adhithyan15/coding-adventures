## You'll want to know: jíxìngzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C469-GENTLE469-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jíxìngzi* — "an impatient sort; a hothead".

> *wǒ bàba shì ge jíxìngzi* — My dad is the impatient sort.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jíxìngzi* is built from *jí* 'hasty', 性 'nature' and 子, a noun ending, all inherited from Middle Chinese, *kip*, *sjengH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'urgent' and 'quick'.
