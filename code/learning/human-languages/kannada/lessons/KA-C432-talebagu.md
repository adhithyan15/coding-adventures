# ತಲೆಬಾಗು (talebāgu) — to bow down; to give in

## You'll want to know: ತಲೆಬಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C432-HATTIKKU432-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲೆಬಾಗು** — *talebāgu* — "to bow down; to give in".

> ಒತ್ತಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಲೆಬಾಗಿ ಕಂಪನಿ ಸಂಬಳ ಹೆಚ್ಚಿಸಿತು.
>
> *ottaḍakke talebāgi kampani sambaḷa heccisitu.*
>
> Giving in to pressure, the company raised pay.

Past (I …): ತಲೆಬಾಗಿದೆ, *talebāgide*.

ತಲೆ (head) + ಬಾಗು (to bend). What one gives in to takes -ಗೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲೆಬಾಗು** (*talebāgu*) is native Dravidian, built from *tale*, "head" (DEDR 3103), and *bāgu*, "to bend, to bow" (DEDR 5335).
