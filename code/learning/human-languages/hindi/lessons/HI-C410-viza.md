## You'll want to know: वीज़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C410-PASPORT410-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वीज़ा** — *vīzā* — "a visa".

> मुझे अभी वीज़ा नहीं मिला।
>
> *mujhe abhī vīzā nahīṃ milā.*
>
> I have not got my visa yet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वीज़ा** (*vīzā*) is borrowed from English *visa*; the English z keeps its ज़.
