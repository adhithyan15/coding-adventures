Lesson 1 of 5.
മൂടി (mūṭi) — a lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for football, then the Malayalam for cricket.

You'll want to know: മൂടി.
മൂടി — mūṭi — "a lid".

The word, taken apart.
മൂടി (mūṭi) is native Dravidian, the noun of the verb "to cover"; DEDR 5034 lists the root with Tamil mūṭu.

ഭരണി — bharaṇi — "a jar".

The word, taken apart.
ഭരണി (bharaṇi) has the shape of Sanskrit bharaṇī, "bearing", also the name of a lunar asterism (CDIAL 9396); whether the jar's name comes from it is uncertain.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a jar, then the Malayalam for a water pot.

You'll want to know: ചാക്ക്.
ചാക്ക് — cākkŭ — "a sack".

The word, taken apart.
ചാക്ക് (cākkŭ) from English sack, a word that goes back through Latin and Greek to a Semitic word for sackcloth.
