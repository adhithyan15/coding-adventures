# मंज़ूरी (maṁzūrī) — approval, sanction

## You'll want to know: मंज़ूरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C395-MANZURI395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मंज़ूरी** — *maṁzūrī* — "approval, sanction".

> अगर सरकार मंज़ूरी देगी तो पुल बनेगा।
>
> *agar sarkār maṁzūrī degī to pul banegā.*
>
> If the government gives approval, the bridge will be built.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंज़ूरी** (*maṁzūrī*) is usually traced to Arabic *manẓūr*, "seen, approved", which reached Hindi through Persian. The Hindi noun adds the ending *-ī*.
