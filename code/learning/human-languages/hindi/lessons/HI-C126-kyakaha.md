## You'll want to know: क्या कहा?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C126-WHAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्या कहा?** (*kyā kahā?*) — "what did you say?".

**कहा** is the past of **कहना**, "to say". Short, and fine among friends; with a stranger, add
**जी**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A quick request to hear it again.
