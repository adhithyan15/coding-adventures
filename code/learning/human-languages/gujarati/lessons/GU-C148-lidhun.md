# લીધું, ખાધું, પીધું — three pasts in -ધું

## Grammar Lens: -ધું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C148-DHUN-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-LEVU, GU-LEX-KHAAVU, GU-LEX-C55-HOUSEWORK-05, GU-LEX-BHAAT-01, GU-LEX-ROTLI, GU-LEX-C86-JOURNEY-02, GU-LEX-C49-CARE-02] -->

**પીધું** has been doing this since the first lesson of the chapter. Three short,
common verbs share it: instead of **-્યું** they take **-ધું**.

- **લેવું** → **લીધું** — *līdhũ* — took;
- **ખાવું** → **ખાધું** — *khādhũ* — ate;
- **પીવું** → **પીધું** — *pīdhũ* — drank.

Their endings are the usual four: **ખાધો**, **ખાધી**, **ખાધું**, **ખાધા**.

> **મેં ભાત ખાધો.** — *mẽ bhāt khādho.* — I ate rice.
> **તેણે રોટલી ખાધી.** — *teṇe roṭlī khādhī.* — She ate a roṭlī.
> **મેં ટિકિટ લીધી.** — *mẽ ṭikiṭ līdhī.* — I took a ticket.
> **તેણે ભાત લીધો.** — *teṇe bhāt līdho.* — He took some rice.
> **તેણે દવા લીધી.** — *teṇe davā līdhī.* — He took the medicine.
