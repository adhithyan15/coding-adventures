# ब — ba without the puff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Hold one hand near your mouth so you can notice the missing puff.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-BA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ब

This is *ba*, the quiet partner of *bha*. Circle the oval body; descend the
right stem; draw the inner diagonal down-right; finish the headline.

## Writing — heard cue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-BA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Cover the model. [YOU HEAR: *ba*, without a puff.] Write the single consonant
from sound alone. Uncover **ब** and repair only the differing run.
