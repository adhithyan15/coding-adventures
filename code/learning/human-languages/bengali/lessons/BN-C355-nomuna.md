## You'll want to know: নমুনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C355-NOMUNA355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**নমুনা** — *nômunā* — "a sample; a specimen".

> ডাক্তার রক্তের নমুনা নিলেন।
>
> *ḍāktār rôkter nômunā nilen.*
>
> The doctor took a blood sample.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নমুনা** (*nômunā*) is usually traced to Persian *namūna*, a pattern or sample: a small part that shows what the whole is like.
