## You'll want to know: गौरव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C322-PURASKAR322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरव** — *gaurav* — "an honour; glory".

> हा पुरस्कार आमच्या शाळेचा गौरव आहे.
>
> *hā puraskār āmacyā śāḷecā gaurav āhe.*
>
> This award is an honour for our school.

Masculine.

गौरव करणे, to honour someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरव** (*gaurav*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *gaurava*, 'weight; dignity, respect' (Monier-Williams).
