## You'll want to know: a bússola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C435-ORIENTAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a bússola** — "the compass".

> Na montanha, levamos sempre uma bússola.
>
> *In the mountains, we always take a compass.*

The needle points north.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bússola* was borrowed from Italian *bussola*, from Late Latin *buxula* 'little box' (EtymDB), on *buxus* 'boxwood': the needle sat in a small wooden box.
