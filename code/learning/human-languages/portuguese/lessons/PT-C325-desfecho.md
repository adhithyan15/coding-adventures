## You'll want to know: o desfecho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C325-HEROI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o desfecho** — "the ending, the outcome".

> O desfecho da história surpreendeu toda a gente.
>
> *The ending of the story surprised everyone.*

*Um desfecho feliz* — a happy ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desfecho* is built on *desfechar*, from *des-* and *fechar* "to close": the knot of the story comes undone. EtymDB traces *fechar* through Vulgar Latin *fesclāre* to Latin *fistulāre*, built on *fistula* "a pipe, a tube" (Lewis & Short).
