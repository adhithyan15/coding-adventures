# ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ (uruḷpoṭṭal) — a landslide

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C376-JEEVAN376-05, ML-LEX-C377-BHUKAMPAM377-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to drown, then the Malayalam for an earthquake.

## You'll want to know: ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C377-BHUKAMPAM377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ** — *uruḷpoṭṭal* — "a landslide".

> മലയിൽ ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ ഉണ്ടായി.
>
> *malayil uruḷpoṭṭal uṇṭāyi.*
>
> There was a landslide on the hill.

Common on the hills in the rainy season.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉരുൾപൊട്ടൽ** (*uruḷpoṭṭal*) is a native Dravidian compound of ഉരുൾ, "a rolling" (DEDR 664), and പൊട്ടൽ, "a bursting", from പൊട്ടുക (DEDR 4490): the hillside bursts and rolls down.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
