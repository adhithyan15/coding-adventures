## You'll want to know: genehmigen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C384-GENEHMIGUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**genehmigen** — "to approve, to permit".

> Mein Chef hat meinen Urlaub genehmigt.
>
> *My boss has approved my holiday.*

Inseparable: *er genehmigt, er hat genehmigt*. *Sich etwas genehmigen* — to treat yourself to something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*genehmigen* is built on the old adjective *genehm* "acceptable", of the family of *nehmen* "to take", Old High German *neman*, Proto-Germanic *nemaną* (EtymDB).
