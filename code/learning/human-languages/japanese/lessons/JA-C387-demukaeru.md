# でむかえる (demukaeru) — to go out to meet (someone arriving)

## You'll want to know: でむかえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C387-DEMUKAERU387-01]; assesses=[] -->

**でむかえる** — *demukaeru* — "to go out to meet (someone arriving)".

> **くうこうで そふを でむかえました。** — *kūkō de sofu o demukaemashita* — "We met my grandfather at the airport."

Polite **でむかえます**, *demukaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ははが くうこうで そふを でむかえました。**, *haha ga kūkō de sofu o demukaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**でむかえる** (*demukaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *de*, "going out" (from *deru*) and *mukaeru*, "to welcome"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
