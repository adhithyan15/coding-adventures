# -ஐவிட (-aiviṭa) — bigger than, most of all

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C41-ADJ-01, TA-LEX-C41-ADJ-02, TA-LEX-C56-AMOUNT-02, TA-LEX-C56-AMOUNT-03, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "big" and "small". (*periya*, *ciṟiya*.) Then "a lot" and "less". (*niṟaiya*, *kuṟaivu*.) Then say "dampness". (*īram*.)

## You'll want to know: X-ஐவிட Y …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C291-THAN-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-VEEDU-01, TA-LEX-C41-ADJ-01, TA-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-01, TA-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-02, TA-LEX-C69-GOING-02, TA-LEX-C69-GOING-03, TA-LEX-C41-ADJ-03, TA-LEX-C56-AMOUNT-03, TA-PRAGMATICS-C80-PRICE-03, TA-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-03] -->

> **இந்த வீட்டைவிட அந்த வீடு பெரியது.**
>
> *inta vīṭṭaiviṭa anta vīṭu periyatu.* — "That house is bigger than this house."

Tamil has no "-er" on the describing word. It says "leaving this house aside, that house is big": the thing you compare **with** takes **-ஐ**, then **விட**, the to-form of **விடு**, "to leave", joined on: **வீட்டைவிட**. **அந்த** (*anta*, "that") is the **a-** of **அது** in front of a noun, as **இந்த** is the **i-**. **பெரியது** (*periyatu*, "a big one; big") is **பெரிய** made into a thing, like **நல்லது**.

> **பேருந்தைவிட ரயில் நல்லது.**
>
> *pēruntaiviṭa rayil nallatu.* — "The train is better than the bus."

With an amount, **அதிகம்**, "more, a lot", and **குறைவு**, "less", do the comparing: **இங்கே விலை குறைவு** (*iṅkē vilai kuṟaivu*, "prices are lower here").

## You'll want to know: the most, எல்லாவற்றையும்விட
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C291-MOST-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C181-GUNAM181-04, TA-LEX-PIDI-01, TA-LEX-C71-DAY-03, TA-LEX-C85-AVAN-AVAL-01, TA-LEX-C145-QUAL145-05] -->

> **எனக்கு எல்லாவற்றையும்விட தமிழ் பிடிக்கும்.**
>
> *eṉakku ellāvaṟṟaiyumviṭa tamiḻ piṭikkum.* — "I like Tamil most of all."

"Most" is "more than everything": **எல்லாம்**, "everything", takes **-ஐ** in the shape **எல்லாவற்றை**, then **-உம்**, "all of it", then **-விட**. For people, **எல்லோரையும்விட** (*ellōraiyumviṭa*, "than everyone"):

> **அவள் எல்லோரையும்விட வேகமாக ஓடினாள்.**
>
> *avaḷ ellōraiyumviṭa vēkamāka ōṭiṉāḷ.* — "She ran fastest of all."

**வேகமாக**, "fast", is the **-ஆக** form of **வேகமான**.
