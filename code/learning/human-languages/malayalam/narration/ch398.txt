Malayalam, chapter 398: The Throne and the Crown.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സിംഹാസനം (siṁhāsanaṁ) — a throne.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a princess, then the Malayalam for a palace.

You'll want to know: സിംഹാസനം.
സിംഹാസനം — siṁhāsanaṁ — "a throne".
രാജാവ് സിംഹാസനത്തിൽ ഇരുന്നു. rājāvŭ siṁhāsanattil irunnu. The king sat on the throne.
Literally a lion seat: സിംഹം, a lion, + ആസനം, a seat.

The word, taken apart.
സിംഹാസനം (siṁhāsanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit siṁhāsana, "a lion's seat, a king's seat, a throne" (Monier-Williams), from siṁha, "a lion" (CDIAL 13384), and āsana, "a seat" (CDIAL 1484).

Lesson 3 of 5.

പുതിയ രാജാവിന്റെ പട്ടാഭിഷേകം ഒരു വലിയ ഉത്സവം ആയിരുന്നു. putiya rājāvinṟe paṭṭābhiṣēkaṁ oru valiya utsavaṁ āyirunnu. The new king's coronation was a great festival.

The word, taken apart.
പട്ടാഭിഷേകം (paṭṭābhiṣēkaṁ) is built from Sanskrit paṭṭa, "cloth; a fillet, a diadem" (CDIAL 7700), and abhiṣeka, "anointing; the inauguration of a king" (Monier-Williams).

അക്കാലത്ത് ആ രാജ്യത്തിന്റെ ഭരണാധികാരി ഒരു രാജ്ഞി ആയിരുന്നു. akkālattŭ ā rājyattinṟe bharaṇādhikāri oru rājñi āyirunnu. In those days the ruler of that country was a queen.
One who holds the authority to rule a land.

ഭരണാധികാരി (bharaṇādhikāri) is built from Sanskrit bharaṇa, "bearing, maintaining", and adhikārin, "possessing authority; a governor" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
വാഴുക (vāḻuka) — to reign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a coronation, then the Malayalam for a sovereign.

You'll want to know: വാഴുക.
വാഴുക — vāḻuka — "to reign".
ആ രാജാവ് നാൽപത് വർഷം വാണു. ā rājāvŭ nālpatŭ varṣaṁ vāṇu. That king reigned for forty years.
Past: വാണു, vāṇu.
Literary and old: to reign, to live in glory. The past is വാണു.

The word, taken apart.
വാഴുക (vāḻuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5372 lists Malayalam vāḻuka, "to live, to live well or happily, to rule, to reign".
