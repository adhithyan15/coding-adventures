# కంగారుపడు (kaṅgārupaḍu) — to panic; to get flustered

## You'll want to know: కంగారుపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కంగారుపడు** — *kaṅgārupaḍu* — "to panic; to get flustered".

> రైలు రాకపోతే అతను కంగారుపడతాడు.
>
> *railu rākapōtē atanu kaṅgārupaḍatāḍu.*
>
> If the train does not come, he gets flustered.

Past (I …): కంగారుపడ్డాను, *kaṅgārupaḍḍānu*.

The noun కంగారు is fluster or panic: కంగారుగా, in a flurry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కంగారుపడు** (*kaṅgārupaḍu*) is built from *kaṅgāru*, "fluster, panic", and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853). The origin of *kaṅgāru* itself is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
