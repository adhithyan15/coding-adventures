## You'll want to know: けっこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C323-ENRYO323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**けっこう** — *kekkō* — "no thank you; fine".

> **さっき たべたから、けっこうです。** — *sakki tabeta kara, kekkō desu* — "I ate a moment ago, so no, thank you."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けっこう** (*kekkō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ketsu*, "tie" (Middle Chinese *ket*) and *kō*, "build" (*kuwH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *kek-kō*.
