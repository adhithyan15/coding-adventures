Lesson 1 of 6.
दोलायते (dolāyate) — wavers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a waste of time, then the Sanskrit for spends time.

You'll want to know: दोलायते.
दोलायते — dolāyate — "wavers".
यदि मनः दोलायते, तर्हि मित्रं पृच्छतु। yadi manaḥ dolāyate, tarhi mitraṃ pṛcchatu. If your mind wavers, ask a friend.
Past (he …): सः बहु दोलायितवान्, saḥ bahu dolāyitavān.

The word, taken apart.
दोलायते (dolāyate) is built on dolā, "a swing": MW gives dolāyate as to rock about like a swing, to move to and fro, to be doubtful (MW).

Lesson 2 of 6.
विमृशति (vimṛśati) — weighs up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for spends time, then the Sanskrit for wavers.

You'll want to know: विमृशति.
विमृशति — vimṛśati — "weighs up".
यदि त्वं विमृशसि, तर्हि उचितः मार्गः स्पष्टः भविष्यति। yadi tvaṃ vimṛśasi, tarhi ucitaḥ mārgaḥ spaṣṭaḥ bhaviṣyati. If you think it over, the right way will become clear.
Past (he …): सः चिरं विमृष्टवान्, saḥ ciraṃ vimṛṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
विमृशति (vimṛśati) is built on vi, "apart", and the root √mṛś, "to touch": to touch with the mind; MW gives vimṛś as to consider, to reflect on, to deliberate; its past participle is vimṛṣṭa (MW).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for wavers, then the Sanskrit for weighs up.

You'll want to know: द्विधा.
द्विधा — dvidhā — "in two ways".
यदि मार्गः द्विधा भवति, तर्हि कुत्र गच्छामि? yadi mārgaḥ dvidhā bhavati, tarhi kutra gacchāmi? If the road splits in two, which way do I go?

The word, taken apart.
द्विधा (dvidhā) is built on dvi, "two", with the ending -dhā, "in... ways"; MW gives dvidhā as in two ways or parts, divided (MW).

You'll want to know: अपरः.
अपरः — aparaḥ — "the other one".
यदि एषः मार्गः कठिनः अस्ति, तर्हि अपरं मार्गं गच्छामः। yadi eṣaḥ mārgaḥ kaṭhinaḥ asti, tarhi aparaṃ mārgaṃ gacchāmaḥ. If this road is hard, we take the other one.

The word, taken apart.
अपरः (aparaḥ) comes from apa, "away, off", as MW gives it; MW records apara as later, latter, the other (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
विभिन्नः (vibhinnaḥ) — different.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for in two ways, then the Sanskrit for the other one.

You'll want to know: विभिन्नः.
विभिन्नः — vibhinnaḥ — "different".
यदि मतानि विभिन्नानि सन्ति, तर्हि वयं पुनः चिन्तयामः। yadi matāni vibhinnāni santi, tarhi vayaṃ punaḥ cintayāmaḥ. If our opinions differ, we think again.

The word, taken apart.
विभिन्नः (vibhinnaḥ) is built on vi, "apart", and the root √bhid, "to split", which also gives bhinatti, as its past participle; MW gives vibhinna as split, divided, and various (MW).
