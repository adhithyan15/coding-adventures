# a condición de que — on condition that

## You'll want to know: a condición de que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C612-SINO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a condición de que** — "on condition that".

> Firmo a condición de que me paguen antes.
>
> *I will sign on condition that they pay me first.*

Formal; followed by the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a condición de que* is built on *condición*, a learned borrowing from Latin *condicio* 'terms, agreement' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
