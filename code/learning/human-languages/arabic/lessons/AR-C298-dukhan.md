## You'll want to know: دخان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C298-HARIQ298-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دخان** — *dukhān* — "smoke".

> خرج دخان أسود من النافذة.
>
> *kharaja dukhān aswad min an-nāfidha.*
>
> Black smoke came out of the window.

In many dialects دخان also means tobacco.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دخان** (*dukhān*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-kh-n* (Lane), where *dakhana* is said of a fire that sends up smoke.
