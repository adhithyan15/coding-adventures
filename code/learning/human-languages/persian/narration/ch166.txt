Persian, chapter 166: Toman, Customer, Size, Cash, Blouse.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
تومان (tumân) — toman (ten rials).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for toothpaste, then the Persian for shampoo.

You'll want to know: تومان.
تومان — tumân — "toman (ten rials)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tumân]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tumân, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say tumân]

[your turn — recall: say the Persian for toothpaste, then the Persian for shampoo, then say tumân again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does تومان (tumân) mean? ("Toman (ten rials)".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

(وگرنه, vagarna.) While? (در حالی که, dar hâli ke.) Especially? (مخصوصا, makhsusan.) A motive? (انگیزه, angize.) A meaning? (منظور, manzur.) An intention? (قصد, qasd.) An event? (اتفاق, ettefâq.) Luck? (شانس, shâns.) Tasty? (خوشمزه, khoshmaze.) Cool? (خنک, khonak.) Raw? (خام, khâm.) Tight? (تنگ, tang.) Loose? (گشاد, goshâd.)
