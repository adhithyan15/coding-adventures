## You'll want to know: شفاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C357-BADUNVANI357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شفاف** — *shaffāf* — "transparent; open".

> حکومت کا کام شفاف ہونا چاہیے۔
>
> *hukūmat kā kām shaffāf honā chāhiye.*
>
> The government's work should be transparent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شفاف** (*shaffāf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shaffāf*, "transparent, see-through".
