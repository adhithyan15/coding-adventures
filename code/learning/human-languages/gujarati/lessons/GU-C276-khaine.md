# ખાઈને, જમ્યા પછી (khāīne, jamyā pachī) — having done, after doing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-GAYO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERGATIVE-01, GU-PHRASE-MEHERBANI-KARINE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "please", the long way. (**મહેરબાની કરીને**.) Its **કરીને** is the form this lesson teaches. Then say "I went home". (*hũ ghare gayo*.)

## Grammar Lens: the -ઈને form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C276-INE-FORM-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-JAVU, GU-LEX-C163-MEAL163-03, GU-LEX-KHAAVU, GU-LEX-LEVU, GU-LEX-JOVU] -->

> **હું ઘરે જઈને જમ્યો.**
>
> *hũ ghare jaīne jamyo.* — "I went home and ate."

One doer, two actions in a row: the first takes **-ઈને**, "having done it", and the second ends the sentence. After a vowel the stem takes **-ઈને**: **જઈને**, **ખાઈને**, **લઈને**, **જોઈને**. After a consonant it takes **-ીને**: **કરીને**, **લખીને**, **વાંચીને**.

The last verb decides the doer's form: **હું ખાઈને આવ્યો** (*hũ khāīne āvyo*), "I ate and came", but **મેં ખાઈને ચા પીધી** (*mẽ khāīne cā pīdhī*), "I ate and then drank tea".

## You'll want to know: -્યા પછી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C276-YA-PACHI-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C77-WHEN-04, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-LEX-BAJAR-01] -->

> **જમ્યા પછી અમે બજાર ગયા.**
>
> *jamyā pachī ame bajār gayā.* — "After eating, we went to the market."

**પછી** after the past stem in **-્યા** says "after doing it": **જમ્યા પછી**, **કામ કર્યા પછી** (*kām karyā pachī*), **ચા પીધા પછી** (*cā pīdhā pachī*). That stem takes no **-એ** doer of its own: **કામ કર્યા પછી હું ઘરે ગયો**.
