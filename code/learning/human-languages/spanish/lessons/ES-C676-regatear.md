## You'll want to know: regatear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C676-TURISMO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**regatear** — "to haggle, to bargain".

> En el mercado puedes regatear un poco.
>
> *At the market you can haggle a little.*

Shops have fixed prices; at a market stall you can try.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regatear* is usually traced, through older *recatear* (EtymDB), to Vulgar Latin *recaptāre*, built on Latin *captāre* "to try to seize".
