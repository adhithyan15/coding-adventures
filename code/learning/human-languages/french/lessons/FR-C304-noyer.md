## You'll want to know: noyer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C304-NOYADE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**noyer** — "to drown (as se noyer); to flood".

> Il s'est presque noyé dans le lac.
>
> *He nearly drowned in the lake.*

Reflexive for a person who drowns: *il s'est noyé*. Without *se*, to drown someone or to flood: *noyer le moteur*. The noun *le noyer* is a walnut tree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*noyer* is inherited from Latin *necāre* "to kill" (EtymDB), which in Late Latin narrowed to killing by water (Lewis & Short).
