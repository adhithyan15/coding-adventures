## You'll want to know: l'intention
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C374-BUT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'intention** — "the intention".

> Je n'ai pas l'intention de partir.
>
> *I have no intention of leaving.*

*Avoir l'intention de* + infinitive. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*intention* comes through Old French from Latin *intentiō* "a stretching, an aim" (EtymDB), from *intendere* "to stretch toward", built on *tendere* "to stretch" (Lewis & Short).
