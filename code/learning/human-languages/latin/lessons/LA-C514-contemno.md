## You'll want to know: contemnō, contemnere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C514-CONT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**contemnō, contemnere** — "to despise, scorn".

> Sapiēns pecūniam contemnit.
>
> *The wise man despises money.*

*contemnō, contemnere, contempsī, contemptum*, with the accusative. English *contempt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**contemnō** is built on *con-* and *temnō* "to scorn" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *contempt* and *contemptible* come from it.
