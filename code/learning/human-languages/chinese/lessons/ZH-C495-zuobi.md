## You'll want to know: zuòbì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C495-MARKS495-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòbì* — "to cheat in an exam".

> *kǎoshì de shíhou bù yīnggāi zuòbì* — You shouldn't cheat in an exam.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòbì* is built from 作 'to do' and *bì* 'harm', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsak* and *bjiejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'worn out' and 'bad'.
