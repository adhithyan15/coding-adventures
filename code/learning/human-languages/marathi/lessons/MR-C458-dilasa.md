## You'll want to know: दिलासा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C458-DILASA458-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलासा** — *dilāsā* — "comfort; reassurance".

> मित्राच्या फोनने मला खूप दिलासा मिळाला.
>
> *mitrācyā phonne malā khūp dilāsā miḷālā.*
>
> My friend's phone call was a great comfort to me.

Masculine.

दिलासा देणे, to reassure; दिलासा मिळणे, to be reassured, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलासा** (*dilāsā*) is a loan from Persian *dilāsā*, "consolation", built on *dil*, "heart".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
