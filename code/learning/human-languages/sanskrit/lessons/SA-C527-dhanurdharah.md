## You'll want to know: धनुर्धरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C527-DHANURDHARA527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धनुर्धरः** — *dhanurdharaḥ* — "an archer".

> धनुर्धरः धनुः गृह्णाति।
>
> *dhanurdharaḥ dhanuḥ gṛhṇāti.*
>
> The archer takes up his bow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धनुर्धरः** (*dhanurdharaḥ*) is built from *dhanus*, "a bow", and *dhara*, "holding", from the root √dhṛ, "to hold"; MW gives *dhanurdhara* as a bow-holder, an archer (MW).
