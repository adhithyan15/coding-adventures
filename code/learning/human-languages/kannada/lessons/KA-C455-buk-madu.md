# ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು (buk māḍu) — to make a booking; to book

## You'll want to know: ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C455-PRAVASA455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು** — *buk māḍu* — "to make a booking; to book".

> ನಾನು ಆಪ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಎರಡು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu āpnalli eraḍu ṭikeṭ buk māḍide.*
>
> I booked two tickets on an app.

Past (I …): ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *buk māḍide*.

Everyday speech for ಕಾಯ್ದಿರಿಸು, to reserve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬುಕ್ ಮಾಡು** (*buk māḍu*) joins a loan from English *book*, "to reserve", and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.
