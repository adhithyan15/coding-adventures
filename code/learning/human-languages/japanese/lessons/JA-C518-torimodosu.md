# とりもどす (torimodosu) — to get back; to regain

## You'll want to know: とりもどす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C518-REGRET518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**とりもどす** — *torimodosu* — "to get back; to regain".

> **もし びょうきが なおったら、かれは げんきを とりもどすでしょう。** — *moshi byōki ga naottara, kare wa genki o torimodosu deshō* — "If his illness gets better, he will get his strength back."

Polite **とりもどします**, *torimodoshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かれが げんきを とりもどしました。**, *kare ga genki o torimodoshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とりもどす** (*torimodosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *toru*, "to take", and *modosu*, "to put back"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
