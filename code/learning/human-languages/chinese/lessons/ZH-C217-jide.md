## You'll want to know: jìde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C217-FINE217-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jìde* — "to remember".

> *nǐ hái jìde wǒ ma* — Do you still remember me?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìde* is built from *jì* 'to note down' and 得 'to get', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kiH* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); in the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
