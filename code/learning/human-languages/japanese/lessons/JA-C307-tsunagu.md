## You'll want to know: つなぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C307-DENWA307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**つなぐ** — *tsunagu* — "to join; to hold (hands)".

> **こどもと てを つないで あるきました。** — *kodomo to te o tsunaide arukimashita* — "I walked hand in hand with the child."

Polite **つなぎます**, *tsunagimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが てを つなぎました。**, *kodomo ga te o tsunagimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つなぐ** (*tsunagu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the transitive partner of *tsunagaru*, "to be connected".
