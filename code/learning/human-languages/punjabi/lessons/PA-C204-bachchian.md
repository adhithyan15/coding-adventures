## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-BACHCHIAN-204]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਬੱਚਿਆਂ** — *bachchiā̃* — children, before a postposition

Hear and say the whole form before reading it. Keep **ਬੱਚਿਆਂ** whole. Use this
form for “children” before the familiar **ਨਾਲ**. The next lesson retrieves the
new form alone before that old postposition returns.

## The word, taken apart — a small, well-marked trail
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-BACHCHIAN-204] -->

Punjabi University Patiala's noun grammar explains the form pattern used here.
Its lexical statistics identifies this exact word as the plural-oblique form of
the familiar child noun, and University Lesson 12 independently presents the
same pattern.
Sources: [Punjabi University noun grammar](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/noun.html),
[Punjabi University lexical statistics](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/statistics.html),
and [Punjabi University Lesson 12](https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/lesson12.aspx?l=12).

Chapter 55 records that the origin of **ਬੱਚਾ** remains unsettled. This lesson
makes no new historical claim and keeps the new form whole.
