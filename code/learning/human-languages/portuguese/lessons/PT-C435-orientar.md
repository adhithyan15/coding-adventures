## You'll want to know: orientar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C435-ORIENTAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**orientar** — "to guide, to orient; (orientar-se) to find your bearings".

> Sem mapa, oriento-me pelo sol.
>
> *Without a map, I find my bearings by the sun.*

*Orientar-se* — to find your way. A teacher also *orienta* a student's work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orientar* is built on *oriente* 'east', which is inherited from Latin *oriēns* 'rising', of the sun (EtymDB), from *orīrī* 'to rise': first to face the east.
