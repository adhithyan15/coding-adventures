## You'll want to know: ひるね
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C498-REST498-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひるね** — *hirune* — "a nap".

> **ごはんの あとで ひるねを します。** — *gohan no ato de hirune o shimasu* — "I take a nap after eating."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひるね** (*hirune*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *hiru*, "noon" and *ne*, "sleep" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
