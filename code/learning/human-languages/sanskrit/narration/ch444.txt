Sanskrit, chapter 444: The Moon's Fortnights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पूर्णिमा (pūrṇimā) — the full-moon day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a dark night, then the Sanskrit for by night.

You'll want to know: पूर्णिमा.
पूर्णिमा — pūrṇimā — "the full-moon day".
पूर्णिमायां चन्द्रः पूर्णः भवति। pūrṇimāyāṃ candraḥ pūrṇaḥ bhavati. On the full-moon day the moon is full.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पूर्णिमा (pūrṇimā) is built on pūrṇa, "full"; MW gives pūrṇimā as the day of full moon (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अमावास्या (amāvāsyā) — the new-moon night.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for by night, then the Sanskrit for the full-moon day.

You'll want to know: अमावास्या.
अमावास्या — amāvāsyā — "the new-moon night".
अमावास्यायाम् आकाशे चन्द्रः न अस्ति। amāvāsyāyām ākāśe candraḥ na asti. On the new-moon night there is no moon in the sky.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अमावास्या (amāvāsyā) is built from amā, "at home, together", and the root √vas, "to dwell": the night when sun and moon dwell together; MW gives amāvāsyā as the night of new moon; it lives on as Hindi amāwas, "the night of the new moon" (MW; CDIAL 565).

Lesson 3 of 5.
शुक्लपक्षः (śuklapakṣaḥ) — the bright fortnight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the full-moon day, then the Sanskrit for the new-moon night.

You'll want to know: शुक्लपक्षः.
शुक्लपक्षः — śuklapakṣaḥ — "the bright fortnight".
शुक्लपक्षे चन्द्रः प्रतिदिनं वर्धते। śuklapakṣe candraḥ pratidinaṃ vardhate. In the bright fortnight the moon grows every day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
शुक्लपक्षः (śuklapakṣaḥ) is built from śukla, "bright, white", and pakṣa, "a wing, a side", which MW also gives as the half of a lunar month; MW gives the compound as the light half of the month (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कृष्णपक्षः (kṛṣṇapakṣaḥ) — the dark fortnight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the new-moon night, then the Sanskrit for the bright fortnight.

You'll want to know: कृष्णपक्षः.
कृष्णपक्षः — kṛṣṇapakṣaḥ — "the dark fortnight".
कृष्णपक्षे रात्रौ अन्धकारः भवति। kṛṣṇapakṣe rātrau andhakāraḥ bhavati. In the dark fortnight there is darkness at night.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कृष्णपक्षः (kṛṣṇapakṣaḥ) is built from kṛṣṇa, "dark, black", and pakṣa, "a wing, a side, a half-month"; MW gives the compound as the dark half of the month, when the moon wanes (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
राजते (rājate) — gleams; shines brightly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the bright fortnight, then the Sanskrit for the dark fortnight.

You'll want to know: राजते.
राजते — rājate — "gleams; shines brightly".
अमावास्यायाः अनन्तरं चन्द्रः पुनः राजते। amāvāsyāyāḥ anantaraṃ candraḥ punaḥ rājate. After the new moon the moon gleams again.
Past (it …): चन्द्रः पुनः राजितवान्, candraḥ punaḥ rājitavān.

The word, taken apart.
राजते (rājate) comes from the root √rāj, "to shine, to be splendid", which MW also gives as to reign, and thinks was perhaps two roots at first; MW gives the participle rājita, "resplendent" (MW).
