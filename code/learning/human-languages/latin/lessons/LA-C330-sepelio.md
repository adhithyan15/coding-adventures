## You'll want to know: sepeliō, sepelīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C330-FUNUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sepeliō, sepelīre** — "to bury".

> Mīlitēs sociōs suōs prope viam sepelīvērunt.
>
> *The soldiers buried their comrades near the road.*

*sepeliō, sepelīre, sepelīvī, sepultum*. Graves lay outside the city walls, along the roads.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sepeliō** is an inherited verb whose earlier origin is not settled (Lewis & Short, EtymDB). English *sepulchre* belongs to its family.
