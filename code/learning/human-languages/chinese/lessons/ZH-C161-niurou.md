## You'll want to know: niúròu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C161-RICE161-04]; assesses=[] -->

*niúròu* — "beef".

> *zhège niúròu hěn hǎochī* — This beef is delicious.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niúròu* is built from *niú* 'ox' and *ròu* 'meat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjuw* and *nyuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
