## You'll want to know: رعب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C489-KHAWF489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رعب** — *ruʿb* — "terror".

> شاهد أخي فيلم رعب أمس.
>
> *shāhada akhī fīlm ruʿb ams.*
>
> My brother watched a horror film yesterday.

فيلم رعب is a horror film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رعب** (*ruʿb*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ʿ-b*, "to fill with fear" (Lane). Ruʿb is fear at its strongest.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
