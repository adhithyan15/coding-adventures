## You'll want to know: 北上
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C422-NORTH422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**北上** — *běishàng* — "to go north; to head up north".

> **明天我们坐飞机北上** *míngtiān wǒmen zuò fēijī běishàng* — Tomorrow we fly north.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**北上** (*běishàng*) is built from 北 'north' and 上 'to go up', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pok* and *dzyangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to climb'.
