Urdu, chapter 455: Faults and Flaws.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
خامی (khāmī) — a shortcoming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a good quality, then the Urdu for a personality.

You'll want to know: خامی.
خامی — khāmī — "a shortcoming".
ہر انسان میں کوئی نہ کوئی خامی ہوتی ہے۔ har insān meṅ koī na koī khāmī hotī hai. Everyone has some shortcoming or other.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
خامی (khāmī) is usually traced to Persian khāmī, "rawness", built on khām, "raw, unripe": a shortcoming is something not yet ripe.
