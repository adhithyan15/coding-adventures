Spanish, chapter 657: Papeles en regla.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
caducar — to expire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to stamp, then the Spanish for to search.

You'll want to know: caducar.
caducar — "to expire".
Mi pasaporte caduca en marzo. My passport expires in March.
Food caduca too: la fecha de caducidad is the use-by date.

The word, taken apart.
caducar is built on caduco "worn out, past its time" (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin cadūcus "falling, fallen" (Lewis & Short), from cadere "to fall".

You'll want to know: la fotocopia.
la fotocopia — "the photocopy".
Trae una fotocopia del pasaporte. Bring a photocopy of your passport.
The verb is fotocopiar.

The word, taken apart.
fotocopia is built from Greek phōs, phōtós "light" and copia "copy", which comes from Latin cōpia "plenty".
