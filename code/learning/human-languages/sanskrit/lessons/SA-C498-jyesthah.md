## You'll want to know: ज्येष्ठः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C498-AGRAJA498-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्येष्ठः** — *jyeṣṭhaḥ* — "eldest".

> ज्येष्ठः पुत्रः पितुः साहाय्यं करोति।
>
> *jyeṣṭhaḥ putraḥ pituḥ sāhāyyaṃ karoti.*
>
> The eldest son helps his father.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्येष्ठः** (*jyeṣṭhaḥ*) MW gives it as a superlative (Pāṇini 5.3.61), "pre-eminent, greatest, eldest", without naming a root, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
