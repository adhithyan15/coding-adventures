## You'll want to know: देवर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C259-SASU259-03]; assesses=[] -->

**देवर** — *devar* — "a husband's younger brother".

> म्हारो देवर ऊंट राखै है।
>
> *mhāro devar ū̃ṭ rākhai hai.*
>
> My husband's younger brother keeps camels.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देवर** (*devar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *devṛ*, "a husband's younger brother"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *devara* under it (CDIAL 6546).
