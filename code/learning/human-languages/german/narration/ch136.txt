Lesson 1 of 5.
wollen — to want.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for cool, then the German for soft.

You'll want to know: wollen.
wollen — "to want".

The word, taken apart.
wollen goes back to Old High German wellen from Proto-Germanic wiljaną, "to want" — inherited, the same verb as English will. (EtymDB 2.1).

The word, taken apart.
bestellen is be- + the verb "to put, to place", Old High German stellen from Proto-Germanic stallijaną (EtymDB 2.1): to arrange for something to be put in place.

Lesson 5 of 5.
lieber — rather, preferably.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to hope, then the German for to order.

You'll want to know: lieber.
lieber — "rather, preferably".

The word, taken apart.
lieber is the comparative of the word for "dear", Old High German liob from Proto-Germanic leubaz (EtymDB 2.1): "more dear". English lief, as in "I'd as lief", is the same word.
