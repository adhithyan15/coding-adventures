## You'll want to know: zhùyuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C241-WARD241-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhùyuàn* — "to be in hospital".

> *shàng ge yuè wǒ bàba zhùyuàn le* — Last month my dad went into hospital.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhùyuàn* is built from *zhù* 'to stay' and *yuàn* 'a walled courtyard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjuH* and *hjwenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stop' and the second 'a wall round a courtyard'.
