Marathi, chapter 474: Tidy and Scatterbrained.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नीटनेटका (nīṭneṭkā) — tidy; neat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for amusement, then the Marathi for to tease.

You'll want to know: नीटनेटका.
नीटनेटका — nīṭneṭkā — "tidy; neat".
तिची खोली नेहमी नीटनेटकी असते. ticī kholī nehmī nīṭneṭkī aste. Her room is always neat and tidy.
Agrees: नीटनेटका (nīṭneṭkā), नीटनेटकी, नीटनेटकं.

The word, taken apart.
नीटनेटका (nīṭneṭkā) is a Marathi pair-word, नीट, "properly", with नेटका, "trim"; the origin of each part is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पसारा (pasārā) — a mess; clutter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for sloppy, then the Marathi for scatterbrained.

You'll want to know: पसारा.
पसारा — pasārā — "a mess; clutter".
मुलांच्या खोलीत खूप पसारा आहे. mulāñcyā kholīt khūp pasārā āhe. There is a big mess in the children's room.
Masculine.
Masculine: पसारा (pasārā) करणे, to make a mess; पसारा (pasārā) आवरणे, to clear it up.

The word, taken apart.
पसारा (pasārā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi pasārā, "stretching out, expansion", under Sanskrit prasāra, "extension" (CDIAL 8835). Things spread out all over a room make a mess.
