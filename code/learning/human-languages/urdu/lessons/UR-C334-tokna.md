## You'll want to know: ٹوکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C334-BAHAS334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوکنا** — *ṭoknā* — "to interrupt; to cut in".

> میں بات کر رہا تھا اور اس نے مجھے ٹوکا۔
>
> *maiṅ bāt kar rahā thā aur us ne mujhe ṭokā.*
>
> I was speaking and he cut me off.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجھے ٹوکا, *us ne mujhe ṭokā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوکنا** (*ṭoknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭoknā*, "to check, to question", under *\*ṭōkk*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5476).
