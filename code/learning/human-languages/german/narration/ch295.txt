German, chapter 295: Eine Panne.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Panne — the breakdown; a mishap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the high water, then the German for to collapse.

You'll want to know: die Panne.
die Panne — "the breakdown; a mishap".
Auf der Autobahn hatten wir eine Panne. We had a breakdown on the motorway.
Plural: die Pannen. Also a small mishap: eine kleine Panne.

The word, taken apart.
Panne was borrowed from French panne "breakdown" (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Plural: die Pannendienste. Der Dienst — service.

The word, taken apart.
Pannendienst joins Panne, borrowed from French panne "breakdown" (EtymDB), and the inherited Dienst "service".

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: abschleppen.
abschleppen — "to tow away".
Das Auto wurde abgeschleppt. The car was towed away.
Separable: Sie schleppen das Auto ab.

The word, taken apart.
abschleppen is built on schleppen "to drag", Middle High German slepen, which came from Middle Low German slēpen "to drag" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
beschädigen — to damage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the breakdown service, then the German for to tow away.

You'll want to know: beschädigen.
beschädigen — "to damage".
Der Ball beschädigte das Fenster. The ball damaged the window.
Regular: hat beschädigt. Built on der Schaden.

The word, taken apart.
beschädigen is built on Schaden "damage", which is usually traced to Old High German scado "damage", from Proto-Germanic skaþô (EtymDB).
