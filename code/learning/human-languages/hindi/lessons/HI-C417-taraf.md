## You'll want to know: तरफ़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C417-DISHA417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तरफ़** — *taraf* — "a side (of a place); towards (with की)".

> नदी की दूसरी तरफ़ एक मंदिर है।
>
> *nadī kī dūsrī taraf ek mandir hai.*
>
> There is a temple on the other side of the river.

Feminine.

की तरफ़, towards: स्टेशन की तरफ़ चलिए, walk towards the station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तरफ़** (*taraf*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṭaraf*, "a side, an edge", which reached Hindi through Persian.
