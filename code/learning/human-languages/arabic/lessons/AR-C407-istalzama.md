## You'll want to know: استلزم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C407-TAJANNUB407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استلزم** — *istalzama* — "to entail".

> يستلزم المشروع وقتا طويلا.
>
> *yastalzimu al-mashrūʿ waqtan ṭawīlan.*
>
> The project entails a long time.

Present: يستلزم, *yastalzim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استلزم** (*istalzama*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-z-m*, "to cling, to stick to" (Lane). It is Form X, to call for what must cling to a thing: to entail.
