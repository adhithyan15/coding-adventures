## You'll want to know: विषय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C397-VISHAY397-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विषय** — *viṣay* — "a subject, a topic".

> अगर तुम्हें विज्ञान पसंद है तो वही विषय चुनो।
>
> *agar tumhẽ vijñān pasand hai to vahī viṣay cuno.*
>
> If you like science, choose that subject.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विषय** (*viṣay*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *viṣaya*, "a sphere, a scope" (Monier-Williams).
