Spanish, chapter 605: Problemas sociales.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la marginación — the social exclusion, the marginalisation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to claim, then the Spanish for to demand.

You'll want to know: la marginación.
la marginación — "the social exclusion, the marginalisation".
Muchas personas viven en la marginación. Many people live on the margins of society.
Built on marginar — to push to the edge.

The word, taken apart.
marginación is built on marginar, a learned borrowing from Late Latin marginare (EtymDB), from margo 'edge, border'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
