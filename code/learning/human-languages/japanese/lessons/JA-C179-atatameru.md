# あたためる (atatameru) — to warm up; to heat

## You'll want to know: あたためる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C179-DRINK179-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あたためる** — *atatameru* — "to warm up; to heat".

> **ぎゅうにゅうを あたためます。** — *gyūnyū o atatamemasu* — "I warm up the milk."

Polite **あたためます**, *atatamemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あたためる** (*atatameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and it is built on the same stem as *atatakai*, "warm" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
