## You'll want to know: известие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C279-IZVESTIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**известие** (*izvéstiye*) — "a piece of news, a report".

> Мы получили известие о его приезде.
>
> *My poluchíli izvéstiye o yevó priyézde.*
>
> We received news of his arrival.

More formal than *новость*. Neuter; plural *известия*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*известие* is built on *весть* "news, tidings", from the old Slavic root of *ведать* "to know", which goes back to PIE *weyd-* "to see, to know" (EtymDB lists *известие* "news" with no parent).
