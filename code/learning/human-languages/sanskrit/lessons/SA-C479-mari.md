## You'll want to know: मारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C479-VYADHI479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मारी** — *mārī* — "a plague".

> मार्याः भयेन जनाः ग्रामं त्यजन्ति।
>
> *māryāḥ bhayena janāḥ grāmaṃ tyajanti.*
>
> In fear of the plague people leave the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मारी** (*mārī*) is built on *māra*, "killing", from the root √mṛ, "to die", the root of **म्रियते**; MW gives "killing" and "pestilence" (MW).
