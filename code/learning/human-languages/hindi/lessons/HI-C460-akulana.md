## You'll want to know: अकुलाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C460-BICHHAD460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अकुलाना** — *akulānā* — "to grow restless; to pine".

> बेटे की चिट्ठी नहीं आई, तो माँ अकुला गई।
>
> *beṭe kī ciṭṭhī nahīṃ āī, to māṁ akulā gaī.*
>
> When no letter came from her son, the mother grew restless with worry.

Past (she …): वह अकुला गई, *vah akulā gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अकुलाना** (*akulānā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *akulānā*, "to be confused, be restless", under Sanskrit *ākula* (CDIAL 1012).
