## You'll want to know: ઓટલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C417-POL417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓટલો** — *oṭlo* — "a raised platform (at the front of a house)".

> સાંજે દાદી ઓટલા પર બેસે છે.
>
> *sā̃je dādī oṭlā par bese che.*
>
> In the evening grandmother sits on the front platform.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓટલો** (*oṭlo*) goes back to *\*ōṭṭā*, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, a raised platform to sit on (CDIAL 2544).
