## You'll want to know: مسلسل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C379-MEHNAT379-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مسلسل** — *musalsal* — "continuous; without a break".

> اگر مسلسل بارش ہوئی تو سیلاب آئے گا۔
>
> *agar musalsal bārish huī to sailāb āe gā.*
>
> If it rains without a break, there will be a flood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسلسل** (*musalsal*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *musalsal*, "linked like a chain".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
