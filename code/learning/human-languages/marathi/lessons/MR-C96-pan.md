## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पान** (*pān*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *parṇa*, "plumage, foliage; leaf" (CDIAL 7918).
