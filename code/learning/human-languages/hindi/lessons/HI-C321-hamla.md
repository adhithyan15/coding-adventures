## You'll want to know: हमला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C321-HAMLA321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हमला** — *hamlā* — "an attack".

> दुश्मन ने रात को क़िले पर हमला किया।
>
> *duśman ne rāt ko qile par hamlā kiyā.*
>
> The enemy attacked the fort at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हमला** (*hamlā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥamla*, "a charge, an onset". What is attacked takes **पर**: **क़िले पर हमला**.
