## You'll want to know: なわとび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C500-TOY500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**なわとび** — *nawatobi* — "skipping rope".

> **こうえんで なわとびを しました。** — *kōen de nawatobi o shimashita* — "I skipped rope in the park."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なわとび** (*nawatobi*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *nawa*, "rope" and *tobi*, "jumping" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
