5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o vulcão — a volcano.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a rock, then the Portuguese for a wave.

You'll want to know: o vulcão.
o vulcão — "a volcano".

The word, taken apart.
vulcão from Latin Vulcanus, "Vulcan, the god of fire" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing from Latin. A fire-breathing mountain was named after the god.
