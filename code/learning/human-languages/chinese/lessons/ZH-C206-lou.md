## You'll want to know: lóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C206-FLOOR206-01]; assesses=[] -->

*lóu* — "a building of several floors; a floor".

> *wǒ zhù de lóu hěn xīn* — The building I live in is new.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lóu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *luw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a house of more than one storey'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
