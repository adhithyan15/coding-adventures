## You'll want to know: ಲಕ್ಷಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C384-GRAHISU384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಕ್ಷಣ** — *lakṣaṇa* — "a feature; a mark".

> ತಾಳ್ಮೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಶಿಕ್ಷಕನ ಲಕ್ಷಣ.
>
> *tāḷme oḷḷeya śikṣakana lakṣaṇa.*
>
> Patience is the mark of a good teacher.

ಲಕ್ಷಣವಾಗಿ, neatly, handsomely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಕ್ಷಣ** (*lakṣaṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lakṣaṇa*, "a mark, a sign, a characteristic" (CDIAL 10882).
