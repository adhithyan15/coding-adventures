## You'll want to know: きゅうけいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C451-COMMUTE451-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうけいする** — *kyūkei suru* — "to take a break".

> **ひるに すこし きゅうけいします。** — *hiru ni sukoshi kyūkei shimasu* — "I take a short break at noon."

Polite **きゅうけいします**, *kyūkeishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **どうりょうが きゅうけいしました。**, *dōryō ga kyūkei shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうけいする** (*kyūkei suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *kyūkei* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyū*, "rest" (Middle Chinese *xjuw*) and *kei*, "recess" (*khjejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
