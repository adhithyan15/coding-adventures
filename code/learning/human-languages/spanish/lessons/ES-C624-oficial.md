## You'll want to know: oficial
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C624-PERMISO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**oficial** — "official; the officer".

> Es el documento oficial.
>
> *It is the official document.*

As a noun it is a rank in the army or the police.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*oficial* is a learned borrowing from Latin *officialis* 'of duty', built on *officium* 'service, duty' (Lewis & Short).
