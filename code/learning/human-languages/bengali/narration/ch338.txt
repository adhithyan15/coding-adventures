Lesson 1 of 5.
সনদ (sônôd) — a certificate; a charter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to identify, then the Bengali for to uncover.

You'll want to know: সনদ.
সনদ — sônôd — "a certificate; a charter".
চাকরির জন্য তোমার সনদ দরকার। chākrir jônyô tomār sônôd dôrkār. You need your certificate for the job.

The word, taken apart.
সনদ (sônôd) is usually traced to Arabic sanad, something to lean on and so a warrant, through Persian. In Bengali it is a paper that vouches for you: a certificate of a degree, a skill or a grant.

Lesson 2 of 5.
তথ্য (tôthyô) — information; facts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to uncover, then the Bengali for a certificate.

You'll want to know: তথ্য.
তথ্য — tôthyô — "information; facts".
এই তথ্যটা কি সত্যি? ei tôthyôṭā ki sôtti? Is this information true?

The word, taken apart.
তথ্য (tôthyô) is Sanskrit tathya, 'being really so', truth (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for facts and information, a modern use of the word.

Warm-up.

সাক্ষ্য — sākkhyô — "testimony; evidence (given by a witness)".
সাক্ষী আদালতে সাক্ষ্য দিল। sākkhī ādālôte sākkhyô dilô. The witness gave evidence in court.

The word, taken apart.
সাক্ষ্য (sākkhyô) is Sanskrit sākṣya, testimony or evidence (Monier-Williams), the word for what a সাক্ষী, a witness, says. It is given with দেওয়া.

Lesson 4 of 5.
মেলানো (melāno) — to match up; to check against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for information, then the Bengali for testimony.

You'll want to know: মেলানো.
মেলানো — melāno — "to match up; to check against".
সনদের নামটা তোমার পরিচয়পত্রের সঙ্গে মেলাও। sônôder nāmṭā tomār pôrichôypôtrer sôngge melāo. Check the name on the certificate against your identity card.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নামগুলো মেলালেন, tini nāmgulo melālen.

The word, taken apart.
মেলানো (melāno) is the causative of মেলা 'to meet, to tally', of the family of Sanskrit milana, meeting (CDIAL 10134 lists Bengali milan): to make two things meet and see whether they agree. The second thing takes -এর সঙ্গে.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: খণ্ডন করা.
খণ্ডন করা — khônḍôn kôrā — "to refute".
মীরা প্রমাণ দিয়ে অভিযোগটা খণ্ডন করল। mīrā prômān diye ôbhijogṭā khônḍôn kôrlô. Mira refuted the charge with evidence.
Past (he/she …): তিনি যুক্তিটা খণ্ডন করলেন, tini juktiṭā khônḍôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
খণ্ডন করা (khônḍôn kôrā) is Sanskrit khaṇḍana, breaking into pieces and, in argument, refuting (Monier-Williams), with করা: to break a claim apart.
