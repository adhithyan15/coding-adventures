## You'll want to know: શપથ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C541-SHAPATH541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શપથ** — *śapath* — "a solemn oath".

> જો તમે શપથ લેશો તો તમારે સાચું બોલવું પડશે.
>
> *jo tame śapath leśo to tamāre sācũ bolvũ paḍśe.*
>
> If you take the oath, you will have to tell the truth.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શપથ** (*śapath*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śapatha*, "a curse; an oath" (Monier-Williams). One takes it formally: **શપથ લેવા**.
