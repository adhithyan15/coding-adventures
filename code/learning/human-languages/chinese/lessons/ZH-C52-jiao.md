## You'll want to know: jiǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C52-BODY52-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎo* — "foot".

> *wǒ de jiǎo hěn lěng* — My feet are cold.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kjak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as the part that tucks back when you sit; whether that meant the leg or the foot is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
