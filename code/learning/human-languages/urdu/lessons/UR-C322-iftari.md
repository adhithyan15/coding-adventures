## You'll want to know: افطاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C322-ROZA322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**افطاری** — *iftārī* — "the food that breaks a fast".

> سب نے مل کر افطاری کی۔
>
> *sab ne mil kar iftārī kī.*
>
> Everyone broke the fast together.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افطاری** (*iftārī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ifṭār*, "a breaking of the fast", with the ending *-ī*: the dates, fruit and fried food set out at sunset.
