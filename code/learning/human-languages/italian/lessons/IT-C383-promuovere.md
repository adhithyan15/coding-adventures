## You'll want to know: promuovere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C383-BOCCIATURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**promuovere** — "to pass (a student); to promote".

> Se mi promuovono, a settembre vado in quinta.
>
> *If they pass me, in September I go into the final year.*

Conjugated like *muovere*; past participle *promosso*. Also to promote a product or a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*promuovere* comes from Latin *prōmovēre* "to move forward, to advance" (Lewis & Short), built from *prō* "forward" and *movēre* "to move".
