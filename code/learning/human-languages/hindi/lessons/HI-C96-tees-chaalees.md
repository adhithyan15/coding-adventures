# तीस चालीस — two rungs past where the counting stopped

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C22-GYARAH-BEES-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Count from **ग्यारह** to **बीस**. Then stop, because that is where
your counting has ended until now.

## You'll want to know: तीस, चालीस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C96-TENS-01, HI-LEX-C96-TENS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तीस** (*tīs*) — thirty.

**चालीस** (*cālīs*) — forty.

## Grammar Lens: the tens are the part worth learning
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C96-TENS-01, HI-LEX-C96-TENS-02] -->

| number | word |
|---|---|
| 20 | बीस |
| 30 | तीस |
| 40 | चालीस |

Say those three in a row and the family resemblance is audible: **–ीस** at the
end of all of them. It is the same ending, worn down from the same Sanskrit
word for ten, and it will carry you as far as fifty.

**तीन** is three and **तीस** is thirty. **चार** is four and **चालीस** is forty.
The openings are not identical to the small numbers, but they are close enough
to hold on to, and that is the most help Hindi gives with numbers at this size.
