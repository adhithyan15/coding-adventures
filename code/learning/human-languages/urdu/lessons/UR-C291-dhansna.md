## You'll want to know: دھنسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C291-ZALZALA291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دھنسنا** — *dhansnā* — "to cave in; to sink in".

> سڑک بیچ میں دھنس گئی۔
>
> *saṛak bīch meṅ dhans gaī.*
>
> The road caved in in the middle.

Past (it …): وہ سڑک دھنس گئی, *voh saṛak dhans gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھنسنا** (*dhansnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dhãsnā*, "to sink into", under Sanskrit *dhváṃsati* (CDIAL 6896).
