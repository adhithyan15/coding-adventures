## You'll want to know: la narine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C478-VISAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la narine** — "nostril".

> Le cheval souffle par les narines.
>
> *The horse is snorting through its nostrils.*

Usually plural: *les narines*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la narine* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *nārīna* (EtymDB), built on Latin *nāris* "nostril" (Lewis & Short).
