## You'll want to know: げんき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C135-GENKI]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-N-01, JA-SCRIPT-KI-01] -->

**げんき** — *genki* — "well, healthy; in good spirits".

The new sign is spent at once. **げ**, **ん** and **き**: three beats,
*ge–n–ki*. Asking after someone, you will hear *o-genki desu ka?*, "are you
well?", and the answer *genki desu*, "I am well". You write every sign in it
now.
