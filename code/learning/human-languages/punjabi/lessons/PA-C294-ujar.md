## You'll want to know: ਉਜਾੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C294-POOR294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਜਾੜ** — *ujāṛ* — "desolate; deserted".

> ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਇਹ ਸੜਕ ਉਜਾੜ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *rāt nū̃ eh saṛak ujāṛ hundī hai.*
>
> At night this road is deserted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਜਾੜ** (*ujāṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ujjaṭa*, "uprooted, laid waste"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ujāṛ*, "waste, desolate", under it (CDIAL 1660b).
