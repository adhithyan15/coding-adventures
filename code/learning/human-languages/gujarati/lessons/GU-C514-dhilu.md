# ઢીલું (ḍhīlũ) — loose

## You'll want to know: ઢીલું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C514-DHILU514-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢીલું** — *ḍhīlũ* — "loose".

> આ ખુરશીનો એક પગ ઢીલો છે.
>
> *ā khurśīno ek pag ḍhīlo che.*
>
> One leg of this chair is loose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢીલું** (*ḍhīlũ*) comes from *\*ḍhila*, "slack", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "loose, slack" (CDIAL 5590). It agrees with its noun: **પગ ઢીલો**, **ગાંઠ ઢીલી**.
