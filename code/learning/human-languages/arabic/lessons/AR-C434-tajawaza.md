## You'll want to know: تجاوز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C434-RUKHSA434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تجاوز** — *tajāwaza* — "to overtake; to exceed".

> تجاوزت سيارة الحافلة على الجسر.
>
> *tajāwazat sayyāra al-ḥāfila ʿalā al-jisr.*
>
> A car overtook the bus on the bridge.

Present: يتجاوز, *yatajāwaz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تجاوز** (*tajāwaza*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-w-z*, "to pass beyond" (Lane). It is Form VI; *jawāz*, "a passport", is a permit to pass.
