## You'll want to know: ભાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C287-PAGLU287-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાળ** — *bhāḷ* — "a trace; whereabouts".

> પોલીસ ચોરની ભાળ કાઢતી હતી.
>
> *polīs cornī bhāḷ kāḍhtī hatī.*
>
> The police were trying to trace the thief.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાળ** (*bhāḷ*) comes from *\*bhāla*, "observation", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists Gujarati *bhāḷ*, "search, investigation" (CDIAL 9473). It is the trail that leads to someone: **ભાળ કાઢવી**, to trace a person.
