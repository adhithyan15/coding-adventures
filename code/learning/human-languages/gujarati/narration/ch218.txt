Gujarati, chapter 218: Sunrise and sunset.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ઊગવું (ūgvũ) — to rise, to sprout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for whirlwind, then the Gujarati for to spread.

You'll want to know: ઊગવું.
ઊગવું — ūgvũ — "to rise, to sprout".
કાલે સૂરજ વહેલો ઊગશે. — kāle sūraj vahelo ūgśe — "The sun will rise early tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
ઊગવું (ūgvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from udgāti, "rises, comes forth", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to rise, come out, grow" (CDIAL 1954).

Lesson 2 of 6.
સૂર્યોદય (sūryoday) — sunrise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spread, then the Gujarati for to rise.

You'll want to know: સૂર્યોદય.
સૂર્યોદય — sūryoday — "sunrise".
આવતીકાલે સૂર્યોદય વહેલો થશે. — āvtīkāle sūryoday vahelo thaśe — "Sunrise will be early tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
સૂર્યોદય (sūryoday) is Sanskrit sūryodaya, "sunrise", built from sūrya, "sun", and udaya, "rising", and taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13574, 1931).

Warm-up.

આથમવું — āthamvũ — "to set".
સાંજે સૂરજ મોડો આથમશે. — sā̃je sūraj moḍo āthamśe — "The sun will set late in the evening."

આથમવું (āthamvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from the Sanskrit phrase astam eti, "goes home, sets (of the sun)", built on asta, "home" (CDIAL 976).

Lesson 5 of 6.
અજવાળું (ajvāḷũ) — daylight, light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to set, then the Gujarati for sunset.

You'll want to know: અજવાળું.
અજવાળું — ajvāḷũ — "daylight, light".
પરોઢે અજવાળું થશે. — paroḍhe ajvāḷũ thaśe — "It will get light at dawn."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
અજવાળું (ajvāḷũ) is usually linked to Sanskrit ujjvala, "blazing up, bright". CDIAL lists the related Gujarati ujḷũ, "bright", there, but not ajvāḷũ itself, so the exact path is not settled (CDIAL 1670).
