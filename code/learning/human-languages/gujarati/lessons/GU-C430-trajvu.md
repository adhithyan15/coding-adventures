## You'll want to know: ત્રાજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C430-SCALES430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્રાજવું** — *trājvũ* — "a pair of scales".

> દુકાનદાર ત્રાજવામાં ખાંડ તોલે છે.
>
> *dukāndār trājvāmā̃ khā̃ḍ tole che.*
>
> The shopkeeper weighs sugar on the scales.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ત્રાજવું** (*trājvũ*) is usually traced to Persian *tarāzū*, "a balance, a pair of scales".
