## You'll want to know: આફરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C477-ODKAR477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**આફરો** — *āphro* — "bloating".

> વધારે ભોજન પછી પેટમાં આફરો ચઢ્યો.
>
> *vadhāre bhojan pachī peṭmā̃ āphro caḍhyo.*
>
> After the big meal my stomach felt bloated.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આફરો** (*āphro*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*āsphara-*, "swelling"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "wind in belly" (CDIAL 1526).

## What you've built
