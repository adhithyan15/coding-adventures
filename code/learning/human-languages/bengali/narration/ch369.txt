Lesson 1 of 6.
কিনা (kinā) — whether (or not).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for as long as, then the Bengali for or else.

You'll want to know: কিনা.
কিনা — kinā — "whether (or not)".
অরুণ আসবে কিনা জানি না; এলে আমরা একসঙ্গে খাব। ôrun āsbe kinā jāni nā; ele āmrā eksôngge khābô. I don't know whether Arun will come; if he does, we'll eat together.

The word, taken apart.
কিনা (kinā) is কি, 'what, whether' (CDIAL 3164 lists Bengali ki), with না, 'not': 'whether or not'. It comes after the verb it questions: আসবে কিনা.

Lesson 2 of 6.
এমনকি (emônki) — even.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for or else, then the Bengali for whether.

You'll want to know: এমনকি.
এমনকি — emônki — "even".
ঝড় হলে এমনকি বাসও বন্ধ থাকে। jhôṛ hôle emônki bāso bôndhô thāke. When there is a storm, even the buses stop running.

The word, taken apart.
এমনকি (emônki) is এমন, 'such, so', with কি, 'what' (CDIAL 3164 lists Bengali ki): 'is it even so?' The source of এমন is not settled. The word marks the most surprising member of a list.

Lesson 3 of 6.
অন্যথায় (ônyôthāy) — failing that; otherwise (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for whether, then the Bengali for even.

You'll want to know: অন্যথায়.
অন্যথায় — ônyôthāy — "failing that; otherwise (formal)".
যদি সময় থাকে, তাহলে আজ এসো, অন্যথায় কাল এসো। jôdi sômôy thāke, tāhôle āj eso, ônyôthāy kāl eso. If you have time, come today; failing that, come tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
অন্যথায় (ônyôthāy) is Sanskrit anyathā, otherwise (Monier-Williams), with the Bengali ending -য়. It is the formal partner of নাহলে, common on notices and in letters.

Lesson 4 of 6.
সত্ত্বেও (sôttveo) — in spite of; despite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for even, then the Bengali for failing that.

You'll want to know: সত্ত্বেও.
সত্ত্বেও — sôttveo — "in spite of; despite".
শীত সত্ত্বেও খেলা হবে, কিন্তু ঝড় হলে বন্ধ হবে। shīt sôttveo khælā hôbe, kintu jhôṛ hôle bôndhô hôbe. The match will go ahead despite the cold, but if there is a storm it will be called off.

The word, taken apart.
সত্ত্বেও (sôttveo) is Sanskrit sattva, being or existence (Monier-Williams), with the Bengali endings -এ and -ও: 'even with its being so'. The sense 'in spite of' is not one the dictionary gives; the word follows its noun: শীত সত্ত্বেও.

Lesson 5 of 6.
নির্ধারণ করা (nirdhārôn kôrā) — to determine; to fix (a date, a price).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for failing that, then the Bengali for in spite of.

You'll want to know: নির্ধারণ করা.
নির্ধারণ করা — nirdhārôn kôrā — "to determine; to fix (a date, a price)".
আবহাওয়া ভালো থাকলে আমরা কাল খেলার তারিখ নির্ধারণ করব। ābôhāoyā bhālo thākle āmrā kāl khelār tārikh nirdhārôn kôrbô. If the weather stays fine, we'll fix the date of the match tomorrow.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দামটা নির্ধারণ করলেন, tini dāmṭā nirdhārôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
নির্ধারণ করা (nirdhārôn kôrā) is Sanskrit nirdhāraṇa, settling or ascertaining (Monier-Williams), with করা: to fix a date, a price or a rule.
