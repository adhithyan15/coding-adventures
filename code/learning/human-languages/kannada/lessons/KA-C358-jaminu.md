# ಜಾಮೀನು (jāmīnu) — bail

## You'll want to know: ಜಾಮೀನು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C358-VICHARANE358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಮೀನು** — *jāmīnu* — "bail".

> ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ ಆರೋಪಿಗೆ ಜಾಮೀನು ಕೊಟ್ಟಿತು.
>
> *nyāyālaya ārōpige jāmīnu koṭṭitu.*
>
> The court granted bail to the accused.

Bail is "given": ಜಾಮೀನು ಕೊಡು. One out on bail is ಜಾಮೀನಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಹೊರಗೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಮೀನು** (*jāmīnu*) is a loan from Persian *zāmin*, "a surety", a word of Arabic origin, which came through Hindustani.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
