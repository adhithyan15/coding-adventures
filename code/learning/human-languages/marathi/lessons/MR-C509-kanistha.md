## You'll want to know: कनिष्ठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C509-BADHATI509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कनिष्ठ** — *kaniṣṭha* — "junior".

> कनिष्ठ कर्मचारी वरिष्ठांकडून काम शिकतात.
>
> *kaniṣṭha karmacārī variṣṭhāṅkaḍūn kām śiktāt.*
>
> Junior staff learn the work from their seniors.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कनिष्ठ** (*kaniṣṭha*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kaniṣṭha*, "youngest, least" (Monier-Williams).
