## You'll want to know: खचणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C293-BHUKAMP293-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खचणे** — *khacṇe* — "to cave in; to sink down".

> पावसामुळे गावातला रस्ता खचला.
>
> *pāvsāmuḷe gāvātlā rastā khaclā.*
>
> Because of the rain, the village road caved in.

Past (he …): तो खचला, *to khaclā*.

Also of courage: तो मनाने खचला, he lost heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खचणे** (*khacṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *khacṇe*, 'to sink down', under *\*khacc*, 'sink down or back' (CDIAL 3764).
