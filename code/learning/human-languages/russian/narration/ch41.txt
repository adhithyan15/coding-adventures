Russian, chapter 41: Listen, Learn, Answer, Wait, Give.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
слушать (slúshat) — to listen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to run, then the Russian for to play.

You'll want to know: слушать.
слушать (slúshat) — "to listen".

The word, taken apart.
слушать (slúshat) from Proto-Slavic slušati, built on слух, "hearing" — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian klausýti, "to listen", and Old High German hlosēn, "to listen"; English listen is a cousin, from PIE ḱlew-, "to hear".

Lesson 2 of 5.
учить (uchít) — to learn; to teach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to play, then the Russian for to listen.

You'll want to know: учить.
учить (uchít) — "to learn; to teach". The same root as учитель.

The word, taken apart.
учить (uchít) from Proto-Slavic učiti — inherited, first "to accustom". Vasmer links it with привыкнуть, "to grow accustomed", Lithuanian jaukìnti, "to tame", and Sanskrit úcyati, "is accustomed": to teach was to accustom someone to a thing.

Lesson 3 of 5.
отвечать (otvechát) — to answer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to listen, then the Russian for to learn; to teach.

You'll want to know: отвечать.
отвечать (otvechát) — "to answer".

The word, taken apart.
отвечать (otvechát) goes with ответ, "an answer": от-, "back", with an old root вет-, "counsel, word". Old Russian větŭ meant "counsel, agreement", and the same root is in привет, "hi", and совет, "advice" (Vasmer).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to learn; to teach, then the Russian for to answer.

You'll want to know: ждать.
ждать (zhdat) — "to wait".

The word, taken apart.
ждать (zhdat) from Proto-Slavic žĭdati — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian geĩsti, "to long for", and Old Prussian gēide, "they wait": to wait was to long for something.

Lesson 5 of 5.
давать (davát) — to give.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to answer, then the Russian for to wait.

You'll want to know: давать.
давать (davát) — "to give".

The word, taken apart.
давать (davát) is the ongoing partner of дать, "to give", built on the same root (Vasmer). Vasmer relates дать to Lithuanian dúoti, Greek didōmi and Sanskrit dadāti, all "to give", from PIE deh₃-, "to give".
