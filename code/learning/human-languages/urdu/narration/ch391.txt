Urdu, chapter 391: Bookings and Queues.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بکنگ (buking) — a booking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for cancelled, then the Urdu for to take down.

You'll want to know: بکنگ.
بکنگ — buking — "a booking".
میں نے کل ٹکٹ کی بکنگ کی۔ maiṅ ne kal ṭikaṭ kī buking kī. I booked the ticket yesterday.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بکنگ (buking) is borrowed from English booking.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قطار (qatār) — a queue; a row.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to take down, then the Urdu for a booking.

You'll want to know: قطار.
قطار — qatār — "a queue; a row".
ٹکٹ کے لیے لمبی قطار تھی۔ ṭikaṭ ke liye lambī qatār thī. There was a long queue for tickets.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qiṭār, "a line of camels"; in Urdu any line of people waiting is a قطار (qatār).

Lesson 4 of 5.
رش (rash) — a rush (of people); a crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a queue, then the Urdu for a counter.

You'll want to know: رش.
رش — rash — "a rush (of people); a crowd".
صبح کے وقت اسٹیشن پر رش ہوتا ہے۔ subah ke vaqt isṭeshan par rash hotā hai. The station is very crowded in the mornings.
Masculine.
Everyday spoken Urdu; news reports and notices write ہجوم (hujūm), "a crowd".

The word, taken apart.
رش (rash) is borrowed from English rush; in Urdu it names the crowd itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
گھسنا (ghusnā) — to push in; to squeeze in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a counter, then the Urdu for a rush (of people).

You'll want to know: گھسنا.
گھسنا — ghusnā — "to push in; to squeeze in".
ایک آدمی قطار میں آگے گھس گیا۔ ek ādmī qatār meṅ āge ghus gayā. A man pushed in near the front of the queue.
Past (he …): وہ ڈبے میں گھس گیا, voh ḍabbe meṅ ghus gayā.

The word, taken apart.
گھسنا (ghusnā) is inherited from ghuss, "thrust in", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4492).
