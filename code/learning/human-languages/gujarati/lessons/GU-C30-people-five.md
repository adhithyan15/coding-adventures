Keep two scores. **Listening** goes from sound to meaning; **speaking** goes from
meaning to sound. A strong score in one cannot cover a miss in the other. The
third row is not new material — it is two older bands at once: the outdoor words of the previous chapter at their second spacing window, and the food words two chapters before that at their third.
