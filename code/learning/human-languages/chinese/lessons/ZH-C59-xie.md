## You'll want to know: xié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C59-WEAR59-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xié* — "shoe".

> *wǒ de jiǎo hěn dà xié hěn xiǎo* — My feet are big and the shoes are small.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xié* is inherited from Middle Chinese *heaj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'shoe'.
