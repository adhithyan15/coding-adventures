## You'll want to know: వియ్యంకుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C530-BANDHUVU530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వియ్యంకుడు** — *viyyaṅkuḍu* — "a co-father-in-law; one's child's father-in-law".

> పెళ్ళి తరువాత నాన్న, వియ్యంకుడు మంచి స్నేహితులు అయ్యారు.
>
> *peḷḷi taruvāta nānna, viyyaṅkuḍu mañci snēhitulu ayyāru.*
>
> After the wedding, dad and his co-father-in-law became good friends.

The fathers of a married couple are each other's వియ్యంకుడు; the mothers are వియ్యపురాలు. వియ్యం is the bond between the two families.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వియ్యంకుడు** (*viyyaṅkuḍu*) is listed in DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 58) beside *viyyamu*, "a marriage alliance"; the family goes back to Sanskrit *vivāha*, "marriage" (CDIAL 11920).
