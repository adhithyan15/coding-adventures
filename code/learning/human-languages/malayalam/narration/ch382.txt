Malayalam, chapter 382: A Riot in the Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കലാപം (kalāpaṁ) — a riot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to whisper, then the Malayalam for to disappear.

You'll want to know: കലാപം.
കലാപം — kalāpaṁ — "a riot".
കലാപം കാരണം കടകൾ അടച്ചു. kalāpaṁ kāraṇaṁ kaṭakaḷ aṭaccu. Shops closed because of the riot.
Also an uproar at a meeting.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of കലാപം (kalāpaṁ) in the sense of a riot is not settled. In the news it is violent disorder in a town.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ആയുധം (āyudhaṁ) — a weapon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to disappear, then the Malayalam for a riot.

You'll want to know: ആയുധം.
ആയുധം — āyudhaṁ — "a weapon".
പോലീസ് കള്ളന്റെ കയ്യിൽ നിന്ന് ആയുധം പിടിച്ചെടുത്തു. pōlīsŭ kaḷḷanṟe kayyil ninnŭ āyudhaṁ piṭicceṭuttu. The police seized a weapon from the robber's hand.
Plural: ആയുധങ്ങൾ, āyudhaṅṅaḷ.
A tool is ഉപകരണം; a weapon is ആയുധം (āyudhaṁ).

The word, taken apart.
ആയുധം (āyudhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit āyudha, "a weapon" (CDIAL 1291), from the root yudh, "to fight", the root of യുദ്ധം (yuddhaṁ).

Lesson 3 of 5.
വളയുക (vaḷayuka) — to surround.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a riot, then the Malayalam for a weapon.

You'll want to know: വളയുക.
വളയുക — vaḷayuka — "to surround".
പോലീസ് ആ വീട് വളഞ്ഞു. pōlīsŭ ā vīṭŭ vaḷaññu. The police surrounded the house.
Past: വളഞ്ഞു, vaḷaññu.
The same form also means to bend, as in വളവ്, a bend.

The word, taken apart.
വളയുക (vaḷayuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5313 lists Malayalam vaḷayuka, "to surround", with വള, "a ring, a bangle": to make a ring round something.

You'll want to know: തുരത്തുക.
തുരത്തുക — turattuka — "to drive away; to chase off".
നാട്ടുകാർ ആനയെ കാട്ടിലേക്ക് തുരത്തി. nāṭṭukār ānaye kāṭṭilēkkŭ turatti. The locals drove the elephant back into the forest.
Past: തുരത്തി, turatti.
Of animals and of enemies.

The word, taken apart.
തുരത്തുക (turattuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3340 lists Malayalam turattuka, "to drive away", beside Tamil tura, "to drive, to beat away".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ശാന്തമാകുക (śāntamākuka) — to calm down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to surround, then the Malayalam for to drive away.

You'll want to know: ശാന്തമാകുക.
ശാന്തമാകുക — śāntamākuka — "to calm down".
കുറച്ച് കഴിഞ്ഞപ്പോൾ നഗരം ശാന്തമായി. kuṟaccŭ kaḻiññappōḷ nagaraṁ śāntamāyi. After a while the city calmed down.
Past: ശാന്തമായി, śāntamāyi.
ശാന്തം, calm, + ആകുക, to become.

The word, taken apart.
ശാന്തമാകുക (śāntamākuka) is built from Sanskrit śānta, "abated, calm" (CDIAL 12390), borrowed as ശാന്തം, and the Malayalam ആകുക, "to become".
