## You'll want to know: reclamar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C434-RECLAMACAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**reclamar** — "to complain; to claim".

> Reclamámos do barulho, mas ninguém fez nada.
>
> *We complained about the noise, but nobody did anything.*

*Reclamar de* — to complain about.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reclamar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *reclāmāre* 'to cry out against' (EtymDB), built on *clāmāre* 'to shout'.
