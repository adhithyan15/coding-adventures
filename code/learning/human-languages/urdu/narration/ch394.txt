Urdu, chapter 394: Potholes and Jolts.
5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گڑھا (gaṛhā) — a pothole; a pit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to edge forward, then the Urdu for to have (something) stopped.

You'll want to know: گڑھا.
گڑھا — gaṛhā — "a pothole; a pit".
اس سڑک میں بڑا گڑھا ہے۔ is saṛak meṅ baṛā gaṛhā hai. There is a big pothole in this road.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
گڑھا (gaṛhā) probably belongs with the family CDIAL files under gaḍḍa, "a hole, a pit", a form it reconstructs: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gāṛā, "a hole", there (CDIAL 3981), but not this form with ṛh.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ڈرائیور (ḍrāivar) — a driver.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (something) stopped, then the Urdu for a pothole.

You'll want to know: ڈرائیور.

ڈرائیور نے گڑھا نہیں دیکھا۔ ḍrāivar ne gaṛhā nahīṅ dekhā. The driver didn't see the pothole.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ڈرائیور (ḍrāivar) is borrowed from English driver.

Lesson 3 of 5.
دھکا (dhakkā) — a shove; a jolt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a pothole, then the Urdu for a driver.

You'll want to know: دھکا.
دھکا — dhakkā — "a shove; a jolt".
گڑھے کی وجہ سے بس کو دھکا لگا۔ gaṛhe kī vajah se bas ko dhakkā lagā. The bus gave a jolt because of the pothole.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
دھکا (dhakkā) is inherited from dhakk, "push, strike", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6701); دھکیلنا, "to push", comes from the same source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
اچھلنا (uchhalnā) — to bounce; to jump up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a driver, then the Urdu for a shove.

You'll want to know: اچھلنا.
اچھلنا — uchhalnā — "to bounce; to jump up".
دھکے سے سب مسافر سیٹوں پر اچھلے۔ dhakke se sab musāfir sīṭoṅ par uchhle. With the jolt, all the passengers bounced in their seats.
Past (he …): وہ سیٹ پر اچھلا, voh sīṭ par uchhlā.

The word, taken apart.
اچھلنا (uchhalnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin uchalnā, "to leap", under ut-śalati, "springs up", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1843).

رات کو یہ سڑک ویران ہوتی ہے۔ rāt ko yih saṛak vīrān hotī hai. At night this road is deserted.

The word, taken apart.
is usually traced to Persian vīrān, "ruined, laid waste"; in Urdu an empty road or an abandoned house is ویران.
