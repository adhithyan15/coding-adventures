## You'll want to know: ಸಂಶಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C498-NAMBIKE498-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಶಯ** — *saṃśaya* — "suspicion; doubt".

> ಆ ಅಪರಿಚಿತನ ಮೇಲೆ ಪೊಲೀಸರಿಗೆ ಸಂಶಯ ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *ā aparicitana mēle polīsarige saṃśaya banditu.*
>
> The police grew suspicious of the stranger.

On whom: … ಮೇಲೆ ಸಂಶಯ. Close to ಅನುಮಾನ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಶಯ** (*saṃśaya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃśaya*, "doubt, uncertainty" (Monier-Williams).
