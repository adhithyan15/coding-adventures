To defend? (dēfendō, dēfendere.) To die? (morior, morī.) To marry? (nūbō, nūbere.) To rest? (quiēscō, quiēscere.) To compare; to get ready? (comparō, comparāre.) To persuade? (persuādeō, persuādēre.) To blame? (reprehendō, reprehendere.) To share? (partior, partīrī.) To avoid? (vītō, vītāre.) To lift? (tollō, tollere.) To reach? (perveniō, pervenīre.) To recognise? (agnōscō, agnōscere.) To refuse? (recūsō, recūsāre.) To fly about? (volitō, volitāre.)
