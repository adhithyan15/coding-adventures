## You'll want to know: solitario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C709-SOLEDAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**solitario** — "lonely, solitary".

> Es un hombre solitario.
>
> *He is a solitary man.*

Also a card game you play alone: *hacer un solitario*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solitario* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sōlitārius* "lonely" (EtymDB); the same Latin word, inherited, gave *soltero*.
