## You'll want to know: zoppicare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C484-GESSO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**zoppicare** — "to limp".

> Dopo la partita zoppicava un po'.
>
> *After the match he was limping a little.*

Built on *zoppo* "lame".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zoppicare* is built on *zoppo* "lame" (EtymDB). The origin of *zoppo* is not settled.
