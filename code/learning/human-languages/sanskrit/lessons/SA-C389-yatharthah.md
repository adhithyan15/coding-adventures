## You'll want to know: यथार्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C389-SHAKYA389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**यथार्थः** — *yathārthaḥ* — "true to fact; real".

> यदि वार्ता यथार्था, तर्हि सः श्वः गमिष्यति।
>
> *yadi vārtā yathārthā, tarhi saḥ śvaḥ gamiṣyati.*
>
> If the news is true, he will go tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यथार्थः** (*yathārthaḥ*) is built from *yathā*, "as", and *artha*, "meaning"; MW gives *yathārtha* "accordant with reality, conformable to truth, true, genuine" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
