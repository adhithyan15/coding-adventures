## You'll want to know: il comunicato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C285-COMUNICATO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il comunicato** — "the press release, the official bulletin".

> L'azienda ha mandato un comunicato ai giornali.
>
> *The company sent a press release to the papers.*

*Un comunicato stampa* — a press release.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comunicato* is the past participle of *comunicare* "to make known", a learned borrowing from Latin *commūnicāre* "to share", built on *commūnis* "common" (Lewis & Short).
