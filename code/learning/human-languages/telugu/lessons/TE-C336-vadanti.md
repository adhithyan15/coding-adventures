# వదంతి (vadanti) — hearsay; a rumour

## You'll want to know: వదంతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C336-PUKARU336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వదంతి** — *vadanti* — "hearsay; a rumour".

> అది నిజం కాదు, వదంతి మాత్రమే.
>
> *adi nijaṁ kādu, vadanti mātramē.*
>
> It is not true, only hearsay.

Close to పుకారు; news writers prefer వదంతి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వదంతి** (*vadanti*) goes back to Sanskrit *kiṃvadantī*, "a common saying, a rumour", literally "what do they say" (Monier-Williams), which Monier-Williams also lists in the short form *vadantī*.
