## You'll want to know: しょうかき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C353-TENKEN353-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうかき** — *shōkaki* — "a fire extinguisher".

> **ひが ちいさかったら、しょうかきを つかって ください。** — *hi ga chiisakattara, shōkaki o tsukatte kudasai* — "If the fire is small, use the fire extinguisher."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうかき** (*shōkaki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "extinguish" (Middle Chinese *sjew*), *ka*, "fire" (*xwaX*) and *ki*, "utensil" (*khijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
