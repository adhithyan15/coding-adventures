## You'll want to know: l'intenzione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C378-SCOPO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'intenzione** — "the intention".

> Non ho intenzione di cambiare lavoro.
>
> *I have no intention of changing jobs.*

*Avere intenzione di* + infinitive — to intend to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*intenzione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *intentiō* "a stretching, an effort, a purpose" (Lewis & Short), from *intendere* "to stretch towards", built on *tendere* "to stretch".
