## You'll want to know: परोसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C524-PAROSNA524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**परोसना** — *parosnā* — "to serve (food)".

> माँ ने मेहमानों को गरम खाना परोसा।
>
> *Māṁ ne mehmānoṁ ko garam khānā parosā.*
>
> Mother served the guests hot food.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दाल परोसी, *usne dāl parosī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परोसना** (*parosnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *parivēṣati*, "serves food" (CDIAL 7888).
