Lesson 1 of 5.
छिद्रम् (chidram) — a hole.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sets spinning, then the Sanskrit for a wheel track.

You'll want to know: छिद्रम्.
छिद्रम् — chidram — "a hole".
मम स्यूते छिद्रम् अस्ति। mama syūte chidram asti. There is a hole in my bag.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
छिद्रम् (chidram) comes from the root √chid, "to cut, to split", which also gives chinatti, "cuts"; MW gives chidra as torn, pierced and, as a noun, a hole (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
क्षरति (kṣarati) — leaks; trickles out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a wheel track, then the Sanskrit for a hole.

You'll want to know: क्षरति.
क्षरति — kṣarati — "leaks; trickles out".
छिद्रात् तैलं क्षरति। chidrāt tailaṃ kṣarati. Oil leaks from the hole.
Past (it …): तैलं छिद्रात् क्षरितवत्, tailaṃ chidrāt kṣaritavat.

The word, taken apart.
क्षरति (kṣarati) comes from the root √kṣar, "to flow, to stream"; MW gives kṣarati as to flow, trickle or drip, and KRM gives the participle kṣarita (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
शिथिलः (śithilaḥ) — loose; slack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a hole, then the Sanskrit for leaks.

You'll want to know: शिथिलः.
शिथिलः — śithilaḥ — "loose; slack".
अक्षस्य कीलः शिथिलः अस्ति। akṣasya kīlaḥ śithilaḥ asti. The axle's pin is loose.

The word, taken apart.
शिथिलः (śithilaḥ) comes from the root √śrath, "to loosen", by way of śithira, "loose", of which MW gives it as a collateral form; MW gives śithila as loose, slack, untied (MW).

विदीर्णः — vidīrṇaḥ — "split; burst open".
शकटस्य फलकं विदीर्णम् अस्ति। śakaṭasya phalakaṃ vidīrṇam asti. The cart's plank is split.

The word, taken apart.
विदीर्णः (vidīrṇaḥ) is built from vi, "apart", and the root √dṝ, "to burst, to split"; MW gives vidīrṇa as rent asunder, split, burst open (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
घर्षति (gharṣati) — rubs against; scrapes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for loose, then the Sanskrit for split open.

You'll want to know: घर्षति.
घर्षति — gharṣati — "rubs against; scrapes".
चक्रं भित्तिं घर्षति। cakraṃ bhittiṃ gharṣati. The wheel scrapes the wall.
Past (it …): चक्रं भित्तिं घृष्टवत्, cakraṃ bhittiṃ ghṛṣṭavat.

The word, taken apart.
घर्षति (gharṣati) comes from the root √ghṛṣ, "to rub, to grind"; MW gives the participle ghṛṣṭa, "rubbed, grazed"; it lives on as Hindi ghisnā, "to rub" (MW; CDIAL 4450).
