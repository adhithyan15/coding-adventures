## You'll want to know: कौल देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C356-LOKSHAHI356-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कौल देणे** — *kaul deṇe* — "to give a mandate; to give a verdict".

> लोकांनी तरुण उमेदवाराला कौल दिला.
>
> *lokānnī taruṇ umedvārālā kaul dilā.*
>
> The people gave their mandate to the young candidate.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कौल दिला, *tyāne kaul dilā*.

The one chosen takes -ला: उमेदवाराला कौल देणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कौल देणे** (*kaul deṇe*) joins कौल, "a verdict, a word given", a loan usually traced to Arabic *qaul*, "saying, word", through Persian, to देणे, "to give".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
