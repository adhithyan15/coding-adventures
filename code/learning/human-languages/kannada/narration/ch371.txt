Kannada, chapter 371: The Chieftain Comes Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತಾಳಿ (tāḷi) — a wedding pendant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a listener, then the Kannada for notorious.

You'll want to know: ತಾಳಿ.
ತಾಳಿ — tāḷi — "a wedding pendant".
ಮದುವೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಗಂಡು ಹೆಣ್ಣಿಗೆ ತಾಳಿ ಕಟ್ಟಿದನು. maduveyalli gaṇḍu heṇṇige tāḷi kaṭṭidanu. At the wedding the groom tied the wedding pendant on the bride.
The gold pendant on a cord tied at a Hindu wedding; ತಾಳಿ (tāḷi) ಕಟ್ಟು, to tie it, means to marry.

The word, taken apart.
ತಾಳಿ (tāḷi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3175 lists Kannada tāli, tāḷi, "a small round plate of gold worn at the neck as a marriage badge", beside Tamil tāli.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Kannada for notorious, then the Kannada for a wedding pendant.

You'll want to know: ಪಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿ.
ಪಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿ — pallakki — "a palanquin".
ರಾಣಿ ಪಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋದಳು. rāṇi pallakkiyalli dēvasthānakke hōdaḷu. The queen went to the temple in a palanquin.
Plural: ಪಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿಗಳು, pallakkigaḷu.
A covered seat carried on poles by bearers.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿ (pallakki) is usually traced to Sanskrit palyaṅka, paryaṅka, "a bed, a couch, a litter" (CDIAL 7964).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪಾಳೆಗಾರ (pāḷegāra) — a chieftain; a local lord.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a wedding pendant, then the Kannada for a palanquin.

You'll want to know: ಪಾಳೆಗಾರ.
ಪಾಳೆಗಾರ — pāḷegāra — "a chieftain; a local lord".
ಆ ಕೋಟೆಯನ್ನು ಒಬ್ಬ ಪಾಳೆಗಾರ ಕಟ್ಟಿದನು. ā kōṭeyannu obba pāḷegāra kaṭṭidanu. A local chieftain built that fort.
Plural: ಪಾಳೆಗಾರರು, pāḷegāraru.
A lord of a small territory under a king; the English wrote "poligar".

The word, taken apart.
ಪಾಳೆಗಾರ (pāḷegāra) is native Dravidian: it is built on pāḷeya, "an army camp, a fief" (DEDR 4117), with the ending -gāra, "one who has, one who does".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಹಲು (mahalu) — a mansion; a palace hall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a palanquin, then the Kannada for a chieftain.

You'll want to know: ಮಹಲು.
ಮಹಲು — mahalu — "a mansion; a palace hall".
ಪಾಳೆಗಾರನ ಮಹಲು ಈಗ ಒಂದು ವಸ್ತುಸಂಗ್ರಹಾಲಯ. pāḷegārana mahalu īga ondu vastusaṅgrahālaya. The chieftain's mansion is now a museum.
Plural: ಮಹಲುಗಳು, mahalugaḷu.
A large grand building; also a storey: ಎರಡು ಮಹಲಿನ ಮನೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಹಲು (mahalu) is a loan from Arabic maḥall, "a place, a palace, a quarter", which came through Persian and Hindustani.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಾಲಿಡು (kāliḍu) — to set foot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a chieftain, then the Kannada for a mansion.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಲಿಡು.
ಕಾಲಿಡು — kāliḍu — "to set foot".
ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಅವನು ತನ್ನ ಊರಿಗೆ ಕಾಲಿಟ್ಟನು. hattu varṣada nantara avanu tanna ūrige kāliṭṭanu. After ten years he set foot in his home town.
Past (I …): ಕಾಲಿಟ್ಟೆ, kāliṭṭe.
Where one steps takes -ಗೆ. It also means to enter a new stage: ಹೊಸ ವರ್ಷಕ್ಕೆ ಕಾಲಿಡು (kāliḍu).

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಲಿಡು (kāliḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: kāl, "foot, leg" (DEDR 1479), and iḍu, "to put, to place" (DEDR 442).
