## You'll want to know: ચિઠ્ઠી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C152-READ152-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C148-YUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-OBJECT-AGREE-01, GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, GU-Q-KYAAN-01] -->

**ચિઠ્ઠી** (*ciṭṭhī*) — "a note, a short written message".

A notice is for everyone; a **ચિઠ્ઠી** is for one person: a line left under a
door, a message from a neighbour.

> **મેં તેને ચિઠ્ઠી લખી.** — *mẽ tene ciṭṭhī lakhī.* — I wrote her a note.
> **ચિઠ્ઠી ક્યાં છે?** — *ciṭṭhī kyā̃ che?* — Where is the note?

**લખી** ends in **-ી** because **ચિઠ્ઠી** is feminine. The **ઠ્ઠ** in the middle
is **ઠ** twice, joined by a virama: a doubled, breathy, tongue-curled *ṭṭh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચિઠ્ઠી** (*ciṭṭhī*) is inherited. CDIAL reconstructs an Indo-Aryan *ciṣṭa*,
"message", and lists Gujarati *ciṭṭhī*, "message", under it, beside Hindi *ciṭṭhī*,
"letter" (CDIAL 4832).

## What you've built

One more word for the notes people leave for each other.
