## You'll want to know: князь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C326-PREDOK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**князь** (*knyaz*) — "a prince of old Rus, a ruling prince".

> Князь жил в большом доме на холме.
>
> *Knyaz zhil v bolshóm dóme na kholmé.*
>
> The prince lived in a big house on a hill.

Plural *князья*. *Княгиня* — a princess, a prince's wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*князь* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *kŭnędzĭ*, an early borrowing from Proto-Germanic *kuningaz* "king" (EtymDB); English *king* is a cousin.
