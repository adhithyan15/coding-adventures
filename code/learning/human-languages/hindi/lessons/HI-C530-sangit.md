## You'll want to know: संगीत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C530-SANGEET530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संगीत** — *saṅgīt* — "music".

> मेरी बहन संगीत सीख रही है।
>
> *Merī bahan saṅgīt sīkh rahī hai.*
>
> My sister is learning music.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संगीत** (*saṅgīt*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *saṃgīta*, "sung together; music" (Monier-Williams).
