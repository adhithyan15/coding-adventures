## Chapters 44-89: 230 headwords, thirty-eight verbs, and Marwadi attains pre-A1

Marwadi's last pre-A1 gap was vocabulary: it was 224 headwords short.

**The vocabulary.** Forty-six chapters of five words each, and four reviews:
two after chapter 66 and two after chapter 89. Every word is chained into the
next two lessons. Verbs are taught in their Marwari infinitive in **-णो**
(**खाणो**, **देखणो**, **बोलणो**), and adjectives in their **-ो** form (**बड़ो**,
**आछो**, **जूनो**).

- **44-66**:
  - the body and health
  - the family on both sides
  - thirty-eight verbs
  - food and the home
- **67-89**:
  - school
  - nature and animals
  - describing words and colours
  - time and place
  - the town
  - clothes
  - the tens to a thousand

Every headword is written only with letters the reader has already written,
so words that need **ध**, **फ**, **झ**, **ढ**, **ऊ** or **ओ** wait: **दूध**,
**फूल**, **समझणो**, **पढणो**, **ऊंट**, **ओढणी** and twenty-four more. **कान**
was held back because earlier lesson text already uses it. Marwadi's
forward-reference count stays at 0.

Marwadi pins one objective activity per lesson, so each new lesson carries a
typed recall. A word lesson asks for its headword and accepts the romanization;
a review asks for the first word its Warm-up retrieves.
