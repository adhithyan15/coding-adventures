# ఆ తరువాత (ā taruvāta) — first, after that, in the end

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C64-JOIN-04, TE-LEX-C75-WHEN-04, TE-LEX-C317-ILOGA-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "after, later" (*taruvāta*) and "before" (*mundu*). Then the word that opened the last
lesson, "in the meantime". (*īlōgā*.) Which letter opens it? (**ఈ**, a long *ī*.)

## You'll want to know: మొదట … ఆ తరువాత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C317-FIRST-THEN-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C39-TII-01, TE-LEX-C103-LOC103-02, TE-LEX-C81-ORDINAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-SIMPLE-01] -->

> **మొదట నేను టీ తాగాను. ఆ తరువాత బడికి వెళ్ళాను.**
>
> *modaṭa nēnu ṭī tāgānu. ā taruvāta baḍiki veḷḷānu.* — "First I drank tea. After that I went to school."

**మొదట** (*modaṭa*, "first, at first") sits beside **మొదటి**, "first", which you know: **మొదటి** goes in front of
a noun, **మొదట** opens a sentence. **ఆ తరువాత** is **తరువాత** with **ఆ**, "that", in front: "after
that". After a time word **తరువాత** means "after, later":

> **రెండు రోజుల తరువాత అతను వచ్చాడు.**
>
> *reṇḍu rōjula taruvāta atanu vaccāḍu.* — "Two days later he came."

## You'll want to know: చివరికి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C317-CIVARIKI-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C253-SAMAYAM253-01, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-03, TE-LEX-C146-ACT146-01, TE-LEX-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-01] -->

> **చివరికి రాత్రి పది గంటలకు ఇంటికి చేరాను.**
>
> *civariki rātri padi gaṇṭalaku iṇṭiki cērānu.* — "In the end I reached home at ten at night."

**చివరికి** is **చివర**, "the end", with **-కి**, "to": "to the end", so "finally, in the end". A told
story often runs on three posts: **మొదట** … **ఆ తరువాత** … **చివరికి**.
