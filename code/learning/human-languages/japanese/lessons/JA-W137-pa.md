## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-PA-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-HA-01, JA-SCRIPT-HANDAKUTEN-01] -->

> **は** *ha* + **゜** → **ぱ** *pa*

Write **は** in full, in the order you learned it. Then add the small circle
of the handakuten at the upper right, where the voicing mark would go. Keep
it small and open, and do not let it touch the sign.

The circle works only on the *h* row, and it always makes a *p*: *ha*
becomes *pa*. You saw it on **ほ** for **ぽ**, in **いっぽん**.

With **いっ** in front and **い** after it, you can now write the word from the
last lesson: **いっぱい**, *ippai*, full.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-PA-01, JA-SCRIPT-HA-01, JA-SCRIPT-HANDAKUTEN-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-IPPAI] -->

1. Write **は**, then **ば**, then **ぱ**, and say *ha*, *ba*, *pa*.
2. Write **ほ ぼ ぽ** and **は ば ぱ** in rows, and say each one.
3. Write **いっぱい** from memory and say *ippai*.
