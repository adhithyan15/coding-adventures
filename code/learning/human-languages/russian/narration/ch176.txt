Russian, chapter 176: To come back, To be late, To hurry, To rest, To get tired.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
возвращаться (vozvrashchátsya) — to come back, to return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to switch off, then the Russian for to light.

You'll want to know: возвращаться.
возвращаться (vozvrashchátsya) — "to come back, to return".

The word, taken apart.
возвращаться (vozvrashchátsya) is воз-, "back", with вращать, "to turn", and -ся: to turn yourself back. вращать is the Church Slavonic shape of native ворочать (Vasmer), on the root of вертеть, "to turn".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: спешить.
спешить (speshít) — "to hurry".

The word, taken apart.
спешить (speshít) comes from спех, "haste" (Vasmer), the root of old spěti, "to succeed, to get on", as in успех, "success".
