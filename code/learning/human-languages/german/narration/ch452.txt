German, chapter 452: Bestellt und geliefert.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Verpackung — the packaging.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to haggle, then the German for the flea market.

You'll want to know: die Verpackung.
die Verpackung — "the packaging".
Die Verpackung ist größer als das Geschenk. The packaging is bigger than the present.
Plural: die Verpackungen.

The word, taken apart.
Verpackung is built on verpacken, from packen 'to pack', which was borrowed from Middle Low German packen (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Karton — the cardboard box; cardboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the packaging, then the German for to wrap up.

You'll want to know: der Karton.
der Karton — "the cardboard box; cardboard".
Für den Umzug brauchen wir viele Kartons. We need lots of cardboard boxes for the move.
Plural: die Kartons.

The word, taken apart.
Karton was borrowed from French carton (EtymDB), which came from Italian cartone 'stiff paper', built on carta 'paper', from Latin charta.

Lesson 4 of 5.
liefern — to deliver; to supply.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to wrap up, then the German for the cardboard box.

You'll want to know: liefern.
liefern — "to deliver; to supply".
Das Geschäft liefert die Möbel am Dienstag. The shop delivers the furniture on Tuesday.
Die Lieferung — the delivery.

The word, taken apart.
liefern is inherited from Middle High German lieveren (EtymDB), usually traced through Middle Low German to French livrer 'to hand over', which goes back to Latin līberāre 'to set free' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
umtauschen — to exchange, to change (in a shop).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the cardboard box, then the German for to deliver.

You'll want to know: umtauschen.
umtauschen — "to exchange, to change (in a shop)".
Die Schuhe sind zu klein, deshalb tauscht er sie um. The shoes are too small, so he exchanges them.
Separable: ich tausche um, ich habe umgetauscht. Der Umtausch — the exchange.

The word, taken apart.
umtauschen is built from um 'over, round' and tauschen 'to swap', inherited from Middle High German tiuschen (EtymDB).
