## You'll want to know: મફત

**મફત** — *maphat* — "free of charge".

> **અહીં પાણી મફત છે.** — *ahī̃ pāṇī maphat che* — "Water is free here."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મફત** (*maphat*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *muft*, "free, gratis". CDIAL has no entry for it.
