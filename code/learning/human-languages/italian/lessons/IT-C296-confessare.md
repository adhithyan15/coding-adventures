## You'll want to know: confessare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C296-MOVENTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**confessare** — "to confess".

> Alla fine ha confessato tutto.
>
> *In the end he confessed everything.*

*Confessarsi* is to go to confession in church.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confessare* is built on Latin *cōnfessus*, the past participle of *cōnfitērī* "to admit" (Lewis & Short).
