## You'll want to know: बूँद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C436-BUND436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बूँद** — *bū̃d* — "a drop".

> छत से पानी की बूँदें टपक रही थीं।
>
> *chat se pānī kī bū̃dẽ ṭapak rahī thīṁ.*
>
> Drops of water were dripping from the roof.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बूँद** (*bū̃d*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *bindu*, "a drop, a spot" (CDIAL 9240), through a form *\*bundu*; CDIAL lists Hindi *bū̃d*, "drop". The dot on a forehead, बिंदी, comes from the same word.
