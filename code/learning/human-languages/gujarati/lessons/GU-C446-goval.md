## You'll want to know: ગોવાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C446-HERD446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોવાળ** — *govāḷ* — "a cowherd".

> ગોવાળ ગાયો સાથે નદી પાસે બેઠો હતો.
>
> *govāḷ gāyo sāthe nadī pāse beṭho hato.*
>
> The cowherd was sitting by the river with the cows.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોવાળ** (*govāḷ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *gōpāla*, "a protector of cows", made of *gō*, "a cow", and *pāla*, "a keeper" (CDIAL 4293).
