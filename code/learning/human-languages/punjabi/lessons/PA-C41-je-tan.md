## The pattern, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-JOIN-JE-TAN-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-JE-01] -->

Punjabi usually marks **both** ends:

> **ਜੇ** *(condition)*, **ਤਾਂ** *(result)*

English says *if it is late, I will go* and marks only the front. Punjabi puts
**ਤਾਂ** at the head of the result as well, so the sentence announces its two
halves. Beginners often leave it out; it is understood, and the full pair is what
you will hear.

The result half is free to be negative, which is where this meets the chapter
that opened this run.
