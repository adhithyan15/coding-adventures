Telugu, chapter 306: Steel, Cement, Leather, Cotton, Wool.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఉక్కు (ukku) — steel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a battery, then the Telugu for a video.

You'll want to know: ఉక్కు.
ఉక్కు — ukku — "steel".

The word, taken apart.
ఉక్కు (ukku) is native Dravidian. DEDR 661 lists ukku, "steel", with Malayalam urukku, "fused metal, steel", under the word for "to melt".
