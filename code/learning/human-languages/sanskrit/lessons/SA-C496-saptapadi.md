## You'll want to know: सप्तपदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C496-VIVAHA496-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सप्तपदी** — *saptapadī* — "the seven steps".

> सप्तपद्याः अनन्तरं विवाहः पूर्णः भवति।
>
> *saptapadyāḥ anantaraṃ vivāhaḥ pūrṇaḥ bhavati.*
>
> After the seven steps the wedding is complete.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सप्तपदी** (*saptapadī*) is built from **सप्त**, "seven", and *pada*, "a step"; MW gives "the seven steps round the sacred fire at the marriage ceremony" (MW).
