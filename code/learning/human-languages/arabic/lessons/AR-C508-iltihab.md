## You'll want to know: التهاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C508-JILD508-04]; assesses=[] -->

**التهاب** — *iltihāb* — "an inflammation".

> عند أختي التهاب في الحلق.
>
> *ʿinda ukhtī iltihāb fī al-ḥalq.*
>
> My sister has a sore throat.

التهاب الحلق is a sore throat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**التهاب** (*iltihāb*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-h-b*, "a flame, to blaze" (Lane). Iltihāb is the verbal noun of Form VIII, a flaring up: the body burning in one spot.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
