## You'll want to know: શ્વાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-05]; assesses=[] -->

**શ્વાસ** — *śvās* — "breath".

> **શું તે શ્વાસ લે છે?** — *śũ te śvās le che?* — "Is he breathing?"

A masculine noun. With **લેવું** it means to breathe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શ્વાસ** (*śvās*) is Sanskrit *śvāsa*, "breath, breathing", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. The inherited form of the same word is Gujarati *sās*, "breath" (CDIAL 12769).
