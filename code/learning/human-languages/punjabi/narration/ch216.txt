Punjabi, chapter 216: Where Did They Go?.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
Return to a familiar went — retrieve the familiar they went by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: oh gae]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਗਏ (uh gae), "they went". Then to go: ਜਾਣਾ.

Grammar Lens.
ਗਏ is the went form for a group, or for one person you respect. Today it gets two partners: one for a man, one for a woman. Hold ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ beside it: every day, and once, in the past.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: they went]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਗਏ (uh gae).
[your turn — hear: he goes to school every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say they went once more. No new form is needed yet.


Lesson 2 of 6.
ਗਿਆ, ਗਈ — went.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say they went. Then home and school.

Grammar Lens: one went for each.
The went form agrees with whoever went: ਗਿਆ for a man or a boy, ਗਈ for a woman or a girl, and the ਗਏ you know for a group.
ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ। — Aman ghar giā. — Aman went home.
ਕੁੜੀ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਈ। — kuṛī sakūl gaī. — The girl went to school.
ਉਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਏ? — uh kitthe gae? — Where did they go?
There is no helper at the end. CDIAL lists Punjabi geā, "gone", under Sanskrit gata, "gone" (CDIAL 4008).

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: the boy went to school]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੁੰਡਾ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਿਆ।.
[your turn — hear: she went home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਘਰ ਗਈ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਗਿਆ and ਗਈ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Went, for a man? (ਗਿਆ.) For a woman? (ਗਈ.) For a group? (ਗਏ.)

Lesson 4 of 6.
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) (kall) — yesterday; tomorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new sense: say Aman went home, then the farewell ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ, "we will meet tomorrow".

You'll want to know: ਕੱਲ੍ਹ.
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) — kall — "yesterday; tomorrow". It names the day one step from today, either way; the verb tells you which.
ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ। — Aman kallh ghar giā. — Aman went home yesterday.
With a went form it is yesterday; in ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ it is tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) (kall) is inherited from Sanskrit kālya, "at dawn; tomorrow": CDIAL lists Punjabi kall, "yesterday, tomorrow" (CDIAL 3104).

What you've built.
One more word for talking about the past.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kall]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: the girl went to school yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੁੜੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) ਸਕੂਲ ਗਈ।.
[your turn — recall: say we will meet tomorrow, then Aman went home yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) once from the model]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kallh) mean? ("Yesterday; tomorrow".) Which one, with ਗਿਆ? (Yesterday.)

Grammar Lens: asking where.
ਕਿੱਥੇ goes right before the went form, where the place will go in the answer:
ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਏ? — uh kallh kitthe gae? — Where did they go yesterday?
ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਗਏ। — uh kallh bāzār gae. — They went to the market yesterday.
ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਿਆ? — Aman kallh kitthe giā? — Where did Aman go yesterday?

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — hear: she went home]
