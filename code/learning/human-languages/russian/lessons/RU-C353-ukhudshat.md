## You'll want to know: ухудшать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C353-USHCHERB-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ухудшать** (*ukhudshát*) — "to make worse".

> Стресс ухудшает сон.
>
> *Stress ukhudsháyet son.*
>
> Stress makes your sleep worse.

Imperfective; perfective *ухудшить*. *Ухудшаться* — to get worse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ухудшать** is built on *худший* "worse", from *худой* "thin, poor, bad", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*xudŭ* (EtymDB).
