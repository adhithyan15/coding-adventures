## You'll want to know: ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C352-BOARD352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ** — *shatranj* — "chess".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਾਦਾ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ ਖੇਡਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā dādā shām nū̃ shatranj kheḍdā hai.*
>
> My grandfather plays chess in the evening.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ਤਰੰਜ** (*shatranj*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *shatranj*, "chess", which goes back to Sanskrit *caturaṅga*, "four-limbed".
