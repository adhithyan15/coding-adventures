## You'll want to know: la proroga
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C360-REPARTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la proroga** — "the extension (of a deadline)".

> Ho chiesto una proroga di due settimane.
>
> *I asked for a two-week extension.*

Stress on the first syllable: *pròroga*. The verb is *prorogare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proroga* is built on *prorogare* "to extend", a learned borrowing from Latin *prōrogāre* "to prolong, to put off" (Lewis & Short), built from *prō* and *rogāre* "to ask".
