## You'll want to know: পিওন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C493-OPHIS493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**পিওন** — *pion* — "an office messenger; a peon".

> পিওন ম্যানেজারের জন্য চা আনল।
>
> *Pion mænejārer jônyô chā ānlô.*
>
> The office messenger brought tea for the manager.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পিওন** (*pion*) is usually traced to Portuguese *peão*, "a foot soldier, a man on foot".
