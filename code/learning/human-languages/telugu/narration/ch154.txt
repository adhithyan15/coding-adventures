Telugu, chapter 154: Recently, Already, Last, Once, To Spend (Time).
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఇటీవల (iṭīvala) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for anyone, then the Telugu for anything.

You'll want to know: ఇటీవల.
ఇటీవల — iṭīvala — "recently". Telugu's past for "I" ends in -ఆను (-ānu): నేను ఇటీవల ఒక పుస్తకం చదివాను — nēnu iṭīvala oka pustakaṁ cadivānu — "I recently read a book."
