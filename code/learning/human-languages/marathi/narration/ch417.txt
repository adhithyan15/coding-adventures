Marathi, chapter 417: Renting a Room.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अनामत (anāmat) — a deposit; money held in trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a porter, then the Marathi for to sprinkle.

You'll want to know: अनामत.
अनामत — anāmat — "a deposit; money held in trust".
खोली सोडताना अनामत परत मिळते. kholī soḍtānā anāmat parat miḷte. You get the deposit back when you leave the room.
Feminine.
It comes back when you leave, if nothing is broken.

The word, taken apart.
अनामत (anāmat) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic amānat, "something held in trust, a deposit".

Lesson 2 of 5.
स्थायिक होणे (sthāyik hoṇe) — to settle down; to make a home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sprinkle, then the Marathi for a deposit.

You'll want to know: स्थायिक होणे.
स्थायिक होणे — sthāyik hoṇe — "to settle down; to make a home".
अरुणचं कुटुंब आता पुण्यात स्थायिक झालं आहे. aruṇcaṁ kuṭumb ātā puṇyāt sthāyik jhālaṁ āhe. Arun's family has now settled in Pune.
Past (he …): तो स्थायिक झाला, to sthāyik jhālā.
The place takes -त: पुण्यात स्थायिक होणे (sthāyik hoṇe), to settle in Pune for good.

The word, taken apart.
स्थायिक होणे (sthāyik hoṇe) joins स्थायिक, "settled for good", a tatsama from Sanskrit sthāyika, "lasting, enduring" (Monier-Williams), to the light verb होणे, "to become".

Lesson 3 of 5.
भाडेकरू (bhāḍekarū) — a tenant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a deposit, then the Marathi for to settle down.

You'll want to know: भाडेकरू.
भाडेकरू — bhāḍekarū — "a tenant".
शेजारच्या घरात नवे भाडेकरू आले. śejārcyā gharāt nave bhāḍekarū āle. New tenants have moved into the house next door.
Masculine.
The owner is the मालक; the plural is the same: दोन भाडेकरू (bhāḍekarū).

The word, taken apart.
भाडेकरू (bhāḍekarū) is built from भाडे, "rent", and the ending -करू, "one who does": the one who pays the rent; the origin of भाडे is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
तिजोरी (tijorī) — a strongbox; a safe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to settle down, then the Marathi for a tenant.

You'll want to know: तिजोरी.
तिजोरी — tijorī — "a strongbox; a safe".
दुकानदार पैसे तिजोरीत ठेवतो. dukāndār paise tijorīt ṭhevto. The shopkeeper keeps the money in the safe.
Feminine.
Also the treasury of a town or a state: सरकारी तिजोरी (tijorī).

The word, taken apart.
तिजोरी (tijorī) is a Marathi word for a steel box with a lock, and for a treasury; where it comes from is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उद्वाहक (udvāhak) — an elevator; a lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tenant, then the Marathi for a strongbox.

You'll want to know: उद्वाहक.
उद्वाहक — udvāhak — "an elevator; a lift".
इमारतीतला उद्वाहक बंद आहे. imāratītlā udvāhak band āhe. The lift in the building is not working.
Masculine.
A formal word for signs; in speech many people say लिफ्ट.

The word, taken apart.
उद्वाहक (udvāhak) is a modern word built from Sanskrit ud-, "up", and vāhaka, "a carrier" (Monier-Williams): a thing that carries up.
