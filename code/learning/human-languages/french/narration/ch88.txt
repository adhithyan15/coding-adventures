French, chapter 88: Ant, Elephant, Rabbit, Frog, Tiger.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la fourmi — an ant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a monkey, then the French for a bee.

You'll want to know: la fourmi.
la fourmi — "an ant".
