Spanish, chapter 501: De viaje otra vez.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el viajero — the traveller, the passenger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the law, then the Spanish for freedom.

You'll want to know: el viajero.
el viajero — "the traveller, the passenger".
Los viajeros deben bajar del tren. Passengers must get off the train.
A woman is la viajera.
