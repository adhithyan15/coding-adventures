## You'll want to know: പത്താം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C68-ORDINAL-05]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C67-ORDINAL-02, ML-CONCEPT-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-01] -->

**പത്താം** (*pathāṁ*) — "**tenth**". **പത്ത്** loses its **ŭ** and takes
**ആം**, and the ten are done.

**പത്താം ദിവസം** (*pathāṁ divasam*), the tenth day.
