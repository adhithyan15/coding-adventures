## You'll want to know: టైరు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C443-VAHANAM443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**టైరు** — *ṭairu* — "a tyre".

> కారు టైరులో గాలి తక్కువగా ఉంది.
>
> *kāru ṭairulō gāli takkuvagā undi.*
>
> The car tyre is low on air.

To pump air into it is గాలి కొట్టు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**టైరు** (*ṭairu*) is a loan from English *tyre*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
