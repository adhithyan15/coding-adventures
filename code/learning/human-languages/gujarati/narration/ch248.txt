Gujarati, chapter 248: Meetings and applications.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
બેઠક (beṭhak) — meeting; sitting room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to earn, then the Gujarati for hardworking.

You'll want to know: બેઠક.
બેઠક — beṭhak — "meeting; sitting room".
આજે બપોરે બેઠક છે. — āje bapore beṭhak che — "There is a meeting this afternoon."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બેઠક (beṭhak), a sitting together, is built on beṭh-, a stem of the taught besvũ, "to sit", with -ak. CDIAL derives besvũ and beṭho from Sanskrit úpaviśati, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245).

Lesson 2 of 6.
અરજી (arjī) — application, petition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for hardworking, then the Gujarati for meeting.

You'll want to know: અરજી.
અરજી — arjī — "application, petition".
તે નોકરી માટે અરજી લખે છે. — te nokrī māṭe arjī lakhe che — "She writes an application for a job."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અરજી (arjī) is a loan usually traced to a Persian ʿarzī, "a petition", built on Arabic ʿarḍ, "a presenting, a showing". Its Arabic root is ʿ-r-ḍ (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 6.
સહી કરવી (sahī karvī) — to sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for meeting, then the Gujarati for application.

You'll want to know: સહી કરવી.
સહી કરવી — sahī karvī — "to sign".
સાહેબ સાંજે બધા કાગળમાં સહી કરે છે. — sāheb sā̃je badhā kāgaḷmā̃ sahī kare che — "The boss signs all the papers in the evening."
સહી, sahī, alone means a signature.

The word, taken apart.
સહી કરવી (sahī karvī) is built from sahī, "signature", and karvī, "to do". Sahī is usually traced to Arabic ṣaḥīḥ, "sound, correct", through Persian; its Arabic root is ṣ-ḥ-ḥ (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 6.
સોંપવું (sõpvũ) — to hand over, to entrust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for application, then the Gujarati for to sign.

You'll want to know: સોંપવું.
સોંપવું — sõpvũ — "to hand over, to entrust".
સાહેબ મને નવું કામ સોંપે છે. — sāheb mane navũ kām sõpe che — "The boss hands me a new task."

The word, taken apart.
સોંપવું (sõpvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sámarpayati, "delivers over"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "hands over" (CDIAL 13192).

Lesson 5 of 6.
નોંધવું (nõdhvũ) — to note down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sign, then the Gujarati for to hand over.

You'll want to know: નોંધવું.
નોંધવું — nõdhvũ — "to note down".
હું બેઠકમાં બધું નોંધું છું. — hũ beṭhakmā̃ badhũ nõdhũ chũ — "I note down everything in the meeting."
Its causative નોંધાવવું means to register.

The word, taken apart.
નોંધવું (nõdhvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from nibandhati, beside Sanskrit níbadhnāti, "binds on; writes down"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to note down, enter in a book" (CDIAL 7223).
