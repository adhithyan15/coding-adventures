## You'll want to know: नियमावली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C542-NIYAM542-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नियमावली** — *niyamāvalī* — "a rulebook".

> शाळेने सगळ्या पालकांना नियमावली दिली.
>
> *śāḷene sagḷyā pālkānnā niyamāvalī dilī.*
>
> The school gave all the parents a rulebook.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियमावली** (*niyamāvalī*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *niyama*, "rule", and *āvali*, "a row" (Monier-Williams): a row of rules.
