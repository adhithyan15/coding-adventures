## You'll want to know: విశ్రాంతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**విశ్రాంతి** (*viśrānti*) — "rest".

**విశ్రాంతి తీసుకోండి** — "please take some rest", what you say to someone with a fever or a
long day behind them. The polite ending **-అండి** is the one you already put on *raṇḍi*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Something to say to the person who is unwell, not only about yourself.
