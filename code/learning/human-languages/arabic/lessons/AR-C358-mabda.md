## You'll want to know: مبدأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C358-HUJJA358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مبدأ** — *mabdaʾ* — "a principle".

> الصدق مبدأ مهم عند أبي.
>
> *aṣ-ṣidq mabdaʾ muhimm ʿinda abī.*
>
> Honesty is an important principle for my father.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مبدأ** (*mabdaʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-d-ʾ*, "to begin" (Lane). *Mabdaʾ* is a noun of place, a starting point: the idea everything else starts from.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
