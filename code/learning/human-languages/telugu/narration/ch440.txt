Telugu, chapter 440: Waiting on the Platform.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఆలస్యం (ālasyaṁ) — a delay; lateness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a railway coach, then the Telugu for a fare.

You'll want to know: ఆలస్యం.
ఆలస్యం — ālasyaṁ — "a delay; lateness".
వర్షం వల్ల రైలు రెండు గంటలు ఆలస్యం అయింది. varṣaṁ valla railu reṇḍu gaṇṭalu ālasyaṁ ayindi. Because of the rain the train was two hours late.
ఆలస్యంగా is late, adverb: ఆలస్యంగా వచ్చాడు, he came late. In Sanskrit the word meant laziness; Telugu uses it for lateness.

The word, taken apart.
ఆలస్యం (ālasyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit ālasya, "sloth, idleness" (CDIAL 1371). In Telugu the slowness of the idle became plain lateness.

Lesson 2 of 5.
వేచి ఉండు (vēci uṇḍu) — to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a fare, then the Telugu for a delay.

You'll want to know: వేచి ఉండు.
వేచి ఉండు — vēci uṇḍu — "to wait".
మేము గంటసేపు బస్సు కోసం వేచి ఉన్నాం. mēmu gaṇṭasēpu bassu kōsaṁ vēci unnāṁ. We waited an hour for the bus.
Past (I …): వేచి ఉన్నాను, vēci unnānu.
What you wait for takes కోసం: బస్సు కోసం వేచి ఉన్నాను. It is a little more careful than ఆగు, to stop and wait.

The word, taken apart.
వేచి ఉండు (vēci uṇḍu) is native Dravidian, a phrase of two verbs: వేచి, from వేచు, "to wait for, to look out for" (DEDR 5533), and ఉండు, "to be, to stay" (DEDR 697). Together they say "to stay waiting".

Lesson 3 of 5.
సమయపాలన (samayapālana) — punctuality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a delay, then the Telugu for to wait.

You'll want to know: సమయపాలన.
సమయపాలన — samayapālana — "punctuality".
మా డ్రైవరుకు సమయపాలన చాలా ముఖ్యం. mā ḍraivaruku samayapālana cālā mukhyaṁ. Punctuality matters a great deal to our driver.
సమయం, time, + పాలన, keeping: keeping time. పాలన alone is rule or administration.

The word, taken apart.
సమయపాలన (samayapālana) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: samaya, "an appointed time" (CDIAL 13185), and pālana, "guarding, keeping" (CDIAL 8128).

Lesson 4 of 5.
విరామం (virāmaṁ) — an interval; a break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to wait, then the Telugu for punctuality.

You'll want to know: విరామం.
విరామం — virāmaṁ — "an interval; a break".
రెండు గంటల ప్రయాణం తరువాత పది నిమిషాల విరామం ఉంది. reṇḍu gaṇṭala prayāṇaṁ taruvāta padi nimiṣāla virāmaṁ undi. After two hours on the road there is a ten-minute break.
A film has an interval, విరామం (virāmaṁ), too. In Sanskrit grammar virāma is also the name of the sign ్, which takes away a consonant's vowel.

The word, taken apart.
విరామం (virāmaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit virāma, "cessation, a stop, a pause" (Monier-Williams), from vi-ram, "to stop".

Lesson 5 of 5.
త్వరపడు (tvarapaḍu) — to hurry up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for punctuality, then the Telugu for an interval.

You'll want to know: త్వరపడు.
త్వరపడు — tvarapaḍu — "to hurry up".
బస్సు వస్తోందని మేము త్వరపడ్డాం. bassu vastōndani mēmu tvarapaḍḍāṁ. Hearing that the bus was coming, we hurried.
Past (I …): త్వరపడ్డాను, tvarapaḍḍānu.
త్వరగా, quickly, holds the same Sanskrit word. తొందరపడు is its everyday native twin.

The word, taken apart.
త్వరపడు (tvarapaḍu) is a Telugu compound: Sanskrit tvarā, "haste" (Monier-Williams), with the native verb పడు, "to fall into, to undergo" (DEDR 3853).
