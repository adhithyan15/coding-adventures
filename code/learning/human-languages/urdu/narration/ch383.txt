Urdu, chapter 383: Getting the Go-Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منظوری (manzūrī) — approval; sanction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for deserving, then the Urdu for to tidy up.

You'll want to know: منظوری.
منظوری — manzūrī — "approval; sanction".
اگر منظوری مل گئی تو کام کل ہو گا۔ agar manzūrī mil gaī to kām kal ho gā. If approval comes through, the work will be done tomorrow.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
منظوری (manzūrī) is built on manzūr, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic manẓūr, "looked at; accepted", with the noun ending -ī.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بٹورنا (baṭornā) — to rake in (money, praise); to gather up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an arrangement, then the Urdu for absolute.

You'll want to know: بٹورنا.
بٹورنا — baṭornā — "to rake in (money, praise); to gather up".
اگر یہ فلم چلی تو خوب پیسے بٹورے گی۔ agar yih film chalī to khūb paise baṭore gī. If this film does well, it will rake in a lot of money.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے خوب پیسے بٹورے, us ne khūb paise baṭore.

The word, taken apart.
بٹورنا (baṭornā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin baṭornā, "to collect", under Sanskrit vartula, "round" (CDIAL 11365): to bring things round into a heap, and so to gather in.

Lesson 5 of 5.
فیل ہونا (fel honā) — to fail an exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for absolute, then the Urdu for to rake in.

You'll want to know: فیل ہونا.
فیل ہونا — fel honā — "to fail an exam".
اگر تم نے نہیں پڑھا تو فیل ہو جاؤ گے۔ agar tum ne nahīṅ paṛhā to fel ho jāo ge. If you do not study, you will fail.
Past (he …): وہ لڑکا امتحان میں فیل ہو گیا, voh laṛkā imtihān meṅ fel ho gayā.

The word, taken apart.
فیل ہونا (fel honā) is built from fel, borrowed from English fail, and ہونا, "to be".
