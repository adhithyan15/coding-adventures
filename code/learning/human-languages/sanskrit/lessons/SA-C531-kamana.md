## You'll want to know: कामना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C531-VANCHATI531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कामना** — *kāmanā* — "a desire".

> यदि तव कामना उचिता अस्ति, तर्हि सा पूर्णा भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi tava kāmanā ucitā asti, tarhi sā pūrṇā bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If your desire is right, it will be fulfilled.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कामना** (*kāmanā*) comes from the root √kam, "to wish, to love"; MW gives *kāmanā* as a wish, a desire (MW).
