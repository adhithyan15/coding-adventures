Russian, chapter 65: Big, Small, Old, Long, Short.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
большой (bolshóy) — big.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for bad, then the Russian for new.

You'll want to know: большой.
большой (bolshóy) — "big".

What you've built.
One more word for big, small, old, long, short.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bolshóy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bolshóy, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bolshóy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for bad, then the Russian for new, then say bolshóy again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does большой (bolshóy) mean? ("Big".) Say it once more.
