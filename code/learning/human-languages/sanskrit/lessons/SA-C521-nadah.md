## You'll want to know: नादः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C521-VADAKA521-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नादः** — *nādaḥ* — "a resonant sound".

> मृदंगस्य नादः दूरं गच्छति।
>
> *mṛdaṅgasya nādaḥ dūraṃ gacchati.*
>
> The sound of the drum carries far.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नादः** (*nādaḥ*) comes from the root √nad, "to sound, to roar"; MW gives *nāda* as a loud sound, a roar, and any sound or tone (MW).
