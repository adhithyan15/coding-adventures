## You'll want to know: مہلت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C525-MOHLAT525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مہلت** — *mohlat* — "extra time; a respite".

> اگر ایک ہفتے کی مہلت ملی تو کام ہو گا۔
>
> *agar ek hafte kī mohlat milī to kām ho gā.*
>
> If I get a week's grace, the work will be finished.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مہلت** (*mohlat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muhla*, "a delay, a respite".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
