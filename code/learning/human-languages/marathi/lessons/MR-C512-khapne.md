# खपणे (khapṇe) — to sell well; to toil

## You'll want to know: खपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C512-KARKHANA512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खपणे** — *khapṇe* — "to sell well; to toil".

> सणांच्या दिवसांत सगळा माल खपला.
>
> *saṇāñcyā divsānt sagḷā māl khaplā.*
>
> In the festival days all the goods sold out.

Past (he …): तो माल लवकर खपला, *to māl lavkar khaplā*.

The goods are the subject: माल खपतो, the goods sell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खपणे** (*khapṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *khapṇẽ*, "to be consumed, to work", under Sanskrit *kṣapyatē*, "is destroyed" (CDIAL 3655); goods that sell are used up.
