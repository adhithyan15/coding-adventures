## You'll want to know: el bienestar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C743-MENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el bienestar** — "well-being".

> El deporte mejora el bienestar.
>
> *Sport improves well-being.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bienestar* is built on *bien* "well", from Latin *bene*, and *estar* "to be", from Latin *stāre* "to stand".
