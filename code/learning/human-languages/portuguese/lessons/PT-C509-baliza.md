## You'll want to know: a baliza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C509-BALIZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a baliza** — "goal (the posts and net)".

> A bola bateu no poste e não entrou na baliza.
>
> *The ball hit the post and did not go into the goal.*

In Brazil: *o gol*. A scored goal is *um golo* in Portugal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *baliza*, once a marker post or a buoy, is not settled.
