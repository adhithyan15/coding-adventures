# если — if, and the if that is a question

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C154-PRICH154-03, RU-GRAMMAR-KOGDA-CLAUSE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-UZHE-PRESENT-DURATION-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if". (*Yésli.*) Then say "I've been learning Russian for
two years". (*Ya uzhé dva góda uchú rússkiy.*) The word for "if" has waited
since the joining words of the A2 chapters. Now it joins.

## Grammar Lens: a condition and a comma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-CLAUSE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KHOTET-01, RU-LEX-MOCH-01, RU-LEX-C88-LOC88-02, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01, RU-GRAMMAR-USTAL-STATE-01, RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-04] -->

**Если** works like *когда*: it hangs one half on the other, and the comma
sits at the seam, whichever half comes first.

> **Если хочешь, мы можем пойти в кино.** — *yésli khóchesh, my mózhem
> poytí v kinó* — If you like, we can go to the cinema.
> **Мы можем пойти в кино, если хочешь.** — the same, turned round.
> **Если ты устал, отдохни.** — *yésli ty ustál, otdokhní* — If you're
> tired, have a rest.

*Пойти* is "to go, to set off", the partner of *идти*; *отдохни* is
*отдохнуть* to a friend.

## Grammar Lens: "if" that means "whether"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-LI-NOT-YESLI-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-LI-01, RU-GRAMMAR-ZNAYU-CHTO-01, RU-LEX-C38-VERB1-01, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-03] -->

English "if" has a second job: "I don't know if the museum is open". That
"if" is a question, "whether", and Russian answers it with **ли**, not
*если*:

> **Я не знаю, работает ли музей.** — *ya ne znáyu, rabótayet li muzéy* —
> I don't know if the museum is open.

The verb comes first, then **ли**: "is-it-working, the museum". A museum
that is open, in Russian, "works".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C154-PRICH154-03] -->

**если** was once *есть ли*, "is it?": *есть*, "is", with the asking
word *ли*. EtymDB 2.1 records it as a compound of *есть* and *ли*, though
it glosses that *есть* as "to eat", the other word with the same
spelling; "is", the form of *быть*, is the one that fits. A condition
began as a question.
