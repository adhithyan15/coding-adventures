## You'll want to know: かいぜんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C526-PROGRESS526-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かいぜんする** — *kaizen suru* — "to improve (something)".

> **もし もんだいを かいぜんしたら、きゃくが ふえるでしょう。** — *moshi mondai o kaizenshitara, kyaku ga fueru deshō* — "If we fix the problems, customers will probably increase."

Polite **かいぜんします**, *kaizenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **てんちょうが みせを かいぜんしました。**, *tenchō ga mise o kaizenshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいぜんする** (*kaizen suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *kaizen* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "reformation" (Middle Chinese *kojX*) and *zen*, "virtuous" (*dzyenX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
