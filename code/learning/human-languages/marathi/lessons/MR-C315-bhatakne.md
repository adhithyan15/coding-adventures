## You'll want to know: भटकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C315-ANK315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भटकणे** — *bhaṭakṇe* — "to wander".

> रस्ता चुकल्यावर आम्ही दोन तास जंगलात भटकलो.
>
> *rastā cukalyāvar āmhī don tās jaṅglāt bhaṭaklo.*
>
> After losing the way we wandered in the forest for two hours.

Past (he …): तो भटकला, *to bhaṭaklā*.

Intransitive; भटकी कुत्री, stray dogs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भटकणे** (*bhaṭakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *bhaṭakṇe*, 'to wander', under *\*bhaṭ*, 'a sudden movement' (CDIAL 9365).
