## You'll want to know: anlegen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C422-KREUZFAHRT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anlegen** — "to dock, to moor; to put on; to invest".

> Das Schiff legt am Morgen im Hafen an.
>
> *The ship docks in the harbour in the morning.*

Separable: *das Schiff legt an*, *es hat angelegt*. *Geld anlegen* — to invest money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anlegen* is built from *an* 'on, at' and *legen* 'to lay', inherited from Old High German *leggen* (EtymDB): a boat laid alongside the quay.
