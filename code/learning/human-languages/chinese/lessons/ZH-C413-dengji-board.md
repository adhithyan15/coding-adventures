## You'll want to know: dēngjī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C413-FLY413-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjī* — "to board a plane".

> *dēngjī yǐqián wǒmen zuò zài zhèr děng* — Until we board, we'll sit here and wait.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjī* is built from *dēng* 'to go up' and 机 'a plane', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong* and *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and, after an older dictionary, 'to get up into a carriage'.
