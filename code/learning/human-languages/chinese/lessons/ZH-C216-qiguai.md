## You'll want to know: qíguài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C216-WRONG216-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qíguài* — "strange; odd".

> *zhège rén hěn qíguài* — That person is strange.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qíguài* is built from *qí* and *guài*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gje* and *kweajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'different' and the second 'strange'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
