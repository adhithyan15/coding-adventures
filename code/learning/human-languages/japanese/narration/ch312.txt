Japanese, chapter 312: History.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
れきし (rekishi) — history.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to lose for good, then the Japanese for to look back (on).

You'll want to know: れきし.
れきし — rekishi — "history".
日本の れきしを ならっています。 — nihon no rekishi o naratte imasu — "I am learning Japanese history."

The word, taken apart.
れきし (rekishi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are reki, "passage of time" (Middle Chinese lek) and shi, "history" (sriX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
