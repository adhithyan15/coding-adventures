Telugu, chapter 542: Painting and Sculpture.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చిత్రలేఖనం (citralēkhanaṁ) — the art of painting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a comedian, then the Telugu for spectators.

You'll want to know: చిత్రలేఖనం.
చిత్రలేఖనం — citralēkhanaṁ — "the art of painting".
నా చెల్లెలు చిత్రలేఖనం నేర్చుకుంటోంది. nā cellelu citralēkhanaṁ nērcukuṇṭōndi. My younger sister is learning to paint.
చిత్రం, picture, + లేఖనం, drawing.

The word, taken apart.
చిత్రలేఖనం (citralēkhanaṁ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit citra, "bright, variegated" (CDIAL 4803), and lēkhana, "scraping", the noun of the root of writing (CDIAL 11103).

Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sculpture, then the Telugu for a sculptor.

You'll want to know: కళాఖండం.
కళాఖండం — kaḷākhaṇḍaṁ — "a masterpiece".
ఆ పాత ఆలయం ఒక కళాఖండం. ā pāta ālayaṁ oka kaḷākhaṇḍaṁ. That old temple is a masterpiece.
కళ, art, + ఖండం, piece.

The word, taken apart.
కళాఖండం (kaḷākhaṇḍaṁ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit kalā, "any practical art" (CDIAL 2927), and khaṇḍa, "a piece, a fragment" (CDIAL 3792).
