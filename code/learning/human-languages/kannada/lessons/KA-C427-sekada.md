## You'll want to know: ಶೇಕಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C427-ANUPATA427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶೇಕಡ** — *śēkaḍa* — "per cent".

> ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಶೇಕಡ ತೊಂಬತ್ತು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳು ಪಾಸಾದರು.
>
> *ī varṣa śēkaḍa tombattu vidyārthigaḷu pāsādaru.*
>
> This year ninety per cent of the students passed.

It comes before the number: ಶೇಕಡ ಹತ್ತು, ten per cent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶೇಕಡ** (*śēkaḍa*) is usually traced to a Hindustani or Marathi word for "per hundred", which goes back to Sanskrit *śata*, "a hundred" (CDIAL 12278).
