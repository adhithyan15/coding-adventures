## You'll want to know: хор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C517-ORCHESTRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**хор** (*khor*) — "a choir".

> В школе я пел в хоре.
>
> *V shkóle ya pel v khóre.*
>
> At school I sang in the choir.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хор* comes from Greek *khorós* 'a company of dancers or singers' (EtymDB).
