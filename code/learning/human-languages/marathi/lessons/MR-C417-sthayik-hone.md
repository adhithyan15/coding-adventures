# स्थायिक होणे (sthāyik hoṇe) — to settle down; to make a home

## You'll want to know: स्थायिक होणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C417-ANAMAT417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थायिक होणे** — *sthāyik hoṇe* — "to settle down; to make a home".

> अरुणचं कुटुंब आता पुण्यात स्थायिक झालं आहे.
>
> *aruṇcaṁ kuṭumb ātā puṇyāt sthāyik jhālaṁ āhe.*
>
> Arun's family has now settled in Pune.

Past (he …): तो स्थायिक झाला, *to sthāyik jhālā*.

The place takes -त: पुण्यात स्थायिक होणे, to settle in Pune for good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थायिक होणे** (*sthāyik hoṇe*) joins **स्थायिक**, "settled for good", a tatsama from Sanskrit *sthāyika*, "lasting, enduring" (Monier-Williams), to the light verb **होणे**, "to become".
