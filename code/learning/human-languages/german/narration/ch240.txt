You'll want to know: das Thema.
das Thema — "a topic".

The word, taken apart.
Thema from Latin thema from Greek théma, "what is set down", from títhēmi, "to put" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. English theme is the same word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Überraschung — a surprise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a tradition, then the German for a dream.

You'll want to know: die Überraschung.
die Überraschung — "a surprise".

The word, taken apart.
Überraschung is the noun of a verb built from the word for "over" + the word for "quick, swift" (EtymDB 2.1 links the verb to it): to come over someone quickly.

die Umgebung — "the surroundings".

The word, taken apart.
Umgebung is the noun of a verb built from um-, "around", + the verb "to give", English give: what surrounds you. EtymDB 2.1 lists Dutch omgeving beside it.
