## You'll want to know: divertido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C713-DIVERTIDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**divertido** — "entertaining, fun".

> La fiesta fue muy divertida.
>
> *The party was a lot of fun.*

With *ser* it means fun; *divertirse* is to have a good time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*divertido* is the participle of *divertir*, which comes from Latin *dīvertere* "to turn aside" (EtymDB), built on *vertere* "to turn".
