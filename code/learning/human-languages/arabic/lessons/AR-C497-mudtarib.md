## You'll want to know: مضطرب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C497-TAWATTUR497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مضطرب** — *muḍṭarib* — "agitated, unsettled".

> كان البحر مضطربا في الليل.
>
> *kāna al-baḥr muḍṭariban fī al-layl.*
>
> The sea was rough in the night.

It is said of a person, the sea or the weather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مضطرب** (*muḍṭarib*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-r-b*, "to strike, to beat" (Lane). Muḍṭarib is the participle of Form VIII, where the t turns into ṭ: one beaten about, restless.

## What you've built
