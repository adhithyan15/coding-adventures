German, chapter 70: Banana, Potato, Tomato, Onion, Carrot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Banane — a banana.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for fruit, then the German for an apple.

You'll want to know: die Banane.
die Banane — "a banana".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Banane]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Banane, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say die Banane]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for fruit, then the German for an apple, then say die Banane again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Banane mean? ("A banana".) Say it once more.
