# اعتبار کرنا (etibār karnā) — to believe someone; to trust their word

## You'll want to know: اعتبار کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C457-TRUST457-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتبار کرنا** — *etibār karnā* — "to believe someone; to trust their word".

> میں نے اس کی بات کا اعتبار کیا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne us kī bāt kā etibār kiyā.*
>
> I took him at his word.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دوست کا اعتبار کیا, *us ne dost kā etibār kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتبار کرنا** (*etibār karnā*) is built from **اعتبار**, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iʿtibār*, "regard, credit", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
