## You'll want to know: il pennello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C521-PITTURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il pennello** — "paintbrush".

> Lavo i pennelli con l'acqua calda.
>
> *I wash the brushes in hot water.*

*A pennello* — perfectly: *ti sta a pennello*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pennello* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *pēnicellus* "a little brush" (Lewis & Short has *pēnicillus*), a little form of a word for a tail.
