Gujarati, chapter 543: Fit for the Job.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અપવાદ (apvād) — an exception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a recommendation, then the Gujarati for a polite request.

You'll want to know: અપવાદ.
અપવાદ — apvād — "an exception".
જો તમે બીમાર હશો તો અમે અપવાદ કરીશું. jo tame bīmār haśo to ame apvād karīśũ. If you are ill, we will make an exception.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અપવાદ (apvād) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit apavāda, "speaking against; a refutation" (Monier-Williams); in grammar and in rules it is the case that does not follow.
