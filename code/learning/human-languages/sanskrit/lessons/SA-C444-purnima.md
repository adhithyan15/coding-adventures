## You'll want to know: पूर्णिमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C444-CANDRA444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्णिमा** — *pūrṇimā* — "the full-moon day".

> पूर्णिमायां चन्द्रः पूर्णः भवति।
>
> *pūrṇimāyāṃ candraḥ pūrṇaḥ bhavati.*
>
> On the full-moon day the moon is full.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्णिमा** (*pūrṇimā*) is built on *pūrṇa*, "full"; MW gives *pūrṇimā* as the day of full moon (MW).
