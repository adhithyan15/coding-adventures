Arabic, chapter 257: To refuse, To graduate, To be glad, To be afraid, To smile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رفض (rafaḍa) — to refuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to translate, then the Arabic for to give back.

You'll want to know: رفض.
رفض — rafaḍa — "to refuse".

The word, taken apart.
رفض (rafaḍa) is built on the native Arabic root r-f-ḍ, "to reject" (Lane). Rafaḍa is the plain verb, "he refused".
