## You'll want to know: अढाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C212-AMOUNT212-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अढाई** — *aḍhāī* — "two and a half".

> ये अढाई सो रुपिया है।
>
> *ye aḍhāī so rupiyā hai.*
>
> This is two hundred and fifty rupees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अढाई** (*aḍhāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ardhatṛtīya*, "two and a half". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Sindhi *aḍhāī* and Hindi *aṛhāī*, "two and a half" (CDIAL 651).
