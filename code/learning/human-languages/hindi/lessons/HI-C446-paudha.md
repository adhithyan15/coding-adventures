## You'll want to know: पौधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C446-PRAKRITI446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पौधा** — *paudhā* — "a plant; a sapling".

> मेरे कमरे में एक छोटा पौधा है।
>
> *mere kamre meṁ ek choṭā paudhā hai.*
>
> There is a small plant in my room.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पौधा** (*paudhā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pravṛddha*, "grown up, great" (CDIAL 8807), which lists Hindi *paudhā*, "young plants for transplanting"; Hindi widened it to any plant.
