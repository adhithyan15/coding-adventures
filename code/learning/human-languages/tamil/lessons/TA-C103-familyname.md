## You'll want to know: குடும்பப் பெயர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C103-NEXTGEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**குடும்பப் பெயர்** (*kuṭumpap peyar*) — "a family name".

**பெயர்** is the "name" of your first introductions; **குடும்பம்** is "family". Many Tamil names
use a father's initial where English uses a surname.
