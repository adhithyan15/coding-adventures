Marathi, chapter 339: Heroes and Villains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नायक (nāyak) — a protagonist; the hero of a story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a witch, then the Marathi for a curse.

You'll want to know: नायक.
नायक — nāyak — "a protagonist; the hero of a story".
चित्रपटाचा नायक एक साधा शिक्षक आहे. citrapaṭācā nāyak ek sādhā śikṣak āhe. The hero of the film is an ordinary teacher.
Masculine.
A heroine is नायिका.

The word, taken apart.
नायक (nāyak) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nāyaka, 'a guide, a leader; the hero of a drama' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
खलनायक (khalnāyak) — a villain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a curse, then the Marathi for a protagonist.

You'll want to know: खलनायक.
खलनायक — khalnāyak — "a villain".
शेवटी खलनायकाला शिक्षा झाली. śevṭī khalnāyakālā śikṣā jhālī. In the end the villain was punished.
Masculine.
खल, wicked, + नायक (nāyak), a lead.

The word, taken apart.
खलनायक (khalnāyak) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit khala, 'a wicked man', and nāyaka, 'a leader; the hero of a drama' (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: पात्र.
पात्र — pātra — "a character".

Neuter.
Plural पात्रं. It also means a vessel, and eligible.

The word, taken apart.
पात्र (pātra) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pātra, 'a vessel; a fit person; an actor' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सावकार (sāvkār) — a moneylender.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a character, then the Marathi for a holy man.

You'll want to know: सावकार.
सावकार — sāvkār — "a moneylender".
गोष्टीतला सावकार गरीब लोकांना फार त्रास द्यायचा. goṣṭītlā sāvkār garīb lokānnā phār trās dyāycā. The moneylender in the story used to give poor people a lot of trouble.
Masculine.
सावकाराकडून कर्ज घेणे, to borrow from a moneylender.

The word, taken apart.
सावकार (sāvkār) is built on sāv, 'a banker', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit sādhu, 'good' (CDIAL 13337), with the ending -kār, 'one who does'.
