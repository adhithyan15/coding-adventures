Russian, chapter 520: Directors and Actresses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
режиссёр (rezhissyór) — a director (of a film or play).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a premiere, then the Russian for to bow.

You'll want to know: режиссёр.
режиссёр (rezhissyór) — "a director (of a film or play)".
Режиссёр долго выбирал актёров. Rezhissyór dólgo vybirál aktyórov. The director took a long time choosing the actors.
Masculine; also of a woman.

The word, taken apart.
режиссёр (rezhissyór) was borrowed from French régisseur 'a manager, a stage manager' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
актриса (aktrísa) — an actress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to bow, then the Russian for a director.

You'll want to know: актриса.
актриса (aktrísa) — "an actress".
Эта актриса часто играет в комедиях. Éta aktrísa chásto igráyet v komédiyakh. This actress often appears in comedies.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
актриса (aktrísa) is the feminine of актёр 'actor', usually traced to French actrice, ultimately from Latin āctor; EtymDB lists актриса (aktrísa) without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
роль (rol) — a role, a part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a director, then the Russian for an actress.

You'll want to know: роль.
роль (rol) — "a role, a part".
Он играет главную роль в новом фильме. On igráyet glávnuyu rol v nóvom fílme. He plays the lead role in the new film.
Feminine; genitive роли.

The word, taken apart.
роль (rol) was borrowed from French rôle and German Rolle, which go back to Latin rotulus 'a small roll, a scroll' (EtymDB), the actor's roll of lines.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: зритель.
зритель (zrítel) — "a viewer, a spectator".
Фильм понравился зрителям. Film ponrávilsya zrítelyam. Viewers liked the film.
Masculine; feminine зрительница. Зрительный зал — the auditorium.

The word, taken apart.
зритель (zrítel) is built on зреть 'to see' with -тель; EtymDB lists зритель (zrítel) without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
сниматься (snimátsya) — to act in a film; to have one's photo taken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a role, then the Russian for a viewer.

You'll want to know: сниматься.
сниматься (snimátsya) — "to act in a film; to have one's photo taken".
Она снимается в кино с детства. Oná snimáyetsya v kinó s détstva. She has been acting in films since childhood.
Imperfective; perfective сняться (снимусь). Сниматься в фильме — to be in a film.

The word, taken apart.
сниматься (snimátsya) is the reflexive of снимать 'to take off; to film', built on с- and the root -ним-/-ня- 'to take'; EtymDB has no entry for the reflexive.
