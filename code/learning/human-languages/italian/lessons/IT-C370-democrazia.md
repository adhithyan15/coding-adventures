## You'll want to know: la democrazia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C370-MAGGIORANZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la democrazia** — "the democracy".

> In una democrazia tutti possono votare.
>
> *In a democracy everyone can vote.*

Stress on *-zìa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*democrazia* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *dēmocratia*, from Greek *dēmokratía* "rule by the people" (EtymDB), built from *dêmos* "the people" and *krátos* "strength, power".
