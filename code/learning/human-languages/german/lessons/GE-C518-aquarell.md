## You'll want to know: das Aquarell
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C518-MALEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Aquarell** — "watercolour".

> Im Urlaub malt mein Vater Aquarelle von der Küste.
>
> *On holiday my father paints watercolours of the coast.*

Plural: *die Aquarelle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Aquarell* came from French *aquarelle* (EtymDB), from Italian *acquarella*, built on Latin *aqua* "water".
