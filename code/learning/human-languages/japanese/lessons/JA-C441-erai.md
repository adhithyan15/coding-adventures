## You'll want to know: えらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C441-PROUD441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**えらい** — *erai* — "admirable; great".

> **まいにち べんきょうして、えらいですね。** — *mainichi benkyō shite, erai desu ne* — "You study every day: well done."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えらい** (*erai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
