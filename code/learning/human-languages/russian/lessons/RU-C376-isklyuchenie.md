## You'll want to know: исключение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C376-ISKLYUCHENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**исключение** (*isklyuchéniye*) — "an exception".

> Все пришли, за исключением одного студента.
>
> *Vse prishlí, za isklyuchéniyem odnogó studénta.*
>
> Everyone came except for one student.

Neuter. *За исключением* + genitive — except for. *Без исключения* — without exception.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**исключение** is built on *исключить* "to exclude", from *из-* "out" and *ключ* "a key", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*ključĭ*: to lock someone out.
