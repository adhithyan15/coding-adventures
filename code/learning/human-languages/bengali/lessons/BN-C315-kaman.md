## You'll want to know: কামান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C315-OSTRO315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**কামান** — *kāmān* — "a cannon".

> দুর্গের ছাদে একটা পুরনো কামান আছে।
>
> *durger chhāde ekṭā purôno kāmān āchhe.*
>
> There is an old cannon on the roof of the fort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কামান** (*kāmān*) is usually traced to Persian *kamān*, 'a bow'; the name passed from the bow to the gun.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
