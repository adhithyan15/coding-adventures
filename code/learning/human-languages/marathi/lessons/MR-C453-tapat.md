## You'll want to know: तापट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C453-TAPAT453-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तापट** — *tāpaṭ* — "hot-tempered".

> आमचे मामा थोडे तापट आहेत.
>
> *āmce māmā thoḍe tāpaṭ āhet.*
>
> Our uncle is a bit hot-tempered.

Unchanging. Built on ताप, heat or fever: a temper that heats up fast. Close to रागीट.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तापट** (*tāpaṭ*) is a Marathi derivation of ताप, "heat, fever", which goes back to Sanskrit *tāpa*, "heat, glow" (CDIAL 5767), with the ending *-aṭ*.
