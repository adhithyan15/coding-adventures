# খণ্ডন করা (khônḍôn kôrā) — to refute

## You'll want to know: খণ্ডন করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C338-DOLIL338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**খণ্ডন করা** — *khônḍôn kôrā* — "to refute".

> মীরা প্রমাণ দিয়ে অভিযোগটা খণ্ডন করল।
>
> *mīrā prômān diye ôbhijogṭā khônḍôn kôrlô.*
>
> Mira refuted the charge with evidence.

Past (he/she …): তিনি যুক্তিটা খণ্ডন করলেন, *tini juktiṭā khônḍôn kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খণ্ডন করা** (*khônḍôn kôrā*) is Sanskrit *khaṇḍana*, breaking into pieces and, in argument, refuting (Monier-Williams), with করা: to break a claim apart.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
