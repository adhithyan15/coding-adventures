## You'll want to know: वर्त्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C409-CAKRA409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्त्म** — *vartma* — "a wheel track; a rut".

> पंके शकटस्य गभीरं वर्त्म अस्ति।
>
> *paṅke śakaṭasya gabhīraṃ vartma asti.*
>
> There is a deep cart track in the mud.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्त्म** (*vartma*) comes from the root √vṛt, "to turn, to roll", which also gives *vartate*, "is present"; MW gives *vartman* as the track or rut of a wheel, a path, a road (MW).
