German, chapter 488: Wieder gesund.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
anstecken — to infect, to pass on (an illness).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to treat, then the German for to prescribe.

You'll want to know: anstecken.
anstecken — "to infect, to pass on (an illness)".
Mein Bruder hat mich mit seiner Grippe angesteckt. My brother gave me his flu.
Separable: er steckt mich an. Sich anstecken — to catch something. Also to pin on: eine Brosche anstecken.

The word, taken apart.
anstecken is built on an "on" and stecken "to put, to stick", inherited from Old High German steckōn (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Infektion — infection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to prescribe, then the German for to infect.

You'll want to know: die Infektion.
die Infektion — "infection".
Gegen die Infektion bekam sie ein Antibiotikum. She got an antibiotic for the infection.
Plural: die Infektionen.

The word, taken apart.
Infektion is a learned word built on infectus, the past participle of Latin inficere "to dip in, to stain, to taint" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
impfen — to vaccinate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to infect, then the German for infection.

You'll want to know: impfen.
impfen — "to vaccinate".
Vor der Reise lassen wir uns gegen Grippe impfen. Before the trip we are getting vaccinated against flu.
Sich impfen lassen — to get vaccinated. The noun: die Impfung.

The word, taken apart.
impfen is inherited from Old High German impfōn, impitōn "to graft a plant" (EtymDB), usually traced to an early borrowing from a Late Latin word for grafting; English imp, first "a graft", is a cousin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
erholen — to get some rest; to recover (sich erholen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for infection, then the German for to vaccinate.

You'll want to know: erholen.
erholen — "to get some rest; to recover (sich erholen)".
Im Urlaub habe ich mich gut erholt. I had a good rest on holiday.
Reflexive: sich erholen von — to recover from.

The word, taken apart.
erholen is built on er- and holen "to fetch", which is inherited from Middle High German holen (EtymDB): to fetch your strength back.

Lesson 5 of 5.
genesen — to recover, to get well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to vaccinate, then the German for to get some rest.

You'll want to know: genesen.
genesen — "to recover, to get well".
Nach zwei Wochen war der Patient wieder genesen. After two weeks the patient had recovered.
Formal. Perfekt with sein: Er ist genesen.

The word, taken apart.
genesen is inherited from Old High German ginesan, from Proto-Germanic ganesaną, built on nesaną, which goes back to a root meaning "to return, to heal" (EtymDB).
