Lesson 1 of 6.
દરમિયાન (darmiyān) — during.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a mention, then the Gujarati for to behold.

You'll want to know: દરમિયાન.
દરમિયાન — darmiyān — "during".
વરસાદ દરમિયાન વીજળી ગઈ. varsād darmiyān vījḷī gaī. The power went off during the rain.

The word, taken apart.
દરમિયાન (darmiyān) is usually traced to Persian darmiyān, "in the middle, between". It follows its noun: વરસાદ દરમિયાન, during the rain.

હાલ — hāl — "at present".
હાલ તે દવાખાનામાં છે. hāl te davākhānāmā̃ che. At present he is in hospital.

The word, taken apart.
હાલ (hāl) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥāl, "a state, the present", the word behind હાલત. It is the news word for "now": હાલ તે સલામત છે.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ગુપ્ત (gupt) — secret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for at present, then the Gujarati for according to.

You'll want to know: ગુપ્ત.
ગુપ્ત — gupt — "secret".
પોલીસે આ માહિતી ગુપ્ત રાખી. polīse ā māhitī gupt rākhī. The police kept this information secret.

The word, taken apart.
ગુપ્ત (gupt) is a tatsama, Sanskrit gupta, "protected, guarded", and so "hidden" (Monier-Williams). A thing is kept secret with રાખવું: ગુપ્ત રાખી.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ટકવું (ṭakvũ) — to last; to hold out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for according to, then the Gujarati for secret.

You'll want to know: ટકવું.
ટકવું — ṭakvũ — "to last; to hold out".
હડતાળ ત્રણ દિવસ ટકી. haḍtāḷ traṇ divas ṭakī. The strike lasted three days.
Past (it …): તે મકાન ટક્યું, te makān ṭakyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ટકવું (ṭakvũ) comes from ṭakk, a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati ṭakvũ, "to stop" (CDIAL 5420); today it is to stay standing, to last: ત્રણ દિવસ ટકી.
