Hindi, chapter 400: Reservations and Berths.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आरक्षण (ārakṣaṇ) — a reservation (of a seat).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a whistle, then the Hindi for to slide.

You'll want to know: आरक्षण.
आरक्षण — ārakṣaṇ — "a reservation (of a seat)".
मेरे पास इस ट्रेन में आरक्षण है। mere pās is ṭren meṁ ārakṣaṇ hai. I have a reservation on this train.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आरक्षण (ārakṣaṇ) is a learned word built from Sanskrit ā- and rakṣaṇa, "guarding, protecting" (Monier-Williams). A reserved seat is one kept safe for you; the railway sense is modern.

मेरी बर्थ ऊपर है और मीरा की नीचे। merī barth ūpar hai aur Mīrā kī nīce. My berth is the upper one and Mira's is the lower one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रतीक्षा (pratīkṣā) — waiting; a wait (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a reservation (of a seat), then the Hindi for a berth.

You'll want to know: प्रतीक्षा.
प्रतीक्षा — pratīkṣā — "waiting; a wait (formal)".
हम दो घंटे से ट्रेन की प्रतीक्षा कर रहे हैं। ham do ghaṇṭe se ṭren kī pratīkṣā kar rahe haiṁ. We have been waiting for the train for two hours.
Feminine.
Station signs read प्रतीक्षालय, the waiting room; a ticket on the waiting list is on the प्रतीक्षा (pratīkṣā) सूची.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतीक्षा (pratīkṣā) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit word pratīkṣā, "expectation, looking out for" (Monier-Williams), kept unchanged. It belongs to signs and announcements; in talk people say इंतज़ार.

Lesson 4 of 5.
जाँचना (jā̃cnā) — to check; to examine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a berth, then the Hindi for waiting.

You'll want to know: जाँचना.
जाँचना — jā̃cnā — "to check; to examine".
एक आदमी ने हमारे टिकट जाँचे। ek ādmī ne hamāre ṭikaṭ jā̃ce. A man checked our tickets.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने टिकट जाँचा, usne ṭikaṭ jā̃cā.

The word, taken apart.
जाँचना (jā̃cnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit yācyate, "is asked" (CDIAL 10449), which lists Hindi jā̃cnā, "to test". Asking turned into questioning a thing until you know it is right.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रवाना (ravānā) — setting off; on its way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for waiting, then the Hindi for to check.

You'll want to know: रवाना.
रवाना — ravānā — "setting off; on its way".
ट्रेन सुबह छह बजे रवाना हुई। ṭren subah chah baje ravānā huī. The train set off at six in the morning.
रवाना (ravānā) होना, to set off; रवाना (ravānā) करना, to send off.

The word, taken apart.
रवाना (ravānā) is usually traced to Persian ravāna, "going, flowing, setting out". It does not change for gender: ट्रेन रवाना हुई, the train left.
