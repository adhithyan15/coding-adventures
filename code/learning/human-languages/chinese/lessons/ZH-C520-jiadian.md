## You'll want to know: 家电
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C520-APPLIANCE520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**家电** — *jiādiàn* — "household appliance".

> **我们去年买了一些新家电** *wǒmen qùnián mǎi le yìxiē xīn jiādiàn* — We bought some new household appliances last year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**家电** (*jiādiàn*) is built from 家 'home' and 电 'electricity', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kae* and *denH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary explains the second as the flash when yin and yang clash, lightning; the sense 'electricity' is modern.
