## You'll want to know: पराक्रम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C332-SHAURYA332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पराक्रम** — *parākram* — "a heroic deed; valour".

> लोक आजही त्याच्या पराक्रमाच्या कथा सांगतात.
>
> *lok ājhī tyācyā parākramācyā kathā sāṅgtāt.*
>
> Stories of his valour are still told today.

Masculine.

पराक्रम गाजवणे, to perform great deeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पराक्रम** (*parākram*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *parākrama*, 'a bold advance, heroism' (Monier-Williams).
