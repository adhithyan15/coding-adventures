# हूं बोलूं हूं — what I do

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C57-VERBS57-03, MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-05, MW-LEX-C95-TIME95-04, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *to speak* and *to hear*. (**बोलणो**, **सुणणो**.) Then *every day*,
**रोज**, and *I am here*.

## Grammar Lens: the stem, -ूं and हूं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C57-VERBS57-03, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01] -->

Take **-णो** off **बोलणो** and **बोल** is left: the stem. Give it **-ूं**, then end
on **हूं**:

> **हूं थोड़ु बोलूं हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ thoṛu bolū̃ hū̃.* — I speak a little.

The **-ूं** sounds like **हूं**, *I*: an easy way to hear that the sentence is
about you. The same two pieces fit any stem that ends in a consonant: **सुणूं हूं**,
**देखूं हूं**. This is central Marwari as this book writes it; other districts
differ.
