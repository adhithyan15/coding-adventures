Hindi, chapter 192: Every day, Sometimes, Always, Often, Never.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
रोज़ (roz) — every day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a moment, then the Hindi for a minute.

You'll want to know: रोज़.
रोज़ — roz — "every day".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: roz]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: roz, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say roz]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for a moment, then the Hindi for a minute, then say roz again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does रोज़ (roz) mean? ("Every day".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: a floor, a key, a cupboard, a mirror, rain]
