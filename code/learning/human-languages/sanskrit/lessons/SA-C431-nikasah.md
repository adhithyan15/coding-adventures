## You'll want to know: निकषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C431-ARGHA431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निकषः** — *nikaṣaḥ* — "a touchstone".

> स्वर्णकारः निकषे सुवर्णं घर्षति।
>
> *svarṇakāraḥ nikaṣe suvarṇaṃ gharṣati.*
>
> The goldsmith rubs the gold on the touchstone.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निकषः** (*nikaṣaḥ*) is built from *ni* and the root √kaṣ, "to rub"; MW gives *nikaṣa* as rubbing in and as the touchstone, the stone on which gold is rubbed to test it (MW).
