# Came and got up — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C160-AYO-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *Ram came yesterday*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C160-AYO-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C160-PAST-YO-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GAYO-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GHARAI-01, MW-LEX-C158-ASK158-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *Ram came yesterday*; *mother came yesterday*; *everyone came in the evening*]
- [YOU SAY: *I got up early in the morning*, as a man and then as a woman]
- [YOU SAY: *everyone sat down*; *Ram reached home*]
- [YOU SAY: *when did Ram come?*; *he came from the market*]
- [YOU SAY: *mother went home*; *he didn't get up*]
