## You'll want to know: زود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C432-WAQUD432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**زود** — *zawwada* — "to supply; to fill up (with)".

> زود العامل السيارة بالبنزين.
>
> *zawwada al-ʿāmil as-sayyāra bi-l-banzīn.*
>
> The worker filled the car up with petrol.

Present: يزود, *yuzawwid*.

It takes *bi-* before the thing supplied.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زود** (*zawwada*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-w-d*, "provisions for a journey" (Lane). It is Form II, "to give someone provisions": the root of *zād*, food for the road.
