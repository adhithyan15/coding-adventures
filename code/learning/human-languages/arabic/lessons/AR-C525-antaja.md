## You'll want to know: أنتج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C525-TASMIM525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أنتج** — *antaja* — "to produce".

> ينتج المصنع ألف سيارة كل شهر.
>
> *yuntiju al-maṣnaʿ alf sayyāra kull shahr.*
>
> The factory produces a thousand cars every month.

Present: ينتج, *yuntij*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أنتج** (*antaja*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-t-j*, "to bring forth young" (Lane). Natīja, a result, which you know, is from the same root: what a thing brings forth.
