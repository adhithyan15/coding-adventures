## You'll want to know: 改
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C392-NEWPLAN392-01]; assesses=[] -->

**改** — *gǎi* — "to alter; to amend".

> **时间改了我们下午三点见面** *shíjiān gǎi le wǒmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn jiànmiàn* — The time's changed: we're meeting at three in the afternoon.

The new character, in pieces: 改 — seven strokes in two pieces: on the left a bar that turns down, a short bar, and a vertical that bends right and flicks up; on the right the right piece of 做. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**改** (*gǎi*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *kojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to change'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
