## You'll want to know: पुत्रिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C517-CATURANGA517-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुत्रिका** — *putrikā* — "a doll".

> बालिका पुत्रिकया क्रीडति।
>
> *bālikā putrikayā krīḍati.*
>
> The girl plays with a doll.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुत्रिका** (*putrikā*) is built on *putra*, "a son, a child", with the ending *-ikā*; MW gives *putrikā* as a daughter, and as a puppet, a doll (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
