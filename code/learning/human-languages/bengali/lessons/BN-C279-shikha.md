## You'll want to know: শিখা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C279-SHIKHA279-02]; assesses=[] -->

**শিখা** — *shikhā* — "a flame".

> মোমবাতির শিখা বাতাসে কাঁপছিল।
>
> *mombātir shikhā bātāse kā̃pchhilô.*
>
> The candle flame was flickering in the breeze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শিখা** (*shikhā*) is Sanskrit *śikhā*, a tuft of hair or a crest (Monier-Williams); the pointed top of a fire is its crest, its flame.
