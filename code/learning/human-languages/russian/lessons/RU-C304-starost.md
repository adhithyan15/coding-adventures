## You'll want to know: старость
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C304-DETSTVO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**старость** (*stárost*) — "old age".

> В старости он жил у дочери.
>
> *V stárosti on zhil u dócheri.*
>
> In his old age he lived with his daughter.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*старость* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *starostĭ*, built on *starŭ* "old", which EtymDB traces to PIE *steh₂-ro-* "large, old".
