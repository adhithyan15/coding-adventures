## You'll want to know: なぜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C318-SETSUMEI318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**なぜ** — *naze* — "for what reason; why".

> **なぜ はんたいしますか。** — *naze hantai shimasu ka* — "Why are you against it?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なぜ** (*naze*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
