## You'll want to know: le sanglier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C459-ANIMAUX-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le sanglier** — "the wild boar".

> Les sangliers sortent du bois le soir.
>
> *The wild boar come out of the wood in the evening.*

*La laie* is the female.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sanglier* goes back to Latin *singulāris* 'solitary', as in *singulāris porcus* 'the pig that lives alone', from *singulus* 'single' (Lewis & Short).
