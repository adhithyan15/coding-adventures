## You'll want to know: جھکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C359-BAVUJUD359-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جھکنا** — *jhuknā* — "to give in; to bow".

> مزدور حکومت کے سامنے نہیں جھکے۔
>
> *mazdūr hukūmat ke sāmne nahīṅ jhuke.*
>
> The workers did not give in to the government.

Past (he …): وہ آخر میں جھک گیا, *voh ākhir meṅ jhuk gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھکنا** (*jhuknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhuknā* under *\*jhukkati*, "stoops", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5399).
