## You'll want to know: سوراخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C496-VARKSHAP496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سوراخ** — *surākh* — "a hole".

> دیوار میں ایک چھوٹا سوراخ ہے۔
>
> *dīvār meṅ ek chhoṭā surākh hai.*
>
> There is a small hole in the wall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سوراخ** (*surākh*) is usually traced to Persian *sūrākh*, "a hole, an opening".
