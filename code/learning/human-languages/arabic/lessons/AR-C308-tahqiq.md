## You'll want to know: تحقيق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C308-TAHQIQ308-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقيق** — *taḥqīq* — "an investigation, an inquiry".

> بدأت الشرطة تحقيقا في الحادث.
>
> *badaʾat ash-shurṭa taḥqīqan fī al-ḥādith.*
>
> The police opened an investigation into the accident.

Plural تحقيقات. In the press, a تحقيق is an investigative report.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقيق** (*taḥqīq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-q-q* (Lane), the root of *ḥaqq*, truth and right. *Taḥqīq* is the verbal noun of Form II: "making sure of the truth".
