## You'll want to know: तुहिनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C441-DURDINA441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तुहिनम्** — *tuhinam* — "frost; hoarfrost".

> शिशिरे प्रातः तृणेषु तुहिनम् अस्ति।
>
> *śiśire prātaḥ tṛṇeṣu tuhinam asti.*
>
> In late winter there is frost on the grass in the morning.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तुहिनम्** (*tuhinam*) is the word MW gives for frost, cold, mist, dew and snow; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
