## You'll want to know: डाक्टर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C107-DOCTOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**डाक्टर** (*ḍākṭar*) — "a doctor", English borrowed whole.

**डाक्टर कहाँ हैं?** — "where is the doctor?", with the respectful plural **हैं** for a person
you are asking after.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The person to ask for.
