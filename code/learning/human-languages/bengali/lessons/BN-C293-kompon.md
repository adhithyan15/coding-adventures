## You'll want to know: কম্পন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C293-BHUMIKOMPO293-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কম্পন** — *kômpôn* — "a tremor; a shaking".

> প্রথমে একটা ছোট কম্পন হল, তারপর একটা বড় কম্পন।
>
> *prôthôme ekṭā chhoṭo kômpôn hôlô, tārpôr ekṭā bôṛo kômpôn.*
>
> First there was a small tremor, then a big one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কম্পন** (*kômpôn*) is Sanskrit *kampana*, 'trembling, shaking' (Monier-Williams), the noun beside *kampa* in **ভূমিকম্প**.
