Telugu, chapter 368: Written in Stone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
శాసనం (śāsanaṁ) — an inscription; an edict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for remains, then the Telugu for to unearth.

You'll want to know: శాసనం.
శాసనం — śāsanaṁ — "an inscription; an edict".
ఆ రాతి మీద ఒక పాత శాసనం ఉంది. ā rāti mīda oka pāta śāsanaṁ undi. There is an old inscription on that stone.
Kings carved their orders and gifts on stone and copper.

The word, taken apart.
శాసనం (śāsanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit śāsana, "a command; a royal edict" (CDIAL 12420).

Lesson 2 of 5.
తాళపత్రం (tāḷapatraṁ) — a palm-leaf manuscript.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to unearth, then the Telugu for an inscription.

You'll want to know: తాళపత్రం.
తాళపత్రం — tāḷapatraṁ — "a palm-leaf manuscript".
మా తాతగారి దగ్గర పాత తాళపత్రాలు ఉన్నాయి. mā tātagāri daggara pāta tāḷapatrālu unnāyi. Grandfather has old palm-leaf manuscripts.
Plural: తాళపత్రాలు, tāḷapatrālu.
తాళ, the palmyra palm, and పత్రం, a leaf: the books of the days before paper.

The word, taken apart.
తాళపత్రం (tāḷapatraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit: tāla, "the palmyra palm", and pattra, "a leaf" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
చరిత్రకారుడు (caritrakāruḍu) — a historian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an inscription, then the Telugu for a palm-leaf manuscript.

You'll want to know: చరిత్రకారుడు.
చరిత్రకారుడు — caritrakāruḍu — "a historian".
చరిత్రకారుడు ఆ శాసనం చదివాడు. caritrakāruḍu ā śāsanaṁ cadivāḍu. The historian read the inscription.
Plural: చరిత్రకారులు, caritrakārulu.
చరిత్ర, history, with -కారుడు, one who makes: compare కళాకారుడు, an artist.

The word, taken apart.
చరిత్రకారుడు (caritrakāruḍu) is built on Sanskrit caritra, "going; behaviour" (CDIAL 4691), the source of చరిత్ర, with -kāruḍu, "one who does".

You'll want to know: నాగరికత.
నాగరికత — nāgarikata — "a civilisation".
ఆ నది ఒడ్డున ఒక పురాతన నాగరికత ఉండేది. ā nadi oḍḍuna oka purātana nāgarikata uṇḍēdi. An ancient civilisation once stood on the bank of that river.
Built on నగరం, a city: the life of cities.

The word, taken apart.
నాగరికత (nāgarikata) is built on Sanskrit nāgarika, "born or living in a town, civic" (Monier-Williams), from nagara, "a city", with the noun ending -ta.

Lesson 5 of 5.
యుగం (yugaṁ) — an era; an age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a historian, then the Telugu for a civilisation.

You'll want to know: యుగం.
యుగం — yugaṁ — "an era; an age".
ఇది కంప్యూటర్ల యుగం. idi kampyūṭarla yugaṁ. This is the age of computers.
Plural: యుగాలు, yugālu.
The old stories count time in యుగాలు, ages; today the word also names an era of history.

The word, taken apart.
యుగం (yugaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit yuga (CDIAL 10482), whose first sense is "a yoke"; it came to mean an age of the world.
