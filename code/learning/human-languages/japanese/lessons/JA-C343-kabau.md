## You'll want to know: かばう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C343-SHINYOU343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かばう** — *kabau* — "to stand up for (someone)".

> **ちちが おこったので、あには おとうとを かばいました。** — *chichi ga okotta node, ani wa otōto o kabaimashita* — "My father got angry, so my older brother stood up for my younger brother."

Polite **かばいます**, *kabaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あねが いもうとを かばいました。**, *ane ga imōto o kabaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かばう** (*kabau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
