## You'll want to know: ఆధిపత్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C433-VYUHAM433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆధిపత్యం** — *ādhipatyaṁ* — "dominance; supremacy".

> ఆ ఆటలో వాళ్ళ ఆధిపత్యం కొనసాగింది.
>
> *ā āṭalō vāḷḷa ādhipatyaṁ konasāgindi.*
>
> Their dominance in that game continued.

ఆధిపత్యం చెలాయించు, to lord it over others.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆధిపత్యం** (*ādhipatyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ādhipatya*, "supremacy, sovereignty, power" (Monier-Williams), from *adhipati*, "a ruler".
