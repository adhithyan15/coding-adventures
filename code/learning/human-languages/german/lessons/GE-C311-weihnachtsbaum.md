## You'll want to know: der Weihnachtsbaum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C311-ADVENT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Weihnachtsbaum** — "the Christmas tree".

> Der Weihnachtsbaum stand im Wohnzimmer.
>
> *The Christmas tree stood in the living room.*

Plural: *die Weihnachtsbäume*. In the south and in Austria also *der Christbaum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Weihnachtsbaum* joins *Weihnachten* "Christmas" and *Baum* "tree", Proto-Germanic *baumaz*, a cousin of English *beam* (EtymDB).
