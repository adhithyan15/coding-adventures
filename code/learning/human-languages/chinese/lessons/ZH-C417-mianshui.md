## You'll want to know: miǎnshuì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C417-CUSTOMS417-05]; assesses=[] -->

*miǎnshuì* — "duty-free; tax-free".

> *jīchǎng lǐ yǒu yì jiā miǎnshuì de shāngdiàn* — There's a duty-free shop in the airport.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miǎnshuì* is built from *miǎn* 'to be spared' and *shuì* 'a tax', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjenX* and *sywejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to put off, to escape' and the second 'a levy'.
