## You'll want to know: खोजना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C294-TAFTISH294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खोजना** — *khojnā* — "to search for".

> पुलिस ने पूरे गाँव में चोर को खोजा।
>
> *pulis ne pūre gā̃v meṁ cor ko khojā.*
>
> The police searched the whole village for the thief.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चाबी खोजी, *usne cābī khojī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from *\*khōjja*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3929), with the sense of following a track. It is close to **ढूँढना**, to look for; **खोजना** is the slower, more thorough search.
