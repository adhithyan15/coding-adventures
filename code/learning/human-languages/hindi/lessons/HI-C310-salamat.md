## You'll want to know: सलामत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C310-BICHHUD310-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सलामत** — *salāmat* — "safe and sound".

> आख़िर में बच्चा सलामत मिला।
>
> *ākhir meṁ baccā salāmat milā.*
>
> In the end the child was found safe and sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सलामत** (*salāmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *salāma*, "safety". It describes a person who has come through unhurt.
