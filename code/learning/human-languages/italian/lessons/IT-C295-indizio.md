## You'll want to know: l'indizio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C295-INDAGINE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'indizio** — "the clue".

> Non ci sono indizi: il caso è difficile.
>
> *There are no clues: the case is a hard one.*

Detective stories live on *indizi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indizio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *indicium* "a sign, a mark" (EtymDB), from *index* "one who points out".
