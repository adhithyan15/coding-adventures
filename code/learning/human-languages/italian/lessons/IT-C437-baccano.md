## You'll want to know: il baccano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C437-RUMORE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il baccano** — "the racket, the uproar".

> I vicini fanno un baccano tremendo.
>
> *The neighbours are making a dreadful racket.*

Informal; *fare baccano* — to make a racket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*baccano* is built on *baccanale* 'a wild party', a learned borrowing from Latin *bacchānal*, a feast of the Greek wine god (EtymDB).
