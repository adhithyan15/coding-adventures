## You'll want to know: सरदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C327-PRAJA327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सरदार** — *sardār* — "a chieftain; a noble".

> राजाने शूर सरदाराला एक गाव भेट दिलं.
>
> *rājāne śūr sardārālā ek gāv bheṭ dilaṁ.*
>
> The king gave the brave chieftain a village as a gift.

Masculine.

सरदाराला, to the chieftain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सरदार** (*sardār*) is a loan from Persian *sardār*, 'a chief, a commander', built from *sar*, 'head', and *-dār*, 'one who holds'.
