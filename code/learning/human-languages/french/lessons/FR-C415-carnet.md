## You'll want to know: le carnet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C415-METRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le carnet** — "the book of tickets; the notebook".

> Elle achète un carnet de dix tickets.
>
> *She buys a book of ten tickets.*

*Un carnet* is also a small notebook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carnet* comes from Middle French *quernet* 'quire of four sheets' (EtymDB), from Latin *quaternī* 'four each'.
