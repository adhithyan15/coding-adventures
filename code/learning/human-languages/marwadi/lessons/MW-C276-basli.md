## You'll want to know: बांसळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C276-BANSLI276-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बांसळी** — *bā̃sḷī* — "a flute".

> म्हारी बांसळी लांबी है।
>
> *mhārī bā̃sḷī lā̃bī hai.*
>
> My flute is long.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बांसळी** (*bā̃sḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vamśī*, "a flute, a pipe", itself from *vamśa*, "bamboo"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati *vā̃sḷī*, "pipe, flute", and Hindi *bãsī*, "flute" (CDIAL 11180).
