## You'll want to know: ઊંડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C307-RUNI307-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંડું** — *ū̃ḍũ* — "deep".

> કૂવો ખૂબ ઊંડો હતો.
>
> *kūvo khūb ū̃ḍo hato.*
>
> The well was very deep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંડું** (*ū̃ḍũ*) comes from *\*ūṇḍa*, "deep", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 2402). It agrees like **મોટું**: **ઊંડો કૂવો**, **ઊંડી નદી**, **ઊંડું પાણી**.
