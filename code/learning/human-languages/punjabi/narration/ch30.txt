[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — one mark.
Add the small dot below: ਫ + ਼ = ਫ਼. This below-letter dot is pairin bindi; here it changes pha to the borrowed sound fa. It is not the above-letter bindi already used in ਹਾਂ.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
Only the base and its below-letter dot are scored here.
[once you have stopped driving — question: Add the below-letter dot to ਫ (pha) and write the result.]
