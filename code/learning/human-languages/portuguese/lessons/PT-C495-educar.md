## You'll want to know: educar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C495-EDUCAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**educar** — "to bring up, to educate".

> Os meus pais educaram-nos com muito carinho.
>
> *My parents brought us up with a lot of love.*

*Bem-educado* — polite, well brought up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*educar* comes from Latin *ēducāre* "to bring up, to rear" (EtymDB).
