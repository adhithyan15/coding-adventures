## You'll want to know: befreundet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C498-FREUNDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**befreundet** — "on friendly terms, friends (with)".

> Ich bin mit meiner Nachbarin seit Jahren befreundet.
>
> *I've been friends with my neighbour for years.*

*Mit jemandem befreundet sein* — to be friends with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*befreundet* is built on *be-* and *Freund* "friend", inherited from Middle High German *vriunt* and Old High German *friunt* (EtymDB).
