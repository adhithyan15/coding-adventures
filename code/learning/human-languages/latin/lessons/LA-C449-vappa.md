## You'll want to know: vappa, vappae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C449-VAPPA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**vappa, vappae** — "flat wine, wine gone off; a good-for-nothing".

> Caupō nōn vīnum bonum sed vappam vendit.
>
> *The innkeeper sells not good wine but flat wine.*

First declension, feminine: *vappa, vappae f.* Wine that has lost its taste; also, of a person, a worthless fellow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vappa* is usually linked with *vapor* 'steam' (Lewis & Short): wine whose spirit has gone up in vapour. Lewis & Short also compare *vapidus* 'flat', the source of English *vapid*.
