# तांई (tā̃ī) — until

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say *he comes from the market*. Then *the shop is
closed*.

## You'll want to know: तांई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-03]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-03] -->

**तांई** — *tā̃ī* — "until".

> **दुकान दिनूगै ६ सूं १० तांई खुली है।**
>
> *dukān dinūgai chha sū̃ das tā̃ī khulī hai.* — The shop is open in the morning from six until ten.

**सूं** opens the stretch of time, and **तांई** closes it. The hours are digits,
read as numbers: **६** is *chha*, **१०** is *das*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तांई** (*tā̃ī*) is probably inherited from Sanskrit *tāvat*, "so long". CDIAL
gives no Marwari form in this entry, but it lists an Old Gujarati word for "up
till" there (CDIAL 5804).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-03, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FIVE-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SIX-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-EIGHT-01] -->

**सूं** … **तांई** frames any stretch: **आथण ५ सूं ८ तांई**, in the evening from
five until eight.
