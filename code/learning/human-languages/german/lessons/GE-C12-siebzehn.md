# siebzehn — "seventeen"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-SECHZEHN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-ZAHL-KLIPPUNG-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Second and last of the clipped ones. After this the rule runs clean
to twenty.

## You'll want to know: siebzehn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-SIEBZEHN-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-SIEBEN-01, GE-GRAMMAR-ZIFFER-PLUS-ZEHN-01] -->

> **siebzehn** — "seventeen." *sieb-* + *zehn*, with the *-en* of *sieben* gone.

*Sieben-zehn* would be four syllables for one number, three of them unstressed.
German trimmed the middle and kept the meaning.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-SIEBZEHN-01] -->

- `ie-long-ee` — *ie* is a long **ee**, as in *sieben*.
- `consonant-b-final` — the *b* stays a *b* here, because it is not really at
  the end of a word: *zehn* is welded on behind it.
- `z-ts` — and *-TSAYN*.

*ZEEB-tsayn*.
