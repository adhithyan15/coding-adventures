## You'll want to know: el callejón
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C675-AFUERAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el callejón** — "the alley, the lane".

> El bar está en un callejón.
>
> *The bar is down an alley.*

*Un callejón sin salida* — a dead end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*callejón* is built on *calleja* "a little street" (EtymDB), and *calle* is inherited from Latin *callis* "a footpath".
