## You'll want to know: der Radfahrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C410-UNFALL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Radfahrer** — "the cyclist".

> In der Stadt gibt es viele Radfahrer.
>
> *There are lots of cyclists in the town.*

Same form in the plural. A woman: *die Radfahrerin*. *Rad fahren* — to cycle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Radfahrer* is built from *Rad* 'wheel', short for *Fahrrad* 'bicycle', and *Fahrer* 'rider, driver', from the inherited verb *fahren* (EtymDB).
