## You'll want to know: servieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C329-ANRICHTEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**servieren** — "to serve (food)".

> Der Kellner servierte die Suppe heiß.
>
> *The waiter served the soup hot.*

Regular, without *ge-*: *hat serviert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*servieren* was borrowed from French *servir*, from Latin *servīre* "to serve" (EtymDB).
