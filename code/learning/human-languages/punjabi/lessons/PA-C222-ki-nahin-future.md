# ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋਗੇ? — will you work tomorrow?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *what will you do tomorrow?* Then *are you a traveller?*

## Grammar Lens: ਕੀ and ਨਹੀਂ with the future
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-NEG-QUESTION-222]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221] -->

Nothing moves. **ਕੀ** goes in front to ask yes or no; **ਨਹੀਂ** goes before the
verb to say no:

> **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋਗੇ?** — *kī tusīṁ kallh kamm karoge?* — Will you work tomorrow?

> **ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰਾਂਗੀ।** — *nahīṁ jī, maiṁ kallh kamm nahīṁ karāṅgī.* — No, I won't work tomorrow.

> **ਕੀ ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ?** — *kī Aman kallh āvegā?* — Will Aman come tomorrow?
