Lesson 1 of 6.
la liquidación — the clearance sale; the settlement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the inflation, then the Spanish for the trade.

You'll want to know: la liquidación.
la liquidación — "the clearance sale; the settlement".
La tienda cierra y hay liquidación. The shop is closing and there is a clearance sale.
Also the final pay you receive when you leave a job.

The word, taken apart.
liquidación is built on liquidar, which comes from líquido, a learned borrowing from Latin liquidus 'fluid, clear' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 6.
la rebaja — the price cut; the sales.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the trade, then the Spanish for the clearance sale.

You'll want to know: la rebaja.
la rebaja — "the price cut; the sales".
En enero empiezan las rebajas. The sales start in January.
In Spain las rebajas are the seasonal sales; Latin America often says las ofertas or las liquidaciones.

The word, taken apart.
rebaja is built on rebajar 'to lower', from bajo 'low', which is inherited from Late Latin bassus 'low' (EtymDB).
