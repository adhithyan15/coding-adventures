## You'll want to know: mītigō, mītigāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C511-CRUD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**mītigō, mītigāre** — "to ease, soften, calm".

> Vīnum dolōrem mītigat.
>
> *Wine eases pain.*

*mītigō, mītigāre, mītigāvī, mītigātum*: *mītis* 'mild' + *agō* 'to make'. English *mitigate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mītigō** is built on *mītis* "mild" and *agō* "to drive, make" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *mitigate* comes from it.
