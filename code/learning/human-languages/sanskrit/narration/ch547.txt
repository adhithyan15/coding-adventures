Sanskrit, chapter 547: Ups and Downs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उत्थानम् (utthānam) — a rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an attainment, then the Sanskrit for a consequence.

You'll want to know: उत्थानम्.
उत्थानम् — utthānam — "a rise".
यदि देशस्य उत्थानं भवति, तर्हि जनाः प्रसन्नाः भविष्यन्ति। yadi deśasya utthānaṃ bhavati, tarhi janāḥ prasannāḥ bhaviṣyanti. If the country rises, the people will be glad.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
उत्थानम् (utthānam) is built on ud, "up", and the root √sthā, "to stand", which also gives uttiṣṭhati; MW gives utthāna as standing up, rising (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अवनतिः (avanatiḥ) — a decline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a consequence, then the Sanskrit for a rise.

You'll want to know: अवनतिः.
अवनतिः — avanatiḥ — "a decline".
यदि व्यापारस्य अवनतिः भवति, तर्हि वणिक् दुःखी भवति। yadi vyāpārasya avanatiḥ bhavati, tarhi vaṇik duḥkhī bhavati. If trade declines, the merchant is unhappy.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अवनतिः (avanatiḥ) is built on ava, "down", and the root √nam, "to bow", which also gives namati; MW gives avanati as bowing down, stooping; "a decline", of trade or fortune, is the modern sense (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
पतनम् (patanam) — a fall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a rise, then the Sanskrit for a decline.

You'll want to know: पतनम्.
पतनम् — patanam — "a fall".
यदि पतनं भवति, तर्हि पुनः उत्तिष्ठतु। yadi patanaṃ bhavati, tarhi punaḥ uttiṣṭhatu. If you fall, get up again.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पतनम् (patanam) comes from the root √pat, "to fall", which also gives patati, with the ending -ana; MW gives patana as flying, falling (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: आरोहः.
आरोहः — ārohaḥ — "a climb; an ascent".
यदि पर्वतस्य आरोहः कठिनः अस्ति, तर्हि मन्दं गच्छतु। yadi parvatasya ārohaḥ kaṭhinaḥ asti, tarhi mandaṃ gacchatu. If the climb up the mountain is hard, go slowly.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आरोहः (ārohaḥ) is built on ā and the root √ruh, "to rise, to grow", which also gives ārohati; MW gives āroha as one who mounts, and as rising, an ascent (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उच्छलति (ucchalati) — springs up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a fall, then the Sanskrit for a climb.

You'll want to know: उच्छलति.
उच्छलति — ucchalati — "springs up".
यदि जले शिला पतति, तर्हि जलम् उच्छलति। yadi jale śilā patati, tarhi jalam ucchalati. If a stone falls into the water, the water splashes up.
Past (it …): जलम् उच्छलितवत्, jalam ucchalitavat.

The word, taken apart.
उच्छलति (ucchalati) is built on ud, "up", and the root √śal, "to move", which after ud is written chal; MW gives ucchal as to fly upwards, to spring up (MW).
