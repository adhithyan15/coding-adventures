## You'll want to know: باک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C423-BENZIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**باک** (*bâk*) — "a fuel tank (of a car)".

> باک ماشین تقریبا خالی است.
>
> *Bâk-e mâshin taqriban khâli ast.*
>
> The car's tank is almost empty.

*Bâk râ por kardan* — to fill the tank. A different, older *bâk* means fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâk* 'fuel tank' was borrowed from Russian and is usually traced to Russian *bak* 'a tank'. EtymDB lists Russian *bak* only with a different, nautical sense, so the line is hedged.
