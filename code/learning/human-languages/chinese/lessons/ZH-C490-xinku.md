## You'll want to know: xīnkǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C490-PRESSURE490-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnkǔ* — "exhausting; toilsome (of work)".

> *gōngzuò yālì dà de shíhou wǒ gǎnjué hěn xīnkǔ* — When the pressure at work is high, I find it really hard going.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnkǔ* is built from *xīn* 'pungent' and *kǔ* 'bitter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sin* and *khuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the taste of ginger and the second 'coarse' and 'toil'.
