French, chapter 309: Les premières années.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la maternité — the maternity hospital; motherhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to breastfeed, then the French for the cradle.

You'll want to know: la maternité.
la maternité — "the maternity hospital; motherhood".
Le bébé est né à la maternité de la ville. The baby was born at the town's maternity hospital.
Le congé de maternité — maternity leave.

The word, taken apart.
maternité is a learned borrowing from Latin māternitās "motherhood" (EtymDB), built on māter "mother".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
le baptême — the christening, the baptism.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the cradle, then the French for the maternity hospital.

You'll want to know: le baptême.
le baptême — "the christening, the baptism".
Toute la famille est venue au baptême. The whole family came to the christening.
The p is silent. Le baptême de l'air — a first flight.

The word, taken apart.
baptême comes through Old French batesme (EtymDB) from Church Latin baptisma, a borrowing from Greek báptisma "a dipping".

You'll want to know: baptiser.
baptiser — "to christen, to baptise; to name".
Le prêtre a baptisé l'enfant dimanche. The priest christened the child on Sunday.
Also to give a name: ils ont baptisé leur bateau.

The word, taken apart.
baptiser comes through Old French baptisier (EtymDB) from Church Latin baptizāre, a borrowing from Greek baptízein "to dip".

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: la poussette.
la poussette — "the pushchair, the stroller".
Elle se promène au parc avec la poussette. She walks in the park with the pushchair.
Built on pousser, to push.

The word, taken apart.
poussette is built on pousser "to push", which is inherited from Latin pulsāre "to beat, to push" (Lewis & Short).
