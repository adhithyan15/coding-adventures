## You'll want to know: die Weide
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C459-BAUERNHOF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Weide** — "the pasture (for animals); the willow".

> Die Kühe stehen den ganzen Tag auf der Weide.
>
> *The cows stand in the pasture all day.*

Plural: *die Weiden*. *Die Weide* is also a willow tree, a different word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Weide* 'pasture' is inherited from Old High German *weida* (EtymDB). *Weide* 'willow' is a separate inherited word, Old High German *wīda* (EtymDB).
