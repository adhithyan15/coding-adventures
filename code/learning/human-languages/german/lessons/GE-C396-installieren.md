## You'll want to know: installieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C396-COMPUTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**installieren** — "to install".

> Mein Sohn hat die App auf meinem Handy installiert.
>
> *My son installed the app on my mobile.*

Perfekt: *hat installiert*. Also of wiring: *eine Lampe installieren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*installieren* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *installāre* (EtymDB), built on *stallum* "a stall, a seat": usually said to have meant first to place someone in a church seat or an office.
