Telugu, chapter 543: At a Performance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కచేరీ (kacērī) — a concert; a recital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sculptor, then the Telugu for a masterpiece.

You'll want to know: కచేరీ.
కచేరీ — kacērī — "a concert; a recital".
శనివారం సాయంత్రం సంగీత కచేరీ జరిగింది. śanivāraṁ sāyantraṁ saṅgīta kacērī jarigindi. There was a music concert on Saturday evening.
Also an old word for a government office.

The word, taken apart.
కచేరీ (kacērī) came into Telugu through Hindustani kacahrī, "a court, an office"; its earlier origin is not settled.

పాట అయ్యాక అందరూ చప్పట్లు కొట్టారు. pāṭa ayyāka andarū cappaṭlu koṭṭāru. When the song ended, everyone clapped.

The word, taken apart.
చప్పట్లు (cappaṭlu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2335 lists Telugu cappaṭlu, "clapping of the hands", with Tamil cappāṇi.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a concert, then the Telugu for applause.

You'll want to know: అభినందించు.
అభినందించు — abhinandiñcu — "to congratulate".
గెలిచిన జట్టును అందరూ అభినందించారు. gelicina jaṭṭunu andarū abhinandiñcāru. Everyone congratulated the winning team.
Past (I …): అభినందించాను, abhinandiñcānu.
అభినందనలు, congratulations.

The word, taken apart.
అభినందించు (abhinandiñcu) is built on Sanskrit abhinandana, "delighting; praising, applauding" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

మేము ఆ సంగీతాన్ని హాయిగా ఆస్వాదించాము. mēmu ā saṅgītānni hāyigā āsvādiñcāmu. We relished that music at our ease.
Past (I …): ఆస్వాదించాను, āsvādiñcānu.
Enjoying food, music or a view slowly.

The word, taken apart.
ఆస్వాదించు (āsvādiñcu) is built on Sanskrit āsvādana, "eating, tasting, enjoying" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 5 of 5.
కోలాటం (kōlāṭaṁ) — a stick dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to congratulate, then the Telugu for to savour.

You'll want to know: కోలాటం.
కోలాటం — kōlāṭaṁ — "a stick dance".
పండుగలో ఆడపిల్లలు కోలాటం ఆడారు. paṇḍugalō āḍapillalu kōlāṭaṁ āḍāru. At the festival the girls did the stick dance.
Dancers strike short sticks in rhythm: కోలు, stick, + ఆటం, play.

The word, taken apart.
కోలాటం (kōlāṭaṁ) is built from కోలు, "a stick", and native āṭaṁ, "play, dance" (DEDR 347).
