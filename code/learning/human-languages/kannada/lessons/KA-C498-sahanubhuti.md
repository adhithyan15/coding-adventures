# ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ (sahānubhūti) — sympathy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C498-NAMBIKE498-03, KA-LEX-C498-NAMBIKE498-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for suspicion, then the Kannada for compassion.

## You'll want to know: ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C498-NAMBIKE498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ** — *sahānubhūti* — "sympathy".

> ನೆರೆಯವರು ನಮ್ಮ ನಷ್ಟಕ್ಕೆ ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ ತೋರಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *nereyavaru namma naṣṭakke sahānubhūti tōrisidaru.*
>
> The neighbours showed sympathy for our loss.

ಸಹ, with + ಅನುಭೂತಿ, feeling: feeling along with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ** (*sahānubhūti*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *saha*, "with", and *anubhūti*, "perception, experience" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
