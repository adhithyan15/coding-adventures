# ふえる (fueru) — to increase; to multiply

## You'll want to know: ふえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C211-ANIMAL211-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ふえる** — *fueru* — "to increase; to multiply".

> **らいねん うさぎが ふえます。** — *rainen usagi ga fuemasu* — "Next year there will be more rabbits."

Polite **ふえます**, *fuemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふえる** (*fueru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
