Urdu, chapter 496: The Workshop.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ورکشاپ (varkshāp) — a workshop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a contractor, then the Urdu for to use up (time or effort).

You'll want to know: ورکشاپ.
ورکشاپ — varkshāp — "a workshop".
ہماری ورکشاپ اسٹیشن کے قریب ہے۔ hamārī varkshāp isṭeshan ke qarīb hai. Our workshop is near the station.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ورکشاپ (varkshāp) is borrowed from English workshop.

Lesson 4 of 5.
چھیدنا (chhednā) — to bore (a hole); to pierce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a clamp, then the Urdu for a hole.

You'll want to know: چھیدنا.
چھیدنا — chhednā — "to bore (a hole); to pierce".
مستری نے دیوار میں دو سوراخ چھیدے۔ mistrī ne dīvār meṅ do surākh chhede. The mechanic bored two holes in the wall.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دروازے میں سوراخ چھیدا, us ne darvāze meṅ surākh chhedā.

The word, taken apart.
چھیدنا (chhednā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin chednā, "to bore", under Sanskrit chidra, "pierced; a hole" (CDIAL 5043).
