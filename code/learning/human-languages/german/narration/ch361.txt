German, chapter 361: Ein neues Land.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
die Einwanderung — immigration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to discriminate against, then the German for equal rights.

You'll want to know: die Einwanderung.
die Einwanderung — "immigration".
Die Stadt ist durch Einwanderung schnell gewachsen. The city has grown quickly through immigration.
From einwandern — to immigrate, with sein. Der Einwanderer — the immigrant.

The word, taken apart.
Einwanderung is the noun of einwandern "to immigrate", built on wandern "to wander", inherited from Old High German wandarōn and Proto-Germanic wandrōną (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 6.
die Flucht — flight, escape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for equal rights, then the German for immigration.

You'll want to know: die Flucht.
die Flucht — "flight, escape".
Nach der Flucht lebte die Familie zwei Jahre in einem Lager. After their flight the family lived in a camp for two years.
Auf der Flucht — on the run. The verb is fliehen — to flee.

The word, taken apart.
Flucht is inherited from Middle High German vluht, Old High German fluht and Proto-Germanic fluhtiz "flight" (EtymDB), the noun of the verb for fleeing.

Lesson 3 of 6.
der Flüchtling — the refugee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for immigration, then the German for flight.

You'll want to know: der Flüchtling.
der Flüchtling — "the refugee".
Die Schule hat zwanzig junge Flüchtlinge aufgenommen. The school has taken in twenty young refugees.
Plural: die Flüchtlinge. Today people also say der Geflüchtete, die Geflüchtete.

The word, taken apart.
Flüchtling is built on flüchten "to flee", from Flucht "flight", Old High German fluht (EtymDB), with the ending -ling for a person.

integrieren — "to integrate".
Der Verein hilft, neue Nachbarn zu integrieren. The club helps to integrate new neighbours.
Sich integrieren — to fit in. Perfekt: hat integriert.

The word, taken apart.
integrieren is a learned borrowing from Latin integrāre "to make whole, to renew" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from integer "whole, untouched".

Lesson 5 of 6.
die Gewalt — violence; power, force.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the refugee, then the German for to integrate.

You'll want to know: die Gewalt.
die Gewalt — "violence; power, force".
Gewalt ist keine Lösung. Violence is no solution.
No plural in this sense. Also power: die Gewalt über das Auto verlieren — to lose control of the car.

The word, taken apart.
Gewalt is inherited from Middle High German gewalt "strength, power" and Old High German giwalt (EtymDB), from Proto-Germanic waldą, built on waldaną "to rule".
