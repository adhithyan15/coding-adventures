Italian, chapter 464: Animali selvatici.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il cinghiale — the wild boar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the tide, then the Italian for to dive.

You'll want to know: il cinghiale.
il cinghiale — "the wild boar".
Di notte i cinghiali scendono in paese. At night the boars come down into the village.
Common in the hills, and a popular sauce for pasta.

The word, taken apart.
cinghiale is usually traced to Late Latin singulāris, short for porcus singulāris 'the pig that lives alone', from singulāris 'alone, solitary' (Lewis & Short); it is inherited, worn down in speech.
