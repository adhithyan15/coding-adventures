# ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ (muccaḷike) — a written undertaking

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C415-AMANATU415-04, KA-LEX-C415-AMANATU415-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to sanction, then the Kannada for an official order.

## You'll want to know: ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C416-PRATIJNE416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ** — *muccaḷike* — "a written undertaking".

> ಮತ್ತೆ ತಪ್ಪು ಮಾಡುವುದಿಲ್ಲ ಅಂತ ಅವನು ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ ಬರೆದುಕೊಟ್ಟನು.
>
> *matte tappu māḍuvudilla anta avanu muccaḷike baredukoṭṭanu.*
>
> He gave a written undertaking not to do wrong again.

ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ ಬರೆದುಕೊಡು, to sign an undertaking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಚ್ಚಳಿಕೆ** (*muccaḷike*) is a Kannada word of the offices and courts whose origin is not settled: neither DEDR nor CDIAL lists it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
