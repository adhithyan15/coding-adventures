## You'll want to know: cozer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C332-FRITAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cozer** — "to cook in water, to boil (food)".

> Cozemos o arroz em água com sal.
>
> *We cook the rice in salted water.*

Not *coser*, to sew; they sound the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cozer* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *cocere*, from Latin *coquere* "to cook" (EtymDB).
