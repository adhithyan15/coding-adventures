## You'll want to know: کاہل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C455-FLAW455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کاہل** — *kāhil* — "slothful".

> چھٹی کے دن میں کاہل ہو جاتا ہوں۔
>
> *chhuṭṭī ke din maiṅ kāhil ho jātā hūṅ.*
>
> On holidays I turn lazy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کاہل** (*kāhil*) is usually traced to Persian *kāhil*, "lazy, slow".
