## You'll want to know: به اشتراک گذاشتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C419-HARIM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**به اشتراک گذاشتن** (*be eshterâk gozâshtan*) — "to share (online)".

> او عکسهای سفرش را به اشتراک گذاشت.
>
> *u 'akshâ-ye safarash râ be eshterâk gozâsht.*
>
> She shared the photos from her trip.

*Eshterâk* "sharing; subscription" + *gozâshtan* "to put" (stem *gozâr-*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به اشتراک گذاشتن** is literally "to put into sharing": *eshterâk* is usually traced to Arabic *ishtirāk* "sharing, taking part", and *gozâshtan* "to put" is native Persian.
