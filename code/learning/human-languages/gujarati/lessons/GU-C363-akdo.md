## You'll want to know: આંકડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C363-ANKDO363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**આંકડો** — *ā̃kḍo* — "a figure (a number)".

> આ આંકડા દર્શાવે છે કે ગામમાં વરસાદ ઓછો થયો.
>
> *ā ā̃kḍā darśāve che ke gāmmā̃ varsād ocho thayo.*
>
> These figures show that the village has had less rain.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આંકડો** (*ā̃kḍo*) is built on Sanskrit *aṅka*, "a mark, a numeral" (Monier-Williams).
