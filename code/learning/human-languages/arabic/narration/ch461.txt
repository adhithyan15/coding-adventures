Arabic, chapter 461: Cancelled and postponed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
إلغاء (ilghāʾ) — a cancellation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to go bad, then the Arabic for to suffer damage.

You'll want to know: إلغاء.
إلغاء — ilghāʾ — "a cancellation".
سمعنا عن إلغاء الرحلة في المطار. samiʿnā ʿan ilghāʾ ar-riḥla fī al-maṭār. We heard about the cancellation of the flight at the airport.

The word, taken apart.
إلغاء (ilghāʾ) is built on the native Arabic root l-gh-w, "to be of no account" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form IV, "to make null".

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.
