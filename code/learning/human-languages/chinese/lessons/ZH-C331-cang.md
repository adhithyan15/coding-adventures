## You'll want to know: cáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C331-MAGIC331-05]; assesses=[] -->

*cáng* — "to hide".

> *yāoguài lái de shíhou tāmen dōu cáng zài mén hòubian* — When the monster came, they all hid behind the door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cáng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to conceal' and 'to keep out of sight'. The dictionary also lists *dzangH*, 'a storehouse', a different word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
