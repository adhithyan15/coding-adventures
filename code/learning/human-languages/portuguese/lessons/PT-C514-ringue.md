## You'll want to know: o ringue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C514-RINGUE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o ringue** — "(boxing) ring".

> Os dois lutadores subiram ao ringue.
>
> *The two fighters climbed into the ring.*

Spelled the Portuguese way: *ringue*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ringue* was borrowed from English *ring* (EtymDB), an old Germanic word for a circle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
