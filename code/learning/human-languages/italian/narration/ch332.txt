Italian, chapter 332: Sul tagliere.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il tagliere — the chopping board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the teaspoon, then the Italian for the bowl.

You'll want to know: il tagliere.
il tagliere — "the chopping board".
Taglia la cipolla sul tagliere. Cut the onion on the chopping board.
Built on tagliare.

The word, taken apart.
tagliere is built on tagliare "to cut", from Late Latin tāliāre "to cut" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
tritare — to chop finely, to mince.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the chopping board, then the Italian for to slice.

You'll want to know: tritare.
tritare — "to chop finely, to mince".
Trita l'aglio e la cipolla. Chop the garlic and the onion finely.
La carne tritata — mince.

The word, taken apart.
tritare is built on trito "worn, ground", from Latin trītus, the past participle of terere "to rub" (Lewis & Short).
