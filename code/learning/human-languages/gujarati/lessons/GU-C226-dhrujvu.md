## You'll want to know: ધ્રૂજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C226-BREATH226-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ધ્રૂજવું** — *dhrūjvũ* — "to shiver, to tremble".

> **શું તમે ઠંડીથી ધ્રૂજો છો?** — *śũ tame ṭhanḍīthī dhrūjo cho?* — "Are you shivering with cold?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધ્રૂજવું** (*dhrūjvũ*) is probably inherited. CDIAL lists a Gujarati *dhujvũ*, "to tremble, be shaken", under Sanskrit *dhūyate*, "is shaken" (CDIAL 6863); the *r* of *dhrūjvũ* is not explained there, so the link is uncertain.
