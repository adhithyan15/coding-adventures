# ू — long *ū*, the deeper hook below

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

You have the short hook. The long one is the same gesture, carried further.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-UU-MATRA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01, MR-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->

> ू

Long *ū* is the short hook drawn **deeper and turned back on itself**, so it
finishes pointing down-left rather than right. Same place, same start, more
travel.

**दू** is *dū* — the first syllable of the word for milk. Say *tu* and *dū* one
after the other and you can hear the length the extra travel is paying for.

## Writing — delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-UU-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **दू** for five seconds, cover it, and write it once. Uncover and
compare the depth of the hook against the short one you wrote a moment ago.
Repair only if the two look the same.
