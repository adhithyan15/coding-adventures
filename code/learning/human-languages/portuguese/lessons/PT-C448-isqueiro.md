## You'll want to know: o isqueiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C448-TABACARIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o isqueiro** — "the lighter (for cigarettes)".

> Ele perdeu o isqueiro outra vez.
>
> *He lost his lighter again.*

Older lighters burned a piece of tinder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*isqueiro* is built on *isca* 'bait, tinder' with *-eiro* (EtymDB); *isca* is inherited from Latin *ēsca* 'food, bait'.
