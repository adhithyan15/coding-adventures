## You'll want to know: amoureux
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C487-AMOUREUX-01]; assesses=[] -->

**amoureux** — "in love; lover".

> Ils sont amoureux depuis le lycée.
>
> *They have been in love since secondary school.*

Feminine *amoureuse*. *Tomber amoureux de quelqu'un* — to fall in love with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amoureux* is inherited from Old French *amoros* (EtymDB), built on *amour*, from Latin *amor* "love" (Lewis & Short).
