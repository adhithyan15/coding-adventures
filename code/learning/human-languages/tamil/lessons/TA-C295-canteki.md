## You'll want to know: சந்தேகி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C295-THUPPU295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்தேகி** — *cantēki* — "to suspect".

> காவலர்கள் அவனுடைய நண்பனைச் சந்தேகித்தார்கள்.
>
> *kāvalarkaḷ avaṉuṭaiya naṇpaṉaic cantēkittārkaḷ.*
>
> The police suspected his friend.

Past (I …): சந்தேகித்தேன், *cantēkittēṉ*.

The one suspected takes -ஐ; the noun is சந்தேகம், a doubt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சந்தேகி** (*cantēki*) is a Tamil verb built on சந்தேகம், "doubt", which is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃdeha*, "doubt, uncertainty" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
