## You'll want to know: পাকস্থলী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C468-HRITPINDO468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পাকস্থলী** — *pākôsthôlī* — "the stomach (the organ)".

> খাবার প্রথমে পাকস্থলীতে যায়।
>
> *khābār prôthôme pākôsthôlīte jāy.*
>
> Food goes first to the stomach.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাকস্থলী** (*pākôsthôlī*) puts Sanskrit *pāka*, "cooking", here digesting, with *sthalī*, "place, ground" (Monier-Williams): the place where food is cooked down.
