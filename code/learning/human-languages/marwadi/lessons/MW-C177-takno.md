## You'll want to know: टांकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C177-PHAD177-03]; assesses=[] -->

**टांकणो** — *ṭā̃kṇo* — "to stitch on".

> मां कमीज माथै बटण टांकै है।
>
> *mā̃ kamīj māthai baṭaṇ ṭā̃kai hai.*
>
> Mother stitches a button onto the shirt.

Past (I …): म्हैं बटण टांक्यो, *mhaĩ baṭaṇ ṭā̃kyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टांकणो** (*ṭā̃kṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ṭaṅkati*, "ties"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ṭā̃knā*, "to stitch, join" (CDIAL 5432). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
