# સાંભરવું (sā̃bharvũ) — to come to mind; to be missed

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for deserted, then the Gujarati for to crave.

## You'll want to know: સાંભરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C458-VIRAH458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંભરવું** — *sā̃bharvũ* — "to come to mind; to be missed".

> રજામાં મને મારું ગામ ઘણું સાંભરે છે.
>
> *rajāmā̃ mane mārũ gām ghaṇũ sā̃bhre che.*
>
> During the holidays I miss my village a lot.

Past (it …): તે ગામ ઘણું સાંભર્યું, *te gām ghaṇũ sā̃bharyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંભરવું** (*sā̃bharvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *saṃsmarati*, "remembers"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to remember" (CDIAL 13057).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
