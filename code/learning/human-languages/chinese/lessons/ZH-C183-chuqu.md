## You'll want to know: 出去
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C183-INOUT183-01]; assesses=[] -->

**出去** — *chūqù* — "to go outside; to go out of here".

> **外边很冷你还要出去吗** *wàibian hěn lěng nǐ hái yào chūqù ma* — It's cold out: are you still going out?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出去** (*chūqù*) is built from 出 'to come out' and 去 'to go', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *khjoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): out and away from here.
