## You'll want to know: ਰਕਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C257-BANK257-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਕਮ** — *rakam* — "a sum of money".

> ਇਹ ਰਕਮ ਬਹੁਤ ਵੱਡੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh rakam bahut vaḍḍī hai.*
>
> This sum is very large.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਕਮ** (*rakam*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *raqam*, "a figure, a written sum".
