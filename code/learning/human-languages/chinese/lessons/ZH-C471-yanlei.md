## You'll want to know: yǎnlèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C471-EYES471-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnlèi* — "tears".

> *wǒ dì-yī cì kàn dào bàba de yǎnlèi* — It was the first time I had seen my dad in tears.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnlèi* is built from *yǎn* 'eye' and *lèi* 'tears', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngeanX* and *lwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the eye' and the second 'tears'.
