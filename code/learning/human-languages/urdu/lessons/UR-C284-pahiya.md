## You'll want to know: پہیا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C284-TAKKAR284-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پہیا** — *pahiyā* — "a wheel".

> گاڑی کا پہیا نکل گیا۔
>
> *gāṛī kā pahiyā nikal gayā.*
>
> The car's wheel came off.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پہیا** (*pahiyā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pahiyā*, "a cart wheel", under Sanskrit *pradhí*, "the rim of a wheel" (CDIAL 8672).
