## You'll want to know: भिड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C321-HAMLA321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भिड़ना** — *bhiṛnā* — "to clash; to come to blows".

> दोनों सेनाएँ नदी के किनारे भिड़ीं।
>
> *donõ senāẽ nadī ke kināre bhiṛīṁ.*
>
> The two armies clashed on the riverbank.

Past (he …): वह सिपाही भिड़ गया, *vah sipāhī bhiṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भिड़ना** (*bhiṛnā*) is inherited from *\*bhiṭ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs for meeting and crowding together (CDIAL 9490): two sides that **भिड़ते हैं** come right up against each other.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
