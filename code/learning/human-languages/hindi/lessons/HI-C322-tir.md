## You'll want to know: तीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C322-HATHIYAR322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तीर** — *tīr* — "an arrow".

> उसने एक ही तीर से फल गिराया।
>
> *usne ek hī tīr se phal girāyā.*
>
> He brought down the fruit with a single arrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तीर** (*tīr*) is usually traced to Persian *tīr*, "an arrow". The bow is **धनुष**; **तीर चलाना** is to shoot an arrow.
