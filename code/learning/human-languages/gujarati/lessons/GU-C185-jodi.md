## You'll want to know: જોડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C185-HAGGLE185-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડી** — *joḍī* — "pair".

> **આ બૂટની જોડી મોંઘી છે.** — *ā būṭnī joḍī mõghī che* — "This pair of shoes is expensive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જોડી** (*joḍī*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "pair", under *\*yuṭati*, "is joined", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 10496).
