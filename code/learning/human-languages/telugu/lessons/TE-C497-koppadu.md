# కోప్పడు (kōppaḍu) — to get angry

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C496-BADHA496-04, TE-LEX-C496-BADHA496-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to grieve inwardly, then the Telugu for despair.

## You'll want to know: కోప్పడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C497-KOPAM497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కోప్పడు** — *kōppaḍu* — "to get angry".

> ఆలస్యంగా వచ్చినందుకు నాన్న నా మీద కోప్పడ్డారు.
>
> *ālasyaṅgā vaccinanduku nānna nā mīda kōppaḍḍāru.*
>
> Dad got angry with me for coming late.

Past (I …): కోప్పడ్డాను, *kōppaḍḍānu*.

The person you are angry with takes మీద: నా మీద కోప్పడ్డారు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కోప్పడు** (*kōppaḍu*) is built from కోపం, "anger", from Sanskrit *kōpa* (CDIAL 3516), and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853); the two have fused into one word with a doubled *p*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
