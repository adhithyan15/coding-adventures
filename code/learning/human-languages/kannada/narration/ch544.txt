Kannada, chapter 544: Power and Water.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸ್ವಿಚ್ (svic) — a switch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for furniture, then the Kannada for to lean back.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಿಚ್.
ಸ್ವಿಚ್ — svic — "a switch".
ಬಾಗಿಲಿನ ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ ದೀಪದ ಸ್ವಿಚ್ ಇದೆ. bāgilina pakkadalli dīpada svic ide. The light switch is beside the door.
ಸ್ವಿಚ್ (svic) ಹಾಕು, to switch on; ಆನ್ ಮಾಡು is also said.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ವಿಚ್ (svic) is a loan from English switch, written with a final virama as Kannada writes many English loans.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕೊಳವೆ (koḷave) — a tube; a pipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a plug, then the Kannada for a gas cylinder.

You'll want to know: ಕೊಳವೆ.
ಕೊಳವೆ — koḷave — "a tube; a pipe".
ತೋಟಕ್ಕೆ ನೀರು ಕೊಳವೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. tōṭakke nīru koḷaveyalli baruttade. Water comes to the garden through a pipe.
ಕೊಳವೆ (koḷave) ಬಾವಿ, a borewell. ಕೊಳಲು, the flute, comes from the same old word for a tube.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಳವೆ (koḷave) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1818 lists the Kannada word for "a tube, a blow-pipe" in the same article as the word for a flute, ಕೊಳಲು.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತಂತಿ (tanti) — a wire; a string.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a gas cylinder, then the Kannada for a tube.

You'll want to know: ತಂತಿ.
ತಂತಿ — tanti — "a wire; a string".
ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಒಣಗಿಸಲು ಅಮ್ಮ ತಂತಿ ಕಟ್ಟಿದರು. baṭṭe oṇagisalu amma tanti kaṭṭidaru. Mother tied up a wire to dry the clothes.
Also a string of the veena, and in older talk a telegram.

The word, taken apart.
ತಂತಿ (tanti) goes back to Sanskrit tantrī, "the string of a lute" (CDIAL 5667).
