## You'll want to know: kāishǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C122-HOURS122-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kāishǐ* — "to begin; to start".

> *wǒmen jǐ diǎn kāishǐ* — What time do we start?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāishǐ* is built from 开 'to open' and shǐ 'beginning', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *syiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
