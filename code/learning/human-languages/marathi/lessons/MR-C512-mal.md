## You'll want to know: माल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C512-KARKHANA512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**माल** — *māl* — "goods; stock".

> कारखान्याचा माल रोज बाजारात जातो.
>
> *kārkhānyācā māl roj bājārāt jāto.*
>
> The factory's goods go to the market every day.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माल** (*māl*) is a loan from Persian *māl*, "goods", usually traced to Arabic *māl*, "property, wealth".
