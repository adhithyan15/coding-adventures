Arabic, chapter 134: Light, Shade, Darkness, Cloud, Rabbit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ضوء (ḍawʾ) — light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for thunder, then the Arabic for a storm.

You'll want to know: ضوء.
ضوء — ḍawʾ — "light".

The word, taken apart.
ضوء (ḍawʾ) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-w-ʾ, "light, brightness" (Lane). The word ḍawʾ is the noun, "light".

What you've built.

You'll want to know: أرنب.
أرنب — arnab — "a rabbit".

The word, taken apart.
أرنب (arnab) is built on the native Arabic root r-n-b, "a hare" (Lane). Lane lists arnab under r-n-b; Hebrew arnevet, "hare", is a cousin.
