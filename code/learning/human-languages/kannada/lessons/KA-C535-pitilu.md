# ಪಿಟೀಲು (piṭīlu) — a violin

## You'll want to know: ಪಿಟೀಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C535-VADYA535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಿಟೀಲು** — *piṭīlu* — "a violin".

> ಸಂಗೀತ ಕಚೇರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅವರು ಪಿಟೀಲು ನುಡಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *saṅgīta kacēriyalli avaru piṭīlu nuḍisidaru.*
>
> He played the violin at the concert.

In southern concerts it follows the singer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಿಟೀಲು** (*piṭīlu*) is the Kannada name of the violin, which came to south India from Europe; the origin of the word is not settled.
