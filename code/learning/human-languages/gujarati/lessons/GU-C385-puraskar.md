# પુરસ્કાર (puraskār) — an award

## You'll want to know: પુરસ્કાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C385-PURASKAR385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પુરસ્કાર** — *puraskār* — "an award".

> જો મીરા હરીફાઈ જીતશે તો શાળા તેને પુરસ્કાર આપશે.
>
> *jo Mīrā harīphāī jītśe to śāḷā tene puraskār āpśe.*
>
> If Mira wins the contest, the school will give her an award.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પુરસ્કાર** (*puraskār*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *puraskāra*, "placing in front, honouring" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
