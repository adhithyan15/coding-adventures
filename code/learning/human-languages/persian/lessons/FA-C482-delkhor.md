## You'll want to know: دلخور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C482-HURT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دلخور** (*delkhor*) — "upset (with someone)".

> خواهرم از حرف من دلخور است.
>
> *khâharam az harf-e man delkhor ast.*
>
> My sister is upset at what I said.

The cause takes *az*: *az kasi delkhor budan*. Built on *del* 'heart' and *khor*, the stem of *khordan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delkhor* is built on two native Persian pieces, usually explained as *del*, 'heart', and *khor*, the present stem of *khordan*, 'to eat': a heart that is eaten at. EtymDB traces *del* to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
