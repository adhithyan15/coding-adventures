## You'll want to know: ಏಕಾಏಕಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C327-THATKSHANA327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏಕಾಏಕಿ** — *ēkāēki* — "abruptly; all at once".

> ಏಕಾಏಕಿ ಬಸ್ ನಿಂತಿತು.
>
> *ēkāēki bas nintitu.*
>
> All at once the bus stopped.

Of something sudden and without warning: ಏಕಾಏಕಿ ನಿರ್ಧಾರ, an abrupt decision.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏಕಾಏಕಿ** (*ēkāēki*) is usually traced to Hindustani *ekāek*, "suddenly, all at once"; its earlier history is not settled.
