## You'll want to know: gōngjīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C526-WEIGHT526-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngjīn* — "kilogram".

> *wǒ bǐ qùnián zhòng le liǎng gōngjīn* — I'm two kilos heavier than last year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngjīn* is built from 公 'public, standard' and *jīn*, a weight, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *kj+n* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as sixteen ounces. The standard weight of a kilogram is a modern use.
