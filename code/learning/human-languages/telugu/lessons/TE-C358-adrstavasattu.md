# అదృష్టవశాత్తు (adṛṣṭavaśāttu) — luckily; fortunately

## You'll want to know: అదృష్టవశాత్తు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C358-JIVITAM358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అదృష్టవశాత్తు** — *adṛṣṭavaśāttu* — "luckily; fortunately".

> అదృష్టవశాత్తు ఆ ప్రమాదంలో ఎవరికీ ఏమీ కాలేదు.
>
> *adṛṣṭavaśāttu ā pramādaṁlō evarikī ēmī kālēdu.*
>
> Luckily nobody was hurt in that accident.

అదృష్టం, luck, with వశాత్తు, by the power of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అదృష్టవశాత్తు** (*adṛṣṭavaśāttu*) is built from Sanskrit *adṛṣṭa*, "unseen, unforeseen" (Monier-Williams), and *vaśāt*, "by force of", from *vaśa*, "will, power" (CDIAL 11430).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
