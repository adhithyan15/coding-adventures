Telugu, chapter 524: Skill and Talent.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ప్రతిభ (pratibha) — talent; flair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an assistant, then the Telugu for a contractor.

You'll want to know: ప్రతిభ.
ప్రతిభ — pratibha — "talent; flair".
ఆ పిల్లవాడికి సంగీతంలో గొప్ప ప్రతిభ ఉంది. ā pillavāḍiki saṅgītaṁlō goppa pratibha undi. That boy has a great talent for music.
ప్రతిభావంతుడు, a gifted man.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రతిభ (pratibha) is borrowed from Sanskrit pratibhā, "light; intelligence, understanding" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
పనితనం (panitanaṁ) — workmanship; craft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for talent, then the Telugu for proficiency.

You'll want to know: పనితనం.
పనితనం — panitanaṁ — "workmanship; craft".
ఆ కుర్చీలో వడ్రంగి పనితనం కనిపిస్తుంది. ā kurcīlō vaḍraṅgi panitanaṁ kanipistundi. The carpenter's craft shows in that chair.
పని, work, + -తనం.

The word, taken apart.
పనితనం (panitanaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3884 lists Telugu pani, "work, labour, workmanship, art", with Tamil paṇi; -తనం makes the abstract noun.

Lesson 4 of 5.
నేర్పరి (nērpari) — a skilled person; an adept.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for proficiency, then the Telugu for workmanship.

You'll want to know: నేర్పరి.
నేర్పరి — nērpari — "a skilled person; an adept".
మా అమ్మ వంటలో నేర్పరి. mā amma vaṇṭalō nērpari. My mother is an expert cook.
నేర్పు, skill, + -అరి, one who has it.

The word, taken apart.
నేర్పరి (nērpari) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3770 lists Telugu nēr(u)pu, "cleverness, skill", beside nēr(u)cu, "to learn"; the ending -అరి makes "one who has it".

చొరవ (corava) తీసుకో, to take the initiative.

The word, taken apart.
