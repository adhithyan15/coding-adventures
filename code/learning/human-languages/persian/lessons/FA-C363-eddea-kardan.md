## You'll want to know: ادعا کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C363-EDDEA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ادعا کردن** (*edde'â kardan*) — "to claim".

> او ادعا میکند که آن مرد را دیده است.
>
> *u edde'â mikonad ke ân mard râ dide ast.*
>
> He claims that he has seen that man.

*Edde'â* "a claim" + *kardan* (stem *kon-*), followed by *ke* and a clause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ادعا کردن** joins *edde'â*, usually traced to Arabic *iddi'ā'* "a claim", to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do".
