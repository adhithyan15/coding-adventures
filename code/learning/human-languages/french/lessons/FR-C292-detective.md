## You'll want to know: le détective
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C292-INDICES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le détective** — "the private detective".

> Il lit des romans de détectives.
>
> *He reads detective novels.*

*Un détective privé* works for clients; police officers are *inspecteurs* or *enquêteurs*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*détective* is usually traced to English *detective* (EtymDB lists no parent), which is built on Latin *dētēctus*, from *dētegere* "to uncover" (Lewis & Short).
