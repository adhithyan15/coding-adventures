# ز (ze) — the letter that opens زبان

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write و and ر once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **زبان** (*zabân*), "language".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ز** — *ze*, the sound *z*.

It opens **زبان** (*zabân*), "language", the thing you are learning. It is **ر**,
from the last lesson, with **one dot above**, and like **ر** it never joins the
letter after it.

## Writing: ز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: begin at the upper tip and descend through the short stroke
- **2.** without lifting, sweep left through the lower curve
- **3.** lift once, then place the dot above — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ز demonstration at 01:13–01:16 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **زبان** and point at ز inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ز three times, saying *z* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ر, then ز, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **زبان** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ز? (**ze**, *z*.) Which word did it
come from? (**زبان**, *zabân*, "language".)
