# تشویق کردن (tashviq kardan) — to cheer (on); to encourage

## You'll want to know: تشویق کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C343-BARANDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تشویق کردن** (*tashviq kardan*) — "to cheer (on); to encourage".

> تماشاگران تیم را تشویق کردند.
>
> *Tamâshâgarân tim râ tashviq kardand.*
>
> The spectators cheered the team on.

Also "to encourage": *marâ be khândan tashviq kard*. Present *tashviq mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tashviq* is usually traced to Arabic *tashwīq*, "a stirring of desire, an encouragement"; EtymDB lists no entry. *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
