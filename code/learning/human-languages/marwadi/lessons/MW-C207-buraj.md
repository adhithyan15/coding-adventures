## You'll want to know: बुरज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C207-GARDEN207-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बुरज** — *buraj* — "a tower (of a fort)".

> बुरज घणो जूनो है।
>
> *buraj ghaṇo jūno hai.*
>
> The tower is very old.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुरज** (*buraj*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *burj*, "a tower".
