## You'll want to know: 生火
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C441-CAMP441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**生火** — *shēnghuǒ* — "to light a fire".

> **天冷了我们在村子外边生火** *tiān lěng le wǒmen zài cūnzi wàibian shēnghuǒ* — It's got cold, so we're lighting a fire outside the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**生火** (*shēnghuǒ*) is built from 生 'to bring to life' and 火 'fire', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjaeng* and *xwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to grow'.
