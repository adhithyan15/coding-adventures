Lesson 1 of 5.
شوكولاتة (shūkūlāta) — chocolate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for complicated, then the Arabic for reasonable.

You'll want to know: شوكولاتة.
شوكولاتة — shūkūlāta — "chocolate".

The word, taken apart.
شوكولاتة (shūkūlāta) is a modern loan from European chocolate, which goes back through Spanish to Nahuatl; it has no Arabic root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
توابل (tawābil) — spices.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for pasta, then the Arabic for flour.

You'll want to know: توابل.
توابل — tawābil — "spices".

The word, taken apart.
توابل (tawābil) is the plural of tābal, seeds for seasoning food such as cumin and coriander, which Lane lists under t-b-l and notes is said to be arabicized. EtymDB derives it from Aramaic tablā, "spice".
