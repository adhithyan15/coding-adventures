## You'll want to know: थोडक्यात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C345-NISHKARSH345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**थोडक्यात** — *thoḍkyāt* — "in short; briefly".

> थोडक्यात सांगायचं तर, आम्हाला उशीर झाला.
>
> *thoḍkyāt sāṅgāycaṁ tar, āmhālā uśīr jhālā.*
>
> To put it briefly, we were late.

थोडक्यात सांगायचं तर, to put it briefly; also narrowly: थोडक्यात वाचला, had a narrow escape.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थोडक्यात** (*thoḍkyāt*) is built from थोडका, a form of थोडा, "a little", with the ending -त, "in": in a little space. थोडा is inherited from Sanskrit *stoka*, "little" (CDIAL 13720).
