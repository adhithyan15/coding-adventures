## You'll want to know: el personaje
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C564-HEROE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el personaje** — "the character (in a story)".

> Mi personaje favorito es el abuelo.
>
> *My favourite character is the grandfather.*

Always masculine, even for a woman: *el personaje principal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*personaje* was borrowed from French *personnage* (EtymDB), built on Latin *persōna* 'an actor's mask, a role'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
