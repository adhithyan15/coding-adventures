# ಹೊಗಳು (hogaḷu) — to praise

## You'll want to know: ಹೊಗಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C497-ABHIMANA497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಗಳು** — *hogaḷu* — "to praise".

> ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರು ನನ್ನ ಕಥೆಯನ್ನು ಹೊಗಳಿದರು.
>
> *śikṣakaru nanna katheyannu hogaḷidaru.*
>
> The teacher praised my story.

Past (I …): ಹೊಗಳಿದೆ, *hogaḷide*.

What is praised takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಹೊಗಳಿಕೆ is praise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಗಳು** (*hogaḷu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4235 lists an older Kannada form in p-, "to praise; praise, renown". Old p- became h- in the modern language.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
