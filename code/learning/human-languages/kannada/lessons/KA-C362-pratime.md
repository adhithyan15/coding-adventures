# ಪ್ರತಿಮೆ (pratime) — a statue

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಮೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C362-NIRMANA362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಮೆ** — *pratime* — "a statue".

> ವೃತ್ತದಲ್ಲಿ ಆ ರಾಜನ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಪ್ರತಿಮೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *vṛttadalli ā rājana doḍḍa pratime ide.*
>
> There is a large statue of that king at the circle.

Plural: ಪ್ರತಿಮೆಗಳು, *pratimegaḷu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಮೆ** (*pratime*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pratimā*, "an image, a likeness, a statue" (Monier-Williams).
