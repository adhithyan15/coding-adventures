## You'll want to know: ರಾಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C322-RAJA322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಣಿ** — *rāṇi* — "a queen".

> ರಾಣಿ ಅರಮನೆಯ ತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದಳು.
>
> *rāṇi aramaneya tōṭadalli naḍeyuttiddaḷu.*
>
> The queen was walking in the palace garden.

Plural: ರಾಣಿಯರು, *rāṇiyaru*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಣಿ** (*rāṇi*) goes back to Sanskrit *rājñī*, "a queen" (CDIAL 10692), the feminine of *rājan*; the spoken languages of the north smoothed it to *rāṇī*, and Kannada took that form.
