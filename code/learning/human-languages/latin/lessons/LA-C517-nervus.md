## You'll want to know: nervus, nervī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C517-VENA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**nervus, nervī** — "a sinew, tendon; a string".

> Faber nervōs firmōs habet.
>
> *The smith has strong sinews.*

Masculine, second declension. In the plural also strength, vigour. English *nerve*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**nervus** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European word for "sinew, tendon", and Greek *neuron* is a cousin (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *nerve* comes from it.
