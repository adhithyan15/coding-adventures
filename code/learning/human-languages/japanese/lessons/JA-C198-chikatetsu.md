## You'll want to know: ちかてつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C198-RIDE198-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちかてつ** — *chikatetsu* — "a subway; an underground railway".

> **ちかてつで まちに いきます。** — *chikatetsu de machi ni ikimasu* — "I go into town on the subway."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちかてつ** (*chikatetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chi*, "earth" (Middle Chinese *dijH*), *ka*, "below" (*haeX*) and *tetsu*, "iron" (*thet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
