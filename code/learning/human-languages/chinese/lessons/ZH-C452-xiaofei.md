## You'll want to know: xiǎofèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C452-AFTERMEAL452-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎofèi* — "tip; a tip for the waiter".

> *zài Zhōngguó chīfàn yìbān bù gěi xiǎofèi* — In China you don't usually leave a tip when you eat out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎofèi* is built from 小 'small' and *fèi* 'a cost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to spend'.
