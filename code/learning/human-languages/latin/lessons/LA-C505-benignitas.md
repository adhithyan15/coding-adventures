## You'll want to know: benignitās, benignitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C505-CLEM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**benignitās, benignitātis** — "kindness, generosity".

> Benignitās hospitis nōs dēlectat.
>
> *Our host's kindness delights us.*

Feminine, third declension, from *benignus* — kind. English *benignity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**benignitās** is built on *benignus* "kind", which Lewis & Short and EtymDB explain as *bonus* or *bene* "good, well" with *genus* "kind": of a good kind. English *benign* comes from *benignus*.
