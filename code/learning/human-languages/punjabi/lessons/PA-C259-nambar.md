# ਨੰਬਰ (nambar) — a number; a phone number

## You'll want to know: ਨੰਬਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C259-PHONE259-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੰਬਰ** — *nambar* — "a number; a phone number".

> ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਨੰਬਰ ਕੀ ਹੈ?
>
> *tuhāḍā fon nambar kī hai?*
>
> What is your phone number?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੰਬਰ** (*nambar*) is borrowed from English *number*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
