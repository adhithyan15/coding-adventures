## You'll want to know: よける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C505-SUMO505-05]; assesses=[] -->

**よける** — *yokeru* — "to dodge; to keep out of the way of".

> **いしを よけて あるきます。** — *ishi o yokete arukimasu* — "I walk around the stones."

Polite **よけます**, *yokemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ねこが くるまを よけました。**, *neko ga kuruma o yokemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よける** (*yokeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
