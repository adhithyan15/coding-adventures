## You'll want to know: недостаток
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C346-PLYUSY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**недостаток** (*nedostátok*) — "a drawback, a shortcoming; a lack".

> Его главный недостаток — лень.
>
> *Yegó glávnyy nedostátok — len.*
>
> His main shortcoming is laziness.

Masculine; genitive *недостатка*, with the *о* dropped. *Недостаток денег* — a lack of money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**недостаток** is *не-* "not" in front of *достаток* "sufficiency", from *достать* "to reach, to be enough", built on *стать*, the partner of *стоять*, Proto-Slavic *\*stojati* "to stand" (EtymDB).
