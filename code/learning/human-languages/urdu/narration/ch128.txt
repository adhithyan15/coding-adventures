Urdu, chapter 128: Deer, Pigeon, Parrot, Bear, Waist.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ہرن (hiran) — a deer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fly, then the Urdu for a rabbit.

You'll want to know: ہرن.
ہرن — hiran — "a deer".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hiran]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hiran, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say hiran]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for a fly, then the Urdu for a rabbit, then say hiran again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ہرن (hiran) mean? ("A deer".) Say it once more.
