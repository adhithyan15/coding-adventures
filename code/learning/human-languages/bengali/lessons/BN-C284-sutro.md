## You'll want to know: সূত্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C284-THANA284-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সূত্র** — *sūtrô* — "a clue; a lead".

> পুলিশ জানালার কাছে একটা সূত্র পেল।
>
> *pulish jānālār kāchhe ekṭā sūtrô pelô.*
>
> The police found a clue near the window.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সূত্র** (*sūtrô*) is Sanskrit *sūtra*, 'a thread, a line' (Monier-Williams); in a mystery it is the thread you follow. **সুতো**, thread, is the everyday word from the same source.
