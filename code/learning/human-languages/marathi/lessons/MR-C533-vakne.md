## You'll want to know: वाकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C533-TANDURUSTI533-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वाकणे** — *vākṇe* — "to bend".

> चेंडू उचलण्यासाठी तो खाली वाकला.
>
> *ceṇḍū ucalṇyāsāṭhī to khālī vāklā.*
>
> He bent down to pick up the ball.

Past (he …): तो वाकला, *to vāklā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाकणे** (*vākṇe*) is built on वाक, "a curve", which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit *vaṅka*, "bent, crooked" (CDIAL 11191).
