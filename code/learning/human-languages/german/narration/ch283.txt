German, chapter 283: Der Zeuge.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Zeuge — the witness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to threaten, then the German for the weapon.

You'll want to know: der Zeuge.
der Zeuge — "the witness".
Ein Zeuge hatte alles gesehen. A witness had seen everything.
Weak noun: den Zeugen, dem Zeugen. Feminine die Zeugin.

The word, taken apart.
Zeuge is usually traced to Middle High German geziuc "evidence", built on the root of ziehen "to pull": someone called in. EtymDB lists only its Dutch cousin getuige.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the German for the weapon, then the German for the witness.

You'll want to know: aussagen.
aussagen — "to testify, to give evidence".
Die Nachbarin sagte vor Gericht aus. The neighbour testified in court.
Separable: sie sagt aus, sie hat ausgesagt.

The word, taken apart.
aussagen joins aus "out" and sagen "to say", an inherited Germanic verb with the Dutch cousin zeggen (EtymDB): to say out.

Regular: beobachtete, hat beobachtet.

The word, taken apart.
beobachten is usually traced to a Low German verb built on achten "to pay heed", from Old High German ahtōn (EtymDB): to keep an eye on.

Lesson 5 of 5.
schildern — to recount, to describe vividly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the statement, then the German for to observe.

You'll want to know: schildern.
schildern — "to recount, to describe vividly".
Der Zeuge schilderte den Unfall genau. The witness recounted the accident precisely.
Stronger than beschreiben: you paint the scene in words.

The word, taken apart.
schildern is built on Schild "shield", a cousin of English shield (EtymDB): it first meant painting coats of arms, then painting a scene in words.
