## You'll want to know: der Stecker
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C392-GERAET-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Stecker** — "the plug".

> Im Ausland passt mein Stecker oft nicht.
>
> *Abroad my plug often does not fit.*

Plural: *die Stecker*. *Den Stecker ziehen* — to pull the plug.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stecker* is formed with *-er* on *stecken* "to put in, to stick", inherited from Old High German *steckōn* (EtymDB): the thing you stick in.
