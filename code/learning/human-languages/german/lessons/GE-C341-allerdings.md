## You'll want to know: allerdings
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C341-OBWOHL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**allerdings** — "however; certainly".

> Die Wohnung ist schön. Allerdings ist die Küche sehr klein.
>
> *The flat is nice. However, the kitchen is very small.*

When it opens the sentence, the verb comes right after it: *Allerdings ist …*. As an answer it means certainly: *Allerdings!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allerdings* grew out of the genitive *aller Dinge* "of all things", built on *Ding* "thing", from Old High German *ding* "assembly" and Proto-Germanic *þingą* (EtymDB), with the adverb ending *-s*.
