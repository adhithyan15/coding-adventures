## You'll want to know: मंडई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C422-MANDAI422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मंडई** — *maṇḍaī* — "a vegetable market".

> आई रोज सकाळी मंडईत जाते.
>
> *āī roj sakāḷī maṇḍaīt jāte.*
>
> Mother goes to the vegetable market every morning.

Feminine.

A covered market for vegetables and fruit, as in Pune.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंडई** (*maṇḍaī*) is a Marathi word for a covered vegetable market; its origin is not settled.
