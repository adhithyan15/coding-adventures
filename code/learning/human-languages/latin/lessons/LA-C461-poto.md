## You'll want to know: pōtō, pōtāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C461-THERMOPOLIUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pōtō, pōtāre** — "to drink, to drink deep".

> Pōtōrēs usque ad mediam noctem pōtant.
>
> *The drinkers drink on until midnight.*

*pōtō, pōtāre, pōtāvī, pōtātum* or *pōtum*. Stronger than *bibō*: drinking hard, drinking for pleasure. English *potion* and *potable* come from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pōtō* is inherited from PIE *peh₃-* 'to drink', with Greek *pinō* 'to drink' as a cousin (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *potion* and *potable* come from it.
