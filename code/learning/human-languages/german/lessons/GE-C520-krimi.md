## You'll want to know: der Krimi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C520-BUECHER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Krimi** — "crime novel, crime film".

> Am Strand lese ich gern einen spannenden Krimi.
>
> *On the beach I like reading an exciting crime novel.*

Plural: *die Krimis*. Short for *der Kriminalroman*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Krimi* is short for *Kriminalroman* "crime novel", built on a learned word from Latin *crīmen* "charge, crime" (Lewis & Short).
