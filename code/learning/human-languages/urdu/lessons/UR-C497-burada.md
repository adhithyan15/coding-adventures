## You'll want to know: برادہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C497-TAKHTA497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**برادہ** — *burādā* — "sawdust".

> بڑھئی کی دکان میں ہر طرف برادہ تھا۔
>
> *baṛhaī kī dukān meṅ har taraf burādā thā.*
>
> There was sawdust all over the carpenter's shop.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برادہ** (*burādā*) is usually traced to Persian *burāda*, "filings, sawdust": what a saw or a file leaves behind.
