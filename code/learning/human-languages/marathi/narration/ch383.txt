Marathi, chapter 383: Predicting and Assuming.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भाकीत (bhākīt) — a prediction; a forecast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for certain, then the Marathi for to seem.

You'll want to know: भाकीत.
भाकीत — bhākīt — "a prediction; a forecast".
हवामान खात्याने पावसाचं भाकीत केलं. havāmān khātyāne pāvsācaṁ bhākīt kelaṁ. The weather department forecast rain.
Neuter.
भाकीत (bhākīt) करणे, to predict; भाकीत (bhākīt) खरं होणे, to come true.

The word, taken apart.
भाकीत (bhākīt) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गृहीत धरणे (gṛhīt dharṇe) — to assume; to take for granted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to seem, then the Marathi for a prediction.

You'll want to know: गृहीत धरणे.
गृहीत धरणे — gṛhīt dharṇe — "to assume; to take for granted".
तू येणार नाहीस असं मी गृहीत धरलं. tū yeṇār nāhīs asaṁ mī gṛhīt dharlaṁ. I assumed that you were not coming.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने गृहीत धरलं, tyāne gṛhīt dharlaṁ.
Also of people: कोणालाही गृहीत धरणे (gṛhīt dharṇe), to take someone for granted.

The word, taken apart.
गृहीत धरणे (gṛhīt dharṇe) joins the tatsama गृहीत, Sanskrit gṛhīta, "seized, taken, accepted" (Monier-Williams), to धरणे, "to hold".

अपेक्षा — apekṣā — "an expectation".
आईवडिलांच्या माझ्याकडून खूप अपेक्षा आहेत. āīvḍilāñcyā mājhyākaḍūn khūp apekṣā āhet. My parents have high expectations of me.
Feminine. Plural: अपेक्षा, apekṣā.
X कडून अपेक्षा (apekṣā), expectations of X; अपेक्षा (apekṣā) करणे, to expect.

The word, taken apart.
अपेक्षा (apekṣā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit apekṣā, "looking round; expectation" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ताडणे (tāḍṇe) — to size up; to guess rightly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an expectation, then the Marathi for a trend.

You'll want to know: ताडणे.
ताडणे — tāḍṇe — "to size up; to guess rightly".
तो खोटं बोलत आहे हे आईने लगेच ताडलं. to khoṭaṁ bolat āhe he āīne lagec tāḍlaṁ. Mother saw at once that he was lying.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ताडलं, tyāne tāḍlaṁ.
Transitive: what is guessed is the object; हे ताडलं, guessed this.

The word, taken apart.
ताडणे (tāḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi tāḍṇẽ, "to investigate, discover, infer", under taḍati, "stretches" (CDIAL 5633).
