## You'll want to know: obsidiō, obsidiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C341-OBSIDIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**obsidiō, obsidiōnis** — "a siege".

> Obsidiō longa erat, et cīvēs famē labōrābant.
>
> *The siege was long, and the citizens were suffering from hunger.*

Feminine, 3rd declension, from *obsideō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**obsidiō** is built on *obsideō*, "to besiege" (EtymDB), with the ending *-iō*.
