Marathi, chapter 471: Good Sense.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शहाणा (śahāṇā) — sensible; wise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for innocent, then the Marathi for to dupe.

You'll want to know: शहाणा.
शहाणा — śahāṇā — "sensible; wise".
शहाणी मुलगी आईला कामात मदत करते. śahāṇī mulgī āīlā kāmāt madat karte. The sensible girl helps her mother with the work.
Agrees: शहाणा (śahāṇā), शहाणी, शहाणं. शहाणं बाळ, a good baby.

The word, taken apart.
शहाणा (śahāṇā) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
समजूतदार (samjūtdār) — understanding; mature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for shrewd, then the Marathi for intelligent.

You'll want to know: समजूतदार.
समजूतदार — samjūtdār — "understanding; mature".
माझा मोठा भाऊ खूप समजूतदार आहे. mājhā moṭhā bhāū khūp samjūtdār āhe. My big brother is very understanding.
समजूत, understanding, from समजणे, + -दार, having.

The word, taken apart.
समजूतदार (samjūtdār) is a Marathi derivation: समजूत, "understanding", the noun of समजणे, with -dār, "having", an ending usually traced to Persian -dār.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अक्कल (akkal) — common sense; wits.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for intelligent, then the Marathi for understanding.

You'll want to know: अक्कल.
अक्कल — akkal — "common sense; wits".
या कामाला थोडी अक्कल लागते. yā kāmālā thoḍī akkal lāgte. This job takes a little common sense.
Feminine.
अक्कल (akkal) लढवणे, to use one's wits; अक्कलहुशार, quick-witted.

The word, taken apart.
अक्कल (akkal) is a loan from Persian aql, "reason", usually traced to Arabic.
