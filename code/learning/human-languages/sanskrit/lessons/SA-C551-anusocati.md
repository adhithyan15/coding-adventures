## You'll want to know: अनुशोचति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C551-PARITYAJATI551-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुशोचति** — *anuśocati* — "regrets".

> यदि त्वं न पठसि, तर्हि पश्चात् अनुशोचसि।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ na paṭhasi, tarhi paścāt anuśocasi.*
>
> If you don't study, you will regret it later.

Past (he …): सः पश्चात् अनुशोचितवान्, *saḥ paścāt anuśocitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुशोचति** (*anuśocati*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √śuc, "to grieve", which also gives *śoka*, "grief"; MW gives *anuśuc* as to mourn over, to regret (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
