## You'll want to know: vencer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C569-LUCHA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vencer** — "to beat, to win; to expire".

> Vencimos por dos a uno.
>
> *We won two-one.*

It takes *z*: *venzo*. On a document it means to expire: *El plazo vence el lunes.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vencer* is inherited from Latin *vincere* 'to conquer' (EtymDB), through old Spanish *vencer*.
