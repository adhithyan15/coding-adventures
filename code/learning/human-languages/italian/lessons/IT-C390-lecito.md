## You'll want to know: lecito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C390-LEGALE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lecito** — "permissible, allowed".

> È lecito chiedere, ma non insistere.
>
> *It is all right to ask, but do not insist.*

Stress on the first syllable: *lècito*. Moral as well as legal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lecito* comes from Latin *licitus* "allowed, lawful" (Lewis & Short), past participle of *licēre* "to be allowed".
