## You'll want to know: венчик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C330-SMESHIVAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**венчик** (*vénchik*) — "a whisk".

> Венчик лежит в ящике.
>
> *Vénchik lezhít v yáshchike.*
>
> The whisk is in the drawer.

Diminutive of *венец* "a crown, a wreath"; also a halo or a corolla.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*венчик* is a diminutive of *венец* "a crown, a wreath" (EtymDB lists *венец* with no parent): a whisk is a little ring of wire.
