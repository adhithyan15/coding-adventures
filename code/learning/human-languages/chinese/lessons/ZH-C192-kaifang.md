## You'll want to know: kāifàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C192-SIGN192-05]; assesses=[] -->

*kāifàng* — "to be open to the public".

> *gōngyuán wǎnshang bù kāifàng* — The park isn't open at night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāifàng* is built from 开 'to open' and *fàng* 'to let go', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *pjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
