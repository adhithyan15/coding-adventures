Spanish, chapter 495: Las noticias.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la noticia — the piece of news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to switch on, then the Spanish for to heat up.

You'll want to know: la noticia.
la noticia — "the piece of news".
Tengo una buena noticia. I have some good news.
Las noticias on television are the news.
