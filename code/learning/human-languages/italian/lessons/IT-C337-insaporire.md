## You'll want to know: insaporire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C337-CONDIRE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**insaporire** — "to flavour, to give taste to".

> Le erbe insaporiscono la carne.
>
> *The herbs flavour the meat.*

Built on *sapore*, ‘flavour’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*insaporire* is built on *sapore* "flavour", from Latin *sapor* "taste" (Lewis & Short).
