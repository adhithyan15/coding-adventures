# قوس قزح (qaws quzaḥ) — a rainbow

## You'll want to know: قوس قزح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C485-SAMA485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قوس قزح** — *qaws quzaḥ* — "a rainbow".

> ظهر قوس قزح بعد المطر.
>
> *ẓahara qaws quzaḥ baʿda al-maṭar.*
>
> A rainbow appeared after the rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قوس قزح** (*qaws quzaḥ*) is "the bow of *quzaḥ*": *qaws*, "a bow", is built on the native root *q-w-s* (Lane), but the origin of *quzaḥ* is not settled.
