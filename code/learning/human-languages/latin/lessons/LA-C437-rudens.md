## You'll want to know: rudēns, rudentis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C437-RUDENS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rudēns, rudentis** — "a ship's rope, a line".

> Nautae rudentēs trahunt et vēlum tollunt.
>
> *The sailors pull the ropes and raise the sail.*

Third declension, masculine: *rudēns, rudentis m.* The ropes of the rigging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *rudēns* is not settled: Lewis & Short call it unknown (ancient writers linked it with *rudō* 'to roar', from the creaking of the ropes), and EtymDB lists it without a parent.
