## You'll want to know: عدالت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C283-ADALAT283-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عدالت** — *adālat* — "a court of law".

> کل عدالت بند تھی۔
>
> *kal adālat band thī.*
>
> The court was closed yesterday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عدالت** (*adālat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿadāla*, "justice, fairness": the place where justice is done. It is feminine: **عدالت بند تھی**.
