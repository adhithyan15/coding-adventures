# -এর চেয়ে / সবচেয়ে (-er cheye / sôbcheye) — than, and most

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C273-DOINIK-01, BN-LEX-C72-ADJ72-01, BN-LEX-C72-ADJ72-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "my daily spending". (*āmār doinik khôrôch*.) Then **বড়** and **ছোট**, "big" and "small".

## You'll want to know: than
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C273-CHEYE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-03, BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-04, BN-LEX-C72-ADJ72-01, BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-04, BN-LEX-C83-TOWN83-02, BN-LEX-C73-ADJ73-04] -->

> **নদী পুকুরের চেয়ে বড়।**
>
> *nôdī pukurer cheye bôṛo.* — "A river is bigger than a pond."

The thing you measure against takes the **-র** form and **চেয়ে** (*cheye*), "than". The describing word does not change: **বড়** is "big" and "bigger". **ট্রেন বাসের চেয়ে তাড়াতাড়ি যায়** (*ṭren bāser cheye tāṛātāṛi jāy*), "a train goes faster than a bus".

## You'll want to know: most
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C273-SOBCHEYE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C142-QUAL142-01, BN-LEX-C223-JINIS223-02, BN-LEX-C55-VERBS55-05, BN-LEX-C74-ADJ74-01, BN-LEX-C63-HOME63-04] -->

**সব** (*sôb*) is "all", so **সবচেয়ে** (*sôbcheye*) is "than all": "most, the -est".

> **আমার সবচেয়ে প্রিয় খাবার মাছ।**
>
> *āmār sôbcheye priyô khābār māchh.* — "My favourite food of all is fish."

**এটা সবচেয়ে দামি বই** (*eṭā sôbcheye dāmi bôi*), "this is the most expensive book".
