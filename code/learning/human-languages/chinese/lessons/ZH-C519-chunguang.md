## You'll want to know: 春光
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C519-OUTDOORS519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**春光** — *chūnguāng* — "the beauty of spring; spring sunshine".

> **春光最好的时候树都开花了** *chūnguāng zuì hǎo de shíhou shù dōu kāihuā le* — When spring is at its loveliest, the trees are all in blossom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**春光** (*chūnguāng*) is built from *chūn* 'spring' and 光 'light', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwin* and *kwang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older book, as the head of the four seasons, when all things come out.
