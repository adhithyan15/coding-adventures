# అభినందించు (abhinandiñcu) — to congratulate

## You'll want to know: అభినందించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C543-KACERI543-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అభినందించు** — *abhinandiñcu* — "to congratulate".

> గెలిచిన జట్టును అందరూ అభినందించారు.
>
> *gelicina jaṭṭunu andarū abhinandiñcāru.*
>
> Everyone congratulated the winning team.

Past (I …): అభినందించాను, *abhinandiñcānu*.

అభినందనలు, congratulations.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అభినందించు** (*abhinandiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *abhinandana*, "delighting; praising, applauding" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
