## You'll want to know: guō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C170-POT170-01]; assesses=[] -->

*guō* — "pot; pan; wok".

> *zhège guō hěn zhòng* — This wok is heavy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guō* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kwa* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a vessel for warming'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
