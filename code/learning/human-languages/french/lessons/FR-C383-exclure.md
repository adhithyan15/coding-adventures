## You'll want to know: exclure
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C383-CRITERE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**exclure** — "to exclude, to rule out".

> On ne peut pas exclure un retard.
>
> *A delay cannot be ruled out.*

Like *conclure*: past participle *exclu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exclure* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exclūdere* "to shut out" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from *ex* "out" and *claudere* "to shut".
