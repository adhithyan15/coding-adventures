## You'll want to know: il marmo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C522-SCULTURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il marmo** — "marble".

> In questa chiesa tutto è di marmo bianco.
>
> *In this church everything is white marble.*

*Freddo come il marmo* — cold as marble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marmo* is inherited from Latin *marmor* "marble" (EtymDB), from Greek *mármaros* "a shining stone".
