## You'll want to know: la caravana
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C666-CAMPING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la caravana** — "the caravan, the camper; the tailback".

> Viajamos por la costa en caravana.
>
> *We travelled along the coast in a caravan.*

In Spain *una caravana* is also a long line of cars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caravana* comes from Persian *kārvān* "a company of travellers" (EtymDB), which reached Spanish through French or Italian.
