## You'll want to know: टलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C302-AILAN302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**टलना** — *ṭalnā* — "to be averted; to be put off".

> सबकी मदद से बड़ा ख़तरा टल गया।
>
> *sabkī madad se baṛā khatrā ṭal gayā.*
>
> With everyone's help a great danger was averted.

Past (it …): वह ख़तरा टल गया, *vah khatrā ṭal gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टलना** (*ṭalnā*) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit *ṭalati*, "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450). A danger that **टल गया** moved away from you; a meeting that **टल गई** was put off.
