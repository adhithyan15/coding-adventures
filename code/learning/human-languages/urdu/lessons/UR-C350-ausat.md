## You'll want to know: اوسط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C350-AUSAT350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اوسط** — *ausat* — "an average".

> اس سال بارش اوسط سے کم ہوئی۔
>
> *is sāl bārish ausat se kam huī.*
>
> This year it rained less than average.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اوسط** (*ausat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *awsaṭ*, "middle, central".
