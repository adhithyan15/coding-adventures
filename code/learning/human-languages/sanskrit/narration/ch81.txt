Sanskrit, chapter 81: Tree, Seed and Root.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वृक्षः (vṛkṣaḥ) — a tree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a tortoise, then the Sanskrit for a frog.

You'll want to know: वृक्षः.
वृक्षः (vṛkṣaḥ) — "a tree", the everyday word beside तरुः.

What you've built.
A second word for tree.
