German, chapter 65: To wait, To pay, To cook, To laugh, To cry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
warten — to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to find, then the German for to look for.

You'll want to know: warten.
warten — "to wait".

The word, taken apart.
warten from Old High German wartēn, "to watch, to look after" from Proto-Germanic wardāną, "to guard" — inherited, a cousin of English ward. (EtymDB 2.1) Waiting began as keeping watch.

Lesson 2 of 5.
bezahlen — to pay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to look for, then the German for to wait.

You'll want to know: bezahlen.
bezahlen — "to pay".

The word, taken apart.
bezahlen is be- + the verb "to pay", which is built on the word for "number", Old High German zala from Proto-Germanic talō (EtymDB 2.1). English tale and tell share the root: paying was counting out.
