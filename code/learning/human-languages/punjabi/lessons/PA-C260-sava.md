# ਸਵਾ (savā) — plus a quarter; a quarter past

## You'll want to know: ਸਵਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C260-TIME260-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਵਾ** — *savā* — "plus a quarter; a quarter past".

> ਬੱਸ ਸਵਾ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *bass savā aṭṭh vaje āundī hai.*
>
> The bus comes at a quarter past eight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਵਾ** (*savā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sapāda*, "increased by a fourth"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *savā*, "plus a quarter", under it (CDIAL 13134).
