## You'll want to know: شرشف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C551-GHURFA551-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شرشف** — *sharshaf* — "a bedsheet".

> غيرت أمي شراشف السرير.
>
> *ghayyarat ummī sharāshif as-sarīr.*
>
> My mother changed the bedsheets.

Plural: شراشف, *sharāshif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرشف** (*sharshaf*) is usually traced to Persian *chādar-shab*, "a night sheet", which reached Arabic through Turkish.
