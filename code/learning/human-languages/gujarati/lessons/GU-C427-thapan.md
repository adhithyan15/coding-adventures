## You'll want to know: થાપણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C427-SAVE427-03]; assesses=[] -->

**થાપણ** — *thāpaṇ* — "a deposit (of money); something held in trust".

> મારી થાપણ પર બેંક વ્યાજ આપે છે.
>
> *mārī thāpaṇ par beṅk vyāj āpe che.*
>
> The bank pays interest on my deposit.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થાપણ** (*thāpaṇ*) seems to go back to Sanskrit *sthāpana*, "setting down, placing" (Monier-Williams): money set down with someone to keep.
