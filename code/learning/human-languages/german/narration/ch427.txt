German, chapter 427: Warm oder kalt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Klimaanlage — the air conditioning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the stay, then the German for to check out.

You'll want to know: die Klimaanlage.
die Klimaanlage — "the air conditioning".
Die Klimaanlage im Zimmer macht viel Lärm. The air conditioning in the room makes a lot of noise.
Plural: die Klimaanlagen. Short: die Klima.

The word, taken apart.
Klimaanlage is built from Klima 'climate', from Latin clima, from Greek klíma 'slope, region' (EtymDB), and Anlage 'installation', from anlegen 'to lay out' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Heizung — the heating; the radiator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to check out, then the German for the air conditioning.

You'll want to know: die Heizung.
die Heizung — "the heating; the radiator".
Im Winter ist die Heizung im Hotel zu schwach. In winter the heating in the hotel is too weak.
Plural: die Heizungen. People often say die Heizung for one radiator too.

The word, taken apart.
Heizung is built on the verb heizen 'to heat', inherited from Old High German heizen and Proto-Germanic haitijaną (EtymDB), a cousin of German heiß 'hot'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Heizkörper — the radiator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the air conditioning, then the German for the heating.

You'll want to know: der Heizkörper.
der Heizkörper — "the radiator".
Sie hängt die nassen Socken über den Heizkörper. She hangs the wet socks over the radiator.
Same form in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
Heizkörper is built from heizen 'to heat', an inherited Germanic verb (EtymDB), and Körper 'body', which goes back to Latin corpus (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
aufdrehen — to turn up, to turn on (a tap, the heating).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the heating, then the German for the radiator.

You'll want to know: aufdrehen.
aufdrehen — "to turn up, to turn on (a tap, the heating)".
Ihm ist kalt, deshalb dreht er die Heizung auf. He is cold, so he turns the heating up.
Separable: ich drehe auf, ich habe aufgedreht. Das Radio aufdrehen — to turn the radio up.

The word, taken apart.
aufdrehen is built from auf 'up, open' and drehen 'to turn', an inherited Germanic verb (EtymDB lists Dutch draaien as a cousin).

Lesson 5 of 5.

zudrehen — "to shut off (a tap, the heating), to turn down".
Vor dem Schlafen dreht sie die Heizung zu. Before going to sleep she turns off the heating.
Separable: ich drehe zu. Its opposite is aufdrehen.

The word, taken apart.
zudrehen is built from zu 'shut, closed' and drehen 'to turn', an inherited Germanic verb (EtymDB).
