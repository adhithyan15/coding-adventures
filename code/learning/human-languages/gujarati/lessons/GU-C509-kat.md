## You'll want to know: કાટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C509-DHAMAN509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કાટ** — *kāṭ* — "rust".

> વરસાદ પછી લોખંડના દરવાજાને કાટ લાગ્યો.
>
> *varsād pachī lokhanḍnā darvājāne kāṭ lāgyo.*
>
> After the rain the iron gate went rusty.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાટ** (*kāṭ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *kiṭṭa*, "secretion, dross", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "rust" (CDIAL 3156). Rust strikes the metal: **દરવાજાને કાટ લાગ્યો**.
