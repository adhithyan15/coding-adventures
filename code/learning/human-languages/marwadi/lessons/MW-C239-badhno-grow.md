## You'll want to know: बधणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C239-CROP239-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बधणो** — *badhṇo* — "to grow; to increase".

> बरसात मांय बाजरो बधै है।
>
> *barsāt mā̃y bājro badhai hai.*
>
> The millet grows in the rains.

Past (it …): वो रूंख बध्यो, *vo rū̃kh badhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बधणो** (*badhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vardhate*, "grows"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *badhaï*, "to increase, to thrive" (CDIAL 11376).
