## You'll want to know: retarder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C420-ESCALE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**retarder** — "to delay, to hold up".

> La neige a retardé tous les vols.
>
> *The snow delayed all the flights.*

*Ma montre retarde* — my watch is slow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*retarder* is a learned borrowing from Latin *retardāre* 'to hold back', from *tardus* 'slow' (Lewis & Short).
