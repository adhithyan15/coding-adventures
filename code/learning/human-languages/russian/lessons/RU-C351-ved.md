# ведь (ved) — after all, you know

## You'll want to know: ведь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C351-POSKOLKU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ведь** (*ved*) — "after all, you know".

> Он не придёт: он ведь болен.
>
> *On ne pridyót: on ved bólen.*
>
> He will not come: he is ill, after all.

It gives a reason the listener already knows. It is unstressed and sits near the front of the clause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ведь** comes from Old East Slavic *vědě* "I know" (EtymDB), a form of the old verb *ведать* "to know"; EtymDB names English *wit* as a cousin. *Ведь* still appeals to what both speakers know.
