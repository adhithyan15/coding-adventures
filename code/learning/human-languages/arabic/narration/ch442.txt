Arabic, chapter 442: Camps and resorts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مخيم (mukhayyam) — a campsite; a camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to depart from, then the Arabic for to vacate.

You'll want to know: مخيم.
مخيم — mukhayyam — "a campsite; a camp".
كان المخيم بجانب البحيرة. kāna al-mukhayyam bi-jānib al-buḥayra. The campsite was beside the lake.

The word, taken apart.
مخيم (mukhayyam) is built on the native Arabic root kh-y-m, "a tent" (Lane). It is the Form II passive participle: a place set with tents.

Lesson 2 of 5.
منتجع (muntajaʿ) — a resort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to vacate, then the Arabic for a campsite.

You'll want to know: منتجع.
منتجع — muntajaʿ — "a resort".
قضينا العطلة في منتجع على البحر. qaḍaynā al-ʿuṭla fī muntajaʿ ʿalā al-baḥr. We spent the holiday at a resort by the sea.

The word, taken apart.
منتجع (muntajaʿ) is built on the native Arabic root n-j-ʿ, "to go in search of pasture" (Lane). It is the place-noun of Form VIII: once a place people went to for fresh pasture.

Lesson 4 of 5.
أشعل (ashʿala) — to set alight; to light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a resort, then the Arabic for a chalet.

You'll want to know: أشعل.
أشعل — ashʿala — "to set alight; to light".
أشعل أبي النار أمام الخيمة. ashʿala abī an-nār amāma al-khayma. My father lit the fire in front of the tent.
Present: يشعل, yushʿil.
It also means to switch on a lamp.

The word, taken apart.
أشعل (ashʿala) is built on the native Arabic root sh-ʿ-l, "to blaze, to flame" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to make blaze".
