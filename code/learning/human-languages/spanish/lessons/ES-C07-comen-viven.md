# comen and viven — agreeing again

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-AR-PRESENT-3PL, ES-LEX-USTEDES] -->

[PAUSE 2s] What is "they speak"? (*Hablan*.) And who else does that form
address? (*Ustedes*.)

## Grammar Lens: one ending for both
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-GRAMMAR-ER-PRESENT-3PL, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-PRESENT-3PL]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-ER-PRESENT-2PL, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-PRESENT-2PL, ES-LEX-USTEDES] -->

Both families take **-en**:

> **comen** — *they eat*, *ustedes* eat.
> **viven** — *they live*, *ustedes* live.

No split here. After two chapters of divergence the families fall back into
step, and they stay there.

That is the shape of the whole system, and it is worth holding as one sentence:

> ***-er* and *-ir* are identical in four of the six slots. They differ only in
> *nosotros* and *vosotros*.**

Two exceptions in a system of twelve forms. You have now met both, and
everything else you already knew.
