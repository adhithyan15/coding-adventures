## You'll want to know: جر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C433-AJALA433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جر** — *jarra* — "to tow; to drag".

> جر رجل سيارتنا إلى القرية.
>
> *jarra rajul sayyāratanā ilā al-qarya.*
>
> A man towed our car to the village.

Present: يجر, *yajurr*.

Grammarians call the genitive case *al-jarr*, "the dragging".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جر** (*jarra*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-r*, "to drag, to pull" (Lane). It is the plain verb of a doubled root.
