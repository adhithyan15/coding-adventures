## You'll want to know: પરું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C416-AREA416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પરું** — *parũ* — "a suburb; an outlying quarter".

> અમારું ઘર શહેરના પરામાં છે.
>
> *amārũ ghar śahernā parāmā̃ che.*
>
> Our house is in a suburb of the city.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરું** (*parũ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pura*, "a town, a fortress"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a suburb" (CDIAL 8278).
