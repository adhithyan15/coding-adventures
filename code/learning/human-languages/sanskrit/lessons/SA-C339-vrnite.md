## You'll want to know: वृणीते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C339-YUVARAJA339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वृणीते** — *vṛṇīte* — "chooses".

> कुमारी युवराजं वृणीते।
>
> *kumārī yuvarājaṃ vṛṇīte.*
>
> The maiden chooses the crown prince.

Past (she …): सा युवराजं वृतवती, *sā yuvarājaṃ vṛtavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृणीते** (*vṛṇīte*) comes from the root √vṛ, "to choose" (MW's second √vṛ), the root of *vara* too; MW gives *vṛta* as chosen, selected (MW).
