## You'll want to know: guarire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C489-GUARIRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**guarire** — "to recover, to get better; to heal".

> Sono guarito in pochi giorni.
>
> *I got better in a few days.*

With *essere* when you get better (*sono guarito*), with *avere* when you heal someone (*il medico mi ha guarito*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guarire* comes from a Germanic verb, Frankish *warjan* "to protect, to defend" (EtymDB): first to guard, then to cure.
