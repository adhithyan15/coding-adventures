# रुपया (rupayā) — a rupee

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Say *paisā*, then write **पैसा**: प with ै above it, then स, then ा.

## You'll want to know: रुपया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C72-COST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रुपया** (*rupayā*) — "a rupee".

Sanskrit रूप्य (*rūpya*) is "wrought silver, a stamped coin", and it comes
straight from **रूप** (*rūpa*), "form, shape". A rupee is a piece of metal that
has been **given a shape** — the name says nothing about value and everything
about the stamping.

That is worth holding beside पैसा. पैसा was named for being a fraction; रुपया was
named for being formed. One word looks at how small it is, the other at how it
was made.

The ु under the र is the short *u* matra, hanging below the letter rather than
above it.

A price is said in रुपये: **दस रुपये**, and you have counted to ten already.
