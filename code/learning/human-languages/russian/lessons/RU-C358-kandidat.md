## You'll want to know: кандидат
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C358-VAKANSIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**кандидат** (*kandidát*) — "a candidate".

> На эту должность пять кандидатов.
>
> *Na étu dólzhnost pyat kandidátov.*
>
> There are five candidates for this post.

Masculine. *Кандидат наук* is also a Russian academic degree, close to a doctorate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**кандидат** was borrowed from Polish *kandydat*, from Latin *candidātus* (EtymDB) "clothed in white", since Roman candidates for office wore a white toga (Lewis & Short); the root is *candidus* "white".
