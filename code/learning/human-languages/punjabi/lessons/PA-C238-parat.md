## You'll want to know: ਪਰਾਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C238-KAM238-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਾਤ** — *parāt* — "a kneading tray".

> ਪਰਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਟਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *parāt vich āṭā hai.*
>
> There is flour in the kneading tray.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਾਤ** (*parāt*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pātra*, "drinking vessel, dish"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *parāt*, "large wooden kneading vessel" (CDIAL 8055).
