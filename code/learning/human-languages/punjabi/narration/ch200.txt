Lesson 2 of 5.
One noun for a vegetable or cooked vegetable dish — vegetable or cooked vegetable dish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar first and last words of the identity frame.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਸਬਜ਼ੀ (sabzi) — sabzī — vegetable; cooked vegetable dish.
Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole noun rather than inventing smaller Punjabi pieces. This lesson owns the word alone; the complete identity frame comes only after a separate retrieval.

The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out.
Punjabi University's Punjabipedia records the present-day Punjabi vegetable category. It supports the bounded everyday vegetable meaning taught here. Source: Punjabi University Punjabipedia.
The exact origin of modern written ਸਬਜ਼ੀ (sabzi) is unresolved in the sources used here. Its origin remains unknown here. This lesson therefore makes no etymological claim. Keep the present-day Punjabi noun whole rather than turning spelling resemblance into a derivation. A Government of Punjab canteen rate list provides a current food-service use of the singular noun. Lesson 4 of 5.
Put the new noun into one identity frame — this is a vegetable or cooked vegetable dish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਇਹ, then the new noun, then ਹੈ.

Grammar Lens.
ਇਹ ਸਬਜ਼ੀ ਹੈ (eh sabzi hai) — eh sabzī hai — this is a vegetable / cooked vegetable dish.
Keep the new whole noun between familiar ਇਹ and ਹੈ. Familiar ਹੈ stays last in the already-owned identity frame. This chapter teaches only this one label; it does not add a form rule or a wider construction.
