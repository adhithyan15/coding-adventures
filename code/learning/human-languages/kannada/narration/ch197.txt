Kannada, chapter 197: Watchman, Daily wages, Bus conductor, Headmaster, Secretary.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಕಾವಲುಗಾರ (kāvalugāra) — a watchman, a security guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a spectator, then the Kannada for a carpenter.

You'll want to know: ಕಾವಲುಗಾರ.
ಕಾವಲುಗಾರ — kāvalugāra — "a watchman, a security guard".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾವಲುಗಾರ (kāvalugāra) is kāvalu, "guarding, watch", of the native kā, "to guard" (DEDR 1416, with Tamil kā), with -gāra, "one who does it".

ಕೂಲಿ (kūli) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kūli, Telugu kūli and Malayalam kūli (DEDR 1905). DEDR lists kūli itself, "hire, wages; day-labourer".
