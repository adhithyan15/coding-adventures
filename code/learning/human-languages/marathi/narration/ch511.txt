Lesson 1 of 6.
व्यवसाय (vyavsāy) — a business; a profession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to retire, then the Marathi for to dismiss.

You'll want to know: व्यवसाय.
व्यवसाय — vyavsāy — "a business; a profession".
माझ्या काकांचा कपड्यांचा व्यवसाय आहे. mājhyā kākāñcā kapaḍyāñcā vyavsāy āhe. My uncle has a clothes business.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
व्यवसाय (vyavsāy) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vyavasāya, "effort, undertaking" (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: नफा.
नफा — naphā — "profit".
या वर्षी दुकानाला चांगला नफा झाला. yā varṣī dukānālā cāṅglā naphā jhālā. The shop made a good profit this year.
Masculine.
Its opposite is तोटा: नफा (naphā) होणे, तोटा होणे.

The word, taken apart.
नफा (naphā) is a loan from Persian nafʿ, "gain", usually traced to Arabic nafʿ, "benefit".

भांडवल — bhāṇḍval — "capital; funds for a business".
दुकान सुरू करायला त्याला भांडवल हवं होतं. dukān surū karāylā tyālā bhāṇḍval havaṁ hotaṁ. He needed capital to start the shop.

The word, taken apart.
भांडवल (bhāṇḍval) is usually traced to Sanskrit bhāṇḍa, "pot, wares, goods" (CDIAL 9440), the stock a trader starts with; the ending is Marathi.

भागीदार — bhāgīdār — "a business partner".
दोन मित्र या व्यवसायात भागीदार आहेत. don mitra yā vyavsāyāt bhāgīdār āhet. Two friends are partners in this business.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
भागीदार (bhāgīdār) is built on भाग, "a share", from Sanskrit bhāga (Monier-Williams), with the Persian ending -dār, "holder": the one who holds a share.

Lesson 5 of 6.
गुंतवणे (guntavṇe) — to invest; to tie up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for capital, then the Marathi for a business partner.

You'll want to know: गुंतवणे.
गुंतवणे — guntavṇe — "to invest; to tie up".
भागीदारांनी नव्या दुकानात पैसे गुंतवले. bhāgīdārānnī navyā dukānāt paise guntavle. The partners invested money in the new shop.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पैसे गुंतवले, tyāne paise guntavle.
Where the money goes takes -त: व्यवसायात पैसे गुंतवणे (guntavṇe).

The word, taken apart.
गुंतवणे (guntavṇe) is built on गुंतणे, "to get tied up", as its causative; CDIAL sets गुंतणे under Sanskrit gupháti, "strings together" (CDIAL 4205): invested money is tied up in a business.
