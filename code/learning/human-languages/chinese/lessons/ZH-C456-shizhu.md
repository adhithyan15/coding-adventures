## You'll want to know: shīzhǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C456-LOST456-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shīzhǔ* — "the owner of a lost item".

> *wǒmen zhǎodào le yí ge qiánbāo zhèngzài zhǎo shīzhǔ* — We found a wallet and we're looking for its owner.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shīzhǔ* is built from *shī* 'to lose' and *zhǔ* 'an owner', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syit* and *tsyuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to err' and the second 'to be in charge'.
