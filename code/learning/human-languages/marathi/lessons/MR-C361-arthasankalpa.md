## You'll want to know: अर्थसंकल्प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C361-ARTHIK361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्थसंकल्प** — *arthasaṅkalpa* — "a budget".

> सरकारने या वर्षीचा अर्थसंकल्प मांडला.
>
> *sarkārne yā varṣīcā arthasaṅkalpa māṇḍlā.*
>
> The government presented this year's budget.

Masculine.

अर्थसंकल्प मांडणे, to present a budget.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्थसंकल्प** (*arthasaṅkalpa*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *artha*, "wealth, aim", and *saṁkalpa*, "will, resolve" (Monier-Williams): a plan for money.
