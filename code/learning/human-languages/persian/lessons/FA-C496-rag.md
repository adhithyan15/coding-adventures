## You'll want to know: رگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C496-NERVES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رگ** (*rag*) — "a vein, a blood vessel".

> پرستار رگ دستش را پیدا نکرد.
>
> *parastâr rag-e dastash râ peydâ nakard.*
>
> The nurse couldn't find a vein in his arm.

Plural *rag-hâ*. Also a streak or vein in stone or wood.
