Marathi, chapter 338: Ogres and Fairies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
राक्षस (rākṣas) — an ogre; a demon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for renowned, then the Marathi for a poet.

You'll want to know: राक्षस.
राक्षस — rākṣas — "an ogre; a demon".
गोष्टीतल्या राक्षसाला सात डोकी होती. goṣṭītalyā rākṣasālā sāt ḍokī hotī. The ogre in the story had seven heads.
Masculine.
Plural राक्षस (rākṣas).

The word, taken apart.
राक्षस (rākṣas) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit rākṣasa, 'a demon, an evil being' (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fairy, then the Marathi for magic.

You'll want to know: चेटकीण.
चेटकीण — ceṭkīṇ — "a witch".
जंगलातल्या चेटकिणीने राजकन्येला झोपवलं. jaṅglātalyā ceṭkiṇīne rājkanyelā jhopavlaṁ. The witch of the forest put the princess to sleep.
Feminine.
The oblique is चेटकिणी-: चेटकिणीने, by the witch.

The word, taken apart.
चेटकीण (ceṭkīṇ) is the Marathi word for a witch, with the feminine ending -īṇ; its origin is not settled.
