## You'll want to know: перерабатывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C369-EKOLOGIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**перерабатывать** (*pererabátyvat*) — "to recycle, to process".

> В нашем городе перерабатывают пластик.
>
> *V náshem górode pererabátyvayut plástik.*
>
> In our town they recycle plastic.

Imperfective; perfective *переработать*. It also means to work overtime.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**перерабатывать** is built from *пере-* "over again" and *работать* "to work", from *работа*, Proto-Slavic *\*orbota*: to work a material over into something new.
