Urdu, chapter 67: Desert, Dog, Cat, Horse, Cow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ریگستان (registān) — a desert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fire, then the Urdu for snow; ice.

You'll want to know: ریگستان.
ریگستان — registān — "a desert".

The word, taken apart.
ریگستان (registān) is a loan, usually traced to Persian rēgistān, "desert". It is built from rēg, "sand", and -stān, "place".

Lesson 2 of 5.
کتا (kuttā) — a dog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for snow; ice, then the Urdu for a desert.

You'll want to know: کتا.
کتا — kuttā — "a dog".

The word, taken apart.
کتا (kuttā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kutta, "dog", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3275). CDIAL calls it imitative, a word made from the sound used to call a dog.

Lesson 4 of 5.
گھوڑا (ghoṛā) — a horse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a dog, then the Urdu for a cat.

You'll want to know: گھوڑا.
گھوڑا — ghoṛā — "a horse".

The word, taken apart.
گھوڑا (ghoṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ghoṭa, "horse" (CDIAL 4516). CDIAL calls the word non-Aryan, probably Dravidian.
