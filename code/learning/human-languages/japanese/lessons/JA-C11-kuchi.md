## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-KUCHI]; assesses=[] -->

> **口** (くち) — *kuchi* — mouth

The three-stroke box you have written since chapter five is the word: **口**
is *kuchi*. Its reading joins two earned signs, **く** and **ち**; keep two even
morae.
