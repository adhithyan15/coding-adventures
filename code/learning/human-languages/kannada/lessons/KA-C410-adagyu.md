# ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ (ādāgyū) — however; nevertheless

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C410-HIGAGI410-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ** — *ādāgyū* — "however; nevertheless".

> ಅವನು ಬಡವ, ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *avanu baḍava, ādāgyū ellarigū sahāya māḍuttāne.*
>
> He is poor; nevertheless he helps everyone.

More formal than ಆದರೂ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ** (*ādāgyū*) is native Dravidian, built on forms of *āgu*, "to become" (DEDR 333), with -*ū*, "even": even so.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
