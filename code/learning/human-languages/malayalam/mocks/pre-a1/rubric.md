# Malayalam pre-A1 mock rubric

This rubric scores either pre-A1 mock against the exact task envelope in
[task-shapes/pre-a1.json](../../task-shapes/pre-a1.json). Reading, listening,
writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner passes the rung
only by earning at least 60/100 on **every** paper. A high score in one skill
never compensates for a failed skill.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
- signs and words: 40 points across 6 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
- phrases and notices: 30 points across 4 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->
- personal details: 30 points across 4 keyed items.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->

Within each part, divide its points equally among the keyed items and retain one
decimal place. Accept only the keyed meaning or situation. Reading aloud does
not earn reading credit. Traditional and reformed Malayalam spellings are both
valid where they differ; a keyed selection must still identify the printed form.

## Listening — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
- sounds and words: 40 points across 6 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
- greeting responses: 30 points across 4 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->
- personal details: 30 points across 4 keyed items.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->

Play every item twice with an eight-second answer pause. Score only the
learner's selected answer. Do not show a transcript, romanization, or
answer-bearing picture label.

## Writing — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
- delayed recall: 20 points, 10 per word;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
- dictation: 40 points, 10 per item;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
- bounded independent production: 40 points, 20 per response.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

For each delayed-recall item, award 4 points for a recognizable ordered
Malayalam letter sequence, 2 for chandrakkala placement, 2 for vowel-sign
placement, and 2 for legibility. For each dictation item, award 4 points for
sound-to-script mapping, 2 for a required word-final chandrakkala or chillu
letter, 2 for word-boundary control, and 2 for legibility. For each independent
response award 8 for the stated purpose, 6 for independent Malayalam word
formation, 4 for internally consistent conjunct and orthography choices, and 2
for legibility. Either reformed or traditional orthography earns full credit
when used consistently. A romanized, traced, visibly copied, or
non-Malayalam-script answer earns zero because it is not independent writing
evidence.

## Speaking — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
- greeting and identity: 30 points;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
- three familiar questions: 40 points;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
- short request: 30 points.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->

For each part award 40% for completing the communicative purpose, 25% for
intelligible Malayalam sounds, 20% for using an expected known word or chunk,
and 15% for responding without an answer script. The interlocutor may repeat
once slowly without penalty. A memorized chunk is valid at pre-A1; an
English-only response is not Malayalam speaking evidence.

## Readiness boundary

These rubrics, papers, and keys make the project-defined mock executable. They
do not by themselves establish exam readiness. The repository must still
record a clean timed administration and qualified human validation before
making a pass claim.
