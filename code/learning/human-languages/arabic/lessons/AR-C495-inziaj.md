## You'll want to know: انزعاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C495-INZIAJ495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انزعاج** — *inziʿāj* — "annoyance".

> تكلم الجار عن الموسيقى العالية بانزعاج.
>
> *takallama al-jār ʿan al-mūsīqā al-ʿāliya bi-inziʿāj.*
>
> The neighbour spoke about the loud music with annoyance.

The adjective مزعج, annoying, is from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انزعاج** (*inziʿāj*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-ʿ-j*, "to disquiet, to unsettle" (Lane). Inziʿāj is the verbal noun of Form VII: being unsettled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
