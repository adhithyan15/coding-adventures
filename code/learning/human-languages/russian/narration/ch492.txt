Russian, chapter 492: Step-parents, Twins and Cousins.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
отчим (ótchim) — a stepfather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a wife's father, then the Russian for a son-in-law.

You'll want to know: отчим.
отчим (ótchim) — "a stepfather".
Отчим относился к нему как к родному сыну. Ótchim otnosílsya k nemú kak k rodnómu sýnu. His stepfather treated him like his own son.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
отчим (ótchim) is built on отец 'father', through the old adjective отчий 'father's'; EtymDB has no entry for отчим (ótchim).

Lesson 2 of 6.
мачеха (máchekha) — a stepmother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a son-in-law, then the Russian for a stepfather.

You'll want to know: мачеха.
мачеха (máchekha) — "a stepmother".
В этой сказке мачеха была очень злой. V étoy skázke máchekha bylá óchen zloy. In this fairy tale the stepmother was very wicked.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
мачеха (máchekha) is usually explained as мать 'mother' with the ending -еха; EtymDB has no entry for мачеха (máchekha), and мать is inherited from Proto-Slavic mati (EtymDB).

У меня есть сводный брат. U menyá yest svódnyy brat. I have a stepbrother.
Сводный брат / сводная сестра — a stepbrother, a stepsister.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 6.
близнецы (bliznetsý) — twins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a stepmother, then the Russian for step-.

You'll want to know: близнецы.
близнецы (bliznetsý) — "twins".
Близнецы так похожи, что их путают даже друзья. Bliznetsý tak pokhózhi, chto ikh pútayut dázhe druzyá. The twins are so alike that even their friends mix them up.
Plural of близнец; genitive близнецов.

The word, taken apart.
близнецы (bliznetsý) is the plural of близнец, which is inherited from Proto-Slavic bliznьcь (EtymDB) and shares its root with близкий 'near, close', from Proto-Slavic blizъ 'near' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 6.
двоюродный (dvoyúrodnyy) — related as cousins.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for step-, then the Russian for twins.

You'll want to know: двоюродный.
двоюродный (dvoyúrodnyy) — "related as cousins".
Двоюродная сестра пригласила меня на свадьбу. Dvoyúrodnaya sestrá priglasíla menyá na svádbu. My cousin invited me to her wedding.
Двоюродный брат / двоюродная сестра — a (first) cousin.

The word, taken apart.
двоюродный (dvoyúrodnyy) is a compound that EtymDB traces to Old East Slavic dъvoju rodu 'of two generations', with род 'kin'.
