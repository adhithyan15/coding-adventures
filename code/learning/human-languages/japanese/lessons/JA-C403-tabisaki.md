## You'll want to know: たびさき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C403-BUNKA403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たびさき** — *tabisaki* — "a place visited on a trip".

> **たびさきで ともだちに てがみを かきました。** — *tabisaki de tomodachi ni tegami o kakimashita* — "I wrote to a friend from the place I was visiting."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たびさき** (*tabisaki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tabi*, "journey" and *saki*, "ahead"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
