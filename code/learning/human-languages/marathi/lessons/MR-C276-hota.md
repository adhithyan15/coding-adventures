# होता (hotā) — was, and there was

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-PHRASE-MI-BARA-AAHE, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I am well": *mī barā āhe*. Then "I went": *mī gelo*.

[PAUSE 1s] **आहे** has a past of its own, and it takes the endings of **गेलो**.

## You'll want to know: होता, 'was'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-AAHE-COPULA, MR-LEX-MI, MR-LEX-TO-TI-TE, MR-LEX-C115-TOWN115-04, MR-LEX-C96-NATURE96-05] -->

> **काल मी घरी होतो.**
>
> *kāl mī gharī hoto.* — "Yesterday I was at home."

- **मी** **होतो** (*hoto*) — I was; a woman says **होते** (*hote*)
- **तू** **होतास** (*hotās*) / **होतीस** (*hotīs*) — you were, to a friend
- **तो** **होता** (*hotā*), **ती** **होती** (*hotī*), **ते** **होतं** (*hotaṁ*) — he, she, it was
- **आम्ही** **होतो**, **तुम्ही** **होतात** (*hotāt*), **ते** **होते** (*hote*) — we, you, they were

It is also "there was":

> **गावात एक नदी होती.**
>
> *gāvāt ek nadī hotī.* — "There was a river in the village."

## You'll want to know: नव्हता, 'was not'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-NOT-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C94-SCHOOL94-01, MR-GRAMMAR-NEGATE-NAHI] -->

"Was not" is one word, with the same endings: **नव्हतो** (*navhto*), **नव्हता** (*navhtā*), **नव्हती** (*navhtī*), **नव्हतं** (*navhtaṁ*).

> **तो शाळेत नव्हता.**
>
> *to śāḷet navhtā.* — "He was not at school."

It is to **होता** what **नाही** is to **आहे**.
