## You'll want to know: کنڈکٹر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C393-JAM393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کنڈکٹر** — *kanḍakṭar* — "a conductor (on a bus)".

> کنڈکٹر نے کرایہ مانگا۔
>
> *kanḍakṭar ne kirāyā māṅgā.*
>
> The conductor asked for the fare.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کنڈکٹر** (*kanḍakṭar*) is borrowed from English *conductor*.
