## You'll want to know: پگھلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C289-BUJHI289-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پگھلنا** — *pighalnā* — "to melt".

> گرمی سے برف پگھل گئی۔
>
> *garmī se barf pighal gaī.*
>
> The ice melted in the heat.

Past (it …): وہ برف پگھلی, *voh barf pighlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پگھلنا** (*pighalnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pighalnā*, "to be melted", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8486).
