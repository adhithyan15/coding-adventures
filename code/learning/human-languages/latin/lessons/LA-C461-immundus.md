## You'll want to know: immundus, immunda, immundum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C461-THERMOPOLIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**immundus, immunda, immundum** — "filthy, dirty, unclean".

> Mēnsae immundae sunt, et cibus malus est.
>
> *The tables are filthy, and the food is bad.*

An adjective of three endings, from *in-* 'not' and *mundus* 'clean, neat'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*immundus* is built from *in-* 'not' and the adjective *mundus* 'clean, neat' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
