## You'll want to know: il pino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C461-ALBERI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il pino** — "the pine (tree)".

> Sulla spiaggia c'è ombra sotto i pini.
>
> *On the beach there is shade under the pines.*

*La pineta* — a pine wood; *i pinoli* are pine nuts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pino* is inherited from Latin *pīnus* 'pine' (EtymDB).
