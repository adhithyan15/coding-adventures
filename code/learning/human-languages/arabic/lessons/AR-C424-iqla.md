## You'll want to know: إقلاع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C424-IQLA424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إقلاع** — *iqlāʿ* — "takeoff".

> جلس المسافرون في مقاعدهم قبل الإقلاع.
>
> *jalasa al-musāfirūn fī maqāʿidihim qabla al-iqlāʿ.*
>
> The passengers sat in their seats before takeoff.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إقلاع** (*iqlāʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-l-ʿ*, "to pull up, to uproot" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form IV *aqlaʿa*, "to take off".
