## You'll want to know: सच में?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C112-RIGHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सच में?** (*sac meṁ?*) — "really?", literally "in truth?".

**सच** is the "true" you know, with **में**, "in".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A way to show surprise.
