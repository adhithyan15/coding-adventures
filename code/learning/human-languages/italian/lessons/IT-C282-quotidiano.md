## You'll want to know: il quotidiano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C282-CRONACA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il quotidiano** — "the daily newspaper".

> Compro un quotidiano ogni mattina.
>
> *I buy a daily paper every morning.*

As an adjective, *quotidiano* means daily, everyday: *la vita quotidiana*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quotidiano* is a learned borrowing from Latin *quotīdiānus* "daily" (EtymDB), built on *quot* "as many as" and *diēs* "day".
