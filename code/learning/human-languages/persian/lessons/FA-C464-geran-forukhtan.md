## You'll want to know: گران فروختن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C464-MAGHAZE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گران فروختن** (*gerân forukhtan*) — "to overcharge, to sell at too high a price".

> این فروشنده به مسافرها گران میفروشد.
>
> *In forushande be mosâfer-hâ gerân miforushad.*
>
> This seller overcharges travellers.

*gerân* 'dear' + *forukhtan* 'to sell'. Present stem *forush-*: *gerân miforushad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gerân forukhtan* is built from *gerân* 'dear, expensive', which EtymDB traces through Middle Persian *grān* to PIE *gʷréh₂us* 'heavy', and *forukhtan* 'to sell', usually traced to Middle Persian *frōxtan*.
