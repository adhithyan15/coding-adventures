## You'll want to know: anschaffen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C391-STEUER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**anschaffen** — "to acquire, to buy (something lasting)".

> Wir wollen uns eine neue Waschmaschine anschaffen.
>
> *We want to buy a new washing machine.*

Separable: *wir schaffen an*, *wir haben angeschafft*. Often with *sich*. *Die Anschaffung* — the purchase.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anschaffen* is built from *an* and *schaffen* "to make, to get done", inherited from Old High German *scaffan* and Proto-Germanic *skapjaną* "to create" (EtymDB), a cousin of English *shape*.
