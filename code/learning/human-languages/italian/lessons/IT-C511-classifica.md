## You'll want to know: la classifica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C511-RISULTATI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la classifica** — "league table, ranking; chart".

> Siamo terzi in classifica.
>
> *We are third in the league.*

For music too: *la classifica dei dischi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*classifica* comes from *classificare* "to classify", built on Latin *classis* "a class, a division" (Lewis & Short) and *facere* "to make".
