Bengali, chapter 464: Grave and Easygoing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গম্ভীর (gômbhīr) — grave; serious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to tease, then the Bengali for to tickle.

You'll want to know: গম্ভীর.
গম্ভীর — gômbhīr — "grave; serious".
খবরটা শুনে বাবা গম্ভীর হয়ে গেলেন। khôbôrṭā shune bābā gômbhīr hôye gelen. Father went grave when he heard the news.

The word, taken apart.
গম্ভীর (gômbhīr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit gambhīra, "deep" (CDIAL 4031), which Monier-Williams gives as a form of gabhīra, the source of গভীর (taught), deep; Bengali uses it for a deep, serious manner.

সাদাসিধে — sādāsidhe — "plain and simple; unpretentious".
দাদুর অনেক টাকা, কিন্তু তিনি সাদাসিধে জীবন কাটান। dādur ônek ṭākā, kintu tini sādāsidhe jībôn kāṭān. Grandfather has plenty of money, but he lives a plain and simple life.

The word, taken apart.
সাদাসিধে (sādāsidhe) pairs সাদা (taught), white and so plain, with sidhe, the Bengali sidhā, "straight, honest", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit siddha, "accomplished" (CDIAL 13401).

Lesson 5 of 5.
মানিয়ে নেওয়া (māniye neoyā) — to adjust; to adapt (to people or a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fussy, then the Bengali for indifferent.

You'll want to know: মানিয়ে নেওয়া.
মানিয়ে নেওয়া — māniye neoyā — "to adjust; to adapt (to people or a place)".
নতুন শহরে মীরা তাড়াতাড়ি মানিয়ে নিল। nôtun shôhôre mīrā tāṛātāṛi māniye nilô. Mira adapted quickly to the new city.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নতুন চাকরিতে মানিয়ে নিলেন, tini nôtun chākrite māniye nilen.

The word, taken apart.
মানিয়ে নেওয়া (māniye neoyā) is māniye, the -এ form of mānāno, "to fit, to suit", from the verb mānā that CDIAL lists for Bengali under Sanskrit manyate (CDIAL 9857), with neoyā, "to take" (CDIAL 6966).
