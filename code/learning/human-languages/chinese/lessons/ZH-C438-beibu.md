## You'll want to know: 北部
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C438-NORTH438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**北部** — *běibù* — "northern part".

> **我们坐火车去中国北部** *wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù Zhōngguó běibù* — We're taking the train up to the north of China.

The new character, in pieces: 部 — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left five strokes, a dot, a bar, two short strokes and a long bar, over 口 kǒu; on the right the right piece of 那, two strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**北部** (*běibù*) is built from 北 'north' and 部 'a part', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pok* and *buX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a body of troops; the sense 'a part of a whole' is not given there.
