## You'll want to know: alloggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C432-ALLOGGIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**alloggiare** — "to lodge, to stay".

> Alloggiamo in un piccolo albergo in centro.
>
> *We are staying in a small hotel in the centre.*

*L'alloggio* — lodging, accommodation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alloggiare* is built on *loggia* (EtymDB), which was borrowed from Old French *loge* 'shelter, arbour', from Frankish *laubja* 'arbour'.
