# ভবিতব্য (bhôbitôbyô) — what is bound to happen

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for to leave out, then the Bengali for destiny.

## You'll want to know: ভবিতব্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C532-BHOBITOBBO532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ভবিতব্য** — *bhôbitôbyô* — "what is bound to happen".

> ট্রেনটা ছেড়ে গেল; এটাই ভবিতব্য ছিল।
>
> *Ṭrenṭā chheṛe gelô; eṭāi bhôbitôbyô chhilô.*
>
> The train left; it was bound to happen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভবিতব্য** (*bhôbitôbyô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *bhavitavya*, "what is to be" (Monier-Williams), from *bhū*, "to be".
