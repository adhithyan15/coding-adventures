Urdu, chapter 465: Bones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ہڈی (haḍḍī) — a bone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an intestine, then the Urdu for a vein.

You'll want to know: ہڈی.
ہڈی — haḍḍī — "a bone".
کتا ہڈی کھا رہا تھا۔ kuttā haḍḍī khā rahā thā. The dog was eating a bone.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ہڈی (haḍḍī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin haḍḍī under Sanskrit haḍḍa, "bone" (CDIAL 13952).
