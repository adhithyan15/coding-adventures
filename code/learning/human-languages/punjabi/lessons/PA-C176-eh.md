# ਇਹ (eh) — this, near here

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-C89-PLACE89-03, PA-LEX-C89-PLACE89-04, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-02, PA-Q-KITTHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Put one familiar contrast back in your voice. Say **ਇੱਥੇ** (*itthe*,
here), then **ਉੱਥੇ** (*utthe*, there). Ask **ਕਿੱਥੇ?** (*kitthe?*, where?).
Now say **ਕਿਤਾਬ** (*kitāb*, book), and the old distant pointer **ਉਹ** (*oh*,
that).

## You'll want to know: ਇਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-DEM-EH-01]; assesses=[PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-C89-PLACE89-03, PA-LEX-C89-PLACE89-04] -->

> **ਇਹ** — *eh* — **this**, near here
>
> **ਉਹ** — *oh* — **that**, farther away

You have known **ਉਹ** from its earlier lesson as *he, she, it*. That lesson also told
you it points to **that**. Nothing new is being added to **ਉਹ** now. The new
half of the pair is **ਇਹ**: use *eh* for a nearby thing.

The same near/far choice already lives in **ਇੱਥੇ / ਉੱਥੇ**, *here / there*.
Keep the pairs aligned:

| near | farther away |
|---|---|
| **ਇਹ** — this | **ਉਹ** — that |
| **ਇੱਥੇ** — here | **ਉੱਥੇ** — there |

Wiktionary traces **ਇਹ** to Sanskrit *eṣa*, “this”.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-DEM-EH-01, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-02, PA-LEX-HAI-02, PA-GRAMMAR-VERB-LAST-02] -->

Keep **ਹੈ** at the end, where Punjabi already taught you to put the verb:

> **ਇਹ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ।** — *eh kitāb hai.* — This is a book.
>
> **ਉਹ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ।** — *oh kitāb hai.* — That is a book.

Only the first word changes. The book and the verb stay still.
