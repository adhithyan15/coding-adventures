## You'll want to know: عادت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C454-CHARACTER454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عادت** — *ādat* — "a habit".

> اسے صبح سویرے اٹھنے کی عادت ہے۔
>
> *use subah savere uṭhne kī ādat hai.*
>
> She is in the habit of getting up early.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عادت** (*ādat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿāda*, "custom, habit".
