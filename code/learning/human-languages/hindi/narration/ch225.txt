Lesson 1 of 5.
साफ़ करना (sāf karnā) — to clean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a team, then the Hindi for a race, running.

You'll want to know: साफ़ करना.
साफ़ करना — sāf karnā — "to clean".

The word, taken apart.
साफ़ करना (sāf karnā) is sāf, "clean", from Arabic ṣāfī, "pure", through Persian (Lane, root ṣ-f-w), with the inherited karnā, "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
पोंछना (põchnā) — to wipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to clean, then the Hindi for to dust.

You'll want to know: पोंछना.
पोंछना — põchnā — "to wipe".

The word, taken apart.
पोंछना (põchnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from proñchati, "wipes off" (CDIAL 9011). CDIAL suggests that the Sanskrit word itself was made over from a Middle Indo-Aryan poṁch-, perhaps itself from Dravidian.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: सुखाना.
सुखाना — sukhānā — "to dry (something)".

The word, taken apart.
सुखाना (sukhānā) is the causing form of sūkhnā, "to dry", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed śuṣkati, "becomes dry", built on Sanskrit śuṣka, "dried" (CDIAL 12552).
