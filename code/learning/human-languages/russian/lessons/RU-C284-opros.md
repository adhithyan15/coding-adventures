## You'll want to know: опрос
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C284-INTERVYU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**опрос** (*oprós*) — "a survey, a poll".

> После опроса все узнали мнение студентов.
>
> *Pósle oprósa vse uználi mnéniye studéntov.*
>
> After the survey everyone learned what the students thought.

*Опрос общественного мнения* — an opinion poll.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*опрос* is built on *о-* and *просить* "to ask", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *prositi*, from PIE *preḱ-* "to ask" (EtymDB): asking round.
