# নিষ্ফল (nishphôl) — fruitless; in vain

## You'll want to know: নিষ্ফল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C542-BHESTE542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষ্ফল** — *nishphôl* — "fruitless; in vain".

> আমাদের সব পরিকল্পনা নিষ্ফল হল।
>
> *Āmāder sôb pôrikôlpônā nishphôl hôlô.*
>
> All our plans came to nothing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষ্ফল** (*nishphôl*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *niṣphala*, "bearing no fruit, useless" (Monier-Williams).
