# వెళ్ళాను (veḷḷānu) — the plain past: what happened

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C32-VELLU-02, TE-LEX-C32-VELLU-01, TE-LEX-C314-PANI314-04, TE-LEX-C314-PANI314-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I go", or "I will go". (*nēnu veḷtānu*.) Then the last two people you
met: the watchman and the milkman. (*kāpalādāru*, *pālavāḍu*.)

[PAUSE 1s] **వెళ్తాను** says what happens, or what will. A story has already happened. It needs the past.

## You'll want to know: the plain past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-SIMPLE-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C75-WHEN-02, TE-LEX-C77-FROM-02] -->

> **నిన్న నేను పట్టణానికి వెళ్ళాను.**
>
> *ninna nēnu paṭṭaṇāniki veḷḷānu.* — "Yesterday I went to the town."

**వెళ్ళాను** is "I went". Where the form you know has **-తా-** (**వెళ్తాను**), the past has **-ఆ-**:
**వెళ్ళ** + **ఆను**. The person ending at the end works the way it does in **వెళ్తాను**:

- **నేను వెళ్ళాను** (*nēnu veḷḷānu*) — I went
- **నువ్వు వెళ్ళావు** (*nuvvu veḷḷāvu*) — you went
- **అతను వెళ్ళాడు** (*atanu veḷḷāḍu*) — he went
- **ఆమె వెళ్ళింది** (*āme veḷḷindi*) — she went, and "it went"
- **మేము వెళ్ళాము** (*mēmu veḷḷāmu*) — we went
- **వాళ్ళు వెళ్ళారు** (*vāḷḷu veḷḷāru*) — they went; with **మీరు**, "you went", said with respect

Only "she" and "it" step out of line: **వెళ్ళింది**, with **-ఇంది**.

## You'll want to know: the stems that change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-STEMS-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C33-CADUVU-01, TE-LEX-C46-ASK-05, TE-LEX-C32-TINU-01, TE-LEX-C40-BHOJANAM-01] -->

Many verbs take the past on their own stem. A **-ఉ-** before the last consonant turns to **-ఇ-**:
**చదువు** → **చదివాను** (*cadivānu*), "I read"; **నడుచు** → **నడిచాను** (*naḍicānu*), "I walked".

A few of the commonest verbs change more, and they are worth learning as pairs:

- **వచ్చు** → **వచ్చాను** (*vaccānu*), "I came"
- **చూడు** → **చూశాను** (*cūśānu*), "I saw"; she saw: **చూసింది** (*cūsindi*)
- **చేయు** → **చేశాను** (*cēśānu*), "I did"; she did: **చేసింది** (*cēsindi*)
- **తిను** → **తిన్నాను** (*tinnānu*), "I ate"
- **ఇవ్వు** → **ఇచ్చాను** (*iccānu*), "I gave"

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "Yesterday I went to the town" — *ninna nēnu paṭṭaṇāniki veḷḷānu*]
- [YOU SAY: "She came in the evening" — *āme sāyantraṁ vaccindi*]
- [YOU SAY: "We drank tea" — *mēmu ṭī tāgāmu*]
- [YOU SAY: "I read a book. After that I had a meal." — *nēnu oka pustakaṁ cadivānu. ā taruvāta bhōjanaṁ cēśānu.*]
