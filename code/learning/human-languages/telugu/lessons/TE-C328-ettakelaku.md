# ఎట్టకేలకు (eṭṭakēlaku) — at long last

## You'll want to know: ఎట్టకేలకు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C328-KRAMAM328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎట్టకేలకు** — *eṭṭakēlaku* — "at long last".

> ఎట్టకేలకు బస్సు వచ్చింది.
>
> *eṭṭakēlaku bassu vaccindi.*
>
> At long last the bus came.

It tells you the wait was long; చివరికి only says what came last.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎట్టకేలకు** (*eṭṭakēlaku*) is a Telugu adverb of time; its origin is not settled, and DEDR has no entry for it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
