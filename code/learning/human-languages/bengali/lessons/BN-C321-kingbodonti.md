# কিংবদন্তি (kingbôdônti) — a legend

## You'll want to know: কিংবদন্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C321-KINGBODONTI321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**কিংবদন্তি** — *kingbôdônti* — "a legend".

> গ্রামের একটা কিংবদন্তি আছে যে পাহাড়ের গুহায় এক রাজা থাকতেন।
>
> *grāmer ekṭā kingbôdônti āchhe je pāhāṛer guhāy ek rājā thākten.*
>
> There's a village legend that a king once lived in the cave in the hills.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কিংবদন্তি** (*kingbôdônti*) is Sanskrit *kiṃvadantī*, 'what do they say?' — a common saying, a rumour, a tradition (Monier-Williams). A legend is what people say.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
