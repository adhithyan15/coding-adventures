## You'll want to know: l'épisode
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C523-SERIES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'épisode** — "episode".

> J'ai regardé trois épisodes hier soir.
>
> *I watched three episodes last night.*

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'épisode* comes from Greek *epeisódion* "an added part" (EtymDB), built on *epí* "upon" and *eísodos* "an entrance".
