## You'll want to know: निश्चय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C556-SANDEH556-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चय** — *niścay* — "a firm decision; a resolve".

> मैंने रोज़ पढ़ने का निश्चय किया।
>
> *Maiṁne roz paṛhne kā niścay kiyā.*
>
> I decided firmly to study every day.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चय** (*niścay*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *niścaya*, "certainty; resolve" (Monier-Williams).
