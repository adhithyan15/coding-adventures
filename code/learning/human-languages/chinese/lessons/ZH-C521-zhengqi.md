## You'll want to know: zhěngqí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C521-CHORES521-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhěngqí* — "neat; orderly".

> *tā de fángjiān bǐ wǒ de zhěngqí* — His room is tidier than mine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhěngqí* is built from *zhěng* and *qí*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyengX* and *dzej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses each with the other: 'upright, even'.
