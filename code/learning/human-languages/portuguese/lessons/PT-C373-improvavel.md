# improvável — unlikely, improbable

## You'll want to know: improvável
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C373-RISCO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**improvável** — "unlikely, improbable".

> É improvável que ele mude de ideias.
>
> *It is unlikely that he will change his mind.*

*É improvável que* takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*improvável* comes from Latin *improbābilis* "not credible" (EtymDB), built from *in-* "not" and *probābilis* "likely", on *probāre* "to prove" (Lewis & Short).
