## You'll want to know: 全国
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C381-FAMILY381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**全国** — *quánguó* — "the whole country; nationwide".

> **今天全国都很冷** *jīntiān quánguó dōu hěn lěng* — It's cold all over the country today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**全国** (*quánguó*) is built from 全 'whole' and 国 'a country', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjwen* and *kwok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a state'.
