## You'll want to know: गृहीत धरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C383-BHAKIT383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहीत धरणे** — *gṛhīt dharṇe* — "to assume; to take for granted".

> तू येणार नाहीस असं मी गृहीत धरलं.
>
> *tū yeṇār nāhīs asaṁ mī gṛhīt dharlaṁ.*
>
> I assumed that you were not coming.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने गृहीत धरलं, *tyāne gṛhīt dharlaṁ*.

Also of people: कोणालाही गृहीत धरणे, to take someone for granted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहीत धरणे** (*gṛhīt dharṇe*) joins the tatsama गृहीत, Sanskrit *gṛhīta*, "seized, taken, accepted" (Monier-Williams), to धरणे, "to hold".
