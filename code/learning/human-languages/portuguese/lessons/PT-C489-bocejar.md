## You'll want to know: bocejar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C489-SONO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**bocejar** — "to yawn".

> Os alunos bocejavam na aula de sexta à tarde.
>
> *The pupils were yawning in the Friday afternoon class.*

A yawn is *um bocejo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bocejar* is usually explained as built on *boca* "mouth", which is inherited from Latin *bucca* "cheek, mouth" (Lewis & Short).
