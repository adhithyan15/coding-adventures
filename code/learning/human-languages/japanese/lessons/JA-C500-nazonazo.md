## You'll want to know: なぞなぞ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C500-TOY500-04]; assesses=[] -->

**なぞなぞ** — *nazonazo* — "a riddle".

> **そふは なぞなぞが すきです。** — *sofu wa nazonazo ga suki desu* — "My grandfather loves riddles."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なぞなぞ** (*nazonazo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
