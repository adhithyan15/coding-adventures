Lesson 1 of 5.
विचित्र (vicitra) — strange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for unpleasant, then the Marathi for enthusiastic.

You'll want to know: विचित्र.
विचित्र — vicitra — "strange".

The word, taken apart.
विचित्र (vicitra) is Sanskrit vicitra, "variegated, wonderful, strange", a tatsama built on citra, "bright, variegated" (CDIAL 4803).

Lesson 2 of 5.
वेगळा / वेगळी (vegḷā / vegḷī) — different.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for enthusiastic, then the Marathi for strange.

You'll want to know: वेगळा / वेगळी.
वेगळा / वेगळी — vegḷā / vegḷī — "different".

The word, taken apart.
वेगळा (vegḷā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from viyagna, "separate", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 11826). CDIAL notes the added -la- of the Marathi form.

You'll want to know: सारखा / सारखी.
सारखा / सारखी — sārkhā / sārkhī — "similar, alike".

The word, taken apart.
सारखा (sārkhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sadṛkṣa, "like" (CDIAL 13119). It is sa-, "same", and dṛś, "to see".
