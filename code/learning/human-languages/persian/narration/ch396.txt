Persian, chapter 396: Changing plans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منصرف شدن (monsaref shodan) — to change one's mind, to give up an idea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for as soon as, then the Persian for as long as.

You'll want to know: منصرف شدن.
منصرف شدن (monsaref shodan) — "to change one's mind, to give up an idea".
او از سفر منصرف شد. u az safar monsaref shod. He decided not to go on the trip.
It takes az: az chizi monsaref shodan. Stem shav-.

The word, taken apart.
منصرف is usually traced to Arabic munṣarif "turning away"; with the Persian verb shodan "to become", you turn away from a plan.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for as long as, then the Persian for to change one's mind.

You'll want to know: عقب انداختن.
عقب انداختن ('aqab andâkhtan) — "to put off, to delay".
جلسه را یک هفته عقب انداختند. jalase râ yek hafte 'aqab andâkhtand. They put the meeting off by a week.
'aqab "back" + andâkhtan "to throw" (present stem andâz-): to throw something back.

The word, taken apart.
عقب انداختن ('aqab andâkhtan) is literally "to throw back": 'aqab "back" is usually traced to Arabic 'aqab, and andâkhtan "to throw" is a native Persian verb listed in EtymDB.

Lesson 3 of 5.
به تعویق انداختن (be ta'viq andâkhtan) — to postpone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to change one's mind, then the Persian for to put off.

You'll want to know: به تعویق انداختن.
به تعویق انداختن (be ta'viq andâkhtan) — "to postpone".
دانشگاه امتحانها را به تعویق انداخت. dâneshgâh emtehânhâ râ be ta'viq andâkht. The university postponed the exams.
Formal. Without an object: be ta'viq oftâdan — to be postponed.

The word, taken apart.
به تعویق انداختن (be ta'viq andâkhtan) is literally "to throw into delay": ta'viq is usually traced to Arabic ta'wīq "delaying", and andâkhtan "to throw" is native Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
هماهنگ کردن (hamâhang kardan) — to coordinate, to arrange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to put off, then the Persian for to postpone.

You'll want to know: هماهنگ کردن.
هماهنگ کردن (hamâhang kardan) — "to coordinate, to arrange".
من با همکارانم زمان جلسه را هماهنگ کردم. man bâ hamkârânam zamân-e jalase râ hamâhang kardam. I arranged the meeting time with my colleagues.
Hamâhang "in harmony": ham "same" + âhang "tune". Bâ kasi hamâhang kardan — to arrange something with someone.

The word, taken apart.
هماهنگ کردن (hamâhang kardan) is native Persian, built from ham "same, together" + âhang "tune", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian āhang, + kardan. To coordinate is to put people in one tune.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اجرا کردن (ejrâ kardan) — to carry out, to perform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to postpone, then the Persian for to coordinate.

You'll want to know: اجرا کردن.
اجرا کردن (ejrâ kardan) — "to carry out, to perform".
شهرداری طرح تازه را اجرا کرد. shahrdâri tarh-e tâze râ ejrâ kard. The town council carried out the new plan.
Ejrâ "carrying out" + kardan. Also to perform music or a play.

The word, taken apart.
اجرا is usually traced to Arabic ijrā' "making something run, putting it into effect"; Persian drops the final hamza and adds kardan "to do".
