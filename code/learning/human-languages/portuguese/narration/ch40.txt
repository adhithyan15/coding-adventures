Lesson 2 of 5.
dançar — to dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a girl, then the Portuguese for to sing.

You'll want to know: dançar.
dançar — "to dance".

The word, taken apart.
dançar from Old French dancier, "to dance" — borrowed. EtymDB 2.1 traces the French verb to Frankish, a Germanic language. English dance is the same word.
