## You'll want to know: ਸੁਲ੍ਹਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C316-QUARREL316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁਲ੍ਹਾ** — *sulhā* — "a reconciliation".

> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਸਾਡੀ ਸੁਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੋਈ।
>
> *shām nū̃ sāḍī sulhā hoī.*
>
> In the evening we made up.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁਲ੍ਹਾ** (*sulhā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ṣulḥ*, "peace, reconciliation".
