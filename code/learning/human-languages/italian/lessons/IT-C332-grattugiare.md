## You'll want to know: grattugiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C332-TAGLIERE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**grattugiare** — "to grate".

> Grattugia un po' di formaggio sulla pasta.
>
> *Grate a little cheese over the pasta.*

Present *grattugio*, *grattugi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grattugiare* is built on *grattugia* "grater", from *grattare* "to scratch", which is usually traced to a Germanic word (EtymDB has no entry).
