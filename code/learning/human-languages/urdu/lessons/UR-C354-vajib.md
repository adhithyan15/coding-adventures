## You'll want to know: واجب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C354-MAQUL354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**واجب** — *vājib* — "due; fair (of a demand)".

> مزدوروں کی بات واجب ہے۔
>
> *mazdūroṅ kī bāt vājib hai.*
>
> The workers have a fair point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واجب** (*vājib*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wājib*, "due, necessary".
