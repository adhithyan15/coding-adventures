## You'll want to know: खडखडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C396-AAGGADI396-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खडखडणे** — *khaḍkhaḍṇe* — "to rattle".

> जुना डबा रुळावर खडखडत होता.
>
> *junā ḍabā ruḷāvar khaḍkhaḍat hotā.*
>
> The old coach was rattling on the rails.

Past (he …): तो खडखडला, *to khaḍkhaḍlā*.

An echo word made into a verb: खड खड is the noise itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खडखडणे** (*khaḍkhaḍṇe*) is inherited and imitates its sound: CDIAL lists Marathi *khaḍkhaḍṇẽ*, "to rattle", under Sanskrit *khaṭakhaṭāyate*, "crackles" (CDIAL 3771).
