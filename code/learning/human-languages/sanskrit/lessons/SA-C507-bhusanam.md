## You'll want to know: भूषणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C507-BHASTRA507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भूषणम्** — *bhūṣaṇam* — "an ornament".

> स्वर्णकारः सुवर्णात् भूषणानि रचयति।
>
> *svarṇakāraḥ suvarṇāt bhūṣaṇāni racayati.*
>
> The goldsmith makes ornaments from gold.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूषणम्** (*bhūṣaṇam*) comes from the root √bhūṣ, "to adorn"; MW gives *bhūṣaṇa* as decorating, an ornament (MW).
