## You'll want to know: غمر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C487-MALJA487-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غمر** — *ghamara* — "to submerge; to flood".

> غمر الماء الشارع بعد المطر.
>
> *ghamara al-māʾ ash-shāriʿ baʿda al-maṭar.*
>
> Water flooded the street after the rain.

Present: يغمر, *yaghmur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غمر** (*ghamara*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-m-r*, "abundant water; to cover" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
