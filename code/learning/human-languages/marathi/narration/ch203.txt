Lesson 2 of 5.
खरोखर (kharokhar) — really.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for up, then the Marathi for certainly.

You'll want to know: खरोखर.
खरोखर — kharokhar — "really".

The word, taken apart.
खरोखर (kharokhar) is kharā, "true", doubled with a linking -ो-. CDIAL lists Marathi kharā, "pure, good, firm", under Sanskrit khara, "hard, sharp" (CDIAL 3819).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सुमारे (sumāre) — about, approximately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for really, then the Marathi for almost.

You'll want to know: सुमारे.
सुमारे — sumāre — "about, approximately".

The word, taken apart.
सुमारे (sumāre) is often traced to Persian shumār, "count, number", but no source here settles it. Neither CDIAL nor DEDR has an entry for it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
फक्त (phakta) — only.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for almost, then the Marathi for about.

You'll want to know: फक्त.
फक्त — phakta — "only".

The word, taken apart.
फक्त (phakta) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic faqaṭ, "only", with फ for the f.
