# ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ (kēḷisalilla) — I didn't hear

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C86-WAIT-02, KA-LEX-C86-WAIT-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for please wait, then the Kannada for please show.

## You'll want to know: ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C86-WAIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ** (*kēḷisalilla*) — "(it) was not heard", the way to say "I didn't
hear". It is built on **ಕೇಳು**, "hear, listen", with **‑ಇಲ್ಲ**, "not".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A known verb with a known no.
