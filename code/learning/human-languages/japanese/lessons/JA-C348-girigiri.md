## You'll want to know: ぎりぎり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C348-KESSEKI348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎりぎり** — *girigiri* — "at the last minute; barely".

> **いま でたら、ぎりぎり まにあうでしょう。** — *ima detara, girigiri maniau deshō* — "If you leave now, you will barely make it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎりぎり** (*girigiri*) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
