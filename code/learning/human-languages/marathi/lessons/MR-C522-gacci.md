## You'll want to know: गच्ची
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C522-KHOLI522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गच्ची** — *gaccī* — "a terrace; a flat roof".

> उन्हाळ्यात आम्ही गच्चीवर झोपायचो.
>
> *unhāḷyāt āmhī gaccīvar jhopāyco.*
>
> In summer we used to sleep on the terrace.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गच्ची** (*gaccī*) is the word for a flat roof or terrace; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
