## You'll want to know: ronfler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C486-NUITS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ronfler** — "to snore".

> Mon colocataire ronfle très fort.
>
> *My flatmate snores very loudly.*

Also of an engine: *le moteur ronfle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ronfler* goes back to Old French *ronfler* (EtymDB); it is usually taken to imitate the sound, perhaps with help from *souffler* "to blow".
