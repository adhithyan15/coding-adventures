# పోషకాహారం (pōṣakāhāraṁ) — nutrition; nourishing food

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-03, TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to grow thin, then the Telugu for to put on weight.

## You'll want to know: పోషకాహారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పోషకాహారం** — *pōṣakāhāraṁ* — "nutrition; nourishing food".

> పిల్లలకు పోషకాహారం చాలా అవసరం.
>
> *pillalaku pōṣakāhāraṁ cālā avasaraṁ.*
>
> Children need nourishing food.

పోషక, nourishing, + ఆహారం, food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పోషకాహారం** (*pōṣakāhāraṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *poṣaka*, "nourishing" (Monier-Williams), and *āhāra*, "taking food, food" (CDIAL 1544).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
