## You'll want to know: देहात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C421-BASTI421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**देहात** — *dehāt* — "the countryside; villages".

> मेरे दादा देहात में रहते हैं।
>
> *mere dādā dehāt meṁ rahte haiṁ.*
>
> My grandfather lives in the countryside.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देहात** (*dehāt*) is usually traced to Persian *dehāt*, "villages", the plural of *deh*, "a village"; in Hindi it is the countryside as a whole, set against the town.
