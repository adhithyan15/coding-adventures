## You'll want to know: جگمگانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C317-EID317-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جگمگانا** — *jagmagānā* — "to glitter; to be lit up".

> بازار روشنی سے جگمگا رہا تھا۔
>
> *bāzār raushnī se jagmagā rahā thā.*
>
> The market was glittering with lights.

Past (it …): وہ شہر جگمگایا, *voh shahr jagmagāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جگمگانا** (*jagmagānā*) is built on *jagmag*, "glitter", which CDIAL lists for Hindi under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5076). The doubled *jag-mag* is the twinkle of many lights.
