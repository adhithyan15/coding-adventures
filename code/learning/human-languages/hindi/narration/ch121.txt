Hindi, chapter 121: Take Care.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ध्यान रखिए (dhyān rakhie) — take care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a person, then the Hindi for nice to meet you.

You'll want to know: ध्यान रखिए.
ध्यान रखिए (dhyān rakhie) — "take care", literally "keep attention".
अपना ध्यान रखिए (dhyān rakhie) — "take care of yourself", the kindest goodbye.

What you've built.
The kindest thing to say at the door.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dhyān rakhie]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dhyān rakhie, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say apnā dhyān rakhie]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for a person, then the Hindi for nice to meet you, then say dhyān rakhie again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ध्यान रखिए (dhyān rakhie) mean? ("Take care".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
शुभ यात्रा (śubh yātrā) — have a good journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for take care, then the Hindi for carefully.

You'll want to know: शुभ यात्रा.
शुभ यात्रा (śubh yātrā) — "have a good journey".
शुभ is the auspicious of शुभ रात्रि; यात्रा is "a journey", a formal cousin of सफ़र.

What you've built.
A wish for someone setting off.

Lesson 4 of 5.
फिर आइएगा (phir āiegā) — do come again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for carefully, then the Hindi for a good journey.

You'll want to know: फिर आइएगा.
फिर आइएगा (phir āiegā) — "do come again".
फिर is "again", and आइएगा is an extra-polite आइए, a request for later rather than now.

What you've built.
An invitation to return.

Lesson 5 of 5.
चलिए (calie) — let's go, shall we.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a good journey, then the Hindi for come again.

You'll want to know: चलिए.
चलिए (calie) — "let's go", "shall we".
It is चलना with the respectful ending of बोलिए, the verb inside चलता हूँ, "I'll be off".

What you've built.
Take care, carefully, a good journey, come again, let's go.
