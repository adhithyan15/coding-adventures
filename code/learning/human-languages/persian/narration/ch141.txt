Persian, chapter 141: To lose, To find, To give back, To settle the bill, To order.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گم کردن (gom kardan) — to lose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to switch off, then the Persian for to wake up.

You'll want to know: گم کردن.
گم کردن — gom kardan — "to lose".

The word, taken apart.
gom kardan pairs gom, "lost", a native word usually traced to Middle Persian gum, with kardan, "to make": "to make lost". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
پس دادن (pas dâdan) — to give back, to return (an item).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to lose, then the Persian for to find.

You'll want to know: پس دادن.
پس دادن — pas dâdan — "to give back, to return (an item)".

The word, taken apart.
pas dâdan pairs pas, "back, behind", with dâdan, "to give": "to give back". pas is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian pas, "then, behind". dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".

The word, taken apart.
hesâb kardan pairs hesâb, borrowed from Arabic ḥisāb, "account, reckoning" (root ḥ-s-b, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

سفارش دادن — sefâresh dâdan — "to order (food, goods)".

The word, taken apart.
sefâresh dâdan pairs sefâresh, "commission, order", with dâdan, "to give". sefâresh is native, usually explained as built on sepâr-, the present stem of sepordan, "to entrust". dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".
