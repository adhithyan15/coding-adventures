## You'll want to know: تفریح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C513-FURSAT513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تفریح** — *tafrīh* — "recreation (an outing for pleasure)".

> ہم تفریح کے لیے ساحل پر گئے۔
>
> *ham tafrīh ke liye sāhil par gae.*
>
> We went to the beach to enjoy ourselves.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفریح** (*tafrīh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tafrīḥ*, "gladdening, cheering up".
