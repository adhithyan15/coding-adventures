## You'll want to know: こいしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C433-LONG433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こいしい** — *koishii* — "longed for; missed dearly".

> **ふゆは あたたかい なつが こいしいです。** — *fuyu wa atatakai natsu ga koishii desu* — "In winter I long for the warm summer."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こいしい** (*koishii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
