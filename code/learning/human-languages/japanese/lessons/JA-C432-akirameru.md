## You'll want to know: あきらめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C432-WORRY432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あきらめる** — *akirameru* — "to give up; to resign oneself".

> **あめで りょこうを あきらめました。** — *ame de ryokō o akiramemashita* — "We gave up on the trip because of the rain."

Polite **あきらめます**, *akiramemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが ゆめを あきらめました。**, *senshu ga yume o akiramemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あきらめる** (*akirameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
