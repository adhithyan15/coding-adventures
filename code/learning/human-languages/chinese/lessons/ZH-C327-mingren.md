## You'll want to know: 名人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C327-HERO327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**名人** — *míngrén* — "famous person; well-known figure".

> **他是一个世界名人** *tā shì yí ge shìjiè míngrén* — He's famous all over the world.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**名人** (*míngrén*) is built from 名 'a name' and 人 'a person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjieng* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a name'.
