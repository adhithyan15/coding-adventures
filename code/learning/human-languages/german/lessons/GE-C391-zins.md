## You'll want to know: der Zins
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C391-STEUER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zins** — "the interest (on money)".

> Die Bank zahlt nur ein Prozent Zinsen.
>
> *The bank pays only one per cent interest.*

Mostly plural: *die Zinsen*. In southern Germany and Austria *der Zins* can also mean rent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zins* is inherited from Middle High German and Old High German *zins* "tax, tribute, interest", an early borrowing from Latin *cēnsus* "assessment, tax" (EtymDB), from *cēnsēre* "to assess".
