## You'll want to know: kǒngpà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C264-REFUSE264-04]; assesses=[] -->

*kǒngpà* — "I'm afraid that; probably".

> *kǒngpà tā bú huì lái le* — I'm afraid he won't come.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kǒngpà* is built from *kǒng* and *pà*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khjowngX* and *phaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses both 'to fear'.
