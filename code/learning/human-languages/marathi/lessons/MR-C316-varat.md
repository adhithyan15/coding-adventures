## You'll want to know: वरात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C316-LAGNA316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वरात** — *varāt* — "a wedding procession".

> वरात वाजत गाजत मंडपात पोहोचली.
>
> *varāt vājat gājat maṇḍpāt pohoclī.*
>
> The wedding procession reached the marquee with music and fanfare.

Feminine.

The groom and his party come in it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वरात** (*varāt*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *varāt*, 'the groom's procession', under Sanskrit *varayātrā*, 'the procession of a suitor or bridegroom' (CDIAL 11322).
