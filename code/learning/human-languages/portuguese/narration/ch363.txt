Portuguese, chapter 363: Cidadãos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o cidadão — the citizen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the retirement, then the Portuguese for retired.

You'll want to know: o cidadão.
o cidadão — "the citizen".
Todos os cidadãos podem votar aos dezoito anos. All citizens can vote at eighteen.
Plural os cidadãos; feminine a cidadã. O cartão de cidadão is the Portuguese identity card.

The word, taken apart.
cidadão is inherited from Old Portuguese çidadão (EtymDB), built on cidade "city", from Latin cīvitās "citizenship, city", on cīvis "citizen" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
o direito — the right; law (as a subject).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the citizen, then the Portuguese for the citizenship.

You'll want to know: o direito.
o direito — "the right; law (as a subject)".
Todas as crianças têm direito à educação. All children have a right to education.
Ter direito a — to have a right to. Estudar Direito — to study law. Also the adjective right: a mão direita.

The word, taken apart.
direito is inherited from Latin dīrēctus "straight", the participle of dīrigere "to set straight" (Lewis & Short), built on regere "to guide, to rule"; -ct- became -it- as in feito.
