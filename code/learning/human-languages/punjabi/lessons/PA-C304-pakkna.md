## You'll want to know: ਪੱਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C304-GROW304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੱਕਣਾ** — *pakkṇā* — "to ripen".

> ਵਿਸਾਖ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਣਕ ਪੱਕੇਗੀ।
>
> *visākh vich kaṇak pakkegī.*
>
> The wheat will ripen in Visakh.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਕੇਲਾ ਪੱਕਿਆ, *oh kelā pakkiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੱਕਣਾ** (*pakkṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pakva*, "cooked, ripe"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pakkṇā*, "to ripen", under it (CDIAL 7621).
