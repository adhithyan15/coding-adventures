## You'll want to know: پرواز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C431-PARVAZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پرواز** (*parvâz*) — "a flight".

> پرواز ما ساعت شش صبح است.
>
> *Parvâz-e mâ sâ'at-e shesh-e sobh ast.*
>
> Our flight is at six in the morning.

*Parvâz-e mostaqim* — a direct flight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parvâz* is a native Persian word, which EtymDB lists with the sense 'flight'. It is usually linked with *par* 'feather, wing', though EtymDB gives no older form.
