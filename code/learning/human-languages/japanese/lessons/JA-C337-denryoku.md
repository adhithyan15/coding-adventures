# でんりょく (denryoku) — electric power

## You'll want to know: でんりょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C337-SETSUYAKU337-02]; assesses=[] -->

**でんりょく** — *denryoku* — "electric power".

> **なつは でんりょくが たりないと おもいます。** — *natsu wa denryoku ga tarinai to omoimasu* — "I think there is not enough electric power in summer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**でんりょく** (*denryoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *den*, "electricity" (Middle Chinese *denH*) and *ryoku*, "power" (*lik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
