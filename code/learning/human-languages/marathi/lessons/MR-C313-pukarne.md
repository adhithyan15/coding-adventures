## You'll want to know: पुकारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C313-AFVA313-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुकारणे** — *pukārṇe* — "to proclaim; to call a strike".

> कामगारांनी उद्यापासून संप पुकारला.
>
> *kāmgārānnī udyāpāsūn samp pukārlā.*
>
> The workers called a strike from tomorrow.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पुकारलं, *tyāne pukārlaṁ*.

संप पुकारणे, to call a strike; it also means to call out to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुकारणे** (*pukārṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *pukārṇe*, 'to shout', under *\*pukkār*, 'shout', a word made from the sound (CDIAL 8246).
