## You'll want to know: दानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C306-SHIBIRA306-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दानम्** — *dānam* — "a donation; giving".

> जनाः शिबिराय अन्नस्य दानं प्रयच्छन्ति।
>
> *janāḥ śibirāya annasya dānaṃ prayacchanti.*
>
> People hand over food to the camp as a donation.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दानम्** (*dānam*) comes from the root √dā, "to give", as in *dadāti*; MW gives *dāna* as the act of giving and a gift (MW).
