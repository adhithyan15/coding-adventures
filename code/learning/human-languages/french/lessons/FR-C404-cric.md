## You'll want to know: le cric
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C404-CREVAISON-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le cric** — "the jack (for lifting a car)".

> Le cric est sous le tapis, à l'arrière de la voiture.
>
> *The jack is under the mat at the back of the car.*

The final *c* is pronounced.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *cric* is not settled. It is usually traced to Middle High German *kriec* 'winch'; EtymDB does not list it.
