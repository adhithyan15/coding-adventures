## You'll want to know: आरात्रिकम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C329-PUJA329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आरात्रिकम्** — *ārātrikam* — "the waving of lamps in worship".

> सायं मन्दिरे आरात्रिकं भवति।
>
> *sāyaṃ mandire ārātrikaṃ bhavati.*
>
> In the evening there is the waving of lamps in the temple.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आरात्रिकम्** (*ārātrikam*) is built on *ā* and *rātri*, "night"; MW gives *ārātrika* as the light waved at night before an image, and the ceremony itself (MW).
