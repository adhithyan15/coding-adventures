## You'll want to know: مجلس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C409-HOKUMAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مجلس** (*majles*) — "parliament; a gathering".

> نماینده ما در مجلس سخنرانی کرد.
>
> *namâyande-ye mâ dar majles sokhanrâni kard.*
>
> Our representative gave a speech in parliament.

Iran's parliament is the *majles*. Also a formal gathering.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجلس** was borrowed from Arabic *majlis* "place of sitting, session" (EtymDB): a parliament is where its members sit.
