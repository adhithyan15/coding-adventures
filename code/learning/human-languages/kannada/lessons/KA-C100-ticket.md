## You'll want to know: ಟಿಕೆಟ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C100-FIND-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟಿಕೆಟ್** (*ṭikeṭ*) — "a ticket", borrowed from English. Kannada hears the English
*t* as the curled **ಟ**.

**ಒಂದು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ** (*ondu ṭikeṭ koḍi*) — "one ticket, please".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

An English word, heard with Kannada ears.
