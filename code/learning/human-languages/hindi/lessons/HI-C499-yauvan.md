## You'll want to know: यौवन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C499-KISHOR499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**यौवन** — *yauvan* — "the prime of youth".

> दादी कहती हैं कि यौवन जल्दी बीत जाता है।
>
> *dādī kahtī haiṁ ki yauvan jaldī bīt jātā hai.*
>
> Grandmother says that youth passes quickly.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यौवन** (*yauvan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *yauvana*, "youth, youthfulness", from *yuvan*, "young" (Monier-Williams); everyday speech says *javānī*.
