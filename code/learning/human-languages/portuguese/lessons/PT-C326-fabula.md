## You'll want to know: a fábula
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C326-LENDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a fábula** — "the fable".

> Na fábula, o leão e o rato ficam amigos.
>
> *In the fable, the lion and the mouse become friends.*

A short story with animals and a lesson at the end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fábula* was borrowed from Latin *fābula* "a story, a tale" (EtymDB), from *fārī* "to speak".
