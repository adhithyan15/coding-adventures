Sanskrit, chapter 392: Asking for Help.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अभ्यर्थयते (abhyarthayate) — requests; asks for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for suitable, then the Sanskrit for spoils.

You'll want to know: अभ्यर्थयते.
अभ्यर्थयते — abhyarthayate — "requests; asks for".
यदि भवान् अभ्यर्थयते, तर्हि सः साहाय्यं करिष्यति। yadi bhavān abhyarthayate, tarhi saḥ sāhāyyaṃ kariṣyati. If you ask him, he will help.
Past (he …): सः साहाय्यम् अभ्यर्थितवान्, saḥ sāhāyyam abhyarthitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अभ्यर्थयते (abhyarthayate) is built on abhi, "towards", and the verb arthaya, "to ask, to wish"; MW gives abhy-√arth "to request, to ask for" and abhy-arthita "asked, invited" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for spoils, then the Sanskrit for requests.

You'll want to know: सहायकः.
सहायकः — sahāyakaḥ — "an assistant; a helper".
यदि कार्यं बहु, तर्हि एकं सहायकम् आनयतु। yadi kāryaṃ bahu, tarhi ekaṃ sahāyakam ānayatu. If there is a lot of work, bring an assistant.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सहायकः (sahāyakaḥ) is built on sahāya, "a companion"; MW gives sahāyaka as the same as sahāya, a companion who helps (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
आश्वासयति (āśvāsayati) — reassures; comforts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for requests, then the Sanskrit for an assistant.

You'll want to know: आश्वासयति.
आश्वासयति — āśvāsayati — "reassures; comforts".
यदि बालः रोदिति, तर्हि माता तम् आश्वासयति। yadi bālaḥ roditi, tarhi mātā tam āśvāsayati. If the child cries, its mother comforts it.
Past (she …): सा बालम् आश्वासितवती, sā bālam āśvāsitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
आश्वासयति (āśvāsayati) is built on ā and the root √śvas, "to breathe", in its causative form; MW gives ā-śvāsita "encouraged, comforted, consoled" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सहकारः (sahakāraḥ) — co-operation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an assistant, then the Sanskrit for reassures.

You'll want to know: सहकारः.
सहकारः — sahakāraḥ — "co-operation".
यदि सहकारः अस्ति, तर्हि कठिनं कार्यम् अपि सुकरम्। yadi sahakāraḥ asti, tarhi kaṭhinaṃ kāryam api sukaram. If there is co-operation, even hard work is easy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सहकारः (sahakāraḥ) is built from saha, "with", and kāra, "a doing"; MW gives saha-kāra "acting with, co-operation, assistance" (MW).
