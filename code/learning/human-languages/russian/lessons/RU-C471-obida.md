## You'll want to know: обида
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C471-HURT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**обида** (*obída*) — "hurt feelings, offence".

> Он до сих пор помнит эту обиду.
>
> *On do sikh pór pómnit étu obídu.*
>
> He still remembers that hurt.

Feminine. *Не в обиду* — no offence meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обида* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *obvida* (EtymDB), derived from *obviděti*, which is built on *ob-* and *viděti* 'to see'.
