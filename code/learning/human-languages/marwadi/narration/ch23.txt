Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 23: First Food Words: No New Signs.
9 lessons. All 9 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 9.
Hear dāl — lentils.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Recall the five-word transport payoff, then say horse and market.

You'll want to know.
dāl — lentils.
Two beats, and the second one is long. Hear it twice, picture a bowl of cooked lentils, and say it once. The spelling stays covered until the sound and the meaning are comfortable.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6.
[question — say your answer, then pause 8 seconds]
What food does dāl name?


Lesson 2 of 9.
दाल (dāl) — lentils on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say lentils.
[once you have stopped driving — write: the word for horse, then ज]

You'll want to know.
दा + ल becomes दाल — dāl — lentils.
Three units, all of them met before: द, the long ा, and ल.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, and wait five seconds.
[once you have stopped driving — write: दाल (dāl) — check that the ा sits after द and not after ल]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
From dāl, write lentils.

Lesson 4 of 9.
घी (ghī) — clarified butter on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say clarified butter, recall the five-word transport payoff, then say bus.

You'll want to know.
घ + ी becomes घी — ghī — clarified butter.
The same घ that opens घर and घोड़ो, carrying the long ी met in पाणी.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, and wait five seconds.
[once you have stopped driving — write: घी (ghī) — keep the ी stroke tall enough to reach the headline]

Lesson 6 of 9.
सबजी (sabjī) — vegetables on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say vegetables and vehicle.
[once you have stopped driving — write: the word for home]

You'll want to know.
स + ब + जी becomes सबजी — sabjī — vegetables.
Four units, every one of them already in the hand. The source spells it with a full ब between स and जी; other Rajasthani writing prints a clustered spelling for the same word, so treat this form as the one attested here rather than as the only one anybody uses.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, and wait five seconds.
[once you have stopped driving — write: सबजी (sabjī) — check that ब keeps its own short vowel and carries no vowel-killer]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From sabjī, write vegetables.


Lesson 7 of 9.
Retrieve three food words — lentils, clarified butter, and vegetables.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — write: the word for vehicle]
Then say temple and home.

Guided Practice.
Hear the three words in a mixed order, give each meaning, and read three shuffled cards.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all three from sound]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6.

Lesson 8 of 9.
Payoff — three food words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Say cart.
[once you have stopped driving — write: the word for temple]

Guided Practice.
Identify three heard words.
Produce three words from meaning cues.
Match three printed cards to meanings.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all three heard words without a model]
Pass each skill separately. Three food names are a vocabulary foundation, not an order placed in a kitchen or a shop.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6.
[question — say your answer, then pause 38 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write the three food words.
