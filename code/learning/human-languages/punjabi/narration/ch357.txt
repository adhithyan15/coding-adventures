Punjabi, chapter 357: My School Things.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਰਬੜ (rabaṛ) — an eraser; rubber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a photo album, then the Punjabi for a picture frame.

You'll want to know: ਰਬੜ.
ਰਬੜ — rabaṛ — "an eraser; rubber".
ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਇੱਕ ਨਵਾਂ ਰਬੜ ਹੈ। mere kol ikk navāṁ rabaṛ hai. I have a new eraser.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਰਬੜ (rabaṛ) is borrowed from English rubber.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an eraser, then the Punjabi for a notebook.

You'll want to know: ਸਿਆਹੀ.
ਸਿਆਹੀ — siāhī — "ink".
ਮੇਰੀ ਕਲਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਨੀਲੀ ਸਿਆਹੀ ਹੈ। merī kalam vich nīlī siāhī hai. There is blue ink in my pen.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਿਆਹੀ (siāhī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian siyāhī, "blackness".

Lesson 5 of 6.
ਪੰਨਾ (pannā) — a page; a leaf of a book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for ink, then the Punjabi for a slate.

You'll want to know: ਪੰਨਾ.
ਪੰਨਾ — pannā — "a page; a leaf of a book".
ਮੇਰੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੌ ਪੰਨੇ ਹਨ। merī kitāb vich sau panne han. My book has a hundred pages.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੰਨਾ (pannā) is inherited from Sanskrit parṇa, "plumage, foliage"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pannā, "leaf, page", under it (CDIAL 7918).
