## You'll want to know: ਤੱਕੜੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C273-WEIGH273-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੱਕੜੀ** — *takkṛī* — "a pair of scales".

> ਦੁਕਾਨ ਦੀ ਤੱਕੜੀ ਪੁਰਾਣੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dukān dī takkṛī purāṇī hai.*
>
> The shop's scales are old.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੱਕੜੀ** (*takkṛī*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *tarka*, "inquiry, conjecture"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *takkaṛī*, "pair of scales", there (CDIAL 5714).
