## You'll want to know: نذر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C344-NAZR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نذر** (*nazr*) — "a vow (to God)".

> او نذر کرد که اگر حالش خوب شود به فقیرها کمک کند.
>
> *U nazr kard ke agar hâlash khub shavad be faqir-hâ komak konad.*
>
> She vowed that if she got well she would help the poor.

Written with ذ. *Nazr kardan* "to make a vow"; it is kept by cooking or giving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nazr* is borrowed from Arabic *nadhr*, "a vow" (EtymDB); Persian says the ذ as *z*.
