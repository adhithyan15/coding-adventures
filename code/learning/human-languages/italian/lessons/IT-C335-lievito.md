## You'll want to know: il lievito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C335-IMPASTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il lievito** — "the yeast, the raising agent".

> Aggiungi il lievito e un po' d'acqua.
>
> *Add the yeast and a little water.*

*Il lievito di birra* — fresh yeast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lievito* goes back to Latin *levātum*, from *levāre* "to raise" (EtymDB).
