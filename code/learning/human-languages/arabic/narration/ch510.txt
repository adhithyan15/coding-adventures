Arabic, chapter 510: Tests and treatment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تشخيص (tashkhīṣ) — a diagnosis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a vaccination, then the Arabic for a swelling.

You'll want to know: تشخيص.
تشخيص — tashkhīṣ — "a diagnosis".
كان تشخيص الطبيب صحيحا. kāna tashkhīṣ aṭ-ṭabīb ṣaḥīḥan. The doctor's diagnosis was correct.

The word, taken apart.
تشخيص (tashkhīṣ) is built on the native Arabic root sh-kh-ṣ, "a person, a figure seen from afar" (Lane). Shakhṣ, a person, which you know, is from the same root; Form II is to make a thing stand out clearly.

Lesson 3 of 5.
أشعة (ashiʿʿa) — X-rays; rays.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a diagnosis, then the Arabic for a lab test.

You'll want to know: أشعة.
أشعة — ashiʿʿa — "X-rays; rays".
صور الطبيب ساقه بالأشعة. ṣawwara aṭ-ṭabīb sāqahu bi-l-ashiʿʿa. The doctor X-rayed his leg.
It is the plural of شعاع, a ray.

The word, taken apart.
أشعة (ashiʿʿa) is built on the native Arabic root sh-ʿ-ʿ, "to spread, to scatter (of light)" (Lane). A shuʿāʿ is a ray spreading out from the sun.

Lesson 4 of 5.
عالج (ʿālaja) — to treat (a patient).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a lab test, then the Arabic for X-rays.

You'll want to know: عالج.
عالج — ʿālaja — "to treat (a patient)".
عالجت الطبيبة الجرح بسرعة. ʿālajat aṭ-ṭabība al-jurḥ bi-surʿa. The doctor treated the wound quickly.
Present: يعالج, yuʿālij.
The noun علاج, treatment, goes with it.

The word, taken apart.
عالج (ʿālaja) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-l-j, "to work at, to struggle with" (Lane). Form III ʿālaja is to work at a thing until it gives way.
