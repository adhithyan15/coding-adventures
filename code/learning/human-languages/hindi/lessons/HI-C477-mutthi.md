## You'll want to know: मुट्ठी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C477-HATHELI477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मुट्ठी** — *muṭṭhī* — "a fist; a handful".

> बच्चे ने मुट्ठी में एक सिक्का छिपाया।
>
> *bacce ne muṭṭhī meṁ ek sikkā chipāyā.*
>
> The child hid a coin in his fist.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुट्ठी** (*muṭṭhī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *muṭṭhī*, "fist, handful", under Sanskrit *muṣṭí*, "fist" (CDIAL 10221).
