## You'll want to know: yìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C134-THING134-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yìng* — "hard; stiff".

> *zhège miànbāo tài yìng le* — This bread is too hard.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ngeangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'firm and sturdy'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
