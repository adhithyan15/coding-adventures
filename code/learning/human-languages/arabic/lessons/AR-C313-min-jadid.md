## You'll want to know: من جديد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C313-FAWR313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**من جديد** — *min jadīd* — "anew, all over again".

> بدأ الطلاب الدرس من جديد.
>
> *badaʾa aṭ-ṭullāb ad-dars min jadīd.*
>
> The students started the lesson all over again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**من جديد** (*min jadīd*) is "from new": *min* with the taught *jadīd*, new, which is built on the native Arabic root *j-d-d* (Lane).
