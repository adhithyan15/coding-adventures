## You'll want to know: поворот
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C439-RAZVILKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поворот** (*povorót*) — "a turn, a bend".

> После поворота будет мост.
>
> *Pósle povoróta búdet most.*
>
> After the bend there will be a bridge.

Masculine. *Поворот направо* — a right turn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поворот* is built from *по-* and *ворот* 'a turning', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic *vortŭ*, on the root of *вертеть* 'to turn'.
