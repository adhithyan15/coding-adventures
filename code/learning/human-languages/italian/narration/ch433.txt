Italian, chapter 433: In camera.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
matrimoniale — double (of a bed or room); of marriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to stay, then the Italian for the porter.

You'll want to know: matrimoniale.
matrimoniale — "double (of a bed or room); of marriage".
Vorrei una camera matrimoniale per due notti. I would like a double room for two nights.
Un letto matrimoniale; a room with two beds is una doppia.

The word, taken apart.
matrimoniale is built on matrimonio 'marriage', a learned borrowing from Latin mātrimōnium, built on māter 'mother' (Lewis & Short).

Before the new one: say the Italian for the porter, then the Italian for double.

You'll want to know: la culla.
la culla — "the cot, the cradle".
Avete una culla per il bambino? Do you have a cot for the baby?
Figuratively: la culla della civiltà — the cradle of civilisation.

The word, taken apart.
culla is inherited from Vulgar Latin cunla (EtymDB), a little form of Latin cūnae 'cradle'.

federa was borrowed from Lombardic fedara 'feather' (EtymDB): first the cover of a feather pillow.

la saponetta — "the bar of soap".
In bagno manca la saponetta. There is no soap in the bathroom.

The word, taken apart.
saponetta is a little sapone 'soap', which is inherited from Latin sāpō, sāpōnis (EtymDB), itself an early loan from a Germanic language (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'asciugacapelli — the hair dryer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the pillowcase, then the Italian for the bar of soap.

You'll want to know: l'asciugacapelli.
l'asciugacapelli — "the hair dryer".
L'asciugacapelli è nel cassetto. The hair dryer is in the drawer.
Plural unchanged; in speech also il phon.

The word, taken apart.
asciugacapelli is built from asciugare 'to dry', inherited from Latin exsūcāre 'to suck the juice out', and capelli 'hair', inherited from Latin capillus (EtymDB).
