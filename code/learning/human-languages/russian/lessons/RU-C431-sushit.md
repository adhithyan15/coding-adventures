## You'll want to know: сушить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C431-SKVOZNYAK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сушить** (*sushít*) — "to dry".

> Мы сушим мокрые вещи на балконе.
>
> *My súshim mókryye véshchi na balkóne.*
>
> We dry the wet things on the balcony.

Imperfective; perfective *высушить*. Present *сушу, сушишь*. *Сушить волосы* — to dry one's hair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сушить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *sušiti* (EtymDB), the 'make dry' verb of *сухой* 'dry'.
