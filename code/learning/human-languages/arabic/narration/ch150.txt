Arabic, chapter 150: Delicious, Low, High, Wide, Narrow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لذيذ (ladhīdh) — delicious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for strange, then the Arabic for enjoyable.
