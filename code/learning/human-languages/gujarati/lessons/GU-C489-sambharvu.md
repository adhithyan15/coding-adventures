# સંભારવું (sambhārvũ) — to recall; to remember (someone) fondly

## You'll want to know: સંભારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C489-KUSHAL489-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભારવું** — *sambhārvũ* — "to recall; to remember (someone) fondly".

> દાદા રોજ જૂના મિત્રોને સંભારે છે.
>
> *dādā roj jūnā mitrone sambhāre che.*
>
> Every day my grandfather remembers his old friends.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે જૂના દિવસો સંભાર્યા, *teṇe jūnā divso sambhāryā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભારવું** (*sambhārvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *saṃsmārayati*, "causes to remember"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to recollect" (CDIAL 13059).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
