# Say what you will do

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] This is a **first-person singular** checkpoint. Every answer tells
your own near-term plan.

## Guided Practice — independent retrieval

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02, SA-PATTERN-C264-NEAR-TERM-PLAN-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s each]
- [YOU SAY: You plan to travel tomorrow. Say when and what you will do. — *śvaḥ gamiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: You plan to study tomorrow. Say when and what you will do. — *śvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: You plan to do the work the day after tomorrow. — *paraśvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: Choose a different one of the three actions and make your own tomorrow plan.]

## Grammar Lens: the boundary

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

These three complete forms make a useful first-person singular ramp. This is
**not a complete future paradigm**. Second person, dual, and plural forms, and
the future stems of other roots, wait for later lessons.

## Wrap-up Recall

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02, SA-PATTERN-C264-NEAR-TERM-PLAN-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Without a model, say one true or imagined plan for tomorrow with
**गमिष्यामि**, **करिष्यामि**, or **पठिष्यामि**.
