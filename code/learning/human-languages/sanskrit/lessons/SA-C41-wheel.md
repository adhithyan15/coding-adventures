## You'll want to know: चक्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C41-CRAFT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्रम्** (*cakram*) — "a wheel".

**चक्रम्** is a wheel, a disc, a circle of anything.

It is an old root for turning, **\*kʷel-**, said twice — and the same doubling gives Greek *kuklos*, which English keeps in *cycle* and *cyclone*. English *wheel* is the same word by the Germanic road.

English later borrowed the Sanskrit itself, unworn, as *chakra*, so the word now sits in the language twice over.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A wheel, and a root that turns in three languages at once.
