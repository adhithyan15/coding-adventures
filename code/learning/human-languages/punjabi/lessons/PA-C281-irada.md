## You'll want to know: ਇਰਾਦਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C281-PLAN281-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਰਾਦਾ** — *irādā* — "an intention".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਇਰਾਦਾ ਪੱਕਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā irādā pakkā hai.*
>
> My intention is firm.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਰਾਦਾ** (*irādā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *irāda*, "will, wish".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what comes next.
