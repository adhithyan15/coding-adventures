# છતાં (chatā̃) — despite (after a noun)

## You'll want to know: છતાં
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C547-CHATA547-03]; assesses=[] -->

**છતાં** — *chatā̃* — "despite (after a noun)".

> જો તાવ છતાં તે શાળાએ જશે તો તે વધારે બીમાર થશે.
>
> *jo tāv chatā̃ te śāḷāe jaśe to te vadhāre bīmār thaśe.*
>
> If he goes to school despite the fever, he'll get worse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છતાં** (*chatā̃*) follows its noun: **તાવ છતાં**, despite the fever. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
