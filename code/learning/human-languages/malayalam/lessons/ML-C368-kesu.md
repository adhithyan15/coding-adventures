# കേസ് (kēsŭ) — a court case

## You'll want to know: കേസ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C368-KODATHI368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**കേസ്** — *kēsŭ* — "a court case".

> ആ കേസ് പത്ത് വർഷം നീണ്ടു.
>
> *ā kēsŭ pattŭ varṣaṁ nīṇṭu.*
>
> That case dragged on for ten years.

A case is filed: കേസ് കൊടുക്കുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കേസ്** (*kēsŭ*) was borrowed from English *case*, in its legal sense. Like many English nouns, it ends in a short -ŭ in Malayalam.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
