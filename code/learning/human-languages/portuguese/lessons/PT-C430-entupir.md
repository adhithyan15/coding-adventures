## You'll want to know: entupir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C430-BANHO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**entupir** — "to block, to clog".

> O lavatório entupiu outra vez.
>
> *The washbasin got blocked again.*

*Estar entupido* — to be blocked; a blocked nose too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
