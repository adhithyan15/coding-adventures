## You'll want to know: కరుణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C505-KARUNA505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కరుణ** — *karuṇa* — "compassion; mercy".

> ఆ డాక్టరు పేదల పట్ల కరుణ చూపించారు.
>
> *ā ḍākṭaru pēdala paṭla karuṇa cūpiñcāru.*
>
> That doctor showed compassion to the poor.

కరుణ చూపించు, to show mercy; a formal word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కరుణ** (*karuṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *karuṇā*, "pity" (CDIAL 2811).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
