Arabic, chapter 82: New, Old, Good, Long, Short.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جديد (jadīd) — new.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for big, then the Arabic for small.

You'll want to know: جديد.
جديد — jadīd — "new".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jadīd]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jadīd, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say jadīd]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for big, then the Arabic for small, then say jadīd again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does جديد (jadīd) mean? ("New".) Say it once more.
