**Contract:** [`assessment.json`](../../assessment.json) · **Envelope:**

These sit one rung below [`../a1/rubric.md`](../a1/rubric.md), and differ from
it in kind rather than in degree. The A1 rubric **transcribes** an awarding
body's real rule: DELE A1 v2020, sourced to the Instituto Cervantes guide, with
the instruction that it "does not improve it." There is no DELE below A1, so
there is nothing here to transcribe. Everything below is a **project standard**
that this repository is choosing, and it says so rather than borrowing
authority it does not have.

No standard-setting study has been run. No score from these papers may be
reported as anything but a practice result until the human layer HL16 §7
describes exists.

**No sitting has been run against these papers.** The A1 pair carries
[`sitting-2026-08-26.md`](../a1/sitting-2026-08-26.md), which measured a
book-only reader at **NO APTO on both forms** and is the reason that rung's
readiness claim is `corpus ready` and not `mock ready`. Nothing equivalent
exists here yet, so pre-A1 has no measured answer to the same question — and
the absence is recorded rather than filled with a guess. What *is* measured is
narrower and mechanical: [`book-bounded-audit.json`](book-bounded-audit.json)
proves every objective item on both forms is answerable from headwords the book
teaches by the end of pre-A1, with **zero** unbounded items. That is a claim
about the papers, not about a reader.

## The pass rule, and why it is the strict one

Four papers of **25 points** each. A candidate must reach **15 of 25 (60%) on
every paper in the same sitting**. The 60-point headline total cannot rescue a
failed paper: 25 in reading and 10 in writing is 60 points and is **not** a
pass.

This is deliberately stricter than DELE's own rule, which groups reading with
writing and listening with speaking and passes each *group* at 30/50. A grouped
rule lets a strong reader carry a weak writer, and the whole claim of this rung
is that all four skills moved together.

An unattempted required part scores **zero** and is not averaged away.

## Timing

| paper | time | notes |
|---|---|---|
| reading | 8 min | paper, no audio |
| listening | 7 min | each recording played **twice**, 8-second gap between items |
| writing | 10 min | continuous; the delayed-recall model is shown for 5 seconds and removed |
| speaking | 5 min | live interlocutor, 2 minutes of preparation before the clock starts |

Written papers run **25 minutes continuously**. Lessons in this course are
capped at five minutes; a mock is not a lesson. It is evidence, and it keeps
the target timing because that is what it is evidence *of*.

## Aids

Allowed: the picture cues printed on the paper; one slow repetition from the
interlocutor in the speaking paper; the two permitted replays in listening.

Forbidden throughout: dictionary, glossary, wordlist, phone, any written
English translation of a stimulus, and — in the writing paper — any visible
model during the response.

## Objective parts: reading, listening, dictation

Marked right or wrong against the answer key. No partial credit, with one
exception named below.

**Spelling in the short written responses.** In `listening-catch-the-name` and
in the dictation, the criterion is *recognisable spelling of a taught word*, not
orthographic perfection. A response earns the mark when a Spanish reader would
read it back as the intended word.

- A missing or misplaced **written accent** costs nothing at this rung. The
  course teaches the accent as a visible exception to a regular stress rule, and
  the rule — not the mark — is what pre-A1 is asking for.
- A wrong **letter** that changes the word does cost the mark.
- **ñ written as n** costs the mark. It is a separate letter, the course teaches
  it as one, and *año* and *ano* are different words.

## Rated parts: bounded writing production, and all of speaking

Each rated item is scored on four equally weighted dimensions, **0–5** each,
then scaled to the item's points.

| # | dimension | what earns 5 | what earns 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | task fulfilment | does what the prompt asked, and only that | a response not meaningfully in Spanish, which cannot earn this dimension at all |
| 2 | comprehensibility and interaction | understood first time by a sympathetic listener or reader | not recoverable |
| 3 | range and control | uses taught forms accurately for the job | no taught form deployed |
| 4 | orthographic control (writing) / pronunciation and fluency (speaking) | legible and readable, or intelligible without strain | unreadable, or unintelligible |

**Length is not a dimension.** The envelope caps each response at a few words on
purpose. A longer answer is not a better one and is never rewarded for length;
it is scored on what the four dimensions say, and an answer that overruns the
cap is marked on its first compliant portion.

**English fallback.** A response that switches to English to complete the task
scores 0 on dimension 1 for that item. A single English word inside an otherwise
Spanish response costs dimension 3, not dimension 1.

## Variety

The assessed variety is contemporary standard Spanish; the course's taught forms
define the initial model. **Documented regional forms receive full credit when
used consistently and when they satisfy the task.**

Specifically and non-exhaustively at this rung: *vos* and its verb forms are
correct wherever the candidate uses them consistently; *ustedes* for a plural
*you* is correct everywhere; seseo, yeísmo and aspirated syllable-final *s* are
pronunciation features, not errors, and dimension 4 must not be reduced for
them. A trained Spanish reviewer adjudicates a form the key does not list.

## Rating and adjudication

Speaking is recorded. Both mocks are double-scored by two trained raters, and a
third adjudicates when scaled totals differ by more than **10 points**, or when
the two raters disagree about a documented regional form. Rater notes are kept
with the recording.

## What a result may be called

- Both mocks passed under the independent thresholds → the learner may be
  reported **mock ready** at pre-A1.
- Anything less → **corpus ready** at most.
- **Learner proven** requires the pre-registered book-only human study and can
  never be inferred from a mock result or from corpus coverage.
