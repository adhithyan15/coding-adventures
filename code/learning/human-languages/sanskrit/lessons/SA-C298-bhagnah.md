## You'll want to know: भग्नः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C298-KSHATI298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भग्नः** — *bhagnaḥ* — "broken".

> गृहस्य छदिः भग्नम् अस्ति।
>
> *gṛhasya chadiḥ bhagnam asti.*
>
> The roof of the house is broken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भग्नः** (*bhagnaḥ*) comes from the root √bhañj, "to break, shatter"; MW gives *bhagna* as broken, shattered, split (MW).
