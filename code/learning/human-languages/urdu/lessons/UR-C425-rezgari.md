## You'll want to know: ریزگاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C425-TARAZU425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ریزگاری** — *rezgārī* — "loose change; coins".

> دکاندار کے پاس ریزگاری نہیں تھی۔
>
> *dukāndār ke pās rezgārī nahīṅ thī.*
>
> The shopkeeper had no change.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ریزگاری** (*rezgārī*) is built on Persian *reza*, "a small piece": it is usually traced to a Persian word for small change.
