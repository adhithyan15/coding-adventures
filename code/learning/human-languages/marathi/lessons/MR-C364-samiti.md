## You'll want to know: समिती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C364-SANSTHA364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**समिती** — *samitī* — "a committee".

> शाळेने पाच जणांची समिती नेमली.
>
> *śāḷene pāc jaṇāñcī samitī nemlī.*
>
> The school appointed a committee of five.

Feminine.

Feminine: समिती नेमली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समिती** (*samitī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *samiti*, "meeting, assembly, council" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
