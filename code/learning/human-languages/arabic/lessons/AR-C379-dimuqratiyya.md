## You'll want to know: ديمقراطية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C379-HIZB379-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ديمقراطية** — *dīmuqrāṭiyya* — "democracy".

> يتكلم الكتاب عن تاريخ الديمقراطية.
>
> *yatakallamu al-kitāb ʿan tārīkh ad-dīmuqrāṭiyya.*
>
> The book talks about the history of democracy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ديمقراطية** (*dīmuqrāṭiyya*) is a modern loan, usually traced to Greek *dēmokratia*, "rule of the people", through European languages. Arabic added its own abstract ending *-iyya*.
