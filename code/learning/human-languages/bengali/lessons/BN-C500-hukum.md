## You'll want to know: হুকুম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C500-BIBHAG500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**হুকুম** — *hukum* — "an order; a command".

> ম্যানেজার নতুন হুকুম দিলেন।
>
> *Mænejār nôtun hukum dilen.*
>
> The manager gave a new order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হুকুম** (*hukum*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥukm*, "judgement, a command", through Persian.
