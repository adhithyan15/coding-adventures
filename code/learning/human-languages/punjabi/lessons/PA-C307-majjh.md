## You'll want to know: ਮੱਝ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C307-BUFF307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੱਝ** — *majjh* — "a buffalo".

> ਸਾਡੀ ਮੱਝ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੁੱਧ ਦਿੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍī majjh bahut dudh dindī hai.*
>
> Our buffalo gives a lot of milk.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੱਝ** (*majjh*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*mahyā*, "buffalo"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *majjh* under it (CDIAL 9980).
