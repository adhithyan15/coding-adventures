# ಮೊಳಗು (moḷagu) — to resound; to boom

## You'll want to know: ಮೊಳಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C363-SANJE363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೊಳಗು** — *moḷagu* — "to resound; to boom".

> ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಗಂಟೆ ಮೊಳಗಿತು.
>
> *dēvasthānadalli gaṇṭe moḷagitu.*
>
> The bell rang out at the temple.

Past (it …): ಮೊಳಗಿತು, *moḷagitu*.

Bells, drums, thunder and slogans ಮೊಳಗು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೊಳಗು** (*moḷagu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4989 lists the older Kannada form of this verb, "to sound as musical instruments, to roar, to thunder".
