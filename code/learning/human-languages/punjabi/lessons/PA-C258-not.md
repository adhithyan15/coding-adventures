## You'll want to know: ਨੋਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C258-LOAN258-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੋਟ** — *noṭ* — "a banknote".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਸੌ ਦਾ ਨੋਟ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere kol sau dā noṭ hai.*
>
> I have a note for one hundred.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੋਟ** (*noṭ*) is borrowed from English *note*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
