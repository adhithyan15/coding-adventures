# छूटणो (chhūṭṇo) — to depart (of a bus or a train); to get loose

## You'll want to know: छूटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C218-RAIL218-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छूटणो** — *chhūṭṇo* — "to depart (of a bus or a train); to get loose".

> बस आथण छूटै है।
>
> *bas āthaṇ chhūṭai hai.*
>
> The bus leaves in the evening.

Past (it …): वो बस छूटी, *vo bas chhūṭī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छूटणो** (*chhūṭṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kṣuṭyate*, "is released"; CDIAL lists the Marwari verb, "to get loose", under it (CDIAL 3707).
