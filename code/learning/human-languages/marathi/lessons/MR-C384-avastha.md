## You'll want to know: अवस्था
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C384-PARISTHITI384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अवस्था** — *avasthā* — "a plight; a stage".

> पुरानंतर गावाची अवस्था वाईट झाली.
>
> *purānantar gāvācī avasthā vāīṭ jhālī.*
>
> After the flood the village was in a bad way.

Feminine.

Also a stage of life: बालावस्था, childhood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवस्था** (*avasthā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *avasthā*, "stability, state, condition" (CDIAL 872).
