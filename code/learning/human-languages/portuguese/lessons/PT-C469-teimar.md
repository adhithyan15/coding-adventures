## You'll want to know: teimar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C469-TEIMOSIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**teimar** — "to insist (stubbornly)".

> O miúdo teima em não comer a sopa.
>
> *The kid insists on not eating his soup.*

*Teimar em* + infinitive — to insist on doing something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*teimar* is built on *teima* "obstinacy", usually traced to Latin *thema* "a theme", from Greek (EtymDB); the origin is not fully settled.
