Malayalam, chapter 497: Along the Coast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
തീരദേശം (tīradēśaṁ) — the coastal region.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a dam, then the Malayalam for to dry up.

You'll want to know: തീരദേശം.
തീരദേശം — tīradēśaṁ — "the coastal region".
തീരദേശത്ത് ധാരാളം മീൻപിടുത്തക്കാർ താമസിക്കുന്നു. tīradēśattŭ dhārāḷaṁ mīnpiṭuttakkār tāmasikkunnu. Many fishing people live in the coastal region.
തീരം, a shore, plus ദേശം, a land.

The word, taken apart.
തീരദേശം (tīradēśaṁ) is a Malayalam compound built from തീരം, a shore, from Sanskrit tīra (CDIAL 5842), and ദേശം, a land, from Sanskrit deśa (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
അഴിമുഖം (aḻimukhaṁ) — an estuary; a river mouth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to dry up, then the Malayalam for the coastal region.

You'll want to know: അഴിമുഖം.
അഴിമുഖം — aḻimukhaṁ — "an estuary; a river mouth".
പുഴ കടലിൽ ചേരുന്നത് അഴിമുഖത്താണ്. puḻa kaṭalil cērunnatŭ aḻimukhattāṇŭ. The river meets the sea at the estuary.
അഴി, an opening to the sea, plus മുഖം, a face: the face of the river towards the sea.

The word, taken apart.
അഴിമുഖം (aḻimukhaṁ) is a Malayalam compound built from അഴി, a bar or opening where a river meets the sea, whose origin is not settled, and മുഖം, a face, from Sanskrit mukha (CDIAL 10158).

Lesson 3 of 5.
വേലിയേറ്റം (vēliyēṟṟaṁ) — high tide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for the coastal region, then the Malayalam for an estuary.

You'll want to know: വേലിയേറ്റം.
വേലിയേറ്റം — vēliyēṟṟaṁ — "high tide".
വേലിയേറ്റത്തിൽ കടൽവെള്ളം കായലിലേക്ക് കയറി. vēliyēṟṟattil kaṭalveḷḷaṁ kāyalilēkkŭ kayaṟi. At high tide sea water came into the backwaters.
വേലി, the tide, plus ഏറ്റം, a rise: the sea comes far up the shore.

The word, taken apart.
വേലിയേറ്റം (vēliyēṟṟaṁ) is a Malayalam compound built from വേലി, the tide, usually traced to Sanskrit velā, "the boundary of sea and land" (Monier-Williams), and the native ഏറ്റം, a rise.

വേലിയിറക്കത്തിൽ കുട്ടികൾ മണലിൽ ഞണ്ടുകളെ പിടിച്ചു. vēliyiṟakkattil kuṭṭikaḷ maṇalil ñaṇṭukaḷe piṭiccu. At low tide the children caught crabs on the sand.

Lesson 5 of 5.
കടലാക്രമണം (kaṭalākramaṇaṁ) — a sea surge; coastal erosion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for high tide, then the Malayalam for low tide.

You'll want to know: കടലാക്രമണം.
കടലാക്രമണം — kaṭalākramaṇaṁ — "a sea surge; coastal erosion".
കടലാക്രമണത്തിൽ തീരത്തെ വീടുകൾ തകർന്നു. kaṭalākramaṇattil tīratte vīṭukaḷ takarnnu. In the sea surge the houses on the shore were destroyed.
Literally an attack by the sea: കടൽ plus ആക്രമണം, an attack. A word of the monsoon news.

The word, taken apart.
കടലാക്രമണം (kaṭalākramaṇaṁ) is a Malayalam compound built from the native കടൽ, the sea (DEDR 1118), and ആക്രമണം, an attack, from Sanskrit ākramaṇa, "stepping upon, attacking" (Monier-Williams).
