## You'll want to know: desorientado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C435-ORIENTAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**desorientado** — "lost, disoriented, confused".

> Saí do metro completamente desorientado.
>
> *I came out of the metro completely disoriented.*

Also confused about what to do: *Estou desorientado com tantas opções*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desorientado* is built from *des-* 'un-' and *orientado* 'oriented' (EtymDB), on *orientar*, from Latin *oriēns* 'rising (sun)'.
