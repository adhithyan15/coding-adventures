## You'll want to know: juzgar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C522-JUEZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**juzgar** — "to judge, to try".

> Lo juzgaron por robo.
>
> *They tried him for robbery.*

It takes *gu* before *e*: *juzgué*. Also everyday: *No me juzgues.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juzgar* is inherited from Latin *iūdicāre* 'to judge', through old Spanish *judgar* (EtymDB). It is built on *iūdex* 'a judge'.
