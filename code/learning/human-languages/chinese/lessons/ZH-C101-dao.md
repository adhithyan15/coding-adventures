## You'll want to know: dāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C101-KITCHEN101-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dāo* — "knife".

> *dāo zài zhuōzi shàngbian* — The knife is on the table.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dāo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *taw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary glosses as a weapon, a blade for cutting things down. In the kitchen it is the knife.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
