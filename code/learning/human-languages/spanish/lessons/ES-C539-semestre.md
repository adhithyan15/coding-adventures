## You'll want to know: el semestre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C539-ANYO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el semestre** — "the semester, the half-year".

> El primer semestre del año fue muy difícil.
>
> *The first half of the year was very hard.*

At university it is a term of six months.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semestre* comes from Latin *sēmestris* 'of six months' (EtymDB), built on *sex* 'six' and *mēnsis* 'month'.
