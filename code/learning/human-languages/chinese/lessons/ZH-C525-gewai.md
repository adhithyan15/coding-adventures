## You'll want to know: 格外
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C525-SERVICE525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**格外** — *géwài* — "more than usual; unusually".

> **今天的菜格外好吃** *jīntiān de cài géwài hǎochī* — The food is especially good today.

The new character, in pieces: 格 — ten strokes in three pieces: on the left the tree piece, four strokes, as in 机; on the right the walking foot, three strokes, as in the middle of 客; and 口 under it, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**格外** (*géwài*) is built from 格 and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaek* and *ngwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'a pattern' and 'a measure', and lists another reading, *kak*, for a branch; outside the measure is beyond the usual, though how the pair came to mean 'especially' is not settled.
