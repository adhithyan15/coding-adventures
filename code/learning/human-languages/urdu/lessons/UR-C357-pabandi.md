## You'll want to know: پابندی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C357-BADUNVANI357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پابندی** — *pābandī* — "a ban; a restriction".

> اس سڑک پر ٹرکوں پر پابندی ہے۔
>
> *is saṛak par ṭrakoṅ par pābandī hai.*
>
> Lorries are banned on this road.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پابندی** (*pābandī*) is usually traced to Persian *pābandī*, "a fettering": *pā*, "a foot", and *band*, "a tie".
