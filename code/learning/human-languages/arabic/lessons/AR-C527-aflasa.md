## You'll want to know: أفلس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C527-MAL527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أفلس** — *aflasa* — "to go bankrupt".

> أفلست الشركة بعد سنتين.
>
> *aflasat ash-sharika baʿda sanatayn.*
>
> The company went bankrupt after two years.

Present: يفلس, *yuflis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أفلس** (*aflasa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-l-s* and is usually traced to *fals* (plural *fulūs*), a small copper coin: to be left with coppers and no silver.
