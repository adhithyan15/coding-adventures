## You'll want to know: અગાસી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C153-ROOMS153-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અગાસી** — *agāsī* — "terrace, flat roof".

> **ઉનાળામાં અમે અગાસીમાં સૂઈએ છીએ.** — *unāḷāmā̃ ame agāsīmā̃ sūīe chīe* — "In summer we sleep on the terrace."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **અગાસી** (*agāsī*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
