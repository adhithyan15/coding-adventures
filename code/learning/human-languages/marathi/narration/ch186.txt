Lesson 1 of 5.
पापड (pāpaḍ) — a papad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a defeat, then the Marathi for a competition.

You'll want to know: पापड.
पापड — pāpaḍ — "a papad".

The word, taken apart.
पापड (pāpaḍ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from parpaṭa, "thin cake of rice or pease-meal" (CDIAL 7934).

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL has no entry for चटणी (caṭṇī). It is often tied to the verb "to lick", Hindi cāṭnā, which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed caṭṭ-, "lick, taste" (CDIAL 4573), but no source settles it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मोदक (modak) — a modak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a salad, then the Marathi for a semolina pudding.

You'll want to know: मोदक.
मोदक — modak — "a modak".

The word, taken apart.
मोदक (modak) is Sanskrit modaka, "small round sweetmeat", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10354). It is built on mud, "to rejoice".
