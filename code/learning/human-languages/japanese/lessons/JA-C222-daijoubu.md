## You'll want to know: だいじょうぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C222-CARE222-04]; assesses=[] -->

**だいじょうぶ** — *daijōbu* — "all right; safe".

> **あしは だいじょうぶですか。** — *ashi wa daijōbu desu ka* — "Is your leg all right?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だいじょうぶ** (*daijōbu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dai*, "big" (Middle Chinese *dajH*), *jō*, "ten feet, a full height" (*drjangX*) and *bu*, "man" (*pju*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
