## You'll want to know: сморкаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C486-FEVER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**сморкаться** (*smorkátsya*) — "to blow one's nose".

> Он громко сморкается в платок.
>
> *On grómko smorkáyetsya v platók.*
>
> He blows his nose loudly into a handkerchief.

Imperfective; perfective высморкаться.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сморкаться* is the reflexive of *сморкать* 'to blow one's nose' (EtymDB), the same root as *насморк* 'a runny nose'.
