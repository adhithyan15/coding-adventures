## You'll want to know: कारागारम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C293-NYAYALAYA293-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कारागारम्** — *kārāgāram* — "a prison".

> चौरः कारागारे वसति।
>
> *cauraḥ kārāgāre vasati.*
>
> The thief lives in prison.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कारागारम्** (*kārāgāram*) is built from *kārā*, "confinement", and *āgāra*, "a house"; MW gives *kārāgāra* as a gaol (MW).
