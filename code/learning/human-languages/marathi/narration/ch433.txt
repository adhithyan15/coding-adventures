Marathi, chapter 433: Rust and Dents.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ओरखडा (orakhḍā) — a scrape; a scratch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get messed up, then the Marathi for to ooze.

You'll want to know: ओरखडा.
ओरखडा — orakhḍā — "a scrape; a scratch".
गाडीच्या दारावर मोठा ओरखडा आहे. gāḍīcyā dārāvar moṭhā orakhḍā āhe. There is a big scratch on the car door.
Masculine.
ओरखडा (orakhḍā) पडणे, a scratch appears.

The word, taken apart.
ओरखडा (orakhḍā) is a Marathi word for a long, thin scratch; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तडा (taḍā) — a split; a crack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to ooze, then the Marathi for a scrape.

You'll want to know: तडा.
तडा — taḍā — "a split; a crack".
भूकंपानंतर भिंतीला तडा गेला. bhūkampānantar bhintīlā taḍā gelā. After the earthquake the wall cracked.
Masculine.
तडा (taḍā) जाणे, to crack: भिंतीला तडा (taḍā) गेला.

The word, taken apart.
तडा (taḍā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi taḍā, "a fissure", beside taḍṇẽ, "to crack, split", under traṭ, "crackle" (CDIAL 5988).

The word, taken apart.
चेपणे (cepṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi cepṇẽ, "to press, squeeze", under capp, "press" (CDIAL 4674).

Lesson 5 of 5.
बंद पडणे (band paḍṇe) — to stop working; to break down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rust, then the Marathi for to get dented.

You'll want to know: बंद पडणे.
बंद पडणे — band paḍṇe — "to stop working; to break down".
घाटात आमची बस बंद पडली. ghāṭāt āmcī bas band paḍlī. Our bus broke down on the ghat road.
Past (he …): तो बंद पडला, to band paḍlā.
Machines, clocks and lifts बंद पडतात; बंद करणे is to switch off.

The word, taken apart.
बंद पडणे (band paḍṇe) joins बंद, "closed", a loan usually traced to Persian band, to पडणे, "to fall": to fall still.
