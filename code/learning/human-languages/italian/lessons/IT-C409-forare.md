## You'll want to know: forare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C409-GOMMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**forare** — "to get a puncture; to pierce".

> Abbiamo forato in autostrada.
>
> *We got a puncture on the motorway.*

*Ho forato* — I have a flat tyre; *forare un foglio* — to punch a hole in a sheet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*forare* is inherited from Latin *forāre* 'to bore, to pierce' (EtymDB).
