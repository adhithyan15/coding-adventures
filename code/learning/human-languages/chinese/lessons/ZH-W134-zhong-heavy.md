## Script — a fall and a bar over 里
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZHONG-HEAVY-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 重

**Two pieces, top to bottom.**

**A short left-falling stroke**, from the right down to the left, then **a long horizontal** across the whole width, as 千 begins.

**里, seven strokes**, in the order your hand knows: the box (its left side, the top that turns down the right side, the middle bar, the bar that closes it), then the vertical, which here starts above the long bar and runs down through it and the box, then the two bars of the foot, the lower one longer.

2 + 7 = nine.
