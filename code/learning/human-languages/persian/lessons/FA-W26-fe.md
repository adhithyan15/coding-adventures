# ف (fe) — the letter that opens فکر

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-TA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-ZA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ط and ظ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **فکر کردن** (*fekr kardan*), "to think".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ف** — *fe*, the sound *f*.

It opens **فکر** (*fekr*), "thought", in **فکر کردن**. A small closed **head** on a
broad bowl, with **one dot above**.

## Writing: ف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop clockwise around the small closed head
- **2.** continue left through the broad bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then place the upper dot last — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ف demonstration at 02:09–02:13 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-TA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-ZA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **فکر کردن** and point at ف inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ف three times, saying *f* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ظ, then ف, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **فکر کردن** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-TA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C25-ZA-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ف? (**fe**, *f*.) Which word did it
come from? (**فکر کردن**, *fekr kardan*, "to think".)
