## You'll want to know: el localizador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C662-RESERVA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el localizador** — "the booking reference".

> Dígame el localizador de su billete, por favor.
>
> *Tell me the booking reference for your ticket, please.*

A short code of letters and numbers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*localizador* is built on *localizar* "to locate", from *local*, a learned borrowing from Late Latin *locālis* (EtymDB), from *locus* "place".
