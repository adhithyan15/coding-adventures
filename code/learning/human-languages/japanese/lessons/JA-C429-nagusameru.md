## You'll want to know: なぐさめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C429-DOWN429-05]; assesses=[] -->

**なぐさめる** — *nagusameru* — "to comfort; to console".

> **ともだちが ないたので、なぐさめました。** — *tomodachi ga naita node, nagusamemashita* — "My friend cried, so I comforted him."

Polite **なぐさめます**, *nagusamemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが がくせいを なぐさめました。**, *sensei ga gakusei o nagusamemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なぐさめる** (*nagusameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
