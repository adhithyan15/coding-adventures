## You'll want to know: agacer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C469-HUMEUR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**agacer** — "to irritate, to get on someone's nerves".

> Le bruit de la rue agace mon voisin.
>
> *The noise from the street irritates my neighbour.*

Spelling: *nous agaçons*, with *ç* before *o*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*agacer* goes back to Old French *agacier*. The origin is not settled: a link with *agace*, an old word for the magpie, is often suggested.
