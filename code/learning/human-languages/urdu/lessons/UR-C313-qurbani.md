## You'll want to know: قربانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C313-TEHRIK313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قربانی** — *qurbānī* — "a sacrifice".

> لوگوں نے بڑی قربانیاں دیں۔
>
> *logoṅ ne baṛī qurbāniyāṅ dīṅ.*
>
> People made great sacrifices.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قربانی** (*qurbānī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qurbān*, "an offering", with the ending *-ī*. It is also the animal sacrifice of Eid.
