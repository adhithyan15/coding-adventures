## You'll want to know: imbarcarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C421-PORTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**imbarcarsi** — "to board (a ship or a plane)".

> Ci imbarchiamo alle nove per l'isola.
>
> *We board at nine for the island.*

Without *si*, *imbarcare* is to take on board: *imbarcare i passeggeri*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imbarcarsi* is built from *in-* and *barca* 'boat', which is inherited from Late Latin *barca* (Lewis & Short).
