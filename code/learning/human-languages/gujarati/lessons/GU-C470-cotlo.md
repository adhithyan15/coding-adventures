## You'll want to know: ચોટલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C470-CAHERO470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોટલો** — *coṭlo* — "a plait of hair".

> દાદી રોજ સવારે મીરાનો ચોટલો ગૂંથે છે.
>
> *dādī roj savāre Mīrāno coṭlo gū̃the che.*
>
> Every morning my grandmother plaits Mira's hair.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોટલો** (*coṭlo*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "hair of head", under Sanskrit *cūḍā*, "topknot on the head" (CDIAL 4883).
