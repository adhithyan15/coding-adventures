## You'll want to know: 本身
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C538-CAUSE538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**本身** — *běnshēn* — "itself (in itself)".

> **这个问题本身不大** *zhège wèntí běnshēn bú dà* — The problem itself isn't a big one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**本身** (*běnshēn*) is built from 本 'the root' and 身 'the body', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pwonX* and *syin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says, after an older dictionary, that the base of a tree is *pwonX*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
