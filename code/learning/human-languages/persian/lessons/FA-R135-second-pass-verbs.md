- [YOU SAY: to break, to sew, to pull; to draw, to pour, to burn]
- [YOU SAY: to plant, to pick, to turn, to arrive, to weep]

(**کاشتن**, *kâshtan*.) To pick? (**چیدن**, *chidan*.) To turn? (**پیچیدن**, *pichidan*.) To arrive? (**رسیدن**, *residan*.) To weep? (**گریستن**, *geristan*.) To hit? (**زدن**, *zadan*.) To be afraid? (**ترسیدن**, *tarsidan*.) To taste? (**چشیدن**, *chashidan*.) To smell? (**بوییدن**, *buyidan*.) To dig? (**کندن**, *kandan*.) To drive? (**راندن**, *rândan*.) To stay? (**ماندن**, *mândan*.) To live? (**زیستن**, *zistan*.) To stick? (**چسباندن**, *chaspândan*.) To spin? (**چرخیدن**, *charkhidan*.)
