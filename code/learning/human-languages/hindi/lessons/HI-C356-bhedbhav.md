## You'll want to know: भेदभाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C356-BARABAR356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भेदभाव** — *bhedbhāv* — "discrimination".

> स्कूल में लड़कियों के साथ भेदभाव नहीं होना चाहिए।
>
> *skūl meṁ laṛkiyoṁ ke sāth bhedbhāv nahīṃ honā cāhiye.*
>
> There should be no discrimination against girls at school.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भेदभाव** (*bhedbhāv*) joins two Sanskrit words, *bheda*, "a split, a distinction", and *bhāva*, "a state, an attitude" (Monier-Williams): treating people as split apart.
