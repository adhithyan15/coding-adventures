## You'll want to know: ingenuo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C473-SINCERITA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ingenuo** — "naive".

> Ero così ingenuo che credevo a tutto.
>
> *I was so naive that I believed everything.*

Do not mix it up with *ingegnoso* "clever, ingenious": *ingenuo* means naive, like English *ingenuous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingenuo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ingenuus* "native, freeborn", and so "frank, open" (Lewis & Short).
