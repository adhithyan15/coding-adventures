## You'll want to know: ज़ोर से
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C110-AGAIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़ोर से** (*zor se*) — "loudly", literally "with force".

**थोड़ा ज़ोर से बोलिए** — "please speak a little louder", with the softening **थोड़ा**.
