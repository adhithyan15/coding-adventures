Italian, chapter 376: Correre il rischio.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il rischio — the risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for final, then the Italian for to fix.

You'll want to know: il rischio.
il rischio — "the risk".
Se non prenoti, corri il rischio di non trovare posto. If you do not book, you run the risk of not finding a seat.
Correre il rischio — to run the risk. A rischio — at risk.

The word, taken apart.
rischio is usually traced to Medieval Latin risicum, resicum, a word of sea trade. The deeper origin is not settled: EtymDB gives no source for rischio (EtymDB), and Greek and Arabic sources have both been proposed.

Lesson 3 of 5.
probabile — likely, probable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the risk, then the Italian for risky.

You'll want to know: probabile.
probabile — "likely, probable".
È probabile che arrivi più tardi. It is likely that he will arrive later.
È probabile che takes the subjunctive. Probabilmente — probably.

The word, taken apart.
probabile is a learned borrowing from Latin probābilis "that may be proved, credible" (Lewis & Short), from probāre "to test, to approve".

The word, taken apart.
improbabile is a learned borrowing from Latin improbābilis "not deserving approval" (Lewis & Short), from in- "not" and probābilis; the sense "unlikely" follows probabile.
