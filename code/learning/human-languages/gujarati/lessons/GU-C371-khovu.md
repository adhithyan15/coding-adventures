# ખોવું (khovũ) — to lose for good

## You'll want to know: ખોવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C371-FAYDO371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોવું** — *khovũ* — "to lose for good".

> જો તમે આ તક ખોશો તો ફરી તક નહીં આવે.
>
> *jo tame ā tak khośo to pharī tak nahī̃ āve.*
>
> If you lose this chance, it will not come again.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાનું પાકીટ ખોયું, *teṇe potānũ pākīṭ khoyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોવું** (*khovũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kṣapayati*, "destroys"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to lose, to waste" (CDIAL 3651). **ખોવાવું** (*khovāvũ*), "to get lost", is its partner.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
