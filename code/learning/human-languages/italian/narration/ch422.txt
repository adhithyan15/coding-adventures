Italian, chapter 422: In barca.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il battello — the boat (on a lake or river), the small steamer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to disembark, then the Italian for to set sail.

You'll want to know: il battello.
il battello — "the boat (on a lake or river), the small steamer".
Sul lago prendiamo il battello. On the lake we take the boat.
For lakes and rivers; on the sea you take il traghetto.

The word, taken apart.
battello was borrowed from Old French batel, a little form of a word taken from Old English bāt 'boat' (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Con il gommone andiamo alle grotte. With the dinghy we go to the caves.
A big thing made of gomma, rubber.

gommone is built on gomma 'rubber', which is inherited from Late Latin gumma, from Latin gummi 'gum' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la canoa.
la canoa — "the canoe".
D'estate facciamo canoa sul fiume. In summer we go canoeing on the river.
Andare in canoa — to go canoeing.

The word, taken apart.
canoa was borrowed from Spanish canoa, which took it from Taíno kanoa 'dugout canoe' (EtymDB), a language of the Caribbean.
