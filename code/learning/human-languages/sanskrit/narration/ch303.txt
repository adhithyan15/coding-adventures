Sanskrit, chapter 303: Famine and Sickness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दुर्भिक्षम् (durbhikṣam) — a famine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for goes under, then the Sanskrit for by degrees.

You'll want to know: दुर्भिक्षम्.
दुर्भिक्षम् — durbhikṣam — "a famine".
तस्मिन् संवत्सरे दुर्भिक्षम् अभवत्। tasmin saṃvatsare durbhikṣam abhavat. That year there was a famine.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
दुर्भिक्षम् (durbhikṣam) is built from dur, "bad, hard", and bhikṣā, "alms, food begged"; MW gives durbhikṣa as scarcity of provisions, famine (MW).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: व्याप्नोति.
व्याप्नोति — vyāpnoti — "spreads through; fills".
रोगः सर्वं नगरं व्याप्नोति। rogaḥ sarvaṃ nagaraṃ vyāpnoti. The disease spreads through the whole city.
Past (it …): रोगः नगरं व्याप्तवान्, rogaḥ nagaraṃ vyāptavān.

The word, taken apart.
व्याप्नोति (vyāpnoti) is built from vi and the root √āp, "to reach", which also gives prāpnoti; MW gives vy-āp as to reach through, pervade, fill (MW).
