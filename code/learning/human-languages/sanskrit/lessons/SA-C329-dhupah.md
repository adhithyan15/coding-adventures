## You'll want to know: धूपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C329-PUJA329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धूपः** — *dhūpaḥ* — "incense".

> मन्दिरं धूपेन पूर्णम् अस्ति।
>
> *mandiraṃ dhūpena pūrṇam asti.*
>
> The temple is full of incense.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूपः** (*dhūpaḥ*) comes from the root √dhū, as MW explains it, comparing *puṣpa* from √puṣ; MW gives *dhūpa* as incense, aromatic smoke (MW).
