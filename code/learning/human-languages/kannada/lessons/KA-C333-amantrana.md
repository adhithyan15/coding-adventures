## You'll want to know: ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C333-NERAVERU333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ** — *āmantraṇa* — "an invitation card; an invitation".

> ಮದುವೆಯ ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ ಅಂಚೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂತು.
>
> *maduveya āmantraṇa añceyalli bantu.*
>
> The wedding invitation came by post.

Plural: ಆಮಂತ್ರಣಗಳು, *āmantraṇagaḷu*.

In full the printed card is ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ; ಆಹ್ವಾನ is the general word for an invitation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ** (*āmantraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āmantraṇa*, "addressing, calling to, summoning" (Monier-Williams).
