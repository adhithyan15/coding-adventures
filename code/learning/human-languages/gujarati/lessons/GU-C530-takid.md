## You'll want to know: તાકીદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C530-TAKID530-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તાકીદ** — *tākīd* — "urgency".

> જો કામમાં તાકીદ હશે તો હું રવિવારે પણ આવીશ.
>
> *jo kāmmā̃ tākīd haśe to hũ ravivāre paṇ āvīś.*
>
> If the work is urgent, I'll come in on Sunday too.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તાકીદ** (*tākīd*) is usually traced to Arabic *taʾkīd*, "emphasis, insistence", through Persian; in Gujarati it is the pressing need to get something done.
