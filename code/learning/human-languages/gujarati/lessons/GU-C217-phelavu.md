# ફેલાવું (phelāvũ) — to spread

## You'll want to know: ફેલાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C217-WIND217-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેલાવું** — *phelāvũ* — "to spread".

> **પવનથી ધૂળ ગામમાં ફેલાશે.** — *pavanthī dhūḷ gāmmā̃ phelāśe* — "With the wind, dust will spread through the village."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેલાવું** (*phelāvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists Gujarati *phelvũ* and *phelāvvũ*, beside Hindi *phailnā*, "to spread", under Sanskrit *prathita*, "spread out", extended with *-ll-* (CDIAL 8651).
