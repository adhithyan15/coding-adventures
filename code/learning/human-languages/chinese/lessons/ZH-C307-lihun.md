## You'll want to know: líhūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C307-SPLIT307-02]; assesses=[] -->

*líhūn* — "to divorce".

> *tāmen jiéhūn shí nián yǐhòu líhūn le* — They divorced after ten years of marriage.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*líhūn* is built from 离 'to leave' and *hūn* 'marriage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lje* and *xwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'marriage'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
