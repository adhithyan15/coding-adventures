Russian, chapter 297: Injuries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
травма (trávma) — an injury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to evacuate, then the Russian for an evacuation.

You'll want to know: травма.
травма (trávma) — "an injury".
Футболист получил травму. Futbolíst poluchíl trávmu. The footballer got injured.
Feminine. Травмпункт — an emergency clinic for injuries.

The word, taken apart.
травма (trávma) goes back to Greek traûma "a wound"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.

ушиб (ushíb) is built on ушибить "to knock, to bruise", from the old verb шибать "to strike"; EtymDB lists both with no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
сознание (soznániye) — consciousness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an injury, then the Russian for a bruise.

You'll want to know: сознание.
сознание (soznániye) — "consciousness".
Он потерял сознание на минуту. On poteryál soznániye na minútu. He lost consciousness for a minute.
Потерять сознание (soznániye) — to faint; прийти в сознание (soznániye) — to come round.

The word, taken apart.
сознание (soznániye) is built on сознать "to realize", from со- and знать "to know", inherited from Proto-Slavic znati, PIE ǵneh₃- (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bruise, then the Russian for consciousness.

You'll want to know: пострадать.
пострадать (postradát) — "to be hurt, to suffer".
В аварии никто не пострадал. V avárii niktó ne postradál. Nobody was hurt in the accident.
Perfective. It takes от + genitive: пострадать (postradát) от пожара.

The word, taken apart.
пострадать (postradát) is built on по- and страдать "to suffer", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists страдать "to suffer" with no parent.
