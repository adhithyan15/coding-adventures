## You'll want to know: ਗਾੜ੍ਹਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C296-SLIP296-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾੜ੍ਹਾ** — *gāṛhā* — "thick (of a liquid)".

> ਇਹ ਦੁੱਧ ਬਹੁਤ ਗਾੜ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh dudh bahut gāṛhā hai.*
>
> This milk is very thick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾੜ੍ਹਾ** (*gāṛhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gāḍha*, "pressed together, thick"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gāṛhā*, "thick, close", under it (CDIAL 4118).
