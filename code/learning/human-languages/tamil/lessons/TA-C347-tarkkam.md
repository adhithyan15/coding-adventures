## You'll want to know: தர்க்கம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C347-ATARAM347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**தர்க்கம்** — *tarkkam* — "logic; reasoning".

> அவன் சொன்னதில் தர்க்கம் இல்லை.
>
> *avaṉ coṉṉatil tarkkam illai.*
>
> There is no logic in what he said.

தர்க்கமாக, logically: an -அம் noun + -ஆக makes the adverb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தர்க்கம்** (*tarkkam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tarka*, "inquiry; conjecture" (CDIAL 5714), the name Indian thought gave to the study of reasoning.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
