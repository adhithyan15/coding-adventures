## You'll want to know: la canción
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C753-MUSICA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la canción** — "the song".

> Esta canción me recuerda a ti.
>
> *This song reminds me of you.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*canción* is inherited from Latin *cantiō*, *cantiōnem* "song, singing" (EtymDB), built on *canere* "to sing".
