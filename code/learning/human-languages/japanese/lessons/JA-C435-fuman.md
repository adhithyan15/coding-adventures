## You'll want to know: ふまん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C435-CONTENT435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふまん** — *fuman* — "discontented; dissatisfied".

> **あには しごとに ふまんが あります。** — *ani wa shigoto ni fuman ga arimasu* — "My elder brother is unhappy with his job."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふまん** (*fuman*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fu*, "negative" (Middle Chinese *pjuw*) and *man*, "full" (*manX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
