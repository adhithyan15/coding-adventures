## You'll want to know: investigar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C294-INVESTIGAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**investigar** — "to investigate".

> A polícia está a investigar o homicídio.
>
> *The police are investigating the killing.*

*Estar a investigar* in Portugal; in Brazil, *estar investigando*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investigar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *investīgāre* "to track down, to search out" (EtymDB), built on *vestīgium* "a footprint".
