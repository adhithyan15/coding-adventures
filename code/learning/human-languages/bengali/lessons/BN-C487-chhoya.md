## You'll want to know: ছোঁয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C487-SOMPORKO487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ছোঁয়া** — *chhõyā* — "to touch".

> বাচ্চাটা গরম কেটলিটা ছুঁল।
>
> *bāchchāṭā gôrôm keṭliṭā chhũlô.*
>
> The child touched the hot kettle.

Past (he/she …): তিনি শিশুর মাথা ছুঁলেন, *tini shishur māthā chhũlen*.

As a noun, a touch: মায়ের ছোঁয়া, a mother's touch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছোঁয়া** (*chhõyā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under Sanskrit *chupati*, "touches" (CDIAL 5055); ছোঁয়াচে, contagious, is built on it.
