Kannada, chapter 276: To scold, To tease, To quarrel, To console, To be proud.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೈ (bai) — to scold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to apply, then the Kannada for to compare.

You'll want to know: ಬೈ.
ಬೈ — bai — "to scold".

The word, taken apart.
ಬೈ (bai) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vai (DEDR 5550). DEDR lists bay(i), bayyu, "to abuse, revile".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ರೇಗಿಸು (rēgisu) — to tease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to compare, then the Kannada for to scold.

You'll want to know: ರೇಗಿಸು.
ರೇಗಿಸು — rēgisu — "to tease".

The word, taken apart.
ರೇಗಿಸು (rēgisu) is native Dravidian, from rēgu, "to rise, be roused, be angry", which DEDR 916 lists under ēṟu, "to rise": to tease is "to make someone rise"; the entry also has Tamil ēṟu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಜಗಳವಾಡು (jagaḷavāḍu) — to quarrel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to scold, then the Kannada for to tease.

You'll want to know: ಜಗಳವಾಡು.
ಜಗಳವಾಡು — jagaḷavāḍu — "to quarrel".

The word, taken apart.
ಜಗಳವಾಡು (jagaḷavāḍu) is jagaḷa, "quarrel", with native āḍu, "to play, act" (DEDR 347). Jagaḷa probably belongs with Prakrit jhagaḍa, "quarrel", from a reconstructed jhagaḍ, "quarrel" (CDIAL 5321), though CDIAL does not list the Kannada word.

ಸಮಾಧಾನ ಮಾಡು (samādhāna māḍu) — to console, to calm (someone) down.

ಸಮಾಧಾನ ಮಾಡು (samādhāna māḍu) is Sanskrit samādhāna, "putting together, reconciliation" (CDIAL 13210), with native māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹೆಮ್ಮೆಪಡು (hemmepaḍu) — to be proud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to quarrel, then the Kannada for to console.

You'll want to know: ಹೆಮ್ಮೆಪಡು.
ಹೆಮ್ಮೆಪಡು — hemmepaḍu — "to be proud".

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆಮ್ಮೆಪಡು (hemmepaḍu) is native Dravidian: hemme, "pride", which DEDR 4411 lists as older perme, "greatness, honour, pride", from the root for "great", and paḍu, "to undergo, feel" (DEDR 3853).
