## You'll want to know: বিকল্প
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C363-SOMOSSHA363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বিকল্প** — *bikôlpô* — "an alternative; an option".

> সাইকেল গাড়ির একটা ভালো বিকল্প।
>
> *sāikel gāṛir ekṭā bhālo bikôlpô.*
>
> A bicycle is a good alternative to a car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিকল্প** (*bikôlpô*) is Sanskrit *vikalpa*, an alternative or option (Monier-Williams). What it replaces takes -এর.
