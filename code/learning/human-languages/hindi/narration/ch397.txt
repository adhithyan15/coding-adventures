Hindi, chapter 397: Choosing What to Study.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
विषय (viṣay) — a subject, a topic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a good deed, then the Hindi for to learn by rote.

You'll want to know: विषय.
विषय — viṣay — "a subject, a topic".
अगर तुम्हें विज्ञान पसंद है तो वही विषय चुनो। agar tumhẽ vijñān pasand hai to vahī viṣay cuno. If you like science, choose that subject.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विषय (viṣay) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit viṣaya, "a sphere, a scope" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 7.
छात्रवृत्ति (chātravṛtti) — a scholarship (a grant for study).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a subject, then the Hindi for a magazine.

You'll want to know: छात्रवृत्ति.
छात्रवृत्ति — chātravṛtti — "a scholarship (a grant for study)".
अगर मुझे छात्रवृत्ति मिलेगी तो मैं शहर में पढ़ूँगा। agar mujhe chātravṛtti milegī to maiṁ śahar meṁ paṛhūṅgā. If I get a scholarship, I'll study in the city.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
छात्रवृत्ति (chātravṛtti) joins two Sanskrit words, chāttra, "a pupil" (Hindi छात्र), and vṛtti, "conduct; a livelihood" (Monier-Williams): a pupil's keep. The compound is a modern one.

[pause 2 seconds]

फ़र्क़ — farq — "a difference".
अगर तुम रोज़ एक घंटा पढ़ोगे तो बड़ा फ़र्क़ पड़ेगा। agar tum roz ek ghaṇṭā paṛhoge to baṛā farq paṛegā. If you study for an hour every day, it will make a big difference.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
फ़र्क़ (farq) is usually traced to Arabic farq, "a separation, a difference", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 5 of 7.
प्राप्त करना (prāpt karnā) — to obtain, to receive (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a scholarship, then the Hindi for a difference.

You'll want to know: प्राप्त करना.
प्राप्त करना — prāpt karnā — "to obtain, to receive (formal)".
अगर तुम परीक्षा में अच्छे अंक प्राप्त करोगे तो तुम्हें इनाम मिलेगा। agar tum parīkṣā meṁ acche aṅk prāpt karoge to tumhẽ inām milegā. If you get good marks in the exam, you'll get a prize.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अच्छे अंक प्राप्त किए, usne acche aṅk prāpt kiye.

The word, taken apart.
प्राप्त करना (prāpt karnā) joins प्राप्त to करना, "to do". प्राप्त is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit prāpta, "attained, obtained" (Monier-Williams).
