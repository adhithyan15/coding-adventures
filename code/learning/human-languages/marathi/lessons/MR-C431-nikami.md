## You'll want to know: निकामी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C431-BIGHAD431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निकामी** — *nikāmī* — "out of action; useless".

> स्टेशनवरचा जुना पंखा आता निकामी झाला आहे.
>
> *sṭeśanvarcā junā paṅkhā ātā nikāmī jhālā āhe.*
>
> The old fan at the station no longer works at all.

It does not change: निकामी पंखा, निकामी गाडी; निकामी होणे, to stop working for good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निकामी** (*nikāmī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *nikāmī*, "idle, useless", under Sanskrit *niṣkarman*, "inactive", that is, without work (CDIAL 7475).
