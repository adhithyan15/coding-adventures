## You'll want to know: однако
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C347-ODNAKO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**однако** (*odnáko*) — "however".

> Было холодно, однако мы пошли гулять.
>
> *Býlo khólodno, odnáko my poshlí gulyát.*
>
> It was cold; however, we went for a walk.

Like *но*, but more formal. It can open the clause or sit inside it: *Мы, однако, пошли гулять*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**однако** is usually traced to *один* "one", with an old suffix; by that account it first meant "in one and the same way, all the same"; *один* is inherited from Proto-Slavic.
