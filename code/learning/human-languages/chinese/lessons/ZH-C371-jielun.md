## You'll want to know: jiélùn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C371-THOUGH371-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiélùn* — "conclusion".

> *wǒ juéde zhège jiélùn bú duì* — I don't think that conclusion is right.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiélùn* is built from *jié* and *lùn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ket* and *lwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tie' and the second 'to discuss'.
