Portuguese, chapter 61: Bird, Mouse, Wolf, Bear, Fox.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o pássaro — a bird.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a cow, then the Portuguese for a pig.

You'll want to know: o pássaro.
o pássaro — "a bird".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o pássaro]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o pássaro, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o pássaro]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a cow, then the Portuguese for a pig, then say o pássaro again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o pássaro mean? ("A bird".) Say it once more.
