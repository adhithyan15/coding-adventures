## You'll want to know: پارہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C430-PARA430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پارہ** — *pārā* — "the temperature (in everyday talk); mercury".

> گرمیوں میں پارہ چالیس سے اوپر جاتا ہے۔
>
> *garmiyoṅ meṅ pārā chālīs se ūpar jātā hai.*
>
> In summer the temperature goes above forty.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پارہ** (*pārā*) comes from Sanskrit *pārada*, "quicksilver" (Monier-Williams); the mercury in a thermometer gave Urdu its everyday word for the temperature.
