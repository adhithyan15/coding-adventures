## You'll want to know: विसंबणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C387-GANANA387-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विसंबणे** — *visambṇe* — "to rely on".

> मी फक्त स्वतःवर विसंबतो.
>
> *mī phakta svataḥvar visambto.*
>
> I rely only on myself.

Past (he …): तो विसंबला, *to visamblā*.

The one relied on takes -वर; विसंबून राहणे, to depend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विसंबणे** (*visambṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *visãbṇẽ*, "to pause, take rest", under Sanskrit *viśrāmyati*, "takes rest" (CDIAL 11959); today it means to rest one's weight on someone.
