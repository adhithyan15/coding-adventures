## You'll want to know: продавщица
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C452-BULOCHNAYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**продавщица** (*prodavshchítsa*) — "a shop assistant (woman)".

> Продавщица улыбнулась и дала мне сдачу.
>
> *Prodavshchítsa ulybnúlas i dalá mne sdáchu.*
>
> The shop assistant smiled and gave me my change.

The feminine of *продавец*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*продавщица* is the feminine of *продавец* 'a seller', from *продавать* 'to sell', on *давать* 'to give', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dāvati* (EtymDB).
