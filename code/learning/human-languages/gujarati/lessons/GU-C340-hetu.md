## You'll want to know: હેતુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C340-HETU340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હેતુ** — *hetu* — "a motive".

> તેના આવવાનો હેતુ શું હતો?
>
> *tenā āvvāno hetu śũ hato?*
>
> What was his motive in coming?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હેતુ** (*hetu*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *hetu*, "a cause, a motive" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
