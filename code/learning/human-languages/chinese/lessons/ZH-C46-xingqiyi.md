## You'll want to know: 星期一
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-02]; assesses=[] -->

**星期一** — *xīngqīyī* — "Monday".

> **今天星期一** *jīntiān xīngqīyī* — Today's Monday.

Tone: 一 keeps its first tone here, *yī*. It counts a day in order, and the change you know from *yìbǎi* does not apply.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**星期一** (*xīngqīyī*) is built from 星期 'week' and 一 'one': day one of the week.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
