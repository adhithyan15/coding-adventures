## You'll want to know: l'archeologo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C330-SCAVI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'archeologo** — "the archaeologist".

> L'archeologo ha studiato le rovine per anni.
>
> *The archaeologist studied the ruins for years.*

Plural *gli archeologi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*archeologo* is built from Greek *arkhaîos* "ancient" and *-lógos* "one who studies" (EtymDB has no Italian entry).
