Spanish, chapter 416: Stay in May.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
quedarse — come to rest and stay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall una habitación, a room. A traveller who keeps that room for several nights is going to quedarse, to stay.

The word, taken apart.
Spanish quedar reaches back to Latin quietāre, to make quiet or bring to rest. The se turns the action toward the person: quedarse is to remain or stay. Think of a traveller coming to rest in one place.

Grammar Lens: say who stays.
The pronoun changes with the person: me quedo means “I stay,” while the infinitive follows another verb in quiero quedarme, “I want to stay.” A duration can follow directly: quedarse tres noches.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: quedarse]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: me quedo tres noches]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: quiero quedarme tres noches]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To stay? (Quedarse.) I am staying three nights? (Me quedo tres noches.)
Source: DLE: quedar.


Lesson 2 of 4.
mayo — Maia's month.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall the months and quedarse, to stay. The hotel dates in this chapter fall in mayo, May.

The word, taken apart.
Spanish mayo continues Latin Maius, the Roman month associated with Maia. English May preserves the same name. Keep it separate from mayor, “bigger” or “older”: their similar spelling does not make them the same word.

Grammar Lens: dates use de.
Spanish links a day to its month with de: el quince de mayo, the fifteenth of May. A range uses del... al...: del quince al dieciocho de mayo. The month name stays lowercase.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mayo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: el quince de mayo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: quedarse tres noches en mayo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
May? (Mayo.) The fifteenth of May? (El quince de mayo.)
Source: DLE: mayo.
