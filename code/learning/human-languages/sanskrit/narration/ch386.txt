Sanskrit, chapter 386: Success.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
सिद्धिः (siddhiḥ) — an accomplishment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rewards (with a prize), then the Sanskrit for excellence.

You'll want to know: सिद्धिः.
सिद्धिः — siddhiḥ — "an accomplishment".
यदि प्रयत्नः दृढः, तर्हि सिद्धिः अवश्यं भविष्यति। yadi prayatnaḥ dṛḍhaḥ, tarhi siddhiḥ avaśyaṃ bhaviṣyati. If the effort is firm, success will surely come.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सिद्धिः (siddhiḥ) comes from the root √sidh, "to succeed"; MW gives siddhi "accomplishment, performance, fulfilment, success" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 6.
अर्जयति (arjayati) — earns; acquires.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an accomplishment, then the Sanskrit for comes off.

You'll want to know: अर्जयति.
अर्जयति — arjayati — "earns; acquires".
यदि सः प्रतिदिनं कार्यं करोति, तर्हि धनम् अर्जयति। yadi saḥ pratidinaṃ kāryaṃ karoti, tarhi dhanam arjayati. If he works every day, he earns money.
Past (he …): सः बहु धनम् अर्जितवान्, saḥ bahu dhanam arjitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अर्जयति (arjayati) comes from the root √ṛj, "to procure", in its causative form; MW gives the senses "to procure, to acquire, to obtain" and arjita "acquired, gained, earned" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 6.
अभ्युदयः (abhyudayaḥ) — a rise in fortune; prosperity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for comes off, then the Sanskrit for earns.

You'll want to know: अभ्युदयः.
अभ्युदयः — abhyudayaḥ — "a rise in fortune; prosperity".
यदि शान्तिः अस्ति, तर्हि देशस्य अभ्युदयः भविष्यति। yadi śāntiḥ asti, tarhi deśasya abhyudayaḥ bhaviṣyati. If there is peace, the country will prosper.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अभ्युदयः (abhyudayaḥ) is built on abhi, ud and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives abhy-udaya "sunrise" and "elevation, increase, prosperity" (MW).
