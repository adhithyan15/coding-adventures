## HL-C329 — Hindi my declares all three taught forms

After the vowel-sign repair, **मेरी** is the next independent modern Hindi
form in the ranked glossed-but-never-taught queue. The Chapter 2 possessive
lesson already teaches that Hindi “my” agrees with what is owned, explicitly
names **मेरा** (*merā*), **मेरी** (*merī*), and **मेरे** (*mere*), and asks
learners to produce all three forms in guided practice.

The old metadata declared only masculine **मेरा**, while the feminine and
plural forms appeared only as Latin-script romanizations in the lesson body.
That made existing teaching invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Print all three Devanagari forms where they are taught, declare
**मेरा / मेरी / मेरे** as the headword, and regenerate the derived Hindi
artifacts. This is a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new
vocabulary claim.
