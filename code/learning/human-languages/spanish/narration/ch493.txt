Spanish, chapter 493: Dar una opinión.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
opinar — to think, to have an opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to choose, then the Spanish for to keep.

You'll want to know: opinar.
opinar — "to think, to have an opinion".
¿Qué opinas del plan? What do you think of the plan?

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: opinar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: opinar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with opinar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for to choose, then the Spanish for to keep, then say opinar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does opinar mean? ("To think, to have an opinion".) Say it once more.
