Arabic, chapter 260: To sprinkle, To knock, To stick, To stir, To wrap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رش (rashsha) — to sprinkle, to spray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to jump, then the Arabic for to press.

You'll want to know: رش.
رش — rashsha — "to sprinkle, to spray".
