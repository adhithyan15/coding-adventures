## You'll want to know: il rigore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C509-ARBITRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il rigore** — "penalty (kick); strictness".

> L'arbitro ha dato il rigore all'ultimo minuto.
>
> *The referee gave the penalty in the last minute.*

Short for *calcio di rigore*. Also strictness: *con rigore*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rigore* is a learned borrowing from Latin *rigor* "stiffness, hardness" (Lewis & Short); the penalty is the strict punishment of a foul.
