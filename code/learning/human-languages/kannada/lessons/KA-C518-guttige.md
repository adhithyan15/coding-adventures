# ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ (guttige) — a contract; a lease

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C518-NEMAKA518-03, KA-LEX-C518-NEMAKA518-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for unemployment, then the Kannada for retirement.

## You'll want to know: ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C518-NEMAKA518-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ** — *guttige* — "a contract; a lease".

> ರಸ್ತೆ ಕೆಲಸದ ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ಕಂಪನಿಗೆ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *raste kelasada guttige ondu kampanige sikkitu.*
>
> The road-building contract went to a company.

ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆದಾರ, a contractor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ** (*guttige*) is a Kannada word for a contract or lease; its origin is not settled.

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
