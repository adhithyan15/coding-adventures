Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 277: Songs and Dancing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भजन (bhajan) — a devotional song.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a clap, then the Marwadi for to ring out.

You'll want to know: भजन.
भजन — bhajan — "a devotional song".
दादी रोज भजन गावै है। dādī roj bhajan gāvai hai. Grandmother sings a hymn every day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भजन (bhajan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit bhajana, "reverence, worship" (Monier-Williams); in Marwadi it is the song of worship.

Lesson 2 of 5.
राग (rāg) — a melody.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to ring out, then the Marwadi for a devotional song.

You'll want to know: राग.
राग — rāg — "a melody".
यो राग जूनो है। yo rāg jūno hai. This melody is old.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
राग (rāg) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit rāga, which Monier-Williams gives as "a musical note, harmony, melody" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
डोलणो (ḍolṇo) — to sway; to wander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a melody, then the Marwadi for rhythm.

You'll want to know: डोलणो.
डोलणो — ḍolṇo — "to sway; to wander".
छोरी नाचै है अर डोलै है। chhorī nāchai hai ar ḍolai hai. The girl dances and sways.
Past (he …): वो गांव मांय डोल्यो, vo gā̃v mā̃y ḍolyo.

The word, taken apart.
डोलणो (ḍolṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit dolāyate, "moves to and fro"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ḍolaï, "wanders" (CDIAL 6585).

Lesson 5 of 5.
रीझणो (rījhṇo) — to be delighted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for rhythm, then the Marwadi for to sway.

You'll want to know: रीझणो.
रीझणो — rījhṇo — "to be delighted".
दादी गीत सुणै है अर रीझै है। dādī gīt suṇai hai ar rījhai hai. Grandmother listens to the song and is delighted.
Past (he …): वो रीझ्यो, vo rījhyo.

The word, taken apart.
रीझणो (rījhṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit ṛdhyati, "increases, prospers"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari rījhaï, "is satisfied", and Hindi rījhnā, "to be enchanted" (CDIAL 2457).
