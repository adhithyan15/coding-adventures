## You'll want to know: थरारक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C340-RAHASYA340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**थरारक** — *tharārak* — "thrilling".

> त्या चित्रपटाचा शेवट फार थरारक होता.
>
> *tyā citrapaṭācā śevaṭ phār tharārak hotā.*
>
> The ending of that film was very thrilling.

थरारक keeps one form; थरार, a thrill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थरारक** (*tharārak*) is built on *tharār*, 'a thrill, a shiver'; CDIAL lists Marathi *tharārṇe*, 'to tremble', under *\*thar*, 'tremble' (CDIAL 6092).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
