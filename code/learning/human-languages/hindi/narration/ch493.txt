Hindi, chapter 493: Long Illnesses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मधुमेह (madhumeh) — diabetes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for ayurveda, then the Hindi for vigour.

You'll want to know: मधुमेह.
मधुमेह — madhumeh — "diabetes".
मधुमेह के मरीज़ को मीठा कम खाना चाहिए। madhumeh ke marīz ko mīṭhā kam khānā cāhiye. Someone with diabetes should eat fewer sweet things.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मधुमेह (madhumeh) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit madhumeha, "honey-like urine, diabetes" (Monier-Williams), built on madhu, "honey", and meha, "urine": old doctors knew the illness by its sweetness. Speech often says śugar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for diabetes, then the Hindi for asthma.

You'll want to know: लकवा.
लकवा — lakvā — "paralysis; a stroke".
लकवे के बाद दादा का एक हाथ कमज़ोर है। lakve ke bād dādā kā ek hāth kamzor hai. Since his stroke, my grandfather has one weak hand.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
लकवा (lakvā) is usually traced to Arabic laqwa, "facial palsy", which reached Hindi through Persian and widened to any paralysis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for paralysis, then the Hindi for cancer.

You'll want to know: जकड़ना.
जकड़ना — jakaṛnā — "to stiffen; to grip tight".
ठंड में दादी के घुटने जकड़ जाते हैं। ṭhaṇḍ meṁ dādī ke ghuṭne jakaṛ jāte haiṁ. In the cold my grandmother's knees stiffen up.
Past (it …): वह घुटना जकड़ गया, vah ghuṭnā jakaṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
जकड़ना (jakaṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi jakaṛnā, "to tighten, bind", under Sanskrit yatá, "held in, restrained" (CDIAL 10400). A stiff joint feels bound tight.
