## You'll want to know: સેના
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C320-YUDDH320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સેના** — *senā* — "an army".

> રાજાની સેના ખૂબ મોટી હતી.
>
> *rājānī senā khūb moṭī hatī.*
>
> The king's army was very large.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સેના** (*senā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sḗnā*, "an army" (CDIAL 13587). It is feminine: **મોટી સેના**, a large army.
