## You'll want to know: повышать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C354-BOLSHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**повышать** (*povyshát*) — "to raise, to increase".

> Кофе повышает давление.
>
> *Kófe povysháyet davléniye.*
>
> Coffee raises blood pressure.

Imperfective; the perfective is *повысить* (*повышу, повысишь*). *Повышать голос* — to raise one’s voice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**повышать** is built from *по-* and *выше* "higher", the comparative of *высокий* "high", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*vysokŭ* "high, tall" (EtymDB).
