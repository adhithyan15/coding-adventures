## You'll want to know: рыдать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C480-LAUGHTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**рыдать** (*rydát*) — "to sob".

> После фильма она рыдала.
>
> *Pósle fílma oná rydála.*
>
> After the film she sobbed.

Imperfective; present рыдаю. Stronger than *плакать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *рыдать* without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *rydati* 'to weep aloud'.
