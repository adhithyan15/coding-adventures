## You'll want to know: اینٹھن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C473-ACHE473-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اینٹھن** — *aiṅṭhan* — "a cramp".

> تیرنے کے بعد پنڈلی میں اینٹھن ہوئی۔
>
> *tairne ke bād pinḍlī meṅ aiṅṭhan huī.*
>
> I got a cramp in my calf after swimming.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اینٹھن** (*aiṅṭhan*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *aĩṭhan*, "a twist", under Sanskrit *āvēṣṭana*, "wrapping round" (CDIAL 1449): a cramp twists the muscle.
