## You'll want to know: மீறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C372-CATTAM372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**மீறு** — *mīṟu* — "to violate; to break (a rule)".

> அவன் சாலை விதிகளை மீறினான்.
>
> *avaṉ cālai vitikaḷai mīṟiṉāṉ.*
>
> He broke the traffic rules.

Past (I …): மீறினேன், *mīṟiṉēṉ*.

What is broken takes -ஐ: விதியை மீறு. The noun is மீறல், a violation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மீறு** (*mīṟu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4884 lists Tamil *mīṟu*, "to go beyond, exceed, transgress, violate".
