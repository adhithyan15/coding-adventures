# لهذا السبب (li-hādhā as-sabab) — for this reason

## You'll want to know: لهذا السبب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C374-RABT374-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لهذا السبب** — *li-hādhā as-sabab* — "for this reason".

> كان الجو ممطرا، لهذا السبب بقينا في المنزل.
>
> *kāna al-jaww mumṭiran, li-hādhā as-sabab baqīnā fī al-manzil.*
>
> The weather was rainy; for this reason we stayed at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لهذا السبب** (*li-hādhā as-sabab*) is a phrase: *li-*, "for", *hādhā*, "this", and *as-sabab*, "the reason", built on the native Arabic root *s-b-b*, "a rope; a means to an end" (Lane).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
