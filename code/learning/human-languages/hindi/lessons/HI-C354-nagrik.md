## You'll want to know: नागरिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C354-NAGRIK354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नागरिक** — *nāgrik* — "a citizen".

> हर नागरिक को वोट देने का अधिकार है।
>
> *har nāgrik ko voṭ dene kā adhikār hai.*
>
> Every citizen has the right to vote.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नागरिक** (*nāgrik*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nāgarika*, "a townsman; polite" (Monier-Williams).
