## You'll want to know: ਵਾਢੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C305-HARVEST305-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਢੀ** — *vāḍhī* — "the harvest; reaping".

> ਵਾਢੀ ਵਿਸਾਖ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ।
>
> *vāḍhī visākh vich hovegī.*
>
> The reaping will be in Visakh.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਢੀ** (*vāḍhī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vṛddhi*, "cutting off"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vāḍhī*, "harvesting, harvest", under it (CDIAL 12075).
