## You'll want to know: निरस्यति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C346-AKSHEPA346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निरस्यति** — *nirasyati* — "rejects (a claim)".

> अध्यापकः मम उत्तरं निरस्यति।
>
> *adhyāpakaḥ mama uttaraṃ nirasyati.*
>
> The teacher rejects my answer.

Past (he …): सः मम उत्तरं निरस्तवान्, *saḥ mama uttaraṃ nirastavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निरस्यति** (*nirasyati*) is built on *nir*, "out", and the root √as, "to throw"; MW gives *nir-√as* "to cast out" and "to reject, to refuse", and *nir-asta* "rejected, refuted" (MW).
