## You'll want to know: диплом
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C308-VYPUSKNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**диплом** (*diplóm*) — "a degree, a diploma".

> Она получила диплом врача.
>
> *Oná poluchíla diplóm vrachá.*
>
> She got her medical degree.

Masculine. *Дипломная работа* — a final-year thesis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*диплом* is usually traced through Latin *diplōma* to Greek *díplōma* "a paper folded double"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
