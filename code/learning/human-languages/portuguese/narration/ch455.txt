Portuguese, chapter 455: No banco.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o levantamento — the cash withdrawal; the survey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to dispatch, then the Portuguese for to register.

You'll want to know: o levantamento.
o levantamento — "the cash withdrawal; the survey".
Fiz um levantamento no multibanco. I made a withdrawal at the cash machine.
Levantar dinheiro — to withdraw cash.

The word, taken apart.
levantamento is built on levantar 'to lift, to raise', which goes back to Latin levāre 'to lighten, to raise' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Cobrar caro — to charge a lot.

Lesson 3 of 5.
debitar — to debit, to take (money) from an account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the cash withdrawal, then the Portuguese for to charge.

You'll want to know: debitar.
debitar — "to debit, to take (money) from an account".
O banco debitou a renda no dia um. The bank took the rent out on the first.
O débito direto — a direct debit.

The word, taken apart.
debitar is built on débito 'debit, debt', a learned borrowing from Latin dēbitum 'what is owed', from dēbēre 'to owe' (Lewis & Short).
