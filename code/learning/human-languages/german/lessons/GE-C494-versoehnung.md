## You'll want to know: die Versöhnung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C494-STREIT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Versöhnung** — "reconciliation".

> Zur Versöhnung kaufte er ihr Blumen.
>
> *To make up, he bought her flowers.*

Plural: *die Versöhnungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Versöhnung* is built on *versöhnen* with *-ung*; the verb comes from Middle High German *versüenen* (EtymDB).
