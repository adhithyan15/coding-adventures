# খনন করা (khônôn kôrā) — to excavate

## You'll want to know: খনন করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C319-PURATON319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**খনন করা** — *khônôn kôrā* — "to excavate".

> বিজ্ঞানীরা দশ বছর ধরে জায়গাটা খনন করলেন।
>
> *bigyānīrā dôsh bôchhôr dhôre jāygāṭā khônôn kôrlen.*
>
> The scientists excavated the site for ten years.

Past (he/she …): তিনি পুরনো শহরটা খনন করলেন, *tini purôno shôhôrṭā khônôn kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খনন করা** (*khônôn kôrā*) puts Sanskrit *khanana*, 'the act of digging or excavating' (Monier-Williams), before করা; **খোঁড়া** is the everyday word for digging.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
