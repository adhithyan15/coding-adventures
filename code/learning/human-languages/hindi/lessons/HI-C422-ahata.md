## You'll want to know: अहाता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C422-KOTHI422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अहाता** — *ahātā* — "a compound, an enclosure".

> स्कूल के अहाते में बच्चे खेल रहे थे।
>
> *skūl ke ahāte meṁ bacce khel rahe the.*
>
> The children were playing in the school compound.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अहाता** (*ahātā*) is usually traced to Arabic *iḥāṭa*, "an enclosing, an enclosure", through Persian: the walled ground round a house, a school or an office.
