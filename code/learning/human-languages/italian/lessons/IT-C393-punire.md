## You'll want to know: punire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C393-CARCERE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**punire** — "to punish".

> Se rompi la regola, ti puniscono.
>
> *If you break the rule, they will punish you.*

Conjugated with *-isc-*: *punisco, punisci*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*punire* is inherited from Latin *pūnīre*, older *poenīre*, "to punish" (Lewis & Short), built on *poena* "penalty".
