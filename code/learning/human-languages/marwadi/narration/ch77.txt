Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 77: Red, Blue, Black, Brown, Sweet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लाल (lāl) — red.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for green, then the Marwadi for yellow.

You'll want to know: लाल.
