## You'll want to know: 找回
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C285-ZHAOHUI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**找回** — *zhǎohuí* — "to get back", something you had lost.

> **我找回了我的手机** *wǒ zhǎohuí le wǒ de shǒujī* — I got my phone back.

Both characters are ones you write.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**找回** (*zhǎohuí*) is built from 找 'to look for' and 回 'to go back'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to turn round'; the dictionary has no entry for 找, so the history of that part is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C63-PHONE63-05, ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-02, ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-COMPLETION-01] -->

找 is to look for, 找到 is to find, and 找回 is to find what you had lost and have it again.

了 after it says it is done: 我找回了我的手机.
