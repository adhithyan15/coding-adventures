## You'll want to know: ಸಂಚಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C443-SANCHARA443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಚಾರ** — *sañcāra* — "traffic; movement".

> ಸಂಜೆ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಸಂಚಾರ ತುಂಬಾ ನಿಧಾನ.
>
> *sañje āru gaṇṭege sañcāra tumbā nidhāna.*
>
> At six in the evening the traffic is very slow.

ಸಂಚಾರ ಪೊಲೀಸ್, the traffic police; ಸಂಚಾರ ನಿಯಮ, a traffic rule.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಚಾರ** (*saṃcāra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃcāra*, "walking about, a passage" (CDIAL 12869), from *sam-* "together" and *car* "to move".
