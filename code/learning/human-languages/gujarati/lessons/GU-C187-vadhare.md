# વધારે (vadhāre) — more, too much

## You'll want to know: વધારે
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C187-PRICE187-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વધારે** — *vadhāre* — "more, too much".

> **આ દુકાનમાં વધારે ગ્રાહક છે.** — *ā dukānmā̃ vadhāre grāhak che* — "This shop has more customers."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વધારે** (*vadhāre*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *vṛddhatara*, "larger", the comparative of *vṛddha*, "grown", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "more, greater" (CDIAL 12074).
