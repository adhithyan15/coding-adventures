## You'll want to know: उधार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C427-UDHAR427-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उधार** — *udhār* — "credit; a loan".

> दुकानदार ने मुझे उधार पर चीनी दी।
>
> *dukāndār ne mujhe udhār par cīnī dī.*
>
> The shopkeeper gave me sugar on credit.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उधार** (*udhār*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *uddhāra*, "raising" and also "a loan" (CDIAL 2018), which lists Hindi *udhār*, "a debt".
