# ۲ ۳ — one tooth apart

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-ZERO, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-ONE, UR-SCRIPT-DIGITS-LTR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **۰** and **۱**, then read **۱۰**.

## Script — the shapes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-TWO, UR-SCRIPT-DIGIT-THREE]; assesses=[UR-LEX-DO, UR-LEX-TEEN] -->

> **۲** — *do*, two  **۳** — *tīn*, three

**۳** is **۲** with one more tooth, and that is the whole difference. It is
small enough that the two have to be written as a pair rather than one at a
time: say *do* and *tīn* aloud as the hand makes them, and the shapes stop
competing.
