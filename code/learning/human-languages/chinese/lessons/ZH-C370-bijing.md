## You'll want to know: bìjìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C370-KEY370-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bìjìng* — "after all; all things considered".

> *tā bìjìng shì háizi* — He's only a child, after all.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bìjìng* is built from *bì* and *jìng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjit* and *kjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the end' and the second 'to use up' and 'the end'.
