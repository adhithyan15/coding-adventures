## What you've built this chapter, in one number
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-KUN-IN-THE-COUNT, JA-LEX-YON, JA-LEX-ICHIDO] -->

**Five numbers, and zero new signs.** Every mark in *ichi, ni, san, yon, go* was
taught before this chapter opened, and two of the five words were already in
your hands: *ichi* inside **もういちど**, and *go* as the kanji **五**.

One thing did arrive that is not a word: at **four**, the native reading stands
beside the borrowed one. Watch for it again.
