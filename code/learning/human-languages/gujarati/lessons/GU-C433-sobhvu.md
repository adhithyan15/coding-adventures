## You'll want to know: શોભવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C433-OUTFIT433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**શોભવું** — *śobhvũ* — "to suit; to look good (on someone)".

> આ લાલ સાડી તને ઘણી શોભે છે.
>
> *ā lāl sāḍī tane ghaṇī śobhe che.*
>
> This red sari suits you very well.

Past (it …): તે સાડી તેને શોભી, *te sāḍī tene śobhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શોભવું** (*śobhvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *śobhā*, "splendour, beauty, grace" (Monier-Williams): to look beautiful in something.
