## You'll want to know: કાપલી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C424-COUNTER424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કાપલી** — *kāplī* — "a slip of paper".

> પૈસા જમા કરવા માટે આ કાપલી ભરો.
>
> *paisā jamā karvā māṭe ā kāplī bharo.*
>
> Fill in this slip to deposit money.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાપલી** (*kāplī*) is built on કાપવું, to cut, a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kalpayati*, "trims, cuts" (CDIAL 2944): a strip of paper cut off.
