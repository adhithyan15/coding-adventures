Lesson 1 of 5.
treffen — to meet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to disturb, then the German for to argue.

You'll want to know: treffen.
treffen — "to meet".

The word, taken apart.
treffen from Old High German treffan, "to hit" from Proto-Germanic drepaną — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) To meet is to hit upon someone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
überlegen — to think over.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to dream, then the German for to practise.

You'll want to know: überlegen.
überlegen — "to think over".

The word, taken apart.
überlegen is the word for "over" + legen, "to lay" from Proto-Germanic lagjaną (EtymDB 2.1): to lay a thing over and look at it from the other side.
