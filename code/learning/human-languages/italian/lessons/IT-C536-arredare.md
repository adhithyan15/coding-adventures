## You'll want to know: arredare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C536-CASA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**arredare** — "to furnish".

> Abbiamo arredato la casa con mobili usati.
>
> *We furnished the house with second-hand furniture.*

The furniture as a whole is *l'arredamento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arredare* comes from Gothic *rēdan* "to provide", through Medieval Latin (EtymDB).
