Gujarati, chapter 87: What We Wear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધોતી (dhotī) — a dhoti.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a phone, then the Gujarati for the post.

You'll want to know: ધોતી.
ધોતી (dhotī) — "a dhoti".

What you've built.
One more word for what we wear.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dhotī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dhotī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dhotī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a phone, then the Gujarati for the post, then say dhotī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ધોતી (dhotī) mean? ("A dhoti".) Say it once more.
