## You'll want to know: आटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C435-TANCHAI435-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आटणे** — *āṭṇe* — "to dry up".

> उन्हाळ्यात नदी आटली.
>
> *unhāḷyāt nadī āṭlī.*
>
> In summer the river dried up.

Past (he …): तो आटला, *to āṭlā*.

For water in a pot too: दूध आटलं, the milk boiled down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आटणे** (*āṭṇe*) is a Marathi verb for water drying away; its origin is not settled.
