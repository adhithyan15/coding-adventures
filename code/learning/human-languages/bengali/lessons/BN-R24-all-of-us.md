[PAUSE 4s] Four chapters ago the letter **ট** did not exist in this book and
nothing could be pointed at, counted, pluralised or owned by anybody but you and
the person in front of you. Run the whole distance, cold.

- [YOU WRITE: ট, then টা]
- [YOU SAY: the classifier and the indefinite — *jāmāṭā*, *ekṭā jāmā*, *ārekṭā jāmā*]
- [YOU SAY: pointing near and far — *ei jāmāṭā*, *oi jāmāṭā*]
- [YOU SAY: the two plurals, sorted by group — *bhāirā*, *jāmāgulo*, *chokhgulo*]
- [YOU SAY: an adjective that never changes — *lāl jāmā*, *lāl jāmāgulo*]
- [YOU SAY: the third person, both rungs — *she bhālo*, *tini bhālo*, *o bhālo*]
- [YOU SAY: whose, singular and plural — *tār pôribār*, *āmāder pôribār*, *tāder bhāirā*]
- [YOU SAY: the whole queue, with an owner on the front — *āmāder ei lāl jāmāṭā*]
- [YOU SAY: asking about it — *eṭā ki?*]
