## You'll want to know: अतिगच्छति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C412-VIRAMA412-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिगच्छति** — *atigacchati* — "passes by; overtakes".

> कारयानं लोकयानम् अतिगच्छति।
>
> *kārayānaṃ lokayānam atigacchati.*
>
> The car passes the bus.

Past (it …): कारयानं लोकयानम् अतिगतवत्, *kārayānaṃ lokayānam atigatavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिगच्छति** (*atigacchati*) is built from *ati*, "beyond, past", and the root √gam, "to go"; MW gives *ati-gam* as to pass by or over, with the participle *atigata*, "having passed" (MW).
