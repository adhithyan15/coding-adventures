# ध — dha, voiced and breathy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Keep the dental voiced position ready; this sign adds breath.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-DHA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-VISARGA-01, MR-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01, MR-SCRIPT-BHA-01] -->

> ध

Say *da*, then add a puff: *dha*. Curl around the upper spiral and sweep through
its shoulder; lift for the lower bowl; descend the right stem; finish the
headline.

## Writing — delayed copy and a wider return
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-DHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Hide **ध** after five seconds and write it once. Then, without models, name the
first three signs of this runway: visarga, independent *ā*, *bha*.
