## You'll want to know: ふりかえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C311-OMOIDE311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ふりかえる** — *furikaeru* — "to look back (on)".

> **そふは ときどき むかしを ふりかえります。** — *sofu wa tokidoki mukashi o furikaerimasu* — "Grandfather sometimes thinks back to the old days."

Polite **ふりかえります**, *furikaerimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが ふりかえりました。**, *haha ga furikaerimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふりかえる** (*furikaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *furi*, "swinging" (from *furu*), and *kaeru*, "to turn back", the intransitive partner of *kaesu*, "to give back" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
