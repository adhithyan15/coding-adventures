## You'll want to know: borgen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C388-KREDIT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**borgen** — "to borrow (sich borgen); to lend".

> Ich habe mir das Fahrrad von meinem Nachbarn geborgt.
>
> *I borrowed the bike from my neighbour.*

*Sich etwas borgen* — to borrow; *jemandem etwas borgen* — to lend. In Austria people also say *ausborgen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*borgen* is inherited from Old High German *borgēn* and Proto-Germanic *burgōną* (EtymDB), usually explained as first "to look after, to give surety", then to lend and borrow.
