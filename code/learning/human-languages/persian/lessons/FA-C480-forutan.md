## You'll want to know: فروتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C480-PRIDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فروتن** (*forutan*) — "humble, modest".

> او با اینکه مشهور است، خیلی فروتن است.
>
> *u bâ inke mashhur ast, kheyli forutan ast.*
>
> Although he is famous, he is very humble.

Praise, not blame. The noun is *forutani* 'humility'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*forutan* is a native Persian compound, usually explained as *foru*, 'down', and *tan*, 'body': someone who bows low. EtymDB has no entry for it, so the analysis is hedged; it is not a loan.
