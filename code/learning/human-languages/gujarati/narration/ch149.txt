Gujarati, chapter 149: What will happen.
6 lessons. You can do the first 4 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
હું બોલીશ — the future, built on મળીશું.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I drank tea and I ate rice. Then the goodbye from the first week: કાલે મળીશું, see you tomorrow, word for word tomorrow we will meet.

Grammar Lens: -ીશું, we will.
મળીશું is મળ-, meet, plus -ીશું, we will. Gujarati needs no separate word for will: the future is an ending on the stem.
આપણે કાલે મળીશું. — āpṇe kāle maḷīśũ. — We'll meet tomorrow. અમે ગુજરાતી બોલીશું. — ame gujarātī bolīśũ. — We will speak Gujarati.

You'll want to know: -ીશ, I will.
Take the last -ું off and you have I will:
હું ગુજરાતી બોલીશ. — hũ gujarātī bolīś. — I will speak Gujarati.
The familiar you takes the same form in the standard language: તું બોલીશ, you will speak. There is no copula and no gender: a man and a woman both say હું બોલીશ.
The -શ of these endings is usually traced to the old Sanskrit future, the -iṣya- of kariṣyati, "will do".

[your turn — say: હું આવતીકાલે લખીશ — hũ āvtīkāle lakhīś, I'll write tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: અમે ઘરે રહીશું — ame ghare rahīśũ, we'll stay at home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: અમે કાલે આવીશું — ame kāle āvīśũ, we'll come tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that tomorrow you will speak Gujarati, then that we, you and I, will meet.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What word for will does Gujarati use? (None; the future is an ending.) Which ending did મળીશું teach you long ago? (-ીશું, we will.)


Lesson 2 of 6.
તે બોલશે — the future for everyone else.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I will speak Gujarati, then we'll meet tomorrow.

Grammar Lens: -શે.
તે આવતીકાલે આવશે. — te āvtīkāle āvśe. — He will come tomorrow. She will come tomorrow. તેઓ ગુજરાતી બોલશે. — teo gujarātī bolśe. — They will speak Gujarati.
-શે sits on the bare stem, with no -ી- before it: બોલ-શે, આવ-શે.

You'll want to know: -શો.
તમે keeps the -ો it has in the present, બોલો છો:
તમે ક્યારે આવશો? — tame kyāre āvśo? — When will you come?
The future for one person:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
who: હું, તું. will speak: બોલીશ.
who: તે. will speak: બોલશે.
And for more than one, or for respect:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
who: અમે, આપણે. will speak: બોલીશું.
who: તમે. will speak: બોલશો.
who: તેઓ. will speak: બોલશે.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: te āvśe, then teo āvśe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — ask: tame kyāre āvśo?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: with હું … આવીશ — hũ … āvīś]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which two persons share -શે? (તે and તેઓ.) What does તમે take? (-શો.)


Lesson 3 of 6.
જઈશ, થશે, હશે — the short verbs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say she will come tomorrow, then ask when will you come? And two short verbs: જવું, to go, and લેવું, to take.

Grammar Lens: જવું, લેવું, કરવું.
જવું and લેવું have short stems, and they put an ઈ where the long verbs have -ી-:
હું જઈશ — jaīś — I will go; અમે જઈશું; તે જશે; તમે જશો;
હું લઈશ — laīś — I will take; તે લેશે.
કરવું is regular: હું કરીશ, અમે કરીશું, તે કરશે.
હું આવતીકાલે બજાર જઈશ. — hũ āvtīkāle bajār jaīś. — I'll go to the market tomorrow.

You'll want to know: થશે and હશે.
થવું, to happen, and હોવું, to be, are heard most often with -શે:
મોડું થશે. — moḍũ thaśe. — It will be late, word for word lateness will happen. તે ઘરે હશે. — te ghare haśe. — She will be at home.
હશે is the future of છે: છે now, હતો before, હશે later. With તમે it is હશો: શું તમે ઘરે હશો?, will you be at home? Said about right now, હશે is a guess: તે ઘરે હશે can also mean she must be at home.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hũ jaīś, te jaśe, tame jaśo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: હું દવા લઈશ — hũ davā laīś]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: સાંજે તેઓ ઘરે હશે — sānje teo ghare haśe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which form is the future of છે? (હશે.) What do જવું and લેવું put before -શ? (ઈ: જઈશ, લઈશ.)


Lesson 4 of 6.
ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) — sleepiness, sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say I will go, then it will be late.

You'll want to know: ઊંઘ.
ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) — "sleepiness, sleep".
Gujarati does not say I am sleepy. Sleep comes to you, the way a fever does:
મને ઊંઘ આવે છે. — mane ūṅgh āve che. — I'm sleepy. Word for word: to me, sleep comes. આજે રાત્રે સારી ઊંઘ આવશે. — āje rātre sārī ūṅgh āvśe. — Tonight there will be good sleep.
ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) is feminine, so good bends to સારી. સૂવું, to sleep, is lying down to sleep; ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) is the sleep itself.

The word, taken apart.
ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) is inherited. CDIAL reconstructs an Indo-Aryan uṅghā, "sleep", and lists the Gujarati word under it, beside Hindi ūṅgh (CDIAL 1633).

What you've built.
One more word, and sleep joins likes and fevers among the things that happen to someone.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ūṅgh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mane ūṅgh āve che]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: શું તમને ઊંઘ આવે છે? — śũ tamne ūṅgh āve che?, are you sleepy?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) mean? (Sleepiness, sleep.) Which letter in it have you read but never written? (ઊ (ū), the next lesson.)


Lesson 5 of 6.
ઊ — the standing vowel ū.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Script — meet one form, Writing — recall after hiding and Wrap-up Recall.

Warm-up.
[your turn — recall: say sleepiness — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the standing vowel ઉ, and the sign ૂ that gives a consonant a long ū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) opens on a long ū with no consonant to carry it.

You'll want to know: ઉ with a long tail.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
short u. after a consonant: ુ. standing alone: ઉ.
long ū. after a consonant: ૂ. standing alone: ?
The missing letter is built on ઉ: the whole of ઉ, then a tail that runs across the top and down the right side.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — meet one form.
ઊ — ū.
Keep ઊ (ū) visible. Write ઉ first: the small upper bowl, the middle cusp, the broad lower bowl, and the tall curve on the outside. Do not lift. Carry on across the upper shoulder, come down the long tail on the right, and finish through its foot. One stroke, no lift.
The tail is the whole difference. Stop before it and the letter reads ઉ.

Writing — notice and trace.
Finger-trace ઊ (ū) once, then air-trace it once. Notice where ઉ ends and the tail begins.

Writing — copy with the model.
Copy visible ઊ (ū) once. Compare only two things: is all of ઉ there, and does the tail reach down the right side? Repair one landmark if needed.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Writing — recall after hiding.
Hide the model and write the standing vowel ū once. Uncover ઊ, compare, and stop after one repair.
[once you have stopped driving — question: Hide the model and write the standing vowel long u from memory.]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read ઉ ઊ (ū) ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) aloud. What turns ઉ into ઊ (ū)? (The long tail.)
