## You'll want to know: सिञ्चति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C327-HOLIKA327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सिञ्चति** — *siñcati* — "sprinkles".

> बालाः मित्रेषु जलं सिञ्चन्ति।
>
> *bālāḥ mitreṣu jalaṃ siñcanti.*
>
> The children sprinkle water on their friends.

Past (he …): सः मित्रे जलं सिक्तवान्, *saḥ mitre jalaṃ siktavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिञ्चति** (*siñcati*) comes from the root √sic, "to pour out, sprinkle", the root of *abhiṣeka* too (MW).
