## You'll want to know: जनता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C315-DAVA315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जनता** — *jantā* — "the public; the people".

> जनता उस दावे से ख़ुश नहीं थी।
>
> *jantā us dāve se khuś nahīṃ thī.*
>
> The public was not happy with that claim.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जनता** (*jantā*) is Sanskrit *janatā*, "a number of people, a community" (Monier-Williams), from *jana*, a person. It is singular and feminine in Hindi: **जनता ख़ुश नहीं थी**.
