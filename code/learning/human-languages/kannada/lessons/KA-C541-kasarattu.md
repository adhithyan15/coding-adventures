## You'll want to know: ಕಸರತ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C541-VYAYAMA541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಸರತ್ತು** — *kasarattu* — "a workout; physical exercise".

> ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಅಪ್ಪ ಅರ್ಧ ಗಂಟೆ ಕಸರತ್ತು ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *pratidina beḷagge appa ardha gaṇṭe kasarattu māḍuttāre.*
>
> Every morning father works out for half an hour.

Also a clever trick or stunt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಸರತ್ತು** (*kasarattu*) is usually traced to Persian *kasrat*, "exercise", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.
