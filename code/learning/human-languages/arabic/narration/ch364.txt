Arabic, chapter 364: Analysing things.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حلل (ḥallala) — to analyse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a phenomenon, then the Arabic for to interpret.

You'll want to know: حلل.
حلل — ḥallala — "to analyse".
حلل الطالب القصيدة بعناية. ḥallala aṭ-ṭālib al-qaṣīda bi-ʿināya. The student analysed the poem carefully.
Present: يحلل, yuḥallil.

The word, taken apart.
حلل (ḥallala) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-l-l, "to untie, to loosen" (Lane). It is Form II; in modern use it means to undo a thing into its parts: to analyse.

You'll want to know: عنصر.
عنصر — ʿunṣur — "an element".
الماء عنصر مهم في الطبخ. al-māʾ ʿunṣur muhimm fī aṭ-ṭabkh. Water is an important element in cooking.
Plural عناصر, ʿanāṣir.

The word, taken apart.
عنصر (ʿunṣur) is an old word for the stock or basic matter of a thing, which Lane uses when he explains other words. Its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
معيار (miʿyār) — a criterion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to analyse, then the Arabic for an element.

You'll want to know: معيار.
معيار — miʿyār — "a criterion".
الجودة هي المعيار الأول عندنا. al-jawda hiya al-miʿyār al-awwal ʿindanā. Quality is our first criterion.
Plural: معايير, maʿāyīr.

The word, taken apart.
معيار (miʿyār) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-y-r (Lane). It is a noun of instrument, usually traced to the checking of weights and measures: a standard that others are measured against.

Lesson 4 of 5.
حدد (ḥaddada) — to specify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an element, then the Arabic for a criterion.

You'll want to know: حدد.
حدد — ḥaddada — "to specify".
حدد المدير وقت الاجتماع. ḥaddada al-mudīr waqt al-ijtimāʿ. The manager set the time of the meeting.
Present: يحدد, yuḥaddid.
حدود, borders, comes from the same root.

The word, taken apart.
حدد (ḥaddada) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-d-d, "to sharpen; to set a limit" (Lane). It is Form II, to mark the limits of: to define, to fix, to specify.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ميز (mayyaza) — to distinguish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a criterion, then the Arabic for to specify.

You'll want to know: ميز.
ميز — mayyaza — "to distinguish".
لا يميز الطفل بين الألوان بعد. lā yumayyizu aṭ-ṭifl bayna al-alwān baʿd. The child cannot tell the colours apart yet.
Present: يميز, yumayyiz.
Often with بين: ميز (mayyaza) بين … و …, told … from ….

The word, taken apart.
ميز (mayyaza) is built on the native Arabic root m-y-z, "to separate, to set apart" (Lane). It is Form II, to set apart: to tell one thing from another.
