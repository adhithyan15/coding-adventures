Urdu, chapter 447: Calm Again.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
اطمینان (itmīnān) — satisfaction; peace of mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to writhe, then the Urdu for to throb.

You'll want to know: اطمینان.
اطمینان — itmīnān — "satisfaction; peace of mind".
کام ختم کر کے مجھے اطمینان ہوا۔ kām khatm kar ke mujhe itmīnān huā. I felt satisfied once the work was finished.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اطمینان (itmīnān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iṭmiʾnān, "being at rest".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
