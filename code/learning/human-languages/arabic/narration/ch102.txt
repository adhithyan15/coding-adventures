Arabic, chapter 102: Roof, Stairs, Ceiling, Behind, Between.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سطح (saṭḥ) — a roof.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bathroom, then the Arabic for a balcony.

You'll want to know: سطح.
سطح — saṭḥ — "a roof".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does سطح (saṭḥ) mean? ("A roof".) Say it once more.
