## You'll want to know: nāvāle, nāvālis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C441-PORTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**nāvāle, nāvālis** — "a dockyard, a place where ships are kept".

> In nāvālī fabrī nāvēs novās aedificant.
>
> *In the dockyard the workmen build new ships.*

Third declension, neuter: *nāvāle, nāvālis n.*, often plural *nāvālia*. It is the neuter of the adjective *nāvālis* 'of ships'; English *naval* comes from that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nāvāle* is the neuter of the adjective *nāvālis* 'of ships', built on *nāvis* 'ship' (Lewis & Short). English *naval* comes from the adjective.
