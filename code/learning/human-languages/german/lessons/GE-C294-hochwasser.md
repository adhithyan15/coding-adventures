## You'll want to know: das Hochwasser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C294-ERDBEBEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Hochwasser** — "the high water, a river in flood".

> Das Hochwasser kam bis an unsere Tür.
>
> *The high water came up to our door.*

Plural: *die Hochwasser*. For rivers that rise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hochwasser* joins *hoch* "high", Old High German *hōh* (EtymDB), and *Wasser* "water", both inherited Germanic words.
