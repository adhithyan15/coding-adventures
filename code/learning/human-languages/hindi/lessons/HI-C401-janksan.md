## You'll want to know: जंक्शन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C401-PHATAK401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जंक्शन** — *jaṅkśan* — "a junction; a railway junction".

> यह स्टेशन एक बड़ा जंक्शन है।
>
> *yah sṭeśan ek baṛā jaṅkśan hai.*
>
> This station is a big junction.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जंक्शन** (*jaṅkśan*) is borrowed from English *junction*, a station where railway lines meet; many station names end in it.
