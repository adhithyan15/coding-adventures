## You'll want to know: la barbilla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C736-CARA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la barbilla** — "the chin".

> Tiene una cicatriz en la barbilla.
>
> *He has a scar on his chin.*

*Levantar la barbilla* — to lift your chin. *El mentón* means the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barbilla* is built on *barba* "beard, chin" with the small ending *-illa*; *barba* is inherited from Latin *barba* (EtymDB).
