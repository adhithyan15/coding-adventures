## You'll want to know: أعلن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C293-AKHBAR293-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أعلن** — *aʿlana* — "to announce".

> أعلنت المدرسة موعد الامتحان.
>
> *aʿlanat al-madrasa mawʿid al-imtiḥān.*
>
> The school announced the date of the exam.

Present: يعلن, *yuʿlin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أعلن** (*aʿlana*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-l-n* (Lane), "to be open, public". It is Form IV, "to make public".
