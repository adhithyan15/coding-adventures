## You'll want to know: incendium, incendiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C317-INCENDIUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**incendium, incendiī** — "a fire, a blaze".

> Noctū magnum incendium in urbe ortum est.
>
> *At night a great fire broke out in the city.*

Neuter, from *incendō*, 'to set on fire' (taught). Fire was the great fear of a crowded city of wooden floors and oil lamps.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**incendium** is built on *incendō*, "to set on fire" (EtymDB), from *in* and *candeō*, "to glow". English *incendiary* comes from its family.
