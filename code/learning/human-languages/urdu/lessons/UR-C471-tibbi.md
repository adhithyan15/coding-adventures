## You'll want to know: طبی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C471-HEALTH471-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طبی** — *tibbī* — "medical".

> ڈاکٹر نے طبی مشورہ دیا۔
>
> *ḍākṭar ne tibbī mashvarā diyā.*
>
> The doctor gave medical advice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طبی** (*tibbī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṭibbī*, "medical", built on *ṭibb*, "medicine".
