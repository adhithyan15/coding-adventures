## You'll want to know: अन्नप्राशनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C493-SAMSKARA493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्नप्राशनम्** — *annaprāśanam* — "a baby's first rice".

> षष्ठे मासे शिशोः अन्नप्राशनं भवति।
>
> *ṣaṣṭhe māse śiśoḥ annaprāśanaṃ bhavati.*
>
> In the sixth month the baby has its first rice.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्नप्राशनम्** (*annaprāśanam*) is built from **अन्नम्**, "food", and *prāśana*, "feeding", from *pra* and the root √aś, "to eat"; MW gives "putting rice into a child's mouth for the first time" (MW).
