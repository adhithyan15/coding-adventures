## You'll want to know: agradecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C484-USAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**agradecer** — "to thank for, to be grateful for".

> Le agradezco su ayuda.
>
> *I am grateful for your help.*

It takes a *z* in the first person present: *agradezco*.
