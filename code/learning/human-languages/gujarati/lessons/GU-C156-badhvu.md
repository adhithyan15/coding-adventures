## You'll want to know: બાંધવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C156-ADORN156-05]; assesses=[] -->

**બાંધવું** — *bā̃dhvũ* — "to tie, to bind".

> **તે વાળ બાંધે છે.** — *te vāḷ bā̃dhe che* — "She ties up her hair."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બાંધવું** (*bā̃dhvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *bandhati*, "binds", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to bind, tie" (CDIAL 9139).
