## You'll want to know: মজুত করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C421-GUDAM421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মজুত করা** — *môjut kôrā* — "to stock up; to store".

> শীতের আগে দোকানদার কম্বল মজুত করলেন।
>
> *shīter āge dokāndār kômbôl môjut kôrlen.*
>
> Before winter the shopkeeper stocked up on blankets.

Past (he/she …): তিনি চাল মজুত করলেন, *tini chāl môjut kôrlen*.

Also said of traders who hold back goods to push up the price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মজুত করা** (*môjut kôrā*) joins মজুত, "in stock", usually traced to Arabic *mawjūd*, "existing, at hand", through Persian, to করা, to do.
