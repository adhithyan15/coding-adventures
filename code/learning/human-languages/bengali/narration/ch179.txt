Bengali, chapter 179: To taste, To swallow, To chew, To look, To pick.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
চাখা (chākhā) — to taste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a seller, then the Bengali for a buyer.

You'll want to know: চাখা.
চাখা — chākhā — "to taste".

The word, taken apart.
চাখা (chākhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cakṣati, "tastes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4557). CDIAL lists Bengali cākhā, "to taste".

Lesson 5 of 5.
বাছা (bāchhā) — to pick, to choose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to chew, then the Bengali for to look.

You'll want to know: বাছা.
বাছা — bāchhā — "to pick, to choose".

The word, taken apart.
বাছা (bāchhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. CDIAL lists Bengali bāchā, "to select, choose", under vraścati, a form of vṛścati, "cuts" (CDIAL 12080), and explains that verbs for "cut" and "choose", such as vṛkṣate, "chooses", ran together there.
