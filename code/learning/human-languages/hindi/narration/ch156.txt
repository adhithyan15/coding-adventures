Hindi, chapter 156: Slow, Sour, Bitter, Salty, Spicy.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
धीमा (dhīmā) — slow, low.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for dirty, then the Hindi for fast.

You'll want to know: धीमा.
धीमा — dhīmā — "slow, low".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dhīmā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dhīmā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dhīmā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for dirty, then the Hindi for fast, then say dhīmā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does धीमा (dhīmā) mean? ("Slow, low".) Say it once more.
