## You'll want to know: جدوجہد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C313-TEHRIK313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جدوجہد** — *jaddojahd* — "a struggle".

> یہ ایک لمبی جدوجہد تھی۔
>
> *yih ek lambī jaddojahd thī.*
>
> It was a long struggle.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جدوجہد** (*jaddojahd*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jidd*, "effort", and *jahd*, "striving", joined by *o*, "and". It is feminine.
