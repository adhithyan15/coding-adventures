# एकं यानपत्रं ददातु (ekaṃ yānapatraṃ dadātu) — a ticket, please

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-02, SA-LEX-C172-TRV172-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "where is the station?" (*sthānakaṃ kutra asti?*) Then say "a ticket". (*yānapatram*.) You are at the counter now.

## You'll want to know: tickets
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C172-TRV172-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-LEX-C29-COURTESY-01, SA-LEX-C185-WHY185-01, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-03] -->

> **एकं यानपत्रं ददातु।**
>
> *ekaṃ yānapatraṃ dadātu.* — "One ticket, please."

**एकम्** (*ekam*), "one", agrees with **यानपत्रम्**, a neuter noun. For two, the number is **द्वे** (*dve*) and the noun takes the dual, **यानपत्रे** (*yānapatre*):

> **कृपया द्वे यानपत्रे ददातु।**
>
> *kṛpayā dve yānapatre dadātu.* — "Two tickets, please."

> **मूल्यं कियत्?**
>
> *mūlyaṃ kiyat?* — "How much is it?"

## You'll want to know: when does it leave?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C18-ASK-01, SA-LEX-C123-THINGS123-05, SA-LEX-C257-TRV257-02, SA-LEX-C45-DAYTIME-03, SA-LEX-C45-DAYTIME-01] -->

The future asks when: **गमिष्यति** (*gamiṣyati*), "will go", is "will leave".

> **धूमयानं कदा गमिष्यति?**
>
> *dhūmayānaṃ kadā gamiṣyati?* — "When will the train leave?"

> **सायं गमिष्यति।**
>
> *sāyaṃ gamiṣyati.* — "It will leave in the evening."

> **लोकयानं कुत्र तिष्ठति?**
>
> *lokayānaṃ kutra tiṣṭhati?* — "Where does the bus stop?"
