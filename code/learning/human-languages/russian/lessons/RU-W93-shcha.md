# щ (shcha) — the shch inside площадь

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-C81-YO-01, RU-SCRIPT-C93-E-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ё and э once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **площадь** (*plóshchad*), "a square".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: щ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SCRIPT-C93-SHCHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**щ** — *shch*.

It is inside **площадь** (*plóshchad*), "a square". It is **ш** with a small
tail at the bottom right, and it sounds like a long, soft *sh*.

## Writing: щ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SCRIPT-C93-SHCHA-01] -->

Put your pen on щ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
