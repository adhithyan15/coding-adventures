## You'll want to know: पक्ष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C347-PAKSH347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्ष** — *pakṣ* — "a side (in a dispute)".

> बहस में मैं मीरा के पक्ष में हूँ।
>
> *bahas meṁ maiṁ Mīrā ke pakṣ meṁ hūṁ.*
>
> In the argument I'm on Mira's side.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्ष** (*pakṣ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pakṣa*, "a wing, a side" (Monier-Williams).
