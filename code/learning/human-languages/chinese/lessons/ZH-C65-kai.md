## You'll want to know: kāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-04]; assesses=[] -->

*kāi* — "to open; to switch on".

> *shāngdiàn jiǔ diǎn kāi* — The shop opens at nine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *khoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to open, to undo'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
