# विश्वासघात (viśvāsghāt) — a betrayal of trust

## You'll want to know: विश्वासघात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C467-KAPAT467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वासघात** — *viśvāsghāt* — "a betrayal of trust".

> दोस्त का विश्वासघात अरुण कभी नहीं भूलेगा।
>
> *dost kā viśvāsghāt Aruṇ kabhī nahīṃ bhūlegā.*
>
> Arun will never forget his friend's betrayal.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वासघात** (*viśvāsghāt*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *viśvāsa-ghāta*, "destruction of confidence, violation of trust" (Monier-Williams): *viśvās*, "trust", and *ghāt*, "a blow".
