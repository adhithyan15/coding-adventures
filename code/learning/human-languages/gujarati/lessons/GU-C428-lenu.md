## You'll want to know: લેણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C428-DEBT428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લેણું** — *leṇũ* — "money owed to you; a sum due".

> તેની પાસે મારું સો રૂપિયાનું લેણું છે.
>
> *tenī pāse mārũ so rūpiyānũ leṇũ che.*
>
> He owes me a hundred rupees.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લેણું** (*leṇũ*) is built on લેવું, to take, a tadbhava of Sanskrit *labhatē*, "catches, takes" (CDIAL 10948): what you still have to take back from someone.
