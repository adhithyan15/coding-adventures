Gujarati, chapter 333: Fairies and Magic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પરી (parī) — a fairy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a goddess, then the Gujarati for a boon.

You'll want to know: પરી.
પરી — parī — "a fairy".
વાર્તામાં એક સુંદર પરી હતી. vārtāmā̃ ek sundar parī hatī. There was a beautiful fairy in the story.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પરી (parī) is usually traced to Persian parī, "a fairy", the winged spirit of Persian tales. It is feminine: સુંદર પરી.

બધાએ કહ્યું કે આ ચમત્કાર છે. badhāe kahyũ ke ā camatkār che. Everyone said it was a miracle.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચમત્કાર (camatkār) is a tatsama, Sanskrit camatkāra, "astonishment, something wonderful" (Monier-Williams): what makes people cry out in wonder.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ગાયબ.
ગાયબ — gāyab — "vanished".
અચાનક પરી ગાયબ થઈ ગઈ. acānak parī gāyab thaī gaī. Suddenly the fairy vanished.

The word, taken apart.
ગાયબ (gāyab) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ġāʾib, "absent, hidden". A thing or a person ગાયબ થાય છે, disappears all at once.

Lesson 5 of 5.
દેખાવું (dekhāvũ) — to appear; to be seen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a miracle, then the Gujarati for vanished.

You'll want to know: દેખાવું.
દેખાવું — dekhāvũ — "to appear; to be seen".
રાત્રે બારીમાં એક પરી દેખાઈ. rātre bārīmā̃ ek parī dekhāī. At night a fairy appeared at the window.
Past (she …): તે પરી દેખાઈ, te parī dekhāī.

The word, taken apart.
દેખાવું (dekhāvũ) belongs with dekhvũ, to see, which CDIAL lists under dṛkṣati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6507): to be seen. What appears is the subject: પરી (parī) દેખાઈ, a fairy appeared.
