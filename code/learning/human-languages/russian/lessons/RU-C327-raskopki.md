## You'll want to know: раскопки
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C327-KLAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**раскопки** (*raskópki*) — "a dig, an excavation".

> Летом студенты ездили на раскопки.
>
> *Létom studénty yézdili na raskópki.*
>
> In the summer the students went on a dig.

Plural; genitive *раскопок*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*раскопки* is built on *раскопать* "to dig up", from *рас-* and *копать* "to dig", inherited from Proto-Slavic *kopati* (EtymDB).
