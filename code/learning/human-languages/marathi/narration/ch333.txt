Marathi, chapter 333: Campaigns.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मोहीम (mohīm) — an expedition; a campaign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sacrifice, then the Marathi for to surrender.

You'll want to know: मोहीम.
मोहीम — mohīm — "an expedition; a campaign".
गिर्यारोहकांची मोहीम यशस्वी झाली. giryārohkāñcī mohīm yaśasvī jhālī. The climbers' expedition was a success.
Feminine.
मोहीम (mohīm) आखणे, to plan a campaign.

The word, taken apart.
मोहीम (mohīm) is a loan from Persian muhimm, 'an important undertaking', usually traced to Arabic muhimm, 'important'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
माघार घेणे (māghār gheṇe) — to retreat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an expedition, then the Marathi for a military campaign.

You'll want to know: माघार घेणे.
माघार घेणे — māghār gheṇe — "to retreat".
पावसामुळे शत्रूने माघार घेतली. pāvsāmuḷe śatrūne māghār ghetlī. Because of the rain the enemy retreated.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने माघार घेतली, tyāne māghār ghetlī.
माघार is feminine, so the past is माघार घेतली.

The word, taken apart.
माघार घेणे (māghār gheṇe) joins māghār, 'a falling back', to gheṇe, 'to take'; the origin of māghār is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मुक्त करणे (mukta karṇe) — to set free.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a military campaign, then the Marathi for to retreat.

You'll want to know: मुक्त करणे.
मुक्त करणे — mukta karṇe — "to set free".
राजाने सगळ्या कैद्यांना मुक्त केलं. rājāne sagaḷyā kaidyānnā mukta kelaṁ. The king set all the prisoners free.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मुक्त केलं, tyāne mukta kelaṁ.
The one freed takes -ला; मुक्त होणे, to become free.

The word, taken apart.
मुक्त करणे (mukta karṇe) joins mukta, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit mukta, 'loosed, set free' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
हाकलणे (hākalṇe) — to drive out; to chase away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to retreat, then the Marathi for to set free.

You'll want to know: हाकलणे.
हाकलणे — hākalṇe — "to drive out; to chase away".
लोकांनी शेतातून माकडांना हाकललं. lokānnī śetātūn mākḍānnā hākallaṁ. People drove the monkeys out of the field.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हाकललं, tyāne hākallaṁ.
The one driven out takes -ला; the place left takes -तून.

The word, taken apart.
हाकलणे (hākalṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi hākalṇe, 'to drive on or away', under Sanskrit hakkayati, 'calls to' (CDIAL 13939).
