## You'll want to know: கதறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C458-AZHUKAI458-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கதறு** — *kataṟu* — "to wail; to cry out".

> அந்தச் செய்தியைக் கேட்டு அவள் கதறி அழுதாள்.
>
> *antac ceytiyaik kēṭṭu avaḷ kataṟi aḻutāḷ.*
>
> Hearing the news, she wailed and wept.

Past (I …): கதறினேன், *kataṟiṉēṉ*.

Often கதறி அழு, to cry aloud.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கதறு** (*kataṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1189); Malayalam has the same verb for roaring.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
