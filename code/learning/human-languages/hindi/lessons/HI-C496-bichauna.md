## You'll want to know: बिछौना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C496-NEEND496-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बिछौना** — *bichaunā* — "a bedroll; bedding laid out".

> दादी ने मेहमानों के लिए फ़र्श पर बिछौना बिछाया।
>
> *dādī ne mehmānoṁ ke liye farś par bichaunā bichāyā.*
>
> Grandmother laid out bedding on the floor for the guests.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिछौना** (*bichaunā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bichaunā*, "bedding", under *\*vicchādana*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11691): what is spread out to sleep on.
