## You'll want to know: wàiguó
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C81-CITY81-04]; assesses=[] -->

*wàiguó* — "foreign country; abroad".

> *tā shì wàiguó rén* — He's a foreigner.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wàiguó* is built from wài 'outside' and 国 'country', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwajH* and *kwok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
