## You'll want to know: साक्षरता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C360-SAMANTA360-05]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्षरता** — *sākṣartā* — "literacy".

> गावातील साक्षरता गेल्या दहा वर्षांत वाढली.
>
> *gāvātīl sākṣartā gelyā dahā varṣānt vāḍhlī.*
>
> Literacy in the village grew in the last ten years.

Feminine.

साक्षर, literate, + -ता; the opposite is निरक्षरता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्षरता** (*sākṣartā*) is a learned noun built from Sanskrit *sākṣara*, "containing letters, lettered" (CDIAL 13320), with *-tā*, "-ness".
