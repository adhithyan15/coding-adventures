## You'll want to know: पुरोहितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C321-ABHISHEKA321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरोहितः** — *purohitaḥ* — "a family priest".

> पुरोहितः अभिषेकं करोति।
>
> *purohitaḥ abhiṣekaṃ karoti.*
>
> The priest performs the coronation.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरोहितः** (*purohitaḥ*) is built from *puras*, "in front", and *hita*, "placed", from the root √dhā; MW gives *purohita* as placed in front, appointed, and especially a family priest (MW).
