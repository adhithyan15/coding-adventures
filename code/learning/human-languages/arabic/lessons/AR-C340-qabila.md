## You'll want to know: قبيلة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C340-HUKM340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قبيلة** — *qabīla* — "a tribe".

> عاشت القبيلة في الصحراء.
>
> *ʿāshat al-qabīla fī aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ.*
>
> The tribe lived in the desert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبيلة** (*qabīla*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-l* (Lane), "to face, to be in front". Lane gives *qabīla* as a company of people from one father.
