# ઋણી (ṛṇī) — indebted; grateful

## You'll want to know: ઋણી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C307-RUNI307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઋણી** — *ṛṇī* — "indebted; grateful".

> છોકરીની માતાએ અરુણને કહ્યું, હું તમારી ઋણી છું.
>
> *chokrīnī mātāe Aruṇne kahyũ, hũ tamārī ṛṇī chũ.*
>
> The girl's mother told Arun that she was in his debt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઋણી** (*ṛṇī*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ṛṇin*, "one who is in debt" (Monier-Williams), built on *ṛṇa*, a debt. It agrees with the speaker: a man says **હું તમારો ઋણી છું**, a woman **તમારી ઋણી**.
