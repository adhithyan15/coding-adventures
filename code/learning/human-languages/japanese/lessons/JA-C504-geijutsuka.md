## You'll want to know: げいじゅつか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C504-ART504-04]; assesses=[] -->

**げいじゅつか** — *geijutsuka* — "an artist".

> **かのじょは げいじゅつかです。** — *kanojo wa geijutsuka desu* — "She is an artist."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**げいじゅつか** (*geijutsuka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gei*, "art" (Middle Chinese *ngjiejH*), *jutsu*, "technique" (*zywit*) and *ka*, "expert" (*kae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
