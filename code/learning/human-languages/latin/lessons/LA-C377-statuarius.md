## You'll want to know: statuārius, statuāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C377-FULLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**statuārius, statuāriī** — "a sculptor of statues".

> Statuārius ex marmore deam fēcit.
>
> *The sculptor made a goddess out of marble.*

*statuārius, statuāriī* m., built on *statua*, a statue. English *statuary*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*statuārius* is built on *statua* "a statue" (Lewis & Short), which comes from *statuō* "to set up". English *statuary* comes from it.
