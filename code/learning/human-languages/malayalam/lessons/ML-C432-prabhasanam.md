## You'll want to know: പ്രഭാഷണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C432-GAVESANAM432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രഭാഷണം** — *prabhāṣaṇaṁ* — "a lecture; a speech".

> അദ്ദേഹത്തിന്റെ പ്രഭാഷണം ഒരു മണിക്കൂർ നീണ്ടു.
>
> *addēhattinṟe prabhāṣaṇaṁ oru maṇikkūr nīṇṭu.*
>
> His lecture lasted an hour.

The speaker is പ്രഭാഷകൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രഭാഷണം** (*prabhāṣaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prabhāṣaṇa*, "explanation" (Monier-Williams), from *pra-bhāṣ*, "to speak out".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
