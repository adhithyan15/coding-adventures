## You'll want to know: गाळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C181-BILOVO181-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गाळणो** — *gāḷṇo* — "to strain".

> मां दूध गाळै है।
>
> *mā̃ dūdh gāḷai hai.*
>
> Mother strains the milk.

Past (I …): म्हैं पाणी गाळ्यो, *mhaĩ pāṇī gāḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गाळणो** (*gāḷṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gālayati*, "filters, strains"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *gāḷvũ* and Hindi *gārnā* (CDIAL 4144). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
