## You'll want to know: argumenter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C345-PARTISAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**argumenter** — "to argue a case, to give reasons".

> Elle argumente avec calme.
>
> *She argues her case calmly.*

Not quarrelling: *argumenter* is to back a view with reasons; to quarrel is *se disputer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*argumenter* is a learned borrowing from Latin *argūmentārī* "to bring forward proof" (Lewis & Short), from *argūmentum* "proof", built on *arguere* "to make clear".
