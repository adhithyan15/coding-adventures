## You'll want to know: elegir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C492-LOGRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**elegir** — "to choose".

> Puedes elegir entre dos menús.
>
> *You can choose between two menus.*

The e breaks into i and the g becomes j: *elijo*.
