## You'll want to know: विजेता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C524-SPARDHA524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विजेता** — *vijetā* — "a victor".

> एतस्यां स्पर्धायाम् अरुणः विजेता अस्ति।
>
> *etasyāṃ spardhāyām aruṇaḥ vijetā asti.*
>
> Arun is the winner of this contest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विजेता** (*vijetā*) is built on *vi* and the root √ji, "to conquer", which also gives *jayati*, with the agent ending *-tṛ*; MW gives *vijetṛ* as a vanquisher, a conqueror (MW).
