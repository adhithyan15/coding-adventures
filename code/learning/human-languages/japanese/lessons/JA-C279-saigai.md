## You'll want to know: さいがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C279-KAZAN279-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さいがい** — *saigai* — "a disaster; a calamity".

> **さいがいの ばあいは、がっこうに ひなんします。** — *saigai no baai wa, gakkō ni hinan shimasu* — "In a disaster, we take shelter at the school."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さいがい** (*saigai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sai*, "disaster" (Middle Chinese *tsoj*) and *gai*, "harm" (*hajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
