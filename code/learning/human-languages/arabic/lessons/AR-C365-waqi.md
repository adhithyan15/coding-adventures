# واقع (wāqiʿ) — reality

## You'll want to know: واقع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C365-HAQIQA365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**واقع** — *wāqiʿ* — "reality".

> الواقع مختلف عن الأفلام.
>
> *al-wāqiʿ mukhtalif ʿan al-aflām.*
>
> Reality is different from the films.

في الواقع, *fī al-wāqiʿ*, in reality, actually.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واقع** (*wāqiʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-q-ʿ*, "to fall, to happen" (Lane). *Wāqiʿ* is the active participle, what has fallen, what happens: reality.
