# ঘটা (ghôṭā) — to happen

## You'll want to know: ঘটা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C264-GHOTA-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-ANCHOR-PARA, BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

**ঘটা** — *ghôṭā* — "to happen".

> **কাল রাতে পাড়ায় কী ঘটল?**
>
> *kāl rāte pāṛāy kī ghôṭlô?* — "What happened in the neighbourhood last night?"

**কী হল?** is the everyday question. **ঘটা** is for something that takes place, an event, and it suits a story. Its past is regular: **ঘটল** (*ghôṭlô*), "it happened".

> **এটা কখন ঘটল?**
>
> *eṭā kôkhôn ghôṭlô?* — "When did this happen?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘটা** (*ghôṭā*) is usually traced to the Sanskrit verb *ghaṭate*, which CDIAL (4407) glosses "is busy with" and, in a later text, "happens". CDIAL lists no Bengali form under that entry, so the line from one to the other is the usual tracing, not a recorded row.
