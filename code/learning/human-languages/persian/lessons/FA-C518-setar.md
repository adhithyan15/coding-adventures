## You'll want to know: سه تار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C518-PLAYING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سه تار** (*setâr*) — "a setar (a three-stringed lute)".

> او شبها برای خودش سه تار میزند.
>
> *u shabhâ barâ-ye khodash setâr mizanad.*
>
> At night she plays the setar for herself.

*se* 'three' + *târ* 'string'. The Indian sitar is usually traced to it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*setâr* is built on two native Persian words, *se*, 'three', and *târ*, 'string'. Hindi and Urdu *sitār* are usually traced to it.
