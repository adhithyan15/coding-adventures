## You'll want to know: انحصار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C374-SAHARA374-04]; assesses=[] -->

**انحصار** — *inhisār* — "reliance; dependence".

> اگر انحصار صرف بارش پر ہے تو مشکل ہو گی۔
>
> *agar inhisār sirf bārish par hai to mushkil ho gī.*
>
> If everything depends on the rain alone, things will be hard.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انحصار** (*inhisār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *inḥiṣār*, "being confined, being limited", of the same family as **منحصر**, "depending".
