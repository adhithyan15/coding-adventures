# This morning: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-IA-218, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I got up early*, then *I didn't go to school*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-IA-218, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I got up early in the morning*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *Aman didn't come yesterday*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਇਆ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਉਹ ਜਲਦੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਉੱਠੀ।**] Give its meaning: she didn't get up early.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਮੈਂ ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠਿਆ।** or its woman's form]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-IA-218, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Tell your morning in two lines: when you got up, and where you
didn't go.
