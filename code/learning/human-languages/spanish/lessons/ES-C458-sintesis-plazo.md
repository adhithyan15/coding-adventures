# (la convocatoria) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-01, ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el plazo** and **admitir**. The notice below puts one
against the other in a single line.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-01, ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-02, ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-03] -->

A notice on a noticeboard. Read it, then answer.

> **CURSOS DE NATACIÓN — INSCRIPCIÓN**

> **Plazo**: del 2 al 20 de septiembre.

> **Entregue** la ficha en el mostrador o por correo.

> Fuera de plazo no se **admiten** fichas, aunque queden plazas.

> Listas de admitidos: el día 25.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: the two ways of handing the form in]
- [YOU SAY: what happens if you are a day late]
- [YOU SAY: when you find out]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-03, ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-01, ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-02] -->

**Aunque queden plazas** is the hard line, and it is where the grammar earns
its keep: *aunque* with the subjunctive — *queden*, not *quedan* — says the
office is not conceding that there are places. Had it written *aunque quedan
plazas* it would have been admitting there are some and refusing anyway, which
reads as spite rather than procedure.

So the subjunctive is doing something worth having: it lets a notice state a
rule without commenting on the facts.

Then **no se admiten** again, with nobody named. Compare the three verbs in
their natural order: *usted entrega*, *la oficina admite*, and you would
*aceptar* only what is offered to you. The notice never says who decides,
because in official Spanish the decision is a thing that happens rather than a
person who acts.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-01, ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-02, ES-LEX-C458-PLAZO-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four lines for a
sign-up notice: give the window of dates, say where to hand the form in, say
late forms are not accepted, and say when the list goes up.

Then check yourself: did you use *entregar* for the handing in and *admitir*
for the office, and did *fuera de plazo* go without an article?
