## You'll want to know: ಒಗೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C545-BATTE545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಗೆ** — *oge* — "to wash clothes".

> ಭಾನುವಾರ ಅಮ್ಮ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಒಗೆಯುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *bhānuvāra amma ellā baṭṭe ogeyuttāre.*
>
> On Sunday mother washes all the clothes.

Past (I …): ಒಗೆದೆ, *ogede*.

For clothes; dishes and hands take ತೊಳೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಗೆ** (*oge*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 933 lists Kannada *oge*, "to wash".
