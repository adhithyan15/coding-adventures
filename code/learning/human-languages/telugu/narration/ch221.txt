Telugu, chapter 221: Brown, Silver, Pale, Round, Sharp.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
గోధుమ రంగు (gōdhuma raṅgu) — brown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for thin, then the Telugu for raw.

You'll want to know: గోధుమ రంగు.
గోధుమ రంగు — gōdhuma raṅgu — "brown".

The word, taken apart.
గోధుమ రంగు (gōdhuma raṅgu) pairs Sanskrit gōdhūma, "wheat" (CDIAL 4287), with raṅgu, "colour", from Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour" (CDIAL 10560): "wheat colour".

You'll want to know: లేత.
లేత — lēta — "pale (of a colour); tender, young".

The word, taken apart.
లేత (lēta) is native Dravidian. DEDR 513 lists a nasalised lēta, "young, tender", with Tamil iḷa and Kannada eḷa, "young, tender". DEDR compares Prakrit līva, "child".
