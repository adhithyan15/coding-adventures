## You'll want to know: ચપળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C462-CANCAL462-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચપળ** — *capaḷ* — "nimble; quick".

> ખિસકોલી ઘણી ચપળ હોય છે.
>
> *khiskolī ghaṇī capaḷ hoy che.*
>
> Squirrels are very nimble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચપળ** (*capaḷ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *capala*, "moving, trembling, unsteady", as Monier-Williams records it.
