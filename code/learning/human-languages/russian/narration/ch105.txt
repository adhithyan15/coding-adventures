Russian, chapter 105: Flour, Chocolate, Biscuits, Sandwich, Sauce.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
мука (muká) — flour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a melon, then the Russian for garlic.

You'll want to know: мука.
мука (muká) — "flour".

The word, taken apart.
мука (muká), "flour", stressed on the last syllable from Proto-Slavic mǫka — inherited, from the root of мягкий, "soft". Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian minkyti, "to knead", and Greek mássō, "I knead". мука (muká), stressed on the first syllable, "torment", is a related word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
шоколад (shokolád) — chocolate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for garlic, then the Russian for flour.

You'll want to know: шоколад.
шоколад (shokolád) — "chocolate".

The word, taken apart.
шоколад (shokolád) — from Peter the Great's time. The ш- form came through German Schokolade, the old ч- form from Italian cioccolata; both go back to Spanish chocolate, from Nahuatl, the language of the Aztecs (Vasmer).

Lesson 3 of 5.
печенье (pechénye) — biscuits.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for flour, then the Russian for chocolate.

You'll want to know: печенье.
печенье (pechénye) — "biscuits".

The word, taken apart.
печенье (pechénye) is a noun from печь, "to bake": "something baked". печь from Proto-Slavic pekti, which Vasmer relates to Sanskrit paktí-, "cooking", and Greek pépsis, "cooking".
