## You'll want to know: собеседование
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C358-VAKANSIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**собеседование** (*sobesédovaniye*) — "a job interview".

> Завтра у меня собеседование в банке.
>
> *Závtra u menyá sobesédovaniye v bánke.*
>
> Tomorrow I have a job interview at the bank.

Neuter. *Пройти собеседование* — to get through an interview.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**собеседование** is built on *собеседник* "the person you talk with", from *со-* "together" and *беседа* "a conversation". The origin of *беседа* is not settled.
