## You'll want to know: مهریه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C335-ARUSI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مهریه** (*mehriye*) — "a mahr, the bride's marriage gift".

> مهریه او صد سکه طلا بود.
>
> *Mehriye-ye u sad sekke-ye talâ bud.*
>
> Her mahr was a hundred gold coins.

Promised by the groom in the marriage contract and owed to the wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mehriye* is built on *mehr*, the marriage gift, with the ending *-iye*. *mehr* is usually traced to Arabic *mahr*, "marriage portion" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), though EtymDB also links the Persian word to an Old Persian form, so its origin is not fully settled.
