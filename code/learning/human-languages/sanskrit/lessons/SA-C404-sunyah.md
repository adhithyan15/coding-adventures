## You'll want to know: शून्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C404-PRANTARA404-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शून्यः** — *śūnyaḥ* — "deserted; empty".

> रात्रौ मार्गः शून्यः भवति।
>
> *rātrau mārgaḥ śūnyaḥ bhavati.*
>
> At night the road is deserted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शून्यः** (*śūnyaḥ*) is the word MW gives for empty, void, deserted; MW gives no sure derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
