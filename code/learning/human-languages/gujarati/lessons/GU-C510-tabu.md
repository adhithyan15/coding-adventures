# તાંબું (tā̃bũ) — copper

## You'll want to know: તાંબું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C510-SONI510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**તાંબું** — *tā̃bũ* — "copper".

> દાદીનું જૂનું વાસણ તાંબાનું છે.
>
> *dādīnũ jūnũ vāsaṇ tā̃bānũ che.*
>
> Grandmother's old pot is made of copper.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તાંબું** (*tā̃bũ*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *tāmrá*, "coppery red; copper", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 5779).
