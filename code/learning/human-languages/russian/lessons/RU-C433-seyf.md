## You'll want to know: сейф
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C433-SHUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**сейф** (*seyf*) — "a safe".

> Паспорт и деньги лежат в сейфе.
>
> *Pásport i déngi lezhát v séyfe.*
>
> The passport and the money are in the safe.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сейф* was borrowed from English *safe* (EtymDB), a strong box.
