## You'll want to know: опасаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C373-OSTOROZHNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**опасаться** (*opasátsya*) — "to fear, to be wary of".

> Врачи опасаются новой эпидемии.
>
> *Vrachí opasáyutsya nóvoy epidémii.*
>
> Doctors fear a new epidemic.

Imperfective only. It takes the genitive: *опасаться дождя*. More formal than *бояться*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**опасаться** is usually traced to *о-* and *пасти* "to guard, to watch over", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*pasti* (EtymDB): to be wary is to keep watch. *Опасный* "dangerous" is built on it.
