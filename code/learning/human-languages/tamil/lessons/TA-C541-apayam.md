## You'll want to know: அபாயம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C541-KAPPEEDU541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**அபாயம்** — *apāyam* — "danger; a hazard".

> பாலம் உடைந்தால் அபாயம் ஏற்படும்.
>
> *pālam uṭaintāl apāyam ēṟpaṭum.*
>
> If the bridge breaks, there will be danger.

A danger sign says அபாயம். The adjective ஆபத்தான (*āpattāṉa*) belongs to a different word, ஆபத்து.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அபாயம்** (*apāyam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *apāya*, "departure, death, misfortune" (CDIAL 481), with the Tamil ending -அம்.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
