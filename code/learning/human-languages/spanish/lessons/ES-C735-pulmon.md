## You'll want to know: el pulmón
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C735-HUESO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el pulmón** — "the lung".

> Fumar es malo para los pulmones.
>
> *Smoking is bad for the lungs.*

Plural *los pulmones*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pulmón* comes from Latin *pulmō*, *pulmōnem* "lung" (EtymDB).
