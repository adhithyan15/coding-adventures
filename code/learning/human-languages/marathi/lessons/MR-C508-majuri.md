## You'll want to know: मजुरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C508-PAGAR508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मजुरी** — *majurī* — "daily wages".

> कामगारांना संध्याकाळी त्यांची मजुरी मिळाली.
>
> *kāmgārānnā sandhyākāḷī tyāñcī majurī miḷālī.*
>
> The workers got their wages in the evening.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मजुरी** (*majurī*) is a loan from Persian *muzdūrī*, "work for hire", built on *muzd*, "wage".
