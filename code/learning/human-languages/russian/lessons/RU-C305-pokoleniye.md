## You'll want to know: поколение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C305-VOSPITYVAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**поколение** (*pokoléniye*) — "a generation".

> Три поколения семьи жили в одном доме.
>
> *Tri pokoléniya semí zhíli v odnóm dóme.*
>
> Three generations of the family lived in one house.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поколение* is built on *колено* "knee", which in older Russian also meant "a branch of a family, a line of descent"; EtymDB lists no parent.
