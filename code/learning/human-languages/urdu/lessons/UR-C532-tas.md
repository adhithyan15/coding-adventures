## You'll want to know: ٹاس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C532-QISMAT532-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹاس** — *ṭās* — "a toss (of a coin)".

> اگر ہم نے ٹاس جیتا تو پہلے ہم کھیلیں گے۔
>
> *agar ham ne ṭās jītā to pehle ham kheleṅ ge.*
>
> If we win the toss, we will play first.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹاس** (*ṭās*) is borrowed from English *toss*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
