## You'll want to know: మెజారిటీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C400-ENNIKA400-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మెజారిటీ** — *mejāriṭī* — "a majority".

> ఆమె పెద్ద మెజారిటీతో గెలిచింది.
>
> *āme pedda mejāriṭītō gelicindi.*
>
> She won by a big majority.

From English; in plain Telugu, ఎక్కువ మంది, most people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మెజారిటీ** (*mejāriṭī*) is a loan from English *majority*.
