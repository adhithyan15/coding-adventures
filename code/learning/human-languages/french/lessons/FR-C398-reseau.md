## You'll want to know: le réseau
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C398-RESEAU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le réseau** — "the network".

> Il n'y a pas de réseau en montagne.
>
> *There is no signal in the mountains.*

*Les réseaux sociaux* — social media.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réseau* is usually traced to Old French *reseuil*, a small form of *rets* "net" (EtymDB has *rets* from Old French *reiz*), which goes back to Latin *rēte* "a net" (Lewis & Short).
