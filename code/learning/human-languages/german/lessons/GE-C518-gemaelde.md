## You'll want to know: das Gemälde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C518-MALEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Gemälde** — "painting".

> Im Museum hängt ein berühmtes Gemälde aus dem Mittelalter.
>
> *A famous painting from the Middle Ages hangs in the museum.*

Plural: *die Gemälde*. Built on *malen* — to paint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gemälde* is built on *malen* "to paint", from Old High German *mālōn* "to mark, to paint"; EtymDB lists the noun without a source.
