## Script — two verticals, two short strokes, a long bar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YE-CAREER-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 业

**Five strokes, in three steps.**

**Two verticals first**, side by side in the middle: the left one, then the right one, which starts a little higher.

**Two short strokes outside them**: a dot on the left that falls to the right, then a short stroke on the right that falls to the left.

**Last, a long bar** along the foot, wider than everything above it.

2 + 2 + 1 = five.
