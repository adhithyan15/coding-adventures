## You'll want to know: पहिया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C296-TAKKAR296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पहिया** — *pahiyā* — "a wheel".

> स्कूटर का पिछला पहिया टूट गया।
>
> *skūṭar kā pichlā pahiyā ṭūṭ gayā.*
>
> The scooter's back wheel broke.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पहिया** (*pahiyā*) goes back to Sanskrit *pradhi*, "the rim of a wheel" (CDIAL 8672), where CDIAL records Hindi *pahiyā*. Its plural is **पहिए**.
