## You'll want to know: la tutoría
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C596-ESTUDIOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la tutoría** — "the tutorial, the office hours".

> Tengo tutoría con la profesora el jueves.
>
> *I have a tutorial with the teacher on Thursday.*

The time when a teacher sees students one by one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tutoría* is built on *tutor*, a learned borrowing from Latin *tutor* 'guardian, protector' (EtymDB), from *tueri* 'to watch over'.
