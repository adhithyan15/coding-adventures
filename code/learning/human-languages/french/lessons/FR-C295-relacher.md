## You'll want to know: relâcher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C295-AVEUX-05]; assesses=[] -->

**relâcher** — "to release, to let go; to loosen".

> La police l'a relâché le lendemain.
>
> *The police let him go the next day.*

*Relâcher ses efforts* — to slacken off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*relâcher* is inherited through Old French *relaschier* (EtymDB) from Latin *relaxāre* "to loosen" (Lewis & Short).
