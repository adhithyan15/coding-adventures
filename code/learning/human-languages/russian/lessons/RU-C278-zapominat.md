## You'll want to know: запоминать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C278-VPERVYYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**запоминать** (*zapominát*) — "to memorize, to commit to memory".

> Я легко запоминаю имена.
>
> *Ya legkó zapomináyu imená.*
>
> I remember names easily.

Imperfective; perfective *запомнить* (*zapómnit*): *я запомнил дорогу* — I memorized the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*запоминать* is built on *за-* and *помнить* "to remember", from the Slavic root of *память* "memory": to fix in memory. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
