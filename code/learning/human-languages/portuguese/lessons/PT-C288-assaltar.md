## You'll want to know: assaltar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C288-ASSALTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**assaltar** — "to rob, to hold up".

> Dois homens assaltaram a bomba de gasolina.
>
> *Two men held up the petrol station.*

Also to mug someone: *assaltaram-me na rua*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assaltar* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *adsaltāre* "to jump at", from Latin *saltāre* "to jump" (Lewis & Short).
