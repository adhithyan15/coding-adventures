## You'll want to know: अनुमान काढणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C345-NISHKARSH345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमान काढणे** — *anumān kāḍhṇe* — "to infer".

> काळं आभाळ पाहून त्याने पावसाचं अनुमान काढलं.
>
> *kāḷaṁ ābhāḷ pāhūn tyāne pāvsācaṁ anumān kāḍhlaṁ.*
>
> Seeing the dark sky, he inferred that it would rain.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अनुमान काढलं, *tyāne anumān kāḍhlaṁ*.

अनुमान, an inference; काढणे, to draw out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुमान काढणे** (*anumān kāḍhṇe*) joins the tatsama अनुमान, Sanskrit *anumāna*, "inference, thought" (CDIAL 328), to काढणे, "to take out".

## What you've built
