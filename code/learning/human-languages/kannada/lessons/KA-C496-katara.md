## You'll want to know: ಕಾತರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C496-ASHE496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾತರ** — *kātara* — "eagerness; anxious longing".

> ಫಲಿತಾಂಶಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳು ಕಾತರದಿಂದ ಕಾಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು.
>
> *phalitāṃśakkāgi vidyārthigaḷu kātaradinda kāyuttiddaru.*
>
> The students were waiting eagerly for the results.

ಕಾತರದಿಂದ, eagerly, impatiently.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾತರ** (*kātara*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kātara*, "confused, discouraged, cowardly" (CDIAL 3027). In Kannada the nervous edge stayed and turned into impatient longing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
