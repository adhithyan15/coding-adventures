## You'll want to know: چھاچھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C509-CHHACHH509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چھاچھ** — *chhāchh* — "buttermilk".

> گرمیوں میں چھاچھ اچھی لگتی ہے۔
>
> *garmiyoṅ meṅ chhāchh achchhī lagtī hai.*
>
> Buttermilk is lovely in the summer.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھاچھ** (*chhāchh*) is inherited from *\*chācchī*, "buttermilk", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5012).
