## You'll want to know: ज़िद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C386-SAHARA386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िद** — *zid* — "stubbornness, insistence".

> ज़िद मत करो, वरना माँ को ग़ुस्सा आएगा।
>
> *zid mat karo, varnā māṁ ko ġussā āegā.*
>
> Don't be stubborn, or your mother will get angry.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िद** (*zid*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḍidd*, "the opposite", which reached Hindi through Persian.
