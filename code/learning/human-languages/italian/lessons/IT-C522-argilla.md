## You'll want to know: l'argilla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C522-SCULTURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'argilla** — "clay".

> I bambini fanno animali con l'argilla.
>
> *The children make animals out of clay.*

*Un vaso di argilla* — a clay pot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*argilla* is a learned borrowing from Latin *argilla* "white clay" (Lewis & Short), from Greek *árgillos*.
