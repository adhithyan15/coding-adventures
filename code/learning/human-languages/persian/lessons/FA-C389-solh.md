## You'll want to know: صلح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C389-DAVTALAB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صلح** (*solh*) — "peace".

> همه مردم صلح میخواهند.
>
> *hame-ye mardom solh mikhâhand.*
>
> All people want peace.

The opposite is *jang*, war. *Solh kardan* — to make peace.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صلح** was borrowed from Arabic *ṣulḥ* "peace, reconciliation" (EtymDB).
