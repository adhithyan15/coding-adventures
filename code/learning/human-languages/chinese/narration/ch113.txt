Mandarin Chinese, chapter 113: Cooking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
qiē — to cut; to slice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to pull, then the Mandarin for stairs.

You'll want to know: qiē.
qiē — "to cut; to slice".
wǒ qiē cài — I'll chop the vegetables.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qiē is inherited from Middle Chinese tshet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to cut' and 'to carve'.

What you've built.
One more word for everyday actions.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: qiē]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: qiē, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wǒ qiē cài — I'll chop the vegetables.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for to pull, then the Mandarin for stairs, then say qiē again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does qiē mean? ("To cut; to slice".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
kǎo — to roast; to bake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to boil, then the Mandarin for to stir-fry.

You'll want to know: kǎo.
kǎo — "to roast; to bake".
wǒmen kǎo miànbāo — We're making toast.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kǎo is the everyday word for roasting and baking. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
jiān — to fry (in a little oil).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to stir-fry, then the Mandarin for to roast.

You'll want to know: jiān.
jiān — "to fry (in a little oil)".
wǒ huì jiān jīdàn — I can fry an egg.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jiān is inherited from Middle Chinese tsjen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to cook until done'. Frying in a little oil is the sense today; the step is not given there.
