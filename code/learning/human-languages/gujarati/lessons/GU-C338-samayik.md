## You'll want to know: સામયિક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C338-CHAPAV338-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સામયિક** — *sāmayik* — "a magazine".

> આ સામયિક દરેક મહિને આવે છે.
>
> *ā sāmayik darek mahine āve che.*
>
> This magazine comes out every month.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સામયિક** (*sāmayik*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sāmayika*, "seasonable, timely" (Monier-Williams); the sense of a magazine that comes out at set times is the modern language's.
