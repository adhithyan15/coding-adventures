Uncover **સાંજ** once. **સ** and the **ા** stroke opened *savār* two lessons ago; the dot above is the **ં** nasal mark from the doorway nine, and **જ** is the doorway consonant that also ends *āj*. Name the four pieces aloud in order, then
read the whole word once.
