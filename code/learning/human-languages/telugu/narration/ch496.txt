Telugu, chapter 496: Sorrow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
బాధపడు (bādhapaḍu) — to feel sad; to grieve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a nightmare, then the Telugu for to be distraught.

You'll want to know: బాధపడు.
బాధపడు — bādhapaḍu — "to feel sad; to grieve".
స్నేహితుడు వెళ్ళిపోయాక నేను చాలా బాధపడ్డాను. snēhituḍu veḷḷipōyāka nēnu cālā bādhapaḍḍānu. After my friend left, I was very sad.
Past (I …): బాధపడ్డాను, bādhapaḍḍānu.
The cause takes -కి: ఆ మాటకి బాధపడ్డాను, that remark upset me.

The word, taken apart.
బాధపడు (bādhapaḍu) joins బాధ, "pain, sorrow", a loan from Sanskrit bādhā, to the native verb paḍu, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: బాధపెట్టు.
బాధపెట్టు — bādhapeṭṭu — "to hurt; to upset".
నా మాటలు ఆమెను బాధపెట్టాయి. nā māṭalu āmenu bādhapeṭṭāyi. My words hurt her.
Past (I …): బాధపెట్టాను, bādhapeṭṭānu.
For feelings rather than the body: మనసును బాధపెట్టు (bādhapeṭṭu), to hurt someone deeply.

The word, taken apart.
బాధపెట్టు (bādhapeṭṭu) joins బాధ, "pain", a loan from Sanskrit bādhā, to the native verb peṭṭu, "to put": to cause someone pain.

Lesson 3 of 5.
కుంగిపోవు (kuṅgipōvu) — to be crushed; to sink in spirit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to feel sad, then the Telugu for to hurt.

You'll want to know: కుంగిపోవు.
కుంగిపోవు — kuṅgipōvu — "to be crushed; to sink in spirit".
ఉద్యోగం పోయాక అతను కుంగిపోయాడు. udyōgaṁ pōyāka atanu kuṅgipōyāḍu. After losing his job, he was crushed.
Past (I …): కుంగిపోయాను, kuṅgipōyānu.
Literally to sink and go: a -పోవు compound, like వెళ్ళిపోవు.

The word, taken apart.
కుంగిపోవు (kuṅgipōvu) is a native compound: DEDR 1767 lists Telugu k(r)uṅgu, "to sink, to fall down", with Kannada kuggu; pōvu, "to go" (DEDR 4572) completes it. Someone who sinks in spirit is crushed.

Lesson 4 of 5.
కుమిలిపోవు (kumilipōvu) — to grieve inwardly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hurt, then the Telugu for to be crushed.

You'll want to know: కుమిలిపోవు.
కుమిలిపోవు — kumilipōvu — "to grieve inwardly".
ఆమె ఎవరికీ చెప్పకుండా కుమిలిపోయింది. āme evarikī ceppakuṇḍā kumilipōyindi. She grieved inside without telling anyone.
Past (I …): కుమిలిపోయాను, kumilipōyānu.
Grief that smoulders without being spoken.

The word, taken apart.
కుమిలిపోవు (kumilipōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1752 lists Telugu kumulu, "to smoulder without flame, to be consumed inwardly, to grieve", and the verb ends in pōvu, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 5 of 5.
నిస్పృహ (nispṛha) — despair; dejection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be crushed, then the Telugu for to grieve inwardly.

You'll want to know: నిస్పృహ.
నిస్పృహ — nispṛha — "despair; dejection".
చాలా ప్రయత్నాల తరువాత అతనికి నిస్పృహ కలిగింది. cālā prayatnāla taruvāta ataniki nispṛha kaligindi. After many attempts, despair set in.
A formal word: నిస్పృహ (nispṛha) కలుగు, despair sets in.

The word, taken apart.
నిస్పృహ (nispṛha) is borrowed from Sanskrit niḥspṛha, "free from desire, not longing for anything" (Monier-Williams), from niḥ-, "without", and spṛhā, "longing". In Telugu, having nothing left to long for is despair.
