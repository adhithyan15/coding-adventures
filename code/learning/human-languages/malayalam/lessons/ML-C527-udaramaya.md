## You'll want to know: ഉദാരമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C527-SVABHAVAM527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉദാരമായ** — *udāramāya* — "generous".

> ഉദാരമായ മനസ്സുള്ള ആളാണ് എന്റെ അമ്മാവൻ.
>
> *udāramāya manassuḷḷa āḷāṇŭ enṟe ammāvan.*
>
> My uncle is a man with a generous heart.

The noun is ഉദാരത, generosity. -ആയ makes an adjective of a Sanskrit noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉദാരമായ** (*udāramāya*) is built from Sanskrit *udāra*, "lofty, noble" (CDIAL 1935), with the Malayalam adjective ending -ആയ. Being noble came to mean giving freely.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
