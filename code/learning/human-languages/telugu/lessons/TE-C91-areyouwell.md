# బాగున్నారా? (bāgunnārā?) — are you well?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-04, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for leave, then the Telugu for let's go.

## You'll want to know: బాగున్నారా?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C91-GREET-01]; assesses=[] -->

**బాగున్నారా?** (*bāgunnārā?*) — "are you well?".

It is the answer you already give, **బాగున్నాను**, turned into a question: the respectful
ending **-ారు** and the question mark **-ఆ** you know from **అవునా?**

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The greeting most people open with, built from your own answer.
