## You'll want to know: das Videospiel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C525-STREAMEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Videospiel** — "video game".

> Zu Weihnachten wünscht sich mein Neffe ein neues Videospiel.
>
> *My nephew wants a new video game for Christmas.*

Plural: *die Videospiele*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Videospiel* is built on *Video*, borrowed from English *video*, from Latin *videō* "I see", and *Spiel* "game", from Old High German *spil* (EtymDB).
