## You'll want to know: きこえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C308-MACHIGAI308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きこえる** — *kikoeru* — "to be heard; can hear".

> **でんわの こえが よく きこえません。** — *denwa no koe ga yoku kikoemasen* — "I can hardly hear the voice on the phone."

Polite **きこえます**, *kikoemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **こえが きこえました。**, *koe ga kikoemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きこえる** (*kikoeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
