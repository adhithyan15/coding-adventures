## You'll want to know: 又
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C315-AGAIN315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**又** — *yòu* — "yet again; it happened again".

> **你又来了** *nǐ yòu lái le* — Here you are again.

The new character, in pieces: 又 — two strokes: a bar that turns into a long sweep to the lower left, then a long stroke falling to the right across it, the fork at the foot of 报 and 发. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**又** (*yòu*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjuwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'further' and 'once more'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
