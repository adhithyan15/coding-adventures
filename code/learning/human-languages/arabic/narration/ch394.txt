Arabic, chapter 394: Learning well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أتقن (atqana) — to master.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to excel, then the Arabic for to evaluate.

You'll want to know: أتقن.
أتقن — atqana — "to master".
أتقن جدي لغتين. atqana jaddī lughatayn. My grandfather mastered two languages.
Present: يتقن, yutqin.

The word, taken apart.
أتقن (atqana) is built on the native Arabic root t-q-n, "to do a thing skilfully" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make a thing firm and exact: to master it. إتقان, mastery, is its verbal noun.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لخص (lakhkhaṣa) — to summarize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to acquire, then the Arabic for to go deeper.

You'll want to know: لخص.
لخص — lakhkhaṣa — "to summarize".
لخص المعلم القصة في جملتين. lakhkhaṣa al-muʿallim al-qiṣṣa fī jumlatayn. The teacher summed up the story in two sentences.
Present: يلخص, yulakhkhiṣ.

The word, taken apart.
لخص (lakhkhaṣa) is built on the native Arabic root l-kh-ṣ, "to make clear, to explain" (Lane). It is Form II; from making a thing clear it came to mean drawing out its gist: to summarize.
