## You'll want to know: jiǎnglǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C351-SENSE351-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎnglǐ* — "to be reasonable; to listen to reason".

> *zhège rén bù jiǎnglǐ* — This man won't listen to reason.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: jiǎng is said with a rising tone before lǐ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎnglǐ* is built from *jiǎng* and 理 'order', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewngX* and *liX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tell' and 'to discuss', and the second 'to put in order'.
