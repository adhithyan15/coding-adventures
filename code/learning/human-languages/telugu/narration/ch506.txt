Telugu, chapter 506: Bearing It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సహించు (sahiñcu) — to tolerate; to put up with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to stand by, then the Telugu for to console.

You'll want to know: సహించు.
సహించు — sahiñcu — "to tolerate; to put up with".
అతని అబద్ధాలను నేను ఇక సహించను. atani abaddhālanu nēnu ika sahiñcanu. I will not put up with his lies any more.
Past (I …): సహించాను, sahiñcānu.
Often in the negative: సహించను, I will not stand for it. The noun సహనం is patience.

The word, taken apart.
సహించు (sahiñcu) is built on Sanskrit sahatē, "bears, suffers" (CDIAL 13304), from the root sah, with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 2 of 5.
తట్టుకో (taṭṭukō) — to withstand; to cope with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to console, then the Telugu for to tolerate.

You'll want to know: తట్టుకో.
తట్టుకో — taṭṭukō — "to withstand; to cope with".
ఆ చలిని ముసలివాళ్ళు తట్టుకోలేరు. ā calini musalivāḷḷu taṭṭukōlēru. Old people cannot withstand that cold.
Past (I …): తట్టుకున్నాను, taṭṭukunnānu.
Mostly with -లే-: తట్టుకోలేను, I cannot bear it.

The word, taken apart.
తట్టుకో (taṭṭukō) is a Telugu verb ending in the reflexive -కో; the origin of its stem is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
నిగ్రహం (nigrahaṁ) — self-restraint; self-control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to tolerate, then the Telugu for to withstand.

You'll want to know: నిగ్రహం.
నిగ్రహం — nigrahaṁ — "self-restraint; self-control".
కోపం వచ్చినా ఆమె నిగ్రహంతో మాట్లాడింది. kōpaṁ vaccinā āme nigrahantō māṭlāḍindi. Though she was angry, she spoke with restraint.
Holding back anger or a craving: నిగ్రహంతో, with restraint. A formal word.

The word, taken apart.
నిగ్రహం (nigrahaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit nigraha, "restraint; a rebuke" (CDIAL 7169), from ni-grah, "to hold down": holding yourself down.

Lesson 4 of 5.
మనశ్శాంతి (manaśśānti) — peace of mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to withstand, then the Telugu for self-restraint.

You'll want to know: మనశ్శాంతి.
మనశ్శాంతి — manaśśānti — "peace of mind".
పల్లెలో ఉంటే నాకు మనశ్శాంతిగా ఉంటుంది. pallelō uṇṭē nāku manaśśāntigā uṇṭundi. In the village I have peace of mind.
మనసు, mind, + శాంతి, peace.

The word, taken apart.
మనశ్శాంతి (manaśśānti) is borrowed from Sanskrit: manas, "mind", and śānti, "rest, peace" (CDIAL 12391); the s of manas becomes ś before ś.
