Portuguese, chapter 96: Rubbish, Broom, Washing machine, Sofa, Cupboard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o lixo — rubbish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for toothpaste, then the Portuguese for a comb.

You'll want to know: o lixo.
