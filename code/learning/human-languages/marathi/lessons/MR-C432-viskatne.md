## You'll want to know: विस्कटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C432-BHOK432-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्कटणे** — *viskaṭṇe* — "to get messed up".

> धावताना तिचे केस विस्कटले.
>
> *dhāvtānā tice kes viskaṭle.*
>
> Her hair got messed up as she ran.

Past (he …): तो विस्कटला, *to viskaṭlā*.

Hair, papers and plans can all विस्कटणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्कटणे** (*viskaṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *viskaṭṇẽ*, "to disorder, derange", under *\*viṣvaskati*, "goes astray" (CDIAL 11995).
