## You'll want to know: guǒzhī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C102-DRINK102-02]; assesses=[] -->

*guǒzhī* — "fruit juice".

> *wǒ yào yì bēi guǒzhī* — A glass of juice for me, please.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guǒzhī* is built from guǒ 'fruit' and zhī 'juice', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaX* and *tsyip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the fruit of a tree' and 'liquid'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
