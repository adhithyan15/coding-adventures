# (reading) — three to open, three to rescue

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson gave you six things on a board. These are six things
you would say.

[PAUSE 1s] Nothing here is new. What is new is meeting them in writing.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SKILL-READ-NOTICES]; assesses=[RU-LEX-PRIVET, RU-LEX-SPASIBO, RU-LEX-IZVINITE-01] -->

> привет
> спасибо
> пожалуйста
> извините
> медленно
> помогите

[PAUSE 3s] Read down once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and notice the list turns over halfway. The first three open
a conversation. The last three rescue one.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-MEDLENNO-01, RU-LEX-POMOGITE-01, RU-SKILL-READ-NOTICES] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **пожалуйста** is the longest word on the list and the one you will
read most often. It is worth recognising by shape rather than by spelling it
out, because it does two jobs: *please*, and *you're welcome*.

[PAUSE 2s] **помогите** and **извините** end the same way, and both are
requests you make to somebody you have not met. That shared ending is the one
piece of grammar visible on this page.

[PAUSE 2s] Six lines, and half of them exist for the moment something goes
wrong.
