## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كرسي** (*kursī*) is listed by Lane under *k-r-s* as a throne or chair. It is usually traced to Aramaic *kursyā*, "throne"; Hebrew *kissē*, "throne", is a cousin.
