Bengali, chapter 331: What It Is For.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উদ্দেশ্য (uddeshyô) — a purpose; an aim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a reaction, then the Bengali for an inspiration.

You'll want to know: উদ্দেশ্য.
উদ্দেশ্য — uddeshyô — "a purpose; an aim".
এই মিটিংয়ের উদ্দেশ্য কী? ei miṭingyer uddeshyô kī? What is this meeting for?

The word, taken apart.
উদ্দেশ্য (uddeshyô) is Sanskrit uddeśya, the thing one points at or has in view (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the purpose behind a plan or a meeting.

Lesson 2 of 5.
উপকার (upôkār) — a benefit; a good turn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an inspiration, then the Bengali for a purpose.

You'll want to know: উপকার.
উপকার — upôkār — "a benefit; a good turn".
তোমার পরামর্শে আমার অনেক উপকার হল। tomār pôrāmôrshe āmār ônek upôkār hôlô. Your advice did me a lot of good.

The word, taken apart.
উপকার (upôkār) is Sanskrit upakāra, help or a favour done (Monier-Williams). With হওয়া the benefit happens to someone: আমার উপকার হল, it did me good.

Lesson 3 of 5.
লাভ (lābh) — a gain; a profit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a purpose, then the Bengali for a good turn.

লাভ — lābh — "a gain; a profit".
এই ব্যবসায় কি কোনো লাভ আছে? ei bæbsāy ki kono lābh āchhe? Is there any profit in this business?

The word, taken apart.
লাভ (lābh) is Sanskrit lābha, getting or gaining (Monier-Williams). It covers money made in trade and any advantage gained.

Lesson 4 of 5.
লোকসান (loksān) — a financial loss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a good turn, then the Bengali for a profit.

You'll want to know: লোকসান.
লোকসান — loksān — "a financial loss".
গত বছর দোকানটার লোকসান হল। gôtô bôchhôr dokānṭār loksān hôlô. The shop made a loss last year.

The word, taken apart.
লোকসান (loksān) is usually traced to Arabic nuqṣān, a shortfall, which would have come into Bengali through Persian, with the first n heard as l. It is the opposite of লাভ (lābh) in trade.

Lesson 5 of 5.
উপযোগী (upôjogī) — useful; serviceable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a profit, then the Bengali for a financial loss.

You'll want to know: উপযোগী.
উপযোগী — upôjogī — "useful; serviceable".
এই বইটা ছাত্রদের জন্য উপযোগী। ei bôiṭā chhātrôder jônyô upôjogī. This book is useful for students.

The word, taken apart.
উপযোগী (upôjogī) is Sanskrit upayogin, serving a use (Monier-Williams), from upayoga, use. What it is useful for takes -এর জন্য: ছাত্রদের জন্য উপযোগী.
