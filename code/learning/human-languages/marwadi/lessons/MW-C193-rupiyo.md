## You'll want to know: रुपियो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C193-MONEY193-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रुपियो** — *rupiyo* — "a rupee".

> म्हारी जेब मांय एक रुपियो है।
>
> *mhārī jeb mā̃y ek rupiyo hai.*
>
> I have one rupee in my pocket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुपियो** (*rupiyo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rūpya*, "bearing a stamp; silver"; CDIAL lists Marwari *rūpo*, "silver", under it, and sets up a second form, *\*rūpiya-*, which the coin's name probably continues (CDIAL 10805).
