## You'll want to know: مغرور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C480-PRIDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مغرور** (*maghrur*) — "arrogant; conceited".

> برادرش خیلی مغرور است و به کسی سلام نمیکند.
>
> *barâdarash kheyli maghrur ast va be kasi salâm nemikonad.*
>
> Her brother is very arrogant and doesn't greet anyone.

An adjective. *maghrur shodan* — to become full of oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maghrur* comes from Arabic *maġrūr* (EtymDB). It is usually linked with Arabic *ġurūr*, 'pride', the source of *ghorur*.
