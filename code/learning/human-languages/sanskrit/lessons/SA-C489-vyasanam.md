## You'll want to know: व्यसनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C489-VYASANA489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यसनम्** — *vyasanam* — "a vice; an addiction".

> द्यूतम् एकं व्यसनम् अस्ति।
>
> *dyūtam ekaṃ vyasanam asti.*
>
> Gambling is a vice.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यसनम्** (*vyasanam*) is built from *vi* and the root √as, "to throw": throwing oneself into a thing; MW gives "attachment or addiction to" and "evil passion, vice" (MW).
