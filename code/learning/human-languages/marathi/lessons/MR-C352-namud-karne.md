## You'll want to know: नमूद करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C352-ULLEKH352-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नमूद करणे** — *namūd karṇe* — "to state (formally)".

> अहवालात सगळे खर्च नमूद केले आहेत.
>
> *ahvālāt sagḷe kharca namūd kele āhet.*
>
> All the expenses are stated in the report.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नमूद केलं, *tyāne namūd kelaṁ*.

Formal, of writing: अर्जात नमूद करणे, to state in an application.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नमूद करणे** (*namūd karṇe*) joins नमूद, a loan usually traced to Persian *namūd*, "appearance, showing", to करणे, "to make".
