## You'll want to know: sticken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C499-HANDARBEIT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sticken** — "to embroider".

> Auf das Kissen hat sie eine Blume gestickt.
>
> *She embroidered a flower on the cushion.*

Regular: *hat gestickt*. Not to be confused with *stecken* — to put, to stick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sticken* is usually traced to Old High German *sticken* "to prick, to stitch", related to *stechen* "to prick"; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
