## You'll want to know: غيور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C492-GHIRA492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**غيور** — *ghayūr* — "jealous".

> زوجها رجل غيور.
>
> *zawjuhā rajul ghayūr.*
>
> Her husband is a jealous man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غيور** (*ghayūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-y-r*, "jealousy, a jealous care for one's own" (Lane). Ghayūr is the pattern of a lasting trait.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
