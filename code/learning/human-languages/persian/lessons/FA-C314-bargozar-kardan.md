## You'll want to know: برگزار کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C314-RUYDAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**برگزار کردن** (*bargozâr kardan*) — "to hold, to organise (an event)".

> دانشگاه هر سال یک جشنواره برگزار میکند.
>
> *Dâneshgâh har sâl yek jashnvâre bargozâr mikonad.*
>
> The university holds a festival every year.

Present *bargozâr mikonad*. The event takes *râ* when it is definite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bargozâr kardan* joins the native *bargozâr* "carried out" (*bar* + *gozâr-*) with *kardan* "to do", inherited Persian.
