# ળ — one new shape, retroflex lla

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-NNA-01] -->

Write retroflex *nna* once from memory. Keep the tongue-curl idea, but put the
old shape away: today it receives a different sound and form.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ળ** visible. It is retroflex *lla*: the tongue curls back for the *l*
sound. Begin at the upper left and circle the broad left bowl. Continue upward
through the narrow middle turn, cross the high right arch, and descend the tall
spine into its lower foot without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ળ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice broad bowl, narrow turn,
high arch, tall spine; no pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ળ** once. Compare only the broad bowl and tall spine; repair one
landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W04-lla-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write retroflex lla from memory.","answer":"ળ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ળ — the broad bowl and tall spine are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ળ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write retroflex *lla* once. Uncover **ળ**, compare, and stop
after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-LLA-01] -->
