## You'll want to know: лопатка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C481-BACK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**лопатка** (*lopátka*) — "a shoulder blade; a small spade".

> У меня болит спина под левой лопаткой.
>
> *U menyá bolít spiná pod lévoy lopátkoy.*
>
> My back hurts under my left shoulder blade.

Feminine. Also a small spade or a trowel: a diminutive of *лопата* — a spade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*лопатка* is a diminutive of *лопата* 'spade, shovel', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *lopata* (EtymDB); the flat bone was named after the flat blade.
