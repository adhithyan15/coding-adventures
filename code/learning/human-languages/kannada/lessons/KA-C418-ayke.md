# ಆಯ್ಕೆ (āyke) — a choice; a selection

## You'll want to know: ಆಯ್ಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C418-PARYAYA418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಯ್ಕೆ** — *āyke* — "a choice; a selection".

> ಯಾವ ಬಣ್ಣ ಬೇಕು ಅನ್ನುವುದು ನಿನ್ನ ಆಯ್ಕೆ.
>
> *yāva baṇṇa bēku annuvudu ninna āyke.*
>
> Which colour you want is your choice.

ಆಯ್ಕೆ ಮಾಡು, to choose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಯ್ಕೆ** (*āyke*) is native Dravidian, a noun from *āy*, "to collect, to select" (DEDR 363).
