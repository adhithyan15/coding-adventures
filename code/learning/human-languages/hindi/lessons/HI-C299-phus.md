## You'll want to know: फूस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C299-AAG299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फूस** — *phūs* — "dry straw; thatch".

> फूस की छत पल भर में जल गई।
>
> *phūs kī chat pal bhar meṁ jal gaī.*
>
> The thatched roof burned in a moment.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फूस** (*phūs*) is inherited from *\*phussa*, "defective", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9100), under which it gathers words for old dry straw, chaff and rubbish. **फूस की छत** is a thatched roof.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
