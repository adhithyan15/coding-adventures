## You'll want to know: ರಥ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C344-AYUDHA344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಥ** — *ratha* — "a chariot".

> ರಾಜ ಬಿಳಿ ಕುದುರೆಗಳ ರಥದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂದನು.
>
> *rāja biḷi kuduregaḷa rathadalli bandanu.*
>
> The king came in a chariot drawn by white horses.

Plural: ರಥಗಳು, *rathagaḷu*.

At temple fairs the god is pulled through the streets in a great wooden ರಥ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಥ** (*ratha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ratha*, "a chariot, a cart" (CDIAL 10602).
