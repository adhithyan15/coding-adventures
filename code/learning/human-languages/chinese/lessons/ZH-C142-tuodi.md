## You'll want to know: tuōdì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C142-CHORE142-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tuōdì* — "to mop the floor".

> *wǒ měitiān tuōdì* — I mop the floor every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuōdì* is built from *tuō* 'to drag' and *dì* 'ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tha* and *dijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to drag' under an older way of writing it, and notes that it is popularly written the modern way; *tha* is also the old form of the word for he, a different word.
