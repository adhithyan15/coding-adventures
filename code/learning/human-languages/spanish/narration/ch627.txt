Spanish, chapter 627: Las cuentas de casa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
derrochar — to waste, to squander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for guilty, then the Spanish for the fraud.

You'll want to know: derrochar.
derrochar — "to waste, to squander".
No derroches el dinero. Do not waste your money.
El derroche — the waste.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of derrochar is not settled; one account links it to French dérocher 'to throw down from a rock', and EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
el ingreso — the income; the deposit; admission.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the fraud, then the Spanish for to waste.

You'll want to know: el ingreso.
el ingreso — "the income; the deposit; admission".
Mis ingresos son bajos. My income is low.
In the plural it is income. El ingreso en el hospital — hospital admission.

The word, taken apart.
ingreso is a learned borrowing from Latin ingressus 'an entering', built on ingredi 'to step in', from gradi 'to step' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la deuda — the debt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to waste, then the Spanish for the income.

You'll want to know: la deuda.
la deuda — "the debt".
Tengo una deuda con el banco. I have a debt with the bank.
Estar en deuda con — to be in debt to, also in a moral sense.

The word, taken apart.
deuda is inherited from Latin debita 'things owed', through Old Spanish debda, from debere 'to owe' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
invertir — to invest; to reverse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the debt, then the Spanish for to get into debt.

You'll want to know: invertir.
invertir — "to invest; to reverse".
Quiero invertir en mi educación. I want to invest in my education.
Like sentir: invierto, invirtió.

The word, taken apart.
invertir is a learned borrowing from Latin invertere 'to turn upside down', built from in and vertere 'to turn' (EtymDB); the money sense came later.
