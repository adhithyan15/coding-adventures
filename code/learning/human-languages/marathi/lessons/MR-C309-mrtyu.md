## You'll want to know: मृत्यू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C309-MRUTYU309-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मृत्यू** — *mṛtyū* — "death".

> त्या अपघातात दोघांचा मृत्यू झाला.
>
> *tyā apghātāt doghāñcā mṛtyū jhālā.*
>
> Two people died in that accident.

Masculine.

X-चा मृत्यू होणे, X dies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मृत्यू** (*mṛtyū*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mṛtyu*, 'death' (Monier-Williams).
