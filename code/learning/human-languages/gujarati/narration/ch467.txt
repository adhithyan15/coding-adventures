Gujarati, chapter 467: Good Manners.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વર્તન (vartan) — behaviour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for grit, then the Gujarati for to wobble.

You'll want to know: વર્તન.
વર્તન — vartan — "behaviour".
મહેમાનો સાથે તમારું વર્તન સારું હતું. mahemāno sāthe tamārũ vartan sārũ hatũ. You behaved well with the guests.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
વર્તન (vartan) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vartana, which Monier-Williams gives as "abiding" and "setting in motion"; Gujarati uses it for the way a person conducts himself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to wobble, then the Gujarati for behaviour.

You'll want to know: અદબ.
અદબ — adab — "respectful manners".
બાળકોએ દાદા સાથે અદબથી બોલવું જોઈએ. bāḷkoe dādā sāthe adabthī bolvũ joīe. Children should speak to their grandfather respectfully.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અદબ (adab) is usually traced to Arabic adab, "good manners, culture", which reached Gujarati through Persian.

ભોજન વખતે નાની બહેન ઘણાં નખરાં કરે છે. bhojan vakhte nānī bahen ghaṇā̃ nakhrā̃ kare che. At mealtimes my little sister makes a lot of fuss.
