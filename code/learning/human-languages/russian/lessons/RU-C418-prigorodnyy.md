## You'll want to know: пригородный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C418-PERESADKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**пригородный** (*prígorodnyy*) — "suburban, local (of trains and buses)".

> Бабушка едет на дачу на пригородном поезде.
>
> *Bábushka yédet na dáchu na prígorodnom póyezde.*
>
> Grandma goes to her country cottage on the local train.

The stress is on the first syllable: *prígorodnyy*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пригородный* is built from *при-* 'near', *город* 'a town' and *-ный*. *Город* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *gordŭ* 'an enclosure' (EtymDB).
