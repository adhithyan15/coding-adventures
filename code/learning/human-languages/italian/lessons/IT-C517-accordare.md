## You'll want to know: accordare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C517-ORCHESTRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**accordare** — "to tune (an instrument)".

> Prima del concerto accordo la chitarra.
>
> *Before the concert I tune the guitar.*

Reflexive *accordarsi* — to agree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accordare* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *accordāre* (EtymDB), usually linked to Latin *cor, cordis* "heart"; the musical sense was helped by *corda* "string".
