## You'll want to know: угощать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C315-UGOSHCHENIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**угощать** (*ugoshchát*) — "to treat (to food or drink)".

> Хозяйка угощала гостей пирогами.
>
> *Khozyáyka ugoshchála gostéy pirogámi.*
>
> The hostess treated the guests to pies.

Imperfective; perfective *угостить* (*ugostít*), *угощу*. The guest is in the accusative, the food in the instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*угощать* is built on *у-* and *гость* "a guest", inherited from Proto-Slavic *gostĭ*, from PIE *gʰóstis* (EtymDB): to treat as a guest.
