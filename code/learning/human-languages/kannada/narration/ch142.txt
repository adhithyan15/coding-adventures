Kannada, chapter 142: Beautiful, Deep, Wide, Narrow, Wet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸುಂದರ (sundara) — beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for dirty, then the Kannada for speed; fast.

You'll want to know: ಸುಂದರ.
ಸುಂದರ — sundara — "beautiful".

The word, taken apart.
ಸುಂದರ (sundara) is Sanskrit sundara, "beautiful", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13474).

Lesson 2 of 5.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಳ (āḷa) is native Dravidian, from āḻ, "to sink, be deep", and DEDR 396 lists āḻa, "depth", itself; the entry also has Tamil āḻ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕಿರಿದು (kiridu) — narrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for deep; depth, then the Kannada for wide.

You'll want to know: ಕಿರಿದು.
ಕಿರಿದು — kiridu — "narrow".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಿರಿದು (kiridu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil ciṟu, Telugu ciṟu and Malayalam ciṟṟu (DEDR 1594). DEDR lists kiṟidu, "that which is small".
