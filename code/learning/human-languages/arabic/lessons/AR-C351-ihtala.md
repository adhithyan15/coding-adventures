# احتال (iḥtāla) — to swindle

## You'll want to know: احتال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C351-KHIDA351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**احتال** — *iḥtāla* — "to swindle".

> احتال رجل على السياح في السوق.
>
> *iḥtāla rajul ʿalā as-suyyāḥ fī as-sūq.*
>
> A man swindled the tourists in the market.

Present: يحتال, *yaḥtāl*.

It takes على. A swindler is محتال.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتال** (*iḥtāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-l* (Lane), "to change, to shift", where *ḥīla* is a shift or wile. It is Form VIII, "to use a wile".

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
