## You'll want to know: निजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C491-ZOP491-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निजणे** — *nijṇe* — "to lie down; to fall asleep".

> बाळ आईच्या कुशीत निजलं.
>
> *bāḷ āīcyā kuśīt nijlaṁ.*
>
> The baby fell asleep in its mother's arms.

Past (he …): तो निजला, *to nijlā*.

A softer, older word beside झोपणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निजणे** (*nijṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *nidrāyati*, "falls asleep"; CDIAL lists Marathi *nijṇẽ* (CDIAL 7201).
