## You'll want to know: مالیات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C452-NOBAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مالیات** (*mâliyât*) — "tax".

> هر سال مالیات خانه را میپردازیم.
>
> *Har sâl mâliyât-e khâne râ mipardâzim.*
>
> Every year we pay the tax on the house.

An Arabic-shaped plural built on *mâl* 'property'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mâliyât* is an Arabic-shaped plural, usually traced to Arabic *māliyya*, from *māl* 'property, wealth'. EtymDB does not list it.
