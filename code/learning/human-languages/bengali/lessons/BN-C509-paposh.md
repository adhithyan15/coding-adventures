## You'll want to know: পাপোশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C509-GALICHA509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পাপোশ** — *pāposh* — "a doormat".

> দরজার সামনে একটা পাপোশ আছে।
>
> *Dôrjār sāmne ekṭā pāposh āchhe.*
>
> There's a doormat in front of the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাপোশ** (*pāposh*) is usually traced to Persian *pāposh*, "a foot covering": *pā*, "foot", and *posh*, "covering".
