## You'll want to know: হাঁপানি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C474-HANPANI474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**হাঁপানি** — *hā̃pāni* — "asthma".

> শীতে দাদুর হাঁপানি বাড়ে।
>
> *shīte dādur hā̃pāni bāṛe.*
>
> Grandfather's asthma gets worse in winter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হাঁপানি** (*hā̃pāni*) is the noun of *hā̃pāno*, "to pant", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed root for panting (CDIAL 13973): the panting illness.
