Hindi, chapter 402: Bundles and Naps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गठरी (gaṭhrī) — a bundle (tied in cloth).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to step over, then the Hindi for to look out (at).

You'll want to know: गठरी.
गठरी — gaṭhrī — "a bundle (tied in cloth)".
दादी के पास कपड़ों की एक गठरी थी। dādī ke pās kapṛoṁ kī ek gaṭhrī thī. Grandmother had a bundle of clothes.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
गठरी (gaṭhrī) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit grathna, "a bunch" (CDIAL 4348), from the root of tying knots; CDIAL lists Hindi gaṭhṛī, "bundle".

Lesson 2 of 5.
अटैची (aṭaicī) — a suitcase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to look out (at), then the Hindi for a bundle.

You'll want to know: अटैची.
अटैची — aṭaicī — "a suitcase".
मेरी अटैची बहुत भारी है। merī aṭaicī bahut bhārī hai. My suitcase is very heavy.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
अटैची (aṭaicī) is borrowed from English attaché, short for attaché case; in India it names any small suitcase, and it is feminine.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सरकाना (sarkānā) — to slide (something) along; to push aside.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a bundle, then the Hindi for a suitcase.

You'll want to know: सरकाना.
सरकाना — sarkānā — "to slide (something) along; to push aside".
मैंने अपनी अटैची सीट के नीचे सरकाई। maiṁne apnī aṭaicī sīṭ ke nīce sarkāī. I slid my suitcase under the seat.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कुर्सी पीछे सरकाई, usne kursī pīche sarkāī.

The word, taken apart.
सरकाना (sarkānā) is the causative partner of सरकना, from Sanskrit sarati, "glides" (CDIAL 13250): सरकना is to slide, सरकाना to make a thing slide.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ऊँघना.
ऊँघना — ū̃ghnā — "to doze".
बस में बहुत लोग ऊँघ रहे थे। bas meṁ bahut log ū̃gh rahe the. A lot of people on the bus were dozing.
Past (he …): वह थोड़ी देर ऊँघा, vah thoṛī der ū̃ghā.

The word, taken apart.
ऊँघना (ū̃ghnā) goes back to uṅghati, "sleeps", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1632), which lists Hindi ū̃ghnā, "to be drowsy".

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुस्ताना (sustānā) — to rest a while.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to slide (something), then the Hindi for to doze.

You'll want to know: सुस्ताना.
सुस्ताना — sustānā — "to rest a while".
हम पेड़ के नीचे थोड़ी देर सुस्ताए। ham peṛ ke nīce thoṛī der sustāe. We rested for a while under the tree.
Past (he …): वह पेड़ के नीचे सुस्ताया, vah peṛ ke nīce sustāyā.

The word, taken apart.
सुस्ताना (sustānā) is built on sust, "slack, weary", usually traced to Persian sust, with the Hindi verb ending -ānā: to let your tiredness go for a while.
