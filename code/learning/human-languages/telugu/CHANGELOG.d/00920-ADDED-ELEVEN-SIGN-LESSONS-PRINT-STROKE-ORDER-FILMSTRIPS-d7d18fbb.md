## Added — eleven sign lessons print stroke-order filmstrips

ం ా ి ీ ు ూ ె ే ొ ో and ్ now have a cited pen path, counted from native
writers' pen traces in HP Labs India's LipiTk Telugu recognizer (counts and
shares only). Each strip draws the sign by itself, with no consonant.

- **New filmstrips (11 lessons, 45 -> 56):** TE-S02-vowel-sign-u (ు),
  TE-S04-sign-anusvara (ం), TE-S05-sign-virama (్), TE-S07-vowel-sign-aa (ా),
  TE-S08-vowel-sign-i (ి), TE-S115-vowel-sign-e (ె), TE-S119-vowel-sign-ii
  (ీ), TE-S120-vowel-sign-ee (ే, the only one with a pen lift, before its
  hook), TE-S134-vowel-sign-uu (ూ), TE-S153-vowel-sign-oo (ో) and
  TE-S154-vowel-sign-o (ొ).
- **Rewritten:** their Writing blocks no longer say the book gives no stroke
  order; they point at the numbered strip. TE-S153 and TE-S154 still ask for
  the sign written on a consonant afterwards.
- **Not yet:** TE-S136-vowel-sign-ai (ై: the recognizer stores only its lower
  part), TE-S131 (ృ) and TE-S133 (ౌ: not in the recognizer), and the digit
  lessons TE-S140 to TE-S149.
