## You'll want to know: el chubasco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C697-LLUVIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el chubasco** — "the shower (of rain)".

> Por la tarde habrá chubascos.
>
> *There will be showers in the afternoon.*

A forecast word for a short heavy shower.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chubasco* comes from Portuguese *chuvasco*, built on *chuva* "rain" (EtymDB), from Latin *pluvia*.
