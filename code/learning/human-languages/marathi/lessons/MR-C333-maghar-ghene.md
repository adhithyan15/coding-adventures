## You'll want to know: माघार घेणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C333-MOHIM333-03]; assesses=[] -->

**माघार घेणे** — *māghār gheṇe* — "to retreat".

> पावसामुळे शत्रूने माघार घेतली.
>
> *pāvsāmuḷe śatrūne māghār ghetlī.*
>
> Because of the rain the enemy retreated.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने माघार घेतली, *tyāne māghār ghetlī*.

माघार is feminine, so the past is माघार घेतली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माघार घेणे** (*māghār gheṇe*) joins *māghār*, 'a falling back', to *gheṇe*, 'to take'; the origin of *māghār* is not settled.
