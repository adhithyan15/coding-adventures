## You'll want to know: a maratona
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C513-CORRIDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a maratona** — "marathon".

> Correu a maratona em menos de quatro horas.
>
> *She ran the marathon in under four hours.*

Also figurative: *uma maratona de estudo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maratona* comes from the Greek place name *Marathṓn* (EtymDB), itself from *márathon* "fennel"; the long race recalls a messenger's run from there.
