## You'll want to know: роддом
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C495-EXPECTING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**роддом** (*roddóm*) — "a maternity hospital".

> Папа встретил маму и сестрёнку у роддома.
>
> *Pápa vstrétil mámu i sestryónku u roddóma.*
>
> Dad met Mum and my baby sister at the maternity hospital.

Masculine. Short for *родильный дом*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*роддом* is a clipped compound of *родильный* 'birth-', from *родить* 'to give birth', and *дом* 'house'; EtymDB has no entry.
