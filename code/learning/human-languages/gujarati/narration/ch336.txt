Gujarati, chapter 336: The Author.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લેખક (lekhak) — an author.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a proverb, then the Gujarati for to recite.

You'll want to know: લેખક.
લેખક — lekhak — "an author".
આ પુસ્તકનો લેખક અમારા ગામનો છે. ā pustakno lekhak amārā gāmno che. The author of this book is from our village.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
લેખક (lekhak) is a tatsama, Sanskrit lekhaka, "a writer, a scribe" (CDIAL 11102), from the same root as લખવું, to write.

Lesson 3 of 5.
રચવું (racvũ) — to compose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an author, then the Gujarati for a poet.

You'll want to know: રચવું.
રચવું — racvũ — "to compose".
કવિએ નદી પર એક ગીત રચ્યું. kavie nadī par ek gīt racyũ. The poet composed a song about the river.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગીત રચ્યું, teṇe gīt racyũ.

The word, taken apart.
રચવું (racvũ) is built on Sanskrit racana, "arranging, composing" (Monier-Williams), from the root rac, to fashion: to put words together into a song or a poem.
