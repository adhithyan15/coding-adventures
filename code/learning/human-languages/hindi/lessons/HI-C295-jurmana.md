## You'll want to know: जुर्माना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C295-ADALAT295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जुर्माना** — *jurmānā* — "a fine".

> चोर को पाँच हज़ार रुपये का जुर्माना भरना पड़ा।
>
> *cor ko pā̃c hazār rupaye kā jurmānā bharnā paṛā.*
>
> The thief had to pay a fine of five thousand rupees.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जुर्माना** (*jurmānā*) is usually traced to Persian *jurmāna*, "a fine", built on *jurm*, a crime: the money a crime costs. You **भरना** a fine, fill it up, as you pay a bill.
