Punjabi, chapter 239: Bread on the Griddle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਤਵਾ (tavā) — a griddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a ball of dough, then the Punjabi for to roll out.

You'll want to know: ਤਵਾ.
ਤਵਾ — tavā — "a griddle".
ਤਵਾ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ। tavā chullhe utte hai. The griddle is on the stove.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਵਾ (tavā) is inherited from a reconstructed tapaka, "frying pan"; CDIAL lists Punjabi tavā, "circular griddle" (CDIAL 5670).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਸੇਕਣਾ (sekṇā) — to bake on a griddle; to warm.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ਸੇਕਣਾ.
ਸੇਕਣਾ — sekṇā — "to bake on a griddle; to warm".
ਮੈਂ ਤਵੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਰੋਟੀ ਸੇਕਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ tave utte roṭī sekdī hāṁ. I bake the roti on the griddle.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਸੇਕੀ, maiṁ roṭī sekī.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੇਕਣਾ (sekṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed sēkk, "heat, foment, roast"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sekṇā, "to warm, toast, foment" (CDIAL 13581).
