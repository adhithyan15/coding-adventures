## You'll want to know: o furo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C405-PNEU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o furo** — "the puncture; the hole".

> Tivemos um furo na estrada para a praia.
>
> *We had a puncture on the road to the beach.*

In journalism, *um furo* is a scoop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*furo* is built on *furar* 'to pierce', which is inherited from Latin *forāre* 'to bore' (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
