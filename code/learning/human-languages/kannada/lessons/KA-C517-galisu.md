## You'll want to know: ಗಳಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C517-PRAGATI517-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಳಿಸು** — *gaḷisu* — "to gain; to earn".

> ಅವನು ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಹೆಸರು ಗಳಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *avanu ūrinalli oḷḷeya hesaru gaḷisidanu.*
>
> He earned a good name in the town.

Past (I …): ಗಳಿಸಿದೆ, *gaḷiside*.

Money, fame or marks: ಅಂಕ ಗಳಿಸು, to score marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಳಿಸು** (*gaḷisu*) is a native Kannada verb, "to gain, to earn"; its origin is not settled.
