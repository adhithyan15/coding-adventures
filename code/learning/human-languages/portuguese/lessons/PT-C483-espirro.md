## You'll want to know: o espirro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C483-ALERGIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o espirro** — "sneeze".

> Ouvi um espirro na sala ao lado.
>
> *I heard a sneeze in the next room.*

After a sneeze, people in Portugal say *santinho!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*espirro* comes from *espirrar* "to sneeze", inherited from Latin *exspīrāre* "to breathe out" (EtymDB).
