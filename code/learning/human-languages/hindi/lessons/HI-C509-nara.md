## You'll want to know: नारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C509-HADTAL509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नारा** — *nārā* — "a slogan; a rallying cry".

> मज़दूरों के नारे कारख़ाने के अंदर तक सुनाई दे रहे थे।
>
> *Mazdūroṁ ke nāre kārkhāne ke andar tak sunāī de rahe the.*
>
> The workers' slogans could be heard right inside the factory.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नारा** (*nārā*) is usually traced to Arabic *naʿra*, "a shout, a cry", through Persian; Hindi uses it for the words a crowd shouts together.
