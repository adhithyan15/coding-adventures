## You'll want to know: ಅಳುಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C420-ALUKU420-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಳುಕು** — *aḷuku* — "to waver; to flinch".

> ಕತ್ತಲೆಯ ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೋಗಲು ನಾನು ಅಳುಕಿದೆ.
>
> *kattaleya dāriyalli hōgalu nānu aḷukide.*
>
> I flinched at going down the dark road.

Past (I …): ಅಳುಕಿದೆ, *aḷukide*.

Often negative: ಅಳುಕದೆ, without flinching.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಳುಕು** (*aḷuku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 306 lists Kannada *aḷuku*, "to fear", beside Tamil *aḷukku*, "to be frightened".
