## You'll want to know: حومه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C450-MARKAZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حومه** (*hume*) — "the outskirts, the suburbs".

> خانواده ما در حومه شهر زندگی میکند.
>
> *Khânevâde-ye mâ dar hume-ye shahr zendegi mikonad.*
>
> Our family lives on the outskirts of the town.

Usually *hume-ye shahr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hume* was borrowed from Arabic *ḥawma*, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'the area around, the edge'.
