# ઇનામ (inām) — a prize; a reward

## You'll want to know: ઇનામ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C309-INAM309-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇનામ** — *inām* — "a prize; a reward".

> ગામના લોકોએ અરુણને ઇનામ આપ્યું.
>
> *gāmnā lokoe Aruṇne inām āpyũ.*
>
> The villagers gave Arun a reward.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇનામ** (*inām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *inʿām*, "something bestowed". It is neuter: **ઇનામ આપ્યું**, gave a prize; **ઇનામ જીત્યું**, won one.
