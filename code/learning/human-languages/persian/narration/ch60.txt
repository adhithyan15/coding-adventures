Persian, chapter 60: Fast, Easy, Hard, Expensive, Cheap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تند (tond) — fast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for cold, then the Persian for slow.

You'll want to know: تند.
تند — tond — "fast".

The word, taken apart.
tond is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian tund, "sharp, quick".

Lesson 4 of 5.
گران (gerân) — expensive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for easy, then the Persian for hard.

You'll want to know: گران.
گران — gerân — "expensive".

The word, taken apart.
gerân is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian grān, "heavy" (EtymDB). EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European word behind Latin gravis, "heavy": a dear price is a heavy one.
