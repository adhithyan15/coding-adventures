Bengali, chapter 510: Cleaning Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ফিনাইল (phināil) — phenyl (a floor disinfectant).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cushion, then the Bengali for to spread out.

You'll want to know: ফিনাইল.
ফিনাইল — phināil — "phenyl (a floor disinfectant)".
আমি জলে ফিনাইল মিশিয়ে মেঝে মুছলাম। Āmi jôle phināil mishiye mejhe muchhlām. I mixed phenyl into the water and mopped the floor.

The word, taken apart.
ফিনাইল (phināil) is borrowed from English phenyl, the name of a disinfectant used for washing floors.

You'll want to know: ঝুল.
ঝুল — jhul — "cobwebs; sooty dust".
সিলিং থেকে আমি ঝুল ঝাড়লাম। Siling theke āmi jhul jhāṛlām. I brushed the cobwebs off the ceiling.

The word, taken apart.
ঝুল (jhul) probably belongs with jhulyati, "swings, hangs" (CDIAL 5406), the root of ঝোলানো: the dust that hangs from the ceiling.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for cobwebs, then the Bengali for cleared away.

You'll want to know: নিংড়ানো.
নিংড়ানো — ningṛāno — "to wring out".
আমি ভিজে গামছাটা নিংড়ালাম। Āmi bhije gāmchhāṭā ningṛālām. I wrung out the wet towel.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কাপড়টা নিংড়ালেন, tini kāpṛṭā ningṛālen.

The word, taken apart.
নিংড়ানো (ningṛāno) comes from a reconstructed nirgaḍati, "drips out"; CDIAL lists Bengali niṅṛāna, "to make drip, to squeeze" (CDIAL 7293).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for cleared away, then the Bengali for to wring out.

You'll want to know: মাজা.
মাজা — mājā — "to scour (pots); to brush (teeth)".
রাতে শোয়ার আগে আমি দাঁত মাজি। Rāte shoyār āge āmi dā̃t māji. I brush my teeth at night before bed.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কড়াইটা মাজলেন, tini kôṛāiṭā mājlen.

The word, taken apart.
মাজা (mājā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali mājā, "to scour", under Sanskrit mārjati, "wipes clean" (CDIAL 10080).
