# ઉત્કંઠા (utkanṭhā) — longing; eager desire

## You'll want to know: ઉત્કંઠા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C449-ATUR449-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્કંઠા** — *utkanṭhā* — "longing; eager desire".

> માતાને દીકરીને જોવાની ઘણી ઉત્કંઠા હતી.
>
> *mātāne dīkrīne jovānī ghaṇī utkanṭhā hatī.*
>
> The mother longed to see her daughter.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉત્કંઠા** (*utkanṭhā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *utkaṇṭhā*, "longing for a beloved person or thing", as Monier-Williams records it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
