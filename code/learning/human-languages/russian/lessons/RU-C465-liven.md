## You'll want to know: ливень
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C465-LIVEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ливень** (*líven*) — "a downpour, a heavy shower".

> После обеда начался сильный ливень.
>
> *Pósle obéda nachalsyá sílnyy líven.*
>
> After lunch a heavy downpour began.

Masculine; the е drops out: *ливня*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ливень* is built on *лить* 'to pour' with *-ень*: rain that pours. EtymDB takes *лить* from Proto-Slavic *liti* 'to pour'.
