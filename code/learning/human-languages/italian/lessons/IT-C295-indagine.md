## You'll want to know: l'indagine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C295-INDAGINE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'indagine** — "the police investigation, the inquiry".

> L'indagine è durata due anni.
>
> *The investigation lasted two years.*

Often plural: *le indagini continuano*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indagine* is a learned borrowing from Latin *indāgō* "a ring of hunters, a search" (EtymDB).
