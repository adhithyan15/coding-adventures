## You'll want to know: घोळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C556-ANUKUL556-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घोळ** — *ghoḷ* — "a muddle; a mess".

> तिकिटांच्या आरक्षणात मोठा घोळ झाला.
>
> *tikiṭāñcyā ārakṣaṇāt moṭhā ghoḷ jhālā.*
>
> There was a big muddle in the ticket booking.

Masculine.

घोळ होणे, things get into a muddle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोळ** (*ghoḷ*) is built on घोळणे, "to stir about"; CDIAL lists Marathi *ghoḷṇẽ* under Sanskrit *ghōlayati*, "mixes, stirs together" (CDIAL 4526).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
