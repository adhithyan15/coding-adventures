## You'll want to know: راه آهن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C429-RAHAHAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**راه آهن** (*râh-âhan*) — "a railway (US: a railroad)".

> این شهر ایستگاه راه آهن ندارد.
>
> *In shahr istgâh-e râh-âhan nadârad.*
>
> This town has no railway station.

Literally 'iron road'. *Istgâh-e râh-âhan* — the railway station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*râh-âhan* is a native compound, an iron road: *râh* 'road' goes back to Middle Persian *rāh* (EtymDB), and *âhan* 'iron' is a native word that EtymDB lists.
