## You'll want to know: ふぶき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C277-OOYUKI277-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふぶき** — *fubuki* — "a snowstorm; a blizzard".

> **ゆうべは ひどい ふぶきでした。** — *yūbe wa hidoi fubuki deshita* — "There was a terrible blizzard last night."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふぶき** (*fubuki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
