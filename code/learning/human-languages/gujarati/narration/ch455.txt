5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગમગીન (gamgīn) — gloomy; sorrowful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for distraught, then the Gujarati for to fidget.

You'll want to know: ગમગીન.
ગમગીન — gamgīn — "gloomy; sorrowful".
મિત્ર ગયા પછી અરુણ આખો દિવસ ગમગીન રહ્યો. mitra gayā pachī Aruṇ ākho divas gamgīn rahyo. After his friend left, Arun stayed gloomy all day.

The word, taken apart.
ગમગીન (gamgīn) is usually traced to Persian ġamgīn, "sorrowful", built on ġam, "grief".

દાદીએ જૂના ફોટા જોઈને નિસાસો નાખ્યો. dādīe jūnā phoṭā joīne nisāso nākhyo. Looking at the old photos, my grandmother sighed.

You'll want to know: રડાવવું.
રડાવવું — raḍāvvũ — "to make someone cry".
આ ફિલ્મ બધાને રડાવે છે. ā philm badhāne raḍāve che. This film makes everyone cry.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને રડાવ્યું, teṇe bāḷakne raḍāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
રડાવવું (raḍāvvũ) is the causative of રડવું (raḍvũ), "to cry", which is inherited from Sanskrit raṭati, "cries, yells" (CDIAL 10590).
