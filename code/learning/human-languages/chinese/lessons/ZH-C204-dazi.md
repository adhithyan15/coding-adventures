## You'll want to know: 打字
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C204-ONLINE204-05]; assesses=[] -->

**打字** — *dǎzì* — "to type".

> **他打字很快** *tā dǎzì hěn kuài* — He types fast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**打字** (*dǎzì*) is built from 打 'to strike' and 字 'a character', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *dziH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): striking out characters. The modern dǎ is not the regular outcome of *taengX*, and how it came is not settled.
