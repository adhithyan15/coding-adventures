## You'll want to know: લીંપવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C516-CHUNO516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**લીંપવું** — *lī̃pvũ* — "to plaster (with mud)".

> ગામમાં દાદી ગારાથી આંગણું લીંપતી હતી.
>
> *gāmmā̃ dādī gārāthī ā̃gaṇũ lī̃ptī hatī.*
>
> In the village, my grandmother used to plaster the courtyard with mud.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આંગણું લીંપ્યું, *teṇe ā̃gaṇũ lī̃pyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લીંપવું** (*lī̃pvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *limpáti*, "smears", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 11066).
