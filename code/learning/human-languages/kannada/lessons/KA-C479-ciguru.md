# ಚಿಗುರು (ciguru) — to sprout; to put out shoots

## You'll want to know: ಚಿಗುರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C479-SASYA479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಗುರು** — *ciguru* — "to sprout; to put out shoots".

> ಮೊದಲ ಮಳೆಯ ನಂತರ ಮರಗಳು ಚಿಗುರಿದವು.
>
> *modala maḷeya nantara maragaḷu ciguridavu.*
>
> After the first rain the trees put out new shoots.

Past (it …): ಚಿಗುರಿತು, *ciguritu*.

Also a noun, a tender shoot: ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಗುರು** (*ciguru*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2489 lists Kannada *igaru*, *cigur*, *cigaru*, "a sprout, a young leaf", and *cigi*, "to sprout".
