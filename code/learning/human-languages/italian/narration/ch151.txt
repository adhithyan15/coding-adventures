Lesson 1 of 5.
il cervello — the brain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to download, then the Italian for to ski.

You'll want to know: il cervello.
il cervello — "the brain".

The word, taken apart.
cervello from Latin cerebellum, "a little brain", from cerebrum, "brain" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
