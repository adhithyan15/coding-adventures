# জনসংখ্যা (jônôsôngkhyā) — population

## You'll want to know: জনসংখ্যা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C349-PROTIBAD349-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জনসংখ্যা** — *jônôsôngkhyā* — "population".

> আমাদের গ্রামের জনসংখ্যা পাঁচ হাজার।
>
> *āmāder grāmer jônôsôngkhyā pā̃ch hājār.*
>
> Our village has a population of five thousand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জনসংখ্যা** (*jônôsôngkhyā*) is a modern compound of Sanskrit *jana*, people, and *saṃkhyā*, a number or a count (Monier-Williams): the number of the people.
