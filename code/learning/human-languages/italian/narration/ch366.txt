Italian, chapter 366: Problemi sociali.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la povertà — the poverty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the prejudice, then the Italian for the stereotype.

You'll want to know: la povertà.
la povertà — "the poverty".
Molte famiglie vivono in povertà. Many families live in poverty.
Built on povero.

The word, taken apart.
povertà is inherited from Latin paupertās, paupertātis "poverty" (Lewis & Short), from pauper "poor".

Lesson 3 of 5.
la discriminazione — the discrimination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the poverty, then the Italian for the unemployment.

You'll want to know: la discriminazione.
la discriminazione — "the discrimination".
La discriminazione sul lavoro è contro la legge. Discrimination at work is against the law.
Discriminazione contro le donne.

The word, taken apart.
discriminazione is a learned borrowing from Late Latin discrīminātiō, a term of rhetoric for setting opposite ideas side by side (Lewis & Short), from discrīmen "a dividing line", built on discernere and cernere "to sift, to separate".

Lesson 4 of 5.
discriminare — to discriminate (against).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the unemployment, then the Italian for the discrimination.

You'll want to know: discriminare.
discriminare — "to discriminate (against)".
Non si può discriminare una persona per la sua religione. You cannot discriminate against a person because of their religion.
Stress: discrìmino. The old sense is to tell apart.

The word, taken apart.
discriminare is a learned borrowing from Latin discrīmināre "to divide, to separate" (Lewis & Short), from discrīmen "a dividing line", built on cernere "to sift".

immigrazione is built on immigrare "to immigrate", a learned borrowing from Latin immigrāre "to move into" (Lewis & Short), built from in and migrāre "to move house".
