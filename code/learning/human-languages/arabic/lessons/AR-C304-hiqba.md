## You'll want to know: حقبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C304-ZAMAN304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حقبة** — *ḥiqba* — "an era, a long period".

> كانت تلك حقبة صعبة في تاريخ البلد.
>
> *kānat tilka ḥiqba ṣaʿba fī tārīkh al-balad.*
>
> That was a hard era in the country’s history.

Plural: حقب, *ḥiqab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حقبة** (*ḥiqba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-q-b* (Lane), where *ḥiqba* and *ḥuqb* name a long span of years, and *ḥaqība*, a bag tied behind the saddle, is the same root.
