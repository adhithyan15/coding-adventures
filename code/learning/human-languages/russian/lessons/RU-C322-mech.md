## You'll want to know: меч
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C322-RYTSAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**меч** (*mech*) — "a sword".

> Рыцарь поднял меч.
>
> *Rýtsar pódnyal mech.*
>
> The knight raised his sword.

Genitive *меча*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*меч* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *mĭčĭ* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to an early Germanic word for a sword.
