## You'll want to know: балл
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C381-SESSIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**балл** (*ball*) — "a point, a mark (in a test)".

> Он получил пять баллов.
>
> *On poluchíl pyat bállov.*
>
> He got five points.

Masculine. *Средний балл* — a grade average. A dance is *бал*, with one *л*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**балл** was borrowed from French *balle* "a ball, a bundle" (EtymDB); votes and marks were once counted with small balls.
