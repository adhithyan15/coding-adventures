## You'll want to know: উদ্ধত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C459-BINOYI459-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্ধত** — *uddhôtô* — "insolent; haughty".

> উদ্ধত ছেলেটা দাদুর সঙ্গে তর্ক করল।
>
> *uddhôtô chheleṭā dādur sôngge tôrkô kôrlô.*
>
> The insolent boy argued with my grandfather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্ধত** (*uddhôtô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *uddhata*, "raised, lifted up", and so "haughty" (Monier-Williams): with the head held too high.
