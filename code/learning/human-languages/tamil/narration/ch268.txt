Lesson 1 of 5.
உற்சாகம் (uṟcākam) — enthusiasm, excitement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for crowded, then the Tamil for easy.

You'll want to know: உற்சாகம்.
உற்சாகம் — uṟcākam — "enthusiasm, excitement".

The word, taken apart.
உற்சாகம் (uṟcākam) is a loan from Sanskrit utsāha, "power, joy" (CDIAL 1882).

The word, taken apart.
குழப்பம் (kuḻappam) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1817 lists Kannada kuḻa, "confusion". It comes from kuḻampu, "to be stirred up, confused".
