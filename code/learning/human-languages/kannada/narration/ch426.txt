Kannada, chapter 426: Snags and Setbacks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತೊಲಗಿಸು (tolagisu) — to get rid of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for valid, then the Kannada for recognition.

You'll want to know: ತೊಲಗಿಸು.
ತೊಲಗಿಸು — tolagisu — "to get rid of".
ಊರಿನವರು ಒಟ್ಟಾಗಿ ಕೆರೆಯ ಕಸವನ್ನು ತೊಲಗಿಸಿದರು. ūrinavaru oṭṭāgi kereya kasavannu tolagisidaru. Together the townspeople got rid of the rubbish in the lake.
Past (I …): ತೊಲಗಿಸಿದೆ, tolagiside.
ತೊಲಗು, to go away, to get lost.

The word, taken apart.
ತೊಲಗಿಸು (tolagisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3519 lists Kannada tolagu, "to go away, to depart", and its causative tolagisu, beside Tamil tolai, "to perish, to be removed".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು (kaḍegaṇisu) — to disregard; to overlook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for recognition, then the Kannada for to get rid of.

You'll want to know: ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು.
ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು — kaḍegaṇisu — "to disregard; to overlook".
ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳು ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆಗಳನ್ನು ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸಿದರು. adhikārigaḷu haḷḷiya samasyegaḷannu kaḍegaṇisidaru. The officials disregarded the village's problems.
Past (I …): ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸಿದೆ, kaḍegaṇiside.
What is disregarded takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಡೆಗಣಿಸು (kaḍegaṇisu) is built as a compound whose first part is native kaḍe, "end, a low or inferior place" (DEDR 1109), as in ಕಡಿಮೆ; the second part looks like ಗಣಿಸು, "to count", but how the word was formed is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತೊಡಕು (toḍaku) — a snag; a complication.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to disregard, then the Kannada for careful.

You'll want to know: ತೊಡಕು.
ತೊಡಕು — toḍaku — "a snag; a complication".
ಮನೆ ಕಟ್ಟುವಾಗ ಹಲವು ತೊಡಕುಗಳು ಬಂದವು. mane kaṭṭuvāga halavu toḍakugaḷu bandavu. Many snags came up while building the house.
Plural: ತೊಡಕುಗಳು, toḍakugaḷu.
ತೊಡಕಾಗು, to become a hitch.

The word, taken apart.
ತೊಡಕು (toḍaku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3480 lists Kannada toḍaku, toḍaṅku, "to get entangled, to be caught"; a snag is what one gets caught on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for careful, then the Kannada for a snag.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಕೂಲ.
ಪ್ರತಿಕೂಲ — pratikūla — "adverse; unfavourable".
ಪ್ರತಿಕೂಲ ಹವಾಮಾನದಿಂದ ವಿಮಾನ ತಡವಾಯಿತು. pratikūla havāmānadinda vimāna taḍavāyitu. The flight was delayed by bad weather.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರತಿಕೂಲ (pratikūla) is borrowed from Sanskrit pratikūla, "against the bank", so "contrary, adverse" (CDIAL 8545).
