## Script — 小 without its hook, and a sweep
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 少

**One piece, built on 小.**

**Three strokes like 小.** A vertical down the middle, but this time with no hook at the foot. A short stroke falling to the left. A dot on the right.

**One more stroke.** A long left-falling sweep from the right, under the vertical.

3 + 1 = four.
