## You'll want to know: беречь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C369-EKOLOGIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**беречь** (*beréch*) — "to look after, to protect; to save".

> Нужно беречь природу.
>
> *Núzhno beréch priródu.*
>
> We must look after nature.

Imperfective; present *берегу, бережёшь, берегут*; past *берёг, берегла*. *Беречь себя* — to take care of oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**беречь** is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*berťi*, from PIE *\*bʰergʰ-* "to defend, to protect" (EtymDB).
