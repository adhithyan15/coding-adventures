## You'll want to know: લોખંડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C508-LUHAR508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લોખંડ** — *lokhanḍ* — "iron".

> આ દરવાજો લોખંડનો છે.
>
> *ā darvājo lokhanḍno che.*
>
> This gate is made of iron.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લોખંડ** (*lokhanḍ*) comes from *\*lōhōpaskara*, "iron tools", a form CDIAL builds from *lōhá*, "iron", and *upaskara*, "tool"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "tools, iron, ironware" (CDIAL 11171).
