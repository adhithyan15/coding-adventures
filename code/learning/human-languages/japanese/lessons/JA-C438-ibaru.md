## You'll want to know: いばる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C438-STUBBORN438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いばる** — *ibaru* — "to act big; to boss people around".

> **あの せんぱいは いつも いばっています。** — *ano senpai wa itsumo ibatte imasu* — "That senior acts big all the time."

Polite **いばります**, *ibarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **しゃちょうが いばりました。**, *shachō ga ibarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いばる** (*ibaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict gives two kanji spellings that differ in their first character, so the spelling was attached to the word later (JMdict).
