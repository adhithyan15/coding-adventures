## You'll want to know: جرادة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C476-AQRAB476-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جرادة** — *jarāda* — "a locust".

> رأيت جرادة خضراء على الشجرة.
>
> *raʾaytu jarāda khaḍrāʾ ʿalā ash-shajara.*
>
> I saw a green locust on the tree.

*jarād* is locusts as a swarm; *jarāda* is one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جرادة** (*jarāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-d*, "to strip bare" (Lane). Locusts strip the land bare.
