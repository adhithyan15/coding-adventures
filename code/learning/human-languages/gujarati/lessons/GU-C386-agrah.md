## You'll want to know: આગ્રહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C386-AASTHA386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**આગ્રહ** — *āgrah* — "insistence".

> જો તમે આગ્રહ કરશો તો હું જમીને જઈશ.
>
> *jo tame āgrah karśo to hũ jamīne jaīś.*
>
> If you insist, I will eat before I go.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આગ્રહ** (*āgrah*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āgraha*, "insisting on, a strong inclination" (Monier-Williams).
