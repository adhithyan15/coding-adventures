## You'll want to know: さいわい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C424-OTOSHIMONO424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**さいわい** — *saiwai* — "fortunately; luckily".

> **さいわい、さいふは こうばんに ありました。** — *saiwai, saifu wa kōban ni arimashita* — "Luckily, my wallet was at the police box."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さいわい** (*saiwai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
