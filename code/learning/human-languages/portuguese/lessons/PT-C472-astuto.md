## You'll want to know: astuto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C472-ESPERTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**astuto** — "cunning, shrewd".

> Nas histórias para crianças, a raposa é astuta.
>
> *In children's stories, the fox is cunning.*

Less informal than *esperto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*astuto* comes from Latin *astūtus* "shrewd, crafty" (EtymDB), built on *astus* "craft, cunning".
