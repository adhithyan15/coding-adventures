## You'll want to know: collābor, collābī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C313-RUINA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**collābor, collābī** — "to fall in, to collapse".

> Pōns vetus subitō collāpsus est.
>
> *The old bridge suddenly collapsed.*

*collābor, collābī, collāpsus sum*, a deponent: *con-* + *lābor* (taught). English *collapse* comes from its participle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**collābor** is built on *lābor*, "to slip, to fall", with *con-* (Lewis & Short): to fall together in ruins. English *collapse* comes from its participle.
