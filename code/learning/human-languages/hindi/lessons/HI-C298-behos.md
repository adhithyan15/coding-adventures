## You'll want to know: बेहोश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C298-GHAYAL298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहोश** — *behoś* — "unconscious".

> ड्राइवर बेहोश था।
>
> *ḍrāivar behoś thā.*
>
> The driver was unconscious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेहोश** (*behoś*) is usually traced to Persian *bē*, "without", and *hōš*, "the senses", the same *hōš* that sits inside **होशियार**, alert.
