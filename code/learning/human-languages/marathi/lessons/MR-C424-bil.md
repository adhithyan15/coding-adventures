## You'll want to know: बिल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C424-VIKRI424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बिल** — *bil* — "a bill".

> जेवणानंतर मी बिल भरलं.
>
> *jevṇānantar mī bil bharlaṁ.*
>
> After the meal I paid the bill.

Neuter.

बिल भरणे, to pay a bill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिल** (*bil*) is a loan from English *bill*.
