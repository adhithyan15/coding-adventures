German, chapter 522: Bücher leihen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ausleihen — to borrow; to lend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to rhyme, then the German for writer.

You'll want to know: ausleihen.
ausleihen — "to borrow; to lend".
In der Bibliothek habe ich drei Bücher ausgeliehen. I borrowed three books from the library.
Separable: lieh aus, hat ausgeliehen. Sich etwas ausleihen — to borrow; jemandem etwas ausleihen — to lend.

The word, taken apart.
ausleihen is built on aus "out" and leihen "to lend", inherited from Old High German līhan, from Proto-Germanic līhwaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Verlag — publisher, publishing house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to leaf, then the German for bookmark.

You'll want to know: der Verlag.
der Verlag — "publisher, publishing house".
Ihr erstes Buch erscheint bei einem kleinen Verlag. Her first book is coming out with a small publisher.
Plural: die Verlage. Built on verlegen in the sense to publish.

The word, taken apart.
Verlag is built on verlegen "to publish", from ver- and legen "to lay", which is inherited from Old High German leggen, from Proto-Germanic lagjaną (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
Übersetzung is built on übersetzen "to translate", from über "across" and setzen "to set", with -ung; EtymDB lists it without a source.
