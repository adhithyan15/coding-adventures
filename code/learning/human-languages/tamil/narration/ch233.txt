Tamil, chapter 233: Every year, Dawn, Day after tomorrow, Day before yesterday, Second.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ஆண்டுதோறும் (āṇṭutōṟum) — every year, yearly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a patient, then the Tamil for a nurse.

You'll want to know: ஆண்டுதோறும்.
ஆண்டுதோறும் — āṇṭutōṟum — "every year, yearly".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: āṇṭutōṟum]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: āṇṭutōṟum, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

To book in advance? (முன்பதிவு செய், muṉpativu cey.) To apply? (விண்ணப்பி, viṇṇappi.) To type? (தட்டச்சு செய், taṭṭaccu cey.) To translate? (மொழிபெயர், moḻipeyar.) To cancel? (ரத்து செய், rattu cey.)
