# గొడవపడు (goḍavapaḍu) — to have a row; to quarrel

## You'll want to know: గొడవపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C534-GODAVA534-01]; assesses=[] -->

**గొడవపడు** — *goḍavapaḍu* — "to have a row; to quarrel".

> చిన్న విషయానికి అన్నదమ్ములు గొడవపడ్డారు.
>
> *cinna viṣayāniki annadammulu goḍavapaḍḍāru.*
>
> The brothers had a row over a small thing.

Past (I …): గొడవపడ్డాను, *goḍavapaḍḍānu*.

-తో గొడవపడు, to quarrel with.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గొడవపడు** (*goḍavapaḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2048 lists Telugu *goḍava*, "bother, worry, fuss", with Kannada *goḍave*; the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853) makes the verb.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
