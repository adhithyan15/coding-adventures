## You'll want to know: ereignen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C279-EREIGNIS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ereignen** — "to occur; reflexive: sich ereignen".

> Der Unfall ereignete sich am frühen Morgen.
>
> *The accident occurred early in the morning.*

Only reflexive, and only in the third person: *es ereignete sich*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ereignen* is usually traced to Middle High German *eröugen* "to show, to bring before the eyes", built on *Auge* "eye"; EtymDB lists no parent.
