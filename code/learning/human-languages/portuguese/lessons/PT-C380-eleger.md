## You'll want to know: eleger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C380-POLITICA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**eleger** — "to elect".

> Elegeram uma mulher para presidente da câmara.
>
> *They elected a woman as mayor.*

Two participles: *elegido* with *ter*, *eleito* with *ser*: *foi eleita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eleger* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēligere* "to pick out, to choose", built from *ex* and *legere* "to gather, to choose" (Lewis & Short).
