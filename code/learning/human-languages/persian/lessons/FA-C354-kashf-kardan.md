## You'll want to know: کشف کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C354-KASHF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کشف کردن** (*kashf kardan*) — "to discover".

> دانشمندان یک شهر قدیمی را زیر زمین کشف کردند.
>
> *Dâneshmandân yek shahr-e qadimi râ zir-e zamin kashf kardand.*
>
> Scientists discovered an old city underground.

Present *kashf mikonad*. *Kashf* alone is "a discovery".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kashf* is borrowed from Arabic *kashf*, "an unveiling, an uncovering" (EtymDB). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
