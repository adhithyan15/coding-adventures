## You'll want to know: सहारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C386-SAHARA386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सहारा** — *sahārā* — "a support, someone to lean on".

> अगर बूढ़ी माँ को सहारा नहीं मिलेगा तो वह गिर जाएगी।
>
> *agar būṛhī māṁ ko sahārā nahīṃ milegā to vah gir jāegī.*
>
> If the old mother has nobody to lean on, she will fall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहारा** (*sahārā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *sahakāra*, "assistance" (CDIAL 13299).
