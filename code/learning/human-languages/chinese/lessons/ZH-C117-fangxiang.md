## You'll want to know: fāngxiàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C117-COMPASS117-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngxiàng* — "direction; way".

> *chēzhàn zài nǎge fāngxiàng* — Which way is the station?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngxiàng* is built from fāng 'side, region' and xiàng 'to face', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjang* and *xjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the side you face.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
