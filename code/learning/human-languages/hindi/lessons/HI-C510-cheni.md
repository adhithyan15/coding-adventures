## You'll want to know: छेनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C510-BADHAI510-02]; assesses=[] -->

**छेनी** — *chenī* — "a chisel".

> छेनी और हथौड़े से वह लकड़ी में फूल बना रहा है।
>
> *Chenī aur hathauṛe se vah lakṛī meṁ phūl banā rahā hai.*
>
> With a chisel and a hammer he is carving flowers into the wood.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छेनी** (*chenī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *chēdana*, "cutting" (CDIAL 5066).
