## You'll want to know: শিরোনাম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C296-SONGBAD296-02]; assesses=[] -->

**শিরোনাম** — *shironām* — "a headline".

> আজ সংবাদপত্রের বড় শিরোনাম ছিল ঘূর্ণিঝড়ের খবর।
>
> *āj sôngbādpôtrer bôṛo shironām chhilô ghūrnijhôṛer khôbôr.*
>
> Today the newspaper's big headline was the news of the cyclone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শিরোনাম** (*shironām*) joins Sanskrit *śiras*, 'the head, the top' (Monier-Williams), to **নাম**, a name: the name at the head of a story. It is a modern learned compound.
