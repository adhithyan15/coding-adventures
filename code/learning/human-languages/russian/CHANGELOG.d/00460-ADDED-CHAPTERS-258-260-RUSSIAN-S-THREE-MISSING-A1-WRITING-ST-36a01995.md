## Added — chapters 258-260, Russian's three missing A1 writing stages

- Three writing lessons on SPINE-SAY-WHY, where потому что and the comma rule
  already live. No new vocabulary, letter or knowledge atom in any of them;
  they sit on a new A1 extension, RU-EXT-258-WRITING (path RU-PATH-258-WRITING).
- **258 — RU-W258-own-line (controlled composition).** One line about yourself,
  by hand, with nothing to copy. Copying always supplied the right shapes, so
  the Cyrillic check arrives here: л's point against м's dip, the bar over a
  handwritten т, and я facing away from Latin R.
- **259 — RU-W259-with-a-clock (timed production).** Built on what
  assessment-spec.md records from the Pushkin Institute: TORFL Elementary Level
  (ТЭУ/A1) is five separate subtests, about three and a half hours in all, and
  this book targets 66% on every one. The writing paper's own minutes and
  length are NOT quoted, because `task-shapes/a1.json` has not been
  transcribed. The clocked task is the book's own: a three-minute timer, then
  at most one minute checking л, м and the bar over т, so the directed time
  (240 s) stays inside the spec's five-minute lesson cap and the declared
  270 s. It ends on the rule that changes how to study: under this book's 66%
  target the weakest of five subtests decides, so strong reading cannot carry
  weak writing.
- **260 — RU-W260-four-lines (connected composition).** Four lines to one
  reader with и, но or а, and потому что. French's parce que moves nothing;
  Russian marks the join on paper: a comma before но, а and потому что, none
  before и joining two halves about the same person, and the task and the
  wrap-up check both keep that scope. The ear never hears it and the examiner
  always sees it. Its romanization follows the track convention (unstressed о
  kept as o, что as shto): potomú shto.
- Russian's cumulative writing ladder now reaches timed production at A1, the
  one blocker the level gate held it at pre-A1 for.
