## You'll want to know: もっと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C316-MONOGATARI316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**もっと** — *motto* — "more; even more".

> **もっと むかしばなしを ききたいです。** — *motto mukashibanashi o kikitai desu* — "I want to hear more old stories."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もっと** (*motto*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
