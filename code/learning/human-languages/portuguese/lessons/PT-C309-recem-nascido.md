## You'll want to know: o recém-nascido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C309-NASCIMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o recém-nascido** — "the newborn baby".

> O recém-nascido dorme quase o dia todo.
>
> *The newborn sleeps almost all day.*

*Recém-* means ‘newly’: *recém-casados*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recém-nascido* joins *recém*, a short form of *recente*, from Latin *recēns* "fresh, new", and *nascido* "born", from Latin *nāscī* (Lewis & Short).
