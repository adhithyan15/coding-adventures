## You'll want to know: じこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C262-JIKO262-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じこ** — *jiko* — "an accident".

> **きのう えきの ちかくで じこが ありました。** — *kinō eki no chikaku de jiko ga arimashita* — "There was an accident near the station yesterday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じこ** (*jiko*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "matter" (Middle Chinese *dzriH*) and *ko*, "circumstances" (*kuH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
