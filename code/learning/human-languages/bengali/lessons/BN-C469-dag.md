## You'll want to know: দাগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C469-GHAM469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**দাগ** — *dāg* — "a mark; a stain; a scar".

> জামায় চায়ের দাগ লাগল।
>
> *jāmāy chāyer dāg lāglô.*
>
> Tea left a stain on the shirt.

কাটা দাগ, the scar of a cut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দাগ** (*dāg*) is usually traced to Persian *dāgh*, "a brand, a mark".
