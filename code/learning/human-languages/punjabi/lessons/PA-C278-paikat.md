## You'll want to know: ਪੈਕਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C278-STOCK278-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੈਕਟ** — *paikaṭ* — "a packet".

> ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਦਾ ਇੱਕ ਪੈਕਟ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ chāh dā ikk paikaṭ kharīdāṅgā.*
>
> I will buy a packet of tea.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੈਕਟ** (*paikaṭ*) is borrowed from English *packet*.
