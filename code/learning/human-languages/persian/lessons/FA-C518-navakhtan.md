## You'll want to know: نواختن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C518-PLAYING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نواختن** (*navâkhtan*) — "to play (an instrument)".

> او پیانو را خیلی زیبا مینوازد.
>
> *u piyâno râ kheyli zibâ minavâzad.*
>
> She plays the piano beautifully.

Formal; present stem *navâz-*. In speech people say *zadan*: *piyâno mizanad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navâkhtan* is a native Persian verb, with the present stem *navâz-* seen in *navâzesh*. EtymDB lists it without a source, so its older history is not settled.
