## You'll want to know: l'aîné
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C490-PARRAIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'aîné** — "eldest, older (sibling)".

> L'aîné de la famille a vingt ans.
>
> *The eldest child in the family is twenty.*

A woman is *l'aînée*. *Mon frère aîné* — my older brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'aîné* comes from Old French *ainsné* (EtymDB), "born before": *ains* "before" goes back to Latin *ante*, and *né* to Latin *nātus*, from *nāscī* "to be born" (Lewis & Short).
