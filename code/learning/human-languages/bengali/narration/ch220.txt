Bengali, chapter 220: Equal, Main, Terrible, Excellent, Previous.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সমান (sômān) — equal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for deserted, then the Bengali for local.

You'll want to know: সমান.
সমান — sômān — "equal".

The word, taken apart.
সমান (sômān) is Sanskrit samāna, "same, alike", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13211).
