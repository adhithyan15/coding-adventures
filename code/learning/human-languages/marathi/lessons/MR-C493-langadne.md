## You'll want to know: लंगडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C493-TOL493-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लंगडणे** — *laṅgaḍṇe* — "to limp".

> पायाला लागल्यामुळे घोडा लंगडत होता.
>
> *pāyālā lāglyāmuḷe ghoḍā laṅgaḍat hotā.*
>
> The horse was limping from a hurt leg.

Past (he …): तो लंगडला, *to laṅgaḍlā*.

लंगडत चालणे, to walk with a limp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लंगडणे** (*laṅgaḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
