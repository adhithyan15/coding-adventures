## You'll want to know: تكرر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C413-TATAWWUR413-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تكرر** — *takarrara* — "to recur".

> تكرر الخطأ نفسه مرتين.
>
> *takarrara al-khaṭaʾ nafsuhu marratayn.*
>
> The same mistake happened twice.

Present: يتكرر, *yatakarrar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تكرر** (*takarrara*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-r-r*, "to return, to come back" (Lane). It is Form V, to come back again and again: to recur.
