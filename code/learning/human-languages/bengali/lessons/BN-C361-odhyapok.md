## You'll want to know: অধ্যাপক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C361-SHIKKHA361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অধ্যাপক** — *ôdhyāpôk* — "a professor; a college lecturer".

> মীরার বাবা কলেজে অধ্যাপক।
>
> *mīrār bābā kôleje ôdhyāpôk.*
>
> Mira's father is a lecturer at the college.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অধ্যাপক** (*ôdhyāpôk*) is Sanskrit *adhyāpaka*, a teacher (Monier-Williams). Bengali keeps it for those who teach at a college or a university.
