## You'll want to know: きれい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C163-HOME163-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きれい** — *kirei* — "clean; pretty".

> **へやは いつも きれいです。** — *heya wa itsumo kirei desu* — "The room is always clean."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きれい** (*kirei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "figured cloth, beautiful" (Middle Chinese *khjeX*) and *rei*, "lovely" (*lejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). It is usually written in kana (JMdict).
