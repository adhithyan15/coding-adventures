## You'll want to know: la discendenza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C312-ANTENATI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la discendenza** — "the descendants, the line of descent".

> Ha una discendenza numerosa: dieci nipoti.
>
> *He has many descendants: ten grandchildren.*

Built on *discendere*, ‘to go down’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discendenza* is built on *discendere* "to descend", from Latin *dēscendere* (EtymDB), from *dē* and *scandere* "to climb".
