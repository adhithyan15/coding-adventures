## You'll want to know: платный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C397-OPLATA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**платный** (*plátnyy*) — "paid, that one pays for".

> Здесь платная стоянка.
>
> *Zdes plátnaya stoyánka.*
>
> This is a paid car park.

*Платная дорога* — a toll road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**платный** is the adjective made from *плата* "a payment", the root of *платить*.
