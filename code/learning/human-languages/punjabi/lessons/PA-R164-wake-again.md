# An old word for waking

One familiar action returns after a long gap. Hear the meaning, pause,
and recall its Punjabi word before the model. No new word or scored mock
is added.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C150-ACT150-04] -->

Hear **to wake up**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਜਾਗਣਾ** (*jāgṇā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C150-ACT150-04] -->

Hear **ਜਾਗਣਾ**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **to wake up**.
Now hear **to wake up** again. [PAUSE 4s] Say **ਜਾਗਣਾ**
before the model. One old word is enough.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C150-ACT150-04] -->

One last cue: **to wake up**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਜਾਗਣਾ**.
Listen and retry once if the word does not come back.
