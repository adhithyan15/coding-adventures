## You'll want to know: أسعف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C301-TARIQ301-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أسعف** — *asʿafa* — "to give first aid".

> أسعف الطبيب الولد في الشارع.
>
> *asʿafa aṭ-ṭabīb al-walad fī ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> The doctor gave the boy first aid in the street.

Present: يسعف, *yusʿif*.

The taught إسعاف, first aid, is its verbal noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أسعف** (*asʿafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ʿ-f* (Lane), "to help, to give someone what he needs". It is Form IV; *isʿāf* is its verbal noun.
