## You'll want to know: 写作
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C251-ESSAY251-03]; assesses=[] -->

**写作** — *xiězuò* — "to write essays and stories; writing".

> **我很喜欢写作** *wǒ hěn xǐhuan xiězuò* — I really enjoy writing.

The new character, in pieces: 写 — five strokes: a cover of a dot and a bar that turns down; under it a short bar, a stroke that comes down, turns right, turns down again and hooks, and a long bar at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**写作** (*xiězuò*) is built from 写 'to write' and 作 'to make', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjaeX* and *tsak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'to copy out a book'.
