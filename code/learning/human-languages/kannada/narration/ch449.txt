Kannada, chapter 449: A Flat Tyre.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚಕ್ರ (cakra) — a wheel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a battery, then the Kannada for to ride.

You'll want to know: ಚಕ್ರ.
ಚಕ್ರ — cakra — "a wheel".
ಸೈಕಲ್ನ ಹಿಂದಿನ ಚಕ್ರ ಡೊಂಕಾಗಿದೆ. saikalna hindina cakra ḍoṅkāgide. The back wheel of the bicycle is bent.
Plural: ಚಕ್ರಗಳು, cakragaḷu.
ದ್ವಿಚಕ್ರ ವಾಹನ, a two-wheeler.

The word, taken apart.
ಚಕ್ರ (cakra) is borrowed from Sanskrit cakra, "a wheel, a potter's wheel, a circle" (CDIAL 4538).

You'll want to know: ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಗು.
ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಗು — paṅkcar āgu — "to get a puncture".
ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಸ್ಕೂಟರ್ನ ಟಯರ್ ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಯಿತು. dāriyalli nanna skūṭarna ṭayar paṅkcar āyitu. On the way my scooter tyre got a puncture.
Past (it …): ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಯಿತು, paṅkcar āyitu.
The tyre is the subject: ಟಯರ್ (ṭayar) ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಯಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಗು (paṅkcar āgu) (paṅcar āgu) joins a loan from English puncture and the native verb ಆಗು, to become.
