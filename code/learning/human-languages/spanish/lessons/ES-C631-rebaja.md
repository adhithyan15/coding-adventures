## You'll want to know: la rebaja
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C631-REBAJAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la rebaja** — "the price cut; the sales".

> En enero empiezan las rebajas.
>
> *The sales start in January.*

In Spain *las rebajas* are the seasonal sales; Latin America often says *las ofertas* or *las liquidaciones*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rebaja* is built on *rebajar* 'to lower', from *bajo* 'low', which is inherited from Late Latin *bassus* 'low' (EtymDB).
