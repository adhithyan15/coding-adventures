## You'll want to know: il reportage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C284-INCHIESTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il reportage** — "the report from the spot, the feature story".

> Ha scritto un reportage sulla vita in montagna.
>
> *She wrote a feature on life in the mountains.*

Said the French way, with the stress on the end. Plural unchanged: *i reportage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reportage* was borrowed from French *reportage* (EtymDB), built on *reporter*, a word that came into French from English.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
