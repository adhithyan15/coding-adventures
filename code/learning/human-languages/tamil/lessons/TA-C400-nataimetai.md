# நடைமேடை (naṭaimēṭai) — a platform; a raised walkway

## You'll want to know: நடைமேடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C400-RAYIL400-01]; assesses=[] -->

**நடைமேடை** — *naṭaimēṭai* — "a platform; a raised walkway".

> ரயில் மூன்றாவது நடைமேடைக்கு வருகிறது.
>
> *rayil mūṉṟāvatu naṭaimēṭaikku varukiṟatu.*
>
> The train is coming to the third platform.

Before -க்கு the final -ஐ stays as it is: நடைமேடைக்கு, to the platform.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நடைமேடை** (*naṭaimēṭai*) is built from *naṭai*, "walking, gait" (DEDR 3582, the family of நட, to walk), and *mēṭai*, "a raised floor, a stage", whose origin is not settled.
