## You'll want to know: ڈکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C469-THROAT469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈکار** — *ḍakār* — "a burp".

> کھانے کے بعد اسے ڈکار آئی۔
>
> *khāne ke bād use ḍakār āī.*
>
> He burped after the meal.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈکار** (*ḍakār*) is inherited from *ḍakkāra*, "belch", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as an imitation of the sound (CDIAL 5521).
