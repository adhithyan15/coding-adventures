## You'll want to know: desde que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C369-CONDICAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**desde que** — "provided that, as long as; since (in time)".

> Podes ir à festa desde que voltes antes da meia-noite.
>
> *You can go to the party as long as you come back before midnight.*

With the subjunctive it sets a condition: *desde que voltes*. With the indicative it means since: *desde que cheguei*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desde que* is built from *desde* "since", usually traced to Latin *dē* + *ex* + *dē*, all meaning "from" (Lewis & Short), and *que*.
