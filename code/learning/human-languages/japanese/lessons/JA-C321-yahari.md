## You'll want to know: やはり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C321-ZETTAI321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**やはり** — *yahari* — "as expected; after all".

> **やはり ははの いけんが ただしいと おもいます。** — *yahari haha no iken ga tadashii to omoimasu* — "After all, I think my mother's opinion is right."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やはり** (*yahari*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
