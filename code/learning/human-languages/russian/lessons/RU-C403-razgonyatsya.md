## You'll want to know: разгоняться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C403-RUL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**разгоняться** (*razgonyátsya*) — "to speed up, to gather speed".

> На шоссе машина быстро разгоняется.
>
> *Na shossé mashína býstro razgonyáyetsya.*
>
> On the motorway the car quickly speeds up.

Imperfective; perfective *разогнаться* (*разгонюсь, разгонишься*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разгоняться* is built from *раз-*, *гонять* 'to drive, to chase' and *-ся*. *Гонять* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *ganjati*, which EtymDB traces to the PIE root *gʷʰen-* 'to strike, to drive'.
