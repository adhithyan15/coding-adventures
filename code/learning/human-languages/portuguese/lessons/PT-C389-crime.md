## You'll want to know: o crime
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C389-CRIME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o crime** — "the crime".

> O crime aconteceu de madrugada.
>
> *The crime happened in the early hours.*

*Cometer um crime* — to commit a crime.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crime* was borrowed from French *crime* (EtymDB), from Latin *crīmen* "an accusation, a charge, a crime" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
