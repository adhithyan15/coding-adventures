## You'll want to know: پرواز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C398-PARVAZ398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پرواز** — *parvāz* — "a flight".

> ہماری پرواز رات دس بجے ہے۔
>
> *hamārī parvāz rāt das baje hai.*
>
> Our flight is at ten at night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پرواز** (*parvāz*) is usually traced to Persian *parvāz*, "flying, a flight".
