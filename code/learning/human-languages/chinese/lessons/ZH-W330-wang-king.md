## Script — two bars, a vertical, a long bar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WANG-KING-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 王

**One piece, four strokes.**

**Two bars first,** the top one, then a shorter one in the middle.

**Then the vertical,** from the top bar down through the middle one to the foot.

**Last, the longest bar,** along the foot, closing the vertical.

2 + 1 + 1 = four.
