## You'll want to know: બળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C172-STALE172-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બળવું** — *baḷvũ* — "to get burnt, to burn".

> **રોટલી તવા ઉપર બળે છે.** — *roṭlī tavā upar baḷe che* — "The rotli burns on the griddle."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બળવું** (*baḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*dvalati*, "burns", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit *jvalati*, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati *baḷvũ*, "burns" (CDIAL 6654).
