# રીઝવું (rījhvũ) — to be pleased; to be won over

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C449-ATUR449-02, GU-LEX-C449-ATUR449-03] -->

## You'll want to know: રીઝવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C449-ATUR449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રીઝવું** — *rījhvũ* — "to be pleased; to be won over".

> દાદા નાની ભેટથી પણ રીઝે છે.
>
> *dādā nānī bheṭthī paṇ rījhe che.*
>
> Grandfather is pleased even by a small gift.

Past (he …): તે રીઝ્યો, *te rījhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રીઝવું** (*rījhvũ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be pleased", under Sanskrit *ṛdhati*, "increases, prospers" (CDIAL 2457).
