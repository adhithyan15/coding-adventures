## You'll want to know: die Windel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C496-BABY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Windel** — "nappy, diaper".

> Das Baby braucht eine frische Windel.
>
> *The baby needs a fresh nappy.*

Plural: *die Windeln*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Windel* is inherited from Middle High German *windel* and Old High German *windila* (EtymDB), built on *winden* "to wind, to wrap".
