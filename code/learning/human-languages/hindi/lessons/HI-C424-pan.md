## You'll want to know: पान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C424-DHABA424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पान** — *pān* — "a betel leaf, paan".

> खाने के बाद दादा जी पान खाते हैं।
>
> *khāne ke bād dādā jī pān khāte haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather has a paan after his meal.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पान** (*pān*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *parṇa*, "a leaf" (CDIAL 7918), which lists Hindi *pān*, "a betel leaf": the leaf above all leaves.
