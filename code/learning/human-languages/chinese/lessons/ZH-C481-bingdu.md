## You'll want to know: bìngdú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C481-SERIOUS481-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bìngdú* — "virus".

> *zhège bìngdú bǐ gǎnmào kěpà* — This virus is worse than a cold.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bìngdú* is built from 病 'illness' and *dú* 'poison', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjaengH* and *dowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'harm' and, after an older book, as a plant that harms people. The sense 'virus' is modern.
