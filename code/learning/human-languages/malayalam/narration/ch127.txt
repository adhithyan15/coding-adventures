Malayalam, chapter 127: Take Care, See You.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
സൂക്ഷിക്കണേ (sūkṣikkaṇē) — take care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for English, then the Malayalam for office.

You'll want to know: സൂക്ഷിക്കണേ.
സൂക്ഷിക്കണേ (sūkṣikkaṇē) — "take care", a warm word at the door.

What you've built.
A caring goodbye.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sūkṣikkaṇē]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sūkṣikkaṇē, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sūkṣikkaṇē!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for English, then the Malayalam for office, then say sūkṣikkaṇē again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does സൂക്ഷിക്കണേ (sūkṣikkaṇē) mean? ("Take care".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 7.

Lesson 6 of 7.
How are you, really? — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Tomorrow is a holiday. Say so, and promise to call.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: you have a cold, a headache and dizziness; you are not well; tell a friend to rest]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: name sadness, fear, anger, laughter and worry]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: you are thirsty; name sweat, a tablet, vomiting and a wound]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: it's cold; it's hot; you're sleepy; name mist, and thunder and lightning]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: slowly; once more; louder; the meaning; please wait]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: please show me; I didn't hear; I didn't understand; please help; please stop here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A cold? (ജലദോഷം.) Headache? (തലവേദന.) Rest? (വിശ്രമം.) Dizziness? (തലകറക്കം.) Not well? (സുഖമില്ല.) Sadness, fear, anger, laughter, worry? (സങ്കടം, പേടി, ദേഷ്യം, ചിരി, ആശങ്ക.) Thirst? (ദാഹം.) Sweat? (വിയർപ്പ്.) Tablet? (ഗുളിക.) Vomiting? (ഛർദ്ദി.) Wound? (മുറിവ്.) Cold, heat, sleep, mist, thunder? (തണുപ്പ്, ചൂട്, ഉറക്കം, മഞ്ഞ്, ഇടിമിന്നൽ.) Slowly? (പതുക്കെ.) Once more? (ഒന്നുകൂടി.) Loudly? (ഉറക്കെ.) Meaning? (അർത്ഥം.) Wait? (കാത്തിരിക്കൂ.) Show? (കാണിക്കൂ.) Didn't hear? (കേട്ടില്ല.) Didn't understand? (മനസ്സിലായില്ല.) Help? (സഹായിക്കൂ.) Stop? (നിർത്തൂ.)


Lesson 7 of 7.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: not possible; not like that; maybe; a different one; wrong]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: congratulations; an invitation; a gift; welcome; good wishes]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: name a grandchild, a relative, the neighbours, an uncle and a nephew]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

(പറ്റില്ല.) Not like that? (അങ്ങനെയല്ല.) Maybe? (ആയിരിക്കാം.) Other? (വേറെ.) Wrong? (തെറ്റ്.) Congratulations? (അഭിനന്ദനങ്ങൾ.) Invitation? (ക്ഷണം.) Gift? (സമ്മാനം.) Welcome? (സ്വാഗതം.) Wishes? (ആശംസകൾ.) Grandchild? (പേരക്കുട്ടി.) Relative? (ബന്ധു.) Neighbours? (അയൽക്കാർ.) Uncle? (അമ്മാവൻ.) Nephew? (മരുമകൻ.) Address? (വിലാസം.) Wedding? (കല്യാണം.) Language? (ഭാഷ.) English? (ഇംഗ്ലീഷ്.) Office? (ഓഫീസ്.) Take care? (സൂക്ഷിക്കണേ (sūkṣikkaṇē).) Late? (വൈകി (vaiki).) Let's go? (പോകാം (pōkāṁ).) Holiday? (അവധി (avadhi).) I'll call? (വിളിക്കാം (viḷikkāṁ).)
