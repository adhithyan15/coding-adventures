## You'll want to know: चाचा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C116-GRAND-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चाचा** (*cācā*) — "uncle", your father's younger brother.

Like the grandparents, an uncle's name says whose brother he is. **मेरे चाचा** — "my uncle".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Four grandparents and an uncle: the family says which side.
