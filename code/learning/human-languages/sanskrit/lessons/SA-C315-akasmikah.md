## You'll want to know: आकस्मिकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C315-VISPHOTA315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आकस्मिकः** — *ākasmikaḥ* — "sudden; unexpected".

> आकस्मिकात् शब्दात् बालाः भीताः।
>
> *ākasmikāt śabdāt bālāḥ bhītāḥ.*
>
> The children are frightened by the sudden noise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकस्मिकः** (*ākasmikaḥ*) is built on *akasmāt*, "without a cause, suddenly" (from *a*, "not", and *kasmāt*, "from what?"); MW gives *ākasmika* as causeless, unforeseen, sudden (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
