Urdu, chapter 464: Inside the Body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دماغ (dimāgh) — the brain; the mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to whisper, then the Urdu for speechless.

You'll want to know: دماغ.
دماغ — dimāgh — "the brain; the mind".
دماغ جسم کو چلاتا ہے۔ dimāgh jism ko chalātā hai. The brain runs the body.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
دماغ (dimāgh) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic dimāgh, "brain".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dimāgh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dimāgh, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dimāgh jism ko chalātā hai. — The brain runs the body.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for to whisper, then the Urdu for speechless, then say dimāgh again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does دماغ (dimāgh) mean? ("The brain; the mind".) Say it once more.
