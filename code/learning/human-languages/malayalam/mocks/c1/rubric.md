# Malayalam C1 mock rubric

Score either C1 mock against [task-shapes/c1.json](../../task-shapes/c1.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers; every
paper must independently reach 60/100. A 240/400 total cannot compensate for a
failed paper, and every section must be attempted.

## Reading and listening

Each of the four reading parts and each of the four listening parts is worth 25
points. Divide the points equally among keyed items and retain one decimal
place. Play every listening text twice at 160–185 words per minute with natural
variation. At least two listening voices across a form must preserve documented
regional Malayalam features without caricature. No transcript, romanization,
dictionary, translator, or audio controls are allowed.

## Writing

The 180–220 word source synthesis is worth 40 points: accurate synthesis 10,
selection and integration 8, organisation and cohesion 7, audience and register
5, Malayalam vocabulary and grammar 7, and orthographic control 3. The 300–380
word extended response is worth 60 points: task fulfilment and intellectual
development 18, organisation and cohesion 12, genre/audience/register control
10, Malayalam vocabulary and grammar 15, and orthographic control 5. Either
traditional or reformed Malayalam orthography earns full credit when used
consistently. Romanized, translated, non-Malayalam-script, or visibly copied
answers earn zero independent-writing credit.

## Speaking

The source-based presentation is worth 35 points, collaborative synthesis 30,
and challenge-and-defence 35. Within each part reward accurate source integration,
sustained argument, interaction and repair, register control, lexical and
grammatical range, pronunciation, and fluency. A high C1 performance qualifies
claims, answers challenge directly, and reformulates without abandoning the
argument. No written answer script is allowed; fifteen-minute preparation notes
may contain keywords, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, regional-voice treatment, and scoring decisions.
