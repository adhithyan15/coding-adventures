Kannada, chapter 484: The Weather Report.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತಾಪಮಾನ (tāpamāna) — temperature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for groundwater, then the Kannada for to evaporate.

You'll want to know: ತಾಪಮಾನ.
ತಾಪಮಾನ — tāpamāna — "temperature".
ಇವತ್ತು ತಾಪಮಾನ ನಿನ್ನೆಗಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು. ivattu tāpamāna ninneginta heccu. Today the temperature is higher than yesterday.
ತಾಪ, heat, plus ಮಾನ, a measure.

The word, taken apart.
ತಾಪಮಾನ (tāpamāna) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: tāpa, "heat, glow" (CDIAL 5767), and māna, "a measure, a standard" (CDIAL 10041).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಆರ್ದ್ರತೆ (ārdrate) — humidity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to evaporate, then the Kannada for temperature.

You'll want to know: ಆರ್ದ್ರತೆ.
ಆರ್ದ್ರತೆ — ārdrate — "humidity".
ಕರಾವಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆರ್ದ್ರತೆ ಹೆಚ್ಚು, ಅದಕ್ಕೇ ಸೆಕೆ ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ. karāvaḷiyalli ārdrate heccu, adakkē seke āguttade. Humidity is high on the coast, and that is why it feels sticky.
Weather reports give it in per cent.

The word, taken apart.
ಆರ್ದ್ರತೆ (ārdrate) is built from Sanskrit ārdra, "wet" (CDIAL 1340), with the noun ending -tā, "-ness": wetness.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹವಾಗುಣ (havāguṇa) — climate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for temperature, then the Kannada for humidity.

You'll want to know: ಹವಾಗುಣ.
ಹವಾಗುಣ — havāguṇa — "climate".
ಗಿರಿಧಾಮದ ಹವಾಗುಣ ತಣ್ಣಗಿದೆ. giridhāmada havāguṇa taṇṇagide. The climate of the hill station is cool.
ಹವಾಮಾನ is the weather of a day; ಹವಾಗುಣ (havāguṇa) is the usual weather of a place.

The word, taken apart.
ಹವಾಗುಣ (havāguṇa) is a Kannada compound of ಹವಾ, "air, weather", usually traced to Arabic hawā, and Sanskrit guṇa, "a quality" (CDIAL 4190).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹದಗೆಡು (hadageḍu) — to worsen; to deteriorate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for climate, then the Kannada for dampness.

You'll want to know: ಹದಗೆಡು.
ಹದಗೆಡು — hadageḍu — "to worsen; to deteriorate".
ಸಂಜೆಯ ಹೊತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹವಾಮಾನ ಹದಗೆಟ್ಟಿತು. sañjeya hottige havāmāna hadageṭṭitu. By evening the weather had turned bad.
Past (it …): ಹದಗೆಟ್ಟಿತು, hadageṭṭitu.
Of weather, roads and health.

The word, taken apart.
ಹದಗೆಡು (hadageḍu) is native Dravidian: hada, "the proper state" (DEDR 3907 lists Kannada pada, hadanu), and keḍu, "to be ruined, to spoil" (DEDR 1942): to fall out of its proper state.
