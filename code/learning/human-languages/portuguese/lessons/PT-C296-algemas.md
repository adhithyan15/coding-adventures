## You'll want to know: as algemas
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C296-ALGEMAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**as algemas** — "the handcuffs".

> O agente pôs as algemas ao suspeito.
>
> *The officer put the handcuffs on the suspect.*

Usually plural; one cuff is *a algema*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*algema* comes from Arabic *al-jāmiʿa* "the fetter", literally "that which joins" (EtymDB).
