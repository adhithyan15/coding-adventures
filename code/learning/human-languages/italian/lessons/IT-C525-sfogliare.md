## You'll want to know: sfogliare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C525-LIBRERIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sfogliare** — "to leaf through".

> Sfoglio una rivista mentre aspetto.
>
> *I leaf through a magazine while I wait.*

Built on *foglio* "sheet": turning the sheets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sfogliare* is built from *s-* and *foglio* "sheet of paper", inherited from Latin *folium* "a leaf" (Lewis & Short).
