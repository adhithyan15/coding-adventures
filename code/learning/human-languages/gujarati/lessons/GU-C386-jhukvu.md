## You'll want to know: ઝૂકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C386-AASTHA386-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂકવું** — *jhūkvũ* — "to give in; to bend".

> જો આપણે બધા સાથે રહીશું તો સરકારે ઝૂકવું પડશે.
>
> *jo āpaṇe badhā sāthe rahīśũ to sarkāre jhūkvũ paḍśe.*
>
> If we all stand together, the government will have to give in.

Past (he …): તે છેવટે ઝૂક્યો, *te chevṭe jhūkyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂકવું** (*jhūkvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to bend", under a reconstructed *jhukkati*, "stoops" (CDIAL 5399).
