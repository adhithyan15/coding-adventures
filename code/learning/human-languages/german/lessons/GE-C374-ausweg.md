## You'll want to know: der Ausweg
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C374-ENTSCHLUSS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ausweg** — "the way out".

> Es gibt immer einen Ausweg.
>
> *There is always a way out.*

Plural: *die Auswege*. *Der letzte Ausweg* — the last resort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ausweg* joins *aus* "out" to *Weg* "way", inherited from Old High German *weg* and Proto-Germanic *wegaz* "road, path" (EtymDB).
