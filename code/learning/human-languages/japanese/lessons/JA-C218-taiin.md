## You'll want to know: たいいん

**たいいん** — *taiin* — "leaving hospital".

> **たいいんは あしたですか。** — *taiin wa ashita desu ka* — "Do you leave hospital tomorrow?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいいん** (*taiin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "to withdraw" (Middle Chinese *thwojH*) and *in*, "courtyard, institution" (*hjwenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
