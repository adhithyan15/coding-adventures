## You'll want to know: 变心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C462-SURPRISE462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**变心** — *biànxīn* — "to fall out of love; to stop being faithful".

> **他没有变心他还爱你** *tā méiyǒu biànxīn tā hái ài nǐ* — He hasn't fallen out of love with you; he still loves you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**变心** (*biànxīn*) is built from 变 'to change' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjenH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to change'.
