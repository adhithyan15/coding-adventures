Kannada, chapter 312: Must, Must Not, and Because.
3 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ಹೋಗಬೇಕು (hōgabēku) — must, should, must not.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I want tea". (nanage cahā bēku.) Then "Yesterday it rained. So I stayed at home." (ninne maḷe bantu. hāgāgi nānu maneyallē idde.)
[pause 1 second]
ಬೇಕು says a thing is needed. Put it on a verb, and the action is needed.

You'll want to know: -ಬೇಕು on a verb.
ನಾನು ನಾಳೆ ಬೇಗ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು. nānu nāḷe bēga hōgabēku. — "I must go early tomorrow."
The verb drops its -ಉ and takes -ಅ: ಹೋಗು becomes ಹೋಗ-, then ಬೇಕು: ಹೋಗಬೇಕು (hōgabēku). In the same way ಮಾಡಬೇಕು, ಓದಬೇಕು, ಬರಬೇಕು. A verb in -ಇ or -ಎ adds -ಯ first: ಕುಡಿ becomes ಕುಡಿಯಬೇಕು, ತೊಳೆ becomes ತೊಳೆಯಬೇಕು.
ಬೇಕು stays the same for every person: ನಾನು ಹೋಗಬೇಕು (hōgabēku), ಅವಳು ಹೋಗಬೇಕು (hōgabēku), ನಾವು ಹೋಗಬೇಕು (hōgabēku). Said about someone else, it is advice:
ನೀವು ವೈದ್ಯರನ್ನು ನೋಡಬೇಕು. nīvu vaidyarannu nōḍabēku. — "You should see a doctor."
ವೈದ್ಯರನ್ನು (vaidyarannu, "the doctor"): ವೈದ್ಯ with the respectful -ರು, as the one you see.

You'll want to know: -ಬಾರದು, must not.
ರಾತ್ರಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಕಾಫಿ ಕುಡಿಯಬಾರದು. rātri tumbā kāphi kuḍiyabāradu. — "You shouldn't drink a lot of coffee at night."
ಬಾರದು sits where ಬೇಕು sat: "must not drink" is ಕುಡಿಯಬಾರದು; "must not go" is ಹೋಗಬಾರದು. With no person named, it is a rule for anyone, and the right shape for a notice: ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಕಸ ಹಾಕಬಾರದು (illi kasa hākabāradu, "do not throw rubbish here").

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You should see a doctor" — nīvu vaidyarannu nōḍabēku]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "We must buy a ticket" — nāvu ṭikeṭ kharīdisabēku]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You shouldn't drink a lot of coffee at night" — rātri tumbā kāphi kuḍiyabāradu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I must go"? (ಹೋಗಬೇಕು (hōgabēku).) "I must drink"? (ಕುಡಿಯಬೇಕು.) "Must not go"? (ಹೋಗಬಾರದು.) Does ಬೇಕು change for "she" or "we"? (No.) Which sentence had the same ಬೇಕು with no verb? (ನನಗೆ ಚಹಾ ಬೇಕು.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
ಬಂದ ಕಾರಣ (banda kāraṇa) — giving the reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "a reason" (kāraṇa) and "because" (yākendare). Then "You should see a doctor." (nīvu vaidyarannu nōḍabēku.)

You'll want to know: the reason first, with ಕಾರಣ.
ಮಳೆ ಬಂದ ಕಾರಣ ನಾನು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಇದ್ದೆ. maḷe banda kāraṇa nānu maneyallē idde. — "Because it rained, I stayed at home."
ಬಂದ is the past without its ending, the piece inside ಬಂದಾಗ and ಮಾಡಿದ ನಂತರ. With ಕಾರಣ, "reason", after it, it reads "(for) the reason (that) it came". Any verb fits:
ಅವನಿಗೆ ಜ್ವರ ಇದ್ದ ಕಾರಣ ಅವನು ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ. avanige jvara idda kāraṇa avanu baralilla. — "Because he had a fever, he did not come."
ಅವನಿಗೆ (avanige, "to him") is ಅವನು in the "to" shape, as ನನಗೆ is ನಾನು.

You'll want to know: the reason after, with ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ.
ನಾನು ಇವತ್ತು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಇರುತ್ತೇನೆ. ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ ನನಗೆ ಜ್ವರ ಇದೆ. nānu ivattu maneyallē iruttēne. yākendare nanage jvara ide. — "I'll stay at home today, because I have a fever."
ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ opens a second sentence, and that sentence is the reason. In writing you will meet its twin, ಏಕೆಂದರೆ. So there are two orders, and both are good Kannada: the reason first, ending in -ಅ ಕಾರಣ, then the result; or the result first, then ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ and the reason.
When someone asks you why, answer in either order:
ನೀನು ನಿನ್ನೆ ಯಾಕೆ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ? nīnu ninne yāke baralilla? — "Why didn't you come yesterday?"

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Because it rained, I stayed at home" — maḷe banda kāraṇa nānu maneyallē idde]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Because he had a fever, he did not come" — avanige jvara idda kāraṇa avanu baralilla]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I'll stay at home today, because I have a fever" — nānu ivattu maneyallē iruttēne. yākendare nanage jvara ide.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Why didn't you come yesterday?" — nīnu ninne yāke baralilla?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Because it rained", reason first? (ಮಳೆ ಬಂದ ಕಾರಣ (banda kāraṇa).) Which word opens a reason that comes after? (ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ, or ಏಕೆಂದರೆ in writing.) "Why didn't you come?" (ಯಾಕೆ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ?)
