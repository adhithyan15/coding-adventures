# لذلك، فـ (li-dhālika, fa-) — so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C40-LIANNA-01, AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-02, AR-SCRIPT-C285-HAMZA-WAW-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I have a question". (*ʿIndī suʾāl*.) Then "because".
(*Li-anna*.)

[PAUSE 1s] *Li-anna* puts the reason after the claim. A story often wants
the opposite order: first the reason, then what came of it.

## Grammar Lens: li-dhālika, after the reason
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C174-SABAB174-03, AR-LEX-C283-MADI283-01, AR-LEX-C147-QUAL147-02, AR-LEX-C170-WAQT170-01] -->

> **نمت مبكرا لأنني كنت متعبا.** — *nimtu mubakkiran li-annanī kuntu
> mutʿaban* — I went to bed early because I was tired.

> **كنت متعبا، لذلك نمت مبكرا.** — *kuntu mutʿaban, li-dhālika nimtu
> mubakkiran* — I was tired, so I went to bed early.

The same two facts. With *li-anna* the claim comes first; with *li-dhālika*,
"therefore, so", the reason comes first and the result follows. The *-an* on
*mutʿaban* after *kuntu* is *kāna*'s doing; chapter two hundred and
eighty-nine comes back to it.

## Grammar Lens: fa-, the short "so"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C285-FA-SO-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C38-WA-01, AR-LEX-C255-ACT255-01, AR-LEX-C91-TOWN91-05] -->

**فـ** — *fa-* — does the same job in one letter. Like *wa-*, it is written
onto the next word, with no space:

> **تأخر القطار، فأخذنا الحافلة.** — *taʾakhkhara al-qiṭār, fa-akhadhnā
> al-ḥāfila* — The train was late, so we took the bus.

*Wa-* adds the next thing; *fa-* says the next thing came of the first.
*Li-dhālika* is the heavier of the two, and it suits a reason that needs
weight: *kāna al-imtiḥān ṣaʿban, li-dhālika qaraʾtu kathīran*, the exam was
hard, so I read a lot.
