## You'll want to know: চিরকাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C329-SHESHPATA329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**চিরকাল** — *chirôkāl* — "forever; for all time".

> এই গল্পটা আমি চিরকাল মনে রাখব।
>
> *ei gôlpoṭā āmi chirôkāl mône rākhbô.*
>
> I will remember this story forever.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চিরকাল** (*chirôkāl*) joins Sanskrit *cira*, 'long, lasting a long time' (Monier-Williams), to *kāla*, time: for a long, lasting time.
