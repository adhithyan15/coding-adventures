## You'll want to know: दोला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C492-JANMA492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दोला** — *dolā* — "a swing; a cradle".

> शिशुः दोलायां शेते।
>
> *śiśuḥ dolāyāṃ śete.*
>
> The baby lies in the cradle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दोला** (*dolā*) MW gives "litter, hammock, palanquin, swing"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
