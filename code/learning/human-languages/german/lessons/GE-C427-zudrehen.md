## You'll want to know: zudrehen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C427-KLIMA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**zudrehen** — "to shut off (a tap, the heating), to turn down".

> Vor dem Schlafen dreht sie die Heizung zu.
>
> *Before going to sleep she turns off the heating.*

Separable: *ich drehe zu*. Its opposite is *aufdrehen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zudrehen* is built from *zu* 'shut, closed' and *drehen* 'to turn', an inherited Germanic verb (EtymDB).
