## You'll want to know: सुधरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C368-VIKAS368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सुधरना** — *sudharnā* — "to get better, to mend one's ways".

> दवा के बाद उसकी तबीयत सुधर रही है।
>
> *davā ke bād uskī tabīyat sudhar rahī hai.*
>
> After the medicine his health is getting better.

Past (he …): वह सुधर गया, *vah sudhar gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुधरना** (*sudharnā*) is inherited from *\*śuddhakāra*, "making clean", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12521).
