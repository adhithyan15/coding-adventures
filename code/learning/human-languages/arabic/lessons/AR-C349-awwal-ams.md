## You'll want to know: أول أمس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C349-MARRA349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أول أمس** — *awwal ams* — "the day before yesterday".

> وصل خالي أول أمس.
>
> *waṣala khālī awwal ams.*
>
> My uncle arrived the day before yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أول أمس** (*awwal ams*) is "the first of yesterday": *awwal*, first, is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-w-l* (Lane), before the taught *ams*, yesterday.
