## You'll want to know: कामळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C225-PACK225-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कामळी** — *kāmḷī* — "a woollen blanket".

> रात ठंडी है, हूं कामळी लेसूं।
>
> *rāt ṭhaṇḍī hai, hū̃ kāmḷī lesū̃.*
>
> The night is cold, so I'll take a blanket.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कामळी** (*kāmḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kambala*, "a woollen blanket"; CDIAL lists Hindi *kamlī*, "a small blanket", and Gujarati *kāmḷī*, "a blanket". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2771).
