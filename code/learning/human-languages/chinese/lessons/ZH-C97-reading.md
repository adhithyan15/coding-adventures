## Reading — the pinyin, after you have tried
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-READ-A1-PASSAGE-01, ZH-SKILL-READ-WORDS, ZH-SCRIPT-TAI-01, ZH-LEX-C93-TAIHAOLE-01] -->

> *jīntiān shì xīngqītiān*
> *wǒ hé māma dōu zài jiā*
> *māma zuò fàn*
> *wǒ kàn shū*
> *wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ*
> *yě xiǎng chī fàn*
> *māma shuō*
> *fàn tài duō le*
> *wǒ shuō*
> *tài hǎo le*
> *xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn*
> *wǒmen qù mǎi shū*
> *yīnwèi wǒ hěn xǐhuan kàn shū*

One line per clause, and no punctuation, as this book prints Chinese: the line breaks carry the pauses. 饭太多了 is 太 … 了 as a complaint, too much rice; 太好了 a line later is the same frame as delight.
