## You'll want to know: o remorso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C480-REMORSO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o remorso** — "remorse".

> Depois da mentira, ficou com remorsos.
>
> *After the lie, he felt remorse.*

Often plural: *ter remorsos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remorso* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *remorsus* "a biting back", from Latin *remordēre* "to bite again" (Lewis & Short): a conscience that bites.
