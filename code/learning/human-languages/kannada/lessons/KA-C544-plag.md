## You'll want to know: ಪ್ಲಗ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C544-VIDYUT544-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ಲಗ್** — *plag* — "a plug".

> ಫೋನಿನ ಚಾರ್ಜರ್ ಪ್ಲಗ್ ಸಡಿಲವಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *phōnina cārjar plag saḍilavāgide.*
>
> The phone charger's plug is loose.

ಪ್ಲಗ್ ಹಾಕು, to plug in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ಲಗ್** (*plag*) is a loan from English *plug*.
