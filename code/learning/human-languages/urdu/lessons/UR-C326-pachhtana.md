## You'll want to know: پچھتانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C326-LANAT326-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پچھتانا** — *pachhtānā* — "to regret; to repent".

> بعد میں بادشاہ پچھتایا۔
>
> *bād meṅ bādshāh pachhtāyā.*
>
> Later the king regretted it.

Past (he …): وہ پچھتایا, *voh pachhtāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پچھتانا** (*pachhtānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pachtānā* under *\*paścōttāpa*, a burning afterwards, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8010): the pain that comes after the deed.
