## You'll want to know: મોકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C381-PASTAVO381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**મોકો** — *moko* — "an opening; a chance".

> જો મારી પાસે આ મોકો હોત તો હું તરત તે લેત.
>
> *jo mārī pāse ā moko hot to hũ tarat te let.*
>
> If I had this opportunity, I would take it at once.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મોકો** (*moko*) is usually traced to Arabic *mauqaʿ*, "a place, an occasion", through Persian.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
