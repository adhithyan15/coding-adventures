## You'll want to know: ちず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C196-ADDR196-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちず** — *chizu* — "map".

> **ちずで みちを さがします。** — *chizu de michi o sagashimasu* — "I look for the way on a map."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちず** (*chizu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chi*, "ground, earth" (Middle Chinese *dijH*) and *zu*, "drawing, plan" (*du*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
