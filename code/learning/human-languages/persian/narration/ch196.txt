You'll want to know: ناودان.
ناودان — nâvdân — "a drainpipe".

The word, taken apart.
nâvdân is built from nâv, "trough, channel", and -dân, "holder": the channel for water. Both are native Persian words, inherited rather than borrowed.

You'll want to know: لبه.
لبه — labe — "an edge, a rim".

The word, taken apart.
labe is built from lab, "lip", with the ending -e: the lip of a thing, its rim. lab is inherited; EtymDB links it to the Indo-European root leb-, "lip".

Lesson 5 of 7.
همکف (hamkaf) — the ground floor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the surroundings, then the Persian for an edge.

You'll want to know: همکف.
همکف — hamkaf — "the ground floor".

The word, taken apart.
hamkaf is ham-, "same", with kaf, "floor": level with the floor. ham- is native Persian; kaf is usually taken as Arabic kaff, "palm, flat of the hand" (root k-f-f, Lane).
