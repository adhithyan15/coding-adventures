## You'll want to know: wàibì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C419-ARRIVE419-03]; assesses=[] -->

*wàibì* — "foreign currency".

> *zhèr kěyǐ huàn wàibì* — You can change foreign money here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wàibì* is built from 外 'outside' and *bì* 'money', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwajH* and *bjiejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as gifts of silk.
