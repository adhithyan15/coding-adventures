## You'll want to know: шампанское
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C315-UGOSHCHENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**шампанское** (*shampánskoye*) — "champagne, sparkling wine".

> В полночь мы открыли шампанское.
>
> *V pólnoch my otkrýli shampánskoye.*
>
> At midnight we opened the champagne.

An adjective serving as a noun, neuter: *бокал шампанского*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*шампанское* is built on the name of the French wine region where it is made, with the Russian adjective ending *-ское*; EtymDB lists no parent.
