5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
झील (jhīl) — a lake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a mountain, then the Hindi for a river.

You'll want to know: झील.
झील — jhīl — "a lake".

The word, taken apart.
झील (jhīl) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jhilla, "swamp, lake", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5392).

Lesson 3 of 5.
जंगल (jaṅgal) — a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a lake, then the Hindi for the sea.

You'll want to know: जंगल.
जंगल — jaṅgal — "a forest".

The word, taken apart.
जंगल (jaṅgal) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jāṅgala, "arid, sparse in trees" (CDIAL 5177). CDIAL notes a likely non-Aryan origin. The sense moved from "waste land" to "forest", and English took it back as jungle.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पेड़ (peṛ) — a tree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the sea, then the Hindi for a forest.

You'll want to know: पेड़.
पेड़ — peṛ — "a tree".

The word, taken apart.
पेड़ (peṛ) is inherited, but only as far back as a reconstruction. CDIAL's addenda set up peḍa, "tree", from Punjabi and Hindi peṛ (CDIAL 8377a), and give it no Sanskrit source. It is a different word from peṛ, "lump" (CDIAL 8377).
