Bengali, chapter 359: Excuses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
অজুহাত (ôjuhāt) — an excuse; a pretext.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for ordinary, then the Bengali for to mix up.

You'll want to know: অজুহাত.
অজুহাত — ôjuhāt — "an excuse; a pretext".
দেরির অজুহাত দিও না। derir ôjuhāt dio nā. Don't make excuses for being late.

The word, taken apart.
অজুহাত (ôjuhāt) is usually traced to Arabic wujūhāt, a plural meaning reasons or aspects, through Persian: a reason offered to get out of something.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ভান (bhān) — a pretence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to mix up, then the Bengali for an excuse.

You'll want to know: ভান.
ভান — bhān — "a pretence".
অরুণ না শোনার ভান করল। ôrun nā shonār bhān kôrlô. Arun pretended not to hear.

The word, taken apart.
ভান (bhān) is Sanskrit bhāna, appearance (Monier-Williams; CDIAL 9451 lists Bengali bhān). Bengali uses it for a false appearance: ভান করা, to pretend.

Lesson 3 of 5.
কৈফিয়ত (koiphiyôt) — a demanded explanation; an account of oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an excuse, then the Bengali for a pretence.

You'll want to know: কৈফিয়ত.
কৈফিয়ত — koiphiyôt — "a demanded explanation; an account of oneself".
ম্যানেজার দেরির জন্য কৈফিয়ত চাইলেন। mænejār derir jônyô koiphiyôt chāilen. The manager asked for an explanation for the delay.

The word, taken apart.
কৈফিয়ত (koiphiyôt) is Persian kaifiyat, how things stand, an account, usually traced to Arabic kayfiyya. In Bengali it is the account you are asked to give of yourself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
পাশ কাটানো (pāsh kāṭāno) — to sidestep; to dodge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pretence, then the Bengali for a demanded explanation.

You'll want to know: পাশ কাটানো.
পাশ কাটানো — pāsh kāṭāno — "to sidestep; to dodge".
মন্ত্রী প্রশ্নটা পাশ কাটালেন। môntrī prôshnoṭā pāsh kāṭālen. The minister sidestepped the question.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বিষয়টা পাশ কাটালেন, tini bishôyṭā pāsh kāṭālen.

The word, taken apart.
পাশ কাটানো (pāsh kāṭāno) is পাশ, the side (CDIAL 8118 lists Bengali pās 'near'), with কাটানো, to cut: to cut past at the side, to go round a question instead of through it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
গ্রহণযোগ্য (grôhônjogyô) — acceptable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a demanded explanation, then the Bengali for to sidestep.

You'll want to know: গ্রহণযোগ্য.
গ্রহণযোগ্য — grôhônjogyô — "acceptable".
তোমার অজুহাতটা গ্রহণযোগ্য নয়। tomār ôjuhātṭā grôhônjogyô nôy. Your excuse is not acceptable.

The word, taken apart.
গ্রহণযোগ্য (grôhônjogyô) is Sanskrit grahaṇa, taking or acceptance, with yogya, fit for (Monier-Williams): fit to be accepted. An excuse, a proposal or a reason can be গ্রহণযোগ্য; the compound itself is not in the dictionary.
