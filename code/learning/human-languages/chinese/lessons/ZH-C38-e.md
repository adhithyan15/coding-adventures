## You'll want to know: è
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C38-EAT38-04]; assesses=[] -->

*è* — "hungry".

> *wǒ hěn è* — I'm starving.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*è* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ngaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'not full'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
