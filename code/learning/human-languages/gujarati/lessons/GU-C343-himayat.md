## You'll want to know: હિમાયત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C343-TEKO343-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હિમાયત** — *himāyat* — "advocacy".

> મીરા છોકરીઓના અભ્યાસની હિમાયત કરે છે.
>
> *Mīrā chokrīonā abhyāsnī himāyat kare che.*
>
> Mira speaks up for girls' education.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હિમાયત** (*himāyat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥimāyat*, "protection", through Persian; Gujarati uses it for speaking up for a cause.
