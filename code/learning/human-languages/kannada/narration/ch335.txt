Kannada, chapter 335: Making a Newspaper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಪಾದಕ (sampādaka) — an editor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a baby shower, then the Kannada for a successor.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಪಾದಕ.
ಸಂಪಾದಕ — sampādaka — "an editor".
ಸಂಪಾದಕರು ಆ ಸುದ್ದಿಯನ್ನು ಓದಿ ಒಪ್ಪಿದರು. sampādakaru ā suddiyannu ōdi oppidaru. The editor read that news report and approved it.
Plural: ಸಂಪಾದಕರು, sampādakaru.
ಸಂಪಾದಿಸು is to edit, and also to earn.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಪಾದಕ (sampādaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit sampādaka, "procuring, bringing about" (Monier-Williams); the sense "an editor", one who puts a work together, is modern.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಲೇಖನ (lēkhana) — an article; a piece of writing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a successor, then the Kannada for an editor.

You'll want to know: ಲೇಖನ.
ಲೇಖನ — lēkhana — "an article; a piece of writing".
ಅವಳ ಲೇಖನ ಭಾನುವಾರದ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂತು. avaḷa lēkhana bhānuvārada patrikeyalli bantu. Her article came out in the Sunday paper.
Plural: ಲೇಖನಗಳು, lēkhanagaḷu.
Not to be confused with ಲೇಖನಿ, a pen. ಲೇಖಕ is a writer.

The word, taken apart.
ಲೇಖನ (lēkhana) is borrowed from Sanskrit lekhana, "scratching, writing" (Monier-Williams), from the root likh, "to scratch, to write".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮುಖಪುಟ (mukhapuṭa) — the front page.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an editor, then the Kannada for an article.

You'll want to know: ಮುಖಪುಟ.
ಮುಖಪುಟ — mukhapuṭa — "the front page".
ಆ ಅಪಘಾತದ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಮುಖಪುಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು. ā apaghātada suddi mukhapuṭadalli ittu. The news of that accident was on the front page.
ಮುಖ, face, and ಪುಟ, a page: the face page. A website has one too.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಖಪುಟ (mukhapuṭa) is a Kannada compound built from two Sanskrit words: mukha, "face, front" (CDIAL 10158), and puṭa, "a fold" (CDIAL 8253), which Kannada uses for "a page".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕರಪತ್ರ (karapatra) — a leaflet; a pamphlet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an article, then the Kannada for the front page.

You'll want to know: ಕರಪತ್ರ.
ಕರಪತ್ರ — karapatra — "a leaflet; a pamphlet".
ಹುಡುಗರು ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದಲ್ಲಿ ಕರಪತ್ರ ಹಂಚಿದರು. huḍugaru bas nildāṇadalli karapatra hañcidaru. The boys handed out leaflets at the bus stand.
Plural: ಕರಪತ್ರಗಳು, karapatragaḷu.
ಕರ, hand, and ಪತ್ರ, a leaf: a sheet put into people's hands.

The word, taken apart.
ಕರಪತ್ರ (karapatra) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of kara, "the hand" (Monier-Williams), and pattra, "a leaf, the leaf of a book" (CDIAL 7733).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕ (prakāśaka) — a publisher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the front page, then the Kannada for a leaflet.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕ.
ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕ — prakāśaka — "a publisher".
ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕರು ಅಜ್ಜನ ಕಥೆಗಳನ್ನು ಪುಸ್ತಕವಾಗಿ ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸಿದರು. prakāśakaru ajjana kathegaḷannu pustakavāgi prakaṭisidaru. The publisher brought out Grandfather's stories as a book.
Plural: ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕರು, prakāśakaru.
ಪ್ರಕಾಶ is light: a publisher brings a work into the light.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಕಾಶಕ (prakāśaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit prakāśaka, "bright, shining, making known" (Monier-Williams), from prakāśa, "light, publicity".
