Lesson 1 of 6.
la pauvreté — the poverty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the prejudice, then the French for the stereotype.

You'll want to know: la pauvreté.
la pauvreté — "the poverty".
Beaucoup de familles vivent dans la pauvreté. Many families live in poverty.
From pauvre.

The word, taken apart.
pauvreté is inherited through Old French poverté from Latin paupertās "poverty" (EtymDB), from pauper "poor" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: l'inégalité.
l'inégalité — "the inequality".
Les inégalités entre riches et pauvres augmentent. Inequalities between rich and poor are growing.
Often plural: les inégalités. Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
inégalité is built from in- "not" and égalité "equality", which rests on égal, from Latin aequālis "equal" (Lewis & Short).
