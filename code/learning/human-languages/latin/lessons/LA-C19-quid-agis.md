# quid agis? — how are you?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[LA-PHONO-VALE-01, LA-GRAMMAR-VALE-03, LA-PHONO-ITA-NON-01, LA-LEX-ITA-NON-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Roman comedy gives you a short wellbeing question: **quid agis?**

## Why it's said this way: one attested question
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-PRAGMATICS-QUID-AGIS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**Quid agis?** literally means “What are you doing?” In the comedies of Plautus
and Terence, it also works like “How are you?” or “How is it going?”

Say it in two pieces: **quid** … **agis**. Keep Latin **qu** as *kw*.

That is the whole step. A second attested question comes next, after this one is
comfortable.
