Portuguese, chapter 356: Deixar o emprego.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
despedir — to fire, to dismiss; (despedir-se) to say goodbye.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the candidate, then the Portuguese for to hire.

You'll want to know: despedir.
despedir — "to fire, to dismiss; (despedir-se) to say goodbye".
A fábrica despediu cinquenta trabalhadores. The factory fired fifty workers.
Despedir-se de alguém — to say goodbye to someone. Conjugated despeço, despedes, despede.

The word, taken apart.
despedir is formed from Latin dē and expetere "to seek after, to ask for" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on petere "to seek"; it first meant "to ask leave to go", and then "to send away".

Lesson 2 of 5.
a demissão — the resignation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to hire, then the Portuguese for to fire.

You'll want to know: a demissão.
a demissão — "the resignation".
Ela apresentou a demissão ao chefe. She handed in her resignation to the boss.
Apresentar a demissão — to hand in your notice. In Brazil, demissão is also being fired.

The word, taken apart.
demissão is a learned borrowing from Latin dīmissiō "a sending out, a sending away" (Lewis & Short), from dīmittere "to send away", built on mittere "to send".

Lesson 3 of 5.
o despedimento — the dismissal, the sacking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to fire, then the Portuguese for the resignation.

You'll want to know: o despedimento.
o despedimento — "the dismissal, the sacking".
Houve muitos despedimentos este ano na fábrica. There were many dismissals at the factory this year.
European Portuguese; in Brazil a demissão. Despedimento coletivo — collective dismissal.

The word, taken apart.
despedimento is built on despedir "to dismiss" with -mento; the verb is formed from Latin dē and expetere "to seek after" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
desempregado — unemployed, out of work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the resignation, then the Portuguese for the dismissal.

You'll want to know: desempregado.
desempregado — "unemployed, out of work".
O meu primo está desempregado há seis meses. My cousin has been out of work for six months.
Also a noun: os desempregados.

The word, taken apart.
desempregado is built from des- "not" and empregado "employed", the participle of empregar, which is inherited from Latin implicāre "to fold into, to involve" (EtymDB).
