## You'll want to know: दफ्तर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C198-OFFICE198-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दफ्तर** — *daphtar* — "an office".

> दफ्तर आज बंद है।
>
> *daphtar āj band hai.*
>
> The office is closed today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दफ्तर** (*daphtar*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *daftar*, "a register".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
