## You'll want to know: వరం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C371-MAYA371-04]; assesses=[] -->

**వరం** — *varaṁ* — "a boon; a blessing".

> మాంత్రికుడు రాజుకు ఒక వరం ఇచ్చాడు.
>
> *māntrikuḍu rājuku oka varaṁ iccāḍu.*
>
> The sorcerer granted the king a boon.

Plural: వరాలు, *varālu*.

Also in daily speech: అది నాకు వరం, that is a blessing for me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వరం** (*varaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vara*, "choice, a boon" (CDIAL 11308).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
