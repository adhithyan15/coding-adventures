## You'll want to know: खलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C461-SATPURUSA461-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खलः** — *khalaḥ* — "a scoundrel".

> खलः सज्जनं पीडयति।
>
> *khalaḥ sajjanaṃ pīḍayati.*
>
> A scoundrel torments a good man.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खलः** (*khalaḥ*) MW's first *khala* is "a threshing-floor"; a later sense is "a mischievous man", and for it MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
