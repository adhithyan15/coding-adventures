## You'll want to know: বাথরুম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C405-BATHRUM405-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বাথরুম** — *bāthrum* — "a bathroom".

> আমাদের ঘরের বাথরুমে গরম জল আছে।
>
> *āmāder ghôrer bāthrume gôrôm jôl āchhe.*
>
> There is hot water in our room's bathroom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাথরুম** (*bāthrum*) is borrowed from English *bathroom*; the English th is said with থ.
