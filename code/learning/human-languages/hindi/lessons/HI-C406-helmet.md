## You'll want to know: हेलमेट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C406-TAIKSI406-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हेलमेट** — *helmeṭ* — "a helmet".

> मोटरसाइकिल पर हमेशा हेलमेट पहनिए।
>
> *moṭarsāikil par hameśā helmeṭ pahaniye.*
>
> Always wear a helmet on a motorcycle.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हेलमेट** (*helmeṭ*) is borrowed from English *helmet*.
