Punjabi, chapter 264: Across the Line.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਾਰ (pār) — across; on the far side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for right-hand, then the Punjabi for left-hand.

You'll want to know: ਪਾਰ.
ਪਾਰ — pār — "across; on the far side".
ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਪੁਲ ਦੇ ਪਾਰ ਹੈ। saṭeshan pul de pār hai. The station is across the bridge.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਾਰ (pār) is inherited from Sanskrit pāra, "further bank, furthest end"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pār, "beyond", under it (CDIAL 8100).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

ਲੰਘਣਾ — laṅghṇā — "to pass through; to go past".
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਤੋਂ ਲੰਘਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ roz bāzār ton laṅghdā hāṁ. I pass through the market every day.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪੁਲ ਤੋਂ ਲੰਘਿਆ, maiṁ pul ton laṅghiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੰਘਣਾ (laṅghṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit laṅghayati, "leaps over"; CDIAL lists Punjabi laṅghṇā, "to pass", under it (CDIAL 10905).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਫਾਟਕ (phāṭak) — a big gate; a level-crossing gate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for across, then the Punjabi for to pass through.

You'll want to know: ਫਾਟਕ.
ਫਾਟਕ — phāṭak — "a big gate; a level-crossing gate".
ਜਦੋਂ ਰੇਲ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ ਤਾਂ ਫਾਟਕ ਬੰਦ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ। jadoṁ rel āundī hai tāṁ phāṭak band hundā hai. When the train comes, the crossing gate is closed.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਫਾਟਕ (phāṭak) is inherited from a reconstructed phāṭṭakka, "gate, door"; CDIAL lists Punjabi phāṭak, "gate", under it (CDIAL 9069).

You'll want to know: ਖੰਭਾ.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੰਭਾ (khambhā) is inherited from Sanskrit skambha, "prop, pillar"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khambhā, "wooden prop, post", under it (CDIAL 13639).
