## You'll want to know: secus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C391-ETIAMSI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**secus** — "differently, otherwise".

> Rēs secus accidit atque spērābāmus.
>
> *Things turned out otherwise than we hoped.*

*Secus* is an adverb: *nōn secus ac*, not otherwise than, in the same way as; *haud secus*, in no other way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*secus* comes from the root of *sequor* "to follow" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): coming after, so less well, otherwise.
