## You'll want to know: глина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C502-CRAFTS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**глина** (*glína*) — "clay".

> Из глины делают кувшины и чашки.
>
> *Iz glíny délayut kuvshíny i cháshki.*
>
> Jugs and cups are made from clay.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*глина* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *glina*, which EtymDB traces to PIE *gleh₁i-n-*.
