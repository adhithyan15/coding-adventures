## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-16

### Changed (review fixes)

- `TokenizeLine` refuses a line over `DefaultMaxLineBytes` (1 MiB) before lexing
  (`ErrLineTooLong`); `TokenizeLineMax` takes another limit.
- `DEAD` is replaced by the exact whole-line tokens `DEAD_OPEN`, `DEAD_WHOLE` and
  `DEAD_RESUMED`; `RESUMED` no longer accepts `???` as a name.
- `DecodeString` documents that its result can hold NUL and invalid UTF-8.
