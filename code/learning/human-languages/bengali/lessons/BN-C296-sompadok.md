## You'll want to know: সম্পাদক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C296-SONGBAD296-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্পাদক** — *sômpādôk* — "an editor".

> সম্পাদক প্রতিবেদনটা পড়ে শিরোনাম বদলালেন।
>
> *sômpādôk prôtibedônṭā pôṛe shironām bôdlālen.*
>
> The editor read the report and changed the headline.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্পাদক** (*sômpādôk*) is Sanskrit *sampādaka*, 'procuring, bringing about' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'an editor', the one who gets a paper made, is the modern one.
