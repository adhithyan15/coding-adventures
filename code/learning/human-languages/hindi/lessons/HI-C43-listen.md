## You'll want to know: सुनना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-LISTEN]; assesses=[] -->

**सुनना** (*sunnā*) — "to listen, to hear".

Hindi does not separate listening from hearing; context does the work that a second verb does in English.

Remove infinitive **-ना** to find the stem **सुन-**. Add the present endings you
already know: **सुनता** (*suntā*, masculine), **सुनती** (*suntī*, feminine),
and **सुनते** (*sunte*, respectful or plural). Later you will say **आप सुनते
हैं** — "you listen."
