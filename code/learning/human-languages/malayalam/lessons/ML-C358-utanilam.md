## You'll want to know: ഉടനീളം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C358-PANDUPANDU358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉടനീളം** — *uṭanīḷaṁ* — "throughout".

> യാത്രയിൽ ഉടനീളം കുട്ടി ഉറങ്ങി.
>
> *yātrayil uṭanīḷaṁ kuṭṭi uṟaṅṅi.*
>
> The child slept throughout the journey.

It follows a noun: വർഷം ഉടനീളം, all through the year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉടനീളം** (*uṭanīḷaṁ*) is native Dravidian. Its second part is നീളം (*nīḷaṁ*), "length" (DEDR 3692): along the whole length of something. The origin of the first part is not settled.
