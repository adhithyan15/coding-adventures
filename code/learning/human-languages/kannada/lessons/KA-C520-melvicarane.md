## You'll want to know: ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C520-TANDA520-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆ** — *mēlvicāraṇe* — "supervision".

> ಎಂಜಿನಿಯರ್ ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲಸ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *eñjiniyar mēlvicāraṇeyalli kelasa naḍeyitu.*
>
> The work went on under the engineer's supervision.

ಮೇಲ್, over + ವಿಚಾರಣೆ, looking into: overseeing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೇಲ್ವಿಚಾರಣೆ** (*mēlvicāraṇe*) joins native *mēl*, "above, over" (DEDR 5086), and Sanskrit *vicāraṇa*, "consideration, examination" (Monier-Williams).
