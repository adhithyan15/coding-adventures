## You'll want to know: reembolsar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C663-TEMPORADA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**reembolsar** — "to refund, to pay back".

> Le reembolsamos el dinero en tres días.
>
> *We will refund your money within three days.*

Close to *devolver el dinero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reembolsar* is built from *re-* "back" and *embolsar* "to put in the purse", on *bolsa*, from Late Latin *bursa*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
