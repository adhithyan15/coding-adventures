## You'll want to know: a investigação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C294-INVESTIGAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a investigação** — "the investigation; research".

> A investigação durou dois anos.
>
> *The investigation lasted two years.*

At a university it means research.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investigação* is built on *investigar*, from Latin *investīgāre* "to track down" (EtymDB), from *vestīgium* "a footprint".
