## You'll want to know: очередной
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C277-OCHEREDNOY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**очередной** (*ocherednóy*) — "next (in a series), yet another".

> Он рассказал очередную смешную историю.
>
> *On rasskazál ocherednúyu smeshnúyu istóriyu.*
>
> He told yet another funny story.

The next in a regular series, or "one more", often a little ironic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*очередной* is built on *очередь* "a queue, a turn", from *черёд*, *череда* "a row, a turn", which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic *čerda* "a file, a herd"; EtymDB lists no parent for *очередной* itself.
