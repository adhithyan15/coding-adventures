# ಮಬ್ಬು (mabbu) — haze; dimness

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C486-HINGARU486-01, KA-LEX-C486-HINGARU486-02] -->

## You'll want to know: ಮಬ್ಬು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C486-HINGARU486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಬ್ಬು** — *mabbu* — "haze; dimness".

> ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ನಗರದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮಬ್ಬು ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *beḷagge nagarada mēle mabbu ittu.*
>
> In the morning there was a haze over the city.

Also the dimness of dusk: ಮಬ್ಬು ಬೆಳಕು, dim light.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಬ್ಬು** (*mabbu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4728 lists Kannada *marvu*, *mabbu*, "darkness, gloom, dimness".
