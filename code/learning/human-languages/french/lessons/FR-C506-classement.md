## You'll want to know: le classement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C506-RESULTATS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le classement** — "ranking, league table".

> Notre équipe est troisième au classement.
>
> *Our team is third in the league.*

Built on *classer* "to sort, to rank".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le classement* is built on *classer* "to rank", from *classe*, a learned borrowing from Latin *classis* "a class, a division" (Lewis & Short).
