## You'll want to know: امداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C299-IMDAD299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**امداد** — *imdād* — "aid; relief supplies".

> حکومت نے امداد بھیجی۔
>
> *hukūmat ne imdād bhejī.*
>
> The government sent aid.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امداد** (*imdād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *imdād*, "a reinforcing, a supply". It is feminine: **امداد بھیجی**.
