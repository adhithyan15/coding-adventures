## You'll want to know: ਅਭਿਆਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C359-STUDY359-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਭਿਆਸ** — *abhiās* — "practice; drill".

> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਅਭਿਆਸ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ har roz abhiās kardā hāṁ.*
>
> I practise every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਭਿਆਸ** (*abhiās*) is taken over from Sanskrit *abhyāsa*, "repetition", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
