# He and that — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-HE-01, MW-LEX-C47-HEALTH47-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *he is ill*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-HE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-THAT-01, MW-LEX-C101-DEF101-01, MW-CONJ-PAN-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ADVERSATIVE-PAN-01, MW-LEX-C117-WHY117-05, MW-GRAMMAR-CAUSAL-KYUNKAI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ALTERNATIVE-CHOICE-01, MW-FUNCTION-ASK-PREFERENCE-ALTERNATIVES-01, MW-CONJ-AR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *he is Ram*; *he is a boy*; *he is here*]
- [YOU SAY: *this is my house*; *that is a shop*; *this or that?*]
- [YOU SAY: *he is ill, but I am fine*]
- [YOU SAY: *I am happy because he is here*]
- [YOU SAY: *tea or water?*, and answer with one of them]
