Marathi, chapter 359: In Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वकील (vakīl) — a lawyer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to cancel, then the Marathi for to grant.

You'll want to know: वकील.
वकील — vakīl — "a lawyer".
घराच्या वादासाठी त्याने वकील नेमला. gharācyā vādāsāṭhī tyāne vakīl nemlā. He appointed a lawyer for the dispute over the house.
Masculine.
Plural वकील (vakīl); वकिली, the legal profession.

The word, taken apart.
वकील (vakīl) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic wakīl, "agent, one entrusted".

खटला — khaṭlā — "a lawsuit; a case".
हा खटला तीन वर्षं चालला. hā khaṭlā tīn varṣaṁ cāllā. This case went on for three years.

खटला (khaṭlā) चालणे, a case is heard; खटला (khaṭlā) भरणे, to file a suit.

The word, taken apart.
खटला (khaṭlā) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सुनावणी (sunāvṇī) — a hearing (in court).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a lawyer, then the Marathi for a lawsuit.

You'll want to know: सुनावणी.
सुनावणी — sunāvṇī — "a hearing (in court)".
खटल्याची पुढची सुनावणी सोमवारी आहे. khaṭalyācī puḍhcī sunāvṇī somvārī āhe. The next hearing of the case is on Monday.
Feminine.
Built on सुनावणे, to pronounce: शिक्षा सुनावणे, to pass sentence.

The word, taken apart.
सुनावणी (sunāvṇī) is built on सुनावणे, "to tell to, to pronounce", with the noun ending -णी; the origin of the verb is not settled.

न्यायाधीशांनी शेवटी निवाडा दिला. nyāyādhīśānnī śevṭī nivāḍā dilā. The judge finally gave the verdict.
Masculine.
निवाडा (nivāḍā) देणे or करणे, to give a ruling.

The word, taken apart.
निवाडा (nivāḍā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi nivāḍā, "settlement", under nirvarati, "restrains" (CDIAL 7392).

Lesson 5 of 5.
दावा (dāvā) — a claim; a lawsuit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a hearing, then the Marathi for a verdict.

You'll want to know: दावा.
दावा — dāvā — "a claim; a lawsuit".
भावाने घरावर दावा केला. bhāvāne gharāvar dāvā kelā. The brother laid claim to the house.
Masculine. Plural: दावे, dāve.
X वर दावा (dāvā) करणे, to lay claim to X; दावा (dāvā) दाखल करणे, to file a suit.

The word, taken apart.
दावा (dāvā) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic daʿwā, "a claim, a lawsuit".
