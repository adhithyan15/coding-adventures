Urdu, chapter 86: Seventy, Eighty, Ninety, World, Colour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ستر (sattar) — seventy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fifty, then the Urdu for sixty.

You'll want to know: ستر.
ستر — sattar — "seventy".

The word, taken apart.
ستر (sattar) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from saptati, "seventy" (CDIAL 13143).

دنیا (dunyā) — the world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for eighty, then the Urdu for ninety.

You'll want to know: دنیا.
دنیا — dunyā — "the world".

The word, taken apart.
دنیا (dunyā) is a loan from Arabic dunyā, "the world, this nearer life", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is d-n-w (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
رنگ (rang) — a colour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for ninety, then the Urdu for the world.

You'll want to know: رنگ.
رنگ — rang — "a colour".

The word, taken apart.
رنگ (rang) has two possible sources: Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour", whose inherited Hindi form CDIAL gives as rā̃g (CDIAL 10560), and Persian rang, "colour". The two may have merged.
