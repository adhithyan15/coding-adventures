## You'll want to know: la solidaridad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C606-SOLIDAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la solidaridad** — "the solidarity".

> Necesitamos más solidaridad.
>
> *We need more solidarity.*

*Solidario* — caring, ready to help.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solidaridad* is built on *solidario* with *-idad* (EtymDB); *solidario* was borrowed from French *solidaire*, from Latin *solidus* 'whole, firm'.
