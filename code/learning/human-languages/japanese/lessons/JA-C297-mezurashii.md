## You'll want to know: めずらしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C297-ONI297-05]; assesses=[] -->

**めずらしい** — *mezurashii* — "rare; unusual".

> **これは めずらしい まつりです。** — *kore wa mezurashii matsuri desu* — "This is an unusual festival."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めずらしい** (*mezurashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
