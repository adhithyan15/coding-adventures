Gujarati, chapter 313: A Rumour Spreads.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અફવા (aphvā) — a rumour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a report, then the Gujarati for to describe.

You'll want to know: અફવા.
અફવા — aphvā — "a rumour".
ગામમાં અફવા ફેલાઈ કે ડાકુ આવ્યા છે. gāmmā̃ aphvā phelāī ke ḍāku āvyā che. A rumour went round the village that bandits had come.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અફવા (aphvā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic afwāh, "mouths": what passes from mouth to mouth. It ફેલાય છે, spreads.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ફેલાવવું (phelāvvũ) — to spread (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to describe, then the Gujarati for a rumour.

You'll want to know: ફેલાવવું.
ફેલાવવું — phelāvvũ — "to spread (something)".
કોઈએ જાણી જોઈને આ અફવા ફેલાવી. koīe jāṇī joīne ā aphvā phelāvī. Someone spread this rumour on purpose.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે અફવા ફેલાવી, teṇe aphvā phelāvī.

The word, taken apart.
ફેલાવવું (phelāvvũ) is the causative of ફેલાવું, to spread, which goes back to Sanskrit prathita, "spread out" (CDIAL 8651): to make something spread. જાણી જોઈને, knowing and seeing, is on purpose.

Lesson 3 of 5.
સત્ય (satya) — the truth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a rumour, then the Gujarati for to spread (something).

You'll want to know: સત્ય.
સત્ય — satya — "the truth".
પોલીસે લોકોને સત્ય જણાવ્યું. polīse lokone satya jaṇāvyũ. The police told people the truth.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સત્ય (satya) is a tatsama, Sanskrit satya, "true, real" (Monier-Williams). The noun is neuter; સાચું is the everyday adjective, true.

Lesson 4 of 5.
માહિતી (māhitī) — information.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spread (something), then the Gujarati for the truth.

You'll want to know: માહિતી.
માહિતી — māhitī — "information".
આ માહિતી ખોટી હતી. ā māhitī khoṭī hatī. This information was wrong.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
માહિતી (māhitī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic māhiyya, "what a thing is, its nature": the facts of a matter. It is feminine and does not take a plural: ખોટી માહિતી, wrong information.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખબર (khabar) — knowledge (of something); word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the truth, then the Gujarati for information.

You'll want to know: ખબર.
ખબર — khabar — "knowledge (of something); word".
મીરાને ચોરીની ખબર સવારે પડી. Mīrāne corīnī khabar savāre paḍī. Mira found out about the theft in the morning.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખબર (khabar) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khabar, "news, a report". In Gujarati it is knowing about something: ખબર પડી, came to know; મને ખબર છે, I know.
