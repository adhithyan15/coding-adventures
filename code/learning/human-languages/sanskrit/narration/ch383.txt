Sanskrit, chapter 383: Setting a Goal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संकल्पः (saṅkalpaḥ) — a resolve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rests, then the Sanskrit for receives with honour.

You'll want to know: संकल्पः.
संकल्पः — saṅkalpaḥ — "a resolve".
यदि संकल्पः दृढः, तर्हि लक्ष्यं प्राप्नोति। yadi saṅkalpaḥ dṛḍhaḥ, tarhi lakṣyaṃ prāpnoti. If the resolve is firm, one reaches the goal.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संकल्पः (saṅkalpaḥ) is built on sam and the root √kḷp, "to be fit, to form"; MW gives saṃ-kalpa "will, volition, purpose, a definite intention" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: आकांक्षति.
आकांक्षति — ākāṅkṣati — "longs for".
यदि सः ज्ञानम् आकांक्षति, तर्हि प्रतिदिनं पठेत्। yadi saḥ jñānam ākāṅkṣati, tarhi pratidinaṃ paṭhet. If he longs for knowledge, he should study every day.
Past (he …): सः ज्ञानम् आकांक्षितवान्, saḥ jñānam ākāṅkṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.

You'll want to know: अभिलाषः.
अभिलाषः — abhilāṣaḥ — "a heartfelt wish".
यदि तव अभिलाषः शुद्धः, तर्हि सः पूर्णः भविष्यति। yadi tava abhilāṣaḥ śuddhaḥ, tarhi saḥ pūrṇaḥ bhaviṣyati. If your wish is pure, it will be fulfilled.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अभिलाषः (abhilāṣaḥ) is built on abhi, "towards", and the root √laṣ, "to desire"; MW gives abhi-lāṣa "desire, wish, affection" (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: उत्तरोत्तरम्.
उत्तरोत्तरम् — uttarottaram — "more and more; step after step".
यदि भवान् यतते, तर्हि उत्तरोत्तरं कुशलः भविष्यति। yadi bhavān yatate, tarhi uttarottaraṃ kuśalaḥ bhaviṣyati. If you keep trying, you will grow more and more skilled.

The word, taken apart.
उत्तरोत्तरम् (uttarottaram) is built from uttara, "higher, later", said twice; MW gives uttarottaram "higher and higher, more and more, in constant continuation" (MW).
