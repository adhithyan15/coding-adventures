## You'll want to know: tànqì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C460-LOW460-02]; assesses=[] -->

*tànqì* — "to sigh".

> *nǐ wèishénme tànqì* — Why are you sighing?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tànqì* is built from *tàn* 'to sigh' and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thanH* and *khj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a long breath and the second 'breath'.
