## You'll want to know: получатель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C451-POSOLSTVO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**получатель** (*poluchátel*) — "an addressee, a recipient".

> Посылка не дошла до получателя.
>
> *Posýlka ne doshlá do poluchátelya.*
>
> The parcel did not reach the addressee.

Masculine. The sender is the *отправитель*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*получатель* is built on *получать* 'to receive' with *-тель* 'one who'. EtymDB lists *получатель* without a deeper source.
