## You'll want to know: победа
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C324-POBEDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**победа** (*pobéda*) — "a victory, a win".

> Победа была очень важной для всех.
>
> *Pobéda bylá óchen vázhnoy dlya vsekh.*
>
> The victory was very important for everyone.

Feminine. *День Победы* — Victory Day, 9 May.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*победа* is inherited from Old East Slavic *poběda* "victory, defeat" (EtymDB), built on *по-* and *беда* "misfortune": first the trouble one side brings on the other.
