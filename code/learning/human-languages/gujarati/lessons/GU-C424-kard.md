## You'll want to know: કાર્ડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C424-COUNTER424-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કાર્ડ** — *kārḍ* — "a card (a bank card)".

> તેણે કાર્ડથી બિલ ચૂકવ્યું.
>
> *teṇe kārḍathī bil cūkavyũ.*
>
> She paid the bill by card.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાર્ડ** (*kārḍ*) is borrowed from English *card*, here a bank card.
