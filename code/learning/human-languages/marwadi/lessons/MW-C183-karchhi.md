## You'll want to know: कड़छी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C183-HATH183-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कड़छी** — *kaṛchhī* — "a ladle".

> कड़छी कड़ाही मांय है।
>
> *kaṛchhī kaṛāhī mā̃y hai.*
>
> The ladle is in the wok.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कड़छी** (*kaṛchhī*) comes from a reconstructed *\*kaṭacchu*, "a ladle"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *kaṛchī*, "an iron ladle" (CDIAL 2633). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
