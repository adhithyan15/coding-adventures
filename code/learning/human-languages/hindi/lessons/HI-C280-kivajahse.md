# की वजह से (kī vajah se) — because of

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-JOIN-KYONKI-01, HI-JOIN-KYON-01, HI-LEX-C106-UNWELL-04, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Answer a **क्यों** with **क्योंकि**: "Why didn't you come? Because I was ill." (**तुम क्यों नहीं आए? क्योंकि मैं बीमार था।**)

[PAUSE 1s] **क्योंकि** needs a whole sentence after it. This lesson gives a reason in a single noun.

## You'll want to know: की वजह से, के कारण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C280-VAJAH-SE-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C222-THG222-02, HI-LEX-C184-THG184-05, HI-LEX-C259-THG259-02, HI-LEX-C74-SIGNS-05, HI-LEX-C273-THG273-05] -->

> **बारिश की वजह से हम घर पर रहे।**
>
> *bāriś kī vajah se ham ghar par rahe.* — "Because of the rain we stayed at home."

**वजह** (*vajah*), "a reason", is feminine, so **की वजह से**: "from the reason of". **कारण**, which you know, is masculine and does the same job a little more formally: **के कारण** (*ke kāraṇ*).

- **भीड़ की वजह से मुझे देर हुई।** (*bhīṛ kī vajah se mujhe der huī.*) — Because of the crowd I was late.
- **बीमारी के कारण वह नहीं आया।** (*bīmārī ke kāraṇ vah nahīṃ āyā.*) — Because of illness he didn't come.

A pronoun takes its **-की** form: **मेरी वजह से** (*merī vajah se*), "because of me"; **उसकी वजह से** (*uskī vajah se*), "because of him", or her.

## You'll want to know: the reason is that …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C280-REASON-IS-01]; assesses=[HI-JOIN-KYONKI-01, HI-JOIN-KI-01, HI-LEX-C94-SPAN-04] -->

> **वजह यह है कि मैं बीमार था।**
>
> *vajah yah hai ki maiṁ bīmār thā.* — "The reason is that I was ill."

**वजह यह है कि …** names the reason and then gives it as a whole sentence. Mind the order of the three ways:

| the reason | where it stands |
|---|---|
| **बारिश की वजह से** | before the result |
| **वजह यह है कि …** | in a sentence of its own, after the result |
| **क्योंकि …** | after the result, in the same sentence |
