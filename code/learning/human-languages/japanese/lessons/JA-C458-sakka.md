## You'll want to know: さっか

**さっか** — *sakka* — "a novelist; an author".

> **ははは あの さっかが すきです。** — *haha wa ano sakka ga suki desu* — "My mother likes that author."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さっか** (*sakka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "make" (Middle Chinese *tsak*) and *ka*, "expert" (*kae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
