Lesson 1 of 5.
ದಸರಾ (dasarā) — Dasara, the autumn festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bandage, then the Kannada for diabetes.

You'll want to know: ದಸರಾ.
ದಸರಾ — dasarā — "Dasara, the autumn festival".

The word, taken apart.
ದಸರಾ (dasarā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit daśaharā, "the (festival) that takes away ten", built on daśa, "ten" (CDIAL 6227): ś became s and the h dropped out.

ದೀಪಾವಳಿ (dīpāvaḷi) — Deepavali, the festival of lights.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ದೀಪಾವಳಿ.
ದೀಪಾವಳಿ — dīpāvaḷi — "Deepavali, the festival of lights".

The word, taken apart.
ದೀಪಾವಳಿ (dīpāvaḷi) comes from Sanskrit dīpāvali, "row of lamps, festival", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 6358). Dīpa, "lamp", and āvali, "row"; Kannada writes the last l as ḷ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸಂಕ್ರಾಂತಿ (saṅkrānti) — Sankranti, the January harvest festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for Dasara, then the Kannada for Deepavali.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಕ್ರಾಂತಿ.
ಸಂಕ್ರಾಂತಿ — saṅkrānti — "Sankranti, the January harvest festival".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಕ್ರಾಂತಿ (saṅkrānti) is Sanskrit saṁkrānti, "passage of a heavenly body", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 12835). Kannada writes the nasal before k as ṅ. The festival marks the sun's passage into a new sign.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಗಣೇಶ ಚತುರ್ಥಿ (gaṇēśa caturthi) — Ganesha Chaturthi.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for Deepavali, then the Kannada for Sankranti.

You'll want to know: ಗಣೇಶ ಚತುರ್ಥಿ.
ಗಣೇಶ ಚತುರ್ಥಿ — gaṇēśa caturthi — "Ganesha Chaturthi".

The word, taken apart.
ಗಣೇಶ ಚತುರ್ಥಿ (gaṇēśa caturthi) is Sanskrit gaṇa, "host" (CDIAL 3988), and īśa, "lord" (CDIAL 1617), joined as gaṇeśa, "lord of the hosts", the god Ganesha, with caturthī, "the fourth" (CDIAL 4600), the fourth day of the lunar fortnight. Kannada shortens its last vowel.
