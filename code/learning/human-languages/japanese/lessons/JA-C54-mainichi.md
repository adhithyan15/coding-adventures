**まいにち** — *mainichi* — "every day".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まいにち** (*mainichi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mai*, "every" (Middle Chinese *mwojX*) and *nichi*, "day" (*nyit*) (Unihan, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
