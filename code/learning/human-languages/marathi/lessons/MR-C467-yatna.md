## You'll want to know: यातना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C467-YATANA467-01]; assesses=[] -->

**यातना** — *yātnā* — "torment; agony".

> आजारपणात आजोबांनी खूप यातना सहन केल्या.
>
> *ājārpaṇāt ājobānnī khūp yātnā sahan kelyā.*
>
> Grandfather bore great agony during his illness.

Feminine.

Often plural: यातना सहन करणे, to bear agony.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यातना** (*yātnā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *yātanā*, "torment" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
