## You'll want to know: героиня
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C523-PLOT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**героиня** (*geroínya*) — "a heroine".

> Героиня романа уезжает в другой город.
>
> *Geroínya romána uyezzháyet v drugóy górod.*
>
> The heroine of the novel moves to another town.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*героиня* was borrowed from Greek *hērōínē* 'a heroine' (EtymDB), the feminine of *hḗrōs* 'hero'.
