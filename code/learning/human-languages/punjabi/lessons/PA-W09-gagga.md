# ਗ — third in the throat row

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-KA-01, PA-SCRIPT-KHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-CHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **ਕ** and **ਖ** once each. Both are made at the back of the
mouth: *ka* dry, *kha* with breath. Then write **ਚ** once, from the session
before this one.

## Script — one letter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-GA-01]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-KA-01, PA-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->

Trace **ਗ** twice and say *ga*. It is made in the same place as **ਕ**, with the
voice switched on — put a finger on your throat and feel it hum on *ga* and go
quiet on *ka*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-GA-01, PA-SCRIPT-KA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Cover the models. Write **ਕ**, then **ਗ**. Compare only the new shape.
