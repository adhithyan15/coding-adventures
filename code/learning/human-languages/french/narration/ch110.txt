French, chapter 110: Right, Left, World, Journey, Holidays.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la droite — the right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the west, then the French for a letterbox.

You'll want to know: la droite.
la droite — "the right".

The word, taken apart.
droite is the feminine of the word for "right, straight", from Latin directus, "straight" (Lewis & Short) — inherited: -ct- became -it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la gauche — the left.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a letterbox, then the French for the right.

You'll want to know: la gauche.
la gauche — "the left".

The word, taken apart.
gauche — formed from the Old French verb gauchir, "to veer, to warp" (EtymDB 2.1), usually traced to Frankish walkan, "to trample". The left was the clumsy hand.

[pause 2 seconds]
The holiday month: say en août.

You'll want to know: les vacances.
les vacances — "holidays". Always plural in this sense.

The word, taken apart.
vacances from Latin vacantia, "empty, free times", from vacare, "to be empty, to be free" (Lewis & Short) — a learned borrowing.
