# Solluṅgaḷ — please tell me

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-TAYAVUSEYTU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You know *tayavu seytu*, the weightier “do me the kindness.” Now
learn one ordinary respectful request by ear before meeting its written shape.

## You'll want to know: one useful request
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-SOLLUNGAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

***Solluṅgaḷ*** means **please tell me**. The ending *-uṅgaḷ* makes the request
respectful. For now, keep the whole expression together: one sound, one useful
meaning.

Say it in three easy pulses: *sol · luṅ · gaḷ*. Then say it smoothly once:
*solluṅgaḷ*.

Keep that sound and meaning in mind. Next you will uncover just one new written
