## You'll want to know: säen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C504-SAEEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**säen** — "to sow".

> Im April säen wir Karotten und Salat.
>
> *In April we sow carrots and lettuce.*

Regular: *hat gesät*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*säen* is inherited from Old High German *sāen*, from Proto-Germanic *sēaną* "to sow" (EtymDB); English *sow* is a cousin.
