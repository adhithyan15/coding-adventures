## You'll want to know: le croisement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C435-PANNEAUX-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le croisement** — "the crossroads, the junction".

> Au croisement, nous tournons à droite.
>
> *At the crossroads we turn right.*

Close to *le carrefour*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*croisement* is built on *croiser* 'to cross' with *-ment*; *croiser* comes from *croix*, inherited from Latin *crux* (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list the noun.
