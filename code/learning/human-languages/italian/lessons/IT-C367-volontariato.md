## You'll want to know: il volontariato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C367-VOLONTARIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il volontariato** — "the voluntary work, volunteering".

> Il sabato faccio volontariato in ospedale.
>
> *On Saturdays I do voluntary work at the hospital.*

*Fare volontariato* — to volunteer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*volontariato* is built on *volontario*, a learned borrowing from Latin *voluntārius* "willing, of free will" (Lewis & Short), from *voluntās* "will", built on *velle* "to want".
