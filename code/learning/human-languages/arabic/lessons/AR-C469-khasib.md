## You'll want to know: خصب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C469-TURBA469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خصب** — *khaṣib* — "fertile".

> الأرض حول النهر خصبة.
>
> *al-arḍ ḥawla an-nahr khaṣiba.*
>
> The land around the river is fertile.

The noun *khiṣb*, "fertility", has the same letters.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خصب** (*khaṣib*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṣ-b*, "to be fruitful, rich in herbage" (Lane).
