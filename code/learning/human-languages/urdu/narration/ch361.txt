Urdu, chapter 361: Is It Possible?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
امکان (imkān) — a possibility; a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sin, then the Urdu for to hold on to.

You'll want to know: امکان.
امکان — imkān — "a possibility; a chance".
اگر بادل ہیں تو بارش کا امکان ہے۔ agar bādal haiṅ to bārish kā imkān hai. If there are clouds, there is a chance of rain.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
امکان (imkān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic imkān, "possibility", of the same family as ممکن, "possible".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a coincidence, then the Urdu for a probability.

You'll want to know: بیتنا.
بیتنا — bītnā — "to go by (of time); to pass".
اگر وقت بیت گیا تو کچھ نہیں ہو سکے گا۔ agar vaqt bīt gayā to kuchh nahīṅ ho sake gā. If the time slips by, nothing can be done.
Past (it …): وہ سال جلدی بیت گیا, voh sāl jaldī bīt gayā.

The word, taken apart.
بیتنا (bītnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bītnā, "to pass (of time)", under Sanskrit vṛttá, "turned, passed" (CDIAL 12069).
