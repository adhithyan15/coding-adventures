# ಪಾರಾಗು (pārāgu) — to escape unharmed; to get through safely

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C472-HALU472-01, KA-LEX-C472-HALU472-02] -->

## You'll want to know: ಪಾರಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C472-HALU472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾರಾಗು** — *pārāgu* — "to escape unharmed; to get through safely".

> ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕರು ಪಾರಾದರು.
>
> *apaghātadalli ellā prayāṇikaru pārādaru.*
>
> All the passengers came through the accident safely.

Past (I …): ಪಾರಾದೆ, *pārāde*.

What one escapes takes -ಇಂದ: ಅಪಾಯದಿಂದ ಪಾರಾಗು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಾರಾಗು** (*pārāgu*) joins Sanskrit *pāra*, "the further bank, the far end" (CDIAL 8100), and the native verb ಆಗು: to reach the other side.
