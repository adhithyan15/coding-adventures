## You'll want to know: झाड़णो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C172-JHADU172-01]; assesses=[] -->

**झाड़णो** — *jhāṛṇo* — "to sweep".

> मां दिनूगै कमरो झाड़ै है।
>
> *mā̃ dinūgai kamro jhāṛai hai.*
>
> Mother sweeps the room early in the morning.

Past (I …): म्हैं कमरो झाड़्यो, *mhaĩ kamro jhāṛyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झाड़णो** (*jhāṛṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhaṭati*, "falls", and its causative, "shakes down"; CDIAL lists Marwari *jhāṛṇo*, "to sweep" (CDIAL 5328). You sweep with the broom you know, **बुहारी**.
