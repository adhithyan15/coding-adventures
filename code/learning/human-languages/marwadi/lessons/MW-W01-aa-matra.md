# ा — make *ra* into *rā*

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **र**. (*ra*.) Copy it once.

## Script — add one mark
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

The mark **ा** sits on the right of a consonant and lengthens its vowel:

> **र** *ra* + **ा** *ā* = **रा** *rā*

Hold *ā* steady; do not turn it into English *ray*.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

- copy **र**
- add **ा** on its right
- read the result: **रा** *rā*

Do that three times. On the last copy, wait five seconds after looking at the
model before you write.
