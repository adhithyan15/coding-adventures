## Added — every word lesson says where its word comes from

The 1376 word lessons that lacked an etymology hook, and the 1318 that lacked a visible origin, now have both. Most gained a `## The word, taken apart` block (or `phrase`) after the word's introduction and a matching `etymology_hook` of 120 characters or fewer; 47 early hand-written lessons that already talk about their word's history state the origin, with its source, inside that existing section instead.

- **Native words** cite the Dravidian Etymological Dictionary by entry number, as in "native Dravidian, one word with Tamil *tēḷ* and Malayalam *tēḷ* (DEDR 3470)". Cognates are named only when the full DEDR article lists them under that language in the same sense. Where DEDR compares a Sanskrit or Prakrit word, puts a query against the Telugu form, or says another language borrowed the word from Telugu (*cukka*, *eḍāri*, *alavāṭu*, *appu*), the lesson says so. Words in DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (*goḍḍali*, *rampaṁ*, *jaḍa*, *baṇḍi*) are not called native, and words DEDR records only from Telugu, every other form marked as borrowed from it (*eḍāri*, *alavāṭu*), are called "Dravidian as far as DEDR records it".
- **Sanskrit loans** say whether they were taken whole (tatsama) or reshaped (tadbhava) and cite Turner's CDIAL where it has the form. Where CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word itself from Dravidian, Munda, an Austroasiatic or another non-Aryan source (*kuṇḍa*, *kalā*, *maṣi*, *mukula*, *paṭa*), or from Iranian (*mudrā*), the lesson says that too. Learned words CDIAL lacks (*sūcana*, *vivaraṇa*, *sampradāya*) are given without a CDIAL number.
- **Other loans** name the donor: English (*kampenī*, *ṭiphinu*), Persian and Arabic, usually through Hindustani (*rasīdu*, *jillā*, *vakīlu*, *jarimānā*), and Portuguese (*pīpā*, *mēstri*, *bālcī*). *Rōju*, "day", is Persian *rōz*.
- **Uncertain origins** are said plainly, 79 of them, with the nearest DEDR or CDIAL entry when there is one (*lōya*, *peraḍu*, *buḍaga*); 26 more give the usual account ("usually traced to ...").
- **Homographs kept apart.** A shared shape is never taken for a shared word: *nuvvu* "you" is not DEDR 3720 *nuvvu* "sesame"; *vēru* "root" (DEDR 5535) is not *vēṟu* "different" (DEDR 5548); *peṭṭe* "box" (DEDR 4388) is not *peṭṭu* "to put" (DEDR 4390); *daṇḍa* "garland" (DEDR 3060) is not *daṇḍa* "staff, salutation" (CDIAL 6128); *pūyu* "to bloom" is not DEDR 4352 *pūyu* "to smear".
- **Older claims corrected** against DEDR and CDIAL. *lēdu*'s *lē-* is filed with the *il-* of Tamil and Kannada (DEDR 2559), not a different root (TE-C01-ledu, TE-C32-undu). *Sarē* is no longer called native; its origin is uncertain, usually traced to Sanskrit *sadṛśa* (TE-C01-sare). *Paravā* is "care, concern" (TE-C03-paravaaledu). *Veḷḷu*, *rā-*, *cūcu*, *teliyu* and *caduvu* gained their DEDR entries and lost "unrelated" and "its own way" claims (TE-C32-vellu, -raa, -cuudu, -telusu, TE-C33-caduvu); *teliyu* is DEDR 3433, not Tamil *teri*. *Kiṭikī* is probably a northern loan (CDIAL 3770), not Portuguese (TE-C67-window). *Dāramu* only possibly came from the north (TE-C62-thread). *Addaṁ* is usually traced through Prakrit to *ādarśa* (TE-C62-mirror). *Baṭṭa*'s possible source, *paṭṭa*, is probably non-Aryan according to CDIAL, not Dravidian (TE-C46-cloth). The garland's *daṇḍa* and the salutation's are no longer one word (TE-C51-salutation, TE-C52-garland), nor are *vēru* and *vēṟu* (TE-C54-root) or *peṭṭe* and *peṭṭu* (TE-C56-box). *Bāṭa* is no longer called inherited (TE-C55-path), and *mannincu* is no longer called native (TE-C94-pardon).
- **Parts are glossed in English** wherever a part would be a word a later lesson teaches.
- **Lessons near the time cap.** TE-C31-subha-madhyahnam, TE-C151-chhaya and TE-C152-jhari give the origin in a block with no Telugu script in it, which keeps them under their declared length.
- **`duration.max_seconds` raised** to the computed length, all under 300 s. All seven were already over their declared value before this change: TE-C01-dhanyavadamulu 260→281; TE-C32-cuudu 255→295; TE-C32-raa 250→287; TE-C32-telusu 260→293; TE-C32-undu 250→290; TE-C32-vellu 250→293; TE-C81-first 220→229. No other lesson's declared length changed, and no lesson's computed length exceeds its declared one.
- **Review corrections.** An independent check of all 1385 entries against the full DEDR and CDIAL texts found 46 citation errors (3.3%), all now fixed:
  - *Wrong entry* (4): *cakkera* is CDIAL 12338 "candied sugar", not 12337 "gravel"; *tommidi* is DEDR 3532 (old Tamil *toṇṭu*), not 1025; Hindi *dhotī* is CDIAL 6881; *aṇṭu* "defilement by touch" is DEDR 120, not 96.
  - *"Not recorded" when it is* (27). DEDR lists *mokka*, *tūgu*/*tūka*, *maluku*, *ulli* (twice), *bugga*, *palukarincu*, Kannada *kuñca*, *giḍḍaṅgi* "godown", *kaṟaku* and *gaṭṭu*. CDIAL has the northern words behind *vaḍḍī* (*vṛddhi*), *kottimīra* (*kustumbarī*), *niccena* (*niśrayaṇī*), *palaka* (*phalaka*), *chātī*, *guṇḍī*, *cīṭī*, *ṭīkā*, *jeṇḍā*, *khātā* and *mōsaṁ*. It cites Telugu *jāḍi* itself (CDIAL 4736), and it offers hedged leads for *sannani*, *pāla-*, *vaḍraṅgi* and *vaḍḍiñcu*.
  - *Misreported* (15): nine words called "from Telugu alone" have sister forms in their DEDR entries (*tōmu*, *tannu*, *piccuka*, *dorlu*, *boggu*, *cīkaṭi*, *tavvu*, *vadulu*, *dūḍa*) and are now plain native. Six comparisons were attached to the wrong word or mis-glossed: *duppaṭi* (CDIAL lacks Hindi *dupaṭṭā*), *kaṭṭu* (DEDR's *ghaṭṭa* goes with *gaṭṭu*), *cemaṭa* (DEDR's query is on Kurux, not Telugu), *kolucu*, *iruguporugu* and *vādiñcu*.
  - Also fixed: 13 dropped CDIAL notes on a compared Sanskrit word's own origin (*kaṭhina*, *kavaca*, *khaṭa*, *kaṣāya*, *nīra*, *pīlu*, *piṭaka* and others), and 3 dropped "probably"s (*paṭṭa*, *addaṁ*, *bhāma*). 5 reshaped words wrongly called tatsamas were relabelled (*vīdhi*, *vaḍa*, *soraṅgaṁ*, *cadaraṅgaṁ*, *vaṅkara*), and *vāyidā* is now named as Arabic *waʿda* through Hindustani. *Snēhituḍu* is Sanskrit *snehita*. Seven "the sense X is the one Telugu gives it" claims were corrected where Sanskrit already had the sense (*ruci*, *snēha*, *vṛtta*, *nāṭaka*, *gaṇita*, *viṣaya*, *uddeśa*). Two hooks were made to agree with their blocks, and three CDIAL numbers were added (*saṅgīta*, *sandarśana*, *samarpaṇa*). Nine hedged leads ("may come from ..., but no source says so") were added to uncertain words.
  - *Left-over contradictions.* The *lē-*/*il-*, *sarē*, *okaṭi* and *enimidi*/*tommidi* corrections now also reach TE-C01-ledu's title, warm-up and recall, TE-C01-practice, TE-C01-sare, TE-C32-undu, TE-C32-tinu, TE-C07-numbers-1-5, TE-C07-numbers-6-10, TE-C34-adugu, TE-C81-first, the track README, the roadmap, the session map and the pronunciation reference.
  - TE-C32-tinu's `max_seconds` is raised 250→280, its computed length before and after this change. Every other edited lesson stays within its declared length.
  - The roadmap and session-map digests in `tests/doc-shard-digests/` are updated for the two edited planning-document shards.
