## You'll want to know: लेपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C483-GUTIKA483-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लेपः** — *lepaḥ* — "an ointment".

> माता व्रणे लेपं स्थापयति।
>
> *mātā vraṇe lepaṃ sthāpayati.*
>
> Mother puts ointment on the wound.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लेपः** (*lepaḥ*) comes from the root √lip, "to smear"; MW gives "smearing" and "ointment, unguent, plaster" (MW).
