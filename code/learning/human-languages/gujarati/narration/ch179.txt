Gujarati, chapter 179: Plans and programmes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
યોજના (yojnā) — plan, scheme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for remaining, then the Gujarati for to go by.

You'll want to know: યોજના.
યોજના — yojnā — "plan, scheme".
અમારી યોજના કાલે શરૂ થશે. — amārī yojnā kāle śarū thaśe — "Our plan will start tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
યોજના (yojnā) is Sanskrit yojanā, "joining, arranging", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, on the root yuj, "to join".

Lesson 2 of 5.
નક્કી કરવું (nakkī karvũ) — to decide, to fix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to go by, then the Gujarati for plan.

You'll want to know: નક્કી કરવું.
નક્કી કરવું — nakkī karvũ — "to decide, to fix".
અમે કાલે તારીખ નક્કી કરીશું. — ame kāle tārīkh nakkī karīśũ — "We will fix the date tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
નક્કી કરવું (nakkī karvũ) is built from nakkī, "settled, fixed", and the inherited karvũ, "to do". The origin of nakkī is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form, and EtymDB has no entry.

Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to decide, then the Gujarati for programme.

You'll want to know: પહેલેથી.
પહેલેથી — pahelethī — "beforehand, in advance".
હું પહેલેથી ટિકિટ લઈશ. — hũ pahelethī ṭikiṭ laīś — "I will get the ticket in advance."

The word, taken apart.
પહેલેથી (pahelethī) is built on the inherited pahelũ, "first", with -thī, "from", so it means from the first.
