## You'll want to know: विवरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C313-PATRAKAR313-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विवरण** — *vivaraṇ* — "a detailed account".

> पत्रकार ने हादसे का पूरा विवरण दिया।
>
> *patrakār ne hādse kā pūrā vivaraṇ diyā.*
>
> The reporter gave a full account of the accident.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विवरण** (*vivaraṇ*) is Sanskrit *vivaraṇa*, "an opening up, an explaining" (Monier-Williams): the facts laid open one by one.
