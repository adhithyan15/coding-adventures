## You'll want to know: মানুষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C276-GHOTONA276-04]; assesses=[] -->

**মানুষ** — *mānush* — "a human being; people".

> ঘটনার সময় ওখানে কিছু মানুষ ছিল।
>
> *ghôṭônār sômôy okhāne kichhu mānush chhilô.*
>
> There were a few people there when it happened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মানুষ** (*mānush*) comes down from Sanskrit *mānuṣa*, 'human; a man' (CDIAL 10049, which lists Bengali *mānus*). It names one person or people in general; **লোক** is the plainer everyday word.
