## You'll want to know: ਭਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C280-CHANGE280-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਨ** — *bhān* — "small change; coins".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ ਭਾਨ ਹੈ?
>
> *kī tuhāḍe kol bhān hai?*
>
> Do you have small change?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਨ** (*bhān*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhājana*, "dividing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhāṇ*, "small change for a rupee", under it (CDIAL 9437).
