Kannada, chapter 345: The Spy in the Palace.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಡಗು (aḍagu) — to hide oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a chariot, then the Kannada for to do battle.

You'll want to know: ಅಡಗು.
ಅಡಗು — aḍagu — "to hide oneself".
ಕಳ್ಳ ಬಾವಿಯ ಹಿಂದೆ ಅಡಗಿದನು. kaḷḷa bāviya hinde aḍagidanu. The thief hid behind the well.
Past (I …): ಅಡಗಿದೆ, aḍagide.
To hide oneself is ಅಡಗು (aḍagu); to hide something is ಅಡಗಿಸು or ಬಚ್ಚಿಡು.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಡಗು (aḍagu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 63 lists Kannada aḍagu beside Tamil aṭaṅku, "to be compressed, to cease, to disappear".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ (talemaresiko) — to go into hiding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to do battle, then the Kannada for to hide oneself.

You'll want to know: ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ.
ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ — talemaresiko — "to go into hiding".
ಆರೋಪಿ ಆರು ತಿಂಗಳು ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊಂಡಿದ್ದನು. ārōpi āru tiṅgaḷu talemaresikoṇḍiddanu. The accused had been in hiding for six months.
Past (I …): ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊಂಡೆ, talemaresikoṇḍe.
Literally "to hide one's head": a news phrase for someone wanted by the police.

The word, taken apart.
ತಲೆಮರೆಸಿಕೊ (talemaresiko) is a native Dravidian compound: tale, "head" (DEDR 3103), and maresu, "to hide", from mare (DEDR 4760), with -ko, from koḷ, "to take" (DEDR 2151).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to hide oneself, then the Kannada for to go into hiding.

You'll want to know: ಬೆನ್ನಟ್ಟು.
ಬೆನ್ನಟ್ಟು — bennaṭṭu — "to chase".
ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಕಳ್ಳನ ಕಾರನ್ನು ಬೆನ್ನಟ್ಟಿದರು. polīsaru kaḷḷana kārannu bennaṭṭidaru. The police chased the thief's car.
Past (I …): ಬೆನ್ನಟ್ಟಿದೆ, bennaṭṭide.
ಬೆನ್ನು, back, and ಅಟ್ಟು, to run after: to run at someone's back.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೆನ್ನಟ್ಟು (bennaṭṭu) is a native Dravidian compound: bennu, "the back" (DEDR 5488), and aṭṭu, "to follow, to run after, to pursue", which DEDR 79 lists for Kannada.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಗೂಢಚಾರ (gūḍhacāra) — a spy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to go into hiding, then the Kannada for to chase.

You'll want to know: ಗೂಢಚಾರ.
ಗೂಢಚಾರ — gūḍhacāra — "a spy".
ರಾಜನ ಗೂಢಚಾರ ಪಕ್ಕದ ರಾಜ್ಯಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋದನು. rājana gūḍhacāra pakkada rājyakke hōdanu. The king's spy went to the neighbouring kingdom.
Plural: ಗೂಢಚಾರರು, gūḍhacāraru.
ಗೂಢ, hidden, and ಚಾರ, a goer.

The word, taken apart.
ಗೂಢಚಾರ (gūḍhacāra) is borrowed from Sanskrit gūḍhacāra, "a spy" (Monier-Williams), from gūḍha, "hidden", and cāra, "going, a goer".

You'll want to know: ನುಸುಳು.

ಗೂಢಚಾರ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಅರಮನೆಯೊಳಗೆ ನುಸುಳಿದನು. gūḍhacāra rātri aramaneyoḷage nusuḷidanu. The spy slipped into the palace at night.
Past (I …): ನುಸುಳಿದೆ, nusuḷide.

The word, taken apart.
ನುಸುಳು (nusuḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3714 lists Kannada nusuḷ beside Tamil words meaning "to creep through a narrow passage, to penetrate".
