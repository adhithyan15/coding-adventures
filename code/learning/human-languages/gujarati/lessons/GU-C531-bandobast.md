## You'll want to know: બંદોબસ્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C531-JHANJHAT531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**બંદોબસ્ત** — *bandobast* — "arrangements (made in advance)".

> જો મહેમાનો રાત્રે રોકાશે તો સૂવાનો બંદોબસ્ત કરવો પડશે.
>
> *jo mahemāno rātre rokāśe to sūvāno bandobast karvo paḍśe.*
>
> If the guests stay the night, sleeping arrangements will have to be made.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બંદોબસ્ત** (*bandobast*) is usually traced to Persian *band-o-bast*, "binding and tying", and so "arrangements".
