## You'll want to know: exīstimō, exīstimāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C357-SENTENTIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**exīstimō, exīstimāre** — "to reckon, to consider; to value".

> Quid dē hōc librō exīstimās?
>
> *What do you think of this book?*

*exīstimō, exīstimāre, exīstimāvī, exīstimātum*. With the accusative and infinitive: *exīstimō tē errāre*, I reckon you are wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exīstimō* is built from *ex* "out" and *aestimō* "to value, to put a price on" (Lewis & Short): to weigh a thing up and give the result. English *estimate* and *esteem* come from *aestimō*.
