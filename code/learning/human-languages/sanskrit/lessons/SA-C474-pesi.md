## You'll want to know: पेशी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C474-SIRA474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पेशी** — *peśī* — "a muscle".

> व्यायामः पेशीं दृढां करोति।
>
> *vyāyāmaḥ peśīṃ dṛḍhāṃ karoti.*
>
> Exercise makes the muscle firm.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेशी** (*peśī*) MW's first sense is "a piece of flesh", then "a muscle"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
