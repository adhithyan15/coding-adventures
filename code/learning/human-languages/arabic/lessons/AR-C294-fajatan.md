## You'll want to know: فجأة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C294-FAJATAN294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فجأة** — *fajʾatan* — "suddenly, all of a sudden".

> انقطعت الكهرباء فجأة.
>
> *inqaṭaʿat al-kahrabāʾ fajʾatan.*
>
> Suddenly the electricity went off.

An adverb with -an, like غالبا. It often opens a sentence: فجأة سمعنا صوتا.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فجأة** (*fajʾatan*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-j-ʾ* (Lane), "to come upon someone unawares". The noun *fajʾa*, "a sudden coming", takes the adverb ending *-an*.
