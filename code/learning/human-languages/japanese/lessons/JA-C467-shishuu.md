## You'll want to know: ししゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C467-CRAFT467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ししゅう** — *shishū* — "embroidery".

> **ははの ししゅうは きれいです。** — *haha no shishū wa kirei desu* — "My mother's embroidery is beautiful."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ししゅう** (*shishū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "prick" (Middle Chinese *tshjeH*) and *shū*, "sew" (*sjuwH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
