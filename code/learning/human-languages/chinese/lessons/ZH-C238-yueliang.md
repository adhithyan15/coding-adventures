## You'll want to know: yuèliang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C238-SKY238-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yuèliang* — "the moon".

> *jīntiān wǎnshang yuèliang hěn dà* — The moon is huge tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuèliang* is built from 月 'moon' and *liang* 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjwot* and *ljangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'bright'; in the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
