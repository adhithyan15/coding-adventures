## You'll want to know: अरे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C112-RIGHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अरे** (*are*) — "oh!", "hey!", a cry of surprise.

**अरे, सच में?** — "oh, really?". It is informal: use it with friends.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Right, wrong, sure, really, oh: five reactions.
