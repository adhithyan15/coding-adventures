## You'll want to know: ammettere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C342-COMPROMESSO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ammettere** — "to admit".

> Devo ammettere che il tuo piano era migliore.
>
> *I have to admit that your plan was better.*

Conjugated like *mettere*; past participle *ammesso*. It also means to let someone in: *ammettere a un corso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ammettere* is inherited from Latin *admittere* "to let in, to allow" (EtymDB), built from *ad* "to" and *mittere* "to send": you let a fact in.
