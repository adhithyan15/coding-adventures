## You'll want to know: نسل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C315-AJDAD315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نسل** — *nasl* — "a generation; a line of descent".

> ہر نسل نے یہ گھر سنبھالا۔
>
> *har nasl ne yih ghar sambhālā.*
>
> Every generation looked after this house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نسل** (*nasl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nasl*, "offspring, descendants". It is feminine: **نئی نسل**, the young generation.
