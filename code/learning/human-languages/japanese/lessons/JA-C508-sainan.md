## You'll want to know: さいなん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C508-LUCK508-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さいなん** — *sainan* — "a misfortune; a calamity".

> **もし りょこうの まえに けがを したら、さいなんですね。** — *moshi ryokō no mae ni kega o shitara, sainan desu ne* — "If you got hurt right before a trip, that would be bad luck."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さいなん** (*sainan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sai*, "disaster" (Middle Chinese *tsoj*) and *nan*, "trouble" (*nanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
