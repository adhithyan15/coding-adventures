## You'll want to know: স্পষ্ট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C362-MONOJOG362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**স্পষ্ট** — *spôshṭô* — "clear; distinct".

> তোমার কথাটা আমার কাছে স্পষ্ট নয়।
>
> *tomār kôthāṭā āmār kāchhe spôshṭô nôy.*
>
> What you said isn't clear to me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্পষ্ট** (*spôshṭô*) is Sanskrit *spaṣṭa*, clearly seen, distinct (Monier-Williams). Speech, writing or a picture can be স্পষ্ট.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
