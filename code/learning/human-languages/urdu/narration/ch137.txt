Lesson 1 of 5.
جامنی (jāmnī) — purple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for ripe, then the Urdu for pink.

You'll want to know: جامنی.
جامنی — jāmnī — "purple".

The word, taken apart.
جامنی (jāmnī) is "jāmun-coloured", the purple of the jambolan fruit, with the ending -ī. CDIAL lists Hindi jāmun and jāman, the tree and its fruit, under jambūna, a form beside Sanskrit jambula (CDIAL 5136).

Lesson 2 of 5.
سنہرا (sunahrā) — golden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for pink, then the Urdu for purple.

You'll want to know: سنہرا.
سنہرا — sunahrā — "golden".

The word, taken apart.
سنہرا (sunahrā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from suvarṇadhara, "containing gold", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13522). It is built from suvarṇa, "gold", and dhara, "holding".
