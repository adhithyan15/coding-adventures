## You'll want to know: じょうきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C349-YOSOU349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうきょう** — *jōkyō* — "the situation; the state of things".

> **じょうきょうが かわったら、れんらくします。** — *jōkyō ga kawattara, renraku shimasu* — "If the situation changes, I will get in touch."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうきょう** (*jōkyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "conditions" (Middle Chinese *dzrjangH*) and *kyō*, "condition" (*xjwangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
