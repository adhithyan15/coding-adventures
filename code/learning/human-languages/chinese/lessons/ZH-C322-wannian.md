## You'll want to know: 晚年
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C322-BACKTHEN322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**晚年** — *wǎnnián* — "one's later years; old age".

> **他晚年的时候回了老家** *tā wǎnnián de shíhou huí le lǎojiā* — In his later years he went back to his home town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**晚年** (*wǎnnián*) is built from 晚 'late' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjonX* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'dusk'.
