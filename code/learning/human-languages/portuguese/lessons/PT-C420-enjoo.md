## You'll want to know: o enjoo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C420-TERRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o enjoo** — "seasickness, nausea".

> Tomei um comprimido para o enjoo.
>
> *I took a pill for the seasickness.*

Also car sickness or morning sickness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enjoo* is built on *enjoar* 'to feel sick', whose own origin is not settled (EtymDB lists *enjoar* without a source).
