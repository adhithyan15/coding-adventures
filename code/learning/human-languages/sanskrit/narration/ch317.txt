Sanskrit, chapter 317: The Envoy.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
दूतः (dūtaḥ) — an envoy; a messenger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a kingdom, then the Sanskrit for governs.

You'll want to know: दूतः.
दूतः — dūtaḥ — "an envoy; a messenger".
शत्रोः दूतः सभाम् आगच्छति। śatroḥ dūtaḥ sabhām āgacchati. The enemy's envoy comes to the court.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दूतः (dūtaḥ) MW thinks it probably comes from the root √du, "to go", and compares dūra, "far"; MW gives dūta as a messenger, envoy, ambassador (MW).

आदिशति — ādiśati — "commands; gives orders".
राजा सेनापतिम् आदिशति। rājā senāpatim ādiśati. The king gives orders to the general.
Past (he …): सः सेनापतिम् आदिष्टवान्, saḥ senāpatim ādiṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
आदिशति (ādiśati) is built from ā and the root √diś, "to point, show", as in diśā; MW gives ā-diś as to point out, assign, and to order, direct, command (MW).

Lesson 4 of 6.
सन्धिः (sandhiḥ) — a peace treaty; an alliance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an order, then the Sanskrit for commands.

You'll want to know: सन्धिः.
सन्धिः — sandhiḥ — "a peace treaty; an alliance".
अन्ते शत्रुः सन्धिं याचते। ante śatruḥ sandhiṃ yācate. In the end the enemy asks for a peace treaty.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सन्धिः (sandhiḥ) is built from sam, "together", and the root √dhā, "to put": a putting together, a junction, and MW also gives saṃdhi as an alliance or peace; in grammar it is the joining of sounds (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
सामन्तः (sāmantaḥ) — a vassal; a feudal chief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for commands, then the Sanskrit for a peace treaty.

You'll want to know: सामन्तः.
सामन्तः — sāmantaḥ — "a vassal; a feudal chief".
सामन्ताः सभायाम् उपविशन्ति। sāmantāḥ sabhāyām upaviśanti. The vassals sit in the assembly.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सामन्तः (sāmantaḥ) is built on sam-anta, "bordering", with the lengthened first vowel: MW gives sāmanta as being on all sides, a neighbour, and a vassal or feudal prince (MW).
