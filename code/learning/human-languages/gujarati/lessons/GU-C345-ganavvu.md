## You'll want to know: ગણાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C345-KHULASO345-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગણાવવું** — *gaṇāvvũ* — "to list (reasons, one by one)".

> અરુણે નોકરી છોડવાનાં ત્રણ કારણો ગણાવ્યાં.
>
> *Aruṇe nokrī choḍvānā̃ traṇ kāraṇo gaṇāvyā̃.*
>
> Arun gave three reasons for leaving his job.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધાં કારણો ગણાવ્યાં, *teṇe badhā̃ kāraṇo gaṇāvyā̃*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગણાવવું** (*gaṇāvvũ*) is the causative of **ગણવું** (*gaṇvũ*), "to count", from Sanskrit *gaṇayati*, "counts" (CDIAL 3993): to count things out for someone.
