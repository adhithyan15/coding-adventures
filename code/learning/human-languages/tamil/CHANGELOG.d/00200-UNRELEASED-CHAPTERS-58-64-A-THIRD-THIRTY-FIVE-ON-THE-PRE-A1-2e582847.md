### Two discards no string search could have reached

**நூல் ('thread')** was the intended word for the made-things chapter and had to
go. It survives every substring and romanization test as a headword, because it
is not one — `TA-C44-book` hands it over inside its own teaching prose: "நூல்
(*nūl*) is a thread, and thread is what bound palm leaves into a book before
there was paper." Teaching it here would have re-taught a word the book had
already given away with its meaning attached. **சீப்பு** ('a comb') took the
slot.

**மீன் ('a fish')** is the same trap in the same place Malayalam found it, and
it is worth recording even though nothing in this tranche wanted it: `TA-C51-star`
takes விண்மீன் apart and writes "*mīṉ* is a fish, and from the oldest Tamil
poetry onward it is a star as well." A search for மீன் finds only விண்மீன், and
a search for the gloss finds nothing at all. The word is spent, and so is விண்.

### Ten near-collisions examined under the same rule and KEPT — five of them
### turned into teaching

A rule that only rejects is a filter. Each of these collides with something
already committed, and in each the colliding gloss is of an **etymon**, an
**English analogy**, a **different word**, or a **numeral** — never of the Tamil
headword itself.

- **பசு** ('a cow') sits inside பசுப்பு, but that word is glossed in
  `TA-C22-paccai-manjal` as "greenish yellow", off the *pac-* green root. A
  different word in a different field. Kept.
- **பசி** ('hunger') sits inside ஐப்பசி, which `TA-C16-thamizh-maadangal` lists
  as a month name and never takes apart. Kept — and then set against பசு in its
  own lesson, one mark apart, as the discrimination it is.
- **வலி** ('pain') folds onto *vaḻi*, which `TA-C53-path` glosses as "the way".
  Read at byte level these are U+006C and U+1E3B: two different words the corpus
  itself keeps apart. Kept, and the lesson now teaches the ல/ழ split on it.
- **அறுவடை** ('the harvest') folds onto *āṟu*, six. That gloss is of a
  **numeral**. Kept, and the lesson names the difference: short அ and the hard ற
  against six's long ஆ.
- **களம்** ('a threshing floor') folds onto *kālam*, whose Sanskrit etymon *kāla*
  is glossed in `TA-C26-kaalai`. That gloss is of an **etymon**, not of களம்.
  Kept, and the lesson holds the two apart.
- **சர்க்கரை** ('sugar') meets the English word *sugar* in `TA-C37-nanban` — used
  purely as a spelling analogy for how a reader supplies voicing. An **English
  analogy**, not a Tamil gloss. Kept.
- **அனுமதி** ('permission') overlaps the English half of `TA-C38-vidai`'s gloss,
  "leave; permission to depart". Different word, narrower sense. Kept, and the
  lesson now distinguishes them outright: விடை is permission to go, அனுமதி is the
  wider one.
- **தயக்கம்** ('hesitancy') opens like the தயவு of தயவுசெய்து, which
  `TA-C38-vidai` traces to *daya*, "kindness". An **etymon** of a different word.
  Kept, and the lesson says so rather than hiding it.
- **புல்** ('grass') is one mark from பல், 'a tooth'. No gloss collision at all;
  a genuine minimal pair. Kept and taught.
- **ஆடு** ('a goat') folds onto *adu*, அது, 'that one'. Different spelling,
  different length. Kept, and the lesson says where to put the length.

**Two more were discarded on their own merits.** அப்படியானால் ('in that case')
was dropped because `TA-C47-just-so` already glosses *appaḍi* as 'in that
manner', so most of the word was spent — and because its own English gloss
carries the word *case*, which the metalanguage ledger counts. **முதலில்** took
the slot. கூட ('also') sits inside கூடை, chapter 54's basket, and **மட்டும்**
took that one.

### The taught-glyph filter

Tamil's 48 writing lessons teach forty glyphs. All 35 headwords and every Tamil
citation in every new body is spelled from those forty, so the tranche adds
**zero** script-closure violations and **zero** exposure-exempted glyphs. ொ and
the Tamil numerals stay untaught, so words needing them — கொஞ்சம் among them —
were not reachable and were not used. விளக்குமாறு ('a broom') was set aside for
a second reason: it carries விளக்கு, chapter 44's lamp, inside it. துடைப்பம்
took the slot.

### The sweep

A whole-tree per-word script-purity sweep read the bytes of all 490 files in the
Tamil tree, classified every character by **block range** rather than by
`unicodedata.name` (which raises on unassigned codepoints), treated Cn as
word-internal so a bad write cannot split a word into two clean halves, treated
ASCII apostrophe and hyphen as word boundaries, and counted Latin Extended
Additional as Latin. It was self-tested against five known-dirty and five
known-clean fixtures built from `chr()` in a script file, plus a ZWJ fixture, and
was then required to **rediscover** all four defects recorded in this repository's
own history from `git show` copies of the pre-fix blobs before its result was
believed.

Result: **zero ZWJ or ZWNJ, zero NUL, zero BOM, zero bidi controls, and one
pre-existing finding this tranche did not introduce and did not touch.**
`narration/ch39.json` and `.txt` carry U+0BB5 TAMIL LETTER VA immediately
followed by an ASCII `s`. `TA-C39-evvalavu` writes "Three" and then the letter
name in bold, then an English plural `s`, and the narration generator drops the
markdown that had separated the two. Both files are
unmodified by this tranche and the same string is in the committed tree at HEAD.
Reported, not removed.
