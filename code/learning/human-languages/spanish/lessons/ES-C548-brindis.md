## You'll want to know: el brindis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C548-BANQUETE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el brindis** — "the toast (with a drink)".

> El padrino hizo un brindis por los novios.
>
> *The best man made a toast to the couple.*

The same form in the plural: *los brindis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brindis* comes from the German toast *bring dir's* 'I bring it to you' (EtymDB), said to have been picked up from German soldiers in the 1500s.
