## You'll want to know: le raisonnement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C344-RAISONNEMENT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le raisonnement** — "the reasoning".

> Ton raisonnement est juste.
>
> *Your reasoning is sound.*

*Un raisonnement* is one chain of thought. Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raisonnement* is built from *raisonner* and the suffix *-ment* (EtymDB). The verb rests on *raison*, from Latin *ratiō* "a reckoning, reason" (Lewis & Short).
