Russian, chapter 277: The one before and the next.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
предыдущий (predydúshchiy) — previous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an interval, then the Russian for a deadline.

You'll want to know: предыдущий.
предыдущий (predydúshchiy) — "previous".
Предыдущий урок был очень длинным. Predydúshchiy urók byl óchen dlínnym. The previous lesson was very long.
Formal; in speech also прошлый. The opposite is следующий.

The word, taken apart.
предыдущий (predydúshchiy) is usually described as a Church Slavonic participle, built on пред- "before" and идти "to go", inherited from Proto-Slavic jĭti "to go" (EtymDB): the one going before.

Lesson 2 of 5.

последующий (posléduyushchiy) — "following, subsequent".
В последующие дни было тепло. V posléduyushchiye dni býlo tepló. In the following days it was warm.

The word, taken apart.
последующий (posléduyushchiy) is the participle of последовать "to follow", built on след "a track" (EtymDB): coming in the footsteps.

Lesson 3 of 5.
очередной (ocherednóy) — next (in a series), yet another.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for previous, then the Russian for following.

You'll want to know: очередной.
очередной (ocherednóy) — "next (in a series), yet another".
Он рассказал очередную смешную историю. On rasskazál ocherednúyu smeshnúyu istóriyu. He told yet another funny story.
The next in a regular series, or "one more", often a little ironic.

The word, taken apart.
очередной (ocherednóy) is built on очередь "a queue, a turn", from черёд, череда "a row, a turn", which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic čerda "a file, a herd"; EtymDB lists no parent for очередной (ocherednóy) itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ежегодный (yezhegódnyy) — annual, yearly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for following, then the Russian for next.

You'll want to know: ежегодный.
ежегодный (yezhegódnyy) — "annual, yearly".
Ежегодный концерт будет в мае. Yezhegódnyy kontsért búdet v máye. The annual concert will be in May.
Also ежедневный — daily, еженедельный — weekly.

The word, taken apart.
ежегодный (yezhegódnyy) is built on еже- "each" and год "year", which is inherited through Old East Slavic godŭ from Proto-Slavic godŭ "the right time" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
дальнейший (dalnéyshiy) — further, subsequent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for next, then the Russian for annual.

You'll want to know: дальнейший.
дальнейший (dalnéyshiy) — "further, subsequent".
О дальнейших планах он не говорил. O dalnéyshikh plánakh on ne govoríl. He did not talk about further plans.
В дальнейшем — in future, later on.

The word, taken apart.
дальнейший (dalnéyshiy) is built on дальний "distant" with the superlative suffix -ейш-; дальний comes from даль "distance", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic dalĭ (EtymDB).
