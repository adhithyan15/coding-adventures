# ജലദോഷം (jaladōṣaṁ) — a cold

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a cough, then the Malayalam for a fever.

## You'll want to know: ജലദോഷം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C115-UNWELL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ജലദോഷം** (*jaladōṣaṁ*) — "a cold", the kind with a runny nose.

**എനിക്ക് ജലദോഷമുണ്ട്** (*enikkŭ jaladōṣamuṇṭŭ*) — "I have a cold". The person sits in
**എനിക്ക്**, "to me", and **ഉണ്ട്** says "there is".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

An illness, said with to-me and there-is.
