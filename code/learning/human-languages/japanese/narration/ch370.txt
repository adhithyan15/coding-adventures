Japanese, chapter 370: Reaching Agreement.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
まとまる (matomaru) — to be settled; to come together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a demand, then the Japanese for a policy.

You'll want to know: まとまる.
まとまる — matomaru — "to be settled; to come together".
いけんが まとまったら、ていあんして ください。 — iken ga matomattara, teian shite kudasai — "Once your ideas come together, please make a proposal."
Polite まとまります, matomarimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): いけんが まとまりました。, iken ga matomarimashita.

The word, taken apart.
まとまる (matomaru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

Lesson 4 of 5.
だいたい (daitai) — roughly; mostly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for each other, then the Japanese for the same.

You'll want to know: だいたい.
だいたい — daitai — "roughly; mostly".
だいたい わかったら、はじめて ください。 — daitai wakattara, hajimete kudasai — "Once you more or less understand, please begin."

The word, taken apart.
だいたい (daitai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are dai, "large" (Middle Chinese dajH) and tai, "body" (thejX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ぴったり (pittari) — perfectly; exactly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the same, then the Japanese for roughly.

You'll want to know: ぴったり.
ぴったり — pittari — "perfectly; exactly".
けいさんが ぴったり あったら、おわりです。 — keisan ga pittari attara, owari desu — "If the sums come out exactly, we are done."

The word, taken apart.
ぴったり (pittari) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
