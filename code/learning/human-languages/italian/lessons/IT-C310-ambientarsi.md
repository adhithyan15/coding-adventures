## You'll want to know: ambientarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C310-TRASFERIRSI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ambientarsi** — "to settle in, to adapt (to new surroundings)".

> I bambini si sono ambientati subito nella nuova scuola.
>
> *The children settled in at the new school straight away.*

Built on *ambiente*, ‘surroundings’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ambientarsi* is built on *ambiente* "surroundings", from Latin *ambiēns* "going round", from *ambīre* (Lewis & Short).
