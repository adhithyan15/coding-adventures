# சடங்கு (caṭaṅku) — a ritual; a rite

## You'll want to know: சடங்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C321-SADANGU321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**சடங்கு** — *caṭaṅku* — "a ritual; a rite".

> திருமணத்தில் பல சடங்குகள் இருந்தன.
>
> *tirumaṇattil pala caṭaṅkukaḷ iruntaṉa.*
>
> There were many rituals at the wedding.

Plural: சடங்குகள், *caṭaṅkukaḷ*.

Plural சடங்குகள்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சடங்கு** (*caṭaṅku*), "a rite, a ceremony", is a Tamil word whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.
