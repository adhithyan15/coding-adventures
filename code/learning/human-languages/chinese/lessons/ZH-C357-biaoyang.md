## You'll want to know: biǎoyáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C357-SORRY357-05]; assesses=[] -->

*biǎoyáng* — "to praise (in front of others)".

> *wǒ juéde nǐ yīnggāi biǎoyáng tā* — I think you should praise him.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biǎoyáng* is built from *biǎo* and *yáng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjewX* and *yang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to make clear' and the second 'to raise' and 'to speak of'.
