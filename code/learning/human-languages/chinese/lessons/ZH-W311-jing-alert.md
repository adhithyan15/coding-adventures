## Script — three pieces: top left, top right and the foot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JING-ALERT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 警

**Three pieces: two side by side on top, one across the foot.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**Top left, eight strokes.** The grass piece of 花: a bar and two short verticals. Under it a short sweep to the left and a stroke that runs right, turns down and hooks back. Inside, 口.

**Top right, four strokes.** The striking piece of 救: a sweep to the left, a bar, a long sweep to the lower left and a long stroke falling to the right.

**The foot, seven strokes.** A dot. A long bar across, then two shorter bars. Then 口 under them.

8 + 4 + 7 = nineteen.
