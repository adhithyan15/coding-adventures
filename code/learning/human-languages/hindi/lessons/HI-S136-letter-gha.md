# घ — a letter arriving before its words

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-118] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: **ँ** — what does it do to the vowel it sits
over?

[PAUSE 2s] This next character is one you have not met inside anything yet.

## Script you'll notice: घ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-136]; assesses=[] -->

**घ** — *gha*, with a puff of breath.

What it is made of:

- a continuous upper curl, middle hook, lower bowl, and rising right side
- a short descending lower stem
- the top shirorekhā

## Script: ahead of its words, on purpose
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-136] -->

Almost every character in this book was taught out of a word you were already
saying — you met the shape inside something familiar and then learned to draw
it. **This one has nothing to be found inside yet.**

That is deliberate and it will not happen again after the next letter. Only two
characters are still ahead of their first word; the rest of the alphabet has
already been overtaken by the vocabulary, and those will have to be met the
other way round.

So learn the shape and the breath now. **The word will arrive already
readable**, which is the order this ought to happen in and rarely does.

## Writing: घ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-136] -->

- **1.** start below the headline at the upper-left, curve down and clockwise around the upper curl, turn back left through the middle hook, continue clockwise around the lower bowl, and rise along the right side to the headline without lifting
- **2.** lift at the lower right-side junction and descend the short stem below the bowl
- **3.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 2.** The pen comes up 2 times and no more.

> Verified three-stroke teaching form fitted to the bundled printed outline; everyday handwriting may divide the curl and bowl differently.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-घ-order.gif’, strokes 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 9 May 2009.
