## You'll want to know: ਧੱਕਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C284-OFF284-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੱਕਾ** — *dhakkā* — "a push; a shove".

> ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ੇ ਨੂੰ ਧੱਕਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।
>
> *Aman ne darvāze nū̃ dhakkā dittā.*
>
> Aman gave the door a push.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੱਕਾ** (*dhakkā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhakk*, "push, strike"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dhakkā*, "a push, blow", under it (CDIAL 6701).
