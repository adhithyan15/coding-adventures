## You'll want to know: نقش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C342-TURATH342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نقش** — *naqsh* — "an inscription; a carving".

> قرأ العالم نقشا على الحجر.
>
> *qaraʾa al-ʿālim naqshan ʿalā al-ḥajar.*
>
> The scholar read an inscription on the stone.

Plural: نقوش, *nuqūsh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقش** (*naqsh*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-sh* (Lane), "to paint or engrave a design"; the taught *nāqasha*, to discuss, is picking at a matter point by point.
