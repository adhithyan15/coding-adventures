6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
સંશોધન (sanśodhan) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to run down, then the Gujarati for to entreat.

You'll want to know: સંશોધન.
સંશોધન — sanśodhan — "research".
મીરા નવી દવા પર સંશોધન કરે છે. Mīrā navī davā par sanśodhan kare che. Mira is doing research on a new medicine.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સંશોધન (sanśodhan) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit saṃśodhana, "thoroughly cleansing" (Monier-Williams); "research", sifting a question clean, is its modern sense.

Lesson 5 of 6.
તારવવું (tāravvũ) — to deduce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a method, then the Gujarati for a sample.

You'll want to know: તારવવું.
તારવવું — tāravvũ — "to deduce".
આ પ્રયોગમાંથી મીરાએ એક વાત તારવી. ā prayogmā̃thī Mīrāe ek vāt tārvī. From this experiment Mira drew one conclusion.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સાચો અર્થ તારવ્યો, teṇe sāco arth tāravyo.

The word, taken apart.
તારવવું (tāravvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit tāráyati, "causes to arrive at, leads across"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to raise, to pluck" (CDIAL 5796). "To deduce", to pluck a conclusion out of the facts, is not among CDIAL's senses.
