Gujarati, chapter 513: On the Factory Floor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઉદ્યોગ (udyog) — an industry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a career, then the Gujarati for a promotion.

You'll want to know: ઉદ્યોગ.
ઉદ્યોગ — udyog — "an industry".
અમારા શહેરમાં ઘણા મોટા ઉદ્યોગ છે. amārā śahermā̃ ghaṇā moṭā udyog che. There are many big industries in our city.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉદ્યોગ (udyog) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit udyoga, "exertion, an undertaking" (Monier-Williams); modern Gujarati uses it for an industry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભંગાર (bhangār) — scrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a machine, then the Gujarati for brass.

You'll want to know: ભંગાર.
ભંગાર — bhangār — "scrap".
આ જૂનું મશીન હવે ભંગારમાં જશે. ā jūnũ maśīn have bhangārmā̃ jaśe. This old machine will now go for scrap.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભંગાર (bhangār) comes from bhaṅgākara, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "broken pots" (CDIAL 9356); today it is any broken metal or junk sold for scrap.
