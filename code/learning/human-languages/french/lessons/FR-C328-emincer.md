## You'll want to know: émincer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C328-COUPER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**émincer** — "to slice thinly".

> Le cuisinier émince les poivrons.
>
> *The cook slices the peppers thinly.*

A cooking word; *couper en fines tranches* says the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*émincer* is built on *mince* "thin", from Old French *mincier* "to cut fine", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *minūtiāre*, built on *minūtus* "small" (Lewis & Short).
