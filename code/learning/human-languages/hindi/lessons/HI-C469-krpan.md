## You'll want to know: कृपण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C469-UDAR469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कृपण** — *kṛpaṇ* — "miserly; tight-fisted".

> मेरा पड़ोसी बहुत कृपण है, अपने बच्चों को भी पैसे नहीं देता।
>
> *merā paṛosī bahut kṛpaṇ hai, apne baccoṁ ko bhī paise nahīṃ detā.*
>
> My neighbour is terribly tight-fisted; he won't give money even to his own children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कृपण** (*kṛpaṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *kṛpaṇa*, first "pitiable" and then "miserly, stingy" (Monier-Williams). It is a formal word, the one books use for a miser.
