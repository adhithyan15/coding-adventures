## You'll want to know: अप्रत्यक्ष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C395-UPAYUKTA395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अप्रत्यक्ष** — *apratyakṣa* — "indirect".

> त्याच्या बोलण्यात अप्रत्यक्ष टीका होती.
>
> *tyācyā bolaṇyāt apratyakṣa ṭīkā hotī.*
>
> There was indirect criticism in his words.

अ + प्रत्यक्ष, not before the eyes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अप्रत्यक्ष** (*apratyakṣa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *apratyakṣa*, "not present to the eyes, invisible" (Monier-Williams).
