## You'll want to know: はかどる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C454-TASK454-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はかどる** — *hakadoru* — "to make good progress".

> **けさは しごとが はかどりました。** — *kesa wa shigoto ga hakadorimashita* — "My work went well this morning."

Polite **はかどります**, *hakadorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **べんきょうが はかどりました。**, *benkyō ga hakadorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はかどる** (*hakadoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
