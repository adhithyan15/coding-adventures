## You'll want to know: परिश्रमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C503-SAHAKARI503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परिश्रमः** — *pariśramaḥ* — "hard work".

> परिश्रमेण सः धनं लभते।
>
> *pariśrameṇa saḥ dhanaṃ labhate.*
>
> He gets money through hard work.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिश्रमः** (*pariśramaḥ*) is built on *pari*, "all round", and the root √śram, "to toil, to grow weary"; MW gives *pariśrama* the senses "exertion, labour" (MW).
