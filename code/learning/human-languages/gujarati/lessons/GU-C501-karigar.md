## You'll want to know: કારીગર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C501-KARIGAR501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કારીગર** — *kārīgar* — "a craftsman".

> આ કારીગર લાકડામાંથી સુંદર રમકડાં બનાવે છે.
>
> *ā kārīgar lākḍāmā̃thī sundar ramakḍā̃ banāve che.*
>
> This craftsman makes beautiful toys out of wood.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કારીગર** (*kārīgar*) is usually traced to Persian *kārīgar*, "a craftsman", a word built on *kār*, "work".
