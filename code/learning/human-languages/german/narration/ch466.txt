German, chapter 466: Neid und Eifersucht.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Eifersucht — jealousy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for at least, then the German for during.

You'll want to know: die Eifersucht.
die Eifersucht — "jealousy".
Seine Eifersucht macht seine Freundin traurig. His jealousy makes his girlfriend sad.
No plural in everyday use. The adjective is eifersüchtig — jealous.

The word, taken apart.
Eifersucht is built on Eifer "zeal, eagerness" and Sucht "sickness, craving"; Sucht is inherited from Old High German suht, from Proto-Germanic suhtiz (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Neid — envy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for during, then the German for jealousy.

You'll want to know: der Neid.
der Neid — "envy".
Er spricht ohne Neid über den Erfolg seines Bruders. He talks about his brother's success without envy.
No plural. Vor Neid platzen — to burst with envy.

The word, taken apart.
Neid is a native Germanic word, usually traced to Old High German nīd "hatred, envy"; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
neidisch — envious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for jealousy, then the German for envy.

You'll want to know: neidisch.
neidisch — "envious".
Meine Schwester ist neidisch auf mein neues Fahrrad. My sister is envious of my new bike.
Neidisch auf + accusative — envious of.

The word, taken apart.
neidisch is built on Neid "envy" with the adjective ending -isch; Neid is usually traced to Old High German nīd "hatred, envy" (EtymDB lists both German words).

Lesson 4 of 5.
beneiden — to envy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for envy, then the German for envious.

You'll want to know: beneiden.
beneiden — "to envy".
Ich beneide dich um deinen ruhigen Job. I envy you your quiet job.
Jemanden um etwas beneiden — to envy someone something. Inseparable: hat beneidet.

The word, taken apart.
beneiden is built on the prefix be- and Neid "envy", a Germanic word usually traced to Old High German nīd (EtymDB lists the verb without a source).

Lesson 5 of 5.
gönnen — to not begrudge; to treat oneself (sich etwas gönnen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for envious, then the German for to envy.

You'll want to know: gönnen.
gönnen — "to not begrudge; to treat oneself (sich etwas gönnen)".
Ich gönne dir den Erfolg von Herzen. I really don't begrudge you your success.
Sich etwas gönnen — to treat yourself: Heute gönne ich mir ein Stück Kuchen.

The word, taken apart.
gönnen is inherited from Old High German giunnan, built with gi- on unnan "to grant", from Proto-Germanic unnaną "to grant, to bestow" (EtymDB).
