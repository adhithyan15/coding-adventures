## You'll want to know: ثورة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C329-THAWRA329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ثورة** — *thawra* — "a revolution, a revolt".

> غيرت الثورة حياة الناس.
>
> *ghayyarat ath-thawra ḥayāt an-nās.*
>
> The revolution changed people’s lives.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ثورة** (*thawra*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-w-r* (Lane), "to rise up, to be stirred", said of dust; the taught *thawr*, a bull, is the same root.
