# どこですか / どうやって (doko desu ka / dō yatte) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C78-LOC78-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-EKI, JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "where" and "a station". (*doko*, *eki*.) Then the **-て** form of **いく**. (*itte*.)

## You'll want to know: どこですか, どうやって
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C256-DIRECTION-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C203-FLY203-02, JA-LEX-C198-RIDE198-04, JA-LEX-C241-OUTING241-05] -->

> **えきは どこですか。**
>
> *eki wa doko desu ka.* — "Where is the station?"

To ask how to get somewhere, **どうやって** (*dō yatte*) asks "how": **どう**, "how", with **やって**, the **-て** form of **やる**, "to do".

> **くうこうまで どうやって いきますか。**
>
> *kūkō made dō yatte ikimasu ka.* — "How do I get to the airport?"

> **ちかてつで いきます。**
>
> *chikatetsu de ikimasu.* — "You go by subway."

## You'll want to know: いって、まがって ください
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C256-DIRECTION-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C194-WAY194-02, JA-LEX-C194-WAY194-01, JA-LEX-C194-WAY194-03, JA-LEX-C79-LOC79-03, JA-LEX-C56-TIME56-05, JA-LEX-C201-ROAD201-01] -->

Directions are **-て ください** requests, one after another, joined with the **-て** form:

> **まっすぐ いって ください。**
>
> *massugu itte kudasai.* — "Please go straight on."

> **まっすぐ いって、みぎに まがって ください。**
>
> *massugu itte, migi ni magatte kudasai.* — "Go straight on, then turn right."

> **つぎの かどを ひだりに まがって ください。**
>
> *tsugi no kado o hidari ni magatte kudasai.* — "Turn left at the next corner."

> **ぎんこうの まえに あります。**
>
> *ginkō no mae ni arimasu.* — "It is in front of the bank."
