## You'll want to know: حمال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C436-KUSHK436-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حمال** — *ḥammāl* — "a porter".

> حمل الحمال حقيبتي الثقيلة.
>
> *ḥamala al-ḥammāl ḥaqībatī ath-thaqīla.*
>
> The porter carried my heavy bag.

The pattern *faʿʿāl* names a trade, as in *najjār*, "a carpenter".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حمال** (*ḥammāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-m-l*, "to carry" (Lane).
