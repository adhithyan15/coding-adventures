## You'll want to know: il rimorso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C477-NOSTALGIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il rimorso** — "remorse, guilt".

> Ho il rimorso di non averla chiamata.
>
> *I feel guilty for not calling her.*

Remorse for something you did; for something lost, say *il rimpianto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimorso* is the past participle of *rimordere* "to bite again", from Latin *remordēre* "to vex, to torment" (Lewis & Short), built on *mordēre* "to bite": a conscience that bites.
