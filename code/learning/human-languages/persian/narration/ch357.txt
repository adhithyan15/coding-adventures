Persian, chapter 357: Dough and bread.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خمیر (khamir) — dough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to sauté, then the Persian for fried onion.

You'll want to know: خمیر.
خمیر (khamir) — "dough".
نانوا خمیر را روی میز پهن کرد. Nânvâ khamir râ ru-ye miz pahn kard. The baker spread the dough out on a table.
Also any paste. Khamir-e nân "bread dough".

The word, taken apart.
khamir is borrowed from Arabic khamīr, "leavened dough" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ورز دادن (varz dâdan) — to knead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for fried onion, then the Persian for dough.

You'll want to know: ورز دادن.
ورز دادن (varz dâdan) — "to knead".
او خمیر را ده دقیقه ورز داد. U khamir râ dah daqiqe varz dâd. She kneaded the dough for ten minutes.
Present varz midahad.

The word, taken apart.
varz "working, toil" is a native Persian word, also seen in varzesh "sport"; dâdan "to give" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ور آمدن (var âmadan) — to rise (of dough).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for dough, then the Persian for to knead.

You'll want to know: ور آمدن.
ور آمدن (var âmadan) — "to rise (of dough)".
خمیر (khamir) در جای گرم ور آمد. Khamir dar jâ-ye garm var âmad. The dough rose in a warm place.
Present var miâyad. Var is an old word for "up".

The word, taken apart.
var "up, on" is an old native Persian word, and âmadan "to come" is inherited Persian: to come up.

Lesson 4 of 5.

خمیرمایه (khamirmâye) — "yeast, leaven".
او کمی خمیرمایه در آب گرم حل کرد. U kami khamirmâye dar âb-e garm hall kard. She dissolved a little yeast in warm water.
khamir "dough" + mâye "substance, starter".

The word, taken apart.
khamirmâye joins khamir "dough", borrowed from Arabic khamīr (EtymDB), and the native Persian mâye "substance, starter".

Lesson 5 of 5.
تنور (tanur) — a clay oven, a bread oven.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to rise, then the Persian for yeast.

You'll want to know: تنور.
تنور (tanur) — "a clay oven, a bread oven".
نان تازه را از تنور بیرون آورد. Nân-e tâze râ az tanur birun âvard. He took the fresh bread out of the clay oven.
A round oven of clay or sunk in the ground; nân-e tanuri "oven bread".

The word, taken apart.
tanur is inherited from Middle Persian tanūr, "oven" (EtymDB), an old word that EtymDB takes back to Akkadian; Arabic and Aramaic have it too.
