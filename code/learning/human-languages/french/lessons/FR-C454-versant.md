## You'll want to know: le versant
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C454-RELIEF-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le versant** — "the mountainside, the slope".

> Les vignes poussent sur le versant sud.
>
> *The vines grow on the south-facing side.*

*Le versant nord* — the north face.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*versant* is the present participle of *verser* 'to pour, to tip', used as a noun (EtymDB lists it); *verser* goes back to Latin *versāre*, from *vertere* 'to turn' (Lewis & Short).
