# ಚಿಕ್ಕಮ್ಮ (cikkamma) — mother's younger sister

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-01, KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for father's younger brother, then the Kannada for father's elder brother.

## You'll want to know: ಚಿಕ್ಕಮ್ಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಕ್ಕಮ್ಮ** — *cikkamma* — "mother's younger sister".

> ರಜೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ಚಿಕ್ಕಮ್ಮನ ಮನೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ.
>
> *rajeyalli nānu cikkammana manege hōguttēne.*
>
> In the holidays I go to my mother's younger sister's house.

Also the wife of ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಕ್ಕಮ್ಮ** (*cikkamma*) is a native Dravidian compound: *cikka*, "small, young" (DEDR 2495), and *amma*, "mother" (DEDR 183).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
