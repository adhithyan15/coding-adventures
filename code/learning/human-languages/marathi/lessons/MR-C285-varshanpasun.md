# तीन वर्षांपासून (tīn varṣāmpāsūn) — how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C285-PEKSHA-01, MR-POSTP-PAASUN, MR-LEX-C201-VEL201-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the train is faster than the bus". (*gāḍī baspekṣā jalad āhe*.) Then "from" and "until". (*pāsūn*, *paryant*.)

## You'll want to know: -पासून, 'for (and still)'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C285-PASUN-SPAN-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C109-ADJ109-04, MR-LEX-RAHNE, MR-LEX-C109-ADJ109-05, MR-LEX-C109-ADJ109-03, MR-LEX-DIVAS] -->

> **मी तीन वर्षांपासून पुण्यात राहतो.**
>
> *mī tīn varṣāmpāsūn puṇyāt rāhto.* — "I have lived in Pune for three years."

Marathi keeps the present: the living still goes on. **-पासून** goes on the stretch of time, in its bent plural: **वर्ष** → **वर्षांपासून** (*varṣāmpāsūn*), **तास** → **तासांपासून** (*tāsāmpāsūn*). To ask: **तुम्ही किती दिवसांपासून इथे आहात?** (*tumhī kitī divsāmpāsūn ithe āhāt?*), "How long have you been here?"

## You'll want to know: from … until, and all … long
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C285-FROM-UNTIL-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-SAKAL, MR-LEX-SANDHYAKAL, MR-LEX-C258-VEL258-05, MR-LEX-KARNE] -->

> **मी सकाळपासून संध्याकाळपर्यंत काम केलं.**
>
> *mī sakāḷpāsūn sandhyākāḷparyant kām kelaṁ.* — "I worked from morning until evening."

**-पासून … -पर्यंत** frames a stretch with two ends. **-भर**, as in **दिवसभर**, fills it: **दिवसभर पाऊस पडत होता** (*divasbhar pāūs paḍat hotā*), "it rained all day". A bare number of hours needs no ending: **मी दोन तास वाचत होतो** (*mī don tās vācat hoto*).
