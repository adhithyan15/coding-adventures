## You'll want to know: شعار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C315-KHABARFOWRI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شعار** (*sho'âr*) — "a slogan, a chant".

> مردم در خیابان شعار میدادند.
>
> *Mardom dar khiyâbân sho'âr midâdand.*
>
> People were chanting slogans in the street.

With *dâdan*: *sho'âr dâdan* "to chant slogans".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sho'âr* is borrowed from Arabic *shi'ār* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a sign, a motto".
