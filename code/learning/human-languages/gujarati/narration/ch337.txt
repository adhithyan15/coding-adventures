Gujarati, chapter 337: Turning the Pages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પાનું (pānũ) — a page.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an autobiography, then the Gujarati for imagination.

You'll want to know: પાનું.
પાનું — pānũ — "a page".
મેં પુસ્તકનાં સો પાનાં વાંચ્યાં. mẽ pustaknā̃ so pānā̃ vā̃cyā̃. I read a hundred pages of the book.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પાનું (pānũ) goes back to Sanskrit parṇá, "a leaf", where CDIAL lists Gujarati pān, a leaf, and pānũ (CDIAL 7918): a book's leaf is its page. પાન is still a leaf on a tree.

પ્રકરણ — prakaraṇ — "a chapter (of a book)".
આ પુસ્તકનું છેલ્લું પ્રકરણ સૌથી સારું છે. ā pustaknũ chellũ prakaraṇ sauthī sārũ che. The last chapter of this book is the best.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રકરણ (prakaraṇ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit prakaraṇa, "treatment, discussion, a section" (Monier-Williams). It is neuter: છેલ્લું પ્રકરણ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
પાત્ર (pātra) — a character (in a story).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a page, then the Gujarati for a chapter (of a book).

You'll want to know: પાત્ર.
પાત્ર — pātra — "a character (in a story)".
આ નવલકથામાં ઘણાં પાત્રો છે. ā navalkathāmā̃ ghaṇā̃ pātro che. There are many characters in this novel.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પાત્ર (pātra) is a tatsama, Sanskrit pātra, "a vessel, a fit receptacle" (Monier-Williams); the sense of a person in a story or a play is the modern language's. It is not પત્ર, a letter: the vowel is long.

The word, taken apart.
વાચક (vācak) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vācaka, "speaking, telling" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the one who reads, close to વાંચવું, to read.
