## You'll want to know: soprar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C465-TEMPORAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**soprar** — "to blow".

> O vento sopra forte do mar.
>
> *The wind is blowing hard off the sea.*

People *sopram* the candles on a cake too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soprar* is inherited from Latin *sufflāre* 'to blow on' (EtymDB), built on *flāre* 'to blow'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
