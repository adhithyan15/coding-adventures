## You'll want to know: la citoyenneté
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C362-CITOYEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la citoyenneté** — "the citizenship".

> Il a demandé la citoyenneté de son nouveau pays.
>
> *He applied for citizenship of his new country.*

From *citoyen* + *-eté*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*citoyenneté* is built from *citoyen* "citizen" and *-eté*. *citoyen* goes back to Latin *cīvitās* "citizenship", from *cīvis* "citizen" (Lewis & Short).
