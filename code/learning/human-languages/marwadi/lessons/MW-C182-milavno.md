## You'll want to know: मिलावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C182-JHATKO182-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मिलावणो** — *milāvṇo* — "to mix".

> हूं दूध मांय खांड मिलाऊं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ dūdh mā̃y khā̃ḍ milāū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I mix sugar into the milk.

Past (I …): म्हैं दूध मांय खांड मिलाई, *mhaĩ dūdh mā̃y khā̃ḍ milāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिलावणो** (*milāvṇo*) is built on Sanskrit *milati*, "meets"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *milaï* (CDIAL 10133). It is the causative partner of **मिलणो**, to meet: to make things meet is to mix them.
