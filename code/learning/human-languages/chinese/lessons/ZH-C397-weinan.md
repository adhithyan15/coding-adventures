## You'll want to know: 为难
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C397-SURELY397-04]; assesses=[] -->

**为难** — *wéinán* — "in an awkward spot; torn (about what to do)".

> **我不想说不所以很为难** *wǒ bù xiǎng shuō bù suǒyǐ hěn wéinán* — I don't want to say no, so I'm in an awkward spot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**为难** (*wéinán*) is built from 为 'to make' and 难 'hard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwe* and *nan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to do' and the second 'difficult'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
