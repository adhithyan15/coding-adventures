## You'll want to know: ادعا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C363-EDDEA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ادعا** (*edde'â*) — "a claim".

> این ادعا درست نیست.
>
> *in edde'â dorost nist.*
>
> This claim is not true.

Plural *edde'âhâ*. Formal; also a claim in court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ادعا** is usually traced to Arabic *iddi'ā'* "a claim, a pretension"; Persian drops the final hamza.
