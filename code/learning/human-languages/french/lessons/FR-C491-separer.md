## You'll want to know: séparer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C491-RUPTURE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**séparer** — "to separate; (se séparer) to split up".

> Après dix ans de vie commune, ils se sont séparés.
>
> *After ten years together, they split up.*

Reflexive for couples: *se séparer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*séparer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sēparāre* "to part, to separate" (EtymDB), built from *sē-* "apart" and a verb *parāre*; which *parāre* is meant is not settled (Lewis & Short).
