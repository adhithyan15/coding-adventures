## You'll want to know: まがる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C194-WAY194-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まがる** — *magaru* — "to turn; to bend".

> **かどを みぎに まがります。** — *kado o migi ni magarimasu* — "I turn right at the corner."

Polite **まがります**, *magarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まがる** (*magaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
