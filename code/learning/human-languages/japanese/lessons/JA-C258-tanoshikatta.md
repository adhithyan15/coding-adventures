# たのしかった / うれしかった (tanoshikatta / ureshikatta) — how it felt

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C23-HEALTH23-04, JA-LEX-C245-JOY245-01, JA-LEX-C204-TRIP204-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "fun" and "glad". (*tanoshii*, *ureshii*.) Then "a trip". (*ryokō*.) Today they move into the past.

## You'll want to know: -かった
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C258-FEELING-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C53-ADJ53-04, JA-LEX-C48-ANIMALS48-03, JA-LEX-C23-HEALTH23-03] -->

> **りょこうは たのしかったです。**
>
> *ryokō wa tanoshikatta desu.* — "The trip was fun."

An i-adjective carries its own past: the last **い** becomes **かった**, and **です** stays as it is to keep the sentence polite. **たのしい** gives **たのしかった** (*tanoshikatta*), **うれしい** gives **うれしかった** (*ureshikatta*).

> **うれしかったです。**
>
> *ureshikatta desu.* — "I was glad."

> **きのうは さむかったです。**
>
> *kinō wa samukatta desu.* — "It was cold yesterday."

**いい** works from its older form **よい**: **よかった** (*yokatta*), "it was good".

> **えいがは よかったです。**
>
> *eiga wa yokatta desu.* — "The film was good."

## You'll want to know: -くなかった, and でした
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C258-FEELING-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C124-QUAL124-05, JA-LEX-C163-HOME163-02, JA-LEX-C135-GENKI, JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-02] -->

"Was not" comes from the **-くない** you know: **おもしろくない** becomes **おもしろくなかった** (*omoshirokunakatta*).

> **えいがは おもしろくなかったです。**
>
> *eiga wa omoshirokunakatta desu.* — "The film was not interesting."

A na-adjective keeps the noun's way: **でした** (*deshita*) after it.

> **うみは しずかでした。**
>
> *umi wa shizuka deshita.* — "The sea was calm."

> **ちちは げんきでした。**
>
> *chichi wa genki deshita.* — "My father was well."
