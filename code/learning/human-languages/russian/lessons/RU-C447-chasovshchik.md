## You'll want to know: часовщик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C447-MASTERSKAYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**часовщик** (*chasovshchík*) — "a watchmaker".

> Часовщик поменял батарейку в часах.
>
> *Chasovshchík pomenyál bataréyku v chasákh.*
>
> The watchmaker changed the battery in the watch.

Masculine. Built on *часы* — a watch, a clock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*часовщик* is built on *часовой* 'of clocks', from *часы* 'a watch', on *час* 'an hour'. EtymDB does not list *часовщик*.
