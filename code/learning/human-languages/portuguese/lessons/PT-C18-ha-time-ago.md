# há três anos — three years ago

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You know **há** as “there is.” Add one nearby meaning: before a
length of time, **há** can mean “ago.”

## You'll want to know: One reusable frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-GRAMMAR-HA-TIME-04]; assesses=[] -->

> **há três anos** — three years ago
> **há dois dias** — two days ago

Build it as **há + amount of time**. Keep **há** fixed; the number and time word
tell you how far back to look.

Say the frame slowly: **há … três … anos**. Then say it as one small unit:
**há três anos**.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-TER-HAVER-02, PT-GRAMMAR-HA-TIME-04] -->
