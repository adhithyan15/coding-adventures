# How are you, again — and the word that answers క్షమించండి

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C03-PARAVAALEDU-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Someone has said **క్షమించండి** to you. One word back — the one
that tells them it does not matter. You have had it since the greetings.

## You'll want to know: పరవాలేదు does three jobs at once
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C03-PARAVAALEDU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C03-PARAVAALEDU-03] -->

**పరవాలేదు** *paravālēdu* covers all of these in ordinary speech:

| what English says | |
|---|---|
| it's okay | after an apology |
| no problem | after a request |
| you're welcome | after thanks |

Look at its tail. **‑లేదు** is the *lēdu* you already carry, the negative of
existence, and *paravā* is the bother — so the word is **"there is no
bother."** That is why one word answers an apology, a request and a thank-you
alike.

## Grammar Lens: the ending is already carrying the person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C03-MIIRU-ELAA-UNNAARU-03, TE-GRAMMAR-C03-NENU-03, TE-LEX-C03-ELAA-01] -->

That question and its answer hide the same point:

- **మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు?** — **‑ారు** already means *you, respectful*, so
  **ఎలా ఉన్నారు?** is a whole question on its own.
- **నేను బాగున్నాను** — **‑ాను** already means *I*, so **నేను** can drop too.

To someone close the ending moves with the pronoun: **నువ్వు ఎలా ఉన్నావు?**

## Script: బ, the letter your answer starts with
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-157, TE-LEX-C03-BAAGAA-01] -->

> బ

**బాగా** and **బాగున్నాను** both open on **బ** *ba* under the long‑ā sign.
Telugu keeps *b* apart from its *p* letter; Tamil writes one shape for both and
lets the position decide.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C03-ELAA-01, TE-LEX-C03-MIIRU-ELAA-UNNAARU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C03-MIIRU-ELAA-UNNAARU-03, TE-LEX-C03-NENU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C03-NENU-03, TE-LEX-C03-BAAGAA-01, TE-LEX-C03-PARAVAALEDU-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C03-PARAVAALEDU-03, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-157] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What hides inside **పరవాలేదు**? (**లేదు** — "no bother.") Which
three English replies does it cover? (**It's okay · no problem · you're
welcome**.) Why can **మీరు** and **నేను** drop out? (**The ending already names
the person.**) Which letter opens your answer? (**బ**.)
