## You'll want to know: ménjìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C379-SAFE379-04]; assesses=[] -->

*ménjìn* — "door entry system; a card-entry door".

> *wǒmen xiǎoqū yǒu ménjìn suǒyǐ hěn ānquán* — Our estate has a door entry system, so it's safe.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ménjìn* is built from 门 'a gate' and *jìn* 'to forbid', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mwon* and *kimH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to restrain' and 'to stop'. The card-and-lock system is a modern use.
