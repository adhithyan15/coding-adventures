## You'll want to know: لٹانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C480-CARE480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لٹانا** — *liṭānā* — "to lay (someone) down".

> نرس نے مریض کو بستر پر لٹایا۔
>
> *nars ne marīz ko bistar par liṭāyā.*
>
> The nurse laid the patient on the bed.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو لٹایا, *us ne bachche ko liṭāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لٹانا** (*liṭānā*) is the causative of **لیٹنا**, "to lie down", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *lēṭyáti* (CDIAL 11109).
