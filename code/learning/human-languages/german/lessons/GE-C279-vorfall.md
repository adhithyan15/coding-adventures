## You'll want to know: der Vorfall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C279-EREIGNIS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Vorfall** — "the incident".

> Nach dem Vorfall rief er die Polizei.
>
> *After the incident he called the police.*

Plural: *die Vorfälle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vorfall* joins *vor* and *Fall* "a fall, a case", from Old High German *val* (EtymDB): something that falls out before you.
