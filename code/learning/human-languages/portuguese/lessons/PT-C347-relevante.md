## You'll want to know: relevante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C347-POLEMICA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**relevante** — "relevant, important".

> Essa informação não é relevante agora.
>
> *That information is not relevant now.*

The opposite is *irrelevante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*relevante* is a learned borrowing from Latin *relevāns* (EtymDB), the present participle of *relevāre* "to lift up, to raise" (Lewis & Short), built on *levāre* "to raise": what is relevant stands out.
