## You'll want to know: ಸಾರಥಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C349-KHADGA349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರಥಿ** — *sārathi* — "a charioteer".

> ಸಾರಥಿ ರಥವನ್ನು ವೇಗವಾಗಿ ಓಡಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *sārathi rathavannu vēgavāgi ōḍisidanu.*
>
> The charioteer drove the chariot fast.

Plural: ಸಾರಥಿಗಳು, *sārathigaḷu*.

Today also a leader who steers a group: ತಂಡದ ಸಾರಥಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾರಥಿ** (*sārathi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sārathi*, "a charioteer, the driver of a car" (Monier-Williams), from *sa-ratha*, "with the chariot".
