## You'll want to know: доставка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C457-DOSTAVKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**доставка** (*dostávka*) — "delivery".

> Доставка по городу бесплатная.
>
> *Dostávka po górodu besplátnaya.*
>
> Delivery within the town is free.

Feminine. *Доставка на дом* — home delivery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*доставка* is built on *доставить* 'to deliver', from *до-* 'up to' and *ставить* 'to put, to place', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *staviti* (EtymDB).
