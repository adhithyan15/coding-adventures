## You'll want to know: ફેફસું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C226-BREATH226-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેફસું** — *phephsũ* — "lung".

> **શું તેનું ફેફસું નબળું છે?** — *śũ tenũ phephsũ nabaḷũ che?* — "Is his lung weak?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેફસું** (*phephsũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *phupphusa*, "lungs" (CDIAL 9090).
