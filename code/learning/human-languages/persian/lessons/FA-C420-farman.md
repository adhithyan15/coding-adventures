## You'll want to know: فرمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C420-FARMAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فرمان** (*farmân*) — "a steering wheel; a command, an order".

> پدرم فرمان ماشین را با دو دست میگیرد.
>
> *Pedaram farmân-e mâshin râ bâ do dast migirad.*
>
> My father holds the steering wheel with both hands.

The older sense is a command or a royal decree. *Posht-e farmân* — at the wheel, driving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*farmân* is inherited: EtymDB traces it through Middle Persian *framān* to Old Persian *framānā* 'command'. The wheel is the part of the car that gives the orders.
