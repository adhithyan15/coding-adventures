## You'll want to know: پیش پرداخت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C438-TAHVIL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیش پرداخت** (*pishpardâkht*) — "a deposit, an advance payment".

> برای رزرو اتاق پیش پرداخت دادیم.
>
> *Barâ-ye rezerv-e otâq pishpardâkht dâdim.*
>
> We paid a deposit to book the room.

*pish* 'before' + *pardâkht* 'payment'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pishpardâkht* is a native compound of *pish* 'before', usually traced to Middle Persian *pēš*, and *pardâkht* 'payment', the past stem of *pardâkhtan* 'to pay'.
