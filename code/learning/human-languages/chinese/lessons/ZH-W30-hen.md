## Script — two pieces, left before right
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 很

**Nine strokes, but two pieces.** Do not count to nine. Read the character
across, left piece first, the way you write 你.

**The left piece, 彳, three strokes.** Its lower two are the person piece 亻 you
have written since 你: a left-falling stroke, then a vertical down to the foot.
彳 adds one more short left-falling stroke above them. So: a short fall, a longer
fall, then the vertical.

**The right piece, 艮, six strokes.** A horizontal that turns down the right
side of a box without lifting. A short horizontal inside it. A horizontal that
closes the box. Then the left side, down to the foot, flicking up to the right.
A short left-falling stroke on the right, and last the long right-falling
stroke.

3 + 6 = nine, and the first three are a shape your hand already knows.
