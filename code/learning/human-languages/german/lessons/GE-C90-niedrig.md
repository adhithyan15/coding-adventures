**niedrig** — "low".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**niedrig** is the word for "down" + *-ig*. That word ← Old High German *nidar* ← Proto-Germanic *\*niþer* (EtymDB 2.1); English *nether*, "lower", is a cousin.
