## You'll want to know: чаепитие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C499-VISITS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**чаепитие** (*chayepítiye*) — "a tea party, tea-drinking".

> После обеда у нас всегда чаепитие.
>
> *Pósle obéda u nas vsegdá chayepítiye.*
>
> After lunch we always sit down to tea.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*чаепитие* is a compound of *чай* 'tea' and *питие* 'drinking', from *пить* 'to drink'; EtymDB has no entry.
