Punjabi, chapter 272: Lost in the Crowd.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਵਿਛੜਨਾ (vichhaṛnā) — to get separated; to go astray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for west, then the Punjabi for to show someone around.

You'll want to know: ਵਿਛੜਨਾ.
ਵਿਛੜਨਾ — vichhaṛnā — "to get separated; to go astray".
ਭੀੜ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਾਡੇ ਤੋਂ ਨਾ ਵਿਛੜੋ। bhīṛ vich sāḍe ton nā vichhṛo. Do not get separated from us in the crowd.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੋਸਤਾਂ ਤੋਂ ਵਿਛੜਿਆ, maiṁ dostāṁ ton vichhṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿਛੜਨਾ (vichhaṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed vicchaṭ, "be separated"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vicchaṛṇā, "to be separated, go astray", under it (CDIAL 11687).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਚੌਕੀ (chaukī) — a police post; a guard post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to get separated, then the Punjabi for a police constable.

You'll want to know: ਚੌਕੀ.
ਚੌਕੀ — chaukī — "a police post; a guard post".
ਪੁਲਿਸ ਦੀ ਚੌਕੀ ਪੁਲ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਹੈ। pulis dī chaukī pul de neṛe hai. The police post is near the bridge.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਚੌਕੀ (chaukī) is inherited from Sanskrit catuṣka, "a set of four"; CDIAL lists Punjabi caukī, "chair", under it (CDIAL 4629). The sense "a police post" is not given there.
