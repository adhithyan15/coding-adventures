## You'll want to know: chōngdiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C109-SCREEN109-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chōngdiàn* — "to charge a battery".

> *wǒ de shǒujī xūyào chōngdiàn* — My phone needs charging.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chōngdiàn* is built from chōng 'to fill' and 电 'electricity', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhuwng* and *denH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to fill with electricity. A modern word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
