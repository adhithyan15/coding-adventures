## You'll want to know: താപനില
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C509-CUTU509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**താപനില** — *tāpanila* — "temperature".

> ഇന്ന് താപനില മുപ്പതിന് മുകളിൽ ആണ്.
>
> *innŭ tāpanila muppatinŭ mukaḷil āṇŭ.*
>
> Today's temperature is above thirty.

താപം, heat, plus നില, a level: the level of heat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**താപനില** (*tāpanila*) is a Malayalam compound built from താപം, heat, from Sanskrit *tāpa* (Monier-Williams), and the native നില, a standing or level (DEDR 3675).
