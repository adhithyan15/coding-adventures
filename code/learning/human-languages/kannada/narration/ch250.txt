Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸುತ್ತ (sutta) — around.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for opposite, then the Kannada for a district.

You'll want to know: ಸುತ್ತ.
ಸುತ್ತ — sutta — "around".

The word, taken apart.
ಸುತ್ತ (sutta) is native Dravidian, from suttu, "to surround, go round", which DEDR 2715 lists; the entry also has Tamil cuṟṟu and Telugu cuṭṭu.

You'll want to know: ಗುಡಿಸಲು.

The word, taken apart.
ಗುಡಿಸಲು (guḍisalu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kuṭi, Telugu koṭika and Malayalam kuṭi (DEDR 1655). DEDR lists guḍisalu, "hut with a thatched roof". DEDR 1655 compares Sanskrit kūṭa.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a hut, then the Kannada for a residential area.

You'll want to know: ಅಂಚೆ ಕಚೇರಿ.
ಅಂಚೆ ಕಚೇರಿ — añce kacēri — "a post office".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂಚೆ ಕಚೇರಿ (añce kacēri) is añce, "the post, a relay", native Dravidian (DEDR 54, with Tamil añcal), and kacēri, "office", from Hindustani kacahrī.
