## You'll want to know: ಮಾನದಂಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C422-MANADANDA422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾನದಂಡ** — *mānadaṇḍa* — "a criterion; a standard".

> ಆಯ್ಕೆಗೆ ಅಂಕಗಳೇ ಮುಖ್ಯ ಮಾನದಂಡ.
>
> *āykege aṅkagaḷē mukhya mānadaṇḍa.*
>
> Marks are the main criterion for selection.

ಮಾನ (measure) + ದಂಡ (rod): a measuring rod.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಾನದಂಡ** (*mānadaṇḍa*) is built from Sanskrit *māna*, "a measure" (Monier-Williams), and *daṇḍa*, "a stick, a rod" (CDIAL 6128): a measuring rod.
