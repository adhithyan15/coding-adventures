## You'll want to know: gǎnxiè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C77-THANKS77-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnxiè* — "to thank (warmly)".

> *gǎnxiè dàjiā* — Thank you, everyone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnxiè* is built from gǎn 'to be moved' and 谢 'to thank', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *komX* and *zjaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for thanking and answering thanks.
