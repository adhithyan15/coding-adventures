Japanese, chapter 402: A Day of Sightseeing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
かんこう (kankō) — tourism; sightseeing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to change one's clothes, then the Japanese for to entertain guests.

You'll want to know: かんこう.
かんこう — kankō — "tourism; sightseeing".
この しまに かんこうに いきました。 — kono shima ni kankō ni ikimashita — "We went sightseeing on this island."

The word, taken apart.
かんこう (kankō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kan, "view" (Middle Chinese kwan) and kō, "light" (kwang) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
めぐる (meguru) — to tour; to go around.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a famous sight, then the Japanese for a local speciality.

You'll want to know: めぐる.
めぐる — meguru — "to tour; to go around".
じてんしゃで しまの めいしょを めぐりました。 — jitensha de shima no meisho o megurimashita — "We toured the island's sights by bicycle."
Polite めぐります, megurimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): かんこうきゃくが まちを めぐりました。, kankōkyaku ga machi o megurimashita.

The word, taken apart.
めぐる (meguru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
