## You'll want to know: dēliciae, dēliciārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C536-BLAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dēliciae, dēliciārum** — "a darling, pet; delight".

> Hic catulus dēliciae puellae est.
>
> *This puppy is the girl's darling.*

Feminine plural, first declension, often with a singular meaning: *meae dēliciae!* — my darling! English *delicious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēliciae** is built on *dēliciō* "to allure", from *dē-* and *laciō* "to entice" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *delicious* and *delicacy* come from the family.
