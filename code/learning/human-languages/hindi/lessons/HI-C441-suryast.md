## You'll want to know: सूर्यास्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C441-SAVERA441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर्यास्त** — *sūryāst* — "sunset".

> हम समुद्र के किनारे सूर्यास्त देख रहे थे।
>
> *ham samudra ke kināre sūryāst dekh rahe the.*
>
> We were watching the sunset by the sea.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर्यास्त** (*sūryāst*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sūryāsta*, "sunset" (Monier-Williams), from *sūrya*, "the sun", and *asta*, "setting".
