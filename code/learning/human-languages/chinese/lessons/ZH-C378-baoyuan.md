## You'll want to know: bàoyuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C378-NOISE378-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bàoyuàn* — "to complain; to grumble".

> *línjū bàoyuàn wǒmen tài chǎo le* — The neighbours complained that we were too noisy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàoyuàn* is built from *bào* 'to hold' and *yuàn* 'resentment', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bawX* and *'jwonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to grasp' and the second 'to resent': holding on to a grudge.
