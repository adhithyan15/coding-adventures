## You'll want to know: угрожать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C373-OSTOROZHNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**угрожать** (*ugrozhát*) — "to threaten".

> Сильный ветер угрожает старым деревьям.
>
> *Sílnyy véter ugrozháyet stárym derévyam.*
>
> The strong wind threatens the old trees.

Imperfective. *Угрожать кому чем*: the person threatened goes in the dative. The noun is *угроза*, a threat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**угрожать** is built from *у-* and *гроза* "a thunderstorm", which in older Russian also meant terror and threat; *з* softens to *ж* in the verb.
