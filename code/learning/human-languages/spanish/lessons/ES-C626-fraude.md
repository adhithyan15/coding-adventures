## You'll want to know: el fraude
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C626-JUICIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el fraude** — "the fraud".

> Fue un fraude de millones de euros.
>
> *It was a fraud worth millions of euros.*

*Fraude fiscal* — tax fraud.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fraude* is a learned borrowing from Latin *fraus, fraudis* 'deceit, harm' (EtymDB).
