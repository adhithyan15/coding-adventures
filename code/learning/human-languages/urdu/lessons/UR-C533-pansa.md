## You'll want to know: پانسہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C533-JUA533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پانسہ** — *pāṅsā* — "a die (for a game)".

> اگر پانسے پر چھ آیا تو میں جیتوں گا۔
>
> *agar pāṅse par chhe āyā to maiṅ jītūṅ gā.*
>
> If the die shows a six, I'll win.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پانسہ** (*pāṅsā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pāsā*, "a die", under Sanskrit *pāśa*, "a die, dice" (CDIAL 8132).
