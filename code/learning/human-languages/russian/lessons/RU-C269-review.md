# Review — if, and it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-CLAUSE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-LI-NOT-YESLI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if you like, we can go to the cinema". (*Yésli khóchesh,
my mózhem poytí v kinó.*) Then "I don't know if the museum is open". (*Ya
ne znáyu, rabótayet li muzéy.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-FUTURE-01, RU-LEX-ZAVISIT-OT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-OT-GENITIVE-01, RU-LEX-C59-WEATHER-04, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-03] -->

Three lessons, and Russian can now look ahead on a condition. **Если**
with a comma at the seam. The future on both sides when the condition
lies ahead, with **будет** and a perfective's forward-pointing forms. **Ли**
when "if" means "whether". And **это зависит от** with the "of" form.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-LEX-ZAVISIT-OT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-OT-GENITIVE-01] -->

> **— Ты пойдёшь в музей в субботу?**
> **— Это зависит от погоды. Если будет дождь, пойду.**

*Poydú*, "I'll go": the perfective, one visit to come. The answer
depends, and then says on what.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-CLAUSE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-COMPARATIVE-EE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-CHEM-THAN-01, RU-GRAMMAR-UZHE-PRESENT-DURATION-01, RU-GRAMMAR-NAZAD-AGO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-GOD-GODA-LET-01, RU-LEX-C114-THINGS114-01, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-02, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-03, RU-LEX-C41-VERB4-02, RU-LEX-C189-KACH189-05] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *Éto zavísit ot pogódy. Yésli búdet dozhd, poydú.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Yésli búdet vrémya, ya pozvonyú.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Póyezd deshévle, chem samolyót, no samolyót bystréye.*]
- [YOU ANSWER: *Ty poydyósh v park?* Make it depend on something, then add one *yésli*]
- [YOU SAY: *Dva góda nazád ya byl v Moskvé, i ya uzhé dva góda uchú rússkiy.*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-LI-NOT-YESLI-01, RU-GRAMMAR-OT-GENITIVE-01] -->

зависит от цены.***)
