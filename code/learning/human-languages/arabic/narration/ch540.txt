Arabic, chapter 540: Reading, clubs and passions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قراءة (qirāʾa) — reading.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for gardening, then the Arabic for to swing.

You'll want to know: قراءة.
قراءة — qirāʾa — "reading".
القراءة هوايتي المفضلة. al-qirāʾa hiwāyatī al-mufaḍḍala. Reading is my favourite hobby.

The word, taken apart.
قراءة (qirāʾa) is built on the native Arabic root q-r-a, "to read, to recite" (Lane). Qirāʾa is the verbal noun of qaraʾa, to read, which you know.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: اشترك.
اشترك — ishtaraka — "to subscribe; to take part".
اشترك أخي في مجلة رياضية. ishtaraka akhī fī majalla riyāḍiyya. My brother subscribed to a sports magazine.
Present: يشترك, yashtarik.
It takes في.

The word, taken apart.
اشترك (ishtaraka) is built on the native Arabic root sh-r-k, "to share, to be a partner" (Lane). Sharika, a company, and shāraka, to share, which you know, are from the same root.

Present: يلتقي, yaltaqī.

The word, taken apart.
التقى (iltaqā) is built on the native Arabic root l-q-y, "to meet; to throw" (Lane). Ilā l-liqāʾ, see you, which you know, is from the same root.

Lesson 4 of 5.
شغف (shaghaf) — passion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to subscribe, then the Arabic for to meet up.

You'll want to know: شغف.
شغف — shaghaf — "passion".
يتكلم المعلم عن الأدب بشغف. yatakallamu al-muʿallim ʿan al-adab bi-shaghaf. The teacher talks about literature with passion.

The word, taken apart.
شغف (shaghaf) is built on the native Arabic root sh-gh-f, "the heart's covering; to strike the heart" (Lane). Lane gives shaghaf as love that reaches the shaghāf, the covering of the heart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ولع (walaʿ) — fondness, keen love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to meet up, then the Arabic for passion.

You'll want to know: ولع.
ولع — walaʿ — "fondness, keen love".
عند ابني ولع بالسيارات. ʿinda ibnī walaʿ bi-s-sayyārāt. My son is crazy about cars.
مولع بـ is keen on.

The word, taken apart.
ولع (walaʿ) is built on the native Arabic root w-l-ʿ, "to make eagerly desirous" (Lane). Lane gives Form IV awlaʿa as to make someone eagerly fond of a thing.
