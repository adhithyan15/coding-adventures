## You'll want to know: هیزم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C470-CHADOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**هیزم** (*hizom*) — "firewood".

> پدرم با هیزم آتش روشن کرد.
>
> *Pedaram bâ hizom âtash roshan kard.*
>
> My father lit a fire with firewood.

*Hizom shekastan* — to chop firewood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hizom* is a native word, usually traced to Middle Persian *ēzm* 'firewood'. EtymDB does not list it.
