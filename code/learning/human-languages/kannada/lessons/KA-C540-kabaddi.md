## You'll want to know: ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C540-KUSTI540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ** — *kabaḍḍi* — "kabaddi".

> ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಹುಡುಗರು ಮೈದಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ ಆಡುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *haḷḷiya huḍugaru maidānadalli kabaḍḍi āḍuttāre.*
>
> The village boys play kabaddi on the ground.

A raider holds his breath, chants, and tags players on the other side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ** (*kabaḍḍi*) is the name of a team game of south Asia; the origin of the name is not settled.
