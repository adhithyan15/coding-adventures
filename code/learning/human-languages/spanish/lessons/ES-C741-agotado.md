## You'll want to know: agotado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C741-CANSANCIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**agotado** — "exhausted; sold out".

> Después del viaje estoy agotado.
>
> *After the trip I am exhausted.*

On a ticket office: *agotado* — sold out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*agotado* is the participle of *agotar*, usually explained as built on *gota* "drop", from Latin *gutta*: emptied down to the last drop.
