## You'll want to know: आगमन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C409-UDAN409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आगमन** — *āgaman* — "arrival (formal)".

> विमान का आगमन सुबह सात बजे है।
>
> *vimān kā āgaman subah sāt baje hai.*
>
> The plane arrives at seven in the morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आगमन** (*āgaman*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *āgamana*, "coming, arriving" (Monier-Williams), from the root *gam*, "go": the word on station and airport boards.
