## You'll want to know: ingénuo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C472-ESPERTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ingénuo** — "naive".

> Era tão ingénuo que acreditava em tudo.
>
> *He was so naive that he believed everything.*

In Brazil: *ingênuo*. Not English *ingenious*: it means naive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingénuo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ingenuus* "native, freeborn", and so "frank, open" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
