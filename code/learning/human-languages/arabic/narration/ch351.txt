Arabic, chapter 351: Tricks and lies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خدع (khadaʿa) — to deceive, to trick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a leaflet, then the Arabic for a supplement.

You'll want to know: خدع.
خدع — khadaʿa — "to deceive, to trick".
خدع الثعلب الغراب في الحكاية. khadaʿa ath-thaʿlab al-ghurāb fī al-ḥikāya. The fox tricked the crow in the tale.
Present: يخدع, yakhdaʿ.
A trick is خدعة.

The word, taken apart.
خدع (khadaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root kh-d-ʿ (Lane), "to hide a thing; to deceive"; khidʿa is a ruse.

Lesson 3 of 5.
احتال (iḥtāla) — to swindle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to deceive, then the Arabic for to cheat.

You'll want to know: احتال.
احتال — iḥtāla — "to swindle".
احتال رجل على السياح في السوق. iḥtāla rajul ʿalā as-suyyāḥ fī as-sūq. A man swindled the tourists in the market.
Present: يحتال, yaḥtāl.
It takes على. A swindler is محتال.

The word, taken apart.
احتال (iḥtāla) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-w-l (Lane), "to change, to shift", where ḥīla is a shift or wile. It is Form VIII, "to use a wile".

Lesson 4 of 5.
زور (zawwara) — to forge, to falsify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to cheat, then the Arabic for to swindle.

You'll want to know: زور.
زور — zawwara — "to forge, to falsify".
زور الرجل جواز سفر. zawwara ar-rajul jawāz safar. The man forged a passport.
Present: يزور, yuzawwir.
Lane files the taught زار, to visit, under the same letters z-w-r; the two verbs differ in form and sense.

The word, taken apart.
زور (zawwara) is built on the native Arabic root z-w-r (Lane), where zūr is falsehood. It is Form II, "to make a false thing look true".
