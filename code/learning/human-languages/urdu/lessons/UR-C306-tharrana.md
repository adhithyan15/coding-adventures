## You'll want to know: تھرانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C306-DHAMAKA306-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تھرانا** — *tharrānā* — "to quake; to shudder".

> دھماکے سے کھڑکیاں تھرا گئیں۔
>
> *dhamāke se khiṛkiyāṅ tharrā gaīṅ.*
>
> The windows shook with the blast.

Past (he …): وہ تھرایا, *voh tharrāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تھرانا** (*tharrānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *tharrānā*, "to tremble", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6092).
