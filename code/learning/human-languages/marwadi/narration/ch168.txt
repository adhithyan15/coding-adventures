Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 168: Sun and Shade.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ऊगणो (ūgṇo) — to rise (of the sun).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for last, then the Marwadi for at once.

You'll want to know: ऊगणो.
ऊगणो — ūgṇo — "to rise (of the sun)".
सूरज दिनूगै ऊगै है। sūraj dinūgai ūgai hai. The sun rises early in the morning.
Past (it …): वो सूरज ऊग्यो, vo sūraj ūgyo.

The word, taken apart.
ऊगणो (ūgṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed udgāti, "rises, comes forth"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ūgaï, "rises, appears" (CDIAL 1954).

Lesson 2 of 5.
धूप (dhūp) — sunshine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for at once, then the Marwadi for to rise.

You'll want to know: धूप.
धूप — dhūp — "sunshine".
आज धूप घणी तेज है। āj dhūp ghaṇī tej hai. The sun is very strong today.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धूप (dhūp) comes from a reconstructed dhuppā, "sunshine"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati dhūp (CDIAL 6825). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry. It is feminine: धूप घणी तेज है.

Lesson 4 of 5.
तुळसी (tuḷsī) — holy basil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for sunshine, then the Marwadi for shade.

You'll want to know: तुळसी.
तुळसी — tuḷsī — "holy basil".
म्हारी तुळसी हरी अर सुंदर है। mhārī tuḷsī harī ar sundar hai. My holy basil is green and beautiful.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तुळसी (tuḷsī) is inherited from Sanskrit tulasī, "the sacred basil plant"; CDIAL lists Gujarati tuḷsī and Hindi tulsī (CDIAL 5885). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सींचणो (sī̃chṇo) — to water plants.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for shade, then the Marwadi for holy basil.

You'll want to know: सींचणो.
सींचणो — sī̃chṇo — "to water plants".
हूं रोज तुळसी सींचूं हूं। hū̃ roj tuḷsī sī̃chū̃ hū̃. I water the holy basil every day.
Past (I …): म्हैं तुळसी सींची, mhaĩ tuḷsī sī̃chī.

The word, taken apart.
सींचणो (sī̃chṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit siñcati, "pours out, sprinkles"; CDIAL lists Hindi sī̃cnā and Gujarati sĩcvũ (CDIAL 13394). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
