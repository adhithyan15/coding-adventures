## You'll want to know: അനുമതി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C444-KATAMA444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുമതി** — *anumati* — "official permission; consent".

> യോഗം നടത്താൻ പോലീസ് അനുമതി നൽകി.
>
> *yōgaṁ naṭattān pōlīsŭ anumati nalki.*
>
> The police gave permission to hold the meeting.

A formal word beside the everyday അനുവാദം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുമതി** (*anumati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anumati*, "assent, permission, approval" (Monier-Williams).
