## You'll want to know: beachten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C381-VORSCHRIFT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**beachten** — "to observe, to pay attention to".

> Viele Autofahrer beachten das Tempolimit nicht.
>
> *Many drivers do not observe the speed limit.*

Inseparable: *er beachtet, er hat beachtet*. *Etwas nicht beachten* — to ignore something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beachten* is inherited from Middle High German *beahten* and Old High German *biahtōn* (EtymDB), built on *Acht* "attention", Old High German *ahta* (EtymDB): you pay attention.
