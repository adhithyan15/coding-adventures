## You'll want to know: うたがう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C248-TRUST248-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うたがう** — *utagau* — "to doubt; to suspect".

> **ははは ちちの こたえを うたがいません。** — *haha wa chichi no kotae o utagaimasen* — "My mother does not doubt my father's answer."

Polite **うたがいます**, *utagaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うたがう** (*utagau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
