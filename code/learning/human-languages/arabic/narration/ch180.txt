Arabic, chapter 180: Injured, Rural, Traditional, Foreign, Flat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مصاب (muṣāb) — injured.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for humid, then the Arabic for rainy.

You'll want to know: مصاب.
مصاب — muṣāb — "injured".

The word, taken apart.
مصاب (muṣāb) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-w-b, "to hit the mark" (Lane). Muṣāb is the Form IV passive participle, "struck, hit", hence "injured".
