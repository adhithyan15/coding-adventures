## You'll want to know: شهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C325-SHAHID325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شهد** — *shahida* — "to witness; to testify".

> شهدت المدينة مطرا شديدا أمس.
>
> *shahidat al-madīna maṭaran shadīdan ams.*
>
> The city saw heavy rain yesterday.

Present: يشهد, *yashhad*.

شهد على or شهد بـ: to testify. A witness is شاهد.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شهد** (*shahida*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-h-d* (Lane), "to be present at a thing and see it"; *shahāda* is testimony, and a certificate.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
