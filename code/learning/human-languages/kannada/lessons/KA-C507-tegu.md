## You'll want to know: ತೇಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C507-SADDU507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೇಗು** — *tēgu* — "to belch; to burp".

> ಊಟದ ನಂತರ ಮಗು ತೇಗಿತು.
>
> *ūṭada nantara magu tēgitu.*
>
> After the meal the baby burped.

Past (I …): ತೇಗಿದೆ, *tēgide*.

ತೇಗು is also the burp itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೇಗು** (*tēgu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3451 lists Kannada *tēgu*, *tēku*, "to belch; a belch", beside Tamil *tēkku*.
