## You'll want to know: एटीएम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C426-RAQAM426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**एटीएम** — *eṭīem* — "a cash machine, an ATM".

> बाज़ार के पास एक एटीएम है।
>
> *bāzār ke pās ek eṭīem hai.*
>
> There is a cash machine near the market.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एटीएम** (*eṭīem*) is borrowed from English *ATM*, said letter by letter.
