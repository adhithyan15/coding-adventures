## You'll want to know: verunglücken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C288-ZUSAMMENSTOSS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**verunglücken** — "to have an accident".

> Mein Onkel verunglückte mit dem Fahrrad.
>
> *My uncle had an accident on his bike.*

Perfekt with *sein*: *Er ist verunglückt*. Built on *das Unglück*, misfortune.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verunglücken* is built on *Unglück* "misfortune", Middle High German *unglück* (EtymDB), from *Glück* "luck", Middle High German *gelücke*.
