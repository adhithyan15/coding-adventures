# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C279-KAR-SEQUENCE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C279-FIRST-THEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I washed my hands and ate". (*maiṁne hāth dhokar khānā khāyā*.) Then tell this morning in two steps, with **पहले** and **फिर**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C279-KAR-SEQUENCE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C279-NE-KE-BAAD-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C279-FIRST-THEN-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C279-USKE-BAAD-01, HI-LEX-C279-AKHIR-01, HI-SKILL-C279-READ-STORY-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C278-PICHLA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First I got up, then I bathed.]
- [YOU SAY: After breakfast I went to the station and bought a ticket.]
- [YOU SAY: I was reading on the train when it suddenly rained.]
- [YOU SAY: After that we sat in the courtyard, and in the end we slept.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira's holiday in four sentences, with pahle, phir, uske bād and ākhir meṁ.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C279-KAR-SEQUENCE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C279-NE-KE-BAAD-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C279-FIRST-THEN-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C279-USKE-BAAD-01, HI-LEX-C279-AKHIR-01, HI-SKILL-C279-READ-STORY-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I ate and fell asleep"? (**मैं खाना खाकर सो गया**: no **ने**, because **सो गया** has no thing.) "After eating"? (**खाना खाने के बाद**.) "After that"? (**उसके बाद**.) "At last"? (**आख़िर**.) Who told Mira a story, and why **सुनाई**? (Her grandmother: **दादी ने … कहानी सुनाई**, and **कहानी** is feminine.)
