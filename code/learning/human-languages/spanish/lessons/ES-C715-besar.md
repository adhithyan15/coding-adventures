## You'll want to know: besar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C715-AMOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**besar** — "to kiss".

> Me besó en la cara.
>
> *She kissed me on the face.*

*Un beso* is a kiss; *besos* closes many messages.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*besar* is usually traced to Latin *bāsiāre* "to kiss" (Lewis & Short), built on *bāsium* "a kiss"; the Latin *-si-* became *-s-*.
