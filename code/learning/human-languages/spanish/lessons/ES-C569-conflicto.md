## You'll want to know: el conflicto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C569-LUCHA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el conflicto** — "the conflict".

> El conflicto duró muchos años.
>
> *The conflict lasted many years.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conflicto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnflīctus* 'a striking together' (Lewis & Short), from *cōnflīgere* 'to strike together'.
