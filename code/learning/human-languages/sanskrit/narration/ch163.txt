क्रीडा (krīḍā) — sport, play.

You'll want to know: क्रीडा.
क्रीडा — krīḍā — "sport, play".

The word, taken apart.
krīḍā comes from the root √krīḍ "play, sport, frolic" (MW), already in the Rigveda. krīḍanaka "a toy" comes from the same root.

Lesson 3 of 5.
उत्सवः (utsavaḥ) — a festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sport, then the Sanskrit for gambling.

You'll want to know: उत्सवः.
उत्सवः — utsavaḥ — "a festival".

The word, taken apart.
utsava comes from ud-√sū "set going, stir up", the root √sū "impel" with ud "up" (MW; CDIAL 1876): in the Rigveda it means "an enterprise, a beginning", and later "a festival".

Lesson 4 of 5.
विवाहः (vivāhaḥ) — a wedding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gambling, then the Sanskrit for a festival.

You'll want to know: विवाहः.
विवाहः — vivāhaḥ — "a wedding".

The word, taken apart.
vivāha comes from vi-√vah "carry away, lead away", the root √vah "carry" with vi "away" (MW; CDIAL 11920): leading the bride away from her father's house. MW sets √vah beside Latin vehere (English vehicle), and it lives on as Hindi byāh "marriage" (CDIAL 11920).

The word, taken apart.
upahāra comes from upa-√hṛ "bring near, offer", the root √hṛ "carry" with upa "near" (MW): "an offering", and so "a gift". It is built like āhāra "food", from the same root.
