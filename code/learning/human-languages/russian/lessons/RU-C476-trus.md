## You'll want to know: трус
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C476-FRIGHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**трус** (*trus*) — "a coward".

> В начале фильма герой был трусом.
>
> *V nachále fílma geróy byl trúsom.*
>
> At the start of the film the hero was a coward.

Masculine. The adjective is *трусливый* — cowardly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *трус* without a source; it is usually linked to *трусить* 'to shake, to tremble', so a coward is someone who trembles.
