**しょうせつ** — *shōsetsu* — "a novel; a story".

> **なつやすみに ながい しょうせつを よみました。** — *natsuyasumi ni nagai shōsetsu o yomimashita* — "In the summer holidays I read a long novel."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうせつ** (*shōsetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "small" (Middle Chinese *sjewX*) and *setsu*, "explanation, theory" (*sywet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
