## You'll want to know: પ્રજા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C352-SARKAR352-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રજા** — *prajā* — "the people of a state".

> સારી સરકાર પ્રજાની વાત સાંભળે છે.
>
> *sārī sarkār prajānī vāt sā̃bhḷe che.*
>
> A good government listens to its people.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રજા** (*prajā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prajā*, "offspring; subjects" (Monier-Williams).
