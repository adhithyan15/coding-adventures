Portuguese, chapter 63: Lion, Monkey, Bee, Ant, Elephant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o leão — a lion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a sheep, then the Portuguese for a goat.

You'll want to know: o leão.
o leão — "a lion".

The word, taken apart.
leão from Latin leo, leonis, "lion" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. Latin took it from Greek leōn.

The word, taken apart.
