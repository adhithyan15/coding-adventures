Kannada, chapter 395: Running Public Offices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಆಡಳಿತ (āḍaḷita) — administration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to boycott, then the Kannada for leadership.

You'll want to know: ಆಡಳಿತ.
ಆಡಳಿತ — āḍaḷita — "administration".
ಊರಿನ ಆಡಳಿತ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತಿದೆ. ūrina āḍaḷita cennāgi naḍeyuttide. The town's administration is running well.
ಆಡಳಿತ (āḍaḷita) ಪಕ್ಷ, the ruling party.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಡಳಿತ (āḍaḷita) is usually traced to the native Dravidian āḷ, "to possess, to rule, to manage" (DEDR 5157); DEDR does not list āḍaḷita itself, so how the word was formed is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ (sacivālaya) — a ministry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for leadership, then the Kannada for administration.

You'll want to know: ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ.
ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ — sacivālaya — "a ministry".
ಅವನ ಅಪ್ಪ ಸಚಿವಾಲಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. avana appa sacivālayadalli kelasa māḍuttāre. His father works in the ministry.
ಸಚಿವ (minister) + ಆಲಯ (house).

The word, taken apart.
ಸಚಿವಾಲಯ (sacivālaya) is built from Sanskrit saciva, "a companion, a king's minister" (Monier-Williams), and ālaya, "a house" (CDIAL 1366).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ (pañcāyiti) — a village council.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for administration, then the Kannada for a ministry.

You'll want to know: ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ.
ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ — pañcāyiti — "a village council".
ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಹೊಸ ಶಾಲೆ ಕಟ್ಟಿಸಿತು. pañcāyiti ūrinalli hosa śāle kaṭṭisitu. The village council had a new school built.
ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ (pañcāyiti) ಚುನಾವಣೆ, a council election.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ (pañcāyiti) is a loan from Hindustani pañcāyat, "a council", once a body of five elders, from Sanskrit pañca, "five" (CDIAL 7655).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಜಾರಿಗೊಳಿಸು (jārigoḷisu) — to implement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ministry, then the Kannada for a village council.

You'll want to know: ಜಾರಿಗೊಳಿಸು.
ಜಾರಿಗೊಳಿಸು — jārigoḷisu — "to implement".
ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಹೊಸ ಯೋಜನೆಯನ್ನು ಜಾರಿಗೊಳಿಸಿತು. sarkāra hosa yōjaneyannu jārigoḷisitu. The government implemented the new scheme.
Past (I …): ಜಾರಿಗೊಳಿಸಿದೆ, jārigoḷiside.
ಜಾರಿಗೆ ಬರು, to come into force.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಾರಿಗೊಳಿಸು (jārigoḷisu) is built on jārī, "current, in force", a loan from Hindustani that comes from Arabic jārī, "flowing", with the Kannada ending -goḷisu, "to make".
