# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C340-IYITE-01, ML-SCRIPT-C340-II-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "recently". (*īyiṭe*.) Then write its first letter, **ഈ**, once from memory.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C340-IYITE-01, ML-SCRIPT-C340-II-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ENNITTU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ATINUSESHAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-APPOL-01, ML-SKILL-C340-READ-STORY-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-PROGRESSIVE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-HABITUAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: "I drank tea. And then I went to the office."]
- [YOU SAY: "When I arrived, Grandmother was waiting."]
- [YOU SAY: the Saturday story: the city, first the market, after that tea, finally home]
- [YOU SAY: "Recently I saw a good film. And then I went home."]
- [YOU SAY: from the reading, what used to happen in the village every day]
- [YOU WRITE: **ഈയിടെ**, then **ഈ വീട്**, "this house"]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C340-IYITE-01, ML-SCRIPT-C340-II-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ENNITTU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ATINUSESHAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-APPOL-01, ML-SKILL-C340-READ-STORY-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "And then", opening a sentence? (**എന്നിട്ട്**.) "After that"? (**അതിനുശേഷം**.)
"When I arrived"? (**എത്തിയപ്പോൾ**.) "Recently"? (**ഈയിടെ**, which opens with **ഈ**.)
In the reading, who was waiting, and what was the writer doing on the bus? (**Grandmother; reading
a book.**)
