## You'll want to know: निवेदक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C312-VAHINI312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निवेदक** — *nivedak* — "a newsreader; a presenter".

> निवेदकाने शांत आवाजात बातमी वाचली.
>
> *nivedakāne śānt āvājāt bātmī vāclī.*
>
> The newsreader read the news in a calm voice.

Masculine.

A woman presenter is निवेदिका.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवेदक** (*nivedak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nivedaka*, 'communicating, relating' (Monier-Williams).
