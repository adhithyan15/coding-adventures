## You'll want to know: مرر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C546-MUBARA546-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مرر** — *marrara* — "to pass the ball".

> مرر اللاعب الكرة إلى زميله.
>
> *marrara al-lāʿib al-kura ilā zamīlihi.*
>
> The player passed the ball to his teammate.

Present: يمرر, *yumarrir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرر** (*marrara*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-r-r*, "to pass, to go by" (Lane). Form II marrara is to make a thing pass.
