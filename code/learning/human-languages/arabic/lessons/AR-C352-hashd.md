## You'll want to know: حشد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C352-MUKHALAFA352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حشد** — *ḥashd* — "a crowd, a gathering".

> انتظر حشد كبير أمام الملعب.
>
> *intaẓara ḥashd kabīr amāma al-malʿab.*
>
> A big crowd waited in front of the stadium.

Plural: حشود, *ḥushūd*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حشد** (*ḥashd*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-sh-d* (Lane), "to gather people together"; *ḥashd* is the gathering.
