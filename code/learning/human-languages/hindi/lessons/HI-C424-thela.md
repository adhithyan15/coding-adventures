## You'll want to know: ठेला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C424-DHABA424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ठेला** — *ṭhelā* — "a handcart".

> एक आदमी ठेले पर सब्ज़ी बेच रहा था।
>
> *ek ādmī ṭhele par sabzī bec rahā thā.*
>
> A man was selling vegetables from a handcart.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठेला** (*ṭhelā*) goes back to *\*ṭhēḍḍ*, "push", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5512), which lists Hindi *ṭhelā*, "a push-cart": the cart you push.
