## You'll want to know: humilde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C726-HUMILDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**humilde** — "humble; poor".

> A pesar de su fama, es muy humilde.
>
> *Despite her fame, she is very humble.*

*Una familia humilde* is a poor family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*humilde* is inherited from Latin *humilis* "low, lowly" (EtymDB), built on *humus* "earth, ground".
