## You'll want to know: कस्टम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C410-PASPORT410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कस्टम** — *kasṭam* — "customs".

> कस्टम पर एक आदमी ने मेरी अटैची खोली।
>
> *kasṭam par ek ādmī ne merī aṭaicī kholī.*
>
> At customs a man opened my suitcase.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कस्टम** (*kasṭam*) is borrowed from English *customs*, the check on goods at an airport or a border; Hindi has dropped the final s.
