## You'll want to know: खोदणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C335-UTKHANAN335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खोदणे** — *khodṇe* — "to dig".

> मजुरांनी तिथे नवी विहीर खोदली.
>
> *majurānnī tithe navī vihīr khodlī.*
>
> The workers dug a new well there.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने खोदलं, *tyāne khodlaṁ*.

What is dug agrees with the past: विहीर खोदली, रस्ता खोदला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खोदणे** (*khodṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *khodṇe*, 'to dig, carve', under *\*khōdd*, 'dig' (CDIAL 3934).
