## You'll want to know: ससुराल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C117-AUNT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ससुराल** (*sasurāl*) — "the in-laws' home", the family a person marries into.

**वह ससुराल गई है** — "she has gone to her in-laws' home".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Aunts, uncles, a sister-in-law, and the home of the in-laws.
