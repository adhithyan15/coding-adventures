Punjabi, chapter 59: To wash, To laugh, To cry, To sing, To dance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਧੋਣਾ (dhoṇā) — to wash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to open, then the Punjabi for to cook.

You'll want to know: ਧੋਣਾ.
ਧੋਣਾ — dhoṇā — "to wash".

The word, taken apart.
ਧੋਣਾ (dhoṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhauvati, "washes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6886).
