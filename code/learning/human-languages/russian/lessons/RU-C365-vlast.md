## You'll want to know: власть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C365-VLAST-03]; assesses=[] -->

**власть** (*vlast*) — "power, authority".

> В этой стране власть часто меняется.
>
> *V étoy strané vlast chásto menyáyetsya.*
>
> In this country power often changes hands.

Feminine. The plural *власти* means the authorities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**власть** "power, authority" has the Church Slavonic *-ла-*: Church Slavonic *vlastĭ* and native *волость* both come from Proto-Slavic *\*volstĭ* "rule, power" (EtymDB). *Волость* was once a district under someone’s rule.
