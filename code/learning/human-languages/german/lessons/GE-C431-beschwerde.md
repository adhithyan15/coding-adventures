## You'll want to know: die Beschwerde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C431-BESCHWERDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Beschwerde** — "the complaint".

> Der Gast schreibt eine Beschwerde an das Hotel.
>
> *The guest writes a complaint to the hotel.*

Plural: *die Beschwerden*. *Beschwerden* also means aches, symptoms: *Magenbeschwerden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Beschwerde* is built on *beschweren* 'to weigh down, to burden', from *schwer* 'heavy', inherited from Old High German *swār* (EtymDB): a complaint is a weight put on someone.
