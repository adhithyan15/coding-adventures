## You'll want to know: సంపద
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C404-PEDARIKAM404-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపద** — *sampada* — "wealth; riches".

> ఆ కుటుంబం దగ్గర చాలా సంపద ఉంది.
>
> *ā kuṭumbaṁ daggara cālā sampada undi.*
>
> That family has a great deal of wealth.

Also a country’s riches: సహజ సంపద, natural resources.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపద** (*sampada*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sampad*, "success" (CDIAL 12932), which Monier-Williams also glosses "accomplishment, perfection"; Telugu uses it for wealth.
