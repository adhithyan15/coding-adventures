## You'll want to know: ತಾಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C371-PALLAKKI371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಳಿ** — *tāḷi* — "a wedding pendant".

> ಮದುವೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಗಂಡು ಹೆಣ್ಣಿಗೆ ತಾಳಿ ಕಟ್ಟಿದನು.
>
> *maduveyalli gaṇḍu heṇṇige tāḷi kaṭṭidanu.*
>
> At the wedding the groom tied the wedding pendant on the bride.

The gold pendant on a cord tied at a Hindu wedding; ತಾಳಿ ಕಟ್ಟು, to tie it, means to marry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಳಿ** (*tāḷi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3175 lists Kannada *tāli*, *tāḷi*, "a small round plate of gold worn at the neck as a marriage badge", beside Tamil *tāli*.
