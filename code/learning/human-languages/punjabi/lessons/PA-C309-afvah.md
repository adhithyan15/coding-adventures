# ਅਫ਼ਵਾਹ (afvāh) — a rumour

## You'll want to know: ਅਫ਼ਵਾਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C309-NEWS309-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਫ਼ਵਾਹ** — *afvāh* — "a rumour".

> ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਦਸੇ ਬਾਰੇ ਇੱਕ ਅਫ਼ਵਾਹ ਸੀ।
>
> *bāzār vich hādse bāre ikk afvāh sī.*
>
> There was a rumour in the market about an accident.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਫ਼ਵਾਹ** (*afvāh*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *afwāh*, "mouths", the plural of the word for a mouth.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
