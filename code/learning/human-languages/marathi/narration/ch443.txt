Marathi, chapter 443: Streams and Dams.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ओढा (oḍhā) — a stream; a brook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a foothill, then the Marathi for a mountain range.

You'll want to know: ओढा.
ओढा — oḍhā — "a stream; a brook".
गावाबाहेरून एक लहान ओढा वाहतो. gāvābāherūn ek lahān oḍhā vāhto. A small stream flows past the village.
Masculine. Plural: ओढे, oḍhe.
Smaller than a नदी; in the dry season many ओढे dry up.

The word, taken apart.
ओढा (oḍhā) is a Marathi word for a small stream; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
धरण (dharaṇ) — a dam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a stream, then the Marathi for a canal.

You'll want to know: धरण.
धरण — dharaṇ — "a dam".
पावसाळ्यात धरण भरतं. pāvsāḷyāt dharaṇ bhartaṁ. In the rainy season the dam fills up.
Neuter.
A noun of its own: धरण (dharaṇ) भरणे, a dam fills up.

The word, taken apart.
धरण (dharaṇ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi dharaṇ, "dam", under Sanskrit dharaṇa, "supporting, a prop" (CDIAL 6742).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सरोवर (sarovar) — a large lake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a canal, then the Marathi for a dam.

You'll want to know: सरोवर.
सरोवर — sarovar — "a large lake".
सरोवराच्या काठावर खूप पक्षी असतात. sarovarācyā kāṭhāvar khūp pakṣī astāt. There are lots of birds on the shore of the lake.
Neuter.
A literary word, bigger than a तलाव.

The word, taken apart.
सरोवर (sarovar) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sarovara, "a lake, a big pond" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
खळखळणे (khaḷkhaḷṇe) — to gurgle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a dam, then the Marathi for a large lake.

You'll want to know: खळखळणे.
खळखळणे — khaḷkhaḷṇe — "to gurgle".
दगडांमधून पाणी खळखळत वाहतं. dagaḍāmmadhūn pāṇī khaḷkhaḷat vāhtaṁ. The water gurgles as it flows between the stones.
Past (he …): तो खळखळला, to khaḷkhaḷlā.
An echo verb: खळखळ is the sound of a running stream.

The word, taken apart.
खळखळणे (khaḷkhaḷṇe) is an echo verb built on the sound khaḷkhaḷ, running water over stones; its origin is not settled.
