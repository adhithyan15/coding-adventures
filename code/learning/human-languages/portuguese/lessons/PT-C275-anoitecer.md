## You'll want to know: o anoitecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C275-ENTARDECER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o anoitecer** — "nightfall".

> Chegámos à aldeia ao anoitecer.
>
> *We reached the village at nightfall.*

Also a verb: *no inverno, anoitece cedo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anoitecer* comes down from Old Portuguese (EtymDB), built on *noite* "night", from Latin *nox, noctis* (Lewis & Short).
