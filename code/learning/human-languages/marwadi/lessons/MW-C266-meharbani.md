## You'll want to know: मेहरबानी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C266-HAJAR266-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मेहरबानी** — *meharbānī* — "a kindness".

> घणी मेहरबानी सा!
>
> *ghaṇī meharbānī sā!*
>
> That's very kind of you!

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेहरबानी** (*meharbānī*) is usually traced to Persian *mihrbānī*, "kindness", a noun built on *mihrbān*, "kind".
