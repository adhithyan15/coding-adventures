## You'll want to know: ख़ौफ़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C557-ANDESHA557-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ौफ़** — *khauf* — "fear, dread".

> रात को जंगल में हमें ख़ौफ़ लगा।
>
> *Rāt ko jaṅgal meṁ hamẽ khauf lagā.*
>
> We felt afraid in the forest at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ौफ़** (*khauf*) is usually traced to Arabic *khawf*, "fear", through Persian.
