## You'll want to know: దోషి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C350-NERAM350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**దోషి** — *dōṣi* — "a guilty person; a culprit".

> న్యాయస్థానం అతన్ని దోషిగా తేల్చింది.
>
> *nyāyasthānaṁ atanni dōṣigā tēlcindi.*
>
> The court found him guilty.

దోషం is a fault: దోషి is the one at fault.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దోషి** (*dōṣi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *doṣin*, "faulty; guilty of an offence" (Monier-Williams), from *doṣa*, "a fault".
