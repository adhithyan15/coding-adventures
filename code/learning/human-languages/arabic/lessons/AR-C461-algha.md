## You'll want to know: ألغى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C461-ILGHA461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ألغى** — *alghā* — "to cancel".

> ألغت الشركة الرحلة بسبب العاصفة.
>
> *alghat ash-sharika ar-riḥla bi-sabab al-ʿāṣifa.*
>
> The company cancelled the flight because of the storm.

Present: يلغي, *yulghī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ألغى** (*alghā*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-gh-w*, "to be of no account" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to make null".
