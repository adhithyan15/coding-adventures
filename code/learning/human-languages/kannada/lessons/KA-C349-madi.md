## You'll want to know: ಮಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C349-KHADGA349-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಡಿ** — *maḍi* — "to fall in battle; to die".

> ಆ ಯುದ್ಧದಲ್ಲಿ ಸಾವಿರಾರು ಸೈನಿಕರು ಮಡಿದರು.
>
> *ā yuddhadalli sāvirāru sainikaru maḍidaru.*
>
> Thousands of soldiers fell in that war.

Past (I …): ಮಡಿದೆ, *maḍide*.

A respectful verb for dying, used of soldiers and heroes. A different ಮಡಿ means ritual cleanness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಡಿ** (*maḍi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4653 lists Kannada *maḍi*, "to die", and *maḍuhu*, "to kill".
