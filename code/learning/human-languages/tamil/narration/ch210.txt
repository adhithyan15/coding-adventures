மனம் — maṉam — "the mind, the heart".

The word, taken apart.
மனம் (maṉam) is a loan from Sanskrit manas, "mind" (CDIAL 9822).

Lesson 3 of 5.
பொறாமை (poṟāmai) — jealousy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for the mind, then the Tamil for character.

You'll want to know: பொறாமை.
பொறாமை — poṟāmai — "jealousy".

The word, taken apart.
பொறாமை (poṟāmai) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4565). The entry lists poṟāmai, "envy", the negative of poṟu, "to bear": jealousy is "not bearing" another's good.
