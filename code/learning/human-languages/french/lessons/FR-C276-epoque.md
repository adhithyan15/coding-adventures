## You'll want to know: l'époque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C276-EPOQUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'époque** — "the time, the era, the period".

> À l'époque, on n'avait pas de téléphone portable.
>
> *Back then, people did not have mobile phones.*

*À l'époque* — back then; *à cette époque-là* — at that time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*époque* is a learned borrowing from Greek *epokhḗ* "a halt, a fixed point in time", which reached French through Medieval Latin (EtymDB lists the French word without a parent).
