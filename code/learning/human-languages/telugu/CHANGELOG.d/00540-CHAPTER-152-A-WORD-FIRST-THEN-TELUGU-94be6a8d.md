## Chapter 152: a word first, then Telugu ఝ

The writing ramp now teaches **ఝరి** (*jhari*, a stream or mountain stream)
before taking the uncommon aspirated consonant ఝ from that known word. The
writing lesson adds a five-movement, five-run filmstrip backed by the packaged
tracing guide, then a short recall reunites the letter with the word and
meaning.
