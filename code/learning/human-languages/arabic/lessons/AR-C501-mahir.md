## You'll want to know: ماهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C501-HIKMA501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ماهر** — *māhir* — "skilful, skilled".

> صنع النجار الماهر طاولة جميلة.
>
> *ṣanaʿa an-najjār al-māhir ṭāwila jamīla.*
>
> The skilful carpenter made a beautiful table.

The noun مهارة, a skill, shares the root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ماهر** (*māhir*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-h-r*, "to be skilled" (Lane). Māhir is the active participle.
