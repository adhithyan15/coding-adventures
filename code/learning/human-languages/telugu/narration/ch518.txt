Telugu, chapter 518: Slips and Falls.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
గాయపడు (gāyapaḍu) — to get hurt; to be injured.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to throw up, then the Telugu for joint pains.

You'll want to know: గాయపడు.
గాయపడు — gāyapaḍu — "to get hurt; to be injured".
ఆ ప్రమాదంలో ఇద్దరు గాయపడ్డారు. ā pramādaṁlō iddaru gāyapaḍḍāru. Two people were injured in that accident.
Past (I …): గాయపడ్డాను, gāyapaḍḍānu.
గాయం, a wound, + పడు. Formal; the news says it.

The word, taken apart.
గాయపడు (gāyapaḍu) joins గాయం, "a wound", usually traced to Sanskrit ghāta, "killing; a blow, a bruise" (CDIAL 4460), to the native verb paḍu, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 2 of 5.
బొప్పి (boppi) — a bump; a lump on the head.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for joint pains, then the Telugu for to get hurt.

You'll want to know: బొప్పి.
బొప్పి — boppi — "a bump; a lump on the head".
తలుపుకు తగిలి తమ్ముడి నుదుటి మీద బొప్పి కట్టింది. talupuku tagili tammuḍi nuduṭi mīda boppi kaṭṭindi. My little brother hit the door and a bump came up on his forehead.
బొప్పి (boppi) కట్టు, a bump comes up. It belongs with పొంగు, to swell.

The word, taken apart.
బొప్పి (boppi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4469 lists Telugu boppi, "a swelling", in the article of poṅgu, "to bubble up, to swell".

Lesson 3 of 5.
తూలు (tūlu) — to stagger; to sway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to get hurt, then the Telugu for a bump.

You'll want to know: తూలు.
తూలు — tūlu — "to stagger; to sway".
నిద్రమత్తులో అతను తూలుతూ నడిచాడు. nidramattulō atanu tūlutū naḍicāḍu. Groggy with sleep, he staggered along.
Past (I …): తూలాను, tūlānu.
Unsteady on your feet, from sleep, illness or weakness.

The word, taken apart.
తూలు (tūlu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3376 lists Telugu tūlu, "to reel, to stagger, to grow feeble".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a bump, then the Telugu for to stagger.

You'll want to know: కుంటు.
కుంటు — kuṇṭu — "to limp".
కాలికి దెబ్బ తగిలి కుక్క కుంటుతోంది. kāliki debba tagili kukka kuṇṭutōndi. The dog hurt its leg and is limping.
Past (I …): కుంటాను, kuṇṭānu.
కుంటి, lame; కుంటుతూ నడుచు, to walk with a limp.

The word, taken apart.
కుంటు (kuṇṭu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1688 lists Telugu kuṇṭu, "to limp, to walk lamely", and kuṇṭi, "lame", with Kota kuṇṭ-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
రక్తస్రావం (raktasrāvaṁ) — bleeding; loss of blood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to stagger, then the Telugu for to limp.

You'll want to know: రక్తస్రావం.
రక్తస్రావం — raktasrāvaṁ — "bleeding; loss of blood".
గాయం నుంచి రక్తస్రావం ఆగలేదు. gāyaṁ nuñci raktasrāvaṁ āgalēdu. The bleeding from the wound did not stop.
రక్తం, blood, + స్రావం, flow.

The word, taken apart.
రక్తస్రావం (raktasrāvaṁ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit rakta, "red; blood" (CDIAL 10539), and srāva, "a flow" (Monier-Williams).
