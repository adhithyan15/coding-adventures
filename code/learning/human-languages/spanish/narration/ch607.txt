6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
el medio ambiente — the environment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the charity, then the Spanish for to take part.

You'll want to know: el medio ambiente.
el medio ambiente — "the environment".
Hay que cuidar el medio ambiente. We have to look after the environment.
Also written medioambiente; the adjective is medioambiental.

The word, taken apart.
el medio ambiente is built on medio, inherited from Latin medius 'middle', and ambiente, from Latin ambiens 'going round', from ambire (Lewis & Short).
