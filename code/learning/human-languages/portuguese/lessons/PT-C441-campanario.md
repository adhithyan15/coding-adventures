## You'll want to know: o campanário
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C441-PRACA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o campanário** — "the bell tower".

> Do campanário vê-se toda a cidade.
>
> *From the bell tower you can see all of the town.*

Built on Latin *campāna*, a bell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*campanário* is a learned formation on Late Latin *campāna* 'bell' (Lewis & Short).
