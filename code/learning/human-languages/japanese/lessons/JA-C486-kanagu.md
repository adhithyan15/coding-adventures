## You'll want to know: かなぐ

**かなぐ** — *kanagu* — "a metal fitting".

> **かばんの かなぐが こわれました。** — *kaban no kanagu ga kowaremashita* — "The metal fitting on my bag broke."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かなぐ** (*kanagu*) mixes the two layers, native and Sino-Japanese: native *kana*, "gold" and Sino-Japanese *gu*, "tool" (Middle Chinese *gjuH*), borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
