## You'll want to know: छितराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C385-BIKHAR385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छितराना** — *chitrānā* — "to scatter, to strew".

> अगर तुम आँगन में चावल छितराओगे तो चिड़ियाँ आएँगी।
>
> *agar tum āṁgan meṁ cāval chitrāoge to ciṛiyā̃ āeṅgī.*
>
> If you scatter rice in the courtyard, the birds will come.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बीज छितराए, *usne bīj chitrāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छितराना** (*chitrānā*) is inherited from *\*chitr*, "scatter, sprinkle", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5040).
