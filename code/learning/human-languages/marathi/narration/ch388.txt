Lesson 1 of 6.
प्राधान्य (prādhānya) — a priority.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to turn out, then the Marathi for to be convinced.

You'll want to know: प्राधान्य.
प्राधान्य — prādhānya — "a priority".
माझ्यासाठी आरोग्याला प्राधान्य आहे. mājhyāsāṭhī ārogyālā prādhānya āhe. For me, health comes first.
Neuter.
X ला प्राधान्य (prādhānya) देणे, to give priority to X.

The word, taken apart.
प्राधान्य (prādhānya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prādhānya, "pre-eminence, predominance" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
अमलात आणणे (amlāt āṇṇe) — to implement; to put into effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for progress, then the Marathi for development.

You'll want to know: अमलात आणणे.
अमलात आणणे — amlāt āṇṇe — "to implement; to put into effect".
समितीने ठरलेली योजना लगेच अमलात आणली. samitīne ṭharlelī yojnā lagec amlāt āṇlī. The committee put the agreed plan into effect at once.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अमलात आणलं, tyāne amlāt āṇlaṁ.
अमलात येणे, to come into effect.

The word, taken apart.
अमलात आणणे (amlāt āṇṇe) joins अमलात, "into effect", from अमल, a loan from Persian usually traced to Arabic ʿamal, "action", to आणणे, "to bring".
