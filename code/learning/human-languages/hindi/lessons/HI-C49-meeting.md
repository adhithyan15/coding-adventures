## You'll want to know: मुलाक़ात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C49-LEAVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मुलाक़ात** (*mulāqāt*) — "a meeting".

Arabic *mulāqāt*, on *laqiya*, 'to meet', arriving through Persian. It covers everything from bumping into somebody on a street to a scheduled interview in an office.

It sits inside the ordinary parting line *phir mulāqāt hogī*, 'there will be a meeting again' — which is what फिर मिलेंगे says using inherited material instead of borrowed. Same farewell, two vocabularies, and a speaker can pick either without changing the meaning by a hair.
