# إلى أن (ilā an) — until

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C327-LAYLA327-02, AR-LEX-C327-LAYLA327-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Arabic for at the same time, then the Arabic for as soon as.

## You'll want to know: إلى أن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C327-LAYLA327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**إلى أن** — *ilā an* — "until".

> انتظرنا إلى أن توقف المطر.
>
> *intaẓarnā ilā an tawaqqafa al-maṭar.*
>
> We waited until the rain stopped.

A verb follows: إلى أن وصل. Before a noun, say حتى.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إلى أن** (*ilā an*) joins the preposition *ilā*, "to, as far as", and *an*, "that": "up to the time that". Lane treats both as old particles; their origin beyond Arabic is not settled.
