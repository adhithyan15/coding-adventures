## You'll want to know: muerto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C555-VEJEZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**muerto** — "dead; the dead person".

> El accidente dejó dos muertos.
>
> *The accident left two people dead.*

Used with *estar*: *Está muerto.* *Muerto de hambre* — starving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*muerto* is inherited from Latin *mortuus* 'dead' (EtymDB), the participle of *morī* 'to die', through spoken Latin *\*mortus*.
