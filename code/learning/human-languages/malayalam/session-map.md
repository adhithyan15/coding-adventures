# Session Map — Malayalam Chapters 1–2

Same spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

Coverage note: this map currently stops at Chapter 5 while canonical lessons
continue through Chapter 31. `HL-M04` records the work to schedule every later
lesson, including the four new vocabulary → support sequences, without
pretending this partial map is already complete.

**Writing grows inside useful words, in tiny steps.** First hear and use a word.
Then meet no more than three shapes at once. A small, already meaningful unit
moves through tracing, guided copy, delayed copy, and dictation before the
whole word is ever produced. Chapter 1 begins with that complete gentle runway
for *namaskāram*; later words keep adding only the shapes they need.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | New letters / reading idea | Origin / family thread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | namaskaram | *(hear only)* | use *namaskāram* and remember “the making of a bow” | Sanskrit loan (vs. Tamil's native *vaṇakkam*) |
| 2 | na-ma-trace | ന · മ | observe and trace two inherent-*a* shapes | first two known sounds |
| 3 | na-ma-guided-copy | നമ | copy the same two-shape unit with the model visible | immediate retrieval |
| 4 | na-ma-delayed-copy | നമ | copy after briefly hiding the model | delayed retrieval |
| 5 | na-ma-dictation | നമ | write the familiar unit from sound | first independent writing |
| 6 | sa-chandrakkala-ka | സ · ് · ക | three shapes; remove *a* and assemble സ്ക | middle of the greeting |
| 7 | aa-ra-anusvaram | ാ · ര · ം | three shapes; assemble കാരം | end of the greeting |
| 8 | namaskaram-read | നമസ്കാരം | assemble and read; one model-visible whole-word copy | no new shape |
| 9 | namaskaram-dictation | നമസ്കാരം | write the whole known greeting from sound | independent payoff |
| 10 | nandi | നന്ദി | ന്ദ conjunct, i-sign | native (= Tamil *naṉṟi*), root *nal* |
| 11 | athe | അതെ | independent അ, e-sign (before consonant) | native; yes as "that [is so]" |
| 12 | illa | ഇല്ല | independent ഇ, ല്ല conjunct | native (root *il* = Tamil *illai*); negation by verb |
| 13 | sari | ശരി | ശ (śa), ri-sign | native; the family word *sari* (Sanskrit ശ) |
| 14 | practice | (recap) | read all five; write *namaskāram* from sound | first chapter checkpoint |

## Chapter 2 — Introducing Yourself

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | peru | പേര് | "name" ← Dravidian *\*pēr* (twin of Tamil *peyar*, not *name*) |
| 16 | enre | എന്റെ | "my" ← *ñāṉ* ("I") |
| 17 | aanu | ആണ് | **"is"** — Malayalam's copula (← *āka*); its sisters use **none** |
| 18 | enre-peru-aanu | എന്റെ പേര് … ആണ് | **"my name is…"**; verb last (unlike Tamil) |
| 19 | nii-ningal | നീ / നിങ്ങൾ | **"you"** familiar/respectful; respect by plural |
| 20 | entu | എന്ത് | "what" ← Dravidian question-stem *\*yā-/\*e-* |
| 21 | ninre-peru-entaanu | നിന്റെ പേര് എന്താണ്? | **"what's your name?"** (*entŭ* + *āṇŭ* fused) |
| 22 | santosham | സന്തോഷം | "pleased to meet you" — **Sanskrit** (vs. native *nandi* for thanks) |
| 23 | practice | (dialogue) | the whole exchange |

## Chapter 3 — How Are You

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | engane | എങ്ങനെ | "how" ← the native *e-* question family |
| 25 | sukhamaano | സുഖമാണോ? | **"how are you?"** = "are you well?"; the copula *āṇŭ* + the question *-ō* |

| 28 | saaramilla | സാരമില്ല | "no matter / you're welcome"; Sanskrit *sāraṁ* + native *illa* |
| 29 | practice | (dialogue) | the whole how-are-you exchange |

## Chapter 4 — Farewells

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | pokuka | പോകുക / വരിക | "to go" / "to come" — the *-uka* infinitive |
| 31 | poyi-varaam | പോയി വരാം | **"I'll go and come back"** — the Dravidian promise-of-return goodbye |
