## You'll want to know: しっぱいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C454-TASK454-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しっぱいする** — *shippai suru* — "to fail".

> **かれは しけんに しっぱいしました。** — *kare wa shiken ni shippai shimashita* — "He failed the exam."

Polite **しっぱいします**, *shippaishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが しっぱいしました。**, *senshu ga shippai shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しっぱいする** (*shippai suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *shippai* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "lose" (Middle Chinese *syit*) and *pai*, "failure" (*baejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
