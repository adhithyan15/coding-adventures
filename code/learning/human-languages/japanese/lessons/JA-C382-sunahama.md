## You'll want to know: すなはま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C382-KAIGAN382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**すなはま** — *sunahama* — "a sandy beach".

> **こどもが すなはまで あそんでいます。** — *kodomo ga sunahama de asonde imasu* — "The children are playing on the sandy beach."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すなはま** (*sunahama*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *suna*, "sand" and *hama*, "beach"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
