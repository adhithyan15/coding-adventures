## You'll want to know: la miseria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C633-RIQUEZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la miseria** — "the poverty, the misery; a pittance".

> Vivían en la miseria.
>
> *They lived in great poverty.*

*Ganar una miseria* — to earn a pittance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miseria* is a learned borrowing from Latin *miseria* 'wretchedness' (EtymDB), built on *miser* 'wretched'.
