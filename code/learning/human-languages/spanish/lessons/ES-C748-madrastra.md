## You'll want to know: la madrastra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C748-NIETOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la madrastra** — "the stepmother".

> Se lleva bien con su madrastra.
>
> *She gets on well with her stepmother.*

Fairy tales give it a bad name; today people often say *la mujer de mi padre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*madrastra* is inherited from spoken Latin *mātrastra* (EtymDB), built on *māter* "mother" with a belittling ending.
