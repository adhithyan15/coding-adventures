# कूकड़ो (kūkṛo) — a rooster

## You'll want to know: कूकड़ो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C236-BEAST236-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कूकड़ो** — *kūkṛo* — "a rooster".

> कूकड़ो तड़कै बोलै है।
>
> *kūkṛo taṛkai bolai hai.*
>
> The rooster crows at daybreak.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूकड़ो** (*kūkṛo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kukkuṭa*, "a cock", a word CDIAL takes to imitate its call; CDIAL lists Hindi *kukṛā*. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 3208).
