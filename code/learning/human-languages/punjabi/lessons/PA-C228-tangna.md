# ਟੰਗਣਾ (ṭaṅgṇā) — to hang up

## You'll want to know: ਟੰਗਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C228-DIN228-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੰਗਣਾ** — *ṭaṅgṇā* — "to hang up".

> ਮੈਂ ਕਮੀਜ਼ ਕਿੱਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਟੰਗਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ kamīz kill utte ṭaṅgdā hāṁ.*
>
> I hang my shirt on a nail.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਮੀਜ਼ ਟੰਗੀ, *maiṁ kamīz ṭaṅgī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੰਗਣਾ** (*ṭaṅgṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭaṅg*, "hang up"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭaṅgṇā*, "to hang up" (CDIAL 5436).
