Le bœuf is written with œ, the joined o and e.
[once you have stopped driving — write: bœuf once]

You'll want to know: le bœuf.
le bœuf — "beef; an ox".

The word, taken apart.
bœuf from Latin bos, bovis, "ox" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited: the stressed o became œu.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: les légumes.
les légumes — "vegetables".

The word, taken apart.
légumes is the plural of the word for "a vegetable", from Latin legumen, "a pulse, a bean" (Lewis & Short) — a learned borrowing.

Lesson 4 of 5.
les haricots — beans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for vegetables, then the French for a mushroom.

You'll want to know: les haricots.
les haricots — "beans".

The word, taken apart.
haricots is the plural of the word for "bean", whose origin is uncertain. It first named a meat stew, from an Old French verb meaning "to chop up"; the bean sense may owe something to Nahuatl ayacotli, "bean".
