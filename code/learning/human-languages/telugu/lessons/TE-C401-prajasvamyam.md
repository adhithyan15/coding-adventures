## You'll want to know: ప్రజాస్వామ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C401-PAURUDU401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రజాస్వామ్యం** — *prajāsvāmyaṁ* — "democracy".

> ప్రజాస్వామ్యంలో జనమే తమ నాయకులను ఎన్నుకుంటారు.
>
> *prajāsvāmyaṁlō janamē tama nāyakulanu ennukuṇṭāru.*
>
> In a democracy the people themselves choose their leaders.

ప్రజా, of the people, + స్వామ్యం, mastery: rule that belongs to the people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రజాస్వామ్యం** (*prajāsvāmyaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *prajā*, "offspring, people", and *svāmya*, "mastership, ownership, power" (Monier-Williams); the compound is a modern one.
