# اشتهى (ishtahā) — to crave

## You'll want to know: اشتهى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C517-ADAT517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتهى** — *ishtahā* — "to crave".

> اشتهت أختي الشوكولاتة في الليل.
>
> *ishtahat ukhtī ash-shūkūlāta fī al-layl.*
>
> My sister craved chocolate in the night.

Present: يشتهي, *yashtahī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتهى** (*ishtahā*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-h-w*, "to desire, to long for" (Lane). Form VIII ishtahā is to long for a thing, often food.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
