## You'll want to know: تعریف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C355-TARIF355-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعریف** — *tārīf* — "praise".

> استاد نے سارا کی تعریف کی۔
>
> *ustād ne sārā kī tārīf kī.*
>
> The teacher praised Sara.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعریف** (*tārīf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taʿrīf*, "making known, description"; Urdu uses it for describing someone well, "praise".
