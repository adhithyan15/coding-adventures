# ప్రాతిపదిక (prātipadika) — a footing; a basis

## You'll want to know: ప్రాతిపదిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C395-KANUKA395-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రాతిపదిక** — *prātipadika* — "a footing; a basis".

> మార్కుల ప్రాతిపదికన బహుమతులు ఇస్తారు.
>
> *mārkula prātipadikana bahumatulu istāru.*
>
> Prizes are given on the basis of marks.

ప్రాతిపదికన, on the basis of, follows a noun in the genitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రాతిపదిక** (*prātipadika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prātipadika*, a grammar word for "the crude form or base of a noun" (Monier-Williams); Telugu widened it to any basis or footing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
