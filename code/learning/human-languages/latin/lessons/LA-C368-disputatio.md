## You'll want to know: disputātiō, disputātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C368-ORATOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**disputātiō, disputātiōnis** — "a discussion, a debate".

> Disputātiō dē lēgibus longa fuit.
>
> *The debate about the laws was long.*

*disputātiō, disputātiōnis* f., the noun of *disputō*, to discuss. English *disputation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disputātiō* is the noun built on *disputō* "to discuss, to argue" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *disputation* comes from it.
