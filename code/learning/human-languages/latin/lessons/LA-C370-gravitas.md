## You'll want to know: gravitās, gravitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C370-AUCTORITAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**gravitās, gravitātis** — "weight; seriousness".

> Cum gravitāte et sine īrā locūtus est.
>
> *He spoke seriously and without anger.*

*gravitās, gravitātis* f., from *gravis*, heavy. A Roman virtue: the seriousness of a man who weighs his words. English *gravity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gravitās* is built on *gravis* "heavy" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), whose cousins include Sanskrit *guru* "heavy". English *gravity* comes from it.
