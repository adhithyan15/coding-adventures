## You'll want to know: つうほう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C270-HANNIN270-03]; assesses=[] -->

**つうほう** — *tsūhō* — "a report to the police; a tip-off".

> **ははが けいさつに つうほうしました。** — *haha ga keisatsu ni tsūhō shimashita* — "My mother called the police about it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つうほう** (*tsūhō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tsū*, "pass through" (Middle Chinese *thuwng*) and *hō*, "report" (*pawH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
