# धन्यवाद — now read what you already mean

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-DHANYAVAD-WRITE-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-DHANYAVAD-01, MR-SCRIPT-DA-01, MR-SCRIPT-DHA-01, MR-SCRIPT-YA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->

Nothing in this word is an unknown shape now. Read **ध · न् · य · वा · द**,
join the middle consonants, and say **धन्यवाद** — *dhanyavād*, "thank you."

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-DHANYAVAD-WRITE-01, MR-SCRIPT-YA-01, MR-SCRIPT-LLA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

- hear *dhanyavād* and point to **धन्यवाद**
- say the word from the English cue "thank you"
- read **धन्यवाद** without romanization
- cover every model and write **धन्यवाद** from the heard cue

Then retrieve the last three signs separately: *ya*, curled-back *ḷa*, *va*.
