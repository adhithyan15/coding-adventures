Russian, chapter 497: Bringing up Children.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
воспитание (vospitániye) — upbringing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to lull to sleep, then the Russian for to swaddle.

You'll want to know: воспитание.
воспитание (vospitániye) — "upbringing".
Хорошее воспитание видно сразу. Khorósheye vospitániye vídno srázu. A good upbringing shows at once.
Neuter. The verbs are воспитывать / воспитать — to bring up.

The word, taken apart.
воспитание (vospitániye) is built on воспитать 'to bring up', from вос- and питать 'to feed, to nourish'; EtymDB has no entry for the noun.

Lesson 2 of 5.
баловать (balovát) — to spoil (a child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to swaddle, then the Russian for upbringing.

You'll want to know: баловать.
баловать (balovát) — "to spoil (a child)".
Бабушка балует внуков сладостями. Bábushka balúyet vnúkov sládostyami. Grandma spoils her grandchildren with sweets.
Imperfective; present балую, балуешь (-ова- becomes -у-). Perfective избаловать.

The word, taken apart.
баловать (balovát) is inherited from Old East Slavic balovati 'to flatter, to heal', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic badlovati.

Lesson 3 of 5.
слушаться (slúshatsya) — to obey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for upbringing, then the Russian for to spoil.

You'll want to know: слушаться.
слушаться (slúshatsya) — "to obey".
Дети не всегда слушаются родителей. Déti ne vsegdá slúshayutsya rodíteley. Children don't always do what their parents say.
Imperfective; perfective послушаться. Takes the genitive: слушаться (slúshatsya) родителей.

The word, taken apart.
слушаться (slúshatsya) is the reflexive of слушать 'to listen': to obey is to listen to someone. EtymDB has no entry for the reflexive.

You'll want to know: шалить.
шалить (shalít) — "to be naughty".
На перемене мальчики шалят в коридоре. Na pereméne málchiki shalyát v koridóre. At break the boys get up to mischief in the corridor.
Imperfective; perfective пошалить. The noun is шалость — a prank.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists шалить (shalít) without a source, and the origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
непослушный (neposlúshnyy) — disobedient, naughty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to obey, then the Russian for to be naughty.

You'll want to know: непослушный.
непослушный (neposlúshnyy) — "disobedient, naughty".
Непослушный ребёнок убежал от мамы. Neposlúshnyy rebyónok ubezhál ot mámy. The naughty child ran away from his mum.
The opposite is послушный — obedient.

The word, taken apart.
непослушный (neposlúshnyy) is built on не- and послушный 'obedient', from слушать 'to listen'; EtymDB has no entry.
