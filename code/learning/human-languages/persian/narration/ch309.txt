Persian, chapter 309: Who said what.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سخنگو (sokhangu) — a spokesperson.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a headline, then the Persian for the news.

You'll want to know: سخنگو.
سخنگو (sokhangu) — "a spokesperson".
سخنگوی پلیس با خبرنگاران صحبت کرد. Sokhangu-ye polis bâ khabarnegârân sohbat kard. The police spokesperson spoke to the journalists.
sokhan "speech" + gu "saying", the present stem of goftan "to say".

The word, taken apart.
sokhangu joins sokhan "speech" and gu-, the present stem of goftan "to say": the speech-sayer. Both parts are native Persian; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بیانیه (bayâniye) — a statement, a communiqué.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the news, then the Persian for a spokesperson.

You'll want to know: بیانیه.
بیانیه (bayâniye) — "a statement, a communiqué".
شرکت دیروز یک بیانیه کوتاه داد. Sherkat diruz yek bayâniye-ye kutâh dâd. Yesterday the company issued a short statement.
Formal; bayâniye dâdan "to issue a statement". Built on bayân "expression".

The word, taken apart.
bayâniye is built on bayân "expression, statement", which is borrowed from Arabic bayān (EtymDB lists both words), with the noun ending -iye.

Lesson 3 of 5.
اطلاعیه (ettelâ'iye) — a notice, an announcement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a spokesperson, then the Persian for a statement.

You'll want to know: اطلاعیه.
اطلاعیه (ettelâ'iye) — "a notice, an announcement".
روی در مدرسه یک اطلاعیه بود. Ru-ye dar-e madrese yek ettelâ'iye bud. There was a notice on the school door.
Built on ettelâ' "information". Also a printed notice in an office or a mosque.

The word, taken apart.
ettelâ'iye is built on ettelâ' "information, knowledge", which is usually traced to Arabic iṭṭilā'; EtymDB lists the Persian word with no parent.

Lesson 4 of 5.
اعلام کردن (e'lâm kardan) — to announce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a statement, then the Persian for a notice.

You'll want to know: اعلام کردن.
اعلام کردن (e'lâm kardan) — "to announce".
مدرسه اعلام کرد که فردا تعطیل است. Madrese e'lâm kard ke fardâ ta'til ast. The school announced that it would be closed tomorrow.
Present stem kon-: e'lâm mikonad. The passive e'lâm shod is "it was announced".

The word, taken apart.
e'lâm is borrowed from Arabic i'lām, "notice, instruction" (EtymDB). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اظهار کردن (ezhâr kardan) — to state, to declare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a notice, then the Persian for to announce.

You'll want to know: اظهار کردن.
اظهار کردن (ezhâr kardan) — "to state, to declare".
مرد اظهار کرد که چیزی ندیده است. Mard ezhâr kard ke chizi nadide ast. The man stated that he had not seen anything.
Formal, mostly in reports. Present ezhâr mikonad. ezhâr-e nazar "a comment".

The word, taken apart.
ezhâr is borrowed from Arabic iẓhār (EtymDB), usually glossed "a showing, a making plain". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.
