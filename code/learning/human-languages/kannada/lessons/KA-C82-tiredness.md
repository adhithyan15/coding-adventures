# ಆಯಾಸ (āyāsa) — tiredness

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C63-HEALTH-03, KA-LEX-C63-HEALTH-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for fever, then the Kannada for pain.

## You'll want to know: ಆಯಾಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಯಾಸ** (*āyāsa*) — "tiredness", the heavy feeling at the end of a long day.

**ನನಗೆ ಆಯಾಸ ಆಗಿದೆ** (*nanage āyāsa āgide*) — "I am tired", literally "to me
tiredness has happened". The person sits in **ನನಗೆ**, "to me", the same way
**ನನಗೆ ಹಸಿವು** says "I am hungry".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A first word for how the body feels at the end of the day.
