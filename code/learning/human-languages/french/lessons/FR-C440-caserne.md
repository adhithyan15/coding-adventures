## You'll want to know: la caserne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C440-QUARTIERS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la caserne** — "the barracks; the fire station".

> La caserne des pompiers est à côté de l'école.
>
> *The fire station is next to the school.*

*La caserne des pompiers* — the fire station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caserne* was borrowed from Provençal *cazerna* 'small hut for four soldiers', which goes back to Latin *quaterna* 'group of four' (EtymDB).
