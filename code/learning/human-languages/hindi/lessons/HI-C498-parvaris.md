## You'll want to know: परवरिश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C498-PARVARISH498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परवरिश** — *parvariś* — "nurture; upbringing".

> दादी ने अरुण की परवरिश की।
>
> *dādī ne Aruṇ kī parvariś kī.*
>
> Grandmother brought Arun up.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परवरिश** (*parvariś*) is usually traced to Persian *parvarish*, "nurture, fostering".
