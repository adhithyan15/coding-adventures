## You'll want to know: aggravare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C355-RIMEDIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aggravare** — "to make worse, to aggravate".

> Se non ti riposi, aggravi la situazione.
>
> *If you do not rest, you will make the situation worse.*

*Aggravarsi* — to get worse: *la situazione si è aggravata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aggravare* is inherited from Latin *aggravāre* "to increase the weight of, to make worse" (EtymDB), built from *ad* and *gravis* "heavy".
