## You'll want to know: しぜん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C336-KANKYOU336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しぜん** — *shizen* — "nature".

> **いなかは しぜんが おおいから、すきです。** — *inaka wa shizen ga ōi kara, suki desu* — "I like the countryside because there is so much nature."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しぜん** (*shizen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "oneself" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*) and *zen*, "so" (*nyen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
