Marathi, chapter 281: Must, Must Not, and Because.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मला जायला पाहिजे (malā jāylā pāhije) — must, and must not.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "to go for a walk": phirāylā jāṇe. फिरायला is "for walking", and it is the form this lesson needs.

You'll want to know: -ायला पाहिजे, 'must, should'.
मला आता घरी जायला पाहिजे. malā ātā gharī jāylā pāhije. — "I must go home now."
Take -णे off and put -ायला on: जाणे becomes जायला (jāylā), करणे becomes करायला (karāylā), येणे becomes यायला (yāylā), वाचणे becomes वाचायला. Then पाहिजे (pāhije), which does not change. The one who must stands in the dative, as with आवडणे: मला, तुला (tulā, "to you, a friend"), त्याला (tyālā, "to him"), तिला (tilā, "to her").
हवं, the हवा you know, does the same work: मला जायला हवं (malā jāylā havaṁ).

You'll want to know: -ऊ नये, 'should not'.
तू रात्री जाऊ नयेस. tū rātrī jāū nayes. — "You shouldn't go at night."
The -ऊ of आम्ही जाऊ with नये, "should not", which takes -स after तू. Said to no one in particular: असं करू नये (asaṁ karū naye), "one shouldn't do that". A flat "don't" uses the नको you know: जाऊ नकोस (jāū nakos), "don't go".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I must go home now" — malā ātā gharī jāylā pāhije]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "He should read this book" — tyālā he pustak vācāylā pāhije]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "You shouldn't go at night" — tū rātrī jāū nayes]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you must go home now, then tell a friend they shouldn't go at night.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I must go"? (मला जायला पाहिजे (malā jāylā pāhije).) Does पाहिजे change for "she"? (No: तिला जायला पाहिजे.) "You shouldn't go"? (तू जाऊ नयेस.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
-मुळे (-muḷe) — because of, and the reason in full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "because of that". (tyāmuḷe.) Its second half is the ending this lesson takes apart.

You'll want to know: -मुळे, 'because of'.
पावसामुळे गाडी उशिरा आली. pāvsāmuḷe gāḍī uśirā ālī. — "Because of the rain, the train came late."
-मुळे (-muḷe) goes on a noun in its bent form. पाऊस bends to पावसा-, the same as in पावसाळा: पावसामुळे (pāvsāmuḷe). उशिरा (uśirā, "late") is उशीर made into "how". On a pronoun: तुमच्यामुळे (tumcyāmuḷe), "because of you"; and त्यामुळे, "because of that".

You'll want to know: the reason in full.
मी काल आलो नाही, कारण मी आजारी होतो. mī kāl ālo nāhī, kāraṇ mī ājārī hoto. — "I didn't come yesterday, because I was ill."
When someone asks तुम्ही का आला नाहीत? (tumhī kā ālā nāhīt?), "Why didn't you come?", answer with the event and then कारण and the reason, a whole sentence in the past. Or the reason first and then म्हणून: मी आजारी होतो, म्हणून मी आलो नाही (mī ājārī hoto, mhaṇūn mī ālo nāhī).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Because of the rain, the train came late" — pāvsāmuḷe gāḍī uśirā ālī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Why didn't you come?" — tumhī kā ālā nāhīt?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I didn't come, because I was ill" — mī ālo nāhī, kāraṇ mī ājārī hoto]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Say that the train came late because of the rain, then give a reason in full with kāraṇ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Because of the rain"? (पावसामुळे.) "Because of you"? (तुमच्यामुळे.) Which takes a whole sentence, -मुळे (-muḷe) or कारण? (कारण.)


Lesson 3 of 5.
ज्ञान (jñān) — knowledge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "a book" and "to grow". (pustak, vāḍhṇe.)

You'll want to know: ज्ञान.
ज्ञान — jñān — "knowledge".
पुस्तकांमुळे ज्ञान वाढतं. pustakāmmuḷe jñān vāḍhtaṁ. — "Knowledge grows because of books."
The first block, ज्ञ, is said dny in Marathi: ज्ञान is said dnyān. The romanization keeps the letters, jñ. ज्ञान महत्त्वाचं आहे (jñān mahattvācaṁ āhe): knowledge is important.

The word, taken apart.
ज्ञान (jñān) is a tatsama: Sanskrit jñāna, "knowledge", on the root jñā, "to know" (CDIAL 5281). Under the same entry CDIAL lists an inherited Marathi form, jāṇ, in the narrower sense "acknowledgement of favours"; ज्ञान is the learned word, taken whole from Sanskrit.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jñān, said dnyān]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Knowledge grows because of books" — pustakāmmuḷe jñān vāḍhtaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Knowledge is important" — jñān mahattvācaṁ āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ज्ञान (jñān) mean? ("Knowledge".) How is ज्ञ said? (dny.)


Lesson 4 of 5.
ञ (ña) — the letter hidden inside ज्ञ.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Script and Wrap-up Recall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ज्ञान (jñān), "knowledge". You have read its first block, ज्ञ, as one shape. Name the letter it starts with: ज. Then the mark that silences a letter's vowel: ्.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script.
ञ (ña).
ञ is ña, a nasal made where j is made. ज्ञ is ज, the ्, and ञ, joined into one shape; Marathi says the pair dny. On its own ञ is rare in Marathi words; you will meet it inside ज्ञ, in ज्ञान (jñān).

Writing: ञ — guided copy.
Keep ञ (ña) in front of you for this first pass.
1. start at the open upper-left tip and curve clockwise around the bowl to the open lower-left tip.
2. lift at the bowl's right junction, sweep right through the shoulder, then curve upward along the right side to the headline.
3. lift at the lower right-side junction and descend the short stem below the bowl.
4. lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right.
Pen lifts: 3. This is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-ञ (ña)-order.gif’, strokes 1–4, Wikimedia Commons, 10 May 2009.
[pause 1 second]
Copy ञ twice beside the model, saying ña as you finish each one. Then write ज्ञान (jñān) once and point at the ञ inside its first block.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Which letter hides inside ज्ञ? (ञ, ña.) Which word did it come from? (ज्ञान, jñān, said dnyān.)
[once you have stopped driving — question: With no model, write ña, then the word for knowledge.]


Lesson 5 of 5.
Review — advice and reasons.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I think it will rain tomorrow, so I will stay at home". (malā vāṭtaṁ kī udyā pāūs paḍel, mhaṇūn mī gharī rāhīn.) Then say ऋतू, and ज्ञान (jñān) with its dny.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I must go home now, because it is late.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: You shouldn't go at night.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Because of the rain, the train came late.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Why didn't you come? I didn't come, because I was ill.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: In my opinion knowledge grows because of books.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Must"? (-ायला पाहिजे.) "Should not"? (-ऊ नये.) "Because of"? (-मुळे (-muḷe).) Which letter hides in ज्ञ? (ञ (ña).) Which starts ऋतू? (ऋ.)
