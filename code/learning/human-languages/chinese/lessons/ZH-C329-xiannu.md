## You'll want to know: xiānnǚ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C329-TALE329-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xiānnǚ* — "fairy; heavenly maiden".

> *chuánshuō lǐ yǒu qī ge xiānnǚ* — In the legend there are seven fairies.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiānnǚ* is built from *xiān* 'an immortal' and 女 'a woman', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjen* and *nrjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says of the first that one who grows old and does not die is called so.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
