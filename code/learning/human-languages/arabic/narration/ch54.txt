Arabic, chapter 54: Hunger, Happy, Sad, Doctor, Grandfather.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جوع (jūʿ) — hunger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for pain, then the Arabic for medicine.

You'll want to know: جوع.
جوع — jūʿ — "hunger".

The word, taken apart.
جوع (jūʿ) is built on the native Arabic root j-w-ʿ, "to be hungry" (Lane). Jūʿ is the verbal noun, "hunger".
