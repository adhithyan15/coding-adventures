## You'll want to know: ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C353-PLAY353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ** — *mukāblā* — "a contest; a competition".

> ਅੱਜ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ajj sakūl vich ikk mukāblā hai.*
>
> There is a contest at school today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ** (*mukāblā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *muqābala*, "facing each other".
