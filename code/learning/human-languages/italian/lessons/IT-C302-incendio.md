## You'll want to know: l'incendio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C302-INCENDIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'incendio** — "the fire, the blaze".

> L'incendio è cominciato in cucina.
>
> *The fire started in the kitchen.*

A destructive fire; a fire in the fireplace is *il fuoco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incendio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *incendium* "a fire" (EtymDB), from *incendere* "to set alight".
