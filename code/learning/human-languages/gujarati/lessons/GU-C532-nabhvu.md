# નભવું (nabhvũ) — to get by

## You'll want to know: નભવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C532-POSAVU532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નભવું** — *nabhvũ* — "to get by".

> જો પગાર ઓછો હશે તો આપણે ઓછા પૈસામાં નભીશું.
>
> *jo pagār ocho haśe to āpaṇe ochā paisāmā̃ nabhīśũ.*
>
> If the pay is low, we will get by on less money.

Past (it …): તે કુટુંબ નભી ગયું, *te kuṭumb nabhī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નભવું** (*nabhvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nírvahati*, "carries out, carries through", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to endure, continue" (CDIAL 7397).

One more word for saying what happens if.
