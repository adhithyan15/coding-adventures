## You'll want to know: হঠাৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C267-HOTHAT-01]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-LEX-C48-FAMILY48-05, BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

**হঠাৎ** — *hôṭhāt* — "suddenly".

> **আমি হাঁটছিলাম, হঠাৎ ঝড় এল।**
>
> *āmi hā̃ṭchhilām, hôṭhāt jhôṛ elô.* — "I was walking, and suddenly a storm came."

A story goes **প্রথমে**, **তারপর**; **হঠাৎ** is where it turns. It comes before the event that breaks into the scene.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হঠাৎ** (*hôṭhāt*) is Sanskrit *haṭhāt*, "by force, forcibly", the ablative of *haṭha*, "force" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams also gives *haṭhāt* as "necessarily, inevitably". The Bengali sense, "suddenly", is not among the senses it lists, and the source does not say how the word came to it.
