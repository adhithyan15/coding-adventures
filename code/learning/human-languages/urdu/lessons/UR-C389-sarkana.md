## You'll want to know: سرکانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C389-UNGHNA389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سرکانا** — *sarkānā* — "to slide (something) along".

> میں نے اپنا تھیلا سیٹ کے نیچے سرکایا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne apnā thailā sīṭ ke nīche sarkāyā.*
>
> I slid my bag under the seat.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گٹھری آگے سرکائی, *us ne gaṭhrī āge sarkāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرکانا** (*sarkānā*) is built on *saraknā*, "to slide", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *sarati*, "runs, glides" (CDIAL 13250): this is the form for moving something else.
