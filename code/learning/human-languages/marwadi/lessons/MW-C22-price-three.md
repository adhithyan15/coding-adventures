# Payoff — one price question, three counters

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PERFORMANCE-SHOPPING-THREE-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-PERFORMANCE-TRANSPORT-THREE-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-PERFORMANCE-FOOD-FIVE-FOUR-SKILL-01] -->

[PAUSE 24s] Recall the three-word shopping payoff, the three-word transport
payoff, and the five-word food payoff.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-PERFORMANCE-PRICE-FOUR-SKILL-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-PRICE-QUESTION-01, MW-SCRIPT-PRICE-QUESTION-01, MW-LEX-TOTAL-QUESTION-01, MW-SCRIPT-TOTAL-QUESTION-01, MW-LEX-KITNO-01, MW-SCRIPT-KITNO-01, MW-LEX-VASTU-01, MW-SCRIPT-VASTU-01, MW-LEX-CHAAY-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01, MW-LEX-RIKSHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-RIKSHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-SHA-01] -->

Only the second word changes.

1. Identify which of the three counters you heard the question asked at.
2. Produce all three from meaning cues alone.
3. Read three printed lines and match each to its counter.
4. Write all three from dictation, then write the total question once.

Pass each skill separately. You can now ask to see a thing and ask what it
costs. Understanding the answer, saying it is too dear, and settling on a price
are still untaught.
