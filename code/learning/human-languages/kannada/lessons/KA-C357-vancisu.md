# ವಂಚಿಸು (vañcisu) — to swindle; to cheat

## You'll want to know: ವಂಚಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C357-VANCHANE357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಂಚಿಸು** — *vañcisu* — "to swindle; to cheat".

> ಆ ಮನುಷ್ಯ ಊರಿನ ಜನರನ್ನು ವಂಚಿಸಿ ಓಡಿಹೋದನು.
>
> *ā manuṣya ūrina janarannu vañcisi ōḍihōdanu.*
>
> That man cheated the people of the town and ran away.

Past (I …): ವಂಚಿಸಿದೆ, *vañciside*.

The person cheated takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಮೋಸ ಮಾಡು says the same in everyday talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಂಚಿಸು** (*vañcisu*) is built on Sanskrit *vañcana*, "cheating, deception, fraud" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
