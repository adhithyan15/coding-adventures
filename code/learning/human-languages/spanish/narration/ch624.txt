Spanish, chapter 624: Papeles oficiales.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
autorizar — to authorise, to allow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to penalise, then the Spanish for to fine.

You'll want to know: autorizar.
autorizar — "to authorise, to allow".
El director autorizó la salida al museo. The head teacher authorised the trip to the museum.
Spelling: autoricé.

The word, taken apart.
autorizar is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin auctorizare, built on auctor 'originator, guarantor', from augere 'to increase' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
el certificado — the certificate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the authorisation, then the Spanish for to process.

You'll want to know: el certificado.
el certificado — "the certificate".
Necesito un certificado médico. I need a medical certificate.
The verb is certificar — to certify.

The word, taken apart.
certificado is the participle of certificar, which EtymDB traces to Late Latin certificāre 'to make sure', built on certus 'sure'.
