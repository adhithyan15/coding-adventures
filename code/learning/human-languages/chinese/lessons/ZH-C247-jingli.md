## You'll want to know: jīnglì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C247-TRIP247-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jīnglì* — "an experience (something lived through)".

> *zhè cì lǚyóu shì yí cì hěn hǎo de jīnglì* — This trip was a great experience.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīnglì* is built from *jīng* 'to pass through' and *lì* 'to go through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keng* and *lek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to pass' and 'to go'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
