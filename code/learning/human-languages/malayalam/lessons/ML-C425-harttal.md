# ഹർത്താൽ (harttāl) — a general strike

## You'll want to know: ഹർത്താൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C425-SAMARAM425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹർത്താൽ** — *harttāl* — "a general strike".

> ഹർത്താൽ കാരണം കടകൾ എല്ലാം അടഞ്ഞുകിടന്നു.
>
> *harttāl kāraṇaṁ kaṭakaḷ ellāṁ aṭaññukiṭannu.*
>
> Because of the general strike, all the shops stayed shut.

On a ഹർത്താൽ day, shops close and buses stop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹർത്താൽ** (*harttāl*) was borrowed from Hindustani *haṛtāl*, "closing shops in mourning or protest", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *haṭṭa*, "a market" (CDIAL 13944).
