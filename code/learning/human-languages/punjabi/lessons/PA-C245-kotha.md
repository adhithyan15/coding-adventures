## You'll want to know: ਕੋਠਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C245-GHR245-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਠਾ** — *koṭhā* — "a flat-roofed house; a room".

> ਪਿੰਡ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਦਾ ਜੀ ਦਾ ਕੋਠਾ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *piṇḍ vich dādā jī dā koṭhā purāṇā hai.*
>
> Grandfather's house in the village is old.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਠਾ** (*koṭhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kōṣṭha*, "granary, storeroom, inner apartment"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *koṭhā*, "house with mud roof and walls" (CDIAL 3546).
