## You'll want to know: भजन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C331-VRAT331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भजन** — *bhajan* — "a hymn; a devotional song".

> आरती के बाद सबने भजन गाए।
>
> *ārtī ke bād sabne bhajan gāe.*
>
> After the arati everyone sang hymns.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भजन** (*bhajan*) is Sanskrit *bhajana*, "worship, adoration" (Monier-Williams), the same family as **भक्त**: a song of worship.
