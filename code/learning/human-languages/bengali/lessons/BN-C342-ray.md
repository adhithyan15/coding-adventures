# রায় (rāy) — a verdict; a judgment

## You'll want to know: রায়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C342-BICHAR342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**রায়** — *rāy* — "a verdict; a judgment".

> আদালত কাল রায় দেবে।
>
> *ādālôt kāl rāy debe.*
>
> The court will give its verdict tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রায়** (*rāy*) is Persian *rāy*, an opinion or judgment, usually traced to Arabic *raʾy*. A court gives it with দেওয়া.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
