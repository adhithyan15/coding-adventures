## You'll want to know: تختہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C497-TAKHTA497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تختہ** — *takhtā* — "a plank; a board".

> بڑھئی نے ایک لمبا تختہ خریدا۔
>
> *baṛhaī ne ek lambā takhtā kharīdā.*
>
> The carpenter bought a long plank.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تختہ** (*takhtā*) is usually traced to Persian *takhta*, "a board, a plank".
