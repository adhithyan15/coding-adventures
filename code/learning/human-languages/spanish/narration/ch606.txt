Spanish, chapter 606: Ayudar a los demás.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la solidaridad — the solidarity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the racism, then the Spanish for the corruption.

You'll want to know: la solidaridad.
la solidaridad — "the solidarity".
Necesitamos más solidaridad. We need more solidarity.
Solidario — caring, ready to help.

The word, taken apart.
solidaridad is built on solidario with -idad (EtymDB); solidario was borrowed from French solidaire, from Latin solidus 'whole, firm'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
