## You'll want to know: ہیضہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C294-QAHAT294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیضہ** — *haizā* — "cholera".

> ہیضہ ایک خطرناک بیماری ہے۔
>
> *haizā ek khatarnāk bīmārī hai.*
>
> Cholera is a dangerous illness.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیضہ** (*haizā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *hayḍa*, "cholera". It spread where water was dirty.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
