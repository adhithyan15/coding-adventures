## Fixed — the etymological dictionary keeps "to write" apart from "to rise", and the lessons now say so

TA-C33-ezhutu said the Dravidian etymological dictionary files **எழுது** (*eḻudu*, "to write") under **எழு** (*eḻu*, "to rise"). TA-C71-rise said the dictionaries disagree on whether one grew out of the other. Both were wrong about the source. Burrow and Emeneau, *A Dravidian Etymological Dictionary* (2nd ed., Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1984; DSAL edition, dsal.uchicago.edu/dictionaries/burrow/) gives them as two separate entries. Neither entry cross-references the other:

- **DEDR 851** (formerly DED(S, N) 723): Ta. *er̤u* (-v-, -nt-) "to rise (as from seat or bed), ascend (as heavenly body) … begin, commence". This entry also holds *er̤uppu*, *eṭu* and Ma. *er̤uka*. Its only cross-reference is "Cf. 796 Ko. et-".
- **DEDR 853** (formerly DED 725): Ta. *er̤utu* (*er̤uti-*) "to write, paint, draw; become indented by pressure"; *er̤uttu* "letter, writing, painting, drawing, membranous intervals on the skull, fancied as written lines of destiny". The entry also has Ma. *er̤utuka*, *er̤uttu*, Kota *eḷd-*, *eḷt* and Kodagu *ëḷïd-*, *ëḷïtï*. It has no Kannada or Telugu reflex and no cross-reference.

(DEDR writes ழ as *r̤*.) The entries were read from the DSAL text as preserved in the moli-mandala/data CLDF repository (`data/dedr/dedr_new_entry_oct2013_edited.sql`, `data/dedr/dedr_new.csv`), because dsal.uchicago.edu could not be reached from the authoring environment. The Madras *Tamil Lexicon* entries for எழுது and எழு could not be checked for the same reason. The Malayalam track's ML-C33-ezhutuka already said the same thing ("the dictionary keeps them apart").

- **TA-C33-ezhutu:** the etymology block ("The word, taken apart - two entries, not one") and the `etymology_hook` now say that எழுது looks like எழு but is a separate entry. `roots` changes from `dravidian-ezhu-rise` to `dravidian-ezhutu-write`, the slug ML-C33-ezhutuka already uses for the same entry. "Kannada and Telugu, having dropped the sound, dropped the verb" claimed a cause, so it now reads "Kannada and Telugu write with other verbs" (DEDR 853 has no reflex in either).
- **TA-C71-rise:** the "dictionaries do not agree" sentence now says DEDR does not connect the two verbs: it files them as separate entries. The lesson's point is unchanged: two different verbs, one letter apart.
- **TA-C37-avar:** the warm-up recall that practises TA-ETYMON-EZHUTU-03 asked which word *eḻudu* is filed under (answer: *eḻu*). It now asks whether *eḻudu* is filed under *eḻu*. The answer is no: two separate entries.
