## You'll want to know: जड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C514-SUNAR514-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जड़ना** — *jaṛnā* — "to set (a stone); to inlay".

> सुनार ने अंगूठी में एक लाल पत्थर जड़ा।
>
> *Sunār ne aṅgūṭhī meṁ ek lāl patthar jaṛā.*
>
> The goldsmith set a red stone in the ring.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कंगन में पत्थर जड़ा, *usne kaṅgan meṁ patthar jaṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जड़ना** (*jaṛnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*jaḍati*, "joins", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5091).
