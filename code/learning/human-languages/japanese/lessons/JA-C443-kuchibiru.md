## You'll want to know: くちびる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C443-WRIST443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**くちびる** — *kuchibiru* — "lips; a lip".

> **ふゆは くちびるが かわきます。** — *fuyu wa kuchibiru ga kawakimasu* — "My lips get dry in winter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くちびる** (*kuchibiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
