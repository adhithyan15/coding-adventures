# કાખઘોડી (kākhghoḍī) — a crutch

## You'll want to know: કાખઘોડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C482-THOKAR482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કાખઘોડી** — *kākhghoḍī* — "a crutch".

> પડ્યા પછી દાદા કાખઘોડીથી ચાલે છે.
>
> *paḍyā pachī dādā kākhghoḍīthī cāle che.*
>
> Since his fall my grandfather walks with a crutch.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાખઘોડી** (*kākhghoḍī*) is built from *kākh*, "armpit", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *kakṣa* (CDIAL 2588), and *ghoḍī*, "a little horse", from the family of **ઘોડો** (*ghoḍo*) (CDIAL 4516): a little horse under the arm.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
