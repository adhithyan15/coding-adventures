Kannada, chapter 440: Permits and Papers.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಪರವಾನಗಿ (paravānagi) — a permit; a licence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to exchange money, then the Kannada for a cash machine.

You'll want to know: ಪರವಾನಗಿ.
ಪರವಾನಗಿ — paravānagi — "a permit; a licence".
ಕಾರು ಓಡಿಸಲು ಪರವಾನಗಿ ಬೇಕು. kāru ōḍisalu paravānagi bēku. You need a licence to drive a car.
ಚಾಲನಾ ಪರವಾನಗಿ (paravānagi) is a driving licence; in speech many say ಲೈಸೆನ್ಸ್.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರವಾನಗಿ (paravānagi) is usually traced to Persian parvānagī, "permission", built on parvāna, "a written order, a pass", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cash machine, then the Kannada for a permit.

You'll want to know: ನವೀಕರಿಸು.
ನವೀಕರಿಸು — navīkarisu — "to renew".
ನಾನು ನನ್ನ ಪಾಸ್ಪೋರ್ಟನ್ನು ಕಳೆದ ತಿಂಗಳು ನವೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ. nānu nanna pāspōrṭannu kaḷeda tiṅgaḷu navīkariside. I renewed my passport last month.
Past (I …): ನವೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ, navīkariside.
What is renewed takes -ಅನ್ನು: ಪರವಾನಗಿಯನ್ನು ನವೀಕರಿಸು (navīkarisu).

The word, taken apart.
ನವೀಕರಿಸು (navīkarisu) is built from Sanskrit navīkaraṇa, "making new, renewing" (Monier-Williams), on nava, "new" (CDIAL 6983), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ದೃಢಪಡಿಸು (dṛḍhapaḍisu) — to confirm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a permit, then the Kannada for to renew.

You'll want to know: ದೃಢಪಡಿಸು.
ದೃಢಪಡಿಸು — dṛḍhapaḍisu — "to confirm".
ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಇಮೇಲ್ ಮೂಲಕ ನಮ್ಮ ಕೋಣೆಯನ್ನು ದೃಢಪಡಿಸಿತು. hōṭel imēl mūlaka namma kōṇeyannu dṛḍhapaḍisitu. The hotel confirmed our room by email.
Past (I …): ದೃಢಪಡಿಸಿದೆ, dṛḍhapaḍiside.
ದೃಢ means firm or sure; -ಪಡಿಸು makes a verb of it: to make sure.

The word, taken apart.
ದೃಢಪಡಿಸು (dṛḍhapaḍisu) is built from Sanskrit dṛḍha, "fixed, firm, solid" (Monier-Williams), and the Kannada verb-maker -paḍisu: to make a thing firm.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿ (rāyabhāra kacēri) — an embassy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to renew, then the Kannada for to confirm.

You'll want to know: ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿ.
ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿ — rāyabhāra kacēri — "an embassy".
ನಾನು ವೀಸಾಗಾಗಿ ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿಗೆ ಹೋದೆ. nānu vīsāgāgi rāyabhāra kacērige hōde. I went to the embassy for a visa.
ರಾಯಭಾರಿ is an ambassador; ಕಚೇರಿ is an office.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಯಭಾರ ಕಚೇರಿ (rāyabhāra kacēri) joins ರಾಯಭಾರ, "an envoy's charge", and ಕಚೇರಿ, "an office". ರಾಯ comes from Sanskrit rājan, "a king", through Prakrit rāya (CDIAL 10679); how bhāra, "a burden", came to mean an envoy's office is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಅಂತರರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ (antararāṣṭrīya) — international.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to confirm, then the Kannada for an embassy.

You'll want to know: ಅಂತರರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ.
ಅಂತರರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ — antararāṣṭrīya — "international".
ಈ ನಗರದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಅಂತರರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ ವಿಮಾನ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಇದೆ. ī nagaradalli ondu antararāṣṭrīya vimāna nildāṇa ide. This city has an international airport.
ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರ is a nation and ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ national; ಅಂತರ- adds between.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂತರರಾಷ್ಟ್ರೀಯ (antararāṣṭrīya) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: antara, "inside, between" (CDIAL 357), and rāṣṭra, "a kingdom, a country" (CDIAL 10721), with the adjective ending -īya.
