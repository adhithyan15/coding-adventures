## You'll want to know: كآبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C494-KAABA494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**كآبة** — *kaʾāba* — "gloom, low spirits".

> يسبب الشتاء الطويل الكآبة لبعض الناس.
>
> *yusabbibu ash-shitāʾ aṭ-ṭawīl al-kaʾāba li-baʿḍ an-nās.*
>
> The long winter causes gloom for some people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كآبة** (*kaʾāba*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-ʾ-b*, "to be sad, broken in spirit" (Lane). Kaʾāba is that low, heavy state.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
