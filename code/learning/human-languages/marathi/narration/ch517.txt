Lesson 1 of 5.
संकेतशब्द (saṅketśabda) — a password.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a video clip, then the Marathi for to use.

You'll want to know: संकेतशब्द.
संकेतशब्द — saṅketśabda — "a password".
मी माझा संकेतशब्द कोणालाही सांगत नाही. mī mājhā saṅketśabda koṇālāhī sāṅgat nāhī. I do not tell anyone my password.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संकेतशब्द (saṅketśabda) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit saṁketa, "an agreed sign", and śabda, "word" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
आंतरजाल (āntarjāl) — the internet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to use, then the Marathi for a password.

You'll want to know: आंतरजाल.
आंतरजाल — āntarjāl — "the internet".
गावात आता आंतरजाल चांगलं चालतं. gāvāt ātā āntarjāl cāṅglaṁ cāltaṁ. The internet works well in the village now.
Neuter.
In speech, इंटरनेट; आंतरजाल (āntarjāl) is the Marathi word, a net between.

The word, taken apart.
आंतरजाल (āntarjāl) is a modern tatsama coinage from Sanskrit antara, "between", and jāla, "net" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
साठवणे (sāṭhavṇe) — to store; to save up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a message, then the Marathi for a link.

You'll want to know: साठवणे.
साठवणे — sāṭhavṇe — "to store; to save up".
मी सगळी छायाचित्रं संगणकात साठवली. mī sagḷī chāyācitraṁ saṅgaṇakāt sāṭhavlī. I stored all the photos on the computer.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाणी साठवलं, tyāne pāṇī sāṭhavlaṁ.
Where things go takes -त: संगणकात साठवणे (sāṭhavṇe).

The word, taken apart.
साठवणे (sāṭhavṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi sā̃ṭhaviṇẽ, "to heap up", under Sanskrit saṁsthāpayati, "sets up, establishes" (CDIAL 13051).
