Bengali, chapter 144: Shy, Sociable, Quiet, Smooth, Rough.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
লাজুক (lājuk) — shy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for small, then the Bengali for cheerful.

You'll want to know: লাজুক.
লাজুক — lājuk — "shy".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: lājuk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lājuk, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say lājuk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for small, then the Bengali for cheerful, then say lājuk again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does লাজুক (lājuk) mean? ("Shy".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for smooth and rough.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: busy, clever, foolish, kind, brave]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: timid, calm, urgent, straight, crooked]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sharp, blunt, fresh, stale, raw]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Busy? (ব্যস্ত, byāstô.) Clever? (চালাক, chālāk.) Foolish? (বোকা, bokā.) Kind? (দয়ালু, dôyālu.) Brave? (সাহসী, sāhôsi.) Timid? (ভীতু, bhītu.) Calm? (শান্ত, shāntô.) Urgent? (জরুরি, jôruri.) Straight? (সোজা, sojā.) Crooked? (বাঁকা, bā̃kā.) Sharp? (ধারালো, dhārālo.) Blunt? (ভোঁতা, bhõtā.) Fresh? (টাটকা, ṭāṭkā.) Stale? (বাসি, bāsi.) Raw? (কাঁচা, kā̃chā.)

(পরিচ্ছন্ন, pôrichchhônnô.) Sturdy? (মজবুত, môjbut.) Weak? (দুর্বল, durbôl.) Powerful? (শক্তিশালী, shôktishāli.) Tasty? (সুস্বাদু, susbādu.) Fairly big? (বড়সড়, bôṛôsôṛô.) Small? (ছোটখাটো, chhoṭôkhāṭo.) Cheerful? (হাসিখুশি, hāsikhushi.) Shy? (লাজুক, lājuk.) Sociable? (মিশুক, mishuk.) Quiet? (চুপচাপ, chupchāp.) Smooth? (মোলায়েম, molāyem.) Rough? (খসখসে, khôskhôse.)
