# Punjabi A1 named-reader message runway

This is the implementation contract for #13533, under the controlled-writing
parent #13462. The six-field form is already rehearsed separately; this runway
teaches the other untimed A1 writing task. It does not award timed-paper or
whole-exam readiness.

## End state

The learner independently writes a 30–40-word Gurmukhi message to a named
reader for a stated purpose. The message uses only sentence chunks, words,
grammar, tone, register, and script forms introduced and practised earlier.
The final prompt offers a reader and purpose, but no romanization, copyable
answer, visible sentence bank, dictionary, translator, or spell-checker.

## One small demand at a time

1. Choose a taught greeting for the named reader; separately choose a taught
   closing. Compare respectful and familiar choices before composing either.
2. Retrieve each chosen phrase in Gurmukhi, first with a visible bank and then
   after a delay without one. Check the exact spelling and final punctuation.
3. Order two already-taught sentence chunks for one purpose. Practise the
   sentence boundary and spacing without adding a new grammar demand.
4. Join and contrast taught chunks only after each chunk is independently
   retrievable. Check agreement separately from spelling and punctuation.
5. Assemble short, separately mastered message blocks for the same reader and
   purpose. Grow the total to 30–40 words across lessons, never by making one
   lesson carry a sudden 30-word vocabulary load.
6. Repair one dimension per pass: reader/register, order and meaning,
   agreement, word boundaries, Gurmukhi spelling, then punctuation. A delayed
   rehearsal precedes the no-model checkpoint.

Each authored lesson is at most 300 seconds by the repository's computed
duration gate. A final checkpoint is *untimed* in the assessment sense: it has
no exam clock or timed-production credit, although its lesson remains a
five-minute micro-session. The no-model evidence counts as controlled
composition only when the learner independently chooses and orders known
language; copying or selecting a displayed model does not count.

## Verification

Pin the chapter's lesson order, duration, prerequisite closure, scaffold
removal, and distinct repair passes in a Punjabi corpus test. Check genuine
later reinforcement for any newly introduced writing atoms, the Punjabi
curriculum digest and A1 level gate, and the generated book, narration,
modality, and shard owners. Do not lower an existing gate or claim A1 readiness
before both full mocks and human validation land.
