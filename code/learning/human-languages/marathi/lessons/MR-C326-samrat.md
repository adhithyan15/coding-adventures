## You'll want to know: सम्राट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C326-SAMRAT326-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्राट** — *samrāṭ* — "an emperor".

> तो सम्राट फार शूर आणि दयाळू होता.
>
> *to samrāṭ phār śūr āṇi dayāḷū hotā.*
>
> That emperor was very brave and kind.

Masculine.

साम्राज्य, an empire, is built on the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्राट** (*samrāṭ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *samrāj*, 'a supreme ruler', in its nominative form *samrāṭ* (Monier-Williams).
