## You'll want to know: ちぎる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C474-PREP474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちぎる** — *chigiru* — "to tear into pieces".

> **やさいを てで ちぎりました。** — *yasai o te de chigirimashita* — "I tore the vegetables by hand."

Polite **ちぎります**, *chigirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが のりを ちぎりました。**, *kodomo ga nori o chigirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちぎる** (*chigiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana; its kanji spelling writes *chi* with the kanji for "thousand", whose native reading is *chi*, and the rest with the kanji of *kiru*, "to cut" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
