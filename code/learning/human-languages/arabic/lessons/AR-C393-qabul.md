## You'll want to know: قبول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C393-TAQYIM393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قبول** — *qabūl* — "admission".

> حصل أخي على قبول من الجامعة.
>
> *ḥaṣala akhī ʿalā qabūl min al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> My brother got admission from the university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبول** (*qabūl*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-l*, "to be in front; to receive" (Lane). *Qabūl* is a verbal noun of *qabila*, to accept: acceptance, admission.
