## You'll want to know: मुहूर्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C320-UTSAV320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मुहूर्त** — *muhūrta* — "an auspicious time".

> नव्या घरात जाण्यासाठी त्यांनी चांगला मुहूर्त पाहिला.
>
> *navyā gharāt jāṇyāsāṭhī tyānnī cāṅglā muhūrta pāhilā.*
>
> They picked an auspicious time to move into the new house.

Masculine.

मुहूर्त पाहणे, to fix an auspicious time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुहूर्त** (*muhūrta*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *muhūrta*, 'a moment; a fixed span of time' (Monier-Williams).
