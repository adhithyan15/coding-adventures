## You'll want to know: व्यवस्थापयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C510-AVAKARA510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवस्थापयति** — *vyavasthāpayati* — "puts in order".

> सः पुस्तकानि व्यवस्थापयति।
>
> *saḥ pustakāni vyavasthāpayati.*
>
> He puts the books in order.

Past (he …): सः पुस्तकानि व्यवस्थापितवान्, *saḥ pustakāni vyavasthāpitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यवस्थापयति** (*vyavasthāpayati*) is built on *vi*, "apart", *ava*, "down", and the causative of the root √sthā, "to stand": to set each thing down in its place; MW gives the causative *vyavasthāpayati* as to put down, to place, to fix (MW).
