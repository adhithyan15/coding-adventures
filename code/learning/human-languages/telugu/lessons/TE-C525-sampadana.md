## You'll want to know: సంపాదన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C525-SAMPADANA525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపాదన** — *sampādana* — "earnings".

> అతని సంపాదనతో కుటుంబం గడుస్తుంది.
>
> *atani sampādanatō kuṭumbaṁ gaḍustundi.*
>
> The family lives on his earnings.

The money a person earns; its verb is సంపాదించు, to earn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంపాదన** (*sampādana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sampādana*, "procuring; accomplishing" (Monier-Williams).
