## You'll want to know: استقالة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C522-AMAL522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**استقالة** — *istiqāla* — "a resignation".

> كتب الموظف استقالته وأعطاها للمدير.
>
> *kataba al-muwaẓẓaf istiqālatahu wa-aʿṭāhā li-l-mudīr.*
>
> The employee wrote his resignation and gave it to the manager.

The verb استقال, to resign, goes with it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استقالة** (*istiqāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-y-l*, "to release from a bargain" (Lane). Istiqāla is asking to be released.
