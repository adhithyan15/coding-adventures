## You'll want to know: ਥੰਮ੍ਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C245-GHR245-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥੰਮ੍ਹ** — *thammh* — "a pillar".

> ਵਿਹੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੋ ਥੰਮ੍ਹ ਹਨ।
>
> *vihṛe vich do thammh han.*
>
> There are two pillars in the courtyard.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥੰਮ੍ਹ** (*thammh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *stambha*, "pillar, post"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *thammh*, "pillar, post" (CDIAL 13682).
