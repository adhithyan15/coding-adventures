## You'll want to know: 安心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C379-SAFE379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**安心** — *ānxīn* — "with a mind at rest; settled".

> **这个社区很好我很安心** *zhège shèqū hěn hǎo wǒ hěn ānxīn* — This is a good neighbourhood, and my mind is at rest.

The new character, in pieces: 安 — six strokes: the roof of 家 on top, three strokes, and 女 under it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**安心** (*ānxīn*) is built from 安 'calm' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'an* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'peaceful' and 'to come to rest'.
