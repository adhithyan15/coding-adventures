## You'll want to know: errer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C438-FLANER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**errer** — "to wander, to roam".

> Le chien errait seul dans la rue.
>
> *The dog was wandering alone in the street.*

Stronger than *flâner*: no aim at all, sometimes lost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*errer* is inherited through Old French *errer* from Latin *errāre* 'to wander, to go astray' (EtymDB).
