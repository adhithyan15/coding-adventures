## You'll want to know: उड़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C440-GHIRNA440-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उड़ाना** — *uṛānā* — "to blow away; to fly (a kite)".

> हवा ने मेरी टोपी उड़ा दी।
>
> *havā ne merī ṭopī uṛā dī.*
>
> The wind blew my cap away.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पतंग उड़ाई, *usne patang uṛāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उड़ाना** (*uṛānā*) is the causative partner of उड़ना, "to fly", from Sanskrit *uḍḍīyate*, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697), which lists Hindi *uṛānā*, "to cause to fly".
