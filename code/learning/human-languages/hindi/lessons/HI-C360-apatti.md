## You'll want to know: आपत्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C360-APATTI360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्ति** — *āpatti* — "an objection".

> मुझे तुम्हारे विचार पर कोई आपत्ति नहीं है।
>
> *mujhe tumhāre vicār par koī āpatti nahīṃ hai.*
>
> I have no objection to your idea.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्ति** (*āpatti*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āpatti*, "an occurrence; a mishap" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams does not give the sense of an objection, which is a later Hindi one.
