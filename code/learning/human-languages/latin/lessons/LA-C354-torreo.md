## You'll want to know: torreō, torrēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C354-ASSUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**torreō, torrēre** — "to roast, to toast, to parch".

> Ad focum pānem torrēbat.
>
> *He was toasting bread by the hearth.*

*torreō, torrēre, torruī, tostum*. English *toast* comes from *tostum*, and *torrid* from the verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**torreō** is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*torzeō*, "to scorch" (EtymDB), a cousin of English *thirst*. English *toast* and *torrid* come from it.
