## You'll want to know: il carnevale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C319-CARNEVALE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il carnevale** — "the carnival".

> Il carnevale finisce il martedì grasso.
>
> *Carnival ends on Shrove Tuesday.*

*A carnevale ogni scherzo vale* — at carnival any prank goes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carnevale* is inherited from Medieval Latin *carnelevāle* (EtymDB), from *carō* "meat" and *levāre* "to take away": the farewell to meat before Lent.
