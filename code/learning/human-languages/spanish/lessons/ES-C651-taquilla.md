## You'll want to know: la taquilla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C651-TREN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la taquilla** — "the ticket office, the box office; the locker".

> Compramos los billetes en la taquilla.
>
> *We bought the tickets at the ticket office.*

In Latin America also *la boletería*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taquilla* is a little *taca* "a small cupboard". Where *taca* comes from is not settled.
