Gujarati, chapter 278: Should, must, and so.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
તમારે જવું જોઈએ (tamāre javũ joīe) — should, and have to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "because of the rain". (varsādne līdhe.) Then "Arun drank tea". (Aruṇe cā pīdhī.) Keep that -એ.

Grammar Lens: the one who should takes -એ.
તમારે દાક્તર પાસે જવું જોઈએ. tamāre dāktar pāse javũ joīe. — "You should go to the doctor."
The verb's name in -વું, then જોઈએ (joīe), which stays as it is. The one who should takes an -એ form:
[a table of 6 rows, read aloud]
who: I. should go: મારે જવું જોઈએ.
who: you, to a friend. should go: તારે જવું જોઈએ.
who: he, she. should go: તેણે જવું જોઈએ.
who: we. should go: અમારે જવું જોઈએ.
who: you, with respect. should go: તમારે જવું જોઈએ (tamāre javũ joīe).
who: Arun, Mira. should go: અરુણે, મીરાએ.
With an object, the -વું follows the thing, as a past does: તમારે દવા લેવી જોઈએ (tamāre davā levī joīe), feminine for દવા; મારે પત્ર લખવો જોઈએ (māre patra lakhvo joīe), masculine for પત્ર.

You'll want to know: have to.
મારે કાલે વહેલા જવું પડશે. māre kāle vahelā javũ paḍśe. — "I'll have to go early tomorrow."
Put પડવું in place of જોઈએ and "should" becomes "have to": પડે છે, as a rule; પડશે, later; પડ્યું, it was so. મારે રોજ બસમાં જવું પડે છે (māre roj basmā̃ javũ paḍe che); ગઈકાલે મારે ચાલીને જવું પડ્યું (gaīkāle māre cālīne javũ paḍyũ), "yesterday I had to walk".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "He should rest" — teṇe ārām karvo joīe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Yesterday I had to walk" — gaīkāle māre cālīne javũ paḍyũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Tell someone, with respect, that they should take the medicine; then say that you will have to go tomorrow.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"You should go"? (તમારે જવું જોઈએ (tamāre javũ joīe).) "I have to go every day"? (મારે રોજ જવું પડે છે.) Why લેવી in તમારે દવા લેવી જોઈએ? (It follows દવા.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
એટલે (eṭle) — so; that is why.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "it was raining". (varsād paḍto hato.) Then "that much". (એટલું, eṭlũ.)

You'll want to know: એટલે.
એટલે — eṭle — "so; that is why". The reason comes first, then એટલે, then what followed from it.
વરસાદ પડતો હતો, એટલે અમે ઘરે રહ્યા. varsād paḍto hato, eṭle ame ghare rahyā. — "It was raining, so we stayed at home."
હું થાકી ગઈ હતી, એટલે હું ઘરે રહી. hũ thākī gaī hatī, eṭle hũ ghare rahī. — "I was tired, so I stayed at home."

The word, taken apart.
એટલે (eṭle) is probably built from એટલું, "that much", with the -ે of સવારે: "to that point", and so "for that reason". CDIAL lists Gujarati eṭlũ under Sanskrit iyattaka, "so little" (CDIAL 1589); it has no row for એટલે itself.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: eṭle]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It was raining, so we stayed at home" — varsād paḍto hato, eṭle ame ghare rahyā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "The train was late, so I came late" — ṭren moḍī hatī, eṭle hũ moḍo āvyo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does એટલે (eṭle) mean? ("So; that is why".) Which comes first, the reason or the result? (The reason.)


Lesson 3 of 4.
તેથી, એટલે (tethī, eṭle) — so, therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "so" in the everyday way. (એટલે (eṭle).) Then "therefore", from A2. (તેથી.)

Grammar Lens: reason, then result.
તમને તાવ છે, એટલે તમારે આરામ કરવો જોઈએ. tamne tāv che, eṭle tamāre ārām karvo joīe. — "You have a fever, so you should rest."
એટલે (eṭle) is the everyday "so". તેથી (tethī), "therefore", says the same and suits a letter or a notice: ટ્રેન મોડી હતી, તેથી અમે મોડા પહોંચ્યા (ṭren moḍī hatī, tethī ame moḍā pahõcyā).

You'll want to know: turning a reason round.
કારણ કે puts the reason after; એટલે (eṭle) puts it before. The same two facts, either way:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
result first: હું મોડો આવ્યો, કારણ કે ટ્રેન મોડી હતી. reason first: ટ્રેન મોડી હતી, એટલે (eṭle) હું મોડો આવ્યો.
result first: અમે ઘરે રહ્યા, કારણ કે વરસાદ પડતો હતો. reason first: વરસાદ પડતો હતો, એટલે (eṭle) અમે ઘરે રહ્યા.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "You have a fever, so you should rest" — tamne tāv che, eṭle tamāre ārām karvo joīe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "The bus was late, so I had to walk" — bas moḍī hatī, eṭle māre cālīne javũ paḍyũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Turn this round with eṭle: 'I came late, because the train was late.'

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"So", in everyday speech? (એટલે (eṭle).) In a letter? (તેથી.) Turn અમે ઘરે રહ્યા, કારણ કે વરસાદ પડતો હતો round. (વરસાદ પડતો હતો, એટલે (eṭle) અમે ઘરે રહ્યા.)


Lesson 4 of 4.
Review — should, must, and so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I think it will rain". (mane lāge che ke varsād paḍśe.) Then "because of the rain". (varsādne līdhe.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: You should go to the doctor. You should take the medicine.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: He should rest. I should write a letter.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I have to go by bus every day. Tomorrow I'll have to go early.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: What do you think? I think you have a fever, so you should rest.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: The train was late, so I came late. Now turn it round, with kāraṇ ke.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I should go"? (મારે જવું જોઈએ.) "I had to go"? (મારે જવું પડ્યું.) Turn હું મોડો આવ્યો, કારણ કે ટ્રેન મોડી હતી round with એટલે (eṭle). (ટ્રેન મોડી હતી, એટલે (eṭle) હું મોડો આવ્યો.)
