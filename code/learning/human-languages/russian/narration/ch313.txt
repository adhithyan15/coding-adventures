Russian, chapter 313: The New Year tree.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ёлка (yólka) — a New Year tree; a fir tree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to wish, then the Russian for a toast.

You'll want to know: ёлка.
ёлка (yólka) — "a New Year tree; a fir tree".
Перед Новым годом мы купили ёлку. Péred Nóvym gódom my kupíli yólku. Before New Year we bought a tree.
The Russian holiday tree, put up for New Year. Ель is the tree in the forest.

The word, taken apart.
ёлка (yólka) (EtymDB jolka) is built on ель "spruce, fir", inherited from Proto-Slavic jedlĭ, with the ending -ка.

You'll want to know: гирлянда.
гирлянда (girlyánda) — "fairy lights; a garland".
На окне горела гирлянда. Na okné goréla girlyánda. Fairy lights were glowing in the window.
Feminine. A string of lights or of paper flowers.

The word, taken apart.
гирлянда (girlyánda) is usually traced through German or French to Italian ghirlanda "a garland"; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
украшать (ukrashát) — to decorate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a New Year tree, then the Russian for fairy lights.

You'll want to know: украшать.
украшать (ukrashát) — "to decorate".
Дети украшали ёлку весь вечер. Déti ukrasháli yólku ves vécher. The children decorated the tree all evening.
Imperfective; perfective украсить (ukrásit), украшу.

The word, taken apart.
украшать (ukrashát) is built on у- and краса "beauty", an inherited Slavic word, in the shape -краш-: to make beautiful. EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to decorate, then the Russian for a decoration.

You'll want to know: наряжать.
наряжать (naryazhát) — "to dress up; to trim (a tree)".
В субботу мы нарядили ёлку. V subbótu my naryadíli yólku. On Saturday we trimmed the tree.
Imperfective; perfective нарядить (naryadít), наряжу. Наряжаться — to dress up.

The word, taken apart.
наряжать (naryazhát) is usually traced to на- and ряд "a row, order": to put in order, to array. EtymDB lists no parent.
