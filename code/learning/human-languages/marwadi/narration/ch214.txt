Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 214: Taking It Back.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फेरणो (pherṇo) — to give back; to return (a thing).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for free of charge, then the Marwadi for to be sold.

You'll want to know: फेरणो.
फेरणो — pherṇo — "to give back; to return (a thing)".
हूं काल ये कुरतो फेरसूं। hū̃ kāl ye kurto phersū̃. I'll take this kurta back tomorrow.
Past (I …): म्हैं कुरतो फेर्यो, mhaĩ kurto pheryo.

The word, taken apart.
फेरणो (pherṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed phirati, "turns"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari phiraï, "turns, walks about" (CDIAL 9078). Giving a purchase back is, probably, turning it back; फेर, "again", comes from the same word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
थैली (thailī) — a small bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to give back, then the Marwadi for alike.

You'll want to know: थैली.
थैली — thailī — "a small bag".
सामान थैली मांय राखो। sāmān thailī mā̃y rākho. Put the things in the bag.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
थैली (thailī) is inherited from Sanskrit sthavi, "sack, bag". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi thailī, "bag" (CDIAL 13746).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पलटणो (palaṭṇo) — to flip over; to change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for alike, then the Marwadi for a small bag.

You'll want to know: पलटणो.
पलटणो — palaṭṇo — "to flip over; to change".
आप थैली पलटो। āp thailī palṭo. Please turn the bag over.
Past (I …): म्हैं थैली पलटी, mhaĩ thailī palṭī.

The word, taken apart.
पलटणो (palaṭṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed pallaṭṭ, "turn, overturn", whose origin CDIAL calls very doubtful; CDIAL lists Old Marwari palaṭaï, "alters", and Hindi palaṭnā, "to overturn" (CDIAL 7968).

Lesson 5 of 5.
लाधणो (lādhṇo) — to be found; to turn up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a small bag, then the Marwadi for to flip over.

You'll want to know: लाधणो.
लाधणो — lādhṇo — "to be found; to turn up".
म्हारी रसीद कोनी लाधै। mhārī rasīd konī lādhai. I can't find my receipt.
Past (it …): वो सिक्को लाध्यो, vo sikko lādhyo.

The word, taken apart.
लाधणो (lādhṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit labdha, "taken, seized"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb lādhaï, "finds" (CDIAL 10946).
