## Script — the roof of 家, 八, then 大 with a dot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-TU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 突

**Three pieces, and you write every one of them already.**

**The roof, three strokes**, as on top of 家: the dot, a short left stroke, then a horizontal that hooks down at its right end.

**八, two strokes**, under the roof: a left-falling stroke, then a dot falling to the right.

**大 with a dot, four strokes**, at the foot, as on the right of 然: the bar, the left-falling stroke, the right-falling stroke, and the dot at its upper right last.

3 + 2 + 4 = nine.
