## You'll want to know: ぶぶん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C368-ZENTAI368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶぶん** — *bubun* — "a part; a section".

> **この ぶぶんに しつもんが あったら、きいて ください。** — *kono bubun ni shitsumon ga attara, kiite kudasai* — "If you have a question about this part, please ask."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶぶん** (*bubun*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bu*, "section" (Middle Chinese *buX*) and *bun*, "part" (*pjun*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
