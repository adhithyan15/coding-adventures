## You'll want to know: ਤਵਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C239-KAM239-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਵਾ** — *tavā* — "a griddle".

> ਤਵਾ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *tavā chullhe utte hai.*
>
> The griddle is on the stove.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਵਾ** (*tavā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*tapaka*, "frying pan"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tavā*, "circular griddle" (CDIAL 5670).
