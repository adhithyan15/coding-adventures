## You'll want to know: la rabia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C707-ENFADO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la rabia** — "rage, fury; rabies".

> Me da rabia perder el autobús.
>
> *It makes me furious to miss the bus.*

*Me da rabia* — it makes me mad. The same word names the disease rabies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rabia* is inherited from Latin *rabiēs* "rage, madness" through a spoken form *rabia* (EtymDB); English *rabies* and *rabid* share it.
