## You'll want to know: etenim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C365-ETENIM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**etenim** — "for indeed, and in fact".

> Domī mānsī; etenim pluēbat.
>
> *I stayed at home: for it was raining.*

*Etenim* (= *et* + *enim*) opens a sentence that gives the reason for the last one. Like *enim* and *nam*, it takes the indicative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*etenim* is built from *et* "and" and *enim* "for, you see" (Lewis & Short): and the reason is.
