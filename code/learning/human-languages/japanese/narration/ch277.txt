Japanese, chapter 277: Snowed In.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
おおゆき (ōyuki) — heavy snow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a cliff, then the Japanese for awful.

You'll want to know: おおゆき.
おおゆき — ōyuki — "heavy snow".
ことしは おおゆきでした。 — kotoshi wa ōyuki deshita — "We had a lot of snow this year."

The word, taken apart.
おおゆき (ōyuki) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from ō, "big", and yuki, "snow" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 2 of 5.
つもる (tsumoru) — to accumulate; to lie (of snow).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for awful, then the Japanese for heavy snow.

You'll want to know: つもる.
つもる — tsumoru — "to accumulate; to lie (of snow)".
やねに ゆきが つもっています。 — yane ni yuki ga tsumotte imasu — "There is snow lying on the roof."
Polite つもります, tsumorimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ゆきが つもりました。, yuki ga tsumorimashita.

The word, taken apart.
つもる (tsumoru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ていでん (teiden) — a power cut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to accumulate, then the Japanese for a snowstorm.

You'll want to know: ていでん.
ていでん — teiden — "a power cut".
あらしで ていでんに なりました。 — arashi de teiden ni narimashita — "The storm cut the power."

The word, taken apart.
ていでん (teiden) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are tei, "halt" (Middle Chinese deng) and den, "electricity" (denH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
