## You'll want to know: विद्रोह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C324-VIDROH324-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विद्रोह** — *vidroh* — "a rebellion; a revolt".

> भूख की वजह से किसानों ने विद्रोह किया।
>
> *bhūkh kī vajah se kisānoṁ ne vidroh kiyā.*
>
> The farmers rose up because they were going hungry.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विद्रोह** (*vidroh*) is built from *vi-* and Sanskrit *droha*, "injury, treachery"; Monier-Williams gives *droha* and the verb *vi-druh*, "to injure, to do wrong", but not *vidroha* itself, and the sense of a revolt is probably the later language's own. Hindi uses it for a revolt: **विद्रोह करना** is to rise up against a ruler.
