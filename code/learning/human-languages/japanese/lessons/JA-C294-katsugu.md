## You'll want to know: かつぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C294-MIKOSHI294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かつぐ** — *katsugu* — "to carry on one's shoulders".

> **わかい おとこが みこしを かついでいます。** — *wakai otoko ga mikoshi o katsuide imasu* — "Young men are carrying the shrine on their shoulders."

Polite **かつぎます**, *katsugimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが みこしを かつぎました。**, *ani ga mikoshi o katsugimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かつぐ** (*katsugu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
