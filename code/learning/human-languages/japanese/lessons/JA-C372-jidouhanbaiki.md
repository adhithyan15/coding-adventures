## You'll want to know: じどうはんばいき

**じどうはんばいき** — *jidōhanbaiki* — "a vending machine".

> **じどうはんばいきで おちゃを かいました。** — *jidōhanbaiki de ocha o kaimashita* — "I bought tea from the vending machine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じどうはんばいき** (*jidōhanbaiki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): five Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "oneself" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*), *dō*, "move" (*duwngX*), *han*, "trade" (*pjonH*), *bai*, "sell" (*meaH*) and *ki*, "machine" (*kj+j*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
