## You'll want to know: خطب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C306-URS306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خطب** — *khaṭaba* — "to propose marriage; to make a speech".

> خطب أخي جارتنا الشهر الماضي.
>
> *khaṭaba akhī jāratanā ash-shahr al-māḍī.*
>
> My brother asked our neighbour to marry him last month.

Present: يخطب, *yakhṭub*.

خطب فتاة: to ask for her hand. خطب في الناس: to speak to a crowd.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خطب** (*khaṭaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-b* (Lane), "to address, to speak to". Lane gives both senses: *khiṭba*, asking for a woman in marriage, and *khuṭba*, a speech.
