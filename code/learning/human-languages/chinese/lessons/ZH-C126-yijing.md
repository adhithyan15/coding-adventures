## You'll want to know: yǐjīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C126-OFTEN126-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yǐjīng* — "already".

> *wǒ yǐjīng chī fàn le* — I've already eaten.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǐjīng* is built from *yǐ* and *jīng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yiX* and *keng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to stop' and 'finished', a different word from the 以 of 以前, and the second 'constant' and 'the warp of cloth'; how the pair came to mean already is not settled.
