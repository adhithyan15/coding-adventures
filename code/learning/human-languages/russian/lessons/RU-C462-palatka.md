## You'll want to know: палатка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C462-KOSTYOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**палатка** (*palátka*) — "a tent".

> Мы поставили палатку у реки.
>
> *My postávili palátku u rekí.*
>
> We put up the tent by the river.

Feminine. *Ставить палатку* — to pitch a tent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*палатка* is usually traced to *палата* 'a chamber, a hall', which goes back through Greek to Latin *palātium* 'a palace' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists *палатка* with the sense 'tent' but no source.
