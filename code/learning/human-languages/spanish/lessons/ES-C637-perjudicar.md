## You'll want to know: perjudicar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C637-FAVOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**perjudicar** — "to harm, to damage".

> Fumar perjudica la salud.
>
> *Smoking harms your health.*

Spelling: *perjudiqué*. *Perjudicial* — harmful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perjudicar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praeiudicare* 'to judge in advance, to harm a case', built on *iudicare* and *iudex* 'judge' (EtymDB).
