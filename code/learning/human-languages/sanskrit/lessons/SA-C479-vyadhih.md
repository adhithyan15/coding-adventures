## You'll want to know: व्याधिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C479-VYADHI479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याधिः** — *vyādhiḥ* — "a disease".

> वृद्धस्य व्याधिः चिरं तिष्ठति।
>
> *vṛddhasya vyādhiḥ ciraṃ tiṣṭhati.*
>
> The old man's disease lingers.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याधिः** (*vyādhiḥ*) is built from *vi*, *ā* and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW thinks a link with √vyadh, "to pierce", less likely, and gives "disorder, disease, ailment" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
