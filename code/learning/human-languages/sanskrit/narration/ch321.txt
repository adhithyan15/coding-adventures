Sanskrit, chapter 321: A Coronation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अभिषेकः (abhiṣekaḥ) — a coronation; an anointing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for drives out, then the Sanskrit for freedom.

You'll want to know: अभिषेकः.
अभिषेकः — abhiṣekaḥ — "a coronation; an anointing".
अद्य प्रासादे अभिषेकः भवति। adya prāsāde abhiṣekaḥ bhavati. Today there is a coronation in the palace.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अभिषेकः (abhiṣekaḥ) is built from abhi and the root √sic, "to sprinkle"; MW gives abhiṣeka as anointing or consecrating by sprinkling water, and the inauguration of a king (MW).

पुरोहितः — purohitaḥ — "a family priest".
पुरोहितः अभिषेकं करोति। purohitaḥ abhiṣekaṃ karoti. The priest performs the coronation.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पुरोहितः (purohitaḥ) is built from puras, "in front", and hita, "placed", from the root √dhā; MW gives purohita as placed in front, appointed, and especially a family priest (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उच्चारयति (uccārayati) — pronounces; utters.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a sacred verse, then the Sanskrit for anoints.

You'll want to know: उच्चारयति.
उच्चारयति — uccārayati — "pronounces; utters".
बालः श्लोकं स्पष्टम् उच्चारयति। bālaḥ ślokaṃ spaṣṭam uccārayati. The boy pronounces the verse clearly.
Past (he …): सः मन्त्रम् उच्चारितवान्, saḥ mantram uccāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
उच्चारयति (uccārayati) is built from ud, "up, out", and the causative of the root √car, "to move"; MW gives uc-cārayati as to cause to go forth, to utter, pronounce (MW).
