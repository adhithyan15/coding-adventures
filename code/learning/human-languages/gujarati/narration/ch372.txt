Gujarati, chapter 372: Insurance and Guarantees.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વીમો (vīmo) — insurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a debt, then the Gujarati for to lose for good.

You'll want to know: વીમો.
વીમો — vīmo — "insurance".
જો ગાડીનો વીમો હશે તો મરામતના પૈસા બચશે. jo gāḍīno vīmo haśe to marāmatnā paisā bacśe. If the car is insured, you will save on repairs.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વીમો (vīmo) is usually traced to Persian bīma, "fear; insurance".

Lesson 3 of 5.
કવચ (kavac) — a protective cover; armour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for insurance, then the Gujarati for a guarantee.

You'll want to know: કવચ.
કવચ — kavac — "a protective cover; armour".
જો ઘર પર વીમાનું કવચ હશે તો આગ પછી મરામતની ચિંતા નહીં રહે. jo ghar par vīmānũ kavac haśe to āg pachī marāmatnī cintā nahī̃ rahe. If the house has insurance cover, there will be no worry about repairs after a fire.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
કવચ (kavac) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit kavaca, "armour, a coat of mail; any covering" (Monier-Williams): what covers and guards.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સચેત (sacet) — alert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a guarantee, then the Gujarati for a protective cover.

You'll want to know: સચેત.
સચેત — sacet — "alert".
ભીડમાં સચેત રહો, નહીંતર પાકીટ ખોવાઈ જશે. bhīḍmā̃ sacet raho, nahī̃tar pākīṭ khovāī jaśe. Stay alert in the crowd, or you will lose your wallet.

The word, taken apart.
સચેત (sacet) is usually traced to Sanskrit sacetas, "conscious, intelligent" (Monier-Williams), built from sa-, "with", and cetas, "mind": with one's mind awake to what goes on.

Lesson 5 of 5.
આવરવું (āvarvũ) — to include.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a protective cover, then the Gujarati for alert.

You'll want to know: આવરવું.
આવરવું — āvarvũ — "to include".
જો આ વીમો આગ અને પૂર બંનેને આવરશે તો હું તે લઈશ. jo ā vīmo āg ane pūr bannene āvarśe to hũ te laīś. If this policy covers both fire and flood, I will take it.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધા પ્રશ્નો આવર્યા, teṇe badhā praśno āvaryā.

The word, taken apart.
આવરવું (āvarvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to cover, to spread over", under a reconstructed āvarati, "covers" (CDIAL 1414).
