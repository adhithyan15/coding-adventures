## You'll want to know: a fortaleza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C442-HISTORICO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a fortaleza** — "the fortress; strength".

> A fortaleza protegia o porto contra os piratas.
>
> *The fortress protected the harbour against pirates.*

Also strength of mind: *a fortaleza de uma pessoa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fortaleza* came from Occitan *fortalessa* (EtymDB), built on *fort* 'strong', from Latin *fortis*.
