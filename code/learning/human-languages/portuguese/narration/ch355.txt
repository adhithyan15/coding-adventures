Portuguese, chapter 355: Procuro emprego.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a vaga — the vacancy, the opening; the wave (of heat).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to ease, then the Portuguese for to make up for.

You'll want to know: a vaga.
a vaga — "the vacancy, the opening; the wave (of heat)".
Há uma vaga para cozinheiro no restaurante da esquina. There is a vacancy for a cook at the restaurant on the corner.
Also uma vaga de calor — a heatwave.

The word, taken apart.
vaga "vacancy" is built on the verb vagar "to fall vacant", which comes from Latin vacāre "to be empty, to be free" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
o currículo — the CV, the résumé.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to make up for, then the Portuguese for the vacancy.

You'll want to know: o currículo.
o currículo — "the CV, the résumé".
Enviei o meu currículo a cinco empresas. I sent my CV to five companies.
Also o curriculum vitae or o CV. In schools, o currículo is also the syllabus.

The word, taken apart.
currículo is a learned borrowing from Latin curriculum "a running, a course, a race" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on currere "to run": a life's course.

Lesson 5 of 5.
contratar — to hire, to take on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the application, then the Portuguese for the candidate.

You'll want to know: contratar.
contratar — "to hire, to take on".
A empresa contratou dez pessoas este mês. The company hired ten people this month.
Contratar alguém — to hire someone. Um contrato — a contract.

The word, taken apart.
contratar is a learned borrowing from Latin contractāre (EtymDB), a form of contrahere "to draw together, to make a contract", built on trahere "to draw" (Lewis & Short).
