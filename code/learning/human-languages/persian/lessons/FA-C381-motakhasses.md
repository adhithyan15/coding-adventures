## You'll want to know: متخصص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C381-SARPARAST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**متخصص** (*motakhasses*) — "a specialist (doctor), an expert".

> دکتر مرا پیش یک متخصص قلب فرستاد.
>
> *doktor marâ pish-e yek motakhasses-e qalb ferestâd.*
>
> The doctor sent me to a heart specialist.

Above all for doctors and skilled trades. Plural *motakhassesân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متخصص** is usually traced to Arabic *mutakhaṣṣiṣ* "specialised", from the same Arabic family as *khosusi* "particular, private".
