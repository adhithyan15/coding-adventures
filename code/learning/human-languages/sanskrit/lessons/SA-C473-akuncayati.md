## You'll want to know: आकुञ्चयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C473-ANGA473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आकुञ्चयति** — *ākuñcayati* — "bends a limb".

> वृद्धः शनैः जानु आकुञ्चयति।
>
> *vṛddhaḥ śanaiḥ jānu ākuñcayati.*
>
> The old man slowly bends his knee.

Past (he …): सः जानु आकुञ्चितवान्, *saḥ jānu ākuñcitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकुञ्चयति** (*ākuñcayati*) is built from *ā* and the root √kuñc, "to bend"; MW gives the causative "to bend (as a limb)" and the ppp *ākuñcita* "bent (as the arm or the knee)" (MW).
