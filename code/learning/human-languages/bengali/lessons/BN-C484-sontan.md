# সন্তান (sôntān) — a child (as offspring); offspring

## You'll want to know: সন্তান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C484-SONTAN484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সন্তান** — *sôntān* — "a child (as offspring); offspring".

> আমার কাকার দুই সন্তান।
>
> *āmār kākār dui sôntān.*
>
> My uncle has two children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সন্তান** (*sôntān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṃtāna*, "continued succession", and so "offspring" (Monier-Williams): what carries the family on.
