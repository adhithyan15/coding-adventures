Malayalam, chapter 370: Testimony.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മൊഴി (moḻi) — testimony; a statement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an innocent person, then the Malayalam for to pass sentence.

You'll want to know: മൊഴി.
മൊഴി — moḻi — "testimony; a statement".
പോലീസ് സാക്ഷിയുടെ മൊഴി എടുത്തു. pōlīsŭ sākṣiyuṭe moḻi eṭuttu. The police took the witness's statement.
The police take it: മൊഴി (moḻi) എടുക്കുക. In old poetry it is any word or speech.

The word, taken apart.
മൊഴി (moḻi) is native Dravidian: its Tamil twin moḻi, "to say, to speak; a word, a language", stands in DEDR 4989. In the police station it is the word a witness gives.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ദൃക്സാക്ഷി (dṛksākṣi) — an eyewitness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to pass sentence, then the Malayalam for testimony.

You'll want to know: ദൃക്സാക്ഷി.
ദൃക്സാക്ഷി — dṛksākṣi — "an eyewitness".
അപകടത്തിന് രണ്ട് ദൃക്സാക്ഷികൾ ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു. apakaṭattinŭ raṇṭŭ dṛksākṣikaḷ uṇṭāyirunnu. There were two eyewitnesses to the accident.
Plural: ദൃക്സാക്ഷികൾ, dṛksākṣikaḷ.
ദൃക്, seeing, + സാക്ഷി, a witness: one who saw it happen.

The word, taken apart.
ദൃക്സാക്ഷി (dṛksākṣi) is built from Sanskrit dṛk, the form of dṛś, "sight, seeing", used in compounds (Monier-Williams), and സാക്ഷി (sākṣi), "a witness", from Sanskrit sākṣin (CDIAL 13321).

Lesson 3 of 5.
കള്ളം (kaḷḷaṁ) — a lie; a falsehood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for testimony, then the Malayalam for an eyewitness.

You'll want to know: കള്ളം.
കള്ളം — kaḷḷaṁ — "a lie; a falsehood".
സാക്ഷി കോടതിയിൽ കള്ളം പറഞ്ഞു. sākṣi kōṭatiyil kaḷḷaṁ paṟaññu. The witness told a lie in court.
To lie is കള്ളം (kaḷḷaṁ) പറയുക, the same as നുണ പറയുക. കള്ളൻ, a thief, comes from the same root.

The word, taken apart.
കള്ളം (kaḷḷaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1372 lists Malayalam kaḷḷam, "theft, untruth, false", from the root that also gives കള്ളൻ (kaḷḷan), "a thief".

Lesson 4 of 5.
മൊഴി നൽകുക (moḻi nalkuka) — to testify; to give a statement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an eyewitness, then the Malayalam for a lie.

You'll want to know: മൊഴി നൽകുക.
മൊഴി നൽകുക — moḻi nalkuka — "to testify; to give a statement".
ദൃക്സാക്ഷി പോലീസിന് മൊഴി നൽകി. dṛksākṣi pōlīsinŭ moḻi nalki. The eyewitness gave a statement to the police.
Past: മൊഴി നൽകി, moḻi nalki.
Who receives it takes -ക്ക് or -ന്: പോലീസിന് മൊഴി (moḻi) നൽകി.

The word, taken apart.
മൊഴി നൽകുക (moḻi nalkuka) joins മൊഴി (moḻi), "a statement", a native Dravidian word (DEDR 4989), and നൽകുക, "to give".

Lesson 5 of 5.
സത്യം ചെയ്യുക (satyaṁ ceyyuka) — to swear an oath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a lie, then the Malayalam for to testify.

You'll want to know: സത്യം ചെയ്യുക.
സത്യം ചെയ്യുക — satyaṁ ceyyuka — "to swear an oath".
സാക്ഷി കോടതിയിൽ സത്യം ചെയ്തു. sākṣi kōṭatiyil satyaṁ ceytu. The witness swore an oath in court.
Past: സത്യം ചെയ്തു, satyaṁ ceytu.
More than telling the truth: promising by oath that you are. A new verb, though both parts are known.

The word, taken apart.
സത്യം ചെയ്യുക (satyaṁ ceyyuka) joins സത്യം (satyaṁ), borrowed from Sanskrit satya, "true", and also "a promise, an oath" (CDIAL 13112), and ചെയ്യുക, "to do": to make an oath.
