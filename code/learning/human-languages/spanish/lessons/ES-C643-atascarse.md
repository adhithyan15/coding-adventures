## You'll want to know: atascarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C643-VOLANTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**atascarse** — "to get stuck; to get jammed".

> El coche se atascó en la arena.
>
> *The car got stuck in the sand.*

*Un atasco* is a traffic jam: the same family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *atascarse* is not settled; it is the verb behind *atasco* "traffic jam".
