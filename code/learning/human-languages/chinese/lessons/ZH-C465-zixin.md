## You'll want to know: 自信
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C465-FUN465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**自信** — *zìxìn* — "self-confident; sure of oneself".

> **看到他这么自信我很高兴** *kàn dào tā zhème zìxìn wǒ hěn gāoxìng* — I'm really glad to see him so sure of himself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**自信** (*zìxìn*) is built from 自 'self' and 信 'to trust', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH* and *sinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'from' and the second 'faithful' and 'proof'.
