Hindi, chapter 460: Missing Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विरह (virah) — the pain of separation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to kiss, then the Hindi for to soothe with soft words.

You'll want to know: विरह.
विरह — virah — "the pain of separation".
पति के विरह में पत्नी बहुत उदास थी। pati ke virah meṁ patnī bahut udās thī. Parted from her husband, the wife was very sad.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विरह (virah) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit viraha, "parting, separation, esp. of lovers" (Monier-Williams). Songs and poems are full of it.

You'll want to know: सूना.
सूना — sūnā — "desolate; empty".
बच्चों के बिना घर सूना लगता है। baccoṁ ke binā ghar sūnā lagtā hai. The house feels empty without the children.

The word, taken apart.
सूना (sūnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi sūnā, "empty, hollow", under Sanskrit śūnyá, "empty" (CDIAL 12567). It is said of places that feel empty because someone is missing.
