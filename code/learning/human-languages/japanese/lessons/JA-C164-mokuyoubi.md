## You'll want to know: もくようび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C164-WEEK164-03]; assesses=[] -->

**もくようび** — *mokuyōbi* — "Thursday".

> **もくようびに はたらく つもりです。** — *mokuyōbi ni hataraku tsumori desu* — "I plan to work on Thursday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もくようび** (*mokuyōbi*) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese *moku*, "tree, wood" (Middle Chinese *muwk*), and *yō*, "planet, weekday" (*yewH*), borrowed from Chinese, with native *hi*, "day", voiced to *bi* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
