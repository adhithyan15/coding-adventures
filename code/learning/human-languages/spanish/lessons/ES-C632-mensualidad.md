## You'll want to know: la mensualidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C632-PAGO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la mensualidad** — "the monthly payment".

> Pago una mensualidad de cincuenta euros.
>
> *I pay fifty euros a month.*

One monthly payment out of several.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mensualidad* is built on *mensual*, a learned borrowing from Late Latin *mensualis*, from *mensis* 'month' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
