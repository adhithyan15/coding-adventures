## You'll want to know: じすいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C497-MEAL497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じすいする** — *jisui suru* — "to cook for oneself".

> **あには まいにち じすいしています。** — *ani wa mainichi jisuishiteimasu* — "My elder brother cooks for himself every day."

Polite **じすいします**, *jisuishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **あにが じすいしました。**, *ani ga jisuishimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じすいする** (*jisui suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *jisui* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "oneself" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*) and *sui*, "cook" (*tsyhwe*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
