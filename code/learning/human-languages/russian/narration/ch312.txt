Russian, chapter 312: Congratulations and wishes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поздравлять (pozdravlyát) — to congratulate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for solemn, then the Russian for festive.

You'll want to know: поздравлять.
поздравлять (pozdravlyát) — "to congratulate".
Друзья поздравили меня с днём рождения. Druzyá pozdrávili menyá s dnyom rozhdéniya. My friends wished me a happy birthday.
Imperfective; perfective поздравить (pozdrávit), поздравлю. The occasion takes с + instrumental: с праздником!

The word, taken apart.
поздравлять (pozdravlyát) (EtymDB: "to congratulate") is built on по- and здравый "healthy", the Church Slavonic shape (EtymDB: Church Slavonic sŭdravŭ) of native здоровый: to wish someone health.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: пожелание.

Бабушка написала добрые пожелания в открытке. Bábushka napisála dóbryye pozhelániya v otkrýtke. Grandma wrote kind wishes in the card.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
желать (zhelát) — to wish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for congratulations, then the Russian for a wish.

You'll want to know: желать.
желать (zhelát) — "to wish".
Желаю тебе счастья! Zheláyu tebé schástya! I wish you happiness!
Imperfective; perfective пожелать (pozhelát). The person is in the dative, the wish in the genitive: желаю удачи.

The word, taken apart.
желать (zhelát) is inherited from Proto-Slavic želati "to wish, to long for" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
тост (tost) — a toast (with drinks).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a wish, then the Russian for to wish.

You'll want to know: тост.
тост (tost) — "a toast (with drinks)".
Отец произнёс тост за молодых. Otéts proiznyós tost za molodýkh. Father proposed a toast to the newlyweds.
Поднять тост (tost) за + accusative. Also toasted bread.

The word, taken apart.
тост (tost) was borrowed from English toast (EtymDB), from Old French toster "to roast", from Latin tostus "roasted", the participle of torrēre "to parch".
