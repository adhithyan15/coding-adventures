Portuguese, chapter 418: De barco.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o bote — the dinghy, the small boat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to go ashore, then the Portuguese for to set sail.

You'll want to know: o bote.
o bote — "the dinghy, the small boat".
Fomos de bote até à praia do outro lado. We went by dinghy to the beach on the other side.
O bote salva-vidas — the lifeboat.

The word, taken apart.
bote came from Old French bot, which was taken from Middle English boot, Old English bāt 'boat' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
o barqueiro — the boatman, the ferryman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to row, then the Portuguese for the canoe.

You'll want to know: o barqueiro.
o barqueiro — "the boatman, the ferryman".
O barqueiro leva as pessoas de uma margem à outra. The boatman takes people from one bank to the other.
Built on o barco.

The word, taken apart.
barqueiro is built on barco 'boat' with -eiro, a person who works with it; the family goes back to Late Latin barca (Lewis & Short).
