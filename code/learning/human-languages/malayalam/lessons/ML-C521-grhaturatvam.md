# ഗൃഹാതുരത്വം (gṛhāturatvaṁ) — nostalgia; homesickness

## You'll want to know: ഗൃഹാതുരത്വം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C521-VISHADAM521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗൃഹാതുരത്വം** — *gṛhāturatvaṁ* — "nostalgia; homesickness".

> പഴയ പാട്ടുകൾ കേൾക്കുമ്പോൾ എനിക്ക് ഗൃഹാതുരത്വം തോന്നും.
>
> *paḻaya pāṭṭukaḷ kēḷkkumpōḷ enikkŭ gṛhāturatvaṁ tōnnuṁ.*
>
> When I hear old songs, I feel nostalgic.

ഗൃഹം house + ആതുരം sick + -ത്വം -ness: the state of being sick for home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗൃഹാതുരത്വം** (*gṛhāturatvaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *gṛha*, "a house, a home", and *ātura*, "sick in body or mind" (Monier-Williams), with the noun ending -ത്വം.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
