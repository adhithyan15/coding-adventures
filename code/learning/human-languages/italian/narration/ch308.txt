Italian, chapter 308: Il battesimo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il battesimo — the christening, the baptism.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to wean, then the Italian for the dummy.

You'll want to know: il battesimo.
il battesimo — "the christening, the baptism".
Il battesimo è domenica in chiesa. The christening is on Sunday in church.
Il battesimo del volo — your first flight.

The word, taken apart.
battesimo comes from Church Latin baptismus, from Greek baptismós "a dipping" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la madrina — the godmother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to christen, then the Italian for the godfather.

You'll want to know: la madrina.
la madrina — "the godmother".
La madrina mi ha regalato una catenina d'oro. My godmother gave me a little gold chain.
A new ship has una madrina too, who names it.

The word, taken apart.
madrina is the feminine pair of padrino, built on madre "mother", from Latin māter (Lewis & Short).
