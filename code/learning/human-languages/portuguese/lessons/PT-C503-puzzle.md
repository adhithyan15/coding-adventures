## You'll want to know: o puzzle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C503-JOGOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o puzzle** — "jigsaw puzzle".

> Fizemos um puzzle de mil peças nas férias.
>
> *We did a thousand-piece jigsaw on holiday.*

In Brazil: *o quebra-cabeça*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*puzzle* was borrowed from English *puzzle*; the English word has no settled origin.

## What you've built
