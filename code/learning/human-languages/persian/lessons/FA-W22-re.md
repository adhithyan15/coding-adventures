# ر (re) — the letter that ends چطور

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-HE-01, FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ه and و once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **چطور** (*chetor*), "how".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ر** — *re*, the sound *r*.

It ends **چطور** (*chetor*), "how", from "how are you?". **ر** is a
**non-connector**, like **ا** and **و**, which you already write: it joins the
letter before it but never the one after, so a small gap always follows it.

## Writing: ر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: begin at the upper tip and descend through the short stroke
- **2.** without lifting, sweep left through the lower curve — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 0.** The pen never leaves the paper.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, ر demonstration at 01:10–01:12 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-HE-01, FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **چطور** and point at ر inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ر three times, saying *r* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: و, then ر, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **چطور** aloud once more]

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-HE-01, FA-SCRIPT-VAV-01, FA-SCRIPT-C22-RE-01] -->

come from? (**چطور**, *chetor*, "how".)
