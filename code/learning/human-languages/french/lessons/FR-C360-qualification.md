## You'll want to know: la qualification
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C360-CARRIERE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la qualification** — "the qualification".

> Ce travail ne demande pas de qualification.
>
> *This job does not require any qualification.*

In sport, *la qualification* is qualifying for the next round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qualification* comes from Medieval Latin *quālificātiō*, built on Latin *quālis* "of what kind" (Lewis & Short).
