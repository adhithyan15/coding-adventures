## You'll want to know: সুদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C415-SUD415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সুদ** — *sud* — "interest (on money)".

> ব্যাংক সঞ্চয়ের ওপর সুদ দেয়।
>
> *bængk sôñchôyer opôr sud dey.*
>
> The bank pays interest on savings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সুদ** (*sud*) is usually traced to Persian *sūd*, "profit, gain, interest".
