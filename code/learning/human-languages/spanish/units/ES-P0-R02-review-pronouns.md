# Review: Subject Pronouns

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Out loud, no peeking: say the Spanish word for "I," then "you"
(informal), then "we."

## New Material

No new vocabulary. Fresh combination: instead of drilling the pronouns in
isolation like Unit 2 did, place them next to people you actually know.

- Think of yourself: "**yo**"
- Think of a close friend you'd text casually: "**tú**"
- Think of your boss, or someone you just met: "**usted**"
- Think of you and your family together: "**nosotros**"
- Think of a group of your friends, not including you: "**ellos**" (or
  "**ellas**" if they're all women)

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the pronoun for yourself]
- [YOU SAY: the pronoun for a close friend]
- [YOU SAY: the pronoun you'd use for your boss]
- [YOU SAY: the pronoun for "you and your family"]

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Quick recall: which pronoun literally traces back to "your
mercy/grace"? ... *usted* — from *vuestra merced*.
