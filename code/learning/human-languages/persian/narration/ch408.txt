Persian, chapter 408: Fraud and corruption.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کلاهبرداری (kolâhbardâri) — fraud, a scam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for innocent, then the Persian for to sentence.

You'll want to know: کلاهبرداری.
کلاهبرداری (kolâhbardâri) — "fraud, a scam".
این ایمیل یک کلاهبرداری است. in imeyl yek kolâhbardâri ast. This email is a scam.
Literally hat-lifting: kolâh "hat" + bardâri "taking up". Kolâhbardâr — a swindler.

The word, taken apart.
کلاهبرداری (kolâhbardâri) is a native Persian compound built from kolâh "hat", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian kulāf, + bardâri "lifting", from bar "up" and dâshtan "to hold". A swindler lifts your hat.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سرقت (serqat) — theft, robbery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to sentence, then the Persian for fraud.

You'll want to know: سرقت.
سرقت (serqat) — "theft, robbery".
دیشب در محله ما یک سرقت اتفاق افتاد. dishab dar mahalle-ye mâ yek serqat ettefâq oftâd. Last night there was a robbery in our neighbourhood.
The formal word; everyday speech says dozdi.

The word, taken apart.
سرقت (serqat) is usually traced to Arabic sariqa "theft".

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: رشوه.
رشوه (reshve) — "a bribe".
او رشوه نگرفت. u reshve nagereft. He did not take a bribe.
Reshve dâdan / gereftan — to give / take a bribe.

The word, taken apart.
رشوه (reshve) was borrowed from Arabic rashwa "a bribe" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
فساد (fesâd) — corruption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for theft, then the Persian for a bribe.

You'll want to know: فساد.
فساد (fesâd) — "corruption".
مردم از فساد خسته شده اند. mardom az fesâd khaste shode and. People are tired of corruption.
Also decay: fesâd-e mive, fruit going bad.

The word, taken apart.
فساد (fesâd) was borrowed from Arabic fasād "corruption, decay" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
اتهام (ettehâm) — a charge, an accusation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: اتهام.
اتهام (ettehâm) — "a charge, an accusation".
او این اتهام را قبول نکرد. u in ettehâm râ qabul nakard. He did not accept this accusation.
Plural ettehâmât. Be ettehâm-e … — on a charge of ….

The word, taken apart.
اتهام (ettehâm) was borrowed from Arabic ittihām "accusation" (EtymDB).
