Italian, chapter 171: Arrival, Departure, Destination, Landscape, View.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'arrivo — arrival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a drum, then the Italian for a ball.

You'll want to know: l'arrivo.
l'arrivo — "arrival".

The word, taken apart.
arrivo is the noun of arrivare, "to arrive", which from Vulgar Latin arripare, from ad, "to", and ripa, "a riverbank": to come to shore. (EtymDB 2.1).
