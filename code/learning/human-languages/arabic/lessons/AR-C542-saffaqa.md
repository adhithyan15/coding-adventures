# صفق (ṣaffaqa) — to applaud

## You'll want to know: صفق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C542-MASRAH542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صفق** — *ṣaffaqa* — "to applaud".

> صفق الناس للممثلين.
>
> *ṣaffaqa an-nās li-l-mumaththilīn.*
>
> The people applauded the actors.

Present: يصفق, *yuṣaffiq*.

It takes لـ for the one applauded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صفق** (*ṣaffaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-f-q*, "to strike, to clap" (Lane). Form II ṣaffaqa is to clap again and again.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
