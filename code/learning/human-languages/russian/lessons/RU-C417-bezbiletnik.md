## You'll want to know: безбилетник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C417-MARSHRUTKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**безбилетник** (*bezbilétnik*) — "a fare dodger".

> В автобусе поймали двух безбилетников.
>
> *V avtóbuse poymáli dvukh bezbilétnikov.*
>
> Two fare dodgers were caught on the bus.

Built on *без билета* — without a ticket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*безбилетник* is built from *без* 'without', *билет* 'a ticket' and *-ник* 'one who'. *Билет* came from French *billet* 'a note, a ticket'; EtymDB does not list the compound.
