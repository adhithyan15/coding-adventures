## You'll want to know: प्रयोगशाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C361-SHODH361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोगशाला** — *prayogśālā* — "a laboratory".

> हमारे स्कूल में एक नई प्रयोगशाला है।
>
> *hamāre skūl meṁ ek naī prayogśālā hai.*
>
> There is a new laboratory in our school.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोगशाला** (*prayogśālā*) is built from *prayoga*, "use, application", and *śālā*, "a hall, a house" (Monier-Williams): a hall for experiments.
