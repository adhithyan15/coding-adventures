# Eat, drink, go, come — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *I go to the market every day*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-LEX-C155-FOOD155-01, MW-SCRIPT-C155-DHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-C153-UU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *I eat roti*; *he eats roti*; *you eat roti*, to **आप**]
- [YOU SAY: *I drink milk*; *mother drinks milk every day*]
- [YOU SAY: *I come every day*; *he comes every day*]
- [YOU SAY: *he goes to the market*; *you go to the market*]
- [YOU READ: **दूध**, and name its last letter]
- [YOU READ: **जाऊं**, and name the letter after **जा**]
