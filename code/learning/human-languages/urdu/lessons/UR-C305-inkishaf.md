## You'll want to know: انکشاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C305-MUAMMA305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**انکشاف** — *inkishāf* — "a revelation; a disclosure".

> اخبار میں ایک نیا انکشاف تھا۔
>
> *akhbār meṅ ek nayā inkishāf thā.*
>
> The paper had a new revelation.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انکشاف** (*inkishāf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *inkishāf*, "a becoming uncovered": something hidden comes to light.
