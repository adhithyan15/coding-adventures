French, chapter 356: Sans emploi.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'emploi — the job, employment; the use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to resign, then the French for the notice.

You'll want to know: l'emploi.
l'emploi — "the job, employment; the use".
Mon frère cherche un emploi dans une banque. My brother is looking for a job in a bank.
Un emploi du temps — a timetable. Le mode d'emploi — the instructions. Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
emploi is formed from employer (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin implicāre "to enfold, to involve" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
le chômage — the unemployment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the notice, then the French for the job.

You'll want to know: le chômage.
le chômage — "the unemployment".
Il est au chômage depuis trois mois. He has been out of work for three months.
Être au chômage — to be out of work. Toucher le chômage — to get unemployment benefit; in Québec the benefit is l'assurance-emploi.

The word, taken apart.
chômage is built on chômer "to stop work". The verb is usually traced to Late Latin caumāre "to rest during the heat", from Greek kaûma "burning heat".

Lesson 4 of 5.
embaucher — to hire, to take on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the unemployment, then the French for the unemployed person.

You'll want to know: embaucher.
embaucher — "to hire, to take on".
Le restaurant embauche des serveurs pour l'été. The restaurant is taking on waiters for the summer.
Le restaurant embauche — the restaurant is hiring.

The word, taken apart.
embaucher is usually explained as built from en- and an old word bauche "workshop": to bring someone into the workshop. The origin of bauche is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
recruter — to recruit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the unemployed person, then the French for to hire.

You'll want to know: recruter.
recruter — "to recruit".
L'entreprise recrute des ingénieurs. The company is recruiting engineers.
The person recruited is une recrue.

The word, taken apart.
recruter is built on recrue "a recruit" (EtymDB), the old participle of recroître "to grow again": new growth for a group. croître is inherited from Latin crēscere "to grow" (Lewis & Short).
