## You'll want to know: ઓપરેશન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C485-DAKHAL485-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓપરેશન** — *opreśan* — "an operation".

> દાદાનું ઓપરેશન સારી રીતે થયું.
>
> *dādānũ opreśan sārī rīte thayũ.*
>
> Grandfather's operation went well.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓપરેશન** (*opreśan*) is borrowed from English *operation*.
