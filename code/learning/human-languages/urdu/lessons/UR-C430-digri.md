## You'll want to know: ڈگری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C430-PARA430-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈگری** — *ḍigrī* — "a degree (of temperature)".

> آج چالیس ڈگری گرمی ہے۔
>
> *āj chālīs ḍigrī garmī hai.*
>
> It's forty degrees today.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈگری** (*ḍigrī*) is borrowed from English *degree*.
