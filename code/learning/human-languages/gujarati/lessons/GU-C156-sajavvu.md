## You'll want to know: સજાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C156-ADORN156-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સજાવવું** — *sajāvvũ* — "to decorate".

> **અમે દિવાળીમાં ઘર સજાવીએ છીએ.** — *ame divāḷīmā̃ ghar sajāvīe chīe* — "We decorate the house at Diwali."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **સજાવવું** (*sajāvvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of the verb; under Sanskrit *sajja*, "put in place, equipped", it lists Gujarati *sāj*, "dress, equipment", which may be related (CDIAL 13095).
