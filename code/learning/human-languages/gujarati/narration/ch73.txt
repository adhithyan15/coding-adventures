Gujarati, chapter 73: Light, New, Old, Hot, Cheap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હલકું (halkũ) — light (not heavy).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for short, then the Gujarati for heavy.

You'll want to know: હલકું.
હલકું (halkũ) — "light (not heavy)". The opposite of ભારે.

What you've built.
One more word for light, new, old, hot, cheap.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: halkũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: halkũ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say halkũ]

[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for short, then the Gujarati for heavy, then say halkũ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does હલકું (halkũ) mean? ("Light (not heavy)".) Say it once more.
