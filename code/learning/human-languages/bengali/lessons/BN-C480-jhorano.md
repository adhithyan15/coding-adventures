## You'll want to know: ঝরানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C480-PUSHTI480-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝরানো** — *jhôrāno* — "to shed (weight, tears)".

> ব্যায়াম করে বাবা পাঁচ কেজি ওজন ঝরালেন।
>
> *bæyām kôre bābā pā̃ch keji ojôn jhôrālen.*
>
> By exercising, my father shed five kilos.

Past (he/she …): তিনি দশ কেজি ওজন ঝরালেন, *tini dôsh keji ojôn jhôrālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝরানো** (*jhôrāno*) is the causative of ঝরা, to fall away, which CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5346): to make something fall away.
