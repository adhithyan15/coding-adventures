## You'll want to know: విశాలమైన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C467-ANTASTU467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**విశాలమైన** — *viśālamaina* — "spacious; broad".

> హోటలులో మాకు విశాలమైన గది ఇచ్చారు.
>
> *hōṭalulō māku viśālamaina gadi iccāru.*
>
> At the hotel they gave us a spacious room.

It stands before its noun: విశాలమైన రోడ్డు, a broad road. విశాలంగా is the adverb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**విశాలమైన** (*viśālamaina*) is built from Sanskrit *viśāla*, "spacious, broad" (CDIAL 11936), with the Telugu adjective ending -మైన.
