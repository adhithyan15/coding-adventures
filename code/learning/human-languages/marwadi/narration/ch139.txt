Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 139: Crow, Pigeon, Parrot, Koel, Kite.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.

Butter? (मक्खण, makkhaṇ.) Buttermilk? (छाछ, chhāchh.) Lassi? (लस्सी, lassī.) A kachori? (कचोरी, kachorī.) Bhujia? (भुजिया, bhujiyā.)
