## You'll want to know: विलीयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C440-HIMA440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विलीयते** — *vilīyate* — "melts".

> ग्रीष्मे पर्वते हिमं विलीयते।
>
> *grīṣme parvate himaṃ vilīyate.*
>
> In summer the snow on the mountain melts.

Past (it …): हिमं विलीनवत्, *himaṃ vilīnavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विलीयते** (*vilīyate*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and the root √lī, "to cling, to dissolve"; MW gives *vi-lī* as to be dissolved, to melt, with the participle *vilīna*, "melted" (MW).
