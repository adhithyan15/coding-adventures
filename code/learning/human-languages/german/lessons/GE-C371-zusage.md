## You'll want to know: die Zusage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C371-ZUSAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Zusage** — "the acceptance, the promise; the job offer".

> Nach dem Vorstellungsgespräch habe ich eine Zusage bekommen.
>
> *After the interview I received a job offer.*

Plural: *die Zusagen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zusage* is the noun of *zusagen*, from *zu* and *sagen* "to say", Proto-Germanic *sagjaną* (EtymDB).
