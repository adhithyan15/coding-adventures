## You'll want to know: diàoyú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C141-FUN141-05]; assesses=[] -->

*diàoyú* — "to go fishing".

> *wǒ bàba xǐhuan diàoyú* — My dad loves fishing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diàoyú* is built from *diào* 'to angle' and *yú* 'fish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tewH* and *ngjo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first with this very word, 'to fish'.
