(**たいせつ**, *taisetsu*.) Necessary? (**ひつよう**, *hitsuyou*.) Simple? (**かんたん**, *kantan*.) Complicated? (**ふくざつ**, *fukuzatsu*.) Well-behaved? (**おとなしい**, *otonashii*.) Dear? (**なつかしい**, *natsukashii*.) Frustrating? (**くやしい**, *kuyashii*.) Suspicious? (**あやしい**, *ayashii*.) Detailed? (**くわしい**, *kuwashii*.) Itchy? (**かゆい**, *kayui*.) Smelly? (**くさい**, *kusai*.) Humid? (**むしあつい**, *mushiatsui*.) Funny; strange? (**おかしい**, *okashii*.) Interesting? (**おもしろい**, *omoshiroi*.) Amazing? (**すごい**, *sugoi*.) Terrible? (**ひどい**, *hidoi*.) Young? (**わかい**, *wakai*.)
