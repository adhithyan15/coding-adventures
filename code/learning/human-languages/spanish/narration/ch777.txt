Lesson 1 of 7.
congelar — to freeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to update, then the Spanish for the keyboard.

You'll want to know: congelar.
congelar — "to freeze".
Si no vas a comerlo hoy, congélalo. If you aren't going to eat it today, freeze it.
Congelado — frozen. Los congelados — frozen food.

The word, taken apart.
congelar is a learned borrowing from Latin congelāre 'to cause to freeze' (EtymDB), built on gelū 'frost', the word that gave hielo.

Lesson 2 of 7.
enfriar — to cool, to chill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the keyboard, then the Spanish for to freeze.

You'll want to know: enfriar.
enfriar — "to cool, to chill".
Si la sopa está muy caliente, déjala enfriar. If the soup is very hot, let it cool.
It takes an accent: enfrío. Enfriarse is to cool down, and also to catch a cold.

The word, taken apart.
enfriar is built on frío 'cold' with en- and -ar. Frío is inherited from Latin frīgidus 'cold' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 7.
enchufar — to plug in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to cool, then the Spanish for to iron.

You'll want to know: enchufar.
enchufar — "to plug in".
Si no carga, enchufa el cable otra vez. If it isn't charging, plug the cable in again.
El enchufe is the plug or socket; in Spain it also means connections, knowing the right people.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of enchufar is not settled. It is usually treated as imitative, built on a sound like chuf, the noise of one pipe pushed into another.
