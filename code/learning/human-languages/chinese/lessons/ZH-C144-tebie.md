## You'll want to know: tèbié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tèbié* — "special; especially".

> *zhège dìfang hěn tèbié* — This place is special.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tèbié* is built from *tè* 'alone' and *bié* 'different', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dok* and *bjet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
