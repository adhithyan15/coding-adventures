## You'll want to know: راکت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C514-BALL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**راکت** (*râket*) — "a racket (for sport)".

> راکت تنیس او خیلی گران است.
>
> *râket-e tenis-e u kheyli gerân ast.*
>
> Her tennis racket is very expensive.

Plural *râkethâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*râket* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *raquette*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
