## You'll want to know: vorschlagen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C335-DISKUSSION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vorschlagen** — "to suggest, to propose".

> Mein Freund schlägt vor, dass wir zusammen kochen.
>
> *My friend suggests that we cook together.*

Separable and strong: *er schlägt vor*, *er hat vorgeschlagen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vorschlagen* is built from *vor* "forward" and *schlagen* "to strike", which is usually traced to Proto-Germanic *slahaną*. The sense of putting an idea forward grew out of the parts; EtymDB has no entry for the compound.
