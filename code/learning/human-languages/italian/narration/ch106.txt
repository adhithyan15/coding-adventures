Italian, chapter 106: Beef, Vegetables, Mushroom, Beans, Cucumber.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.

il manzo — beef.

Warm-up.

A washing machine? (la lavatrice.) An armchair? (la poltrona.) A wardrobe? (l'armadio.) A sofa? (il divano.) A pot? (la pentola.) A frying pan? (la padella.) A vase; a jar? (il vaso.) A bucket? (il secchio.) A rope? (la corda.) A nail? (il chiodo.) A hammer? (il martello.) Glue? (la colla.) Scissors? (le forbici.) An alarm clock? (la sveglia.) A mobile phone? (il telefonino.) A camera? (la macchina fotografica.) A ring? (l'anello.)
