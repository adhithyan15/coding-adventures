## You'll want to know: voveō, vovēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C334-SACERDOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**voveō, vovēre** — "to vow, to promise solemnly".

> Dux deae templum novum vōvit.
>
> *The general vowed a new temple to the goddess.*

*voveō, vovēre*, perfect *vōvī*: short *o* in the present, long in the perfect and in its noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**voveō** is inherited; EtymDB traces it to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to vow", though Lewis & Short call the origin doubtful. English *vow* and *vote* come from its noun *vōtum*.
