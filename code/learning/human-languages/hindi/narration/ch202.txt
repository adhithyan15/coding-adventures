Hindi, chapter 202: A cave, A palace, A fort, A tower, A garden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गुफ़ा (gufā) — a cave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a pond, then the Hindi for a mine.

You'll want to know: गुफ़ा.
गुफ़ा — gufā — "a cave".

The word, taken apart.
गुफ़ा (gufā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from gupphā, "cave", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4204). CDIAL's suggestion, Sanskrit guhā, "cave", crossed with gup-, "to hide", is marked very doubtful.

You'll want to know: महल.
महल — mahal — "a palace".

The word, taken apart.
महल (mahal) is a loan from Arabic maḥall, "a place of alighting, a quarter", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḥ-l-l (Lane).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: बाग़.
बाग़ — bāg — "a garden".

The word, taken apart.
बाग़ (bāg) is a loan, usually traced to Persian bāgh, "garden". It is a different word from Hindi bāg, "rein", from Sanskrit valgā, "bridle" (CDIAL 11420).
