Portuguese, chapter 438: Subir e descer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a subida — the climb, the way up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the narrow lane, then the Portuguese for the fork.

You'll want to know: a subida.
a subida — "the climb, the way up".
A subida até ao castelo é cansativa. The climb up to the castle is tiring.
Also a rise: uma subida de preços.

The word, taken apart.
subida is built on subir 'to go up' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin subīre 'to go under, to come up'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 5 of 5.
desembocar — to come out (into); to flow (into).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the steep street, then the Portuguese for to run alongside.

You'll want to know: desembocar.
desembocar — "to come out (into); to flow (into)".
Esta rua desemboca na praça da igreja. This street comes out into the church square.
A river desemboca in the sea.

The word, taken apart.
desembocar is built from des-, em and boca 'mouth', inherited from Latin bucca 'cheek, mouth' (Lewis & Short): to come out of a mouth.
