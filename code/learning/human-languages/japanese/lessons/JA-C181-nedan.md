## You'll want to know: ねだん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C181-SHOP181-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ねだん** — *nedan* — "price; cost".

> **ねだんは せんえんです。** — *nedan wa sen'en desu* — "The price is a thousand yen."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねだん** (*nedan*) is built from native *ne*, "price, value", and Sino-Japanese *dan*, "grade, step", a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *dwanH*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
