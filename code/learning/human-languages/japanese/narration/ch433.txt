Japanese, chapter 433: Longing and Envy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
あこがれる (akogareru) — to yearn after; to be attracted by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for futile, then the Japanese for to give up.

You'll want to know: あこがれる.
あこがれる — akogareru — "to yearn after; to be attracted by".
おとうとは せんしゅに あこがれています。 — otōto wa senshu ni akogarete imasu — "My younger brother admires the athletes."
Polite あこがれます, akogaremasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): むすこが せんぱいに あこがれました。, musuko ga senpai ni akogaremashita.

The word, taken apart.
あこがれる (akogareru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

The word, taken apart.
きょうみ (kyōmi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kyō, "interest" (Middle Chinese xingH) and mi, "taste" (mj+jH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
