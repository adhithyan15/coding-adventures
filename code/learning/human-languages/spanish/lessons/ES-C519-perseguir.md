## You'll want to know: perseguir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C519-POLICIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**perseguir** — "to chase, to pursue".

> El perro persiguió al gato por la calle.
>
> *The dog chased the cat down the street.*

It changes like *seguir*: *persigo*, *persiguió*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perseguir* is inherited from Latin *persequī* 'to follow up, to pursue' (EtymDB), built on *per* 'through' and *sequī* 'to follow'.
