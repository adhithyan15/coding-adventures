## You'll want to know: ustionarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C302-INCENDIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ustionarsi** — "to get burned, to burn oneself".

> Mi sono ustionato la mano.
>
> *I burned my hand.*

For burns on the skin: *si è ustionato le dita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ustionarsi* is built on *ustione* "burn", from Latin *ustiō* "a burning", from *ūrere* "to burn" (Lewis & Short).
