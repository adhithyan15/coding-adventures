## You'll want to know: 上车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-02]; assesses=[] -->

**上车** — *shàngchē* — "to get on (a bus or train)".

> **下雨快上车** *xiàyǔ kuài shàngchē* — It's raining: quick, get in!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上车** (*shàngchē*) is built from 上 'to go up' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
