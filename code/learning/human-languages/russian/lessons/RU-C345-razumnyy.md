## You'll want to know: разумный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C345-SPRAVEDLIVO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**разумный** (*razúmnyy*) — "reasonable, sensible".

> Это разумное решение.
>
> *Éto razúmnoye reshéniye.*
>
> That is a sensible decision.

Short form *разумен*. *В разумных пределах* — within reason.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**разумный** is built on *разум* "reason", which Russian borrowed from Church Slavonic; it comes from Proto-Slavic *\*orzumŭ*, built on *\*umŭ* "mind", the word that gives *ум* (EtymDB). The *ра-* at the start is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian also has *роз-*.
