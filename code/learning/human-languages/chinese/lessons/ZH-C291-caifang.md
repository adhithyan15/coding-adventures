## You'll want to know: cǎifǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C291-INVENT291-01]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎifǎng* — "to interview someone for the news".

> *jìzhě cǎifǎng le wǒmen* — A reporter interviewed us.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones meet in this word: cǎi is said with a rising tone before fǎng, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎifǎng* is built from *cǎi* 'to pick' and *fǎng* 'to ask about', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshojX* and *phjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to take' and the second 'to consult': to gather news by asking. *phjangH* would regularly give fàng; the dipping tone of fǎng is irregular.
