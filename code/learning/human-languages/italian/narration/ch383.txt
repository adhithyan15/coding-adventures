Italian, chapter 383: Promosso o bocciato.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bocciare — to fail (a student); to reject.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the notes, then the Italian for the textbook.

You'll want to know: bocciare.
bocciare — "to fail (a student); to reject".
Se non studi, ti bocciano. If you do not study, they will fail you.
Essere bocciato — to fail a year or an exam. Of a plan: il progetto è stato bocciato.

The word, taken apart.
bocciare is built on boccia "a ball for bowls" (EtymDB): first to knock away another player's ball, then to knock out a student. The origin of boccia itself is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
promuovere — to pass (a student); to promote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to fail, then the Italian for the failing.

You'll want to know: promuovere.
promuovere — "to pass (a student); to promote".
Se mi promuovono, a settembre vado in quinta. If they pass me, in September I go into the final year.
Conjugated like muovere; past participle promosso. Also to promote a product or a person.

The word, taken apart.
promuovere comes from Latin prōmovēre "to move forward, to advance" (Lewis & Short), built from prō "forward" and movēre "to move".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la valutazione — the assessment, the evaluation.

You'll want to know: la valutazione.
la valutazione — "the assessment, the evaluation".
La valutazione finale dipende anche dai compiti a casa. The final assessment also depends on the homework.
Built on valutare.

The word, taken apart.
valutazione is built on valutare "to assess", from valuta "value" and valere "to be worth", inherited from Latin valēre "to be strong, to be worth" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
il punteggio — the score.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to pass, then the Italian for the assessment.

You'll want to know: il punteggio.
il punteggio — "the score".
Con questo punteggio entri all'università. With this score you get into university.
In games and sport too: il punteggio finale.

The word, taken apart.
punteggio is built on punto "point" with the ending -eggio; punto is inherited from Latin punctum "a prick, a point" (Lewis & Short).
