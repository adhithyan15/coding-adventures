## You'll want to know: अपमान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C461-APMAN461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अपमान** — *apmān* — "an insult; humiliation".

> सगळ्यांसमोर झालेला अपमान तो विसरला नाही.
>
> *sagḷyāṁsamor jhālelā apmān to visarlā nāhī.*
>
> He did not forget being humiliated in front of everyone.

Masculine.

अपमान करणे, to insult; अपमान होणे, to be humiliated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपमान** (*apmān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *apamāna*, "disrespect, disgrace" (CDIAL 433).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
