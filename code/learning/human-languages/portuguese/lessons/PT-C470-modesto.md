## You'll want to know: modesto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C470-MODESTIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**modesto** — "modest".

> É um rapaz modesto e não fala das suas notas.
>
> *He is a modest boy and does not talk about his marks.*

Of things too: *um salário modesto* — a modest salary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modesto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *modestus* "keeping within measure, moderate" (Lewis & Short), built on *modus* "a measure".
