## You'll want to know: báyá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C473-TEETH473-04]; assesses=[] -->

*báyá* — "to have a tooth pulled".

> *wǒ jīntiān qù báyá le xiànzài hái hěn téng* — I had a tooth out today, and it still really hurts.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*báyá* is built from *bá* 'to pull up' and 牙 'tooth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *beat* and *ngae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under three readings and glosses this one 'to pull up' and 'to promote'.
