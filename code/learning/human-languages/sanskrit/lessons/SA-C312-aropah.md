## You'll want to know: आरोपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C312-NIRAKARANA312-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आरोपः** — *āropaḥ* — "an allegation".

> पत्रिका न्यायाधीशे आरोपं प्रकाशयति।
>
> *patrikā nyāyādhīśe āropaṃ prakāśayati.*
>
> The newspaper publishes an allegation against the judge.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आरोपः** (*āropaḥ*) is built from *ā* and the causative of the root √ruh, "to rise", as in *āropayati*; MW gives *āropa* as imposing a burden, charging with, attributing to, and an allegation is its modern use (MW).
