## You'll want to know: el regreso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C561-PARTIDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el regreso** — "the return".

> A su regreso, todo había cambiado.
>
> *On his return, everything had changed.*

*De regreso* — on the way back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regreso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *regressus* 'a going back' (Lewis & Short), from *regredī* 'to step back'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
