Arabic, chapter 418: Stronger or weaker.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عزز (ʿazzaza) — to strengthen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to become evident, then the Arabic for to refer.

You'll want to know: عزز.
عزز — ʿazzaza — "to strengthen".
عززت الرياضة ثقته بنفسه. ʿazzazat ar-riyāḍa thiqatahu bi-nafsih. Sport strengthened his self-confidence.
Present: يعزز, yuʿazziz.

The word, taken apart.
عزز (ʿazzaza) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-z-z, "to be strong, mighty" (Lane). It is Form II, to make strong: to strengthen, to reinforce.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مستقل (mustaqill) — independent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to develop, then the Arabic for effectiveness.

You'll want to know: مستقل.
مستقل — mustaqill — "independent".
أصبح أخي مستقلا عن أسرته. aṣbaḥa akhī mustaqillan ʿan usratih. My brother became independent of his family.

The word, taken apart.
مستقل (mustaqill) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-l, "to be few; to lift" (Lane). Lane gives Form X for one who manages his affairs by himself alone; mustaqill, its active participle, is independent.
