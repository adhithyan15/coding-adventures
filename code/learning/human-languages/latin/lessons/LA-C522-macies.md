## You'll want to know: maciēs, maciēī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C522-PALL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**maciēs, maciēī** — "leanness, thinness".

> Maciēs eius morbum ostendit.
>
> *His thinness shows his illness.*

Feminine, fifth declension. English *emaciated* comes from the related verb *ēmaciō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**maciēs** belongs with *macer* "thin" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *emaciated* comes from the related verb *ēmaciō*.
