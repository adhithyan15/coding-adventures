## You'll want to know: প্রত্যক্ষদর্শী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C301-BIBORON301-01]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রত্যক্ষদর্শী** — *prôtyôkkhôdôrshī* — "an eyewitness".

> প্রত্যক্ষদর্শীরা বলল যে বাসটা লাল ছিল।
>
> *prôtyôkkhôdôrshīrā bôllô je bāsṭā lāl chhilô.*
>
> Eyewitnesses said that the bus was red.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রত্যক্ষদর্শী** (*prôtyôkkhôdôrshī*) is Sanskrit *pratyakṣadarśin*, 'one who has seen with his own eyes' (Monier-Williams): *pratyakṣa*, 'before the eyes', and *darśin*, 'seeing'.
