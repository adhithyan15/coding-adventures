Lesson 2 of 5.
पाड़ोसी (pāṛosī) — a neighbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a man, then the Marwadi for a woman.

You'll want to know: पाड़ोसी.
पाड़ोसी — pāṛosī — "a neighbour".

The word, taken apart.
पाड़ोसी (pāṛosī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from prātiveśya, "neighbouring; neighbour". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8936); the match is through Gujarati pāṛośī and Sindhi pāṛosī. It is built on prati, "near", and veśa, "house".

Lesson 5 of 5.
खाणो (khāṇo) — to eat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: खाणो.
खाणो — khāṇo — "to eat".

The word, taken apart.
खाणो (khāṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khādati, "chews, bites; eats". CDIAL lists Marwari khābo under it (CDIAL 3865). khābo is the same verb with another infinitive ending, -bo.
