## You'll want to know: la propina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C491-COMPRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la propina** — "the tip".

> Le dejamos una propina al camarero.
>
> *We left the waiter a tip.*

In much of Latin America a waiter is *el mesero*.
