# wir haben gesagt — "we said"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-PERFEKT-ER-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The fourth person, and the easiest of the four, because its form is
one you have been saying since the verb was introduced.

## You'll want to know: wir haben gesagt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-GRAMMAR-PERFEKT-WIR-01]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-VERB-ENDINGS-01, GE-LEX-GESAGT-01] -->

> **wir haben gesagt** — "we said."

The helper here is *haben* itself, unchanged from the dictionary form. German
does that everywhere: the *wir* form of any verb is the infinitive over again,
which is one of the few completely free pieces of the ending system.

So the sentence has *haben* in it twice over in a sense — as the recipe's helper
and as its own plain shape — and the participle at the back is still *gesagt*.
