## You'll want to know: planificar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C615-PLAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**planificar** — "to plan".

> Planificamos el viaje con tiempo.
>
> *We planned the trip in good time.*

More formal than *planear*. Spelling: *planifiqué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*planificar* is built on *plan* with the ending *-ificar*; *plan* rests on *plano*, a learned borrowing from Latin *planus* 'flat' (Lewis & Short).
