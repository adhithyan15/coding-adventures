## You'll want to know: ಕೊಠಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C460-VASATI460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಠಡಿ** — *koṭhaḍi* — "a chamber; a room".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಕೊಠಡಿ ಮೂರನೇ ಮಹಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *namma koṭhaḍi mūranē mahaḍiyalli ittu.*
>
> Our room was on the third floor.

A formal word; ಕೋಣೆ is the everyday room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಠಡಿ** (*koṭhaḍi*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *kōṣṭha*, "a storeroom, an inner apartment" (CDIAL 3546), through Prakrit.
