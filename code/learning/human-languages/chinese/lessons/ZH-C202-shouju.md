## You'll want to know: shōujù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C202-TILL202-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shōujù* — "receipt".

> *wǒ yào yì zhāng shōujù* — I need a receipt.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shōujù* is built from *shōu* 'to receive' and *jù* 'evidence', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuw* and *kjoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to rely on'.

One more word for asking for what I want.
