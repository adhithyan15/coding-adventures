## You'll want to know: रिमझिम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C438-VALIV438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रिमझिम** — *rimjhim* — "a drizzle".

> सकाळपासून रिमझिम चालू आहे.
>
> *sakāḷpāsūn rimjhim cālū āhe.*
>
> It has been drizzling since morning.

Feminine.

Also an adverb: रिमझिम पाऊस, gentle rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रिमझिम** (*rimjhim*) is an echo word that imitates light, steady rain; its origin is not settled.
