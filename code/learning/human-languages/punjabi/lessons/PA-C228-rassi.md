## You'll want to know: ਰੱਸੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C228-DIN228-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੱਸੀ** — *rassī* — "a rope; a washing line".

> ਗਿੱਲੇ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਰੱਸੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *gille kappṛe rassī utte han.*
>
> The wet clothes are on the line.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੱਸੀ** (*rassī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *raśmi*, "rope, bridle, ray of light"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *rassī*, "rope" (CDIAL 10648).
