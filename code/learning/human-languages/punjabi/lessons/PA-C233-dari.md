## You'll want to know: ਦਰੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C233-DIN233-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਰੀ** — *darī* — "a cotton rug".

> ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਨੀਲੀ ਦਰੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *farsh utte ikk nīlī darī hai.*
>
> There is a blue rug on the floor.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਰੀ** (*darī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*darita*, "split cane, mat"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *darī*, "carpet" (CDIAL 6194).
