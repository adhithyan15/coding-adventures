## You'll want to know: ಹೋರಾಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C398-PRATIBHATISU398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೋರಾಟ** — *hōrāṭa* — "a struggle; a fight".

> ನೀರಿಗಾಗಿ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯವರ ಹೋರಾಟ ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *nīrigāgi haḷḷiyavara hōrāṭa hattu varṣa naḍeyitu.*
>
> The villagers' struggle for water went on for ten years.

From ಹೋರಾಡು, to fight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೋರಾಟ** (*hōrāṭa*) is a native Dravidian compound, the noun beside ಹೋರಾಡು: *pōr*, "to fight, to strive" (DEDR 4540), whose *p-* became *h-*, and *āṭa*, "moving, playing", from *āḍu* (DEDR 347).
