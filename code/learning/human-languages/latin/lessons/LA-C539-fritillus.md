## You'll want to know: fritillus, fritillī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C539-ALEA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**fritillus, fritillī** — "a dice-box".

> Senex tālōs ē fritillō iacit.
>
> *The old man throws the knucklebones from the dice-box.*

Masculine, second declension. A little cup for shaking and throwing the dice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fritillus** is a native Latin noun whose origin is not settled (Lewis & Short gives none). The flower *fritillary* is named after it.
