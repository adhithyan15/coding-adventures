Russian, chapter 506: In the Vegetable Garden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
грядка (gryádka) — a garden bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to bite, then the Russian for a catch.

You'll want to know: грядка.
грядка (gryádka) — "a garden bed".
На этой грядке растёт морковь. Na étoy gryádke rastyót morkóv. Carrots are growing in this bed.
Feminine. A diminutive of гряда — a ridge.

The word, taken apart.
грядка (gryádka) is a diminutive of гряда 'a ridge, a row'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
полоть (polót) — to weed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a garden bed, then the Russian for a watering can.

You'll want to know: полоть.
полоть (polót) — "to weed".
Летом бабушка часто полет огород. Létom bábushka chásto pólet ogoród. In the summer Grandma often weeds the vegetable garden.
Imperfective; present полю, полешь. Perfective прополоть.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists полоть (polót) without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic pelti.
