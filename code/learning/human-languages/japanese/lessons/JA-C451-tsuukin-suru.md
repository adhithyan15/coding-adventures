## You'll want to know: つうきんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C451-COMMUTE451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**つうきんする** — *tsūkin suru* — "to commute".

> **あには でんしゃで つうきんしています。** — *ani wa densha de tsūkin shite imasu* — "My elder brother commutes by train."

Polite **つうきんします**, *tsūkinshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ははが じてんしゃで つうきんしました。**, *haha ga jitensha de tsūkin shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つうきんする** (*tsūkin suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *tsūkin* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tsū*, "commute" (Middle Chinese *thuwng*) and *kin*, "serve" (*gj+n*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
