# ਟੁੱਟਣਾ (ṭuṭṭṇā) — to get broken

## You'll want to know: ਟੁੱਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C310-SLIP310-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੁੱਟਣਾ** — *ṭuṭṭṇā* — "to get broken".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਗਲਾਸ ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਡਿੱਗਿਆ ਅਤੇ ਟੁੱਟਿਆ।
>
> *merā galās farsh utte ḍiggiā ate ṭuṭṭiā.*
>
> My glass fell on the floor and broke.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ਾ ਟੁੱਟਿਆ, *oh shīshā ṭuṭṭiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੁੱਟਣਾ** (*ṭuṭṭṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *truṭyati*, "is broken, falls asunder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭuṭṭṇā*, "is broken", under it (CDIAL 6065).

## What you've built
