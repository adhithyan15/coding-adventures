## You'll want to know: гавань
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C427-MAYAK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**гавань** (*gávan*) — "a harbour".

> Вечером в гавань вернулись все лодки.
>
> *Vécherom v gávan vernúlis vse lódki.*
>
> In the evening all the boats came back to the harbour.

Feminine, though it ends in -ь.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гавань* was borrowed from Dutch *haven* 'a harbour' (EtymDB), from Middle Dutch *havene*.
