## You'll want to know: गिधाड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C447-BAGLA447-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गिधाड** — *gidhāḍ* — "a vulture".

> आजकाल गिधाडं फार कमी दिसतात.
>
> *ājkāl gidhāḍaṁ phār kamī distāt.*
>
> These days vultures are seldom seen.

Neuter.

A neuter noun: one गिधाड, many गिधाडं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गिधाड** (*gidhāḍ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *gidhāḍ*, "vulture, kite", under Sanskrit *gṛdhra*, "greedy; a vulture" (CDIAL 4233).
