## You'll want to know: まぜる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C175-COOK175-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まぜる** — *mazeru* — "to mix; to stir, to blend".

> **さとうと しおを まぜます。** — *satō to shio o mazemasu* — "I mix sugar and salt."

Polite **まぜます**, *mazemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まぜる** (*mazeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
