## You'll want to know: じゅぎょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C224-SCHOOL224-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅぎょう** — *jugyō* — "a class; a lesson".

> **ごごは きょうしつで じゅぎょうが あります。** — *gogo wa kyōshitsu de jugyō ga arimasu* — "In the afternoon there is a class in the classroom."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅぎょう** (*jugyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ju*, "impart" (Middle Chinese *dzyuwH*) and *gyō*, "work, vocation" (*ngjaep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
