## You'll want to know: ascender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C595-CARRERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ascender** — "to be promoted; to rise".

> Ascendió a directora en dos años.
>
> *She was promoted to director in two years.*

Stem change e→ie: *asciendo*. *Ascender a* + post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ascender* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ascendere* 'to climb up', built from *ad* and *scandere* 'to climb' (EtymDB).
