## You'll want to know: معبد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C318-HADARA318-04]; assesses=[] -->

**معبد** — *maʿbad* — "a temple".

> زرنا معبدا قديما على النهر.
>
> *zurnā maʿbadan qadīman ʿalā an-nahr.*
>
> We visited an ancient temple on the river.

Plural: معابد, *maʿābid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معبد** (*maʿbad*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-b-d* (Lane), "to serve, to worship". The *mafʿal* pattern names a place: "a place of worship".
