## Chapters 148-178: 155 A2 headwords, thirty of them verbs

The first of four Bengali A2 vocabulary tranches. It has thirty-one chapters of
five words each, in four runs of at most nine chapters. Each run closes with
two reviews.

- **Verbs (30):** housework (*রান্না করা*, *সেলাই করা*), the pool and rest
  (*সাঁতার কাটা*, *বিশ্রাম নেওয়া*), arguing (*সন্দেহ করা*, *তর্ক করা*),
  making and mending (*গড়া*, *সারানো*), laundry and grooming (*শুকানো*,
  *কামড়ানো*) and talk (*আলোচনা করা*, *প্রশংসা করা*).
- **Nouns and qualities (125):** illness (*রোগ*, *সর্দি*, *স্বাস্থ্য*),
  dates (*তারিখ*, *জন্মদিন*), the town (*ফুটপাত*, *চিড়িয়াখানা*,
  *প্রাসাদ*, *দুর্গ*), linking words (*তাই*, *যেহেতু*, *যদি*), character
  (*বুদ্ধিমান*, *পরিশ্রমী*, *ভদ্র*), smells (*সুগন্ধি*), luggage and
  clothes (*মালপত্র*, *শ্যাম্পু*, *জিনস*), sweets (*সন্দেশ*, *পরোটা*,
  *জিলিপি*, *লিচু*), sea creatures (*কাঁকড়া*), transport (*রিকশা*), a
  wedding (*বিয়ে*), feelings (*আনন্দ*, *শান্তি*), a procession
  (*মিছিল*), flowers (*গোলাপ*), school (*বৃত্তি*), the car (*চাকা*), the
  kitchen (*বঁটি*, *শিলনোড়া*), money (*পেশা*, *সঞ্চয়*) and work
  (*বিজ্ঞানী*, *দমকল*, *ক্রেতা*).
- **Screening:** every headword uses only letters the course has taught, so
  script closure gains no violation, and a candidate that an earlier lesson
  already uses untaught was dropped. Glosses that would collide with a taught
  word were sharpened (*বুদ্ধিমান* "intelligent" beside *চালাক*, *কাচা*
  "to launder" beside *ধোয়া*), and *ছাত্রী* reads "a female student", since
  "a (female) student" would have left a bare "A" in the chapter title.
- **Language review:** every spelling and romanization was checked. In this
  tranche the review corrected *birôktô* and *bõṭi*, and chose the Kolkata
  *ঝাঁট দেওয়া* for "to sweep".
