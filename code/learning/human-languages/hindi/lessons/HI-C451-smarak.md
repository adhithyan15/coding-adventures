## You'll want to know: स्मारक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C451-SAIR451-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मारक** — *smārak* — "a monument, a memorial".

> शहर के बीच में एक पुराना स्मारक है।
>
> *śahar ke bīc meṁ ek purānā smārak hai.*
>
> There is an old monument in the middle of the town.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मारक** (*smārak*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *smāraka*, "recalling, reminding of" (Monier-Williams), from the root *smṛ*, "to remember": a monument is what reminds.
