## You'll want to know: ನದೀಮುಖ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C481-KARAVALI481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ನದೀಮುಖ** — *nadīmukha* — "an estuary; a river mouth".

> ನದೀಮುಖದಲ್ಲಿ ನದಿ ಸಮುದ್ರವನ್ನು ಸೇರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *nadīmukhadalli nadi samudravannu sēruttade.*
>
> At the estuary the river joins the sea.

ನದಿ, a river, plus ಮುಖ, a mouth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನದೀಮುಖ** (*nadīmukha*) is built from two Sanskrit words: *nadī*, "a river" (CDIAL 6943), and *mukha*, "a mouth, an entrance" (CDIAL 10158).
