# गर्तः (gartaḥ) — a hole in the ground; a pit

## You'll want to know: गर्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C300-BHUKAMPA300-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्तः** — *gartaḥ* — "a hole in the ground; a pit".

> मार्गे महान् गर्तः अस्ति।
>
> *mārge mahān gartaḥ asti.*
>
> There is a big pit in the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्तः** (*gartaḥ*) MW gives this *garta* as the same word as *karta*, a hollow, hole or cave, and gives it no root, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
