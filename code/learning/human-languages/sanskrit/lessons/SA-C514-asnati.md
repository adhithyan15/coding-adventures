## You'll want to know: अश्नाति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C514-PARIVESAYATI514-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अश्नाति** — *aśnāti* — "dines".

> पिता सायं भोजनम् अश्नाति।
>
> *pitā sāyaṃ bhojanam aśnāti.*
>
> Father has his meal in the evening.

Past (he …): सः भोजनम् अशितवान्, *saḥ bhojanam aśitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अश्नाति** (*aśnāti*) comes from the root √aś, "to eat, to consume", whose present is *aśnāti*; its past participle is *aśita* (MW).
