## You'll want to know: болото
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C460-RUCHEY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**болото** (*bolóto*) — "a swamp, a marsh".

> За деревней большое болото.
>
> *Za derévney bolshóye bolóto.*
>
> Beyond the village there is a big swamp.

Neuter. The native *-оло-* shape; Church Slavonic has *блато*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*болото* is inherited through Old East Slavic *boloto* from Proto-Slavic *bolto* 'a swamp' (EtymDB); its *-оло-* is the native East Slavic shape.
