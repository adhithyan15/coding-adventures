## You'll want to know: candidarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C356-CANDIDATURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**candidarsi** — "to apply, to put yourself forward".

> Mi sono candidata per un posto in banca.
>
> *I applied for a job at a bank.*

Reflexive, with *essere*: *mi sono candidata*. Also to stand in an election.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*candidarsi* is built on *candidato*, a learned borrowing from Latin *candidātus* "dressed in white" (Lewis & Short): a Roman seeking office wore a whitened toga.
