## You'll want to know: اعتراض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C348-ETIRAZ348-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتراض** — *etirāz* — "an objection".

> کیا آپ کو کوئی اعتراض ہے؟
>
> *kyā āp ko koī etirāz hai?*
>
> Do you have any objection?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتراض** (*etirāz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iʿtirāḍ*, "standing across the way; objecting".
