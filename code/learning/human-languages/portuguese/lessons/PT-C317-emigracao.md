## You'll want to know: a emigração
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C317-REFORMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a emigração** — "emigration".

> Nos anos sessenta, a emigração esvaziou muitas aldeias.
>
> *In the sixties emigration emptied many villages.*

Leaving the country; the opposite movement is immigration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emigração* is a learned word from Late Latin *ēmigrātiō* "a moving out", built on *migrāre* "to move from place to place" (Lewis & Short).
