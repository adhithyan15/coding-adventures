## You'll want to know: سده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C353-DOWRAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سده** (*sade*) — "a century".

> این مسجد در سده دوازدهم ساخته شد.
>
> *In masjed dar sade-ye davâzdahom sâkhte shod.*
>
> This mosque was built in the twelfth century.

The native word for a century; *qarn* is the Arabic one. Also the name of a winter fire festival.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sade* is a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; it is usually linked with *sad* "hundred".
