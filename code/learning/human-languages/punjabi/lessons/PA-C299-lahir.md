## You'll want to know: ਲਹਿਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C299-WATER299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਹਿਰ** — *lahir* — "a wave".

> ਝੀਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਛੋਟੀਆਂ ਲਹਿਰਾਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *jhīl vich chhoṭīāṁ lahirāṁ han.*
>
> There are small waves on the lake.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲਹਿਰ** (*lahir*) is inherited from Sanskrit *laharī*, "large wave"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *lahir*, "wave", under it (CDIAL 10999).
