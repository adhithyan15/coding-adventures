## You'll want to know: ਫਾਟਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C264-CROSS264-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫਾਟਕ** — *phāṭak* — "a big gate; a level-crossing gate".

> ਜਦੋਂ ਰੇਲ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ ਤਾਂ ਫਾਟਕ ਬੰਦ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *jadoṁ rel āundī hai tāṁ phāṭak band hundā hai.*
>
> When the train comes, the crossing gate is closed.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫਾਟਕ** (*phāṭak*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*phāṭṭakka*, "gate, door"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *phāṭak*, "gate", under it (CDIAL 9069).
