## You'll want to know: સંતાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C287-PAGLU287-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સંતાવું** — *santāvũ* — "to hide oneself".

> ચોર દાદર નીચે સંતાઈ ગયો.
>
> *cor dādar nīce santāī gayo.*
>
> The thief hid under the stairs.

Past (he …): તે ચોર સંતાઈ ગયો, *te cor santāī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to Sanskrit *sáṁtāyatē*, "is stretched out, is covered", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to conceal oneself" (CDIAL 12887): to be covered over, out of sight. A person hides himself with **સંતાવું**: **સંતાઈ ગયો**; a thing is hidden with **છુપાવવું**.
