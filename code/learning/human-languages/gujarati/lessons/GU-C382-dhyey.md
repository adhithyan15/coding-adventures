## You'll want to know: ધ્યેય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C382-DHYEY382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ધ્યેય** — *dhyey* — "a goal".

> જો ધ્યેય નક્કી હશે તો મહેનત કરવી સહેલી લાગશે.
>
> *jo dhyey nakkī haśe to mahenat karvī sahelī lāgśe.*
>
> If the goal is clear, the hard work will feel easier.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધ્યેય** (*dhyey*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *dhyeya*, "to be meditated on, to be pondered" (Monier-Williams): what the mind is fixed on.
