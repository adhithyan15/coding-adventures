## You'll want to know: طحلب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C476-AQRAB476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**طحلب** — *ṭuḥlub* — "moss; algae".

> كان الطحلب على الصخور.
>
> *kāna aṭ-ṭuḥlub ʿalā aṣ-ṣukhūr.*
>
> There was moss on the rocks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طحلب** (*ṭuḥlub*) names moss and the green film on water; the origin is not settled.
