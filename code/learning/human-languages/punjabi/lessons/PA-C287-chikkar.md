## You'll want to know: ਚਿੱਕੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C287-WET287-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿੱਕੜ** — *chikkaṛ* — "mud".

> ਮੀਂਹ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਚਿੱਕੜ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ।
>
> *mīṁh ton bāad saṛak utte chikkaṛ hovegā.*
>
> After the rain there will be mud on the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿੱਕੜ** (*chikkaṛ*) goes back to Sanskrit *cikka*, "gummy matter"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cikkaṛ*, "mud", under it (CDIAL 4780).
