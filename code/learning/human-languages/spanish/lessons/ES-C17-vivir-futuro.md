# viviré — one future row for three verb families

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-PREDICTION-INTENTION, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-FUTURE-SINGULAR, ES-PATTERN-ER-FUTURE-SINGULAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **Hablaré** kept **-ar**. **Comeré** kept **-er**. Predict the yo
future of **vivir** before reading on. It is **viviré**.

## Grammar Lens: the singular -ir row
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-GRAMMAR-IR-FUTURE-SINGULAR]; assesses=[ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-PREDICTION-INTENTION] -->

| person | simple future |
|---|---|
| yo | vivir**é** |
| tú | vivir**ás** |
| él / ella / usted | vivir**á** |

Say **viviré · vivirás · vivirá**. Now the bounded pattern is complete:
**hablaré, comeré, viviré**. The same three endings attach to all three whole
infinitives. Plural persons wait.

## Grammar Lens: meaning with known words
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-IR-FUTURE-SINGULAR, ES-LEX-EN] -->

**Viviré en Madrid** says “I will live in Madrid.” Every lexical piece was
available before this lesson; only the future form is new.
