## You'll want to know: रद्दी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C519-DHOOL519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रद्दी** — *raddī* — "waste paper; scrap".

> हमने पुराने अख़बार रद्दी में बेच दिए।
>
> *Hamne purāne akhbār raddī meṁ bec diye.*
>
> We sold the old newspapers as waste paper.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रद्दी** (*raddī*) is usually traced to Persian *raddī*, "rejected, worthless". The Persian word is built on Arabic *radd*, "rejection".
