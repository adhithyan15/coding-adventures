Kannada, chapter 126: Lemon, Grapes, Jackfruit, Pepper, Cumin.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ನಿಂಬೆ (nimbe) — a lemon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for upma, then the Kannada for finger millet.

You'll want to know: ನಿಂಬೆ.
ನಿಂಬೆ — nimbe — "a lemon".
