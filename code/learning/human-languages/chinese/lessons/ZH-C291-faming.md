## You'll want to know: 发明
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C291-INVENT291-02]; assesses=[] -->

**发明** — *fāmíng* — "to invent; an invention".

> **他发明了一个小东西** *tā fāmíng le yí ge xiǎo dōngxi* — He invented a little gadget.

The new character, in pieces: 发 — five strokes in three small groups: a short stroke that drops and turns to the right, and a long sweep down to the left from the top; then a fork of two strokes crossing below; then a dot at the top right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**发明** (*fāmíng*) is built from 发 'to send out' and 明 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *mjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to start' and 'to make clear' and the second 'light' and 'clear': to bring something to light.
