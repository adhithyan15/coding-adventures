## You'll want to know: ਜੋਤਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C301-PLOUGH301-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੋਤਣਾ** — *jotṇā* — "to yoke; to harness".

> ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਬਲਦ ਜੋਤਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ savere balad jotdā hāṁ.*
>
> I yoke the oxen in the morning.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਲਦ ਜੋਤੇ, *maiṁ balad jote*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੋਤਣਾ** (*jotṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *yōktrayati*, "fastens, ties"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jotṇā*, "to yoke, plough", under it (CDIAL 10524).
