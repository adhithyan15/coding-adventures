## You'll want to know: ছাঁটা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C435-GHAS435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ছাঁটা** — *chhā̃ṭā* — "to trim; to prune; to cut back".

> মালি বাগানের গাছগুলো ছাঁটলেন।
>
> *māli bāgāner gāchhgulo chhā̃ṭlen.*
>
> The gardener trimmed the plants in the garden.

Past (he/she …): তিনি গাছ ছাঁটলেন, *tini gāchh chhā̃ṭlen*.

Also of hair and of staff: চুল ছাঁটা, to trim hair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছাঁটা** (*chhā̃ṭā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *chā̃ṭā*, "to lop", under the reconstructed *chāṇṭ*, "cut" (CDIAL 5013).
