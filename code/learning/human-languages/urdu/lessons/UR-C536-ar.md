## You'll want to know: آڑ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C536-MOHTAT536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آڑ** — *āṛ* — "cover (to shelter behind)".

> اگر بارش ہوئی تو ہم درخت کی آڑ لیں گے۔
>
> *agar bārish huī to ham darakht kī āṛ leṅ ge.*
>
> If it rains, we'll shelter under a tree.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آڑ** (*āṛ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *āṛ*, "an interruption, a covering", under *\*aḍḍ*, "obstruct", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 188).

## What you've built
