जूस — jūs — "juice".

The word, taken apart.
जूस (jūs) is a loan from English juice. It is a different word from Hindi jūs, "broth", from Sanskrit yūṣa (CDIAL 10521).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पहाड़ (pahāṛ) — a mountain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for lassi, then the Hindi for juice.

You'll want to know: पहाड़.
पहाड़ — pahāṛ — "a mountain".

The word, taken apart.
पहाड़ (pahāṛ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pāhāḍa, "rock, hill", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 8141). CDIAL connects it, probably, with Sanskrit pāṣāṇa, "stone".
