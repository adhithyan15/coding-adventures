# నావికుడు (nāvikuḍu) — a sailor

## You'll want to know: నావికుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C452-NAUKA452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నావికుడు** — *nāvikuḍu* — "a sailor".

> నా మామయ్య నావికుడిగా పనిచేశాడు.
>
> *nā māmayya nāvikuḍigā panicēśāḍu.*
>
> My uncle worked as a sailor.

నావ, a boat, + -ఇకుడు, one who works with it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నావికుడు** (*nāvikuḍu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nāvika*, "a sailor, a helmsman" (CDIAL 7082), from *nāvā*, "a boat".
