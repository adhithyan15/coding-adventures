Marathi, chapter 18: The Doorway Signs at Long Distance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
The doorway word, much later — distant doorway warm-up — meaning before signs.

Warm-up.
Close every earlier model and listen before picking up the pencil.

Wrap-up Recall.
Hear dhanyavād. Say its meaning, read धन्यवाद, then cover the model and write it once. This reconnects meaning and script without adding anything new.
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Hear dhanyavad; give its meaning and write it in Devanagari.


Lesson 2 of 5.
First batch at long distance — distant script retrieval — first batch.

Warm-up.
Keep every model closed and one blank line ready.

Wrap-up Recall.
From sound and spoken names only, write dhanyavād, visarga, independent ā, and bha. Compare once: धन्यवाद; ः आ भ.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Write dhanyavad, visarga, independent aa, and bha at long distance.
