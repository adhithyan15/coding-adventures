## You'll want to know: がいしゅつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C153-OUTING153-01]; assesses=[] -->

**がいしゅつ** — *gaishutsu* — "going out; being out of the office".

> **ちちは いま がいしゅつちゅうです。** — *chichi wa ima gaishutsuchū desu* — "My father is out right now."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がいしゅつ** (*gaishutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gai*, "outside" (Middle Chinese *ngwajH*) and *shutsu*, "go out" (*tsyhwit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
