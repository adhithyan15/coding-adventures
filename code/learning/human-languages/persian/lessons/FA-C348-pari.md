## You'll want to know: پری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C348-PARI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پری** (*pari*) — "a fairy, a peri".

> در قصه یک پری زیبا به پسر کمک کرد.
>
> *Dar qesse yek pari-ye zibâ be pesar komak kard.*
>
> In the tale a beautiful fairy helped the boy.

Also a girl's name. *Pari-ru* "fairy-faced", very beautiful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pari* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian *parīg*, a female spirit that was once feared as a witch and later became the lovely fairy of poetry.
