Hindi, chapter 220: A flag, Childhood, Youth, Old age, Life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
झंडा (jhaṇḍā) — a flag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a war, then the Hindi for an army.

You'll want to know: झंडा.
झंडा — jhaṇḍā — "a flag".

The word, taken apart.
झंडा (jhaṇḍā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhvajadaṇḍa, "flagstaff", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6898). It is built from dhvaja, "flag", and daṇḍa, "staff".
