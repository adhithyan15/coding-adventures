## You'll want to know: desaparecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C495-NOTICIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**desaparecer** — "to disappear".

> Desapareció un perro en el parque.
>
> *A dog disappeared in the park.*

It takes a *z* in the first person present: *desaparezco*.
