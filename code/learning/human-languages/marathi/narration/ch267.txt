Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for a licence, then the Marathi for a helmet.

You'll want to know: ज्वालामुखी.
ज्वालामुखी — jvālāmukhī — "a volcano".

The word, taken apart.
ज्वालामुखी (jvālāmukhī) is "flame-mouthed": Sanskrit jvālā, "flame", and mukha, "mouth", with the ending -ī.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for a helmet, then the Marathi for a volcano.

You'll want to know: पठार.
पठार — paṭhār — "a plateau".

The word, taken apart.
पठार (paṭhār) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pṛṣṭhadhāra, "plateau", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 8372). It is pṛṣṭha, "back, top", and dhārā, "edge".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a volcano, then the Marathi for a plateau.

You'll want to know: गोटा.
गोटा — goṭā — "a pebble".

The word, taken apart.
गोटा (goṭā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from goṭṭa, "something round", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4271). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "roundish stone". In the sense "fruit, kernel" it takes the word from Dravidian.

The word, taken apart.
चिखल (cikhal) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cikhalla, "mud, mire" (CDIAL 4784). CDIAL lists the Marathi word under the form cikkhalla-.
