Sanskrit, chapter 479: Diseases.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
व्याधिः (vyādhiḥ) — a disease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for suffering, then the Sanskrit for unbearable.

You'll want to know: व्याधिः.
व्याधिः — vyādhiḥ — "a disease".
वृद्धस्य व्याधिः चिरं तिष्ठति। vṛddhasya vyādhiḥ ciraṃ tiṣṭhati. The old man's disease lingers.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
व्याधिः (vyādhiḥ) is built from vi, ā and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW thinks a link with √vyadh, "to pierce", less likely, and gives "disorder, disease, ailment" (MW).

You'll want to know: सञ्चरति.
सञ्चरति — sañcarati — "goes about; spreads".
ग्रीष्मे ज्वरः ग्रामे सञ्चरति। grīṣme jvaraḥ grāme sañcarati. In summer fever goes about the village.
Past (it …): ज्वरः ग्रामे सञ्चरितवान्, jvaraḥ grāme sañcaritavān.

The word, taken apart.
सञ्चरति (sañcarati) is built from sam, "together", and the root √car, "to move", the root of चरति; MW gives "to go or wander about" (MW).
