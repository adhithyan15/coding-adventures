## You'll want to know: die Schlagzeile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C271-MELDUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schlagzeile** — "the headline".

> Die Schlagzeile war groß und rot.
>
> *The headline was big and red.*

Plural: *die Schlagzeilen*. *Schlagzeilen machen* — to hit the headlines.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schlagzeile* joins *Schlag* "a blow", from *schlagen* "to strike", and *Zeile* "a line"; both are inherited Germanic words. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
