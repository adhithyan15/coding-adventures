## You'll want to know: sennò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C373-PURCHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sennò** — "or else, otherwise (casual)".

> Mettiti la giacca, sennò hai freddo.
>
> *Put your jacket on, or else you will be cold.*

Spoken and informal; also written *se no*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sennò* is *se no* "if not" written as one word: *se* is inherited from Latin *sī* "if" and *no* from Latin *nōn* "not" (Lewis & Short).
