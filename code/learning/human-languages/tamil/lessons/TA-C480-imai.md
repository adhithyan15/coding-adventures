## You'll want to know: இமை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C480-MUKAM480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**இமை** — *imai* — "an eyelid".

> குழந்தையின் இமைகள் மெதுவாக மூடின.
>
> *kuḻantaiyiṉ imaikaḷ metuvāka mūṭiṉa.*
>
> The baby’s eyelids slowly closed.

Plural: இமைகள், *imaikaḷ*.

The same word is a verb, to wink.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இமை** (*imai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2545), both the eyelid and the verb "to wink".
