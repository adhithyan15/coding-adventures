## You'll want to know: भाग्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C369-BHAGYA369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भाग्यम्** — *bhāgyam* — "luck; fortune".

> यदि भाग्यम् अस्ति, तर्हि सर्वं सुखं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhāgyam asti, tarhi sarvaṃ sukhaṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If we have luck, all will be well.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाग्यम्** (*bhāgyam*) comes from the root √bhaj, "to share" (MW takes *bhāgya* as "to be shared"), and MW gives the neuter *bhāgya* "fate, destiny, fortune, good fortune" (MW).
