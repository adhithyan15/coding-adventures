## You'll want to know: शाप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C338-RAKSHAS338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शाप** — *śāp* — "a curse".

> शापामुळे राजकुमार बेडूक झाला.
>
> *śāpāmuḷe rājkumār beḍūk jhālā.*
>
> Because of a curse the prince became a frog.

Masculine.

शाप देणे, to curse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शाप** (*śāp*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śāpa*, 'a curse' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
