Arabic, chapter 354: After the blast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حطام (ḥuṭām) — wreckage, debris.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to break into, then the Arabic for to spread.

You'll want to know: حطام.
حطام — ḥuṭām — "wreckage, debris".
بحث الرجال في حطام السيارة. baḥatha ar-rijāl fī ḥuṭām as-sayyāra. The men searched the wreckage of the car.

The word, taken apart.
حطام (ḥuṭām) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-ṭ-m (Lane), "to break a dry thing in pieces"; ḥuṭām is what is left in pieces.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تسرب (tasarraba) — to leak, to seep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a spark, then the Arabic for a flame.

You'll want to know: تسرب.
تسرب — tasarraba — "to leak, to seep".
تسرب الماء من السقف. tasarraba al-māʾ min as-saqf. Water leaked through the ceiling.
Present: يتسرب, yatasarrab.
Of news: تسرب (tasarraba) الخبر, the news leaked out.

The word, taken apart.
تسرب (tasarraba) is built on the native Arabic root s-r-b (Lane), where sarab is a burrow or tunnel under the ground. It is Form V, "to pass in, little by little".
