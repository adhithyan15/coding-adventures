## You'll want to know: ಸಮಿತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C395-ADALITA395-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಿತಿ** — *samiti* — "a committee".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಒಂದು ಸಮಿತಿ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *habbada kelasakke ondu samiti māḍidaru.*
>
> They formed a committee for the festival work.

ಸಮಿತಿಯ ಅಧ್ಯಕ್ಷ, the chair of a committee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಿತಿ** (*samiti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samiti*, "a meeting, an association" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
