## You'll want to know: சுவர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C70-ROOM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சுவர்** (*suvar*) — "a wall".

The wall of a room or a house.

It is short and it is bare — two letters and a closing **ர்**, no vowel signs hanging off the
front of anything. Words shaped this plainly are usually old ones, and this is one of them.

It is also the surface that makes உள்ளே and வெளியே mean anything: a சுவர் is what there is an
inside and an outside of.
