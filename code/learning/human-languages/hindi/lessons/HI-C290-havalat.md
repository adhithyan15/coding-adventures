## You'll want to know: हवालात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C290-THANA290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हवालात** — *havālāt* — "a lock-up".

> पुलिस ने चोर को रात भर हवालात में रखा।
>
> *pulis ne cor ko rāt bhar havālāt meṁ rakhā.*
>
> The police kept the thief in the lock-up all night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥawāla*, "a handing over, custody": the room where a person handed over to the police is held. It is not **जेल**, prison: **हवालात** is for a night or two.
