## You'll want to know: protester
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C363-MANIFESTATION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**protester** — "to protest".

> Les voisins protestent contre le bruit.
>
> *The neighbours are protesting about the noise.*

*Protester contre quelque chose*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protester* comes from Latin *prōtestārī* "to declare publicly, to bear witness" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *testis* "witness".
