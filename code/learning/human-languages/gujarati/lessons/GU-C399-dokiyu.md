## You'll want to know: ડોકિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C399-DELAY399-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ડોકિયું** — *ḍokiyũ* — "a peep; a quick look".

> મેં બારીમાંથી બહાર ડોકિયું કર્યું.
>
> *mẽ bārīmā̃thī bahār ḍokiyũ karyũ.*
>
> I took a peep out of the window.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડોકિયું** (*ḍokiyũ*) is built on ડોક, the neck, which CDIAL lists under *\*ḍōka*, "head" (CDIAL 5566): you stretch your neck out to look.
