## You'll want to know: உதறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C490-NIMIR490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**உதறு** — *utaṟu* — "to shake off; to shake out".

> அவள் போர்வையை உதறி மடித்தாள்.
>
> *avaḷ pōrvaiyai utaṟi maṭittāḷ.*
>
> She shook out the blanket and folded it.

Past (I …): உதறினேன், *utaṟiṉēṉ*.

Also to brush aside: பயத்தை உதறினான், he shook off his fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உதறு** (*utaṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 613), of shaking out a cloth or casting off a tie.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
