## You'll want to know: टिकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C365-SIDDHANT365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**टिकना** — *ṭiknā* — "to hold up, to last".

> तुम्हारा तर्क ज़्यादा देर नहीं टिकेगा।
>
> *tumhārā tark zyādā der nahīṃ ṭikegā.*
>
> Your argument won't hold up for long.

Past (it …): वह तर्क नहीं टिका, *vah tark nahīṃ ṭikā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टिकना** (*ṭiknā*) is inherited from *\*ṭakk*, "stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5420).
