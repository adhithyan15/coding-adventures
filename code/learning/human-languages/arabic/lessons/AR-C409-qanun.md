## You'll want to know: قانون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C409-QANUN409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قانون** — *qānūn* — "a law".

> يمنع القانون التدخين في المطاعم.
>
> *yamnaʿu al-qānūn at-tadkhīn fī al-maṭāʿim.*
>
> The law bans smoking in restaurants.

Plural: قوانين, *qawānīn*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قانون** (*qānūn*) was borrowed from Greek *kanōn*, "a measuring rod, a rule" (EtymDB). Arabic gave it the sense of a law.
