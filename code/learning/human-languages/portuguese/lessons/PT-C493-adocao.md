## You'll want to know: a adoção
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C493-GERACOES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a adoção** — "adoption".

> A adoção demorou quase dois anos.
>
> *The adoption took almost two years.*

Before AO90 spelled *adopção* in Portugal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adoção* comes from Latin *adoptiō* "adoption" (EtymDB), from *adoptāre*.
