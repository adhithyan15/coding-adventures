## You'll want to know: o terramoto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C302-SISMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o terramoto** — "the earthquake".

> Em 1755, um terramoto destruiu a cidade.
>
> *In 1755 an earthquake destroyed the city.*

In Brazil: *terremoto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terramoto* goes back to Latin *terrae mōtus* "a movement of the earth", from *terra* "earth" and *movēre* "to move" (Lewis & Short).
