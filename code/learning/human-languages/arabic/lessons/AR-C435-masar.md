## You'll want to know: مسار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C435-MASAR435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مسار** — *masār* — "a route; a lane".

> غيرت الحافلة مسارها بسبب الثلج.
>
> *ghayyarat al-ḥāfila masārahā bi-sabab ath-thalj.*
>
> The bus changed its route because of the snow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسار** (*masār*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-y-r*, "to go, to travel" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿal* makes it the place of going.
