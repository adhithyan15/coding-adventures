## You'll want to know: けんどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C461-SUMO461-02]; assesses=[] -->

**けんどう** — *kendō* — "kendo".

> **おとうとは けんどうを ならっています。** — *otōto wa kendō o naratte imasu* — "My younger brother is learning kendo."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんどう** (*kendō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "sword" (Middle Chinese *kjaemH*) and *dō*, "way" (*dawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
