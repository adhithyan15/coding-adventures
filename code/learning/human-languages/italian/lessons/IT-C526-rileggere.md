## You'll want to know: rileggere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C526-RECENSIONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rileggere** — "to reread".

> Rileggo spesso i miei libri preferiti.
>
> *I often reread my favourite books.*

Like *leggere*: past participle *riletto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rileggere* is built from *ri-* "again" and *leggere* "to read", inherited from Latin *legere* "to gather, to read" (Lewis & Short).
