## You'll want to know: समझावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C198-OFFICE198-05]; assesses=[] -->

**समझावणो** — *samjhāvṇo* — "to explain".

> अफसर नियम समझावै है।
>
> *aphsar niyam samjhāvai hai.*
>
> The officer explains the rule.

Past (I …): म्हैं बात समझाई, *mhaĩ bāt samjhāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समझावणो** (*samjhāvṇo*) is the causative of **समझणो**, "to understand": to make someone understand. Both are inherited from Sanskrit *sambudhyate*, "wakes up, understands"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *samajhāi*, "persuades" (CDIAL 12959).
