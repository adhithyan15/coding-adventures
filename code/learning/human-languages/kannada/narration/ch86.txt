Kannada, chapter 86: One Minute, Please.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ (ondu nimiṣa) — one minute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for meaning, then the Kannada for please write it.

You'll want to know: ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ.
ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ (ondu nimiṣa) — "one minute". ಒಂದು is "one", from the numbers chapter; ನಿಮಿಷ (nimiṣa) is "minute".
Say it with a raised hand when you need a moment.

What you've built.
A number you know and a new word for time.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ondu nimiṣa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ondu nimiṣa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ondu nimiṣa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for meaning, then the Kannada for please write it, then say ondu nimiṣa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ (ondu nimiṣa) mean? ("One minute".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕಾಯಿರಿ (kāyiri) — please wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for please write it, then the Kannada for one minute.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಯಿರಿ.
ಕಾಯಿರಿ (kāyiri) — "please wait", the polite form of ಕಾಯು (kāyu), "to wait".
ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ ಕಾಯಿರಿ (ondu nimiṣa kāyiri) — "please wait a minute".

What you've built.
The polite ending ‑ಇರಿ asks gently.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ತೋರಿಸಿ (tōrisi) — please show me.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for one minute, then the Kannada for please wait.

You'll want to know: ತೋರಿಸಿ.
ತೋರಿಸಿ (tōrisi) — "please show (me)".
ದಾರಿ ತೋರಿಸಿ (dāri tōrisi) — "please show me the way", with ದಾರಿ, "way, road".

What you've built.
When words fail, ask to be shown.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ (kēḷisalilla) — I didn't hear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for please wait, then the Kannada for please show.

You'll want to know: ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ.
ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ (kēḷisalilla) — "(it) was not heard", the way to say "I didn't hear". It is built on ಕೇಳು, "hear, listen", with ‑ಇಲ್ಲ, "not".

What you've built.
A known verb with a known no.
