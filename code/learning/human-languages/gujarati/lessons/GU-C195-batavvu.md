## You'll want to know: બતાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C195-LOST195-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બતાવવું** — *batāvvũ* — "to show".

> **પોલીસ મને રસ્તો બતાવે છે.** — *polīs mane rasto batāve che* — "A policeman shows me the way."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **બતાવવું** (*batāvvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form, and EtymDB lists no source.
