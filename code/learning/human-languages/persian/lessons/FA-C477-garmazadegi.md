## You'll want to know: گرمازدگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C477-SHARJI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گرمازدگی** (*garmâzadegi*) — "heatstroke".

> کارگر در آفتاب گرمازدگی گرفت.
>
> *Kârgar dar âftâb garmâzadegi gereft.*
>
> The worker got heatstroke in the sun.

*garmâ* 'heat' + *zade* 'struck' + *-gi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garmâzadegi* is a native compound of *garmâ* 'heat', from *garm* 'warm', *zade* 'struck', from *zadan* 'to strike', and the noun ending *-gi*: being struck by the heat.
