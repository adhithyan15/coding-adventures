## You'll want to know: वधः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C334-RAKSHASA334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वधः** — *vadhaḥ* — "the slaying; a killing".

> वीरः राक्षसस्य वधं करोति।
>
> *vīraḥ rākṣasasya vadhaṃ karoti.*
>
> The hero slays the demon.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वधः** (*vadhaḥ*) MW gives *vadha* as one who kills, a deadly weapon, and the act of striking or killing, without a root in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
