## You'll want to know: દસ્તાવેજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C350-KARAR350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દસ્તાવેજ** — *dastāvej* — "a document".

> મિલકતના બધા દસ્તાવેજ સાચવીને રાખો.
>
> *milkatnā badhā dastāvej sācvīne rākho.*
>
> Keep all the property documents safe.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દસ્તાવેજ** (*dastāvej*) is usually traced to Persian *dastāvez*, "a document, a deed".
