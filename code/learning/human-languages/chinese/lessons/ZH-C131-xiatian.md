## You'll want to know: xiàtiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C131-SEASON131-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàtiān* — "summer".

> *Běijīng de xiàtiān hěn rè* — Summers in Beijing are hot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàtiān* is built from *xià* 'summer' and 天 'sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeH* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the first under the words 'spring and summer'.
