## You'll want to know: उद्देश्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C345-UDDESHYA345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्देश्य** — *uddeśya* — "a purpose, an aim".

> हमारी बैठक का उद्देश्य क्या है?
>
> *hamārī baiṭhak kā uddeśya kyā hai?*
>
> What is the purpose of our meeting?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्देश्य** (*uddeśya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *uddeśya*, "that which one has in view" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
