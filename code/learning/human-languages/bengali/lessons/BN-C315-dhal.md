## You'll want to know: ঢাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C315-OSTRO315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢাল** — *ḍhāl* — "a shield".

> সৈন্যরা ঢাল আর তলোয়ার নিয়ে এগোল।
>
> *soinyôrā ḍhāl ār tôloyār niye egolô.*
>
> The soldiers advanced with shields and swords.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢাল** (*ḍhāl*) comes down from Sanskrit *ḍhāla*, 'a shield' (CDIAL 5583, which lists Bengali *ḍhāl*). Another ঢাল, a slope, is a different word.
