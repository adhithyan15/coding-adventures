## You'll want to know: la contravvenzione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C412-VIGILE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la contravvenzione** — "the fine, the traffic ticket".

> Il vigile mi ha fatto una contravvenzione.
>
> *The warden gave me a ticket.*

Formal; in speech people say *la multa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contravvenzione* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *contrāventiō*, built on *contrā* 'against' and *venīre* 'to come': coming against a rule.
