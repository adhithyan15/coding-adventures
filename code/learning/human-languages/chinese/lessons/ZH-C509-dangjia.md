## You'll want to know: 当家
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C509-PARENTS509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**当家** — *dāngjiā* — "to run the household".

> **我们家是我妈妈当家** *wǒmen jiā shì wǒ māma dāngjiā* — In our family, it's my mum who runs things.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**当家** (*dāngjiā*) is built from 当 'to be in charge' and 家 'a home', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tang* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be in charge' and the second 'a dwelling'.
