## You'll want to know: gēnjù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C350-FACTS350-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gēnjù* — "basis; what something rests on".

> *yīnwèi méiyǒu gēnjù suǒyǐ wǒ bù xiāngxìn* — There's nothing to back it up, so I don't believe it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gēnjù* is built from *gēn* and *jù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kon* and *kjoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a root' and the second 'to rely on'.
