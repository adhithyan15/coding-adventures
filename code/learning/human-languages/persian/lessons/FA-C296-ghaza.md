# غذا (ghazâ) — food, and how it was

## You'll want to know: غذا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C296-GHAZA-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C73-TOWN73-02, FA-GRAMMAR-EZAFE-OWNER, FA-LEX-C82-TIME82-03, FA-LEX-C35-VERBS35-01] -->

**غذا** — *ghazâ* — "food", and "a meal".

> **غذای این رستوران خیلی خوشمزه بود.** — *ghazâ-ye in resturân kheyli khoshmaze
> bud* — The food at this restaurant was very tasty.

After its long *â* the ezafe is *-ye*, and here it is written, as **ی**:
**غذای**. *Ghazâ khordan* is "to eat, to have a meal": *ghazâ khordim*, we
ate.

Say *gha-zâ*, with the *gh* far back, like a soft gargle. Its first letter
is new, and the next lesson puts it in your hand. The second is **ذ**, which
you wrote in chapter two hundred and ninety.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C290-ZAL-01] -->

*ghazâ* is borrowed from Arabic *ghidhāʾ*, "nourishment, food" (EtymDB
2.1). Arabic says its middle letter as the *th* of English *this*. Persian
kept the letter **ذ** in the spelling and says it *z*, which is why *ghazâ*
sounds nothing like the Arabic it is spelled from.
