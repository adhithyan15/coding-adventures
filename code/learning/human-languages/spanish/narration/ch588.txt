Spanish, chapter 588: Ordenar las ideas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
por tanto — therefore, so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the certainty, then the Spanish for apparently.

You'll want to know: por tanto.
por tanto — "therefore, so".
No tengo dinero; por tanto, no voy. I have no money; therefore, I am not going.
Means the same as por lo tanto and así que, a little more formal.

The word, taken apart.
por tanto is built on tanto, inherited from Latin tantus 'so great, so much' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
o sea — I mean, that is.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for apparently, then the Spanish for therefore.

You'll want to know: o sea.
o sea — "I mean, that is".
Llego el lunes, o sea, dentro de tres días. I arrive on Monday, that is, in three days.
Very common in speech to explain or correct what you said.

The word, taken apart.
o sea 'or let it be' is built on o, inherited from Latin aut 'or' (EtymDB), and sea, the subjunctive of ser, usually traced to Latin sedeat, from sedere 'to sit'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
en cambio — whereas, by contrast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for therefore, then the Spanish for I mean.

You'll want to know: en cambio.
en cambio — "whereas, by contrast".
Yo trabajo; mi hermano, en cambio, estudia. I work; my brother, by contrast, studies.
It shows a contrast between two people or things.

The word, taken apart.
en cambio is built on cambio, from cambiar, which is inherited from Late Latin cambiare 'to exchange', a word of Gaulish origin (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for I mean, then the Spanish for whereas.

You'll want to know: sobre todo.
sobre todo — "above all, especially".
Me gusta, sobre todo, el precio. I like, above all, the price.
Written as two words; el sobretodo is an overcoat.

The word, taken apart.
sobre todo is built on sobre, inherited from Latin super 'over', and todo, from totus 'whole' (EtymDB).
