Urdu, chapter 429: Damp and Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نمی (namī) — moisture; damp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for steam, then the Urdu for to gather (of clouds).

You'll want to know: نمی.
نمی — namī — "moisture; damp".
بارش کے بعد دیوار میں نمی ہے۔ bārish ke bād dīvār meṅ namī hai. After the rain the wall is damp.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
نمی (namī) is built on nam, "damp, moist", usually traced to Persian nam, with the ending -ī.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ٹھنڈ (ṭhanḍ) — a chill; the cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to gather (of clouds), then the Urdu for moisture.

You'll want to know: ٹھنڈ.
ٹھنڈ — ṭhanḍ — "a chill; the cold".
صبح کی ٹھنڈ میں ہم نے چائے پی۔ subah kī ṭhanḍ meṅ ham ne chāe pī. We drank tea in the morning chill.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھنڈ (ṭhanḍ) is inherited: it belongs with Hindi ṭhaṇḍā, "cold", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit stabdha, "stiff, numb" (CDIAL 13676).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: سکڑنا.
سکڑنا — sikuṛnā — "to shrink; to huddle up".
سردی میں بچے کمبل میں سکڑ گئے۔ sardī meṅ bachche kambal meṅ sikuṛ gae. In the cold the children huddled up under the blanket.
Past (it …): وہ کرتا سکڑ گیا, voh kurtā sikuṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
سکڑنا (sikuṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins sãkaṛnā and sikaṛnā, "to be contracted", under Sanskrit saṃkuṭati, "bends oneself together" (CDIAL 12825).

Lesson 5 of 5.
اکڑنا (akaṛnā) — to go stiff; to go numb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the cold season, then the Urdu for to shrink.

You'll want to know: اکڑنا.
اکڑنا — akaṛnā — "to go stiff; to go numb".
ٹھنڈ سے میری انگلیاں اکڑ گئیں۔ ṭhanḍ se merī ungliyāṅ akaṛ gaīṅ. My fingers went stiff with the cold.
Past (he …): وہ اکڑ گیا, voh akaṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
اکڑنا (akaṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin akaṛnā, "to be numbed", under ākkaḍa, "cramped, stiff", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1013).
