## You'll want to know: कळ येणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C482-DUKH482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कळ येणे** — *kaḷ yeṇe* — "to get a twinge".

> जिना चढताना माझ्या गुडघ्यात कळ आली.
>
> *jinā caḍhtānā mājhyā guḍghyāt kaḷ ālī.*
>
> I felt a twinge in my knee climbing the stairs.

Past (to him …): त्याला कळ आली, *tyālā kaḷ ālī*.

कळ, a sharp twinge: पोटात कळ येणे, a cramp in the stomach. Dative: मला कळ आली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कळ येणे** (*kaḷ yeṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on कळ, "a sharp twinge", whose origin is not settled, with येणे, "to come".

## What you've built
