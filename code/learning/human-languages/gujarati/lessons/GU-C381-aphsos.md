## You'll want to know: અફસોસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C381-PASTAVO381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અફસોસ** — *aphsos* — "a pity; regret".

> જો હું ત્યાં હોત તો તમને મદદ કરત; મને તેનો અફસોસ છે.
>
> *jo hũ tyā̃ hot to tamne madad karat; mane teno aphsos che.*
>
> If I had been there I would have helped you; I am sorry for that.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અફસોસ** (*aphsos*) is usually traced to Persian *afsōs*, "regret, alas".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
