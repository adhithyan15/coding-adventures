Malayalam, chapter 137: Getting There.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വണ്ടി (vaṇṭi) — a vehicle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a well, then the Malayalam for a harbour.

You'll want to know: വണ്ടി.
വണ്ടി (vaṇṭi) — "a vehicle", any cart, car or bus.

The word, taken apart.
വണ്ടി (vaṇṭi) is listed in DEDR appendix 50, among Dravidian words taken from Indo-Aryan, with Tamil vaṇṭi and Kannada baṇḍi, "cart". DEDR traces the word ultimately to Sanskrit bhāṇḍa, "goods, wares" (CDIAL 9440): the cart was named for what it carried.

What you've built.
The general word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
തീവണ്ടി (tīvaṇṭi) — a train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a vehicle, then the Malayalam for a car.

You'll want to know: തീവണ്ടി.
തീവണ്ടി (tīvaṇṭi) — "a train", literally "fire-vehicle": തീ (tī), "fire", with വണ്ടി (vaṇṭi).

The word, taken apart.
തീവണ്ടി (tīvaṇṭi) is "the fire-cart": native tī, "fire" (DEDR 3266, with Tamil tī), joined to vaṇṭi, "cart" (DEDR appendix 50, with Tamil vaṇṭi).

What you've built.
A vehicle word you can take apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
വിമാനം (vimānaṁ) — a plane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a car, then the Malayalam for a train.

You'll want to know: വിമാനം.
വിമാനം (vimānaṁ) — "an aeroplane".

The word, taken apart.
വിമാനം (vimānaṁ) from Sanskrit vimāna, "measure; a car of the gods", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 11823). The flying chariot of the old stories gave its name to the aeroplane.

What you've built.
The way to Kerala from far away.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ഓട്ടോ (ōṭṭō) — an autorickshaw.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ഓട്ടോ.
ഓട്ടോ (ōṭṭō) — "an autorickshaw", the three-wheeled taxi.

The word, taken apart.
ഓട്ടോ (ōṭṭō) from English auto, short for autorickshaw; auto- is Greek for "self", and rickshaw is from Japanese jinrikisha, "man-power vehicle".
