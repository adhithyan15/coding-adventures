Mandarin Chinese, chapter 369: Making It Clear.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
说明 (shuōmíng) — to make something clear; to explain clearly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to encourage, then the Mandarin for to make a choice.

You'll want to know: 说明.
说明 — shuōmíng — "to make something clear; to explain clearly".
你必须说明为什么不能准时来 nǐ bìxū shuōmíng wèishénme bù néng zhǔnshí lái — You have to explain why you couldn't be on time.

The word, taken apart.
说明 (shuōmíng) is built from 说 'to say' and 明 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sywet and mjaeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tell' and the second 'bright'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
但是 (dànshì) — but; however.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to make a choice, then the Mandarin for to make something clear.

You'll want to know: 但是.
但是 — dànshì — "but; however".
我觉得他很好但是我不喜欢他 wǒ juéde tā hěn hǎo dànshì wǒ bù xǐhuan tā — I think he's nice, but I don't like him.
The new character, in pieces: 但 (dàn) — seven strokes in two pieces: the person piece on the left, two strokes; on the right 日 rì, four strokes, over a long bar, five strokes in all. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
但是 (dànshì) is built from dàn and 是, both inherited from Middle Chinese, danX and dzyeX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a particle and 'only', and the second 'right, as against wrong'. How the pair came to mean but is not settled.

Script — the person piece, then the sun over a line.
但 (dàn).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, 亻, two strokes, as in 你: a left-falling stroke, then a vertical.
The right piece, five strokes: 日, four strokes, a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, a short horizontal inside and the bottom one; then 一, a long horizontal under it, with a little space between.
2 + 5 = seven.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]

[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 但 (dàn)? (Seven.) What sits on the right? (日 over 一.) Which piece comes first? (亻, the person piece.)

jǔlì — "to give an example".

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Lesson 6 of 6.
lìngwài — besides; in addition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to give an example, then the Mandarin for secondly.

You'll want to know: lìngwài.
lìngwài — "besides; in addition".
zhège tài guì le lìngwài wǒ yě bù xǐhuan — This one's too expensive, and besides, I don't like it.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
lìngwài is built from lìng 'separate' and 外 'outside'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese ngwajH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the outside' and 'far'. No dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
