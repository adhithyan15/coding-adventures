Telugu, chapter 130: Television, Computer, Phone, Ticket, Paisa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
టీవీ (ṭīvī) — a television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a cupboard, then the Telugu for a table.

You'll want to know: టీవీ.
టీవీ — ṭīvī — "a television".
