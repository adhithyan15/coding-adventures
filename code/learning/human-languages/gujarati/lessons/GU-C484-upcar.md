## You'll want to know: ઉપચાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C484-ILAJ484-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપચાર** — *upcār* — "a remedy".

> દાદી શરદી માટે આદુનો ઉપચાર કરે છે.
>
> *dādī śardī māṭe āduno upcār kare che.*
>
> Grandmother treats a cold with ginger.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપચાર** (*upcār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *upacāra*, "approach, service, attendance", as Monier-Williams records it; the medical sense grew from attending on the sick.
