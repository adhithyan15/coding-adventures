## You'll want to know: yùyuē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C212-SERVICE212-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yùyuē* — "to make an appointment; to book".

> *kěyǐ yùyuē ma* — Can I make an appointment?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùyuē* is built from *yù* 'beforehand' and *yuē* 'to bind, to agree', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yoH* and *'jak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'before' and the second 'to tie up'.
