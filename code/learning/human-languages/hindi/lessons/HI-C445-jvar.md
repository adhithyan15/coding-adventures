## You'll want to know: ज्वार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C445-KISHTI445-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वार** — *jvār* — "the high tide, a flood tide".

> शाम को ज्वार आता है और पानी ऊपर चढ़ता है।
>
> *śām ko jvār ātā hai aur pānī ūpar caṛhtā hai.*
>
> In the evening the tide comes in and the water rises.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वार** (*jvār*) goes back to *\*javacāra*, "a flood tide", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5167), which lists Hindi *jvār*. A different word with the same spelling names the millet sorghum.
