## You'll want to know: سنوارنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C382-INAM382-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سنوارنا** — *sanvārnā* — "to tidy; to make (something) look its best".

> اگر تم نے کمرہ سنوارا تو امی خوش ہوں گی۔
>
> *agar tum ne kamrā sanvārā to ammī khush hoṅ gī.*
>
> If you tidy the room, your mother will be pleased.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بال سنوارے, *us ne bāl sanvāre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سنوارنا** (*sanvārnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sãwārnā*, "to arrange, to deck", under Sanskrit *saṃvārayati* (CDIAL 13021).
