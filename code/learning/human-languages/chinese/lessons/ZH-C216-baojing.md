## You'll want to know: bàojǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C216-WRONG216-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bàojǐng* — "to call the police".

> *wǒmen yào bàojǐng* — We're going to call the police.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàojǐng* is built from *bào* 'to report' and *jǐng* 'to warn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawH* and *kjaengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
