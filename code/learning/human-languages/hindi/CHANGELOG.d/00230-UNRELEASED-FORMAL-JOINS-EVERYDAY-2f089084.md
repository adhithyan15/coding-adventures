## Unreleased — formal आयु joins everyday उम्र

Chapter 19 already defines **आयु** as the formal or literary Sanskrit-derived
alternative to everyday Perso-Arabic **उम्र**, asks learners to contrast the two
registers, and assesses the alternative in wrap-up recall. The headword now
declares **उम्र / आयु**, exposing both taught choices to reports and generated
study artifacts.
