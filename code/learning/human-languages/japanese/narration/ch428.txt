Japanese, chapter 428: Excited and Moved.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
わくわくする (wakuwaku suru) — to be thrilled; to be excited.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to get irritated, then the Japanese for to hesitate.

You'll want to know: わくわくする.
わくわくする — wakuwaku suru — "to be thrilled; to be excited".
あしたの りょこうに わくわくしています。 — ashita no ryokō ni wakuwaku shite imasu — "I am thrilled about tomorrow's trip."
Polite わくわくします, wakuwakushimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): こどもが わくわくしました。, kodomo ga wakuwaku shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
わくわくする (wakuwaku suru) is a native Japanese mimetic word, wakuwaku, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with suru, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.
こうふんする (kōfun suru) — to get worked up; to get excited.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to hesitate, then the Japanese for to be thrilled.

You'll want to know: こうふんする.
こうふんする — kōfun suru — "to get worked up; to get excited".
しあいを みて、みんな こうふんしました。 — shiai o mite, minna kōfun shimashita — "Everyone got worked up at the match."
Polite こうふんします, kōfunshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): おとうとが こうふんしました。, otōto ga kōfun shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
こうふんする (kōfun suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) kōfun with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kō, "revive" (Middle Chinese xing) and fun, "stirred up" (pjunH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
