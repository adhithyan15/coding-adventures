Persian, chapter 499: Sleep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بیخوابی (bikhâbi) — insomnia, sleeplessness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a vitamin, then the Persian for being overweight.

You'll want to know: بیخوابی.
بیخوابی (bikhâbi) — "insomnia, sleeplessness".
او از بیخوابی رنج میبرد. u az bikhâbi ranj mibarad. She suffers from insomnia.
bi- 'without' + khâb 'sleep' + -i. az chizi ranj bordan — to suffer from something.

The word, taken apart.
bikhâbi is built on native Persian parts: bi-, 'without', khâb, 'sleep', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian and on to PIE swep-, 'to sleep' (EtymDB), and the ending -i.

Lesson 2 of 5.
خروپف کردن (khoropof kardan) — to snore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for being overweight, then the Persian for insomnia.

You'll want to know: خروپف کردن.
خروپف کردن (khoropof kardan) — "to snore".
پدربزرگ شبها بلند خروپف میکند. pedarbozorg shabhâ boland khoropof mikonad. Grandfather snores loudly at night.
khoropof copies the sound: khor breathing in, pof breathing out. + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
khoropof is usually explained as an imitative word built on the sound of snoring, khor and pof joined by o, 'and'. kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

You'll want to know: خوابالود.
خوابالود (khâbâlud) — "sleepy, drowsy".
او صبحها خوابالود است. u sobhhâ khâbâlud ast. He's sleepy in the mornings.
khâb 'sleep' + -âlud 'smeared with', the same piece as in âlude 'polluted'.

The word, taken apart.
khâbâlud is built on native Persian khâb, 'sleep', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and -âlud, 'smeared with', the piece behind âlude, 'polluted'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خواب ماندن (khâb mândan) — to oversleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a nightmare, then the Persian for sleepy.

You'll want to know: خواب ماندن.
خواب ماندن (khâb mândan) — "to oversleep".
امروز خواب ماندم و دیر به کلاس رسیدم. emruz khâb mândam va dir be kelâs residam. Today I overslept and got to class late.
khâb 'sleep' + mândan 'to stay'; present stem mân-: to stay asleep.

The word, taken apart.
khâb mândan is built on two inherited words: khâb, 'sleep', and mândan, 'to stay', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
