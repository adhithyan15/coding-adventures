## You'll want to know: le givre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C463-FROID-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le givre** — "the frost (on surfaces), the hoarfrost".

> Le matin, il y a du givre sur le pare-brise.
>
> *In the morning there is frost on the windscreen.*

White frost on windows and grass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *givre* is not settled; it may come from a language spoken in the region before Latin. EtymDB lists it without a source.
