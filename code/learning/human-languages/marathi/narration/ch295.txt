Marathi, chapter 295: After the Disaster.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
नुकसान (nuksān) — damage; a loss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a volunteer, then the Marathi for to get stuck.

You'll want to know: नुकसान.
नुकसान — nuksān — "damage; a loss".
जर नुकसान मोठं असेल, तर गावाला मदत मिळेल. jar nuksān moṭhaṁ asel, tar gāvālā madat miḷel. If the damage is great, the village will get help.
Neuter.
नुकसान (nuksān) होणे, a loss is suffered; जर … असेल तर, if it is, for a state.

The word, taken apart.
नुकसान (nuksān) is a loan from Persian nuqsān, usually traced to Arabic nuqṣān, 'a shortfall, a deficit'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
दुर्घटना (durghaṭnā) — a disaster; a terrible accident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get stuck, then the Marathi for damage.

You'll want to know: दुर्घटना.
दुर्घटना — durghaṭnā — "a disaster; a terrible accident".
त्या दुर्घटनेत अनेक घरं वाहून गेली. tyā durghaṭnet anek gharaṁ vāhūn gelī. Many houses were swept away in that disaster.
Feminine.
Built from दुर्-, ill, and घटना, an event.

The word, taken apart.
दुर्घटना (durghaṭnā) is a learned compound built from the Sanskrit prefix dur-, 'bad, ill', and ghaṭanā, 'a happening' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 6.
आपत्ती (āpattī) — a calamity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for damage, then the Marathi for a disaster.

You'll want to know: आपत्ती.
आपत्ती — āpattī — "a calamity".
अशा आपत्तीत सगळे एकमेकांना मदत करतात. aśā āpattīt sagḷe ekmekānnā madat kartāt. In such a calamity everyone helps one another.
Feminine.
नैसर्गिक आपत्ती (āpattī), a natural calamity.

The word, taken apart.
आपत्ती (āpattī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit āpatti, 'a happening, an occurrence' (Monier-Williams); Marathi keeps it for the happenings that bring ruin.

संकट (saṅkaṭ) येणे, a crisis comes; संकटात, in trouble.

संकट (saṅkaṭ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit saṅkaṭa, 'narrow; a strait, a difficulty' (Monier-Williams).
