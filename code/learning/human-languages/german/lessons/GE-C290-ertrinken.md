## You'll want to know: ertrinken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C290-BEWUSSTLOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ertrinken** — "to drown".

> Ein Mann holte das Kind aus dem Wasser, bevor es ertrank.
>
> *A man pulled the child out of the water before it drowned.*

Strong, with *sein*: *ertrank*, *ist ertrunken*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ertrinken* is built on *trinken* "to drink", from Proto-Germanic *drinkaną* (EtymDB), with *er-*: to drink until the end.
