## You'll want to know: tōngxìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C319-CALL319-04]; assesses=[] -->

*tōngxìn* — "to write to each other; to correspond".

> *wǒ hé wǒ de bǐyǒu tōngxìn le wǔ nián* — My pen pal and I wrote to each other for five years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tōngxìn* is built from *tōng* 'to get through' and 信 'a letter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thuwng* and *sinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to reach' and the second 'true'.
