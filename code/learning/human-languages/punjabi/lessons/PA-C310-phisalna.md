# ਫਿਸਲਣਾ (phisalṇā) — to slip

## You'll want to know: ਫਿਸਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C310-SLIP310-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫਿਸਲਣਾ** — *phisalṇā* — "to slip".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਮਨ ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਫਿਸਲਿਆ।
>
> *kall mīṁh vich Aman saṛak utte phisliā.*
>
> Yesterday Aman slipped on the road in the rain.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪੌੜੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਫਿਸਲਿਆ, *maiṁ pauṛī utte phisliā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫਿਸਲਣਾ** (*phisalṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*phiss*, "slip"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *phisalṇā*, "to slip", under it (CDIAL 9080).

One more word for saying what happened.
