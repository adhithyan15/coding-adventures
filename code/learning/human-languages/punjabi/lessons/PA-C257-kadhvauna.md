# ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ (kaḍhvāuṇā) — to withdraw; to have something taken out

## You'll want to know: ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C257-BANK257-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ** — *kaḍhvāuṇā* — "to withdraw; to have something taken out".

> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਬੈਂਕ ਤੋਂ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਢਵਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ har mahīne baiṅk ton paise kaḍhvāundā hāṁ.*
>
> I withdraw money from the bank every month.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੈਂਕ ਤੋਂ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਢਵਾਏ, *maiṁ baiṅk ton paise kaḍhvāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਢਵਾਉਣਾ** (*kaḍhvāuṇā*) is the causative of *kaḍḍhṇā*, "to pull out", inherited from a reconstructed *\*kaḍḍhati*, "pulls, draws"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaḍḍhṇā* under it (CDIAL 2660).
