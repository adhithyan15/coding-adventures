# ਕੁੜਮ (kuṛam) — a child's father-in-law

## You'll want to know: ਕੁੜਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C329-INLAW329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੁੜਮ** — *kuṛam* — "a child's father-in-law".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਕੁੜਮ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਆਏ।
>
> *sāḍe kuṛam kall sāḍe ghar āe.*
>
> Our daughter's father-in-law came to our house yesterday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੁੜਮ** (*kuṛam*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kuṭumbin*, "a householder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kuṛam*, "a relation by marriage, child's father-in-law", under it (CDIAL 3235).
