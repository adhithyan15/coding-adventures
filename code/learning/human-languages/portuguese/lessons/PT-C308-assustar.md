## You'll want to know: assustar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C308-SUSTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**assustar** — "to frighten, to scare".

> O barulho assustou as crianças.
>
> *The noise frightened the children.*

*Assustar-se* — to get a fright.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assustar* is built on *susto* "a fright"; EtymDB lists it beside Spanish *asustar*, and the origin is not settled.
