Lesson 1 of 5.
сериал (seriál) — a TV series.

You'll want to know: сериал.
сериал (seriál) — "a TV series".
Мы смотрим этот сериал уже год. My smótrim étot seriál uzhé god. We've been watching this series for a year.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
сериал (seriál) is usually traced to English serial; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
эпизод (epizód) — an episode.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to reread, then the Russian for a TV series.

You'll want to know: эпизод.
эпизод (epizód) — "an episode".
Я посмотрел три эпизода подряд. Ya posmotrél tri epizóda podryád. I watched three episodes in a row.
Masculine. Also a short scene or incident.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists эпизод (epizód) without a source; it is usually traced through French épisode to Greek epeisódion 'an added scene'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
субтитры (subtítry) — subtitles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a TV series, then the Russian for an episode.

You'll want to know: субтитры.
субтитры (subtítry) — "subtitles".
Я смотрю фильмы с субтитрами. Ya smotryú fílmy s subtítrami. I watch films with subtitles.
Plural.

The word, taken apart.
субтитры (subtítry) is built on суб- 'under' and титр 'a caption', usually traced through French titre to Latin titulus 'a label'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
переключать (pereklyuchát) — to switch (channels).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an episode, then the Russian for subtitles.

You'll want to know: переключать.
переключать (pereklyuchát) — "to switch (channels)".
Папа всё время переключает каналы. Pápa vsyo vrémya pereklyucháyet kanály. Dad keeps switching channels.
Imperfective; perfective переключить. Переключать на другой канал.

The word, taken apart.
переключать (pereklyuchát) is built on пере- and the root of ключ 'a key, a switch'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
экранизация (ekranizátsiya) is built on экран 'a screen', which was borrowed from French écran (EtymDB); EtymDB has no entry for экранизация (ekranizátsiya).
