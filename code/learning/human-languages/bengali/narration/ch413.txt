Bengali, chapter 413: Drains and Rubbish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নর্দমা (nôrdômā) — a drain; a gutter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a lamp post, then the Bengali for to climb over.

You'll want to know: নর্দমা.
নর্দমা — nôrdômā — "a drain; a gutter".
আমাদের গলির নর্দমা খোলা। āmāder gôlir nôrdômā kholā. The drain in our lane is open.

The word, taken apart.
নর্দমা (nôrdômā) is the open drain along a lane; its origin is not settled, and CDIAL gives no Bengali form for it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ময়লা (môylā) — dirt; rubbish; dirty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pipe, then the Bengali for a dustbin.

You'll want to know: ময়লা.
ময়লা — môylā — "dirt; rubbish; dirty".
ময়লা ডাস্টবিনে ফেলতে হবে। môylā ḍāsṭbine phelte hôbe. Rubbish has to go in the dustbin.
Also the adjective dirty, of clothes and water: ময়লা (môylā) জামা, a dirty shirt.

The word, taken apart.
ময়লা (môylā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali mayalā, "dirt", under the reconstructed malin-, "dirty", beside Sanskrit malina (CDIAL 9904).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: জঞ্জাল.
জঞ্জাল — jônjāl — "junk; clutter; a heap of rubbish".
নর্দমাটা জঞ্জালে ভর্তি। nôrdômāṭā jônjāle bhôrti. The drain is choked with junk.
Old things piled up and in the way: ঘরের জঞ্জাল (jônjāl), the clutter in a room.

The word, taken apart.
জঞ্জাল (jônjāl) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali jañjāl, "disorder, rubbish", under the reconstructed jañjāla (CDIAL 5085).
