## You'll want to know: मनपसंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C550-CHAYAN550-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मनपसंद** — *manpasand* — "of one's own choosing, favourite".

> मैंने अपनी मनपसंद किताब ख़रीदी।
>
> *Maiṁne apnī manpasand kitāb kharīdī.*
>
> I bought the book I wanted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मनपसंद** (*manpasand*) joins *man*, "mind", from Sanskrit *manas* (CDIAL 9822), and *pasand*, "liked", which is usually traced to Persian *pasand*: what the mind likes.
