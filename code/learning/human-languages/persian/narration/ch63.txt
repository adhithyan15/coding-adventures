Lesson 1 of 5.
مهم (mohem) — important.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for heavy, then the Persian for right.

You'll want to know: مهم.
مهم — mohem — "important".

The word, taken apart.
mohem is borrowed from Arabic muhimm, "important". Its Arabic root is h-m-m (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
قرمز (ghermez) — red.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for green, then the Persian for yellow.

You'll want to know: قرمز.
قرمز — ghermez — "red".

The word, taken apart.
ghermez is borrowed from Arabic qirmiz, the kermes insect and its crimson dye (EtymDB). Lane records the Arabic word as itself taken from Persian, and it is usually traced to an Indo-Iranian word for "worm", the word of Persian kerm. English crimson comes from the same Arabic word.
