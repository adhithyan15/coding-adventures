## You'll want to know: रेखा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C518-TULIKA518-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रेखा** — *rekhā* — "a line".

> बालः ऋजुं रेखां लिखति।
>
> *bālaḥ ṛjuṃ rekhāṃ likhati.*
>
> The child draws a straight line.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेखा** (*rekhā*) probably comes from the root √likh (older *rikh*), "to scratch", beside which MW sets it; MW gives *rekhā* as a scratch, a streak, a line, a row (MW).
