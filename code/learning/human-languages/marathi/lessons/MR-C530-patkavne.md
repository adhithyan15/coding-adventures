# पटकावणे (paṭkāvṇe) — to carry off; to win a prize

## You'll want to know: पटकावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C530-PADAK530-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पटकावणे** — *paṭkāvṇe* — "to carry off; to win a prize".

> आमच्या शाळेने पहिलं बक्षीस पटकावलं.
>
> *āmcyā śāḷene pahilaṁ bakṣīs paṭkāvlaṁ.*
>
> Our school carried off the first prize.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पदक पटकावलं, *tyāne padak paṭkāvlaṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पटकावणे** (*paṭkāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb for seizing a prize; its origin is not settled.
