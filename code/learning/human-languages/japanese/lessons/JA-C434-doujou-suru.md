## You'll want to know: どうじょうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C434-THANK434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**どうじょうする** — *dōjō suru* — "to feel sympathy".

> **かれが けがを して、みんなが どうじょうしました。** — *kare ga kega o shite, minna ga dōjō shimashita* — "He got hurt, and everyone felt for him."

Polite **どうじょうします**, *dōjōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ともだちが どうじょうしました。**, *tomodachi ga dōjō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どうじょうする** (*dōjō suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *dōjō* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dō*, "same" (Middle Chinese *duwng*) and *jō*, "sympathy" (*dzjeng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
