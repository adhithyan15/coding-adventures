## You'll want to know: la tata
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C498-BAMBINI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la tata** — "nanny".

> La tata porta i bambini a scuola.
>
> *The nanny takes the children to school.*

Friendly speech; also *la babysitter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tata* is a word from child speech, made by doubling a syllable as small children do; the origin is not settled beyond that.
