## You'll want to know: полоть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C506-VEGETABLES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**полоть** (*polót*) — "to weed".

> Летом бабушка часто полет огород.
>
> *Létom bábushka chásto pólet ogoród.*
>
> In the summer Grandma often weeds the vegetable garden.

Imperfective; present полю, полешь. Perfective прополоть.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *полоть* without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *pelti*.
