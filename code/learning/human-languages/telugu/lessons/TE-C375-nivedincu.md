# నివేదించు (nivēdiñcu) — to submit a report

## You'll want to know: నివేదించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C375-PATRIKEYUDU375-04]; assesses=[] -->

**నివేదించు** — *nivēdiñcu* — "to submit a report".

> విలేకరి ఆ సంఘటన గురించి నివేదించాడు.
>
> *vilēkari ā saṅghaṭana guriñci nivēdiñcāḍu.*
>
> The reporter reported on the incident.

Past (I …): నివేదించాను, *nivēdiñcānu*.

You know the noun నివేదిక, a report.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నివేదించు** (*nivēdiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *nivedana*, "announcing, proclaiming" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
