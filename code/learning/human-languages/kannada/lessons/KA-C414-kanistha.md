## You'll want to know: ಕನಿಷ್ಠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C414-GARISHTHA414-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕನಿಷ್ಠ** — *kaniṣṭha* — "minimum; the least".

> ಈ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಕನಿಷ್ಠ ಮೂರು ದಿನ ಬೇಕು.
>
> *ī kelasakke kaniṣṭha mūru dina bēku.*
>
> This job needs at least three days.

ಕನಿಷ್ಠ ವೇತನ, the minimum wage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕನಿಷ್ಠ** (*kaniṣṭha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kaniṣṭha*, "the youngest, the least" (CDIAL 2718).
