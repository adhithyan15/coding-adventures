## You'll want to know: बाहर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C288-CHORI288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बाहर** — *bāhar* — "outside".

> चोरी के बाद हम सब घर से बाहर आए।
>
> *corī ke bād ham sab ghar se bāhar āe.*
>
> After the theft we all came out of the house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाहर** (*bāhar*) is inherited from *\*bāhira*, "outer", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9226). It pairs with **अंदर**: **घर से बाहर**, out of the house, against **घर के अंदर**, inside it.
