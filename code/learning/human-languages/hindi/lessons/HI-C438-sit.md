## You'll want to know: शीत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C438-JADA438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शीत** — *śīt* — "cold; the cold (formal)".

> शीत लहर की वजह से स्कूल बंद हैं।
>
> *śīt lahar kī vajah se skūl band haiṁ.*
>
> The schools are closed because of the cold wave.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शीत** (*śīt*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śīta*, "cold, chilly", and as a noun "cold weather" (Monier-Williams); the weather news speaks of शीत लहर, a cold wave.
