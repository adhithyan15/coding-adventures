## You'll want to know: दन्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C25-BODY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दन्तः** (*dantaḥ*) — "tooth".

**दन्तः** is a tooth, and its family is as secure as पादः. Latin *dens*, in its other forms *dentem* — whence *dentist*, *dental*, *trident*, the three-tooth. Greek *odous*, *odontos* — whence *mastodon*.

English *tooth* belongs here too, by the same Germanic shift that turned *p* into *f*: an old *d* becomes *t*. So दन्तः, *dens* and *tooth* are three worn copies of one word, and you have now seen the same machinery produce पिता/*father*, पादः/*foot* and दन्तः/*tooth*.
