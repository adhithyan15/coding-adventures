## You'll want to know: લાકડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C501-KARIGAR501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લાકડું** — *lākḍũ* — "wood".

> આ ખુરશી જૂના લાકડાની છે.
>
> *ā khurśī jūnā lākḍānī che.*
>
> This chair is made of old wood.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાકડું** (*lākḍũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *lakuṭa*, "a club, a stick", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the sense "stick" (CDIAL 10875). In everyday Gujarati it names wood itself, the stuff a chair or a door is made of.
