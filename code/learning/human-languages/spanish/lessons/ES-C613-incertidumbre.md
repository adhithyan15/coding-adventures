## You'll want to know: la incertidumbre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C613-REQUIERE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la incertidumbre** — "the uncertainty".

> Hay mucha incertidumbre sobre el futuro.
>
> *There is a lot of uncertainty about the future.*

The opposite of *la certeza*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incertidumbre* is usually traced to Late Latin *incertitūdō* 'uncertainty', a learned word built on *incertus* 'not sure', from *certus* 'sure' (Lewis & Short).
