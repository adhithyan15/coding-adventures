## You'll want to know: охрипнуть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C486-FEVER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**охрипнуть** (*okhrípnut*) — "to go hoarse".

> После концерта певец охрип.
>
> *Pósle kontsérta pevéts okhríp.*
>
> After the concert the singer went hoarse.

Perfective; past охрип, охрипла. Imperfective хрипнуть.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*охрипнуть* is built on *о-* and *хрипнуть*, from *хрип* 'a wheeze'; EtymDB lists *хрип* and *хрипнуть* without a source, and the root is usually taken as imitative.
