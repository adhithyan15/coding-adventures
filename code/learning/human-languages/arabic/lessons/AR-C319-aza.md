## You'll want to know: عزاء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C319-AZA319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عزاء** — *ʿazāʾ* — "condolence; a gathering of mourners".

> ذهبنا إلى بيت العزاء مساء أمس.
>
> *dhahabnā ilā bayt al-ʿazāʾ masāʾa ams.*
>
> We went to the house of mourning yesterday evening.

بيت العزاء is the home where the family receives visitors after a death.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عزاء** (*ʿazāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-z-y* (Lane), where *ʿazāʾ* is patience in the face of loss. To offer it to a family is to wish them that patience.
