# Recognition at distance: two signs and one sound

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-HA-01, MR-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 5s] The first two shapes this book ever asked you to make were **ह** and
the **ो** mark, and together they wrote **हो**. Make them both from memory.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SOUND-CHA-TSAA, MR-LEX-CHALNE] -->

<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"MR-R25-recognition-script-check","kind":"text","assesses":["MR-SOUND-CHA-TSAA"],"prompt":"How is cha said before a long aa in most Marathi mouths?","answer":"nearer ts than ch","accepted":["tsa","ts"],"feedback":{"correct":"Right: chaar comes out nearer tsaar.","incorrect":"Repair: before a long aa, cha leans toward ts."},"response_seconds":15} -->

[PAUSE 1s] Say **चालणे** and **चार** one after the other and hear the same
leaning in both.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-HA-01, MR-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, MR-SOUND-CHA-TSAA] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Write **हो** once without a model, say **चार** with its leaning
consonant, then stop.
