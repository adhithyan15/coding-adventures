## You'll want to know: jìngjiǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C361-GUEST361-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jìngjiǔ* — "to propose a toast".

> *zài Zhōngguó chī fàn de shíhou dàjiā chángcháng jìngjiǔ* — In China people often propose toasts at meals.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìngjiǔ* is built from *jìng* and *jiǔ*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjaengH* and *tsjuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'respectful' and the second 'wine'.
