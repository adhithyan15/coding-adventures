## You'll want to know: anhēlō, anhēlāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C520-SPIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anhēlō, anhēlāre** — "to pant, gasp".

> Canis post cursum anhēlat.
>
> *The dog pants after the run.*

*anhēlō, anhēlāre, anhēlāvī, anhēlātum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**anhēlō** is built, as Lewis & Short explains it, on a prefix *an-* and *hālō* "to breathe out", but the details are not settled.
