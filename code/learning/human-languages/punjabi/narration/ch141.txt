You'll want to know: ਚਲਾਕ.
ਚਲਾਕ — calāk — "cunning".

The word, taken apart.
ਚਲਾਕ (calāk) is a loan, usually traced to Persian chālāk, "quick, clever".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਸਿਆਣਾ (siāṇā) — wise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for heavy, then the Punjabi for cunning.

You'll want to know: ਸਿਆਣਾ.
ਸਿਆਣਾ — siāṇā — "wise".

The word, taken apart.
ਸਿਆਣਾ (siāṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sajāna, "clever", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13088). CDIAL explains it as sajñāna, "knowing", with its j reshaped after jānāti, "knows".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸਿੱਧਾ (siddhā) — straight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for innocent, then the Punjabi for round.

You'll want to know: ਸਿੱਧਾ.
ਸਿੱਧਾ — siddhā — "straight".

The word, taken apart.
ਸਿੱਧਾ (siddhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from siddha, "accomplished, perfected" (CDIAL 13401). CDIAL lists Lahnda siddhā, "straight, upright", beside the Punjabi form.
