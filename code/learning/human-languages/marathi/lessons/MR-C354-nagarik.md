## You'll want to know: नागरिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C354-NAGARIK354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नागरिक** — *nāgarik* — "a citizen".

> प्रत्येक नागरिकाला मत देण्याचा हक्क आहे.
>
> *pratyek nāgarikālā mat deṇyācā hakka āhe.*
>
> Every citizen has the right to vote.

Masculine.

नागरिक, both a man and a woman; नागरिकत्व, citizenship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नागरिक** (*nāgarik*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nāgarika*, "born or living in a town, civic" (Monier-Williams).
