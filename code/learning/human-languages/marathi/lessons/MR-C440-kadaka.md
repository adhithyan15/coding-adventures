## You'll want to know: कडाका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C440-DUSHKAL440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कडाका** — *kaḍākā* — "a severe spell".

> यंदा थंडीचा कडाका जास्त आहे.
>
> *yandā thaṇḍīcā kaḍākā jāsta āhe.*
>
> This year the cold is especially severe.

Masculine.

Of cold or heat: थंडीचा कडाका, उन्हाचा कडाका.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कडाका** (*kaḍākā*) is inherited, from the family of Sanskrit *kaṭakaṭā*, where CDIAL lists Marathi *kaḍakṇẽ*, "to crackle" (CDIAL 2632): cold sharp enough to crack.
