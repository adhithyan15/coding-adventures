## You'll want to know: hospitaliser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C313-RETRAITE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**hospitaliser** — "to take into hospital, to hospitalise".

> Ma grand-mère a été hospitalisée pendant une semaine.
>
> *My grandmother was in hospital for a week.*

Mostly in the passive: *il a été hospitalisé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hospitaliser* is built on *hospital*, the old form of *hôpital*, from Latin *hospitālis* "of a guest", built on *hospes* "guest, host" (Lewis & Short).
