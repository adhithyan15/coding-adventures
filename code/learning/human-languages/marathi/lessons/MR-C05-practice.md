# Chapter 12 recap — the first verbs

## You'll want to know — sentences about yourself
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-PHRASE-MI-MARATHI-BOLTO, MR-LEX-RAHNE, MR-LEX-KARNE] -->

| Marathi | Say it | English |
|---|---|---|
| मी मराठी बोलतो / बोलते. | *mī marāṭhī bolto / bolte.* | I speak Marathi. |
| मी पुण्यात राहतो / राहते. | *mī puṇyāt rāhto / rāhte.* | I live in Pune. |
| मी काम करतो / करते. | *mī kām karto / karte.* | I work. |

## What you've built — the engine, in one look
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-INFINITIVE-NE, MR-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-GENDERED-ENDING, MR-GRAMMAR-POSTPOSITION-ON-NOUN, MR-GRAMMAR-NO-SEPARATE-COPULA] -->

- **Infinitive** ends in **-णे** (*-ṇe*): *bolṇe, rāhṇe, karṇe*.
- **Present** = stem + a **gendered** ending — *-to* (m.) / *-te* (f.): *bolto/
  bolte, rāhto/rāhte, karto/karte*. No separate "am" — the ending carries it.
- **Verb goes last**; **"in"** attaches to the noun's end (*puṇyāt*).

## What you've built — roots banked
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-BOLNE, MR-ETYMON-MARATHI-MAHARASHTRA, MR-LEX-RAHNE, MR-LEX-KARNE, MR-ETYMON-KARNE-KWER] -->

- **बोलणे** (*bolṇe*) — to speak (native *bol-*).
- **मराठी** (*marāṭhī*) — from *Mahārāṣṭra*, "great realm."
- **राहणे** (*rāhṇe*) — to live (Sanskrit *rah-*).
- **करणे** (*karṇe*) — to do/work (√*kṛ* — root of *namaskār*, *karma*; *kām* ←
  *karma*).
