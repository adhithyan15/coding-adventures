## You'll want to know: agradecido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C720-GRACIAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**agradecido** — "grateful".

> Te estoy muy agradecido.
>
> *I am very grateful to you.*

*Estar agradecido por algo*. *Muy agradecido* — much obliged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*agradecido* is the participle of *agradecer*, built on old *gradecer*, from *grado* "goodwill", which is usually traced to Latin *grātum* "pleasing".
