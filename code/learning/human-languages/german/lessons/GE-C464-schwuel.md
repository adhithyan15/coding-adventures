## You'll want to know: schwül
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C464-HITZEWELLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**schwül** — "muggy, sultry".

> Vor dem Gewitter ist es oft schwül.
>
> *Before a thunderstorm it is often muggy.*

*Die Schwüle* — the muggy heat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schwül* came into the standard language from Low German; it is usually linked to *schwelen* 'to smoulder'. EtymDB lists it with the sense 'sultry' but gives no source.
