Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 204: Out and About.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घूमणो (ghūmṇo) — to stroll; to go round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to lose one's way, then the Marwadi for to be visible.

You'll want to know: घूमणो.
घूमणो — ghūmṇo — "to stroll; to go round".
आथण हूं बाजार मांय घूमूं हूं। āthaṇ hū̃ bājār mā̃y ghūmū̃ hū̃. In the evening I stroll round the market.
Past (I …): हूं सहर मांय घूम्यो, hū̃ sahar mā̃y ghūmyo.

The word, taken apart.
घूमणो (ghūmṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ghummati, "revolves". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi ghūmnā, "to revolve, wander" (CDIAL 4485).

Lesson 2 of 5.
भीड़ (bhīṛ) — a crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to be visible, then the Marwadi for to stroll.

You'll want to know: भीड़.
भीड़ — bhīṛ — "a crowd".
आज बाजार मांय घणी भीड़ है। āj bājār mā̃y ghaṇī bhīṛ hai. The market is very crowded today.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भीड़ (bhīṛ) is inherited from a reconstructed bhiṭ, "meet, throng"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bheṭi, "meeting", and Hindi bhīṛ, "crowd", under it (CDIAL 9490).

Lesson 3 of 5.
मैदान (maidān) — an open ground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to stroll, then the Marwadi for a crowd.

You'll want to know: मैदान.
मैदान — maidān — "an open ground".
मैदान स्कूल कनै है। maidān skūl kanai hai. The open ground is near the school.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मैदान (maidān) is usually traced to Persian maidān, "an open plain".
