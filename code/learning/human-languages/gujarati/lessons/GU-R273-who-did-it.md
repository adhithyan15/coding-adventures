# Review — who did it, and what has happened

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERGATIVE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-GAYO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I drank tea" and "she went home". (*mẽ cā pīdhī*, *te ghare gaī*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C273-NOUN-AGENT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-NE-OBJECT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-PERFECT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-PLUPERFECT-01, GU-LEX-BHAAT-01, GU-LEX-C88-CARRY-04, GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-02, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Arun ate rice. Mira drank water.]
- [YOU SAY: My sister wrote a letter, and Arun read it.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira saw Arun at the market.]
- [YOU SAY: Arun has gone home. I have done the work.]
- [YOU SAY: I reached the station, but the train had left.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C273-NOUN-AGENT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-NE-OBJECT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-PERFECT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-PLUPERFECT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In **મીરાએ પત્ર લખ્યો**, which word sets **લખ્યો**? (**પત્ર**.) Now the other way round: put Arun in Mira's place. Does the verb change? (No: **અરુણે પત્ર લખ્યો**.) Put **વાર્તા** in place of **પત્ર**? (Yes: **લખી**.) And "had gone", for a woman? (**ગઈ હતી**.)
