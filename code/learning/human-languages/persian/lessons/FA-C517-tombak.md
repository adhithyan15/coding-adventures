## You'll want to know: تنبک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C517-INSTRUMENTS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تنبک** (*tombak*) — "a tombak (a goblet drum)".

> او در گروه موسیقی تنبک میزند.
>
> *u dar goruh-e musiqi tombak mizanad.*
>
> He plays the tombak in a music group.

Spelled with *n* but said with *m* before *b*: *tombak*. Also *tonbak*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tombak* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Middle Persian word for 'drum' (EtymDB).
