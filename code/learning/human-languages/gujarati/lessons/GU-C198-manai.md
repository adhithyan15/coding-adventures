## You'll want to know: મનાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C198-SIGNS198-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મનાઈ** — *manāī* — "ban, prohibition".

> **મંદિરમાં બૂટની મનાઈ છે.** — *mandirmā̃ būṭnī manāī che* — "Shoes are not allowed in the temple."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મનાઈ** (*manāī*) is a loan that reached Gujarati from Arabic through Persian, but its Arabic source is not settled. It may rest on *manʿ*, "prevention, forbidding", root *m-n-ʿ*, or on the root *n-h-y*, "to forbid"; Lane gives both roots (Lane).
