## You'll want to know: सभा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C364-SANSTHA364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सभा** — *sabhā* — "an assembly; a public meeting".

> संध्याकाळी गावात सभा भरली.
>
> *sandhyākāḷī gāvāt sabhā bharlī.*
>
> In the evening an assembly gathered in the village.

Feminine.

सभा भरणे, an assembly meets; सभागृह, a hall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सभा** (*sabhā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sabhā*, "assembly, hall, council" (CDIAL 13167).
