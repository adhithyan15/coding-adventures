# Plans and perhaps — a review

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C163-FUT-PLAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *the day after tomorrow I'll go to the market*.

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *this evening I'll come home*]
- [YOU SAY: *tomorrow I'll get up early and go to the market*]
- [YOU SAY: *perhaps I'll come tomorrow*; *perhaps I won't come tomorrow*]
- [YOU SAY: *I'll come again tomorrow*; *Ram came again*]
- [YOU READ: **फेर**, and name its first letter]
