## You'll want to know: कोपर

**कोपर** — *kopar* — "an elbow".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोपर** (*kopar*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *kūrpara*, "elbow" (CDIAL 3413).
