## You'll want to know: கூறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C309-VASAKAR309-04]; assesses=[] -->

**கூறு** — *kūṟu* — "to state; to tell".

> சாட்சி நடந்ததை எல்லாம் கூறினார்.
>
> *cāṭci naṭantatai ellām kūṟiṉār.*
>
> The witness stated everything that had happened.

Past (I …): கூறினேன், *kūṟiṉēṉ*.

A written word for சொல்; news reports say கூறினார், he stated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கூறு** (*kūṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1921): to speak, assert, proclaim.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
