## You'll want to know: دراسة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C391-BAHTH391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دراسة** — *dirāsa* — "a study".

> قرأت دراسة عن النوم.
>
> *qaraʾtu dirāsa ʿan an-nawm.*
>
> I read a study about sleep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دراسة** (*dirāsa*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-s*, "to wear away; to study" (Lane). *Dirāsa* is a verbal noun of *darasa*, to study: studying, a study.
