Sanskrit, chapter 448: Naming a Feeling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भावः (bhāvaḥ) — a feeling; a state of mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for radio, then the Sanskrit for the internet.

You'll want to know: भावः.
भावः — bhāvaḥ — "a feeling; a state of mind".
भावः हृदये जायते। bhāvaḥ hṛdaye jāyate. A feeling arises in the heart.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भावः (bhāvaḥ) is built on the root √bhū, "to be, to become"; MW gives bhāva the senses "a state of mind or body, a way of feeling" and, in poetics, "emotion" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the internet, then the Sanskrit for a feeling.

You'll want to know: अनुतापः.
अनुतापः — anutāpaḥ — "regret; repentance".
कलहात् अनन्तरं मम अनुतापः भवति। kalahāt anantaraṃ mama anutāpaḥ bhavati. After a quarrel I feel regret.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अनुतापः (anutāpaḥ) is built from anu, "after", and tāpa, "heat", from the root √tap, "to burn", the root of तापः: a burning that comes afterwards; MW gives "repentance" (MW).

You'll want to know: हृष्यति.
हृष्यति — hṛṣyati — "thrills with joy".
बालः उपहारं पश्यति, हृष्यति च। bālaḥ upahāraṃ paśyati, hṛṣyati ca. The child sees the present and thrills with joy.
Past (he …): सः हृष्टवान्, saḥ hṛṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
हृष्यति (hṛṣyati) comes from the root √hṛṣ, "to bristle, to thrill", the root of हर्षः: delight felt as the hair standing on end; MW compares Latin horreo (MW).

उल्लासः — ullāsaḥ — "merriment; high spirits".
उत्सवे सर्वत्र उल्लासः अस्ति। utsave sarvatra ullāsaḥ asti. At the festival there is merriment everywhere.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उल्लासः (ullāsaḥ) is built from ud, "up", and the root √las, "to shine, to play"; MW gives "light" first and then "joy, merriness" (MW).
