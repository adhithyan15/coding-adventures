## You'll want to know: રાજા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C318-RAJA318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**રાજા** — *rājā* — "a king".

> જૂના વખતમાં અહીં એક દયાળુ રાજા રહેતો હતો.
>
> *jūnā vakhatmā̃ ahī̃ ek dayāḷu rājā raheto hato.*
>
> Long ago a kind king lived here.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રાજા** (*rājā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rājan*, "a chieftain, a king" (CDIAL 10679). It ends in *-ā* but does not change like **છોકરો**: **રાજાનું ઘર**, the king's house.
