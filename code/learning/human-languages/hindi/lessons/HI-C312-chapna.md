## You'll want to know: छपना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C312-SAMACHAR312-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छपना** — *chapnā* — "to be printed; to appear in print".

> हादसे की ख़बर सब अख़बारों में छपी।
>
> *hādse kī khabar sab akhbāroṁ meṁ chapī.*
>
> The news of the accident was printed in every newspaper.

Past (it …): वह ख़बर छपी, *vah khabar chapī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छपना** (*chapnā*) is the intransitive partner of **छापना**, to print, which CDIAL 4994 sets under *\*chapp*, a pressing: what is printed **छपता है**.
