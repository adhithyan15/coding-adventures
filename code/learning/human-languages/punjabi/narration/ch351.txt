Punjabi, chapter 351: Ball Games.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਗੇਂਦ (gend) — a ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to make someone dance, then the Punjabi for to entertain.

You'll want to know: ਗੇਂਦ.
ਗੇਂਦ — gend — "a ball".
ਬੱਚੇ ਬਾਹਰ ਗੇਂਦ ਨਾਲ ਖੇਡਦੇ ਹਨ। bachche bāhar gend nāl kheḍde han. The children play outside with a ball.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੇਂਦ (gend) is inherited from Sanskrit genduka, "a ball for play"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gẽd, "ball", under it (CDIAL 4248).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਹਰਾਉਣਾ (harāuṇā) — to defeat; to beat (in a game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a team, then the Punjabi for a match.

You'll want to know: ਹਰਾਉਣਾ.
ਹਰਾਉਣਾ — harāuṇā — "to defeat; to beat (in a game)".
ਸਾਡੀ ਟੀਮ ਹਰ ਮੈਚ ਵਿੱਚ ਉਸ ਟੀਮ ਨੂੰ ਹਰਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। sāḍī ṭīm har maich vich us ṭīm nū̃ harāundī hai. Our team beats that team in every match.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਹਰਾਇਆ, maiṁ Aman nū̃ harāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਹਰਾਉਣਾ (harāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit hārayati, "causes to be taken, loses"; CDIAL lists Punjabi harāuṇā, "to defeat", under it (CDIAL 14061).
