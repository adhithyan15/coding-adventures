# こまる (komaru) — to be in trouble; to be at a loss

## You'll want to know: こまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C247-PRAISE247-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こまる** — *komaru* — "to be in trouble; to be at a loss".

> **あには しごとで こまりますか。** — *ani wa shigoto de komarimasu ka* — "Does my elder brother have trouble at work?"

Polite **こまります**, *komarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こまる** (*komaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
