## You'll want to know: চিহ্ন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C400-THIKANA400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**চিহ্ন** — *chihnô* — "a sign; a mark".

> রাস্তার চিহ্ন দেখে চালক ধীরে চালালেন।
>
> *rāstār chihnô dekhe chālôk dhīre chālālen.*
>
> Seeing the road sign, the driver drove slowly.

Said chinho: the h is heard before the n.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চিহ্ন** (*chihnô*) is Sanskrit *cihna*, "a mark, a stamp, a sign" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.
