## You'll want to know: दुष्काळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C440-DUSHKAL440-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दुष्काळ** — *duṣkāḷ* — "a drought; a famine".

> त्या वर्षी मोठा दुष्काळ पडला.
>
> *tyā varṣī moṭhā duṣkāḷ paḍlā.*
>
> That year there was a great drought.

Masculine.

दुष्काळ पडणे, a drought strikes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुष्काळ** (*duṣkāḷ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *duṣkāla*, "a bad time, a famine" (CDIAL 6470), where CDIAL also lists the inherited Marathi *dukāḷ*.
