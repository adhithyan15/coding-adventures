## You'll want to know: शल्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C482-CIKITSA482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शल्यम्** — *śalyam* — "a splinter".

> पादे शल्यम् अस्ति, अतः सः शनैः चलति।
>
> *pāde śalyam asti, ataḥ saḥ śanaiḥ calati.*
>
> There is a splinter in his foot, so he walks slowly.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शल्यम्** (*śalyam*) MW's first sense is "a dart, a lance", then "anything causing pain" and, in medicine, "anything lodged in the body, a splinter"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
