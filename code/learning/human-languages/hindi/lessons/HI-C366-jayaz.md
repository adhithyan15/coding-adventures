# जायज़ (jāyaz) — legitimate, justified

## You'll want to know: जायज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C366-VAJIB366-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जायज़** — *jāyaz* — "legitimate, justified".

> तुम्हारा ग़ुस्सा जायज़ है।
>
> *tumhārā ġussā jāyaz hai.*
>
> Your anger is justified.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जायज़** (*jāyaz*) is usually traced to Arabic *jāʾiz*, "permitted", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
