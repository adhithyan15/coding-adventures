## You'll want to know: جھمیلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C529-JHAMELA529-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جھمیلا** — *jhamelā* — "a tiresome muddle; a hassle".

> اگر ٹکٹ کھو گیا تو بڑا جھمیلا ہو گا۔
>
> *agar ṭikaṭ kho gayā to baṛā jhamelā ho gā.*
>
> If the ticket gets lost, it will be a real hassle.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھمیلا** (*jhamelā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhamelā*, "wrangling", under *\*jhamm*, "entanglement", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5340).
