## You'll want to know: おじぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C327-REIGI327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おじぎ** — *ojigi* — "a bow (as a greeting)".

> **日本人は おれいの とき、おじぎを します。** — *nihonjin wa orei no toki, ojigi o shimasu* — "Japanese people bow when they thank someone."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おじぎ** (*ojigi*) is built from the native polite prefix *o-* and the Sino-Japanese *jigi*: two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese, *ji*, "word" (Middle Chinese *zi*) and *gi*, "ceremony" (*ngje*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
