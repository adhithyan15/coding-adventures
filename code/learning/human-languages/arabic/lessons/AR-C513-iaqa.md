## You'll want to know: إعاقة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C513-MUZMIN513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إعاقة** — *iʿāqa* — "a disability".

> يعمل في الشركة موظف عنده إعاقة.
>
> *yaʿmalu fī ash-sharika muwaẓẓaf ʿindahu iʿāqa.*
>
> An employee with a disability works at the company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إعاقة** (*iʿāqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-w-q*, "to hinder, to hold back" (Lane). Iʿāqa is the verbal noun of Form IV: a hindrance.

## What you've built
