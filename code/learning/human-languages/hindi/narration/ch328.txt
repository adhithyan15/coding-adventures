Hindi, chapter 328: A Night of Lights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रंगोली (raṅgolī) — a rangoli, a coloured pattern on the floor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an age, then the Hindi for to be ruined.

You'll want to know: रंगोली.
रंगोली — raṅgolī — "a rangoli, a coloured pattern on the floor".
मीरा ने आँगन में रंगोली बनाई। Mīrā ne āṁgan meṁ raṅgolī banāī. Mira made a rangoli in the courtyard.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रंगोली (raṅgolī) is probably built on रंग, colour, from Sanskrit raṅga (Monier-Williams); the rest of the word is not settled. It is drawn on the floor at the door for a festival.

You'll want to know: जगमगाना.
जगमगाना — jagmagānā — "to glitter; to be lit up".
त्योहार की रात पूरा शहर जगमगा रहा था। tyohār kī rāt pūrā śahar jagmagā rahā thā. On the night of the festival the whole city was lit up.
Past (it …): वह घर जगमगाया, vah ghar jagmagāyā.

The word, taken apart.
जगमगाना (jagmagānā) is the verb of jagmag, glittering, which CDIAL 5076 sets under jag, a form it reconstructs for shining: a place full of lights जगमगाता है.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to glitter, then the Hindi for to twinkle.

You'll want to know: पटाख़ा.
पटाख़ा — paṭākhā — "a firecracker".
बच्चों ने रात भर पटाख़े चलाए। baccoṁ ne rāt bhar paṭākhe calāe. The children set off firecrackers all night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पटाख़ा (paṭākhā) is probably imitative, built on paṭ, the sound of a sharp crack. You पटाख़े चलाते हो: you set them off.

Lesson 5 of 5.
आतिशबाज़ी (ātiśbāzī) — fireworks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to twinkle, then the Hindi for a firecracker.

You'll want to know: आतिशबाज़ी.
आतिशबाज़ी — ātiśbāzī — "fireworks".
आतिशबाज़ी से आसमान रंगों से भर गया। ātiśbāzī se āsmān raṅgoṁ se bhar gayā. The fireworks filled the sky with colour.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
आतिशबाज़ी (ātiśbāzī) is usually traced to Persian ātišbāzī, built from ātiš, fire, and bāzī, play: playing with fire, for a festival.
