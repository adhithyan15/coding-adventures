## You'll want to know: evakuieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C292-RETTUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**evakuieren** — "to evacuate".

> Wegen eines Feuers wurde die Schule evakuiert.
>
> *Because of a fire the school was evacuated.*

Regular, without *ge-*: *hat evakuiert*. Stress: *e-va-ku-IE-ren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*evakuieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēvacuāre* "to empty out" (EtymDB), built on *vacuus* "empty" (Lewis & Short).
