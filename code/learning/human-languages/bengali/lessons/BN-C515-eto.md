# এঁটো (ẽṭo) — leftover from a meal; touched by eating

## You'll want to know: এঁটো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C515-PORIBESHON515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**এঁটো** — *ẽṭo* — "leftover from a meal; touched by eating".

> খাওয়ার পরে আমি এঁটো থালাগুলো বেসিনে রাখলাম।
>
> *Khāoyār pôre āmi ẽṭo thālāgulo besine rākhlām.*
>
> After the meal I put the used plates in the basin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**এঁটো** (*ẽṭo*) comes from a reconstructed *ācaṣṭa*, "tasted"; CDIAL lists Bengali *ẽṭo*, "the leavings of a meal" (CDIAL 1067). Food, or a plate, that someone has already eaten from is এঁটো.
