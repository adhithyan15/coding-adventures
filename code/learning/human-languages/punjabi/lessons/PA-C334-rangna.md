## You'll want to know: ਰੰਗਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C334-WOOL334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੰਗਣਾ** — *raṅgṇā* — "to dye".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਚਿੱਟੀ ਉੱਨ ਨੂੰ ਲਾਲ ਰੰਗਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī chiṭṭī unn nū̃ lāl raṅgdī hai.*
>
> Grandmother dyes the white wool red.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੀ ਚੁੰਨੀ ਪੀਲੀ ਰੰਗੀ, *maiṁ āpṇī chunnī pīlī raṅgī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੰਗਣਾ** (*raṅgṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*raṅgayati*, "dyes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *raṅgṇā*, "to dye, paint", under it (CDIAL 10570).
