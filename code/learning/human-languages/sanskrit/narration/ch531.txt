Sanskrit, chapter 531: Wishes and Prayers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वाञ्छति (vāñchati) — yearns for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a sherbet, then the Sanskrit for stale.

You'll want to know: वाञ्छति.
वाञ्छति — vāñchati — "yearns for".
यदि सः सुखं वाञ्छति, तर्हि सः परिश्रमं करोतु। yadi saḥ sukhaṃ vāñchati, tarhi saḥ pariśramaṃ karotu. If he wants happiness, let him work hard.
Past (he …): सः सुखं वाञ्छितवान्, saḥ sukhaṃ vāñchitavān.

The word, taken apart.
वाञ्छति (vāñchati) comes from the root √vāñch, "to desire, to wish"; MW compares German wünschen and English wish (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
मनोरथः (manorathaḥ) — a heart's wish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for stale, then the Sanskrit for yearns for.

You'll want to know: मनोरथः.
मनोरथः — manorathaḥ — "a heart's wish".
यदि मनोरथः दृढः अस्ति, तर्हि मार्गः अपि भवति। yadi manorathaḥ dṛḍhaḥ asti, tarhi mārgaḥ api bhavati. If your wish is strong, a way appears too.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मनोरथः (manorathaḥ) is built from manas, "the mind, the heart", and ratha, which MW here takes as "joy": a heart's joy, a wish, a desire (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
आराधयति (ārādhayati) — propitiates; honours.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for yearns for, then the Sanskrit for a heart's wish.

You'll want to know: आराधयति.
आराधयति — ārādhayati — "propitiates; honours".
यदि त्वं गुरुम् आराधयसि, तर्हि सः प्रसन्नः भविष्यति। yadi tvaṃ gurum ārādhayasi, tarhi saḥ prasannaḥ bhaviṣyati. If you honour your teacher, he will be pleased.
Past (he …): सः गुरुम् आराधितवान्, saḥ gurum ārādhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
आराधयति (ārādhayati) is built on ā and the causative of the root √rādh, "to succeed"; MW gives ārādhayati as to conciliate, to propitiate, to honour, to worship (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a heart's wish, then the Sanskrit for propitiates.

You'll want to know: प्रार्थना.
प्रार्थना — prārthanā — "a prayer; a request".
यदि प्रार्थना शुद्धा अस्ति, तर्हि मनः शान्तं भवति। yadi prārthanā śuddhā asti, tarhi manaḥ śāntaṃ bhavati. If the prayer is pure, the mind grows calm.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रार्थना (prārthanā) is built on pra and the root √arth, "to ask for"; MW gives prārthanā as a request, a petition, and a prayer in worship (MW).
