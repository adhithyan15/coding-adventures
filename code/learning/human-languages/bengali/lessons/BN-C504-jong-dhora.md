## You'll want to know: জং ধরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C504-JONTRO504-04]; assesses=[] -->

**জং ধরা** — *jông dhôrā* — "to rust".

> বৃষ্টিতে পেরেকগুলোতে জং ধরল।
>
> *Brishṭite perekgulote jông dhôrlô.*
>
> The nails rusted in the rain.

Past (he/she …): সেই সাইকেলে জং ধরল, *sei sāikele jông dhôrlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জং ধরা** (*jông dhôrā*) joins জং, "rust", usually traced to Persian *zang*, to ধরা, "to catch, to take hold": rust takes hold.
