Tamil, chapter 92: Slowly, Again, Louder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மெதுவாக (metuvāka) — slowly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for I am sleepy, then the Tamil for well.

You'll want to know: மெதுவாக.
மெதுவாக (metuvāka) — "slowly".
மெதுவாகப் பேசுங்கள் — "please speak slowly", with the verb பேசு you read long ago.

What you've built.
The repair a beginner needs most.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: metuvāka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: metuvāka, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say metuvākap pēcuṅkaḷ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for I am sleepy, then the Tamil for well, then say metuvāka again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does மெதுவாக (metuvāka) mean? ("Slowly".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
சத்தமாக (cattamāka) — loudly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for slowly, then the Tamil for again.

You'll want to know: சத்தமாக.
சத்தமாக (cattamāka) — "loudly".
சத்தம் is "a noise, a sound". கொஞ்சம் சத்தமாகப் பேசுங்கள் — "please speak a little louder".

What you've built.
Slowly, again, louder.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.

What does சத்தமாக (cattamāka) mean? ("Loudly".) Say it once more.


Lesson 4 of 5.
ஒரு நிமிடம் (oru nimiṭam) — one minute, a moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for again, then the Tamil for loudly.

You'll want to know: ஒரு நிமிடம்.
ஒரு நிமிடம் (oru nimiṭam) — "one minute", said the way English says "a moment".
ஒரு is "one"; நிமிடம் is "a minute".

What you've built.
A way to buy time.

Lesson 5 of 5.
