Persian, chapter 355: Getting ready to cook.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
دستور پخت (dastur-e pokht) — a recipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an inscription, then the Persian for a ruin.

You'll want to know: دستور پخت.
دستور پخت (dastur-e pokht) — "a recipe".
این دستور پخت را از مادربزرگم یاد گرفتم. In dastur-e pokht râ az mâdarbozorgam yâd gereftam. I learned this recipe from my grandmother.
dastur "instruction" + pokht "cooking", from pokhtan "to cook".

The word, taken apart.
dastur "command, instruction" is a Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent, and pokht "cooking" is the past stem of the native verb pokhtan "to cook".

Lesson 2 of 6.
مواد (mavâd) — ingredients; materials.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a ruin, then the Persian for a recipe.

You'll want to know: مواد.
مواد (mavâd) — "ingredients; materials".
همه مواد را روی میز آشپزخانه گذاشت. Hame-ye mavâd râ ru-ye miz-e âshpazkhâne gozâsht. She put all the ingredients out on the kitchen table.
The Arabic broken plural of mâdde "a substance". Mavâd-e lâzem "the ingredients needed".

The word, taken apart.
mavâd is usually traced to Arabic mawādd, the broken plural of mādda "substance, matter"; EtymDB lists Arabic mādda "matter, substance", though not the plural.

Lesson 3 of 6.
پیمانه (peymâne) — a measuring cup, a measure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a recipe, then the Persian for ingredients.

You'll want to know: پیمانه.
پیمانه (peymâne) — "a measuring cup, a measure".
دو پیمانه برنج را شست. Do peymâne berenj râ shost. She washed two cups of rice.
In recipes, a cup used as a measure. In old poetry, a wine cup.

The word, taken apart.
peymâne is a native Persian word, usually linked with the verb peymudan "to measure"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 4 of 6.
الک کردن (alak kardan) — to sift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for ingredients, then the Persian for a measuring cup.

You'll want to know: الک کردن.
الک کردن (alak kardan) — "to sift".
آرد را دو بار الک کرد. Ârd râ do bâr alak kard. She sifted the flour twice.
Alak "a sieve". Present alak mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
alak "a sieve" is usually traced to Turkic elek; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 6.
خیس کردن (khis kardan) — to soak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a measuring cup, then the Persian for to sift.

You'll want to know: خیس کردن.
خیس کردن (khis kardan) — "to soak".
نخودها را از شب قبل خیس کرد. Nokhod-hâ râ az shab-e qabl khis kard. She soaked the chickpeas from the night before.
Khis "wet". Present khis mikonad; khis khordan "to stand soaking".

The word, taken apart.
khis "wet" is a native Persian word whose deeper origin is not settled; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.
