## You'll want to know: ख़ुराक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C489-MARHAM489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ुराक** — *khurāk* — "a dose".

> दवा की पहली ख़ुराक सुबह लेनी है।
>
> *davā kī pahlī khurāk subah lenī hai.*
>
> The first dose of the medicine is to be taken in the morning.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ुराक** (*khurāk*) is usually traced to Persian *khurāk*, "food, diet"; Hindi uses it for a measured amount of food or medicine, a dose.
