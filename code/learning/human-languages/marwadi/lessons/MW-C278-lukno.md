## You'll want to know: लुकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C278-KAHANI278-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लुकणो** — *lukṇo* — "to hide (oneself)".

> छोरो खाट हेठै लुकै है।
>
> *chhoro khāṭ heṭhai lukai hai.*
>
> The boy hides under the bed.

Past (he …): वो लुक्यो, *vo lukyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुकणो** (*lukṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lupyate*, "is lost, is removed"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *lukaï*, "hides" (CDIAL 11083).
