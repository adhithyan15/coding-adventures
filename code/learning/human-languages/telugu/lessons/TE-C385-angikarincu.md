# అంగీకరించు (aṅgīkariñcu) — to accept; to agree to

## You'll want to know: అంగీకరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C385-ANGIKARAM385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**అంగీకరించు** — *aṅgīkariñcu* — "to accept; to agree to".

> కంపెనీ మా షరతులు అంగీకరించింది.
>
> *kampenī mā ṣaratulu aṅgīkariñcindi.*
>
> The company accepted our conditions.

Past (I …): అంగీకరించాను, *aṅgīkariñcānu*.

Formal beside the everyday ఒప్పుకో.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంగీకరించు** (*aṅgīkariñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *aṅgīkāra*, "agreement, a promise" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
