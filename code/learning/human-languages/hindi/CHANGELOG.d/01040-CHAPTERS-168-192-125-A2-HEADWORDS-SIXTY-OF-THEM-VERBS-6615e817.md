## Chapters 168-192: 125 A2 headwords, sixty of them verbs

- Hindi's A2 gate asked for 120 verb headwords (it had 71) and 1,200
  headwords (it had 660). Twenty-five chapters of five add 125 words, each
  spelled only with letters the book teaches and none already a headword.
- **Chapters 168-179: sixty verbs** on `SPINE-NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS`:
  - change, movement and hands: मानना, मिलाना, बदलना, चलाना, छोड़ना, जोड़ना,
    उठाना, दिखाना, चढ़ना, उतरना, खींचना, धकेलना, फेंकना;
  - the kitchen: चखना, पीसना, उबालना, तलना;
  - feeling and conflict: डरना, लड़ना, चिल्लाना, थकना, सहना;
  - pairs that differ by who acts: बचाना/बचना, छिपाना/छिपना, रोकना/रुकना,
    जलाना/जलना, हटाना/हटना.
- **Chapters 180-190: fifty-five nouns** on `SPINE-NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS`:
  health (दवा, मरीज़, इलाज), travel (यात्रा, सामान, नक़्शा, चौराहा), work
  (तनख़्वाह, मालिक, कारख़ाना, ग्राहक; *ख़* is romanized *kh*, as elsewhere in the track), the house (रसोई, चाबी, अलमारी), weather
  (बारिश, धूप, तूफ़ान), feelings (ग़ुस्सा, प्यार, उम्मीद), the town (थाना,
  डाकिया, सिनेमा) and everyday talk (सवाल, जवाब, ग़लती, मदद, ख़बर).
- **Chapter 191**, time spans on `SPINE-TIME-OF-DAY`: हफ़्ता, साल, सदी, पल, मिनट.
- **Chapter 192**, how often, on `SPINE-SAY-WHAT-I-DO`: रोज़, कभी, हमेशा,
  अक्सर, कभी नहीं.
- Two reviews close each of chapters 175, 183 and 192, so every new atom is
  revisited twice and no review runs past the five-minute budget.
- Level gate at A2: verb vocabulary 49 -> 0 short, vocabulary 540 -> 415.
