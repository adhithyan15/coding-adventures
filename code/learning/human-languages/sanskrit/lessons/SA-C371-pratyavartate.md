## You'll want to know: प्रत्यावर्तते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C371-VIKALPA371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्यावर्तते** — *pratyāvartate* — "turns back; returns".

> यदि मार्गे जलम् अस्ति, तर्हि यात्री प्रत्यावर्तते।
>
> *yadi mārge jalam asti, tarhi yātrī pratyāvartate.*
>
> If there is water on the road, the traveller turns back.

Past (he …): सः ग्रामं प्रत्यावृत्तवान्, *saḥ grāmaṃ pratyāvṛttavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्यावर्तते** (*pratyāvartate*) is built on *prati*, "back", *ā* and the root √vṛt, "to turn"; MW gives *praty-ā-√vṛt* "to return, to come back" (MW).

## What you've built
