# শ (sha) — the letter that opens শাড়ি

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-JHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C41-PHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ঝ and ফ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **শাড়ি** (*shari*), "sari".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: শ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-SHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**শ** — *sha*, the sound *sh*.

It opens **শাড়ি** (*shari*), "sari", a word that went into English almost
unchanged.

## Writing: শ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-SHA-01] -->

Put your pen on শ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-JHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C41-PHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C41-SHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **শাড়ি** and point at শ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: শ three times, saying *sh* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ফ, then শ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **শাড়ি** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C41-JHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C41-PHA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C41-SHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — শ? (**sha**, *sh*.) Which word did it
come from? (**শাড়ি**, *shari*, "sari".)
