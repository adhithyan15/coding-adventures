## You'll want to know: o miradouro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C447-PASSEIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o miradouro** — "the viewpoint, the lookout".

> Do miradouro vemos o rio e as pontes.
>
> *From the viewpoint we can see both the river and the bridges.*

In Brazil: *o mirante*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*miradouro* is built on *mirar* 'to gaze', which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *mīrāre* (EtymDB), from Latin *mīrārī* 'to wonder at'.
