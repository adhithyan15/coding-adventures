## You'll want to know: بلغم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C469-THROAT469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بلغم** — *balgham* — "phlegm".

> کھانسی کے ساتھ بلغم آتا ہے۔
>
> *khāṅsī ke sāth balgham ātā hai.*
>
> Phlegm comes up with the cough.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بلغم** (*balgham*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *balgham*, "phlegm", which Arabic probably took from Greek *phlegma*.
