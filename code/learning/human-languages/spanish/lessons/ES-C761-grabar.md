## You'll want to know: grabar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C761-SERIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**grabar** — "to record; to engrave".

> Grabamos el concierto con el móvil.
>
> *We recorded the concert on our phones.*

For sound and video; also to engrave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grabar* was borrowed from French *graver* "to engrave" (EtymDB), which comes from Frankish *graban* "to dig"; a recording is sound cut into a surface.
