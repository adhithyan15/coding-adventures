Gujarati, chapter 132: Clean, Dirty, Sour, Spicy, Soft.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wet, then the Gujarati for dry.

You'll want to know: ચોખ્ખું.
ચોખ્ખું (cokhkhũ) — "clean".

The word, taken apart.
ચોખ્ખું (cokhkhũ) belongs with Gujarati cokh, "clean", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit cokṣa, "pure, clean" (CDIAL 4918).

You'll want to know: ખાટું.
ખાટું (khāṭũ) — "sour".

The word, taken apart.
ખાટું (khāṭũ) is a tadbhava of the lexical Sanskrit khaṭṭa, "sour" (CDIAL 3777). CDIAL asks whether the word is connected with kaṭu, "pungent".

The word, taken apart.
તીખું (tīkhũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tīkṣṇa, "sharp; hot, pungent" (CDIAL 5839).
