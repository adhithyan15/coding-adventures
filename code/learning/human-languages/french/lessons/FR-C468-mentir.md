## You'll want to know: mentir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C468-FRANCHISE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mentir** — "to lie, to tell lies".

> Il a menti à ses parents sur ses notes.
>
> *He lied to his parents about his marks.*

Present: *je mens, il ment, nous mentons*. Past: *j'ai menti*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mentir* is inherited, through Old French, from Vulgar Latin *mentīre*, a form of Latin *mentīrī* "to lie" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
