Telugu, chapter 389: Analysing and Comparing.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
విశ్లేషించు (viślēṣiñcu) — to analyse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for certainty, then the Telugu for uncertainty.

You'll want to know: విశ్లేషించు.
విశ్లేషించు — viślēṣiñcu — "to analyse".
మేము పరీక్ష ఫలితాలను విశ్లేషించాము. mēmu parīkṣa phalitālanu viślēṣiñcāmu. We analysed the exam results.
Past (I …): విశ్లేషించాను, viślēṣiñcānu.
To take a question apart to find out how it works.

The word, taken apart.
విశ్లేషించు (viślēṣiñcu) is built on Sanskrit viśleṣa, "loosening, separation, dissolution" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 6.
పోలిక (pōlika) — a likeness; a resemblance; a comparison.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an analysis, then the Telugu for to examine closely.

You'll want to know: పోలిక.
పోలిక — pōlika — "a likeness; a resemblance; a comparison".
అన్నకు తమ్ముడికి చాలా పోలిక ఉంది. annaku tammuḍiki cālā pōlika undi. The two brothers look very much alike.
Plural: పోలికలు, pōlikalu.
From the same root as పోల్చు, to compare, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
పోలిక (pōlika) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4597 lists Telugu pōlika, "resemblance", beside pōl(u)cu, "to compare", and Tamil pōl, "to resemble".

Lesson 5 of 6.
వ్యత్యాసం (vyatyāsaṁ) — a distinction; a contrast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to examine closely, then the Telugu for a likeness.

You'll want to know: వ్యత్యాసం.
వ్యత్యాసం — vyatyāsaṁ — "a distinction; a contrast".
రెండు ధరల మధ్య పెద్ద వ్యత్యాసం ఉంది. reṇḍu dharala madhya pedda vyatyāsaṁ undi. There is a big difference between the two prices.
Formal beside తేడా, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
వ్యత్యాసం (vyatyāsaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit vyatyāsa, "exchange; change, inverted order" (Monier-Williams). Telugu uses it for the difference between two things.
