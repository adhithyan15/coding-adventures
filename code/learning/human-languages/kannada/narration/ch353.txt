Kannada, chapter 353: Grandfather's Will.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಯಿಲು (uyilu) — a will (testament).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to set free, then the Kannada for to capture.

You'll want to know: ಉಯಿಲು.
ಉಯಿಲು — uyilu — "a will (testament)".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಸಾಯುವ ಮುಂಚೆ ಉಯಿಲು ಬರೆದಿದ್ದರು. ajja sāyuva muñce uyilu barediddaru. Grandfather had written a will before he died.
A will is "written": ಉಯಿಲು (uyilu) ಬರೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಯಿಲು (uyilu) is usually traced to English will, the legal paper, which reached the South Indian languages under British courts.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವಾರಸುದಾರ (vārasudāra) — an heir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to capture, then the Kannada for a will.

You'll want to know: ವಾರಸುದಾರ.
ವಾರಸುದಾರ — vārasudāra — "an heir".
ಅಜ್ಜನ ಮನೆಗೆ ಅವರ ಮೊಮ್ಮಗನೇ ವಾರಸುದಾರ. ajjana manege avara mommaganē vārasudāra. The grandson is the heir to Grandfather's house.
Plural: ವಾರಸುದಾರರು, vārasudāraru.
-ದಾರ means "holder", as in ಜಮೀನುದಾರ, a landholder.

The word, taken apart.
ವಾರಸುದಾರ (vārasudāra) is a loan from Arabic wāris, "an heir", with the Persian ending -dār, "holder"; the word came through Hindustani.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬಳುವಳಿ (baḷuvaḷi) — a legacy; an inheritance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a will, then the Kannada for an heir.

You'll want to know: ಬಳುವಳಿ.
ಬಳುವಳಿ — baḷuvaḷi — "a legacy; an inheritance".
ಈ ಹಳೆಯ ಮನೆ ಅಜ್ಜಿಯ ಬಳುವಳಿ. ī haḷeya mane ajjiya baḷuvaḷi. This old house is Grandmother's legacy.
Something handed down: property, a skill or a habit.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಳುವಳಿ (baḷuvaḷi) is usually traced to the native Dravidian family of baḷe, "to increase, to grow"; DEDR 5304 lists Kannada baḷuvaḷike, "increase, growth".

ಪರಂಪರೆ — parampare — "a heritage; a tradition".
ಈ ಹಬ್ಬ ನಮ್ಮ ಊರಿನ ಹಳೆಯ ಪರಂಪರೆ. ī habba namma ūrina haḷeya parampare. This festival is an old tradition of our town.
What passes from generation to generation: ಸಾಂಸ್ಕೃತಿಕ ಪರಂಪರೆ (parampare), cultural heritage.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಂಪರೆ (parampare) is borrowed from Sanskrit paramparā, "an uninterrupted series, succession, lineage" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪೀಡಿಸು (pīḍisu) — to pester; to torment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a legacy, then the Kannada for a heritage.

You'll want to know: ಪೀಡಿಸು.
ಪೀಡಿಸು — pīḍisu — "to pester; to torment".
ಮನೆಗಾಗಿ ಸಂಬಂಧಿಕರು ಅಜ್ಜಿಯನ್ನು ತುಂಬಾ ಪೀಡಿಸಿದರು. manegāgi sambandhikaru ajjiyannu tumbā pīḍisidaru. The relatives pestered Grandmother a lot for the house.
Past (I …): ಪೀಡಿಸಿದೆ, pīḍiside.
The person troubled takes -ಅನ್ನು. Illness too ಪೀಡಿಸು (pīḍisu): ರೋಗ ಪೀಡಿಸಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೀಡಿಸು (pīḍisu) is built on Sanskrit pīḍā, "pain, damage" (CDIAL 8227), from the root pīḍ, "to press, to hurt", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
