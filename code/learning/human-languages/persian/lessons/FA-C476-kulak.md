## You'll want to know: کولاک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C476-KULAK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کولاک** (*kulâk*) — "a snowstorm".

> کولاک جاده کوهستانی را بست.
>
> *Kulâk jâdde-ye kuhestâni râ bast.*
>
> The snowstorm closed the mountain road.

Snow with strong wind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *kulâk* is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.
