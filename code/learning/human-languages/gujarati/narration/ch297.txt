Gujarati, chapter 297: Warnings Nobody Heeded.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચેતવવું (cetavvũ) — to warn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a stitch, then the Gujarati for a condition.

You'll want to know: ચેતવવું.
ચેતવવું — cetavvũ — "to warn".
ચોકીદારે મુસાફરોને ચેતવ્યા કે પુલ નબળો છે. cokīdāre musāphrone cetavyā ke pul nabḷo che. The watchman warned the passengers that the bridge was weak.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધાને ચેતવ્યા, teṇe badhāne cetavyā.

The word, taken apart.
ચેતવવું (cetavvũ) goes back to Sanskrit cētáyati, "makes attentive, observes" (CDIAL 4906): to make someone notice a danger. The person warned takes ને: મુસાફરોને ચેતવ્યા.

Lesson 2 of 5.
સાવધાન (sāvdhān) — careful; on guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a condition, then the Gujarati for to warn.

You'll want to know: સાવધાન.
સાવધાન — sāvdhān — "careful; on guard".
રાત્રે આ રસ્તા પર સાવધાન રહો. rātre ā rastā par sāvdhān raho. Be careful on this road at night.

The word, taken apart.
સાવધાન (sāvdhān) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sāvadhāna, "attentive, heedful" (Monier-Williams). On a sign it is a warning by itself: સાવધાન!, look out!

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઊંધું (ū̃dhũ) — upside down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to warn, then the Gujarati for careful.

You'll want to know: ઊંધું.
ઊંધું — ū̃dhũ — "upside down".
અકસ્માત પછી ગાડી ઊંધી પડી હતી. akasmāt pachī gāḍī ū̃dhī paḍī hatī. After the accident the car lay upside down.

The word, taken apart.
ઊંધું (ū̃dhũ) goes back to Sanskrit avamūrdha, "head down", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "upside down" (CDIAL 804). It agrees like an adjective: ઊંધી ગાડી, ઊંધું વાસણ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ટોળું (ṭoḷũ) — a gathered crowd; a mob.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for careful, then the Gujarati for upside down.

You'll want to know: ટોળું.
ટોળું — ṭoḷũ — "a gathered crowd; a mob".
અકસ્માત પછી ત્યાં લોકોનું મોટું ટોળું ભેગું થયું. akasmāt pachī tyā̃ lokonũ moṭũ ṭoḷũ bhegũ thayũ. After the accident a big crowd gathered there.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટોળું (ṭoḷũ) comes from ṭōla, "a division, a party", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists Gujarati ṭoḷũ there, glossed "a flock of sheep" (CDIAL 5483). Today it is mostly a crowd of people that gathers at one spot, where ભીડ is any crowd.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ધક્કો (dhakko) — a push.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for upside down, then the Gujarati for a gathered crowd.

You'll want to know: ધક્કો.
ધક્કો — dhakko — "a push".
ટોળામાં કોઈએ મને ધક્કો માર્યો. ṭoḷāmā̃ koīe mane dhakko māryo. Someone in the crowd pushed me.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધક્કો (dhakko) comes from dhakk, "push, strike", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun beside ધકેલવું, to push (CDIAL 6701). A push is given with મારવું: ધક્કો માર્યો.
