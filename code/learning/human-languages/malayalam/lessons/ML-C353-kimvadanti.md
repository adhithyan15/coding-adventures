# കിംവദന്തി (kiṁvadanti) — a rumour

## You'll want to know: കിംവദന്തി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C353-KIMVADANTI353-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കിംവദന്തി** — *kiṁvadanti* — "a rumour".

> ആ കിംവദന്തി സത്യമല്ല എന്ന് പോലീസ് പറഞ്ഞു.
>
> *ā kiṁvadanti satyamalla ennŭ pōlīsŭ paṟaññu.*
>
> The police said that the rumour is not true.

A written word for talk that passes from mouth to mouth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കിംവദന്തി** (*kiṁvadanti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kiṁvadantī*, "common talk, a rumour" (Monier-Williams), a word made from *kim*, "what", and *vadanti*, "they say": a rumour is what people are saying.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
