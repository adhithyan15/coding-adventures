## You'll want to know: huánghūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C517-LIGHT517-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huánghūn* — "twilight; the fading light of evening".

> *wǒ zuì xǐhuan huánghūn de shíhou qù sànbù* — Twilight is my favourite time for a walk.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huánghūn* is built from *huáng* 'yellow' and *hūn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwang* and *xwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the colour of the centre and, after an older dictionary, the second as the sun gone dark.
