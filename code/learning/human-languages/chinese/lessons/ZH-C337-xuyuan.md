## You'll want to know: xǔyuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C337-WISH337-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xǔyuàn* — "to make a wish".

> *shēngrì nà tiān tā xiān xǔyuàn ránhòu chī dàngāo* — On his birthday he made a wish first and then ate the cake.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǔyuàn* is built from *xǔ* and *yuàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xjoX* and *ngjwonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to permit' and 'to grant', and the second 'to desire'.
