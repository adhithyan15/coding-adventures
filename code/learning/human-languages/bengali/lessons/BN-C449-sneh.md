## You'll want to know: স্নেহ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C449-SNEHO449-01]; assesses=[] -->

**স্নেহ** — *sneh* — "affection; tender love for someone younger".

> দাদু নাতিকে স্নেহ করেন।
>
> *dādu nātike sneh kôren.*
>
> Grandfather is very fond of his grandson.

স্নেহ runs downward, from an elder to someone younger; what runs back up is সম্মান (taught), respect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্নেহ** (*sneh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sneha*, which Monier-Williams gives first as "oiliness, smoothness" and then as "tenderness, fondness"; Bengali keeps only the tenderness.
