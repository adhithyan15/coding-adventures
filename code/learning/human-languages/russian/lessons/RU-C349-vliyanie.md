## You'll want to know: влияние
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C349-VLIYANIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**влияние** (*vliyániye*) — "influence".

> Родители имеют большое влияние на детей.
>
> *Rodíteli iméyut bolshóye vliyániye na detéy.*
>
> Parents have a great influence on their children.

Neuter. *Под влиянием* + genitive — under the influence of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**влияние** is built on *влиять* with the noun suffix *-ние*; the verb comes from Church Slavonic *vŭlĭjati* "to pour in" (EtymDB), on the root of *лить* "to pour".
