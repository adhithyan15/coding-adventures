Spanish, chapter 507: La sociedad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
votar — to vote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to congratulate, then the Spanish for to hug.

You'll want to know: votar.
votar — "to vote".
Mañana votamos en el colegio del barrio. Tomorrow we vote at the local school.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: votar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: votar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with votar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for to congratulate, then the Spanish for to hug, then say votar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does votar mean? ("To vote".) Say it once more.
