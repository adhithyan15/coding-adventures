## You'll want to know: regretter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C472-NOSTALGIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**regretter** — "to regret; to miss".

> Je regrette mon ancien quartier.
>
> *I miss my old neighbourhood.*

*Je regrette* also means I am sorry: *je regrette, c'est complet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regretter* comes from Old French *regreter* "to lament the dead" (EtymDB), built from *re-* and an old verb for weeping, probably of Germanic origin.
