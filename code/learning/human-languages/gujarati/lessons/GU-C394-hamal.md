## You'll want to know: હમાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C394-PORTER394-01]; assesses=[] -->

**હમાલ** — *hamāl* — "a porter".

> હમાલે મારો સામાન ટ્રેનમાં મૂક્યો.
>
> *hamāle māro sāmān ṭrenmā̃ mūkyo.*
>
> The porter put my luggage on the train.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હમાલ** (*hamāl*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥammāl*, "a carrier of loads", which reached Gujarati through Persian; at a big station the porters carry bags on their heads.
