## You'll want to know: धीरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C314-SHAURYA314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धीरः** — *dhīraḥ* — "steadfast; brave".

> धीरः सैनिकः न बिभेति।
>
> *dhīraḥ sainikaḥ na bibheti.*
>
> The steadfast soldier is not afraid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धीरः** (*dhīraḥ*) MW gives *dhīra* as steady, firm, resolute, brave, and suggests, with a question mark, the root √dhṛ, "to hold", or √dhā, "to put", so its origin is uncertain (MW).
