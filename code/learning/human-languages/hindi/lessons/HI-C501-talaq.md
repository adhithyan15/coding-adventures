## You'll want to know: तलाक़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C501-NATA501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तलाक़** — *talāq* — "a divorce".

> दो साल बाद पति और पत्नी का तलाक़ हो गया।
>
> *do sāl bād pati aur patnī kā talāq ho gayā.*
>
> Two years later the husband and wife got divorced.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तलाक़** (*talāq*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṭalāq*, "release, divorce", which reached Hindi through Persian.
