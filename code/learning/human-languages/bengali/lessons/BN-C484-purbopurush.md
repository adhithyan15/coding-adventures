## You'll want to know: পূর্বপুরুষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C484-SONTAN484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পূর্বপুরুষ** — *pūrbôpurush* — "an ancestor; the forefathers".

> আমাদের পূর্বপুরুষরা নদীর ধারে থাকতেন।
>
> *āmāder pūrbôpurushrā nôdīr dhāre thākten.*
>
> Our ancestors lived on the riverbank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পূর্বপুরুষ** (*pūrbôpurush*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pūrvapuruṣa*, "a forefather" (Monier-Williams): *pūrva* "earlier" with *puruṣa* "man".
