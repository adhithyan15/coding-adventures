## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-NU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-ME-01] -->

> **ぬ** — *nu*

Two strokes, one lift:

1. a short **diagonal**, from the upper left down to the right
2. start at the top and cut **down to the left** across the first stroke; loop
   round the lower left, rise and run over the **arch**, come down the right
   side, then tie a small **loop** at the bottom and flick out to the right

Up to the bottom of the right side, this is **め**. Only the ending differs:

| you write | it ends with |
|---|---|
| **め**, *me* | an open curve |
| **ぬ**, *nu* | a small closed loop and a flick |

With **い** in front of it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**いぬ**, *inu*, a dog.
