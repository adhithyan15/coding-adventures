## You'll want to know: wòshǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C336-SEEOFF336-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wòshǒu* — "to shake hands".

> *gàobié de shíhou wǒmen wòshǒu le* — When we said goodbye, we shook hands.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wòshǒu* is built from *wò* 'to grasp' and 手 'a hand', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'aewk* and *syuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hold'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
