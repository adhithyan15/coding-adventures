## You'll want to know: वरिष्ठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C509-BADHATI509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वरिष्ठ** — *variṣṭha* — "senior".

> वरिष्ठ अधिकारी आज बैठकीला आले.
>
> *variṣṭha adhikārī āj baiṭhkīlā āle.*
>
> The senior officers came to the meeting today.

As a noun in the plural: वरिष्ठ, one's seniors.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वरिष्ठ** (*variṣṭha*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *variṣṭha*, "best, foremost", a superlative (Monier-Williams).
