## You'll want to know: pelear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C510-TRATO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pelear** — "to fight, to argue".

> Los hermanos pelean por todo.
>
> *The brothers fight about everything.*

*Pelearse con alguien* — to fall out with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pelear** is built on *pelo*, "hair" (EtymDB 2.1): a fight was first a pulling of hair. *Pelo* is Latin *pilus*, "a hair" — inherited: the short *i* became *e*.
