## You'll want to know: संदेसो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C197-PHONE197-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संदेसो** — *sandeso* — "a message".

> म्हैं संदेसो सुण्यो।
>
> *mhaĩ sandeso suṇyo.*
>
> I listened to the message.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संदेसो** (*sandeso*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sandeśa*, "message, information". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *sãdesā*, "news" (CDIAL 12904).
