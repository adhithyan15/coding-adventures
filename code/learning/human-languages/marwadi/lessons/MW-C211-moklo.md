## You'll want to know: मोकळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C211-SIZE211-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मोकळो** — *mokḷo* — "roomy; loose".

> ये कुरतो मोकळो है।
>
> *ye kurto mokḷo hai.*
>
> This kurta is roomy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोकळो** (*mokḷo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*mukna*, "loosed"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *mokaï*, "sends", and Hindi *moklā*, "loose, free", under it (CDIAL 10157).
