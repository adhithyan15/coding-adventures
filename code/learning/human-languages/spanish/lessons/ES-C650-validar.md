## You'll want to know: validar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C650-LINEA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**validar** — "to validate (a ticket)".

> Valide el billete al subir.
>
> *Validate your ticket when you get on.*

Buses and trams have a small machine for this.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*validar* is built on *válido* "valid", a learned borrowing from Latin *validus* "strong, able" (Lewis & Short), from *valēre* "to be strong".
