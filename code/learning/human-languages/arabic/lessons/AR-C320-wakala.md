## You'll want to know: وكالة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C320-ILAM320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**وكالة** — *wakāla* — "an agency".

> نشرت وكالة الأنباء الخبر صباحا.
>
> *nasharat wakālat al-anbāʾ al-khabar ṣabāḥan.*
>
> The news agency put out the story in the morning.

وكالة سفر: a travel agency.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وكالة** (*wakāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-k-l* (Lane), "to entrust an affair to someone". A *wakīl* acts for others; a *wakāla* is the office that does.
