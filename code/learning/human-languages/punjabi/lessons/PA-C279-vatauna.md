## You'll want to know: ਵਟਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C279-SWAP279-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਟਾਉਣਾ** — *vaṭāuṇā* — "to exchange; to swap".

> ਮੈਂ ਖ਼ਰਾਬ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਵਟਾਵਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ kharāb fon kall vaṭāvāṅgā.*
>
> I will exchange the faulty phone tomorrow.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਵਟਾਈ, *maiṁ juttī vaṭāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਟਾਉਣਾ** (*vaṭāuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vartayati*, "causes to turn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *baṭāuṇā*, "to cause to be twisted, change", under it (CDIAL 11356).
