## You'll want to know: つながる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C307-DENWA307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**つながる** — *tsunagaru* — "to be connected; to get through".

> **やっと でんわが つながりました。** — *yatto denwa ga tsunagarimashita* — "At last the call went through."

Polite **つながります**, *tsunagarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **でんわが つながりました。**, *denwa ga tsunagarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つながる** (*tsunagaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
