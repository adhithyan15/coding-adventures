## You'll want to know: नियुक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C504-AVEDANA504-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नियुक्तिः** — *niyuktiḥ* — "a post; an appointment".

> मम भ्रातुः नूतना नियुक्तिः अस्ति।
>
> *mama bhrātuḥ nūtanā niyuktiḥ asti.*
>
> My brother has a new post.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियुक्तिः** (*niyuktiḥ*) is built on *ni* and the root √yuj, "to yoke, to appoint", with the ending *-ti*; MW gives *niyukti* as an injunction, a charge, an office (MW).
