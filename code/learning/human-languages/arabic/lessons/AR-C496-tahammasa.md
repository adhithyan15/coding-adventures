# تحمس (taḥammasa) — to get excited

## You'll want to know: تحمس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C496-HAMAS496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تحمس** — *taḥammasa* — "to get excited".

> تحمس الأولاد عندما بدأت المباراة.
>
> *taḥammasa al-awlād ʿindamā badaʾat al-mubārā.*
>
> The boys got excited when the match began.

Present: يتحمس, *yataḥammas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحمس** (*taḥammasa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-m-s*, "to be hard, firm, vehement" (Lane). Form V taḥammasa is to grow keen.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
