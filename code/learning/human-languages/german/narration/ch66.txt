German, chapter 66: To sing, To travel, To show, To bring, To forget.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
singen — to sing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to laugh, then the German for to cry.

You'll want to know: singen.
singen — "to sing".

The word, taken apart.
singen from Old High German singan from Proto-Germanic singwaną — inherited, the same verb as English sing. (EtymDB 2.1).
