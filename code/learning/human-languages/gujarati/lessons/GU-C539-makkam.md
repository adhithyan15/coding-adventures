## You'll want to know: મક્કમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C539-MAKKAM539-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મક્કમ** — *makkam* — "resolute".

> જો તમે મક્કમ રહેશો તો તમે જીતશો.
>
> *jo tame makkam raheśo to tame jītśo.*
>
> If you stay firm, you will win.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મક્કમ** (*makkam*) is usually traced to Arabic *muḥkam*, "made firm, strong", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
