## You'll want to know: temerē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C371-ROSTRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**temerē** — "rashly, blindly; at random".

> Nihil temerē faciēbat.
>
> *He did nothing rashly.*

An adverb; its noun is *temeritās*, rashness. *Nōn temerē*, not without good reason.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*temerē* is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*temezi* "in the dark" (EtymDB), kin to *tenebrae* "darkness" (Lewis & Short): to act blindly.
