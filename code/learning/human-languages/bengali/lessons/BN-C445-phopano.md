## You'll want to know: ফোঁপানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C445-SHOK445-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ফোঁপানো** — *phõpāno* — "to sob".

> বাবা বকার পরে ছেলেটা বিছানায় শুয়ে ফোঁপাচ্ছিল।
>
> *bābā bôkār pôre chheleṭā bichhānāy shuye phõpāchchhilô.*
>
> After his father scolded him, the boy lay on the bed sobbing.

Past (he/she …): সেই শিশু ফোঁপাল, *sei shishu phõpālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফোঁপানো** (*phõpāno*) is an imitative verb, its sound standing for the catch of breath in sobbing. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
