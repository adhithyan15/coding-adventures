Lesson 1 of 5.
ਵਾਲੀ (vālī) — an earring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a ring, then the Punjabi for a bangle.

You'll want to know: ਵਾਲੀ.
ਵਾਲੀ — vālī — "an earring".

The word, taken apart.
ਵਾਲੀ (vālī) is inherited. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit vālaka, "bracelet", whose feminine vālikā the old word-lists give as "ear ornament" (CDIAL 11573).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਕੜਾ (kaṛā) — a steel bracelet, a kara.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a bangle, then the Punjabi for an earring.

You'll want to know: ਕੜਾ.
ਕੜਾ — kaṛā — "a steel bracelet, a kara".

The word, taken apart.
ਕੜਾ (kaṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kaṭaka, "twist of straw; bracelet" (CDIAL 2629). CDIAL notes that, outside Gujarati, the word grew into "ring, bracelet, chain".

You'll want to know: ਗੁੜ.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੁੜ (guṛ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from guḍa, "boiled sugarcane juice, molasses" (CDIAL 4182). CDIAL asks whether it is the same word as guḍa, "ball".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਛੋਲੇ (chole) — chickpeas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a steel bracelet, then the Punjabi for jaggery.

You'll want to know: ਛੋਲੇ.
ਛੋਲੇ — chole — "chickpeas".

The word, taken apart.
Where ਛੋਲੇ (chole) comes from is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it. The Punjabi word CDIAL does list for the chickpea is caṇā, from Sanskrit caṇa (CDIAL 4579).
