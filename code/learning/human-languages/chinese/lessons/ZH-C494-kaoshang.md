## You'll want to know: 考上
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C494-GAOKAO494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**考上** — *kǎoshàng* — "to get into (a school) by exam; to pass the entrance exam for".

> *tā fùxí le yì nián cái kǎoshàng dàxué* — He revised for a year before he got into university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**考上** (*kǎoshàng*) is built from 考 'to examine' and 上 'to go up', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khawX* and *dzyangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to climb'.
