## You'll want to know: वृक्षारोपण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C449-NISARGA449-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वृक्षारोपण** — *vṛkṣāropaṇ* — "tree planting".

> शाळेने वृक्षारोपणाचा कार्यक्रम घेतला.
>
> *śāḷene vṛkṣāropaṇācā kāryakram ghetlā.*
>
> The school held a tree planting event.

Neuter.

वृक्ष, a tree, and आरोपण, planting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वृक्षारोपण** (*vṛkṣāropaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vṛkṣāropaṇa*, "planting trees" (Monier-Williams).
