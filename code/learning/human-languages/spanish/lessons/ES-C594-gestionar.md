## You'll want to know: gestionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C594-EQUIPO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**gestionar** — "to manage, to handle".

> Gestiono los pedidos de la tienda.
>
> *I manage the shop's orders.*

For paperwork too: *gestionar un permiso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gestionar* is built on *gestión*, a learned borrowing from Latin *gestio* 'management', from *gerere* 'to carry, to carry on' (EtymDB).
