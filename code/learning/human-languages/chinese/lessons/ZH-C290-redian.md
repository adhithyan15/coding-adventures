## You'll want to know: 热点
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C290-NEWS290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**热点** — *rèdiǎn* — "hot topic; what everyone is talking about".

> **这是今天的热点** *zhè shì jīntiān de rèdiǎn* — This is today's hot topic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**热点** (*rèdiǎn*) is built from 热 'hot' and 点 'a dot', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyet* and *temX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'burning, as by fire' and the second 'a dot or stroke': a hot spot, the story everyone is talking about.
