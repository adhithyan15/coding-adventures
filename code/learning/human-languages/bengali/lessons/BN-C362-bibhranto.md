## You'll want to know: বিভ্রান্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C362-MONOJOG362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিভ্রান্ত** — *bibhrāntô* — "confused; misled".

> ভুল খবর শুনে অনেকে বিভ্রান্ত হল।
>
> *bhul khôbôr shune ôneke bibhrāntô hôlô.*
>
> Many people were misled by the false news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিভ্রান্ত** (*bibhrāntô*) is Sanskrit *vibhrānta*, wandering about, and also confused or bewildered (Monier-Williams): a mind that has lost its way.
