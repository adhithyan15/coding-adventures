Lesson 1 of 6.
અલબત્ત (albatt) — of course (granted).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for in addition to, then the Gujarati for ever.

You'll want to know: અલબત્ત.
અલબત્ત — albatt — "of course (granted)".
જો તમે બોલાવશો તો અલબત્ત હું આવીશ. jo tame bolāvśo to albatt hũ āvīś. If you invite me, of course I'll come.

The word, taken apart.
અલબત્ત (albatt) is usually traced to Persian albatta, "certainly", a word Persian took from Arabic.

જો વરસાદ પડશે તો હું હરગિજ બહાર નહીં જાઉં. jo varsād paḍśe to hũ hargij bahār nahī̃ jāũ. If it rains, I will by no means go out.

હરગિજ (hargij) is usually traced to Persian hargiz, "ever", which with a negative means "never"; in Gujarati it comes with નહીં.

Lesson 3 of 6.
છતાં (chatā̃) — despite (after a noun).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for of course, then the Gujarati for by no means.

You'll want to know: છતાં.
છતાં — chatā̃ — "despite (after a noun)".
જો તાવ છતાં તે શાળાએ જશે તો તે વધારે બીમાર થશે. jo tāv chatā̃ te śāḷāe jaśe to te vadhāre bīmār thaśe. If he goes to school despite the fever, he'll get worse.

The word, taken apart.
છતાં (chatā̃) follows its noun: તાવ છતાં, despite the fever. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
વિપરીત (viprīt) — adverse (going against you).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for despite, then the Gujarati for rather.

You'll want to know: વિપરીત.
વિપરીત — viprīt — "adverse (going against you)".
જો હવામાન વિપરીત હશે તો અમે પ્રવાસ ઠેલીશું. jo havāmān viprīt haśe to ame pravās ṭhelīśũ. If the weather turns against us, we will put off the trip.

The word, taken apart.
વિપરીત (viprīt) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit viparīta, "turned round; contrary" (Monier-Williams). Said of weather or of luck, it means going against you.
