## You'll want to know: el espectáculo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C759-ESCENARIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el espectáculo** — "the show".

> El espectáculo empieza a las ocho.
>
> *The show starts at eight.*

*Dar un espectáculo* — to make a scene.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*espectáculo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *spectāculum* "sight, show" (EtymDB), from *spectāre* "to watch".
