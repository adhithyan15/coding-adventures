## You'll want to know: उछलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C405-GATI405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उछलना** — *uchalnā* — "to bounce; to jump up".

> गड्ढे में बस उछली और सब लोग हँस पड़े।
>
> *gaḍḍhe meṁ bas uchlī aur sab log hãs paṛe.*
>
> The bus bounced in the pothole and everyone burst out laughing.

Past (he …): वह बच्चा ख़ुशी से उछला, *vah baccā khuśī se uchlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उछलना** (*uchalnā*) goes back to *\*ut-śalati*, "springs up", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1843), which lists Hindi *uchalnā*, "to leap".
