## You'll want to know: la discriminazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C366-SOCIETA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la discriminazione** — "the discrimination".

> La discriminazione sul lavoro è contro la legge.
>
> *Discrimination at work is against the law.*

*Discriminazione contro le donne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discriminazione* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *discrīminātiō*, a term of rhetoric for setting opposite ideas side by side (Lewis & Short), from *discrīmen* "a dividing line", built on *discernere* and *cernere* "to sift, to separate".
