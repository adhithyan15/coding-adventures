## You'll want to know: ચહેકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C445-BIRDS445-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચહેકવું** — *cahekvũ* — "to chirp; to twitter".

> સવારે ચકલીઓ ચહેકે છે.
>
> *savāre caklīo caheke che.*
>
> In the morning the sparrows chirp.

Past (it …): તે ચકલી ચહેકી, *te caklī cahekī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચહેકવું** (*cahekvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
