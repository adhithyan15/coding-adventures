## You'll want to know: જાગરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**જાગરણ** — *jāgraṇ* — "staying up all night".

> દર્દીને સંભાળવા મેં આખી રાત જાગરણ કર્યું.
>
> *dardīne sambhāḷvā mẽ ākhī rāt jāgraṇ karyũ.*
>
> I stayed up all night to look after the patient.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાગરણ** (*jāgraṇ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *jāgaraṇa*, "waking, keeping awake", as Monier-Williams records it.
