## You'll want to know: la laverie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C447-PRESSING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la laverie** — "the launderette".

> Les étudiants lavent leur linge à la laverie.
>
> *The students wash their clothes at the launderette.*

*Une laverie automatique*; in Québec, *une buanderie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*laverie* is built on *laver* 'to wash', which is inherited from Latin *lavāre* (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
