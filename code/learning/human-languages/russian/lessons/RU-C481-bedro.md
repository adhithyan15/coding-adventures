## You'll want to know: бедро
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C481-BACK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**бедро** (*bedró*) — "a thigh; the hip".

> Он упал и ударился бедром.
>
> *On upál i udárilsya bedróm.*
>
> He fell and hit his hip.

Neuter; plural бёдра.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бедро* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *bedro*, which EtymDB traces back to PIE *bʰedʰros.
