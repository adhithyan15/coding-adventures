## You'll want to know: شهریه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C400-DANESHKADE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شهریه** (*shahriye*) — "tuition fees".

> شهریه این دانشگاه گران است.
>
> *shahriye-ye in dâneshgâh gerân ast.*
>
> The fees at this university are high.

Also the monthly fee for a private class or a kindergarten.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شهریه** is usually traced to Arabic *shahriyya* "monthly", from Arabic *shahr* "month" (a different word from Persian *shahr* "city"): a fee once paid by the month.
