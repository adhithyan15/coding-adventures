## You'll want to know: ਕੱਲਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C294-POOR294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਲਰ** — *kallar* — "salty barren soil".

> ਕੱਲਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਣਕ ਚੰਗੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *kallar vich kaṇak chaṅgī nahīṁ hundī hai.*
>
> Wheat does not grow well in salty soil.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਲਰ** (*kallar*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kallara*, "salt barren soil"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kallar*, "salt barren soil", under it (CDIAL 2954).
