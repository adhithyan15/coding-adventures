## You'll want to know: bāozhuāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C527-ORDER527-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bāozhuāng* — "to wrap goods for sale".

> *zhè jiā shāngdiàn de dōngxi bāozhuāng de zuì hǎokàn* — This shop packages its things the most beautifully.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bāozhuāng* is built from 包 'to wrap' and *zhuāng* 'to pack', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *paew* and *tsrjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to wrap up' and the second 'to bind up, to dress'.
