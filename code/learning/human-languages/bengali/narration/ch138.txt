Bengali, chapter 138: Busy, Clever, Foolish, Kind, Brave.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ব্যস্ত (byāstô) — busy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for tired, then the Bengali for healthy.

You'll want to know: ব্যস্ত.
ব্যস্ত — byāstô — "busy".

The word, taken apart.
ব্যস্ত (byāstô) is Sanskrit vyasta, "scattered, distracted", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. In Bengali it means "busy".
