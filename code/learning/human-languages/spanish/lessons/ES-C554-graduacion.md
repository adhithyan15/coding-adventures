## You'll want to know: la graduación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C554-GRADUA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la graduación** — "the graduation".

> Mis padres vinieron a mi graduación.
>
> *My parents came to my graduation.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*graduación* is built on *graduar*, which is usually traced to Medieval Latin *graduāre*, from Latin *gradus* 'a step' (Lewis & Short).
