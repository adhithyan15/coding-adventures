## You'll want to know: しゅうかく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C489-FARM489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうかく** — *shūkaku* — "a harvest; a crop".

> **あきは しゅうかくの きせつです。** — *aki wa shūkaku no kisetsu desu* — "Autumn is harvest season."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうかく** (*shūkaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shū*, "income" (Middle Chinese *syuw*) and *kaku*, "harvest" (*hwak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
