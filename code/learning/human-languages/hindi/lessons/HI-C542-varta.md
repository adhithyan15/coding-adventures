## You'll want to know: वार्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C542-SAMJHAUTA542-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ता** — *vārtā* — "talks; a negotiation (formal)".

> दोनों पक्षों की वार्ता अच्छी रही।
>
> *Donõ pakṣoṁ kī vārtā acchī rahī.*
>
> The talks between the two sides went well.

Feminine.

A formal word, heard in the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ता** (*vārtā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vārttā*, "news, an account of what has happened" (Monier-Williams); Hindi uses it, in a formal register, for talks between two sides.
