Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 160: Came and Got Up.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
आयो, आई, आया — came.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say everyone went home. Then to come, आवणो, and he comes every day.

Grammar Lens: the past of आवणो.
आयो, आई and आया do for आवणो what गयो, गई and गया do for जाणो:
राम काल आयो। rām kāl āyo. — Ram came yesterday.
मां दिनूगै आई। mā̃ dinūgai āī. — Mother came early in the morning.
सगळा आथण आया। sagḷā āthaṇ āyā. — Everyone came in the evening.
CDIAL lists Old Marwari āīyo, "he came" (CDIAL 1045).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: वो बाजार सूं आयो। — vo bājār sū̃ āyo. — He came from the market.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: राम कद आयो? — rām kad āyo? — When did Ram come?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: मां काल आई। — mā̃ kāl āī. — Mother came yesterday.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
He came? (आयो.) She came, of mother? (आई.) They came? (आया.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that Ram came yesterday.


Lesson 2 of 3.
उठ्यो, बैठ्यो — got up, sat down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say to get up, to sit and to walk: उठणो, बैठणो, चालणो. Then Ram came yesterday.

Grammar Lens: -यो on a consonant stem.
A stem that ends in a consonant takes -्यो in the past: उठ, उठ्यो; बैठ, बैठ्यो; चाल, चाल्यो; पूग, पूग्यो.
हूं दिनूगै उठ्यो। hū̃ dinūgai uṭhyo. — I got up early in the morning.
For a woman it is -ी, and for more than one -्या:
मां दिनूगै उठी। mā̃ dinūgai uṭhī. — Mother got up early in the morning.
सगळा बैठ्या। sagḷā baiṭhyā. — Everyone sat down.
CDIAL lists Old Marwari ūṭhyo, "stood up" (CDIAL 1907).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: राम घरै पूग्यो। — rām gharai pūgyo. — Ram reached home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: मां घरै पूगी। — mā̃ gharai pūgī. — Mother reached home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो कोनी उठ्यो। — vo konī uṭhyo. — He didn't get up.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
He got up? (उठ्यो.) She got up? (उठी.) They sat down? (बैठ्या.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that mother got up early in the morning.


Lesson 3 of 3.
Came and got up — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say Ram came yesterday.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Ram came yesterday; mother came yesterday; everyone came in the evening]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I got up early in the morning, as a man and then as a woman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: everyone sat down; Ram reached home]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: when did Ram come?; he came from the market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mother went home; he didn't get up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Came, for a man, a woman, and more than one? (आयो, आई, आया.) Got up? (उठ्यो, उठी, उठ्या.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that mother reached home.
