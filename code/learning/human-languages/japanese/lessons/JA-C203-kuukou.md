## You'll want to know: くうこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C203-FLY203-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くうこう** — *kūkō* — "an airport".

> **くうこうまで ちかてつで いきます。** — *kūkō made chikatetsu de ikimasu* — "I go to the airport by subway."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くうこう** (*kūkō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kū*, "empty, sky" (Middle Chinese *khuwng*) and *kō*, "harbour" (*kaewngX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
