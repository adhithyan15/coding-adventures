## You'll want to know: थळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C226-DUNE226-04]; assesses=[] -->

**थळ** — *thaḷ* — "dry land; the open desert".

> थळ मांय रेत घणी है।
>
> *thaḷ mā̃y ret ghaṇī hai.*
>
> There's a lot of sand in the open desert.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थळ** (*thaḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sthala*, "dry land"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *thala*, "land" (CDIAL 13744).
