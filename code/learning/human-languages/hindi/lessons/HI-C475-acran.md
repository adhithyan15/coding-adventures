## You'll want to know: आचरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C475-CHARITRA475-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आचरण** — *ācraṇ* — "moral conduct; behaviour".

> अध्यापक बच्चों के आचरण पर ध्यान देते हैं।
>
> *adhyāpak baccoṁ ke ācraṇ par dhyān dete haiṁ.*
>
> Teachers pay attention to how the children behave.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आचरण** (*ācraṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ācaraṇa*, "conduct, behaviour" (Monier-Williams), from the same *car* as *caritra*.
