Punjabi, chapter 235: Sweeping the Floor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਬੁਹਾਰਨਾ (buhārnā) — to sweep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to put out, then the Punjabi for a wooden bed.

You'll want to know: ਬੁਹਾਰਨਾ.
ਬੁਹਾਰਨਾ — buhārnā — "to sweep".
ਮੈਂ ਝਾੜੂ ਨਾਲ ਕਮਰਾ ਬੁਹਾਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ jhāṛū nāl kamrā buhārdī hāṁ. I sweep the room with a broom.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਮਰਾ ਬੁਹਾਰਿਆ, maiṁ kamrā buhāriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਬੁਹਾਰਨਾ (buhārnā) is inherited: CDIAL sets it under Sanskrit bahukāra, "effecting much" (also a late word for a sweeper), and lists Punjabi buhārnā, "to sweep" (CDIAL 9188).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ਝਾੜਨਾ.
ਝਾੜਨਾ — jhāṛnā — "to dust; to shake out".
ਮੈਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਨਾਲ ਅਲਮਾਰੀ ਝਾੜਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ kappṛe nāl almārī jhāṛdā hāṁ. I dust the cupboard with a cloth.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦਰੀ ਝਾੜੀ, maiṁ darī jhāṛī.

The word, taken apart.
ਝਾੜਨਾ (jhāṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed jhāṭayati, "shakes down"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jhāṛṇā, "to shake off, thresh, sweep" (CDIAL 5328).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪੋਚਾ (pochā) — a floor cloth (for mopping).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to sweep, then the Punjabi for to dust.

You'll want to know: ਪੋਚਾ.
ਪੋਚਾ — pochā — "a floor cloth (for mopping)".
ਮੈਂ ਗਿੱਲੇ ਪੋਚੇ ਨਾਲ ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਸਾਫ਼ ਕਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ gille poche nāl farsh sāf kardī hāṁ. I clean the floor with a wet floor cloth.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੋਚਾ (pochā) is inherited from Sanskrit pōta, "cloth"; CDIAL lists Punjabi poccā, "cloth or brush for smearing" (CDIAL 8400).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਗਾਰਾ (gārā) — mud plaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to dust, then the Punjabi for a floor cloth.

You'll want to know: ਗਾਰਾ.
ਗਾਰਾ — gārā — "mud plaster".
ਗਾਰਾ ਗਿੱਲਾ ਅਤੇ ਭਾਰੀ ਹੈ। gārā gillā ate bhārī hai. The mud plaster is wet and heavy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗਾਰਾ (gārā) is inherited from a reconstructed gāra, "dripping, oozing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gārā, "kneaded potter's clay, mud used for mortar" (CDIAL 4137).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਲਿੱਪਣਾ (lippṇā) — to plaster (with mud).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a floor cloth, then the Punjabi for mud plaster.

You'll want to know: ਲਿੱਪਣਾ.
ਲਿੱਪਣਾ — lippṇā — "to plaster (with mud)".
ਦਾਦੀ ਗਾਰੇ ਨਾਲ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਲਿੱਪਦੀ ਹੈ। dādī gāre nāl chullhā lippdī hai. Grandmother plasters the stove with mud.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਲਿੱਪਿਆ, maiṁ chullhā lippiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਲਿੱਪਣਾ (lippṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit lipyatē, "is smeared"; CDIAL lists Punjabi lippṇā among the verbs for plastering a wall (CDIAL 11061).
