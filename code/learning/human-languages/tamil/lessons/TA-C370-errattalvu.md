# ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு (ēṟṟattāḻvu) — inequality; ups and downs

## You'll want to know: ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C370-VARUMAI370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு** — *ēṟṟattāḻvu* — "inequality; ups and downs".

> சமூகத்தில் ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு குறைய வேண்டும்.
>
> *camūkattil ēṟṟattāḻvu kuṟaiya vēṇṭum.*
>
> Inequality in society must come down.

Literally a rising and a falling (தாழ்வு): highs and lows.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஏற்றத்தாழ்வு** (*ēṟṟattāḻvu*) is native: *ēṟṟam*, "mounting, rising", from *ēṟu*, "to rise" (DEDR 916), and *tāḻvu*, "lowness", from *tāḻ*, "to be low" (DEDR 3178).
