## You'll want to know: दफ़नाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C505-SHOK505-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दफ़नाना** — *dafnānā* — "to bury (the dead)".

> चिड़िया मर गई, तो बच्चों ने उसे बाग़ में दफ़नाया।
>
> *ciṛiyā mar gaī, to baccoṁ ne use bāġ meṁ dafnāyā.*
>
> The bird died, so the children buried it in the garden.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चिड़िया को दफ़नाया, *usne ciṛiyā ko dafnāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दफ़नाना** (*dafnānā*) is *dafn*, "burial", made into a verb with *-ānā*; *dafn* is usually traced to Arabic *dafn*, "burial", which reached Hindi through Persian.
