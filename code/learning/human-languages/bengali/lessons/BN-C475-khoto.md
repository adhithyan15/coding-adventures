## You'll want to know: ক্ষত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C475-KHOTO475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষত** — *khôtô* — "a wound".

> নার্স ক্ষতটায় মলম লাগালেন।
>
> *nārs khôtôṭāy môlôm lāgālen.*
>
> The nurse put ointment on the wound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ক্ষত** (*khôtô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kṣata*, "wounded", and as a noun "a wound" (CDIAL 3645).
