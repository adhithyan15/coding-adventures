# வசீகரம் (vacīkaram) — charm; attractiveness

## You'll want to know: வசீகரம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C473-AZHAKU473-01]; assesses=[] -->

**வசீகரம்** — *vacīkaram* — "charm; attractiveness".

> அவருடைய புன்னகையில் ஒரு வசீகரம் இருந்தது.
>
> *avaruṭaiya puṉṉakaiyil oru vacīkaram iruntatu.*
>
> There was a charm in his smile.

வசீகரமான, charming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வசீகரம்** (*vacīkaram*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vaśīkaraṇa*, "bringing under control; bewitching" (Monier-Williams).
