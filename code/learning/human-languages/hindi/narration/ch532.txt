Hindi, chapter 532: A Song to Dance To.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गीत (gīt) — a song, a lyric.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a musical instrument, then the Hindi for a mridang (a barrel drum).

You'll want to know: गीत.
गीत — gīt — "a song, a lyric".
शादी में औरतों ने पुराने गीत गाए। Śādī meṁ aurtoṁ ne purāne gīt gāe. At the wedding the women sang old songs.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
गीत (gīt) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit gīta, "sung; a song" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 5 of 5.
नचाना (nacānā) — to make dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for practice (of music), then the Hindi for a gathering (for music or poetry).

You'll want to know: नचाना.
नचाना — nacānā — "to make dance".
ढोल ने सब लोगों को नचाया। Ḍhol ne sab logoṁ ko nacāyā. The drum got everyone dancing.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चों को नचाया, usne baccoṁ ko nacāyā.

The word, taken apart.
नचाना (nacānā) is the causative of nācnā, "to dance", which comes from Sanskrit nṛtyati, "dances" (CDIAL 7583).
