# చరణం (caraṇaṁ) — a stanza; a verse of a song

## You'll want to know: చరణం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C539-PADYAM539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**చరణం** — *caraṇaṁ* — "a stanza; a verse of a song".

> రెండో చరణం నాకు గుర్తులేదు.
>
> *reṇḍō caraṇaṁ nāku gurtulēdu.*
>
> I do not remember the second verse.

The verses that come between the refrains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చరణం** (*caraṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *caraṇa*, "a foot; a line of a stanza" (Monier-Williams).
