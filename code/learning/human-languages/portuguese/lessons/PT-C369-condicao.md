## You'll want to know: a condição
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C369-CONDICAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a condição** — "the condition".

> Vou contigo, com uma condição: voltamos cedo.
>
> *I will go with you on one condition: we come back early.*

*Com a condição de* + infinitive. *Condições de trabalho* — working conditions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condição* is a learned borrowing from Latin *condiciō* "an agreement, a condition" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), often spelt *conditiō* in later Latin, built on *condīcere* "to talk together, to agree on", from *dīcere* "to say".
