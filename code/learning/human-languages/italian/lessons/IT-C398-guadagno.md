## You'll want to know: il guadagno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C398-GUADAGNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il guadagno** — "the earnings, the gain".

> Con il guadagno della vendita ho comprato una moto.
>
> *With the money from the sale I bought a motorbike.*

Built on *guadagnare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guadagno* is built on *guadagnare* "to earn", from a Germanic verb that EtymDB traces through Vulgar Latin *wadanio* "to pursue, to win" to Proto-Germanic *waiþanjaną* (EtymDB).
