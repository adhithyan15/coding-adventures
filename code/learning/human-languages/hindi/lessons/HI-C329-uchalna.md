## You'll want to know: उछालना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C329-RANG329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उछालना** — *uchālnā* — "to toss up; to throw into the air".

> लोगों ने हवा में रंग उछाला।
>
> *logoṁ ne havā meṁ raṅg uchālā.*
>
> People tossed colour into the air.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने गेंद उछाली, *usne gẽd uchālī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from *\*ut-śālayati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1848), with the sense of throwing up. You **उछालना** a ball, a coin or colour.
