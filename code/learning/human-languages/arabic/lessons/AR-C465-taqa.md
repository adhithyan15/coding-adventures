## You'll want to know: طاقة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C465-TAQA465-01]; assesses=[] -->

**طاقة** — *ṭāqa* — "energy; power".

> نستعمل طاقة الشمس في منزلنا.
>
> *nastaʿmilu ṭāqat ash-shams fī manzilinā.*
>
> We use the sun's energy in our house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طاقة** (*ṭāqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-w-q*, "to be able" (Lane). A *ṭāqa* was first strength, what one is able to do.
