## You'll want to know: సమాధానపరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C427-MADHYAVARTI427-03]; assesses=[] -->

**సమాధానపరచు** — *samādhānaparacu* — "to reassure; to calm down".

> ఏడుస్తున్న పిల్లవాడిని అమ్మ సమాధానపరిచింది.
>
> *ēḍustunna pillavāḍini amma samādhānaparicindi.*
>
> Mother calmed the crying child.

Past (I …): సమాధానపరిచాను, *samādhānaparicānu*.

సమాధానం, an answer, and also calm, + -పరచు, an ending that makes a verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమాధానపరచు** (*samādhānaparacu*) is built from Sanskrit *samādhāna*, "putting together; reconciliation" (CDIAL 13210), and -పరచు, a native ending that makes a verb (DEDR 3853 lists *paṟacu*, "to cause to feel", beside *paḍu*).
