## You'll want to know: torrēns, torrentis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C482-AMNIS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**torrēns, torrentis** — "a torrent, a rushing stream".

> Post pluviam torrēns ē monte currit.
>
> *After the rain a torrent rushes down from the mountain.*

Third declension, masculine: *torrēns, torrentis m.* The present participle of *torreō* 'to burn', used as a noun: a stream that is fierce. English *torrent* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*torrēns* is the present participle of *torreō* 'to parch, to burn' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), used as a noun for a raging, boiling stream. English *torrent* comes from it.
