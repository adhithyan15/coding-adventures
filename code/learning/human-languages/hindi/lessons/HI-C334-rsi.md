## You'll want to know: ऋषि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C334-KATHA334-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ऋषि** — *ṛṣi* — "a sage; a seer".

> जंगल में एक बूढ़े ऋषि रहते थे।
>
> *jaṅgal meṁ ek būṛhe ṛṣi rahte the.*
>
> An old sage lived in the forest.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऋषि** (*ṛṣi*) is Sanskrit *ṛṣi*, "a seer, a sage" (Monier-Williams). The verb is plural for respect: **ऋषि रहते थे**.
