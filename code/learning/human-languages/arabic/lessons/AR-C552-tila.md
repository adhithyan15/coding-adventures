# طلاء (ṭilāʾ) — a coat of paint

## You'll want to know: طلاء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C552-TARMIM552-01]; assesses=[] -->

**طلاء** — *ṭilāʾ* — "a coat of paint".

> جف الطلاء الأزرق على الباب.
>
> *jaffa aṭ-ṭilāʾ al-azraq ʿalā al-bāb.*
>
> The blue paint dried on the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طلاء** (*ṭilāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-l-y*, "to smear, to coat" (Lane). Ṭilāʾ is what is smeared on.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
