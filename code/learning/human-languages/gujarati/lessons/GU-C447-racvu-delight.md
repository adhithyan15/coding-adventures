# રાચવું (rācvũ) — to delight in; to revel in

## You'll want to know: રાચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-05]; assesses=[] -->

**રાચવું** — *rācvũ* — "to delight in; to revel in".

> બાળકો નવાં રમકડાંમાં રાચે છે.
>
> *bāḷko navā̃ ramakḍā̃mā̃ rāce che.*
>
> The children are delighting in their new toys.

Past (he …): તે રમતમાં રાચ્યો, *te ramatmā̃ rācyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રાચવું** (*rācvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rajyatē*, "takes pleasure in"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be pleased" (CDIAL 10584).
