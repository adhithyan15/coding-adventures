# دکلمه کردن (dekleme kardan) — to recite (poetry)

## You'll want to know: دکلمه کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C522-VERSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دکلمه کردن** (*dekleme kardan*) — "to recite (poetry)".

> شاگردها در جشن شعر دکلمه کردند.
>
> *shâgerdhâ dar jashn she'r dekleme kardand.*
>
> The pupils recited poems at the celebration.

*dekleme* 'recitation' + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dekleme* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *déclamer*, 'to declaim'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *kardan* is inherited Persian.
