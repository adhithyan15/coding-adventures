## You'll want to know: بصمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C308-TAHQIQ308-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بصمة** — *baṣma* — "a fingerprint".

> وجدت الشرطة بصمة على الباب.
>
> *wajadat ash-shurṭa baṣma ʿalā al-bāb.*
>
> The police found a fingerprint on the door.

Plural بصمات. بصمة الإصبع in full.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بصمة** (*baṣma*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-ṣ-m* (Lane), where *buṣm* is the span between the little finger and the third finger spread apart. *Baṣma*, a fingerprint, is a modern sense.
