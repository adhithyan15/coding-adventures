## You'll want to know: el desfile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C559-DESFILE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el desfile** — "the parade".

> Vimos el desfile desde la ventana.
>
> *We watched the parade from the window.*

*Un desfile de moda* — a fashion show.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desfile* is built on *desfilar* (EtymDB), from French *défiler* 'to march in a line', built on *fil* 'thread', from Latin *fīlum*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
