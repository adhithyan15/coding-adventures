## You'll want to know: ख़ातिर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C345-UDDESHYA345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ातिर** — *khātir* — "for the sake of (की ख़ातिर)".

> मीरा ने परिवार की ख़ातिर नौकरी छोड़ी।
>
> *Mīrā ne parivār kī khātir naukrī choṛī.*
>
> Mira gave up her job for the sake of her family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ातिर** (*khātir*) is usually traced to Arabic *khāṭir*, "the mind, regard", which reached Hindi through Persian.
