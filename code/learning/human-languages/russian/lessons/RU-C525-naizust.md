## You'll want to know: наизусть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C525-POETRY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**наизусть** (*naizúst*) — "by heart".

> Бабушка знает наизусть много стихов.
>
> *Bábushka znáyet naizúst mnógo stikhóv.*
>
> Grandma knows a lot of poems by heart.

Adverb. *Учить наизусть* — to learn by heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наизусть* is usually explained as *на*, *из* and the old word *уста* 'lips, mouth', so it means 'from the mouth'; EtymDB has no entry for *наизусть*, and *уста* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *usta* (EtymDB).
