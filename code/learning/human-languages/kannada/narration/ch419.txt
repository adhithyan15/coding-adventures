Kannada, chapter 419: Success and Failure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಿಫಲವಾಗು (viphalavāgu) — to fall through; to fail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for permanent, then the Kannada for to modify.

You'll want to know: ವಿಫಲವಾಗು.
ವಿಫಲವಾಗು — viphalavāgu — "to fall through; to fail".
ಮಳೆ ಬಂದಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನಮ್ಮ ಯೋಜನೆ ವಿಫಲವಾಯಿತು. maḷe bandiddarinda namma yōjane viphalavāyitu. Because it rained, our plan failed.
Past (it …): ವಿಫಲವಾಯಿತು, viphalavāyitu.
ವಿಫಲ, fruitless; ವಿಫಲತೆ, failure.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಫಲವಾಗು (viphalavāgu) is built from Sanskrit viphala, "fruitless, vain" (CDIAL 11803), with native āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕೈಗೂಡು (kaigūḍu) — to come true; to work out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to fall through, then the Kannada for to bear fruit.

You'll want to know: ಕೈಗೂಡು.
ಕೈಗೂಡು — kaigūḍu — "to come true; to work out".
ಮನೆ ಕಟ್ಟುವ ಅವರ ಕನಸು ಕೈಗೂಡಿತು. mane kaṭṭuva avara kanasu kaigūḍitu. Their dream of building a house came true.
Past (it …): ಕೈಗೂಡಿತು, kaigūḍitu.
Of plans and wishes, never of people.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೈಗೂಡು (kaigūḍu) is native Dravidian, built from kai, "hand" (DEDR 2023), and kūḍu, "to join" (DEDR 1882): to come into the hand.
