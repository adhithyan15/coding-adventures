Lesson 1 of 5.
ટુકડી (ṭukḍī) — a squad; a small party (of police or soldiers).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to applaud, then the Gujarati for pride.

You'll want to know: ટુકડી.
ટુકડી — ṭukḍī — "a squad; a small party (of police or soldiers)".
પોલીસની એક ટુકડી જંગલમાં ગઈ. polīsnī ek ṭukḍī jangalmā̃ gaī. A police squad went into the forest.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટુકડી (ṭukḍī) is built on ṭukḍo, a piece, which CDIAL lists under ṭukk, "small", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5466): a small piece of a larger body of people.

You'll want to know: જીવલેણ.
જીવલેણ — jīvleṇ — "deadly".
આ જંગલમાં જીવલેણ સાપ છે. ā jangalmā̃ jīvleṇ sāp che. There are deadly snakes in this forest.

The word, taken apart.
જીવલેણ (jīvleṇ) is built from jīv, life, from Sanskrit jīvá, "living; life" (CDIAL 5239), and leṇ, a taking, from લેવું: what takes a life. જીવલેણ સાપ, a deadly snake.

Lesson 3 of 5.
સહાય (sahāy) — assistance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a squad, then the Gujarati for deadly.

You'll want to know: સહાય.
સહાય — sahāy — "assistance".
ટુકડીએ ગામના લોકોની સહાય લીધી. ṭukḍīe gāmnā lokonī sahāy līdhī. The squad took the villagers' help.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સહાય (sahāy) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sahāya, "a companion, a helper" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the help itself, a more formal word than મદદ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
મૂંઝાવું (mū̃jhāvũ) — to be at a loss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for deadly, then the Gujarati for assistance.

You'll want to know: મૂંઝાવું.
મૂંઝાવું — mū̃jhāvũ — "to be at a loss".
જંગલમાં બે રસ્તા જોઈને ટુકડી મૂંઝાઈ ગઈ. jangalmā̃ be rastā joīne ṭukḍī mū̃jhāī gaī. When the squad saw two paths in the forest, it was at a loss.
Past (he …): તે મૂંઝાઈ ગયો, te mū̃jhāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
મૂંઝાવું (mū̃jhāvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit múhyati, "is bewildered", and CDIAL lists Gujarati mujhāvũ, "to be perplexed" (CDIAL 10230). It is not knowing what to do next: મૂંઝાઈ ગઈ.

Lesson 5 of 5.
વિખેરાવું (vikherāvũ) — to scatter; to disperse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for assistance, then the Gujarati for to be at a loss.

You'll want to know: વિખેરાવું.
વિખેરાવું — vikherāvũ — "to scatter; to disperse".
પોલીસ આવી ત્યારે ટોળું વિખેરાઈ ગયું. polīs āvī tyāre ṭoḷũ vikherāī gayũ. When the police came, the crowd dispersed.
Past (it …): તે ટોળું વિખેરાઈ ગયું, te ṭoḷũ vikherāī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
વિખેરાવું (vikherāvũ) is the intransitive partner of vikhervũ, to scatter, which CDIAL lists under viṣkirati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11985). A crowd or a cloud વિખેરાઈ જાય છે: it breaks up.
