## You'll want to know: तैयार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C538-PRABANDH538-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तैयार** — *taiyār* — "ready; prepared".

> खाना तैयार है, सब लोग आइए।
>
> *Khānā taiyār hai, sab log āie.*
>
> The food is ready; come along, everyone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तैयार** (*taiyār*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṭayyār*, "swift, flying", through Persian, where it came to mean "ready".
