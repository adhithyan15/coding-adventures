## You'll want to know: उपयोगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C341-PRAYOJANA341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उपयोगः** — *upayogaḥ* — "a use".

> एतस्य यन्त्रस्य उपयोगः कः?
>
> *etasya yantrasya upayogaḥ kaḥ?*
>
> What is the use of this machine?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपयोगः** (*upayogaḥ*) is built on *upa*, "towards", and the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives *upa-yoga* the senses "employment, use, application" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
