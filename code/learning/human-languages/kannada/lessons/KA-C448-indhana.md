# ಇಂಧನ (indhana) — fuel

## You'll want to know: ಇಂಧನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C448-INDHANA448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಂಧನ** — *indhana* — "fuel".

> ವಿಮಾನಕ್ಕೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಇಂಧನ ಬೇಕು.
>
> *vimānakke tumbā indhana bēku.*
>
> An aeroplane needs a lot of fuel.

Petrol, diesel, gas and firewood are all ಇಂಧನ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇಂಧನ** (*indhana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *indhana*, "lighting, kindling, fuel" (CDIAL 1584), from *indh*, "to kindle".
