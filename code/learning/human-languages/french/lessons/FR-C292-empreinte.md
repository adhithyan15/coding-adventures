## You'll want to know: l'empreinte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C292-INDICES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'empreinte** — "the print, the fingerprint".

> Il y avait des empreintes sur le verre.
>
> *There were prints on the glass.*

*Les empreintes digitales* — fingerprints; *l'empreinte carbone* — the carbon footprint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*empreinte* is the past participle of Old French *empreindre* "to press in", which is inherited from Latin *imprimere* "to press into" (Lewis & Short).
