# Your own line — the first writing with no model

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-MI-CHIAMO-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Everything you have written so far had something to reach for. You
traced **ciao**, copied it, wrote it with the model covered, and wrote it from
the sound.

[PAUSE 1s] This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed:
only what this book has taught you.

## Writing — controlled composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-MI-CHIAMO-04, IT-GRAMMAR-IL-MIO-NOME-02, IT-LEX-ESSERE-02, IT-GRAMMAR-ESSERE-03] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **one line** about yourself, on paper: your name, then one
more fact about you. You hold two ways to give a name — the reflexive verb, and
*my name is* with its article kept. Choose one; nobody is choosing for you.

[PAUSE 2s] One thing to decide deliberately rather than by accident before you
start: **the accent on è is a letter, not decoration.**

[PAUSE 2s] With its accent, **è** is *is*. Without it, **e** is *and*. A hand
that drops the accent has not written Italian untidily: *il mio nome e Marco*
says "my name and Marco", and leaves the reader to guess the verb you meant.

[PAUSE 3s] Write the line. Then read it back and check every **e** — does it
mean *is* or *and*? — **before** you check anything else.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-ESSERE-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Is the accent on **è** decoration? (**No** — without it the word is
*and*.)

[PAUSE 2s] And what did you choose here that the earlier lessons chose for you?
(**The words.**)
