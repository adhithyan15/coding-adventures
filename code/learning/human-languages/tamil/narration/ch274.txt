Tamil, chapter 274: Music, Song, Story, God, Animal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
இசை (icai) — music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for twins, then the Tamil for a brother-in-law.

You'll want to know: இசை.
இசை — icai — "music".

The word, taken apart.
இசை (icai) is native Dravidian. DEDR 470 lists Malayalam ica, "song, music". The entry glosses the Tamil verb icai as "to sound, to sing".

Lesson 5 of 5.
விலங்கு (vilaṅku) — an animal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a story, then the Tamil for God.

You'll want to know: விலங்கு.
விலங்கு — vilaṅku — "an animal".

The word, taken apart.
விலங்கு (vilaṅku) is usually explained as native vilaṅku, "to lie across" (DEDR 5423): an animal's body lies crosswise, unlike a person's.
