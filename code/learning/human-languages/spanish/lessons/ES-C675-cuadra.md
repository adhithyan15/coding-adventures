## You'll want to know: la cuadra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C675-AFUERAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la cuadra** — "the block (of a street) (Latin America); the stable".

> El hotel está a dos cuadras.
>
> *The hotel is two blocks away.*

Spain says *la manzana* for a block; there *la cuadra* is a stable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cuadra* comes from Latin *quadra* "a square" (Lewis & Short): a city block is a square of streets.
