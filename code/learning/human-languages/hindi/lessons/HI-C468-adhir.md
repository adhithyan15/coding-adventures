## You'll want to know: अधीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C468-DHAIRYA468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अधीर** — *adhīr* — "impatient".

> ट्रेन देर से आई, इसलिए सब यात्री अधीर थे।
>
> *ṭren der se āī, isliye sab yātrī adhīr the.*
>
> The train came late, so all the passengers were impatient.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधीर** (*adhīr*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *adhīra*, "not steady, excitable", built on *a-*, "not", and *dhīra*, "steady" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for someone who cannot wait.
