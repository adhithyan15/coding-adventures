## You'll want to know: വെയിറ്റർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C483-SEVANAM483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വെയിറ്റർ** — *veyiṟṟar* — "a waiter".

> വെയിറ്റർ ഞങ്ങൾക്ക് ചൂടുള്ള ചായ കൊണ്ടുവന്നു.
>
> *veyiṟṟar ñaṅṅaḷkkŭ cūṭuḷḷa cāya koṇṭuvannu.*
>
> The waiter brought us hot tea.

In a small eating place people call out ചേട്ടാ to him instead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വെയിറ്റർ** (*veyiṟṟar*) was borrowed from English *waiter*, a person who serves food at tables.
