## You'll want to know: ಸೈನ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C336-SAINYA336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೈನ್ಯ** — *sainya* — "an army".

> ರಾಜನ ಸೈನ್ಯ ನದಿಯನ್ನು ದಾಟಿತು.
>
> *rājana sainya nadiyannu dāṭitu.*
>
> The king's army crossed the river.

A soldier is a ಸೈನಿಕ; the army is the ಸೈನ್ಯ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೈನ್ಯ** (*sainya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sainya*, "belonging to an army", and as a noun "an army" (CDIAL 13602), from *senā*, "an army".
