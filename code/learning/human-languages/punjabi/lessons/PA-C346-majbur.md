## You'll want to know: ਮਜਬੂਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C346-SORRY346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਜਬੂਰ** — *majbūr* — "helpless; forced by circumstances".

> ਮੈਂ ਮਜਬੂਰ ਹਾਂ ਅਤੇ ਅੱਜ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਵਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ majbūr hāṁ ate ajj nahīṁ āvāṅgā.*
>
> I can't help it, and I won't come today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਜਬੂਰ** (*majbūr*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *majbūr*, "compelled".
