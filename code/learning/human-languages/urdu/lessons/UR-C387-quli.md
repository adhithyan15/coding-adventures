## You'll want to know: قلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C387-QULI387-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قلی** — *qulī* — "a porter".

> اسٹیشن پر قلی لال قمیض پہنتے ہیں۔
>
> *isṭeshan par qulī lāl qamīz pahante haiṅ.*
>
> At the station the porters wear red shirts.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قلی** (*qulī*) is often traced to Tamil *kūli*, "wages, hire" (DEDR 1905): the porter is the man paid by the load. Other sources have been proposed, so the link is likely rather than certain.
