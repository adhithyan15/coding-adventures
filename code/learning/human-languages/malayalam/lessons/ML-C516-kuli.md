## You'll want to know: കുഴി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C516-TATASSAM516-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കുഴി** — *kuḻi* — "a pothole; a pit".

> വഴിയിലെ വലിയ കുഴിയിൽ ബൈക്ക് വീണു.
>
> *vaḻiyile valiya kuḻiyil baikkŭ vīṇu.*
>
> The motorbike fell into a big pothole in the road.

Plural: കുഴികൾ, *kuḻikaḷ*.

Any hole in the ground: a pit, a grave, a pothole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുഴി** (*kuḻi*) is native Dravidian, "a hollow, a hole, a pit" (DEDR 1818).
