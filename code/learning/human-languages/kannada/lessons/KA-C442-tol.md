## You'll want to know: ಟೋಲ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C442-HEDDARI442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟೋಲ್** — *ṭōl* — "a toll".

> ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವು ಎರಡು ಕಡೆ ಟೋಲ್ ಕಟ್ಟಿದೆವು.
>
> *heddāriyalli nāvu eraḍu kaḍe ṭōl kaṭṭidevu.*
>
> We paid a toll at two places on the highway.

ಟೋಲ್ ಕಟ್ಟು, to pay a toll; ಟೋಲ್ ಗೇಟ್, a toll gate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟೋಲ್** (*ṭōl*) is a loan from English *toll*, a charge for using a road or a bridge.
