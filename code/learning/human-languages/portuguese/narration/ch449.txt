Portuguese, chapter 449: Lojas pequenas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a drogaria — the household goods shop (cleaning things, paint).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the scratch card, then the Portuguese for to leaf through.

You'll want to know: a drogaria.
a drogaria — "the household goods shop (cleaning things, paint)".
Comprei lâmpadas e sacos do lixo na drogaria. I bought light bulbs and bin bags at the household shop.
Not a chemist: medicine is at a farmácia.

The word, taken apart.
drogaria is built on droga with -aria; droga came from French drogue (EtymDB), probably from Low German droge 'dry (goods)'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

retrosaria is built on retrós 'silk thread' with -aria; the origin of retrós is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a perfumaria — the perfume shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the household goods shop, then the Portuguese for the haberdashery.

You'll want to know: a perfumaria.
a perfumaria — "the perfume shop".
Ela trabalha numa perfumaria do centro comercial. She works in a perfume shop in the shopping centre.
Built on o perfume.

The word, taken apart.
perfumaria is built on perfume, which probably came through Italian or French from Latin per 'through' and fūmus 'smoke' (Lewis & Short): first a scent rising in smoke.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a ourivesaria — the jeweller's shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the perfume shop, then the Portuguese for the herbalist's shop.

You'll want to know: a ourivesaria.
a ourivesaria — "the jeweller's shop".
O anel veio de uma ourivesaria antiga. The ring came from an old jeweller's.
O ourives — the goldsmith.

The word, taken apart.
ourivesaria is built on ourives 'goldsmith' with -aria (EtymDB); ourives is inherited from Latin aurificem, from aurum 'gold' and facere 'to make'.
