Gujarati, chapter 181: Hurry and delay.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઉતાવળ કરવી (utāvaḷ karvī) — to hurry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to stay on, then the Gujarati for to pass.

You'll want to know: ઉતાવળ કરવી.
ઉતાવળ કરવી — utāvaḷ karvī — "to hurry".
આજે તે ઉતાવળ કરશે. — āje te utāvaḷ karśe — "Today she will be in a hurry."

The word, taken apart.
ઉતાવળ કરવી (utāvaḷ karvī) is built from the feminine noun utāvaḷ, "haste", and karvī, the feminine of karvũ, "to do". CDIAL lists utāvaḷ, glossed "impetuous", under uttāpala, a reconstructed form on the root tap, "to heat" (CDIAL 1788).

Lesson 2 of 5.
મુલતવી રાખવું (multavī rākhvũ) — to postpone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pass, then the Gujarati for to hurry.

You'll want to know: મુલતવી રાખવું.
મુલતવી રાખવું — multavī rākhvũ — "to postpone".
અમે મુલાકાત મુલતવી રાખીશું. — ame mulākāt multavī rākhīśũ — "We will postpone the meeting."

The word, taken apart.
મુલતવી રાખવું (multavī rākhvũ) is built from multavī, "postponed", and the inherited rākhvũ, "to keep". Multavī is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic multawī, "twisted", on the root l-w-y, "to twist; to wait for" (Lane); Lane does not give the sense "postponed".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ટાળવું (ṭāḷvũ) — to avoid, to put off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hurry, then the Gujarati for to postpone.

You'll want to know: ટાળવું.
ટાળવું — ṭāḷvũ — "to avoid, to put off".
તે ફરી કામ ટાળશે. — te pharī kām ṭāḷśe — "He will dodge the work again."

The word, taken apart.
ટાળવું (ṭāḷvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit ṭālayati, the causative of ṭalati, "moves aside", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to avoid" (CDIAL 5450).

Lesson 4 of 5.
રદ કરવું (rad karvũ) — to cancel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to postpone, then the Gujarati for to avoid.

You'll want to know: રદ કરવું.
રદ કરવું — rad karvũ — "to cancel".
વરસાદમાં તેઓ કાર્યક્રમ રદ કરશે. — varsādmā̃ teo kāryakram rad karśe — "In the rain they will cancel the programme."

The word, taken apart.
રદ કરવું (rad karvũ) is built from rad, "cancelled", and the inherited karvũ, "to do". Rad is a loan from Arabic radd, "a turning back, a rejecting", through Persian; its root is r-d-d, "to send back" (Lane).

You'll want to know: લંબાવવું.
લંબાવવું — lambāvvũ — "to extend, to lengthen".
અમે રજા બે દિવસ લંબાવીશું. — ame rajā be divas lambāvīśũ — "We will extend the holiday by two days."
Built on લાંબું, lā̃bũ, long.

The word, taken apart.
લંબાવવું (lambāvvũ) is built on the inherited lā̃bũ, "long", with the causative -āv-. CDIAL traces lā̃bũ to Sanskrit lamba, "pendent" (CDIAL 10951).
