Persian, chapter 403: Degrees and graduation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فارغ التحصیل (fâregh ot-tahsil) — a graduate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a thesis, then the Persian for a lecture.

You'll want to know: فارغ التحصیل.
فارغ التحصیل (fâregh ot-tahsil) — "a graduate".
او سال گذشته فارغ التحصیل شد. u sâl-e gozashte fâregh ot-tahsil shod. He graduated last year.
An Arabic phrase, literally "free of study". Fâregh ot-tahsil shodan — to graduate.

The word, taken apart.
فارغ التحصیل (fâregh ot-tahsil) is usually traced to the Arabic phrase fārigh al-taḥṣīl, "free of study": fārigh "free, done" + al- "the" + taḥṣīl "study". A graduate has finished studying.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تحصیلات (tahsilât) — education, schooling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lecture, then the Persian for a graduate.

You'll want to know: تحصیلات.
تحصیلات (tahsilât) — "education, schooling".
پدرم تحصیلات دانشگاهی ندارد. pedaram tahsilât-e dâneshgâhi nadârad. My father does not have a university education.
The Arabic-style plural of tahsil "study".

The word, taken apart.
تحصیلات (tahsilât) is the Arabic-style plural of tahsil "study", usually traced to Arabic taḥṣīl "obtaining, acquiring": education is what you have acquired.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ارائه دادن (erâ'e dâdan) — to present, to submit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a graduate, then the Persian for education.

You'll want to know: ارائه دادن.
ارائه دادن (erâ'e dâdan) — "to present, to submit".
هر دانشجو باید یک مقاله ارائه دهد. har dâneshju bâyad yek maqâle erâ'e dahad. Every student must present a paper.
Erâ'e "presentation" + dâdan (stem deh-). Formal; spelled with ئ.

The word, taken apart.
ارائه is usually traced to Arabic irā'a "showing"; with dâdan "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE deh₃-, it means to put something before people.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لیسانس (lisâns) — a bachelor's degree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for education, then the Persian for to present.

You'll want to know: لیسانس.
لیسانس (lisâns) — "a bachelor's degree".
او لیسانس زبان انگلیسی دارد. u lisâns-e zabân-e engelisi dârad. She has a bachelor's degree in English.
A French loan; the official term is kârshenâsi. Fowq-e lisâns — a master's degree.

The word, taken apart.
لیسانس (lisâns) is usually traced to French licence, a university degree, from Latin licentia "freedom, leave" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
دکترا (doktorâ) — a doctorate, a PhD.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to present, then the Persian for a bachelor's degree.

You'll want to know: دکترا.
دکترا (doktorâ) — "a doctorate, a PhD".
عمویم دکترای شیمی دارد. 'amuyam doktorâ-ye shimi dârad. My uncle has a doctorate in chemistry.
Doktorâ gereftan — to get a PhD.

The word, taken apart.
دکترا (doktorâ) is usually traced to French doctorat "doctorate", built on Latin doctor "teacher" (Lewis & Short), from docere "to teach".
