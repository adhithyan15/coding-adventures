## You'll want to know: في تلك الليلة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C327-LAYLA327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**في تلك الليلة** — *fī tilka al-layla* — "that night".

> في تلك الليلة لم ينم أحد في البيت.
>
> *fī tilka al-layla lam yanam aḥad fī al-bayt.*
>
> That night nobody in the house slept.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**في تلك الليلة** (*fī tilka al-layla*) is "in that night": *layla*, one night, is built on the inherited Semitic root *l-y-l* (Lane), the root of the taught *layl*; EtymDB sets it beside Hebrew *laylā* and Aramaic *lēlyā*.
