## You'll want to know: tararear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C753-MUSICA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tararear** — "to hum".

> Siempre tarareo en la ducha.
>
> *I always hum in the shower.*

To sing a tune without the words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tararear* comes from the syllables *ta-ra-ra* people sing when they do not know the words; it is an imitative word.
