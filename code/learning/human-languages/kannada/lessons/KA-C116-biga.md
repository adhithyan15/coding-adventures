## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೀಗ** (*bīga*) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil *pīkam* and Telugu *bīgamu* (DEDR 4211). DEDR lists Kannada *bīga*, "lock", with Tamil *pīkam*, "padlock".
