## You'll want to know: shāngxīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shāngxīn* — "sad; heartbroken".

> *tā hěn shāngxīn* — He's heartbroken.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shāngxīn* is built from *shāng* 'to hurt' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syang* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to harm'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
