## You'll want to know: ਤੋਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C273-WEIGH273-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੋਲਣਾ** — *tolṇā* — "to weigh".

> ਦੁਕਾਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੁੰਡਾ ਆਲੂ ਤੋਲਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *dukān vich muṇḍā ālū toldā hai.*
>
> In the shop the boy weighs the potatoes.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਲੂ ਤੋਲੇ, *maiṁ ālū tole*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੋਲਣਾ** (*tolṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tolayati*, "lifts, weighs"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *toḷnā*, "to weigh", under it (CDIAL 5979).
