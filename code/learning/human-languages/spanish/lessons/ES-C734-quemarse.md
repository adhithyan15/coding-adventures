## You'll want to know: quemarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C734-HERIDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**quemarse** — "to burn oneself; to get sunburnt".

> Me quemé la mano con la sartén.
>
> *I burned my hand on the frying pan.*

*Quemarse* + body part. *Quemarse al sol* — to get sunburnt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quemarse* is the reflexive of *quemar*, which is inherited from Latin *cremāre* "to burn" (EtymDB); English *cremate* is the same Latin verb.
