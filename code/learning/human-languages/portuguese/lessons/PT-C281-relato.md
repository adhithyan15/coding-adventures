## You'll want to know: o relato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C281-RELATAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o relato** — "the account, the narration".

> O relato dela foi muito claro.
>
> *Her account was very clear.*

A spoken or written telling of events.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*relato* is a learned borrowing from Latin *relātus* "a telling, a narration" (EtymDB), from *referre* "to bring back, to report".
