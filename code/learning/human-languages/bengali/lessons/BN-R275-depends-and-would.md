# Review — it depends, and what would be

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "It depends on the weather", then "If I had time, I would go". (*eṭā ābôhāoyār opôr nirbhôr kôre*; *jôdi sômôy thāktô, tāhôle āmi jetām*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-ON-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-USED-TO-01, BN-LEX-C275-SONGGE-01, BN-SCRIPT-C275-UMA-01, BN-LEX-C274-DHOKA-01, BN-SCRIPT-C274-DDHA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-THEN-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-NOT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C274-LE-FORM-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C274-NAHOLE-01, BN-LEX-C229-JINIS229-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Will we go tomorrow? It depends on the weather. If Mira doesn't come, we won't go.]
- [YOU SAY: It's up to you. If you come, I'll come with you.]
- [YOU SAY: If I had money, I would buy a boat.]
- [YOU SAY: In the old days I used to go to the river with Grandfather every day.]
- [YOU SAY: We went into the shop with Mira.]
- [YOU WRITE: **সঙ্গে**, then **ঢোকা**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-DEPENDS-ON-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-WOULD-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C275-USED-TO-01, BN-LEX-C275-SONGGE-01, BN-SCRIPT-C275-UMA-01] -->
