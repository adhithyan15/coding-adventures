## You'll want to know: l'intervista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C283-REDAZIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'intervista** — "the interview".

> L'intervista è durata un'ora.
>
> *The interview lasted an hour.*

*Fare un'intervista a qualcuno* — to interview someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*intervista* is usually said to be modelled on English *interview*, which goes back to French *entrevue* "a meeting", from *entrevoir* "to glimpse"; the root is Latin *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
