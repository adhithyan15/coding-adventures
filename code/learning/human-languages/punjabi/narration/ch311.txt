Punjabi, chapter 311: Gone Missing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਭਾਲਣਾ (bhālṇā) — to seek; to hunt for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to break something, then the Punjabi for to get spilt.

You'll want to know: ਭਾਲਣਾ.
ਭਾਲਣਾ — bhālṇā — "to seek; to hunt for".
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਪਣੀ ਚਾਬੀ ਭਾਲੀ। kall maiṁ bāzār vich āpṇī chābī bhālī. Yesterday I hunted for my key in the market.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਪਣੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਭਾਲੀ, maiṁ kamre vich āpṇī kitāb bhālī.

The word, taken apart.
ਭਾਲਣਾ (bhālṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit bhālayate, "describes, beholds"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bhālṇā, "to seek", under it (CDIAL 9474).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਖੋਹਣਾ (khohṇā) — to snatch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a thief, then the Punjabi for to run away.

You'll want to know: ਖੋਹਣਾ.
ਖੋਹਣਾ — khohṇā — "to snatch".
ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚੇ ਦੇ ਹੱਥ ਤੋਂ ਚਾਕੂ ਖੋਹਿਆ। maiṁ bachche de hatth ton chākū khohiā. I snatched the knife out of the child's hand.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੁੱਤੇ ਤੋਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖੋਹੀ, maiṁ kutte ton roṭī khohī.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੋਹਣਾ (khohṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed skoṣati, "plucks out, pokes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khohṇā, "to take away by force", under it (CDIAL 13661).
