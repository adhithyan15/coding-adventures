## You'll want to know: માત્રા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C485-DAKHAL485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**માત્રા** — *mātrā* — "a dose".

> દવાની માત્રા દાક્તરને પૂછીને લો.
>
> *davānī mātrā dāktarne pūchīne lo.*
>
> Ask the doctor about the dose before you take the medicine.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માત્રા** (*mātrā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mātrā*, "measure, quantity, size", as Monier-Williams records it.
