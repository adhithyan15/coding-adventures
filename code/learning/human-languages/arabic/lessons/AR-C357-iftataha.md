# افتتح (iftataḥa) — to inaugurate, to open officially

## You'll want to know: افتتح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C357-KHITAB357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**افتتح** — *iftataḥa* — "to inaugurate, to open officially".

> افتتح الوزير مستشفى جديدا.
>
> *iftataḥa al-wazīr mustashfā jadīdan.*
>
> The minister opened a new hospital.

Present: يفتتح, *yaftatiḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افتتح** (*iftataḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-t-ḥ* (Lane), "to open", the root of the taught *fataḥa*. It is Form VIII, "to make a beginning".
