## You'll want to know: चेचक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C487-SANKRAMAN487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चेचक** — *cecak* — "smallpox".

> टीके की वजह से अब चेचक नहीं होती।
>
> *ṭīke kī vajah se ab cecak nahīṃ hotī.*
>
> Thanks to vaccination, smallpox no longer occurs.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेचक** (*cecak*) is usually traced to Persian *chēchak*, "smallpox".
