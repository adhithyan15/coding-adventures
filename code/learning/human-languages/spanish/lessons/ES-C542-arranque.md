## You'll want to know: el arranque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C542-COMIENZO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el arranque** — "the start; the outburst".

> El arranque del curso fue difícil.
>
> *The start of the school year was hard.*

A car's starter is also *el arranque*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arranque* is built on *arrancar* 'to pull out, to start'. The origin of *arrancar* is not settled.

## What you've built
