## You'll want to know: आधारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C342-ADHARA342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आधारः** — *ādhāraḥ* — "a basis; a support".

> तव मतस्य आधारः कः?
>
> *tava matasya ādhāraḥ kaḥ?*
>
> What is the basis of your opinion?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आधारः** (*ādhāraḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √dhṛ, "to hold, to support"; MW gives *ā-dhāra* the senses "support, prop, substratum" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
