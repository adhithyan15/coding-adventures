# સળવળવું (saḷvaḷvũ) — to stir; to fidget

## You'll want to know: સળવળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C454-UCAT454-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સળવળવું** — *saḷvaḷvũ* — "to stir; to fidget".

> ચિંતાને લીધે અરુણ આખી રાત પથારીમાં સળવળતો હતો.
>
> *cintāne līdhe Aruṇ ākhī rāt pathārīmā̃ saḷvaḷto hato.*
>
> Arun was tossing and fidgeting in bed all night with worry.

Past (he …): તે પથારીમાં સળવળ્યો, *te pathārīmā̃ saḷvaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સળવળવું** (*saḷvaḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *salati*, "moves"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb beside *saḷvaḷ*, "writhing about" (CDIAL 13284).
