## You'll want to know: bīnguǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C200-HOTEL200-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bīnguǎn* — "hotel".

> *wǒmen de bīnguǎn hěn gānjìng* — Our hotel is very clean.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bīnguǎn* is built from *bīn* 'a guest' and *guǎn* 'a lodging', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjin* and *kwanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to welcome' and the second as a lodging house.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
