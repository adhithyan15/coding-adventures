Sanskrit, chapter 310: What Happened Today.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घटना (ghaṭanā) — an event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for main, then the Sanskrit for a news reporter.

You'll want to know: घटना.
घटना — ghaṭanā — "an event".
अद्य नगरे एका घटना घटिता। adya nagare ekā ghaṭanā ghaṭitā. Something happened in the city today.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
घटना (ghaṭanā) comes from the root √ghaṭ, which also gives ghaṭate, "happens"; MW gives ghaṭanā as exertion, motion and a manner of acting, and an event is its modern sense (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
आपतति (āpatati) — befalls.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a news reporter, then the Sanskrit for an event.

You'll want to know: आपतति.
आपतति — āpatati — "befalls".
ग्रामे सहसा संकटम् आपतति। grāme sahasā saṅkaṭam āpatati. Danger suddenly befalls the village.
Past (it …): संकटं सहसा आपतितवत्, saṅkaṭaṃ sahasā āpatitavat.

The word, taken apart.
आपतति (āpatati) is built from ā, "towards", and the root √pat, "to fall, fly", as in patati; MW gives ā-pat as to fall upon, happen, befall (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
विवरणम् (vivaraṇam) — a detailed account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an event, then the Sanskrit for befalls.

You'll want to know: विवरणम्.
विवरणम् — vivaraṇam — "a detailed account".
वार्ताहरः घटनायाः विवरणं ददाति। vārtāharaḥ ghaṭanāyāḥ vivaraṇaṃ dadāti. The reporter gives a detailed account of the event.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
विवरणम् (vivaraṇam) is built from vi, "apart", and the root √vṛ, "to cover": an uncovering, a laying open, and MW also gives vivaraṇa as an explanation or exposition; a detailed account of an event is its modern use (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
प्रकाशयति (prakāśayati) — publishes; makes known.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for befalls, then the Sanskrit for a detailed account.

You'll want to know: प्रकाशयति.
प्रकाशयति — prakāśayati — "publishes; makes known".
पत्रिका घटनायाः विवरणं प्रकाशयति। patrikā ghaṭanāyāḥ vivaraṇaṃ prakāśayati. The newspaper publishes a detailed account of the event.
Past (it …): पत्रिका विवरणं प्रकाशितवती, patrikā vivaraṇaṃ prakāśitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
प्रकाशयति (prakāśayati) is built from pra and the causative of the root √kāś, "to shine", as in prakāśa, "light"; MW gives it as to make visible, display, reveal, proclaim, and to publish is its modern use (MW).

विशेषः (viśeṣaḥ) is built from vi and the root √śiṣ, "to leave, distinguish"; MW gives viśeṣa as a distinction, a peculiar mark, a peculiarity (MW).
