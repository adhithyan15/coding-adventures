## You'll want to know: 成果
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C395-RESULT395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**成果** — *chéngguǒ* — "fruits of work; achievements".

> **我们今年取得了很好的成果** *wǒmen jīnnián qǔdé le hěn hǎo de chéngguǒ* — We got good results this year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**成果** (*chéngguǒ*) is built from 成 'to complete' and 果 'fruit', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *kwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the fruit of a tree' and 'resolute'.
