Gujarati, chapter 199: Queues and crowds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભીડ (bhīḍ) — crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ban, then the Gujarati for danger.

You'll want to know: ભીડ.
ભીડ — bhīḍ — "crowd".
બજારમાં ઘણી ભીડ છે. — bajārmā̃ ghaṇī bhīḍ che — "The market is very crowded."

The word, taken apart.
ભીડ (bhīḍ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "crowd", under bhiṭ, "meet, throng", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9490).

Lesson 2 of 5.
કતાર (katār) — queue, line.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for danger, then the Gujarati for crowd.

You'll want to know: કતાર.
કતાર — katār — "queue, line".
બેંકમાં લાંબી કતાર છે. — bẽkmā̃ lā̃bī katār che — "There is a long queue at the bank."

The word, taken apart.
કતાર (katār) is a loan from Arabic qiṭār, "a line of camels", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-ṭ-r (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
નિયમ (niyam) — rule, regulation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for crowd, then the Gujarati for queue.

You'll want to know: નિયમ.
નિયમ — niyam — "rule, regulation".
આ બેંકનો નિયમ છે. — ā bẽkno niyam che — "This is the bank's rule."

The word, taken apart.
નિયમ (niyam) is Sanskrit niyama, "restraint, rule", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL lists the verb niyamayati, "restrains" (CDIAL 7251).
