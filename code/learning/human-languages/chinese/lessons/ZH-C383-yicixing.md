## You'll want to know: yícìxìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C383-ENERGY383-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yícìxìng* — "disposable; used once".

> *wǒmen zuìhǎo bú yòng yícìxìng kuàizi* — We'd better not use disposable chopsticks.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Tone: 一 is said yí here, rising, because cì after it is a falling tone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yícìxìng* is built from 一 'one', *cì* 'a time' and *xìng* 'nature', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit*, *tshijH* and *sjengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a stopping place' and 'an order'. The word is modern.
