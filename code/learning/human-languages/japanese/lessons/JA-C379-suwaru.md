## You'll want to know: すわる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C379-SHARYOU379-01]; assesses=[] -->

**すわる** — *suwaru* — "to sit down".

> **でんしゃで ははの となりに すわりました。** — *densha de haha no tonari ni suwarimashita* — "On the train I sat next to my mother."

Polite **すわります**, *suwarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おじいさんが ざせきに すわりました。**, *ojiisan ga zaseki ni suwarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すわる** (*suwaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
