## You'll want to know: बसेरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C416-KHEMA416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बसेरा** — *baserā* — "a roost; a night's shelter".

> शाम को चिड़ियाँ अपने बसेरे में लौटती हैं।
>
> *śām ko ciṛiyā̃ apne basere meṁ lauṭtī haiṁ.*
>
> In the evening the birds go back to their roost.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बसेरा** (*baserā*) goes back to *\*vāsakara*, "making a stay", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11594), which lists Hindi *baserā*, "a place of staying": a bird's roost, or a traveller's bed for one night.
