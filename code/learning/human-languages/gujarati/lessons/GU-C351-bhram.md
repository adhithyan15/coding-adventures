## You'll want to know: ભ્રમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C351-HAKIKAT351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ભ્રમ** — *bhram* — "an illusion".

> તે સાચું નથી, તારો ભ્રમ છે.
>
> *te sācũ nathī, tāro bhram che.*
>
> It isn't true; you are imagining it.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભ્રમ** (*bhram*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *bhrama*, "wandering about; confusion" (Monier-Williams).
