## You'll want to know: ఆత్మవిశ్వాసం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C510-GUNAM510-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆత్మవిశ్వాసం** — *ātmaviśvāsaṁ* — "self-confidence".

> మంచి మార్కులు రావడంతో అతనికి ఆత్మవిశ్వాసం పెరిగింది.
>
> *mañci mārkulu rāvaḍantō ataniki ātmaviśvāsaṁ perigindi.*
>
> With good marks his self-confidence grew.

ఆత్మ, self, + విశ్వాసం, trust: trust in yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆత్మవిశ్వాసం** (*ātmaviśvāsaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *ātman*, "breath, self" (CDIAL 1135), and *viśvāsa*, "trust, confidence" (CDIAL 11966).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
