Tamil, chapter 450: Real or Fake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அசல் (acal) — the original; genuine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an investment, then the Tamil for to pawn.

You'll want to know: அசல்.
அசல் — acal — "the original; genuine".
இது அசல் சான்றிதழ், நகல் அல்ல. itu acal cāṉṟitaḻ, nakal alla. This is the original certificate, not a copy.
The opposite is நகல், a copy.

The word, taken apart.
அசல் (acal) is a loan from Arabic aṣl, "root, origin", through Hindustani.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 2 of 5.
போலி (pōli) — a fake; an imitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to pawn, then the Tamil for the original.

You'll want to know: போலி.
போலி — pōli — "a fake; an imitation".
அந்தக் கடையில் போலிப் பொருட்களை விற்கிறார்கள். antak kaṭaiyil pōlip poruṭkaḷai viṟkiṟārkaḷ. They sell fake goods in that shop.
As a modifier: போலிப் பொருள், a fake item.

The word, taken apart.
போலி (pōli) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4597), "likeness, a pretence", from the root of போல், "like".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ஆடம்பரம் (āṭamparam) — luxury; show, pomp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for the original, then the Tamil for a fake.

You'll want to know: ஆடம்பரம்.
ஆடம்பரம் — āṭamparam — "luxury; show, pomp".
அவர்கள் திருமணத்தை ஆடம்பரம் இல்லாமல் நடத்தினார்கள். avarkaḷ tirumaṇattai āṭamparam illāmal naṭattiṉārkaḷ. They held the wedding without any show.
ஆடம்பரமான, luxurious, is the describing form.

The word, taken apart.
ஆடம்பரம் (āṭamparam) is borrowed from Sanskrit āḍambara, "a great noise, bombast" (Monier-Williams); in Tamil it came to mean show and pomp.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a fake, then the Tamil for luxury.

You'll want to know: விலைவாசி.
விலைவாசி — vilaivāci — "the cost of living; prices".
இந்த ஆண்டு விலைவாசி மிகவும் ஏறிவிட்டது. inta āṇṭu vilaivāci mikavum ēṟiviṭṭatu. This year prices have gone up a lot.
Built on விலை, a price.

The word, taken apart.
விலைவாசி (vilaivāci) is built on விலை, "a price" (DEDR 5421); the origin of its second part is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
நோட்டு (nōṭṭu) — a banknote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for luxury, then the Tamil for the cost of living.

You'll want to know: நோட்டு.
நோட்டு — nōṭṭu — "a banknote".
அவர் ஐநூறு ரூபாய் நோட்டைக் கொடுத்தார். avar ainūṟu rūpāy nōṭṭaik koṭuttār. He gave a five-hundred-rupee note.
Before -ஐ the final -உ drops: நோட்டை, the note (object).

The word, taken apart.
நோட்டு (nōṭṭu) is a loan from English note, with the doubled stop and final -உ Tamil gives loans.
