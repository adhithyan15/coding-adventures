# Review — advice and reasons

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, KA-GRAMMAR-C311-HAGAGI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ** aloud, and name its small letter. (*svaccha*; **ಛ**.) Then "So I stayed at home." (*hāgāgi nānu maneyallē idde*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C312-OBLIGATIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C312-PROHIBITIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C312-KARANA-CLAUSE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C312-REASON-ORDER-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C311-THINK-THAT-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C311-IN-MY-OPINION-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C311-SO-WORDS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: You have a fever. You should see a doctor.]
- [YOU SAY: You must not go to school today.]
- [YOU SAY: Because the bus did not come, I walked.]
- [YOU SAY: I must go early tomorrow, because Grandmother is coming.]
- [YOU SAY: I think we must clean the house.]
- [YOU SAY: The bus didn't come. That's why I came late.]
