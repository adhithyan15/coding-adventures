- [YOU SAY: "Two tickets to Kochi, please. How much is it?"]
- [YOU SAY: "What time does the train leave? Is there a seat?"]
- [YOU SAY: "I missed the bus. The train was an hour late."]
- [YOU SAY: "Oh no, my bag has got lost. Excuse me, could you help me?"]
- [YOU SAY: "Tomorrow suits me."]
- [YOU SAY: "Where is the station? How do I get there?"]
