## You'll want to know: संकट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C383-SANKAT383-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संकट** — *saṅkaṭ* — "a crisis".

> अगर बारिश नहीं होगी तो किसानों पर संकट आएगा।
>
> *agar bāriś nahīṃ hogī to kisānoṁ par saṅkaṭ āegā.*
>
> If it doesn't rain, the farmers will face a crisis.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकट** (*saṅkaṭ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃkaṭa*, "narrow; dangerous, critical" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
