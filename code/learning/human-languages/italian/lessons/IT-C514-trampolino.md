## You'll want to know: il trampolino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C514-ACQUA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il trampolino** — "diving board; springboard".

> Ha paura di saltare dal trampolino alto.
>
> *He is afraid to jump from the high board.*

Also a ski jump.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trampolino* is built on *trampolo* "stilt" (EtymDB), which comes from a German word like *trampeln* "to trample".
