# ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು (parigaṇisu) — to consider

## You'll want to know: ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C381-PARIGANISU381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು** — *parigaṇisu* — "to consider".

> ನಾನು ನಿನ್ನ ಸಲಹೆಯನ್ನು ಪರಿಗಣಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu ninna salaheyannu parigaṇiside.*
>
> I considered your suggestion.

Past (I …): ಪರಿಗಣಿಸಿದೆ, *parigaṇiside*.

Also to count as: ಇದನ್ನು ತಪ್ಪು ಎಂದು ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು, to count this as a mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು** (*parigaṇisu*) is built on Sanskrit *pari-gaṇ*, "to count over, to reckon up completely" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
