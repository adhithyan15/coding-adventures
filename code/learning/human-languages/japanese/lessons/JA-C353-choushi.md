## You'll want to know: ちょうし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C353-TENKEN353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうし** — *chōshi* — "how things are running; condition".

> **ちょうしが なおったら、しごとに もどって ください。** — *chōshi ga naottara, shigoto ni modotte kudasai* — "When you are back in shape, please come back to work."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうし** (*chōshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "tune" (Middle Chinese *dewH*, the reading for a tone or melody) and *shi*, "child" (*tsiX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
