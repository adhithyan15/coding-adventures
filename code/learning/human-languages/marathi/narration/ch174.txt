Marathi, chapter 174: Comfortable, Polite, Rude, Unpleasant, Enthusiastic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आरामदायक (ārāmdāyak) — comfortable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for unfortunately, then the Marathi for luckily.

You'll want to know: आरामदायक.
आरामदायक — ārāmdāyak — "comfortable".

The word, taken apart.
आरामदायक (ārāmdāyak) is "giving ease": ārām, "rest, ease", usually traced to Persian ārām, and Sanskrit dāyaka, "giving".
