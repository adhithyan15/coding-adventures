German, chapter 162: A sofa, A radio, The weather, The wind, A thunderstorm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Sofa — a sofa.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a balcony, then the German for a cellar.
