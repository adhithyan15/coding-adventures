## You'll want to know: स्वतन्त्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C354-NAGARIKA354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वतन्त्रः** — *svatantraḥ* — "independent".

> भारतं स्वतन्त्रं राष्ट्रम् अस्ति।
>
> *bhārataṃ svatantraṃ rāṣṭram asti.*
>
> India is an independent nation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वतन्त्रः** (*svatantraḥ*) is built from *sva*, "own", and *tantra*, "a loom, a system"; MW gives *sva-tantra* "self-dependence, independence, freedom" (MW).
