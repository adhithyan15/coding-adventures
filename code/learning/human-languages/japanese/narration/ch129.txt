Lesson 1 of 5.
かわいい (kawaii) — cute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for fine, then the Japanese for ugly.

You'll want to know: かわいい.
かわいい — kawaii — "cute".

The word, taken apart.
かわいい (kawaii) is a native word; JMdict lists kawayui as another reading of it, the older shape of the word. JMdict marks its kanji, "can" and "love", as ateji. It is inherited rather than borrowed.

The word, taken apart.
やかましい (yakamashii) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana; its kanji, when used, is a kun'yomi attached later (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

たのもしい — tanomoshii — "reliable".

The word, taken apart.
たのもしい (tanomoshii) is native, built on tanomu, "to rely on", with the adjective ending -shii: "to be relied on" (JMdict). It is inherited rather than borrowed.
