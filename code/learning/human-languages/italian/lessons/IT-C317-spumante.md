## You'll want to know: lo spumante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C317-SPUMANTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**lo spumante** — "the sparkling wine".

> A mezzanotte apriamo lo spumante.
>
> *At midnight we open the sparkling wine.*

Built on *spuma*, ‘foam’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spumante* is the present participle of *spumare* "to foam", from Latin *spūma* "foam" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
