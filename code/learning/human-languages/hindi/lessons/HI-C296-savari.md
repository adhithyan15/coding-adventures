## You'll want to know: सवारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C296-TAKKAR296-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सवारी** — *savārī* — "a passenger; a ride".

> बस में चालीस सवारियाँ थीं।
>
> *bas meṁ cālīs savāriyā̃ thīṁ.*
>
> There were forty passengers on the bus.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सवारी** (*savārī*) is usually traced to Persian *savārī*, "riding", from *savār*, a rider. It is feminine whatever the passenger: **चालीस सवारियाँ**.
