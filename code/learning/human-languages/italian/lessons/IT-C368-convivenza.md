## You'll want to know: la convivenza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C368-CONVIVENZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la convivenza** — "the living together, the coexistence".

> La convivenza con i vicini non è sempre facile.
>
> *Living alongside the neighbours is not always easy.*

It also means a couple living together without being married.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convivenza* is built on *convivere* "to live together", from Latin *convīvere* "to live with" (Lewis & Short), built on *vīvere* "to live".
