## You'll want to know: अनुवादक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C515-VISHESH515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुवादक** — *anuvādak* — "a translator".

> अनुवादकाने इंग्रजी पत्राचं मराठीत भाषांतर केलं.
>
> *anuvādakāne iṅgrajī patrācaṁ marāṭhīt bhāṣāntar kelaṁ.*
>
> The translator turned the English letter into Marathi.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुवादक** (*anuvādak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *anuvādaka*, "repeating with explanation" (Monier-Williams).
