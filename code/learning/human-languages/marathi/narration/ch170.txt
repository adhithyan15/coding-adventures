Marathi, chapter 170: Shame, Jealousy, Boredom, Patience, Luck.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
लाज (lāj) — shame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the liver, then the Marathi for a kidney.

You'll want to know: लाज.
लाज — lāj — "shame".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: lāj]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lāj, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say lāj]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for the liver, then the Marathi for a kidney, then say lāj again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does लाज (lāj) mean? ("Shame".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To light? (पेटवणे, petavṇe.) To put out? (विझवणे, vijhavṇe.) To jump? (उडी मारणे, uḍī mārṇe.) To call over? (बोलावणे, bolāvṇe.) To thank? (आभार मानणे, ābhār mānṇe.) To cool? (थंड करणे, thaṇḍ karṇe.) To empty? (रिकामे करणे, rikāme karṇe.) To weigh? (वजन करणे, vajan karṇe.) To check? (तपासणे, tapāsṇe.) To sign? (सही करणे, sahī karṇe.) To announce? (जाहीर करणे, jāhīr karṇe.) To clap? (टाळ्या वाजवणे, ṭāḷyā vājavṇe.) To cross? (ओलांडणे, olāṇḍṇe.) To increase? (वाढवणे, vāḍhavṇe.) To reduce? (कमी करणे, kamī karṇe.) To miss; to go wrong? (चुकणे, cukṇe.) To improve? (सुधारणे, sudhārṇe.) To get worse? (बिघडणे, bighaḍṇe.) To observe? (निरीक्षण करणे, nirīkṣaṇ karṇe.) To take part? (भाग घेणे, bhāg gheṇe.)
