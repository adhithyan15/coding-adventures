## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൂട്** (*kūṭŭ*) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil *kūṭu* and Kannada *gūḍu* (DEDR 1883). DEDR 1883 compares Sanskrit *kūṭa*.
