## You'll want to know: खाड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C444-BANDARGAH444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खाड़ी** — *khāṛī* — "a bay, a gulf".

> हमारा गाँव खाड़ी के किनारे है।
>
> *hamārā gā̃v khāṛī ke kināre hai.*
>
> Our village is on the shore of the bay.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खाड़ी** (*khāṛī*) goes back to *\*khaḍḍa*, "a hole, a pit", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3790), which lists Hindi *khāṛī*, "a creek, an inlet": the hollow the sea fills.
