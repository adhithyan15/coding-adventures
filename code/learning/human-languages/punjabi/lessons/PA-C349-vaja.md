## You'll want to know: ਵਾਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C349-SONG349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਜਾ** — *vājā* — "a harmonium; a musical instrument".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਾਦਾ ਵਾਜਾ ਵਜਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਗਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā dādā vājā vajāundā hai ate gāundā hai.*
>
> My grandfather plays the harmonium and sings.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਜਾ** (*vājā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vādya*, "musical instrument"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vājjā*, "musical instrument", under it (CDIAL 11511). CDIAL does not give the narrower sense "harmonium".
