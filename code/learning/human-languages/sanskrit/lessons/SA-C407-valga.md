## You'll want to know: वल्गा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C407-YANTA407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वल्गा** — *valgā* — "a rein".

> यन्ता हस्ते वल्गां धारयति।
>
> *yantā haste valgāṃ dhārayati.*
>
> The charioteer holds the rein in his hand.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वल्गा** (*valgā*) is the word MW gives for a bridle or rein; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
