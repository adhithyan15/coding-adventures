## You'll want to know: पदक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C530-PADAK530-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पदक** — *padak* — "a medal".

> स्पर्धेत अरुणला पदक मिळालं.
>
> *spardhet aruṇlā padak miḷālaṁ.*
>
> Arun won a medal in the competition.

Neuter.

सुवर्णपदक, a gold medal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पदक** (*padak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *padaka*, a derivative of *pada*, glossed as a kind of ornament (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it for a medal.
