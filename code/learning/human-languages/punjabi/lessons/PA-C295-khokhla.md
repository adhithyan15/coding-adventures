## You'll want to know: ਖੋਖਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C295-CRACK295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੋਖਲਾ** — *khokhlā* — "hollow".

> ਇਹ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਰੁੱਖ ਖੋਖਲਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh purāṇā rukkh khokhlā hai.*
>
> This old tree is hollow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੋਖਲਾ** (*khokhlā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khōkkha*, "hollow"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khokhlā* under it (CDIAL 3927).
