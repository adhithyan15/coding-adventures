## You'll want to know: рождение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C303-ROZHDENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**рождение** (*rozhdéniye*) — "birth".

> Рождение сына изменило их жизнь.
>
> *Rozhdéniye sýna izmenílo ikh zhizn.*
>
> The birth of their son changed their life.

Neuter. *День рождения* — a birthday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рождение* (EtymDB: "birth") is built on *родить* "to give birth", inherited from Proto-Slavic *roditi* (EtymDB), with the Church Slavonic *-жд-* where native Russian has *-ж-*.
