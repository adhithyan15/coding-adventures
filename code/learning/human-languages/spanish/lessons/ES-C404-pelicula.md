# la película — from a thin skin to a film

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C354-WHERE-28, ES-LEX-EMPEZAR] -->

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *pe-LEE-ku-la*: the written accent keeps the stress on **lí**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-PELICULA]; assesses=[] -->

**la película** — “the film” or “the movie.”

Latin **pellicula** meant a little skin, a diminutive of **pellis**, “skin.” A
thin photographic layer, and then the strip coated with it, carried the word
into cinema. The material's name became the movie's name.
