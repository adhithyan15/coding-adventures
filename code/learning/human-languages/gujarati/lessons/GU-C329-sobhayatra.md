## You'll want to know: શોભાયાત્રા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C329-RANGOLI329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**શોભાયાત્રા** — *śobhāyātrā* — "a procession".

> સાંજે ગામમાં મોટી શોભાયાત્રા નીકળી.
>
> *sā̃je gāmmā̃ moṭī śobhāyātrā nīkḷī.*
>
> In the evening a big procession went through the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શોભાયાત્રા** (*śobhāyātrā*) is built from Sanskrit *śobhā*, "splendour", and *yātrā*, "a going, a journey" (Monier-Williams), the word behind **યાત્રા**: a splendid going through the streets. A procession **નીકળે છે**, sets out.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
