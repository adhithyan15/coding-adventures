Lesson 2 of 5.
टीका करणे (ṭīkā karṇe) — to criticise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get bored, then the Marathi for to convince.

You'll want to know: टीका करणे.
टीका करणे — ṭīkā karṇe — "to criticise".

The word, taken apart.
टीका is Sanskrit ṭīkā, "commentary", a tatsama. करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अवलंबून असणे (avlambūn asṇe) — to depend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to criticise, then the Marathi for to prove.

You'll want to know: अवलंबून असणे.
अवलंबून असणे — avlambūn asṇe — "to depend".

The word, taken apart.
अवलंबून is "hanging on", from Sanskrit avalamba, "hanging down; support". CDIAL lists असणे under Sanskrit āste, "sits, exists, remains" (CDIAL 1480), or perhaps ākṣeti, "abides" (CDIAL 1031).

Lesson 5 of 5.
टाळणे (ṭāḷṇe) — to avoid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: टाळणे.
टाळणे — ṭāḷṇe — "to avoid".

The word, taken apart.
टाळणे (ṭāḷṇe) comes from Sanskrit ṭālayati, the "make" form of ṭalati, a verb CDIAL glosses "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450): to avoid is to make a thing move aside.
