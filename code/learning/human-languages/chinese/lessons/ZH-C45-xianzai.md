## You'll want to know: xiànzài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànzài* — "now".

> *nǐ xiànzài máng ma* — Are you busy right now?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànzài* is built from xiàn 'to appear' and zài 'to be present', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *henH* and *dzojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
