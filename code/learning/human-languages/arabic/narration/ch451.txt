Arabic, chapter 451: Offices and enquiries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مخفر (makhfar) — a police station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a line of people, then the Arabic for to apply.

You'll want to know: مخفر.
مخفر — makhfar — "a police station".
ذهب الرجل إلى المخفر بعد الحادث. dhahaba ar-rajul ilā al-makhfar baʿda al-ḥādith. The man went to the police station after the accident.

The word, taken apart.
مخفر (makhfar) is built on the native Arabic root kh-f-r, "to guard, to protect" (Lane). The pattern mafʿal makes it a guard post.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.
استفسر (istafsara) — to enquire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a police station, then the Arabic for a library.

You'll want to know: استفسر.
استفسر — istafsara — "to enquire".
استفسر أبي عن الحافلة الأخيرة. istafsara abī ʿan al-ḥāfila al-akhīra. My father enquired about the last bus.
Present: يستفسر, yastafsir.
It takes ʿan: istafsara ʿan, "to ask about".

The word, taken apart.
استفسر (istafsara) is built on the native Arabic root f-s-r, "to explain" (Lane). It is Form X, "to ask for an explanation".
