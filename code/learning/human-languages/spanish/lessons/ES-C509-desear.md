## You'll want to know: desear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C509-META-02]; assesses=[] -->

**desear** — "to wish, to want".

> Te deseo un feliz año.
>
> *I wish you a happy new year.*

In a shop, *¿qué desea?* — what would you like?
