Bengali, chapter 69: Cow, Goat, Sheep, Bird, Mouse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গরু (gôru) — a cow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a dog, then the Bengali for a cat.

You'll want to know: গরু.
গরু — gôru — "a cow".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gôru]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gôru, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gôru]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for a dog, then the Bengali for a cat, then say gôru again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does গরু (gôru) mean? ("A cow".) Say it once more.
