## You'll want to know: भंगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C535-SATYASANDHA535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भंगः** — *bhaṅgaḥ* — "a breaking".

> यदि नियमस्य भंगः भवति, तर्हि अध्यापकः कुपितः भवति।
>
> *yadi niyamasya bhaṅgaḥ bhavati, tarhi adhyāpakaḥ kupitaḥ bhavati.*
>
> If a rule is broken, the teacher gets angry.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भंगः** (*bhaṅgaḥ*) comes from the root √bhañj, "to break"; MW gives *bhaṅga* as breaking, splitting, breaking down (MW).
