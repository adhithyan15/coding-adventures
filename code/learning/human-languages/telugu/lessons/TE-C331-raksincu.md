# రక్షించు (rakṣiñcu) — to rescue; to save

## You'll want to know: రక్షించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C331-MANTA331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**రక్షించు** — *rakṣiñcu* — "to rescue; to save".

> పోలీసులు పిల్లలను మంటల నుంచి రక్షించారు.
>
> *pōlīsulu pillalanu maṇṭala nuñci rakṣiñcāru.*
>
> The police rescued the children from the flames.

Past (I …): రక్షించాను, *rakṣiñcānu*.

Who is saved takes -ను; what from takes నుంచి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రక్షించు** (*rakṣiñcu*) is built on the Sanskrit root *rakṣ*, "to guard, protect" (CDIAL 10547), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
