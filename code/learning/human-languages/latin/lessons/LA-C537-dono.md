## You'll want to know: dōnō, dōnāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C537-IURG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**dōnō, dōnāre** — "to give as a present".

> Avus nepōtī librum dōnat.
>
> *Grandfather gives his grandson a book as a present.*

*dōnō, dōnāre, dōnāvī, dōnātum*: *alicui aliquid dōnāre*, or *aliquem aliquā rē dōnāre*. English *donate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dōnō** is built on *dōnum* "a gift" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *donate* comes from it.
