## You'll want to know: l'humeur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C469-HUMEUR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'humeur** — "mood".

> Ce matin, je suis de bonne humeur.
>
> *This morning I am in a good mood.*

*De bonne humeur* — in a good mood; *de mauvaise humeur* — in a bad mood. It is feminine: *une humeur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'humeur* is inherited from Latin *ūmor* "moisture, fluid" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): old medicine linked a person's mood to the fluids of the body.
