Telugu, chapter 457: A Place to Stay.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వసతి (vasati) — lodging; accommodation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for dusk, then the Telugu for a stopover.

You'll want to know: వసతి.
వసతి — vasati — "lodging; accommodation".
పండుగ రోజుల్లో హోటళ్ళలో వసతి దొరకలేదు. paṇḍuga rōjullō hōṭaḷḷalō vasati dorakalēdu. In the festival days we could not find lodging in the hotels.
Also a hostel for students: విద్యార్థి వసతి (vasati) గృహం.

The word, taken apart.
వసతి (vasati) is borrowed from Sanskrit vasati, "staying, dwelling", from vas, "to stay" (CDIAL 11435).

Lesson 2 of 5.
సత్రం (satraṁ) — an inn; a rest house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a stopover, then the Telugu for lodging.

You'll want to know: సత్రం.
సత్రం — satraṁ — "an inn; a rest house".
పాత రోజుల్లో ప్రయాణికులు సత్రంలో ఉండేవారు. pāta rōjullō prayāṇikulu satraṁlō uṇḍēvāru. In the old days travellers used to stay at an inn.
A free or cheap rest house for travellers and pilgrims, often run by a trust.

The word, taken apart.
సత్రం (satraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit sattra, first a long sacrificial session and later a house where food and shelter were given (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
లిఫ్టు (liphṭu) — an elevator; a lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an inn, then the Telugu for a reception desk.

You'll want to know: లిఫ్టు.
లిఫ్టు — liphṭu — "an elevator; a lift".
లిఫ్టు పనిచేయలేదు, అందుకే మెట్లు ఎక్కాం. liphṭu panicēyalēdu, andukē meṭlu ekkāṁ. The lift was not working, so we climbed the stairs.
లిఫ్టు (liphṭu) ఎక్కు, to take the lift. In Telugu it also means a free ride: లిఫ్టు (liphṭu) ఇచ్చాడు.

The word, taken apart.
లిఫ్టు (liphṭu) is a loan from English lift, with the Telugu final -ఉ.

Lesson 5 of 5.
బస చేయు (basa cēyu) — to stay overnight; to put up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a reception desk, then the Telugu for an elevator.

You'll want to know: బస చేయు.
బస చేయు — basa cēyu — "to stay overnight; to put up".
ఆ రాత్రి మేము సత్రంలో బస చేశాం. ā rātri mēmu satraṁlō basa cēśāṁ. That night we put up at an inn.
Past (I …): బస చేశాను, basa cēśānu.
బస, a halt, a lodging, + చేయు. Used for a stay on a journey, away from home.

The word, taken apart.
బస చేయు (basa cēyu) is a Telugu phrase: బస, "a halt, a lodging", whose origin is not settled, and the native చేయు, "to do" (DEDR 1957).
