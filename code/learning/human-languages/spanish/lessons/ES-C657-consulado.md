## You'll want to know: el consulado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C657-PAPELES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el consulado** — "the consulate".

> El consulado abre por la mañana.
>
> *The consulate opens in the morning.*

The person is *el cónsul*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consulado* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnsulātus* "the office of consul" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *cōnsul*.
