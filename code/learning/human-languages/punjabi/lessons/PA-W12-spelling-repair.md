# One missing mark inside a known word

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

Say the familiar word for Punjabi, then picture its Gurmukhi spelling.
This is a one-word check, not a new sentence.

## Reading — look inside the word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

In **ਮੈਂ ਪਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।**, the spaces, verb, and final mark are right.
The word for the language has lost its nasal mark. Compare the taught
**ਪੰਜਾਬੀ** with **ਪਜਾਬੀ** and point to the missing mark.

## Writing — repair one spelling
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Write only **ਪੰਜਾਬੀ**. Check the mark above **ਪ**. Do not copy the rest of
the sentence or turn this into an agreement pass.
