## You'll want to know: jiàoshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàoshì* — "classroom".

> *wǒmen de jiàoshì hěn dà* — Our classroom is big.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàoshì* is built from jiào 'to teach' and shì 'room', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewH* and *syit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
