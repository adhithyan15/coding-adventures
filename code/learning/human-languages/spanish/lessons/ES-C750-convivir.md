## You'll want to know: convivir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C750-CONVIVIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**convivir** — "to live together".

> Convivo con dos compañeros de piso.
>
> *I live with two flatmates.*

*La convivencia* — living together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convivir* comes from Latin *convīvere* "to live together" (EtymDB), built on *vīvere* "to live".
