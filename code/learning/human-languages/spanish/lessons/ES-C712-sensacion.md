## You'll want to know: la sensación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C712-ANIMO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la sensación** — "the sensation, the feeling".

> Tengo la sensación de que va a llover.
>
> *I have a feeling it is going to rain.*

*Tengo la sensación de que...* — I have the feeling that...

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sensación* is usually described as a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *sēnsātiō*, built on *sēnsus* "feeling", from *sentīre* "to feel".
