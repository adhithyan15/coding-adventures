## You'll want to know: das Standesamt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C299-EHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Standesamt** — "the registry office".

> Sie heirateten zuerst auf dem Standesamt.
>
> *They got married at the registry office first.*

Plural: *die Standesämter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Standesamt* joins *Stand* "standing, status" and *Amt* "office" with the linking *-es-* (EtymDB): the office for civil status.
