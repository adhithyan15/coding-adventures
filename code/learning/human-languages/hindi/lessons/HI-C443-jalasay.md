## You'll want to know: जलाशय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C443-NAHAR443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जलाशय** — *jalāśay* — "a reservoir".

> गर्मी में जलाशय का पानी बहुत कम हो जाता है।
>
> *garmī meṁ jalāśay kā pānī bahut kam ho jātā hai.*
>
> In the summer the water in the reservoir gets very low.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जलाशय** (*jalāśay*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *jalāśaya*, "a reservoir, a pond, a lake" (Monier-Williams), from *jala*, "water", and *āśaya*, "a resting place".
