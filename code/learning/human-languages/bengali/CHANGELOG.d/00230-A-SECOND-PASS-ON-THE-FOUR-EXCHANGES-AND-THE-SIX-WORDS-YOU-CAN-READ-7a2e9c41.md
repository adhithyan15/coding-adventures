## A second pass on the four exchanges, and the six words you can read

Three `review` lessons. Fourteen atoms had been revisited once and no more — two
of them not at all — and the pre-A1 gate wants two. `bengali: reinforcement 14 -> 0`.

- **BN-R08-second-pass-four-exchanges** — the four chapter exchanges run
  together, and the two goodbyes set side by side. **আবার দেখা হবে** and
  **কাল দেখা হবে** share **দেখা হবে**, "a meeting will happen"; only the word in
  front changes, and **কাল** is the one to watch, being *yesterday* as well as
  *tomorrow*.
- **BN-R21-second-pass-reading-what-you-say** — the six words the script
  lessons taught you to read, with nothing beside them: পড়া, দুধ, ভাই, পরিবার,
  হওয়া, হৃদয়. Each kept the point it was taught with — পরিবার is where the
  inherent vowel is finally *heard*, ভাই puts an independent vowel mid-word, and
  হৃদয় needed **ৃ**, a sign for a vowel that was once an *r*.
- **BN-R30-second-pass-slowly-and-two-signs** — **ধীরে**, and why it is said
  twice. Doubled, **ধীরে ধীরে** is a request; single, it can sound like an
  instruction. It is the half of the repair kit a beginner reaches for most.

No new words and no new atoms: `review` is outside `CONTENT_TYPES`, so the
vocabulary count is unchanged by design.
