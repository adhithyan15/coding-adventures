Arabic, chapter 427: Planning a trip.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مسافة (masāfa) — a distance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to empty out, then the Arabic for to confiscate.

You'll want to know: مسافة.
مسافة — masāfa — "a distance".
المسافة من القرية إلى المدينة قصيرة. al-masāfa min al-qarya ilā al-madīna qaṣīra. The distance from the village to the city is short.

The word, taken apart.
مسافة (masāfa) is built on the native Arabic root s-w-f, "to smell" (Lane). Lane records the old account: a desert guide smelled the ground to know the way, so the stretch he sniffed out was a masāfa.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تأكد (taʾakkada) — to make sure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to confiscate, then the Arabic for a distance.

You'll want to know: تأكد.
تأكد — taʾakkada — "to make sure".
تأكد أبي من الحجز. taʾakkada abī min al-ḥajz. My father made sure of the booking.
Present: يتأكد, yataʾakkad.
It takes min: taʾakkada min, "to make sure of".

The word, taken apart.
تأكد (taʾakkada) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-k-d, "to make firm" (Lane). It is Form V, "to become firm in one's mind"; mutaʾakkid, "sure", is its participle.

Lesson 5 of 5.
حقيبة ظهر (ḥaqībat ẓahr) — a backpack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to adjust, then the Arabic for to hire.

You'll want to know: حقيبة ظهر.
حقيبة ظهر — ḥaqībat ẓahr — "a backpack".
حمل أخي حقيبة ظهر كبيرة. ḥamala akhī ḥaqībat ẓahr kabīra. My brother carried a big backpack.

The word, taken apart.
حقيبة ظهر (ḥaqībat ẓahr) is built from two native Arabic words: ḥaqība, "a bag", on the root ḥ-q-b (Lane), and ẓahr, "the back", on the root ẓ-h-r (Lane): a bag for the back.
