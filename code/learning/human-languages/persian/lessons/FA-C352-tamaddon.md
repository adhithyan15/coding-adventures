## You'll want to know: تمدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C352-BASTAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تمدن** (*tamaddon*) — "a civilisation".

> تمدن در کنار رودخانه ها شروع شد.
>
> *Tamaddon dar kenâr-e rudkhâne-hâ shoru' shod.*
>
> Civilisation began on the banks of rivers.

Plural *tamaddon-hâ*. The Arabic word is linked with *madina* "city": living in cities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tamaddon* is borrowed from Arabic *tamaddun*, "civilisation" (EtymDB), a word usually linked with *madīna* "city".
