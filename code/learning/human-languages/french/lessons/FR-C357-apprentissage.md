## You'll want to know: l'apprentissage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C357-STAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'apprentissage** — "the apprenticeship; learning".

> L'apprentissage d'une langue prend du temps.
>
> *Learning a language takes time.*

*Être en apprentissage* — to be an apprentice. Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apprentissage* is built from *apprenti* and *-age*. *apprenti* rests on *apprendre*, from Latin *apprehendere* "to seize, to grasp" (Lewis & Short).
