## You'll want to know: حسرت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C369-KASH369-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حسرت** — *hasrat* — "a wistful regret; an unfulfilled wish".

> اگر میں پڑھتا تو آج یہ حسرت نہ ہوتی۔
>
> *agar maiṅ paṛhtā to āj yih hasrat na hotī.*
>
> If I had studied, I would not have this regret today.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حسرت** (*hasrat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥasra*, "sorrow, regret".
