## You'll want to know: صراع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C386-TAAWUN386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صراع** — *ṣirāʿ* — "a conflict".

> انتهى الصراع بعد سنوات.
>
> *intahā aṣ-ṣirāʿ baʿda sanawāt.*
>
> The conflict ended after years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صراع** (*ṣirāʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-r-ʿ*, "to throw down, to wrestle" (Lane). *Ṣirāʿ* is the Form III verbal noun, wrestling with one another: a struggle, a conflict.
