Hindi, chapter 371: Even So.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हालाँकि (hālā̃ki) — although.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a political party, then the Hindi for to fix (a date).

You'll want to know: हालाँकि.
हालाँकि — hālā̃ki — "although".
हालाँकि बारिश हो रही थी, फिर भी हम बाज़ार गए। hālā̃ki bāriś ho rahī thī, phir bhī ham bāzār gae. Although it was raining, we still went to the market.

The word, taken apart.
हालाँकि (hālā̃ki) is usually traced to Persian hālānki, "although".

बीमारी के बावजूद वह काम पर आया। bīmārī ke bāvjūd vah kām par āyā. Despite being ill, he came to work.

The word, taken apart.
बावजूद (bāvjūd) is usually traced to Persian bāvujūd, "with the existence (of)". Its second part is Arabic wujūd, "existence".

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: अड़ना.
अड़ना — aṛnā — "to dig one's heels in, to insist".
बच्चा नए खिलौने के लिए अड़ गया। baccā nae khilaune ke liye aṛ gayā. The child dug his heels in over a new toy.
Past (he …): वह अपनी बात पर अड़ गया, vah apnī bāt par aṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
अड़ना (aṛnā) is inherited from aḍ, "stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 187).
