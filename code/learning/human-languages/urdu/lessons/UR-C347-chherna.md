## You'll want to know: چھیڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C347-MUAMALA347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**چھیڑنا** — *chheṛnā* — "to bring up a topic; to tease".

> اس نے میٹنگ میں پیسے کی بات چھیڑی۔
>
> *us ne mīṭing meṅ paise kī bāt chheṛī.*
>
> She brought up the money question at the meeting.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نئی بات چھیڑی, *us ne naī bāt chheṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھیڑنا** (*chheṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cheṛnā*, "to touch, to irritate", under *\*ciḍ*, "be angry", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4794).
