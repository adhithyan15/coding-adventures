Kannada, chapter 274: To dance, To press, To deposit, To withdraw, To sign.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕುಣಿ (kuṇi) — to dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to drive, then the Kannada for to slip.

You'll want to know: ಕುಣಿ.
ಕುಣಿ — kuṇi — "to dance".

The word, taken apart.
ಕುಣಿ (kuṇi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kuṉi, Telugu guniyu and Malayalam kunikka (DEDR 1863). DEDR lists kuṇi, "to hop, dance".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ಜಮಾ ಮಾಡು.
ಜಮಾ ಮಾಡು — jamā māḍu — "to deposit (money)".

The word, taken apart.
ಜಮಾ ಮಾಡು (jamā māḍu) is Hindustani jamā, "a sum, a deposit", from Arabic jamʿ, "collecting", with native māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).

ಹಿಂಪಡೆ (himpaḍe) is native Dravidian: him, "behind, back", which DEDR 4205 lists with older Kannada pin, and paḍe, "to get, obtain" (DEDR 3853): "to get back".
