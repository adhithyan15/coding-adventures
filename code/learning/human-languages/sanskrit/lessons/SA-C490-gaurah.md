## You'll want to know: गौरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C490-RUPA490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरः** — *gauraḥ* — "pale; whitish".

> शिशुः गौरः सुन्दरः च अस्ति।
>
> *śiśuḥ gauraḥ sundaraḥ ca asti.*
>
> The baby is fair and lovely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरः** (*gauraḥ*) MW gives "white, yellowish, reddish, pale red"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
