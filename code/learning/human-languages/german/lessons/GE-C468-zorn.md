## You'll want to know: der Zorn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C468-WUT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zorn** — "anger, wrath".

> Der Zorn des Vaters war schnell vorbei.
>
> *The father's anger was soon over.*

No plural. More literary than *die Wut*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zorn* is inherited from Middle High German *zorn* (EtymDB), already Old High German *zorn*; its older origin is not settled.
