## You'll want to know: der Sekt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C308-ANSTOSSEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sekt** — "the sparkling wine".

> Zur Feier gab es eine Flasche Sekt.
>
> *For the celebration there was a bottle of sparkling wine.*

German sparkling wine: *ein Glas Sekt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sekt* is usually traced to French *vin sec* or Italian *vino secco* "dry wine", from Latin *siccus* "dry" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.
