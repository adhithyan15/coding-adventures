## You'll want to know: سویاں
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C318-EIDMORNING318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سویاں** — *sivayāṅ* — "sweet vermicelli".

> امی نے سویاں پکائیں۔
>
> *ammī ne sivayāṅ pakāīṅ.*
>
> Mum cooked sweet vermicelli.

Feminine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سویاں** (*sivayāṅ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *sewaī̃* and *sẽwiyā̃*, "vermicelli", under Sanskrit *śamitā* (CDIAL 12307). Urdu says it in the plural: **سویاں پکائیں**.
