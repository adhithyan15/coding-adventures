## Chapters 38-87: 250 headwords, thirty-seven verbs, three lessons split, and Urdu attains pre-A1

Urdu had three pre-A1 gaps: 240 headwords, 4 verbs, and three lessons that
introduced more than three atoms.

**The budget.** **آپ / تم / تو**, **میں ... ہوں** and **خدا حافظ** introduced
four or five atoms each. Each now keeps the atom-introducing sections that fit
within three atoms, in their original order, and passes the rest to a new
lesson placed right after it, headed "(…, continued)" and romanized the same
way. The atom ids are kept, and every later lesson that uses a moved atom now
lists the new lesson as a prerequisite.

Chapters 3-5 keep one objective activity per lesson:

- The **tum**/**thou** continuation gains a question on the English relative.
- The **خدا حافظ** continuation gains one on Urdu's visible space between the
  two words.
- **میں ... ہوں** gains one for **میں**, because its frame activity moved with
  the frame atom.

**The vocabulary.** Fifty chapters of five words each, and four reviews: two
after chapter 62 and two after chapter 87. Every word is chained into the next
two lessons.

- **38-62**:
  - the body and health
  - the family on both sides
  - thirty-seven verbs
  - food, the home and school
- **63-87**:
  - nature and animals
  - describing words
  - time and place
  - the town
  - clothes
  - the numbers from eleven to ninety

Every headword is spelled only with letters the script ladder has already
taught. That is why **زبان**, **میز**, **صبح**, **عورت** and **ڈاکٹر** wait:
ز, ص, ع and ڈ have not been written yet. **کرنا** and **آسان** were held back
too: the first is already inside **مدد کرنا**, and the second is already used
in the alif-madda letter lessons.
