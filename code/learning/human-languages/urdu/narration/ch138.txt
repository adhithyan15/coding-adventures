Urdu, chapter 138: To fall, To fly, To swim, To play, To touch.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گرنا (girnā) — to fall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for open, then the Urdu for to wake up.

You'll want to know: گرنا.
گرنا — girnā — "to fall".

The word, taken apart.
گرنا (girnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from girati, "drips, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4159).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to fall, then the Urdu for to fly.

You'll want to know: تیرنا.
تیرنا — tairnā — "to swim".

The word, taken apart.
تیرنا (tairnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tirate, "crosses over" (CDIAL 5821). CDIAL rejects a Dravidian source proposed by Bloch, and explains the ai as a blend with another verb.
