## Script — 人 twice, then 土
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZUO-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 坐

**Three pieces, and you write all of them already.**

**人, twice, side by side**, the left one first. Each is a left-falling stroke and then a dot: neither has the long right-falling stroke of 人 standing alone.

**土, three strokes**, under them, as at the lower right of 在: the short horizontal; then the vertical, from the top between the two 人, down through it; then the long horizontal along the foot.

2 + 2 + 3 = seven.
