## You'll want to know: વેવાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C491-JAMAI491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વેવાઈ** — *vevāī* — "the father-in-law of one's child".

> લગ્નમાં બંને વેવાઈ ખુશ હતા.
>
> *lagnamā̃ banne vevāī khuś hatā.*
>
> At the wedding both fathers-in-law were happy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વેવાઈ** (*vevāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vaivāhika*, "nuptial, related by marriage"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "son's or daughter's father-in-law" (CDIAL 12148).
