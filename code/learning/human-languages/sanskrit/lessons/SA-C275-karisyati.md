# करिष्यति (kariṣyati) — the future, person by person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-LEX-SHVAH] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I will do", **करिष्यामि** (*kariṣyāmi*), and "I will go", **गमिष्यामि** (*gamiṣyāmi*). Then say "tomorrow". (*śvaḥ*.) This lesson gives that future to every person.

## You'll want to know: I, you, he and she
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-AHAM, SA-LEX-TVAM, SA-REL-SAH-01, SA-LEX-C170-WRK170-01] -->

> **अहं करिष्यामि।**
>
> *ahaṃ kariṣyāmi.* — "I will do it."

> **त्वं करिष्यसि।**
>
> *tvaṃ kariṣyasi.* — "You will do it."

> **सः करिष्यति।**
>
> *saḥ kariṣyati.* — "He will do it."

**करिष्य-** (*kariṣya-*) is the future stem of "do", and the present's endings follow it: *kariṣyāmi* like *gacchāmi*, *kariṣyati* like *gacchati*.

> **सः श्वः कार्यं करिष्यति।**
>
> *saḥ śvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyati.* — "He will do the work tomorrow."

## You'll want to know: we, you all, they, and the two
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-02]; assesses=[SA-JOIN-CA-01, SA-LEX-KIM, SA-LEX-NA-NO, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-05] -->

**वयम्** (*vayam*) is "we", **यूयम्** (*yūyam*) "you all", and **ते** (*te*) "they".

> **वयं करिष्यामः।**
>
> *vayaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ.* — "We will do it."

> **ते करिष्यन्ति।**
>
> *te kariṣyanti.* — "They will do it."

| who | the future of "do" |
|---|---|
| **अहम्** (*aham*), I | **करिष्यामि** (*kariṣyāmi*) |
| **त्वम्** (*tvam*), you | **करिष्यसि** (*kariṣyasi*) |
| **सः**, **सा** (*saḥ*, *sā*), he, she | **करिष्यति** (*kariṣyati*) |
| **वयम्** (*vayam*), we | **करिष्यामः** (*kariṣyāmaḥ*) |
| **यूयम्** (*yūyam*), you all | **करिष्यथ** (*kariṣyatha*) |
| **ते** (*te*), they | **करिष्यन्ति** (*kariṣyanti*) |

Exactly two people take the dual, as **वदावः** (*vadāvaḥ*), "we two speak", did:

> **अरुणः मीरा च करिष्यतः।**
>
> *aruṇaḥ mīrā ca kariṣyataḥ.* — "Arun and Mira will do it."

A question puts **किम्** (*kim*) first; "not" puts **न** (*na*) before the verb:

> **किं त्वं श्वः गमिष्यसि?**
>
> *kiṃ tvaṃ śvaḥ gamiṣyasi?* — "Will you go tomorrow?"

> **न, अहं न गमिष्यामि।**
>
> *na, ahaṃ na gamiṣyāmi.* — "No, I will not go."
