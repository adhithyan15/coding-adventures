# मैं पढ़ रहा था (maiṁ paṛh rahā thā) — what was going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C89-CONT-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I am doing" and "he was". (*maiṁ kar rahā hūṁ*, *vah thā*.) Then "she came". (*vah āī*.) The first two make this lesson; the third will interrupt it.

## You'll want to know: was …-ing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C89-CONT-01, HI-LEX-C89-CONT-02, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-02, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-03, HI-CONCEPT-C34-PADHNA-01, HI-LEX-C159-QUAL159-02, HI-LEX-C184-THG184-05] -->

> **मैं किताब पढ़ रहा था।**
>
> *maiṁ kitāb paṛh rahā thā.* — "I was reading a book."

You know **कर रहा हूँ**, "I am doing". Put **था** where **हूँ** stood and the same action moves into the past. Both **रहा** and **था** follow the one doing it:

- **मैं पढ़ रहा था** (*maiṁ paṛh rahā thā*) — I was reading; a woman says **मैं पढ़ रही थी** (*maiṁ paṛh rahī thī*)
- **वह सो रहा था** (*vah so rahā thā*) — he was sleeping; **वह सो रही थी** (*vah so rahī thī*) — she was sleeping
- **बारिश हो रही थी** (*bāriś ho rahī thī*) — it was raining

There is no **ने** here, even with a thing: **मैं खाना खा रहा था**, never **मैंने**. **ने** belongs to the plain past alone.

## Grammar Lens: a scene and an event
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C278-SCENE-EVENT-01]; assesses=[HI-JOIN-JAB-TAB-01, HI-LEX-C144-THINGS144-05, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

> **जब मैं खाना खा रहा था, तब मीरा आई।**
>
> *jab maiṁ khānā khā rahā thā, tab Mīrā āī.* — "When I was eating, Mira came."

A story needs two kinds of past. **खा रहा था** is the scene: it was going on, and the sentence does not say when it began or ended. **आई** is the event: it happened, once, inside the scene. **जब … तब …**, the frame you know for "when … then …", holds the two together.

- **जब बारिश हो रही थी, तब बिजली गई।** (*jab bāriś ho rahī thī, tab bijlī gaī.*) — While it was raining, the power went.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C278-ONGOING-PAST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C278-SCENE-EVENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I was reading"? (**मैं पढ़ रहा था**, or **पढ़ रही थी**.) "It was raining"? (**बारिश हो रही थी**.) In **जब मैं खाना खा रहा था, तब मीरा आई**, which is the scene and which is the event?
