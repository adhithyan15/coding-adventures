Bengali, chapter 174: Plug, Engine, Wheel, Tyre, Brake.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
প্লাগ (plāg) — a plug.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a black plum, then the Bengali for a lychee.

You'll want to know: প্লাগ.
প্লাগ — plāg — "a plug".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: plāg]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: plāg, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say plāg]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for a black plum, then the Bengali for a lychee, then say plāg again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does প্লাগ (plāg) mean? ("A plug".) Say it once more.
