## You'll want to know: ارتفع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C480-NASHRA480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتفع** — *irtafaʿa* — "to increase; to rise".

> ارتفعت درجة الحرارة عند الظهر.
>
> *irtafaʿat darajat al-ḥarāra ʿinda aẓ-ẓuhr.*
>
> The temperature rose at noon.

Present: يرتفع, *yartafiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتفع** (*irtafaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-f-ʿ*, "to raise" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to rise".
