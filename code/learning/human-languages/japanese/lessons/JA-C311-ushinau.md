## You'll want to know: うしなう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C311-OMOIDE311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うしなう** — *ushinau* — "to lose for good; to be robbed of".

> **ちちは じしんで しごとを うしないました。** — *chichi wa jishin de shigoto o ushinaimashita* — "My father lost his job in the earthquake."

Polite **うしないます**, *ushinaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが しごとを うしないました。**, *otoko ga shigoto o ushinaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うしなう** (*ushinau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
