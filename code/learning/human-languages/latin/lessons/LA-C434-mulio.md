## You'll want to know: mūliō, mūliōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C434-STABULUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mūliō, mūliōnis** — "a muleteer, a mule driver".

> Mūliō mūlōs ad flūmen dūcit et aquam dat.
>
> *The muleteer leads the mules to the river and gives them water.*

Third declension, masculine: *mūliō, mūliōnis m.* Muleteers hired out mules and carts to travellers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mūliō* is built on *mūlus* 'mule' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short) with the suffix *-iō* for a person who works with something.
