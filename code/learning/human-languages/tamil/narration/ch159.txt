Tamil, chapter 159: Next, From Now On, a Plan, To Decide, To Expect.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அடுத்த (aṭutta) — next, coming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for once, then the Tamil for to spend.

You'll want to know: அடுத்த.
அடுத்த — aṭutta — "next, coming". Tamil's future for "I" ends in -வேன் or -ப்பேன்: அடுத்த வாரம் நான் போவேன் — aṭutta vāram nāṉ pōvēṉ — "I will go next week."
