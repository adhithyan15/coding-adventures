## You'll want to know: વિઝા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C406-BORDER406-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વિઝા** — *vijhā* — "a visa".

> પાસપોર્ટ સાથે વિઝા પણ જોઈએ.
>
> *pāsporṭ sāthe vijhā paṇ joīe.*
>
> As well as a passport you need a visa.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિઝા** (*vijhā*) is borrowed from English *visa*, the stamp or paper that lets you into another country.
