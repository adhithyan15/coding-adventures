## You'll want to know: 就业
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C487-CAREER487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**就业** — *jiùyè* — "to find employment; to get a job".

> *xiànzài dàxuésheng jiùyè bù róngyì* — It isn't easy for graduates to find jobs these days.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**就业** (*jiùyè*) is built from 就 'to go to' and 业 'work', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjuwH* and *ngjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to accomplish' and 'to go towards'.
