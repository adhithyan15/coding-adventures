## You'll want to know: बलिदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C332-SHAURYA332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बलिदान** — *balidān* — "a sacrifice".

> देशासाठी अनेकांनी बलिदान दिलं.
>
> *deśāsāṭhī anekānnī balidān dilaṁ.*
>
> Many gave their lives for the country.

Neuter.

बलिदान देणे, to give one's life for a cause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बलिदान** (*balidān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *balidāna*, 'the giving of an offering' (Monier-Williams).
