## You'll want to know: la délinquance
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C290-CRIME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la délinquance** — "crime, delinquency".

> La délinquance a baissé dans le quartier.
>
> *Crime has gone down in the neighbourhood.*

Crime in general, as a problem of society.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*délinquance* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *dēlinquentia* (EtymDB), from *dēlinquere* "to do wrong".
