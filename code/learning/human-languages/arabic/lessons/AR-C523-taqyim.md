## You'll want to know: تقييم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C523-INJAZ523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تقييم** — *taqyīm* — "an evaluation, an assessment".

> حصل الموظف على تقييم ممتاز.
>
> *ḥaṣala al-muwaẓẓaf ʿalā taqyīm mumtāz.*
>
> The employee got an excellent evaluation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تقييم** (*taqyīm*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-w-m*, "to stand, to rise" (Lane). Qīma, value, is from the same root: taqyīm is fixing a thing's value.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
