## You'll want to know: sbarcare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C421-PORTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sbarcare** — "to disembark, to get off (a ship)".

> Siamo sbarcati dopo tre ore di mare.
>
> *We landed after three hours at sea.*

*Sbarcare il lunario* — to make ends meet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sbarcare* is built from *s-* 'out of' and *barca* 'boat', which is inherited from Late Latin *barca* (Lewis & Short).
