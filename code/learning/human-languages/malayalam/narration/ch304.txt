Malayalam, chapter 304: To breathe, To sneeze, To shiver, To swell, To itch.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ശ്വസിക്കുക (śvasikkuka) — to breathe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to save, then the Malayalam for to bargain.

You'll want to know: ശ്വസിക്കുക.
ശ്വസിക്കുക — śvasikkuka — "to breathe".

The word, taken apart.
ശ്വസിക്കുക (śvasikkuka) is built on Sanskrit śvas, "to breathe", as in śvāsa, "breath" (CDIAL 12769), with the ending that makes a Malayalam verb.
