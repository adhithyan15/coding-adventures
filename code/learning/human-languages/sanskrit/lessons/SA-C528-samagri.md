## You'll want to know: सामग्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C528-AYOJAYATI528-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सामग्री** — *sāmagrī* — "materials".

> यदि सामग्री सज्जा अस्ति, तर्हि अहं पायसं पचामि।
>
> *yadi sāmagrī sajjā asti, tarhi ahaṃ pāyasaṃ pacāmi.*
>
> If the materials are ready, I'll make rice pudding.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सामग्री** (*sāmagrī*) comes from *samagra*, "whole, complete", as MW derives it: entirety, and so a complete set of implements or materials (MW).
