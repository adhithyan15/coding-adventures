## You'll want to know: il bagnino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C306-SALVATAGGIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il bagnino** — "the lifeguard".

> Il bagnino ha salvato un bambino.
>
> *The lifeguard saved a child.*

At the beach; a woman is *la bagnina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bagnino* is built on *bagno* "bath, bathing", from Latin *balneum* (Lewis & Short).
