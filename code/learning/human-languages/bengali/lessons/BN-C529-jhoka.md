## You'll want to know: ঝোঁকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C529-DOTANA529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝোঁকা** — *jhõkā* — "to lean; to stoop".

> সামনে বেশি ঝুঁকলে তুমি পড়ে যাবে।
>
> *Sāmne beshi jhũkle tumi pôṛe jābe.*
>
> If you lean too far forward, you'll fall.

Past (he/she …): তিনি সামনে ঝুঁকলেন, *tini sāmne jhũklen*.

The stem vowel rises in the past: ঝুঁকলেন, he leaned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝোঁকা** (*jhõkā*) comes from a reconstructed *jhukkati*, "stoops"; CDIAL lists Bengali *jhũkā*, "to stoop" (CDIAL 5399). ঝুঁকি, a risk, is probably from the same root.
