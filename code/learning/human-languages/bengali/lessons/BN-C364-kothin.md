## You'll want to know: কঠিন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C364-SONGKOT364-01]; assesses=[] -->

**কঠিন** — *kôṭhin* — "hard; difficult".

> পরীক্ষাটা কঠিন ছিল।
>
> *pôrīkkhāṭā kôṭhin chhilô.*
>
> The exam was hard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কঠিন** (*kôṭhin*) is Sanskrit *kaṭhina*, hard or firm (Monier-Williams). It is hard to the touch and hard to do; সহজ, easy, is its opposite.
