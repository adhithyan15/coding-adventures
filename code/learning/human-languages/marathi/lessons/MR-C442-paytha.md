## You'll want to know: पायथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पायथा** — *pāythā* — "a foothill; the foot of a hill".

> डोंगराच्या पायथ्याशी एक लहान गाव आहे.
>
> *ḍoṅgarācyā pāyathyāśī ek lahān gāv āhe.*
>
> There is a small village at the foot of the hill.

Masculine.

पायथ्याशी, at the foot; the opposite is the शिखर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पायथा** (*pāythā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *pāythā*, "extremity of the feet", under Sanskrit *pādānta*, "the end at the feet" (CDIAL 8072).
