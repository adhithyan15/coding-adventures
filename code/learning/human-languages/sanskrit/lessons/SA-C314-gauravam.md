## You'll want to know: गौरवम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C314-SHAURYA314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरवम्** — *gauravam* — "honour; respect".

> ग्रामः तस्मै गौरवं ददाति।
>
> *grāmaḥ tasmai gauravaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The village honours him.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गौरवम्** (*gauravam*) is built on *guru*, "heavy, venerable", with the lengthened first vowel; MW gives *gaurava* as weight, venerableness, and respect shown to a person (MW).
