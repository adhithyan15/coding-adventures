## You'll want to know: اسٹیج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C517-STAGE517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اسٹیج** — *isṭej* — "a stage (in a theatre)".

> اداکار اسٹیج پر آیا۔
>
> *adākār isṭej par āyā.*
>
> The actor came onto the stage.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اسٹیج** (*isṭej*) is borrowed from English *stage*.
