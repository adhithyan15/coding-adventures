## You'll want to know: かんどうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C428-JOY428-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かんどうする** — *kandō suru* — "to be deeply moved".

> **あの えいがに かんどうしました。** — *ano eiga ni kandō shimashita* — "I was deeply moved by that film."

Polite **かんどうします**, *kandōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ははが かんどうしました。**, *haha ga kandō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんどうする** (*kandō suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *kandō* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "emotion" (Middle Chinese *komX*) and *dō*, "move" (*duwngX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
