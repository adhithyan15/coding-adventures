Spanish, chapter 677: Monumentos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el monumento — the monument, the sight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the leaflet, then the Spanish for to haggle.

You'll want to know: el monumento.
el monumento — "the monument, the sight".
Visitamos el monumento más famoso. We visited the most famous monument.
Monumentos are the sights of a town.

The word, taken apart.
monumento is a learned borrowing from Latin monumentum "that which keeps a memory, a memorial" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from monēre "to remind".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: la catedral.
la catedral — "the cathedral".
La catedral es del siglo XIII. The cathedral is from the thirteenth century.
The bishop’s church of a city.

The word, taken apart.
catedral is a learned borrowing from Latin (ecclesia) cathedrālis "the church with the chair", from cathedra "a chair" (Lewis & Short), Greek kathédra: the bishop’s seat.

Hacer castillos en el aire — to build castles in the air.

The word, taken apart.
castillo is inherited from Latin castellum "a castle, a fort" (Lewis & Short), a little castrum "fortified camp".

el palacio — "the palace".
El rey vivía en este palacio. The king lived in this palace.
El Palacio de Justicia — the law courts.

The word, taken apart.
palacio is a learned borrowing from Latin palātium (EtymDB), named after the Palatine hill in Rome, where the emperors had their houses.
