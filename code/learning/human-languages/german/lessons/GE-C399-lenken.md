## You'll want to know: lenken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C399-LENKRAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lenken** — "to steer; to direct".

> Auf dem Eis kann er das Auto kaum lenken.
>
> *On the ice he can hardly steer the car.*

Also figurative: *das Gespräch auf ein Thema lenken* — to steer the talk towards a topic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lenken* is an inherited Germanic verb that first meant 'to bend, to turn' (EtymDB gives 'to turn'). EtymDB links it with English *lank* 'limp, thin'.
