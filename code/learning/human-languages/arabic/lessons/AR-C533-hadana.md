## You'll want to know: حضن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C533-RIAYA533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حضن** — *ḥaḍana* — "to cradle; to hold in one's arms".

> حضنت الأم طفلها الباكي.
>
> *ḥaḍanat al-umm ṭiflahā al-bākī.*
>
> The mother held her crying child in her arms.

Present: يحضن, *yaḥḍun*.

It also means to foster a child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حضن** (*ḥaḍana*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ḍ-n*, "to hold to the bosom; to rear" (Lane). Lane gives yaḥḍunu as its present.

## What you've built
