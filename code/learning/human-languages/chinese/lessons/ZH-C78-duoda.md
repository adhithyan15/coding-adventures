## You'll want to know: 多大
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C78-AGE78-01]; assesses=[] -->

**多大** — *duō dà* — "how old (asking a person's age)".

> **生日快乐你今年多大** *shēngrì kuàilè nǐ jīnnián duō dà* — Happy birthday! How old are you now?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**多大** (*duō dà*) is built from 多 'much' and 大 'big': how big, said of a person's years.
