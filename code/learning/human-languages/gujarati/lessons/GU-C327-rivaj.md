## You'll want to know: રિવાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C327-UTSAV327-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રિવાજ** — *rivāj* — "a custom".

> લગ્નનો આ રિવાજ ખૂબ જૂનો છે.
>
> *lagnano ā rivāj khūb jūno che.*
>
> This wedding custom is very old.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રિવાજ** (*rivāj*) is usually traced to Persian *rivāj*, "currency, custom", a word Persian took from Arabic: what is current among people. It is masculine: **જૂનો રિવાજ**.
