## You'll want to know: ಸರಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C368-KRAMA368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಣಿ** — *saraṇi* — "a series; a string".

> ಆ ತಿಂಗಳು ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕಳ್ಳತನಗಳ ಸರಣಿ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *ā tiṅgaḷu ūrinalli kaḷḷatanagaḷa saraṇi naḍeyitu.*
>
> That month there was a series of thefts in the town.

Plural: ಸರಣಿಗಳು, *saraṇigaḷu*.

Also a TV or book series, and a cricket series.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಣಿ** (*saraṇi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saraṇi*, "a road, a path, a straight or continuous line" (Monier-Williams).
