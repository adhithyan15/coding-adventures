Arabic, chapter 78: Fire, Snow, Wind, Sky, Horse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نار (nār) — fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the moon, then the Arabic for the sea.

You'll want to know: نار.
نار — nār — "fire".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nār, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for the moon, then the Arabic for the sea, then say nār again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does نار (nār) mean? ("Fire".) Say it once more.
