## You'll want to know: забивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C509-FOOTBALL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**забивать** (*zabivát*) — "to score (a goal); to hammer in".

> Он забивает почти в каждом матче.
>
> *On zabiváyet pochtí v kázhdom mátche.*
>
> He scores in almost every match.

Imperfective; perfective забить (забью). Also to hammer in a nail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*забивать* is built on *за-* and the root of *бить* 'to strike'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
