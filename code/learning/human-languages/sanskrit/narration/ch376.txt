Sanskrit, chapter 376: Who Can Do It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
योग्यः (yogyaḥ) — qualified; fit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a drought, then the Sanskrit for remedies.

You'll want to know: योग्यः.
योग्यः — yogyaḥ — "qualified; fit".
यदि अरुणः योग्यः अस्ति, तर्हि सः नायकः भवेत्। yadi aruṇaḥ yogyaḥ asti, tarhi saḥ nāyakaḥ bhavet. If Arun is qualified, he should be the leader.

The word, taken apart.
योग्यः (yogyaḥ) is built on yoga, "a yoking, union", from the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives yogya "fit for the yoke" and "useful, proper, qualified, able" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for qualified, then the Sanskrit for capable.

You'll want to know: नियच्छति.
नियच्छति — niyacchati — "controls; restrains".
यदि भवान् मनः नियच्छति, तर्हि शान्तिः भविष्यति। yadi bhavān manaḥ niyacchati, tarhi śāntiḥ bhaviṣyati. If you control your mind, there will be peace.
Past (he …): सः क्रोधं नियतवान्, saḥ krodhaṃ niyatavān.

The word, taken apart.
नियच्छति (niyacchati) is built on ni, "down", and the root √yam, "to hold, to restrain"; MW gives ni-√yam "to restrain, to curb, to control, to govern" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for capable, then the Sanskrit for controls.

You'll want to know: नियन्त्रणम्.
नियन्त्रणम् — niyantraṇam — "control; restraint".
यदि चालकस्य नियन्त्रणं न अस्ति, तर्हि भयम् अस्ति। yadi cālakasya niyantraṇaṃ na asti, tarhi bhayam asti. If the driver has no control, there is danger.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
नियन्त्रणम् (niyantraṇam) is built on ni and the root √yantr, "to restrain, to bind"; MW gives ni-yantraṇa "restraining, checking, governing" (MW).
