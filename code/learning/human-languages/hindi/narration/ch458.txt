Hindi, chapter 458: Worry and Relief.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तनाव (tanāv) — stress; tension.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for cowardly, then the Hindi for to shudder.

You'll want to know: तनाव.
तनाव — tanāv — "stress; tension".
नौकरी के तनाव से अरुण की नींद चली गई। naukrī ke tanāv se Aruṇ kī nīnd calī gaī. The stress of his job has cost Arun his sleep.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
तनाव (tanāv) is probably a noun built on the root of tānnā, "to stretch, pull, tighten", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit tānáyati (CDIAL 5762): a rope pulled tight, and then a mind. CDIAL gives no row for tanāv itself.

Lesson 2 of 5.
फ़िक्र (fikr) — concern; a worry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to shudder, then the Hindi for stress.

You'll want to know: फ़िक्र.
फ़िक्र — fikr — "concern; a worry".
माँ को हमेशा बच्चों की फ़िक्र रहती है। māṁ ko hameśā baccoṁ kī fikr rahtī hai. Mother always worries about the children.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
फ़िक्र (fikr) is usually traced to Arabic fikr, "thought, reflection", which reached Hindi through Persian; in Hindi the thought became a worry.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for stress, then the Hindi for concern.

You'll want to know: बोझ.
बोझ — bojh — "a burden; a weight on the mind".
सच कहने के बाद अरुण के मन से बोझ उतर गया। sac kahne ke bād Aruṇ ke man se bojh utar gayā. Once he had told the truth, a weight lifted from Arun's mind.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
बोझ (bojh) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi bojh, "load", under Sanskrit vahyá (CDIAL 11465). A load on the back and a load on the mind are both bojh.

दाँत के दर्द से अरुण पूरी रात तड़पा। dā̃t ke dard se Aruṇ pūrī rāt taṛpā. Arun writhed all night with toothache.
Past (he …): वह दर्द से तड़पा, vah dard se taṛpā.

The word, taken apart.
तड़पना (taṛapnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi taṛapnā, "to toss, roll about, be anxious", under taḍapphaḍ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5634).

The word, taken apart.
आश्वासन (āśvāsan) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit āśvāsana, "reviving; consoling, encouraging" (Monier-Williams).
