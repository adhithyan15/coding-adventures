## You'll want to know: критиковать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C343-KRITIKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**критиковать** (*kritikovát*) — "to criticise".

> Мне не нравится, когда меня критикуют при всех.
>
> *Mne ne nrávitsya, kogdá menyá kritikúyut pri vsekh.*
>
> I do not like being criticised in front of everyone.

Imperfective, with the present *критикую, критикуешь*; perfective *раскритиковать*, to tear apart. It takes the accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**критиковать** is built on *критика*, which is usually traced, through western languages, to Greek *kritikḗ* "the art of judging", from *krínein* "to separate, to judge".
