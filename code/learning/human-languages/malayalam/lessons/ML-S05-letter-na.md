## Script you'll notice: ന
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ന** — *na*.

It is a **consonant**, and in this script a consonant is never bare: it comes with an *a* already in it. So it is not *n*, it is **na**.

You already say these, and every one of them has ന somewhere inside it:

- **നമസ്കാരം** *namaskāraṁ* — hello / greetings (namaskāraṁ — "a making of a bow")
- **നന്ദി** *nandi* — thank you
- **എന്റെ** *enṟe* — my
- **സന്തോഷം** *santōṣaṁ* — joy / pleased to meet you

## Writing: ന — follow the numbered strip
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-05] -->

Follow the numbered strip: start where movement 1 begins, and let each panel
show you the next movement before your pen makes it. Then write ന yourself —
slowly, and larger than it is printed.

> The strip shows **where to start the character and which way to travel**, in
> one attested order. That is taught with real variation from school to school,
> so the strip names its source beneath it: treat it as a sound way in, not the
> only one.
