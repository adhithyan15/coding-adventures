गली (galī) — a lane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a tenant, then the Hindi for a neighbourhood.

You'll want to know: गली.
गली — galī — "a lane".

The word, taken apart.
गली (galī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from galī, "defile, lane", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4085).

Lesson 4 of 5.
जुलूस (jalūs) — a procession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a street corner, then the Hindi for a fair.

You'll want to know: जुलूस.
जुलूस — jalūs — "a procession".

The word, taken apart.
जुलूस (jalūs) (julūs) is a loan from Arabic julūs, "a sitting; enthronement", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is j-l-s (Lane). Hindi uses it for the procession that went with it.
