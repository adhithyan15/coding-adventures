Latin, chapter 213: Foreign, Honest, Patient, Generous, Idle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
peregrīnus, peregrīna, peregrīnum — foreign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for peaceful, then the Latin for silent.

You'll want to know: peregrīnus, peregrīna, peregrīnum.
peregrīnus, peregrīna, peregrīnum — "foreign".

The word, taken apart.
peregrīnus is built on peregrē, "abroad, from abroad" (Lewis & Short), from per, "through", and ager, "field, territory": beyond one's own lands. English pilgrim comes from it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
probus, proba, probum — honest, upright.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for silent, then the Latin for foreign.

You'll want to know: probus, proba, probum.
probus, proba, probum — "honest, upright".

The word, taken apart.
probus is inherited, usually traced to Proto-Indo-European pro-bhwo-, "being in front, outstanding" (EtymDB 2.1), from pro, "forward", and bhu-, "to be"; Sanskrit prabhu-, "mighty", is a close cousin (Lewis & Short).
