## You'll want to know: نافذ ہونا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C367-QAIDA367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نافذ ہونا** — *nāfiz honā* — "to come into force; to take effect".

> اگر نیا قانون نافذ ہوا تو کرایہ بڑھے گا۔
>
> *agar nayā qānūn nāfiz huā to kirāyā baṛhe gā.*
>
> If the new law comes into force, rents will rise.

Past (it …): وہ قانون نافذ ہو گیا, *voh qānūn nāfiz ho gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نافذ ہونا** (*nāfiz honā*) is built from *nāfiz*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nāfidh*, "piercing; effective", and **ہونا**, "to be".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
