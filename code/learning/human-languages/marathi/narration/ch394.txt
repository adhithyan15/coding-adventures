Marathi, chapter 394: Being Aware.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भान (bhān) — awareness; consciousness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to request, then the Marathi for to follow up.

You'll want to know: भान.
भान — bhān — "awareness; consciousness".
खेळताना मुलांना वेळेचं भान राहिलं नाही. kheḷtānā mulānnā veḷecaṁ bhān rāhilaṁ nāhī. While playing, the children lost track of time.
Neuter.
X चं भान (bhān) ठेवणे, to keep X in mind.

The word, taken apart.
भान (bhān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit bhāna, "appearance" (CDIAL 9451): what appears to the mind.

You'll want to know: जाणीव.
जाणीव — jāṇīv — "a realization; awareness".
मला माझ्या चुकीची जाणीव झाली. malā mājhyā cukīcī jāṇīv jhālī. I realized my mistake.
Feminine.
X ची जाणीव (jāṇīv) होणे, to become aware of X.

The word, taken apart.
जाणीव (jāṇīv) is a noun built on जाणणे, "to know", with the ending -ईव; CDIAL lists the verb under Sanskrit jānāti, "knows" (CDIAL 5193).

आरोग्याबद्दल लोकांमध्ये जागरूकता वाढली आहे. ārogyābaddal lokāmmadhye jāgarūktā vāḍhlī āhe. Awareness about health has grown among people.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उमगणे (umagṇe) — to dawn on; to become clear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for alertness, then the Marathi for good judgement.

You'll want to know: उमगणे.
उमगणे — umagṇe — "to dawn on; to become clear".
खूप वेळाने मला त्याच्या बोलण्याचा अर्थ उमगला. khūp veḷāne malā tyācyā bolaṇyācā artha umaglā. Much later the meaning of his words dawned on me.
Past (to him …): त्याला उमगलं, tyālā umaglaṁ.
Dative: मला उमगलं, it dawned on me.

The word, taken apart.
उमगणे (umagṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi umagṇẽ, "to search out; to become known", under unmārgati, "searches out" (CDIAL 2126).
