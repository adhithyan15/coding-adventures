## You'll want to know: सैलाब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C305-BAADH305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सैलाब** — *sailāb* — "a deluge; a flood".

> इस साल का सैलाब पिछले साल से ज़्यादा बड़ा था।
>
> *is sāl kā sailāb pichle sāl se zyādā baṛā thā.*
>
> This year's flood was bigger than last year's.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सैलाब** (*sailāb*) is usually traced to Persian *sailāb*, "a flood", which joins Arabic *sayl*, a torrent, and Persian *āb*, water. Beside **बाढ़**, it is the word for a great rush of water.
