# ಸ್ಮಶಾನ (smaśāna) — a cremation ground; a cemetery

## You'll want to know: ಸ್ಮಶಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C337-NIDHANA337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಮಶಾನ** — *smaśāna* — "a cremation ground; a cemetery".

> ಸ್ಮಶಾನ ಊರಿನ ಹೊರಗೆ ನದಿಯ ದಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *smaśāna ūrina horage nadiya daḍadalli ide.*
>
> The cremation ground is outside the village, on the river bank.

Used for both a cremation ground and a burial ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಮಶಾನ** (*smaśāna*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śmaśāna*, "a place for burning the dead, a burial place" (CDIAL 12658).
