## You'll want to know: le cordonnier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C446-CORDONNIER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le cordonnier** — "the cobbler, the shoe mender".

> Le cordonnier a réparé mes bottes en une journée.
>
> *The cobbler mended my boots in a day.*

*Les cordonniers sont toujours les plus mal chaussés* — the cobbler's children go barefoot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cordonnier* is an altered form of an older word for a worker in fine leather, named after a city famous for it. EtymDB lists it without a source.
