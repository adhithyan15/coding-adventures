## You'll want to know: नाकपुडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C476-CEHRA476-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नाकपुडी** — *nākpuḍī* — "a nostril".

> सर्दीमुळे एक नाकपुडी बंद झाली.
>
> *sardīmuḷe ek nākpuḍī band jhālī.*
>
> With the cold, one nostril got blocked.

Feminine. Plural: नाकपुड्या, *nākpuḍyā*.

नाक + पुडी, a little packet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाकपुडी** (*nākpuḍī*) is a Marathi compound built from नाक, "nose", which CDIAL lists under *\*nakka* (CDIAL 6909), and पुडी, "a little packet".
