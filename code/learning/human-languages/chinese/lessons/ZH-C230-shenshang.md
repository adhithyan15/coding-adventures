# 身上 (shēnshang) — on your person; on one's body

## You'll want to know: 身上
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C230-BODY230-01]; assesses=[] -->

**身上** — *shēnshang* — "on your person; on one's body".

> **我身上没有钱** *wǒ shēnshang méiyǒu qián* — I haven't got any money on me.

The new character, in pieces: 身 — seven strokes: a short fall to the left, a short vertical, a bar that turns down into a long hook, two short bars inside, a long stroke rising to the right, and a long stroke falling to the left. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**身上** (*shēnshang*) is built from 身 'body' and 上 'on', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syin* and *dzyangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'oneself' and 'the body'; in the word the second loses its tone.
