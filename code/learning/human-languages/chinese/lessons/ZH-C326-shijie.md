## You'll want to know: 世界
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C326-WORLD326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**世界** — *shìjiè* — "world".

> **我想去看世界** *wǒ xiǎng qù kàn shìjiè* — I want to go and see the world.

The new character, in pieces: 界 — nine strokes: on top a field, a box of five strokes cut into four; under it 人 opened wide, and under that a short stroke falling to the left and a vertical. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**世界** (*shìjiè*) is built from 世 'a generation' and *jiè* 'a boundary', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syejH* and *keajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a generation' and the second 'a border' and 'an edge'. The sense 'the world' is not given there.
