## You'll want to know: もやす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C338-MUDA338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**もやす** — *moyasu* — "to burn something".

> **ごみを もやしたら、くうきが よごれます。** — *gomi o moyashitara, kūki ga yogoremasu* — "Burning rubbish makes the air dirty."

Polite **もやします**, *moyashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが ごみを もやしました。**, *otoko ga gomi o moyashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もやす** (*moyasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
