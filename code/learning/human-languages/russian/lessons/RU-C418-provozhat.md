## You'll want to know: провожать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C418-PERESADKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**провожать** (*provozhát*) — "to see off".

> Родители провожают сына на вокзале.
>
> *Rodíteli provozháyut sýna na vokzále.*
>
> The parents are seeing their son off at the station.

Imperfective; perfective *проводить* (*провожу, проводишь*). The imperfective *проводить* is a different verb — to spend (time).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*провожать* is built on *проводить* 'to lead along, to accompany', from *про-* and *водить* 'to lead'. *Водить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *voditi* (EtymDB).
