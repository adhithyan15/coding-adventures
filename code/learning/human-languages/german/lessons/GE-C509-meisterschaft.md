## You'll want to know: die Meisterschaft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C509-WETTKAMPF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Meisterschaft** — "championship".

> Unser Verein hat die Meisterschaft gewonnen.
>
> *Our club won the championship.*

Plural: *die Meisterschaften*. *Die Weltmeisterschaft* — the World Cup, a world championship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Meisterschaft* is built on *Meister* "master, champion", which came through Old High German *meistar* from Latin *magister* (EtymDB), with *-schaft*.
