## You'll want to know: ગર્વ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C309-INAM309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ગર્વ** — *garva* — "pride".

> માતાને દીકરા પર ખૂબ ગર્વ હતો.
>
> *mātāne dīkrā par khūb garva hato.*
>
> The mother was very proud of her son.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગર્વ** (*garva*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *garva*, "pride, arrogance" (CDIAL 4064). In Gujarati it is often good pride: **દીકરા પર ગર્વ**, proud of a son.
