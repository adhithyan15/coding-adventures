## You'll want to know: ગાર્ડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C396-GUARD396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાર્ડ** — *gārḍ* — "a guard (on a train or at a gate)".

> ટ્રેનના છેલ્લા ડબ્બામાં ગાર્ડ બેસે છે.
>
> *ṭrennā chellā ḍabbāmā̃ gārḍ bese che.*
>
> The guard sits in the last carriage of the train.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાર્ડ** (*gārḍ*) is borrowed from English *guard*: on the railway, the guard who rides at the back of the train.
