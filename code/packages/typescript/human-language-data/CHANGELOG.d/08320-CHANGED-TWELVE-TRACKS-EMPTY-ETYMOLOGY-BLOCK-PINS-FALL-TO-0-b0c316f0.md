### Changed — twelve tracks' empty etymology block pins fall to 0

`EMPTY_ETYMOLOGY_BLOCK_PINS` in `tests/root-ledger.test.ts` falls to 0 for twelve tracks: French 66, Russian 52, Spanish 48, German 43, Latin 31, Tamil 12, Kannada 8, Telugu 8, Hindi 7, Italian 7, Malayalam 6 and Portuguese 5, 293 lessons in all. Arabic (19), Chinese (43) and Sanskrit (12) are unchanged and left to their own work. The pin's comment records the pass.

- **What the lessons gained.** Each flagged "taken apart" block now says where its word, or each part of its phrase, comes from: 227 gained a new origin sentence, and 66 that already stated an origin in a shape no signal reads (such as "Behind it is papyrus") were minimally reworded. Sources are EtymDB 2.1 and Lewis & Short for the Romance tracks, Latin and German, Vasmer (paraphrased) for Russian, Turner's CDIAL for Hindi, and DEDR with CDIAL for the Dravidian tracks. Ten hooks that were not etymology, or were wrong, were rewritten, and four errors in existing text were corrected: German *viel* is not English *full*, German *warum*'s *war-* is "where", not "what", Spanish *pastilla* comes from Latin *pastillus*, and Russian *пожалуй* has two readings in Vasmer. Each track changelog has the detail.
- **Measured, not loosened.** `src/etymology-coverage.ts` is unchanged; every lesson was cleared by its own text. No `roots:` slug was added (`check:root-slug-splits`: 77 known entries, none new), no corpus pin moved, and no `max_seconds` was raised. Lessons computing above 270 s were reworded or trimmed so they did not grow.
