## You'll want to know: وارث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C308-SHAHENSHAH308-05]; assesses=[] -->

**وارث** — *vāris* — "an heir".

> بادشاہ کا کوئی وارث نہیں تھا۔
>
> *bādshāh kā koī vāris nahīṅ thā.*
>
> The king had no heir.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وارث** (*vāris*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wārith*, "one who inherits". Urdu says its last letter as *s*.
