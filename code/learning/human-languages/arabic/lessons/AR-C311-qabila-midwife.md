## You'll want to know: قابلة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C311-WILADA311-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قابلة** — *qābila* — "a midwife".

> ساعدت القابلة الأم في الولادة.
>
> *sāʿadat al-qābila al-umm fī al-wilāda.*
>
> The midwife helped the mother with the birth.

Not the taught verb قابل, to meet: قابلة is a noun here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قابلة** (*qābila*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-l* (Lane), "to face; to receive". *Qābila* is the active participle, "she who receives": the midwife is usually explained as the woman who receives the child at its birth.
