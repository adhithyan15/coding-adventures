## You'll want to know: اشترط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C404-SHART404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اشترط** — *ishtaraṭa* — "to stipulate".

> اشترطت الشركة خبرة سنتين.
>
> *ishtaraṭat ash-sharika khibrat sanatayn.*
>
> The company stipulated two years of experience.

Present: يشترط, *yashtariṭ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشترط** (*ishtaraṭa*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-ṭ*, "to set a mark; to stipulate" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to set a condition for oneself: to stipulate.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
