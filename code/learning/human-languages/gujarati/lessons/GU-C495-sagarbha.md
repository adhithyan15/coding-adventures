## You'll want to know: સગર્ભા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C495-SAGARBHA495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સગર્ભા** — *sagarbhā* — "pregnant; a pregnant woman".

> મારી ભાભી સગર્ભા છે.
>
> *mārī bhābhī sagarbhā che.*
>
> My sister-in-law is pregnant.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સગર્ભા** (*sagarbhā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sagarbhā*, "a pregnant woman", as Monier-Williams records it: *sa-*, "with", and *garbha*, "womb, child".
