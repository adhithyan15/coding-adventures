## You'll want to know: ōtiōsus, ōtiōsa, ōtiōsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C538-OTIU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ōtiōsus, ōtiōsa, ōtiōsum** — "at leisure, idle".

> Diē fēstō cīvēs ōtiōsī in forō ambulant.
>
> *On the holiday the citizens stroll at leisure in the forum.*

From *ōtium*: free of business, with no blame meant. English *otiose*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ōtiōsus** is built on *ōtium* "leisure" with *-ōsus* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *otiose* comes from it.
