## You'll want to know: ગપ્પું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C468-JUTHU468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગપ્પું** — *gappũ* — "a tall tale".

> દાદાનાં ગપ્પાં સાંભળીને બધા હસ્યા.
>
> *dādānā̃ gappā̃ sā̃bhḷīne badhā hasyā.*
>
> Everyone laughed at my grandfather's tall tales.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગપ્પું** (*gappũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*gappa-*, "talk", probably an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *gap*, "tittle-tattle" (CDIAL 4022).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
