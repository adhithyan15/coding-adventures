## You'll want to know: 文明
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C326-WORLD326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**文明** — *wénmíng* — "civilization".

> **中国有很古老的文明** *Zhōngguó yǒu hěn gǔlǎo de wénmíng* — China has a very ancient civilization.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**文明** (*wénmíng*) is built from 文 'writing' and 明 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjun* and *mjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'writing' and 'beautiful' and the second 'light'. The sense 'civilization' is not given there.
