## You'll want to know: obwohl
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C341-OBWOHL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**obwohl** — "although".

> Wir gehen spazieren, obwohl es regnet.
>
> *We are going for a walk although it is raining.*

*Obwohl* sends the verb to the end, like *weil*: *…, obwohl es regnet*. *Trotzdem* says the same thing from the other side: *Es regnet. Trotzdem gehen wir spazieren.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obwohl* joins *ob* "if, whether" and *wohl* "well", inherited from Old High German *wola* and Proto-Germanic *wela* (EtymDB): "even if it may well be so".
