## You'll want to know: временный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C380-BUDUSHCHEYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**временный** (*vrémennyy*) — "temporary".

> Это временная работа на лето.
>
> *Éto vrémennaya rabóta na léto.*
>
> It is a temporary job for the summer.

The opposite of *постоянный*. *Временно* — for the time being.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**временный** is built on *время* "time", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*vermę*; the *-ре-* is the Church Slavonic shape, beside native *веремя* in old texts.
