## You'll want to know: définitif
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C371-ANNULER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**définitif** — "final, definitive".

> Sa décision est définitive.
>
> *Her decision is final.*

Feminine *définitive*. *En définitive* — in the end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*définitif* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēfīnītīvus* (EtymDB), from *dēfīnīre* "to set limits, to define", built on *fīnis* "end, boundary" (Lewis & Short).
