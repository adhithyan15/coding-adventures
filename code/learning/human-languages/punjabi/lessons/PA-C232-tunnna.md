## You'll want to know: ਤੁੰਨਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C232-DIN232-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੁੰਨਣਾ** — *tunnṇā* — "to stuff; to cram".

> ਮੈਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਬਸਤੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਤੁੰਨਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ kappṛe baste vich tunndā hāṁ.*
>
> I stuff my clothes into the bag.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਤੁੰਨੇ, *maiṁ kappṛe tunne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੁੰਨਣਾ** (*tunnṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tunna*, "struck"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tunnṇā*, "to stow, stuff" (CDIAL 5863).
