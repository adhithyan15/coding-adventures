## You'll want to know: 心想事成
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C533-WISH533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**心想事成** — *xīnxiǎng shìchéng* — "may your wishes all come true".

> **祝你们好运心想事成** *zhù nǐmen hǎoyùn xīnxiǎng shìchéng* — Good luck to you all, and may all your wishes come true.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**心想事成** (*xīnxiǎng shìchéng*) is built from 心 'the heart', 想 'to wish', 事 'a matter' and 成 'done', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim*, *sjangX*, *dzriH* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): what the heart thinks of, may it be done.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
