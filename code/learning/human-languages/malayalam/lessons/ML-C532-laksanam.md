# ലക്ഷണം (lakṣaṇaṁ) — a symptom; a sign

## You'll want to know: ലക്ഷണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C532-ROGAM532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ലക്ഷണം** — *lakṣaṇaṁ* — "a symptom; a sign".

> പനിയും ചുമയും ആ രോഗത്തിന്റെ ലക്ഷണങ്ങളാണ്.
>
> *paniyuṁ cumayuṁ ā rōgattinṟe lakṣaṇaṅṅaḷāṇŭ.*
>
> Fever and a cough are symptoms of that illness.

Plural: ലക്ഷണങ്ങൾ, *lakṣaṇaṅṅaḷ*.

It also means a sign or an omen: നല്ല ലക്ഷണം, a good sign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ലക്ഷണം** (*lakṣaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lakṣaṇa*, "a line, a mark, a sign" (CDIAL 10882). A symptom is the mark an illness leaves.

## What you've built
