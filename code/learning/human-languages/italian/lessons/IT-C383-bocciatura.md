## You'll want to know: la bocciatura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C383-BOCCIATURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la bocciatura** — "the failing (of a year or an exam)".

> Dopo la bocciatura ha cambiato scuola.
>
> *After failing the year he changed school.*

Built on *bocciare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bocciatura* is built on *bocciare* "to fail (a student)", from *boccia* "a ball for bowls" (EtymDB); the origin of *boccia* is not settled.
