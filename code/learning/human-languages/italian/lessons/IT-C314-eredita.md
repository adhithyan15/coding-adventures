## You'll want to know: l'eredità
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C314-EREDITA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'eredità** — "the inheritance; heritage".

> L'eredità è divisa tra tre fratelli.
>
> *The inheritance is split between three brothers.*

Also a cultural heritage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eredità* is inherited from Latin *hērēditās* "inheritance" (EtymDB).
