## You'll want to know: やくそく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C248-TRUST248-03]; assesses=[] -->

**やくそく** — *yakusoku* — "a promise; an appointment".

> **あしたの やくそくは ごごですか。** — *ashita no yakusoku wa gogo desu ka* — "Is tomorrow's appointment in the afternoon?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やくそく** (*yakusoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yaku*, "promise" (Middle Chinese *'jak*) and *soku*, "bundle, tie" (*syowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
