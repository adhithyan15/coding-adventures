## You'll want to know: तिलकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C330-PARAMPARA330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तिलकः** — *tilakaḥ* — "a forehead mark; a tilaka".

> माता पुत्रस्य ललाटे तिलकं करोति।
>
> *mātā putrasya lalāṭe tilakaṃ karoti.*
>
> Mother puts a mark on her son's forehead.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिलकः** (*tilakaḥ*) MW gives *tilaka* as a mark on the forehead, of coloured earth, sandal or unguents, and as a freckle, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
