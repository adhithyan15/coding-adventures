Arabic, chapter 60: To cook, To wash, To laugh, To cry, To dance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طبخ (ṭabakha) — to cook (he cooked).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to open, then the Arabic for to close.

You'll want to know: طبخ.
طبخ — ṭabakha — "to cook (he cooked)".

The word, taken apart.
طبخ (ṭabakha) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-b-kh, "to cook" (Lane). The same root gives maṭbakh, "a kitchen".
