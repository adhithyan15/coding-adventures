## You'll want to know: निष्कर्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C366-NISHKARSHA366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कर्षः** — *niṣkarṣaḥ* — "a conclusion; the gist".

> अनुसन्धानस्य निष्कर्षः स्पष्टः अस्ति।
>
> *anusandhānasya niṣkarṣaḥ spaṣṭaḥ asti.*
>
> The conclusion of the research is clear.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्कर्षः** (*niṣkarṣaḥ*) is built on *nis*, "out", and the root √kṛṣ, "to draw"; MW gives *niṣ-karṣa* "drawing out" and "the essence, the chief point", and "a conclusion" is its modern sense (MW).
