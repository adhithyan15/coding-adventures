Italian, chapter 367: Dare una mano.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il volontariato — the voluntary work, volunteering.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to discriminate, then the Italian for the immigration.

You'll want to know: il volontariato.
il volontariato — "the voluntary work, volunteering".
Il sabato faccio volontariato in ospedale. On Saturdays I do voluntary work at the hospital.
Fare volontariato — to volunteer.

The word, taken apart.
volontariato is built on volontario, a learned borrowing from Latin voluntārius "willing, of free will" (Lewis & Short), from voluntās "will", built on velle "to want".

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'associazione — the association, the organisation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the voluntary work, then the Italian for the volunteer.

You'll want to know: l'associazione.
l'associazione — "the association, the organisation".
Lavoro per un'associazione che aiuta i bambini. I work for an organisation that helps children.
Un'associazione culturale — a cultural club.

The word, taken apart.
associazione is built on associare "to join together", a learned borrowing from Late Latin associāre "to join to" (Lewis & Short), built on socius "a companion, an ally".
