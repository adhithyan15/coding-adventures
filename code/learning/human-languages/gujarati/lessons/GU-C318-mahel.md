## You'll want to know: મહેલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C318-RAJA318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેલ** — *mahel* — "a palace".

> રાજા અને રાણી મોટા મહેલમાં રહેતાં હતાં.
>
> *rājā ane rāṇī moṭā mahelmā̃ rahetā̃ hatā̃.*
>
> The king and queen lived in a big palace.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેલ** (*mahel*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maḥall*, "a place, a residence". A king and a queen together take the plural **રહેતાં હતાં**, the form for a man and a woman.
