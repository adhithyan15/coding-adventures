# إذا أمطرت (idhā amṭarat) — if it rains

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IDHA-PAST-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if I arrive early, I'll have a coffee". (*Idhā waṣaltu
mubakkiran, ashrabu qahwa*.) Then "if you arrive early", with the other "if".
(*In waṣalta mubakkiran*.)

[PAUSE 1s] The result in that sentence was a plain present. Many results are
not, and then Arabic marks the seam.

## Grammar Lens: fa- before the result
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C196-ACT196-03, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-FUTURE-01, AR-LEX-C228-ACT228-01, AR-LEX-C184-SHAY184-05, AR-LEX-C69-FOOD69-03] -->

> **إذا أمطرت، فسنشاهد فيلما في المنزل.** — *idhā amṭarat, fa-sa-nushāhidu
> fīlman fī al-manzil* — If it rains, we'll watch a film at home.

*Amṭarat* is "it rained", and after *idhā*, "it rains". The result opens with
*sa-*, and so it takes **فـ** in front: *fa-sa-nushāhidu*. It is the *fa-*
of "so": the condition comes true, so this follows.

## Grammar Lens: four results that take fa-
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-LAN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01, AR-LEX-C255-ACT255-03, AR-LEX-C64-VERBS64-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-ELATIVE-01, AR-LEX-C91-TOWN91-05] -->

A result needs *fa-* when it opens with *sa-* or *sawfa*, with *lan*, with
an order, or with a noun. Four sentences, one of each:

> *idhā amṭarat, fa-sa-nushāhidu fīlman* · *idhā amṭarat, fa-lan nakhruja*,
> we won't go out · *idhā waṣalta, fa-ttaṣil bī*, call me · *idhā taʾakhkhara
> al-qiṭār, fa-l-ḥāfila afḍal*, the bus is better

A plain present, like *ashrabu qahwa*, needs none. When you are unsure, the
*fa-* before *sa-* is the one you will use most.
