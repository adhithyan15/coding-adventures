## You'll want to know: ಜಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C328-DAROODE328-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಡು** — *jāḍu* — "a track; a trail".

> ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಕಾರಿನ ಜಾಡು ಹಿಡಿದು ಹೋದರು.
>
> *polīsaru kārina jāḍu hiḍidu hōdaru.*
>
> The police followed the car's trail.

ಜಾಡು ಹಿಡಿ, to follow a trail. Not to be confused with ಜಾಡಿ, a jar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಡು** (*jāḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2442 lists Kannada *jāḍu*, "the mark of a footstep or of a cart wheel, a track, a trace".
