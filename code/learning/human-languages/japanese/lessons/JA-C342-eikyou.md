## You'll want to know: えいきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C342-GENIN342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**えいきょう** — *eikyō* — "an influence; an effect".

> **たいふうの えいきょうで、でんしゃが とまりました。** — *taifū no eikyō de, densha ga tomarimashita* — "The trains stopped because of the typhoon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えいきょう** (*eikyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ei*, "shadow" (Middle Chinese *'jaengX*) and *kyō*, "echo" (*xjangX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
