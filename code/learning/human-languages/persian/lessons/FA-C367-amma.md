## You'll want to know: اما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C367-AMMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اما** (*ammâ*) — "but, however".

> هوا سرد بود، اما ما بیرون رفتیم.
>
> *havâ sard bud, ammâ mâ birun raftim.*
>
> It was cold, but we went out.

A little more formal than *vali*; both join two clauses, and *ammâ* can also open a sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اما** comes from Arabic *ammā* "as for, as regards" (EtymDB); Persian uses it to mean "but".
