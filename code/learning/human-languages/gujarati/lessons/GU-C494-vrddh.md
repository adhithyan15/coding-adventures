## You'll want to know: વૃદ્ધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C494-BALPAN494-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વૃદ્ધ** — *vṛddh* — "elderly; an old person".

> બસમાં વૃદ્ધ માણસને બેસવાની જગ્યા આપો.
>
> *basmā̃ vṛddh māṇasne besvānī jagyā āpo.*
>
> On the bus, give your seat to an elderly person.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વૃદ્ધ** (*vṛddh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vṛddha*, "grown, old", as Monier-Williams records it.
