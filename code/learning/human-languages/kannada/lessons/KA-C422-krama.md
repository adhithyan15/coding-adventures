## You'll want to know: ಕ್ರಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C422-MANADANDA422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕ್ರಮ** — *krama* — "a course of action; a measure".

> ಕಳ್ಳತನ ತಡೆಯಲು ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಹೊಸ ಕ್ರಮ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡರು.
>
> *kaḷḷatana taḍeyalu polīsaru hosa krama tegedukoṇḍaru.*
>
> The police took new measures to stop theft.

ಕ್ರಮವಾಗಿ, in order; ಕ್ರಮ ಕೈಗೊಳ್ಳು, to take action.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕ್ರಮ** (*krama*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *krama*, "a step, going, order" (CDIAL 3577).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
