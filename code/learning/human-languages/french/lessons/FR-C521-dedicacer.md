## You'll want to know: dédicacer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C521-AUTEURS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dédicacer** — "to sign (a book for someone)".

> L'écrivain dédicace ses livres à la librairie samedi.
>
> *The writer is signing his books at the bookshop on Saturday.*

Spelling: *nous dédicaçons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dédicacer* is built on *dédicace*, a learned borrowing from Latin *dēdicātiō*, from *dēdicāre* "to dedicate" (Lewis & Short).
