## You'll want to know: اوایل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C307-AVAYEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اوایل** (*avâyel*) — "early in, the early part".

> اوایل بهار به خانه جدید رفتیم.
>
> *Avâyel-e bahâr be khâne-ye jadid raftim.*
>
> Early in the spring we moved to the new house.

An Arabic broken plural of *avval* "first". With the ezafe: *avâyel-e mâh* "early in the month".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avâyel* is usually traced to Arabic *awā'il*, the broken plural of *awwal* "first", which Persian also has as *avval*. EtymDB lists no entry for the plural.
