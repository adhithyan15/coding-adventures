# أعتقد أن (aʿtaqidu anna) — I think that

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C40-ANNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "in my opinion". (*Fī raʾyī*.) Then say *aktub anna Sāmī
jārī*, "I write that Sami is my neighbour".

[PAUSE 1s] *Anna* hangs a whole sentence on a verb. Hang it on a verb of
thinking, and you can give an opinion.

## Grammar Lens: aʿtaqidu anna, aẓunnu anna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C285-OPINION-ANNA-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C164-ACT164-04, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PRESENT-PERSONS-01, AR-LEX-C221-AMAL221-04, AR-LEX-C83-ADJ83-04] -->

*Iʿtaqada*, to believe, from chapter one hundred and sixty-four, has the
present *aʿtaqidu*, I believe, I think. Put *anna* after it:

> **أعتقد أن الامتحان صعب.** — *aʿtaqidu anna al-imtiḥāna ṣaʿb* — I think
> the exam is hard.

The noun after *anna* takes *-a*: *al-imtiḥāna*. With "it" the pronoun
joins *anna* itself: *aʿtaqidu annahu ṣaʿb*, I think it's hard.

**أظن أن** — *aẓunnu anna* — is "I suppose", a little less sure:
*aẓunnu anna al-qiṭāra sarīʿ*, I suppose the train is fast.

## Grammar Lens: agreeing and not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C285-OPINION-ANNA-01, AR-LEX-C240-SIFA240-05, AR-LEX-C184-SHAY184-05] -->

For "I don't think so", put *lā* in front and *dhālika*, "that", after:
**لا أعتقد ذلك** — *lā aʿtaqidu dhālika*.

> — *mā raʾyuka fī al-fīlm?* — *aʿtaqidu annahu mumtāz.* — *lā aʿtaqidu
> dhālika!*

Three ways to start an opinion, then: *fī raʾyī*, *aʿtaqidu anna*, *aẓunnu
anna*. English drops its "that"; Arabic keeps *anna*.

- [YOU SAY: "I think the lawyer is here" — *aʿtaqidu anna al-muḥāmiya hunā*]
