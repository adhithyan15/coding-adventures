Lesson 2 of 5.
One bounded feminine adjective form — blue, feminine singular direct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve familiar ਨੀਲਾ, “blue,” and the familiar rule that a variable adjective agrees with a feminine noun.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਨੀਲਾ — nīlā — blue, masculine singular direct ਨੀਲੀ (nili) — nīlī — blue, feminine singular direct.
For this one form, change the final -ਾ ending of familiar ਨੀਲਾ to feminine singular -ੀ. The result is ਨੀਲੀ (nili). Keep the stem and meaning steady; only the ending changes.

Grammar Lens: source boundary.
Punjabi University Patiala's reference grammar says that inflectable adjectives agree with their head noun in gender, number, and case. Its noun-phrase examples contrast masculine ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ with feminine ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ. Source: Punjabi University reference grammar.
That source supports this one feminine singular direct ending change. This chapter does not teach a full agreement paradigm, plural or oblique forms, a new colour, or a general contrast between attributive and predicative uses.

[once you have stopped driving — check: familiar ਨੀਲਾ ends in -ਾ; exact feminine singular ਨੀਲੀ (nili) ends in -ੀ]

Lesson 4 of 5.
Put the new form before one familiar feminine noun — this is a blue shoe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the new feminine colour form, familiar “shoe,” familiar “this,” and familiar “is.”

Grammar Lens.
ਇਹ ਨੀਲੀ ਜੁੱਤੀ ਹੈ (eh nili jutti hai) — eh nīlī juttī hai — This is a blue shoe.
For this one frame, begin with familiar ਇਹ, place new ਨੀਲੀ (nili) before the familiar feminine noun ਜੁੱਤੀ, and keep familiar ਹੈ at the end. The adjective is feminine singular direct because it agrees with this one noun.
