## You'll want to know: le baptême
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C309-BAPTEME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le baptême** — "the christening, the baptism".

> Toute la famille est venue au baptême.
>
> *The whole family came to the christening.*

The *p* is silent. *Le baptême de l'air* — a first flight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*baptême* comes through Old French *batesme* (EtymDB) from Church Latin *baptisma*, a borrowing from Greek *báptisma* "a dipping".
