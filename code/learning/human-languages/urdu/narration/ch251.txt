5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گڑیا (guṛiyā) — a doll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a clay oven, then the Urdu for a kettle.

You'll want to know: گڑیا.
گڑیا — guṛiyā — "a doll".

The word, taken apart.
گڑیا (guṛiyā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from guḍḍa, "doll, effigy", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4189).

Lesson 2 of 5.
پھولدان (phūldān) — a vase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a kettle, then the Urdu for a doll.

You'll want to know: پھولدان.
پھولدان — phūldān — "a vase".

The word, taken apart.
پھولدان (phūldān) is "flower-holder". phūl, "flower", comes from Sanskrit phulla, "blown, opened out" (CDIAL 9092), and -dān, "holder, vessel", is Persian.
