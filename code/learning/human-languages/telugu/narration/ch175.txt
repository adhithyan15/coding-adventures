Telugu, chapter 175: Crowd, World, Earthquake, Dust, Smoke.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
గుంపు (gumpu) — a crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a cave, then the Telugu for a valley.

You'll want to know: గుంపు.
గుంపు — gumpu — "a crowd".

The word, taken apart.
గుంపు (gumpu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1741 lists gumpu, "a crowd, group", beside gumi, "crowd", with Tamil kumi, "to be heaped up, crowd together".
