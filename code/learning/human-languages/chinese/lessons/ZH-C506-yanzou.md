## You'll want to know: yǎnzòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C506-BAND506-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnzòu* — "to perform on an instrument".

> *tā yǎnzòu de hěn hǎo* — He plays beautifully.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnzòu* is built from *yǎn* 'to perform' and *zòu* 'to present', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yenX* and *tsuwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to spread wide' and the second 'to offer up'; the sense 'to play music' is not given there.
