Telugu, chapter 492: Mistakes and Mix-ups.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పొరబడు (porabaḍu) — to be mistaken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to crack, then the Telugu for to deteriorate.

You'll want to know: పొరబడు.
పొరబడు — porabaḍu — "to be mistaken".
ఆ బస్సు మనదే అని పొరబడ్డాను. ā bassu manadē ani porabaḍḍānu. I mistook that bus for ours.
Past (I …): పొరబడ్డాను, porabaḍḍānu.
పొర, an error, + పడు. పొరపాటు, a mistake, comes from the same పొర.

The word, taken apart.
పొరబడు (porabaḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4293 lists Telugu pora, "error, misunderstanding", and porabaḍu, "to go the wrong way".

Lesson 2 of 5.
తడబడు (taḍabaḍu) — to falter; to stumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to deteriorate, then the Telugu for to be mistaken.

You'll want to know: తడబడు.
తడబడు — taḍabaḍu — "to falter; to stumble".
కొత్త భాషలో మాట్లాడుతూ తడబడ్డాను. kotta bhāṣalō māṭlāḍutū taḍabaḍḍānu. I faltered while speaking the new language.
Past (I …): తడబడ్డాను, taḍabaḍḍānu.
Said of the feet, the tongue and a nervous speaker.

The word, taken apart.
తడబడు (taḍabaḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3033 lists Telugu taḍabaḍu, "to be confused, bewildered", with Tamil taṭavu, "to be halting".

Lesson 3 of 5.
అంచనా వేయు (añcanā vēyu) — to estimate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be mistaken, then the Telugu for to falter.

You'll want to know: అంచనా వేయు.
అంచనా వేయు — añcanā vēyu — "to estimate".
ప్రయాణానికి ఎంత ఖర్చు అవుతుందో అంచనా వేశాం. prayāṇāniki enta kharcu avutundō añcanā vēśāṁ. We estimated how much the trip would cost.
Past (I …): అంచనా వేశాను, añcanā vēśānu.
అంచనా, an estimate, + వేయు, to put. The weather news also gives అంచనాలు, forecasts.

The word, taken apart.
అంచనా వేయు (añcanā vēyu) is a Telugu phrase: అంచనా, "an estimate", whose origin is not settled, and the native వేయు, "to put" (DEDR 5549).

Lesson 4 of 5.
తప్పటడుగు (tappaṭaḍugu) — a false step; a blunder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to falter, then the Telugu for to estimate.

You'll want to know: తప్పటడుగు.
తప్పటడుగు — tappaṭaḍugu — "a false step; a blunder".
తొందరపడి ఆ ఉద్యోగం వదలడం నా తప్పటడుగు. tondarapaḍi ā udyōgaṁ vadalaḍaṁ nā tappaṭaḍugu. Leaving that job in a hurry was my false step.
తప్పు, wrong, + అడుగు, a step. A toddler's first wobbly steps are తప్పటడుగులు too.

The word, taken apart.
తప్పటడుగు (tappaṭaḍugu) is native Dravidian: తప్పు, "to slip, to go wrong" (DEDR 3071), and అడుగు, "a footstep, a step" (DEDR 72), joined by -ట-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
గందరగోళం (gandaragōḷaṁ) — confusion; a muddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to estimate, then the Telugu for a false step.

You'll want to know: గందరగోళం.
గందరగోళం — gandaragōḷaṁ — "confusion; a muddle".
టిక్కెట్లు తారుమారు కావడంతో స్టేషన్లో గందరగోళం మొదలైంది. ṭikkeṭlu tārumāru kāvaḍantō sṭēṣanlō gandaragōḷaṁ modalaindi. When the tickets got mixed up, confusion broke out at the station.
గందరగోళంగా ఉంది, it is confusing; గందరగోళం (gandaragōḷaṁ) చేయు, to make a mess of things.

The word, taken apart.
గందరగోళం (gandaragōḷaṁ) is an everyday Telugu word for confusion and uproar whose origin is not settled.
