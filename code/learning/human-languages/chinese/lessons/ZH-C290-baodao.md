## You'll want to know: bàodào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C290-NEWS290-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bàodào* — "to report on; a news report".

> *diànshì bàodào le zhège xiāoxi* — The news was reported on TV.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàodào* is built from *bào* 'to report' and *dào*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawH* and *dawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to report' and the second 'a road' and 'a principle'; its sense 'to say' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
