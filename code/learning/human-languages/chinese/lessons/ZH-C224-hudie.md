## You'll want to know: húdié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C224-BLOOM224-04]; assesses=[] -->

*húdié* — "butterfly".

> *huāyuán lǐ yǒu liǎng zhī húdié* — There are two butterflies in the garden.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*húdié* is built from two characters, and the dictionary glosses the second, *dep* there (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), as a butterfly. No dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
