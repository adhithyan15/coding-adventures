# Review — ask, listen, check, thank

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-DESTRA-SINISTRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "on the left", then "on the right". (*A sinistra, a destra.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-SCUSI-01, IT-GRAMMAR-PER-ANDARE-A-02, IT-GRAMMAR-GIRI-A-DESTRA-02, IT-GRAMMAR-POI-01] -->

Asking the way has four steps, and you now have Italian for every one.

[PAUSE 2s] **Ask**: *Scusi, per andare alla stazione?* **Listen**: *Vada
dritto, poi giri a sinistra all'incrocio.* **Check**, by saying the answer
back as a question: *Dritto, poi a sinistra?* **Thank**: *Grazie!*

The check is the step travellers skip, and it is the one that saves the walk.
A stranger who hears their own directions said back will correct the one
piece you got wrong.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-SCUSI-01, IT-GRAMMAR-GIRI-A-DESTRA-02, IT-LEX-SECONDO-ME-01, IT-LEX-DOVREI-01, IT-GRAMMAR-SPIEGAZIONE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What are the four steps? (**Ask**, **listen**, **check**,
**thank**.) Which one do travellers skip? (**The check.**) And if you think a
friend is lost, how do you advise them to ask, with a reason? (*Secondo me
**dovresti** chiedere, perché la stazione è lontana.*)
