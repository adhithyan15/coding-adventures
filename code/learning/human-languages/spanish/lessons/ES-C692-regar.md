## You'll want to know: regar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C692-SEMBRAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**regar** — "to water (plants)".

> Riego las plantas por la noche.
>
> *I water the plants in the evening.*

The e becomes ie under stress: *riego*, *riega*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regar* is inherited from Latin *rigāre* "to water, to moisten" (EtymDB).
