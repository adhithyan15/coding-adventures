# Ask do-you questions again

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-HABITUAL-215] -->

[YOU HEAR: *do you drink tea?*] Say **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ?**

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-HABITUAL-215, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-05, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *does she go to school every day?*] Say **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *yes, she goes to school every day*] Say **ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੇ ਹੋ?**] Give its meaning.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹੈ?** once]
