# কর্মঠ (kôrmôṭh) — industrious; always busy at work

## You'll want to know: কর্মঠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C462-OLOS462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কর্মঠ** — *kôrmôṭh* — "industrious; always busy at work".

> দিদিমা সারাদিন কাজ করেন, তিনি খুব কর্মঠ।
>
> *didimā sārādin kāj kôren, tini khub kôrmôṭh.*
>
> My grandmother works all day long: she is very industrious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কর্মঠ** (*kôrmôṭh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *karmaṭha*, "capable of work, working diligently" (Monier-Williams).
