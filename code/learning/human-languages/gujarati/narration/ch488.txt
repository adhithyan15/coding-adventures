Gujarati, chapter 488: Bath Time for the Baby.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નવડાવવું (navḍāvvũ) — to bathe someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to massage by pressing, then the Gujarati for to cover someone with a blanket.

You'll want to know: નવડાવવું.
નવડાવવું — navḍāvvũ — "to bathe someone".
માતા રોજ સવારે બાળકને નવડાવે છે. mātā roj savāre bāḷakne navḍāve che. The mother bathes the baby every morning.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને નવડાવ્યું, teṇe bāḷakne navḍāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
નવડાવવું (navḍāvvũ) is the causative of નાહવું (nāhvũ), "to bathe", which is inherited from Sanskrit snāti, "bathes" (CDIAL 13786).
