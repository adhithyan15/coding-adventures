German, chapter 60: Healthy, Thirst, Hunger, Sad, Happy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
gesund — healthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a hospital, then the German for ill.

You'll want to know: gesund.
gesund — "healthy".

The word, taken apart.
gesund from Old High German gisunt from Proto-Germanic gasundaz, "sound, whole, healthy" — inherited, a cousin of English sound, as in "safe and sound". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 5 of 5.
glücklich — happy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for hunger, then the German for sad.

You'll want to know: glücklich.
glücklich — "happy".

The word, taken apart.
glücklich is the word for "luck", Middle High German gelücke (EtymDB 2.1), + -lich. English luck is the same word, taken from Low German or Dutch; where gelücke itself came from is uncertain.
