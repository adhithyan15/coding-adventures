## You'll want to know: दर्जा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C376-MULYAMAPAN376-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्जा** — *darjā* — "a standard; a grade".

> या शाळेचा दर्जा खूप वरचा आहे.
>
> *yā śāḷecā darjā khūp varcā āhe.*
>
> This school's standard is very high.

Masculine.

दर्जेदार, of good quality.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्जा** (*darjā*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *daraja*, "step, rank".
