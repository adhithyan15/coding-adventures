## You'll want to know: der Abschied
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C302-RUHESTAND-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Abschied** — "the farewell, the parting".

> Beim Abschied weinten alle.
>
> *At the farewell everyone cried.*

Plural: *die Abschiede*. *Abschied nehmen* — to say goodbye.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Abschied* is built on *abscheiden* "to depart", from *scheiden* "to separate", Proto-Germanic *skaiþaną* (EtymDB).
