## You'll want to know: نسبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C412-IZDIYAD412-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نسبة** — *nisba* — "a proportion".

> نسبة النجاح عالية هذه السنة.
>
> *nisbat an-najāḥ ʿāliya hādhihi as-sana.*
>
> The proportion of passes is high this year.

بالنسبة إلى, *bi-n-nisba ilā*, regarding, as for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نسبة** (*nisba*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-s-b*, "to trace lineage, to relate" (Lane). A *nisba* is a relation, and so a ratio: a proportion, a percentage.
