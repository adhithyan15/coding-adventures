## You'll want to know: 世代
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C325-PASSED325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**世代** — *shìdài* — "for generations".

> **他们家世代都是医生** *tāmen jiā shìdài dōu shì yīshēng* — Their family have been doctors for generations.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**世代** (*shìdài*) is built from 世 'a generation' and 代 'an age', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syejH* and *dojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with the second: a generation.
