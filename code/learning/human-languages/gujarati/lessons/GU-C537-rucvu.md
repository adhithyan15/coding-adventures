## You'll want to know: રુચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C537-PASANDGI537-05]; assesses=[] -->

**રુચવું** — *rucvũ* — "to appeal (to someone)".

> જો આ યોજના તમને રુચશે તો આપણે કાલે શરૂ કરીશું.
>
> *jo ā yojnā tamne rucśe to āpaṇe kāle śarū karīśũ.*
>
> If this plan appeals to you, we will start tomorrow.

Past (it …): તે વાત તેને રુચી, *te vāt tene rucī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રુચવું** (*rucvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rucyatē*, "is pleasing", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be liked" (CDIAL 10765). The person it appeals to takes **ને**.
