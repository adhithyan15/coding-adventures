## You'll want to know: amābilis, amābile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C533-FAMI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**amābilis, amābile** — "lovable, delightful".

> Fīliola tua amābilis est.
>
> *Your little daughter is lovable.*

Two endings, from *amō* with *-bilis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**amābilis** is built on *amō* "to love" with *-bilis* "able to be" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
