## You'll want to know: べんきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C134-BENKYOU]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-N-01, JA-SCRIPT-KI-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YO-01, JA-SCRIPT-U-01] -->

**べんきょう** — *benkyō* — "study, studying": the work of learning, as you
are doing now.

The new sign is spent at once. **べ** and **ん**, then **き** with a small
**ょ**, then **う**: four beats, *be–n–kyo–o*. The **う** at the end stretches
the *o*, as it does in **きのう**. You write every sign in it now.
