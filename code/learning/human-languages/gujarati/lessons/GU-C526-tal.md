## You'll want to know: તાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C526-SUR526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તાલ** — *tāl* — "a rhythm (a beat cycle)".

> તબલાંનો તાલ ઘણો ઝડપી હતો.
>
> *tablā̃no tāl ghaṇo jhaḍpī hato.*
>
> The rhythm of the tabla was very fast.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તાલ** (*tāl*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tāla*, which Monier-Williams gives for the palm tree and also for a musical beat (Monier-Williams).
