## You'll want to know: تنهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C488-MAZAJ488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تنهد** — *tanahhada* — "to sigh".

> تنهدت أمي وجلست على الكرسي.
>
> *tanahhadat ummī wa-jalasat ʿalā al-kursī.*
>
> My mother sighed and sat down on the chair.

Present: يتنهد, *yatanahhad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنهد** (*tanahhada*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-h-d*, "to swell, to rise" (Lane). Lane gives Form V tanahhada as to sigh: to let the chest rise with a long breath.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
