## You'll want to know: гастроли
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C518-REHEARSAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**гастроли** (*gastróli*) — "a tour (by performers)".

> Летом театр едет на гастроли.
>
> *Létom teátr yédet na gastróli.*
>
> In the summer the theatre company goes on tour.

Plural. *Ехать на гастроли* — to go on tour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гастроли* is the plural of *гастроль*, which was borrowed from German *Gastrolle* 'a guest role' (EtymDB).
