## You'll want to know: شکست خوردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C384-ETESAB-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شکست خوردن** (*shekast khordan*) — "to be defeated, to lose".

> تیم ما در مسابقه شکست خورد.
>
> *tim-e mâ dar mosâbeqe shekast khord.*
>
> Our team was defeated in the match.

*Shekast* "defeat" + *khordan* "to eat" (stem *khor-*): literally to eat a defeat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شکست خوردن** is native Persian, built from *shekast*, the past stem of *shekastan* "to break", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian *škastan* and PIE *skeyd-*, and *khordan* "to eat", inherited too.
