## You'll want to know: てんしょくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C460-GOAL460-05]; assesses=[] -->

**てんしょくする** — *tenshoku suru* — "to change jobs".

> **あには きょねん てんしょくしました。** — *ani wa kyonen tenshoku shimashita* — "My elder brother changed jobs last year."

Polite **てんしょくします**, *tenshokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ちちが てんしょくしました。**, *chichi ga tenshoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てんしょくする** (*tenshoku suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *tenshoku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ten*, "change" (Middle Chinese *trjwenX*) and *shoku*, "employment" (*tsyik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
