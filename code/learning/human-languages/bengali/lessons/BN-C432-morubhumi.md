## You'll want to know: মরুভূমি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C432-CHURA432-04]; assesses=[] -->

**মরুভূমি** — *môrubhūmi* — "a desert".

> মরুভূমিতে দিনে গরম, রাতে ঠান্ডা।
>
> *môrubhūmite dine gôrôm, rāte ṭhānḍā.*
>
> In the desert it is hot by day and cold by night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মরুভূমি** (*môrubhūmi*) joins Sanskrit *maru*, "a wilderness, a sandy waste", to *bhūmi*, "land, ground" (Monier-Williams for both).
