## You'll want to know: うれしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C245-JOY245-01]; assesses=[] -->

**うれしい** — *ureshii* — "glad; pleased".

> **この おくりものは うれしいですか。** — *kono okurimono wa ureshii desu ka* — "Are you pleased with this present?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うれしい** (*ureshii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
