## You'll want to know: بقایا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C425-TARAZU425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بقایا** — *baqāyā* — "money still owed; arrears".

> میں نے دکاندار کا بقایا دیا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne dukāndār kā baqāyā diyā.*
>
> I paid the shopkeeper what I still owed him.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بقایا** (*baqāyā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *baqāyā*, "remainders, what is left over".
