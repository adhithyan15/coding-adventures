## You'll want to know: બંબો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C302-BAMBO302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બંબો** — *bambo* — "a fire engine".

> પાંચ મિનિટમાં બંબો આવી ગયો.
>
> *pā̃c miniṭmā̃ bambo āvī gayo.*
>
> The fire engine arrived within five minutes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બંબો** (*bambo*) is probably from Portuguese *bomba*, "a pump": the fire engine began as a pump on wheels. The firemen are **બંબાવાળા**, the men of the pump.
