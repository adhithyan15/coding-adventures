## You'll want to know: ધરપકડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C288-KABUL288-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ધરપકડ** — *dharpakaḍ* — "an arrest".

> પોલીસે ગઈકાલે બે ચોરોની ધરપકડ કરી.
>
> *polīse gaīkāle be coronī dharpakaḍ karī.*
>
> Yesterday the police arrested two thieves.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધરપકડ** (*dharpakaḍ*) is built from the stem of *dharvũ*, to hold, from Sanskrit *dhárati* (CDIAL 6747), and *pakaḍ*, a grip, the noun of **પકડવું**: a holding and a seizing. The police **ધરપકડ કરે છે**, make an arrest.
