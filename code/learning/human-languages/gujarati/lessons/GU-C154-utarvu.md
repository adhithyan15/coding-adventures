## You'll want to know: ઉતારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C154-CLOTH154-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉતારવું** — *utārvũ* — "to take off, to take down".

> **તે ઘરે બૂટ ઉતારે છે.** — *te ghare būṭ utāre che* — "He takes off his shoes at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉતારવું** (*utārvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *uttārayati*, "makes come out, makes descend", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to set down, bring down" (CDIAL 1770).
