## You'll want to know: शिकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C448-CHONCH448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिकार** — *śikār* — "hunting; prey".

> शेर ने एक हिरन का शिकार किया।
>
> *śer ne ek hiran kā śikār kiyā.*
>
> The lion hunted down a deer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिकार** (*śikār*) is usually traced to Persian *šikār*, "hunting, the hunt, prey".
