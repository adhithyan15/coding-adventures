## You'll want to know: шина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C405-SHINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**шина** (*shína*) — "a tyre".

> Передняя шина совсем старая.
>
> *Perédnyaya shína sovsém stáraya.*
>
> The front tyre is quite old.

Feminine. In everyday speech people also say *колесо* — a wheel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*шина* was borrowed from German *Schiene* 'a rail, a strip' (EtymDB), from Old High German *skina*; the word probably named the iron band round a cartwheel before the rubber tyre.
