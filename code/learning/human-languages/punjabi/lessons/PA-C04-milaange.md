# ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ (milāṁge) — "(we) will meet"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A future-tense verb — the other half of "see you again."

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-MA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-LAVA-01] -->

**ਮਿ** (*mi*) + **ਲਾਂ** (*lāṁ*, *la* + long *ā* + nasal) + **ਗੇ** (*ge*) →
**ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** (*milāṁge*).

You already read **ਮ** and **ਲ**. The rest are shapes to notice for now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-MILAANGE-04, PA-ETYMON-MIL-JOIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** (*milāṁge*, "we will meet") is the future of *milṇā* ("to
meet, to be found"), from Sanskrit *mil-* ("to join, unite") — the root
of *milāp* ("a union, meeting").

## Grammar Lens: the future is an ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-AANGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

Punjabi marks the future with an ending on the stem: *mil-* ("meet") + *-āṁge*
("we will") → *milāṁge*. No helper-word like English "will" — the tense lives in
the ending.
