# Two old words for strength

Stay with two words only. Both were taught before this return; nothing here
changes the earlier writing score.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-04] -->

Hear the cue **strong**. [PAUSE 4s] Say the old Punjabi word before the
spoken model: **ਤਕੜਾ** (*takṛā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-04, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-05] -->

Hear the contrast, then say the familiar word after a pause:

- **weak** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ** (*kamzor*).
- **strong** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਤਕੜਾ** (*takṛā*).

The cues ask for meanings already learned, not a new sentence pattern.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-04, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-05] -->

Hear **strong**, then **weak**. Pause after each cue and say its Punjabi
word before the model: **ਤਕੜਾ**, **ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ**.
