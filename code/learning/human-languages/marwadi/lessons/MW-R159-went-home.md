## Guided Practice

|---|---|
| **राम**, a man | **गयो** |

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *I went to the market yesterday*, as a man and then as a woman]
- [YOU SAY: *mother went home*; *everyone went home*]
- [YOU SAY: *he didn't go*]
- [YOU SAY: *do you go home every day?*; *yes, I go home every day*]
