Telugu, chapter 362: Truce.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పోరాడు (pōrāḍu) — to battle; to fight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to conquer, then the Telugu for a defeat.

You'll want to know: పోరాడు.
పోరాడు — pōrāḍu — "to battle; to fight".
సైనికులు చివరి వరకు పోరాడారు. sainikulu civari varaku pōrāḍāru. The soldiers fought to the end.
Past (I …): పోరాడాను, pōrāḍānu.
Also to struggle for something: జీవితం కోసం పోరాడు (pōrāḍu).

The word, taken apart.
పోరాడు (pōrāḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4540 lists Telugu pōru, "to fight, struggle; a battle", with Tamil poru.

Lesson 2 of 5.
తరిమికొట్టు (tarimikoṭṭu) — to drive away; to chase off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a defeat, then the Telugu for to battle.

You'll want to know: తరిమికొట్టు.
తరిమికొట్టు — tarimikoṭṭu — "to drive away; to chase off".
గ్రామస్తులు దొంగలను తరిమికొట్టారు. grāmastulu doṅgalanu tarimikoṭṭāru. The villagers drove the robbers away.
Past (I …): తరిమికొట్టాను, tarimikoṭṭānu.
తరుము, to chase, with కొట్టు: to chase right off.

The word, taken apart.
తరిమికొట్టు (tarimikoṭṭu) is a Telugu compound: tarumu, "to chase", and native koṭṭu, "to beat, strike" (DEDR 2063).

Lesson 3 of 5.
తలవంచు (talavañcu) — to bow one's head; to submit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to battle, then the Telugu for to drive away.

You'll want to know: తలవంచు.
తలవంచు — talavañcu — "to bow one's head; to submit".
చివరికి రాజు శత్రువుకు తలవంచాడు. civariki rāju śatruvuku talavañcāḍu. In the end the king submitted to the enemy.
Past (I …): తలవంచాను, talavañcānu.
Literally to bend the head: తల, head, and వంచు, to bend.

The word, taken apart.
తలవంచు (talavañcu) is a native compound: tala, "the head" (DEDR 3103), and vañcu, "to bend, humble" (DEDR 5335).

Lesson 4 of 5.
సంధి (sandhi) — a truce; a treaty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to drive away, then the Telugu for to bow one's head.

You'll want to know: సంధి.
సంధి — sandhi — "a truce; a treaty".
రెండు రాజ్యాలు సంధి చేసుకున్నాయి. reṇḍu rājyālu sandhi cēsukunnāyi. The two kingdoms made a truce.
సంధి (sandhi) చేసుకో, to make peace; in grammar, సంధి (sandhi) is the joining of sounds.

The word, taken apart.
సంధి (sandhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁdhi, "a joint, a point of union; a compact" (CDIAL 12913).
