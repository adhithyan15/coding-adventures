## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-AMBITION-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-AMBITION-01, ZH-LEX-C287-DANG-01, ZH-LEX-C287-MENGXIANG-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-ZUI-MOST-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-GUO-EXPERIENCE-01, ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-04, ZH-LEX-C32-FAMILY32-01, ZH-LEX-C35-WORK35-01] -->

> **A:** 以后你想当什么
>
> *yǐhòu nǐ xiǎng dāng shénme* — What do you want to be later on?
>
> **B:** 我想当医生
>
> *wǒ xiǎng dāng yīshēng* — I want to be a doctor.
>
> **A:** 为什么
>
> *wèishénme* — Why?
>
> **B:** 因为我妈妈是医生
>
> *yīnwèi wǒ māma shì yīshēng* — Because my mother is a doctor.
>
> **A:** *nǐ de mèngxiǎng shì shénme* — What's your dream?
>
> **B:** *wǒ de mèngxiǎng shì qù Zhōngguó gōngzuò* — My dream is to work in China.
>
> **A:** 你去过中国吗
>
> *nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma* — Have you been to China?
>
> **B:** 去过 我最喜欢中国菜
>
> *qù guo, wǒ zuì xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài* — I have. I like Chinese food best.

当 answers 当什么; 因为 answers 为什么; and B's last line looks back with 过 before it looks forward again.

## What you've built — looking ahead
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-AMBITION-01, ZH-SCRIPT-DANG-01, ZH-LEX-C287-DANG-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-AMBITION-01, ZH-LEX-C287-MENGXIANG-01, ZH-LEX-C287-MUBIAO-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-ZUI-MOST-01] -->

What you want, what you dream of, what you aim at, and what you like best:

- **以后我想当医生** *yǐhòu wǒ xiǎng dāng yīshēng* — a wish for later.
- *wǒ de mèngxiǎng shì …* — a dream.
- *wǒ de mùbiāo shì …* — a goal.
- **我最喜欢中国菜** *wǒ zuì xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài* — the best of all.

**Listen.** *Yǐhòu nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme gōngzuò?*

**Speak.** Say what you want to be or do later, and one goal on the way.

[YOU WRITE: 当 — the top of 常, then the foot of 雪]
