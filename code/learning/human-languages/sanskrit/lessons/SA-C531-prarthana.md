## You'll want to know: प्रार्थना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C531-VANCHATI531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रार्थना** — *prārthanā* — "a prayer; a request".

> यदि प्रार्थना शुद्धा अस्ति, तर्हि मनः शान्तं भवति।
>
> *yadi prārthanā śuddhā asti, tarhi manaḥ śāntaṃ bhavati.*
>
> If the prayer is pure, the mind grows calm.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रार्थना** (*prārthanā*) is built on *pra* and the root √arth, "to ask for"; MW gives *prārthanā* as a request, a petition, and a prayer in worship (MW).
