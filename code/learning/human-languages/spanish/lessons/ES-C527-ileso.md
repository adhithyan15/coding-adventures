## You'll want to know: ileso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C527-CHOQUE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ileso** — "unhurt, unharmed".

> Los pasajeros salieron ilesos del accidente.
>
> *The passengers came out of the accident unhurt.*

*Salir ileso* — to escape unhurt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ileso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *illaesus* 'unhurt, unharmed' (EtymDB), built on *in-* 'not' and *laesus* 'hurt', from *laedere* 'to hurt'.
