# وإلا (wa-illā) — otherwise

## You'll want to know: وإلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C399-SHART399-05]; assesses=[] -->

**وإلا** — *wa-illā* — "otherwise".

> يجب أن يدرس، وإلا سيرسب.
>
> *yajibu an yadrusa, wa-illā sa-yarsub.*
>
> He has to study, otherwise he will fail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وإلا** (*wa-illā*) joins the native Arabic *wa-*, "and", with *illā*, which here is *in*, "if", fused with *lā*, "not": and if not, otherwise. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
