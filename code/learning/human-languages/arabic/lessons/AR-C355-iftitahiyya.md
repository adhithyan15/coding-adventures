## You'll want to know: افتتاحية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C355-AJIL355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**افتتاحية** — *iftitāḥiyya* — "an editorial".

> قرأ أبي افتتاحية الصحيفة.
>
> *qaraʾa abī iftitāḥiyyat aṣ-ṣaḥīfa.*
>
> My father read the newspaper’s editorial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افتتاحية** (*iftitāḥiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-t-ḥ* (Lane), "to open", the root of the taught *fataḥa*. The editorial is the piece that opens the paper.
