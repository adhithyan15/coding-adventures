## You'll want to know: 手机
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-01]; assesses=[] -->

**手机** — *shǒujī* — "mobile phone".

> **这是我的手机** *zhè shì wǒ de shǒujī* — This is my phone.

The new character, in pieces: 机 — the tree piece on the left, the one at the top left of 想, and on the right a two-stroke piece read jǐ, as in the word for how many. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**手机** (*shǒujī*) is built from 手 'hand' and 机 'machine'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as the catch that sets off a crossbow. From that catch to any machine is the usual explanation, but the step is not given there.
