## You'll want to know: قفص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C478-QATI478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قفص** — *qafaṣ* — "a cage".

> كان الببغاء في قفص صغير.
>
> *kāna al-babbaghāʾ fī qafaṣ ṣaghīr.*
>
> The parrot was in a small cage.

Plural: أقفاص, *aqfāṣ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قفص** (*qafaṣ*) is linked by EtymDB to Aramaic *qupsā*, a word usually glossed "a box, a basket"; the details of the borrowing are not settled.
