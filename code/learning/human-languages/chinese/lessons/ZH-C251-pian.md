## You'll want to know: piān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C251-ESSAY251-01]; assesses=[] -->

*piān* — "measure word for essays and stories".

> *zhè piān xiǎoshuō hěn cháng* — This story is a long one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piān* is inherited from Middle Chinese *phjien* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as a section of a book.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what I do.
