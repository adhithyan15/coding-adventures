Italian, chapter 189: Cheerful, Shy, Brave, Fearful, Curious.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
allegro — cheerful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for although, then the Italian for despite.

You'll want to know: allegro.
allegro — "cheerful".

The word, taken apart.
allegro goes back to Latin alacer, "lively, brisk" (Lewis & Short), through a Vulgar Latin form alecrus. EtymDB 2.1 calls it borrowed from French allègre, which this book could not confirm.

The word, taken apart.
coraggioso — built from the word for "courage" with -oso, "full of". That word from Old Provençal coratge, built on Latin cor, "heart" — borrowed. (EtymDB 2.1).
