## You'll want to know: हन्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C334-RAKSHASA334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हन्ति** — *hanti* — "kills; slays".

> वीरः खड्गेन राक्षसं हन्ति।
>
> *vīraḥ khaḍgena rākṣasaṃ hanti.*
>
> The hero kills the demon with a sword.

Past (he …): सः राक्षसं हतवान्, *saḥ rākṣasaṃ hatavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हन्ति** (*hanti*) comes from the root √han, "to strike, kill"; MW compares Greek *theinō* and *phonos* (MW).
