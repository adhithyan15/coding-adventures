## You'll want to know: തളരുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C533-VAKSIN533-04]; assesses=[] -->

**തളരുക** — *taḷaruka* — "to grow weak; to tire out".

> ഒരുപാട് നടന്ന് ഞാൻ തളർന്നു.
>
> *orupāṭŭ naṭannŭ ñān taḷarnnu.*
>
> I was worn out after walking so much.

Past: തളർന്നു, *taḷarnnu*.

The past is തളർന്നു. The noun is തളർച്ച, exhaustion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തളരുക** (*taḷaruka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3127 gives the Malayalam verb as to relax, slacken and grow faint (DEDR 3127).
