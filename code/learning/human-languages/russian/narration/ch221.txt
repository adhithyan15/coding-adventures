7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
игрушка (igrúshka) — a toy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a printer, then the Russian for a remote control.

You'll want to know: игрушка.
игрушка (igrúshka) — "a toy".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: igrúshka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — recall: say the Russian for a printer, then the Russian for a remote control, then say igrúshka again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
