## You'll want to know: વિના
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C546-VAGAR546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વિના** — *vinā* — "without (formal)".

> જો છોડ પાણી વિના રહેશે તો તે કરમાઈ જશે.
>
> *jo choḍ pāṇī vinā raheśe to te karmāī jaśe.*
>
> If the plant goes without water, it will wilt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિના** (*vinā*) goes back to Sanskrit *vínā*, "without" (Monier-Williams), which CDIAL lists with Gujarati forms (CDIAL 11772). It is a little more formal than **વગર**.
