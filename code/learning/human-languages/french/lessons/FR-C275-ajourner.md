## You'll want to know: ajourner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C275-DELAI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ajourner** — "to postpone, to adjourn".

> Le match a été ajourné parce qu'il pleuvait.
>
> *The match was postponed because it was raining.*

Formal; in everyday speech, *remettre à plus tard*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ajourner* comes from Old French *ajorner* (EtymDB), built on *jor* "day", from Latin *diurnum* "daily, a day's portion": to put to another day.
