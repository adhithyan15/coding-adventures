## You'll want to know: گزارشگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C308-GOZARESH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گزارشگر** (*gozâreshgar*) — "a reporter, a correspondent".

> گزارشگر از جلوی بیمارستان صحبت میکرد.
>
> *Gozâreshgar az jelo-ye bimârestân sohbat mikard.*
>
> The reporter was speaking from outside the hospital.

*-gar* makes a person who does something, as in *kârgar* "worker".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gozâreshgar* adds the native suffix *-gar* "one who does" to *gozâresh* "a report". EtymDB lists no entry.
