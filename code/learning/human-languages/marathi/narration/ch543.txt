Lesson 1 of 6.
मंजुरी (mañjurī) — approval; sanction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to heed, then the Marathi for to defy.

You'll want to know: मंजुरी.
मंजुरी — mañjurī — "approval; sanction".
नवीन रस्त्याला सरकारची मंजुरी मिळाली. navīn rastyālā sarkārcī mañjurī miḷālī. The new road got the government's approval.
Feminine.
What is approved takes -ला: रस्त्याला मंजुरी (mañjurī).

The word, taken apart.
मंजुरी (mañjurī) is a loan from Persian manẓūrī, "approval", usually traced to Arabic manẓūr, "seen, accepted".

Lesson 3 of 6.
होकार (hokār) — assent; a yes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for approval, then the Marathi for consent.

You'll want to know: होकार.
होकार — hokār — "assent; a yes".
मीराने लग्नाला होकार दिला. mīrāne lagnālā hokār dilā. Meera said yes to the marriage.
Masculine.
होकार (hokār) देणे, to say yes; its opposite is नकार.

The word, taken apart.
होकार (hokār) is a Marathi derivation built on हो, "yes", with -कार, "a saying", from Sanskrit kāra (Monier-Williams), formed like नकार, "a no".

Lesson 5 of 6.
ओळखपत्र (oḷakhpatra) — an identity card.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for assent, then the Marathi for valid.

You'll want to know: ओळखपत्र.
ओळखपत्र — oḷakhpatra — "an identity card".
प्रवेशद्वारावर सगळ्यांनी ओळखपत्र दाखवलं. praveśadvārāvar sagḷyānnī oḷakhpatra dākhavlaṁ. Everyone showed an identity card at the entrance.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
ओळखपत्र (oḷakhpatra) is a Marathi compound built from ओळख, "knowing someone", and पत्र, "a leaf, a letter", from Sanskrit patra (Monier-Williams).
