## You'll want to know: affronter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C507-TOURNOI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**affronter** — "to face, to take on".

> Demain, notre équipe affronte les champions.
>
> *Tomorrow our team takes on the champions.*

*S'affronter* — to face each other.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*affronter* comes from Old French *afronter* "to face" (EtymDB), built on *front* "forehead", from Latin *frōns* (Lewis & Short): to stand forehead to forehead.
