## You'll want to know: 同事
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C255-PARTTIME255-03]; assesses=[] -->

**同事** — *tóngshì* — "colleague".

> **他是我的同事我们一起工作** *tā shì wǒ de tóngshì wǒmen yìqǐ gōngzuò* — He's a colleague of mine; we work together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**同事** (*tóngshì*) is built from 同 'the same' and 事 'affairs', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duwng* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'together': people who share the work.
