## You'll want to know: احسان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C456-KIND456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**احسان** — *ehsān* — "a kindness done; a favour".

> پڑوسی نے مشکل وقت میں ہم پر احسان کیا۔
>
> *paṛosī ne mushkil vaqt meṅ ham par ehsān kiyā.*
>
> Our neighbour did us a kindness in hard times.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احسان** (*ehsān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iḥsān*, "doing good".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
