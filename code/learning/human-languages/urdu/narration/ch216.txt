Urdu, chapter 216: Pulse, Breath, Sleep, Dream, Weakness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نبض (nabz) — a pulse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to doubt, then the Urdu for to hate.

You'll want to know: نبض.
نبض — nabz — "a pulse".

The word, taken apart.
نبض (nabz) is a loan from Arabic nabḍ, "pulse, a throbbing", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is n-b-ḍ (Lane).

سانس — sāṅs — "breath".

The word, taken apart.
سانس (sāṅs) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śvāsa, "breath, breathing" (CDIAL 12769).

Lesson 5 of 5.
کمزوری (kamzorī) — weakness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for sleep, then the Urdu for a dream.

You'll want to know: کمزوری.
کمزوری — kamzorī — "weakness".

The word, taken apart.
کمزوری (kamzorī) is kamzor, "weak", usually traced to Persian kamzōr, with the ending -ī that makes a noun. kamzōr is built from kam, "little", and zōr, "strength".
