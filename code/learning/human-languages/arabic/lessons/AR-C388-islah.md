## You'll want to know: إصلاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C388-FASAD388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إصلاح** — *iṣlāḥ* — "reform".

> تحتاج الشركة إلى إصلاح كبير.
>
> *taḥtāju ash-sharika ilā iṣlāḥ kabīr.*
>
> The company needs major reform.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إصلاح** (*iṣlāḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-l-ḥ*, "to be good, right" (Lane). *Iṣlāḥ* is the Form IV verbal noun of *aṣlaḥa*, to put right: repair, reform.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
