# ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು (meluku hāku) — to reminisce; to chew over

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-03, KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for humour, then the Kannada for a lullaby.

## You'll want to know: ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು** — *meluku hāku* — "to reminisce; to chew over".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ತಮ್ಮ ಬಾಲ್ಯದ ದಿನಗಳನ್ನು ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕಿದರು.
>
> *ajja tamma bālyada dinagaḷannu meluku hākidaru.*
>
> Grandfather went back over his childhood days.

Past (I …): ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕಿದೆ, *meluku hākide*.

ಮೆಲುಕು is a cow chewing the cud: going back over memories again and again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು** (*meluku hāku*) is a native Kannada phrase: *meluku*, "bringing food up again to chew the cud" (DEDR 5077), with ಹಾಕು, "to put".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
