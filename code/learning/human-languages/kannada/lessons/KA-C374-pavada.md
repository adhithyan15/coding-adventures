## You'll want to know: ಪವಾಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C374-JADU374-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪವಾಡ** — *pavāḍa* — "a miracle; a wonder".

> ಆ ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಗು ಬದುಕುಳಿದಿದ್ದು ಒಂದು ಪವಾಡ.
>
> *ā apaghātadalli magu badukuḷididdu ondu pavāḍa.*
>
> It was a miracle that the child survived that accident.

Plural: ಪವಾಡಗಳು, *pavāḍagaḷu*.

ಪವಾಡ ಮಾಡು, to work a miracle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪವಾಡ** (*pavāḍa*) is a Kannada word whose origin is not settled.
