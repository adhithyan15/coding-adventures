## You'll want to know: semear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C501-JARDIM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**semear** — "to sow".

> Em março semeamos tomates.
>
> *In March we sow tomatoes.*

Present: *semeio*, *semeias*, *semeia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semear* is inherited, through Old Portuguese (EtymDB), from Latin *sēmināre* "to sow", built on *sēmen* "seed" (Lewis & Short).
