# ঝাড়বাতি (jhāṛbāti) — a chandelier

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for darkness, then the Bengali for bright.

## You'll want to know: ঝাড়বাতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C505-ALO505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝাড়বাতি** — *jhāṛbāti* — "a chandelier".

> প্রাসাদে একটা বড় ঝাড়বাতি আছে।
>
> *Prāsāde ekṭā bôṛo jhāṛbāti āchhe.*
>
> There's a big chandelier in the palace.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝাড়বাতি** (*jhāṛbāti*) joins ঝাড়, "a bush, a thicket" (CDIAL 5362), to বাতি, "a lamp": a bush of lamps.
