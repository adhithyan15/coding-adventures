## You'll want to know: کھپانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C495-AJRAT495-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کھپانا** — *khapānā* — "to use up (time or effort); to toil over".

> اس نے اس کام میں دو سال کھپائے۔
>
> *us ne is kām meṅ do sāl khapāe.*
>
> He spent two whole years toiling over this work.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کام میں تین گھنٹے کھپائے, *us ne kām meṅ tīn ghanṭe khapāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھپانا** (*khapānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khapānā*, "to destroy", under Sanskrit *kṣapyatē*, "is destroyed, is used up" (CDIAL 3655); CDIAL glosses it "to destroy", and the everyday sense "to use up time or effort" is a later one it does not record.
