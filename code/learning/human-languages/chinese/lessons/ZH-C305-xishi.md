## You'll want to know: 喜事
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C305-ENGAGE305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**喜事** — *xǐshì* — "a happy occasion; a wedding in the family".

> **我们家有喜事了** *wǒmen jiā yǒu xǐshì le* — There's a happy occasion in our family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**喜事** (*xǐshì*) is built from 喜 'joy' and 事 'a matter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xiX* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'joy'.
