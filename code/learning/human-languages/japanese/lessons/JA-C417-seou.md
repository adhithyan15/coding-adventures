## You'll want to know: せおう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C417-TENIMOTSU417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**せおう** — *seou* — "to carry on one's back".

> **おもい にもつを せおって、やまに のぼりました。** — *omoi nimotsu o seotte, yama ni noborimashita* — "I climbed the mountain with a heavy load on my back."

Polite **せおいます**, *seoimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが にもつを せおいました。**, *ani ga nimotsu o seoimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せおう** (*seou*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *se*, "back", and *ou*, "to carry on one's back"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
