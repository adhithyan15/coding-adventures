## You'll want to know: империя
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C325-IMPERIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**империя** (*impériya*) — "an empire".

> Империя существовала тысячу лет.
>
> *Impériya sushchestvovála týsyachu let.*
>
> The empire lasted a thousand years.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*империя* goes back to Latin *imperium* "command, rule" (EtymDB), from *imperāre* "to command".
