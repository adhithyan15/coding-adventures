## You'll want to know: штрафовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C408-SKOROST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**штрафовать** (*shtrafovát*) — "to fine".

> Здесь часто штрафуют за скорость.
>
> *Zdes chásto shtrafúyut za skórost.*
>
> People often get fined for speeding here.

Imperfective; perfective *оштрафовать*. Present *штрафую*. Takes the accusative and *за*: *штрафовать водителя за скорость*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*штрафовать* was borrowed from German *strafen* 'to punish' (EtymDB), alongside the noun *штраф* 'a fine' from German *Strafe*.
