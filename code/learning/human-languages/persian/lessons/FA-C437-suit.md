## You'll want to know: سوئیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C437-MOSAFERKHANE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سوئیت** (*su'it*) — "a suite; a small self-contained flat".

> در شمال یک سوئیت کوچک اجاره کردیم.
>
> *Dar shomâl yek su'it-e kuchek ejâre kardim.*
>
> In the north we rented a small flat.

At the seaside in Iran a *su'it* is often a one-room holiday flat with a kitchen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*su'it* was borrowed from a European language and is usually traced to French *suite* 'a set of rooms'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
