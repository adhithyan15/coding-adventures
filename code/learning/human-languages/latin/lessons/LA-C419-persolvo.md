## You'll want to know: persolvō, persolvere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C419-PERSOLVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**persolvō, persolvere** — "to pay in full".

> Tandem omne aes aliēnum persolvit.
>
> *At last he paid off his whole debt.*

*persolvō, persolvere, persolvī, persolūtum*, *per-* through + *solvō*: to loosen the whole debt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*persolvō* is built from *per-* "through, completely" and *solvō* "to loosen, to pay" (Lewis & Short).
