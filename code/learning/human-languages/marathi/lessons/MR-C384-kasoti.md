## You'll want to know: कसोटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C384-PARISTHITI384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कसोटी** — *kasoṭī* — "a test; a touchstone".

> कठीण काळ हीच मैत्रीची खरी कसोटी असते.
>
> *kaṭhīṇ kāḷ hīc maitrīcī kharī kasoṭī aste.*
>
> Hard times are the true test of friendship.

Feminine.

कसोटीला उतरणे, to stand the test.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसोटी** (*kasoṭī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *kasoṭī*, "touchstone", under Sanskrit *kaṣapaṭṭikā*, "touchstone" (CDIAL 2973).
