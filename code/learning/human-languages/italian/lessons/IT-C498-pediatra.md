## You'll want to know: il pediatra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C498-BAMBINI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il pediatra** — "paediatrician".

> Domani porto mio figlio dal pediatra.
>
> *Tomorrow I am taking my son to the paediatrician.*

The same form for a woman: *la pediatra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pediatra* is a modern learned word built from Greek *paîs, paidós* "child" and *iatrós* "doctor" (EtymDB).
