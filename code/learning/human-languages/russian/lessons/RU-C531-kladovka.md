## You'll want to know: кладовка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C531-CLUTTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**кладовка** (*kladóvka*) — "a storeroom".

> Пылесос стоит в кладовке.
>
> *Pylesós stoít v kladóvke.*
>
> The vacuum cleaner is in the storeroom.

Feminine, colloquial for *кладовая*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*кладовка* is built on *кладовая* 'a storeroom', from the root of *класть* 'to put'; EtymDB has no entry.
