## You'll want to know: la falaise
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C454-RELIEF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la falaise** — "the cliff".

> Du haut de la falaise, on voit toute la baie.
>
> *From the top of the cliff the whole bay is in view.*

Usually over the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falaise* comes from Old French *faleise*, from Frankish *falisa* 'rock, cliff' (EtymDB).
