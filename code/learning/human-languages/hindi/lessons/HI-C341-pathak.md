## You'll want to know: पाठक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C341-PANNA341-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पाठक** — *pāṭhak* — "a reader".

> उस किताब के लाखों पाठक हैं।
>
> *us kitāb ke lākhoṁ pāṭhak haiṁ.*
>
> That book has hundreds of thousands of readers.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाठक** (*pāṭhak*) is Sanskrit *pāṭhaka*, "a reader, a reciter" (Monier-Williams), the same family as **पाठ**, a lesson.
