## You'll want to know: کارت به کارت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C461-KARTKHAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کارت به کارت کردن** (*kârt-be-kârt kardan*) — "to transfer money from card to card".

> پول بلیت را برای دوستم کارت به کارت کردم.
>
> *Pul-e bilit râ barâ-ye dustam kârt-be-kârt kardam.*
>
> I transferred the ticket money to my friend by card.

Very common in Iran: money goes from one bank card to another by phone or cash machine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kârt-be-kârt kardan* is built from *kârt* 'card', which EtymDB takes from French *carte*, twice over with *be* 'to', and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
