## You'll want to know: સમાચાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C311-SAMACHAR311-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાચાર** — *samācār* — "the news".

> મેં સવારે સમાચાર સાંભળ્યા.
>
> *mẽ savāre samācār sā̃bhaḷyā.*
>
> I listened to the news this morning.

A masculine noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાચાર** (*samācār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *samācāra*, "procedure, conduct, custom" (Monier-Williams); the sense "news" is the modern language's. The verb with it is plural: **સમાચાર સાંભળ્યા**.
