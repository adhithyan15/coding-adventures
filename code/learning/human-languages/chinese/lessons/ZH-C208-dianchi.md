## You'll want to know: diànchí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C208-FIX208-05]; assesses=[] -->

*diànchí* — "battery".

> *shǒujī de diànchí huài le* — The phone's battery has gone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diànchí* is built from 电 'electricity' and *chí* 'a pool', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *drje* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as standing water: a pool of electricity.
