German, chapter 525: Streamen und zocken.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
streamen — to stream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for subtitle, then the German for to dub.

You'll want to know: streamen.
streamen — "to stream".
Wir streamen die meisten Filme. We stream most films.
Regular: hat gestreamt. Said as in English.

The word, taken apart.
streamen was borrowed from English to stream (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Videospiel — video game.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: das Videospiel.
das Videospiel — "video game".
Zu Weihnachten wünscht sich mein Neffe ein neues Videospiel. My nephew wants a new video game for Christmas.
Plural: die Videospiele.

The word, taken apart.
Videospiel is built on Video, borrowed from English video, from Latin videō "I see", and Spiel "game", from Old High German spil (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
zocken — to play video games; to gamble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to subscribe to, then the German for video game.

You'll want to know: zocken.
zocken — "to play video games; to gamble".
Die Jungen zocken den ganzen Nachmittag. The boys are gaming all afternoon.
Informal. It first meant to gamble.

The word, taken apart.
zocken was borrowed from Yiddish tsokn "to gamble" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Hebrew ṣāḥaq "to laugh, to play".

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Kopfhörer — headphones.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for video game, then the German for to play video games.

You'll want to know: der Kopfhörer.
der Kopfhörer — "headphones".
Im Zug hört sie Musik mit Kopfhörern. On the train she listens to music with headphones.
Plural: die Kopfhörer; one pair is often ein Kopfhörer.

The word, taken apart.
Kopfhörer is built on Kopf "head", from Old High German kopf "cup", Proto-Germanic kuppaz (EtymDB), and Hörer "listener", from hören "to hear".
