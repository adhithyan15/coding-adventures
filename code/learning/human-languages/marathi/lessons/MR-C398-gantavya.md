## You'll want to know: गंतव्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C398-TIKITGHAR398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गंतव्य** — *gantavya* — "a destination".

> आमचं गंतव्य अजून खूप दूर आहे.
>
> *āmcaṁ gantavya ajūn khūp dūr āhe.*
>
> Our destination is still very far away.

Neuter.

A formal word; in speech people say जिथे जायचं आहे ते ठिकाण.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गंतव्य** (*gantavya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *gantavya*, "to be gone to", a form of the verb *gam*, "to go" (Monier-Williams).
