## You'll want to know: しゅっこく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C386-ZEIKAN386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっこく** — *shukkoku* — "departure from a country".

> **しゅっこくの まえに おみやげを かいました。** — *shukkoku no mae ni omiyage o kaimashita* — "I bought souvenirs before leaving the country."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっこく** (*shukkoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shutsu*, "exit" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwit*) and *koku*, "country" (*kwok*), joined as *shukkoku* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
