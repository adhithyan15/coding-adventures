## You'll want to know: el ingenio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C728-SABIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el ingenio** — "wit, ingenuity".

> Tiene mucho ingenio para resolver problemas.
>
> *She is very ingenious at solving problems.*

*Un ingenio* can also be a clever device; in Latin America *un ingenio* is a sugar mill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ingenio* was borrowed from Latin *ingenium* "inborn nature, talent" (EtymDB), built on *in* and the root of *gignere* "to beget": the gifts you are born with.
