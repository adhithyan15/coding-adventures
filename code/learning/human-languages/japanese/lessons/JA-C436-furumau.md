## You'll want to know: ふるまう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C436-BRIGHT436-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるまう** — *furumau* — "to behave; to conduct oneself".

> **むすこは あかるく ふるまいました。** — *musuko wa akaruku furumaimashita* — "My son acted cheerful."

Polite **ふるまいます**, *furumaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが おとなしく ふるまいました。**, *kodomo ga otonashiku furumaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふるまう** (*furumau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
