(**तुष्यति**, *tuṣyati*.) Gets tired? (**श्राम्यति**, *śrāmyati*.) Composes? (**रचयति**, *racayati*.) Shows? (**दर्शयति**, *darśayati*.) Worships? (**पूजयति**, *pūjayati*.) Comes back? (**प्रत्यागच्छति**, *pratyāgacchati*.) Lies down? (**शेते**, *śete*.) Sits? (**आस्ते**, *āste*.) Is afraid? (**बिभेति**, *bibheti*.) Meditates? (**ध्यायति**, *dhyāyati*.) Shares? (**भजति**, *bhajati*.) Weighs? (**तोलयति**, *tolayati*.) Flies up? (**उत्पतति**, *utpatati*.) Puts in? (**निक्षिपति**, *nikṣipati*.) Joins? (**योजयति**, *yojayati*.)
