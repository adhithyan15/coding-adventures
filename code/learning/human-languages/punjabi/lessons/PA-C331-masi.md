## You'll want to know: ਮਾਸੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C331-UNCLE331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਸੀ** — *māsī* — "a mother's sister".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਸੀ ਹਰ ਸਾਲ ਸਾਡੇ ਲਈ ਤੋਹਫ਼ੇ ਲਿਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī māsī har sāl sāḍe laī tohfe liāundī hai.*
>
> My mother's sister brings us gifts every year.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਸੀ** (*māsī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mātuḥṣvasṛ*, "a mother's sister"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *māssī*, "mother's sister", under it (CDIAL 10001).
