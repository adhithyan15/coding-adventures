Russian, chapter 292: Search and questioning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обыскивать (obýskivat) — to search (a person or a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a suspect, then the Russian for to suspect.

You'll want to know: обыскивать.
обыскивать (obýskivat) — "to search (a person or a place)".
Полицейские обыскали квартиру. Politséyskiye obyskáli kvartíru. The police searched the flat.
Imperfective; perfective обыскать (obyskát), обыщу. Искать is to look for; обыскивать (obýskivat) goes through a place. Обыск — a search.

The word, taken apart.
обыскивать (obýskivat) is built on об- "all round" and искать "to look for", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic jĭskati, from PIE h₂eys- "to wish, to seek" (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: арестовывать.
арестовывать (arestóvyvat) — "to arrest".
Его арестовали в аэропорту. Yevó arestováli v aeroportú. He was arrested at the airport.
Imperfective; perfective арестовать (arestovát). Арест — an arrest.

The word, taken apart.
арестовывать (arestóvyvat) is built on арест "an arrest", which is usually traced through German Arrest to Medieval Latin arrestum "a stopping"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
допрашивать (dopráshivat) — to question, to interrogate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to search, then the Russian for to arrest.

You'll want to know: допрашивать.
допрашивать (dopráshivat) — "to question, to interrogate".
Следователь допрашивал свидетелей весь день. Slédovatel dopráshival svidételey ves den. The investigator questioned witnesses all day.
Imperfective; perfective допросить (doprosít), допрошу. It takes the accusative.

The word, taken apart.
допрашивать (dopráshivat) is built on до- and the root of просить "to ask", inherited from Proto-Slavic prositi, from PIE preḱ- "to ask" (EtymDB): to ask to the end.

Lesson 5 of 5.
показания (pokazániya) — a statement, testimony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to question, then the Russian for questioning.

You'll want to know: показания.
показания (pokazániya) — "a statement, testimony".
Свидетель дал показания. Svidétel dal pokazániya. The witness gave a statement.
Usually plural in this sense. Давать показания (pokazániya) — to testify. The same plural also means the readings of a meter.

The word, taken apart.
показания (pokazániya) is built on показать "to show", from казать, inherited from Proto-Slavic kazati (EtymDB): what a witness shows.
