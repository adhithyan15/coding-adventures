## You'll want to know: つうか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C388-RYOHI388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**つうか** — *tsūka* — "a currency".

> **がいこくの つうかを すこし もっています。** — *gaikoku no tsūka o sukoshi motte imasu* — "I have a little foreign currency."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つうか** (*tsūka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tsū*, "pass through" (Middle Chinese *thuwng*) and *ka*, "goods" (*xwaH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
