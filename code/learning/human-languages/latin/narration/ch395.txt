Latin, chapter 395: Sooner or Later.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
mātūrō, mātūrāre — to hasten; to ripen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to set about, then the Latin for to foresee.

You'll want to know: mātūrō, mātūrāre.
mātūrō, mātūrāre — "to hasten; to ripen".
Iter mātūrāvit. He hurried on his journey.
mātūrō, mātūrāre, mātūrāvī, mātūrātum, built on mātūrus, ripe, early: to bring on early. English mature.

The word, taken apart.
mātūrō is built on mātūrus "ripe, early, timely" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English mature comes from the adjective.

Lesson 2 of 5.
differō, differre — to put off; to differ; to spread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to foresee, then the Latin for to hasten.

You'll want to know: differō, differre.
differō, differre — "to put off; to differ; to spread".
Rem in crāstinum diem distulimus. We put the matter off to the next day.
differō, differre, distulī, dīlātum: irregular, like ferō. To put off, and, without an object, to differ. English defer and differ.

The word, taken apart.
differō is built from dis- "apart" and ferō "to carry" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to carry in different directions, or forward in time. English defer and differ both come from it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
dīlātiō, dīlātiōnis — a putting off, a delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to put off, then the Latin for to extend.

You'll want to know: dīlātiō, dīlātiōnis.
dīlātiō, dīlātiōnis — "a putting off, a delay".
Rēs dīlātiōnem nōn patitur. The matter allows no delay.
dīlātiō, dīlātiōnis f., the noun of differō, from its supine dīlātum. English dilatory.

The word, taken apart.
dīlātiō is the noun made on dīlātum, the supine of differō "to put off" (Lewis & Short), whose -lātum comes from a different old verb than ferō.

Lesson 5 of 5.
exspectātiō, exspectātiōnis — expectation, anticipation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to extend, then the Latin for a putting off.

You'll want to know: exspectātiō, exspectātiōnis.
exspectātiō, exspectātiōnis — "expectation, anticipation".
Omnēs in magnā exspectātiōne erant. Everyone was in great expectation.
exspectātiō, exspectātiōnis f., the noun of exspectō. Praeter exspectātiōnem, beyond expectation. English expectation.

The word, taken apart.
exspectātiō is the noun built on exspectō "to wait for" (Lewis & Short), from ex "out" and spectō "to look": to look out for. English expectation comes from it.
