## You'll want to know: わん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C383-SHIMAGUNI383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**わん** — *wan* — "a bay".

> **わんの なかは なみが しずかです。** — *wan no naka wa nami ga shizuka desu* — "Inside the bay the waves are calm."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わん** (*wan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese: *wan*, "bay" (Middle Chinese *'waen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
