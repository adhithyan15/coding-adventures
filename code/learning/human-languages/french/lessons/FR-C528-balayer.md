## You'll want to know: balayer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C528-MENAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**balayer** — "to sweep".

> Tous les soirs, il balaie la cuisine.
>
> *Every evening he sweeps the kitchen.*

*Je balaie* or *je balaye*; both are correct.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*balayer* is built on *balai* "broom" (EtymDB), a word of Celtic origin, first the name of the broom plant.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
