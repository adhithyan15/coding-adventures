## You'll want to know: ਆਰਾਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C233-DIN233-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਆਰਾਮ** — *ārām* — "rest".

> ਦੁਪਹਿਰ ਨੂੰ ਦਾਦਾ ਜੀ ਆਰਾਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *dupahir nū̃ dādā jī ārām karde han.*
>
> My grandfather rests in the afternoon.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਆਰਾਮ** (*ārām*) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian *ārām*, "rest, quiet".
