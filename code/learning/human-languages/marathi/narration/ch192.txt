Lesson 4 of 7.
करार (karār) — a contract, an agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a colleague, then the Marathi for a meeting.

You'll want to know: करार.
करार — karār — "a contract, an agreement".

The word, taken apart.
करार (karār) is a loan from Arabic qarār, "settledness, an agreement", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-r-r (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 7.
मुलाखत (mulākhat) — an interview.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a meeting, then the Marathi for a contract.

You'll want to know: मुलाखत.
मुलाखत — mulākhat — "an interview".

The word, taken apart.
मुलाखत (mulākhat) is a loan from Arabic mulāqāt, "meeting", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is l-q-y (Lane). Marathi writes the q as ख.
