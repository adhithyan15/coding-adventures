## You'll want to know: जगणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C467-YATANA467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जगणे** — *jagṇe* — "to stay alive; to live".

> पाण्याशिवाय कोणीही जगू शकत नाही.
>
> *pāṇyāśivāy koṇīhī jagū śakat nāhī.*
>
> No one can live without water.

Past (he …): तो जगला, *to jaglā*.

Staying alive, where राहणे is living somewhere.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जगणे** (*jagṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; CDIAL lists the older Marathi verb of living, *jiṇẽ*, under Sanskrit *jīvati* (CDIAL 5241).
