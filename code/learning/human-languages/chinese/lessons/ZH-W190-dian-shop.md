## Script — a roof over a small piece and 口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DIAN-SHOP-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 店

**Three pieces: a roof, and under it a small piece over 口.**

**The roof, three strokes.** A dot at the top, a bar across, then a long stroke from the left end of the bar, falling down to the left all the way to the foot.

**The small piece, two strokes.** A vertical under the roof, then a short stroke from its middle out to the right.

**口, three strokes:** a short vertical, a stroke that runs right and turns down, then the bar that closes the foot.

3 + 2 + 3 = eight.
