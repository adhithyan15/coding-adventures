## You'll want to know: हिदायत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C547-CHAUKANNA547-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हिदायत** — *hidāyat* — "an instruction; a word of warning".

> डाक्टर ने मुझे आराम करने की हिदायत दी।
>
> *Ḍākṭar ne mujhe ārām karne kī hidāyat dī.*
>
> The doctor told me to rest.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिदायत** (*hidāyat*) is usually traced to Arabic *hidāya*, "guidance", through Persian.
