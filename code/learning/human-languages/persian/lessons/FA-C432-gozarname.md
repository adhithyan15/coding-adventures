## You'll want to know: گذرنامه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C432-GIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گذرنامه** (*gozarnâme*) — "a passport (formal word)".

> مامور فرودگاه گذرنامه من را نگاه کرد.
>
> *Ma'mur-e forudgâh gozarnâme-ye man râ negâh kard.*
>
> The airport officer looked at my passport.

The formal word; in speech *pâsport* is more common. *gozar* 'passing' + *nâme* 'letter'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gozarnâme* is a native compound, a letter for passing: *gozar* is the present stem of *gozashtan* 'to pass', which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian verb 'to pass, to cross', and *nâme* 'letter' is usually traced to Middle Persian *nāmag*.
