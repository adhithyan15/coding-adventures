## You'll want to know: پیمانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C355-DASTURPOKHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پیمانه** (*peymâne*) — "a measuring cup, a measure".

> دو پیمانه برنج را شست.
>
> *Do peymâne berenj râ shost.*
>
> She washed two cups of rice.

In recipes, a cup used as a measure. In old poetry, a wine cup.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peymâne* is a native Persian word, usually linked with the verb *peymudan* "to measure"; EtymDB lists no entry.
