## You'll want to know: प्रवक्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C312-NIRAKARANA312-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवक्ता** — *pravaktā* — "a spokesman".

> प्रवक्ता सभायां वदति।
>
> *pravaktā sabhāyāṃ vadati.*
>
> The spokesman speaks at the meeting.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवक्ता** (*pravaktā*) is built from *pra*, "forth", and *vaktṛ*, "a speaker", from the root √vac, "to speak"; MW gives *pravaktṛ* as one who tells or relates, an announcer, and a spokesman is its modern use (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
