## You'll want to know: দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C335-PROSONGO335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি** — *drishṭibhônggi* — "a point of view; an outlook".

> তার দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি আমার থেকে আলাদা।
>
> *tār drishṭibhônggi āmār theke ālādā.*
>
> Her outlook is different from mine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি** (*drishṭibhônggi*) is Sanskrit *dṛṣṭi*, sight, with *bhaṅgi*, a bend or a manner (Monier-Williams): the angle from which you look. The compound is a modern one.

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
