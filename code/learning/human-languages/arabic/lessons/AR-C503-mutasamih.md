## You'll want to know: متسامح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C503-TABA503-01]; assesses=[] -->

**متسامح** — *mutasāmiḥ* — "tolerant, forgiving".

> أبي رجل متسامح ولا يغضب كثيرا.
>
> *abī rajul mutasāmiḥ wa-lā yaghḍabu kathīran.*
>
> My father is a tolerant man and does not get angry often.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متسامح** (*mutasāmiḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-m-ḥ*, "to be generous, to allow" (Lane). Samaḥa, to allow, and sāmaḥa, to forgive, which you know, share the root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
