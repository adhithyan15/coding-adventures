## You'll want to know: जीरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C521-TADKA521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जीरा** — *jīrā* — "cumin".

> माँ ने गरम घी में जीरा डाला।
>
> *Māṁ ne garam ghī meṁ jīrā ḍālā.*
>
> Mother put cumin into the hot ghee.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जीरा** (*jīrā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *jīraka*, "cumin" (CDIAL 5234).
