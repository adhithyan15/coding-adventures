## You'll want to know: trair
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C497-SEPARACAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**trair** — "to betray; to cheat on".

> Ele traiu a confiança dos amigos.
>
> *He betrayed his friends' trust.*

Present: *traio*, *trais*, *trai*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trair* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *traer*, from Latin *trādere* "to hand over, to betray" (EtymDB).
