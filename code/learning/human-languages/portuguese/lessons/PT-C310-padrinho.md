## You'll want to know: o padrinho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C310-BATIZADO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o padrinho** — "the godfather; the best man".

> O meu tio é o meu padrinho.
>
> *My uncle is my godfather.*

At a wedding, *os padrinhos* are the witnesses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*padrinho* goes back to Vulgar Latin *patrīnus* "godfather" (EtymDB), from *pater* "father"; Italian and Spanish *padrino* are its cousins.
