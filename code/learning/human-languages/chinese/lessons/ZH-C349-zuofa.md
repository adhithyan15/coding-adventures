## You'll want to know: 做法
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C349-VIEW349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**做法** — *zuòfǎ* — "the way something is done; method".

> **我觉得这个做法不好** *wǒ juéde zhège zuòfǎ bù hǎo* — I don't think that's a good way to go about it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**做法** (*zuòfǎ*) is built from 做 'to do' and 法 'a rule'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a rule'. No dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
