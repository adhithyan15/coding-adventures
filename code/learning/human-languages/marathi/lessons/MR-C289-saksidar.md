## You'll want to know: साक्षीदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C289-GUNHA289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्षीदार** — *sākṣīdār* — "a witness".

> त्या साक्षीदाराने सगळं पाहिलं असतं तर त्याने पोलिसांना सांगितलं असतं.
>
> *tyā sākṣīdārāne sagḷaṁ pāhilaṁ astaṁ tar tyāne polisānnā sāṅgitlaṁ astaṁ.*
>
> If that witness had seen everything, he would have told the police.

Masculine.

साक्षीदाराने, by the witness; a past form with असतं says what would have happened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साक्षीदार** (*sākṣīdār*) is built from Sanskrit *sākṣin*, 'an eyewitness' (Monier-Williams), and the Persian suffix *-dār*, 'one who holds': the one who holds the testimony.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
