## You'll want to know: સ્વતંત્ર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C356-BANDHARAN356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વતંત્ર** — *svatantra* — "independent".

> ન્યાયાધીશ સ્વતંત્ર હોવા જોઈએ.
>
> *nyāyādhīś svatantra hovā joīe.*
>
> Judges should be independent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વતંત્ર** (*svatantra*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *svatantra*, "self-dependence, freedom" (Monier-Williams): under one's own rule.
