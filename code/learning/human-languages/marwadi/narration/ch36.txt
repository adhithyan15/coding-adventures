Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 36: One Exchange, Two Figures.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
The whole count, at a distance — twelve words and ten figures, retrieved four chapters after the first of them.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Say the bargaining request, recall the five-number payoff from four chapters back, then write ए.
[your turn — recall: ७ and ८, and which Latin figure each is mistaken for]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Guided Practice.
Everything, shuffled, with no chapter to give the answer away.
Hear twelve amounts named singly and out of order; say each back.
Read twelve printed figures and say each as a word.
Write six amounts from dictation in words and six in figures, the choice named only as you write.
Say the three that rhyme, the two that end in the tens स, and the four figures a Latin eye reads wrongly.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9; Omniglot, Numbers in Rajasthani.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Write a hundred as a word, then nine as a figure.


Lesson 2 of 7.
The shopping map, with prices on it — the seven shopping words, each given a price and a counter-offer.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Write the bargaining request, then recall the seven-word shopping payoff.

Guided Practice.
Take the seven shopping words one at a time. For each one:
Ask its price with the question from chapter 27.
Hear a figure back and say the amount aloud.
Offer a lower figure with करो।.
Write the seller's amount as a Devanagari figure and your own as a word.
The word भाव has meant price since chapter 18 and could not be answered until now. Say what changed.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
The cloth seller says bis. Write your offer of ten.


Lesson 3 of 7.
The food map, ordered and paid for — the seven food words, ordered and paid for.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Recall the seven-word food payoff, then write the bring request and the word that carries it.
[your turn — recall: दस करो।, and the chapter-28 request it replaced]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Guided Practice.
Take the seven food words one at a time.
Order it with लावो।.
Ask what it costs and hear a figure between ek and das.
Say the amount, then hand over the money with the chapter-30 turn.
Write the amount twice: once as a word, once as a figure.
A stall prices tea at pāṅch and a plate at das. This is where the first five numbers stop being a recitation.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9; Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6; Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Write tea, then the price five.


Lesson 4 of 7.
The transport map, with a fare on it — the five transport words, with a fare named on both sides.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Recall the five-word transport payoff and the four-skill bargaining payoff, then write bus.
[your turn — recall: १०० and सो, and which of the two is longer]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Guided Practice.
Take the five transport words one at a time.
Ask the fare with the price question.
Hear a figure — a rickshaw across a town centre asks bīs rather than pāṅch.
Offer a lower one with करो।, and say the amount aloud twice so the driver hears the same figure you meant.
Write both figures down, the asked and the offered.
Say why this exchange has to be finished before the journey and not after it.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
The driver says bis. Write the figure he named, then your offer of ten.


Lesson 5 of 7.
The five least-drilled signs — the least-drilled signs of this slice, retrieved alone.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Write the price question and the word for very, then recall the ticket payoff.

Guided Practice.
Five signs, alone, from spoken cues and with no word around them: ए, ४, ५, ७, ८.
Then put each back to work once. ए opens एक and its mark rides over the second spelling of six; the four figures write four of this slice's twelve amounts. Write those six things and check each sign against the model only after writing it.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Unicode Devanagari chart; Omniglot, Numbers in Rajasthani.
[question — say your answer, then pause 14 seconds]
Write the standing e, then the digit for seven.


Lesson 6 of 7.
Payoff — one exchange, with an amount in it.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Run the five-turn counter exchange from chapter 31, say the take-it word, write the paying turn, and write ए.

Guided Practice.
Six turns now, and two of them are numbers.
ये कपड़ा दिखावो। — show me this cloth.
ये कितणे कू है? — how much is this?
बीस। — twenty.
ये घणो महंगो है। — this is very expensive.
दस करो। — make it ten.
पैसा ले लो। — take the money.
Run it in four separately scored skills, over the shopping words, the food words and the transport words in turn, changing nothing but the noun and the two figures.
Turn 3 is what chapter 31 could not hear and turn 5 is what it could not say. Everything else on this list has been in the book since chapter 30.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 9; Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 6; Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 55 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write the two numbered turns.


Lesson 7 of 7.
Close the numeral slice — the whole numeral slice, written once more and closed.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Recall the six-turn payoff, write the take-it word, and write the bargaining request it now shares a chapter with.

Guided Practice.
Everything this slice added, from dictation, with no model in view.
The letter: ए, and the mark े beside it.
The twelve words, shuffled, from एक to सो.
The ten figures, shuffled, from ० to ९.
The two turns: बीस। and दस करो।.
Repair only what missed. Then say what the whole slice cost the hand: one letter and ten figures, against twelve numbers and a bargaining turn that finally has a figure in it.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Unicode Devanagari chart; Omniglot, Numbers in Rajasthani.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Write the seller's one-word answer of twenty, then your offer of ten.
