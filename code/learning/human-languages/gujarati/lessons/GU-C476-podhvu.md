# પોઢવું (poḍhvũ) — to lie down to sleep

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-02, GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for slumber, then the Gujarati for staying up all night.

## You'll want to know: પોઢવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પોઢવું** — *poḍhvũ* — "to lie down to sleep".

> બાળક પલંગ પર પોઢી ગયું.
>
> *bāḷak palaṅg par poḍhī gayũ.*
>
> The baby fell asleep on the bed.

Past (it …): તે બાળક પોઢી ગયું, *te bāḷak poḍhī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પોઢવું** (*poḍhvũ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to sleep", under Sanskrit *pravardhate*, "grows up", whose sense "lie down" CDIAL explains as "stretched out" (CDIAL 8789).
