## You'll want to know: স্বস্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C446-SANTVONA446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**স্বস্তি** — *svôsti* — "relief; ease of mind".

> ছেলে বাড়ি ফেরার পরে বাবা স্বস্তি পেলেন।
>
> *chhele bāṛi pherār pôre bābā svôsti pelen.*
>
> Father felt relieved once his son was home.

স্বস্তি পাওয়া, to feel relieved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্বস্তি** (*svôsti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *svasti*, "well-being, success" (Monier-Williams): the feeling that all is well.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
