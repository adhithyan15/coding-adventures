## You'll want to know: en absoluto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C589-MATIZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**en absoluto** — "not at all".

> —¿Te molesta? —En absoluto.
>
> *Does it bother you? Not at all.*

Alone it means no, not at all, even without *no*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*en absoluto* is built on *absoluto*, a learned borrowing from Latin *absolutus* 'freed, complete', from *absolvere* 'to set loose' (EtymDB).
