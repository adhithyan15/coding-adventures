## You'll want to know: l'effraction
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C287-CAMBRIOLAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'effraction** — "the forced entry, breaking in".

> La police n'a trouvé aucune trace d'effraction.
>
> *The police found no sign of a forced entry.*

*Entrer par effraction* — to break in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*effraction* is a learned word built on Latin *effractus*, the past participle of *effringere* "to break open", from *ex* and *frangere* "to break" (Lewis & Short).
