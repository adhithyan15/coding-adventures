## You'll want to know: جننا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C483-BABY483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جننا** — *jannā* — "to give birth to".

> بلی نے چار بچے جنے۔
>
> *billī ne chār bachche jane.*
>
> The cat had four kittens.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دو بچے جنے, *us ne do bachche jane*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جننا** (*jannā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *janáyati*, "causes to be born, begets" (CDIAL 5102).
