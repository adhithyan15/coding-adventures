## You'll want to know: कुंकुमम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C327-HOLIKA327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंकुमम्** — *kuṅkumam* — "saffron".

> पायसे कुंकुमम् अस्ति।
>
> *pāyase kuṅkumam asti.*
>
> There is saffron in the rice pudding.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुंकुमम्** (*kuṅkumam*) MW gives *kuṅkuma* as saffron, the plant and the pollen of its flowers, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
