Urdu, chapter 316: Escape from the Fort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قیدی (qaidī) — a prisoner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a century, then the Urdu for to become deserted.

You'll want to know: قیدی.
قیدی — qaidī — "a prisoner".
قلعے میں سو قیدی تھے۔ qile meṅ sau qaidī the. There were a hundred prisoners in the fort.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
قیدی (qaidī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qayd, "a fetter", with the ending -ī: one held in chains.

Lesson 2 of 5.
پہرہ (pahrā) — a guard kept; a watch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to become deserted, then the Urdu for a prisoner.

You'll want to know: پہرہ.
پہرہ — pahrā — "a guard kept; a watch".
رات کو دروازے پر پہرہ تھا۔ rāt ko darvāze par pahrā thā. At night the gate was guarded.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
پہرہ (pahrā) is usually traced to Persian pahra, "a watch, a guard". CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin paharā, "a watch, a watchman", under Sanskrit prahara, a watch of about three hours, and asks whether that Sanskrit word is itself the Persian one (CDIAL 8900). پہرہ دینا is to stand guard.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سرنگ (surang) — a tunnel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a prisoner, then the Urdu for a guard kept.

You'll want to know: سرنگ.
سرنگ — surang — "a tunnel".
قیدیوں نے ایک سرنگ کھودی۔ qaidiyoṅ ne ek surang khodī. The prisoners dug a tunnel.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سرنگ (surang) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin suraṅg, "a burglar's hole, a mine", under Sanskrit suruṅgā (CDIAL 13506). It is feminine: سرنگ کھودی.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کھسکنا (khisaknā) — to slip away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a guard kept, then the Urdu for a tunnel.

You'll want to know: کھسکنا.
کھسکنا — khisaknā — "to slip away".
قیدی رات کو سرنگ سے کھسک گئے۔ qaidī rāt ko surang se khisak gae. The prisoners slipped away through the tunnel at night.
Past (he …): وہ کھسک گیا, voh khisak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
کھسکنا (khisaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin khisaknā, "to slip away", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3888). It is leaving quietly, so that nobody sees.

Lesson 5 of 5.
رینگنا (rengnā) — to crawl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: رینگنا.
رینگنا — rengnā — "to crawl".
وہ سرنگ میں رینگ رہے تھے۔ voh surang meṅ reng rahe the. They were crawling through the tunnel.
Past (he …): وہ رینگا, voh rengā.

The word, taken apart.
رینگنا (rengnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin rẽgnā, "to crawl", under Sanskrit ríṅgati (CDIAL 10739). Babies, snakes and soldiers under wire all رینگتے ہیں.
