## You'll want to know: رخص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C457-TAKHFID457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رخص** — *rakhuṣa* — "to get cheaper".

> رخصت الفاكهة في الصيف.
>
> *rakhuṣat al-fākiha fī aṣ-ṣayf.*
>
> Fruit got cheaper in the summer.

Present: يرخص, *yarkhuṣ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رخص** (*rakhuṣa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-kh-ṣ*, "to be soft, easy, cheap" (Lane). It is the plain verb beside *rakhīṣ*, "cheap".
