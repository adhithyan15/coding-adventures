## You'll want to know: o candidato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C355-CANDIDATURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o candidato** — "the candidate, the applicant".

> Havia trinta candidatos para um só lugar.
>
> *There were thirty applicants for a single place.*

Feminine *a candidata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*candidato* is a learned borrowing from Latin *candidātus* "clothed in white" (Lewis & Short), from *candidus* "shining white": Roman candidates for office wore a whitened toga.
