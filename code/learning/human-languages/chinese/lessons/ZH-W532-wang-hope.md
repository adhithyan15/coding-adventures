## Script — a small piece and 月, then 王 under them
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WANG-HOPE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 望

**Three pieces: two side by side on top, then one across the foot.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The top left piece, three strokes**: a dot, a bar under it, then a short vertical that turns right along the bottom.

**The top right piece, 月, four strokes**, as you wrote it: a stroke down that falls to the left, a bar that turns down and hooks, then two short bars inside.

**The bottom piece, 王, four strokes**, as you wrote it: a bar, a second bar, a vertical down through them, then the long bar at the foot.

3 + 4 + 4 = eleven.
