Russian, chapter 443: A stroll round town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
бродить (brodít) — to wander, to stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sight, then the Russian for to look round.

You'll want to know: бродить.
бродить (brodít) — "to wander, to stroll".
Мы любим бродить по старым улицам. My lyúbim brodít po stárym úlitsam. We love wandering the old streets.
Imperfective. Present брожу, бродишь. Of wine: to ferment.

The word, taken apart.
бродить (brodít) is inherited through Old East Slavic broditi from Proto-Slavic broditi (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
прогуливаться (progúlivatsya) — to take a stroll, to stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to look round, then the Russian for to wander.

You'll want to know: прогуливаться.
прогуливаться (progúlivatsya) — "to take a stroll, to stroll".
Вечером люди прогуливаются в парке. Vécherom lyúdi progúlivayutsya v párke. In the evening people stroll in the park.
Imperfective; perfective прогуляться.

The word, taken apart.
прогуливаться (progúlivatsya) is built from про-, гулять 'to go for a walk' and -ся. EtymDB does not list the verb, and lists гулять without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
квартал (kvartál) — a block (in a city); a quarter (of a year).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to wander, then the Russian for to take a stroll.

You'll want to know: квартал.
квартал (kvartál) — "a block (in a city); a quarter (of a year)".
Аптека в соседнем квартале. Aptéka v sosédnem kvartále. The chemist's is in the next block.
Masculine. Первый квартал (kvartál) — the first quarter of the year.

The word, taken apart.
квартал (kvartál) was borrowed from German Quartal (EtymDB), a word built on Latin quartus 'fourth' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
пригород (prígorod) — a suburb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to take a stroll, then the Russian for a block.

You'll want to know: пригород.
пригород (prígorod) — "a suburb".
Мы живём в пригороде и ездим в город на поезде. My zhivyóm v prígorode i yézdim v górod na póyezde. We live in a suburb and go into town by train.
Masculine; the stress is on при- (prí-).

The word, taken apart.
пригород (prígorod) is built from при- 'near' and город 'a town', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic gordŭ 'an enclosure' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
мостовая (mostováya) — a roadway; a paved street.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a block, then the Russian for a suburb.

You'll want to know: мостовая.
мостовая (mostováya) — "a roadway; a paved street".
Машины едут по мостовой, а люди идут по тротуару. Mashíny yédut po mostovóy, a lyúdi idút po trotuáru. The cars drive on the roadway, and people walk on the pavement.
An adjective used as a noun: по мостовой.

The word, taken apart.
мостовая (mostováya) is built on мостить 'to pave', from мост 'a bridge', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic mostŭ (EtymDB).
