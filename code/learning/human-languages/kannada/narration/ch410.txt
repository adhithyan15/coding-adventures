Kannada, chapter 410: Linking Ideas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹೀಗಾಗಿ (hīgāgi) — thus; for this reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to present, then the Kannada for a note.

You'll want to know: ಹೀಗಾಗಿ.
ಹೀಗಾಗಿ — hīgāgi — "thus; for this reason".
ಬಸ್ ತಡವಾಯಿತು, ಹೀಗಾಗಿ ನಾನು ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದೆ. bas taḍavāyitu, hīgāgi nānu taḍavāgi bande. The bus was late, so I came late.
ಹೀಗೆ (like this) + ಆಗಿ (being).

The word, taken apart.
ಹೀಗಾಗಿ (hīgāgi) is native Dravidian, a phrase grown into one word: hīge, "in this way" (DEDR 410), and āgi, "having become" (DEDR 333).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ (ādāgyū) — however; nevertheless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a note, then the Kannada for thus.

You'll want to know: ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ.
ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ — ādāgyū — "however; nevertheless".
ಅವನು ಬಡವ, ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ. avanu baḍava, ādāgyū ellarigū sahāya māḍuttāne. He is poor; nevertheless he helps everyone.
More formal than ಆದರೂ.

The word, taken apart.
ಆದಾಗ್ಯೂ (ādāgyū) is native Dravidian, built on forms of āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333), with -ū, "even": even so.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ನಿಮಿತ್ತ (nimitta) — on account of; an occasion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for thus, then the Kannada for however.

You'll want to know: ನಿಮಿತ್ತ.
ನಿಮಿತ್ತ — nimitta — "on account of; an occasion".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ನಿಮಿತ್ತ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಎರಡು ದಿನ ರಜೆ ಇದೆ. habbada nimitta śālege eraḍu dina raje ide. On account of the festival, the school has two days off.
It follows a noun in -ದ / -ಯ: ಮದುವೆಯ ನಿಮಿತ್ತ (nimitta), for the wedding. A formal word for a reason or an occasion.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಮಿತ್ತ (nimitta) is borrowed from Sanskrit nimitta, "a cause, a reason" (CDIAL 7241); in Sanskrit, too, its case forms mean "on account of" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮೇಲಾಗಿ (mēlāgi) — above all; moreover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for however, then the Kannada for on account of.

You'll want to know: ಮೇಲಾಗಿ.
ಮೇಲಾಗಿ — mēlāgi — "above all; moreover".
ಈ ಮನೆ ದೊಡ್ಡದು, ಮೇಲಾಗಿ ಬಾಡಿಗೆ ಕಡಿಮೆ. ī mane doḍḍadu, mēlāgi bāḍige kaḍime. This house is big; moreover the rent is low.
ಮೇಲೆ (above) + ಆಗಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೇಲಾಗಿ (mēlāgi) is native Dravidian: mēl, "what is above" (DEDR 5086), with āgi, "being" (DEDR 333).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮಾತ್ರವಲ್ಲದೆ (mātravallade) — not only that; besides.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for on account of, then the Kannada for above all.

You'll want to know: ಮಾತ್ರವಲ್ಲದೆ.
ಮಾತ್ರವಲ್ಲದೆ — mātravallade — "not only that; besides".
ಅವಳು ಹಾಡುತ್ತಾಳೆ ಮಾತ್ರವಲ್ಲದೆ ನೃತ್ಯವನ್ನೂ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾಳೆ. avaḷu hāḍuttāḷe mātravallade nṛtyavannū māḍuttāḷe. She not only sings but dances too.
ಮಾತ್ರ (only) + ಅಲ್ಲದೆ (not being).

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾತ್ರವಲ್ಲದೆ (mātravallade) is built from mātra, "only", from Sanskrit mātra, "measure" (Monier-Williams), joined to native allade, "not being" (DEDR 234).
