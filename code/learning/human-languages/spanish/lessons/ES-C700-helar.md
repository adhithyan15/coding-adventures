## You'll want to know: helar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C700-NIEVE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**helar** — "to freeze".

> Anoche heló y la calle está blanca.
>
> *It froze last night and the street is white.*

The e becomes ie: *hiela*. *El helado* is ice cream.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*helar* is inherited from Latin *gelāre* "to freeze" (EtymDB), from *gelū* "frost"; Latin *g* before *e* became Spanish *h* here.
