## You'll want to know: ਬਰਾਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C313-WED313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਰਾਤ** — *barāt* — "a groom's wedding party".

> ਬਰਾਤ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਆਈ ਅਤੇ ਸਭ ਨੱਚੇ।
>
> *barāt shām nū̃ āī ate sabh nachche.*
>
> The groom's party arrived in the evening, and everyone danced.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਰਾਤ** (*barāt*) is inherited from Sanskrit *varayātrā*, "the procession of a suitor or bridegroom"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *barāt*, "groom's procession", under it (CDIAL 11322).
