## You'll want to know: суд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C293-SUD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**суд** (*sud*) — "a court; a trial".

> Суд будет в понедельник.
>
> *Sud búdet v ponedélnik.*
>
> The trial will be on Monday.

*В суде* — in court; *подать в суд* — to sue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*суд* is inherited through Old East Slavic *sudŭ* from Proto-Slavic *sǫdŭ*, which EtymDB builds from PIE *som-* "together" and *dʰeh₁-* "to put": a putting together.
