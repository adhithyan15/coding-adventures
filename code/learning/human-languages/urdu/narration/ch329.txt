Urdu, chapter 329: A Story in a Book.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
افسانہ (afsānā) — a short story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for deceit, then the Urdu for to lead astray.

You'll want to know: افسانہ.
افسانہ — afsānā — "a short story".
میں نے کل ایک افسانہ پڑھا۔ maiṅ ne kal ek afsānā paṛhā. I read a short story yesterday.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
افسانہ (afsānā) is usually traced to Persian afsāna, "a tale, a fable"; in Urdu writing it is the short story.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

مصنف (musannif) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muṣannif, "one who composes a book".

Lesson 3 of 5.
کردار (kirdār) — a character (in a story).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a short story, then the Urdu for an author.

You'll want to know: کردار.
کردار — kirdār — "a character (in a story)".
اس ناول میں دس کردار ہیں۔ is nāvil meṅ das kirdār haiṅ. There are ten characters in this novel.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
کردار (kirdār) is usually traced to Persian kirdār, "a deed, conduct"; the sense of a person in a story is thought to have come from the part one plays.

[pause 2 seconds]

باب — bāb — "a chapter of a book".
پہلا باب سب سے اچھا ہے۔ pehlā bāb sab se achchhā hai. The first chapter is the best.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
باب (bāb) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic bāb, "a door", and so a way into one part of a book.
