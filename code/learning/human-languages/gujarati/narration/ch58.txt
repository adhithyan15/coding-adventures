Gujarati, chapter 58: In the Pantry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચોખા (cokhā) — rice (uncooked).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for rupees, then the Gujarati for a coin.

You'll want to know: ચોખા.
ચોખા (cokhā) — "rice (uncooked)". Cooked, it is ભાત.

The word, taken apart.
ચોખા (cokhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cokṣa, "pure, clean" (CDIAL 4918). CDIAL lists Gujarati cokhā, "rice", under it.
