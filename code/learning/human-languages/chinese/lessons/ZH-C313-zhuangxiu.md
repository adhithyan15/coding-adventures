## You'll want to know: zhuāngxiū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C313-MOVE313-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuāngxiū* — "to do up a home; to renovate".

> *wǒmen de xīn jiā zhèngzài zhuāngxiū* — Our new home is being done up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuāngxiū* is built from *zhuāng* 'to pack' and *xiū* 'to repair', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrjang* and *sjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to pack for a journey' and the second 'to put in order'.
