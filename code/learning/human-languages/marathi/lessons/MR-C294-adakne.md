## You'll want to know: अडकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C294-PUR294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अडकणे** — *aḍakṇe* — "to get stuck; to be caught".

> पाण्यात दोन मुलं अडकली होती.
>
> *pāṇyāt don mulaṁ aḍaklī hotī.*
>
> Two children were stuck in the water.

Past (he …): तो अडकला, *to aḍaklā*.

The place takes -त: गर्दीत अडकणे, to be stuck in a crowd.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अडकणे** (*aḍakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *aḍakṇe*, 'to stick fast', under *\*aḍ*, 'obstruct, stop' (CDIAL 187).
