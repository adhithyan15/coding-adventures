French, chapter 313: La retraite.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la retraite — retirement; the pension.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the tenant, then the French for the lease.

You'll want to know: la retraite.
la retraite — "retirement; the pension".
Mon père a pris sa retraite l'année dernière. My father retired last year.
Prendre sa retraite — to retire; être à la retraite — to be retired.

The word, taken apart.
retraite is the old past participle of Old French retraire "to withdraw", which is inherited from Latin retrahere "to draw back" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
hospitaliser — to take into hospital, to hospitalise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the retired person, then the French for old age.

You'll want to know: hospitaliser.
hospitaliser — "to take into hospital, to hospitalise".
Ma grand-mère a été hospitalisée pendant une semaine. My grandmother was in hospital for a week.
Mostly in the passive: il a été hospitalisé.

The word, taken apart.
hospitaliser is built on hospital, the old form of hôpital, from Latin hospitālis "of a guest", built on hospes "guest, host" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
le décès — the death (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for old age, then the French for to take into hospital.

You'll want to know: le décès.
le décès — "the death (formal)".
Le décès a eu lieu à l'hôpital. The death took place at the hospital.
The formal and official word; in daily speech, la mort.

The word, taken apart.
décès is inherited from Latin dēcessus "a going away, a death" (EtymDB), from dēcēdere "to go away".
