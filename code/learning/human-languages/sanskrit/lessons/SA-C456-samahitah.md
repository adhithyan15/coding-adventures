## You'll want to know: समाहितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C456-SAMA456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**समाहितः** — *samāhitaḥ* — "composed; collected".

> परीक्षायाम् अपि छात्रः समाहितः अस्ति।
>
> *parīkṣāyām api chātraḥ samāhitaḥ asti.*
>
> Even in the exam the student stays composed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समाहितः** (*samāhitaḥ*) is built from *sam*, *ā* and *hita*, "put", from the root √dhā, "to put": put together; MW gives "put or held together" and then "composed, collected, concentrated" (MW).

## What you've built
