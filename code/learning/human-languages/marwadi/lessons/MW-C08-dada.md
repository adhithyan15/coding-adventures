# दादा — the repeated word

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DADA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-LEX-BAHAN-01, MW-DIALOGUE-WELLBEING-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Ask and answer wellbeing once. Write **द** and familiar **ा**, then
say sister from memory.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-DADA-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DADA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01] -->

> **दा** + **दा** → **दादा** — *dādā* — paternal grandfather

The spelling repeats exactly what the ear heard: two matching long beats.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DADA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DADA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **दादा**, cover it, wait five seconds, and write it once. Point to both
copies of **ा** before reading the whole word.
