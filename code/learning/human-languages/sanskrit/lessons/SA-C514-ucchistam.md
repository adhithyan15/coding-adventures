# उच्छिष्टम् (ucchiṣṭam) — leftovers

## You'll want to know: उच्छिष्टम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C514-PARIVESAYATI514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उच्छिष्टम्** — *ucchiṣṭam* — "leftovers".

> उच्छिष्टं धेनवे ददातु।
>
> *ucchiṣṭaṃ dhenave dadātu.*
>
> Please give the leftovers to the cow.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उच्छिष्टम्** (*ucchiṣṭam*) is built on *ud*, "out, over", and the root √śiṣ, "to leave", as its past participle; MW gives *ucchiṣṭa* as left over, and the remnants of food (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
