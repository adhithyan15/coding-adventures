Urdu, chapter 378: Setting a Goal.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ہدف (hadaf) — a target; a goal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to get worse, then the Urdu for to get better.

You'll want to know: ہدف.
ہدف — hadaf — "a target; a goal".
اگر ہدف واضح ہے تو کام آسان ہو گا۔ agar hadaf vāzeh hai to kām āsān ho gā. If the target is clear, the work will be easier.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ہدف (hadaf) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic hadaf, "a target, a mark aimed at".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

You'll want to know: منصوبہ.
منصوبہ — mansūbā — "a scheme; a plan (worked out)".
اگر منصوبہ اچھا ہے تو حکومت پیسے دے گی۔ agar mansūbā achchhā hai to hukūmat paise de gī. If the scheme is good, the government will fund it.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
منصوبہ (mansūbā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic manṣūba, "something set up, arranged"; the sense "a plan" is the one Urdu uses.

Lesson 5 of 6.
حاصل کرنا (hāsil karnā) — to achieve; to obtain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a step, then the Urdu for a scheme.

You'll want to know: حاصل کرنا.
حاصل کرنا — hāsil karnā — "to achieve; to obtain".
اگر آپ کوشش کریں گے تو ہدف حاصل کریں گے۔ agar āp koshish kareṅ ge to hadaf hāsil kareṅ ge. If you try, you will reach the target.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے ہدف حاصل کیا, us ne hadaf hāsil kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
حاصل کرنا (hāsil karnā) is built from hāsil, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥāṣil, "resulting, obtained", and کرنا, "to do".
