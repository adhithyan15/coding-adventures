## You'll want to know: ખંજવાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C228-SKIN228-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ખંજવાળવું** — *khanjvāḷvũ* — "to scratch, to itch".

> **બાળક આંખ નથી ખંજવાળતું.** — *bāḷak ā̃kh nathī khanjvāḷtũ* — "The child is not scratching its eye."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખંજવાળવું** (*khanjvāḷvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to itch", under the lexical Sanskrit *kharju*, "itching, scratching", a word it takes from Dravidian (CDIAL 3827).
