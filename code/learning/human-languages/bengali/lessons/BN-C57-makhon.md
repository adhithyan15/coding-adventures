## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মাখন** (*mākhôn*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *mrakṣaṇa*, "rubbing in; ointment, oil" (CDIAL 10378).
