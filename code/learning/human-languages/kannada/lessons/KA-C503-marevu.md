# ಮರೆವು (marevu) — forgetfulness

## You'll want to know: ಮರೆವು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C503-BUDDHI503-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರೆವು** — *marevu* — "forgetfulness".

> ವಯಸ್ಸಾದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಜ್ಜನಿಗೆ ಮರೆವು ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *vayassāda mēle ajjanige marevu heccāgide.*
>
> With age, grandfather's forgetfulness has grown.

The verb is ಮರೆ, to forget; ಮರೆಯಬೇಡಿ, please do not forget.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರೆವು** (*marevu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4760 lists Kannada *maṟe*, "to forget, be forgotten"; -*vu* makes the noun.
