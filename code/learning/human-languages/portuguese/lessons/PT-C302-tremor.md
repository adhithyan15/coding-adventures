## You'll want to know: o tremor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C302-SISMO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o tremor** — "the tremor, the trembling".

> Sentimos um tremor às três da manhã.
>
> *We felt a tremor at three in the morning.*

*Tremor de terra* — earth tremor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tremor* comes from Latin *tremor* "a trembling" (EtymDB), from *tremere* "to tremble".
