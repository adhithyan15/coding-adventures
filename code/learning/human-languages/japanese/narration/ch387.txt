Japanese, chapter 387: Meeting the Plane.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
でむかえる (demukaeru) — to go out to meet (someone arriving).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for departure from a country, then the Japanese for to take something out.

You'll want to know: でむかえる.
でむかえる — demukaeru — "to go out to meet (someone arriving)".
くうこうで そふを でむかえました。 — kūkō de sofu o demukaemashita — "We met my grandfather at the airport."
Polite でむかえます, demukaemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): ははが くうこうで そふを でむかえました。, haha ga kūkō de sofu o demukaemashita.

The word, taken apart.
でむかえる (demukaeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from de, "going out" (from deru) and mukaeru, "to welcome"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

くうこうで せんせいを みかけました。 — kūkō de sensei o mikakemashita — "I happened to see my teacher at the airport."
Polite みかけます, mikakemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): あねが えきで せんせいを みかけました。, ane ga eki de sensei o mikakemashita.

The word, taken apart.
みかける (mikakeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). Its first part is mi, from miru, "to see" (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
とうじょう (tōjō) — boarding (a plane or ship).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to go out to meet, then the Japanese for to happen to see.

You'll want to know: とうじょう.
とうじょう — tōjō — "boarding (a plane or ship)".
とうじょうの じかんは くじです。 — tōjō no jikan wa kuji desu — "Boarding time is nine o'clock."

The word, taken apart.
とうじょう (tōjō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are tō, "board" (Middle Chinese thap) and jō, "ride" (zying) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

なつやすみの こうくうけんは たかかったです。 — natsuyasumi no kōkūken wa takakatta desu — "The plane tickets for the summer holidays were expensive."
