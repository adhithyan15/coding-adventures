## You'll want to know: कराना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C488-JANCH488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कराना** — *karānā* — "to have something done".

> अरुण ने अस्पताल में ख़ून की जाँच कराई।
>
> *Aruṇ ne aspatāl meṁ khūn kī jā̃c karāī.*
>
> Arun had a blood test done at the hospital.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने जाँच कराई, *usne jā̃c karāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कराना** (*karānā*) is the causative partner of *karnā*, "to do": CDIAL lists Hindi *karānā*, "to cause to be made", under Sanskrit *karṓti*, "does" (CDIAL 2814). Someone else does the work for you.
