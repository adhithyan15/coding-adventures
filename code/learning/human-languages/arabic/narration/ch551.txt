Arabic, chapter 551: Sheets, cushions and a couch.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
شرشف (sharshaf) — a bedsheet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to polish, then the Arabic for to maintain.

You'll want to know: شرشف.
شرشف — sharshaf — "a bedsheet".
غيرت أمي شراشف السرير. ghayyarat ummī sharāshif as-sarīr. My mother changed the bedsheets.
Plural: شراشف, sharāshif.

The word, taken apart.
شرشف (sharshaf) is usually traced to Persian chādar-shab, "a night sheet", which reached Arabic through Turkish.

Lesson 2 of 6.
مخدة (mikhadda) — a cushion; a pillow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to maintain, then the Arabic for a bedsheet.

You'll want to know: مخدة.
مخدة — mikhadda — "a cushion; a pillow".
وضعت المخدة تحت رأسي. waḍaʿtu al-mikhadda taḥta raʾsī. I put the cushion under my head.

The word, taken apart.
مخدة (mikhadda) is built on the native Arabic root kh-d-d, "to furrow; the cheek" (Lane). Lane gives mikhadda as a pillow, so called because it is put under the cheek, khadd, which you know.

حوض — ḥawḍ — "a basin, a tub".
ملأ الولد حوض الاستحمام بالماء. malaʾa al-walad ḥawḍ al-istiḥmām bi-l-māʾ. The boy filled the bathtub with water.
Plural: أحواض, aḥwāḍ.
حوض (ḥawḍ) الاستحمام is a bathtub.

The word, taken apart.
حوض (ḥawḍ) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-w-ḍ, "a watering trough" (Lane). Lane gives ḥawḍ as a trough or cistern for water.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a cushion, then the Arabic for a basin.

You'll want to know: ممسحة.
ممسحة — mimsaḥa — "a mop".
نظفت الأرض بالممسحة. naẓẓaftu al-arḍ bi-l-mimsaḥa. I cleaned the floor with the mop.

The word, taken apart.
ممسحة (mimsaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root m-s-ḥ, "to wipe" (Lane). Masaḥa, to wipe, which you know, is from the same root; the pattern mifʿala names a tool.
