## Chapter 19 — People and simple identity *(planned)*

- extend **میں ... ہوں** *maiṅ ... hūṅ* from state to identity;
- add further masculine and feminine agreement one contrast at a time;
- contrast **ہے** *hai*, **ہیں** *haiṅ*, and **ہوں** *hūṅ* without a paradigm dump;
- generalize **مجھے** *mujhe* into the wider oblique-plus-postposition system;
- introduce more retroflex and aspirated letters only when useful words need them.

### Chapters 19-22 — negation, joining, and repair *(authored)*

- **Ch. 19 — The Word That Was Deferred**: نہیں in front of the verb, which is
  the sentence chapter 1 explicitly postponed → کیا at the front, marking a
  yes-or-no question → نہ … نہ, the one negator that stands before a noun →
  review. Empties SPINE-NEGATE-AND-ASK's omission ledger.
- **Ch. 20 — Two Things at Once**: اور (inherited) → یا (Persian) → لیکن
  (Arabic) → ٹھیک ہے, agreement built on chapter 4's adjective and chapter 2's
  copula → review, which runs all three joiners over every noun in chapters
  8-18.
- **Ch. 21 — Why, and What For**: کیوں → کون, which completes the k- question
  family chapter 17 opened → کیونکہ, built from this chapter's own two words →
  کہ → کے لیے plus an infinitive → review. Empties SPINE-SAY-WHY's ledger.
- **Ch. 22 — When You Are Lost**: کہنا → کیا کہا؟ → آرام سے → review, which
  replays the opening conversation of chapters 3-5 with the repair kit in it.

Every headword in these four chapters was chosen from the fifteen letters the
script ladder has taught. معاف, مہربانی, مگر, جب, جو, بھی and مت were all
rejected on that test and are recorded in the inventory beside the letters that
block them.

Twenty of the thirty-five glyphs this track shows are still untaught.
**ھ** *do-chashmī he* and **ٹ** are next by reach: together they finish
**ٹھیک**, the one line of the opening exchange chapters 17 and 18 could not
reach. After them, **د**, **ت**, **ب**, **ج** and **ق** are what the whole time
column waits on, and script-ductus already holds sourced Nastaliq records for
fifteen of the twenty never-taught letters, so the filmstrips can be cited rather
than invented.
