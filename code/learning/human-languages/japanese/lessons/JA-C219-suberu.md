# すべる (suberu) — to slip; to slide

## You'll want to know: すべる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C219-FALL219-02]; assesses=[] -->

**すべる** — *suberu* — "to slip; to slide".

> **ゆきの みちは すべりますか。** — *yuki no michi wa suberimasu ka* — "Is the snowy road slippery?"

Polite **すべります**, *suberimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すべる** (*suberu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
