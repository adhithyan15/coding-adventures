## You'll want to know: fūnus, fūneris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C330-FUNUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**fūnus, fūneris** — "a funeral; death".

> Fūnus māne per forum dūcēbātur.
>
> *In the morning the funeral was being led through the forum.*

Neuter, 3rd declension. The funerals of great men passed through the forum with music. English *funeral*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fūnus** is a Latin word whose origin is not settled; Lewis & Short link it, with doubt, with a word for smoke. English *funeral* comes from its family.
