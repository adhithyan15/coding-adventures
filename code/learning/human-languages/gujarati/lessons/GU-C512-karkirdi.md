## You'll want to know: કારકિર્દી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C512-NOKRI512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કારકિર્દી** — *kārkirdī* — "a career".

> મીરાએ સંગીતમાં કારકિર્દી બનાવી.
>
> *Mīrāe sangītmā̃ kārkirdī banāvī.*
>
> Mira made a career in music.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કારકિર્દી** (*kārkirdī*) is usually traced to Persian *kārkird*, "work done, a record of deeds", built on *kār*, "work".
