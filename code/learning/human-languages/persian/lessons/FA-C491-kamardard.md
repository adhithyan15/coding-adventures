## You'll want to know: کمردرد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C491-ACHES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کمردرد** (*kamardard*) — "backache, lower back pain".

> پدرم بعد از کار در باغ کمردرد گرفت.
>
> *pedaram ba'd az kâr dar bâgh kamardard gereft.*
>
> My father got backache after working in the garden.

*kamar* 'lower back, waist' + *dard* 'pain'. *kamardard gereftan* — to get backache.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kamardard* is built on two inherited words: *kamar*, 'waist, lower back', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *dard*, 'pain'.
