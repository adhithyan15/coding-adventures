## You'll want to know: проветривать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C431-SKVOZNYAK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**проветривать** (*provétrivat*) — "to air (a room)".

> Каждое утро мама проветривает комнату.
>
> *Kázhdoye útro máma provétrivayet kómnatu.*
>
> Every morning Mum airs the room.

Imperfective; perfective *проветрить*. Built on *ветер* — wind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*проветривать* is built from *про-* 'through' and *ветр-*, the root of *ветер* 'wind': to let the wind through. EtymDB does not list the verb.
