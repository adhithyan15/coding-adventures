## You'll want to know: નમૂનો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C362-SANSHODHAN362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નમૂનો** — *namūno* — "a sample".

> દાક્તરે લોહીનો નમૂનો લીધો.
>
> *dāktare lohīno namūno līdho.*
>
> The doctor took a blood sample.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નમૂનો** (*namūno*) is usually traced to Persian *namūna*, "a pattern, a sample".
