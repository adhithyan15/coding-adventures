## You'll want to know: la probabilità
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C376-RISCHIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la probabilità** — "the probability, the chance".

> Ci sono poche probabilità di vincere.
>
> *There is little chance of winning.*

*Con ogni probabilità* — in all likelihood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*probabilità* is a learned borrowing from Latin *probābilitās* "likelihood" (Lewis & Short), from *probābilis* "credible", on *probāre* "to test".
