## You'll want to know: مرجان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C471-KHALIJ471-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مرجان** — *marjān* — "coral".

> رأينا المرجان تحت الماء.
>
> *raʾaynā al-marjān taḥta al-māʾ.*
>
> We saw the coral under the water.
