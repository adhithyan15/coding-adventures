## You'll want to know: соска
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C496-BABY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**соска** (*sóska*) — "a dummy, a pacifier".

> Без соски малыш не засыпает.
>
> *Bez sóski malýsh ne zasypáyet.*
>
> The baby won't go to sleep without his dummy.

Feminine. Also the teat on a baby's bottle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*соска* is built on the root of *сосать* 'to suck'; EtymDB lists *соска* without a source.
