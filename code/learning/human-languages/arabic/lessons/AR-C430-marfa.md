## You'll want to know: مرفأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C430-MARFA430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مرفأ** — *marfaʾ* — "a harbour".

> كانت قوارب الصيادين في المرفأ.
>
> *kānat qawārib aṣ-ṣayyādīn fī al-marfaʾ.*
>
> The fishermen's boats were in the harbour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرفأ** (*marfaʾ*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *r-f-ʾ*, "to bring a ship close to the shore". The pattern *mafʿal* makes it a place.
