## You'll want to know: مضخة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C433-AJALA433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مضخة** — *miḍakhkha* — "a pump".

> نفخ أخي الإطار بمضخة صغيرة.
>
> *nafakha akhī al-iṭār bi-miḍakhkha ṣaghīra.*
>
> My brother pumped up the tyre with a small pump.

The pattern *mifʿala* names a tool.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مضخة** (*miḍakhkha*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ḍ-kh-kh*, "to spurt (of water)".
