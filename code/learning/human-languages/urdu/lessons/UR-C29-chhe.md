# چھ — six, and a puff of air

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-FIVE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Count one to five, then five to one.

## You'll want to know first — one word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-CHHE]; assesses=[UR-LEX-CHAR] -->

> **چھ** — *chhe* — **six**

Two signs and the shortest number in the whole count. The first is the **چ** of
**چار**; the second is the two-eyed **ھ** that puts a puff of air on the
consonant in front of it.

Hand at the mouth: *chār* moves nothing, *chhe* pushes air out. The tongue does
the same thing in both; the lungs do not.

## The word, taken apart — six, sex, ṣaṣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-ETYMON-CHHE-SIX-PIE]; assesses=[UR-LEX-CHHE] -->

**چھ** continues Sanskrit *ṣaṣ*, from Indo-European \**sweks*,
"six." English **six**, Latin **sex** and Greek **héx** are the same word.
