# ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ (koṇḍuko) — to buy for oneself; to purchase

## You'll want to know: ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C467-MARUKATTE467-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ** — *koṇḍuko* — "to buy for oneself; to purchase".

> ನಾನು ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೊಸ ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ ಕೊಂಡುಕೊಂಡೆ.
>
> *nānu mārukaṭṭeyalli hosa cappali koṇḍukoṇḍe.*
>
> I bought new sandals at the market.

Past (I …): ಕೊಂಡುಕೊಂಡೆ, *koṇḍukoṇḍe*.

ಕೊಂಡು is the past stem of ಕೊಳ್ಳು, to take or buy; ಕೊ adds for oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಂಡುಕೊ** (*koṇḍuko*) is native Dravidian: *koṇḍu*, the past stem of *koḷ*, *koḷḷu*, "to take, to obtain, to buy" (DEDR 2151), with the reflexive -*ko*.
