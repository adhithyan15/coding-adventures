Japanese, chapter 295: New Year at the Shrine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
はつもうで (hatsumōde) — the New Year shrine visit; the first shrine visit of the year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to liven up, then the Japanese for a Bon festival dance.

You'll want to know: はつもうで.
はつもうで — hatsumōde — "the New Year shrine visit; the first shrine visit of the year".
しょうがつに かぞくで はつもうでに いきました。 — shōgatsu ni kazoku de hatsumōde ni ikimashita — "At the new year the family made its first shrine visit."

The word, taken apart.
はつもうで (hatsumōde) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from hatsu, "first", and mōde, "a visit to a shrine" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ならす (narasu) — to ring; to sound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a shrine fortune slip, then the Japanese for a charm.

You'll want to know: ならす.
ならす — narasu — "to ring; to sound".
てらで かねを ならしました。 — tera de kane o narashimashita — "We rang the bell at the temple."
Polite ならします, narashimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): おとこが かねを ならしました。, otoko ga kane o narashimashita.

The word, taken apart.
ならす (narasu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
