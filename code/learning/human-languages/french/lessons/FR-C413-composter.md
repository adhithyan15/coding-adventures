## You'll want to know: composter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C413-QUAI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**composter** — "to stamp (a ticket)".

> Avant de monter, il a composté son billet.
>
> *Before getting on, he stamped his ticket.*

Paper tickets were stamped in a small machine on the platform; in Switzerland, *oblitérer* is also heard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*composter* is built on *composteur*, a stamping machine, which is usually traced to Italian *compositore* 'typesetter', from Latin *compōnere* 'to put together'. EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
