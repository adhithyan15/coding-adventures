## You'll want to know: معده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C495-ORGANS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**معده** (*me'de*) — "the stomach".

> بعد از غذای تند معده درد دارم.
>
> *ba'd az ghazâ-ye tond me'de dard dâram.*
>
> After spicy food I get a stomach ache.

*shekam* is the belly seen from outside; *me'de* is the organ. *me'de dard* — stomach ache.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*me'de* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *maʿida*, 'stomach'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
