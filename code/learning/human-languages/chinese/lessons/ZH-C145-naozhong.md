## You'll want to know: nàozhōng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C145-LATE145-03]; assesses=[] -->

*nàozhōng* — "alarm clock".

> *wǒ méiyǒu nàozhōng suǒyǐ wǒ chídào le* — I haven't got an alarm clock, so I was late.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nàozhōng* is built from *nào* 'to stir up', the *nao* of *rènao*, and *zhōng* 'bell', the *zhōng* of *fēnzhōng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nraewH* and *tsyowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a bell that stirs you up.
