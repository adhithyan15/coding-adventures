## You'll want to know: paulisper
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C293-PAULATIM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**paulisper** — "for a little while".

> Paulisper in tabernā mānsimus; deinde discessimus.
>
> *We stayed in the shop for a little while; then we left.*

It says how long, briefly. In Plautus a slave begs his master to wait *paulisper*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**paulisper** is built on *paulum*, "a little", the neuter of *paulus*, "small", and *per*, "through" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short): through a small stretch of time.
