Malayalam, chapter 254: Sparrow, Squirrel, Crab, Tail, Wing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കുരുവി (kuruvi) — a sparrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a hibiscus, then the Malayalam for holy basil.

You'll want to know: കുരുവി.
കുരുവി — kuruvi — "a sparrow".

The word, taken apart.
കുരുവി (kuruvi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kuruvi and Kannada gubbi (DEDR 1793).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a squirrel, then the Malayalam for a crab.

You'll want to know: വാൽ.
വാൽ — vāl — "a tail".

The word, taken apart.
വാൽ (vāl) from Sanskrit vāla, "tail, hair of the tail" (CDIAL 11572). DEDR appendix 57, which gathers Dravidian words taken from Indo-Aryan, lists it with Tamil vāl and Kannada bāla.
