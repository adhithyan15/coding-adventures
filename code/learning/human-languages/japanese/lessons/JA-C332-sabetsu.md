## You'll want to know: さべつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C332-KENRI332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**さべつ** — *sabetsu* — "discrimination".

> **さべつを しない ほうが いいと おもいます。** — *sabetsu o shinai hō ga ii to omoimasu* — "I think it is better not to discriminate."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さべつ** (*sabetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "distinction" (Middle Chinese *tsrhae*) and *betsu*, "separate" (*bjet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
