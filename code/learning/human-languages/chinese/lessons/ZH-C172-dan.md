## You'll want to know: dàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C172-TASTE172-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dàn* — "bland; weak (in taste)".

> *tāng tài dàn le* — The soup is too bland.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *damX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a thin taste'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
