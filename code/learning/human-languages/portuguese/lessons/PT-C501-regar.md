## You'll want to know: regar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C501-JARDIM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**regar** — "to water".

> Rego as plantas todas as manhãs.
>
> *I water the plants every morning.*

*Regar o jardim* — to water the garden.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regar* is inherited from Latin *rigāre* "to water, to wet" (EtymDB).
