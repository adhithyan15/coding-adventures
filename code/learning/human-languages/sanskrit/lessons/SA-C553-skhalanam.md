## You'll want to know: स्खलनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C553-SKHALANA553-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्खलनम्** — *skhalanam* — "a slip".

> यदि स्खलनं न अभविष्यत्, तर्हि सः विजेता अभविष्यत्।
>
> *yadi skhalanaṃ na abhaviṣyat, tarhi saḥ vijetā abhaviṣyat.*
>
> If there had been no slip, he would have been the victor.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्खलनम्** (*skhalanam*) comes from the root √skhal, "to stumble, to trip", with the ending *-ana*; MW gives *skhalana* as stumbling, tripping, faltering (MW).
