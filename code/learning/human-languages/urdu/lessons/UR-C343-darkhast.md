## You'll want to know: درخواست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C343-MAHKAMA343-02]; assesses=[] -->

**درخواست** — *darkhāst* — "an application; a request".

> میں نے نوکری کے لیے درخواست دی۔
>
> *maiṅ ne naukrī ke liye darkhāst dī.*
>
> I applied for a job.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درخواست** (*darkhāst*) is usually traced to Persian *darkhwāst*, "a request".
