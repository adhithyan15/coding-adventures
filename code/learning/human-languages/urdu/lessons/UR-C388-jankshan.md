## You'll want to know: جنکشن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C388-SIGNAL388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جنکشن** — *jankshan* — "a railway junction".

> یہ اسٹیشن ایک بڑا جنکشن ہے۔
>
> *yih isṭeshan ek baṛā jankshan hai.*
>
> This station is a big junction.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جنکشن** (*jankshan*) is borrowed from English *junction*: a station where railway lines meet.
