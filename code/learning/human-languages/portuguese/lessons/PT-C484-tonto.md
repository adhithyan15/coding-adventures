## You'll want to know: tonto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C484-ENXAQUECA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tonto** — "dizzy; silly".

> Depois da viagem de barco, fiquei tonto.
>
> *After the boat trip, I felt dizzy.*

It also means silly: *não sejas tonto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tonto* is inherited from Latin *attonitus* "thunderstruck, stunned" (EtymDB).
