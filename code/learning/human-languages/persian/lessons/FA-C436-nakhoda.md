## You'll want to know: ناخدا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C436-ESKELE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ناخدا** (*nâkhodâ*) — "a captain (of a ship), a skipper".

> ناخدا سی سال روی دریا کار کرده است.
>
> *Nâkhodâ si sâl ru-ye daryâ kâr karde ast.*
>
> The captain has worked at sea for thirty years.

Literally 'ship-master'. The navy rank is also *nâkhodâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nâkhodâ* is a native compound, which EtymDB analyses as *nâv* 'ship' and *khodâ* 'chief, master': the master of the ship. *Khodâ* is usually traced to Middle Persian *xwadāy* 'lord'.
