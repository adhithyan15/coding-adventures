## You'll want to know: крепость
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C444-RATUSHA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**крепость** (*krépost*) — "a fortress".

> На холме стоит старая крепость.
>
> *Na kholmé stoít stáraya krépost.*
>
> An old fortress stands on the hill.

Feminine. Also strength: *крепость чая* — the strength of tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*крепость* is inherited through Old East Slavic *krěpostĭ* 'strength' from Proto-Slavic *krěpostĭ* (EtymDB), on the root of *крепкий* 'strong': first strength, then a strong place.
