## You'll want to know: кипеть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C329-KIPYATIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**кипеть** (*kipét*) — "to boil (of a liquid)".

> Вода уже кипит.
>
> *Vodá uzhé kipít.*
>
> The water is already boiling.

Imperfective; perfective *вскипеть* (*vskipét*). *Кипятить* boils something; *кипеть* is the liquid itself boiling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*кипеть* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *kypěti* "to boil", from PIE *kwep-* "to smoke, to boil, to move violently" (EtymDB).
