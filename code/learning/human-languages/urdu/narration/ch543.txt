Urdu, chapter 543: A Good Guess.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
توقع (tavaqqo) — what one expects (to happen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a point (in an argument), then the Urdu for a summary.

You'll want to know: توقع.
توقع — tavaqqo — "what one expects (to happen)".
اگر نتیجہ توقع سے بہتر ہوا تو جشن منائیں گے۔ agar natījā tavaqqo se behtar huā to jashn manāeṅ ge. If the result is better than expected, we'll celebrate.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
توقع (tavaqqo) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tawaqquʿ, "expecting, waiting for".

Lesson 2 of 5.
پیش گوئی (pesh goī) — a prediction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a summary, then the Urdu for what one expects.

You'll want to know: پیش گوئی.
پیش گوئی — pesh goī — "a prediction".
اگر پیش گوئی ٹھیک ہے تو کل بارش ہو گی۔ agar pesh goī ṭhīk hai to kal bārish ho gī. If the forecast is right, it will rain tomorrow.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
پیش گوئی (pesh goī) is usually traced to Persian pēshgōī, "a prediction", built from pēsh, "before", and gō-, "to say", with the noun ending -ī: saying a thing before it happens.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تاڑنا (tāṛnā) — to see through (someone's plan); to catch on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for what one expects, then the Urdu for a prediction.

You'll want to know: تاڑنا.
تاڑنا — tāṛnā — "to see through (someone's plan); to catch on".
اگر امی نے ہماری بات تاڑی تو مشکل ہو گی۔ agar ammī ne hamārī bāt tāṛī to mushkil ho gī. If our mother sees through our plan, there'll be trouble.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سب کچھ تاڑا, us ne sab kuchh tāṛā.

The word, taken apart.
تاڑنا (tāṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin tāṛnā, "to understand", under taḍati, "stretches", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5633).

آنکنا — āṅknā — "to put a value on; to appraise".
اگر سنار نے انگوٹھی آنکی تو قیمت بتائے گا۔ agar sunār ne angūṭhī āṅkī to qīmat batāe gā. If the goldsmith values the ring, he'll tell us the price.
