## You'll want to know: битва
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C323-VOYNA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**битва** (*bítva*) — "a battle".

> Битва была долгой и трудной.
>
> *Bítva bylá dólgoy i trúdnoy.*
>
> The battle was long and hard.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*битва* is inherited through Old East Slavic *bitva* from Proto-Slavic *bitva*, from PIE *bʰih₂-tweh₂* (EtymDB), the root of *бить* "to beat".
