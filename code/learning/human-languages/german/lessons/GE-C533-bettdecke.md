## You'll want to know: die Bettdecke
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C533-GEMUETLICH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Bettdecke** — "duvet, quilt".

> Unter der dicken Bettdecke ist es schön warm.
>
> *It's lovely and warm under the thick duvet.*

Plural: *die Bettdecken*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bettdecke* is built on *Bett* "bed", from Proto-Germanic *badją*, and *Decke* "cover", inherited from Middle High German *decke* (EtymDB).
