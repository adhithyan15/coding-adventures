## You'll want to know: nàixīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C258-KIND258-03]; assesses=[] -->

*nàixīn* — "patient with people".

> *lǎoshī hěn nàixīn* — The teacher is very patient.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nàixīn* is built from *nài* 'to endure' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nojH* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to bear'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
