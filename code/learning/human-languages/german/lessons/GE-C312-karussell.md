## You'll want to know: das Karussell
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C312-VOLKSFEST-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Karussell** — "the merry-go-round, the carousel".

> Die Kinder fuhren dreimal Karussell.
>
> *The children went on the merry-go-round three times.*

Plural: *die Karussells*. *Karussell fahren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Karussell* was borrowed from French *carrousel*, which came from Italian *carosello* (EtymDB).
