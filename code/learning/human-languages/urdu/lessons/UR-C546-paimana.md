## You'll want to know: پیمانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C546-MASHAQQAT546-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پیمانہ** — *paimānā* — "a yardstick; a measure".

> اگر پیسہ ہی پیمانہ ہوتا تو استاد امیر ہوتے۔
>
> *agar paisā hī paimānā hotā to ustād amīr hote.*
>
> If money were the only yardstick, teachers would be rich.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیمانہ** (*paimānā*) is usually traced to Persian *paymāna*, "a measure; a measuring cup".
