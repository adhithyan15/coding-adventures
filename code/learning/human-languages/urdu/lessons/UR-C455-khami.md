## You'll want to know: خامی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C455-FLAW455-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خامی** — *khāmī* — "a shortcoming".

> ہر انسان میں کوئی نہ کوئی خامی ہوتی ہے۔
>
> *har insān meṅ koī na koī khāmī hotī hai.*
>
> Everyone has some shortcoming or other.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خامی** (*khāmī*) is usually traced to Persian *khāmī*, "rawness", built on *khām*, "raw, unripe": a shortcoming is something not yet ripe.
