# નિશ્ચિત (niścit) — definite; settled

## You'll want to know: નિશ્ચિત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C367-SAMBHAV367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નિશ્ચિત** — *niścit* — "definite; settled".

> જો તારીખ નિશ્ચિત થશે તો હું તમને ફોન કરીશ.
>
> *jo tārīkh niścit thaśe to hũ tamne phon karīś.*
>
> If the date is settled, I will phone you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિશ્ચિત** (*niścit*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *niścita*, "determined, settled" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
