## You'll want to know: धारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C297-NIRVAPANA297-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धारा** — *dhārā* — "a stream of water".

> जलस्य धारा अग्नौ पतति।
>
> *jalasya dhārā agnau patati.*
>
> A stream of water falls on the fire.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धारा** (*dhārā*) MW gives *dhārā* as a stream or current of water, without a root in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
