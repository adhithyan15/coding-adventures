# ગીઝર (gījhar) — a water heater; a geyser

## You'll want to know: ગીઝર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C410-BATH410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગીઝર** — *gījhar* — "a water heater; a geyser".

> હોટેલના ગીઝરમાં ગરમ પાણી નથી.
>
> *hoṭelnā gījharmā̃ garam pāṇī nathī.*
>
> There is no hot water in the hotel's water heater.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગીઝર** (*gījhar*) is borrowed from English *geyser*, which in India means a water heater in the bathroom.
