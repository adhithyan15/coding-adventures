# Check the labels and their places

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-AGE-01, PA-FORM-LABEL-PHONE-01, PA-FORM-LABEL-DATE-01] -->

Read the known labels once: **ਉਮਰ:**, **ਫ਼ੋਨ:**, **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼:**. The little
dot marks in **ਫ਼** and **ਖ਼** belong to their spellings.

## Writing — three different layout passes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AGE-PHONE-DATE-REPAIR-01, PA-FORM-AGE-PHONE-DATE-ORDER-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

First compare label spelling; restore a missing dot without touching digits.
Then compare spacing: one readable space follows each colon, and the phone
has one group space. Finally compare placement: age is line one, phone line
two, date line three. Move a whole correct value to its proper line; do not
rewrite its digits.

## Guided Practice — a single spelling repair
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-DATE-01, PA-FORM-AGE-PHONE-DATE-REPAIR-01] -->

In **ਤਾਰੀਖ: ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫**, fix only the label to **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼**. The date stays
unchanged. In **ਫੋਨ: ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫**, fix only the label to **ਫ਼ੋਨ**.
