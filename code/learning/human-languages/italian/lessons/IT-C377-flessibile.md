## You'll want to know: flessibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C377-IMPEGNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**flessibile** — "flexible".

> Ho un orario flessibile: posso cominciare alle dieci.
>
> *I have flexible hours: I can start at ten.*

Of things too: *un tubo flessibile* — a hose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flessibile* is a learned borrowing from Latin *flexibilis* "pliant, easily bent" (Lewis & Short), from *flectere* "to bend".
