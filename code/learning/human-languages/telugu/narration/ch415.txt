Telugu, chapter 415: Learning and Teaching.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
విద్య (vidya) — education; learning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a scholarship, then the Telugu for to question.

You'll want to know: విద్య.
విద్య — vidya — "education; learning".
ప్రతి పిల్లవాడికి విద్య అవసరం. prati pillavāḍiki vidya avasaraṁ. Every child needs education.
విద్యార్థి, a student, and విశ్వవిద్యాలయం, a university, which you know, are built on it.

The word, taken apart.
విద్య (vidya) is borrowed from Sanskrit vidyā, "knowledge" (CDIAL 11741), from vid, "to know"; Telugu shortens the final long a.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: తర్కించు.
తర్కించు — tarkiñcu — "to debate; to reason".
వాళ్ళు రాత్రంతా ఆ ప్రశ్న గురించి తర్కించారు. vāḷḷu rātrantā ā praśna guriñci tarkiñcāru. They debated that question all night.
Past (I …): తర్కించాను, tarkiñcānu.
The verb of తర్కం, logic.

The word, taken apart.
తర్కించు (tarkiñcu) is built on Sanskrit tarka, "inquiry" (CDIAL 5714), "reasoning" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; the noun is తర్కం.

Lesson 5 of 5.
బోధించు (bōdhiñcu) — to instruct; to teach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for study, then the Telugu for to debate.

You'll want to know: బోధించు.
బోధించు — bōdhiñcu — "to instruct; to teach".
ఆమె పిల్లలకు గణితం బోధిస్తుంది. āme pillalaku gaṇitaṁ bōdhistundi. She teaches mathematics to children.
Past (I …): బోధించాను, bōdhiñcānu.
Formal beside నేర్పు, which you know; the person taught takes -కి.

The word, taken apart.
బోధించు (bōdhiñcu) is built on Sanskrit bodhana, "causing to awake; enlightening, teaching" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
