## You'll want to know: die Ermittlung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C284-VERDACHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ermittlung** — "the investigation".

> Die Ermittlungen dauerten zwei Monate.
>
> *The investigations took two months.*

Mostly plural: *die Ermittlungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ermittlung* is built on *ermitteln* "to investigate", from *Mittel* "middle, means" (EtymDB), with the ending *-ung*.
