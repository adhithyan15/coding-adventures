# ચસકો (casko) — a twinge; a sudden sharp pain

## You'll want to know: ચસકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C483-VEDNA483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચસકો** — *casko* — "a twinge; a sudden sharp pain".

> સવારે કમરમાં ચસકો આવ્યો.
>
> *savāre kamarmā̃ casko āvyo.*
>
> This morning I felt a twinge in my back.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચસકો** (*casko*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*cassakk-*, "throb, twitch, sudden pain"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *casak*, "acute pain" (CDIAL 4730).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
