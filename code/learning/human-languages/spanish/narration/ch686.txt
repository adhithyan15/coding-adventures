Spanish, chapter 686: Hacia la cima.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la cima — the top, the summit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the mountain, then the Spanish for the desert.

You'll want to know: la cima.
la cima — "the top, the summit".
Llegamos a la cima a mediodía. We reached the top at midday.
En la cima de — at the top of.

The word, taken apart.
cima comes from Latin cyma "a young sprout" (EtymDB), from Greek kŷma "a swelling, a wave": the top of a plant, then the top of a hill.

Y pico — and a bit: las tres y pico.

The word, taken apart.
pico is inherited from Latin beccus "beak" (EtymDB), which the Romans took from Gaulish.

You'll want to know: la subida.
la subida — "the climb, the way up; the rise".
La subida al pico dura dos horas. The climb to the peak takes two hours.
The opposite is la bajada, the way down.

The word, taken apart.
subida is built on subir "to go up", which is inherited from Latin subīre.
