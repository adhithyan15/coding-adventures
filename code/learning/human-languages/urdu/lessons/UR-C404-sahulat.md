## You'll want to know: سہولت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C404-GHUSL404-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سہولت** — *sahūlat* — "a facility; a convenience".

> اس ہوٹل میں ہر سہولت ہے۔
>
> *is hoṭal meṅ har sahūlat hai.*
>
> This hotel has every facility.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سہولت** (*sahūlat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *suhūla*, "ease, easiness".
