## You'll want to know: caducar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C657-PAPELES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**caducar** — "to expire".

> Mi pasaporte caduca en marzo.
>
> *My passport expires in March.*

Food *caduca* too: *la fecha de caducidad* is the use-by date.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caducar* is built on *caduco* "worn out, past its time" (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *cadūcus* "falling, fallen" (Lewis & Short), from *cadere* "to fall".
