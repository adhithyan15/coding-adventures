## You'll want to know: કાયદો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C347-KAYDO347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કાયદો** — *kāydo* — "a law".

> નવો કાયદો ગરીબ માણસો માટે સારો છે.
>
> *navo kāydo garīb māṇso māṭe sāro che.*
>
> The new law is good for poor people.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાયદો** (*kāydo*) is usually traced to Arabic *qāʿida*, "a base, a rule", through Persian.
