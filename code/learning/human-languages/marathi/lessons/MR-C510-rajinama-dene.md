# राजीनामा देणे (rājīnāmā deṇe) — to resign

## You'll want to know: राजीनामा देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C510-SODNE510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**राजीनामा देणे** — *rājīnāmā deṇe* — "to resign".

> मीराने जुन्या कंपनीचा राजीनामा दिला.
>
> *mīrāne junyā kampanīcā rājīnāmā dilā.*
>
> Meera resigned from her old company.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने राजीनामा दिला, *tyāne rājīnāmā dilā*.

The job takes -चा: नोकरीचा राजीनामा देणे; राजीनामा is the letter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राजीनामा देणे** (*rājīnāmā deṇe*) is राजीनामा, a loan from Persian *rāżī-nāma*, "a letter of consent", with देणे, "to give".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
