## You'll want to know: संवर्धन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C449-NISARGA449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संवर्धन** — *saṁvardhan* — "conservation".

> वाघांच्या संवर्धनासाठी हे अभयारण्य आहे.
>
> *vāghāñcyā saṁvardhanāsāṭhī he abhayāraṇya āhe.*
>
> This sanctuary is for the conservation of tigers.

Neuter.

संवर्धन करणे, to conserve and help grow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संवर्धन** (*saṁvardhan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṁvardhana*, "growing up, promoting, rearing" (Monier-Williams).
