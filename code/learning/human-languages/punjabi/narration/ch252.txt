Punjabi, chapter 252: Bundles and Curtains.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਪੋਟਲੀ (poṭlī) — a small bundle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a bread basket, then the Punjabi for copper.

You'll want to know: ਪੋਟਲੀ.
ਪੋਟਲੀ — poṭlī — "a small bundle".
ਦਾਦੀ ਦੀ ਪੋਟਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੁੜ ਹੈ। dādī dī poṭlī vich guṛ hai. There is jaggery in my grandmother's little bundle.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੋਟਲੀ (poṭlī) is inherited from Sanskrit pōṭṭalī, "bundle", a late word that CDIAL sets under a reconstructed pōṭṭa, "bundle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi poṭlī, "small bag" (CDIAL 8396).

Lesson 3 of 7.
ਵਾਣ (vāṇ) — cot rope; coarse rope for weaving a cot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a small bundle, then the Punjabi for a large bundle.

You'll want to know: ਵਾਣ.
ਵਾਣ — vāṇ — "cot rope; coarse rope for weaving a cot".
ਮੰਜੇ ਦਾ ਵਾਣ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਹੈ। manje dā vāṇ purāṇā hai. The rope of the cot is old.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਾਣ (vāṇ) is inherited from a reconstructed vāṇa, "a sort of rush, twisted grass rope"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vāṇ, "rush for rope-making, coarse twine" (CDIAL 11483).

Lesson 4 of 7.
ਤਖ਼ਤਾ (takhtā) — a wooden board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a large bundle, then the Punjabi for cot rope.

You'll want to know: ਤਖ਼ਤਾ.
ਤਖ਼ਤਾ — takhtā — "a wooden board".
ਮੈਂ ਤਖ਼ਤੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਰੱਖਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ takhte utte bhāṇḍe rakkhdā hāṁ. I keep the pots on a wooden board.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਖ਼ਤਾ (takhtā) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian takhta, "a board, a plank".
