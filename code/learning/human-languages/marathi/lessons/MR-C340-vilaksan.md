## You'll want to know: विलक्षण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C340-RAHASYA340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विलक्षण** — *vilakṣaṇ* — "extraordinary; strange".

> त्या रात्री एक विलक्षण गोष्ट घडली.
>
> *tyā rātrī ek vilakṣaṇ goṣṭa ghaḍlī.*
>
> Something extraordinary happened that night.

विलक्षण keeps one form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विलक्षण** (*vilakṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vilakṣaṇa*, 'having different marks, unlike others' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
