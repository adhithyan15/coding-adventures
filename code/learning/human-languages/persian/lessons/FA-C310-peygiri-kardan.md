## You'll want to know: پیگیری کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C310-TAKZIB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پیگیری کردن** (*peygiri kardan*) — "to follow up, to pursue".

> خبرنگار این خبر را تا آخر پیگیری کرد.
>
> *Khabarnegâr in khabar râ tâ âkhar peygiri kard.*
>
> The journalist followed this story through to the end.

*pey* "footstep, track" + *giri* "taking": to keep following the trail of something. Present *peygiri mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peygiri* joins *pey* "footstep, track" and *giri* "taking", from *gereftan* "to take", both native Persian; *kardan* "to do" is inherited too. EtymDB lists no entry for the compound.
