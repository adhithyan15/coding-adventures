Arabic, chapter 519: Trainees and volunteers.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
سيرة ذاتية (sīra dhātiyya) — a CV; a life story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an adviser, then the Arabic for to aspire.

You'll want to know: سيرة ذاتية.
سيرة ذاتية — sīra dhātiyya — "a CV; a life story".
أرسلت سيرتي الذاتية إلى الشركة. arsaltu sīratī adh-dhātiyya ilā ash-sharika. I sent my CV to the company.
سيرة alone is a life story, a biography.

The word, taken apart.
سيرة ذاتية (sīra dhātiyya) is built on the native Arabic root s-y-r, "to go, to travel" (Lane): a sīra is a way of going, the path of a life. Dhātiyya is "of the self", from dhāt, self.

تطوع — taṭawwaʿa — "to volunteer".
تطوعت أختي في المستشفى في الصيف. taṭawwaʿat ukhtī fī al-mustashfā fī aṣ-ṣayf. My sister volunteered at the hospital in the summer.
Present: يتطوع, yataṭawwaʿ.

The word, taken apart.
تطوع (taṭawwaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-w-ʿ, "to comply, to be willing" (Lane). Lane gives Form V taṭawwaʿa as to do a good thing that is not required.

تبرع أبي بالمال للمدرسة. tabarraʿa abī bi-l-māl li-l-madrasa. My father donated money to the school.
Present: يتبرع, yatabarraʿ.
It takes بـ for the gift: تبرع (tabarraʿa) بالدم, to give blood.

The word, taken apart.
تبرع (tabarraʿa) is built on the native Arabic root b-r-ʿ, "to excel, to surpass" (Lane). Form V tabarraʿa is to give more than is asked: to give freely.

يعمل في المكتب ثلاثة متدربين. yaʿmalu fī al-maktab thalāthat mutadarribīn. Three trainees work in the office.

The word, taken apart.
متدرب (mutadarrib) is built on the native Arabic root d-r-b, "to be used to, to be practised in" (Lane). Mutadarrib is the participle of Form V: one being trained.
