## You'll want to know: gotear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C769-GOTERA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**gotear** — "to drip, to leak".

> El grifo goteó toda la noche.
>
> *The tap dripped all night.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gotear* is built on *gota* 'a drop' with the ending *-ear*. *Gota* is inherited from Latin *gutta* 'a drop' (Lewis & Short).
