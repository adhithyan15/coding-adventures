## You'll want to know: duǒyǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C234-RAIN234-05]; assesses=[] -->

*duǒyǔ* — "to shelter from the rain".

> *xiàyǔ le wǒmen qù fàndiàn duǒyǔ* — It started raining, so we sheltered in a restaurant.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Tone: duǒ and yǔ are both third tones, so the first rises and it sounds like duóyǔ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duǒyǔ* is built from *duǒ* 'to hide' and 雨 'rain'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.
