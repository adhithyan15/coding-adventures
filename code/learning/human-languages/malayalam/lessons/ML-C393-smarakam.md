## You'll want to know: സ്മാരകം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C393-SMARAKAM393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**സ്മാരകം** — *smārakaṁ* — "a memorial; a monument".

> യുദ്ധത്തിൽ മരിച്ചവരുടെ സ്മാരകം നഗരത്തിന്റെ നടുവിൽ ആണ്.
>
> *yuddhattil mariccavaruṭe smārakaṁ nagarattinṟe naṭuvil āṇŭ.*
>
> The memorial to those who died in the war is in the middle of the city.

Anything built to keep a memory: a statue, a hall, a pillar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സ്മാരകം** (*smārakaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *smāraka*, "recalling, reminding of" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
