## You'll want to know: मजला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C421-BANDHKAM421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मजला** — *majlā* — "a floor; a storey".

> आमचं घर चौथ्या मजल्यावर आहे.
>
> *āmcaṁ ghar cauthyā majalyāvar āhe.*
>
> Our flat is on the fourth floor.

Masculine.

तळमजला, the ground floor; पहिला मजला, the first floor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मजला** (*majlā*) is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *manzil*, "a stage of a journey, a halting place", through Persian.
