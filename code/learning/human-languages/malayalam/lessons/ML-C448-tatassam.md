## You'll want to know: തടസ്സം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C448-PRATISANDHI448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**തടസ്സം** — *taṭassaṁ* — "an obstacle".

> മഴ കളിക്ക് തടസ്സമായി.
>
> *maḻa kaḷikkŭ taṭassamāyi.*
>
> The rain became an obstacle to the game.

To become an obstacle to: -ക്ക് തടസ്സമാകുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തടസ്സം** (*taṭassaṁ*) is usually traced to native Dravidian തട, "resistance, what impedes or stops" (DEDR 3031); the ending -സ്സം is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
