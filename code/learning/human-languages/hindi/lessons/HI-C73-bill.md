# बिल (bil) — the bill

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Which of your two words is the place, and which is the meal?

## You'll want to know: बिल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C73-EATING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बिल** (*bil*) — "the bill".

English *bill*, borrowed for the slip of paper and used for nothing else. Three
letters: ब, the ि matra written to its left, and ल.

Now a warning your eye needs. You already hold **बिलकुल**, "absolutely", and you
have met **बिल्ली**, a cat. All three open on the same three letters and none of
them is related to the others — बिलकुल is Arabic, बिल्ली is Sanskrit, बिल is
English. Reading the first syllable and guessing will send you to the wrong word.

**बिल दीजिए** asks for it, in the same polite frame that got you a ticket and a
cup of tea.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Three: होटल, नाश्ता, बिल. A place, a meal, and what arrives afterwards.
