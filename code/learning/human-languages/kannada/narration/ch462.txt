ಮಂಚದ ಮೇಲೆ ಒಂದು ಸೊಳ್ಳೆಪರದೆ ಇತ್ತು. mañcada mēle ondu soḷḷeparade ittu. There was a mosquito net over the bed.

ಸೊಳ್ಳೆಪರದೆ (soḷḷeparade) is a Kannada compound of ಸೊಳ್ಳೆ, "a mosquito", and ಪರದೆ, "a curtain", which is usually traced to Persian parda.

ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟು (kiruguṭṭu) — to creak; to squeak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mosquito net, then the Kannada for a heater.

You'll want to know: ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟು.
ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟು — kiruguṭṭu — "to creak; to squeak".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ಕೊಠಡಿಯ ಹಳೆಯ ಬಾಗಿಲು ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟಿತು. rātri koṭhaḍiya haḷeya bāgilu kiruguṭṭitu. At night the old door of the room creaked.
Past (it …): ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟಿತು, kiruguṭṭitu.
Of doors, brakes, sandals and birds: a thin, sharp sound.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟು (kiruguṭṭu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1590 lists Kannada kiṟuguṭṭu, kirguṭṭu among words for crying out and shrill sounds.
