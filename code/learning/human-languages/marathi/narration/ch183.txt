You'll want to know: किल्ला.
किल्ला — killā — "a fort".

The word, taken apart.
किल्ला (killā) is a loan from Arabic qalʿa, "fortress", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-l-ʿ (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पुतळा (putaḷā) — a statue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tower, then the Marathi for a monument.

You'll want to know: पुतळा.
पुतळा — putaḷā — "a statue".

The word, taken apart.
पुतळा (putaḷā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from putrala, "puppet, pupil of the eye", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 8269). CDIAL compares Sanskrit putrī, "puppet". It glosses the Marathi word "doll".
