## You'll want to know: die Sehnsucht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C472-SEHNSUCHT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Sehnsucht** — "longing, yearning".

> Er hatte große Sehnsucht nach seiner Familie.
>
> *He longed for his family.*

Plural: *die Sehnsüchte*. *Sehnsucht nach* + dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sehnsucht* is built on *sehnen* "to long" and *Sucht* "sickness, craving" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Old High German *suht*, from Proto-Germanic *suhtiz*.
