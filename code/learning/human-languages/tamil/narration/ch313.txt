Tamil, chapter 313: Time Words for a Story.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
இதற்கிடையில் (itaṟkiṭaiyil) — meanwhile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to recite, then the Tamil for oral tradition.

You'll want to know: இதற்கிடையில்.
இதற்கிடையில் — itaṟkiṭaiyil — "meanwhile".
இதற்கிடையில், காவலர்கள் வீட்டுக்கு வந்தார்கள். itaṟkiṭaiyil, kāvalarkaḷ vīṭṭukku vantārkaḷ. Meanwhile, the police came to the house.
இது + இடை, the space between, + -இல்: in the meantime.

The word, taken apart.
இதற்கிடையில் (itaṟkiṭaiyil) is a Tamil phrase grown into one word, built from இதற்கு, "to this", and இடையில், "in between", from native இடை, "space, interval" (DEDR 434).

Lesson 2 of 5.
உடனடியாக (uṭaṉaṭiyāka) — immediately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for oral tradition, then the Tamil for meanwhile.

You'll want to know: உடனடியாக.
உடனடியாக — uṭaṉaṭiyāka — "immediately".
தீயணைப்பு வீரர்கள் உடனடியாக வந்தார்கள். tīyaṇaippu vīrarkaḷ uṭaṉaṭiyāka vantārkaḷ. The firefighters came immediately.
Stronger and more formal than உடனே.

The word, taken apart.
உடனடியாக (uṭaṉaṭiyāka) is built on native உடன், "together with, at once" (DEDR 945), with அடி and the adverb ending -ஆக.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ஒருவழியாக (oruvaḻiyāka) — at long last.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for immediately, then the Tamil for gradually.

You'll want to know: ஒருவழியாக.
ஒருவழியாக — oruvaḻiyāka — "at long last".
ஒருவழியாக, பேருந்து வந்தது. oruvaḻiyāka, pēruntu vantatu. At long last, the bus came.
Said with relief after a wait or a struggle.

The word, taken apart.
ஒருவழியாக (oruvaḻiyāka) is built from ஒரு, "one", and native வழி, "way, means" (DEDR 5297), with the adverb ending -ஆக: somehow, one way or another.
