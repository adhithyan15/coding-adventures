## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-AVENIDA]; assesses=[] -->

**la avenida** — "the avenue," usually a broad street.

The Academy derives **avenida** from Spanish **avenir**. The older noun could
name something that came in force — especially a river's sudden rise — and
also a road that leads, or lets people come, to a particular place. The modern
broad city street grew from that road sense.

Keep the scale contrast:

- **la calle** — the general word for a street;
- **la avenida** — usually a broad street, sometimes tree-lined.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

You can name the broad street that begins a set of directions.
