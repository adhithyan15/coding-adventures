# ਆਇਆ, ਆਈ, ਆਏ — came

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AUNA, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *to come*. Then *Aman went home yesterday*.

## Grammar Lens: came, three ways
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AUNA, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-NEG-NOT-UNDERSTOOD-01, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

**ਆਉਣਾ** has three came forms, like the three went forms: **ਆਇਆ** for a man,
**ਆਈ** for a woman, **ਆਏ** for a group or for respect.

> **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਇਆ।** — *Aman kallh āiā.* — Aman came yesterday.

> **ਕੁੜੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ।** — *kuṛī kallh āī.* — The girl came yesterday.

> **ਉਹ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਏ।** — *uh kallh āe.* — They came yesterday.

The **ਆਈ** in **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ** is the woman's form: **ਸਮਝ**,
"understanding", is a feminine word, and it is the one that did not come.
CDIAL lists Punjabi *āiā*, "he came" (CDIAL 1045).
