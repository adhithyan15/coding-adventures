## You'll want to know: urceus, urceī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C554-SUBS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**urceus, urceī** — "a jug, pitcher".

> Ancilla aquam urceō portat.
>
> *The maid carries water in a jug.*

Masculine, second declension.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**urceus** is a word of uncertain origin; Lewis & Short compares Greek *hurche* "a jar", and EtymDB links it with *orca* "a cask", but nothing is settled.
