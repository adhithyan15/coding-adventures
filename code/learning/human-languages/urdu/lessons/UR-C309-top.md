## You'll want to know: توپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C309-HAMLA309-04]; assesses=[] -->

**توپ** — *top* — "a cannon".

> قلعے پر چار توپیں تھیں۔
>
> *qile par chār topeṅ thīṅ.*
>
> There were four cannons on the fort.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**توپ** (*top*) is usually traced to Turkish *top*, "a ball", and so a cannon, which came into Urdu through Persian. It is feminine: **چار توپیں**.
