## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಡತ** (*kaḍata*) is a loan from Indo-Aryan. DEDR lists Kannada *kaḍita*, *kaḍata*, "a book of folded cloth covered with charcoal paste", in its appendix of such loans (DEDR App. 23): the old account book became the file.
