## You'll want to know: समीक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C366-NISHKARSHA366-02]; assesses=[] -->

**समीक्षा** — *samīkṣā* — "a review; a close inspection".

> समितिः योजनायाः समीक्षां करोति।
>
> *samitiḥ yojanāyāḥ samīkṣāṃ karoti.*
>
> The committee reviews the plan.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समीक्षा** (*samīkṣā*) is built on *sam* and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives *sam-īkṣā* "thorough or close inspection" and "a view, an opinion" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
