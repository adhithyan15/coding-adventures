## You'll want to know: la condizione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C387-REQUISITO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la condizione** — "the condition".

> Ti aiuto a una condizione: poi tu aiuti me.
>
> *I will help you on one condition: then you help me.*

*A condizione che* + subjunctive — on condition that. *In buone condizioni* — in good shape.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condizione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *condiciō*, later spelled *conditiō*, "agreement, terms" (EtymDB), from *condīcere* "to agree upon", built on *dīcere* "to say".
