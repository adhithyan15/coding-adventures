Gujarati, chapter 123: Turtle, Crocodile, Mosquito, Fly, Bee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કાચબો (kācbo) — a turtle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a pigeon, then the Gujarati for a duck.

You'll want to know: કાચબો.
કાચબો (kācbo) — "a turtle".

The word, taken apart.
કાચબો (kācbo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kacchapa, "turtle, tortoise" (CDIAL 2619).

Lesson 3 of 5.
મચ્છર (macchar) — a mosquito.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a turtle, then the Gujarati for a crocodile.

You'll want to know: મચ્છર.
મચ્છર (macchar) — "a mosquito".

The word, taken apart.
મચ્છર (macchar) is a tadbhava of the lexical Sanskrit matsara, "mosquito, fly" (CDIAL 9757). CDIAL lists Gujarati machrũ, "gnat", there, and finds no support for the idea that the Sanskrit word was made up from Prakrit.
