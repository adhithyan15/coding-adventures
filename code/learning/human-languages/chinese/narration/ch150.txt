Lesson 1 of 7.
过去 (guòqù) — in the past; to go over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
A new chapter, and it opens on time that has already gone by.
[your turn — recall: say 我正在吃饭 — wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn — R1, the chapter you have finished]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 一边 — yìbiān, at the same time — R1]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 以前 — yǐqián, before — R2, a few chapters back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 过去.
过去 — guòqù — "in the past; to go over (there)".
过去 我家在大学里边 guòqù, wǒ jiā zài dàxué lǐbian — In the past, our home was on the university grounds.
我过去 wǒ guòqù — I'll go over there.
The next lesson puts the first character in your hand. It is the guò of guònián and nánguò.

The word, taken apart.
过去 (guòqù) is built from 过 (guò) 'to pass' and 去 'to go'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, kwaH and khjoH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to go beyond; to exceed' and 'to leave'. 'The past', time that has gone by, is the pair's own sense.

What you've built.
过去 (guòqù) and 以前 both look back. 以前 is before some point; 过去 (guòqù) is the past at large, set against now, xiànzài.
As a verb, 过去 (guòqù) is going over there, away from the speaker.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — 寸, then the walking piece last.
过 (guò).
Two pieces you own, in the order you know from 这.
寸, three strokes, on the upper right: the bar, the vertical that hooks to the left at its foot, then the dot. It is the right half of 对 and of 时.
辶, three strokes, last: the dot at the upper left; the short stroke that turns down, turns again and falls to the left in one movement; then the long base, sweeping out to the right under 寸.
3 + 3 = six. The walking piece goes in last, as in 这 and 进, though it sits under everything.

Lesson 3 of 7.
你去过中国吗 (nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma) — 过 (guò) for what you have done.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The character you have written turns up again, light, straight after a verb.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 过去, aloud — guòqù]
[your turn — recall: say 我去了中国 — wǒ qù le Zhōngguó, I went to China]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 我没去 — wǒ méi qù, I didn't go]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens: 了 says it happened; 过 says it has happened at least once.
过 (guò) straight after a verb says this has happened at least once, at some time. Here it is said light: guo.
你去过中国吗 nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma — Have you ever been to China? 我去过 wǒ qù guo — I have.
The negative keeps 过 (guò) and puts 没 in front; 不 has no place here, because the question is about what has happened:
我没去过 wǒ méi qù guo — I've not been.
Set it beside 了. 我去了中国 reports one trip; 我去过中国 says China is somewhere you have been, at some point in your life:
我去了中国 wǒ qù le Zhōngguó — I went to China. 我去过中国 wǒ qù guo Zhōngguó — I've been to China.

Lesson 4 of 7.
后来 (hòulái) — afterwards; later.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: read the last lesson's question, then the day you wrote 后 for.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 你去过中国吗, aloud — nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma]
[your turn — recall: say 后天 — hòutiān, the day after tomorrow — R2, a few chapters back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 后来.
后来 — hòulái — "afterwards; later (in the past)".
后来我们去了大学 hòulái wǒmen qù le dàxué — Afterwards we went to the university.

The word, taken apart.
后来 (hòulái) is built from 后 'after' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, huwX and loj (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): what came after.

What you've built.
后来 (hòulái) looks back: it follows something that already happened and says what came next. For what will come later, Mandarin says yǐhòu: yí ge xiǎoshí yǐhòu.
One more way to set a time in the past: de shíhou after a clause turns it into a when, the shíhou of xiǎoshíhou:
wǒ xiǎo de shíhou, wǒ jiā zài Zhōngguó, hòulái wǒmen lái zhèr le — When I was small my family lived in China; later we came here.

Lesson 5 of 7.
ránhòu — then; after that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: last lesson's word, then a word that ends the same way.
[your turn — recall: say 后来 — hòulái, afterwards — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say yǐhòu, after — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: ránhòu.
ránhòu — "then; after that".
wǒ xiān chī fàn, ránhòu kàn shū — First I eat, then I read.
Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
ránhòu is built from rán 'so; thus' and 后 'after'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, nyen and huwX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the Guangyun glosses nyen as a particle, 'thus', and 'then' for the pair is assumed here, not quoted.

What you've built.
ránhòu joins two steps in order, the second after the first, now or in the past: wǒ xiān chī fàn, ránhòu kàn shū.
后来 (hòulái) only looks back at what happened next; ránhòu can plan tomorrow too: míngtiān wǒ qù dàxué, ránhòu qù kàn shū.

You'll want to know: cónglái.
cónglái — "ever"; before 没, "never once".
wǒ cónglái méi qù guo — I've never once been there.
Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
cónglái is built from cóng 'from' and 来 'to come': from then until now. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, dzjowng and loj (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), dzjowng glossed 'to go to; to follow'. 'Ever' is the pair's own sense, not the gloss's.

What you've built.
cónglái stands in front of 没 … 过 (guò) and makes it stronger: 我没去过, I've not been; wǒ cónglái méi qù guo, not once.
It needs 没 after it to say never; on its own it is rare.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

The exchange.
A: 你去过中国吗 nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma — Have you ever been to China? B: 去过 qù guo — I have. B: 过去我家在中国 guòqù wǒ jiā zài Zhōngguó — My family used to live there. A: 后来呢 hòulái ne — And afterwards? B: 后来我们来这儿了 hòulái wǒmen lái zhèr le — Afterwards we came here. B: 你呢 你去过吗 nǐ ne, nǐ qù guo ma — And you? Have you been? A: 我没去过 wǒ méi qù guo — I've not been. B: 你开过车吗 nǐ kāi guo chē ma — Have you ever driven a car? A: wǒ cónglái méi kāi guo — Never once. B: wǒ xiān qù Zhōngguó, ránhòu qù Běijīng — I'll go to China first, then to Beijing.
去过 answers 你去过中国吗 (nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma) on its own: the verb and 过 (guò), nothing else. The no is 没去过, with 过 (guò) kept.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]

What you've built — ever, and what came next.
过 (guò) after a verb asks about a whole life; 后来 (hòulái) and ránhòu put events in order:
你去过中国吗 nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma — have you ever been to China?
我没去过 wǒ méi qù guo — I've not been.
后来我们来这儿了 hòulái wǒmen lái zhèr le — afterwards we came here.
wǒ xiān chī fàn, ránhòu kàn shū — first I eat, then I read.
Listen. Nǐ qù guo Běijīng ma?
