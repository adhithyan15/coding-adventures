## You'll want to know: der Skilift
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C512-WINTERSPORT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Skilift** — "ski lift".

> Am Skilift mussten wir lange warten.
>
> *We had to wait a long time at the ski lift.*

Plural: *die Skilifte*. *Ski* sounds like *Schi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Skilift* is built on *Ski*, borrowed from Norwegian *ski* "a strip of wood", and *Lift*, borrowed from English *lift*; EtymDB lists both German words without a source.
