## You'll want to know: xióngmāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C223-ZOO223-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xióngmāo* — "a panda; giant panda".

> *xióngmāo hěn kě'ài* — Pandas are so cute.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xióngmāo* is built from *xióng* 'bear' and *māo* 'cat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuwng* and *mjew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the first as a beast like a pig and the second as the animal that catches mice; the level tone of māo is not the regular outcome of that form.
