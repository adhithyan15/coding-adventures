## You'll want to know: निश्चित
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C382-SHAKYA382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चित** — *niścit* — "certain; fixed".

> लग्नाची तारीख अजून निश्चित नाही.
>
> *lagnācī tārīkh ajūn niścit nāhī.*
>
> The wedding date is not fixed yet.

निश्चित करणे, to fix; निश्चितपणे, certainly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चित** (*niścit*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *niścita*, "ascertained, settled" (Monier-Williams).
