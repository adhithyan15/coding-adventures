Lesson 1 of 5.
le maire — a mayor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a motorbike, then the French for a platform.

You'll want to know: le maire.
le maire — "a mayor".

The word, taken apart.
maire from Old French maire from Latin maior, "greater" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited: the noun behind mairie.

You'll want to know: les élections.
les élections — "elections".

The word, taken apart.
élections is the plural of the word for "an election", from Latin electio, "a choosing", from eligere, "to pick out" (Lewis & Short) — a learned borrowing.
