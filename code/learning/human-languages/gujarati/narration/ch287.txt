Gujarati, chapter 287: Clues at the Door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભાળ (bhāḷ) — a trace; whereabouts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for very, then the Gujarati for to hide (something).

You'll want to know: ભાળ.
ભાળ — bhāḷ — "a trace; whereabouts".
પોલીસ ચોરની ભાળ કાઢતી હતી. polīs cornī bhāḷ kāḍhtī hatī. The police were trying to trace the thief.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભાળ (bhāḷ) comes from bhāla, "observation", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists Gujarati bhāḷ, "search, investigation" (CDIAL 9473). It is the trail that leads to someone: ભાળ કાઢવી, to trace a person.

Lesson 2 of 5.
પગલું (paglũ) — a footstep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hide (something), then the Gujarati for a trace.

You'll want to know: પગલું.
પગલું — paglũ — "a footstep".
હવે બધું આ પગલાં પર આધાર રાખે છે. have badhũ ā paglā̃ par ādhār rākhe che. Now everything rests on these footprints.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પગલું (paglũ) is built on પગ, a foot, which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit padga- (CDIAL 7766), with the ending -lũ. It is a step, and the sound or the print a step leaves: કોઈનાં પગલાં, someone's footsteps.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ખખડાવવું (khakhḍāvvũ) — to knock (at a door).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a trace, then the Gujarati for a footstep.

You'll want to know: ખખડાવવું.
ખખડાવવું — khakhḍāvvũ — "to knock (at a door)".
અડધી રાત્રે કોઈએ બારણું ખખડાવ્યું. aḍdhī rātre koīe bārṇũ khakhḍāvyũ. In the middle of the night someone knocked at the door.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બારણું ખખડાવ્યું, teṇe bārṇũ khakhḍāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ખખડાવવું (khakhḍāvvũ) is the causative of khakhaḍvũ, to rattle, an imitative word: CDIAL sets Gujarati khaṛkhaṛvũ, "to rattle", under Sanskrit khaṭakhaṭāyatē, "crackles" (CDIAL 3771), and this verb probably belongs with it. To knock is to make the door rattle.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સંતાવું (santāvũ) — to hide oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a footstep, then the Gujarati for to knock.

You'll want to know: સંતાવું.
સંતાવું — santāvũ — "to hide oneself".
ચોર દાદર નીચે સંતાઈ ગયો. cor dādar nīce santāī gayo. The thief hid under the stairs.
Past (he …): તે ચોર સંતાઈ ગયો, te cor santāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
goes back to Sanskrit sáṁtāyatē, "is stretched out, is covered", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to conceal oneself" (CDIAL 12887): to be covered over, out of sight. A person hides himself with સંતાવું (santāvũ): સંતાઈ ગયો; a thing is hidden with છુપાવવું.

Lesson 5 of 5.
તોડવું (toḍvũ) — to break (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to knock, then the Gujarati for to hide oneself.

You'll want to know: તોડવું.
તોડવું — toḍvũ — "to break (something)".
ચોરે તાળું તોડ્યું. core tāḷũ toḍyũ. The thief broke the lock.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે તાળું તોડ્યું, teṇe tāḷũ toḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
તોડવું (toḍvũ) goes back to Sanskrit trōṭáyati, "breaks, tears", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 6079). It is the doing partner of તૂટવું, to get broken: તાળું તૂટ્યું says that it broke, ચોરે તાળું તોડ્યું says who broke it.
