## You'll want to know: курьер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C457-DOSTAVKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**курьер** (*kuryér*) — "a courier".

> Курьер привёз посылку утром.
>
> *Kuryér privyóz posýlku útrom.*
>
> The courier brought the parcel in the morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*курьер* is usually traced to French *courrier* 'a messenger', which EtymDB takes from Italian *corriere*, a word usually linked with Latin *currere* 'to run' (Lewis & Short).
