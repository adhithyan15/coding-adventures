## You'll want to know: కాగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C396-VIRUDDHAM396-03]; assesses=[] -->

**కాగా** — *kāgā* — "whereas; while".

> అన్న వైద్యుడు కాగా, తమ్ముడు రైతు.
>
> *anna vaidyuḍu kāgā, tammuḍu raitu.*
>
> Whereas the elder brother is a doctor, the younger is a farmer.

It sets two facts side by side; it is a form of అవు, to be.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కాగా** (*kāgā*) is native Dravidian, a form of *avu*, "to be, to become" (DEDR 333), whose stem takes the shape *kā-* in some forms.
