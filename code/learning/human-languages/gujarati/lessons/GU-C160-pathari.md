## You'll want to know: પથારી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C160-BED160-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પથારી** — *pathārī* — "bedding, bed".

> **મારી પથારી તૈયાર છે.** — *mārī pathārī taiyār che* — "My bed is ready."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પથારી** (*pathārī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *prastāra*, "spreading out; bed of straw", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "bedding" (CDIAL 8864).
