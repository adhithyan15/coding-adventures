# طفح جلدي (ṭafḥ jildī) — a rash

## You'll want to know: طفح جلدي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C508-JILD508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**طفح جلدي** — *ṭafḥ jildī* — "a rash".

> ظهر طفح جلدي على يد الطفل.
>
> *ẓahara ṭafḥ jildī ʿalā yad aṭ-ṭifl.*
>
> A rash appeared on the child's hand.

Often shortened to طفح.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طفح جلدي** (*ṭafḥ jildī*) is built on two native Arabic roots: *ṭafḥ* from *ṭ-f-ḥ*, "to fill to the brim, to overflow" (Lane), and *jildī*, "of the skin", from *jild*, skin, which you know. A rash is the skin brimming over.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
