## You'll want to know: अरित्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C416-NAVIKA416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अरित्रम्** — *aritram* — "an oar".

> नाविकस्य हस्ते अरित्रम् अस्ति।
>
> *nāvikasya haste aritram asti.*
>
> The boatman has an oar in his hand.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अरित्रम्** (*aritram*) is built on the root √ṛ, "to move", with the ending *-itra*: MW gives *aritra* as propelling and as an oar (MW).
