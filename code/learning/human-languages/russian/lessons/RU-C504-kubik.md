## You'll want to know: кубик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C504-BOARDGAMES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**кубик** (*kúbik*) — "a die; a small cube".

> Он бросил кубик, и выпала шестёрка.
>
> *On brósil kúbik, i výpala shestyórka.*
>
> He threw the die and got a six.

Masculine. A diminutive of *куб* — a cube. *Кубики* are also a child's building blocks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*кубик* is a diminutive of *куб* 'cube', which EtymDB traces through German *Kubus* and Latin *cubus* to Greek *kýbos* 'a cube'; EtymDB has no entry for *кубик*.
