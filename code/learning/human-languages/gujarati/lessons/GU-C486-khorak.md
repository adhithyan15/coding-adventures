## You'll want to know: ખોરાક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C486-AAROGYA486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોરાક** — *khorāk* — "food; diet".

> તાજો ખોરાક શરીર માટે સારો છે.
>
> *tājo khorāk śarīr māṭe sāro che.*
>
> Fresh food is good for the body.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોરાક** (*khorāk*) is usually traced to Persian *xurāk*, "food", built on *xurdan*, "to eat".
