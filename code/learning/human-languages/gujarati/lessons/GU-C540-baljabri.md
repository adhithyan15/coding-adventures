## You'll want to know: બળજબરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C540-BALJABRI540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બળજબરી** — *baḷjabrī* — "coercion".

> જો તમે બળજબરી કરશો તો તે કામ નહીં કરે.
>
> *jo tame baḷjabrī karśo to te kām nahī̃ kare.*
>
> If you use force, he won't do the work.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બળજબરી** (*baḷjabrī*) is built from *baḷ*, "strength", from Sanskrit *bala* (Monier-Williams), and *jabrī*, usually traced to Arabic *jabr*, "compulsion", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
