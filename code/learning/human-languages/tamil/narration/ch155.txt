Tamil, chapter 155: To push, To pull, To put, To tie, To wash.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தள்ளு (taḷḷu) — to push.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to count, then the Tamil for to shout.

You'll want to know: தள்ளு.
தள்ளு — taḷḷu — "to push".

The word, taken apart.
தள்ளு (taḷḷu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3135 lists Malayalam taḷḷuka and Kannada taḷḷu.
