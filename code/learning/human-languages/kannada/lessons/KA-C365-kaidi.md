## You'll want to know: ಕೈದಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C365-HODEDATA365-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈದಿ** — *kaidi* — "a prisoner".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ಒಬ್ಬ ಕೈದಿ ಸೆರೆಮನೆಯಿಂದ ತಪ್ಪಿಸಿಕೊಂಡನು.
>
> *rātri obba kaidi seremaneyinda tappisikoṇḍanu.*
>
> At night a prisoner escaped from the prison.

Plural: ಕೈದಿಗಳು, *kaidigaḷu*.

A prisoner is held in a ಸೆರೆಮನೆ, a prison; ಕೈದಿ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ is his number.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈದಿ** (*kaidi*) is a loan from Arabic *qaidī*, "a captive, a prisoner", which came through Persian and Hindustani.
