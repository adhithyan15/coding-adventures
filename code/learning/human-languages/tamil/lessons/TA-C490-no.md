## You'll want to know: நோ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C490-NIMIR490-05]; assesses=[] -->

**நோ** — *nō* — "to ache; to hurt".

> நீண்ட நேரம் நின்றதால் கால் நொந்தது.
>
> *nīṇṭa nēram niṉṟatāl kāl nontatu.*
>
> My legs ached from standing a long time.

Past (it …): நொந்தது, *nontatu*.

More literary than வலி, the everyday verb. Also of the heart: மனம் நொந்தது, I was hurt. The past shortens the vowel: நொந்தது.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நோ** (*nō*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3793); நோய் "disease" comes from the same root.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
