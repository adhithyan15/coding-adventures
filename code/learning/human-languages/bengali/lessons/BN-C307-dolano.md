# দোলানো (dolāno) — to swing; to sway

## You'll want to know: দোলানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C307-DHAK307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**দোলানো** — *dolāno* — "to swing; to sway".

> আরতির সময় পুজারি ধুনুচি দোলালেন।
>
> *ārôtir sômôy pujāri dhunuchi dolālen.*
>
> During the evening worship the priest swung the incense burner.

Past (he/she …): তিনি প্রদীপটা দোলালেন, *tini prôdīpṭā dolālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দোলানো** (*dolāno*) is filed in CDIAL 6453 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *dulati*, 'swings', which lists Bengali *dulā*; দোলানো is the -ānō verb, to make something swing.
