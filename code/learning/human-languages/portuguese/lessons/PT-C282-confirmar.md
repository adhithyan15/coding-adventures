## You'll want to know: confirmar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C282-AFIRMAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**confirmar** — "to confirm".

> A escola confirmou a data da festa.
>
> *The school confirmed the date of the party.*

Also to confirm a booking: *confirmar a reserva*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confirmar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnfirmāre* "to strengthen, to make sure", built on *firmus* "firm" (Lewis & Short).
