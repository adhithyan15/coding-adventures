# दादा (dādā) — grandfather, the father's father

## You'll want to know: दादा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C116-GRAND-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दादा** (*dādā*) — "grandfather", your FATHER's father.

English has one word for both grandfathers; Hindi has two, and says which side of the family
you mean. **मेरे दादा** — "my grandfather", with the respectful plural **मेरे**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A grandfather, on your father's side.
