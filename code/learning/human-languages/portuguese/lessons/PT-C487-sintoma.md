## You'll want to know: o sintoma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C487-CONSULTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o sintoma** — "symptom".

> A febre e a tosse são sintomas da gripe.
>
> *Fever and a cough are symptoms of flu.*

Masculine, though it ends in *-a*: *o sintoma*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sintoma* goes back through Latin to Greek *sýmptōma* "a happening, a chance event, a symptom" (EtymDB).
