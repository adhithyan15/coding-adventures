## You'll want to know: کاهش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C373-AFZAYESH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کاهش** (*kâhesh*) — "a decrease, a reduction".

> کاهش باران برای کشاورزها مشکل بزرگی است.
>
> *kâhesh-e bârân barâye keshâvarzhâ moshkel-e bozorgi ast.*
>
> Less rain is a big problem for farmers.

From *kâh-*, the present stem of *kâstan* "to lessen". *Kâhesh yâftan* — to decrease.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کاهش** is native Persian, built on *kâh-*, the present stem of *kâstan* "to lessen, to reduce", with the noun suffix *-esh*.
