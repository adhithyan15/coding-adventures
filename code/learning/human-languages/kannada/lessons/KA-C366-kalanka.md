# ಕಳಂಕ (kaḷaṅka) — a stain; a disgrace

## You'll want to know: ಕಳಂಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C366-GHANATE366-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಳಂಕ** — *kaḷaṅka* — "a stain; a disgrace".

> ಆ ಮೋಸ ಅವರ ಹೆಸರಿಗೆ ಕಳಂಕ ತಂದಿತು.
>
> *ā mōsa avara hesarige kaḷaṅka tanditu.*
>
> That fraud brought a stain on his name.

A stain on a name or a family: ಕಳಂಕ ತರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಳಂಕ** (*kaḷaṅka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kalaṅka*, "a stain, a spot" (CDIAL 2911), and also "defamation" (Monier-Williams).
