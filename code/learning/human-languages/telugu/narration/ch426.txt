Telugu, chapter 426: Before You Act.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సంకోచించు (saṅkōciñcu) — to hold back; to hesitate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to finalise, then the Telugu for to reconsider.

You'll want to know: సంకోచించు.
సంకోచించు — saṅkōciñcu — "to hold back; to hesitate".
ఆమె నిజం చెప్పడానికి సంకోచించింది. āme nijaṁ ceppaḍāniki saṅkōciñcindi. She hesitated to tell the truth.
Past (I …): సంకోచించాను, saṅkōciñcānu.
What you hesitate over takes -డానికి. Close to వెనకాడు, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
సంకోచించు (saṅkōciñcu) is built on Sanskrit saṁkoca, "contraction, shrinking; shyness" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

You'll want to know: సిద్ధపడు.
సిద్ధపడు — siddhapaḍu — "to be willing; to get ready".
అతను ఎంత దూరమైనా వెళ్ళడానికి సిద్ధపడ్డాడు. atanu enta dūramainā veḷḷaḍāniki siddhapaḍḍāḍu. He was ready to go however far it took.

సిద్ధం, ready, + పడు.

The word, taken apart.
సిద్ధపడు (siddhapaḍu) is built from Sanskrit siddha, "accomplished; prepared" (CDIAL 13401), the word in సిద్ధం, and the native verb paḍu (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hold back, then the Telugu for to be willing.

You'll want to know: ఆశ్రయించు.
ఆశ్రయించు — āśrayiñcu — "to turn to; to resort to".
న్యాయం కోసం వాళ్ళు కోర్టును ఆశ్రయించారు. nyāyaṁ kōsaṁ vāḷḷu kōrṭunu āśrayiñcāru. They turned to the court for justice.
Past (I …): ఆశ్రయించాను, āśrayiñcānu.
For turning to someone for help or shelter.

The word, taken apart.
ఆశ్రయించు (āśrayiñcu) is built on Sanskrit āśraya, "resting place, refuge" (CDIAL 1466), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; విమానాశ్రయం, an airport, ends in the same word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఉద్దేశపూర్వకంగా (uddēśapūrvakaṅgā) — deliberately; on purpose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be willing, then the Telugu for to turn to.

You'll want to know: ఉద్దేశపూర్వకంగా.
ఉద్దేశపూర్వకంగా — uddēśapūrvakaṅgā — "deliberately; on purpose".
అతను ఉద్దేశపూర్వకంగా ఆ విషయం దాచాడు. atanu uddēśapūrvakaṅgā ā viṣayaṁ dācāḍu. He hid the matter on purpose.
ఉద్దేశం, an intention, + పూర్వకంగా, with... in front.

The word, taken apart.
ఉద్దేశపూర్వకంగా (uddēśapūrvakaṅgā) is built from Sanskrit uddeśa, "aim, direction", and pūrvaka, "earlier, preceded by" (Monier-Williams), with the adverb ending -గా: with the aim already in front.
