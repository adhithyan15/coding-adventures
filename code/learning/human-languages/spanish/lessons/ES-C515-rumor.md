## You'll want to know: el rumor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C515-RUMOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el rumor** — "the rumour".

> Corre el rumor de que van a cerrar la fábrica.
>
> *There is a rumour going round that they are going to close the factory.*

*Corre el rumor* — word goes round. It also means a soft noise: *el rumor del mar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rumor* is a learned borrowing from Latin *rūmor* 'common talk, a noise' (EtymDB). The Spanish word kept both senses, the gossip and the murmur.
