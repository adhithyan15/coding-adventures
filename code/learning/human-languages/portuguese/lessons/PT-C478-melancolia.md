## You'll want to know: a melancolia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C478-SOLIDAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a melancolia** — "melancholy".

> Os dias de chuva enchem-me de melancolia.
>
> *Rainy days fill me with melancholy.*

The adjective is *melancólico*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*melancolia* goes back, through Old Portuguese *melanconia* (EtymDB), to Latin *melancholia* (Lewis & Short), from Greek *melankholía* "black bile", which old medicine blamed for sadness.
