## You'll want to know: أهدى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C336-HADIYYA336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أهدى** — *ahdā* — "to gift; to dedicate".

> أهدت البنت وردة إلى أمها.
>
> *ahdat al-bint wardatan ilā ummihā.*
>
> The girl gave her mother a rose.

Present: يهدي, *yuhdī*.

The taught هدية, a gift, is its noun. Of a book: أهدى الكتاب إلى أمه, he dedicated it to his mother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أهدى** (*ahdā*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-d-y* (Lane), "to guide", the root of the taught *hadiyya*, a gift. It is Form IV, "to send a gift".
