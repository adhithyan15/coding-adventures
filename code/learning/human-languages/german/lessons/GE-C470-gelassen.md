## You'll want to know: gelassen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C470-GEDULD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gelassen** — "calm, composed".

> Trotz des Stresses blieb mein Chef ganz gelassen.
>
> *Despite the stress, my boss stayed quite calm.*

Also the past participle of *lassen*: *Ich habe den Schirm im Bus gelassen.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gelassen* is the old past participle of *lassen* "to let, to let go", which is inherited from Old High German *lāzan*, from Proto-Germanic *lētaną* (EtymDB): someone who has let things go.
