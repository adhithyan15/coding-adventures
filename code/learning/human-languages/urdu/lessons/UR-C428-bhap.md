## You'll want to know: بھاپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C428-BOOND428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بھاپ** — *bhāp* — "steam".

> گرم چائے سے بھاپ نکل رہی تھی۔
>
> *garm chāe se bhāp nikal rahī thī.*
>
> Steam was rising from the hot tea.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھاپ** (*bhāp*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *bāph* and *bhāp*, "steam", under Sanskrit *bāṣpá*, "tears, vapour" (CDIAL 9223).
