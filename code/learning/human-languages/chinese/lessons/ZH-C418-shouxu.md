## You'll want to know: shǒuxù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C418-CHECKIN418-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒuxù* — "formalities; procedure".

> *chūguó de shǒuxù hěn máfan* — The formalities for going abroad are a real bother.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒuxù* is built from 手 'a hand' and *xù* 'to carry on', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuwX* and *zjowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to continue, to join'; how the pair came to mean formalities is not settled.
