## You'll want to know: il tovagliolo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C533-TAVOLA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il tovagliolo** — "napkin".

> Mi passi un tovagliolo, per favore?
>
> *Can you pass me a napkin, please?*

Built on *tovaglia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tovagliolo* is a little form of *tovaglia* "tablecloth", which comes from Frankish *thwahillja* "a cloth" (EtymDB).
