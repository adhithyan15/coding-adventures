[PAUSE 3s] An occasion? (**निमित्तम्**, *nimittam*.) An influence? (**प्रभावः**, *prabhāvaḥ*.) A lack? (**अभावः**, *abhāvaḥ*.) Comes about? (**उत्पद्यते**, *utpadyate*.) Gives rise to? (**जनयति**, *janayati*.) A purpose? (**प्रयोजनम्**, *prayojanam*.) An aim? (**उद्देश्यम्**, *uddeśyam*.) A use? (**उपयोगः**, *upayogaḥ*.) Urges on? (**प्रेरयति**, *prerayati*.) Has something done? (**कारयति**, *kārayati*.) A basis? (**आधारः**, *ādhāraḥ*.) A line of reasoning? (**युक्तिः**, *yuktiḥ*.) That is to say? (**अर्थात्**, *arthāt*.) As a result? (**फलतः**, *phalataḥ*.) Argues for? (**उपपादयति**, *upapādayati*.) A debate? (**वादः**, *vādaḥ*.) Reasoning? (**तर्कः**, *tarkaḥ*.) A speaker? (**वक्ता**, *vaktā*.) A listener? (**श्रोता**, *śrotā*.) Sets out (a case)? (**प्रतिपादयति**, *pratipādayati*.) The opposing side? (**प्रतिपक्षः**, *pratipakṣaḥ*.) Opposition? (**विरोधः**, *virodhaḥ*.) Supports (a view)? (**समर्थयति**, *samarthayati*.) Consents? (**अनुमन्यते**, *anumanyate*.) Is it not so? (**ननु**, *nanu*.) Conviction? (**प्रत्ययः**, *pratyayaḥ*.) Testimony? (**साक्ष्यम्**, *sākṣyam*.) A fact? (**तथ्यम्**, *tathyam*.) Proves? (**साधयति**, *sādhayati*.) Certainty? (**निश्चयः**, *niścayaḥ*.) An objection? (**आक्षेपः**, *ākṣepaḥ*.) Refutes? (**खण्डयति**, *khaṇḍayati*.) Rejects (a claim)? (**निरस्यति**, *nirasyati*.) A doubt? (**संशयः**, *saṃśayaḥ*.) In fact? (**वस्तुतः**, *vastutaḥ*.) An example? (**उदाहरणम्**, *udāharaṇam*.) An illustration (in an argument)? (**दृष्टान्तः**, *dṛṣṭāntaḥ*.) In this way? (**इत्थम्**, *ittham*.) Makes (someone) understand? (**बोधयति**, *bodhayati*.) What someone means? (**अभिप्रायः**, *abhiprāyaḥ*.)
