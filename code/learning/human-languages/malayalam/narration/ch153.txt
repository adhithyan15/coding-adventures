Malayalam, chapter 153: Dove, Butterfly, Ant, Mosquito, Shoulder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പ്രാവ് (prāvŭ) — a dove, a pigeon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a peacock, then the Malayalam for a parrot.
[pause 1 second]
Which "day" ending do sāyāhnaṁ and madhyāhnaṁ share? (-ahna.)
