Kannada, chapter 381: Thinking It Through.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು (parigaṇisu) — to consider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an inducement, then the Kannada for to attract.

You'll want to know: ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು.
ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು — parigaṇisu — "to consider".
ನಾನು ನಿನ್ನ ಸಲಹೆಯನ್ನು ಪರಿಗಣಿಸಿದೆ. nānu ninna salaheyannu parigaṇiside. I considered your suggestion.
Past (I …): ಪರಿಗಣಿಸಿದೆ, parigaṇiside.
Also to count as: ಇದನ್ನು ತಪ್ಪು ಎಂದು ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು (parigaṇisu), to count this as a mistake.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಿಗಣಿಸು (parigaṇisu) is built on Sanskrit pari-gaṇ, "to count over, to reckon up completely" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to attract, then the Kannada for to consider.

You'll want to know: ಭಾವಿಸು.
ಭಾವಿಸು — bhāvisu — "to suppose; to assume".
ನೀನು ಬರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ ಅಂತ ನಾನು ಭಾವಿಸಿದೆ. nīnu baruvudilla anta nānu bhāviside. I assumed you would not come.
Past (I …): ಭಾವಿಸಿದೆ, bhāviside.
What is supposed comes before ಅಂತ or ಎಂದು.

The word, taken apart.
ಭಾವಿಸು (bhāvisu) is built on Sanskrit bhāva, "being, state, disposition" (CDIAL 9475), from bhū, "to be", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to consider, then the Kannada for to suppose.

You'll want to know: ವಿವೇಕ.
ವಿವೇಕ — vivēka — "good sense; discernment".
ಕಷ್ಟದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವಳು ವಿವೇಕದಿಂದ ನಡೆದುಕೊಂಡಳು. kaṣṭada samayadalli avaḷu vivēkadinda naḍedukoṇḍaḷu. In hard times she acted with good sense.
ವಿವೇಕಿ, a sensible person.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿವೇಕ (vivēka) is borrowed from Sanskrit viveka, "discrimination, the power to tell things apart" (Monier-Williams).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಮನನ ಮಾಡು.
ಮನನ ಮಾಡು — manana māḍu — "to reflect on".
ಗುರುಗಳ ಮಾತನ್ನು ನಾನು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮನನ ಮಾಡಿದೆ. gurugaḷa mātannu nānu maneyalli manana māḍide. At home I reflected on my teacher's words.
Past (I …): ಮನನ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, manana māḍide.
What you reflect on takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನನ ಮಾಡು (manana māḍu) is built from Sanskrit manana, "thinking, reflection" (Monier-Williams), from man, "to think", with māḍu, "to do".
