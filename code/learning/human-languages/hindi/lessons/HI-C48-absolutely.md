## You'll want to know: बिलकुल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C48-REPLY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बिलकुल** (*bilkul*) — "absolutely".

Arabic *bi-l-kull*, 'with the whole' — a preposition, the definite article *al-*, and *kull*, 'all', fused into one Hindi word. That same *al-* is sitting, equally hidden, inside अलविदा.

It answers a yes-or-no question with more weight than a bare yes. Put *nahīṁ* after it and it does exactly the same for no, which makes it the most emphatic thing on this page in either direction.
