## You'll want to know: কোষাগার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C348-RAJOSHO348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**কোষাগার** — *koshāgār* — "a treasury".

> সরকারের কোষাগার এখন খালি।
>
> *sôrkārer koshāgār ekhôn khāli.*
>
> The government's treasury is empty now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কোষাগার** (*koshāgār*) is Sanskrit *kośāgāra*, *kośa* 'a store of wealth' with *āgāra* 'a house': a treasure-house (Monier-Williams).
