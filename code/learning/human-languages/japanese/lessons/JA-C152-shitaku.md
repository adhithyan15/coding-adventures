## You'll want to know: したく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C152-READY152-01]; assesses=[] -->

**したく** — *shitaku* — "preparations; getting things ready".

> **あさの したくは かんたんです。** — *asa no shitaku wa kantan desu* — "Getting ready in the morning is simple."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**したく** (*shitaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "branch, support" (Middle Chinese *tsye*) and *taku*, "degrees, consider" (*dak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
