## You'll want to know: necessitās, necessitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C392-CAVEO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**necessitās, necessitātis** — "necessity, need; compulsion".

> Necessitās mē coēgit.
>
> *Necessity forced me.*

*necessitās, necessitātis* f., the noun of *necesse*, needful. *Necessitās nōn habet lēgem*, necessity knows no law. English *necessity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*necessitās* is built on *necesse* "needful" (Lewis & Short), which EtymDB takes as *ne-* "not" with *cessus*, from *cēdō* "to give way": what does not give way. English *necessity* comes from it.
