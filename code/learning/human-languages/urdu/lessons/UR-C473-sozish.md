## You'll want to know: سوزش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C473-ACHE473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سوزش** — *sozish* — "an inflammation".

> گلے میں سوزش ہے۔
>
> *gale meṅ sozish hai.*
>
> There is an inflammation in the throat.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سوزش** (*sozish*) is usually traced to Persian *sōzish*, "burning".
