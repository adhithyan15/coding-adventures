## You'll want to know: bàibai
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C76-GOODBYE76-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bàibai* — "bye-bye".

> *bàibai míngtiān jiàn* — Bye, see you tomorrow!

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàibai* is borrowed from English *bye-bye*, written with a character read bài, 'to bow', for the sound.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
