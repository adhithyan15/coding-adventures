## You'll want to know: मूल्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C366-MULYA366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मूल्य** — *mūlya* — "a value".

> आईवडिलांनी आम्हाला चांगली मूल्यं शिकवली.
>
> *āīvḍilānnī āmhālā cāṅglī mūlyaṁ śikavlī.*
>
> Our parents taught us good values.

Neuter. Plural: मूल्यं, *mūlyaṁ*.

Also price, in formal use: वस्तूचं मूल्य.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूल्य** (*mūlya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mūlya*, "original value, price" (CDIAL 10257).
