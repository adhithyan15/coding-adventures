Lesson 1 of 5.
должность (dólzhnost) — a post, a position.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the truth, then the Russian for a lie.

You'll want to know: должность.
должность (dólzhnost) — "a post, a position".

The word, taken apart.
должность (dólzhnost) comes from долг, "a debt, a duty", and first meant "an obligation" (Vasmer). Vasmer relates долг to Gothic dulgs, "a debt", and Old Irish dligid, "a duty".

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a lie, then the Russian for a post.

You'll want to know: зарплата.
зарплата (zarpláta) — "a salary".

The word, taken apart.
зарплата (zarpláta) is заработная плата, "earned pay", cut short. Vasmer explains плата, "pay", from плат, "a cloth", since pieces of cloth once served as money.

Lesson 3 of 5.
налог (nalóg) — a tax.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a post, then the Russian for a salary.

You'll want to know: налог.
налог (nalóg) — "a tax".

The word, taken apart.
налог (nalóg) is на-, "on", with -лог, from the root of лежать, "to lie", and положить, "to lay": a tax is what is laid on you. Vasmer derives лог from лежать, Proto-Slavic ležati, kin to Greek léchos, "a bed".

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: счёт.
счёт (schyot) — "a bill; an account".

The word, taken apart.
счёт (schyot) is с- with чёт, "a count", from считать, "to count", on the root of читать, "to read", which once meant "to count".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a tax, then the Russian for a bill; an account.

You'll want to know: наличные.
наличные (nalíchnyye) — "cash".

The word, taken apart.
наличные (nalíchnyye) is short for наличные (nalíchnyye) деньги, "money at hand", from налицо, "present, in view", на, "on", with лицо, "face".
