## You'll want to know: बरसणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C221-RAIN221-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बरसणो** — *barasṇo* — "to rain; to pour down".

> सावण मांय मेह घणो बरसै है।
>
> *sāvaṇ mā̃y meh ghaṇo barsai hai.*
>
> In the monsoon month it rains hard.

Past (it …): वो मेह बरस्यो, *vo meh barsyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बरसणो** (*barasṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *varṣati*, "rains"; CDIAL lists Hindi *barasnā*, "to rain". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11394). **बरसात**, rain, belongs to the same family.
