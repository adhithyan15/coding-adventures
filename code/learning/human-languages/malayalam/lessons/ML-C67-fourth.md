## You'll want to know: നാലാം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C67-ORDINAL-06]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C67-ORDINAL-02, ML-CONCEPT-C07-NUMBERS-1-5-01] -->

**നാലാം** (*nālāṁ*) — "**fourth**".

| cardinal | ordinal |
|---|---|
| **നാല്** (*nālŭ*) | **നാലാം** (*nālāṁ*) |

Written, the change is smaller than it sounds. **നാല്** ends in ല with the
virama **്** under it, the sign that says "no vowel here". The ordinal replaces
that sign with the **ാ** of *ā* and adds the **ം**: നാല് becomes നാലാം.

**നാലാം പൂവ്** (*nālāṁ pūvŭ*), the fourth flower.
