Arabic, chapter 185: Dolphin, Parrot, Ant, Bull, Insect.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
دلفين (dulfīn) — a dolphin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for decorations, then the Arabic for a film.

You'll want to know: دلفين.
دلفين — dulfīn — "a dolphin".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dulfīn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dulfīn, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dulfīn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for decorations, then the Arabic for a film, then say dulfīn again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does دلفين (dulfīn) mean? ("A dolphin".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Expensive? (مكلف, mukallif.) Free? (مجاني, majjānī.) Raw? (نيء, nayʾ.) Cooked? (مطبوخ, maṭbūkh.) Fried? (مقلي, maqlī.) Official? (رسمي, rasmī.) Private? (خاص, khāṣṣ.) In a hurry? (مستعجل, mustaʿjil.) Clear? (واضح, wāḍiḥ.) Hard-working? (مجتهد, mujtahid.) Grey? (رمادي, ramādī.) Golden? (ذهبي, dhahabī.) Thick? (سميك, samīk.) Humid? (رطب, raṭb.) Rainy? (ممطر, mumṭir.) Injured? (مصاب, muṣāb.) Rural? (ريفي, rīfī.) Traditional? (تقليدي, taqlīdī.) Foreign? (أجنبي, ajnabī.) Flat? (مسطح, musaṭṭaḥ.)
