## You'll want to know: redémarrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C399-PANNE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**redémarrer** — "to restart, to reboot".

> Si ça ne marche pas, il faut redémarrer l'ordinateur.
>
> *If it does not work, you have to restart the computer.*

*Re-* means again: to start again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*redémarrer* is built from *re-* and *démarrer* "to start", first a sailing word for casting off a moored boat, from *dé-* and *amarrer* "to moor", a word borrowed from Dutch (EtymDB).
