## You'll want to know: মেসো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C482-JETHA482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**মেসো** — *meso* — "a maternal aunt's husband".

> মেসো আমাকে একটা নতুন সাইকেল কিনে দিলেন।
>
> *meso āmāke ekṭā nôtun sāikel kine dilen.*
>
> My aunt's husband bought me a new bicycle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেসো** (*meso*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a mother's sister's husband, under Sanskrit *mātuḥṣvasṛ*, "mother's sister" (CDIAL 10001); মাসি (taught) is the aunt herself.
