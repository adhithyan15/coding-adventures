## You'll want to know: દોસ્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C264-FRIENDS264-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દોસ્ત** — *dost* — "pal, close friend".

> **શું તે તમારો દોસ્ત છે?** — *śũ te tamāro dost che?* — "Is he your pal?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દોસ્ત** (*dost*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *dost*, "friend". CDIAL has no entry for it.
