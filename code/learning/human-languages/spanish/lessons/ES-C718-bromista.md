## You'll want to know: bromista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C718-SONRISA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bromista** — "joker; fond of jokes".

> Mi tío es muy bromista.
>
> *My uncle loves a joke.*

Same form for men and women: *es una bromista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bromista* is built on *broma* "joke" with the ending *-ista*; *broma* comes from Greek *brôma* "food" (EtymDB).
