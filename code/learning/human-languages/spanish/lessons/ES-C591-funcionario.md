## You'll want to know: el funcionario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C591-DESPIDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el funcionario** — "the civil servant, the public employee".

> Mi madre es funcionaria del ayuntamiento.
>
> *My mother is a civil servant at the town council.*

A woman is *la funcionaria*. In Spain many people sit public exams to become one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*funcionario* is built on *función*, a learned borrowing from Latin *functio* 'a performance, a duty' (EtymDB), from *fungi* 'to carry out'.
