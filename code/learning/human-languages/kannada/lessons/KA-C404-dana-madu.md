# ದಾನ ಮಾಡು (dāna māḍu) — to donate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C403-SAMAJA403-05, KA-LEX-C404-SADASYA404-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to devise, then the Kannada for a member.

## You'll want to know: ದಾನ ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C404-SADASYA404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಾನ ಮಾಡು** — *dāna māḍu* — "to donate".

> ನಾನು ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ ರಕ್ತ ದಾನ ಮಾಡಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu āspatrege rakta dāna māḍide.*
>
> I donated blood at the hospital.

Past (I …): ದಾನ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *dāna māḍide*.

ದಾನ alone is a gift or a donation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಾನ ಮಾಡು** (*dāna māḍu*) is built from Sanskrit *dāna*, "giving, a gift" (CDIAL 6265), with *māḍu*, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
