## You'll want to know: निरागस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C470-LABAD470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निरागस** — *nirāgas* — "innocent".

> बाळाचा चेहरा किती निरागस आहे!
>
> *bāḷācā cehrā kitī nirāgas āhe!*
>
> How innocent the baby's face is!

Unchanging; निरागसपणा, innocence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निरागस** (*nirāgas*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nirāgas*, "sinless, innocent" (Monier-Williams).
