## You'll want to know: प्रतिभा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C507-DAFTAR507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिभा** — *pratibhā* — "talent; a gift".

> मीरा में गाने की बड़ी प्रतिभा है।
>
> *Mīrā meṁ gāne kī baṛī pratibhā hai.*
>
> Mira has a great gift for singing.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिभा** (*pratibhā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *pratibhā*, "light; intelligence, genius" (Monier-Williams).
