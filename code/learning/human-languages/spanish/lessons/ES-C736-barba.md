## You'll want to know: la barba
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C736-CARA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la barba** — "the beard; the chin".

> Mi padre lleva barba.
>
> *My father has a beard.*

*Llevar barba* — to have a beard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barba* is inherited from Latin *barba* "beard" (EtymDB); English *barber* comes from the same word.
