## You'll want to know: बर्थ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C400-AARAKSHAN400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बर्थ** — *barth* — "a berth (on a train)".

> मेरी बर्थ ऊपर है और मीरा की नीचे।
>
> *merī barth ūpar hai aur Mīrā kī nīce.*
>
> My berth is the upper one and Mira's is the lower one.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बर्थ** (*barth*) is borrowed from English *berth*, a sleeping place on a ship or a train; the English th is said with थ.
