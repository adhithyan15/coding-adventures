## You'll want to know: auschecken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C426-REZEPTION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**auschecken** — "to check out".

> Bis elf Uhr müssen die Gäste auschecken.
>
> *The guests have to check out by eleven o'clock.*

Separable: *ich checke aus*, *ich habe ausgecheckt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*auschecken* is built from *aus* 'out' and *checken*, borrowed from English *check* (EtymDB), on the model of English *check out*.
