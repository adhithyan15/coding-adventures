## You'll want to know: വാശി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C530-USHAR530-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വാശി** — *vāśi* — "obstinacy; determination".

> ജയിക്കണമെന്ന വാശിയോടെ അവർ കളിച്ചു.
>
> *jayikkaṇamenna vāśiyōṭe avar kaḷiccu.*
>
> They played with a fierce will to win.

Good or bad: stubbornness in a child, determination in a player.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വാശി** (*vāśi*) is an everyday word; no source we can open explains it, so its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
