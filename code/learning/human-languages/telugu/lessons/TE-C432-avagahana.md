## You'll want to know: అవగాహన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C432-ADARSAM432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అవగాహన** — *avagāhana* — "awareness; understanding".

> జనంలో ఆరోగ్యం మీద అవగాహన పెరిగింది.
>
> *janaṁlō ārōgyaṁ mīda avagāhana perigindi.*
>
> People’s awareness of health has grown.

అవగాహన కల్పించు, to raise awareness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అవగాహన** (*avagāhana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *avagāhana*, "immersion, bathing" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for being steeped in a subject, understanding.
