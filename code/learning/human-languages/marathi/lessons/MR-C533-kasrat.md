## You'll want to know: कसरत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C533-TANDURUSTI533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कसरत** — *kasrat* — "a workout; exercise".

> धावपटू रोज दोन तास कसरत करतात.
>
> *dhāvpaṭū roj don tās kasrat kartāt.*
>
> Runners work out for two hours every day.

Feminine.

कसरत करणे, to work out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कसरत** (*kasrat*) is usually traced to Persian *kasrat*, "exercise".
