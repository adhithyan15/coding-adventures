## You'll want to know: уборка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C529-CLEANING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**уборка** (*ubórka*) — "cleaning, tidying up".

> В субботу у нас генеральная уборка.
>
> *V subbótu u nas generálnaya ubórka.*
>
> On Saturday we're having a big clean.

Feminine. *Генеральная уборка* — a thorough clean.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*уборка* is built on *убрать* 'to tidy away', from *у-* and *брать* 'to take'; EtymDB lists *уборка* without a source.
