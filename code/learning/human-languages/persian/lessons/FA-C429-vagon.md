## You'll want to know: واگن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C429-RAHAHAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**واگن** (*vâgon*) — "a carriage (of a train), a railway car".

> واگن ما آخر قطار بود.
>
> *Vâgon-e mâ âkhar-e qatâr bud.*
>
> Our carriage was at the end of the train.

*Vâgon-e ghazâkhori* — the dining car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vâgon* was borrowed from a European language: it is usually traced to French *wagon*, which itself came from English *wagon*. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
