## You'll want to know: চত্বর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C410-MATH410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**চত্বর** — *chôtvôr* — "a square; an open paved area".

> মন্দিরের চত্বরে শিশুরা খেলছিল।
>
> *mondirer chôtvôre shishurā khelchhilô.*
>
> Children were playing in the temple square.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চত্বর** (*chôtvôr*) is Sanskrit *catvara*, "a quadrangular place, a place where many ways meet" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.
