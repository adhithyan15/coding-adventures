## You'll want to know: l'agression
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C289-AGRESSION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'agression** — "the attack, the assault".

> Une agression a eu lieu près du marché.
>
> *An assault took place near the market.*

An attack on a person. A hostile mood is *l'agressivité*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*agression* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aggressiō* "a going towards", from *aggredī* "to approach, to attack", built on *gradī* "to step" (Lewis & Short).
