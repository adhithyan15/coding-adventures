## You'll want to know: die Ausrede
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C348-VERANTWORTUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Ausrede** — "the excuse".

> Das ist doch nur eine Ausrede!
>
> *That is only an excuse!*

Plural: *die Ausreden*. *Eine Ausrede finden* — to find an excuse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ausrede* is built from *aus* "out" and *Rede* "speech", from the family of *reden* "to talk", Old High German *redōn* (EtymDB): talk that gets you out of trouble.
