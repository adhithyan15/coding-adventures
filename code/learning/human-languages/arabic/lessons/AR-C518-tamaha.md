# طمح (ṭamaḥa) — to aspire

## You'll want to know: طمح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C518-MIHNA518-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طمح** — *ṭamaḥa* — "to aspire".

> يطمح ابني إلى أن يصبح طيارا.
>
> *yaṭmaḥu ibnī ilā an yuṣbiḥa ṭayyāran.*
>
> My son aspires to become a pilot.

Present: يطمح, *yaṭmaḥ*.

It takes إلى. Ṭumūḥ, ambition, which you know, is its noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طمح** (*ṭamaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-m-ḥ*, "to raise the eyes towards" (Lane). Lane gives ṭamaḥa of the sight, raised towards a thing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
