## You'll want to know: आंतरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C203-STEPS203-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आंतरो** — *ā̃tro* — "a distance".

> अठै सूं टेसण तांई घणो आंतरो है।
>
> *aṭhai sū̃ ṭesaṇ tā̃ī ghaṇo ā̃tro hai.*
>
> It's a long way from here to the station.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आंतरो** (*ā̃tro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *antara*, "interior; an interval". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati *ā̃tro*, "distance, interval" (CDIAL 357).
