## You'll want to know: झूठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C244-JHAGRO244-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झूठ** — *jhūṭh* — "a lie; a falsehood".

> झूठ मत बोलो।
>
> *jhūṭh mat bolo.*
>
> Don't tell lies.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झूठ** (*jhūṭh*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhūṭṭha*, "false"; CDIAL lists Marwari *jhūṭh*, "lie", under it (CDIAL 5407).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
