## You'll want to know: angeben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C475-ANGEBER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**angeben** — "to show off; to state".

> Mein Cousin gibt immer mit seinen Noten an.
>
> *My cousin is forever showing off about his marks.*

Separable: *er gibt an*, *hat angegeben*. On forms it means to state: *das Alter angeben* — to state your age.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*angeben* is built on *an* "on" and *geben* "to give" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Old High German *geban*, from Proto-Germanic *gebaną*.
