# Ask the price and hear the answer

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C249-THG249-01, SA-PRON-C262-YUSMAN-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “news,” then “you two” as an object. (*Vārtā; yuṣmān.*)

## Grammar Lens: one price turn

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C266-MARKET-EXCHANGE-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-03, SA-LEX-C87-NUMBERS-03, SA-LEX-C185-WHY185-01, SA-LEX-C248-THG248-03] -->

Put two known words together to ask the question used in the A1 mocks:

> **मूल्यं कियत्?**
>
> *Mūlyaṃ kiyat?*
>
> How much is the price?

One short answer is:

> **विंशतिः रूप्यकाणि।**
>
> *Viṃśatiḥ rūpyakāṇi.*
>
> Twenty rupees.

You already know **रूप्यकम्**, one rupee. Keep **रूप्यकाणि** together inside
this useful price answer. This small exchange does not teach the complete
neuter plural system or every way to state a price.
