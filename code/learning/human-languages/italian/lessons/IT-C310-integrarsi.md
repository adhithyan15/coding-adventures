## You'll want to know: integrarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C310-TRASFERIRSI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**integrarsi** — "to fit in, to integrate".

> Si è integrato bene nel nuovo paese.
>
> *He has fitted in well in his new country.*

With *essere*: *si è integrato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*integrarsi* is built on *integrare* "to make whole, to complete", a learned borrowing from Latin *integrāre*, from *integer* "whole, untouched" (Lewis & Short).
