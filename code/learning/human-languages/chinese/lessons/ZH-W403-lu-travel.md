## Script — a left piece of four strokes, then a right piece of six
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-LU-TRAVEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 旅

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, four strokes.** A dot on top. A bar under it. A stroke that runs right, turns down and hooks to the left at the foot. Then a long stroke falling to the left from under the bar.

**The right piece, six strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left and a bar to its right, like a small roof. Under them a short stroke falling to the left, a vertical that turns up to the right at the foot, another short stroke falling to the left, and last a long stroke falling to the right.

4 + 6 = ten.
