## You'll want to know: पानो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C285-AKHAR285-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पानो** — *pāno* — "a page".

> किताब मांय सो पाना है।
>
> *kitāb mā̃y so pānā hai.*
>
> The book has a hundred pages.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पानो** (*pāno*) is inherited from Sanskrit *parṇa*, "a leaf"; CDIAL lists Marwari *pān*, "leaf", Sindhi *pano*, "page of book", and Hindi *pannā*, "leaf, page" (CDIAL 7918).
