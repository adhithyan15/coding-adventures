## You'll want to know: कर्मचारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C369-SANSTHA369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मचारी** — *karmacārī* — "an employee, a member of staff".

> बैंक के कर्मचारी आज छुट्टी पर हैं।
>
> *baiṅk ke karmacārī āj chuṭṭī par haiṁ.*
>
> The bank staff are on leave today.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मचारी** (*karmacārī*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *karmacārin*, "engaged in work" (Monier-Williams).
