## You'll want to know: ಹೊಡೆದಾಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C365-HODEDATA365-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಡೆದಾಟ** — *hoḍedāṭa* — "a fistfight; a brawl".

> ಪಂದ್ಯದ ನಂತರ ಎರಡು ಗುಂಪುಗಳ ನಡುವೆ ಹೊಡೆದಾಟ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *pandyada nantara eraḍu gumpugaḷa naḍuve hoḍedāṭa āyitu.*
>
> After the match there was a fight between two groups.

ಹೊಡೆದು, having hit, and ಆಟ, play: hitting back and forth. The verb is ಹೊಡೆದಾಡು, to come to blows.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊಡೆದಾಟ** (*hoḍedāṭa*) is a native Dravidian compound: ಹೊಡೆದು, "having struck", from *hoḍe*, "to strike, to beat" (DEDR 4252), and ಆಟ, "play", from *āḍu* (DEDR 347).
