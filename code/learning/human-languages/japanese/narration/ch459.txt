Japanese, chapter 459: Studying Further.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
だいがく (daigaku) — a university.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a novelist, then the Japanese for a craftsman.

You'll want to know: だいがく.
だいがく — daigaku — "a university".
あには だいがくで べんきょうしています。 — ani wa daigaku de benkyō shite imasu — "My elder brother studies at university."

The word, taken apart.
だいがく (daigaku) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are dai, "large" (Middle Chinese dajH) and gaku, "study" (haewk) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

You'll want to know: りゅうがくする.
りゅうがくする — ryūgaku suru — "to study abroad".
ともだちは らいねん りゅうがくします。 — tomodachi wa rainen ryūgaku shimasu — "My friend is studying abroad next year."
Polite りゅうがくします, ryūgakushimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): あねが りゅうがくしました。, ane ga ryūgaku shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
りゅうがくする (ryūgaku suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) ryūgaku with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ryū, "detain" (Middle Chinese ljuw) and gaku, "study" (haewk) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
