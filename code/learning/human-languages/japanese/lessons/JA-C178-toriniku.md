## You'll want to know: とりにく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C178-MEAT178-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とりにく** — *toriniku* — "chicken meat; poultry".

> **ばんごはんに とりにくを やきます。** — *bangohan ni toriniku o yakimasu* — "I grill chicken for dinner."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とりにく** (*toriniku*) is built from native *tori*, "bird", and Sino-Japanese *niku*, "meat", a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *nyuwk*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
