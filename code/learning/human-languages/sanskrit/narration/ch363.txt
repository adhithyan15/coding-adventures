6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
अध्ययनम् (adhyayanam) — studying; study.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for inspects, then the Sanskrit for devises.

You'll want to know: अध्ययनम्.
अध्ययनम् — adhyayanam — "studying; study".
मम अध्ययनं प्रातः भवति। mama adhyayanaṃ prātaḥ bhavati. I do my studying in the morning.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अध्ययनम् (adhyayanam) is built on adhi, "over, upon", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives adhy-ayana "reading, studying" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for devises, then the Sanskrit for studying.

You'll want to know: विषयः.
विषयः — viṣayaḥ — "a topic; a subject (of study)".
मम प्रियः विषयः इतिहासः अस्ति। mama priyaḥ viṣayaḥ itihāsaḥ asti. My favourite subject is history.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विषयः (viṣayaḥ) MW gives viṣaya "an object of sense" and "any object of concern or attention", and "a subject of study" is how modern Sanskrit uses it; MW gives no sure derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

पण्डितः शास्त्रं पाठयति। paṇḍitaḥ śāstraṃ pāṭhayati. The scholar teaches a branch of learning.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शास्त्रम् (śāstram) comes from the root √śās, "to instruct, to order"; MW gives śāstra "teaching, instruction" and "any book or treatise" (MW).

अभ्यस्यति — abhyasyati — "practises (a skill)".
मीरा प्रतिदिनं वीणाम् अभ्यस्यति। mīrā pratidinaṃ vīṇām abhyasyati. Mira practises the veena every day.
Past (she …): सा वीणाम् अभ्यस्तवती, sā vīṇām abhyastavatī.

The word, taken apart.
अभ्यस्यति (abhyasyati) is built on abhi, "towards", and the root √as, "to throw"; MW gives abhy-√as "to practise, to exercise, to study" (MW).
