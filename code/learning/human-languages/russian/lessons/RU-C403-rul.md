## You'll want to know: руль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C403-RUL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**руль** (*rul*) — "a steering wheel; a handlebar".

> Папа держал руль обеими руками.
>
> *Pápa derzhál rul obéimi rukámi.*
>
> Dad held the steering wheel with both hands.

Masculine; the stress moves to the ending: *руля* (*rulyá*), *за рулём* (*za rulyóm*) — at the wheel, driving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*руль* was borrowed from Dutch *roer* 'a rudder' (EtymDB): the word for steering a ship came to mean steering a car or a bicycle.
