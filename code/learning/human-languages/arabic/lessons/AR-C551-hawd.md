## You'll want to know: حوض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C551-GHURFA551-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حوض** — *ḥawḍ* — "a basin, a tub".

> ملأ الولد حوض الاستحمام بالماء.
>
> *malaʾa al-walad ḥawḍ al-istiḥmām bi-l-māʾ.*
>
> The boy filled the bathtub with water.

Plural: أحواض, *aḥwāḍ*.

حوض الاستحمام is a bathtub.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حوض** (*ḥawḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-ḍ*, "a watering trough" (Lane). Lane gives ḥawḍ as a trough or cistern for water.
