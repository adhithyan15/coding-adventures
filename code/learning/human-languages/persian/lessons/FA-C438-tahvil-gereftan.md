## You'll want to know: تحویل گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C438-TAHVIL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تحویل گرفتن** (*tahvil gereftan*) — "to receive, to take delivery of; to check in (to a room)".

> ساعت دو اتاق را تحویل گرفتیم.
>
> *Sâ'at-e do otâq râ tahvil gereftim.*
>
> We got our room at two o'clock.

The opposite of *tahvil dâdan* 'to hand over'. Colloquially also to make a fuss of someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tahvil gereftan* is built from *tahvil* 'handing over', a loan usually traced to Arabic *taḥwīl* 'transfer, turning over' (EtymDB does not list it), and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian *grab-*.
