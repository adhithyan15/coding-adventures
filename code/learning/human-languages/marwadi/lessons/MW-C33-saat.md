# सात — seven on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHHA-SIX-01] -->

[PAUSE 16s] Write tea, then write six.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-SAAT-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-SA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-TA-01, MW-LEX-SAAT-01] -->

> **सा** + **त** → **सात** — *sāt* — seven

**सा** was the second word-piece this book asked the hand to build, in chapter
1. Seven is that piece with a dental **त** after it, and nothing else.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-SAAT-01, MW-SCRIPT-TEEN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **सात**, cover it, wait five seconds, write it. Then write **तीन** under
it and mark the **त** in both.
