## You'll want to know: थोरला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C497-THORLA497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**थोरला** — *thorlā* — "elder".

> थोरल्या बहिणीने मला गणित शिकवलं.
>
> *thorlyā bahiṇīne malā gaṇit śikavlaṁ.*
>
> My elder sister taught me maths.

Agrees; its pair is धाकटा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थोरला** (*thorlā*) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
