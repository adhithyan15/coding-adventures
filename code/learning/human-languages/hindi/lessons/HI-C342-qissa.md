## You'll want to know: क़िस्सा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C342-PRAKASHAK342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क़िस्सा** — *qissā* — "an anecdote; a tale".

> दादा जी के पास युद्ध के बहुत क़िस्से थे।
>
> *dādā jī ke pās yuddh ke bahut qisse the.*
>
> Grandfather had lots of stories from the war.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़िस्सा** (*qissā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qiṣṣa*, "a story". It is a tale told aloud, often a true one from someone's life.
