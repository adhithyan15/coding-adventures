## You'll want to know: شباب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C486-GROWUP486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شباب** — *shabāb* — "youth; the prime of life".

> شباب کے دن جلدی گزرتے ہیں۔
>
> *shabāb ke din jaldī guzarte haiṅ.*
>
> The days of youth pass quickly.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شباب** (*shabāb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shabāb*, "youth".
