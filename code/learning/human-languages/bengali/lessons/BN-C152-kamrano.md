## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **কামড়ানো** (*kāmṛāno*) is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it, and a search by form and by the gloss "bite" finds nothing close.
