## You'll want to know: बरगडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C478-HAD478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बरगडी** — *bargaḍī* — "a rib".

> पडल्यामुळे त्याची एक बरगडी मोडली.
>
> *paḍlyāmuḷe tyācī ek bargaḍī moḍlī.*
>
> He broke a rib when he fell.

Feminine. Plural: बरगड्या, *bargaḍyā*.

Plural बरगड्या, the ribs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बरगडी** (*bargaḍī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
