# Review — a plan that depends on the weather

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-AGAR-SUBJ-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BIYAYAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if the weather is good", then "if it rains". (*Agar havâ
khub bâshad*, *agar bârân biyâyad*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-AGAR-SUBJ-01, FA-GRAMMAR-REAL-CONDITION-01, FA-LEX-BASTEGI-DARAD-01, FA-LEX-C298-ZHAKAT-01, FA-LEX-C87-LOC87-03] -->

Four lessons, and a plan can now hang on something. *Agar* opens the
condition and takes the subjunctive, *bâshad* or *biyâyad*. The result is a
present, a polite instruction, or *bâyad*. And when you cannot say yet,
*bastegi dârad* holds the answer open.

[PAUSE 2s] Two friends, making a plan:

> — *fardâ be pârk miravim?*
> — *bastegi dârad be havâ. agar havâ khub bâshad, miravim. agar bârân
> biyâyad, dar khâne mimânim.*
> — *agar sard bâshad?*
> — *zhâkat bepush!*
