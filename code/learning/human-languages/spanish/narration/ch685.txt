Spanish, chapter 685: El paisaje.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el paisaje — the landscape, the scenery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to reside, then the Spanish for the neighbourhood.

You'll want to know: el paisaje.
el paisaje — "the landscape, the scenery".
El paisaje es precioso. The scenery is beautiful.
Un paisaje de montaña — a mountain landscape.

The word, taken apart.
paisaje was borrowed from French paysage, built on pays "country", which goes back to Latin pāgus "a district, the countryside".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for the neighbourhood, then the Spanish for the landscape.

You'll want to know: la naturaleza.
la naturaleza — "nature".
Me gusta pasear por la naturaleza. I like walking in nature.
Por naturaleza — by nature.

The word, taken apart.
naturaleza is built on natural with -eza (EtymDB); natural comes from Latin nātūrālis, built on nātus "born".

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: la sierra.
la sierra — "the mountain range; the saw".
Pasamos el fin de semana en la sierra. We spent the weekend in the mountains.
A row of peaks looks like the teeth of a saw.

The word, taken apart.
sierra is inherited from Latin serra "a saw" (EtymDB); a jagged line of peaks looks like its teeth.

Lesson 5 of 5.
el desierto — the desert; deserted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the mountain range, then the Spanish for the mountain.

You'll want to know: el desierto.
el desierto — "the desert; deserted".
Cruzamos el desierto en coche. We drove across the desert.
As an adjective it means empty: una calle desierta.

The word, taken apart.
desierto is inherited from Latin dēsertus "abandoned, forsaken" (EtymDB): a desert is land that people have left.
