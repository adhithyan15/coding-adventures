## You'll want to know: کارواش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C423-BENZIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کارواش** (*kârvâsh*) — "a car wash".

> جمعه ماشین را به کارواش بردیم.
>
> *Jom'e mâshin râ be kârvâsh bordim.*
>
> On Friday we took the car to the car wash.

A loan word, said as in English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kârvâsh* was borrowed from English and is usually traced to English *car wash*. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
