## You'll want to know: castīgō, castīgāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C415-CASTIGO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**castīgō, castīgāre** — "to punish, to correct; to rebuke".

> Pater fīlium verbīs castīgāvit.
>
> *The father rebuked his son.*

*castīgō, castīgāre, castīgāvī, castīgātum*, built on *castus*, pure: to set right. English *castigate* and *chastise*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*castīgō* is built on *castus* "pure" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to set a person right. English *castigate* and *chastise* come from it.
