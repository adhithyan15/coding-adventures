## You'll want to know: ಸೊಳ್ಳೆಪರದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C462-KOTHADI462-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೊಳ್ಳೆಪರದೆ** — *soḷḷeparade* — "a mosquito net".

> ಮಂಚದ ಮೇಲೆ ಒಂದು ಸೊಳ್ಳೆಪರದೆ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *mañcada mēle ondu soḷḷeparade ittu.*
>
> There was a mosquito net over the bed.

ಸೊಳ್ಳೆ, a mosquito, plus ಪರದೆ, a curtain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೊಳ್ಳೆಪರದೆ** (*soḷḷeparade*) is a Kannada compound of ಸೊಳ್ಳೆ, "a mosquito", and ಪರದೆ, "a curtain", which is usually traced to Persian *parda*.
