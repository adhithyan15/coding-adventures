Persian, chapter 130: Healthy, Coarse, Tiny, Little, Much.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سالم (sâlem) — healthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for wise, then the Persian for ignorant.

You'll want to know: سالم.
سالم — sâlem — "healthy".

The word, taken apart.
sâlem is borrowed from Arabic sālim, "sound, safe". Its Arabic root is s-l-m (Lane). It shares its root with salâm.

You'll want to know: درشت.
درشت — dorosht — "coarse, large".

The word, taken apart.
dorosht is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian društ, "coarse, rough".

Lesson 5 of 5.
زیاد (ziyâd) — much, many.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for tiny, then the Persian for little.

You'll want to know: زیاد.
زیاد — ziyâd — "much, many".

The word, taken apart.
ziyâd is borrowed from Arabic ziyāda, "increase". Its Arabic root is z-y-d (Lane). Persian uses it as "much, many" (EtymDB).
