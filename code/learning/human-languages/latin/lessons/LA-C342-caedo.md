## You'll want to know: caedō, caedere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C342-CLADES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**caedō, caedere** — "to cut down, to kill; to beat".

> Mīlitēs hostēs fugientēs caedēbant.
>
> *The soldiers were cutting down the fleeing enemy.*

*caedō, caedere, cecīdī, caesum*. Also to fell trees and to beat. Its noun is *caedēs*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**caedō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to cut, to hew" (EtymDB). English words in *-cide*, such as *homicide*, come from its family.
