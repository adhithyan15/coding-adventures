## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-NOW-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGZAI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-ZAI-PROGRESSIVE-01, ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGHAO-01, ZH-LEX-C149-YIBIAN-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01, ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-01, ZH-LEX-C63-PHONE63-02] -->

> **A:** 你在做什么
>
> *nǐ zài zuò shénme* — What are you doing?
>
> **B:** 我正在吃饭 你呢
>
> *wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn, nǐ ne* — I'm eating. And you?
>
> **A:** 我在看书
>
> *wǒ zài kàn shū* — I'm reading.
>
> **B:** 你妈妈呢
>
> *nǐ māma ne* — And your mum?
>
> **A:** 妈妈在做饭
>
> *māma zài zuò fàn* — She's cooking.
>
> **B:** *nǐ bàba ne* — And your dad?
>
> **A:** 他没在家 他在开车
>
> *tā méi zài jiā, tā zài kāichē* — He isn't home. He's driving.
>
> **B:** 我一边吃饭一边打电话
>
> *wǒ yìbiān chī fàn yìbiān dǎ diànhuà* — I'm eating and on the phone at once.
>
> **A:** 正好 我也在吃饭
>
> *zhènghǎo, wǒ yě zài chī fàn* — As it happens, I'm eating too.

Every answer keeps 在 before its verb. 他没在家 is 在 before a place, at home; 他在开车 is 在 before a verb, driving.

## What you've built — now, and two things at once
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-NOW-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZHENG-01, ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGZAI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-ZAI-PROGRESSIVE-01, ZH-LEX-C146-NIGHT146-01, ZH-LEX-C146-NIGHT146-03, ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-01] -->

在 or 正在 before the verb says what is going on now:

- **我正在吃饭** *wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn* — I'm eating right now.
- **他在开车** *tā zài kāichē* — he's driving.
- **白天我在看书** *báitiān wǒ zài kàn shū* — in the daytime I'm reading.
- *bànyè tā zhèngzài shuìjiào* — in the middle of the night he's asleep.

**Listen.** *Nǐ zài zuò shénme ne?*

**Speak.** Say what you are doing now, then what somebody at home is doing.

[YOU WRITE: 正在 — 一, then the middle of 上, then the long bar]
