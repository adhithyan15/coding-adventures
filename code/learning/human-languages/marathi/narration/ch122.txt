Marathi, chapter 122: Means, Owner, Strength, Wanted, Wish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साधन (sādhan) — a means, a tool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for ability, then the Marathi for a right.

You'll want to know: साधन.
साधन — sādhan — "a means, a tool".

The word, taken apart.
साधन (sādhan) is Sanskrit sādhana, "furthering; completion", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13334). It is built on sādh, "to accomplish".

Lesson 2 of 5.
मालक (mālak) — an owner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a right, then the Marathi for a means.

You'll want to know: मालक.
मालक — mālak — "an owner".

The word, taken apart.
मालक (mālak) is a loan from Arabic mālik, "owner", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is m-l-k (Lane). Marathi says mālak.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ताकद (tākad) — strength.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a means, then the Marathi for an owner.

You'll want to know: ताकद.
ताकद — tākad — "strength".

The word, taken apart.
ताकद (tākad) is a loan from Arabic ṭāqa, "power, strength", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṭ-w-q (Lane). Persian and Hindustani say ṭāqat; Marathi ends it in द.

Lesson 4 of 5.
हवा (havā) — wanted, needed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an owner, then the Marathi for strength.

You'll want to know: हवा.
हवा — havā — "wanted, needed".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of हवा (havā) is not settled. Neither CDIAL nor DEDR has an entry for it. It is not the Hindi havā, "air", which is Arabic.
