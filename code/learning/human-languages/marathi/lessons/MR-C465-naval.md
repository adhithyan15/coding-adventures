## You'll want to know: नवल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C465-NAVAL465-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नवल** — *naval* — "wonder; a marvel".

> पहिल्यांदा समुद्र पाहून मुलांना नवल वाटलं.
>
> *pahilyāndā samudra pāhūn mulānnā naval vāṭlaṁ.*
>
> Seeing the sea for the first time, the children marvelled.

Neuter.

नवल वाटणे, to marvel, with the dative; नवलाची गोष्ट, a wonderful thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नवल** (*naval*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *naval*, "new", under *\*navala*, "new" (CDIAL 7012); a new thing is a wonder.
