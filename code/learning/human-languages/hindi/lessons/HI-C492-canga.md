## You'll want to know: चंगा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C492-SUDHAR492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चंगा** — *caṅgā* — "well again; cured".

> आराम से तुम जल्दी चंगे हो जाओगे।
>
> *ārām se tum jaldī caṅge ho jāoge.*
>
> With rest you'll soon be well again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चंगा** (*caṅgā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *caṅgā*, "good, sound, well", under Sanskrit *caṅga* (CDIAL 4564).
