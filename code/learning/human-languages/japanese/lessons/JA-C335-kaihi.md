## You'll want to know: かいひ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C335-DANTAI335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かいひ** — *kaihi* — "a membership fee".

> **かいひが たかいので、やめました。** — *kaihi ga takai node, yamemashita* — "The membership fee is high, so I quit."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいひ** (*kaihi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "meeting" (Middle Chinese *hwajH*) and *hi*, "expense" (*phj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
