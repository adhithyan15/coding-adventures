## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-VALA-ASSOCIATIVE-195]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-04, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-01, PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-PRENOMINAL-01, PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-GENDER-AGREEMENT-01, PA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-01] -->

> **ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਕਮਰਾ** — *khiṛkī vālā kamrā* — a room with a window

Here noun **ਖਿੜਕੀ** plus **ਵਾਲਾ** forms an adjective phrase describing the
masculine singular noun **ਕਮਰਾ**. Learn this one direct room phrase before any
broader pattern.

## Grammar Lens: source and scope boundary
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VALA-ASSOCIATIVE-195] -->

Gill and Gleason describe noun plus **ਵਾਲਾ** as an adjective phrase and show
the masculine adjective direct–oblique pattern. This chapter follows only one
room contrast. It is not a rule for every noun or postposition. The earlier
**ਵਾਲੀ**, “earring,” word lesson owns a separate lexical homograph, not a grammatical form
licensed by that old word lesson. Source: Harjeet Singh Gill and Henry A.
Gleason, Jr., *A Reference Grammar of Punjabi* (1969), pp. 71–72 and 107,
[adjective phrases](https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/assets/A%20Reference%20Grammar_Final.pdf).
