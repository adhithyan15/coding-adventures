## You'll want to know: zuǒyòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C205-FAR205-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zuǒyòu* — "about; or so (after a number)".

> *zǒulù èrshí fēnzhōng zuǒyòu* — It's about twenty minutes on foot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuǒyòu* is built from *zuǒ* 'left' and *yòu* 'right', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsaX* and *hjuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a little to either side of the number.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
