# తలదించుకో (taladiñcukō) — to hang one's head; to be ashamed

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be proud, then the Telugu for to hold back politely.

## You'll want to know: తలదించుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తలదించుకో** — *taladiñcukō* — "to hang one's head; to be ashamed".

> అబద్ధం బయటపడగానే అతను తలదించుకున్నాడు.
>
> *abaddhaṁ bayaṭapaḍagānē atanu taladiñcukunnāḍu.*
>
> As soon as the lie came out, he hung his head.

Past (I …): తలదించుకున్నాను, *taladiñcukunnānu*.

తల, head, + దించుకో, to lower your own: the gesture of shame. Its opposite is తలెత్తుకో, to hold your head high.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తలదించుకో** (*taladiñcukō*) is native Dravidian: తల, "head" (DEDR 3103), and దించు, "to lower, to put down" (DEDR 502), with -కో, "for oneself", from కొను, "to take" (DEDR 2151): to lower one's own head.
