## You'll want to know: clēmentia, clēmentiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C505-CLEM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**clēmentia, clēmentiae** — "mercy, clemency".

> Victor clēmentiam hostibus ostendit.
>
> *The victor shows mercy to the enemy.*

Feminine, first declension. A virtue of rulers: mercy toward those in your power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**clēmentia** is built on *clēmēns* "merciful" (Lewis & Short). English *clemency* comes from it.
