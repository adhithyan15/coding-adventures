## You'll want to know: കുറ്റബോധം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C524-APAMANAM524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**കുറ്റബോധം** — *kuṟṟabōdhaṁ* — "guilt; a guilty conscience".

> അമ്മയോട് നുണ പറഞ്ഞതിൽ അവൾക്ക് കുറ്റബോധം തോന്നി.
>
> *ammayōṭŭ nuṇa paṟaññatil avaḷkkŭ kuṟṟabōdhaṁ tōnni.*
>
> She felt guilty about lying to her mother.

കുറ്റം a fault + ബോധം awareness: knowing one has done wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുറ്റബോധം** (*kuṟṟabōdhaṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from കുറ്റം, "a fault", a native word (DEDR 1851), and Sanskrit *bodha*, "consciousness, awareness" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
