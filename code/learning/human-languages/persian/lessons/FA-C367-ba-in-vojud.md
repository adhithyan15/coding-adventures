## You'll want to know: با این وجود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C367-AMMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**با این وجود** (*bâ in vojud*) — "nevertheless, even so".

> باران میآمد. با این وجود، مسابقه ادامه پیدا کرد.
>
> *bârân miâmad. bâ in vojud, mosâbeqe edâme peydâ kard.*
>
> It was raining. Nevertheless, the match went on.

Like *bâ in hâl*; a whole clause follows it. Compare *bâ vojud-e* + a noun, "despite".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**با این وجود** is literally "with this existing": *bâ* "with" and *in* "this" are inherited Persian, and *vojud* comes from Arabic *wujūd* "existence" (EtymDB).
