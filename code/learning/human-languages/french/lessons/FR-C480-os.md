## You'll want to know: l'os
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C480-PLATRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'os** — "bone".

> Le chien ronge un os dans le jardin.
>
> *The dog is gnawing a bone in the garden.*

Masculine. Singular *un os* with a sounded *s*; plural *des os*, where the *s* is usually silent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'os* is inherited from Latin *os*, *ossis* "bone" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
