## You'll want to know: 保安
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C382-GUARD382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**保安** — *bǎo'ān* — "security guard".

> **小区门口有两个保安** *xiǎoqū ménkǒu yǒu liǎng ge bǎo'ān* — There are two security guards at the gate of the estate.

The new character, in pieces: 保 — nine strokes in three pieces: the person piece 亻, then 口 high on the right over a tree of four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**保安** (*bǎo'ān*) is built from 保 'to keep' and 安 'calm', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *'an* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to guard' and 'to keep safe': keeping the peace, and then the person who does it.
