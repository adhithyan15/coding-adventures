Persian, chapter 382: Projects and planning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پروژه (prozhe) — a project.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a specialist, then the Persian for a duty.

You'll want to know: پروژه.
پروژه (prozhe) — "a project".
این پروژه شش ماه طول میکشد. in prozhe shesh mâh tul mikeshad. This project takes six months.
A loan from French. Plural prozhehâ. ژ sounds like the s in English pleasure.

The word, taken apart.
پروژه (prozhe) was borrowed from French projet "project" (EtymDB), from Latin prōiectus "thrown forward", built on iacere "to throw" (Lewis & Short): a plan thrown ahead of you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مدیریت کردن (modiriyat kardan) — to manage, to run.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for a duty, then the Persian for a project.

You'll want to know: مدیریت کردن.
مدیریت کردن (modiriyat kardan) — "to manage, to run".
مادرم یک رستوران کوچک را مدیریت میکند. mâdaram yek resturân-e kuchek râ modiriyat mikonad. My mother runs a small restaurant.
Modiriyyat "management", built on modir "manager", + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
مدیریت is built on modir "manager", borrowed from Arabic mudīr, with the abstract ending -iyat; kardan "to do" is the inherited Persian verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
برنامه ریزی کردن (barnâme rizi kardan) — to plan, to schedule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a project, then the Persian for to manage.

You'll want to know: برنامه ریزی کردن.
برنامه ریزی کردن (barnâme rizi kardan) — "to plan, to schedule".
ما برای تعطیلات تابستان برنامه ریزی کردیم. mâ barâye ta'tilât-e tâbestân barnâme rizi kardim. We made plans for the summer holidays.
Barnâme "programme" + rizi "pouring", from rikhtan "to pour", + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
برنامه ریزی کردن (barnâme rizi kardan) is native Persian, built from barnâme "programme", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian barnāmag, + rizi, built on riz-, the present stem of rikhtan "to pour", + kardan. To plan is to pour out a programme.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سازمان (sâzmân) — an organisation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to manage, then the Persian for to plan.

You'll want to know: سازمان.
سازمان (sâzmân) — "an organisation".
او در یک سازمان بین المللی کار میکند. u dar yek sâzmân-e beyn ol-melali kâr mikonad. She works for an international organisation.
From sâz-, the present stem of sâkhtan "to make", + -mân. Plural sâzmânhâ.

The word, taken apart.
سازمان (sâzmân) is native Persian, built on sâz-, the present stem of sâkhtan "to make, to build", with the suffix -mân: an organisation is something built and arranged.

Lesson 5 of 5.
حرفه ای (herfe'i) — professional.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to plan, then the Persian for an organisation.

You'll want to know: حرفه ای.
حرفه ای (herfe'i) — "professional".
او یک راننده حرفه ای است. u yek rânande-ye herfe'i ast. He is a professional driver.
Herfe "trade, profession" + -i, written apart. The opposite is âmâtor or gheyr-e herfe'i.

The word, taken apart.
حرفه ای (herfe'i) is herfe, from Arabic ḥirfa "occupation" (EtymDB), with the Persian adjective suffix -i.
