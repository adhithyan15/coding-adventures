## You'll want to know: आपत्कालः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C295-CETANA295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्कालः** — *āpatkālaḥ* — "an emergency".

> आपत्काले वैद्यः शीघ्रम् आगच्छति।
>
> *āpatkāle vaidyaḥ śīghram āgacchati.*
>
> In an emergency the doctor comes quickly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्कालः** (*āpatkālaḥ*) is built from *āpad*, "misfortune, distress", and *kāla*, "time"; MW gives it as a time of distress (MW).
