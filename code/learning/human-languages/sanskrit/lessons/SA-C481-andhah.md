## You'll want to know: अन्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C481-ANDHA481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्धः** — *andhaḥ* — "blind".

> अन्धः याचकः मार्गे गायति।
>
> *andhaḥ yācakaḥ mārge gāyati.*
>
> A blind beggar sings by the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्धः** (*andhaḥ*) MW gives "blind" and "dark"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
