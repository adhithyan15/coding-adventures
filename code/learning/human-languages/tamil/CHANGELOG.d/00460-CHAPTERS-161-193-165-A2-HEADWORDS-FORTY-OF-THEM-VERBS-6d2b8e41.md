## Chapters 161-193 — 165 A2 headwords, forty of them verbs

The first of four Tamil A2 vocabulary tranches: thirty-three chapters of five
words, in four runs, each closed by two review lessons.

- 40 verbs: cooking, moving about, serving and sharing, school, festivals,
  getting on with people, everyday handling, and the farm.
- 125 nouns and qualities: the body and illness, time words, rooms and
  vehicles, places, linking words, abstract nouns, describing words, the
  kitchen and South Indian dishes, messages, family, animals and plants,
  technology, and people at work.
- The word list was drafted by theme, checked against every headword the
  course already teaches, and reviewed by a native-level reader. Words that
  an earlier lesson already uses in its text were left out, so the track
  gains no forward reference.
- Every headword uses only letters the course has taught. Extensions carry
  stage A2.
- Level gate: 401 headwords and 5 verbs short of A2.
