Bengali, chapter 251: Dance, Exhibition, Fair, Party, Journey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নাচ (nāch) — a dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a spider, then the Bengali for a kite.

You'll want to know: নাচ.
নাচ — nāch — "a dance".

The word, taken apart.
নাচ (nāch) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nṛtya, "dancing" (CDIAL 7582). CDIAL lists Bengali nāc, "dancing".
