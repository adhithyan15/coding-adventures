## You'll want to know: 父亲
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C511-KIN511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**父亲** — *fùqīn* — "father (the formal word)".

> **我父亲在大学教了三十年汉语** *wǒ fùqīn zài dàxué jiāo le sānshí nián Hànyǔ* — My father taught Chinese at a university for thirty years.

Many speakers make the second syllable light: fùqin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**父亲** (*fùqīn*) is built from 父 'a father' and 亲 'close', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjuX* and *tshin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
