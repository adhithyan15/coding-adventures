# কাঁথা (kā̃thā) — a kantha (an embroidered quilt)

## You'll want to know: কাঁথা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C509-GALICHA509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঁথা** — *kā̃thā* — "a kantha (an embroidered quilt)".

> শীতে দিদিমা আমাকে একটা কাঁথা দিলেন।
>
> *Shīte didimā āmāke ekṭā kā̃thā dilen.*
>
> In winter my grandmother gave me a kantha quilt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঁথা** (*kā̃thā*) goes back to Sanskrit *kanthā*, "a patched garment, a rag" (Monier-Williams): old cloth stitched into new use.
