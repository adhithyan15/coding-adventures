Sanskrit, chapter 446: Peaks and Plateaus.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अधित्यका (adhityakā) — a plateau; high table-land.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for by day, then the Sanskrit for passes the time.

You'll want to know: अधित्यका.
अधित्यका — adhityakā — "a plateau; high table-land".
पर्वतस्य उपरि विशाला अधित्यका अस्ति। parvatasya upari viśālā adhityakā asti. On top of the mountain there is a wide plateau.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अधित्यका (adhityakā) is built on adhi, "over, above", through adhi-tya, as MW gives it (Pāṇini 5.2.34); MW gives adhityakā as land on the upper part of a mountain, table-land (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
शिखरम् (śikharam) — a summit; a peak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for passes the time, then the Sanskrit for a plateau.

You'll want to know: शिखरम्.
शिखरम् — śikharam — "a summit; a peak".
पर्वतस्य शिखरे हिमम् अस्ति। parvatasya śikhare himam asti. There is snow on the summit of the mountain.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शिखरम् (śikharam) is the word MW gives for pointed, crested and, as a noun, a point, a peak, a summit; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

गुल्मः (gulmaḥ) is the word MW gives for a cluster or clump of trees, a thicket, a bush, a shrub; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

नदीतीरे कुशाः वर्धन्ते। nadītīre kuśāḥ vardhante. Kusha grass grows on the riverbank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कुशः (kuśaḥ) is the word MW gives for the sacred grass of the rites, Poa cynosuroides; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
