## You'll want to know: yùnjī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C414-CABIN414-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yùnjī* — "to get airsick".

> *wǒ zuò fēijī de shíhou huì yùnjī* — I get airsick when I fly.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùnjī* is built from *yùn* 'dizzy' and 机 'a plane'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first, *hjunH* there, as a halo round the sun or moon, so how it came to mean dizziness is not settled.
