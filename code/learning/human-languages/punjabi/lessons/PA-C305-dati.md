## You'll want to know: ਦਾਤੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C305-HARVEST305-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਾਤੀ** — *dātī* — "a sickle".

> ਕਿਸਾਨ ਦਾਤੀ ਨਾਲ ਕਣਕ ਲੁਣਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *kisān dātī nāl kaṇak luṇdā hai.*
>
> The farmer reaps the wheat with a sickle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਾਤੀ** (*dātī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dātra*, "knife, sickle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dātī*, "sickle", under it (CDIAL 6260).
