Marathi, chapter 196: To go for a walk, To fold, To prepare, To introduce, To lend.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फिरायला जाणे (phirāylā jāṇe) — to go for a walk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to knock, then the Marathi for to compare.

You'll want to know: फिरायला जाणे.
फिरायला जाणे — phirāylā jāṇe — "to go for a walk".

The word, taken apart.
फिरायला is "for wandering", from फिरणे, "to wander, turn", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed phirati, "moves, wanders" (CDIAL 9078). जाणे comes from Sanskrit yāti, "goes" (CDIAL 10452).

Lesson 2 of 5.
घडी करणे (ghaḍī karṇe) — to fold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to compare, then the Marathi for to go for a walk.

You'll want to know: घडी करणे.
घडी करणे — ghaḍī karṇe — "to fold".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of घडी, "a fold", is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it in this sense; its Marathi ghaḍī is a different word, "water-clock" (CDIAL 4406). करणे is "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
तयारी करणे (tayārī karṇe) — to prepare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to go for a walk, then the Marathi for to fold.

You'll want to know: तयारी करणे.
तयारी करणे — tayārī karṇe — "to prepare".

The word, taken apart.
तयारी is built on tayār, "ready", a loan usually traced through Persian to Arabic ṭayyār, "flying, swift". करणे is "to do".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ओळख करून देणे (oḷakh karūn deṇe) — to introduce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to fold, then the Marathi for to prepare.

You'll want to know: ओळख करून देणे.
ओळख करून देणे — oḷakh karūn deṇe — "to introduce".

The word, taken apart.
ओळख, "acquaintance", CDIAL lists under Sanskrit upalakṣa, "distinguishing", from lakṣ, "to mark" (CDIAL 2224). करून देणे is "to do for someone": देणे comes from Sanskrit dadāti, "gives" (CDIAL 6141).
