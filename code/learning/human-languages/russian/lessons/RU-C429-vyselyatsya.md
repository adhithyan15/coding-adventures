## You'll want to know: выселяться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C429-ZASELENIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**выселяться** (*vyselyátsya*) — "to check out; to move out".

> Из номера надо выселяться до двенадцати.
>
> *Iz nómera nádo vyselyátsya do dvenádtsati.*
>
> You have to check out of the room before twelve.

Imperfective; the perfective is *выселиться*. In hotels people also say *выезжать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выселяться* is built from *вы-* 'out', *селить* 'to settle' and *-ся*. *Селить* goes with *село* 'a village', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *selo* (EtymDB).
