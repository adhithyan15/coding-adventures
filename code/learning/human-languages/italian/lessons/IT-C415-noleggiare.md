## You'll want to know: noleggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C415-NOLEGGIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**noleggiare** — "to hire, to rent (a car or a bike)".

> In vacanza noleggiamo una macchina.
>
> *On holiday we hire a car.*

For a car, a bike or a boat; for a flat, *affittare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*noleggiare* is built on *nolo* 'hire, freight charge', which is usually traced through Medieval Latin *naulum* to Greek *naûlon* 'the fare for a passage by ship'; it came into Italian as a borrowing.
