# సాధించు (sādhiñcu) — to achieve; to accomplish

## You'll want to know: సాధించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C527-VIJAYAM527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సాధించు** — *sādhiñcu* — "to achieve; to accomplish".

> పట్టుదలతో ఆమె తన లక్ష్యం సాధించింది.
>
> *paṭṭudalatō āme tana lakṣyaṁ sādhiñcindi.*
>
> With determination she achieved her goal.

Past (I …): సాధించాను, *sādhiñcānu*.

Its noun సాధన is practice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సాధించు** (*sādhiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *sādhana*, "furthering; completion" (CDIAL 13334), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
