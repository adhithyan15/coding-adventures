Russian, chapter 394: Debts and loans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
долг (dolg) — a debt; a duty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a power of attorney, then the Russian for a will.

You'll want to know: долг.
долг (dolg) — "a debt; a duty".
У него большие долги. U negó bolshíye dolgí. He has big debts.
Masculine; plural долги. Брать в долг (dolg) — to borrow. Долг is also a duty: мой долг (dolg) — помочь.

The word, taken apart.
долг (dolg) is inherited from Proto-Slavic dŭlgŭ (EtymDB) "a debt, an obligation".

Lesson 2 of 5.
кредит (kredít) — a loan, credit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a will, then the Russian for a debt.

You'll want to know: кредит.
кредит (kredít) — "a loan, credit".
Они взяли кредит на машину. Oní vzyáli kredít na mashínu. They took out a loan for a car.
Masculine. Брать кредит (kredít) — to take out a loan. В кредит (kredít) — on credit.

The word, taken apart.
кредит (kredít) is usually traced, through German and French, to Latin crēditum "a loan", from crēdere "to trust, to lend" (Lewis & Short): a loan rests on trust.

Lesson 3 of 5.
одалживать (odálzhivat) — to lend; to borrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a debt, then the Russian for a loan.

You'll want to know: одалживать.
одалживать (odálzhivat) — "to lend; to borrow".
Я часто одалживаю брату деньги. Ya chásto odálzhivayu brátu déngi. I often lend my brother money.
Imperfective; perfective одолжить. Одалживать кому — to lend to; одалживать (odálzhivat) у кого — to borrow from.

The word, taken apart.
одалживать (odálzhivat) is the imperfective of одолжить, built on долг (dolg) "a debt", Proto-Slavic dŭlgŭ (EtymDB): to put someone in your debt. The о turns to а in the imperfective, as in спрашивать.

Lesson 4 of 5.
возвращать (vozvrashchát) — to give back, to return (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a loan, then the Russian for to lend.

You'll want to know: возвращать.
возвращать (vozvrashchát) — "to give back, to return (something)".
Он всегда возвращает книги вовремя. On vsegdá vozvrashcháyet knígi vovrémya. He always returns books on time.
Imperfective; perfective вернуть or возвратить. Возвращать долг (dolg) — to pay back a debt. Возвращаться is to come back.

The word, taken apart.
возвращать (vozvrashchát) is built from воз- "back" and вращать "to turn", the Church Slavonic twin of native ворочать: to turn a thing back to its owner.

Lesson 5 of 5.
процент (protsént) — a per cent; interest (on money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to lend, then the Russian for to give back.

You'll want to know: процент.
процент (protsént) — "a per cent; interest (on money)".
Банк даёт пять процентов в год. Bank dayót pyat protséntov v god. The bank pays five per cent a year.
Masculine. Сто процентов — a hundred per cent. Проценты is also interest on money.

The word, taken apart.
процент (protsént) is usually traced, through German Prozent, to Latin prō centum "for a hundred" (Lewis & Short).
