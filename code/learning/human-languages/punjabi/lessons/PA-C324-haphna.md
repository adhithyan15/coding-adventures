## You'll want to know: ਹਫਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C324-BREATH324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਫਣਾ** — *haphṇā* — "to pant".

> ਦਾਦਾ ਜੀ ਪੌੜੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਫਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *dādā jī pauṛī utte haphde han.*
>
> Grandfather pants on the stairs.

Past (he …): ਉਹ ਪੌੜੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਫਿਆ, *oh pauṛī utte haphiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਫਣਾ** (*haphṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*hamph*, "pant"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *haphṇā*, "to pant", under it (CDIAL 13973).
