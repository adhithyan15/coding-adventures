## You'll want to know: l'averse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C461-BRUINE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'averse** — "the rain shower, the downpour".

> Nous avons attendu la fin de l'averse sous un arbre.
>
> *We waited for the shower to end under a tree.*

A feminine noun. Short and heavy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*averse* comes from *à verse* 'pouring down', from *verser* 'to pour', which goes back to Latin *versāre*, from *vertere* 'to turn' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
