Marathi, chapter 422: Noise in the Market.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मंडई (maṇḍaī) — a vegetable market.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a village gate, then the Marathi for a boundary.

You'll want to know: मंडई.
मंडई — maṇḍaī — "a vegetable market".
आई रोज सकाळी मंडईत जाते. āī roj sakāḷī maṇḍaīt jāte. Mother goes to the vegetable market every morning.
Feminine.
A covered market for vegetables and fruit, as in Pune.

The word, taken apart.
मंडई (maṇḍaī) is a Marathi word for a covered vegetable market; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कोलाहल (kolāhal) — an uproar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a vegetable market, then the Marathi for noise.

You'll want to know: कोलाहल.
कोलाहल — kolāhal — "an uproar".
बातमी ऐकून सभेत कोलाहल माजला. bātmī aikūn sabhet kolāhal mājlā. Hearing the news, an uproar broke out in the meeting.
Masculine.
A literary word; कोलाहल (kolāhal) माजणे, an uproar breaks out.

The word, taken apart.
कोलाहल (kolāhal) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kolāhala, "a great din" (CDIAL 3534), where CDIAL also lists the inherited Marathi kolhāḷ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
गजबजलेला (gajbajlelā) — bustling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for noise, then the Marathi for an uproar.

You'll want to know: गजबजलेला.
गजबजलेला — gajbajlelā — "bustling".
सणाच्या दिवशी बाजार गजबजलेला असतो. saṇācyā divśī bājār gajbajlelā asto. On a festival day the market is bustling.
It agrees like a -ा adjective: गजबजलेली पेठ, गजबजलेलं शहर.

The word, taken apart.
गजबजलेला (gajbajlelā) is the -लेला form of the echo verb gajbajṇe, "to hum with people"; its origin is not settled.
