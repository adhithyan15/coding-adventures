## You'll want to know: قدم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C378-HADAF378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قدم** — *qadam* — "a step".

> اگر آپ نے قدم اٹھایا تو راستہ آسان ہو گا۔
>
> *agar āp ne qadam uṭhāyā to rāstā āsān ho gā.*
>
> If you take the step, the way will get easier.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قدم** (*qadam*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qadam*, "a foot; a step".
