## You'll want to know: la cathédrale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C439-CLOCHERS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la cathédrale** — "the cathedral".

> La cathédrale est au centre de la vieille ville.
>
> *The cathedral is in the middle of the old town.*

The bishop's church.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cathédrale* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *(ecclēsia) cathedrālis* 'church with a bishop's chair', from *cathedra* 'chair', from Greek *kathédra* (Lewis & Short).
