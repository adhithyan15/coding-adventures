## You'll want to know: comparar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C599-INVESTIGA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**comparar** — "to compare".

> Comparamos los resultados de dos grupos.
>
> *We compared the results of two groups.*

*Comparar con* — to compare with.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comparar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *comparare* 'to put together, to match', built on *par* 'equal' (EtymDB).
