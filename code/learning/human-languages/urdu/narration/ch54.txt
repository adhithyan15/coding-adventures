Urdu, chapter 54: Rice, Lentils, Fruit, Apple, Banana.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چاول (chāval) — rice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for meat, then the Urdu for a fish.

You'll want to know: چاول.
چاول — chāval — "rice".

The word, taken apart.
چاول (chāval) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cāvala, "husked rice", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4749). CDIAL suggests, with a "possibly", that it shares a non-Aryan origin with Sanskrit taṇḍula, "rice".

The word, taken apart.
دال (dāl) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dāla, "kind of grain, split peas" (CDIAL 6309). CDIAL asks whether it is the same word as dāla, "splitting".
