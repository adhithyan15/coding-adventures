## You'll want to know: সভাপতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C360-BOKTRITA360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সভাপতি** — *sôbhāpôti* — "a chairperson; the chair (of a meeting)".

> সভাপতি সভা শুরু করলেন।
>
> *sôbhāpôti sôbhā shuru kôrlen.*
>
> The chair opened the meeting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সভাপতি** (*sôbhāpôti*) is Sanskrit *sabhāpati*, the head of an assembly (Monier-Williams), from সভা with *pati* 'master'.
