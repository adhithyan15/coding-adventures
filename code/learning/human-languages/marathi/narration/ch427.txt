Marathi, chapter 427: Interest and Arrears.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
व्याज (vyāj) — interest on money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sum of money, then the Marathi for to deposit.

You'll want to know: व्याज.
व्याज — vyāj — "interest on money".
मी दर महिन्याला कर्जावर व्याज भरतो. mī dar mahinyālā karjāvar vyāj bharto. Every month I pay interest on the loan.
Neuter.
व्याज (vyāj) मिळणे, to earn interest; व्याज (vyāj) भरणे, to pay it.

The word, taken apart.
व्याज (vyāj) is the Marathi word for interest on money; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 6.
थकबाकी (thakbākī) — arrears.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to deposit, then the Marathi for interest on money.

You'll want to know: थकबाकी.
थकबाकी — thakbākī — "arrears".
त्याच्यावर विजेच्या बिलाची थकबाकी आहे. tyācyāvar vijecyā bilācī thakbākī āhe. He has arrears on his electricity bill.
Feminine.
Money owed and overdue; the person owing takes -वर.

The word, taken apart.
थकबाकी (thakbākī) is built from the stem of थकणे, "to tire, to fall behind", and बाकी (bākī), "what remains", a loan usually traced to Arabic bāqī.

Lesson 3 of 6.
उधार (udhār) — on credit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for interest on money, then the Marathi for arrears.

You'll want to know: उधार.
उधार — udhār — "on credit".
तो वाण्याकडून उधार सामान घेतो. to vāṇyākaḍūn udhār sāmān gheto. He buys groceries on credit from the grocer.
उधार (udhār) घेणे, to take on credit; उधार (udhār) देणे, to give on credit.

The word, taken apart.
उधार (udhār) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi udhār, "buying and selling on credit", under Sanskrit uddhāra, "raising; a loan" (CDIAL 2018).

Lesson 4 of 6.
बाकी (bākī) — the remainder; the rest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for arrears, then the Marathi for on credit.

You'll want to know: बाकी.
बाकी — bākī — "the remainder; the rest".
मी अर्धे पैसे दिले, बाकी उद्या देईन. mī ardhe paise dile, bākī udyā deīn. I paid half the money; I will give the rest tomorrow.
Feminine.
Also the balance of an account: बाकी (bākī) किती?

The word, taken apart.
बाकी (bākī) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic bāqī, "remaining".

Lesson 5 of 6.
फेडणे (pheḍṇe) — to repay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for on credit, then the Marathi for the remainder.

You'll want to know: फेडणे.
फेडणे — pheḍṇe — "to repay".
त्याने पाच वर्षांत कर्ज फेडलं. tyāne pāc varṣānt karja pheḍlaṁ. He repaid the loan in five years.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने फेडलं, tyāne pheḍlaṁ.
कर्ज फेडणे (pheḍṇe), to pay off a debt; the debt is the object.

The word, taken apart.
फेडणे (pheḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi pheḍṇẽ, "to loosen", under sphiṭati, "gets loose" (CDIAL 13838); paying off a debt is its everyday sense today.
