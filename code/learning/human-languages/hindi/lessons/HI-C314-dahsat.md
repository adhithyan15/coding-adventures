## You'll want to know: दहशत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C314-AFVAH314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दहशत** — *dahśat* — "terror; panic".

> अफ़वाह से लोगों में दहशत फैल गई।
>
> *afvāh se logoṁ meṁ dahśat phail gaī.*
>
> The rumour spread panic among the people.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दहशत** (*dahśat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dahsha*, "bewilderment, alarm". It is a fear that spreads through a whole place.
