Latin, chapter 94: New, Old, Light, Fast, Slow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
novus, nova, novum — new.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for short, then the Latin for high; deep.

You'll want to know: novus, nova, novum.
novus, nova, novum — "new". English novel.

What you've built.
One more word for new, old, light, fast, slow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: novus]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
