## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಬಂಧ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C409-PRABANDHA409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಬಂಧ** — *prabandha* — "an essay".

> ನಾನು ನಮ್ಮ ಊರಿನ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಬಂಧ ಬರೆದೆ.
>
> *nānu namma ūrina bagge ondu prabandha barede.*
>
> I wrote an essay about our town.

Also a thesis or a paper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಬಂಧ** (*prabandha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prabandha*, "a connection, a continuous composition" (CDIAL 8695).
