## You'll want to know: ਚਬਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C325-BETTER325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਬਾਉਣਾ** — *chabāuṇā* — "to chew".

> ਬੱਚਾ ਰੋਟੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਚਬਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *bachchā roṭī haulī chabāundā hai.*
>
> The child chews his bread slowly.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਚਬਾਈ, *maiṁ roṭī haulī chabāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਬਾਉਣਾ** (*chabāuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *carvati*, "chews"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cabāuṇā* under it (CDIAL 4711).
