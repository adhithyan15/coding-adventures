## You'll want to know: अपव्ययः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C432-VYAYA432-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अपव्ययः** — *apavyayaḥ* — "wasteful spending".

> अपव्ययः उचितः न अस्ति।
>
> *apavyayaḥ ucitaḥ na asti.*
>
> Wasteful spending is not right.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपव्ययः** (*apavyayaḥ*) is built from *apa*, "away, wrongly", and *vyaya*, "expense"; MW gives *apavyaya* as prodigality (MW).
