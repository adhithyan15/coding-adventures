## You'll want to know: la convivencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C610-CONVIVIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la convivencia** — "the living together, the coexistence".

> La convivencia en el barrio es buena.
>
> *People get on well together in the neighbourhood.*

*Las normas de convivencia* — the rules for living together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convivencia* is built on *convivir*, a learned borrowing from Latin *convivere* 'to live together', from *vivere* 'to live' (EtymDB).
