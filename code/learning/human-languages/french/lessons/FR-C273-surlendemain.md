## You'll want to know: le surlendemain
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C273-VEILLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le surlendemain** — "two days later, the day after next".

> Il est arrivé le lundi et il est reparti le surlendemain.
>
> *He arrived on the Monday and left again two days later.*

*Sur* + *lendemain*: one day beyond the next day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*surlendemain* is built on *lendemain* with *sur* "over, beyond"; *demain* is inherited from Latin *dē māne* "from the morning" (Lewis & Short).
