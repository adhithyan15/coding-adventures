## You'll want to know: planchar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C777-ENCHUFAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**planchar** — "to iron".

> Si tienes tiempo, plancha la camisa.
>
> *If you have time, iron the shirt.*

*La plancha* is the iron; *a la plancha* is grilled on a hot plate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*planchar* is built on *plancha* 'a flat iron plate', which comes from French *planche* 'a board' (EtymDB), from Latin *planca* 'a plank' (Lewis & Short).
