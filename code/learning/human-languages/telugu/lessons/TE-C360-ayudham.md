## You'll want to know: ఆయుధం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C360-AYUDHAM360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆయుధం** — *āyudhaṁ* — "a weapon".

> దొంగల దగ్గర ఆయుధాలు దొరికాయి.
>
> *doṅgala daggara āyudhālu dorikāyi.*
>
> Weapons were found on the robbers.

Plural: ఆయుధాలు, *āyudhālu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆయుధం** (*āyudhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āyudha*, "a weapon" (CDIAL 1291), from *yudh*, "to fight", the root of యుద్ధం.
