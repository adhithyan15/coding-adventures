## Script — two dots over a shape like 大
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-TOU-HEAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 头

**Two pieces: two dots, then a shape like 大.**

**Two dots,** one under the other, each falling to the right, a little left of the middle.

**Then three strokes, almost 大:** a long bar across, then a long stroke from the top falling down to the left. The last stroke is not the long stroke of 大 but a short dot at the lower right.

2 + 3 = five.
