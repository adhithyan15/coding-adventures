## You'll want to know: પૂર્વ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C414-EAST414-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂર્વ** — *pūrva* — "the east".

> સૂરજ પૂર્વમાં ઊગે છે.
>
> *sūraj pūrvamā̃ ūge che.*
>
> The sun rises in the east.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂર્વ** (*pūrva*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pūrva*, "in front, before", and so "eastern" (Monier-Williams): facing the sunrise, the east is in front of you.
