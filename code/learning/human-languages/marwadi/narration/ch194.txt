Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 194: Earning and Spending.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कमावणो (kamāvṇo) — to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for in cash, then the Marwadi for to pay.

You'll want to know: कमावणो.
कमावणो — kamāvṇo — "to earn".
मां हाट मांय पैसा कमावै है। mā̃ hāṭ mā̃y paisā kamāvai hai. Mother earns money at the weekly market.
Past (I …): म्हैं सो रुपिया कमाया, mhaĩ so rupiyā kamāyā.

The word, taken apart.
कमावणो (kamāvṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed karmāpayati, "works, earns". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi kamānā, "earns" (CDIAL 2897).

Lesson 2 of 5.
खरचणो (kharchṇo) — to spend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to pay, then the Marwadi for to earn.

You'll want to know: खरचणो.
खरचणो — kharchṇo — "to spend".
हूं बाजार मांय घणा पैसा खरचूं हूं। hū̃ bājār mā̃y ghaṇā paisā kharchū̃ hū̃. I spend a lot of money at the market.
Past (I …): म्हैं दस रुपिया खरच्या, mhaĩ das rupiyā kharchyā.

The word, taken apart.
खरचणो (kharchṇo) is a verb made on kharch, "expense", which is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic kharj, "expenditure".

Lesson 5 of 5.
उधार (udhār) — credit; a loan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a salary, then the Marwadi for a profit.

You'll want to know: उधार.
उधार — udhār — "credit; a loan".
अठै उधार कोनी। aṭhai udhār konī. No credit here.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उधार (udhār) is inherited from Sanskrit uddhāra, "raising; a loan". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi udhār, "debt", and Gujarati udhāro, "credit account" (CDIAL 2018).
