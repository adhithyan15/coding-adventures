## You'll want to know: আতঙ্ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C441-ATONKO441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**আতঙ্ক** — *ātôngkô* — "terror; panic".

> আগুন দেখে গ্রামে আতঙ্ক ছড়াল।
>
> *āgun dekhe grāme ātôngkô chhôṛālô.*
>
> Seeing the fire, terror spread through the village.

আতঙ্কে, in terror: আতঙ্কে কাঁপা, to shake with terror.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আতঙ্ক** (*ātôngkô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ātaṅka*, which Monier-Williams gives for sickness and pain and also for fear; Bengali keeps the fear.
