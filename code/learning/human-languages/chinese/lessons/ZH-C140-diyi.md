## You'll want to know: dìyī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C140-ORDER140-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dìyī* — "first".

> *zhè shì wǒ de dìyī ge shǒujī* — This is my first phone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

一 keeps its first tone here: in a number that gives an order, yī does not change its tone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìyī* is built from *dì* 'order, rank' and 一 'one', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dejH* and *'jit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
