## You'll want to know: لص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C300-JARIMA300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**لص** — *liṣṣ* — "a thief".

> دخل لص البيت في الليل.
>
> *dakhala liṣṣ al-bayt fī al-layl.*
>
> A thief got into the house at night.

Plural: لصوص, *luṣūṣ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لص** (*liṣṣ*) is filed by Lane under the root *l-ṣ-ṣ*, with the plural *luṣūṣ*. It was borrowed from Greek *lēistēs*, a robber (EtymDB); the path it took into Arabic is not settled.
