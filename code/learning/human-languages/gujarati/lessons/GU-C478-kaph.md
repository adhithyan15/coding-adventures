## You'll want to know: કફ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C478-KAPH478-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કફ** — *kaph* — "phlegm".

> શરદીને લીધે મારી છાતીમાં કફ છે.
>
> *śardīne līdhe mārī chātīmā̃ kaph che.*
>
> My chest is full of phlegm because of the cold.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કફ** (*kaph*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kapha*, "phlegm", one of the three humours of the old medicine, as Monier-Williams records it.
