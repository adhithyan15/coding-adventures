Punjabi, chapter 179: Morning Open, Afternoon Closed.
7 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਸਮਾਂ (samaa) — one word for time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the familiar schedule once: ਅਮਨ ਨਾਲ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੱਕ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੋ। ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਘਰ ਆਉਣਾ।.

Grammar Lens: one schedule noun.
ਸਮਾਂ (samaa) — samā̃ — time.
This lesson gives one useful schedule word. Keep it small: it does not add a new clock frame.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: samā̃; YOU SAY: ਸਮਾਂ (samaa)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਸਮਾਂ (samaa)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say time. (ਸਮਾਂ (samaa).)
Source: NCERT, Punjabi activity material, uses ਸਮਾਂ (samaa) in day-part contexts.


Lesson 2 of 7.
ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (savere atth vaje) — place the morning before the hour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਸਮਾਂ (samaa). Then retrieve ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ.

Grammar Lens: morning precedes the hour.
ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (savere atth vaje) — savere aṭṭh vaje — at eight in the morning.
Put ਸਵੇਰੇ before the familiar whole-hour phrase. This lesson owns that bounded day-part use.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: savere aṭṭh vaje; YOU SAY: ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (savere atth vaje)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ (savere atth vaje)]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say at eight in the morning, then say time.
Source: UK Government, Punjabi asylum accommodation guide, uses ਸਵੇਰੇ in public-hours language.
