## You'll want to know: رافق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C440-TIDHKAR440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رافق** — *rāfaqa* — "to accompany".

> رافقتنا جدتي في الرحلة.
>
> *rāfaqatnā jaddatī fī ar-riḥla.*
>
> My grandmother came with us on the trip.

Present: يرافق, *yurāfiq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رافق** (*rāfaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-f-q*, "to be gentle; a companion" (Lane). It is Form III, "to be a companion to"; a *rafīq* is a companion.
