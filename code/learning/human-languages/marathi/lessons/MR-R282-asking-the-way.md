# Review — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C281-AYLA-PAHIJE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-U-NAYE-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I must go to the station" and "you shouldn't go at night". (*malā sṭeśanlā jāylā pāhije*, *tū rātrī jāū nayes*.) Then say **ज्ञान** and **ऋतू**, and name the letter each one holds: **ञ** and **ऋ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-HOW-FAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-LANDMARK-01, MR-LEX-C128-LOC128-04, MR-LEX-C271-VASTU271-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Please, how do I get to the airport?]
- [YOU SAY: How far is it from here? Is it near?]
- [YOU SAY: Go straight, then turn left at the square.]
- [YOU SAY: The bus station is near the shop, opposite the temple.]
- [YOU SAY: the way from your door to the nearest shop, in three sentences]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-HOW-FAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-LANDMARK-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "How do I get to …?" (**… ला कसं जायचं?**) "Turn"? (**वळा**.) "Near the shop"? (**दुकानाच्या जवळ**.) Which letters did **ऋतू** and **ज्ञान** bring? (**ऋ** and **ञ**.)
