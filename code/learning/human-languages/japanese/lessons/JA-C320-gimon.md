## You'll want to know: ぎもん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C320-TACHIBA320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎもん** — *gimon* — "a doubt; a question in one's mind".

> **ぎもんが あるので、しつもんします。** — *gimon ga aru node, shitsumon shimasu* — "I have a doubt, so I will ask a question."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎもん** (*gimon*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gi*, "doubt" (Middle Chinese *ngi*) and *mon*, "question" (*mjunH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
