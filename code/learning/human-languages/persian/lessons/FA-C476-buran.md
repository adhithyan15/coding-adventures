## You'll want to know: بوران
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C476-KULAK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بوران** (*burân*) — "a blizzard (with driving snow)".

> در بوران راننده ها جاده را نمیدیدند.
>
> *Dar burân rânande-hâ jâdde râ nemididand.*
>
> In the blizzard the drivers couldn't make out the road.

Close in sense to *kulâk*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*burân* is usually traced to Turkic *buran* 'a snowstorm'. EtymDB lists an Arabic *būrān* without a gloss, so the line is hedged.
