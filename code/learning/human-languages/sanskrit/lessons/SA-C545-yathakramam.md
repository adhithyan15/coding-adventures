## You'll want to know: यथाक्रमम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C545-ANUKRAMA545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**यथाक्रमम्** — *yathākramam* — "in due order".

> यदि सर्वे यथाक्रमम् आगच्छन्ति, तर्हि शब्दः न भवति।
>
> *yadi sarve yathākramam āgacchanti, tarhi śabdaḥ na bhavati.*
>
> If everyone comes in turn, there is no noise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यथाक्रमम्** (*yathākramam*) is built from *yathā*, "as", and *krama*, "a step, an order", from the root √kram; MW gives *yathākramam* as according to order, in due succession (MW).
