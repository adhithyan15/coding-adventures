# ತಂಗು (taṅgu) — to stay overnight; to halt

## You'll want to know: ತಂಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C458-YATRE458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಂಗು** — *taṅgu* — "to stay overnight; to halt".

> ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವು ಒಂದು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ತಂಗಿದೆವು.
>
> *dāriyalli nāvu ondu rātri haḷḷiyalli taṅgidevu.*
>
> On the way we stayed one night in a village.

Past (I …): ತಂಗಿದೆ, *taṅgide*.

A stop on a journey; ಇರು is to stay in general.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಂಗು** (*taṅgu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3014 lists Kannada *taṅgu*, "to stop, to stay, to sojourn, to lodge".
