## You'll want to know: शतरंज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C526-FURSAT526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शतरंज** — *śatrañj* — "chess".

> दादा जी हर शाम अपने दोस्त के साथ शतरंज खेलते हैं।
>
> *Dādā jī har śām apne dost ke sāth śatrañj khelte haiṁ.*
>
> My grandfather plays chess with his friend every evening.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शतरंज** (*śatrañj*) is usually traced to Persian *shatranj*, which goes back to Sanskrit *caturaṅga*, "having four limbs", the four arms of an army, and already "a kind of chess" (CDIAL 4596).
