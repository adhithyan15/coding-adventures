Lesson 2 of 7.
Add one bounded association to room — a room with a window.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਖਿੜਕੀ, then ਕਮਰਾ.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਕਮਰਾ (khirkii vaalaa kamraa) — khiṛkī vālā kamrā — a room with a window.
Here noun ਖਿੜਕੀ plus ਵਾਲਾ forms an adjective phrase describing the masculine singular noun ਕਮਰਾ. Learn this one direct room phrase before any broader pattern.

Grammar Lens: source and scope boundary.
Gill and Gleason describe noun plus ਵਾਲਾ as an adjective phrase and show the masculine adjective direct–oblique pattern. This chapter follows only one room contrast. It is not a rule for every noun or postposition. The earlier ਵਾਲੀ, “earring,” word lesson owns a separate lexical homograph, not a grammatical form licensed by that old word lesson. Source: Harjeet Singh Gill and Henry A. Gleason, Jr., A Reference Grammar of Punjabi (1969), pp. 71–72 and 107, adjective phrases.

Lesson 4 of 7.
Change the association inside one location frame — in the room with a window.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਕਮਰਾ (khirkii vaalaa kamraa), then ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਕਮਰਾ (khirkii vaalaa kamraa) — khiṛkī vālā kamrā — a room with a window.
ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ (khirkii vaale kamre vich) — khiṛkī vāle kamre vicc — in the room with a window.
In this second phrase, ਵਾਲੇ is masculine singular oblique. It agrees with masculine singular oblique ਕਮਰੇ before ਵਿੱਚ. Here ਵਾਲੇ is not a plural or honorific form. This chapter does not extend the pattern to every noun or postposition.
