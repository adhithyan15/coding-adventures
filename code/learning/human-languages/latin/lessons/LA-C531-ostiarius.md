## You'll want to know: ostiārius, ostiāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C531-IANI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ostiārius, ostiāriī** — "a porter, doorman".

> Ostiārius canem ad portam habet.
>
> *The porter keeps a dog at the gate.*

Masculine, second declension, from *ostium* — door, entrance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ostiārius** is built on *ostium* "door, entrance" with *-ārius* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), and EtymDB relates *ostium* to *ōs* "mouth".
