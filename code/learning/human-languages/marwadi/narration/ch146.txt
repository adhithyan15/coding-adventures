You'll want to know: चमकीलो.
चमकीलो — chamkīlo — "shiny".

The word, taken apart.
चमकीलो (chamkīlo) is built with -īlo on chamak, "shine". CDIAL lists Hindi camaknā, "to glitter", under camakka, "sudden movement" (CDIAL 4676).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सांत (sānt) — calm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for orange, then the Marwadi for shiny.

You'll want to know: सांत.
सांत — sānt — "calm".

The word, taken apart.
सांत (sānt) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit śānta, "abated, calm", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped (CDIAL 12390). Marwadi says the ś as s.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बावळो (bāvaḷo) — silly, foolish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for calm, then the Marwadi for clever.

You'll want to know: बावळो.
बावळो — bāvaḷo — "silly, foolish".

The word, taken apart.
बावळो (bāvaḷo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vātula, "affected by wind-disease, crazy". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11504); the match is through Hindi bāulā and Gujarati vāulũ. CDIAL thinks the sense "mad" may come from Middle Indo-Aryan vāula, "perplexed", from Sanskrit vyākula.
