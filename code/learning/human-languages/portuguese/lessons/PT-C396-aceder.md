## You'll want to know: aceder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C396-CONTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aceder** — "to access, to log in (to)".

> Não consigo aceder à minha conta.
>
> *I cannot log in to my account.*

European Portuguese *aceder a*; in Brazil *acessar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aceder* comes from Latin *accēdere* "to come near, to approach" (EtymDB), built on *cēdere* "to go" (Lewis & Short).
