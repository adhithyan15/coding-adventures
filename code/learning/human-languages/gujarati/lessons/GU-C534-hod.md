## You'll want to know: હોડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C534-JUGAR534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**હોડ** — *hoḍ* — "a wager".

> જો આજે વરસાદ પડશે તો હું હોડ હારી જઈશ.
>
> *jo āje varsād paḍśe to hũ hoḍ hārī jaīś.*
>
> If it rains today, I'll lose the bet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હોડ** (*hoḍ*) comes from *\*hōḍḍa*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a wager" (CDIAL 14175).
