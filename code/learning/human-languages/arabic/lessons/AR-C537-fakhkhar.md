## You'll want to know: فخار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C537-HIWAYA537-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فخار** — *fakhkhār* — "pottery".

> صنعت أختي إناء من الفخار.
>
> *ṣanaʿat ukhtī ināʾ min al-fakhkhār.*
>
> My sister made a pot out of clay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فخار** (*fakhkhār*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-kh-r*, "to boast, to glory in" (Lane). Lane gives fakhkhār, under the same root as fakhūr, proud, as baked pottery and baked clay.
