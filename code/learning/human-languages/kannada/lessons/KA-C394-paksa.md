## You'll want to know: ಪಕ್ಷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C394-RAJAKIYA394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಕ್ಷ** — *pakṣa* — "a political party; a side".

> ಚುನಾವಣೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾಲ್ಕು ಪಕ್ಷಗಳು ಸ್ಪರ್ಧಿಸಿದವು.
>
> *cunāvaṇeyalli nālku pakṣagaḷu spardhisidavu.*
>
> Four parties contested the election.

Plural: ಪಕ್ಷಗಳು, *pakṣagaḷu*.

Also a side: ಯಾರ ಪಕ್ಷ?, whose side?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಕ್ಷ** (*pakṣa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pakṣa*, "a wing, a side" (CDIAL 7627).
