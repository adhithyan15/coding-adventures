## You'll want to know: cōnor, cōnārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C393-STATUO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnor, cōnārī** — "to try, to attempt".

> Cōnābar dormīre, sed nōn poteram.
>
> *I was trying to sleep, but I could not.*

Deponent: *cōnor, cōnārī, cōnātus sum* + infinitive. *Cōnātus*, an attempt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnor* is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*kōnāō* "to try, to exert oneself" (EtymDB); its deeper origin is not settled.
