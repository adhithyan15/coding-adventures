Marathi, chapter 108: Black, White, Brown, Pink, Young.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
काळा (kāḷā) — black.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for red, then the Marathi for blue.

You'll want to know: काळा.
काळा — kāḷā — "black".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kāḷā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kāḷā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kāḷā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for red, then the Marathi for blue, then say kāḷā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does काळा (kāḷā) mean? ("Black".) Say it once more.
