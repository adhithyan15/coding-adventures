## You'll want to know: ориентир
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C438-UKAZATEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ориентир** (*oriyentír*) — "a landmark (to steer by)".

> Хороший ориентир — церковь на площади.
>
> *Khoróshiy oriyentír — tsérkov na plóshchadi.*
>
> A good landmark is the church on the square.

Masculine. Also a guideline: *ориентир для работы*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ориентир* is built on *ориентировать* 'to orient', usually traced to French *orienter* (EtymDB), on Latin *oriēns* 'the east' (Lewis & Short).
