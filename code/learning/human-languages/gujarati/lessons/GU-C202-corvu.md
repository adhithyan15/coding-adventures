## You'll want to know: ચોરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C202-THEFT202-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોરવું** — *corvũ* — "to steal".

> **કોઈએ મારું પાકીટ ચોર્યું.** — *koīe mārũ pākīṭ coryũ* — "Someone stole my wallet."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોરવું** (*corvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *cōráyati*, "steals", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to steal" (CDIAL 4933).
