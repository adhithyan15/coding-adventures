## You'll want to know: मंजूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C272-PHAGAT272-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मंजूर** — *mañjūr* — "agreed".

> यो भाव मंजूर है।
>
> *yo bhāv mañjūr hai.*
>
> This price is agreed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंजूर** (*mañjūr*) is usually traced, through Persian *manzūr*, to Arabic *manẓūr*, "looked at", hence "approved".
