## You'll want to know: स्वेदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C438-NIDAGHA438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वेदः** — *svedaḥ* — "sweat".

> आतपे कृषकस्य मुखे स्वेदः अस्ति।
>
> *ātape kṛṣakasya mukhe svedaḥ asti.*
>
> In the sunshine there is sweat on the farmer's face.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वेदः** (*svedaḥ*) comes from the root √svid, "to sweat"; MW gives *sveda* as sweating, sweat, and also warmth (MW).
