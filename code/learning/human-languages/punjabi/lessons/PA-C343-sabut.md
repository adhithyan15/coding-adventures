## You'll want to know: ਸਬੂਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C343-MIXUP343-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਬੂਤ** — *sabūt* — "proof; evidence".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ ਕੋਈ ਸਬੂਤ ਹੈ?
>
> *kī tuhāḍe kol koī sabūt hai?*
>
> Do you have any proof?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਬੂਤ** (*sabūt*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *thubūt*, "firmness".
