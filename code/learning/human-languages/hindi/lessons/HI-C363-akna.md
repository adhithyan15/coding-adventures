## You'll want to know: आँकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C363-ADHYAYAN363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आँकना** — *ā̃knā* — "to assess, to estimate (a value)".

> बैंक ने घर की क़ीमत आँकी।
>
> *baiṅk ne ghar kī qīmat ā̃kī.*
>
> The bank assessed the value of the house.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नुक़सान आँका, *usne nuqsān ā̃kā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आँकना** (*ā̃knā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *aṅkayati*, "marks" (CDIAL 104).
