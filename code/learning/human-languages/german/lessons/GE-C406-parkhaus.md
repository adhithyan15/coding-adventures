## You'll want to know: das Parkhaus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C406-PARKEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Parkhaus** — "the multi-storey car park (US: parking garage)".

> Das Parkhaus am Bahnhof ist am Wochenende voll.
>
> *The car park at the station is full at the weekend.*

Plural: *die Parkhäuser*. An underground one is *die Tiefgarage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Parkhaus* is built from *parken* 'to park' and *Haus* 'house', inherited from Proto-Germanic *hūsą* (EtymDB).
