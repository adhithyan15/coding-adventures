Gujarati, chapter 34: People, Paper, and a Book.
8 lessons. You can do the first 6 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
છોકરો (chhokro) — hear boy.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the Gujarati words for brother and for sister. Now listen twice: chhokro. It means boy — younger than the bhāī you already know, and ending in the masculine -o.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "chhokro"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "chhokro" twice, once slowly and once at speed]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does chhokro mean? (Boy.)
Source: Cambridge: boy.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which names a child rather than a sibling: bhāī or chhokro?

Lesson 7 of 8.
Kāgaḷ reaches the page — paper, now written with signs taught long before this chapter.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
Before anything is uncovered, say the Gujarati word for paper.

Guided Practice.
Uncover કાગળ (kāgaḷ) once. Four pieces, none new: ક from the doorway nine, the ા stroke from the greeting, and ગ and the curled ળ from the forms taught before the verbs. Watch ગ and ળ side by side; they are the two most easily confused shapes here. Name the pieces aloud in order, then read the whole word once. This is the only word of the chapter the hand is asked for; the other four stay in the ear for now, which is the gloss-first order rather than an omission.
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Read this word aloud and give its meaning: કાગળ (kāgaḷ).

Writing — guided copy, model in view.
Keep the model in view and copy કાગળ (kāgaḷ) once. Repair one shape if it needs it; do not start a row.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
With the model in view, copy paper once.

Guided Practice — the first map at its fourth window.
The first three-place map was read and written more than eighty positions ago, which is its fourth spacing window; the river reached the page eight lessons ago, which is its second. Both rows here are cold reading.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
