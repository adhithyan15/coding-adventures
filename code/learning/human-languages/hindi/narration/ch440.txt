Hindi, chapter 440: Clouds Gather and Clear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धूल (dhūl) — dust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a degree, then the Hindi for to scorch.

You'll want to know: धूल.
धूल — dhūl — "dust".
आँधी के बाद हर चीज़ पर धूल थी। ā̃dhī ke bād har cīz par dhūl thī. After the dust storm everything was covered in dust.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
धूल (dhūl) comes from Sanskrit dhūli, "dust, powder", which CDIAL files under dhūḍi (CDIAL 6835) with Hindi dhūl, "dust".

You'll want to know: घिरना.
घिरना — ghirnā — "to be surrounded; to gather round".
बाढ़ में हमारा गाँव पानी से घिर गया। bāṛh meṁ hamārā gā̃v pānī se ghir gayā. In the flood our village was cut off by water.
Past (it …): वह मैदान पेड़ों से घिरा, vah maidān peṛoṁ se ghirā.

The word, taken apart.
घिरना (ghirnā) goes back to ghir, "go round", a form CDIAL reconstructs and thinks may come from Dravidian (CDIAL 4474); it lists Hindi ghirnā, "to be surrounded".

Lesson 3 of 5.
थमना (thamnā) — to let up; to stop (of rain or wind).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for dust, then the Hindi for to be surrounded.

You'll want to know: थमना.
थमना — thamnā — "to let up; to stop (of rain or wind)".
दो घंटे बाद बारिश थमी। do ghaṇṭe bād bāriś thamī. The rain let up after two hours.
Past (it …): वह आँधी थम गई, vah ā̃dhī tham gaī.

The word, taken apart.
थमना (thamnā) belongs with Sanskrit stambhate, "supports, arrests" (CDIAL 13683), which lists Hindi thāmnā, "to prop, stop"; थमना is its intransitive: the rain stops itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
छँटना (chãṭnā) — to clear (of clouds); to thin out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be surrounded, then the Hindi for to let up.

You'll want to know: छँटना.
छँटना — chãṭnā — "to clear (of clouds); to thin out".
बारिश के बाद बादल छँट गए। bāriś ke bād bādal chãṭ gae. After the rain the clouds cleared.
Past (it …): वह कोहरा छँट गया, vah kohrā chãṭ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
छँटना (chãṭnā) goes back to chaṇṭ, "scatter", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4970), which lists Hindi chā̃ṭnā, "to sift, select"; clouds that छँट जाते are scattered and thinned away.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उड़ाना (uṛānā) — to blow away; to fly (a kite).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to let up, then the Hindi for to clear (of clouds).

You'll want to know: उड़ाना.
उड़ाना — uṛānā — "to blow away; to fly (a kite)".
हवा ने मेरी टोपी उड़ा दी। havā ne merī ṭopī uṛā dī. The wind blew my cap away.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पतंग उड़ाई, usne patang uṛāī.

The word, taken apart.
उड़ाना (uṛānā) is the causative partner of उड़ना, "to fly", from Sanskrit uḍḍīyate, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697), which lists Hindi uṛānā, "to cause to fly".
