Urdu, chapter 122: Sandal, Button, Tie, Gloves, Hill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چپل (chappal) — a sandal, a slipper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a jacket, then the Urdu for pyjamas.

You'll want to know: چپل.
چپل — chappal — "a sandal, a slipper".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of چپل (chappal) is uncertain, and CDIAL has no entry for it. DEDR, under Dravidian words for "sandal" such as Telugu ceppu, adds "Cf. H. cappal" (DEDR 1963): a comparison, not a settled source.

دستانے — dastāne — "gloves".

The word, taken apart.
دستانے (dastāne) is the plural of dastānā, "a glove", which is usually traced to Persian dastāna, built on dast, "hand".

Lesson 5 of 5.
پہاڑی (pahāṛī) — a hill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a tie, then the Urdu for gloves.

You'll want to know: پہاڑی.
پہاڑی — pahāṛī — "a hill".

The word, taken apart.
پہاڑی (pahāṛī) is pahāṛ, "a mountain", with the ending -ī that makes a smaller one. pahāṛ comes from a reconstructed pāhāḍa, "rock, hill", which CDIAL connects, probably, with Sanskrit pāṣāṇa, "stone" (CDIAL 8141).
