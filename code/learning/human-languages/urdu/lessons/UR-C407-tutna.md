## You'll want to know: ٹوٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C407-MARAMMAT407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوٹنا** — *ṭūṭnā* — "to get broken; to break".

> کھڑکی کا شیشہ ٹوٹ گیا۔
>
> *khiṛkī kā shīshā ṭūṭ gayā.*
>
> The window pane has broken.

Past (it …): وہ گلاس ٹوٹ گیا, *voh glās ṭūṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹوٹنا** (*ṭūṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭūṭnā*, "is broken", under Sanskrit *truṭyati* (CDIAL 6065); **توڑنا**, "to break", is the form for breaking something.
