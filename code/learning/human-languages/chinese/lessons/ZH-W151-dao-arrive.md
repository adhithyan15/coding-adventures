## Script — 至 on the left, then the blade of 前
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DAO-ARRIVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 到

**Two pieces, and both have parts you write.**

**至, six strokes**, on the left: a bar across the top; a stroke that falls to the left and turns right; a dot at its end; then 土, as at the foot of 在: a short bar, a vertical through it, and the bottom bar, rising a little to the right.

**刂, two strokes**, on the right, as on the right of 前: a short vertical, then a long vertical that hooks to the left at its foot.

6 + 2 = eight.
