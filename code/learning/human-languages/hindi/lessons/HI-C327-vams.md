## You'll want to know: वंश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C327-VANSH327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वंश** — *vaṁś* — "a family line; a dynasty".

> उस राजा का वंश तीन सौ साल चला।
>
> *us rājā kā vaṁś tīn sau sāl calā.*
>
> That king's dynasty lasted three hundred years.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वंश** (*vaṁś*) is Sanskrit *vaṁśa* (Monier-Williams), a bamboo and then a lineage, one joint growing out of the last.
