## You'll want to know: भर देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C352-ULLEKH352-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भर देणे** — *bhar deṇe* — "to emphasize; to stress".

> शिक्षकांनी वाचनावर भर दिला.
>
> *śikṣakānnī vācnāvar bhar dilā.*
>
> The teacher stressed reading.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भर दिला, *tyāne bhar dilā*.

The point stressed takes -वर: वाचनावर भर देणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भर देणे** (*bhar deṇe*) joins भर, "weight, load", inherited from Sanskrit *bhara*, "carrying, weight" (CDIAL 9393), to देणे, "to give": to put weight on a point.
