## You'll want to know: અવતરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C495-SAGARBHA495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અવતરવું** — *avtarvũ* — "to come into the world; to be born".

> ગઈકાલે રાતે અમારા ઘરે દીકરી અવતરી.
>
> *gaīkāle rāte amārā ghare dīkrī avtarī.*
>
> Last night a baby daughter was born in our home.

Past (it …): તે બાળક અવતર્યું, *te bāḷak avtaryũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવતરવું** (*avtarvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *ava-tṛ*, "to come down, to descend" (Monier-Williams), the verb behind *avatāra*: a child who comes down into the world.
