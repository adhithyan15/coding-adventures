## You'll want to know: ہتھکڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C283-ADALAT283-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ہتھکڑی** — *hathkaṛī* — "handcuffs".

> چور کے ہاتھوں میں ہتھکڑیاں تھیں۔
>
> *chor ke hāthoṅ meṅ hathkaṛiyāṅ thīṅ.*
>
> The thief was in handcuffs.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہتھکڑی** (*hathkaṛī*) is built from **ہاتھ**, a hand (CDIAL 14024), and *kaṛī*, a metal ring, which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit *káṭa* (CDIAL 2629): rings for the hands. The first part shortens, as **ہاتھ** does in compounds.
