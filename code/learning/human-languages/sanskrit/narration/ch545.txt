Sanskrit, chapter 545: In the Right Order.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अनुक्रमः (anukramaḥ) — a sequence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for separately, then the Sanskrit for at present.

You'll want to know: अनुक्रमः.
अनुक्रमः — anukramaḥ — "a sequence".
यदि अनुक्रमः सम्यक् अस्ति, तर्हि कार्यं सुकरं भवति। yadi anukramaḥ samyak asti, tarhi kāryaṃ sukaraṃ bhavati. If the order is right, the work is easy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अनुक्रमः (anukramaḥ) is built on anu, "after", and krama, "a step", from the root √kram, "to step"; MW gives anukrama as succession, arrangement, order (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ऋते (ṛte) — except.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a sequence, then the Sanskrit for in due order.

You'll want to know: ऋते.
ऋते — ṛte — "except".
यदि माम् ऋते सर्वे आगच्छन्ति, तर्हि अहं दुःखी भविष्यामि। yadi mām ṛte sarve āgacchanti, tarhi ahaṃ duḥkhī bhaviṣyāmi. If everyone comes but me, I will be sad.

The word, taken apart.
ऋते (ṛte) is the word MW gives for excepting, without, unless, and reports as perhaps a case form of the past participle of the root √ṛ, "to go", so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आदौ (ādau) — at first.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for in due order, then the Sanskrit for except.

You'll want to know: आदौ.
आदौ — ādau — "at first".
यदि आदौ कठिनम् अस्ति, तर्हि अनन्तरं सुकरं भविष्यति। yadi ādau kaṭhinam asti, tarhi anantaraṃ sukaraṃ bhaviṣyati. If it is hard at first, it will be easy later.

The word, taken apart.
आदौ (ādau) is the locative of ādi, "a beginning", which MW records as beginning, commencement; MW gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
आलोचयति (ālocayati) — reviews; considers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for except, then the Sanskrit for at first.

You'll want to know: आलोचयति.
आलोचयति — ālocayati — "reviews; considers".
यदि त्वं योजनाम् आलोचयसि, तर्हि कार्यं सम्यक् भविष्यति। yadi tvaṃ yojanām ālocayasi, tarhi kāryaṃ samyak bhaviṣyati. If you go over the plan, the work will go well.
Past (he …): सः योजनाम् आलोचितवान्, saḥ yojanām ālocitavān.

The word, taken apart.
आलोचयति (ālocayati) is built on ā and the causative of the root √loc, "to see"; MW gives ālocayati as to behold, to consider, to reflect (MW).
