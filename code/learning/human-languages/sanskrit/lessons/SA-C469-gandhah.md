## You'll want to know: गन्धः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C469-INDRIYA469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गन्धः** — *gandhaḥ* — "a smell".

> पुष्पस्य गन्धः मधुरः अस्ति।
>
> *puṣpasya gandhaḥ madhuraḥ asti.*
>
> The flower's smell is sweet.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गन्धः** (*gandhaḥ*) MW gives "smell, odour" and lists nine kinds of smell; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
