## You'll want to know: छाछ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C522-CHAACH522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**छाछ** — *chāch* — "buttermilk".

> गर्मी में हम दोपहर को ठंडी छाछ पीते हैं।
>
> *Garmī meṁ ham dopahar ko ṭhaṇḍī chāch pīte haiṁ.*
>
> In summer we drink cold buttermilk at midday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छाछ** (*chāch*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*chācchī*, "buttermilk", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5012).
