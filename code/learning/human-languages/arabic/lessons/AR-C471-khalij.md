## You'll want to know: خليج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C471-KHALIJ471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خليج** — *khalīj* — "a gulf; a bay".

> سبحنا في خليج صغير.
>
> *sabaḥnā fī khalīj ṣaghīr.*
>
> We swam in a small bay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خليج** (*khalīj*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-l-j*, "to draw away" (Lane). A *khalīj* was a channel drawn off a river, and then an arm of the sea.
