## You'll want to know: xiànxuè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C479-BLOOD479-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànxuè* — "to give blood".

> *wǒ měi nián dōu qù xiànxuè* — I give blood every year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànxuè* is built from *xiàn* 'to offer' and 血 'blood', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xjonH* and *xwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under two readings and glosses *xjonH* 'to present'.
