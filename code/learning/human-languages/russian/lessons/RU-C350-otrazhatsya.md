## You'll want to know: отражаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C350-POSLEDSTVIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**отражаться** (*otrazhátsya*) — "to affect, to tell on; to be reflected".

> Усталость отражается на работе.
>
> *Ustálost otrazháyetsya na rabóte.*
>
> Tiredness tells on your work.

Imperfective; perfective *отразиться*. *Отражаться на* + prepositional — to affect. Literally to be reflected: *в воде отражается луна*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**отражаться** is built from *от-* "back" and the root of *разить* "to strike" (EtymDB): light that strikes a surface and comes back is reflected, and so is a cause that comes back in its effects.
