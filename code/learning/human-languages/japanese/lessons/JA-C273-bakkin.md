## You'll want to know: ばっきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C273-SAIBAN273-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ばっきん** — *bakkin* — "a fine; a penalty".

> **ちちは ばっきんを はらいました。** — *chichi wa bakkin o haraimashita* — "My father paid a fine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばっきん** (*bakkin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *batsu*, "penalty" (Middle Chinese *bjot*) and *kin*, "gold" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The first is shortened from *batsu* to *bak-* before *kin*.
