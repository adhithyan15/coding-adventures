## You'll want to know: نبات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C468-NABAT468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نبات** — *nabāt* — "plant life; a plant".

> رأينا نباتا غريبا بجانب الشلال.
>
> *raʾaynā nabātan gharīban bi-jānib ash-shallāl.*
>
> We saw a strange plant beside the waterfall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نبات** (*nabāt*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-b-t*, "to grow, to sprout" (Lane).
