Persian, chapter 523: At the Theatre.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تئاتر (te'âtr) — a theatre; a play.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be inspired, then the Persian for imagination.

You'll want to know: تئاتر.
تئاتر (te'âtr) — "a theatre; a play".
ما دیشب به تئاتر رفتیم. mâ dishab be te'âtr raftim. We went to the theatre last night.
Both the place and the play.

The word, taken apart.
te'âtr was borrowed from French théâtre, which EtymDB traces through Latin theatrum to Greek theatron, 'a place for viewing' (EtymDB); Lewis & Short gloss theatrum 'a theatre' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
صحنه (sahne) — a stage; a scene.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for imagination, then the Persian for a theatre.

You'll want to know: صحنه.
صحنه (sahne) — "a stage; a scene".
بازیگرها روی صحنه آمدند. bâzigarhâ ru-ye sahne âmadand. The actors came on stage.
Also a scene in a film or a play. Plural sahnehâ.

The word, taken apart.
sahne is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ṣaḥn, 'courtyard, open space'. EtymDB lists it without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
نمایشنامه (namâyeshnâme) — a play (script).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a theatre, then the Persian for a stage.

You'll want to know: نمایشنامه.
نمایشنامه (namâyeshnâme) — "a play (script)".
او یک نمایشنامه کوتاه برای بچه ها نوشت. u yek namâyeshnâme-ye kutâh barâ-ye bachchehâ nevesht. She wrote a short play for children.
namâyesh 'show' + nâme 'writing, letter'. Add -nevis 'writer' for a playwright.

The word, taken apart.
namâyeshnâme is built on native Persian parts: namâyesh, 'show', from the verb namudan, 'to show', and nâme, 'letter, writing'. EtymDB has no entry for the compound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تماشاچی (tamâshâchi) — a spectator, a member of the audience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a stage, then the Persian for a play.

You'll want to know: تماشاچی.
تماشاچی (tamâshâchi) — "a spectator, a member of the audience".
تماشاچی (tamâshâchi) ها برای بازیگرها دست زدند. tamâshâchihâ barâ-ye bâzigarhâ dast zadand. The audience applauded the actors.
tamâshâ 'watching' + the Turkic ending -chi 'one who'.

The word, taken apart.
tamâshâchi is built in Persian on tamâshâ, 'watching', a loan from Arabic, and the ending -chi, 'one who', which was borrowed from Turkic. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نقش (naqsh) — a role, a part; a pattern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a play, then the Persian for a spectator.

You'll want to know: نقش.
نقش (naqsh) — "a role, a part; a pattern".
او در این فیلم نقش یک پدر را دارد. u dar in film naqsh-e yek pedar râ dârad. He plays a father in this film.
Also 'design, pattern' on a carpet or a tile.

The word, taken apart.
naqsh is a loan, usually traced to Arabic naqsh, 'a painting, an engraving'; Persian widened it to a pattern and then to a part in a play. EtymDB has no entry for it.
