## You'll want to know: പ്രായോഗികമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C460-AGOLAM460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രായോഗികമായ** — *prāyōgikamāya* — "practical".

> അവളുടെ ആശയം വളരെ പ്രായോഗികമായിരുന്നു.
>
> *avaḷuṭe āśayaṁ vaḷare prāyōgikamāyirunnu.*
>
> Her idea was very practical.

In practice is പ്രായോഗികമായി.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രായോഗികമായ** (*prāyōgikamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *prāyogika*, "applied, used, applicable" (Monier-Williams), from *prayoga*, "use", with the adjective ending -ആയ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
