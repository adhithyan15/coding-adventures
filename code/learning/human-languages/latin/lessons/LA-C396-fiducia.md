## You'll want to know: fīdūcia, fīdūciae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C396-POLLICEOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**fīdūcia, fīdūciae** — "trust, confidence; a thing held in trust".

> Magnam fīdūciam in amīcīs habēbat.
>
> *He had great trust in his friends.*

*fīdūcia, fīdūciae* f., built on *fīdō*, to trust. In law it is a thing handed over in trust. English *fiduciary*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fīdūcia* is built on *fīdō* "to trust" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the verb behind *fīdus* "faithful". English *fiduciary* comes from it.
