## You'll want to know: きく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きく** — *kiku* — "to listen; to hear; to ask".

> **けさ うちで うたを ききました。** — *kesa uchi de uta o kikimashita* — "This morning I listened to songs at home."

Polite **ききます**, *kikimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きく** (*kiku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
