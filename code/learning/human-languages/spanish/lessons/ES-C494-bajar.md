## You'll want to know: bajar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C494-MANANA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**bajar** — "to go down, to come down; to lower".

> Bajo a comprar el pan.
>
> *I go down to buy the bread.*

The opposite of *subir*. *Bajar el volumen* — to turn the volume down.
