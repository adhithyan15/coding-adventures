## You'll want to know: streuen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C442-GLATTEIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**streuen** — "to grit (a road); to scatter, to sprinkle".

> Die Stadt streut Salz auf die Straßen.
>
> *The town spreads salt on the roads.*

*Gestreut* — gritted. *Zucker auf den Kuchen streuen* — to sprinkle sugar on the cake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*streuen* is inherited from Middle High German *ströuwen* (EtymDB), and English *strew* is a cousin.
