# ಒಂದು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ (ondu ṭikeṭ koḍi) — at the ticket window

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C314-SITU-01, KA-LEX-C217-GUNA217-02, KA-LEX-GIVE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a seat" (*sīṭu*) and "Is this seat free?" (*ī sīṭu khāli ideyā?*). Then "to give". (*koḍu*.)

## You'll want to know: buying a ticket
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C314-BUY-TICKET-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C100-FIND-02, KA-LEX-C07-ONDU-01, KA-LEX-C07-ERADU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C19-VAYASSU-02, KA-LEX-C111-LOC111-02, KA-LEX-C161-KRIYE161-04, KA-LEX-C04-NAALE-SIGONA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01] -->

> **ಮೈಸೂರಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ.**
>
> *maisūrige ondu ṭikeṭ koḍi.* — "One ticket to Mysuru, please."

**ಕೊಡಿ** is the polite "give", like the **ಹೋಗಿ** of directions. The place takes "to": **ಮೈಸೂರಿಗೆ**, as **ಊರಿಗೆ**. For two people: **ಎರಡು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ**.

> **ಎಷ್ಟು ಆಯಿತು?**
>
> *eṣṭu āyitu?* — "How much is it?"

Literally "how much has it come to?", with the **ಆಯಿತು** of "it happened, it became".

> **ನಾನು ನಾಳೆಯ ರೈಲಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ಸೀಟು ಕಾಯ್ದಿರಿಸಿದೆ.**
>
> *nānu nāḷeya railige ondu sīṭu kāydiriside.* — "I booked a seat on tomorrow's train."

**ಕಾಯ್ದಿರಿಸಿದೆ** is the past of **ಕಾಯ್ದಿರಿಸು**, "to book"; **ನಾಳೆಯ** (*nāḷeya*, "tomorrow's"); **ರೈಲಿಗೆ** (*railige*, "for the train").

## You'll want to know: when it leaves
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C314-WHEN-LEAVES-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03, KA-ETYMON-C18-GANTE-01, KA-LEX-C50-LEAVE-04, KA-LEX-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-01, KA-LEX-C32-BAA-01] -->

> **ಬಸ್ ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಹೊರಡುತ್ತದೆ?**
>
> *bas eṣṭu gaṇṭege horaḍuttade?* — "At what time does the bus leave?"

**ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ**, "at how many hours", is "at what time". **ಹೊರಡುತ್ತದೆ** is "it sets out". The answer keeps the same shape:

> **ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಹೊರಡುತ್ತದೆ.**
>
> *āru gaṇṭege horaḍuttade.* — "It leaves at six."

For the arrival, **ಬರುತ್ತದೆ**, "it comes", takes the place of **ಹೊರಡುತ್ತದೆ**.
