## You'll want to know: कमाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C379-MARYADA379-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कमाल** — *kamāl* — "maximum".

> आज कमाल तापमान चाळीस अंश होतं.
>
> *āj kamāl tāpmān cāḷīs aṁś hotaṁ.*
>
> Today's maximum temperature was forty degrees.

In speech also amazing: कमाल आहे!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कमाल** (*kamāl*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *kamāl*, "perfection, completeness": the fullest amount.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
