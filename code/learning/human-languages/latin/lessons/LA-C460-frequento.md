## You'll want to know: frequentō, frequentāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C460-TURBA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**frequentō, frequentāre** — "to throng, to crowd into; to visit often".

> Cīvēs thermās post merīdiem frequentant.
>
> *The citizens throng the baths in the afternoon.*

*frequentō, frequentāre, frequentāvī, frequentātum*, with an accusative. English *frequent* as a verb comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frequentō* is built on *frequēns, frequentis* 'crowded, frequent' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
