## You'll want to know: વિરહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C458-VIRAH458-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વિરહ** — *virah* — "the pain of separation".

> પતિ દૂર ગયો છે, તેથી પત્નીને વિરહનું દુઃખ છે.
>
> *pati dūr gayo che, tethī patnīne virahnũ duḥkh che.*
>
> Her husband has gone far away, so the wife suffers the pain of separation.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિરહ** (*virah*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *viraha*, "abandonment, parting, separation, especially of lovers", as Monier-Williams records it.
