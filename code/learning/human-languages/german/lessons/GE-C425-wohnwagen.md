## You'll want to know: der Wohnwagen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C425-CAMPING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Wohnwagen** — "the caravan (US: trailer)".

> Hinter dem Auto zieht er einen Wohnwagen.
>
> *He is pulling a caravan behind the car.*

Same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wohnwagen* is built from *wohnen* 'to live', inherited from Old High German *wonēn*, and *Wagen* 'cart, vehicle', from Proto-Germanic *wagnaz* (EtymDB).
