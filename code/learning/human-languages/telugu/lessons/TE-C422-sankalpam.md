# సంకల్పం (saṅkalpaṁ) — a resolve; a firm intention

## You'll want to know: సంకల్పం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C422-HAMI422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సంకల్పం** — *saṅkalpaṁ* — "a resolve; a firm intention".

> రోజూ నడవాలని నా సంకల్పం.
>
> *rōjū naḍavālani nā saṅkalpaṁ.*
>
> My resolve is to walk every day.

సంకల్పించు, to resolve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంకల్పం** (*saṅkalpaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁkalpa*, "will, desire" (CDIAL 12819).
