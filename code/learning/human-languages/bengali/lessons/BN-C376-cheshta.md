## You'll want to know: চেষ্টা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C376-LOKKHYO376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**চেষ্টা** — *cheshṭā* — "an attempt; an effort".

> চেষ্টা করলে তুমি পারবে।
>
> *cheshṭā kôrle tumi pārbe.*
>
> If you try, you'll manage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চেষ্টা** (*cheshṭā*) is Sanskrit *ceṣṭā*, effort or exertion (Monier-Williams). With করা it is the everyday verb 'to try'.
