# شبع (shabiʿa) — to be full (from eating)

## You'll want to know: شبع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C517-ADAT517-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شبع** — *shabiʿa* — "to be full (from eating)".

> أكلت كثيرا وشبعت.
>
> *akaltu kathīran wa-shabiʿtu.*
>
> I ate a lot and was full.

Present: يشبع, *yashbaʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شبع** (*shabiʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-b-ʿ*, "to be satisfied with food" (Lane). Shabiʿa is the opposite of jāʿa, to get hungry.
