## You'll want to know: ज्वाळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C292-AAG292-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वाळा** — *jvāḷā* — "a tongue of flame; a blaze".

> इमारतीच्या खिडक्यांतून ज्वाळा बाहेर येत होत्या.
>
> *imārtīcyā khiḍakyāntūn jvāḷā bāher yet hotyā.*
>
> Flames were coming out of the windows of the building.

Feminine.

ज्वाळा keeps its form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज्वाळा** (*jvāḷā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *jvālā*, 'a flame' (Monier-Williams), with Marathi ळ for the Sanskrit l.
