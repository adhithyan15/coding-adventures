## You'll want to know: perficiō, perficere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C297-INCIPIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**perficiō, perficere** — "to complete, to carry out".

> Mūrum novum tandem perfēcērunt.
>
> *At last they completed the new wall.*

*perficiō, perficere, perfēcī, perfectum*: *per* 'through' + *faciō*. English *perfect*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**perficiō** is built on *faciō*, "to make, to do", with *per-*, "through" (EtymDB): to do right through. English *perfect* comes from its participle.
