## HL-C327 — the Hindi child lesson declares its full taught family

After the written-mark terminology repair, **बच्ची** is the highest-ranked
independent modern Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 39
already derives it productively from masculine **बच्चा**, practises the gender
pair with matching possessives, and assesses the feminine form in wrap-up
recall.

The same lesson also teaches, practises, and assesses inherited **बछड़ा**
(“calf”) as the Sanskrit-side cousin of Persian-derived **बच्चा**. The old
headword declared only the masculine child noun, leaving both prominent parts
of the lesson invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Declare **बच्चा / बच्ची / बछड़ा** as the lesson headword, align the title,
gloss, and romanization with the three forms, and regenerate the derived Hindi
artifacts. This is a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new
vocabulary claim.
