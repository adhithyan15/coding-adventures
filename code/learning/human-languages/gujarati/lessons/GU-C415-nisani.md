## You'll want to know: નિશાની
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C415-LANDMARK415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નિશાની** — *niśānī* — "a landmark; a sign".

> અમારા ઘરની નિશાની એક મોટો લીમડો છે.
>
> *amārā gharnī niśānī ek moṭo līmḍo che.*
>
> The landmark for our house is a big neem tree.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિશાની** (*niśānī*) is usually traced to Persian *nišānī*, "a mark, a token", built on *nišān*, "a sign": something you know a place by.
