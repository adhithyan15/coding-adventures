## You'll want to know: चञ्चलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C465-UDYAMIN465-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चञ्चलः** — *cañcalaḥ* — "fickle".

> वानरस्य मनः चञ्चलं भवति।
>
> *vānarasya manaḥ cañcalaṃ bhavati.*
>
> A monkey's mind is fickle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चञ्चलः** (*cañcalaḥ*) is built on the intensive of the root √cal, "to move", the root of **चलति**: moving to and fro; MW gives "unsteady, inconstant" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
