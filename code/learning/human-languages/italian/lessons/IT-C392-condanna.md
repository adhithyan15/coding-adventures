## You'll want to know: la condanna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C392-DIFESA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la condanna** — "the conviction, the sentence".

> La condanna è di due anni.
>
> *The sentence is two years.*

Built on *condannare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condanna* is built on *condannare* "to sentence", inherited from Latin *condemnāre* "to condemn" (Lewis & Short), built on *damnāre*, from *damnum* "loss, penalty".
