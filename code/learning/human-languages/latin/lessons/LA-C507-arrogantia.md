## You'll want to know: arrogantia, arrogantiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C507-SUPB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**arrogantia, arrogantiae** — "arrogance, conceit".

> Arrogantia eius omnibus molesta est.
>
> *His arrogance annoys everyone.*

Feminine, first declension. English *arrogance*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**arrogantia** is built on *arrogāns* "arrogant" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *arrogance* comes from it.
