Malayalam, chapter 395: Ancestors and Customs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പൂർവികൻ (pūrvikan) — an ancestor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a widow, then the Malayalam for heritage.

You'll want to know: പൂർവികൻ.
പൂർവികൻ — pūrvikan — "an ancestor".
ഞങ്ങളുടെ പൂർവികർ കർഷകർ ആയിരുന്നു. ñaṅṅaḷuṭe pūrvikar karṣakar āyirunnu. Our ancestors were farmers.
Plural: പൂർവികർ, pūrvikar.
Usually in the plural, പൂർവികർ, our forefathers.

The word, taken apart.
പൂർവികൻ (pūrvikan) is borrowed from Sanskrit pūrvika, "former, ancient" (Monier-Williams), from pūrva, "before, earlier", with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
പിൻഗാമി (pingāmi) — a successor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for heritage, then the Malayalam for an ancestor.

You'll want to know: പിൻഗാമി.
പിൻഗാമി — pingāmi — "a successor".
മാനേജർ തന്റെ പിൻഗാമിയെ പരിചയപ്പെടുത്തി. mānējar tanṟe pingāmiye paricayappeṭutti. The manager introduced his successor.
പിൻ, after, + -ഗാമി, one who goes: the one who comes after.

The word, taken apart.
പിൻഗാമി (pingāmi) joins the native പിൻ (pin), "behind, after" (DEDR 4205), and Sanskrit gāmin, "going" (CDIAL 4134): the one who goes after.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ആചാരം.
ആചാരം — ācāraṁ — "a custom; a rite".
ഓരോ നാടിനും അതിന്റേതായ ആചാരങ്ങൾ ഉണ്ട്. ōrō nāṭinuṁ atinṟētāya ācāraṅṅaḷ uṇṭŭ. Every land has its own customs.
Plural: ആചാരങ്ങൾ, ācāraṅṅaḷ.
Plural ആചാരങ്ങൾ, the customs and rites of a place.

The word, taken apart.
ആചാരം (ācāraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit ācāra, "mode of action, conduct; custom, usage" (CDIAL 1071).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ഉത്ഭവം (utbhavaṁ) — an origin; a source.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a successor, then the Malayalam for a custom.

You'll want to know: ഉത്ഭവം.
ഉത്ഭവം — utbhavaṁ — "an origin; a source".
ആ പുഴയുടെ ഉത്ഭവം ഒരു മലയിൽ ആണ്. ā puḻayuṭe utbhavaṁ oru malayil āṇŭ. The source of that river is on a hill.
Also the origin of a word or a custom.

The word, taken apart.
ഉത്ഭവം (utbhavaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit udbhava, "origin, birth" (CDIAL 2039), from ud-bhū, "to come up, to arise".

Lesson 5 of 5.
മുത്തശ്ശിക്കഥ (muttaśśikkatha) — a grandmother's tale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a custom, then the Malayalam for an origin.

You'll want to know: മുത്തശ്ശിക്കഥ.
മുത്തശ്ശിക്കഥ — muttaśśikkatha — "a grandmother's tale".
രാത്രി മുത്തശ്ശി ഞങ്ങൾക്ക് ഒരു മുത്തശ്ശിക്കഥ പറഞ്ഞുതന്നു. rātri muttaśśi ñaṅṅaḷkkŭ oru muttaśśikkatha paṟaññutannu. At night grandmother told us an old tale.
A fixed word, not two: also said of a story nobody believes.

The word, taken apart.
മുത്തശ്ശിക്കഥ (muttaśśikkatha) joins മുത്തശ്ശി (muttaśśi), "grandmother", and കഥ (katha), "a story", borrowed from Sanskrit kathā (CDIAL 2704).
