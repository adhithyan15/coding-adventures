## You'll want to know: विजेरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C519-UJED519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विजेरी** — *vijerī* — "a torch; a flashlight".

> वीज गेली, म्हणून मी विजेरी लावली.
>
> *vīj gelī, mhaṇūn mī vijerī lāvlī.*
>
> The power went, so I switched on a torch.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विजेरी** (*vijerī*) is built on वीज, "lightning, electricity", inherited from Sanskrit *vidyut* (CDIAL 11742), with a Marathi ending.
