## You'll want to know: جبان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C500-GHURUR500-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جبان** — *jabān* — "cowardly; a coward".

> هرب الرجل الجبان من الكلب.
>
> *haraba ar-rajul al-jabān min al-kalb.*
>
> The cowardly man ran away from the dog.

The noun جبن, cowardice, is written like jubn, cheese.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جبان** (*jabān*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-b-n*, "to be cowardly" (Lane). Jabān is the adjective of a lasting trait.
