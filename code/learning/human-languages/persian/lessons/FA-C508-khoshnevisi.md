## You'll want to know: خوشنویسی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C508-CRAFTS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خوشنویسی** (*khoshnevisi*) — "calligraphy".

> او سالها خوشنویسی یاد گرفت.
>
> *u sâlhâ khoshnevisi yâd gereft.*
>
> He studied calligraphy for years.

*khosh* 'fine' + *nevis* 'writing' (stem of *neveshtan*) + *-i*. A calligrapher is *khoshnevis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khoshnevisi* is built on native Persian parts: *khosh*, 'fine', *nevis*, the present stem of *neveshtan*, which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *-i*: fine writing.
