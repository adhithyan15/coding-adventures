## You'll want to know: أثمر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C468-NABAT468-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أثمر** — *athmara* — "to bear fruit".

> أثمرت شجرة التين في الصيف.
>
> *athmarat shajarat at-tīn fī aṣ-ṣayf.*
>
> The fig tree bore fruit in summer.

Present: يثمر, *yuthmir*.

Work can bear fruit too: the sense carries over as in English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أثمر** (*athmara*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-m-r*, "fruit" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to put out fruit".
