Marathi, chapter 380: Must or May.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अनिवार्य (anivārya) — compulsory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to relax, then the Marathi for to be manageable.

You'll want to know: अनिवार्य.
अनिवार्य — anivārya — "compulsory".
शाळेत मराठी हा विषय अनिवार्य आहे. śāḷet marāṭhī hā viṣay anivārya āhe. Marathi is a compulsory subject at school.
Invariable; in speech सक्तीचं.

The word, taken apart.
अनिवार्य (anivārya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit anivārya, "not to be kept off, unavoidable" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be manageable, then the Marathi for compulsory.

You'll want to know: ऐच्छिक.
ऐच्छिक — aicchik — "optional".
हा विषय ऐच्छिक आहे, तो घेतला नाही तरी चालेल. hā viṣay aicchik āhe, to ghetlā nāhī tarī cālel. This subject is optional; it is fine if you don't take it.
Built on इच्छा, wish: by choice.

The word, taken apart.
ऐच्छिक (aicchik) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit aicchika, "optional, at will" (Monier-Williams). It is built on icchā, "wish".

Lesson 4 of 5.
लादणे (lādṇe) — to impose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for optional, then the Marathi for unavoidable.

You'll want to know: लादणे.
लादणे — lādṇe — "to impose".
आईवडिलांनी मुलांवर आपली मतं लादू नयेत. āīvḍilānnī mulāṁvar āplī mataṁ lādū nayet. Parents should not impose their views on their children.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लादलं, tyāne lādlaṁ.
The one burdened takes -वर; also to load a cart.

The word, taken apart.
लादणे (lādṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi lādṇẽ, "to heap up, load", under Sanskrit lardayati, "loads" (CDIAL 10966).

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तरतूद (tartūd) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
