## You'll want to know: источник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C282-ISTOCHNIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**источник** (*istóchnik*) — "a source; a spring".

> Журналист не назвал свой источник.
>
> *Zhurnalíst ne nazvál svoy istóchnik.*
>
> The journalist did not name his source.

First sense: a spring of water. *Источник информации* — a source of information.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*источник* is built on *истечь* "to flow out", from *из-* "out" and the Slavic verb *течь* "to flow": first a spring, then any source. EtymDB lists no parent.
