# భయపడు (bhayapaḍu) — to be afraid

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-04, TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to panic, then the Telugu for eagerness.

## You'll want to know: భయపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C495-BHAYAM495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**భయపడు** — *bhayapaḍu* — "to be afraid".

> చిన్నప్పుడు నేను చీకటికి భయపడేవాడిని.
>
> *cinnappuḍu nēnu cīkaṭiki bhayapaḍēvāḍini.*
>
> As a child I used to be afraid of the dark.

Past (I …): భయపడ్డాను, *bhayapaḍḍānu*.

What you fear takes -కి or -అంటే: కుక్కలంటే భయపడు, to be afraid of dogs. The noun is భయం.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భయపడు** (*bhayapaḍu*) joins a Sanskrit noun to a native verb: *bhaya*, "fear, peril" (CDIAL 9390), borrowed as భయం, and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853). A noun of feeling + -పడు makes many Telugu verbs.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
