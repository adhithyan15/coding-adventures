## You'll want to know: くずれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C274-JISHIN274-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くずれる** — *kuzureru* — "to crumble; to give way".

> **じしんで ふるい たてものが くずれました。** — *jishin de furui tatemono ga kuzuremashita* — "Old buildings came down in the earthquake."

Polite **くずれます**, *kuzuremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **やまが くずれました。**, *yama ga kuzuremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くずれる** (*kuzureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
