Kannada, chapter 501: Gentle and Cruel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸೌಮ್ಯ (saumya) — gentle; mild.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for attitude, then the Kannada for to imitate.

You'll want to know: ಸೌಮ್ಯ.
ಸೌಮ್ಯ — saumya — "gentle; mild".
ನಮ್ಮ ವೈದ್ಯರು ತುಂಬಾ ಸೌಮ್ಯ ಸ್ವಭಾವದವರು. namma vaidyaru tumbā saumya svabhāvadavaru. Our doctor has a very gentle nature.
Also mild weather or a mild taste.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೌಮ್ಯ (saumya) is borrowed from Sanskrit saumya, "relating to soma", and later "cool and mild" (CDIAL 13620).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮುಗ್ಧ (mugdha) — innocent; naive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to imitate, then the Kannada for gentle.

You'll want to know: ಮುಗ್ಧ.
ಮುಗ್ಧ — mugdha — "innocent; naive".
ಮಗುವಿನ ಮುಗ್ಧ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗೆ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ನಕ್ಕರು. maguvina mugdha praśnege ellarū nakkaru. Everyone laughed at the child's innocent question.
ಮುಗ್ಧತೆ, innocence.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಗ್ಧ (mugdha) is borrowed from Sanskrit mugdha, "gone astray, bewildered, foolish" (CDIAL 10175). The sense softened in Kannada to "artless, innocent".

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: ನಿಷ್ಠುರ.
ನಿಷ್ಠುರ — niṣṭhura — "harshly frank; unsparing".
ಅವನು ನಿಷ್ಠುರವಾಗಿ ಸತ್ಯ ಹೇಳಿದನು. avanu niṣṭhuravāgi satya hēḷidanu. He told the truth bluntly.
ನಿಷ್ಠುರ (niṣṭhura) ಕಟ್ಟಿಕೊ, to make enemies by plain speaking.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಷ್ಠುರ (niṣṭhura) is borrowed from Sanskrit niṣṭhura, "hard, cruel" (CDIAL 7505); Kannada uses it most for hard, frank words.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅಣಕಿಸು (aṇakisu) — to mimic mockingly; to mock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for cruel, then the Kannada for harshly frank.

You'll want to know: ಅಣಕಿಸು.
ಅಣಕಿಸು — aṇakisu — "to mimic mockingly; to mock".
ಹುಡುಗರು ಮುದುಕನ ನಡಿಗೆಯನ್ನು ಅಣಕಿಸಿದರು. huḍugaru mudukana naḍigeyannu aṇakisidaru. The boys mocked the old man's walk.
Past (I …): ಅಣಕಿಸಿದೆ, aṇakiside.
Mocking by copying someone's voice or walk; ಅಣಕ, mockery.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಣಕಿಸು (aṇakisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 111 lists Kannada aṇaka, "mockery, derision", and aṇakisu, "to mock, deride".
