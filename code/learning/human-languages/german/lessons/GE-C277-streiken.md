## You'll want to know: streiken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C277-STREIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**streiken** — "to go on strike".

> Die Busfahrer streikten zwei Tage lang.
>
> *The bus drivers were on strike for two days.*

Regular: *sie streikt*, *sie hat gestreikt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*streiken* is built on *Streik*, which was borrowed from English *strike* (EtymDB).
