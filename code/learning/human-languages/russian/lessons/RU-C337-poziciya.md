## You'll want to know: позиция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C337-TOCHKAZRENIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**позиция** (*pozítsiya*) — "a position, a stance".

> Моя позиция проста: надо больше читать.
>
> *Moyá pozítsiya prostá: nádo bólshe chitát.*
>
> My position is simple: we need to read more.

Feminine. *Занимать позицию* — to take a position. It is also a place in a list or a team: *первая позиция*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**позиция** is usually traced, through western European languages, to Latin *positiō* "a placing, a position" (Lewis & Short), which is built on *pōnere* "to put, to place". A stance is where you have placed yourself.
