## You'll want to know: campeggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C502-CAMPEGGIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**campeggiare** — "to camp".

> D'estate campeggiamo vicino al fiume.
>
> *In summer we camp near the river.*

Built on *il campeggio* "campsite, camping".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*campeggiare* is built on *campo* "field", inherited from Latin *campus* "a plain, a field" (Lewis & Short).
