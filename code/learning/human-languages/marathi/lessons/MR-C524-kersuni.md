## You'll want to know: केरसुणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C524-SAPHAI524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**केरसुणी** — *kersuṇī* — "a grass broom".

> आईने केरसुणीने खोली झाडली.
>
> *āīne kersuṇīne kholī jhāḍlī.*
>
> Mother swept the room with a grass broom.

Feminine.

Softer than a झाडू, for sweeping indoors.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**केरसुणी** (*kersuṇī*) is a compound of केर, "sweepings", and सुणी; the origin of both parts is not settled.
