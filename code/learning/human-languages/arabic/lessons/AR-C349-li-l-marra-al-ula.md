## You'll want to know: للمرة الأولى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C349-MARRA349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**للمرة الأولى** — *li-l-marra al-ūlā* — "for the first time".

> رأيت البحر للمرة الأولى في حياتي.
>
> *raʾaytu al-baḥr li-l-marra al-ūlā fī ḥayātī.*
>
> I saw the sea for the first time in my life.

Also لأول مرة.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**للمرة الأولى** (*li-l-marra al-ūlā*) is "for the first passing": *marra* is built on the native Arabic root *m-r-r*, "to pass" (Lane), and *ūlā* is the feminine of *awwal*, first.
