Gujarati, chapter 439: Hills and Rocks.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ડુંગર (ḍungar) — a rocky hill; a hill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for hazy, then the Gujarati for to twinkle.

You'll want to know: ડુંગર.
ડુંગર — ḍungar — "a rocky hill; a hill".
ગામની પાછળ મોટો ડુંગર છે. gāmnī pāchaḷ moṭo ḍungar che. There is a big hill behind the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ડુંગર (ḍungar) goes back to a group of forms CDIAL reconstructs for "a hill", ṭakka and ṭuṅga; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a hillock" (CDIAL 5423).

Lesson 2 of 6.
શિખર (śikhar) — a summit; a peak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to twinkle, then the Gujarati for a rocky hill.

You'll want to know: શિખર.
શિખર — śikhar — "a summit; a peak".
પર્વતના શિખર પર બરફ છે. parvatnā śikhar par baraph che. There is snow on the summit of the mountain.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
શિખર (śikhar) is a tatsama, Sanskrit śikhara, "a point, a peak" (Monier-Williams); it is also the spire of a temple.

Lesson 5 of 6.
પડઘાવું (paḍghāvũ) — to echo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a crag, then the Gujarati for a pebble.

You'll want to know: પડઘાવું.
પડઘાવું — paḍghāvũ — "to echo".
ખીણમાં અમારો અવાજ પડઘાયો. khīṇmā̃ amāro avāj paḍghāyo. Our voices echoed in the valley.
Past (it …): તે અવાજ પડઘાયો, te avāj paḍghāyo.

The word, taken apart.
પડઘાવું (paḍghāvũ) is built on પડઘો, an echo. CDIAL lists the Kachchhi word paṛgho, "an echo", under pratighōṣa, "echo", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8555a); it gives no Gujarati form, so the link is hedged.
