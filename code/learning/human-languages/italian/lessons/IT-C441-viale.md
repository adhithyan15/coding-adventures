## You'll want to know: il viale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C441-VICOLI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il viale** — "the avenue, the boulevard".

> Passeggiamo sul viale sotto gli alberi.
>
> *We stroll along the avenue under the trees.*

Wide and lined with trees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viale* is built on *via* 'road', which is inherited from Latin *via* 'way' (Lewis & Short).
