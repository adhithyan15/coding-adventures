Telugu, chapter 513: Diseases.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వ్యాధి (vyādhi) — a disorder; a disease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be bedridden, then the Telugu for to come round.

You'll want to know: వ్యాధి.
వ్యాధి — vyādhi — "a disorder; a disease".
ఆ వ్యాధికి ఇంకా మందు లేదు. ā vyādhiki iṅkā mandu lēdu. There is still no cure for that disease.
More formal than రోగం or జబ్బు; doctors and the news say వ్యాధి (vyādhi).

The word, taken apart.
వ్యాధి (vyādhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit vyādhi, "disease" (CDIAL 12202).

Lesson 2 of 5.
అంటువ్యాధి (aṇṭuvyādhi) — a contagious disease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to come round, then the Telugu for a disorder.

You'll want to know: అంటువ్యాధి.
అంటువ్యాధి — aṇṭuvyādhi — "a contagious disease".
అంటువ్యాధి వచ్చినప్పుడు బడులు మూసేశారు. aṇṭuvyādhi vaccinappuḍu baḍulu mūsēśāru. When the contagious disease broke out, the schools were shut.
అంటు, contact, + వ్యాధి (vyādhi): a disease that spreads by contact.

The word, taken apart.
అంటువ్యాధి (aṇṭuvyādhi) joins native aṇṭu, "to touch; contact, defilement by touch" (DEDR 120), to Sanskrit vyādhi, "disease" (CDIAL 12202).

Lesson 3 of 5.
గుండెపోటు (guṇḍepōṭu) — a heart attack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a disorder, then the Telugu for a contagious disease.

You'll want to know: గుండెపోటు.
గుండెపోటు — guṇḍepōṭu — "a heart attack".
మా తాతయ్యకు గత సంవత్సరం గుండెపోటు వచ్చింది. mā tātayyaku gata saṁvatsaraṁ guṇḍepōṭu vaccindi. My grandfather had a heart attack last year.
A heart attack comes: గుండెపోటు (guṇḍepōṭu) వచ్చింది. పోటు is a stab or a throb.

The word, taken apart.
గుండెపోటు (guṇḍepōṭu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1693 lists Telugu guṇḍe, "the chest, the heart, courage", with Tamil kuṇṭi, "heart"; pōṭu is a stab of pain.

Lesson 4 of 5.
పక్షవాతం (pakṣavātaṁ) — paralysis; a stroke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a contagious disease, then the Telugu for a heart attack.

You'll want to know: పక్షవాతం.
పక్షవాతం — pakṣavātaṁ — "paralysis; a stroke".
పక్షవాతం వల్ల ఆయన కుడి చెయ్యి పనిచేయదు. pakṣavātaṁ valla āyana kuḍi ceyyi panicēyadu. Because of the stroke, his right hand does not work.
పక్షం, side, + వాతం, wind: in old medicine, a wind that strikes one side.

The word, taken apart.
పక్షవాతం (pakṣavātaṁ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit pakṣa, "wing, side" (CDIAL 7627), and vāta, "wind; wind as a humour of the body" (CDIAL 11491).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a heart attack, then the Telugu for paralysis.

You'll want to know: ఉబ్బసం.
ఉబ్బసం — ubbasaṁ — "asthma".
చలికాలంలో అమ్మకు ఉబ్బసం ఎక్కువవుతుంది. calikālaṁlō ammaku ubbasaṁ ekkuvavutundi. In winter mum's asthma gets worse.
Also called ఆస్తమా, from English.

The word, taken apart.
ఉబ్బసం (ubbasaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 632 lists Telugu ubbasamu, "asthma", and marks Tamil uppacam as borrowed from Telugu.
