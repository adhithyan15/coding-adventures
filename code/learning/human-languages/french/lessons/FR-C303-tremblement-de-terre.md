## You'll want to know: le tremblement de terre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C303-SEISME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le tremblement de terre** — "the earthquake".

> Le tremblement de terre a duré trente secondes.
>
> *The earthquake lasted thirty seconds.*

News reports also say *le séisme*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tremblement* is built on *trembler*, inherited from Vulgar Latin *tremulāre*, built on Latin *tremulus* "trembling" (Lewis & Short); *terre* is inherited from Latin *terra* "earth" (EtymDB).
