## You'll want to know: பதறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C304-ATHIRCHI304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**பதறு** — *pataṟu* — "to get flustered".

> சத்தம் கேட்டதும் எல்லோரும் பதறினார்கள்.
>
> *cattam kēṭṭatum ellōrum pataṟiṉārkaḷ.*
>
> Everyone got flustered when they heard the noise.

Past (I …): பதறினேன், *pataṟiṉēṉ*.

பதறாதே, do not panic: a common way to calm someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பதறு** (*pataṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3910): to be flurried, confused, overhasty.

One more word for telling what happened.
