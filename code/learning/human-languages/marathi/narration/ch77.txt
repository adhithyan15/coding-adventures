Marathi, chapter 77: Neighbour, Guest, Teacher, To hear, To get up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शेजारी (shejārī) — a neighbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a person, then the Marathi for a woman.

You'll want to know: शेजारी.
शेजारी — shejārī — "a neighbour".

The word, taken apart.
शेजारी (shejārī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sagṛhya, "neighbouring", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13077). CDIAL builds it on Sanskrit sagṛha, "with one's household".

You'll want to know: ऐकणे.
ऐकणे — aikṇe — "to hear, to listen".

The word, taken apart.
ऐकणे (aikṇe) goes back to ācikhyati, "communicates", a form CDIAL reconstructs from Sanskrit ākhyāti, "tells". CDIAL prefers it to ācakṣati, "looks at" (CDIAL 1074, 1063).

उठणे (uṭhṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from utsthāti, "stands up", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 1900). It is ud, "up", and sthā, "to stand".
