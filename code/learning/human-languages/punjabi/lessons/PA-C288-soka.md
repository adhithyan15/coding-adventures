## You'll want to know: ਸੋਕਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C288-HEAT288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੋਕਾ** — *sokā* — "a drought".

> ਜੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਵੇਗਾ ਤਾਂ ਸੋਕਾ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ।
>
> *je mīṁh nahīṁ āvegā tāṁ sokā hovegā.*
>
> If the rain does not come, there will be a drought.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੋਕਾ** (*sokā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*śauṣkya*, "dryness"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sok* and *sokkā*, "dryness, drought", under it (CDIAL 12652).
