## You'll want to know: جھلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C430-PARA430-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جھلنا** — *jhalnā* — "to fan (someone)".

> گرمی میں ماں نے بچے کو پنکھا جھلا۔
>
> *garmī meṅ māṅ ne bachche ko pankhā jhalā.*
>
> In the heat the mother fanned the child.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پنکھا جھلا, *us ne pankhā jhalā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھلنا** (*jhalnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhalnā*, "to move a fan to and fro", under *jhal*, "a sudden movement", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5351).
