## You'll want to know: عدالت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C386-BARABARI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عدالت** (*'edâlat*) — "justice".

> مردم از دولت عدالت میخواهند.
>
> *mardom az dowlat 'edâlat mikhâhand.*
>
> People want justice from the government.

*'Âdel* — fair, righteous. In speech *mikhâhand* is *mikhân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عدالت** was borrowed from Arabic *'adāla* "justice, fairness" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as *'adl* "justice".
