## HL-C335 — Hindi action chains declare their taught examples

After the doing-form repair, **देखकर** is the highest-ranked independent
modern Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 73 already
derives regular **देखकर** and irregular **करके** with the **-कर** pattern,
models **देखकर** in a complete sentence, and asks learners to produce both
examples in guided practice and an assessed activity.

The old metadata declared only suffix **-कर**, leaving its explicitly taught
regular and irregular examples invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Declare **-कर / देखकर / करके** as the lesson headword, print the Devanagari
forms in guided practice, and regenerate the derived Hindi artifacts. This is
a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim.
