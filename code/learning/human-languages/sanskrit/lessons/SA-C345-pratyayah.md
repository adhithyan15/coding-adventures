## You'll want to know: प्रत्ययः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C345-PRATYAYA345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्ययः** — *pratyayaḥ* — "conviction; firm belief".

> मम प्रत्ययः अस्ति यत् सः सत्यं वदति।
>
> *mama pratyayaḥ asti yat saḥ satyaṃ vadati.*
>
> I am convinced that he is telling the truth.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्ययः** (*pratyayaḥ*) is built on *prati*, "towards", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *praty-aya* "belief, firm conviction, trust" and "proof, ascertainment" (MW).
