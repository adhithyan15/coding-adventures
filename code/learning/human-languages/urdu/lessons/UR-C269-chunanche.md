# چنانچہ (chunāṅche) — therefore

## You'll want to know: چنانچہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C269-CHUNANCHE-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-02, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04] -->

**چنانچہ** — *chunāṅche* — "therefore".

> **بارش ہو رہی تھی،**
>
> *bārish ho rahī thī,*
>
> **چنانچہ ہم گھر پر رہے۔**
>
> *chunāṅche ham ghar par rahe.* — "It was raining; therefore we stayed at home."

It is formal: you will meet it in writing, in the news and in speeches. In talk, **اس لیے** (*is liye*) says the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چنانچہ** (*chunāṅche*) is usually traced to Persian *chunānche*, "as, accordingly", built on *chunān*, "so, thus".
