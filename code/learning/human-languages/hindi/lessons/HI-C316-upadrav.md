## You'll want to know: उपद्रव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C316-UPADRAV316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उपद्रव** — *upadrav* — "a disturbance; rioting".

> अफ़वाह के बाद शहर में उपद्रव हुआ।
>
> *afvāh ke bād śahar meṁ upadrav huā.*
>
> After the rumour there was rioting in the city.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपद्रव** (*upadrav*) is Sanskrit *upadrava*, "what attacks or happens suddenly" (Monier-Williams). In the news it is unrest in the streets.
