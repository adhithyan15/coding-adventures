## You'll want to know: বিদ্যা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C361-SHIKKHA361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদ্যা** — *bidyā* — "learning; knowledge".

> বিদ্যা কেউ চুরি করতে পারে না।
>
> *bidyā keu churi kôrte pāre nā.*
>
> No one can steal your learning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদ্যা** (*bidyā*) is Sanskrit *vidyā*, knowledge or learning (Monier-Williams). It is the learning a person carries, as in বিদ্যালয়, a school, 'the house of learning'.
