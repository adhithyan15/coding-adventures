Marathi, chapter 290: At the Police Station.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हवालदार (havāldār) — a constable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pickpocket, then the Marathi for to cheat.

You'll want to know: हवालदार.
हवालदार — havāldār — "a constable".
आम्हाला किती वेळ थांबावं लागेल, हे हवालदारावर अवलंबून आहे. āmhālā kitī veḷ thāmbāvaṁ lāgel, he havāldārāvar avlambūn āhe. How long we will have to wait depends on the constable.
Masculine.
हवालदाराला, to the constable.

The word, taken apart.
हवालदार (havāldār) is a loan from Persian ḥavāladār, 'one entrusted with a charge', usually traced to Arabic ḥawāla, 'a charge, a trust'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
अटक करणे (aṭak karṇe) — to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to cheat, then the Marathi for a constable.

You'll want to know: अटक करणे.
अटक करणे — aṭak karṇe — "to arrest".
पोलिसांनी चोराला संध्याकाळी अटक केली. polisānnī corālā sandhyākāḷī aṭak kelī. The police arrested the thief in the evening.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अटक केली, tyāne aṭak kelī.
The person arrested takes -ला; अटक is feminine, so the past is अटक केली.

The word, taken apart.
अटक करणे (aṭak karṇe) is built from aṭak, 'a hold, a stop', of the family CDIAL lists under aṭṭakk, 'obstruct, stop', with Marathi aṭakṇe, 'to be stopped' (CDIAL 182), and karṇe, 'to do'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
चौकशी करणे (caukśī karṇe) — to inquire; to question.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a constable, then the Marathi for to arrest.

You'll want to know: चौकशी करणे.
चौकशी करणे — caukśī karṇe — "to inquire; to question".
हवालदाराने दुकानदाराकडे चौकशी केली. havāldārāne dukāndārākaḍe caukśī kelī. The constable made inquiries with the shopkeeper.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चौकशी केली, tyāne caukśī kelī.
The person asked takes -कडे; the matter takes -ची: अपघाताची चौकशी करणे (caukśī karṇe), to look into an accident.

The word, taken apart.
चौकशी करणे (caukśī karṇe) joins caukśī, 'an inquiry', to karṇe, 'to do'; the origin of caukśī is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कैदी (kaidī) — a prisoner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to inquire, then the Marathi for a prison.

You'll want to know: कैदी.
कैदी — kaidī — "a prisoner".
एक कैदी रात्री तुरुंगातून पळाला. ek kaidī rātrī turuṅgātūn paḷālā. A prisoner ran away from the prison at night.
Masculine.
कैदी (kaidī) keeps its form in the plural: दोन कैदी (kaidī).

The word, taken apart.
कैदी (kaidī) is a loan from Persian qaidī, 'a captive', usually traced to Arabic qayd, 'a fetter'.
