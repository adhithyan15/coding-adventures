## You'll want to know: けいたい

**けいたい** — *keitai* — "a mobile phone".

> **けいたいを うちに わすれました。** — *keitai o uchi ni wasuremashita* — "I left my mobile at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けいたい** (*keitai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kei*, "carry (in hand)" (Middle Chinese *hwej*) and *tai*, "belt" (*tajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
