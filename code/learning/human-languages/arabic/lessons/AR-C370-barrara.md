## You'll want to know: برر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C370-NAQD370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برر** — *barrara* — "to justify".

> برر الموظف تأخره بالمطر.
>
> *barrara al-muwaẓẓaf taʾakhkhurahu bi-l-maṭar.*
>
> The employee gave the rain as the reason for his lateness.

Present: يبرر, *yubarrir*.

The noun تبرير, *tabrīr*, a justification.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برر** (*barrara*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-r*, "to be true, to be good" (Lane). It is Form II, to show to be right: to justify.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
