## You'll want to know: कौशल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C507-DAFTAR507-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कौशल** — *kauśal* — "skill; know-how (formal)".

> हर कारीगर के पास अपना कौशल होता है।
>
> *Har kārīgar ke pās apnā kauśal hotā hai.*
>
> Every craftsman has his own skill.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कौशल** (*kauśal*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *kauśala*, "well-being; skilfulness, cleverness" (Monier-Williams).
