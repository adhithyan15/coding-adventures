# ぬま (numa) — a marsh; a pond

## You'll want to know: ぬま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C303-RYUU303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬま** — *numa* — "a marsh; a pond".

> **りゅうは ぬまに すんでいました。** — *ryū wa numa ni sunde imashita* — "The dragon lived in a marsh."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬま** (*numa*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
