## You'll want to know: gāofēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C409-TRAFFIC409-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gāofēng* — "peak (of traffic); rush hour".

> *gāofēng de shíhou zài chē lǐ zhǎo bu dào zuòwèi* — At rush hour you can't find a seat on the bus.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gāofēng* is built from 高 'high' and *fēng* 'a peak', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaw* and *phjowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the second under another spelling, glossed as a mountain peak.
