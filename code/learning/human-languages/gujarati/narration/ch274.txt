Gujarati, chapter 274: What won't happen, and what was going to.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
હું નહીં આવું (hũ nahī̃ āvũ) — won't.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I will come tomorrow" and "she will go". (hũ kāle āvīś, te jaśe.) Now the same two, but they won't happen.

Grammar Lens: નહીં and a short form.
હું કાલે નહીં આવું. hũ kāle nahī̃ āvũ. — "I won't come tomorrow."
"Won't" is નહીં with a short form of the verb: no -ીશ, no -શે. Learn it as one piece. The short form follows the person, and it is the same for a man and a woman:
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
who: હું. આવવું: નહીં આવું. કરવું: નહીં કરું.
who: તું, તે, તેઓ. આવવું: નહીં આવે. કરવું: નહીં કરે.
who: અમે, આપણે. આવવું: નહીં આવીએ. કરવું: નહીં કરીએ.
who: તમે. આવવું: નહીં આવો. કરવું: નહીં કરો.

You'll want to know: the short verbs.
તે આજે નહીં જાય. te āje nahī̃ jāy. — "She won't go today."
જવું gives નહીં જાઉં (nahī̃ jāũ), નહીં જાય, નહીં જઈએ, નહીં જાઓ. લેવું gives નહીં લઉં, નહીં લે; ખાવું gives નહીં ખાઉં, નહીં ખાય. થવું gives નહીં થાય: મોડું નહીં થાય (moḍũ nahī̃ thāy), "it won't be late".
Keep these short forms after નહીં for now. "I will" still takes -ીશ: હું જઈશ, but હું નહીં જાઉં.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "We won't eat at home" — ame ghare nahī̃ khāīe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I won't take the medicine" — hũ davā nahī̃ laũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 22 seconds]
Say that you won't come tomorrow, then that she won't go today.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I won't do it"? (હું નહીં કરું.) "He won't come"? (તે નહીં આવે.) "You won't go", with respect? (તમે નહીં જાઓ.) And "I will go"? (હું જઈશ: the short form stays with નહીં.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
હું જવાનો હતો (hũ javāno hato) — was going to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I'm going to go tomorrow", as a plan already made. (hũ kāle javāno chũ.) Then "I won't go". (hũ nahī̃ jāũ.)

Grammar Lens: છું becomes હતો.
હું ગઈકાલે બજાર જવાનો હતો. hũ gaīkāle bajār javāno hato. — "I was going to go to the market yesterday."
Put હતો where છું stood, and the plan moves into the past. Both halves agree, as જવાનો છું did: a woman says જવાની હતી, a group જવાના હતા.
With a verb that has an object, the doer stays plain: હું પત્ર લખવાનો હતો (hũ patra lakhvāno hato), not મેં. It is a plan, not a past of લખવું.

You'll want to know:..., પણ...
અમે જવાના હતા, પણ વરસાદ પડ્યો. ame javānā hatā, paṇ varsād paḍyo. — "We were going to go, but it rained."
A plan in the past often did not happen. પણ names what got in the way: મીરા આવવાની હતી, પણ ટ્રેન મોડી હતી (Mīrā āvvānī hatī, paṇ ṭren moḍī hatī), "Mira was going to come, but the train was late."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Mira was going to come, but the train was late" — Mīrā āvvānī hatī, paṇ ṭren moḍī hatī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — contrast: hũ javāno chũ and hũ javāno hato: which plan can still happen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say that you were going to cook at home, but you came late.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I was going to go"? (હું જવાનો હતો (hũ javāno hato), or જવાની હતી.) "We were going to go, but it rained"? (અમે જવાના હતા, પણ વરસાદ પડ્યો.) "I was going to write a letter": મેં or હું? (હું.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — what won't happen, and what was going to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "Arun has gone home". (Aruṇ ghare gayo che.) Then "I will come tomorrow". (hũ kāle āvīś.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I won't come tomorrow. She won't go today.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: We won't eat at home. He won't take the medicine.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I was going to go to the market, but it rained.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Mira was going to come, but the train was late.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I was going to write a letter, but I came home late.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I won't go"? (હું નહીં જાઉં.) Does it change for a woman? (No.) "We were going to go"? (અમે જવાના હતા.) What turns જવાનો છું into a plan that is over? (હતો in place of છું.)
