## You'll want to know: مرحلہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C545-MUSTAQIL545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مرحلہ** — *marhalā* — "a stage (of a process)".

> اگر ہم نے پہلا مرحلہ جیتا تو آگے جائیں گے۔
>
> *agar ham ne pehlā marhalā jītā to āge jāeṅ ge.*
>
> If we win the first stage, we'll go through.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرحلہ** (*marhalā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *marḥala*, "a day's journey, a stage".
