## You'll want to know: sùliàodài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C186-THINGS186-03]; assesses=[] -->

*sùliàodài* — "plastic bag".

> *wǒ bú yào sùliàodài* — I don't need a plastic bag.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sùliàodài* is built from *sùliào* 'plastic' and *dài* 'bag'. Of its parts, *liào* 'material' and *dài* are inherited from Middle Chinese, *lew* and *dojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the first is listed there as *suH*, glossed 'a modelled figure': a material that can be modelled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
