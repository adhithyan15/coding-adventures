## You'll want to know: جا گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C434-PELLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جا گرفتن** (*jâ gereftan*) — "to get a seat; to take up room".

> در مترو جا گرفتم و نشستم.
>
> *Dar metro jâ gereftam va neshastam.*
>
> In the metro I got a seat and sat down.

*jâ* 'place' + *gereftan* 'to take'. *Barâ-ye kasi jâ gereftan* — to save someone a seat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jâ gereftan* is built from *jâ* 'place', inherited from Middle Persian *gyāg* (EtymDB), and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian *grab-* 'to seize'.
