## You'll want to know: хохотать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C480-LAUGHTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**хохотать** (*khokhotát*) — "to roar with laughter".

> Мы хохотали весь вечер.
>
> *My khokhotáli ves vécher.*
>
> We roared with laughter all evening.

Imperfective; present хохочу, хохочешь (т becomes ч). Perfective захохотать — to burst out laughing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хохотать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *xoxotati* (EtymDB), an imitative word built on the sound of loud laughter.
