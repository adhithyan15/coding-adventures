## You'll want to know: पुलिनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C417-PULINA417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पुलिनम्** — *pulinam* — "a sandbank".

> नद्याः मध्ये पुलिनम् अस्ति।
>
> *nadyāḥ madhye pulinam asti.*
>
> There is a sandbank in the middle of the river.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुलिनम्** (*pulinam*) is the word MW gives for a sandbank, a small island left by a river; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
