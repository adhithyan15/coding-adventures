Punjabi, chapter 313: A Wedding in the Family.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਵਿਆਹ (viāh) — a wedding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a host, then the Punjabi for a gift.

You'll want to know: ਵਿਆਹ.
ਵਿਆਹ — viāh — "a wedding".
ਪਿਛਲੇ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਭਰਾ ਦਾ ਵਿਆਹ ਸੀ। pichhle mahīne mere bharā dā viāh sī. My brother's wedding was last month.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿਆਹ (viāh) is inherited from Sanskrit vivāha, "marriage"; CDIAL lists Punjabi viāh, "marriage", under it (CDIAL 11920).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a gift, then the Punjabi for a wedding.

You'll want to know: ਬਰਾਤ.
ਬਰਾਤ — barāt — "a groom's wedding party".
ਬਰਾਤ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਆਈ ਅਤੇ ਸਭ ਨੱਚੇ। barāt shām nū̃ āī ate sabh nachche. The groom's party arrived in the evening, and everyone danced.
