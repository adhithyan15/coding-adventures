## You'll want to know: एकंदरीत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C390-EKUN390-02]; assesses=[] -->

**एकंदरीत** — *ekandarīt* — "on the whole".

> एकंदरीत सहल छान झाली.
>
> *ekandarīt sahal chān jhālī.*
>
> On the whole, the trip went well.

एकंदर, whole, + -ईत.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एकंदरीत** (*ekandarīt*) is built on एकंदर, "whole, total", with the ending -ईत, "in"; the origin of एकंदर is not settled.
