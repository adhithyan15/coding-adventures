Telugu, chapter 527: Success and Failure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సాధించు (sādhiñcu) — to achieve; to accomplish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to cooperate, then the Telugu for training.

You'll want to know: సాధించు.
సాధించు — sādhiñcu — "to achieve; to accomplish".
పట్టుదలతో ఆమె తన లక్ష్యం సాధించింది. paṭṭudalatō āme tana lakṣyaṁ sādhiñcindi. With determination she achieved her goal.
Past (I …): సాధించాను, sādhiñcānu.
Its noun సాధన is practice.

The word, taken apart.
సాధించు (sādhiñcu) is built on Sanskrit sādhana, "furthering; completion" (CDIAL 13334), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to achieve, then the Telugu for to excel.

You'll want to know: విఫలమవు.
విఫలమవు — viphalamavu — "to fail; to be unsuccessful".
మొదటి ప్రయత్నంలో మేము విఫలమయ్యాము. modaṭi prayatnaṁlō mēmu viphalamayyāmu. We failed at the first attempt.
Past (I …): విఫలమయ్యాను, viphalamayyānu.
Formal: విఫలం, fruitless, + అవు, to become.

The word, taken apart.
విఫలమవు (viphalamavu) is built from Sanskrit viphala, "fruitless" (CDIAL 11803) and the Telugu verb అవు, "to become".

జీతం సరిపోక అతను రాజీనామా చేశాడు. jītaṁ saripōka atanu rājīnāmā cēśāḍu. His pay was not enough, so he resigned.

The word, taken apart.
రాజీనామా (rājīnāmā) is a loan from Persian rāzīnāma, "a written agreement, a letter of consent", which reached Telugu through Hindustani.

Lesson 5 of 5.
మానేయు (mānēyu) — to quit; to stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to fail, then the Telugu for a resignation.

You'll want to know: మానేయు.
మానేయు — mānēyu — "to quit; to stop".
అన్నయ్య పాత ఉద్యోగం మానేశాడు. annayya pāta udyōgaṁ mānēśāḍu. My elder brother quit his old job.
Past (I …): మానేశాను, mānēśānu.
With a job, a habit or study: చదువు మానేయు (mānēyu), to drop out of school.

The word, taken apart.
మానేయు (mānēyu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4811 lists Telugu mānu, "to cease, to stop, to give up, to quit"; vēyu, "to put", makes the stopping final.
