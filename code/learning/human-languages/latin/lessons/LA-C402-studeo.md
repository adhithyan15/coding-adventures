## You'll want to know: studeō, studēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C402-EDISCO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**studeō, studēre** — "to be eager (for); to study".

> Litterīs studet.
>
> *He is devoted to literature.*

*studeō, studēre, studuī* + dative: *litterīs studēre*, to give oneself to literature. *Studium* is its noun. English *student* and *study*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*studeō* is usually traced to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to push, to strike" (EtymDB): to press on towards a thing. English *student* and *study* come from it.
