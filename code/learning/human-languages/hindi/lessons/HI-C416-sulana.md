## You'll want to know: सुलाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C416-KHEMA416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सुलाना** — *sulānā* — "to put to sleep; to lull (someone) to sleep".

> माँ ने बच्चे को ख़ेमे में सुलाया।
>
> *māṁ ne bacce ko kheme meṁ sulāyā.*
>
> The mother put the child to sleep in the tent.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्ची को सुलाया, *usne baccī ko sulāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुलाना** (*sulānā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *svāpayati*, "lulls to sleep" (CDIAL 13928), which lists Hindi *sulānā*: the causative partner of सोना, to sleep.
