## You'll want to know: स्पन्दते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C474-SIRA474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पन्दते** — *spandate* — "throbs".

> भयेन मम हृदयं स्पन्दते।
>
> *bhayena mama hṛdayaṃ spandate.*
>
> My heart throbs with fear.

Past (it …): हृदयं स्पन्दितवत्, *hṛdayaṃ spanditavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पन्दते** (*spandate*) comes from the root √spand, "to quiver, to throb" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
