## You'll want to know: ಸದಸ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C404-SADASYA404-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸದಸ್ಯ** — *sadasya* — "a member".

> ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣ ಈ ಸಂಘದ ಸದಸ್ಯ.
>
> *nanna aṇṇa ī saṅghada sadasya.*
>
> My elder brother is a member of this association.

Plural: ಸದಸ್ಯರು, *sadasyaru*.

ಸದಸ್ಯತ್ವ, membership.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸದಸ್ಯ** (*sadasya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sadasya*, "one who belongs to an assembly" (Monier-Williams), from *sadas*, "a seat, an assembly".
