## You'll want to know: ಮೆನು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C464-UPAHARA464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆನು** — *menu* — "a menu".

> ಮೆನುವಿನಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ದೋಸೆಗಳಿವೆ.
>
> *menuvinalli tumbā dōsegaḷive.*
>
> There are lots of dosas on the menu.

The ending -ವಿನಲ್ಲಿ: ಮೆನು takes -ವ- before a case ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆನು** (*menu*) is a loan from English *menu*.
