Russian, chapter 500: Knitting and Embroidery.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
вязать (vyazát) — to knit; to tie.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a tea party, then the Russian for to stay as a guest.

You'll want to know: вязать.
вязать (vyazát) — "to knit; to tie".
Бабушка вяжет мне тёплый шарф. Bábushka vyázhet mne tyóplyy sharf. Grandma is knitting me a warm scarf.
Imperfective; present вяжу, вяжешь (з becomes ж). Perfective связать.

The word, taken apart.
вязать (vyazát) is inherited from Proto-Slavic vęzati 'to tie, to bind' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 6.
спицы (spítsy) — knitting needles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to stay as a guest, then the Russian for to knit.

You'll want to know: спицы.
спицы (spítsy) — "knitting needles".
Для этого свитера нужны толстые спицы. Dlya étogo svítera nuzhný tólstyye spítsy. You need thick needles for this sweater.
Plural of спица, which also means a spoke of a wheel.

The word, taken apart.
спицы (spítsy) is the plural of спица 'a spoke, a knitting needle', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic stъpica; EtymDB has no entry for the plural.

Lesson 3 of 6.
клубок (klubók) — a ball of yarn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to knit, then the Russian for knitting needles.

You'll want to know: клубок.
клубок (klubók) — "a ball of yarn".
Котёнок играет с клубком шерсти. Kotyónok igráyet s klubkóm shérsti. The kitten is playing with a ball of wool.
Masculine; genitive клубка (the vowel drops).

The word, taken apart.
клубок (klubók) is built on клуб 'a ball, a puff (of smoke)' with -ок; EtymDB lists клубок (klubók) without a source.

Мама вышивает цветы на подушке. Máma vyshiváyet tsvetý na podúshke. Mum is embroidering flowers on a cushion.
Imperfective; perfective вышить (вышью). The noun is вышивка — embroidery.

Lesson 5 of 6.
кружево (krúzhevo) — lace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a ball of yarn, then the Russian for to embroider.

You'll want to know: кружево.
кружево (krúzhevo) — "lace".
На ней было белое платье с кружевами. Na ney býlo béloye plátye s kruzhevámi. She was wearing a white dress with lace.
Neuter; usually plural кружева.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists кружево (krúzhevo) without a source; it is usually traced to the root of круг 'circle', after the round patterns of lace.
