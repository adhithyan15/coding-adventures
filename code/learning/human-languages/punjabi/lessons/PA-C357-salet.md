## You'll want to know: ਸਲੇਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C357-SCHOOL357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਲੇਟ** — *saleṭ* — "a slate (to write on)".

> ਛੋਟੇ ਬੱਚੇ ਸਲੇਟ ਉੱਤੇ ਲਿਖਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *chhoṭe bachche saleṭ utte likhde han.*
>
> Little children write on a slate.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਲੇਟ** (*saleṭ*) is borrowed from English *slate*.
