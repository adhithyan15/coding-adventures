## You'll want to know: గతం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C338-KRAMANGA338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**గతం** — *gataṁ* — "the past".

> ఆమె తరచుగా గతం గుర్తుచేసుకుంటుంది.
>
> *āme taracugā gataṁ gurtucēsukuṇṭundi.*
>
> She often recalls the past.

You know గత, last or past: గత వారం. గతం is the noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గతం** (*gataṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gata*, "gone, past" (CDIAL 4008); as a noun it is what has gone by.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
