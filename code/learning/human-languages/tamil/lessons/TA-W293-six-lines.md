# Six lines about a trip

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C284-WHEN-PAST-01, TA-SKILL-C285-READ-STORY-01, TA-LEX-UUR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last connected message you wrote was about a familiar day. This one tells a story: six lines about a trip, to your home town or anywhere else.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one reader: a friend, by name.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C285-FIRST-THEN-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C285-VERBAL-PARTICIPLE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C286-SO-RESULT-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C287-REASON-ORDER-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C290-FEELING-DATIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C293-UNREAL-IF-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C293-WOULD-01] -->

A told story is not a list of events. It has a **scene**: **-கொண்டிருந்தேன்** or **-த போது**. It has **events** in the plain past, kept in order with **முதலில்**, **அதன் பிறகு**, the **-உ** / **-இ** form and **கடைசியில்**. It has at least one **reason**, with **அதனால்**, **-ததால்** or **ஏனென்றால்**, and a **feeling**, with **எனக்கு … வந்தது** or **எனக்கு … -ஆக இருந்தது**.

A good one ends by looking at what might have been: **… -இருந்தால், … -இருப்பேன்**.

<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] On paper, in Tamil script, write **six lines** to your reader about a trip, real or invented, one line for each of these:

1. the scene: when it was, and what you were doing as it began, with **-கொண்டிருந்தேன்** or **-த போது**;
2. the first thing you did, with **முதலில்**: a ticket, a seat, or the way you asked;
3. what happened next, with **அதன் பிறகு** or an **-உ** / **-இ** form, and one thing that went wrong: a missed bus, a missing bag, or rain and a wet shirt;
4. why, or what followed, with **அதனால்**, **-ததால்** or **ஏனென்றால்**;
5. how it ended and how you felt, with **கடைசியில்** and **எனக்கு … வந்தது** or **-ஆக இருந்தது**;
6. one line about what might have been, with **-இருந்தால் … -இருப்பேன்**.

[PAUSE 3s] There is no model on this page, and no word bank. Use only words you already write. The whole exercise is choosing the right past for each line, and joining the lines.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C284-ONGOING-PAST-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-WHEN-PAST-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C293-UNREAL-IF-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C293-WOULD-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141, TA-LEX-C289-IRUKKAI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Now read your lines aloud. Mark every verb **s** for scene and **e** for event. Line one should be mostly **s**; lines two and three mostly **e**.

[PAUSE 2s] Then a spelling check. If it rained in your story, did you write **ஈரம்** or **ஈரமாக**? It opens with the letter **ஈ**: a bar, two legs, two dots, not the curled **இ**. Inside a word, after a consonant, the same long *ī* is the sign ீ.

[PAUSE 2s] Last, read line six alone. Does the if-clause end in **-இருந்தால்**, and the main verb in **-இருப்பேன்**?
