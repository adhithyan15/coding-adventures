## You'll want to know: दैनिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C310-VRUTTAPATRA310-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दैनिक** — *dainik* — "a daily paper; daily".

> माझे बाबा रोज दोन दैनिकं वाचतात.
>
> *mājhe bābā roj don dainikaṁ vāctāt.*
>
> My father reads two daily papers every day.

Neuter.

Neuter as a noun, plural दैनिकं; as an adjective: दैनिक काम, daily work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दैनिक** (*dainik*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *dainika*, 'daily' (Monier-Williams), built on *dina*, 'a day'.
