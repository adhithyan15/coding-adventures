Persian, chapter 57: Bear, Camel, Donkey, Butterfly, Ant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خرس (khers) — a bear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a monkey, then the Persian for a snake.

You'll want to know: خرس.
خرس — khers — "a bear".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
