## You'll want to know: شہزادہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C328-SHAHZADA328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شہزادہ** — *shahzādā* — "a prince".

> شہزادہ رات کو شہر میں گھومتا تھا۔
>
> *shahzādā rāt ko shahr meṅ ghūmtā thā.*
>
> The prince used to walk about the city at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شہزادہ** (*shahzādā*) is usually traced to Persian *shāhzāda*, "born of a king", built from *shāh*, a king, and *zāda*, "born".
