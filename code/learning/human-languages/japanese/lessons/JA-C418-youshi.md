## You'll want to know: ようし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C418-SHOMEI418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ようし** — *yōshi* — "a blank form".

> **まどぐちで ようしを うけとりました。** — *madoguchi de yōshi o uketorimashita* — "I got a form at the counter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ようし** (*yōshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yō*, "use" (Middle Chinese *yowngH*) and *shi*, "paper" (*tsyeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
