## You'll want to know: o detetive
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C294-INVESTIGAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o detetive** — "the detective".

> O detetive seguiu o homem até casa.
>
> *The detective followed the man home.*

*Um romance policial* is a detective novel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*detetive* was borrowed from English *detective* (EtymDB), a word that goes back to Latin *dētegere* "to uncover" (Lewis & Short).
