## You'll want to know: गैरसोय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C434-GAIRSOY434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गैरसोय** — *gairsoy* — "inconvenience".

> पाणी नसल्यामुळे सगळ्यांची गैरसोय झाली.
>
> *pāṇī nasalyāmuḷe sagaḷyāñcī gairsoy jhālī.*
>
> Everyone was put out because there was no water.

Feminine.

गैरसोय होणे, to be inconvenienced: the person takes -ची.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गैरसोय** (*gairsoy*) is built from *gair-*, "un-, other than", a loan usually traced to Arabic *ġair*, and **सोय**, "convenience".
