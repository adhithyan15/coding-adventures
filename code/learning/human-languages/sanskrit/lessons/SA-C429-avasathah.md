## You'll want to know: आवसथः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C429-UDGHATANA429-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आवसथः** — *āvasathaḥ* — "a travellers' lodge".

> आवसथे पथिकाः रात्रिं वसन्ति।
>
> *āvasathe pathikāḥ rātriṃ vasanti.*
>
> Wayfarers spend the night at the lodge.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आवसथः** (*āvasathaḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √vas, "to dwell", which also gives *vasati*, "dwells"; MW gives *āvasatha* as a dwelling-place, an abode, a night's lodging (MW).
