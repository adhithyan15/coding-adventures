## You'll want to know: lītoreus, lītorea, lītoreum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C444-MALACIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lītoreus, lītorea, lītoreum** — "of the shore, on the beach".

> Līberī harēnam lītoream in saccōs pōnunt.
>
> *The children put beach sand into sacks.*

An adjective of three endings, from *lītus, lītoris* 'shore'. English *littoral* comes from the related *lītorālis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lītoreus* is built on *lītus, lītoris* 'shore' with the adjective suffix *-eus* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *littoral* comes from the related adjective *lītorālis*.
