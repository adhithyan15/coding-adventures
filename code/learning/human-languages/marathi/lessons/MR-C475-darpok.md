## You'll want to know: डरपोक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C475-SAHAS475-02]; assesses=[] -->

**डरपोक** — *ḍarpok* — "chicken-hearted".

> मी डरपोक नाही, पण साप पाहिला की घाबरतो.
>
> *mī ḍarpok nāhī, paṇ sāp pāhilā kī ghābarto.*
>
> I am no coward, but I get scared when I see a snake.

Unchanging, and blunter than भित्रा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डरपोक** (*ḍarpok*) is a loan from Hindustani *ḍarpok*, "timid", built on *ḍar*, "fear".
