## You'll want to know: जनमत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C354-NAGRIK354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जनमत** — *janmat* — "public opinion".

> जनमत नए क़ानून के ख़िलाफ़ है।
>
> *janmat nae qānūn ke khilāf hai.*
>
> Public opinion is against the new law.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जनमत** (*janmat*) joins two Sanskrit words, *jana*, "people", and *mata*, "an opinion" (Monier-Williams): what the people think. The compound is a modern one.
