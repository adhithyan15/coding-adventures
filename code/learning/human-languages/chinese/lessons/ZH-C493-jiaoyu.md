## You'll want to know: jiàoyù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C493-TUTOR493-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàoyù* — "to educate; to bring up (the young); education".

> *jiàoyù háizi bù róngyì* — Bringing up children isn't easy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàoyù* is built from 教 'to instruct' and *yù* 'to rear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewH* and *yuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to instruct' and the second 'to nourish' and 'to grow'.
