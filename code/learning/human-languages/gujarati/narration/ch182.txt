Gujarati, chapter 182: These days and for good.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
આજકાલ (ājkāl) — these days, nowadays.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cancel, then the Gujarati for to extend.

You'll want to know: આજકાલ.
આજકાલ — ājkāl — "these days, nowadays".
આજકાલ હું વહેલો ઉઠું છું. — ājkāl hũ vahelo uṭhũ chũ — "These days I get up early."

The word, taken apart.
આજકાલ (ājkāl) is built from two taught words, āj, "today", and kāl, "tomorrow, yesterday", so it means about now.

Lesson 2 of 7.
કાયમ (kāyam) — permanently, for good.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to extend, then the Gujarati for these days.

You'll want to know: કાયમ.
કાયમ — kāyam — "permanently, for good".
આ ઘર કાયમ અમારું રહેશે. — ā ghar kāyam amārũ raheśe — "This house will be ours for good."

The word, taken apart.
કાયમ (kāyam) is a loan from Arabic qāʾim, "standing, lasting", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-w-m, "to stand" (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 7.
અચાનક (acānak) — suddenly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for these days, then the Gujarati for permanently.

You'll want to know: અચાનક.
અચાનક — acānak — "suddenly".
કદાચ તે અચાનક આવશે. — kadāc te acānak āvśe — "Perhaps he will turn up suddenly."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of અચાનક (acānak) is not settled. EtymDB lists it as borrowed from Hindi acānak, and CDIAL lists no Gujarati form.

Lesson 4 of 7.
યાદ રાખવું (yād rākhvũ) — to keep in mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for permanently, then the Gujarati for suddenly.

You'll want to know: યાદ રાખવું.
યાદ રાખવું — yād rākhvũ — "to keep in mind".
હું આ તારીખ યાદ રાખીશ. — hũ ā tārīkh yād rākhīś — "I will remember this date."
Word for word, to keep in memory.

The word, taken apart.
યાદ રાખવું (yād rākhvũ) is built from yād, "memory", usually traced to Persian yād, "memory", and the inherited rākhvũ, "to keep", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit rakṣati, "guards" (CDIAL 10547).

Lesson 5 of 7.
ગુમાવવું (gumāvvũ) — to let slip, to lose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for suddenly, then the Gujarati for to keep in mind.

You'll want to know: ગુમાવવું.
ગુમાવવું — gumāvvũ — "to let slip, to lose".
તમે સમય ગુમાવશો. — tame samay gumāvśo — "You will lose time."

The word, taken apart.
ગુમાવવું (gumāvvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to squander, lose, pass (time)", under Sanskrit gamayati, "causes to go" (CDIAL 4028).
