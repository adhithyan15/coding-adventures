Urdu, chapter 82: Clothes, Kurta, Trousers, Sock, Pocket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کپڑے (kapṛe) — clothes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a boat, then the Urdu for a bicycle.

You'll want to know: کپڑے.
کپڑے — kapṛe — "clothes".

The word, taken apart.
کپڑے (kapṛe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from karpaṭa, "patched garment, rag" (CDIAL 2871). kapṛe, "clothes", is the plural of kapṛā, "cloth". CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word from an Austroasiatic language.
