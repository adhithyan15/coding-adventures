## You'll want to know: la separazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C494-SEPARARSI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la separazione** — "separation".

> Dopo la separazione lui è andato a vivere da solo.
>
> *After the separation he went to live alone.*

In law it comes before the divorce.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*separazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sēparātiō* "a severing" (EtymDB), from *sēparāre*.
