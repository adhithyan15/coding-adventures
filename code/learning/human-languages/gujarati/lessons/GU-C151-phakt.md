## You'll want to know: ફક્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C151-READ151-03]; assesses=[GU-LEX-DUKAAN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01, GU-LEX-C57-MARKET-04] -->

**ફક્ત** (*phakt*) — "only, no more than".

Notices are full of limits, and **ફક્ત** sets them:

> **દુકાન ફક્ત સવારે ખુલ્લી છે.** — *dukān phakt savāre khullī che.* — The shop is open only in the morning.
> **ફક્ત પાંચ રૂપિયા.** — *phakt pā̃c rūpiyā.* — Only five rupees.

It stands right before the word it limits.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફક્ત** (*phakt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *faqaṭ*,
"only, and that is enough". Lane treats *faqaṭ* under the root *q-ṭ*, whose *qaṭ*
means "enough, sufficient" (Lane).
