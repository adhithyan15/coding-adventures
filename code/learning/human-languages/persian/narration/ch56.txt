Persian, chapter 56: Mouse, Tiger, Elephant, Monkey, Snake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
موش (mush) — a mouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sheep, then the Persian for a bird.

You'll want to know: موش.
موش — mush — "a mouse".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mush]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mush, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mush]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for a sheep, then the Persian for a bird, then say mush again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does موش (mush) mean? ("A mouse".) Say it once more.
