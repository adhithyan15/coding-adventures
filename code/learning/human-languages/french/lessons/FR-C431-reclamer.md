## You'll want to know: réclamer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C431-RECLAMATION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**réclamer** — "to ask for, to demand; to claim".

> Il a réclamé une autre chambre, plus calme.
>
> *He asked for another, quieter room.*

*Réclamer son argent* — to demand one's money back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*réclamer* comes from Old French *reclamer*, from Latin *reclāmāre* 'to cry out against', built on *clāmāre* 'to shout' (EtymDB).
