## You'll want to know: der Ehrengast
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C307-GASTGEBER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ehrengast** — "the guest of honour".

> Der Ehrengast saß neben dem Brautpaar.
>
> *The guest of honour sat next to the bride and groom.*

Plural: *die Ehrengäste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ehrengast* joins *Ehre* "honour", Old High German *ēra* (EtymDB), and *Gast* "guest", both inherited Germanic words.
