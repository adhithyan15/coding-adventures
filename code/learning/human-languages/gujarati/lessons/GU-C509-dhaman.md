## You'll want to know: ધમણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C509-DHAMAN509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમણ** — *dhamaṇ* — "bellows".

> લુહારનો દીકરો ધમણ ચલાવે છે.
>
> *luhārno dīkro dhamaṇ calāve che.*
>
> The blacksmith's son works the bellows.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમણ** (*dhamaṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhamanī*, "bellows", from a root meaning "to blow", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 6734).
