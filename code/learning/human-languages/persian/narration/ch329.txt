Persian, chapter 329: Explosions and leaks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انفجار (enfejâr) — an explosion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for smoke, then the Persian for a spark.

You'll want to know: انفجار.
انفجار (enfejâr) — "an explosion".
صدای انفجار همه را بیدار کرد. Sedâ-ye enfejâr hame râ bidâr kard. The sound of the explosion woke everyone up.
Enfejâr-e gâz "a gas explosion".

The word, taken apart.
enfejâr is borrowed from Arabic infijār, "explosion, bursting" (EtymDB lists both the Arabic and the Persian word).

Lesson 2 of 5.
منفجر شدن (monfajer shodan) — to explode, to blow up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a spark, then the Persian for an explosion.

You'll want to know: منفجر شدن.
منفجر شدن (monfajer shodan) — "to explode, to blow up".
کپسول گاز در آشپزخانه منفجر شد. Kapsul-e gâz dar âshpazkhâne monfajer shod. The gas cylinder exploded in the kitchen.
Present monfajer mishavad; monfajer kardan is "to blow something up".

The word, taken apart.
monfajer is usually traced to Arabic munfajir, "bursting", of the same root as enfejâr; EtymDB lists no entry. shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ترکیدن (tarakidan) — to burst, to crack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an explosion, then the Persian for to explode.

You'll want to know: ترکیدن.
ترکیدن (tarakidan) — "to burst, to crack".
لوله آب در زمستان ترکید. Lule-ye âb dar zemestân tarakid. The water pipe burst in the winter.
Spoken terekidan. Present stem tarak-: mitarakad, in speech mitereke. Also of a balloon or a tyre.

The word, taken apart.
tarakidan is a native Persian verb, built with the verb suffix -idan; its deeper origin is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
نشت کردن (nasht kardan) — to leak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to explode, then the Persian for to burst.

You'll want to know: نشت کردن.
نشت کردن (nasht kardan) — "to leak".
گاز از لوله نشت میکرد. Gâz az lule nasht mikard. Gas was leaking from the pipe.
Present nasht mikonad. The noun nasht is "a leak".

The word, taken apart.
nasht "a leak, seepage" is a native word whose origin is not settled; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خاکستر (khâkestar) — ash, ashes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to burst, then the Persian for to leak.

You'll want to know: خاکستر.
خاکستر (khâkestar) — "ash, ashes".
از خانه فقط خاکستر ماند. Az khâne faqat khâkestar mând. Only ashes were left of the house.
khâk "earth, dust" + -estar. Zir-e khâkestar "under the ashes", smouldering.

The word, taken apart.
khâkestar is built on khâk "dust, earth", a native Persian word; EtymDB lists no entry for the compound.
