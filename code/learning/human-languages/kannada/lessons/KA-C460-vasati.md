## You'll want to know: ವಸತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C460-VASATI460-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಸತಿ** — *vasati* — "lodging; accommodation".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ವಸತಿ ಸಿಗುವುದು ಕಷ್ಟ.
>
> *habbada samayadalli vasati siguvudu kaṣṭa.*
>
> At festival time lodging is hard to find.

ವಸತಿ ಶಾಲೆ, a boarding school; ವಸತಿಗೃಹ, a lodge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಸತಿ** (*vasati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vasati*, "a dwelling, a lodging", from *vas*, "to stay, to dwell" (CDIAL 11435).
