## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**chercheur** — *chercher*, "to look for", with *-eur*: one who searches. *Chercher* ← Old French *cerchier*, from Late Latin *circāre*, "to go around" (EtymDB 2.1).
