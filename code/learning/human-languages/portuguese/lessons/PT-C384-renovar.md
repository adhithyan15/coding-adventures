## You'll want to know: renovar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C384-VALIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**renovar** — "to renew; to renovate".

> Tenho de renovar o cartão de cidadão.
>
> *I have to renew my identity card.*

Also to renovate a house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*renovar* comes from Latin *renovāre* "to renew, to restore" (Lewis & Short), built on *novus* "new" (EtymDB).
