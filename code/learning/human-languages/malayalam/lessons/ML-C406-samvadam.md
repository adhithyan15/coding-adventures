# സംവാദം (saṁvādaṁ) — a debate

## You'll want to know: സംവാദം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C406-VADAM406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**സംവാദം** — *saṁvādaṁ* — "a debate".

> കോളേജിൽ ഇന്നലെ ഒരു സംവാദം നടന്നു.
>
> *kōḷējil innale oru saṁvādaṁ naṭannu.*
>
> A debate was held at the college yesterday.

A debate is നടക്കുക, held, or നടത്തുക, organised.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സംവാദം** (*saṁvādaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁvāda*, "speaking together, a conversation" (Monier-Williams): *sam-*, "together", and *vāda*, "speech".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
