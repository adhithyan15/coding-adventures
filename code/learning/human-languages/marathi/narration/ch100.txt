Marathi, chapter 100: Horse, Cow, Goat, Sheep, Bird.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घोडा (ghoḍā) — a horse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a dog, then the Marathi for a cat.

You'll want to know: घोडा.
घोडा — ghoḍā — "a horse".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ghoḍā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ghoḍā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ghoḍā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a dog, then the Marathi for a cat, then say ghoḍā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does घोडा (ghoḍā) mean? ("A horse".) Say it once more.
