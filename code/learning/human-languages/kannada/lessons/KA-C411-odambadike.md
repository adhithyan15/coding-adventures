# ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ (oḍambaḍike) — a pact; a treaty

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C411-SHARATTU411-01, KA-LEX-C411-SHARATTU411-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a condition, then the Kannada for an agreement.

## You'll want to know: ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C411-SHARATTU411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ** — *oḍambaḍike* — "a pact; a treaty".

> ಎರಡು ದೇಶಗಳ ನಡುವೆ ಹೊಸ ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *eraḍu dēśagaḷa naḍuve hosa oḍambaḍike āyitu.*
>
> A new treaty was made between the two countries.

More formal than ಒಪ್ಪಂದ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ** (*oḍambaḍike*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 945 lists Kannada *oḍam-baḍu*, "to agree", and *oḍa-baḍike*, "an agreement, a treaty".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
