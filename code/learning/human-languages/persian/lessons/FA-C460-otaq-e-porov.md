## You'll want to know: اتاق پرو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C460-VITRIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اتاق پرو** (*otâq-e porov*) — "a fitting room, a changing room".

> اتاق پرو شلوغ بود و منتظر ماندم.
>
> *Otâq-e porov sholugh bud va montazer mândam.*
>
> The fitting room was busy and I waited.

*otâq* 'room' + *porov* 'fitting'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*otâq-e porov* is built from *otâq* 'room', a loan usually traced to Turkic *otaq* 'tent, room', and *porov* 'a fitting', usually traced to French *preuve*.
