## You'll want to know: وہم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C354-MAQUL354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وہم** — *vahm* — "a groundless fear; a delusion".

> یہ آپ کا وہم ہے، سب ٹھیک ہے۔
>
> *yih āp kā vahm hai, sab ṭhīk hai.*
>
> You are imagining it; everything is fine.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وہم** (*vahm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wahm*, "a fancy, a supposition".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
