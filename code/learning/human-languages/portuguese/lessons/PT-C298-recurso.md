## You'll want to know: o recurso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C298-RECURSO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o recurso** — "the appeal; a resource".

> O tribunal aceitou o recurso.
>
> *The court accepted the appeal.*

Also a resource: *os recursos naturais*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recurso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *recursus* "a running back, a return", from *recurrere* "to run back" (Lewis & Short).
