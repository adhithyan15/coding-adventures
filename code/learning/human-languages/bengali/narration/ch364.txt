Lesson 1 of 5.
কঠিন (kôṭhin) — hard; difficult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an answer, then the Bengali for to come through.

You'll want to know: কঠিন.
কঠিন — kôṭhin — "hard; difficult".
পরীক্ষাটা কঠিন ছিল। pôrīkkhāṭā kôṭhin chhilô. The exam was hard.

The word, taken apart.
কঠিন (kôṭhin) is Sanskrit kaṭhina, hard or firm (Monier-Williams). It is hard to the touch and hard to do; সহজ, easy, is its opposite.

Lesson 2 of 5.
সংকট (sôngkôṭ) — a crisis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to come through, then the Bengali for difficult.

You'll want to know: সংকট.
সংকট — sôngkôṭ — "a crisis".
দেশে এখন জলের সংকট। deshe ekhôn jôler sôngkôṭ. There is a water crisis in the country now.

The word, taken apart.
সংকট (sôngkôṭ) is Sanskrit saṃkaṭa, narrow and so dangerous or critical (Monier-Williams). In Bengali it is the noun, a crisis or a severe shortage.

Lesson 4 of 5.
বিপর্যস্ত হওয়া (bipôrjôstô hôoyā) — to be devastated; to be thrown into turmoil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a crisis, then the Bengali for poverty.

You'll want to know: বিপর্যস্ত হওয়া.
বিপর্যস্ত হওয়া — bipôrjôstô hôoyā — "to be devastated; to be thrown into turmoil".
বন্যায় পুরো গ্রাম বিপর্যস্ত হল। bônyāy puro grām bipôrjôstô hôlô. The whole village was devastated by the flood.
Past (he/she …): সেই শহরটা ঝড়ে বিপর্যস্ত হল, sei shôhôrṭā jhôṛe bipôrjôstô hôlô.

The word, taken apart.
বিপর্যস্ত হওয়া (bipôrjôstô hôoyā) is Sanskrit viparyasta, turned over or upset (Monier-Williams), with হওয়া: a town or a life turned upside down by a disaster. The sense 'devastated' is a Bengali use of that picture; the cause takes -এ: বন্যায়.

Lesson 5 of 5.
চরম (chôrôm) — extreme; utmost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for poverty, then the Bengali for to be devastated.

You'll want to know: চরম.
চরম — chôrôm — "extreme; utmost".
বন্যার পরে গ্রামের মানুষ চরম বিপদে পড়ল। bônyār pôre grāmer mānush chôrôm bipôde pôṛlô. After the flood the villagers were in dire trouble.

The word, taken apart.
চরম (chôrôm) is Sanskrit carama, last or ultimate (Monier-Williams): at the far end of the scale. Bengali uses it for the worst a hardship can get, চরম বিপদ, or the height of anything, চরম আনন্দ; that use is not one the dictionary gives.
