Lesson 2 of 5.
ठंडो (ṭhaṇḍo) — cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for long, then the Marwadi for hot.

You'll want to know: ठंडो.
ठंडो — ṭhaṇḍo — "cold".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Hindi ṭhaṇḍā, "cold", under a reconstructed ṭhaṇḍha, which it sets under Sanskrit stabdha, "stiff, solidified", tracing the sense from "firm" through "sluggish" to "cold" (CDIAL 13676); it gives no Marwari form. DEDR compares Dravidian words for "cool", such as Tamil taṇ (DEDR 3045).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सोरो (soro) — easy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for cold, then the Marwadi for fast.

You'll want to know: सोरो.
सोरो — soro — "easy".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of सोरो (soro) is not settled; CDIAL has no entry for it. A likely relative is Hindi suhelā and Gujarati sahelũ, "easy", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit sukha, "easy, pleasant" (CDIAL 13451), but that entry does not explain the r of सोरो (soro).
