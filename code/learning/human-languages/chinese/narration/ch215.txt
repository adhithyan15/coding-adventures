Mandarin Chinese, chapter 215: Deliveries and the Laundry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
sònghuò — to deliver goods.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a way to deal with it, then the Mandarin for what should I do.

You'll want to know: sònghuò.
sònghuò — "to deliver goods".
tāmen míngtiān sònghuò — They're delivering tomorrow.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
sònghuò is built from sòng 'to send' and huò 'goods', both inherited from Middle Chinese, suwngH and xwaH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to send off'.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
qiānshōu — to sign for (a delivery).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for what should I do, then the Mandarin for to deliver goods.

You'll want to know: qiānshōu.
qiānshōu — "to sign for (a delivery)".
kuàidì dào le qǐng qiānshōu — Your delivery is here; please sign for it.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qiānshōu is built from qiān 'to sign' and shōu 'to receive'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese syuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.
洗衣店 (xǐyīdiàn) — laundry shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to sign for, then the Mandarin for to leave in safekeeping.

You'll want to know: 洗衣店.
洗衣店 — xǐyīdiàn — "laundry shop".
洗衣店在书店东边 xǐyīdiàn zài shūdiàn dōngbian — The laundry is to the east of the bookshop.

The word, taken apart.
洗衣店 (xǐyīdiàn) is built from 洗 'to wash', 衣 'clothes' and 店 'a shop', all inherited from Middle Chinese, sejX, 'j+j and temH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
