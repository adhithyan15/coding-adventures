## You'll want to know: tǐwēn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C245-MASK245-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐwēn* — "body temperature".

> *nǐ de tǐwēn yǒudiǎnr gāo* — Your temperature's a bit high.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐwēn* is built from 体 'body' and *wēn* 'warm', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thejX* and *'won* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
