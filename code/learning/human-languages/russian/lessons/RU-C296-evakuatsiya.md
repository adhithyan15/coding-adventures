## You'll want to know: эвакуация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C296-SPASATEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**эвакуация** (*evakuátsiya*) — "an evacuation".

> Эвакуация началась в пять утра.
>
> *Evakuátsiya nachalás v pyat utrá.*
>
> The evacuation began at five in the morning.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*эвакуация* goes back to Latin *ēvacuātiō* "an emptying", from *ēvacuāre* and *vacuus* "empty" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.
