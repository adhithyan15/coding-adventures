## You'll want to know: amar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C715-AMOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**amar** — "to love".

> Te amo con todo mi corazón.
>
> *I love you with all my heart.*

Deep, romantic love; with friends and family people usually say *te quiero*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amar* is inherited from Latin *amāre* "to love" (EtymDB); English *amateur*, one who does something for love, comes from it through French.
