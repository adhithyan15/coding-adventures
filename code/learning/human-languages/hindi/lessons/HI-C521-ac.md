## You'll want to know: आँच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C521-TADKA521-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आँच** — *ā̃c* — "the heat of a flame; a flame".

> दाल को धीमी आँच पर पकाइए।
>
> *Dāl ko dhīmī ā̃c par pakāie.*
>
> Cook the dal on a low flame.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आँच** (*ā̃c*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *arcis*, "a flame, a ray" (CDIAL 635).
