Hindi, chapter 435: Change, Sizes and Clothes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रेज़गारी (rezgārī) — loose coins, change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to suit, then the Hindi for a touchstone.

You'll want to know: रेज़गारी.
रेज़गारी — rezgārī — "loose coins, change".
बस के लिए रेज़गारी रखिए। bas ke liye rezgārī rakhiye. Keep some loose change for the bus.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रेज़गारी (rezgārī) is usually traced to Persian reza, "a small piece, a fragment": money broken into small pieces.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सिमटना (simaṭnā) — to huddle up; to draw together; to shrink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an outfit, then the Hindi for a handkerchief.

You'll want to know: सिमटना.
सिमटना — simaṭnā — "to huddle up; to draw together; to shrink".
ठंड में बच्चा कंबल में सिमट गया। ṭhaṇḍ meṁ baccā kambal meṁ simaṭ gayā. In the cold the child curled up in the blanket.
Past (it …): वह भीड़ एक कोने में सिमट गई, vah bhīṛ ek kone meṁ simaṭ gaī.

The word, taken apart.
सिमटना (simaṭnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit saṁveṣṭayati, "wraps up, winds round" (CDIAL 13026), which lists Hindi simaṭnā, "to be collected, shrink"; समेटना, to gather up, is its partner.
