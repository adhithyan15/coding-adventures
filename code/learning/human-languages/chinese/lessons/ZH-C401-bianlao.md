## You'll want to know: 变老
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C401-FUTURE401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**变老** — *biànlǎo* — "to get old".

> **人都会变老** *rén dōu huì biànlǎo* — Everyone gets old.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**变老** (*biànlǎo*) is built from 变 'to change' and 老 'old', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjenH* and *lawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'aged'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
