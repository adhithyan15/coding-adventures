## You'll want to know: たびだつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C384-HIKOUJOU384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たびだつ** — *tabidatsu* — "to set off on a journey".

> **あには あした くうこうから たびだちます。** — *ani wa ashita kūkō kara tabidachimasu* — "My brother sets off from the airport tomorrow."

Polite **たびだちます**, *tabidachimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが くうこうから たびだちました。**, *ani ga kūkō kara tabidachimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たびだつ** (*tabidatsu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tabi*, "journey" and *datsu*, "setting out" (from *tatsu*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
