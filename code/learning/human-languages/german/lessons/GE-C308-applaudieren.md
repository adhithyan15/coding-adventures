## You'll want to know: applaudieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C308-ANSTOSSEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**applaudieren** — "to applaud".

> Die Leute applaudierten zehn Minuten lang.
>
> *People applauded for ten minutes.*

With the dative: *Sie applaudierten dem Sänger*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*applaudieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *applaudere* "to strike upon, to applaud", built on *plaudere* (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.
