# (el aviso de entrega) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-03, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el seguimiento** and **el almacén**. The card below sends
you to one using the other.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-01, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-03, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-04] -->

A card through the letterbox. Read it, then answer.

> **AVISO DE ENTREGA**

> Hemos pasado a las 11:40 y no había nadie.

> Su paquete vuelve al **almacén** esta tarde.

> Consulte el **seguimiento** con el número 4471-XK.

> Puede recogerlo mañana o pedir una segunda entrega a la hora que le resulte
> más **cómoda**.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: where the parcel is going now]
- [YOU SAY: the two things you are allowed to do tomorrow]
- [YOU SAY: what the number is for]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-04, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-02, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-01] -->

**A la hora que le resulte más cómoda** is the phrase to take away whole. It
is how Spanish says "whenever suits you", and *cómoda* there has nothing to do
with comfort — it is the convenience half of the word, agreeing with *la
hora*.

Then notice what the card does **not** say. There is no *nosotros* after the
first line: *su paquete vuelve*, *puede recogerlo*. Spanish keeps the company
out of it and lets the parcel be the subject. The one place the firm appears —
*hemos pasado* — is the one fact it is claiming responsibility for.

And **consulte** is the *usted* command again, the register of every counter
and every card of this kind.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-01, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-02, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-03, ES-LEX-C452-REPARTO-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four lines for a
delivery card: say nobody was in, say the parcel goes back to the warehouse,
give a tracking number to check, and offer a time that suits them.

Then check yourself: did *cómoda* agree with *la hora*, and did you use the
*usted* command?
