## You'll want to know: انتخابات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C409-HOKUMAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**انتخابات** (*entekhâbât*) — "an election, elections".

> انتخابات سال آینده برگزار میشود.
>
> *entekhâbât sâl-e âyande bargozâr mishavad.*
>
> The election will be held next year.

The Arabic-style plural of *entekhâb* "choice", the usual word for an election.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتخابات** is the Arabic-style plural of *entekhâb* "choice", from Arabic *intikhāb* "choosing": an election is many choices.
