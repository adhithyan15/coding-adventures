## You'll want to know: はっきり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C367-HAKKIRI367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はっきり** — *hakkiri* — "clearly; plainly".

> **しつもんが あったら、はっきり 言って ください。** — *shitsumon ga attara, hakkiri itte kudasai* — "If you have a question, please say it plainly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はっきり** (*hakkiri*) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
