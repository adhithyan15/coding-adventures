## You'll want to know: perfidus, perfida, perfidum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C399-PERFIDIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**perfidus, perfida, perfidum** — "treacherous, faithless".

> Perfidō amīcō nēmō crēdit.
>
> *Nobody trusts a faithless friend.*

*perfidus, perfida, perfidum*: the opposite of *fīdus*, faithful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perfidus* is built from *per-* "past, beyond" and *fidēs* "faith" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): one who goes beyond his word.
