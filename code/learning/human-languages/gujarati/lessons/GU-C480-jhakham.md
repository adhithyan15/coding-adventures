## You'll want to know: ઝખમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C480-KHAS480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝખમ** — *jhakham* — "a gash; a deep wound".

> છરીથી તેના હાથમાં મોટો ઝખમ થયો.
>
> *charīthī tenā hāthmā̃ moṭo jhakham thayo.*
>
> He got a big gash on his hand from the knife.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝખમ** (*jhakham*) is usually traced to Persian *zaxm*, "a wound".
