शिकायत (śikāyat) — a complaint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: शिकायत.
शिकायत — śikāyat — "a complaint".
पुलिस क्या करेगी, यह आपकी शिकायत पर निर्भर करता है। pulis kyā karegī, yah āpkī śikāyat par nirbhar kartā hai. What the police will do depends on your complaint.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic shikāya, "a complaint". शिकायत (śikāyat) करना is to complain; at a police station, शिकायत (śikāyat) is the formal complaint.

Lesson 2 of 5.
रिपोर्ट (riporṭ) — a report (to the police).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a chase, then the Hindi for a complaint.

You'll want to know: रिपोर्ट.
रिपोर्ट — riporṭ — "a report (to the police)".
मीरा ने थाने जाकर चोरी की रिपोर्ट की। Mīrā ne thāne jākar corī kī riporṭ kī. Mira went to the police station and reported the theft.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रिपोर्ट (riporṭ) is borrowed from English report, taken into Hindi as a feminine noun: चोरी की रिपोर्ट, the report of a theft, and रिपोर्ट करना, to report it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लिखवाना (likhvānā) — to have written down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a complaint, then the Hindi for a report (to the police).

You'll want to know: लिखवाना.
लिखवाना — likhvānā — "to have written down".
अरुण ने पुलिस से अपनी शिकायत लिखवाई। Aruṇ ne pulis se apnī śikāyat likhvāī. Arun had the police write down his complaint.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने रिपोर्ट लिखवाई, usne riporṭ likhvāī.

The word, taken apart.
लिखवाना (likhvānā) is the second causative of लिखना, to write, which goes back to Sanskrit likhati, "scratches, writes" (CDIAL 11048). The ending -vā- hands the work to someone else: शिकायत (śikāyat) लिखवाई, got the complaint written down.

Lesson 4 of 5.
दरोगा (darogā) — a police inspector.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a report (to the police), then the Hindi for to have written down.

You'll want to know: दरोगा.
दरोगा — darogā — "a police inspector".
दरोगा ने चौकीदार से बात की। darogā ne caukīdār se bāt kī. The inspector talked to the watchman.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
दरोगा (darogā) is usually traced to Persian dārōġa, "an overseer", a title that once named many kinds of official. In Hindi it is the inspector in charge of a police station.

Lesson 5 of 5.
हवालात (havālāt) — a lock-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to have written down, then the Hindi for a police inspector.

You'll want to know: हवालात.
हवालात — havālāt — "a lock-up".
पुलिस ने चोर को रात भर हवालात में रखा। pulis ne cor ko rāt bhar havālāt meṁ rakhā. The police kept the thief in the lock-up all night.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥawāla, "a handing over, custody": the room where a person handed over to the police is held. It is not जेल, prison: हवालात (havālāt) is for a night or two.
