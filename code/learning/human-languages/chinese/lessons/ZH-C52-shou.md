## You'll want to know: 手
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C52-BODY52-01]; assesses=[] -->

**手** — *shǒu* — "hand".

> **我的手很小** *wǒ de shǒu hěn xiǎo* — My hands are small.

The new character, in pieces: 手 — a short fall, two bars, and a vertical that hooks at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**手** (*shǒu*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *syuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed with the pair 'hand and foot'.
