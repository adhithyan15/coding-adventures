## You'll want to know: せいせき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C515-EXAM515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せいせき** — *seiseki* — "grades; results".

> **ごうかくは せいせきに よります。** — *gōkaku wa seiseki ni yorimasu* — "Passing depends on your grades."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいせき** (*seiseki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "turn into" (Middle Chinese *dzyeng*) and *seki*, "exploits" (*tsek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
