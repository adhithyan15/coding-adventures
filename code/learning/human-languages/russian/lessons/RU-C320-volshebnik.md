## You'll want to know: волшебник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C320-VOLSHEBNIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**волшебник** (*volshébnik*) — "a wizard, a magician".

> В сказке волшебник помог бедной девушке.
>
> *V skázke volshébnik pomóg bédnoy dévushke.*
>
> In the tale a wizard helped a poor girl.

*Волшебный* — magic, magical.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*волшебник* is built on *волшебный* "magic" and *-ик*, from *волшба* "sorcery", Old East Slavic *vŭlšĭba* "magic, sorcery" (EtymDB).
