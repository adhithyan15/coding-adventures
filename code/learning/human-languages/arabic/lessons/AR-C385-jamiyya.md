## You'll want to know: جمعية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C385-MUNAZZAMA385-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جمعية** — *jamʿiyya* — "an association".

> تعمل أمي في جمعية للنساء.
>
> *taʿmalu ummī fī jamʿiyya li-n-nisāʾ.*
>
> My mother works in a women's association.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جمعية** (*jamʿiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-m-ʿ*, "to gather, to bring together" (Lane). *Jamʿiyya* is a gathering: an association, a society.
