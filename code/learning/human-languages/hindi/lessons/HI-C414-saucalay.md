## You'll want to know: शौचालय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C414-GUSALKHANA414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शौचालय** — *śaucālay* — "a toilet (formal)".

> स्टेशन पर शौचालय कहाँ है?
>
> *sṭeśan par śaucālay kahā̃ hai?*
>
> Where is the toilet at the station?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शौचालय** (*śaucālay*) is a learned compound of Sanskrit *śauca*, "cleanness, purification", and *ālaya*, "a house, a dwelling" (Monier-Williams); it is the word on public signs.
