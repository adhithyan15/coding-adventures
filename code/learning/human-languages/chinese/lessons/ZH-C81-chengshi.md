## You'll want to know: chéngshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C81-CITY81-02]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngshì* — "city".

> *Běijīng shì yí ge dà chéngshì* — Beijing is a big city.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngshì* is built from chéng 'city walls' and shì 'market', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *dzyiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
