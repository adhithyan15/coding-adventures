## You'll want to know: نقاہت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C470-BREATH470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نقاہت** — *naqāhat* — "frailty (after an illness)".

> بخار کے بعد اسے نقاہت ہے۔
>
> *bukhār ke bād use naqāhat hai.*
>
> She still feels frail after the fever.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقاہت** (*naqāhat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naqāha*, "the weakness of someone getting over an illness".
