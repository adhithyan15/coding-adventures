## You'll want to know: bǔyá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C473-TEETH473-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔyá* — "to have a tooth filled".

> *wǒ zuì pà bǔyá* — Having a filling is what I dread most.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔyá* is built from *bǔ* 'to patch' and 牙 'tooth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *puX* and *ngae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to mend' and, after an older book, mending clothes.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how it was.
