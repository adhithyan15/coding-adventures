## You'll want to know: o artesanato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C504-OLARIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o artesanato** — "handicrafts, crafts".

> Na feira comprámos artesanato da região.
>
> *At the fair we bought local crafts.*

The craftsman is *o artesão*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*artesanato* is built on *artesão* "craftsman", which is usually traced to Italian *artigiano*; that word goes back to Latin *ars* "skill, craft" (Lewis & Short).
