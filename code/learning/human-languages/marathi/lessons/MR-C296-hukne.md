## You'll want to know: हुकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C296-DHADAK296-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हुकणे** — *hukṇe* — "to be missed; to miss a chance".

> मला उशीर झाला आणि माझी बस हुकली.
>
> *malā uśīr jhālā āṇi mājhī bas huklī.*
>
> I was late and missed my bus.

Past (he …): तो हुकला, *to huklā*.

As with चुकणे, the thing missed is the subject and agrees: बस हुकली, संधी हुकली, the chance was missed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुकणे** (*hukṇe*) is a Marathi verb for a chance, a bus or an aim that slips by; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
