## You'll want to know: maritimus, maritima, maritimum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C442-SALUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**maritimus, maritima, maritimum** — "coastal, by the sea; of the sea".

> Oppida maritima multōs mercātōrēs habent.
>
> *Towns by the sea have many merchants.*

An adjective of three endings, from *mare* 'sea'. *Ōra maritima*, the sea coast. English *maritime* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maritimus* is built on *mare* 'sea' with an old suffix also seen in *fīnitimus* 'neighbouring' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *maritime* comes from it.
