## You'll want to know: आतपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C438-NIDAGHA438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आतपः** — *ātapaḥ* — "sunshine; the sun's heat".

> मध्याह्ने आतपः उष्णः भवति।
>
> *madhyāhne ātapaḥ uṣṇaḥ bhavati.*
>
> At noon the sunshine is hot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आतपः** (*ātapaḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √tap, "to heat, to burn", which also gives *tapati*, "heats"; MW gives *ātapa* as heat, especially of the sun, sunshine (MW).
