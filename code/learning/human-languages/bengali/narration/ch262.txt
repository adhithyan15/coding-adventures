Bengali, chapter 262: Writer, Poet, Artist, Female singer, Female dancer.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
লেখক (lekhôk) — a writer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a gatekeeper, then the Bengali for an army.

You'll want to know: লেখক.
লেখক — lekhôk — "a writer".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: lekhôk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lekhôk, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say lekhôk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for a gatekeeper, then the Bengali for an army, then say lekhôk again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does লেখক (lekhôk) mean? ("A writer".) Say it once more.

Art; industry? (শিল্প, shilpô.) Literature? (সাহিত্য, sāhityô.) Chhana? (ছানা, chhānā.) Paneer? (পনির, pônir.) Ice cream? (আইসক্রিম, āiskrim.) Chira? (চিঁড়ে, chĩṛe.) Phuchka? (ফুচকা, phuchkā.) An email? (ইমেল, imel.) A message? (বার্তা, bārtā.) A password? (পাসওয়ার্ড, pāsoyārḍ.) A battery? (ব্যাটারি, bæṭāri.) A switch? (সুইচ, suich.)
