## You'll want to know: शिंपी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C504-KAMGAR504-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शिंपी** — *śimpī* — "a tailor".

> शिंप्याने माझा सदरा शिवला.
>
> *śimpyāne mājhā sadrā śivlā.*
>
> The tailor stitched my shirt.

Masculine.

Before -ने the -ई of शिंपी becomes -या: शिंप्याने.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिंपी** (*śimpī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *śĩpī*, the tailors' caste, under Sanskrit *śilpin*, "craftsman" (CDIAL 12471).
