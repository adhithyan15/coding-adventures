## HL-C123 — Japanese hiragana na closes the four-run loop gap

After hiragana **て** landed, Japanese hiragana **な** led the measured queue at
**3 affected realizations**.

Sirgazil's CC0 32-frame Wikimedia Commons animation supplies the four-run order:
draw the upper-left horizontal, crossing left-falling stem, and upper-right
diagonal as three lifted marks, then descend through the lower-right stem and
continue around its loop into the outward sweep. The learner filmstrip fits
that order to the bundled Noto Sans JP outline without presenting print
geometry as an independent handwriting source.

This closes all 3 affected realizations for **な** and reranks Kannada
independent vowel **ಏ** first at **3**.
