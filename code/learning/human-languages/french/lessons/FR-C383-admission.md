## You'll want to know: l'admission
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C383-CRITERE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'admission** — "the admission, the entry".

> Les résultats d'admission arrivent en juin.
>
> *The admission results come out in June.*

Feminine. From *admettre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*admission* is a learned borrowing from Latin *admissiō* "a letting in" (EtymDB), from *admittere* "to let in", built on *mittere* "to send" (Lewis & Short).
