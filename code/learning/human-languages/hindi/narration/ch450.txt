Hindi, chapter 450: On the Farm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
झड़ना (jhaṛnā) — to fall off; to be shed (of leaves).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to graze, then the Hindi for to take to graze.

You'll want to know: झड़ना.
झड़ना — jhaṛnā — "to fall off; to be shed (of leaves)".
पतझड़ में पेड़ों से पत्ते झड़ते हैं। patjhaṛ meṁ peṛoṁ se patte jhaṛte haiṁ. In autumn the leaves fall from the trees.
Past (it …): वह पत्ता पेड़ से झड़ गया, vah pattā peṛ se jhaṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
झड़ना (jhaṛnā) goes back to jhaṭati, "falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5328), which lists Hindi jhaṛnā, "to fall"; पतझड़, autumn, is the leaf-fall.

Lesson 3 of 5.
जोतना (jotnā) — to plough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be shed, then the Hindi for to be uprooted.

You'll want to know: जोतना.
जोतना — jotnā — "to plough".
किसान सुबह से खेत जोत रहा है। kisān subah se khet jot rahā hai. The farmer has been ploughing the field since morning.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने खेत जोता, usne khet jotā.

The word, taken apart.
जोतना (jotnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit yoktrayati, "fastens, ties" (CDIAL 10524), which lists Hindi jotnā, "to yoke, plough": to yoke the oxen, and so to plough.

You'll want to know: उपजना.
उपजना — upajnā — "to grow (of crops); to arise".
इस खेत में अच्छा अनाज उपजता है। is khet meṁ acchā anāj upajtā hai. Good grain grows in this field.
Past (it …): वह फ़सल अच्छी उपजी, vah fasal acchī upjī.

The word, taken apart.
उपजना (upajnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit utpadyate, "arises, originates" (CDIAL 1814), which lists Hindi upajnā, "to originate, grow"; उपज, a crop, is its noun.
