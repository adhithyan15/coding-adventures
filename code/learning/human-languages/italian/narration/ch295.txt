Italian, chapter 295: Le indagini.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
indagare — to investigate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the video camera, then the Italian for the chase.

You'll want to know: indagare.
indagare — "to investigate".
La polizia indaga sulla rapina. The police are investigating the robbery.
Indagare su qualcosa.

The word, taken apart.
indagare is a learned borrowing from Latin indāgāre "to track down" (Lewis & Short), first said of hunting dogs; EtymDB links it with indāgō "a ring of hunters".

Lesson 4 of 5.
l'indizio — the clue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the police investigation, then the Italian for the inspector.

You'll want to know: l'indizio.
l'indizio — "the clue".
Non ci sono indizi: il caso è difficile. There are no clues: the case is a hard one.
Detective stories live on indizi.

The word, taken apart.
indizio is a learned borrowing from Latin indicium "a sign, a mark" (EtymDB), from index "one who points out".

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'impronta — the footprint; the fingerprint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: l'impronta.
l'impronta — "the footprint; the fingerprint".

The word, taken apart.
impronta is built on improntare "to stamp, to press a mark into" (EtymDB); where improntare comes from is not settled.
