## You'll want to know: a travessa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C437-RUAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a travessa** — "the side street; the serving dish".

> A padaria fica numa travessa da rua principal.
>
> *The bakery is in a side street off the main road.*

For serving food, *uma travessa* is a big dish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*travessa* goes back to Latin *trānsversa* 'lying across', from *trānsvertere* 'to turn across' (Lewis & Short): a street that crosses another.
