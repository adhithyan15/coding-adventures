## You'll want to know: विरक्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C551-PARITYAJATI551-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विरक्तः** — *viraktaḥ* — "detached; indifferent".

> यदि सः धनात् विरक्तः अस्ति, तर्हि सः सुखेन जीवति।
>
> *yadi saḥ dhanāt viraktaḥ asti, tarhi saḥ sukhena jīvati.*
>
> If he does not care for wealth, he lives happily.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरक्तः** (*viraktaḥ*) is built on *vi*, "away", and the root √rañj, "to be coloured, to be attached", as its past participle; MW gives *virakta* as changed in feeling, averse, indifferent (MW).
