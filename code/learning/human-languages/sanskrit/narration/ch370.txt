Sanskrit, chapter 370: As Things Stand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्थितिः (sthitiḥ) — a situation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for comes by, then the Sanskrit for a likelihood.

You'll want to know: स्थितिः.
स्थितिः — sthitiḥ — "a situation".
यदि स्थितिः कठिना भवति, तर्हि अहं साहाय्यं करिष्यामि। yadi sthitiḥ kaṭhinā bhavati, tarhi ahaṃ sāhāyyaṃ kariṣyāmi. If the situation becomes hard, I will help.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्थितिः (sthitiḥ) comes from the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives sthiti "staying or being in any state or condition" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: अवस्था.
अवस्था — avasthā — "a state; a condition".
यदि वृद्धस्य अवस्था उत्तमा, तर्हि सः श्वः गमिष्यति। yadi vṛddhasya avasthā uttamā, tarhi saḥ śvaḥ gamiṣyati. If the old man's condition is good, he will go tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
अवस्था (avasthā) is built on ava, "down", and the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives ava-sthā "state, condition, situation" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
परिणमति (pariṇamati) — turns into; changes into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a situation, then the Sanskrit for a state of things.

You'll want to know: परिणमति.
परिणमति — pariṇamati — "turns into; changes into".
यदि शीतं भवति, तर्हि जलं हिमेन परिणमति। yadi śītaṃ bhavati, tarhi jalaṃ himena pariṇamati. If it is cold, water turns into ice.
Past (it …): जलं हिमेन परिणतवत्, jalaṃ himena pariṇatavat.

The word, taken apart.
परिणमति (pariṇamati) is built on pari, "around", and the root √nam, "to bend"; MW gives pari-√ṇam "to bend aside" and "to change or be transformed into" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनुकूलः (anukūlaḥ) — favourable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a state of things, then the Sanskrit for turns into.

You'll want to know: अनुकूलः.
अनुकूलः — anukūlaḥ — "favourable".
यदि वायुः अनुकूलः, तर्हि नौका शीघ्रं गमिष्यति। yadi vāyuḥ anukūlaḥ, tarhi naukā śīghraṃ gamiṣyati. If the wind is favourable, the boat will go fast.

The word, taken apart.
अनुकूलः (anukūlaḥ) is built from anu, "along", and kūla, "a slope, a bank"; MW gives anu-kūla "favourable, agreeable" (MW).
