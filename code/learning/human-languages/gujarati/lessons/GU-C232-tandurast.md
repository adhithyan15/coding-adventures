## You'll want to know: તંદુરસ્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C232-HEALTHY232-03]; assesses=[] -->

**તંદુરસ્ત** — *tandurast* — "healthy, fit".

> **શું તમારો દીકરો તંદુરસ્ત છે?** — *śũ tamāro dīkro tandurast che?* — "Is your son healthy?"

It does not change for gender.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તંદુરસ્ત** (*tandurast*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *tandurust*, "healthy", built from *tan*, "body", and *durust*, "sound"; EtymDB lists the Persian word.
