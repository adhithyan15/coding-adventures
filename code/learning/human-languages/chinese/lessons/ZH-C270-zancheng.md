## You'll want to know: zànchéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C270-AGREE270-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zànchéng* — "to approve of; to be in favour".

> *dàjiā dōu zànchéng* — Everyone's in favour.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zànchéng* is built from *zàn* 'to assist' and *chéng* 'to complete', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsanH* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to assist' and the second 'to finish'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
