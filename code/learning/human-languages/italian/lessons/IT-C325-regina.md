## You'll want to know: la regina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C325-REGNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la regina** — "the queen".

> La regina ha tre figli.
>
> *The queen has three children.*

Also the queen in chess.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regina* is inherited from Latin *rēgīna* "queen" (EtymDB), built on *rēx* "king".
