## You'll want to know: le hérisson
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C459-ANIMAUX-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le hérisson** — "the hedgehog".

> La nuit, un hérisson traverse le jardin.
>
> *At night a hedgehog crosses the garden.*

The *h* is aspirate: *le hérisson*, not *l'hérisson*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hérisson* comes from Old French *heriçun* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *ēricius* 'hedgehog'.
