Marathi, chapter 160: The Future, Soon, Next, From Now On, To Plan.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
भविष्य (bhaviṣya) — the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the past, then the Marathi for to happen.

You'll want to know: भविष्य.
भविष्य — bhaviṣya — "the future". Marathi's future adds endings to the verb: मी जाईन — mī jāīn — "I will go"; तो जाईल — to jāīl — "he will go".

The word, taken apart.
भविष्य (bhaviṣya) is Sanskrit bhaviṣya, "about to be, future", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. It is built on bhū, "to be, become".

Lesson 2 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: लवकरच.
लवकरच — lavkarac — "soon". लवकर, "early, quickly", with the emphatic च: मी लवकरच येईन. — mī lavkarac yeīn — "I will come soon."

The word, taken apart.
लवकरच (lavkarac) is लवकर, "early, soon", with the emphatic -च. The origin of लवकर is not settled: neither CDIAL nor DEDR has an entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 7.
पुढचा / पुढची / पुढचे (puḍhcā / puḍhcī / puḍhce) — next.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the future, then the Marathi for soon.

You'll want to know: पुढचा / पुढची / पुढचे.
पुढचा / पुढची / पुढचे — puḍhcā / puḍhcī / puḍhce — "next". It agrees with its noun: पुढच्या आठवड्यात — puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt — "next week".

The word, taken apart.
पुढचा (puḍhcā) is पुढे, "ahead", with the possessive ending -cā, "of". The origin of पुढे is not settled: neither CDIAL nor DEDR has an entry for it.

Lesson 4 of 7.
यानंतर (yānantar) — from now on, after this.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for soon, then the Marathi for next.

You'll want to know: यानंतर.
यानंतर — yānantar — "from now on, after this". It is या, "this", with नंतर, "after": यानंतर मी रोज चालेन. — yānantar mī roj cālen — "From now on I will walk every day."

The word, taken apart.
यानंतर (yānantar) is yā-, "this", with नंतर, "after", which comes from Sanskrit anantaram, "immediately after" (CDIAL 287).

Lesson 5 of 7.
आखणे (ākhṇe) — to plan, to draw up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for next, then the Marathi for from now on.

You'll want to know: आखणे.
आखणे — ākhṇe — "to plan, to draw up". Said of a plan or a line: मी प्रवासाची योजना आखीन. — mī pravāsācī yojanā ākhīn — "I will plan the trip."

The word, taken apart.
आखणे (ākhṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aṅkayati, "marks, brands" (CDIAL 104). CDIAL glosses the Marathi verb "to mark, trace".

Lesson 6 of 7.
मी जाईन (mī jāīn) — the future, person by person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "to plan", then "tomorrow", then उद्या भेटू.

You'll want to know: who will do it?
उद्या मी पुण्याला जाईन. udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn. — "Tomorrow I will go to Pune."
Use मी जाईन (mī jāīn), तू जाशील, तो / ती जाईल, आम्ही जाऊ, तुम्ही जाल, ते जातील. The future changes with the person, but not gender: a man and a woman both say मी जाईन (mī jāīn).

You'll want to know: a few future stems.
The endings stay while the stem changes: येणे becomes येईन / येईल, करणे becomes करीन / करेल, वाचणे becomes वाचीन / वाचेल, पडणे becomes पडेल. Ask काय होईल?, "What will happen?"

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I will go"? (जाईन.) "We will go"? (जाऊ.) "You will go" with respect? (जाल.) "They will go"? (जातील.) "It will rain"? (पडेल.)


Lesson 7 of 7.
मी जाणार आहे (mī jāṇār āhe) — a plan, and what will not happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I will go", then जाणे, then आहे.

You'll want to know: -णार आहे for a plan.
पुढच्या आठवड्यात मी गावाला जाणार आहे. puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt mī gāvālā jāṇār āhe. — "Next week I am going to go to the village."
Take -णे off the verb and add -णार: जाणे becomes जाणार, करणे becomes करणार, येणे becomes येणार. Add आहे for a present plan. -णार stays the same with every person in this bounded A2 pattern.

You'll want to know: -णार नाही for won't.
Replace आहे with नाही for a future refusal or firm negative: मी उद्या येणार नाही, "I won't come tomorrow."

Wrap-up Recall.
