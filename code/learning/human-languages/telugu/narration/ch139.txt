Telugu, chapter 139: Wet, Dry, Poor, Old, Famous.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
తడి (taḍi) — wet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for wide, then the Telugu for narrow.

You'll want to know: తడి.
తడి — taḍi — "wet".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: taḍi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: taḍi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say taḍi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for wide, then the Telugu for narrow, then say taḍi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does తడి (taḍi) mean? ("Wet".) Say it once more.
