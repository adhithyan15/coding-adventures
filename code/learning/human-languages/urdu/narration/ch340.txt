Urdu, chapter 340: In Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فیصلہ (faislā) — a verdict; a decision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to act on, then the Urdu for to fulfil.

You'll want to know: فیصلہ.
فیصلہ — faislā — "a verdict; a decision".
جج نے آج فیصلہ دیا۔ jaj ne āj faislā diyā. The judge gave the verdict today.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
فیصلہ (faislā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic faiṣala, "a decision".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ملزم (mulzim) — an accused person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a verdict, then the Urdu for bail.

You'll want to know: ملزم.
ملزم — mulzim — "an accused person".
ملزم نے جج سے کچھ نہیں کہا۔ mulzim ne jaj se kuchh nahīṅ kahā. The accused said nothing to the judge.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ملزم (mulzim) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mulzam, "one held liable"; Urdu says it mulzim and uses it for a person charged with a crime. It is of the same family of words as الزام, "an accusation".

Lesson 5 of 5.
گواہی دینا (gavāhī denā) — to testify; to bear witness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an accused person, then the Urdu for an arbiter.

You'll want to know: گواہی دینا.
گواہی دینا — gavāhī denā — "to testify; to bear witness".
سارا نے اس کے حق میں گواہی دی۔ sārā ne us ke haq meṅ gavāhī dī. Sara testified in his favour.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گواہی دی, us ne gavāhī dī.

The word, taken apart.
گواہی دینا (gavāhī denā) is built from gavāhī, "testimony", usually traced to Persian gavāhī, from gavāh, "a witness", and دینا, "to give".
