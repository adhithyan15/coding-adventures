## You'll want to know: la cima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C686-CIMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la cima** — "the top, the summit".

> Llegamos a la cima a mediodía.
>
> *We reached the top at midday.*

*En la cima de* — at the top of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cima* comes from Latin *cyma* "a young sprout" (EtymDB), from Greek *kŷma* "a swelling, a wave": the top of a plant, then the top of a hill.
