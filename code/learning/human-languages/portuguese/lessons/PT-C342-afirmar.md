## You'll want to know: afirmar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C342-AFIRMAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**afirmar** — "to state, to assert".

> O jornal afirma que o preço do pão vai subir.
>
> *The newspaper states that the price of bread is going to rise.*

*Afirmar que* takes the indicative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*afirmar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *affirmāre* "to present as firm, to assert" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *ad* and *firmāre* "to make firm", from *firmus* "firm".
