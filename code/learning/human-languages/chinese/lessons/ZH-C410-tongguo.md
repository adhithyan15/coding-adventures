## You'll want to know: 通过
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C410-COACH410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**通过** — *tōngguò* — "to pass through".

> **检票以后我们从这儿通过** *jiǎnpiào yǐhòu wǒmen cóng zhèr tōngguò* — After the ticket check we go through here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**通过** (*tōngguò*) is built from 通 'to go through' and 过 'to cross', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thuwng* and *kwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to go beyond'.
