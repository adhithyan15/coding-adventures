Gujarati, chapter 444: Trunks, Bark and Buds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
થડ (thaḍ) — a tree trunk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a mango tree, then the Gujarati for to sprout.

You'll want to know: થડ.
થડ — thaḍ — "a tree trunk".
વડનું થડ ઘણું મોટું છે. vaḍnũ thaḍ ghaṇũ moṭũ che. The banyan's trunk is very big.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
થડ (thaḍ) goes back to thuḍa, "a trunk, a branch", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 6098).

Lesson 2 of 5.
છાલ (chāl) — bark; peel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sprout, then the Gujarati for a tree trunk.

You'll want to know: છાલ.
છાલ — chāl — "bark; peel".
આ ઝાડની છાલ ઘણી કઠણ છે. ā jhāḍnī chāl ghaṇī kaṭhaṇ che. This tree's bark is very hard.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
છાલ (chāl) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit challi, "bark"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "bark, peel" (CDIAL 5005). છોલવું, to peel, is its neighbour.

Lesson 4 of 5.

ઝૂમખું — jhūmkhũ — "a bunch; a cluster".
આંબા પર કેરીનું ઝૂમખું લટકે છે. ā̃bā par kerīnũ jhūmkhũ laṭke che. A bunch of mangoes hangs on the mango tree.

ઝૂમખું (jhūmkhũ) goes back to jhuppa, "a tuft, a bunch", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a cluster" (CDIAL 5404).

Lesson 5 of 5.
મહેકવું (mahekvũ) — to be fragrant; to smell sweet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bud, then the Gujarati for a cluster.

You'll want to know: મહેકવું.
મહેકવું — mahekvũ — "to be fragrant; to smell sweet".
સાંજે બગીચામાં ગુલાબનાં ફૂલ મહેકે છે. sā̃je bagīcāmā̃ gulābnā̃ phūl maheke che. In the evening the roses smell sweet in the garden.
Past (it …): તે ફૂલ મહેક્યું, te phūl mahekyũ.

The word, taken apart.
મહેકવું (mahekvũ) goes back to Sanskrit magha, a word CDIAL takes, with some doubt, to have meant "fragrance"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to give off a scent" (CDIAL 9703).
