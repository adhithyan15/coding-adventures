## You'll want to know: a lealdade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C498-CONVIVIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a lealdade** — "loyalty".

> Admiro a lealdade do meu cão.
>
> *I admire my dog's loyalty.*

The adjective is *leal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lealdade* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Medieval Latin *lēgālitās* (EtymDB), built on *lēgālis* "of the law", from *lēx* "law".
