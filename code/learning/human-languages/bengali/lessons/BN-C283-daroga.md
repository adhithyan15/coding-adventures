## You'll want to know: দারোগা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C283-OPORADH283-05]; assesses=[] -->

**দারোগা** — *dārogā* — "a police inspector".

> থানার দারোগা সাক্ষীকে অনেক প্রশ্ন জিজ্ঞাসা করলেন।
>
> *thānār dārogā sākkhīke ônek prôshno jigyāsā kôrlen.*
>
> The inspector at the station asked the witness a lot of questions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দারোগা** (*dārogā*) is usually traced to Persian *dārogha*, an overseer or chief officer; in Bengali it is the officer in charge of a police station.
