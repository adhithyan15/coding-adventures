# ठोकणो (ṭhokṇo) — to hammer; to knock in

## You'll want to know: ठोकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C264-LUHAR264-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकणो** — *ṭhokṇo* — "to hammer; to knock in".

> लुहार खीलो ठोकै है।
>
> *luhār khīlo ṭhokai hai.*
>
> The blacksmith hammers in a nail.

Past (I …): म्हैं खीलो ठोक्यो, *mhaĩ khīlo ṭhokyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकणो** (*ṭhokṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭhōkk*, "knock". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *ṭhoknā*, "to knock, make firm" (CDIAL 5513).
