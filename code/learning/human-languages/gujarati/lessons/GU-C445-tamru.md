## You'll want to know: તમરું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C445-BIRDS445-03]; assesses=[] -->

**તમરું** — *tamrũ* — "a cricket (the insect)".

> રાત્રે ઘાસમાં તમરાં બોલે છે.
>
> *rātre ghāsmā̃ tamrā̃ bole che.*
>
> At night crickets chirp in the grass.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તમરું** (*tamrũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
