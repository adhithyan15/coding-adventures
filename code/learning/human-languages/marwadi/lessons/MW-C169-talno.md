## You'll want to know: तळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C169-ROTI169-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तळणो** — *taḷṇo* — "to fry".

> मां कड़ाही मांय पापड़ तळै है।
>
> *mā̃ kaṛāhī mā̃y pāpaṛ taḷai hai.*
>
> Mother fries papads in the wok.

Past (I …): म्हैं पापड़ तळ्यो, *mhaĩ pāpaṛ taḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तळणो** (*taḷṇo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*talati*, "fries"; CDIAL lists Hindi *talnā* and Gujarati *taḷvũ* (CDIAL 5736). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
