## You'll want to know: révéler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C284-RAPPORTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**révéler** — "to reveal, to disclose".

> Le journal a révélé le secret du chanteur.
>
> *The paper revealed the singer's secret.*

*Se révéler* — to turn out: *il s'est révélé très utile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*révéler* is a learned borrowing from Latin *revēlāre* "to unveil" (EtymDB), from *re-* "back" and *vēlum* "a veil".
