## You'll want to know: dēnglóng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C335-LANTERN335-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dēnglóng* — "lantern".

> *ménkǒu yǒu liǎng ge hóng dēnglóng* — There are two red lanterns at the door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēnglóng* is built from *dēng* 'a lamp' and *lóng* 'a basket', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong* and *luwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a lamp' and quotes an older book for the second, a basket for a fire.
