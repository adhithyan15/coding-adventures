# Session Map — French Chapters 1–2

How the lessons compose into commute sessions. Mechanics in
[`HL00`](../../../specs/HL00-human-language-curriculum-framework.md); same
spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | New lesson(s) | Introduces |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | salut | informal "hi" (← *salus*, health) |
| 2 | bien | "well" (← *bene*); Spanish, another Latin daughter, kept the same word |
| 3 | bon / bonne | "good" (adjective) + its four forms |
| 4 | le / la / les | grammatical **gender** (← *ille/illa*) |
| 5 | jour | first noun (masculine); *diurnum* → journal/journey |
| 6 | bonjour | assembled; singular vs Spanish plural |
| 7 | soir · bonsoir | evening; "late" parallel to *tarde* |
| 8 | nuit · bonne nuit | night (fem.); *-ct-→-it-* vs Spanish *-ch-*; feminine agreement |
| 9 | practice | all the greetings together |

## Chapter 2 — Introducing Yourself

| Session | New lesson(s) | Introduces |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | je | "I" (← *ego*; English *ego*) |
| 11 | me | "myself" (← *mē*; English *me/my/mine*); the reflexive set me/te/se |
| 12 | (s')appeler | "to call [oneself]" (← *appellāre*); **reflexive verbs** |
| 13 | je m'appelle… | **"my name is…"** assembled from je + me + appelle |
| 14 | tu / vous | familiar / formal **"you"** (← *tū / vōs*); politeness by plural |
| 15 | comment | "how" (← *quo modo*; Spanish *cómo*) |
| 16 | comment vous appelez-vous? | **"what's your name?"** by inversion |
| 17 | enchanté(e) | "pleased to meet you" (← *in-cantāre*); gender agreement |
| 18 | practice | the whole introduction dialogue |

Reviews of earlier words fold into each session per the interval schedule.

## Chapter 38 — Five Ways to Point

| Session | What it adds |
|---|---|
| il y a | there is — *it has there*, and a **y** that is Latin *ibi* |
| c'est | this is — *ce* is *ecce hoc*, "behold this" |
| ici, là, là-bas | three distances, and the accent that separates *là* from *la* |
| voici | here is — a frozen command, *vois* + *ci* |
| ce, cet, cette, ces | pointing at a noun, and the *t* that keeps two vowels apart |
| Voici tout | the table pointed at five ways |

## Chapter 39 — Where Two Words Melt Into One

| Session | What it adds |
|---|---|
| à | to, at — Latin *ad*, and an accent with one job |
| de | of, from — the word already inside *du* |
| au, aux | *à* + *le*, and the *l* that became a *u* |
| de la | the half of the partitive the food chapter left out |
| des | the plural of *un*, which English never built |
| Au café | every noun placed and possessed |

## Chapter 40 — Two Ideas, One Sentence

| Session | What it adds |
|---|---|
| sujet, verbe, objet | the order, and the endings French lost that made it compulsory |
| et | and — the one final *t* that never makes a liaison |
| ou | or — the third pair an accent keeps apart |
| mais | but — Latin *magis*, "more" |
| parce que | because — *par* + *ce* + *que* |
| Deux idées | every verb given a reason |

## Chapter 41 — Three More Ways to Use a Verb

| Session | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Parle ! | the command, and the pronoun French otherwise insists on |
| je vais partir | the near future — a fourth verb in the modal chain |
| finir | the second regular family, and its fossil *-iss-* |
| je sais pas | the *ne* speech drops, and the *pas* that was a step |
| on | Latin *homo*, "man", promoted to a pronoun |
| On y va ! | every verb commanded, futured and refused |

## Chapter 42 — What the Ear Cannot Hear

| Session | What it adds |
|---|---|
| la consonne finale | the rule under half the puzzles in this book |
| le pluriel en -s | written and never said, so the article carries it |
| petit, petite | the silent *-e* that wakes the letter before it |
| les bateaux | the *-x* plural, which is the *l* that became a *u* |
| pas de pain | four partitives collapsing into one bare *de* |
| Ce qu'on n'entend pas | one sound rule and its four consequences |

## Chapter 43 — Standing Alone

| Session | What it adds |
|---|---|
| qui | who — Latin *quis*, one letter shorter |
| que | what — the one that always elides |
| quoi | what, standing alone, and after a preposition |
| moi et toi | the heavy twins of *je* and *tu* |
| lui et elle | the third pair, and the one that did not split |
| Qui est-ce ? | every person and thing asked about |

## Chapter 44 — How Much, and What to Call People

| Session | What it adds |
|---|---|
| beaucoup | a lot — *beau* + *coup*, "a beautiful blow" |
| un peu | a little — Latin *paucus*, English *paucity* |
| très | very — Latin *trans*, "across a line" |
| assez | enough, and rather — the word that ties the chapter together |
| Monsieur, Madame | *mon sieur* and *ma dame*, worn into titles |
| Combien ? | the question that finally has four answers |
