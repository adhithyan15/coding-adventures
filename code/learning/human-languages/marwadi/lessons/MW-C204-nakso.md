## You'll want to know: नक्सो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C204-OUT204-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नक्सो** — *nakso* — "a map".

> नक्सो जेब मांय है।
>
> *nakso jeb mā̃y hai.*
>
> The map is in my pocket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नक्सो** (*nakso*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naqsh*, "a drawing, an engraving".
