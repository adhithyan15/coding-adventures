## You'll want to know: मोहल्लो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C201-TOWN201-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहल्लो** — *mohallo* — "a neighbourhood".

> म्हारो मोहल्लो कोट कनै है।
>
> *mhāro mohallo koṭ kanai hai.*
>
> My neighbourhood is near the fort.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहल्लो** (*mohallo*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maḥalla*, "a quarter of a town".
