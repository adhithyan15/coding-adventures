# પૈસા — hear money first

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-PAISA-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-HAATH-01] -->

Say hand. Now listen twice: **paisā** means **money**. Do not read the Gujarati
shape yet; one vowel sign still needs its own tiny lesson.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-PAISA-01, GU-LEX-HAATH-01] -->

Choose money or hand as you hear *paisā* and *hāth*. Then say *paisā* from the
meaning cue.
