Persian, chapter 264: Anniversary, Festival, Childhood, Youth, Day after tomorrow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سالگرد (sâlgard) — an anniversary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a stomach ache, then the Persian for a bruise.

You'll want to know: سالگرد.
سالگرد — sâlgard — "an anniversary".

The word, taken apart.
sâlgard is sâl, "year", with gard, "turn": the turn of the year. Both are native; sâl continues Middle Persian sāl (EtymDB).
