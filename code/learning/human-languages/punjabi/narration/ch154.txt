Punjabi, chapter 154: Join Three Fictional Form Fields.
7 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Three known labels, one gentle order — place the three previously taught labels in a fixed form order.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Point to the three labels you have already written: ਉਮਰ:, ਫ਼ੋਨ:, ਤਾਰੀਖ਼:. No new field or value is added here.

Writing — first labels, then values.
This little fictional form puts age on the first line, phone on the second, and date on the third. Copy only the three labels in that order, with one empty space after each colon. Leave the values blank for now.
ਉਮਰ: ______ ਫ਼ੋਨ: ______ ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ______.

Guided Practice — cover and point.
Cover the form. Point to three empty lines and say which known field belongs on each. Uncover it to check only the line order, not any digits.

Wrap-up recall.
Today only the labels and their places matter. The next step reunites the small closed banks already learned one at a time.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Put the three familiar labels on lines 1, 2, and 3; leave values blank.


Lesson 2 of 7.
One card chooses three already-known values — choose a matching set from the three previously taught closed banks.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
The old closed banks are visible again. Age A is ੧੫, B is ੨੫. Phone A is ੦੧੨ ੨੫੧, B is ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫. Date A is ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫, B is ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫. These are fictional practice values, not personal data.

You'll want to know — a single shared cue.
On this practice card ਕ chooses A from each bank; ਖ chooses B from each bank. You already met these shapes in phone and date. For age, the card now points to its old A or B bank too. The card mark is never copied into a form field.

Guided Practice — select before writing.
Point to ਕ. Point to the A value in each bank, top to bottom: age, phone, date. Do not write yet. Repeat for ਖ and its B values. Check selection before checking any digits.

Wrap-up recall.
The banks stay visible here. Selection is the only new step.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
The fictional card is ਖ. Which old bank letter is chosen for age, phone, and date, in that order?


Lesson 3 of 7.
Fill the little form with the bank still open — copy one selected set of known fictional values into the three-line form.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
The card ਕ asks for A in each old bank. The three A values are visible: age ੧੫, phone ੦੧੨ ੨੫੧, date ੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫.

Writing — one familiar line at a time.
For ਕ, copy the selected value after each printed label. Finish one line before moving to the next. Keep the phone's single group space and the date's two slash marks.
ਉਮਰ: ______ ਫ਼ੋਨ: ______ ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ______.

Guided Practice — three small checks.
Compare the selected bank letter first, the value on each line second, and line order third. Correct just one difference at a time. This supported copy does not count as independent writing.

Wrap-up recall.
The bank remains visible throughout this gentle supported attempt.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
With the bank visible, fill the fictional three-line form for ਕ.


Lesson 4 of 7.
Hide the bank for one short turn — hide the taught value bank briefly and recover the three selected fictional fields.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read the familiar B set once: ਉਮਰ: ੨੫, ਫ਼ੋਨ: ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫, ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫. Point to one line at a time.

Writing — brief delayed copy.
Cover the model. Wait five seconds. The card is ਖ. Write the three lines in the known order, recalling just one line at a time. Keep the model covered until all three lines are finished.
ਉਮਰ: ______ ਫ਼ੋਨ: ______ ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ______.

Guided Practice — uncover only to compare.
Uncover the model. Compare age first, phone second, and date third. This is still delayed-copy practice, not an independent checkpoint.

Wrap-up recall.
If one line was hard, revisit only that old field before continuing.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
After covering the model for five seconds, fill the three familiar fields for ਖ.


Lesson 5 of 7.
Check the values in three little passes — repair selection, digit order, and date format as separate passes.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
The card ਖ still selects B for all three fictional fields. Before checking any digit, ask: did every line use B, not a mixture of A and B?

Grammar lens — selection, order, format.
Make three separate passes. First check the selected bank letter for each line. Next check digits left to right, including the phone's group of three and three. Last check the date's day/month/year boxes and two slash marks. Do not erase a correct line to fix another one.

Guided Practice — one change at a time.
For ਖ, suppose the age line says ੧੫. Change only its selected value to ੨੫. If the phone line says ੦੨੫ ੧੫੨, keep the first group and repair only the last two digit positions. If the date says ੨੫/੦੨੨੦੨੫, keep all digits and add only the missing slash.

Wrap-up recall.
Label spelling, spacing, and placement get their own pass next.
[question — say your answer, then pause 28 seconds]
For ਖ, repair only the wrong digit order in ਫ਼ੋਨ: ੦੨੫ ੧੫੨.


Lesson 6 of 7.
Check the labels and their places — repair label spelling, spaces, and line placement without changing correct values.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Read the known labels once: ਉਮਰ:, ਫ਼ੋਨ:, ਤਾਰੀਖ਼:. The little dot marks in ਫ਼ and ਖ਼ belong to their spellings.

Writing — three different layout passes.
First compare label spelling; restore a missing dot without touching digits. Then compare spacing: one readable space follows each colon, and the phone has one group space. Finally compare placement: age is line one, phone line two, date line three. Move a whole correct value to its proper line; do not rewrite its digits.

Guided Practice — a single spelling repair.
In ਤਾਰੀਖ: ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫, fix only the label to ਤਾਰੀਖ਼. The date stays unchanged. In ਫੋਨ: ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫, fix only the label to ਫ਼ੋਨ.

Wrap-up recall.
You have now practised six distinct checks: choice, digit order, date format, label spelling, spacing, and placement. The final short attempt hides the value bank again.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Repair only the label spelling in ਤਾਰੀਖ: ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫.


Lesson 7 of 7.
One no-model fictional three-field form — select and write three known fictional fields from one cue without a value model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Close the earlier lessons. No value bank, support-language label, Latin-digit version, romanization, or copyable Gurmukhi value appears below.

Writing — independent controlled choice.
Complete the fictional form for the one card mark. Use only the old known values. Write all three in Gurmukhi digits from memory. Do not reopen a lesson until all three lines are filled.
ਖ ਉਮਰ: ______ ਫ਼ੋਨ: ______ ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ______.

Guided Practice — repair after the attempt.
Only after writing, check six dimensions separately: selection, digit order, date format, label spelling, spacing, then field placement. Repair the first different dimension without erasing a correct one.

Wrap-up recall.
This closes only an untimed three-field checkpoint. The complete six-field form, timed writing, two full mocks, and human A1 validation remain separate dependency-linked work.
[question — say your answer, then pause 55 seconds]
With no value bank or copyable answer, complete all three fictional form fields for ਖ.
