Persian, chapter 426: Traffic jams and parking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
راهبندان (râhbandân) — a traffic jam, a hold-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for traffic, then the Persian for to swerve.

You'll want to know: راهبندان.
راهبندان (râhbandân) — "a traffic jam, a hold-up".
به خاطر راهبندان دیر به خانه رسیدیم. Be khâter-e râhbandân dir be khâne residim. Because of the traffic jam we got home late.
Literally 'road-blocking': râh 'road' + band 'tie, block' + -ân.

The word, taken apart.
râhbandân is a native compound: râh 'road' goes back to Middle Persian rāh (EtymDB), and band 'a tie, a block' to the root of bastan 'to bind', which EtymDB traces to PIE bʰendʰ-.

Lesson 2 of 5.
پارک کردن (pârk kardan) — to park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to swerve, then the Persian for a traffic jam.

You'll want to know: پارک کردن.
پارک کردن (pârk kardan) — "to park".
جلوی بیمارستان نمیشود پارک کرد. Jolow-ye bimârestân nemishavad pârk kard. You cannot park in front of the hospital.
pârk + kardan. Present stem kon-: pârk mikonam.

The word, taken apart.
pârk kardan is built from pârk, a loan usually traced to French parc 'park', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a traffic jam, then the Persian for to park.

You'll want to know: دور زدن.
دور زدن (dowr zadan) — "to turn round, to make a U-turn; to go round".
راه را اشتباه رفتیم و دور زدیم. Râh râ eshtebâh raftim va dowr zadim. We went the wrong way and turned round.
dowr 'turn, round' + zadan. Dowr-e meydân — round the square.

The word, taken apart.
dowr zadan is built from dowr 'a turn, a round', borrowed from Arabic dawr 'turn' (EtymDB), and the native verb zadan 'to strike'.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to park, then the Persian for to turn round.

You'll want to know: یک طرفه.
یک طرفه (yektarafe) — "one-way (of a street)".
این کوچه یک طرفه است. In kuche yektarafe ast. This lane is one-way.
yek 'one' + taraf 'side' + -e. A two-way street is dotarafe.

The word, taken apart.
yektarafe is built from native yek 'one' and taraf 'side', a loan from Arabic ṭaraf 'side, edge', with the ending -e.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for to turn round, then the Persian for one-way.

You'll want to know: گواهینامه.
گواهینامه (govâhinâme) — "a driving licence; a certificate".
برادرم پارسال گواهینامه گرفت. Barâdaram pârsâl govâhinâme gereft. My brother got his driving licence last year.
govâhi 'witness, proof' + nâme 'letter'. In full: govâhinâme-ye rânandegi.

The word, taken apart.
govâhinâme is a native compound of govâhi 'witness, proof', from govâh 'a witness', and nâme 'letter, document', usually traced to Middle Persian nāmag.
