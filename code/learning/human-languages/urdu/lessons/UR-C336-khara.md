## You'll want to know: کھرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C336-SABIT336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کھرا** — *kharā* — "genuine; straight (honest)".

> وہ کھرا آدمی ہے، جھوٹ نہیں بولتا۔
>
> *voh kharā ādmī hai, jhūṭ nahīṅ boltā.*
>
> He is a straight man; he does not lie.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھرا** (*kharā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kharā*, "sharp, pure, good", under Sanskrit *khára*, "hard, sharp" (CDIAL 3819).
