## You'll want to know: அனுசரி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C502-NERUKKAM502-01]; assesses=[] -->

**அனுசரி** — *aṉucari* — "to accommodate; to adjust to".

> புது வீட்டில் எல்லோரையும் அனுசரித்து அவள் வாழ்ந்தாள்.
>
> *putu vīṭṭil ellōraiyum aṉucarittu avaḷ vāḻntāḷ.*
>
> In her new home she lived in harmony with everyone.

Past (I …): அனுசரித்தேன், *aṉucarittēṉ*.

Also to observe a custom: விரதம் அனுசரி.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அனுசரி** (*aṉucari*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anusarati*, "runs after, follows" (CDIAL 338), made into a Tamil verb in -இ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
