## You'll want to know: おしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C518-REGRET518-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おしい** — *oshii* — "so close; regrettable".

> **もし いちてんで まけたら、おしいでしょう。** — *moshi ichiten de maketara, oshii deshō* — "If we lose by one point, it will be a real pity."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おしい** (*oshii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
