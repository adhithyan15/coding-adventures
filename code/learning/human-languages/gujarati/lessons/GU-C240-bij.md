## You'll want to know: બીજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C240-GARDEN240-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બીજ** — *bīj* — "seed".

> **મેં ખેતરમાં બીજ વાવ્યાં.** — *mẽ khetarmā̃ bīj vāvyā̃* — "I sowed seeds in the field."

Not **બીજું**, second.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બીજ** (*bīj*) is Sanskrit *bīja*, "seed", taken over almost whole, a tatsama. The inherited forms of the same word are Gujarati *bī* and *bīyũ*, "seed" (CDIAL 9250).
