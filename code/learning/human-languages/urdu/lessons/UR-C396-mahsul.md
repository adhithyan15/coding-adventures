## You'll want to know: محصول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C396-SHAHRAH396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**محصول** — *mahsūl* — "a toll; a duty (on goods)".

> شاہراہ پر ہر گاڑی محصول دیتی ہے۔
>
> *shāhrāh par har gāṛī mahsūl detī hai.*
>
> Every vehicle pays a toll on the highway.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محصول** (*mahsūl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maḥṣūl*, "what is gathered, revenue"; in Urdu it is a toll, or a duty paid on goods.
