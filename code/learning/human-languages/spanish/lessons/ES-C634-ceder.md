## You'll want to know: ceder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C634-PACTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ceder** — "to give way, to give in; to hand over".

> Al final, mi padre cedió.
>
> *In the end, my father gave in.*

*Ceda el paso* on a road sign — give way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ceder* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cedere* 'to go away, to yield' (EtymDB).
