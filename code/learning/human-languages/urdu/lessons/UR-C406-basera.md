## You'll want to know: بسیرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C406-BASERA406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بسیرا** — *baserā* — "a night's shelter; a roost".

> پرندے شام کو درختوں پر بسیرا کرتے ہیں۔
>
> *parinde shām ko darakhtoṅ par baserā karte haiṅ.*
>
> In the evening the birds roost in the trees.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بسیرا** (*baserā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *baserā*, "a place of staying", under *vāsakara*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11594).
