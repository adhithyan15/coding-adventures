## You'll want to know: पेंच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C515-PENCH515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पेंच** — *peñc* — "a screw".

> पंखे का एक पेंच ढीला हो गया।
>
> *Paṅkhe kā ek peñc ḍhīlā ho gayā.*
>
> One of the fan's screws has come loose.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेंच** (*peñc*) is usually traced to Persian *pēch*, "a twist, a screw".
