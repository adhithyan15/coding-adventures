## You'll want to know: el coraje
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C722-VALOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el coraje** — "guts, courage; anger".

> Tuvo el coraje de hablar.
>
> *She had the courage to speak.*

In Mexico and other countries *me da coraje* means it makes me angry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coraje* was borrowed from Old Occitan *coratge* (EtymDB), built on Latin *cor* "heart"; English *courage* is the French form of the same word.
