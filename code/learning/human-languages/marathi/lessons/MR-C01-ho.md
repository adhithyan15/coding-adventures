## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-HO-01] -->

*(Skim if you read Devanagari.)* **ह** (*ha*) + the *o*-mātrā **ो** → **हो**
(*ho*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-HO-01] -->

**हो** (*ho*, "yes") is Marathi's plain affirmative — distinct from Hindi's
*hāṁ*. Its origin is not settled: CDIAL lists a Marathi *ho*, with no gloss, under Sanskrit *ho*, a cry of summons or surprise (CDIAL 14172). The
polite, fuller "yes" is *ho, baraṃ āhe* ("yes, it's fine").
