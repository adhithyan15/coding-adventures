# ఈలోగా (īlōgā) — in the meantime, before then

## You'll want to know: ఈలోగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C317-ILOGA-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C144-ACT144-01, TE-LEX-C46-ASK-05, TE-LEX-C04-REEPU-KALUDDAAM-01, TE-LEX-C133-THINGS133-05] -->

**ఈలోగా** — *īlōgā* — "in the meantime, before then".

> **అమ్మ వండుతోంది. ఈలోగా నేను ఒక పుస్తకం చదివాను.**
>
> *amma vaṇḍutōndi. īlōgā nēnu oka pustakaṁ cadivānu.* — "Mother was cooking. In the meantime I read a book."

> **రైలు ఆరు గంటలకు వస్తుంది. ఈలోగా భోజనం చేద్దాం.**
>
> *railu āru gaṇṭalaku vastundi. īlōgā bhōjanaṁ cēddāṁ.* — "The train comes at six. Before then, let's eat."

**చేద్దాం**, "let's do", has the ending of **కలుద్దాం**, "let's meet".

**ఇంతలో**, which you know, and **ఈలోగా** overlap, and you will hear both. **ఇంతలో** leans toward
"at that very moment": something broke into the middle of a scene. **ఈలోగా** leans toward the time before something
else arrives, and what someone did with it.

Its first letter is one you have not written yet. The next lesson takes it out of this word and puts it in
your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఈలోగా** (*īlōgā*) is native Dravidian, and it reads as "within this". **ఈ**, *ī*, is "this" in front of a
noun, on the near-pointing base that also gives **ఇక్కడ** and **ఇప్పుడు** (DEDR 410). **లోగా**, *lōgā*,
"within", is listed in DEDR 698, in an older spelling, beside *lō*, the **లో** of **ఇంట్లో** and
**బాల్యంలో**.
