Lesson 1 of 5.
شبکه (shabake) — a network; a TV channel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to delete, then the Persian for an update.

You'll want to know: شبکه.
شبکه (shabake) — "a network; a TV channel".
این شبکه فیلمهای خوبی پخش میکند. in shabake filmhâ-ye khubi pakhsh mikonad. This channel shows good films.
Plural shabakehâ. A computer network too.

The word, taken apart.
شبکه (shabake) was borrowed from Arabic shabaka "a net" (EtymDB): a network is a net of links.

Lesson 2 of 5.
شبکه اجتماعی (shabake-ye ejtemâ'i) — a social network.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an update, then the Persian for a network.

You'll want to know: شبکه اجتماعی.
شبکه اجتماعی (shabake-ye ejtemâ'i) — "a social network".
او در یک شبکه اجتماعی عکس میگذارد. u dar yek shabake-ye ejtemâ'i 'aks migozârad. She posts photos on a social network.
Shabake "network" + ezafe + ejtemâ'i "social".

The word, taken apart.
شبکه اجتماعی (shabake-ye ejtemâ'i) joins shabake, from Arabic shabaka "a net", to ejtemâ'i, from Arabic ijtimā'ī "social" (both in EtymDB), with a Persian ezafe.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دسترسی (dastresi) — access.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a social network, then the Persian for a connection.

You'll want to know: دسترسی.
دسترسی (dastresi) — "access".
در روستا دسترسی به اینترنت کم است. dar rustâ dastresi be internet kam ast. In the village there is little access to the internet.
Dast "hand" + res-, from residan "to reach", + -i. Dastresi dâshtan be — to have access to.

The word, taken apart.
دسترسی (dastresi) is native Persian, built from dast "hand" + res-, the present stem of residan "to reach, to arrive", + -i. Access is what your hand can reach.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مجازی (majâzi) — virtual, online.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a connection, then the Persian for access.

You'll want to know: مجازی.
مجازی (majâzi) — "virtual, online".
این ترم کلاسها مجازی است. in term kelâshâ majâzi ast. This term the classes are online.
Fazâ-ye majâzi — cyberspace, the internet. It also means figurative.

The word, taken apart.
مجازی (majâzi) is built on majâz "figure of speech, a non-literal sense", usually traced to Arabic majāz, with the suffix -i: not real in the literal sense.
