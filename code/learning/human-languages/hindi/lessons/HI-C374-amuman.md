## You'll want to know: अमूमन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C374-ANDAZA374-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अमूमन** — *amūman* — "usually, generally".

> अगर आसमान साफ़ होता है तो अमूमन रात को ठंड होती है।
>
> *agar āsmān sāf hotā hai to amūman rāt ko ṭhaṇḍ hotī hai.*
>
> If the sky is clear, it is usually cold at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अमूमन** (*amūman*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿumūman*, "generally", which reached Hindi through Persian.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
