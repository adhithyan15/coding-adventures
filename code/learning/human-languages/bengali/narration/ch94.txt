Lesson 1 of 5.
দিনকাল (dinkāl) — these days, the times.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the beginning, then the Bengali for nowadays.

You'll want to know: দিনকাল.
দিনকাল — dinkāl — "these days, the times".

The word, taken apart.
দিনকাল (dinkāl) is din, "day", from Sanskrit dina (CDIAL 6328), + kāl, "time", from Sanskrit kāla (CDIAL 3084).

Lesson 2 of 5.
রাত্তির (rāttir) — night-time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for nowadays, then the Bengali for these days.

You'll want to know: রাত্তির.
রাত্তির — rāttir — "night-time".

The word, taken apart.
রাত্তির (rāttir) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit rātri, "night", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped (CDIAL 10702). The word inherited from it is rāt, which CDIAL lists.

Lesson 4 of 5.
কখনো (kôkhôno) — ever, sometimes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for night-time, then the Bengali for often.

You'll want to know: কখনো.
কখনো — kôkhôno — "ever, sometimes".

The word, taken apart.
কখনো (kôkhôno) is kôkhôn, "when", + -o, "even". CDIAL lists kakhan with takhan, "then", under Sanskrit tatkṣaṇa, "that very moment" (CDIAL 5640): the question head ka- on kṣaṇa, "moment".

Lesson 5 of 5.
শিগগির (shiggir) — soon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for often, then the Bengali for ever.

You'll want to know: শিগগির.
শিগগির — shiggir — "soon".

The word, taken apart.
শিগগির (shiggir) is usually traced to Sanskrit śīghra, "quick", with its cluster turned into a doubled g. CDIAL has that entry but lists no Bengali form under it (CDIAL 12484).
