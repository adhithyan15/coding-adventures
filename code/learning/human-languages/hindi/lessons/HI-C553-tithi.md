## You'll want to know: तिथि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C553-KRAM553-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तिथि** — *tithi* — "a date (formal); a day of the lunar month".

> परीक्षा की तिथि बदल गई है।
>
> *Parīkṣā kī tithi badal gaī hai.*
>
> The date of the exam has changed.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिथि** (*tithi*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *tithi*, "a lunar day" (Monier-Williams).
