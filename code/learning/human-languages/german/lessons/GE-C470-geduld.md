## You'll want to know: die Geduld
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C470-GEDULD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Geduld** — "patience".

> Mit kleinen Kindern braucht man viel Geduld.
>
> *With small children you need a lot of patience.*

No plural. *Die Geduld verlieren* — to lose patience.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Geduld* is inherited from Middle High German *gedult* and Old High German *gidult* (EtymDB), usually traced to Proto-Germanic *gaþuldiz*; *dulden* "to endure" is related.
