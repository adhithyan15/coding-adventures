## You'll want to know: लोलुपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C468-KRTAVEDIN468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लोलुपः** — *lolupaḥ* — "greedy".

> लोलुपः बालः मोदकान् खादति, मित्राय न ददाति।
>
> *lolupaḥ bālaḥ modakān khādati, mitrāya na dadāti.*
>
> The greedy boy eats the sweets and gives his friend none.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोलुपः** (*lolupaḥ*) MW builds it on the intensive of the root √lup, "to break", and for the sense "covetous, ardently longing" thinks it probably a corruption of *lolubha*, from √lubh, "to covet"; its history is not settled (MW).
