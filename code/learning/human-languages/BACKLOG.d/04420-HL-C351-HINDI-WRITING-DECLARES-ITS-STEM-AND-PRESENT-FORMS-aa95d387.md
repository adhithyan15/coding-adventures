## HL-C351 — Hindi writing declares its stem and present forms

Chapter 34's writing lesson explicitly isolates the stem **लिख-** and builds
**लिखता / लिखती / लिखते** in its central explanation, but its declaration
exposes only infinitive **लिखना**. The forms consequently appear as
glossed-but-never-taught candidates even though the lesson directly teaches
them.

Declare the stem and three present forms beside the infinitive. This makes the
lesson's complete modern Hindi family visible to reports and downstream study
artifacts without adding new material.
