## You'll want to know: le retraité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C313-RETRAITE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le retraité** — "the retired person, the pensioner".

> Les retraités se retrouvent au café le matin.
>
> *The pensioners meet at the café in the morning.*

Feminine: *la retraitée*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*retraité* is built on *retraite* "retirement", from Old French *retraire*, inherited from Latin *retrahere* "to draw back" (Lewis & Short).
