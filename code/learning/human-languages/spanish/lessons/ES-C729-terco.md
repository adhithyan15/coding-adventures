## You'll want to know: terco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C729-PEREZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**terco** — "stubborn".

> Es tan terco como su padre.
>
> *He is as stubborn as his father.*

*Cabezota* is the colloquial word in Spain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

For *terco* the origin is not settled. It is found in old Spanish, and no Latin or Germanic source has been proved.
