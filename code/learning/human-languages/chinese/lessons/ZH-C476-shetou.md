## You'll want to know: shétou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C476-NECK476-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shétou* — "tongue".

> *wǒ de shétou téng le liǎng tiān* — My tongue has been sore for two days.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shétou* is built from *shé* 'the tongue' and 头, a noun ending here, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zyet* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the first under two readings and glosses *zyet* as the tongue in the mouth; the second is said light.
