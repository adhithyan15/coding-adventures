# Retrieval 1 — greeting, or question?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-NIHAO, ZH-LEX-NIHAOMA-01, ZH-LEX-MA-01, ZH-LEX-SHU-01, ZH-PERFORMANCE-PRIMARY-THREE-FOUR-SKILL-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **hello**, then **how are you**, and name the difference between
them. Then say **book**, and recall your four scores from the primary-school payoff.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-NIHAO, ZH-LEX-NIHAOMA-01, ZH-ORTHO-NIHAOMA-01, ZH-SCRIPT-MA-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-MA-QUESTION-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Hear the two meanings in mixed order. Say each Mandarin answer, read its
unpointed card, then write it. Look at the last character before deciding
whether you were greeted or asked.
