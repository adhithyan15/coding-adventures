Arabic, chapter 391: Research.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
باحث (bāḥith) — a researcher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a scholarship, then the Arabic for to enrol.

You'll want to know: باحث.
باحث — bāḥith — "a researcher".
يعمل الباحث في الجامعة منذ سنوات. yaʿmalu al-bāḥith fī al-jāmiʿa mundhu sanawāt. The researcher has worked at the university for years.

The word, taken apart.
باحث (bāḥith) is built on the native Arabic root b-ḥ-th, "to dig, to search" (Lane). Bāḥith is the active participle of baḥatha, to search: a researcher.

درست الموسيقى في معهد صغير. darastu al-mūsīqā fī maʿhad ṣaghīr. I studied music at a small institute.

معهد (maʿhad) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-h-d, "to enjoin; to know a place" (Lane). A maʿhad is a place one knows and comes back to; in modern use, an institute.
