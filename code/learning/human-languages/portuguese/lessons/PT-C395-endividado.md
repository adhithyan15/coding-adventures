## You'll want to know: endividado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C395-LUCRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**endividado** — "in debt".

> Muitas famílias estão endividadas.
>
> *Many families are in debt.*

Built on *dívida* — debt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*endividado* is built from *en-* and *dívida* "debt", from Latin *dēbita* "things owed", on *dēbēre* "to owe" (Lewis & Short).
