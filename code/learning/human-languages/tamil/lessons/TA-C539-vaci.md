## You'll want to know: வாசி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C539-ISAI539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**வாசி** — *vāci* — "to play an instrument; to read aloud".

> அவர் கச்சேரியில் வீணை வாசித்தார்.
>
> *avar kaccēriyil vīṇai vācittār.*
>
> He played the veena at the concert.

Past (I …): வாசித்தேன், *vācittēṉ*.

Any instrument can take வாசி; a flute may also take ஊது (*ūtu*), to blow. It also means "to read".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **வாசி** (*vāci*) is not settled; one suggestion links it with Sanskrit *vādya*, "a musical instrument, to be played" (CDIAL 11511).
