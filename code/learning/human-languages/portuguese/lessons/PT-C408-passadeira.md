## You'll want to know: a passadeira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C408-PEOES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a passadeira** — "the zebra crossing".

> O carro parou para nós na passadeira.
>
> *The car stopped for us at the zebra crossing.*

In Brazil: *a faixa de pedestres*. *Uma passadeira* is also a long, narrow rug.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*passadeira* is built on *passar* 'to cross, to pass' (EtymDB) with *-deira*, a place for doing it; *passar* goes back to Latin *passus* 'step'.
