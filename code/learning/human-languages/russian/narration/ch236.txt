Russian, chapter 236: To count, To measure, To compare, To check, To fill in.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
считать (schitát) — to count; to consider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to smile, then the Russian for to be angry.

You'll want to know: считать.
считать (schitát) — "to count; to consider".

The word, taken apart.
считать (schitát) is с- with читать, "to read", which once meant "to count", as in число, "a number".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Russian for to be angry, then the Russian for to count; to consider.

You'll want to know: измерять.
измерять (izmeryát) — "to measure".

The word, taken apart.
измерять (izmeryát) is из-, "out, through", with мерять, from мера, "a measure", which Vasmer relates to Latin mētior, "I measure".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to count; to consider, then the Russian for to measure.

You'll want to know: сравнивать.
сравнивать (srávnivat) — "to compare".

The word, taken apart.
сравнивать (srávnivat) is с- with -равнивать, from равный, "equal", the Church Slavonic shape of ровный, "level": to compare is to set things level.
