## You'll want to know: पाद्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C402-DHARMASHALA402-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पाद्यम्** — *pādyam* — "water for washing the feet".

> स्वामी अतिथये पाद्यं ददाति।
>
> *svāmī atithaye pādyaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The host gives the guest water to wash his feet.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाद्यम्** (*pādyam*) is built on *pāda*, "a foot", with the ending *-ya*; MW gives *pādya* as belonging to the foot and, as a noun, water for washing the feet, offered to a guest (MW).
