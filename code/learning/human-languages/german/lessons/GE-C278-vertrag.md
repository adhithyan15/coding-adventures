## You'll want to know: der Vertrag
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C278-VERHANDELN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Vertrag** — "the contract, the treaty".

> Sie lasen den Vertrag genau.
>
> *They read the contract carefully.*

Plural: *die Verträge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vertrag* is built on *vertragen* "to agree, to bear with", from *tragen* "to carry", Proto-Germanic *draganą* "to draw, to drag" (EtymDB).
