## You'll want to know: kètīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C159-ROOM159-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kètīng* — "living room; sitting room".

> *kèrén zài kètīng* — The guests are in the living room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kètīng* is built from 客 'guest' and *tīng* 'hall'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *khaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the second is listed there as *theng*, glossed 'a hall', a different word from the *tīng* of listening: the hall for guests.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
