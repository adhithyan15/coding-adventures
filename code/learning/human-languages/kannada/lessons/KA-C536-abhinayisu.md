# ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು (abhinayisu) — to act

## You'll want to know: ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C536-NATAKA536-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು** — *abhinayisu* — "to act".

> ಆ ಸಿನಿಮಾದಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ ಅಭಿನಯಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *ā sinimādalli nanna snēhita abhinayisidanu.*
>
> My friend acted in that film.

Past (I …): ಅಭಿನಯಿಸಿದೆ, *abhinayiside*.

ಅಭಿನಯ is acting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು** (*abhinayisu*) is built on Sanskrit *abhinaya*, from *abhi-nī*, "to bring near, to act out" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*.
