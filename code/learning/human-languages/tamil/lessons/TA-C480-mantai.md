## You'll want to know: மண்டை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C480-MUKAM480-01]; assesses=[] -->

**மண்டை** — *maṇṭai* — "the skull; the head".

> வெயிலில் நின்றதால் எனக்கு மண்டை வலித்தது.
>
> *veyilil niṉṟatāl eṉakku maṇṭai valittatu.*
>
> Standing in the sun gave me a headache.

Everyday and blunt for the head; தலை is the neutral word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மண்டை** (*maṇṭai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4682); the entry also gives the senses of a begging bowl and an earthen vessel.
