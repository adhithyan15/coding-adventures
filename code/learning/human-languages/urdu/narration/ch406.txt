Urdu, chapter 406: Nights Outdoors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لکڑی (lakṛī) — wood; firewood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to send for, then the Urdu for a tip (for service).

You'll want to know: لکڑی.
لکڑی — lakṛī — "wood; firewood".
ہم جنگل سے لکڑی لائے۔ ham jangal se lakṛī lāe. We brought wood back from the forest.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
لکڑی (lakṛī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lakṛī, "a stick, firewood", under Sanskrit lakuṭa, "a club" (CDIAL 10875).

Lesson 2 of 5.
سلگانا (sulgānā) — to kindle (a fire); to light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a tip (for service), then the Urdu for wood.

You'll want to know: سلگانا.
سلگانا — sulgānā — "to kindle (a fire); to light".
سارا نے لکڑی سے آگ سلگائی۔ sārā ne lakṛī se āg sulgāī. Sara got a fire going with the wood.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چولہا سلگایا, us ne chūlhā sulgāyā.

The word, taken apart.
سلگانا (sulgānā) is built on sulagnā, "to smoulder, to catch fire", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit saṃlagna, "attached, stuck together" (CDIAL 12999): this is the form for setting a fire going.

Lesson 4 of 5.
شامیانہ (shāmiyānā) — a canopy; a large awning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to kindle (a fire), then the Urdu for to wrap oneself in.

You'll want to know: شامیانہ.
شامیانہ — shāmiyānā — "a canopy; a large awning".
میدان میں ایک بڑا شامیانہ تھا۔ maidān meṅ ek baṛā shāmiyānā thā. There was a big canopy on the field.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
شامیانہ (shāmiyānā) is usually traced to Persian shāmiyāna, "a canopy"; weddings and camps put one up against the sun and the rain.
