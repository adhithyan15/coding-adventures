Kannada, chapter 337: Saying Goodbye.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಿಧನ (nidhana) — a passing away; a death.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to fight, then the Kannada for to retreat.

You'll want to know: ನಿಧನ.
ನಿಧನ — nidhana — "a passing away; a death".
ಹಿರಿಯ ಲೇಖಕರ ನಿಧನದ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಬಂತು. hiriya lēkhakara nidhanada suddi beḷagge bantu. The news of the senior writer's passing came in the morning.
The polite, formal word for a death, used in the news: ಅವರು ನಿಧನರಾದರು, he passed away.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಧನ (nidhana) is borrowed from Sanskrit nidhana, "settling down, residence", and also "conclusion, death" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಅಂತ್ಯಕ್ರಿಯೆ (antyakriye) — a funeral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to retreat, then the Kannada for a passing away.

You'll want to know: ಅಂತ್ಯಕ್ರಿಯೆ.
ಅಂತ್ಯಕ್ರಿಯೆ — antyakriye — "a funeral".
ಅಜ್ಜನ ಅಂತ್ಯಕ್ರಿಯೆಗೆ ಊರಿನ ಜನರೆಲ್ಲ ಬಂದರು. ajjana antyakriyege ūrina janarella bandaru. The whole village came to Grandfather's funeral.
ಅಂತ್ಯ, the last, and ಕ್ರಿಯೆ, a rite: the last rites.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂತ್ಯಕ್ರಿಯೆ (antyakriye) is borrowed from Sanskrit antyakriyā, "the last rites, a funeral", from antya "last" and kriyā "an act, a rite" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸ್ಮಶಾನ (smaśāna) — a cremation ground; a cemetery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a passing away, then the Kannada for a funeral.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಮಶಾನ.
ಸ್ಮಶಾನ — smaśāna — "a cremation ground; a cemetery".
ಸ್ಮಶಾನ ಊರಿನ ಹೊರಗೆ ನದಿಯ ದಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ. smaśāna ūrina horage nadiya daḍadalli ide. The cremation ground is outside the village, on the river bank.
Used for both a cremation ground and a burial ground.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಮಶಾನ (smaśāna) is borrowed from Sanskrit śmaśāna, "a place for burning the dead, a burial place" (CDIAL 12658).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಶ್ರದ್ಧಾಂಜಲಿ (śraddhāñjali) — a tribute (to the dead).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a funeral, then the Kannada for a cremation ground.

You'll want to know: ಶ್ರದ್ಧಾಂಜಲಿ.
ಶ್ರದ್ಧಾಂಜಲಿ — śraddhāñjali — "a tribute (to the dead)".
ಶಾಲೆಯ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಸೈನಿಕರಿಗೆ ಶ್ರದ್ಧಾಂಜಲಿ ಅರ್ಪಿಸಿದರು. śāleya makkaḷu sainikarige śraddhāñjali arpisidaru. The school children paid tribute to the soldiers.
A tribute is offered to the dead, often with a minute of silence.

The word, taken apart.
ಶ್ರದ್ಧಾಂಜಲಿ (śraddhāñjali) is built from Sanskrit: śraddhā, "faith, trust" (CDIAL 12678), and añjali, "the hollowed hands placed together" (CDIAL 171), an offering made with joined hands.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಂತೈಸು (santaisu) — to comfort; to console.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cremation ground, then the Kannada for a tribute.

You'll want to know: ಸಂತೈಸು.
ಸಂತೈಸು — santaisu — "to comfort; to console".
ನೆರೆಯವರು ಅಳುತ್ತಿದ್ದ ಅಜ್ಜಿಯನ್ನು ಸಂತೈಸಿದರು. nereyavaru aḷuttidda ajjiyannu santaisidaru. The neighbours consoled the grandmother, who was crying.
Past (I …): ಸಂತೈಸಿದೆ, santaiside.
The person comforted takes -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂತೈಸು (santaisu) is usually traced to Sanskrit sāntva, "consolation, gentle words" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
