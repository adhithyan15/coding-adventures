## You'll want to know: えんそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-05]; assesses=[] -->

**えんそう** — *ensō* — "a musical performance; a recital".

> **せんしゅう いもうとの えんそうを ききました。** — *senshū imōto no ensō o kikimashita* — "Last week I listened to my younger sister's recital."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんそう** (*ensō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *en*, "perform" (Middle Chinese *yenX*) and *sō*, "play music" (*tsuwH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
