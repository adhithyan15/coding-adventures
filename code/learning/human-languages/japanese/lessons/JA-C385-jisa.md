## You'll want to know: じさ

**じさ** — *jisa* — "a time difference".

> **じさで つかれました。** — *jisa de tsukaremashita* — "The time difference left me tired."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じさ** (*jisa*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "time" (Middle Chinese *dzyi*) and *sa*, "difference" (*tsrhae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
