## You'll want to know: abonar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C632-PAGO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**abonar** — "to pay; to credit".

> Puede abonar la cantidad con tarjeta.
>
> *You can pay the amount by card.*

Formal, on bills and notices. It also means to fertilise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abonar* is built on *bueno*, inherited from Latin *bonus* 'good' (EtymDB): it first meant to make good, then to pay.
