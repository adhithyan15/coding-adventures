## You'll want to know: ഉടയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C572-VITTUJOLI572-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉടയുക** — *uṭayuka* — "to shatter; to break into pieces".

> കയ്യിൽ നിന്ന് വീണ് ഗ്ലാസ് ഉടഞ്ഞു.
>
> *kayyil ninnŭ vīṇŭ glāsŭ uṭaññu.*
>
> The glass fell from my hand and shattered.

Past: ഉടഞ്ഞു, *uṭaññu*.

Of glass, pots and eggs; ഉടയ്ക്കുക is to break something. The past is ഉടഞ്ഞു.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉടയുക** (*uṭayuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 946 gives the Malayalam verb as to break, as pots or nuts do, beside a transitive partner for breaking something to pieces (DEDR 946).
