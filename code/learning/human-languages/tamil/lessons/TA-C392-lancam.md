## You'll want to know: லஞ்சம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C392-ULAL392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**லஞ்சம்** — *lañcam* — "a bribe".

> அவர் லஞ்சம் வாங்கினால் வேலையை இழப்பார்.
>
> *avar lañcam vāṅkiṉāl vēlaiyai iḻappār.*
>
> If he takes a bribe, he will lose his job.

லஞ்சம் வாங்கு, to take a bribe; லஞ்சம் கொடு, to give one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**லஞ்சம்** (*lañcam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lañcā*, "a present, a bribe" (Monier-Williams), with the Tamil noun ending -அம்.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
