# ನಾಶಮಾಡು (nāśamāḍu) — to destroy

## You'll want to know: ನಾಶಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C367-KHALA367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಶಮಾಡು** — *nāśamāḍu* — "to destroy".

> ಖಳನಾಯಕ ಇಡೀ ಊರನ್ನು ನಾಶಮಾಡಲು ಬಯಸಿದನು.
>
> *khaḷanāyaka iḍī ūrannu nāśamāḍalu bayasidanu.*
>
> The villain wanted to destroy the whole town.

Past (I …): ನಾಶಮಾಡಿದೆ, *nāśamāḍide*.

ನಾಶ, ruin, with ಮಾಡು. Something destroyed ನಾಶವಾಯಿತು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಶಮಾಡು** (*nāśamāḍu*) is built from Sanskrit *nāśa*, "destruction, ruin" (CDIAL 7084), and native *māḍu*, "to do" (DEDR 4797).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
