Urdu, chapter 332: What Follows From It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نقصان (nuqsān) — harm; a loss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be linked, then the Urdu for connected.

You'll want to know: نقصان.
نقصان — nuqsān — "harm; a loss".
بارش سے کسانوں کو نقصان ہوا۔ bārish se kisānoṅ ko nuqsān huā. The rain did the farmers harm.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
نقصان (nuqsān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic nuqṣān, "a decrease, a loss".

Lesson 3 of 5.
رد عمل (radd-e-amal) — a reaction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for harm, then the Urdu for a means.

You'll want to know: رد عمل.
رد عمل — radd-e-amal — "a reaction".
اس خبر پر لوگوں کا رد عمل کیا تھا؟ is khabar par logoṅ kā radd-e-amal kyā thā? How did people react to this news?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
رد عمل (radd-e-amal) is built from two loans, each usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic: Arabic radd, "a turning back", and Arabic ʿamal, "an action". An action sent back.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بگاڑنا (bigāṛnā) — to spoil; to make worse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a means, then the Urdu for a reaction.

You'll want to know: بگاڑنا.
بگاڑنا — bigāṛnā — "to spoil; to make worse".
بارش نے ہمارا کھیل بگاڑ دیا۔ bārish ne hamārā khel bigāṛ diyā. The rain spoiled our game.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کام بگاڑا, us ne kām bigāṛā.

The word, taken apart.
بگاڑنا (bigāṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bigāṛnā, "to spoil", under Sanskrit vighaṭate, "breaks apart" (CDIAL 11673).

Past (it …): وہ بیماری پھیل گئی, voh bīmārī phail gaī.

The word, taken apart.
پھیلنا (phailnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phailnā, "to spread", under Sanskrit prathita, "spread out" (CDIAL 8651).
