## You'll want to know: servīlis, servīle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C530-ERUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**servīlis, servīle** — "of a slave, slavish".

> Labor servīlis dūrus est.
>
> *A slave's work is hard.*

Two endings, from *servus*. English *servile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**servīlis** is built on *servus* "slave" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *servile* comes from it.
