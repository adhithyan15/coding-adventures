## You'll want to know: происходить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C273-PROISKHODIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**происходить** (*proiskhodít*) — "to happen, to take place".

> Это происходит каждую зиму.
>
> *Éto proiskhódit kázhduyu zímu.*
>
> This happens every winter.

Imperfective; the perfective is *произойти* (*proizoytí*), past *произошёл* (*proizoshyól*): *что произошло?* — what happened?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*происходить* is built on *про-*, *из-* and *ходить* "to go", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *xoditi* (EtymDB): to come out, to come about.
