## You'll want to know: ひたす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C492-BOIL492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひたす** — *hitasu* — "to dip in liquid; to steep".

> **まめを みずに ひたします。** — *mame o mizu ni hitashimasu* — "I soak the beans in water."

Polite **ひたします**, *hitashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが まめを みずに ひたしました。**, *haha ga mame o mizu ni hitashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひたす** (*hitasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
