Arabic, chapter 320: The media.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
إعلام (iʿlām) — the media; informing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to announce a death, then the Arabic for mourning.

You'll want to know: إعلام.
إعلام — iʿlām — "the media; informing".
تكلم الإعلام كثيرا عن الحادث. takallama al-iʿlām kathīran ʿan al-ḥādith. The media talked a lot about the accident.

The word, taken apart.
إعلام (iʿlām) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-l-m (Lane), "to know", the root of ʿilm, knowledge. Iʿlām is the verbal noun of Form IV aʿlama, "to let someone know".

نشرت وكالة (wakāla) الأنباء الخبر صباحا. nasharat wakālat al-anbāʾ al-khabar ṣabāḥan. The news agency put out the story in the morning.
وكالة (wakāla) سفر: a travel agency.

The word, taken apart.
وكالة (wakāla) is built on the native Arabic root w-k-l (Lane), "to entrust an affair to someone". A wakīl acts for others; a wakāla is the office that does.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the media, then the Arabic for an agency.

You'll want to know: مصدر.
مصدر — maṣdar — "a source".
قال مصدر في الشرطة إن اللص هرب. qāla maṣdar fī ash-shurṭa inna al-liṣṣ haraba. A police source said the thief had escaped.
Plural: مصادر, maṣādir.
In grammar the مصدر (maṣdar) is the verbal noun.

The word, taken apart.
مصدر (maṣdar) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-d-r (Lane), "to go back from the watering place". Maṣdar is the place a thing comes out of: a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
أفاد (afāda) — to give news; to be of use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an agency, then the Arabic for a source.

You'll want to know: أفاد.
أفاد — afāda — "to give news; to be of use".
أفادت الشرطة بأن الطريق مغلق. afādat ash-shurṭa bi-anna aṭ-ṭarīq mughlaq. The police reported that the road was closed.
Present: يفيد, yufīd.
In the news: أفاد (afāda) مراسلنا بأن …, our correspondent reports that …. Elsewhere: هذا يفيد, this helps.

The word, taken apart.
أفاد (afāda) is built on the native Arabic root f-y-d (Lane), where fāʾida is a gain or benefit. Form IV afāda is "to give someone a benefit", and so to give him information.

أوضح (awḍaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root w-ḍ-ḥ (Lane), "to be plain, clear", the root of wāḍiḥ, clear. It is Form IV, "to make clear".
