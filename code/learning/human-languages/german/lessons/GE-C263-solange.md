## You'll want to know: solange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C263-NACHDEM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**solange** — "as long as".

> Solange es regnete, blieben wir im Haus.
>
> *As long as it was raining, we stayed in the house.*

The verb goes to the end. It can also set a condition: *solange du pünktlich bist*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solange* joins *so* and *lange* "for a long time", built on *lang* "long", an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB).
