## You'll want to know: विस्मयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C449-VISMAYA449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्मयते** — *vismayate* — "is amazed".

> रात्रौ मीरा ताराः पश्यति, विस्मयते च।
>
> *rātrau mīrā tārāḥ paśyati, vismayate ca.*
>
> At night Mira sees the stars and is amazed.

Past (she …): सा विस्मितवती, *sā vismitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विस्मयते** (*vismayate*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and the root √smi, "to smile", the verb behind **विस्मयः**; MW gives "to wonder, be surprised or astonished at" (MW).
