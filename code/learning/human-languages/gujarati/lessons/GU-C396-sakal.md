## You'll want to know: સાંકળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C396-GUARD396-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંકળ** — *sā̃kaḷ* — "a chain".

> મીરાએ બારણાની સાંકળ ખોલી.
>
> *Mīrāe bārṇānī sā̃kaḷ kholī.*
>
> Mira undid the chain on the door.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંકળ** (*sā̃kaḷ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *śṛṅkhala*, "a chain"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 12580). On a train it is the chain you pull to stop it.
