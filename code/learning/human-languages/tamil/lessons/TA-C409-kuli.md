## You'll want to know: குழி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C409-KUZHI409-01]; assesses=[] -->

**குழி** — *kuḻi* — "a pit; a hole in the ground".

> சாலையில் ஒரு பெரிய குழி இருந்தது.
>
> *cālaiyil oru periya kuḻi iruntatu.*
>
> There was a big pit in the road.

The plural is குழிகள்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**குழி** (*kuḻi*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1818), "a pit, a hole, a hollow".
