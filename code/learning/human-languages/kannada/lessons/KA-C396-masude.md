# ಮಸೂದೆ (masūde) — a draft law; a bill

## You'll want to know: ಮಸೂದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C396-MASUDE396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಸೂದೆ** — *masūde* — "a draft law; a bill".

> ಮಂತ್ರಿ ಹೊಸ ಮಸೂದೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಮಾತಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *mantri hosa masūdeya bagge mātāḍidaru.*
>
> The minister spoke about the new bill.

A ಮಸೂದೆ becomes a ಕಾಯ್ದೆ once it is passed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಸೂದೆ** (*masūde*) is usually traced to Arabic *musawwada*, "a draft", through Hindustani *masauda*.
