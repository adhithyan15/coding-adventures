## You'll want to know: a embraiagem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C402-VOLANTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a embraiagem** — "the clutch (of a car)".

> No carro do meu pai, a embraiagem é muito pesada.
>
> *In my father's car, the clutch is very heavy.*

In Brazil: *a embreagem*. A car with automatic gears has no clutch pedal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embraiagem* was borrowed from French *embrayage*, built on *embrayer* 'to put into gear' with the ending *-age* (EtymDB).
