## You'll want to know: جابجا کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C537-CHANGES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جابجا کردن** (*jâbejâ kardan*) — "to move (things), to rearrange".

> ما مبلها را جابجا کردیم.
>
> *mâ moblhâ râ jâbejâ kardim.*
>
> We moved the sofas around.

*jâ* 'place' + *be* 'to' + *jâ* 'place': place to place. + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jâbejâ* is built on native Persian *jâ*, 'place', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), said twice with *be*, 'to': from place to place. *kardan* is inherited.
