## You'll want to know: बहू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C260-BAHU260-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बहू** — *bahū* — "a daughter-in-law; a young wife of the house".

> म्हारी बहू घणी हुसियार है।
>
> *mhārī bahū ghaṇī husiyār hai.*
>
> My daughter-in-law is very clever.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहू** (*bahū*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vadhū*, "a bride, a young wife". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *bahū*, "son's wife" (CDIAL 11250).
