Gujarati, chapter 379: Very, Extremely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અત્યંત (atyant) — extremely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a proportion, then the Gujarati for to tolerate.

You'll want to know: અત્યંત.
અત્યંત — atyant — "extremely".
જો તમે બપોરે ચાલશો તો તમને અત્યંત થાક લાગશે. jo tame bapore cālśo to tamne atyant thāk lāgśe. If you walk in the midday heat, you will be extremely tired.

The word, taken apart.
અત્યંત (atyant) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit atyanta, "beyond the proper end or limit" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
