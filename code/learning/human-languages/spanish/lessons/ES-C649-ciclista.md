## You'll want to know: el ciclista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C649-RUEDAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el ciclista** — "the cyclist".

> Hay muchos ciclistas en la carretera.
>
> *There are a lot of cyclists on the road.*

One form for both: *el ciclista*, *la ciclista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ciclista* comes from French *cycliste* (EtymDB), built on *cycle*, from Greek *kýklos* "circle, wheel".
