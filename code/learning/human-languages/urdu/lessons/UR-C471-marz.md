## You'll want to know: مرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C471-HEALTH471-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مرض** — *marz* — "an ailment; a disease".

> یہ پرانا مرض ہے۔
>
> *yih purānā marz hai.*
>
> This is a long-standing ailment.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرض** (*marz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maraḍ*, "illness".
