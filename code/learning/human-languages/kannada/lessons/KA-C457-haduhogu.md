# ಹಾದುಹೋಗು (hāduhōgu) — to pass through; to pass by

## You'll want to know: ಹಾದುಹೋಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C457-VIHARA457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾದುಹೋಗು** — *hāduhōgu* — "to pass through; to pass by".

> ನಮ್ಮ ರೈಲು ಸುರಂಗದ ಮೂಲಕ ಹಾದುಹೋಯಿತು.
>
> *namma railu suraṅgada mūlaka hāduhōyitu.*
>
> Our train passed through a tunnel.

Past (I …): ಹಾದುಹೋದೆ, *hāduhōde*.

ಮೂಲಕ ಹಾದುಹೋಗು, to pass through; ಪಕ್ಕದಿಂದ ಹಾದುಹೋಗು, to pass by.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾದುಹೋಗು** (*hāduhōgu*) is native Dravidian: *hādu*, the participle of *hāy*, "to cross, to step over" (DEDR 4087), with ಹೋಗು, to go.
