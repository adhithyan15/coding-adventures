## You'll want to know: दिवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C445-ULKA445-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दिवा** — *divā* — "by day".

> दिवा आकाशे सूर्यः अस्ति, नक्तं चन्द्रः।
>
> *divā ākāśe sūryaḥ asti, naktaṃ candraḥ.*
>
> By day the sun is in the sky, by night the moon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिवा** (*divā*) comes from *div*, "the sky, day", of which MW gives it as an old case form; the same word gives *divya*, "divine"; MW gives *divā* as by day, often set against *naktam*, "by night" (MW).
