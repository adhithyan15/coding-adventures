## You'll want to know: کسٹم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C399-HIFAZAT399-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کسٹم** — *kasṭam* — "customs (at a border)".

> کسٹم والے نے میرا صندوق کھولا۔
>
> *kasṭam vāle ne merā sandūq kholā.*
>
> The customs officer opened my trunk.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کسٹم** (*kasṭam*) is borrowed from English *customs*.
