## You'll want to know: fīō, fierī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C298-EVENIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**fīō, fierī** — "to become; to happen, to be done".

> Quid hīc fit? Cūr omnēs currunt?
>
> *What is going on here? Why is everyone running?*

*fīō, fierī, factus sum*. It serves as the passive of *faciō*: *fit* 'it is done, it happens'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fīō** is an inherited verb that serves as the passive of *faciō* (Lewis & Short). It is usually traced to the Proto-Indo-European root *\*bʰuH-*, "to become, to be", which also gives the Latin perfect *fuī* and English *be*.
