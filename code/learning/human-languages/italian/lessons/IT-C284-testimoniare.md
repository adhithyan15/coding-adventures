## You'll want to know: testimoniare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C284-INCHIESTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**testimoniare** — "to testify, to bear witness".

> Due persone hanno testimoniato contro di lui.
>
> *Two people testified against him.*

*Testimoniare a favore di qualcuno* — to testify for someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testimoniare* is built on Latin *testimōnium* "evidence", from *testis* "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
