## You'll want to know: ਘੋਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C244-KAM244-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੋਟਣਾ** — *ghoṭṇā* — "to mash; to grind smooth".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਸਾਗ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੇਰ ਘੋਟਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī sāg bahut der ghoṭdī hai.*
>
> Grandmother mashes the greens for a long time.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਾਗ ਘੋਟਿਆ, *maiṁ sāg ghoṭiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੋਟਣਾ** (*ghoṭṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ghaṭṭayati*, "rubs, touches, shakes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ghoṭṇā*, "to grind, rub, smoothe" (CDIAL 4417).
