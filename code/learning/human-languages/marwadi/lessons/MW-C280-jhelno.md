## You'll want to know: झेलणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C280-MAIDAN280-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झेलणो** — *jhelṇo* — "to catch a ball".

> भाई गेंद झेलै है।
>
> *bhāī gẽd jhelai hai.*
>
> My brother catches the ball.

Past (I …): म्हैं गेंद झेली, *mhaĩ gẽd jhelī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झेलणो** (*jhelṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhēlati*, "undergoes"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *jhelaï*, "to undergo, suffer", Hindi *jhelnā*, "to take", and Marathi *jhelṇẽ*, "to catch" (CDIAL 5413). CDIAL gives the catching sense only for Marathi; the Marwadi sense here is the everyday one.
