## You'll want to know: l'entusiasmo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C479-ALLEGRIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'entusiasmo** — "enthusiasm".

> Ha cominciato il nuovo lavoro con entusiasmo.
>
> *She started the new job with enthusiasm.*

The adjective is *entusiasta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entusiasmo* is a learned borrowing, usually traced through Late Latin *enthusiasmus* to Greek *enthousiasmós* "inspiration by a god", from *éntheos* "possessed by a god" (EtymDB, under French *enthousiasme*).
