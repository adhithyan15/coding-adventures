Portuguese, chapter 52: Pen, Paper, Notebook, Pencil, Bag.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a caneta — a pen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a knife, then the Portuguese for a fork.

You'll want to know: a caneta.
a caneta — "a pen".

The word, taken apart.
caneta is built from the word for a reed and -eta, "little" (EtymDB 2.1): a little reed, the old pen. The reed word from Latin canna, from Greek kanna (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a fork, then the Portuguese for a pen.

You'll want to know: o papel.

The word, taken apart.
papel from Catalan paper — borrowed. EtymDB 2.1 traces papel only to Old Portuguese papel; it derives Spanish papel from Catalan paper, and paper from Latin papyrus, "papyrus".

bolsa from Late Latin bursa, "purse", from Greek byrsa, "hide" — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 traces it only to Old Portuguese bolssa; it derives Spanish bolsa from bursa.
