Sanskrit, chapter 539: Being Careful.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अप्रमत्तः (apramattaḥ) — heedful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an astrologer, then the Sanskrit for draws near.

You'll want to know: अप्रमत्तः.
अप्रमत्तः — apramattaḥ — "heedful".
यदि त्वम् अप्रमत्तः असि, तर्हि त्वं न पतसि। yadi tvam apramattaḥ asi, tarhi tvaṃ na patasi. If you are heedful, you will not fall.

The word, taken apart.
अप्रमत्तः (apramattaḥ) is built on a, "not", and pramatta, "careless", from pra and the root √mad, "to be drunk"; MW gives apramatta as not careless, careful, attentive, vigilant (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: जागरूकः.
जागरूकः — jāgarūkaḥ — "watchful".
यदि रक्षकः जागरूकः अस्ति, तर्हि चौरः न आगच्छति। yadi rakṣakaḥ jāgarūkaḥ asti, tarhi cauraḥ na āgacchati. If the guard is watchful, the thief does not come.

The word, taken apart.
जागरूकः (jāgarūkaḥ) comes from the root √jāgṛ, "to be awake", with the ending -ūka; MW gives jāgarūka as wakeful, watchful (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: आशंकते.
आशंकते — āśaṅkate — "suspects; fears".
यदि माता चौरम् आशंकते, तर्हि सा द्वारं पिदधाति। yadi mātā cauram āśaṅkate, tarhi sā dvāraṃ pidadhāti. If my mother suspects a thief, she shuts the door.
Past (she …): सा चौरम् आशंकितवती, sā cauram āśaṅkitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
आशंकते (āśaṅkate) is built on ā and the root √śaṅk, "to doubt, to fear"; MW gives āśaṅk as to suspect, to fear, to doubt (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
संरक्षति (saṃrakṣati) — keeps safe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for watchful, then the Sanskrit for suspects.

You'll want to know: संरक्षति.
संरक्षति — saṃrakṣati — "keeps safe".
यदि त्वं धनं संरक्षसि, तर्हि तत् न नश्यति। yadi tvaṃ dhanaṃ saṃrakṣasi, tarhi tat na naśyati. If you keep the money safe, it will not be lost.
Past (he …): सः धनं संरक्षितवान्, saḥ dhanaṃ saṃrakṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
संरक्षति (saṃrakṣati) is built on sam, "wholly", and the root √rakṣ, "to protect", which also gives rakṣati; MW gives saṃrakṣ as to protect, to guard, to keep, to secure (MW).
