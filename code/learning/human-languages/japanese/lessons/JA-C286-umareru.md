## You'll want to know: うまれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C286-AKACHAN286-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うまれる** — *umareru* — "to be born".

> **いもうとが うまれた とき、月が きれいでした。** — *imōto ga umareta toki, tsuki ga kirei deshita* — "When my little sister was born, the moon was beautiful."

Polite **うまれます**, *umaremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あかちゃんが うまれました。**, *akachan ga umaremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うまれる** (*umareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
