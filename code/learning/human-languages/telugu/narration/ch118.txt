Telugu, chapter 118: Tomato, Potato, Aubergine, Ginger, Garlic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
టమాటా (ṭamāṭā) — a tomato.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for coconut, then the Telugu for an onion.

You'll want to know: టమాటా.
టమాటా — ṭamāṭā — "a tomato".

The word, taken apart.
టమాటా (ṭamāṭā) is a loan from English tomato, a word that reached English from Nahuatl through Spanish.

Lesson 2 of 5.
బంగాళాదుంప (baṅgāḷādumpa) — a potato.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an onion, then the Telugu for a tomato.

You'll want to know: బంగాళాదుంప.
బంగాళాదుంప — baṅgāḷādumpa — "a potato".

The word, taken apart.
బంగాళాదుంప (baṅgāḷādumpa) is "the Bengal tuber": baṅgāḷā, "Bengal", and native dumpa, "bulbous root", which DEDR 3326A lists with Kannada dumpa, "a root".

Lesson 3 of 5.
వంకాయ (vaṅkāya) — an aubergine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a tomato, then the Telugu for a potato.

You'll want to know: వంకాయ.
వంకాయ — vaṅkāya — "an aubergine".

The word, taken apart.
వంకాయ (vaṅkāya) is vaṅga, "brinjal", which DEDR 5301 lists with Kannada badane; and kāya, "unripe fruit". DEDR compares Sanskrit bhaṇṭākī, vaṅga and vṛntāka, "brinjal" (CDIAL 11503), names that CDIAL itself thinks probably go back to Dravidian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
అల్లం (allaṁ) — ginger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a potato, then the Telugu for an aubergine.

You'll want to know: అల్లం.
అల్లం — allaṁ — "ginger".

The word, taken apart.
అల్లం (allaṁ) is usually traced to Sanskrit ārdraka, "fresh ginger" (CDIAL 1341), reshaped to Telugu sounds: a tadbhava. Pali already has adda-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
వెల్లుల్లి (vellulli) — garlic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an aubergine, then the Telugu for ginger.

You'll want to know: వెల్లుల్లి.
వెల్లుల్లి — vellulli — "garlic".

The word, taken apart.
వెల్లుల్లి (vellulli) is "white onion": native vel-, "white" (DEDR 5496), and ulli, "onion, garlic", one word with Tamil uḷḷi (DEDR 705).
