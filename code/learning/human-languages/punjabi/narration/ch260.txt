Punjabi, chapter 260: Quarter Past and Half Past.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਵਾ (savā) — plus a quarter; a quarter past.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a voice, then the Punjabi for to dial a number.

You'll want to know: ਸਵਾ.
ਸਵਾ — savā — "plus a quarter; a quarter past".
ਬੱਸ ਸਵਾ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। bass savā aṭṭh vaje āundī hai. The bus comes at a quarter past eight.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਵਾ (savā) is inherited from Sanskrit sapāda, "increased by a fourth"; CDIAL lists Punjabi savā, "plus a quarter", under it (CDIAL 13134).

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: savā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: savā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bass savā aṭṭh vaje āundī hai. — The bus comes at a quarter past eight.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a voice, then the Punjabi for to dial a number, then say savā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਸਵਾ (savā) mean? ("Plus a quarter; a quarter past".) Say it once more.
