## You'll want to know: wěnzhòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C469-GENTLE469-04]; assesses=[] -->

*wěnzhòng* — "steady; calm and reliable".

> *tā bǐ tā jiějie wěnzhòng* — She's steadier than her big sister.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wěnzhòng* is built from *wěn* 'settled' and 重 'heavy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'wonX* and *drjowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as grain gathered and 'safe and settled', and the second 'thick' and 'careful'.
