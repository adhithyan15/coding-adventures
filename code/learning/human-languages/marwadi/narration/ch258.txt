Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 258: Worry and Patience.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चिंता (chintā) — worry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a sigh, then the Marwadi for a reproach.

You'll want to know: चिंता.
चिंता — chintā — "worry".
चिंता मत करो। chintā mat karo. Don't worry.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चिंता (chintā) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit cintā, "thought, care", taken over unchanged (CDIAL 4816).

Lesson 2 of 5.
भरोसो (bharoso) — trust; confidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a reproach, then the Marwadi for worry.

You'll want to know: भरोसो.
भरोसो — bharoso — "trust; confidence".
भाई माथै म्हारो पूरो भरोसो है। bhāī māthai mhāro pūro bharoso hai. I trust my brother completely.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भरोसो (bharoso) is inherited from a reconstructed bharavaśya, "dependence on support"; CDIAL lists Marwari bharoso, "confidence", under it (CDIAL 9398).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सहणो (sahṇo) — to bear; to put up with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for trust, then the Marwadi for patience.

You'll want to know: सहणो.
सहणो — sahṇo — "to bear; to put up with".
हूं दरद सहूं हूं। hū̃ darad sahū̃ hū̃. I bear the pain.
Past (I …): म्हैं दरद सह्यो, mhaĩ darad sahyo.

The word, taken apart.
सहणो (sahṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit sahate, "bears, suffers"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari sahaï, "bears", under it (CDIAL 13304).
