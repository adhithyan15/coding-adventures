Punjabi, chapter 270: Searching for an Address.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਢੂੰਢਣਾ (ḍhūṇḍhṇā) — to search for; to hunt for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to draw near, then the Punjabi for a pothole.

You'll want to know: ਢੂੰਢਣਾ.
ਢੂੰਢਣਾ — ḍhūṇḍhṇā — "to search for; to hunt for".
ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ ਢੂੰਢਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ shahir vich ḍākkhānā ḍhūṇḍhdā hāṁ. I search the town for the post office.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਥਾਣਾ ਢੂੰਢਿਆ, maiṁ thāṇā ḍhūṇḍhiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਢੂੰਢਣਾ (ḍhūṇḍhṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍhūṇḍh, "seek"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍhū̃ḍhṇā, "to seek", under it (CDIAL 6839).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਖੜ੍ਹਨਾ (khaṛhnā) — to stand; to stand waiting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a pothole, then the Punjabi for to search for.

You'll want to know: ਖੜ੍ਹਨਾ.
ਖੜ੍ਹਨਾ — khaṛhnā — "to stand; to stand waiting".
ਲੋਕ ਕਤਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੜ੍ਹਦੇ ਹਨ। lok katār vich khaṛhde han. People stand in the queue.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਤਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੜ੍ਹਿਆ, maiṁ katār vich khaṛhiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੜ੍ਹਨਾ (khaṛhnā) goes back to Sanskrit khaḍaka, "erect; a post", beside a reconstructed khaḍati, "stands"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khaṛā, "standing erect", under it (CDIAL 3784).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਲੰਘਾਉਣਾ (laṅghāuṇā) — to see someone across; to help across.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to search for, then the Punjabi for to stand.

You'll want to know: ਲੰਘਾਉਣਾ.
ਲੰਘਾਉਣਾ — laṅghāuṇā — "to see someone across; to help across".
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਸੜਕ ਲੰਘਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ roz bachchiāṁ nū̃ saṛak laṅghāundā hāṁ. Every day I help the children across the road.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਪੁਲ ਲੰਘਾਇਆ, maiṁ bachchiāṁ nū̃ pul laṅghāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੰਘਾਉਣਾ (laṅghāuṇā) is the causative partner of laṅghṇā, "to pass"; CDIAL lists Punjabi laṅghṇā under Sanskrit laṅghayati, "leaps over" (CDIAL 10905).
