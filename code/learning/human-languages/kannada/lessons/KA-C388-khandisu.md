# ಖಂಡಿಸು (khaṇḍisu) — to condemn

## You'll want to know: ಖಂಡಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C388-NAITIKA388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಂಡಿಸು** — *khaṇḍisu* — "to condemn".

> ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಆ ಕೆಟ್ಟ ಕೆಲಸವನ್ನು ಖಂಡಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *ellarū ā keṭṭa kelasavannu khaṇḍisidaru.*
>
> Everyone condemned that wicked act.

Past (I …): ಖಂಡಿಸಿದೆ, *khaṇḍiside*.

What you condemn takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಖಂಡನೆ, condemnation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖಂಡಿಸು** (*khaṇḍisu*) is built on Sanskrit *khaṇḍana*, "breaking, refuting" (CDIAL 3796), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
