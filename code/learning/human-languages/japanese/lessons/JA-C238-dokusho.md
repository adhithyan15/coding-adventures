## You'll want to know: どくしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C238-BOOKS238-03]; assesses=[] -->

**どくしょ** — *dokusho* — "reading; reading books".

> **せんしゅう としょかんで どくしょを しました。** — *senshū toshokan de dokusho o shimashita* — "Last week I did some reading at the library."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どくしょ** (*dokusho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *doku*, "read" (Middle Chinese *duwk*) and *sho*, "write" (*syo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
