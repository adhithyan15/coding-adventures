## You'll want to know: ǒurán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C528-CHANCE528-05]; assesses=[] -->

*ǒurán* — "accidental; happening by chance".

> *rúguǒ zhǐ fāshēng yí cì kěnéng shì ǒurán* — If it only happens once, it may be chance.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ǒurán* is built from *ǒu* and 然 'so', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nguwH* and *nyen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses *nguwH* 'unexpected', and lists a rising-tone reading, *nguwX*, glossed 'a pair'; the rising tone of ǒu matches that other reading.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
