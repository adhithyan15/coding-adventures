Arabic, chapter 350: Publishing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ناشر (nāshir) — a publisher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for someday, then the Arabic for right after.

You'll want to know: ناشر.
ناشر — nāshir — "a publisher".
قبل الناشر كتاب أختي. qabila an-nāshir kitāb ukhtī. The publisher accepted my sister’s book.
A publishing house is دار نشر.

The word, taken apart.
ناشر (nāshir) is built on the native Arabic root n-sh-r (Lane), "to spread out", the root of the taught nashara. Nāshir is the active participle, "one who publishes".
