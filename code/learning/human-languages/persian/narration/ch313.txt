Persian, chapter 313: Numbers and summaries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آمار (âmâr) — statistics, figures.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a viewer, then the Persian for news coverage.

You'll want to know: آمار.
آمار (âmâr) — "statistics, figures".
آمار (âmâr) تصادف ها امسال بالا بود. Âmâr-e tasâdof-hâ emsâl bâlâ bud. The accident figures were high this year.
Singular in form: âmâr neshân midahad "the statistics show".

The word, taken apart.
âmâr is a native Persian word; EtymDB glosses it "numeration, calculation" and gives no parent, so its deeper origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
برآورد کردن (barâvard kardan) — to estimate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for news coverage, then the Persian for statistics.

You'll want to know: برآورد کردن.
برآورد کردن (barâvard kardan) — "to estimate".
پلیس تعداد مردم را ده هزار نفر برآورد کرد. Polis te'dâd-e mardom râ dah hezâr nafar barâvard kard. The police estimated the crowd at ten thousand people.
barâvard "an estimate" is built on bar "up" and âvard, from âvardan "to bring": to bring up a figure. Present barâvard mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
barâvard joins bar "up, on" and âvard, the past stem of âvardan "to bring", both native Persian; kardan "to do" is inherited too. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تاکید کردن (ta'kid kardan) — to stress, to emphasise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for statistics, then the Persian for to estimate.

You'll want to know: تاکید کردن.
تاکید کردن (ta'kid kardan) — "to stress, to emphasise".
پزشک تاکید کرد که بیمار باید استراحت کند. Pezeshk ta'kid kard ke bimâr bâyad esterâhat konad. The doctor stressed that the patient had to rest.
With bar or a ke clause: bar in nokte ta'kid kard. Careful print adds a hamza sign. Present ta'kid mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
ta'kid is borrowed from Arabic ta'kīd, "emphasis, confirmation" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خلاصه (kholâse) — a summary; in short.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to estimate, then the Persian for to stress.

You'll want to know: خلاصه.
خلاصه (kholâse) — "a summary; in short".
گوینده خلاصه اخبار را خواند. Guyande kholâse-ye akhbâr râ khând. The newsreader read a summary of the news.
In speech also "in short, anyway": kholâse, dir residim "anyway, we arrived late".

The word, taken apart.
kholâse is borrowed from Arabic khulāṣa (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "the essence, an extract".
