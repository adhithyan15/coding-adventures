## You'll want to know: grattarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C486-PRURITO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**grattarsi** — "to scratch (oneself)".

> Non grattarti, peggiora!
>
> *Do not scratch, it makes it worse!*

Without *si*: *grattare* — to scratch, to grate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grattarsi* is built on *grattare* "to scratch", usually traced to a Germanic verb *krattōn* "to scratch".
