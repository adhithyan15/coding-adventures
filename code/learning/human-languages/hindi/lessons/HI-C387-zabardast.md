## You'll want to know: ज़बरदस्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C387-BEHAD387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़बरदस्त** — *zabardast* — "tremendous, powerful".

> अगर हमारी टीम जीत गई तो गाँव में ज़बरदस्त दावत होगी।
>
> *agar hamārī ṭīm jīt gaī to gā̃v meṁ zabardast dāvat hogī.*
>
> If our team wins, there will be a tremendous feast in the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़बरदस्त** (*zabardast*) is usually traced to Persian *zabardast*, "strong-handed, superior".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
