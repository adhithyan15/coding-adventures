## You'll want to know: みおろす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C404-FUUKEI404-05]; assesses=[] -->

**みおろす** — *miorosu* — "to look down on (from above)".

> **おかの うえから みなとを みおろしました。** — *oka no ue kara minato o mioroshimashita* — "From the top of the hill we looked down on the harbour."

Polite **みおろします**, *mioroshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが おかから まちを みおろしました。**, *kodomo ga oka kara machi o mioroshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みおろす** (*miorosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *mi*, "seeing" (from *miru*) and *orosu*, "to lower"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
