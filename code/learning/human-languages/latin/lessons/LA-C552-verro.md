## You'll want to know: verrō, verrere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C552-DOMI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**verrō, verrere** — "to sweep".

> Ancilla pavīmentum ātriī verrit.
>
> *The maid sweeps the floor of the hall.*

*verrō, verrere, verrī, versum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**verrō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to sweep, drag along the ground" (EtymDB).
