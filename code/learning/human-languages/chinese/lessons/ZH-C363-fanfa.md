## You'll want to know: fànfǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C363-PUBLIC363-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fànfǎ* — "to break the law".

> *hējiǔ yǐhòu kāichē fànfǎ* — Driving after drinking is against the law.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fànfǎ* is built from *fàn* and 法 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjomX* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to offend against' and 'to intrude', and the second 'a rule'.
