# ನಾಲ್ಕು — four, held together by a mark you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-MUURU-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Three was *mūru*. And the small slanting mark **್** — what did it
do to the letter under it?

## You'll want to know: ನಾಲ್ಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C07-NAALKU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಲ್ಕು** (*nālku*) — "**four**."

Tamil says *nāṉku*. Kannada turned that middle **ṉ** into an **l**, and left
everything else alone.

Read the word slowly: **ನಾ** (*nā*) · **ಲ್** (*l*, with your virama stripping
the vowel away) · **ಕು** (*ku*). Four is where the virama earns its keep — it is
what lets *l* and *k* sit together with no vowel between them.
