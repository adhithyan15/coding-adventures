## You'll want to know: jǔlì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C369-CLEAR369-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jǔlì* — "to give an example".

> *nǐ néng jǔlì shuōmíng ma* — Can you give an example?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jǔlì* is built from *jǔ* and *lì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjoX* and *ljejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to lift' and the second 'to compare'.
