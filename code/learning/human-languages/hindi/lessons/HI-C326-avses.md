## You'll want to know: अवशेष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C326-KHANDAHAR326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अवशेष** — *avśeṣ* — "remains".

> खंडहर में एक पुराने मंदिर के अवशेष भी हैं।
>
> *khaṇḍhar meṁ ek purāne mandir ke avśeṣ bhī haiṁ.*
>
> Among the ruins are the remains of an old temple too.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवशेष** (*avśeṣ*) is Sanskrit *avaśeṣa*, "a remainder, what is left" (Monier-Williams). It is mostly used in the plural sense: what is left of a building or a body.
