## You'll want to know: xuānbù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C291-INVENT291-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xuānbù* — "to announce officially".

> *xuéxiào xuānbù míngtiān fàngjià* — The school announced a day off tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuānbù* is built from *xuān* 'to make known' and *bù* 'to spread', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjwen* and *puH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to spread' and 'to make clear' and the second 'to spread everywhere'.
