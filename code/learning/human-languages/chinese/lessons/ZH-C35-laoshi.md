## You'll want to know: lǎoshī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C35-WORK35-03]; assesses=[] -->

*lǎoshī* — "teacher".

> *wǒ māma shì lǎoshī* — My mum is a teacher.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǎoshī* is built from lǎo 'old, respected' and shī 'teacher', both inherited from Middle Chinese: *lawX*, glossed 'aged', and *srij*, glossed 'a model to follow' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
