## You'll want to know: رعى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C478-QATI478-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رعى** — *raʿā* — "to graze; to tend (animals)".

> ترعى الأبقار في الحقل.
>
> *tarʿā al-abqār fī al-ḥaql.*
>
> The cows graze in the field.

Present: يرعى, *yarʿā*.

A shepherd is *rāʿī*, the one who tends.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رعى** (*raʿā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ʿ-y*, "to pasture, to tend" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
