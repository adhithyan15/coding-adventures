## You'll want to know: narrare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C323-FAVOLA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**narrare** — "to narrate, to tell (a story)".

> Il libro narra la storia di un ragazzo.
>
> *The book tells the story of a boy.*

A literary *raccontare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narrare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nārrāre* "to tell, to relate" (Lewis & Short), built on *gnārus* "knowing".
