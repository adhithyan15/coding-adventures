## You'll want to know: قاتل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C318-JENAYAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قاتل** (*qâtel*) — "a murderer, a killer".

> پلیس هنوز قاتل را پیدا نکرده است.
>
> *Polis hanuz qâtel râ peydâ nakarde ast.*
>
> The police have not found the murderer yet.

Same Arabic root as *qatl*: *qâtel* is "the one who kills". Plural *qâtelân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qâtel* is borrowed from Arabic *qātil*, "killer" (EtymDB), the active participle of the same root as *qatl*.
