# বৈঠকখানা (boiṭhôkkhānā) — a sitting room; a drawing room

## You'll want to know: বৈঠকখানা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C508-GHOR508-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বৈঠকখানা** — *boiṭhôkkhānā* — "a sitting room; a drawing room".

> দাদু বৈঠকখানায় বসে পত্রিকা পড়েন।
>
> *Dādu boiṭhôkkhānāy bôse pôtrikā pôṛen.*
>
> My grandfather sits in the drawing room and reads the magazine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বৈঠকখানা** (*boiṭhôkkhānā*) joins বৈঠক, "a sitting, a meeting", usually traced to Hindi *baiṭhak*, to *khāna*, "a house, a room", which is usually traced to Persian.
