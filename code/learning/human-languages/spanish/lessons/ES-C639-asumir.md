## You'll want to know: asumir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C639-FINAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**asumir** — "to take on; to accept".

> Asumo la responsabilidad.
>
> *I take responsibility.*

*Asumir un riesgo* — to take a risk. It does not usually mean to suppose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*asumir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *assumere* 'to take up, to adopt', built from *ad* and *sumere* 'to take' (EtymDB).
