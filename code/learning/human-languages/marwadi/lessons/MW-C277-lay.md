## You'll want to know: लय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C277-BHAJAN277-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लय** — *lay* — "rhythm".

> लय तेज है।
>
> *lay tej hai.*
>
> The rhythm is fast.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लय** (*lay*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *laya*, which Monier-Williams gives, in music, as "time" (Monier-Williams).
