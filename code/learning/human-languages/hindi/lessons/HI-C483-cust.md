## You'll want to know: चुस्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C483-TAQAT483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चुस्त** — *cust* — "spry; fit".

> अस्सी की उम्र में भी नानी बहुत चुस्त हैं।
>
> *assī kī umr meṁ bhī nānī bahut cust haiṁ.*
>
> Even at eighty, my grandmother is very spry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुस्त** (*cust*) is usually traced to Persian *chust*, "quick, active, tight". Clothes can be *cust* too: close-fitting.
