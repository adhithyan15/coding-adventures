# ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಗು (paṅkcar āgu) — to get a puncture

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C449-CAKRA449-02, KA-LEX-C449-CAKRA449-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tyre, then the Kannada for a spare tyre.

## You'll want to know: ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C449-CAKRA449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಗು** — *paṅkcar āgu* — "to get a puncture".

> ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಸ್ಕೂಟರ್ನ ಟಯರ್ ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *dāriyalli nanna skūṭarna ṭayar paṅkcar āyitu.*
>
> On the way my scooter tyre got a puncture.

Past (it …): ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಯಿತು, *paṅkcar āyitu*.

The tyre is the subject: ಟಯರ್ ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಯಿತು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಂಕ್ಚರ್ ಆಗು** (*paṅcar āgu*) joins a loan from English *puncture* and the native verb ಆಗು, to become.
