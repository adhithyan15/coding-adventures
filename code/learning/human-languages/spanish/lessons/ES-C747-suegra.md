## You'll want to know: la suegra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C747-POLITICA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la suegra** — "the mother-in-law".

> Mi suegra viene a comer el domingo.
>
> *My mother-in-law is coming for lunch on Sunday.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suegra* is inherited from spoken Latin *socra*, Latin *socrus* "mother-in-law" (EtymDB).
