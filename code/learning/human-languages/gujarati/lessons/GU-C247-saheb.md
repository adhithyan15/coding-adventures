## You'll want to know: સાહેબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C247-LIVING247-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સાહેબ** — *sāheb* — "boss, sir".

> **અમારા સાહેબ સવારે વહેલા આવે છે.** — *amārā sāheb savāre vahelā āve che* — "Our boss comes early in the morning."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાહેબ** (*sāheb*) is a loan from Arabic *ṣāḥib*, "companion, owner", which reached Gujarati through Persian as a respectful word for a master. Its Arabic root is *ṣ-ḥ-b* (Lane).
