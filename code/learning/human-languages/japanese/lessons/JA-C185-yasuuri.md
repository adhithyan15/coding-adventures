## You'll want to know: やすうり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C185-SALE185-02]; assesses=[] -->

**やすうり** — *yasuuri* — "bargain sale".

> **しゅうまつは やすうりが あります。** — *shūmatsu wa yasuuri ga arimasu* — "There is a bargain sale at the weekend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やすうり** (*yasuuri*) is native Japanese, built from *yasu*, the stem of *yasui*, "cheap", and *uri*, "selling", from *uru*, "to sell" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
