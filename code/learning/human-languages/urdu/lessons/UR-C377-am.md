## You'll want to know: عام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C377-BADTAR377-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عام** — *ām* — "common; ordinary".

> اگر یہ بیماری عام ہے تو دوا بھی ملے گی۔
>
> *agar yih bīmārī ām hai to davā bhī mile gī.*
>
> If this illness is common, the medicine will be easy to find.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عام** (*ām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿāmm*, "general, common to all".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
