Marathi, chapter 93: Door, Kitchen, Book, Exercise book, Pen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दार (dār) — a door.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a knife, then the Marathi for a bottle.

You'll want to know: दार.
दार — dār — "a door".

The word, taken apart.
दार (dār) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dvāra, "door" (CDIAL 6663). The same ancient word as English door.

The word, taken apart.
स्वयंपाकघर (svayampākghar) is Sanskrit svayampāka, "cooking for oneself": svayam, "self", and pāka, "cooking", with घर, "house". घर is usually taken from Sanskrit gṛha, "house" (CDIAL 4428).

Lesson 3 of 5.
पुस्तक (pustak) — a book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a door, then the Marathi for a kitchen.

You'll want to know: पुस्तक.
पुस्तक — pustak — "a book".

The word, taken apart.
पुस्तक (pustak) is Sanskrit pustaka, "book", a tatsama. CDIAL traces the Sanskrit word itself to Iranian, comparing Sogdian pwstk, "book", and Persian pōst, "skin" (CDIAL 8413). The inherited Marathi word is pothī, "book".

Lesson 4 of 5.
वही (vahī) — an exercise book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a kitchen, then the Marathi for a book.

You'll want to know: वही.
वही — vahī — "an exercise book".

The word, taken apart.
वही (vahī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vahikā, "diary, in rājavahikā 'king's diary'" (CDIAL 11460). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "account-book, ledger".
