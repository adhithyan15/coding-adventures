## You'll want to know: جمع کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C536-CHORES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جمع کردن** (*jam' kardan*) — "to tidy up; to gather".

> بچه ها اتاقشان را جمع کردند.
>
> *bachchehâ otâqashân râ jam' kardand.*
>
> The children tidied their room.

*jam'* 'gathering' + *kardan*. Also 'to collect' and, in sums, 'to add'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jam'* was borrowed from Arabic *jamʿ*, 'gathering' (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
