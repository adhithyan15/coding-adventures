## You'll want to know: bedeckt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C460-WETTERBERICHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bedeckt** — "overcast; covered".

> Morgens ist der Himmel bedeckt, später kommt die Sonne.
>
> *In the morning the sky is overcast; later the sun comes out.*

The past participle of *bedecken* 'to cover', used as an adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bedeckt* is the participle of *bedecken* 'to cover', from *decken*, inherited from Old High German *decchen* and Proto-Germanic *þakjaną* (EtymDB), the root of *Dach* 'roof'.
