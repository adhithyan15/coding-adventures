## You'll want to know: टोलवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C531-KHELADU531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**टोलवणे** — *ṭolavṇe* — "to hit away; to fend off".

> फलंदाजाने पहिला चेंडू लांब टोलवला.
>
> *phalandājāne pahilā ceṇḍū lāmb ṭolavlā.*
>
> The batsman hit the first ball a long way.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चेंडू टोलवला, *tyāne ceṇḍū ṭolavlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टोलवणे** (*ṭolavṇe*) is a Marathi verb for knocking something away; its origin is not settled.
