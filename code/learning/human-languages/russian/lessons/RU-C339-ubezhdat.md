## You'll want to know: убеждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C339-UBEZHDAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**убеждать** (*ubezhdát*) — "to convince, to persuade".

> Я убеждал друга поехать с нами, но он не захотел.
>
> *Ya ubezhdál drúga poyékhat s námi, no on ne zakhotél.*
>
> I tried to persuade my friend to come with us, but he did not want to.

Imperfective; perfective *убедить*, which has no form for *я* in the future. *Убеждать кого-то* + infinitive, or *в* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**убеждать** is the imperfective of *убедить*, on the root of *беда* "trouble", Proto-Slavic *\*běda* (EtymDB). The *жд* is the Church Slavonic way of softening *д*; native Russian softens it to *ж*, as in *вожу* beside *водить*.
