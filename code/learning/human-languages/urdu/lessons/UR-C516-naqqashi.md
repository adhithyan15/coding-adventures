## You'll want to know: نقاشی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C516-KHATTATI516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نقاشی** — *naqqāshī* — "carved or painted decoration".

> اس پرانے دروازے پر نقاشی ہے۔
>
> *is purāne darvāze par naqqāshī hai.*
>
> This old door has carving all over it.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقاشی** (*naqqāshī*) is built on *naqqāsh*, "a carver, a painter", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naqqāsh*, with the noun ending *-ī*.
