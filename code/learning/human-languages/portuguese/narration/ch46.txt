Portuguese, chapter 46: Potato, Tomato, Onion, Carrot, Beans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a batata — a potato.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for an orange, then the Portuguese for a grape.

You'll want to know: a batata.
a batata — "a potato".

What you've built.
