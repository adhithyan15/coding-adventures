Spanish, chapter 695: Fauna y flora.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
picar — to bite, to sting (insects); to be spicy; to nibble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the butterfly, then the Spanish for the small lizard.

You'll want to know: picar.
picar — "to bite, to sting (insects); to be spicy; to nibble".
Me picó un mosquito en el brazo. A mosquito bit me on the arm.
Esta salsa pica — this sauce is hot. Picar algo — to have a snack.

The word, taken apart.
picar comes from Vulgar Latin piccāre "to prick, to strike" (EtymDB); where that verb came from is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 6.
la fauna — the fauna, the wildlife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the small lizard, then the Spanish for to bite.

You'll want to know: la fauna.
la fauna — "the fauna, the wildlife".
La fauna de la isla es única. The wildlife on the island is unique.
The animals of a place: la fauna marina — sea life.

The word, taken apart.
fauna is a learned borrowing from Latin Fauna, a Roman goddess, the sister of the woodland god Faunus (Lewis & Short).

pescar — "to fish; to catch".
Mi padre va a pescar al río. My father goes fishing in the river.
El pescado is fish as food; el pez is a live fish.

The word, taken apart.
pescar is inherited from Vulgar Latin piscāre, Latin piscārī "to fish" (EtymDB), from piscis "fish".
