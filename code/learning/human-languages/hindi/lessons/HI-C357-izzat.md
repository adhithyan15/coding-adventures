## You'll want to know: इज़्ज़त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C357-MULYA357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**इज़्ज़त** — *izzat* — "honour, standing".

> गाँव में सब उसकी इज़्ज़त करते हैं।
>
> *gā̃v meṁ sab uskī izzat karte haiṁ.*
>
> Everyone in the village respects him.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इज़्ज़त** (*izzat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿizza*, "might, honour", which reached Hindi through Persian.
