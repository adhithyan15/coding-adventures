## You'll want to know: विभजति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C308-SAMUHA308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विभजति** — *vibhajati* — "divides; shares out".

> ते अन्नं सर्वेषु विभजन्ति।
>
> *te annaṃ sarveṣu vibhajanti.*
>
> They share the food out among everyone.

Past (he …): सः अन्नं विभक्तवान्, *saḥ annaṃ vibhaktavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विभजति** (*vibhajati*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and the root √bhaj, "to share", as in *bhajati*; MW gives *vi-bhaj* as to divide, distribute, apportion (MW).
