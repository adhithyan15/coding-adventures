## You'll want to know: filmer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C283-ANTENNE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**filmer** — "to film".

> Un homme a tout filmé avec son téléphone.
>
> *A man filmed everything with his phone.*

*Se faire filmer* — to get filmed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*filmer* is built on *film*, which was borrowed from English *film* "a thin skin, a coating" (EtymDB lists the verb without a parent).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
