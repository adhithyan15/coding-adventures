Mandarin Chinese, chapter 265: Shy or Brave.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
hàixiū — shy; bashful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for afraid that, then the Mandarin for as a matter of fact.

You'll want to know: hàixiū.
hàixiū — "shy; bashful".
nàge háizi hěn hàixiū — That child is shy.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
hàixiū is built from hài 'to harm' and xiū 'shame', both inherited from Middle Chinese, hajH and sjuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to harm' and the second 'shame'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
yǒnggǎn — brave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for as a matter of fact, then the Mandarin for shy.

You'll want to know: yǒnggǎn.
yǒnggǎn — "brave".
tā hěn yǒnggǎn — He's very brave.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Two third tones meet in this word: yǒng is said with a rising tone before gǎn, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
yǒnggǎn is built from yǒng and gǎn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, yowngX and kamX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'fierce' and the second 'brave' and 'to dare'.

Lesson 3 of 6.

dàfang — "generous; open-handed".
wǒ gēge hěn dàfang — My brother is very generous.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dàfang is built from 大 'big' and fāng 'square', both inherited from Middle Chinese, daH and pjang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the four quarters' and 'upright'. In the word the second loses its tone; how the pair came to mean generous is not settled.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: 小气.
小气 — xiǎoqì — "stingy; mean (with money)".
他很小气 tā hěn xiǎoqì — He's stingy.

The word, taken apart.
小气 (xiǎoqì) is built from 小 'small' and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sjewX and khj+jH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'breath'.
