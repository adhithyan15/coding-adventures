## You'll want to know: ないか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C450-CLINIC450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ないか** — *naika* — "internal medicine".

> **ねつが あるので、ないかに いきます。** — *netsu ga aru node, naika ni ikimasu* — "I have a fever, so I am going to internal medicine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ないか** (*naika*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nai*, "inside" (Middle Chinese *nwojH*) and *ka*, "department" (*khwa*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
