Hindi, chapter 399: Lines and Carriages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लाइन (lāin) — a line; a railway line.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to load (onto), then the Hindi for to seat (someone).

You'll want to know: लाइन.
लाइन — lāin — "a line; a railway line".
रेल की लाइन नदी के किनारे जाती है। rel kī lāin nadī ke kināre jātī hai. The railway line runs along the river bank.
Feminine.
Also a queue of people: लाइन (lāin) में खड़े रहिए, stay in the line.

The word, taken apart.
लाइन (lāin) is borrowed from English line, used in Hindi for a railway line, a row of people and a line on a page.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सीटी (sīṭī) — a whistle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an engine, then the Hindi for a sleeper carriage.

You'll want to know: सीटी.
सीटी — sīṭī — "a whistle".
ट्रेन ने लंबी सीटी दी। ṭren ne lambī sīṭī dī. The train gave a long whistle.
Feminine.
सीटी (sīṭī) देना or सीटी (sīṭī) बजाना, to blow a whistle.

The word, taken apart.
सीटी (sīṭī) goes back to sīṭṭa, "whistle", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants and treats as imitative of the sound itself (CDIAL 13427).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सरकना (saraknā) — to slide; to shift along.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a sleeper carriage, then the Hindi for a whistle.

You'll want to know: सरकना.
सरकना — saraknā — "to slide; to shift along".
ट्रेन धीरे आगे सरक रही थी। ṭren dhīre āge sarak rahī thī. The train was sliding slowly forward.
Past (it …): वह मेज़ थोड़ी आगे सरकी, vah mez thoṛī āge sarkī.

The word, taken apart.
सरकना (saraknā) goes back to Sanskrit sarati, "runs, flows, glides" (CDIAL 13250), which lists Hindi saraknā in the sense of drawing back; in everyday Hindi it is a slow slide along a surface.
