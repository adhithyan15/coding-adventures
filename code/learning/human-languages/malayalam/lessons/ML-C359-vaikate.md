## You'll want to know: വൈകാതെ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C359-VAIKATHE359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**വൈകാതെ** — *vaikāte* — "before long".

> വൈകാതെ ആംബുലൻസ് എത്തി.
>
> *vaikāte āmbulansŭ etti.*
>
> Before long the ambulance arrived.

The negative form of വൈകുക, to be late: without being late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വൈകാതെ** (*vaikāte*) is native Dravidian, the negative participle of വൈകുക (*vaikuka*), "to stay, to be late" (DEDR 5554): without delaying.
