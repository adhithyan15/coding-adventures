## You'll want to know: প্রকৃতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C434-PROKRITI434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রকৃতি** — *prôkriti* — "nature; the natural world".

> গ্রামে এসে মীরা প্রকৃতির শান্তি পেল।
>
> *grāme ese mīrā prôkritir shānti pelô.*
>
> Coming to the village, Mira found the peace of nature.

Also a person's nature, temperament.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রকৃতি** (*prôkriti*) is Sanskrit *prakṛti*, "the original or natural form of a thing, nature" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.
