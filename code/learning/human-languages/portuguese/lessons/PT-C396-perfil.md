## You'll want to know: o perfil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C396-CONTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o perfil** — "the profile".

> Mudei a foto do meu perfil.
>
> *I changed my profile photo.*

Also the side view of a face.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perfil* was borrowed from Spanish *perfil* (EtymDB), from Old Provençal *perfil* "outline", usually traced to Latin *fīlum* "a thread" (Lewis & Short).
