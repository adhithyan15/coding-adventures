## You'll want to know: le talon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C477-JAMBE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le talon** — "heel".

> Ces chaussures me font mal au talon.
>
> *These shoes hurt my heel.*

Also the heel of a shoe: *des talons hauts* — high heels.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le talon* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *tālo* (EtymDB), from Latin *tālus* "the ankle, the anklebone" (Lewis & Short).
