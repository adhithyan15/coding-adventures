## You'll want to know: ほしい

**ほしい** — *hoshii* — "wanted, wanting to have".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほしい** (*hoshii*) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
