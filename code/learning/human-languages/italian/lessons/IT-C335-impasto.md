## You'll want to know: l'impasto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C335-IMPASTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'impasto** — "the dough, the mixture".

> L'impasto deve essere morbido.
>
> *The dough must be soft.*

Any mixture you work with your hands.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impasto* is built on *impastare* "to knead", from *pasta*, Late Latin *pasta* "dough" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
