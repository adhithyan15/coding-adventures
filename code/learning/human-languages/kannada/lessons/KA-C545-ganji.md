## You'll want to know: ಗಂಜಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C545-BATTE545-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಂಜಿ** — *gañji* — "starch; rice gruel".

> ಅಮ್ಮ ಹತ್ತಿ ಸೀರೆಗೆ ಗಂಜಿ ಹಾಕಿದರು.
>
> *amma hatti sīrege gañji hākidaru.*
>
> Mother starched the cotton sari.

Also the water rice is cooked in, a light food for the sick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಂಜಿ** (*gañji*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1107 lists Kannada *gañji*, "rice-gruel, starch".
