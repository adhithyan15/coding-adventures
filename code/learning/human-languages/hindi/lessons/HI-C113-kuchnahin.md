## You'll want to know: कुछ नहीं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C113-NOTHING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कुछ नहीं** (*kuch nahīṁ*) — "nothing".

**क्या हुआ? — कुछ नहीं.** — "what happened? — nothing". It ends on the **नहीं** you know.
