## You'll want to know: கோழை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C466-TUNIVU466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**கோழை** — *kōḻai* — "a coward".

> பயந்து ஓடியதால் அவனைக் கோழை என்று சொன்னார்கள்.
>
> *payantu ōṭiyatāl avaṉaik kōḻai eṉṟu coṉṉārkaḷ.*
>
> Because he ran away in fear, they called him a coward.

X-ஐ Y என்று சொல், to call X a Y.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கோழை** (*kōḻai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2250), where it names both timidity and a timid person.
