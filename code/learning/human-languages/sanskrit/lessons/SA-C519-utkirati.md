## You'll want to know: उत्किरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C519-MURTI519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्किरति** — *utkirati* — "carves".

> शिल्पी शिलायां पुष्पम् उत्किरति।
>
> *śilpī śilāyāṃ puṣpam utkirati.*
>
> The sculptor carves a flower on the stone.

Past (he …): सः शिलायां नाम उत्कीर्णवान्, *saḥ śilāyāṃ nāma utkīrṇavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्किरति** (*utkirati*) is built on *ud*, "up, out", and the root √kṝ, "to scatter"; MW gives *utkṝ* as to dig out, and to engrave; its past participle is *utkīrṇa* (MW).
