## You'll want to know: বিদায়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C308-BISORJON308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদায়** — *bidāy* — "a farewell; a parting".

> বিদায়ের সময় দিদিমা কাঁদলেন।
>
> *bidāyer sômôy didimā kā̃dlen.*
>
> Grandmother cried when it was time to say goodbye.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদায়** (*bidāy*) is Sanskrit *vidāya*; Monier-Williams gives 'dismissal with good wishes' and suspects that this sense is not Sanskrit at all but Arabic *wadāʿ*, 'farewell'. বিদায় নেওয়া, to take your leave.
