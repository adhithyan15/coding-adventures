Bengali, chapter 389: Drivers and Conductors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
চালক (chālôk) — a driver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a van, then the Bengali for to race.

You'll want to know: চালক.
চালক — chālôk — "a driver".
বাসের চালক হঠাৎ বাস থামালেন। bāser chālôk hôṭhāt bās thāmālen. The bus driver suddenly stopped the bus.
Beside চালানো, to drive: the চালক (chālôk) is the one who চালায়.

The word, taken apart.
চালক (chālôk) is a Sanskrit-style formation built from cāl-, the causing stem of cal, "to move, to stir" (Monier-Williams), with the ending -ক for the one who does a thing: one who makes something go. Monier-Williams records cālaka only in an unrelated old sense, so the driver is a modern formation, the noun beside চালানো.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ইশারা করা (ishārā kôrā) — to signal; to make a sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bus conductor, then the Bengali for a helmet.

You'll want to know: ইশারা করা.
ইশারা করা — ishārā kôrā — "to signal; to make a sign".
কন্ডাক্টর হাত তুলে চালককে থামার ইশারা করলেন। kônḍākṭôr hāt tule chālôkke thāmār ishārā kôrlen. The conductor raised his hand and signalled the driver to stop.
Past (he/she …): তিনি চালককে ইশারা করলেন, tini chālôkke ishārā kôrlen.
The noun alone is ইশারা, a sign or a hint: চোখের ইশারা, a sign with the eyes.

The word, taken apart.
ইশারা করা (ishārā kôrā) joins ইশারা, "a sign, a gesture", usually traced to Arabic ishāra, "a pointing, a signal", through Persian, to করা, to do.

Lesson 5 of 5.
হাঁকা (hā̃kā) — to call out; to cry (stops or wares).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a helmet, then the Bengali for to signal.

You'll want to know: হাঁকা.
হাঁকা — hā̃kā — "to call out; to cry (stops or wares)".
কন্ডাক্টর পাড়ার নাম হাঁকলেন। kônḍākṭôr pāṛār nām hā̃klen. The conductor called out the name of the neighbourhood.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নাম হাঁকলেন, tini nām hā̃klen.
Conductors হাঁকে the stops from the door; hawkers হাঁকে their goods in the lane.

The word, taken apart.
হাঁকা (hā̃kā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali hā̃kā, "to call, to drive off", under Sanskrit hakkayati, "calls to" (CDIAL 13939).
