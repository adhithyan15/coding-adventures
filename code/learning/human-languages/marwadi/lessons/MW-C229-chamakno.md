## You'll want to know: चमकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C229-LIGHT229-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चमकणो** — *chamakṇo* — "to shine; to glitter".

> रात तारो चमकै है।
>
> *rāt tāro chamkai hai.*
>
> At night a star shines.

Past (it …): वो तारो चमक्यो, *vo tāro chamkyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चमकणो** (*chamakṇo*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*camakka*, "a sudden movement"; CDIAL lists Hindi *camaknā* and Gujarati *camakvũ*, "to glitter". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4676). **चमकीलो**, shiny, is built on it.
