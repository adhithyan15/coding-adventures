## You'll want to know: おさら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C174-KITCH174-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おさら** — *osara* — "plate; dish".

> **さかなを おさらに だします。** — *sakana o osara ni dashimasu* — "I serve the fish on a plate."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おさら** (*osara*) is built from polite *o-* and native *sara*, "dish", inherited rather than borrowed; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
