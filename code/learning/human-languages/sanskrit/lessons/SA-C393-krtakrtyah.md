## You'll want to know: कृतकृत्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C393-VIJAYA393-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कृतकृत्यः** — *kṛtakṛtyaḥ* — "fulfilled; content with what one has done".

> यदि सः लक्ष्यं प्राप्नोति, तर्हि कृतकृत्यः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi saḥ lakṣyaṃ prāpnoti, tarhi kṛtakṛtyaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If he reaches his goal, he will feel fulfilled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कृतकृत्यः** (*kṛtakṛtyaḥ*) is built from *kṛta*, "done", and *kṛtya*, "what is to be done", both from the root √kṛ; MW gives *kṛta-kṛtya* "one who has done his duty" and "contented, satisfied" (MW).
