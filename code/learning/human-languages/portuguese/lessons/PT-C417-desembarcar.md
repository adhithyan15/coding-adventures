## You'll want to know: desembarcar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C417-PORTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**desembarcar** — "to go ashore; to get off, to land".

> Desembarcámos numa ilha pequena.
>
> *We went ashore on a small island.*

Also for planes: *desembarcar no aeroporto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desembarcar* is built from *des-* and *embarcar* 'to board', on *barca* 'boat', inherited from Late Latin *barca* (Lewis & Short).
