Lesson 1 of 6.
वार्ताहर (vārtāhar) — a reporter; a correspondent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a headline, then the Marathi for to print.

You'll want to know: वार्ताहर.
वार्ताहर — vārtāhar — "a reporter; a correspondent".
वार्ताहराने अपघाताची माहिती फोनवर दिली. vārtāharāne apghātācī māhitī phonvar dilī. The reporter gave the details of the accident over the phone.
Masculine.
Built from वार्ता, news, and -हर, carrier.

The word, taken apart.
वार्ताहर (vārtāhar) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vārttāhara, 'a carrier of tidings, a messenger' (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: संपादक.
संपादक — sampādak — "an editor".
संपादकांनी लेखात काही बदल केले. sampādakānnī lekhāt kāhī badal kele. The editor made some changes to the article.
Masculine.
The respectful plural संपादकांनी, by the editor.

The word, taken apart.
संपादक (sampādak) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sampādaka, 'one who brings about, accomplishes' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'editor' is modern.

Lesson 4 of 6.
अग्रलेख (agralekh) — an editorial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an editor, then the Marathi for an article.

You'll want to know: अग्रलेख.
अग्रलेख — agralekh — "an editorial".
आजचा अग्रलेख पाण्याच्या प्रश्नावर आहे. ājcā agralekh pāṇyācyā praśnāvar āhe. Today's editorial is about the water problem.
Masculine.
अग्र-, front, + लेख (lekh): the leading article.

The word, taken apart.
अग्रलेख (agralekh) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit agra, 'front, foremost', and lekha, 'a writing' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
प्रकाशित करणे (prakāśit karṇe) — to publish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an article, then the Marathi for an editorial.

You'll want to know: प्रकाशित करणे.
प्रकाशित करणे — prakāśit karṇe — "to publish".
त्या लेखकाने नवं पुस्तक प्रकाशित केलं. tyā lekhkāne navaṁ pustak prakāśit kelaṁ. That writer published a new book.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने प्रकाशित केलं, tyāne prakāśit kelaṁ.
What is published agrees; प्रकाशित होणे, to be published.

The word, taken apart.
प्रकाशित करणे (prakāśit karṇe) joins prakāśit, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit prakāśita, 'brought to light, made public' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.
