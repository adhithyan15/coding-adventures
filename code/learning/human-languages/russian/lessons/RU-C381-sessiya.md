## You'll want to know: сессия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C381-SESSIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сессия** (*séssiya*) — "an exam period (at university); a session".

> В январе у студентов сессия.
>
> *V yanvaré u studéntov séssiya.*
>
> In January the students have their exams.

Feminine. At a Russian university, the weeks of exams at the end of each term. *Сдавать сессию* — to sit one’s exams.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**сессия** is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin *sessiō* "a sitting" (Lewis & Short), built on *sedēre* "to sit": a time when a body sits to do its business.
