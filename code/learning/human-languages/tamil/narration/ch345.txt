Tamil, chapter 345: Telling a Tale.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கதைசொல்லி (kataicolli) — a storyteller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to curse, then the Tamil for to change shape.

You'll want to know: கதைசொல்லி.
கதைசொல்லி — kataicolli — "a storyteller".
அந்தக் கதைசொல்லி குழந்தைகளைச் சிரிக்க வைத்தார். antak kataicolli kuḻantaikaḷaic cirikka vaittār. That storyteller made the children laugh.
கதை + சொல்லி, one who tells.

The word, taken apart.
கதைசொல்லி (kataicolli) joins கதை, "story", from Sanskrit kathā (CDIAL 2704), with சொல்லி, "one who tells", from native சொல் (DEDR 2855).

Lesson 2 of 5.
நீதிக்கதை (nītikkatai) — a moral tale; a fable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to change shape, then the Tamil for a storyteller.

You'll want to know: நீதிக்கதை.
நீதிக்கதை — nītikkatai — "a moral tale; a fable".
ஒவ்வொரு நீதிக்கதையும் ஒரு பாடம் சொல்கிறது. ovvoru nītikkataiyum oru pāṭam colkiṟatu. Every moral tale teaches a lesson.
A story for children that ends with a lesson.

The word, taken apart.
நீதிக்கதை (nītikkatai) is built from நீதி, "right conduct", borrowed from Sanskrit nīti (CDIAL 7546), with கதை, "story", from Sanskrit kathā (CDIAL 2704).

Lesson 3 of 5.
வில்லுப்பாட்டு (villuppāṭṭu) — a bow-song.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a storyteller, then the Tamil for a moral tale.

You'll want to know: வில்லுப்பாட்டு.
வில்லுப்பாட்டு — villuppāṭṭu — "a bow-song".
திருவிழாவில் வில்லுப்பாட்டு நடந்தது. tiruviḻāvil villuppāṭṭu naṭantatu. There was a bow-song performance at the festival.
A sung story, told with a large bow as the main instrument.

The word, taken apart.
வில்லுப்பாட்டு (villuppāṭṭu) is a Tamil compound built from வில், "bow" (DEDR 5422), and பாட்டு, "song" (DEDR 4065).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a moral tale, then the Tamil for a bow-song.

You'll want to know: கதைக்களம்.
கதைக்களம் — kataikkaḷam — "a setting; a plot".
இந்தப் படத்தின் கதைக்களம் ஒரு சிறிய கிராமம். intap paṭattiṉ kataikkaḷam oru ciṟiya kirāmam. The setting of this film is a small village.
களம், field: the ground the story stands on.

The word, taken apart.
கதைக்களம் (kataikkaḷam) joins கதை, "story", from Sanskrit kathā (CDIAL 2704), with native களம், "place, open space" (DEDR 1376).

Lesson 5 of 5.
அரங்கேறு (araṅkēṟu) — to be staged.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a bow-song, then the Tamil for a setting.

You'll want to know: அரங்கேறு.
அரங்கேறு — araṅkēṟu — "to be staged".
அவளுடைய நடனம் நேற்று அரங்கேறியது. avaḷuṭaiya naṭaṉam nēṟṟu araṅkēṟiyatu. Her dance was staged for the first time yesterday.
Past (it …): அரங்கேறியது, araṅkēṟiyatu.
Also of a dancer's first public show.

The word, taken apart.
அரங்கேறு (araṅkēṟu) joins அரங்கு, "a stage, a hall", which DEDR lists among its loans (DEDR App. 8), with native ஏறு, "to climb, go up" (DEDR 916).
