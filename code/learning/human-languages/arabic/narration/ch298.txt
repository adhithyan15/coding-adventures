Arabic, chapter 298: A fire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حريق (ḥarīq) — a blaze, a fire that spreads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to put on air, then the Arabic for on air.

You'll want to know: حريق.
حريق — ḥarīq — "a blaze, a fire that spreads".
رأى الجيران حريقا في المصنع. raʾā al-jīrān ḥarīqan fī al-maṣnaʿ. The neighbours saw a fire at the factory.
نار is fire itself; حريق (ḥarīq) is a fire that destroys.

The word, taken apart.
حريق (ḥarīq) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-r-q (Lane), "to burn". Ḥarīq is the fire that burns a thing up.

Lesson 2 of 5.
دخان (dukhān) — smoke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for on air, then the Arabic for a blaze.

You'll want to know: دخان.
دخان — dukhān — "smoke".
خرج دخان أسود من النافذة. kharaja dukhān aswad min an-nāfidha. Black smoke came out of the window.
In many dialects دخان (dukhān) also means tobacco.

The word, taken apart.
دخان (dukhān) is built on the native Arabic root d-kh-n (Lane), where dakhana is said of a fire that sends up smoke.

اشتعل (ishtaʿala) — to catch fire, to blaze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a blaze, then the Arabic for smoke.

You'll want to know: اشتعل.
اشتعل — ishtaʿala — "to catch fire, to blaze".
اشتعلت النار في الخشب بسرعة. ishtaʿalat an-nār fī al-khashab bi-surʿa. The fire caught in the wood quickly.
Present: يشتعل, yashtaʿil.

The word, taken apart.
اشتعل (ishtaʿala) is built on the native Arabic root sh-ʿ-l (Lane), "to kindle a fire". It is Form VIII, "to take fire"; shuʿla is a flame.

Lesson 5 of 5.
رجل إطفاء (rajul iṭfāʾ) — a firefighter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to catch fire, then the Arabic for to burn down.

You'll want to know: رجل إطفاء.
رجل إطفاء — rajul iṭfāʾ — "a firefighter".
صعد رجل الإطفاء إلى السطح. ṣaʿida rajul al-iṭfāʾ ilā as-saṭḥ. The firefighter climbed up to the roof.
Plural رجال الإطفاء. The fire brigade is الإطفاء.

The word, taken apart.
رجل إطفاء (rajul iṭfāʾ) is "a man of extinguishing": iṭfāʾ is the verbal noun of the taught aṭfaʾa, built on the native Arabic root ṭ-f-ʾ, "(of fire) to go out" (Lane).
