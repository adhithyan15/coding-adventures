## You'll want to know: la marea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C463-SCOGLI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la marea** — "the tide".

> Con l'alta marea la spiaggia sparisce.
>
> *At high tide the beach disappears.*

*L'alta marea*, *la bassa marea*; also a flood of people: *una marea di gente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marea* is built on *mare* 'sea', which is inherited from Latin *mare* (EtymDB).
