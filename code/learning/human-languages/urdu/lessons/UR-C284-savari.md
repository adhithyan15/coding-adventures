## You'll want to know: سواری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C284-TAKKAR284-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سواری** — *savārī* — "a ride; a passenger".

> بس میں بیس سواریاں تھیں۔
>
> *bas meṅ bīs savāriyāṅ thīṅ.*
>
> There were twenty passengers on the bus.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سواری** (*savārī*) is usually traced to Persian *savārī*, "riding", from *savār*, a rider. It is feminine whoever the passenger is: **بیس سواریاں**.
