## You'll want to know: दावा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C315-DAVA315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दावा** — *dāvā* — "a claim".

> मंत्री ने दावा किया कि सब ठीक है।
>
> *mantrī ne dāvā kiyā ki sab ṭhīk hai.*
>
> The minister claimed that everything was fine.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दावा** (*dāvā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *daʿwā*, "a claim, a lawsuit". **दावा करना** is to claim; the claim is masculine: **दावा किया**.
