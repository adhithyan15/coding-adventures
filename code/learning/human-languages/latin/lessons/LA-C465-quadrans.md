## You'll want to know: quadrāns, quadrantis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C465-MODIUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**quadrāns, quadrantis** — "a quarter-as (a small coin); a quarter".

> Prō balneō tantum quadrantem solvimus.
>
> *We pay only a quarter-as for the bath.*

Third declension, masculine: *quadrāns, quadrantis m.* A quarter of an *as*, the price of a public bath. English *quadrant* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quadrāns* is built on *quattuor* 'four' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): a fourth part. English *quadrant* comes from it.
