## You'll want to know: ਡਾਕੀਆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C256-DAK256-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕੀਆ** — *ḍākīā* — "a postman".

> ਡਾਕੀਆ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਾਡੀ ਗਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ḍākīā roz sāḍī galī vich āundā hai.*
>
> The postman comes to our lane every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡਾਕੀਆ** (*ḍākīā*) is built on *ḍāk*, "the post", with the ending *-īā* for the person who does a job; for *ḍāk*, CDIAL gives no Punjabi form (CDIAL 5519).
