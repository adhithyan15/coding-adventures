# (el curso de cocina) — follow it, then say your own

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-03, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-06] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **avanzado** and **perderse**. The first explains the second in
everything below.

## Reading: two friends, one question
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-03, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-04] -->

> — ¿Qué tal el curso de cocina? ¿Sigues yendo?
> — Sí, pero he cambiado de grupo. El de los lunes era demasiado **avanzado**
>   para mí; todos habían hecho cursos antes y yo me **perdía**.
> — ¿Y ahora?
> — Ahora voy los miércoles con los **principiantes** y disfruto mucho más.
>   Hacemos cosas **sencillas**, pero las hacemos bien.

**El de los lunes.** The noun *grupo* is dropped and *el* carries it — the
same move the printer notice made with *la de la tercera planta*.

## Grammar Lens: she never says the class was bad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-05, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-06] -->

Every word of the complaint is about **fit**, not quality.

| what she says | what she does not say |
|---|---|
| *demasiado avanzado para mí* | that the class was bad |
| *yo me perdía* | that the teacher was bad |

*Para mí* puts the mismatch between the class and her; *me perdía* reports a
state rather than blaming anyone; and *sencillas, pero las hacemos bien*
refuses the obvious conclusion.

Now read it the way somebody else would report it:

> El **nivel** era **demasiado alto** para ella.

Not a word of that is in what she said, and it is what she meant. She talks
about the **class**; a summary talks about its **nivel**. Move between the two,
because the person asking what happened will use the second.

Spanish gives you a way to leave something that is not working without
criticising it, and an exam will ask you to hear the difference between *it
did not suit me* and *it was no good*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-01, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-02, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-03, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-04, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-05, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-06] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say why she changed groups, using *para mí* the way she does.

[PAUSE 3s] Now say it about something of your own — a class, a book, a game.
Name what was too advanced, say you lost the thread, and say what you do now
instead. Keep the blame out of it.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-01, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-02, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-03, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-04, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-05, ES-LEX-C471-CURSO-06] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Why did she change? (**Era demasiado avanzado para ella**.) What
happened to her in class? (**Se perdía**.) Who is she with now? (**Los
principiantes**.) Are the new dishes worse? (**No — sencillas, pero bien
hechas**.)
