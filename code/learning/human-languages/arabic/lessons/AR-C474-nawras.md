## You'll want to know: نورس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C474-SAQR474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نورس** — *nawras* — "a seagull".

> وقف نورس على القارب.
>
> *waqafa nawras ʿalā al-qārib.*
>
> A seagull stood on the boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نورس** (*nawras*) names the gull; the origin is not settled.
