Italian, chapter 316: La torta di compleanno.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la candelina — the birthday candle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for best wishes, then the Italian for to wish.

You'll want to know: la candelina.
la candelina — "the birthday candle".
Sulla torta ci sono trenta candeline. There are thirty candles on the cake.
A little candela, ‘candle’.

The word, taken apart.
candelina is a little candela "candle", inherited from Latin candēla, from candēre "to shine white" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
il festeggiato — the person being celebrated, the birthday boy or girl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to wish, then the Italian for the birthday candle.

You'll want to know: il festeggiato.
il festeggiato — "the person being celebrated, the birthday boy or girl".
Il festeggiato ha aperto i regali. The birthday boy opened his presents.
Built on festeggiare; a woman is la festeggiata.

The word, taken apart.
festeggiato is the past participle of festeggiare "to celebrate", built on festa, from Latin fēsta "feast days" (Lewis & Short).
