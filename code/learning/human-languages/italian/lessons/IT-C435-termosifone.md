## You'll want to know: il termosifone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C435-RISCALDAMENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il termosifone** — "the radiator".

> Metti i calzini sul termosifone.
>
> *Put the socks on the radiator.*

*Accendere i termosifoni* — to switch the heating on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*termosifone* is a learned formation from Greek *thermós* 'hot' and *sifone* 'siphon', from Greek *síphōn* 'pipe, tube'.
