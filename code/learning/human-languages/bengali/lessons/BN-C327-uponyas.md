## You'll want to know: উপন্যাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C327-UPONNAS327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উপন্যাস** — *upônyās* — "a novel".

> আমি ছুটিতে একটা লম্বা উপন্যাস পড়লাম।
>
> *āmi chhuṭite ekṭā lômbā upônyās pôṛlām.*
>
> I read a long novel during the holidays.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপন্যাস** (*upônyās*) is Sanskrit *upanyāsa*, 'a putting down, a placing near' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a novel' is the modern Bengali one.
