## You'll want to know: ಘನತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C366-GHANATE366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘನತೆ** — *ghanate* — "dignity".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಕಷ್ಟದಲ್ಲೂ ಘನತೆಯಿಂದ ಬದುಕಿದರು.
>
> *ajja kaṣṭadallū ghanateyinda badukidaru.*
>
> Grandfather lived with dignity even in hardship.

ಘನ, weighty, with -ತೆ, -ness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಘನತೆ** (*ghanate*) is built on Sanskrit *ghana*, "compact, firm, dense" (CDIAL 4424), with the Kannada abstract ending -*te*: weight of character.
