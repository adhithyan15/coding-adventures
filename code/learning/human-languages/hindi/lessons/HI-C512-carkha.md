## You'll want to know: चरखा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C512-KARGHA512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चरखा** — *carkhā* — "a spinning wheel".

> मेरी नानी हर शाम चरखा चलाती थी।
>
> *Merī nānī har śām carkhā calātī thī.*
>
> My grandmother used to work the spinning wheel every evening.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरखा** (*carkhā*) is usually traced to Persian *charkha*, "a wheel".
