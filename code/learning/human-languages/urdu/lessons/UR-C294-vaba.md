## You'll want to know: وبا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C294-QAHAT294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**وبا** — *vabā* — "an epidemic".

> لوگوں کو وبا کا ڈر تھا۔
>
> *logoṅ ko vabā kā ḍar thā.*
>
> People were afraid of the epidemic.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وبا** (*vabā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wabā*, "a plague". It is feminine.
