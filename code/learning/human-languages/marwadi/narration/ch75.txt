Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 75: Low, Empty, Poor, Dirty, Fat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नीचो (nīcho) — low.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for easy, then the Marwadi for difficult.

You'll want to know: नीचो.
नीचो — nīcho — "low".

The word, taken apart.
नीचो (nīcho) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nīca, "low". CDIAL lists Old Marwari nīcaï under it (CDIAL 7540). The Old Marwari row means "below".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: खाली.
खाली — khālī — "empty".

The word, taken apart.
खाली (khālī) is a loan from Arabic ḫālī, "empty, vacant", which reached Marwadi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḫ-l-w (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
गरीब (garīb) — poor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for low, then the Marwadi for empty.

You'll want to know: गरीब.
गरीब — garīb — "poor".

The word, taken apart.
गरीब (garīb) is a loan from Arabic ġarīb, "a stranger", which reached Marwadi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ġ-r-b (Lane). The sense moved from "stranger, far from home" to "poor".

Lesson 4 of 5.
मैलो (mailo) — dirty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for empty, then the Marwadi for poor.

You'll want to know: मैलो.
मैलो — mailo — "dirty".

The word, taken apart.
मैलो (mailo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from malin, "dirty", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari mailā under it (CDIAL 9904). It is built on Sanskrit mala, "dirt".

Lesson 5 of 5.
मोटो (moṭo) — fat, thick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for poor, then the Marwadi for dirty.

You'll want to know: मोटो.
मोटो — moṭo — "fat, thick".

The word, taken apart.
मोटो (moṭo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from muṭṭa, "defective", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari moṭaü under it (CDIAL 10187). CDIAL lists it among a large group of reconstructed forms headed muṭṭa; how the sense "fat, big" fits that group is not settled.
