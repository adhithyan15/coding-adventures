# HL73 — Punjabi A1 noun-phrase agreement

## Status

Proposed for issue #17699 under the Punjabi bridge #13384 and the
Indian-language C2 programme #13295.

## Problem

Punjabi's partial project-defined A1 inventory still reports five tightly
related gaps. The learner already knows **ਮੁੰਡਾ**, **ਕੁੜੀ**, and **ਚੰਗਾ**, but
the course has never said that nouns have grammatical gender, placed a
declinable adjective before a noun, contrasted **ਚੰਗਾ / ਚੰਗੀ**, added a degree
word, or retrieved the resulting multiword noun phrase before the verb.

## Source boundary

The sequence is bounded by two Punjabi teaching references rather than by
transfer from Hindi:

- the University of Waterloo PUN 1100 course package identifies **ਮੁੰਡਾ** as
  masculine and **ਕੁੜੀ** as feminine (pp. 54–55), states that variable
  adjectives agree with a noun's gender and number (p. 71), lists **ਚੰਗਾ** as
  a variable adjective (p. 73), and demonstrates adjective-before-noun phrases
  plus **ਬਹੁਤ** before an adjective (pp. 72–74); and
- Columbia University's Punjabi Language Resource Center gives **ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ**
  as its singular masculine agreement example and states the beginner rule
  that a modifying adjective sits immediately before its noun.

These sources support only this singular direct contrast. The chapter must not
infer plural, oblique, genitive, article, predicate-adjective, or universal
adjective behaviour.

## Required learner experience

Add one chapter-addressable five-lesson sequence after chapter 176. It must add
one joint at a time:

1. retrieve familiar **ਮੁੰਡਾ / ਕੁੜੀ** and name their masculine/feminine noun
   genders;
2. place already-known **ਚੰਗਾ** immediately before **ਮੁੰਡਾ** in one attributive
   noun phrase;
3. contrast **ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ / ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ** and change only the adjective ending;
4. put **ਬਹੁਤ** before the adjective to form **ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗਾ ਮੁੰਡਾ / ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ
   ਕੁੜੀ**; and
5. retrieve **ਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਚੰਗੀ ਕੁੜੀ ਹੈ** through listening, speaking, reading, and
   one bounded Gurmukhi copy after every form has been taught.

Every lesson stays at or below five minutes. No lesson introduces an idiom,
new sense, or culture claim.

## Inventory evidence

Wire only the five named points to five exact new atoms:

- `PA-A1-N-04` → `PA-GRAMMAR-NOUN-GENDER-01`;
- `PA-A1-ADJ-03` → `PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-PRENOMINAL-01`;
- `PA-A1-ADJ-02` → `PA-GRAMMAR-ADJ-GENDER-AGREEMENT-01`;
- `PA-A1-ADJ-05` → `PA-ADV-BAHUT-DEGREE-01`; and
- `PA-A1-S-08` → `PA-GRAMMAR-MULTIWORD-NP-01`.

Coverage must move from 142/227 to 147/227, with unmapped points moving from 85
to 80. No other inventory point changes state.
