## You'll want to know: घुसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C297-CHOR297-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसणे** — *ghusṇe* — "to barge in; to force one's way in".

> रात्री दोन चोर खिडकीतून घरात घुसले.
>
> *rātrī don cor khiḍkītūn gharāt ghusle.*
>
> At night two thieves forced their way into the house through the window.

Past (he …): तो घुसला, *to ghuslā*.

The place entered takes -त: घरात घुसणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसणे** (*ghusṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ghusṇe*, 'to enter forcibly', under *\*ghuss*, 'thrust in, pierce' (CDIAL 4492).
