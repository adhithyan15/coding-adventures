## You'll want to know: चौपाटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C410-KHADI410-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चौपाटी** — *caupāṭī* — "a beachfront; a seafront".

> रविवारी चौपाटीवर खूप गर्दी असते.
>
> *ravivārī caupāṭīvar khūp gardī aste.*
>
> On Sunday the beachfront is very crowded.

Feminine.

A town beach with food stalls and evening walkers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चौपाटी** (*caupāṭī*) names an open town beach where people walk and eat in the evening; its origin is not settled.
