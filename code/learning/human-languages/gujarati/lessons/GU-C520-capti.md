## You'll want to know: ચપટી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C520-VAGHAR520-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચપટી** — *capṭī* — "a pinch (of something)".

> શાકમાં એક ચપટી હળદર નાખો.
>
> *śākmā̃ ek capṭī haḷdar nākho.*
>
> Put a pinch of turmeric in the vegetables.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચપટી** (*capṭī*) is as much as fits between finger and thumb. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
