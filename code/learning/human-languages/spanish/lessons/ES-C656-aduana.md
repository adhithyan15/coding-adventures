## You'll want to know: la aduana
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C656-FRONTERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la aduana** — "customs".

> En la aduana revisan las maletas.
>
> *At customs they check the suitcases.*

*Pasar la aduana* — to go through customs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aduana* comes from Andalusian Arabic *ad-dīwān* "the office", from Arabic *dīwān* "administrative office, register" (EtymDB).
