## Chapter 156 builds an untimed message for one named reader

Nineteen sessions of at most five minutes move from a chosen greeting and
closing through two-sentence order, separate agreement, spacing, spelling, and
punctuation repairs, then supported and delayed assembly. The final 30–40-word
Gurmukhi message addresses one named reader without a visible model,
romanization, exact-answer key, or clock. It reuses taught language and follows
the six-field form; timed A1 message production remains for a later paper.
