## You'll want to know: நெகிழ்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C465-ANPU465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**நெகிழ்** — *nekiḻ* — "to be moved; to be touched".

> அவர்களுடைய அன்பைப் பார்த்து நான் நெகிழ்ந்தேன்.
>
> *avarkaḷuṭaiya aṉpaip pārttu nāṉ nekiḻntēṉ.*
>
> I was moved by their affection.

Past (I …): நெகிழ்ந்தேன், *nekiḻntēṉ*.

மனம் நெகிழ்ந்தது, my heart was touched.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நெகிழ்** (*nekiḻ*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2923); the entry runs from loosening, as bangles slip, to a softened heart.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
