## You'll want to know: विश्वासघात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C503-MAPH503-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वासघात** — *viśvāsghāt* — "a betrayal".

> जवळच्या मित्रानेच विश्वासघात केला.
>
> *javaḷcyā mitrānec viśvāsghāt kelā.*
>
> It was a close friend who betrayed him.

Masculine.

विश्वास, trust, + घात, a blow: विश्वासघात करणे, to betray.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वासघात** (*viśvāsghāt*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *viśvāsaghāta*, "destruction of confidence, treachery" (Monier-Williams).
