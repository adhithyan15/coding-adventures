## You'll want to know: совпадать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C275-ZATYAGIVATSYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**совпадать** (*sovpadát*) — "to coincide, to match".

> Наш отпуск совпал с их отпуском.
>
> *Nash ótpusk sovpál s ikh ótpuskom.*
>
> Our holiday coincided with theirs.

Imperfective; perfective *совпасть* (*sovpást*), past *совпал*. It takes *с* + instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*совпадать* is built on *со-* "together" and *падать* "to fall", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *padati* (EtymDB): to fall together.
