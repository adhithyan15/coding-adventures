## You'll want to know: அறிக்கை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C393-ARIKKAI393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**அறிக்கை** — *aṟikkai* — "a report; a statement".

> அறிக்கை வந்தால் உண்மை தெரியும்.
>
> *aṟikkai vantāl uṇmai teriyum.*
>
> If the report comes out, the truth will be known.

அறிக்கை வெளியிடு, to issue a statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அறிக்கை** (*aṟikkai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 314 lists Tamil *aṟi*, "to know", and *aṟikkai*, "notice": what makes a thing known.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
