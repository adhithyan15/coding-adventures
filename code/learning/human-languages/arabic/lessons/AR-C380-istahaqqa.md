## You'll want to know: استحق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C380-ADALA380-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استحق** — *istaḥaqqa* — "to deserve".

> يستحق هذا العامل راتبا أعلى.
>
> *yastaḥiqqu hādhā al-ʿāmil rātiban aʿlā.*
>
> This worker deserves a higher salary.

Present: يستحق, *yastaḥiqq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استحق** (*istaḥaqqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-q-q*, "to be true, to be due" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek what is due: to deserve.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
