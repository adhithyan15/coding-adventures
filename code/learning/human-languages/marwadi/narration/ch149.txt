Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 149: Complete, Happy, Timid, Brave, Crazy.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.

[your turn — say: a mouse, a fly, a mosquito, an ant, a scorpion]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: good, bad, narrow, wide, deep]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: light, hard, soft, wet, dry]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hot, salty, sour, bitter, spicy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: beautiful, good-looking, young, thin, short]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Wide? (चौड़ो, chauṛo.) Deep? (गैरो, gairo.) Light? (हळको, haḷko.) Hard? (कड़क, kaṛak.) Soft? (नरम, naram.) Wet? (गीलो, gīlo.) Dry? (सूको, sūko.) Hot? (तातो, tāto.) Salty? (खारो, khāro.) Sour? (खाटो, khāṭo.) Bitter? (कड़वो, kaṛvo.) Spicy? (तीखो, tīkho.) Beautiful? (सुंदर, sundar.) Good-looking? (रूपाळो, rūpāḷo.) Young? (जवान, javān.) Thin? (दुबळो, dubaḷo.) Short? (ठिगणो, ṭhigṇo.)
