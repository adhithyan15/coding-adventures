## You'll want to know: 路过
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C283-LUGUO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**路过** — *lùguò* — "to pass by", on the way somewhere else.

> **我每天路过学校** *wǒ měitiān lùguò xuéxiào* — I go past the school every day.

> **我路过你家** *wǒ lùguò nǐ jiā* — I'm going past your house.

Both characters are ones you write.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**路过** (*lùguò*) is built from 路 'road' and 过 'to pass', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luH* and *kwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a road' and the second, among other senses, 'to go beyond'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-05] -->

路过 is passing a place on your way somewhere else: you do not stop there. 去 is going there.

It is a verb, and the place follows it: 路过学校.
