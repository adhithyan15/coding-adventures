## You'll want to know: أقام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C328-HAFL328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أقام** — *aqāma* — "to hold an event; to reside".

> أقامت المدرسة حفلا في نهاية السنة.
>
> *aqāmat al-madrasa ḥaflan fī nihāyat as-sana.*
>
> The school held a party at the end of the year.

Present: يقيم, *yuqīm*.

أقام حفلا: to throw a party. أقام في: to live in a place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أقام** (*aqāma*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-w-m* (Lane), "to stand up", the root of *qāma*. It is Form IV, "to set a thing up", and "to stay".
