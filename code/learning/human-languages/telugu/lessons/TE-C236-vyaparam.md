## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యాపారం** (*vyāpāraṁ*) is Sanskrit *vyāpāra*, "occupation, business", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending *-ṁ* (CDIAL 12205).
