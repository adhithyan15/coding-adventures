## You'll want to know: o impacto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C351-IMPACTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o impacto** — "the impact".

> A nova fábrica teve um grande impacto na aldeia.
>
> *The new factory had a big impact on the village.*

*Ter impacto em* — to have an impact on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impacto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *impāctus* (EtymDB), the participle of *impingere* "to push, to strike or drive into" (Lewis & Short), built from *in* and *pangere* "to fix".
