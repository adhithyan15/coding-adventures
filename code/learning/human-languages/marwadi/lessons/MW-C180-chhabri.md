## You'll want to know: छाबड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C180-DHARO180-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छाबड़ी** — *chhābṛī* — "a flat basket".

> छाबड़ी मांय रोटी है।
>
> *chhābṛī mā̃y roṭī hai.*
>
> There are rotis in the flat basket.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छाबड़ी** (*chhābṛī*) is inherited, by CDIAL's account, from Sanskrit *chadman*, "a roof"; CDIAL lists Hindi *chabṛī*, "a strainer, a bamboo basket" (CDIAL 4981). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
