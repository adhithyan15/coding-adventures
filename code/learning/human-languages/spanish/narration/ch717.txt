Spanish, chapter 717: Días difíciles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
deprimido — depressed, low.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for disappointed, then the Spanish for disappointment.

You'll want to know: deprimido.
deprimido — "depressed, low".
Desde que perdió el trabajo está deprimido. Since he lost his job he has been depressed.
Used with estar. For the illness, say tener depresión.

The word, taken apart.
deprimido is the participle of deprimir, which comes from Latin dēprimere "to press down" (EtymDB), a learned word built on premere "to press".

Lesson 2 of 5.
desahogarse — to let off steam, to pour one's heart out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for disappointment, then the Spanish for depressed.

You'll want to know: desahogarse.
desahogarse — "to let off steam, to pour one's heart out".
Necesito hablar contigo para desahogarme. I need to talk to you to get it off my chest.
Desahogarse con alguien — to open up to someone. El desahogo is the relief it brings.

The word, taken apart.
desahogarse is built on ahogar "to choke, to drown" with the prefix des-: to stop choking. Ahogar comes from spoken Latin affōcāre, Latin offōcāre "to choke" (EtymDB), from faucēs "throat".

desesperado is built on desesperar "to despair", which EtymDB traces to Latin dēspērāre "to lose hope", from spērāre "to hope".
