7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
cuit / cuite — cooked.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for useful, then the French for useless.

You'll want to know: cuit / cuite.
cuit / cuite — "cooked".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cuit / cuite]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does cuit / cuite mean? ("Cooked".) Say it once more.
