Bengali, chapter 56: Lentils, Fruit, Apple, Banana, Potato.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ডাল (ḍāl) — lentils.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for meat, then the Bengali for a fish.

You'll want to know: ডাল.
ডাল — ḍāl — "lentils".

The word, taken apart.
ডাল (ḍāl) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dāla, "split peas" (CDIAL 6309). CDIAL asks whether it is one word with another dāla, "piece split off".
