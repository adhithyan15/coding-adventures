## You'll want to know: જડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C202-THEFT202-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જડવું** — *jaḍvũ* — "to be found, to turn up".

> **મને પાકીટ જડ્યું.** — *mane pākīṭ jaḍyũ* — "I found my wallet."

The finder takes **-ને**: **મને જડ્યું**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જડવું** (*jaḍvũ*) is listed by CDIAL as *jaṛvũ*, "to join, meet with, set jewels", under *\*jaḍati*, "joins, sets", a word that CDIAL takes from Dravidian, citing Bloch (CDIAL 5091). Of its senses, "meet with" is the nearest to "to be found".
