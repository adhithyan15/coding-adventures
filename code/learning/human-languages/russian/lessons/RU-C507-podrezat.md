## You'll want to know: подрезать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C507-BLOSSOM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**подрезать** (*podrezát*) — "to prune; to trim".

> Осенью дедушка подрезает старые ветки.
>
> *Ósenyu dédushka podrezáyet stáryye vétki.*
>
> In the autumn Grandad prunes the old branches.

Imperfective; perfective подрезать (подрежу), with the stress on the stem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подрезать* is built on *под-* and *резать* 'to cut', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *rězati* (EtymDB).
