## You'll want to know: el vínculo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C750-CONVIVIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el vínculo** — "the bond, the link".

> Tengo un vínculo especial con mi abuela.
>
> *I have a special bond with my grandmother.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vínculo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vinculum* "bond, link" (EtymDB), built on *vincīre* "to bind".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
