## Added — B1 vocabulary: 1,120 words in chapters 326–549; Telugu attains B1

- **224 new chapters (326–549) teach 1,120 new headwords, one word per lesson.** 388 of them are verbs, each given in the dictionary form the A2 chapters use (-ు / -ో) with its past: the first person singular (దొంగిలించు, past దొంగిలించాను *doṅgiliñcānu*), or the third person neuter for an impersonal verb (-ింది). Each lesson gives:
  - the word in Telugu script with its romanization, computed from the script by the track's A2 scheme (ISO 15919: ṁ word-finally, the class nasal before a stop, ē / ō, ḷ ṇ ś ṣ);
  - a short example, its romanization word for word, and its translation;
  - a note on the past, the plural, the case a verb takes, or a set phrase;
  - a "The word, taken apart" origin.

  Each word is practised again in the next two lessons, and a review closes every eight chapters (32 reviews). Every chapter sits on one B1 spine node:
  - 326–381, SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS (280 words, 85 verbs): time words for telling, news and the press, incidents and the courts, life events and ceremonies, history and stories.
  - 382–437, SPINE-GIVE-REASONS and SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION (280 words, 93 verbs): opinion and argument, society and public life, the law, education, then conditions and likelihood.
  - 438–493, SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL (280 words, 102 verbs): transport, lodging, the town and its offices, nature, the weather, shopping and things going wrong.
  - 494–549, SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE (280 words, 108 verbs): feelings and character, health, work, family, free time, the arts, sport and the home.
- **Telugu now teaches 2,560 headwords at or below B1**, past the level gate's target of 2,500. Together with the B1 core (chapters 316–325), this means Telugu attains B1.
- **Sources:**
  - native Dravidian words cite DEDR;
  - Sanskrit words cite CDIAL or Monier-Williams;
  - English, Persian, Arabic and Hindustani loans name their donor;
  - anything the sources do not settle is hedged.
- **Independent review:** each tranche was reviewed on its own. The reviews fixed errors in 122 of the 1,120 words (10.9%: 15, 17, 58 and 32 per tranche) and replaced 20 words outright (8 in the travel tranche, 12 in the experience tranche).
- **The core's new letters are revisited.** The first word of chapters 340–344 practises one letter each, ఈ ఓ ఊ ఔ and the visarga ః, through a note that uses the core word anchoring it (ఈలోగా, ఓటు, ఊరు, ఔషధం, దుఃఖం).
- **"It depends on" is revisited.** Two notes in chapters 326 and 327 use … మీద ఆధారపడి ఉంటుంది, so the core's last grammar lesson (TE-C325-adharapadi-untundi) is practised twice more; with one revisit it held the gate's reinforcement criterion at A2.
