## You'll want to know: たいふう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C208-FORECAST208-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たいふう** — *taifū* — "a typhoon".

> **たいふうは らいしゅうでしょう。** — *taifū wa raishū deshō* — "The typhoon will probably come next week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいふう** (*taifū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango), read with on'yomi borrowed from Chinese. Its second part is *fū*, "wind" (Middle Chinese *pjuwng*); how the first part, *tai*, came to name a great storm is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Baxter's transcription).
