## You'll want to know: die Erleichterung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C469-ENTTAEUSCHUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Erleichterung** — "relief".

> Zu unserer Erleichterung war niemand verletzt.
>
> *To our relief, nobody was hurt.*

No plural in this sense. *Erleichtert* — relieved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Erleichterung* is built on *erleichtern* "to make lighter", from *leicht* "light", which is inherited from Old High German *līht*, from Proto-Germanic *linhtaz* (EtymDB).
