## You'll want to know: مسبح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C454-IMARA454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مسبح** — *masbaḥ* — "a swimming pool".

> سبح الأطفال في مسبح الفندق.
>
> *sabaḥa al-aṭfāl fī masbaḥ al-funduq.*
>
> The children swam in the hotel pool.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسبح** (*masbaḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-b-ḥ*, "to swim" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿal* makes it the place of swimming.
