## You'll want to know: gubernāculum, gubernāculī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C437-RUDENS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gubernāculum, gubernāculī** — "a rudder, a steering oar".

> Gubernātor gubernāculum tenet et nāvem ad portum dūcit.
>
> *The helmsman holds the rudder and guides the ship to harbour.*

Second declension, neuter: *gubernāculum, gubernāculī n.* Also the helm of the state. English *govern* comes from the verb *gubernāre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gubernāculum* is built on *gubernō* 'to steer' (Lewis & Short), which was borrowed from Greek *kybernaō*. English *govern* comes from *gubernō* through French.
