## Writing

1. **നന്ദി**
2. **മലയാളം**
3. **പേര്**
4. **വെള്ളം**
5. **നമസ്കാരം**
6. **വീട്**
7. Accept an independently written recognizable Malayalam place name, for
   example **കൊച്ചി**, **കേരളം**, or **തിരുവനന്തപുരം**.
8. Accept an independently written appropriate thanks expression such as
   **നന്ദി**. Romanization or copying earns no credit.

## Speaking

1. Accept **നമസ്കാരം** or an equally appropriate taught Malayalam greeting.
2. Accept a comprehensible identity frame such as **എന്റെ പേര് … ആണ്.**
3. Accept a relevant state such as **എനിക്ക് സുഖമാണ്.**
4. Accept a comprehensible place response such as **ഞാൻ കേരളത്തിൽ താമസിക്കുന്നു.**
5. Accept a relevant yes/no response or short Malayalam-speaking frame.
6. Accept a comprehensible polite tea request containing **ചായ** and an
   appropriate request signal.

Score communicative success and intelligibility with the rubric; do not require
word-for-word reproduction of the examples.
