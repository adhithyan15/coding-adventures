## You'll want to know: zhù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C35-WORK35-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhù* — "to live somewhere; to stay".

> *tā zhù wǒ jiā* — He's staying at our place.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhù* is inherited from Middle Chinese *drjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to stop, to stay'. Staying somewhere becoming living there is a likely step, but it is not settled.
