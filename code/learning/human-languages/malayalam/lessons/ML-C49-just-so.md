## You'll want to know: അങ്ങനെ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C49-REPLY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**അങ്ങനെ** (*aṅṅane*) — "that way, just so".

This one the book has half-taught already. The chapter on pointing laid out a pattern: *i-* for what is near, *a-* for what is far, *e-* for what is being asked about. എങ്ങനെ (*eṅṅane*), 'how?', was the question member of exactly this set.

Swap its opening for the far one and the question becomes an answer. അങ്ങനെ is 'that way' — the manner just spoken of, agreed to.

Kannada does the same trick with ಹಾಗೆ (*hāge*), built on its own far-pointer. The machinery is inherited; each sister runs it with her own parts.
