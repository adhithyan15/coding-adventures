## You'll want to know: くさる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C177-TASTE177-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くさる** — *kusaru* — "to go bad; to rot, to spoil".

> **なつは たべものが よく くさります。** — *natsu wa tabemono ga yoku kusarimasu* — "In summer food often goes bad."

Polite **くさります**, *kusarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くさる** (*kusaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
