## You'll want to know: શરદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C223-FEEL223-04]; assesses=[] -->

**શરદી** — *śardī* — "common cold, a chill".

> **મને શરદી નથી.** — *mane śardī nathī* — "I don't have a cold."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શરદી** (*śardī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *sardī*, "coldness", from *sard*, "cold"; EtymDB lists the Persian word. CDIAL has no entry for it.
