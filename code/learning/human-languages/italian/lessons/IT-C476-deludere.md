## You'll want to know: deludere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C476-DELUSIONE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**deludere** — "to disappoint, to let down".

> Il film mi ha deluso: mi aspettavo di più.
>
> *The film disappointed me: I expected more.*

A false friend: it means to disappoint, not to delude. The past participle is *deluso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*deludere* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēlūdere* "to make fun of, to deceive" (Lewis & Short), built on *lūdere* "to play".
