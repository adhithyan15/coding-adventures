## You'll want to know: ઝાકળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C437-COLD437-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાકળ** — *jhākaḷ* — "heavy dew; morning mist".

> સવારે ઘાસ પર ઝાકળ હતું.
>
> *savāre ghās par jhākaḷ hatũ.*
>
> In the morning there was heavy dew on the grass.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાકળ** (*jhākaḷ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
