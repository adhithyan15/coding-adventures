## You'll want to know: ఖచ్చితమైన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C394-VIVARANA394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖచ్చితమైన** — *khaccitamaina* — "exact; precise".

> అతను ఖచ్చితమైన లెక్క చెప్పాడు.
>
> *atanu khaccitamaina lekka ceppāḍu.*
>
> He gave the exact figure.

ఖచ్చితంగా, exactly, for certain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖచ్చితమైన** (*khaccitamaina*) has an origin that is not settled: it may go back to Sanskrit *khacita*, "set, inlaid" (Monier-Williams), but the step to "exact" is unclear. The ending -మైన makes an adjective.
