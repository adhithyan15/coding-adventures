## You'll want to know: स्नायुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C474-SIRA474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्नायुः** — *snāyuḥ* — "a sinew".

> स्नायुः अस्थि बध्नाति।
>
> *snāyuḥ asthi badhnāti.*
>
> Sinew binds the bone.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्नायुः** (*snāyuḥ*) MW compares, with a question mark, German *Sehne* and English *sinew*, and reports that some trace it to a root meaning "to bind"; its origin is not settled (MW).
