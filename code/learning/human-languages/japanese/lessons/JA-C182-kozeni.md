## You'll want to know: こぜに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C182-PAY182-05]; assesses=[] -->

**こぜに** — *kozeni* — "small coins; small change".

> **こぜにで はらいます。** — *kozeni de haraimasu* — "I pay with small coins."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こぜに** (*kozeni*) is built from native *ko-*, "small", and *zeni*, "coin"; KANJIDIC2 lists *zeni* as a kun reading, but it looks like an early loan from Chinese (Middle Chinese *dzjen*), so its history is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
