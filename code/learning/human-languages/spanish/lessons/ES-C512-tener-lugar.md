## You'll want to know: tener lugar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C512-PASO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tener lugar** — "to take place".

> La reunión tuvo lugar en el ayuntamiento.
>
> *The meeting took place at the town hall.*

Used for planned events: *La boda tendrá lugar en mayo.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tener lugar* is built on *tener*, inherited from Latin *tenēre* 'to hold' (EtymDB), and *lugar*, which is usually traced to Latin *locālis* 'of a place', from *locus* 'a place' (Lewis & Short). To take place is, word for word, to hold a place.
