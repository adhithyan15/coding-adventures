# ఎండిపోవు (eṇḍipōvu) — to dry up; to wither

## You'll want to know: ఎండిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C481-VADAGALI481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎండిపోవు** — *eṇḍipōvu* — "to dry up; to wither".

> కరువులో చెరువులన్నీ ఎండిపోయాయి.
>
> *karuvulō ceruvulannī eṇḍipōyāyi.*
>
> In the drought all the ponds dried up.

Past (it …): ఎండిపోయింది, *eṇḍipōyindi*.

ఎండు, to dry in the sun, + పోవు. Said of ponds, plants and throats. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: ఎండిపోయింది, it dried up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎండిపోవు** (*eṇḍipōvu*) is native Dravidian: ఎండు, "to be heated by the sun, to dry up" (DEDR 869), with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
