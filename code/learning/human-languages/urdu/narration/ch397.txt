Urdu, chapter 397: Distances and Passes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فاصلہ (fāslā) — a distance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a convoy, then the Urdu for to drive (animals or a cart).

You'll want to know: فاصلہ.
فاصلہ — fāslā — "a distance".
اسٹیشن سے ہوٹل کا فاصلہ کم ہے۔ isṭeshan se hoṭal kā fāslā kam hai. It isn't far from the station to the hotel.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
فاصلہ (fāslā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic fāṣila, "an interval, a space between".

Lesson 4 of 5.
موڑنا (moṛnā) — to turn (something); to bend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a kilometre, then the Urdu for a mountain pass.

You'll want to know: موڑنا.
موڑنا — moṛnā — "to turn (something); to bend".
ڈرائیور نے موڑ پر گاڑی بائیں موڑی۔ ḍrāivar ne moṛ par gāṛī bāeṅ moṛī. At the bend the driver turned the car to the left.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سائیکل موڑی, us ne sāikil moṛī.

The word, taken apart.
موڑنا (moṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin moṛnā, "twists, bends", under Sanskrit muṭati and its variant mōṭati (CDIAL 10186). مڑنا is its partner, to turn by yourself, and موڑ is the bend.

Lesson 5 of 5.
گھمانا (ghumānā) — to show (someone) around; to turn round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a mountain pass, then the Urdu for to turn (something).

You'll want to know: گھمانا.
گھمانا — ghumānā — "to show (someone) around; to turn round".
ایک دوست نے ہمیں شہر گھمایا۔ ek dost ne hameṅ shahr ghumāyā. A friend showed us round the city.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چابی گھمائی, us ne chābī ghumāī.

The word, taken apart.
گھمانا (ghumānā) is built on گھومنا, "to go round", which comes from ghummati, "revolves", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4485): it is the form for making something, or someone, go round.
