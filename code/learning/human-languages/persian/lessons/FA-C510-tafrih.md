## You'll want to know: تفریح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C510-LEISURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تفریح** (*tafrih*) — "recreation, fun; an outing".

> آخر هفته برای تفریح به کوه میرویم.
>
> *âkhar-e hafte barâ-ye tafrih be kuh miravim.*
>
> At the weekend we go to the mountains for fun.

*tafrih kardan* — to have fun, to go on an outing. Plural *tafrihât*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tafrih* comes from Arabic *tafrīḥ* (EtymDB), a word usually glossed 'cheering up, gladdening'.
