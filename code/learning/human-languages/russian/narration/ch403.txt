Russian, chapter 403: Behind the wheel.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
руль (rul) — a steering wheel; a handlebar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a task, then the Russian for experience.

You'll want to know: руль.
руль (rul) — "a steering wheel; a handlebar".
Папа держал руль обеими руками. Pápa derzhál rul obéimi rukámi. Dad held the steering wheel with both hands.
Masculine; the stress moves to the ending: руля (rulyá), за рулём (za rulyóm) — at the wheel, driving.

The word, taken apart.
руль (rul) was borrowed from Dutch roer 'a rudder' (EtymDB): the word for steering a ship came to mean steering a car or a bicycle.

Lesson 2 of 5.
тормоз (tórmoz) — a brake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for experience, then the Russian for a steering wheel.

You'll want to know: тормоз.
тормоз (tórmoz) — "a brake".
У этой старой машины плохие тормоза. U étoy stároy mashíny plokhíye tormozá. This old car has bad brakes.
Plural тормоза. Нажать на тормоз (tórmoz) — to put on the brake.

The word, taken apart.
тормоз (tórmoz) goes back to Greek tórmos 'a socket into which a peg fits' (EtymDB). How the word reached Russian is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
тормозить (tormozít) — to brake; to slow down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a steering wheel, then the Russian for a brake.

You'll want to know: тормозить.
тормозить (tormozít) — "to brake; to slow down".
Перед школой водитель всегда тормозит. Péred shkóloy vodítel vsegdá tormozít. In front of the school the driver always brakes.
Imperfective; the perfective is затормозить. Present торможу, тормозишь: з becomes ж in the first person.

The word, taken apart.
тормозить (tormozít) is built on тормоз (tórmoz) 'a brake' with the verb ending -ить. Тормоз goes back to Greek tórmos 'a socket for a peg' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
разгоняться (razgonyátsya) — to speed up, to gather speed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a brake, then the Russian for to brake.

You'll want to know: разгоняться.
разгоняться (razgonyátsya) — "to speed up, to gather speed".
На шоссе машина быстро разгоняется. Na shossé mashína býstro razgonyáyetsya. On the motorway the car quickly speeds up.
Imperfective; perfective разогнаться (разгонюсь, разгонишься).

The word, taken apart.
разгоняться (razgonyátsya) is built from раз-, гонять 'to drive, to chase' and -ся. Гонять is inherited from Proto-Slavic ganjati, which EtymDB traces to the PIE root gʷʰen- 'to strike, to drive'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
сигналить (signálit) — to sound the horn, to honk; to signal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to brake, then the Russian for to speed up.

You'll want to know: сигналить.
сигналить (signálit) — "to sound the horn, to honk; to signal".
Возле дома водитель такси долго сигналил. Vózle dóma vodítel taksí dólgo signálil. Outside the house the taxi driver kept sounding his horn.
Imperfective; perfective просигналить. Built on сигнал — a signal.

The word, taken apart.
сигналить (signálit) is built on сигнал 'a signal'. Сигнал is usually traced to French signal, which EtymDB derives from Latin signālis, from signum 'a sign'.
