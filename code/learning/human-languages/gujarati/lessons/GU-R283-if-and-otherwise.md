# Review — if, then, or else

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C282-KARTAN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C282-THI-SINCE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C281-WISH-VANI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the city is bigger than the village". (*śaher gām kartā̃ moṭũ che*.) Then "I have lived here for two years". (*hũ be varasthī ahī̃ rahũ chũ*.) And "my wish is to live in the village". (*mārī icchā gāmmā̃ rahevānī che*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C283-JO-TO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-JO-PRESENT-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-FUTURE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-PERFECTIVE-01, GU-LEX-C283-NAHINTAR-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C282-SAUTHI-MOST-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C282-THI-SUDHI-01, GU-LEX-C48-BODY3-04, GU-LEX-VARSAAD-01, GU-LEX-C87-CLOTHES-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: If you have a fever, then you should rest.]
- [YOU SAY: If you're hungry, go ahead and eat.]
- [YOU SAY: If it rains, we'll stay at home. If it doesn't rain, we'll go.]
- [YOU SAY: If I miss the train, I'll come by bus. The bus is the cheapest.]
- [YOU SAY: The shop is open from morning until evening. Go quickly, otherwise it will close.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C283-JO-TO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-FUTURE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C283-IF-PERFECTIVE-01, GU-LEX-C283-NAHINTAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "If it rains", about tomorrow: which tense? (The future: **પડશે**.) "If I miss the train"? (**જો ટ્રેન છૂટી ગઈ**.) "Or else"? (**નહીંતર**.)
