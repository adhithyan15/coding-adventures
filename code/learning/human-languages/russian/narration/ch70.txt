Russian, chapter 70: Monday to Saturday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
понедельник (ponedélnik) — Monday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a minute, then the Russian for time.

You'll want to know: понедельник.
понедельник (ponedélnik) — "Monday". "After the week-day": неделя once meant Sunday.

What you've built.
One more word for monday to saturday.
