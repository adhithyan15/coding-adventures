German, chapter 326: Ab in den Ofen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
vorheizen — to preheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to roll out, then the German for the rolling pin.

You'll want to know: vorheizen.
vorheizen — "to preheat".
Er heizte den Ofen auf hundertachtzig Grad vor. He preheated the oven to a hundred and eighty degrees.
Separable: er heizt vor, er hat vorgeheizt.

The word, taken apart.
vorheizen is built on heizen "to heat", from Old High German heizen, Proto-Germanic haitijaną "to make hot" (EtymDB), with vor "before".

Backblech joins backen "to bake", a cousin of English bake (EtymDB), and Blech "sheet metal", an inherited Germanic word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Auflaufform — the baking dish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to grease, then the German for the baking tray.

You'll want to know: die Auflaufform.
die Auflaufform — "the baking dish".
Er gab die Nudeln in eine Auflaufform. He put the pasta into a baking dish.
Plural: die Auflaufformen. Der Auflauf — a bake, a gratin.

The word, taken apart.
Auflaufform joins Auflauf "a dish that rises", from laufen "to run" (EtymDB), and Form "shape, tin", from Latin forma "shape" (Lewis & Short).

bräunen is built on braun "brown", Old High German brūn, Proto-Germanic brūnaz (EtymDB).
