## You'll want to know: شرمنده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C482-HURT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شرمنده** (*sharmande*) — "ashamed; very sorry".

> او از رفتار بدش شرمنده بود.
>
> *u az raftâr-e badash sharmande bud.*
>
> He was ashamed of his bad behaviour.

Also a polite formula: *sharmande-am* 'I'm so sorry (to trouble you)'. Built on *sharm* 'shame'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sharmande* is built on the native Persian noun *sharm*, 'shame', with the ending *-ande*. EtymDB lists Hindi *sharmindā* as a borrowing of it, but gives no source for the Persian word itself (EtymDB).
