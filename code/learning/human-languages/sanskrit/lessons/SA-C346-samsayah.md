## You'll want to know: संशयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C346-AKSHEPA346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संशयः** — *saṃśayaḥ* — "a doubt".

> अत्र संशयः न अस्ति।
>
> *atra saṃśayaḥ na asti.*
>
> There is no doubt about it.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संशयः** (*saṃśayaḥ*) is built on *sam* and the root √śī, "to lie, to rest"; MW gives *saṃ-śaya* "uncertainty, irresolution, hesitation, doubt" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
