Hindi, chapter 343: What Caused It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चूँकि (cū̃ki) — since, as (giving a cause).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for turning over (in bed), then the Hindi for a stretch (of the body).

You'll want to know: चूँकि.
चूँकि — cū̃ki — "since, as (giving a cause)".
चूँकि बारिश हो रही थी, इसलिए हम घर पर रहे। cū̃ki bāriś ho rahī thī, isliye ham ghar par rahe. Since it was raining, we stayed at home.

The word, taken apart.
चूँकि (cū̃ki) is usually traced to Persian chūnki, "since, because".

Lesson 2 of 5.
असर (asar) — an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a stretch (of the body), then the Hindi for since.

You'll want to know: असर.
असर — asar — "an effect".
दवा का असर दो घंटे बाद हुआ। davā kā asar do ghaṇṭe bād huā. The medicine took effect two hours later.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
असर (asar) is usually traced to Arabic athar, "a trace, a mark", which reached Hindi through Persian. Hindi uses it for the effect one thing has on another.

पेड़ की हर डाली उसकी जड़ से जुड़ती है। peṛ kī har ḍālī uskī jaṛ se juṛtī hai. Every branch of a tree is joined to its root.
Past (it …): वह पुल सड़क से जुड़ गया, vah pul saṛak se juṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
जुड़ना (juṛnā) is inherited from yuṭati, "joins", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10496).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an effect, then the Hindi for to be joined.

आधार — ādhār — "a basis, grounds".
तुम्हारी बात का आधार क्या है? tumhārī bāt kā ādhār kyā hai? What are your grounds for saying that?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आधार (ādhār) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit ādhāra, "a support, a prop" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ज़िम्मेदार (zimmedār) — responsible.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ज़िम्मेदार.
ज़िम्मेदार — zimmedār — "responsible".
दुर्घटना के लिए ड्राइवर ज़िम्मेदार था। durghaṭnā ke liye ḍrāivar zimmedār thā. The driver was responsible for the accident.

The word, taken apart.
ज़िम्मेदार (zimmedār) is usually traced to Persian zimmadār, "answerable, in charge". The ending -dār is the Persian "holding".
