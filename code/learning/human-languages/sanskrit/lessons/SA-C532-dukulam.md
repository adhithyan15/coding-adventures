## You'll want to know: दुकूलम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C532-UPAYANA532-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दुकूलम्** — *dukūlam* — "fine cloth".

> यदि मात्रे दुकूलं रोचते, तर्हि अहं तत् क्रीणामि।
>
> *yadi mātre dukūlaṃ rocate, tarhi ahaṃ tat krīṇāmi.*
>
> If my mother likes the fine cloth, I'll buy it.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुकूलम्** (*dukūlam*) is the word MW gives for a plant and for the very fine cloth made from its inner bark; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
