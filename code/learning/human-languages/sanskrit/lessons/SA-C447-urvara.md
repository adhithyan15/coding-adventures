## You'll want to know: उर्वरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C447-KEDARA447-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उर्वरा** — *urvarā* — "fertile land".

> कृषकः उर्वरायां धान्यं वपति।
>
> *kṛṣakaḥ urvarāyāṃ dhānyaṃ vapati.*
>
> The farmer sows grain in the fertile land.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उर्वरा** (*urvarā*) is probably connected, as MW suggests, with *uru*, "wide", so its origin is uncertain; MW gives *urvarā* as fertile soil, a field yielding crops (MW).
