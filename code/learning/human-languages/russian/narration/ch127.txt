Russian, chapter 127: Cheerful, Kind, Angry, Serious, Right.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
весёлый (vesyólyy) — cheerful, fun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for interesting, then the Russian for boring.

You'll want to know: весёлый.
весёлый (vesyólyy) — "cheerful, fun".

The word, taken apart.
весёлый (vesyólyy) from Proto-Slavic veselŭ — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Latvian vesels, "healthy, whole", and Sanskrit vásu-, "good".

Lesson 2 of 5.
добрый (dóbryy) — kind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for boring, then the Russian for cheerful.

You'll want to know: добрый.
добрый (dóbryy) — "kind".

The word, taken apart.
добрый (dóbryy) from Proto-Slavic dobrŭ — inherited, with доба, "a fitting time", and доблесть, "valour". Vasmer relates it to Latin faber, "a craftsman", and Armenian darbin, "a smith": good was first "fitting, well made".

Lesson 5 of 5.
правильный (právilnyy) — right, correct.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for angry; wicked, then the Russian for serious.

You'll want to know: правильный.
правильный (právilnyy) — "right, correct".

The word, taken apart.
правильный (právilnyy) is правило, "a rule", with -ный. правило is built on правый, "right, straight", which Vasmer derives, probably, from prō-vos, "forward".
