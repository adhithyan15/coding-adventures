## You'll want to know: ਫ਼ਰੇਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C356-PHOTO356-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਰੇਮ** — *farem* — "a picture frame".

> ਤਸਵੀਰ ਲਈ ਇੱਕ ਨਵਾਂ ਫ਼ਰੇਮ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੋ।
>
> *tasvīr laī ikk navāṁ farem kharīdo.*
>
> Buy a new frame for the picture.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਰੇਮ** (*farem*) is borrowed from English *frame*.
