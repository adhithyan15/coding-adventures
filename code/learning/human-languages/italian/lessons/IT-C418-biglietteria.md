## You'll want to know: la biglietteria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C418-BIGLIETTERIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la biglietteria** — "the ticket office".

> La biglietteria apre alle sei.
>
> *The ticket office opens at six.*

*La biglietteria automatica* is the ticket machine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biglietteria* is built on *biglietto* 'ticket', which was borrowed from French *billet* 'note' (EtymDB).
