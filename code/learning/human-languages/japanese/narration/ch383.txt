Lesson 1 of 6.
しまぐに (shimaguni) — an island country.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the horizon (over the sea), then the Japanese for to sparkle.

You'll want to know: しまぐに.
しまぐに — shimaguni — "an island country".
日本は しまぐにです。 — nihon wa shimaguni desu — "Japan is an island country."

The word, taken apart.
しまぐに (shimaguni) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from shima, "island" and kuni, "country", joined as shimaguni; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

The word, taken apart.
わん (wan) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese: wan, "bay" (Middle Chinese 'waen) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

かいすいよく — kaisuiyoku — "swimming in the sea".
なつやすみに かぞくで かいすいよくに いきました。 — natsuyasumi ni kazoku de kaisuiyoku ni ikimashita — "In the summer holidays my family went swimming in the sea."

The word, taken apart.
かいすいよく (kaisuiyoku) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kai, "sea" (Middle Chinese xojX), sui, "water" (sywijX) and yoku, "bathe" (yowk) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

You'll want to know: すきとおる.
すきとおる — sukitōru — "to be clear; to be see-through".
この うみは みずが すきとおっています。 — kono umi wa mizu ga sukitōtte imasu — "The water of this sea is crystal clear."
Polite すきとおります, sukitōrimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): いけの みずが すきとおりました。, ike no mizu ga sukitōrimashita.

The word, taken apart.
すきとおる (sukitōru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
