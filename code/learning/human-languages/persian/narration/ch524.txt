Persian, chapter 524: Making Films.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کارگردان (kârgardân) — a film director.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a spectator, then the Persian for a role.

You'll want to know: کارگردان.
کارگردان (kârgardân) — "a film director".
کارگردان این فیلم جایزه گرفت. kârgardân-e in film jâyeze gereft. The director of this film won a prize.
kâr 'work' + gardân 'turning' (stem of gardândan): the one who keeps the work turning.

The word, taken apart.
kârgardân is built on native Persian kâr, 'work', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and gardân, the stem of gardândan, 'to turn, to run'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
فیلمنامه (filmnâme) — a screenplay, a film script.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a role, then the Persian for a film director.

You'll want to know: فیلمنامه.
فیلمنامه (filmnâme) — "a screenplay, a film script".
او فیلمنامه را در یک ماه نوشت. u filmnâme râ dar yek mâh nevesht. He wrote the screenplay in a month.
film + nâme 'writing'. A screenwriter is filmnâme-nevis.

The word, taken apart.
filmnâme is built in Persian on film, a loan usually traced to English film, and native nâme, 'letter, writing'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
نقش بازی کردن (naqsh bâzi kardan) — to play a role, to act a part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a screenplay, then the Persian for a cameraman.

You'll want to know: نقش بازی کردن.
نقش بازی کردن (naqsh bâzi kardan) — "to play a role, to act a part".
او در این فیلم نقش یک پزشک را بازی میکند. u dar in film naqsh-e yek pezeshk râ bâzi mikonad. She plays a doctor in this film.
naqsh-e kasi râ bâzi kardan — to play someone's part.

The word, taken apart.
naqsh bâzi kardan is built in Persian on naqsh, 'role', borrowed from Arabic naqsh, and the native phrase bâzi kardan, 'to play'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
هنرپیشه (honarpishe) — an actor, an actress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cameraman, then the Persian for to play a role.

You'll want to know: هنرپیشه.
هنرپیشه (honarpishe) — "an actor, an actress".
این هنرپیشه در تئاتر هم بازی میکند. in honarpishe dar te'âtr ham bâzi mikonad. This actor also works in the theatre.
honar 'art' + pishe 'trade'. It means the same as bâzigar.

The word, taken apart.
honarpishe is built on native Persian honar, 'art', usually traced to Middle Persian hunar, and pishe, 'trade, craft': one whose trade is art.
