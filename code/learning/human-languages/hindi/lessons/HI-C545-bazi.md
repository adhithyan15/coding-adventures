## You'll want to know: बाज़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C545-JUA545-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बाज़ी** — *bāzī* — "a round (of a game); a bet".

> खेल की पहली बाज़ी मीरा ने जीती।
>
> *Khel kī pahlī bāzī Mīrā ne jītī.*
>
> Mira won the first round of the game.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाज़ी** (*bāzī*) is usually traced to Persian *bāzī*, "play, a game".
