German, chapter 321: Neues entsteht.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
erfinden — to invent; to make up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the ancestor, then the German for the downfall.

You'll want to know: erfinden.
erfinden — "to invent; to make up".
Sie erfand eine neue Maschine. She invented a new machine.
Strong: erfand, hat erfunden. Also to make up a story.

The word, taken apart.
erfinden is built on finden "to find", a cousin of English find (EtymDB), with er-: to find out something new.

Lesson 4 of 5.
entstehen — to come into being, to arise.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for the invention, then the German for to found.

You'll want to know: entstehen.
entstehen — "to come into being, to arise".
Die Stadt entstand im Mittelalter. The town came into being in the Middle Ages.
Strong, with sein: entstand, ist entstanden.

The word, taken apart.
entstehen is built on stehen "to stand", from Proto-Germanic stāną (EtymDB), with ent-: to come to stand.

Lesson 5 of 5.
zurückkehren — to return, to come back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to found, then the German for to come into being.

You'll want to know: zurückkehren.
zurückkehren — "to return, to come back".
Nach zwanzig Jahren kehrte er in sein Dorf zurück. After twenty years he returned to his village.
Separable, with sein: Er ist zurückgekehrt. More formal than zurückkommen.

The word, taken apart.
zurückkehren joins zurück "back" and kehren "to turn", from Old High German kēran, Proto-Germanic karzijaną (EtymDB): to turn back.
