## You'll want to know: હાડકું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C227-INJURY227-03]; assesses=[] -->

**હાડકું** — *hāḍkũ* — "bone".

> **આ હાડકું નબળું નથી.** — *ā hāḍkũ nabaḷũ nathī* — "This bone is not weak."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાડકું** (*hāḍkũ*) is built on *hāḍ*, "bone", with the suffix *-kũ*. CDIAL lists *hāḍ* under the lexical Sanskrit *haḍḍa*, "bone" (CDIAL 13952).
