## You'll want to know: तटस्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C542-UTKRSTA542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तटस्थः** — *taṭasthaḥ* — "neutral".

> यदि भवान् तटस्थः अस्ति, तर्हि भवान् निर्णयं करोतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān taṭasthaḥ asti, tarhi bhavān nirṇayaṃ karotu.*
>
> If you are neutral, you decide.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तटस्थः** (*taṭasthaḥ*) is built from *taṭa*, "a bank, a slope", and *stha*, "standing": standing on the bank, out of the stream; MW gives *taṭastha* also as an indifferent person, neither friend nor foe (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
