## You'll want to know: dǎzhēn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C228-ACHE228-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎzhēn* — "to have an injection; to give one".

> *wǒ pà dǎzhēn* — I'm scared of injections.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎzhēn* is built from 打 'to strike, to do' and *zhēn* 'needle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *tsyim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'needle and thread'.
