## You'll want to know: नर्तक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C538-NRUTYA538-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नर्तक** — *nartak* — "a dancer".

> रंगमंचावर दहा नर्तक नाचत होते.
>
> *raṅgmañcāvar dahā nartak nācat hote.*
>
> Ten dancers were dancing on the stage.

Masculine.

A woman dancer is नर्तकी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नर्तक** (*nartak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nartaka*, "a dancer" (Monier-Williams), from the root of नृत्य.
