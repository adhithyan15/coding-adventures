Lesson 1 of 5.
आवेदनम् (āvedanam) — an application.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an employer, then the Sanskrit for employs.

You'll want to know: आवेदनम्.
आवेदनम् — āvedanam — "an application".
अहं कार्यालयं प्रति आवेदनं प्रेषयामि। ahaṃ kāryālayaṃ prati āvedanaṃ preṣayāmi. I send an application to the office.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
आवेदनम् (āvedanam) comes from ā and the causative of the root √vid, "to know": a making known; MW gives "announcing" and "addressing respectfully"; "an application for a post" is the modern sense (MW).

नियुक्तिः — niyuktiḥ — "a post; an appointment".
मम भ्रातुः नूतना नियुक्तिः अस्ति। mama bhrātuḥ nūtanā niyuktiḥ asti. My brother has a new post.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
नियुक्तिः (niyuktiḥ) is built on ni and the root √yuj, "to yoke, to appoint", with the ending -ti; MW gives niyukti as an injunction, a charge, an office (MW).

प्रतिभा — pratibhā — "talent; a gift".
सर्वे तस्य प्रतिभां प्रशंसन्ति। sarve tasya pratibhāṃ praśaṃsanti. Everyone praises his talent.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिभा (pratibhā) is built on prati, "towards", and the root √bhā, "to shine, to appear": what lights up in the mind; MW gives pratibhā as light, then intelligence, presence of mind, genius (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a post, then the Sanskrit for talent.

You'll want to know: उपजीवति.
उपजीवति — upajīvati — "lives by; earns a living from".
सः वेतनेन उपजीवति। saḥ vetanena upajīvati. He lives on his wages.
Past (he …): सः वेतनेन उपजीवितवान्, saḥ vetanena upajīvitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपजीवति (upajīvati) is built on upa, "near, under", and the root √jīv, "to live", which also gives jīvati; MW gives upajīv as to live upon, to subsist by, to make use of (MW).
