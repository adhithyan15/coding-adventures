## You'll want to know: занавес
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C519-CURTAIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**занавес** (*zánaves*) — "a stage curtain".

> Занавес поднялся, и спектакль начался.
>
> *Zánaves podnyálsya, i spektákl nachalsyá.*
>
> The curtain went up and the play began.

Masculine. A window curtain is *штора* or *занавеска*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*занавес* is derived from *навес* 'an awning' (EtymDB), built on the root of *вешать* 'to hang'.
