## You'll want to know: পাঠানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C411-DAKGHOR411-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পাঠানো** — *pāṭhāno* — "to send".

> আমি ডাকঘর থেকে দিদিমাকে একটা পার্সেল পাঠালাম।
>
> *āmi ḍākghôr theke didimāke ekṭā pārsel pāṭhālām.*
>
> I sent my grandmother a parcel from the post office.

Past (he/she …): তিনি চিঠি পাঠালেন, *tini chiṭhi pāṭhālen*.

The one you send to takes -কে: বাবাকে টাকা পাঠানো, to send money to Father. Also for people: ছেলেকে স্কুলে পাঠানো, to send a son to school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাঠানো** (*pāṭhāno*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *paṭhāna*, "sends", under Sanskrit *prátiṣṭhati*, "sets out", and its causing form *prásthāpayati*, "sends" (CDIAL 8607): to send is to make someone set out.
