## You'll want to know: zhīhòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C185-WHEN185-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīhòu* — "after (a time); after (an event)".

> *xiàbān zhīhòu wǒ qù mǎi dōngxi* — After work I'm going shopping.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīhòu* is built from *zhī*, the old linking word, and 后 'after', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyi* and *huwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
