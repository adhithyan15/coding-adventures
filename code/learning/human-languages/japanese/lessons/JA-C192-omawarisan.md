## You'll want to know: おまわりさん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C192-POLICE192-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おまわりさん** — *omawarisan* — "police officer".

> **こうばんに おまわりさんが います。** — *kōban ni omawarisan ga imasu* — "There is a police officer at the police box."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おまわりさん** (*omawarisan*) is built from polite *o-*, native *mawari*, "going round, a patrol", from *mawaru*, "to go round", and the polite *-san* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
