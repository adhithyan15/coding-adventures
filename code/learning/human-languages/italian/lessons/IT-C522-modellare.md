## You'll want to know: modellare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C522-SCULTURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**modellare** — "to model, to shape".

> Modello l'argilla con le mani.
>
> *I shape the clay with my hands.*

Built on *modello* "model".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modellare* is built on *modello* "model", from a little form of Latin *modulus* "a small measure" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
