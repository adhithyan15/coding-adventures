## You'll want to know: माळियो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C187-KOTHO187-05]; assesses=[] -->

**माळियो** — *māḷiyo* — "a loft".

> माळियो खाली कोनी।
>
> *māḷiyo khālī konī.*
>
> The loft isn't empty.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माळियो** (*māḷiyo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*māḍa*, "the upper storey of a house"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *māḷiyũ*, "a loft" (CDIAL 9996). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
