## Added — every Marathi word lesson says where its word comes from

Every one of the track's 1,217 word lessons now has an `etymology_hook`, and every one says in its body where its word comes from. Before this change 1,163 had no hook and 1,116 had no visible origin (human-language-data `ETYMOLOGY_GAP_PINS.marathi`, now 0 / 0).

- **New "taken apart" blocks (1,116).** Each sits after the section that introduces the word, opens with `<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->`, and agrees with the lesson's new hook. Phrase lessons get "The phrase, taken apart".
- **Sources.** Inherited words and Sanskrit loans cite Turner's *Comparative Dictionary of the Indo-Aryan Languages* (CDIAL) beside the fact, read from the moli-mandala data files: about 1,660 citations of some 650 entries, each checked by script for the entry number, the Sanskrit or reconstructed form, and the Marathi form among the entry's Marathi rows. CDIAL's own hedges are carried: "probably", queried links, Dravidian, Munda and Austroasiatic sources, Bloch's differing views, and forms CDIAL marks as taken from Hindi or Gujarati. Reconstructed forms are starred. Nine lessons also cite DEDR where CDIAL or DEDR points to Dravidian: तूप, आई, डावा, -कडे, ताट, थंड, चप्पल, घुबड and चेंडू. Arabic loans name their root, checked against Lane's *Arabic-English Lexicon* (फक्त says no root, because Lane has none for *faqaṭ*). Persian loans are written "usually traced to Persian …", with Arabic, Turkic or Mongolic donors behind them named. English and Portuguese loans name the donor.
- **Rough mix.** About 550 inherited words, 270 Sanskrit tatsamas and modern Sanskrit compounds, 70 Persian, 65 Arabic, 40 English and 5 Portuguese loans, and about 140 whose origin is not settled after a search of CDIAL and DEDR by form and by meaning. Those say so; none is guessed.
- **Lessons that already had a block or a hook (58).** 41 blocks that already named an origin gained a matching hook; 17 lessons gained a corrected or added sentence instead of a new block (येतो, आहे, मी, घर, दुसरा and others). Hooks that were not etymology (the Molesworth quotations on the ordinals) were rewritten.
- **Corrections to older text that contradicted CDIAL (about 15).** मी is from *mayā*, "by me", which took the place of *aham*; the same fix reaches MR-C03-practice and the session map. आहे is listed under *asti* with Bloch's other view noted. पुन्हा no longer claims Latin *re-* or Hindi *phir* as kin; उद्या no longer comes from *śvaḥ*. घर is "usually taken" from *gṛha*, a link CDIAL calls phonetically difficult, and is no longer built on *grah*. लहान is *ślakṣṇa*, not *laghu*. किती is *\*kiyatta*, not *kati*. धन्यवाद's *dhanya* is "fortunate", not "worthy". हो, बरा and नको no longer claim origins no source gives; कुठे and कधी are "usually traced"; राहणे is *\*rahati*; सुंदर carries CDIAL's query.
- **Font.** Long vowels with a nasal are written with ṁ, the track's convention, because the book font has no long vowel with a tilde. No candra vowel is used.
- **Durations.** After review, 36 lessons stay above their old limit, at their computed length and all under 300 s: MR-C01-ho 120 → 144, MR-C07-jane 250 → 263, MR-C30-kinva 200 → 212, MR-C31-dusra 210 → 226, MR-C32-nako 190 → 241, MR-C37-kon 210 → 221, MR-C38-kuthe 220 → 225, MR-C41-kholi 200 → 207, MR-C42-khaali 200 → 206, MR-C43-javal 210 → 217, MR-C43-laamb 210 → 222, MR-C44-daava 200 → 210, MR-C45-ne 220 → 224, MR-C45-pron-oblique 220 → 234, MR-C46-kuthun 210 → 251, MR-C47-laa-recipient 220 → 227, MR-C49-adj-prenominal 210 → 216, MR-C53-reduplication 220 → 229, MR-C54-kiti-excl 220 → 262, MR-C60-dusra-ordinal 220 → 291, MR-C60-pachva 240 → 278, MR-C60-pahila 230 → 280, MR-C60-tisra 210 → 242, MR-C61-satva 210 → 235, MR-C66-divas 210 → 212, MR-C66-dupar 210 → 213, MR-C66-ratra 210 → 240, MR-C66-sakal 210 → 226, MR-C66-sandhyakal 210 → 244, MR-C67-vajle 210 → 286, MR-C67-vajta 210 → 228, MR-C68-shubh-dupar 210 → 234, MR-C68-shubh-sakal 210 → 257, MR-C68-shubh-sandhyakal 210 → 293, MR-C69-ha 210 → 237, MR-C69-to 210 → 251. Seven are back at their old limit because review tightened their text: MR-C02-tumche-naav-kaay-aahe, MR-C30-pan, MR-C47-laa-object, MR-C48-possession, MR-C49-motha, MR-C53-khuup, MR-C61-sahava. Most of these lessons were already over their limit before the change; the new text adds a few seconds.
- **Roots.** Each lesson's `roots:` gained the slugs for its sources, reusing existing slugs where the word is the same, and separate slugs for homographs (*ghaṭa* "pot" beside *ghāta* "blow", *kuha* "cheat" beside *\*kuha* "fog"). `check:root-slug-splits` reports nothing new.

### Review corrections

An independent review checked every lesson the change touched (1,175) against the CDIAL and DEDR data files and Lane's lexicon. It fixed:

- **Wrong entry, sub-entry or reading (18).** काय's hook now says *\*kādṛk* was made after *yādṛk* and *tādṛk*. तारा is under the sub-entry *tāraka*; मठ under *\*maṭṭha*; कपाळ under *\*kappāla*. कच्चा is marked as taken from Punjabi. जड's "heavy" is given for Konkani. दाखवणे is in the main entry, not only the addenda. वाचवणे names the *v-* form CDIAL lists and marks the *b-* form as from Hindi. घंटा and गोळी no longer misquote CDIAL. मेणबत्ती no longer calls *battī* Marathi. कोण cites *kaḥ punar* (CDIAL 2575). सुंदर no longer ties its *su-* to सुद्धा. कुत्रा cites CDIAL 3275 for "imitative". पैसे keeps CDIAL's plain "from Hindi". वही's quotation is untangled; बोली writes *\*bōll*; the stale "दोन wearing an ending" line in दुसरा is gone.
- **Hedges that had been dropped (11).** Bloch's other views (बनवणे, विझवणे, समजावणे), CDIAL's "very doubtful" (वरण), "more probably" (भाडे), the Dravidian source of माळा, the *cā-* of चाळीस as from Hindi, *pūpalā* beside पोळी, *svidyate* beside शिजवणे, and the *b-* forms of बचत and बचाव as from Hindi.
- **"Not settled" or "no entry" when CDIAL has the word (27).** 19 Sanskrit loans that CDIAL lists as a headword or a "cf." (फुफ्फुस, कष्ट, प्राचीन, सैन्य, सैनिक, आगमन, प्रस्थान, एकत्र, शिक्षण, भावना, मैत्री, विश्वास, दया, उत्सुक, उत्पन्न, पदवी, पर्यटक, मंद, संघ). Eight words with an entry or a clear lead: छप्पर, कोपरा, टोपली, भोवती, चूक, ठेवणे in चालू ठेवणे, कचेरी, and खूप, now "usually traced to Persian *khūb*". Four "not settled" lessons gained a hedged lead: मागे, जांभळा, मनुका, सकाळ.
- **Persian and Arabic (3).** अवजार: Arabic *awzār* is "burdens; gear of war", not "tools". टास: the gong story is hedged. सवलत names Persian *suhūlat*. Every Arabic root named in the track was found in Lane.
- **येणे.** The lesson, the session map and the roadmap said going and coming are one Sanskrit verb. CDIAL lists येणे under *aiti*, *ā-* with *eti* (CDIAL 2534), and जाणे under *yāti*; they now say so, matching MR-C01-yeto.
- **Ordinals.** CDIAL lists पाचवा, सातवा, आठवा, नववा and दहावा under the Sanskrit ordinals, so the lessons no longer say Marathi stopped inheriting at five. They now say the first four are irregular and the rest follow a rule.
- **Roots and forward references.** 11 lessons' `roots:` were corrected to match their sources. सहावा no longer names सातवा before it is taught.
- **Durations.** Review tightened the new text and lowered the limits above: the 43 lessons now total 9,994 s, against 10,190 s before review (196 s less). MR-C60-chautha (220 → 235) and MR-R61-sixth-to-tenth (240 → 247) are set to their computed length; both were already over before this change.
- **"Taken apart" blocks that never named a source (15).** Thirteen modern Sanskrit compounds said only "a compound of Sanskrit parts" and listed the parts; each now says it is built from Sanskrit and cites CDIAL for every part CDIAL has as a headword (शुभेच्छा, कार्यक्रम, गृहपाठ, संग्रहालय, महाविद्यालय, मनोरंजक, रुग्णवाहिका, प्रशिक्षक, कर्मचारी, अर्थव्यवस्था, पर्यावरण, वास्तुविशारद, मूत्रपिंड; *mahā*, *rañjaka*, *vāhikā*, *pra-*, *śikṣaka*, *vyavasthā*, *pari* and *viśārada* have no headword and stay uncited). आनंद is now "Sanskrit *ānanda*, a tatsama (CDIAL 1172)", matching its hook. नको says its origin is not settled, that it opens with *na*, "not" (CDIAL 6906), and that CDIAL has no entry for the whole word; a line elsewhere in the lesson was tightened so it stays at 241 s. `EMPTY_ETYMOLOGY_BLOCK_PINS.marathi` falls 5 → 0.
