Marathi, chapter 92: Cup, Plate, Spoon, Knife, Bottle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कप (kap) — a cup.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fan, then the Marathi for a key.

You'll want to know: कप.
कप — kap — "a cup".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kap]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kap, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kap]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a fan, then the Marathi for a key, then say kap again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does कप (kap) mean? ("A cup".) Say it once more.
