## You'll want to know: तफ़्तीश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C294-TAFTISH294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तफ़्तीश** — *taftīś* — "an investigation".

> अगर तफ़्तीश ठीक से हुई तो सच सामने आएगा।
>
> *agar taftīś ṭhīk se huī to sac sāmne āegā.*
>
> If the investigation is done properly, the truth will come out.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तफ़्तीश** (*taftīś*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taftīsh*, "a searching, an inspection". It is the word of police reports: **पुलिस तफ़्तीश कर रही है**, the police are investigating.
