## You'll want to know: ಚಿಮ್ಮು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C482-TORE482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಮ್ಮು** — *cimmu* — "to spurt; to splash up".

> ಬಂಡೆಗೆ ಬಡಿದ ನೀರು ಮೇಲಕ್ಕೆ ಚಿಮ್ಮಿತು.
>
> *baṇḍege baḍida nīru mēlakke cimmitu.*
>
> Striking the rock, the water splashed up.

Past (it …): ಚಿಮ್ಮಿತು, *cimmitu*.

Of water, blood or sparks that shoot out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಚಿಮ್ಮು** (*cimmu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2548 lists Kannada *cimmu*, "to flick, to squirt, to burst forth, to gush out".
