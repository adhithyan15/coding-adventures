## You'll want to know: કવિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C336-LEKHAK336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કવિ** — *kavi* — "a poet".

> કવિએ સભામાં એક કવિતા વાંચી.
>
> *kavie sabhāmā̃ ek kavitā vā̃cī.*
>
> The poet read a poem at the gathering.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કવિ** (*kavi*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kavi*, "a wise man, a poet" (CDIAL 2964). His work is **કવિતા**, a poem.
