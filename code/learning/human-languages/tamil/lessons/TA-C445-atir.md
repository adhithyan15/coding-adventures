## You'll want to know: அதிர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C445-MINVETTU445-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அதிர்** — *atir* — "to shake; to vibrate".

> லாரி போனபோது சன்னல்கள் அதிர்ந்தன.
>
> *lāri pōṉapōtu caṉṉalkaḷ atirntaṉa.*
>
> When the lorry went past, the windows rattled.

Past (it …): அதிர்ந்தது, *atirntatu*.

Said of the ground, of windows and of loud sounds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அதிர்** (*atir*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 137), "to shake, to quake, to tremble".
