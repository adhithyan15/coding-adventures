## You'll want to know: नटः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C523-NATA523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नटः** — *naṭaḥ* — "an actor".

> मम मित्रं नाटके नटः अस्ति।
>
> *mama mitraṃ nāṭake naṭaḥ asti.*
>
> My friend is an actor in the play.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नटः** (*naṭaḥ*) comes from the root √naṭ, "to dance", as MW derives it; MW gives *naṭa* as an actor, a dancer, a mime (MW).
