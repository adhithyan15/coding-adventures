# प्रिय — the word a letter starts with

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C97-ADDRESS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Address somebody called Mira out loud. Today's word does the same
work on the page.

## You'll want to know: प्रिय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C97-PRIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रिय** (*priya*) — *dear*.

It goes **before** the name: **प्रिय मीरा**.

## Grammar Lens: the opposite side of the name
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C97-PRIYA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C97-ADDRESS-01] -->

| written | said |
|---|---|
| प्रिय मीरा | मीरा जी |

**One goes in front and one goes behind**, which is worth noticing because it is
the kind of thing a reader gets backwards once and then remembers. **प्रिय** is
an adjective describing the person, so it leads; **जी** is an honorific attached
to them, so it follows.

**प्रिय** belongs to writing. Saying it to somebody's face is the sort of thing
that would make them look up. A note, a card or a letter is where it lives, and
that is the one place this chapter is heading.
