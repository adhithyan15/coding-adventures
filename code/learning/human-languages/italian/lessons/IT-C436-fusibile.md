## You'll want to know: il fusibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C436-LUCE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il fusibile** — "the fuse".

> È saltato un fusibile.
>
> *A fuse has blown.*

*Saltare* is the verb: the fuse jumps.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fusibile* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *fūsibilis* 'that can be melted', from *fundere* 'to pour, to melt' (Lewis & Short): the wire melts and cuts the current.
