## You'll want to know: تنقید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C348-ETIRAZ348-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تنقید** — *tanqīd* — "criticism".

> اس نے حکومت پر سخت تنقید کی۔
>
> *us ne hukūmat par sakht tanqīd kī.*
>
> He criticised the government harshly.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنقید** (*tanqīd*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tanqīd*, "sorting good coins from bad": judging what holds up.
