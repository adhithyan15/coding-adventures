## You'll want to know: فیاض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C459-GIVE459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فیاض** — *fayyāz* — "generous; bountiful".

> ہمارے پڑوسی بڑے فیاض ہیں۔
>
> *hamāre paṛosī baṛe fayyāz haiṅ.*
>
> Our neighbours are very generous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فیاض** (*fayyāz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fayyāḍ*, "overflowing, bountiful".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
