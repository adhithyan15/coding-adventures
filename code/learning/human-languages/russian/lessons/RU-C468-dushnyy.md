## You'll want to know: душный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C468-ZHARA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**душный** (*dúshnyy*) — "stuffy, close".

> Был душный летний вечер.
>
> *Byl dúshnyy létniy vécher.*
>
> It was a stuffy summer evening.

*Душно* — it is stuffy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*душный* is built on *дух* 'breath, air' with *-ный*: air that chokes the breath. EtymDB lists *душный* without a deeper source.
