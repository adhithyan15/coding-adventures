Mandarin Chinese, chapter 134: Heavy and Soft.
7 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
重 (zhòng) — heavy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to wipe, then the Mandarin for to fold.

You'll want to know: 重.
重 — zhòng — "heavy".
这个太重了 zhège tài zhòng le — This one is too heavy.
The new character, in pieces: 重 (zhòng) — a short fall and a long bar on top, and under them 里, its vertical running up through the bar. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
重 (zhòng) is inherited from Middle Chinese drjowngX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'much', 'thick' and 'careful'. 'Heavy' is not among the glosses, so the step to it is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for describing things.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zhòng, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zhège tài zhòng le — This one is too heavy.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for to wipe, then the Mandarin for to fold, then say zhòng again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does 重 (zhòng) mean? ("Heavy".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 7.
重 (zhòng) — a fall and a bar over 里.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — a fall and a bar over 里 and Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The word first, then the character inside it.
[your turn — recall: say zhòng — R1, one lesson back, before it decays]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
里 千.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — lǐ, qiān]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — a fall and a bar over 里.
重 (zhòng).
Two pieces, top to bottom.
A short left-falling stroke, from the right down to the left, then a long horizontal across the whole width, as 千 begins.
里, seven strokes, in the order your hand knows: the box (its left side, the top that turns down the right side, the middle bar, the bar that closes it), then the vertical, which here starts above the long bar and runs down through it and the box, then the two bars of the foot, the lower one longer.
2 + 7 = nine.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 重 (zhòng), all nine, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What sits under the long bar of 重 (zhòng)? (里.) Where does the vertical start? (Above the long bar.) Which is the last stroke? (The long bar at the foot.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 重 (zhòng), pinned stroke paths and medians.
