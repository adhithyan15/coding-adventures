## You'll want to know: இறக்குமதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C511-VANIKAM511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**இறக்குமதி** — *iṟakkumati* — "imports".

> நம் நாடு எண்ணெயை இறக்குமதி செய்கிறது.
>
> *nam nāṭu eṇṇeyai iṟakkumati ceykiṟatu.*
>
> Our country imports oil.

The pair of ஏற்றுமதி (*ēṟṟumati*): goods unloaded, brought in. With செய் it is a verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இறக்குமதி** (*iṟakkumati*) is built on the native இறக்கு (*iṟakku*), "to unload, to bring down"; as with ஏற்றுமதி, the origin of -மதி is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
