## You'll want to know: die Skulptur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C519-GALERIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Skulptur** — "sculpture".

> Vor dem Rathaus steht eine moderne Skulptur aus Metall.
>
> *In front of the town hall there's a modern metal sculpture.*

Plural: *die Skulpturen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Skulptur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sculptūra*, from *sculpere* "to carve" (Lewis & Short).
