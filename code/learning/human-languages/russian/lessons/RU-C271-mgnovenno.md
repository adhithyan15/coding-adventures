## You'll want to know: мгновенно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C271-VNEZAPNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**мгновенно** (*mgnovénno*) — "instantly, in a flash".

> Он мгновенно понял, что случилось.
>
> *On mgnovénno pónyal, shto sluchílos.*
>
> He instantly understood what had happened.

The adverb of *мгновенный* (*mgnovénnyy*) "instant". *Мгновенно* is faster still than *быстро*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мгновенно* is built on *мгновение* "an instant", which is inherited through Old East Slavic *mĭgnovenije* from Proto-Slavic *mĭgnovenĭje*, built on *mĭgnǫti* "to blink" (EtymDB): as quick as a blink.
