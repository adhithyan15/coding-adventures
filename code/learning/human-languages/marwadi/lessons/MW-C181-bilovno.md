## You'll want to know: बिलोवणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C181-BILOVO181-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बिलोवणो** — *bilovṇo* — "to churn".

> दादी दिनूगै दही बिलोवै है।
>
> *dādī dinūgai dahī bilovai hai.*
>
> Grandmother churns the curd early in the morning.

Past (I …): म्हैं दही बिलोयो, *mhaĩ dahī biloyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिलोवणो** (*bilovṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vilobhayati*, "perplexes, stirs up"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bilovaṇā*, "churnings" (CDIAL 11913a).
