## You'll want to know: la canoa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C422-BARCA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la canoa** — "the canoe".

> D'estate facciamo canoa sul fiume.
>
> *In summer we go canoeing on the river.*

*Andare in canoa* — to go canoeing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*canoa* was borrowed from Spanish *canoa*, which took it from Taíno *kanoa* 'dugout canoe' (EtymDB), a language of the Caribbean.
