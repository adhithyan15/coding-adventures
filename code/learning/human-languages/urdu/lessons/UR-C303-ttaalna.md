## You'll want to know: ٹالنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C303-INKAR303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹالنا** — *ṭālnā* — "to put off; to dodge".

> اس نے میرا سوال ٹالا۔
>
> *us ne merā savāl ṭālā.*
>
> He dodged my question.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بات ٹالی, *us ne bāt ṭālī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭālnā*, "to avert, to evade", under Sanskrit *ṭálati* (CDIAL 5450). It is the causing partner of **ٹلنا**: a danger **ٹلنا**, and you **ٹالنا** a question.
