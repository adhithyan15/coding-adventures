## You'll want to know: reagire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C353-REAZIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**reagire** — "to react".

> Come hai reagito quando hai sentito la notizia?
>
> *How did you react when you heard the news?*

Conjugated with *-isc-*: *reagisco, reagisci*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reagire* is built from *re-* "back" and *agire* "to act", a learned borrowing from Latin *agere* "to do, to drive" (Lewis & Short).
