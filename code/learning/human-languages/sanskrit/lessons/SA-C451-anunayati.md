## You'll want to know: अनुनयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C451-ANUNAYA451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुनयति** — *anunayati* — "coaxes; soothes".

> शिशुः रोदिति, माता तम् अनुनयति।
>
> *śiśuḥ roditi, mātā tam anunayati.*
>
> The baby cries, and the mother soothes it.

Past (she …): सा शिशुम् अनुनीतवती, *sā śiśum anunītavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुनयति** (*anunayati*) is built from *anu*, "after", and the root √nī, "to lead", the root of **नयति**; MW gives "to conciliate, pacify, win over" (MW).
