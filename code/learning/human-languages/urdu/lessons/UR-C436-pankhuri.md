## You'll want to know: پنکھڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C436-QUDRAT436-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پنکھڑی** — *pankhuṛī* — "a petal".

> اس پھول کی پنکھڑیاں نرم اور لال ہیں۔
>
> *is phūl kī pankhuṛiyāṅ narm aur lāl haiṅ.*
>
> This flower's petals are soft and red.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پنکھڑی** (*pankhuṛī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *pakhuṛī* and *pā̃khṛī*, "a petal", under Sanskrit *pákṣman*, "eyelashes, the petal of a flower" (CDIAL 7638).
