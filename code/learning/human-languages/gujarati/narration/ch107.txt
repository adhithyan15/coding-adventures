Gujarati, chapter 107: Mattress, Bedsheet, Blanket, Step, Stove.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગાદલું (gādlũ) — a mattress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for soap, then the Gujarati for a towel.

You'll want to know: ગાદલું.
ગાદલું (gādlũ) — "a mattress".

The word, taken apart.
ગાદલું (gādlũ) belongs with gādī, "pad, raised seat", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed garda, "seat" (CDIAL 4053). CDIAL thinks the word probably came from Dravidian, after T. Burrow, and does not list ગાદલું (gādlũ) itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
પગથિયું (pagthiyũ) — a step, a stair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bedsheet, then the Gujarati for a blanket.

You'll want to know: પગથિયું.
પગથિયું (pagthiyũ) — "a step, a stair".

The word, taken apart.
પગથિયું (pagthiyũ) begins with pag, "foot", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit padga, "afoot" (CDIAL 7766). CDIAL has no entry for the whole word, so the rest of its history is not settled.
