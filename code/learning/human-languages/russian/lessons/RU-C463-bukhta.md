## You'll want to know: бухта
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C463-PRILIV-03]; assesses=[] -->

**бухта** (*búkhta*) — "a bay, a cove".

> Лодки стоят в тихой бухте.
>
> *Lódki stoyát v tíkhoy búkhte.*
>
> The boats are moored in a quiet bay.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бухта* was borrowed from German *Bucht* 'a bay, a bight' (EtymDB).
