## You'll want to know: प्रमुख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C352-ULLEKH352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमुख** — *pramukh* — "leading; chief".

> शहरातील प्रमुख रस्ते आज बंद आहेत.
>
> *śahrātīl pramukh raste āj band āhet.*
>
> The main roads in the city are closed today.

Also a noun, the head: संस्थेचा प्रमुख.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमुख** (*pramukh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pramukha*, "facing; foremost, chief" (Monier-Williams).
