## You'll want to know: सोनार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C505-KARAGIR505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सोनार** — *sonār* — "a goldsmith".

> आईने सोनाराकडून नवीन बांगडी घेतली.
>
> *āīne sonārākaḍūn navīn bāṅgḍī ghetlī.*
>
> Mother bought a new bangle from the goldsmith.

Masculine.

From a person: -कडून, as in सोनाराकडून.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सोनार** (*sonār*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *suvarṇakāra*, "goldsmith" (CDIAL 13520), from *suvarṇa*, "gold", and *kāra*, "maker".
