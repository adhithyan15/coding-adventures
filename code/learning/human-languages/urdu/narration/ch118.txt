Lesson 3 of 5.
قہوہ (qahvā) — green tea, coffee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a biscuit, then the Urdu for chocolate.

You'll want to know: قہوہ.
قہوہ — qahvā — "green tea, coffee".

The word, taken apart.
قہوہ (qahvā) is a loan from Arabic qahwa, "coffee", which reached Urdu through Persian. English coffee comes from the same Arabic word, through Turkish.

Lesson 4 of 5.
شربت (sharbat) — a sweet drink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for chocolate, then the Urdu for green tea.

You'll want to know: شربت.
شربت — sharbat — "a sweet drink".

The word, taken apart.
شربت (sharbat) is a loan from Arabic sharba, "a drink", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is sh-r-b (Lane). The root means "to drink".

Lesson 5 of 5.
لسی (lassī) — lassi, a yoghurt drink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for green tea, then the Urdu for a sweet drink.

You'll want to know: لسی.
لسی — lassī — "lassi, a yoghurt drink".

The word, taken apart.
لسی (lassī) goes back to a reconstructed lassī, "buttermilk", which CDIAL builds from Punjabi and Lahnda lassī and Sindhi lasī (CDIAL 10998). It lists no Hindi or Urdu form, and it gives no older source.
