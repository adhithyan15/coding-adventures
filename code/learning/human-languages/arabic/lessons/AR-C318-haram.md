## You'll want to know: هرم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C318-HADARA318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**هرم** — *haram* — "a pyramid".

> الهرم الكبير قديم جدا.
>
> *al-haram al-kabīr qadīm jiddan.*
>
> The Great Pyramid is very old.

Plural: أهرام, *ahrām*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هرم** (*haram*) shares its letters with the root *h-r-m*, "to grow very old" (Lane). Whether the pyramid's name comes from it or from another language is not settled.
