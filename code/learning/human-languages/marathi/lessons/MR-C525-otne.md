## You'll want to know: ओतणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C525-BHANDI525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ओतणे** — *otṇe* — "to pour".

> मी माठातलं पाणी कळशीत ओतलं.
>
> *mī māṭhātlaṁ pāṇī kaḷśīt otlaṁ.*
>
> I poured the water from the clay pot into the water pot.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाणी ओतलं, *tyāne pāṇī otlaṁ*.

Where it goes takes -त: कळशीत ओतणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओतणे** (*otṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *otṇẽ*, "to pour", under *\*avōtta*, "flowed down" (CDIAL 902).
