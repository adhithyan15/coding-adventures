## You'll want to know: ひきうける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C454-TASK454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきうける** — *hikiukeru* — "to take on (a task)".

> **かれは ぶちょうの しごとを ひきうけました。** — *kare wa buchō no shigoto o hikiukemashita* — "He took on the department head's work."

Polite **ひきうけます**, *hikiukemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **どうりょうが しごとを ひきうけました。**, *dōryō ga shigoto o hikiukemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきうける** (*hikiukeru*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *hiki*, "pulling (hiku)" and *ukeru*, "to receive" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
