## You'll want to know: રોકાણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C427-SAVE427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**રોકાણ** — *rokāṇ* — "an investment".

> તેણે બચતનું રોકાણ બેંકમાં કર્યું.
>
> *teṇe bacatnũ rokāṇ beṅkmā̃ karyũ.*
>
> She invested her savings in the bank.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રોકાણ** (*rokāṇ*) is built on રોકવું, to stop, to hold back, which goes back to *\*rōkk*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10827): money held back and put to work.
