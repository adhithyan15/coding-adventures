## You'll want to know: prīncipium, prīncipiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C296-INITIUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**prīncipium, prīncipiī** — "a start, an origin".

> Ā prīncipiō rem tōtam nārrāvit.
>
> *He told the whole affair from the start.*

Neuter, 2nd declension. *Ā prīncipiō*: from the start. English *principle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**prīncipium** is built on *prīnceps*, "first, the one who takes first place" (EtymDB): what comes first. English *principle* comes from it through French.
