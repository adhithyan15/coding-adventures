## You'll want to know: tuonare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C467-TEMPORALE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tuonare** — "to thunder".

> Tuona da un'ora, ma non piove.
>
> *It has been thundering for an hour, but it is not raining.*

*Il tuono* — thunder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
