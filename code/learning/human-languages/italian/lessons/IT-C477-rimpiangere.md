## You'll want to know: rimpiangere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C477-NOSTALGIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rimpiangere** — "to miss, to regret (something lost)".

> Rimpiango gli anni dell'università.
>
> *I miss my university years.*

Built on *piangere*; it takes a direct object: *rimpiango quei giorni*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimpiangere* is built from *ri-*, *in-* and *piangere* "to weep", which is inherited from Latin *plangere* "to beat the breast in grief" (Lewis & Short).
