Punjabi, chapter 50: Finger, Lip, Neck, Face, Blood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉਂਗਲ (uṅgal) — a finger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a tooth, then the Punjabi for the throat.

You'll want to know: ਉਂਗਲ.
ਉਂਗਲ — uṅgal — "a finger".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: uṅgal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: uṅgal, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say uṅgal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a tooth, then the Punjabi for the throat, then say uṅgal again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਉਂਗਲ (uṅgal) mean? ("A finger".) Say it once more.
