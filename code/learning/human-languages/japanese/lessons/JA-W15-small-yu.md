## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-YU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **ゅ** — small *yu*

Same shape as **ゆ**, written small and sitting low — the way **っ** is a small
**つ**.

The two small signs do opposite jobs, and it is worth putting them side by side:

| sign | what it does |
|---|---|
| **っ** | holds a silent beat *before* the next consonant |
| **ゅ** | joins to the sign *before* it, and the two become **one beat** |

So **し** plus **ゅ** is not *shi-yu*. It is one beat, *shu*. Voice the **し**
with the dakuten first and **じ** plus **ゅ** is one beat, *ju*.

That is the sign the last number needs.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YU-01, JA-SCRIPT-YU-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01] -->

1. Write **ゆ**, then **ゅ** beneath it, half the height.
2. Write **っ** and **ゅ** side by side; say which one holds and which one joins.
3. Read **じゅ** as a single beat. Do not let a *yu* escape between the two.
