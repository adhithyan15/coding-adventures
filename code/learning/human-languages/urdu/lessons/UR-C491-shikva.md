## You'll want to know: شکوہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C491-MENDING491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شکوہ** — *shikvā* — "a grievance; a complaint (to someone close)".

> اسے اپنے دوست سے شکوہ ہے۔
>
> *use apne dost se shikvā hai.*
>
> He has a grievance against his friend.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شکوہ** (*shikvā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shakwā*, "a complaint".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
