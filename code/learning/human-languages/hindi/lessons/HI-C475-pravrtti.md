## You'll want to know: प्रवृत्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C475-CHARITRA475-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवृत्ति** — *pravṛtti* — "a tendency; a disposition".

> बच्चों में नई चीज़ सीखने की प्रवृत्ति होती है।
>
> *baccoṁ meṁ naī cīz sīkhne kī pravṛtti hotī hai.*
>
> Children have a natural tendency to learn new things.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवृत्ति** (*pravṛtti*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *pravṛtti*, first "moving onwards" and then "a course or tendency towards, an inclination" (Monier-Williams).
