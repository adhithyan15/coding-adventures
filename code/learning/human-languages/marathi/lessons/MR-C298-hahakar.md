## You'll want to know: हाहाकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C298-GONDHAL298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हाहाकार** — *hāhākār* — "havoc; an outcry".

> पुरामुळे गावात हाहाकार माजला.
>
> *purāmuḷe gāvāt hāhākār mājlā.*
>
> The flood caused havoc in the village.

Masculine.

हाहाकार माजणे, havoc breaks out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाहाकार** (*hāhākār*) is built from the cry of distress *hā hā* and Sanskrit *kāra*, 'a making, a doing' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
