## You'll want to know: 年画
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C503-ART503-05]; assesses=[] -->

**年画** — *niánhuà* — "a new year print (hung at the spring festival)".

> *guònián de shíhou wǒ nǎinai mǎi le yìxiē niánhuà* — At the new year, my grandma bought some new year prints.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**年画** (*niánhuà*) is built from 年 'a year' and 画 'a painting', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nen* and *hweaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the grain is ripe'.
