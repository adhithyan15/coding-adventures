## You'll want to know: предъявлять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C416-PROVODNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**предъявлять** (*predyavlyát*) — "to present (a ticket or a document)".

> В вагоне пассажиры предъявляют билеты и паспорта.
>
> *V vagóne passazhíry predyavlyáyut biléty i pasportá.*
>
> In the carriage the passengers present their tickets and passports.

Imperfective; perfective *предъявить* (*предъявлю, предъявишь*). Formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*предъявлять* is built from *пред-* 'before' and *являть* 'to show': to put something before someone's eyes. EtymDB traces *явить* to Proto-Slavic *aviti* 'to make visible'.
