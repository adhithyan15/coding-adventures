French, chapter 89: Tortoise, New, Old, Fast, Easy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la tortue — a tortoise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a frog, then the French for a tiger.

You'll want to know: la tortue.
la tortue — "a tortoise".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does la tortue mean? ("A tortoise".) Say it once more.
