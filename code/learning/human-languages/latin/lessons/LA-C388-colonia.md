## You'll want to know: colōnia, colōniae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C388-MUNICEPS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**colōnia, colōniae** — "a colony, a settlement".

> Mīlitēs in colōniam missī sunt.
>
> *Soldiers were sent to the settlement.*

*colōnia, colōniae* f., built on *colōnus*, a settler, a farmer. Many cities of Europe began as Roman colonies. English *colony*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*colōnia* is built on *colōnus* "a settler, a farmer" (EtymDB), which comes from *colō* "to till, to dwell" (Lewis & Short). English *colony* comes from it.
