## You'll want to know: りえき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C361-MOUKERU361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**りえき** — *rieki* — "a profit; gains".

> **りえきが あったら、みんなで わけます。** — *rieki ga attara, minna de wakemasu* — "If there is a profit, we share it among us."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りえき** (*rieki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ri*, "advantage" (Middle Chinese *lijH*) and *eki*, "benefit" (*'jiek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
