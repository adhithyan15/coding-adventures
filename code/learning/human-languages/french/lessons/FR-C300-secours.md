## You'll want to know: les secours
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C300-SECOURS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**les secours** — "the emergency services, help".

> Les secours sont arrivés en dix minutes.
>
> *The emergency services arrived in ten minutes.*

*Au secours !* — help! *Les premiers secours* — first aid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*secours* is inherited from Latin *succursus* "help" (EtymDB), from *succurrere* "to run to help".
