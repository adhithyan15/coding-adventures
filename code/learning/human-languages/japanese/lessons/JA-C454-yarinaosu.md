## You'll want to know: やりなおす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C454-TASK454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**やりなおす** — *yarinaosu* — "to do over; to redo".

> **まちがえたので、さいしょから やりなおしました。** — *machigaeta node, saisho kara yarinaoshimashita* — "I made a mistake, so I did it over from the start."

Polite **やりなおします**, *yarinaoshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが しゅくだいを やりなおしました。**, *gakusei ga shukudai o yarinaoshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やりなおす** (*yarinaosu*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *yari*, "doing (yaru)" and *naosu*, "to fix" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
