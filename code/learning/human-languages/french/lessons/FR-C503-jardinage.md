## You'll want to know: le jardinage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C503-JARDIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le jardinage** — "gardening".

> Le jardinage me détend après le travail.
>
> *Gardening relaxes me after work.*

*Faire du jardinage* — to do some gardening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le jardinage* is built on *jardin* "garden" with *-age*; *jardin* goes back to Frankish *gardo* "an enclosure", a Germanic word.
