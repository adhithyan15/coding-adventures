Lesson 1 of 5.
درمانگاه (darmângâh) — a clinic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for immediately, then the Persian for soon.

You'll want to know: درمانگاه.
درمانگاه — darmângâh — "a clinic".

The word, taken apart.
darmângâh is darmân, "cure, treatment", with gâh, "place": the place of treatment. darmân is native, usually traced to Middle Persian darmān.

Lesson 2 of 5.
باشگاه (bâshgâh) — a gym, a club.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for soon, then the Persian for a clinic.

You'll want to know: باشگاه.
باشگاه — bâshgâh — "a gym, a club".

The word, taken apart.
bâshgâh is built from bâsh-, the present stem of budan, "to be", and gâh, "place": a place to be, a modern Persian coinage for a club. gâh is inherited, usually traced to Middle Persian gāh.

Lesson 3 of 5.
حیاط (hayât) — a yard, a courtyard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a clinic, then the Persian for a gym.

You'll want to know: حیاط.
حیاط — hayât — "a yard, a courtyard".

The word, taken apart.
hayât is usually traced to Arabic, to the root ḥ-w-ṭ, "to guard, to surround" (Lane), the root of ḥāʾiṭ, "wall": the walled yard. It is spelled differently from hayât, "life".
