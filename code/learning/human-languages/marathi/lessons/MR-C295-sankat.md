## You'll want to know: संकट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C295-SANKAT295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संकट** — *saṅkaṭ* — "a crisis; a danger".

> गावावर पाण्याचं मोठं संकट आलं.
>
> *gāvāvar pāṇyācaṁ moṭhaṁ saṅkaṭ ālaṁ.*
>
> A great water crisis struck the village.

Neuter.

संकट येणे, a crisis comes; संकटात, in trouble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकट** (*saṅkaṭ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṅkaṭa*, 'narrow; a strait, a difficulty' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
