## You'll want to know: شمع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C341-MEHMANI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شمع** (*sham'*) — "a candle".

> او شمع های کیک تولد را خاموش کرد.
>
> *U sham'-hâ-ye keyk-e tavallod râ khâmush kard.*
>
> She put out the candles on the birthday cake.

*Sham' roshan kardan* "to light a candle".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sham'* is borrowed from Arabic *sham'*, "wax, a candle" (EtymDB).
