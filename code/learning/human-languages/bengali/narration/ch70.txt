Bengali, chapter 70: Lion, Elephant, Monkey, Snake, Crow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সিংহ (singhô) — a lion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bird, then the Bengali for a mouse.

You'll want to know: সিংহ.
সিংহ — singhô — "a lion".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
