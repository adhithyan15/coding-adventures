Bengali, chapter 382: Keeping It Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নিষ্ঠা (nishṭhā) — dedication; devotion (to work).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for skill, then the Bengali for to practise.

You'll want to know: নিষ্ঠা.
নিষ্ঠা — nishṭhā — "dedication; devotion (to work)".
নিষ্ঠা থাকলে তুমি কঠিন কাজও শিখতে পারবে। nishṭhā thākle tumi kôṭhin kājo shikhte pārbe. With dedication, you can learn even a hard skill.

The word, taken apart.
নিষ্ঠা (nishṭhā) is Sanskrit niṣṭhā, firmness and steady devotion (Monier-Williams): staying with a task out of care for it.

অধ্যবসায় — ôdhyôbôsāy — "perseverance".
অধ্যবসায় থাকলে সাফল্য আসবেই। ôdhyôbôsāy thākle sāphôlyô āsbei. If you persevere, success will surely come.

The word, taken apart.
অধ্যবসায় (ôdhyôbôsāy) is Sanskrit adhyavasāya, resolve or effort of mind (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the patience to keep trying.

এই ফর্মটা পূরণ করলে তুমি ছাড় পাবে। ei phôrmṭā pūrôn kôrle tumi chhāṛ pābe. If you fill in this form, you'll get a discount.
Past (he/she …): তিনি তাঁর স্বপ্ন পূরণ করলেন, tini tā̃r svôpnô pūrôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
পূরণ করা (pūrôn kôrā) is Sanskrit pūraṇa, filling or completing (Monier-Williams), with করা: to fill a form, a need or a dream.

Lesson 4 of 5.
বজায় রাখা (bôjāy rākhā) — to maintain; to keep up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for perseverance, then the Bengali for to fulfil.

You'll want to know: বজায় রাখা.
বজায় রাখা — bôjāy rākhā — "to maintain; to keep up".
এই গতি বজায় রাখলে আমরা সময়মতো পৌঁছাব। ei gôti bôjāy rākhle āmrā sômôymôto poũchhābô. If we keep up this pace, we'll arrive on time.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শান্তি বজায় রাখলেন, tini shānti bôjāy rākhlen.

The word, taken apart.
বজায় রাখা (bôjāy rākhā) is usually traced to Persian ba-jā, in its place, with রাখা 'to keep': to keep a thing where it is, to maintain it.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: সংশোধন করা.
সংশোধন করা — sôngshodhôn kôrā — "to correct; to amend".
ভুল থাকলে প্রবন্ধটা সংশোধন করো। bhul thākle prôbôndhôṭā sôngshodhôn kôro. If there are mistakes, correct the essay.
Past (he/she …): তিনি আইনটা সংশোধন করলেন, tini āinṭā sôngshodhôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
সংশোধন করা (sôngshodhôn kôrā) is Sanskrit saṃśodhana, thorough cleansing (Monier-Williams), with করা. Bengali cleans out errors: to correct a text or amend a law.
