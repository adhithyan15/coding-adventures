# Retrieve the completed form

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KITA-190] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Hear “did or made” and prepare the single completed form.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KITA-190, PA-LEX-KARNA-05, PA-FORM-MILIA-188] -->

Keep **ਕੀਤਾ** distinct from the infinitive **ਕਰਨਾ**. Familiar **ਮਿਲਿਆ** is a
completed-action comparison, not an agreement template. In the coming
transitive frame, **ਕੀਤਾ** will agree with the unmarked masculine-singular
object **ਕੰਮ**.
