## You'll want to know: ترجیح دینا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C385-DAKHILA385-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ترجیح دینا** — *tarjīh denā* — "to prefer; to give priority to".

> اگر انتخاب میرا ہوتا تو میں تاریخ کو ترجیح دیتا۔
>
> *agar intikhāb merā hotā to maiṅ tārīkh ko tarjīh detā.*
>
> If the choice were mine, I would pick history.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے ترجیح دی, *us ne tarjīh dī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترجیح دینا** (*tarjīh denā*) is built from *tarjīh*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tarjīḥ*, "giving more weight (to one side)", and **دینا**, "to give".
