## You'll want to know: अग्निः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C38-EARTH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्निः** (*agniḥ*) — "fire".

**अग्निः** is fire itself — the flame on a hearth, and the fire an offering is poured into.

You have had the doing-word for a while: दहति, "burns". **अग्निः** is the thing that does it.

Latin *ignis* is the very same word, and English carries it in *ignite* and *igneous*; Russian *ogon'* is another of its brothers. It went west almost unchanged, and in Sanskrit it stands as the first word of the oldest hymn there is.
