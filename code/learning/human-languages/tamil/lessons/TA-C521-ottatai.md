# ஒட்டடை (oṭṭaṭai) — a cobweb

## You'll want to know: ஒட்டடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C521-VEEDU521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒட்டடை** — *oṭṭaṭai* — "a cobweb".

> பழைய வீட்டின் மூலைகளில் ஒட்டடை நிறைந்திருந்தது.
>
> *paḻaiya vīṭṭiṉ mūlaikaḷil oṭṭaṭai niṟaintiruntatu.*
>
> The corners of the old house were full of cobwebs.

To clear cobwebs is ஒட்டடை அடி, with a long-handled brush.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ஒட்டடை** (*oṭṭaṭai*) is not settled: no article of the Dravidian dictionary gives it, though it looks native.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
