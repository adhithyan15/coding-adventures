## You'll want to know: राक्षसः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C334-RAKSHASA334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राक्षसः** — *rākṣasaḥ* — "a demon".

> वने एकः भयंकरः राक्षसः वसति।
>
> *vane ekaḥ bhayaṅkaraḥ rākṣasaḥ vasati.*
>
> A terrible demon lives in the forest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राक्षसः** (*rākṣasaḥ*) is built on *rakṣas*, "a demon, an evil being"; MW gives *rākṣasa* as belonging to the Rakṣas and, as a noun, a demon (MW).
