## You'll want to know: o congelador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C464-GEADA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o congelador** — "the freezer".

> Há peixe no congelador.
>
> *There is fish in the freezer.*

Part of *o frigorífico* or a separate box.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*congelador* is built on *congelar* 'to freeze', a learned borrowing from Latin *congelāre* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
