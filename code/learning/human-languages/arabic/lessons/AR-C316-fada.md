# فاض (fāḍa) — to overflow, to flood

## You'll want to know: فاض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C316-KARITHA316-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فاض** — *fāḍa* — "to overflow, to flood".

> فاض النهر بعد الأمطار.
>
> *fāḍa an-nahr baʿda al-amṭār.*
>
> The river overflowed after the rains.

Present: يفيض, *yafīḍ*.

The taught فيضان, a flood, is its verbal noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فاض** (*fāḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-y-ḍ* (Lane), "to overflow, to run over", the root of the taught *fayaḍān*, a flood.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
