## You'll want to know: রুচি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C529-DOTANA529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**রুচি** — *ruchi* — "personal taste".

> আমার আর আমার বোনের রুচি আলাদা।
>
> *Āmār ār āmār boner ruchi ālādā.*
>
> My sister and I have different tastes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রুচি** (*ruchi*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ruci*, whose senses in Monier-Williams run from "light, lustre" to "liking, taste".
