Lesson 1 of 5.
इकट्ठा (ikaṭṭhā) — gathered together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to sway (with joy or music), then the Hindi for merriment.

You'll want to know: इकट्ठा.
इकट्ठा — ikaṭṭhā — "gathered together".
मेले में पूरा गाँव इकट्ठा हुआ। mele meṁ pūrā gā̃v ikaṭṭhā huā. The whole village gathered at the fair.

The word, taken apart.
इकट्ठा (ikaṭṭhā) goes back to Sanskrit ekastha, "standing together" (CDIAL 2480): all in one spot. इकट्ठा होना is to gather; इकट्ठा करना, to collect.

You'll want to know: झाँकी.
झाँकी — jhā̃kī — "a tableau; a float in a procession".
जुलूस में बच्चों की झाँकी सबसे सुंदर थी। julūs meṁ baccoṁ kī jhā̃kī sabse sundar thī. The children's float was the most beautiful in the procession.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
झाँकी (jhā̃kī) is built on jhā̃knā, to peep, which CDIAL 5325 records as jhā̃khnā under jhaṅkh: a glimpse, and then a scene set up for people to look at.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for gathered together, then the Hindi for a tableau.

You'll want to know: झूलना.
झूलना — jhūlnā — "to swing".
बच्चे झूले पर झूल रहे थे। bacce jhūle par jhūl rahe the. The children were swinging on the swing.
Past (he …): वह झूले पर झूला, vah jhūle par jhūlā.

The word, taken apart.
झूलना (jhūlnā) is inherited from jhulyati, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5406). झूला, a swing, belongs with it: you झूलते हो on a झूला.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a tableau, then the Hindi for to swing.

You'll want to know: शोभायात्रा.
शोभायात्रा — śobhāyātrā — "a festive procession".
शोभायात्रा कितने बजे चलेगी? śobhāyātrā kitne baje calegī? What time will the procession set off?
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
शोभायात्रा (śobhāyātrā) joins Sanskrit śobhā, "splendour, beauty" (Monier-Williams), and यात्रा, a journey or procession: a procession for show, with music and decorated floats. Like जुलूस, it निकलती है.
