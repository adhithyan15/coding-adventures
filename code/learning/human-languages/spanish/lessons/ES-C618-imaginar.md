## You'll want to know: imaginar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C618-SUPONE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**imaginar** — "to imagine".

> Imagina que ganas la lotería.
>
> *Imagine that you win the lottery.*

*Me imagino que* — I suppose that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imaginar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *imaginari* 'to picture to oneself', built on *imago* 'image' (EtymDB).
