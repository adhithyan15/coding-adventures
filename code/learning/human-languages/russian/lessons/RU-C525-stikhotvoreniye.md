## You'll want to know: стихотворение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C525-POETRY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**стихотворение** (*stikhotvoréniye*) — "a poem".

> В школе мы учили это стихотворение.
>
> *V shkóle my uchíli éto stikhotvoréniye.*
>
> We learnt this poem at school.

Neuter. *Стихи* — verse, poems.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стихотворение* is a compound of *стих* 'a line of verse' and *творить* 'to create'; EtymDB has no entry.
