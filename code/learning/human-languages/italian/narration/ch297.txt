Italian, chapter 297: In manette.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
arrestare — to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to question, then the Italian for to confess.

You'll want to know: arrestare.
arrestare — "to arrest".
Hanno arrestato due persone. They arrested two people.
In formal Italian also ‘to stop’: arrestare la corsa.

The word, taken apart.
arrestare is usually traced to Vulgar Latin arrestāre "to make stop", built on Latin restāre "to stay behind" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
catturare — to capture, to catch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the arrest, then the Italian for the handcuffs.

You'll want to know: catturare.
catturare — "to capture, to catch".
Hanno catturato il rapinatore dopo una settimana. They caught the robber after a week.
Also for animals: catturare un leone.

The word, taken apart.
catturare is built on cattura "capture", from Latin captūra "a catching", from capere "to take" (Lewis & Short).
