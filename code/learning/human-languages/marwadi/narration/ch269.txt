Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 269: Not Now.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बहानो (bahāno) — an excuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a meaning, then the Marwadi for a good word for someone.

You'll want to know: बहानो.
बहानो — bahāno — "an excuse".
यो बहानो साचो कोनी। yo bahāno sācho konī. This excuse isn't true.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बहानो (bahāno) is usually traced to Persian bahāna, "a pretext"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: इनकार.
इनकार — inkār — "a refusal".
म्हैं इनकार कियो। mhaĩ inkār kiyo. I refused.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
इनकार (inkār) is usually traced, through Persian inkār, to Arabic inkār, "denial". With कियो it says you refused: म्हैं इनकार कियो.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अड़णो (aṛṇo) — to dig in one's heels; to insist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an excuse, then the Marwadi for a refusal.

You'll want to know: अड़णो.
अड़णो — aṛṇo — "to dig in one's heels; to insist".
छोरो अड़ै है अर स्कूल कोनी जावै। chhoro aṛai hai ar skūl konī jāvai. The boy digs in his heels and won't go to school.
Past (he …): वो अड़्यो, vo aṛyo.

The word, taken apart.
अड़णो (aṛṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed aḍ, "obstruct, stop"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi aṛnā, "to come to a stop", and Kumaoni aṛṇo, "to persist" (CDIAL 187). Someone who stops and will not move on is digging in.

Lesson 5 of 5.
टोकणो (ṭokṇo) — to interrupt; to object.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to dig in one's heels, then the Marwadi for stubbornness.

You'll want to know: टोकणो.
टोकणो — ṭokṇo — "to interrupt; to object".
मास्टर बोलै है, मत टोको। māsṭar bolai hai, mat ṭoko. The teacher is speaking: don't interrupt.
Past (I …): म्हैं थारी बात कोनी टोकी, mhaĩ thārī bāt konī ṭokī.

The word, taken apart.
टोकणो (ṭokṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ṭōkk, "strike, bite"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi ṭoknā, "to check, blame, question" (CDIAL 5476).
