## You'll want to know: стирка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C530-LAUNDRY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**стирка** (*stírka*) — "laundry, washing".

> Стирка занимает у меня целый вечер.
>
> *Stírka zanimáyet u menyá tsélyy vécher.*
>
> Doing the laundry takes me a whole evening.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стирка* is built on *стирать* 'to wash clothes', first 'to rub off', from the root of *тереть* 'to rub'; EtymDB has no entry.
