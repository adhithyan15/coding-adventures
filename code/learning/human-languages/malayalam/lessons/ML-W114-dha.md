# ധ (dha) — the letter inside ബുധൻ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-LLA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C113-KHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ള and ഖ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ബുധൻ** (*budhan*), "Wednesday".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ധ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-DHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ധ** — *dha*, a *d* with a puff of breath.

It is inside **ബുധൻ** (*budhan*), "Wednesday", the day named for the planet
Mercury.

## Writing: ധ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-DHA-01] -->

Put your pen on ധ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
