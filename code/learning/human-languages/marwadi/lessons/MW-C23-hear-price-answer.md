# Hear *ye ghaṇo mahaṅgo hai* — this is very expensive

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 24s] Say the price question, write *very* and expensive, then say and
write the sweet crumbled wheat dessert.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-PRICE-ANSWER-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-GHANO-01, MW-LEX-MAHANGO-01, MW-LEX-YE-01, MW-LEX-HAI-01] -->

> *ye ghaṇo mahaṅgo hai* — **this is very expensive**

Four words, every one of them already yours. *ye* points, *ghaṇo* strengthens,
*mahaṅgo* describes, *hai* closes. The order is fixed: the strengthener always
comes before the word it strengthens.

Say it once flat, as a fact, and once slowly, the way you would actually say it
across a counter when you mean to keep talking. The words do not change; the
pace does, and the pace is what tells a seller a bargain has begun.
