## You'll want to know: اسٹیڈیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C523-STADIUM523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اسٹیڈیم** — *isṭeḍiyam* — "a stadium".

> اسٹیڈیم شائقین سے بھرا تھا۔
>
> *isṭeḍiyam shāiqīn se bharā thā.*
>
> The stadium was full of fans.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اسٹیڈیم** (*isṭeḍiyam*) is borrowed from English *stadium*.
