# bezweifeln — to doubt (something), to question

## You'll want to know: bezweifeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C337-IRRTUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bezweifeln** — "to doubt (something), to question".

> Der Arzt bezweifelt, dass das Medikament hilft.
>
> *The doctor doubts that the medicine helps.*

Inseparable, with a direct object: *Ich bezweifle das.* *Zweifeln* takes *an*; *bezweifeln* takes the thing itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bezweifeln* is built with *be-* on *zweifeln*, the verb of *Zweifel* "doubt", which goes back to Proto-Germanic *twīflaz* (EtymDB).
