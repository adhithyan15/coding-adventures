## You'll want to know: inciampare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C483-FERITE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**inciampare** — "to trip, to stumble".

> Sono inciampato sulle scale e sono caduto.
>
> *I tripped on the stairs and fell.*

It takes *essere*: *sono inciampato*. *Inciampare in* something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *inciampare* is not settled; it may be built on an old dialect word *ciampa* "paw, foot".
