## You'll want to know: o boato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C283-BOATO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o boato** — "the rumour".

> Corre um boato na escola: o diretor vai sair.
>
> *There is a rumour going round the school: the head is leaving.*

*Corre um boato* — a rumour is going round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*boato* is a learned borrowing from Latin *boātus* "a bellowing, a loud cry" (EtymDB), from *boāre* "to cry aloud".
