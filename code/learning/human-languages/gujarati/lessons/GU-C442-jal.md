## You'll want to know: જાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C442-SEA442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જાળ** — *jāḷ* — "a fishing net".

> સવારે હોડીમાંથી તેમણે દરિયામાં જાળ નાખી.
>
> *savāre hoḍīmā̃thī temṇe dariyāmā̃ jāḷ nākhī.*
>
> In the morning they cast a net into the sea from the boat.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાળ** (*jāḷ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *jāla*, "a net"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 5213).
