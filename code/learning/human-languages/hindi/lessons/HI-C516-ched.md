## You'll want to know: छेद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C516-RISNA516-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छेद** — *ched* — "a hole".

> बाल्टी में एक छोटा छेद है।
>
> *Bālṭī meṁ ek choṭā ched hai.*
>
> There is a small hole in the bucket.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छेद** (*ched*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *cheda*, "cutting off; a section, an incision" (Monier-Williams); Hindi uses it for a hole.
