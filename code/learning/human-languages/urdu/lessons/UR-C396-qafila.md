## You'll want to know: قافلہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C396-SHAHRAH396-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قافلہ** — *qāfilā* — "a convoy; a caravan".

> ناکے پر ٹرکوں کا ایک قافلہ رکا۔
>
> *nāke par ṭrakoṅ kā ek qāfilā rukā.*
>
> A convoy of lorries stopped at the checkpoint.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قافلہ** (*qāfilā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qāfila*, "a caravan".
