## You'll want to know: संभ्रम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C556-ANUKUL556-05]; assesses=[] -->

**संभ्रम** — *sambhram* — "confusion; doubt".

> दोन वेगळ्या सूचनांमुळे सगळ्यांच्या मनात संभ्रम होता.
>
> *don vegaḷyā sūcanāmmuḷe sagḷyāñcyā manāt sambhram hotā.*
>
> Because of two different instructions, everyone was confused.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संभ्रम** (*sambhram*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sambhrama*, "whirling round, confusion" (Monier-Williams).
