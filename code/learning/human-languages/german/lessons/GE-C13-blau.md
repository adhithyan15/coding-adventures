# blau — "blue"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-ROT-01, GE-ETYMON-ROT-RUBER-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The fourth and last colour of the set — and the second one German
lent abroad.

## You'll want to know: blau
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-BLAU-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-ROT-01] -->

> **blau** — "blue."

Four letters, one syllable, and the vowel is the trap.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-SOUND-BLAU-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-BLAU-01] -->

- `diphthong-au` — German *au* is the vowel of English *house* or *cow*. It is
  **not** the *aw* of *law* and not the *oo* of *blue*.

So *blau* rhymes with English **cow**: *blow* as in *plough*, never *blow* as in
*blew*.

That one vowel is what stops an English speaker recognising the word by ear even
though it is the same word.
