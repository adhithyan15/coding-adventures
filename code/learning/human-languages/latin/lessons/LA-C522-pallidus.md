# pallidus, pallida, pallidum — pale

## You'll want to know: pallidus, pallida, pallidum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C522-PALL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**pallidus, pallida, pallidum** — "pale".

> Post morbum puella pallida est.
>
> *After her illness the girl is pale.*

From *palleō*. English *pallid*, and *pale* through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pallidus** is built on *palleō* "to be pale" with *-idus* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *pallid* comes from it, and *pale* came the same way through French.
