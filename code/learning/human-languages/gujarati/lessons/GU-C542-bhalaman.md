## You'll want to know: ભલામણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C542-SAMADHAN542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભલામણ** — *bhalāmaṇ* — "a recommendation".

> જો સાહેબ ભલામણ કરશે તો મને નોકરી મળશે.
>
> *jo sāheb bhalāmaṇ karśe to mane nokrī maḷśe.*
>
> If the boss recommends me, I'll get the job.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભલામણ** (*bhalāmaṇ*) is a good word put in for someone. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
