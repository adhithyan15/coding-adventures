# ਪਰੇਸ਼ਾਨ (pareshān) — upset

## You'll want to know: ਪਰੇਸ਼ਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C328-CARE328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰੇਸ਼ਾਨ** — *pareshān* — "upset".

> ਪੁੱਤਰ ਦੇ ਬੁਖ਼ਾਰ ਤੋਂ ਪਿਤਾ ਜੀ ਪਰੇਸ਼ਾਨ ਸਨ।
>
> *puttar de bukhār ton pitā jī pareshān san.*
>
> Father was upset about his son's fever.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰੇਸ਼ਾਨ** (*pareshān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *parēshān*, "scattered, distressed".
