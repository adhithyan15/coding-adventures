## You'll want to know: کتابفروشی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C528-BOOKS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کتابفروشی** (*ketâbforushi*) — "a bookshop".

> نزدیک خانه ما یک کتابفروشی قدیمی هست.
>
> *nazdik-e khâne-ye mâ yek ketâbforushi-ye qadimi hast.*
>
> There's an old bookshop near our house.

*ketâb* 'book' + *forush* 'selling' (stem of *forukhtan*) + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ketâbforushi* is built in Persian on *ketâb*, 'book', from Arabic *kitāb* (EtymDB), and *forush*, the stem of native *forukhtan*, 'to sell', with *-i* for the shop.
