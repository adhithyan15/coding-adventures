## You'll want to know: تڑپنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C446-WORRY446-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تڑپنا** — *taṛapnā* — "to writhe; to be in anguish".

> پرندہ زمین پر تڑپ رہا تھا۔
>
> *parindā zamīn par taṛap rahā thā.*
>
> The bird was writhing on the ground.

Past (he …): وہ درد سے تڑپا, *voh dard se taṛpā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تڑپنا** (*taṛapnā*) is inherited from *taḍapphaḍ*, "agitate", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5634).
