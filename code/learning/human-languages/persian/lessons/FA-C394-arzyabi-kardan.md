## You'll want to know: ارزیابی کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C394-RISK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ارزیابی کردن** (*arzyâbi kardan*) — "to assess, to evaluate".

> معلم کار هر دانش آموز را ارزیابی میکند.
>
> *mo'allem kâr-e har dânesh âmuz râ arzyâbi mikonad.*
>
> The teacher assesses each pupil's work.

*Arz* "worth" + *yâbi* "finding", from *yâftan*, + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارزیابی** is a modern native coinage built from *arz* "worth", the stem behind *arzesh* "value", + *yâbi* "finding", built on *yâb-*, the present stem of *yâftan* "to find". To assess is to find the worth.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
