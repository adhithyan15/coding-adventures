# A two-sentence card for a new classmate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-01, HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-02, HI-CONCEPT-C02-MERANAAMHAI-01] -->

You have already practised both pieces separately:

- the greeting **नमस्ते।**
- the name sentence **मेरा नाम मीरा है।**

## Writing — assemble for one reader and purpose
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-01, HI-CONCEPT-W05-WRITE-NAMASTE-02, HI-CONCEPT-C02-MERANAAMHAI-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

Your reader is a **new classmate**. Your purpose is to **greet them and give the
practice name**. Put the greeting first and the name second:

> **नमस्ते। मेरा नाम मीरा है।**

Write the two separately practised pieces once. Check two sentence stops, then
the space between the sentences. No new word is introduced here.
