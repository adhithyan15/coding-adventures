## You'll want to know: へんじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C231-PHONE231-04]; assesses=[] -->

**へんじ** — *henji* — "a response; a reply".

> **ともだちは いつも てがみの へんじを かきます。** — *tomodachi wa itsumo tegami no henji o kakimasu* — "My friend always answers letters."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へんじ** (*henji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hen*, "return" (Middle Chinese *pjonX*) and *ji*, "matter" (*dzriH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
