Russian, chapter 523: Plot and Characters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сюжет (syuzhét) — a plot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a statue, then the Russian for to inspire.

You'll want to know: сюжет.
сюжет (syuzhét) — "a plot".
У этого романа интересный сюжет. U étogo romána interésnyy syuzhét. This novel has an interesting plot.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
сюжет (syuzhét) was borrowed from French sujet 'subject', which comes from Latin subiectum (EtymDB), from subicere 'to place under'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
персонаж (personázh) — a character (in a story).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to inspire, then the Russian for a plot.

You'll want to know: персонаж.
персонаж (personázh) — "a character (in a story)".
Мой любимый персонаж — старый капитан. Moy lyubímyy personázh — stáryy kapitán. My favourite character is the old captain.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
персонаж (personázh) is usually traced to French personnage, built on personne, from Latin persōna 'a mask, a part in a play'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
героиня (geroínya) was borrowed from Greek hērōínē 'a heroine' (EtymDB), the feminine of hḗrōs 'hero'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
развязка (razvyázka) — an ending, a denouement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a character, then the Russian for a heroine.

You'll want to know: развязка.
развязка (razvyázka) — "an ending, a denouement".
Развязка фильма меня удивила. Razvyázka fílma menyá udivíla. The ending of the film surprised me.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
развязка (razvyázka) is built on развязать 'to untie', from вязать 'to tie', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic vęzati (EtymDB): the ending unties the knot of the story.

Lesson 5 of 5.
зачитываться (zachítyvatsya) — to get engrossed in reading.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a heroine, then the Russian for an ending.

You'll want to know: зачитываться.
зачитываться (zachítyvatsya) — "to get engrossed in reading".
Я часто зачитываюсь до глубокой ночи. Ya chásto zachítyvayus do glubókoy nóchi. I often get so absorbed in a book that I read late into the night.
Imperfective; the perfective is зачитаться. Takes the instrumental: зачитываться (zachítyvatsya) книгой.

The word, taken apart.
зачитываться (zachítyvatsya) is built on за- and читать 'to read', with the reflexive -ся; EtymDB has no entry.
