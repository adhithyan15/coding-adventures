Lesson 1 of 5.
आरामखुर्ची (ārāmkhurcī) — an armchair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for busy, then the Marathi for free.

You'll want to know: आरामखुर्ची.
आरामखुर्ची — ārāmkhurcī — "an armchair".

The word, taken apart.
आरामखुर्ची (ārāmkhurcī) is "rest-chair": ārām, "rest, ease", usually traced to Persian ārām, and खुर्ची, "chair", from Arabic kursī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
खाऊ (khāū) — a snack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an armchair, then the Marathi for a button.

You'll want to know: खाऊ.
खाऊ — khāū — "a snack".

The word, taken apart.
खाऊ (khāū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khāduka, "biting" (CDIAL 3871). It is built on khād, "to eat". CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "eating".
