## You'll want to know: चिमटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C460-BICHHAD460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चिमटना** — *cimaṭnā* — "to cling tightly".

> स्टेशन पर बेटी माँ से चिमट गई।
>
> *sṭeśan par beṭī māṁ se cimaṭ gaī.*
>
> At the station the daughter clung tightly to her mother.

Past (she …): वह माँ से चिमट गई, *vah māṁ se cimaṭ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिमटना** (*cimaṭnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *cimaṭnā*, "to embrace closely", under *\*cimb*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4822).
