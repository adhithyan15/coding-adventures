## You'll want to know: مفصل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C497-BONES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مفصل** (*mafsal*) — "a joint (of the body)".

> مادربزرگ در زمستان درد مفصل دارد.
>
> *mâdarbozorg dar zemestân dard-e mafsal dârad.*
>
> Grandmother has joint pain in winter.

Plural *mafâsel* (Arabic). The same letters read *mofassal* mean 'detailed'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mafsal* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *mafṣil*, 'joint'. EtymDB's entry under this spelling is the other reading, *mufaṣṣal*, 'divided' (EtymDB), which Persian keeps as *mofassal*, 'detailed'.
