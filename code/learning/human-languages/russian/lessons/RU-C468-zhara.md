## You'll want to know: жара
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C468-ZHARA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**жара** (*zhará*) — "hot weather, heat".

> В июле в городе стояла жара.
>
> *V iyúle v górode stoyála zhará.*
>
> In July the town had a spell of hot weather.

Feminine. *В жару* — in hot weather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*жара* goes with *жар* 'heat'. EtymDB takes *жара* from Proto-Slavic *žārĭ* and the PIE root *gʷʰer-* 'to heat'; the same root is in *гореть* 'to burn'.
