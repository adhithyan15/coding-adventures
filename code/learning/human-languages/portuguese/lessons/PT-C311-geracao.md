## You'll want to know: a geração
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C311-IDADES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a geração** — "the generation".

> Na minha geração, poucas pessoas tinham telemóvel.
>
> *In my generation few people had a mobile.*

*De geração em geração* — from one generation to the next.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*geração* comes down from Old Portuguese *geeraçon* (EtymDB), from Latin *generātiō* "a begetting", built on *genus* "birth, kind".
