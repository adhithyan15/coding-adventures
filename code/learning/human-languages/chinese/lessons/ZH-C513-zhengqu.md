## You'll want to know: zhēngqǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C513-AMBITION513-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhēngqǔ* — "to strive for; to fight for".

> *wǒ yào zhēngqǔ kǎoshàng míngxiào* — I'm going to do my best to get into a top school.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhēngqǔ* is built from *zhēng* 'to contend' and 取 'to take', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsreang* and *tshjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to compete' and the second 'to receive'.
