## You'll want to know: じゅうどう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C235-BALL235-04]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうどう** — *jūdō* — "judo".

> **あには きょねん じゅうどうを ならいました。** — *ani wa kyonen jūdō o naraimashita* — "My elder brother took judo lessons last year."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅうどう** (*jūdō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jū*, "soft, gentle" (Middle Chinese *nyuw*) and *dō*, "way" (*dawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
