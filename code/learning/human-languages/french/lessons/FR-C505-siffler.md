## You'll want to know: siffler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C505-TERRAIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**siffler** — "to whistle; to boo".

> Le public a sifflé l'équipe après la défaite.
>
> *The crowd booed the team after the defeat.*

In France, a crowd that whistles is booing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*siffler* is inherited from Late Latin *sīfilāre*, a form of Latin *sībilāre* "to hiss, to whistle" (Lewis & Short).
