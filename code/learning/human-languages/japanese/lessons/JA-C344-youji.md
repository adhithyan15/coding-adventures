## You'll want to know: ようじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C344-KEIKAKU344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ようじ** — *yōji* — "an errand; something to do".

> **ようじが あったら、はやく かえって ください。** — *yōji ga attara, hayaku kaette kudasai* — "If you have something to do, please go home early."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ようじ** (*yōji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yō*, "utilize" (Middle Chinese *yowngH*) and *ji*, "matter" (*dzriH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
