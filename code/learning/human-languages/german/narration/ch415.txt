German, chapter 415: Am großen Bahnhof.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Schließfach — the locker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to commute, then the German for the commuter.

You'll want to know: das Schließfach.
das Schließfach — "the locker".
Er legt seinen Rucksack für zwei Stunden in ein Schließfach. He puts his rucksack in a locker for two hours.
Plural: die Schließfächer. Also a safe-deposit box in a bank.

The word, taken apart.
Schließfach is built from schließen 'to lock' and Fach 'compartment', both inherited: Fach goes back to Old High German fah (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: die Gepäckaufbewahrung.
die Gepäckaufbewahrung — "the left-luggage office".
Die Gepäckaufbewahrung ist neben dem Ausgang. The left-luggage office is next to the exit.
No plural in everyday use. Aufbewahren — to keep, to store.

The word, taken apart.
Gepäckaufbewahrung is built from Gepäck 'luggage', from packen 'to pack', and Aufbewahrung 'keeping', from aufbewahren 'to keep safe', which is built on wahren 'to guard'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Rolltreppe — the escalator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the locker, then the German for the left-luggage office.

You'll want to know: die Rolltreppe.
die Rolltreppe — "the escalator".
Die Rolltreppe zum Bahnsteig ist kaputt. The escalator to the platform is broken.
Plural: die Rolltreppen.

The word, taken apart.
Rolltreppe is built from rollen 'to roll', which goes back through French to Latin rotula 'little wheel', and Treppe 'stairs', an inherited word that came into the standard language from Low German (EtymDB lists Rolltreppe under Treppe).

Lesson 4 of 5.
schwarzfahren — to ride without a ticket, to dodge the fare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the left-luggage office, then the German for the escalator.

You'll want to know: schwarzfahren.
schwarzfahren — "to ride without a ticket, to dodge the fare".
Ein junger Mann fährt schwarz und muss sechzig Euro zahlen. A young man rides without a ticket and has to pay sixty euros.
Separable; Perfekt with sein: er ist schwarzgefahren. Der Schwarzfahrer — the fare dodger.

The word, taken apart.
schwarzfahren is built from schwarz 'black', in its old sense 'secret, against the rules', and the inherited verb fahren (EtymDB). Dutch zwartrijden is built the same way.

Lesson 5 of 5.
anstellen — to queue (mostly reflexive: sich anstellen); to switch on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the escalator, then the German for to ride without a ticket.

You'll want to know: anstellen.
anstellen — "to queue (mostly reflexive: sich anstellen); to switch on".
Vor dem Fahrkartenautomaten stellen wir uns hinten an. At the ticket machine we join the back of the queue.
Mostly reflexive and separable: ich stelle mich an. Das Radio anstellen — to switch the radio on.

The word, taken apart.
anstellen is built from an 'at, on' and stellen 'to place, to set', inherited from Old High German stellen (EtymDB): to place oneself at the end of a line.
