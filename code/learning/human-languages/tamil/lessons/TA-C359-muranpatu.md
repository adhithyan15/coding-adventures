# முரண்படு (muraṇpaṭu) — to contradict; to clash

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C359-TIRI359-01, TA-LEX-C359-TIRI359-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to twist, then the Tamil for to exaggerate.

## You'll want to know: முரண்படு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C359-TIRI359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**முரண்படு** — *muraṇpaṭu* — "to contradict; to clash".

> அவர் சொன்னது ஆதாரத்துடன் முரண்படுகிறது.
>
> *avar coṉṉatu ātārattuṭaṉ muraṇpaṭukiṟatu.*
>
> What he said contradicts the evidence.

Past (I …): முரண்பட்டேன், *muraṇpaṭṭēṉ*.

With -உடன்: X-உடன் முரண்படு. The noun is முரண்பாடு, a contradiction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**முரண்படு** (*muraṇpaṭu*) is native: DEDR 4971 lists Tamil *muraṇ*, "to be at variance, be opposed; variance", here with *paṭu*, "to undergo".
