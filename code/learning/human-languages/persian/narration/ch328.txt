Persian, chapter 328: Fire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آتش سوزی (âtash suzi) — a fire, a blaze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to survive, then the Persian for a lifeguard.

You'll want to know: آتش سوزی.
آتش سوزی (âtash suzi) — "a fire, a blaze".
دیشب در یک انبار آتش سوزی شد. Dishab dar yek anbâr âtash suzi shod. Last night there was a fire in a warehouse.
âtash "fire" + suzi "burning", from suz-, the present stem of sukhtan "to burn".

The word, taken apart.
âtash "fire" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and suzi "burning" comes from suz-, the present stem of the native verb sukhtan "to burn".

Lesson 2 of 5.
آتش گرفتن (âtash gereftan) — to catch fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lifeguard, then the Persian for a fire.

You'll want to know: آتش گرفتن.
آتش گرفتن (âtash gereftan) — "to catch fire".
آشپزخانه ناگهان آتش گرفت. Âshpazkhâne nâgahân âtash gereft. The kitchen suddenly caught fire.
Present âtash migirad. Âtash zadan is "to set on fire".

The word, taken apart.
âtash "fire" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB); gereftan "to take" is inherited too, from the Old Persian root grab- "to seize".

Lesson 3 of 5.
شعله (sho'le) — a flame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fire, then the Persian for to catch fire.

You'll want to know: شعله.
شعله (sho'le) — "a flame".
شعله (sho'le) ها تا سقف رسید. Sho'le-hâ tâ saqf resid. The flames reached the ceiling.
Plural sho'le-hâ. On a cooker, sho'le-ye gâz is the burner flame.

The word, taken apart.
sho'le is borrowed from Arabic shu'la, "flame" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
دود (dud) — smoke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to catch fire, then the Persian for a flame.

You'll want to know: دود.
دود (dud) — "smoke".
دود (dud) سیاه از پنجره بیرون میآمد. Dud-e siyâh az panjere birun miâmad. Black smoke was coming out of the window.
Dud kardan "to smoke; to give off smoke".

The word, taken apart.
dud is a native Persian word; EtymDB glosses it "smoke; sorrow" and lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
جرقه (jaraqe) — a spark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a flame, then the Persian for smoke.

You'll want to know: جرقه.
جرقه (jaraqe) — "a spark".
از سیم برق جرقه پرید. Az sim-e barq jaraqe parid. A spark flew from the electric wire.
Also figurative: jaraqe-ye fekr "the spark of an idea".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of jaraqe "a spark" is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.
