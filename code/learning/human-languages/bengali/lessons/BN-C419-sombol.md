## You'll want to know: সম্বল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C419-ABHAB419-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্বল** — *sômbôl* — "one's means; what one has to live on".

> ওই টাকাটাই ছিল আমাদের পথের সম্বল।
>
> *oi ṭākāṭāi chhilô āmāder pôther sômbôl.*
>
> That money was all we had for the journey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্বল** (*sômbôl*) is Sanskrit *śambala*, also written *sambala*, "provisions for a journey" (Monier-Williams): পথের সম্বল keeps the old sense, and the word has grown to mean all one has to live on.
