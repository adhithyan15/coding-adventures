## You'll want to know: そろえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C330-CHANTO330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**そろえる** — *soroeru* — "to line up neatly; to put in order".

> **げんかんで くつを そろえた ほうが いいです。** — *genkan de kutsu o soroeta hō ga ii desu* — "You should line your shoes up neatly in the entrance."

Polite **そろえます**, *soroemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが くつを そろえました。**, *kodomo ga kutsu o soroemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そろえる** (*soroeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
