## You'll want to know: l'appello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C381-DOCENTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'appello** — "the exam session; the roll call; the appeal".

> Se non passo l'esame a giugno, provo all'appello di luglio.
>
> *If I do not pass the exam in June, I will try at the July session.*

At school, *fare l'appello* is to call the register.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*appello* is built on *appellare* (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *appellāre* "to address, to call upon, to call by name" (Lewis & Short).
