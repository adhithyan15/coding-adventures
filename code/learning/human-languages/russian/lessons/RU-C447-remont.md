## You'll want to know: ремонт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C447-MASTERSKAYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ремонт** (*remónt*) — "repairs, a repair; renovation".

> Ремонт туфель занял два дня.
>
> *Remónt túfel zányal dva dnya.*
>
> Repairing the shoes took two days.

Masculine. *Ремонт квартиры* — doing up a flat. The verb is *ремонтировать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ремонт* is usually traced to French *remonte*; EtymDB links it with *remonter* 'to put back up, to restock'. The word probably first named fresh horses for the army.
