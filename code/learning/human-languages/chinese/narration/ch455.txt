Mandarin Chinese, chapter 455: Forms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
xìngmíng — full name.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to show someone the way, then the Mandarin for a travel brochure.

You'll want to know: xìngmíng.
xìngmíng — "full name".
qǐng zài zhèr xiě nǐ de xìngmíng — Please put your full name here.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xìngmíng is built from xìng 'a surname' and 名 'a name', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sjengH and mjieng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.
过期 (guòqī) — to be out of date; to have expired.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for one's full name, then the Mandarin for one's citizenship.

You'll want to know: 过期.
过期 — guòqī — "to be out of date; to have expired".
wǒ de qiānzhèng guòqī le — My visa has expired.

The word, taken apart.
过期 (guòqī) is built from 过 'to pass' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwaH and gi (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a time agreed on; today's level-toned qī is irregular beside it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
shēnqǐng — to apply for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to be out of date, then the Mandarin for still valid.

You'll want to know: shēnqǐng.
shēnqǐng — "to apply for".
wǒ zuò huǒchē qù Běijīng shēnqǐng qiānzhèng — I'm taking the train to Beijing to apply for a visa.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shēnqǐng is built from shēn 'to state' and 请 'to ask', both inherited from Middle Chinese, syin and tshjengX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stretch' and 'to repeat' and the second 'to beg'.
