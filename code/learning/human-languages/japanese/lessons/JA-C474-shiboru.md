## You'll want to know: しぼる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C474-PREP474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しぼる** — *shiboru* — "to squeeze; to wring".

> **みかんを しぼって、のみました。** — *mikan o shibotte, nomimashita* — "I squeezed a mandarin and drank the juice."

Polite **しぼります**, *shiborimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが ふくを しぼりました。**, *haha ga fuku o shiborimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しぼる** (*shiboru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
