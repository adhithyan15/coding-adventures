## You'll want to know: la nostalgia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C709-SOLEDAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la nostalgia** — "nostalgia, homesickness".

> Siento nostalgia de mi pueblo.
>
> *I feel homesick for my village.*

*Nostalgia de* + place or time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nostalgia* was borrowed from New Latin *nostalgia* (EtymDB), a word a Swiss doctor coined from Greek *nóstos* "return home" and *álgos* "pain".
