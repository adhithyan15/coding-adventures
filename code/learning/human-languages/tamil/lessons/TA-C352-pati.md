## You'll want to know: பாதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C352-ERPATUTTU352-05]; assesses=[] -->

**பாதி** — *pāti* — "to affect; to harm".

> வெள்ளம் பல விவசாயிகளைப் பாதித்தது.
>
> *veḷḷam pala vivacāyikaḷaip pātittatu.*
>
> The flood affected many farmers.

Past (it …): பாதித்தது, *pātittatu*.

Who is affected takes -ஐ. Not the noun பாதி, a half: that is another word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பாதி** (*pāti*), "to affect", is a verb built on the Sanskrit root *bādh-*, "to press, oppress, harass" (Monier-Williams). Tamil writes the Sanskrit b as ப.
