German, chapter 365: Es sei denn ….
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
es sei denn — unless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for provided that, then the German for as far as.

You'll want to know: es sei denn.
es sei denn — "unless".
Wir fahren morgen ans Meer, es sei denn, es regnet. We are going to the seaside tomorrow, unless it rains.
After es sei denn the clause keeps normal order, with the verb second: es sei denn, es regnet. Sei is the subjunctive (Konjunktiv I) of sein.

The word, taken apart.
es sei denn is a set phrase built on sei, the subjunctive of sein "to be", and denn "then", of the family of dann (EtymDB): "unless it be that …".

Lesson 2 of 5.
außer wenn — except when, unless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for as far as, then the German for unless.

You'll want to know: außer wenn.
außer wenn — "except when, unless".
Ich gehe jeden Tag zu Fuß, außer wenn es schneit. I walk every day, except when it snows.
Wenn sends the verb to the end: außer wenn es schneit.

The word, taken apart.
außer wenn joins außer "except, outside", from Middle High German ūzer and Old High German ūzar (EtymDB), to wenn "if, when".

Lesson 3 of 5.
andernfalls — otherwise, if not.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for unless, then the German for except when.

You'll want to know: andernfalls.
andernfalls — "otherwise, if not".
Wir müssen jetzt gehen, andernfalls verpassen wir den Bus. We have to go now; otherwise we will miss the bus.
The verb comes right after it: andernfalls verpassen wir …. A formal sonst.

The word, taken apart.
andernfalls grew out of andern Falls "in the other case", from ander "other" and Fall "fall, case", Old High German val (EtymDB), with the adverb ending -s.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ansonsten — apart from that; otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for except when, then the German for otherwise.

You'll want to know: ansonsten.
ansonsten — "apart from that; otherwise".
Die Suppe war etwas salzig, ansonsten war das Essen sehr gut. The soup was a bit salty; apart from that, the meal was very good.
Two senses: apart from that, and otherwise, like sonst. The verb comes right after it.

The word, taken apart.
ansonsten is built from an and sonst "otherwise", inherited from Middle High German sunst (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
notfalls — if need be, if necessary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for otherwise, then the German for apart from that.

You'll want to know: notfalls.
notfalls — "if need be, if necessary".
Notfalls schlafen wir im Auto. If need be, we will sleep in the car.
The verb comes right after it: notfalls schlafen wir …. From der Notfall — the emergency.

The word, taken apart.
notfalls is built from Not "need", a cousin of English need (EtymDB), and Fall "case", Old High German val (EtymDB), with the adverb ending -s: in case of need.
