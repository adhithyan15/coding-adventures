# The old fictional date A

Stop before looking at or writing on this date card. This is a short return
to a familiar fictional example, not a real personal date or a scored A1 mock.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-A-01] -->

Look at the closed bank's A card: **੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫**. The three pieces are day
**੧੫**, January month position **੦੧**, and year **੨੦੨੫**. The slash marks
keep this form's day/month/year order visible.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-A-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep A visible. Copy only its day and first slash, then its month and second
slash, then its year. Check one box at a time against **੧੫/੦੧/੨੦੨੫**.
No new date or birthday is requested.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-DATE-A-01] -->

Cover A for a moment. Point to its January box, then try the familiar A line
once on paper. Uncover the card and repair only the first mismatched box.
This is gentle retrieval, not independent scored writing.
