## You'll want to know: బస చేయు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C457-VASATI457-05]; assesses=[] -->

**బస చేయు** — *basa cēyu* — "to stay overnight; to put up".

> ఆ రాత్రి మేము సత్రంలో బస చేశాం.
>
> *ā rātri mēmu satraṁlō basa cēśāṁ.*
>
> That night we put up at an inn.

Past (I …): బస చేశాను, *basa cēśānu*.

బస, a halt, a lodging, + చేయు. Used for a stay on a journey, away from home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బస చేయు** (*basa cēyu*) is a Telugu phrase: బస, "a halt, a lodging", whose origin is not settled, and the native చేయు, "to do" (DEDR 1957).
