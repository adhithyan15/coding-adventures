## You'll want to know: ਕੋਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C265-SIDE265-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਹ** — *koh* — "a kos, about two miles".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿੰਡ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਤੋਂ ਦੋ ਕੋਹ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā piṇḍ shahir ton do koh dūr hai.*
>
> My village is two kos from the town.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਹ** (*koh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *krośa*, "a shout; a measure of distance, as far as a voice carries"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *koh*, "a distance of about two miles", under it (CDIAL 3611).
