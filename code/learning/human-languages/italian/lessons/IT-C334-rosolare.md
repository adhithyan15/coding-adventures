## You'll want to know: rosolare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C334-SOFFRITTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**rosolare** — "to brown".

> Rosola la carne da tutti e due i lati.
>
> *Brown the meat on both sides.*

Until it turns a golden colour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *rosolare* is not settled; it is usually linked with *rosso* "red", from Latin *russus* (Lewis & Short).
