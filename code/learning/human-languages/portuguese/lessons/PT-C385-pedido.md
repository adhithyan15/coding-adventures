## You'll want to know: o pedido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C385-BUROCRACIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o pedido** — "the request, the application; the order (in a restaurant)".

> Fiz um pedido à câmara para abrir uma esplanada.
>
> *I made an application to the council to open a terrace café.*

*Fazer um pedido* — to make a request; *a pedido de* — at the request of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pedido* is the participle of *pedir* used as a noun, from Latin *petītus* "sought" (EtymDB); *pedir* is inherited from Latin *petere* "to seek, to ask" (Lewis & Short).
