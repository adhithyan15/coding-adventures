## You'll want to know: ਪੇੜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C238-KAM238-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੇੜਾ** — *peṛā* — "a ball of dough".

> ਮੈਂ ਆਟੇ ਦਾ ਪੇੜਾ ਬਣਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ āṭe dā peṛā baṇāundī hāṁ.*
>
> I make a ball of dough.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੇੜਾ** (*peṛā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*pēḍa*, "lump"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *peṛā*, "lump" (CDIAL 8377).
