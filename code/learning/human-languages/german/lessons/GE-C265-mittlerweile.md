## You'll want to know: mittlerweile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C265-INZWISCHEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**mittlerweile** — "by now, in the meanwhile".

> Mittlerweile wohnt sie in einer größeren Wohnung.
>
> *By now she lives in a bigger flat.*

Close to *inzwischen*, and a little more conversational.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mittlerweile* is built on *mittler* "middle" and *Weile* "a while", both inherited Germanic words; *Weile* is a cousin of English *while*. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
