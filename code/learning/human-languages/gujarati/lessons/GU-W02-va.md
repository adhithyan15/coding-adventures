# વ — rounded body, separate spine, va

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **દ** once from memory. Then look at the new model only.

## Script — meet the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-VA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **વ** visible. It is **va**. Begin at the upper right, circle
counterclockwise around the broad rounded left body, and return into the right
shoulder. Lift once. Descend the tall right spine and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace the rounded body, lift, then trace the spine. Air-trace the same two runs.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **વ** once. Check body, shoulder, spine; do not add a third run.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W02-va-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-VA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model. Write the Gujarati consonant va from memory.","answer":"વ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"વ — rounded body, then the separate right spine.","incorrect":"Look once at વ, hide it, and rebuild body then spine."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide **વ**. Write *va* once, uncover the model, and make at most one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Where is the lift? (**Between the rounded body and right spine.**)
