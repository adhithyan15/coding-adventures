## You'll want to know: clēmēns, clēmentis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C505-CLEM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**clēmēns, clēmentis** — "merciful, mild".

> Rēx clēmēns captīvīs ignōscit.
>
> *The merciful king pardons the prisoners.*

One ending; ablative usually *clēmentī*. Of a ruler or a judge: gentle in punishing. English *clement*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**clēmēns** is a native Latin adjective; Lewis & Short calls its origin doubtful, so it is not settled. English *clement* and *inclement* come from it.
