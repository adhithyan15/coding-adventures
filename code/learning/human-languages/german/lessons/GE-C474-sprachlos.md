## You'll want to know: sprachlos
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C474-STAUNEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sprachlos** — "speechless".

> Über das teure Geschenk war sie sprachlos.
>
> *She was speechless at the expensive present.*

*Ich bin sprachlos!* — I'm lost for words!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sprachlos* is built on *Sprache* "speech", inherited from Old High German *sprāhha*, from Proto-Germanic *sprēkijō* (EtymDB), with *-los* "without".
