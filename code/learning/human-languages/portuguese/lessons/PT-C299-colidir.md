## You'll want to know: colidir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C299-ACIDENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**colidir** — "to collide".

> Dois camiões colidiram na ponte.
>
> *Two lorries collided on the bridge.*

Formal, a news word; in speech, *bater* or *chocar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*colidir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *collīdere* "to strike together", built on *laedere* "to strike, to hurt" (Lewis & Short).
