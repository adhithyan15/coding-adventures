## You'll want to know

> *ghaṇo* — **very**

Two beats. The first carries the breathed *gh* of *ghar*; the second carries the
same retroflex *ṇ* as *kitṇo* and *pāṇī*.

The source prints it in front of a description, where English puts *very*: the
food is *ghaṇo* tasty. Put it in front of *mahaṅgo* and you have said more than
that a thing costs money: you have said it costs too much, which is the whole
reason a bargain begins.
