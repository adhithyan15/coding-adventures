# சுபாவம் (cupāvam) — temperament; disposition

## You'll want to know: சுபாவம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C471-KUNAM471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**சுபாவம்** — *cupāvam* — "temperament; disposition".

> என் அண்ணனுக்கு அமைதியான சுபாவம்.
>
> *eṉ aṇṇaṉukku amaitiyāṉa cupāvam.*
>
> My elder brother has a calm temperament.

Dative: அவனுக்கு அமைதியான சுபாவம், he is calm by nature.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சுபாவம்** (*cupāvam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *svabhāva*, "one's own nature" (Monier-Williams); Tamil drops the *v* of *sv*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
