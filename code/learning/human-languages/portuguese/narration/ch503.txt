Portuguese, chapter 503: Jogos de mesa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o xadrez — chess; check pattern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for fish hook, then the Portuguese for to hunt.

You'll want to know: o xadrez.
o xadrez — "chess; check pattern".
O meu avô ensinou-me a jogar xadrez. My grandfather taught me to play chess.
Also a check pattern: uma camisa de xadrez.

The word, taken apart.
Like its Spanish and Mirandese cousins, xadrez came through Andalusian Arabic from Arabic šaṭranj "chess", which goes back to Persian (EtymDB); the Persian word is usually traced to Sanskrit caturaṅga "four-limbed", the name of an army.

Lesson 3 of 5.
o tabuleiro — (game) board; tray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for chess, then the Portuguese for pack of cards.

You'll want to know: o tabuleiro.
o tabuleiro — "(game) board; tray".
As peças do xadrez estão no tabuleiro. The chess pieces are on the board.
Also a tray: um tabuleiro de bolos.

The word, taken apart.
tabuleiro is inherited, through Old Portuguese tavoleiro "gameboard" (EtymDB), from a form built on Latin tabula "a board, a plank".

Lesson 5 of 5.
as palavras cruzadas — crossword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for (game) board, then the Portuguese for jigsaw puzzle.

You'll want to know: as palavras cruzadas.
as palavras cruzadas — "crossword".
O meu pai faz as palavras cruzadas do jornal todos os dias. My father does the newspaper's crossword every day.
Literally "crossed words". Plural in Portuguese.

The word, taken apart.
palavra is inherited from Latin parabola "a comparison, a word", and cruzada is built on cruz "cross", inherited from Latin crux (Lewis & Short).
