## You'll want to know: पेठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C419-VASTI419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पेठ** — *peṭh* — "an old town quarter".

> पुण्यात अनेक जुन्या पेठा आहेत.
>
> *puṇyāt anek junyā peṭhā āhet.*
>
> Pune has many old quarters.

Feminine. Plural: पेठा, *peṭhā*.

In Pune each old quarter is a पेठ; the plural is पेठा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेठ** (*peṭh*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *peṭh*, "town, quarter of a town, market", under Sanskrit *pratiṣṭhā*, "foundation, homestead" (CDIAL 8608).
