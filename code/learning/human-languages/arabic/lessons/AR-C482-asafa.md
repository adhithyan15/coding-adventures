# عصف (ʿaṣafa) — to blow hard (of wind)

## You'll want to know: عصف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C482-RIH482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عصف** — *ʿaṣafa* — "to blow hard (of wind)".

> عصفت الريح في الليل.
>
> *ʿaṣafat ar-rīḥ fī al-layl.*
>
> The wind blew hard in the night.

Present: يعصف, *yaʿṣif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عصف** (*ʿaṣafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṣ-f*, "to blow violently (of wind)" (Lane). It is the plain verb; *ʿāṣifa*, "a storm", is its participle.
