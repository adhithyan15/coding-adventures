## You'll want to know: huáxuě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C221-SNOW221-02]; assesses=[] -->

*huáxuě* — "to ski".

> *nǐ huì huáxuě ma* — Can you ski?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huáxuě* is built from *huá* 'slippery' and 雪 'snow', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hweat* and *sjwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary's gloss for the first is 'keen' or 'smooth', not 'slippery', so how it came to that sense is not settled.
