Kannada, chapter 147: To drink, To run, To sleep, To cut, To break.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕುಡಿ (kuḍi) — to drink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for golden, then the Kannada for silver.

You'll want to know: ಕುಡಿ.
ಕುಡಿ — kuḍi — "to drink".

The word, taken apart.
ಕುಡಿ (kuḍi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kuṭi and Malayalam kuṭi (DEDR 1654). DEDR lists kuḍi itself, "to drink". DEDR 1654 compares Sanskrit kuṭī.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಕತ್ತರಿಸು.
ಕತ್ತರಿಸು — kattarisu — "to cut".

The word, taken apart.
ಕತ್ತರಿಸು (kattarisu) is kattari, "scissors", from Sanskrit kartarī (CDIAL 2858), made into a verb with -isu: "to scissor", to cut.
