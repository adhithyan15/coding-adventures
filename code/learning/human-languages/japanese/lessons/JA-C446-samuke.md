## You'll want to know: さむけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C446-CHILL446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さむけ** — *samuke* — "a chill; the shivers".

> **ねつが あって、さむけが します。** — *netsu ga atte, samuke ga shimasu* — "I have a fever and the shivers."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さむけ** (*samuke*) is a mixed word: native *samu*, "cold", the stem of *samui*, joined to Sino-Japanese *ke*, "spirit, feeling" (Middle Chinese *khj+jH*), a reading borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
