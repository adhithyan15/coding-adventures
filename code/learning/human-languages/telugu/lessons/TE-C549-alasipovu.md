# అలసిపోవు (alasipōvu) — to get tired; to wear out

## You'll want to know: అలసిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C549-GRHAM549-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అలసిపోవు** — *alasipōvu* — "to get tired; to wear out".

> ఇల్లు సర్దిన తరువాత మేము బాగా అలసిపోయాము.
>
> *illu sardina taruvāta mēmu bāgā alasipōyāmu.*
>
> After tidying the house we were very tired.

Past (I …): అలసిపోయాను, *alasipōyānu*.

Its noun అలసట is tiredness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అలసిపోవు** (*alasipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 236 lists Telugu *alayu*, "to be tired", and *alasaṭa*, "fatigue"; the verb ends in *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
