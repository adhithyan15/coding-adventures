## You'll want to know: naviguer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C398-RESEAU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**naviguer** — "to browse; to sail".

> Il navigue sur internet pendant des heures.
>
> *He browses the internet for hours.*

First of ships: *naviguer sur la mer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naviguer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nāvigāre* "to sail" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *nāvis* "ship".
