# à — one word, three goodbyes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-APLUSTARD-03, FR-LEX-ABIENTOT-04, FR-LEX-ADEMAIN-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Three soft goodbyes, from memory. Say all three, then say the one
word every one of them begins with.

## You'll want to know: the soft goodbyes are all à plus a time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-A-FAREWELLS-06, FR-LEX-APLUSTARD-03, FR-LEX-ABIENTOT-04, FR-LEX-ADEMAIN-05, FR-LEX-BONNE-NUIT-11] -->

| | |
|---|---|
| **à plus tard** | to more late — later, the default |
| **à bientôt** | to well-soon — soon |
| **à demain** | to tomorrow |

One preposition and a word for *when*. Choosing between them is choosing a
distance in time, and nothing else about the sentence moves.

**bonne nuit** is not in that family and is worth keeping apart: it is for
someone going to **bed**, not for someone leaving a room. Said on the way out of
an afternoon meeting it lands oddly.

## Grammar Lens: tu and vous, and where the choice actually shows
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-REGISTER-09] -->

*tu* is the familiar you, for a friend or a peer; *vous* is the formal or plural
one, for a stranger, an elder, a group. The climb is visible in the question:
**Comment vas-tu ?** against **Comment allez-vous ?**

The verb changes with the pronoun, so the choice is not a single word you can
swap late — it is decided before the sentence starts.

## What you've built — thanks, and the shrug
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-CULTURE-MERCI-03, FR-LEX-DERIEN-04, FR-CULTURE-SHRUG-12, FR-LEX-COMMECI-11] -->

**merci** and its reply **de rien**, "of nothing" — the same modesty Spanish
reaches for with *de nada*.

And the shrug: **comme ci, comme ça**, "like this, like that". Every language
paints that gesture differently — Spanish weighs it (*más o menos*, more or
less), Italian doubles a word (*così così*), French points twice. One gesture,
three pictures.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-BONNE-NUIT-11, FR-CULTURE-MERCI-03, FR-LEX-DERIEN-04, FR-GRAM-REGISTER-09, FR-CULTURE-SHRUG-12, FR-LEX-COMMECI-11, FR-LEX-APLUSTARD-03, FR-LEX-ABIENTOT-04, FR-GRAM-A-FAREWELLS-06, FR-LEX-ADEMAIN-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What do the three soft goodbyes share? (**à**, plus a word for when.) Who is **bonne nuit** for? (**Someone going to bed.**) Where does the tu-vous choice show itself? (**In the verb** — *vas-tu* against *allez-vous*.) And what does **de rien** say literally? (**Of nothing.**)
