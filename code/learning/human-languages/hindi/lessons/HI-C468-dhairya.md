## You'll want to know: धैर्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C468-DHAIRYA468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धैर्य** — *dhairya* — "forbearance; patience".

> बच्चों को पढ़ाने के लिए बहुत धैर्य चाहिए।
>
> *baccoṁ ko paṛhāne ke liye bahut dhairya cāhiye.*
>
> Teaching children takes a great deal of patience.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धैर्य** (*dhairya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *dhairya*, "firmness, constancy, calmness, patience" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal sister of *dhīraj*.
