# कृष्णपक्षः (kṛṣṇapakṣaḥ) — the dark fortnight

## You'll want to know: कृष्णपक्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C444-CANDRA444-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कृष्णपक्षः** — *kṛṣṇapakṣaḥ* — "the dark fortnight".

> कृष्णपक्षे रात्रौ अन्धकारः भवति।
>
> *kṛṣṇapakṣe rātrau andhakāraḥ bhavati.*
>
> In the dark fortnight there is darkness at night.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कृष्णपक्षः** (*kṛṣṇapakṣaḥ*) is built from *kṛṣṇa*, "dark, black", and *pakṣa*, "a wing, a side, a half-month"; MW gives the compound as the dark half of the month, when the moon wanes (MW).
