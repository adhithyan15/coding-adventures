## You'll want to know: mentiroso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C724-MENTIRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mentiroso** — "lying; the liar".

> Eres un mentiroso.
>
> *You are a liar.*

Feminine *mentirosa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mentiroso* is built on *mentira* "lie" with the ending *-oso*; *mentir* comes from Latin *mentīrī* (EtymDB).
