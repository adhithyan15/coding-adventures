You'll want to know: tirer.

The word, taken apart.
tirer from Old French tirer, "to draw out" (EtymDB 2.1), of uncertain origin; EtymDB links it to Gothic tairan, "to tear", one of several proposals.

The word, taken apart.
gaspiller — from a dialect word for scattering chaff (EtymDB 2.1 has Old French gaspail, "chaff of wheat"); its deeper origin is uncertain.

The word, taken apart.
dépasser — built from dé-, "beyond", and the verb for "to pass", from Vulgar Latin passare, from Latin passus, "a step" (EtymDB 2.1).
