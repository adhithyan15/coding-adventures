## You'll want to know: ਖਿਡਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C353-PLAY353-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਿਡਾਉਣਾ** — *khiḍāuṇā* — "to play with a child; to keep a child amused".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਬੱਚੇ ਨੂੰ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਖਿਡਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ bachche nū̃ vihṛe vich khiḍāundī hai.*
>
> My sister plays with the baby in the courtyard.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚੇ ਨੂੰ ਖਿਡਾਇਆ, *maiṁ bachche nū̃ khiḍāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਿਡਾਉਣਾ** (*khiḍāuṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khēḍ*, "play"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khiḍāuṇā*, "play", under it (CDIAL 3918).
