## You'll want to know: противоречить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C341-KOMPROMISS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**противоречить** (*protivoréchit*) — "to contradict".

> Ты сам себе противоречишь.
>
> *Ty sam sebé protivoréchish.*
>
> You are contradicting yourself.

Imperfective only. It takes the dative: *противоречить фактам*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**противоречить** is built from *противо-* "against" and *речь* "speech", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*rěčĭ*, from *\*rekti* "to say" (EtymDB). To contradict is to speak against.
