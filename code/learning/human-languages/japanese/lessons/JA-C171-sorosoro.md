## You'll want to know: そろそろ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C171-ORDER171-05]; assesses=[] -->

**そろそろ** — *sorosoro* — "soon; any time now".

> **そろそろ かえります。** — *sorosoro kaerimasu* — "I will be going home soon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そろそろ** (*sorosoro*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
