Arabic, chapter 176: Then, While, More, Less, Difference.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ثم (thumma) — then, next.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for despite, then the Arabic for also.

You'll want to know: ثم.
ثم — thumma — "then, next".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: thumma]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: thumma, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for despite, then the Arabic for also, then say thumma again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ثم (thumma) mean? ("Then, next".) Say it once more.

(لياقة, liyāqa.) An injury? (إصابة, iṣāba.) A pill? (حبة, ḥabba.) Early? (مبكرا, mubakkiran.) Daily? (يوميا, yawmiyyan.) A delay? (تأخير, taʾkhīr.) Late? (متأخرا, mutaʾakhkhiran.) Never? (أبدا, abadan.) The future? (مستقبل, mustaqbal.) A period? (مدة, mudda.) A beginning? (بداية, bidāya.) Rarely? (نادرا, nādiran.) Often? (كثيرا, kathīran.)
