Kannada, chapter 481: Along the Coast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕರಾವಳಿ (karāvaḷi) — a coastline; a coast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a plateau, then the Kannada for to wear away.

You'll want to know: ಕರಾವಳಿ.
ಕರಾವಳಿ — karāvaḷi — "a coastline; a coast".
ಕರಾವಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮೀನುಗಾರರ ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳು ಇವೆ. karāvaḷiyalli mīnugārara haḷḷigaḷu ive. There are fishing villages along the coast.
ಕರಾವಳಿ (karāvaḷi) ಪ್ರದೇಶ, the coastal region.

The word, taken apart.
ಕರಾವಳಿ (karāvaḷi) is a Kannada word for a coastline whose origin is not settled.

ಕೊಲ್ಲಿ — kolli — "a bay; a gulf".
ಕೊಲ್ಲಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅಲೆಗಳು ಶಾಂತವಾಗಿರುತ್ತವೆ. kolliyalli alegaḷu śāntavāgiruttave. The waves are calm in the bay.
Water held in a curve of the coast.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಲ್ಲಿ (kolli) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2137 lists Kannada kolli, kolle, "a bend, a corner, a gulf, a bay".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹವಳ (havaḷa) — coral.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a coastline, then the Kannada for a bay.

You'll want to know: ಹವಳ.
ಹವಳ — havaḷa — "coral".
ಸಮುದ್ರದ ತಳದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಣ್ಣಬಣ್ಣದ ಹವಳ ಇದೆ. samudrada taḷadalli baṇṇabaṇṇada havaḷa ide. There is colourful coral on the sea bed.
ಹವಳದ ಸರ, a coral necklace.

The word, taken apart.
ಹವಳ (havaḷa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3998 lists Kannada pavaḷa, "coral", with the old p- that modern Kannada turns into h-. Sanskrit pravāla, "coral", was itself taken from Dravidian, as Turner notes (CDIAL 8794).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಚಿಪ್ಪು (cippu) — a shell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bay, then the Kannada for coral.

You'll want to know: ಚಿಪ್ಪು.
ಚಿಪ್ಪು — cippu — "a shell".
ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಕಡಲತೀರದಲ್ಲಿ ಚಿಪ್ಪುಗಳನ್ನು ಸಂಗ್ರಹಿಸಿದರು. makkaḷu kaḍalatīradalli cippugaḷannu saṅgrahisidaru. The children collected shells on the beach.
Plural: ಚಿಪ್ಪುಗಳು, cippugaḷu.
Also the shell of a coconut: ತೆಂಗಿನ ಚಿಪ್ಪು (cippu).

The word, taken apart.
ಚಿಪ್ಪು (cippu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2535 lists Kannada cippu, sippu, "an oyster shell, a mussel, a piece of coconut shell".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನದೀಮುಖ (nadīmukha) — an estuary; a river mouth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for coral, then the Kannada for a shell.

You'll want to know: ನದೀಮುಖ.
ನದೀಮುಖ — nadīmukha — "an estuary; a river mouth".
ನದೀಮುಖದಲ್ಲಿ ನದಿ ಸಮುದ್ರವನ್ನು ಸೇರುತ್ತದೆ. nadīmukhadalli nadi samudravannu sēruttade. At the estuary the river joins the sea.
ನದಿ, a river, plus ಮುಖ, a mouth.

The word, taken apart.
ನದೀಮುಖ (nadīmukha) is built from two Sanskrit words: nadī, "a river" (CDIAL 6943), and mukha, "a mouth, an entrance" (CDIAL 10158).
