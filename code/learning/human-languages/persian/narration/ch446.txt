Persian, chapter 446: Directions and distances.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جهت (jahat) — a direction; a side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fork, then the Persian for to cross.

You'll want to know: جهت.
جهت (jahat) — "a direction; a side".
ما در جهت اشتباه رانندگی میکردیم. Mâ dar jahat-e eshtebâh rânandegi mikardim. We were driving in the wrong direction.
Arabic plural jahât. Be in jahat — for this reason.

The word, taken apart.
jahat was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic jiha 'side, direction'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
قطب نما (qotbnamâ) — a compass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to cross, then the Persian for a direction.

You'll want to know: قطب نما.
قطب نما (qotbnamâ) — "a compass".
در کوه با قطب نما راه را پیدا کردیم. Dar kuh bâ qotbnamâ râh râ peydâ kardim. In the mountains we found the way with a compass.
Literally 'pole-shower': qotb 'pole' + namâ 'showing'.

The word, taken apart.
qotbnamâ is built from qotb 'pole', borrowed from Arabic quṭb (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), and native namâ 'showing', the stem of nemudan 'to show'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
کیلومتر (kilometr) — a kilometre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a direction, then the Persian for a compass.

You'll want to know: کیلومتر.
کیلومتر (kilometr) — "a kilometre".
تا شهر بعدی صد کیلومتر راه است. Tâ shahr-e ba'di sad kilometr râh ast. It is a hundred kilometres to the next town.
Speed is kilometr dar sâ'at.

The word, taken apart.
kilometr was borrowed from French kilomètre (EtymDB), which was built from Greek khílioi 'a thousand' and métron 'measure'.

مسافت (masâfat) — "a distance (to be travelled)".
مسافت (masâfat) خانه تا مدرسه زیاد نیست. Masâfat-e khâne tâ madrese ziyâd nist. The distance from home to school isn't great.
Close to fâsele, but used for a journey's length.

Lesson 5 of 5.
دور شدن (dur shodan) — to move away, to get further away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a kilometre, then the Persian for a distance.

You'll want to know: دور شدن.
دور شدن (dur shodan) — "to move away, to get further away".
قایق کم کم از ساحل دور شد. Qâyeq kam-kam az sâhel dur shod. The boat slowly moved away from the shore.
dur 'far' + shodan. The opposite is nazdik shodan.

The word, taken apart.
dur shodan is built from native dur 'far', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian duHráh 'far' and PIE duh₂-ró-s 'long', and the native verb shodan 'to become'.
