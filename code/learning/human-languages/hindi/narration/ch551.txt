Hindi, chapter 551: Free to Choose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मंज़ूर (manzūr) — approved; accepted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for favourite, then the Hindi for a selection.

You'll want to know: मंज़ूर.
मंज़ूर — manzūr — "approved; accepted".
मालिक को मेरी छुट्टी मंज़ूर है। Mālik ko merī chuṭṭī manzūr hai. The owner has approved my leave.

The word, taken apart.
मंज़ूर (manzūr) is usually traced to Arabic manẓūr, "seen, looked upon", through Persian. Persian and Hindi use it for something approved.

अड़ियल (aṛiyal) is built on aṛnā, "to come to a stop, to dig one's heels in", which CDIAL files under aḍ, "obstruct, stop" (CDIAL 187), with the ending -iyal for someone given to it.
