## You'll want to know: ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C347-GOSHTHI347-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸು** — *ullēkhisu* — "to mention; to refer to".

> ಅವರು ಮಾತಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಿನ್ನೆಯ ಘಟನೆಯನ್ನು ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *avaru mātinalli ninneya ghaṭaneyannu ullēkhisidaru.*
>
> In the talk he mentioned yesterday's incident.

Past (I …): ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸಿದೆ, *ullēkhiside*.

What is mentioned takes -ಅನ್ನು. A formal word; in talk people say ಹೇಳು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸು** (*ullēkhisu*) is built on Sanskrit *ullekha*, "causing to come forth clearly" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
