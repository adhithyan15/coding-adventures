## You'll want to know: cavarsela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C300-FERITI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cavarsela** — "to come through all right, to get by".

> È stato un brutto incidente, ma ce la siamo cavata.
>
> *It was a bad accident, but we came through it.*

An idiom: *me la cavo*, *se l'è cavata*. Also ‘to manage’: *me la cavo con l'inglese*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cavarsela* is built on *cavare* "to dig out, to pull out", which is inherited from Latin *cavāre* "to hollow out" (Lewis & Short): to pull oneself out of trouble.
