## You'll want to know: guérir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C485-GUERIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**guérir** — "to heal, to cure; to get better".

> Ma grand-mère a guéri de sa grippe en une semaine.
>
> *My grandmother got over her flu in a week.*

*Guérir de* — to recover from. A regular *-ir* verb: *nous guérissons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guérir* comes from Frankish *warjan* "to protect, to defend" (EtymDB), a Germanic word; the sense moved from "protect" to "heal".
