## You'll want to know: व्यायामभूमिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C526-ABHYASYATI526-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यायामभूमिः** — *vyāyāmabhūmiḥ* — "a gymnasium; an exercise ground".

> अरुणः प्रतिदिनं व्यायामभूमिं गच्छति।
>
> *aruṇaḥ pratidinaṃ vyāyāmabhūmiṃ gacchati.*
>
> Arun goes to the exercise ground every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यायामभूमिः** (*vyāyāmabhūmiḥ*) is built from *vyāyāma*, "exercise", and *bhūmi*, "ground"; MW gives *vyāyāmabhūmi* as an exercising ground, a gymnasium (MW).
