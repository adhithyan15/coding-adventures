## You'll want to know: provenir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C349-INFLUENCE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**provenir** — "to come from, to originate in".

> Ce bruit provient de la cuisine.
>
> *This noise is coming from the kitchen.*

Conjugated like *venir*, with *être*. *En provenance de* — arriving from.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*provenir* comes from Latin *prōvenīre* "to come forth, to arise" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *venīre* "to come".
