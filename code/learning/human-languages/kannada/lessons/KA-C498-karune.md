## You'll want to know: ಕರುಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C498-NAMBIKE498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರುಣೆ** — *karuṇe* — "compassion; mercy".

> ಬಡವರ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಳಿಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಕರುಣೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *baḍavara mēle avaḷige tumbā karuṇe ide.*
>
> She has great compassion for the poor.

Close to ದಯೆ, kindness; ಕರುಣೆ is pity that moves you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕರುಣೆ** (*karuṇe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *karuṇā*, "pity" (CDIAL 2811); the final -ā became -ೆ, as in many Kannada loans.
