## You'll want to know: मोहयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C335-MAYA335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहयति** — *mohayati* — "bewilders; deludes".

> राक्षसः मायया जनान् मोहयति।
>
> *rākṣasaḥ māyayā janān mohayati.*
>
> The demon bewilders people with illusion.

Past (he …): सः जनान् मोहितवान्, *saḥ janān mohitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहयति** (*mohayati*) comes from the root √muh, "to be confused, go astray", in its causative form: to bewilder, delude (MW).
