## You'll want to know: かんぱ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C354-TENKI354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かんぱ** — *kanpa* — "a cold wave; a cold snap".

> **かんぱが きたら、すいどうが こおるでしょう。** — *kanpa ga kitara, suidō ga kōru deshō* — "If a cold snap comes, the water pipes will probably freeze."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんぱ** (*kanpa*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "cold" (Middle Chinese *han*) and *pa*, "waves" (*pa*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
