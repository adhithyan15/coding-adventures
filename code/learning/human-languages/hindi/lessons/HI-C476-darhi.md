## You'll want to know: दाढ़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C476-CHEHRA476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दाढ़ी** — *dāṛhī* — "a beard".

> चाचा की दाढ़ी सफ़ेद हो गई है।
>
> *cācā kī dāṛhī safed ho gaī hai.*
>
> My uncle's beard has gone white.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाढ़ी** (*dāṛhī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *dāṛhī*, "beard", under *\*dāṁṣṭra*, a form it reconstructs beside Sanskrit *daṁṣṭrā*, "tusk, fang" (CDIAL 6250): the hair of the jaw.
