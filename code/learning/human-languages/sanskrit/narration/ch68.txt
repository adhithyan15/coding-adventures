Sanskrit, chapter 68: How It Feels.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दुःखम् (duḥkham) — sorrow, pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rest, then the Sanskrit for ease.

You'll want to know: दुःखम्.
दुःखम् (duḥkham) — "sorrow", the opposite of सुखम्. The two words share their ending.

What you've built.
The other side of ease.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: duḥkham]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: duḥkham, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say duḥkham]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for rest, then the Sanskrit for ease, then say duḥkham again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does दुःखम् (duḥkham) mean? ("Sorrow, pain".) Say it once more.
