## You'll want to know: लाइसेंस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C406-TAIKSI406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लाइसेंस** — *lāisens* — "a licence".

> ड्राइवर के पास लाइसेंस नहीं था।
>
> *ḍrāivar ke pās lāisens nahīṃ thā.*
>
> The driver did not have a licence.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाइसेंस** (*lāisens*) is borrowed from English *licence*, a driving licence above all.
