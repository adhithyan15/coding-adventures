# అనుకోకుండా (anukōkuṇḍā) — unexpectedly

## You'll want to know: అనుకోకుండా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C340-ANUKOKUNDA340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అనుకోకుండా** — *anukōkuṇḍā* — "unexpectedly".

> బజారులో అనుకోకుండా మా మాస్టారు కనిపించారు.
>
> *bajārulō anukōkuṇḍā mā māsṭāru kanipiñcāru.*
>
> I unexpectedly ran into our teacher in the market.

The negative of అనుకో, to think: without thinking of it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అనుకోకుండా** (*anukōkuṇḍā*) is native Telugu: *anukonu*, "to think" (DEDR 868), with *-kuṇḍā*, "without -ing": without having thought of it.
