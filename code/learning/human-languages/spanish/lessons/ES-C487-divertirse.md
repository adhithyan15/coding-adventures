## You'll want to know: divertirse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C487-ANIMO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**divertirse** — "to have fun, to enjoy yourself".

> Nos divertimos mucho en la fiesta.
>
> *We had a lot of fun at the party.*

The e breaks into ie under stress: *me divierto*.
