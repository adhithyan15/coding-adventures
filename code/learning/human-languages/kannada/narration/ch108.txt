Lesson 6 of 6.
ಶಾಲೆಗೆ (śālege) — to school, not from school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the Kannada for “I”, “go”, “school”, “in front”, “in the middle” and “road”. Now say the answer you already know: “I have come from Bengaluru.” One direction is already yours.

You'll want to know: a destination.
Listen first, then say this one short sentence:
ನಾನು ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ. nānu śālege hōguttēne. — “I go to school.”
You know ಶಾಲೆ (śāle, school) and ಹೋಗು (hōgu, go). In this fixed sentence, ಶಾಲೆಗೆ (śālege) means “to school”: -ಗೆ points to the destination. Keep ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ as the whole “I go” form here. This is one place-and-verb frame, not a promise that you can attach -ಗೆ to every noun or conjugate every verb.

Grammar lens: to and from.
ನಾನು ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ. (nānu śālege hōguttēne) — I go to school. ನಾನು ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿನಿಂದ ಬಂದಿದ್ದೇನೆ. — I have come from Bengaluru.
The destination ends in -ಗೆ; the starting place ends in -ಇಂದ. You already learned the whole Bengaluru answer. Listen for the difference, then say each sentence once. Do not swap “to” and “from”.

Guided practice.
[pause 3 seconds]
[your turn — say: “I go to school” in Kannada, without looking.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “I have come from Bengaluru” in Kannada, without looking.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — write: ಶಾಲೆಗೆ from memory, then check the printed word.]

Wrap-up recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which ending points towards a place? (-ಗೆ, as in ಶಾಲೆಗೆ.) Which marks the place one came from? (-ಇಂದ, as in ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿನಿಂದ.)
