## You'll want to know: schwindlig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C480-UEBELKEIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**schwindlig** — "dizzy".

> Wenn ich zu schnell aufstehe, wird mir schwindlig.
>
> *When I stand up too fast, I get dizzy.*

With a dative: *Mir ist schwindlig.* Also spelt *schwindelig*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schwindlig* is built on *Schwindel* "giddiness" with *-ig*; *Schwindel* comes from *schwindeln*, inherited from Middle High German *swindeln* and Old High German *swintilōn* (EtymDB).
