## You'll want to know: zhěntou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C181-BED181-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhěntou* — "pillow".

> *zhège zhěntou tài gāo le* — This pillow is too high.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhěntou* is built from *zhěn* 'pillow' and *tóu* 'head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyimX* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); under its falling-tone reading the dictionary glosses the first with this very word. In the word the second loses its tone.
