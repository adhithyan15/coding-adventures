## You'll want to know: xíngchéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C428-MOVEON428-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xíngchéng* — "itinerary; plan for a trip".

> *wǒ zhǎo bu dào wǒmen de xíngchéng le* — I can't find our itinerary.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xíngchéng* is built from *xíng* 'to go' and *chéng* 'a stage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeng* and *drjeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
