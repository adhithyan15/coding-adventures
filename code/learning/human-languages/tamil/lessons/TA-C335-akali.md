## You'll want to know: அகழி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C335-KOTTAI335-02]; assesses=[] -->

**அகழி** — *akaḻi* — "a moat".

> கோட்டையைச் சுற்றி ஆழமான அகழி இருந்தது.
>
> *kōṭṭaiyaic cuṟṟi āḻamāṉa akaḻi iruntatu.*
>
> There was a deep moat around the fort.

From அகழ், to dig.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அகழி** (*akaḻi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 11 gives it under அகழ், "to excavate, dig out".
