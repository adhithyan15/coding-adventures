## You'll want to know: o arranhão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C485-ARRANHAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o arranhão** — "scratch (the mark)".

> Tenho um arranhão no braço.
>
> *I have a scratch on my arm.*

Built on *arranhar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arranhão* is built on *arranhar* "to scratch" with *-ão*; the origin of the verb is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
