French, chapter 208: To applaud, To cross, To increase, To decrease, To warn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
applaudir — to applaud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to marry, then the French for to divorce.

You'll want to know: applaudir.
applaudir — "to applaud".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: applaudir]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: applaudir, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say applaudir]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the French for to marry, then the French for to divorce, then say applaudir again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does applaudir mean? ("To applaud".) Say it once more.
