## You'll want to know: chronométrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C508-COURSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**chronométrer** — "to time (with a stopwatch)".

> L'entraîneur chronomètre chaque coureur.
>
> *The coach times each runner.*

*Un chronomètre*, informally *un chrono* — a stopwatch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chronométrer* is built on *chronomètre* "stopwatch", a learned word made from Greek *khrónos* "time" and *métron* "measure".
