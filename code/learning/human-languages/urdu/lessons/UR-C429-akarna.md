## You'll want to know: اکڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C429-THAND429-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اکڑنا** — *akaṛnā* — "to go stiff; to go numb".

> ٹھنڈ سے میری انگلیاں اکڑ گئیں۔
>
> *ṭhanḍ se merī ungliyāṅ akaṛ gaīṅ.*
>
> My fingers went stiff with the cold.

Past (he …): وہ اکڑ گیا, *voh akaṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکڑنا** (*akaṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *akaṛnā*, "to be numbed", under *ākkaḍa*, "cramped, stiff", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1013).
