## You'll want to know: じまんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C441-PROUD441-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じまんする** — *jiman suru* — "to boast; to be proud of".

> **ちちは むすこを じまんしています。** — *chichi wa musuko o jiman shite imasu* — "My father is proud of his son."

Polite **じまんします**, *jimanshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かれは くるまを じまんしました。**, *kare wa kuruma o jiman shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じまんする** (*jiman suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *jiman* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "oneself" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*) and *man*, "ridicule" (*maenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
