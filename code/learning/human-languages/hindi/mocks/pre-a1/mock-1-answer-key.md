## Writing

1. **नमस्ते**
2. **नाम**
3. **मैं**
4. **आप**
5. **हिंदी**
6. **धन्यवाद**
7. Accept the learner's chosen name when it is independently written in
   recognizable Devanagari; do not require the name **रवि**.
8. Accept an appropriate independently written Hindi greeting such as
   **नमस्ते**. Do not accept a Romanized or visibly copied answer.

## Speaking

1. Accept **नमस्ते** or an equally appropriate taught Hindi greeting.
2. Accept a chosen identity in a comprehensible frame such as
   **मेरा नाम … है।**
3. Accept a relevant state such as **मैं ठीक हूँ।**
4. Accept a comprehensible place response such as **मैं दिल्ली से हूँ।**
5. Accept a relevant yes/no response or a short Hindi-speaking frame.
6. Accept a comprehensible polite water request containing **पानी** and an
   appropriate request signal such as **कृपया**.

Apply the speaking rubric to the whole part; the examples above are not scripts
the learner must reproduce word for word.
