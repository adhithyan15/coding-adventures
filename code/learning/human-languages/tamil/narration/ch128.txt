Tamil, chapter 128: Wave, Buffalo, Horse, Elephant, Tiger.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அலை (alai) — a wave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for lightning, then the Tamil for a storm.

You'll want to know: அலை.
அலை — alai — "a wave".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: alai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: alai, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say alai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for lightning, then the Tamil for a storm, then say alai again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does அலை (alai) mean? ("A wave".) Say it once more.
