## You'll want to know: raucus, rauca, raucum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C448-STERTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**raucus, rauca, raucum** — "hoarse; harsh-sounding".

> Post noctem longam vōx caupōnis rauca est.
>
> *After a long night the innkeeper's voice is hoarse.*

An adjective of three endings. English *raucous* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raucus* is built on *rāvis* 'hoarseness' with an adjective suffix (EtymDB); Lewis & Short trace it to a root meaning 'to make a loud noise'. English *raucous* comes from it.
