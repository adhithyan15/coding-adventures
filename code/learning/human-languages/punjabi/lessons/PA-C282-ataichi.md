# ਅਟੈਚੀ (aṭaichī) — a suitcase

## You'll want to know: ਅਟੈਚੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C282-BAGS282-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਟੈਚੀ** — *aṭaichī* — "a suitcase".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਅਟੈਚੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਭਾਰੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī aṭaichī bahut bhārī hai.*
>
> My suitcase is very heavy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਅਟੈਚੀ** (*aṭaichī*) is borrowed from English *attaché*, as in an attaché case.
