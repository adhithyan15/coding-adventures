## You'll want to know: la caverna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C462-MONTAGNA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la caverna** — "the cave, the cavern".

> Nella caverna fa freddo anche d'estate.
>
> *In the cave it is cold even in summer.*

Bigger and deeper than *una grotta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caverna* is a learned borrowing from Latin *caverna* 'cave, hollow' (EtymDB), from *cavus* 'hollow'.
