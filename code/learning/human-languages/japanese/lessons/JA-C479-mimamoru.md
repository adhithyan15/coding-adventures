## You'll want to know: みまもる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C479-BOND479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**みまもる** — *mimamoru* — "to watch over".

> **りょうしんは こどもを みまもっています。** — *ryōshin wa kodomo o mimamotte imasu* — "The parents are watching over the child."

Polite **みまもります**, *mimamorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが こどもを みまもりました。**, *haha ga kodomo o mimamorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みまもる** (*mimamoru*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *mi*, "seeing (miru)" and *mamoru*, "to protect" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
