## You'll want to know: かんじょう

**かんじょう** — *kanjō* — "paying the bill; a calculation".

> **かんじょうを おねがいします。** — *kanjō o onegai shimasu* — "The bill, please."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんじょう** (*kanjō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "check" (Middle Chinese *khomH*) and *jō*, "determine" (*dengH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
