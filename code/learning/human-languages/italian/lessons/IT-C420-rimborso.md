## You'll want to know: il rimborso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C420-RITARDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il rimborso** — "the refund".

> Ho chiesto il rimborso del biglietto.
>
> *I asked for a refund on the ticket.*

*Un rimborso parziale* — a partial refund.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimborso* is built on *borsa* 'purse', which is inherited from Late Latin *bursa*, from Greek *býrsa* 'hide, leather': money back into the purse.
