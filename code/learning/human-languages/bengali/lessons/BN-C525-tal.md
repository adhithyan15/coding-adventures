## You'll want to know: তাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C525-SUR525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**তাল** — *tāl* — "rhythm; a beat".

> তবলা গানের তাল রাখে।
>
> *Tôblā gāner tāl rākhe.*
>
> The tabla keeps the rhythm of the song.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তাল** (*tāl*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *tāl*, "clapping", under Sanskrit *tāḍa*, where *tāla* means a clap and musical time (CDIAL 5748).
