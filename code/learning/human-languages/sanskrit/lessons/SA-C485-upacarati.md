## You'll want to know: उपचरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C485-SUSRUSA485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपचरति** — *upacarati* — "attends on".

> पुत्री रुग्णां मातरम् उपचरति।
>
> *putrī rugṇāṃ mātaram upacarati.*
>
> The daughter looks after her sick mother.

Past (she …): सा मातरम् उपचरितवती, *sā mātaram upacaritavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपचरति** (*upacarati*) is built from *upa*, "near", and the root √car, "to move"; MW gives "to wait upon, serve, attend" (MW).
