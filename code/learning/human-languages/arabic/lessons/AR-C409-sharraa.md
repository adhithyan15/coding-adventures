## You'll want to know: شرع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C409-QANUN409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شرع** — *sharraʿa* — "to legislate".

> يشرع البرلمان القوانين.
>
> *yusharriʿu al-barlamān al-qawānīn.*
>
> Parliament makes the laws.

Present: يشرع, *yusharriʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرع** (*sharraʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-ʿ*, "to lay down a way" (Lane). It is Form II, to lay down a way for others: to legislate.
