## You'll want to know: la tolérance
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C381-AUTORISER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la tolérance** — "the tolerance".

> Il faut un peu de tolérance pour vivre ensemble.
>
> *Living together takes a bit of tolerance.*

*Tolérance zéro* — zero tolerance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tolérance* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerantia* "endurance, bearing" (Lewis & Short), from *tolerāre* "to bear".
