7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Words from chapters 156-159 — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a quilt and a carpet.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a festival, New Year, an era, every week, every year]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a place, a neighbourhood, a pavement, a town square, a canal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a nation, a settlement, a port, a restaurant, a café]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: so, since, if, even so, otherwise]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A festival? (تہوار, tahvār.) New Year? (نیا سال, nayā sāl.) An era? (زمانہ, zamānā.) Every week? (ہر ہفتے, har hafte.) Every year? (ہر سال, har sāl.) A place? (جگہ, jagah.) A neighbourhood? (محلہ, mohallā.) A pavement? (فٹ پاتھ, fuṭpāth.) A town square? (چوک, chauk.) A canal? (نہر, nahar.) A nation? (قوم, qaum.) A settlement? (بستی, bastī.) A port? (بندرگاہ, bandargāh.) A restaurant? (ریسٹورنٹ, resṭorenṭ.) A café? (کیفے, kaife.) So? (اس لیے, is liye.) Since? (چونکہ, chūṅki.) If? (اگر, agar.) Even so? (پھر بھی, phir bhī.) Otherwise? (ورنہ, varnā.)
