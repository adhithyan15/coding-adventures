Marathi, chapter 84: To fill, To change, To hold, To fall, Meat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भरणे (bharṇe) — to fill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to hide, then the Marathi for to win.

You'll want to know: भरणे.
भरणे — bharṇe — "to fill".

The word, taken apart.
भरणे (bharṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bharati, "bears, brings; fills" (CDIAL 9397).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बदलणे (badalṇe) — to change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to win, then the Marathi for to fill.

You'll want to know: बदलणे.
बदलणे — badalṇe — "to change".

The word, taken apart.
बदलणे (badalṇe) is a loan from Arabic badal, "a substitute, an exchange", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is b-d-l (Lane). Marathi made a verb of it with -ṇe.

You'll want to know: मांस.
मांस — māṁs — "meat".

The word, taken apart.
मांस (māṁs) is Sanskrit māṁsa, "flesh", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 9982). CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi forms mās and māṁs.
