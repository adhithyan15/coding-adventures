Kannada, chapter 83: How You Feel Inside.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದುಃಖ (duḥkha) — sadness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a headache, then the Kannada for rest.

You'll want to know: ದುಃಖ.
ದುಃಖ (duḥkha) — "sadness, sorrow". The two small circles ಃ after ದು are the visarga: a breath at the end of the syllable, duḥ-kha.
It is the opposite of ಸಂತೋಷ (santōṣa), "happiness", which you met at the start.

What you've built.
The other side of santōṣa.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: duḥkha]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: duḥkha, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say duḥkha and then santōṣa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a headache, then the Kannada for rest, then say duḥkha again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ದುಃಖ (duḥkha) mean? ("Sadness".) Say it once more.
