Sanskrit, chapter 71: Run, Walk, Sit, Stand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धावति (dhāvati) — runs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the shoulder, then the Sanskrit for the lip.

You'll want to know: धावति.
धावति (dhāvati) — "(he, she) runs". It ends in -ति like गच्छति: one person, doing it now.

What you've built.
A verb in the frame you know.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dhāvati]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dhāvati, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bālaḥ dhāvati]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for the shoulder, then the Sanskrit for the lip, then say dhāvati again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does धावति (dhāvati) mean? ("Runs".) Say it once more.
