## You'll want to know: ਕਰਜ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C258-LOAN258-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਰਜ਼ਾ** — *karzā* — "a debt".

> ਅਮਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਬੈਂਕ ਦਾ ਕਰਜ਼ਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *Aman utte baiṅk dā karzā hai.*
>
> Aman has a debt to the bank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਰਜ਼ਾ** (*karzā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *qard*, "a loan".
