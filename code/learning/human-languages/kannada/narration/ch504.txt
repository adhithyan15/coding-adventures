Lesson 1 of 6.
ಸಿಡುಕು (siḍuku) — to snap irritably.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for forgetfulness, then the Kannada for concentration.

You'll want to know: ಸಿಡುಕು.
ಸಿಡುಕು — siḍuku — "to snap irritably".
ಹಸಿವಾದಾಗ ನನ್ನ ತಮ್ಮ ಎಲ್ಲರ ಮೇಲೆ ಸಿಡುಕುತ್ತಾನೆ. hasivādāga nanna tamma ellara mēle siḍukuttāne. When he is hungry, my younger brother snaps at everyone.
Past (I …): ಸಿಡುಕಿದೆ, siḍukide.
At whom: … ಮೇಲೆ ಸಿಡುಕು (siḍuku). Also a noun, irritability: ಸಿಡುಕಿನ ಮುಖ, a sour face.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿಡುಕು (siḍuku) is a native Kannada verb, "to be irritable, to snap"; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಮಾತುಗಾರ (mātugāra) — a talker; a good speaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for concentration, then the Kannada for to snap irritably.

You'll want to know: ಮಾತುಗಾರ.
ಮಾತುಗಾರ — mātugāra — "a talker; a good speaker".
ನನ್ನ ಮಾವ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಮಾತುಗಾರ. nanna māva doḍḍa mātugāra. My uncle is a great talker.
ಮಾತು, speech + -ಗಾರ, "one who does it", as in ಆಟಗಾರ.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾತುಗಾರ (mātugāra) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4834 lists Kannada mātu, "a word, speech", and mātugāṟa, "a talkative man", with the suffix -gāra, "one who does", as in ಆಟಗಾರ.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಮುಂಗೋಪಿ (muṅgōpi) — short-tempered.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to snap irritably, then the Kannada for a talker.

You'll want to know: ಮುಂಗೋಪಿ.
ಮುಂಗೋಪಿ — muṅgōpi — "short-tempered".
ನಮ್ಮ ಮೇಲಧಿಕಾರಿ ಮುಂಗೋಪಿ, ಬೇಗ ಕೋಪಗೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾರೆ. namma mēladhikāri muṅgōpi, bēga kōpagoḷḷuttāre. Our boss is short-tempered; he gets angry quickly.
ಮುನ್, before + ಕೋಪ, anger: angry before anything has happened.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಂಗೋಪಿ (muṅgōpi) is a Kannada compound: native mun, "before, in front" (DEDR 5020), and kōpa, "anger", borrowed from Sanskrit (CDIAL 3516), with -i for the person.

You'll want to know: ಹೇಡಿ.
ಹೇಡಿ — hēḍi — "a coward".
ಹೇಡಿ ಆಗಬೇಡ ಅಂತ ಅಪ್ಪ ಹೇಳಿದರು. hēḍi āgabēḍa anta appa hēḷidaru. Father said, do not be a coward.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೇಡಿ (hēḍi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4434 sets Kannada hēḍi beside Tamil pēṭi, where fear is one of the senses.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ತೊದಲು (todalu) — a stammer; baby talk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for short-tempered, then the Kannada for a coward.

You'll want to know: ತೊದಲು.
ತೊದಲು — todalu — "a stammer; baby talk".
ಮಗುವಿನ ತೊದಲು ಮಾತು ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಇಷ್ಟ. maguvina todalu mātu ellarigū iṣṭa. Everyone loves the baby's lisping talk.
ತೊದಲು (todalu) ಮಾತು, baby talk. It is a verb as well: ತೊದಲುತ್ತಾ ಮಾತಾಡು, to speak stammering.

The word, taken apart.
ತೊದಲು (todalu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3503 lists Kannada todal(u), "faltering, stammering, speaking indistinctly".
