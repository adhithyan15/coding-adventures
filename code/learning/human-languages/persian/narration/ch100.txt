Persian, chapter 100: Apricot, Cherries, Date, Pomegranate, Walnut.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
زردآلو (zardâlu) — an apricot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a melon, then the Persian for a peach.

You'll want to know: زردآلو.
زردآلو — zardâlu — "an apricot".

The word, taken apart.
zardâlu is zard, "yellow", with âlu, "plum": "yellow plum". Both parts are native Persian; zard is usually traced to Middle Persian zard.
