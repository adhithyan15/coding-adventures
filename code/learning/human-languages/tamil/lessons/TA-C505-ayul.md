## You'll want to know: ஆயுள்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C505-MARANAM505-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆயுள்** — *āyuḷ* — "a lifespan".

> நல்ல உணவு ஆயுளை அதிகரிக்கும்.
>
> *nalla uṇavu āyuḷai atikarikkum.*
>
> Good food lengthens one’s life.

ஆயுள் முழுவதும், all one’s life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆயுள்** (*āyuḷ*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *āyus*, "life, the length of life"; our sources do not list the Tamil form.
