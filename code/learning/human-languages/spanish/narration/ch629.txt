Spanish, chapter 629: Ganar y perder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la ganancia — the gain, the profit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the interest, then the Spanish for to withdraw.

You'll want to know: la ganancia.
la ganancia — "the gain, the profit".
La tienda tuvo ganancias este año. The shop made a profit this year.
Often plural: las ganancias.

The word, taken apart.
ganancia is built on ganar 'to earn, to win', which was probably borrowed from Gothic ganan 'to covet' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
el beneficio — the profit; the benefit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the gain, then the Spanish for the loss.

You'll want to know: el beneficio.
el beneficio — "the profit; the benefit".
La empresa tuvo un beneficio de un millón. The company made a profit of a million.
Also a benefit: en beneficio de todos — for the good of all.

The word, taken apart.
beneficio is a learned borrowing from Latin beneficium 'a kindness, a favour', built from bene 'well' and facere 'to do' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
financiar — to finance, to fund.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the profit, then the Spanish for the investment.

You'll want to know: financiar.
financiar — "to finance, to fund".
El ayuntamiento financia el proyecto. The town council funds the project.
La financiación — the funding.

The word, taken apart.
financiar is built on finanzas, borrowed from French finance 'payment, settlement' (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin finis 'end': to settle a debt was to end it.
