## You'll want to know: la violencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C605-PROBSOC-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la violencia** — "the violence".

> Estamos contra la violencia.
>
> *We are against violence.*

*Violento* — violent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*violencia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *violentia*, built on *violentus* 'violent', from *vis* 'force' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
