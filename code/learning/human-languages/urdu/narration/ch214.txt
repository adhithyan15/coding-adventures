Urdu, chapter 214: To hurry, To be late, To conceal, To earn, To look after.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جلدی کرنا (jaldī karnā) — to hurry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to throw, then the Urdu for to pull.

You'll want to know: جلدی کرنا.
جلدی کرنا — jaldī karnā — "to hurry".

The phrase, taken apart.
جلدی کرنا (jaldī karnā) is "to do quickness". jaldī is jald, "quick", usually traced to Persian jald, a word of Arabic origin, with the ending -ī; karnā comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

دیر کرنا — der karnā — "to be late, to delay".

دیر کرنا (der karnā) joins der, "lateness", usually traced to Persian dēr, "late, a long time", and karnā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 4 of 5.
کمانا (kamānā) — to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be late, then the Urdu for to conceal.

You'll want to know: کمانا.
کمانا — kamānā — "to earn".

The word, taken apart.
کمانا (kamānā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from karmāpayati, "works, earns", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2897). It is built on karman, "work", the source of kām.
