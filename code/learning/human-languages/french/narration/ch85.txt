French, chapter 85: Cow, Pig, Bird, Mouse, Wolf.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la vache — a cow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a desert, then the French for a wave.

You'll want to know: la vache.
la vache — "a cow". It gives le lait.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does la vache mean? ("A cow".) Say it once more.
