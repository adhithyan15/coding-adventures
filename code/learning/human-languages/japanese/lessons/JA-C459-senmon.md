**せんもん** — *senmon* — "one's field of study; a specialty".

> **あねの せんもんは おんがくです。** — *ane no senmon wa ongaku desu* — "My elder sister's field is music."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんもん** (*senmon*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "specialty" (Middle Chinese *tsywen*) and *mon*, "gate" (*mwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
