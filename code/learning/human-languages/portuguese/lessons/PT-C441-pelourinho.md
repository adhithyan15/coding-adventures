## You'll want to know: o pelourinho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C441-PRACA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o pelourinho** — "the pillory (stone pillar)".

> No centro da praça há um pelourinho de pedra.
>
> *In the middle of the square there is a stone pillory.*

A carved stone pillar that showed a town had its own courts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pelourinho* comes from French *pilori* 'pillory' (EtymDB), reshaped with the small-size ending *-inho*; the French word's origin is not settled.
