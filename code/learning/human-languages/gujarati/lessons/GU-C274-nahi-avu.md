# હું નહીં આવું (hũ nahī̃ āvũ) — won't

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-ISH-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NAHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C273-NOUN-AGENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I will come tomorrow" and "she will go". (*hũ kāle āvīś*, *te jaśe*.) Now the same two, but they won't happen.

## Grammar Lens: નહીં and a short form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C274-NEGATIVE-FUTURE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-AAVVU, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01, GU-PRON-AME-01] -->

> **હું કાલે નહીં આવું.**
>
> *hũ kāle nahī̃ āvũ.* — "I won't come tomorrow."

"Won't" is **નહીં** with a short form of the verb: no **-ીશ**, no **-શે**. Learn it as one piece. The short form follows the person, and it is the same for a man and a woman:

| who | આવવું | કરવું |
|---|---|---|
| **હું** | **નહીં આવું** | **નહીં કરું** |
| **તું**, **તે**, **તેઓ** | **નહીં આવે** | **નહીં કરે** |
| **અમે**, **આપણે** | **નહીં આવીએ** | **નહીં કરીએ** |
| **તમે** | **નહીં આવો** | **નહીં કરો** |

## You'll want to know: the short verbs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C274-NEGATIVE-SHORT-VERBS-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-JAVU, GU-LEX-LEVU, GU-LEX-KHAAVU, GU-LEX-C147-PAST147-01] -->

> **તે આજે નહીં જાય.**
>
> *te āje nahī̃ jāy.* — "She won't go today."

**જવું** gives **નહીં જાઉં** (*nahī̃ jāũ*), **નહીં જાય**, **નહીં જઈએ**, **નહીં જાઓ**. **લેવું** gives **નહીં લઉં**, **નહીં લે**; **ખાવું** gives **નહીં ખાઉં**, **નહીં ખાય**. **થવું** gives **નહીં થાય**: **મોડું નહીં થાય** (*moḍũ nahī̃ thāy*), "it won't be late".

Keep these short forms after **નહીં** for now. "I will" still takes **-ીશ**: **હું જઈશ**, but **હું નહીં જાઉં**.
