## You'll want to know: はってんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C526-PROGRESS526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はってんする** — *hatten suru* — "to develop; to grow".

> **もし こうじょうが きたら、まちは はってんするでしょう。** — *moshi kōjō ga kitara, machi wa hatten suru deshō* — "If a factory comes, the town will probably grow."

Polite **はってんします**, *hattenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **まちが はってんしました。**, *machi ga hattenshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はってんする** (*hatten suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *hatten* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ha*, "departure" (Middle Chinese *pjot*) and *ten*, "unfold" (*trjenX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
