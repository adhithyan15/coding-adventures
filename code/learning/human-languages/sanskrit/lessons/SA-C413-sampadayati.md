## You'll want to know: सम्पादयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C413-UNNAYA413-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्पादयति** — *sampādayati* — "arranges; procures".

> पिता यात्रायै यानपत्राणि सम्पादयति।
>
> *pitā yātrāyai yānapatrāṇi sampādayati.*
>
> Father arranges the tickets for the journey.

Past (he …): सः यानपत्राणि सम्पादितवान्, *saḥ yānapatrāṇi sampāditavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्पादयति** (*sampādayati*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the causative of the root √pad, "to go, to fall"; MW gives its participle *sampādita* as brought about, accomplished (MW).
