# ਗਿਆ, ਗਈ — went

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-LEX-JANA, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *they went*. Then *home* and *school*.

## Grammar Lens: one went for each
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206, PA-LEX-JANA, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-04, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-05, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03] -->

The went form agrees with whoever went: **ਗਿਆ** for a man or a boy, **ਗਈ** for
a woman or a girl, and the **ਗਏ** you know for a group.

> **ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ।** — *Aman ghar giā.* — Aman went home.

> **ਕੁੜੀ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਈ।** — *kuṛī sakūl gaī.* — The girl went to school.

> **ਉਹ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਏ?** — *uh kitthe gae?* — Where did they go?

There is no helper at the end. CDIAL lists Punjabi *geā*, "gone", under
Sanskrit *gata*, "gone" (CDIAL 4008).
