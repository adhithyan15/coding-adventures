Marathi, chapter 366: Values.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मूल्य (mūlya) — a value.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to resolve, then the Marathi for to fight for.

You'll want to know: मूल्य.
मूल्य — mūlya — "a value".
आईवडिलांनी आम्हाला चांगली मूल्यं शिकवली. āīvḍilānnī āmhālā cāṅglī mūlyaṁ śikavlī. Our parents taught us good values.
Neuter. Plural: मूल्यं, mūlyaṁ.
Also price, in formal use: वस्तूचं मूल्य (mūlya).

The word, taken apart.
मूल्य (mūlya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit mūlya, "original value, price" (CDIAL 10257).

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
नैतिकता (naitiktā) is a learned word built on Sanskrit nīti, "conduct, guidance" (CDIAL 7546), through the adjective नैतिक, "moral", with -tā, "-ness".

The word, taken apart.
सचोटी (sacoṭī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi saċoṭī, "honesty", under Sanskrit satyavṛtti, "devotion to truth" (CDIAL 13115).
