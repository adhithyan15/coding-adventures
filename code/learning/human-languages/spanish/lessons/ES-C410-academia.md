# la academia — from a place-name to a place of learning

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-ESTUDIANTE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **estudiante**, a student. A student may learn at **la
academia**, an academy or teaching centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-ACADEMIA]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish **academia** came through Latin **academia** from Greek **Akadēmía**,
the place-name associated with Plato's school. English **academy** belongs to
the same travelled family. In an A1 message, **una academia de idiomas** is a
language school, not necessarily a university institution.

## Grammar Lens: what kind of academy?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-ACADEMIA] -->

Use **de** to name the subject: **una academia de idiomas**. The noun is
feminine: **la academia**, **una academia**.
