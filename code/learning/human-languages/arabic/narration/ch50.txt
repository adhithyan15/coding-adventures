Arabic, chapter 50: Eye, Ear, Nose, Mouth, Tooth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عين (ʿayn) — an eye.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for because, then the Arabic for when.

You'll want to know: عين.
عين — ʿayn — "an eye".

The word, taken apart.
عين (ʿayn) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-y-n, "an eye; a spring of water" (Lane). The letter ʿayn carries the same name, and Hebrew ʿayin, "eye", is its cousin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
أذن (udhun) — an ear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for when, then the Arabic for an eye.

You'll want to know: أذن.
أذن — udhun — "an ear".

The word, taken apart.
أذن (udhun) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-dh-n, "the ear; to listen" (Lane). The call to prayer, adhān, is built on the same root; Hebrew ʾozen, "ear", is a cousin.
