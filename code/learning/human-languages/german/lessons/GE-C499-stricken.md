## You'll want to know: stricken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C499-HANDARBEIT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**stricken** — "to knit".

> Im Winter strickt meine Mutter Schals für die ganze Familie.
>
> *In winter my mother knits scarves for the whole family.*

Regular: *hat gestrickt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stricken* is built on Old High German *stric* "cord, loop" (EtymDB): knitting makes loops of yarn.
