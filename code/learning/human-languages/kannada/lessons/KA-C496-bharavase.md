# ಭರವಸೆ (bharavase) — hope; assurance

## You'll want to know: ಭರವಸೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C496-ASHE496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭರವಸೆ** — *bharavase* — "hope; assurance".

> ವೈದ್ಯರ ಮಾತಿನಿಂದ ನಮಗೆ ಭರವಸೆ ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *vaidyara mātininda namage bharavase banditu.*
>
> The doctor's words gave us hope.

ಭರವಸೆ ಕೊಡು, to give an assurance, to promise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭರವಸೆ** (*bharavase*) is usually traced to Hindustani *bharosā*, "trust, reliance"; the path into Kannada is not settled. In Kannada it is both hope and a promise you can lean on.
