# തുരത്തുക (turattuka) — to drive away; to chase off

## You'll want to know: തുരത്തുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C382-KALAPAM382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**തുരത്തുക** — *turattuka* — "to drive away; to chase off".

> നാട്ടുകാർ ആനയെ കാട്ടിലേക്ക് തുരത്തി.
>
> *nāṭṭukār ānaye kāṭṭilēkkŭ turatti.*
>
> The locals drove the elephant back into the forest.

Past: തുരത്തി, *turatti*.

Of animals and of enemies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തുരത്തുക** (*turattuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3340 lists Malayalam *turattuka*, "to drive away", beside Tamil *tura*, "to drive, to beat away".
