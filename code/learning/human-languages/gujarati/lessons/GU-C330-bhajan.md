## You'll want to know: ભજન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C330-GARBO330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભજન** — *bhajan* — "a hymn".

> રાત્રે મારા દાદાએ ભજન ગાયાં.
>
> *rātre mārā dādāe bhajan gāyā̃.*
>
> At night my grandfather sang hymns.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભજન** (*bhajan*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *bhajana*, "worship, adoration" (Monier-Williams): a song of devotion. It is neuter: **ભજન ગાયાં**, sang hymns.
