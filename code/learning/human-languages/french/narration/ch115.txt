French, chapter 115: Vacuum, Laundry, Washing powder, Iron, Pocket.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'aspirateur — a vacuum cleaner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a bin, then the French for a broom.

You'll want to know: l'aspirateur.
l'aspirateur — "a vacuum cleaner".

The word, taken apart.
aspirateur — built from the verb for "to breathe in, to suck in", with -ateur, "a thing that does". The verb from Latin aspirare, "to breathe upon", from spirare, "to breathe" (Lewis & Short).

The word, taken apart.
lessive from Old French lissive (EtymDB 2.1) from Latin lixivus, "made into lye", from lix, "ashes, lye" (Lewis & Short) — inherited: clothes were washed in lye.
