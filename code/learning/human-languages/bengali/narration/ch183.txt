You'll want to know: চুলকানো.
চুলকানো — chulkāno — "to scratch".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of চুলকানো (chulkāno) is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it, and a search by form and by the glosses "itch" and "scratch" finds only words of other shapes, such as Bengali khos, "itch" (CDIAL 3854).

হাই তোলা — hāi tolā — "to yawn".

The phrase, taken apart.
হাই তোলা (hāi tolā) is hāi, "a yawn", whose origin is not settled, as CDIAL has no entry for it, + tolā, "to raise", from Sanskrit tōlayati, "lifts, weighs" (CDIAL 5979).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: নাক ডাকা.
নাক ডাকা — nāk ḍākā — "to snore".

The phrase, taken apart.
নাক ডাকা (nāk ḍākā) is nāk, "nose", from nakka, "nose" (CDIAL 6909), + ḍākā, "to call", from ḍakk, "shout" (CDIAL 5516), both forms CDIAL reconstructs: "the nose calls".

Lesson 4 of 5.
জাগা (jāgā) — to wake up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to yawn, then the Bengali for to snore.

You'll want to know: জাগা.
জাগা — jāgā — "to wake up".

The word, taken apart.
জাগা (jāgā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jāgrati, "are awake" (CDIAL 5175). CDIAL lists Bengali jāgā, "to wake, watch".

Lesson 5 of 5.
স্নান করা (snān kôrā) — to bathe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to snore, then the Bengali for to wake up.

You'll want to know: স্নান করা.
স্নান করা — snān kôrā — "to bathe".

The phrase, taken apart.
স্নান করা (snān kôrā) is snān, "a bath", Sanskrit snāna taken over whole, + kôrā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814). The verb inherited from the same root is nāoyā, "to bathe", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit snāti, "bathes" (CDIAL 13786).
