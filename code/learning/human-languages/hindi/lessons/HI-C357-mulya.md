## You'll want to know: मूल्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C357-MULYA357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मूल्य** — *mūlya* — "a value (moral); worth".

> सच बोलना हमारे परिवार का सबसे बड़ा मूल्य है।
>
> *sac bolnā hamāre parivār kā sabse baṛā mūlya hai.*
>
> Telling the truth is our family's greatest value.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूल्य** (*mūlya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mūlya*, "price, worth" (Monier-Williams). The moral sense, a value one lives by, is not among the senses Monier-Williams gives.
