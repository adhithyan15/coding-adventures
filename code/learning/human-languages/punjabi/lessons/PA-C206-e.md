# ਏ — one vowel from the went form you can say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਗਏ**, the whole “went” form from the previous lesson.

## Script you'll notice: ਏ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-E-206]; assesses=[PA-GRAM-GAE-206] -->

> **ਗ** + **ਏ** → **ਗਏ**

> **ਏ** — *e* — an independent Gurmukhi vowel

Keep **ਏ** in view. It is the second letter in the known word **ਗਏ**.

No stroke order is taught here: the track does not yet have a verified source
for one.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-E-206] -->

Keep **ਏ** in front of you. Trace its printed shape once with a finger, then
copy it once with a pencil while the model stays visible. Say *e* when you
finish.

> This is observe-and-copy with the model visible, not writing from memory.
