Russian, chapter 368: Helping people in need.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
благотворительность (blagotvorítelnost) — charity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for wealth, then the Russian for unemployment.

You'll want to know: благотворительность.
благотворительность (blagotvorítelnost) — "charity".
Она много лет занимается благотворительностью. Oná mnógo let zanimáyetsya blagotvorítelnostyu. She has done charity work for many years.
Feminine. Благотворительный концерт — a charity concert.

The word, taken apart.
благотворительность (blagotvorítelnost) is built from благо "good", a Church Slavonic word with -ла-, and творить "to make, to do": doing good.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: волонтёр.
волонтёр (volontyór) — "a volunteer".
Волонтёры помогают в больнице. Volontyóry pomogáyut v bolnítse. Volunteers help at the hospital.
Masculine. Доброволец means the same.

The word, taken apart.
волонтёр (volontyór) was borrowed from French volontaire (EtymDB) "voluntary, a volunteer", from Latin voluntās "will, choice" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 6.
пособие (posóbiye) — a benefit, an allowance; a study guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for charity, then the Russian for a volunteer.

You'll want to know: пособие.
пособие (posóbiye) — "a benefit, an allowance; a study guide".
Он получает пособие по безработице. On polucháyet posóbiye po bezrabótitse. He receives unemployment benefit.
A neuter noun. Пособие по безработице — unemployment benefit. Учебное пособие (posóbiye) — a study guide.

The word, taken apart.
пособие (posóbiye) is built on the old verb пособить "to help". The origin of the root is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
жертвовать (zhértvovat) — to donate, to give; to sacrifice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a volunteer, then the Russian for a benefit.

You'll want to know: жертвовать.
жертвовать (zhértvovat) — "to donate, to give; to sacrifice".
Каждый год мы жертвуем деньги детскому дому. Kázhdyy god my zhértvuyem déngi détskomu dómu. Every year we donate money to a children’s home.
Imperfective; the present is жертвую, жертвуешь; the perfective is пожертвовать. With the instrumental it means to sacrifice: жертвовать (zhértvovat) временем.

The word, taken apart.
жертвовать (zhértvovat) is built on жертва "a sacrifice, a victim", which is usually traced to an old Slavic verb meaning to praise or to offer to the gods.

Lesson 5 of 6.
нуждаться (nuzhdátsya) — to need, to be in need.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a benefit, then the Russian for to donate.

You'll want to know: нуждаться.
нуждаться (nuzhdátsya) — "to need, to be in need".
Эта семья нуждается в помощи. Éta semyá nuzhdáyetsya v pómoshchi. This family is in need of help.
Imperfective only. Нуждаться в + prepositional. More formal than нужен.

The word, taken apart.
нуждаться (nuzhdátsya) is built on нужда "need, want", the noun behind нужный "necessary", inherited from Proto-Slavic.
