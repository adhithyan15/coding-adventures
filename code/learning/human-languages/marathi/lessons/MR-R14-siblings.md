# Sibling retrieval

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-BHAU, MR-LEX-BAHIN] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Say **brother, sister**: **भाऊ, बहीण**.

## The two words and their histories
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-ETYMON-BHAU-BROTHER] -->

**भाऊ** is a secure cousin of English **brother**. Do not extend that claim to
**बहीण** and English *sister*; those roots are unrelated.
