# ಪಕ್ಷಿಧಾಮ (pakṣidhāma) — a bird sanctuary

## You'll want to know: ಪಕ್ಷಿಧಾಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C476-VANYAJIVI476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಕ್ಷಿಧಾಮ** — *pakṣidhāma* — "a bird sanctuary".

> ಚಳಿಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಪಕ್ಷಿಧಾಮಕ್ಕೆ ದೂರದ ಹಕ್ಕಿಗಳು ಬರುತ್ತವೆ.
>
> *caḷigāladalli pakṣidhāmakke dūrada hakkigaḷu baruttave.*
>
> In winter birds from far away come to the bird sanctuary.

ಪಕ್ಷಿ, a bird, plus ಧಾಮ, a dwelling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಕ್ಷಿಧಾಮ** (*pakṣidhāma*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: *pakṣin*, "winged; a bird" (CDIAL 7636), and *dhāman*, "a dwelling place" (Monier-Williams).
