## You'll want to know: अनुवाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C342-PRAKASHAK342-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुवाद** — *anuvād* — "a translation".

> मीरा ने उस कहानी का अंग्रेज़ी में अनुवाद किया।
>
> *Mīrā ne us kahānī kā angrezī meṁ anuvād kiyā.*
>
> Mira translated that story into English.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुवाद** (*anuvād*) is Sanskrit *anuvāda*, "a saying after, a repeating" (Monier-Williams): saying again in another language what was first said in one.
