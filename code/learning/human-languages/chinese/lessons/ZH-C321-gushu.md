## You'll want to know: 古书
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C321-ANCIENT321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**古书** — *gǔshū* — "ancient book; old book".

> **我在书店里看到了一本古书** *wǒ zài shūdiàn lǐ kàndào le yì běn gǔshū* — I came across an old book in the bookshop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**古书** (*gǔshū*) is built from 古 'ancient' and 书 'a book', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *syo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'former' and says of the second that it sets things down.
