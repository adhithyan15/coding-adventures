## You'll want to know: わりあい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C367-HAKKIRI367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**わりあい** — *wariai* — "a proportion; a rate".

> **こうれいしゃの わりあいが ふえたら、ねんきんが すくなく なるでしょう。** — *kōreisha no wariai ga fuetara, nenkin ga sukunaku naru deshō* — "If the share of elderly people grows, pensions will probably shrink."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わりあい** (*wariai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *wari*, "dividing (waru)" and *ai*, "meeting (au)", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
