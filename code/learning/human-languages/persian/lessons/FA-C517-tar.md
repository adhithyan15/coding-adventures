## You'll want to know: تار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C517-INSTRUMENTS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تار** (*târ*) — "a tar (a long-necked lute); a string".

> پدربزرگم تار میزد.
>
> *pedarbozorgam târ mizad.*
>
> My grandfather used to play the tar.

*târ* also means 'string, thread': the instrument is 'the string'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*târ* is a native Persian word for a string or thread, and the instrument takes its name from it. EtymDB lists it without a source, so its older origin is not settled.
