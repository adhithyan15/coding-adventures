## You'll want to know: erschöpft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C486-MUEDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**erschöpft** — "exhausted".

> Nach dem Umzug waren wir völlig erschöpft.
>
> *After the move we were completely exhausted.*

The participle of *erschöpfen* — to exhaust. The noun: *die Erschöpfung*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erschöpft* is built on *er-* and *schöpfen* "to scoop, to draw water", from Middle High German *schepfen* and Old High German *scaphan* (EtymDB): scooped empty.
