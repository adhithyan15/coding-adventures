## You'll want to know: die Umleitung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C409-UMLEITUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Umleitung** — "the diversion (US: detour)".

> Die Umleitung führt durch drei kleine Dörfer.
>
> *The diversion goes through three small villages.*

Plural: *die Umleitungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Umleitung* is built from *um* 'round, around' and *leiten* 'to lead, to guide', inherited from Old High German *leiten* and a cousin of English *lead* (EtymDB).
