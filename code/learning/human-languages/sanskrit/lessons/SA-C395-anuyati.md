## You'll want to know: अनुयाति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C395-SARTHA395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुयाति** — *anuyāti* — "goes along with; follows".

> मम मित्रं माम् अनुयाति।
>
> *mama mitraṃ mām anuyāti.*
>
> My friend goes along with me.

Past (he …): सः माम् अनुयातवान्, *saḥ mām anuyātavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुयाति** (*anuyāti*) is built from *anu*, "after, along", and the root √yā, "to go"; MW gives *anu-yā* as to go after or follow, with the participle *anuyāta* (MW).
