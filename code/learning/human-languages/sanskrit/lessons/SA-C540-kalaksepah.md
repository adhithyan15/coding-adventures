## You'll want to know: कालक्षेपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C540-ASANNA540-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कालक्षेपः** — *kālakṣepaḥ* — "a waste of time; delay".

> यदि कालक्षेपः भवति, तर्हि वयं समये न आगमिष्यामः।
>
> *yadi kālakṣepaḥ bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ samaye na āgamiṣyāmaḥ.*
>
> If there is a delay, we will not arrive on time.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कालक्षेपः** (*kālakṣepaḥ*) is built from *kāla*, "time", and *kṣepa*, "a throwing, a letting go", from the root √kṣip, "to throw"; MW gives *kālakṣepa* as letting time pass, delay, loss of time (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
