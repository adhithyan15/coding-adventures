Lesson 1 of 5.
আদর্শ (ādôrshô) — an ideal; a model to follow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a policy, then the Bengali for population.

You'll want to know: আদর্শ.
আদর্শ — ādôrshô — "an ideal; a model to follow".
সত্যি বলা আমাদের পরিবারের আদর্শ। sôtti bôlā āmāder pôribārer ādôrshô. Telling the truth is our family's ideal.

The word, taken apart.
আদর্শ (ādôrshô) is Sanskrit ādarśa, a mirror (Monier-Williams). The sense 'an ideal', the image you shape yourself by, is a later one.

মূল্যবোধ — mūlyôbodh — "moral values".
পরিবার থেকেই আমরা মূল্যবোধ শিখি। pôribār thekei āmrā mūlyôbodh shikhi. We learn our values from our families.

The word, taken apart.
মূল্যবোধ (mūlyôbodh) is a modern Bengali compound of Sanskrit mūlya, value or price, and bodha, understanding (Monier-Williams): a sense of what things are worth.

সহিষ্ণুতা — sôhishnutā — "tolerance".
সমাজে সহিষ্ণুতা দরকার। sômāje sôhishnutā dôrkār. Society needs tolerance.

The word, taken apart.
সহিষ্ণুতা (sôhishnutā) is Sanskrit sahiṣṇutā, the abstract of sahiṣṇu, patient and forbearing (Monier-Williams): the patience to live with views you do not share.

আমি ন্যায্য দাম চাই। āmi næjyô dām chāi. I want a fair price.

ন্যায্য (næjyô) is Sanskrit nyāyya, right or proper (Monier-Williams), from nyāya, what is right. A price, a demand or a share can be ন্যায্য.
