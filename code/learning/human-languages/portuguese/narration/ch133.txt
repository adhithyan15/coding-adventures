Portuguese, chapter 133: To go down, To rent, To repair, To tell, To walk.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
descer — to go down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to fall, then the Portuguese for to go up.

You'll want to know: descer.
descer — "to go down".

The word, taken apart.
descer — Old Portuguese said decer (EtymDB 2.1). The usual account traces it to Latin decidere, "to fall down", later reshaped after descendere, "to climb down"; the details are uncertain.

Lesson 3 of 7.
reparar — to repair; to notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to go down, then the Portuguese for to rent.

You'll want to know: reparar.
reparar — "to repair; to notice".

The word, taken apart.
reparar from Latin reparare, "to restore, to get back" (Lewis & Short) — a learned borrowing from Latin. The sense "to notice" grew later. EtymDB 2.1 traces it only to Old Portuguese reparar.

You'll want to know: caminhar.
caminhar — "to walk".

The word, taken apart.
caminhar is built from the word for a road or path (EtymDB 2.1), which from Late Latin camminus, "way", a word Latin took from Celtic (EtymDB 2.1).
