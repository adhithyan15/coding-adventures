# ഈയിടെ (īyiṭe) — recently, lately

## You'll want to know: ഈയിടെ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C340-IYITE-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C98-CINEMA-01, ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-03, ML-LEX-GRANDMOTHER, ML-LEX-C115-UNWELL-05] -->

**ഈയിടെ** — *īyiṭe* — "recently, lately".

> **ഈയിടെ ഞാൻ ഒരു നല്ല സിനിമ കണ്ടു.**
>
> *īyiṭe ñān oru nalla sinima kaṇṭu.* — "Recently I saw a good film."

With a past verb it means "recently, a short while back". With a present one it means
"lately, these days":

> **ഈയിടെ മുത്തശ്ശിക്ക് സുഖമില്ല.**
>
> *īyiṭe muttaśśikkŭ sukhamilla.* — "Lately Grandmother has not been well."

**അടുത്തിടെ**, which you know, says the same. You will hear both, and a story often opens on
one of them.

Its first letter is one you have not written yet. The next lesson takes it out of this word
and puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഈയിടെ** (*īyiṭe*) is native Dravidian, and it is read as "in this interval". **ഈ**, *ī*, is
the "this" that stands before a noun, from the near demonstrative base (DEDR 410). **ഇട**,
*iṭa*, is "interval, space between", with Tamil *iṭai* (DEDR 448). The **-എ** on the end places
it in time. The same **ഇട** sits inside **ഇതിനിടെ**, "meanwhile".
