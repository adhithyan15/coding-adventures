## You'll want to know: यन्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C407-YANTA407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**यन्ता** — *yantā* — "a driver of horses; a charioteer".

> यन्ता रथं मार्गे चालयति।
>
> *yantā rathaṃ mārge cālayati.*
>
> The charioteer drives the chariot along the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यन्ता** (*yantā*) is built on the root √yam, "to hold, to restrain", with the agent ending *-tṛ*; MW gives *yantṛ* as a driver of horses or elephants, a charioteer (MW).
