Spanish, chapter 515: Según dicen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el rumor — the rumour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to spread, then the Spanish for to announce.

You'll want to know: el rumor.
el rumor — "the rumour".
Corre el rumor de que van a cerrar la fábrica. There is a rumour going round that they are going to close the factory.
Corre el rumor — word goes round. It also means a soft noise: el rumor del mar.

The word, taken apart.
rumor is a learned borrowing from Latin rūmor 'common talk, a noise' (EtymDB). The Spanish word kept both senses, the gossip and the murmur.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
declarar — to declare, to state.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the official statement, then the Spanish for the statement.

You'll want to know: declarar.
declarar — "to declare, to state".
El presidente declaró que no sabía nada. The president stated that he knew nothing.
In court it means to testify, and at customs to declare goods: ¿Algo que declarar?

The word, taken apart.
declarar comes from Latin dēclārāre 'to make clear, to show' (EtymDB), a learned word built on clārus 'clear'. English declare is the same Latin verb.
