# el concierto — music brought together

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-MUSICA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **la música**. A scheduled event where people perform music
is **el concierto**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-CONCIERTO]; assesses=[] -->

**Concierto** belongs to **concertar**, to bring things into agreement, from
Latin **concertāre**. Italian and French helped the musical event-sense travel,
and English **concert** is a close relative. Think of performers bringing
their parts together.

## Grammar Lens: concert of what?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-CONCIERTO, ES-LEX-MUSICA] -->

Use **de** to name the kind: **un concierto de música clásica**. The event is
masculine: **el concierto**, **un concierto**.
