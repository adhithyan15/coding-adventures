## You'll want to know: umkehren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C435-UMWEG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**umkehren** — "to turn back, to turn round".

> Die Straße ist gesperrt, deshalb kehren wir um.
>
> *The road is closed, so we turn back.*

Separable; Perfekt with *sein*: *wir sind umgekehrt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*umkehren* is inherited from Middle High German *ummekēren* (EtymDB), built from *um* 'round' and *kehren* 'to turn'; Old English had a matching verb, *ymbċierran*.
