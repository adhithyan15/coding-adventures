## You'll want to know: 难道
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C397-SURELY397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**难道** — *nándào* — "surely not (in a question)".

> **难道你不想去吗** *nándào nǐ bù xiǎng qù ma* — Surely you want to go?

The new character, in pieces: 难 — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left 又 made narrow, with a dot for its last stroke; on the right the bird piece, eight strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**难道** (*nándào*) is built from 难 'hard' and *dào* 'to say', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nan* and *dawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'difficult' and the second 'a way'. The sense 'to say' is not given there, and how the pair came to ask whether something can really be so is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
