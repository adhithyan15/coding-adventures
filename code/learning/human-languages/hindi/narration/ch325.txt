Hindi, chapter 325: A Movement for Freedom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अंग्रेज़ (aṅgrez) — an English person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a rebellion, then the Hindi for to challenge.

You'll want to know: अंग्रेज़.
अंग्रेज़ — aṅgrez — "an English person".
अंग्रेज़ दो सौ साल तक इस देश में रहे। aṅgrez do sau sāl tak is deś meṁ rahe. The English stayed in this country for two hundred years.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
अंग्रेज़ (aṅgrez) is usually traced to Portuguese inglês, "English", through Persian angrez. अंग्रेज़ी, the English language, is built on it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
आंदोलन (āndolan) — a movement (of people).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to challenge, then the Hindi for an English person.

You'll want to know: आंदोलन.
आंदोलन — āndolan — "a movement (of people)".
आज़ादी का आंदोलन पूरे देश में फैल गया। āzādī kā āndolan pūre deś meṁ phail gayā. The freedom movement spread across the whole country.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आंदोलन (āndolan) is Sanskrit āndolana, "a swinging, a rocking" (Monier-Williams). Modern Hindi uses it for a political movement; the dictionary has only the older sense.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an English person, then the Hindi for a movement (of people).

संघर्ष — saṅgharṣ — "a struggle".
आज़ादी के लिए लोगों ने लंबा संघर्ष किया। āzādī ke liye logoṁ ne lambā saṅgharṣ kiyā. People waged a long struggle for freedom.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संघर्ष (saṅgharṣ) is Sanskrit saṁgharṣa (Monier-Williams), a rubbing together and so a rivalry: in Hindi, a long fight against hardship or against a ruler.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बलिदान (balidān) — a sacrifice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a movement (of people), then the Hindi for a struggle.

You'll want to know: बलिदान.
बलिदान — balidān — "a sacrifice".
उस आंदोलन में हज़ारों लोगों ने बलिदान दिया। us āndolan meṁ hazāroṁ logoṁ ne balidān diyā. Thousands of people gave their lives in that movement.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
बलिदान (balidān) is Sanskrit balidāna, bali, an offering, and dāna, giving (Monier-Williams). In history it is giving one's life for others.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सौंपना (saumpnā) — to entrust; to hand over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a struggle, then the Hindi for a sacrifice.

You'll want to know: सौंपना.
सौंपना — saumpnā — "to entrust; to hand over".
जाने से पहले राजा ने क़िले की चाबी अपने बेटे को सौंपी। jāne se pahle rājā ne qile kī cābī apne beṭe ko saumpī. Before leaving, the king handed the key of the fort to his son.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चाबी सौंपी, usne cābī saumpī.

The word, taken apart.
सौंपना (saumpnā) goes back to Sanskrit samarpayati, "hands over" (CDIAL 13192). What is handed over agrees with the verb: चाबी सौंपी; the person who receives it takes को.
