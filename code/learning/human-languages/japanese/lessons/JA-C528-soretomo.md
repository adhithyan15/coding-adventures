## You'll want to know: それとも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C528-OR528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**それとも** — *soretomo* — "or (in a question)".

> **あめが ふったら、えいがに しますか、それとも うちに いますか。** — *ame ga futtara, eiga ni shimasu ka, soretomo uchi ni imasu ka* — "If it rains, shall we see a film, or stay at home?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**それとも** (*soretomo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
