# हाँ, थोड़ी — two words, and one of them is the test

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C07-HAAN-01, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have **हाँ** for yes and the hedge from this chapter. Put them
together and you have a complete answer.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C92-DEG-01, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-04, HI-CONCEPT-C07-HAAN-01] -->

> — क्या आप हिंदी बोलती हैं?
> — हाँ, थोड़ी।

That answer is a listening item on the first practice paper. Two words, and
**the second one carries the whole thing**: a bare *hā̃* would claim fluency, and
*thoṛī* is what makes the answer true.

The ending is not decoration either. It is **थोड़ी** and not **थोड़ा** because
*hindī* is feminine — so a listener who hears the wrong ending hears a speaker
who has not noticed what they are talking about.

## How to answer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C92-DEG-01, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-02, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-05] -->

Say each of these out loud:

- yes, a little
- I speak a little Hindi
- he speaks well
- what a good book!

[PAUSE 5s]

Every one of the four ends in a word that had to agree with something. Say them
again and listen for the ending each time rather than for the word.

## Grammar Lens: the chapter in one sentence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C92-DEG-03, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-02, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-04] -->

| what a paper asks | what it is testing |
|---|---|
| how much do you speak? | the hedge, and its ending |
| how well does he speak? | an adjective doing an adverb's job |
| which is bigger? | **से**, and an unchanged adjective |

Three questions, and none of them needs a word you did not already half have.
**This chapter added five words and no new machinery.**
