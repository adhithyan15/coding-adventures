## You'll want to know: ਤਬੀਅਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C319-HEALTH319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਬੀਅਤ** — *tabīat* — "how someone is feeling".

> ਅੱਜ ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਤਬੀਅਤ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਹੈ?
>
> *ajj tuhāḍī tabīat kiveṁ hai?*
>
> How are you feeling today?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਬੀਅਤ** (*tabīat*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ṭabīʿa*, "nature, temperament".
