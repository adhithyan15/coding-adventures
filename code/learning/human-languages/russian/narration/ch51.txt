Russian, chapter 51: Cup, Plate, Spoon, Knife, Fork.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
чашка (cháshka) — a cup.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the bathroom, then the Russian for a clock.

You'll want to know: чашка.
чашка (cháshka) — "a cup". For чай or кофе.

What you've built.
One more word for cup, plate, spoon, knife, fork.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cháshka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cháshka, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say cháshka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for the bathroom, then the Russian for a clock, then say cháshka again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does чашка (cháshka) mean? ("A cup".) Say it once more.
