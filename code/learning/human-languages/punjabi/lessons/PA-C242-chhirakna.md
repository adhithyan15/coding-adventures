## You'll want to know: ਛਿੜਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C242-KAM242-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਿੜਕਣਾ** — *chhiṛakṇā* — "to sprinkle".

> ਮੈਂ ਸਬਜ਼ੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਲੂਣ ਛਿੜਕਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ sabzī utte lūṇ chhiṛakdī hāṁ.*
>
> I sprinkle salt on the vegetables.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਲੂਣ ਛਿੜਕਿਆ, *maiṁ lūṇ chhiṛkiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਿੜਕਣਾ** (*chhiṛakṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*chiṭ*, "sudden movement, splash, scatter"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chiṛkaṇā*, "to sprinkle" (CDIAL 5035).
