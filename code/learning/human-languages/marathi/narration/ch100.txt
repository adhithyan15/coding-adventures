Marathi, chapter 100: Horse, Cow, Goat, Sheep, Bird.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घोडा (ghoḍā) — a horse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a dog, then the Marathi for a cat.

You'll want to know: घोडा.
घोडा — ghoḍā — "a horse".

The word, taken apart.
घोडा (ghoḍā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit ghoṭa, "horse" (CDIAL 4516). CDIAL calls the Sanskrit word non-Aryan, probably from Dravidian.

शेळी (sheḷī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from chagala, "goat" (CDIAL 4963).

Lesson 4 of 5.
मेंढी (meṇḍhī) — a sheep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cow, then the Marathi for a goat.

You'll want to know: मेंढी.
मेंढी — meṇḍhī — "a sheep".

The word, taken apart.
मेंढी (meṇḍhī) is the feminine of Marathi meṇḍhā, "ram", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit meṇḍha, "ram", a word from the old word-lists (CDIAL 10310). CDIAL takes it as a loan-word, probably of Austroasiatic origin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पक्षी (pakṣī) — a bird.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a goat, then the Marathi for a sheep.

You'll want to know: पक्षी.
पक्षी — pakṣī — "a bird".

The word, taken apart.
पक्षी (pakṣī) is Sanskrit pakṣin, "winged; bird", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 7636). It is built on pakṣa, "wing".
