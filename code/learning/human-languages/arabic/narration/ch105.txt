Arabic, chapter 105: Entrance, Exit, Straight ahead, Around, At.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مدخل (madkhal) — an entrance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a place, then the Arabic for an address.

You'll want to know: مدخل.
مدخل — madkhal — "an entrance".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does مدخل (madkhal) mean? ("An entrance".) Say it once more.
