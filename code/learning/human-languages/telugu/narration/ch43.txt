Telugu, chapter 43: More Everyday Verbs.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
రా (rā) — come.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
A short run of everyday verbs, one at a time.

You'll want to know: రా.
రా (rā) — "come".
The bare command, and one of the shortest words in the language: a single syllable that is a complete sentence.

What you've built.
One more everyday action. Three more follow, and each reuses the ones before it.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: rā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: it again, to someone standing in a doorway]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does రా (rā) mean? ("come".) Say it once more.

నిల్చో (nilcō) — "stand".
Its pair. Telugu, like the other Dravidian languages, makes the bare stem do the work of a command with nothing added.
