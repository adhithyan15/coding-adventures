## You'll want to know: استثنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C353-NAZARIYA353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استثنا** — *istisnā* — "an exception".

> ہر اصول میں کوئی استثنا ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *har usūl meṅ koī istisnā hotā hai.*
>
> Every rule has some exception.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استثنا** (*istisnā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *istithnāʾ*, "leaving out, excepting".
