Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 282: Spinning and Beads.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सूत (sūt) — yarn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to carve, then the Marwadi for a kiln.

You'll want to know: सूत.
सूत — sūt — "yarn".
यो सूत लाल है। yo sūt lāl hai. This yarn is red.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सूत (sūt) is inherited from Sanskrit sūtra, "thread, cord"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi sūt, "thread" (CDIAL 13561).

Lesson 2 of 5.
चरखो (charkho) — a spinning wheel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a kiln, then the Marwadi for yarn.

You'll want to know: चरखो.
चरखो — charkho — "a spinning wheel".
दादी कनै एक चरखो है। dādī kanai ek charkho hai. Grandmother has a spinning wheel.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चरखो (charkho) is usually traced to Persian charkh, "a wheel"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कातणो (kātṇo) — to spin thread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for yarn, then the Marwadi for a spinning wheel.

You'll want to know: कातणो.
कातणो — kātṇo — "to spin thread".
दादी रोज सूत कातै है। dādī roj sūt kātai hai. Grandmother spins yarn every day.
Past (I …): म्हैं सूत कात्यो, mhaĩ sūt kātyo.

The word, taken apart.
कातणो (kātṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed kartati, "spins"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari kātaï, "spins" (CDIAL 2855).

Lesson 4 of 5.
माळा (māḷā) — a garland; a string of beads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a spinning wheel, then the Marwadi for to spin thread.

You'll want to know: माळा.
माळा — māḷā — "a garland; a string of beads".
म्हारी माळा नुवी है। mhārī māḷā nuvī hai. My garland is new.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
माळा (māḷā) is inherited from Sanskrit mālā, "a wreath, a garland", which CDIAL takes from Dravidian; it lists Old Marwari māla, "garland" (CDIAL 10092).

Lesson 5 of 5.
गूंथणो (gū̃thṇo) — to string beads; to plait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to spin thread, then the Marwadi for a garland.

You'll want to know: गूंथणो.
गूंथणो — gū̃thṇo — "to string beads; to plait".
छोरी माळा गूंथै है। chhorī māḷā gū̃thai hai. The girl strings a garland.
Past (I …): म्हैं माळा गूंथी, mhaĩ māḷā gū̃thī.

The word, taken apart.
गूंथणो (gū̃thṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit guphati, "strings together"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari guthaï, "strings, threads" (CDIAL 4205).
