## You'll want to know: बाधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C380-HAL380-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बाधा** — *bādhā* — "an obstacle".

> अगर रास्ते में कोई बाधा आएगी तो हम दूसरा रास्ता लेंगे।
>
> *agar rāste meṁ koī bādhā āegī to ham dūsrā rāstā leṅge.*
>
> If there's an obstacle on the way, we'll take another road.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाधा** (*bādhā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *bādhā*, "an obstacle, a hindrance" (Monier-Williams).
