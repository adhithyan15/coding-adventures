## You'll want to know: ਚੋਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C311-LOST311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੋਰ** — *chor* — "a thief".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਰਾਤ ਇੱਕ ਚੋਰ ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਆਇਆ।
>
> *kall rāt ikk chor sāḍe ghar āiā.*
>
> A thief came to our house last night.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੋਰ** (*chor*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cora*, "a thief"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cor*, "thief", under it (CDIAL 4931).
