Malayalam, chapter 134: Laying the Table.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഗ്ലാസ് (glās) — a glass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for ginger, then the Malayalam for sugar.

You'll want to know: ഗ്ലാസ്.
ഗ്ലാസ് (glās) — "a glass". ഒരു ഗ്ലാസ് വെള്ളം — "a glass of water".

What you've built.
Something to drink from.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: glās]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: glās, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask oru glās veḷḷaṁ tarū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for ginger, then the Malayalam for sugar, then say glās again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ഗ്ലാസ് (glās) mean? ("A glass".) Say it once more.
