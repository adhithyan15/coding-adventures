# ਕੜੀ (kaṛī) — a roof beam; a rafter

## You'll want to know: ਕੜੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C246-GHR246-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੜੀ** — *kaṛī* — "a roof beam; a rafter".

> ਛੱਤ ਦੀ ਹਰ ਕੜੀ ਲੱਕੜ ਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *chhatt dī har kaṛī lakkaṛ dī hai.*
>
> Every beam of the roof is made of wood.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੜੀ** (*kaṛī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaṭa*, "thin piece of wood, plank"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaṛī*, "rafter" (CDIAL 2631).
