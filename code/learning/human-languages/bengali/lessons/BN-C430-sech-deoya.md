# সেচ দেওয়া (sech deoyā) — to irrigate; to water (fields)

## You'll want to know: সেচ দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C430-KHET430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সেচ দেওয়া** — *sech deoyā* — "to irrigate; to water (fields)".

> শুকনো দিনে কৃষক খেতে সেচ দেন।
>
> *shukno dine krishôk khete sech den.*
>
> On dry days the farmer irrigates the field.

Past (he/she …): তিনি খেতে সেচ দিলেন, *tini khete sech dilen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সেচ দেওয়া** (*sech deoyā*) joins সেচ, "irrigation", of the family CDIAL sets under Sanskrit *siñcáti*, "pours out, sprinkles", with Bengali *sẽcā*, "to irrigate, to bale out" (CDIAL 13394), to দেওয়া, to give.
