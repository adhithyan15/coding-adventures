## You'll want to know: شجاعت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C487-HOPE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شجاعت** (*shojâ'at*) — "courage, bravery".

> آتش نشان با شجاعت بچه را نجات داد.
>
> *âtashneshân bâ shojâ'at bachche râ nejât dâd.*
>
> The firefighter bravely saved the child.

*bâ shojâ'at* — bravely. The adjective is *shojâ'* 'brave'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shojâ'at* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *šajāʿa*, 'courage'. Its partner *shojâ'*, 'brave', was borrowed from Arabic *šujāʿ* (EtymDB).
