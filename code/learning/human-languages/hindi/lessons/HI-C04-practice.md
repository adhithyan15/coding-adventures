# Chapter 4 — The farewells

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. Four ways to part, from the formal to the offhand.

## The farewells, by register

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: formal, final (Perso-Arabic) — *alvidā*]
- [YOU SAY: everyday, "we'll meet again" — *phir milenge*]
- [YOU SAY: "see you tomorrow" — *kal milte haiṁ*]
- [YOU SAY: casual "I'll be off" (m./f.) — *caltā hūṁ / caltī hūṁ*]

## The atoms

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: "again" and "(we) will meet" (*phir*, *milenge*)]
- [YOU SAY: the word that means BOTH tomorrow and yesterday (*kal*)]
- [YOU SAY: the verb behind "I'll be off" (*calnā*, "to move")]

## Roots you now carry

[PAUSE 2s]
- *milenge* ← *mil-* "to join" (*milan*, "a meeting")
- *kal* ← *kāla* "time" (cousin of Punjabi *akāl*, "timeless")
- *caltā* ← *cal-* "to move"; the *-tā/-tī* ending marks the speaker's gender
- *alvidā* ← Perso-Arabic *al-widā*, "the farewell"

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] You're leaving a friend and expect to see them next week. Which goodbye
fits, and how would a woman sign off casually? (*Phir milenge*; *caltī hūṁ*.)
