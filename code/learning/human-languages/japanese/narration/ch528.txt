Japanese, chapter 528: This or That.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
あるいは (aruiwa) — or perhaps; or.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for from now on, then the Japanese for the present day.

You'll want to know: あるいは.
あるいは — aruiwa — "or perhaps; or".
あした さむく なったら、あめ あるいは ゆきでしょう。 — ashita samuku nattara, ame aruiwa yuki deshō — "If it gets cold tomorrow, there will be rain or snow."

The word, taken apart.
あるいは (aruiwa) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for or (in a question), then the Japanese for or else.

You'll want to know: いずれか.
いずれか — izureka — "either one; one of them".
もし じかんが あったら、いずれかの しあいに きて ください。 — moshi jikan ga attara, izureka no shiai ni kite kudasai — "If you have time, please come to one of the matches."

The word, taken apart.
いずれか (izureka) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from izure, "which", and the particle ka (JMdict).
