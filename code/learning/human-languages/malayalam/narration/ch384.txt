Malayalam, chapter 384: Births, Deaths and Life Stories.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ജനനം (jananaṁ) — birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to find out, then the Malayalam for to retrieve.

You'll want to know: ജനനം.
ജനനം — jananaṁ — "birth".
കുഞ്ഞിന്റെ ജനനം വീട്ടിൽ എല്ലാവർക്കും വലിയ സന്തോഷമായി. kuññinṟe jananaṁ vīṭṭil ellāvarkkuṁ valiya santōṣamāyi. The baby's birth was a great joy for everyone at home.
The verb is ജനിക്കുക, to be born.

The word, taken apart.
ജനനം (jananaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit janana, "birth" (CDIAL 5099), from the root jan, "to be born", the root of ജനിക്കുക (janikkuka).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ചരമം (caramaṁ) — an obituary; a death.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for birth, then the Malayalam for death.

You'll want to know: ചരമം.
ചരമം — caramaṁ — "an obituary; a death".
അച്ഛൻ എന്നും രാവിലെ പത്രത്തിലെ ചരമം വായിക്കും. acchan ennuṁ rāvile patrattile caramaṁ vāyikkuṁ. Every morning father reads the obituaries in the paper.
Literally the last: a formal word for a death, and for the death notices in a newspaper.

The word, taken apart.
ചരമം (caramaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit carama, "last, ultimate, final" (Monier-Williams): the last event of a life.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ആത്മകഥ (ātmakatha) — an autobiography.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for death, then the Malayalam for an obituary.

You'll want to know: ആത്മകഥ.
ആത്മകഥ — ātmakatha — "an autobiography".
ആ നടന്റെ ആത്മകഥ ഒരുപാട് പേർ വായിച്ചു. ā naṭanṟe ātmakatha orupāṭŭ pēr vāyiccu. Many people read that actor's autobiography.
ആത്മ-, self, + കഥ, a story: the story of oneself.

The word, taken apart.
ആത്മകഥ (ātmakatha) is built from two Sanskrit words, ātman, "breath, soul, self" (CDIAL 1135), and kathā, "speech, a story" (CDIAL 2704), the source of കഥ (katha).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an obituary, then the Malayalam for an autobiography.

You'll want to know: ജീവചരിത്രം.
ജീവചരിത്രം — jīvacaritraṁ — "a biography".
ഞാൻ ഒരു കവിയുടെ ജീവചരിത്രം വായിക്കുന്നു. ñān oru kaviyuṭe jīvacaritraṁ vāyikkunnu. I am reading a poet's biography.
ജീവ-, life, + ചരിത്രം, history: the history of one life.

The word, taken apart.
ജീവചരിത്രം (jīvacaritraṁ) is built from Sanskrit jīva, "life" (CDIAL 5239), and caritra, "going; behaviour" (CDIAL 4691), the source of ചരിത്രം (caritraṁ), "history".
