You'll want to know: कठै.
कठै — kaṭhai — "where".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of कठै (kaṭhai) is not settled. CDIAL, searched by form and by meaning, has no entry for it; its k- is the old question stem, as in kuṇ, "who".

Lesson 3 of 5.
आगै (āgai) — in front, ahead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for where, then the Marwadi for over there.

You'll want to know: आगै.
आगै — āgai — "in front, ahead".

The word, taken apart.
आगै (āgai) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from agra, "top, front". CDIAL lists Old Marwari āgai under it (CDIAL 68).

Lesson 4 of 5.
लारै (lārai) — behind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for over there, then the Marwadi for in front.

You'll want to know: लारै.
लारै — lārai — "behind".

The word, taken apart.
लारै (lārai) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from lārā, "string, line", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari lāra under it (CDIAL 11024). The Old Marwari row means "at the back, behind"; Gujarati has lāre, "behind".

Lesson 5 of 5.
माथै (māthai) — on top of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for in front, then the Marwadi for behind.

You'll want to know: माथै.
माथै — māthai — "on top of".

The word, taken apart.
माथै (māthai) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from masta, "head". CDIAL lists Marwari mātho under it (CDIAL 9926). माथै (māthai) is a form of mātho, "head": "at the head of", so "on top of".
