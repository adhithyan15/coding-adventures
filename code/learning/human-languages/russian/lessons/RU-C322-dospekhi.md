## You'll want to know: доспехи
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C322-RYTSAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**доспехи** (*dospékhi*) — "armour".

> Доспехи рыцаря были очень тяжёлыми.
>
> *Dospékhi rýtsarya býli óchen tyazhyólymi.*
>
> The knight's armour was very heavy.

Plural; genitive *доспехов*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*доспехи* is usually traced to *доспеть* "to get ready", from the root of *спех* "haste" and *успех* "success": the gear made ready. EtymDB lists no parent.
