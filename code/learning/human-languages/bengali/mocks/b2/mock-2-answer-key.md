## Writing

Task 1 must address Lina, identify who loses access when the final evening bus
is removed, give one concrete example, and propose a testable alternative in
100–130 independently produced Bengali words. Task 2 must sustain a developed
220–280-word argument about mandatory student input into school environmental
decisions, considering both sides, evidence, an implementation condition, and
a reasoned conclusion.

## Speaking

Allow keyword notes only. The presentation must develop a lower-waste local
festival plan with participation, an objection, and a measure. In the riverbank
safety collaboration, require candidates to compare priorities and expose
trade-offs before reaching a defended joint plan. The five follow-ups must probe
assumptions, access, cost, counterargument, and evidence without supplying
language.

## Regional voice record

The multiparty transaction includes one speaker from the Rarh region of West
Bengal; the interview panel includes one speaker from the Varendra region of
northwestern Bengal. Record natural educated regional pronunciation while the
learner paper retains contemporary standard Bengali spelling. Record speaker
provenance and reviewer confirmation with the produced audio.
