# కిక్కిరిసిపోవు (kikkirisipōvu) — to be packed; to be crammed

## You'll want to know: కిక్కిరిసిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C442-PATTA442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కిక్కిరిసిపోవు** — *kikkirisipōvu* — "to be packed; to be crammed".

> సాయంత్రం బస్సు జనంతో కిక్కిరిసిపోయింది.
>
> *sāyantraṁ bassu janantō kikkirisipōyindi.*
>
> The evening bus was packed with people.

Past (it …): కిక్కిరిసిపోయింది, *kikkirisipōyindi*.

What fills the place takes -తో: జనంతో, with people. పోవు at the end marks a change that has happened completely. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: కిక్కిరిసిపోయింది, it was packed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కిక్కిరిసిపోవు** (*kikkirisipōvu*) is a Telugu compound whose first part, కిక్కిరియు, "to be crammed", has an origin that is not settled; the second part is the native పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572), which marks a change that is complete.
