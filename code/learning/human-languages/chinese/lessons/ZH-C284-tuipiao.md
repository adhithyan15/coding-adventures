## You'll want to know: tuìpiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C284-TUIPIAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìpiào* — "to return a ticket", for your money back.

> *wǒ xiǎng tuìpiào* — I'd like to return my ticket.

Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìpiào* is built from tuì 'to give back' and 票 'a ticket'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *thwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to draw back'; the dictionary has no entry for 票, so that part's history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C200-HOTEL200-05, ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-05] -->

You know *tuìfáng*, to give back a hotel room; *tuìpiào* gives back a ticket the same way.

It is a verb on its own, with the ticket inside it: *wǒ xiǎng tuìpiào*.
