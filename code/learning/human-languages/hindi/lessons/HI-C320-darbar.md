## You'll want to know: दरबार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C320-SAMRAT320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दरबार** — *darbār* — "a royal court".

> दरबार में कवि और गायक भी थे।
>
> *darbār meṁ kavi aur gāyak bhī the.*
>
> There were poets and singers at the royal court too.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरबार** (*darbār*) is usually traced to Persian *darbār*, "a royal court", whose first part is *dar*, a door, as in **दरवाज़ा**: those who stand at the king's door.
