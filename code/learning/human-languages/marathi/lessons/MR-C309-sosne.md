## You'll want to know: सोसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C309-MRUTYU309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सोसणे** — *sosṇe* — "to endure; to bear".

> त्या कुटुंबाने खूप दुःख सोसलं.
>
> *tyā kuṭumbāne khūp duḥkh soslaṁ.*
>
> That family endured a lot of sorrow.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सोसलं, *tyāne soslaṁ*.

Transitive: दुःख सोसणे, to bear grief; सहन करणे is the formal cousin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सोसणे** (*sosṇe*) is a Marathi verb of bearing pain; its origin is not settled.
