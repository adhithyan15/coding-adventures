## You'll want to know: la résolution
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C374-BUT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la résolution** — "the resolution, the firm decision".

> Ma bonne résolution pour cette année : faire du sport.
>
> *My resolution for this year: to do some sport.*

*Les bonnes résolutions* — New Year resolutions.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*résolution* is a learned borrowing from Latin *resolūtiō* "a loosening, a solution" (EtymDB), from *resolvere* "to loosen", built on *solvere* "to loosen" (Lewis & Short).
