# ఆప్యాయత (āpyāyata) — warmth; loving care

## You'll want to know: ఆప్యాయత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C503-PREMA503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆప్యాయత** — *āpyāyata* — "warmth; loving care".

> అమ్మమ్మ ఆప్యాయతతో అన్నం వడ్డించింది.
>
> *ammamma āpyāyatatō annaṁ vaḍḍiñcindi.*
>
> Grandma served the rice with loving care.

ఆప్యాయంగా, affectionately, is the adverb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆప్యాయత** (*āpyāyata*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *āpyāyana*, "causing fullness; gladdening" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త: the warmth that makes others feel cared for.
