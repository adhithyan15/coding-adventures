## You'll want to know: ادخر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C527-MAL527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ادخر** — *iddakhara* — "to put aside (savings)".

> ادخرت أختي مالا لشراء سيارة.
>
> *iddakharat ukhtī mālan li-shirāʾ sayyāra.*
>
> My sister put money aside to buy a car.

Present: يدخر, *yaddakhir*.

وفر, to save, which you know, is the everyday verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ادخر** (*iddakhara*) is built on the native Arabic root *dh-kh-r*, "to store up" (Lane). Form VIII iddakhara, with dh turning into d, is to store a thing for later.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
