## You'll want to know: વેદના
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C483-VEDNA483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વેદના** — *vednā* — "agony; deep pain".

> દર્દીની વેદના જોઈને નર્સ ઉદાસ થઈ.
>
> *dardīnī vednā joīne nars udās thaī.*
>
> Seeing the patient's agony, the nurse grew sad.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વેદના** (*vednā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vedanā*, "pain, torture, agony", as Monier-Williams records it.
