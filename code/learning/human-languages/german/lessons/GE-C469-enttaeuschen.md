## You'll want to know: enttäuschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C469-ENTTAEUSCHUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**enttäuschen** — "to disappoint".

> Der Film hat mich enttäuscht.
>
> *The film disappointed me.*

*Enttäuscht sein von* — to be disappointed by or in. Inseparable: *hat enttäuscht*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enttäuschen* is built on *ent-* "away" and *täuschen* "to deceive", from Middle High German *tiuschen* (EtymDB); it is usually explained as first meaning "to free someone from a delusion".
