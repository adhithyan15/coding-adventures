## You'll want to know: ફાળો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C355-VERO355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાળો** — *phāḷo* — "a contribution".

> મંદિર માટે દરેક કુટુંબે ફાળો આપ્યો.
>
> *mandir māṭe darek kuṭumbe phāḷo āpyo.*
>
> Every family contributed to the temple.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાળો** (*phāḷo*) CDIAL lists Gujarati *phāḷo*, "a share", under a reconstructed *phāla*, "a piece split off" (CDIAL 9073): one's share of a common cost.
