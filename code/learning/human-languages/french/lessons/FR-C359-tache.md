## You'll want to know: la tâche
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C359-TACHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la tâche** — "the task, the duty".

> Chaque matin, je fais la liste de mes tâches.
>
> *Every morning, I make a list of my tasks.*

Feminine: *une tâche difficile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tâche* is usually traced to Medieval Latin *taxa* "an assessed amount, set work", from Latin *taxāre* "to appraise" (Lewis & Short).
