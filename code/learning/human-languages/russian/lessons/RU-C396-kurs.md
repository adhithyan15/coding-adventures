## You'll want to know: курс
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C396-TSENY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**курс** (*kurs*) — "an exchange rate; a course; a year (of study)".

> Какой сегодня курс доллара?
>
> *Kakóy segódnya kurs dóllara?*
>
> What is the dollar exchange rate today?

Masculine. *На третьем курсе* — in the third year at university. *Курс английского* — an English course.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**курс** is usually traced to German *Kurs*, from Latin *cursus* "a running, a course" (Lewis & Short), from *currere* "to run".
