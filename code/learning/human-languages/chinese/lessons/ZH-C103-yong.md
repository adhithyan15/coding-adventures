## You'll want to know: 用
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C103-USE103-01]; assesses=[] -->

**用** — *yòng* — "to use".

> **我用水洗菜** *wǒ yòng shuǐ xǐ cài* — I wash the vegetables in water.

The new character, in pieces: 用 — the frame of the moon piece in 明, open at the foot, with two bars inside and a vertical down the middle. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**用** (*yòng*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *yowngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to employ' and 'by means of'.
