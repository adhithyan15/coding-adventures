# ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸು (puraskarisu) — to felicitate; to honour

## You'll want to know: ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C389-SHLAGHISU389-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸು** — *puraskarisu* — "to felicitate; to honour".

> ಶಾಲೆ ಉತ್ತಮ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳನ್ನು ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸಿತು.
>
> *śāle uttama vidyārthigaḷannu puraskarisitu.*
>
> The school honoured its best students.

Past (I …): ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸಿದೆ, *puraskariside*.

ಪುರಸ್ಕಾರ, an honour or an award.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸು** (*puraskarisu*) is built on Sanskrit *puraskāra*, "placing in front, honouring" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
