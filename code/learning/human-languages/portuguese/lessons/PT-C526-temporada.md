## You'll want to know: a temporada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C526-SERIES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a temporada** — "season (of a series)".

> A segunda temporada é melhor do que a primeira.
>
> *The second season is better than the first.*

Also a season of the year for work or holidays: *a temporada alta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*temporada* is built on *tempo* "time", which is inherited from Latin *tempus* (Lewis & Short).
