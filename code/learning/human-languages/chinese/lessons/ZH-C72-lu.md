## You'll want to know: lù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-04]; assesses=[] -->

*lù* — "road; way".

> *lù hěn yuǎn* — It's a long way.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lù* is inherited from Middle Chinese *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'road'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
