German, chapter 386: Vor Gericht.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
klagen — to sue; to complain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for legally, then the German for the rule.

You'll want to know: klagen.
klagen — "to sue; to complain".
Die Nachbarn klagen gegen den Lärm vom Flughafen. The neighbours are suing over the noise from the airport.
Gegen jemanden klagen — to sue someone. In everyday speech: über Kopfschmerzen klagen — to complain of a headache.

The word, taken apart.
klagen is inherited from Proto-Germanic klagōną (EtymDB), first "to lament"; the legal sense, bringing your complaint before a court, grew out of it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Prozess — the trial; the process.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to sue, then the German for the lawsuit.

You'll want to know: der Prozess.
der Prozess — "the trial; the process".
Der Prozess gegen den Politiker beginnt im Herbst. The trial of the politician starts in the autumn.
Plural: die Prozesse. Also a process: ein langer Prozess.

The word, taken apart.
Prozess was borrowed into Middle High German as process (EtymDB) from Medieval Latin prōcessus "a lawsuit", Latin "a going forward", from prōcēdere "to go forward, to advance" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
haften — to be liable; to stick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the lawsuit, then the German for the trial.

You'll want to know: haften.
haften — "to be liable; to stick".
Eltern haften für ihre Kinder. Parents are liable for their children.
Für etwas haften — to be liable for something. Also to stick: Der Aufkleber haftet nicht.

The word, taken apart.
haften goes back to Old High German haftēn "to cling, to be fastened", usually traced to haft "held, captive". EtymDB has the word but no history, so the account is hedged.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Haftung — liability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the trial, then the German for to be liable.

You'll want to know: die Haftung.
die Haftung — "liability".
Für Jacken und Taschen übernehmen wir keine Haftung. We accept no liability for coats and bags.
GmbH stands for Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung — limited liability company.

The word, taken apart.
Haftung is the noun of haften "to be liable", with -ung; the verb goes back to Old High German haftēn "to cling".
