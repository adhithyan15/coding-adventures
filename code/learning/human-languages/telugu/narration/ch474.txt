Telugu, chapter 474: Mountains and Plateaus.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
శిఖరం (śikharaṁ) — a peak; a summit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a tidal surge, then the Telugu for to roar.

You'll want to know: శిఖరం.
శిఖరం — śikharaṁ — "a peak; a summit".
ఉదయమే మేము కొండ శిఖరం చేరుకున్నాం. udayamē mēmu koṇḍa śikharaṁ cērukunnāṁ. Early in the morning we reached the hilltop.
The top of a hill or a mountain, and the top of a temple tower.

The word, taken apart.
శిఖరం (śikharaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit śikhara, "a point, a peak" (CDIAL 12435).

Lesson 2 of 5.
పీఠభూమి (pīṭhabhūmi) — a plateau.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to roar, then the Telugu for a peak.

You'll want to know: పీఠభూమి.
పీఠభూమి — pīṭhabhūmi — "a plateau".
ఆ పీఠభూమి మీద వర్షం తక్కువ. ā pīṭhabhūmi mīda varṣaṁ takkuva. There is little rain on that plateau.
పీఠ, a seat, a stool, + భూమి, land: flat land raised up like a stool.

The word, taken apart.
పీఠభూమి (pīṭhabhūmi) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: pīṭha, "a stool, a bench" (CDIAL 8222), and bhūmi, "earth, ground" (CDIAL 9557).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a peak, then the Telugu for a plateau.

You'll want to know: ఖండం.
ఖండం — khaṇḍaṁ — "a continent".
ప్రపంచంలో ఏడు ఖండాలు ఉన్నాయి. prapañcaṁlō ēḍu khaṇḍālu unnāyi. There are seven continents in the world.
Plural: ఖండాలు, khaṇḍālu.
Plural ఖండాలు. It also means a piece, a section.

The word, taken apart.
ఖండం (khaṇḍaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit khaṇḍa, "broken; a piece, a part" (CDIAL 3792): a continent is one great piece of the earth.

Lesson 4 of 5.
అగ్నిపర్వతం (agniparvataṁ) — a volcano.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a plateau, then the Telugu for a continent.

You'll want to know: అగ్నిపర్వతం.
అగ్నిపర్వతం — agniparvataṁ — "a volcano".
ఆ ద్వీపంలో ఒక అగ్నిపర్వతం ఉంది. ā dvīpaṁlō oka agniparvataṁ undi. There is a volcano on that island.
అగ్ని, fire, + పర్వతం, a mountain: a fire mountain.

The word, taken apart.
అగ్నిపర్వతం (agniparvataṁ) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: agni, "fire", and parvata, "a rock, a mountain" (CDIAL 7945).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ప్రశాంతమైన (praśāntamaina) — peaceful; calm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a continent, then the Telugu for a volcano.

You'll want to know: ప్రశాంతమైన.
ప్రశాంతమైన — praśāntamaina — "peaceful; calm".
కొండల మధ్య ఒక ప్రశాంతమైన గ్రామం ఉంది. koṇḍala madhya oka praśāntamaina grāmaṁ undi. There is a peaceful village among the hills.
ప్రశాంతంగా is the adverb: ప్రశాంతంగా నిద్రపోయాడు, he slept peacefully.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రశాంతమైన (praśāntamaina) is built from Sanskrit praśānta, "calmed, tranquil" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu adjective ending -మైన.
