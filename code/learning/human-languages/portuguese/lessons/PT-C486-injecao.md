## You'll want to know: a injeção
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C486-LIGADURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a injeção** — "injection".

> A enfermeira deu-me uma injeção no braço.
>
> *The nurse gave me an injection in the arm.*

Before AO90 spelled *injecção* in Portugal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*injeção* is built on *injetar* "to inject", a learned borrowing from Latin *iniectāre* (EtymDB), from *iacere* "to throw".
