Gujarati, chapter 217: Wind and dust.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હવા (havā) — air.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for temperature, then the Gujarati for degree.

You'll want to know: હવા.
હવા — havā — "air".
સવારે હવા ઠંડી હશે. — savāre havā ṭhanḍī haśe — "The air will be cool in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
હવા (havā) is a loan from Arabic hawā', "air", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is h-w-y (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ફૂંકાવું (phū̃kāvũ) — to blow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for degree, then the Gujarati for air.

You'll want to know: ફૂંકાવું.
ફૂંકાવું — phū̃kāvũ — "to blow".
રાત્રે ઠંડો પવન ફૂંકાશે. — rātre ṭhanḍo pavan phū̃kāśe — "A cold wind will blow at night."

The word, taken apart.
ફૂંકાવું (phū̃kāvũ) is built on phū̃kvũ, "to blow", with the -ā- that makes a verb happen by itself, as wind does. CDIAL lists phū̃kvũ under phūtka, "blowing", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9102).

Lesson 3 of 5.
આંધી (ā̃dhī) — dust storm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for air, then the Gujarati for to blow.

You'll want to know: આંધી.
આંધી — ā̃dhī — "dust storm".
બપોરે રણમાં આંધી આવશે. — bapore raṇmā̃ ā̃dhī āvśe — "A dust storm will come in the desert at midday."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આંધી (ā̃dhī) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "duststorm", under Sanskrit andhikā, "night", built on andha, "blind", so the storm is one that brings darkness (CDIAL 390).

Lesson 4 of 5.
વંટોળ (vanṭoḷ) — whirlwind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to blow, then the Gujarati for dust storm.

You'll want to know: વંટોળ.
વંટોળ — vanṭoḷ — "whirlwind".
ઉનાળામાં વંટોળ આવશે. — unāḷāmā̃ vanṭoḷ āvśe — "Whirlwinds will come in summer."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of વંટોળ (vanṭoḷ) is not settled. CDIAL has Sanskrit vātālī, "whirlwind", built on vāta, "wind", but lists no Gujarati form under it (CDIAL 11500).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ફેલાવું (phelāvũ) — to spread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for dust storm, then the Gujarati for whirlwind.

You'll want to know: ફેલાવું.
ફેલાવું — phelāvũ — "to spread".
પવનથી ધૂળ ગામમાં ફેલાશે. — pavanthī dhūḷ gāmmā̃ phelāśe — "With the wind, dust will spread through the village."

The word, taken apart.
ફેલાવું (phelāvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists Gujarati phelvũ and phelāvvũ, beside Hindi phailnā, "to spread", under Sanskrit prathita, "spread out", extended with -ll- (CDIAL 8651).
