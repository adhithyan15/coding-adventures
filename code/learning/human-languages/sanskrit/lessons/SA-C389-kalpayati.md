## You'll want to know: कल्पयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C389-SHAKYA389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्पयति** — *kalpayati* — "imagines; supposes".

> यदि बालः एकाकी, तर्हि सः मित्राणि कल्पयति।
>
> *yadi bālaḥ ekākī, tarhi saḥ mitrāṇi kalpayati.*
>
> If the child is alone, he imagines friends.

Past (he …): सः नूतनं जगत् कल्पितवान्, *saḥ nūtanaṃ jagat kalpitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्पयति** (*kalpayati*) comes from the root √kḷp, "to be fit", in its causative form; MW gives the senses "to frame, to form, to invent" and "to imagine" (MW).
