## You'll want to know: c'est pourquoi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C347-PUISQUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**c'est pourquoi** — "that is why".

> Je n'ai pas d'argent ; c'est pourquoi je reste ici.
>
> *I have no money; that is why I am staying here.*

It brings in the result, after the cause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pourquoi* is built from *pour* "for", from Latin *prō*, and *quoi* "what", from Latin *quid* (Lewis & Short): for what.
