# Au café — the chapter, run cold

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-A-PREP-01, FR-LEX-DE-PREP-01, FR-GRAM-AU-AUX-01, FR-GRAM-DE-LA-01, FR-GRAM-DES-01, FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Two prepositions and four articles make eight combinations. Four melt
into one word and four do not. Say which four melt, and say the one rule that
decides it.

## Guided Practice: the four that melt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-AU-AUX-01, FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07, FR-GRAM-DES-01, FR-GRAM-DE-LA-01, FR-LEX-CAFE-01, FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-LEX-EAU-03, FR-LEX-AMI-01, FR-SOUND-LIAISON-10] -->

- [YOU SAY: *à* melting — "au café", "aux cafés"]
- [YOU SAY: *de* melting — "du pain", "des amis"]
- [YOU SAY: the four that stay apart — "à la maison", "à l'eau", "de la viande",
  "de l'eau"]
- [YOU SAY: the two liaisons — "aux amis", "des amis"]

## Guided Practice: the distant band — everything, placed and possessed
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-A-PREP-01, FR-LEX-DE-PREP-01, FR-GRAM-AU-AUX-01, FR-GRAM-DES-01, FR-GRAM-DE-LA-01, FR-LEX-CAFE-01, FR-ETYMON-CAFE-02, FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-ETYMON-PANIS-COMPAGNON-02, FR-LEX-VIN-05, FR-ETYMON-VINUM-VIN-06, FR-LEX-LAIT-05, FR-ETYMON-LAIT-06, FR-LEX-THE-03, FR-ETYMON-THE-04, FR-LEX-FROMAGE-01, FR-LEX-SEL-05, FR-LEX-SUCRE-07, FR-LEX-BEURRE-03, FR-LEX-OEUF-07, FR-LEX-MERE-03, FR-LEX-PERE-01, FR-LEX-FRERE-06, FR-LEX-SOEUR-08, FR-LEX-AMI-01, FR-LEX-ENFANT-06, FR-LEX-CHIEN-02, FR-LEX-CHAT-04, FR-LEX-HEURE-01, FR-GRAM-IL-EST-HEURES-03, FR-LEX-MIDI-05, FR-LEX-MINUIT-07, FR-LEX-ILYA-01, FR-GRAM-CEST-01, FR-LEX-ICI-01, FR-LEX-LABAS-03] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Every noun the book owns, put in somebody's hands and put somewhere.

- [YOU SAY: the table, possessed — "le pain de Marie, le vin de Paris, le café
  d'Anne"]
- [YOU SAY: the table, in the plural — "des œufs, des cafés, du sel, du sucre,
  du beurre"]
- [YOU SAY: the household, possessed — "la mère de Marie, le frère d'Anne, la
  sœur de Paul"]
- [YOU SAY: the animals — "le chien de l'enfant, le chat de l'ami"]
- [YOU SAY: the clock, at last with a preposition — "à midi", "à minuit", "à
  trois heures"]
- [YOU SAY: put it all together — "Il y a des cafés là-bas. C'est le café de
  Marie. Je suis au café."]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-AU-AUX-01, FR-GRAM-DES-01, FR-GRAM-DE-LA-01, FR-LEX-A-PREP-01, FR-LEX-DE-PREP-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Which two articles contract, and which two never do? (***Le*** and
***les*** contract; ***la*** and ***l'*** do not.) Name the four melted words.
(***Au, aux, du, des***.) How does French say "Marie's bread"? (**Le pain de
Marie**.) And what does English have that French does not need? (**An
apostrophe.**)
