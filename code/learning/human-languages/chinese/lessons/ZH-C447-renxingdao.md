## You'll want to know: rénxíngdào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C447-WALK447-05]; assesses=[] -->

*rénxíngdào* — "pavement; sidewalk".

> *qǐng zǒu rénxíngdào bié zǒu mǎlù* — Please keep to the pavement and stay off the road.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rénxíngdào* is built from 人 'people', *xíng* 'to walk' and 道 'a way', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyin*, *haeng* and *dawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
