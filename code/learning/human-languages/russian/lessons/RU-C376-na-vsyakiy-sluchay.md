## You'll want to know: на всякий случай
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C376-ISKLYUCHENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**на всякий случай** (*na vsyákiy slúchay*) — "in case, to be on the safe side".

> Я возьму зонтик на всякий случай.
>
> *Ya vozmú zóntik na vsyákiy slúchay.*
>
> I will take an umbrella, to be on the safe side.

A fixed phrase; *всякий* means any.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**на всякий случай** is "for any case": *всякий* "any" and *случай* "a case", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*sŭlučajĭ* (EtymDB).
