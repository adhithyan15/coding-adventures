## You'll want to know: el marido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C549-CASADO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el marido** — "the husband".

> Su marido trabaja en un hospital.
>
> *Her husband works in a hospital.*

*El esposo* means the same, a little more formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marido* is inherited from Latin *marītus* 'a husband' (EtymDB).
