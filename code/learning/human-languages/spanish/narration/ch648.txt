Spanish, chapter 648: Dónde dejar el coche.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el parquímetro — the parking meter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the pedestrian, then the Spanish for the roundabout.

You'll want to know: el parquímetro.
el parquímetro — "the parking meter".
Pon dinero en el parquímetro. Put money in the parking meter.
You pay at the machine and leave the ticket in the car.

The word, taken apart.
parquímetro is built on parking, borrowed from English (EtymDB), with -metro, from Greek métron "measure".

For a boat, a horse or a small caravan.

remolque is built on remolcar "to tow", from a Vulgar Latin verb (EtymDB) built on Latin remulcum "a tow-rope" (Lewis & Short).

El carril bici — the cycle lane.

The word, taken apart.
carril is usually traced to carro "cart", from Latin carrus; it first named the rut that a cart wheel leaves on a road.

Lesson 5 of 5.
remolcar — to tow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the trailer, then the Spanish for the lane.

You'll want to know: remolcar.
remolcar — "to tow".
La grúa remolca el coche hasta el taller. The tow truck tows the car to the garage.
The c becomes qu before e: remolqué. El remolque is a trailer.

The word, taken apart.
remolcar comes from a Vulgar Latin verb (EtymDB) built on Latin remulcum "a tow-rope" (Lewis & Short), a word of seafaring that the Romans took from Greek.
