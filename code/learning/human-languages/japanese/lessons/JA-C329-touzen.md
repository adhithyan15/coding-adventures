## You'll want to know: とうぜん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C329-JOUSHIKI329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とうぜん** — *tōzen* — "naturally; as a matter of course".

> **まいにち れんしゅうしたので、とうぜん かちました。** — *mainichi renshū shita node, tōzen kachimashita* — "We practised every day, so naturally we won."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とうぜん** (*tōzen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tō*, "right" (Middle Chinese *tang*) and *zen*, "so" (*nyen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
