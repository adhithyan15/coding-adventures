## You'll want to know: kāilǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C467-PERSON467-04]; assesses=[] -->

*kāilǎng* — "cheerful and outgoing".

> *wǒ de péngyou lǐ tā zuì kāilǎng* — Of all my friends, she's the most cheerful.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāilǎng* is built from 开 'to open' and *lǎng* 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *langX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'bright'. The sense of a cheerful nature is not given there.
