Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रकाशक (prakāśak) — a publisher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to keep the beat, then the Marathi for to sway.

You'll want to know: प्रकाशक.
प्रकाशक — prakāśak — "a publisher".
प्रकाशकाने माझ्या मावशीचं पुस्तक छापलं. prakāśakāne mājhyā māvśīcaṁ pustak chāplaṁ. The publisher printed my aunt's book.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रकाशक (prakāśak) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prakāśaka, "bringing to light" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
दाद देणे (dād deṇe) — to applaud; to show appreciation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an exhibition, then the Marathi for a film director.

You'll want to know: दाद देणे.
दाद देणे — dād deṇe — "to applaud; to show appreciation".
गायिकेच्या गाण्याला लोकांनी मनापासून दाद दिली. gāyikecyā gāṇyālā lokānnī manāpāsūn dād dilī. People warmly applauded the singer's song.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दाद दिली, tyāne dād dilī.
What is praised takes -ला: गाण्याला दाद देणे (dād deṇe).

The word, taken apart.
दाद देणे (dād deṇe) is दाद, a loan from Persian dād, "justice, redress", with देणे: to give a performer their due.
