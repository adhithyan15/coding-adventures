## You'll want to know: اگنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C437-JHARI437-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اگنا** — *ugnā* — "to come up (of plants); to grow".

> بارش کے بعد کھیتوں میں گھاس اگی۔
>
> *bārish ke bād khetoṅ meṅ ghās ugī.*
>
> After the rain grass came up in the fields.

Past (it …): وہ پودا اگا, *voh paudā ugā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اگنا** (*ugnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ugānā*, "to cause to spring up", under *udgāti*, "rises, comes forth", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1954); **اگانا**, "to grow (crops)", is the partner for making things come up.
