## You'll want to know: அசல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C450-ASAL450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**அசல்** — *acal* — "the original; genuine".

> இது அசல் சான்றிதழ், நகல் அல்ல.
>
> *itu acal cāṉṟitaḻ, nakal alla.*
>
> This is the original certificate, not a copy.

The opposite is நகல், a copy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அசல்** (*acal*) is a loan from Arabic *aṣl*, "root, origin", through Hindustani.
