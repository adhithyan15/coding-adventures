## You'll want to know: lo streaming
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C530-ONLINE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lo streaming** — "streaming".

> Guardo i film in streaming sul computer.
>
> *I watch films by streaming on the computer.*

From English; *in streaming* — streamed online.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*streaming* was borrowed from English *streaming*, from *stream*, a Germanic word.
