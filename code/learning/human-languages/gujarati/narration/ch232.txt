Gujarati, chapter 232: Staying healthy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રોગ (rog) — disease, illness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for treatment, then the Gujarati for to be cured.

You'll want to know: રોગ.
રોગ — rog — "disease, illness".
શું આ રોગ ખરાબ છે? — śũ ā rog kharāb che? — "Is this disease bad?"
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રોગ (rog) is Sanskrit roga, "disease", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10829).

Lesson 3 of 5.
તંદુરસ્ત (tandurast) — healthy, fit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for disease, then the Gujarati for infection.

You'll want to know: તંદુરસ્ત.
તંદુરસ્ત — tandurast — "healthy, fit".
શું તમારો દીકરો તંદુરસ્ત છે? — śũ tamāro dīkro tandurast che? — "Is your son healthy?"
It does not change for gender.

The word, taken apart.
તંદુરસ્ત (tandurast) is a loan, usually traced to Persian tandurust, "healthy", built from tan, "body", and durust, "sound"; EtymDB lists the Persian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
કાળજી લેવી (kāḷjī levī) — to take care, to look out for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for infection, then the Gujarati for healthy.

You'll want to know: કાળજી લેવી.
કાળજી લેવી — kāḷjī levī — "to take care, to look out for".
શું તમે તબિયતની કાળજી લો છો? — śũ tame tabiyatnī kāḷjī lo cho? — "Do you take care of your health?"
Word for word, to take care: કાળજી, kāḷjī, and લેવું, levũ.

The word, taken apart.
કાળજી લેવી (kāḷjī levī) is built from kāḷjī, "care, anxiety", and levũ, "to take". CDIAL lists kāḷjī under Sanskrit kāleyaka, a part of the inner body near the liver, so care is felt in the heart (CDIAL 3103).

Lesson 5 of 5.
સાજું થવું (sājũ thavũ) — to get well, to recover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for healthy, then the Gujarati for to take care.

You'll want to know: સાજું થવું.
સાજું થવું — sājũ thavũ — "to get well, to recover".
શું દર્દી સાજો થયો? — śũ dardī sājo thayo? — "Did the patient get well?"
સાજું, sājũ, well, agrees with the person: સાજો, સાજી.

The word, taken apart.
સાજું થવું (sājũ thavũ) is built from sājũ, "sound, healthy", and thavũ, "to become". CDIAL lists sājũ under Sanskrit sajya, "strung (of a bow)", so to be well is to be ready again (CDIAL 13095).
