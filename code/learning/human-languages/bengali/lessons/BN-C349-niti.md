## You'll want to know: নীতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C349-PROTIBAD349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**নীতি** — *nīti* — "a policy; a principle".

> এই নীতি নিয়ে অনেক বিতর্ক হল।
>
> *ei nīti niye ônek bitôrkô hôlô.*
>
> There was a lot of debate about this policy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নীতি** (*nīti*) is Sanskrit *nīti*, guidance, wise conduct and policy (Monier-Williams). It is both a government's policy and a person's principle.
