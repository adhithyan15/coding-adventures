# ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ (nivṛtti) — retirement

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C518-NEMAKA518-02, KA-LEX-C518-NEMAKA518-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to dismiss, then the Kannada for unemployment.

## You'll want to know: ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C518-NEMAKA518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ** — *nivṛtti* — "retirement".

> ಅರವತ್ತನೇ ವಯಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅಪ್ಪ ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ ಪಡೆದರು.
>
> *aravattanē vayassinalli appa nivṛtti paḍedaru.*
>
> Father retired at the age of sixty.

ನಿವೃತ್ತ, retired.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ** (*nivṛtti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nivṛtti*, "returning; ceasing, abstaining" (Monier-Williams), from *ni-*, "back", and *vṛt*, "to turn".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
