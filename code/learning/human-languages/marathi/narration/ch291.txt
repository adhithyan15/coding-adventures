Marathi, chapter 291: A Lucky Escape.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निसटणे (nisaṭṇe) — to slip away; to escape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a prison, then the Marathi for a prisoner.

You'll want to know: निसटणे.
निसटणे — nisaṭṇe — "to slip away; to escape".
दार उघडं असतं तर मांजर बाहेर निसटलं असतं. dār ughḍaṁ astaṁ tar māñjar bāher nisaṭlaṁ astaṁ. If the door had been open, the cat would have slipped out.
Past (he …): तो निसटला, to nisaṭlā.
The place left takes -तून: खोलीतून निसटणे (nisaṭṇe), to slip out of a room.

The word, taken apart.
निसटणे (nisaṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi nisaṭṇe, 'to slip away', under niḥsṛṣṭa, 'sent forth' (CDIAL 7132).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सुटका (suṭkā) — a rescue; a release.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a prisoner, then the Marathi for to slip away.

You'll want to know: सुटका.
सुटका — suṭkā — "a rescue; a release".
तीन तासांनंतर मुलाची सुटका झाली. tīn tāsānnantar mulācī suṭkā jhālī. After three hours the child was rescued.
Feminine.
सुटका (suṭkā) होणे, to be rescued or set free; सुटका (suṭkā) करणे, to rescue.

The word, taken apart.
सुटका (suṭkā) is built on the stem suṭ-, 'to come loose, be set free': CDIAL lists Marathi suṭṇẽ, 'to get loose', and suṭī, 'remission', under kṣuṭyate, 'is released' (CDIAL 3707).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सुखरूप (sukhrūp) — safe and sound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to slip away, then the Marathi for a rescue.

You'll want to know: सुखरूप.
सुखरूप — sukhrūp — "safe and sound".
सगळे प्रवासी सुखरूप घरी पोहोचले. sagḷe pravāsī sukhrūp gharī pohocle. All the passengers reached home safe and sound.
सुखरूप (sukhrūp) keeps one form: तो सुखरूप (sukhrūp) आहे, ती सुखरूप (sukhrūp) आहे.

The word, taken apart.
सुखरूप (sukhrūp) is built from Sanskrit sukha, 'ease, well-being', and rūpa, 'form' (Monier-Williams): in a good state.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बेपत्ता (bepattā) — missing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rescue, then the Marathi for safe and sound.

You'll want to know: बेपत्ता.
बेपत्ता — bepattā — "missing".
तो मुलगा दोन दिवसांपासून बेपत्ता आहे. to mulgā don divsāmpāsūn bepattā āhe. That boy has been missing for two days.
बेपत्ता (bepattā) keeps one form: बेपत्ता (bepattā) मुलगी, बेपत्ता (bepattā) माणूस. पत्ता is an address: with no address, no trace.

The word, taken apart.
बेपत्ता (bepattā) is built from the Persian prefix be-, 'without', and pattā, 'an address, a trace'; the origin of pattā itself is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
धुंडाळणे (dhuṇḍāḷṇe) — to comb through; to search high and low.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for safe and sound, then the Marathi for missing.

You'll want to know: धुंडाळणे.
धुंडाळणे — dhuṇḍāḷṇe — "to comb through; to search high and low".
आम्ही हरवलेल्या मुलासाठी सगळं गाव धुंडाळलं. āmhī haravlelyā mulāsāṭhī sagḷaṁ gāv dhuṇḍāḷlaṁ. We searched the whole village for the lost child.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने धुंडाळलं, tyāne dhuṇḍāḷlaṁ.
The place searched is the object: घर धुंडाळणे (dhuṇḍāḷṇe), to search the whole house.

The word, taken apart.
धुंडाळणे (dhuṇḍāḷṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi dhuṇḍāḷṇe, 'to search', under dhūṇḍh, 'seek' (CDIAL 6839).
