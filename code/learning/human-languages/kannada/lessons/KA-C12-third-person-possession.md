# ಅವನ, ಅವಳ, ಅವರ — whose family member?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-PRESENT-THIRD-05, KA-GRAMMAR-C12-THIRD-PERSON-03, KA-ETYMON-C12-KUTUMBA-01, KA-ETYMON-C12-KUTUMBA-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say he, she and they/respectful he or she. Then say father, mother,
older brother and older sister. Now join each person to a family word.

## Grammar Lens: remove the final pronoun ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-THIRD-PERSON-POSSESSIVE-06]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-THIRD-PERSON-03, KA-ETYMON-C12-KUTUMBA-01, KA-ETYMON-C12-KUTUMBA-02] -->

- **ಅವನ ಅಪ್ಪ** (*avana appa*) — his father
- **ಅವಳ ಅಮ್ಮ** (*avaḷa amma*) — her mother
- **ಅವರ ಅಕ್ಕ** (*avara akka*) — their older sister, or a respected person's older sister

For this small set, hear the pair: **ಅವನು → ಅವನ**, **ಅವಳು → ಅವಳ**,
**ಅವರು → ಅವರ**. This lesson teaches those three possessive forms as a set; it
does not yet turn the ending into a rule for every noun.
