## You'll want to know: स्वयम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C543-HATHA543-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयम्** — *svayam* — "by oneself".

> यदि त्वं स्वयं पठसि, तर्हि त्वं शीघ्रं शिक्षसे।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ svayaṃ paṭhasi, tarhi tvaṃ śīghraṃ śikṣase.*
>
> If you study by yourself, you learn fast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयम्** (*svayam*) probably comes from an old nominative of *sva*, "own", as MW takes it; MW gives *svayam* as self, by oneself, of one's own accord (MW).
