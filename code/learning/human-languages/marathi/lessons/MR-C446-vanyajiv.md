## You'll want to know: वन्यजीव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C446-ABHAYARANYA446-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वन्यजीव** — *vanyajīv* — "wildlife".

> या जंगलात खूप वन्यजीव आहेत.
>
> *yā jaṅgalāt khūp vanyajīv āhet.*
>
> There is a lot of wildlife in this forest.

Masculine.

A formal word for the wild animals and birds of a region.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वन्यजीव** (*vanyajīv*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *vanya*, "growing or living in a forest", and *jīva*, "a living being" (Monier-Williams).
