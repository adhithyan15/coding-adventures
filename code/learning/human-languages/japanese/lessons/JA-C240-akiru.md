## You'll want to know: あきる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あきる** — *akiru* — "to get tired of; to lose interest".

> **いもうとは にんぎょうに あきました。** — *imōto wa ningyō ni akimashita* — "My younger sister got tired of her dolls."

Polite **あきます**, *akimasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あきる** (*akiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
