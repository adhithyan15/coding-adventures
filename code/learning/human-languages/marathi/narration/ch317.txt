Marathi, chapter 317: Gifts and Blessings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आहेर (āher) — a wedding gift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a wedding procession, then the Marathi for a marquee.

You'll want to know: आहेर.
आहेर — āher — "a wedding gift".
मामांनी नवरीला साडीचा आहेर दिला. māmānnī navrīlā sāḍīcā āher dilā. Uncle gave the bride a sari as a wedding gift.
Masculine.
आहेर (āher) करणे or देणे, to give the customary gift.

The word, taken apart.
आहेर (āher) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi āher, 'presents to a family celebrating a marriage', under Sanskrit abhihāra, 'a bringing near' (CDIAL 534).

Lesson 2 of 5.
आशीर्वाद (āśīrvād) — a blessing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a marquee, then the Marathi for a wedding gift.

You'll want to know: आशीर्वाद.
आशीर्वाद — āśīrvād — "a blessing".
लग्नानंतर त्यांनी आजीचे आशीर्वाद घेतले. lagnānantar tyānnī ājīce āśīrvād ghetle. After the wedding they took Grandma's blessings.
Masculine.
आशीर्वाद (āśīrvād) देणे, to bless; आशीर्वाद (āśīrvād) घेणे, to seek a blessing.

The word, taken apart.
आशीर्वाद (āśīrvād) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit āśīrvāda, 'a benediction' (Monier-Williams): āśis, 'a wish, a blessing', and vāda, 'a saying'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अक्षता (akṣatā) — blessing rice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a wedding gift, then the Marathi for a blessing.

You'll want to know: अक्षता.
अक्षता — akṣatā — "blessing rice".
पाहुण्यांनी नवरा नवरीवर अक्षता टाकल्या. pāhuṇyānnī navrā navrīvar akṣatā ṭākalyā. The guests showered blessing rice over the bride and groom.
Feminine.
A feminine plural: whole grains of rice, coloured with turmeric or kumkum, showered on a couple as a blessing or offered in worship.

The word, taken apart.
अक्षता (akṣatā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit akṣata, 'unbroken; unhusked grain' (Monier-Williams): the grains are kept whole.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सनई (sanaī) — a shehnai.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a blessing, then the Marathi for blessing rice.

You'll want to know: सनई.
सनई — sanaī — "a shehnai".
लग्नघरात सकाळपासून सनई वाजत होती. lagnagharāt sakāḷpāsūn sanaī vājat hotī. The shehnai had been playing in the wedding house since morning.
Feminine.
A reed pipe played at weddings and festivals.

The word, taken apart.
सनई (sanaī) names the reed pipe of weddings. It is usually traced to Persian, to surnāy, 'a festive pipe', or to šahnāy, 'a royal pipe'; which one, and by what path, is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उखाणा (ukhāṇā) — a rhyming couplet; a riddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for blessing rice, then the Marathi for a shehnai.

You'll want to know: उखाणा.
उखाणा — ukhāṇā — "a rhyming couplet; a riddle".
लग्नात नवरीने एक सुंदर उखाणा घेतला. lagnāt navrīne ek sundar ukhāṇā ghetlā. At the wedding the bride recited a lovely rhyming couplet.
Masculine.
उखाणा (ukhāṇā) घेणे, to recite one: at a wedding the bride, and often the groom, weaves the spouse's name into a short rhyme.

The word, taken apart.
उखाणा (ukhāṇā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi ukhāṇā, 'a riddle', under Sanskrit upākhyāna, 'a subordinate story, an episode' (CDIAL 2289).
