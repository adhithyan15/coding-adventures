## You'll want to know: निवर्तते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C412-VIRAMA412-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निवर्तते** — *nivartate* — "turns round; turns back".

> वर्षायां पथिकः मार्गात् निवर्तते।
>
> *varṣāyāṃ pathikaḥ mārgāt nivartate.*
>
> In the rain the wayfarer turns back from the road.

Past (he …): सः मार्गात् निवृत्तवान्, *saḥ mārgāt nivṛttavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवर्तते** (*nivartate*) is built from *ni*, "back", and the root √vṛt, "to turn", which also gives *vartate*, "is present"; MW gives *ni-vṛt* as to turn back, with the participle *nivṛtta*, "turned back, returned" (MW).
