## You'll want to know: der Untergang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C320-MITTELALTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Untergang** — "the downfall, the sinking".

> Der Untergang des Schiffes war eine Katastrophe.
>
> *The sinking of the ship was a disaster.*

Plural: *die Untergänge*. *Der Sonnenuntergang* — sunset.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Untergang* is built on *untergehen* "to go down", from *unter* "under" and *Gang* "a going", from the root of *gehen* "to go" (EtymDB).
