## You'll want to know: सांस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C252-HAD252-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सांस** — *sā̃s* — "breath".

> लांबी सांस लो।
>
> *lā̃bī sā̃s lo.*
>
> Take a long breath.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सांस** (*sā̃s*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvāsa*, "breath"; CDIAL lists Marwari *sā̃so*, "panting", and Hindi *sā̃s*, "breath", under it (CDIAL 12769).
