Lesson 1 of 6.
пароль (paról) — a password.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a web browser, then the Russian for a search engine.

You'll want to know: пароль.
пароль (paról) — "a password".
Я забыл пароль от почты. Ya zabýl paról ot póchty. I have forgotten my email password.
Masculine. Пароль от + genitive — the password for.

The word, taken apart.
пароль (paról) was borrowed from French parole "a word, a word of honour" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin parabola "a comparison" (Lewis & Short), later "a saying": first the word a sentry asks for.

Lesson 2 of 6.
безопасность (bezopásnost) — safety, security.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a search engine, then the Russian for a password.

You'll want to know: безопасность.
безопасность (bezopásnost) — "safety, security".
Безопасность детей — главное. Bezopásnost detéy — glávnoye. Children’s safety comes first.
Feminine. В безопасности — safe. Built on опасный, dangerous.

The word, taken apart.
безопасность (bezopásnost) is built from без "without" and опасность "danger", from опасный, which is built on опасаться "to be wary", from пасти "to guard".

Lesson 4 of 6.
взламывать (vzlámyvat) — to break into, to hack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for safety, then the Russian for a hacker.

You'll want to know: взламывать.
взламывать (vzlámyvat) — "to break into, to hack".
Кто-то взломал мою почту. Kto-to vzlomál moyú póchtu. Someone has hacked my email.
Imperfective; perfective взломать. Of doors and of accounts alike.

The word, taken apart.
взламывать (vzlámyvat) is built from вз- and ломать "to break", inherited from Proto-Slavic lomiti; the о turns to а in the imperfective.

Lesson 5 of 6.
вирус (vírus) — a virus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a hacker, then the Russian for to break into.

You'll want to know: вирус.
вирус (vírus) — "a virus".
На компьютере вирус. Na kompyútere vírus. There is a virus on the computer.
Masculine. For computers and for illness alike.

The word, taken apart.
вирус (vírus) is usually traced to Latin vīrus "slime, poison" (Lewis & Short); the computer sense came through English.
