## You'll want to know: मैदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C204-OUT204-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मैदान** — *maidān* — "an open ground".

> मैदान स्कूल कनै है।
>
> *maidān skūl kanai hai.*
>
> The open ground is near the school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मैदान** (*maidān*) is usually traced to Persian *maidān*, "an open plain".
