# અમે ગુજરાતી બોલીએ છીએ — the present for we, and for you

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01, GU-SCRIPT-C145-O-01, GU-PRON-AME-01, GU-PRON-AAPNE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *they speak Gujarati*. Then the two words for *we*: **અમે**, which
leaves the listener out, and **આપણે**, which takes the listener in.

- [YOU RECALL: *teo gujarātī bole che*; *ame*, *āpṇe*]
- [YOU WRITE: **ઓ** once from memory]

## Grammar Lens: we
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-PRESENT-IE-01]; assesses=[GU-PRON-AME-01, GU-PRON-AAPNE-01] -->

> **અમે ગુજરાતી બોલીએ છીએ.** — *ame gujarātī bolīe chīe.* — We speak Gujarati.

The ending is **-ીએ**, and the copula has a form you have not met before:
**છીએ** (*chīe*). Both finish on the same **-ીએ**, so the sentence chimes.
**આપણે** takes the same pair: **આપણે ગુજરાતી બોલીએ છીએ**, *we (you and I) speak
Gujarati*.

## You'll want to know: respectful you
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-PRESENT-O-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-TUTAME-01, GU-CONCEPT-C03-TAMEKEMCHHO-01] -->

**તમે કેમ છો?** has carried **છો** since the second day. The verb beside **તમે**
takes the matching **-ો**:

> **તમે ગુજરાતી બોલો છો.** — *tame gujarātī bolo cho.* — You speak Gujarati.

The present endings for one person:

| who | verb | copula |
|---|---|---|
| હું | બોલું | છું |
| તું, તે | બોલે | છે |

And for more than one, or for respect:

| who | verb | copula |
|---|---|---|
| અમે, આપણે | બોલીએ | છીએ |
| તમે | બોલો | છો |
| તેઓ | બોલે | છે |
