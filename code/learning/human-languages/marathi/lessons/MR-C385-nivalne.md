## You'll want to know: निवळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C385-CHIGHALNE385-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निवळणे** — *nivaḷṇe* — "to calm down; to clear".

> दोन दिवसांनी गावातील तणाव निवळला.
>
> *don divsānnī gāvātīl taṇāv nivaḷlā.*
>
> After two days the tension in the village eased.

Past (he …): तो निवळला, *to nivaḷlā*.

Of muddy water as well: पाणी निवळलं, the water cleared.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवळणे** (*nivaḷṇe*) is a verb built on Marathi *nivaḷ*, "pure, clear", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *nirmala*, "spotless, clean" (CDIAL 7366).
