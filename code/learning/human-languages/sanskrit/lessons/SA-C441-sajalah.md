## You'll want to know: सजलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C441-DURDINA441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सजलः** — *sajalaḥ* — "rain-laden; watery".

> सजलम् अभ्रं पर्वतं प्रति गच्छति।
>
> *sajalam abhraṃ parvataṃ prati gacchati.*
>
> A rain-laden cloud moves towards the mountain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सजलः** (*sajalaḥ*) is built from *sa*, "with", and *jala*, "water"; MW gives *sajala* as containing water, watery, wet (MW).
