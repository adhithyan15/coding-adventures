## You'll want to know: પહોંચી વળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C380-PURTU380-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પહોંચી વળવું** — *pahõcī vaḷvũ* — "to cope".

> જો બધા મદદ કરશે તો આપણે આ કામને પહોંચી વળીશું.
>
> *jo badhā madad karśe to āpaṇe ā kāmne pahõcī vaḷīśũ.*
>
> If everyone helps, we will cope with this work.

Past (he …): તે બધી મુશ્કેલીઓને પહોંચી વળ્યો, *te badhī muśkelīone pahõcī vaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પહોંચી વળવું** (*pahõcī vaḷvũ*) is built from **પહોંચવું** (*pahõcvũ*), "to reach" (CDIAL 8716), and **વળવું** (*vaḷvũ*), "to turn" (CDIAL 11405): to reach a task and turn it round.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
