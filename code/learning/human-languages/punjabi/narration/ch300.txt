Punjabi, chapter 300: Crops of the Year.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਫ਼ਸਲ (fasal) — a crop; a harvest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for brackish, then the Punjabi for beautiful.

You'll want to know: ਫ਼ਸਲ.
ਫ਼ਸਲ — fasal — "a crop; a harvest".
ਅਗਲੇ ਸਾਲ ਫ਼ਸਲ ਚੰਗੀ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ। agle sāl fasal chaṅgī hovegī. Next year the crop will be good.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਫ਼ਸਲ (fasal) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic fasl, "season"; its sense here, "crop", is a shift from that.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ (hāṛhī) — the spring harvest; the winter-sown crop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for beautiful, then the Punjabi for a crop.

You'll want to know: ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ.
ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ — hāṛhī — "the spring harvest; the winter-sown crop".
ਕਣਕ ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ ਦੀ ਫ਼ਸਲ ਹੈ। kaṇak hāṛhī dī fasal hai. Wheat is a crop of the spring harvest.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਹਾੜ੍ਹੀ (hāṛhī) is inherited from Sanskrit āṣāḍhīya, "pertaining to the month Āṣāḍha", the month you know as ਹਾੜ; CDIAL lists Punjabi hāṛhī and hāṛī, "crops sown in winter", under it (CDIAL 1474).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the spring harvest, then the Punjabi for the autumn harvest.

You'll want to know: ਕਪਾਹ.

ਸਾਡੇ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਪਾਹ ਹੈ। sāḍe khet vich kapāh hai. There is cotton in our field.

The word, taken apart.
