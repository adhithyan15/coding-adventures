## You'll want to know: il carro attrezzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C410-GUASTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il carro attrezzi** — "the tow truck, the breakdown lorry".

> Abbiamo chiamato il carro attrezzi.
>
> *We called the tow truck.*

Also written *carroattrezzi*. *Gli attrezzi* are tools.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*carro attrezzi* is built from *carro* 'cart', inherited from Latin *carrus*, a word of Gaulish origin (EtymDB), and *attrezzi* 'tools', whose origin is less clear.
