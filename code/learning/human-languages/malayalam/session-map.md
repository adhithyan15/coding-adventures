# Session Map — Malayalam Chapter 1

Same spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

**There is no reading course.** Malayalam is learned *through* the words: each
lesson's *"The letters in this word"* section introduces exactly the letters
that word needs. By the end of Chapter 1 you have met the inherent vowel, the
common vowel signs, the chandrakkala and conjuncts, the anusvāram, and the
independent vowels — all in the service of real greetings.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | New letters / reading idea | Origin / family thread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | namaskaram | നമസ്കാരം | inherent *a*, ā-sign, chandrakkala, സ്ക conjunct, anusvāram ം | Sanskrit loan (vs. Tamil's native *vaṇakkam*) |
| 2 | nandi | നന്ദി | ന്ദ conjunct, i-sign | native (= Tamil *naṉṟi*), root *nal* |
| 3 | athe | അതെ | independent അ, e-sign (before consonant) | native; yes as "that [is so]" |
| 4 | illa | ഇല്ല | independent ഇ, ല്ല conjunct | native (root *il* = Tamil *illai*); negation by verb |
| 5 | sari | ശരി | ശ (śa), ri-sign | native; the family word *sari* (Sanskrit ശ) |
| 6 | practice | (recap) | read all five | the *pōyi varām* farewell (≈ Tamil *pōy varugiṟēṉ*) |
