## You'll want to know: wěiqu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C459-MIND459-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wěiqu* — "wronged; hurt by unfair blame".

> *biérén bù xiāngxìn wǒ wǒ gǎnjué hěn wěiqu* — Nobody believes me, and I feel really hard done by.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wěiqu* is built from *wěi* and *qū*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jweX* and *khjut* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses both as bent; the sense of a person feeling wronged is not given there.
