## You'll want to know: delegar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C359-DEPARTAMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**delegar** — "to delegate".

> A chefe delegou o trabalho em dois colegas.
>
> *The boss delegated the work to two colleagues.*

*Delegar em alguém* — to delegate to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delegar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēlēgāre* "to send, to assign, to delegate" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *dē* and *lēgāre* "to send, to depute".
