## You'll want to know: libertar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C298-RECURSO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**libertar** — "to set free, to release".

> Libertaram o recluso ao fim de dez anos.
>
> *They released the inmate after ten years.*

*Libertar um refém* — to free a hostage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*libertar* was borrowed from Medieval Latin *lībertāre* (EtymDB), built on *lībertās* "freedom", from *līber* "free".
