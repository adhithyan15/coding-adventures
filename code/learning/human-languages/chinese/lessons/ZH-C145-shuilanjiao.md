## You'll want to know: shuì lǎnjiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C145-LATE145-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shuì lǎnjiào* — "to sleep in; to lie in".

> *jīntiān shì xīngqītiān wǒ xiǎng shuì lǎnjiào* — It's Sunday, so I want a lie-in.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuì lǎnjiào* is built from *shuì* 'to sleep', *lǎn* 'lazy' and *jiào* 'a sleep', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyweH*, *lanX* and *kaewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the last with the word *shuìjiào* itself.
