# ജീവഹാനി (jīvahāni) — loss of life

## You'll want to know: ജീവഹാനി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C378-ABHAYAM378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ജീവഹാനി** — *jīvahāni* — "loss of life".

> ഭാഗ്യത്തിന് അപകടത്തിൽ ജീവഹാനി ഉണ്ടായില്ല.
>
> *bhāgyattinŭ apakaṭattil jīvahāni uṇṭāyilla.*
>
> Luckily there was no loss of life in the accident.

A news word; in speech people say ആരും മരിച്ചില്ല, nobody died.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ജീവഹാനി** (*jīvahāni*) is built from Sanskrit *jīva*, "life" (CDIAL 5239), and *hāni*, "abandonment, loss" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
