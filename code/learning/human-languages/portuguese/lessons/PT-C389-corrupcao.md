## You'll want to know: a corrupção
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C389-CRIME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a corrupção** — "the corruption".

> Há corrupção em muitos países.
>
> *There is corruption in many countries.*

*Corrupto* — corrupt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*corrupção* is a learned borrowing from Latin *corruptiō* "a corrupting, a spoiling" (EtymDB), from *corrumpere*, built on *rumpere* "to break" (Lewis & Short).
