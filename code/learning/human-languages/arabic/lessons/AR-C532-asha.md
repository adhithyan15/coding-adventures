## You'll want to know: عاش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C532-HAYAT532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عاش** — *ʿāsha* — "to lead a life".

> عاش جدي حياة بسيطة في الريف.
>
> *ʿāsha jaddī ḥayāh basīṭa fī ar-rīf.*
>
> My grandfather lived a simple life in the countryside.

Present: يعيش, *yaʿīsh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عاش** (*ʿāsha*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-y-sh*, "to live" (Lane). The noun ʿaysh is life and, in Egypt, bread.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
