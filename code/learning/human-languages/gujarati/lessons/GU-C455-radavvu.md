# રડાવવું (raḍāvvũ) — to make someone cry

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for anguish, then the Gujarati for a sigh.

## You'll want to know: રડાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C455-GAMGIN455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રડાવવું** — *raḍāvvũ* — "to make someone cry".

> આ ફિલ્મ બધાને રડાવે છે.
>
> *ā philm badhāne raḍāve che.*
>
> This film makes everyone cry.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને રડાવ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne raḍāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રડાવવું** (*raḍāvvũ*) is the causative of **રડવું** (*raḍvũ*), "to cry", which is inherited from Sanskrit *raṭati*, "cries, yells" (CDIAL 10590).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
