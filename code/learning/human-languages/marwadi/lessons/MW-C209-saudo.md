## You'll want to know: सौदो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C209-SHOP209-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सौदो** — *saudo* — "a deal; shopping".

> म्हैं बाजार मांय सौदो कियो।
>
> *mhaĩ bājār mā̃y saudo kiyo.*
>
> I did the shopping at the market.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौदो** (*saudo*) is usually traced to Persian *saudā*, "trade, a bargain".
