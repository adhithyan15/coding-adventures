Hindi, chapter 138: Garlic, Ginger, Tomato, Carrot, Cauliflower.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
लहसुन (lahsun) — garlic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a potato, then the Hindi for an onion.

You'll want to know: लहसुन.
लहसुन — lahsun — "garlic".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: lahsun]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lahsun, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say lahsun]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for a potato, then the Hindi for an onion, then say lahsun again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does लहसुन (lahsun) mean? ("Garlic".) Say it once more.
