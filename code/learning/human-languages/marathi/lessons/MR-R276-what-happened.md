# Review — what happened

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to Pune". (*kāl mī puṇyālā gelo*.) Then "she came" and "we sat". (*tī ālī*, *āmhī baslo*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-STEMS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-NOT-01, MR-LEX-SANDHYAKAL, MR-LEX-C89-FOOD89-02, MR-LEX-C93-HOME93-03, MR-LEX-C113-TOWN113-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I came home in the evening, and then I slept.]
- [YOU SAY: Meera ate a mango. Arun read a book.]
- [YOU SAY: What did you do yesterday? I was at home.]
- [YOU SAY: There was a big shop in the village.]
- [YOU SAY: I didn't go to school. He was not at home either.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-NOT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which past follows the one who went? (**गेलो**, **आली**: no object.) Which follows the thing? (The past with an object: **मीराने आंबा खाल्ला**.) "There was"? (**होता**, **होती**, **होतं**.) "Was not"? (**नव्हता**.)
