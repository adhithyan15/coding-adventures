Bengali, chapter 66: Mountain, Forest, River, Pond, Stone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পাহাড় (pāhāṛ) — a mountain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a flower, then the Bengali for a leaf.

You'll want to know: পাহাড়.
পাহাড় — pāhāṛ — "a mountain".

The word, taken apart.
পাহাড় (pāhāṛ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pāhāḍa, "rock, hill", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 8141). CDIAL thinks it probably connected with Sanskrit pāṣāṇa, "stone".

পুকুর (pukur) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pauṣkara, "pertaining to the blue lotus" (CDIAL 8425). It is built on puṣkara, "blue lotus".
