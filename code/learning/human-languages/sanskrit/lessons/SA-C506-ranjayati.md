## You'll want to know: रञ्जयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C506-VAYATI506-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रञ्जयति** — *rañjayati* — "dyes".

> सः शाटिकां हरिद्रया रञ्जयति।
>
> *saḥ śāṭikāṃ haridrayā rañjayati.*
>
> He dyes the sari with turmeric.

Past (he …): सः वस्त्रं रञ्जितवान्, *saḥ vastraṃ rañjitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रञ्जयति** (*rañjayati*) comes from the root √rañj, "to be coloured"; MW gives its causative *rañjayati*, to dye, to colour (MW).
