## You'll want to know: lo scoglio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C463-SCOGLI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scoglio** — "the rock (in the sea)".

> Prendiamo il sole sugli scogli.
>
> *We sunbathe on the rocks.*

Figuratively, an obstacle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scoglio* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *scoclus*, from Latin *scopulus* 'rock, crag' (EtymDB).
