## You'll want to know: sobrevivir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C526-RESCATE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sobrevivir** — "to survive".

> Todos sobrevivieron al accidente.
>
> *Everyone survived the accident.*

*Sobrevivir a algo* — to survive something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sobrevivir* comes from Latin *supervīvere* 'to outlive' (EtymDB), built on *super* 'over, beyond' and *vīvere* 'to live'.
