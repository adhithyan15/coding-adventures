## You'll want to know: fēstus, fēsta, fēstum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C333-FESTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**fēstus, fēsta, fēstum** — "festive, holiday".

> Diē fēstō nēmō in forō labōrābat.
>
> *On the holiday nobody was working in the forum.*

*Diēs fēstus*: a holiday, the opposite of a working day. English *feast* and *festival* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fēstus** belongs, says Lewis & Short, with *fēriae*, "holidays" (taught). English *feast* comes from it through French, and *festival* from its family.
