## You'll want to know: разменивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C455-OPLATA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**разменивать** (*razménivat*) — "to break (a note), to change (money into smaller notes)".

> В кассе не разменивают крупные деньги.
>
> *V kásse ne razménivayut krúpnyye déngi.*
>
> They do not change large notes at the till.

Imperfective; perfective *разменять*. *Разменять тысячу* — to break a thousand-rouble note.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разменивать* is built from *раз-* and *-менивать*, from *менять* 'to change', on *мена* 'an exchange', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *měna* (EtymDB).
