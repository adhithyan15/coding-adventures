## You'll want to know: આમંત્રણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C180-VISIT180-02]; assesses=[] -->

**આમંત્રણ** — *āmantraṇ* — "invitation".

> **હું તેમને આમંત્રણ આપીશ.** — *hũ temne āmantraṇ āpīś* — "I will invite them."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આમંત્રણ** (*āmantraṇ*) is Sanskrit *āmantraṇa*, "addressing, calling; invitation", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama.
