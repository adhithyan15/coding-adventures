# ਭਾਣਜਾ (bhāṇjā) — a sister's son

## You'll want to know: ਭਾਣਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C332-KIN332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਣਜਾ** — *bhāṇjā* — "a sister's son".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਭਾਣਜਾ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਆਇਆ।
>
> *merā bhāṇjā kall sāḍe ghar āiā.*
>
> My sister's son came to our house yesterday.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਣਜਾ** (*bhāṇjā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhāgineya*, "a sister's son"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhāṇjā*, "sister's son", under it (CDIAL 9433).
