Arabic, chapter 74: Bag, Money, Map, Lesson, Answer.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
حقيبة (ḥaqība) — a bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a sheet of paper, then the Arabic for a school.

You'll want to know: حقيبة.
حقيبة — ḥaqība — "a bag".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ḥaqība]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḥaqība, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ḥaqība]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a sheet of paper, then the Arabic for a school, then say ḥaqība again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does حقيبة (ḥaqība) mean? ("A bag".) Say it once more.
