## You'll want to know: ঠাঁই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C406-THAI406-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠাঁই** — *ṭhā̃i* — "a place to stay; shelter; room".

> রাতে গ্রামের একটা বাড়িতে আমরা ঠাঁই পেলাম।
>
> *rāte grāmer ekṭā bāṛite āmrā ṭhā̃i pelām.*
>
> At night we found shelter in a house in the village.

ঠাঁই নেই, there is no room: the bus was so full there was no ঠাঁই.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠাঁই** (*ṭhā̃i*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ṭhā̃i*, "a place", under Sanskrit *sthā́man*, "a station, a standing place" (CDIAL 13760), from *sthā*, to stand.
