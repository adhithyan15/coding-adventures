## You'll want to know: নোঙর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C396-MAJHI396-03]; assesses=[] -->

**নোঙর** — *nongôr* — "an anchor".

> বন্দরের কাছে জাহাজটা নোঙর ফেলল।
>
> *bôndôrer kāchhe jāhājṭā nongôr phellô.*
>
> The ship dropped anchor near the port.

নোঙর ফেলা, to drop anchor; নোঙর তোলা, to weigh anchor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নোঙর** (*nongôr*) is usually traced to Persian *langar*, "an anchor"; Bengali turned the first l into n.
