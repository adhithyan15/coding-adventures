## You'll want to know: das Feuerwerk
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C309-SCHMUECKEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Feuerwerk** — "the fireworks, the firework display".

> Um Mitternacht gab es ein großes Feuerwerk.
>
> *At midnight there was a big firework display.*

Plural: *die Feuerwerke*. One rocket is *eine Rakete*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Feuerwerk* joins *Feuer* "fire", a cousin of English *fire* (EtymDB), and *Werk* "work", both inherited Germanic words.
