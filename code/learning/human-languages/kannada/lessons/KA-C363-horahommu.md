# ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು (horahommu) — to emerge; to come out

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C363-SANJE363-02, KA-LEX-C363-SANJE363-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to rise, then the Kannada for to resound.

## You'll want to know: ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C363-SANJE363-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು** — *horahommu* — "to emerge; to come out".

> ಕತ್ತಲೆಯಿಂದ ಒಂದು ಬೆಳಕು ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮಿತು.
>
> *kattaleyinda ondu beḷaku horahommitu.*
>
> A light came out of the darkness.

Past (it …): ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮಿತು, *horahommitu*.

Also of results and talents: ಹೊಸ ಆಟಗಾರ ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮಿದನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು** (*horahommu*) is built on native *hora*, "outside" (DEDR 4333), and *hommu*, "to rise, to spring up, to come forth", which DEDR 4482 lists only with a query, so its source is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
