Arabic, chapter 522: Starting and stopping work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
باشر (bāshara) — to set to work, to start on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to promote, then the Arabic for to get tired.

You'll want to know: باشر.
باشر — bāshara — "to set to work, to start on".
باشر الموظف الجديد عمله يوم الأحد. bāshara al-muwaẓẓaf al-jadīd ʿamalahu yawm al-aḥad. The new employee started work on Sunday.
Present: يباشر, yubāshir.

The word, taken apart.
باشر (bāshara) is built on the native Arabic root b-sh-r, "the skin; to touch directly" (Lane). Form III bāshara is to put one's own hand to a task. Mubāshir, direct, which you know, is its participle.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: استقالة.
استقالة — istiqāla — "a resignation".
كتب الموظف استقالته وأعطاها للمدير. kataba al-muwaẓẓaf istiqālatahu wa-aʿṭāhā li-l-mudīr. The employee wrote his resignation and gave it to the manager.
The verb استقال, to resign, goes with it.

The word, taken apart.
استقالة (istiqāla) is built on the native Arabic root q-y-l, "to release from a bargain" (Lane). Istiqāla is asking to be released.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عاطل (ʿāṭil) — unemployed, out of work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a resignation, then the Arabic for retired.

You'll want to know: عاطل.
عاطل — ʿāṭil — "unemployed, out of work".
ابن عمي عاطل عن العمل. ibn ʿammī ʿāṭil ʿan al-ʿamal. My cousin is out of work.
The full phrase is عاطل (ʿāṭil) عن العمل.

The word, taken apart.
عاطل (ʿāṭil) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ṭ-l, "to be empty, without use" (Lane). Muʿaṭṭal, out of order, which you know, is from the same root.
