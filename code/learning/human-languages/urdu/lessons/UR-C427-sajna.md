## You'll want to know: سجنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C427-POSHAK427-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سجنا** — *sajnā* — "to look good (on someone); to be decorated".

> یہ پوشاک سارا پر سجتی ہے۔
>
> *yih poshāk sārā par sajtī hai.*
>
> This outfit suits Sara.

Past (it …): وہ کمرہ پھولوں سے سجا, *voh kamrā phūloṅ se sajā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سجنا** (*sajnā*) is probably related to Hindi *sājnā*, "to be prepared", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *sajjyate*, "is equipped" (CDIAL 13093); **سجانا**, "to decorate", is its partner.
