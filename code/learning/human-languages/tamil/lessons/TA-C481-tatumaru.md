# தடுமாறு (taṭumāṟu) — to falter; to fumble

## You'll want to know: தடுமாறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C481-KAL481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**தடுமாறு** — *taṭumāṟu* — "to falter; to fumble".

> மேடையில் பேசும்போது அவள் தடுமாறினாள்.
>
> *mēṭaiyil pēcumpōtu avaḷ taṭumāṟiṉāḷ.*
>
> She faltered while speaking on the stage.

Past (I …): தடுமாறினேன், *taṭumāṟiṉēṉ*.

Of feet, of words and of memory alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தடுமாறு** (*taṭumāṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3033), in an entry of words for halting and swaying.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
