Hindi, chapter 118: Boys, Girls, Husbands, Wives.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लड़का (laṛkā) — a boy; a son.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a brother's wife, then the Hindi for the in-laws' home.

You'll want to know: लड़का.
लड़का (laṛkā) — "a boy", and in a family, "a son".
बेटा is "son" as a family word; लड़का (laṛkā) is any boy.

What you've built.
A boy, beside the word for son you know.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: laṛkā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: laṛkā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ek laṛkā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for a brother's wife, then the Hindi for the in-laws' home, then say laṛkā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does लड़का (laṛkā) mean? ("A boy; a son".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.

पत्नी (patnī) — "a wife".
It is built on पति (pati), with the -ई that marks a woman. मेरी पत्नी (patnī) — "my wife".

Husband and wife, one built on the other.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रिश्तेदार (riśtedār) — a relative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a husband, then the Hindi for a wife.

You'll want to know: रिश्तेदार.
रिश्तेदार (riśtedār) — "a relative".
रिश्ता is "a relationship", and -दार is "one who has": a relative is one who has a tie to you. वह मेरा रिश्तेदार (riśtedār) है — "he is my relative".

What you've built.
Boy, girl, husband, wife, relative: the people an introduction reaches for.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does रिश्तेदार (riśtedār) mean? ("A relative".) Say it once more.
