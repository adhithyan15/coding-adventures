## You'll want to know: هدأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C448-DAJIJ448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**هدأ** — *hadaʾa* — "to calm down; to grow quiet".

> هدأ الشارع بعد منتصف الليل.
>
> *hadaʾa ash-shāriʿ baʿda muntaṣaf al-layl.*
>
> The street grew quiet after midnight.

Present: يهدأ, *yahdaʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هدأ** (*hadaʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-d-ʾ*, "to be still, quiet" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
