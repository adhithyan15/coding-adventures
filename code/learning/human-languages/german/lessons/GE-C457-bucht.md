## You'll want to know: die Bucht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C457-KUESTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Bucht** — "the bay, the cove".

> In der kleinen Bucht ist das Wasser ruhig und warm.
>
> *In the little bay the water is calm and warm.*

Plural: *die Buchten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bucht* came into the standard language from Low German *bucht* 'bend, curve', related to *biegen* 'to bend' (EtymDB lists Dutch *bocht* 'bend' as a cousin): the coast bends inward.
