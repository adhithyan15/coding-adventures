Tamil, chapter 108: Greetings People Actually Say.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
சாப்பிட்டீர்களா? (cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā?) — have you eaten?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a good journey, then the Tamil for come again.

You'll want to know: சாப்பிட்டீர்களா?
சாப்பிட்டீர்களா? (cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā?) — "have you eaten?".
Around a mealtime it is a greeting and a kindness, built on சாப்பிடு, "eat".

What you've built.
A greeting about food.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā?, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for a good journey, then the Tamil for come again, then say cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā? again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does சாப்பிட்டீர்களா? (cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā?) mean? ("Have you eaten?".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Hello and goodbye, the everyday way — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
You meet an old friend around lunchtime. Say two things.

Grammar Lens: written and spoken.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
spoken: நல்லா. written: நன்றாக.
spoken: இருக்கீங்க. written: இருக்கிறீர்கள்.
Spoken Tamil wears the written forms short. Both are right; they are for different places.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: ask a friend how they are, the spoken way; then what's new]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: at the door: shall we leave? see you later]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wish a traveller a good journey; ask them to come again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: take your leave formally]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I take my leave? (விடைபெறுகிறேன்.) Shall we leave? (கிளம்பலாமா?) See you later? (அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம்.) A good journey? (இனிய பயணம்.) Come again? (மீண்டும் வாருங்கள்.) Have you eaten? (சாப்பிட்டீர்களா? (cāppiṭṭīrkaḷā?)) Long time? (ரொம்ப நாளாச்சு (rompa nāḷāccu).) What's new? (என்ன விசேஷம்? (eṉṉa vicēṣam?)) Are you well? (நல்லா இருக்கீங்களா? (nallā irukkīṅkaḷā?)) How are you? (எப்படி இருக்கீங்க? (eppaṭi irukkīṅka?))
