# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "my bag is lost" and "one ticket, please". (*mama syūtaḥ naṣṭaḥ*, *ekaṃ yānapatraṃ dadātu*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-FEELING-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C282-FEELING-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C282-AMBITION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C282-AMBITION-02, SA-LEX-C282-UTSUKAH-01, SA-LEX-C37-COURTESY-03, SA-LEX-C157-QUAL157-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I am pleased today, because Mira has come.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira is tired, and Arun is afraid.]
- [YOU SAY: I like music.]
- [YOU SAY: I want to become a teacher.]
- [YOU SAY: My dream is that I will see the city. I am eager.]

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] **श्रान्ता**: a man or a woman? (A woman.) **भवितुम्** is the *-tum* form of which verb? (**भवति**.) Now the other way round: "I am eager", said by a woman? (**अहम् उत्सुका अस्मि**.)
