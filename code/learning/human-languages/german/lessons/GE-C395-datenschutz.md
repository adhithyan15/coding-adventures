## You'll want to know: der Datenschutz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C395-PASSWORT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Datenschutz** — "data protection, privacy".

> Wegen des Datenschutzes darf ich Ihnen die Nummer nicht geben.
>
> *Because of data protection I am not allowed to give you the number.*

No plural. *Die Daten* — the data.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Datenschutz* joins *Daten* "data", from Latin *datum* "something given" (EtymDB), to *Schutz* "protection", Middle High German *schuz* (EtymDB).
