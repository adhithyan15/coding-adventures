# Chinese pre-A1 mock 2 — examiner key and delivery script

This is the independent form-2 key; do not use form 1's cue order, card, or
answers. Keep it away from the learner and apply the
[form-2 rubric](mock-2-rubric.md). Record each original Chinese cue at a
natural, gentle pace (about 1–3 seconds), play it **twice**, and pause for
the response. Do not add pinyin or English to a Chinese audio cue.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->
Reading, 5 points each: **1 b** (I), **2 c** (not / no), **3 a** (good
morning), **4 b** (thank you), **5 a** (name).
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-pinyin-signs-and-doorway-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->
| item | cue (twice) | correct selection | taught source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **mà** (fourth tone; isolated syllable) | **a** | `ZH-C01-tones` |
| 7 | **早上好** (*zǎoshang hǎo*) | **c**, good morning | `ZH-C07-zaoshanghao` |
| 8 | **谢谢** (*xièxie*) | **a**, 不谢 | `ZH-C10-practice` |
| 9 | **再见** (*zàijiàn*) | **c**, two people leaving | `ZH-C06-zaijian` |
| 10 | **什么** (*shénme?*) | **b**, what? | `ZH-C09-shenme` |

Each correct selection earns 5 points. Do not improvise a longer exchange
that changes the stimulus window or gives away an answer.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-tones-and-doorway-turns -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->
Item 11 card: show **我** alone for ten seconds, then cover it. Expected
independent handwritten **我**, taught in `ZH-C08-wo`. Award up to 5 as the
rubric specifies; visible copying is not delayed recall.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-character-recall -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->
Item 12 audio: **好** (*hǎo*); expected handwritten pinyin **hǎo** with the
third-tone mark. Item 13 audio: **不** (*bù*); expected handwritten simplified
**不**. Both are taught (`ZH-C01-hao`, `ZH-C04-bu`). Speak each twice without
showing a transcript. Score the two 5-point items independently.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation-and-transcription -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
14. Sample: **谢谢** (thanks after being let through, `ZH-C10-xiexie`).
15. Sample: **你好** (returning a greeting, `ZH-C01-nihao`).

Accept another appropriate taught, independent Chinese response. Do not
show these examples during the sitting. Apply the form-2 rubric's 5-point
criteria to each response.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->
16. Examiner says **早上好**; expected **早上好** or another taught fitting
    morning greeting.
17. Examiner says **请** at a doorway; expected **谢谢**.
18. English support-language cue: “Please say who you are.” Expected
    **我是** (*wǒ shì*) plus the learner's own name. The name itself need not
    be a taught Chinese word.
19. Examiner says **再见**; expected **再见**.
20. Examiner says **谢** (*xiè*, fourth tone) without displaying text;
    candidate repeats **xiè** with an intelligible falling tone.

The examiner speaks each prompt once and may give one requested slow repeat.
Each response is one short turn of at most 15 seconds. Apply the separate
5-point analytic mark to every item and retain the recorded performance.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-tone-and-doorway-exchange -->
