## You'll want to know: あわてる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C263-KYUUKYUU263-04]; assesses=[] -->

**あわてる** — *awateru* — "to panic; to be flustered".

> **あわてたら、あぶないでしょう。** — *awatetara, abunai deshō* — "If you panicked, it would be dangerous."

Polite **あわてます**, *awatemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが あわてました。**, *chichi ga awatemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あわてる** (*awateru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
