## You'll want to know: వివాహం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C346-VIVAHAM346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వివాహం** — *vivāhaṁ* — "a marriage; a wedding".

> వాళ్ళ వివాహం గుడిలో జరిగింది.
>
> *vāḷḷa vivāhaṁ guḍilō jarigindi.*
>
> Their marriage took place in the temple.

A formal word; in speech పెళ్ళి is usual.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వివాహం** (*vivāhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vivāha*, "marriage" (CDIAL 11920).
