## You'll want to know: transformar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C572-CAMBIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**transformar** — "to transform".

> Transformaron la fábrica en un museo.
>
> *They turned the factory into a museum.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transformar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *trānsfōrmāre* 'to change in shape' (EtymDB), built on *trāns* 'across' and *forma* 'shape'.
