## You'll want to know: ēligō, ēligere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C384-SUFFRAGIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ēligō, ēligere** — "to pick out, to choose".

> Ex multīs hunc ēlēgimus.
>
> *Out of many we chose this one.*

*ēligō, ēligere, ēlēgī, ēlēctum*: *ē* + *legō*, to pick. English *elect* and *election*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ēligō* is built from *ē* "out" and *legō* "to pick, to gather" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *elect* comes from its participle *ēlēctus*.
