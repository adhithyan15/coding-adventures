## You'll want to know: قاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C330-SAFHA330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قاری** — *qārī* — "a reader".

> یہ کتاب ہر قاری کو پسند آئی۔
>
> *yih kitāb har qārī ko pasand āī.*
>
> Every reader liked this book.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قاری** (*qārī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qāriʾ*, "one who reads aloud"; in Urdu it is any reader of a book or a paper.
