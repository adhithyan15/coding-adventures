## You'll want to know: धँसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C303-BHUKAMP303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धँसना** — *dhãsnā* — "to cave in; to sink in".

> भूकंप में सड़क नीचे धँस गई।
>
> *bhūkamp meṁ saṛak nīce dhãs gaī.*
>
> In the earthquake the road caved in.

Past (it …): वह सड़क धँस गई, *vah saṛak dhãs gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धँसना** (*dhãsnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *dhvaṁsati*, "falls to pieces" (CDIAL 6896). Ground, a road or a foot in mud **धँसता है**: it goes down into what is under it.
