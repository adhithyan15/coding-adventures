## You'll want to know: quietschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C429-SCHIMMEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**quietschen** — "to squeak, to creak; to screech".

> Das alte Bett quietscht bei jeder Bewegung.
>
> *The old bed squeaks every time you move.*

*Die Reifen quietschen* — the tyres screech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quietschen* is a sound word that imitates a high, sharp squeak. EtymDB does not list it.
