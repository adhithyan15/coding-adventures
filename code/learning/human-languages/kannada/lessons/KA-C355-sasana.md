## You'll want to know: ಶಾಸನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C355-UTKHANANA355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಸನ** — *śāsana* — "an inscription; an edict".

> ಈ ಕಲ್ಲಿನ ಶಾಸನ ಸಾವಿರ ವರ್ಷ ಹಳೆಯದು.
>
> *ī kallina śāsana sāvira varṣa haḷeyadu.*
>
> This stone inscription is a thousand years old.

Plural: ಶಾಸನಗಳು, *śāsanagaḷu*.

Old kings cut their orders and gifts into stone: ಶಿಲಾಶಾಸನ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಸನ** (*śāsana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śāsana*, "a command", and "a royal edict" (CDIAL 12420), from *śās*, "to order".
