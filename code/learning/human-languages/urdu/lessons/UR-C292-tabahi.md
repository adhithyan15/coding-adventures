## You'll want to know: تباہی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C292-TUFAN292-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تباہی** — *tabāhī* — "destruction; havoc".

> طوفان سے ہر جگہ تباہی ہوئی۔
>
> *tūfān se har jagah tabāhī huī.*
>
> The storm caused destruction everywhere.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تباہی** (*tabāhī*) is usually traced to Persian *tabāhī*, "ruin", from *tabāh*, "ruined".
