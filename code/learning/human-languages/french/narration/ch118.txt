Lesson 1 of 5.
le bijou — a jewel, a piece of jewellery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for size; the waist, then the French for a wallet.

You'll want to know: le bijou.
le bijou — "a jewel, a piece of jewellery". Plural les bijoux, with an x.

The word, taken apart.
bijou from Breton bizou, "a ring", from biz, "finger" — borrowed. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a ring, then the French for a necklace.

You'll want to know: le parfum.
le parfum — "perfume".

The word, taken apart.
parfum — formed from the verb for "to scent", which came from Italian perfumare, "to smoke through", from Latin per, "through", and fumare, "to smoke". (EtymDB 2.1 records the Latin parts.)
