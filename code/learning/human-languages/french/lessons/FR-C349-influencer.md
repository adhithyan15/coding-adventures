## You'll want to know: influencer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C349-INFLUENCE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**influencer** — "to influence".

> La publicité influence nos choix.
>
> *Advertising influences our choices.*

*Nous influençons*, with *ç*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*influencer* is built on *influence*, from Medieval Latin *influentia* "a flowing in", on Latin *influere* "to flow into", from *fluere* "to flow" (Lewis & Short).
