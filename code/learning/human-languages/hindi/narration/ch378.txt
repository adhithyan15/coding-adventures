Hindi, chapter 378: What Must Happen First.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आवश्यक (āvaśyak) — essential, required (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a hardship, then the Hindi for to trap.

You'll want to know: आवश्यक.
आवश्यक — āvaśyak — "essential, required (formal)".
अगर बीमारी गंभीर है तो आराम आवश्यक है। agar bīmārī gambhīr hai to ārām āvaśyak hai. If the illness is serious, rest is essential.

The word, taken apart.
आवश्यक (āvaśyak) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit āvaśyaka, "necessary, inevitable" (Monier-Williams).

अगर तुम्हारे सिवाय कोई नहीं आएगा तो हम क्या करेंगे? agar tumhāre sivāy koī nahīṃ āegā to ham kyā kareṅge? If nobody but you comes, what will we do?

Lesson 5 of 5.
मना करना (manā karnā) — to forbid; to say no.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for solely, then the Hindi for except.

You'll want to know: मना करना.
मना करना — manā karnā — "to forbid; to say no".
अगर डाक्टर मना करेगा तो मैं मिठाई नहीं खाऊँगा। agar ḍākṭar manā karegā to maiṁ miṭhāī nahīṃ khāūṅgā. If the doctor forbids it, I won't eat sweets.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मना किया, usne manā kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
मना करना (manā karnā) joins मना to करना, "to do". मना is usually traced to Arabic manʿ, "prevention, holding back", which reached Hindi through Persian.
