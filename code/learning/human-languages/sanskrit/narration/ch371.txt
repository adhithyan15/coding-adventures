Lesson 1 of 6.
विकल्पः (vikalpaḥ) — an option; an alternative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for favourable, then the Sanskrit for adverse.

You'll want to know: विकल्पः.
विकल्पः — vikalpaḥ — "an option; an alternative".
यदि धूमयानं न अस्ति, तर्हि लोकयानं विकल्पः अस्ति। yadi dhūmayānaṃ na asti, tarhi lokayānaṃ vikalpaḥ asti. If there is no train, the bus is an option.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विकल्पः (vikalpaḥ) is built on vi, "apart", and the root √kḷp, "to be fit, to arrange"; MW gives vi-kalpa "alternation, alternative, option" (MW).

यदि अवधिः श्वः अस्ति, तर्हि अद्य एव कार्यं करोतु। yadi avadhiḥ śvaḥ asti, tarhi adya eva kāryaṃ karotu. If the deadline is tomorrow, do the work today.

अवधिः (avadhiḥ) is built on ava, "down", and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives ava-dhi "a term, a limit" and "a conclusion" (MW).
