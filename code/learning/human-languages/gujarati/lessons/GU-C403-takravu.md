## You'll want to know: ટકરાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C403-HIGHWAY403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ટકરાવું** — *ṭakrāvũ* — "to crash (into); to collide head-on".

> ધુમ્મસમાં બે ટ્રક ટકરાઈ.
>
> *dhummasmā̃ be ṭrak ṭakrāī.*
>
> Two trucks crashed into each other in the fog.

Past (it …): તે ટ્રક ટકરાઈ, *te ṭrak ṭakrāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટકરાવું** (*ṭakrāvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *ṭakkarā*, "a blow on the head" (CDIAL 5424); CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to collide".
