## You'll want to know: 小偷
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C309-THIEF309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**小偷** — *xiǎotōu* — "thief".

> **小偷偷了我的钱包** *xiǎotōu tōu le wǒ de qiánbāo* — A thief stole my wallet.

The new character, in pieces: 偷 — eleven strokes in two pieces: the person piece of 你 on the left, two strokes; on the right a roof of two strokes with a short bar under it, then 月 on the left and the two strokes on the right of 到. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**小偷** (*xiǎotōu*) is built from 小 'small' and 偷 'to steal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *thuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to rob'.
