# ಮನವೊಲಿಸು (manavolisu) — to persuade

## You'll want to know: ಮನವೊಲಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C380-MANAVOLISU380-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನವೊಲಿಸು** — *manavolisu* — "to persuade".

> ಅಮ್ಮನನ್ನು ಮನವೊಲಿಸಲು ನನಗೆ ಒಂದು ವಾರ ಬೇಕಾಯಿತು.
>
> *ammanannu manavolisalu nanage ondu vāra bēkāyitu.*
>
> It took me a week to persuade Mother.

Past (I …): ಮನವೊಲಿಸಿದೆ, *manavoliside*.

The person persuaded takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮನವೊಲಿಸು** (*manavolisu*) is built from two parts: *mana*, "mind" (Sanskrit *manas*, CDIAL 9822), and *olisu*, "to win over", from native *ol* / *oli*, "to be pleased, to favour" (DEDR 1006).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
