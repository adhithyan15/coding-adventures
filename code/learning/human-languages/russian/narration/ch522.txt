Russian, chapter 522: Marble and Statues.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
скульптор (skúlptor) — a sculptor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a landscape, then the Russian for to pose.

You'll want to know: скульптор.
скульптор (skúlptor) — "a sculptor".
Скульптор работал над памятником целый год. Skúlptor rabótal nad pámyatnikom tsélyy god. The sculptor worked on the monument for a whole year.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
скульптор (skúlptor) is a learned borrowing from Latin sculptor, built on sculpere 'to carve' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
мрамор (mrámor) — marble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a sculptor, then the Russian for a sculpture.

You'll want to know: мрамор.
мрамор (mrámor) — "marble".
Лестница во дворце сделана из мрамора. Léstnitsa vo dvortsé sdélana iz mrámora. The staircase in the palace is made of marble.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
мрамор (mrámor) was borrowed from Church Slavonic mramorъ, the -ра- shape of Proto-Slavic mormorъ, which came from Latin marmor and Greek mármaros (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
вдохновлять (vdokhnovlyát) — to inspire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for marble, then the Russian for a statue.

You'll want to know: вдохновлять.
вдохновлять (vdokhnovlyát) — "to inspire".
Море вдохновляет художников. Móre vdokhnovlyáyet khudózhnikov. The sea inspires artists.
Imperfective; perfective вдохновить (вдохновлю). The noun is вдохновение — inspiration.

The word, taken apart.
вдохновлять (vdokhnovlyát) is built on вдохнуть 'to breathe in', from the root of дух 'breath, spirit', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic duxъ.
