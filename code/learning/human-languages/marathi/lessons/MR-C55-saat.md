## You'll want to know: सात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-SAAT]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **सात** — *sāt* — **7**

Sanskrit **सप्त** (*sapta*), which is Latin *septem* and English **seven**. The
**प** dropped out and the **त** stayed, and that collapse is the single most
common thing that happened to these words on their way from Sanskrit.
