## You'll want to know: concordia, concordiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C386-SOCIETAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**concordia, concordiae** — "harmony, concord".

> Concordiā parvae rēs crēscunt.
>
> *Through harmony small states grow.*

*concordia, concordiae* f., built on *cor, cordis*, heart: hearts together. English *concord*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*concordia* is built on *concors* "of one heart" (Lewis & Short), from *con-* "together" and *cor, cordis* "heart". English *concord* comes from it.
