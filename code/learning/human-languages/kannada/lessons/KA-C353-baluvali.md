## You'll want to know: ಬಳುವಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C353-UYILU353-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಳುವಳಿ** — *baḷuvaḷi* — "a legacy; an inheritance".

> ಈ ಹಳೆಯ ಮನೆ ಅಜ್ಜಿಯ ಬಳುವಳಿ.
>
> *ī haḷeya mane ajjiya baḷuvaḷi.*
>
> This old house is Grandmother's legacy.

Something handed down: property, a skill or a habit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಳುವಳಿ** (*baḷuvaḷi*) is usually traced to the native Dravidian family of *baḷe*, "to increase, to grow"; DEDR 5304 lists Kannada *baḷuvaḷike*, "increase, growth".
