## You'll want to know: そくたつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C305-YUUBIN305-03]; assesses=[] -->

**そくたつ** — *sokutatsu* — "express delivery".

> **この てがみを そくたつで おくって ください。** — *kono tegami o sokutatsu de okutte kudasai* — "Please send this letter express."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そくたつ** (*sokutatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *soku*, "fast" (Middle Chinese *suwk*) and *tatsu*, "arrive" (*dat*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
