## You'll want to know: der Ehepartner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C491-PATEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ehepartner** — "spouse".

> Auf dem Formular steht auch der Name des Ehepartners.
>
> *The spouse's name is also on the form.*

Plural: *die Ehepartner*. A woman is *die Ehepartnerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ehepartner* is built on *Ehe* "marriage" and *Partner*, which was borrowed from English *partner* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
