## You'll want to know: ಲಾಂದ್ರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C543-GRHA543-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಾಂದ್ರ** — *lāndra* — "a lantern".

> ಕರೆಂಟ್ ಹೋದಾಗ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಲಾಂದ್ರ ಹಚ್ಚಿದರು.
>
> *kareṇṭ hōdāga ajji lāndra haccidaru.*
>
> When the power went off, grandmother lit the lantern.

A glass-covered oil lamp with a handle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಾಂದ್ರ** (*lāndra*) is usually traced to a European word for a lantern, such as English *lantern*; the path into Kannada is not settled.
