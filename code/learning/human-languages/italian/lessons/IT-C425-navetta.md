## You'll want to know: la navetta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C425-AEROPORTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la navetta** — "the shuttle (bus)".

> Una navetta va dall'aeroporto all'albergo.
>
> *A shuttle goes from the airport to the hotel.*

Literally a little ship; also the shuttle of a loom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navetta* is a little *nave* 'ship', which is inherited from Latin *nāvis* (Lewis & Short).
