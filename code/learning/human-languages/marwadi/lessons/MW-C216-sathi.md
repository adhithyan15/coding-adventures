## You'll want to know: साथी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C216-OUT216-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साथी** — *sāthī* — "a companion; a fellow traveller".

> राम म्हारो साथी है।
>
> *rām mhāro sāthī hai.*
>
> Ram is my travelling companion.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साथी** (*sāthī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*sārthin*, "having part in a company", built on Sanskrit *sārtha*, a band of travellers; CDIAL lists Marwari *sāthī*, "a comrade" (CDIAL 13366).
