## You'll want to know: vēna, vēnae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C517-VENA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**vēna, vēnae** — "a vein".

> Sanguis per vēnās currit.
>
> *Blood runs through the veins.*

Feminine, first declension. Also a vein of metal, or of talent. English *vein*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vēna** is a native Latin noun; Lewis & Short guesses a link with *vehō* "to carry", but the origin is not settled. English *vein* comes from it through French.
