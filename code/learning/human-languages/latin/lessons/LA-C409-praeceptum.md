## You'll want to know: praeceptum, praeceptī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C409-EDICTUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**praeceptum, praeceptī** — "a rule, a precept, an instruction".

> Praecepta medicī servāvit.
>
> *He followed the doctor's instructions.*

*praeceptum, praeceptī* n., the participle of *praecipiō*. English *precept*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*praeceptum* is the participle of *praecipiō* "to instruct" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *prae* and *capiō*. English *precept* comes from it.
