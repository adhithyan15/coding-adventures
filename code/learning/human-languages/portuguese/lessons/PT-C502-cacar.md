## You'll want to know: caçar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C502-PESCA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**caçar** — "to hunt".

> Antigamente, os homens da aldeia caçavam coelhos.
>
> *In the old days, the men of the village hunted rabbits.*

Hunting is *a caça*; a hunter is *um caçador*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caçar* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Late Latin *captiāre* "to try to catch" (EtymDB), built on *capere* "to take".
