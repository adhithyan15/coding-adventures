## You'll want to know: la siesta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C741-CANSANCIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la siesta** — "the nap, the siesta".

> Después de comer echo la siesta.
>
> *After lunch I have a nap.*

*Echar la siesta* (Spain) or *dormir la siesta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*siesta* is inherited from Latin *sexta*, short for *sexta hōra* "the sixth hour" (EtymDB): counted from dawn, the sixth hour is midday.
