## You'll want to know: o riacho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C456-BOSQUE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o riacho** — "the brook".

> Um riacho passa pelo meio do bosque.
>
> *A brook runs through the middle of the wood.*

Smaller than *um rio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*riacho* was borrowed from Spanish *riacho* (EtymDB), a small form of *río* 'river', from Latin *rīvus* 'stream' (Lewis & Short).
