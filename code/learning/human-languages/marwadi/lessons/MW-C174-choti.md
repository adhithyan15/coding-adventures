## You'll want to know: चोटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C174-ODHNI174-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चोटी** — *choṭī* — "a plait of hair".

> म्हारी चोटी लांबी है।
>
> *mhārī choṭī lā̃bī hai.*
>
> My plait is long.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चोटी** (*choṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cūḍa*, "a topknot"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *coṭī*, "a tuft of hair" (CDIAL 4883). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
