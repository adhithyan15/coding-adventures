## You'll want to know: संस्थापयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C322-PURVAJA322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्थापयति** — *saṃsthāpayati* — "founds; establishes".

> राजा नवं नगरं संस्थापयति।
>
> *rājā navaṃ nagaraṃ saṃsthāpayati.*
>
> The king founds a new city.

Past (he …): सः नगरं संस्थापितवान्, *saḥ nagaraṃ saṃsthāpitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्थापयति** (*saṃsthāpayati*) is built from *sam* and the causative of the root √sthā, "to stand", as in *sthāpayati*; MW gives *saṃ-sthāpayati* as to make stand firm, raise up, restore (MW).
