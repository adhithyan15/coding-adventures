## You'll want to know: le décalage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C420-ESCALE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le décalage** — "the gap, the shift; jet lag".

> Avec le décalage horaire, il dort mal pendant trois jours.
>
> *With jet lag he sleeps badly for three days.*

*Le décalage horaire* — jet lag, the time difference.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*décalage* is built on *décaler* 'to shift' with *-age* (EtymDB).
