## You'll want to know: संक्रमण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C487-SANKRAMAN487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संक्रमण** — *saṅkramaṇ* — "an infection".

> गंदे घाव में संक्रमण हो सकता है।
>
> *gande ghāv meṁ saṅkramaṇ ho saktā hai.*
>
> A dirty wound can become infected.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संक्रमण** (*saṅkramaṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁkramaṇa*, "going together, entrance into, transference" (Monier-Williams). The medical sense, a disease passing into the body, is modern.
