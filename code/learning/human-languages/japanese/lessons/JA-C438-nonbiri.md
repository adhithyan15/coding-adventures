## You'll want to know: のんびり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C438-STUBBORN438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のんびり** — *nonbiri* — "leisurely; in a relaxed way".

> **しゅうまつは うちで のんびりしました。** — *shūmatsu wa uchi de nonbiri shimashita* — "I took it easy at home at the weekend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のんびり** (*nonbiri*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
