## You'll want to know: очевидец
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C282-ISTOCHNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**очевидец** (*ochevídets*) — "an eyewitness".

> Очевидец рассказал, что видел.
>
> *Ochevídets rasskazál, shto vídel.*
>
> The eyewitness told what he had seen.

Genitive *очевидца*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*очевидец* is built on *очи* "eyes", plural of *око*, Proto-Slavic *oko*, and *видеть* "to see", Proto-Slavic *viděti* (EtymDB): one who saw with his own eyes.
