Marathi, chapter 389: How Much and How Often.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अंशतः (aṁśataḥ) — partly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for development, then the Marathi for to implement.

You'll want to know: अंशतः.
अंशतः — aṁśataḥ — "partly".
तुमचं म्हणणं अंशतः खरं आहे. tumcaṁ mhaṇṇaṁ aṁśataḥ kharaṁ āhe. What you say is partly true.
Formal; अंश, a part.

The word, taken apart.
अंशतः (aṁśataḥ) is a learned adverb built from Sanskrit aṁśa, "share, part" (CDIAL 2), with the ending -taḥ, "by, in".

You'll want to know: पूर्णपणे.
पूर्णपणे — pūrṇapaṇe — "completely".
पावसामुळे रस्ता पूर्णपणे बंद झाला. pāvsāmuḷe rastā pūrṇapaṇe band jhālā. Because of the rain the road was completely closed.
पूर्ण, full, + -पणे, the adverb ending.

The word, taken apart.
पूर्णपणे (pūrṇapaṇe) is built from पूर्ण, "full", a tatsama from Sanskrit pūrṇa, "filled, full" (CDIAL 8339), with the adverb ending -पणे.

हे काम केवळ एका दिवसात झालं. he kām kevaḷ ekā divsāt jhālaṁ. This work got done in only one day.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सहसा (sahsā) — usually.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for merely, then the Marathi for purely.

You'll want to know: सहसा.
सहसा — sahsā — "usually".
मी सहसा सकाळी लवकर उठतो. mī sahsā sakāḷī lavkar uṭhto. I usually get up early in the morning.
With a negative, not readily: तो सहसा (sahsā) रागावत नाही.

The word, taken apart.
सहसा (sahsā) is usually traced to Sanskrit sahasā, "forcibly, suddenly" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it means as a rule.
