## You'll want to know: चेक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C426-RAQAM426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चेक** — *cek* — "a cheque".

> उसने किराये के लिए चेक दिया।
>
> *usne kirāye ke liye cek diyā.*
>
> He gave a cheque for the rent.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेक** (*cek*) is borrowed from English *cheque*.
