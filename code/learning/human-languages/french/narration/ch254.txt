Lesson 1 of 5.
le tronc — a trunk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a building site, then the French for a block of flats.

You'll want to know: le tronc.
le tronc — "a trunk".

The word, taken apart.
tronc from Latin truncus, "a trunk, a stem" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
