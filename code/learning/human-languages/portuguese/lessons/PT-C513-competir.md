## You'll want to know: competir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C513-CORRIDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**competir** — "to compete".

> Cem atletas vão competir na prova.
>
> *A hundred athletes are going to compete in the race.*

Present: *compito*, *competes*, *compete*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*competir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *competere* "to come together, to strive for" (EtymDB), built on *petere* "to seek".
