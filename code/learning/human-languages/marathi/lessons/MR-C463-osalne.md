# ओशाळणे (ośāḷṇe) — to feel sheepish

## You'll want to know: ओशाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C463-GARVA463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ओशाळणे** — *ośāḷṇe* — "to feel sheepish".

> खोटं पकडलं गेलं तेव्हा तो ओशाळला.
>
> *khoṭaṁ pakaḍlaṁ gelaṁ tevhā to ośāḷlā.*
>
> He felt sheepish when his lie was caught out.

Past (he …): तो ओशाळला, *to ośāḷlā*.

Embarrassment after being caught out: ओशाळलेला चेहरा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओशाळणे** (*ośāḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
