# incrēdibilis, incrēdibile — unbelievable, incredible

## You'll want to know: incrēdibilis, incrēdibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C349-FABULOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**incrēdibilis, incrēdibile** — "unbelievable, incredible".

> Nauta rēs incrēdibilēs nārrābat.
>
> *The sailor was telling unbelievable things.*

Two endings: *in-* 'not' + *crēdibilis* 'believable', from *crēdō* (taught). English *incredible*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**incrēdibilis** joins *in-*, "not", and *crēdibilis*, "worthy of belief", from *crēdō*, "to believe" (EtymDB). English *incredible* comes from it.
