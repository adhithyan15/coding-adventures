Marathi, chapter 239: Result, Difference, Example, Truth, Rule.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निकाल (nikāl) — a result.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tourist, then the Marathi for an adult.

You'll want to know: निकाल.
निकाल — nikāl — "a result".

The word, taken apart.
निकाल (nikāl) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from niṣkāla, "exit, expulsion", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7483). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "passage", and suggests, with a query, a source form niṣkālya-.

Lesson 2 of 5.
फरक (pharak) — a difference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an adult, then the Marathi for a result.

You'll want to know: फरक.
फरक — pharak — "a difference".

The word, taken apart.
फरक (pharak) is a loan from Arabic farq, "separation, difference", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is f-r-q (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सत्य (satya) — the truth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a difference, then the Marathi for an example.

You'll want to know: सत्य.
सत्य — satya — "the truth".

The word, taken apart.
सत्य (satya) is Sanskrit satya, "true; truth", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13112). CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as sāc, "true".
