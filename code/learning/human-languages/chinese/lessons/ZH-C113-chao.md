## You'll want to know: chǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C113-COOK113-03]; assesses=[] -->

*chǎo* — "to stir-fry".

> *nǐ huì chǎo cài ma* — Can you stir-fry vegetables?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsrhaewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to roast dry'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
