## You'll want to know: مات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C532-HAYAT532-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مات** — *māta* — "to die".

> مات الكلب العجوز في الشتاء.
>
> *māta al-kalb al-ʿajūz fī ash-shitāʾ.*
>
> The old dog died in the winter.

Present: يموت, *yamūt*.

توفي is the polite word for a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مات** (*māta*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-w-t*, "to die" (Lane). Mayyit, dead, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
