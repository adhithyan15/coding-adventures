## You'll want to know: wēndù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C243-SEASON243-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wēndù* — "temperature".

> *jīntiān wēndù bù gāo* — It's not very warm today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wēndù* is built from *wēn* 'warm' and *dù* 'a measure', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'won* and *duH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a rule'; the sense of a degree on a scale is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
