## You'll want to know: der Reim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C521-GEDICHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Reim** — "rhyme".

> Das Kinderlied hat lustige Reime.
>
> *The children's song has funny rhymes.*

Plural: *die Reime*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Reim* is usually traced to Old French *rime* "rhyme", whose own origin is disputed; EtymDB sets the German word beside French *rime*.
