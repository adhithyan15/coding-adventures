## You'll want to know: બદલાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C377-DASHA377-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બદલાવું** — *badlāvũ* — "to become different".

> જો હવામાન બદલાશે તો અમે ઘરે રહીશું.
>
> *jo havāmān badlāśe to ame ghare rahīśũ.*
>
> If the weather changes, we will stay at home.

Past (it …): તે શહેર ઘણું બદલાયું, *te śaher ghaṇũ badlāyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બદલાવું** (*badlāvũ*) is the *-āvũ* partner of **બદલવું** (*badalvũ*), "to change", which is usually traced to Arabic *badal*, "a substitute, an exchange", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
