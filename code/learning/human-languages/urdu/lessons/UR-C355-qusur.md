## You'll want to know: قصور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C355-TARIF355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قصور** — *qusūr* — "fault; blame".

> اس میں میرا کوئی قصور نہیں ہے۔
>
> *is meṅ merā koī qusūr nahīṅ hai.*
>
> None of this is my fault.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قصور** (*qusūr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *quṣūr*, "a shortcoming".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
