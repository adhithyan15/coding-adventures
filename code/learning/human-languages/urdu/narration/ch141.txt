5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ٹھہرنا (ṭhaharnā) — to stay, to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to explain, then the Urdu for to ask for.

You'll want to know: ٹھہرنا.
ٹھہرنا — ṭhaharnā — "to stay, to wait".

The word, taken apart.
ٹھہرنا (ṭhaharnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from stabhira, "fixed, firm", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13680). It is built on stambh-, "to make firm".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to stay, then the Urdu for to come back.

You'll want to know: پہنچنا.
پہنچنا — pahuṅchnā — "to arrive".

The word, taken apart.
پہنچنا (pahuṅchnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from prabhūta, "much, great" (CDIAL 8716). Prakrit made a verb of it, "is strong, reaches", and Urdu kept "to reach".

نکلنا — nikalnā — "to come out, to leave".

The word, taken apart.
نکلنا (nikalnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from niṣkalati, "comes out", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7478). CDIAL takes it as formed back from Sanskrit niṣkālayati, "drives out".
