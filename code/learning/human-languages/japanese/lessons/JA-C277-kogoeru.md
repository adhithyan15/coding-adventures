## You'll want to know: こごえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C277-OOYUKI277-05]; assesses=[] -->

**こごえる** — *kogoeru* — "to go numb with cold; to freeze".

> **ゆきの なかで あしが こごえました。** — *yuki no naka de ashi ga kogoemashita* — "My feet went numb in the snow."

Polite **こごえます**, *kogoemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **てが こごえました。**, *te ga kogoemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こごえる** (*kogoeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
