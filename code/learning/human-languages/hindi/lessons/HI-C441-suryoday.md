## You'll want to know: सूर्योदय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C441-SAVERA441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर्योदय** — *sūryoday* — "sunrise".

> पहाड़ से सूर्योदय बहुत सुंदर दिखता है।
>
> *pahāṛ se sūryoday bahut sundar dikhtā hai.*
>
> The sunrise looks beautiful from the mountain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूर्योदय** (*sūryoday*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sūryodaya* (Monier-Williams), from *sūrya*, "the sun", and *udaya*, "going up"; the two vowels a + u have merged into o.
