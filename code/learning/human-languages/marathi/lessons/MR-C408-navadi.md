## You'll want to know: नावाडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C408-NAVADI408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नावाडी** — *nāvāḍī* — "a boatman".

> नावाड्याने आम्हाला नदीपलीकडे नेलं.
>
> *nāvāḍyāne āmhālā nadīpalīkaḍe nelaṁ.*
>
> The boatman took us across the river.

Masculine.

Built on नाव, a boat; before an ending it becomes नावाड्या-.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नावाडी** (*nāvāḍī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *nāvāḍī*, "boatman", under Sanskrit *nāvā*, "boat" (CDIAL 7081).
