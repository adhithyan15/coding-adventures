## You'll want to know: মুনাফা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C418-MUNAFA418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মুনাফা** — *munāphā* — "profit (in trade)".

> এই বছর ব্যবসায় ভালো মুনাফা হল।
>
> *ei bôchhôr bæbsāy bhālo munāphā hôlô.*
>
> The business made a good profit this year.

লাভ, gain, is the wider word; মুনাফা is the trader's profit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মুনাফা** (*munāphā*) is usually traced to Arabic *manfaʿa*, "use, benefit, profit", which reached Bengali through Persian.
