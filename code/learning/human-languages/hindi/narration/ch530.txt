Hindi, chapter 530: Learning a Raga.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संगीत (saṅgīt) — music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a character, then the Hindi for a spectator.

You'll want to know: संगीत.
संगीत — saṅgīt — "music".
मेरी बहन संगीत सीख रही है। Merī bahan saṅgīt sīkh rahī hai. My sister is learning music.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संगीत (saṅgīt) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit saṃgīta, "sung together; music" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सुर (sur) — a musical note; a pitch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a spectator, then the Hindi for music.

You'll want to know: सुर.
सुर — sur — "a musical note; a pitch".
गायक का सुर बहुत मीठा है। Gāyak kā sur bahut mīṭhā hai. The singer's voice is very sweet.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सुर (sur) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi sur, "sound, words, tune", under Sanskrit sura, "sound", a variant of svara, "a musical note" (CDIAL 13498).

Lesson 5 of 5.
गुनगुनाना (gungunānā) — to hum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a beat, then the Hindi for a raga.

You'll want to know: गुनगुनाना.
गुनगुनाना — gungunānā — "to hum".
मीरा रसोई में एक पुराना गाना गुनगुना रही है। Mīrā rasoī meṁ ek purānā gānā gungunā rahī hai. Mira is humming an old song in the kitchen.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने एक गाना गुनगुनाया, usne ek gānā gungunāyā.

The word, taken apart.
गुनगुनाना (gungunānā) is a tadbhava, inherited from gunaguna, "murmur", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4013).
