## You'll want to know: chúfēi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C396-NOMATTER396-03]; assesses=[] -->

*chúfēi* — "unless".

> *chúfēi nǐ bìng le bùrán míngtiān yídìng yào lái* — Unless you're ill, be sure to come tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chúfēi* is built from *chú* 'to take away' and *fēi* 'not', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjo* and *pj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to remove' and the second 'it is not so'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
