# ভিত্তি (bhitti) — a basis; a foundation

## You'll want to know: ভিত্তি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C337-PROMAN337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভিত্তি** — *bhitti* — "a basis; a foundation".

> এই অভিযোগের কোনো ভিত্তি নেই।
>
> *ei ôbhijoger kono bhitti nei.*
>
> This charge has no basis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভিত্তি** (*bhitti*) is Sanskrit *bhitti*, a wall (Monier-Williams). The Bengali sense, the foundation or basis of a building or a claim, is not one the dictionary gives.
