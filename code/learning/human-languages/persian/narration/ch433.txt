Persian, chapter 433: Packing and goodbyes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ساک (sâk) — a holdall, a travel bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to search, then the Persian for a flight attendant.

You'll want to know: ساک.
ساک (sâk) — "a holdall, a travel bag".
یک ساک کوچک برای سفر کافی است. Yek sâk-e kuchek barâ-ye safar kâfi ast. A small bag is enough for the trip.
Sâk-e varzeshi — a sports bag.

The word, taken apart.
sâk was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French sac 'bag'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بار بستن (bâr bastan) — to pack (for a journey).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a flight attendant, then the Persian for a holdall.

You'll want to know: بار بستن.
بار بستن (bâr bastan) — "to pack (for a journey)".
شب قبل از سفر بار بستیم. Shab-e qabl az safar bâr bastim. We packed the night before the trip.
bâr 'load' + bastan 'to tie'. Present stem band-: bâr mibandim.

The word, taken apart.
bâr bastan is built from native bâr 'load', which EtymDB lists, and bastan 'to tie, to bind', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root bʰendʰ- 'to bind'. Loads were once tied onto pack animals.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بدرقه کردن (badraqe kardan) — to see off (someone leaving).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a holdall, then the Persian for to pack.

You'll want to know: بدرقه کردن.
بدرقه کردن (badraqe kardan) — "to see off (someone leaving)".
مادرم ما را تا ایستگاه بدرقه کرد. Mâdaram mâ râ tâ istgâh badraqe kard. My mother saw us off at the station.
badraqe 'a send-off' + kardan. Iranian families often see travellers off with water poured behind them.

The word, taken apart.
badraqe kardan is built from badraqe 'a send-off, an escort', whose origin is not settled, and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
استقبال کردن (esteqbâl kardan) — to meet (someone arriving), to welcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pack, then the Persian for to see off.

You'll want to know: استقبال کردن.
استقبال کردن (esteqbâl kardan) — "to meet (someone arriving), to welcome".
دوستانم در فرودگاه از من استقبال کردند. Dustânam dar forudgâh az man esteqbâl kardand. My friends met me at the airport.
The person met takes az: az u esteqbâl kardim. Also to welcome an idea.

The word, taken apart.
esteqbâl kardan is built from esteqbâl 'a welcome', borrowed from Arabic istiqbāl, which EtymDB derives from istaqbala 'to receive, to meet, to face', and kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عجله کردن ('ajale kardan) — to hurry, to be in a hurry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to see off, then the Persian for to meet.

You'll want to know: عجله کردن.
عجله کردن ('ajale kardan) — "to hurry, to be in a hurry".
عجله کردیم ولی قطار رفته بود. 'Ajale kardim vali qatâr rafte bud. We hurried, but the train had gone.
'ajale 'haste' + kardan. 'Ajale dâshtan — to be in a hurry.

The word, taken apart.
'ajale kardan is built from 'ajale 'haste', a loan usually traced to Arabic ʿajala 'haste', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
