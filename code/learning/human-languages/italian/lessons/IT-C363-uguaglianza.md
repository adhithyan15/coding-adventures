## You'll want to know: l'uguaglianza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C363-CITTADINO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'uguaglianza** — "the equality".

> Lottiamo per l'uguaglianza tra uomini e donne.
>
> *We fight for equality between men and women.*

Also spelled *eguaglianza*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*uguaglianza* is built on *uguale* "equal", inherited from Latin *aequālis* "equal", from *aequus* "level, even" (Lewis & Short).
