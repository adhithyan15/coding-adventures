## You'll want to know: ひ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C214-STORM214-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひ** — *hi* — "a fire; a flame".

> **ひの ちかくは あついです。** — *hi no chikaku wa atsui desu* — "It is hot near the fire."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひ** (*hi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
