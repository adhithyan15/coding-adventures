## You'll want to know: yìyì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C347-MEANING347-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yìyì* — "significance; meaning".

> *wǒ juéde zhège gōngzuò hěn yǒu yìyì* — I think this work really matters.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yìyì* is built from 意 'intent' and *yì* 'right conduct', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'iH* and *ngjeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'will' and the second 'benevolence and right conduct'.
