## You'll want to know: अभिप्रायः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C347-UDAHARANA347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिप्रायः** — *abhiprāyaḥ* — "what someone means; an intention".

> तव अभिप्रायः कः?
>
> *tava abhiprāyaḥ kaḥ?*
>
> What do you mean?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिप्रायः** (*abhiprāyaḥ*) is built on *abhi*, *pra* and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives *abhi-prāya* "aim, purpose, intention" and "meaning, sense" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
