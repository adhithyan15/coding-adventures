## You'll want to know: છાપરું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C303-VAVAZODU303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**છાપરું** — *chāprũ* — "a thatched roof".

> વાવાઝોડામાં અમારું છાપરું ઊડી ગયું.
>
> *vāvājhoḍāmā̃ amārũ chāprũ ūḍī gayũ.*
>
> Our roof blew off in the cyclone.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to Sanskrit *chattvará*, "a house, a bower", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "a thatched roof, a cottage" (CDIAL 4976). **છત** is a ceiling or a flat roof; a **છાપરું** is a sloping roof of thatch or tiles.
