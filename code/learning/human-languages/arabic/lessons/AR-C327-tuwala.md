# طوال (ṭuwāla) — throughout, all through

## You'll want to know: طوال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C327-LAYLA327-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طوال** — *ṭuwāla* — "throughout, all through".

> بقي المريض في السرير طوال الأسبوع.
>
> *baqiya al-marīḍ fī as-sarīr ṭuwāla al-usbūʿ.*
>
> The patient stayed in bed all week.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طوال** (*ṭuwāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-w-l* (Lane), "to be long", the root of the taught *ṭawīl*. *Ṭuwāla* is "for the length of".
