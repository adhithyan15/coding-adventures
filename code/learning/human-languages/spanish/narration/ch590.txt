Spanish, chapter 590: Buscar trabajo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la vacante — the vacancy, the open post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for not at all, then the Spanish for deep down.

You'll want to know: la vacante.
la vacante — "the vacancy, the open post".
Hay una vacante en la tienda. There is a vacancy in the shop.
Also an adjective: un puesto vacante.

The word, taken apart.
vacante is a learned borrowing from Latin vacans 'being empty', from vacare 'to be empty, to be free' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
seleccionar — to select, to choose.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for deep down, then the Spanish for the vacancy.

You'll want to know: seleccionar.
seleccionar — "to select, to choose".
Seleccionamos a tres personas para la entrevista. We selected three people for the interview.
More formal than elegir.

The word, taken apart.
seleccionar is built on selección, a learned borrowing from Latin selectio 'a picking out' (EtymDB), from seligere, built on legere 'to gather'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
el empleado — the employee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the vacancy, then the Spanish for to select.

You'll want to know: el empleado.
el empleado — "the employee".
Es empleado de un banco. He is a bank employee.
A woman is la empleada; in Spain la empleada del hogar is a domestic worker.

The word, taken apart.
empleado is the participle of emplear, which was borrowed from Old French empleier, from Latin implicare 'to fold in, to involve' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: el perfil.
el perfil — "the profile".
Buscamos un perfil técnico. We are looking for someone with a technical profile.
In a job offer it is the kind of person wanted. Mi perfil is also your profile online.

The word, taken apart.
perfil was borrowed from Occitan perfil 'outline' (EtymDB), usually traced to Latin filum 'thread': a line drawn round the edge.
