## You'll want to know: اشتراك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C435-MASAR435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتراك** — *ishtirāk* — "a subscription; a travel pass".

> اشترى أخي اشتراكا شهريا للمترو.
>
> *ishtarā akhī ishtirākan shahriyyan li-l-mitrū.*
>
> My brother bought a monthly pass for the metro.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشتراك** (*ishtirāk*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-k*, "to share" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form VIII, "taking a share in something".
