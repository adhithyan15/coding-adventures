## You'll want to know: たがいに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C370-MATOMARU370-02]; assesses=[] -->

**たがいに** — *tagaini* — "each other; mutually".

> **たがいに たすけたら、はやく おわるでしょう。** — *tagaini tasuketara, hayaku owaru deshō* — "If we help each other, we will probably finish sooner."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たがいに** (*tagaini*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
