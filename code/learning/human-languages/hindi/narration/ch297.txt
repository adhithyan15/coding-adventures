Hindi, chapter 297: A Slip and a Fall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फिसलना (phisalnā) — to slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wheel, then the Hindi for to overturn.

You'll want to know: फिसलना.
फिसलना — phisalnā — "to slip".
बारिश के बाद मीरा सीढ़ी पर फिसल गई। bāriś ke bād Mīrā sīṛhī par phisal gaī. After the rain Mira slipped on the stairs.
Past (she …): वह फिसल गई, vah phisal gaī.

The word, taken apart.
फिसलना (phisalnā) is inherited from phiss, a form CDIAL reconstructs for slipping (CDIAL 9080). A slippery floor is one you फिसलते हैं on.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ठोकर (ṭhokar) — a stumble; a knock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to overturn, then the Hindi for to slip.

You'll want to know: ठोकर.
ठोकर — ṭhokar — "a stumble; a knock".
अरुण को पत्थर से ठोकर लगी। Aruṇ ko patthar se ṭhokar lagī. Arun tripped over a stone.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ठोकर (ṭhokar) is inherited from ṭhōkk, a knock, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5513). The person who trips takes को: अरुण को ठोकर लगी, a knock struck Arun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लड़खड़ाना (laṛkhaṛānā) — to stagger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to slip, then the Hindi for a stumble.

You'll want to know: लड़खड़ाना.
लड़खड़ाना — laṛkhaṛānā — "to stagger".
ठोकर के बाद वह लड़खड़ाया और गिर गया। ṭhokar ke bād vah laṛkhaṛāyā aur gir gayā. After the stumble he staggered and fell.
Past (he …): वह लड़खड़ाया, vah laṛkhaṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
लड़खड़ाना (laṛkhaṛānā) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit laṭati, "shakes" (CDIAL 10916), where Hindi appears as larkharānā, to stumble. The repeated -khaṛ- sound gives the unsteady steps.

Lesson 4 of 5.
छिलना (chilnā) — to be grazed; to be scraped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a stumble, then the Hindi for to stagger.

You'll want to know: छिलना.
छिलना — chilnā — "to be grazed; to be scraped".
गिरने से उसका घुटना छिल गया। girne se uskā ghuṭnā chil gayā. He grazed his knee when he fell.
Past (it …): वह घुटना छिल गया, vah ghuṭnā chil gayā.

The word, taken apart.
छिलना (chilnā) is the intransitive of chīlnā, to peel, which CDIAL 5051 lists for Hindi under chilla: when you fall, the skin छिल जाती है, peels off by itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
लहूलुहान (lahūluhān) — covered in blood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to stagger, then the Hindi for to be grazed.

You'll want to know: लहूलुहान.
लहूलुहान — lahūluhān — "covered in blood".
गिरने के बाद उसका हाथ लहूलुहान था। girne ke bād uskā hāth lahūluhān thā. After the fall his hand was covered in blood.

The word, taken apart.
लहूलुहान (lahūluhān) is lahū, blood, which CDIAL 11165 lists for Hindi under Sanskrit lohita, "red", followed by an expressive echo, -luhān, that makes the picture stronger. It describes a person or a body part: हाथ लहूलुहान था.
