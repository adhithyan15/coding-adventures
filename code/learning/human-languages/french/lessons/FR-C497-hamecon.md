## You'll want to know: l'hameçon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C497-PECHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'hameçon** — "fish hook".

> Le poisson a mordu à l'hameçon.
>
> *The fish took the hook.*

Masculine. *Mordre à l'hameçon* is also to fall for a trick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'hameçon* comes from Old French *ameçon*, built on a Vulgar Latin form of Latin *hāmus* "a hook" (Lewis & Short).
