## You'll want to know: अपेक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C383-BHAKIT383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अपेक्षा** — *apekṣā* — "an expectation".

> आईवडिलांच्या माझ्याकडून खूप अपेक्षा आहेत.
>
> *āīvḍilāñcyā mājhyākaḍūn khūp apekṣā āhet.*
>
> My parents have high expectations of me.

Feminine. Plural: अपेक्षा, *apekṣā*.

X कडून अपेक्षा, expectations of X; अपेक्षा करणे, to expect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपेक्षा** (*apekṣā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *apekṣā*, "looking round; expectation" (Monier-Williams).
