# भी (bhī) — also, too

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: भी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भी** (*bhī*) — "also, too".

Sanskrit अपि (*api*), "also", is the ancestor. The Prakrits dropped the opening
vowel and left a bare *vi*; that hardened on the way into Hindi and came out as
भी. Two letters at the end of two thousand years, and the meaning has not moved
at all.

The thing to hold on to is where it stands: भी comes **after** the word it adds
to, never before. **मैं भी** is "me too". **आज भी** is "today as well". Put it
first and a Hindi ear hears nothing.

Watch out for one collision. You already own **अभी**, "right now", and भी is
sitting inside it — but they are separate words with separate jobs, the way
**अब** and **अभी** were. The eye will confuse them long before the ear does.
