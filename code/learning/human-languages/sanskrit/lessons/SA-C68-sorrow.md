# दुःखम् (duḥkham) — sorrow, pain

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C67-NEEDS-04, SA-LEX-C67-NEEDS-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rest, then the Sanskrit for ease.

## You'll want to know: दुःखम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C68-FEELINGS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दुःखम्** (*duḥkham*) — "sorrow", the opposite of **सुखम्**. The two words share
their ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

CDIAL 6375 says *duḥkha* was formed after *sukha*, with *duḥ-* "bad" in place of
*su-* "good"; MW also suggests a Prakrit form of *duḥ-stha*. It lives on as Hindi
*dukh*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The other side of ease.
