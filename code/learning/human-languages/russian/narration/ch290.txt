Russian, chapter 290: Tricks and hiding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
мошенник (moshénnik) — a swindler, a fraudster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an attack, then the Russian for to attack.

You'll want to know: мошенник.
мошенник (moshénnik) — "a swindler, a fraudster".
Мошенник позвонил бабушке. Moshénnik pozvoníl bábushke. A swindler phoned Grandma.
Мошенничество — fraud.

The word, taken apart.
мошенник (moshénnik) is usually traced to the old word мошна "a purse", so first "a cutpurse"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
обманывать (obmányvat) — to deceive, to cheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to attack, then the Russian for a swindler.

You'll want to know: обманывать.
обманывать (obmányvat) — "to deceive, to cheat".
Он никогда не обманывает друзей. On nikogdá ne obmányvayet druzéy. He never deceives his friends.
Imperfective; perfective обмануть (obmanút). Обман — a deceit, a trick.

The word, taken apart.
обманывать (obmányvat) is built on об- and манить "to lure, to beckon", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic maniti "to deceive" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
угонять (ugonyát) — to hijack, to steal (a vehicle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a swindler, then the Russian for to deceive.

You'll want to know: угонять.
угонять (ugonyát) — "to hijack, to steal (a vehicle)".
Ночью у соседа угнали машину. Nóchyu u soséda ugnáli mashínu. During the night the neighbour's car was stolen.
Imperfective; perfective угнать (ugnát), угоню. For a car or a plane; угон — a car theft.

The word, taken apart.
угонять (ugonyát) is built on у- "away" and гнать "to drive", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic gŭnati, from PIE gʷʰen- "to strike" (EtymDB): to drive away.

You'll want to know: похищать.
похищать (pokhishchát) — "to kidnap, to abduct; to steal".
В фильме похитили дочь богатого человека. V fílme pokhítili doch bogátovo chelovéka. In the film the daughter of a rich man was kidnapped.
Imperfective; perfective похитить (pokhítit), я похищу. Bookish.

The word, taken apart.
похищать (pokhishchát) is built on по- and an old verb хитить "to seize", the root of хищник "a predator"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
прятаться (pryátatsya) — to hide (oneself).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to hijack, then the Russian for to kidnap.

You'll want to know: прятаться.
прятаться (pryátatsya) — "to hide (oneself)".
Кошка прячется под кроватью. Kóshka pryáchetsya pod krovátyu. The cat is hiding under the bed.
Imperfective; perfective спрятаться (spryátatsya). Present прячусь, прячешься. Without -ся, прятать hides something else.

The word, taken apart.
прятаться (pryátatsya) is прятать "to hide" with the reflexive -ся (EtymDB): to hide oneself. The origin of прятать is not settled.
