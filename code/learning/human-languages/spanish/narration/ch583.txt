Spanish, chapter 583: Lo que influye.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
influir — to influence, to have an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for consequently, then the Spanish for so.

You'll want to know: influir.
influir — "to influence, to have an effect".
El tiempo influye en mi humor. The weather influences my mood.
Like construir: influyo, influye. With en: influir en algo.

The word, taken apart.
influir is a learned borrowing from Latin influere 'to flow in', built from in and fluere 'to flow' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 6.
la influencia — the influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for so, then the Spanish for to influence.

You'll want to know: la influencia.
la influencia — "the influence".
Sus padres tienen mucha influencia en él. His parents have a lot of influence on him.
Tener influencia en or sobre someone.

The word, taken apart.
influencia is a learned word usually traced to Medieval Latin influentia, first the power thought to flow in from the stars; it is built on Latin influere 'to flow in' (Lewis & Short).
