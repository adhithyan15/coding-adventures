French, chapter 51: Skin, Nail, Chin, Throat, Cheek.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la peau — skin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the face, then the French for blood.

You'll want to know: la peau.
la peau — "skin".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: la peau]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: la peau, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say la peau]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the French for the face, then the French for blood, then say la peau again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does la peau mean? ("Skin".) Say it once more.
