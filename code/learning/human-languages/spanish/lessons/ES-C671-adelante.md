## You'll want to know: adelante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C671-LEJOS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**adelante** — "forward, ahead".

> Siga adelante hasta el cruce.
>
> *Keep going to the junction.*

*¡Adelante!* also means come in! or go ahead!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adelante* comes from *delante* "in front" and Old Spanish *denante* (EtymDB), a fusion of Latin *de*, *in* and *ante* "before".
