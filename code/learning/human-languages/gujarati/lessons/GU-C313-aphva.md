## You'll want to know: અફવા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C313-AFVA313-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અફવા** — *aphvā* — "a rumour".

> ગામમાં અફવા ફેલાઈ કે ડાકુ આવ્યા છે.
>
> *gāmmā̃ aphvā phelāī ke ḍāku āvyā che.*
>
> A rumour went round the village that bandits had come.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અફવા** (*aphvā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *afwāh*, "mouths": what passes from mouth to mouth. It **ફેલાય છે**, spreads.
