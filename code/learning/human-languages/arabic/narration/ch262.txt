(اتصل, ittaṣala.) To get better? (تحسن, taḥassana.) To examine? (فحص, faḥaṣa.) To fail? (رسب, rasaba.) To pass? (اجتاز, ijtāza.) To explain? (شرح, sharaḥa.) To translate? (ترجم, tarjama.) To give back? (أعاد, aʿāda.) To refuse? (رفض, rafaḍa.) To graduate? (تخرج, takharraja.) To be glad? (فرح, fariḥa.) To be afraid? (خاف, khāfa.) To smile? (ابتسم, ibtasama.)
