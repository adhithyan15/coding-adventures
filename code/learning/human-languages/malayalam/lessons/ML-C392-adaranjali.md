# ആദരാഞ്ജലി (ādarāñjali) — a tribute; homage

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C391-SAVAM391-05, ML-LEX-C392-ANUSOCHANAM392-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to bury, then the Malayalam for condolence.

## You'll want to know: ആദരാഞ്ജലി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C392-ANUSOCHANAM392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ആദരാഞ്ജലി** — *ādarāñjali* — "a tribute; homage".

> നാട്ടുകാർ കവിക്ക് ആദരാഞ്ജലി അർപ്പിച്ചു.
>
> *nāṭṭukār kavikkŭ ādarāñjali arppiccu.*
>
> The local people paid tribute to the poet.

It is offered: ആദരാഞ്ജലി അർപ്പിക്കുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആദരാഞ്ജലി** (*ādarāñjali*) is built from two Sanskrit words, *ādara*, "respect" (CDIAL 1142), and *añjali*, "the hollowed hands placed together" (CDIAL 171): hands joined in respect.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
