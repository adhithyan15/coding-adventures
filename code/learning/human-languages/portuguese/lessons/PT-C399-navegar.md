## You'll want to know: navegar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C399-REDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**navegar** — "to browse, to surf; to sail".

> Passo muito tempo a navegar no telemóvel.
>
> *I spend a lot of time browsing on my phone.*

First to sail a ship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navegar* comes from Latin *nāvigāre* "to sail" (EtymDB), built on *nāvis* "ship" (Lewis & Short).
