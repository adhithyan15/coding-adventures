Russian, chapter 79: Bus, Train, Plane, Car, Bridge.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
автобус (avtóbus) — a bus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a theatre, then the Russian for a restaurant.

You'll want to know: автобус.
автобус (avtóbus) — "a bus".

What you've built.
