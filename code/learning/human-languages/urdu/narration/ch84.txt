Urdu, chapter 84: Fourteen, Fifteen, Sixteen, Seventeen, Eighteen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چودہ (chaudah) — fourteen.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Urdu for twelve, then the Urdu for thirteen.
