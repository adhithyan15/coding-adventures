## You'll want to know: باغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C467-BAGH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**باغ** (*bâgh*) — "a garden; an orchard".

> خانه پدربزرگم یک باغ بزرگ دارد.
>
> *Khâne-ye pedarbozorgam yek bâgh-e bozorg dârad.*
>
> My grandfather's house has a big garden.

The Persian garden, with water and trees in a walled square, is famous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâgh* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian *bāg* 'garden, orchard'.
