## You'll want to know: l'immigrazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C366-SOCIETA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'immigrazione** — "the immigration".

> Il governo vuole cambiare le leggi sull'immigrazione.
>
> *The government wants to change the immigration laws.*

*Un immigrato* comes in; *un emigrato* has left.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*immigrazione* is built on *immigrare* "to immigrate", a learned borrowing from Latin *immigrāre* "to move into" (Lewis & Short), built from *in* and *migrāre* "to move house".
