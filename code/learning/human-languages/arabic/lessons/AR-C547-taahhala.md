# تأهل (taʾahhala) — to qualify

## You'll want to know: تأهل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C547-BUTULA547-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تأهل** — *taʾahhala* — "to qualify".

> تأهل فريقنا إلى البطولة.
>
> *taʾahhala farīqunā ilā al-buṭūla.*
>
> Our team qualified for the championship.

Present: يتأهل, *yataʾahhal*.

It takes إلى or لـ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأهل** (*taʾahhala*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-h-l*, "a family; to be fit for" (Lane). Form V taʾahhala is to become fit for a thing.

## What you've built
