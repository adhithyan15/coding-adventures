# であう (deau) — to come across; to meet by chance

## You'll want to know: であう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C244-MEET244-02]; assesses=[] -->

**であう** — *deau* — "to come across; to meet by chance".

> **まちで よく しりあいに であいますか。** — *machi de yoku shiriai ni deaimasu ka* — "Do you often run into acquaintances in town?"

Polite **であいます**, *deaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**であう** (*deau*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *de*, from *deru*, "to come out", and *au*, "to meet" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
