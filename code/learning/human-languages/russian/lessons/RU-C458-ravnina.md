## You'll want to know: равнина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C458-DOLINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**равнина** (*ravnína*) — "a plain (flat land)".

> За рекой начинается широкая равнина.
>
> *Za rekóy nachináyetsya shirókaya ravnína.*
>
> Beyond the river a wide plain begins.

Feminine. *Равнина* has the Church Slavonic *ра-* where native *ровный* has *ро-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*равнина* is built on *равный*, the Church Slavonic shape of native *ровный* 'level, even', with *-ина*. EtymDB takes *ровный* from Proto-Slavic *orvĭnŭ*.
