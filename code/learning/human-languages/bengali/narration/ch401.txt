Bengali, chapter 401: Points of the Compass.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দক্ষিণ (dôkkhin) — south.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an instruction, then the Bengali for to point (to).

You'll want to know: দক্ষিণ.
দক্ষিণ — dôkkhin — "south".
সমুদ্র এই গ্রামের দক্ষিণে। sômudrô ei grāmer dôkkhine. The sea lies to the south of this village.
ক্ষ is said kkh in the middle of a word. উত্তর, north, is also the word for an answer.

The word, taken apart.
দক্ষিণ (dôkkhin) is Sanskrit dakṣiṇa, "on the right hand" and so "southern" (Monier-Williams): facing the sunrise, the south is on your right.

Lesson 2 of 5.
পূর্ব (pūrbô) — east.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to point (to), then the Bengali for south.

You'll want to know: পূর্ব.
পূর্ব — pūrbô — "east".
ভোরে পূর্ব আকাশ লাল হল। bhore pūrbô ākāsh lāl hôlô. At dawn the eastern sky turned red.
Also former, earlier, in careful speech: পূর্বে, before.

The word, taken apart.
পূর্ব (pūrbô) is Sanskrit pūrva, "being before, in front", and so "eastern" and "earlier" (Monier-Williams): the east was the side you faced.

Lesson 5 of 5.
দিগন্ত (digôntô) — the horizon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for west, then the Bengali for a compass.

You'll want to know: দিগন্ত.
দিগন্ত — digôntô — "the horizon".
সৈকত থেকে আমরা দিগন্তে একটা জাহাজ দেখলাম। soikôt theke āmrā digônte ekṭā jāhāj dekhlām. From the beach we saw a ship on the horizon.

The word, taken apart.
দিগন্ত (digôntô) is Sanskrit dig-anta, "the end of the horizon, a far distance" (Monier-Williams), from diś, a quarter of the sky, and anta, an end.
