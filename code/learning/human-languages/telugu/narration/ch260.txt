Telugu, chapter 260: Smooth, Warm, Rare, Strange, Kind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నునుపైన (nunupaina) — smooth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for busy, then the Telugu for compulsory.

You'll want to know: నునుపైన.
నునుపైన — nunupaina — "smooth".

The word, taken apart.
నునుపైన (nunupaina) is native Dravidian: nunupu, "smoothness", one word with Kannada nuṇupu, "smoothness" (DEDR 3700), with aina, "which is" (DEDR 333).
