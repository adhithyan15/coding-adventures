## You'll want to know: حريق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C298-HARIQ298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حريق** — *ḥarīq* — "a blaze, a fire that spreads".

> رأى الجيران حريقا في المصنع.
>
> *raʾā al-jīrān ḥarīqan fī al-maṣnaʿ.*
>
> The neighbours saw a fire at the factory.

نار is fire itself; حريق is a fire that destroys.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حريق** (*ḥarīq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-q* (Lane), "to burn". *Ḥarīq* is the fire that burns a thing up.
