Bengali, chapter 387: Ways and Destinations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পথ (pôth) — a way; a path.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to unload, then the Bengali for to carry (a load).

You'll want to know: পথ.
পথ — pôth — "a way; a path".
গ্রামের পথে আমরা একটা পুকুর দেখলাম। grāmer pôthe āmrā ekṭā pukur dekhlām. On the village path we saw a pond.
পথে, on the way: পথে দেখা হল, we met on the way. রাস্তা is the road itself; পথ (pôth) is also the way you take.

The word, taken apart.
পথ (pôth) is Sanskrit patha, "a way, a path, a road" (Monier-Williams), the stem Sanskrit uses at the end of compounds, taken over whole.

Lesson 2 of 5.
গন্তব্য (gôntôbyô) — a destination.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to carry (a load), then the Bengali for a path.

You'll want to know: গন্তব্য.
গন্তব্য — gôntôbyô — "a destination".
আমাদের গন্তব্য একটা ছোট গ্রাম। āmāder gôntôbyô ekṭā chhoṭo grām. Our destination is a small village.
A word of signs and tickets as much as of talk: গন্তব্য (gôntôbyô) স্টেশন, the destination station.

The word, taken apart.
গন্তব্য (gôntôbyô) is Sanskrit gantavya, "to be gone to, to be reached" (Monier-Williams), a form of gam, to go: the place you must go to.

Lesson 3 of 5.
সফর (sôphôr) — a trip; a tour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a path, then the Bengali for a destination.

You'll want to know: সফর.
সফর — sôphôr — "a trip; a tour".
আমাদের সফর সাত দিনের ছিল। āmāder sôphôr sāt diner chhilô. Our trip lasted seven days.
Often a trip with a purpose: an official tour, a team's tour abroad.

The word, taken apart.
সফর (sôphôr) is usually traced to Arabic safar, "a journey", which reached Bengali through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
যাতায়াত করা (jātāyāt kôrā) — to commute; to go back and forth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a destination, then the Bengali for a trip.

You'll want to know: যাতায়াত করা.
যাতায়াত করা — jātāyāt kôrā — "to commute; to go back and forth".
মীরা রোজ বাসে যাতায়াত করে। mīrā roj bāse jātāyāt kôre. Mira commutes by bus every day.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ট্রেনে যাতায়াত করতেন, tini ṭrene jātāyāt kôrten.
The noun alone is যাতায়াত, the coming and going: এই রাস্তায় যাতায়াত, the traffic on this road.

The word, taken apart.
যাতায়াত করা (jātāyāt kôrā) joins Sanskrit yātāyāta, "going and coming" (Monier-Williams), a pairing of yāta, gone, and āyāta, come, to করা, to do.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ফিরতি (phirti) — return (of a journey); on the way back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a trip, then the Bengali for to commute.

You'll want to know: ফিরতি.
ফিরতি — phirti — "return (of a journey); on the way back".
ফিরতি পথে আমরা মামার বাড়িতে থামলাম। phirti pôthe āmrā māmār bāṛite thāmlām. On the way back we stopped at my uncle's house.
It stands before its noun: ফিরতি (phirti) টিকিট, a return ticket; ফিরতি (phirti) ট্রেন, the train back.

The word, taken apart.
ফিরতি (phirti) is built on the stem ফির- of ফেরা, "to return", which CDIAL lists as Bengali phirā, "to turn", under the reconstructed phirati, "moves, wanders, turns" (CDIAL 9078).
