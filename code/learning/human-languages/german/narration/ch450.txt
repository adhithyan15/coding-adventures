German, chapter 450: Geld und Konto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Geldautomat — the cash machine, the ATM.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the card reader, then the German for the change.

You'll want to know: der Geldautomat.
der Geldautomat — "the cash machine, the ATM".
Der Geldautomat ist direkt neben dem Supermarkt. The cash machine is right next to the supermarket.
Plural: die Geldautomaten. In Austria: der Bankomat.

The word, taken apart.
Geldautomat is built from Geld 'money' and Automat 'machine' (EtymDB), which goes back to Greek autómatos 'acting by itself'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Geheimzahl — the PIN (number).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the change, then the German for the cash machine.

You'll want to know: die Geheimzahl.
die Geheimzahl — "the PIN (number)".
Er hat seine Geheimzahl vergessen. He has forgotten his PIN.
Plural: die Geheimzahlen. Also die PIN.

The word, taken apart.
Geheimzahl is built from geheim 'secret', which is usually explained as first meaning 'belonging to the home', from Heim 'home', and Zahl 'number', inherited from Old High German zala (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: der Kontoauszug.
der Kontoauszug — "the bank statement".
Auf dem Kontoauszug sieht sie alle Zahlungen. She can see all the payments on her bank statement.
Plural: die Kontoauszüge.

The word, taken apart.
Kontoauszug is built from Konto 'account', borrowed from Italian conto 'calculation' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin computāre 'to reckon', and Auszug 'extract', from ausziehen 'to pull out'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
einlösen — to redeem (a voucher), to cash (a cheque).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the PIN, then the German for the bank statement.

You'll want to know: einlösen.
einlösen — "to redeem (a voucher), to cash (a cheque)".
An der Kasse löst sie einen Gutschein ein. At the till she redeems a voucher.
Separable: ich löse ein, ich habe eingelöst. Ein Versprechen einlösen — to keep a promise.

The word, taken apart.
einlösen is built from ein 'in' and lösen 'to release', inherited from Proto-Germanic lausijaną, built on lausaz 'loose' (EtymDB): to get back what a pledge held.

auszahlen is built from aus 'out' and zahlen 'to pay', from Zahl 'number', inherited from Old High German zala (EtymDB).
