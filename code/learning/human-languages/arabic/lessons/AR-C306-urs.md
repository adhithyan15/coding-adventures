## You'll want to know: عرس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C306-URS306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عرس** — *ʿurs* — "a wedding feast, a wedding".

> حضرنا عرس ابن عمي في القرية.
>
> *ḥaḍarnā ʿurs ibn ʿammī fī al-qarya.*
>
> We went to my cousin’s wedding in the village.

Plural: أعراس, *aʿrās*.

The taught عروس, bride, and عريس, groom, are the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عرس** (*ʿurs*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-s* (Lane), the root of the taught *ʿarūs*, a bride, and *ʿarīs*, a groom. *ʿUrs* is the feast held for them.
