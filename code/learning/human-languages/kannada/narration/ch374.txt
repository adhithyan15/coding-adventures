Kannada, chapter 374: Magic and Miracles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಜಾದೂ (jādū) — magic; a magic trick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a narrative, then the Kannada for a triumph.

You'll want to know: ಜಾದೂ.
ಜಾದೂ — jādū — "magic; a magic trick".
ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಬ್ಬ ಮನುಷ್ಯ ಮಕ್ಕಳಿಗೆ ಜಾದೂ ತೋರಿಸಿದನು. jātreyalli obba manuṣya makkaḷige jādū tōrisidanu. At the fair a man showed the children magic tricks.
A magician on stage is a ಜಾದೂಗಾರ.

The word, taken apart.
ಜಾದೂ (jādū) is a loan from Persian jādū, "magic, sorcery", which came through Hindustani.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹುಸಿ (husi) — false; empty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for magic, then the Kannada for a miracle.

You'll want to know: ಹುಸಿ.
ಹುಸಿ — husi — "false; empty".
ಆ ಜಾದೂಗಾರನ ಮಾತು ಹುಸಿ ಆಯಿತು. ā jādūgārana mātu husi āyitu. The magician's words turned out to be false.
ಹುಸಿ (husi) ಬೆದರಿಕೆ, an empty threat. ಹುಸಿ (husi) is also a lie.

The word, taken apart.
ಹುಸಿ (husi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4531 lists Kannada pusi, "to become or prove untrue, to lie", and as a noun "a falsehood"; old p- became h-.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸದ್ದು (saddu) — a noise; a sound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a miracle, then the Kannada for false.

You'll want to know: ಸದ್ದು.
ಸದ್ದು — saddu — "a noise; a sound".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ಅಡುಗೆಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಏನೋ ಸದ್ದು ಆಯಿತು. rātri aḍugemaneyalli ēnō saddu āyitu. At night there was some noise in the kitchen.
ಸದ್ದು (saddu) ಮಾಡು, to make a noise. ಸದ್ದಿಲ್ಲದೆ, without a sound.

The word, taken apart.
ಸದ್ದು (saddu) is usually traced to Sanskrit śabda, "sound, noise" (CDIAL 12298), through a Prakrit form sadda.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕೊಲ್ಲು (kollu) — to kill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for false, then the Kannada for a noise.

You'll want to know: ಕೊಲ್ಲು.
ಕೊಲ್ಲು — kollu — "to kill".
ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ವೀರ ರಾಜಕುಮಾರ ಆ ರಾಕ್ಷಸನನ್ನು ಕೊಂದನು. katheyalli vīra rājakumāra ā rākṣasanannu kondanu. In the story the brave prince killed the demon.
Past (I …): ಕೊಂದೆ, konde.
The past is irregular: ಕೊಂದೆ, I killed; ಕೊಂದನು, he killed.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಲ್ಲು (kollu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2132 lists Kannada kol, kollu, "to kill", and kole, "a killing".
