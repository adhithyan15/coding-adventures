## You'll want to know: प्रकाशक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C342-PRAKASHAK342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकाशक** — *prakāśak* — "a publisher".

> प्रकाशक ने वह आत्मकथा छापी।
>
> *prakāśak ne vah ātmakathā chāpī.*
>
> The publisher printed that autobiography.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकाशक** (*prakāśak*) is Sanskrit *prakāśaka*, "one who brings to light, who makes known" (Monier-Williams), from *prakāśa*, light.
