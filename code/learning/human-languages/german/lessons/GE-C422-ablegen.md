## You'll want to know: ablegen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C422-KREUZFAHRT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ablegen** — "to cast off, to leave port; to take off (a coat); to take (an exam)".

> Um zehn Uhr legt das Schiff wieder ab.
>
> *At ten o'clock the ship casts off again.*

Separable. *Eine Prüfung ablegen* — to take an exam. *Den Mantel ablegen* — to take off one's coat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ablegen* is built from *ab* 'off, away' and *legen* 'to lay', inherited from Old High German *leggen* (EtymDB).
