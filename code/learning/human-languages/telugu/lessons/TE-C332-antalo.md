## You'll want to know: అంతలో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C332-KSHANAM332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతలో** — *antalō* — "in the meantime".

> నేను ఫోను చేశాను, అంతలో అతనే వచ్చాడు.
>
> *nēnu phōnu cēśānu, antalō atanē vaccāḍu.*
>
> I phoned, and in the meantime he came himself.

అంత, that much, with -లో, in: within that span. It joins two happenings that overlap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతలో** (*antalō*) is native Telugu: *anta*, "that much", from the far demonstrative *a-* (DEDR 1), with the locative ending *-lō*, "in".
