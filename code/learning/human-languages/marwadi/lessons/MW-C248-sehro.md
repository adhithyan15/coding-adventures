## You'll want to know: सेहरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C248-MEHDI248-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सेहरो** — *sehro* — "a bridegroom's garland".

> सेहरो पीळो अर लाल है।
>
> *sehro pīḷo ar lāl hai.*
>
> The bridegroom's garland is yellow and red.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेहरो** (*sehro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śekhara*, "a chaplet". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *sehrā*, "a garland worn at a wedding" (CDIAL 12604).
