Gujarati, chapter 87: What We Wear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધોતી (dhotī) — a dhoti.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a phone, then the Gujarati for the post.

You'll want to know: ધોતી.
ધોતી (dhotī) — "a dhoti".

The word, taken apart.
ધોતી (dhotī) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under dhotta, "cloth", a reconstructed form, and weighs it against a lexical Sanskrit dhotra (CDIAL 6881).

What you've built.
One more word for what we wear.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: dhotī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dhotī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dhotī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a phone, then the Gujarati for the post, then say dhotī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ધોતી (dhotī) mean? ("A dhoti".) Say it once more.

The word, taken apart.
ખમીસ (khamīs) is a loan from Arabic qamīṣ, "shirt", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-m-ṣ (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ચંપલ (campal) — sandals, slippers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a dhoti, then the Gujarati for a shirt.

You'll want to know: ચંપલ.
ચંપલ (campal) — "sandals, slippers".

The word, taken apart.
ચંપલ (campal) is another form of cappal, "sandal". CDIAL has no entry for it. DEDR compares Hindi cappal, with a "Cf.", to the Dravidian words for a sandal, such as Telugu ceppu (DEDR 1963).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ટોપી (ṭopī) — a cap, a hat.

ટોપી (ṭopī) — "a cap, a hat".

The word, taken apart.
ટોપી (ṭopī) is a tadbhava. It comes from ṭoppa, "hat, covering", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5481). CDIAL thinks a link with Sanskrit āṭopa very doubtful.

The word, taken apart.
છત્રી (chatrī) is built on Sanskrit chattra, "parasol", which had a feminine chattrikā (CDIAL 4972). CDIAL lists no Gujarati form there.
