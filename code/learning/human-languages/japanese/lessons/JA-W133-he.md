## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-HE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-HO-01] -->

> **へ** — *he*

One stroke, and the pen never lifts:

1. start low on the left and **rise** to a low peak
2. turn over the peak and run **down to the right**

The peak sits left of the middle, so the way down is longer than the way up.
Keep the peak low and the whole sign wide.

On its own after a place word, **へ** is also a particle, and then it is read
*e*, the way **は** is read *wa* as the topic marker. That use comes later.

With **や** after it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**へや**, *heya*, a room.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-HE-01, JA-SCRIPT-HO-01, JA-SCRIPT-YA-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-HEYA] -->

1. Trace **へ** saying "up, over, down".
2. Write the whole row **は ひ ふ へ ほ**, and say it.
3. Write **へや** from memory and say *heya*.
