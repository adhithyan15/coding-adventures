## You'll want to know: نند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C489-KIN489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نند** — *nanad* — "a husband's sister".

> میری نند ہمارے گھر کے پاس رہتی ہے۔
>
> *merī nanad hamāre ghar ke pās rahtī hai.*
>
> My husband's sister lives near our house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نند** (*nanad*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nánāndṛ*, "husband's sister" (CDIAL 6946).
