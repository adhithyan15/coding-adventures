## You'll want to know: गलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C384-BARF384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गलना** — *galnā* — "to soften (in cooking), to dissolve".

> अगर दाल नहीं गलेगी तो उसे थोड़ी देर और पकाओ।
>
> *agar dāl nahīṃ galegī to use thoṛī der aur pakāo.*
>
> If the lentils don't soften, cook them a little longer.

Past (it …): वह दाल गल गई, *vah dāl gal gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गलना** (*galnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *galati*, "drips, oozes" (CDIAL 4074).
