## You'll want to know: शेकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C490-PATHYA490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शेकणे** — *śekṇe* — "to apply heat; to warm".

> आजीने गरम पाण्याच्या पिशवीने पाठ शेकली.
>
> *ājīne garam pāṇyācyā piśvīne pāṭh śeklī.*
>
> Grandmother warmed her back with a hot-water bag.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाठ शेकली, *tyāne pāṭh śeklī*.

Gentle heat on a sore spot; शेक, a warm compress.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शेकणे** (*śekṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *śekṇẽ*, "to warm oneself before a fire, foment", under the reconstructed *\*sēkk* (CDIAL 13581).
