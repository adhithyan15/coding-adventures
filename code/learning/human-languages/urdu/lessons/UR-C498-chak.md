## You'll want to know: چاک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C498-AVA498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چاک** — *chāk* — "a potter's wheel".

> کمہار چاک پر مٹی کا برتن بناتا ہے۔
>
> *kumhār chāk par miṭṭī kā bartan banātā hai.*
>
> The potter makes a clay pot on the wheel.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چاک** (*chāk*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cāk*, "any kind of wheel", under Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel" (CDIAL 4538).
