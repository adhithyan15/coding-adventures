Punjabi, chapter 319: How Are You Feeling?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਤਬੀਅਤ (tabīat) — how someone is feeling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to sink, then the Punjabi for a boatman.

You'll want to know: ਤਬੀਅਤ.
ਤਬੀਅਤ — tabīat — "how someone is feeling".
ਅੱਜ ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਤਬੀਅਤ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਹੈ? ajj tuhāḍī tabīat kiveṁ hai? How are you feeling today?
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਤਬੀਅਤ (tabīat) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic ṭabīʿa, "nature, temperament".

You'll want to know: ਰਾਜ਼ੀ.
ਰਾਜ਼ੀ — rāzī — "well again after an illness".
ਮੈਂ ਬੀਮਾਰ ਸੀ ਪਰ ਹੁਣ ਮੈਂ ਰਾਜ਼ੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ bīmār sī par huṇ maiṁ rāzī hāṁ. I was ill, but now I am well again.

The word, taken apart.
ਰਾਜ਼ੀ (rāzī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian rāzī, "content, satisfied"; how it came to mean "well again" is not traced in the sources used here.
