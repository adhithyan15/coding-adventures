## You'll want to know: अकस्मात्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C369-BHAGYA369-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अकस्मात्** — *akasmāt* — "by accident; without warning".

> यदि अकस्मात् वर्षा भवति, तर्हि अहं गृहे स्थास्यामि।
>
> *yadi akasmāt varṣā bhavati, tarhi ahaṃ gṛhe sthāsyāmi.*
>
> If it rains without warning, I will stay at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अकस्मात्** (*akasmāt*) is built from *a-*, "not", and *kasmāt*, "from what? why?"; MW gives *a-kasmāt* "without a why or a wherefore, accidentally, suddenly" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
