## Writing lessons print their first stroke-order filmstrips

29 writing lessons now open their Writing (or Script) block with a filmstrip. 25 draw one letter: ਹ ਮ ਨ ਸ ਤ (PA-W01), ਅ (PA-W02), ਬ ਭ ਦ
ਜ ਪ (PA-W03), ਡ ਰ (PA-W04), ਕ ਖ (PA-W05), ਫ (PA-W08), ਚ ਛ ਗ ਲ ਠ (PA-W09),
ਥ (PA-W10), ਪ (PA-S08-pappa), ਚ (PA-S10-chachcha) and ਭ (PA-S11-bhabbha).
Four draw a list of letters: ਸ · ਤ · ਕ (PA-S02-sassa-tatta-sihari),
ਟ · ਠ · ਡ (PA-S03-retroflex-row), ਣ · ਜ · ਵ (PA-S07-naana-jajja-vavva) and
ੜ · ਘ · ਦ (PA-S07-rara-ghagga-dadda).

The order each strip shows is the teaching order of the Alphabet Tracing
lesson in GNPS's Gurmukhi Sikho app (Apache-2.0), fitted to the printed Noto
Sans Gurmukhi letter. The headline comes first, as that lesson teaches it.
Each citation notes that fluent writers are often described as adding it
last. ਛ, ਨ and ਬ lift once less than the app, which matches the most common
count among Omniglot's copyists.

The track's script now has an inventory, `data/scripts/gurmukhi.json`: its 33
letters, 14 signs and 4 digits. The vowel signs, bindi, tippi, addak, halant
and the dot below have no stroke-order source, so the lessons that teach them
(and every word, which shares one headline) stay undrawn. This change is in
the book only: narration, modality and the lessons themselves do not change.
