## You'll want to know: भला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C381-KASH381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भला** — *bhalā* — "good, decent".

> अगर तुम भले आदमी होते तो सच बोलते।
>
> *agar tum bhale ādmī hote to sac bolte.*
>
> If you were a decent man, you would tell the truth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भला** (*bhalā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *bhalla*, "auspicious" (CDIAL 9408).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
