## You'll want to know: el máster
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C596-ESTUDIOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el máster** — "the master's degree".

> Quiero hacer un máster en historia.
>
> *I want to do a master's in history.*

This is the word in Spain; much of Latin America says *la maestría*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*máster* was borrowed from English *master*, which comes from Latin *magister* 'master, teacher' (EtymDB).
