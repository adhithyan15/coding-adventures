# بطالة (baṭāla) — unemployment

## You'll want to know: بطالة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C381-MUJTAMA381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بطالة** — *baṭāla* — "unemployment".

> البطالة مشكلة كبيرة في المدن.
>
> *al-baṭāla mushkila kabīra fī al-mudun.*
>
> Unemployment is a big problem in the cities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بطالة** (*baṭāla*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-ṭ-l*, "to be vain, to come to nothing" (Lane). *Baṭāla* is being idle, out of work: unemployment.
