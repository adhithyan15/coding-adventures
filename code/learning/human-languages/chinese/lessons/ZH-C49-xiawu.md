## You'll want to know: xiàwǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C49-DAY49-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàwǔ* — "afternoon".

> *xiàwǔ wǒ hěn máng* — I'm busy in the afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàwǔ* is built from xià 'lower, after' and 午 'noon', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *nguX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
