# আঁতকে ওঠা (ā̃tke oṭhā) — to recoil in fright; to gasp with alarm

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be startled, then the Bengali for terror.

## You'll want to know: আঁতকে ওঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C441-ATONKO441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**আঁতকে ওঠা** — *ā̃tke oṭhā* — "to recoil in fright; to gasp with alarm".

> সাপটা দেখে মীরা আঁতকে উঠল।
>
> *sāpṭā dekhe mīrā ā̃tke uṭhlô.*
>
> Mira recoiled in fright at the sight of the snake.

Past (he/she …): তিনি খবর শুনে আঁতকে উঠলেন, *tini khôbôr shune ā̃tke uṭhlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আঁতকে ওঠা** (*ā̃tke oṭhā*) is *ā̃tke*, the -এ form of a verb of sudden fright whose origin is not settled, with *oṭhā*, "to rise" (CDIAL 1793).
