## You'll want to know: بسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C315-AJDAD315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بسنا** — *basnā* — "to settle; to make a home".

> وہ دریا کے کنارے بس گئے۔
>
> *voh daryā ke kināre bas gae.*
>
> They settled on the bank of the river.

Past (he …): وہ یہاں بس گیا, *voh yahāṅ bas gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بسنا** (*basnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *basnā*, "dwells", under Sanskrit *vásati* (CDIAL 11435). **بستی**, a settlement, belongs with it.
