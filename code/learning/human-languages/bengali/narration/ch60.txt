Bengali, chapter 60: Chair, Table, Wall, Roof, Bed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
চেয়ার (cheyār) — a chair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a house, then the Bengali for a window.
