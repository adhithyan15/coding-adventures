## You'll want to know: ફી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C406-BORDER406-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ફી** — *phī* — "an official fee; a charge".

> વિઝાની ફી ઘણી વધારે છે.
>
> *vijhānī phī ghaṇī vadhāre che.*
>
> The visa fee is very high.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફી** (*phī*) is borrowed from English *fee*.
