Malayalam, chapter 358: Once Upon a Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പണ്ടുപണ്ട് (paṇṭupaṇṭŭ) — once upon a time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for ever since, then the Malayalam for all of a sudden.

You'll want to know: പണ്ടുപണ്ട്.
പണ്ടുപണ്ട് — paṇṭupaṇṭŭ — "once upon a time".
പണ്ടുപണ്ട് ഒരു ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ ഒരു പാവം കർഷകൻ ജീവിച്ചിരുന്നു. paṇṭupaṇṭŭ oru grāmattil oru pāvaṁ karṣakan jīviccirunnu. Once upon a time a poor farmer lived in a village.
The doubled പണ്ട്, long ago, opens a folk tale.

The word, taken apart.
പണ്ടുപണ്ട് (paṇṭupaṇṭŭ) doubles പണ്ട് (paṇṭŭ), "long ago", which DEDR 3999 sets with പഴയ, "old": native Dravidian. Said twice, it pushes the story back into the time of tales.

Lesson 2 of 5.
അക്കാലത്ത് (akkālattŭ) — in those days.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for all of a sudden, then the Malayalam for once upon a time.

You'll want to know: അക്കാലത്ത്.
അക്കാലത്ത് — akkālattŭ — "in those days".
അക്കാലത്ത് ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ ബസ് ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നില്ല. akkālattŭ grāmattil basŭ uṇṭāyirunnilla. In those days there was no bus in the village.
The opposite of ഇക്കാലത്ത്, nowadays: അ-, that, + കാലം, time, in the locative.

The word, taken apart.
അക്കാലത്ത് (akkālattŭ) is built from the far deictic അ-, "that", and കാലം (kālaṁ), "time", from Sanskrit kāla, "time" (CDIAL 3084), with the locative ending -ത്ത്.

Lesson 3 of 5.
അന്നേരം (annēraṁ) — at that moment; right then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for once upon a time, then the Malayalam for in those days.

You'll want to know: അന്നേരം.
അന്നേരം — annēraṁ — "at that moment; right then".
അന്നേരം വാതിലിൽ ആരോ മുട്ടി. annēraṁ vātilil ārō muṭṭi. At that moment someone knocked at the door.
അ-, that, + നേരം, time: the exact moment in a story.

The word, taken apart.
അന്നേരം (annēraṁ) is native Dravidian: the far deictic അ-, "that", and നേരം (nēraṁ), "time, season" (DEDR 3774).

Lesson 4 of 5.
തുടർന്ന് (tuṭarnnŭ) — following that; subsequently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for in those days, then the Malayalam for at that moment.

You'll want to know: തുടർന്ന്.
തുടർന്ന് — tuṭarnnŭ — "following that; subsequently".
ശക്തമായ മഴ പെയ്തു, തുടർന്ന് വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കം ഉണ്ടായി. śaktamāya maḻa peytu, tuṭarnnŭ veḷḷappokkaṁ uṇṭāyi. Heavy rain fell, and following that there was a flood.
A form of തുടരുക, to continue: in what followed. Common in the news.

The word, taken apart.
തുടർന്ന് (tuṭarnnŭ) is native Dravidian, the past participle of തുടരുക (tuṭaruka), "to continue, to follow on" (DEDR 3480). In the news it links one event to the next.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ഉടനീളം (uṭanīḷaṁ) — throughout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for at that moment, then the Malayalam for following that.

You'll want to know: ഉടനീളം.
ഉടനീളം — uṭanīḷaṁ — "throughout".
യാത്രയിൽ ഉടനീളം കുട്ടി ഉറങ്ങി. yātrayil uṭanīḷaṁ kuṭṭi uṟaṅṅi. The child slept throughout the journey.
It follows a noun: വർഷം ഉടനീളം (uṭanīḷaṁ), all through the year.

The word, taken apart.
ഉടനീളം (uṭanīḷaṁ) is native Dravidian. Its second part is നീളം (nīḷaṁ), "length" (DEDR 3692): along the whole length of something. The origin of the first part is not settled.
