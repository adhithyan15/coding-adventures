## You'll want to know: la didascalia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C286-EDIZIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la didascalia** — "the caption".

> La didascalia sotto la foto è sbagliata.
>
> *The caption under the photo is wrong.*

Also the stage directions in a play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*didascalia* is a learned word usually traced to Greek *didaskalía* "teaching", built on *didáskein* "to teach"; EtymDB has no Italian entry.
