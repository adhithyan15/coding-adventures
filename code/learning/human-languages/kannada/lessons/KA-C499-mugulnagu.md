## You'll want to know: ಮುಗುಳ್ನಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C499-MANASTHITI499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಗುಳ್ನಗು** — *muguḷnagu* — "to smile".

> ಹಳೆಯ ಫೋಟೋ ನೋಡಿ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಮುಗುಳ್ನಕ್ಕರು.
>
> *haḷeya phōṭō nōḍi ajji muguḷnakkaru.*
>
> Looking at the old photo, grandmother smiled.

Past (I …): ಮುಗುಳ್ನಕ್ಕೆ, *muguḷnakke*.

ಮುಗುಳು, a bud + ನಗು, to laugh: a laugh still in the bud. The noun is ಮುಗುಳ್ನಗೆ, a smile.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಗುಳ್ನಗು** (*muguḷnagu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *muguḷ*, "a bud; to close like a bud" (DEDR 4893), and *nagu*, "to laugh" (DEDR 3569).
