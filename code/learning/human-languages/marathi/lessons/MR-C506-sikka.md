## You'll want to know: शिक्का
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C506-KACHERI506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिक्का** — *śikkā* — "a rubber stamp; a seal".

> लिपिकाने कागदावर शिक्का मारला.
>
> *lipikāne kāgdāvar śikkā mārlā.*
>
> The clerk stamped the paper.

Masculine.

शिक्का मारणे, to stamp: the stamp is struck.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिक्का** (*śikkā*) is a loan from Persian *sikka*, "a stamp, a coin die", usually traced to Arabic *sikka*.
