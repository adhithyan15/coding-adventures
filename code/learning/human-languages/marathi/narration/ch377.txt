Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रभाव (prabhāv) — an influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for quality, then the Marathi for a standard.

You'll want to know: प्रभाव.
प्रभाव — prabhāv — "an influence".
त्याच्यावर वडिलांचा मोठा प्रभाव आहे. tyācyāvar vaḍilāñcā moṭhā prabhāv āhe. His father has a great influence on him.
Masculine.
X वर Y चा प्रभाव (prabhāv), Y's influence on X.

The word, taken apart.
प्रभाव (prabhāv) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prabhāva, "power, might" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दबाव (dabāv) — outside pressure; pressure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a standard, then the Marathi for an influence.

You'll want to know: दबाव.
दबाव — dabāv — "outside pressure; pressure".
घरच्यांच्या दबावामुळे तिने नोकरी सोडली. gharacyāñcyā dabāvāmuḷe tine nokrī soḍlī. Under pressure from her family, she left her job.
Masculine.
दबाव (dabāv) आणणे, to put pressure on.

The word, taken apart.
दबाव (dabāv) is built on दाबणे, "to press", with the noun ending -आव; CDIAL lists Marathi dābṇẽ, "to press", under dabb-, "press" (CDIAL 6173).

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रोत्साहन देणे (protsāhan deṇe) — to encourage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an influence, then the Marathi for outside pressure.

You'll want to know: प्रोत्साहन देणे.
प्रोत्साहन देणे — protsāhan deṇe — "to encourage".
शिक्षकांनी मला गाणं शिकायला प्रोत्साहन दिलं. śikṣakānnī malā gāṇaṁ śikāylā protsāhan dilaṁ. The teacher encouraged me to learn singing.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने प्रोत्साहन दिलं, tyāne protsāhan dilaṁ.
The one encouraged takes -ला, the aim -ला or -साठी.

The word, taken apart.
प्रोत्साहन देणे (protsāhan deṇe) joins the tatsama प्रोत्साहन, Sanskrit protsāhana, "encouraging, inciting" (Monier-Williams), to देणे, "to give".

Lesson 5 of 5.
खटकणे (khaṭakṇe) — to bother; to jar on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to encourage, then the Marathi for inspiration.

You'll want to know: खटकणे.
खटकणे — khaṭakṇe — "to bother; to jar on".
त्याचं उद्धट बोलणं मला खटकलं. tyācaṁ uddhaṭ bolṇaṁ malā khaṭaklaṁ. His rude way of talking bothered me.
Past (to him …): त्याला खटकलं, tyālā khaṭaklaṁ.
Dative: मला खटकलं, it bothered me; what jars is the subject.

The word, taken apart.
खटकणे (khaṭakṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
