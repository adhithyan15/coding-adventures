## You'll want to know: مردود شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C399-KONKUR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مردود شدن** (*mardud shodan*) — "to fail (an exam)".

> او در امتحان ریاضی مردود شد.
>
> *u dar emtehân-e riyâzi mardud shod.*
>
> He failed the maths exam.

Formal; in speech *rad shodan* or *oftâdan*. Stem *shav-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مردود** is usually traced to Arabic *mardūd* "rejected, sent back", from the same Arabic family as *radd*, the source of Persian *rad*; *shodan* "to become" is inherited.
