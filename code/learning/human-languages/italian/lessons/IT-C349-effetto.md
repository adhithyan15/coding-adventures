## You'll want to know: l'effetto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C349-CAUSA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'effetto** — "the effect".

> La medicina ha fatto effetto dopo dieci minuti.
>
> *The medicine took effect after ten minutes.*

*Fare effetto* — to take effect. *In effetti* — in fact, indeed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*effetto* comes from Latin *effectus* "an effect, a result, a purpose" (EtymDB), from *efficere* "to bring about", built from *ex* "out" and *facere* "to do, to make".
