## You'll want to know: रेखा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C527-KALA527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रेखा** — *rekhā* — "a line".

> उसने काग़ज़ पर एक सीधी रेखा खींची।
>
> *Usne kāġaz par ek sīdhī rekhā khīñcī.*
>
> He drew a straight line on the paper.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रेखा** (*rekhā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *rekhā*, "a line, a stroke" (Monier-Williams).
