Persian, chapter 377: Crises and solutions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بحران (bohrân) — a crisis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to happen, then the Persian for to admit.

You'll want to know: بحران.
بحران (bohrân) — "a crisis".
کشور در یک بحران بزرگ است. keshvar dar yek bohrân-e bozorg ast. The country is in a big crisis.
Plural bohrânhâ. Bohrâni — critical.

The word, taken apart.
بحران (bohrân) was borrowed from Arabic buḥrān "crisis" (EtymDB); Persian also forms the adjective bohrâni "critical".

Lesson 2 of 5.
راه حل (râh-e hall) — a solution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to admit, then the Persian for a crisis.

You'll want to know: راه حل.
راه حل (râh-e hall) — "a solution".
برای این مشکل یک راه حل ساده پیدا کردیم. barâye in moshkel yek râh-e hall-e sâde peydâ kardim. We found a simple solution to this problem.
Râh "way" + ezafe + hall "solving". Plural râh-e hallhâ.

The word, taken apart.
راه حل (râh-e hall) is literally "the way of solving": râh "road, way" is inherited Persian (EtymDB traces it back through Proto-Iranian), and hall "solving" comes from Arabic ḥall.

Lesson 3 of 5.
جلوگیری کردن (jolowgiri kardan) — to prevent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a crisis, then the Persian for a solution.

You'll want to know: جلوگیری کردن.
جلوگیری کردن (jolowgiri kardan) — "to prevent".
پلیس از تصادف جلوگیری کرد. polis az tasâdof jolowgiri kard. The police prevented an accident.
Takes az: az chizi jolowgiri kardan. Jolow "front" + giri "taking".

The word, taken apart.
جلوگیری کردن (jolowgiri kardan) is native Persian, built from jolow "front" + giri "taking", from gir-, the present stem of gereftan "to take", + kardan. To prevent is to take the front, to block the way.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پیشگیری (pishgiri) — prevention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a solution, then the Persian for to prevent.

You'll want to know: پیشگیری.
پیشگیری (pishgiri) — "prevention".
پیشگیری بهتر از درمان است. pishgiri behtar az darmân ast. Prevention is better than cure.
Pish "before" + giri "taking". Pishgiri kardan — to prevent (an illness).

The word, taken apart.
پیشگیری (pishgiri) is a native Persian compound built from pish "before" + giri "taking", built on gir-, the present stem of gereftan "to take": taking hold of a problem before it comes.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اهمیت داشتن (ahamiyat dâshtan) — to matter, to be important.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to prevent, then the Persian for prevention.

You'll want to know: اهمیت داشتن.
اهمیت داشتن (ahamiyat dâshtan) — "to matter, to be important".
برای من نظر تو اهمیت دارد. barâye man nazar-e to ahamiyat dârad. Your opinion matters to me.
Ahamiyyat "importance" + dâshtan. Ahamiyyat nadârad — it doesn't matter.

The word, taken apart.
اهمیت is usually traced to Arabic ahammiyya "importance", from the same Arabic family as mohem "important"; dâshtan "to have" is the inherited Persian verb.
