Arabic, chapter 232: Dizziness, Ointment, Surgeon, Crutch, Bleeding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دوار (duwār) — dizziness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a pharmacy, then the Arabic for a nurse.

You'll want to know: دوار.
دوار — duwār — "dizziness".

The word, taken apart.
دوار (duwār) is built on the native Arabic root d-w-r, "to turn round" (Lane). Duwār has the fuʿāl shape of illness words: the head turning round.

The word, taken apart.
مرهم (marham) is disputed in Lane's sources: one calls it arabicized from Persian marham, and others derive it from rihma, "soft rain", for its softness.
