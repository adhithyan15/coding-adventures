Sanskrit, chapter 433: Income and Shares.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
आयः (āyaḥ) — income.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for saves up, then the Sanskrit for savings.

You'll want to know: आयः.
आयः — āyaḥ — "income".
कृषकस्य आयः अल्पः अस्ति। kṛṣakasya āyaḥ alpaḥ asti. The farmer's income is small.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आयः (āyaḥ) is built from ā, "towards", and the root √i, "to go": what comes in; MW gives āya as arrival and as income, revenue (MW).

वणिक् मूलधनेन व्यापारं करोति। vaṇik mūladhanena vyāpāraṃ karoti. The merchant trades with his capital.

Lesson 5 of 6.
प्रतिभूः (pratibhūḥ) — a guarantor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a portion, then the Sanskrit for a share.

You'll want to know: प्रतिभूः.
प्रतिभूः — pratibhūḥ — "a guarantor".
मम मित्रं मम प्रतिभूः भवति। mama mitraṃ mama pratibhūḥ bhavati. My friend stands as my guarantor.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिभूः (pratibhūḥ) is built from prati, "in place of", and the root √bhū, "to be", which also gives bhavati, "is": one who stands in for another; MW gives pratibhū as a surety, security, bail (MW).
