French, chapter 187: In that case, Late, Suddenly, Until, Since.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
alors — in that case, so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for courage, then the French for shame.

You'll want to know: alors.
alors — "in that case, so".

The word, taken apart.
alors — à and Old French lors, "then", from Latin illa hora, "at that hour". (EtymDB 2.1).
