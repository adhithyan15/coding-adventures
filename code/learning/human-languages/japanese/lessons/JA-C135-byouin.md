## You'll want to know: びょういん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-BYOUIN]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YO-01, JA-SCRIPT-U-01, JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-SCRIPT-N-01] -->

**びょういん** — *byōin* — "a hospital".

Four beats: *byo–o–i–n*. The first sign is **ひ** with the voicing mark, so
*hi* becomes *bi*, and a small **ょ** joins it into one beat, *byo*. The rest,
**う**, **い** and **ん**, you already write. The next lesson writes the first
sign.

Keep the **ょ** small. With a full-size **よ** the word would be *biyōin*, a
hair salon.
