Mandarin Chinese, chapter 503: Art.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
书法 (shūfǎ) — calligraphy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a famous painting, then the Mandarin for an art exhibition.

You'll want to know: 书法.
书法 — shūfǎ — "calligraphy".
wǒ yéye xué le shí nián shūfǎ — My grandpa studied calligraphy for ten years.

The word, taken apart.
书法 (shūfǎ) is built from 书 'writing' and 法 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, syo and pjop (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a rule' and 'a norm'.

Lesson 4 of 6.
chuàngzuò — to create (art or writing).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the arts, then the Mandarin for a work of art.

You'll want to know: chuàngzuò.
chuàngzuò — "to create (art or writing)".
tā yòng le wǔ nián chuàngzuò zhège zuòpǐn — He spent five years creating this work.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
chuàngzuò is built from chuàng 'to found' and 作 'to make', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsrhjangH and tsak (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the level-tone reading 'a wound', and gives the falling-tone one as another spelling of a word glossed 'to begin' and 'to found a craft'.

Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a work of art, then the Mandarin for to create (art or writing).

You'll want to know: 年画.
年画 — niánhuà — "a new year print (hung at the spring festival)".
guònián de shíhou wǒ nǎinai mǎi le yìxiē niánhuà — At the new year, my grandma bought some new year prints.

The word, taken apart.
年画 (niánhuà) is built from 年 'a year' and 画 'a painting', both inherited from Middle Chinese, nen and hweaH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the grain is ripe'.
