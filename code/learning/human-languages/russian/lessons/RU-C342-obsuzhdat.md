## You'll want to know: обсуждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C342-OBSUZHDAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**обсуждать** (*obsuzhdát*) — "to discuss".

> Вчера мы обсуждали план на лето.
>
> *Vcherá my obsuzhdáli plan na léto.*
>
> Yesterday we discussed the plan for the summer.

Imperfective; perfective *обсудить*. It takes the accusative with no preposition: *обсуждать фильм*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**обсуждать** is built from *об-* "around" and *судить* "to judge", on *суд* "a court, a judgement", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*sǫdŭ* (EtymDB). To discuss is to judge a thing from all sides.
