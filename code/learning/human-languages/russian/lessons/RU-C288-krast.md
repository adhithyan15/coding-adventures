## You'll want to know: красть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C288-KRAZHA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**красть** (*krast*) — "to steal".

> Кто-то украл мой велосипед.
>
> *Kto-to ukrál moy velosipéd.*
>
> Someone stole my bicycle.

Imperfective; perfective *украсть* (*ukrást*). Present *краду*, *крадёшь* (*kradyósh*); past *крал*, *крала*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*красть* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *krasti* "to steal", which EtymDB traces to PIE *kreh₂d(ʰ)-*.
