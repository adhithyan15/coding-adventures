## You'll want to know: इक्का
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C526-FURSAT526-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इक्का** — *ikkā* — "an ace (in cards)".

> मीरा के हाथ में चार इक्के थे।
>
> *Mīrā ke hāth meṁ cār ikke the.*
>
> Mira had four aces in her hand.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इक्का** (*ikkā*) is usually traced to Persian *yakka*, "single, one alone".
