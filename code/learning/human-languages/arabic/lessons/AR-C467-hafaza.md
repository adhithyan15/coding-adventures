## You'll want to know: حافظ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C467-MAHMIYYA467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حافظ** — *ḥāfaẓa* — "to protect; to preserve".

> يحافظ الناس هنا على الغابة.
>
> *yuḥāfiẓu an-nās hunā ʿalā al-ghāba.*
>
> People here look after the forest.

Present: يحافظ, *yuḥāfiẓ*.

It takes *ʿalā*: *ḥāfaẓa ʿalā*, "to take care of".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حافظ** (*ḥāfaẓa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-f-ẓ*, "to guard, to keep" (Lane). It is Form III.
