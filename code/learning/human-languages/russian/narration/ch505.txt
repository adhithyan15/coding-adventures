Russian, chapter 505: Gone Fishing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
рыбалка (rybálka) — fishing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pack of cards, then the Russian for to cheat.

You'll want to know: рыбалка.
рыбалка (rybálka) — "fishing".
В субботу мы с папой едем на рыбалку. V subbótu my s pápoy yédem na rybálku. On Saturday Dad and I are going fishing.
Feminine. Ехать на рыбалку — to go fishing.

The word, taken apart.
рыбалка (rybálka) is built on рыба 'fish', through рыбак 'fisherman'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
удочка (údochka) — a fishing rod.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cheat, then the Russian for fishing.

You'll want to know: удочка.
удочка (údochka) — "a fishing rod".
Он взял удочку и пошёл к озеру. On vzyal údochku i poshyól k ózeru. He took his fishing rod and went to the lake.
Feminine. A diminutive of the old word уда.

The word, taken apart.
удочка (údochka) is a diminutive of the old word уда 'a fishing line'; EtymDB has no entry, and уда is usually traced to Proto-Slavic ǫda.

Lesson 4 of 5.
клевать (klevát) — to bite (of fish); to peck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fishing rod, then the Russian for a hook.

You'll want to know: клевать.
клевать (klevát) — "to bite (of fish); to peck".
Сегодня рыба плохо клюёт. Segódnya rýba plókho klyuyót. The fish aren't biting well today.
Imperfective; present клюю, клюёшь (-ева- becomes -ю-). Perfective клюнуть.

The word, taken apart.
клевать (klevát) is inherited from Proto-Slavic kľьvati 'to peck, to bite' (EtymDB).
