## You'll want to know: séjourner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C424-HEBERGEMENT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**séjourner** — "to stay for a while, to spend time (somewhere)".

> Ils ont séjourné une semaine au bord du lac.
>
> *They stayed a week by the lake.*

More formal than *rester*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*séjourner* goes back to Vulgar Latin *subdiurnāre* 'to spend the day', built on Latin *diurnus* 'of the day', from *diēs* 'day' (Lewis & Short).
