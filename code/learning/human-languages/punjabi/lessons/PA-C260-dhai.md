# ਢਾਈ (ḍhāī) — two and a half

## You'll want to know: ਢਾਈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C260-TIME260-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਾਈ** — *ḍhāī* — "two and a half".

> ਮੈਂ ਢਾਈ ਵਜੇ ਘਰ ਆਵਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ ḍhāī vaje ghar āvāṅgā.*
>
> I will come home at half past two.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢਾਈ** (*ḍhāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ardhatṛtīya*, "two and a half"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhāī*, "two and a half", under it (CDIAL 651).
