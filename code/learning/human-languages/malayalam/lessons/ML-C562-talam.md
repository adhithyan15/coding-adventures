## You'll want to know: താളം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C562-RAGAM562-01]; assesses=[] -->

**താളം** — *tāḷaṁ* — "rhythm; a beat".

> കുട്ടികൾ പാട്ടിന്റെ താളത്തിൽ കൈ കൊട്ടി.
>
> *kuṭṭikaḷ pāṭṭinṟe tāḷattil kai koṭṭi.*
>
> The children clapped to the rhythm of the song.

താളം തെറ്റുക, to lose the beat, also means to go wrong.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**താളം** (*tāḷaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tāla*, which CDIAL gives as a cymbal (CDIAL 5801); in Indian music it came to mean the beat the cymbals keep.
