## You'll want to know: qiē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C113-COOK113-01]; assesses=[] -->

*qiē* — "to cut; to slice".

> *wǒ qiē cài* — I'll chop the vegetables.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiē* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to cut' and 'to carve'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
