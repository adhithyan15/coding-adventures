## You'll want to know: نظافة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C443-MULAA443-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نظافة** — *naẓāfa* — "cleanliness".

> كانت نظافة الغرفة ممتازة.
>
> *kānat naẓāfat al-ghurfa mumtāza.*
>
> The cleanliness of the room was excellent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نظافة** (*naẓāfa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ẓ-f*, "to be clean" (Lane). It is the noun beside *naẓīf*, "clean".
