## You'll want to know: schildern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C283-ZEUGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**schildern** — "to recount, to describe vividly".

> Der Zeuge schilderte den Unfall genau.
>
> *The witness recounted the accident precisely.*

Stronger than *beschreiben*: you paint the scene in words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schildern* is built on *Schild* "shield", a cousin of English *shield* (EtymDB): it first meant painting coats of arms, then painting a scene in words.
