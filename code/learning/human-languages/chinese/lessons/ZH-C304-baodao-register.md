## You'll want to know: 报到
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C304-SCHOOL304-03]; assesses=[] -->

**报到** — *bàodào* — "to report in on arrival; to sign in".

> **新生明天来学校报到** *xīnshēng míngtiān lái xuéxiào bàodào* — The new students sign in at school tomorrow.

It sounds the same as *bàodào* 'to report the news'; this one means arriving and signing in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**报到** (*bàodào*) is built from 报 'to report' and 到 'to arrive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawH* and *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to reach': to report that you have arrived.
