Persian, chapter 125: Hard, Ugly, Young, Old, Lazy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سفت (seft) — hard, firm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for dry, then the Persian for soft.

You'll want to know: سفت.
سفت — seft — "hard, firm".

What you've built.
