## You'll want to know: نوبت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C541-SURAT541-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نوبت** — *naubat* — "a point (that things come to)".

> اگر نوبت یہاں تک پہنچی تو پولیس کو بتائیں گے۔
>
> *agar naubat yahāṅ tak pahuṅchī to polīs ko batāeṅ ge.*
>
> If it comes to that, we will tell the police.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نوبت** (*naubat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nawba*, "a turn, a time".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
