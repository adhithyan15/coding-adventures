# వస్తే (vastē) — if

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C32-VELLU-02, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-BETTER-TO-01, TE-LEX-C64-JOIN-03, TE-LEX-C177-KARANAM177-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C323-SPAN-GA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I will go" (*nēnu veḷtānu*) and "you had better go to the hospital" (*mīru āsupatriki veḷtē mañcidi*). Then "in that case" and "if by chance". (*ayitē*, *okavēḷa*.)

## You'll want to know: -తే, 'if'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C324-TE-CONDITIONAL-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C64-JOIN-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C32-VELLU-02, TE-LEX-C64-JOIN-03] -->

> **నువ్వు వస్తే నేను కూడా వస్తాను.**
>
> *nuvvu vastē nēnu kūḍā vastānu.* — "If you come, I will come too."

Where the form you know ends in **-తాను**, **-తే** takes the place of the ending: **వస్తాను** → **వస్తే**,
**వెళ్తాను** → **వెళ్తే**, **చేస్తాను** → **చేస్తే**, **ఉంటాను** → **ఉంటే**, **తింటాను** →
**తింటే**. Some verbs put **-ఇతే** on the past stem instead: **చదివితే**, **పడితే**, **అడిగితే**.

It does not change for the person: **నేను వస్తే**, **అతను వస్తే**, **వాళ్ళు వస్తే**. The **-తే మంచిది**
of advice was this ending. So is **అయితే**, "in that case": the **-తే** of the verb "to be", "if it is so".

## You'll want to know: ఒకవేళ … -తే
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C324-OKAVELA-TE-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C177-KARANAM177-01, TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-03] -->

> **ఒకవేళ వర్షం పడితే, మనం ఇంట్లోనే ఉందాం.**
>
> *okavēḷa varṣaṁ paḍitē, manaṁ iṇṭlōnē undāṁ.* — "If by chance it rains, let's stay at home."

**ఒకవేళ** in front warns that the condition may well not happen; the **-తే** still does the work.
**మనం** (*manaṁ*, "we, you and I") and **ఉందాం** (*undāṁ*, "let's stay"), with the **-దాం** of **కలుద్దాం**.
**ఇంట్లోనే** (*iṇṭlōnē*, "right at home") leans on **ఇంట్లో** with an **-ఏ**.
