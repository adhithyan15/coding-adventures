# હસમુખું (hasmukhũ) — cheerful; smiling

## You'll want to know: હસમુખું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C463-VACAL463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**હસમુખું** — *hasmukhũ* — "cheerful; smiling".

> અમારી નર્સ હંમેશા હસમુખી રહે છે.
>
> *amārī nars hammeśā hasmukhī rahe che.*
>
> Our nurse is always cheerful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હસમુખું** (*hasmukhũ*) is built from the stem of **હસવું** (*hasvũ*), "to laugh", and *mukh*, "face", Sanskrit *mukha* (Monier-Williams): a laughing face.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
