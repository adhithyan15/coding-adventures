## HL-C365 — Hindi seventh declares its taught feminine form

Chapter 83 presents and practises feminine **सातवीं** beside masculine
**सातवाँ**, but its declaration exposes only the masculine form. The corpus report
therefore leaves the explicitly taught feminine agreement form looking untaught.

Declare **सातवीं** beside **सातवाँ** so the metadata matches both the lesson's
presentation and its guided practice without introducing an unsupported form.
