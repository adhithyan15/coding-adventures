German, chapter 83: Forest, Stone, Island, Field, Sky.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Wald — a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a leaf; a sheet of paper, then the German for a mountain.

You'll want to know: der Wald.
der Wald — "a forest".

The word, taken apart.
Wald from Old High German wald from Proto-Germanic walþuz, "high land covered with wood" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) English wold, as in the Cotswolds, is the same word.
