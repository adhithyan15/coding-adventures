Lesson 1 of 7.
दर्वी (darvī) — a ladle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a chair, then the Sanskrit for a board.

You'll want to know: दर्वी.
दर्वी — darvī — "a ladle".

The word, taken apart.
darvī "a ladle" is old, in the Rigveda: MW explains it as "the wooden one", and CDIAL 6206 refers it to dru "tree, wood". MW sets that word beside Greek drys "oak" and English tree.

कटाहः — kaṭāhaḥ — "a frying pan".

The word, taken apart.
kaṭāha "a frying pan, a cauldron with handles" appears from the Mahābhārata on, and neither MW nor CDIAL gives a derivation, so its origin is uncertain. It lives on as Hindi kaṛāhī "frying pan" (CDIAL 2638).

Lesson 3 of 7.
पात्रम् (pātram) — a vessel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a ladle, then the Sanskrit for a frying pan.

You'll want to know: पात्रम्.
पात्रम् — pātram — "a vessel".

The word, taken apart.
pātra comes from the root √pā "drink" with the tool suffix -tra (CDIAL 8055): "the thing to drink from". It is inherited from Indo-European, a cousin of Latin pōculum "cup" (EtymDB), and lives on as Punjabi pātar "vessel" (CDIAL 8055).

Lesson 4 of 7.
कूपी (kūpī) — a bottle, a flask.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a frying pan, then the Sanskrit for a vessel.

You'll want to know: कूपी.
कूपी — kūpī — "a bottle, a flask".

The word, taken apart.
kūpī is a feminine of kūpa "a hole, a hollow, a well" (MW), and its sense "flask, bottle" comes from a late dictionary. MW compares Greek kypē "hollow"; kūpa itself lives on as Gujarati kuvo "small well" (CDIAL 3400).

Lesson 5 of 7.
छत्रिका (chatrikā) — a small umbrella.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a vessel, then the Sanskrit for a bottle.

You'll want to know: छत्रिका.
छत्रिका — chatrikā — "a small umbrella".

The word, taken apart.
chatrikā is chattra "a parasol" with the small-thing suffix -ikā, and chattra comes from the root √chad "cover" (CDIAL 4972). It lives on as Hindi chātā and Punjabi chatrī "umbrella" (CDIAL 4972).
