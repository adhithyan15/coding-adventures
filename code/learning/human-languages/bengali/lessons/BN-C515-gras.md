## You'll want to know: গ্রাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C515-PORIBESHON515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রাস** — *grās* — "a mouthful".

> শিশুটা দুই গ্রাস ভাত খেল।
>
> *Shishuṭā dui grās bhāt khelô.*
>
> The child ate two mouthfuls of rice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রাস** (*grās*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *grāsa*, "a mouthful, a morsel" (Monier-Williams).
