# சூழ்ச்சி (cūḻcci) — a scheme; a ploy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C336-VETRI336-05, TA-LEX-C337-SATHI337-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to occupy, then the Tamil for a conspiracy.

## You'll want to know: சூழ்ச்சி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C337-SATHI337-02]; assesses=[] -->

**சூழ்ச்சி** — *cūḻcci* — "a scheme; a ploy".

> தளபதியின் சூழ்ச்சி கடைசியில் வெளிப்பட்டது.
>
> *taḷapatiyiṉ cūḻcci kaṭaiciyil veḷippaṭṭatu.*
>
> The commander's scheme came out in the end.

சூழ்ச்சி செய், to scheme.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சூழ்ச்சி** (*cūḻcci*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2735 gives it under சூழ், "to deliberate, conspire", as "deliberation, counsel".
