## You'll want to know: આગિયો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C445-BIRDS445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**આગિયો** — *āgiyo* — "a firefly".

> ચોમાસાની રાતે ખેતરમાં આગિયા ચમકે છે.
>
> *comāsānī rāte khetarmā̃ āgiyā camke che.*
>
> On monsoon nights fireflies glow in the fields.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આગિયો** (*āgiyo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *agnika*, "fiery"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a glow-worm" (CDIAL 57): the little insect with a fire in it.
