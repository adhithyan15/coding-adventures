## You'll want to know: તરસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C458-VIRAH458-04]; assesses=[] -->

**તરસવું** — *tarasvũ* — "to long for; to crave".

> દાદી દીકરાને જોવા તરસે છે.
>
> *dādī dīkrāne jovā tarse che.*
>
> Grandmother is longing to see her son.

Past (he …): તે ઘરે જવા તરસતો હતો, *te ghare javā tarasto hato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તરસવું** (*tarasvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*tṛṣati*, "is thirsty"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "is thirsty" (CDIAL 5935), and Gujarati also uses it for longing, a thirst of the heart.
