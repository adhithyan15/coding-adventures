## You'll want to know: verber, verberis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C408-MENDUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**verber, verberis** — "a lash, a blow; (plural) a beating".

> Puer verbera timet.
>
> *The boy fears a beating.*

*verber, verberis* n., mostly plural: *verbera*, blows, a flogging. English *reverberate* comes from its verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verber* is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to turn, to bend" (EtymDB), which also gives English *warp*. English *reverberate* comes from its verb *verberō* "to beat".
