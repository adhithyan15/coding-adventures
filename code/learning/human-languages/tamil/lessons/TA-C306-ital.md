## You'll want to know: இதழ்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C306-THALAIPPU306-03]; assesses=[] -->

**இதழ்** — *itaḻ* — "an issue; a magazine".

> இந்த வார இதழில் ஒரு நல்ல கதை வந்தது.
>
> *inta vāra itaḻil oru nalla katai vantatu.*
>
> A good story appeared in this week's issue.

In older Tamil இதழ் is a petal or a palm leaf; நாளிதழ், a daily paper, is நாள் + இதழ்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இதழ்** (*itaḻ*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 459): a petal or a palmyra leaf, the leaf people once wrote on, and so an issue of a paper.
