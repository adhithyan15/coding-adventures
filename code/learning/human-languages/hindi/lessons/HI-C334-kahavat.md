## You'll want to know: कहावत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C334-KATHA334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कहावत** — *kahāvat* — "a saying; a proverb".

> दादी हर कहानी के बाद एक कहावत कहती थीं।
>
> *dādī har kahānī ke bād ek kahāvat kahtī thīṁ.*
>
> After every story my grandmother would say a proverb.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कहावत** (*kahāvat*) is inherited from *\*kathāvārttā*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 2706), joining words for telling and for talk: a saying everyone repeats.
