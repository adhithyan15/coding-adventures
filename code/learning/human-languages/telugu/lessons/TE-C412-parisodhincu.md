# పరిశోధించు (pariśōdhiñcu) — to research; to investigate

## You'll want to know: పరిశోధించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C412-PARISODHANA412-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిశోధించు** — *pariśōdhiñcu* — "to research; to investigate".

> ఆమె తన కుటుంబ చరిత్రను పరిశోధించింది.
>
> *āme tana kuṭumba caritranu pariśōdhiñcindi.*
>
> She researched her family history.

Past (I …): పరిశోధించాను, *pariśōdhiñcānu*.

Also for the police: a case is investigated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పరిశోధించు** (*pariśōdhiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *pariśodhana*, "cleaning, purification" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; the noun is పరిశోధన.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
