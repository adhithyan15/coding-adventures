## You'll want to know: trébucher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C455-RANDONNEE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**trébucher** — "to stumble, to trip".

> Sur le sentier, elle a trébuché sur une pierre.
>
> *On the path she tripped over a stone.*

*Trébucher sur un mot* — to stumble over a word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trébucher* comes from Old French *trebuchier* 'to topple' (EtymDB), from *tres-*, from Latin *trāns* 'across', and a Frankish word for 'trunk, body'.
