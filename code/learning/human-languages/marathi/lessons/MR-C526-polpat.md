## You'll want to know: पोळपाट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C526-OTA526-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पोळपाट** — *poḷpāṭ* — "a rolling board".

> आजी पोळपाटावर पोळ्या लाटते.
>
> *ājī poḷpāṭāvar poḷyā lāṭte.*
>
> Grandmother rolls out chapatis on the rolling board.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोळपाट** (*poḷpāṭ*) is a Marathi compound of पोळी, "chapati", and पाट, "a flat board", inherited from Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "slab" (CDIAL 7699).
