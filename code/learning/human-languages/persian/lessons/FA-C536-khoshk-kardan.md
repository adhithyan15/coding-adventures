## You'll want to know: خشک کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C536-CHORES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خشک کردن** (*khoshk kardan*) — "to dry".

> او موهایش را با حوله خشک کرد.
>
> *u muhâyash râ bâ hule khoshk kard.*
>
> She dried her hair with a towel.

*khoshk* 'dry' + *kardan*. *khoshk shodan* — to dry out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khoshk kardan* is built on two inherited words: *khoshk*, 'dry', usually traced to Middle Persian *hušk*, and *kardan*, 'to do'.
