## You'll want to know: fúwùtái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C454-INFO454-01]; assesses=[] -->

*fúwùtái* — "service desk; information desk".

> *qù fúwùtái zěnme zǒu* — How do I get to the information desk?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fúwùtái* is built from *fúwù* 'to serve' and *tái* 'a platform', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjuwk*, *mjuH* and *doj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to serve', the second 'affairs' and the last as a high square terrace.
