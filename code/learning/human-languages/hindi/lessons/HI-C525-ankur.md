## You'll want to know: अंकुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C525-PAUDHA525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अंकुर** — *aṅkur* — "a sprout; a shoot".

> बीज से एक छोटा अंकुर निकला।
>
> *Bīj se ek choṭā aṅkur niklā.*
>
> A small shoot came up from the seed.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंकुर** (*aṅkur*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *aṅkura*, "a sprout, a shoot" (Monier-Williams).
