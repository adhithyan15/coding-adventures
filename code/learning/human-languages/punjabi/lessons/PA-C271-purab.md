# ਪੂਰਬ (pūrab) — the east

## You'll want to know: ਪੂਰਬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C271-COMPASS271-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੂਰਬ** — *pūrab* — "the east".

> ਸੂਰਜ ਪੂਰਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੜ੍ਹਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *sūraj pūrab vich chaṛhdā hai.*
>
> The sun rises in the east.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੂਰਬ** (*pūrab*) goes back to Sanskrit *pūrva*, "in front; eastern" (Monier-Williams), in a half-learned form reshaped to Punjabi sounds; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form under it (CDIAL 8343).
