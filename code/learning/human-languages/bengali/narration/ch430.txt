Bengali, chapter 430: Fields and Farming.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
খেত (khet) — a field (of crops).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to shake (something), then the Bengali for to bend low.

You'll want to know: খেত.
খেত — khet — "a field (of crops)".
গ্রামের চারপাশে ধানের খেত। grāmer chārpāshe dhāner khet. All round the village are rice fields.
মাঠ is open ground; খেত (khet) is ground that grows something: ধানের খেত (khet), a rice field.

The word, taken apart.
খেত (khet) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali khet, "a field", under Sanskrit kṣḗtra, "land" (CDIAL 3735).

Lesson 3 of 6.
কৃষক (krishôk) — a farmer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: কৃষক.
কৃষক — krishôk — "a farmer".
কৃষক ভোরে খেতে যান। krishôk bhore khete jān. The farmer goes to the field at dawn.
A careful word, common in the news and in schoolbooks.

The word, taken apart.
কৃষক (krishôk) is Sanskrit kṛṣaka, "a ploughman, a husbandman" (Monier-Williams), from kṛṣ, to plough.

Lesson 4 of 6.
সেচ দেওয়া (sech deoyā) — to irrigate; to water (fields).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a plough, then the Bengali for a farmer.

You'll want to know: সেচ দেওয়া.
সেচ দেওয়া — sech deoyā — "to irrigate; to water (fields)".
শুকনো দিনে কৃষক খেতে সেচ দেন। shukno dine krishôk khete sech den. On dry days the farmer irrigates the field.
Past (he/she …): তিনি খেতে সেচ দিলেন, tini khete sech dilen.

The word, taken apart.
সেচ দেওয়া (sech deoyā) joins সেচ, "irrigation", of the family CDIAL sets under Sanskrit siñcáti, "pours out, sprinkles", with Bengali sẽcā, "to irrigate, to bale out" (CDIAL 13394), to দেওয়া, to give.

Lesson 5 of 6.
চাষ করা (chāsh kôrā) — to farm; to grow (a crop).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a farmer, then the Bengali for to irrigate.

You'll want to know: চাষ করা.
চাষ করা — chāsh kôrā — "to farm; to grow (a crop)".
এই গ্রামের লোকেরা ধান চাষ করে। ei grāmer lokerā dhān chāsh kôre. The people of this village grow rice.
Past (he/she …): তিনি জমিতে সবজি চাষ করলেন, tini jômite sôbji chāsh kôrlen.
চাষ alone is farming, the work of the fields.

The word, taken apart.
চাষ করা (chāsh kôrā) joins চাষ, which CDIAL lists as Bengali cās, "ploughing", under the reconstructed carṣati, "ploughs" (CDIAL 4712), to করা, to do.
