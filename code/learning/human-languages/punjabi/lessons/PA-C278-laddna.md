## You'll want to know: ਲੱਦਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C278-STOCK278-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੱਦਣਾ** — *laddṇā* — "to load".

> ਮੁੰਡਾ ਸਾਮਾਨ ਬੱਸ ਉੱਤੇ ਲੱਦਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *muṇḍā sāmān bass utte ladddā hai.*
>
> The boy loads the luggage onto the bus.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਾਮਾਨ ਲੱਦਿਆ, *maiṁ sāmān laddiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੱਦਣਾ** (*laddṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lardayati*, "loads"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *laddṇā*, "to load", under it (CDIAL 10966).
