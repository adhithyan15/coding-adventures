## You'll want to know: પોપડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C515-POPDO515-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પોપડો** — *popḍo* — "a flake (of plaster)".

> દીવાલ પરથી એક મોટો પોપડો પડ્યો.
>
> *dīvāl parthī ek moṭo popḍo paḍyo.*
>
> A big flake of plaster fell off the wall.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પોપડો** (*popḍo*) comes from *\*pōppa*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as a detached scaly crust (CDIAL 8405).
