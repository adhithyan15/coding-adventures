Bengali, chapter 190: Rather, Perhaps, Certainly, Unfortunately, Result.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বরং (bôrông) — rather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a ravine, then the Bengali for a waterfall.

You'll want to know: বরং.
বরং — bôrông — "rather".

The word, taken apart.
বরং (bôrông) is Sanskrit varam, "better than", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 11308). It is the adverb of vara, "choice, boon", whose inherited form bar, "boon", CDIAL lists.

ফলাফল — phôlāphôl — "a result".

ফলাফল (phôlāphôl) is Sanskrit phala, "fruit, result", + aphala, "no result", both taken over whole: "result or no result". CDIAL lists the inherited Bengali phal, "fruit" (CDIAL 9051).
