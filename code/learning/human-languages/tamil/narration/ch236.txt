Tamil, chapter 236: Museum, Lane, Harbour, Fort, Palace.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அருங்காட்சியகம் (aruṅkāṭciyakam) — a museum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for east, then the Tamil for west.

You'll want to know: அருங்காட்சியகம்.
அருங்காட்சியகம் — aruṅkāṭciyakam — "a museum".

The word, taken apart.
அருங்காட்சியகம் (aruṅkāṭciyakam) is a modern coinage from native parts: aru, "rare" (DEDR 221), kāṭci, "sight, show" (DEDR 1443), and akam, "house" (DEDR 7): "the house of rare sights".

Lesson 2 of 5.
சந்து (cantu) — a lane, an alley.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for west, then the Tamil for a museum.

You'll want to know: சந்து.
சந்து — cantu — "a lane, an alley".

The word, taken apart.
சந்து (cantu) is usually traced to Sanskrit saṁdhi, "joint, junction" (CDIAL 12913): a lane is a gap between houses.
