## You'll want to know: रथयात्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C400-PARIKRAMA400-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रथयात्रा** — *rathayātrā* — "a chariot procession".

> रथयात्रायां जनाः रथं कर्षन्ति।
>
> *rathayātrāyāṃ janāḥ rathaṃ karṣanti.*
>
> In the chariot procession people pull the chariot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रथयात्रा** (*rathayātrā*) is built from *ratha*, "a chariot", and *yātrā*, "a journey, a procession"; MW gives the compound as the festive procession of an image on a car (MW).
