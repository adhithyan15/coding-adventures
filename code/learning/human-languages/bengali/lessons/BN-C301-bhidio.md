## You'll want to know: ভিডিও
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C301-BIBORON301-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভিডিও** — *bhiḍio* — "a video".

> এক প্রত্যক্ষদর্শী ফোনে ঘটনার একটা ভিডিও তুলল।
>
> *ek prôtyôkkhôdôrshī phone ghôṭônār ekṭā bhiḍio tullô.*
>
> An eyewitness filmed the incident on a phone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভিডিও** (*bhiḍio*) is English *video*, from the Latin for 'I see'. ভিডিও তোলা, to film, as **ছবি তোলা** is to take a photo.
