## You'll want to know: a subida
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C438-SUBIDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a subida** — "the climb, the way up".

> A subida até ao castelo é cansativa.
>
> *The climb up to the castle is tiring.*

Also a rise: *uma subida de preços*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*subida* is built on *subir* 'to go up' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *subīre* 'to go under, to come up'.
