## Script — the hand piece, then three bars
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SAO-SWEEP-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 扫

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The hand piece, three strokes**, as in 打: a horizontal, a vertical that hooks to the left at its foot, then a stroke that rises to the right across it.

**The right piece, three strokes.** A horizontal that turns down at its right end, a horizontal across the middle, then a horizontal along the foot, which runs out past the right side.

3 + 3 = six.
