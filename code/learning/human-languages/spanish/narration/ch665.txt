Spanish, chapter 665: Una queja.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la queja — the complaint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the socket, then the Spanish for the tap.

You'll want to know: la queja.
la queja — "the complaint".
Tengo una queja sobre el ruido. I have a complaint about the noise.
The verb is quejarse.

The word, taken apart.
queja is built on quejarse "to complain", which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin form of quassāre "to shake hard, to batter" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: el desperfecto.
el desperfecto — "the minor damage, the defect".
La habitación tenía varios desperfectos. The room had several things damaged.
Small damage: a broken lamp, a stain on the wall.

The word, taken apart.
desperfecto is built from des- and perfecto "perfect", a learned borrowing from Latin perfectus "finished" (EtymDB), from perficere, built on facere "to make".

Lesson 3 of 5.
compensar — to compensate, to make up for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the complaint, then the Spanish for the minor damage.

You'll want to know: compensar.
compensar — "to compensate, to make up for".
El hotel nos compensó con una noche gratis. The hotel made up for it with a free night.
Compensar a alguien por algo — to compensate someone for something.

The word, taken apart.
compensar is a learned borrowing from Latin compēnsāre "to weigh things against one another, to balance" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built on pēnsāre "to weigh".

Lesson 5 of 5.
el dueño — the owner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to compensate, then the Spanish for the manager.

You'll want to know: el dueño.
el dueño — "the owner".
El dueño del hostal es muy simpático. The owner of the guesthouse is very nice.
A woman is la dueña. El dueño del perro — the owner of the dog.

The word, taken apart.
dueño is inherited from Late Latin domnus, a short form of Latin dominus "master" (EtymDB), built on domus "house": the master of the house.
