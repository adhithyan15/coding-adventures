## You'll want to know: la ruptura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C745-RUPTURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la ruptura** — "the break-up".

> Después de la ruptura, se fue a vivir sola.
>
> *After the break-up, she went to live alone.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruptura* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ruptūra* "a breaking" (EtymDB), from *rumpere* "to break", the source of *romper*.
