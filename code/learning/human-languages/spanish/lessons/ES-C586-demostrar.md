## You'll want to know: demostrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C586-JUSTIF-05]; assesses=[] -->

**demostrar** — "to show, to prove".

> Los datos demuestran que tengo razón.
>
> *The data show that I am right.*

Stem change o→ue: *demuestro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*demostrar* is inherited from Latin *demonstrare* 'to point out', built on *monstrare* 'to show', the verb behind *mostrar* (EtymDB).
