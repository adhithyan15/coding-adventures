Spanish, chapter 487: Cómo nos sentimos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
alegrarse — to be glad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the aspirin, then the Spanish for the vaccine.

You'll want to know: alegrarse.
alegrarse — "to be glad".
Me alegro de verte. I am glad to see you.
It goes with de: alegrarse de algo.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: alegrarse]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: alegrarse, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with alegrarse]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the aspirin, then the Spanish for the vaccine, then say alegrarse again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does alegrarse mean? ("To be glad".) Say it once more.
