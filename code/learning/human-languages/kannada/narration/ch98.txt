Kannada, chapter 98: Moods.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೇಸರ (bēsara) — boredom, weariness of spirit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for don't forget, then the Kannada for kindness.

You'll want to know: ಬೇಸರ.
ಬೇಸರ (bēsara) — "boredom", and the low mood that comes with it.
ನನಗೆ ಬೇಸರ ಆಗಿದೆ (nanage bēsara āgide) — "I'm bored", with the person in ನನಗೆ once more.

What you've built.
A mood, said like hunger and tiredness.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bēsara]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bēsara, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nanage bēsara āgide]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for don't forget, then the Kannada for kindness, then say bēsara again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಬೇಸರ (bēsara) mean? ("Boredom, weariness of spirit".) Say it once more.
