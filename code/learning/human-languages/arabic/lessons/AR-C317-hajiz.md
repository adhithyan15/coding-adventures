## You'll want to know: حاجز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C317-TAWQIF317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حاجز** — *ḥājiz* — "a checkpoint; a barrier".

> وقفت السيارات عند الحاجز.
>
> *waqafat as-sayyārāt ʿinda al-ḥājiz.*
>
> The cars stopped at the checkpoint.

Plural: حواجز, *ḥawājiz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حاجز** (*ḥājiz*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-j-z* (Lane), "to keep two things apart". *Ḥājiz* is the participle: "what stands between".
