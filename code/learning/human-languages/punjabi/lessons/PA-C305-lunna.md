# ਲੁਣਨਾ (luṇnā) — to reap; to cut a crop

## You'll want to know: ਲੁਣਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C305-HARVEST305-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੁਣਨਾ** — *luṇnā* — "to reap; to cut a crop".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਕਣਕ ਲੁਣਾਂਗੇ।
>
> *asīṁ agle hafte kaṇak luṇāṅge.*
>
> We will reap the wheat next week.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਣਕ ਲੁਣੀ, *maiṁ kaṇak luṇī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੁਣਨਾ** (*luṇnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lunāti*, "cuts, reaps"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *luṇnā*, "cuts, reaps", under it (CDIAL 11082).
