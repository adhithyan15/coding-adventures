## You'll want to know: वस्तुतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C346-AKSHEPA346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वस्तुतः** — *vastutaḥ* — "in fact; really".

> वस्तुतः सः दरिद्रः अस्ति।
>
> *vastutaḥ saḥ daridraḥ asti.*
>
> In fact he is poor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वस्तुतः** (*vastutaḥ*) is built on *vastu*, "a thing, the real", with the ending *-tas*; MW gives *vastu-tas* "in fact, in reality, actually" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
