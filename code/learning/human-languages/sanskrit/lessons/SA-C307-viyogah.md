## You'll want to know: वियोगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C307-VIYOGA307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वियोगः** — *viyogaḥ* — "a separation".

> जलौघे कुटुम्बस्य वियोगः अभवत्।
>
> *jalaughe kuṭumbasya viyogaḥ abhavat.*
>
> In the flood the family was separated.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वियोगः** (*viyogaḥ*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives *viyoga* as disjunction, separation, and the loss of something (MW).
