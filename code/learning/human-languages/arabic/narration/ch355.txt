Arabic, chapter 355: Breaking news.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عاجل (ʿājil) — urgent; breaking news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a flame, then the Arabic for to leak.

You'll want to know: عاجل.
عاجل — ʿājil — "urgent; breaking news".
قطع التلفاز البرنامج لخبر عاجل. qaṭaʿa at-tilfāz al-barnāmaj li-khabar ʿājil. The television interrupted the programme for breaking news.
خبر عاجل (ʿājil): breaking news.

The word, taken apart.
عاجل (ʿājil) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-j-l (Lane), "to hasten", the root of the taught mustaʿjil, in a hurry. ʿĀjil is "what comes quickly".

Lesson 2 of 5.
مدونة (mudawwana) — a blog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to leak, then the Arabic for urgent.

You'll want to know: مدونة.
مدونة — mudawwana — "a blog".
تكتب أختي مدونة عن الطبخ. taktubu ukhtī mudawwana ʿan aṭ-ṭabkh. My sister writes a blog about cooking.
Also a code of law: مدونة (mudawwana) الأسرة.

The word, taken apart.
مدونة (mudawwana) is built on the native Arabic root d-w-n (Lane), where dawwana is "to write into a register", a dīwān. Mudawwana is "a thing written down"; the blog is its modern sense.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نفى (nafā) — to deny officially; to exile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a comment, then the Arabic for an editorial.

You'll want to know: نفى.
نفى — nafā — "to deny officially; to exile".
نفت الوزارة الخبر. nafat al-wizāra al-khabar. The ministry denied the report.
Present: ينفي, yanfī.
Also to exile: نفي, exile. In grammar النفي is negation.

The word, taken apart.
نفى (nafā) is built on the native Arabic root n-f-y (Lane), "to drive away, to banish". To deny a report is to drive it away.
