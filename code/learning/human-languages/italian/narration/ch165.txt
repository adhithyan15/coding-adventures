Lesson 1 of 5.
la regola — a rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a widower, then the Italian for failure.

You'll want to know: la regola.
la regola — "a rule".

The word, taken apart.
regola from Latin regula, "a straight stick, a rule" — a learned borrowing. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for regola; it records French règle and Portuguese regra as taken from regula.

Lesson 2 of 5.
il permesso — permission.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for failure, then the Italian for a rule.

You'll want to know: il permesso.
il permesso — "permission".

The word, taken apart.
permesso is "a thing allowed": the past participle of the verb for "to allow", which from Latin permittere, "to let through". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
il divieto — a ban.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a rule, then the Italian for permission.

You'll want to know: il divieto.
il divieto — "a ban".

The word, taken apart.
divieto is the noun of an old verb divietare, "to forbid", built on Latin vetare, "to forbid". (EtymDB 2.1 records vetare behind the modern verb.)
