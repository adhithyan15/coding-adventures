# திண்ணை (tiṇṇai) — a raised veranda seat

## You'll want to know: திண்ணை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C522-PAZHAYAVEEDU522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**திண்ணை** — *tiṇṇai* — "a raised veranda seat".

> மாலையில் தாத்தா திண்ணையில் உட்கார்ந்து பேசுவார்.
>
> *mālaiyil tāttā tiṇṇaiyil uṭkārntu pēcuvār.*
>
> In the evenings grandfather sits on the veranda and talks.

The raised platform at the front of a house, where people sit and talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**திண்ணை** (*tiṇṇai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3227 gives திண்ணை (*tiṇṇai*) for the raised platform or veranda of a house.
