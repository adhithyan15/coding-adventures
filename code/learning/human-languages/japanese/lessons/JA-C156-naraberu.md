# ならべる (naraberu) — to arrange in a row; to line up (things)

## You'll want to know: ならべる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C156-CLOSET156-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ならべる** — *naraberu* — "to arrange in a row; to line up (things)".

> **たなに ほんを ならべます。** — *tana ni hon o narabemasu* — "I line up the books on the shelf."

Polite **ならべます**, *narabemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ならべる** (*naraberu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
