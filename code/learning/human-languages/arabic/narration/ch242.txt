Arabic, chapter 242: Noisy, Dead, Wild, Tame, Poisonous.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صاخب (ṣākhib) — noisy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for funny, then the Arabic for serious.

You'll want to know: صاخب.
