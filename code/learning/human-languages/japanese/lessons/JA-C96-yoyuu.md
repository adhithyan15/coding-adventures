## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よゆう** (*yoyuu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yo*, "surplus" (Middle Chinese *yo*) and *yū*, "abundant" (*yuH*) (Unihan, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
