Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪುರಭವನ (purabhavana) — a town hall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a guide, then the Kannada for worth seeing.

You'll want to know: ಪುರಭವನ.
ಪುರಭವನ — purabhavana — "a town hall".
ಪುರಭವನದ ಮುಂದೆ ಒಂದು ದೊಡ್ಡ ಗಡಿಯಾರ ಇದೆ. purabhavanada munde ondu doḍḍa gaḍiyāra ide. There is a big clock in front of the town hall.
ಪುರ, a town, plus ಭವನ, a building or mansion.

The word, taken apart.
ಪುರಭವನ (purabhavana) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: pura, "a fortress, a town" (CDIAL 8278), and bhavana, "a place of abode" (CDIAL 9417).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಗೋಪುರ (gōpura) — a tower; a temple tower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for worth seeing, then the Kannada for a town hall.

You'll want to know: ಗೋಪುರ.
ಗೋಪುರ — gōpura — "a tower; a temple tower".
ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದ ಗೋಪುರ ದೂರದಿಂದಲೇ ಕಾಣುತ್ತದೆ. dēvasthānada gōpura dūradindalē kāṇuttade. The temple tower can be seen from far away.
ಗಡಿಯಾರ ಗೋಪುರ (gōpura), a clock tower.

The word, taken apart.
ಗೋಪುರ (gōpura) is borrowed from Sanskrit gōpura, "a town gate" (Monier-Williams); in South India it names the tall tower over a temple gate.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು (heggurutu) — a landmark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tower, then the Kannada for a fountain.

You'll want to know: ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು.
ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು — heggurutu — "a landmark".
ಆ ಗೋಪುರ ನಗರದ ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು. ā gōpura nagarada heggurutu. That tower is the landmark of the city.
ಹೆ-, big, plus ಗುರುತು, a mark: the big sign people steer by.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆಗ್ಗುರುತು (heggurutu) is a native Kannada compound of per, hē-, "big" (DEDR 4411), and ಗುರುತು, "a mark, a sign" (DEDR 1847).

ವೀಕ್ಷಿಸು (vīkṣisu) — to view; to watch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a fountain, then the Kannada for a landmark.

You'll want to know: ವೀಕ್ಷಿಸು.
ವೀಕ್ಷಿಸು — vīkṣisu — "to view; to watch".
ಪ್ರವಾಸಿಗರು ಬೆಟ್ಟದಿಂದ ಸೂರ್ಯಾಸ್ತವನ್ನು ವೀಕ್ಷಿಸಿದರು. pravāsigaru beṭṭadinda sūryāstavannu vīkṣisidaru. The tourists watched the sunset from the hill.
Past (I …): ವೀಕ್ಷಿಸಿದೆ, vīkṣiside.
A formal word for ನೋಡು: ಟೀವಿ ವೀಕ್ಷಕರು, TV viewers.

The word, taken apart.
ವೀಕ್ಷಿಸು (vīkṣisu) is built from Sanskrit vīkṣ, "to behold, to see" (CDIAL 12041, vīkṣatē), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
