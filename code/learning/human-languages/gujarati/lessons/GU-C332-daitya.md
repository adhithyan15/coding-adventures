## You'll want to know: દૈત્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C332-DANTKATHA332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દૈત્ય** — *daitya* — "a demon".

> જંગલમાં એક દૈત્ય રહેતો હતો.
>
> *jangalmā̃ ek daitya raheto hato.*
>
> A demon lived in the forest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દૈત્ય** (*daitya*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *daitya*, "a son of Diti, a demon" (Monier-Williams): in the old stories, the enemies of the gods.
