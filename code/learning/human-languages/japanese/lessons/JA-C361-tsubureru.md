# つぶれる (tsubureru) — to go out of business; to be crushed

## You'll want to know: つぶれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C361-MOUKERU361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**つぶれる** — *tsubureru* — "to go out of business; to be crushed".

> **きゃくが すくなく なったら、みせは つぶれるでしょう。** — *kyaku ga sukunaku nattara, mise wa tsubureru deshō* — "If the customers fall away, the shop will probably go under."

Polite **つぶれます**, *tsuburemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **みせが つぶれました。**, *mise ga tsuburemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つぶれる** (*tsubureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
