Marathi, chapter 323: Farewells and Anniversaries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निरोप (nirop) — a farewell; a message.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to congratulate, then the Marathi for to inaugurate.

You'll want to know: निरोप.
निरोप — nirop — "a farewell; a message".
शाळेत गुरुजींना निवृत्तीच्या दिवशी निरोप दिला. śāḷet gurujīnnā nivṛttīcyā divśī nirop dilā. At school the teacher was given a farewell on the day he retired.
Masculine.
निरोप (nirop) देणे, to see someone off; निरोप (nirop) घेणे, to take one's leave.

The word, taken apart.
निरोप (nirop) is the Marathi word for a message and a leave-taking; its origin is not settled.

निवृत्ती — nivṛttī — "retirement".
निवृत्तीनंतर बाबा रोज बागेत काम करतात. nivṛttīnantar bābā roj bāget kām kartāt. Since retiring Dad works in the garden every day.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
निवृत्ती (nivṛttī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nivṛtti, 'a turning back, ceasing from work' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
वर्धापनदिन (vardhāpandin) — an anniversary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for retirement, then the Marathi for a housewarming.

You'll want to know: वर्धापनदिन.
वर्धापनदिन — vardhāpandin — "an anniversary".
आज आमच्या कंपनीचा पन्नासावा वर्धापनदिन आहे. āj āmacyā kampnīcā pannāsāvā vardhāpandin āhe. Today is our company's fiftieth anniversary.
Masculine.
वर्धापन, a birthday rite, + दिन, a day.

The word, taken apart.
वर्धापनदिन (vardhāpandin) is built from Sanskrit vardhāpana, a rite kept on a birthday (Monier-Williams), and dina, 'a day'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
समारोप (samārop) — a closing; a conclusion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a housewarming, then the Marathi for an anniversary.

You'll want to know: समारोप.
समारोप — samārop — "a closing; a conclusion".
संमेलनाचा समारोप एका गाण्याने झाला. sammelanācā samārop ekā gāṇyāne jhālā. The gathering closed with a song.
Masculine.
समारोप (samārop) करणे, to close an event.

The word, taken apart.
समारोप (samārop) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit samāropa, 'a placing upon' (Monier-Williams); the Marathi sense, the close of an event, is its own.
