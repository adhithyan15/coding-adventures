# An old word for swimming

This is a return to one word you have already met. You need not swim or
perform any movement. Hear a meaning, pause, and recall the word aloud.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C149-ACT149-01] -->

Hear **to swim**. [PAUSE 4s] Say the earlier Punjabi word before the
model: **ਤਰਨਾ** (*tarnā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C149-ACT149-01] -->

Hear **ਤਰਨਾ**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall its old meaning, **to swim**.
Now hear **to swim** and say **ਤਰਨਾ** before the spoken model.
No sentence or new grammar is needed.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C149-ACT149-01] -->

One last cue: **to swim**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਤਰਨਾ**.
Listen and retry once if it does not come back. This is untimed
vocabulary retrieval, not scored A1 writing or mock evidence.
