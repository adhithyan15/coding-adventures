## You'll want to know: el engaño
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C746-ENGANO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el engaño** — "the deception, the trick".

> La oferta era un engaño.
>
> *The offer was a trick.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*engaño* is built on *engañar* "to deceive", which is usually traced to spoken Latin *ingannāre* "to mock".
