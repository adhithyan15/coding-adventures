# ಬಂಕ್ (baṅk) — a fuel station; a petrol pump

## You'll want to know: ಬಂಕ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C448-INDHANA448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಕ್** — *baṅk* — "a fuel station; a petrol pump".

> ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯ ಪಕ್ಕದ ಬಂಕ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಪೆಟ್ರೋಲ್ ತುಂಬಿಸಿದೆವು.
>
> *heddāriya pakkada baṅknalli peṭrōl tumbisidevu.*
>
> We filled up with petrol at the fuel station beside the highway.

ಪೆಟ್ರೋಲ್ ಬಂಕ್ is the full name.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಂಕ್** (*baṅk*) is a loan from English *bunk*, in Indian use, in the phrase *petrol bunk*, "a fuel station".
