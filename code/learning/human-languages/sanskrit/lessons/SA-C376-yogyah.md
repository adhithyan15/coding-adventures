## You'll want to know: योग्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C376-YOGYA376-01]; assesses=[] -->

**योग्यः** — *yogyaḥ* — "qualified; fit".

> यदि अरुणः योग्यः अस्ति, तर्हि सः नायकः भवेत्।
>
> *yadi aruṇaḥ yogyaḥ asti, tarhi saḥ nāyakaḥ bhavet.*
>
> If Arun is qualified, he should be the leader.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**योग्यः** (*yogyaḥ*) is built on *yoga*, "a yoking, union", from the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives *yogya* "fit for the yoke" and "useful, proper, qualified, able" (MW).
