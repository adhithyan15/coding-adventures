## You'll want to know: lucidare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C534-PIATTI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**lucidare** — "to polish, to shine".

> Ho lucidato le scarpe per il matrimonio.
>
> *I polished my shoes for the wedding.*

Built on *lucido* "shiny".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lucidare* is built on *lucido* "shiny", a learned borrowing from Latin *lūcidus* "bright" (Lewis & Short), from *lūx* "light".
