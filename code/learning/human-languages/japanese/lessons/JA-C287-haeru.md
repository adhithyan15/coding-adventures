# はえる (haeru) — to sprout; to come through (of teeth)

## You'll want to know: はえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C287-SODATSU287-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はえる** — *haeru* — "to sprout; to come through (of teeth)".

> **にわに くさが はえています。** — *niwa ni kusa ga haete imasu* — "Grass is coming up in the garden."

Polite **はえます**, *haemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あかちゃんの はが はえました。**, *akachan no ha ga haemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はえる** (*haeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
