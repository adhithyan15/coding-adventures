Marathi, chapter 547: Emergencies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आणीबाणी (āṇībāṇī) — an emergency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to take an oath, then the Marathi for to dare.

You'll want to know: आणीबाणी.
आणीबाणी — āṇībāṇī — "an emergency".
आणीबाणीच्या वेळी हा दरवाजा उघडतात. āṇībāṇīcyā veḷī hā darvājā ughaḍtāt. This door is opened in an emergency.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
आणीबाणी (āṇībāṇī) is a Marathi word for a moment of crisis; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
अग्निशामक (agniśāmak) — a fire extinguisher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to dare, then the Marathi for an emergency.

You'll want to know: अग्निशामक.
अग्निशामक — agniśāmak — "a fire extinguisher".
प्रत्येक मजल्यावर एक अग्निशामक ठेवला आहे. pratyek majlyāvar ek agniśāmak ṭhevlā āhe. A fire extinguisher is kept on every floor.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अग्निशामक (agniśāmak) is a tatsama coinage from Sanskrit agni, "fire" (Monier-Williams), and a noun of śam-, "to quench".

Lesson 4 of 5.
सुरक्षारक्षक (surakṣārakṣak) — a security guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fire extinguisher, then the Marathi for an alarm.

You'll want to know: सुरक्षारक्षक.
सुरक्षारक्षक — surakṣārakṣak — "a security guard".
इमारतीच्या दारात एक सुरक्षारक्षक उभा असतो. imārtīcyā dārāt ek surakṣārakṣak ubhā asto. A security guard stands at the door of the building.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
सुरक्षारक्षक (surakṣārakṣak) is a compound built from सुरक्षा, "safety", and Sanskrit rakṣaka, "a protector" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
आटोक्यात आणणे (āṭokyāt āṇṇe) — to bring under control.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an alarm, then the Marathi for a security guard.

You'll want to know: आटोक्यात आणणे.
आटोक्यात आणणे — āṭokyāt āṇṇe — "to bring under control".
सुरक्षारक्षकाने अग्निशामक वापरून आग आटोक्यात आणली. surakṣārakṣakāne agniśāmak vāprūn āg āṭokyāt āṇlī. The security guard used the extinguisher and brought the fire under control.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आग आटोक्यात आणली, tyāne āg āṭokyāt āṇlī.

The word, taken apart.
आटोक्यात आणणे (āṭokyāt āṇṇe) is आटोका, "reach, control", in the locative, with आणणे, "to bring"; the origin of आटोका is not settled.
