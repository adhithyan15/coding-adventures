## You'll want to know: ફળિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C419-STREET419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફળિયું** — *phaḷiyũ* — "a neighbourhood lane (and its houses)".

> ફળિયાનાં બાળકો સાથે રમે છે.
>
> *phaḷiyānā̃ bāḷko sāthe rame che.*
>
> The children of the lane play together.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફળિયું** (*phaḷiyũ*) is a lane together with the houses that open onto it, and the families in them. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
