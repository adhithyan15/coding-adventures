## You'll want to know: おてあらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C400-TOKONOMA400-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おてあらい** — *otearai* — "a toilet; a restroom".

> **おてあらいは どこですか。** — *otearai wa doko desu ka* — "Where is the toilet?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おてあらい** (*otearai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from polite *o-*, *te*, "hand", and *arai*, "washing" (from *arau*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
