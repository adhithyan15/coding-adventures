## You'll want to know: અધિકારી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C354-ADHIKARI354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અધિકારી** — *adhikārī* — "an official".

> કચેરીમાં એક અધિકારીએ મારી અરજી તપાસી.
>
> *kacerīmā̃ ek adhikārīe mārī arjī tapāsī.*
>
> An official at the office checked my application.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અધિકારી** (*adhikārī*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *adhikārin*, "having authority" (Monier-Williams). It shares its source with **અધિકાર** (*adhikār*), "authority", but names the person who holds it.
