## You'll want to know: अडचण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C349-UPAY349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अडचण** — *aḍcaṇ* — "a difficulty; a snag".

> नव्या शहरात सुरुवातीला खूप अडचणी आल्या.
>
> *navyā śahrāt suruvātīlā khūp aḍcaṇī ālyā.*
>
> There were many difficulties at first in the new city.

Feminine. Plural: अडचणी, *aḍcaṇī*.

अडचण येणे, a difficulty comes up; अडचणीत असणे, to be in trouble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अडचण** (*aḍcaṇ*) is usually traced to the root *\*aḍḍ-*, "obstruct, stop", under which CDIAL lists Marathi *aḍṇẽ*, "to be stopped" (CDIAL 188).
