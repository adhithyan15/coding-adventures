# Eight letters — read them, hear them, write them back

## Warm-up

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-HE-01, UR-LEX-YIH, UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-KAAN, UR-LEX-NAAK, UR-LEX-LAL, UR-LEX-KALA, UR-LEX-NILA, UR-LEX-LENA, UR-SCRIPT-JOINING-01] -->

Nothing new here. Ten words, every letter already yours. Read each from the
right, naming its letters before you say it.

> سلام
>
> کان
>
> ناک
>
> لال
>
> کالا
>
> نیلا
>
> لینا
>
> کام
>
> یہ
>
> ہم

Two of them are new to your eye this chapter: **کام** *kām* "work" and **یہ**
*yih* "this." Two more are old friends whose letters you could not have named a
chapter ago.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-HE-01, UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-YIH, UR-LEX-KAHAN] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Now hide the page. For each word below: look at it, cover it, write it from
memory, then uncover and repair whatever came out wrong. Repairing is the part
that teaches — do not skip to the next word until you have compared.

- [YOU WRITE: یہ — look, cover, write, uncover, repair]
- [YOU WRITE: کام — look, cover, write, uncover, repair]
- [YOU WRITE: ہم — look, cover, write, uncover, repair]
- [YOU SAY: **kahāṅ**, and name the one letter in it you still cannot form]

## Guided Practice — by ear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-YIH, UR-LEX-KAHAN, UR-LEX-KAAN, UR-LEX-NAAK] -->

Now the ear, with the page shut.

- [YOU SAY: **yih kān**, **yih nāk**, **yih kām** — pointing each time]
- [YOU SAY: **kahāṅ** — and the four other k- question words]
- [YOU SAY: **kām** and English **karma**, and which one was inherited]
- [YOU LISTEN: for the nasal hum at the end of *kahāṅ*, and hum it back]
