## You'll want to know: सडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C448-DURGANDHI448-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सडणे** — *saḍṇe* — "to decay".

> टोपलीतले आंबे सडले.
>
> *ṭoplītle āmbe saḍle.*
>
> The mangoes in the basket went rotten.

Past (he …): तो सडला, *to saḍlā*.

Sharper than कुजणे: it often smells.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सडणे** (*saḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *saḍṇẽ*, "to rot", under Sanskrit *śaṭati* (CDIAL 12268).
