## You'll want to know: مجسمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C314-KHANDAR314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مجسمہ** — *mujassamā* — "a statue".

> انہیں ایک پرانا مجسمہ ملا۔
>
> *unheṅ ek purānā mujassamā milā.*
>
> They found an old statue.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجسمہ** (*mujassamā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mujassam*, "given a body, made solid": a figure in stone or metal.
