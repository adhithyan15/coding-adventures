Telugu, chapter 346: A Wedding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నిశ్చితార్థం (niścitārthaṁ) — an engagement (to marry).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a death anniversary, then the Telugu for a will.

You'll want to know: నిశ్చితార్థం.
నిశ్చితార్థం — niścitārthaṁ — "an engagement (to marry)".
వాళ్ళ నిశ్చితార్థం గత నెల జరిగింది. vāḷḷa niścitārthaṁ gata nela jarigindi. Their engagement took place last month.
నిశ్చిత, settled, and అర్థం, a matter: the day the match is settled.

The word, taken apart.
నిశ్చితార్థం (niścitārthaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit: niścita, "ascertained, settled, decided" (Monier-Williams), and artha, "a matter, an aim".

Warm-up.

శుభలేఖ — śubhalēkha — "a wedding invitation".
మా బంధువులు శుభలేఖ పంపించారు. mā bandhuvulu śubhalēkha pampiñcāru. Our relatives sent a wedding invitation.
శుభ, auspicious, and లేఖ, a letter.

The word, taken apart.
శుభలేఖ (śubhalēkha) is a compound borrowed from Sanskrit: śubha, "bright, auspicious" (CDIAL 12532), and lekhā, "a line, a stroke" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
తాళి (tāḷi) — a wedding pendant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a marriage, then the Telugu for a wedding invitation.

You'll want to know: తాళి.
తాళి — tāḷi — "a wedding pendant".
పెళ్ళికొడుకు పెళ్ళికూతురు మెడలో తాళి కట్టాడు. peḷḷikoḍuku peḷḷikūturu meḍalō tāḷi kaṭṭāḍu. The bridegroom tied the wedding pendant round the bride's neck.
తాళి (tāḷi) కట్టు, to tie the tali: the moment a Telugu wedding is sealed.

The word, taken apart.
తాళి (tāḷi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3175 lists Telugu tāli, the small piece of gold the bridegroom ties at the neck of the bride, with Tamil tāli.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఆహ్వానించు (āhvāniñcu) — to invite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a wedding invitation, then the Telugu for a wedding pendant.

You'll want to know: ఆహ్వానించు.
ఆహ్వానించు — āhvāniñcu — "to invite".
వాళ్ళు మమ్మల్ని పెళ్ళికి ఆహ్వానించారు. vāḷḷu mammalni peḷḷiki āhvāniñcāru. They invited us to the wedding.
Past (I …): ఆహ్వానించాను, āhvāniñcānu.
You know the noun ఆహ్వానం, an invitation; the event takes -కి.

The word, taken apart.
ఆహ్వానించు (āhvāniñcu) is built on Sanskrit āhvāna, "calling, an invitation" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -iñcu.
