## You'll want to know: હિસાબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C192-BILL192-01]; assesses=[] -->

**હિસાબ** — *hisāb* — "reckoning, accounts".

> **દુકાનદાર સાંજે હિસાબ કરે છે.** — *dukāndār sā̃je hisāb kare che* — "The shopkeeper does the accounts in the evening."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હિસાબ** (*hisāb*) is a loan from Arabic *ḥisāb*, "counting, reckoning", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *ḥ-s-b*, "to count" (Lane).
