## You'll want to know: سماد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C469-TURBA469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سماد** — *samād* — "fertiliser; manure".

> وضع المزارع السماد في الحقل.
>
> *waḍaʿa al-muzāriʿ as-samād fī al-ḥaql.*
>
> The farmer put fertiliser on the field.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سماد** (*samād*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-m-d*, "to manure the land" (Lane). Lane gives *samād* as manure of dung and earth.
