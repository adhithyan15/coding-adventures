## You'll want to know: しばる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C484-THREAD484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しばる** — *shibaru* — "to tie up; to bind".

> **ふるい しんぶんを ひもで しばります。** — *furui shinbun o himo de shibarimasu* — "I tie up the old newspapers with string."

Polite **しばります**, *shibarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが にもつを しばりました。**, *chichi ga nimotsu o shibarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しばる** (*shibaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
