## You'll want to know: der Farn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C456-LICHTUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Farn** — "the fern".

> Unter den Bäumen wächst grüner Farn.
>
> *Green ferns grow under the trees.*

Plural: *die Farne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Farn* is an inherited Germanic word; EtymDB lists English *fern* and Dutch *varen* as cousins and links them to an old word for a feather or wing.
