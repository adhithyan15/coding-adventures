# مٹنا (miṭnā) — to fade away; to be wiped out

## You'll want to know: مٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C373-BIKHARNA373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مٹنا** — *miṭnā* — "to fade away; to be wiped out".

> اگر بارش ہوئی تو یہ نشان مٹ جائے گا۔
>
> *agar bārish huī to yih nishān miṭ jāe gā.*
>
> If it rains, this mark will wash away.

Past (it …): وہ نشان مٹ گیا, *voh nishān miṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مٹنا** (*miṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *miṭnā*, "to be rubbed out", under Sanskrit *mṛṣṭá*, "rubbed, wiped" (CDIAL 10299); **مٹانا**, "to rub out", is its partner.
