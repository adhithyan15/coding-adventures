## You'll want to know: خود غرض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C459-GIVE459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خود غرض** — *khud gharaz* — "selfish".

> وہ خود غرض ہے، کسی کی مدد نہیں کرتا۔
>
> *voh khud gharaz hai, kisī kī madad nahīṅ kartā.*
>
> He is selfish and helps nobody.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خود غرض** (*khud gharaz*) is built from **خود**, "oneself", usually traced to Persian *khud*, and *gharaz*, "an aim", usually traced to Arabic *gharaḍ*: someone whose aim is himself.
