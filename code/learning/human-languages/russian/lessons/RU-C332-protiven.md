## You'll want to know: противень
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C332-ZAPEKAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**противень** (*prótiven*) — "a baking tray".

> Печенье лежит на противне.
>
> *Pechénye lezhít na prótivne.*
>
> The biscuits are on the baking tray.

Masculine; genitive *противня*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*противень* is usually traced to *против* "opposite", a tray set facing the fire; EtymDB lists no parent.
