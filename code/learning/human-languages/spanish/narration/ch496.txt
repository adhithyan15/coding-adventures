Spanish, chapter 496: Cosas de casa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el hogar — the home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to disappear, then the Spanish for the thief.

You'll want to know: el hogar.
el hogar — "the home".
Productos para el hogar. Products for the home.
A warmer word than la casa; shop signs use it.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: el hogar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: el hogar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with el hogar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for to disappear, then the Spanish for the thief, then say el hogar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does el hogar mean? ("The home".) Say it once more.
