## You'll want to know: नतीजतन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C554-NATIJATAN554-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नतीजतन** — *natījatan* — "as a result".

> वह देर से उठा, नतीजतन उसकी ट्रेन छूट गई।
>
> *Vah der se uṭhā, natījatan uskī ṭren chūṭ gaī.*
>
> He got up late; as a result he missed his train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नतीजतन** (*natījatan*) is usually traced to Arabic *natījatan*, "as a result", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
