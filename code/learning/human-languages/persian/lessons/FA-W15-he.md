# ه — the letter with four costumes

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-HE-01]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-JOINING-01] -->

> ه

A small **round** letter, standing on the line, no dots at all.

Its name is **he**. It says **h** at the start of a word — and at the **end** of
a word it usually says nothing like an h: it carries a final **e** sound, the
vowel that ends so many Persian words.

This letter will test what you learned about joining, because its four forms
differ more than most:

> alone: **ه** — at the start: **هـ** — in the middle: **ـهـ** — at the end: **ـه**

Do not try to memorise four shapes. Recognise the **round body**, notice where in
the run it is sitting, and let the shape follow from that. Persian readers are
not holding four pictures in mind either.
