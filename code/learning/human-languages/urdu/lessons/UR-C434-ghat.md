## You'll want to know: گھاٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C434-MAUJ434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گھاٹ** — *ghāṭ* — "a landing place (on a river)".

> کشتی گھاٹ پر رکی۔
>
> *kashtī ghāṭ par rukī.*
>
> The boat stopped at the landing.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گھاٹ** (*ghāṭ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ghāṭ*, "a bathing or landing place", under Sanskrit *ghaṭṭa* (CDIAL 4414).
