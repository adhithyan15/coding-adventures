Lesson 1 of 6.
مخارج (makhârej) — expenses, costs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to withdraw, then the Persian for to transfer.

You'll want to know: مخارج.
مخارج (makhârej) — "expenses, costs".
مخارج زندگی هر سال بیشتر میشود. makhârej-e zendegi har sâl bishtar mishavad. The cost of living goes up every year.
A plural built in the Arabic way beside kharj "expense".

The word, taken apart.
مخارج (makhârej) is an Arabic-style plural that Persian uses beside kharj "expense"; it is usually traced to an Arabic plural from the same family.

Lesson 2 of 6.
صورتحساب (surathesâb) — a bill, a statement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to transfer, then the Persian for expenses.

You'll want to know: صورتحساب.
صورتحساب (surathesâb) — "a bill, a statement".
بعد از شام صورتحساب را آوردند. ba'd az shâm surathesâb râ âvardand. After dinner they brought the bill.
Surat "form, list" + hesâb "account". Also a bank statement.

The word, taken apart.
صورتحساب (surathesâb) is a Persian compound of two words from Arabic: surat, from ṣūra "form" (EtymDB), and hesâb, from ḥisāb "reckoning, account".

Lesson 3 of 6.
کارمزد (kârmozd) — a fee, a commission.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for expenses, then the Persian for a bill.

You'll want to know: کارمزد.
کارمزد (kârmozd) — "a fee, a commission".
بانک برای هر انتقال کارمزد میگیرد. bânk barâye har enteqâl kârmozd migirad. The bank charges a fee for every transfer.
Kâr "work" + mozd "wage": pay for a service.

The word, taken apart.
کارمزد (kârmozd) is a native Persian compound built from kâr "work" + mozd "wage", usually traced to Middle Persian mizd: the wage for a piece of work.

Lesson 4 of 6.
درصد (darsad) — a percent, a percentage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a bill, then the Persian for a fee.

You'll want to know: درصد.
درصد (darsad) — "a percent, a percentage".
پنجاه درصد مردم رای دادند. panjâh darsad-e mardom ra'y dâdand. Fifty percent of the people voted.
Dar "in" + sad "hundred": per hundred.

The word, taken apart.
درصد (darsad) is a native Persian compound, literally "in a hundred": dar "in" + sad "hundred", which EtymDB lists without a parent and which is usually traced to PIE kmtom "hundred".

Lesson 5 of 6.
ولخرج (velkharj) — a spendthrift; extravagant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fee, then the Persian for a percent.

You'll want to know: ولخرج.
ولخرج (velkharj) — "a spendthrift; extravagant".
برادرم خیلی ولخرج است. barâdaram kheyli velkharj ast. My brother spends money too freely.
Vel "loose" + kharj "spending". Colloquial. Velkharji kardan — to splash out.

The word, taken apart.
ولخرج (velkharj) joins the Persian word vel "loose, let go" to kharj "spending", usually traced to Arabic kharj "outlay": someone whose money runs loose.
