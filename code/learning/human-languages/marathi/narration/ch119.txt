Marathi, chapter 119: Hundred, Thousand, World, Colour, Journey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शंभर (shambhar) — a hundred.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for seventy, then the Marathi for ninety.

You'll want to know: शंभर.
शंभर — shambhar — "a hundred".

The word, taken apart.
शंभर (shambhar) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śatambhara, "full hundred", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 12284). It is built on śata, "hundred".

The word, taken apart.
हजार (hajār) is a loan, usually traced to Persian hazār, "thousand". Marathi writes the z as ज.

Lesson 3 of 5.
जग (jag) — the world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a hundred, then the Marathi for a thousand.

You'll want to know: जग.
जग — jag — "the world".

The word, taken apart.
जग (jag) is Sanskrit jagat, "moving, living", which meant "the world" from the Brahmanas on (CDIAL 5078). Marathi drops the final -t.

Lesson 4 of 5.
रंग (raṅg) — a colour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a thousand, then the Marathi for the world.

You'll want to know: रंग.
रंग — raṅg — "a colour".

The word, taken apart.
रंग (raṅg) may come from Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour" (CDIAL 10560), or from Persian rang, "colour". The two words are alike in sound and sense and may have merged.

Lesson 5 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: प्रवास.
प्रवास — pravās — "a journey".

The word, taken apart.
प्रवास (pravās) is Sanskrit pravāsa, "dwelling abroad", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 8802). It is pra-, "forth", and vāsa, "dwelling".
