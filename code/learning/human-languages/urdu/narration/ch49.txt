Urdu, chapter 49: To open, To cook, To wash, To laugh, To cry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کھولنا (kholnā) — to open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to give, then the Urdu for to put.

You'll want to know: کھولنا.
کھولنا — kholnā — "to open".

The word, taken apart.
کھولنا (kholnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kholl, "to open", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3945).
