Lesson 2 of 15.
字 (zì) — the familiar shape stands alone.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 8 seconds]

The word, taken apart.
字 — zì — a written Chinese character.
Hide the pinyin. Read 字 (zì) from its familiar roof-over-child shape, then say its falling tone. You are attaching a standalone word to writing you already know.

Guided Practice.
Look at 字 (zì) for three seconds. Cover it, wait five seconds, then write it once. Check only two things: roof before child, and six strokes.

Lesson 4 of 15.
汉 (hàn) — watch five strokes.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — the shape until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 8 seconds]
Say Chinese character, then point to the new first half of 汉字 (hànzì).

Script — the shape.
In 汉 (hàn), write 氵 first. On the right, stroke four crosses right and turns down-left without lifting. Stroke five falls down-right. Five strokes; four lifts. Watch the crossing open into two different falling directions.

Guided Practice.
Trace 汉 (hàn) once, slowly. Say dot, dot, rise; turn left; fall right.

Lesson 10 of 15.
语 (yǔ) — speech, five, box.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — the shape until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 8 seconds]
Air-write 讠, then say hànyǔ.

Script — the shape.
Write 讠 first. On the right, build 五 from top to bottom, then close 口 at the base. Nine strokes; eight lifts. Keep the speech side narrow so the right side has room.

Guided Practice.
Trace 语 (yǔ) once. Say speech, five, box as your hand crosses the three parts.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 语 (yǔ), pinned stroke paths and medians.

Lesson 15 of 15.
Checkpoint — 字, 汉字 (hànzì), 汉语 (hànyǔ).

Keep the four checks separate. Repair one missed skill, not the whole set.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Write 汉 (hàn) and 语 (yǔ) without a model.

Guided Practice.
Listening: hear the three words in mixed order and choose each meaning.
Speaking: produce all three from meaning cards, with tones audible.
Reading: read 字, 汉语 (hànyǔ), 汉字 (hànzì) cold and give each meaning.
Writing: hear Chinese character and Chinese language, then write both without a model.
Pass each skill separately. Check the 字 (zì) or 语 (yǔ) ending only after both written words are complete.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Official HSK 3.0 syllabus, Level 1 vocabulary.
