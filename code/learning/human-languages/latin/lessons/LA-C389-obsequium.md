## You'll want to know: obsequium, obsequiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C389-SERVITUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**obsequium, obsequiī** — "compliance, obedience".

> Obsequium amīcōs, vēritās odium parit.
>
> *Giving way wins friends; truth breeds hatred.*

*obsequium, obsequiī* n., built on *obsequor*, to give way to someone. English *obsequious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obsequium* is built on *obsequor* "to comply" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *ob* and *sequor* "to follow". English *obsequious* comes from its adjective.
