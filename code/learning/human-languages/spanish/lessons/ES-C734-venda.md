## You'll want to know: la venda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C734-HERIDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la venda** — "the bandage".

> La enfermera me puso una venda.
>
> *The nurse put a bandage on me.*

A small sticking plaster is *una tirita* (Spain) or *una curita* (Latin America).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*venda* is usually traced to Germanic *binda* "band, strip of cloth", from the root that gives English *bind* (EtymDB gives the Indo-European root *bʰendʰ-* "to bind").
