## You'll want to know: ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C487-CANDAMARUTA487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ** — *suṇṭaragāḷi* — "a whirlwind".

> ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ ಹೊಲದ ಧೂಳನ್ನು ಮೇಲಕ್ಕೆ ಎತ್ತಿತು.
>
> *suṇṭaragāḷi holada dhūḷannu mēlakke ettitu.*
>
> A whirlwind lifted the dust of the field up into the air.

A spinning wind; ಬಿರುಗಾಳಿ is a storm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಂಟರಗಾಳಿ** (*suṇṭaragāḷi*) ends in native ಗಾಳಿ, "wind, air" (DEDR 1499); the source of its first part, *suṇṭara*, is not settled.
