## You'll want to know: અહેવાલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C312-PATRAKAR312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અહેવાલ** — *ahevāl* — "a report".

> બીજા દિવસે છાપામાં તેનો અહેવાલ આવ્યો.
>
> *bījā divse chāpāmā̃ teno ahevāl āvyo.*
>
> The next day his report appeared in the paper.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અહેવાલ** (*ahevāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *aḥwāl*, "states, conditions", the plural of the word behind **હાલત**: an account of how things stand.
