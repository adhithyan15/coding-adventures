## You'll want to know: તરફ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C413-WAY413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**તરફ** — *taraph* — "towards; in the direction of".

> અમે નદી તરફ ચાલ્યા.
>
> *ame nadī taraph cālyā.*
>
> We walked towards the river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તરફ** (*taraph*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṭaraf*, "a side, an edge", which came through Persian; after a noun it means towards it: ઘર તરફ, towards home.
