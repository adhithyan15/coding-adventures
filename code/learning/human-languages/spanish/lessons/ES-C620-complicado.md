## You'll want to know: complicado

**complicado** — "complicated".

> La situación es complicada.
>
> *The situation is complicated.*

The participle of *complicar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*complicado* is the participle of *complicar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *complicare* 'to fold together', built on *plicare* 'to fold' (EtymDB).
