# ફસાવવું (phasāvvũ) — to entrap

## You'll want to know: ફસાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C360-IMAN360-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફસાવવું** — *phasāvvũ* — "to entrap".

> તે નકલી માલ વેચીને ગ્રાહકોને ફસાવે છે.
>
> *te naklī māl vecīne grāhkone phasāve che.*
>
> He traps customers by selling them fake goods.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે એક ગ્રાહકને ફસાવ્યો, *teṇe ek grāhakne phasāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફસાવવું** (*phasāvvũ*) is the causative partner of **ફસાવું** (*phasāvũ*), "to get stuck"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to ensnare", under Sanskrit *spāśáyati*, "causes to be bound" (CDIAL 13814).
