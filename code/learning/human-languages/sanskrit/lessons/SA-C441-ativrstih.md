# अतिवृष्टिः (ativṛṣṭiḥ) — too much rain

## You'll want to know: अतिवृष्टिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C441-DURDINA441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिवृष्टिः** — *ativṛṣṭiḥ* — "too much rain".

> अतिवृष्ट्या क्षेत्राणि नश्यन्ति।
>
> *ativṛṣṭyā kṣetrāṇi naśyanti.*
>
> Too much rain ruins the fields.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिवृष्टिः** (*ativṛṣṭiḥ*) is built from *ati*, "beyond, too much", and *vṛṣṭi*, "rain", on the root √vṛṣ; MW gives the compound as excessive rain (MW).
