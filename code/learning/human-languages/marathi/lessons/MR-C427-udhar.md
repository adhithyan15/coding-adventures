## You'll want to know: उधार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C427-VYAJ427-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उधार** — *udhār* — "on credit".

> तो वाण्याकडून उधार सामान घेतो.
>
> *to vāṇyākaḍūn udhār sāmān gheto.*
>
> He buys groceries on credit from the grocer.

उधार घेणे, to take on credit; उधार देणे, to give on credit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उधार** (*udhār*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *udhār*, "buying and selling on credit", under Sanskrit *uddhāra*, "raising; a loan" (CDIAL 2018).
