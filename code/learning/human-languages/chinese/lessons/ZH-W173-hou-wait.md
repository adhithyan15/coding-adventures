## Script — the person piece, a thin stroke, then the right side
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HOU-WAIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 候

**Three pieces, left to right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The person piece, 亻, two strokes,** as in 你.

**A thin vertical,** one short stroke standing beside it.

**The right side, seven strokes.** On top a short horizontal that turns and falls to the left, like a small hook. A horizontal under it. Then the base, five strokes: a short stroke falling to the left, a short horizontal, a longer horizontal, a long stroke falling to the left through them, and a long stroke falling to the right.

2 + 1 + 7 = ten.
