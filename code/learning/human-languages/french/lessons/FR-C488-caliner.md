## You'll want to know: câliner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C488-CALINS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**câliner** — "to cuddle".

> Le père câline son bébé sur le canapé.
>
> *The father cuddles his baby on the sofa.*

*Câlin* as an adjective means affectionate: *un chat câlin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*câliner* is usually traced to a western dialect verb for resting in the shade or the warmth, which goes back to Latin *calēre* "to be warm" (Lewis & Short); the origin is not fully settled.
