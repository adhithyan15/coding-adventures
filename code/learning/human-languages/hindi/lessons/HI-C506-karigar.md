## You'll want to know: कारीगर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C506-KARIGAR506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कारीगर** — *kārīgar* — "a craftsman; an artisan".

> यह कुर्सी गाँव के एक कारीगर ने बनाई।
>
> *Yah kursī gā̃v ke ek kārīgar ne banāī.*
>
> A craftsman from the village made this chair.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कारीगर** (*kārīgar*) is usually traced to Persian *kārīgar*, "a craftsman".
