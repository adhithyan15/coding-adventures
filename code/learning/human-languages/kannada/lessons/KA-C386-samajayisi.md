## You'll want to know: ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C386-SAMAJAYISHI386-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ** — *samajāyiṣi* — "an explanation".

> ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಅವನು ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ ಕೊಟ್ಟನು.
>
> *taḍavāgi bandiddakke avanu samajāyiṣi koṭṭanu.*
>
> He gave an explanation for coming late.

ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ ಕೊಡು, to explain oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ** (*samajāyiṣi*) is usually traced to Hindustani *samjhāish*, "an explanation", from *samajhnā*, "to understand"; DEDR and CDIAL do not list the Kannada form.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
