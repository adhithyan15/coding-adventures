# તેણે, તેમણે — who did it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERGATIVE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-OBJECT-AGREE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I drank tea* and *I drank water*, and say why the two verbs end
differently.

## Grammar Lens: one person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERG-SG-01]; assesses=[GU-PRON-TE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-TUTAME-01, GU-LEX-CHA] -->

| who | plain | in the object past |
|---|---|---|
| I | હું | મેં |
| you (familiar) | તું | તેં |
| he, she | તે | તેણે |

> **તેણે ચા પીધી.** — *teṇe cā pīdhī.* — He drank tea. She drank tea.

**તેણે** is the form CDIAL records for Gujarati under the old Sanskrit stem
*ta-* (CDIAL 5612), the stem behind **તે** and **તેઓ**.

## Grammar Lens: more than one
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERG-PL-01]; assesses=[GU-PRON-AME-01, GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01, GU-LEX-PAANI] -->

| who | plain | in the object past |
|---|---|---|
| we | અમે | અમે |
| you (respectful) | તમે | તમે |
| they | તેઓ | તેમણે |

**અમે** and **તમે** do not change at all: **અમે ચા પીધી**, **તમે પાણી પીધું**.
Only **તેઓ** moves, to **તેમણે**, built on the **તેમ-** of **તેમને**.
