## You'll want to know: ستون فقرات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C497-BONES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ستون فقرات** (*sotun-e faqârât*) — "the spine, backbone".

> تصادف به ستون فقرات او آسیب زد.
>
> *tasâdof be sotun-e faqârât-e u âsib zad.*
>
> The accident injured his spine.

*sotun* 'pillar' + *faqârât* 'vertebrae' (an Arabic plural): the pillar of vertebrae.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sotun-e faqârât* is built in Persian on inherited *sotun*, 'pillar', usually traced to Middle Persian *stūn*, and *faqârât*, 'vertebrae', a loan usually traced to the Arabic plural *faqārāt*.
