## You'll want to know: свекровь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C491-INLAWS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**свекровь** (*svekróv*) — "a husband's mother, a mother-in-law".

> Моя свекровь прекрасно готовит.
>
> *Moyá svekróv prekrásno gotóvit.*
>
> My mother-in-law is a wonderful cook.

Feminine. The husband's mother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*свекровь* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *svekry*, which EtymDB traces to PIE *sweḱrúh₂ 'mother-in-law'.
