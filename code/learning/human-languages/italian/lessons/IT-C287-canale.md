## You'll want to know: il canale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C287-DIRETTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il canale** — "the TV channel; the canal".

> Cambia canale, per favore!
>
> *Change the channel, please!*

In a city built on water, *il canale* is a canal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*canale* is inherited from Latin *canālis* "a pipe, a channel" (EtymDB), built on *canna* "reed".
