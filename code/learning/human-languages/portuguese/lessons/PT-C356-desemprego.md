## You'll want to know: o desemprego
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C356-DESPEDIMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o desemprego** — "the unemployment".

> O desemprego subiu no inverno.
>
> *Unemployment rose in the winter.*

*Estar no desemprego* — to be out of work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desemprego* is built from *des-* and *emprego* "job", on *empregar*, which is inherited from Latin *implicāre* "to fold into, to involve" (EtymDB).
