## You'll want to know: a poupança
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C391-ORCAMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a poupança** — "the saving; savings".

> Usei as minhas poupanças para comprar um carro.
>
> *I used my savings to buy a car.*

*Conta poupança* — savings account.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poupança* is built on *poupar* "to save", which is inherited from Latin *palpāre* "to touch softly, to stroke" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short): to handle money gently.
