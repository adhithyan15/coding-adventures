Marathi, chapter 30: Two Sentences, One Breath.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
आणि (āṇi) — and.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say two sentences you already own: मला चहा आवडतो, and मला दूध आवडतं. Two sentences, two breaths. This chapter makes them one.

You'll want to know: आणि.
आणि — āṇi — and.
मला चहा आवडतो आणि दूध आवडतं. — malā chahā āvaḍto āṇi dūdh āvaḍtaṁ. — I like tea and I like milk.
आणि (āṇi) sits BETWEEN the two pieces and changes nothing on either side. Each half keeps its own agreement: चहा is masculine and keeps आवडतो, दूध is neuter and keeps आवडतं. The joining word does not reach inside.
It joins two nouns just as happily: भाऊ आणि (āṇi) बहीण, brother and sister.
The word is old and slightly surprising. It comes from Sanskrit अन्य (anya), "other." A word for the other one wore down into a word for one more. English does the same trick from the other side: another is an other.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bhāū āṇi bahīṇ — brother and sister]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: malā chahā āvaḍto āṇi dūdh āvaḍtaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: which half of that sentence changed when you joined it — neither]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Join brother and sister into one Marathi phrase.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Where does आणि (āṇi) sit, and what does it change? (Between the two pieces; nothing.) What did it originally mean? (Other.)
Sources: Wiktionary: आणि (āṇi).


Lesson 2 of 4.
किंवा (kiṁvā) — or.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
One lesson ago you got आणि (āṇi), which adds. Say चहा आणि (āṇi) पाणी. Now you need the word that does the opposite: it puts two things side by side and asks you to take only one.

You'll want to know: किंवा.
किंवा — kiṁvā — or.
चहा किंवा पाणी? — chahā kiṁvā pāṇī? — Tea or water?
It sits exactly where आणि (āṇi) sat. The two words are a matched pair: same slot, opposite job. आणि (āṇi) hands you both, किंवा (kiṁvā) makes you choose.
Taken apart, किंवा (kiṁvā) is two Sanskrit words carried over whole: किम् (kim), "what" — the same stem that gave Marathi काय — plus वा (vā), an ancient particle meaning "or." That वा is genuinely old: Latin kept it as the -ve in sīve, "or if." Marathi's word for choosing is, underneath, "what-or."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: chahā kiṁvā pāṇī?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dūdh kiṁvā pāṇī?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the same pair with āṇi, and hear the offer become a list]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Offer a guest a choice between tea and milk, then offer them both.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which of the two joining words makes the listener choose? (किंवा (kiṁvā).) What two Sanskrit words is it made of? (किम् + वा, "what or".)
Sources: Wiktionary: किंवा (kiṁvā).


Lesson 3 of 4.
पण (paṇ) — but.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Two joining words are yours: आणि (āṇi) adds, किंवा (kiṁvā) offers. Say चहा आणि (āṇi) दूध, then चहा किंवा (kiṁvā) दूध. Both are friendly. Neither can disagree with anything.

You'll want to know: पण.
पण — paṇ — but.
चहा आहे पण दूध नाही. — chahā āhe paṇ dūdh nāhī. — There is tea but there is no milk.
पण (paṇ) stands in the same slot as the other two and does the one thing they cannot: it lets the second half push back against the first. Until you have it, every sentence you can build only agrees with itself.
पण (paṇ) and पुन्हा — the again in पुन्हा भेटू — are the same Sanskrit word, पुनर् (punar), "again, back." One descendant kept the time meaning; the other kept only the back, and turned it into contradiction. English does this too: but began as be-utan, "outside."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: chahā āhe paṇ dūdh nāhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the three joining words in a row — āṇi, kiṁvā, paṇ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: which Marathi word for again is पण (paṇ)'s twin — punhā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Fill the same slot three ways with chahā and dūdh: add, offer, contradict.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which joining word lets the second half disagree with the first? (पण (paṇ).) Which everyday farewell word is its twin? (पुन्हा.)
Sources: Wiktionary: पण (paṇ).
