## You'll want to know: esconder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C532-SECRETO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**esconder** — "to hide".

> Escondió el dinero debajo de la cama.
>
> *He hid the money under the bed.*

*Esconderse* is to hide yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esconder* is inherited from Latin *abscondere* 'to put away, to hide' (Lewis & Short), built on *abs* 'away' and *condere* 'to put, to store'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
