6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
گروی (girvī) — pawned; held as a pledge (for a loan).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a piece of advice, then the Urdu for to bow (one's head).

You'll want to know: گروی.
گروی — girvī — "pawned; held as a pledge (for a loan)".
اگر قرض نہیں ملا تو گھر گروی رکھنا پڑے گا۔ agar qarz nahīṅ milā to ghar girvī rakhnā paṛe gā. If we don't get a loan, we'll have to mortgage the house.

The word, taken apart.
گروی (girvī) is usually traced to Persian girav, "a pledge, a pawn", with the ending -ī.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 6.
اخراجات (ikhrājāt) — expenses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to bow (one's head), then the Urdu for pawned.

You'll want to know: اخراجات.
اخراجات — ikhrājāt — "expenses".
اگر اخراجات بڑھے تو ہمیں زیادہ کام کرنا پڑے گا۔ agar ikhrājāt baṛhe to hameṅ zyādā kām karnā paṛe gā. If our expenses go up, we will have to work more.
Masculine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
اخراجات (ikhrājāt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ikhrājāt, "outgoings", the plural of ikhrāj, "taking out".

Lesson 5 of 6.
لٹنا (luṭnā) — to be robbed; to be ruined.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for compensation, then the Urdu for damages (to be paid).

You'll want to know: لٹنا.
لٹنا — luṭnā — "to be robbed; to be ruined".
اگر آپ نے جوا کھیلا تو آپ لٹ جائیں گے۔ agar āp ne juā khelā to āp luṭ jāeṅ ge. If you gamble, you will be fleeced.
Past (he …): وہ سفر میں لٹ گیا, voh safar meṅ luṭ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
لٹنا (luṭnā) is the intransitive partner of lūṭnā, "to plunder", which CDIAL lists under luṭṭati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11078).
