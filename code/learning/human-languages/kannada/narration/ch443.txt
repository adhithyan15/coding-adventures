Kannada, chapter 443: City Traffic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಚಾರ (sañcāra) — traffic; movement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mountain pass, then the Kannada for a route.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಚಾರ.
ಸಂಚಾರ — sañcāra — "traffic; movement".
ಸಂಜೆ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಸಂಚಾರ ತುಂಬಾ ನಿಧಾನ. sañje āru gaṇṭege sañcāra tumbā nidhāna. At six in the evening the traffic is very slow.
ಸಂಚಾರ (sañcāra) ಪೊಲೀಸ್, the traffic police; ಸಂಚಾರ (sañcāra) ನಿಯಮ, a traffic rule.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಚಾರ (sañcāra) (saṃcāra) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃcāra, "walking about, a passage" (CDIAL 12869), from sam- "together" and car "to move".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪಾದಚಾರಿ (pādacāri) — a pedestrian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for traffic, then the Kannada for a traffic signal.

You'll want to know: ಪಾದಚಾರಿ.
ಪಾದಚಾರಿ — pādacāri — "a pedestrian".
ಪಾದಚಾರಿಗಳು ರಸ್ತೆಯ ಅಂಚಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಡೆಯಬೇಕು. pādacārigaḷu rasteya añcinalli naḍeyabēku. Pedestrians should walk at the edge of the road.
Plural: ಪಾದಚಾರಿಗಳು, pādacārigaḷu.
ಪಾದ, a foot, plus -ಚಾರಿ, one who goes: one who goes on foot.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಾದಚಾರಿ (pādacāri) is borrowed from Sanskrit pādacārin, "going on foot, a pedestrian" (Monier-Williams), from pāda, "a foot", and cārin, "one who moves".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a traffic signal, then the Kannada for a pedestrian.

You'll want to know: ದಟ್ಟಣೆ.
ದಟ್ಟಣೆ — daṭṭaṇe — "congestion; crowding".
ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ವಾಹನ ದಟ್ಟಣೆ ಇತ್ತು. rasteyalli vāhana daṭṭaṇe ittu. There was a traffic jam on the road.
ವಾಹನ ದಟ್ಟಣೆ (daṭṭaṇe), a traffic jam; ದಟ್ಟ means dense or thick.

The word, taken apart.
ದಟ್ಟಣೆ (daṭṭaṇe) is native Dravidian, built on Kannada daṭṭa, "thick, crowded, dense" (DEDR 3020).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸು (niyantrisu) — to control; to regulate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pedestrian, then the Kannada for congestion.

You'll want to know: ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸು.
ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸು — niyantrisu — "to control; to regulate".
ಒಬ್ಬ ಪೊಲೀಸ್ ಸಂಚಾರವನ್ನು ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. obba polīs sañcāravannu niyantrisuttiddanu. A policeman was controlling the traffic.
Past (I …): ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸಿದೆ, niyantriside.
What is controlled takes -ಅನ್ನು; ನಿಯಂತ್ರಣ is control.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಯಂತ್ರಿಸು (niyantrisu) is built from Sanskrit niyantraṇa, "restraining, checking, governing" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.
