## You'll want to know: evaluar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C597-APRENDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**evaluar** — "to assess, to evaluate".

> El profesor evalúa el trabajo de todo el año.
>
> *The teacher assesses the work of the whole year.*

Written accent in *evalúo, evalúas, evalúa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*evaluar* was borrowed from French *évaluer*, built on *value* 'worth', from Latin *valere* 'to be strong, to be worth' (EtymDB).
