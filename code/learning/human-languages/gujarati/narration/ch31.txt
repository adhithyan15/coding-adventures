Gujarati, chapter 31: The Table Is Set.
8 lessons. You can do the first 6 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
ભાત (bhāt) — hear rice.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the Gujarati word for the flatbread you met in the water-and-tea chapter. Now listen twice: bhāt. It means rice — cooked rice, the base of the plate, not the raw grain.

Guided Practice.

Lesson 6 of 8.
Five things on one plate, by ear — five food words retrieved by ear alone, with the eight time words returning at R3.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
Nothing new arrives here. Say the first and the last word of the chapter before any list is read to you.

Guided Practice.
Keep two scores. Listening goes from sound to meaning; speaking goes from meaning to sound. A strong score in one cannot cover a miss in the other. The third row is not new material — it is the eight time words of the day-and-its-times chapter, returning about thirty lessons on at their third spacing window.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Hear this shuffled row and give the five English meanings: shāk — kerī — bhāt — tel — dāḷ.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Say the five Gujarati words from the English cues alone: rice, lentils, vegetable, oil, mango.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Now the older band, by ear only: at which part of the day would you eat — say savār, bapor, sānj, and rāt in day order, and give the English of divas, mahino, āj, and atyāre.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Sources: rice, lentil, vegetable, and mango.


Lesson 7 of 8.
Bhāt reaches the page — rice, now written with signs taught long before this chapter.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 5 seconds]
Before anything is uncovered, say the Gujarati word for rice.

Guided Practice.
Uncover ભાત (bhāt) once. Three pieces, none new: ભ from the courtesy chapter, the ા stroke from the greeting, and ત beside it. Name the pieces aloud in order, then read the whole word once. This is the only word of the chapter the hand is asked for; the other four stay in the ear for now, which is the gloss-first order rather than an omission.
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Read this word aloud and give its meaning: ભાત (bhāt).

Writing — guided copy, model in view.
Keep the model in view and copy ભાત (bhāt) once. Repair one shape if it needs it; do not start a row.
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]

Wrap-up Recall.

Source: Cambridge: rice.
