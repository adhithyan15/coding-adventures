## You'll want to know: مجفف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C447-DUSH447-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مجفف** — *mujaffif* — "a dryer; a hairdryer".

> لم يعمل مجفف الشعر في الحمام.
>
> *lam yaʿmal mujaffif ash-shaʿr fī al-ḥammām.*
>
> The hairdryer in the bathroom did not work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجفف** (*mujaffif*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-f-f*, "to be dry" (Lane). It is the Form II participle, "a thing that dries".
