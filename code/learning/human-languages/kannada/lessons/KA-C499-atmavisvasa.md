## You'll want to know: ಆತ್ಮವಿಶ್ವಾಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C499-MANASTHITI499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆತ್ಮವಿಶ್ವಾಸ** — *ātmaviśvāsa* — "self-confidence".

> ತರಬೇತಿಯ ನಂತರ ಅವಳಿಗೆ ಆತ್ಮವಿಶ್ವಾಸ ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *tarabētiya nantara avaḷige ātmaviśvāsa banditu.*
>
> After the training she gained self-confidence.

ಆತ್ಮ, self + ವಿಶ್ವಾಸ, trust: trust in yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆತ್ಮವಿಶ್ವಾಸ** (*ātmaviśvāsa*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit: *ātman*, "self", and *viśvāsa*, "trust, confidence" (CDIAL 11966).
