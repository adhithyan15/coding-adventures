## You'll want to know: घातक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C383-SANKAT383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घातक** — *ghātak* — "deadly, fatal".

> अगर इलाज नहीं होगा तो यह बीमारी घातक हो सकती है।
>
> *agar ilāj nahīṃ hogā to yah bīmārī ghātak ho saktī hai.*
>
> Without treatment this illness can be fatal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घातक** (*ghātak*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ghātaka*, "killing, destructive" (Monier-Williams).
