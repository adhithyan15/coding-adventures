## You'll want to know: dēprehendō, dēprehendere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C304-COMPREHENDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dēprehendō, dēprehendere** — "to catch, to catch in the act".

> Servus fūrem in hortō dēprehendit.
>
> *A slave caught the thief in the garden.*

*dēprehendō, dēprehendere, dēprehendī, dēprehēnsum*, also *dēprendō*. *In fūrtō dēprehendere*: to catch someone stealing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēprehendō** is built on *prehendō*, "to seize", with *dē-* (Lewis & Short): to snatch away, to catch something on the move.
