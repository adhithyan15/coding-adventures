## You'll want to know: さる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C211-ANIMAL211-02]; assesses=[] -->

**さる** — *saru* — "a monkey".

> **やまに さるが います。** — *yama ni saru ga imasu* — "There are monkeys on the mountain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さる** (*saru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
