## You'll want to know: मोहीम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C333-MOHIM333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहीम** — *mohīm* — "an expedition; a campaign".

> गिर्यारोहकांची मोहीम यशस्वी झाली.
>
> *giryārohkāñcī mohīm yaśasvī jhālī.*
>
> The climbers' expedition was a success.

Feminine.

मोहीम आखणे, to plan a campaign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहीम** (*mohīm*) is a loan from Persian *muhimm*, 'an important undertaking', usually traced to Arabic *muhimm*, 'important'.
