## You'll want to know: हाकलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C333-MOHIM333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हाकलणे** — *hākalṇe* — "to drive out; to chase away".

> लोकांनी शेतातून माकडांना हाकललं.
>
> *lokānnī śetātūn mākḍānnā hākallaṁ.*
>
> People drove the monkeys out of the field.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हाकललं, *tyāne hākallaṁ*.

The one driven out takes -ला; the place left takes -तून.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाकलणे** (*hākalṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *hākalṇe*, 'to drive on or away', under Sanskrit *hakkayati*, 'calls to' (CDIAL 13939).
