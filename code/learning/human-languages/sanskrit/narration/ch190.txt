Loudly? (उच्चैः, uccaiḥ.) Softly? (नीचैः, nīcaiḥ.) How much?? (कियत्, kiyat.) Of what kind?? (कीदृशः, kīdṛśaḥ.) For? (हि, hi.) Surely? (नूनम्, nūnam.) Certainly? (अवश्यम्, avaśyam.)
