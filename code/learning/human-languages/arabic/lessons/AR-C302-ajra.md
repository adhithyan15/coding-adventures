## You'll want to know: أجرى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C302-HIWAR302-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أجرى** — *ajrā* — "to conduct, to carry out".

> أجرت الصحفية مقابلة مع الطبيب.
>
> *ajrat aṣ-ṣaḥafiyya muqābala maʿa aṭ-ṭabīb.*
>
> The journalist did an interview with the doctor.

Present: يجري, *yujrī*.

أجرى مقابلة, to conduct an interview; أجرى عملية, to perform an operation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أجرى** (*ajrā*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-y* (Lane), "to run, to flow", the root of *jarā*, to run. It is Form IV, "to make a thing run": to carry it through.
