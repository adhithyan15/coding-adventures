## You'll want to know: प्रच्छन्नः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C339-YUVARAJA339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रच्छन्नः** — *pracchannaḥ* — "disguised; hidden".

> युवराजः प्रच्छन्नः नगरे भ्रमति।
>
> *yuvarājaḥ pracchannaḥ nagare bhramati.*
>
> The crown prince wanders the city in disguise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रच्छन्नः** (*pracchannaḥ*) is built from *pra* and *channa*, "covered", from the root √chad, "to cover"; MW gives *pracchanna* as covered, hidden, secret, disguised (MW).
