## You'll want to know: ferver
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C332-FRITAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ferver** — "to boil".

> A água já está a ferver.
>
> *The water is already boiling.*

The water *ferve*; the food *coze*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferver* is inherited from Latin *fervēre* "to boil, to be hot" (EtymDB).
