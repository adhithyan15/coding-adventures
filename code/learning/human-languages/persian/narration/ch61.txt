Persian, chapter 61: Full, Empty, Rich, Poor, Clean.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
پر (por) — full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for expensive, then the Persian for cheap.

You'll want to know: پر.
پر — por — "full".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: por]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: por, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say por]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for expensive, then the Persian for cheap, then say por again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does پر (por) mean? ("Full".) Say it once more.
