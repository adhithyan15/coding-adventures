## You'll want to know: معمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C305-MUAMMA305-01]; assesses=[] -->

**معمہ** — *muammā* — "a mystery; a puzzle".

> یہ ایک معمہ تھا۔
>
> *yih ek muammā thā.*
>
> This was a mystery.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معمہ** (*muammā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muʿammā*, "a thing made obscure, a riddle".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
