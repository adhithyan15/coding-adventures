Japanese, chapter 341: Persuading Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
せっとくする (settoku suru) — to persuade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to make an effort, then the Japanese for a warning.

You'll want to know: せっとくする.
せっとくする — settoku suru — "to persuade".
ははが せっとくしたので、ちちは やめました。 — haha ga settoku shita node, chichi wa yamemashita — "My mother talked him round, so my father gave it up."
Polite せっとくします, settokushimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): ははが ちちを せっとくしました。, haha ga chichi o settoku shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
せっとくする (settoku suru) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb suru, "to do". The noun, settoku, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: setsu, "explanation" (Middle Chinese sywet) and toku, "gain" (tok) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as set-toku.

Lesson 2 of 5.
うながす (unagasu) — to urge; to prompt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a warning, then the Japanese for to persuade.

You'll want to know: うながす.
うながす — unagasu — "to urge; to prompt".
じかんが ないので、せんせいが がくせいを うながしました。 — jikan ga nai node, sensei ga gakusei o unagashimashita — "There was no time, so the teacher hurried the students along."
Polite うながします, unagashimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): せんせいが うながしました。, sensei ga unagashimashita.

The word, taken apart.
うながす (unagasu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ひきとめる (hikitomeru) — to ask someone to stay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to persuade, then the Japanese for to urge.

You'll want to know: ひきとめる.
ひきとめる — hikitomeru — "to ask someone to stay".
あめが ふったので、ともだちを ひきとめました。 — ame ga futta node, tomodachi o hikitomemashita — "It rained, so I asked my friend to stay."
Polite ひきとめます, hikitomemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): ははが ともだちを ひきとめました。, haha ga tomodachi o hikitomemashita.

The word, taken apart.
ひきとめる (hikitomeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from hiki, "pulling (hiku)" and tomeru, "to stop", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
