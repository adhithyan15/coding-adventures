## You'll want to know: 离
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C205-FAR205-01]; assesses=[] -->

**离** — *lí* — "from; away from (in distance)".

> *wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn* — My home is close to the school.

The new character, in pieces: 离 — ten strokes in three pieces: the dot and the bar that cap 文; two short strokes crossing inside a box open at the top; then a frame that hooks, holding a turning stroke and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**离** (*lí*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *lje* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says that being apart while near is *lí*, and being apart while far is another word; in another tone it is glossed 'to leave'. Its use for distance from a place is not given there.
