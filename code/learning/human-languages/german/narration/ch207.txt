Lesson 1 of 5.
der Krankenwagen — an ambulance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an injection, then the German for an emergency.

You'll want to know: der Krankenwagen.
der Krankenwagen — "an ambulance".

The word, taken apart.
Krankenwagen from krank + -n- + the word for "vehicle", Middle High German wagen from Proto-Germanic wagnaz, English wagon (EtymDB 2.1).

Feuerwehr is Feuer, "fire", + the word for "defence", Old High German werī from Proto-Germanic warīniz (EtymDB 2.1): the defence against fire. English weir is a cousin.
