## You'll want to know: zhàngpeng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C441-CAMP441-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàngpeng* — "tent".

> *wǎnshang wǒmen zài zhàngpeng lǐ shuìjiào* — At night we sleep in the tent.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàngpeng* is built from *zhàng* 'a curtain' and *péng* 'an awning', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjangH* and *buwng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a woven cover over a boat; in this word it is said light.
