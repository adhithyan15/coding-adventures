## You'll want to know: ಊದು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C546-NEMMADI546-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಊದು** — *ūdu* — "to puff; to blow into".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಒಲೆಯ ಬೆಂಕಿಯನ್ನು ಕೊಳವೆಯಿಂದ ಊದಿದರು.
>
> *ajji oleya beṅkiyannu koḷaveyinda ūdidaru.*
>
> Grandmother blew on the stove fire through a pipe.

Past (I …): ಊದಿದೆ, *ūdide*.

With the mouth; the wind's blowing is ಬೀಸು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಊದು** (*ūdu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 741 lists Kannada *ūdu*, "to blow, to become inflated, swell".
