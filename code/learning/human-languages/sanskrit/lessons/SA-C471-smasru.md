## You'll want to know: श्मश्रु
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C471-MASTISKA471-03]; assesses=[] -->

**श्मश्रु** — *śmaśru* — "a beard".

> पितामहस्य श्मश्रु श्वेतम् अस्ति।
>
> *pitāmahasya śmaśru śvetam asti.*
>
> Grandfather's beard is white.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्मश्रु** (*śmaśru*) MW calls it of unknown derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it gives "the beard, esp. the moustache" (MW).
