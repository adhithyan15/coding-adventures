## You'll want to know: дуршлаг
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C334-FARSH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**дуршлаг** (*durshlág*) — "a colander".

> Макароны откинули на дуршлаг.
>
> *Makaróny otkínuli na durshlág.*
>
> The pasta was drained in a colander.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дуршлаг* was borrowed from German *Durchschlag* (EtymDB), from *durch* "through" and *Schlag* "a blow".
