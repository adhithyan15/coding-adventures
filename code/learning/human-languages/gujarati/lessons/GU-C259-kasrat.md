## You'll want to know: કસરત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C259-MATCH259-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કસરત** — *kasrat* — "exercise, workout".

> **મેં સવારે કસરત કરી.** — *mẽ savāre kasrat karī* — "I exercised in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **કસરત** (*kasrat*) is not settled. It is usually linked with a Persian word of the same shape, but CDIAL has no entry for it and our sources do not confirm the link.
