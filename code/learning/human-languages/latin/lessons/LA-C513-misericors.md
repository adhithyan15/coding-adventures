## You'll want to know: misericors, misericordis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C513-MISE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**misericors, misericordis** — "compassionate, merciful".

> Fēmina misericors pauperibus cibum dat.
>
> *The compassionate woman gives food to the poor.*

One ending: *miser* 'wretched' + *cor* 'heart' — with a heart for the wretched.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**misericors** is built on *miser* "wretched" and *cor* "heart" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
