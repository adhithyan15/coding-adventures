## You'll want to know: yízhì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C348-TALK348-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yízhì* — "all of one mind; in agreement".

> *dàjiā de yìjiàn hěn yízhì* — Everyone was of one mind.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The tone of yī changes here: before a falling tone it is said yí, and the spelling writes the change.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yízhì* is built from 一 'one' and *zhì* 'to arrive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *trijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the start of the numbers' and the second 'to arrive'. How the pair came to mean agreement is not settled.
