## Chapters 302-337: 180 more A2 headwords

The last of four Malayalam A2 vocabulary tranches: thirty-six chapters of five
words, in four runs, each closed by two review lessons; fifty are verbs.
Malayalam reached A2 with the third tranche, 60 headwords over the floor; this
one takes it to 1,440 headwords and 272 verbs at or below A2, and its verbs
close the B1 verb gap (250) as well.
