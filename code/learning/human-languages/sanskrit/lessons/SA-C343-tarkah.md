## You'll want to know: तर्कः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C343-VADA343-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्कः** — *tarkaḥ* — "reasoning".

> वादे तर्कः आवश्यकः।
>
> *vāde tarkaḥ āvaśyakaḥ.*
>
> In a debate, reasoning is necessary.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्कः** (*tarkaḥ*) comes from the root √tark, "to conjecture, to reason"; MW gives *tarka* the senses "conjecture" and "reasoning, inquiry" (MW).
