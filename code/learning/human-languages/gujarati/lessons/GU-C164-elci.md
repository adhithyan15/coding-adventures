## You'll want to know: એલચી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C164-SPICE164-05]; assesses=[] -->

**એલચી** — *elcī* — "cardamom".

> **એલચી મોંઘી છે.** — *elcī moṅghī che* — "Cardamom is expensive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **એલચી** (*elcī*) is not settled. CDIAL lists Sanskrit *ēlā*, "cardamom", which it takes from Dravidian, but no Gujarati form under it (CDIAL 2522).
