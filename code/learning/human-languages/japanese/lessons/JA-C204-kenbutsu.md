## You'll want to know: けんぶつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C204-TRIP204-03]; assesses=[] -->

**けんぶつ** — *kenbutsu* — "sightseeing".

> **ともだちと まちの けんぶつに いきます。** — *tomodachi to machi no kenbutsu ni ikimasu* — "I go sightseeing in town with a friend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんぶつ** (*kenbutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "to see" (Middle Chinese *kenH*) and *butsu*, "thing" (*mjut*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
