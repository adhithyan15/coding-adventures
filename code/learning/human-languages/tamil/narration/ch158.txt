Tamil, chapter 158: Recently, Already, Last, Once, To Spend (Time).
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சமீபத்தில் (camīpattil) — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to burn, then the Tamil for to put out.

You'll want to know: சமீபத்தில்.
சமீபத்தில் — camīpattil — "recently". Tamil's past for "I" ends in -ஏன் after a past stem: நான் சமீபத்தில் ஒரு புத்தகம் படித்தேன் — nāṉ camīpattil oru puttakam paṭittēṉ — "I recently read a book."

The word, taken apart.
சமீபத்தில் (camīpattil) is a loan from Sanskrit samīpa, "near" (CDIAL 13226). Camīpam, "nearness", takes the locative -il: "in the near (past)".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ஏற்கனவே (ēṟkaṉavē) — already.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to put out, then the Tamil for recently.

You'll want to know: ஏற்கனவே.
ஏற்கனவே — ēṟkaṉavē — "already". நான் ஏற்கனவே சாப்பிட்டேன் — nāṉ ēṟkaṉavē cāppiṭṭēṉ — "I have already eaten."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ஏற்கனவே (ēṟkaṉavē) is uncertain. It may contain ēṟka, a form of ēl, "to happen, to be fitting" (DEDR 905), but DEDR does not list the word itself.

Lesson 3 of 5.
போன (pōṉa) — last, previous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for recently, then the Tamil for already.

You'll want to know: போன.
போன — pōṉa — "last, previous". It is the past form of போ, "to go", used before a noun: போன வாரம் — pōṉa vāram — "last week"; போன வருடம் — pōṉa varuṭam — "last year".

The word, taken apart.
போன (pōṉa) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4572). It is the past of pō, "to go": "the one that has gone".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ஒரு காலத்தில் (oru kālattil) — once, at one time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for already, then the Tamil for last.

You'll want to know: ஒரு காலத்தில்.
ஒரு காலத்தில் — oru kālattil — "once, at one time". It points far back: ஒரு காலத்தில் இங்கே ஒரு குளம் இருந்தது — oru kālattil iṅkē oru kuḷam iruntatu — "There was once a pond here." இருந்தது is the past of இரு.

The word, taken apart.
ஒரு காலத்தில் (oru kālattil) is native oru, "one" (DEDR 990), and kālam, "time", from Sanskrit kāla (CDIAL 3084), with the locative -il: "at one time".

Lesson 5 of 5.
கழி (kaḻi) — to spend (time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for last, then the Tamil for once.

You'll want to know: கழி.
கழி — kaḻi — "to spend (time)". Said of time: விடுமுறையைக் கிராமத்தில் கழித்தேன் — viṭumuṟaiyaik kirāmattil kaḻittēṉ — "I spent the holiday in the village."

The word, taken apart.
கழி (kaḻi) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1356 lists Malayalam kaḻiyuka, "to pass, be spent".
