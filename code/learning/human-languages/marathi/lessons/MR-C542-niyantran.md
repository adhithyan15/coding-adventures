## You'll want to know: नियंत्रण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C542-NIYAM542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नियंत्रण** — *niyantraṇ* — "control".

> आग लवकरच नियंत्रणात आली.
>
> *āg lavkarac niyantraṇāt ālī.*
>
> The fire was soon brought under control.

Neuter.

नियंत्रणात येणे, to come under control.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियंत्रण** (*niyantraṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *niyantraṇa*, "restraining, governing" (Monier-Williams).
