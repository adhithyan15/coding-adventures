Bengali, chapter 466: The Body.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
শরীর (shôrīr) — the body.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for grateful, then the Bengali for to weigh up.

You'll want to know: শরীর.
শরীর — shôrīr — "the body".
সারাদিন কাজ করে আমার শরীর ক্লান্ত। sārādin kāj kôre āmār shôrīr klāntô. After working all day my body is tired.
শরীর (shôrīr) ভালো নেই, I'm not feeling well; শরীর (shôrīr) খারাপ, unwell.

The word, taken apart.
শরীর (shôrīr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit śarīra, "the body" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বাহু (bāhu) — an arm (from shoulder to hand).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to weigh up, then the Bengali for the body.

You'll want to know: বাহু.
বাহু — bāhu — "an arm (from shoulder to hand)".
মীরা শিশুটিকে দুই বাহুতে তুলে নিল। mīrā shishuṭike dui bāhute tule nilô. Mira lifted the child up in both arms.
হাত (taught) is the hand and, loosely, the whole arm; বাহু (bāhu) is the arm itself.

The word, taken apart.
বাহু (bāhu) is a tatsama, Sanskrit bāhu, "arm" (CDIAL 9229), beside which CDIAL lists a worn-down Bengali bāh.
