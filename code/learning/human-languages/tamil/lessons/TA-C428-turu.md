# தூறு (tūṟu) — to drizzle

## You'll want to know: தூறு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C428-SARAL428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தூறு** — *tūṟu* — "to drizzle".

> வெளியே மழை தூறிக்கொண்டிருந்தது.
>
> *veḷiyē maḻai tūṟikkoṇṭiruntatu.*
>
> It was drizzling outside.

Past (it …): தூறியது, *tūṟiyatu*.

The noun is தூறல், a drizzle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தூறு** (*tūṟu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3398), "to drizzle"; the same article gives தூறல், "drizzling".
