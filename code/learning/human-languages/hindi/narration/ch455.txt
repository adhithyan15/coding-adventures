Hindi, chapter 455: Sadness and Regret.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ग़म (ġam) — grief; sorrow at a loss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for strange, then the Hindi for a marvel.

You'll want to know: ग़म.
ग़म — ġam — "grief; sorrow at a loss".
पूरा परिवार दादा की मौत के ग़म में है। pūrā parivār dādā kī maut ke ġam meṁ hai. The whole family is grieving over my grandfather's death.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ग़म (ġam) is usually traced to Arabic ġamm, "grief, distress", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
कुढ़ना (kuṛhnā) — to fret; to grieve inwardly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for disappointed, then the Hindi for dejected.

You'll want to know: कुढ़ना.
कुढ़ना — kuṛhnā — "to fret; to grieve inwardly".
अरुण अपनी हार पर बहुत कुढ़ा। Aruṇ apnī hār par bahut kuṛhā. Arun fretted bitterly over his defeat.
Past (he …): वह बहुत कुढ़ा, vah bahut kuṛhā.

The word, taken apart.
कुढ़ना (kuṛhnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi kuṛhnā, "to be annoyed, to grieve", under Sanskrit kruddhá, "angry" (CDIAL 3598). It is the anger or sorrow one swallows instead of showing.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दुखाना (dukhānā) — to hurt someone's feelings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for dejected, then the Hindi for to fret.

You'll want to know: दुखाना.
दुखाना — dukhānā — "to hurt someone's feelings".
मीरा ने कड़वी बात कहकर अपनी माँ का दिल दुखाया। Mīrā ne kaṛvī bāt kahkar apnī māṁ kā dil dukhāyā. Mira hurt her mother's feelings with a harsh remark.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दिल दुखाया, usne dil dukhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
दुखाना (dukhānā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi dukhānā, the causative, beside dukhnā, "to ache", under Sanskrit duḥkhati, "pains" (CDIAL 6376). dil dukhānā, "to make the heart ache", is the usual phrase.
