## You'll want to know: el parto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C544-PARTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el parto** — "the delivery (of a baby), the birth".

> El parto fue largo pero todo salió bien.
>
> *The birth was long but everything went well.*

*Estar de parto* — to be in labour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parto* is inherited from Latin *partus* 'a birth' (EtymDB), built on *parere* 'to bring forth, to give birth'. It is not the *parere* 'to appear' behind *parecer*.
