## You'll want to know: خرید کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C462-HARAJ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خرید کردن** (*kharid kardan*) — "to do the shopping, to go shopping".

> شنبه ها مادرم در بازار خرید میکند.
>
> *Shanbe-hâ mâdaram dar bâzâr kharid mikonad.*
>
> On Saturdays my mother does the shopping in the bazaar.

*kharid* 'purchase' + *kardan*. *Kharidan* is to buy a thing; *kharid kardan* is shopping in general.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kharid kardan* is built from *kharid* 'purchase', the past stem of *kharidan* 'to buy', usually traced to Middle Persian *xrīdan*, and *kardan* 'to do'.
