Russian, chapter 511: Medals and Champions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
турнир (turnír) — a tournament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a loss, then the Russian for to be in the lead.

You'll want to know: турнир.
турнир (turnír) — "a tournament".
В турнире участвуют восемь команд. V turníre uchástvuyut vósem kománd. Eight teams are taking part in the tournament.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists турнир (turnír) without a source; it is usually traced through German Turnier to Old French tornei.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: чемпионат.
чемпионат (chempionát) — "a championship".
Чемпионат мира по футболу смотрят миллионы людей. Chempionát míra po futbólu smótryat millióny lyudéy. Millions of people watch the football World Cup.
Masculine. Чемпионат мира — a world championship.

The word, taken apart.
чемпионат (chempionát) is built on чемпион (chempión) 'a champion', which was borrowed from English champion (EtymDB).

Спортсмен получил золотую медаль. Sportsmén poluchíl zolotúyu medál. The athlete won a gold medal.

EtymDB lists медаль (medál), glossed 'medal', without a source; it is usually traced through French médaille to Italian medaglia.

Lesson 5 of 5.
награждать (nagrazhdát) — to award.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a champion, then the Russian for a medal.

You'll want to know: награждать.
награждать (nagrazhdát) — "to award".
Победителей награждают медалями. Pobedíteley nagrazhdáyut medályami. The winners are awarded medals.
Imperfective; perfective наградить (награжу). Награждать кого чем.

The word, taken apart.
награждать (nagrazhdát) is built on награда 'a reward'; EtymDB has no entry, and the origin of награда is not settled.
