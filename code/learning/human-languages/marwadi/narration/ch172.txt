Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 172: Sweeping and Plastering.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
झाड़णो (jhāṛṇo) — to sweep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for clean, then the Marwadi for rubbish.

You'll want to know: झाड़णो.
झाड़णो — jhāṛṇo — "to sweep".
मां दिनूगै कमरो झाड़ै है। mā̃ dinūgai kamro jhāṛai hai. Mother sweeps the room early in the morning.
Past (I …): म्हैं कमरो झाड़्यो, mhaĩ kamro jhāṛyo.

The word, taken apart.
झाड़णो (jhāṛṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed jhaṭati, "falls", and its causative, "shakes down"; CDIAL lists Marwari jhāṛṇo, "to sweep" (CDIAL 5328). You sweep with the broom you know, बुहारी.

Lesson 3 of 6.
जाळो (jāḷo) — a cobweb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to sweep, then the Marwadi for dust.

You'll want to know: जाळो.
जाळो — jāḷo — "a cobweb".
भींत माथै जाळो है। bhī̃t māthai jāḷo hai. There's a cobweb on the wall.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जाळो (jāḷo) is inherited from Sanskrit jāla, "a net, a snare"; CDIAL lists Marwari jāḷ, "a net" (CDIAL 5213). A cobweb is the spider's net.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for dust, then the Marwadi for a cobweb.

You'll want to know: लीपणो.
लीपणो — līpṇo — "to plaster (a floor or wall with mud)".
मां आंगणो लीपै है। mā̃ ā̃gaṇo līpai hai. Mother plasters the courtyard.
Past (I …): म्हैं आंगणो लीप्यो, mhaĩ ā̃gaṇo līpyo.

The word, taken apart.
लीपणो (līpṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit lipyate, "is smeared"; CDIAL lists Hindi līpnā, "to smear" (CDIAL 11061). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 5 of 6.
गारो (gāro) — mud plaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a cobweb, then the Marwadi for to plaster.

You'll want to know: गारो.
गारो — gāro — "mud plaster".
गारो गीलो है। gāro gīlo hai. The mud plaster is wet.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गारो (gāro) comes from a reconstructed gāra, "dripping, oozing"; CDIAL lists Hindi gārā, "thick mud, plaster", and Sindhi gāro, "mud plaster" (CDIAL 4137). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
