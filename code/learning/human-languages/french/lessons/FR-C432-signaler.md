## You'll want to know: signaler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C432-GERANT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**signaler** — "to report, to point out".

> J'ai signalé la fuite à la réception.
>
> *I reported the leak to reception.*

*Signaler un problème* — to report a problem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*signaler* is built on *signal*, from Late Latin *signāle*, from *signum* 'mark, sign' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
