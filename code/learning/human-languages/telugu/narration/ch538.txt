Telugu, chapter 538: Music.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
స్వరం (svaraṁ) — a musical note; a voice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for kabaddi, then the Telugu for to hop.

You'll want to know: స్వరం.
స్వరం — svaraṁ — "a musical note; a voice".
ఆమె స్వరం చాలా మధురంగా ఉంటుంది. āme svaraṁ cālā madhuraṅgā uṇṭundi. Her voice is very sweet.
Plural: స్వరాలు, svarālu.
Plural స్వరాలు: the seven notes of Indian music.

The word, taken apart.
స్వరం (svaraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit svara, "sound; a musical note" (CDIAL 13908).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hop, then the Telugu for a musical note.

You'll want to know: గాత్రం.
గాత్రం — gātraṁ — "a singing voice".
ఆమె గాత్రం విని అందరూ ఆశ్చర్యపోయారు. āme gātraṁ vini andarū āścaryapōyāru. Everyone was amazed to hear her singing voice.
In music, singing as against instruments.

The word, taken apart.
గాత్రం (gātraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit gātra, "a limb; the body" (CDIAL 4124); in Telugu music it is the voice, the body's own instrument.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a musical note, then the Telugu for a singing voice.

You'll want to know: వాయించు.
వాయించు — vāyiñcu — "to play an instrument".
మా అన్నయ్య గిటారు బాగా వాయిస్తాడు. mā annayya giṭāru bāgā vāyistāḍu. My elder brother plays the guitar well.
Past (I …): వాయించాను, vāyiñcānu.
For instruments; ఆడు is for games.

The word, taken apart.
వాయించు (vāyiñcu) is usually traced to Sanskrit vādayati, "causes to sound" (CDIAL 11510), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
