## You'll want to know: céder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C337-COMPROMIS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**céder** — "to give in, to give way; to hand over".

> Il a insisté, et j'ai fini par céder.
>
> *He insisted, and I ended up giving in.*

*Céder à quelqu'un* — to give in to someone. *Je cède*, with *è* before a silent ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*céder* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cēdere* "to go, to withdraw, to yield" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
