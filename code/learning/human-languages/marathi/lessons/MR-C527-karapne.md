## You'll want to know: करपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C527-SHIJAVNE527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**करपणे** — *karapṇe* — "to get burnt".

> माझं लक्ष नव्हतं, म्हणून भात करपला.
>
> *mājhaṁ lakṣa navhataṁ, mhaṇūn bhāt karaplā.*
>
> I was not paying attention, so the rice got burnt.

Past (he …): तो भात थोडा करपला, *to bhāt thoḍā karaplā*.

करपणे is for food that burns in the pan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**करपणे** (*karapṇe*) is a Marathi verb for food that scorches in the pan; its origin is not settled.
