## You'll want to know: वावटळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C439-VAVTAL439-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वावटळ** — *vāvṭaḷ* — "a whirlwind".

> शेतात अचानक वावटळ उठली.
>
> *śetāt acānak vāvṭaḷ uṭhlī.*
>
> Suddenly a whirlwind rose in the field.

Feminine.

वावटळ उठणे, a whirlwind rises.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वावटळ** (*vāvṭaḷ*) is a Marathi word for a spinning pillar of wind and dust; its origin is not settled.
