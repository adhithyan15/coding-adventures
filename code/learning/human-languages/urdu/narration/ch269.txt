Urdu, chapter 269: Should, Must, and So.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
جانا چاہیے (jānā chāhiye) — should, and have to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I need water". (mujhe pānī chāhiye.) چاہیے (chāhiye) after a noun is "is needed". After a verb it is advice.

You'll want to know: should.
جانا چاہیے۔ jānā chāhiye. — "One should go."
آپ کو جانا چاہیے۔ āp ko jānā chāhiye. — "You should go."
The one who should takes کو (ko), and the verb stays in its -نا form:
آپ کو آرام کرنا چاہیے۔ āp ko ārām karnā chāhiye. — "You should rest."
مجھے پانی پینا چاہیے۔ mujhe pānī pīnā chāhiye. — "I should drink water."
آپ کو نہیں جانا چاہیے۔ āp ko nahīṅ jānā chāhiye. — "You shouldn't go."

You'll want to know: have to.
You know پڑنا (paṛnā) from بیمار پڑنا (bīmār paṛnā), "to fall ill". After a verb's -نا form it is "to have to", and it carries the time:
مجھے جانا پڑے گا۔ mujhe jānā paṛe gā. — "I'll have to go."
مجھے روز کام کرنا پڑتا ہے۔ mujhe roz kām karnā paṛtā hai. — "I have to work every day."
کل مجھے جانا پڑا۔ kal mujhe jānā paṛā. — "Yesterday I had to go."
چاہیے is advice; پڑنا is no choice.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I'll have to go" — mujhe jānā paṛe gā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I'll have to work" — mujhe kām karnā paṛe gā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I'll have to rest" — mujhe ārām karnā paṛe gā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Tell a friend, politely, that they should rest; then say that you will have to go.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"You should go"? (آپ کو جانا چاہیے (jānā chāhiye).) "Yesterday I had to go"? (کل مجھے جانا پڑا.) Which one leaves no choice? (پڑنا.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
چنانچہ (chunāṅche) — therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "so, that is why". (is liye.) Then "I'll have to go". (mujhe jānā paṛe gā.)

You'll want to know: چنانچہ.
چنانچہ — chunāṅche — "therefore".
بارش ہو رہی تھی، bārish ho rahī thī, چنانچہ ہم گھر پر رہے۔ chunāṅche ham ghar par rahe. — "It was raining; therefore we stayed at home."
It is formal: you will meet it in writing, in the news and in speeches. In talk, اس لیے (is liye) says the same.

The word, taken apart.
چنانچہ (chunāṅche) is usually traced to Persian chunānche, "as, accordingly", built on chunān, "so, thus".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: chunāṅche, then its everyday partner is liye]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: چنانچہ (chunāṅche) ہم گھر پر رہے aloud, as if from a newspaper]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does چنانچہ (chunāṅche) mean? ("Therefore".) Where do you meet it? (In writing and speeches.) Its everyday partner? (اس لیے (is liye).)


Lesson 3 of 4.
اس لیے (is liye) — so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "because I was ill". (kyūṅki maiṅ bīmār thā.) That gives the reason. This lesson gives what follows from it.

You'll want to know: اس لیے.
اس لیے میں نہیں آیا۔ is liye maiṅ nahīṅ āyā. — "So I didn't come."
اس لیے (is liye), "for this", opens the result. The reason comes first, then the result:
دیر ہو رہی تھی، der ho rahī thī, اس لیے ہم نے ٹیکسی لی۔ is liye ham ne ṭaiksī lī. — "It was getting late, so we took a taxi."
ٹیکسی (ṭaiksī) is feminine: لی (lī).

You'll want to know: تو, "then".
باہر بارش ہے۔ bāhar bārish hai. — "It's raining outside."
تو میں گھر پر رہوں گا۔ to maiṅ ghar par rahūṅ gā. — "Then I'll stay at home."
تو (to) opens an answer that follows from what someone said: "so, then". In writing, چنانچہ (chunāṅche) does the work of اس لیے (is liye).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I was ill, so I stayed at home" — maiṅ bīmār thā, is liye maiṅ ghar par rahā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It was raining, so I stayed at home" — bārish ho rahī thī, is liye maiṅ ghar par rahā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "It was getting late, so I stayed at home" — der ho rahī thī, is liye maiṅ ghar par rahā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Your friend says it is raining outside. Answer: then you'll stay at home. Then say: I was ill, so I didn't come.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which comes first, the reason or اس لیے (is liye)? (The reason.) "Then I'll stay at home"? (تو میں گھر پر رہوں گا.) And the formal word? (چنانچہ (chunāṅche).)


Lesson 4 of 4.
Review — should, must, and so.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I think it will rain". (mujhe lagtā hai ki bārish ho gī.) Then "because of the rain". (bārish kī vajah se.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: You should rest. You shouldn't go.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I have to work every day.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Yesterday I had to go to the office.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I was ill, so I didn't come.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It's raining? Then I'll stay at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"You should drink water"? (آپ کو پانی پینا چاہیے.) Now the other way round: which word says there is no choice, چاہیے or پڑنا? (پڑنا.) The formal word for اس لیے (is liye)? (چنانچہ (chunāṅche).)
