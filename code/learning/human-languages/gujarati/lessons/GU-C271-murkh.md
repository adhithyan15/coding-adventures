## You'll want to know: મૂર્ખ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C271-MANNERS271-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂર્ખ** — *mūrkh* — "foolish, a fool".

> **શું હું મૂર્ખ છું?** — *śũ hũ mūrkh chũ?* — "Am I a fool?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂર્ખ** (*mūrkh*) is Sanskrit *mūrkha*, "foolish, stupid", taken over whole as a tatsama. CDIAL has no entry for it.
