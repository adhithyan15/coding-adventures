# خيانة (khiyāna) — betrayal

## You'll want to know: خيانة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C535-RIFAQ535-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خيانة** — *khiyāna* — "betrayal".

> الكذب على صديق خيانة.
>
> *al-kadhib ʿalā ṣadīq khiyāna.*
>
> Lying to a friend is a betrayal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خيانة** (*khiyāna*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-w-n*, "to be false, to betray" (Lane). Khiyāna is the verbal noun of khāna.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
