## You'll want to know: закон
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C388-ZAKON-01]; assesses=[] -->

**закон** (*zakón*) — "a law".

> Новый закон начнёт действовать в январе.
>
> *Nóvyy zakón nachnyót déystvovat v yanvaré.*
>
> The new law will come into force in January.

Masculine. *По закону* — by law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**закон** is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*zakonŭ* (EtymDB), built from *за-* and *кон*, an old word for a limit or a starting line that is still heard in *испокон веков* "from time immemorial".
