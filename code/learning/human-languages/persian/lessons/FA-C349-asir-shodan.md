## You'll want to know: اسیر شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C349-KHIYANAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اسیر شدن** (*asir shodan*) — "to be taken prisoner".

> سربازهای زیادی در جنگ اسیر شدند.
>
> *Sarbâz-hâ-ye ziyâdi dar jang asir shodand.*
>
> Many soldiers were taken prisoner in the war.

*Asir* "a captive". Present *asir mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*asir* is borrowed from Arabic *asīr*, "captive, prisoner" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
