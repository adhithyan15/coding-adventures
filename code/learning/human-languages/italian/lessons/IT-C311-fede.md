## You'll want to know: la fede
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C311-SPOSARSI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la fede** — "the wedding ring; faith".

> Porta la fede alla mano sinistra.
>
> *She wears her wedding ring on her left hand.*

The ring is a sign of *fede*, faith and trust.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fede* is inherited from Latin *fidēs* "trust, faith" (EtymDB): the ring is a pledge of faith.
