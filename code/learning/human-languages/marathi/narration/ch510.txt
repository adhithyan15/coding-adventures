Marathi, chapter 510: Leaving Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रजा (rajā) — time off work; leave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for junior, then the Marathi for to strive.

You'll want to know: रजा.
रजा — rajā — "time off work; leave".
आजारी असल्यामुळे मी दोन दिवस रजा घेतली. ājārī aslyāmuḷe mī don divas rajā ghetlī. Because I was ill I took two days off.
Feminine.
रजा (rajā) घेणे, to take leave; रजेवर असणे, to be on leave.

The word, taken apart.
रजा (rajā) is a loan from Persian razā, "leave, permission", usually traced to Arabic riḍā, "consent".

Lesson 3 of 5.
राजीनामा देणे (rājīnāmā deṇe) — to resign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for time off work, then the Marathi for unemployment.

You'll want to know: राजीनामा देणे.
राजीनामा देणे — rājīnāmā deṇe — "to resign".
मीराने जुन्या कंपनीचा राजीनामा दिला. mīrāne junyā kampanīcā rājīnāmā dilā. Meera resigned from her old company.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने राजीनामा दिला, tyāne rājīnāmā dilā.
The job takes -चा: नोकरीचा राजीनामा देणे (rājīnāmā deṇe); राजीनामा is the letter.

The word, taken apart.
राजीनामा देणे (rājīnāmā deṇe) is राजीनामा, a loan from Persian rāżī-nāma, "a letter of consent", with देणे, "to give".

Lesson 4 of 5.
निवृत्त होणे (nivṛtta hoṇe) — to retire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for unemployment, then the Marathi for to resign.

You'll want to know: निवृत्त होणे.
निवृत्त होणे — nivṛtta hoṇe — "to retire".
माझे वडील गेल्या वर्षी निवृत्त झाले. mājhe vaḍīl gelyā varṣī nivṛtta jhāle. My father retired last year.
Past (he …): तो निवृत्त झाला, to nivṛtta jhālā.
For a father, out of respect, the plural: वडील निवृत्त झाले.

The word, taken apart.
निवृत्त होणे (nivṛtta hoṇe) is the tatsama निवृत्त, from Sanskrit nivṛtta, "turned back, ceased" (Monier-Williams), with होणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
बडतर्फ करणे (baḍtarph karṇe) — to dismiss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to resign, then the Marathi for to retire.

You'll want to know: बडतर्फ करणे.
बडतर्फ करणे — baḍtarph karṇe — "to dismiss".
चोरीमुळे मालकाने त्या कामगाराला बडतर्फ केलं. corīmuḷe mālkāne tyā kāmgārālā baḍtarph kelaṁ. The owner dismissed that worker because of the theft.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कामगाराला बडतर्फ केलं, tyāne kāmgārālā baḍtarph kelaṁ.
The person dismissed takes -ला: कामगाराला बडतर्फ करणे (baḍtarph karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
बडतर्फ करणे (baḍtarph karṇe) is बडतर्फ, a loan from Persian bar-taraf, "set aside, removed", with करणे.
