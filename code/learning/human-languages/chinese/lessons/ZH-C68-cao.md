## You'll want to know: cǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C68-NATURE68-03]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎo* — "grass".

> *zhèr de cǎo hěn gāo* — The grass here is tall.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed, through an older book, as all the plants that grow.
