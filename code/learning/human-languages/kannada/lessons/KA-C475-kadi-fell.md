# ಕಡಿ (kaḍi) — to fell; to chop

## You'll want to know: ಕಡಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C475-ARANYA475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಡಿ** — *kaḍi* — "to fell; to chop".

> ರಸ್ತೆ ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸಲು ಅವರು ತುಂಬಾ ಮರಗಳನ್ನು ಕಡಿದರು.
>
> *raste vistarisalu avaru tumbā maragaḷannu kaḍidaru.*
>
> To widen the road they cut down a lot of trees.

Past (I …): ಕಡಿದೆ, *kaḍide*.

ಮರ ಕಡಿ, to fell a tree; ಕತ್ತರಿಸು is to cut with a blade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಡಿ** (*kaḍi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1125 lists Kannada *kaḍi*, "to cut, to chop, to fell".
