Arabic, chapter 126: Candle, Carpet, Doll, Toy, Fridge.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شمعة (shamʿa) — a candle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a basket, then the Arabic for a box.
[pause 1 second]
Say "he came." (Jāʾa — the weak ي of its root has dissolved.)
