German, chapter 67: To lose, To believe, To need, To live, To call.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
verlieren — to lose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bring, then the German for to forget.

You'll want to know: verlieren.
verlieren — "to lose".
