## You'll want to know: こおる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C212-TEMP212-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こおる** — *kōru* — "to freeze".

> **あしたの あさ いけが こおります。** — *ashita no asa ike ga kōrimasu* — "The pond will freeze tomorrow morning."

Polite **こおります**, *kōrimasu*; a godan verb. The noun **こおり**, *kōri*, means "ice".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こおる** (*kōru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
