Spanish, chapter 749: Más parientes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el gemelo — the twin; the calf (muscle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the stepmother, then the Spanish for the stepfather.

You'll want to know: el gemelo.
el gemelo — "the twin; the calf (muscle)".
Mi hermana tiene dos gemelos. My sister has twins.
Los gemelos are also the calf muscles and cufflinks. Non-identical twins are mellizos.

The word, taken apart.
gemelo is a learned borrowing from Latin gemellus "twin" (EtymDB), a small form of geminus "twin".

You'll want to know: el bisabuelo.

Mi bisabuelo vivió cien años. My great-grandfather lived to a hundred.

The word, taken apart.
bisabuelo is built on Latin bis "twice" and abuelo "grandfather", which is usually traced to Latin aviolus, from avus "grandfather".
