Marathi, chapter 395: Useful or Not.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
उपयुक्त (upyukta) — useful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for good judgement, then the Marathi for to dawn on.

You'll want to know: उपयुक्त.
उपयुक्त — upyukta — "useful".
हे पुस्तक परीक्षेसाठी खूप उपयुक्त आहे. he pustak parīkṣesāṭhī khūp upyukta āhe. This book is very useful for the exam.
Invariable; X साठी उपयुक्त (upyukta), useful for X.

The word, taken apart.
उपयुक्त (upyukta) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit upayukta, "fit, suitable, useful" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 7.
थेट (theṭ) — direct; straight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for useful, then the Marathi for tricky.

You'll want to know: थेट.
थेट — theṭ — "direct; straight".
ही बस थेट पुण्याला जाते. hī bas theṭ puṇyālā jāte. This bus goes straight to Pune.
Also an adverb: थेट (theṭ) सांगणे, to say straight out.

The word, taken apart.
थेट (theṭ) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled. CDIAL lists a Marathi theṭ, "genuine", under Sanskrit sthēṣṭha, "most fixed, durable" (CDIAL 13778); whether the sense direct comes from it is not clear.
