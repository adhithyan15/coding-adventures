## You'll want to know: la multinacional
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C592-EMPRESA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la multinacional** — "the multinational company".

> Trabaja en una multinacional.
>
> *She works for a multinational.*

Also an adjective: *una empresa multinacional*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*multinacional* is built on *nación*, a learned borrowing from Latin *natio* 'birth, a people' (EtymDB), from *nasci* 'to be born', with Latin *multi-* 'many'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
