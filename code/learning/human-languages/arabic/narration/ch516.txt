Arabic, chapter 516: Stretching and shaking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تمدد (tamaddada) — to stretch out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to get up, then the Arabic for fainting.

You'll want to know: تمدد.
تمدد — tamaddada — "to stretch out".
تمدد القط في الشمس. tamaddada al-qiṭṭ fī ash-shams. The cat stretched out in the sun.
Present: يتمدد, yatamaddad.

The word, taken apart.
تمدد (tamaddada) is built on the native Arabic root m-d-d, "to stretch, to extend" (Lane). Mudda, a period, which you know, is from the same root: a stretch of time.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
