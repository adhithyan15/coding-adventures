Sanskrit, chapter 430: At the Bazaar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हट्टः (haṭṭaḥ) — a bazaar; a market fair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a door bolt, then the Sanskrit for rent.

You'll want to know: हट्टः.
हट्टः — haṭṭaḥ — "a bazaar; a market fair".
सोमवासरे ग्रामे हट्टः भवति। somavāsare grāme haṭṭaḥ bhavati. On Monday there is a bazaar in the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
हट्टः (haṭṭaḥ) is the word MW gives for a market, a fair; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Hindi hāṭ, "a market" (MW; CDIAL 13944).

Lesson 2 of 5.
विक्रेता (vikretā) — a seller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rent, then the Sanskrit for a bazaar.

You'll want to know: विक्रेता.
विक्रेता — vikretā — "a seller".
विक्रेता उच्चैः वदति। vikretā uccaiḥ vadati. The seller calls out loudly.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विक्रेता (vikretā) is built from vi and the root √krī, "to buy", which with vi means to sell, as in vikrīṇāti, "sells", with the agent ending -tṛ; MW gives vikretṛ as a seller (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
क्रेता (kretā) — a buyer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bazaar, then the Sanskrit for a seller.

You'll want to know: क्रेता.
क्रेता — kretā — "a buyer".
क्रेता हट्टे मूल्यं पृच्छति। kretā haṭṭe mūlyaṃ pṛcchati. The buyer asks the price at the bazaar.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
क्रेता (kretā) is built on the root √krī, "to buy", which also gives krīṇāti, "buys", with the agent ending -tṛ; MW gives kretṛ as a buyer, a purchaser (MW).

The word, taken apart.
ग्राहकः (grāhakaḥ) is built on the root √grah, "to take", which also gives gṛhṇāti, "takes", with the agent ending -aka; MW gives grāhaka as one who takes and as a purchaser (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पणते (paṇate) — haggles; bargains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a buyer, then the Sanskrit for a customer.

You'll want to know: पणते.
पणते — paṇate — "haggles; bargains".
ग्राहकः हट्टे पणते। grāhakaḥ haṭṭe paṇate. The customer haggles at the bazaar.
Past (he …): सः हट्टे पणितवान्, saḥ haṭṭe paṇitavān.

The word, taken apart.
पणते (paṇate) comes from the root √paṇ, "to barter, to bargain", which also gives paṇa, "a stake, a coin"; MW gives the participle paṇita, "transacted as business" (MW).
