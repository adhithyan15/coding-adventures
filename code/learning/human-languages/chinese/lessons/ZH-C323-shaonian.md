## You'll want to know: 少年
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C323-ERA323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**少年** — *shàonián* — "teenage boy; teenager".

> **他十五岁的时候是一个快乐的少年** *tā shíwǔ suì de shíhou shì yí ge kuàilè de shàonián* — At fifteen he was a happy teenager.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**少年** (*shàonián*) is built from 少 'young' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syewH* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, in this falling tone, 'young'; in its rising tone, *syewX*, it is 'few'.
