## You'll want to know: der Sarg
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C303-BEERDIGUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sarg** — "the coffin".

> Der Sarg war aus hellem Holz.
>
> *The coffin was made of light wood.*

Plural: *die Särge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sarg* is inherited from Old High German *saruh* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Late Latin *sarcophagus* "a stone coffin".
