## You'll want to know: उपचार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C488-RUGNA488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उपचार** — *upcār* — "treatment".

> योग्य उपचारांनंतर तो बरा झाला.
>
> *yogya upcārānnantar to barā jhālā.*
>
> After proper treatment he got well.

Masculine.

Often plural: उपचार करणे, to treat; उपचार घेणे, to have treatment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपचार** (*upcār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *upacāra*, "approach, service" (CDIAL 2163).
