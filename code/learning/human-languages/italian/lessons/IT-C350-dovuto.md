## You'll want to know: dovuto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C350-SICCOME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dovuto** — "due (to), caused (by); owed".

> Il ritardo è dovuto al traffico.
>
> *The delay is due to the traffic.*

*Essere dovuto a* — to be due to. It is also the past participle of *dovere*: *ho dovuto aspettare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dovuto* is the past participle of *dovere*, inherited from Latin *dēbēre* "to owe, to be bound to" (Lewis & Short): what is owed to a cause.
