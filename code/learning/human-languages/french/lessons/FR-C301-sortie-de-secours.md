## You'll want to know: la sortie de secours
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C301-ALERTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la sortie de secours** — "the emergency exit".

> Les gens sont sortis par la sortie de secours.
>
> *People went out through the emergency exit.*

Signs also say *issue de secours*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sortie* is built on *sortir* "to go out", which is usually traced to Latin *sortīrī* "to draw lots" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB lists no parent); *secours* is inherited from Latin *succursus* "help".
