## You'll want to know: नणद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C259-SASU259-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नणद** — *naṇad* — "a husband's sister".

> म्हारी नणद सहर मांय रैवै है।
>
> *mhārī naṇad sahar mā̃y raivai hai.*
>
> My husband's sister lives in the city.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नणद** (*naṇad*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nanāndṛ*, "a husband's sister"; CDIAL lists Marwari *naṇad* under it (CDIAL 6946).
