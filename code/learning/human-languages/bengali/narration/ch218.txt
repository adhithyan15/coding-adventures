Bengali, chapter 218: Although, Such as, So that, Then, That is.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
যদিও (jôdio) — although.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a room (in a hotel), then the Bengali for a stadium.

You'll want to know: যদিও.
যদিও — jôdio — "although".

The word, taken apart.
যদিও (jôdio) is jôdi, "if", Sanskrit yadi (CDIAL 10410), + -o, "even": "even if".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: তাহলে.
তাহলে — tāhôle — "then, in that case".

The word, taken apart.
তাহলে (tāhôle) is tā, "that", listed under the Sanskrit pronoun base ta- (CDIAL 5612), + hôle, "if it is", from hôoyā, "to be", from Sanskrit bhavati (CDIAL 9416): "if that is so".

Lesson 5 of 5.
মানে (māne) — that is, it means.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for so that, then the Bengali for then.

You'll want to know: মানে.
মানে — māne — "that is, it means".

The word, taken apart.
মানে (māne) is a loan from Arabic maʿnā, "meaning, sense", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is ʿ-n-y (Lane). Bengali adds the ending -e.
