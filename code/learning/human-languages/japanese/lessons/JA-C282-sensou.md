## You'll want to know: せんそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C282-SEKAI282-02]; assesses=[] -->

**せんそう** — *sensō* — "a war".

> **その くには せんそうを しています。** — *sono kuni wa sensō o shite imasu* — "That country is at war."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんそう** (*sensō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "war" (Middle Chinese *tsyenH*) and *sō*, "contend" (*tsreang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
