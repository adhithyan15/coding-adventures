## You'll want to know: प्रतोदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C407-YANTA407-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतोदः** — *pratodaḥ* — "a goad; a long whip".

> कृषकः प्रतोदेन वृषभं नुदति।
>
> *kṛṣakaḥ pratodena vṛṣabhaṃ nudati.*
>
> The farmer drives the bull on with a goad.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतोदः** (*pratodaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "forth", and the root √tud, "to strike, to push"; MW gives *pratoda* as a goad or long whip (MW).
