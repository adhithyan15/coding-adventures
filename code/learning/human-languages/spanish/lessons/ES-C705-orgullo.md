## You'll want to know: el orgullo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C705-ORGULLO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el orgullo** — "pride".

> Mis hijos son mi orgullo.
>
> *My children are my pride.*

It can be good (pride in your work) or bad (too proud to say sorry).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orgullo* was borrowed from Catalan *orgull*, which comes from Frankish *urgōli* "pride", a Germanic word (EtymDB). French *orgueil* is the same word.
