Gujarati, chapter 173: A week and a fortnight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અઠવાડિયું (aṭhvāḍiyũ) — week.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for orange, then the Gujarati for juice.

You'll want to know: અઠવાડિયું.
અઠવાડિયું — aṭhvāḍiyũ — "week".
હું એક અઠવાડિયું ગામમાં રહીશ. — hũ ek aṭhvāḍiyũ gāmmā̃ rahīś — "I will stay in the village for a week."

The word, taken apart.
અઠવાડિયું (aṭhvāḍiyũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from aṣṭavāra, "period of eight days", a reconstructed form built from Sanskrit aṣṭā, "eight", and vāra, "day"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati aṭhvāḍiyũ and takes it to be crossed with compounds of vāṭa, "enclosure" (CDIAL 940).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for juice, then the Gujarati for week.

You'll want to know: પખવાડિયું.
પખવાડિયું — pakhvāḍiyũ — "fortnight".
તે એક પખવાડિયું રજા લેશે. — te ek pakhvāḍiyũ rajā leśe — "She will take a fortnight off."

The word, taken apart.
પખવાડિયું (pakhvāḍiyũ) is built like aṭhvāḍiyũ, "week", on pakh, "side, half". CDIAL lists Gujarati pākh, "side", under Sanskrit pakṣa, "wing, side", but not this compound, so its path is not settled (CDIAL 7627).

Lesson 3 of 5.
આવતું (āvtũ) — next, coming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for week, then the Gujarati for fortnight.

You'll want to know: આવતું.
આવતું — āvtũ — "next, coming".
આવતા અઠવાડિયે હું આવીશ. — āvtā aṭhvāḍiye hũ āvīś — "I will come next week."
Built on આવવું, āvvũ, to come.

The word, taken apart.
આવતું (āvtũ) is built on the inherited āvvũ, "to come", with the participle ending -tũ, so its first sense is "coming".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

હું આખો દિવસ કામ કરીશ. — hũ ākho divas kām karīś — "I will work the whole day."

The word, taken apart.
આખું (ākhũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit akṣata, "unhurt, not crushed", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "whole" (CDIAL 28).
