Sanskrit, chapter 468: Gratitude and Greed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लोलुपः (lolupaḥ) — greedy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for flattery, then the Sanskrit for affable.

You'll want to know: लोलुपः.
लोलुपः — lolupaḥ — "greedy".
लोलुपः बालः मोदकान् खादति, मित्राय न ददाति। lolupaḥ bālaḥ modakān khādati, mitrāya na dadāti. The greedy boy eats the sweets and gives his friend none.

The word, taken apart.
लोलुपः (lolupaḥ) MW builds it on the intensive of the root √lup, "to break", and for the sense "covetous, ardently longing" thinks it probably a corruption of lolubha, from √lubh, "to covet"; its history is not settled (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अपकारः (apakāraḥ) — a wrong; an injury.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for greedy, then the Sanskrit for a favour in return.

You'll want to know: अपकारः.
अपकारः — apakāraḥ — "a wrong; an injury".
सज्जनः अपकारं क्षमते। sajjanaḥ apakāraṃ kṣamate. A good person forgives a wrong.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अपकारः (apakāraḥ) is built from apa, "away, off", and kāra, "doing", from the root √kṛ of करोति, the opposite of उपकारः; MW gives "wrong, offence, injury, hurt" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a favour in return, then the Sanskrit for a wrong done.

You'll want to know: स्मारयति.
स्मारयति — smārayati — "reminds".
माता मां प्रतिदिनम् औषधं स्मारयति। mātā māṃ pratidinam auṣadhaṃ smārayati. Mother reminds me about my medicine every day.
Past (she …): सा मां स्मारितवती, sā māṃ smāritavatī.

The word, taken apart.
स्मारयति (smārayati) is built on the causative of the root √smṛ, "to remember", the root of स्मरति: to make someone remember; MW's ppp smārita is "reminded" (MW).
