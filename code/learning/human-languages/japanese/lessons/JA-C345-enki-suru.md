## You'll want to know: えんきする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C345-HENKOU345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**えんきする** — *enki suru* — "to postpone".

> **たいふうが きたら、しあいを えんきします。** — *taifū ga kitara, shiai o enki shimasu* — "If the typhoon comes, we will postpone the match."

Polite **えんきします**, *enkishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **がっこうが しあいを えんきしました。**, *gakkō ga shiai o enki shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんきする** (*enki suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *enki*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *en*, "prolong" (Middle Chinese *yen*) and *ki*, "period" (*gi*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
