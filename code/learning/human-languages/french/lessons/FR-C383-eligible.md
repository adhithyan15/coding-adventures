## You'll want to know: éligible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C383-CRITERE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**éligible** — "eligible".

> À dix-huit ans, on est éligible au conseil municipal.
>
> *At eighteen, you are eligible for the town council.*

One form for both genders. *Éligible à une aide* — eligible for aid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*éligible* is usually traced to Late Latin *ēligibilis* "that may be chosen", from *ēligere* "to pick out", built on *legere* "to choose, to gather" (Lewis & Short).
