## You'll want to know: રિઝર્વેશન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C395-BERTH395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**રિઝર્વેશન** — *rijharveśan* — "a reservation (of a seat)".

> અમે એક મહિના પહેલાં રિઝર્વેશન કર્યું હતું.
>
> *ame ek mahinā pahelā̃ rijharveśan karyũ hatũ.*
>
> We had made the reservation a month before.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રિઝર્વેશન** (*rijharveśan*) is borrowed from English *reservation*, a seat or berth kept in your name.
