## You'll want to know: слушаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C497-UPBRINGING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**слушаться** (*slúshatsya*) — "to obey".

> Дети не всегда слушаются родителей.
>
> *Déti ne vsegdá slúshayutsya rodíteley.*
>
> Children don't always do what their parents say.

Imperfective; perfective послушаться. Takes the genitive: *слушаться родителей*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*слушаться* is the reflexive of *слушать* 'to listen': to obey is to listen to someone. EtymDB has no entry for the reflexive.
