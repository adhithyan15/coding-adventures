## You'll want to know: la cafetera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C775-NEVERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la cafetera** — "the coffee maker, the coffee pot".

> Por la mañana la cafetera hace mucho ruido.
>
> *In the morning the coffee maker makes a lot of noise.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cafetera* comes from French *cafetière* 'coffee pot' (EtymDB), built on *café*, which reached Europe through Ottoman Turkish *kahve* from Arabic *qahwa*.
