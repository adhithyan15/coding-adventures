## You'll want to know: el bordillo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C682-CALLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el bordillo** — "the kerb, the curb".

> El autobús para junto al bordillo.
>
> *The bus stops next to the kerb.*

A Spain word; elsewhere *el borde de la acera* or *el cordón*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bordillo* is built on *borde* "edge", which comes from French *bord* (EtymDB), a word of Germanic origin.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
