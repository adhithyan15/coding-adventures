# ひきょう (hikyō) — unfair; foul

## You'll want to know: ひきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C439-BRAVE439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきょう** — *hikyō* — "unfair; foul".

> **うそは ひきょうです。** — *uso wa hikyō desu* — "Lying is unfair."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきょう** (*hikyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hi*, "lowly" (Middle Chinese *pjie*) and *kyō*, "cowardice" (*khjaep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
