## You'll want to know: मंच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C529-NATAK529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मंच** — *mañc* — "a stage; a platform".

> गायक मंच पर खड़ा होकर गा रहा था।
>
> *Gāyak mañc par khaṛā hokar gā rahā thā.*
>
> The singer was standing on the stage and singing.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंच** (*mañc*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *mañca*, "a stage, a platform, a raised seat" (Monier-Williams).
