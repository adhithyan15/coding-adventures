Bengali, chapter 230: Leader, Minister, Prince, Society, Religion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নেতা (netā) — a leader.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a ribbon, then the Bengali for the weather.

You'll want to know: নেতা.
নেতা — netā — "a leader".

The word, taken apart.
নেতা (netā) is Sanskrit netā, "leader", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. It is the naming form of netṛ.
