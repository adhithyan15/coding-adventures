## You'll want to know: ख़िलाफ़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C347-PAKSH347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़िलाफ़** — *khilāf* — "against (के ख़िलाफ़)".

> गाँव के लोग नई सड़क के ख़िलाफ़ हैं।
>
> *gā̃v ke log naī saṛak ke khilāf haiṁ.*
>
> The villagers are against the new road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़िलाफ़** (*khilāf*) is usually traced to Arabic *khilāf*, "opposition, the contrary", which reached Hindi through Persian.
