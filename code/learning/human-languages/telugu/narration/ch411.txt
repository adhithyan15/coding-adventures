Telugu, chapter 411: Counting People.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
జనగణన (janagaṇana) — a census.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an activist, then the Telugu for a donor.

You'll want to know: జనగణన.
జనగణన — janagaṇana — "a census".
పది సంవత్సరాలకు ఒకసారి జనగణన జరుగుతుంది. padi saṁvatsarālaku okasāri janagaṇana jarugutundi. A census takes place once every ten years.
జన, people, + గణన, counting.

The word, taken apart.
జనగణన (janagaṇana) is built from Sanskrit jana, "a person, people" (CDIAL 5098), and gaṇana, "counting" (CDIAL 3992).

Lesson 3 of 5.
గణాంకాలు (gaṇāṅkālu) — statistics; figures.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a census, then the Telugu for a survey.

You'll want to know: గణాంకాలు.
గణాంకాలు — gaṇāṅkālu — "statistics; figures".
ఆ గణాంకాలు సరైనవి కావు. ā gaṇāṅkālu sarainavi kāvu. Those figures are not correct.
A plural noun: గణ, a number, + అంకాలు, marks, numerals.

The word, taken apart.
గణాంకాలు (gaṇāṅkālu) is built from Sanskrit gaṇa, "a troop, a number" (CDIAL 3988), and aṅka, "a mark, a sign" (CDIAL 100); Telugu uses the plural for statistics.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ప్రజాభిప్రాయం.
ప్రజాభిప్రాయం — prajābhiprāyaṁ — "public opinion".
ప్రజాభిప్రాయం ఆ ప్రతిపాదనకు వ్యతిరేకంగా ఉంది. prajābhiprāyaṁ ā pratipādanaku vyatirēkaṅgā undi. Public opinion is against that proposal.
ప్రజా, of the people, + అభిప్రాయం, an opinion, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రజాభిప్రాయం (prajābhiprāyaṁ) is built from Sanskrit prajā, "offspring, people", and abhiprāya, "aim, intention" (Monier-Williams), the word Telugu has as అభిప్రాయం.

Lesson 5 of 5.
శాతం (śātaṁ) — a percentage; per cent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for statistics, then the Telugu for public opinion.

You'll want to know: శాతం.
శాతం — śātaṁ — "a percentage; per cent".
మా తరగతిలో తొంభై శాతం మంది పాసయ్యారు. mā taragatilō tombhai śātaṁ mandi pāsayyāru. Ninety per cent of our class passed.
With numbers spelt out: యాభై శాతం (śātaṁ), fifty per cent.

The word, taken apart.
శాతం (śātaṁ) goes back to Sanskrit śata, "a hundred" (CDIAL 12278): so many out of a hundred.
