Sanskrit, chapter 463: Courage and Fear.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
भीषयते (bhīṣayate) — frightens.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for trustworthy, then the Sanskrit for a betrayal of trust.

You'll want to know: भीषयते.
भीषयते — bhīṣayate — "frightens".
सिंहः गर्जनेन मृगान् भीषयते। siṃhaḥ garjanena mṛgān bhīṣayate. The lion frightens the deer with its roar.
Past (it …): सिंहः मृगान् भीषितवान्, siṃhaḥ mṛgān bhīṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
भीषयते (bhīṣayate) is built on the causative of the root √bhī, "to fear", the root of बिभेति and भयम्: to make afraid; MW records this causative bhīṣayate, citing Pāṇini 1.3.68 (MW).

त्रस्यति — trasyati — "trembles with fear".
मूषकः मार्जारात् त्रस्यति। mūṣakaḥ mārjārāt trasyati. The mouse trembles in fear of the cat.
Past (it …): मूषकः मार्जारात् त्रस्तवान्, mūṣakaḥ mārjārāt trastavān.

The word, taken apart.
त्रस्यति (trasyati) comes from the root √tras, "to tremble, to be afraid of", which takes the ablative of what is feared (MW).
