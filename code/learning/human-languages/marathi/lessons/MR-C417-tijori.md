## You'll want to know: तिजोरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C417-ANAMAT417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तिजोरी** — *tijorī* — "a strongbox; a safe".

> दुकानदार पैसे तिजोरीत ठेवतो.
>
> *dukāndār paise tijorīt ṭhevto.*
>
> The shopkeeper keeps the money in the safe.

Feminine.

Also the treasury of a town or a state: सरकारी तिजोरी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिजोरी** (*tijorī*) is a Marathi word for a steel box with a lock, and for a treasury; where it comes from is not settled.
