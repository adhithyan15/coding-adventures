## You'll want to know: جھڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C428-BOOND428-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جھڑی** — *jhaṛī* — "a spell of rain; a steady shower".

> دو دن سے بارش کی جھڑی ہے۔
>
> *do din se bārish kī jhaṛī hai.*
>
> It has been raining steadily for two days.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھڑی** (*jhaṛī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhaṛī*, "showers", under *jhaḍī*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5329).
