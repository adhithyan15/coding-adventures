## You'll want to know: বউমা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C483-JAMAI483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বউমা** — *boumā* — "a daughter-in-law".

> শাশুড়ি বউমাকে একটা শাড়ি দিলেন।
>
> *shāshuṛi boumāke ekṭā shāṛi dilen.*
>
> The mother-in-law gave her daughter-in-law a sari.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বউমা** (*boumā*) puts *bou*, "wife, bride", from Sanskrit *vadhū* (CDIAL 11250), with মা, mother: the bride who becomes a daughter of the house.
