## You'll want to know: വായ്പ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C458-VAYPA458-04]; assesses=[] -->

**വായ്പ** — *vāypa* — "a loan".

> വീട് പണിയാൻ അവർ ബാങ്കിൽ നിന്ന് വായ്പ എടുത്തു.
>
> *vīṭŭ paṇiyān avar bāṅkil ninnŭ vāypa eṭuttu.*
>
> They took a loan from the bank to build a house.

A bank loan is വായ്പ; a private debt is usually കടം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വായ്പ** (*vāypa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5355 lists Malayalam *vāypa*, "a loan", with Tamil *vāyppai*, "a debt".
