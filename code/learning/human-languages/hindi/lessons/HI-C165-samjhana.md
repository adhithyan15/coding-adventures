## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समझाना** (*samjhānā*) is the causing form of *samajhnā*, "to understand": to explain is to make someone understand. CDIAL lists the verb under Sanskrit *saṁbudhyate*, "wakes up, understands" (CDIAL 12959).
