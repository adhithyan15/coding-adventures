Italian, chapter 139: Opening Hours, a Notice, the Rent, a Receipt, an Appointment.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
l'orario — opening hours, a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to foresee, then the Italian for to organise.

You'll want to know: l'orario.
l'orario — "opening hours, a timetable".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the door]
The door says Orario: lunedì – venerdì, 9 – 18. Open on weekdays from nine to six: not on sabato.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: l'orario]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: l'orario, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say l'orario]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Italian for to foresee, then the Italian for to organise, then say l'orario again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does l'orario mean? ("Opening hours, a timetable".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
