## You'll want to know: ほえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C269-DOROBOU269-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ほえる** — *hoeru* — "to bark; to howl".

> **もし どろぼうが きたら、いぬが ほえるでしょう。** — *moshi dorobō ga kitara, inu ga hoeru deshō* — "If a burglar came, the dog would bark."

Polite **ほえます**, *hoemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **いぬが ほえました。**, *inu ga hoemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほえる** (*hoeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
