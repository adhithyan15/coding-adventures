## You'll want to know: பதற்றம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C89-FEEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**பதற்றம்** (*pataṟṟam*) — "nervousness, tension, a flutter".

**பதற்றமாக இருக்கிறது** — "I feel nervous".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The opposite of calm.
