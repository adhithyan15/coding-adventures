## You'll want to know: たまに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C169-OFTEN169-02]; assesses=[] -->

**たまに** — *tamani* — "once in a while; occasionally".

> **らいげつは たまに ともだちと あいます。** — *raigetsu wa tamani tomodachi to aimasu* — "Next month I will meet friends once in a while."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たまに** (*tamani*) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana (JMdict).
