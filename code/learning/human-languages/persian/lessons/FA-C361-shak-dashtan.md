## You'll want to know: شک داشتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C361-MOVAFEQAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شک داشتن** (*shak dâshtan*) — "to doubt, to be unsure".

> من در درستی این خبر شک دارم.
>
> *man dar dorosti-ye in khabar shak dâram.*
>
> I doubt that this news is true.

*Shak* "doubt" + *dâshtan* (stem *dâr-*). Takes *dar* or *be*: *dar chizi shak dâshtan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شک** was borrowed from Arabic *shakk* "doubt" (EtymDB); *dâshtan* "to have" is the inherited Persian verb, so the phrase is literally to have a doubt.
