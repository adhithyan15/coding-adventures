## You'll want to know: la bocacalle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C647-CRUZAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la bocacalle** — "the turning, the side-street opening".

> Gire en la segunda bocacalle a la derecha.
>
> *Take the second turning on the right.*

Built from *boca* and *calle*: the mouth of a street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bocacalle* is built from *boca* "mouth", inherited from Latin *bucca* "cheek", and *calle* "street", from Latin *callis* "footpath".
