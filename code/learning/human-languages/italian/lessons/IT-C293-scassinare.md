## You'll want to know: scassinare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C293-SCASSO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**scassinare** — "to force open, to break into".

> Qualcuno ha scassinato la finestra.
>
> *Someone forced the window.*

For locks, doors, safes and cars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scassinare* is built on *scassare* "to break open", from *cassa* "box" and Latin *capsa* "a case" (Lewis & Short).
