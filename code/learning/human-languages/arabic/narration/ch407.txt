Arabic, chapter 407: Avoiding trouble.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعذر (taʿadhdhara) — to prove impossible.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to lack, then the Arabic for a procedure.

You'll want to know: تعذر.
تعذر — taʿadhdhara — "to prove impossible".
تعذر علينا السفر بسبب الطقس. taʿadhdhara ʿalaynā as-safar bi-sabab aṭ-ṭaqs. Travelling proved impossible for us because of the weather.
Present: يتعذر, yataʿadhdhar.
It takes على for the person: تعذر (taʿadhdhara) عليه ….

The word, taken apart.
تعذر (taʿadhdhara) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-dh-r, "to excuse" (Lane). It is Form V; Lane gives it for an affair that became hard to bring about, and so impracticable: to prove impossible.

تجنب (tajannaba) is built on the native Arabic root j-n-b, "to put aside, to turn away" (Lane). It is Form V, to put oneself to one side of a thing: to avoid it. بجانب, beside, shares the root.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: تفادى.
تفادى — tafādā — "to avert".
تفادى الطفل الكرة. tafādā aṭ-ṭifl al-kura. The child got out of the way of the ball.
Present: يتفادى, yatafādā.

The word, taken apart.
تفادى (tafādā) is built on the native Arabic root f-d-y, "to ransom" (Lane). It is Form VI. How its sense, keeping clear of a thing, grew from ransoming is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: طرأ.
طرأ — ṭaraʾa — "to arise unexpectedly".
طرأ تغيير على الخطة. ṭaraʾa taghyīr ʿalā al-khiṭṭa. A change came up in the plan.
Present: يطرأ, yaṭraʾ.
It takes على: طرأ (ṭaraʾa) على ….

The word, taken apart.
طرأ (ṭaraʾa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-r-ʾ, "to come upon unexpectedly" (Lane). Ṭaraʾa is a Form I verb: to come from far off without warning, to arise.
