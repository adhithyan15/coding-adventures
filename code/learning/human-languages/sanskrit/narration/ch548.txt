Lesson 1 of 6.
विश्वासयति (viśvāsayati) — inspires confidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a climb, then the Sanskrit for springs up.

You'll want to know: विश्वासयति.
विश्वासयति — viśvāsayati — "inspires confidence".
यदि बालः भीतः अस्ति, तर्हि माता तं विश्वासयति। yadi bālaḥ bhītaḥ asti, tarhi mātā taṃ viśvāsayati. If the child is afraid, my mother gives him confidence.
Past (she …): सा बालं विश्वासितवती, sā bālaṃ viśvāsitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
विश्वासयति (viśvāsayati) is built on vi and the causative of the root √śvas, "to breathe", the root of viśvasiti, "trusts"; MW gives viśvāsayati as to cause to trust, to inspire with confidence, to console (MW).

सान्त्वना — sāntvanā — "consolation".
यदि भ्राता दुःखी अस्ति, तर्हि तस्मै सान्त्वनां ददातु। yadi bhrātā duḥkhī asti, tarhi tasmai sāntvanāṃ dadātu. If your brother is unhappy, offer him comfort.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सान्त्वना (sāntvanā) comes from the root √sāntv, "to console, to conciliate"; MW gives sāntvana, beside sāntvanā, as soothing with kind words, consolation (MW).

आकुलः — ākulaḥ — "flustered; agitated".
यदि भवान् आकुलः अस्ति, तर्हि माम् आह्वयतु। yadi bhavān ākulaḥ asti, tarhi mām āhvayatu. If you are flustered, please call me.

The word, taken apart.
आकुलः (ākulaḥ) is a word MW traces, with a query, to ā and the root √kṝ, "to scatter"; MW gives ākula as confounded, confused, agitated, flurried, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for consolation, then the Sanskrit for flustered.

You'll want to know: शरणम्.
शरणम् — śaraṇam — "a refuge; shelter".
यदि वात्या आगच्छति, तर्हि वयं शरणं गच्छामः। yadi vātyā āgacchati, tarhi vayaṃ śaraṇaṃ gacchāmaḥ. If a storm comes, we go to shelter.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शरणम् (śaraṇam) comes from the root √śri, "to lean on, to resort to", as MW derives it (through a form √śṛ); MW gives śaraṇa as protecting, and a shelter, a place of refuge or rest, a house (MW).
