## You'll want to know: dextrōrsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C454-DEXTRORSUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**dextrōrsum** — "to the right, towards the right".

> Post pontem via dextrōrsum it.
>
> *After the bridge the road goes to the right.*

An adverb, also *dextrōrsus*, from *dexter* 'right' and *vorsum* 'turned'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dextrōrsum* is a shortened *dextrō-vorsum*, built from *dexter* 'right' and *vorsum*, an old form of *versum* 'turned', from *vertō* 'to turn' (Lewis & Short).
