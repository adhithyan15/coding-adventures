# A four-line plan

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SHAID-223, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *perhaps Aman will come tomorrow*. Then *next week we will go to
the market*.

## Grammar Lens: sure, and perhaps
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-PLAN-223, PA-LEX-SHAID-223, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-3SG-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-NEG-QUESTION-222, PA-FORM-AGLI-184] -->

Four lines, each with its time first. **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ** marks the one that is not sure:

> **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ।** — *kallh maiṁ bāzār jāvāṅgī.* — Tomorrow I will go to the market.

> **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੇਗਾ।** — *Aman kallh kamm karegā.* — Aman will work tomorrow.

> **ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।** — *shāid asīṁ agle hafte jāvāṅge.* — Perhaps we will go next week.

> **ਅਗਲੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ।** — *aglī yātrā rel nāl hovegī.* — The next journey will be by train.
