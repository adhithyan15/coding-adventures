# મધ્યસ્થી (madhyasthī) — a mediator

## You'll want to know: મધ્યસ્થી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C542-SAMADHAN542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મધ્યસ્થી** — *madhyasthī* — "a mediator".

> જો કોઈ મધ્યસ્થી મદદ કરશે તો બંને ભાઈ ફરી મળશે.
>
> *jo koī madhyasthī madad karśe to banne bhāī pharī maḷśe.*
>
> If a mediator helps, the two brothers will meet again.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મધ્યસ્થી** (*madhyasthī*) is built on the tatsama Sanskrit *madhyastha*, "standing in the middle" (Monier-Williams): the one who stands between two sides.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
