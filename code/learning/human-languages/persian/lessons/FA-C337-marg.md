## You'll want to know: مرگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C337-MARG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مرگ** (*marg*) — "death".

> بعد از مرگ پدرش به شهر برگشت.
>
> *Ba'd az marg-e pedarash be shahr bargasht.*
>
> After his father's death he went back to the city.

Plain and strong; in polite speech people say *dargozasht* or *fowt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marg* is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian *marg*, "death"; EtymDB lists it without a parent.
