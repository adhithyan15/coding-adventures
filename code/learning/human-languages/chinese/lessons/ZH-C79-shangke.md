## You'll want to know: 上课
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C79-SCHOOL79-01]; assesses=[] -->

**上课** — *shàngkè* — "to have class; to start class".

> **今天有汉语课我们一会儿上课** *jīntiān yǒu hànyǔ kè wǒmen yíhuìr shàngkè* — There's a Chinese class today; it starts in a moment.

The new character, in pieces: 课 — 讠 on the left, and on the right guǒ, the fruit of shuǐguǒ: a box over the tree piece. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上课** (*shàngkè*) is built from 上 'to go up to' and 课 'lesson', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *khwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second as 'a tax' and 'a test'; the sense lesson is not given there.
