## You'll want to know: मरणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C246-PIHAR246-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मरणो** — *marṇo* — "to die".

> म्हारी गाय काल मरी।
>
> *mhārī gāy kāl marī.*
>
> My cow died yesterday.

Past (it …): वो बळद मर्यो, *vo baḷad maryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मरणो** (*marṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *marate*, "dies"; CDIAL lists Marwari *marṇo* under it (CDIAL 9871).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
