## You'll want to know: сделка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C361-PRIBYL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сделка** (*sdélka*) — "a deal, a transaction".

> Компании заключили сделку.
>
> *Kompánii zaklyuchíli sdélku.*
>
> The companies struck a deal.

Feminine. *Заключить сделку* — to strike a deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**сделка** is built on *сделать* "to do, to make", from *делать*, inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*dělati*: a deal is a thing done between two sides.
