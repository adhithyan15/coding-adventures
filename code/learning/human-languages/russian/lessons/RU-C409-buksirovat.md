## You'll want to know: буксировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C409-POLOMKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**буксировать** (*buksírovat*) — "to tow".

> Сосед буксировал нашу машину до дома.
>
> *Soséd buksíroval náshu mashínu do dóma.*
>
> The neighbour towed our car home.

Imperfective; perfective *отбуксировать*. Present *буксирую*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*буксировать* is built on *буксир* 'a tug, a towrope'. *Буксир* is usually traced to a Dutch sailors' word for towing; EtymDB lists it without a source, so the line is hedged.
