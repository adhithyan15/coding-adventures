## You'll want to know: واردات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C276-CHORI276-05]; assesses=[] -->

**واردات** — *vārdāt* — "a crime (as an incident); a raid".

> یہ اس گلی میں دوسری واردات ہے۔
>
> *yih is galī meṅ dūsrī vārdāt hai.*
>
> This is the second crime in this lane.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واردات** (*vārdāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wāridāt*, "things that arrive, happenings"; in Urdu the word has narrowed, it seems, to an incident of crime: **چوری کی واردات**, a theft as the police report it.
