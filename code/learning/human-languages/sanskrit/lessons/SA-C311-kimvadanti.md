## You'll want to know: किंवदन्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C311-KIMVADANTI311-01]; assesses=[] -->

**किंवदन्ती** — *kiṃvadantī* — "a rumour".

> नगरे एका किंवदन्ती प्रसरति।
>
> *nagare ekā kiṃvadantī prasarati.*
>
> A rumour is spreading in the city.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किंवदन्ती** (*kiṃvadantī*) is built from *kim*, "what", and a form of the root √vad, "to say": MW explains it as the question what do they say, common talk, rumour (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
