## You'll want to know: le collectionneur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C501-COLLECTIONS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le collectionneur** — "collector".

> Ce collectionneur possède deux mille timbres.
>
> *This collector owns two thousand stamps.*

A woman is *la collectionneuse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le collectionneur* is built on *collectionner* "to collect", from *collection*, a learned borrowing from Latin *collēctiō*, built on *legere* "to gather" (Lewis & Short).
