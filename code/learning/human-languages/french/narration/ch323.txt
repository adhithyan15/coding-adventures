French, chapter 323: Le règne.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
régner — to reign, to rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the myth, then the French for the moral.

You'll want to know: régner.
régner — "to reign, to rule".
Ce roi a régné pendant quarante ans. This king reigned for forty years.
Figurative: le silence régnait — silence reigned.

The word, taken apart.
régner comes from Old French reignier, from Latin rēgnāre "to rule" (EtymDB), built on rēgnum "kingship", from rēx "king" (Lewis & Short).

la couronne — "the crown; the wreath".
Le roi portait une couronne d'or. The king wore a gold crown.
Also a ring of flowers or of bread: une couronne de fleurs.

The word, taken apart.
couronne is inherited from Latin corōna "a wreath, a crown" (EtymDB), itself a borrowing from Greek.
