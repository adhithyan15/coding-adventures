## You'll want to know: ارث بردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C506-TIES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ارث بردن** (*ers bordan*) — "to inherit".

> او این خانه را از پدربزرگش ارث برد.
>
> *u in khâne râ az pedarbozorgash ers bord.*
>
> He inherited this house from his grandfather.

*ers* 'inheritance' + *bordan* 'to carry off'; present stem *bar-*. The giver takes *az*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ers* is a loan, usually traced to the Arabic word for 'inheritance'; EtymDB has no entry for it. *bordan*, 'to carry', is inherited Persian.
