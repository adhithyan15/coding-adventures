Spanish, chapter 674: De paseo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pasear — to go for a walk, to stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to go round, then the Spanish for to go along the edge of.

You'll want to know: pasear.
pasear — "to go for a walk, to stroll".
Por la tarde paseamos por el puerto. In the evening we stroll round the harbour.
Pasear al perro — to walk the dog.

The word, taken apart.
pasear is built on paso "step" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin passus "a step".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
recorrer — to travel around, to go all over; to cover (a distance).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the walk, then the Spanish for to wander the streets.

You'll want to know: recorrer.
recorrer — "to travel around, to go all over; to cover (a distance)".
Queremos recorrer todo el país. We want to travel all over the country.
It takes a direct object: recorrer la ciudad.

The word, taken apart.
recorrer is inherited from Latin recurrere "to run back" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built on currere "to run".
