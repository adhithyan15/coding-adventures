## You'll want to know: تردید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C393-ETMINAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تردید** (*tardid*) — "doubt, hesitation".

> بدون تردید این بهترین راه است.
>
> *bedun-e tardid in behtarin râh ast.*
>
> Without doubt this is the best way.

*Tardid dâshtan* — to be in doubt, to hesitate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تردید** is usually traced to Arabic *tardīd*, a verbal noun usually explained as going back and forth: a doubting mind wavers.
