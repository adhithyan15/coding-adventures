## You'll want to know: guàduàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C320-PHONE320-01]; assesses=[] -->

*guàduàn* — "to hang up the phone; to end a call".

> *tā hái méi shuō wán jiù guàduàn le diànhuà* — He hung up before he had finished.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guàduàn* is built from *guà* 'to hang' and *duàn* 'to cut off', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kweaH* and *dwanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to suspend' and the second 'to sever'.
