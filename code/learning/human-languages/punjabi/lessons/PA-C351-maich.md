## You'll want to know: ਮੈਚ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C351-BALL351-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੈਚ** — *maich* — "a match (a game against a side)".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਾਡਾ ਹਾਕੀ ਦਾ ਮੈਚ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ।
>
> *kall sāḍā hākī dā maich hovegā.*
>
> Our hockey match will be tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੈਚ** (*maich*) is borrowed from English *match*.
