## You'll want to know: la boussole
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C434-ORIENTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la boussole** — "the compass".

> En forêt, il garde toujours sa boussole sur lui.
>
> *In the woods he keeps his compass on him the whole time.*

*Perdre la boussole* — to lose one's head.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*boussole* was borrowed from Italian *bussola* 'compass' (EtymDB), from Late Latin *buxula* 'small box', built on *buxus* 'box tree'.
