## You'll want to know: پیتل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C501-LOHA501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پیتل** — *pītal* — "brass".

> یہ پرانا برتن پیتل کا ہے۔
>
> *yih purānā bartan pītal kā hai.*
>
> This old pot is made of brass.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیتل** (*pītal*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pittala*, "brass" (CDIAL 8184).
