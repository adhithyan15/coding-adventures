## You'll want to know: ねらう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C463-TRAIN463-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ねらう** — *nerau* — "to aim at".

> **せんしゅは ゆうしょうを ねらっています。** — *senshu wa yūshō o neratte imasu* — "The players are aiming for the championship."

Polite **ねらいます**, *neraimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かれが まとを ねらいました。**, *kare ga mato o neraimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねらう** (*nerau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
