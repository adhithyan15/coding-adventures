## You'll want to know: la mariposa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C694-ANIMALES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la mariposa** — "the butterfly".

> En primavera hay muchas mariposas.
>
> *There are a lot of butterflies in spring.*

A moth is *la polilla*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *mariposa* is not settled; it is often explained as *Mari, posa* "Mary, settle down", from a children’s song.
