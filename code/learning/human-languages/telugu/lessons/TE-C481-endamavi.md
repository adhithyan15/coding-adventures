# ఎండమావి (eṇḍamāvi) — a mirage

## You'll want to know: ఎండమావి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C481-VADAGALI481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎండమావి** — *eṇḍamāvi* — "a mirage".

> ఎడారిలో దూరంగా ఎండమావి కనిపించింది.
>
> *eḍārilō dūraṅgā eṇḍamāvi kanipiñcindi.*
>
> A mirage appeared far off in the desert.

ఎండ, sunshine, + మావి. Something hoped for that is never reached is also an ఎండమావి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎండమావి** (*eṇḍamāvi*) is a Telugu compound: ఎండ, "sunshine, the heat of the sun", native Dravidian (DEDR 869), and -మావి, whose origin is not settled.
