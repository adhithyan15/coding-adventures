German, chapter 82: Telephone, Tree, Flower, Leaf, Mountain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Telefon — a telephone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a bag; a pocket, then the German for money.

You'll want to know: das Telefon.
das Telefon — "a telephone".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does das Telefon mean? ("A telephone".) Say it once more.
