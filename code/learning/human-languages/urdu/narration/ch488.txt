Urdu, chapter 488: After the Wedding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ولیمہ (valīmā) — a wedding feast (given by the groom).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a bridegroom's veil of flowers, then the Urdu for to see off.

You'll want to know: ولیمہ.
ولیمہ — valīmā — "a wedding feast (given by the groom)".
ولیمے میں بریانی تھی۔ valīme meṅ biryānī thī. There was biryani at the wedding feast.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ولیمہ (valīmā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic walīma, "a wedding feast".

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سسرال (susrāl) — the in-laws' home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to see off, then the Urdu for a wedding feast.

You'll want to know: سسرال.
سسرال — susrāl — "the in-laws' home".
شادی کے بعد وہ سسرال گئی۔ shādī ke bād voh susrāl gaī. After the wedding she went to her in-laws' home.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سسرال (susrāl) is inherited from śvaśuraśālā, "the father-in-law's house", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 12755); سسر, "a father-in-law", is its first part.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بہو (bahū) is inherited from Sanskrit vadhū, "bride, young wife", already "son's wife" in later texts (CDIAL 11250).

Lesson 5 of 5.
بیاہنا (byāhnā) — to marry off; to wed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a daughter-in-law, then the Urdu for a son-in-law.

You'll want to know: بیاہنا.
بیاہنا — byāhnā — "to marry off; to wed".
انہوں نے بیٹی کو لاہور میں بیاہا۔ unhoṅ ne beṭī ko lāhaur meṅ byāhā. They married their daughter into a family in Lahore.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بیٹے کو بیاہا, us ne beṭe ko byāhā.

The word, taken apart.
بیاہنا (byāhnā) is inherited from Sanskrit vivāhayati, "leads away (a bride), marries" (CDIAL 11923).
