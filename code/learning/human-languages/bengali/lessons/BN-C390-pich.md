## You'll want to know: পিচ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C390-SOROK390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**পিচ** — *pich* — "tar (on a road)".

> গরমে রাস্তার পিচ নরম হয়ে গেল।
>
> *gôrôme rāstār pich nôrôm hôye gelô.*
>
> In the heat the tar on the road went soft.

পিচের রাস্তা, a tarred road; a কাঁচা রাস্তা is a dirt road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পিচ** (*pich*) is borrowed from English *pitch*, the black tar that roads are sealed with.
