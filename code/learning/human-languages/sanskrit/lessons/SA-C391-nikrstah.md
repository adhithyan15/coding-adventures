# निकृष्टः (nikṛṣṭaḥ) — inferior; low

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C390-LAKSHANA390-05, SA-LEX-C391-PARAMA391-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for experiences, then the Sanskrit for supreme.

## You'll want to know: निकृष्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C391-PARAMA391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निकृष्टः** — *nikṛṣṭaḥ* — "inferior; low".

> यदि वस्तु निकृष्टम्, तर्हि तत् न क्रीणातु।
>
> *yadi vastu nikṛṣṭam, tarhi tat na krīṇātu.*
>
> If an article is inferior, do not buy it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निकृष्टः** (*nikṛṣṭaḥ*) is built on *ni*, "down", and the root √kṛṣ, "to draw"; MW gives *ni-kṛṣṭa* "debased, vile, low, despised" (MW).
