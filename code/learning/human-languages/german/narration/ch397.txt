German, chapter 397: Der Computer stürzt ab.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
abstürzen — to crash; to fall (from a height).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to install, then the German for the program.

You'll want to know: abstürzen.
abstürzen — "to crash; to fall (from a height)".
Mein Computer ist schon wieder abgestürzt. My computer has crashed yet again.
Separable, Perfekt with sein: er stürzt ab, er ist abgestürzt. Also of planes and climbers.

The word, taken apart.
abstürzen is built from ab "down, off" and stürzen "to plunge", inherited from Middle High German stürzen, Old High German sturzen and Proto-Germanic sturtijaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 7.
aktualisieren — to update.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the program, then the German for to crash.

You'll want to know: aktualisieren.
aktualisieren — "to update".
Das Handy aktualisiert die Apps in der Nacht. The phone updates the apps during the night.
Perfekt: hat aktualisiert. From aktuell — current, up to date; a false friend, not actual.

The word, taken apart.
aktualisieren is built on aktuell "current", from Late Latin āctuālis "active, practical" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from āctus "a doing", of the family of agere "to do, to drive".

Lesson 3 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: die Fehlermeldung.
die Fehlermeldung — "the error message".
Beim Start kommt jedes Mal eine Fehlermeldung. Every time it starts, an error message comes up.
Plural: die Fehlermeldungen.

The word, taken apart.
Fehlermeldung joins Fehler "mistake", from fehlen "to be missing", to Meldung "report", from melden "to report", Old High German meldōn (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 7.
die Sicherung — the backup; the fuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the error message, then the German for to restore.

You'll want to know: die Sicherung.
die Sicherung — "the backup; the fuse".
Zum Glück hatte ich eine Sicherung von allen Dateien. Luckily I had a backup of all the files.
Plural: die Sicherungen. Also a fuse: Die Sicherung ist durchgebrannt. Many people also say das Backup.

The word, taken apart.
Sicherung is the noun of sichern "to make safe" (EtymDB), from sicher "safe", which goes back through Old High German sihhur to Latin sēcūrus "free from care".
