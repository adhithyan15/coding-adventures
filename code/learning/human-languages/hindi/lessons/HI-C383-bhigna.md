## You'll want to know: भीगना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C383-SANKAT383-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भीगना** — *bhīgnā* — "to get wet, to get soaked".

> छाता ले लो, वरना भीग जाओगे।
>
> *chātā le lo, varnā bhīg jāoge.*
>
> Take an umbrella, or you'll get soaked.

Past (he …): वह बारिश में भीग गया, *vah bāriś meṁ bhīg gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भीगना** (*bhīgnā*) is inherited from *\*bhiyagna*, "smeared, wet", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9500).
