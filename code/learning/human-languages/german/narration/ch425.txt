German, chapter 425: Auf dem Campingplatz.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Campingplatz — the campsite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the overnight stay, then the German for the half board.

You'll want to know: der Campingplatz.
der Campingplatz — "the campsite".
Der Campingplatz liegt direkt am Fluss. The campsite is right by the river.
Plural: die Campingplätze.

The word, taken apart.
Campingplatz is built from Camping, borrowed from English camping, and Platz 'place', which came through Old French place from Latin platea (EtymDB). English camp goes back to Latin campus 'field' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the caravan, then the German for the camper van.

You'll want to know: aufbauen.
aufbauen — "to put up, to set up".
Am Abend bauen sie einen Tisch und Stühle vor dem Wohnwagen auf. In the evening they set up a table and chairs in front of the caravan.
Separable: wir bauen auf, wir haben aufgebaut.

The word, taken apart.
aufbauen is built from auf 'up' and bauen 'to build', inherited from Proto-Germanic būaną (EtymDB).
