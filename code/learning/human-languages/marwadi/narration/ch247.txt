Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 247: A Wedding in the Village.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ब्याव (byāv) — a wedding; a marriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to pass (of time), then the Marwadi for to die.

You'll want to know: ब्याव.
ब्याव — byāv — "a wedding; a marriage".
आज गांव मांय ब्याव है। āj gā̃v mā̃y byāv hai. There is a wedding in the village today.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ब्याव (byāv) is inherited from Sanskrit vivāha, "marriage"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari byāha under it (CDIAL 11920).

Lesson 3 of 6.
परणणो (paraṇṇo) — to marry; to get married.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a wedding, then the Marwadi for a mark on the forehead.

You'll want to know: परणणो.
परणणो — paraṇṇo — "to marry; to get married".
म्हारो मामो काल परण्यो। mhāro māmo kāl paraṇyo. My uncle got married yesterday.
Past (he …): वो सावण मांय परण्यो, vo sāvaṇ mā̃y paraṇyo.

The word, taken apart.
परणणो (paraṇṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit pariṇayati, "leads round", and so "marries", from the rounds of the wedding; CDIAL lists Old Marwari paraṇaï, "marries", under it (CDIAL 7819).

Lesson 4 of 6.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: लाडी.
लाडी — lāḍī — "a bride".
लाडी घणी सुंदर है। lāḍī ghaṇī sundar hai. The bride is very beautiful.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
लाडी (lāḍī) is inherited from a reconstructed lāḍya, "fondling"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari lāḍalī, "fondling", and Gujarati and Sindhi lāḍī, "bride", under it; the bride is probably named as the family's darling (CDIAL 11013).

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: मांडवो.
मांडवो — mā̃ḍvo — "a wedding pavilion".
घर कनै मांडवो है। ghar kanai mā̃ḍvo hai. There is a wedding pavilion beside the house.

The word, taken apart.
मांडवो (mā̃ḍvo) is inherited from Sanskrit maṇḍapa, "an open shed, a pavilion"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari maṇḍavo and māḍhivo, "a pavilion", and Gujarati mā̃ḍvo, "a booth", under it (CDIAL 9740).
