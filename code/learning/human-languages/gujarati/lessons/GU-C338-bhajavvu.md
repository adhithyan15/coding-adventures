## You'll want to know: ભજવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C338-CHAPAV338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભજવવું** — *bhajavvũ* — "to perform (a play or a part)".

> અરુણે નાટકમાં રાજાનું પાત્ર ભજવ્યું.
>
> *Aruṇe nāṭakmā̃ rājānũ pātra bhajavyũ.*
>
> Arun played the king in the play.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નાટક ભજવ્યું, *teṇe nāṭak bhajavyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભજવવું** (*bhajavvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. Actors **નાટક ભજવે છે**, put on a play, and each **પાત્ર ભજવે છે**, plays a part.
