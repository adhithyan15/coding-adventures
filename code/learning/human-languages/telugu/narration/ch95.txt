Telugu, chapter 95: How Do You Say It?.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
తెలుగులో (telugulō) — in Telugu.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for need, then the Telugu for an introduction.

You'll want to know: తెలుగులో.
తెలుగులో (telugulō) — "in Telugu".
It is తెలుగు with -లో, "in", the ending you met on ఉండు. Any language takes it: ఇంగ్లీషులో, "in English".

What you've built.
A way to name the language you want an answer in.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: telugulō]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: telugulō, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say telugulō, then iṅglīṣulō]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for need, then the Telugu for an introduction, then say telugulō again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does తెలుగులో (telugulō) mean? ("In Telugu".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 7.
ఎలా అంటారు? (elā aṇṭāru?) — how do you say it?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an introduction, then the Telugu for in Telugu.

You'll want to know: ఎలా అంటారు?
ఎలా అంటారు? (elā aṇṭāru?) — "how does one say it?".
దీన్ని తెలుగులో (telugulō) ఎలా అంటారు? (elā aṇṭāru?) — "how do you say this in Telugu?". అను, "to say", is the verb inside అని and అనుకో.

What you've built.
The one question that lets a learner learn from anyone.

[your turn — say: ask telugulō elā aṇṭāru?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for an introduction, then the Telugu for in Telugu, then say elā aṇṭāru? again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 3 of 7.
మెల్లగా (mellagā) — gently, softly, slowly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for in Telugu, then the Telugu for how do you say it.

You'll want to know: మెల్లగా.
మెల్లగా (mellagā) — "gently, softly", and "slowly".
It overlaps with నెమ్మదిగా. మెల్లగా (mellagā) leans to softness: a quiet voice, a careful hand.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 7.
చూపించండి (cūpiñcaṇḍi) — please show me.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for how do you say it, then the Telugu for gently.

You'll want to know: చూపించండి.
చూపించండి (cūpiñcaṇḍi) — "please show me".
It is built on చూడు, "see": to show is to make someone see. When a word will not come, point and say చూపించండి (cūpiñcaṇḍi).

What you've built.
A request for when words run out.

Lesson 5 of 7.
వినిపించలేదు (vinipiñcalēdu) — I could not hear it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for gently, then the Telugu for please show me.

You'll want to know: వినిపించలేదు.
వినిపించలేదు (vinipiñcalēdu) — "I could not hear it", literally "it did not make itself heard".
It ends in the లేదు you know. Pair it with a fix: వినిపించలేదు (vinipiñcalēdu), గట్టిగా చెప్పండి.

What you've built.
In Telugu, how do you say, gently, show me, I could not hear: a learner's toolkit.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Asking about the language — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Point at something and ask how to say it in Telugu.

Grammar Lens: two kinds of polite.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
you ask: మన్నించండి. you answer: పరవాలేదు.
you ask: టీ కావాలా? you answer: అవసరం లేదు.
An apology is met with "no bother"; an offer is refused with "no need". Both answers end in లేదు.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: beg pardon of an elder, then say you owe them an apology]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: offer tea; then refuse help politely]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say you are glad to have met someone]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say you could not hear; ask them to speak gently, then to show you]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Pardon me? (మన్నించండి.) An apology? (క్షమాపణ.) Do you want it? (కావాలా?) No need? (అవసరం లేదు.) An introduction? (పరిచయం.) In Telugu? (తెలుగులో (telugulō).) How do you say it? (ఎలా అంటారు? (elā aṇṭāru?)) Gently? (మెల్లగా (mellagā).) Show me? (చూపించండి (cūpiñcaṇḍi).) I could not hear? (వినిపించలేదు (vinipiñcalēdu).)


Lesson 7 of 7.
From ill to well — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
You are ill. Say you have a cold, you feel weak, and your body aches.

Grammar Lens: ill and well in one frame.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
not well: నాకు నీరసంగా ఉంది. well again: హాయిగా ఉంది.
not well: జలుబు చేసింది. well again: క్షేమంగా ఉన్నారా?
The -గా ఉంది frame carries both halves: the weakness and the ease.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: ask where the hospital is; say you have been hurt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tell a friend to take the medicine]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a week later: ask an elder if they are keeping well, and a guest if they are comfortable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: agree gladly; then, when a word fails, ask to be shown, or say you could not hear]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A cold? (జలుబు.) Medicine? (మందు.) A hospital? (ఆసుపత్రి.) A wound? (గాయం.) Weakness? (నీరసం.) Wellbeing? (క్షేమం.) Comfortably? (హాయిగా.) Ease? (సుఖం.) Gladly? (సంతోషంగా.) The body? (ఒళ్ళు.)
