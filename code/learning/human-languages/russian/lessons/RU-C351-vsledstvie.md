## You'll want to know: вследствие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C351-POSKOLKU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**вследствие** (*vslédstviye*) — "owing to, in consequence of".

> Поезд опаздывает вследствие снегопада.
>
> *Póyezd opázdyvayet vslédstviye snegopáda.*
>
> The train is late owing to the snowfall.

It takes the genitive. Formal, written style: notices and the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вследствие** is fused from *в* "in" and *следствие* "a result", built on *след* "a track, a trail" (EtymDB): in the wake of something.
