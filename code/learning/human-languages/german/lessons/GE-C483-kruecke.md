## You'll want to know: die Krücke
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C483-GIPS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Krücke** — "crutch".

> Nach der Operation ging er an Krücken.
>
> *After the operation he walked on crutches.*

Plural: *die Krücken*. *An Krücken gehen* — to walk on crutches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Krücke* is inherited from Middle High German *krucke* (EtymDB), a Germanic word; English *crutch* is a cousin.
