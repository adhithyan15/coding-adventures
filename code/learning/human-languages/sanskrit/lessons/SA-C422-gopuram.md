## You'll want to know: गोपुरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C422-PRAKARA422-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गोपुरम्** — *gopuram* — "a town gate".

> यात्रिकाः गोपुरेण नगरं प्रविशन्ति।
>
> *yātrikāḥ gopureṇa nagaraṃ praviśanti.*
>
> The pilgrims enter the city by the town gate.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोपुरम्** (*gopuram*) is built from *go*, "a cow", and *pura*, "a town"; MW gives *gopura* as a town gate, and in modern use it also names the tower over a temple gate (MW).
