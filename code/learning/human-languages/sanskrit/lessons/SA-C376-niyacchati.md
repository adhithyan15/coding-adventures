## You'll want to know: नियच्छति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C376-YOGYA376-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नियच्छति** — *niyacchati* — "controls; restrains".

> यदि भवान् मनः नियच्छति, तर्हि शान्तिः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān manaḥ niyacchati, tarhi śāntiḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you control your mind, there will be peace.

Past (he …): सः क्रोधं नियतवान्, *saḥ krodhaṃ niyatavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियच्छति** (*niyacchati*) is built on *ni*, "down", and the root √yam, "to hold, to restrain"; MW gives *ni-√yam* "to restrain, to curb, to control, to govern" (MW).
