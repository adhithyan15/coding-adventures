## You'll want to know: दीर्घः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C403-PANTHA403-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दीर्घः** — *dīrghaḥ* — "long".

> पर्वतं प्रति पन्थाः दीर्घः अस्ति।
>
> *parvataṃ prati panthāḥ dīrghaḥ asti.*
>
> The route to the mountain is long.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीर्घः** (*dīrghaḥ*) is the adjective MW gives for long, in space and in time; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
