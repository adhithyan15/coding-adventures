## You'll want to know: تحمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C458-PATIENCE458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تحمل** — *tahammul* — "forbearance".

> استاد نے بڑے تحمل سے سوال سنا۔
>
> *ustād ne baṛe tahammul se savāl sunā.*
>
> The teacher listened to the question with great forbearance.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحمل** (*tahammul*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taḥammul*, "bearing, enduring".
