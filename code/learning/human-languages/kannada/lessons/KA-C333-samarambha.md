## You'll want to know: ಸಮಾರಂಭ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C333-NERAVERU333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾರಂಭ** — *samārambha* — "a ceremony; a function".

> ಶಾಲೆಯ ವಾರ್ಷಿಕ ಸಮಾರಂಭದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಹಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *śāleya vārṣika samārambhadalli makkaḷu hāḍidaru.*
>
> The children sang at the school's annual function.

Plural: ಸಮಾರಂಭಗಳು, *samārambhagaḷu*.

Any formal gathering: a wedding, a prize-giving, an opening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾರಂಭ** (*samārambha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samārambha*, "an undertaking, an enterprise" (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it became an organised event.
