## You'll want to know: మునుపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C338-KRAMANGA338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**మునుపు** — *munupu* — "previously; before".

> మునుపు ఇక్కడ ఒక చెరువు ఉండేది.
>
> *munupu ikkaḍa oka ceruvu uṇḍēdi.*
>
> Previously there was a pond here.

Close to ముందు; మునుపటి means former: మునుపటి రాజు, the former king.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మునుపు** (*munupu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5020 lists Telugu *munupu*, "former time; before", with Tamil *muṉpu*; ముందు comes from the same root.
