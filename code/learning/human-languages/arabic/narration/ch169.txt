Arabic, chapter 169: Mood, Exercise, Fitness, Injury, Pill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مزاج (mizāj) — a mood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the throat, then the Arabic for a headache.

You'll want to know: مزاج.
مزاج — mizāj — "a mood".

The word, taken apart.
مزاج (mizāj) is built on the native Arabic root m-z-j, "to mix" (Lane). Lane's mizāj is a mixture, the blend of humours that makes a temperament; "mood" grew from it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
حبة (ḥabba) — a pill, a tablet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for fitness, then the Arabic for an injury.

You'll want to know: حبة.
حبة — ḥabba — "a pill, a tablet".

The word, taken apart.
حبة (ḥabba) is one grain of ḥabb, "grain, seed", a native noun, filed under the root ḥ-b-b (Lane); a pill is a later sense.
