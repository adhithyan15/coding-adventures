## You'll want to know: मुख्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C309-VRITTANTA309-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुख्यः** — *mukhyaḥ* — "main; chief".

> अद्य मुख्या वार्ता जलौघः अस्ति।
>
> *adya mukhyā vārtā jalaughaḥ asti.*
>
> Today the main news is the flood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुख्यः** (*mukhyaḥ*) is built on *mukha*, "mouth, face, front"; MW gives *mukhya* as being at the head, first, principal, chief (MW).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
