## You'll want to know: पात्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C339-NAYAK339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पात्र** — *pātra* — "a character".

> या नाटकात फक्त तीन पात्रं आहेत.
>
> *yā nāṭkāt phakta tīn pātraṁ āhet.*
>
> There are only three characters in this play.

Neuter.

Plural पात्रं. It also means a vessel, and eligible.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पात्र** (*pātra*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pātra*, 'a vessel; a fit person; an actor' (Monier-Williams).
