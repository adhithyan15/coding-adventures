# じょじょに (jojoni) — step by step; gradually

## You'll want to know: じょじょに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C521-GRADUAL521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じょじょに** — *jojoni* — "step by step; gradually".

> **おとうとは れんしゅうしたら、じょじょに はやく なるでしょう。** — *otōto wa renshū shitara, jojoni hayaku naru deshō* — "If my younger brother practises, he will slowly get faster."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょじょに** (*jojoni*) is Sino-Japanese (kango), with the particle *ni*: one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *jo*, "gradually" (Middle Chinese *zjo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
