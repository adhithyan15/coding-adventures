## You'll want to know: तट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C444-BANDARGAH444-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तट** — *taṭ* — "a coast; a bank".

> हम समुद्र के तट पर टहले।
>
> *ham samudra ke taṭ par ṭahle.*
>
> We strolled along the seashore.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तट** (*taṭ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *taṭa*, "a slope, a bank, a shore" (Monier-Williams): the formal partner of किनारा.
