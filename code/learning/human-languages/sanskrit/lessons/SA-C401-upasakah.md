## You'll want to know: उपासकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C401-BHIKSHU401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उपासकः** — *upāsakaḥ* — "a lay follower".

> उपासकः भिक्षवे भोजनं ददाति।
>
> *upāsakaḥ bhikṣave bhojanaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The lay follower gives food to the monk.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपासकः** (*upāsakaḥ*) is built from *upa*, "near", and the root √ās, "to sit", which also gives *āste*, "sits": one who sits near and serves; MW gives *upāsaka* as a servant, a worshipper and a follower (MW).
