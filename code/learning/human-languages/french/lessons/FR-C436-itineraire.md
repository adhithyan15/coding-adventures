## You'll want to know: l'itinéraire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C436-RACCOURCI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'itinéraire** — "the route, the itinerary".

> Avant de partir, elle prépare son itinéraire.
>
> *Before leaving, she plans her route.*

A masculine noun. *Un itinéraire bis* — an alternative route.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*itinéraire* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *itinerārium* 'travel guide' (EtymDB), from *iter, itineris* 'journey'.
