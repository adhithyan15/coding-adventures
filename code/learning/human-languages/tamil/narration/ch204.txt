Tamil, chapter 204: Stairs, Pharmacy, Restaurant, Supermarket, Clothes shop.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
படிக்கட்டு (paṭikkaṭṭu) — stairs, steps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for until then, then the Tamil for childhood.

You'll want to know: படிக்கட்டு.
படிக்கட்டு — paṭikkaṭṭu — "stairs, steps".

The word, taken apart.
படிக்கட்டு (paṭikkaṭṭu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3850 lists Malayalam paṭi, "step" and Telugu paḍi-kaṭṭu. The entry lists paṭikkaṭṭu, "flight of steps", itself.

The word, taken apart.
உணவகம் (uṇavakam) is a modern coinage from native parts: uṇavu, "food" (DEDR 600), and akam, "house" (DEDR 7): "the food house".

Lesson 4 of 5.
பல்பொருள் அங்காடி (palporuḷ aṅkāṭi) — a supermarket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a pharmacy, then the Tamil for a restaurant.

You'll want to know: பல்பொருள் அங்காடி.
பல்பொருள் அங்காடி — palporuḷ aṅkāṭi — "a supermarket".

The word, taken apart.
பல்பொருள் அங்காடி (palporuḷ aṅkāṭi) is native: pal, "many" (DEDR 3987), poruḷ, "thing" (DEDR 4544), and aṅkāṭi, "bazaar" (DEDR 35; Malayalam aṅṅāṭi): "the many-things bazaar". DEDR compares aṅkāṭi, with a query, to Sanskrit aṅgaṇa, "courtyard".
