## You'll want to know: jiāohuā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C142-CHORE142-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāohuā* — "to water the plants".

> *jīntiān nǐ jiāohuā wǒ tuōdì* — Today you water the plants and I'll mop the floor.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāohuā* is built from *jiāo* 'to pour water on' and *huā* 'flower', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kew* and *xwae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
