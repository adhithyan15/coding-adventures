## You'll want to know: o padroeiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C446-ARRAIAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o padroeiro** — "the patron saint, the patron".

> A festa do padroeiro é em agosto.
>
> *The patron saint's festival is in August.*

Feminine: *a padroeira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*padroeiro* goes back to Latin *patrōnus* 'protector' (EtymDB has *patrono*), built on *pater* 'father'.
