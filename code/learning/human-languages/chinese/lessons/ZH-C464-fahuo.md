## You'll want to know: 发火
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C464-ANGRY464-01]; assesses=[] -->

**发火** — *fāhuǒ* — "to lose one's temper".

> **他发火的时候我很怕他** *tā fāhuǒ de shíhou wǒ hěn pà tā* — I'm really scared of him when he loses his temper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**发火** (*fāhuǒ*) is built from 发 'to send out' and 火 'fire', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *xwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to start' and 'to raise'. Anger as a fire that breaks out is the usual explanation, not one the dictionary gives.
