## You'll want to know: राज्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C316-SAMRAT316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**राज्यम्** — *rājyam* — "a kingdom".

> तस्य राज्यं विशालम् अस्ति।
>
> *tasya rājyaṃ viśālam asti.*
>
> His kingdom is vast.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राज्यम्** (*rājyam*) is built on *rājan*, "a king" (*rājā*), with the ending *-ya*; MW gives *rājya* as kingship, sovereignty, and a kingdom (MW).
