## You'll want to know: معتقد بودن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C360-AQIDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**معتقد بودن** (*mo'taqed budan*) — "to believe, to be convinced".

> من معتقدم که ورزش برای همه لازم است.
>
> *man mo'taqedam ke varzesh barâye hame lâzem ast.*
>
> I believe that sport is necessary for everyone.

*Mo'taqed* "convinced" + *budan* "to be": *mo'taqedam ke* … — I believe that … . A belief in something takes *be*: *be chizi mo'taqed budan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معتقد** is usually traced to Arabic *mu'taqid* "believing, holding firmly", from the same Arabic family as *'aqīda*. *Budan* "to be" is the inherited Persian verb.
