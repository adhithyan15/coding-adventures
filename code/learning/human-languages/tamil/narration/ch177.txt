Lesson 1 of 5.
விளைவு (viḷaivu) — a result, a consequence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for for example, then the Tamil for otherwise.

You'll want to know: விளைவு.
விளைவு — viḷaivu — "a result, a consequence".

The word, taken apart.
விளைவு (viḷaivu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5437 lists Malayalam viḷa, "crop". It is the noun of viḷai, "to be produced, to result".

Lesson 2 of 5.
பிரச்சினை (piracciṉai) — a problem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for otherwise, then the Tamil for a result.

You'll want to know: பிரச்சினை.
பிரச்சினை — piracciṉai — "a problem".

The word, taken apart.
பிரச்சினை (piracciṉai) is a loan from Sanskrit praśna, "question" (CDIAL 8818). Tamil broke up the pr- and the śn, and the sense moved from a question to a problem.
