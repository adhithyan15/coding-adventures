## You'll want to know: mǐfàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C38-EAT38-03]; assesses=[] -->

*mǐfàn* — "cooked rice".

> *wǒ xǐhuan chī mǐfàn* — I like rice.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mǐfàn* is built from mǐ 'husked grain' and 饭 'cooked grain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mejX* and *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
