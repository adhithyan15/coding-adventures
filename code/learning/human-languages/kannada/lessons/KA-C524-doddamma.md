# ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮ (doḍḍamma) — mother's elder sister

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-02, KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for father's elder brother, then the Kannada for mother's younger sister.

## You'll want to know: ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C524-BANDHU524-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮ** — *doḍḍamma* — "mother's elder sister".

> ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮ ರುಚಿಯಾದ ಹೋಳಿಗೆ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *doḍḍamma ruciyāda hōḷige māḍuttāre.*
>
> My mother's elder sister makes tasty holige.

Also the wife of ದೊಡ್ಡಪ್ಪ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮ** (*doḍḍamma*) is a native Dravidian compound: *doḍḍa*, "big" (DEDR 3491), and *amma*, "mother" (DEDR 183).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
