## You'll want to know: убеждение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C339-UBEZHDAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**убеждение** (*ubezhdéniye*) — "a conviction, a firm belief".

> Он не ест мясо по убеждению.
>
> *On ne yest myáso po ubezhdéniyu.*
>
> He does not eat meat out of conviction.

Neuter. *По убеждению* — out of conviction. The plural *убеждения* means one’s beliefs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**убеждение** is built on *убедить* "to convince" with the noun suffix *-ение*, keeping the Church Slavonic *жд*. The root is that of *беда*, Proto-Slavic *\*běda* (EtymDB).
