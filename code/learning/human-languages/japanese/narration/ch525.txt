Japanese, chapter 525: Real or Fake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ほんもの (honmono) — the real thing; genuine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for reasoning, then the Japanese for to be realized.

You'll want to know: ほんもの.
ほんもの — honmono — "the real thing; genuine".
もし ほんものを かったら、たかいでしょう。 — moshi honmono o kattara, takai deshō — "If you buy the real thing, it will probably be expensive."

The word, taken apart.
ほんもの (honmono) mixes the two layers, native and Sino-Japanese: Sino-Japanese hon, "true" (Middle Chinese pwonX), borrowed from Chinese and native mono, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
にせもの (nisemono) — a fake; a forgery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be realized, then the Japanese for the real thing.

You'll want to know: にせもの.
にせもの — nisemono — "a fake; a forgery".
もし にせものが みつかったら、けいさつに しらせて ください。 — moshi nisemono ga mitsukattara, keisatsu ni shirasete kudasai — "If you find a fake, please inform the police."

The word, taken apart.
にせもの (nisemono) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from nise, "false" and mono, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 5.
たとえる (tatoeru) — to compare (to); to liken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a fake, then the Japanese for (not) in the least.

You'll want to know: たとえる.
たとえる — tatoeru — "to compare (to); to liken".
もし いのちを はなに たとえたら、どの はなでしょう。 — moshi inochi o hana ni tatoetara, dono hana deshō — "If you compared life to a flower, which flower would it be?"
Polite たとえます, tatoemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): しじんが こいを はなに たとえました。, shijin ga koi o hana ni tatoemashita.

The word, taken apart.
たとえる (tatoeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
