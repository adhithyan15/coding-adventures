Portuguese, chapter 373: Correr riscos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o risco — the risk; the line, the scratch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for final, then the Portuguese for flexible.

You'll want to know: o risco.
o risco — "the risk; the line, the scratch".
Não quero correr o risco de perder o comboio. I do not want to run the risk of missing the train.
Correr o risco — to run the risk. Also a line or a scratch: um risco no carro.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of risco "risk" is not settled; it goes with Italian rischio and Spanish riesgo, while risco "line, scratch" belongs with the verb riscar "to scratch" (EtymDB lists risco "line").

Lesson 2 of 5.
arriscado — risky.

Before the new one: say the Portuguese for flexible, then the Portuguese for the risk.

You'll want to know: arriscado.
arriscado — "risky".
Atravessar a rua com o semáforo vermelho é arriscado. Crossing the street on a red light is risky.
Built on arriscar — to risk.

The word, taken apart.
arriscado is the participle of arriscar "to risk", which EtymDB analyses as a- + risco + -ar (EtymDB); the origin of risco "risk" is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
provável — likely, probable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the risk, then the Portuguese for risky.

You'll want to know: provável.
provável — "likely, probable".
É provável que chova amanhã. It is likely to rain tomorrow.
É provável que takes the subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
provável comes from Latin probābilis "that may be believed or proved, likely" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on probāre "to test, to prove".

Lesson 4 of 5.
improvável — unlikely, improbable.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Portuguese for risky, then the Portuguese for likely.

You'll want to know: improvável.
improvável — "unlikely, improbable".
É improvável que ele mude de ideias. It is unlikely that he will change his mind.
É improvável que takes the subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
improvável comes from Latin improbābilis "not credible" (EtymDB), built from in- "not" and probābilis "likely", on probāre "to prove" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
a probabilidade — the probability, the chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for likely, then the Portuguese for unlikely.

You'll want to know: a probabilidade.
a probabilidade — "the probability, the chance".
Há uma grande probabilidade de nevar. There is a good chance of snow.
Com toda a probabilidade — in all likelihood.

The word, taken apart.
probabilidade is a learned borrowing from Latin probābilitās "probability, credibility" (EtymDB), from probābilis "likely", on probāre "to prove" (Lewis & Short).
