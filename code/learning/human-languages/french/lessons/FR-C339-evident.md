## You'll want to know: évident
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C339-PREUVE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**évident** — "obvious, evident".

> C'est évident : il est fatigué.
>
> *It is obvious: he is tired.*

*Ce n'est pas évident* also means it is not easy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*évident* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēvidēns* "apparent, visible, clear" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from *ē* "out" and *vidēre* "to see".
