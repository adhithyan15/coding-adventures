## You'll want to know: der Karton
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C452-LIEFERUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Karton** — "the cardboard box; cardboard".

> Für den Umzug brauchen wir viele Kartons.
>
> *We need lots of cardboard boxes for the move.*

Plural: *die Kartons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Karton* was borrowed from French *carton* (EtymDB), which came from Italian *cartone* 'stiff paper', built on *carta* 'paper', from Latin *charta*.
