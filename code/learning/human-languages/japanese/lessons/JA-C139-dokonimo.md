## You'll want to know: どこにも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C139-DOKONIMO]; assesses=[] -->

**どこにも** — *doko ni mo* — "nowhere", "not anywhere". It is **どこ**,
"where", with **に** for the place you go to and **も**, and like the other
two it needs a negative verb.

> **あしたは どこにも いきません。** — *ashita wa doko ni mo ikimasen* —
> "I'm not going anywhere tomorrow."

Now you can ask and answer:

> **あした はたらきますか。** — "Are you working tomorrow?"
> **いいえ、はたらきません。どこにも いきません。** — "No, I'm not. I'm not
> going anywhere."
