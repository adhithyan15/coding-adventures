## You'll want to know: receitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C487-CONSULTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**receitar** — "to prescribe".

> O médico receitou-me um xarope.
>
> *The doctor prescribed me a syrup.*

Built on *a receita*, which is a prescription and also a recipe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*receitar* is built on *receita* "a prescription, a recipe", from Latin *recepta* "things received" (EtymDB), from *recipere* "to take back" (Lewis & Short).
