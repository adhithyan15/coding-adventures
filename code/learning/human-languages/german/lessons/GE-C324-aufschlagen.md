## You'll want to know: aufschlagen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C324-RUEHREN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**aufschlagen** — "to crack open (an egg); to open (a book)".

> Sie schlug drei Eier auf.
>
> *She cracked three eggs.*

Separable: *sie schlägt auf*. Also to open a book: *das Buch aufschlagen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aufschlagen* joins *auf* "open" and *schlagen* "to strike", an inherited Germanic verb; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
