Punjabi, chapter 297: Rough and Smooth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮੁਲਾਇਮ (mulāim) — smooth; soft to the touch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for slick, then the Punjabi for to melt.

You'll want to know: ਮੁਲਾਇਮ.
ਮੁਲਾਇਮ — mulāim — "smooth; soft to the touch".
ਇਹ ਕੱਪੜਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਮੁਲਾਇਮ ਹੈ। eh kappṛā bahut mulāim hai. This cloth is very smooth.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੁਲਾਇਮ (mulāim) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic mulāyim, "gentle, soft".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for smooth, then the Punjabi for fine-grained.

ਕੂਲਾ — kūlā — "tender; soft".
ਬੱਚੇ ਦੇ ਹੱਥ ਕੂਲੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ। bachche de hatth kūle hunde han. A child's hands are soft.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੂਲਾ (kūlā) is inherited from Sanskrit kōmala, "tender, soft"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kūlā under it (CDIAL 3523).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਚੁਭਣਾ (chubhṇā) — to prick; to stick into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for tender, then the Punjabi for blunt.

You'll want to know: ਚੁਭਣਾ.
ਚੁਭਣਾ — chubhṇā — "to prick; to stick into".
ਸੂਈ ਉਂਗਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੁਭਦੀ ਹੈ। sūī uṅgal vich chubhdī hai. The needle pricks the finger.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਸੂਈ ਚੁਭੀ, oh sūī chubhī.

The word, taken apart.
ਚੁਭਣਾ (chubhṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed cubhyatē, "is pierced"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cubhṇā, "to be pierced", under it (CDIAL 4867).
