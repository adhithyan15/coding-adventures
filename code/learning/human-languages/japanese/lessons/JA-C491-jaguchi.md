## You'll want to know: じゃぐち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C491-STOVE491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**じゃぐち** — *jaguchi* — "a tap; a faucet".

> **じゃぐちで てを あらいます。** — *jaguchi de te o araimasu* — "I wash my hands at the tap."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゃぐち** (*jaguchi*) mixes the two layers, native and Sino-Japanese: Sino-Japanese *ja*, "snake" (Middle Chinese *zyae*), borrowed from Chinese and native *guchi*, "mouth" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
