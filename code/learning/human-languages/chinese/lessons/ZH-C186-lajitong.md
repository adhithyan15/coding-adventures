## You'll want to know: lājītǒng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C186-THINGS186-02]; assesses=[] -->

*lājītǒng* — "rubbish bin".

> *lājītǒng mǎn le* — The bin is full.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lājītǒng* is built from *lājī* 'rubbish' and *tǒng* 'tub'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *thuwngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a wooden tub'; for *lājī* no dictionary we use records an older form, so the history of that part is not settled.

## What you've built
