## You'll want to know: さいご
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C171-ORDER171-04]; assesses=[] -->

**さいご** — *saigo* — "the last; the very end".

> **あしたは さいごに おちゃを のみます。** — *ashita wa saigo ni ocha o nomimasu* — "Tomorrow I will drink tea last."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さいご** (*saigo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sai*, "most, utmost" (Middle Chinese *tswajH*) and *go*, "behind, after" (*huwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
