## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YO-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-YO-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YA-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YU-01] -->

> **ょ** — small *yo*

Same shape as **よ**, in the same order, written small and sitting low: the short
bar from left to right, then the stem down through it and round the loop. Two
strokes, one lift.

You now have all three small signs that join forward. Each one turns the sign
before it into a single beat:

| you write | you say, one beat each |
|---|---|
| **ちゃ**, **ちゅ**, **ちょ** | *cha*, *chu*, *cho* |
| **しゃ**, **しゅ**, **しょ** | *sha*, *shu*, *sho* |

So **ちょ** is one beat, *cho*, and **ちょっと** is the three beats you said in
the last lesson: *cho*, a held silence, *to*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YO-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YA-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-CHOTTO] -->

1. Write **よ**, then **ょ** beneath it, half the height.
2. Write **ちょっと** from memory: one small sign that joins, one that holds.
3. Write **しゃ**, **しゅ**, **しょ** in a row and read them as three single beats.
