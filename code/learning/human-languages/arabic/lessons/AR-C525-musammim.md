## You'll want to know: مصمم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C525-TASMIM525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مصمم** — *muṣammim* — "a designer".

> يعمل ابن خالتي مصمما في شركة.
>
> *yaʿmalu ibn khālatī muṣammiman fī sharika.*
>
> My cousin works as a designer in a company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصمم** (*muṣammim*) is the participle of Form II *ṣammama*, "to design", which is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ṣ-m-m*, "to be solid".
