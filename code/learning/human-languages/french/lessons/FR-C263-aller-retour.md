# un aller-retour — there and back

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-C80-CHOSES32-03, FR-LEX-C231-CHOSES231-01, FR-GRAMMAR-MODAL-CHAIN-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a ticket". (*Un billet.*) Say "the return". (*Le retour.*)
Then ask a stranger for a coffee. (*Je voudrais un café.*)

[PAUSE 1s] You are at the ticket window, and you know the polite opener
already.

## You'll want to know: un aller-retour, un aller simple
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-ALLER-RETOUR-01]; assesses=[FR-LEX-C191-QUAL191-01, FR-LEX-ALLER-06] -->

**un aller-retour** — "a return ticket". **un aller simple** — "a one-way
ticket".

Here *aller* is a noun: **l'aller** is the outward journey, and **le
retour** is the way back. So an *aller-retour* is "a going and a coming
back", and an *aller simple* is the going on its own. Both are masculine,
*un*, and the word *billet* is usually left out.

## Grammar Lens: at the window
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-GRAM-BILLET-POUR-02]; assesses=[FR-LEX-C99-VILLE51-04, FR-LEX-C247-CHOSES247-05, FR-LEX-COMBIEN-06, FR-GRAM-IL-EST-HEURES-03] -->

The place you are going to takes **pour**, "for":

> ***Je voudrais un aller-retour pour Paris**, s'il vous plaît.*
> *— Un aller simple pour Lyon, s'il vous plaît.*

Then the three questions that get you onto the right train, said in one
breath: *À quelle heure part le train ? De quel quai ? C'est combien ?* —
"What time does the train leave? From which platform? How much is it?"

And the answer you will hear back puts the hour and the platform side by
side: *Le train part à dix heures, quai trois.*

## The phrase, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-ETYMON-ALLER-07] -->

**aller** has three Latin parents, and its infinitive is the one usually
traced to Latin *ambulāre*, "to walk". **retour** is formed from the verb for
"to return", *re-* plus **tourner**, "to turn", which is inherited from Latin
*tornāre* (EtymDB 2.1). **simple** is inherited from Latin *simplex*,
"single, plain" (EtymDB 2.1). So *un aller simple* is, at root, "a single
walk out", and the ticket home is a turning back.
