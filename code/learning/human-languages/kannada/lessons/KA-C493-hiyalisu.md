# ಹೀಯಾಳಿಸು (hīyāḷisu) — to ridicule; to taunt

## You'll want to know: ಹೀಯಾಳಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C493-KOPA493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೀಯಾಳಿಸು** — *hīyāḷisu* — "to ridicule; to taunt".

> ಅವನ ಹಳೆಯ ಬಟ್ಟೆ ನೋಡಿ ಕೆಲವರು ಹೀಯಾಳಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *avana haḷeya baṭṭe nōḍi kelavaru hīyāḷisidaru.*
>
> Some people ridiculed him for his old clothes.

Past (I …): ಹೀಯಾಳಿಸಿದೆ, *hīyāḷiside*.

The one ridiculed takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಹೀಯಾಳಿಕೆ, ridicule.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೀಯಾಳಿಸು** (*hīyāḷisu*) is a native Kannada verb, "to ridicule, to taunt"; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
