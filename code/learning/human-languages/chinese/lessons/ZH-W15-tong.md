# 同 — frame, line, box

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-TONGXUE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-KOU] -->

[PAUSE 9s] Say **classmate**, then air-write 口.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-TONG-01]; assesses=[] -->

Draw the outer left side. Lift for the joined outer top and right side, ending
with its small hook. Add the inner horizontal. Finish the familiar 口 inside:
left, joined top-right, closing bottom. Six strokes; five lifts.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-TONG-01] -->
