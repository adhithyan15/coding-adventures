## You'll want to know: మంచితనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C507-SVABHAVAM507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**మంచితనం** — *mañcitanaṁ* — "goodness; decency".

> అతని మంచితనం వల్ల అందరూ అతన్ని నమ్ముతారు.
>
> *atani mañcitanaṁ valla andarū atanni nammutāru.*
>
> Because of his decency, everyone trusts him.

-తనం makes an abstract noun: మంచి, good; మంచితనం, goodness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మంచితనం** (*mañcitanaṁ*) is built from మంచి, "good", and -తనం, the ending that makes abstract nouns; the origin of మంచి is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
