## You'll want to know: आकलन होणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C375-BUDDHI375-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आकलन होणे** — *ākalan hoṇe* — "to comprehend; to grasp".

> पुन्हा वाचल्यावर मला धड्याचं नीट आकलन झालं.
>
> *punhā vācalyāvar malā dhaḍyācaṁ nīṭ ākalan jhālaṁ.*
>
> After reading it again, I grasped the lesson properly.

Past (to him …): त्याला आकलन झालं, *tyālā ākalan jhālaṁ*.

Dative subject: मला आकलन होतं; what is grasped takes -चं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकलन होणे** (*ākalan hoṇe*) joins the tatsama आकलन, Sanskrit *ākalana*, "fastening, reckoning" (Monier-Williams), to होणे, "to become"; Marathi uses आकलन for taking in what something means.
