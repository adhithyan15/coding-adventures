## You'll want to know: सहमति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C542-SAMJHAUTA542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सहमति** — *sahmati* — "agreement; consent (formal)".

> दोनों दोस्तों में पूरी सहमति है।
>
> *Donõ dostoṁ meṁ pūrī sahmati hai.*
>
> The two friends are in complete agreement.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहमति** (*sahmati*) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, *saha*, "with", and *mati*, "mind, opinion" (Monier-Williams): being of one mind with someone.
