## You'll want to know: अनुजः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C498-AGRAJA498-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुजः** — *anujaḥ* — "a younger brother".

> अनुजः अग्रजेन सह क्रीडति।
>
> *anujaḥ agrajena saha krīḍati.*
>
> The younger brother plays with the elder.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुजः** (*anujaḥ*) is built from *anu*, "after", and *ja*, "born", from the root √jan; MW gives "born after, younger" and "a younger brother" (MW).
