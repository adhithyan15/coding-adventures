## You'll want to know: मोहरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C545-JUA545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहरा** — *mohrā* — "a piece (in chess or a board game)".

> उसने अपना मोहरा आगे बढ़ाया।
>
> *Usne apnā mohrā āge baṛhāyā.*
>
> He moved his piece forward.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोहरा** (*mohrā*) is usually traced to Persian *muhra*, "a bead, a playing piece".
