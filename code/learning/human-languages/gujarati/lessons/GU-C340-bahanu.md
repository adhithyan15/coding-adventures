## You'll want to know: બહાનું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C340-HETU340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બહાનું** — *bahānũ* — "an excuse".

> તેણે મોડા આવવાનું એક નવું બહાનું કાઢ્યું.
>
> *teṇe moḍā āvvānũ ek navũ bahānũ kāḍhyũ.*
>
> He came up with a new excuse for being late.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બહાનું** (*bahānũ*) is usually traced to Persian *bahāna*, "a pretext".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
