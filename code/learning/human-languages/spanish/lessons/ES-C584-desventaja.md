## You'll want to know: la desventaja
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C584-VENTAJA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la desventaja** — "the disadvantage".

> La desventaja es el precio.
>
> *The disadvantage is the price.*

The opposite of *la ventaja*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desventaja* is built on *ventaja* with the prefix *des-*; *ventaja* was borrowed from French *avantage*, from Latin *ab ante* 'in front' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
