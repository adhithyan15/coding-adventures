Arabic, chapter 129: Beans, Garlic, Cucumber, Apples, Dates.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
فول (fūl) — beans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for lentils, then the Arabic for chickpeas.

You'll want to know: فول.
فول — fūl — "beans".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: fūl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: fūl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say fūl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for lentils, then the Arabic for chickpeas, then say fūl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does فول (fūl) mean? ("Beans".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Jam? (مربى, murabbā.) A pomegranate? (رمان, rummān.) Strawberries? (فراولة, farāwila.) Lettuce? (خس, khass.) An aubergine? (باذنجان, bādhinjān.) A cabbage? (ملفوف, malfūf.) Mint? (نعناع, naʿnāʿ.) Parsley? (بقدونس, baqdūnis.) Thyme? (زعتر, zaʿtar.) Cumin? (كمون, kammūn.) A cupboard? (خزانة, khizāna.) A comb? (مشط, mushṭ.) Soap? (صابون, ṣābūn.) A towel? (منشفة, minshafa.) A mattress? (فراش, firāsh.) A blanket? (بطانية, baṭṭāniyya.) A pillow? (وسادة, wisāda.) A ladder? (سلم, sullam.) A stove? (موقد, mawqid.) A vessel? (إناء, ināʾ.) Scissors? (مقص, miqaṣṣ.) A needle? (إبرة, ibra.) A thread? (خيط, khayṭ.) A button? (زر, zirr.) A nail? (مسمار, mismār.)
