# பாசம் (pācam) — family affection; attachment

## You'll want to know: பாசம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C465-ANPU465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பாசம்** — *pācam* — "family affection; attachment".

> தாத்தாவுக்குப் பேரன் மேல் நிறைய பாசம்.
>
> *tāttāvukkup pēraṉ mēl niṟaiya pācam.*
>
> Grandfather is very attached to his grandson.

மேல் "on" marks the one loved: அவன் மேல் பாசம்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பாசம்** (*pācam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pāśa*, "a noose, a cord" (CDIAL 8133): in Tamil, the tie of love that binds a family.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
