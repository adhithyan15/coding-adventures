Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 288: Hard Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मेहनत (mehnat) — hard work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a pupil, then the Marwadi for a wrestling ground.

You'll want to know: मेहनत.
मेहनत — mehnat — "hard work".
मेहनत चोखी बात है। mehnat chokhī bāt hai. Hard work is a good thing.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मेहनत (mehnat) is usually traced, through Persian mihnat, "labour", to Arabic miḥna, "trial, hardship". मेहनती, hard-working, is built on it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
धंधो (dhandho) — a trade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for capable, then the Marwadi for a master of a craft.

You'll want to know: धंधो.
धंधो — dhandho — "a trade".
म्हारो धंधो सोरो कोनी। mhāro dhandho soro konī. My trade isn't easy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धंधो (dhandho) is inherited from Sanskrit dhandha, "awkwardness"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi dhandhā, "craft, way of life", and Sindhi dhandho, "business" (CDIAL 6727). How the sense moved to work is not settled.
