## You'll want to know: ہانپنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C392-STAND392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ہانپنا** — *hāṅpnā* — "to pant".

> سارا دوڑ کر اسٹاپ پر پہنچی اور ہانپ رہی تھی۔
>
> *sārā dauṛ kar isṭāp par pahuṅchī aur hāṅp rahī thī.*
>
> Sara ran to the stop and was out of breath.

Past (he …): وہ سیڑھی پر ہانپا, *voh sīṛhī par hāṅpā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہانپنا** (*hāṅpnā*) is inherited from *hamph*, an imitative form CDIAL reconstructs for panting (CDIAL 13973).
