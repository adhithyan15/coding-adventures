## You'll want to know: સાબિતી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C344-SABITI344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાબિતી** — *sābitī* — "proof".

> તમારા દાવાની સાબિતી ક્યાં છે?
>
> *tamārā dāvānī sābitī kyā̃ che?*
>
> Where is the proof of your claim?

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાબિતી** (*sābitī*) is usually traced to Arabic *thābit*, "firm, proven", which came through Persian; Gujarati adds the ending *-ī* to make the noun.
