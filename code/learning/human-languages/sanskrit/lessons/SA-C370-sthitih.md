## You'll want to know: स्थितिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C370-STHITI370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थितिः** — *sthitiḥ* — "a situation".

> यदि स्थितिः कठिना भवति, तर्हि अहं साहाय्यं करिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi sthitiḥ kaṭhinā bhavati, tarhi ahaṃ sāhāyyaṃ kariṣyāmi.*
>
> If the situation becomes hard, I will help.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्थितिः** (*sthitiḥ*) comes from the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives *sthiti* "staying or being in any state or condition" (MW).
