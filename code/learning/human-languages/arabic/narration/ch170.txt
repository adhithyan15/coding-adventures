Arabic, chapter 170: Early, Daily, Delay, Late, Never.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مبكرا (mubakkiran) — early.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an injury, then the Arabic for a pill.

You'll want to know: مبكرا.
مبكرا — mubakkiran — "early".

The word, taken apart.
مبكرا (mubakkiran) is built on the native Arabic root b-k-r, "to come early" (Lane). Mubakkiran is the Form II participle, "coming early", with the adverb ending -an.
