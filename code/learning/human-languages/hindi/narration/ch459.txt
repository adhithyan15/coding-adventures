Lesson 1 of 6.
स्नेह (sneh) — affection; fondness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to writhe, then the Hindi for an assurance.

You'll want to know: स्नेह.
स्नेह — sneh — "affection; fondness".
दादी सब बच्चों से बहुत स्नेह करती हैं। dādī sab baccoṁ se bahut sneh kartī haiṁ. Grandmother is very fond of all the children.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
स्नेह (sneh) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sneha, first "oiliness" and then "tenderness, love, fondness" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ममता (mamtā) — a mother's love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an assurance, then the Hindi for affection.

You'll want to know: ममता.
ममता — mamtā — "a mother's love".
माँ की ममता की कोई क़ीमत नहीं होती। māṁ kī mamtā kī koī qīmat nahīṃ hotī. No price can be put on a mother's love.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ममता (mamtā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit mamatā, "the state of 'mine'", built on mama, "mine" (Monier-Williams). Hindi turned the sense of ownership into the tender love of a mother.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for fond pampering, then the Hindi for to kiss.

You'll want to know: पुचकारना.
पुचकारना — puckārnā — "to soothe with soft words".
बच्चा रो रहा था, तो दादी ने उसे पुचकारा। baccā ro rahā thā, to dādī ne use puckārā. The child was crying, so my grandmother soothed him with soft words.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को पुचकारा, usne bacce ko puckārā.

The word, taken apart.
पुचकारना (puckārnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi puckārnā, "to caress", under pucca, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 8248). It is the soft clicking and cooing used to calm a child or an animal.
