# வரதட்சணை (varataṭcaṇai) — a dowry

## You'll want to know: வரதட்சணை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C499-MANAM499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**வரதட்சணை** — *varataṭcaṇai* — "a dowry".

> அவர்கள் வரதட்சணை வாங்காமல் திருமணம் செய்தார்கள்.
>
> *avarkaḷ varataṭcaṇai vāṅkāmal tirumaṇam ceytārkaḷ.*
>
> They married without taking a dowry.

வரதட்சணை வாங்கு, to take a dowry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வரதட்சணை** (*varataṭcaṇai*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, built from *vara*, "bridegroom" (CDIAL 11309), and *dakṣiṇā*, "a gift" (CDIAL 6120); Tamil writes *kṣ* as ட்ச.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
