## You'll want to know: la mercerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C445-BOUTIQUES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la mercerie** — "the haberdasher’s, the sewing shop".

> Ma grand-mère achète ses boutons à la mercerie.
>
> *My grandmother buys her buttons at the haberdasher's.*

Buttons, thread, ribbons.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mercerie* is built on *mercier* 'dealer in small goods' (EtymDB lists *mercerie*), which goes back to Latin *merx, mercis* 'goods'.
