## You'll want to know: 比
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C153-BI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**比** — *bǐ* — "than; compared with".

> **我比他大** *wǒ bǐ tā dà* — I'm older than him.

The next lesson puts the character in your hand. It sounds like *bǐ*, a pen, and it is a different word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**比** (*bǐ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *pjijX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to compare; side by side'. It is the bǐ of *bǐsài*, a match. The *bǐ* of *qiānbǐ*, a pencil, is another word with the same sound.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C135-SPORT135-04] -->

比 sits between the two things compared, and the adjective comes last: 我, 比, 他, 大.

大 for people is old: 我比他大 is I'm older than him. 比 itself is the bǐ of *bǐsài*, a match: two sides set against each other.
