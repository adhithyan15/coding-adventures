Kannada, chapter 87: Quick Replies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಿಜವಾಗಲೂ (nijavāgalū) — really?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for I didn't hear, then the Kannada for I didn't understand.

You'll want to know: ನಿಜವಾಗಲೂ.
ನಿಜವಾಗಲೂ? (nijavāgalū?) — "really?", built on ನಿಜ (nija), "true".
Said with rising voice it is surprise; said flat, it is "truly".

What you've built.
True, turned into a question.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nijavāgalū]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nijavāgalū, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nijavāgalū?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for I didn't hear, then the Kannada for I didn't understand, then say nijavāgalū again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ನಿಜವಾಗಲೂ (nijavāgalū) mean? ("Really?".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ಏನೂ ಇಲ್ಲ (ēnū illa) — nothing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for I didn't understand, then the Kannada for really.

You'll want to know: ಏನೂ ಇಲ್ಲ.
ಏನೂ ಇಲ್ಲ (ēnū illa) — "nothing", literally "anything, there isn't". ಏನು (ēnu), "what", takes ‑ಊ to become "anything".

What you've built.
What, and not, make nothing.

ಅಯ್ಯೋ (ayyō) — "oh no!", "oh dear!". You hear it everywhere in the south when something goes wrong.

What you've built.
The sound of a small disaster.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅದೇ (adē) — the same one, that very one.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for oh no, then the Kannada for is that so.

You'll want to know: ಅದೇ.
ಅದೇ (adē) — "that very one, the same". It is ಅದು (adu), "that", with the emphatic ‑ಏ.
ಅದೇ (adē)! — "that's it! exactly that one!"

What you've built.
That, made emphatic.
