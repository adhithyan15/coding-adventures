## You'll want to know: પોળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C417-POL417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પોળ** — *poḷ* — "a pol (a gated old-city neighbourhood)".

> જૂના શહેરની પોળમાં સાંકડી ગલીઓ છે.
>
> *jūnā śahernī poḷmā̃ sā̃kḍī galīo che.*
>
> The pol in the old city has narrow lanes.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પોળ** (*poḷ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pratōlī*, "a main street; a town gate"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a street" (CDIAL 8633). A પોળ is a street of houses shut in by its own gate.
