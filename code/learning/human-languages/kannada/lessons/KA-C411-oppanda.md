# ಒಪ್ಪಂದ (oppanda) — an agreement; a deal

## You'll want to know: ಒಪ್ಪಂದ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C411-SHARATTU411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಪ್ಪಂದ** — *oppanda* — "an agreement; a deal".

> ಎರಡು ಕಂಪನಿಗಳು ಒಂದು ಒಪ್ಪಂದ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡವು.
>
> *eraḍu kampanigaḷu ondu oppanda māḍikoṇḍavu.*
>
> The two companies made an agreement.

ಒಪ್ಪಂದ ಮಾಡಿಕೊ, to make a deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಪ್ಪಂದ** (*oppanda*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 924 lists Kannada *oppanda*, "an agreement", beside *oppu*, "to agree".
