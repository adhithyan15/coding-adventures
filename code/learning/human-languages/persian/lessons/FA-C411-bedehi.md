## You'll want to know: بدهی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C411-QARZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بدهی** (*bedehi*) — "a debt".

> او همه بدهیهایش را پرداخت.
>
> *u hame-ye bedehihâyash râ pardâkht.*
>
> He paid off all his debts.

From *bedeh* "owing", built on *dâdan* "to give", + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بدهی** is native Persian, built on *bedeh* "owing", from *deh-*, the present stem of *dâdan* "to give", with the noun suffix *-i*: what you still have to give.
