# रुजू होणे (rujū hoṇe) — to join a post; to report for duty

## You'll want to know: रुजू होणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C507-ROJGAR507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रुजू होणे** — *rujū hoṇe* — "to join a post; to report for duty".

> अरुण सोमवारी नवीन कार्यालयात रुजू झाला.
>
> *aruṇ somvārī navīn kāryālayāt rujū jhālā.*
>
> Arun joined the new office on Monday.

Past (he …): तो कामावर रुजू झाला, *to kāmāvar rujū jhālā*.

The place takes -त or -वर: कार्यालयात रुजू होणे, कामावर रुजू होणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुजू होणे** (*rujū hoṇe*) is रुजू, a loan usually traced to Arabic *rujūʿ*, "turning to", through Persian, with होणे: to present oneself for duty.
