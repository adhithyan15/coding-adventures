# (reading) — a note about a repair

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C165-NOTE165-01, MW-LEX-C50-FAMILY50-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *a mechanic* and *a neighbour*.

[PAUSE 1s] This one is written to you, by someone you know. Read it for one thing:
when you have to be at home.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SKILL-C165-READ-NOTE-01]; assesses=[MW-SKILL-C164-READ-NOTICE-01, MW-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING, MW-LEX-C165-NOTE165-01, MW-LEX-C50-FAMILY50-02, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-ISNOT-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C160-AYO-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-03, MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GHARAI-01] -->

> **राम-राम सा!**
>
> **हूं पाड़ोसी राम हूं।**
> **काल सूं अठै पाणी कोनी।**
> **आज मिस्त्री कोनी आयो।**
> **हूं काल दिनूगै मिस्त्री लावसूं।**
> **मिस्त्री चोखो है।**
> **आप काल दिनूगै ९ सूं ११ तांई घरै रैवो।**
> **दरवाजो खुलो राखो।**
> **दोपार हूं घरै कोनी।**
> **कोई सवाल? आज आथण ७ सूं ९ तांई हूं घरै हूं।**
> **आभार।**
> **राम**

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: the note once, straight through, without translating]

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: it again, and find the two lines with hours in them]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C105-ACT105-01, MW-LEX-C53-VERBS53-05, MW-LEX-C55-VERBS55-04, MW-LEX-C100-LOC100-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01, MW-LEX-C141-QUAL141-01, MW-LEX-C102-DEF102-04, MW-LEX-C66-SCHOOL66-03, MW-SCRIPT-AABHAAR-01, MW-LEX-RAAM-RAAM-SAA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **रैवो** and **राखो** are requests, like **लावो**: *please stay*, *please
keep*. **लावसूं** is Ram's own future: *I will bring*. **काल सूं** means *since
yesterday*: **सूं** marks where the time starts.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SKILL-C165-READ-NOTE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-ISNOT-01, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU ANSWER: what is wrong? (There has been no water since yesterday.)]
- [YOU ANSWER: did the mechanic come today? (No: **आज मिस्त्री कोनी आयो।**)]
- [YOU ANSWER: if you have a question, when is Ram at home? (This evening, seven until nine.)]
