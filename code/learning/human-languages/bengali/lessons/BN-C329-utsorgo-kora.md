# উৎসর্গ করা (utsôrgô kôrā) — to dedicate

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for forever, then the Bengali for unforgettable.

## You'll want to know: উৎসর্গ করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C329-SHESHPATA329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**উৎসর্গ করা** — *utsôrgô kôrā* — "to dedicate".

> লেখক বইটা তাঁর দাদুকে উৎসর্গ করলেন।
>
> *lekhôk bôiṭā tā̃r dāduke utsôrgô kôrlen.*
>
> The writer dedicated the book to his grandfather.

Past (he/she …): তিনি বইটা বাবাকে উৎসর্গ করলেন, *tini bôiṭā bābāke utsôrgô kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উৎসর্গ করা** (*utsôrgô kôrā*) puts Sanskrit *utsarga*, 'a pouring forth, a letting go' (Monier-Williams), before করা; to dedicate a book is to give it away to someone.
