## You'll want to know: বঞ্চিত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C352-BOISHOMMO352-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বঞ্চিত** — *bônchitô* — "deprived (of)".

> গ্রামের মানুষ ভালো হাসপাতাল থেকে বঞ্চিত।
>
> *grāmer mānush bhālo hāspātāl theke bônchitô.*
>
> Villagers are deprived of a good hospital.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বঞ্চিত** (*bônchitô*) is Sanskrit *vañcita*, deceived or cheated (Monier-Williams). The Bengali sense, deprived of something, is not one the dictionary gives; what is missing takes থেকে.
