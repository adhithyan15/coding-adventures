## You'll want to know: o amanhecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C275-ENTARDECER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o amanhecer** — "the dawn, daybreak".

> Saímos de casa ao amanhecer.
>
> *We left home at dawn.*

Also a verb: *amanhece cedo no verão* — it gets light early in summer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amanhecer* is built on *manhã* "morning" (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *māneāna*, from Latin *māne* "in the morning".
