## You'll want to know: bergen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C292-RETTUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**bergen** — "to bring out alive, to recover".

> Die Feuerwehr barg zwei Menschen aus dem Auto.
>
> *The fire brigade brought two people out of the car.*

Strong verb: *barg*, *hat geborgen*. *Geborgen* also means 'safe and sheltered'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bergen* is inherited from Old High German *bergan*, which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic *berganą* and a root meaning "to protect".
