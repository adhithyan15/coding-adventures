## You'll want to know: collectionner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C501-COLLECTIONS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**collectionner** — "to collect".

> Mon voisin collectionne les vieilles cartes postales.
>
> *My neighbour collects old postcards.*

As a hobby. To pick up someone is *aller chercher*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*collectionner* is built on *collection* (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *collēctiō* "a gathering", from *colligere* "to gather", built on *legere* "to gather, to read" (Lewis & Short).
