## You'll want to know: ఎంపిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C420-ENPIKA420-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎంపిక** — *empika* — "a choice; a selection".

> ఆ ఉద్యోగానికి ఆమెను తీసుకోవడం మంచి ఎంపిక.
>
> *ā udyōgāniki āmenu tīsukōvaḍaṁ mañci empika.*
>
> Taking her on for that job was a good choice.

ఎంపిక చేయు, to select; ఎంపికైన, selected.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎంపిక** (*empika*) is a Telugu noun whose origin is not settled; DEDR does not list it.
