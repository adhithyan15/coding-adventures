Arabic, chapter 435: Routes and fares.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مسار (masār) — a route; a lane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to issue, then the Arabic for to overtake.

You'll want to know: مسار.
مسار — masār — "a route; a lane".
غيرت الحافلة مسارها بسبب الثلج. ghayyarat al-ḥāfila masārahā bi-sabab ath-thalj. The bus changed its route because of the snow.

The word, taken apart.
مسار (masār) is built on the native Arabic root s-y-r, "to go, to travel" (Lane). The pattern mafʿal makes it the place of going.

The word, taken apart.
أجرة (ujra) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-j-r, "a wage, a reward" (Lane). A fare is the wage paid for the ride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a fare, then the Arabic for a subscription.

You'll want to know: ممر مشاة.
ممر مشاة — mamarr mushāh — "a pedestrian crossing".
عبر الأطفال الشارع من ممر المشاة. ʿabara al-aṭfāl ash-shāriʿ min mamarr al-mushāh. The children crossed the street at the pedestrian crossing.

The word, taken apart.
ممر مشاة (mamarr mushāh) is built from two native Arabic words: mamarr, "a passage", on the root m-r-r, "to pass" (Lane), and mushāh, "walkers", on the root m-sh-y, "to walk" (Lane).
