Mandarin Chinese, chapter 434: With a Tour Group.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
lǚyóutuán — tour group.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a Chinese person settled abroad, then the Mandarin for to go out to sea.

You'll want to know: lǚyóutuán.
lǚyóutuán — "tour group".
wǒ gēn yí ge lǚyóutuán zuò fēijī qù Běijīng — I'm flying to Beijing with a tour group.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
lǚyóutuán is built from 旅游 'to travel' and tuán 'a group', all inherited from Middle Chinese, ljoX, yuw and dwan (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last 'round'; the sense 'a group' is not given there.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.
gòuwù — to go shopping; to do some shopping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a tour group, then the Mandarin for to meet up as a group.

You'll want to know: gòuwù.
gòuwù — "to go shopping; to do some shopping".
xiàwǔ wǒmen zuò gōngjiāo qù shìzhōngxīn gòuwù — In the afternoon we're taking the bus into the centre to do some shopping.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gòuwù is built from gòu 'to buy' and wù 'a thing', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kuwH and mjut (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to buy back' and the second as the ten thousand things.
