## You'll want to know: けいざい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C365-KEIZAI365-01]; assesses=[] -->

**けいざい** — *keizai* — "the economy".

> **けいざいが よく なったら、しごとが ふえるでしょう。** — *keizai ga yoku nattara, shigoto ga fueru deshō* — "If the economy gets better, there will probably be more jobs."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けいざい** (*keizai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kei*, "pass thru" (Middle Chinese *keng*) and *zai*, "settle" (*tsejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
