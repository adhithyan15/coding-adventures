## You'll want to know: আগলানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C487-SOMPORKO487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**আগলানো** — *āglāno* — "to shelter; to guard protectively".

> মুরগিটা বাচ্চাদের আগলে রাখল।
>
> *murgiṭā bāchchāder āgle rākhlô.*
>
> The hen kept her chicks sheltered.

Past (he/she …): তিনি নাতিকে আগলে রাখলেন, *tini nātike āgle rākhlen*.

Usually with রাখা: আগলে রাখা, to keep sheltered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আগলানো** (*āglāno*) is a Bengali verb for keeping someone safe inside your guard; its origin is not settled.
