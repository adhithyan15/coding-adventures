## You'll want to know: la hazaña
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C566-AVENTURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la hazaña** — "the feat, the great deed".

> Cruzar el país a pie fue una hazaña.
>
> *Crossing the country on foot was a great feat.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hazaña* comes from Andalusi Arabic *ḥasána* 'a good deed', from Arabic *ḥasana* (EtymDB). The good deed grew into a heroic one.
