## You'll want to know: delicado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C473-MANEIRAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**delicado** — "polite, considerate; delicate".

> Foi muito delicado da tua parte telefonar.
>
> *It was very thoughtful of you to phone.*

Of things it means delicate or fragile: *uma situação delicada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delicado* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēlicātus* "charming, dainty, delicate" (EtymDB).
