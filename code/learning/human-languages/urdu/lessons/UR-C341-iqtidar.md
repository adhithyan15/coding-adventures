## You'll want to know: اقتدار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C341-HUKMRAN341-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتدار** — *iqtidār* — "the power to rule".

> وہ دس سال اقتدار میں رہا۔
>
> *voh das sāl iqtidār meṅ rahā.*
>
> He was in power for ten years.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتدار** (*iqtidār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iqtidār*, "being able; power".
