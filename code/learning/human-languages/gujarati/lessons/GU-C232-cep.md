## You'll want to know: ચેપ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C232-HEALTHY232-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચેપ** — *cep* — "infection, contagion".

> **શું તેને ચેપ છે?** — *śũ tene cep che?* — "Does he have an infection?"

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ચેપ** (*cep*) is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form for it.
