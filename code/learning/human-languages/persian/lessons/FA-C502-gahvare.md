## You'll want to know: گهواره
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C502-BABIES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گهواره** (*gahvâre*) — "a cradle".

> مادر بچه را در گهواره گذاشت.
>
> *mâdar bachche râ dar gahvâre gozâsht.*
>
> The mother put the baby in the cradle.

Plural *gahvârehâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gahvâre* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Middle Persian word meaning 'cot, cradle' (EtymDB).
