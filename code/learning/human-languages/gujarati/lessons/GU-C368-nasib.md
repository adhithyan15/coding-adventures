## You'll want to know: નસીબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C368-NASIB368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નસીબ** — *nasīb* — "luck".

> જો નસીબ સારું હશે તો આપણે જીતીશું.
>
> *jo nasīb sārũ haśe to āpaṇe jītīśũ.*
>
> If our luck holds, we will win.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નસીબ** (*nasīb*) is usually traced to Arabic *naṣīb*, "a share, a portion", through Persian: the share life deals out.
