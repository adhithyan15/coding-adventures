## You'll want to know: યાદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C528-YOJVU528-01]; assesses=[] -->

**યાદી** — *yādī* — "a list".

> જો તમે યાદી બનાવશો તો તમે કશું નહીં ભૂલો.
>
> *jo tame yādī banāvśo to tame kaśũ nahī̃ bhūlo.*
>
> If you make a list, you won't forget anything.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**યાદી** (*yādī*) is usually traced to Persian *yād*, "memory", with the ending *-ī*: something written down to remember by.
