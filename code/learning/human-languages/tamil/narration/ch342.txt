Tamil, chapter 342: Old Things Made New.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
புனரமை (puṉaramai) — to restore; to renovate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to dig out, then the Tamil for to come upon.

You'll want to know: புனரமை.
புனரமை — puṉaramai — "to restore; to renovate".
அரசு அந்தப் பழைய கோட்டையைப் புனரமைத்தது. aracu antap paḻaiya kōṭṭaiyaip puṉaramaittatu. The government restored that old fort.
Past (I …): புனரமைத்தேன், puṉaramaittēṉ.
புனர், again + அமை, to build.

The word, taken apart.
புனரமை (puṉaramai) joins Sanskrit punar, "back, again" (CDIAL 8273), with native அமை, "to be settled, built" (DEDR 161).

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
யுகம் (yukam) — an age; an era.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to engrave, then the Tamil for ancient.

You'll want to know: யுகம்.
யுகம் — yukam — "an age; an era".
இது கணினி யுகம். itu kaṇiṉi yukam. This is the computer age.
Also one of the long ages of Indian tradition.

The word, taken apart.
யுகம் (yukam) is borrowed from Sanskrit yuga, "yoke, a pair" (CDIAL 10482), which also came to mean an age of the world.

Lesson 5 of 5.
தொல்லியல் (tolliyal) — archaeology.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for ancient, then the Tamil for an age.

You'll want to know: தொல்லியல்.
தொல்லியல் — tolliyal — "archaeology".
அவள் பல்கலைக்கழகத்தில் தொல்லியல் படிக்கிறாள். avaḷ palkalaikkaḻakattil tolliyal paṭikkiṟāḷ. She studies archaeology at university.
தொல், ancient + இயல், a field of study.

The word, taken apart.
தொல்லியல் (tolliyal) is a Tamil compound built from தொல், "old, ancient" (DEDR 3516), and இயல், "nature, a branch of learning".
