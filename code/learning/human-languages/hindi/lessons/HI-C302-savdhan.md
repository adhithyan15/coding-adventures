## You'll want to know: सावधान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C302-AILAN302-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सावधान** — *sāvdhān* — "careful; on guard".

> ऐलान के बाद सब लोग सावधान हो गए।
>
> *ailān ke bād sab log sāvdhān ho gae.*
>
> After the announcement everyone was on their guard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सावधान** (*sāvdhān*) is Sanskrit *sāvadhāna*, "attentive, heedful" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole. Said alone on a sign it means "Beware!"
