## You'll want to know: ತಂತ್ರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C423-HANTA423-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಂತ್ರ** — *tantra* — "a strategy; a technique".

> ಆಟ ಗೆಲ್ಲಲು ನಮ್ಮ ತಂಡ ಹೊಸ ತಂತ್ರ ಬಳಸಿತು.
>
> *āṭa gellalu namma taṇḍa hosa tantra baḷasitu.*
>
> Our team used a new strategy to win the game.

ತಂತ್ರಜ್ಞಾನ, technology.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಂತ್ರ** (*tantra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tantra*, "a loom, the warp; a system, a device" (CDIAL 5663).
