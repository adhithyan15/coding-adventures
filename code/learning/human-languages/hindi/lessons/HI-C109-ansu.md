## You'll want to know: आँसू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C109-MOOD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आँसू** (*ā̃sū*) — "tears", a teardrop.

**आँख** is the eye you know; **आँसू** is what it sheds. Both open on the same nasal **आँ**.

## What you've built
