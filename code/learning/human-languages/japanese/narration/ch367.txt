Japanese, chapter 367: Being Clear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
はっきり (hakkiri) — clearly; plainly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to put first, then the Japanese for to be impatient.

You'll want to know: はっきり.
はっきり — hakkiri — "clearly; plainly".
しつもんが あったら、はっきり 言って ください。 — shitsumon ga attara, hakkiri itte kudasai — "If you have a question, please say it plainly."

The word, taken apart.
はっきり (hakkiri) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
じゅうぶん (jūbun) — enough; plenty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for clearly, then the Japanese for in other words.

You'll want to know: じゅうぶん.
じゅうぶん — jūbun — "enough; plenty".
じかんが じゅうぶん あったら、えきまで あるきます。 — jikan ga jūbun attara, eki made arukimasu — "If there is plenty of time, I walk to the station."
A na-adjective: な before a noun.

The word, taken apart.
じゅうぶん (jūbun) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are jū, "ten" (Middle Chinese dzyip) and bun, "part" (pjun) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
わりあい (wariai) — a proportion; a rate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for enough, then the Japanese for a degree.

You'll want to know: わりあい.
わりあい — wariai — "a proportion; a rate".
こうれいしゃの わりあいが ふえたら、ねんきんが すくなく なるでしょう。 — kōreisha no wariai ga fuetara, nenkin ga sukunaku naru deshō — "If the share of elderly people grows, pensions will probably shrink."

The word, taken apart.
わりあい (wariai) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from wari, "dividing (waru)" and ai, "meeting (au)", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
