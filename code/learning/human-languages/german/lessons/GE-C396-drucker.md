## You'll want to know: der Drucker
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C396-COMPUTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Drucker** — "the printer".

> Der Drucker hat kein Papier mehr.
>
> *The printer has run out of paper.*

Plural: *die Drucker*. Also a printer by trade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Drucker* is formed with *-er* on *drucken* "to print", of the family of *drücken* "to press", from Proto-Germanic *þrukjaną* "to press" (EtymDB).
