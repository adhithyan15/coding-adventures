## You'll want to know: そつぎょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C228-LEARN228-05]; assesses=[] -->

**そつぎょう** — *sotsugyō* — "graduation".

> **あねは らいねん そつぎょうです。** — *ane wa rainen sotsugyō desu* — "My elder sister graduates next year."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そつぎょう** (*sotsugyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sotsu*, "graduate, finish" (Middle Chinese *tswit*) and *gyō*, "work, vocation" (*ngjaep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
