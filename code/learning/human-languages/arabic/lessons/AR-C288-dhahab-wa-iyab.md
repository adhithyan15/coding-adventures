# تذكرة ذهاب وإياب (tadhkirat dhahāb wa-iyāb) — a return ticket

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-DIRECTIONS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Give the way to the station in two orders. (*Idhhab mustaqīman,
thumma khudh ash-shāriʿa al-awwala ʿalā al-yamīn*.)

[PAUSE 1s] You are at the station now, at the counter, and the person behind
the glass is waiting for you to speak.

## You'll want to know: تذكرة ذهاب وإياب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C118-THINGS118-02, AR-CONCEPT-C28-DHAHABA-01, AR-LEX-C207-SABAB207-01, AR-LEX-C61-VERBS61-03] -->

**تذكرة ذهاب وإياب** — *tadhkirat dhahāb wa-iyāb* — "a return ticket", word
for word "a ticket of going and coming back".

> **أريد تذكرة ذهاب وإياب، من فضلك.** — *urīdu tadhkirat dhahāb wa-iyāb, min
> faḍlik* — I'd like a return ticket, please.

*Tadhkira* ends in **ة**, and before the noun that follows it you hear its
*t*: *tadhkirat dhahāb*. For one way only, say *tadhkirat dhahāb faqaṭ*, "a
ticket of going only".

## Grammar Lens: three questions at the counter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C288-COUNTER-QUESTIONS-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C235-MAKAN235-03, AR-LEX-C226-ACT226-01, AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-01] -->

> **بكم التذكرة؟** — *bi-kam at-tadhkira?* — How much is the ticket?

> **من أي رصيف؟** — *min ayyi raṣīf?* — From which platform?

> **متى ينطلق القطار؟** — *matā yanṭaliqu al-qiṭār?* — When does the train
> leave?

*Bi-kam* is "for how much", *ayy* is "which", and *matā* is "when" in a
question. *Yanṭaliqu* is the present of *inṭalaqa*, to set off.

## The phrase, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذهاب** (*dhahāb*) is the verbal noun of *dhahaba*, "to go", on the root
*dh-h-b*. **إياب** (*iyāb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-w-b*, "to
return from a journey" (Lane), who gives *iyāb* among its verbal nouns and
notes that a *y* has taken the place of the root's *w*. The pair rhymes,
which is part of why it sticks.
