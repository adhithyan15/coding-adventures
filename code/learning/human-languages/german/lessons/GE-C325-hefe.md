## You'll want to know: die Hefe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C325-TEIG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Hefe** — "the yeast".

> Für das Brot braucht man frische Hefe.
>
> *For the bread you need fresh yeast.*

No plural. In Austria: *die Germ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hefe* is inherited from Old High German *hevo*, from Proto-Germanic *hafjô* "yeast" (EtymDB), related to *heben* "to lift": what makes dough rise.
