Gujarati, chapter 254: Music and song.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
સંગીત (sangīt) — music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get bored, then the Gujarati for to try out.

You'll want to know: સંગીત.
સંગીત — sangīt — "music".
ગઈકાલે અમે સંગીત સાંભળ્યું. — gaīkāle ame sangīt sā̃bhaḷyũ — "Yesterday we listened to music."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સંગીત (sangīt) is Sanskrit saṁgīta, "chorus, concert", taken over whole as a tatsama (CDIAL 12849); EtymDB also lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit.

વગાડવું (vagāḍvũ) — to play an instrument, to ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for music, then the Gujarati for song.

You'll want to know: વગાડવું.
વગાડવું — vagāḍvũ — "to play an instrument, to ring".
તેણે ઢોલ વગાડ્યો. — teṇe ḍhol vagāḍyo — "He played the drum."

The word, taken apart.
વગાડવું (vagāḍvũ), to make something sound, is the causative of the inherited vāgvũ, "to sound". CDIAL lists vāgvũ under Sanskrit vādyatē, "is made to sound" (CDIAL 11513).

Lesson 4 of 6.
વાંસળી (vā̃sḷī) — flute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for song, then the Gujarati for to play an instrument.

You'll want to know: વાંસળી.
વાંસળી — vā̃sḷī — "flute".
મેં કાલે વાંસળી વગાડી. — mẽ kāle vā̃sḷī vagāḍī — "I played the flute yesterday."

The word, taken apart.
વાંસળી (vā̃sḷī) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vaṁśī, "flute, pipe", built on vaṁśa, "bamboo"; CDIAL lists Gujarati vā̃sḷī, "pipe, flute" (CDIAL 11180).

Lesson 5 of 6.
તબલું (tablũ) — tabla drum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to play an instrument, then the Gujarati for flute.

You'll want to know: તબલું.
તબલું — tablũ — "tabla drum".
મારા ભાઈએ તબલું વગાડ્યું. — mārā bhāīe tablũ vagāḍyũ — "My brother played the tabla."
The pair is તબલાં, tablā̃.

The word, taken apart.
તબલું (tablũ) is a loan from Arabic ṭabl, "drum", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is ṭ-b-l, "to beat a drum" (Lane).
