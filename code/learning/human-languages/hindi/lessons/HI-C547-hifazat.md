# हिफ़ाज़त (hifāzat) — safekeeping; protection

## You'll want to know: हिफ़ाज़त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C547-CHAUKANNA547-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हिफ़ाज़त** — *hifāzat* — "safekeeping; protection".

> गहनों की हिफ़ाज़त के लिए हमने एक तिजोरी ख़रीदी।
>
> *Gahnoṁ kī hifāzat ke liye hamne ek tijorī kharīdī.*
>
> We bought a safe to keep the jewellery safe.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिफ़ाज़त** (*hifāzat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥifāẓa*, "guarding, keeping", through Persian.
