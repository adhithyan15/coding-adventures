Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 169: Making Rotis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पोवणो (povṇo) — to knead (dough); to make rotis by hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for holy basil, then the Marwadi for to water plants.

You'll want to know: पोवणो.
पोवणो — povṇo — "to knead (dough); to make rotis by hand".
मां दिनूगै रोटी पोवै है। mā̃ dinūgai roṭī povai hai. Mother makes the rotis by hand early in the morning.
Past (I …): म्हैं रोटी पोई, mhaĩ roṭī poī.

The word, taken apart.
पोवणो (povṇo) is inherited, by CDIAL's account, from Sanskrit pratudati, "cuts through, pierces"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari povaï twice, as "kneads (bread)" and as "threads (beads)" (CDIAL 8627).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बेलणो (belṇo) — to roll out (dough).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to water plants, then the Marwadi for to knead.

You'll want to know: बेलणो.
बेलणो — belṇo — "to roll out (dough)".
हूं रोज रोटी बेलूं हूं। hū̃ roj roṭī belū̃ hū̃. I roll out the rotis every day.
Past (I …): म्हैं रोटी बेली, mhaĩ roṭī belī.

The word, taken apart.
बेलणो (belṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vellayati, "causes to shake, kneads"; CDIAL lists Hindi belnā, "to roll out" (CDIAL 12121). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry. The rolling pin, बेलण, is its tool.

Lesson 3 of 5.
उकळणो (ukaḷṇo) — to boil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to knead, then the Marwadi for to roll out.

You'll want to know: उकळणो.
उकळणो — ukaḷṇo — "to boil".
हांडी मांय पाणी उकळै है। hā̃ḍī mā̃y pāṇī ukḷai hai. The water boils in the pot.
Past (it …): वो दूध उकळ्यो, vo dūdh ukaḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
उकळणो (ukaḷṇo) comes from a reconstructed utkalati, "goes out or up"; CDIAL lists Hindi ukalnā, "to boil over", and Gujarati ukaḷvũ (CDIAL 1716). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
