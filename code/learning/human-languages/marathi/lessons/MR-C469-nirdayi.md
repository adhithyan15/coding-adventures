## You'll want to know: निर्दयी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C469-KATHOR469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्दयी** — *nirdayī* — "merciless".

> कुत्र्याला मारणारा माणूस निर्दयी असतो.
>
> *kutryālā mārṇārā māṇūs nirdayī asto.*
>
> A man who beats a dog is merciless.

निर्, without, + दया, pity, + the ending -ई.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्दयी** (*nirdayī*) is built on the tatsama *nirdaya*, Sanskrit for "pitiless, cruel" (Monier-Williams), with the Marathi adjective ending *-ī*.
