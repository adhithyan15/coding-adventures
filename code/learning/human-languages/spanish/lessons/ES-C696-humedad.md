## You'll want to know: la humedad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C696-CLIMA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la humedad** — "the humidity, the damp".

> Con tanta humedad hace más calor.
>
> *With so much humidity it feels hotter.*

*Hay humedad en la pared* — there is damp in the wall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*humedad* is built on *húmedo* "humid", a learned borrowing from Latin *hūmidus* "moist".
