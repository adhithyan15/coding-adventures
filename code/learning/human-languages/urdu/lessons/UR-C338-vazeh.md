## You'll want to know: واضح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C338-MISAL338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**واضح** — *vāzeh* — "clear in meaning; plain".

> آپ کا جواب واضح نہیں ہے۔
>
> *āp kā javāb vāzeh nahīṅ hai.*
>
> Your answer is not clear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**واضح** (*vāzeh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wāḍiḥ*, "clear, evident".
