# आद्रियते (ādriyate) — holds in regard; respects

## You'll want to know: आद्रियते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C358-ACHARA358-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आद्रियते** — *ādriyate* — "holds in regard; respects".

> छात्राः गुरुम् आद्रियन्ते।
>
> *chātrāḥ gurum ādriyante.*
>
> The students hold their teacher in regard.

Past (he …): सः गुरुम् आदृतवान्, *saḥ gurum ādṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आद्रियते** (*ādriyate*) is built on *ā* and the root √dṛ, "to heed"; MW gives *ā-√dṛ* "to regard with attention" and "to respect, to honour" (MW).
