## You'll want to know: a dormida
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C428-PERNOITAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a dormida** — "the overnight stay; a place to sleep".

> A dormida custa trinta euros com pequeno-almoço.
>
> *A night costs thirty euros with breakfast.*

Built on *dormir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dormida* is built on *dormir* 'to sleep', which is inherited from Latin *dormīre* (Lewis & Short).
