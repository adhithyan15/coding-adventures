## You'll want to know: இடர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C541-KAPPEEDU541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**இடர்** — *iṭar* — "a risk; a hardship".

> இந்த முதலீட்டில் இடர் அதிகம்.
>
> *inta mutalīṭṭil iṭar atikam.*
>
> There is a lot of risk in this investment.

A literary word; in insurance it means the risk covered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இடர்** (*iṭar*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 435 gives இடர் (*iṭar*), "affliction, distress, trouble".
