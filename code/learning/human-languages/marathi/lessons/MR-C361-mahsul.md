## You'll want to know: महसूल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C361-ARTHIK361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**महसूल** — *mahsūl* — "revenue; land tax".

> सरकारला करातून मोठा महसूल मिळतो.
>
> *sarkārlā karātūn moṭhā mahsūl miḷto.*
>
> The government gets a large revenue from taxes.

Masculine.

Masculine: मोठा महसूल; mostly money the state takes in, from taxes and land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महसूल** (*mahsūl*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *maḥṣūl*, "produce, yield": what the land yields to the state.
