Russian, chapter 244: Ancient, Loud, Bright, Dark, Light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
древний (drévniy) — ancient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for low, then the Russian for ugly.

You'll want to know: древний.
древний (drévniy) — "ancient".

The word, taken apart.
древний (drévniy) — Old Church Slavonic древьнь, "ancient", with the Church Slavonic -ре-. Vasmer derives it, with a "perhaps", from drewos, compare Gothic triggws, "faithful", and Lithuanian drẽvė, "a hollow tree".

Lesson 4 of 5.
тёмный (tyómnyy) — dark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for loud, then the Russian for bright.

You'll want to know: тёмный.
тёмный (tyómnyy) — "dark".

The word, taken apart.
тёмный (tyómnyy) is built on тьма, "darkness", Proto-Slavic tĭma, which Vasmer relates to Lithuanian tamsà, Sanskrit támas, "darkness", and Latin tenebrae, "darkness".
