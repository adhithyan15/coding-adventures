## You'll want to know: دھکا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C394-GARHA394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دھکا** — *dhakkā* — "a shove; a jolt".

> گڑھے کی وجہ سے بس کو دھکا لگا۔
>
> *gaṛhe kī vajah se bas ko dhakkā lagā.*
>
> The bus gave a jolt because of the pothole.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھکا** (*dhakkā*) is inherited from *dhakk*, "push, strike", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6701); **دھکیلنا**, "to push", comes from the same source.
