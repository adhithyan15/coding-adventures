## You'll want to know: خاط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C538-IBRA538-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خاط** — *khāṭa* — "to sew".

> خاط الخياط زرا على قميصي.
>
> *khāṭa al-khayyāṭ zirran ʿalā qamīṣī.*
>
> The tailor sewed a button on my shirt.

Present: يخيط, *yakhīṭ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خاط** (*khāṭa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-y-ṭ*, "to sew" (Lane). Khayṭ, a thread, and khayyāṭ, a tailor, which you know, share the root.

## What you've built
