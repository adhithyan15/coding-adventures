# ઉપરાંત (uprā̃t) — in addition to

## You'll want to know: ઉપરાંત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C546-VAGAR546-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપરાંત** — *uprā̃t* — "in addition to".

> જો પગાર ઉપરાંત ઘર પણ મળશે તો હું આ નોકરી લઈશ.
>
> *jo pagār uprā̃t ghar paṇ maḷśe to hũ ā nokrī laīś.*
>
> If I get a house as well as the pay, I'll take this job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉપરાંત** (*uprā̃t*) follows its noun: **પગાર ઉપરાંત**, on top of the pay. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
