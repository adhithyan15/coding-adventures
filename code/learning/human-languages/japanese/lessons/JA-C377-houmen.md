## You'll want to know: ほうめん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C377-CHIEN377-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうめん** — *hōmen* — "a direction (of travel); an area".

> **みなと ほうめんの でんしゃに のりました。** — *minato hōmen no densha ni norimashita* — "I took a train going toward the harbour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうめん** (*hōmen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hō*, "direction" (Middle Chinese *pjang*) and *men*, "face" (*mjienH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
