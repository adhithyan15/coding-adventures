## You'll want to know: ঠোঁট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C467-THOT467-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠোঁট** — *ṭhõṭ* — "a lip".

> শীতে মীরার ঠোঁট শুকনো হয়ে যায়।
>
> *shīte mīrār ṭhõṭ shukno hôye jāy.*
>
> In winter Mira's lips get dry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠোঁট** (*ṭhõṭ*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a bird's beak, under Sanskrit *tuṇḍa*, "beak, snout" (CDIAL 5853); everyday Bengali uses it for the human lip.
