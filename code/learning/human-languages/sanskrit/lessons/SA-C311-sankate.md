## You'll want to know: शंकते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C311-KIMVADANTI311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शंकते** — *śaṅkate* — "doubts; suspects".

> मीरा किंवदन्तीं शंकते।
>
> *mīrā kiṃvadantīṃ śaṅkate.*
>
> Mira doubts the rumour.

Past (she …): सा किंवदन्तीं शंकितवती, *sā kiṃvadantīṃ śaṅkitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शंकते** (*śaṅkate*) comes from the root √śaṅk, "to be anxious about, doubt, distrust" (MW).
