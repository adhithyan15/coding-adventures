## You'll want to know: إعلام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C320-ILAM320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**إعلام** — *iʿlām* — "the media; informing".

> تكلم الإعلام كثيرا عن الحادث.
>
> *takallama al-iʿlām kathīran ʿan al-ḥādith.*
>
> The media talked a lot about the accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إعلام** (*iʿlām*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-l-m* (Lane), "to know", the root of *ʿilm*, knowledge. *Iʿlām* is the verbal noun of Form IV *aʿlama*, "to let someone know".
