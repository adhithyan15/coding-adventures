## You'll want to know: 那么
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C153-NAME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**那么** — *nàme* — "so; that (much)".

> **我没有他那么大** *wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme dà* — I'm not as old as he is.

> **那么大** *nàme dà* — that big

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**那么** (*nàme*) is built from 那 'that' and 么, the 么 of 什么. The Guangyun records 那 as *na* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'how; what'; how it came to mean 'that', and where the ending 么 comes from, are not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-BI-COMPARE-01, ZH-LEX-C87-MEIYOU-01] -->

没有 … 那么, with an adjective after it, says not as much as: 我没有他那么大.

It is the other side of 比: 他比我大, so 我没有他那么大.
