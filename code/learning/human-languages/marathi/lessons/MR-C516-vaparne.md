## You'll want to know: वापरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C516-PHON516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वापरणे** — *vāparṇe* — "to use".

> आजोबांनी पहिल्यांदा भ्रमणध्वनी वापरला.
>
> *ājobānnī pahilyāndā bhramaṇdhvanī vāparlā.*
>
> Grandfather used a mobile phone for the first time.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने संगणक वापरला, *tyāne saṅgaṇak vāparlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वापरणे** (*vāparṇe*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *vyāpārayati*, "employs". CDIAL lists under it the related Marathi *vāvarṇẽ*, "to manage, to do the business of" (CDIAL 12206), but not वापरणे itself, so the path is not settled.
