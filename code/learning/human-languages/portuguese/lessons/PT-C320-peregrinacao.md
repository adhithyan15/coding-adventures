## You'll want to know: a peregrinação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C320-CERIMONIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a peregrinação** — "the pilgrimage".

> Todos os anos, a minha tia faz uma peregrinação a pé.
>
> *Every year my aunt goes on a pilgrimage on foot.*

Built on *peregrino*, ‘pilgrim’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peregrinação* is a learned borrowing from Latin *peregrīnātiō* "a travelling abroad", from *peregrīnus* "foreign", built on *per* and *ager* "a field" (Lewis & Short).
