## Script — a lid, then two strokes crossing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JIAO-CROSS-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 交

**Two pieces, top to bottom.**

**The lid, two strokes.** A dot, then a long bar across, as on top of 文.

**The lower piece, four strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left and a short dot falling to the right, apart, like two feet. Then a long stroke falling to the left, and across it a long stroke falling to the right to the foot.

2 + 4 = six.
