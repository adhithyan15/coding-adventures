German, chapter 493: Zuneigung und Geborgenheit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Zuneigung — affection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to kiss, then the German for kiss.

You'll want to know: die Zuneigung.
die Zuneigung — "affection".
Zwischen den beiden wuchs langsam eine tiefe Zuneigung. A deep affection slowly grew between the two.
No plural in this sense. Built on sich zuneigen — to lean towards.

The word, taken apart.
Zuneigung is built on zu "towards" and neigen "to lean, to bow", inherited from Middle High German and Old High German neigen (EtymDB): leaning towards someone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Geborgenheit — feeling of safety, security.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for hug, then the German for tender.

You'll want to know: die Geborgenheit.
die Geborgenheit — "feeling of safety, security".
Bei meinen Großeltern spürte ich immer Geborgenheit. At my grandparents' I always felt safe and secure.
No plural. Built on geborgen — safe, sheltered.

The word, taken apart.
Geborgenheit is built on geborgen "sheltered", the participle of bergen "to shelter, to save", inherited from Old High German bergan, from Proto-Germanic berganą (EtymDB).

The word, taken apart.
Zusammenhalt is built on zusammen "together" and Halt "hold", from halten "to hold" (EtymDB), a Germanic verb from Proto-Germanic haldaną.
