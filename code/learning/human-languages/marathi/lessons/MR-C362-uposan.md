## You'll want to know: उपोषण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C362-ANDOLAN362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उपोषण** — *upoṣaṇ* — "a hunger strike; a fast".

> मागण्यांसाठी त्याने तीन दिवस उपोषण केलं.
>
> *māgaṇyāṁsāṭhī tyāne tīn divas upoṣaṇ kelaṁ.*
>
> He fasted for three days for the demands.

Neuter.

उपोषण करणे, to go on a fast; उपास, an ordinary fast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपोषण** (*upoṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *upoṣaṇa*, "fasting" (Monier-Williams).
