## You'll want to know: અત્યંત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C379-ATYANT379-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અત્યંત** — *atyant* — "extremely".

> જો તમે બપોરે ચાલશો તો તમને અત્યંત થાક લાગશે.
>
> *jo tame bapore cālśo to tamne atyant thāk lāgśe.*
>
> If you walk in the midday heat, you will be extremely tired.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અત્યંત** (*atyant*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *atyanta*, "beyond the proper end or limit" (Monier-Williams).
