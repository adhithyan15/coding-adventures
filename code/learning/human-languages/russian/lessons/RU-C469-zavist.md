## You'll want to know: зависть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C469-JEALOUSY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**зависть** (*závist*) — "envy".

> Коллеги говорили о его успехе без зависти.
>
> *Kollégi govoríli o yegó uspékhe bez závisti.*
>
> His colleagues talked about his success without envy.

Feminine. *С завистью* — enviously.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*зависть* is built on *за-* and the root of *видеть* 'to see', so envy is a way of looking at what another person has; *видеть* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *viděti*, from PIE *weyd- 'to see, to know' (EtymDB).
