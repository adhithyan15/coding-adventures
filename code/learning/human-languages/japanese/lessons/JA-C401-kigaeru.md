## You'll want to know: きがえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C401-CHOUSHOKU401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きがえる** — *kigaeru* — "to change one's clothes".

> **おふろの あとで ゆかたに きがえました。** — *ofuro no ato de yukata ni kigaemashita* — "After the bath I changed into a cotton kimono."

Polite **きがえます**, *kigaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あにが ゆかたに きがえました。**, *ani ga yukata ni kigaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きがえる** (*kigaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ki*, "wearing" (from *kiru*) and *gaeru*, "changing" (from *kaeru*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
