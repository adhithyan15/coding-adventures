## You'll want to know: ધમકી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C290-DAKU290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમકી** — *dhamkī* — "a threat".

> ડાકુઓએ ડ્રાઈવરને ધમકી આપી.
>
> *ḍākuoe ḍrāīvarne dhamkī āpī.*
>
> The bandits threatened the driver.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધમકી** (*dhamkī*) comes from *\*dhammakka*, "a threat", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun (CDIAL 6736). You give a threat: **ધમકી આપી**.
