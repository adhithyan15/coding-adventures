# A new message under a short clock

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-MESSAGE-PACING-01] -->

Close all earlier message pages. Set a three-minute timer and keep a blank
page. The task below uses known language, but the reader, speaker, and purpose
combination is not the old independent note. No romanization, sentence bank,
or copyable answer is available while the clock runs.

## Writing — independent timed message
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-A1-MESSAGE-TIMED-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-NAMASTE-01, PA-PHRASE-HOW-ARE-YOU-03, PA-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-LIKING, PA-LEX-PAANI, PA-LEX-PASAND, PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

As Aman, write Manan a **30–40-word** Gurmukhi message. Greet Manan and ask
about his wellbeing. Say that you like water and one other familiar thing.
Then suggest meeting again and close in a fitting tone. You may add other
already taught words to reach the band. Choose and order your own sentences;
the old supported Aman-to-Manan model did not include the water purpose.

Start the timer. Stop at three minutes, even if unfinished. Record the word
count and elapsed time on a separate line. Do not use a dictionary,
translator, spell-checker, or earlier page during this first attempt.

## Guided Practice — record before repair
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-MESSAGE-TIMED-01] -->

Before reopening anything, note whether the named reader, water detail,
meeting purpose, and 30–40-word band are present. Keep the first attempt
unchanged. Repair comes in the next short lesson, not inside the timed score.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-MESSAGE-TIMED-01] -->

This short message checkpoint is timed writing evidence, not a complete
twenty-minute A1 paper. The two full mocks and human validation remain
separate gates to readiness.
