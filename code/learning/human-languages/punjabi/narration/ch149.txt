Punjabi, chapter 149: To swim, To climb, To call, To use, To make.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā) — to swim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to lift, then the Punjabi for to jump.

You'll want to know: ਤਰਨਾ.
ਤਰਨਾ — tarnā — "to swim".

The word, taken apart.
ਤਰਨਾ (tarnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tarati, "crosses; floats, swims" (CDIAL 5702).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਬੁਲਾਉਣਾ (bulāuṇā) — to call.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to swim, then the Punjabi for to climb.

You'll want to know: ਬੁਲਾਉਣਾ.
ਬੁਲਾਉਣਾ — bulāuṇā — "to call".

The word, taken apart.
ਬੁਲਾਉਣਾ (bulāuṇā) is the causing form of bolṇā, "to speak": to call someone is to "make speak". CDIAL lists Hindi bulānā this way under a reconstructed bōll, "speak" (CDIAL 9321).

ਵਰਤਣਾ — vartṇā — "to use".

The word, taken apart.
ਵਰਤਣਾ (vartṇā) is usually linked with Sanskrit vartate, "turns, moves; takes place", but CDIAL lists no Punjabi form of that verb (CDIAL 11352), so its history is not settled.

ਬਣਾਉਣਾ (baṇāuṇā) is the causing form of baṇnā, "to be made, to become". CDIAL lists baṇnā, with Lahnda baṇṇaṇ, "to become", under Sanskrit vanati, "desires, gains, makes ready" (CDIAL 11260).
