## You'll want to know: ભમરડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C524-SANGRAH524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ભમરડો** — *bhamarḍo* — "a spinning top".

> મારો નાનો ભાઈ ભમરડા સાથે રમે છે.
>
> *māro nāno bhāī bhamarḍā sāthe rame che.*
>
> My little brother plays with a spinning top.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભમરડો** (*bhamarḍo*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *bhramara*, "whirling; a whirlpool", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for a spinning top (CDIAL 9650).
