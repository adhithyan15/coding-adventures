## You'll want to know: गोबर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C235-YARD235-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गोबर** — *gobar* — "cow dung".

> खेत मांय गोबर है।
>
> *khet mā̃y gobar hai.*
>
> There's cow dung on the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोबर** (*gobar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *govara*, "powdered cow dung"; CDIAL lists Hindi *gobar*, "cow dung". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4316).
