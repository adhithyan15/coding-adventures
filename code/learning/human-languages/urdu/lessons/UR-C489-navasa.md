## You'll want to know: نواسا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C489-KIN489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نواسا** — *navāsā* — "a grandson (a daughter's son)".

> نانا اپنے نواسے کے ساتھ کھیلتے ہیں۔
>
> *nānā apne navāse ke sāth khelte haiṅ.*
>
> My grandfather plays with his grandson.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نواسا** (*navāsā*) is usually traced to Persian *navāsa*, "grandchild".
