Lesson 1 of 5.
le magasin — a shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a town, then the French for a street.

You'll want to know: le magasin.
le magasin — "a shop".

The word, taken apart.
magasin from Italian magazzino, "a storehouse", from Arabic makhazin, "storehouses" — borrowed. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.
le train — a train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a school, then the French for a bank.

You'll want to know: le train.
le train — "a train".

The word, taken apart.
train — formed from the verb for "to drag, to pull along", which from Vulgar Latin traginare, from Latin trahere, "to draw" (Lewis & Short): a train is a line of things pulled along.
