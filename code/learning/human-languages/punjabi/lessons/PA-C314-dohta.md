## You'll want to know: ਦੋਹਤਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C314-BABY314-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੋਹਤਾ** — *dohtā* — "a daughter's son".

> ਨਾਨੀ ਆਪਣੇ ਦੋਹਤੇ ਨਾਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਖੇਡੀ।
>
> *nānī āpṇe dohte nāl bahut kheḍī.*
>
> Grandmother played a lot with her daughter's son.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੋਹਤਾ** (*dohtā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dauhitra*, "a daughter's son"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dohtā*, "daughter's son", under it (CDIAL 6605).
