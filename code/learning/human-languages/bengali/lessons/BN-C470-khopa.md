## You'll want to know: খোঁপা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C470-TAK470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**খোঁপা** — *khõpā* — "a bun; a knot of hair".

> মীরা চুলে একটা খোঁপা বাঁধল।
>
> *mīrā chule ekṭā khõpā bā̃dhlô.*
>
> Mira put her hair up in a bun.

খোঁপা বাঁধা, to put one's hair up in a bun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খোঁপা** (*khõpā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a knot of hair, under a reconstructed *khōppa*, "a bunch of hair" (CDIAL 3939), which CDIAL takes to be a borrowing from Dravidian.
