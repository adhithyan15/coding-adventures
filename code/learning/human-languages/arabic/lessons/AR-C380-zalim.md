## You'll want to know: ظالم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C380-ADALA380-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ظالم** — *ẓālim* — "unjust".

> كان القرار ظالما للعمال.
>
> *kāna al-qarār ẓāliman li-l-ʿummāl.*
>
> The decision was unjust to the workers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ظالم** (*ẓālim*) is built on the native Arabic root *ẓ-l-m*, "to put a thing in the wrong place" (Lane). *Ẓālim* is the active participle: one who does wrong, unjust.
