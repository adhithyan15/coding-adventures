## You'll want to know: ambāgēs, ambāgum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C452-ABERRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ambāgēs, ambāgum** — "a roundabout way, a winding path; long-winded talk".

> Pastor nōn rēctā viā sed per ambāgēs nōs dūcit.
>
> *The shepherd leads us not by the direct road but by a roundabout way.*

Third declension, feminine, mostly plural: *ambāgēs, ambāgum f.*; the singular appears only in the ablative *ambāge*. Also talk that goes round the point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ambāgēs* is built from *ambi-* 'around' and *agō* 'to drive, to go' (EtymDB): a going round. Lewis & Short give a roundabout way, then roundabout speech.
