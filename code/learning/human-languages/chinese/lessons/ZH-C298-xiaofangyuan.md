## You'll want to know: xiāofángyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C298-FIRE298-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāofángyuán* — "firefighter".

> *xiāofángyuán hěn kuài jiù lái le* — The firefighters were there in no time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiāofángyuán* is built from *xiāo* 'to put out', *fáng* 'to guard against' and *yuán* 'a member', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjew*, *bjang* and *hjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to put out', the second 'to keep off' and the third 'a count of things': one of those who put out fires.
