Kannada, chapter 524: Uncles and Aunts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ (cikkappa) — father's younger brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the pain of parting, then the Kannada for married life.

You'll want to know: ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ.
ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ — cikkappa — "father's younger brother".
ನನ್ನ ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ರೈತರು. nanna cikkappa haḷḷiyalli raitaru. My father's younger brother is a farmer in the village.
ಚಿಕ್ಕ, small + ಅಪ್ಪ, father: a "small father".

The word, taken apart.
ಚಿಕ್ಕಪ್ಪ (cikkappa) is a native Dravidian compound: cikka, "small, young" (DEDR 2495), and appa, "father" (DEDR 156).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸೋದರಳಿಯ (sōdaraḷiya) — a nephew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for mother's younger sister, then the Kannada for mother's elder sister.

You'll want to know: ಸೋದರಳಿಯ.
ಸೋದರಳಿಯ — sōdaraḷiya — "a nephew".
ನನ್ನ ಸೋದರಳಿಯ ಕಾಲೇಜಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಓದುತ್ತಾನೆ. nanna sōdaraḷiya kālējinalli ōduttāne. My nephew studies at college.
A sister's son: ಸೋದರ, sibling + ಅಳಿಯ. A niece is ಸೋದರ ಸೊಸೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೋದರಳಿಯ (sōdaraḷiya) joins Sanskrit sodara, "born of the same womb" (Monier-Williams), and the native aḷiya, "son-in-law; sister's son" (DEDR 2410).
