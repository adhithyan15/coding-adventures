## You'll want to know: ਹਾਕੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C351-BALL351-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਕੀ** — *hākī* — "hockey".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਹਾਕੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ hākī bahut pasand hai.*
>
> I like hockey very much.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਕੀ** (*hākī*) is borrowed from English *hockey*.
