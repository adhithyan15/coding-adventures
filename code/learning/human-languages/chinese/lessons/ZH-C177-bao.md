## You'll want to know: bǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C177-FULL177-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎo* — "full after eating; full up".

> *wǒ hěn bǎo bù chī le* — I'm full: no more for me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *paewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to have eaten much'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
