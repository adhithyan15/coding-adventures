## You'll want to know: સોસાયટી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C418-SOCIETY418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સોસાયટી** — *sosāyṭī* — "a housing society; a residential colony".

> અમારી સોસાયટીમાં ચાળીસ ઘર છે.
>
> *amārī sosāyṭīmā̃ cāḷīs ghar che.*
>
> There are forty houses in our housing society.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સોસાયટી** (*sosāyṭī*) is borrowed from English *society*; in Gujarat a સોસાયટી is a planned group of houses with its own gate and committee.
