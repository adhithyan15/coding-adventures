## You'll want to know: colocar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C496-HOGAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**colocar** — "to put, to place".

> Coloca los libros en la estantería.
>
> *Put the books on the shelf.*

It takes *qu* before *e*: *coloqué*.
