## You'll want to know: અંધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C475-SENSES475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અંધ** — *andh* — "blind (the respectful word)".

> અરુણે એક અંધ માણસને રસ્તા પર મદદ કરી.
>
> *Aruṇe ek andh māṇasne rastā par madad karī.*
>
> Arun helped a blind man in the street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is a tatsama, Sanskrit *andha*, "blind", as Monier-Williams records it; CDIAL lists the everyday inherited Gujarati forms *ā̃dhũ* and *ā̃dhḷũ* under the same word (CDIAL 385), but for a person the careful word is **અંધ**.
