## You'll want to know: वातावरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C436-UKADA436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वातावरण** — *vātāvaraṇ* — "the atmosphere; the weather".

> आज वातावरण ढगाळ आहे.
>
> *āj vātāvaraṇ ḍhagāḷ āhe.*
>
> Today the weather is cloudy.

Neuter.

Also the mood of a place: घरात आनंदी वातावरण होतं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वातावरण** (*vātāvaraṇ*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *vāta*, "wind, air", and *āvaraṇa*, "a covering" (Monier-Williams): the cover of air.
