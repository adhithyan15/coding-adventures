## You'll want to know: बांधकाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C421-BANDHKAM421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बांधकाम** — *bāndhkām* — "construction".

> आमच्या गल्लीत नवीन बांधकाम चालू आहे.
>
> *āmcyā gallīt navīn bāndhkām cālū āhe.*
>
> New construction is going on in our lane.

Neuter.

बांधकाम चालू आहे, building work is going on; बांधकाम करणे, to build.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बांधकाम** (*bāndhkām*) is built from the stem of **बांधणे**, "to tie, to build", and **काम**, "work", inherited from Sanskrit *karman* (CDIAL 2892).
