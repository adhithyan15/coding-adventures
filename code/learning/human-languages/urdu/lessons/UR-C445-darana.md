# ڈرانا (ḍarānā) — to frighten

## You'll want to know: ڈرانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C445-FEAR445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈرانا** — *ḍarānā* — "to frighten".

> بلی نے چوہے کو ڈرایا۔
>
> *billī ne chūhe ko ḍarāyā.*
>
> The cat frightened the mouse.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچوں کو ڈرایا, *us ne bachchoṅ ko ḍarāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈرانا** (*ḍarānā*) is the causative of *ḍarnā*, "to be afraid", inherited from Sanskrit *darati*, "is frightened" (CDIAL 6190); the noun **ڈر**, "fear", belongs to the same word.
