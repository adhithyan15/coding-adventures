## You'll want to know: il banchetto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C317-SPUMANTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il banchetto** — "the banquet, the feast".

> Il banchetto è durato fino a notte.
>
> *The banquet went on into the night.*

A little *banco*, ‘bench, table’; also a market stall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*banchetto* is a little *banco* "bench" (EtymDB), which comes from a Longobard word for bench.
