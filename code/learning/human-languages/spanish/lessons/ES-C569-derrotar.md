## You'll want to know: derrotar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C569-LUCHA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**derrotar** — "to defeat".

> Nuestro club derrotó al favorito.
>
> *Our club beat the favourite.*

*Derrotar a alguien* — to defeat someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*derrotar* is built on *derrota* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *dīrumpere* 'to break apart', from *rumpere* 'to break'.
