## You'll want to know: నిధి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C366-NIDHI366-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నిధి** — *nidhi* — "a treasure; a fund".

> పాత కోటలో ఒక నిధి ఉందని పుకారు ఉంది.
>
> *pāta kōṭalō oka nidhi undani pukāru undi.*
>
> There is a rumour that a treasure lies in the old fort.

Also a fund: విద్యా నిధి, an education fund.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిధి** (*nidhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nidhi*, "a setting down; a hoard" (CDIAL 7207).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
