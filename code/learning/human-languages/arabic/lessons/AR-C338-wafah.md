## You'll want to know: وفاة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C338-MAWTA338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**وفاة** — *wafāh* — "a death, a passing".

> بكى الجميع بعد وفاة الجدة.
>
> *bakā al-jamīʿ baʿda wafāt al-jadda.*
>
> Everyone wept after the grandmother’s death.

The polite noun beside موت.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وفاة** (*wafāh*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-f-y* (Lane), "to be complete, to fulfil". *Wafāh* is a life brought to its full term.
