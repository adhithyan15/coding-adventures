# Review — what happened, and who did it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to the market". (*kal maiṁ bāzār gayā*.) Then "she came" and "what happened?" (*vah āī*, *kyā huā?*)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-STEMS-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, HI-CONCEPT-C36-GHAR-01, HI-CONCEPT-C29-SHAAM-01, HI-LEX-C69-GOING-03, HI-LEX-C209-THG209-05, HI-CONCEPT-C37-KHAANA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I came home in the evening, and then I slept.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira ate a roti. Arun ate the meal.]
- [YOU SAY: What did you do yesterday? I went to the market.]
- [YOU SAY: She saw Arun at the station.]
- [YOU SAY: He said that he would come tomorrow.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-STEMS-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which past follows the one who went? (A verb with no thing: **वह गई**.) Which follows the thing? (The past with **ने**: **मीरा ने रोटी खाई**.) Now the other way round: in **उसने खाना खाया**, which word tells you that **खाना** is masculine? (**खाया**.) And when the thing takes **को**? (The verb stays **-आ**: **उसने मीरा को देखा**.)
