## You'll want to know: उल्लास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C453-ANAND453-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्लास** — *ullās* — "elation; high spirits".

> त्योहार के दिन पूरे गाँव में उल्लास था।
>
> *tyohār ke din pūre gā̃v meṁ ullās thā.*
>
> On the day of the festival the whole village was in high spirits.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्लास** (*ullās*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *ullāsa*, "light, splendour" and also "joy, happiness, merriness" (Monier-Williams): joy that shines out.
