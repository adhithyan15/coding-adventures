## You'll want to know: править
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C325-IMPERIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**править** (*právit*) — "to rule, to govern; to correct".

> Царь правил страной тридцать лет.
>
> *Tsar právil stranóy trídtsat let.*
>
> The tsar ruled the country for thirty years.

Imperfective. It takes the instrumental: *править страной*. Also to correct a text: *править статью*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*править* (EtymDB: "to govern") is built on *правый* "right, straight", an inherited Slavic word: to keep things straight.
