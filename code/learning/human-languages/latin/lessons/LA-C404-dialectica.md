## You'll want to know: dialectica, dialecticae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C404-RHETORICA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**dialectica, dialecticae** — "logic, dialectic".

> Dialectica docet vēra ā falsīs distinguere.
>
> *Logic teaches us to tell true from false.*

*dialectica, dialecticae* f., from Greek *dialektikē*, the art of discussion: the logic of the philosophers. English *dialectic*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dialectica* was borrowed from Greek *dialektikē* "the art of discussion" (Lewis & Short), built on a verb meaning "to talk things through". English *dialectic* comes from it.
