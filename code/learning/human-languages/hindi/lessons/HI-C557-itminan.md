## You'll want to know: इत्मीनान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C557-ANDESHA557-02]; assesses=[] -->

**इत्मीनान** — *itmīnān* — "peace of mind, ease".

> सब काम पूरा करके मैं इत्मीनान से सोया।
>
> *Sab kām pūrā karke maiṁ itmīnān se soyā.*
>
> With all the work finished I slept with my mind at rest.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इत्मीनान** (*itmīnān*) is usually traced to Arabic *iṭmiʾnān*, "tranquillity", through Persian.
