## You'll want to know: أرشد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C437-BUSALA437-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أرشد** — *arshada* — "to guide".

> أرشدنا رجل إلى الفندق.
>
> *arshadanā rajul ilā al-funduq.*
>
> A man guided us to the hotel.

Present: يرشد, *yurshid*.

It takes *ilā* before the place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أرشد** (*arshada*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-sh-d*, "to follow the right way" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to lead someone the right way".
