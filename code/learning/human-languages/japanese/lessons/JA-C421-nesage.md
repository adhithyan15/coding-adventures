## You'll want to know: ねさげ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C421-NEGIRU421-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ねさげ** — *nesage* — "a price cut".

> **この みせは ゆうがた ねさげを します。** — *kono mise wa yūgata nesage o shimasu* — "This shop cuts its prices in the evening."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねさげ** (*nesage*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ne*, "price" and *sage*, "lowering" (from *sageru*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
