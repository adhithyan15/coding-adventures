## You'll want to know: róngyì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C144-MATTER144-02]; assesses=[] -->

*róngyì* — "easy".

> *hànzì bù róngyì* — Chinese characters aren't easy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*róngyì* is built from *róng* 'to hold' and *yì* 'easy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yowng* and *yeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second 'hard and easy' and 'simple'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
