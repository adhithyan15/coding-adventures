# ભૂંસવું (bhū̃svũ) — to rub out, to erase

## You'll want to know: ભૂંસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C245-SPELL245-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ભૂંસવું** — *bhū̃svũ* — "to rub out, to erase".

> **તે ખોટો શબ્દ ભૂંસે છે.** — *te khoṭo śabda bhū̃se che* — "He rubs out the wrong word."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ભૂંસવું** (*bhū̃svũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this form and meaning, and our other sources do not trace it.
