## You'll want to know: کمال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C381-KAMYAB381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کمال** — *kamāl* — "a marvel; excellence".

> اگر وہ یہ کر سکا تو کمال ہو گا۔
>
> *agar voh yih kar sakā to kamāl ho gā.*
>
> If he manages this, it will be a marvel.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کمال** (*kamāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *kamāl*, "perfection".
