## You'll want to know: सज़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C295-ADALAT295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सज़ा** — *sazā* — "a punishment; a sentence".

> अगर आपको सज़ा का डर है, तो सच कहिए।
>
> *agar āpko sazā kā ḍar hai, to sac kahiye.*
>
> If you are afraid of punishment, tell the truth.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सज़ा** (*sazā*) is usually traced to Persian *sazā*, "what is fitting, what one deserves". It is feminine: **अदालत ने चोर को दो साल की सज़ा दी**, the court gave the thief two years.
