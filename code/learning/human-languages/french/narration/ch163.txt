Lesson 1 of 5.
l'appartement — a flat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for married, then the French for divorced.

You'll want to know: l'appartement.
l'appartement — "a flat".

The word, taken apart.
appartement from Italian appartamento (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed; built on Latin ad and pars, "a part": a set of rooms apart.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la salle à manger — a dining room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for divorced, then the French for a flat.

You'll want to know: la salle à manger.
la salle à manger — "a dining room".

The phrase, taken apart.
salle à manger — "room for eating": salle, "a room", à, "for", and manger, "to eat". Salle from Frankish sal, "hall", à from Latin ad, and manger from Latin manducāre, "to chew" (EtymDB 2.1).
