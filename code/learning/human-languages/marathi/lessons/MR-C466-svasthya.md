## You'll want to know: स्वास्थ्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C466-SAMADHAN466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वास्थ्य** — *svāsthya* — "well-being; ease".

> गोंगाटामुळे मनाचं स्वास्थ्य बिघडतं.
>
> *goṅgāṭāmuḷe manācaṁ svāsthya bighaḍtaṁ.*
>
> Noise upsets one's peace of mind.

Neuter.

मनाचं स्वास्थ्य, peace of mind; also health of body.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वास्थ्य** (*svāsthya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *svāsthya*, "sound state of body or soul; ease, contentment" (Monier-Williams).
