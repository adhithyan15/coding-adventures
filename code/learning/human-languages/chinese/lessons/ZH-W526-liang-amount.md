## Script — 日, a long bar, then 里
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-LIANG-AMOUNT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 量

**Three pieces, top to bottom.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The top piece, 日, four strokes**, small, as you wrote it: a vertical, a bar that turns down, a bar across the middle, a bar closing the foot.

**One long bar** across the middle, the widest stroke of the character.

**The bottom piece, 里, seven strokes**, as you wrote it: a vertical, a bar that turns down, a bar across the middle, a bar closing the box, a vertical down through the middle, a short bar, then the long bar at the foot.

4 + 1 + 7 = twelve.
