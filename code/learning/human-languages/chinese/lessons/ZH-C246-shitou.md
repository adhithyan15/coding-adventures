## You'll want to know: shítou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C246-HILLS246-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shítou* — "stone; rock".

> *nà kuài shítou hěn dà* — That rock is huge.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shítou* is built from *shí* 'stone' and 头, here a light noun ending, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyek* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as the body of a mountain.
