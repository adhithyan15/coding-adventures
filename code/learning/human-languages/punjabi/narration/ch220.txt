Punjabi, chapter 220: Aman Bought a Book.
7 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Return to the small marker — retrieve the familiar Aman did the work and to buy by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: Aman ne kamm kītā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ, "Aman did the work".

Grammar Lens.
After a name, the doer of a past like ਕੀਤਾ takes the small marker ਨੇ. After ਮੈਂ it takes nothing: ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ. Hold one more verb ready: ਖ਼ਰੀਦਣਾ, "to buy".

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: Aman did the work]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ.
[your turn — hear: I drank tea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ.
[your turn — hear: to buy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਖ਼ਰੀਦਣਾ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Where does ਨੇ go? (Right after the named doer.)


Lesson 2 of 7.
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ — Aman bought a book.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say Aman did the work. Then to buy and a book.

Grammar Lens: ਨੇ beyond the work.
The marker works with any verb that is done to something. The past still agrees with the thing: ਕਿਤਾਬ is feminine, so ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ.
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ। — Aman ne kitāb kharīdī. — Aman bought a book.
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ। — Aman ne chāh pītī. — Aman drank tea.
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਸੇਬ ਖਾਧਾ। — Aman ne seb khādhā. — Aman ate an apple.
Going and coming are not done to anything, so they take no ਨੇ: ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: Aman ate a roti]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ।.
[your turn — hear: Aman went to school]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਸਕੂਲ ਗਿਆ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ। (Aman ne kitāb kharīdī.)]
Point to ਨੇ, then to the thing the ending agrees with.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ। (Aman ne kitāb kharīdī.) once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Does ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ take ਨੇ? (No: going is not done to anything.)


Lesson 3 of 7.
ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) — he or she, before the marker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say Aman bought a book. Then he, she, ਉਹ, and the familiar ਉਸ.

Grammar Lens: ਉਹ turns into ਉਸ.
Before ਨੇ, ਉਹ turns into ਉਸ. The verb still agrees with the thing:
ਉਸ ਨੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ। — us ne duddh pītā. — He drank milk; she drank milk.
ਉਸ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ। — us ne kitāb kharīdī. — He bought a book; she bought a book.
So ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) can mean he or she: the sentence does not say which.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: she ate a roti]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ।.
[your turn — hear: he drank tea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
He before ਨੇ? (ਉਸ.) He went? (ਉਹ ਗਿਆ, with no ਨੇ.)

Lesson 5 of 7.
ਲਿਆ, ਦਿੱਤਾ — took, gave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say to take and to give: ਲੈਣਾ, ਦੇਣਾ. Then Aman bought a book.

Grammar Lens: two short pasts.
ਲੈਣਾ and ਦੇਣਾ have short pasts of their own: ਲਿਆ, ਦਿੱਤਾ. They agree with the thing, like every past you have met:
ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਲਿਆ। — maiṁ pāṇī liā. — I took water.
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ। — Aman ne paisā dittā. — Aman gave money.
ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਦਿੱਤੀ। — maiṁ Aman nūṁ kitāb dittī. — I gave Aman a book.
The one who receives takes ਨੂੰ. CDIAL lists Punjabi dittā, "given" (CDIAL 6140).

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: she gave the book]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) ਕਿਤਾਬ ਦਿੱਤੀ।.
[your turn — hear: I took milk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਲਿਆ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਦਿੱਤੀ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਲਿਆ and ਦਿੱਤਾ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Took? (ਲਿਆ.) Gave, a book? (ਦਿੱਤੀ.)


Lesson 6 of 7.
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ? (kī tusīṁ roṭī khādhī?) — did you eat the roti?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask do you drink tea? Then say you ate a roti.

Grammar Lens: ਕੀ in the past.
ਕੀ turns a past sentence into a question the same way it turns a present one:
ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ? — kī tusīṁ roṭī khādhī? — Did you eat the roti?
ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ। — hāṁ jī, maiṁ roṭī khādhī. — Yes, I ate the roti.
ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਅਜੇ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਧੀ। — nahīṁ jī, maiṁ aje roṭī nahīṁ khādhī. — No, I haven't eaten the roti yet.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: did you drink tea?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ?
[your turn — hear: did Aman buy the book?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ?
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਅਜੇ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਧੀ।]
Give its meaning.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ? (kī tusīṁ roṭī khādhī?) once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask a guest whether they ate the roti. Then answer as the guest.


Lesson 7 of 7.
Who did what: a four-skill payoff — say and ask what someone bought, gave, took, ate or drank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say Aman bought a book, then she drank tea.

Guided Practice: four skills.
[your turn — hear: Aman gave money]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।.
[your turn — hear: did he drink milk?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੀ ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ?
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉਸ ਨੇ (us ne) ਕਿਤਾਬ ਲਈ ਪੈਸਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ।]
Give its meaning: he paid money for the book.
[once you have stopped driving — write: cover the line and write ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਖ਼ਰੀਦੀ। (Aman ne kitāb kharīdī.)]
Check: ਨੇ after the named doer, and the ending after the thing.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Tell what Aman did yesterday: he went to the market, bought a book, and drank tea.
