## You'll want to know: syrtis, syrtis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C443-FLUCTUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**syrtis, syrtis** — "a sandbank, a shoal".

> Gubernātor syrtēs vītat et nāvem in altum dūcit.
>
> *The helmsman avoids the sandbanks and steers the ship into deep water.*

Third declension, feminine: *syrtis, syrtis f.* Any sandbank, and above all the two dangerous sandy gulfs off the African coast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*syrtis* was borrowed from Greek *syrtis* 'sandbank' (Lewis & Short), above all the two gulfs off Africa full of shoals. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
