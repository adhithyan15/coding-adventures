## You'll want to know: شرجی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C477-SHARJI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شرجی** (*sharji*) — "muggy, humid (and hot)".

> تابستان در بندر هوا شرجی است.
>
> *Tâbestân dar bandar havâ sharji ast.*
>
> In summer the air in the port is muggy.

Said of the Gulf coast in summer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *sharji* is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.
