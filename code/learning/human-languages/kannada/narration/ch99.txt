Kannada, chapter 99: The Body Again.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಸಿರು (usiru) — breath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for alarm, then the Kannada for peace of mind.

You'll want to know: ಉಸಿರು.
ಉಸಿರು (usiru) — "breath".
ಉಸಿರು ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ (usiru tegedukoḷḷi) — "take a breath", with ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ, "take", as in ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಸಿರು (usiru) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil uy (DEDR 645). DEDR lists Kannada usir, usuru, "breath, life", with Telugu usuru.

What you've built.
What the body does all day.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಚರ್ಮ (carma) — skin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for peace of mind, then the Kannada for breath.

You'll want to know: ಚರ್ಮ.
ಚರ್ಮ (carma) — "skin". The ರ್ sits on top of ಮ as a small hook.

The word, taken apart.
ಚರ್ಮ (carma) is Sanskrit carman, "hide, skin", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4701). Kannada drops the final -n.

What you've built.
The body's cover.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ರಕ್ತ (rakta) — blood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for breath, then the Kannada for skin.

You'll want to know: ರಕ್ತ.
ರಕ್ತ (rakta) — "blood". The ಕ್ತ is k and t stacked into one.

The word, taken apart.
ರಕ್ತ (rakta) is Sanskrit rakta, "coloured, red; blood", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10539). Blood was named for its colour.

What you've built.
A word for the doctor and the hospital.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಹೊಟ್ಟೆನೋವು (hoṭṭenōvu) — a stomach ache.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for skin, then the Kannada for blood.

You'll want to know: ಹೊಟ್ಟೆನೋವು.
ಹೊಟ್ಟೆನೋವು (hoṭṭenōvu) — "a stomach ache", built exactly like ತಲೆನೋವು: ಹೊಟ್ಟೆ (hoṭṭe), "stomach", with ನೋವು, "pain".

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊಟ್ಟೆನೋವು (hoṭṭenōvu) is hoṭṭe, "belly" (DEDR 4494, older Kannada poṭṭe), and nōvu, "pain", native Dravidian (DEDR 3793). DEDR 4494 compares Prakrit poṭṭa, "belly".

What you've built.
The pattern of headache, used again.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ತೂಕ (tūka) — weight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for blood, then the Kannada for a stomach ache.

You'll want to know: ತೂಕ.
ತೂಕ (tūka) — "weight".
ನಿಮ್ಮ ತೂಕ ಎಷ್ಟು? (nimma tūka eṣṭu?) — "what is your weight?", a doctor's question, with ಎಷ್ಟು, "how much".

The word, taken apart.
ತೂಕ (tūka) is native Dravidian, of the root tūgu, "to weigh, swing", which DEDR 3376 lists; the entry also has Tamil tūṅku.

What you've built.
A number the doctor asks for.
