Urdu, chapter 408: North, South, East and West.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سمت (simt) — a direction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a repair, then the Urdu for to move (something) aside.

You'll want to know: سمت.
سمت — simt — "a direction".
اسٹیشن کس سمت میں ہے؟ isṭeshan kis simt meṅ hai? Which direction is the station in?
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
سمت (simt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic samt, "a way, a direction".

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 5 of 5.
مغرب (maghrib) — the west.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the south, then the Urdu for the east.

You'll want to know: مغرب.
مغرب — maghrib — "the west".
شام کو سورج مغرب میں چھپتا ہے۔ shām ko sūraj maghrib meṅ chhuptā hai. In the evening the sun goes down in the west.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مغرب (maghrib) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maghrib, "the place where the sun sets"; it is also the name of the prayer at sunset.
