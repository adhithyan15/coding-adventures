## You'll want to know: वमति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C477-BHRAMA477-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वमति** — *vamati* — "vomits".

> बालः मलिनं जलं पिबति, अनन्तरं वमति।
>
> *bālaḥ malinaṃ jalaṃ pibati, anantaraṃ vamati.*
>
> The child drinks dirty water and afterwards vomits.

Past (he …): सः वान्तवान्, *saḥ vāntavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वमति** (*vamati*) comes from the root √vam, "to vomit", the root of **वमनम्**; MW compares Greek *emeō* and Latin *vomere* (MW).
