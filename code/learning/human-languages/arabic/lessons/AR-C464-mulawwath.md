## You'll want to know: ملوث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C464-TALAWWUTH464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ملوث** — *mulawwath* — "polluted".

> كان ماء النهر ملوثا.
>
> *kāna māʾ an-nahr mulawwathan.*
>
> The river water was polluted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملوث** (*mulawwath*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-w-th*, "to soil, to stain" (Lane). It is the Form II passive participle.
