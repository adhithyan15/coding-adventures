Punjabi, chapter 273: Two Kilos, Please.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਤੋਲਣਾ (tolṇā) — to weigh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a rickshaw, then the Punjabi for a park.

You'll want to know: ਤੋਲਣਾ.
ਤੋਲਣਾ — tolṇā — "to weigh".
ਦੁਕਾਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੁੰਡਾ ਆਲੂ ਤੋਲਦਾ ਹੈ। dukān vich muṇḍā ālū toldā hai. In the shop the boy weighs the potatoes.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਲੂ ਤੋਲੇ, maiṁ ālū tole.

The word, taken apart.
ਤੋਲਣਾ (tolṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit tolayati, "lifts, weighs"; CDIAL lists Punjabi toḷnā, "to weigh", under it (CDIAL 5979).
