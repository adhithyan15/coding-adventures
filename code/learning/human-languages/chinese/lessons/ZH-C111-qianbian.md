## You'll want to know: 前边
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-01]; assesses=[] -->

**前边** — *qiánbian* — "in front; ahead".

> **前边是火车站** *qiánbian shì huǒchēzhàn* — The railway station is up ahead.

The new character, in pieces: 边 — lì, the foot of 男, inside the walking piece that runs down the left and along the foot of 这 and 进. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**前边** (*qiánbian*) is built from 前 'front' and 边 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzen* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'first, before' and the second 'an edge'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
