## You'll want to know: ஓய்வு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C87-UNWELL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ஓய்வு** (*ōyvu*) — "rest".

**ஓய்வு எடுங்கள்** — "please take some rest", what you say to someone who is unwell.
