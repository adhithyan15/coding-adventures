## You'll want to know: ফিরতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C387-POTH387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিরতি** — *phirti* — "return (of a journey); on the way back".

> ফিরতি পথে আমরা মামার বাড়িতে থামলাম।
>
> *phirti pôthe āmrā māmār bāṛite thāmlām.*
>
> On the way back we stopped at my uncle's house.

It stands before its noun: ফিরতি টিকিট, a return ticket; ফিরতি ট্রেন, the train back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিরতি** (*phirti*) is built on the stem ফির- of **ফেরা**, "to return", which CDIAL lists as Bengali *phirā*, "to turn", under the reconstructed *phirati*, "moves, wanders, turns" (CDIAL 9078).
