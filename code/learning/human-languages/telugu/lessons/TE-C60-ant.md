## You'll want to know: చీమ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C60-ANIMAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**చీమ** (*cīma*) — "an ant".

చీమ is the ant. After a గేదె and a కోడి it is a jump in scale worth making, because the same plain word-making covers whatever lives in a house, however small it is.

The word is inherited. Telugu has a stock comparison about a చీమ carrying many times its own weight, and it is the ordinary way to praise somebody small who works hard.

A line of చీమలు across a floor usually ends at a పండు somebody left out.
