## You'll want to know: shōuyíntái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C202-TILL202-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shōuyíntái* — "checkout; till".

> *shōuyíntái zài ménkǒu* — The checkout is by the door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shōuyíntái* is built from *shōu* 'to take in', *yín* 'silver, money' and *tái* 'a counter', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuw*, *ngin* and *doj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the last as a raised square platform of earth.
