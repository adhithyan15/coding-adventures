## You'll want to know: gâter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C494-EDUCATION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gâter** — "to spoil (a child); to ruin".

> Mon oncle gâte beaucoup ses neveux.
>
> *My uncle really spoils his nephews.*

*Un enfant gâté* — a spoilt child. *Il fait un temps gâté* — the weather has turned bad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gâter* is inherited from Latin *vastāre* "to lay waste" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); its first sound was reshaped under a related Germanic word.
