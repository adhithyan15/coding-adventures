## You'll want to know: हिंसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C352-AHIMSA352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंसा** — *hiṃsā* — "violence; harm".

> हिंसा कदापि उचिता न भवति।
>
> *hiṃsā kadāpi ucitā na bhavati.*
>
> Violence is not right at any time.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिंसा** (*hiṃsā*) comes from the root √hiṃs, "to injure"; MW gives *hiṃsā* "injury, harm, hurt, mischief" (MW).
