Urdu, chapter 43: Mum, Son, Daughter, Grandfather, Grandmother.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
امی (ammī) — mum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a father, then the Urdu for dad.

You'll want to know: امی.
امی — ammī — "mum".

The word, taken apart.
امی (ammī) is the fond form of ammā, "mother", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit ambā, "mother" (CDIAL 574). CDIAL lists ammā, not ammī.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بیٹا (beṭā) — a son.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for dad, then the Urdu for mum.

You'll want to know: بیٹا.
بیٹا — beṭā — "a son".

The word, taken apart.
بیٹا (beṭā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from beṭṭa, "boy", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9238). CDIAL lists it in an entry headed biḍḍa, "defective", beside Prakrit biṭṭa, "boy"; no Sanskrit source is agreed.
