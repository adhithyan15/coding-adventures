## You'll want to know: लढणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C328-LADHAI328-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लढणे** — *laḍhṇe* — "to fight".

> सैनिक शेवटपर्यंत धैर्याने लढले.
>
> *sainik śevaṭparyant dhairyāne laḍhle.*
>
> The soldiers fought bravely to the end.

Past (he …): तो लढला, *to laḍhlā*.

The opponent takes -शी or -विरुद्ध: शत्रूशी लढणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लढणे** (*laḍhṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *laḍhṇe*, 'fight', under *\*laḍ*, 'fight' (CDIAL 10920).
