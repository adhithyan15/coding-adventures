# शपथ घेणे (śapath gheṇe) — to take an oath

## You'll want to know: शपथ घेणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C546-NISHCHAY546-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शपथ घेणे** — *śapath gheṇe* — "to take an oath".

> नव्या महापौरांनी सभेत शपथ घेतली.
>
> *navyā mahāpaurānnī sabhet śapath ghetlī.*
>
> The new mayor took the oath at the meeting.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने शपथ घेतली, *tyāne śapath ghetlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शपथ घेणे** (*śapath gheṇe*) is the tatsama शपथ, from Sanskrit *śapatha*, "a curse, an oath" (Monier-Williams), with घेणे.
