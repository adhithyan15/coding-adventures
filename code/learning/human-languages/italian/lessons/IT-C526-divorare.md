## You'll want to know: divorare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C526-RECENSIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**divorare** — "to devour".

> Ho divorato il libro in due giorni.
>
> *I devoured the book in two days.*

Of books: to read very fast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*divorare* comes from Latin *dēvorāre* "to swallow down" (EtymDB), from *vorāre* "to swallow".
