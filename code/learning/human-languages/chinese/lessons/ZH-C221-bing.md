## You'll want to know: bīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C221-SNOW221-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bīng* — "ice".

> *bīngxiāng lǐ yǒu bīng* — There's ice in the fridge.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bīng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ping* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'water frozen'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
