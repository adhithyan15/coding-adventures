## You'll want to know: వ్యాసం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C416-VYASAM416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యాసం** — *vyāsaṁ* — "an essay; an article".

> ఆమె పత్రికలో ఒక వ్యాసం రాసింది.
>
> *āme patrikalō oka vyāsaṁ rāsindi.*
>
> She wrote an article in the magazine.

Plural: వ్యాసాలు, *vyāsālu*.

వ్యాసరచన, essay writing, a school exercise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యాసం** (*vyāsaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vyāsa*, "a detailed account" (CDIAL 12214), and also "extension, diffusion" (Monier-Williams).
