## You'll want to know: वाढदिवस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C318-BARSE318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वाढदिवस** — *vāḍhdivas* — "a birthday".

> काल माझ्या मुलीचा पाचवा वाढदिवस होता.
>
> *kāl mājhyā mulīcā pācvā vāḍhdivas hotā.*
>
> Yesterday was my daughter's fifth birthday.

Masculine.

वाढदिवसाच्या शुभेच्छा, happy birthday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाढदिवस** (*vāḍhdivas*) is built from *vāḍh*, 'increase, growth', which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *vardha* (CDIAL 11373), and *divas*, 'a day': the day one grows a year.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
