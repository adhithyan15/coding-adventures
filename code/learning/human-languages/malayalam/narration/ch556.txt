Malayalam, chapter 556: Betrayal and Care.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
പിണക്കം (piṇakkaṁ) — a falling-out; a sulk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to split up, then the Malayalam for to be reconciled.

You'll want to know: പിണക്കം.
പിണക്കം — piṇakkaṁ — "a falling-out; a sulk".
ചെറിയ പിണക്കം കാരണം അവർ രണ്ട് ദിവസം സംസാരിച്ചില്ല. ceṟiya piṇakkaṁ kāraṇaṁ avar raṇṭŭ divasaṁ saṁsāriccilla. Because of a small falling-out they did not talk for two days.
The noun of പിണങ്ങുക, to sulk, already familiar.

The word, taken apart.
പിണക്കം (piṇakkaṁ) is native Dravidian, the noun of പിണങ്ങുക, "to fall out" (DEDR 4160).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ചതിക്കുക (catikkuka) — to betray; to cheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to be reconciled, then the Malayalam for a falling-out.

You'll want to know: ചതിക്കുക.
ചതിക്കുക — catikkuka — "to betray; to cheat".
വിശ്വസിച്ച സുഹൃത്ത് അവനെ ചതിച്ചു. viśvasicca suhṛttŭ avane caticcu. The friend he trusted cheated him.
Past: ചതിച്ചു, caticcu.
The noun is ചതി, a trick; one who cheats is ചതിയൻ.

The word, taken apart.
ചതിക്കുക (catikkuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2323 lists ചതി, "deceit, treachery", and the verb, to cheat and betray (DEDR 2323).

Lesson 3 of 6.
വഞ്ചന (vañcana) — betrayal; deceit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a falling-out, then the Malayalam for to betray.

You'll want to know: വഞ്ചന.
വഞ്ചന — vañcana — "betrayal; deceit".
പണം എടുത്ത് ഓടിപ്പോയത് വലിയ വഞ്ചനയായിരുന്നു. paṇaṁ eṭuttŭ ōṭippōyatŭ valiya vañcanayāyirunnu. Running off with the money was a great betrayal.
Formal; വഞ്ചിക്കുക is to deceive.

The word, taken apart.
വഞ്ചന (vañcana) is borrowed from Sanskrit vañcana, "cheating, deception, fraud" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 6.
കരുതൽ (karutal) — care; concern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to betray, then the Malayalam for betrayal.

You'll want to know: കരുതൽ.
കരുതൽ — karutal — "care; concern".
രോഗിയായ അച്ഛനോട് മകൾ വലിയ കരുതൽ കാണിച്ചു. rōgiyāya acchanōṭŭ makaḷ valiya karutal kāṇiccu. The daughter showed great care for her sick father.
From കരുതുക, to keep in mind; കരുതലോടെ means carefully.

The word, taken apart.
കരുതൽ (karutal) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1283 gives it as care and regard, beside കരുതുക, "to think, to attend to" (DEDR 1283).

Lesson 5 of 6.
പരിഗണന (parigaṇana) — consideration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for betrayal, then the Malayalam for care.

You'll want to know: പരിഗണന.
പരിഗണന — parigaṇana — "consideration".
പ്രായമായവർക്ക് ബസിൽ പ്രത്യേക പരിഗണന കിട്ടണം. prāyamāyavarkkŭ basil pratyēka parigaṇana kiṭṭaṇaṁ. Elderly people should get special consideration on the bus.
പരിഗണിക്കുക is to consider.

The word, taken apart.
പരിഗണന (parigaṇana) is borrowed from Sanskrit parigaṇana, a reckoning or counting over (Monier-Williams): to consider someone is to count them in.
