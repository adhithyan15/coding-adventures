Italian, chapter 410: Il guasto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il guasto — the breakdown, the fault.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the jack, then the Italian for to pump up.

You'll want to know: il guasto.
il guasto — "the breakdown, the fault".
C'è un guasto al motore. There is a fault in the engine.
As an adjective, guasto means out of order: il telefono è guasto.

The word, taken apart.
guasto is built on guastare 'to spoil', which is inherited from Latin vāstāre 'to lay waste' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
il carro attrezzi — the tow truck, the breakdown lorry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the breakdown, then the Italian for to break down.

You'll want to know: il carro attrezzi.
il carro attrezzi — "the tow truck, the breakdown lorry".
Abbiamo chiamato il carro attrezzi. We called the tow truck.
Also written carroattrezzi. Gli attrezzi are tools.

The word, taken apart.
carro attrezzi is built from carro 'cart', inherited from Latin carrus, a word of Gaulish origin (EtymDB), and attrezzi 'tools', whose origin is less clear.

Lesson 4 of 5.
rimorchiare — to tow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to break down, then the Italian for the tow truck.

You'll want to know: rimorchiare.
rimorchiare — "to tow".
Il carro attrezzi ci rimorchia fino al paese. The tow truck tows us to the village.
Il rimorchio is the towing, or the vehicle being towed; in slang, rimorchiare is to chat someone up.

The word, taken apart.
rimorchiare is built on rimorchio 'towing' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin remulcum 'a towing rope' (Lewis & Short), itself from Greek.

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'officina — the garage (for repairs), the workshop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the tow truck, then the Italian for to tow.

You'll want to know: l'officina.
l'officina — "the garage (for repairs), the workshop".
La macchina resta in officina tre giorni. The car stays at the garage for three days.
A workshop for repairs; il garage is where you keep the car.

The word, taken apart.
officina is a learned borrowing from Latin officīna 'workshop' (EtymDB), a shortened opificīna, from opifex 'worker', built on opus 'work'.
