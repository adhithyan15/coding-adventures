## You'll want to know: ગ્રહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C535-SHUKAN535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગ્રહ** — *grah* — "a planet (in astrology)".

> જો ગ્રહો સારા હશે તો દાદા આ મહિને નવી દુકાન ખોલશે.
>
> *jo graho sārā haśe to dādā ā mahine navī dukān kholśe.*
>
> If the planets are favourable, my grandfather will open a new shop this month.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગ્રહ** (*grah*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *graha*, "a seizer", and so "a planet", one held to seize on a person's fortunes (Monier-Williams).
