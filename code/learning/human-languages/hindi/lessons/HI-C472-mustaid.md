## You'll want to know: मुस्तैद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C472-PHURTI472-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मुस्तैद** — *mustaid* — "alert; ready for action".

> चुनाव के दिन पुलिस मुस्तैद थी।
>
> *cunāv ke din pulis mustaid thī.*
>
> On election day the police were on the alert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुस्तैद** (*mustaid*) is usually traced to Arabic *mustaʿidd*, "ready, prepared", which reached Hindi through Persian.
