## You'll want to know: shīwàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shīwàng* — "disappointed".

> *tā méi lái wǒ hěn shīwàng* — He didn't come, and I'm disappointed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shīwàng* is built from *shī* 'to lose' and *wàng* 'to watch for', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syit* and *mjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to let slip' and the second 'to watch for someone's return'.
