Persian, chapter 441: Making a complaint.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
درخواست کردن (darkhâst kardan) — to request, to ask for (formally).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for smelly, then the Persian for a water heater.

You'll want to know: درخواست کردن.
درخواست کردن (darkhâst kardan) — "to request, to ask for (formally)".
از مدیر هتل یک اتاق دیگر درخواست کردیم. Az modir-e hotel yek otâq-e digar darkhâst kardim. We asked the hotel manager for another room.
darkhâst 'a request' + kardan. The person asked takes az.

The word, taken apart.
darkhâst kardan is built from darkhâst 'a request', which EtymDB lists and which is built on khâstan 'to want', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: رسیدگی کردن.
رسیدگی کردن (residegi kardan) — "to look into, to deal with (a problem)".
مدیر قول داد به مشکل ما رسیدگی کند. Modir qol dâd be moshkel-e mâ residegi konad. The manager promised to look into our problem.
residegi 'attention, follow-up', from residan. The matter takes be: be in kâr residegi mikonim.

The word, taken apart.
residegi kardan is built from residegi 'attending to', formed on residan 'to arrive, to reach', usually traced to Middle Persian rasīdan, and kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ایراد گرفتن (irâd gereftan) — to find fault, to pick holes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to request, then the Persian for to look into.

You'll want to know: ایراد گرفتن.
ایراد گرفتن (irâd gereftan) — "to find fault, to pick holes".
او از همه چیز هتل ایراد میگرفت. U az hame chiz-e hotel irâd migereft. He found fault with everything about the hotel.
irâd 'objection, fault' + gereftan. The thing criticized takes az.

The word, taken apart.
irâd gereftan is built from irâd 'an objection, a flaw', a loan usually traced to Arabic īrād 'bringing forward', and the native verb gereftan 'to take'. EtymDB does not list irâd.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کوتاه آمدن (kutâh âmadan) — to back down, to give in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to look into, then the Persian for to find fault.

You'll want to know: کوتاه آمدن.
کوتاه آمدن (kutâh âmadan) — "to back down, to give in".
مسافر عصبانی بود ولی آخر کوتاه آمد. Mosâfer 'asabâni bud vali âkhar kutâh âmad. The passenger was angry but in the end he backed down.
Literally 'to come short'. Present: kutâh miâyad.

The word, taken apart.
kutâh âmadan is built from native kutâh 'short', usually traced to Middle Persian kōtāh, and âmadan 'to come', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root gʷem-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ناراضی (nârâzi) — dissatisfied, unhappy (with something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to find fault, then the Persian for to back down.

You'll want to know: ناراضی.
ناراضی (nârâzi) — "dissatisfied, unhappy (with something)".
مشتریها از غذای رستوران ناراضی بودند. Moshtari-hâ az ghazâ-ye restorân nârâzi budand. The customers were unhappy with the restaurant's food.
nâ- 'not' + râzi 'satisfied'. The cause takes az.

The word, taken apart.
nârâzi is built from the native prefix nâ- 'not' and râzi 'satisfied', borrowed from Arabic rāḍī 'content, pleased' (EtymDB).
