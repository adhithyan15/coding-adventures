> **A:** 明天会下雨吗
>
> *míngtiān huì xiàyǔ ma* — Will it rain tomorrow?
>
> **B:** *tiānqì yùbào shuō kěnéng huì* — The forecast says it might.
>
> **B:** 后天不会
>
> *hòutiān bú huì* — Not the day after.
>
> **A:** 后天下午开会 你来吗
>
> *hòutiān xiàwǔ kāihuì, nǐ lái ma* — There's a meeting the afternoon after tomorrow. Are you coming?
>
> **B:** *wǒ yídìng lái* — I'll be there, for sure.
>
> **A:** 从两点到四点
>
> *cóng liǎng diǎn dào sì diǎn* — From two till four.
>
> **B:** *nǐ kàndào tōngzhī le ma* — Did you see the notice?
>
> **A:** *kàndào le, wǒ yě huífù le* — I saw it, and I've replied.
>
> **B:** 下个月你要去中国吗
>
> *xià ge yuè nǐ yào qù Zhōngguó ma* — Are you going to China next month?
>
> **A:** 要
>
> *yào* — I am.
>
> **A:** 你去过中国吗
>
> *nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma* — Have you ever been?
>
> **B:** 没去过
>
> *méi qù guo* — No, never.
>
> **B:** 你汉语说得很好
>
> *nǐ Hànyǔ shuō de hěn hǎo* — Your Chinese is good.
>
> **A:** 我没有他那么好
>
> *wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme hǎo* — Not as good as his.
>
> **B:** 你要水还是要饭
>
> *nǐ yào shuǐ háishi yào fàn* — Water or food?
>
> **A:** 饭 我还没吃
>
> *fàn, wǒ hái méi chī* — Food. I haven't eaten yet.
>
> **B:** 我正在做饭
>
> *wǒ zhèngzài zuò fàn* — I'm cooking right now.
>
> **B:** *bié zǒu* — Don't go.
>
> **A:** *wǒ de huǒchē bā diǎn chūfā* — My train leaves at eight.
>
> **A:** *wǒ kàndào yí ge guǎnggào* — I saw an advert:
>
> **A:** 书十元一本
>
> *shū shí yuán yì běn* — books at ten yuan each.
>
> **B:** 星期天不开门
>
> *xīngqītiān bù kāimén* — That shop is closed on Sundays.
