## You'll want to know: गर्भिणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C492-JANMA492-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्भिणी** — *garbhiṇī* — "a pregnant woman".

> गर्भिणी पथ्यं भोजनं खादति।
>
> *garbhiṇī pathyaṃ bhojanaṃ khādati.*
>
> The pregnant woman eats wholesome food.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गर्भिणी** (*garbhiṇī*) is built on *garbha*, "the womb"; MW's *garbhin* is "pregnant", and this is its feminine (MW).
