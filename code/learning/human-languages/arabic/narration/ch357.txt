Arabic, chapter 357: Speeches and openings.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
خطاب (khiṭāb) — a speech, an address.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a photographer, then the Arabic for to cover.

You'll want to know: خطاب.
خطاب — khiṭāb — "a speech, an address".
كان خطاب المدير قصيرا. kāna khiṭāb al-mudīr qaṣīran. The manager’s speech was short.
In some countries خطاب (khiṭāb) is also a letter.

The word, taken apart.
خطاب (khiṭāb) is built on the native Arabic root kh-ṭ-b (Lane), "to address, to speak to", the root of khaṭaba and khuṭūba. Khiṭāb is the address itself.

Lesson 2 of 7.
ألقى (alqā) — to deliver a speech; to throw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to cover, then the Arabic for a speech.

You'll want to know: ألقى.
ألقى — alqā — "to deliver a speech; to throw".
ألقت الطالبة كلمة في الحفل. alqat aṭ-ṭāliba kalima fī al-ḥafl. The student gave a speech at the ceremony.
Present: يلقي, yulqī.
ألقى (alqā) خطابا, محاضرة: to give a speech, a lecture. ألقى (alqā) القبض على: to arrest.

The word, taken apart.
ألقى (alqā) is built on the native Arabic root l-q-y (Lane), "to meet", the root of liqāʾ, a meeting, as in the taught ilā l-liqāʾ. Form IV alqā is "to cast a thing so that it meets the ground", and words cast to listeners.

Lesson 3 of 7.
انعقد (inʿaqada) — to convene, to be held.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a speech, then the Arabic for to deliver a speech.

You'll want to know: انعقد.
انعقد — inʿaqada — "to convene, to be held".
انعقد الاجتماع في الفندق. inʿaqada al-ijtimāʿ fī al-funduq. The meeting was held at the hotel.
Present: ينعقد, yanʿaqid.

The word, taken apart.
انعقد (inʿaqada) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-q-d (Lane), "to tie a knot", the root of the taught ʿaqd, a contract. It is Form VII, "to be tied": a meeting is tied together.
