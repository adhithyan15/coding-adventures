## You'll want to know: el ciervo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C695-FAUNA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el ciervo** — "the deer, the stag".

> Por la mañana vimos un ciervo en el bosque.
>
> *In the morning we saw a deer in the woods.*

The female is *la cierva*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ciervo* is inherited from Latin *cervus* "a stag, a deer" (Lewis & Short).
