# మాడ్చు (māḍcu) — to scorch; to burn food

## You'll want to know: మాడ్చు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C548-VANTA548-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మాడ్చు** — *māḍcu* — "to scorch; to burn food".

> ఫోనులో మాట్లాడుతూ నేను కూర మాడ్చాను.
>
> *phōnulō māṭlāḍutū nēnu kūra māḍcānu.*
>
> Talking on the phone, I burnt the curry.

Past (I …): మాడ్చాను, *māḍcānu*.

మాడు, to get burnt, is its intransitive partner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మాడ్చు** (*māḍcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4801 lists Telugu *māḍu*, "to be burnt, to be scorched black", and *māḍ(u)cu*, "to burn, to scorch".
