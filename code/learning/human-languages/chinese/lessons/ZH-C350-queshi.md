## You'll want to know: quèshí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C350-FACTS350-05]; assesses=[] -->

*quèshí* — "indeed; it is true that".

> *tā shuō de quèshí shì shìshí* — What he said really is a fact.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quèshí* is built from *què* and *shí*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaewk* and *zyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'firm' and the second 'full' and 'sincere'.
