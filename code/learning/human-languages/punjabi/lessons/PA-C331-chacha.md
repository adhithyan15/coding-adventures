## You'll want to know: ਚਾਚਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C331-UNCLE331-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਾਚਾ** — *chāchā* — "a father's younger brother".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਚਾਚਾ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā chāchā khet vich kamm kardā hai.*
>
> My father's younger brother works in the fields.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਾਚਾ** (*chāchā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*cācca*, "uncle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cācā*, "father's younger brother", under it (CDIAL 4734).
