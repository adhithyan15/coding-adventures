## You'll want to know: der Adventskranz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C311-ADVENT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Adventskranz** — "the Advent wreath".

> Auf dem Adventskranz brannten zwei Kerzen.
>
> *Two candles burned on the Advent wreath.*

Plural: *die Adventskränze*. Four candles, one more each Sunday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Adventskranz* joins *Advent*, from Latin *adventus* "arrival", and *Kranz* "wreath", Old High German *kranz* (EtymDB).
