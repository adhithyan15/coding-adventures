## You'll want to know: буксовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C405-SHINA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**буксовать** (*buksovát*) — "to spin (of wheels), to get stuck".

> В снегу колёса долго буксовали.
>
> *V snegú kolyósa dólgo buksováli.*
>
> In the snow the wheels spun for a long time.

Imperfective; perfective *забуксовать*. Present *буксует*. Also figurative: *проект буксует* — the project is stuck.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *буксовать* is not settled. It is often linked with *буксир* 'a tug, a towrope', a sailors' word usually traced to Dutch; EtymDB does not list *буксовать*.
