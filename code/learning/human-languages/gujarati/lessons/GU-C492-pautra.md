## You'll want to know: પૌત્ર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C492-PIYAR492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પૌત્ર** — *pautra* — "a grandson (a son's son)".

> દાદાને પૌત્ર સાથે રમવું ગમે છે.
>
> *dādāne pautra sāthe ramvũ game che.*
>
> Grandfather likes playing with his grandson.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૌત્ર** (*pautra*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pautra*, "a son's son, a grandson", as Monier-Williams records it.
