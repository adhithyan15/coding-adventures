## You'll want to know: मुनिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C332-AKHYANA332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मुनिः** — *muniḥ* — "a hermit; a silent sage".

> मुनिः मौनं धारयति।
>
> *muniḥ maunaṃ dhārayati.*
>
> The hermit keeps silent.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुनिः** (*muniḥ*) MW reports a grammarians' derivation from the root √man, "to think", which it does not settle, so its origin is uncertain; MW gives *muni* as an inspired person, a sage, ascetic or hermit, especially one under a vow of silence (MW).
