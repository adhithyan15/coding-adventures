# રિસાવું (risāvũ) — to sulk, to take offence

## You'll want to know: રિસાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C267-UPSET267-05]; assesses=[] -->

**રિસાવું** — *risāvũ* — "to sulk, to take offence".

> **શું તે ફરી રિસાય છે?** — *śũ te pharī risāy che?* — "Is she sulking again?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રિસાવું** (*risāvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *ríṣyati*, "is hurt, suffers wrong"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to be offended" (CDIAL 10749).
