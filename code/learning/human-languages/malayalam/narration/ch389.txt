Malayalam, chapter 389: New Homes and New Names.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം (gṛhapravēśaṁ) — a housewarming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to congratulate, then the Malayalam for to bestow.

You'll want to know: ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം.
ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം — gṛhapravēśaṁ — "a housewarming".
അവരുടെ പുതിയ വീടിന്റെ ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം ഞായറാഴ്ച ആണ്. avaruṭe putiya vīṭinṟe gṛhapravēśaṁ ñāyaṟāḻca āṇŭ. Their new house's housewarming is on Sunday.
Literally entering the house: ഗൃഹം, a house, + പ്രവേശം, entry.

The word, taken apart.
ഗൃഹപ്രവേശം (gṛhapravēśaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit gṛhapraveśa, "solemn entrance into a house" (Monier-Williams), from gṛha, "a house", and praveśa, "entering".

Lesson 2 of 5.
നാമകരണം (nāmakaraṇaṁ) — a naming ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to bestow, then the Malayalam for a housewarming.

You'll want to know: നാമകരണം.
നാമകരണം — nāmakaraṇaṁ — "a naming ceremony".
കുഞ്ഞിന്റെ നാമകരണം ഇരുപത്തിയെട്ടാം ദിവസം ആയിരുന്നു. kuññinṟe nāmakaraṇaṁ irupattiyeṭṭāṁ divasaṁ āyirunnu. The baby's naming was on the twenty-eighth day.
Also the naming of anything new, a road or a building.

The word, taken apart.
നാമകരണം (nāmakaraṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāmakaraṇa, "the ceremony of naming after birth" (CDIAL 7066), from nāman, "a name", and karaṇa, "a making".

Lesson 3 of 5.
മധുവിധു (madhuvidhu) — a honeymoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a housewarming, then the Malayalam for a naming ceremony.

You'll want to know: മധുവിധു.
മധുവിധു — madhuvidhu — "a honeymoon".
വിവാഹം കഴിഞ്ഞ് അവർ മധുവിധുവിന് ഒരു ദ്വീപിൽ പോയി. vivāhaṁ kaḻiññŭ avar madhuvidhuvinŭ oru dvīpil pōyi. After the wedding they went to an island for their honeymoon.
Usually explained as a calque on the English word: മധു, honey, + വിധു, the moon.

The word, taken apart.
മധുവിധു (madhuvidhu) is a modern compound built from two Sanskrit words, madhu, "honey" (CDIAL 9784), and vidhu, a name of the moon (Monier-Williams). It is usually explained as a calque on English honeymoon.

Lesson 4 of 5.
താമസമാക്കുക (tāmasamākkuka) — to settle; to take up residence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a naming ceremony, then the Malayalam for a honeymoon.

You'll want to know: താമസമാക്കുക.
താമസമാക്കുക — tāmasamākkuka — "to settle; to take up residence".
അവർ ഇപ്പോൾ നഗരത്തിൽ താമസമാക്കി. avar ippōḷ nagarattil tāmasamākki. They have now settled in the city.
Past: താമസമാക്കി, tāmasamākki.
താമസം, a stay, + ആക്കുക, to make: to make a place your home.

The word, taken apart.
താമസമാക്കുക (tāmasamākkuka) joins താമസം, "a stay, a residence", usually traced to Sanskrit tāmasa, "dark; dull, slow" (CDIAL 5775), as is താമസിക്കുക (tāmasikkuka), "to stay", and ആക്കുക, "to make".

Lesson 5 of 5.
കുടിയേറുക (kuṭiyēṟuka) — to migrate; to settle in a new land.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a honeymoon, then the Malayalam for to settle.

You'll want to know: കുടിയേറുക.
കുടിയേറുക — kuṭiyēṟuka — "to migrate; to settle in a new land".
പണ്ട് ഒരുപാട് കുടുംബങ്ങൾ വിദേശത്തേക്ക് കുടിയേറി. paṇṭŭ orupāṭŭ kuṭumbaṅṅaḷ vidēśattēkkŭ kuṭiyēṟi. Long ago many families migrated abroad.
Past: കുടിയേറി, kuṭiyēṟi.
കുടി, a home, + ഏറുക, to climb in: to set up home in a new place.

The word, taken apart.
കുടിയേറുക (kuṭiyēṟuka) is a native Dravidian compound of കുടി, "a house, a hut, a family" (DEDR 1655), and ഏറുക, "to climb, to go up into".
