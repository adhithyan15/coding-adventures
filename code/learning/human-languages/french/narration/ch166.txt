French, chapter 166: Photographer, Journalist, Architect, Dentist, Pharmacist.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le photographe — a photographer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a soldier, then the French for a tradition.

You'll want to know: le photographe.
le photographe — "a photographer".
