## You'll want to know: tián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C71-TASTE71-02]; assesses=[] -->

*tián* — "sweet".

> *nǐ de kāfēi hěn tián* — Your coffee is very sweet.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tián* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dem* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'sweet'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
