## Script — a tree and an eye, over a heart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XIANG-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 想

**Three pieces: the tree, then the eye, then the heart below.** Count three, not thirteen.

**The tree, four strokes**, narrow on the top left: a horizontal, a vertical through it, a left-falling stroke, and a dot.

**The eye, five strokes**, on the top right: a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, and three short horizontals inside, the last one closing it. It is the box at the bottom of 看.

**The heart, four strokes**, wide underneath: a dot, a long curve that sweeps right and hooks up, then two more dots.

4 + 5 + 4 = thirteen.
