## You'll want to know: えんげき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C502-STAGE502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**えんげき** — *engeki* — "drama; the theatre (as an art)".

> **あねは えんげきが すきです。** — *ane wa engeki ga suki desu* — "My elder sister loves the theatre."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんげき** (*engeki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *en*, "performance" (Middle Chinese *yenX*) and *geki*, "drama" (*gjaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
