## You'll want to know: ਮੁਫ਼ਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C276-PRICE276-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਫ਼ਤ** — *mufat* — "free of charge".

> ਦੁਕਾਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਥੈਲਾ ਮੁਫ਼ਤ ਹੈ।
>
> *dukān vich thailā mufat hai.*
>
> The bag is free in the shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੁਫ਼ਤ** (*mufat*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *muft*, "free, for nothing".
