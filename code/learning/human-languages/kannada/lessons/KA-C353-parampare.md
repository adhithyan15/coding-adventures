# ಪರಂಪರೆ (parampare) — a heritage; a tradition

## You'll want to know: ಪರಂಪರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C353-UYILU353-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಂಪರೆ** — *parampare* — "a heritage; a tradition".

> ಈ ಹಬ್ಬ ನಮ್ಮ ಊರಿನ ಹಳೆಯ ಪರಂಪರೆ.
>
> *ī habba namma ūrina haḷeya parampare.*
>
> This festival is an old tradition of our town.

What passes from generation to generation: ಸಾಂಸ್ಕೃತಿಕ ಪರಂಪರೆ, cultural heritage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಂಪರೆ** (*parampare*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paramparā*, "an uninterrupted series, succession, lineage" (Monier-Williams).
