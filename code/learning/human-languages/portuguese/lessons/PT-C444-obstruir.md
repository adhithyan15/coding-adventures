## You'll want to know: obstruir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C444-MOBILIARIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**obstruir** — "to obstruct, to block".

> Um camião obstruiu a rua durante a manhã.
>
> *A truck blocked the street all morning.*

*Obstruir a passagem* — to block the way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obstruir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obstruere* 'to build up against, to block' (EtymDB), on *struere* 'to build'.
