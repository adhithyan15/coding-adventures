## You'll want to know: अवश्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C376-ZAHIR376-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अवश्य** — *avaśya* — "without fail, certainly (formal)".

> अगर समय मिलेगा तो मैं अवश्य आऊँगा।
>
> *agar samay milegā to maiṁ avaśya āūṅgā.*
>
> If I get the time, I'll come without fail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवश्य** (*avaśya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *avaśyam*, "necessarily, inevitably" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
