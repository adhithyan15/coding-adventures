# es kommt darauf an — it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-WENN-PRAESENS-01, GE-LEX-WETTER-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains tomorrow, we won't go". (*Wenn es morgen
regnet, fahren wir nicht.*)

[PAUSE 1s] Now someone asks if you are coming, and the honest answer is
"it depends".

## You'll want to know: es kommt darauf an
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-ES-KOMMT-DARAUF-AN-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-C174-VERB174-03, GE-ETYMON-WARUM-WAR-UM-02] -->

**Es kommt darauf an.** — "It depends": *ess kommt dah-ROWF ahn*.

> *Kommst du morgen?* — *Es kommt darauf an.*

It is *ankommen*, split as usual, with *darauf*, "on that", in the middle.
In quick speech it shrinks to *kommt drauf an*.

To name the thing it depends on, swap *darauf* for *auf* and the thing:

> *Es kommt auf das Wetter an.* — It depends on the weather.

And to ask "on what?", German builds the question the way it built
*warum*: **worauf?**

## Grammar Lens: das hängt davon ab, ob …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-HAENGT-DAVON-AB-02]; assesses=[GE-LEX-C147-VERB147-02, GE-GRAMMAR-VERB-LETZT-01, GE-LEX-ZEIT-01] -->

The second way is **das hängt davon ab**, "that depends on it". It is
*abhängen*, to hang off, split like *ankommen*:

> *Das hängt vom Wetter ab.* — That depends on the weather.

With *abhängen* the thing comes after **von**: *vom Wetter*, from *von
dem*.

When what it depends on is a whole question, German opens it with **ob**,
"whether", and the verb goes to the end:

> *Das hängt davon ab, ob es* **regnet**. — That depends on whether it
> rains.
> *Es kommt darauf an, ob ich Zeit* **habe**. — It depends on whether I
> have time.

*Ob* is the "if" of "I don't know if": it asks, where *wenn* sets a
condition.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-C174-VERB174-03] -->

**ankommen** is *an-* + *kommen*, and EtymDB 2.1 sets Old English
*oncuman* beside it. **hängen** ← Middle High German *hengen* ← Old High
German *hengen* (EtymDB 2.1). *Abhängen*, to hang off, gives the same
picture as English *depend*, from Latin *dēpendēre*, "to hang down from":
what depends on a thing hangs from it.
