## You'll want to know: ನರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C509-ELUBU509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ನರ** — *nara* — "a nerve; a sinew".

> ವೈದ್ಯರು ಅವನ ಕೈಯ ನರವನ್ನು ಪರೀಕ್ಷಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *vaidyaru avana kaiya naravannu parīkṣisidaru.*
>
> The doctor examined the nerve in his arm.

Plural: ನರಗಳು, *naragaḷu*.

In everyday talk also a vein; ನರಗಳು, the nerves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನರ** (*nara*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2903 lists Kannada *nara*, *naravu*, "sinew, tendon, nerve, vein".
