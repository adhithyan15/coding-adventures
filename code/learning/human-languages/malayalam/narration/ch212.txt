Malayalam, chapter 212: Art, Music, Competition, God, Bamboo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കല (kala) — art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a stranger, then the Malayalam for a party.

You'll want to know: കല.
കല — kala — "art".

The word, taken apart.
കല (kala) is Sanskrit kalā, "any practical art" (CDIAL 2927). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word itself from Dravidian, and DEDR 1297 sets it beside Tamil kal, "to learn", so the word may have come home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: സംഗീതം.
സംഗീതം — saṁgītaṁ — "music".

The word, taken apart.
സംഗീതം (saṁgītaṁ) from Sanskrit saṁgīta, "chorus, concert", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 12849). It is built on the root gai, "to sing".
