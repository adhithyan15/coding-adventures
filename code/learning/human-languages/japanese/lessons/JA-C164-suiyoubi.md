## You'll want to know: すいようび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C164-WEEK164-02]; assesses=[] -->

**すいようび** — *suiyōbi* — "Wednesday".

> **すいようびは あめでしょう。** — *suiyōbi wa ame deshō* — "It will probably rain on Wednesday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいようび** (*suiyōbi*) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese *sui*, "water" (Middle Chinese *sywijX*), and *yō*, "planet, weekday" (*yewH*), borrowed from Chinese, with native *hi*, "day", voiced to *bi* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
