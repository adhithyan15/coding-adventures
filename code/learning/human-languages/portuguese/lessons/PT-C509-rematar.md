## You'll want to know: rematar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C509-BALIZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rematar** — "to shoot (at goal); to finish off".

> O avançado rematou com o pé esquerdo.
>
> *The striker shot with his left foot.*

A shot is *um remate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rematar* is built from *re-* and *matar* in its old sense "to finish, to end"; *matar* is usually traced to Latin *mactāre* "to slaughter" (EtymDB).
