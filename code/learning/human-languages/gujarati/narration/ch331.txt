ઉપવાસ (upvās) — a day of fasting; a fast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a hymn, then the Gujarati for a devotee.

You'll want to know: ઉપવાસ.
ઉપવાસ — upvās — "a day of fasting; a fast".
દાદી દરેક સોમવારે ઉપવાસ કરે છે. dādī darek somvāre upvās kare che. My grandmother fasts every Monday.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉપવાસ (upvās) is a tatsama, Sanskrit upavāsa, "fasting" (CDIAL 2244). It is kept with કરવું: ઉપવાસ કરે છે, fasts.

માતાએ દીકરા માટે વ્રત રાખ્યું. mātāe dīkrā māṭe vrat rākhyũ. The mother kept a vow for her son.

The word, taken apart.
વ્રત (vrat) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vrata, "a vow, a religious observance" (Monier-Williams). It is kept, વ્રત રાખ્યું, and often means fasting on set days.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ચઢાવવું (caḍhāvvũ) — to offer (to a god); to raise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a prayer, then the Gujarati for a blessing.

You'll want to know: ચઢાવવું.
ચઢાવવું — caḍhāvvũ — "to offer (to a god); to raise".
ભક્તે મંદિરમાં ફૂલ ચઢાવ્યાં. bhakte mandirmā̃ phūl caḍhāvyā̃. The devotee offered flowers at the temple.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ફૂલ ચઢાવ્યાં, teṇe phūl caḍhāvyā̃.

The word, taken apart.
ચઢાવવું (caḍhāvvũ) is the causative of ચઢવું, to climb, from caḍhati, "rises", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4578): to make something go up, and so to offer it up to a god.
