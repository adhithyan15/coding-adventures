## You'll want to know: ゆうめい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C405-TEIEN405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうめい** — *yūmei* — "famous".

> **この ていえんは ゆうめいです。** — *kono teien wa yūmei desu* — "This garden is famous."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうめい** (*yūmei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yū*, "have" (Middle Chinese *hjuwX*) and *mei*, "name" (*mjieng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
