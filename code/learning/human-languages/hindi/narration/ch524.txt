Hindi, chapter 524: Cooking and Serving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गलाना (galānā) — to cook soft; to soften.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to sprinkle (a powder), then the Hindi for to grind coarsely.

You'll want to know: गलाना.
गलाना — galānā — "to cook soft; to soften".
दाल को पूरी तरह गलाइए। Dāl ko pūrī tarah galāie. Cook the dal until it is completely soft.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने चावल गलाए, usne cāval galāe.

The word, taken apart.
गलाना (galānā) is the causative of galnā, "to melt, soften", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit galati, "drips, oozes, trickles" (CDIAL 4074); CDIAL gives Hindi galnā as "to melt, trickle".

माँ पत्थर पर चटनी घोंट रही है। Māṁ patthar par caṭnī ghõṭ rahī hai. Mother is grinding chutney on a stone.

Lesson 4 of 5.
जमाना (jamānā) — to set (curd); to make (something) set.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to serve (food), then the Hindi for to grind into a paste.

You'll want to know: जमाना.
जमाना — jamānā — "to set (curd); to make (something) set".
रात को माँ ने दूध में जामन डालकर दही जमाया। Rāt ko māṁ ne dūdh meṁ jāman ḍālkar dahī jamāyā. At night my mother added starter to the milk and set the yoghurt.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बर्फ़ जमाई, usne barf jamāī.

The word, taken apart.
जमाना (jamānā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit yamyatē, "is held, is fixed" (CDIAL 10428). CDIAL lists jamānā, "to collect", beside the intransitive jamnā.

Lesson 5 of 5.
खँगालना (khãgālnā) — to rinse out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to grind into a paste, then the Hindi for to set (curd).

You'll want to know: खँगालना.
खँगालना — khãgālnā — "to rinse out".
खाने के बाद उसने गिलास पानी से खँगाले। Khāne ke bād usne gilās pānī se khãgāle. After the meal he rinsed the tumblers with water.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बोतल खँगाली, usne botal khãgālī.

The word, taken apart.
खँगालना (khãgālnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from khakkhālayati, "rinses", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3762).
