# جیٹھ (jeṭh) — a husband's elder brother

## You'll want to know: جیٹھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C489-KIN489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جیٹھ** — *jeṭh* — "a husband's elder brother".

> میرے جیٹھ ڈاکٹر ہیں۔
>
> *mere jeṭh ḍākṭar haiṅ.*
>
> My husband's elder brother is a doctor.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جیٹھ** (*jeṭh*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jeṭh*, "the eldest", under Sanskrit *jyḗṣṭha*, "first, eldest" (CDIAL 5286).
