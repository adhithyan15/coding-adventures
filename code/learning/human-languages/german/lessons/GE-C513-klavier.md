## You'll want to know: das Klavier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C513-INSTRUMENTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Klavier** — "piano".

> Meine Tochter spielt seit drei Jahren Klavier.
>
> *My daughter has been playing the piano for three years.*

Plural: *die Klaviere*. No article in *Klavier spielen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Klavier* was borrowed from French *clavier* "keyboard", which is built on Latin *clāvis* "key" (EtymDB).
