## You'll want to know: coçar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C483-ALERGIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**coçar** — "to scratch an itch".

> O cão coça a orelha com a pata.
>
> *The dog scratches its ear with its paw.*

*Coçar-se* — to scratch yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coçar* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Vulgar Latin *coctiāre* "to scratch" (EtymDB).
