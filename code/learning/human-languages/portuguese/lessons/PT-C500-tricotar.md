## You'll want to know: tricotar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C500-COSTURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**tricotar** — "to knit".

> No inverno, a tia tricota camisolas para todos.
>
> *In winter, our aunt knits jumpers for everyone.*

Also *fazer tricô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tricotar* was borrowed from French *tricoter* "to knit" (EtymDB), which in Old French meant "to beat with sticks".
