Bengali, chapter 361: Learning and Teaching.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
শিক্ষা (shikkhā) — education.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for in attendance, then the Bengali for to address a crowd.

You'll want to know: শিক্ষা.
শিক্ষা — shikkhā — "education".
শিক্ষা সবার অধিকার। shikkhā sôbār ôdhikār. Education is everyone's right.

The word, taken apart.
শিক্ষা (shikkhā) is Sanskrit śikṣā, learning or study (Monier-Williams). The everyday verb শেখা, to learn, comes from the same root by the spoken road (CDIAL 12430 śíkṣatē lists Bengali sikhā).

Lesson 2 of 6.
বিদ্যা (bidyā) — learning; knowledge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to address a crowd, then the Bengali for education.

You'll want to know: বিদ্যা.
বিদ্যা — bidyā — "learning; knowledge".
বিদ্যা কেউ চুরি করতে পারে না। bidyā keu churi kôrte pāre nā. No one can steal your learning.

The word, taken apart.
বিদ্যা (bidyā) is Sanskrit vidyā, knowledge or learning (Monier-Williams). It is the learning a person carries, as in বিদ্যালয়, a school, 'the house of learning'.

অধ্যাপক — ôdhyāpôk — "a professor; a college lecturer".
মীরার বাবা কলেজে অধ্যাপক। mīrār bābā kôleje ôdhyāpôk. Mira's father is a lecturer at the college.

The word, taken apart.
অধ্যাপক (ôdhyāpôk) is Sanskrit adhyāpaka, a teacher (Monier-Williams). Bengali keeps it for those who teach at a college or a university.

Lesson 5 of 6.
পড়ানো (pôṛāno) — to teach (a subject); to tutor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a professor, then the Bengali for a university degree.

You'll want to know: পড়ানো.
পড়ানো — pôṛāno — "to teach (a subject); to tutor".
মীরা সন্ধ্যায় শিশুদের ইংরেজি পড়ায়। mīrā sôndhyāy shishuder ingreji pôṛāy. Mira teaches children English in the evenings.
Past (he/she …): তিনি অনেক বছর ইতিহাস পড়ালেন, tini ônek bôchhôr itihās pôṛālen.

The word, taken apart.
পড়ানো (pôṛāno) is the causative of পড়া 'to read, to study' (CDIAL 7712 páṭhati lists Bengali paṛā): to make someone study. শেখানো teaches a skill; পড়ানো teaches a subject from books.
