## You'll want to know: ほこうしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C391-OUDAN391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほこうしゃ** — *hokōsha* — "a pedestrian".

> **ほこうしゃは ほどうを あるきます。** — *hokōsha wa hodō o arukimasu* — "Pedestrians walk on the pavement."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほこうしゃ** (*hokōsha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ho*, "walk" (Middle Chinese *buH*), *kō*, "going" (*haeng*) and *sha*, "someone" (*tsyaeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
