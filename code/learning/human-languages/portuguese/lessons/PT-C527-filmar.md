## You'll want to know: filmar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C527-FILMAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**filmar** — "to film".

> Filmaram o casamento com o telemóvel.
>
> *They filmed the wedding on their mobile.*

Built on *o filme*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*filmar* is built on *filme*, which was borrowed from English *film* "a thin skin".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
