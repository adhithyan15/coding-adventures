## You'll want to know: ताल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C538-NRUTYA538-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ताल** — *tāl* — "rhythm; a beat".

> ढोलाचा ताल ऐकून सगळे नाचू लागले.
>
> *ḍholācā tāl aikūn sagḷe nācū lāgle.*
>
> Hearing the drum's rhythm, everyone began to dance.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताल** (*tāl*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tāla*, "clapping the hands, musical time" (Monier-Williams).
