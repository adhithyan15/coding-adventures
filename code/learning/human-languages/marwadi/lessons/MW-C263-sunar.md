## You'll want to know: सुनार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C263-TELI263-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सुनार** — *sunār* — "a goldsmith".

> सुनार चूड़ी अर नथ बेचै है।
>
> *sunār chūṛī ar nath bechai hai.*
>
> The goldsmith sells bangles and nose rings.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुनार** (*sunār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *suvarṇakāra*, "goldsmith". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *sunār* (CDIAL 13520).
