## You'll want to know: डालना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C521-TADKA521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**डालना** — *ḍālnā* — "to put in; to pour in".

> गरम तेल में थोड़ा नमक डालिए।
>
> *Garam tel meṁ thoṛā namak ḍāliye.*
>
> Put a little salt into the hot oil.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दाल में पानी डाला, *usne dāl meṁ pānī ḍālā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डालना** (*ḍālnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*ḍāl*, "pour, put", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5545).
