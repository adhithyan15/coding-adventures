Russian, chapter 341: Reaching a compromise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
противоречить (protivoréchit) — to contradict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to object, then the Russian for an objection.

You'll want to know: противоречить.
противоречить (protivoréchit) — "to contradict".
Ты сам себе противоречишь. Ty sam sebé protivoréchish. You are contradicting yourself.
Imperfective only. It takes the dative: противоречить (protivoréchit) фактам.

The word, taken apart.
противоречить (protivoréchit) is built from противо- "against" and речь "speech", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic rěčĭ, from rekti "to say" (EtymDB). To contradict is to speak against.

Lesson 2 of 5.
отвергать (otvergát) — to reject.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an objection, then the Russian for to contradict.

You'll want to know: отвергать.
отвергать (otvergát) — "to reject".
Начальник отверг наше предложение. Nachálnik otvérg náshe predlozhéniye. The boss rejected our proposal.
Imperfective; perfective отвергнуть (past отверг, отвергла). Formal; in conversation people say не принять.

The word, taken apart.
отвергать (otvergát) is built from от- "away" and вергать "to throw", a bookish verb. EtymDB traces отвергнуть to Proto-Slavic vĭrgnǫti (EtymDB), usually glossed "to throw". To reject is to throw away.

Lesson 3 of 5.
компромисс (kompromíss) — a compromise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to contradict, then the Russian for to reject.

You'll want to know: компромисс.
компромисс (kompromíss) — "a compromise".
После долгого спора мы нашли компромисс. Pósle dólgogo spóra my nashlí kompromíss. After a long argument we found a compromise.
Masculine. Пойти на компромисс (kompromíss) — to make a compromise.

The word, taken apart.
компромисс (kompromíss) is usually traced to French compromis, from Latin compromissum "a mutual promise to accept what an arbiter decides" (Lewis & Short), built on mittere "to send".

Lesson 4 of 5.
настаивать (nastáivat) — to insist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to reject, then the Russian for a compromise.

You'll want to know: настаивать.
настаивать (nastáivat) — "to insist".
Врач настаивает, чтобы я больше спал. Vrach nastáivayet, chtóby ya bólshe spal. The doctor insists that I sleep more.
Imperfective; perfective настоять. Настаивать на + prepositional, or настаивать (nastáivat), чтобы + past tense.

The word, taken apart.
настаивать (nastáivat) is built from на- "on" and стоять "to stand", inherited from Proto-Slavic stojati (EtymDB). To insist is to stand on your point.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: признавать.
признавать (priznavát) — "to admit, to acknowledge".
Он не любит признавать свои ошибки. On ne lyúbit priznavát svoí oshíbki. He does not like admitting his mistakes.
Imperfective; present признаю, признаёшь; perfective признать. Built on знать.

The word, taken apart.
признавать (priznavát) is built from при- and знать "to know", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic znati (EtymDB). To admit a thing is to own that you know it.
