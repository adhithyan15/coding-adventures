Urdu, chapter 467: Taste and the Mouth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تالو (tālū) — the palate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the sole of the foot, then the Urdu for a paw.

You'll want to know: تالو.
تالو — tālū — "the palate".
تالو منہ کی چھت ہے۔ tālū muṅh kī chhat hai. The palate is the roof of the mouth.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تالو (tālū) is inherited from Sanskrit tālu, "palate" (CDIAL 5803).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

The word, taken apart.
مسوڑھا (masūṛhā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin masūṛā under māṃsapuṭaka, "the gums", a form it reconstructs from the words for "flesh" and "fold" (CDIAL 9984).

Lesson 3 of 5.
چاٹنا (chāṭnā) — to lick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the palate, then the Urdu for a gum.

You'll want to know: چاٹنا.
چاٹنا — chāṭnā — "to lick".
بلی نے دودھ چاٹا۔ billī ne dūdh chāṭā. The cat licked up the milk.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چمچ چاٹا, us ne chammach chāṭā.

The word, taken apart.
چاٹنا (chāṭnā) is inherited from caṭṭ, "lick, taste", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4573).
