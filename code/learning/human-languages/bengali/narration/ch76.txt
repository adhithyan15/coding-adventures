মোটা (moṭā) — fat, thick.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: মোটা.
মোটা — moṭā — "fat, thick".

The word, taken apart.
মোটা (moṭā) comes from mōṭṭa, one of a group of forms CDIAL heads muṭṭa, "defective", and reconstructs from their descendants (CDIAL 10187).

Lesson 2 of 5.
রোগা (rogā) — thin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for sour, then the Bengali for fat.

You'll want to know: রোগা.
রোগা — rogā — "thin".

The word, taken apart.
রোগা (rogā) is rog, "illness", with the adjective ending -ā: "sickly", and so "thin". Rog is Sanskrit roga, "disease", a tatsama; CDIAL has the entry but no Bengali form (CDIAL 10829).

Lesson 3 of 5.
হালকা (hālkā) — light (in weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fat, then the Bengali for thin.

You'll want to know: হালকা.
হালকা — hālkā — "light (in weight)".

The word, taken apart.
হালকা (hālkā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from laghu, "light" (CDIAL 10896). CDIAL lists halkā as an extended form with its consonants swapped.

Lesson 5 of 5.
সত্যি (sôtti) — true.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for light, then the Bengali for heavy.

You'll want to know: সত্যি.
সত্যি — sôtti — "true".

The word, taken apart.
সত্যি (sôtti) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit satya, "true", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped (CDIAL 13112). The word inherited from it is sāṁcā, which CDIAL lists.
