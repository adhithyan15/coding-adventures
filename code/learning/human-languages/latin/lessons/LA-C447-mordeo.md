## You'll want to know: mordeō, mordēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C447-CIMEX-02]; assesses=[] -->

**mordeō, mordēre** — "to bite".

> Tōtam noctem cīmicēs hospitem mordent.
>
> *All night the bedbugs bite the guest.*

*mordeō, mordēre, momordī, morsum*, with a reduplicated perfect. English *remorse* (a biting back) and *mordant* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mordeō* is inherited from Proto-Italic *mordeō*, from PIE *(s)merd-* 'to bite, to sting' (EtymDB). English *smart* 'to sting' is a cousin, and *remorse* and *mordant* come from the Latin verb.
