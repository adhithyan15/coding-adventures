# かれる (kareru) — to wither; to die (of a plant)

## You'll want to know: かれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C210-PLANT210-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かれる** — *kareru* — "to wither; to die (of a plant)".

> **ふゆに くさが かれます。** — *fuyu ni kusa ga karemasu* — "The grass withers in winter."

Polite **かれます**, *karemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かれる** (*kareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
