Spanish, chapter 542: El comienzo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
comenzar — to begin, to start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to go on, then the Spanish for to go by.

You'll want to know: comenzar.
comenzar — "to begin, to start".
La película comenzó a las nueve. The film began at nine.
Stem change: comienza, comienzan. A little more formal than empezar. Comenzar a + infinitive.

The word, taken apart.
comenzar is inherited from spoken Latin cominitiāre (EtymDB), built on com- and initiāre 'to begin', from initium 'a beginning'.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for to go by, then the Spanish for to begin.

You'll want to know: iniciar.
iniciar — "to launch, to start, to begin".
La policía inició una investigación. The police started an investigation.
Formal; common in the news. Iniciar sesión — to log in.

The word, taken apart.
iniciar is a learned borrowing from Latin initiāre 'to begin, to initiate' (EtymDB), built on initium 'a going in, a beginning'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
finalizar — to end, to finish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the beginning, then the Spanish for the start.

You'll want to know: finalizar.
finalizar — "to end, to finish".
La reunión finalizó a las seis. The meeting ended at six.
Formal; in speech people say terminar or acabar.

The word, taken apart.
finalizar is built on final with the ending -izar. Final comes from Latin fīnālis, from fīnis 'end' (Lewis & Short).
