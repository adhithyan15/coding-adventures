## You'll want to know: عشية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C336-HADIYYA336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عشية** — *ʿashiyya* — "the eve; the evening before".

> سافرنا عشية العيد.
>
> *sāfarnā ʿashiyyat al-ʿīd.*
>
> We travelled on the eve of the holiday.

عشية الانتخابات: on the eve of the election.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عشية** (*ʿashiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-sh-w* (Lane), where *ʿashiyy* is the evening; the taught *ʿashāʾ*, dinner, is the evening meal.
