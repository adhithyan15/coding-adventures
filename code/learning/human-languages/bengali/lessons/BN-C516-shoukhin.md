## You'll want to know: শৌখিন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C516-BINODON516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**শৌখিন** — *shoukhin* — "keen on a pastime; amateur".

> আমার কাকা একজন শৌখিন গায়ক।
>
> *Āmār kākā ekjôn shoukhin gāyôk.*
>
> My uncle is an amateur singer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শৌখিন** (*shoukhin*) is usually traced to Arabic *shawq*, "eagerness, longing", through Persian; শখ, a hobby, belongs to the same family.
