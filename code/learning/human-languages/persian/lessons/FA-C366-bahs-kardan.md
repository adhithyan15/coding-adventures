## You'll want to know: بحث کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C366-BAHS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بحث کردن** (*bahs kardan*) — "to discuss, to argue".

> آنها درباره پول بحث میکنند.
>
> *ânhâ darbâre-ye pul bahs mikonand.*
>
> They are arguing about money.

*Bahs kardan darbâre-ye* … — to argue about … ; with someone: *bâ kasi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بحث کردن** joins *bahs*, from Arabic *baḥth* "discussion" (EtymDB), to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
