> भ

**bha** — and the *h* is not decoration. This is **b** followed by a real puff of
breath, and Hindi has a **separate letter** for it.

Hold your palm in front of your mouth. English *bin* gives almost no air; this
letter demands a clear push. English treats the difference as an accident of
emphasis; Hindi treats it as the difference between two words.

Devanagari is systematic about it: nearly every consonant has an unaspirated and an
aspirated partner, written as different letters, and the aspirated one usually
looks like a variation on its plain twin. **Once you know the pattern you can read
half the alphabet from the other half.**
