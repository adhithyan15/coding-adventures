## You'll want to know: sublinhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C343-ESCLARECER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sublinhar** — "to stress, to emphasise; to underline".

> A médica sublinhou que é preciso descansar.
>
> *The doctor stressed that rest is needed.*

Literally to draw a line under a word; figuratively to stress a point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sublinhar* is built from *sub-* "under" and *linha* "line", which is inherited from Latin *līnea* "a linen thread, a string, a line" (Lewis & Short).
