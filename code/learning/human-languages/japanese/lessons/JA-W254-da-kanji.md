# 出 — out, five strokes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C254-IIDASU-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-NEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *iidasu*, to suggest. Its first kanji is **言**, which you write. Then call up **年**: six strokes, the long stem last.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-DASU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-NEN-01] -->

> **出** — *da*

Its parts:

- two open boxes, one stacked on the other
- one long centre stem through both

Five strokes, four lifts:

1. draw the long centre stem from the top down
2. lift, draw the upper left side down, then without lifting turn right along the base of the upper part
3. lift, draw the upper right side down
4. lift, draw the lower left side down, then without lifting turn right along the bottom
5. lift, draw the lower right side down

Unlike **年**, the long stem comes first here, and the four sides hang from it. Here it is read **だ** (*da*), as in **言い出す**; this lesson asks only for that reading.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-DASU-01, JA-LEX-C254-IIDASU-01] -->

1. Find **出** in **言い出す** and say *iidasu*.
2. Copy **出** three times, saying *da* at each one.
3. Find it once more, in **言い出しました** (*iidashimashita*), and say the word.
