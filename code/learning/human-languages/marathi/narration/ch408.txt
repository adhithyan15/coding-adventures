Marathi, chapter 408: Boats and Oars.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नावाडी (nāvāḍī) — a boatman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a signpost, then the Marathi for a long trudge.

You'll want to know: नावाडी.
नावाडी — nāvāḍī — "a boatman".
नावाड्याने आम्हाला नदीपलीकडे नेलं. nāvāḍyāne āmhālā nadīpalīkaḍe nelaṁ. The boatman took us across the river.
Masculine.
Built on नाव, a boat; before an ending it becomes नावाड्या-.

The word, taken apart.
नावाडी (nāvāḍī) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi nāvāḍī, "boatman", under Sanskrit nāvā, "boat" (CDIAL 7081).

Lesson 2 of 5.
वल्हे (valhe) — an oar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a long trudge, then the Marathi for a boatman.

You'll want to know: वल्हे.
वल्हे — valhe — "an oar".
होडीत दोन वल्ही होती. hoḍīt don valhī hotī. There were two oars in the boat.
Neuter. Plural: वल्ही, valhī.
A neuter noun: one वल्हे (valhe), two वल्ही.

The word, taken apart.
वल्हे (valhe) is a Marathi word for an oar; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
वल्हवणे (valhavṇe) — to row.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a boatman, then the Marathi for an oar.

You'll want to know: वल्हवणे.
वल्हवणे — valhavṇe — "to row".
नावाडी हळूहळू होडी वल्हवत होता. nāvāḍī haḷūhaḷū hoḍī valhavat hotā. The boatman was slowly rowing the boat.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वल्हवलं, tyāne valhavlaṁ.
Built on वल्हे (valhe), an oar: होडी वल्हवणे (valhavṇe), to row a boat.

The word, taken apart.
वल्हवणे (valhavṇe) is a Marathi verb built on वल्हे (valhe), "an oar", with the ending -वणे; the origin of the noun is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
हेलकावणे (helkāvṇe) — to toss about; to rock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an oar, then the Marathi for to row.

You'll want to know: हेलकावणे.
हेलकावणे — helkāvṇe — "to toss about; to rock".
वादळात होडी हेलकावत होती. vādaḷāt hoḍī helkāvat hotī. The boat was tossing about in the storm.
Past (he …): तो हेलकावला, to helkāvlā.
The noun हेलकावा is one toss: हेलकावे खाणे, to be tossed about.

The word, taken apart.
हेलकावणे (helkāvṇe) is a Marathi verb for a boat or a load swinging from side to side; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
नांगर (nāṅgar) — an anchor; a plough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to row, then the Marathi for to toss about.

You'll want to know: नांगर.
नांगर — nāṅgar — "an anchor; a plough".
जहाजाने बंदरात नांगर टाकला. jahājāne bandarāt nāṅgar ṭāklā. The ship dropped anchor in the harbour.
Masculine.
नांगर (nāṅgar) टाकणे, to drop anchor; on a farm the same word is the plough.

The word, taken apart.
नांगर (nāṅgar) is inherited in the sense "plough": CDIAL lists Marathi nā̃gar under Sanskrit lāṅgala, "plough" (CDIAL 11006); how the word also came to mean a ship's anchor is not settled.
