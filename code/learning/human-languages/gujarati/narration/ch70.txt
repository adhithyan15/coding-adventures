Gujarati, chapter 70: Red, Yellow, Green.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લાલ (lāl) — red.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a sparrow, then the Gujarati for a fish.

You'll want to know: લાલ.
લાલ (lāl) — "red".

The word, taken apart.
લાલ (lāl) is a loan, usually traced to Persian lāl, "red".

What you've built.
One more word for red, yellow, green.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: lāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lāl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say lāl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a sparrow, then the Gujarati for a fish, then say lāl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does લાલ (lāl) mean? ("Red".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
લીલો (līlo) — green.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for red, then the Gujarati for yellow.

You'll want to know: લીલો.
લીલો (līlo) — "green". The colour of a પાન.

The word, taken apart.
લીલો (līlo) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit nīla, "dark blue, dark green, black" (CDIAL 7563). CDIAL lists it beside nīlũ and nīḷũ; the Gujarati word changed its first n to l.

વાદળી (vādaḷī) — blue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: વાદળી.
વાદળી (vādaḷī) — "blue". It is built from વાદળ, cloud.

The word, taken apart.
વાદળી (vādaḷī) is built on vādaḷ, "cloud", which CDIAL lists, with vādḷī, "small cloud", under Sanskrit vārdala, "rainy day, bad weather" (CDIAL 11567).
