Spanish, chapter 649: Sobre dos ruedas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la moto — the motorbike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the lane, then the Spanish for to tow.

You'll want to know: la moto.
la moto — "the motorbike".
Voy al trabajo en moto. I go to work by motorbike.
Short for la motocicleta, so it is feminine.

The word, taken apart.
moto is short for motocicleta, which comes from French motocyclette (EtymDB), built on words for an engine and a cycle.

Lesson 2 of 5.
el motorista — the motorcyclist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to tow, then the Spanish for the motorbike.

You'll want to know: el motorista.
el motorista — "the motorcyclist".
Los motoristas llevan chaqueta y guantes. Motorcyclists wear jackets and gloves.
One form for both: el motorista, la motorista.

The word, taken apart.
motorista is built on motor, a learned borrowing from Latin mōtor "a mover" (Lewis & Short), with -ista, the ending for a person, from Greek -istḗs.

Lesson 3 of 5.
el patinete — the scooter (kick or electric).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the motorbike, then the Spanish for the motorcyclist.

You'll want to know: el patinete.
el patinete — "the scooter (kick or electric)".
Muchos jóvenes usan patinete eléctrico. A lot of young people use electric scooters.
In Latin America people often say el scooter or el monopatín.

The word, taken apart.
patinete is built on patín "skate", which was borrowed from French patin, built on patte "paw, foot" (EtymDB).
