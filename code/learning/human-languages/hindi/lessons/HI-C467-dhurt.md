## You'll want to know: धूर्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C467-KAPAT467-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धूर्त** — *dhūrt* — "crafty; a cheat".

> धूर्त आदमी ने बूढ़ी औरत से उसके पैसे ले लिए।
>
> *dhūrt ādmī ne būṛhī aurat se uske paise le liye.*
>
> A crafty man took the old woman's money from her.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूर्त** (*dhūrt*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *dhūrta*, "cunning, crafty", and as a noun "a rogue, a cheat" (Monier-Williams).
