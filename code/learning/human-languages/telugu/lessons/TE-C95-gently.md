## You'll want to know: మెల్లగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C95-ASK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**మెల్లగా** (*mellagā*) — "gently, softly", and "slowly".

It overlaps with **నెమ్మదిగా**. **మెల్లగా** leans to softness: a quiet voice, a careful hand.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A second slowly, with softness in it.
