## You'll want to know: شیو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C482-GROOM482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شیو** — *shev* — "a shave".

> ابو روز صبح شیو بناتے ہیں۔
>
> *abbū roz subah shev banāte haiṅ.*
>
> Dad shaves every morning.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شیو** (*shev*) is borrowed from English *shave*.
