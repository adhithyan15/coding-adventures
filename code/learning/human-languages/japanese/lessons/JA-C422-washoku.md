## You'll want to know: わしょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C422-WASHOKU422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**わしょく** — *washoku* — "food in the Japanese style".

> **たびさきで わしょくを たべました。** — *tabisaki de washoku o tabemashita* — "I ate Japanese food where I was staying."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わしょく** (*washoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *wa*, "Japanese style" (Middle Chinese *hwa*) and *shoku*, "food" (*zyik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
