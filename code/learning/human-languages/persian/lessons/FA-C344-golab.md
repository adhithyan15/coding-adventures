## You'll want to know: گلاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C344-NAZR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گلاب** (*golâb*) — "rosewater".

> بوی گلاب در همه خانه بود.
>
> *Bu-ye golâb dar hame-ye khâne bud.*
>
> The scent of rosewater filled the whole house.

*gol* "rose, flower" + *âb* "water".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*golâb* is a compound of *gol* "rose" and *âb* "water", both native Persian words (EtymDB lists the compound).
