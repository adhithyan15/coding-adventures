## You'll want to know: interpréter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C515-SCENE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**interpréter** — "to perform, to play (a role); to interpret".

> Elle interprète le rôle de la mère.
>
> *She plays the role of the mother.*

Also to interpret a dream or a text. An interpreter between languages is *un interprète*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*interpréter* is a learned borrowing from Latin *interpretārī* "to explain, to translate" (EtymDB), from *interpres* "a go-between, an interpreter" (Lewis & Short).
