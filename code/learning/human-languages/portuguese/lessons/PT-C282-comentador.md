## You'll want to know: o comentador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C282-AFIRMAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o comentador** — "the commentator, the pundit".

> Na televisão, um comentador explicou os resultados.
>
> *On television a pundit explained the results.*

Portugal’s TV is full of *comentadores* of politics and football. A woman is *a comentadora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comentador* is built on *comentar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *commentārī* "to think over, to study"; Latin itself had *commentātor* "a deviser, an interpreter" (Lewis & Short).
