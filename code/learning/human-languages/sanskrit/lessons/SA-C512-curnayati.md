## You'll want to know: चूर्णयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C512-CHURIKA512-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चूर्णयति** — *cūrṇayati* — "grinds to powder".

> सा मरीचं चूर्णयति।
>
> *sā marīcaṃ cūrṇayati.*
>
> She grinds the pepper.

Past (she …): सा मरीचं चूर्णितवती, *sā marīcaṃ cūrṇitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूर्णयति** (*cūrṇayati*) is built on *cūrṇa*, "powder, flour": MW gives *cūrṇayati* as to reduce to powder, to grind, to pound (MW).
