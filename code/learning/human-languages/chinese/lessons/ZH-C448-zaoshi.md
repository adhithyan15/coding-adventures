## You'll want to know: 早市
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C448-MARKET448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**早市** — *zǎoshì* — "morning market; a market held at dawn".

> **我们早上去早市买菜** *wǒmen zǎoshang qù zǎoshì mǎi cài* — In the morning we go to the early market to buy vegetables.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**早市** (*zǎoshì*) is built from 早 'early' and 市 'a market', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsawX* and *dzyiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the morning'.
