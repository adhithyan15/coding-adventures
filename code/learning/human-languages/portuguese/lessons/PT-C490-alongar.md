## You'll want to know: alongar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C490-FORMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**alongar** — "to stretch; to lengthen".

> Depois de correr, alongo as pernas.
>
> *After running, I stretch my legs.*

*Fazer alongamentos* — to do stretches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alongar* is built from *a-* and *longo* "long", which is inherited from Latin *longus* (Lewis & Short).
