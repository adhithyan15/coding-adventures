# Review — what will happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to the market". (*kal maiṁ bāzār gayā*.) Now move it: "Tomorrow I will go to the market". (*kal maiṁ bāzār jāūṅgā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C277-WILL-BE-01, HI-LEX-C94-SPAN-04, HI-LEX-C88-TIME-01, HI-LEX-C185-THG185-02, HI-CONCEPT-C36-GHAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Tomorrow I will go to the market, and then I will come home.]
- [YOU SAY: Will you come?, to a friend. Yes, I will come.]
- [YOU SAY: The train will come at five o'clock.]
- [YOU SAY: It will be cold tomorrow, and it will rain.]
- [YOU SAY: Arun and Mira will eat at home.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C277-WILL-BE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "You will go", to a friend? (**जाओगे**, or **जाओगी**.) "He will give"? (**देगा**.) "It will be"? (**होगा**, or **होगी** for a feminine thing.) "The train will come"? (**ट्रेन आएगी**: **ट्रेन** is feminine.)
