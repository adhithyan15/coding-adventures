Punjabi, chapter 150: To sew, To fill, To pull, To wake up, To drive.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā) — to sew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to use, then the Punjabi for to make.

You'll want to know: ਸਿਉਣਾ.
ਸਿਉਣਾ — siuṇā — "to sew".

The word, taken apart.
ਸਿਉਣਾ (siuṇā) is inherited. CDIAL lists it, as siūṁṇā, under sīvayati, the second form beside Sanskrit sīvyati, "sews" (CDIAL 13444).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਖਿੱਚਣਾ (khiccṇā) — to pull.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to sew, then the Punjabi for to fill.

You'll want to know: ਖਿੱਚਣਾ.
ਖਿੱਚਣਾ — khiccṇā — "to pull".

The word, taken apart.
ਖਿੱਚਣਾ (khiccṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khiñc, "drag, pull", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3881).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā) — to drive, to run (a machine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to pull, then the Punjabi for to wake up.

You'll want to know: ਚਲਾਉਣਾ.
ਚਲਾਉਣਾ — calāuṇā — "to drive, to run (a machine)".

The word, taken apart.
ਚਲਾਉਣਾ (calāuṇā) is the causing form of calṇā, "to move, to go": to drive is "to make go". CDIAL lists the verb under Sanskrit calati, "goes away; shakes, trembles" (CDIAL 4715).
