## You'll want to know: إعجاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C490-DAHSHA490-05]; assesses=[] -->

**إعجاب** — *iʿjāb* — "admiration".

> تكلم المدير عن عمل الفريق بإعجاب.
>
> *takallama al-mudīr ʿan ʿamal al-farīq bi-iʿjāb.*
>
> The manager spoke about the team's work with admiration.

It takes بـ for what is admired: إعجاب بالفريق.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إعجاب** (*iʿjāb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-j-b*, "to wonder, to marvel" (Lane). Iʿjāb is the verbal noun of Form IV, to make someone marvel.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
