## You'll want to know: zhíde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C366-ADVICE366-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíde* — "to be worth it; to be worth doing".

> *zhè běn shū zhíde kàn* — This book is worth reading.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíde* is built from *zhí* and 得 'to get', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *driH* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hold' and 'to meet with', and the second 'gain'. The second is said light here.
