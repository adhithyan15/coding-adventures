5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
leiden — to suffer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to land, then the German for to smile.

You'll want to know: leiden.
leiden — "to suffer".

The word, taken apart.
leiden from Middle High German līden from Old High German līdan, "to go, to undergo" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. To suffer is to go through something. Dutch lijden is the same verb.
