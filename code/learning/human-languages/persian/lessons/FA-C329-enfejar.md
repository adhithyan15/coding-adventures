## You'll want to know: انفجار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C329-ENFEJAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انفجار** (*enfejâr*) — "an explosion".

> صدای انفجار همه را بیدار کرد.
>
> *Sedâ-ye enfejâr hame râ bidâr kard.*
>
> The sound of the explosion woke everyone up.

*Enfejâr-e gâz* "a gas explosion".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enfejâr* is borrowed from Arabic *infijār*, "explosion, bursting" (EtymDB lists both the Arabic and the Persian word).
