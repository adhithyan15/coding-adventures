## You'll want to know: хамить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C435-OBSLUZHIVANIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**хамить** (*khamít*) — "to be rude (to someone)".

> Официант хамил всем посетителям.
>
> *Ofitsiánt khamíl vsem posetítelyam.*
>
> The waiter was rude to all the customers.

Imperfective; perfective *нахамить*. Takes the dative. Present *хамлю, хамишь*. Colloquial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хамить* is built on *хам* 'a boor'. *Хам* is usually traced to a name from the Bible, a son who mocked his father; EtymDB lists it without a source, so the line is hedged.
