## You'll want to know: खाँसी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C106-UNWELL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खाँसी** (*khā̃sī*) — "a cough". It is feminine.

**मुझे खाँसी है** — "I have a cough". Same frame, new word in the middle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A second complaint in the same frame.
