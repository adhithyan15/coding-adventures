Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 22: Rickshaw and Horse: One New Consonant.
8 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.

Lesson 2 of 8.
श (śa) — the sh in rickshaw.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one sign until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Recall the five-word shopping payoff, say rickshaw, then write wife and familiar स.

Script — one sign.
श (śa) — sha.
Notice the vertical stem and the looped left side. Keep it distinct from familiar स.

Guided Practice.
Trace श (śa) once. Copy it once. Cover it, wait five seconds, and write it from the sh sound cue.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Unicode Devanagari chart.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Write the Devanagari sha consonant.


Lesson 3 of 8.
रिक्शा (rikśā) — rickshaw on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Say rickshaw and child, then write श (śa) and ्.

You'll want to know.
रि + क् + शा becomes रिक्शा — rikśā — rickshaw.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write रिक्शा (rikśā). Check that ि appears before र but is read after it, and that ् joins क to श (śa).

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 14 seconds]
From rikśā, write rickshaw.

Lesson 7 of 8.
Payoff — five transport words in four skills.

Lesson 8 of 8.
