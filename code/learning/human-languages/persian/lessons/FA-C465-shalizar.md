## You'll want to know: شالیزار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C465-MAZRAE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شالیزار** (*shâlizâr*) — "a rice paddy, a paddy field".

> در شمال شالیزارهای سبز زیادی هست.
>
> *Dar shomâl shâlizâr-hâ-ye sabz-e ziyâdi hast.*
>
> In the north there are many green rice paddies.

*shâli* 'rice in the husk' + *-zâr* 'a place full of'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shâlizâr* is built from *shâli* 'rice in the husk' and the native ending *-zâr* 'a place full of', as in *golzâr* 'a rose garden'. The origin of *shâli* is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.
