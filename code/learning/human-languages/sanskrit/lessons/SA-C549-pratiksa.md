## You'll want to know: प्रतीक्षा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C549-LALASA549-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतीक्षा** — *pratīkṣā* — "a wait; expectation".

> यदि प्रतीक्षा कठिना अस्ति, तर्हि पुस्तकं पठतु।
>
> *yadi pratīkṣā kaṭhinā asti, tarhi pustakaṃ paṭhatu.*
>
> If the wait is hard, read a book.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतीक्षा** (*pratīkṣā*) is built on *prati*, "towards", and the root √īkṣ, "to see", which also gives *pratīkṣate*, "waits"; MW gives *pratīkṣā* as expectation (MW).
