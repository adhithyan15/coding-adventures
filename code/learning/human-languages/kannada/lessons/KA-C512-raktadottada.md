## You'll want to know: ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C512-ROGA512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ** — *raktadottaḍa* — "blood pressure".

> ಉಪ್ಪು ಹೆಚ್ಚು ತಿಂದರೆ ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ ಏರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *uppu heccu tindare raktadottaḍa ēruttade.*
>
> If you eat a lot of salt, your blood pressure rises.

ರಕ್ತ, blood + ಒತ್ತಡ, pressure, joined with a ದ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ** (*raktadottaḍa*) joins Sanskrit *rakta*, "red; blood" (CDIAL 10539), and the native *ottaḍa*, "pressure", from *ottu*, "to press" (DEDR 1021).
