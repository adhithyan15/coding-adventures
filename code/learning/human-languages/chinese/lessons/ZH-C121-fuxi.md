## You'll want to know: fùxí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C121-LEARN121-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fùxí* — "to revise; to go over again".

> *wǒ wǎnshang fùxí hànyǔ* — I go over my Chinese in the evening.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fùxí* is built from fù 'again' and xí 'to learn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjuwk* and *zip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to learn again. The dictionary glosses the first 'to return, to repeat' and lists it with a departing tone too, *bjuwH*, glossed 'again': fù continues that one, since the regular change from *bjuwk* gives fú.
