## You'll want to know: きめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C168-DELAY168-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きめる** — *kimeru* — "to decide".

> **あした ははと よていを きめます。** — *ashita haha to yotei o kimemasu* — "Tomorrow I will decide the plan with my mother."

Polite **きめます**, *kimemasu*; an ichidan verb. Its intransitive partner is **きまる**, *kimaru*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きめる** (*kimeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *kimaru*, "to be decided"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
