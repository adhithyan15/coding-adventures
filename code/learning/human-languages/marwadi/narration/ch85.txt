Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 85: Sock, Pocket, Ring, Watch, Cap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मोजो (mojo) — a sock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for pyjama trousers, then the Marwadi for a shoe.

You'll want to know: मोजो.
मोजो — mojo — "a sock".

The word, taken apart.
मोजो (mojo) is a loan, usually traced to Persian mūza, "boot, stocking". Marwadi says the z as j.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अंगूठी (aṅgūṭhī) — a ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a sock, then the Marwadi for a pocket.

You'll want to know: अंगूठी.
अंगूठी — aṅgūṭhī — "a ring".

The word, taken apart.
अंगूठी (aṅgūṭhī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aṅguṣṭhya, "pertaining to the thumb". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 138); the match is through Hindi ãgūṭhī. It is built on Sanskrit aṅguṣṭha, "thumb".

घड़ी — ghaṛī — "a watch, a clock".

The word, taken apart.
घड़ी (ghaṛī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ghaṭī, "pot, water-clock". CDIAL lists Marwari ghaṛī under it (CDIAL 4406). The water-clock was a pot that sank in a set time, about twenty-four minutes.

Lesson 5 of 5.
टोपी (ṭopī) — a cap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a ring, then the Marwadi for a watch.

You'll want to know: टोपी.
टोपी — ṭopī — "a cap".

The word, taken apart.
टोपी (ṭopī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ṭoppa, "hat, covering", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5481); the match is through Hindi ṭopī and Gujarati ṭopī. CDIAL calls a link with Sanskrit āṭopa, which one proposal takes from Munda, very doubtful.
