## You'll want to know: അലർച്ച
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C380-NILAVILI380-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അലർച്ച** — *alarcca* — "a roar; a bellow".

> രാത്രി കാട്ടിൽ നിന്ന് ഒരു കടുവയുടെ അലർച്ച കേട്ടു.
>
> *rātri kāṭṭil ninnŭ oru kaṭuvayuṭe alarcca kēṭṭu.*
>
> At night a tiger's roar was heard from the forest.

The cry of a tiger, or of an angry crowd.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അലർച്ച** (*alarcca*) is native Dravidian, the noun of അലറുക, which DEDR 245 lists as "to roar, to bellow, to cry".
