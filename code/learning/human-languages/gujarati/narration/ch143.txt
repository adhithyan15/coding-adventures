You'll want to know: ભાગવું.
ભાગવું (bhāgvũ) — "to run away".

The word, taken apart.
ભાગવું (bhāgvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit bhagna, "broken, defeated", beside bhajyate, "is broken" (CDIAL 9361). It reports that J. Bloch separated the words meaning "flee" from this root.

A short Gujarati A1 writing checkpoint — a three-minute independent form attempt before the separate full A1 writing paper.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Warm-up.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
This is a short independent attempt, not a model. You will write the answers, and this page will not show you any copyable Gujarati answer.
Put away romanization, dictionaries, translators, and spell-checkers. Set a three-minute timer. Write in Gujarati script. Short, legible answers are enough.

Writing — timed assessment production.
One practical form — three minutes.
Complete all six fields in Gujarati script for a neighbourhood language exchange. Write one short answer in every field. Start the timer when you begin the first field; leave any unfinished field as evidence when it rings.
Your name.
Your city or village.
A language you speak.
One everyday action you do.
One drink or food you like.
One short question for a new member asking where they live.
When the three-minute timer rings, stop. Record the number of completed fields. An honest short attempt is more useful than a perfect untimed one.

Wrap-up Recall.
Spend at most one minute looking at this first attempt. Note one field you completed clearly and one field to practise next; do not rewrite the timed answer. Keep the page as evidence.
This checkpoint is not the complete A1 writing paper. On another day, close this lesson and open the separate A1 writing-only learner paper for the authentic form and named-reader message under one 20-minute assessment clock. That paper and its later rubric review are a separate assessment session, not work inside this under-five-minute lesson. It is writing practice only; it does not establish full A1 exam readiness.
