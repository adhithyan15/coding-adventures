## You'll want to know: verhandeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C278-VERHANDELN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**verhandeln** — "to negotiate".

> Die beiden Firmen verhandelten eine ganze Woche.
>
> *The two companies negotiated for a whole week.*

*Über etwas verhandeln* — to negotiate about something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verhandeln* is built on *handeln* "to deal", from Old High German *hantalōn*, which EtymDB derives from Proto-Germanic *handuz* "hand".
