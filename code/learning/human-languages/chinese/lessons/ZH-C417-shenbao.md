## You'll want to know: shēnbào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C417-CUSTOMS417-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shēnbào* — "to declare goods at customs".

> *nǐ yǒu dōngxi yào shēnbào ma* — Do you have anything to declare?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēnbào* is built from *shēn* 'to state' and *bào* 'to report', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syin* and *pawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stretch' and 'to repeat' and the second 'to report'.
