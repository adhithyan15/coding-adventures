## You'll want to know: das Treppenhaus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C532-WG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Treppenhaus** — "stairwell".

> Im Treppenhaus riecht es nach Essen.
>
> *The stairwell smells of food.*

Plural: *die Treppenhäuser*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Treppenhaus* is built on *Treppe* "stairs", usually traced to Low German, and *Haus* "house"; EtymDB lists *Treppe* without a source.
