## You'll want to know: флейта
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C515-INSTRUMENTS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**флейта** (*fléyta*) — "a flute".

> Звук флейты очень нежный.
>
> *Zvuk fléyty óchen nézhnyy.*
>
> The sound of the flute is very gentle.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
