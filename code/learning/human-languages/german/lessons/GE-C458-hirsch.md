## You'll want to know: der Hirsch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C458-WILDTIERE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Hirsch** — "the stag, the deer".

> Im Herbst hört man die Hirsche im Wald rufen.
>
> *In autumn you can hear the stags calling in the forest.*

Plural: *die Hirsche*. The female is *die Hirschkuh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hirsch* is inherited through Middle High German *hirz* from Old High German *hiruz* and Proto-Germanic *herutaz* (EtymDB); English *hart* 'male deer' is a cousin.
