## You'll want to know: முறி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C482-KAYAM482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**முறி** — *muṟi* — "to snap; to break".

> மரத்திலிருந்து விழுந்து அவனுடைய கை முறிந்தது.
>
> *marattiliruntu viḻuntu avaṉuṭaiya kai muṟintatu.*
>
> He fell from a tree and broke his arm.

Past (it …): முறிந்தது, *muṟintatu*.

Of bones and branches: கிளை முறிந்தது, the branch snapped.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**முறி** (*muṟi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4975 lists the verb, spelled *muri*, "to break off, to snap".
