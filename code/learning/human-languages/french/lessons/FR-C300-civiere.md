## You'll want to know: la civière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C300-SECOURS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la civière** — "the stretcher".

> Ils l'ont transporté sur une civière.
>
> *They carried him on a stretcher.*

Carried by two people.
