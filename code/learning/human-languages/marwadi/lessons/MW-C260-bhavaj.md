## You'll want to know: भावज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C260-BAHU260-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भावज** — *bhāvaj* — "a brother's wife".

> म्हारी भावज रोटी पकावै है।
>
> *mhārī bhāvaj roṭī pakāvai hai.*
>
> My brother's wife is cooking flatbread.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भावज** (*bhāvaj*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhrātṛjāyā*, "a brother's wife"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bhāvaja* under it (CDIAL 9660).
