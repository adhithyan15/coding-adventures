## You'll want to know: નાણાં
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C429-SAFE429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**નાણાં** — *nāṇā̃* — "money; funds".

> દાદાએ નવી શાળા માટે નાણાં આપ્યાં.
>
> *dādāe navī śāḷā māṭe nāṇā̃ āpyā̃.*
>
> Grandfather gave funds for a new school.

A neuter noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નાણાં** (*nāṇā̃*) goes back to Sanskrit *nāṇaka*, "a coin"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a coin, money" (CDIAL 7050). The word is plural.
