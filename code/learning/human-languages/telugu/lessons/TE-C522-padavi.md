## You'll want to know: పదవి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C522-VRTTI522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పదవి** — *padavi* — "a position; a post".

> ఆమె కంపెనీలో పెద్ద పదవిలో ఉంది.
>
> *āme kampenīlō pedda padavilō undi.*
>
> She holds a senior position in the company.

పదవిలో ఉండు, to hold a post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పదవి** (*padavi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *padavī*, "a road, a track; a station, a place" (Monier-Williams).
