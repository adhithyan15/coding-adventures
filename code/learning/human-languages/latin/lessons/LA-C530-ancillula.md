## You'll want to know: ancillula, ancillulae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C530-ERUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ancillula, ancillulae** — "a young maidservant".

> Ancillula dominae speculum dat.
>
> *The little maid gives her mistress a mirror.*

Feminine, a diminutive of *ancilla*, already known, which is itself a diminutive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ancillula** is a diminutive of *ancilla* "maidservant" (Lewis & Short).
