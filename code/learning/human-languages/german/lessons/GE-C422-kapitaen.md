## You'll want to know: der Kapitän
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C422-KREUZFAHRT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kapitän** — "the captain".

> Der Kapitän begrüßt die Gäste an Bord.
>
> *The captain welcomes the passengers on board.*

Plural: *die Kapitäne*. Also the captain of a team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kapitän* came into German through Middle High German *kapitān* from Medieval Latin *capitāneus* 'chief' (EtymDB), built on Latin *caput* 'head' (Lewis & Short).
