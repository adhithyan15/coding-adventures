## You'll want to know: dùchuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C411-FERRY411-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dùchuán* — "ferry".

> *wǒmen zuò dùchuán qù nàr* — We're taking the ferry over there.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dùchuán* is built from *dù* 'to cross water' and 船 'a boat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *duH* and *zywen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to cross over'.
