Sanskrit, chapter 530: Planning a Feast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भोज्यम् (bhojyam) — eatables; food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for hurries along, then the Sanskrit for haste.

You'll want to know: भोज्यम्.
भोज्यम् — bhojyam — "eatables; food".
यदि अतिथयः आगच्छन्ति, तर्हि बहु भोज्यं पचामः। yadi atithayaḥ āgacchanti, tarhi bahu bhojyaṃ pacāmaḥ. If the guests come, we will cook a lot of food.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
भोज्यम् (bhojyam) comes from the root √bhuj, "to enjoy, to eat", with the ending -ya; MW gives bhojya as eatable, what is eaten (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
लड्डुकः (laḍḍukaḥ) — a laddu.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for eatables, then the Sanskrit for a drink.

You'll want to know: लड्डुकः.
लड्डुकः — laḍḍukaḥ — "a laddu".
यदि उत्सवः भवति, तर्हि माता लड्डुकान् पचति। yadi utsavaḥ bhavati, tarhi mātā laḍḍukān pacati. If there is a festival, my mother makes laddus.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
लड्डुकः (laḍḍukaḥ) is the word MW gives, beside laḍḍu, for a sweet made of ground pulse or flour with sugar and spice, fried in ghee; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पानकम् (pānakam) — a sherbet; a cool drink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a drink, then the Sanskrit for a laddu.

You'll want to know: पानकम्.
पानकम् — pānakam — "a sherbet; a cool drink".
यदि अतिथिः श्रान्तः अस्ति, तर्हि तस्मै पानकं ददातु। yadi atithiḥ śrāntaḥ asti, tarhi tasmai pānakaṃ dadātu. If the guest is tired, please give him a sherbet.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पानकम् (pānakam) is built on pāna, "drinking", from the root √pā, "to drink"; MW gives pānaka as a draught, a drink, a beverage (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पर्युषितः (paryuṣitaḥ) — stale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a laddu, then the Sanskrit for a sherbet.

You'll want to know: पर्युषितः.
पर्युषितः — paryuṣitaḥ — "stale".
यदि अन्नं पर्युषितम् अस्ति, तर्हि अहं न खादामि। yadi annaṃ paryuṣitam asti, tarhi ahaṃ na khādāmi. If the food is stale, I do not eat it.

The word, taken apart.
पर्युषितः (paryuṣitaḥ) is built on pari and the root √vas, "to stay, to pass the night", as its past participle: kept overnight; MW gives paryuṣita as not fresh, stale (MW).
