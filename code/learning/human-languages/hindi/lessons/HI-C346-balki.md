## You'll want to know: बल्कि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C346-BAHAS346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बल्कि** — *balki* — "but rather, on the contrary".

> यह सस्ता नहीं है, बल्कि बहुत महँगा है।
>
> *yah sastā nahīṃ hai, balki bahut mahãgā hai.*
>
> It isn't cheap; on the contrary, it's very expensive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बल्कि** (*balki*) is usually traced to Persian *balki*, "but rather".
