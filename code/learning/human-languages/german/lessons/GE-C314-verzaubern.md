## You'll want to know: verzaubern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C314-ZAUBERN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**verzaubern** — "to bewitch, to put a spell on; to charm".

> Die Hexe verzauberte das Mädchen.
>
> *The witch put a spell on the girl.*

Also figurative: *Die Musik verzauberte alle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verzaubern* is built on *zaubern*, Middle High German *zoubern* (EtymDB), with *ver-*: to change by magic.
