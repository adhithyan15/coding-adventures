# ডুব দেওয়া (ḍub deoyā) — to take a dip; to dive under

## You'll want to know: ডুব দেওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C398-SROT398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ডুব দেওয়া** — *ḍub deoyā* — "to take a dip; to dive under".

> গরমে অরুণ পুকুরে ডুব দিল।
>
> *gôrôme ôrun pukure ḍub dilô.*
>
> In the heat Arun took a dip in the pond.

Past (he/she …): তিনি পুকুরে ডুব দিলেন, *tini pukure ḍub dilen*.

ডুব is a plunge under water; ডোবা, to sink, is the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডুব দেওয়া** (*ḍub deoyā*) joins ডুব, "a plunge under water", from the reconstructed *ḍubb*, "sink", under which CDIAL lists Bengali *ḍubā*, "to sink" (CDIAL 5561), to দেওয়া, to give.
