## You'll want to know: yěwài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C441-CAMP441-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yěwài* — "the open country; the wild".

> *zhōumò wǒmen zuò chē qù yěwài* — At the weekend we're driving out into the open country.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yěwài* is built from *yě* 'open fields' and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yaeX* and *ngwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older dictionary, as the land beyond the town.
