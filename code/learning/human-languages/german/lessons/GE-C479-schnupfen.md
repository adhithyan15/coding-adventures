## You'll want to know: der Schnupfen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C479-SCHNUPFEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schnupfen** — "head cold, runny nose".

> Ich habe Schnupfen und brauche viele Taschentücher.
>
> *I have a runny nose and need lots of tissues.*

Usually without an article: *Ich habe Schnupfen.* No plural in everyday use.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schnupfen* is built on the verb *schnupfen* "to sniff" (EtymDB), probably an imitative word for the sound of a blocked nose.
