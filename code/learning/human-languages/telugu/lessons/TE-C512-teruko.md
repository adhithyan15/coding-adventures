# తేరుకో (tērukō) — to come round; to recover from a shock

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: తేరుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C512-JABBU512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తేరుకో** — *tērukō* — "to come round; to recover from a shock".

> ఆ వార్త నుంచి అమ్మ తేరుకోవడానికి వారం పట్టింది.
>
> *ā vārta nuñci amma tērukōvaḍāniki vāraṁ paṭṭindi.*
>
> It took mum a week to get over that news.

Past (I …): తేరుకున్నాను, *tērukunnānu*.

-నుంచి తేరుకో, to get over a shock, a fright or a faint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తేరుకో** (*tērukō*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3471 lists Telugu *tēṟu* with Tamil *tēṟu*, "to recover from swooning, to be comforted, to cheer up"; -కో is the reflexive ending.
