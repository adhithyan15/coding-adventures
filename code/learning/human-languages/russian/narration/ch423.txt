Russian, chapter 423: Delays and cancellations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
задержка (zadérzhka) — a delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a terminal, then the Russian for a flight attendant.

You'll want to know: задержка.
задержка (zadérzhka) — "a delay".
Из-за тумана была большая задержка. Iz-za tumána bylá bolsháya zadérzhka. Because of the fog there was a long delay.
Feminine. Рейс с задержкой — a delayed flight.

The word, taken apart.
задержка (zadérzhka) is built on задержать 'to hold back', from за- and держать 'to hold'. Держать is inherited from Proto-Slavic dĭržati 'to hold' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
компенсация (kompensátsiya) — compensation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cancellation, then the Russian for to cancel.

You'll want to know: компенсация.
компенсация (kompensátsiya) — "compensation".
За задержку пассажиры получили компенсацию. Za zadérzhku passazhíry poluchíli kompensátsiyu. The passengers got compensation for the delay.
Feminine; formal.

The word, taken apart.
компенсация (kompensátsiya) is usually traced through French compensation to Latin compensātiō 'a balancing' (EtymDB lists both), from compensāre 'to weigh one thing against another' (Lewis & Short).
