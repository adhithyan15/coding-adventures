## You'll want to know: nóngmín
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C225-FARM225-02]; assesses=[] -->

*nóngmín* — "farmer".

> *tā bàba shì nóngmín* — His dad is a farmer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nóngmín* is built from *nóng* 'farming' and *mín* 'the people', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nowng* and *mjin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the second, after an older book, as the multitude.
