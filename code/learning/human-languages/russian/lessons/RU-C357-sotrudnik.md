## You'll want to know: сотрудник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C357-SOTRUDNIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сотрудник** (*sotrúdnik*) — "an employee, a member of staff".

> В нашей фирме двадцать сотрудников.
>
> *V náshey fírme dvádtsat sotrúdnikov.*
>
> There are twenty employees in our firm.

Masculine; a woman is *сотрудница*. Formal; at work people also say *коллега*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**сотрудник** is built from *со-* "together" and *труд* "work, labour", the root of *трудный* "hard": one who works alongside you.
