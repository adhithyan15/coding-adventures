## You'll want to know: gǎndào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎndào* — "to feel".

> *wǒ gǎndào hěn kāixīn* — I feel really happy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎndào* is built from *gǎn* and 到 'to reach', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *komX* and *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
