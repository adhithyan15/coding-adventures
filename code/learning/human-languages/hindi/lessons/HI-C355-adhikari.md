## You'll want to know: अधिकारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C355-VIBHAG355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिकारी** — *adhikārī* — "an official, an officer".

> अधिकारी ने मेरी अर्ज़ी पढ़ी।
>
> *adhikārī ne merī arzī paṛhī.*
>
> The official read my application.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिकारी** (*adhikārī*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *adhikārin*, "a superintendent, a governor" (Monier-Williams).
