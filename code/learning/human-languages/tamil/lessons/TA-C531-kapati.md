## You'll want to know: கபடி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C531-PANTHU531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**கபடி** — *kapaṭi* — "kabaddi".

> கிராமத்துத் திருவிழாவில் கபடிப் போட்டி நடந்தது.
>
> *kirāmattut tiruviḻāvil kapaṭip pōṭṭi naṭantatu.*
>
> There was a kabaddi match at the village festival.

A team game: a raider crosses into the other side, touches players and must get back on one breath.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **கபடி** (*kapaṭi*) is not settled: no dictionary we can check gives its source.
