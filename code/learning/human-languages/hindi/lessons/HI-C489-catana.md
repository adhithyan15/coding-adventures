## You'll want to know: चटाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C489-MARHAM489-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चटाना** — *caṭānā* — "to give (a baby) something to lick".

> माँ ने बच्चे को थोड़ा शहद चटाया।
>
> *māṁ ne bacce ko thoṛā śahad caṭāyā.*
>
> Mother gave the baby a little honey to lick.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को दवा चटाई, *usne bacce ko davā caṭāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चटाना** (*caṭānā*) is the causative partner of *cāṭnā*, "to lick", which CDIAL lists under *\*caṭṭ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4573). Medicine for small children is often given this way, mixed with honey.
