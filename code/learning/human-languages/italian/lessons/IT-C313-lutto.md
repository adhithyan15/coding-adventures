## You'll want to know: il lutto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C313-LUTTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il lutto** — "mourning, bereavement".

> La città è in lutto.
>
> *The city is in mourning.*

*Essere in lutto* — to be in mourning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lutto* is inherited from Latin *lūctus* "grief, sorrow" (EtymDB).
