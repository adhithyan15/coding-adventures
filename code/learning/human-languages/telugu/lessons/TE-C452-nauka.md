## You'll want to know: నౌక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C452-NAUKA452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నౌక** — *nauka* — "a ship; a vessel".

> పెద్ద నౌక సముద్రంలో దూరంగా కనిపించింది.
>
> *pedda nauka samudraṁlō dūraṅgā kanipiñcindi.*
>
> A big ship appeared far out at sea.

Plural: నౌకలు, *naukalu*.

A formal word. Plural నౌకలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నౌక** (*nauka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *naukā*, "a boat, a ship" (Monier-Williams).
