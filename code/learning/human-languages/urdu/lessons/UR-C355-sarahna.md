## You'll want to know: سراہنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C355-TARIF355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سراہنا** — *sarāhnā* — "to commend; to praise".

> سب نے اس کے کام کو سراہا۔
>
> *sab ne us ke kām ko sarāhā.*
>
> Everyone praised her work.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کوشش کو سراہا, *us ne koshish ko sarāhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سراہنا** (*sarāhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sarāhnā* under Sanskrit *ślāghate*, "praises" (CDIAL 12734).
