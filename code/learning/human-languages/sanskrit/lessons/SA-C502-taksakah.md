## You'll want to know: तक्षकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C502-KARMASALA502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तक्षकः** — *takṣakaḥ* — "a carpenter".

> तक्षकः नूतनाम् आसन्दीं रचयति।
>
> *takṣakaḥ nūtanām āsandīṃ racayati.*
>
> The carpenter makes a new chair.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तक्षकः** (*takṣakaḥ*) comes from the root √takṣ, "to pare, to fashion by cutting", with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *takṣaka* the sense "a carpenter" (MW).
