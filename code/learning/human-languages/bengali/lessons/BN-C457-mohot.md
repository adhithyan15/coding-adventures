## You'll want to know: মহৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C457-UDAR457-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মহৎ** — *môhôt* — "noble; great-hearted".

> গরিব ছাত্রটির পড়ার খরচ দিয়ে তিনি একটা মহৎ কাজ করলেন।
>
> *gôrib chhātrôṭir pôṛār khôrôch diye tini ekṭā môhôt kāj kôrlen.*
>
> By paying for the poor student's studies, he did a noble thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মহৎ** (*môhôt*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mahat*, "great, high, eminent" (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for greatness of heart. It ends in the new letter ৎ.
