## You'll want to know: o extrato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C455-BANCO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o extrato** — "the bank statement; the extract".

> Pedi um extrato da conta no balcão.
>
> *I asked for an account statement at the counter.*

*Extrato de tomate* — tomato purée.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*extrato* is a learned borrowing from Latin *extractum* 'something drawn out' (EtymDB), from *extrahere* 'to draw out', on *trahere* 'to pull'.
