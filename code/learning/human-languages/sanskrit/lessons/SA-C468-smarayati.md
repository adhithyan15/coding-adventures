## You'll want to know: स्मारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C468-KRTAVEDIN468-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मारयति** — *smārayati* — "reminds".

> माता मां प्रतिदिनम् औषधं स्मारयति।
>
> *mātā māṃ pratidinam auṣadhaṃ smārayati.*
>
> Mother reminds me about my medicine every day.

Past (she …): सा मां स्मारितवती, *sā māṃ smāritavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मारयति** (*smārayati*) is built on the causative of the root √smṛ, "to remember", the root of **स्मरति**: to make someone remember; MW's ppp *smārita* is "reminded" (MW).
