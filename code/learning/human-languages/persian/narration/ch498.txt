Persian, chapter 498: Weight and Diet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
لاغر شدن (lâghar shodan) — to lose weight, to get thin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the spine, then the Persian for a skull.

You'll want to know: لاغر شدن.
لاغر شدن (lâghar shodan) — "to lose weight, to get thin".
او با ورزش پنج کیلو لاغر شد. u bâ varzesh panj kilo lâghar shod. He lost five kilos through exercise.
lâghar 'thin' + shodan 'to become'; present stem shav-. The opposite is châq shodan.

The word, taken apart.
lâghar is an everyday Persian word for thin, but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled. shodan, 'to become', is inherited.

Lesson 2 of 5.
چاق شدن (châq shodan) — to put on weight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a skull, then the Persian for to lose weight.

You'll want to know: چاق شدن.
چاق شدن (châq shodan) — "to put on weight".
او در تعطیلات کمی چاق شد. u dar ta'tilât kami châq shod. He put on a bit of weight over the holidays.
châq 'fat' + shodan. châq is blunt; topol 'chubby' is kinder.

The word, taken apart.
châq is often linked with a Turkic word, but EtymDB lists it without a source and its origin is not settled. shodan, 'to become', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
رژیم گرفتن (rezhim gereftan) — to go on a diet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to lose weight, then the Persian for to put on weight.

You'll want to know: رژیم گرفتن.
رژیم گرفتن (rezhim gereftan) — "to go on a diet".
مادرم از اول ماه رژیم گرفته است. mâdaram az avval-e mâh rezhim gerefte ast. My mother has been on a diet since the start of the month.
rezhim 'diet; regime' + gereftan 'to take'; present stem gir-.

The word, taken apart.
rezhim was borrowed from French régime, 'rule, order', which EtymDB traces to Latin regimen (EtymDB); Lewis & Short gloss regimen 'a guiding, a rule' (Lewis & Short). gereftan, 'to take', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ویتامین (vitâmin) — a vitamin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to put on weight, then the Persian for to go on a diet.

You'll want to know: ویتامین.
ویتامین (vitâmin) — "a vitamin".
پرتقال ویتامین زیادی دارد. porteqâl vitâmin-e ziyâdi dârad. Oranges have a lot of vitamins.
Plural vitâminhâ.

The word, taken apart.
vitâmin is a modern loan, usually traced to French vitamine. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
