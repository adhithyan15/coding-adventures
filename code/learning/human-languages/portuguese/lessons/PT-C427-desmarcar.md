## You'll want to know: desmarcar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C427-RESERVA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**desmarcar** — "to call off, to cancel (an appointment or booking)".

> Desmarquei o quarto porque fiquei doente.
>
> *I cancelled the room because I fell ill.*

*Marcar* — to book; *desmarcar* — to call it off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desmarcar* is built from *des-* and *marcar* 'to mark, to book'. *Marcar* is usually linked with *marco* 'boundary stone', from Germanic *markō* 'boundary' (EtymDB).
