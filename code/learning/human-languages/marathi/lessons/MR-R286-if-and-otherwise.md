# Review — if and otherwise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C285-PEKSHA-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C285-SARVAT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C285-PASUN-SPAN-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C285-FROM-UNTIL-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the train is faster than the bus" and "I have lived in Pune for three years". (*gāḍī baspekṣā jalad āhe*, *mī tīn varṣāmpāsūn puṇyāt rāhto*.) Then "the biggest" and "from morning until evening". (*sarvāt moṭhā*, *sakāḷpāsūn sandhyākāḷparyant*.) Last, **ऋतू** and **ज्ञान**, and their letters **ऋ** and **ञ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C286-JAR-TAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C286-IF-ASEL-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C286-LYAS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C286-NAHITAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C281-U-NAYE-01] -->

- [YOU SAY: If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: If you have time, come.]
- [YOU SAY: Leave early, otherwise you'll miss the train.]
- [YOU SAY: If it rains, you shouldn't go out.]
- [YOU SAY: one plan of yours for next week, with **जर … तर**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C286-JAR-TAR-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C286-IF-ASEL-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C286-LYAS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C286-NAHITAR-01, MR-SCRIPT-NYA-01, MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "If … then"? (**जर … तर**.) Which form does the condition take? (The past: **पडला**.) "Should it rain"? (**पाऊस पडल्यास**.) "Otherwise"? (**नाहीतर**.)
