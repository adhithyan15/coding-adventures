## You'll want to know: hùzhào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C116-CARRY116-02]; assesses=[] -->

*hùzhào* — "passport".

> *nǐ dài hùzhào le ma* — Have you brought your passport?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hùzhào* is built from hù 'to protect' and zhào 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huH* and *tsyewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The passport is a modern sense of the pair, and how it came to it is not settled.
