Italian, chapter 95: Shower, Bathtub, Shelf, Curtain, Cushion.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
la doccia — a shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a fridge, then the Italian for an oven.

You'll want to know: la doccia.
la doccia — "a shower".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: la doccia]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: la doccia, once more]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 6 of 7.
Can, want and places — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a curtain; a tent and a cushion.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: to be able to, to have to, to manage, a need, a trade]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to want, I would like, to prefer, to hope, to wish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an airport, a stop, a village; a country, a cinema, a theatre]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a town hall, a supermarket, a petrol station, a swimming pool, a stadium]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a factory, an office, a corner, a crossroads, traffic lights]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To be able to? (potere.) To have to? (dovere.) To manage? (riuscire.) A need? (il bisogno.) A trade? (il mestiere.) To want? (volere.) I would like? (vorrei.) To prefer? (preferire.) To hope? (sperare.) To wish? (desiderare.) An airport? (l'aeroporto.) A stop? (la fermata.) A village; a country? (il paese.) A cinema? (il cinema.) A theatre? (il teatro.) A town hall? (il municipio.) A supermarket? (il supermercato.) A petrol station? (il distributore.) A swimming pool? (la piscina.) A stadium? (lo stadio.) A factory? (la fabbrica.) An office? (l'ufficio.) A corner? (l'angolo.) A crossroads? (l'incrocio.) Traffic lights? (il semaforo.)
