## You'll want to know: سر زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C506-TIES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سر زدن** (*sar zadan*) — "to drop in (on someone)".

> بعد از کار به مادرم سر میزنم.
>
> *ba'd az kâr be mâdaram sar mizanam.*
>
> After work I drop in on my mother.

*be kasi sar zadan*; literally 'to strike a head'. Present stem *zan-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sar zadan* is built on two inherited words: *sar*, 'head', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *zadan*, 'to strike'.
