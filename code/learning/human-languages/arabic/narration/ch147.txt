Arabic, chapter 147: Busy, Tired, Active, Kind, Angry.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
مشغول (mashghūl) — busy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for open, then the Arabic for closed.

You'll want to know: مشغول.
مشغول — mashghūl — "busy".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mashghūl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mashghūl, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mashghūl]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for open, then the Arabic for closed, then say mashghūl again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does مشغول (mashghūl) mean? ("Busy".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: a palm, a fingernail, a brain, a heel, a lip]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A palm? (كف, kaff.) A fingernail? (ظفر, ẓufr.) A brain? (دماغ, dimāgh.) A heel? (كعب, kaʿb.) A lip? (شفة, shafa.) A tongue? (لسان, lisān.) A belly? (بطن, baṭn.) A leg? (ساق, sāq.) Skin? (جلد, jild.) A bone? (عظم, ʿaẓm.) A bicycle? (دراجة, darrāja.) A ship? (سفينة, safīna.) A traveller? (مسافر, musāfir.) A factory? (مصنع, maṣnaʿ.) A square? (ساحة, sāḥa.) An eraser? (ممحاة, mimḥāh.) A class? (صف, ṣaff.) A student? (طالب, ṭālib.) A job? (وظيفة, waẓīfa.) A salary? (راتب, rātib.)
