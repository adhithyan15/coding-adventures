## You'll want to know: bāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bāo* — "bag".

> *wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí ge bāo* — I'd like to buy a bag.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bāo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *paew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to wrap'. The sense 'bag' is not given there; a thing that wraps is the likely step, but it is not settled.
