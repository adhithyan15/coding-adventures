## You'll want to know: बादशाह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C320-SAMRAT320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बादशाह** — *bādśāh* — "a sovereign; a king of kings".

> बादशाह ने क़िले में एक बड़ी दावत दी।
>
> *bādśāh ne qile meṁ ek baṛī dāvat dī.*
>
> The emperor gave a great feast in the fort.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बादशाह** (*bādśāh*) is usually traced to Persian *pādšāh*, "a king", the title of the Mughal rulers. In stories it is the king of a Persian-style court.
