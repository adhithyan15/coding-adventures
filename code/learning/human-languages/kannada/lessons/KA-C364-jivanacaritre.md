## You'll want to know: ಜೀವನಚರಿತ್ರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C364-DINACHARI364-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೀವನಚರಿತ್ರೆ** — *jīvanacaritre* — "a biography".

> ನಾನು ಆ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಿಯ ಜೀವನಚರಿತ್ರೆ ಓದಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu ā vijñāniya jīvanacaritre ōdide.*
>
> I read that scientist's biography.

Plural: ಜೀವನಚರಿತ್ರೆಗಳು, *jīvanacaritregaḷu*.

ಜೀವನ, life, and ಚರಿತ್ರೆ, an account of deeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೀವನಚರಿತ್ರೆ** (*jīvanacaritre*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of *jīvana*, "life" (CDIAL 5243), and *caritra*, "going, behaviour, deeds" (CDIAL 4691).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
