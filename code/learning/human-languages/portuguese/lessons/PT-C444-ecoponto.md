## You'll want to know: o ecoponto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C444-MOBILIARIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o ecoponto** — "the recycling bins, the recycling point".

> Levamos as garrafas ao ecoponto.
>
> *We take the bottles to the recycling bins.*

Green for glass, blue for paper, yellow for plastic and metal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ecoponto* is a modern formation from *eco-*, cut from *ecologia*, and *ponto* 'point', inherited from Latin *pūnctum* (Lewis & Short).
