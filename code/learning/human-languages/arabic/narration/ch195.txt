Arabic, chapter 195: To resign, To manage, To meet, To get lost, To show the way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استقال (istaqāla) — to resign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to smell, then the Arabic for to hold.

You'll want to know: استقال.
استقال — istaqāla — "to resign".

The word, taken apart.
استقال (istaqāla) is built on the native Arabic root q-y-l, "to cancel a bargain" (Lane). Istaqāla is Form X, "to ask to be released".
