Bengali, chapter 410: Fields and Parks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মাঠ (māṭh) — a field; an open ground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a resident, then the Bengali for to hang out (chatting).

You'll want to know: মাঠ.
মাঠ — māṭh — "a field; an open ground".
বিকেলে ছেলেরা মাঠে ফুটবল খেলে। bikele chhelerā māṭhe phuṭbôl khele. In the afternoons the boys play football on the field.
Open ground for games, meetings and grazing; a field of crops is a খেত.

The word, taken apart.
মাঠ (māṭh) is the open ground of every town and village; where the word comes from is not settled, and CDIAL gives no Bengali form for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
উঠোন (uṭhon) — a courtyard (of a house).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an open ground, then the Bengali for a park.

You'll want to know: উঠোন.
উঠোন — uṭhon — "a courtyard (of a house)".
দিদিমা উঠোনে বসে চাল বাছছিলেন। didimā uṭhone bôse chāl bāchhchhilen. Grandmother sat in the courtyard picking over the rice.
The open yard in the middle of a village house, where work is done and children play.

The word, taken apart.
উঠোন (uṭhon) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali uṭhān, "a courtyard", under Sanskrit upasthā́na, "approach, attendance" and later "an abode" (CDIAL 2275); উঠোন is the everyday spoken form.

মন্দিরের চত্বরে শিশুরা খেলছিল। mondirer chôtvôre shishurā khelchhilô. Children were playing in the temple square.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পায়চারি করা (pāychāri kôrā) — to pace up and down; to stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a courtyard, then the Bengali for a square (open place).

You'll want to know: পায়চারি করা.
পায়চারি করা — pāychāri kôrā — "to pace up and down; to stroll".
অরুণ রাতে ছাদে পায়চারি করল। ôrun rāte chhāde pāychāri kôrlô. At night Arun paced up and down on the roof.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বারান্দায় পায়চারি করলেন, tini bārāndāy pāychāri kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
পায়চারি করা (pāychāri kôrā) joins পায়চারি, "walking to and fro", to করা, to do; the origin of পায়চারি is not settled.
