## You'll want to know: marcharse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C561-PARTIDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**marcharse** — "to leave, to go away".

> Se marchó sin decir adiós.
>
> *He left without saying goodbye.*

Common in Spain; *irse* is used everywhere.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marcharse* is built on *marchar*, which was borrowed from French *marcher* 'to walk, to march' (EtymDB). The French verb is usually traced to a Frankish word for marking out, pressing with the feet.
