# फ़ुटपाथ (fuṭpāth) — a pavement, a footpath

## You'll want to know: फ़ुटपाथ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C403-STAIND403-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ुटपाथ** — *fuṭpāth* — "a pavement, a footpath".

> फ़ुटपाथ पर बहुत भीड़ थी।
>
> *fuṭpāth par bahut bhīṛ thī.*
>
> The pavement was very crowded.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फ़ुटपाथ** (*fuṭpāth*) is borrowed from English *footpath*, in India the raised pavement beside a city road.
