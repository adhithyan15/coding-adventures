## You'll want to know: jiǎndāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C186-THINGS186-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎndāo* — "scissors".

> *jiǎndāo zài nǎr* — Where are the scissors?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎndāo* is built from two parts inherited from Middle Chinese (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): *tsjenX*, glossed 'to cut off' and 'to make even', and *taw*, the word for a knife: a cutting knife.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
