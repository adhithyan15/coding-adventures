# సిద్ధపడు (siddhapaḍu) — to be willing; to get ready

## You'll want to know: సిద్ధపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C426-ARAMBHAM426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సిద్ధపడు** — *siddhapaḍu* — "to be willing; to get ready".

> అతను ఎంత దూరమైనా వెళ్ళడానికి సిద్ధపడ్డాడు.
>
> *atanu enta dūramainā veḷḷaḍāniki siddhapaḍḍāḍu.*
>
> He was ready to go however far it took.

Past (I …): సిద్ధపడ్డాను, *siddhapaḍḍānu*.

సిద్ధం, ready, + పడు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సిద్ధపడు** (*siddhapaḍu*) is built from Sanskrit *siddha*, "accomplished; prepared" (CDIAL 13401), the word in సిద్ధం, and the native verb *paḍu* (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built

One more word for saying what happens if.
