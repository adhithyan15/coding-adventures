## You'll want to know: a escola secundária
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C375-ESCOLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a escola secundária** — "the secondary school".

> A minha irmã anda na escola secundária.
>
> *My sister goes to secondary school.*

In Portugal, years 10 to 12; in speech *a secundária*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escola* is inherited from Latin *schola* "leisure given to learning, a school" (Lewis & Short), from Greek *skholḗ*, and *secundária* comes from Latin *secundārius* "of the second rank", on *secundus* "following".
