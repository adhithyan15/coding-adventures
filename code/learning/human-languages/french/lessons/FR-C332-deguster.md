## You'll want to know: déguster
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C332-ASSAISONNER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**déguster** — "to taste, to savour".

> Nous avons dégusté les fromages de la région.
>
> *We tasted the cheeses of the region.*

To eat or drink slowly, with pleasure. Informal: *il a dégusté* — he really suffered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*déguster* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēgustāre* "to taste" (EtymDB), built on *gustus* "taste".
