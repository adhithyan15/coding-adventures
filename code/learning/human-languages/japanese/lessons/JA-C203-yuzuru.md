## You'll want to know: ゆずる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C203-FLY203-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆずる** — *yuzuru* — "to give up (a seat); to hand over".

> **でんしゃで ざせきを ゆずります。** — *densha de zaseki o yuzurimasu* — "I give up my seat on the train."

Polite **ゆずります**, *yuzurimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆずる** (*yuzuru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
