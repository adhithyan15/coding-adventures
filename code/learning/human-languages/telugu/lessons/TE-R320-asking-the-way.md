# Review — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C320-URU-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "one's home place", "a vote" and "in the meantime". (*ūru*, *ōṭu*, *īlōgā*.)
- [YOU WRITE: the first letter of each, **ఊ**, **ఓ** and **ఈ**, once]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C320-URU-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, TE-LEX-C320-SUTIGA-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C320-ASK-WAY-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C320-GIVE-WAY-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-OBLIGATIVE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-ADAM-VALLA-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-REASON-ORDER-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C319-BETTER-TO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Which town are you from? My home town is small.]
- [YOU SAY: Excuse me, how do I get to the station?]
- [YOU SAY: Go straight on. At the second turning, turn right. It is close.]
- [YOU SAY: It is far, so you had better take an auto.]
- [YOU WRITE: **ఊరు**, **ఓటు**, **ఈలోగా**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C320-URU-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176, TE-LEX-C320-SUTIGA-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C320-ASK-WAY-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C320-GIVE-WAY-01] -->

right"? (**కుడి వైపు**.) "One's home town"? (**ఊరు**, which opens with **ఊ**.)
