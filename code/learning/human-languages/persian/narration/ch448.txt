Persian, chapter 448: Shortcuts, hills and bridges.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
میانبر (miyânbor) — a shortcut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for main, then the Persian for side.

You'll want to know: میانبر.
میانبر (miyânbor) — "a shortcut".
راننده از یک میانبر رفت و زود رسیدیم. Rânande az yek miyânbor raft va zud residim. The driver took a shortcut and we arrived early.
miyân 'middle' + bor 'cutting'.

The word, taken apart.
miyânbor is a native compound of inherited words: miyân 'middle', usually traced to Middle Persian mayān, and bor, the present stem of boridan 'to cut': a way that cuts through the middle.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سربالایی (sarbâlâyi) — an uphill slope, a climb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for side, then the Persian for a shortcut.

You'll want to know: سربالایی.
سربالایی (sarbâlâyi) — "an uphill slope, a climb".
دوچرخه سواری در سربالایی سخت است. Docharkhe-savâri dar sarbâlâyi sakht ast. Cycling uphill is hard.
sar 'head' + bâlâ 'up' + -i.

The word, taken apart.
sarbâlâyi is a native compound of sar 'head', from Middle Persian sar (EtymDB), and bâlâ 'up', usually traced to Middle Persian bālāy, with the ending -i.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سرازیری (sarâziri) — a downhill slope, a descent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a shortcut, then the Persian for an uphill slope.

You'll want to know: سرازیری.
سرازیری (sarâziri) — "a downhill slope, a descent".
در سرازیری ماشین تند میرود. Dar sarâziri mâshin tond miravad. Going downhill the car goes fast.
The opposite of sarbâlâyi.

The word, taken apart.
sarâziri is usually analysed as native sar 'head' and a form of zir 'under, down', with the ending -i: head-downwards. EtymDB does not list the compound.

برای رد شدن از اتوبان از زیرگذر رفتیم. Barâ-ye rad shodan az otobân az zirgozar raftim. To cross the motorway we went through the underpass.

zirgozar is a native compound of zir 'under', which EtymDB lists, and gozar 'passage', the present stem of gozashtan 'to pass'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
روگذر (rugozar) — a flyover, an overpass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a downhill slope, then the Persian for an underpass.

You'll want to know: روگذر.
روگذر (rugozar) — "a flyover, an overpass".
روی روگذر ترافیک سنگین بود. Ru-ye rugozar terâfik sangin bud. The traffic on the flyover was heavy.
ru 'over' + gozar 'passage'; the opposite of zirgozar.

The word, taken apart.
rugozar is a native compound of ru 'face, on top' and gozar 'passage', the present stem of gozashtan 'to pass', which is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Middle Persian verb 'to pass, to cross'.
