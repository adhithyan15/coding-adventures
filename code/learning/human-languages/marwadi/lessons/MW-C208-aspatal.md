## You'll want to know: अस्पताल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C208-OFFICES208-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्पताल** — *aspatāl* — "a hospital".

> अस्पताल नदी कनै है।
>
> *aspatāl nadī kanai hai.*
>
> The hospital is near the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्पताल** (*aspatāl*) is borrowed from English *hospital*, reshaped in everyday speech.
