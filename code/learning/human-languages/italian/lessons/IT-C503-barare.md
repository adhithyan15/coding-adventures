## You'll want to know: barare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C503-GIOCHI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**barare** — "to cheat (at games)".

> Non gioco più con te: bari sempre!
>
> *I am not playing with you any more: you always cheat!*

Built on *il baro* "a card cheat". In exams, *copiare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barare* is built on *baro* "a cheat at cards" (EtymDB); the origin of *baro* is not settled.
