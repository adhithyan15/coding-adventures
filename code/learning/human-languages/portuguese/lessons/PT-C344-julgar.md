## You'll want to know: julgar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C344-JUIZO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**julgar** — "to judge; to think, to believe".

> Não é justo julgar uma pessoa pela roupa.
>
> *It is not fair to judge a person by their clothes.*

Also to think: *julgo que sim* — I think so.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*julgar* is inherited from Latin *iūdicāre* "to judge, to pass sentence" (EtymDB), built on *iūdex* "judge" (Lewis & Short).
