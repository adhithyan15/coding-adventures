## You'll want to know: جيش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C307-JAYSH307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جيش** — *jaysh* — "an army".

> عبر الجيش النهر في الليل.
>
> *ʿabara al-jaysh an-nahr fī al-layl.*
>
> The army crossed the river at night.

Plural: جيوش, *juyūsh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جيش** (*jaysh*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-y-sh* (Lane), where *jāsha* means "to rise, to heave, to surge". Lane gives *jaysh* as an army, or men going out to war.
