## You'll want to know: 要么
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C541-FINAL541-05]; assesses=[] -->

**要么** — *yàome* — "either one or the other; either … or".

> **要么你来要么我去** *yàome nǐ lái yàome wǒ qù* — Either you come here or I go there.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**要么** (*yàome*) is built from 要 and the light ending 么, as in 怎么. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *'jiewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the second is said light, and its history in this use is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
