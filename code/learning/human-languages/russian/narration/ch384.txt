Russian, chapter 384: Cramming and crib sheets.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
зубрить (zubrít) — to cram, to learn by rote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a faculty, then the Russian for a student grant.

You'll want to know: зубрить.
зубрить (zubrít) — "to cram, to learn by rote".
Перед экзаменом он зубрил всю ночь. Péred ekzámenom on zubríl vsyu noch. Before the exam he crammed all night.
Imperfective; present зубрю, зубришь; perfective вызубрить. Colloquial.

The word, taken apart.
зубрить (zubrít) is usually linked with зуб "a tooth", inherited from Proto-Slavic zǫbŭ: to gnaw away at a subject until it is learned.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for to memorise, then the Russian for to skip.

You'll want to know: шпаргалка.
шпаргалка (shpargálka) — "a crib sheet".
На экзамене у него нашли шпаргалку. Na ekzámene u negó nashlí shpargálku. They found a crib sheet on him during the exam.
Feminine. Colloquial; students often say шпора.

The word, taken apart.
шпаргалка (shpargálka) is usually traced to Polish szpargał "an old scrap of paper"; the further origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
отвлекаться (otvlekátsya) — to get distracted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to skip, then the Russian for a crib sheet.

You'll want to know: отвлекаться.
отвлекаться (otvlekátsya) — "to get distracted".
Когда я работаю, я часто отвлекаюсь на телефон. Kogdá ya rabótayu, ya chásto otvlekáyus na telefón. When I work, I often get distracted by my phone.
Imperfective; perfective отвлечься. Отвлекаться на + accusative — to be distracted by.

The word, taken apart.
отвлекаться (otvlekátsya) is built from от- "away" and влечь "to drag, to pull", an inherited Slavic verb: to be pulled away from your work.
