## You'll want to know: gurgeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C530-BAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gurgeln** — "to gargle".

> Bei Halsschmerzen gurgelt sie mit Salzwasser.
>
> *When she has a sore throat, she gargles with salt water.*

Regular: *hat gegurgelt*. Also of water running away: *Das Wasser gurgelt im Abfluss.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gurgeln* is built on *Gurgel* "throat", which comes from Latin *gurguliō* "throat, windpipe" (EtymDB).
