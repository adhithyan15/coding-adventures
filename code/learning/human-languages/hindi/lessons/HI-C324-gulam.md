## You'll want to know: ग़ुलाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C324-VIDROH324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ग़ुलाम** — *ġulām* — "a slave".

> उस राजा के महल में सौ ग़ुलाम थे।
>
> *us rājā ke mahal meṁ sau ġulām the.*
>
> There were a hundred slaves in that king's palace.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ग़ुलाम** (*ġulām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghulām*, "a youth, a servant". In the history of freedom movements it also means a people ruled by others.
