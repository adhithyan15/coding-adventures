## You'll want to know: उपकरोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C308-SAMUHA308-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपकरोति** — *upakaroti* — "does a kindness; helps out".

> सः दरिद्रस्य उपकरोति।
>
> *saḥ daridrasya upakaroti.*
>
> He does a poor man a kindness.

Past (he …): सः दरिद्रस्य उपकृतवान्, *saḥ daridrasya upakṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपकरोति** (*upakaroti*) is built from *upa*, "near, towards", and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives *upa-kṛ* as to assist, help, favour (MW).
