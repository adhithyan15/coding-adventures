# కాకపోతే (kākapōtē) — if not; otherwise

## You'll want to know: కాకపోతే
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C419-KAKAPOTE419-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కాకపోతే** — *kākapōtē* — "if not; otherwise".

> బస్సు దొరికితే వస్తాను, కాకపోతే రేపు వస్తాను.
>
> *bassu dorikitē vastānu, kākapōtē rēpu vastānu.*
>
> If I get a bus I will come; if not, I will come tomorrow.

A cousin of లేకపోతే: if it does not turn out so.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కాకపోతే** (*kākapōtē*) is native Dravidian: *kā-*, a stem of *avu*, "to be" (DEDR 333), and *pōtē*, "if it goes", a form of *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
