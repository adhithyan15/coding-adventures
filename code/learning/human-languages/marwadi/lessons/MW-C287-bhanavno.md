## You'll want to know: भणावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C287-GURU287-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भणावणो** — *bhaṇāvṇo* — "to teach lessons".

> मास्टर भासा भणावै है।
>
> *māsṭar bhāsā bhaṇāvai hai.*
>
> The teacher teaches language.

Past (I …): म्हैं पाठ भणायो, *mhaĩ pāṭh bhaṇāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भणावणो** (*bhaṇāvṇo*) is built on **भणणो**, to study, as its causative: "to make study". CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bhaṇaï*, "recites" (CDIAL 9383).
