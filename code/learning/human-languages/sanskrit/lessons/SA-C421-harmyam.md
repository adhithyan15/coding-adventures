## You'll want to know: हर्म्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C421-PALLI421-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हर्म्यम्** — *harmyam* — "a mansion".

> नगरे धनिकस्य विशालं हर्म्यम् अस्ति।
>
> *nagare dhanikasya viśālaṃ harmyam asti.*
>
> In the city there is a rich man's huge mansion.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हर्म्यम्** (*harmyam*) is the word MW gives for a large house, a palace, a mansion; MW doubts the old derivation from √hṛ and connects it rather with *gharma*, "heat", so its origin is uncertain (MW).
