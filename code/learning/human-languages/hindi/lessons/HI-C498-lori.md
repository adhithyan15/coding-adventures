## You'll want to know: लोरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C498-PARVARISH498-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लोरी** — *lorī* — "a lullaby".

> माँ ने लोरी गाकर बच्चे को सुलाया।
>
> *māṁ ne lorī gākar bacce ko sulāyā.*
>
> Mother sang a lullaby and got the baby to sleep.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोरी** (*lorī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *lorī*, "lullaby", under *\*lōḍa-*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11136).
