## You'll want to know: le cerf
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C459-ANIMAUX-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le cerf** — "the stag, the deer".

> Au lever du jour, nous avons vu un cerf dans la clairière.
>
> *At daybreak we saw a stag in the clearing.*

The *f* is silent: *le cerf* rhymes with *serre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cerf* is inherited through Old French from Latin *cervus* 'deer' (EtymDB).
