## You'll want to know: वंश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C308-PIDHI308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वंश** — *vaṁś* — "a lineage; a dynasty".

> त्या घराण्याचा वंश अजून चालू आहे.
>
> *tyā gharāṇyācā vaṁś ajūn cālū āhe.*
>
> That family's line still continues.

Masculine.

राजवंश, a royal dynasty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वंश** (*vaṁś*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vaṁśa*, 'a bamboo; a lineage' (Monier-Williams): a family line grows joint by joint, like bamboo.
