## You'll want to know: ひらく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C285-MOYOOSHI285-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ひらく** — *hiraku* — "to hold (an event); to open".

> **にちようびに こうえんで まつりを ひらきます。** — *nichiyōbi ni kōen de matsuri o hirakimasu* — "We hold a festival in the park on Sunday."

Polite **ひらきます**, *hirakimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せいふが かいぎを ひらきました。**, *seifu ga kaigi o hirakimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひらく** (*hiraku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
