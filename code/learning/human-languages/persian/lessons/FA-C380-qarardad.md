## You'll want to know: قرارداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C380-REZUME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قرارداد** (*qarârdâd*) — "a contract".

> او یک قرارداد یک ساله امضا کرد.
>
> *u yek qarârdâd-e yek sâle emzâ kard.*
>
> He signed a one-year contract.

*Qarâr* "agreement" + *dâd* "gave". *Qarârdâd bastan* — to sign a contract.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرارداد** joins *qarâr*, from Arabic *qarār* "a settled decision", to *dâd*, the past stem of *dâdan* "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE *deh₃-*: an agreement that has been given.
