Bengali, chapter 498: A Business of One's Own.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উদ্যোগ (udyog) — an initiative; an enterprise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for dismissed, then the Bengali for to lay off.

You'll want to know: উদ্যোগ.
উদ্যোগ — udyog — "an initiative; an enterprise".
এই নতুন উদ্যোগে গ্রামের মহিলারা কাজ পেল। Ei nôtun udyoge grāmer môhilārā kāj pelô. Through this new initiative the village women found work.

The word, taken apart.
উদ্যোগ (udyog) is a tatsama, Sanskrit udyoga, "the act of undertaking something, exertion" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
অংশীদার (ôngshīdār) — a business partner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a line of business, then the Bengali for capital.

You'll want to know: অংশীদার.
অংশীদার — ôngshīdār — "a business partner".
আমার বন্ধু এই ব্যবসায় আমার অংশীদার। Āmār bôndhu ei bæbsāy āmār ôngshīdār. My friend is my partner in this business.

The word, taken apart.
অংশীদার (ôngshīdār) joins Sanskrit aṃśa, "a share" (Monier-Williams), to the Persian ending -dār, "one who holds": a holder of a share.
