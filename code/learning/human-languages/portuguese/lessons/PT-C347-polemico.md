## You'll want to know: polémico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C347-POLEMICA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**polémico** — "controversial".

> É um tema polémico: as pessoas discutem muito.
>
> *It is a controversial topic: people argue a lot.*

Feminine *polémica*. Brazil *polêmico*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*polémico* is usually traced to French *polémique*, which EtymDB derives from Greek *polemikós* "of war", built on *pólemos* "war" (EtymDB).
