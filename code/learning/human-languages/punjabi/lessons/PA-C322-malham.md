## You'll want to know: ਮਲ੍ਹਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C322-HURT322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਲ੍ਹਮ** — *malham* — "an ointment".

> ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਸੱਟ ਉੱਤੇ ਮਲ੍ਹਮ ਮਲੀ।
>
> *rāt nū̃ maiṁ saṭṭ utte malham malī.*
>
> At night I rubbed ointment on the injury.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਲ੍ਹਮ** (*malham*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *marham*, "an ointment".
