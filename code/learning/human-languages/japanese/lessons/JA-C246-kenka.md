## You'll want to know: けんか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C246-ANGER246-02]; assesses=[] -->

**けんか** — *kenka* — "a quarrel; a fight".

> **あには ともだちと けんかを しません。** — *ani wa tomodachi to kenka o shimasen* — "My elder brother does not quarrel with his friends."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんか** (*kenka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "noisy, boisterous" (Middle Chinese *xjwon*), and *ka*, "noisy", whose Middle Chinese *xwae* is listed under a variant spelling that JMdict also gives (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
