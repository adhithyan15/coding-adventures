## You'll want to know: تاس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C509-GAMES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تاس** (*tâs*) — "a die (pl. dice)".

> او تاس را انداخت و شش آمد.
>
> *u tâs râ andâkht va shesh âmad.*
>
> He threw the die and got a six.

*tâs andâkhtan* or *tâs rikhtan* — to roll the dice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tâs* is the Persian word for a die. EtymDB's only entry under this spelling is *tâs*, 'cup', listed without a source, and the origin of the word for a die is not settled.
