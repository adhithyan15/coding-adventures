Lesson 1 of 5.
le drap — a sheet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for clear, light (of a colour), then the French for distant.

You'll want to know: le drap.
le drap — "a sheet".

The word, taken apart.
drap from Late Latin drappus, "cloth", probably a Gaulish word — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la casserole — a saucepan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a drawer, then the French for an armchair.

You'll want to know: la casserole.
la casserole — "a saucepan".

The word, taken apart.
casserole — built from an older word for "a pan", from Old Provençal cassa, with a little-thing ending. cassa goes back to Medieval Latin cattia, "a ladle, a pan".
