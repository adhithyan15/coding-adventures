## You'll want to know: चटका बसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C487-CATKA487-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चटका बसणे** — *caṭkā basṇe* — "to get a burn".

> गरम तव्याला हात लागला आणि चटका बसला.
>
> *garam tavyālā hāt lāglā āṇi caṭkā baslā.*
>
> My hand touched the hot griddle and I got burnt.

Past (to him …): त्याला चटका बसला, *tyālā caṭkā baslā*.

Dative: मला चटका बसला. Also a sudden pang of grief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चटका बसणे** (*caṭkā basṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on चटका, which CDIAL lists as Marathi *caṭkā*, "glow", under *\*caṭa* (CDIAL 4570), with बसणे.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
