## You'll want to know: aprovar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C379-CHUMBAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aprovar** — "to pass (a student); to approve".

> O professor aprovou todos os alunos.
>
> *The teacher passed all the students.*

*Ser aprovado* — to pass. Also to approve a plan or a law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aprovar* comes from Latin *approbāre* "to regard as good, to approve" (EtymDB), built from *ad* and *probāre* "to esteem as good, to prove" (Lewis & Short).
