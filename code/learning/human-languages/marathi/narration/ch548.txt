Marathi, chapter 548: Obstacles.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अडथळा (aḍthaḷā) — an obstacle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a security guard, then the Marathi for to bring under control.

You'll want to know: अडथळा.
अडथळा — aḍthaḷā — "an obstacle".
रस्त्यात पडलेलं झाड हा मोठा अडथळा होता. rastyāt paḍlelaṁ jhāḍ hā moṭhā aḍthaḷā hotā. The tree lying on the road was a big obstacle.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अडथळा (aḍthaḷā) begins with अड, "obstruction": CDIAL lists Marathi aḍ, "obstruction", and aḍṇẽ, "to be stopped", under aḍ, "obstruct, stop" (CDIAL 187). The second part is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तोडगा (toḍgā) — a way out; a solution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to bring under control, then the Marathi for an obstacle.

You'll want to know: तोडगा.
तोडगा — toḍgā — "a way out; a solution".
दोन्ही भावांनी भांडणावर एक चांगला तोडगा काढला. donhī bhāvānnī bhāṇḍṇāvar ek cāṅglā toḍgā kāḍhlā. The two brothers found a good way out of the quarrel.
Masculine.
तोडगा (toḍgā) काढणे, to find a way out.

The word, taken apart.
तोडगा (toḍgā) is usually traced to तोडणे, "to break", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit trōṭayati, "breaks" (CDIAL 6079): a way of breaking a deadlock. The ending -गा is not explained there.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पेलणे (pelṇe) — to bear a load; to shoulder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an obstacle, then the Marathi for a way out.

You'll want to know: पेलणे.
पेलणे — pelṇe — "to bear a load; to shoulder".
घराची सगळी जबाबदारी तिने एकटीने पेलली. gharācī sagḷī jabābdārī tine ekṭīne pellī. She shouldered all the household's responsibility alone.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने जबाबदारी पेलली, tyāne jabābdārī pellī.

The word, taken apart.
पेलणे (pelṇe) may be inherited: CDIAL lists a Marathi pelṇẽ, "to push", under Sanskrit prērayati, "sets in motion" (CDIAL 9002), but not the sense "to bear a load", so the link is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
निस्तरणे (nistarṇe) — to sort out; to set right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a way out, then the Marathi for to bear a load.

You'll want to know: निस्तरणे.
निस्तरणे — nistarṇe — "to sort out; to set right".
भावाने केलेली चूक मला निस्तरावी लागली. bhāvāne kelelī cūk malā nistarāvī lāglī. I had to sort out the mistake my brother made.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चूक निस्तरली, tyāne cūk nistarlī.

The word, taken apart.
निस्तरणे (nistarṇe) is usually traced to Sanskrit nistaraṇa, "getting across, rescue" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: डळमळणे.
डळमळणे — ḍaḷmaḷṇe — "to waver; to wobble".
अडचणी आल्या तरी त्याचा निर्धार डळमळला नाही. aḍcaṇī ālyā tarī tyācā nirdhār ḍaḷmaḷlā nāhī. Even when difficulties came, his determination did not waver.
Past (he …): तो डळमळला, to ḍaḷmaḷlā.

The word, taken apart.
डळमळणे (ḍaḷmaḷṇe) is an expressive Marathi verb for wobbling; its origin is not settled.
